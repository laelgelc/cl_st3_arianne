What has been technical and executive feasibility of your organization 's concept ? <p> Rising : Nuclear power is a proven technology which has consistently provided affordable and reliable low-carbon electricity around the world since the 1950s . History has shown , time and time again , that nuclear power plants can be built quickly . France , which today has one of the world 's cleanest electricity systems thanks to nuclear , managed to expand nuclear from 5 percent to 75 percent of the country-wide supply in about 20 years , and almost eliminated fossil fuels from the power sector .
