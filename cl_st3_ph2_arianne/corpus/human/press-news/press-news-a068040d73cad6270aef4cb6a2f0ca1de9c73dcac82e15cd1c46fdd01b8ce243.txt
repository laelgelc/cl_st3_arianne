<h> Change is inevitable , but you can help design it , says Collingwood Climate Action Team <p> Sue Volpe and Catherine Daw are part of the leadership team for the Collingwood Climate Action Team . Erika Engel/CollingwoodToday <p> A newly formed team in Collingwood aims to take the dread out of climate crisis with individual action . <p> The Collingwood Climate Action Team formed in October with a screening of the film Resilience : Transforming our Community . " I think it 's been a catalyst for the group .
