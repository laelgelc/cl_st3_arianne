<h> Ireland will see dramatic changes in climate by mid-century , report finds <p> Ireland will see dramatic changes in climate by the middle of this century . This comes from climate scientists at the Irish Centre for High-End Computing ( ICHEC ) . <p> The team completed a series of sophisticated high-resolution ( 4km ) Regional Climate Model ( RCM ) simulations for Ireland , which indicated temperature increases of 1-1.6 ? c with enhanced warming in the east , as well as more extreme weather events , and less frost and snow . <p> The first systematic study of this kind at this scale , ICHEC climate scientist and project lead , Dr Paul Nolan , ran an ensemble of RCMs on the national supercomputer , Kay , to generate high-resolution climate projection data , which allowed for a more detailed assessment of the potential regional and local effects of climate change .
