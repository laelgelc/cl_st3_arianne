<h> UN lauds launch of market tool assessing firms ' climate risk <p> The United Nations is applauding the launch of a financial tool to track companies ' efforts to prepare for climate change . Thirteen funds and five firms managing over 2 trillion pounds ( USD 2.4 trillion ) launched the online tool at the London Stock Exchange today . Called the Transition Pathway Initiative , it allows asset managers to check what companies have done to prepare for a low-carbon economy . Burning fossil fuels , which releases carbon into the atmosphere , is considered one of the main drivers of man-made climate change . UN climate chief Patricia Espinosa said the new tool could help ensure financial investments support efforts to limit and prepare for climate change .
