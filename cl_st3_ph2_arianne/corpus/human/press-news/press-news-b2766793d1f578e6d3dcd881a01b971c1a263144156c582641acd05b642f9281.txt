These sources of stress are likely to diverge under climate change , with a globally enhanced VPD but more variable and uncertain changes in soil moisture . Here , using a model -- data fusion approach , we demonstrate that the common empirical approach used in most Earth system models to evaluate the ET response to soil moisture and VPD , which neglects plant hydraulics , underestimates ET sensitivity to VPD and compensates by overestimating the sensitivity to soil moisture stress @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plant better captures ET under high VPD conditions for wide-ranging soil moisture states .
