On Friday , Federal Energy Minister Josh Frydenberg will meet his state counterparts in Canberra for a Council of Australian Governments meeting that will decide if work is to continue on the Turnbull Government 's National Energy Guarantee . <p> The NEG is far from perfect but it is the most promising compromise that has appeared for a long time on the horizon of the interlocking climate and energy debates . It imposes a twin obligation on energy retailers to reduce carbon emissions and at the same time maintain reliability standards .
