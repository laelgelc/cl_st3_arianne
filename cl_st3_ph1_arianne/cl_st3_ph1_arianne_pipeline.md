#### WRI listing manifest

- Saved listing pages 10–46 are in `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_listings/`. The local parser `corpus/00_sources/ngo/parse_wri_listings.py` generated `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_list.jsonl` with 740 unique records. This is an unfiltered listing manifest, not the final article sample.
- The manifest records titles, URLs, content types, tags, listing dates and listing-page provenance. Dates are date-only; authors are not available from the listings. Duplicate URLs are merged, and conflicting metadata causes an error.
- Run the parser from the project root with `python cl_st3_ph1_arianne/corpus/00_sources/ngo/parse_wri_listings.py` (requires `beautifulsoup4`). It reads local files and overwrites the manifest without making network requests. See `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_manifest.md` for options and validation details.
