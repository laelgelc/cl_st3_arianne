#### WRI article collection and extraction

- Target 67 articles originally published from 1 November 2015 through 30 November 2022, inclusive. Verify the original publication date on each article; listing dates alone do not establish eligibility.
- A reproducible page-stratified sample of 67 listing-date-eligible candidates is recorded in `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_article_collection_log.md`, generated by `select_wri_articles.py` with seed `20261118`: one from each of pages 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40, and two from each other page. This is not a uniform random sample of all eligible articles; verify original dates and English language and record exclusions and replacements.
- The page 23 entry `wri-p23-02.html` was manually replaced because the original article was in French: [original, 2019-11-18](https://www.wri.org/insights/pour-la-durabilite-de-la-production-du-cacao-les-pays-et-les-societes-doivent-sengager); [replacement, 2019-11-08](https://www.wri.org/insights/us-climate-action-week-hope-amidst-despair).
- The selected pages were saved in `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_pages/` using the filenames and URLs in the collection log. Regenerating the log overwrites any manual notes; see `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri_manifest.md` for details.

  Run the selector to regenerate the log:

  ```shell
  python corpus/00_sources/ngo/select_wri_articles.py
  ```

- Extract the saved pages locally with `beautifulsoup4` installed:

  ```shell
  python corpus/00_sources/ngo/parse_wri_articles.py
  ```

  The parser creates `corpus/00_sources/ngo/wri.jsonl` exclusively and will not overwrite an existing file; use `--output` to write a different path. It reconciles the log and canonical URLs, checks dates and English page metadata, and reports excluded or missing pages. Records contain paragraphs and list items in source order, with repeated article metadata, including topics and explicit regions; missing regions are null. `Post Date` is the displayed original date as a date-only ISO string, not an inferred timestamp. The saved version may include later edits; tables, captions and callouts are excluded.

- The output is provisional. Manually check the language, original dates, text boundaries and any later updates, and resolve the reported exclusions or document replacements in the collection log before treating the sample as final. No extra WWF keyword filter is applied to this climate-listing sample.
- The current output contains 1,144 records from 67 articles. For `wri-p18-01.html` and `wri-p43-01.html`, the displayed dates (9 December 2020 and 15 December 2015) are used instead of the conflicting metadata dates. The four articles with external canonical URLs (`wri-p20-01.html`, `wri-p21-01.html`, `wri-p28-01.html` and `wri-p31-01.html`) are included as WRI material, preserving both URLs. The saved `wri-p28-01.html` is an excerpt directing readers to CNN, not the complete article. No explicit regions were extracted.
