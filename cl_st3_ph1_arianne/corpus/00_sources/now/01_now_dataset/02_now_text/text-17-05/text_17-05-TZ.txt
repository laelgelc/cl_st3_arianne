@@18734207 <h> NGVA Europe validates natural gas for decarbonisation <p> NGVA 's study ' Greenhouse gas intensity of natural gas ' shows that natural gas reduces GHG emissions from passenger cars on a well-to-wheel ( WtW ) basis by 23% compared with petrol and by 7% compared with diesel .
