It allows countries , companies , or organizations to buy and sell permits that represent the right to emit a certain amount of carbon dioxide or other greenhouse gases . Companies receive or buy carbon credits , which allow them to emit a certain amount of CO2 . If a company emits less than its allowance , it can sell its excess credits to others . If a company exceeds its limit , it must buy more credits or face penalties . <p> Carbon trading is a benefit of reducing emissions because it creates financial incentives for businesses to go green . However , it works best when proper regulations and transparency ensure that actual emission reductions occur . <h> What Is the Process of Carbon Credit Sale ? <p> The process of selling carbon credits involves several steps , from generating the credits to finding buyers and completing the transaction . These credits come from projects that remove or reduce CO2 emissions , such as reforestation , renewable energy , and carbon capture . <h> Retirement or Trading <p> Buyers can retire credits which means removal from circulation to claim the offset . Retirement is recorded in the registry to ensure transparency . However , they can also resell credits to other buyers . <h> Benefits of Carbon Trading <p> The system of carbon emissions trading can encourage emission reductions . Companies have a financial incentive to cut emissions to save or sell credits . <h> Challenges and Limitations of Carbon Trading <p> Carbon trading can create the risk of " Greenwashing " ; which means some companies may buy credits instead of making real emission cuts . <p> Price volatility is another challenge in carbon emissions trading . Carbon credit prices fluctuate , making it uncertain for businesses . <p> Enforcement issues can also hinder the process of carbon trading in many countries . Weak regulations or loopholes can allow companies to exploit the system @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ financial incentives to industries and businesses to reduce emissions .
