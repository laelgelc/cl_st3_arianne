@@1840261 <h> Kenyans to get 3.3mn energy saving bulbs <p> Compact fluorescent lamps ( CFLs ) which will benefit about one million Kenya Power customers . <p> NAIROBI , Kenya , Jun 13 -- The government , in conjunction with Kenya Power , is set to distribute 3.3 million free energy saving bulbs in a countrywide exercise set to begin later this year . <p> The project , implemented under the Clean Development Mechanism 's ( CDM ) efficient lighting programme entails replacement of ordinary bulbs ( incandescent lamps ) with compact fluorescent lamps ( CFLs ) which will benefit about one million Kenya Power customers . <p> " Selected target customers for the project include low income households and Kenya Power 's Stima Loan ( revolving fund ) customers in various parts of the country , who will get a maximum of four bulbs , " read the statement sent to newsrooms . <p> Kenya Power says the project will help reduce demand for electricity as the bulbs use less electricity and consequently reduce power bills for customers .
