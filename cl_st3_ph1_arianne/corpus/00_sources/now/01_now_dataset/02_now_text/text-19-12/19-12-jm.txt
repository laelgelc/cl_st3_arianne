Ten years later , petroleum accounts for 74.1 per cent , wind 10 per cent and hydro 2.8 per cent . <p> Natural gas and solar , which were added to the mix , provide 11.1 per cent and two per cent , respectively , of energy solutions . <p> " These statistics demonstrate that we are on a mission to secure Jamaica 's energy future through diversification and the provision of alternative , sustainable and environmentally friendly sources of energy , " Mrs. Williams said . <p> Meanwhile , the Minister noted that renewables and other clean energy sources will be at the core of the development of the country 's electricity sector . <p> " We boast some of the largest solar and wind facilities in the Caribbean with the 20-megawatt Content Solar and the 37-megawatt Eight Rivers Facility , in addition to the 62.7-megawatt Wigton Windfarm and the 36-megawatt BMR Wind facilities , " she said . <p> She said the Government has set a target of 30 per cent of renewables in electricity generation by 2030 , and by 2020 is expected to generate 18 per cent of its electricity from renewable sources . <p> Permanent Secretary in the Ministry , Carol Palmer , for her part , said she is pleased to be celebrating the anniversary of the National Energy Policy , which was the first long-term strategy to ensure that the country achieves a modern , efficient , diversified and environmentally sustainable energy sector by 2030 . <p> She said that the policy must not be taken for granted , as energy plays a major role in the development of the economy . <p> " Having a policy to strategically guide our plans has become absolutely critical , especially at a time when we are battling the effects of climate change and volatility in prices for imported petroleum , " Mrs. Palmer noted . <p> " We are regional leaders in energy because of our national policy .
