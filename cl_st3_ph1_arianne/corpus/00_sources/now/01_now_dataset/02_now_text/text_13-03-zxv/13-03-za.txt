Malawi currently produces approximately 287 MW from mainly hydro resources which are subject to climate change and unlike coal-fired plants , are not base load generation . The coal supply for this generating facility will be supplied from IEC 's Malcoal mine in Malawi and be backed by available coal from IEC 's mining operations in Tanzania .
