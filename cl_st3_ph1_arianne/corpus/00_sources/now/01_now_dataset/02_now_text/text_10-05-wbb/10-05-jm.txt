@@4514325 <h> Environment <h> Youths shine in IOJ 's annual Earth Day competition <h> Our Habitat <p> THE Natural History Museum of Jamaica , a division of the Institute of Jamaica , joined with the United Nations in celebrating the International Year of Biodiversity ( 2010 ) by hosting its annual Earth Day competition under the theme " Biodiversity at risk ! " <p> The International Year of Biodiversity is themed " Biodiversity is life : Biodiversity is our Life " and is a celebration of life on earth and of the value of biodiversity to our lives . The world is invited to take action in 2010 to safeguard biodiversity : the variety of life on earth . <p> Sixty-six students from 20 secondary schools around the island competed for prizes in the three categories of the competition .
