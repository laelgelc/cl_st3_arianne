@@19450265 <h> Financing secured for Bangladesh 's first LNG project <p> IFC , a member of the World Bank Group , and Excelerate Energy Bangladesh Limited ( Excelerate ) are co-developing the Moheshkhali Floating LNG project -- Bangladesh 's first LNG import terminal . IFC and Excelerate have announced securing the debt financing for the project to help expand access to clean energy in Bangladesh .
