Geoengineering -- the large-scale manipulation of the Earth 's natural systems -- has been popularized as a means of counteracting the negative effects of climate change . <p> Proponents of this science feed the illusion that there is a way to engineer an exit from the climate crisis , meet the goals of the 2015 Paris climate agreement and maintain a consumption-heavy lifestyle . <p> But this solution is not as simple as proponents would have us believe . Betting on climate engineering -- either as a planetary insurance policy or as a last-ditch measure to combat rising temperatures -- is not only risky ; it also directs attention away from the only solution we know will work : reducing carbon emissions . <p> Each of the engineered technologies being discussed carries dangers and uncertainties .
