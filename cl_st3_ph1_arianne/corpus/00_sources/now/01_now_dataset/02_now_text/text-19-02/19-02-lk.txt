A reconstruction strategy centred on building infrastructure , expansion of credit to promote self-employment and a narrow focus on private-sector investment , however , have failed to meet expectations in delivering economic growth and distributing it in ways to make a real difference to people 's lives . The task of reviewing the effectiveness of development strategies came to the fore following public meetings led by the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka on the burgeoning indebtedness crisis in the North in November 2017 . Commissioned by the Central Bank and authored by an independent committee of academics and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for a Northern Province Master Plan ' launched on February 8 , 2019 offers a forward-looking blueprint for the North 's development .
