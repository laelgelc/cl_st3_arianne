The concept of resilience has gained momentum in the international development agendas -- especially in countries where climate change will hit hard , like Vietnam . The concept has also entered private sector discourse for companies with long-term thinking and who want to address the underlying factors that make a community or system vulnerable to external stresses . Communities and economies that can respond to , absorb , or recover from external stresses or shocks such as natural disasters simply make for a better business environment . With crises inevitable , investing in resilience building makes sense . However , it requires a skill-set that is not typically found in executive education : understanding and addressing risks before external shocks or stresses throw a community into crises .
