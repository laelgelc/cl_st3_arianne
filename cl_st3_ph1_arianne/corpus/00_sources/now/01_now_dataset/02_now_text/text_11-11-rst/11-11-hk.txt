TAB , whose product offerings include ready-to-drink tea , bottled water and juice beverages , is one of the leading beverage manufacturers in China . <p> PepsiCo currently operates its China beverage business through 24 company-owned and joint venture bottling operations . PepsiCo will retain branding and marketing responsibilities for these products . <p> TAB also will begin co-branding its juice products under the Tropicana brand name under a license from PepsiCo .
