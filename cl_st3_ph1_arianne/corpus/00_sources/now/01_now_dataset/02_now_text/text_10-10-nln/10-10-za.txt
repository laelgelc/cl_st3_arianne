@@1656487 <h> Social development <h> ' Use technology to improve housing ' <p> 1 October 2010 The Department of Human Settlements is on a quest to find new technologies that are both cost-effective and quality-oriented to provide better housing for poorer South Africans . The two-day indaba brought together officials from all spheres of government , the private sector , regulatory authorities , scientific , research and tertiary institutions .
