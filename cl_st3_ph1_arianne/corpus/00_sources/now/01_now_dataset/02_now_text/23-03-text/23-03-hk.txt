@@97861124 <h> New draft rules for CETP plants <p> Draft rules for regulating common effluent treatment plants ( CETP ) were issued by the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change in January 2023 . <p> The rules seek to regulate the responsibilities of state governments and CETP managers in meeting wastewater standards under the Environment ( Protection ) Rules , 1986 . Industries that use CETPs are required to meet the standards for primary wastewater treatment facilities specified individually , monitor wastewater quality parameters and flow rates , and submit monitoring data to the CETP operating agency in real time . <p> CETP operating agencies shall have the authority to access industry premises without notice to collect wastewater samples . The online continuous effluent monitoring system , if installed , shall also be accessible to the CETP operating agencies for cross-verification of the monitored values with online data . <p> The ministry has given powers to CETP operating agencies to take action against non-compliant member units . The rules will come into effect one year after publication in the Official Gazette .
