We also accept falcons as checked baggage , " Etihad 's website says . <p> An Etihad spokesperson told Business Insider that based on the cabin and flight crew uniforms , it 's unlikely the Reddit photo was taken on one of the airline 's planes . <p> So if you 're looking to transport a falcon in the near future , it may not be as hard as you think -- depending on where you 're going , of course .
