the size of two three-room flats - was launched yesterday and is the brainchild of the Building and Construction Authority ( BCA ) . <p> It will be used to test-bed technologies to bring more " positive energy " buildings to life . <p> Positive-energy buildings are those that can produce more energy than they consume by , for instance , harnessing solar energy , while cutting energy use with more efficient air-conditioning or lighting systems . <p> The medium-term aspiration is to develop such positive-energy low-rise and medium-rise buildings that are energy self-sufficient , and energy-efficient high-rise buildings , said Dr John Keung , chief executive of BCA . <p> Launching the BCA SkyLab at the BCA Academy in Braddell , Prime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economy developed , its building activities grew in scale and complexity , and the focus is now on environmental sustainability . <p> " The BCA SkyLab that we are opening today will play an important role in our environmental sustainability drive , " he said . <p> The $4.5 million rotatable lab , which takes 30 minutes for one round to be completed , will allow researchers to test new systems with actual weather and climatic conditions , as opposed to simulating them using historical data that may not be accurate due to climate change . <p> The centre was developed in collaboration with the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory in California , which has a similar rotating lab suited for a temperate climate . <p> The BCA facility is the first of its kind in the tropics . <p> " The good thing about a rotatable lab is that you can tune it by a few degrees and see if it makes a difference , " said Dr Keung . <p> So if a research team has tested a technology when the lab faces east , rotating it by 20 degrees @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the team can tell if the effectiveness of the system is affected , he explained . <p> " It gives you the flexibility to answer many ' what ifs ' .
