Second , there is widespread poaching of native and rare orchids . <p> Our native orchids are amazingly beautiful and they thrive in their natural habitat , where they get adequate sunlight , rain , and air . It is very difficult to grow orchid species in the artificial environment at home and there is a strong possibility that these orchid species would eventually perish .
