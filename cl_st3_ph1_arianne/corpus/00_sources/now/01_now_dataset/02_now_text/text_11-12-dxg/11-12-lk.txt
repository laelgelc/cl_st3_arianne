Karunawathie cultivates runner beans , she spent around Rs.15,000 for poles and other inputs . Sadly her harvest was a mere 70 kg ! H.P. Somasiri , a father of-four stares helplessly at his destroyed chena cultivation . He was depending on the harvest to purchase school books for his children . The authorities have shown no concern for farmers battered as a result of climate change , he said .
