@@1988059 <p> Over 1.6 billion people worldwide depend on forests for food , medicines and fuel , as well as their jobs and livelihoods . Forests are essential to the protection of biodiversity , containing 30 million species of plants and animals . That represents half of the Earth 's wildlife and two-thirds of its plant species . Forests regulate the climate and are second only to the oceans as the largest global store of carbon . When they are cleared or burned , they turn from a carbon sink to a carbon source , releasing huge amounts of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere . Deforestation is responsible for up to 15 percent of global annual carbon dioxide emissions -- greater than the entire global transport sector . Many businesses rely on forests along their supply chains for the materials that they need . <p> Given that context , a timely new report from CDP has highlighted supply chain risks for companies sourcing from countries where deforestation is rife .
