The term 21st century coal was introduced by the Government of China and the US in 2009 , realizing the prime role of coal in the 21st century cleaner energy supply . Clean coal technology is thus , well established . MIT Press published a book titled , " Sustainable Energy : Choosing amongst options , " authored by four leading energy experts to discuss electricity economics . In it the cost comparison of electricity generation in the USA is presented as 31.1-35.9 $/MWh from coal , 29.3-47.4 from natural gas and 50-70 from nuclear energy . It concludes that cleaner coal power is cheaper than every alternative . " Modern Energy For All , " IEA , states that in contrast to concerns about potential climate change , coal and electricity have been and continue to be linked to people 's well being , poverty eradication and increasing standards of living through energy access and affordable power .
