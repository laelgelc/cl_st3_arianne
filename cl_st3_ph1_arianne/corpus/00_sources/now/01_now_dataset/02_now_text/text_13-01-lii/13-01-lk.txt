Jon Evans looks at the detective chemistry behind such environmental forensic work <p> The Earth is not particularly good at keeping records , especially of its past climate . Like those of a disorganised businessman , its climate records are difficult to find , hard to interpret and often contradictory . But like diligent auditors , scientists are making great efforts to get to the bottom of the Earth 's disorganised records , both to understand how the Earth 's climate behaved in the past and to provide an indication of how it might behave in the future . <p> Adding to the general disorganisation , the Earth 's climate records are scattered all over the place : in ice cores , ocean and lake sediment , corals , trees , and stalagmites and stalactites , collectively known as archives .
