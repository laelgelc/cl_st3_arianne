Speaking about the weather is no longer something done by strange English people ; it is no longer un-Ghanaian . It is our present day reality . <p> Let us talk about it , let us inform ourselves about the scientific facts that we need to know . And let us not only talk about it , let us do something about the things that aggravate the situation . <p> We still use language to describe a country that no longer exists : geography lessons @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; we no longer have lush , thick forests . <p> Let us face the reality of our situation and maybe we can then be persuaded to do something about it . We owe it to the generations yet unborn and the new ones we welcome into being every day .
