The mangrove is a transitional territory between the freshwater rivers originating from the Ganges and the Bay of Bengal . The ecosystems of Sundarban react with the increase of salt concentrations . The environmental parameters with the direct influences on Sundarbans in terms of global climate change are sea-level rise , natural calamities like cyclones , rising temperature , salinity and drought .
