Upon the return of OCdts to the JDF , there is a requirement for doctrine and operating procedure standardization due to the varying concepts and differing contents of the training they had undergone . This is normally done at the Unit level and later , through a Young Officers ' Course . The advent of COVID-19 added a new level of complexity to travel , thus negatively affecting the process of sending OCdts overseas . The expectation is that on commissioning , an OCdt will be fully cognizant of the responsibilities and personal conditions that being an Officer imposes upon them .
