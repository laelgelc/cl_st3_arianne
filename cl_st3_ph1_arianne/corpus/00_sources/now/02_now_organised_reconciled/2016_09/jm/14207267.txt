<h> News <h> Caribbean still feeling effect of global financial crisis <p> BRIDGETOWN , Barbados ( CMC ) -- The Secretary General of the Barbados-based Caribbean Tourism Organization ( CTO ) , Hugh Riley , says regional countries are still trying to recover from the effects of the 2008 financial crisis . <p> Speaking on a radio programme here ahead of next week 's CTO 's " State of the Industry Conference ( SOTIC ) , " which will be held here from September 14-16 , Riley said " whereas arrivals keep increasing , which is a good thing ... the spend is six or seven years back . So we 're only now catching up with the spend . <p> " If something happens tomorrow to cause you to start dropping your rates precipitously the experts will show you it will take five to seven years to get those rates back . It is that long before you can come back to where you really ought to be .
