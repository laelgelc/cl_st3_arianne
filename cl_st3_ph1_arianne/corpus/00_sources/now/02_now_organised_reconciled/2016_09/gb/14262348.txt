<p> The ESI was developed by the World Ports Climate Initiative ( WPCI ) and ranks ships ' environmental performance based on common pollutants linked to climate change such nitrogen oxide and carbon dioxide . <p> " Shipping is the most environmentally friendly method of moving large volumes of goods and materials . Nevertheless , more can be done to make shipping even cleaner , " said Chairman of the Port of London Authoriy , Christopher Rodrigues . " As ships represent a substantial capital investment , we want to encourage faster adoption of new green technologies by giving an added incentive to adopt cleaner technologies . " <p> " The ESI provides a measure of the environmental performance of ocean going vessels , " said Director of WPCI , Fer van de Laar . " We are excited to see the Port of London utilising the ESI for one of its intended purposes , by encouraging shipping lines to improve environmental performance and to reduce the port 's environmental impact . "
