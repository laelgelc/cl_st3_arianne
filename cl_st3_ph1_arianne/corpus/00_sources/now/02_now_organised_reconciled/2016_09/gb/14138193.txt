<h> Glasgow named as one of 38 zones breaching legal air quality limits <p> Glasgow is one of 38 zones across the UK which is breaching legal air quality limits . <p> In 2013 , only five clean air zones across the country met EU standards on levels of a pollutant which causes respiratory disease . <p> As a result , the chairwoman of a UK government committee has called on the Department of Transport to introduce a clear strategy to increase the use of ultra-low emission vehicles to reduce air pollution and meet air quality targets . <p> Mary Creagh MP , who leads the environmental audit committee , said : " We need nine per cent of all new cars to be ultra-low emission vehicles by 2020 to meet our climate change targets but the department 's forecasts show it will get only around half way .
