<h> Underwater exhibit at the London 's Burning Festival <h> Underwater exhibit at the London 's Burning Festival <h> 1/5 <h> 2/5 <h> 3/5 <p> Bettina Strenske <h> 4/5 <p> Bettina Strenske <h> 5/5 <p> Bettina Strenske <p> Performers submerge themselves underwater in an installation for the London 's Burning Festival marking 350 years since the Great Fire . <p> Holoscenes , in a giant aquarium in Liverpool Street until Sunday , shows performers struggling with daily tasks such as cleaning as the water rises . Creators Early Morning Opera said they wanted to explore an issue , climate change , that could hit London like the 1666 fire . <p> The fire broke out in the early morning of Sunday September 2 , 1666 , in a baker 's shop in Pudding Lane . The fire spread quickly and lasted for four days , fanned east across the capital by winds , and causing destruction to the timber-frame houses .
