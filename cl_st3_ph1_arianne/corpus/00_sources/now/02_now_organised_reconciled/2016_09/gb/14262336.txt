<p> Humans have destroyed a tenth of Earth 's remaining wilderness in the last 25 years and there may be none left within a century if trends continue , according to an authoritative new study . <p> Researchers found a vast area the size of two Alaskas -- 3.3m square kilometres -- had been tarnished by human activities between 1993 and today , which experts said was a " shockingly bad " and " profoundly large number " . <p> The loss of the world 's last untouched refuges would not just be disastrous for endangered species but for climate change efforts , the authors said , because some of the forests store enormous amounts of carbon . <p> " Without any policies to protect these areas , they are falling victim to widespread development .
