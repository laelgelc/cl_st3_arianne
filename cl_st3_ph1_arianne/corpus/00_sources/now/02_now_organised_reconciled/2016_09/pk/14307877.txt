PHOTO : FILE <p> TORONTO : Actor and environmentalist Leonardo DiCaprio said he rushed to release his upcoming climate change documentary Before the Flood ahead of the US presidential elections in November , to issue a clarion call to voters and influence their decisions . <p> " We wanted this film to come out before the next election because the US is the largest contributor to this issue , " DiCaprio said , following the film 's premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival . " We can not afford , at this critical moment in time , to have leaders in office that do not believe in the modern science of climate change , " he added . <p> The film follows the Oscar-winning DiCaprio and actor-cum-film-maker Fisher Stevens as they travel from Canada 's oil sands to tiny Pacific islands . In the process , they interview world leaders such as the Catholic Church 's Pope Francis and current US President Barack Obama , climate scientists and academics @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ negative impacts of industrialisation and increasing consumption on the health of the planet . The film shed light on threatened gorillas in the Democratic Republic of Congo . It calls out the sizable minority of Republican lawmakers who flatly deny the broad , scientific evidence that human activity is causing environmental damage and even names presidential candidate Donald Trump and former candidates Marco Rubio and Ted Cruz .
