<h> Efforts underway to promote environment-friendly shopping bags in ICT <p> Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) has so far registered four companies to facilitate implementation of regulations regarding the use of environment-friendly plastic shopping bags in Islamabad Capital Territory ( ICT ) area . <p> These companies have been registered to import , manufacture , stockpile , trade , supply , distribute , sell or use any scheduled plastic products which is bio-degradable . <p> Sources at Climate Change Division said due to this step , a number of major stores in Islamabad are now using environment-friendly plastic shopping bags . <p> Highlighting the steps taken by the ministry and its attached department , the sources said other small shopkeepers in ICT have also been contacted to use only bio-degradable shopping bags . <p> Furthermore , Pak-EPA is making efforts to implement the regulations in its true letter and spirit . <p> The sources said Islamabad Capital Territory ( ICT ) and Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) have also been asked to provide an update about a number of manufacturing units in Islamabad , whereas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Moreover , about steps being taken to control deforestation in the country , the sources said under the direction of the prime minster , National Forest Policy 2015 has been prepared in consultation with provinces and stakeholders . They said Pakistan is actively pursuing the objective of conserving mangrove forests under Mangrove for the Future Programme ( MFF ) . <p> The main focus of this initiative is to empower coastal communities to conserve and ensure sustainable use of coastal resources including mangrove forests of Pakistan with the participation of local communities . <p> In collaboration with the provinces , Ministry of Climate Change is implementing a project on REDD+ with the ultimate objective to control deforestation in the country .
