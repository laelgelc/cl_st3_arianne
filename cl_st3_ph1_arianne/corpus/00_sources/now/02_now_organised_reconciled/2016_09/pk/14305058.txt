<h> Rain storm in Swat : Death toll reaches 5 <p> Swat : After latest thunderstorm and several accidents followed by it in Madyan area of Swat , death toll reached five , confirmed official sources . <p> After continuous rain and thunderstorm in Swat , several houses were left damaged and standing crops affected especially in Madyan . <p> As per reports in local media , more than ten houses were damaged . Besides several mud houses damaged severely and it caused panic and fear in the residents who came running out of their houses and started reciting Quran aloud . <p> Pakistan remains one of the most affected country due to calamities caused by climate change . There are severe heat waves , storms , floods , hail storms which cause damage to life and property of the people every year with varying intensity .
