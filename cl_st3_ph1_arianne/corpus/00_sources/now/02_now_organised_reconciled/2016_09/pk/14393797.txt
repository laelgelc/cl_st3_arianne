<h> Pakistan participates in climate conference in Washington <p> WASHINGTON , United States : Pakistan attended an international conference on climate held in Washington from September 15-16 to discuss problems and identify solutions to protect and conserve ocean and its resources . <p> Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid led Pakistan 's delegation at the " Our Ocean " conference hosted by US State Department at Washington D.C. <p> Pakistan 's participation in the conference signifies that protection of environment and combating climate change is one of the key priorities of the current government . <p> President Barak Obama while addressing the plenary session of the conference designated the first marine national monument in the Atlantic Ocean , protecting fragile deep-sea ecosystems off the coast of New England as the Northeast Canyons and Seamounts Marine National Monument . <p> The Secretary of State John F. Kerry also delivered the keynote address at the inaugural session which was attended by high-level delegations of more than 70 countries including three Heads of States . <p> It was the third conference in a row with an overarching objective to inspire @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ scientists and civil society to identify solutions and commit to actions to protect and conserve ocean and its resources . <p> The conference deliberated on issues related to marine protected areas , sustainable fisheries , marine pollution and climate-related impacts on the ocean . <p> Pakistan 's participation in the event would contribute towards being part of the international discourse with a view to joining and complementing international efforts in the area of increasingly important climate change . <p> On Thursday , the Minister for Climate Change also met with senior officials of the State Department including with US Special Envoy for Climate Change Jonathan Pershing . Both sides discussed bilateral cooperation in the area of climate change .
