<p> ISLAMABAD : EU Ambassador to Pakistan Jean-Francois Cautain has said that war is no solution to any issue . Therefore , both Pakistan and India should sit together in order to sort out their problems . He was talking to a group of journalists on the occasion of climate change diplomatic week celebration at PNCA on Sunday . The ambassador further said the EU does not believe in proliferation of nuclear arms , and should therefore call upon all stakeholders to undertake sincere efforts in this regard . <p> He noted Pakistan was ranked amongst the countries most vulnerable to the effects of climate change . " Pakistan is gifted with many natural resources and this potential needs to be exploited properly . Politicians here need to focus more on climate change issues in order to overcome the energy crisis . " <p> The EU is working with Pakistan in this field and will continue to work with this country both on short term and longer term basis .
