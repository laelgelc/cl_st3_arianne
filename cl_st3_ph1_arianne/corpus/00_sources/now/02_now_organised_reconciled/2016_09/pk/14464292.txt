<h> 10,000 to be sent to US for PhD : HEC chief <p> islamabad - The Higher Education Commission ( HEC ) will send 10,000 scholars to the United States for PhD studies from the next year in critical areas of agriculture and food security , medical and allied health sciences , energy , water , climate change , advanced digital technology and others . <p> Chairman HEC Dr Mukhtar Ahmad told APP here yesterday that Pakistan-US Corridor was conceptualized in June 2015 as a result of US-Pakistan Education , Science and Technology working group under the bilateral strategic dialogue . He said the government of Pakistan envisions enhancing enrolment in tertiary education up to 5 million by 2025 and outreach of HEIs at district level . <p> At this stage , the universities are facing shortage of 30,000 PhD qualified faculty members .
