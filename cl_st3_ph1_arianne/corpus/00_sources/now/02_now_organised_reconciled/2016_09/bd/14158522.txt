<h> Bangladesh against conflict in Indian Ocean <h> The foreign minister has called upon the countries to follow the " path of cooperation and collaboration , not competition and conflict " to tap the full potentials of the Indian Ocean . <p> " Indian Ocean has remained at the centre of interest to the major players of the world because of its strategic location and natural resources , " AH Mahmood Ali said on Friday , speaking at a conference on the Indian Ocean in Singapore . <p> India Foundation , in collaboration with the Bangladesh Institute of International and Strategic Studies ( BIISS ) , Institute of Policy Studies Sri Lanka and S Rajaratnam School of International Studies ( RSIS ) , Singapore , organised the conference , the foreign ministry said . <p> Singapore Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan inaugurated the conference which brought together ministers , senior civil and military bureaucrats , diplomats , academicians , researchers and civil societies of the countries along the Indian Ocean . <p> The Indian Ocean region has emerged as the most prominent global economic highway with 66 percent of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bulk cargo and 50 percent of the world 's container traffic passing through its waters . <p> The oil arteries of the world flow through the Indian Ocean , the foreign minister said . <p> " It is not only trade and economy , competition among some of the major powers in securing visible and credible presence in the Indian Ocean has shaped the dynamics of the Indian Ocean region , " he said . <p> " We see growing interests among countries in the region to develop new infrastructures in the Indian Ocean . Countries also seek to build , expand and strengthen their naval infrastructures in the Indian Ocean . <p> " Overlapping territorial claims and disputes have added new dimension to this evolving security scenario , " he said . <p> Ali noted that countries in Asia and the Pacific are four times more likely to be affected by a natural catastrophe than those in Africa , and 25 times more vulnerable than Europe and North America . <p> The climate change impacts and resultant sea level rise have further enhanced the risks and vulnerabilities of the Indian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ posing danger to maritime peace and stability .
