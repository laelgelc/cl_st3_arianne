<p> The US Fish and Wildlife Service on Wednesday proposed listing the rusty patched bumble bee , a prized but vanishing pollinator once widely found in the upper Midwest and Northeastern United States , for federal protection as an endangered species . <p> One of numerous wild bee species facing sharp declines , the rusty patched bumble bee is the first in the continental United States formally proposed for listing under the US Endangered Species Act , according to the Xerces Society , which petitioned for its protection . <p> Once abundant and widespread with hundreds of populations across its range , the rusty patched bumble bee 's numbers and distribution have plunged by more than 90 per cent since the late 1990s , according to the service . <p> The agency attributes the decline to a number of factors , including disease , pesticides , climate change and habitat loss . <p> Bumble bees , as distinguished from domesticated honey bees , are important pollinators of wildflowers and many important crops . <p> The rusty patched bumble bee is one of 47 varieties of native bumble bees @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of which face a risk of extinction , according to the International Union for the Conservation of Nature . <p> The public comment period initiated by the proposed listing runs until November 21 , after which the agency could revise its proposal or finalise a decision .
