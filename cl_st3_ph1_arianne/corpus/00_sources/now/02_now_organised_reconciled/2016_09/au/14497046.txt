In June , an analysis by Greenpeace Russia claimed that 3.5 million hectares of land -- a region larger than all of Australia 's capital cities combined -- had burned this year so far . But the Russian government only reported 669,000 hectares burnt for the same period . <p> More worrisome than a single bad fire season is that we could be witnessing the new normal for Siberia . As the planet warms and the human population soars , fire seasons are growing longerand more intense around the world , meaning more and more heat-trapping carbon is going up in smoke . <p> " It seems that autumn fires and wide range of summer fires occurred due to global warming , " Verkhovets said .
