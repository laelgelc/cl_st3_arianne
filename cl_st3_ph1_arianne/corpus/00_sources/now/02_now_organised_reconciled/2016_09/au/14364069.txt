<h> Lib senator lashes Greens Hanson snub <p> By <p> AAP <p> A Liberal backbencher has launched a scathing attack on the Greens in parliament over their " disrespectful " snub of Pauline Hanson . <p> The Senate was debating a private Greens marriage equality bill on Thursday when Linda Reynolds lashed the minor party for walking out of the chamber half way through the One Nation leader 's first speech . <p> She was " deeply angered and greatly saddened " by their response to her " very passionate " speech . <p> Senator Reynolds said their behaviour was unprecedented and put great shame upon all senators in the chamber . <p> If they were so offended , they could have exited out the back rather than make a show of it , she said . <p> It highlighted what was wrong with the state of debate in Australia . <p> " So delicate were their ears they could n't even stay and show respect to Senator Hanson , who was elected , " Senator Reynolds said . <p> " What hope does freedom of expression have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ever listen to and tolerate their own truths ? " <p> Senator Reynolds has linked the left side of politics to Soviet dictator Joseph Stalin and Nazi propagandist Joseph Goebbels , arguing both were in favour of selective free-speech . <p> She wants the words " offend " and " insult " removed from section 18C of the Racial Discrimination Act , backing a private bill to water down the laws despite the prime minister insisting the government is not interested in the change . <p> The Greens have defended the walk-out , vowing to call out Senator Hanson 's racism after she used her first speech to call for a halt to all immigration and warn Australia was in danger of being swamped by Muslims . <p> Greens senators also refused to congratulate One Nation senator Malcolm Roberts , a vocal climate change sceptic , after he made his first speech on Tuesday .
