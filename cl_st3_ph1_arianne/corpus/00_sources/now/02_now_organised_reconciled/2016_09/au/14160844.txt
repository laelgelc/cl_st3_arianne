<h> A ' fourth way ' in cutting carbon emissions <p> Just as they say , there 's more than one way to skin a cat , so there are a lot of ways to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and " decarbonise " the economy . We 've tried three ways so far , and now we may try a fourth . <p> The Rudd government tried to introduce an emissions trading scheme in 2009 , but it was blocked in the Senate when the Greens joined the Tony Abbott-led opposition in voting it down . <p> But this week the government 's Climate Change Authority recommended that the fund be supplemented by imposing an " emissions intensity scheme " on the nation 's generators of electricity , so we could be sure of achieving the commitments the Abbott government made at the Paris climate conference last year . <p> Confused ? Let me explain how an emissions intensity scheme would work and how it differs from its three predecessors .
