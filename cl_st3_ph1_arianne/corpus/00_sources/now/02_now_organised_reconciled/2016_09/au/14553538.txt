The vice-like grip of drought on this great country of ours has been so tenacious over the past decade , that to see it green is a true blessing . <p> We happened to be in Longreach , QLD camping by the Thomson River when the rains came . And it bucketed down . Heavy rain fell continuously for several days and nights . The rainfall broke an eighty one year old record . <p> We were hopelessly bogged and had to wait several days until the mud dried before we were able to get out .
