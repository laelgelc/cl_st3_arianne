<p> Despite appearances , the oceans are far from silent places . If you dunk your head underwater you 'll hear a cacophony of sounds from wildlife great and small , crashing waves , and even rain . And it 's louder still for creatures attuned to these sounds . <p> However , humans are changing these ocean soundscapes . Our recent research showed that changes caused by people , from ocean acidification to pollution , are silencing the seas ' natural noises . ( We 're also filling the oceans with human noise ) . <p> This is bad news for the species that depend on these noises to find their way . <h> Ocean soundscapes <p> All over the world you can hear a lively crackling sound made by thousands of snapping shrimp that live along coastlines . <p> These common shrimp , often referred to as pistol shrimp , have a large claw that they can close with such force that a cavitation bubble is formed . Urchins and parrotfish make clearly audible chomping sounds as they scrape algae off rocks . Sounds and odours from coastal habitats are key cues that allow marine animals to find their new homes and replenish adult populations . <h> Going quiet <p> Humans are increasingly dominating the physical and chemical environment .
