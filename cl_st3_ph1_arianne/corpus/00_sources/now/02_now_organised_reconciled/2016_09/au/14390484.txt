<p> THE worst kept secret in town is finally confirmed -- rock great Sting will play at this year 's AFL grand final . <p> The former Police front man and winner of 16 Grammy Awards has been rumoured for weeks to headline one of the biggest days on the national sporting calendar but the AFL finally confirmed tonight that he will round out a pre-game entertainment line-up that also features Melbourne hometown heroes , singer-songwriter Vance Joy and rock veterans The Living End . <p> " I know what a big honour it is and that it 's extremely important , " said Sting . " As a Pom , to be invited there , it 's a great honour so I am dead excited . " <p> Sting is no stranger to the game and proclaims himself to be a fan , but admits it 's been a while since he saw it played .
