<h> Buhari To Lead Nigeria 's Delegation To 71st UN General Assembly <p> President Muhammadu Buhari is to lead a high powered delegation to the United Nations General Assembly starting in New York on Tuesday . <p> He is expected to seek support from other world leaders on challenges of security , governance , poverty reduction and economic growth among other things . <p> The Minister of Foreign Affairs , Mr Godfrey onyeama , said that President Buhari would also engage with other world leaders to push for the reform of the United Nations system towards a more responsive and inclusive United Nations that empowers countries other than the usual few , especially developing nations . <p> The President is also expected to sign the Paris agreement on climate change in New York as part of prospective discussions in environmental sustainability . <p> Other key priority areas which he is expected to raise in engagements with other leaders are the challenges of internally displaced persons in Nigeria , refugees and migration .
