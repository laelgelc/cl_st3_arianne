I wanted to get a firsthand feel of how things would unfold , and not rely on other people 's account of events . <p> Was it worth it ? Arguable . Arguable , because on one hand , I did n't exactly get the robust kind of debate that should without question be expected of the caliber of candidates vying for the top job in a country like America .
