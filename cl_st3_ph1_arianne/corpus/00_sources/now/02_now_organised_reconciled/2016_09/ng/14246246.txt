Rarely has a country evolved without a sustained structural transformation from an agrarian or resource-based economy towards an industrial or service-based economy . This transformation is important to ensure wealth creation through increased economic integration and productivity . <p> The way forward <p> According to the report , Africa and LDCs should move away from the " generalized " industrial policies that have proved ineffective over the last three decades . They also need to build strong institutions and viable investment climates .
