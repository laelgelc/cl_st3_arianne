<h> Buhari invites UN to mediate with Boko Haram to free Chibok girls <p> As a show of commitment to swapping the abducted schoolgirls from Chibok with Boko Haram fighters in custody , President Muhammadu Buhari has told the United Nations Secretary General , Ban Ki-moon , that Nigeria would welcome intermediaries from the global body . <p> Speaking during a bilateral meeting with the UN scribe at the sidelines of the 71st UN General Assembly in New York , President Buhari said the Nigerian government was willing to bend over backwards , to get the Chibok girls released from captivity . <p> " The challenge is in getting credible and bona fide leadership of Boko Haram to discuss with , " the President said , adding that , " The split in the insurgent group is not helping matters . Government had reached out , ready to negotiate , but it became difficult to identify credible leaders . We will welcome intermediaries such as UN outfits , to step in .
