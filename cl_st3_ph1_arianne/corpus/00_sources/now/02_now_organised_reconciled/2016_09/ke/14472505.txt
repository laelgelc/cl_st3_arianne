<p> Tackling the impact of climate changeCodiagro to present new product line <p> The Spanish company Codiagro will be exhibiting again at the next edition of FRUIT ATTRACTION , one of the key fairs in our industry worldwide . This will be the 5th consecutive year they have taken part with their own stand . Codiagro will be in Hall 4 , Stand 4G07A . The fair will take place in Madrid from 5 to 7 October . <p> In this edition , the firm will present its new line of products , specially developed to help producers tackle the adverse effects of climate change on their crops . <p> " Agriculture is extremely vulnerable to climate change .
