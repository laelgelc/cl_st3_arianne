This product is highly appreciated by the international market , especially in the United States , which buys 91% of Ecuador 's supply . <p> The VI International Congress of Ecuadorian Mango , which will be attended by some 400 people , including producers , exporters , and buyers , is starting today , September 1 , in Guayaquil . <p> Johnny Jara , executive director of the Ecuador 's Mango Foundation , said the Congress would last until Saturday and that one of its goals was to train producers . Ecuador has 5,300 hectares dedicated to the production of mango for export and , according to estimates , some 3,000 hectares for domestic consumption . <p> " We have to train Ecuadorian producers . We will address different issues , such as diseases , irrigation , harvesting , post-harvest , and climate change , among others , " Jara said .
