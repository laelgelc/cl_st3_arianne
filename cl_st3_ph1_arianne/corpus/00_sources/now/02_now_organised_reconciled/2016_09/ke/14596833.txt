<h> Stem urban migration to manage housing crisis <h> share <p> None of you reading this column In Nairobi County probably have slept out in the cold . You have a decent night 's sleep in a decent house . However , do you know that proper , habitable shelter is still a pipe dream to about one quarter of the world 's urban population , who live in slums and informal settlements ? <p> According to a report by civil society group Pamoja Trust , it is estimated that half of Nairobi 's four million residents live in informal settlements . These hapless people are crammed into only five per cent of the city 's residential area , and just one per cent of all Nairobi land . <p> They live in inadequate housing with little or no access to clean water , sanitation , healthcare , schools and other essential facilities . According to UN-Habitat , an increasing number of urban dwellers , especially the poor and vulnerable groups like women , migrants , persons with disabilities , youth and the elderly and are living in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ housing , they lack access to basic services and are vulnerable to forced evictions or homelessness . This is the general scenario facing the international community as it marks the World Habitat Day ( WHD ) on Monday October 3 , . This year 's theme is " Housing at the Centre " . <p> The purpose of WHD is to reflect on the state of our towns and cities , and on the basic right of adequate shelter for all . The WHD was first celebrated in 1986 after the UN General Assembly designated the first Monday of October of every year for its celebration in Resolution 40/202 of 17 December 1985 . <p> It is part of the international environmental discourse contained in various UN statutes and treaties . Adequate housing was recognised as part of the right to an adequate standard of living in international instruments , including the 1948 Universal Declaration of Human Rights and in the 1966 International Covenant on Economic , Social and Cultural Rights . <p> Today , more than half the world 's population lives in cities . Going by Nairobi 's massive population living in inadequate housing , the city needs at least a million affordable houses in the short to medium term to stem the crisis . <p> Goal 11 of UN 's Sustainable Development Goals involves making cities and human settlements inclusive , safe , resilient and sustainable . Despite numerous planning challenges , cities still offer more efficient economies of scale in relation to aspects like provision of goods , services and transportation . Indeed , with proper planning and management , cities can continue being incubators for innovation and growth , and drivers of development . <p> In addition to adequate housing consisting of four walls and a roof , it also includes factors like security of tenure , availability of services , affordability , accessibility and location . It is about proximity . <p> People should live where they are most comfortable with in relation to what they need . Residences must take into consideration availability of the necessary conveniences @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ultimately , the solution lies in ensuring devolution works so that we do not have thousands of young men and women streaming into the city in search of the so called urban advantage . Indeed , this was one of the main reasons behind the clamour for devolution ; to enable every county invest its resources in priority areas , starting with the low lying fruits in terms of return on investment . <p> Poor urban governance means the current crisis might escalate in the coming years rather than being effectively managed . At this rate , we might end up with cities like Lagos in Nigeria and New Delhi in India which have become gridlocked at every level .
