<p> Kenya seeks to restore 5.1 million acres of degraded forests and landscapes by 2030 to take advantage of the global funds that developed countries will allocate to developing nations . Last week , the US and China formally ratified the Paris Agreement on Climate Change , opening windows for developing countries to reap from the fund . They made the announcement on Saturday , on the eve of the G20 summit in Hangzhou , China . The agreement will review progress every five years and pump Sh10.1 trillion a year in climate finance for developing countries by 2020 , with a commitment for further assistance in the future . Environment CS Judi Wakhungu said Kenya is ready to receive the funds .
