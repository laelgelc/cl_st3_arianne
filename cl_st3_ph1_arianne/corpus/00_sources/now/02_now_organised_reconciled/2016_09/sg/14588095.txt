<p> The Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister Narendra Modi has given its approval to ratify the Paris Agreement ( on Climate Change ) on 2 October , the day of Gandhi Jayanti . <p> Paris Agreement was adopted by 185 nations last year on 12 December 2015 , and India signed the Paris Agreement in New York early this year on 22 April 2016 . <p> A total of 191 countries have signed the Paris Agreement so far . As per the provisions of the Paris Agreement , the treaty will come into force as and when 55 countries contributing to 55% of total global emission ratify the agreement . So far , 61 countries have deposited their instruments of ratification , acceptance or approval accounting in total for 47.79% of the total global greenhouse gas emissions . <p> India 's decision to ratify the agreement will take the number of cumulative level of emission of countries that have ratified the agreement so far to 51.89% . Given the critical role that India played in securing international consensus on Paris Agreement , today 's decision will further underline India 's responsive leadership in the community of nations committed to global cause of environmental protection and climate justice . <p> While agreeing to ratify the Paris Agreement , the Cabinet has also decided that India should declare that India will treat its national laws , its development agenda , and availability of means of implementation , its assessment of global commitment to combating climate change , and predictable and affordable access to cleaner source of energy as the context in which the Agreement is being ratified . <p> Paris Agreement pertains to post-2020 climate actions . In the pre-2020 period , developed countries are to act as per Kyoto Protocol and some developing countries have taken voluntary pledges .
