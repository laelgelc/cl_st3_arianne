<p> SINGAPORE : Singapore 's built environment sector has to constantly evolve to address pressing needs and challenges facing the industry - especially in reducing the reliance on foreign manpower and the impact of climate change . <p> This according to Senior Minister of State for National Development Desmond Lee , who opened the Architecture &amp; Building Services 2016 ( ArchXpo 2016 ) at the Marina Bay Sands on Wednesday ( Sep 28 ) morning . <p> Mr Lee called on the sector to adopt high impact productive technologies upfront in order to reduce the reliance on foreign manpower . <p> In particular , the use of Building Information Modelling ( BIM ) which allows companies to build virtually before building for real .
