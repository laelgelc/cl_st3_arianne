<h> Scientists : How the new focus on 1.5C is reshaping climate research <h> Limiting global temperature rise to 1.5C has long been the goal of developing countries and those most at risk from climate change . Since becoming enshrined in the Paris Agreement last December , the 1.5C goal has come under increased scrutiny and examination . <p> The heat is on scientists who are either under pressure or rising to the challenge to report on the climate impacts of the .5 degree margin between the 1.5 and 2-degree C temperature rise .
