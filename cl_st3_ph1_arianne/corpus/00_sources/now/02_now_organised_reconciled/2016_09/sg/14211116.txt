<h> Climate change spells worse typhoons for China , Japan : study <p> PARIS ( AFP ) - China , Taiwan , Japan and the Koreas will experience more violent typhoons under climate change , said researchers on Monday , presenting evidence for a recent rise in storm intensity caused by ocean warming . <p> Scientists have struggled to identify changes in the intensity and frequency of typhoons over the north-west Pacific ocean - never mind trying to pinpoint a role for global warming . <p> Contradictory trends emerge from records such as the Joint Typhoon Warming Center and the Japan Meteorological Agency - the two most widely-used data sets in typhoon research , according to the US-based study authors Wei Mei and Shang-Ping Xie . <p> They have now corrected the available data for differences in methodology and discovered a single , clear trend . <p> " Over the past 37 years , typhoons that strike East and South-east Asia have intensified by 12-15 per cent , " they wrote in the journal Nature Geoscience . <p> And the data showed this intensification , in turn , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change , though this is yet to be proven . <p> " Given disproportionate damages by intense typhoons , this represents a heightened threat to people and properties in the region .
