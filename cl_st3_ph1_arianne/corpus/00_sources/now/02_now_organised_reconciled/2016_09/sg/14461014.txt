<h> In final address to UN , he tells them to press on with better model of cooperation , integration <p> President Barack Obama has called on world leaders not to back away from global integration , even as he admitted that the current path required a course correction . <p> Making his final address to the United Nations General Assembly as United States president yesterday , Mr Obama acknowledged issues such as inequality which have been caused by the push for globalisation in recent years , but stressed that this should not mean reverting to isolationism and nationalism . <p> Noting that globalisation has improved lives while also exposing fault lines , he said : " This is the paradox that defines our world today . A quarter-century after the end of the Cold War , the world is by many measures less violent and more prosperous than ever before , and yet our societies are filled with uncertainty , unease and strife . " <p> He added : " I believe at this moment , we all face a choice . And while he said that those sentiments can not be dismissed , they would ultimately be self-defeating . <p> He noted that those pushing the benefits of global integration have " ignored inequality within and among nations , have ignored the enduring appeal of ethnic and sectarian identities , have left international institutions ill-equipped , underfunded and under-resourced , in order to handle transnational challenges " . <p> CRITICAL CHOICE <p> I believe at this moment , we all face a choice .
