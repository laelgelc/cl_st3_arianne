<h> Warming oceans are sick , global scientists warn <p> HONOLULU ( AFP ) - Global warming is making the oceans sicker than ever before , spreading disease among animals and humans and threatening food security across the planet , a major scientific report said on Monday ( Sept 5 ) . <p> The findings , based on peer-reviewed research , were compiled by 80 scientists from 12 countries , experts said at the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) World Conservation Congress in Hawaii . <p> " We all know that the oceans sustain this planet . We all know that the oceans provide every second breath we take , " IUCN director-general Inger Andersen told reporters at the meeting , which has drawn 9,000 leaders and environmentalists to Honolulu . " And yet we are making the oceans sick .
