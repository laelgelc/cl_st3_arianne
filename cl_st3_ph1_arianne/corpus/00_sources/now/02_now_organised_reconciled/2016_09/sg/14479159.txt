<p> Minister for Foreign Affairs Vivian Balakrishnan deposited Singapore 's instrument of ratification at the United Nations headquarters in New York yesterday . <p> The Republic has pledged to reduce its emissions intensity , which is the ratio of carbon emissions to each dollar of the gross domestic product , by 36 per cent from 2005 levels by 2030 and stabilise emissions with the aim of peaking around 2030 . <p> In April , Singapore was one of 175 countries to sign the Paris Agreement . <p> The Republic is also on track to meet its existing commitment to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 16 per cent by 2020 , said the Ministry of Foreign Affairs ( MFA ) in a statement to the media yesterday . <p> In July , Singapore unveiled its Climate Action Plan to meet its targets under the Paris Agreement , outlining various measures to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions and enhance resilience to climate change . <p> " Improving energy efficiency will continue to be Singapore 's key strategy to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , " said the MFA , adding that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ power generation , industry and transport . <p> In a Facebook post , Dr Balakrishnan said the ratification of the agreement was a " profound and moving journey " for the team of negotiators from various ministries undertaken over a number of years . <p> He added : " We may only be a tiny state , but we made a positive contribution in a quiet but effective way .
