<h> Ocean drone startup Saildrone raises $14M in series A funding <h> The round was led by Social Capital with investments from the Capricorn Investment Group and Lux Capital <p> Saildrone an ocean drone startup that that provides real data by a fleet of unmanned and autonomous sailing drones solutions , has raised $14 million in Series A investment round . The round was led by Social Capital with investments from the Capricorn Investment Group and Lux Capital . The company is planning to use this funding for expanding its fleet of sailing drones , enhancing data processing capacity , and scaling commercial operations . <p> The California-based firm offers a wide range of solutions that includes Oceanography and Climate Data Solutions , Fisheries Data Solutions and Environmental Data Solutions . The company claims that by augmenting traditional ships with a fleet of Saildrones , one can cost-effectively and autonomously gather data over large ocean areas in any conditions . Launched and retrieved from a dock , the Saildrone fleet navigates to its destination using wind power alone , transiting at three to five knots .
