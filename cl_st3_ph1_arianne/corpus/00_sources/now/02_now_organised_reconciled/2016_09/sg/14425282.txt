<p> Minister for Foreign Affairs Vivian Balakrishnan will be addressing the United Nations General Assembly ( UNGA ) in New York . <p> The 71st session of the UNGA started yesterday and will end next Monday . <p> In previous years , Singapore has used the platform to speak on issues such as terrorism and the needs of small nation states . <p> Dr Balakrishnan will also attend other meetings , including the 9th Ministerial Meeting of the Global Governance Group , the Informal Asean Foreign Ministers ' Meeting and the Foreign Ministers segment of the Major Economies Forum on Energy and Climate Change , said the Ministry of Foreign Affairs yesterday . He will be accompanied by senior ministry officials . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on September 19 , 2016 , with the headline ' Vivian to address UN General Assembly ' .
