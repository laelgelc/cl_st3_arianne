<h> Environmentalists submit infra project study to CDC <h> Environmentalists submit infra project study to CDC <p> CLARK FREEPORT --- Environment advocates have recently submitted to Clark Development Corporation ( CDC ) a study on the proposed infrastructure development to assist the state-run firm to plan and design future infrastructure development inside this premiere investment destination in Central Luzon . <p> Kabang Kalikasan Ng Pilipinas Foundation Inc . ( KKPFI ) has turned over recently " maps and e-files " which will serve as CDC 's planning tool on infrastructure development inside the Freeport . <p> The study was committed by the Yuchengco Group of Companies ( YGC ) for CDC at no cost to the government . <p> Last December 2015 , CDC entered into an agreement with KKPFI-- also known as the World Wide Fund for Nature -- Philippines ( WWF-Philippines ) --for the development of " All-Weather Urban Corridor Project " funded by the YGC . <p> The project is a map comprising of road networks with good drainage systems installed which are passable to vehicles despite the presence of water , with low impact @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> KKPFI is a non-stock , non-profit organization engaged in the conservation and preservation of the environment in selected areas of the Philippines by helping communities adapt to climate change , switch to clean renewable sources of energy and infuse sustainability into the lives and businesses of Filipinos . <p> Clark has been selected by WWF-Philippines because of its strategic location and high elevation . <p> CDC and KKPFI have tied up to spread awareness on the urgency of the climate change crisis , including its adverse impacts and how to provide solutions . <p> The foundation conducted transport access mapping study focusing on the connection of Clark and Angeles City . <p> The project study includes an assessment of " road vulnerability " which refers to the inability of the road network to withstand the effects of extreme weather conditions . <p> Published in the Sun.Star Pampanga newspaper on September 09 , 2016 . <p> Latest issues of Sun.Star Pampanga also available on your mobile phones , laptops , and tablets .
