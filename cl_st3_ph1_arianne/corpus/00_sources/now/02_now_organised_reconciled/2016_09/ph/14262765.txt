<h> September 9th , 2016 12:14 AM <p> Like the world 's largest and developed economies , the Philippines relies on different sources for its power supply . Coal , natural gas , geothermal energy , hydro and oil are used by power plants to produce electricity . Sunlight and wind are now also used for the electrification of the countryside . This mix of energy sources provides the country with a stable power supply that keeps the economy running and Filipino homes lighted . <p> However , some of these sources of power are not environment-friendly and even pose many hazards to the public . When burned , they emit harmful greenhouse gases which are now blamed for global warming or climate change . On the other hand , both the extraction and use of coal destroy mountains , forests and rivers -- thus affecting the health and livelihood of rural folk living close to coal-mining sites . <p> That is why Environment Secretary Gina Lopez wants to stop destructive coal mining in the country .
