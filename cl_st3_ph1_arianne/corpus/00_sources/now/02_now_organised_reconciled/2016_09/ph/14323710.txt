<h> The G-20 meeting in China <p> The G-20 meeting of the world 's leading industrialized nations was held , for the first time this year , in China . And this was the last such meeting that President Obama would attend , since he leaves office next January . But it was his 8th meeting with China 's President , Xi Jinping . Their agenda included such maritime issues as the South China Sea , where China has built fortifications and an airstrip on two the previously uninhabited islands , which are claimed by several countries , including the Philippines . They also discussed trade , which Alibaba 's founder , Jack Ma , who is based in Hangzhou where the meeting took place , famously said , could stop wars .
