<h> The Standard <h> Clinton to millennials : Pick me to stop Trump <p> DEMOCRATS , fearful that third-party presidential candidates could attract enough millennials to cost Hillary Clinton key states are stepping up efforts to woo young voters with one message : stop Trump . <p> Gary Johnson , the Libertarian candidate , and Jill Stein , the left-wing Green Party aspirant , are attracting much of their support from younger voters . Some recent polls show them attracting a total of over 10 percent of the vote nationally and doing much better than that with millennials . <p> " There are lots of potential Clinton voters who could be lost to these third-party candidates , " acknowledges Geoff Garin , the pollster for Priorities USA , the Clinton Super PAC . " We are making a first-class effort to reach them through digital media " and saying " that their vote could mean Donald Trump is president . " <p> Bill Weld , Johnson 's vice presidential candidate , said that the Libertarian ticket was taking equally from both sides .
