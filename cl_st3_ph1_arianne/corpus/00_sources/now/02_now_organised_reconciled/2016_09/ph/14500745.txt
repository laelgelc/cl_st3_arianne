<h> Local climate change action plan for LGUs formulated <h> Local climate change action plan for LGUs formulated <p> Friday , September 23 , 2016 <p> CITY OF SAN FERNANDO -- In line with the efforts of addressing Philippines ' vulnerability when it comes to natural hazards , the Department of Interior and Local Government Region III ( DILG-3 ) conducted a seminar-workshop on the formulation of the Local Climate Change Action Plan ( LCCAP ) for LGUs on September 21 to 23 , 2016 at Angeles City with participants from selected local government units ( LGUs ) from the provinces of Aurora , Bataan , Bulacan , and Nueva Ecija . <p> The LCCAP contains the policies and measures that a local government must implement and streamline to be able to adapt to climate change and to mitigate its effects . <p> In her opening remarks , MED Chief Lerrie S. Hernandez emphasized the importance of formulating the LCCAP in facing and handling the effects of climate change . <p> " With these changing times , we in the government sector , must be the frontliners in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ own action plan in handling these is a major step in effectively enabling our LGUs to adapt and be resilient to the effects of climate change , " Hernandez said . <p> The three-day activity is comprised of various discussions and workshops which focus on relevant national laws and policies pertaining to climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction and management , components and steps in the formulation of LCCAP , and its monitoring and evaluation . <p> Pursuant to the Climate Change Act of 2009 or R.A. 9729 , LGUs shall be the frontline agencies in the formulation , planning , and implementation of climate change action plans in their respective areas .
