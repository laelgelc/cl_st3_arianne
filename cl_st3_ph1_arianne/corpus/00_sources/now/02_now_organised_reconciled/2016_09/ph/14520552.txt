<h> ( LOOK ) A seed vault in the middle of the North Pole <p> Somewhere in North Pole stands a gargantuan vault of seeds to prepare the world in case the dreaded doomsday comes . <p> The Svalbard Global Seed Vault is at the Plataberget Mountain , Svalbard Norway , according to Mashable . <p> The location is said to be " deep enough to protect from air temperature rises and high enough to avoid flooding if sea levels rise . " <p> The vault was built to last up to 1,000 years and designed to resist climate change , nuclear war , or even an asteroid strike . <p> As of May 2016 , the vault has already over 8,000 varieties of crops , including about 200,000 and 125,000 varieties of rice and wheat , respectively . <p> " If anything were to go wrong in the plant gene banks around the world the idea is that we could have the material in the seed bank in Svalbard and can retrieve it there , " said executive director ? slaug Haga . <p> Haga added the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unique plant variety around the globe , " which is especially vital in considering seed types to suit the given environment . <p> " Diversity is disappearing in the field and seed banks are disappearing due to war and natural disasters , " Haga said . <p> In 2015 , the vault conducted its first seed withdrawal .
