<h> ' Jai or Vidarbha , I 'm concerned with people 's problems ' <p> Bhandara-Gondia MP Nana Patole might be considered a maverick for being the lone politician taking on top forest officials of Vidarbha for their failure to protect the region 's most popular tiger ' Jai ' . The animal , which assumed legendary status due to its size , has gone missing without a trace for the last five months . The forest department has shown only half-hearted measures to launch search operations for ' Jai ' which became the darling of tourists at the newly created Umred-Karhandla Wildlife Sanctuary . <p> Patole says his espousing the cause of the tiger has been misconstrued by state forest minister and party colleague Sudhir Mugantiwar .
