<p> In a dense forest , trees race to the sky to get as much sunlight as possible , while underground roots battle it out for precious moisture . This is what we 've been taught . Managers of timber forests thin out some trees in order to speed up the growth of others . Even tree huggers will sometimes cut down a tree in order to conserve another judged as more worthy . <h> The Hidden Life of Trees by Peter Wohlleben , Penguin Random House India <p> But those who spend long years in the embrace of a forest see a very different story . When a domineering tree has been cut down to allow more light to another tree , the second one also dies . Clearly there 's more than just competition for resources going on . What we do to one tree has an unexpected and inexplicable effect on nearby trees .
