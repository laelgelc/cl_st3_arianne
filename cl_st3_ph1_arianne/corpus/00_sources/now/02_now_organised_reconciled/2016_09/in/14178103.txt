<h> Jayalalithaa urges PM Modi to act on Siruvani resolution <p> A day after the Tamil Nadu assembly unanimously adopted a resolution condemning Kerala for its Siruvani dam plan , chief minister J Jayalalithaa on Saturday requested PM Narendra Modi to instruct the environment and forest and climate change ministries to take immediate action on it . <p> TNN Sep 4 , 2016 , 01.02 AM IST <p> Chennai : A day after the Tamil Nadu assembly unanimously adopted a resolution condemning Kerala for its Siruvani dam plan , chief minister J Jayalalithaa on Saturday requested Prime Minister Narendra Modi to instruct the ministries of environment and forest and climate change , and water resources to take immediate action on it . <p> The resolution had strongly urged the Centre not to accept the recommendations of the Expert Appraisal Committee for River Valley and Hydroelectric projects made in its August meeting . The committee had granted sanction to Kerala for standard terms of reference to conduct an environmental impact assessment to build a dam across river Siruvani , as part of Attappady Valley Irrigation Project .
