<h> Modi govt mulling sanctuaries for rescued cattle , union minister of state for home affairs , Hansraj Ahir <p> Chandrapur : The union government is contemplating creation of livestock sanctuaries which will be safe havens for rescued cattle and thousands of cows kept in Gaushalas , said union minister of state for home affairs , Hansraj Ahir on Tuesday . He said he has already held a meeting in this regard with union minister for environment , forest and climate change Anil Madhav Dave . <p> " There is more than 6 crore hectare of forest land in the country . Our plan is to seek 50 lakh hectares for livestock sanctuaries . These sites will offer grazing pastures for livestock from the villages around , " Ahir said , while interacting with reporters during meet-the-press programme organized by Chandrapur Working Journalists Union . He emphasized that such facilities will help in curbing slaughter of cows and their progeny . <p> Villagers from surrounding areas can graze their cattle on the pastures , helping increase their numbers . Ahir claimed that the concept had backing of yoga guru Baba Ramdev . <p> Such sanctuaries for domestic animals are common in developed countries . Old , traumatized , abused or neglected domestic animals are provided with a homelike setting conducive to long term stay . The government appears to have plans for creation of such facilities in the form of livestock sanctuaries on a much larger scale . Ahir added that creation of the sanctuaries will require amendments in law , which will done after the plans gets final nod . He also proposed naming these livestock sanctuaries as Gomata Abhayaranya and Godhan Abhayaranya . <p> Speaking about the anti-Pakistan sentiment prevailing in the country after the Uri attack , the minister that the outpouring , particularly on social media , was quite natural .
