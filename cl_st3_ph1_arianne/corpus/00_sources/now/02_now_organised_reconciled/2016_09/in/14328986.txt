<h> Local activists want Animal Welfare Bill to be cleared <p> Nagpur : The existing Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act , 1960 , is not strong enough as it does not contain strict punishments for animal abusers . Demanding that Animal Welfare Bill 2014 should be passed immediately in the parliament , animal activists all over the country have launched a worldwide campaign under the aegis of India Unites For Animals . <p> City 's animal welfare NGOs and animal lovers are preparing for the movement which will be held on September 18 at RBI square in the city . " We will be extending our solidarity towards immediate passing of the bill . We are trying to get together as many like-minded people as we can , " said animal activist Aparna Modak , who is the city coordinator of the campaign . <p> The bill , which was drawn out by Animal Welfare Board of India ( AWBI ) and submitted to the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MOEFCC ) , has stricter laws and heavy penalties . He added that the campaign will be held in over 70 cities in India as well as other countries . " We will be demanding more accountability from candidates contesting for legislative assembly or local body elections , " said Siva . <p> With increasing number of brutal animal cruelty cases in the city , local animal activists feel that the bill will create fear and awareness among people . " In most of the cases , police did not file a FIR . Even in severe cases where animals were burned alive , no action was taken , " said People for Animals ( PFA ) city chief Karishma Galani . As reported by TOI , a two-year-old pet horse ' Bittu ' was brutally burned by its owner who applied hot paste on its body . The horse died during treatment in April this year .
