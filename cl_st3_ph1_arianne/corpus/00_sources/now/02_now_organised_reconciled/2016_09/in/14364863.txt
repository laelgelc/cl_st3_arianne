<h> Government sets new targets to fight poverty in state <p> TNN Sep 15 , 2016 , 06.47 AM IST <p> ( Representative image ) <p> SHIMLA : Himachal Pradesh has set the target to bring the percentage of population living below poverty line ( BPL ) from existing 8.1% to 2% by 2022 against the set timeline of 2030 under sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) , said state chief secretary V C Pharka . <p> Besides , the target has also been fixed to ensure universal coverage of safe drinking water supply to all households of the state , creating additional 30,000 hectares of cumulative command area ( CCA ) for irrigation and connectivity to all the panchayats with motorable roads . <p> Pharka was speaking during an interactive session to discuss about the sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) .
