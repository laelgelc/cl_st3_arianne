<h> Goa to host meet of BRICS environment ministers on September 16 <p> NEW DELHI : Environment ministers of the BRICS nations will meet in Goa on September 16 to strengthen mutual cooperation in critical areas like air and water pollution , liquid and solid waste management , climate change and conservation of biodiversity . <p> The BRICS is a group of nations , comprising Brazil , Russia , India , China and South Africa . The block together accounts for 41.6% of global population , 29.31 % of total world 's territory and about 22% of world 's total GDP . <p> Since the BRICS nations have rich bio-diversity , the choices made by these countries have a global significance . <p> India will be represented in the meeting by the country 's environment , forests and climate change minister Anil Madhav Dave who assumed charge in July . <p> The meeting , bring organised by the Union environment ministry , assumes significance after the adoption and signing of the landmark global climate deal ( Paris Agreement ) , Addis Ababa Action Agenda on Financing for Development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ last year . <p> " It is one in a series of meetings being organized by various central government ministries in the run-up to the BRICS Heads of States Summit in October at Goa , after India assumed BRICS Presidency this year from Russia " , said the environment ministry 's statement while announcing the Goa meet . <p> It said , " The meeting is expected to discuss Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) and the Joint Working Group on environment .
