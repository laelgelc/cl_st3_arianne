<h> Uttarakhand one among 6 states for National Forestry Monitoring System <p> Seema Sharma <p> Sep 2 , 2016 , 10.58 PM IST <p> Representative image <p> DEHRADUN : Uttarakhand is one of the six states where pilot project for National Forestry Monitoring System ( NFMS ) is going to be started . Other states are Madhya Pardesh , Karnatka , Punjab , West Bengal and Meghalaya . NFMS will be conducted under the overall guidance of ministry of environment and forests and climate change ( MoEF ) and with the technical assistance of Food and Agriculture Organisation of United Nations , The pilot project will come to an end by December next year . This project will also train forestry personnel of these state Governments to collect the ground data of forests . <p> Director General of Forest in MOEF , SS Negi said , " The largest region in Uttarakhand is consisting of forest so it is quite an important state for NFMS study . Punjab which does not have much of forest too has been included in the pilot study . Punjab has cultivated large @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not ignored . Similarly Meghalaya has most of their forest which are owned by the communities so that will be a different experience . Similarly West Bengal is known for its mangrove . So each state has been selected for their unique forestry management . They represent different locations in the country . " <p> According to Saibal Dasgupta , director general of Forest Survey of India , initially forest personal of six states will be trained in NFMS , then more states will be taken . " The forestry personal will collect ground data about presence of various aspects of forest which include the various vegetative species such as tree , bush , grass , invasive species such as lantana , diseases in wood , regeneration or regression of species , etc . The NFMS data will be used to carry out national and state level integrated assessments related to forestry .
