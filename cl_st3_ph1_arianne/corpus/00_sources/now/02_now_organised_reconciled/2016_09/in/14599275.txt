This would help integrate CBEC IT systems with the Goods and Services Tax Network ( GSTN ) . <p> The project would facilitate extension of the Indian Customs Single Window Interface for Facilitating Trade ( SWIFT ) and other taxpayer initiatives under ' Digital India ' and ease of doing business of the CBEC , the government said in a statement . <p> The implementation strategy for the project will be to ensure readiness of CBEC 's IT systems by April 1 , 2017 , when GST is to be introduced @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ out while keeping the existing taxpayer services running . <p> With the GSTN for processing of registration , payment and returns data sent by GSTN systems to CBEC , as well as act as a front-end for other modules like audit , appeal and investigation . <p> There is no overlap in the GST-related systems of CBEC and GSTN . <p> This IT infrastructure is also required for continuation of CBEC 's e-services in customs , central excise and service tax , implementation of
