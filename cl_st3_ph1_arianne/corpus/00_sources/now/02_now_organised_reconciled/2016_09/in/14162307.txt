The project will train forestry personnel to collect ground data from forests . <p> TNN Sep 2 , 2016 , 10.11 PM IST <p> DEHRADUN : Uttarakhand has been shortlisted among six states where a pilot project to develop a National Forestry Monitoring System ( NFMS ) will take place . The project will train forestry personnel to collect ground data from forests . <p> Other states where the pilot project will be carried out include Madhya Pradesh , Karnataka , Punjab , West Bengal and Meghalaya . NFMS will be conducted under the guidance of the ministry of environment and forests and climate change ( MoEF ) and with the technical assistance of Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations . <p> Director general of forest in MOEF , S S Negi said , " Each state has been selected for its unique forestry management . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ important for the study , Meghalaya has community-owned forests . West Bengal is known for its mangroves . " <p> Saibal Dasgupta , director general of Forest Survey of India , added , " The forestry personnel will collect ground data from forests . This includes data about various vegetative species and invasive species . The NFMS data will be used to carry out national and state level integrated assessments related to forestry .
