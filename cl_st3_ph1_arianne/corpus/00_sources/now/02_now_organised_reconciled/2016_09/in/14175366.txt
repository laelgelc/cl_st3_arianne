This marks a major step toward the enactment of the pact making way for other countries to follow suit . <p> The world 's two biggest emitters account for 38 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions , with China , the G20 host , alone accounting for 20 per cent of emissions , as per data made available by Climate Interactive and MIT Sloan .
