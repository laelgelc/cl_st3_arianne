<h> India to seek equitable agreement on phasing out of HFCs : Dave <p> New Delhi , Sep 27 ( PTI ) India will seek an " equitable " agreement that is in the best interest of the nation at the forthcoming negotiations on phasing down of harmful greenhouse gas hydrofluorocarbons ( HFCs ) scheduled to take place in Rwanda later this year . <p> Environment Minister Anil Madhav Dave stressed the need for focussing on issues like energy efficiency through research and development and changing architecture and consumption patterns to reduce cooling needs . <p> " India remains committed to address the issue of climate change .
