<h> Cabinet nod to ratify Paris pact <p> G.S . Mudur <p> New Delhi , Sept. 28 : The Union cabinet today approved India 's intention to ratify the Paris agreement on October 2 , pushing the document closer to coming into force and ensuring India 's contribution to the rulebook on how the world combats climate change from 2020 onwards . <p> India , which accounts for 4.1 per cent of global greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions , and the 61 countries , including China and the US , that have already ratified the agreement , will cumulatively represent 51.89 per cent of global GHG emissions . The agreement comes into force when at least 55 countries representing at least 55 per cent emissions join . <p> Most climate change analysts view the decision by India - the world 's fourth largest greenhouse gas emitter - to ratify as a way to project India as a " serious and responsible player " .
