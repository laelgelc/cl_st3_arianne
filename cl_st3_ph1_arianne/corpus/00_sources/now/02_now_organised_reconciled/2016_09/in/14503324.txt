<h> India Ranked A Poor 143rd Among 188 Countries On The Health Front <p> A global study on a range of health indicators has ranked India 143rd among 188 countries . India was placed just ahead of Pakistan and Bangladesh which were ranked 149th and 151st respectively . <p> BCCL/representational image <p> India 's poor performance on hygiene , air pollution and mortality were among the factors that placed it lower than countries like Bhutan , Botswana , Syria and Sri Lanka . <p> " Despite rapid economic growth , India was ranked below Comoros and Ghana , " the first annual assessment of Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) health performance published in medical journal Lancet and launched at a special event at the United Nations general assembly in New York said . <p> BCCL/representational image <p> For malaria , which was one of the health indicators assessed , India merely scored 10 points and remained in the red zone . Similarly for hygiene , the study gave India just eight points , while for PM 2.5 levels it scored just 18 points .
