<h> G20 countries score poorly in climate goals report <h> Carbon dioxide emissions of these nations went up by 56% between 1990 and 2013 . <p> Global greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions of G20 countries are continuing to increase , a report from Climate Transparency , an open global consortium , has shown ahead of the 2016 G20 Hangzhou summit to be held on September 4-5 in China . Between 1990 and 2013 , the absolute carbon dioxide emissions of G20 countries , which account for three-fourths of global CO2 emissions , went up by 56 per cent , the report shows . <p> Funded by Climate Works Foundation , Stiftung Mercator and the World Bank Group , Climate Transparency analysed key indicators , including carbon intensity and share of coal in total electricity produced , to assess the performance of these countries and found that half of G20 countries are " inadequate " as regards actions taken to curb climate change .
