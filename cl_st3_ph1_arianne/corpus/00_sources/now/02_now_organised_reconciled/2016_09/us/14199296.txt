Since its initial observation , the quasi-biennial oscillation remains unchanged . This disruption of the pattern held for nearly half a year , with the usual pattern returning back in July 2016 . <p> " The quasi-biennial oscillation is the stratosphere 's Old Faithful , " said Paul Newman , Chief Scientist for Earth Sciences at NASA 's Goddard Space Flight Center , Greenbelt , Maryland , and lead author of the study , in a press release .
