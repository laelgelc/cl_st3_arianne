<h> The cost of climate change to millennials <p> My generation -- the millennials -- is now as large a bloc of eligible voters as the baby boomers , giving us the power to demand real solutions to the most urgent problems facing our nation -- but only if we vote . <p> This is a critical election with a lot at stake , especially on the issue of climate change . Climate change is our nation 's greatest challenge . If left unaddressed it will have devastating impacts on our economy , our environment , our communities and our families . <p> According to a recent report conducted by NextGen Climate and the public policy group Demos , " The Price Tag of Being Young : Climate Change and Millennials ' Economic Future , " the millennial generation will lose nearly $8.8 trillion in lifetime income because of climate change . <p> Stopping the climate crisis is absolutely critical for young people 's long-term economic well-being . A 21-year-old graduate in the class of 2015 earning a median income will lose $187,000 in wealth .
