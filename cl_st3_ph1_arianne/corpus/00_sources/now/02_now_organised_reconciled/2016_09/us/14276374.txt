<p> Due to widespread human developments , the world has encountered catastrophic declines in wilderness areas around the planet over the last two decades . The losses have been so alarming that they comprise a tenth of global wilderness since the 1990s -- an area that is twice the size of Alaska and half the size of the Amazon , a new study reveals . <p> According to the study , published in the journal Current Biology on Thursday , the Amazon and Central Africa have been the hardest hit regions .
