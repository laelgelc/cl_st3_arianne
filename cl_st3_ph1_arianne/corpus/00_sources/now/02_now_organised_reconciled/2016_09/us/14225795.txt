<p> This is my last column for U.S. News &amp; World Report . In a few days , I am headed around the world on a " family gap year " -- nine months of backpacking through South America , Asia and Africa . One purpose of a gap year is to spend time with family while minimizing outside responsibilities , so for now this is farewell . <p> But what a strange time for someone who writes about politics to be leaving the country . The world is on fire -- from Syria to Russia to Europe to our own unbelievable presidential election . I 've always appreciated those sweeping titles that history books assign to long stretches of time : The Revolutionary Age ; Manifest Destiny ; The World at War ; and so on . The era we are now living through may end up titled The Failure of Governance . <p> What felt like an inexorable march towards democracy at the end of the Cold War has stalled , or even ground into reverse .
