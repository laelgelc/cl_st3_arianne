<p> It 's no surprise that the Republican and Democratic party platforms depict starkly different visions for America 's open spaces . But specifically , we found the platforms hinge on one underlying -- and in the Republican party 's case , unspoken -- question : Is climate change real , and a real threat ? <p> While Democrats answer the question in their chapter 's title , " Combat Climate Change , Build a Clean Energy Economy , and Secure Environmental Justice , " the Republican platform 's section on energy and the environment , titled " America 's Natural Resources , " never once uses the words " climate change " or " global warming .
