If someone or something is being criticized , they should be given the chance to respond . While " both sides " of an issue do not need to be referenced exactly equally , the " other " side should , at least , be represented fairly and accurately . <p> But , increasingly , journalists have twisted that ethos -- especially during presidential elections -- into false balance , or an attempt to normalize what is not normal .
