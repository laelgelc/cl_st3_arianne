<p> Today presents one of the nicer days of the past few months and tomorrow , more or less , follows suit . But some heat and humidity returns midweek before a cold front gets rid of it , setting up a blissful Thursday and Friday . Under sunny skies , afternoon highs are mostly in the low 80s -- moderated by a light breeze from the north and low humidity ( dew points in the 50s ) .
