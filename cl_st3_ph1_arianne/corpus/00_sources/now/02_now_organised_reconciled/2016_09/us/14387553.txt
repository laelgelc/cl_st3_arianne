<p> European Union leaders gave a political nod to the fast ratification of the global climate accord , a step that could enable the pact to come into force less than a year after it was agreed to by more than 190 nations in Paris . <p> " Not only will the European Parliament vote in October ; now all members of the European Union stand ready to ratify the accord as soon as possible , " French President Francois Hollande said Friday after an informal summit of 27 leaders from the bloc in Bratislava . The U.K. did n't participate in the summit , which was called to debate the future of the region after Brexit . <p> The most ambitious scenario sketched by the Slovak presidency of the EU assumes the approval procedure at the union level can be finished by Oct. 7 , said Jos Delbeke , director general for climate at the European Commission , the bloc 's executive .
