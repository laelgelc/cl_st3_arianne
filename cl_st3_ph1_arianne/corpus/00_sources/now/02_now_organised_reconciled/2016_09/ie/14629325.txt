Photograph : Vincent Hyland/Wild Derrynane <p> Southerly winds forecast for the next seven days are expected to sweep more Portuguese men o'war onto the Atlantic seaboard , amid continued warnings about their extremely dangerous sting . <p> Fish farmers , sea swimmers , surfers and beach walkers have been advised to exercise caution on sighting the marine animals , which are not jellyfish but siphonophores or colonies of zooids . <p> First recorded almost a month ago , most of the purple/pink animals with a balloon form resembling a Cornish pastie are being stranded on the Cork and Kerry coasts , according to NUI Galway Ryan Institute scientist Dr Tom Doyle . <p> He has had reports of about 700 since early September in locations from Cork to Donegal .
