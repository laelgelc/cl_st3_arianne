<h> Kildare aid worker moved to tears by child poverty in Malawi <h> Girl playing with doll made from clay <p> A seasoned Kildare aid worker has spoken about how he was moved to tears when he saw a little girl playing with a clay doll in one of the poorest countries in the world . <p> Malawian girl Cynthia Sikaenela , 5 , does n't have any manufactured toys but makes her own out of clay and small sticks . <p> Concern board member Michael Kenny , 59 , from Maynooth , who was a Concern volunteer in Rwanda in 1994 , was visiting Cynthia 's village in the southeast African country recently when he saw her playing with one of her clay dolls . <p> The experienced aid worker said : " The way she was carrying it and looking at it reminded me of my own granddaughter caring for her doll , and wrapping it in a blanket like a tender fragile baby . <p> " So I asked the girl , with her mothers ' permission , to show me her doll .
