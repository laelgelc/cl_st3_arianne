Recycle where possible , and get a compost bin . Most operators collect brown bin waste , or you can produce compost for use in the garden . Try and buy refills of household staples like coffee or washing powder , which use less packaging . <p> 3 Green your commute <p> Walk , cycle or use public transport where possible . Both emissions and congestion are cut when fewer cars are on the road , and you 'll save money , too .
