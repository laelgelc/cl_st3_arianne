<h> Bill Nye is Coming to Netflix <p> Also known as " The Science Guy , " the popular scientist wants to save the world with his new show . <p> You may remember him from his popular science show Bill Nye the Science Guy , but he 's also gone on to be one of the most outspoken advocates for science , including the danger and reality of climate change and the importance of science education . <p> Now he 's got a new talk show in the works , Bill Nye Saves the World . Each episode will have him discuss a different topic and refute common science myths , particularly those purported by people of power . The show is expected to cover popular topics such as vaccinations , GMOs , and , of course , climate change . Bill Nye hopes to enlighten his audience and , hopefully , make a difference .
