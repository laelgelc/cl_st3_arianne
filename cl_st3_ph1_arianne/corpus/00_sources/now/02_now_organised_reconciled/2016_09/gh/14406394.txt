<p> Opening the three-day Fifth West Africa Clean Energy and Environment Exhibition and Conference ( WACEE ) in the Ghanaian capital , the envoy said Ghana 's leadership role in Africa and in the South in setting Greenhouse Adaptation and mitigation targets was recognized by the EU . <p> Energy sector players in the West African sub-region are meeting to find linkages between policy and practice and solutions to energy security , waste and environmental and Climate Change Adaptation issues . <p> " As European Union , this morning we recognize this ratification by Ghana on the 4th of August an important step in moving forward and it confirms the leadership that Ghana has been playing in Africa and in this question . All of these are practical initiatives Ghana has put forward and which we would like to support , " he remarked . <p> Hanna also pledged the EU 's support for various activities lined up for Climate Change Adaptation and Renewable Energy . <p> " Together as European Union , with all our 28 member states , we have earmarked 160 million Euros to support @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he announced . <p> According to him , the EU had also set up a much wider fund of three billion Euros for cooperation on Energy over the next seven years to leverage between 15 billion and 30 billion Euros in loans . <p> The idea of leveraging , he explained , was to use the EU 's grant assistance to try to leverage loans to ensure that more and more resources went towards fighting the issue of Climate Change . <p> Hundreds of Renewable Energy and Environmental service companies , including Chinese firms , mounted stalls to participate in the three-day exhibition . <p> According to him , the region also has enormous potential in wind , biomass among many other Renewable Energy sources . <p> " So unlike Germany and other places which have minimum amount of sun exposure but are still able to make the best use of it , I think we in the sub-Saharan African region have a possibility that is overwhelming , " he stressed .
