<h> China and US approve Paris climate agreement <p> Chinese President Xi Jinping and US President Barack Obama handed over their countries ' instruments of joining the Paris Agreement on Climate Change to UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon successively on Saturday , a day before the start of the G20 Summit in Hangzhou . <p> The simultaneous ratification of China and the US , as the world 's top two economies and emitters , demonstrate their determination to address global challenges together . The two nations also launched productive dialogues and cooperation in coping with climate changes previously . <p> In his speech at the submission ceremony , President Xi stressed that as a responsible major developing nation , China is doing its utmost in energy conservation , emission reduction and low-carbon development . <p> He also urged that developed nations to fulfill their promise to help improve developing countries ' capability in dealing with climate change by providing capital and technological support . <p> President Obama said that the simultaneous ratification and acceptance of Paris Agreement by the US and China is of historic significance . <p> Ban @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in coping with climate change , saying that the ratification will draw UN 's goal of bringing the pact into effect within this year closer .
