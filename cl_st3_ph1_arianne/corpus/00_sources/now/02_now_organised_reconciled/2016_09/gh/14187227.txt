<h> Sept. 4 China-related world news summary at 1000 GMT <p> HANGZHOU -- U.N . Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon on Sunday spoke highly of the ratification of the landmark Paris climate change agreement by China and the United States , calling for accelerated efforts from other countries to join the ambitious pact . " With China and the United States making this historic step , we now have 26 parties in the UNFCCC ( U.N . Framework Convention on Climate Change Convention ) and 39 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions accounted for , " Ban said at a press conference on the sidelines of the 11th Group of 20 ( G20 ) summit in this eastern Chinese city .
