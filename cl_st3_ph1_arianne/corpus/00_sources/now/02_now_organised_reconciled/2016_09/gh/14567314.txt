<h> UN says South Sudan 's high temperature hurts agriculture <p> Biplove Choudhary , UN Development Programme ( UNDP ) Senior Program Advisor , revealed in Juba that the change in temperature coupled with deforestation is a major threat to food security in the country and put 4.8 million people in danger of hunger . <p> " The temperature in South Sudan is 2.5 degrees higher than in neighboring countries that is having impact on agriculture , on incidents of flooding and deforestation since 1.5 percent of the forests are cut down in South Sudan , " Choudhary said . <p> South Sudan 's population is estimated at 12 million , of which 80 percent are involved in agriculture despite the country 's abundant oil and mineral wealth . <p> The country experiences sporadic rainfall patterns and last year , droughts caused hunger in areas of Ikotos , Magwi and Kapoeta in Eastern Equatoria region . <p> The UN Environment Programme ( UNEP ) Country Director Arshad Khan said there is need for introducing weather early warning systems and high-yielding crop breeds . <p> " There is need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change , " he added . <p> Meanwhile , Choudhary disclosed that UNDP has launched an ambitious program of supporting the women and youth to start small enterprises in a bid to create self-employment . <p> " We do not have enough employment for the vast majority of the people in this country because government jobs are already taken and the private sector is just evolving , therefore trust in self-employment is valuable , " he added . <p> South Sudan descended into civil conflict in December 2013 , which has left tens of thousands dead and more than 2 million others homeless .
