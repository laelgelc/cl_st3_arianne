<h> Global airlines embrace pollution plan <p> New York - The aviation industry is supporting a United Nations proposal to limit pollution from international flights even though the measure may eventually cost companies $24 billion annually . <p> Trade groups representing United Continental Holdings , Boeing and other industry leaders are pushing nations to join the agreement , which would require companies to offset their emissions growth by funding environmental initiatives . The accord , being brokered in Montreal during 11 days of talks beginning on Tuesday , would be the first global climate pact targeting a single industry . <p> File picture : Jared Wong . With aviation emissions forecast to triple by 2050 , airlines believe that regional or global regulation is inevitable .
