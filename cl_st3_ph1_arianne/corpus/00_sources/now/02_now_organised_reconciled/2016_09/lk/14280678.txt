<p> Prime Minister Ranil Wickremesinghe presented the legislation to the House and called on all parliamentarians to approve the bill to ratify the landmark accord that sets outs a global action plan to put the world on track to avoid dangerous global warming . <p> Sri Lanka at the 21st session of the United Nations Framework Convention held in the French capital of Paris on Environmental disaster ( UNFCCC ) last year agreed to contribute to the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> At the UNFCCC meeting in Paris 180 countries agreed to take all possible measures to maintain the global warmth level at least at 1.5 degree Celsius and to prevent it increasing beyond 2 degree Celsius by the year 2100 . <p> Sri Lanka in April this year joined 170 other countries in the world in signing the Paris climate accord to set in motion a plan to reduce global warming . <p> Sri Lanka 's Minister of Science , Technology and Research Susil Premajayantha signed the agreement on April 22 , 2016 on behalf of the Sri Lankan government .
