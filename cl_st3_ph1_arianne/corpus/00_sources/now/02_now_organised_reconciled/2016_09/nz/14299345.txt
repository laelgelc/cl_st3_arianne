<h> Climate effect on species needs global research effort <p> Scientists are calling for a coordinated , global effort to research how climate change will affect different species - including many of New Zealand 's unique creatures . <p> The paper , published in the international journal Science , said changes in global temperatures were affecting the diversity and distribution of living species worldwide - but not enough is known about how to protect them . <p> The paper called for research to be done so outcomes for every species affected by climate change could be forecast . <p> Lincoln University researcher Dr William Godsoe , who was involved in writing the paper , said climate change was already affecting some of New Zealand 's beloved native species . <p> Tuatara were one species where the effects were already being documented , he said . <p> " If it 's really warm out , tuataras produce more males and in effect that means they 're wasting resources that could be used to produce females that are more useful for producing more offspring in the next generation .
