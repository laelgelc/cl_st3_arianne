<h> New solar array to cut Kiribati fossil fuel bill by $290K <p> A World Bank project involving large scale solar panels which was launched in Tarawa on Friday is expected to save the Kiribati government $US290,000 in fuel bills . <p> In a statement , the bank says this is the equivalent of 230,000 litres less diesel used per year , reducing the countries already negligible greenhouse gas emissions by 765 tons per year . <p> At peak capacity the large scale solar panels installed at four government owned facilties can generate 548 kilowatts of power , about the same as 5,480 standard light bulbs . <p> This will be fed directly into the South Tarawa electricity grid helping to power schools , hospitals and other public buildings . <p> South Tarawa will benefit directly from the new solar project Photo : Supplied <p> Speaking at the launch , Kiribati Vice President Korabi Nenem said as a nation profoundly affected by the impacts of climate change it was important for Kiribati to practice what it preached and reduce its reliance on fossil fuels . <p> In 2008 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
