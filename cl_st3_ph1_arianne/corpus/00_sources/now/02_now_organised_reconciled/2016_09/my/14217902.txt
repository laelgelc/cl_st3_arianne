<h> Black Lives Matter protesters shut London airport <p> All flights due to land at the airport were diverted to Southend and Gatwick airports near London . <p> LONDON : British Black Lives Matter protesters on Tuesday chained themselves together on the runway of London 's City Airport , forcing all flights in and out of the business travel hub to be diverted . <p> Police officers were negotiating with nine protesters , who had " erected a tripod and have locked themselves together , " according to a statement from the Metropolitan Police . <p> " At 05:40hrs ( 0440 GMT ) on Tuesday , 6 September , officers were alerted to a number of protesters who had made their way airside at London City Airport , " it added . <p> " We are awaiting the arrival of specialist resources that are able to ' unlock ' the protesters . No arrests have been made . " <p> The airport said in a statement that it was " currently experiencing disruption to all flights due to protesters .
