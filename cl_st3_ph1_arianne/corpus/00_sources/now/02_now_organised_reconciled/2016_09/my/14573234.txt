<p> A new book by Michael Mann and Tom Toles takes a fresh look on the effects humans are having on our climate and the additional impacts on our politics . While there have been countless books about climate change over the past two decades , this one -- entitled The Madhouse Effect - distinguishes itself by its clear and straightforward science mixed with clever and sometimes comedic presentation . <p> In approximately 150 pages , this books deals with the basic science and the denial industry , which has lost the battle in the scientific arena and is working feverishly to confuse the public . The authors also cover potential solutions to halt or slow our changing climate .
