<h> Researchers rescued from polar bears in Arctic <p> A dozen polar bears fell upon five Russian researchers for two weeks in an Arctic weather station before rescue arrived Wednesday . <p> For two weeks , polar bears have encircled the the Troynoy Island weather station , located in the Kara Sea north of Siberia , trapping five scientists inside . The Russian news agency TASS initially reported that supplies would not arrive for another month , but in a stroke of good fortune , a passing ship delivered the flares and dogs by helicopter on Wednesday . <p> " A helicopter that took off from the Akademik Treshnikov expedition vessel of the Arctic and Antarctic Research Institute of the Federal Service for Hydrometeorology and Environmental Monitoring has delivered three puppies and pyrotechnical devices to the station to scare the bears away " , Vassiliy Shevchenko , the head of the Sevgidromet State Monitoring Network that owns the station , told TASS .
