<h> Terry Glavin : The glorious delusion of the Northwest Passage <p> It was a mania that had got into the heads of European kings and merchant princes from the accounts of the 13th-century Italian adventurer Marco Polo . Within three centuries , on maps depicting Polo 's travels in Xanadu , the realm of the Great Khan of Cathay , it was showing up as the Strait of Anian . The Northwest Passage , as the British came to call it , was a direct sea route from the North Atlantic to the fabulous riches of China . Except it did n't exist . <p> It never existed .
