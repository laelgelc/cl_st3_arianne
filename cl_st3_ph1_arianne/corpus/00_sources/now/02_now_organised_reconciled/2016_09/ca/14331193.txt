<h> News briefs : Audain launches grizzly bear charity <h> The Grizzly Bear Foundation will launch inquiry into state of the animals around the province <p> Alyssa Noel / Whistler Question <p> September 12 , 2016 06:58 PM <p> Michael Audain , the philanthropist behind Whistler 's Audain Art Museum , has launched a new non-profit organization dedicated to the welfare of British Columbia 's grizzly bears . <p> As its first task , the Grizzly Bear Foundation will travel around the province -- stopping in places like Cranbrook , Fort St. John , Vancouver , Victoria and Prince Rupert -- to talk with residents and bear experts about the best ways to help the animals , Audain said during a press conference on Thursday ( Sept. 8 ) . <p> " After we 've heard from the people throughout B.C. we 'll then be forming a report and the report will give us some guidelines as to our own projects -- what we should be doing as a foundation to address the concerns that people have about the grizzly bears , " Audain said . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bears face , ranging from climate change to food sources . <p> Audain said he developed an interest in grizzly bears " later in life " after a trip to the Great Bear Rainforest . He recounted sitting on a log in the forest when a mother and three cubs walked by . " She knew exactly where we were , that we were sitting there , but she accepted us and got on with her business , " he said . " That was a really great experience . I 'd only seen animals the size of a grizzly bear in zoos before . I never imagined I 'd be in their territory . " <p> The inquiry -- for which the Audain Family Foundation donated $500,000 -- and report is expected to be finished by February 2017 . <p> BikeBC funds Valley Trail extension <p> The Resort Municipality of Whistler ( RMOW ) has received $90,102 in funding from BikeBC -- a provincial program to help communities build bike projects -- the BC Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure to extend the Valley Trail around the Meadow Park Sports Centre @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enhance accessibility by the road and through the parking lot to the sports centre .
