<h> News Local <h> MP Ellis creating new youth council <p> Neil Ellis , Member of Parliament for the riding of Bay of Quinte , is inviting all youth from the ages of 15 to 24 to apply to the new Bay of Quinte Constituency Youth Council ( CYC ) . Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has said " young voices must be part of our decision making process . " This is why Ellis is launching this new initiative , to bring together 10 to 20 people from the Bay of Quinte riding and to engage youth and hear their perspective on priorities and interests . " People have often said that our youth are disengaged from the political system . I say that it is the politicians that have failed to engage youth . I am proud to have the opportunity to reverse this trend and work with youth in our community to reinvigorate an interest in politics and inspire the next generation of Canadian leaders , " stated Ellis in a press release . The CYC will be inclusive , youth will be recruited from local schools , neighbourhoods and associations . Once selected , the council will meet several times throughout the year to discuss a number of issues that could include climate change , access to employment , LGBTQ issues , peacekeeping , and other topics that matter to the community . The CYC aims to affect local young people in a number of ways . Youth can connect with the other participants as well as develop a better understanding and to participate in the Canadian political process and use the experience to build valuable career skills . In addition , Ellis will learn about the views , values and priorities of youth in the region . Those interested in applying must complete and submit a short application form , which is available at Ellis ' official website at www.nellis.liberal.ca . Applications are due by midnight on Friday , Sept. 30.
