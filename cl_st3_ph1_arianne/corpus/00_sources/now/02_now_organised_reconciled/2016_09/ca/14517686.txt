<p> News that one of British Columbia 's only two oil refineries is up for sale and could be closed is bad news for environmentalists . <p> Because if Burnaby 's Chevron refinery disappears we will not only lose 430 good , green jobs -- we will also increase our carbon footprint . <p> Confused ? One environmentalist I recently talked to certainly was . But it 's actually not contradictory at all . <p> First , if Chevron closes , where will the light crude oil be refined for use in B.C. cars and trucks for jet fuel and heating oil ? <p> The obvious answer is " somewhere else " , but that means it will need to be transported back to B.C. -- unless we all immediately start driving electric cars . <p> So , we could be sending more oil tankers out of Vancouver without any new Kinder Morgan pipeline and welcoming more tankers full of gasoline back into the harbour . <p> Shipping oil out and gas back in is kind of crazy , and certainly would increase greenhouse gas emissions substantially , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the refined gas comes from the United States , it could be trucked in , also a significant GHG contributor . <p> And if the product gets refined in China , Indonesia , Malaysia or any other country where there are less-strict environmental controls in place than Canada , GHG emission could go up in addition to the tanker traffic . <p> So , we close this oil refinery but there 's an actual increase in GHG production worldwide ?
