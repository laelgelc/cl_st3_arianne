<h> research <h> Fifty years of research and innovation at SFU : ACT for Canada 's sustainable future <p> Scientists say the dawn of the Anthropocene is imminent . This geological epoch is characterized by the undeniable impact of human activity on Earth , including global warming and climate change . <p> And , as the frequency of severe natural events ramps up , we are increasingly seeing unprecedented economic and social consequences such as record insurance payouts and the emergence of climate change refugees . <p> Despite this gloomy outlook , experts are confident that these effects , while not necessarily reversible , can be mitigated through strategic policy planning .
