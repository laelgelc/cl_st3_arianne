<h> Partnering with FCM to advocate for a high quality of life <h> Regional Chair Gary Carr <p> This week , Halton Region hosted the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) fall Board Meeting , which concludes this Friday in Oakville . <p> FCM is the national voice of municipal governments , improving the quality of life in Canada by promoting strong , effective and accountable municipal government . Its members work with the Prime Minister , Cabinet Ministers and other federal leaders to represent the priorities of almost 2,000 Canadian municipalities . They highlight the need to align federal and local priorities based on the principle that strong hometowns make for a strong Canada . <p> The fall Board Meeting in Halton assembled FCM 's Board of Directors , comprised of nearly 100 elected municipal officials from across the country . It allowed us to join other municipalities in highlighting priority issues facing our community and others like it , advocating on behalf of all Canadians . <p> Halton is strongly represented on the national stage -- Regional Councillor Clark Somerville of Halton Hills was elected @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ strong representative of Halton in FCM for several years . His leadership , strategic perspectives and collaborative approach have greatly contributed to this meeting 's discussions about our nation 's future . <p> A strong Halton helps build a strong Canada , and this Board Meeting will help ensure that the voices of all Canadians are heard . <p> Our work with FCM is just one way that we advocate on your behalf to help maintain a high quality of life for all people of Halton , today and into the future . Many of the priority issues under discussion this week closely align with those that Halton Regional Council has raised with other levels of government . These include funding for affordable housing , adapting to climate change and transportation infrastructure , as well as water and wastewater infrastructure . <p> The Region continues to advocate to the provincial and federal governments for long-term , predictable funding and legislative amendments on issues facing the region through our Advocating for a Strong Halton campaign . To learn more , please visit halton.ca/advocacy . <p> As Halton bids farewell to the FCM @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ service and dedication to Canadian communities .
