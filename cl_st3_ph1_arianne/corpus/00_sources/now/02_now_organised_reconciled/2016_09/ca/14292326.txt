<h> Cannon : Alberta First : Answering the global call-to-action on energy research <p> The U of C will benefit from a $75-million research grant awarded from the federal government 's Canada First Research Excellence Fund in Calgary.Lyle Aspinall Lyle Aspinall / Lyle Aspinall/Postmedia Network <p> Alberta and its universities are at a critical juncture . Alberta 's geography and history have made the energy industry a pillar of the province 's economy , and energy research and innovation a focus of its universities for decades . Researchers have been examining ways to discover , access and expand Alberta 's resources since the 1920s when Karl Clark patented a process for separating and recovering oil from the oilsands . In the last decade alone , more than 90 per cent of the world 's oilsands research publications have come from Canadian universities and research agencies , the majority from the University of Alberta and University of Calgary , with many of them resulting in major efficiencies and reductions to the environmental impacts of the industry . <p> As acknowledged world leaders in energy research , the University of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the research and the education of future energy leaders further . We are being challenged to think differently and develop new technologies , policies , innovations and programs that will allow Albertans and the global community to make the necessary transition from a hydrocarbon to low-carbon economy . And , we are being challenged to do so with the greatest benefit to all environments and communities . <p> Multiple factors are compelling this change in global energy systems ; thus , no one strategy will suffice in response . Energy research today must encompass a full-range of approaches , from sustainable development of fossil fuels to research on renewables , biofuels , climate change , pollution controls , land reclamation , water use , environmental law and policy , and much more . Again Alberta -- with its innovators , geography and history -- finds itself at the nexus of these issues and in a position to lead . <p> Thanks to visionary new Government of Canada funding announced this week , the University of Calgary and University of Alberta will be able to meet the global call-to-action with unprecedented vigour @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ $75-million Canada First Research Excellence Fund grant for two distinct but complementary proposals . <p> What will this groundbreaking investment make possible at our universities ? <p> The University of Calgary 's Global Research Initiative in Sustainable Low Carbon Unconventional Resources will accelerate Canada 's transition to a low-carbon future . Through collaborative research programs , involving partners from around the world , the goal is to transform the ways we extract energy from unconventional hydrocarbon resources such as the oilsands to improve efficiency and greatly reduce Canada 's carbon footprint . Research will focus not only on seeking new fossil fuels-based energy systems that are low or even zero carbon , but also on transforming how we capture , store and convert CO2 so that we can meet our long-term climate commitments at much lower cost . <p> Complementing this focus , the University of Alberta 's Future Energy Systems Research Institute will support research projects on the development of multiple forms of energy , including hydrocarbons , wind and solar , as well as critical work studying their impact on various ecological systems , the climate , health , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ collaboration and strategic alignment of our goals , the University of Alberta and University of Calgary will play a pivotal role in strengthening and diversifying Alberta 's economy , building a global hub of clean energy technology and innovation . Our efforts will expand Alberta 's global reach and influence , opening new avenues of connection . <p> Perhaps most important , however , is the investment in people .
