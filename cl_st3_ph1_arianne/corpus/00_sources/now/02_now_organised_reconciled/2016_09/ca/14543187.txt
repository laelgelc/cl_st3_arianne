" We 're convinced that these policies are not just good for us , they 're good for everyone from Red Deer to Fredericton ... Many remote communities depend on ice roads to allow ground access to the wider world in the winter , including communities along the James Bay coast . <p> But its not just communities that need these roads , said Stewart , companies operating in northern regions depend on them too . <p> " De Beers is a great example .
