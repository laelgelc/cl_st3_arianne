<p> A new government report says by the end of this century , a changing climate is expected to at least double the area burned each year by forest fires in Canada . <p> The annual forest assessment of 2015 data by Natural Resources Canada says a warming climate will contribute to a 50 per cent increase in large fires , new tree diseases and more insect infestations .
