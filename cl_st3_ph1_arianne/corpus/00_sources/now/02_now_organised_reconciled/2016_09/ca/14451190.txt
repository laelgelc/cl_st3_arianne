A tax based on greenhouse gas emissions ( GHG ) generated from burning fuels . It puts a price on each tonne of GHG emitted . In practice , it is designed to send a price signal that will , over time , elicit a market response across the entire economy , resulting in reduced emissions . <p> What is cap-and-trade ? The " cap " sets a limit on emissions , which is lowered over time to reduce the amount of pollutants released into the atmosphere .
