<p> To mark the week comes a new survey looking at just how " science literate " Canadians are -- which seems to suggest there are some pretty big gaps in our understanding . <p> The survey comes from the Ontario Science Centre , and is based on an online poll by Leger of 1,578 Canadians . A probabilistic sample of this size would yield a margin of error of plus or minus 2.5 per cent , 19 times out of 20 . <p> It reveals what the Ontario Science Centre calls " significant gaps " in our understanding of issues like climate change , vaccinations and genetically modified organisms . <p> Maurice Bitran , the CEO and chief science officer at the Ontario Science Centre , spoke with CBC Radio about the results . <p> Eighty-nine per cent of respondents felt they understood the science behind vaccinations well , and 71 per cent said they formed their opinions on vaccinations based on science . <p> Although a study linking autism and vaccines has been discredited , 19 per cent of Canadians still believe there is a connection @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ survey .
