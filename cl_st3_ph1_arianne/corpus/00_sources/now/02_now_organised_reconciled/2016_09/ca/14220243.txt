<h> Bananas and olives now thriving in the Banana Belt of Canada <p> Mistaken Identity is one of several new locations serving wine , cider or beer on Salt Spring island . Michael McCarthy <p> On Salt Spring Island they are now growing olives . Not only olives , but bananas , palm trees , limes , figs , hops and grapes growing in abundance . <p> The view of the valley below from the garden at the Salt Spring Winery is terrific , but if you know where to look you 'll also spot an olive grove that has just been planted . This is the hottest location on the island , not only in its real estate location but in climate . The Garry Oaks and Salt Spring Island Wineries have been growing their own grapes here for several years , and now olive trees are basking in the sun . <p> Just down the road near Burgoyne Bay , hidden in the woods , is the new Salt Spring brewery with a tiny tasting room . No food available . <p> -- Salt Spring Wild is a new cidery serving a potent brew from the island 's apple orchards . Michael McCarthy <p> Just outside of the town of Ganges , out of nowhere has popped up Salt Spring Wild , a cidery making good use of the bountiful apple crop from the island 's old apple orchards , the original industry on the island that went dormant years ago . Picnickers can bask in the backyard at Wild at one of several tables set up to sip on the sweet apple , pear , elderberry and hopped apricot beverages ( 6 percent alcohol ) available . Just up the road from Wild the intrepid explorer will find Mistaken Identity , the third and newest winery on the island , with a fabulous south facing view of its vineyards and a large lawn with picnic tables to enjoy one of their several excellent wines . <p> Food for your picnics ? You can bring your own , or if you ask nicely @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fabulous picnic lunch in wicker baskets including wine glasses , knives and forks , with local cheeses and snacks . So you 'll be the envy of all as you spread your tablecloth out on a picnic table under the shade of the trees . Deep in the forest of the north end of the island , Fraser 's Thimble Farm will be happy to sell you a wide variety of exotic flowering and fruit trees including lime , black fruiting and contorted fruiting mulberries . <p> Jason at Tour Saltpring.com will be pleased to escort you to all the tasting rooms , farms and studios around the island , or even to a secluded beach , so you can enjoy your beverages and a picnic and leave the driving up to him . Accommodations are hard to find in the holiday months , but there are frequent flights from Coal Harbour and the South Airport via floatplane , so you can make a day of tastings and noshing and still be home for dinner . Fares from $135 . <p> Salt Spring enjoys the finest weather in Canada , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ facing hills on the island are warm enough to grow fruit , figs and ( thanks to local resident Banana Joe Clemente ) palm and banana trees are starting to appear in select locations . Abundant rain fills the many little lakes in the winter to provide a water supply , but summer is one long dry season that allows local farmers to grow a wide variety of organic vegetables , and now tropical fruit . Bananas actually growing in the Banana Belt ? Who knew ? It may not be climate change , but it sure is a big change from just a few years ago , when you bought your tropical fruit at the supermarket .
