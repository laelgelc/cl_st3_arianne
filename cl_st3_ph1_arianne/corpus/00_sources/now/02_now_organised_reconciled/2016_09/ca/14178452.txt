<p> In his first event at the G20 leaders ' summit , Justin Trudeau urged his peers Saturday to drive away the anti-globalization and protectionist attitudes that have been fuelling " divisive , fearful rhetoric " in different parts of the world . <p> The prime minister pointed to slowing global economic growth as a source of the anxiety behind the movements . He noted how it has surfaced in the Brexit debates in the United Kingdom and in election campaigns , an apparent reference to the United States . <p> Trudeau 's message was driven by one of Canada 's goals at the gathering in the Chinese city of Hangzhou : encouraging countries to beat back the growing sentiment that trade is bad . <p> Justin Trudeau is seen on a billboard in Beijing with a Chinese message that translates to : ' Welcome , Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau for his first official trip in China and to participate at the G20 summit in Hangzhou . His first G20 summit last November came only a couple of weeks after his Liberals took office . <p> Canada 's objectives at this summit are expected to range from promoting trade and investment , encouraging other countries to move forward in the fight against climate change and pressing them to use the necessary policy tools to generate growth . <p> Trudeau picks up a lobster during a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Alibaba Group , at the company 's Xixi Campus in Hangzhou , Zhejiang Province , China , on Saturday .
