<h> Mandy Nolan 's Soapbox : Find your E Spot <p> The E Spot . Find it . Use it . <p> Years ago women 's magazines delivered the exciting news that we ladies have a G spot . While some conspiracists believed the elusive G was concocted by aforementioned media to sell magazines and G spot-seeking products , there are many of us who will attest that yes , yes , YESSSSS ! ... the G definitely exists . <p> And yes , finding it did change my life . Or at least enhance it . The good news is : there 's a new life-changing spot ! An amazing spot we all have that can create an incredible sense of complete wellbeing and joy . In fact people who find this spot are happier than people who do n't . They 're also known to live more meaningful lives with more purpose . And people like them . Actually , they love them . <p> This elusive spot is also non-gendered -- everyone has one . It 's the E spot ! They all found their E spot and used it so much people are STILL talking about them . <p> It 's your Empathy Spot . <p> Basically it means you have to give a shit about people . Oh , and there 's one more thing -- you have to do something about it . Sound easy enough ? Apparently it 's a lot harder than you 'd think . Right now most of us are all over the A spot .
