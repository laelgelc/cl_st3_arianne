<p> The turmoil in the Liberal Party highlighted by the downfall of Malcolm Turnbull has been the most important development in Australian politics over the last year . It reflects that politics here is catching up with international developments ( the rise of Trump , the surge in support for the far right in Europe , increasing authoritarianism and xenophobic nationalism and so on ) as the crisis of neoliberalism continues to erode the credibility of the long-dominant mainstream parties and faith in parliamentary democracy . <p> The Liberals ' so-called moderates may have narrowly prevented home affairs minister Peter Dutton from becoming prime minister . However , they have no alternative program that offers a way forward for conservative politics or the capitalist class .
