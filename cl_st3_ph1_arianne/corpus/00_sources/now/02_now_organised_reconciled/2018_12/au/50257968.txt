<h> Why I support the Carmichael mine , not Adani <p> IMAGINE this scene : Children , usually orphans and runaways , scouring the garbage heaps desperately for scraps of food to stave off their pangs of hunger . If the food is n't rotten , it 's a bonus . And this in a megapolis like Mumbai , India 's premier city , its financial capital no less . <p> Have a conscience ? Then , surely , it is a heartbreaking sight . The tragedy is there is little you can do . Yes , you can buy these scavengers some food . But the sheer numbers mean you can only help a few . Even that poses difficulties . Give one of them some food and soon you 'll be surrounded by a dozen . They 'll beg you , touch you , plead , hang on to you . You become hostage to them until you empty your pockets - or dash off to get into a taxi or autorickshaw ( three-wheeler ) and flee . What would you do ? <p> It is one of the reasons I ran away . I emigrated . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ myself with my conscience , wanting to help yet not being able to . Judge me as you may , I could n't care less , because you will seldom be in such a predicament . I wrestled with my impotency to help every single day . <p> Luckily , those in remote villages fare a little better , but not much . True , the villagers have some form of rudimentary shelter and mercifully , they grow their own food , usually rice , their staple . The daily menu : plain rice , a couple of chillies , a piece of onion , and a plain , watery curry . That 's it . The rest of the food grown is sold . <p> What they sorely lack is toilet facilities and clean water . The biggest handicap is lack of power . This is the crux of the issue . <p> Without the benefit of electricity , the women cook in a wood-burning ' chulah ' - a stove . Every day . The smoke and hazardous gases cut short their lives . Dignity is usually all they have . Or can afford . What they yearn for most of all , though , is a better future for their children . Children who ca n't study at night , albeit from the shoddy education they receive , for want of light . <p> This is not hearsay . I lived with people like these for a short time .
