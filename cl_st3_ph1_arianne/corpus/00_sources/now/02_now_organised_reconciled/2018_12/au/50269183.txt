<h> Students walk out of class , demand climate action now <p> Last Friday students all across Australia walked out of school to strike against the government 's inaction on climate change . Thousands attended protests held in at least 30 cities and towns . <p> These protests happened alongside breaking news that the proposed Adani coal mine in Queensland will likely go ahead , despite the large public backlash . Neither major party is willing to stop it . <p> The Australian students were inspired by Swedish student Greta Thunberg , who skips classes every Friday to protest outside her parliament in Stockholm . <p> Thunberg calls on young people to join her . She wrote in The Guardian : " Sit outside your parliament or local government wherever you are and demand that they get on track to keep the world below 1.5 degrees of warming . " <p> Young people will live to face the most dire consequences of climate change .
