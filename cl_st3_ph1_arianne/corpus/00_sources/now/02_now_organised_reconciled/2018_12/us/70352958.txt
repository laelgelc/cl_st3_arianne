But parks officials and environmental groups have a $3.5 million plan to protect the majestic trees . <p> In 1998 , two researchers discovered the hidden grove in a remote section of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park , where trees tower 320 feet high and stretch nearly 30 feet in diameter . Rangers tried to keep the location secret , but amateur adventurers found the trees , some of the largest redwoods on Earth .
