Both studies highlight the risks of rising greenhouse gas emission ( GHG ) concentrations in the Earth 's atmosphere , and the potential consequences should these trends continue . The threats posed by a warming world are not just dangerous for the climate-dependent sectors of our economy ( crops , livestock , and global fisheries ) , but bad for global security as well . The Trump Administration 's Pentagon calls climate change a ' threat multiplier ' because it aggravates pre-existing societal stress factors . Instances of state collapse , refugee flows , and conflicts over basic resources , including food and water attributable to climate change , have already been studied . <p> But battling climate change will not be easy -- or cheap . The increased concentration of these gases in our atmosphere prevents solar energy from dissipating , leading to increases in average global temperatures ( the Greenhouse Effect ) .
