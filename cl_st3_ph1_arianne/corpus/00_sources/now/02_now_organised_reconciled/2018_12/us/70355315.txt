It is the manifest will of its members ; it is the overwhelming preference of young people ( 80% ) , who also by wild coincidence -- or is it ? -- favour Jeremy Corbyn . It is the ardent desire of environmentalists , feminists and most trade unionists . Brexit is a Tory shambles ; it has capsized all other government business . The political capital to be gained from shaking it off is almost unimaginable . A Labour party that campaigned for remain and won would become , de facto , the party of government .
