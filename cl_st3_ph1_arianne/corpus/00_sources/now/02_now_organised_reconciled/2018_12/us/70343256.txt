<p> Winter got off to a fast start in the Lower 48 even before it was technically winter . Waves of cold gripped the eastern two-thirds of the United States and several winter storms tracked across the region . <p> Conditions have since eased some , but the heart of winter lies ahead . Will cold and snowy conditions return and turn more harsh ? <p> The polar vortex , the roaring river of air winding around the North Pole , holds the cards . What they reveal could be very disturbing and a harbinger of extreme winter weather in the Eastern United States . <p> Judah Cohen , a climate researcher at Atmospheric and Environmental Research , monitors the condition of the vortex , every day checking the latest prediction models for any sign of disturbance .
