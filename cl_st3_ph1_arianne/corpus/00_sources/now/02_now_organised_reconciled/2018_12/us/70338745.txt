Photograph : Sonja Horsman for the Observer <p> On a mild Tuesday night on 28 March last year a minibus containing 15 protesters inched up to the perimeter of Stansted airport away from the public terminal and towards the cargo field . Shaking with adrenaline , they clutched at boltcutters to rip a hole through the barbed-wire fence and scattered across the tarmac . Within eight minutes , their lives were changed for ever . <p> Last week , 623 days later , the Stansted 15 , who that night had locked themselves on to a Titan Airways Boeing 767 secretly chartered by the Home Office , were convicted of terrorism offences at Chelmsford crown court . The group of nine women and six men had been protesting to stop the deportation of 60 vulnerable migrants to Ghana , Nigeria and Sierra Leone .
