Disruption is expected across the entire value chain over the coming decade . The energy and materials markets are converging into a broad and revolutionary sustainable manufacturing transformation ( SMT ) , triggered by changes in energy production and consumption , the shift toward more circular material flows , the emergence of new technologies and business models , and changing consumer demand . According to Navigant 's new Sustainable Manufacturing Transformation white paper , the expected developments in the energy and materials arena resulting from SMT represent market opportunities of more than $2 trillion for manufacturing companies . <p> Sustainable Feedstocks : A Multibillion Dollar Opportunity <p> One of the many SMT opportunities is in plastics , which results from a shift in fossil feedstocks to sustainable feedstocks . As a result , the transition will require the support of government regulation and corporate leadership to initiate the adoption of sustainable alternatives . When the transition takes place , the cost increase will make plastics too expensive for certain low value applications -- e.g. , disposable cups , plastic bags , and types of packaging . This will result in downstream product manufacturers likely switching to cheaper materials or different products altogether .
