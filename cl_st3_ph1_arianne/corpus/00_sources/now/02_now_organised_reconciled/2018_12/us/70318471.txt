The constant darkness offers the chance to revel in the wonders of the Northern Lights at any time , day or night . As the snow smothers the land this sub-polar realm , which has no roads going anywhere beyond the paltry stretches within each of the three tiny towns , suddenly becomes accessible by snowmobile , snowcat trucks and dog sled .
