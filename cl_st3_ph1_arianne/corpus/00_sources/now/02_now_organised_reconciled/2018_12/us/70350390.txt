It was a year full of devastating storms and disasters , scandal after scandal from tech companies , and chaotic politics from around the world . If there was any bright point in the year , it was that 2018 also brought with it a bumper crop of fantastic science fiction , fantasy , and horror novels that served as an oasis to examine the world around us , or to escape for brighter pastures . <p> The best books of this year told stories of interstellar colonization , of fantastic magical civilizations , optimistic alternate worlds , and devastating potential futures .
