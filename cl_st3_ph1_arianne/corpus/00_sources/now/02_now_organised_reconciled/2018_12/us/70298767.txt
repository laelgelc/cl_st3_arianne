<h> Guest column : New council will face a number of landmines <p> The interior of council chambers in the new City Hall is pictured Thursday , Oct. 25 , 2018 . Dax Melmer / Windsor Star <p> The newly elected Windsor City Council , as soon as they get over their post-election euphoria , will find some landmines waiting for them , such as the following : <p> Parking enforcement outsourcing : This was outsourced several years ago , resulting in savings to the taxpayer , but CUPE is now asking that the job be carried out once again by city employees . Expect strong pressure from the labour sector and a divided council . <p> The Paul Martin Building : In cutting costs , Premier Doug Ford has picked the $20-million low-hanging fruit for the conversion of the building to a Law School for the University of Windsor and the work will not proceed now . What to do with an outmoded office building ? <p> The mega-hospital : Is Ford looking at reducing the provincial deficit by $2 billion by cancelling the project ?
