<h> Op-ed Does state of nuclear industry require government action ? <p> Two of the state 's five nuclear facilities ( Three Mile Island , outside of Harrisburg , and Beaver Valley in western Pennsylvania ) will be prematurely shut down starting next year because of an inability to compete in today 's energy market flooded with abundant natural gas . <p> The public policy question before all Pennsylvanians is : Does the state of our nuclear industry require government action ? <p> Nearly two years ago , we formed the Nuclear Energy Caucus , a bipartisan group of both senators and representatives of the Pennsylvania General Assembly -- and the first of its kind in the nation -- to help answer that question . We were joined by dozens of our Republican and Democrat colleagues , from every corner of our state , who came together to address this issue . <p> Our goal was to provide decision-makers with the hard data , economic forecasts and possible scenarios they now need to make some hard choices .
