This year , scientists reported catching one of these ghostly particles with a massive instrument in Antarctica - the first time humanity has ever detected a high-energy neutrino and traced it back to where it came from . Paired with improvements in our ability to detect gravitational waves , the landmark achievement heralds a new era in astronomy in which researchers can learn about the universe using neutrinos and gravitational waves as well as ordinary light . The detection also helped solve a 100-year-old mystery about cosmic rays - extremely energetic particles that rain down from space .
