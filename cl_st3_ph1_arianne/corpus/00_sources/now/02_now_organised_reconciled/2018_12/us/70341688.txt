<h> Teen of the Week : Mechanicsburg teen stays humble while reaching to change the world <p> Humility is an ideal that many people claim or pursue , but many do not meet . Susanna Gaither aims to be the exception . <p> The Mechanicsburg Area Senior High School senior is first in her class and headed to an Ivy League college in the fall . However , without prompting when asked to explain why she was selected for Teen of the Week , Gaither credited her parents , Milton and Elizabeth Gaither , family and friends with helping her reach all of her achievements . <p> Gaither said her drive to succeed in school comes from a love of learning , saying she had just finished a test in chemistry that she enjoyed and was excited to be learning about the French Revolution . <p> Her drive does not come from a desire for personal accolades , she said . <p> " I think I have a lot of motivation because I want people to be happy , " she said .
