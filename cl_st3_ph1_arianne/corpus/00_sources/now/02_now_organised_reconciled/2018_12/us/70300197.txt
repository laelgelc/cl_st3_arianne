<h> Storm surge : ' Climate change in the age of denial ' <p> Dec 2 , 2018 <p> Storm Surge is pleased to bring Dr. Andrew Rosenberg of the Union of Concerned Scientists to Newburyport to discuss " Climate Change In the Age of Denial , " on Monday Dec. 10 at 7 p.m. at the Newburyport Community Center at 331 High St. in Newburyport . Doors open at 6:30 p.m . <p> The current administration has sidelined science and science-based policies at every turn , even while warning of the dire consequences of climate change in the newly released Fourth National Climate Assessment . <p> Rosenberg will describe the Union of Concerned Scientists ' efforts to mobilize opposition to the sidelining of science and will also help the public understand the economic and social impacts described in the new report . His talk will focus on how to mobilize citizens to take action , despite an administration that believes climate change is a hoax . <p> Rosenberg leads the Union of Concerned Scientists ' efforts to advance the essential role that science , evidence-based decision making , and constructive debate play in American policymaking .
