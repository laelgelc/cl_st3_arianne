Even if salmon swimming upstream can jump high enough to reach the culvert , the water flow is often so strong that it pushes them back out . <p> Executive Constantine included $12.5 million in the county 's 2019-2020 budget -- recently approved by the King County Council -- to design and build 36 projects that will remove barriers to 150 miles of healthy salmon habitat . <p> King County and other land managers are continuing their habitat-restoration work that will improve the health of the kokanee salmon population . They have a unique genetic signature , having adapted over centuries to the unique Lake Sammamish ecosystem , making them impossible to replace . Genetic diversity makes the natural environment healthier and more resilient , which is particularly important in the face of climate change . <h> Emergency actions and ongoing long-term work <p> The kokanee run that occurs in November and December -- known as " the late run " -- is the only remaining native run .
