<h> The Digital Hub selects architect and project management firms to support its development <p> The Digital Hub , Ireland 's largest and most established cluster of technology , digital media and internet companies , has announced the appointment of Scott Tallon Walker Architects and KSN Project Management to support the development of The Digital Hub . <p> Following an EU-wide tendering process , Scott Tallon Walker Architects has been selected as the Design Team and KSN Project Management as The Digital Hub 's project management services provider up until 2022 . Both companies will support the vision of The Digital Hub in developing a diverse and creative digital technology quarter in Dublin 8 . <p> One of the key parts of The Digital Hub campus relates to a 2.6 acre site on the northside of Thomas Street , Dublin 8 , known as ' The Windmill Site ' that incorporates the well-known landmark of St. Patrick 's Tower and two of The Digital Hub 's flagship office spaces comprising ' Digital Depot ' and ' The Grainstore ' .
