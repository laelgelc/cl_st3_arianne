<h> Adaption costs in Pacific pegged to be at $494.62m by 2020 <p> Vishaal Kumar <p> 12 December , 2018 , 6:00 pm <p> THE adaptation cost for coastal protection in Pacific Island Countries would be $US234 million ( $F494.62m ) per year by 2020 and $US285m($F602.42m) per year by 2040 , says deputy secretary general of the Pacific Islands Forum Secretariat Cristelle Pratt . <p> Ms Pratt highlighted this while addressing the Third High-Level Ministerial Dialogue on Climate Finance at Conference of Parties ( COP24 ) in Poland . <p> " In the last three years , the Pacific has been impacted by several extreme climate related weather events at magnitudes we have not seen before , " Ms Pratt said . <p> " The Pacific Island Countries(PIC's) were using the Pacific Climate Change Finance Assessment Framework to support their readiness . " <p> She added the impacts could reverse decades of development gains in just a few hours . <p> *More in tomorrow 's edition of The Fiji Times and on our e-edition .
