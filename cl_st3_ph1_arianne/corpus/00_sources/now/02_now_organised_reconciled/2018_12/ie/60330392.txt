<h> Seven young people bring the Lutheran communion experience to COP 24 <p> ( LWI ) -The planet 's future lies in the balance as world leaders , civil society and climate activists meet in Katowice , a coal mining town in Poland , to figure out how to implement the Paris Agreement on curbing climate change . <p> This week delegates at the 24th session of Conference of the Parties ( COP 24 ) to the United Nations ( UN ) Framework Convention on Climate Change are wrestling with the small print of the global climate deal , the rule book on exactly how to cut emissions and transition to a low-carbon economy . <p> The Lutheran World Federation ( LWF ) has an all-youth delegation comprising seven climate activists from the communion 's member churches in Argentina , Germany , Iceland , Indonesia , Poland , South Africa and the United States . Supported by two LWF staff , the young people bring perspectives from their respective countries and regions about the experience of climate change .
