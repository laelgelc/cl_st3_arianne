<h> ICBF and Teagasc to tackle methane emissions from cattle <p> Speaking at the 2018 Irish Cattle Breeding Federation ( ICBF ) Genetics Conference , Stephan Conroy of ICBF described the research project that is currently on-going at Tully Progeny Test Centre , Co . Kildare . <p> In collaboration with Teagasc , this project aims to tackle the issue of methane emissions from beef cattle , in an effort to reduce the sectors contribution to climate change . <p> He highlighted that : " Agriculture is seen as a big offender in terms of greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions such as methane . " <p> Furthermore , the potential of methane ( CH4 ) to affect global warming is 25 times greater than carbon dioxide . <p> Stephen noted : " When retailers visit Tully , the first thing they mention is the environmental footprint of the cattle ; they mention GHG emissions such as methane .
