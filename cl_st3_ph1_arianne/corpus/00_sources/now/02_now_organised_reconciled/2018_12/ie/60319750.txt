<h> Four NUI Galway researchers in top 1% of world 's most highly cited researchers <p> Four researchers at NUI Galway are among the top 1% of the world 's most highly cited researchers , it has been revealed . <p> Professor Henry Curran , Professor Colin O'Dowd , Professor Donal O'Regan and Dr Derek Morris , have all ranked in the top 1% of Clarivate Analytics list of Highly Cited Researchers for 2018 . <p> Approximately 6,000 researchers are named Highly Cited Researchers in 2018 -- some 4,000 in specific fields and about 2,000 for Cross-Field performance . <p> Professor Henry Curran is Director of the Combustion Chemistry Centre at NUI Galway 's School of Chemistry and of the Energy Research Centre in the Ryan Institute . His research interest lies in the study of the chemistry of how fuels burn in combustors in order to increase efficiency and reduce emissions for a cleaner world . <p> Professor Colin O'Dowd is Director of the Centre for Climate and Air Pollution Studies at the Ryan Institute , NUI Galway , and a Professor in the School of Physics , NUI Galway . He has written over 1,300 peer-reviewed mathematical articles , making him one of the most prolific authors in the history of mathematics in the world . <p> Dr Derek Morris is a Principal Investigator in the Neuroimaging and Cognitive Genomics ( NICOG ) Centre and is Senior Lecturer in Biomedical Science within the School of Natural Sciences at NUI Galway . <p> His research , which began during his time in TCD ( 2001-2013 ) , is based on using genetics and genomics to understand the biological basis of schizophrenia and other common psychiatric disorders . <p> Vice President for Research at NUI Galway , Professor Lokesh Joshi , said : " Professors ' Curran , O'Dowd , O'Regan and Dr Morris are outstanding in their fields and have deservedly earned respect and recognition globally for their research .
