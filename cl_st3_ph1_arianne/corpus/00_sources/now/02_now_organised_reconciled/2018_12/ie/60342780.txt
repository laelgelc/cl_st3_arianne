As we move further into a period characterised by extreme weather events , the resilience of our urban infrastructure will be tested . <p> As cities grow , their infrastructure becomes more complex . Eventually , networks such as roads , water infrastructure or electricity grids reach a critical state that is neither predicted nor designed by those that operate them . <p> In these networks , small errors or outages in one part of the network can quickly propagate to become a much larger failure .
