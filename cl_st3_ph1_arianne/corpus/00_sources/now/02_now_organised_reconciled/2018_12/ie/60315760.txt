<h> Minister deploys plan to tackle climate change impacts <p> A set of guidelines have been released in order to help local authorities prepare for the impacts of climate change . <p> They were launched today ( Monday , December 3 ) by the Minister of State for Community Development , Natural Resources and Digital Development , Sean Canney . <p> The guidelines come as the Government is developing a plan to make Ireland a leader in responding to climate change , according to a statement from Canney 's department . <p> The statement outlined that the local authorities , as the level of Government closest to communities , have a key role to play in this . <p> All local authorities will be required to adopt a local adaptation strategy in line with the published Local Authority Adaptation Strategy Development Guidelines . <p> This approach aims to ensure consistency and coherency across all local authorities . <p> Launching the guidelines today in Athlone , Co . " <p> He added that under the National Adaptation Framework , 12 national sectors are also preparing sectoral adaptation plans and noted that the local authorities will work with these sectors through the Climate Action Regional Offices to " ensure effective coordination and coherence on actions proposed " . <p> The deadline for the completion of both sectoral plans and local strategies is September 30 , 2019.
