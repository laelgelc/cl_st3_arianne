<h> World leaders must see ' urgency ' of climate crisis at COP24 <p> There was concern coming into COP24 that negotiators would n't be able to complete the task given to them and agree on a rulebook for achieving Paris Agreement targets to limit warming to 1.5 . <p> Following on from Bangkok , where negotiators did n't manage to finish , there was concern that this could be repeated in Katowice . <p> Another concern was that a rulebook would be created but it would have no teeth , a document for creating documents sake , with the more substantive points being decided down the line . <p> We need decisions and a rulebook to achieve Paris which has real substance .
