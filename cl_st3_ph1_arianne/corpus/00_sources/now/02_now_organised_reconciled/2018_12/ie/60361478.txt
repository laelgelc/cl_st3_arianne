Protests that were originally fired up in response to a new ' fuel tax ' that was announced by the French government , have taken on immense proportions and have even poured over the national borders into other European countries . But this Gilets Jaunes movement has been widely misunderstood and misinterpreted - one of the more famous examples being the American president 's interpretation of the movement as an " anti-environmental protection " . <p> Although the crowds have attracted a fair share of random protesters that range from grumpy citizens to full-on anarchists , it can be said that - in its heart - the initial sentiment does not trace its core @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change , but rather a group of people seeking a more economically just approach to mitigating the effects of climate change . In fact , groups of protesters have actually clamored for hydrogen alternatives to gasoline and diesel vehicles , as well as better maintenance of public transit in rural areas . <p> All things considered , this explosive phenomenon does touch upon a challenge that governments around the world are currently facing : what are the best methods to regulate and limit carbon emissions and promote greener alternatives ? <p> France is currently on a steep learning curve , pushing hard to reach the targets set at the Paris Climate Agreements it hosted and so fervently seeks to embody .
