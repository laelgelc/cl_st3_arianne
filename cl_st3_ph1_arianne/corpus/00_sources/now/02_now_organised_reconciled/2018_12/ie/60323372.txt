<p> As 2018 draws to a close , it is good to reflect on a difficult year for many farmers and see what lessons can be learned . <p> It is often said there are two certainties in life , death and taxes ; in farming the two certainties are weather volatility and price volatility . <p> Many farmers live in hope of the perfect year - that year when the weather is perfect , and prices are good , but the reality is that such years are few and far between . <p> In any case , the weather or price which is favourable for one farmer may be unfavourable for another farmer . For example , when feed prices go up , it suits the tillage farmer but does not suit the livestock farmer . <p> This ' perfect year ' mentality , perpetuating constant disappointment , is the root cause of the ' moaning farmer ' reputation our industry has acquired over the years .
