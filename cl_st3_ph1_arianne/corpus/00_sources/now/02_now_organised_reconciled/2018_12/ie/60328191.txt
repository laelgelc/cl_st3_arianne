<h> Climate challenge for vanua <p> NATIVE Lands Commission chairman Ratu Vananalagi Vesikula has challenged the vanua of Cakaudrove to have a positive attitude towards the environment . <p> In opening the Cakaudrove Provincial Council meeting in Somosomo Village on Taveuni yesterday , Ratu Vananalagi said the basic practice of not using plastic bags should be adopted in villages and at homes . <p> He said Fiji had championed climate change issues and despite the handing over to Poland , communities should remain committed to saving the environment . <p> Regarding the iTaukei culture , Ratu Vananalagi pleaded with traditional leaders to enforce with their children the dialect and cultural traditions of the iTaukei community . <p> Ratu Vananalagi said it was important to preserve the various dialects and iTaukei tradition . This , he said , should be done to ensure that the younger generation stay connected to their culture .
