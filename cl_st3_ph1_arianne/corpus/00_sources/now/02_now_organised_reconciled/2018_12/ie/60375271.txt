<h> Scientists Discover Secret to How Plants Branch to Locate Water <p> New research has revealed the secret as to how plants sense moisture in the soil and adapt the shape of their roots to optimize acquisition . The science could be used to breed crops that are more adaptive to climate change conditions such as low rainfall . <p> The discovery could be the key to ensuring food security in arid and dry regions . The study was conducted as part of a collaboration between the universities of Nottingham and Durham . <h> Roots only branch after contact with moisture <p> Roots are the part of the plant that helps them take in water and nutrients from the soil .
