<p> Ireland has been ranked as the worst-performing country in Europe on climate action for the second year . <p> Experts say that Ireland 's response to global warming , which is among the worst in the world , will leave it unable to " achieve either its EU 2020 or 2030 targets " . <p> The Climate Change Performance Index ( CCPI ) 2019 report states that the country 's " longstanding lack of implementation " of substantive measures to put it on a pathway to limiting warming to well below 2C has left it with a very low rating worldwide . <p> The report , which was launched at the UN Climate Conference in Poland on Monday , examined 56 countries and ranked them in order of their performance to tackle climate change . <p> It ranked Ireland 48th out of the 56 countries , up one place from last year . <p> Speaking from Poland , Clodagh Daly , of Friends of the Irish Environment , said : " This report highlights the gap Minister Richard Bruton ( Minister for Communications , Climate Action and Environment ) has to close between his new rhetoric on climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " His ministerial colleagues do n't seem to have got the memo .
