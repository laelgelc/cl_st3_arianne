<p> A ? 200,000 project has been launched to finance 20 short films that will provide a spectrum of perspectives on the UK in 2019 . <p> The initiative been inspired by a variety of topics including Brexit , climate change , diversity and #MeToo . <p> Named The Uncertain Kingdom , the initiative comes from John Jencks , director , writer and producer at The Electric Shadow Company , whose credits include Out Of Blue and The Hippopotamus . <p> The Levelling filmmaker Hope Dickson Leach and McQueen director Ian Bonhote are among the first four directors to be selected . Rising actor/filmmakers Ray Panthaki and Nathaniel Martello-White have also signed up . <p> Further established and rising names will be invited to participate in the project , while up to 10 new talent filmmakers will be selected via an open application , which will begin on Jan 14 and close on Feb 3 . All filmmakers must be resident in the UK but do not need to be British . <p> The ? 200,000 will be split equally between the 20 selected filmmakers ( ? 10,000 per project ) .
