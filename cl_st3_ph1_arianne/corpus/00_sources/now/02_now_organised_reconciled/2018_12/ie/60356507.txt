<h> Stakeholders meet to develop National Organic Policy <p> Repeka Nasiko <p> 17 December , 2018 , 2:35 pm <p> Organic farming is expected to boost agriculture and provide food and income security for all . Picture : JONACANI LALAKOBAU <p> THE Foundation for Rural Integrated Enterprises and Development ( FRIEND ) Fiji is holding a policy developing meeting with stakeholders from the tourism and agriculture sectors in support of the Ministry of Agriculture 's efforts to develop a National Organic Policy . <p> The two day stakeholder discussions will include presentations from the public and private sector focusing on the need to mitigate climate change , increase in trade opportunities and encourage healthy living to combat NCDs . <p> The two day meeting will begin tomorrow at the Friend Fiji headquarters in Tuvu , Lautoka . <p> *More on this story in tomorrow 's edition of The Fiji Times and on our e-edition .
