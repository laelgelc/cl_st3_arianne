<h> RAK firm to use fuel from waste in cement production <h> RAS AL KHAIMAH , 1 days ago <p> Ras Al Khaimah-based Pioneer Cement Industries has signed an agreement with Emirates RDF that will lead to the use of fuel derived from household waste in its production plant . <p> In a statement , the company , a subsidiary of Oman 's largest cement manufacturer Raysut Cement , said it will be one of the first cement plants in the region to use waste fuel contributing to the vision of sustainable development and environment of the UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment . <p> " The UAE government 's support to Emirates RDF to generate fuel from common household waste is a significant milestone in the country 's journey to become a global leader in sustainable waste management .
