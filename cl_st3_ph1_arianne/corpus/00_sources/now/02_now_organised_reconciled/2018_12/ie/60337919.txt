<h> Readers ' Blog : Time is running out on climate change <p> Monday , December 10 , 2018 - 12:00 AM <p> Thanks to humankind the course of destruction of our environment has been set . <p> Arrogance and a refusal to live within the laws of nature ; population control , balanced use of resources and respect for our living space , sees the human race -- and all of the other our allied non-human species -- up the proverbial creek without a paddle . <p> Ireland , as a country , is as ever behind the curve in facing this Armageddon . <p> Lacking a political class to lead and deal with the issues that seep into every aspect of our life on this island . <p> Tugging at the hem of the climate change issue is not political action . <p> As a society , we need to buy into measures , no matter how lifestyle changing and financially extracting , as a commitment towards saving our environment . <p> This is serious . As humans , we must look to ourselves to make changes in our way of living as a micro-action deposit in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ come to pass . The usual tropes of saving the planet apply : Adopt a minimalist lifestyle ; remove animal-based products from our diets ; do n't ' breed ' adopt instead ; reduce our travel footprints and recycle/upcycle . Many other actions can be added to this call to arms . <p> It may seem futile . But each action sends out a ripple that laps up on the boundary of another action thus creating an unstoppable wave . <p> But knowing there is a problem is half the battle , doing something is the other half . As a society , we may be supping in the last chance saloon . <p> Indeed , last call on planet Earth may already have been shouted . <p> But drawing on the wisdom from the saying ' the best way to predict your future is to create it ' means humankind holds one chance to create a sustainable future on this planet .
