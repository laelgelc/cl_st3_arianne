<p> The founder of organic veg box company Riverford has hit back at a new study that claims organic food has a higher climate impact than its conventional counterpart . <p> Guy Singh-Watson said that while he wholeheartedly agrees that climate change is the most critical challenge facing the planet , organic farming 's many other environmental benefits must not be ignored . <p> The study , conducted by Chalmers University of Technology in Sweden and published in the science journal Nature , arrives at its conclusion because organic farming currently requires more land to produce food .
