<h> Lack of organic food consumption <p> INSTANCES of non-communicable diseases are increasing in Fiji because of the shift of local consumption from organic to processed foods , says a nutritionist . <p> Nutritionist Asaeli Naika said this during his speech at the Terra Madre event held at the Fiji Museum in Suva on Thursday . <p> Mr Naika stood on behalf of the National Food and Nutritional Centre to bring to light the effects of industrial production of food items and globalisation on NCDs rate in the Fiji Islands . <p> " According to our surveys and assessments of our food supply and our population food intake , majority of the foods we eat daily are processed foods that are high in salt , sugar and fat and all other chemical additives used in their production , " he said . <p> He reiterated the fact that Fiji had the highest rate of diabetes in the Pacific with increasing rates of hypertension , cardiovascular diseases , stroke and cancers . <p> He claimed the country was becoming increasingly unhealthy and that it would soon become poor because of low productivity .
