<h> TOPICS <h> Luxembourg steps up as a leading financial centre for green finance <p> At first sight , Luxembourg does not seem to be a country with strong green credentials . It imports electricity , locally producing only 13 percent of what it consumes , and only 9.25 percent of that from renewable sources . Moreover , it sells high volumes of fuel , boosted by low tariffs compared to its neighbours . <p> This small nation might not gain high marks with environmentalists , but it would be a mistake to write off the Grand Duchy 's ecological commitment . Luxembourg is a world-class financial center and a leader in innovation , so combining finance with environmental protection comes naturally . <p> The COP21 Paris Agreement , signed at the end of 2015 , aims to limit global warming to below 2 degrees Celsius . Achieving this objective means mobilising vast financial resources .
