<p> New Delhi : Addressing the nation on the 51st edition of his radio show ' Mann Ki Baat ' , Prime Minister Narendra Modi said India secured a place of pride &amp; glory in the entire world . The highest United Nations Environment Award ' Champions of the Earth ' was conferred upon India and the country clinched a large number of medals in Asian Games and performed very well in Para Asian Games too . <p> Extending New Years ' greetings to countrymen , PM Modi spoke about the Prayagraj Kumbh Mela , which is all set to start from January 15 . <p> " Kumbh Mela is beginning from Jan 15 in Prayagraj . Kumbh is huge as well as divine . UNESCO listed Kumbh in Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity last year , " PM Modi said . <p> Remarking that the year 2018 was a phenomenal one for the countrymen , Modi said 2018 saw launching of world 's biggest health insurance scheme ' Ayushman Bharat ' . Also , Electricity reached each &amp; every village of the country this year . <p> The PM also hailed the first ever celebration of the 75th @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a flag hoisting ceremony at Delhi 's historic Red Fort . <p> PM Modi also informed that top world 's rated agencies have accepted that India is pulling its people out of poverty . <p> Mann Ki Baat is a monthly radio show hosted by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in which he addresses the people of the nation on All India Radio , DD National and DD News . <p> In its previous episodes , he has covered issues of social , national as well as international implication : education of the girl child , tackling exam stress , climate change and drug menace .
