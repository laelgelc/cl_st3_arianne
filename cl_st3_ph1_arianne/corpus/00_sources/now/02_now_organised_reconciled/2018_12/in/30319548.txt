<h> India for no ' undue burden ' on developing nations post 2020 <p> Katowice : The 24th session of the Conference of the Parties ( COP ) hosted by the United Nations Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCC ) opened in Katowice , Poland on Sunday , with the COP president urging nations to come together for making the Paris Agreement 2015 " fully functional " . In an exceptional move , four former UN climate talks presidents issued a statement on the same day , warning that " decisive action in the next two years will be crucial " . Around 35 delegates from India are expected to participate in the conference , in which global leaders will be finalizing guidelines for implementing the Paris Agreement adopted in 2016 . Union Environment minister Dr Harsh Vardhan will be leading the Indian delegation and is expected to arrive in Katowice on Monday . <p> According to the ministry , the key concern for India would be to ensure that no " undue burden " is shifted on developing countries in the post 2020 period . <p> Another priority of the Indian delegation would be voicing the need for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ annum by 2020 made by the developed countries .
