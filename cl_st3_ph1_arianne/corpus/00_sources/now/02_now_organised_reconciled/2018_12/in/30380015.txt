An investment tribunal can not require the provincial government to reverse the coal phaseout ; it can only award the company damages . Westmoreland is asking for $470 million . It is the federal government , rather than Alberta , that would have to pay compensation to Westmoreland if the company 's claim was successful . However , Ontario did agree to pay the award in a recent North American Free Trade Agreement case . <p> What is more concerning than any potential payout is that Westmoreland 's suit could hinder efforts to implement similar plans to combat climate change in other jurisdictions.
