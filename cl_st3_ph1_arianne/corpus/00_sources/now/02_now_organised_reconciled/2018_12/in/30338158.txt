<h> Share <p> The National Tiger Conservation Authority ( NTCA ) on Friday informed the Supreme Court that the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) has given no objection for translocation of cheetahs in India from Namibia in Africa . <p> NTCA told a bench comprising Justices Madan B Lokur and Deepak Gupta that IUCN , which provides inputs to Governments and institutions on biodiversity , climate change and sustainable development , has said it would co-operate with India in translocation of Cheetahs from Namibia . <p> However , the bench was apprised by an advocate appearing in the matter that as per report of an expert body , ground condition in India was not conducive for cheetahs . <p> NTCA referred to the apex court 's 2013 decision rendered in a wildlife case and said it does not prevent the authorities from taking steps in conformity with the law to relocated cheetahs from Africa to suitable sites in India . <p> " Why there is such a tearing hurry ? It is not that you have to bring cheetahs today itself , " the bench told NTCA 's counsel .
