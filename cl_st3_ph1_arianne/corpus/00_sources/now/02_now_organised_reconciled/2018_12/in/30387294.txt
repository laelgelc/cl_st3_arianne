<h> Nauni varsity to have BSI 's Himalayan regional circle <p> TNN Dec 27 , 2018 , 07:57 IST <p> Shimla : The Botanical Survey of India ( BSI ) , Western Himalayan high altitude regional circle , will come up at Dr Y S Parmar University of Horticulture and Forestry , Nauni in Solan district . A three-member team of the BSI and Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change visited the proposed site in the university . The team comprising of A K Pathak , director , BSI , B K Sinha , additional director , BSI , and Sandeep Chauhan , joint director , ministry of environment , forest and climate change , had visited the proposed site and inspected the infrastructure allotted by the university to set up the new circle . The team had also conducted a meeting with university 's vice-chancellor H C Sharma and registrar Rajesh Kumar . <p> Over the past one year , several discussions were held between the university and the environment ministry regarding the opening of the new circle after which the two institutions signed a memorandum of understanding for opening the Western Himalayan high altitude @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ getting an in-principle permission from the state government . <p> Recently , the university 's board of management approved around 6.6 acres for the establishment of the centre . The cost of developing the centre , along with the running cost , will be borne by the ministry of environment , forest and climate change . <p> The university and the ministry will also work towards developing a joint collaboration in research , teaching and extension in plant research with special emphasis on classical taxonomy , biodiversity , plant conservation and molecular taxonomy . Besides , the students of the university will be able to choose BSI scientists posted at the centre as research guides . <p> " Once established , the new BSI circle will be helpful in promoting research work on the rich flora and biodiversity of the Himalayan region . Moreover , the students of the university will be the biggest gainers because both institutions will work towards increasing collaborations in research and teaching . The researchers from both institutions will be able to utilize each other 's facilities , " said Sharma .
