<h> Government mulls blanket ban on certain plastic products <p> THIRUVANANTHAPURAM : In what could be the first step towards a complete ban on plastic in the state , the government has constituted a five-member technical expert committee to make a list of plastic products to be banned . <p> The committee has to list out all the consumer plastic products that can be banned completely irrespective of their thickness , including plastic-coated products , plastic-wrapped products , their plastic content , plastic percentage and their harmful impact . It will also submit a recommendation on how to bring in a law to effect a long-time regulation of plastic in the state and will suggest alternative materials that can replace plastic . The committee will submit a report to the government by February 15 . <p> The chief secretary had convened a meeting in November to discuss the issues related to imposition of a complete ban on one time use/throw away plastic items/carry bags .
