The talks had started on December 3 and were scheduled to end on December 14 , but discussions are still on . And climate scientists say that in the end , it does not matter as much as people think it does . <p> The primary aim of the climate change talks -- called COP 24 -- is to form a set of rules for reporting and transparency , and to persuade countries to increase their ambitions for reduction of emissions .
