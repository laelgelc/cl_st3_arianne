<h> Global warming-up in cold Katowice <p> It was like a fresher 's first day in an elite college . Everything was intimidating -- the more appropriately dressed crowd , the erudite discussions and the perfect gestures . Try as one might but the impostor syndrome keeps screaming ' you do n't belong here ' in your ears.It was also like the fifth or sixth day of the swimming class . You are reluctant to take that plunge but the coach pushes you in . But unlike the real waters where one reflexively learns to float , the first dive in the United Nations Conference of the Parties ( COP ) in Poland drowns you for a while . <p> Even before the critical climate change conference started , there were no illusions that it was going to be some kind of a vacation . The bus that started from Krakow went past a series of thermal power plants to reach Katowice , where nearly 200 countries came together to find ways to reduce global carbon imprints . <p> One glance was not enough to soak in the entire venue . As a dear friend Arthur Wyns rightly said , " Two weeks , 25,000 people , one venue could almost be a sociological experiment . " <p> And you ? You are a deer caught in the headlights . <p> The first few days are spent being lost , elbowed out , locating plenaries , meeting rooms and affordable food joints . But the several failed attempts lead you on . From delegates casually leaving behind your business cards to people walking up to you asking for your cards , you come a long way . <p> Most doors are literally closed for you and the constant challenge is to get out the inside information . Having an ego in the wild chase is prohibited ; you run behind ministers , top negotiators , the chief of World Health Organization ( WHO ) as they move gracefully go on minding their business . Sometimes , washrooms are the only places where you manage to take their exclusive bytes . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ would never know who the person walking next to you is . It starts with buying a classic black dress for the night , goes on to be unnerving because you do n't know which cutlery to use for what and ends with a sense of gratitude . <p> If it 's your first COP , you might want to be prepared for facing humiliations for being young and inexperienced .
