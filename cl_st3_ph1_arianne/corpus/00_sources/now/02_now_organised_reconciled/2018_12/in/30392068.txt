<p> When we think of circuses , we think of huge tents , jokers , trapeze performances and a lot of fun . The concept of a circus came to India in the year 1880 , when Vishnupant Chatre , after getting inspired by the Royal Italian Circus , came up with his own circus -- The Great Indian Circus . Not just in India , he also travelled across the United States . <p> Circuses have grown since , not just in India but across the world . And unfortunately , with them , grew the exploitation of animals . Tigers jumping through burning rings , lions walking in a line , elephants standing on stools , hippos playing with balls , dogs on bicycles and parrots balancing balls on their heads were considered ' natural ' activities in a circus , misleading children and adults alike . What happens to these animals behind the closed doors is anyone 's worst nightmare . These animals are taken away from their families , beaten , abused and tortured to train in order to perform unnatural tricks for human amusement , and are often left to suffer without adequate food @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Welfare board of India and FIAPO 's recommendation , the Central Zoo Authority ( CZA ) banned the use of wild animals for performances in circuses in the year 2017 . The ban came into effect after year-long inspections and monitoring by officials in which extreme cruelty to animals was reported . The ministry also deregistered several circuses in the country for training , exhibition and use of elephants for performances . <p> Very recently on November 28 , 2018 , the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MOEF&amp;CC ) issued a notice , proposing a ban on the use of all animals in circuses . This proposal is currently up for debate for a 30-day time period , after which the ban would be implemented in circuses across the country . An ecstatic moment for all animal lovers in the country , this notification will serve as the end of animal abuse in circuses once and for all . The Federation of Indian Animal Protection Organisations , along with several others , worked with the government for more than four years for this ban . Several @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Dr Shashi Tharoor and Prashant Bhushan also shared their support to FIAPO 's End Circus Suffering campaign . Thousands of individuals showed their support by signing the petition and more than a thousand school children wrote postcards to the ministry asking for a ban on use of animals in circuses . <p> " As a result of the campaign by the Federation of Indian Animal Protection Organisations to expose cruelty to animals in circuses , the government has proposed a complete ban on use of all animals in traveling entertainment . I welcome this move by the government in the wake of repeated violation of rules and abuse of animals by circuses . I proudly endorse entertainment which is cruelty free . Let us move towards a compassionate tomorrow , " said Raveena Tandon . <p> The government notification could not come at a better time -- just when India is hosting the largest circus in the world which does not use animals in their performances , Cirque Du Soleil . This circus is a model and should be taken as an example for others to evolve and include @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sword fighting , mallakhamb , Gatka , Huyen langlon , Kalaripayattu , Mardani khel , Silambam and Sqay . With major players like Ringling Bros. and Barnum &amp; Bailey Circus closing their doors in 2017 , it is a clear proof that using animals in circuses is not only unethical , cruel and unjust , but also a recipe for economic failure . Use of different art forms and a clever use of trained individuals to entertain the audience are some ways by which the dwindling future of circuses in India can be brought back to life , also generating employment to thousands of talented individuals who are masters of several art forms across the country . <p> The writer is the executive director of Federation of Indian Animal Protection Organisations ( FIAPO ) .
