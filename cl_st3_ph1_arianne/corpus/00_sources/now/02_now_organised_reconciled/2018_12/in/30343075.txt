Additionally , countries have failed to come to a consensus on adopting the scientific study on global climate commissioned at the 2015 conference . Halfway through the conference , the countries did not agree on draft negotiating texts for the Paris rule book . Key issues including climate finance remained unsettled by the end of the first week , informed the negotiator of a developing country . <p> " The technical meetings have now been extended by two days as the negotiators did not agree on many items in the agenda .
