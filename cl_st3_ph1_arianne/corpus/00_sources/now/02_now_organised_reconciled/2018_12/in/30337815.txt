<h> Rich , poor countries divided over funds to fight climate change <p> At the negotiations in Poland , there continues to be divergences between developed and developing countries over funds to fight climate change . <p> KATOWICE ( POLAND ) : The calls for ramping up efforts to reduce carbon dioxide pollution are getting louder and there is little disagreement that countries need to do more to slow down global warming . The problem , however , is one of resources . More ambitious climate action can not happen without increased availability of finance , particularly for poor developing countries . <p> However , climate finance flows from developed to developing countries has not been not quite satisfactory . <p> The Paris Agreement attempts to address the issue of ensuring predictable flows of finance through specific provisions that require developed countries to communicate an indicative level of support . At the negotiations in Poland , there continues to be divergences between developed and developing countries over this issue . With industrialised countries arguing that their laws and practices bars them from providing indicative figures beyond the budgetary period .
