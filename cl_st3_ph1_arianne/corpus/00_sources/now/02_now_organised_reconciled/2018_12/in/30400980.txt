The report was submitted online to the CBD Secretariat by Union Environment Minister Dr. Harsh Vardhan during the inaugural session of the 13th National Meeting of the State Biodiversity Boards ( SBBs ) , which was organised by the National Biodiversity Authority ( NBA ) in the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) , New Delhi . <p> The Minister also released the document '
