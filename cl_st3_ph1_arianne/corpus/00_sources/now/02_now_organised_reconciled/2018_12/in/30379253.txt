<h> IISER will school next gen on climate change <p> The work on the course started in February 2017 , but the content was rolled out in Uganda , Austria and New Delhi earlier this year <p> The subject has been included in 11 different disciplines ; modules will employ visual lessons and simulation games involving earth models <p> The Indian Institute of Science Education and Research ( IISER ) has devised a course on climate change to sensitise the younger generation regarding its impacts and prepare them to deal with future challenges . <p> The course embodies a global project titled ' Trans-disciplinary Research Oriented Pedagogy for Improving Climate Studies and Understanding ' , funded by International Science Council ( ISC ) , where IISER has been given the responsibility to understand the regional and cultural specifications across the globe . <p> " Climate change is going to impact the young generation on a large scale . Hence , awareness is the need of the hour and training them to impart better solution seems like a resilient move , " said coordinator of Climate Education Project of the International Science Council , Rahul Chopra . <p> Educational resources @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that are to be integrated into higher secondary and undergraduate college curriculum . The subject has been included in 11 different disciplines like geography , mathematics , earth sciences , humanities , environmental sciences , statistics , biological sciences , physics , chemistry , social sciences and economics . " The idea is not to make climate science as a standalone subject . At the same time , we have to ensure that it is embedded in every aspect of teaching , " he said . <p> There are modules created on the effects of climate change on glaciers , acidification of the oceans , epidemics and changing statistical data to simulate earth conditions with different carbon dioxide levels and other parameters . " To avoid a monotonous pattern in teaching methods , different pedagogical tools are created which include models/simulators , interactive visualisation , classroom or laboratory activity , video lectures , games , mobile apps and audio/audio podcasts , " Chopra told Mirror . <p> Chopra also a convener at National Resource Centre on Climate Change at IISER said that the teaching tools have also been localised to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hurricane in America may not appeal to an Indian student . However , melting glaciers in the Himalayas would be more contextual . Such sensitivities have been taken into consideration , " he added . <p> They have also included games related to climate change , which include the user creating their own earth and modifying the atmosphere by adding or deleting different layers , changing the axis of the planet and even shifting the position of the planet to understand how it impacts lives on earth . <p> The work on the course started in February last year , but the content was rolled out to the students and teachers earlier this year in Austria , New Delhi and Uganda and was shared at the Katowice climate change conference held in Poland earlier this month . <p> Shefali Shukla , a chemistry teacher who attended one of the workshops held in New Delhi in October , said , " The idea is unique to educate the children and make them sensitive to the environment .
