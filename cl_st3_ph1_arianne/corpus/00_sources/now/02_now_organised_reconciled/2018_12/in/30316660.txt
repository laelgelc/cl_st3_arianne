<p> Meant for tourism , picturesque Bengre is now a haven for illegal activities <h> Meant for tourism , picturesque Bengre is now a haven for illegal activities <p> TNN Dec 2 , 2018 , 04:05 IST <p> An aerial view of Bengre in Mangaluru <p> Mangaluru : Exactly 10 km to the west of NH-66 from Kulur Bridge across the Phalguni river , lies Bengre , a quaint location , where the river meets the sea . What should ideally have been a place bustling with tourist activities recently hogged the limelight for an infamous reason : a woman was gangraped by seven youth at the location . This is also the area which according to the local media is a hub of illegal activities such as drug peddling and consumption . <p> Realising Bengre 's huge potential for tourism , the government planned a golf course and a hanging bridge there many years back . However , these proposals are unlikely to see the light of day any time soon . The place has rather turned out to be a haven for criminals and anti-social elements , and an isolated area with communal overtones . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . There is a huge potential for tourism here , as several scenic spots dot the stretch . There are at least 50 acres in Bengre under the government , " said deputy conservator of forests V Karikalan . <p> One of the main reasons why this area is unlikely to be developed is the unwillingness of the locals . They fear tourism will hit their fishing business . Moreover , they are seeking housing from the government in the area . Development of the area for tourism would mean that they lose out on the chance of having houses . <p> " Around 400 applicants have sought housing in Bengre under various government schemes . The forest department has agreed to clear the vegetation here . If the place is developed for tourism , the local fishermen will be in trouble . On the other hand , we can not maintain the status quo in the area as illegal activities such as consumption of drugs is taking place , which is done under the cover of the vegetation and bushes , " said Mohan Karkera , president , Bengre @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , opined that the area should never be developed for tourism , as there is a threat of communal clashes breaking out any time . " Thota Bengre is an area which has a majority of Hindus , save for four Muslim families . A kilometre away is Kasaba Bengre , where there are only Muslims . Smallest of incidents such as a sheep being hit by a vehicle can take a communal turn , if the owner of the sheep and the driver of the vehicle are from different religions , " he said . " It is best to leave this place to the locals , so that they live in peace , " he added . <p> Sasikanth Senthil , deputy commissioner , however , said the entire stretch from Kulur to Thota Bengre was " meant " for tourism . He said various proposals for promotion of tourism were pending clearance from the ministry of environment , forest and climate change . Regarding the locals demand for housing , he said the area was fragile and there was no question of building houses .
