<h> Five Stories Of Good Governance That Hold Out Hope For India <p> In February 2018 , IndiaSpend launched India Governance Report ( IGR ) , a monthly newsletter that brings together stories of good governance and policy-making from across India . <p> Over the course of 10 months , IGR has highlighted successful initiatives on issues including health , gender parity , climate change , education , and Kerala 's post-flood rehabilitation . Here is a selection of five stories , each covering one of our focus areas , that made an impact in 2018 and hold out hope for India 's future . <p> As the country braces for the 2019 general elections , the impact of good governance , or the lack of it , will be under the spotlight .
