The good news is global growth is strong . World leaders re-affirmed their commitment to rules-based international order , while conceding to the US ' demand for urgent reform at the World Trade Organisation ( WTO ) . International trade and investment are important engines of growth , productivity , innovation , job creation and development , says the final communique .
