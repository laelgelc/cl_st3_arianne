He says the loss was a turning point for him , and he decided he would do something about it when he grew up . <p> In 2010 , at 32 , Oye founded Green Energy Biofuels ( GEB ) , a company that manufactures and distributes an innovative , smoke-free cook stove . It uses ethanol gel as fuel , a clean energy source made from biomass .
