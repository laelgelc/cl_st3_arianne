<h> India to update UN body of its climate actions , hinting to meet its national goals ahead of schedule <p> NEW DELHI : India will release its second Biennial Update Report ( BUR ) , containing its national greenhouse gas inventory , at the ongoing UN climate conference in Katowice , Poland stating that the country is well on track to meet its voluntary pre-2020 targets and eventually its 2021-2030 goals under the Paris Agreement . <p> The BUR will hint at meeting the country 's national climate goals ahead of schedule . <p> Maintaining an inventory of GHG ( sector-wise and overall emissions ) is key to any nation 's climate policy , showing whether its actions are actually in sync with its targets or not . <p> The report will be submitted to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) towards fulfilment of reporting obligation . As per the provisions , all countries are required to periodically provide information in the form of their national communication . <p> India had made a voluntary pledge in 2009 to reduce its emission intensity ( emission per unit of the GDP ) by 20-25 per @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agriculture ) . <p> The country later enhanced its climate action targets in 2015 outlining its goals for 2021-2030 , including reduction of the emission intensity of its GDP by 33-35 per cent from 2005 levels by 2030 . It also pledged to create additional ' carbon sink ' of 2.5 to 3 billion tonnes of Co2 equivalent through additional forest and tree cover and increase its share of non-fossil fuel in the total energy mix to about 40 per cent by 2030 . <p> Officials familiar with the basic points of the country 's second BUR noted that India would certainly meet its Paris Agreement targets ahead of the 2030 deadline . <p> India 's first BUR , submitted to the UNFCCC in January 2016 , show that a slew of policy measures to promote low carbon strategies have resulted in the decline of emission intensity by 12 per cent between 2005 and 2010 . <p> As per its first BUR , India emitted 2,136.84 million tonnes of CO2 equivalent GHG in 2010 .
