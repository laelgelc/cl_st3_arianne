<h> ' Goa needs agriculture policy with focus on irrigation ' <p> TNN Updated : Dec 22 , 2018 , 11:00 IST <p> Representative image <p> PANAJI : While projecting a disbursal of Rs 7,458 crore in loans in the state for 2019-20 , and with 14% of this potential credit earmarked for the agriculture sector , the National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development ( Nabard ) on Friday recommended that the state government formulate and implement an agriculture policy for Goa . <p> Nabard , in its state credit potential document , has also recommended that Goa must assess the effect of climate change on agriculture . <p> " Immediate formulation of state policy on agriculture , incorporating various aspects such as developing irrigation , micro-irrigation , supply of quality seeds and nutrients as per soil health , organic farming , infrastructure to prevent post-harvest losses , animal husbandry , fisheries , and other ancillary activities is required , " Nabard said . <p> Releasing the its State Focus Paper for the coming financial year , Nabard said it projected a demand for loans worth Rs 3,891 crore from the Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ credit potential in the state .
