<h> UN climate talks open in Poland with calls for urgent action to save the world <p> NEW DELHI : The 13-day UN climate conference kicked off in Katowice , Poland on Sunday with negotiators from across the globe assembling there to finalise a rulebook for implementation of the Paris Agreement post-2020 . <p> India is represented by a 17-member team , led by country 's environment minister Harsh Vardhan , at the 24th edition of the conference of parties ( COP24 ) which is being held in the backdrop devastating climate disasters around the globe , from California to Kerala , and Tonga to Japan . <p> Two recent global reports set the tone for the conference where leaders on its opening day were urged for rapid action to save the world .
