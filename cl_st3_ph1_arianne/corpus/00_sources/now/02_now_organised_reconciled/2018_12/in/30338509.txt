<h> ' Goa must prepare for rising seas ' <p> TNN Updated : Dec 8 , 2018 , 09:47 IST <p> Goa beach <p> PANAJI : Lead environmental economist with the World Bank , Susmita Dasgupta , on Friday said coastal states like Goa should immediately begin preparing for a rise in sea levels in light of scientific consensus that it may increase by one metre or more by the turn of the century . <p> " We do not know by when sea levels will rise and by how much . Location-specific elevation is needed to figure out which activities , assets and infrastructure will be affected so that one can plan accordingly , " she said . Pointing out that they were lessons to be learnt from the floods that paralysed Mumbai , Chennai and Kerala , Dasgupta warned that the same situation could happen " any day and anywhere " . <p> The economist also said that while the total rainfall may remain the same as the year before , showers are likely to be more intense for shorter periods of time .
