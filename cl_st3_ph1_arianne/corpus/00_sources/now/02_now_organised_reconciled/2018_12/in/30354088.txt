Water scarcity is the lack of sufficient available water resources to meet the demands of water usage within a region . It affects every continent and around 2.8 billion people across the world at least one month out of every year . More than 1.2 billion people lack access to clean drinking water . <p> Water scarcity can manifest itself as water stress , shortage or deficits and water crisis .
