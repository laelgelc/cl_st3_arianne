<h> So , organic food 's bad ? <p> Nature , it turns out , gives with one hand , takes with the other . That seems to be the case with organic food- -- while food grown without the use of fertilisers is good for health , it has a negative impact on the environment . So , if you want turn a blind eye to green issues , ignore the caveat and keep eating organic food . <p> The impact on the climate from organic farming is reportedly the same with biofuels such as ethanol made from wheat , sugarcane and corn , as well as biodiesel from palm oil , rape-seed and soya . <p> In a paper , ' Assessing the efficiency of changes in land use for mitigating climate change ' , published in Nature ( December 12 ) , researchers reported that organic food and biofuels have a much bigger climate impact than conventionally farmed food . According to them , this is even more so because of the greater areas of land required to produce these alternative foods .
