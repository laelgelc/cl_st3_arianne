<h> Canacona locals for more protection to Western Ghats <p> TNN Updated : Dec 13 , 2018 , 11:49 IST <p> The taluka and its villages are among the state 's biodiversity hotspots . <p> PANAJI : Residents of Loliem and Poinguinim have urged the ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) to refrain from excluding any part of the Canacona villages demarcated as ecologically sensitive areas ( ESA ) from the draft notification in order to ensure that water resources , biodiversity and traditional communities are protected . <p> On October 4 this year , the Union ministry notified a proposal to earmark just 56,825 sq km ( 37% ) of the Western Ghats area as ESA in six states and invited suggestions and criticism to prepare a final notification . <p> The Sahyadris , running north to south in this region , shoot a unique extension towards the Arabian Sea in the west , making the Canacona taluka and its villages one of the state 's richest biodiversity areas . <p> Dattaprasad Prabhu Gaonkar and Pradeep Mokhardkar , secretaries of the Loliem and Poinginkarancho Ekvott , said the conservation of Canacona 's ecosensitive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ living there for thousands of years . With land resources being scarce , the balance that has been struck between the communities ' needs and the Western Ghats ' conservation should not be disturbed by any large land use changes that may adversely impact the people , they added . <p> The plateaus and headlands of the taluka serve as large overhead tanks , recharging groundwater for drinking , agriculture and for proliferation of flora and fauna . As the Canacona taluka is a narrow strip of land , most of the rainwater flows to the sea , resulting in severe scarcity during the summers . <p> Destruction of plateaus by land use changes will wipe out biodiversity and ecology , the memorandum submitted by the citizens ' group to the Union ministry , stated .
