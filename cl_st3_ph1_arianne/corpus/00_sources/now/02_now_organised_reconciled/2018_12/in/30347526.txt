<h> Give more teeth to KSHRC : P Sathasivam <p> THIRUVANANTHAPURAM : Governor P Sathasivam has called for more power to the Kerala state human rights commission ( KSHRC ) and stressed the need for an audit on ' how seriously the official machinery has been implementing the commission 's directions ' . <p> " If there are instances of official neglect , we must solve them by strengthening the KSHRC by according more power to its directions , " he said while inaugurating the World Human Rights Day observance organized by KSHRC here on Monday . <p> Some interventions of the commission , the governor said , have gone long way in enlightening people about their human rights . " Most recently , the commission had visited Sabarimala and stressed the need to ensure facilities for pilgrims . It has also directed schools to refrain from vacation classes , " he said . <p> Pointing out several human rights challenges ' that continue to confront people in the country ' , Sathasivam said some of these cases have global implications . " The violation of an individual 's right to a clean environment is one instance , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ right to life , adequate food , water , health and the right to adequate housing , " he said . <p> The recent floods , he said , have shattered the common belief that Kerala would not be affected by climate change or similar issues . " Protection of environment is therefore vital for ensuring human rights , " he said . <p> The governor also said democracy could flourish only if people know their rights , privileges , duties and responsibilities . " The prime duty of those at the helm of legal affairs is to educate the public on the rights and privileges they are entitled to in our democratic setup .
