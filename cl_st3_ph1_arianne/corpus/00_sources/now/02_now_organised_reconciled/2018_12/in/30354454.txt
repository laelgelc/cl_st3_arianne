Read More <p> Thiruvananthapuram : The gates of Kerala University 's Kariavattom campus did n't close at 10pm on Wednesday like it does on all working days . The otherwise buzzling campus will go to sleep with students moving into their hostels by 9pm every day . But , it was music playing loud on the campus on Wednesday night . The campus stayed alive for 24 hours as part of an event titled ' campus@24 ' , organized by research students ' union . The event marked the launch of a campaign seeking extended working hours on the campus in line with campuses of central universities . <p> Activities began with an orientation session on civil services examination . Pro vice-chancellor of Kerala University P P Ajayakumar inaugurated the proceedings by 10am at the Botany Seminar Hall . Research Union vice-chairperson Lekshmi Prabha presided over the ceremony . <p> According to Ajayakumar , the campuses should always be open for students @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ open throughout . Students , especially those who are doing post-graduation and research , might have to access the library or lab at odd hours for academic purposes . So our universities should initiate changes in this regard , especially in case of residential campuses . " <p> The coaching classes were followed by an honouring ceremony for students who passed the NET exam last year . A book stall was also setup nearby the campus library , which had rare books and collector 's editions for sale . <p> As students , majority of them women , gathered on the courtyard near the campus library , puppet show began . Artist Sunil Pattimatam and his team from Vision Puppet Drama Team enthralled the students with the show themed on drug addiction . <p> The newly formed campus band ' Hymavati ' also performed their first ever gig at midnight . The team , which is comprised of PG students , MPhil and research scholars also gave the chance for students to exhibit their artistic side to their fellow batch mates . She stressed on the need for go-green drive for which she has been campaigning . She said it is the need of the hour to reduce our carbon footprints in day-to-day activities . " Man is no longer a social animal , but , a social media animal . Most of us are unaware about the fact that climate change and chemical pollution contribute to about 60-70% of environmental pollution . Unless we decrease consumption , it will be impossible to maintain the sustainability of our planet , " she said . Vasuki further recommended installation of solar panels to reduce pollution and thereby convert it into a green campus . <p> A play by Kerala Police on traffic awareness was held at Golden Jubilee Engineering Hall .
