<h> After DNA analysis of shells , Amt scientist claims rock painting is of an ostrich <p> Nagpur : Amravati-based professor and amateur archaeologist Dr VT Ingole has claimed that the painting of a bird he found in 2007 is that of an ostrich . When he and his team discovered the painting in a cave in Amravati , they had no idea that it could be that of the flightless bird.Ingole has correlated his discovery to the recent DNA analysis of some broken egg shells which reveals that it matches 92% with the African ostrich . The exact date when the shells were discovered in Central India in Nagpur , Jalgaon and Ujjain is not known . However , the study said the shells are 25,000 years old . <p> The scientist learnt about the DNA analysis completed by some Tamil Nadu scientists in 2017 only recently . Ingole then revisited his discovery and connected the dots . He has written a paper and will be publishing it soon in some well-known journals . <p> The 72-year-old Ingole claims he and his team found the ostrich painting in the Ambadevi rock shelter caves in Dharul near Morshi in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hirurkar , Manokar Khode , Shirishkumar Patil , and Dnyaneshwar Damahe . <p> " Our team was the first to discover more than 300 rock shelters having Palaeolithic to Prehistoric rock painting in Gavilgarh range of Satpura-Tapti valley , bordering Vidarbha and Madhya Pradesh . In one of the shelters , a bird painting resembling ostrich was also discovered . The broken shells too were found in the same Central India region , " said Ingole . The rock painting site dates back to upper/lower Palaeolithic age ( 15000-25,000BC ) . <p> TOI shared the photo of the painting with Giriraj Kumar , an ostrich expert and the secretary-general of Rock Art Society of India ( RASI ) in Agra . He feels it is not of an ostrich . " The bird in the painting has a very long neck but does not appear to be an ostrich . Secondly , the body is too short and its plumes are too long as compared to an ostrich . These birds have two toes while in the painting it is not clear , " he said . <p> On the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ I have mentioned it in my paper , " he said . <p> The figure in the painting has a long slender neck and legs , and a small body . It appears to be around 6 feet tall . <p> " What is important is that the painting and egg shells were found within the predicted area . From the dating of paintings it can be said that ostrich roamed in Vidarbha region during Palaeolithic era but got extinct thereafter .
