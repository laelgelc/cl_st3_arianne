" HE Mkapa <p> Who is TKP and what is it all about ? <p> The Kilimanjaro Project is an environmental and social impact initiative that aims to mitigate the effects of climate change in the Kilimanjaro region and across Tanzania . <p> Our vision is to plant millions of trees each year to impact climate change and to improve livelihoods of people living around the mountain . We aim to find solutions that make it more economically viable to keep a tree in the ground than to cut it down . Our focus is on environmental and economic sustainability . Taking this into account , The Kilimanjaro Project promotes not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ immediate action , not just in Kilimanjaro , but in Tanzania and around the world . <p> April - December 2018 marked the first of our annual tree planting season . We will reach 100,000 trees planted by the end of year , however , through our various campaigns we hope to raise funding for one million before the end of the year , to plant in 2019 . Our efforts are not just directed to tree planting ; we are actively monitoring the trees through our partnership with Greenstand and their tree tracking app , to ensure that they thrive and continue to grow . We engage with local communities and schools , encouraging them to also support tree growing efforts . <p> The trees planted are predominantly native species to Tanzania , providing sustenance and habitats for improved biodiversity .. Some of those planted in community areas , such as schools , are fruit trees ; providing additional benefit to the students and people in the area . <p> We are very excited about the progress made so far ; with plans to upscale in 2019 .
