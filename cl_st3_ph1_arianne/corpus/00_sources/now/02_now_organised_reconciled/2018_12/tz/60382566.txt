<h> EAC called to heavily finance climate change mitigation <h> Thursday December 27 2018 <p> Arusha . The East African Community ( EAC ) has been tasked to increase financing projects that would ameliorate the adverse impacts of climate change in the region . <p> The effects are manifested by erratic rain patterns leading to floods and chronic droughts and rising temperatures threatening ice on the highest mountain , Kilimanjaro , and sea level rise , among others . <p> A report by the Committee on Agriculture , Tourism and Natural Resources of the East African Legislative Assembly ( Eala ) said climate change posed a threat to economic development . <p> " It is affecting agricultural production , water availability , energy use , both social welfare and economic development , " said the report tabled during the just-ended Eala session here . <p> Part of the solution to the crisis , the report strongly suggested , lay on increased focus on climate financing through budget instruments in the EAC partner states . <p> EAC has lately been trying to support its partner states - Tanzania , Uganda , Kenya , Burundi , Rwanda and South Sudan - @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Fund . <p> GCF is a new global fund created in 2010 by 194 countries which are parties to the UN Climate Convention to support the efforts of developing countries to respond to the challenge of climate change . It is a financial mechanism which aims to deliver equal amounts of funding to mitigation and adaptation while being guided by the Convention 's principles and provisions . <p> When the Paris Agreement was reached in 2015 , the Fund was given an important role in serving the agreement and supporting the goal of keeping climate change well below 2 degrees Celsius . <p> The Adaptation Fund was established under the 1997 Kyoto Protocol of the UN Climate Convention . <p> It has since 2010 committed $532 million to climate adaptation and resilience activities , including supporting 80 concrete adaptation projects . <p> The Fund is financed in part by government and private donors , and also from a two percent share of proceeds of Certified Emission Reductions ( CERs ) issued under the Protocol 's Clean Development Mechanism projects .
