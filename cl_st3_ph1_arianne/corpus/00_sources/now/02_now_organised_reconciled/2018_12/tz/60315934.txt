<h> AfDB issues first ever SOFR-linked transaction in green bond market <p> The African Development Bank rated Aaa/AAA/AAA by Moody's/S&amp;P/Fitch ( all stable ) successfully priced a dual-tranche US$ 500 million 3-year and US$ 100 million 2-year SOFR-linked Green bond , becoming the first issuer to bring to the market a SOFR-linked Green Bond transaction . <p> The fixed rate tranche , oversubscribed within three hours of formal book opening with the support from high quality green investors , was priced at mid-swaps ( MS ) flat , in line with Initial Price Thoughts ( IPTs ) and providing a very limited new issue premium . <p> The SOFR-linked tranche , the result of a green reverse enquiry , priced at SOFR + 32 basis points ( bps ) and was placed almost entirely in the hands of green-dedicated investors . <p> Building on the Bank 's Ten-Year Strategy for 2013-2022 , whose overarching objective is to promote inclusive and sustainable growth by supporting African countries transition to " green growth " , the Bank established a Green Bond Framework in 2013 with the goal of supporting the Bank 's commitments towards climate change mitigation and adaptation . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Framework and 3rd in USD benchmark format , highlights the Bank 's consistent effort to fully play its role as one of the main actors of climate finance in relevant sectors such as renewable energy , energy efficiency , clean transportation , biosphere conservation and sustainable water &amp; wastewater management . <p> The transaction was announced on Monday 26 November 2018 at 3pm London time , with IPTs for the US$ 500 million 3-year tranche released at MS flat area . Strong interest from real money accounts and central banks started flowing into the books from the outset , with indications of interest exceeding US$ 400 million ( excluding Joint-Lead Managers ( JLM ) interest ) the next morning . <p> Books officially opened at 08:25am London time on Tuesday 27 November for both tranches , with price guidance of MS flat bps area for the fixed rate tranche and SOFR + 32 bps area for the floating rate tranche . <p> Books closed at 1:30pm , London time , in excess of US$ 590 million ( excluding JLM interest ) for the fixed rate tranche . The SOFR-linked tranche was set with US$ 100 million size at SOFR + 32 bps . <p> There was very strong support for the transaction from green dedicated portfolios but also from investors who integrate Environmental , Social and Governance ( ESG ) considerations in their investment strategies . The participation of such investors reflects The African Development Bank 's solid green framework and the high quality of its impact reporting . <p> The final order book included more than 40 investors across both tranches .
