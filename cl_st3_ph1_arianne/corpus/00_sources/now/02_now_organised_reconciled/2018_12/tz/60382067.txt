<h> Babati DC encourages people to venture into beekeeping enterprises <p> BABATI District Council has embarked in a campaign to promote beekeeping , an environmentally friendly venture which is being used to relieve people from poverty trap . <p> Noel Mosha , beekeeping officer in Babati District Council described beekeeping as an eco-friendly way of engaging in economic activities , where the community invests in nature conservation then waits for the fruits that come with it . <p> According to Mosha , beekeeping is a nature-based enterprise , which in recent years is gaining momentum in parts of Babati and its neighboring districts of Manyara Region . <p> " We 're encouraging more farmers to chip in and venture into the new enterprise , which is an alternative to agriculture -- the sector which perturbed by the changing of weather patterns as a result of climate change , " the beekeeping expert said . <p> He said that there are about 4,600 beekeepers from 87 groups of beekeepers , who rely on the enterprise to earn a living .
