<h> Peru builds thermal houses to keep highlands dwellers warm <p> Thirty families from a rural community more than 4,300 meters above sea level will have warm houses that will protect them from the freezing temperatures that each year cause deaths and diseases among children and older adults in this region of the southeastern Peruvian Andes . <p> Jos ?
