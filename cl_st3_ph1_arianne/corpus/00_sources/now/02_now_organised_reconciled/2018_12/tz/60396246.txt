<h> Factors affecting migration of bird species to Kitulo national park <p> CLIMATE change , cold weather and heavy downpour are among the factors contributing into some bird species from different oversees countries failing to migrate to the Kitulo national park in Makete , Njombe region . <p> Assistant Ecologist at the Kitulo national park , Frank Mapunda told journalists from the Tanzania Journalist Association of Tourism and Investment ( TAJATI ) that the changes in climate are caused by rising levels of the Indian Ocean and Lake Nyasa . <p> He said that the Kitulo plateau characterized with flowers is also known as Bustani ya Mungu ( Garden of God ) . He said the highland forest has been receiving variety bird species from Europe and some African countries such as Stork -- the long-necked large birds related to the herons and flamingos as well as the Bustard-White-bellied . <p> Mapunda said the Kitulo national park is among the few areas around the world with more than 40 species of plants and flowers , of which 31 species are only found in Tanzania . He said officials from the Tanzania National Park ( TANAPA ) have promised to provide them with other animal species to boost tourism at the botanical garden . <p> In May this year , the government approved 25 new wild animals to be introduced to the Kitulo national park <p> The then Deputy Minister for natural Resources and Tourism , Japhet Hasunga told the national Assembly that all the process including the legal requirements have been followed to release the wild animals . <p> The park 's Acting Tourism Officer , Jakson Shirima said the number of visitors has increased following introduction of animals at the botanical paradise . He said since the arrival of the animals at the park in October 12 this year , the number of tourists who visited the area in that specific period reached 103.
