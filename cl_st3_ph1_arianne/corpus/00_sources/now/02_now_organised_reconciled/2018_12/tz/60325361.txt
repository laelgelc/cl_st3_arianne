<h> COP24 for unalloyed commitment in combating climate change impact <p> The United Nations World Climate Conference ( COP24 ) is currently taking place in Katowice , Poland . During the days of the conference , participants are called on to create a " Rule Book " , a system of shared rules to put the commitments made in Paris in 2015 into action . <p> " It is high time the COP recognized the industrial food system 's responsibility in the creation of greenhouse gas emissions , " says Ursula Hudson , president of Slow Food Germany and a member of the Slow Food International Executive Committee . <p> She says it is widely acknowledged that agriculture , and land use in general , causes a quarter of total greenhouse gas emissions globally ( IPCC ) . <p> " In Europe , if we also include the energy consumed for the cultivation and production of food , transport , refrigeration and preparation , the figure rises to 40 per cent , " she further notes . <p> According to the Slow Food Germany president , the three largest meat producers in the world generate more emissions than France @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ oil companies . <p> If these companies were a country , she says , they would be the seventh largest producer of greenhouse gas emissions in the world . <p> Ursula recommends that meat consumption be significantly reduced , as two-thirds of agricultural emissions are due to livestock farming , adding that marine ecosystems are capable of sequestering large quantities of CO2 and therefore must be protected . <p> " Time has come for the international community to take these data into account . We need courageous politicians who are not afraid to sanction industries that do not meet climate targets , " she says . <p> All of this must be done at a global level and the COP24 is the world 's best shot .
