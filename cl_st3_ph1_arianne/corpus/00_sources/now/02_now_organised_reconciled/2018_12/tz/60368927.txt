<h> Katavi villages set for tree planting drive <p> ILALANGURU village in Mpibwe district is likely to face water blues despite being endowed with natural resources including forest reserves , if villagers will not be trained on environmental conservation , villagers says . <p> Polycarp Kandege , a farmer in the village , said this when briefing journalists who toured the region recently with the help of non-governmental organizations . <p> Most villagers in the district are not aware of what is involved in environmental conservation and benefits it has for them . <p> Deforestation in the village is rampant , so there is need to create awareness so that people understand the benefits of conservation , Kandege says . <p> The project is implemented by ActionAid Tanzania , Haki Ardhi and the Lawyers ' Environmental Action Team ( LEAT ) in collaboration with Nsimbo , Mpimbwe and Sumbawanga district council in Katavi region . <p> People invade the forest reserves for various activities such as agriculture , timber production and firewood , he said . <p> Women and girls are already affected by climate change than men because once the environmental situation is not good , women walk @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water sources dry up and there is a food shortage caused by drought . <p> Currently , the village wants to ensure that through their village meetings they are brought on the tree planting campaign since many trees have been destroyed as farmers move to open more farms . <p> Vilolo is one area that depends on farming activities , but also search firewood and timber for building and sale of wood for construction . <p> Ilalanguru village is also listed by district water authorities as likely to experience increasing water stress at least by 2022 given the rapid depletion of natural resources .
