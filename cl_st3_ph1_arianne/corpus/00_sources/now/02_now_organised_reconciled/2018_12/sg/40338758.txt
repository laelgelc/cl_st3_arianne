<p> BUENOS AIRES - Finance ministers from the world 's 20 largest economies were concerned about the state of financial markets this year , and some of their work at the Group of 20 ( G-20 ) summit over the weekend focused on preparing for the eventuality of a downturn , said Finance Minister Heng Swee Keat . <p> " We have had a prolonged period of economic upturn . The question is , when is the downturn coming ? And how do we prepare for that ? " said Mr Heng in an interview with Singapore media on Saturday ( Dec 1 ) after the Argentina summit , which he attended .
