<h> We want it in our wardrobe ASAP <p> Photos : Burberry / David Sims <p> Since it was first announced in July , we have been eagerly anticipating the collaboration between Burberry and Vivienne Westwood . Last week , the two fashion powerhouses revealed their join campaign and limited edition collection . <p> It 's a rather unexpected mash-up , but a perfect first collaboration for Burberry 's newly appointed Chief Creative Officer Riccardo Tisci . Tisci 's overhaul of the 162-year-old British brand saw a modernising rebirth -- changing the iconic Burberry logo , and launching a 24-hour Instagram-only pop-up shop to debut his first Burberry collection . <p> Westwood was an obvious choice for his first collaboration . " Vivienne Westwood was one of the first designers who made me dream of becoming a designer myself , " Tisci says . <p> The new joint capsule collection combines the Westwood 's punk aesthetic with Burberry 's vintage prints . The result is exquisite pieces for both men and women -- shirts with an oversized collar , lace-up platform shoes , berets , even a mini kilt , and more , all sporting the iconic Burberry check @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Westwood campaign features an eclectic cast of many famous faces in the arts industry , such as Kate Moss , Sistren , Leonard Emmanuel , as well as Westwood and her partner Andreas Kronthaler . <p> A closer look at the lace-up platform shoes in full colour . Guests wore them to the ' Vivienne Westwood &amp; Burberry ' launch party . Celebrities the likes of Matt Smith , Miley Cyrus , Winnie Harlow , Lily Collins , Ezra Miller , and Halsey also attended the party to celebrate the collaboration . <p> Burberry and Westwood also teamed up to support UK-based nonprofit organization Cool Earth , which works with rainforest communities to halt deforestation and climate change . To raise awareness for Cool Earth , they designed the oversized t-shirt featuring the slogan ( handwritten by Westwood herself ) : " Cool Earth has a plan to save the rainforest . " <p> In addition , Westwood will also customise four exclusive items from the collection to be auctioned , with proceeds going to Cool Earth .
