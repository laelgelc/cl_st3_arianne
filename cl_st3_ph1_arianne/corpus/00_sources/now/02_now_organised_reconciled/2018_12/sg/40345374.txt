<p> BY MOST accounts , impact investing has caught the attention of investors , particularly young and wealthy millennials who wish to do well and do good with their funds . Typically , the avenue would be through investments into a private company or organisation with a social and/or environmental mission , or through a private equity portfolio . These investments , however , are typically pitched at high net worth or accredited investors . <p> Now , a new segment of liquid funds is emerging which represents the " second chapter " of impact investments , called " positive impact " equities . In contrast to philanthropy , impact investments are made with an expectation of return . <p> Says Victoria Leggett , head of responsible investment of the asset management arm of Union Bancaire Privee ( UBP ) : " We believe very strongly in impact as a way to invest money for retail and large institutions . In order for that shift to happen , the area of impact investing has to broaden and become accessible .
