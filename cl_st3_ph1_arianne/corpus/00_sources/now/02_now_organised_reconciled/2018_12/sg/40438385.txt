The fashion conglomerate aims to economize on water resources by focusing on product innovation and improving efficiency at its fabric mills and laundries , and hopes that the initiative will help affected communities achieve better access to clean water and sanitation. <h> 5/5UN Charter <p> This month also saw 43 fashion businesses , including Burberry , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fighting climate change with the signing of a new ' Fashion Industry Charter for Climate Action ' , a UN Climate Change initiative .
