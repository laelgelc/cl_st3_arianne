Many Cubans used vacation days so they could attend the screenings . <p> Actors and directors from around the world wandered the colorful streets of Havana , talking with locals . <p> The festival succeeded thanks to its founder Alfredo Guevara , Cuban leader Fidel Castro and Colombian writer Gabriel Garcia Marquez , who was president of the new Latin American film foundation . <p> - Filmgoers still flock to festival - <p> But then the economic crisis of the 1990s , sparked by the collapse of the Soviet Union , took its toll . <p> Small neighborhood movie theaters simply vanished . Now , there are only 21 left , and most of them are outdated , lacking air conditioning and digital technology .
