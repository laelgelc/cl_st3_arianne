<h> ' Going green ' is good business says private sector at UN 's COP24 climate conference <h> On its second week , a key message in the United Nations COP24 was that businesses across the world should no longer be viewed solely as greenhouse gas emissions culprits , but more as indispensable partners for climate action . <p> In 2016 , 190 of the Fortune 500 listed companies of US corporations captured a total of US$3.7 billion in savings thanks to their emissions reduction measures , says the Global Compact .
