<h> Philip Colbert updates pop art for the digital age in new show <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Artist and designer Philip Colbert , whose surrealist clothes have been worn by Lady Gaga and Rita Ora , brings pop art back to British painting with a new show that comments on consumerist culture , climate change and our appetite for social media . <p> Scottish born Colbert , who has worked across music , fashion and design , says his large-scale oil paintings interweave art history references with Internet and social media images , such as emojis and like buttons , in bright cartoon-like collages featuring his lobster alter-ego . <p> " People are consuming art a lot through phones and the computer so I like the idea that those elements are reflected in my paintings , " he said at his at his studio in London , where his latest show is running at the Saatchi Gallery . <p> His preoccupation with digital media has led Colbert to create a virtual reality world to explore the boundaries of art . <p> " It 's a language you can create and it can be free from the realities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and that 's the beauty of it , the potential freedom .
