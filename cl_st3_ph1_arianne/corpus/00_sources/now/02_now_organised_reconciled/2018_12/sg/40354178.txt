<p> PARIS * The single most effective weapon in the fight against climate change is the tax code - imposing costs on those who emit greenhouse gases , economists say . <p> But as French President Emmanuel Macron learnt over the past three weeks , implementing such taxes can be politically explosive . Yesterday , France delayed for six months a plan to raise already steep taxes on diesel fuel and petrol . <p> Mr Macron argued that the taxes were needed to curb climate change by weaning motorists off petroleum products , but violent demonstrations in the streets of Paris and other French cities forced him to backtrack - at least for now . <p> " No tax is worth putting in danger the unity of the nation , " said Prime Minister Edouard Philippe , who was trotted out to announce the concession . <p> It was a setback for the French President , who has been trying to carry the torch of climate action in the wake of the Paris accords of December 2015 . <p> Mr Macron is hardly alone in his frustration .
