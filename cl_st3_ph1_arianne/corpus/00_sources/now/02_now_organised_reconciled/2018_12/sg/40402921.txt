<h> Anil Agarwal Dialogue 2019 <h> December 20 , 2018 <p> A congress of journalists , environmentalists and experts . A state-of-the-art symposium to discuss , debate and demystify the year 's key issues of environment and development . <p> From climate change , extreme weather events , air pollution and e-mobility to the agrarian distress , organics and the anatomy of droughts . From environmental governance , human-wildlife conflicts and sanitation and Swachh Bharat to renewable energy , plastics and fate of the Ganga . <p> We invite journalists in India writing on environment and development to join us over these three days at our beautiful residential campus at Nimli in the foothills of the Aravallis . CSE would be supporting the travel and stay of selected delegates . <p> Interested journalists can apply here . The last date for receiving applications is January 10 , 2019 . <p> CSE 's decision on shortlisting the participants for this conclave would be final . Only the shortlisted participants will receive a response from CSE .
