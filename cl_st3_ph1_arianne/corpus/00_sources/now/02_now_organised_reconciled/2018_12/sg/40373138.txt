As cameras swarmed around them , some of the protesters explained the extraction of coal , oil and natural gas affects their communities . <p> The U.S. event , titled " U.S. Innovative Technologies Spur Economic Dynamism , " took place on the sidelines of the ongoing U.N . meeting in Katowice , Poland . After several minutes , the activists left the room chanting " Shame on you . " Their actions mirrored a similar protest during a U.S.-hosted panel at last year 's U.N . climate talks in Bonn , Germany . <p> Wells Griffith , a Trump administration adviser at the Department of Energy , said after the interruption that the United States would continue extracting fossil fuels , including through hydraulic fracking .
