<p> PARIS ( AFP ) - A petition launched by French environmental groups calling for legal action against the state for failing to act to curb climate change has received unprecedented public support after weeks of fuel-price protests . <p> By Friday ( Dec 28 ) , the project , launched by four climate NGOs , had gained just under two million signatures a mere 10 days after going live . <p> That already makes it the most popular online petition in France 's history , easily eclipsing the 1.37 million signatories who resisted changes to the nation 's labour laws in 2016 . <p> It also dwarfs the 1.17 million supporters of a demand by one of France 's " yellow vest " protesters calling for lower petrol and diesel prices at the pump , launched in May . <p> " It 's not just a petition , but also a call for legal action , " said Jean-Francois Julliard , CEO of Greenpeace France , one of the groups behind the initiative . <p> The " yellow vest " movement began this year as a protest
