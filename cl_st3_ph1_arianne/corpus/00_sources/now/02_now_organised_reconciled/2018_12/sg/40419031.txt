<p> As a layman , I think NParks has already done all that is humanly possible to check on the health of the approximately two million trees in our nation . More checks will definitely increase costs and drain our manpower demands . <p> As we are living in a garden city , we should anticipate act-of-God accidents like trees falling occasionally due to unexpected bad weather . These are even more likely with the current climate change that is being experienced globally . <p> Instead , as individuals , we should take precautions and avoid taking shelter under big trees during extreme weather . <p> I hope fewer cases of injuries or deaths will then occur in tree-falling incidents . <p> Chan Chee Beng <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on December 25 , 2018 , with the headline ' Falling tree incidents can be expected in garden city ' .
