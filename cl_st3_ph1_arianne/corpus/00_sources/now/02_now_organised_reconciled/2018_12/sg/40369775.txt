<h> Key issue is reassuring developing nations on cost of switching to low-carbon economy <p> KATOWICE ( Poland ) * Half-way through talks to breathe life into the Paris climate deal , negotiators haggled over how to share the cost of curbing global warming and struggled to bridge deep political divides . <p> The two weeks of talks , which began at the start of last week , are billed as the most important United Nations conference since the Paris 2015 agreement on climate change . <p> The challenge is to meet a year-end deadline to agree on a rule book to limit global warming , when the unity that underpinned the Paris talks has fragmented . US President Donald Trump has repeated his call to scrap the Paris climate pact . <p> Negotiators had on Saturday aimed to have a simplified draft for the high-level ministerial debate starting today . <p> " We still have a lot to do , " Mr Michal Kurtyka , the Polish president of the UN talks , told a news conference .
