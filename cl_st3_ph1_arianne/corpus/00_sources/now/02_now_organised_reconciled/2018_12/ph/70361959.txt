<h> Pangasinenses overcome disaster-filled 2018 <p> MALASIQUI , Pangasinan -- Pangasinenses showed resilience after facing several weather disturbances that caused severe flooding resulting in damage to the agricultural sector and increased cases of leptospirosis and dengue . <p> Months after the summer annual celebration of Pistay Dayat ( Feast of the Sea ) in Pangasinan , weather disturbances started to affect the province one after the other . <p> On July 23 , the Sangguniang Panlalawigan of Pangasinan passed a resolution declaring the entire province under a state of calamity due to flooding brought about by the intensified southwest monsoon and onslaught of Tropical Depression Josie . <p> Based on the report of the Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office ( PDRRMO ) , 381 out of the total 1,364 barangays in the province were flooded , with 1,820 families temporarily evacuated from their homes due to severe flooding . <p> In September , another weather disturbance hit the country , including the province of Pangasinan . Typhoon Ompong left the province with PHP1.65 billion in damage to agriculture and PHP52 million to infrastructures , the PDRRMO said . <p> ' Ompong ' affected 40,743 hectares of rice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and 664 hectares of assorted vegetable plantation in 20 localities of the province . <p> " Although , our province was not directly hit by the typhoon , the ' palays ' ( rice stalks ) are in maturity period , while some are flowering so damages in our agriculture are high , " Provincial Agriculturist Dalisay Moya said . <p> She noted that agricultural losses caused by the typhoon were about 7 percent of the total expected production of the province for this year . <p> " It may seem small in percentage , but this would amount to days of rice or vegetable consumption of Pangasinenses , " Moya said . <p> Agricultural interventions <p> The Department of Agriculture ( DA ) provided 32,000 bags of hybrid-certified palay seeds worth PHP81 million for the 32,000 hectares of disaster-affected farm lands in the province , as well as 100 bags of corn and 50 fishing boats . <p> It also allotted a PHP50-million fund through its Agricultural Credit Policy Council ( ACPC ) as loan-aid to disaster-stricken farmers and fishermen in Pangasinan . <p> DA Secretary Emmanuel Pi ? @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ACPC to make the loaning process and requirements for Production Loan Easy Access ( PLEA ) and Survival Recovery ( SURE ) Assistance Program easy for the beneficiaries . <p> " They ( farmers and fishermen ) can loan from PHP5,000 to PHP25,000 through SURE without collateral and without interest and payable in three years , while in PLEA , they could loan PHP25,000 to PHP50,000 without collateral with only 6-percent interest and 1-percent service fee , " he said . <p> The Philippine Crop Insurance Corp .
