<h> ABS-CBN News <p> A climate change advocate raised concerns on the refusal of several world powers to recognize the findings of a landmark report on the impact of global warming . <p> According to the report , the world is now completely off track to limit the rise in temperature by 1.5 degrees because it is now heading towards a 3-degree increase . <p> Denise Fontanilla of the Institute for Climate and Sustainable Cities said the ongoing climate talks in Poland failed to adopt the key study because 4 major oil and gas producers -- the United States , Saudi Arabia , Kuwait and Russia -- do n't want to recognize the report . <p> She also stressed the report 's significance in helping vulnerable countries curb the effects of global warming . <p> Fontanilla , however , said there is still hope that the world can work together to limit global warming . -- Top Story , ANC , December 9 , 2018
