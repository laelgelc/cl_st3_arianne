<h> 2018 PH 's banner year as ' protector ' of Coral Triangle -- DENR <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) has marked 2018 as a banner year for the Philippines as a " protector " of the Coral Triangle -- a region known as the center of marine biodiversity in the world . <p> As a member of a six-nation partnership , DENR said the Philippines has made significant headway in effectively managing its marine protected areas ( MPAs ) and fisheries , especially in the Coral Triangle region . <p> ( MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> " The year 2018 was a good one for the Philippines in terms of our efforts and achievements as a member of the Coral Triangle Initiative on Coral Reefs , Fisheries and Food Security ( CTI-CFF ) , " said Crisanta Marlene Rodriguez , director of the DENR-Biodiversity Management Bureau ( BMB ) . <p> CTI-CFF is a multilateral partnership of six countries -- Indonesia , Malaysia , Papua New Guinea , Solomon Islands , Timor-Leste , and the Philippines -- working together to sustain extraordinary marine and coastal resources in the region known as the " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ such as food security and climate change . <p> According to Rodriguez , the country has delivered on its commitment to create more MPAs with the enactment of Republic Act 11038 , or the Expanded National Integrated Protected Areas System ( E-NIPAS ) Act , earlier this year . <p> The E-NIPAS Law has brought to 43 the total number of nationally-managed MPAs and 1,816 locally managed MPAs , all covering 1.4 percent of the country 's total sea area . MPAs are areas in seas and oceans where human activity is regulated for conservation and protection of the natural resources within them . <p> " One of the milestones of the Philippines is the legislation of additional 10 ( nationally managed ) MPAs under the ( E-NIPAS Act ) , " Rodriguez said . <p> Rodriguez also reported the full implementation of the Coastal and Marine Ecosystems Management Program , which covered activities such as habitat assessment for the NIPAS MPAs , MPA networking technical assistance for local government units , support to 25 MPA networks and some 200 biodiversity-friendly enterprises , and inclusion of ocean acidification baseline study @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ assessment , mapping , impact monitoring , resiliency studies and updating of coral reef baseline data were conducted in the West Philippine Sea and the Philippine Rise , formerly known as Benham Rise . <p> Other accomplishments of the country in MPA management include the establishment of a two million hectare MPA in northeast Palawan , approval of MPA management plan for Davao Gulf , and creation of five-province MPA and MPA network within the Verde Island Passage , which has been dubbed as the " center of the center of marine shorefish biodiversity in the world . " The five provinces are Batangas , Marinduque , Romblon , Oriental Mindoro , and Occidental Mindoro . <p> Rodriguez noted that effective MPA management is one of the five targets under the country 's national plan of action ( NPOA ) for the CTI-CFF . " Among the NPOA goals , that of the MPA truly mirrors how much we have achieved in terms of implementation and regional cooperation , " Rodriguez pointed out . <p> Aside from MPA management , Rodriguez said the country was also successful in adopting the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ goal set by the Philippines in its NPOA . <p> She said the country had intensified its effort to combat illegal fishing nationwide to improve food security and livelihood .
