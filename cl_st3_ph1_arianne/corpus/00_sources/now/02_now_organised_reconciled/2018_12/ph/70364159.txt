Teddy Brawner Baguilat . <p> Baguilat cited the need for the government to pursue sustainable development , recognize the rights of indigenous cultural communities/indigenous peoples ( ICCs/IPs ) , promote biodiversity , and step up climate change mitigation and adaptation efforts . <p> " To give life to the constitutional mandate and the international covenants , and to integrate and further strengthen the Indigenous People 's Right Act ( IPRA ) of 1997 , National Integrated Protected Area System ( NIPAS ) Act of 1992 , and the Wildlife Resources Conservation and Protection Act , it is necessary to pass the bill to protect the indigenous community conserved areas ( ICCA ) , " he said . <p> Baguilat said the measure provides for a system of recognition , registration , protection and promotion of covered lands , providing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " A law specifically for ICCAs would provide the necessary government mandate , especially the annual budget and people needed to manage the ICCAs , " he said . <p> Under the proposed Indigenous People 's and Local Communities Conserved areas and Territories Act , ICCAs are declared as no-go zone for mining and other destructive forms of natural resource , exploration , development , and utilization . <p> It provides that the place , names and designation of ICCAs in the native dialect of the ICCs/IPs shall be accorded respect . <p> The measure provides that it shall be unlawful for any person to commit the following acts within an ICCA : unauthorized and/or unlawful intrusion upon , or use of any portion of , the ICCA ;
