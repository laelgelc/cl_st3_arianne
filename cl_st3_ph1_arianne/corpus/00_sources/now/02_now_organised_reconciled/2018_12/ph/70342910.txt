<p> LOS BANOS , Laguna , Dec. 18 -- The issuance of a Joint Statement on Biodiversity Conservation at the recently concluded UN Conference on Biodiversity ( Fourteenth Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity ) by the 10 ASEAN Member States further pushes the region towards a more harmonized and coordinated effort to fight biodiversity loss , expressed Dr. Theresa Mundita S. Lim , Executive Director of the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity ( ACB ) . <p> " The statement from the 10 ASEAN Member States signifies the unity of the region in curbing the threats to life and natural resources found in biodiversity . It is a clear commitment from the ASEAN Member States in taking the steps towards biodiversity mainstreaming , " Dr. Lim emphasized . <p> " Mainstreaming , " according to the Conference on Biological Diversity ( CBD ) , is the integration of the conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity in both sectoral ( agriculture , health , energy , tourism , etc. ) and cross-sectoral plans ( sustainable development , poverty reduction , and climate change adaptation/mitigation ) . According to a joint publication of the International Union for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " mainstreaming " entails changes in development models , strategies and paradigms . <p> Through the joint statement , the ASEAN Member States agreed to pursue a comprehensive agenda for transformational change to address the drivers of biodiversity loss ; accelerate actions to mainstream biodiversity ; and enhance regional cooperation and partnerships to support national efforts . <p> The ASEAN region boasts of three out of the 17 mega-diverse countries in terms of biodiversity , namely , Indonesia , Malaysia , and the Philippines . It is also home to almost 20 per cent of global plants and animals , 34 per cent of coral reefs , 35 per cent of mangrove forests , and over 200 million hectares of forest cover . <p> According to the ASEAN Joint Statement , the region 's rich terrestrial and marine biodiversity and its resources and ecosystem functions and services are challenged by climate change , rapid urbanization , and continuing habitat loss from agricultural expansion , industrial activities , and wildlife trafficking . <p> The ASEAN Member States reiterated the region 's commitment to implement and achieve the CBD 's Strategic Plan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Target 11 , on increasing well-conserved and connected terrestrial and marine protected areas ; and the United Nations ' 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and its goals , particularly on Sustainable Development Goals 14 and 15 on protecting the oceans and the environment . <p> Dr. Lim , who also served as Director of the Philippines Department of Environment and Natural Resources ' Biodiversity Management Bureau , Chair of the ASEAN Wildlife Enforcement Network and Chair of the Subsidiary Body for Scientific and Technological Advice of the CBD , has openly expressed that ACB 's main thrust is mainstreaming biodiversity . This year , the Centre has conducted a series of mainstreaming workshops that touch-based with the health , agriculture , climate change , tourism , and banking and finance sectors . The activities identified points of cooperation for the integration of biodiversity conservation in the said sectors .
