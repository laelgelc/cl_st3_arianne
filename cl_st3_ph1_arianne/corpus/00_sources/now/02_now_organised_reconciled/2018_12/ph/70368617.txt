<h> The people of Mbomo tell their tales <p> CONGO Tales , a new book published recently by Prestel , began as a call to action to save the Odzala-Kokoua National Park in the heart of the Congo Basin , which is the second-largest tropical forest in the world after the Amazon , from the threats of climate change . <p> It soon became a book about the stories of the people who live there . <p> A team including Pieter Henket , a Dutch photographer ; Eva Vonk , a Dutch producer ; Steve Regis " Kovo " N'Sond ? , a Congolese artist and philosopher ; his brother Wilfried N'Sond ? , a Congolese writer and musician ; and a group of conservationists and researchers spent five years in the basin . There , they collected and translated the tales of the people of the Mbomo region .
