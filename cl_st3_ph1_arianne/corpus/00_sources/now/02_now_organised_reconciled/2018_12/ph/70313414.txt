<h> Standing united at 73 <p> The United Nations recently celebrated its 73rd anniversary with a cultural program and reception at the SM Aura Premier Samsung Hall . <p> A joint partnership between the United Nations Philippines , in collaboration with the Department of Foreign Affairs and SM , the event highlighted the strong cooperation between the government and the world body in the delivery of the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , as well as the contribution of " changemakers " in achieving these goals . <p> UN Philippines Resident Coordinator Ola Almgren and Foreign Affairs Undersecretary for Policy Enrique Manalo led the festivities , which gathered together top government officials , UN agency heads and members of the diplomatic corps . <p> Almgren paid tribute to the contributions of the Philippines to the United Nations , adding he looked forward to future partnerships .
