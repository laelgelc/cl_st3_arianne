<h> Climate talks shift to nitty-gritty details of Paris accord <p> Negotiators gathered for the UN climate talks in Poland are getting down to the nitty-gritty part of the two-week meeting . <p> Visitors to the U.N . climate conference watch a speech by Arnold Schwarzenegger , in Katowice , Poland , Monday , Dec. 3 , 2018 . The COP24 UN Climate Change Conference is taking place in Katowice , Poland . Negotiators from around the world are meeting for talks on curbing climate change . ( AP Photo/Czarek Sokolowski ) <p> Diplomats from almost 200 countries began holding a series of technical talks Tuesday aimed at finalizing the rules that define the 2015 Paris accord on curbing global warming . <p> Decisions on crunch issues are expected to be left to ministers when they return to the southern Polish city of Katowice next week . <p> U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres made a dramatic appeal to governments Monday to confront the threat of unchecked climate change . <p> Scientists say manmade emissions of greenhouses gases must be reduced to net zero by 2050 to stop catastrophic warming this century .
