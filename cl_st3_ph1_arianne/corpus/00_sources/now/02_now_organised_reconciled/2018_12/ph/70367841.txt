<h> Salo wants homeland security training in PPSC curriculum <p> A party-list solon recently filed a bill seeking to upgrade the curriculum of the Philippine Public Safety College ( PPSC ) . <p> Assistant Majority Floor Leader and Kabayan Partylist Rep. Ron Salo said Homeland security career training and higher continuing education should be included in the curriculum of PPSC . <p> " In the 21st century environment , public safety and security issues and concerns are wide-ranging , sophisticated and daunting , " said Salo . <p> House Bill 8760 seeks to expand the mission of the PPSC to include Homeland Security career training and higher and continuing education for the Philippine National Police ( PNP ) , Bureau of Fire Protection ( BFP ) and the Bureau of Jail Management and Penology ( BJMP ) . <p> Salo said the " pandemics , natural disaster , climate change , transnational organized crimes , terrorism and violent extremism , cybercrime , protection of critical infrastructures , biosafety hazards and other emerging threats expand the domains of public safety and bring into fore a more comprehensive and integrative homeland security orientation for the country .
