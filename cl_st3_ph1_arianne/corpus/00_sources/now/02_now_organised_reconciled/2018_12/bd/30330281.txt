<h> Major cities can fight climate change and make billions : researchers <p> Implementing green strategies like bike lanes and better building codes could bring 94 world cities a collective $583 billion worth of benefits , while fighting climate change , according to research by a global network of cities . <p> C40 Cities pinpointed transport , buildings and industry as priorities to be incorporated into climate change policies . Doing so would encourage large investments and avert 223,000 premature deaths , as people live and work longer . <p> " This research quantifies and provides the business case for what mayors have long known to be true : taking bold climate action also improves public health , " C40 executive director Mark Watts said in a statement . <p> " There is no longer any trade-off for cities between delivering policies that benefit the environment , drive economic growth and improve the health of citizens . " <p> Cities are key in the fight against climate change , according to the World Bank Group 's International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) . <p> More than half the global population lives in cities , which consume over two-thirds of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 70 percent of all carbon dioxide emissions , the IFC says . <p> Cities should also adopt strict regulations for new buildings and retrofit older buildings to improving heating , ventilation , air conditioning , water heating and lighting systems , researchers added . <p> Industry and businesses should use energy efficient technologies . Emissions capturing and maintenance and monitoring are all important if climate goals are to be achieved , they said . <p> By focusing on green approaches to transport , buildings and industry , cities could reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 87 percent and hazardous airborne pollutants known as PM2.5 by 49 percent , according to the researchers .
