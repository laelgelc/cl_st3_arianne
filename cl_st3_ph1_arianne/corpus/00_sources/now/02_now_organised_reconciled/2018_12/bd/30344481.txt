<h> First-ever Bangladesh-Portugal foreign office consultations held <p> Senior Correspondent bdnews24.com Published : 2018-12-10 03:36:19 BdST <h> The first-ever bilateral political consultation between Bangladesh and Portugal has been held in Lisbon , the foreign ministry says . <p> Secretary , Bilateral and Consular , to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Kamrul Ahsan and Director General of Foreign Policy of Portuguese Foreign Ministry Pedro Sanchez Da Costa Pereira led their sides during the Dec 6 meeting . <p> They agreed " to have enhanced cooperation to deepen and widen " the bilateral relations . <p> The discussions covered trade and investment , power and energy , particularly renewable energy , blue economy and maritime resources , tourism and culture , employment opportunity for Bangladeshi workforce , regional and international issues such as Brexit , Rohingya , climate change , and other issues of mutual interest . <p> Portugal " congratulated " Bangladesh on the socio-economic development , and " appreciated " Bangladesh 's important humanitarian role in hosting over a million Rohingyas of Myanmar . <p> It also expressed interest to take part in the upcoming projects particularly in infrastructure , ICT , power and renewable energy sectors . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ regular exchange of visits at all levels including political , business , civil society and people 's group to boost relations .
