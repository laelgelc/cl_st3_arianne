<h> 4 out of 5 Five-Yrs plans fail to achieve growth target <p> Abu Sazzad : All 5-Year Plans ( FYPs ) had failed to achieve the GDP ( gross domestic product ) growth target due to political instability of the country . Apart from this , only the seventh FYP is exception to consider the GDP growth of the last three fiscal years . Political turmoil had played the major hindrance to achieve the projected GDP growth target of all the six 5-Year Plans ( FYPs ) of the government since 1973 . Inefficient institutions and their lack of democratic practices were behind the non-fulfillment of achieving the targeted of GDP growth , said renowned economist and former Bangladesh Bank governor Dr Atiur Rahman to Daily Industry on Monday . However , the Planning Commission produced the country 's first 5-Year Plan ( 1973-78 ) under the leadership of Professor Nurul Islam . Unfortunately , implementation suffered from extremely fragile institutions , the huge challenge of building up a war ravaged economy , weak administrative capacities , inadequate resources and political turmoil . Along with growth , the plan will have to emphasize social protection , urban transition and a sustainable development pathway resilient to disaster and climate change , said former Bangladesh Bank Governor Dr Mohammad Farasuddin . He said that the current plan should focus on equity and social protection in addition to accelerating growth . " We will not be able to empower every citizen , but we will be able to provide them with social protection " , he also said . Farasuddin said achieving SDG status should be driven by performance in the Human Development Index , not by that of per capita income . Becoming a middle income country on the basis of per capital income is misleading , he added .
