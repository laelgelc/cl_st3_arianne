<h> GREENHOUSE GAS PLEDGE <h> India on track to achieve 2030 goal <p> India is firmly on track to meet its target on power generation from non-fossil sources and reduce greenhouse gases well ahead of the deadline , Environment Minister Harsh Vardhan said on Monday at a climate summit in Poland . <p> " In terms of commitment , we are already on track . We will achieve our target ahead of the 2030 deadline " , Harsh Vardhan said . <p> He said India is committed to win the battle against global warming and the country has made " great strides " in its journey towards meeting climate change adaptation and mitigation targets . <p> India has set an ambitious target on three fronts . It has pledged to produce 40 percent of its power from non-fossil fuels by 2030 . It has also committed to cut down the emissions of greenhouse gases by 33-35 per cent from the 2005-levels and create an additional carbon sink of 2.5-3 billion tonnes of carbon dioxide through planting trees . <p> Vardhan said India is working hard for achieving its target to get 175 GW power from renewable energy by 2022 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ renewable power , he added . <p> He also said that just in the last two years India has achieved a one percent increase in its forest cover whereas in many countries across the globe it is depleting . <p> India has also pledged to get rid of single-use plastic by 2022 across the country .
