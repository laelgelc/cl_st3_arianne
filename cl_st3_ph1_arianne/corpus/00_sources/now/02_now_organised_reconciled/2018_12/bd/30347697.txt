<h> COP24 SUMMIT <h> US , Saudi rank last on curbing climate change <p> The United States and Saudi Arabia rank last when it comes to curbing climate change among the 56 nations accounting for 90 percent of planet-warming greenhouse gas emissions , researchers said yesterday . <p> A large number of laggards means the world is dangerously off-track when it comes to slashing the carbon pollution that has already amplified droughts , flooding and deadly heatwaves worldwide , they reported on the margins of UN climate talks in Katowice , Poland . <p> " Only a few countries have started to implement strategies to limit global warming well below 2 degrees Celsius ( 3.6 degrees Fahrenheit ) , " the cornerstone target of the 2015 Paris climate treaty , according to NewClimate Institute and Germanwatch , an NGO . <p> Most governments " lack the political will to phase out fossil fuels with the necessary speed . " <p> Global emissions of carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) , which accounts for 80 percent of global warming , will climb nearly three percent in 2018 , scientists confirmed last week . <p> After holding steady from 2014 through 2016 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change -- carbon pollution started to climb again last year , driven by increased use of oil , gas and coal . <p> Sweden and Morocco scored highest in the annual ranking , the survey showed , with Britain , India , Norway , Portugal and the European Union as a whole in the top tranche as well . <p> Other nations at the bottom of the list included Iran , South Korea , Australia , Canada , Russia , Turkey and Japan .
