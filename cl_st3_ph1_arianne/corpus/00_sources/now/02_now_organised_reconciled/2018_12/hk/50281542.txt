<h> MDBs agree to align with the Paris Agreement <h> There is growing awareness within the financial markets that matters related to climate change need to be a major consideration , now MDBs agree to cooperate on six key areas <p> By Michael Marray <p> The various multilateral development banks ( MDBs ) will in future be working under a joint framework for aligning their activities with the goals of the Paris Agreement , reinforcing their commitment to combat climate change . <p> In a joint declaration issued at the Katowice climate change summit in early December , the MDBs committed to working together in six key areas considered central to meeting the goals of the Agreement , which aims to limit the increase in global temperatures to well below 2 ? C , and to pursue efforts towards achieving a 1.5 ? C target . <p> " The global development agenda is at a pivotal point , " the joint declaration says . " There is international consensus on the urgent need to ensure that policy engagements and financial flows are consistent with a pathway towards low greenhouse gas emissions and climate-resilient development .
