<p> UN Climate Change News , 12 December 2018 -- Today , at the closing of the Talanoa Dialogue , the Presidencies of this and last years ' UN Climate Change Conferences ( COP24 and COP23 ) issued the Talanoa Call for Action . This statement calls for the urgent and rapid mobilization of all societal actors to step up their efforts with a view to meeting the global climate goals agreed in Paris in 2015 . The calls to action were delivered by youth champions Timoci Naulusala from Fiji and Hanna Wojdowska from Poland . <p> The closing session concluded 21 ministerial roundtables -- convened on the previous day at COP24 , which runs to the end of the week here in Katowice , Poland . The roundtables brought together nearly 100 ministers and over 40 non-Party stakeholders to chart a way forward for global climate action . <p> " It is with great joy and commitment that the Polish Presidency co-leads with Fiji the Talanoa Dialogue , " said COP 24 President Michal Kurtyka .
