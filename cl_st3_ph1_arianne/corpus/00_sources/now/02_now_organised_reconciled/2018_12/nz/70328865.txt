He has weaponised public distrust of the media . <p> OPINION : Whenever I read something about Donald Trump , my eyes go straight to the credit line at the bottom of the story to see where it came from . <p> If it 's sourced from the Washington Post or the New York Times , I read it with a degree of scepticism . These once-great newspapers have dangerously compromised their credibility by allowing their almost obsessive dislike of the American president to contaminate their reportage . <p> This is made worse by their tendency to allow fact and opinion to become so entangled that it 's hard to tell where one ends and the other starts . It 's open season on Trump , and many American journalists make it clear that they despise him . <p> And actually , I understand why they feel that way .
