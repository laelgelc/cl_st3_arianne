<h> Genesis to electrify light vehicle fleet by 2020 <p> Words : Nile Bijoux <p> 11 Dec 2018 <p> While it might seem odd to read that a company dealing in electricity has pledged to go electric , that 's in fact what Genesis is doing .... with its light vehicle fleet . <p> The energy company joined The Climate Group 's EV100 initiative during COP24 , this year 's UN climate change conference held in Poland . As a result , Genesis has committed to converting every car in its light vehicle fleet to electric or hybrid power by 2020 , with the same ambition for 50 per cent of its heavy vehicle fleet by 2025 . <p> In New Zealand , the transport sector accounts for 18 per cent of carbon emissions ; globally , the sector is the fastest growing contributor to climate change with businesses owning more than half of all registered vehicles . <p> Genesis ' fleet comprises 86 light vehicles smaller than 3500kg and 82 heavy vehicles , such as LPG delivery trucks . Nearly half of its light vehicles are now of a hybrid or fully electric nature , and Genesis will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ year to encourage on-site charging . <p> The company will also install three chargers at its Tuai , Tokaanu and Tekapo generation sites.Marc England , CEO of Genesis , says joining the EV100 was a logical next step , following the release in August of Genesis ' Sustainability Framework , which included a goal around EV transition . <p> " We recognise that climate change is a global challenge and requires international collaboration , which motivated us to join the EV100 group , " said Marc . <p> " In New Zealand , we have a unique carbon emissions profile where 85 per cent of what we generate comes from renewable sources . That means our electric cars will be mostly powered by renewable energy too -- an advantage we have over many other countries and one that really enables us to set an example . Our challenge is to continue to increase New Zealand 's renewable energy generation to cater for increased demand from electric vehicles .
