<h> Onus on GDC for strategic guidance <p> While there may be nothing new in it , a report on the effects of climate change for Tairawhiti to be presented at tomorrow 's Gisborne District Council meeting is another much needed wake-up call for the council and the community . <p> The report does take things to a higher level with facts and graphs on potential impacts here , including a worst-case scenario -- where global greenhouse gas emissions continue at current levels ( a scary and extremely unlikely scenario , but an appropriate baseline ) -- of average temperatures 3 degrees Celsius higher and 4 percent less rainfall than now by 2090 . <p> It is further warning , if that is still needed , of how the coming risk of more severe storms , droughts and coastal erosion pose a particular threat to this land-based economy . <p> It also rightly says that strategic guidance is needed as to how the council and its communities can respond to climate change . <p> The report says climate change is stated as an issue of significance in the Long-Term Plan , however : " To date , council has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change mitigation and/or adaptation .
