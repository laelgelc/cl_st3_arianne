Nations also responded to the IPCC Special Report on 1.5 ? C of Global Warming . <p> New Zealand played a small yet important role in the talks , as a member of the High Ambition Coalition . This is inconsistent with holding warming below 2 ? C , let alone 1.5 ? C.
