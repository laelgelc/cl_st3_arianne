<p> A Shoreline Management Plan ( SMP ) provides a large-scale assessment of the risks associated with coastal evolution and presents a framework to address these risks to people and the developed , historic and natural environment in a sustainable manner . <p> In doing so , an SMP is a high-level document that forms an important part of our Council 's Coastal Management Strategy . <p> * To define , in general terms , the flooding and erosion risks to people and the developed , historic and natural environment within the SMP area over the next century ; * To identify the preferred framework for managing those risks ; * To identify the consequences of implementing the preferred framework ; * To set out procedures for monitoring the effectiveness of the SMP ; * To inform planners , developers and others of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ considering future development of the shoreline and land use changes ; * To comply with international and national nature conservation legislation and biodiversity obligations ; * To highlight areas where knowledge gaps exist ; and , * To provide an action plan to facilitate implementation of the SMP " policies " and monitor progress . <p> Currently , we are in the process of procuring internal and external resources to kick-off our journey towards a resilient community . <p> Data is being collated and analysed and a steering group has been established in partnership with Waikato Regional Council and in liaison with Hauraki District Council . <p> Over the years many investigative reports and surveys have been completed on which we want to build , i.e. we do n't want to reinvent the wheel , making best use of the resources available . <p> The detailed holistic hazard and risk assessments will commence in earnest within the next two months for our three-year project . <p> Coastal projects update <p> We 've been underway with several high-priority , reactive coastal projects over the last few months .
