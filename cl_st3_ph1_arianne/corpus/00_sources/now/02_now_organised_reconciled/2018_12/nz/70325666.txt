<p> OPINION : This week , more than 20,000 Earth and planetary scientists from all over the world are converging on Washington for the annual gathering of the American Geophysical Union . <p> They come together to celebrate the wonder of our planet and to swap clues about the evolution of the Earth system from the deep past to the distant future . It is a treasured geo-family reunion . <p> But to avoid the carbon-dioxide emissions from flying , one of us will travel to Washington by train , and two of us have decided not to attend . <p> As Earth scientists , we understand the urgency of climate change better than anyone . Our findings are inscribed in international and national reports dating back several decades , each more urgent than the last .
