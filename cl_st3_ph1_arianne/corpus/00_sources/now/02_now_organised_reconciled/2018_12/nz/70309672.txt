<p> While some have advocated for net zero emissions across all gases , the IPCC report says that the most " transformative " scenario is about a 35 per cent cut in methane emissions by 2050 . Given some caveats , this is achievable . <p> We should accept ( and the report acknowledges ) that changes to farming and forestry systems could affect current ecosystems and their services ( the positive things they do for the environment ) and " potentially threaten food , water and livelihood security . " <p> It is a little repeated fact that the Paris Climate Change Agreement , while calling for mitigation and adaptation , recognised the importance of food security and requires that mitigation should not threaten food production .
