<h> 30 Articles of the UDHR - Article 29 : Duty to Your Community <p> So far , the Universal Declaration of Human Rights ( UDHR ) has concentrated on rights that every person has simply by virtue of being born human . Now Article 29 says the corollary of rights is duties . We all have a duty to other people , and we should protect their rights and freedoms . <p> Fernand Dehousse , the Belgian representative to the United Nations while the UDHR was being drafted , said that Article 29 's first paragraph " quite properly established a sort of contract between the individual and community , involving a fair exchange of benefits .
