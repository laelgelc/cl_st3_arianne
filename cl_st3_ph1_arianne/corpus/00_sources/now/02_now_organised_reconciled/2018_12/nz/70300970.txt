What 's the big deal ? <p> Climate change is a threat multiplier that touches everything , from our health to our economy to our coasts to our infrastructure . It makes heat waves stronger , heavy precipitation events more frequent and hurricanes more intense , and it nearly doubles the area burned by wildfires .
