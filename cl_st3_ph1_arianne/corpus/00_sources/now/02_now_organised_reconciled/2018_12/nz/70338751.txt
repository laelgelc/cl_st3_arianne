<p> We need climate institutions with a focus on our agreed long term emission reduction targets , and with the tools to ensure that these goals are not sacrificed to short term political pressure . There is n't much point if the proposed Climate Commission is just another advisory group . <p> There are analogies between the Climate Commission and the introduction of the independent Reserve Bank . The Reserve Bank was an institutional response to the perceived long term problem of inflation and financial stability . <p> It was given the teeth to act independently from short term political pressures . If climate change is as great a threat as inflation - and it is more so - then the Climate Commission needs to be at least this ambitious . <p> We need an independent Climate Commission with real tools to influence emissions , and not just issue guidance . It needs to be able to independently influence the price of carbon as a critical way to reduce emissions .
