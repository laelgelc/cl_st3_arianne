<h> Christmas 2018 : How to have a carbon-friendly time without being a Grinch <p> OPINION : Christmas is undeniably the best time of the year , but between all the overeating , awkward family photos and overexcitable kids on sugar highs , it can also be needlessly tough on the environment . <p> Millions of us jump on flights , drive hundreds of kilometres , buy untold quantities of gifts , and generally consume as much as we can . <p> Down here in New Zealand , we 're becoming far more proactive about reducing waste and carbon emissions , ditching single-use bags at supermarkets , and using fewer disposable cups and straws . <p> 123RF <p> A few small changes can make your Christmas far more environmentally friendly . <p> Here are five small things you can do this Christmas to reduce your environmental footprint , without losing any of the magic on the big day . <p> Give gifts that do n't get binned <p> A truly great Christmas gift is one that you hold onto for years , or even decades . Choose a locally made sauce with seriously low food miles . A nice sweet fruit chutney is a great cranberry sauce alternative . <p> Recycle paper <p> Wrapped gifts look great under the tree but that paper is usually carelessly ripped off the gifts and discarded , never to be thought of again . In New Zealand , the amount of wrapping paper thrown away from Christmas gifts is estimated to be 26,000 km -- enough to stretch from Auckland to London ( and half way back again ) or from Sydney to Perth six and a half times . <p> Do n't over-cater <p> A Kiwi Christmas often involves setting out @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ festivities , resulting in huge food waste .
