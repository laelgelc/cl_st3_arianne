Some do n't like discussing that , as proving its viability at scale could weaken the fragile political consensus to reduce emissions . In reality , we will need to do both . <p> A focus on what must be foregone to address climate change also applies the lens of today 's energy system only , which is understandable considering the urgency , but ignores the technological possibilities and the ability of market forces to bring about rapid change .
