<h> Councils criticised in Rotorua flooding report <p> An independent report into the April flooding that struck parts of Rotorua has revealed residents ' frustration at what it called the ' ping ponging ' of responsibilities between Rotorua Lakes Council and the Bay of Plenty Regional Council . <p> The panel was established in the wake of flooding that saw 62.5mm of rain fall in just one hour on Sunday April 29 , prompting Rotorua Mayor Steve Chadwick to declare a State of Emergency . <p> In their 110 page report the panel found " many had stories to tell of where they had contacted the Regional Council ( BOPRC ) or Rotorua Lakes Council ( RLC ) about an issue in the Ngongotaha stream , council staff had come out and discussed a solution , but then nothing came of it " . <p> " Others had talked about the frustration of ' ping ponging ' between the two councils several times to try and get some support when they saw a need for some work to be done on the stream . Others talked of having been discouraged by bureaucracy .
