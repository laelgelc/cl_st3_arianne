<h> Genesis joins global EV100 initiative <p> Genesis Energy has joined The Climate Group 's EV100 initiative which brings together companies from all over the world that are committed to switching their global fleets to electric vehicles to reduce carbon emissions . <p> Genesis has committed to convert every car in its light vehicle fleet to electric or hybrid by 2020 , with the same ambition for 50% of its heavy vehicle fleet by 2025 . <p> Genesis joined EV100 during COP24 , this year 's UN climate change conference held in Poland . <p> In New Zealand , the transport sector accounts for 18% of carbon emissions ; globally , the sector is the fastest growing contributor to climate change with businesses owning more than half of all registered vehicles . <p> Introducing fleet electric vehicles offers an opportunity to curb greenhouse gas emissions along with air and noise pollution , and increases the number of low-emissions vehicles in the New Zealand second-hand-car marketplace . <p> Genesis has already converted almost half of its light vehicles to hybrid or fully electric vehicles , including the addition of three Hyundai Konas in November . <p> In 2019 , Genesis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Christchurch corporate offices to encourage staff and customers to charge their own cars on site . <p> Genesis will also install three chargers at its Tuai , Tokaanu and Tekapo generation sites . <p> Marc England , CEO of Genesis , says joining the EV100 was a logical next step , following the release in August of Genesis ' Sustainability Framework , which included a goal around EV transition . <p> " We recognise that climate change is a global challenge that requires international collaboration , which motivated us to join the EV100 group , " said Marc .
