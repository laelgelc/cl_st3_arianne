<p> A majority of Canadians said the federal government 's carbon pricing plan will not alter their own behaviour to fight climate change , according to a recent poll . <p> A recent Forum Research poll showed that 62 per cent of Canadians were aware of the federal government 's carbon pricing plan , but only 40 per cent said it would change their behaviour . On the other hand , 53 per cent said the government 's policy would not change their behaviour . <p> Lorne Bozinoff , the president and founder of Forum Research , told 1310 NEWS ' The Rick Gibbons Show that while most people believe climate change is happening and that it is due to human activity , they are not necessarily willing to do anything about it . <p> He said part of the problem might be that the federal government has n't communicated the plan very well . <p> " They may be aware there 's a carbon pricing plan out there , but I 'm not sure if they 're aware of the impact of the plan , " he said .
