Scientists say the deal 's most ambitious goal -- limiting the rise to 1.5 degrees C ( 2.7 C ) -- is still feasible , but increasingly challenging . <p> The two-week meeting brings together diplomats from almost 200 countries , often with differing agendas . Some , such as the small Pacific islands , are pressing for urgent and drastic action , especially from developed countries , to reduce greenhouse gas emissions .
