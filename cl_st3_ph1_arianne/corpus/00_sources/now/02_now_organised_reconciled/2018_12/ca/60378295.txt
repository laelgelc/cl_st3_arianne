<h> UBC students want to build and study effectiveness of ' tree canopy ' bus shelters <p> VANCOUVER -- Students at the University of British Columbia are hoping to build bus shelters with environmental benefits . <p> Tabinda Shah , a final-year urban forestry student , said she and several other students are working to build a " tree canopy bus shelter , " which would not only shelter people from the rain as they wait for their ride , but also help the environment . <p> " The aim of the project is to bring ecologically conscious infrastructure into dense urban areas by maximizing opportunities for green infrastructure in small spaces , " she said in an email . <p> The roof or shelter would be made of treated wood that can withstand the elements and host a layer of plants that are hardy and succulent , and can thrive in not just the rain but the dry months too . The excess water from the roof would run off into the ground to recharge the water table . <p> The students are crowdfunding the project and want to build at least three bus shelters to measure their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the team is hoping to have a prototype shelter built by sometime next year . <p> Daniel Roehr , associate professor at UBC , said while the team does not have any arrangement with the City of Vancouver or the transit agency , they do have permits to build three structures on the University of British Columbia campus . <p> Shah said Vancouver is a very walkable city , but that hardly anyone wants to walk in it during the winter because of a lack of pedestrian shelter from the rain . <p> " Being an urban forestry student , I wanted to bring a multifaceted solution to the table that would not only increase walkability in the city , but also create habitat space , more sustainable stormwater management and a biophilic city , " she said . <p> Roehr said Vancouver has a number of green roofs but most of them need to be irrigated , so one of the main design aims of these tree canopy bus shelters was that they would be self-sufficient . <p> Roehr and Shah are working with a team of other @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We have flow devices to measure rainwater runoff from these roofs and how effective they are , " Roehr said .
