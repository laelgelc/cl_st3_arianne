<h> MUNICIPAL MATTERS : Municipal councils get lump of coal <p> In 2017 , the federal finance department announced the end of a long-standing perk municipal officials receive effective Jan. 1 , 2019 : the one-third tax free exemption to cover non-receipted expenses they could n't claim in carrying out municipal business . The feds cited transparency and fairness in that other Canadians do n't enjoy that benefit . Some larger centres , such as Calgary , got rid of their tax-free portion years ago , but many municipalities across Canada examined options to offset the loss of income . <p> This fall , each one across the Valley agreed to the most popular : increasing the gross pay of council members to keep their net pay at the same level . Taking no action would have seen a reduction of pay in the range of $3,000 to 5,000 . <p> Before you screech like a Scrooge , the loss would have put more rural councillors under the low-income threshold -- not a great way to attract the youth and skilled people we need sitting in the council chambers of today .
