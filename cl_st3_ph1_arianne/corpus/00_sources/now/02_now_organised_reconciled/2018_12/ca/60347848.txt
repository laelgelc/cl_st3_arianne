<h> The Drilldown : Daimler to buy billions worth of batteries <p> The parent company of Mercedes-Benz is planning on purchasing $23-billion of battery cells by 2030 as it prepares for a mass transition to hybrid and electric vehicles . <p> German automaker Daimler declined to disclose what companies had been awarded the contracts for the batteries , though it does have supply deals in place with Korea 's SK Innovation , LG Chem and China 's Contemporary Amperex Technology . <p> Daimler is also reportedly attempting to bolster in-house battery cell research and reduce its reliance on cobalt , as part of the impending production shift . The Mercedes-Benz EQ electric car , slated for release in 2019 , will use battery cells that are 60 per cent nickel , 20 per cent manganese and 20 per cent cobalt . Going forward , Daimler hopes to change their recipe by using cells that are 80 per cent nickel , 10 per cent manganese and 10 per cent cobalt .
