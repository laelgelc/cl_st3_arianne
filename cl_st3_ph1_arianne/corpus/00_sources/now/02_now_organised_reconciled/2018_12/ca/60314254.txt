<p> Plastic water bottles overload landfills needlessly . The pressure against plastic waste is growing : zero plastic packaging in Canada 's landfills by 2040 , and no litter from single-use plastics by 2025 . But we ca n't wait for domestic and international efforts to eliminate plastics in landfills and oceans . We can individually make purchase choices to force changes in packaging to eliminate plastics everywhere . According to Environment and Climate Change Canada , less than 11 per cent of plastics are currently recycled . Marine litter from merchant ships and mega cruise ships keeps growing . Single-use plastics are the biggest source of plastic waste- water bottles , bags , plates , cups , Styrofoam takeout food containers . The preference for bottled water over reusable water containers or tap water has made water the most prevalent beverage in North America , ahead of beer . Pure tap water is shunned in favour of convenience . I came from a country where tap water was dangerous , and value safe tap water here . The Canadian plastics industry aims to make all plastic packaging 100 per cent recyclable but plastics take hundreds of years to disappear . I say replace plastic immediately with cardboard , glass , metal or cellophane . These other materials were used by the food industry until plastics appeared in the 1900s . Cellophane was invented in 1900 ; it is a thin , transparent sheet made of regenerated cellulose or plant material . It was the only packaging available until plastic came along , and is still in wide use . Cellophane has low permeability to air , oils , greases , bacteria , and water which makes it useful for food packaging , and is ecologically sound . We managed very well for years with cellophane which is made from wood and biodegradable in three to six months , much less than the hundreds of years for synthetic plastics to degrade . Two litre milk comes in cardboard containers in Canada , and I wo n't buy milk in plastic jugs . Consumer demand can reshape the packaging industry . In 1986 McDonalds served burgers and chicken nuggets in foam " clamshells , " but dropped them in 1990 . Now it uses environmentally friendly paper wrappings and cardboard containers . As an industry leader its example can encourage other fast-food companies to reduce plastic use . Plastic straws are replacing paper straws , and foam for cups and takeout food can be replaced by cardboard containers . The next step is banning plastic PVC pipe for water , electrical and gas construction as these are costly to recycle . Tim Hortons offer china mugs instead of paper for in store use . Choose environmentally friendly options wherever possible . Consumers have the power to change packaging by rejecting products in plastic containers and demanding alternatives .
