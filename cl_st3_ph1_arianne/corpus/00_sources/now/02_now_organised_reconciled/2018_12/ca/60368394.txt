<p> LETHBRIDGE - The Lethbridge County Administrative Building , located across from City Hall on 4 Ave . S and Stafford Drive , is now able to produce its own power . <p> According to a news release from the County , 220 solar panels were installed on the east , west and south-facing slopes of the roof of the building . <p> ENMAX Generation Portfolio subcontracted a local company called SolarWind Renewable Energy to install the panels , capable of producing nearly 100,000 kWh of energy each year . <p> Total cost of the project was $156,403.51 . Twenty-five per cent of the project was funded through the Municipal Climate Change Action Centre ( MCCAC ) .
