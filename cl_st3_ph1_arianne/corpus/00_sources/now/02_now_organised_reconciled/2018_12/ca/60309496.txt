Franzen fans will know the Illinois-born author is a devoted birder ; many pieces here derive from trips he 's taken to catch a glimpse of an opal-rumped tanager or a chestnut-bellied cuckoo . <p> Birds serve as an entry point into conversations about capitalism , habitat loss and conservation , as Franzen makes connections between dwindling bird populations and our doomed future , primarily owing to climate change . <p> That last one is a sticky point ; one of the previously published essays , Save What You Love , found Franzen embroiled in more controversy .
