<h> Teens stage school strike in Vancouver to press for more action on climate <p> For young people , the relentless increase in greenhouse gas emissions poses an existential threat . <p> So today , a bunch of them walked out of classes in Vancouver as part of an international movement to push for greater actions to deal with climate change . <p> The #climatestrike movement was launched by a Swedish teenager , Greta Thunberg , who spends her Fridays in front of the Swedish parliament . <p> In Vancouver , teenagers skipped school to hold a rally and then sit in the office of B.C. Environment Minister George Heyman , who 's the MLA for Vancouver-Fairview . <p> " As teenagers , we want to be excited about our future , not scared about impending climate catastrophe , " a 16-year-old organizer , Rebecca Hamilton , said in a news release . " We are part of a global movement of youth rising to defend our collective future . We are n't here out of anger .
