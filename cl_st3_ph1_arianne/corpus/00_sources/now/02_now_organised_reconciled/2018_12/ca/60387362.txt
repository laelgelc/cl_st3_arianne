<p> The Ontario government is ignoring a growing crisis in housing the poor , London housing advocates charge . <p> The Progressive Conservative government is asking the city for suggestions on how to lower home prices and rents and increase the housing supply , but there 's no mention of a growing crisis in affordable and social housing , Coun. Stephen Turner said Friday . <p> In London 3,700 families are on a waiting list for social or geared-to-income homes , which also need $230 million in upgrades . <p> " We met with MPPs on this and the implication was there is not enough high-end housing , " said Turner , adding the government suggests lowering rents across all markets will result in lower rents for the poor . <p> " They were proposing trickle-down housing economics . It was absurd . " <p> Doug Ford 's Tory government has already slashed $9 million from social housing for London by cancelling the province 's cap and trade program to fight climate change , he said .
