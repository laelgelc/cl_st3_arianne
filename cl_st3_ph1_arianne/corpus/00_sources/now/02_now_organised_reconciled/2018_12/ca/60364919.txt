It also provides wildlife habitat and connectivity with the Chebucto Peninsula . - Hanna Nicholls <p> A decade ago , an immense community effort , referred to as the " Buy Back the Mersey " movement , led the Nova Scotian government to purchase the Mersey-Bowater lands , some of which are just inland from St. Margarets Bay -- a great move . <p> The area is easy to reach for many residents in the southern outskirts of HRM and is widely used for camping , Scouting , hiking , four-wheelers , dog walking , fishing and just exploring nature . At the same time , it provides wildlife habitat and connectivity with the Chebucto Peninsula . The same community involved in the " Buy Back the Mersey " movement has repeatedly made efforts to have community involvement in the way these lands are managed . Further , Lahey indicates that harvests should not be next to protected areas , and that old growth ( present in some of these parcels ) should always be spared from cutting . <p> I ask that there be a moratorium on further harvesting of the forests in this area so there can be meaningful interactions with the community and a serious evaluation of the potential for establishing a wilderness area in the Indian and Ingram River watersheds . Prior to the government 's purchase of these lands , the area was not a candidate as a potential wilderness area because it was privately owned . Clearcutting would make most alternative uses of this land impossible for a very long time .
