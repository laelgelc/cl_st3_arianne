<h> Saturday 's letters to the editor <p> Santa can bring affordable housing Dear Santa , All I want for Christmas is an affordable place to live . In case you are not aware , in Belleville , there is no affordable housing available . In 2006 I applied for geared-to-income housing . At that time I was told there was an expected five to 10 year wait to get housing . Twelve years later I am still waiting and have matured to a senior 's list . I know I am not alone in my struggles . I have been told in Belleville there are 1,700 other names also on this same waiting list . Being on a limited , fixed income is a constant struggle . Seems like I am always robbing Peter to pay Paul . I have already given up extras , things like buying vitamins or getting a haircut . I buy Kraft Dinner instead of meat and veggies . I no longer go out to dinner or even to Timmies for a coffee . I have n't been out to see a play or a movie or played bingo in months as these @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ point where there are no extras left to give up . Becoming homeless in a serious possibility . Even with a roommate , my apartment is costing me 75 per cent of my mo nthly income . A geared to income apartment would cost 30 per cent . You can see what that difference would make to anyone , especially those who are on limited fixed incomes . Knowing I am not alone and there are 1,700 others having the same struggles does n't make it easier . I have quit listening to the news as I get frustrated hearing yet another group of immigrants are provided with housing while the wait lists keep growing for those of us who were born here . Although I ca n't afford to bake any cookies for you this year I am hoping , dear Santa , that you would grant my Christmas wish and do whatever you can to help us all who are waiting for affordable housing.Del PackardBelleville <p> Change plans Thank you for discussing how our governments are addressing climate change .
