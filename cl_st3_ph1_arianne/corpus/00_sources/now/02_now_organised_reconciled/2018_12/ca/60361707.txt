The electric car policy B.C. announced in its climate plan is known as a Zero Emission Vehicle mandate , or " ZEV " for short . The gist of the policy is that it sets clear sales goals for vehicles that do n't contribute to climate change . Within seven years , 10 per cent of the sales done by B.C. new car dealers must be zero-emission vehicles . In 2030 , it rises to 30 per cent , then 100 per cent in 2040 . <p> The province of Quebec and 10 U.S. states have some version of the policy . Like many climate innovations , it was first adopted in California .
