<h> ' I have no intention of losing ' : Rachel Notley talks Election 2019 <p> Premier Rachel Notley says her party is playing to win in the 2019 election , but regardless of its fate she will stay on as MLA for Edmonton-Strathcona if she wins her seat . <p> " I have no intention of losing , but ... when I run to represent Edmonton-Strathcona , then I am doing that because I am running to represent the area , " Notley told Postmedia during a year-end interview Monday . <p> " Of course I would ( stay on as MLA ) , because that 's what I 'm asking them to elect me to do . " <p> In the face of opposition calls for an election as early as possible , Notley remains tight-lipped about when exactly voters will head to the polls . <p> No matter the date , she thinks the campaign will boil down to " a clear choice of values , " but for now she 's focused on governing . <p> " There 's no question that we 're getting closer to that time of campaigning , and when we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Notley said . <p> " I was born and raised to fight for the values I believe in .
