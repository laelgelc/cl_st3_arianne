<h> Sandra Oh <p> Eve Polastri ca n't help herself . Sitting idly in a London bus shelter , she spots a tiny crack in one of the glass walls , reaches out her index finger and pushes at the fracture -- tentatively at first , then with almost adolescent determination . We 're in the fifth episode of Bravo/BBC America 's addictive summer thriller Killing Eve and Polastri , a low-level intelligence agent played with anxious verve by Sandra Oh , is poking at cracks all around her , both terrified and thrilled by the prospect of seeing it all shatter to pieces .
