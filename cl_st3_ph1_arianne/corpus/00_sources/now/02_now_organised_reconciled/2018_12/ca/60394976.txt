<h> City of Powell River places emphasis on climate change <p> ENERGY EFFICIENCY : City of Powell River councillor CaroleAnn Leishman has taken on the new climate change portfolio tasked with improving energy efficiency and reducing Powell River 's carbon emissions . David Brindle photo <p> Keeping the global perspective on climate change is important but it is the little things that matter , according to City of Powell River sustainability consultant Anastasia Lukyanova . <p> " At the end of the day it 's the actions of little communities like ours that are going to add up , " said Lukyanova . " We hear the world leaders say that it 's been the municipal and local governments that have the most power to make real change happen . " <p> The city is putting more emphasis on that change with the new Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation Portfolio headed by councillor CaroleAnn Leishman . <p> Leishman said the city 's general climate change plan targets a reduction in carbon emissions to 30 or 40 per cent by 2032 . <p> In its recently announced CleanBC plan , the province upgraded its targets to 40 per cent by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cent by 2050 . All new buildings will be net-zero energy by 2032 and by 2040 all new cars must be zero emission . <p> As a municipality , Lukyanova said Powell River has a lot of opportunities to improve on energy efficiency and reducing carbon emission levels . According to her report to city council in July , emissions from all city operated buildings , fleet and equipment in 2017 was 1,923 tonnes , and cost the city $1,662,442 . <p> " There 's been a lot of great groundwork done , " said Lukyanova . " We 've done all the energy studies ; we had a lighting audit done in 2014/2015 . We have an excellent community emission plan and a carbon-neutral action plan . But there has n't been as much action on those plans as there could have been . " It would be great if Powell River got onboard for that sooner rather than later so when the province does mandate it and we have to go into the energy step code at say , step three , it 's not going to be a big shock to everyone . " <p> Currently , developers are required by the city to install wiring for solar energy and installation for electric-vehicle charging stations in new homes . <p> There 's definitely a gap between political will and the will of the public , according to Leishman . <p> " Unless things are in policy and actually mandated they quite often do not happen , " said Leishman .
