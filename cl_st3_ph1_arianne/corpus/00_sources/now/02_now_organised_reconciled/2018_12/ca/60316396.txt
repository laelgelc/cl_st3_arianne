The midterms also saw the average age of congressional representatives go down by 10 years . <p> Likewise , in the 2015 Canadian federal election , 58 per cent of newly eligible voters turned out to vote , an increase of almost 18 per cent over the 2011 election . <p> There have been similar increases in voting among 18- to 24-year-olds in provincial elections . Dramatic wins in 2015 for the NDP in Alberta and British Columbia can be attributed in part to youth voting , because younger voters lean progressive . <p> Young voters in Canada have shown up for parties focused on issues that are important to their generation , in particular climate change , diversity , equality and the regulatory role of government . <p> Youth movements , in fact , are playing a role in voter turnout . Barriers to youth participation should also be addressed . Like adults , youth report lack of time as their No. 1 reason for not voting .
