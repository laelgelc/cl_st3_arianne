( John MacDougall/pool photo via AP ) <p> The Associated Press Published Sunday , December 30 , 2018 6:38PM EST <p> BERLIN -- Chancellor Angela Merkel says Germany will keep pushing for global solutions to challenges in 2019 and also has to take greater responsibility in the world . <p> Closing a politically turbulent 2018 in Germany , Merkel devotes a significant part of her annual New Year 's address to the merits of bringing a multilateral approach to international problems -- a style she has consistently defended in the face of U.S. President Donald Trump 's " America First " tactics . <p> The fourth-term chancellor pointed to curbing climate change , managing migration and combating terrorism as the kinds of challenges that benefit from a wide view . Germany starts a two-year stint on the U.N . Security Council on Jan. 1 . <p> " We want to resolve all these @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that best if we consider the interests of others , " Merkel said in a text of the message her office released ahead of a scheduled Monday broadcast . <p> " That is the lesson from the two world wars of the last century , " she added . " But this conviction is no longer shared today by everyone , and certainties of international co-operation are coming under pressure . " <p> " In such a situation , we must again stand up for , argue and fight more strongly for our convictions , " Merkel said . " And we must take on more responsibility in our own interests . " <p> She said Germany will push for " global solutions " at the U.N . and noted the country is spending more on humanitarian aid and defence . She said Berlin wants to make the European Union " more robust and able to make decisions . " <p> Turning to home , Merkel acknowledged that many Germans have " struggled very much " with her latest government amid persistent infighting since it took office in March after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said it had been " an extremely difficult political year . " <p> Germany 's leader for 13 years said she set the stage for a " new beginning " in late October by announcing she wo n't seek a fifth term . She also gave up the leadership of the conservative Christian Democratic Union , Germany 's main centre-right party , which has been led since Dec. 7 by ally Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer . <p> Merkel has said she plans to remain chancellor for the rest of this parliamentary term , which is supposed to run until 2021 . But questions remain over whether she will actually stay that long , not least because of tensions within her governing coalition . <p> " Democracy lives from change , " she said in her new year message . " We build on what our predecessors left us , and shape things in the present for those who will come after us. "
