Let 's throw caution to the wind and dare to hope for the best . How can we make the city a better place in the year ahead ? For what 's it 's worth , here are a few suggestions : <p> 1 . Get on with Rail Deck Park : The proposed 21-acre green space over the railroad tracks west of Spadina is one of the best ideas of recent years . So what 's happened to it ? True , Mayor John Tory called it a personal priority during the last election campaign , but so was SmartTrack . Let 's give the project to citizen philanthropists . Regardless , we treat winter as a huge inconvenience that slows traffic and makes us late for work . Our aversion to cold weather tells us how disconnected we can become when we spend too much time in the car or on the couch . <p> 3 . Act on climate change : The United Nation 's International Panel on Climate Change gives us 11 years to reduce greenhouse gas emissions or face devastating and irreversible consequences . Yet neither the mayor nor council seem aware . Is n't it time to say and do something ? Toronto is n't immune . <p> 4 . Urbanize Yonge : A 2017 city report @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was n't the word the authors used , but they wanted six lanes reduced to four , trees planted , bike lanes added and sidewalks widened . In short , the report proposed turning this nasty stretch of urban highway into a neighbourhood main street . Naturally , John Tory and his allies objected . For them , more interested in parking than parks , cars take precedence over people . In March , council deferred the matter to 2019 ; now 's time to do the right thing . <p> Article Continued Below <p> 5 . Clean up the city 's ravines : From a distance , Toronto 's ravines look great . They 're green , full of trees and babbling brooks , but up close , they 're a mess . Ignored , neglected and degraded , they have become garbage dumps overrun by invasive species from dog-strangling vine to garlic mustard and Japanese knotweed . Though celebrated as Toronto 's most distinctive geographical feature , our ravines need rescuing . <p> 6 . The stumbling block is our reluctance to inconvenience drivers . That 's why Toronto 's Vision Zero road safety program , a.k.a. the Zero Vision plan , has failed . If other cities can take bold steps to reduce car use so can we . <p> 7 . Remake Doug Ford 's Toronto remake : Some say the premier was right to cut Toronto city council in half , others argue he did n't go far enough . They feel Ford should also have cut terms and imposed term limits . Instead of four-year terms , the mayor and councillors should be restricted to two , maybe three , two-year terms . That would eliminate careerists and reduce the tendency of politicians to confuse their interests with the city 's . It might also encourage Torontonians to rely on more than name recognition when voting . <p> 8 . Smarten up about public transit : Is there anything the city and province have messed up more than transit ? Walk the walk : There was a time , before John Tory and Rob Ford led Toronto into its Dark Age , when the city entertained ideas that would get you laughed out of the room today . Pedestrian-only areas are an example . The need is greater than ever . But how can we walk the walk if we do n't talk the talk ? <p> 10 .
