<p> Will Goodon of Brandon has returned after participating in the 2018 United Nations Climate Change Conference in Katowice , Poland . <p> Goodon was part of the large Canadian delegation . He 's Minister of Housing and Property Management with the Manitoba Metis Federation and also is involved with environmental work with the Metis National Council . <p> " This year 's conference was held in an area that is a big coal mining region so it was kind of ironic to have the conference in that area but maybe it was on purpose and a way of saying we need to do something about climate change but we have to also understand the importance of economics in the world . " <p> He said it was about fighting climate change while still allowing people a chance to make a living . <p> " I was part of the Canadian delegation and our country always takes people who represent indigenous people so I was there representing the Metis Nation and we had some direct input into the policy that Canada was pushing forward at the conference .
