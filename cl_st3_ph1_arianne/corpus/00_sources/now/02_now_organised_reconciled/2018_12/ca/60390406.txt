The lines were from a Horatio Nelson Powers poem titled The New Year . <p> What waits for us ? At this point it 's all possibility . We can look at our landscapes and plots and survey our collection of containers for planting and imagine all sorts of things -- brilliant blooms , fragrant herbs , juicy tomatoes , the first still warm , sun-ripened strawberry . <p> Hopes and dreams for next year 's gardens will be as widely diverse as gardeners are , but for many of us , looming over the prospective picture will be an item highlighted in the news a few weeks ago : Produce prices are predicted to rise dramatically in 2019 . <p> This was a significant message in the gardening community . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Nursery in North Saanich , added this note to an email : " Did you hear the forecast for increased prices on produce next year ? Perhaps a patch of lawn could be dug up , and the soil readied for planting in the spring . Spaces between plants in ornamental plots cold be used for food plants that are handsome and would fit in . Swiss chard with coloured stalks , parsley , peppers , frilly kale and lettuces come to mind . <p> Limited growing space ? Grow as much as possible vertically , on netting or wire fencing . Cucumbers , pole and runner beans , tomatoes , peas , climbing zucchini ( Tromboncino , West Coast Seeds ) are suitable for growing on supports . Small plants such as lettuces can be grown at the bases of the vining plants , all in very limited space . <p> No garden ? Inquire about nearby allotment spaces . " <p> A wide variety of food plants can be grown in containers . Last summer , my friend Caron had an impressive array of vegetables on her condo deck . She did n't need any of my carrots . She had her own . <p> Salad vegetables are naturals for containers . Seeds for mixtures of salad plants ( mesclun blends ) are readily available and easy to grow . There are sweet and crisp mixtures . Others are spicy . Most salad blends produce attractive plantings that can be cut up to three times before they need replacing . <p> The compact , potted tomatoes I grow every year on my patio deliver masses of tasty treats all through the summer and into early autumn . Most herbs do well in pots . Unbelievably productive in pots is Redskin -- a thick-walled , blocky sweet pepper from T&amp;T seeds . <p> Lightweight containers are available at garden centres .
