When it launched , there were many broken promises , along with many other issues with this game . <p> I had some great discussions through email with readers who still enjoy the game , even with its many problems . The issue this game brings to light is n't whether or not you enjoy the game . We , as gamers , get hyped about games with commercials and little teases from the developers . <p> Gamers always expect that games are not broken or unfinished when they throw $90 down to purchase a copy .
