<h> MP Gudie Hutchings saw a lot of positives in her riding in 2018 <p> Gudie Hutchings was pleased to see a healthy investment in the people she serves in the vast riding of Long Range Mountains in 2018 . <p> The MP was pleased to see the federal government make progress in making life better for seniors and children . <p> She said she believes every dollar invested was money well spent and acknowledges progress has been made in many areas , so it was a pretty good 2018 . She also recognizes that more can be done and said she will focus her energy on delivering more good news in 2019 . <p> The Star chatted with Hutchings about the past year and asked her to list the five most important things that happened in her riding in 2018 : <p> * Search and rescue . One of the highlights of 2018 was the Canadian Coast Guard and search and rescue reopening in St. Anthony .
