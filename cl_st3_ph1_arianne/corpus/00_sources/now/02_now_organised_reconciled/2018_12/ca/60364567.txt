<p> The field of dead , red trees are a potential result of damage from uncontrolled mountain pine beetle ( inset ) populations . <p> ANNA KROUPINA <p> Pass Herald Reporter <p> About the size of a grain of rice , the mountain pine beetle is a tiny insect that poses a mighty threat to forests in Western Canada , including those close to home . <p> At the beginning of December , an operation was carried out in the McGillivray area to clear 173 pine trees infected with mountain pine beetles . Surveying was conducted in the Crowsnest Pass area to determine the infestation levels of mountain pine beetle this summer and fall . From that surveying , they found these infected trees which required control and on November 30 , 2018 , crews entered the area and successfully cut and burned all infected trees to limit the future spread of the beetles . <p> While some residents are concerned that crews are killing trees , Louis Price , Forest Health Technician with Alberta Agriculture and Forestry , says that once the beetles are in the trees , the trees are already as good as dead . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as " the most destructive pest of mature pine forests in North America " and states that it poses a serious threat to Alberta 's forests , particularly the lodgepole pine because of its abundance in the province and in western Canada . <p> " They cause significant and substantial tree mortality of lodgepole pine and that can have significant negative effects on social , economic and environmental factors , " says Price . <p> These small bark beetles kill all types of pine trees by clogging and destroying the conductive tissue of the tree by introducing a blue-stain fungus when attacking the tree . Its larvae also feed on the phloem of the tree . These actions together can kill the tree within one month of the attack . <p> continued below ... <p> " The beetle detects trees that are more stressed than others , as it will have better odds of survival in a more stressed tree that is less able to defend itself . A stressed tree will typically emit pheromones that attract the beetle , " says Price .
