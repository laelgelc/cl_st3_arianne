<h> The Drilldown : World Bank doubles climate-change fund in face of ' existential threat ' <p> The World Bank announced Monday it will double climate investments to $200 billion from 2021 to 2025 alongside the International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) . The investment has two goals : to curb emissions and to help developing nations deal with the " disastrous effects " of global warming . <p> The latest figures show that most money going to poor countries to fight climate change is being used for energy efficiency and clean-energy measures , while barely a quarter has been spent on adapting to climate change . <p> " We live in a new normal , in which disasters are more severe and more frequent , " World Bank CEO Kristalina Georgieva told the Thomson Reuters Foundation at UN climate talks in Poland . " We have to prioritize adaptation everywhere , but especially in the most vulnerable parts of the world .
