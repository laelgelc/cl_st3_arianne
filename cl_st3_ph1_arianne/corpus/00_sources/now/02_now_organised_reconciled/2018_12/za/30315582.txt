<p> The 2015 Paris Agreement is the first pact under which all nations commit to taking actions to curb global warming , caused mainly by emissions from burning coal , oil and gas . <p> Here 's what you need to know . <h> The climate club <p> A total of 196 nations , including the Palestinian government , have endorsed the landmark deal and more than 183 have officially ratified it as of 1 December . <p> International agreements can be signed , but only become binding through ratification . <p> The accord -- which becomes operational in 2020 -- entered into force in November 2016 when it crossed the threshold of 55 ratifying parties representing at least 55 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The world 's top carbon polluters , in descending order , are China , the United States , the European Union , and India . <p> The biggest polluter not to have ratified the deal is Russia , which ranks 5th .
