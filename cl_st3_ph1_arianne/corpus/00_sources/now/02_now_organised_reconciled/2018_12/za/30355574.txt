<h> Fun social ride for bicycle lovers <p> BICYCLE lovers are invited to join the first Family Fun Bicycle Ride , set to take this Saturday as part of Kouga 's Opening of the Season festivities . <p> The social ride , open to cyclists of all ages , will start at the Fountains Mall at 15:30 and end at the main beachfront . <p> " The aim of the event is to give everyone a taste of what 's to come , " Kouga Mayor Horatio Hendricks said . <p> " The benefits of cycling are immense and we would like to turn Jeffreys Bay into a bicycle-friendly town . <p> " Cycling is a fun way to keep fit and it is also a practical mode of transport when traveling shorter distances . <p> " Not only is it more affordable , but it is also a means of combating air pollution and climate change by reducing the amount of harmful emissions being pumped into the air by cars , motorcycles and the like .
