<h> Be aware of mosquitoes bites <p> With people doing all they could to be ready for the water problem that was predicted for Cape Town , people went to great lengths to keep and catch water . <p> Little did they know that by doing so they were inviting problems to their doorsteps . Due to stagnant water and open water collections , some areas have seen an increase in mosquitoes <p> Kevin Rack of Muizenberg Lakeside Ratepayers ' Association ( MLRA ) : Environmental Portfolio , who is also a volunteer at Muizenberg Improvement District : Environment , says they have seen an increase in mosquitoes . " Yes indeed we in **26;1669;TOOLONG Da Gamma ( everywhere there are rainwater tanks ) started having mosquito issues when the first heatwave struck around a month ago . I spend a lot of time around Zandvlei conducting litter cleanups and had no mosquito bites until I got home . Then a few of my colleagues complained too so it got my curiosity piqued . <p> " I lived in Australia for 13 years and the issues of water tanks and mosquitoes is common knowledge , so much so that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ strainers on all inlets and outlets . It only took me five minutes to realise where the problem lay . So as soon as I opened the rainwater tank lid a huge swarm exited from the tank . Then when I looked into the water of the tank it was dark with larvae , ' ' he says . <p> Now Rack is urging residents to be careful as mosquitoes can be a health hazard . " Please note that mosquitoes do have the ability to transmit disease . Cape Town is clear of many of the major diseases , but due to climate change and evolution , when there is an excess of one species , disease can occur . Let 's not be caught out by the poor management of the drought , but rather be proactive citizens and manage the problem ourselves before there is another disaster . <p> Below are some interventions by Rack to help the situation . <p> Proactive prevention : <p> . Water tanks : Seal the lid with gaffer tape and plastic . Use a ball of netting in both the inlet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are removable . <p> . Gutters : Make sure there is no standing water in your gutters and they are cleaned of all debris ( fire prevention maintenance ) . <p> . Pools and ponds : Drain them of all water . <p> . Bird baths : Change all water regularly . <p> Chemical solutions : <p> . Do not add any chemicals to water tanks used for drinking water . The best natural way to kill the larvae is to starve them of oxygen . <p> . Oil : Olive oil or vegetable oil creates a skin on top of the water , starving the larvae of oxygen . Use 1 cup/250ml . <p> . Apple cider vinegar : A great non-toxic method to kill larvae and can be used as a repellent . Use 15% to 85% water .
