<h> Government relaxes Western Cape water restrictions <p> Following good seasonal rainfalls , the Department of Water and Sanitation has decided to relax water restrictions from for urban and agricultural use in the Western Cape . <p> According to Rashid Khan the Department 's Regional Head of the Western Cape , water availability at is currently much healthier than a year or two ago which is why national government has decided to relax water restrictions by 45% to 10% for domestic use and 60% to 10% for agricultural use . <p> Khan notes that the decision to relax water restrictions is based on the hydrological data that was recently updated by the department through a scientific model . <p> " The model shows that reducing water restrictions will bring the system into balance and it will then be operated to ensure security of supply to both urban and agricultural users , " he explains . <h> Ensuring uninterrupted water supply <p> Khan says the reduction of restrictions is residual and is also aligned to climate change impacts . <p> " It must be noted that these restrictions apply to bulk water .
