Picture : Bloomberg <p> Ambitiously branded as ' the premier forum for international economic cooperation ' , the annual summit of the G20 leaders , held this year in Buenos Aires on November 30 and December 1 , seems to have lost much of its clarity and promise . <p> The ' Group of 20 ' -- finance ministers and central bank governors of 19 countries and the European Union -- represents 85% of global GDP , 80% global investments , 75% of international trade , and 66% of the world population . Its objectives are to develop global policies to address today 's most pressing challenges . In 2008 , for example , the G20 was seen to be influential in stabilising the world economy . <p> Pretty powerful stuff . <p> This year 's summit , however , was overshadowed by ' bigger ' issues -- from Brexit and the Russia-Ukraine incident ( Russian border guards fired on and seized three Ukrainian vessels days @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and tensions between Turkey and Saudi Arabia arising from the macabre incident in which journalist Jamal Khashoggi was murdered . <p> Blunders <p> Then there were the diplomatic gaffes , the most notable being French president Emmanuel Macron having to shake the hands of ground staff as no one had pitched up to meet him at the airport , and the ceremonial band launching into the welcome intended for president Xi Jinping of China when an advisor was the first to step off the plane . <p> While such matters grab headlines , the main focus of the 2018 summit was fair and sustainable development . <p> Ministerial meetings had been held during the year to discuss global developments in health , excess steel capacity , education and employment , the digital economy , agriculture , finance and energy .
