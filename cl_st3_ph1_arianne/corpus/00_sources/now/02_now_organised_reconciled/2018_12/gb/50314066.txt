<h> Power to the People : Best Irish Podcasts of 2018 <p> 2018 has been a great year for alternative media in Ireland . The podcast scene , in particular , has provided an important anchor in a year of political turmoil . <p> Beyond the spun headlines of rage and fury , simplistic panel set-ups for and against , the cat-fights of social media , podcasts have have created space for challenging conversations and depth of analysis . <p> They have provided journalists with a platform to tackle difficult questions , given all kinds of activists a voice , brought historical depth to current issues , and arguably done a better job than traditional broadcasters at getting to the heart of what matters on the ground . <p> Here are some of my highlights from the year ... <p> 1 .
