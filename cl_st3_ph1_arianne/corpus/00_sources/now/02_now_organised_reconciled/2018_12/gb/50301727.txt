<h> National Theatre of Scotland asks ' vital ' questions in 2019 season <p> VITAL questions facing Scots are central to next year 's productions from the National Theatre of Scotland , according to its artistic director and chief executive Jackie Wylie . <p> Twenty productions by Scotland 's " theatre without walls " will tour stages , schools and community centres across the country in 2019 and work by Scots artists will also visit the US and Canada following acclaimed runs here at home . <p> New productions include Thank You Very Much , Claire Cunningham 's look at personal acceptance through the phenomenon of Elvis impersonators ; Red Dust Road , the first stage adaptation of the best-selling memoir by Scotland 's Makar Jackie Kay ; and Interference , a trilogy of plays set in the near future and staged in a Glasgow office block .
