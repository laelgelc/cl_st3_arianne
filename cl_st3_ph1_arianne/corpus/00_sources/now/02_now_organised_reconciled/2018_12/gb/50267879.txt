<p> In total , 2017 saw 451 accidental oil and chemical releases , with more than half those reaching the North Sea marine environment . <p> The environmental report claims 2017 saw 34 fewer releases than 2016 and a five-fold decrease in the amount of accidental oil releases , down from 115 in 2016 to 23 tonnes last year . <p> The OGUK also said that accidental oil reaching the North Sea only accounts for 0.00003% of the 81 million tonnes of oil equivalent produced in 2017 . <p> The report added that the majority of accidental chemical releases ( 96% ) are " labelled as low hazard " chemicals . <p> Oil and Gas UK Environment Manager Katie Abbott said : " The UK Continental Shelf is a mature and complex basin , and the challenges that accompany the production of hydrocarbons here mean that the data outlined in this report are equally complex . <p> " While innovative technology is contributing to environmental performance , through enhanced oil recovery which includes produced water re-injection , and the reduction in associated gas flared , challenges remain in other aspects . <p> " We continue to appraise longer term @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ frequency , high mass incidents . <p> " As a major hazard and heavily regulated industry , continued engagement with regulators , government and the sector is key in supporting efforts to reduce environmental risk , ensuring continued safe operation . " <p> OGUK 's Health and Safety Report released in October claimed almost 100 hydrocarbon releases in the North Sea in 2017 . <p> Of the 255 reportable incidents -- the lowest on record -- 99 of those were oil and gas releases , a three-year upward trend . <p> " Major " releases with the potential for multiple casualties have plateaued at two per year since 2016 . <p> Sam Gardier , acting director at WWF Scotland , added that it was " time to see clear recognition from the oil and gas sector that they can not continue to fuel runaway climate change " . <p> He insisted the industry must " work with all partners to ensure a just transition that delivers a safe climate future and high quality jobs for all .
