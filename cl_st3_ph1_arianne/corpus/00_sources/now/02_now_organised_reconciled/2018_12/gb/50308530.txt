Will there be a General Election and will the SNP fight it with a manifesto pledge of seeking a second indyref ? <p> If they win , how will the UK Prime Minister respond and who will that PM be ? There is precious little certainty about right now and that feels like a very bad thing . Right ? <p> Well , it depends .
