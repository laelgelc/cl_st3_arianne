While skating , views of the city 's most famous landmarks such as the Shard and other towering buildings , are beautifully presented next to the rink , which are not only exceptional to skate alongside , but also make for great , scenic pictures ! The alluring tower which surrounds the ice rink is also lit up with blazing lights , projecting the overall , magical ambience of the setting . <p> Sessions run all day from morning until evening ( 11am -- 9pm ) available at prices ranging from ? 10.50 - ? 14.50 , depending on the ages of those skating ; sessions last 45 minutes and occur every hour . Penguin skating aids were also available for younger skaters ( or anyone ! ) who need extra ice-skating support .
