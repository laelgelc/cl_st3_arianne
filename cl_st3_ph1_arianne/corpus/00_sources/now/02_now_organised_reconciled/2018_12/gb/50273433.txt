<p> Foreign Office Minister with responsibility for Climate Change Mark Field attended the UN 's major climate change conference COP24 in Poland on 6-8 December . <p> COP24 brought together representatives from governments across the world along with experts from science , business and finance to advance work on tackling climate change . <p> During his visit , the Minister met with senior Ministers and officials from a range of partner countries , including China , Germany , Indonesia , and Poland . He also hosted a series of events at the UK Pavilion , showcasing British expertise and green innovation , including an event to celebrate the UK-Poland Partnership on electro mobility and zero emission vehicles . The Minister highlighted the need for all countries to demonstrate higher ambition in their international commitments , and pushed for a robust set of rules to underpin the Paris Agreement . <p> The UK is committed to sharing its expertise and help developing countries in the global challenge of tackling climate change .
