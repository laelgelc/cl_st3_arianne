<h> New plans revealed to protect Shoebury and Thorpe Bay from flooding <h> Views wanted over plans to protect a mile of coastline <p> The Shoebury community is being invited to have its say on a proposed scheme to protect the area from tidal flooding and coastal erosion . <p> Marine engineers have worked up four options for feasible coastal management schemes along the 1.2mile stretch of coast between Thorpe Bay Yacht Club and the World War II Quick Battery . <p> The aim of the scheme is to improve protection to residential properties , beach-front businesses , and critical infrastructure including roads , railway , and utilities services within the area . <p> The four proposals are maintaining existing defences , strengthening the base of the seawall with rocks , burying " geotextile " bags beneath the sand to raise the beach height and replacing ageing wooden groynes as well as recharging the beach with more sand . <p> Former controversial plans for a ? 35million , 7ft high wall to be built across Shoebury Common have not been resurrected <p> Helen Boyd , councillor for public protection , said : " This area is an important tourist @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ beach award and the Quality Coast Award .
