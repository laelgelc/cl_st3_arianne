<p> In 2010 , Apple fired up a truly giant data centre located in some open fields near the rural town of Maiden in North Carolina . Apple chose the site because land was inexpensive , the state provided tasty tax breaks and energy from nearby coal plants was cheap . But coal was key . As one of the world 's largest data centres , the iDataCenter required enough energy to power a small city . And the local utility , Duke Energy , had lots of excess capacity . <p> Fast forward eight years , and Apple does n't use any coal-powered energy at all .
