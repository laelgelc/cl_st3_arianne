<h> Breakdown of traditional family to blame for child issues , says Dame Esther <h> Breakdown of traditional family to blame for child issues , says Dame Esther <p> THE breakdown of traditional family units , with fewer grandparents , aunts and uncles around for children to talk to about their problems , is behind a surge in mental health issues among young people , Dame Esther Rantzen said . <p> Dame Esther , who has a home in Bramshaw in the New Forest is founder and president of Childline . <p> She said the decrease in extended family units , where children can talk about problems they may be unable to express to their parents , was a key contributing factor to the unhappiness of youngsters . <p> Young people were also growing up in a world that was far more hostile and worrying than when the charity opened in 1986 , she added , with everyone from Sir David Attenborough warning " about the end of the world " to school bullies adding to the pressure children face . <p> Childline said the number of counselling sessions mentioning anxiety in 2017/18 had more than doubled in two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2017/18 . <p> And more than a third of the counselling sessions provided over the phone or online in the last year were for young people experiencing problems with their mental and emotional health and wellbeing , the charity said . <p> Speaking at the launch of Childline 's annual review , The Courage To Talk , in central London on Thursday , Dame Esther said : " The question I 'm sure that everyone here is asking is why ? Why has this huge increase happened among young people ? " It would be comfortable to think that it 's not actually a real increase in mental health issues , it 's just that they are empowered to talk about it ... I do n't think that 's right . " Instead , she said much of the increase was because families today are far less interconnected , with more members living abroad or in different areas of the country from their relatives . <p> " I had an extended family around me -- grandparents , aunts and uncles -- and for the things I could n't talk about @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unconditionally because I was family , " she said . <p> " Families need to ask themselves if there is enough emotional support , whether they 're giving young people enough time .
