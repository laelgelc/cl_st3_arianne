<h> Letters from Westminster : ' 2018 has been a year of exceptional political turbulence ' <p> The arrival of a New Year always invites reflection on what has been and what may be to come . <p> It is impossible to avoid the conclusion that 2018 has been a year of exceptional political turbulence . The unresolved issues relating to Brexit have dominated our news to the exclusion of almost all else . <p> And the closer we have got to the date of our departure , so the complexities involved and the divergent views on them have become more apparent . <p> All this takes place against the background of an increasingly dangerous world , whether it be conflict in the Middle East or the the Salisbury poison attack which has highlighted Russia 's ruthlessness and aggression and which is a growing threat to our security . <p> There is also , as I pick up from my mailbag , anxiety over many matters ranging from climate change to economic growth . <p> Fortunately however our country , for all its shortcomings , remains a resilient and attractive place to live and to which to give our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ basis the willingness locally to work together to grow our economy and to help those in need is outstanding . We are fortunate in what we have and capable of building on it for our common good . <p> So my message as we come to the New Year is that if we engage with each other constructively , even when we may disagree , we have the capacity to do great things . <p> It is the miracle of a free and democratic country that out of debate and difference can come progress and the capacity to overcome any challenge . <p> I would like to take this opportunity to wish all my constituents a happy and prosperous New Year . <h> Ipsoregulated <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
