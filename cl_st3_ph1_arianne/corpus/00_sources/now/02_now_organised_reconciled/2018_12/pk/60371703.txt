<h> Naeem Ahmad <p> Climate-Smart Agriculture ( CSA ) can be defined as a technique of reorienting and transforming agricultural development with accordance to the new fact of climate change or Agriculture which sustainably enhances productivity , increases resilience ( adaptation ) , removes or reduces GHGS ( mitigation ) when possible or enhances procurements of national food security and development goals . <p> The principal goal of CSA is food security and development while productivity , adaptation , and mitigation are identified as the three interlinked pillars necessary for achieving this goal . The need for shifting towards CSA ( Climate Smart Agriculture ) practices in due to climate change . Climate change is a radical change in weather condition on long term basis . <p> For example , it is change in the temperature , precipitation of a specific place from one decade to the next decade . The previous decade of 20th century and current decade of 21st century has registered as warmest period in the overall global temperature records .
