<h> Pakistan suffered losses worth $384m because of climate change <p> In this photograph taken on September 28 , 2015 , a general view of Passu glacier is seen in Pakistan 's Gojal Valley . PHOTO : AFP <p> Pakistan suffered a loss worth $384 million last year due to extreme weather events , according to the Global Climate Risk Index 2019 . <p> The report was released at the ongoing climate change summit COP 24 in the Polish city of Katowice . <p> More than 526,000 people have died across the globe as a result of 11,500 extreme weather events in 2017 . Economic losses due to climate change , between 1998 and 2017 , amounted to around $3.47 trillion . <p> On a global scale , Puerto Rico is at the top of the chart and five Asian countries feature among the top ten worst-affected countries due to extreme weather events , Chinese state-run TV reported . <p> " In 2017 , Puerto Rico and Dominica were hit by ' Maria , ' one of the deadliest and costliest hurricanes on record , " the report said .
