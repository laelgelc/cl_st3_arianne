<h> Indonesian tsunami <p> The deadly tsunami that struck Indonesia over the weekend has killed close to 400 , injured some 1,459 and left 128 missing . And it has now been confirmed that it was provoked by a chunk of the Anak Krakatoa volcano plunging into the ocean . <p> Experts had previously contended that since such events occur with little no indication -- no early warning system would be sufficiently quick to respond to such disasters . Yet that is not entirely true . For Indonesia is home to mechanisms that detect earthquake-triggered tsunamis . Tragically , this is lacking when it comes to those generated by volcanic eruptions . It is therefore hoped that this will remedied at the earliest . Especially given that Anak Krakatoa had been spewing lava and ash for months . This is not to mention the presence of inhabited areas perilously close to the volcano . <p> Indonesia sits on the geologically active Pacific Ring of Fire and is regularly hit by earthquakes as well as tsunamis . Indeed , the Boxing Day tsunami of 2004 remains one of the deadliest natural disasters in recorded history ; killing an estimated 227,898 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 9.1-magnitude earthquake , the third-largest ever recorded and boasted the longest duration of faulting ever observed ; lasting between 8-10 minutes . Since then , quakes and tsunamis have claimed 123,000 lives in Indonesia . <p> Unfortunately , in this part of the world citizens are praised for their ' resilience ' in the face of natural disasters . Whereas demonstrating such steadfast courage is not the job of ordinary folk . And buying into this false narrative simply deflects responsibility from particular governments of the day . Pakistan has borne witness to this when devastating floods struck in 2010 ; killing close to 2,000 and displacing some 20 million . Back then , local media talked of the nation 's bravery and the ability of the people to pick themselves up and carry on . When , in fact , the focus should have been on early warning systems and the impact of climate change . <p> It is therefore expected that the aforementioned will be urgently taken on board by the Jakarta authorities . It is the least that the Indonesian people deserve . *
