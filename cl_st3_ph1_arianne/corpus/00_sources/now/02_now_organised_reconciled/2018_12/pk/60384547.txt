<h> Poetry in the presidency <h> Islamabad <p> Islamabad : A colourful ' Mushaira Ghalib-e-Naam awaram ' was held in the presidency . <p> Earlier this month , when President Arif Alvi opened ' Art for the Climate Change Exhibition ' at the Pakistan National Council of the Arts , he announced holding of cultural celebrations and tributes at the presidency . This then perhaps was the beginning of chain of events which would take place in the Presidency in the weeks and months to come . <p> The ' Mushaira ' , a tribute to Ghalib on his anniversary , held at the presidency was arranged by the Ministry of Information , Broadcasting , National Heritage and its allied departments including Pakistan National Council of the Arts . President Arif Alvi was there in person along with Fawad Chaudhry ( Minister for Information &amp; Broadcasting , National Heritage ) and Omar Asad ( Minister of Finance ) . <p> Fawad Chaudhry briefly spoke pointing out that culture has been given a back seat in the state affairs but time has come that it should get its proper place and recognition . However , Fawad Chaudhry and his team were determined to go ahead with or without financial grants . <p> And now the poets and their poetry . The event glittered with galaxy of prominent poets from Karachi , Multan , Faisalabad , Lahore , Islamabad and elsewhere . The names ranged from Iftikhar Arif to Amjad Islam Amjad to wit and humour of Anwar Masood . And there were many other notable names who came out in their best with tribute to Ghalib . <p> Shields were presented to all poets by the President and now it was time for President to grace the occasion with his words .
