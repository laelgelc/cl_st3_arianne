<p> Steps being taken for welfare of Pakistani workers in Gulf countries , NA informed <p> G+ <p> December 10 , 2018 <p> The house was informed that the government is taking several steps for well-being of Pakistani workers in the Gulf countries . <p> Responding to a question in the National Assembly during question hour , Minister of State for Communication Murad Saeed said Pakistani Missions abroad are providing legal assistance to resolve issues of Pakistani Diaspora . <p> Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs Ali Muhammad Khan informed the House that a total of 2041 families comprising over ten thousand Temporarily Displaced Persons are still residing at Baka-Khel Camp . <p> He said currently the camp is used as a transit hub of TDPs who fled to Afghanistan and it will be de-commissioned as soon as areas of their return are declared safe and repatriation of these families commences . <p> Parliamentary Secretary for Foreign Affairs Andleeb Abbas told the House that Pakistan has been continuously raising the issue of Pakistani prisoners detained in Afghanistan . <p> She said it is regrettable that Afghan government has not provided information about the number of Pakistani nationals imprisoned in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on a MoU for transfer of offenders . <p> Andleeb Abbas said Pakistan Embassy and Consulates in Afghanistan , through other sources like International Committee of Red Cross and released prisoners , get information about Pakistani detainees and extend all possible assistance . <p> She said fifteen agreements and MoUs relating various sectors were signed between Islamabad and Beijing during the recent visit of Prime Minister Imran Khan to China . <p> She said the focus of MoUs is on enhancing cooperation between the two countries in socio-economic development , agricultural cooperation , poverty reduction , strengthening trade and economic relations and expanding , ties in Science and Technology , Climate Change and Security fields . <p> The House unanimously passed a resolution expressing its commitment to undeniable rights of every individual and urged upon the government to ensure the fundamentals rights guaranteed by the constitution of Pakistan . <p> The resolution , moved by Minister of Human Rights Dr. Shireen Mazari , also called for freedom of speech including freedom of press . It said the House believes in a dire need to reduce poverty and hunger in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ implementation of all obligations related to social and economic wellbeing of the people . <p> The resolution said all political parties resolve to work together for prosperity , interfaith harmony , minorities ' rights , women empowerment , sustainable development and peace . <p> It said both sides of the House also resolve to strengthen democratic values , rule of law and human rights while reducing corruption , injustice and extremism in the country . <p> Minister for Human Rights Shireen Mazari has said the government is committed to resolve the issue of missing persons . <p> Responding to a point of order in the National Assembly , she said no democratic society permit abduction of citizens in the darkness of night . <p> She said all the fair demands of Baloch and Pashtoon people , minorities and other oppressed segments should be accepted and stressed the need to adopt an inclusive approach in this regard .
