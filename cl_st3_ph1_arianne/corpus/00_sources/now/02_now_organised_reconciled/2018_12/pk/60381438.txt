<h> ' Zigzag ' kiln technology introduced to reduce emissions <h> Lahore <p> Islamabad : Ministry of Climate Change has introduced a ' Zig-Zag ' kiln technology to enhance fuel efficiency , energy savings and reduction in emissions up to 70 per cent . <p> The technology has been introduced in coordination with Environmental Protection Agencies ( EPAs ) , National Energy Efficiency &amp; Conservation Authority ( NEECA ) and Pakistan Brick Kiln Association as part of Climate Change Division initiatives to addressing issue of smog in the country . <p> Official sources on Wednesday said the Ministry in collaboration with NEECA is organising on-site awareness-cum-training sessions in various cities . In Punjab , more than 1000 kiln owners and people associated with brick kiln sector participated in training sessions . <p> Highlighting the other steps taken for addressing issue of smog , the sources said 10 billion Tree Tsunami initiative has also been inaugurated by Prime Minster on September 2 , 2018 .
