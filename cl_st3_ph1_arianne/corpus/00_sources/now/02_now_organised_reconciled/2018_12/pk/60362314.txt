<h> Govt. to set up Water Regulatory Authority soon : Senate told <p> ISLAMABAD : Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul on Tuesday said the government has decided to constitute a Water Regulatory Authority to regulate ground water resources as the country was facing an acute shortage of potable water . <p> Responding to a motion moved by the Senator Mir Kabeer Ahmed Muhammad Shahi to discuss the ecological/environmental change arising out of Climate Change and their overall impact on the country , she said her ministry has already started work on the formation of regulatory authority in collaboration with the provinces . <p> She said in the absence of any regulatory mechanism , groundwater resources were being depleted and contaminated through boring tubewells . <p> " No one will be allowed to establish new tubewells and bores without approval , " she said . Because of urbanisation and mushroom growth of industries , drilling out underground water continued unchecked that resulted in the depletion of water . <p> She said that Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) government has initiated Clean and Green Pakistan initiative as per vision of Prime Minister Imran Khan to tackle the issue of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the list of ten countries which have been badly affected by the climate change . <p> The remedy to mitigate the impact of climate change undoubtedly lies in planting more and more trees , she added . <p> About six to ten degree centigrade temperature can be reduced in different parts of the country by planting trees following the international concept of urban forestry , she added . <p> She said the federal government had devised a mechanism for coordination with the provinces to curb deforestation and increase forestation in the country . <p> The minister said that Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf has successfully completed trees plantation drive in Khyber Paktunkhwan province . <p> She regretted chopping of trees in Lahore has increased the pollution in the city .
