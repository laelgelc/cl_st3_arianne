<p> Xie made the remarks at the High-Level Forum on South-South Cooperation on Climate Change held in the China Pavilion at the UN Climate Change Conference in Katowice , Poland , reports People 's Daily . <p> He noted that China has implemented proactive national strategies on climate change , striving for a fair and reasonable global climate governance system that features win-win cooperation . <p> China will keep donating goods and materials , supporting the establishment of related policies , and promoting climate-friendly technologies under the framework of South-South Cooperation , so as to offer capital , technologies and capability building for the least developed countries , small island countries and African countries , Xie stressed . <p> The special representative also suggested that countries should enhance mutual trust , give play to synergetic effects , and build bridges for communication , in order to further strengthen international cooperation on climate change .
