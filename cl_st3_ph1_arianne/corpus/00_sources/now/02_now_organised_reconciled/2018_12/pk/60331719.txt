Both countries are bound in diplomatic , political social and economic relations spreading over decades , which are constantly gaining strength with the passage of time . The CPEC is a symbol of Pakistan and China 's time-tested economic and strategic partnership . " The project promises opportunities for the people of Pakistan and the entire region as well .
