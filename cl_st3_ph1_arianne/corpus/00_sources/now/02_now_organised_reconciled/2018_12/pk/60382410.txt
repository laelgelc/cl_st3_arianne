<h> Scientists study effects of climate change on Pacific coastline <p> Four researchers at Scripps Institution of Oceanography at the University of California San Diego have earned state grants to study how climate change is shifting conditions on the Pacific Coast . <p> They were awarded 1 million U.S. dollars from the California Ocean Protection Council ( OPC ) to study topics ranging from saltwater bass populations to coastal cliff erosion , according to a release of the institution . <p> They will create maps , models and other data sources that will help chart the changes to California 's coastline , and guide strategies to deal with them . <p> The grants of 250,000 U.S. dollars to each scientist are part of a larger project to fund research on topics such as sea-level rise and coastal resilience , marine pollution , and renewable energy . The four scientists will lead their teams to focus on respective projects . <p> Jennifer Smith , an associate professor of marine biology , is building 3D images of rocky intertidal zones to study how sea-level rise will affect the wealth of marine life in tide pools .
