<p> ISLAMABAD : President Dr. Arif Alvi Tuesday stressed upon creation of mass awareness about the impacts of environmental hazards and climate changes , observing that art could play a significant role by making people conscious about such challenges . <p> He also called for sharing the collective responsibility to tackle the issues of environmental and climatic changes . <p> The President was addressing a an art exhibition ceremony titled " Art for climate change " arranged by the Pakistan National Council of Arts ( PNCA ) here to highlight artists ' endeavours for preserving the natural beauty of the country . <p> Dr Alvi regretted that scenic valleys and pristine environment of the northern areas and spots like Naran etc , were being ravaged by the human activities of felling trees and contaminating the environment with the construction of residential units . <p> He recounted how Naran appeared to offer during the 60s and 70s and said now , the human activities were also posing threat to the famed Fairy meadows of Gilgit Baltistan . <p> The existential threat to the human lives that climate change posed required immediate action by all the stakeholders , he stressed . @ @ @
