<p> ISLAMABAD : Under Prime Minister 's initiative of Green Pakistan , National Highway Authority ( NHA ) has launched a campaign through which different types of trees have been planted all along motorway projects of NHA as well as the existing NHA network . Total targeted plantation was 187,000 plants during 100 days , however , due to weather constraint , the plantation drive was stopped by 20 Oct . 76,105 plants were planted , now plantation will resume in February as weather permits , an official of NHA told APP on Wednesday . <p> He said that other initiatives taken during plantation drive were beatification of Golra round about , plantation on M-1 , M-2 and airport road junction Islamabad through M-2 concession . NHA and Khyber Pakhtoonkhaw Forest Department have planned through an MOU plantation on major national highways from February 2019 , he said . <p> NHA with the assistance of Ministry of Climate Change under 10 billion tree campaign will plant 2.6 million trees along existing national highways network and on all upcoming mega projects , he said . <p> Khyber Pakhtoonkhaw Agriculture department has agreed to provide NHA with 11,000 Olive trees which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ February 2019 , he said . <p> Moreover under Clean and Green Pakistan initiative , NHA has earmarked on campaign for cleaning of road networks by removing wild growing grass , bushes shrubs , removal of garbage and debris from right of way of NHA roads . Under the drive , the NHA has completed cleanliness of over 4300 kilometers of road length .
