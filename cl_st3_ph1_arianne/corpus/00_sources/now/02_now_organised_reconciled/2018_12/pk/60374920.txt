PSDP ) 2018-19 for various ongoing and new schemes against the total allocations of Rs675 billion . <p> The released funds include Rs76.67 billion for federal ministries and Rs18.511 billion for special areas , according to a latest data released by Ministry of Planning , Development and Reform . <p> Out of these allocations , the government released Rs81.26 billion for National Highway earmarked Rs33.36 billion in its fiscal development programme 2018-19 . <p> Similarly , Rs2.78 billion have been released for Communication Division ( other than National Highway Authority ) for which
