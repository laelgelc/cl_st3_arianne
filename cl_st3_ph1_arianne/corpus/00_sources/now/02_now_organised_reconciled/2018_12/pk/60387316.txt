<h> Tree plantation Cartoon for Dec 28 <p> Sir : Tree cutting and less plantation is a real problem that is facing Karachiites . At the time of heart wave , many promises are made by the authorities but with the cold wind their commitments also blow . As different NGOs and individuals have made many campaign run-on their own but these are in sufficient and more steps should be taken in order to reduce the risk of climate change . <p> Planting trees are an essential component of environment as trees gives us a cooling and soothing effect and shades as well . It could save many lives and give pleasure , relief and instant gratification . Deforestation is a real threat to the ecosystem of our country . Serious efforts of tree plantation should be taken to reduce the temperature of our city . There is a dire need to promote forest culture as 80 percent of our land is covered with concrete .
