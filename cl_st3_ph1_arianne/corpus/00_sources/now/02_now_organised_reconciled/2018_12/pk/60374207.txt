<h> Pakistan 's Elected as Vice President of COP to UNFCCC <p> Adviser to Prime Minister on Climate Change , Malik Amin Aslam Khan on Friday said that at Conference of Parties ( COP ) 24 , Pakistan effectively presented its case during climate negotiations that despite least polluter and most vulnerable , Pakistan is committed to fighting climate change . This year owing to proactive participation and contributions in climate talks , Pakistan has been elected as Vice President of COP to UNFCCC , which is real change and success of this government , he added . <p> He said this while speaking at media debriefing on ' COP24 : Poland and Way forward for Pakistan ' , organized by Sustainable Development Policy Institute ( SDPI ) in collaboration with Ministry of Climate Change and Climate Action Network South Asia ( CANSA ) Pakistan here at Islamabad . <p> Malik Amin Aslam Khan , said that climate change is a stark reality for us and we can not ignore it . We have to adapt and prepare ourselves to fight climate change .
