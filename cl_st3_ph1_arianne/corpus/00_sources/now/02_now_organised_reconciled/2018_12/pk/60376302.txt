More specifically credits are defined as a tradable certificates/permits representing the right to emit one tonne of carbon dioxide or the mass of another greenhouse gas with a carbon dioxide equivalent to one tonne of carbon dioxide . By the same token , carbon penalties are imposed on those countries/companies that fail to meet the targets set .
