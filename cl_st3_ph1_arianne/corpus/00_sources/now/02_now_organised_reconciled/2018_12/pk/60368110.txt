<h> England has no eternal friends <p> " England has no eternal friends , England has no perpetual enemies , England has only eternal and perpetual interests . " Broadly applied , this timeless axiom of international diplomacy attributed to Lord Palmerston , British foreign sectary and later prime minister , is as true today as it was in the mid-nineteenth century when the British Empire was at its zenith . The doctrine dictates that unlike individuals , countries owe no enduring loyalties . <p> Applications of this abiding principle can be seen in many world events . Since the end of the Second World War , the US has had a shifting pattern of friendships and alliances in response to the changing political landscape . During the cold war , it led a block of western countries in a military alliance , NATO , to confront the Soviet Union and its allies in central and eastern Europe , countries bound in the Warsaw Pact .
