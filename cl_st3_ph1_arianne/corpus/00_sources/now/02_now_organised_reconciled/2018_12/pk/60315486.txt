<h> Shell sets carbon cutting targets after investor pressure <p> LONDON : Royal Dutch Shell caved in to growing investor pressure over climate change on Monday with plans to set short-term targets for reducing its carbon footprint . <p> BP and Total have already set short-term targets , but Shell Chief Executive Officer Ben van Beurden had previously resisted setting hard goals , saying it would be " foolhardy " to expose Shell to legal challenges . <p> But following discussions with investors , the Anglo-Dutch oil and gas giant said that from 2020 it will set three- to five-year targets every year which will include specific net carbon footprint targets . <p> Shareholders had criticised Shell for last year setting long-term " ambitions " to halve its emissions of carbon dioxide by 2050 , which lacked binding targets for implementation . <p> Shell , which did not specify any targets on Monday , plans to link these targets and other measures to its executive remuneration policy . The revised remuneration policy will be put to shareholders for approval at its annual meeting in 2020 . <p> The move comes as governments meet in Poland for a conference hosted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " rule book " to implement a 2015 climate accord . <p> The Paris agreement set goals to phase out fossil fuel use this century , shift towards cleaner energies and help limit a rise in temperatures . <p> Shell signed a joint statement with a group of 310 investors with over $32 trillion of assets under management , dubbed Climate Action 100+ , outlining the targets and review process . <p> " When it comes to meeting the demands of the Paris Agreement on climate change , we believe it is necessary to strengthen partnerships between investors and their investee companies to accelerate progress towards reaching such an ambitious common goal , " Peter Ferket , Chief Investment Officer of Robeco said in the joint statement .
