<h> IMF boss Christine Lagarde expected in Ghana on Dec. 16 <h> Christine Lagarde , the Managing Director of the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) , is expected in Ghana on December 16 , 2018 . <p> The visit comes weeks ahead of Ghana 's conclusion of the IMF programme . <p> Mr Pius Enam Hadzide who is the Deputy Minister of Information made this known at a press briefing and added that the visit of the IMF boss " marks an appreciation of the policy direction and solid economic fundamentals . " <p> " Our fiscal rules which ensure irreversibility signal to the world that Ghana is ready for investments , " he said.He explained that the visit of Lagarde " has the potential to unlock for us as a nation good coupon rates on the international bonds market . " <p> On Monday December 17 , Madame Lagarde will participate in the Future of Work in sub-Saharan Africa Conference in Accra . <p> The one-day conference would be an opportunity for the Managing Director to engage with thought-leaders and influencers from sub-Saharan Africa on the future of work . <p> The objective of the conference it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sub-Saharan Africa and the policies required to create jobs for the continent 's growing population . How the region can manage and leverage the impact of technological change , demographics , climate change , and the course of globalization . <p> The IMF boss will on the same day hold bilateral talks with President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo at the Jubilee House.She is also expected to have lunch with some Ghanaian women entrepreneurs who are doing well in their field of work . <p> On Tuesday December 18 , the IMF Managing Director will give a key note address at a forum in Accra to be attended by government agencies , central bank , private sector , think tanks , civil society , NGOs and academia . <p> She will depart Accra same day to Johannesburg in South Africa on a four Africa Nation tour . <p> " It is our hope that the visit of the IMF boss will further strengthen the cordial relationship between Ghana and the IMF on economic policies , " Mr Hadzide said .
