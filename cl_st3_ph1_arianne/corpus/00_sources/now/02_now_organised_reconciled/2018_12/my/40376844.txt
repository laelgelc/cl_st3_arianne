<h> 130 Lynas managers and staff protest at Parliament to save their jobs <p> KUALA LUMPUR : Some 130 employees from Lynas Corporation ( Lynas ) gathered in front of Parliament on Tuesday ( Dec 11 ) to urge the government to save their jobs . <p> This followed an announcement that the government wants the company to remove its radioactive waste or else the licence for the RM3.3bil plant in Gebeng will not be renewed . <p> Jumaat Mansor , a human resources manager representing the group of workers , said they have gathered to handover a memorandum in protest against the government 's threats to revoke the company 's licence if it fails to remove its 451,564 metric tonnes of Water Leech Purification ( WLP ) residue , which contains radioactive substances . <p> " We are representing Lynas workers , not the management . We want justice and job security , " he told reporters when met outside the Parliament . <p> Jumaat said the group consisted of supervisors , technicians , and other staff members .
