<h> PM : B10 biodiesel to drive green efforts and economy <p> PUTRAJAYA : Malaysia 's initiatives to use palm-based biodiesel as fuel will reduce stock and stabilise commodity prices amid attempts by others to restrict its export and sales , said the Prime Minister . <p> Tun Dr Mahathir Mohamad , who drove home the point when he arrived for the launch of the B10 biodiesel at the wheel of a Peugeot 508 , said such efforts would also be beneficial to the environment , lessening pollution and greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The Peugeot 508 uses the B10 biodiesel . <p> Dr Mahathir said he wanted the biodiesel programme to be given due attention in the National Automotive Policy currently being drafted by the International Trade and Industry Ministry . <p> Among the things to be considered was to only allow diesel vehicles that use more than 10% biodiesel to be sold in Malaysia . <p> " The biodiesel initiative is important because oil palm is our golden crop and helps generate the economy .
