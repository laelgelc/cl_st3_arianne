<h> Malaysians think one in two people are jobless -- survey <p> KUALA LUMPUR : Malaysians are way off the mark about the number of their countrymen who are unemployed and seeking work , according to market research company Ipsos Malaysia . <p> In its latest study Perils of Perception 2018 published yesterday , Ipsos Malaysia said on average , Malaysians estimate that half of Malaysia ( 49% ) are unemployed and looking for work when the actual figure is as low as 3% ( a 46% gap ) . <p> " Malaysians believe half of the population is currently unemployed and that the majority are going to be 65+ when we reach 2050 , " Ipsos Malaysia managing director Arun Menon said in a statement . <p> " This shows the extent of our overestimation of the actual situation , irrespective of the facts , " he said . There are many different reasons why people are far from accurate about social and economic realities , said Menon . <p> " These can include external influences on us , such as what we hear from the media or what is emphasised on online platforms .
