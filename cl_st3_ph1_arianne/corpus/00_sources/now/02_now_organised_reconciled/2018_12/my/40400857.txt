<p> KUCHING:Ipsos ' latest global study , which highlights our misperceptions about the economy , population , the environment and other key issues , has shown that Malaysians tend to be over apprehensive on these facts . <p> Ipsos ' Perils of Perception study shows which key facts the online public in Malaysia and 36 other countries get right about their society -- and which they get wrong . <p> Now in its fifth year , the survey aims to highlight how we 're wired to think in certain ways and how our environment influences our ( mis ) perceptions . <p> The study found that Malaysians did not quite get it right , and tend to be over apprehensive . <p> " Ipsos ' global Perils of Perceptions study shows that , around the world , people overestimate the real extent of social issues .
