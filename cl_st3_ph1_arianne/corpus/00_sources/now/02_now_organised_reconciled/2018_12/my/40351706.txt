<p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Dec 5 ) : The Parliament Caucus Committee Monitoring Lynas today called for the Australian-based corporation to " stop bullying Malaysia " and to take away the radioactive waste accumulated at its rare earth plant in Gebeng , Pahang , saying it has polluted underground waters at the site . <p> At a press conference in Parliament today , the committee supported the Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change 's ( MESTECC ) decision , which has set pre-conditions for Lynas Corp Ltd to remove its accumulated radioactive water leach purification solid waste ( WLP ) from Malaysia . <p> MESTECC 's decision follows the release of an executive committee report on the operation of the plant Lynas Advanced Materials Plant ( LAMP ) in Kuantan , Pahang . <p> Among others , the report found 451,564 tonnes of WLP waste are currently stored at the plant 's temporary storage . <p> " We regret and are disappointed with the findings of the committee of several breaches of certain heavy metals such as nickel , lead , chromium and mercury in the underground waters . <p> " This proves @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ caused pollution to groundwater , " said Chairman of the Caucus , Wong Tack , adding that Lynas must take back its wastes to Australia . <p> According to the report , the waste has been stored on-site since 2012 . The levels of heavy minerals in underground water is " worrying " , the report had noted , especially with regards to nickel and chromium . Around 12.5% of the people located in areas surrounding LAMP still use well waters in their daily lives . <p> " The Caucus supports MESTECC 's decision to call for Lynas to fulfill their promise in a letter of undertaking dated March 6 , 2012 , to the Atomic Energy Licensing Board ( LPTA ) , which underlined its commitment to return LAMP waste to its country of origin , " Wong , who is also the Bentong MP , said . <p> To recap , Lynas Corp Ltd executive chairman Nicholas Curtis wrote a letter to Raja Datuk Abdul Aziz Raja Adnan , LPTA director-general of the time , stating Lynas Corp Australia " gives a full undertaking to , if necessary @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ LAMP in Kuantan during its temporary operating license 's period ending September 2014 at the time .
