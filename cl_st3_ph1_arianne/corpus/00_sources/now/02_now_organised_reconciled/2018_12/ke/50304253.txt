<h> Migrants ' contribution to building blue economy <p> I am a migrant . I have been a migrant since my childhood . For various opportunities , from studies to work , I have spent more time overseas than in my own country of origin . I can also say that both my heart and my home are in more than one country . <p> As Chief of Mission of IOM ( the UN Migration Agency ) in Kenya , I was honored to be one of the partners of Kenya 's Ministry of Foreign Affairs for the Global Diaspora Forum , a side event in the recently-concluded Sustainable Blue Economy Conference in Nairobi . It was the first global conference on the sustainable blue economy , bringing together more than 17,000 participants from 183 countries . The participants learnt how to build a blue economy that harnesses the potential of oceans , seas , lakes and rivers to improve the lives of all , particularly people in developing States , women , youth and indigenous people .
