<h> Kenya Signs Kshs35 Billion Loan Agreement With Germany for Youth <p> Kenya and Germany have signed a Kshs35 Billion loan agreement which will go towards the financing of various projects in the country , with most emphasis placed on the youth . <p> According to the Treasury Cabinet Secretary , Henry Rotich , the funds are meant for projects under the Big 4 Agenda , which would provide technical skills to youth , women , and people with disabilities . <p> In doing so , Germany also promised to support Kenya in matters of good governance , decentralization , agriculture , irrigation , energy , health and climate change . <p> The Kenyan government , the private sector and development stakeholders all agreed that the majority of the population consisted of the youth . <p> Kenyan money <p> They pointed out that the youth needed to have more employment opportunities availed to them . <p> The deal between the two countries is a culmination of long-overdue negotiations , that would enable citizens to have access to previous and new German programs in the country . <p> In June , Mount Kenya University signed an agreement with Bonn -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cooperate in teaching , research , development and other areas . <p> Mt Kenya and Kenyatta Universities , were the only two African institutions in the four-year-long project . <p> The project was to be cascaded to other universities in Kenya and Africa in general . <p> Similarly in July , while bidding farewell to the outgoing German Ambassador to Kenya , Ms Jutta Frasch , President Uhuru Kenyatta , appreciated the support given by Berlin particularly in areas of the economy and governance .
