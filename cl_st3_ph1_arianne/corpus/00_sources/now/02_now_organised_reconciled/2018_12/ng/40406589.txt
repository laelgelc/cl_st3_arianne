<h> Rethinking Agro-Tourism In Nigeria <p> For long , people had been traveling to places outside their own homes and staying there for a while for leisure , business , or relaxation . That is simply what tourism is all about . <p> According to World Tourism Organisation ( UNWTO ) , " Tourism comprises the activities of persons traveling to and staying in places outside their usual environment for not more than one consecutive year for leisure , business and other purposes . " <p> There are different types of tourism based on the type of activity , location or duration of stay . Thus , you have such things such as cultural tourism , heritage tourism , geo-tourism , historical tourism etc . <p> What is then agro-tourism ? Agro-tourism in like manner can be described as all activities of farmers aimed at attracting , accommodating and entertaining tourists in an agricultural and rural setting for a fee . <p> Potentials <p> It has been long since Agro-tourism was discovered to have the potential of creating fabulous wealth for farmers . <p> A study conducted in 2004 by Agri Tourism Development Corporation ( ATDC ) , showed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ left in the villages . It also found that 97% of urban population wants to experience the rustic beauty of the village life . That shows that in agricultural activities there is a rewarding opportunity to develop agro-tourism centre in the villages . <p> Having recognised that farmer 's incomes are seasonal because of the seasonal nature of farming , while their expenses were regular , Shri Pandurang Taware , sought to find a way of making farming sustainable . After three years of research , he discovered that there was need for agro-tourism to generate incomes all year round to compensate for the regular expenses of farmers . Consequently , Taware in 2005 in Malegaon near Baramati in Maharashtra embarked on Agriculture Development Trust 's farm with a two-year project , which become very successful . <p> Thus in 2007 , the ATDC launched Maharashtra State Agri Tourism Vistar Yojana 2007 in which he selected 52 farmers to pioneer the Agri-tourism centres across the state of Maharashtra . In 2008 , he also recruited another set of 25 farmers making it 72 Agro-tourism centres currently operational in the state @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ under co-operative basis , the farmers started several agro-tourism development co-operatives in the area and they were successful . <p> The Songhai Farms in Porto-Novo , Republic of Benin is run on a similar principle of agro-tourism . <p> Songhai operates a kind of integrated farming system with sections for plant production , animal production , fish farming , all linked together in the primary production system with each being complementary to the other . <p> There is no waste there as the residues from after harvests and food processing are reinvested for further production . <p> There structure has other ancillary facilities for restaurants where organic food produced in the farms are served , hotel accommodation for visitors , swimming pool , chapel , conference and communication facilities , cars for hire , among other services they provide for tourists . <p> How It Works <p> The idea can be replicated here . One or a group of farmers can join hands to establish agro-tourism centres where they can embark on large scale poultry , fish farming , piggery , grass cutter and rabbit farming , fruit and vegetable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> There should be provision of facilities for hotel/lodging , recreational facilities such as field for games , swimming pool , children 's play centres as well as training facilities . <p> An agro-tourist centre offers visitors opportunity to try other things such as riding horses , tasting pure honey , savouring local dishes , picking fresh fruits and vegetables , visiting stands for local and regional produce and shopping in hand-craft gift items shops . Students of colleges and universities can take time off to visit to try out organic farming in a rural setting . They can as well share thoughts and experiences with farmers on indigenous farming methods , share indigenous farmers ' experiences on a number of issues such as climate change , global warming , crisis relating to ground water and arsenic pollutions , bio-fertilizers , among others . <p> Agro-Tourism Centres In Nigeria <p> There are some semblances of Songhai model of agro-tourism in Nigeria . We have some state initiatives such as in Abi , Cross River State , Abudu Cattle Ranch in Calabar , Avia in Lagos , Meruwa and Makera @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Enugu , Okomu Oil Palm Plantation in Benin , Nigeria , to mention but a few . However , they are not many and not as developed as the Songhai Farms of the Republic of Benin . <p> Farmers and those interested in farming should maximise the full potentials of agro-tourism such as growing multiple streams of income . It is reminiscent of Farm Settlements of old . It is a veritable means of opening up and developing rural areas , promoting entrepreneurship and sills acquisition in rural areas as well as stemming migration of youths from rural areas to city centres in search of few or non-existent jobs . <p> Corporate entities and farmers ' cooperatives should be interested in the development of agro-tourism in the country for it is one of the fastest ways to empower jobless youths , create sustainable wealth for farmers , ensure food security and improve health of the citizens . This has the ripple effect of diversifying the economy through agriculture . <p> The Federal and state governments and wealthy individuals should promote agro-tourism centres all over the country as a way of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of their entrepreneurial skills and training in various aspects of agriculture .
