Momoh stated this in his paper titled : " The Nigerian Power Supply Question : Challenges and Solution " , made available to The Guardian . <p> The NERC Chairman pointed that countries of Sub-Saharan Africa , due to their inability to provide the energy needs of their people , can not adequately provide health services , schools , clean water , food security and industries to their people . <p>
