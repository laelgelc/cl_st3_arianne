<h> 7.2 million Africans lifted out of poverty through IITA 's research innovations -- DG <p> Dr Nteranya Sanginga , the Director-General , International Institute of Tropical Agriculture ( IITA ) , Ibadan , on Monday said that in achieving its goals to raise over 11 million Africans out of poverty , 7.2 million had been lifted out of poverty . <p> Sanginga made this known in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Ibadan . <p> He noted that part of the goal of the institute was to redirect over 7.5 million hectares of under-utilised , marginal , degraded lands to more productive , sustainable use and increase yields of major crops by 60 per cent . <p> Sanginga said that in pursuance of the goal , IITA obtained about 895,000 ha of restored degraded land associated with the adoption of improved technologies developed by IITA and partners . <p> The director-general said that on-farm yield assessment showed that the adoption of improved varieties and good management practices by farmers resulted in 50 to 80 per cent yield grains in maize and soybean . <p> According to him , the institute 's Research @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Drought Tolerant Maize ( DTM ) in the dry savanna and excellent research on reducing the breeding cycle of banana and plantain contributed to the above goals . <p> Sanginga also noted that the liquidity status of the institute 's Business Incubation Platform ( BIP ) enterprises , measured by cash balances relative to the size of operating cash needs , had improved from 303 days to 94 days of actual sales . <p> He said that in addition to improved cash management , the BIP enterprises achieved operational profitability in 2018 . <p> Sanginga said IITA focused on exploring the synergies between R4D and Partnership for Delivery ( P4D ) , ensuring that it became a culture widely adopted in the institute to achieve agricultural transformation across Africa . <p> " The BIP signed agreements with three commercial private parties to invest in BIP . This resulted in technology transfer and commercialisation via companies , shared production and investments in inputs and equipment , " he said . <p> Sanginga said that in its achievement this year , IITA was awarded the Africa Food Prize in recognition of its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agriculture across Africa . <p> " In awarding the prize , the committee chairman , former President Obasanjo , recognized IITA for generating solutions on and off the farm that have improved the lives of millions in the face of climate change , surge of crop pests , disease and urgent need for youth employment across Africa . <p> " The prize received worldwide recognition during the World Food Prize award ceremony in Des Moines , Lowa , on Oct. 18 . <p> " The energy generated by the two events is being channelled into proactive engagement with our external and local partners and donors for resource mobilisation , ' ' he said . <p> Sanginga explained that IITA refined its strategy to direct youth from diverse academic disciplines who had completed their training toward agribusiness careers through the IITA Youth in Agribusiness ( IYA ) programme . <p> " IYA has become a high-profile mechanism toward strengthening youth participation in agricultural transformation through agribusiness establishment and growth . <p> " Between Nov. 2017 and Nov. 2018 , we completed three new studies on impact of soybean innovations in Malawi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ improved varieties in Northern Nigeria ( 1,525 households in 10 states ) . <p> " We also have the Adaptive Yam Minisett Technique ( AYMT ) in the yam belts of Ghana and Nigeria ( 1,400 households ) . <p> " We have numerous successes not mentioned here ; although the situation is still challenging , I am optimistic that we will make more important giant strides in 2019 , ' ' he said .
