<h> N/Delta : FG expresses concern over effects of climate change in the region <p> THE Minister of Niger Delta Affairs , Dr Usani Uguru Usani has disclosed that the preparation of the Document : " Niger Delta Region Climate Change Programme and Action Plan " was necessitated by the need to address the social and economic development of the Niger Delta region of Nigeria . <p> The Minister who was represented by the Permanent Secretary in the Ministry of Niger Delta Affairs , Alhaji Aminu Aliyu Bisalla made the remark at the side event of Cop 24 at the Climate Change Conference , Katowice , Poland 2018 . <p> Dr Usani said that the pace and effects of climate change in the Niger Delta are increasing and unless the trend is reversed , human as well as physical ecologies are bound to be seriously impacted as it is being presently observed in many of the communities in the Niger Delta region . <p> The Minister disclosed that the document which was launched in Calabar Cross River State , Nigeria , last month investigates and reports on the effects of Climate Change in the Niger Delta region , national @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ relationship with the Niger Delta . <p> According to the Honourable Minister , the essence of the document was to guide the Niger Delta region and other stakeholders on the implementation of collective measures to address climate change impacts and causes through adaptation , mitigation and other measures . <p> Dr Usani disclosed that some of the climate change programmes and projects in the document for poverty alleviation and sustainable livelihood include:restoration of depleted habitats and sustainable management of biological resources in the Niger , climate change resilience and vulnerability mapping , flood control and management and early warning systems , erosion control projects , shore protection , land reclamation , ecosystem reclamation among others . <p> He said that the set of programmes and action plan provide an integrated , harmonised , and multisectoral framework for responding and combating climate change in the Niger Delta through comprehensive adaptation , mitigation and other measures . <p> The Minister 's representatives thereafter presented the document " Niger Delta Region Climate Change Programme and Action Plan " to the international community . <p> Stakeholders , development partners , donor
