<p> The Federal Executive Council ( FEC ) has approved a contract for the construction of a national freight office in Jibia , a border town in Katsina state . <p> The approval was granted Wednesday during the meeting of the council which held inside the council chamber of State House Presidential Villa Abuja . <p> The meeting was chaired by Vice President Yemi Osinbajo , in the absence of President Muhammadu Buhari who is attending a climate change conference in Poland . <p> The Minister of Transportation , Rotimi Ameachi , who briefed State House correspondents after the meeting , said the contract was awarded at a cost of N551.8 million while the implementation is expected to last for 36 weeks . <p> " You know that the national freight office is usually located at border posts .
