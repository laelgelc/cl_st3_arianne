<h> Buhari rallies West African leaders against terrorism , climate change <p> President Muhammadu Buhari on Saturday called for stronger solidarity among West African leaders to tackle the challenges of crime , terrorism and climate change , which he said had negative effects on agriculture . <p> Addressing the 54th ordinary session of the ECOWAS Authority of Heads of State and Government , in Abuja on Saturday , Buhari said important decisions to address these realities should be taken in the course of the meeting . <p> According to him , the meeting of the ECOWAS leaders was a clear testimony of their collective resolve and commitment to the effective integration of West Africa . <p> He applauded the reported progress made by the community particularly in the areas of governance , peace and security as well as in the economic and social fields . <p> Buhari said , " It is a matter of concern that terrorism and violent extremism have continued to threaten the peace and security in our sub-region .
