<p> A statement by the Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Publicity , Mr Garba Shehu , quoted as Buhari citing rising temperatures , desertification , floods , low agricultural yields and drying up of water bodies as some of the manifestations of climate change . <p> For instance , the Nigerian President raised the case of the receding Lake Chad , North-East of Nigeria , where he said communities were already paying a huge price like fall in agricultural activities . <p> ' ' Obviously , no country can confront the phenomenon alone . In this regard , Nigeria believes in joint and cooperative efforts to tackle the problem . <p> ' ' We urge that efforts to address the challenges of climate change be pursued within multilateral frameworks . Concerted efforts should be made to strengthen sub-regional and regional organisations to serve as hubs for Climate Action and partnership . " <p> On the Lake Chad , Mr Buhari informed the summit that up to 40million lives were at the risk of extinction if it completely dried up . He expressed Nigeria 's commitment to rescue the lake . <p> The President stated that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ looked into the possibility of channelling water from the Congo Basin into the Lake Chad Basin as part of plans to rescue it . <p> Mr Buhari , on behalf of the member countries of the Lake Chad Basin Commission , commended the Italian Government for donating 1.5 million Euros toward the completion of the feasibility studies on the proposed inter-basin water transfer project .
