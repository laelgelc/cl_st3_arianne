<h> Al-Hikmah Varsity Graduates 876 at 8th Convocation <p> Al-Hikmah University , Ilorin recently graduated 876 students , just at 20 of the 798 first degree graduands bagged first class . <p> Of the total graduands , 61 bagged postgraduate degree , while 17 obtained sub-degree diplomas . Further breakdown of the undergraduates showed that 206 made second class upper division ; 421 graduated with second class lower division ; 144 made third class ; while seven earned the pass degree . <p> The university also awarded honorary doctorate degrees to three prominent Nigerians : the Katsina State Governor , Aminu Bello Masari and a former Minister of Transport , Alhaji Yusuf Suleiman were awarded Honorary Doctorate of Political Science and Public Administration ; while the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer , Max Air Limited , Alhaji Dahiru Barau Mangal was awarded honorary Doctorate of Business Administration . <p> In his remarks , the Vice-Chancellor , Prof. Ibrahim Taofeek , congratulated all the graduands and wished them success in their future endeavors . <p> He said the 14-year-old university thrives on its motto , ' Learning for Wisdom and Morality ' and urged the students to be good ambassadors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future . <p> He said the institution needs funding for more student hostels , library expansion , lecture theatres , among others . <p> The Chancellor , Alhaji Sayyu Dantata , who is the Chairman/CEO , MRS Oils Plc , to mobilise philanthropic support for the development of the institution . <p> While calling on well-meaning Nigerians and government agencies to support the university , he said no amount of money spent on education is too much . <p> Also speaking , the Pro-Chancellor and Chairman of Council , M.D Abubakar , urged the entire university community to be prudent in running the institution with dedication , honesty and the fear of Almighty Allah . <p> He added that curriculum of schools should be reviewed , that that universities should start training youths that would not only be self-employed but also be employers of labour .
