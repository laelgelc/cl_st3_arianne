In a world where we are constantly faced with powerful natural disasters ranging from devastating hurricanes , floods , wildfires ; as well as non-natural tragedies such as famine , illegal migrations , and local and regional conflicts ; which can all be correlated to the effect of climate change , it is always important that attention is given to the bigger picture , of which when we lose sight of it by allowing climate change implications to go unrestrained , then these devastating events will be more regular , more severe , and even more difficult to predict . <p> At the heart of these continuing climate change discussions , there has been a resolve to constantly build on existing knowledge and commitments of the last deliberations , and to also advance solutions and spread out cooperative frameworks .
