<h> Nigeria lacks political will to fight climate change -- Don <p> A professor of Environmental Health Toxicology and Aquatic Pollution in the Department of Pure and Applied Biology , Ladoke Akintola University of Technology , Ogbomoso , Oyo State , Solomon Adewoye , has blamed a lack of political will and institutional weakness for Nigeria 's inability to tackle the consequences of climate change . <p> Adewole spoke on the topic , " Climate Change Impact on Migration , Displacement and Social Conflicts in Nigeria : Historical Perspective , " at the Environmental Health Officers Association of Nigeria 's 50th Annual Scientific Workshop and 1st International Conference on Environmental Health in Lagos on Saturday . <p> He said despite the fact that Nigeria was a signatory to the United Nations on environmental sustainability and had developed many policies and programmes as well as received financial support and technical assistance from various international partners such as the World Bank , UNDP and CIDA , the country had yet to get it right . <p> He said , " None of these efforts has resulted in a significant fight against climate change . " <p> The don added , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ technical know-how , solid administrative structures and unwavering legal frameworks ; all which are not provided by the Nigerian government . <p> " All aspects of climate change , including the amelioration of consequences and prevention of future impacts must be holistically addressed to be able to fight effectively against it in Nigeria . <p> He also called for measures that would help to reduce the impact of climate change while simultaneously improving peoples ' surviving capability through sustainable agricultural practices , stopping encroachment of wetlands and floodplains , development of drought and heat resistant crops , construction of storm and flood barriers and strengthening public health . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
