<h> World Bank to Invest $200bn in Fight against Climate Change <p> The World Bank Group has announced plans to double its investments in the fight against climate change to around $200 billion for more than five years . <p> " Climate change is an existential threat to the world 's poorest and most vulnerable . These new targets demonstrate how seriously we are taking this issue , " World Bank Group President Jim Yong Kim said . <p> The investments , announced in a statement released Monday , will apply to the investment period for 2021-2025 , which would support countries in their efforts to take " ambitious climate action " . <p> The funds would be allocated for countries " to build better adapted homes , schools and infrastructure , and invest in climate smart agriculture , sustainable water management and responsive social safety nets , " said World Bank Chief Executive Kristalina Georgieva . <p> The announcement from the World Bank comes just before the opening of a UN climate conference at Katowice in Poland . <p> For two weeks , the delegates will focus on fleshing out the rules for financing and implementing the 2015 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for countries working together to limit the earth 's warming to between 1.5 and 2 degrees Celsius . <p> Most efforts to prevent the planet heating up relate to stopping the release of greenhouse gases as soon as possible . <p> However , the measures promised so far worldwide to reduce harmful greenhouse gases are far from sufficient to meet this goal .
