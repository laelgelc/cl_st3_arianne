<h> Nigeria Partners Israeli Firm for Methanol Production <p> As part of its incentive to boost local methanol production , Nigeria has signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with an Israeli firm , DOR Group , on methanol production technology . <p> The Minister of Science and Technology , Mr Ogbonnaya Onu and Mr Vincent Asor , Chief Operating Officer , DOR Group of Israel signed the MOU on Wednesday in Abuja , as a decisive step toward implementing the methanol production technology . <p> According to Mr Mohammed Abdullahi , Minister of State for Science and Technology , the federal government was determined to execute the terms of the agreement . <p> Mr Abdullahi , in a statement , said the signing of the MoU would change the fortunes of Nigeria significantly . <p> According to him , the methanol technology shall expand the chemical industry value-chain , replacing kerosene as household cooking fuel , and contribute to efficient and effective transportation . <p> He added that other advantages of methanol production technology were to mitigate climate change and improve electricity generation . <p> Mr Abdullahi further said the policy would create an enabling
