<h> Retaining nature 's beauty <p> It was about 2 o'clock in the afternoon when we took a tour of Horizon Hills in Iskandar Puteri , Johor Bahru . The sun was beaming ruthlessly , but there was still a refreshing breeze to provide some respite , making us thankful for the nature-rich surroundings . <p> Horizon Hills , a 1,228-acre freehold township jointly developed by Gamuda Land and UEM Sunrise Bhd , dedicates 35% of its land to green lungs and water features . To promote a low-carbon lifestyle , the developer has planted about 55,187 trees within the township to date . The trees supply fresh air and reduce the surrounding temperature for almost 25,000 residents living within and nearby . With greater awareness on global warming , this quality has become an important pull factor for people to live in Horizon Hills . <p> " One of our main tenets of sustainability is to be responsible in combating climate change , " Chief Operating Officer of Gamuda Parks Khariza Abdul Khalid notes . <p> Gamuda Parks is a sustainable landscape initiative launched by Gamuda Land in May 2018 to look after the design and maintenance of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a project in the township The Cove @ Horizon Hills also won Gold in the below 10 years non-strata residential category . The awards validate our dedication to good town-making , " Khariza says . <p> With a gross development value of RM7.2 billion , Horizon Hills will house about 6,000 residential units upon completion . Currently , it is 70% completed , with a total of 4,598 completed units and a commercial centre , a clubhouse and a 178-acre golf course . <p> An asset not to be sacrificed <p> When planning landscape at the township , the team is mindful against sacrificing nature for man 's convenience , Khariza shares . Among them are six vulnerable species trees and eight endangered ones as stated under the IUCN Red List . <p> " Not only would flora and fauna appreciate unadulterated nature , but people too . It has been proven that by staying in an environment with more natural elements such as greenery or water features , people are less susceptible to anxiety . There is also a link between greeneries and reduced risk of various illnesses , " Khariza elaborates . <p> " The flourishing of nature within Horizon Hills is an affirmation of the overall health of the landscape .
