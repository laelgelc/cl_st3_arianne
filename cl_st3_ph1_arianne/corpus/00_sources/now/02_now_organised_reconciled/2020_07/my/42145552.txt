But in 2020 , The Onion 's headlines just do n't have the same wit that they used to . Is it because the writers are slipping ? No . It 's because 2020 is beyond parody . <p> We 're experiencing a year that most would agree is among the worst of our collective lives . The Covid-19 pandemic . The ensuing economic crisis . The repercussions of 2020 will be felt well past this cruddy year .
