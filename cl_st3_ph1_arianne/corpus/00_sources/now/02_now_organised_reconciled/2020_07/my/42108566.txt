The Guiana Shield is the oldest intact tropical forest in the world and must combat deforestation and mining in the drive against climate change . -- EPA pic , July 10 , 2020 . <p> COLOMBIA lost 159,000ha of forest -- an area the size of Brazilian megacity Sao Paulo -- to deforestation in 2019 , according to an official report presented yesterday . <p> Although considerable , it represents a 19% reduction in deforestation compared with the 197,000ha destroyed in 2018 .
