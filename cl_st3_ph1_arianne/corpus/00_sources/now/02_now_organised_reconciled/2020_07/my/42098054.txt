<p> BEACHES , clear blue seas , scorching temperatures and long days . Forget the Caribbean , your next summer beach holiday could be on the shores of Russia 's Arctic Ocean . <p> Temperatures at Nizhnyaya Pesha , some 840 miles ( 1,352km ) northeast of Moscow and just 12 miles from Arctic Ocean coast , reached 86oF ( 24oC ) in early June -- a disaster for anyone worried about the planet 's future . Further to the east and further inland , things got even hotter . <p> Russia 's state weather authority confirmed that the temperature at the small town of Verkhoyansk -- which sits about 70 miles north of the Arctic Circle and boasts the Pole of Cold District Museum of Local Lore as its only tourist attraction listed on Tripadvisor -- hit 100.4oF on June 20 . <p> Most alarming , though , is not the temperature itself , but the fact that this was n't an isolated incident .
