<p> On 23 March 2020 , the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) issued the draft Environmental Impact Assessment ( EIA ) notification , 2020 , which will replace the EIA notification of 2006 . <p> The Ministry has sought comments from the public for the draft , and the deadline for this is 11 August 2020 . <h> So , what is the EIA all about ? <p> It is a process by which major industrial , mining , real estate or public infrastructure projects are scrutinized for their potential adverse environmental impact and negative externalities before they commence . Its purpose is to establish a legal framework for regulating industries who wish to access or utilise ( and eventually pollute ) natural resources . <p> Every major development project has to undergo an EIA and obtain environmental clearance from the government before any ground is broken . Depending on the assessment carried out , the project either proceeds as proposed , is modified or ( rarely ) scrapped altogether . <p> In a column for The Indian Express , Satyajit Sarna , a lawyer , writer and member of the Green Justice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this process entails . <p> " The assessment is carried out by an Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) , which consists of scientists and project management experts . The EAC frames the scope of the EIA study and a preliminary report is prepared . That report is published , and a public consultation process takes place , where objections can be heard including from project-affected people . The EAC can then make a final appraisal of the project and forward that to the regulatory authority , which is the Ministry of Environment and Forests ( MoEF ) . The regulatory authority is ordinarily obliged to accept the decision of the EAC , " he writes . <p> It was in 1994 when the first norms of the EIA were notified under the Environment ( Protection ) Act , 1986 . This notification was replaced by another one in 2006 , and the one issued in March earlier this year seeks to replace that to make the EIA process " more transparent and expedient " . <p> However , if you ask environmental activists and business leaders , particularly the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of conducting the EIA has been lacklustre .
