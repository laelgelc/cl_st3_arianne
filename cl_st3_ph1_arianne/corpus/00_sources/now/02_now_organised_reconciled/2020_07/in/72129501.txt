<p> The world is no longer only talking about closing the loop ; it is also taking action at household , national and global levels to do the same . Children are marching in a quest to control climate change while adults try to balance mindful consumption and peer pressure-induced spending . The path is difficult and many find it easier to veer off into the business-as-usual scenario that has been the norm for many unsustainable decades . Commitment from individuals becomes very shaky ( and all the more essential ) as unconvinced governments put off taking the pledge to ring in the Green movement . <p> Even as the on-going COVID-19 pandemic highlights the need for pro-environment behaviour , humans are unfortunately coming across ( like always ) as a difficult species to teach green behaviour to . Trees are felled just because they are in the way or " do n't go with the look of " the house , solid waste is sent to the landfill just because it is time consuming to recycle , plastic is used just because it is easier to buy as compared to carrying a cloth bag , and kitchen waste @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " smelly " to compost . Even though there are scattered pockets of populace that have turned zero-waste with a vengeance , many of us have sadly forgotten our extremely sustainable ancestral roots and take pride in the " not-in-my-backyard " ( NIMBY ) approach as we turn the earth into a humongous dump yard . <p> Man is considered superior to all other beings on earth because of his highly developed brain . But does this brain always produce superior thinking ? Sadly not ! It , many a times , makes us cut off our nose to spite our face . And if this helps in furthering our game of one-upmanship , all the better ! A recent case in point is the story of an up-market gated community that decided more than a year ago not to send kitchen waste to the landfill and compost it in the premises instead . When the residents ' association voted in a new committee ( and a rival ego to boot ) , the agenda changed from doing something green to undoing the green already done . The superior brain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only of the uppermost uncomposted layer , were made to believe that it was merely a stinking heap of accumulated food waste that was attracting rodents aplenty . No one saw ( nor were they shown ) the many layers of black , nutrient-rich compost underneath , that nature had produced over the period of one year . The smart superior brains went into overdrive , hired labour , spent hard-earned money and got the heap of " rotting " waste ( " treasure " for those who understand it ) thrown out of the layout . NIMBY ironically had gone sour ! <p> It is indeed a tragedy that we have learnt nothing from the so called inferior beings that cohabit this earth with us -- from the majestic and powerful lion that hunts only when it is hungry and only enough to satiate its hunger , to the microscopic microbes that feed on our waste and convert it unselfishly to agricultural gold for us . The Earth can support mankind 's exploits only so much . After that , pandemics like the one we are currently in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in sustainable living . It is up to us to apply our superior brains and use these lessons to treat nature with respect , share its resources with all creatures and live amicably with each other because after all , there is only one life and only one Earth to live it on .
