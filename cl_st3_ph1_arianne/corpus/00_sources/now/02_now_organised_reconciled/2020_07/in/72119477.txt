But we 're also experiencing ' transition impacts ' from steps countries take to prevent further warming , such as transitioning away from coal . <p> Combined , these impacts of climate change create financial risks . For example , by damaging property , assets and operations , or by reducing demand for fossil fuels with the risk coal mines and reserves become stranded assets . <p> This thinking is becoming mainstream among Australian economists . These time-frames dovetail with scientific projections about when the world will see severe impacts and costs from climate change . <p> And climate change is likely to hit Australia particularly hard .
