<p> The Jammu and Kashmir government Thursday ordered the fast tracking of Environment Clearance Process in minor minerals mining operations across J&amp;K.The order issued by the Forest , Ecology and Environment Department and Industries and Commerce Department said in view of the acute and unprecedented shortage of key material for development works and challenging Covid-19 pandemic , it is hereby ordered that the Terms of Reference ( ToRs ) , Public Consultation and Environment Clearance , till whichever stage already obtained /completed in favour of the previous allottees of the minor mineral blocks shall be deemed to have been transferred in favour of the new allottees of e-auction process , on the same terms and conditions of the TORs , Public Consultation and EC . " The new allottees shall complete the remaining elements of the EC/CTO process and shall commence mining operation only after the completion of the due procedure , " the order said.Quoting a reference invited to the amendment made by the Ministry of Mines , in the provisions of the Central , Mines and Minerals ( Development and Regulation ) Act , 1957 in January , 2020 .
