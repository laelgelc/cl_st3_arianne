<h> SPECIALCORRESPONDENT <p> JAMMU : Concern is growing in Jammu over the use of life threatening Chinese Manja-a polymer made kite flying string-despite ban on its procurement , stocking , sale and use by the administration . Sources told STATE TIMES that the availability and use of the hazardous Manja is not possible without the active connivance of police . " How come the nylon strings are available in the market despite restrictions " , said a conscious citizen , adding that it ca n't happen without the ' tacit approval ' of police at lower levels . He expressed apprehensions about loss of lives this year in particular , saying the children forced inside their vicinities due to closure of schools in view of COVID-19 will be tempted to indulge in kite flying ahead as a pastime ahead of ensuing Raksha Bandhan and Krishna Janamashtami . In fact , he said , the Jammu skies remain hovered with thousands of kites , some of these gliding down due to ' pecha ' by adversaries . Sources alleged that the Chinese Manja being seized during raids finds way into the homes of police officers to pamper their children or get reverted back to the clandestine market for a price . The charges being serious in nature , the Jammu citizens have been pitching for investigations into the alleged complaints about nexus and connivance of police in hazardous business deals . The administration seems to be doing pretty little in checking the use of hazardous Manja , which has consumed several precious lives in the City of Temples as also across the country besides slitting numberless birds . The public awareness too is awkwardly poor , which is why the use of Manja trends high despite hate for use of Chinese goods , more so in the aftermath of the martyrdom of 20 Indian soldiers by Chinese troops in an act of treachery . The use of Chinese Manja stands banned by the National Green Tribunal on the grounds that it posed threat to life and the environment . In 2013 , the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( then known as the Ministry of Environment and Forests ) issued a notification , urging the chief secretaries of all states and union territories to take appropriate action with regard to the use of Manja , while the Animal Welfare Board of India -- which operates under this ministry -- has called for an outright ban on the deadly string . Jammu Civil Society had , in fact , launched a vigorous campaign to generate awareness against the killer Chinese string by roping in school children and youngest a few year ago .
