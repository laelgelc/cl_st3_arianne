<h> Rs 1710 crore received from user agencies ; 13902 hectares of forest land diverted for projects <p> JAMMU , July 3 : Nearly eight months after re-organisation of Jammu &amp; Kashmir , a proposal is being worked out to distribute compensatory afforestation funds between Jammu &amp; Kashmir and Ladakh Union Territories , documents reveal.According to agenda note of 25th meeting of executive committee of Jammu &amp; Kashmir Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority ( CAMPA ) , a copy of order said that a proposal for distribution of money received from user agencies between the two UTs is being worked out for submission to the National Authority set up under CAMPA . " With the reorganization of the erstwhile state of Jammu and Kashmir ( J&amp;K ) into the UTs of Jammu &amp; Kashmir and Ladakh w.e.f. 31 October 2019 , the separate J&amp;K and Ladakh CAMPAs have been established . Therefore , the money received by the erstwhile J&amp;K CAMPA from user agencies on account of diversion of forest lands for non-forestry purposes is to be distributed between the successor J&amp;K and Ladakh CAMPAs , " reads the note.The move comes after authorities of Jammu and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ CAMPA , New Delhi on January 23 . The proposal is being prepared in consultation with Finance Department of J&amp;K and UT Government of Ladakh.As many as 13902 hectares of forest land has been diverted for non- forestry purposes in J&amp;K from 1990 to March 2020 . So far user agencies have deposited a Rs 1710.44 crore with the J&amp;K CAMPA for using forestland.Of this amount , 1424 .19 crore have been deposited from projects falling in J&amp;K UT and Rs 286.26 crore from Ladakh region.According to the note , Rs 1298.3 Crore ( which includes Rs. 286 crore from projects in Ladakh region ) has been credited to adhoc CAMPA , Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change Out of the interest on the principal amount of the deposit , the adhoc CAMPA has released Rs 379 crore.Out of the principal amount deposited by J&amp;K CAMPA into the account of Adhoc CAMPA ( Now National Authority ) the authority has transferred Rs 408.35 crore into State Fund in public account of UT of Jammu and Kashmir vide their communication dated 07-02-2020.
