<h> With state under lockdown , J&amp;K FAC issues record forest clearances <h> Last Updated : Friday 18 October 2019 <p> The Forest Advisory Committee ( FAC ) of Jammu and Kashmir ( J&amp;K ) gave clearances to 125 projects involving diversion of forest land between August and October 2019 . <p> In its 117th meeting , the FAC cleared 41 projects . This was also the last meeting of the body , before the forest clearance process shifts to Chandigarh , the regional office of the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) after October 31 , when the presidential order abrogating Article 370 will come into force . <p> " In the 117th meeting held on October 17 , 2019 , 41 projects were cleared . In the 116th meeting , 54 projects were cleared and in the 115th meeting , 30 projects were cleared by the FAC , " Manoj Kr Dwivedi , commissioner secretary to the forest , environment and ecology department of the state of J&amp;K , told Down to Earth ( DTE ) . <p> " These clearance recommendations will be sent to the J&amp;K cabinet and then @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ own FAC , under the Jammu And Kashmir Forest ( Conservation ) Act , 1997 . The committee is headed by the chief secretary of the J&amp;K government as the chairman , with officials of departments like forest , revenue , finance , soil and water conservation as members . <p> The committee makes recommendations , which are then accepted by the J&amp;K cabinet . <p> While Dwivedi declined to share the details of the area of forest land diverted or the nature of projects , media reports said that around 271 hectares of forest land was diverted in the 117th meeting for various development projects like laying of transmission lines , drilling of tube wells and others . <p> The number of clearances given in the last three meeting of the FAC is more than the total number of clearances given in 2018 . Under it , forest land diversion takes place on the resolution of the cabinet , based on the recommendation of the state FAC . <p> However , objections are being raised to the diversions in the absence of the application of the Scheduled Tribes and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers ( Recognition of Forest Rights ) Act , 2006 in the state . <p> " Diversion of forest land without the consent of local communities and gram sabhas cause violation of their forest rights . This move comes at a time when there is a larger concern among the local communities over withdrawal of the constitutional protection of democratic rights including special protection to their land rights , " Tushar Dash of Community Forest Rights-Learning and Advocacy ( CFR-LA ) , a forest rights advocacy platform of activists and experts , told DTE . <p> We are a voice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
