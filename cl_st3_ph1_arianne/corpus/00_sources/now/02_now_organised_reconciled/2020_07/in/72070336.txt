<h> Govt to come up with SOP for shooting films in India in wake of COVID-19 pandemic : Javadekar <p> Union Minister for Environment , Forest and Climate Change and Minister of Information and Broadcasting Prakash Javadekar briefing newsmen about the cabinet decision , in New Delhi on Wednesday .
