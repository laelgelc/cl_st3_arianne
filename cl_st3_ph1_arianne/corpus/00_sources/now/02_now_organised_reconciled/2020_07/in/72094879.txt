Off Season by James Sturm <p> How can something so seemingly simple be so moving ? That question crops up often as you follow the story of Mark and his estranged wife Lisa . Set in an America just before the rise of Donald Trump , it holds a mirror to how political choices now have the power to tear families apart -- a frightening possibility that has begun to dawn in India over the past couple of years . Does Mark choose a candidate as an act of resistance against a world that seems unfair ? The answer may help us understand the twilight zone India is currently in.
