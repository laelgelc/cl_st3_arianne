<p> WASHINGTON ( Reuters ) - Hopes that the rise in average global temperatures by 2100 might be capped below 2.5C can be all but ruled out if greenhouse gas emissions continue at the current rate , new research reassessing the atmosphere 's sensitivity to CO2 suggests . <p> The study , under the Geneva-based World Climate Research Program , offers the first clear progress in decades toward narrowing the range of temperature rise caused by doubling of carbon dioxide levels since pre-industrial times . <p> Its findings show that doubling would trigger 2.6 to 4.1 degrees Celsius in average warming above pre-industrial levels , putting the lowest rise more than one degree above scientists ' previous estimated range of 1.5-4.5C . <p> " To put that in perspective , we 're on track to double CO2 at our current rate of emissions by around 2080 , " said co-author Zeke Hausfather , a climate scientist at the Breakthrough Institute research center in Oakland , Calif . <p> " Climate change is about as bad as we thought it was .
