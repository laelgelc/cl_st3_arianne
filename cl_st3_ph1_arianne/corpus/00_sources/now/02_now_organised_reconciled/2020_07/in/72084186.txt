<h> Adelli-Boath road will benefit people of two districts : Indrakaran <h> The Minister was speaking after laying the foundation stone for works pertaining to black top the stretch at a cost of Rs 8 crore , at Adelli village in Saranga mandal . Boath MLA Rathod Bapu Rao was also present . <p> Minister Indrakaran Reddy waters a sapling which was planted by him on the premises of Sri Pochamma temple at Adelli village in Sarangapur mandal on Wednesday <p> Nirmal : Minister for Forests and Environment Allola Indrakaran Reddy on Wednesday said that people of both Adilabad and Nirmal districts will benefit in many ways with the construction of a road between Adelli village and Boath via Raghunathpur in the district . <p> The Minister was speaking after laying the foundation stone for works pertaining to black top the stretch at a cost of Rs 8 crore , at Adelli village in Saranga mandal . Boath MLA Rathod Bapu Rao was also present . <p> Indrakaran Reddy said people of Boath , Adilabad and Nirmal Assembly constituencies have been demanding the construction of the road for many decades . He said that their dreams were coming true @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ travelling distance between certain parts of Boath and Nirmal segments would be reduced by 40 kms once the road is completed . <p> The Minister said the facility would strengthen bonds between Nirmal and Boath constituencies . Devotees of Boath region could easily visit the famous Sri Pochamma temple in Adelli village , with the help of the facility . They could not throng the shrine though the village was situated on the borders of the two constituencies . He instructed officials concerned to complete the works at the earliest . <p> Indrakaran requested citizens to plant saplings as part of Telanganaku Harita Haram . He underlined the need to protect forest cover . He appealed to the public not to fell the trees and cause damage to the environment . He expressed concern over climate change . He said that Adilabad was registering maximum temperature around 48 degrees due to declining forest cover . <p> Earlier , the minister and Bapu Rao visited the temple and performed special prayers . They later planted saplings on the premises of the shrine .
