<p> The 300 mg / Nm3 limit was according to a December 2015 notification by the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) . <p> The Ministry of Power had proposed revising it up because meeting 300 mg / Nm3 and below was not possible at varying load . The Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) was asked to examine it further by MoEF&amp;CC earlier . <p> To verify the claim of load-affecting emissions , CPCB in collaboration with National Thermal Power Corporation Ltd ( NTPC ) monitored emissions at seven units of four thermal power plants between February 13 and April 2 , 2019 . <p> Five of them complied with NOx emission standards of 300 mg / Nm3 at full load . Some units did not comply during partial load operations even after combustion modification . <p> Combustion modification is a NOx-control technique where no new investment is required to set up pollution control systems and marginal reductions can be achieved .
