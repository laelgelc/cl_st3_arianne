<h> Daily Court Digest : Major environment orders ( July 3 , 2020 ) <h> Last Updated : Friday 03 July 2020 <p> Maintain Kudlu Chikkakere lake : NGT <p> The National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) in a July 1 , 2020 order directed the commissioner of the Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike ( BBMP ) to remove illegal construction in the buffer zone of Kudlu Chikkakere lake , just south of Karnataka 's Bengaluru . <p> Compensation should be assessed and recovered according to norms in addition to following due procedure , the NGT said . The work would be overseen by a secretary of the Urban Development department . <p> A June 12 report by the Karnataka State Pollution Control Board ( KSPCB ) told the tribunal that the lake was filled with water . There was an increase in water collection in the lake observed during the previous inspection on February 4 as well , the report said . <p> The municipal corporation authorities stopped entry of sewage into the lake by creating a barrier to sewage channels / storm water drains joining the lake at three points . <p> Sewage was diverted from the lake @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with a 500 kilolitres per day capacity . The STP worked at the time of inspection and the treated sewage was discharged into the lake . <p> The report recommended that the municipal corporation 's commissioner should take action against illegal constructions in the lake 's buffer zone . <p> Necessary directions will be issued from the KSPCB office to the municipal corporation to stop the entry of sewage into the lake by providing permanent barriers at all entry points , the report said . <p> Preventing animal-vehicle collisions <p> The National Tiger Conservation Authority ( NTCA ) submitted a modified action plan July 3 for executing all mitigation measures on different road stretches of tiger corridors in Maharashtra for the protection of the environment and wildlife . <p> The modified action plan will be valid for three years . <p> A joint report by the NTCA and the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change prescribed structural and non-structural mitigation measures . <p> These include regulation of traffic , speed reduction measures , signboards indicating presence of animals , monitoring of vulnerable road sections and verge management @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trees located between a roadway and a sidewalk . <p> An application was filed in the NGT in response to a news report published in The Times of India newspaper titled ' String of new road projects in Maha to cut off tiger corridors ' .
