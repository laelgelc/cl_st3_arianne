<h> Tiger Conservation is classic example of Atma Nirbharta as envisaged by PM : Javadekar <p> New Delhi , Jul 29 ( UNI ) Saluting country 's front line forest personnel on the occasion of International Tiger Day , Union Minister for Environment , Forests and Climate Change Prakash Javadekar on Wednesday said Tiger Conservation is a classic example of Atma Nirbharta ( self reliance ) as envisaged by Prime Minister Narendra Modi . ' On this Global Tiger Day , I salute our front line forest personnel whose sweat and toil have taken the tiger on the assured path of recovery in India . ' Tiger Conservation is a classic example of AtmaNirbharta as envisaged by PM Narendra Modiji , ' Mr Javadekar said in his tweet . <p> He said Project Tiger was launched in 1973 with just nine tiger reserves . ' Today , India has 50 reserves having 2967 tigers . Tiger sits at the peak of the food chain and the increased numbers is a testimony of the robust bio-diversity , ' he maintained . Mr Javadekar has released the detailed Status of Tigers Report 2018 on Tuesday .
