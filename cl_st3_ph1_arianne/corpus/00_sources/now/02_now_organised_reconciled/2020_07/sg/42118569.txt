She said her ambition is to bring about " a step-change " in understanding the sustainability credentials of investment portfolios . <p> " Ultimately , we want to see robust portfolio-level impact reporting become something that all institutional investors adopt , and see a measurable shift in capital to support the transition to the Sustainable Development Goals , " she told Eco-Business . <p> Reflecting on her time in Asia , Pretorius said that when @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sessions she ran were on sustainable finance 101 .
