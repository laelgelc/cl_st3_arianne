<p> Young people in Singapore today may be even more aware of hard truths that are becoming harder . <p> They are leading the discussion on climate change , gender equality , race issues and economic equality , among many other matters . <p> These are issues our founding leaders have faced before . As idealistic young people , these leaders forged their ideas in the West , through the Malayan Forum . They improvised and adapted , leading the population in building Singapore into a thriving city state . <p> Today 's young people are no different . For this impossible nation to continue succeeding , they are discussing the hardest issues . The methods may have changed , but the spirit remains the same . <p> The result of the recent general election shows that the Singapore spirit of the 1950s and 1960s remains very much alive .
