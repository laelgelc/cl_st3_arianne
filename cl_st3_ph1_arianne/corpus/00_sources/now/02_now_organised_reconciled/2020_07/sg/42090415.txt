<h> Leadership in sustainability : Where does Singapore stand ? <h> Covid-19 has disrupted the global economy and upended life as we know it . In these unprecedented times , competent leadership has never been more critical , and this requires a deep understanding of sustainability issues . <h> Share <p> The pandemic is wreaking havoc on businesses and the world economy . The Asian Development Bank estimates that the financial cost caused by disruption related to the coronavirus spread could reach up to US$8.8 trillion ( S$12.5 trillion ) . <p> The virus has exposed fault lines in societies and put the spotlight on weak institutions that have failed to mount an adequate response for its people .
