<h> To disarm dangerous young men , government of Zamfara province is trying to use the allure of cattle instead of threat of violence <p> The problem of gun violence is most often associated with the United States , but Nigeria has been dealing with this problem also , albeit , of a local variety . For years , Zamfara province , located in the northwestern part of the country , has been witnessing raids by armed gangs who terrorize locals and take away their cattle . <p> These criminals do n't just carry away animals at gunpoint but often indulge in killing and kidnapping also . The government has deployed military but they too failed to end the menace . Now , the government has changed tack and is using incentives to lure people away from armed crime .
