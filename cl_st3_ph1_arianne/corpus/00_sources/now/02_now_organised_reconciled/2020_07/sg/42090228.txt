<h> South Korea to pump public money into new Indonesian coal despite green election pledges <h> A South Korean public institution has announced it will back a controversial new coal project in Indonesia . The move is at odds with election promises made earlier this year , say experts . In Jakarta , activists took to the streets to protest the decision . <p> A coal-fired power plant in Indonesia .
