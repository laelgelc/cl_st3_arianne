The term " Anthropocene " that is now applied to our age has given us a false sense of control over the environment . <p> Covid-19 has forced us to recognise that the Anthropocene signifies a relentless feedback loop in which our behavior unleashes chain reactions that accelerate both climate change and the spread of pandemics . <p> With no infrastructural or geopolitical boundaries between us and the natural world , neither nationalism nor protectionism can stop this process . <p> Related to this , we no longer have the luxury of ignoring tail risks -- the low-probability but high-impact events that show up on the margin of any probability distribution . First , the phased reopenings across North America , Europe , and Asia have been accompanied by social-distancing guidelines . <p> Given the population density of major cities in these regions , warm temperatures will not eradicate the virus on their own ; public-health instructions must be followed .
