<h> Governments are unwilling and market forces are not enough . Tackling a problem as complex as climate change in the post-Covid era requires a systemic policy approach , argues Roland Kupers . <p> Roland Kupers , author of the book A Climate Policy Revolution : What the Science of Complexity Reveals about Saving the Planet , says the time has run out for gradual , linear change ; systemic change is required to address climate change .
