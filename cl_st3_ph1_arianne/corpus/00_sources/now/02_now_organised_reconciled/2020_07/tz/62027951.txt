<h> SAT and Agriculture Ministry out to support training institutions <p> THE Sustainable Agriculture Tanzania ( SAT ) in collaboration with the Ministry of Agriculture has launched a special programme aimed to support public and private training institutions to produce graduates with enough knowledge , skills and expertise in climate change . <p> Dubbed ' Curriculum Implementation Support for Training Institutes ( CISTI ) ' , the three-year project is expected to benefit students and tutors from 29 agricultural Training institutes in the country . <p> According to Janet Maro , Executive Director of SAT , the project is aimed to ensure that every year ; more than 3,000 Tanzanian students acquire the necessary skills so as to spur sustainable farming in the country . <p> She said the project is carried out during 3 years from 2020 to 2022 and it is funded by the LED Liechtenstein Development Service and coordinated by the government . <p> She also said that the project also involves training tutors and garden-attendants on organic gardens ' management using natural botanical extracts so as to enable them impart the knowledge to their students easily . <p> " SAT is working closely with the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Services and Research ( DTER ) . DTER supervises , oversees , advises , and controls the quality of curriculum implementation in the Ministry of Agriculture Training Institutes ( MATIs ) , " she said . <p> According to her , the long-term impact goal of the CISTI project is to have competent technical personnel who work effectively to meet the demand of Tanzania 's agriculture sector , increase climate resilience and strengthen livelihoods of small holder farmers , thus alleviating poverty and food insecurity for sustainable development . <p> " At outcome level , the project purpose is to support public and private training institutions to produce graduates who are in line with the need of the country through successfully integrating and implementing organic agriculture , gender , environmental management , cooperatives and communication skills through the new training curriculum for agriculture on certificate and diploma levels , " Maro said . <p> She said that the CISTI project began as a pilot project from January 1 , to December 3 , 2019 .
