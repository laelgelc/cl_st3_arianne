<h> Unearth GBV incidences , journalists urged <p> MEDIA practitioners have been urged to invest heavily in unearthing , writing and reporting Gender Based Violence ( GBV ) incidences in communities , a move which will contribute immensely to the reduction of the actions in the country . <p> Speaking during a tele-media conference recently , the director of the country 's Transformative and Integrative Build Out for All ( TIBA ) , Malcela Lungu said that there are several violence acts which are yet to be unearthed and reported . <p> He stressed that the role of journalists is to be the instrument of change by writing or reporting on GBV issues , help people and authorities to make informed decisions about what is going on in their societies . <p> " We see that journalists have a very important role in raising awareness , so by using much of their efforts to unearth acts of harm will increase awareness to the public and thus enable them to act whenever they come across any kind of harm . The move will also facilitate authorities to take tough decisions on the acts , " she said . <p> She @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are starving of violence but they do not know what to do and where to go due to lack of accurate information and education . <p> According to her , the media can continue educating the public on the types of violence and their impacts so as to enable the public to take immediate measures when someone faces the challenge . <p> Lungu further urged all groups of people in the country to wake up and stop hiding violence acts for the benefit of the whole community . <p> " We should all together , everyone in his/her position stand up , condemn and speak out about violence acts , and this is where we can help those who are living a difficult life due to violence . Men have to also join the race , because I believe that there are some good number of men who do not support the actions , " she added . <p> She however said that in order to accelerate the fight against violence , the organisation implemented a project which is aimed to increase male participation in the GBV war . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where usually men meet to among other things have cups of drinks , discuss and share views on various issues including violence and gender equality . <p> " Use of coffee hubs is the first approach being used in Tanzania and I am sure that it will transform men 's perception about gender and will also produce male champions who will be in forefront campaigning for gender equality and fight against violence , " she noted . <p> Lungu further said that discrimination and violence was not only confined to women but children as well thus needed intensive efforts to fight it as it had adversely impacted the community and the country as whole . <p> TIBA is a local non-for profit organisation that works to promote gender equality and women 's empowerment and also strives to ensure access to education for all children , with a specific focus on the girl child and children with disabilities . The organisation moreover addresses the impact of climate change on women and youth , as well as sexual and reproductive health , amongst other issues .
