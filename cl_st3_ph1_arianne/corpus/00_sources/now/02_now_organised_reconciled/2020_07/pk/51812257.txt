<p> European Council President Charles Michel on Friday sought to offer concessions to countries across the EU in plans for the EU 's long-term budget and economic recovery , hoping to bridge differences between national leaders when they meet next week . <p> Michel , who will chair the first face-to-face meeting of European Union leaders since coronavirus lockdowns were lifted , proposed a smaller 2021-27 budget in a bid to make a mass economic stimulus more palatable to thrifty northern countries . <p> He proposed a long-term EU budget of 1.074 trillion euros - down from the European Commission 's suggested 1.094 trillion - and a recovery fund of 750 billion euros for pandemic-hammered economies , with two-thirds in grants and a third in loans . <p> " The COVID-19 crisis presents Europe with a challenge of historic proportions , " Michel said in a statement . " We are slowly exiting the acute health crisis . While utmost vigilance is still required on the sanitary situation , the emphasis is now shifting to mitigating the socio-economic damage .
