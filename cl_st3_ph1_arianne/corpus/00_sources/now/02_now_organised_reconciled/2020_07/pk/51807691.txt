<h> South Asia 's covid responses ; a comparison of India , Pakistan and Sri Lanka <p> As COVID-19 brought the developed world to its knees the challenge is far bigger for the developing world . Countries that are already struggling with their enormous populations , high rate of poverty , unemployment , impacts of Climate Change and security challenges of all types imaginable . South Asia as a sub-region stands in the same line . The impacts of the pandemic are likely to reverse the progress made by the region by multiple years , as a vast majority of the population is dependent on informal economy and impoverished while the states have little capacity to mitigate the socio-economic impacts . <p> India is the most affluent country in the region , yet it is one of those struggling with the gravest of challenges . The population of India an enormous 1.3 billion is roughly six times that of Pakistan , the second largest state in the region . With a much better state of economy and a relatively better health system than Pakistan , though not compatible with the developed world 's standards .
