<h> Pakistan 's tree-planting push has a sweetener : more honey <p> CHANGA MANGA:When authorities started planting millions of trees in eastern Pakistan 's Changa Manga Forest five years ago , the helping to boost the local bee population and honey production in the area . <p> As part of Pakistan 's efforts to offset the impacts of climate change by rehabilitating forests , conserving soil and improving water management , 3.5 million trees were planted on 6,000 acres ( 2,428 hectares ) in Changa Manga , known as one of the world 's largest man-made forests , near the city of Lahore . <p>
