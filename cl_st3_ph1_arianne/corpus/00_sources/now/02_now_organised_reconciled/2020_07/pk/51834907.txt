<h> International Day of the Girl Child : Obstacles galore continue to affect female education <h> Literacy rate for girls currently stands at just 55% <p> A child girl stands near her house on the occasion of International Day of the Girl Child . PHOTO : ONLINE <p> LAHORE : On the International Day of the Girl Child , adolescent females continued to face a plethora of problems when it comes to access to education as schools in Punjab were far away from their homes . <p> The problem is exacerbated by inadequate transport facilities , while economic conditions force families not to send their children , especially girls , to schools . <p> According to Punjab Gender Parity Report 2017 by the Punjab Commission on the Status of Women ( PCSW ) , the literacy rate for girls is 55% , lower that the average of the province at 63% . According to the report , Rajanpur had the highest rate with 39% of children out of school and 22% of these were girls . <p> The dropout and survival rate of girls in formal education also showed similar patterns with districts of South Punjab showing a dismal situation .
