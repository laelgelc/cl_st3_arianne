increasing demands of the country 's burgeoning population . <p> According to the National Annual Plan 2019-20 , during the last 71 years , the per capita availability of water in the country has come down to an alarming level of 935 cubic meters from 5,260 cubic metres . <p> If an effective water storage strategy is not adopted , the per capita water availability in the country would decrease to 860 cubic meters by the year 2025 . <p> It would further lower to 500 cubic meters by 2040 if measures are not taken to conserve water in the country . <p> According to international standards , a country must have 1,800 cubic metres of water per person , per year , while a level below than 1,000 cubic metres means that a country faces shortage of water . <p> Only 36% Pakistanis have access to
