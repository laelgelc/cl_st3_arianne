<h> IHC orders shifting of zoo animals to sanctuaries <h> Court hands control of Marghazar Zoo to IWMB , bars addition of new animals <p> ISLAMABAD : The Islamabad High Court ( IHC ) on Thursday directed to shift all animals in the Marghazar zoo to sanctuaries within a month , particularly the pachyderm Kaavan . <p> Starting with a hadith from Prophet Muhammad ( PBUH ) regarding the treatment of animals , the 67-page written verdict by IHC Chief Justice Athar Minallah clubbed three petitions together . The first was related to the relocation of seized Brown Bear , relocation of Kaavan , the elephant to a sanctuary and handing over the zoo from the Islamabad Metropolitan Corporation ( IMC ) to the Islamabad Wildlife Management Board ( IWMB ) and stopping the shooting of stray dogs as a mechanism to control their population . <p> Chief Justice Minallah said that 878 non-human species were being held in captivity in the zoo . <p> On Kavaan , the verdict read that the elephant had endured much pain in the ill-equipped zoo for a very long time now and that this should end . However , the MoCC did not keep its word either . <p> " Except for the petitioner board ( IWMB ) and some passionate and dedicated private individuals including the counsels for the petitioners , others appeared to be more interested in gaining control of the management rather than securing the wellbeing of the animals , " the order read . <p> Raising the question that " whether the animals , i.e. non-human living creatures , have independent rights or , is there a duty on the part of the human race , through the state and its public functionaries , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the court deemed it as illegal and unethical to confine animals in a zoo that was short of facilities . <p> The court directed the government to form a board under the IWMB chairman for making arrangements to move the animals to the sanctuaries in six days . <p> Further , the IHC ordered IWMB to take control of the Marghazar zoo . <p> Bringing in any new animals to the zoo was banned by the court until an international inspection agency declares it fit for keeping the animals . <p> Moreover , the government has recommended to include a chapter on animal welfare in its curriculum of Islamic Studies for matriculation students . <p> Published in The Express Tribune , May 22nd , 2020 . <h> Facebook Conversations <h> COMMENTS <p> Replying to X <p> Saved !
