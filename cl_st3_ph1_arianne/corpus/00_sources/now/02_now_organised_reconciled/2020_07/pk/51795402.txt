<p> PM announces establishment of 15 national parks in all federating units to protect environment <p> July 02 , 2020 <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan has announced the establishment of fifteen national parks in all the federating units as part of efforts to protect the nature . <p> Launching the ' Protected Area Initiative ' in Islamabad on Thursday , he said guidelines will be given to the provinces to preserve these national parks . He said this is a major initiative for our future generations . <p> He expressed satisfaction that all the provinces are participating in this initiative which will also provide job opportunities to the youth . <p> The Prime Minister pointed out that global warming and climate change pose serious threat to Pakistan . He said this is the reason we launched ten billion tree tsunami program to counter this threat . Protecting green areas and parks will also help in this endeavour . <p> Imran Khan directed the provinces to take the threat seriously and undertake the requisite steps for the protection of green areas . <p> The Prime Minister said Pakistan has a diverse landscape and has twelve ecological climatic zones .
