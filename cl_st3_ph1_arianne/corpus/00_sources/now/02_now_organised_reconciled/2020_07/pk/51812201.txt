<h> Beaconhouse launches its first fully virtual School of Tomorrow Conference <p> LAHORE-Beaconhouse Friday launched its first fully virtual School of Tomorrow ( SOT ) Conference : A World of Tomorrow : Negotiating a Better Future with a special video message from the Director General of the World Health Organization , Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus . " It 's an honour to join the opening session of the School of Tomorrow Conference . I want to recognise the government of Pakistan for its comprehensive response to COVID-19 , " Dr Ghebreyesus said , acknowledging Pakistan 's efforts in countering the pandemic and saying that the WHO is working closely with the Government of Pakistan . <p> The opening session of the conference , moderated by Beaconhouse CEO Kasim Kasuri , included Andreas Schleicher , the OECD 's Director General for Education &amp; Skills , Shafqat Mahmood , Federal Minister of Education , Dr Mishal Khan , a leading epidemiologist from London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine , the UK 's leading public health institution , and The Baroness Mobarik CBE , member of the UK 's House of Lords . <p> Shafqat Mahmood emphasized the importance of SOPs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ he suggested might take place on 15 September .
