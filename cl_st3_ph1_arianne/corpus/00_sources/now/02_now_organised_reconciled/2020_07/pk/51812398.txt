<h> 37% area of Pakistan vulnerable to locust attack <p> ISLAMABAD : The entire country is under the threat of an attack by desert locust but around 37% area of Pakistan is more vulnerable to the attack . This includes 60% area of Balochistan , 25% area of Sindh and 15% area of Punjab . <p> " If the desert locust is not contained , Punjab and Sindh may become summer breeding zone of the pest , " said a report submitted to the Supreme Court by Punjab government through Additional Advocate General Chaudhry Faisal Hussain . <p> It said desert locust appeared in Punjab 's Cholistan area in July last year . In November last year , locust swarms started migration toward Balochistan and South Punjab . <p> " So far crops over an area of 715 acres in Punjab have been damaged , " it said . <p> According to the report , the federal government is responsible to control desert locust but the province of Punjab has managed to contain locust despite limited resources .
