<h> Pakistan elected as president of UN 's Economic and Social Council <h> Share <p> Pakistan 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations , Ambassador Munir Akram , was elected unanimously as the President of the UN Economic and Social Council for a year ( 2020-2021 ) . <p> The 54-Member ECOSOC is the third principal organ , along with the General Assembly and Security Council , established in 1945 under the UN Charter to promote international economic cooperation and oversee the work of all international economic organizations . <p> This is the sixth time that Pakistan has been elected as ECOSOC President . This is the second time Ambassador Akram has been elected to this post , having served as ECOSOC President in 2005 . The only other Ambassador to have had this distinction was Juan Somavia of Chile , the former Director-General of ILO . <p> In his acceptance speech , Ambassador Akram outlined the objectives of the Pakistan Presidency . <p> He said today UN Member States face three simultaneous challenges : the COVID , health and economic crisis , the realization of the Sustainable Development Goals and the existential threat of climate change .
