" Accordingly , end-user markup rate on housing units measuring up to five marlas will be 5pc for the first five years and 7pc for the next five years . " <p> For housing units measuring 10 marlas , end-user markup rate would be 7pc for the first five years and 9pc for the next five years . <p> The subsidy would be given on units where the price of
