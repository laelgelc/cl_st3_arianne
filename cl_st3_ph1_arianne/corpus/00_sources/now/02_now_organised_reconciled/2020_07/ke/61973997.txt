Deciding what to do about it is the hard part . <p> Among the 18 declared candidates , there is no broad consensus on taxing polluters on their carbon emissions -- a measure most experts say is needed to slow global warming . And when it comes to building new nuclear power plants or adding federal regulations , there is even less agreement . <p> Those divisions were apparent in the candidates ' responses to a new climate policy questionnaire from The New York Times . They unanimously supported remaining in the Paris Agreement and restoring Obama-era policies that Trump has abandoned . But scientists are clear that preventing catastrophic climate change will require going well beyond those policies . <p> While the candidates agreed with that assessment , few offered detailed strategies for getting it done .
