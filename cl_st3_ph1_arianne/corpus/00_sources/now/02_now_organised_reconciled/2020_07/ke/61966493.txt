Research has shown that human beings are not only ' choking ' the environment with plastics , but themselves . <p> " Plastics eventually get into the food chain as animals and fish eat them , confusing them for food , " states UN Environment in a report.In Kenya , protected areas including national @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ most affected by plastic pollution .
