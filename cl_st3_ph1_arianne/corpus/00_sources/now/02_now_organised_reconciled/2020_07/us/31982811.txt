<p> A perfect storm of crises is forming across the United States . Above our heads , a " heat dome " of high pressure could blast 80 percent of the continental US with temperatures over 90 degrees for the next few weeks . This coming in a summer when the Covid-19 lockdown has trapped people indoors , many without air-conditioning -- and mass unemployment may mean that residents with AC units ca n't afford to run them .
