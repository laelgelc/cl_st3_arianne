<h> Oakton names its STEM Student of the Year <p> Oakton student Maryana Nissan of Niles was named Oakton Community College 's Honors Program STEM Student of the Year . Courtesy of Oakton Community College <p> Submitted by Oakton Community College <p> Posted7/3/2020 12:44 PM <p> Maryana Nissan of Niles credits Oakton Community College 's Honors Program for much of her academic success . <p> This fall , Nissan will attend the University of Illinois-Chicago , where she will major in neuroscience . However , she does n't plan on stopping there . Her ultimate goal is to ally herself with the humanitarian group Doctors Without Borders . <p> " Joining the Honors Program at Oakton was one of the best decisions I ever made , " Nissan , a graduate of Maine East High School , said . " The program ignited my passion for higher education . I learned the beauty of learning is that it never stops . " <p> The Honors Program at Oakton consists of small classes , dedicated faculty and motivated students . The program encourages the exploration of intellectual curiosity through research , seminars and more . <p> Kristin McCartney , co-coordinator @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Humanities , says that Nissan will go far in life because of her compassion . <p> " Maryana cares deeply for others , as well as our shared community . She was extremely active at Oakton while working over 20 hours a week in a doctor 's office and caring for her younger sister , " McCartney said . <p> " I lived in Syria until I was 10 years old . I know what it 's like to be without access to medicine . There are so many people out there who are underprivileged . The ultimate reward for me is to help others , as everyone has a right to medical treatment . " <p> Nissan is one of just 11 students who distinguished themselves by completing 18 credit hours within the Honors Program during the 2019-2020 academic year , earning the Honors Scholar designation . <p> This is the highest achievement possible within the Honors Program and requires an exceptional level of dedication to academic excellence . She also earned a 4.0 GPA this past spring . <p> In addition , she was named the Honors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Nissan earned recognition as a President 's Scholar and served as the vice president for the campus chapter of the International UNICEF organization , co-president of the Honors Student Organization and on a student panel to address climate change concerns . <p> " Oakton helped me step out of my comfort zone , allowed me to figure out who I wanted to be and shaped me into the person I am now , " Nissan says . <p> Visit the Honors at Oakton page on Oakton 's website for information about opportunities available to students through the Honors Program . <p> Guidelines : Keep it civil and on topic ; no profanity , vulgarity , slurs or personal attacks .
