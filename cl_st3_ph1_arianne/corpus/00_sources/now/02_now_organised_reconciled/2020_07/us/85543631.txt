<h> After three decades , most polluted U.S. neighborhoods have n't changed <p> ( Reuters ) - If your neighborhood was among the most polluted in 1981 , it probably still is . <p> Likewise , the least polluted areas are still faring the best , according to a study published on Thursday in the journal Science that analyzed concentrations of fine particulate matter over more than three decades in the United States . <p> Overall , pollution from fine particulate matter fell about 70% between 1981 and 2016 , the study found . In that time , air quality has improved dramatically across the country thanks to tighter air quality regulations , cleaner vehicles and fewer coal-fired power plants , experts have found . <p> But the communities most exposed to higher levels of pollution remain the same , the study found .
