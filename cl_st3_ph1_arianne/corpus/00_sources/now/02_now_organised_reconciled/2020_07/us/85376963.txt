<h> Ann Arbor schools begin solar roof installations on school buildings <p> ANN ARBOR , MI -- Whenever students return to Ann Arbor 's Pattengill Elementary for in-person classes , they 'll have a new education tool waiting for analysis on the school 's rooftop . <p> Ann Arbor Public Schools is in the midst of commissioning a 300-panel solar array installed atop the school that is expected to generate 100kW of solar power . <p> Ann Arbor-based Homeland Solar installed the panels , making Pattengill the first of five schools in the district to have solar power installed on its roof . <p> The project , which costs $194,000 and was paid for out of the district 's sinking fund , is expected to generate 150,000kW hours of power per year , Homeland Solar Managing Member Dave Friedrichs said . <p> Four remaining solar installations will take place at Huron High School , Haisley Elementary , Forsythe Middle School and A2 STEAM once bids are received for the projects in August , said Emile Lauzzana , Ann Arbor Public Schools executive director of physical properties . <p> " These solar projects are a great way for the district @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ community , " Lauzzana said , referencing the district 's commitment to green energy in its $1 billion bond proposal approved last November . " We 're really excited about that possibility , as well as demonstrating to our students clean energy solutions that will work in a climate change future for them .
