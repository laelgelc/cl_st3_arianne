Reena Rose Sibayan The Jersey Journal <p> There 's a census happening in Jersey City and it 's for one of the planets most vital lifeforms : trees ! Sustainable JC is beginning a climate change impact initiative for Jersey City in an attempt to restore the City 's tree canopy . <p> Sustainable JC , an organization that encourages community partnerships and neighborhood actions toward making Jersey City a more sustainable place to live and work -- with focus areas include waste streams , green infrastructure , and emissions reduction/energy efficiency -- is hosting the Jersey City Tree Mapping Census beginning with training on July 9 from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m . <p> Originally , Sustainable JC had planned to train folks during neighborhood walkabouts .
