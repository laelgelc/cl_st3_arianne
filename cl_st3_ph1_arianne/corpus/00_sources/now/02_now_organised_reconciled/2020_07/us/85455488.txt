<p> LONDON -- Before she sent scripts to the BBC , Michaela Coel sent mood boards . <p> Her series " I May Destroy You , " which airs on HBO in the United States , had been greenlit by the broadcaster without executives having " a clue " what she was planning , Coel said in a recent Zoom interview . So she collected images from the internet , put them onto PDFs and sent them to Piers Wenger , the BBC 's head of drama programming . <p> " I was trying to help him imagine what it might be , " she said , laughing , " but it 's only in hindsight I realize that I was just sending pictures .
