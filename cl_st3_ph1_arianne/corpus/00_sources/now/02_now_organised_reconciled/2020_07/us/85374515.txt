<h> UN forecasts even warmer temperatures over next 5 years <p> The annual mean global temperature is likely to be at least one degree Celsius above pre-industrial levels in each of the next five years , the World Meteorological Organization said Thursday . <p> The WMO said there was a 20 percent chance that it will exceed 1.5 C above 1850 to 1900 average levels in at least one year , according to its annual climate predictions for the coming five years . <p> The last five-year period has been the warmest five years on record , said the Geneva-based United Nations agency , which counts 193 member states . <p> Temperatures over the next five years , 2020 to 2024 , are very likely to be within the range of 0.91 C to 1.59 C above pre-industrial levels . <p> Almost all regions , except parts of the southern oceans , are likely to be warmer than the recent past , which is defined as 1981 to 2010 . <p> " This study shows -- with a high level of scientific skill -- the enormous further to 1.5 C , " said WMO secretary-general Petteri Taalas . <p> The WMO forecasts do not take into account changes in greenhouse gas and aerosol emissions due to the shutdown of economic and industrial activity triggered by the coronavirus crisis . <p> High latitude regions and the Sahel are likely to be wetter than the recent past , the World Meteorological Organization says <p> " Due to the very long lifetime of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere , the impact of the drop in emissions this year is not expected to lead to a reduction of CO2 atmospheric concentrations which are driving global temperature increases , " Taalas said . <p> The planet 's average temperature is already more than 1.0 C warmer than the pre-industrial period , the WMO said . <p> Looking at the five-year
