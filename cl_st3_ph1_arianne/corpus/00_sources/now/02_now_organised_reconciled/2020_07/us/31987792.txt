The COVID-19 pandemic is no exception . Researchers , universities , funders , philanthropies , journals , and journalists have all pivoted , en masse , to COVID-19 . Everyone is ' Covidized ' , and it should worry us . <p> Millions of dollars are being poured into COVID-19 research . Some funding agencies have done this at the cost of cancelling or delaying regular open competitions . Journals are dealing with a deluge of submissions on COVID-19 . The pace of normal scientific progress seems hard to justify in the middle of a global crisis .
