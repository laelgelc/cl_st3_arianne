<p> The Solid Waste Master Plan , required by statute to be published every decade since 1990 , is intended to be a framework for engaging in a deliberative planning process that explores short- and long-term programs to manage solid waste generated , recycled , or disposed of in Massachusetts by both residents and businesses .
