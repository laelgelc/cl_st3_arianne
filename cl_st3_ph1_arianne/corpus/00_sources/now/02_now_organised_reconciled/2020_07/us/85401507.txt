<p> As a 2007 graduate of Anderson High School , I am relieved to hear the results of the Forest Hills School District Board of Education vote to replace the mascot at AHS . While I believe it is important to acknowledge our history , it is imp serative to do so in a way that his honest and respectful . <p> The symbols we choose to emblazon our community institutions with reflect our cultural values at large and signal to us on an individual , subconscious level what is valued . Racial slurs have no place in civil society . I would like everyone to take this moment to reflect on the opportunity we have to shape the values that will reflect and inform the culture of our community through the selection of the next school mascot . <p> I am fortunate to have had the experience of living in Spain for two years , where I taught English in a state school .
