<h> State to procure 2,500 more megawatts of offshore wind power <p> New York State will seek bids for another 2,500 megawatts of offshore wind power . The move follows last year 's awarding of nearly 1,700 megawatts to two developers . Credit : Getty Images/Xinhua News Agency <p> New York State this year will seek bids for another 2,500 megawatts of offshore wind power , along with 1,500 megawatts of land-based renewable energy projects in what the state is calling the country 's largest clean-energy solicitation of its kind . <p> The new plans for wind , solar and other green energy projects follow last year 's awarding of nearly 1,700 megawatts of offshore wind to two developers , Equinor of Norway and Orsted of Denmark -- part of the state 's march toward some 9,000 megawatts of offshore wind for the New York grid by 2035 . LIPA has contracted for another 130 megawatts . <p> The combined 4,000 megawatts of new green energy , if built , will be enough to power some 1.5 million homes , the state said .
