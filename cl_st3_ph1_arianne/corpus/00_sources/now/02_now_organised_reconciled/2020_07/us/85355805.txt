<p> San Diego is trying to reduce chronic flooding in neighborhoods like Mission Valley , Sorrento Valley and the Tijuana River Valley with a comprehensive new plan to streamline the clearing of clogged stormwater channels across the city . <p> The plan aims to shrink the number of evacuations and the amount of property damage that residents and businesses typically suffer in San Diego during annual winter rains -- particularly in El Nino years with unusually heavy rainfall . <p> The Municipal Waterways Maintenance Plan , which the City Council unanimously adopted last week , provides a new approval framework for clearing channels that is more proactive and transparent , city officials said . <p> The plan also cuts through bureaucracy the city faces when a proposed channel-clearing must be approved by state and federal wildlife agencies and regional water authorities . <p> " We will be able to get work done quicker , which will help prevent flooding incidents and save money and staff time , " said Councilwoman Vivian Moreno . <p> The typical approval timeline will shrink from about two years to somewhere between 60 and 90 days , Moreno said . <p> The new plan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with sea level rise and other local impacts of climate change . <p> " As we face the climate crisis , it 's going to be critical that we put more emphasis on our waterways , " Council President Georgette G ? mez said . <p> The new plan was praised by businesses and residents across the city .
