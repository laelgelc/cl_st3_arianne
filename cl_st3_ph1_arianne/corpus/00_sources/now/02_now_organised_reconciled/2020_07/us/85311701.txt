Tom Cotton at the New York Times and Twitter signals serious danger for conservative and other dissenting views in the United States . And it is just one example among many . <p> The Left 's control of almost all the major outlets for opinion in this country -- major newspapers and magazines , television , Hollywood , social media , and university faculties , among others -- is a familiar phenomenon . But in the past , popular support for the constitutional guarantee of free expression under the First Amendment ( even among liberals ) was always a protection for the dissemination of conservative and other alternative ideas . <p> Now , however , a new group of progressives is moving into positions of power in all these news and cultural outlets , and they do not appear to acknowledge the rights of conservatives ( or even liberals ) to hold and express views that dissent from the progressive catechism .
