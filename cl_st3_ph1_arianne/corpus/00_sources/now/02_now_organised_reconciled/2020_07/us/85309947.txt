<h> Elise Gout <h> Research Associate <p> Elise Gout is a research associate for the Energy and Environment team at the Center for American Progress , focusing on climate policy . Prior to joining American Progress , Gout worked as a government contractor to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency , where she primarily supported projects in water quality , water security , and climate resilience . She has also researched the impact of natural disasters on vulnerable populations through her time at the Earth Institute and Sabin Center for Climate Change Law .
