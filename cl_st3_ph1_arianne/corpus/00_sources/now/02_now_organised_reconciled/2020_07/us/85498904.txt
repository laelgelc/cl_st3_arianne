<h> Letter : Protect the Tongass rainforest by preserving the Roadless Rule <h> Saturday <p> Jul 25 , 2020 at 4:10 AM <p> Situated in southeast Alaska , the Tongass National Forest is the United States ' largest carbon sink and is home to 31 different Alaskan communities . According to U.S. Forest Service research , the Tongass holds about 8% of all carbon stored in U.S. forests . It plays a key role in the prevention of climate change through carbon sequestration . <p> The Tongass is currently protected by the 2001 Roadless Rule , which prevents road building and industrial-scale clear cut logging in 38 states . The Trump administration wants to exempt the Tongass from this rule , putting our largest intact national forest in danger , as well as the communities that rely on it . <p> The loss of the Roadless Rule in the Tongass would result in a substantial increase of U.S. greenhouse gas emissions . Even in Ohio , we would feel the effects of the loss of this forest . <p> Drought caused by climate change becomes more destructive to our land and economy every year .
