<h> Japan rocket carrying UAE Mars probe ready for Monday launch <p> by Mari Yamaguchi <p> This June 1 , 2020 , file rendering provided by Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre shows the Hope probe . A Japanese H-IIA rocket carrying a United Arab Emirates Mars spacecraft has been placed on the launch pad for Monday 's scheduled liftoff for the Arab world 's first interplanetary mission , officials said Sunday , July 19 , 2020 , in Japan . The launch of the orbiter -- named Amal , or Hope -- from Tanegashima Space Center on a small southern Japanese island was initially scheduled for this past Wednesday , but was delayed due to bad weather in the region .
