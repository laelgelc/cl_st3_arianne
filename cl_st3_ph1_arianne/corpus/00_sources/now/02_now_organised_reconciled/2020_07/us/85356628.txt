<p> Any uptake in electric vehicle use must be mirrored by the development of clean energy grids to mitigate both climate change and air pollution . <p> That is the key finding of a new study by researchers at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory , California , and Stanford University , California . <p> Published today in the IOP Publishing journal Environmental Research Letters , the study examined the most beneficial vehicle fuel technology for transportation in the US , and the trade-off between decarbonization ( climate ) and air pollution ( health ) mitigation . <p> Co-author Professor In ? s M.L. Greenhouse gases disperse globally , stay in the atmosphere for decades to centuries , and their effects differ globally , but such an effect does not depend on where the emissions originate .
