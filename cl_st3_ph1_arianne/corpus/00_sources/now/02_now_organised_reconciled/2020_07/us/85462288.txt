<p> Monday 's 21% drop was triggered by an announcement on Friday that Nikola ( ticker : NKLA ) stock warrants -- which trade under the symbol NKLAW -- were exercisable . Tuesday 's gains are about the warrants again , but a couple of other factors are boosting shares . <p> Nikola warrants did n't drop on Monday , in fact they rallied 10.1% and were are up an additional 5.6% Tuesday . <p> The warrants are rallying because Friday 's announcement makes them less risky . Now a warrant can be immediately exercised , which means paying $11.50 to receive a Nikola share worth about $41 . Before the warrants were exercisable , holders had a little more risk , because they could n't lock in an arbitrage profit as easily as they can with exercisable warrants . <p> Exercising a warrant and selling the received stock implies a profit of about $1 now . Before they were exercisable , the implied profit was closer to $10 per warrant . That 's one way to express the increased risk . <p> Tuesday 's gains are due , in part , to a bounce from Monday .
