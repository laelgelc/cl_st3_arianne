Credit : Joe Mendoza/CSU Photography <p> Agricultural production is highly sensitive to weather and climate , which affect when farmers and land managers plant seeds or harvest crops . These conditions also factor into decision-making , when people decide to make capital investments or plant trees in an agroforestry system . <p> A new report from the U.S. Department of Agriculture focuses on how agricultural systems are impacted by climate change and offers a list of 20 indicators that provide a broad look at what 's happening across the country . <p> The report , " Climate Indicators for Agriculture , " is co-authored by Colorado State University 's Peter Backlund , associate director of the School of Global Environmental Sustainability . <p> Backlund said the research team started with the scientific fact that climate change is underway . <p> " We looked at the U.S. agricultural @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . These indicators offer ways to measure the impacts of change , " said Backlund . <p> 20 climate indicators , based on robust data <p> Pollinators and pollinator management are one of the 20 climate indicators in the report .
