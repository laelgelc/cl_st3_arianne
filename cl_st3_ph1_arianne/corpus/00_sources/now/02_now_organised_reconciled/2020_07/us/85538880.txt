<h> Should Banks Be Forced to Price In Climate Change ? <p> Since the 2008 financial crisis , regulators each year have required big banks to prove they could keep lending through new calamities . The rules , so far , have focused on purely economic disasters : Do they have enough capital to keep their doors open when markets collapse , or if unemployment explodes ? <p> But there 's another kind of risk that did n't burst onto the radar during that crisis but could trigger just as much of an economic disaster : climate change . <p> Advertisement <p> With increasingly severe storms , floods and fires , many forecasters envision a crisis that pivots from the physical to the economic .
