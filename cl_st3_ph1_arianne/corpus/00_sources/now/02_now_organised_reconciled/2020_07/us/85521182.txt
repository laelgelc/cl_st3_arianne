The world needs US resources , conflict resolution and democracy support . <p> Madeleine Albright and Stephen Hadley <p> Opinion contributors <p> With its staggering human toll , the COVID-19 pandemic already constitutes one of the greatest challenges America has ever faced . It is also a reminder to all Americans that what happens in far off corners of the world directly impacts their lives and livelihoods . <p> In the 20th century , we learned that two massive oceans were not enough to keep distant conflicts from coming to our shores . In the 21st century , we have learned that we can not rely on geography to protect us from borderless threats such as pandemic disease , terrorism and the impacts of climate change . <p> During these months of turmoil , U.S. leaders have been rightly focused on the economic and health crisis within our borders . Second , as members of the U.S. Institute of Peace 's Task Force on Extremism in Fragile States , we are deeply worried about the long-term impact of COVID-19 on conflict-affected areas around the world .
