<p> In Oliver Stone 's new memoir , he writes about when he first understood that his screenplay about a lost soldier in Vietnam connected back to the oldest tales of humankind . The protagonist of what was then titled " The Platoon " was like Odysseus in Homer 's epic , he saw , a young man who 'd traveled into hell and who would only find the path home if he opened his eyes to the truth around him . <p> It was a lesson Stone had retained from his days at NYU when a classics professor used to hammer home the message to the students . <p> " He used to pound it into the blackboard , ' Consciousness ! Consciousness , gentlemen ! Wake up , ' " Stone says recently from his home in Brentwood .
