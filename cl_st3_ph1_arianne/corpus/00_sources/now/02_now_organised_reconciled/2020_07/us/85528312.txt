Over time , this forest would march steadily northwards . <p> " The migration of a forest is communal , it 's constant . It is accomplished over many generations ... " St. George explains . " It 's a question of the species succeeding more in one part of its range , becoming more abundant in one part of its range , and less abundant in another part of its range . " <p> This has happened over millennia , and climate change tends to be the driving force -- pushing and pulling forests around the globe .
