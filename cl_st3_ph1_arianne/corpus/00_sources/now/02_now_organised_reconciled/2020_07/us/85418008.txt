<h> Burger King Unveils Whopper From Cows On Green Diet <p> For those seeking to tackle climate change and get a fast food fix , Burger King has the answer -- a Whopper from cows that fart and burp less . <p> The fast-food giant announced Tuesday that select restaurants in five US cities -- New York , Miami , Portland , Los Angeles and Austin , Texas -- would be serving Whoppers made from " reduced methane emissions " beef . <p> The chain says that adding just 100 grams of lemongrass leaves to a cow 's diet late in life could reduce their output of methane , a greenhouse gas responsible for global warming . <p> Initial study results revealed an up to 33 percent reduction in methane emissions from cows on the new diet in the last three to four months of their lives , Burger King said . <p> Burger King has unveiled a new low-methane diet for its cows in a move towards sustainability and to tackle climate changePhoto : AFP / Paul J.
