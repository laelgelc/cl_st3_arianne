' <p> The recent 6-3 Supreme Court decision in Bostock v. Clayton County was good news -- and we all need good news these days . The historic ruling means that lesbian , gay , bisexual , and transgender workers will no longer be carved out of protection under Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 , and sends guidance and a moral message across the country and across the law , beyond even its immediate and substantial legal effect . <p> For one thing , the victory brought urgently needed protection to workers in Pennsylvania , where the state legislature has year after year failed to pass a basic civil rights law to prohibit employment discrimination on the basis of sexual orientation or gender identity .
