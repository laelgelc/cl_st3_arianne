Demand has surged for everything from bikes and masks to flour and puppies as people shift the way they shop and behave . For some producers , though , securing the packaging to sell their products is proving the hardest bit . <p> In response to relentless demand for hygiene products , soap companies ramped up production and untraditional players such as distillers and perfume makers have piled into the market . Sales of hand sanitizer are up almost fivefold in the first half of this year , according to research firm Nielsen .
