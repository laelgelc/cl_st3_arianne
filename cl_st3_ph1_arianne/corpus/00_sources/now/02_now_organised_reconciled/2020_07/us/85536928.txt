<h> Record number of environmental activists killed in 2019 <p> CNN -- A record 212 land and environmental defenders were killed last year , equivalent to an average of more than four per week , according to a new report from NGO Global Witness . <p> The figure makes 2019 the deadliest year on record for activists defending land and water resources from mining , agribusiness and fossil fuel interests , said Global Witness in a report published Wednesday . <p> This represents a significant spike from 164 killings in 2018 , and the true number is likely far higher , said the NGO , adding that cases often go undocumented . <p> Defenders are those who take a stand against environmental and human rights abuses driven by the exploitation of natural resources , according to Global Witness . <p> Colombia was the deadliest country in 2019 with 64 killings -- up from 24 in 2018 -- and was 30% of the global total last year . <p> Next on the list was the Philippines , with 43 killings .
