He is , the Trump camp would have Americans believe , really just a radical in moderate 's clothing . <p> It 's a strategy that 's as likely to succeed as the the campaign 's discarded salvos against " Sleepy Joe Biden " or " Joe Biden : China 's best friend . " <p> But it must be said that the Trump team 's latest attack contains an element of truth . Biden is not a socialist . He 's not a radical . But he is running on the most liberal policy platform of any Democratic candidate in modern American history .
