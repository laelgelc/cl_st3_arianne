An asset manager , since it is a single institution with a single corporate view of the world , is likely to focus on only a narrow set of priorities . <p> Surely the financial health of the corporations held by a manager is best secured when there is adequate space for independent voices to contribute their views which may dissent from the conventional wisdom of the day .
