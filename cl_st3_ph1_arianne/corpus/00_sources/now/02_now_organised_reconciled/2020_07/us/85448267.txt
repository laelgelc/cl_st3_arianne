Andrew Cuomo announced this past week in conjunction with 14 other states and the District of Columbia is his latest effort to address the issue . <p> " With a lack of federal leadership and an outright failure to follow science , it has fallen to the states to address the climate crisis by working together to eliminate greenhouse gas emissions from all sources , " Cuomo said . " Reducing pollution from medium- and heavy-duty vehicles will result in cleaner air for New Yorkers , particularly low-income neighborhoods and communities of color that have historically and disproportionately borne the brunt of the worst environmental consequences .
