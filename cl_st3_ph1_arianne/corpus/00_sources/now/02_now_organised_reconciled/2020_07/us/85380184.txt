( Dean Fosdick via AP ) <p> Every year now , gardeners should be rethinking what they grow and where because of climate change , experts say . <p> The growing season has become longer , delivering bigger harvests but requiring more weeding and controls . Plants are under stress because of rising temperatures , less frequent but more intense precipitation , and changed pest and disease problems . Flowers and the insects that pollinate them are falling out of sync . <p> " Conditions have become more challenging for gardeners since the weather has become more unpredictable , " says Richie Steffen , executive director and curator of the Elizabeth Miller Botanical Garden in Seattle . <p> " We 've had much hotter summers , while our winters have been all over the place , " he says .
