Not Pam from the television series The Office but primary amoebic meningoencephalitis ( PAM ) . <p> PAM is rare but typically fatal , because you need your brain to do important stuff like tweet , post selfies , and live . Those clearly are bad odds . Death can occur one to 18 days after symptoms began .
