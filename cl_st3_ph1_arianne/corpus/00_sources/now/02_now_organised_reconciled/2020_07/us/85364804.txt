<h> Biden groups seek to unify Democratic Party with policy proposals <p> ( Reuters ) - Groups assembled by Democratic presidential candidate Joe Biden and former rival for the nomination Bernie Sanders proposed policies on Wednesday aimed at uniting the party , including putting the United States on a path to cut carbon emissions to zero by 2050 . <p> Biden and Sanders , a liberal who was his last major primary opponent , formed the six groups in May to resolve friction between the party 's progressives and centrists as they head into the Nov. 3 election against Republican President Donald Trump . <p> The group on climate brought together Democratic U.S. Representative and Sanders ally Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez as well as former Secretary of State John Kerry , who had supported Biden . The recommendations will inform the Democratic Party platform , a statement of goals the party will run on once they have been adopted at its August convention . <p> The documents propose several new policies that build on Biden 's ideas .
