<p> TOKYO -- A United Arab Emirates spacecraft began its journey to Mars with a blastoff in Japan on Monday in what is the Arab world 's first interplanetary mission . <p> The launch of the spacecraft named Amal , or Hope , marks the start of the seven-month journey to the red planet . <p> The launch , initially planned for July 15 , had been delayed for five days due to bad weather . <p> The probe will study the upper atmosphere and monitor climate change while circling Mars for at least two years .
