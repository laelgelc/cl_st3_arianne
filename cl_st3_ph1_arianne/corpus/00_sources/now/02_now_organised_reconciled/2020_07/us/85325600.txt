Maybe -- but you have to continue to practice social distancing and hand hygiene to ensure safety in the pool . <p> Chlorination in swimming pool water kills viruses , and scientists do not believe COVID-19 is an exception to that rule , said Dr. Janak Patel , infectious disease specialist and director of Infection Control and Healthcare Epidemiology at UTMB . <p> Longtime studies have show chlorine is effective in killing germs in water -- it 's why the chemical was first used in a swimming pool in the early 1900s .
