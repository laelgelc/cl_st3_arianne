<h> NextGen America contacts 275,000 young Arizonans about voting by mail <p> A progressive advocacy group so far has contacted 275,000 young Arizonans by phone and text to ask them to make sure they are registered to vote and consider using a mail-in ballot . <p> " During a pandemic , voting by mail is absolutely essential to ensure that everyone casts their ballots safely , " Azza Abuseif , NextGen America 's Arizona state director , said in a written statement . " Right now , the president is spreading misinformation about stoking fear around mail-in ballots in an attempt to restrict the electorate . " <p> The deadline to request a mail-in ballot for the Arizona primary election has already passed . The deadline for registered voters to request a mail-in ballot for the November general election is Oct. 23 . <p> NextGen America used a team of 375 volunteers to call thousands of people this election , said Kristi Johnston , NextGen America 's Arizona press secretary . The organization aims to register 30,000 young people to vote in the state and asks people to enlist three of their friends to vote . <p> NextGen America @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ makes it a mission of " defeating GOP Senator Martha McSally , and sending electoral votes to the Democratic presidential nominee in 2020 . " <p> " Young voters know that this is the most critical election of their lifetime and everything is at stake for them , especially with the issues they care about the most when it comes to climate change , student debt , the economy , health care . It can all be voted on at the ballot in November , " Johnston said . <p> Changing demographics of young voters in Arizona and the Latino population will determine the outcome of this year 's election , she said . <p> NextGen America also held a virtual event Tuesday over Zoom addressing voter suppression and has campaigned to increase voter turnout among members of the Navajo Nation . <p> " A lot of what 's going on at the federal level and also at the state level are so many efforts to block the vote , and the war against vote by mail is one of those tactics , " Johnston said .
