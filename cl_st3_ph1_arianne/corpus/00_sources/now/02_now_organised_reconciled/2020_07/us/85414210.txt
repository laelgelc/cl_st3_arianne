( Burger King via AP ) <p> Burger King is staging an intervention with its cows . <p> The chain has rebalanced the diet of some of the cows by adding lemon grass in a bid to limit bovine contributions to climate change . By tweaking their diet , Burger King said Tuesday that it believes it can reduce a cow 's daily methane emissions by about 33% . <p> Cows emit methane as a by-product of their digestion , and that has become a potential public relations hurdle for major burger chains . <p> Greenhouse gas emissions from the agriculture @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in 2018 , according to the Environmental Protection Agency .
