<p> Hanna , the first hurricane of the 2020 Atlantic season , is barging into South Texas unleashing wind gusts over 100 mph , torrential rain , and storm surge inundation along the coast . The storm officially made landfall at 5:00 p.m. central over Padre Island , Texas . It comes as Pacific storm Hurricane Douglas bears down on Hawaii and as two other systems whirl out over the Atlantic during what it is expected to be an abnormally active season . A record number of storms have already occurred to this point . <p> A hurricane warning remains in effect between Port Mansfield and Baffin Bay .
