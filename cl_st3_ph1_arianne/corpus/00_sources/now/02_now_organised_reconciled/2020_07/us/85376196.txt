<p> PORTLAND , Maine ( AP ) -- A worldwide conservation organization said Thursday that the status of a rare species of whale has worsened to the point where it deserves greater attention from the global environmental community . <p> The International Union for Conservation of Nature said it is moving the North Atlantic right whale from " endangered " to " critically endangered " on its Red List of jeopardized species . The Switzerland-based organization 's Red List is one of the most-cited endangered species lists in the world . <p> The IUCN said the total population of the whales declined about 15% from 2011 to 2018 . The population is estimated to be about 400 animals now . The species has been plagued by high mortality and poor reproduction in recent years . <p> The IUCN said the whale 's decline " is being driven by a combination of increased mortality due to entanglement in fishing gear and vessel strikes , and a lower reproduction rate compared to previous years .
