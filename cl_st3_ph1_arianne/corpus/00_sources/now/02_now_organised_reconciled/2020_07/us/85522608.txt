Tisch College of Civic Life . The survey covered adults between the ages of 18 and 29 who will be eligible to vote in the United Stated by the 2020 General Election . The sample was drawn from the Gallup Panel , a probability-based panel that is representative of the U.S. adult population , and from the Dynata Panel , a non-probability panel . A total of 2,232 eligible adults completed the survey , which includes oversamples of 18- to 21-year-olds ( N=671 ) , Asian American youth ( N=306 ) , Black youth ( N=473 ) , Latino youth ( N=559 ) and young Republicans ( N=373 ) . The survey was conducted in English . <p> Of the total completes , 1,019 were from the Gallup Panel , and 1,238 were from the Dynata Panel .
