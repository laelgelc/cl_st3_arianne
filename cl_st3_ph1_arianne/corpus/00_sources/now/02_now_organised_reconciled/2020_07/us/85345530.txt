<p> David Brooks lists daunting societal challenges , from economic depression to the deadly virus . How many Black children set their ambitions one notch higher when they saw a Black president in their textbooks ? <p> Telling activists to forgo symbolic gestures is like telling a company that sales will go up if they stop advertising .
