Indeed , goats are more like us than one might think : incredibly inquisitive , intelligent , and social , as well as the most adaptable and geographically widespread livestock species . When humans began domesticating goats ( Capra hircus ) in the Middle East 's Fertile Crescent 10,000 to 12,000 years ago , they made a huge lifestyle change . They switched from being hunter-gathers to being tillers of the soil and herders of animals . When the first farmers moved into Europe about 7,500 years ago , goats came with them , helping to revolutionize society . This was one of the most fundamental changes in our history and goats were there to lend four helping feet .
