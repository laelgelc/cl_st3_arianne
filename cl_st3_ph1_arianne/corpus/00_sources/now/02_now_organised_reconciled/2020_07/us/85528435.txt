logistics , banking , legal services , professional services , education and tourism . <p> It also includes investment , science and technology cooperation , data transfers , payments systems , currency and migration ( " Communist China and the Free World 's Future " , U.S. Department of State , July 22 ) . <p> Unpicking this web of interdependencies will not be easy or cheap . <p> INDEPENDENCE <p> Like the United States before it , China 's response is likely to focus on reducing its need to import through a combination of developing domestic resources , increased efficiency , and investing in alternatives . <p> China 's focus on domestic energy sources ( nuclear , hydro , wind and solar ) and electric vehicles can largely be explained by the need to lessen its dependence on imported oil and gas , as well as concerns about air pollution and to a lesser extent climate change . <p> Deteriorating relations with the United States are likely to encourage China 's following the United States , which also focused on coal , nuclear and renewables during the era of energy insecurity from the 1970s to the 2000s . <p> Domestic coal provided a policy hedge against
