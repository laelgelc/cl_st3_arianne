<h> Hillsborough Democrats Award Scholarships <h> In an unusual year without an awards ceremony , Democrats give honorary scholarships <h> Jul 25 , 2020 1:00 am ET <p> In June , the Hillsborough Democratic Alliance awarded scholarships to two deserving Hillsborough High School graduating seniors . <p> The Democrats wish to congratulate the hard work and perseverance of the Hillsborough High School Class of 2020 . Participation , involvement , awareness , and education are the cornerstone of our democracy . The Alliance is proud to award Jeremy Perlmutter Scholarships of $500 each to Maya Karkhanis and Alex Ascencio . <p> This year 's award is made in honor of Hillsborough resident Jeremy Perlmutter -- a retired high school biology and chemistry teacher and a lifelong Democrat . An Amherst College graduate , he went on to receive his Masters from NYU . His 43-year teaching career was primarily based at Clifford Scott High School in East Orange and included a two-year leave to teach chemistry at the American School in Japan . It was powerfully inspiring to work with our Committee volunteers . And it has been deeply satisfying to be part of an organization that turns , not to its treasury , but to the heartfelt generosity of its individual members for the funding of its awards . " Jeremy 's hope for the HHS Class of 2020 is that they stay actively and positively involved in embracing the challenges of today and the future . <p> The Hillsborough Democratic Alliance holds regular monthly meetings , usually on the first Wednesday of every month at Avalon Assisted Living , 393 Amwell Road ( east of Route 206 ) .
