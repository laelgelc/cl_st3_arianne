<h> China raises flood alert to second highest level <p> BEIJING/SINGAPORE ( Reuters ) - China on Sunday raised its flood response alert to the second highest grade as downpours continued to batter regions along the Yangtze River , with the eastern provinces of Jiangsu and Jiangxi among the worst hit , state media reported . <p> Regional flooding in the Poyang county of Jiangxi has made water levels of China 's Lake Poyang , its biggest freshwater lake , surge to above 22.52 meters , a historical high and well above the alert level of 19.50 metres . <p> By Saturday evening , provincial military authorities had dispatched thousands of soldiers to help bolster nearly 9 km of the lake 's banks to prevent them from bursting , state television said . <p> China has a four-tier flood control emergency response system , with level one representing the most severe . <p> Citing data from the Ministry of Water Resources , 212 rivers have since early July exceeded alerting levels including 19 of them rising to historical highs . <p> China has blamed extreme weather conditions as a result of climate change for the torrential rain that has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ over 60 billion yuan of economic losses . <p> ( Reporting by Liangping Gao in Beijing and Chen Aizhu in Singapore ; Editing by Raju Gopalakrishnan )
