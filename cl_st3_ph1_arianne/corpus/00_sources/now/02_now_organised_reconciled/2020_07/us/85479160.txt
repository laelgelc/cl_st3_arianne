<h> Morgan Stanley is spilling the climate tea <p> It 's Thursday , July 23 , and Morgan Stanley is disclosing how its business affects climate change . <p> Morgan Stanley has joined the Partnership for Carbon Accounting Financials ( PCAF ) , an international collaboration founded in 2019 that aims to " standardize carbon accounting for the financial sector " by tracking how banks ' and investment firms ' assets are contributing to climate change . <p> Morgan Stanley is the first U.S.-based global bank to join the more than 60 PCAF members . Together , all PCAF members manage more than $6 trillion in assets . The group already includes international firms based in Europe , Africa , and Latin America , as well as smaller banks in the U.S. and around the world . <p> This is n't the first time the bank has made moves toward making its investments more environmentally friendly .
