<h> +1.25(+0.04%) <h> -7.00(-0.03%) <h> +32.25(+0.30%) <h> TransAlta Announces Retirement of Sundance 3 Coal Unit <p> CALGARY , AB , July 22 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ - TransAlta Corporation ( " TransAlta " or the " Company " ) ( TSX : TA ) ( NYSE : TAC ) announced that it gave notice today to the Alberta Electric System Operator ( " AESO " ) of its intention to retire the currently mothballed coal-fired Sundance Unit 3 effective July 31 , 2020 . The retirement decision was largely driven by TransAlta 's assessment of future market conditions , the age and condition of the unit , and our ability to supply energy and capacity from our generation portfolio in Alberta . <p> This decision advances the Company 's transition to 100 per cent clean electricity by 2025 .
