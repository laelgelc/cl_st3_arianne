The Permian-Triassic extinction event was unfolding , in which 70 percent of land species and 96 percent of marine species disappeared . Runaway global warming had raised equatorial ocean temperatures to 104 degrees Fahrenheit . The seas rapidly acidified , so shelled critters struggled to build their protective homes . Indeed , the fossil record shows these species got it the worst -- strong evidence that the extinction 's culprit was CO2 mucking with the oceans ' pH balance , and the rest of the planet , for that matter .
