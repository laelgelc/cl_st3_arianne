In doing so , Eni will become the fifth oil major to have made a similar pledge within the past year . ( Repsol SA , BP Plc , Royal Dutch Shell Plc and Total SA got there first . ) <p> The timing is no coincidence . In June 2019 the U.K. and France passed laws requiring carbon neutrality by 2050 , and the EU announced in December its intention to set a similar goal .
