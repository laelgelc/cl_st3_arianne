<h> Will China change its policy on dams ? <h> Seasonal rains are common in China , but this year 's flooding has been exceptional and early . <p> Floods have swamped large parts of southern and central China after the worst torrential rains in decades . Many people are dead or missing . Whole villages are inundated and millions of people displaced . <p> The economic losses in dollars are believed to be in the billions . Nearly two months of intense rains have swelled 433 rivers - with water in some reaching record levels . <p> Chinese authorities have raised flood alerts and deployed soldiers to evacuate people and shore up levees . <p> Some environmentalists blame climate change . Others are raising concerns about the country 's dams - including the Three Gorges , which has been struggling with extreme flows of water . The dam was once a source of great national pride . <p> But what effects do such megaprojects have on people and the environment ?
