<p> NAIROBI , KENYA -- The world is seeing a rise in diseases caused by viruses that have jumped from animal hosts to the human population , with COVID-19 just one example , a group of experts said . <p> Ebola , MERS as well as West Nile and Rift Valley fevers were other examples of zoonotic diseases , which are driven by the degradation of our natural environment , said the United Nations Environment Department ( UNEP ) and International Livestock Research Institute ( ILRI ) . <p> " While many in the world were surprised by COVID-19 , those of us who work on animal disease were not . This was a highly predictable pandemic , " said Delia Randolph , ILRI veterinary epidemiologist . <p> Randolph described a " very clear trend " since the 1930s that showed that 75% of emerging human diseases stemmed from wildlife .
