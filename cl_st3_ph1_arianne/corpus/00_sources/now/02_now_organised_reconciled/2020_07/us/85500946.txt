That 's the geological era where humanity has become a dominant force of nature . <p> In addition to that common heritage , epidemics and climate change interact directly . <p> Climate change can make a difference in how severe the pandemic is . <p> In 2014 , then-Secretary of Defense Chuck Hagel called climate change a " threat multiplier ... because it has the potential to exacerbate many challenges , including infectious disease .
