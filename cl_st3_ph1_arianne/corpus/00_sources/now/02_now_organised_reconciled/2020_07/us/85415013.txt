<p> As Covid-19 spread across the globe early this year , Facebook went into action to combat potentially dangerous disinformation on its site . The company labeled and suppressed misleading content , removed disinformation and directed users to reputable sources , including the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention website . <p> This swift action stood in sharp contrast to Facebook 's stance on another divisive and complex danger : climate change . That 's because , under the company 's guidelines , climate content can be classified as opinion and therefore exempted from fact-checking procedures . <p> The policy means that peer-reviewed science can be lumped into the same category as industry statements and even blatant disinformation . In September , for example , the CO2 Coalition , a nonprofit group that says increased carbon emissions are good for the planet , successfully overturned a fact-check when Facebook quietly labeled its post as " opinion .
