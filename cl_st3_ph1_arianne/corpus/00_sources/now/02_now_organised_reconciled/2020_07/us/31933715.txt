She seemed like an alien who had dropped in on our world of stay-at-home schlumps , or maybe just a visitor from another year , a normal person highlighting just how abnormal our city had become . A few weeks later , New York is starting to feel like New York again , though fitfully , and still with a streak of the surreal .
