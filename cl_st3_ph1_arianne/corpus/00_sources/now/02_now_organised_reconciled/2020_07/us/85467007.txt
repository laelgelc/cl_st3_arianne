<p> In accordance with its goal , Applied has joined other technology leaders in committing to purchase energy from the 500 megawatt ( MW ) White Mesa Wind project being developed by Apex Clean Energy in Crockett County , Texas . Applied 's power purchase agreement ( PPA ) covers 10 percent of the project 's clean energy output , an amount equivalent to the power needs of more than 13,000 homes . <p> " Our PPA is an important step in helping Applied Materials reach our reduced carbon footprint targets as it will enable us to achieve 100 percent renewable energy sourcing in the U.S. and 73 percent worldwide by 2022 , " said Scott Hambleton , managing director of Environmental , Health and Safety at Applied Materials . " We are excited to accelerate our environmental sustainability goals with a clean energy project that is supported by other leading technology companies . " <p> " White Mesa clearly demonstrates the power of corporate energy purchasers working together to bring new renewable energy projects to life , " said Mark Goodwin , president and CEO of Apex Clean Energy .
