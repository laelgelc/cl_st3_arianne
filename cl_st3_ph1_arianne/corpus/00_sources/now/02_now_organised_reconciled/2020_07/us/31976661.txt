<p> The Democratic primary for District 7 on the Pinellas County Commission is a showdown between three of the area 's more experienced politicians . All three have held local or state office , and two of them have held both . But one candidate has a track record of asking the right questions and the uncomfortable questions in service of his constituents -- Wengay Newton , who the Tampa Bay Times recommends for the District 7 seat . <p> Wengay Newton <p> Newton , 56 , was born and raised in St. Petersburg and went to school at Northeast High . He was elected to St. Petersburg City Council in 2008 and served until 2016 . Since then , he has represented District 70 in the Florida House of Representatives , which extends from south St. Petersburg to parts of Hillsborough , Manatee and Sarasota counties . In interviews and on his website , Newton paints himself as an everyman who grew up in Midtown in St. Petersburg and wants to prioritize people over politicians in the policymaking process . <p> His background has informed his legislative priorities .
