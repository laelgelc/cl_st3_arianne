They left out an important detail -- CMP 's project is one of 45 applicants to fulfill Massachusetts ' new clean energy quota . This amount of so called clean energy , will be added to the New England grid to supply Massachusetts ratepayers : and it is not Maine 's ' burden to bear ! <p> In fact , CMP 's corridor project was one of three to deliver Hydro Quebec 's energy to Massachusetts , and it was n't even the first choice . TDI proposed a buried line through Vermont . This fully permitted project would have minimal impacts to Vermont 's ' natural resources or view sheds .
