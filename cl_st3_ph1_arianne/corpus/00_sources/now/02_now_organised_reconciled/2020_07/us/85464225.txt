<h> Biden pledges free preschool as part of sweeping caregiving proposal <p> NEW CASTLE , Del . ( Reuters ) - Democratic presidential candidate Joe Biden outlined a sweeping plan on Tuesday aimed at breathing life into the coronavirus-battered U.S. economy by investing $775 billion in caregiving programs for children , the elderly and the disabled . <p> As part of the proposal , which Biden said would create 5 million jobs , he pledged to provide all 3-year-old and 4-year-old children access to free preschool . He also promised to make it easier for aging relatives and loved ones with disabilities to receive home or community-based care . <p> " We 're trapped in a caregiving crisis within an economic crisis within a healthcare crisis , " Biden told educators at a campaign event in New Castle , Delaware . " You 're doing everything you can , but this president is not .
