Some bodies acclimate better to the heat and do n't sweat as much as others . <p> Older people sweat less than younger people , which puts them at an increased risk for heat exhaustion and heat stroke , Katta said . <p> Sweat gets a bad wrap as the cause of bodily odor , but that 's not the case either .
