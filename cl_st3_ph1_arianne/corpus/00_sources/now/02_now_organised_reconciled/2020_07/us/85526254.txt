<h> Column : We should have seen Householder 's fall coming <h> Tuesday <p> Jul 28 , 2020 at 4:15 AM <p> " Eighteen years changes a lot of people . Now , 18 months later , Ohioans know the Perry County Republican is n't one of those people -- unless the change means becoming more brazen , power-hungry and careless . <p> Last Tuesday , federal agents arrested Householder and four others , including Neil Clark , the longtime Statehouse lobbyist known for plying his trade at the ethical edge . The charges involve a $60 million racketeering and bribery " enterprise , " orchestrated by Householder and funded by FirstEnergy and FirstEnergy Solutions , now spun off as Energy Harbor . The U.S. attorney described the operation as " likely the largest bribery , money-laundering scheme ever perpetrated against the people of Ohio . " <p> Also evident is the logical extension of all things Householder when it comes to power games . He wanted to become speaker .
