<p> Electric vehicles have become increasingly controversial . What was once innovative technology for early adopters has now taken on a distinctly political edge . Fossil fuel enthusiasts feel so threatened that diesel truck owners are regularly " coal rolling " Tesla TSLAs -- a practice whereby the truck driver deliberately guns their engine to emit black smoke over the unsuspecting victim . There are many bones of contention between those evangelizing electric and aficionados of internal combustion , but one topic that regularly appears in discussions is : could the rapid growth in electric vehicle sales we have seen recently occur without state-backed incentives ?
