<p> Hankook 's most high-profile motorsport involvement to date has come as the exclusive supplier to the DTM since 2011 , and it added W Series to its repertoire in 2019 . <p> The company will create bespoke tyres for FE to be used in both wet and dry conditions , and will contain bio-sourced material and sustainable rubber . <p> Michelin has provided the championship with all-weather tyres since it began in 2014 , but will not be part of the new Gen3 era . <p> An FIA statement said : " The tender process for selecting suppliers of the chassis , battery and tyres overcame all the expectations and the submissions were compared in relation to their compliance with the technical , environmental , commercial and financial requirements . <p> " The FIA 's World Motor Sport Council recently voted on the bids and Spark Racing Technology will continue producing the common chassis , including the front powertrain kit , Williams Advanced Engineering will be responsible for the battery system whilst the Gen3 will be fitted with all-weather Hankook Tire &amp; Technology tyres . " <p> Hankook Tire &amp; Technology president and COO Sooil Lee added : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has been selected by the FIA following tender process and FIA World Motor Sport Council approval as the future tyre and technology partner for the FIA Formula E Championship . <p> " Our company coincides with Formula E 's move towards sustainable growth and we are excited to be a part of this journey as their Gen3 partner . <p> " It is impressive how the FIA and the FIA Formula E Championships are addressing climate change and air pollution in our urban environments by promoting the adoption of electric vehicles through this all-electric race series and bringing it to the attention of a broad global audience .
