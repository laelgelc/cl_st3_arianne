<h> Climate change turning US mountain lakes green with algae <p> The concentration of algae in two remote mountain lakes more than doubled in the past 70 years <p> Global warming is turning clear mountain lakes green in the western United States because of an increase in algae blooms " without historical precedent " , researchers reported on Tuesday . <p> The concentration of algae in two remote mountain lakes more than doubled in the past 70 years , researchers at Colorado State University found . <p> Their results , published in the Proceedings of the Royal Society B , highlight the potentially harmful effects of climate change on pristine and remote ecosystems . <p> " Even in relatively remote lakes located in protected areas ... the fingerprint of human perturbation of Earth System is evident , " lead researcher Isabella Oleksy of Colorado State University told AFP . <p> " Rapid warming of high elevation environments has resulted in the rapid acceleration and dominance of green algae , which until recently were found in low abundance in these lakes .
