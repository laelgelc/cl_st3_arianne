<h> Siemens Energy to set out plan to exit coal-generated electricity : CEO <p> Siemens ' Chief Executive said on Thursday he wanted the future managing board of Siemens Energy to quickly set out plans to exit coal-generated electricity . <p> " The fight against climate change requires a decisive change in power generation , as it is responsible for about 40 percent of global energy-related CO2 emissions , " Joe Kaeser told an online general meeting on spinning out the power business . <p> " That is why I have asked the Managing Board of Siemens Energy AG to quickly present a stakeholder-friendly plan to phase out coal-based power generation . " <p> Siemens shareholders are set to vote on spinning off the company 's energy business , Siemens Energy , on Thursday . <p> Under the plans , 55% of Siemens Energy will be spun-off to the firm 's shareholders , with Siemens retaining a 35.1% stake and Siemens Pension-Trust e.V having a 9.9% share . <p> Speaking to CNBC 's " Squawk Box Europe " on Thursday , Kaeser said that the firm was " separating the energy business from the industrial automation and software business @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , technology and transformation . " <p> On the potential loss of synergies in areas such as research and development and cost structure that a separation of units could bring about , Kaeser said that " synergies do n't create growth , focus does .
