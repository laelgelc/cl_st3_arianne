The post - -- and the AGGI -- - mentioned carbon dioxide , methane , nitrous oxide and other gases , but failed to mention the biggest contributor to global warming : plain old water vapor . <p> " I want to comment that the way-dominant greenhouse gas in the atmosphere is not mentioned , namely water vapor , " writes Ken Saunders of Pacific Palisades . " Water vapor accounts for about 97 percent of the total ( natural plus man-emitted ) greenhouse warming of the planet . See , e.g. , John Houghton 's ' The Physics of Atmospheres , 3rd edition , ' Cambridge University Press , 2002 .
