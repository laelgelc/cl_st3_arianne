Few investments enhance wellbeing and reduce inequity so effectively , while also delivering such high economic returns . <p> SAN FRANCISCO -- The COVID-19 pandemic and its repercussions have devastated communities and economies around the world . Barely six months old , COVID-19 now looks set to become the most expensive malady in history , costing the global economy an estimated 3-8% of GDP this year alone . <p> The worse economic fundamentals and forecasts become , the more mysterious stock-market outcomes in the US appear . In our research , we examined the health challenges of about 200 countries .
