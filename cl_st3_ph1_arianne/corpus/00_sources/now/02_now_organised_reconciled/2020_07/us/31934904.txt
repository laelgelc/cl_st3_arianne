" <p> Brown 's nearly five-decade career at the U was fueled by his own curiosity . <p> " He loved learning and had a sense of curiosity and it was the driving factor in his career , " said Brown Polson . <p> Brown , of Falcon Heights , died on June 19 at Regions Hospital from natural causes .
