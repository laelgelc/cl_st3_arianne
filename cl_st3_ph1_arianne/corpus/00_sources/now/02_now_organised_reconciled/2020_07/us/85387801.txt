<h> Hungarian paprika farmers fend off drought with new ideas and EU help <p> BATYA , Hungary ( Reuters ) - Paprika farmer Peter Szabo from Batya , a village by the Danube in central Hungary , had battled against worsening drought for years until he found a natural method to drastically reduce his crops ' need for irrigation . <p> Szabo began mixing volcanic minerals into the soil , which helps it retain water . Since then he has had to irrigate his vast paprika fields only two or three times in a year and this only in the hottest month of August , when the area around Batya , in the middle of landlocked Hungary , gets zero rainfall . <p> Previously , his fields needed watering every 8-10 days in the driest months . <p> " The extreme weather has been a problem for years and we are trying to get a grip on it , " Szabo said .
