<h> Air quality alerts issued for Wednesday and Thursday <p> This week , the Mid-Ohio Regional Planning Commission issued four days of air quality alerts for sensitive groups , such as children , the elderly and people with asthma , due to ozone pollution . <p> On Tuesday , MORPC issued orange alerts for the region , which includes Delaware , Fairfield , Franklin , and Licking counties , for Wednesday and Thursday . The air quality index lists any level at 101 or higher as unhealthy for sensitive groups . Wednesday is expected to reach levels at 101 , followed by 112 levels on Thursday . <p> Those come on the heels of alerts that MORPC , which monitors air quality , issued Sunday and Monday . Temperatures throughout central Ohio have peaked in the mid-90s this week . They are expected to dip down to highs in the upper 80s by the weekend . <p> Conditions for ozone are worse on hot days when the pollution bakes in the atmosphere . On a particularly hot day , ozone can make breathing harder for those with asthma or other lung conditions .
