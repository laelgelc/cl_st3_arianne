And do we ever need uplifting stories these days ! <p> Atlantic Sea Farms is the largest commercial seaweed farm in the U.S. They line-grow their seaweed in clear , icy cold Maine waters . The seaweed -- which is sold frozen in pureed cubes and in ready to eat cut strands and fermented products -- is never dyed or dehydrated . <p> Warner saw a need for a viable , sustainable business to help Maine lobstermen and women diversify their business . Demand for lobsters has fallen sharply because of COVID-19 as restaurants scale back their business and fewer tourists visit Maine . <p> Climate change is also a big factor .
