<h> Apple says its supply chains and products will be carbon neutral by 2030 <p> Apple this morning announced plans to make its entire business carbon neutral within the next 10 years . The news follows the company 's push toward a fully carbon neutral corporate structure , adding its manufacturing supply chain and resulting products into the mix . <p> The roadmap to sustainability as released today is part of the company 's annual Environmental Progress Report . Reducing every device it sells to zero climate impact means a couple of things . The primary concern is finding ways to reduce emissions from productions by 75% .
