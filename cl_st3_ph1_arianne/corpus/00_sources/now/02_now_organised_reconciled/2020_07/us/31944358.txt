They say local and national governments are already planting more shrubs , herbs and other forms of native vegetation that need less water . <h> Greening China <p> The Gobi Desert and similarly arid regions in China are expanding as processes such as overgrazing deplete vegetation on their borders , allowing wind and gravity to erode soil . China 's largest tree-planting drive , the Three-North Shelter Forest Program , also called the Great Green Wall , is designed to halt that encroachment . The government says that it has planted more than 66 billion trees across 13 provinces in the country 's north since the programme began in 1978 . <p> Around the year 2000 , deserts across the country were expanding by 10,400 square kilometres a year , says the government .
