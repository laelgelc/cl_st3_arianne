<h> Letter to the editor : Elect Skold in District 38 to help find climate solutions <p> Share <p> I 'm supporting Charles Skold for the Democratic nomination for state representative in Maine House District 38 . <p> I got to know Charles through the Portland Climate Action Team , and was delighted to see that the Sierra Club of Maine has also given Charles their first-choice endorsement . He 's a member of the generation that will live through worsening climate change , and needs to find solutions that have largely eluded my generation . ( I was born in 1939 . ) <p> Charles sees that the state must be a major part of those solutions . Since more than 50 percent of Maine 's greenhouse-gas emissions come from transportation , regional public transportation systems can drive those emissions down while increasing public mobility . <p> As we emerge from lockdown , we have an unprecedented opportunity to make long-overdue changes .
