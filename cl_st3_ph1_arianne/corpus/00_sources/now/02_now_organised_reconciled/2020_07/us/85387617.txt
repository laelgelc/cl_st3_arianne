<p> After unloading 2 to 6 inches of rain from Ocean City , Md. to New York City , and causing pockets of flooding , Tropical Storm Fay is steadily weakening as it moves inland . The storm made landfall just north of Atlantic City late Friday afternoon . <p> The storm developed suddenly on Thursday just off the Mid-Atlantic coastline , becoming the earliest " F " storm in Atlantic history amid a record busy start to hurricane season . It swirled northward soaking the northern Mid-Atlantic Friday but it is now expected to be just a modest rainmaker over the interior Northeast before scooting into Canada Saturday afternoon . <p> The storm was at its peak Friday morning and afternoon , as it slowly approached the Jersey shore . Maximum sustained winds briefly hit 60 mph for a time over the ocean , and gusts along the coast reached 40 to 50 mph from Delaware to New York . <p> Ocean City was among the areas under a flash flood warning on Friday morning .
