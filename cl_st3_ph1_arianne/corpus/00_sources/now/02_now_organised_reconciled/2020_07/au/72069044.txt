<p> Investor confidence in Australia 's clean energy sector improved in the first half of 2020 , buoyed by strong and steady policies from state and territory governments and defying global disruptions caused by Covid-19 and an ongoing lack of meaningful policies from the Morrison federal government . <p> Investor confidence surged to its highest level recorded by the Clean Energy Council 's investor survey , on the back of strong policies from State and Territory governments -- both Labor and Liberal -- were providing investors sufficient confidence to expand portfolios of wind , solar and energy storage projects . <p> Since that time , the South Australian Liberal government has reinforced its commitment to reach net 100 per cent renewables by 2030 , the Tasmania Liberal government has announced a goal of 200 per cent renewables by 2040 , and the NSW Liberal government has moved forward with its plans to create three new renewable energy zones to ensure enough new generation and storage to replace its ageing coal fleet . <p> The Victoria and Queensland Labor governments remain committed to their targets of 50 per cent renewables by 2030 , the W.A.
