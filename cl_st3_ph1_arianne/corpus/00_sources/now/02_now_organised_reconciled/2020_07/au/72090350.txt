In public , Andrews comes across as the man for whom the phrase " firm but fair " would seem to have been invented . It also helps that Dan the Builder takes a boy-like pleasure in constructing things , like railways , tunnels and bridges and it would be impossible to spend any time in Melbourne these days not noticing how much work is going into basic public infrastructure -- and not a moment too soon . <p> The size of the Labor win in terms of electoral seats is impressive -- so impressive that it can give a misleading and confusing impression of what actually happened at the election .
