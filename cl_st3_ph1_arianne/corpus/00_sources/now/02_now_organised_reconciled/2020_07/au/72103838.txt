<p> The head of the state 's peak union body says there is little in a high-level coronavirus recovery report to advance the case of disadvantaged workers . The Premier 's Economic and Social Recovery Advisory Council on Monday released its interim which outlined 64 recommendations to steer Tasmania 's social and economic recovery from the pandemic . Within it were suggestions for more targeted support for industry , business and disadvantaged Tasmanians . Unions Tasmania secretary Jess Munday said although the report highlighted inequalities with economic distribution in Tasmania , it did not make any recommendations on how the wages of workers could be made more secure . She said there was a missed opportunity to recommend industrial relations reforms to counter casualisation of the state 's workforce and to ensure people who wanted to work more hours had an opportunity to do so . Ms Munday said the report mentioned COVID-safe workplace provisions had been acknowledged as a burden for businesses . " This sends the completely wrong message about workplace safety , " she said .
