Credit : Image : Allianz Australia and University of Technology Sydney . <p> A focus on sustainability and economic impact will result in future generations deconstructing the traditional family home , according to a recent study conducted by Allianz Australia in partnership with the University of Technology Sydney . <p> The Future of Living report suggested this new age of dwellings would remove the divide between indoors and out , with homes functioning as a seamless extension of the natural world . These homes will likely be smaller , incorporating community living , an abundance of native plants , recyclable materials and clever climate management . <p> Allianz Australia General Manager Home and Lifestyle Rachael Poole said the study highlighted the shared responsibility to invest in a sustainable future for our communities and our world . <p> " The Future of Living report provides Australians with an understanding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ living in their homes in years to come , " she said . <p> Speaking to New Homes , Allianz Sustainability Advocate and House Rules : High Stakes co-host Jamie Durie said the report came at an important time . <p> " This is stuff people need to know , " he said . " We 've got to be more aware of the current trends , the impacts on the planet and the impact both socially and economically . There 's been this return to nature , and that 's not just driven by all of the awareness around climate change over the last couple of decades . <p> " I think the whole COVID-19 issue has made us think very carefully about the way we invest in our homes , how much we spend and how we design our homes to have minimal impacts on our wallets and the environment that surrounds us .
