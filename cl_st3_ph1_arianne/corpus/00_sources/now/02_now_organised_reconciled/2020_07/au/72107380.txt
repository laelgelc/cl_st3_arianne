It respects the inherent hunting and harvesting rights of Inuit and is an important step towards addressing food security in Inuit Nunangat . <p> Earlier this year , the Government of Canada also announced support for the Aqqiumavvik Society 's Young Hunters Program . The community-led program works with Elders to document the knowledge and skills required for youth to become masters in sustainable harvesting . The Young Hunters Program connects youth , age 8 to 18 , with Elders to learn these skills and also to develop skills and share knowledge to monitor and adapt to the impacts of climate change . <p> Together , the Harvesters Support Grant and the Young Hunters Program are helping Inuit communities .
