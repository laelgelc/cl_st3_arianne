<h> A fall or a stumble ? Daniel Andrews takes a hit from the second surge <p> Only a few weeks ago , Daniel Andrews looked set to become Australia 's Jacinda Ardern , widely celebrated as a standout political leader whose stern , resilient approach had sent the new coronavirus packing . He was so close . His reputation had grown well beyond the borders of his home state . He was the straight talker . Victoria 's infection numbers were small and manageable as the state slowly opened up . <p> Unlike Gladys Berejiklian in NSW , he did n't have the deadly Ruby Princess system failure as a drag on his reputation . But then the second wave of COVID-19 infections rolled across Victoria , turning Andrews into a national whipping boy and giving his state instant pariah status , its citizens unwelcome and sometimes abused on the streets of other states . <p> Premier Daniel Andrews announcing on Friday that Victoria had recorded 288 new COVID-19 cases.Credit:Luis Ascui <p> And at home , millions of Victorians are back in lockdown well into next month , generating a heightened sense of uncertainty across the community and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , not just in Victoria , but nationally . <p> Has Andrews fallen or is this a stumble ? That will be up to him . And when I say " him " , I mean it . He is the government . Yes , he heads a cabinet made up of ministers , some of them highly capable . They make contributions , administer their portfolios dutifully , and appear in the media . <p> Advertisement <p> But when it comes to the big calls , the Premier is the great decider . He is a powerful personality and is highly confident in putting forward his views to his colleagues . <p> The government is tightly run from the top and few , if any , of his senior colleagues push back against him when he sets the policy direction . And why would they when the government has enjoyed so much electoral success ? <p> Almost all of Labor 's history of being in office in Victoria spans the past 40 years and no other ALP premier in that time has so completely embodied his government - as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Daniel Andrews does his administration . <p> In the previous Labor government , a core of MPs who had driven the ALP 's revival in opposition - Steve Bracks , John Brumby , John Thwaites and Rob Hulls -- were prominent , influential figures throughout its long run in office . This government is much more of a one-man show . <p> Loading <p> Until now , Andrews ' way of doing things has benefited him and his party . Having taken over in 2010 , he is one of the ALP 's longest serving leaders and has notched up two election wins in two attempts . The most recent win , in 2018 , was a landslide . <p> That win came about in large part because of Andrews ' style and his judgment of what worked with Victorian electors . His view was that they wanted things done . <p> Scandals , controversies and missteps such as his billion-dollar decision to break the Napthine government 's East-West Link contract mattered less to voters than seeing their new government getting on with the job by building infrastructure and generating @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has been : push on , do n't be distracted , do n't get caught up in prolonged periods of reflection , move quickly , show you can deliver and if you create your own momentum , people will come with you . <p> It is easy to be wise after the event , especially from the sidelines , but this ethos could well have created the conditions that led to the awful choice of private security firms to look after quarantined travellers . <p> By handing over responsibility for quarantine to private companies rather than public sector agencies -- the police , the defence forces or the Department of Corrections -- the program could be about jobs . Also , it was quick . Recruitment , training , back-up and quality assurance were , it seems , taken on by the providers . It was done , and the government could keep moving . <p> This fits with the framing of many of the government 's policies from its earliest time in office : that they are primarily about the creation of jobs . There 's little evidence that it has caused a calamitous collapse in public support , but it has damaged him and moved supporters -- especially some who run businesses -- out of his column permanently . The calculus is simple : they believed him , and he has let them down . <p> The political pain could well return when Jennifer Coate , the former judge appointed to inquire into the quarantine breakdown , produces her findings .
