carbon neutral by the same deadline . <p> But the debate about how best to get to that target , and at what cost , started much earlier since California has been contemplating to reduce its reliance to the end target -- once the low hanging fruit has been exhausted . <p> Some observers have proposed a more gradual approach , extending the deadline farther back while others have argued for an
