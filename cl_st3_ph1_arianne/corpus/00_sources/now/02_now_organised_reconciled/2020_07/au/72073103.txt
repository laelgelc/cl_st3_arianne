Hands down my pick would have to be the new Tourism Tasmania campaign Come Down for Air . <p> For anyone who has visited Tasmania it 's a great reminder of what an amazing and relaxing place it truly is . For those who have n't been this campaign paints the perfect picture of what @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a place like Tassie delivers . <p> The strategy is brilliant , the creative execution could n't be better if you tried , and the line is GOLD .
