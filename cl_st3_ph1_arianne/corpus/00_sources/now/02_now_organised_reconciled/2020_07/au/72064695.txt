<h> From Jennifer Aniston to Off-White : The politics of the fashion face mask <p> One of the big trends Australia has thankfully skipped these last few months is the fashion face mask , something that is being promoted big time on the US fashion websites , from top designer styles to retro- cute versions that can be purchased on Etsy . <p> To our credit , Australians agreed pretty early on that taking the appropriate actions to ensure our communal health was not a partisan political statement nor an attack on our personal freedoms , so we have mostly been spared the ignominy of having to match our masks with our weekend wear . <p> It is astonishing to see so many Americans pushing back on the idea of wearing a mask as the corona virus cuts a deadly swathe through the country .
