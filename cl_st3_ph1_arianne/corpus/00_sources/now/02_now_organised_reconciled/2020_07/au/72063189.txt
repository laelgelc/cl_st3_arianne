<p> Unfailingly , since World War II , the voters in the New South Wales seat of Eden-Monaro have picked the mood of the nation at every federal election . Well , almost every election -- 2016 and 2019 were the exceptions . Today those of the seat 's 114,000 registered electors who have n't voted pre-poll will play the part of political weathervane , if not their more traditional role of bellwether . <p> Labor 's retired member Mike Kelly proved an iconoclast when he won the seat from an incumbent Coalition government and managed to hold on to it by his fingernails the second time around . His departure has put the governing Liberals in with a strong chance of winning the seat back , but nobody thinks whoever takes the prize will do so in a landslide . The sprawling and diverse electorate runs from the suburbs of Canberra down the Hume Highway to the Victorian border and all the way back up the coast . <p> The issues at play are the very challenges confronting Australians everywhere in this time of an unpredictable and devastating coronavirus recession .
