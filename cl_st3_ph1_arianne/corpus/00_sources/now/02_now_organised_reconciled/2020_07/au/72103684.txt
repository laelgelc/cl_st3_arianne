<p> Millennials and Gen Z will put sustainability first when living in and building their future homes , according to new research released by Allianz . We can expect young Australians to deconstruct the family home model as we know it : outdoor and indoor living will be combined ; an eclectic , new interior decorating style created ; and the impact of harsh financial realities may lead to a ' sharing economy ' approach to housing . The Future of Living report , commissioned by Allianz in partnership with University of Technology Sydney ( UTS ) , has revealed how the socioeconomic and environmental factors our younger generation are navigating , will affect house design and home-living . The impact of some of these factors has been magnified recently following the devastating effects of COVID-19 and the bushfires that razed parts of Australia during the past summer .
