<p> The City of Sydney has officially made the switch to 100 per cent renewable electricity , with its power supplies now flowing from wind and solar projects from across regional New South Wales . <p> The council will now source the equivalent of all of its electricity consumption from renewable sources , including energy used throughout its operations at its buildings , depots , street lights , sporting facilities , as well as the iconic Sydney Town Hall . Its milestone came on the same day as the City of Adelaide . <p> " We are in the middle of a climate emergency . If we are to reduce emissions and grow the green power sector , all levels of government must urgently transition to renewable energy , " Sydney Lord Mayor Clover Moore said . <p> " Cities are responsible for 70 per cent of greenhouse gas emissions worldwide , so it is critical that we take effective and evidence-based climate actions .
