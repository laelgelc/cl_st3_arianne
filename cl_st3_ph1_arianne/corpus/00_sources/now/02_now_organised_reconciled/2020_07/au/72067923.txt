<p> To see how much our city 's environment has changed in recent years , one need only to look out the window . Canberra 's backyards have always harboured plenty of birds . This has long been recognised by Canberra 's enthusiastic community of birdwatchers and ornithologists . Thanks to these knowledgeable locals and other organisations , such as Birds Australia , we have a wealth of publicly available backyard bird survey data spanning over 50 years . What this data shows us is how much our own backyard has changed in the last few decades . To illustrate this point , we can compare observations taken from backyard bird surveys over the past five years ( 2015 - 2020 ) with backyard surveys taken 30 years ago ( 1985 - 1990 ) . Some birds of course are relatively unaffected by the winds of change : magpies , galahs and wattlebirds , for example , remain roughly as abundant today as they did 30 years ago . But this is only a superficial view of our local avifauna .
