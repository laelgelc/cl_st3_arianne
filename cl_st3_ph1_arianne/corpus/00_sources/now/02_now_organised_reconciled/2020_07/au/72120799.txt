Yet rather than applauding an effort to protect workers , union leaders fired off a furious letter demanding that the decision be reversed . <p> It was not just the fact that six of the mines had not recorded cases of the virus among miners that angered union bosses . Until the 1980s , the central European nation was one of the world 's top five producers . Even last year , it drew 74 per cent of its electricity from the black fuel . No EU state burned more hard coal . As Poland went into lockdown to stem the virus 's spread , overall energy use fell 5 per cent in the first half of the year compared with the same period in 2019 , according to Ember , an energy think-tank .
