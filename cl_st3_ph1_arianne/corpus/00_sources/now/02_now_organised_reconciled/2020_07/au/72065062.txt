<p> Drought is ravaging the land . Large swaths of eastern Australia are experiencing some of the worst seasons on record . Frosts have wiped out large areas of crops in Western Australia , southern New South Wales and Victoria . Hail has beaten crops into the ground in Queensland . <p> The government is scrambling to be seen to be doing something meaningful for farmers , particularly in Queensland where traditional National voters are looking to desert in favour of minor parties , such as One Nation and Katter 's Australian Party . In some coastal seats in the state , Labor is also doing well .
