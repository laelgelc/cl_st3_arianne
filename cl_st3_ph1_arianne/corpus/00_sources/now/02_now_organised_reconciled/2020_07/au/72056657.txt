<h> Combet exits COVID-19 commission <p> Former Labor minister Greg Combet has resigned from the National COVID-19 Coordination Commission . <p> His departure from the NCCC early last week came despite the government wanting him to stay , commission sources told The Age . <p> As a commissioner , Combet was charged with focusing on a number of sectors including employment , labour force and industrial relations , manufacturing , defence industry , and superannuation . He will remain as an " expert adviser " on the government 's industrial relations working groups . <p> Combet previously headed the Australian Council of Trade Unions , and served as minister for industry and innovation , climate change and energy efficiency , and defence personnel , science and materiel . <p> He currently chairs IFM Investors and Industry Super Australia , and has recently been hitting back at a number of backbench Coalition MPs who have been pushing for the government to delay a rise to the superannuation rate . <p> He argued that not increasing the super guarantee would harm the people who had to make withdrawals from their savings to stay afloat during the coronavirus pandemic.
