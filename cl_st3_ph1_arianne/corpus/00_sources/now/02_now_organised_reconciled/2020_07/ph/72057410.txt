<h> Saving seeds , growing with nature on 7th episode of ' Stories for a Better Normal ' series <p> CALOOCAN CITY , July 1 ( PIA ) -- On the seventh episode of " Stories for a Better Normal : Pandemic and Climate Change Pathways , " with the topic , " Saving Seeds and Growing with Nature , " House Deputy Speaker and Antique Representative Loren Legarda , together with resource persons , will discuss the basics and benefits of permaculture and the importance of saving seeds in ensuring food self-sufficiency . <p> The episode will stream on Facebook Live on Thursday , July 2 , 2020 , 10:00 a.m. at facebook.com/ conglorenlegarda and facebook. com/CCCPhl/ . <p> Joining Legarda are permaculture and seed saving experts and advocates , including Karen Hizola , Executive Director of Global Seed Savers Philippines ; Dr. Blesilda Calub from the University of the Philippines-Los Ba ? os ; Bert Peeters , founder of the Philippine Permaculture Association ; Jabez Flores of the Permaculture Research PH ; and Rina Papio , founder of Green Space Pilipinas . <p> Reactors will include Karla Delgado from Kai Farms and Atty .
