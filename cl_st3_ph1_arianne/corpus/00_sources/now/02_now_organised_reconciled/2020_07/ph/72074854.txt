<p> QUEZON CITY , July 5 -- Contrary to the claims of some stakeholders of the agriculture and fisheries sector , the Department of Agriculture ( DA ) through the Philippine Council for Agriculture and Fisheries ( PCAF ) intensified its policy delivery and other services through innovative means and agency-branded strategies . <p> Despite the fifty percent budget cut for its operating expenses as stipulated under the 2020 General Appropriations Act , coupled with restrictions brought about by the Covid-19-related quarantine measures that further necessitated refocusing 12% of the agency 's budget to other related activities , PCAF ably facilitated various meetings and dialogues with stakeholders . These include thirteen ( 13 ) national dialogues with the poultry , livestock and fisheries and aquaculture sectors , four ( 4 ) region-wide agricultural and fishery council ( AFC ) meetings and supported 115 regional , provincial and city/municipal AFC consultations . These dialogues generated 44 relevant policy recommendations that were all subsequently adopted and endorsed to concerned agencies . <p> The Covid-19 pandemic has also ushered in new thinking and strategies for PCAF to maximize alternative work arrangements - the work from home and skeletal force schemes .
