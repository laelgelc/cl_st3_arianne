The floods have killed 78 people , damaged 97,000 homes and caused 25 billion yuan ( $3.6 billion ) in economic losses , it said . <p> Videos that circulated on social media showed houses collapsing , cars afloat and crops dying in muddy , flooded fields . Multiple cities in southern China also suffered infrastructure damage .
