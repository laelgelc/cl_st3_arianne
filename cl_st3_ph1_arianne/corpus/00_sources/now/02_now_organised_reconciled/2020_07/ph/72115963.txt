<p> QUEZON CITY , July 24 -- " We shall reimagine our relationship with nature by catalyzing structural investments and behavior change . We shall convert this health crisis into opportunities to accelerate climate action . We shall formulate responses to the pandemic through a climate lens , " said Environment Secretary Roy Cimatu yesterday at the " Tatak ng Pagbabago 2020 : The Pre-SONA Forum , " which was held in preparation for President Rodrigo Roa Duterte 's 5th State of the Nation Address ( SONA ) on Monday . <p> The third part of the forum was co-led by the Climate Change Adaptation , Mitigation , and Disaster Risk Reduction Cluster ( CCAM-DRR ) Cluster , which is chaired by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) with the Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) as secretariat . <p> Sec . Cimatu presented the Cluster 's major projects for the past year focusing on scaling up community preparedness against climate hazards and disasters . <p> " No previous pandemic had a more dramatic impact in peoples ' lives in the past century as COVID-19 has in a few short months .
