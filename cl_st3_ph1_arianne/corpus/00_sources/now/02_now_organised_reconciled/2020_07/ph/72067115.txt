<p> QUEZON CITY , July 6 -- Efforts to reduce the vulnerability of agrarian reform areas to climate change was given a boost as the Department of Agrarian Reform ( DAR ) recently turned over two farm machines worth P2.7 million to farmer-members of Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries Organizations ( ARBOs ) in North Cotabato . <p> The Department of Agrarian Reform ( DAR ) recently turned over two farm machines worth P2.7 million to farmer-members of Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries Organizations ( ARBOs ) in North Cotabato . Photo from DAR . <p> Recipients of the farm machines were the Lamuyon Communal Irrigators Association , Inc. of Alamada and the Popoyon CARP Beneficiary Multi-Purpose Cooperative of Tulunan . Both received a 35.5 horsepower four-wheel drive tractor with implements in a separate turn-over ceremonies held on June 23 and June 25 , respectively . <p> " We really want to help our ARBOs through these kinds of interventions , especially those organizations situated in areas that are heavily affected by climate hazards such as floods and dry spell .
