<h> PH accounts for 20% of record 212 environmental activists murdered in 2019 <p> At least 212 environmental campaigners worldwide were murdered in 2019 , making last year the deadliest on record for frontline activists battling the destruction of Nature , watchdog group Global Witness reported Wednesday . <p> Colombia and the Philippines combined accounted for just over half of the confirmed deaths -- 64 and 43 , respectively -- followed by Brazil , Mexico , Honduras and Guatemala . <p> The real number is likely higher due to unreported or misrepresented cases , especially in Africa , the NGO said in its annual review . <p> About 40 percent of victims were indigenous people , and over two-thirds died in Latin America .
