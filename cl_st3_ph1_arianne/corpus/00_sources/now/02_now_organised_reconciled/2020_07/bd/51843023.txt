I do n't know whether the season of floods has advanced , " said AKM Saiful Islam , a professor at the Institute of Water and Flood Management in the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology . <p> As much as 42 percent areas of the country were flooded three years ago , but the floods did not last long like this year when 31 percent of the country has gone under @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ around 4 million people in a total of 31 districts over the past month , according to the disaster management and relief ministry . <p> In 18 of these districts , water rose after receding three times , making entire July the month of flooding . Usually , floodwaters recede in Bangladesh after a week or two . <p> " The water can not move and spread because many roads have been built and they have blocked the tributaries , " Saiful said . <p> " This is a new picture .
