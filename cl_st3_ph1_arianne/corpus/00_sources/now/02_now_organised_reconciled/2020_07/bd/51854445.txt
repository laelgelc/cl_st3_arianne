<h> Role of social safety nets in addressing climate change discussed <p> UNB News <p> Publish- September 02 , 2019 , 07:00 PM <p> UNB NEWS - UNB NEWS <p> Dhaka , Sept 2 ( UNB ) - Representatives from the government , development partners , academia , think tanks and UN agencies came together here on Monday to discuss the role social safety nets play in addressing climate change , building community resilience and helping Bangladesh achieve the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) by 2030 . <p> " This is an important shift in our thought process on disaster management . We 're also at the planning stage for preparing the eighth five-year plan , " said Md Enamur Rahman , State Minister for Disaster Management and Relief . <p> He said this is the right time to organise this symposium on adaptive social protection , and come up with clear policy guidelines , especially on targeting poverty and vulnerability . <p> " Since 2000 , Bangladesh has cut down poverty by half , " said Dandan Chen , World Bank acting Country Director for Bangladesh and Bhutan . <p> " However , there 's still one-fourth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ people living in extreme poverty . By enabling social protection to better address the impacts of climate change , adaptive social protection can be critical in building resilience and creating opportunities for the country 's vulnerable population , " she said . <p> As Bangladesh is well on its way to graduating to a middle-income country status by 2024 , the country 's development trajectory nonetheless faces considerable challenges due to its vulnerability to natural and climate-induced disasters . <p> Over 80 percent of the population is vulnerable to such shocks . <p> Together with rapid urbanisation , these shocks can have far-reaching consequences on the poor and could potentially push more people into poverty . <p> " Because of its unique geographic location , Bangladesh will continue facing adverse effects of global warming and climate change in the future , " said Judith Herbertson , Head of DFID Bangladesh . <p> " DFID is very happy to partner with the government of Bangladesh to address the needs of vulnerable and marginalised people through implementing a range of social protection schemes , such as adaptive social protection . " <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ social protection , disaster risk management and climate change in a holistic manner " said Richard Ragan , WFP Country Director . <p> " The discussions and experience-sharing will help us determine the future direction of adaptive social protection and how it can contribute to the national development agenda in Bangladesh . " <p> Titled " Symposium on Adaptive Social Protection : Technical and Policy Considerations " , the forum was organised by the Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief in partnership with the World Bank , Department for International Development ( DFID ) of the United Kingdom , the Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) and the World Food Programme ( WFP ) .
