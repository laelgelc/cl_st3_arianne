Daryl Vaz , addresses a virtual briefing held on Wednesday ( July 8 ) to provide an update on the Government 's water initiatives and drought management response . <p> The Ministry of Economic Growth and Job Creation has revamped its Drought Management Committee to better equip the body to play a more proactive role in mitigating the impacts of drought on the country . <p> Minister without Portfolio in the Ministry , Hon. Daryl Vaz , made the disclosure during a virtual briefing on Wednesday ( July 8 ) , to provide an update on the Government 's water initiatives and drought management response . <p> He said that the committee will work to improve the resilience and coping mechanisms of the country and spearhead the revision of the existing Drought Management Plan , which was developed in 2000 . <p> " The committee has commenced the revision of the plan and it is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the 2020/21 financial year , " Mr. Vaz said . <p> The IWRMC is a subcommittee of the Integrated Water Resources Management Council ( IWRMC ) for which Cabinet approved the appointment of members in June . <p> The multidisciplinary and multisectoral body , led by the Economic Growth Ministry , is comprised of key stakeholders in the water , physical planning , health , housing , environment and climate change sectors , along with representatives from academia and non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) . <p> Minister Vaz noted that the establishment of the IWRMC seeks to facilitate broad-based consultation among key stakeholders on matters relating to water and water-related resources . <p> " The IWRMC will serve as an advisory body for the management of the island 's water resources as well as advancing the agenda for sustainable development , " he said . <p> In the meantime , Mr. Vaz informed that the Ministry will be developing the framework for a Beneficiary Identification System ( BIS ) . <p> " The BIS will be used to support the provision of social water , which will ensure that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ meet basic needs , " he noted .
