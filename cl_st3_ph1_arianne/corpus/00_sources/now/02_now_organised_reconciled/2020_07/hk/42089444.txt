An excerpt : <p> " Last year 's Congressional Progressive Caucus budget proposed to cut military spending by , in my calculation , 1% . This was not the case in other recent years , when the CPC prominently proposed to end wars and cut particular weapons . With all due respect , how is this censoring of any mention of the military evidence of progressing , rather than regressing ? " <p> I should clarify that when the Progressive Caucus prominently proposed serious cuts to militarism , George W. But as the war in Afghanistan draws to a close , we need a leaner , more agile force to combat realistic twenty-first century threats . " <p> Note that the latest plan is to keep the war on Afghanistan going for decades , and that the CPC has not lifted a finger to end it . So , if that war does n't " draw to a close , " do we still get the " leaner force " ? And what does " agile " mean ? And who gets killed in the " realistic " " agile " wars ? Why only end the one war that people are already pretending has " ended " ? This comes naturally to Congress members , of course , as they 've been conditioned to think of military spending as justified by the jobs it provides in their districts .
