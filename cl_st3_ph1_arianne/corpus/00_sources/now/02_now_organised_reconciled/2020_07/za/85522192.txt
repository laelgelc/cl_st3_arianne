<h> UK pension scheme NEST tightens climate change policy <p> LONDON , July 29 ( Reuters ) - Nest , Britain 's largest pension scheme by number of members , said on Wednesday it would toughen up its climate change investing policy and aimed to fully decarbonise its portfolio by 2050 . <p> The move by the scheme , which invests the retirement savings of 9 million workers , is one of the most ambitious to date and comes as regulatory pressure builds for the industry to better manage climate-related risk . <p> As well as the effects of climate change such as rising sea levels and more extreme weather , companies are at risk of costs associated with regulatory change and litigation linked to the transition to a low-carbon economy . <p> " Just like coronavirus , climate change poses serious risks to both our savers and their investments .
