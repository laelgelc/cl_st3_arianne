<h> Petrobras considers LNG units for pre-salt 's natural gas -executive <p> RIO DE JANEIRO , July 3 ( Reuters ) - Brazilian state-run oil company Petrobras is considering installing offshore units to liquefy the growing natural gas production at the so-called pre-salt area , Viviana Coelho , the company 's emissions and climate change manager , said during a webinar on Friday . <p> The LNG units could be an alternative , she said , for the natural gas associated with oil produced at the pre-salt , a deep-water exploratory region located more than 100 miles from the coast . <p> It was unclear if the program has a timeframe . <p> Petroleo Brasileiro SA , as the firm is formally known , currently relies on offshore pipelines to bring natural gas to the coast , where it is processed . ( Reporting by Sabrina Valle Editing by Chris Reese )
