<h> Nornickel board sets up environmental task team in wake of Arctic spill <p> 2nd July 2020 By : Mariaan Webb - Creamer Media Deputy Editor Online <p> Global palladium and high-grade nickel leader Nornickel has created an independent environmental task team of the board of directors to monitor progress in the Russian major 's environmental programmes . <p> The management team included Andrey Bougrov as Nornickel 's senior VP , who will oversee the company 's sustainable development efforts . <p> Chairperson Gareth Penny said that the task team would lean on the services of independent specialist firms to investigate current issues and to ensure progress in the delivery of the group 's programmes . <p> " Today , we face greater challenges relevant for everyone around the globe -- climate change and its impact on the Arctic region and our operations , " Penny in a July 2 statement . <p> The announcement follows a major diesel spill at the company 's operations in Russia 's Far North at the beginning at the end of May . About 20 000 t of diesel spilled from a reservoir owned Nornickel 's power and utility unit .
