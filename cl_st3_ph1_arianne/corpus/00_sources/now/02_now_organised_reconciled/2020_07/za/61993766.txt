" Global leaders have known about viruses such as Covid-19 for a very long time . If you consider that since the 1980s , similar viruses have killed millions of people , Mother Nature has given humanity plenty of warnings , such as the SARS endemic , which although bad , caused few economic ramifications . There are bad decisions , there is being naive , but by far the worst is being ignorant , " he adds . <p> Speaking of the latter , Murray-Kline says there 's idiocy around the view that increasing tax on higher-earning businesses or individuals is the answer to solve inequality in South Africa . There are enough taxes to achieve those goals if it were only spent where it was needed .
