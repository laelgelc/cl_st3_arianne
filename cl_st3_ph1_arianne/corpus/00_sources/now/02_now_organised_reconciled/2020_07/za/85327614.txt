<p> RIO DE JANEIRO , July 3 ( Reuters ) - Brazilian state-controlled oil company Petrobras is considering installing offshore units to liquefy the growing natural gas production from the so-called pre-salt area , the company 's emissions and climate change manager said during a webinar on Friday . <p> Liquefied natural gas units could be an alternative for the gas associated with oil produced at the deep-water exploratory region located more than 100 miles off the coast , said Viviana Coelho . It was unclear if a projected program has a timeframe . <p> Petroleo Brasileiro SA , as the firm is formally known , currently relies on offshore pipelines to bring natural gas produced offshore to the coast for processing . <p> Lack of infrastructure to ship offshore natural gas to the coast is seen as a possible limitation for rising oil production at the pre-salt , Petrobras has said . <p> The reservoirs , where oil is trapped beneath a thick layer of salt in the Atlantic seabed , have supplied more than 65% of Brazil 's production in just over a decade since their discovery . The rate is expected to keep rising . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ systems at the platforms . <p> Brazil has strict legislation to limit flaring , and Petrobras wants to reduce its routine flaring emissions to zero by 2030 , Coelho said in the presentation . <p> Flaring releases methane , and Petrobras aims to reduce the company 's greenhouse gas emissions by 30% to 50% by 2025 , she said . <p> The Brazilian company used 97.6% of its natural gas in May , according to Brazil 's oil regulator . A significant portion is reinjected into the ground to control reservoir pressure and increase oil production , Petrobras said .
