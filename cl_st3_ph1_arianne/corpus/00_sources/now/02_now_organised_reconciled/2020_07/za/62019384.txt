<h> Women bear the brunt of climate change in Angola <p> By afp.com - 11 March 2020 <p> A woman tends to crops in a village near Lubango in Angola in February . Women face risk of attacks , including rape , when they fetch water for their crops in the Huila province in Angola , where a recent drought dried up most water sources and devastated crops across the southern Africa region .
