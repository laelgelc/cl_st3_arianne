Coun. Kiley is a well-known and passionately vocal defender of the environment and should be commended for his leadership on this subject . If the owners of this proposed facility have faith in the hydrological reports they submitted with their application , they should have no issue providing a substantive financial bond for a period of at least 10 years . <p> I am also greatly concerned that Kingston city staff have refused to further investigate this proposal and how the Ontario Water Resources Act may apply . The purpose of the act is to provide for the conservation , protection and management of Ontario 's waters and for their efficient and sustainable use , in order to promote Ontario 's long-term environmental , social and economic well-being . Otherwise , how are we addressing the questions of hundreds of residents who will be directly affected by the approval of this proposal ? How else can we assure them of the sustainability of their private wells ? <p> This is where consistency is key . We are the council of the Climate Emergency Declaration .
