<h> Trudeau wants to get serious about climate change.This is how he should start . <p> Canada 's new prime minister , Justin Trudeau , has declared that our country 's years of being a " less-than-enthusiastic actor " on climate change policy are over . Public expectations on the issue are high . But what exactly should the federal government do ? <p> Mr. Trudeau acknowledges that an ambitious target for greenhouse gas reductions is worthless without an effective and credible plan to achieve it . He understands that such a plan must involve the provinces and territories , which have jurisdiction over resources and wield important policy tools of their own . Accordingly , he has promised to sit down with the premiers soon to devise a coordinated national plan . <p> As a first principle , all segments of the population must be involved in the fight against climate change . The Canadian Council of Chief Executives -- representing 150 leading employers across the country -- has been on record since 2007 about the need to put a price on carbon .
