<h> 2018 needs to be year feds put flesh on bones of climate change framework <p> THE CANADIAN PRESS -- One year ago , Prime Minister Justin Trudeau got eight provinces and all three territories to agree to put a price on pollution as part of a national climate change plan . <p> The Pan Canadian Framework on Clean Growth and Climate Change is a mouthful to say but it was considered by many to be the first time Canada had , at least on paper , an actual strategy to meet its international commitments to cut greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Twelve months later , environmental advocates say 2018 is going to have to be a banner year for environmental legislation , regulation and strategies or the climate plan is going to end up on the scrap heap of climate promises past . <p> At least six major environment policies , bills or strategies to implement parts of the framework are expected in 2018 .
