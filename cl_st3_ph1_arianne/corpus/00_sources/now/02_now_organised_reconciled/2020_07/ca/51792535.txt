<h> Toronto News Weather &amp; Traffic CTV News Toronto <h> Heat warning issued for Toronto ahead of July 's first weekend <p> A man runs through Humber Bay Shores in Toronto on Tuesday , August 7 , 2018 . THE CANADIAN PRESS/Christopher Katsarov <p> SHARE <p> TORONTO --A heat warning has been issued for Toronto as temperatures are expected to rise significantly over the next few days . <p> Environment Canada warned that daytime temperatures between Thursday and Sunday will range between 31 C and 33 C. <p> On Wednesday , the weather agency also said that overnight temperatures will range between 20 C and 22 C for the next few days . <p> " Environment and Climate Change Canada issues a heat warning when it forecasts two or more consecutive days with daytime maximum temperatures of 31 C or warmer , together with minimum nighttime temperatures of 20 C or warmer , " the weather agency said . <p> The City of Toronto has said that 15 emergency cooling centres will be open starting at 11 a.m. on Thursday for the entire duration of the heat warning . <p> " Extreme heat is associated with negative health impacts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " the city said in a statement Friday evening . <p> " During periods of hot weather , the safety of all residents is the priority . " <p> The city has launched an interactive map online for people looking for an emergency cooling centre near them . <p> " The emergency cooling centres will offer a publically accessible , air-conditioned place for residents to rest indoors and receive a cool drink , " the city said . <p> " Staff who are trained to assist residents affected by the extreme heat will be on hand . Strict infection prevention and control measures will be in place to help prevent the spread of COVID-19 . " <p> All the centres will operate during the heat warnings from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. except the Metro Hall located 55 John Street , which will run 24 hours .
