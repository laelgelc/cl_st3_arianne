<h> The Drilldown : Alberta to make new requirements for oil well clean-up as 91,000 inactive wells loom over province <p> Alberta is in the process of implementing a new liability management framework that will assist with the province 's failed oil and gas well program . The framework will outline a series of regulations concerning mandatory well cleanup , and instead of having a binding timeline wherein companies are required to clean up their wells , the province will develop annual spending targets for site closure over a five-year rolling period . <p> There will also be an opt-in function where landowners can nominate well sites for clean-up . The Alberta Energy Regulator ( AER ) will review applications and well operators will be asked to give reason why they believe a site should not be reclaimed within a certain time frame . <p> Companies applying for regulatory approval of new wells will have to prove to the AER that it will be able to afford the clean-up process by showing that they have twice as many assets as liabilities . <p> " What we 're trying to do with the mandatory reduction target is incorporate and emphasize @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future generations are n't left in a position of cleaning up inventory that accumulated under previous generations , " Alberta Energy Minister Sonya Savage said to The Globe and Mail . <p> There are currently over 91,000 inactive wells in Alberta , and the current regulations surrounding site clean-up were put in place more than twenty years ago .
