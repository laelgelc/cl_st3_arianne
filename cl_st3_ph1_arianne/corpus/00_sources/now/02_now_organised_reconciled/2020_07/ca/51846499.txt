<h> Possible record daily high in Grande Prairie prompts call for caution in heat <p> UPDATE ( 5:10 p.m . July 27 ) : According to Environment Canada , the temperature at 4 and 5 p.m . Monday hit 30.4 degrees at the Grande Prairie Airport , just two-tenths of a degree short of the daily record of 30.6 degrees set in 1932 . <p> With the possibility of having a record breaking temperature on Monday in Grande Prairie , a meteorologist with Environment and Climate Change Canada is warning the public to be cautious , and to look out for others . <p> The forecast for July 27 , 2020 , calls for a high of 32 degrees . The previous record high temperature for that day was set in 1932 , when the temperature was recorded at 30.6 degrees .
