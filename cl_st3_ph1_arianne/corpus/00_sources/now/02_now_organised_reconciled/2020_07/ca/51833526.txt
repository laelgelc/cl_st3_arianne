In places where sea ice has disappeared for the summer , bears must fast and live off of their fat stores until they can resume hunting . <p> For the bears , the question is " how full is your tank before you have to get through this period ? " Dr. Molnar said . <p> In the study , the team looked at the maximum and minimum amount of fat that adult bears can store on their bodies and linked this to the viability of populations in different ecoregions as sea ice diminished and periods of open water increased .
