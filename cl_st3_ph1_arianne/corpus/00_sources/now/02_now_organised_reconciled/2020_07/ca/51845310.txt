<p> Lake Huron is five degrees warmer than average for this time of year , according to data from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Association 's ( NOAA ) Great Lakes Environmental Research Laboratory . <p> Water level and water temperatures are important and interrelated indicators of weather and climate change in the Great Lakes .
