But an equally alarming situation is unfolding just north of Siberia 's shores : sea ice is crashing in a region that scientists consider to be the ice factory of the Arctic . <p> In fact , there 's so little ice cover in the Laptev Sea north of Siberia -- as well as the Barents Sea to the west -- that ice cover across the entire Arctic Ocean is currently at its lowest mid-July extent on record . If sea ice continues to plummet , it could bottom out at a new record low in September .
