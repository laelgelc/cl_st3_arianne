<h> A mixed bag of weather will produce some sun , rain and possible storms <p> A gloomy looking weather forecast over the next week may not be as bad as it looks at first glance . <p> Environment and Climate Change Canada Meteorologist Terri Lang told northeastNOW while there is plenty of cloud in their forecast for the northeast and Prince Albert regions , the cloud and showers are n't hovering around consistently . <p> Lang said they have a lot of weather systems moving through rather quickly , as opposed to a low pressure system sticking around to bring rain , or high pressure to produce consistent sunshine . She said we can expect a mixed bag of unsettled weather into the middle of next week . <p> " Take the sunny breaks while you can , " Lang advised . " Normally in this kind of weather pattern we 'll start out generally in the day sort of sunnier into the early afternoon , and that 's when the showers and thundershowers tend to develop . " <p> Lang said the forecast looks pessimistic , but she believes it will be nicer than how it looks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the middle of next week , with slightly cooler than normal highs for a couple of days . <p> " But as that ridge starts to roll in at the end of next week , temperatures go up , we 're talking ... mid to high 20s , " Lang said . " I think that would be welcome to see that , it 's been kind of an unsettled summer so far . " <p> Lang said the one positive to take from the periodic showers is that it helps keep the wildfire threat low .
