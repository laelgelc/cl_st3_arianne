Five million tonnes of CO2 is equal to the annual emissions from about 1.25 million cars . The cost to operate Quest is about 35% lower than what was forecast in 2015 , and if Quest were to be built today , it would cost about 30% less thanks to capital efficiency improvements . <p> The Quest CCS facility , which celebrated its start-up on November 6 , 2015 , was designed to capture about one million tonnes of CO2 each year from oil sands operations and safely store the CO2 more than 2km underground in a sandstone rock reservoir .
