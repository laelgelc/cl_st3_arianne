Natural Resources Minister Seamus O'Regan is expected to make an official announcement on the appointment Thursday afternoon . <p> De Silva was named Alberta 's top trade representative in 2015 by former NDP premier Rachel Notley , after serving for years as deputy minister in the province 's trade department .
