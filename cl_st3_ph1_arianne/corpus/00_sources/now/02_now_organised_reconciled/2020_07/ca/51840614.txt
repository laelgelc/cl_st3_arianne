<h> YouTube recommendation of misinformation below 1% : Top executive <p> New Delhi , July 24 ( IANS ) Under the scanner for its recommendation algorithms , a top YouTube executive on Friday said consumption of borderline content or harmful misinformation videos that comes from its recommendations is significantly below one per cent of content available on the platform . <p> Borderline content refers to content that comes close to , but does not cross the line of violating YouTube 's policies . <p> This work has focused on four pillars -- removing violative content , raising up authoritative content , reducing the spread of borderline content and rewarding trusted creators -- the 4Rs of responsibility , he said . <p> YouTube removed over 820,000 videos in India alone in the quarter ending March this year . <p> The comments come amid criticisms linked to spread of misinformation and conspiracy theories ? even those related to Covid-19 pandemic . <p> The YouTube Chief Product Officer said the company is taking a look at everything it does through the " responsibility lens " . <p> " We 've spent the last few years investing in systems to tackle the challenges @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ been working hard to invest in the policies , resources and products needed to protect the YouTube community , " Mohan noted . <p> " The vast majority of content on YouTube is not harmful -- in fact this harmful content is a very small part of what 's on YouTube -- a fraction of 1 per cent , " he said . <p> YouTube last year introduced over 30 different changes to reduce recommendations of borderline content and harmful misinformation , including climate change misinformation and other types of conspiracy videos . <p> YouTube said it updated its policies barring hate and harassment to quickly remove any content that violates these policies . <p> " We also updated our policies to remove egregious medical misinformation about COVID .
