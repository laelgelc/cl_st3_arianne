Given the centrality of this proposal it merits special attention . I see three core weaknesses with it : <p> 1 . While $30 a tonne may be an acceptable starting point , holding at this low level ( as the Greens do for their five-year budget plan ) , the fee is unlikely to bring about the kinds of sweeping consumption and investment changes the party hopes . While many like to point to the success of B.C. 's carbon tax , the simple reality is that GHG emissions in BC have increased every year since 2010 ( we have data up to 2013 ) , with a big drop in 2009 primarily due to the recession .
