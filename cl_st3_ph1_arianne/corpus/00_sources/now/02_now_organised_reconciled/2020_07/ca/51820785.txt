( Jason Franson/The Canadian Press ) <p> Premier Jason Kenney 's campaign manager and an outspoken human-caused global-warming skeptic has been hired as the Alberta Energy Regulator 's new vice president of its science and innovation branch . <p> But the decision to hire John Weissenberger will further undermine the public 's trust in the provincial regulator , a leading academic says . <p> " There is n't much trust to be lost at this point , " said University of Calgary law professor Martin Olszynski , who studies energy regulation law . " There should be no trust . " <p> AER has come under fire following the recent appointment of Laurie Pushor as chief executive officer . Pushor was a central figure in a Saskatchewan government land-deal scandal that cost taxpayers millions of dollars . <p> Also , the AER 's recent decisions to suspend oil sands environmental monitoring during the pandemic , its failure to address the province 's massive orphan well and oil @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ self-dealing by senior AER executives have been heavily criticized . <p> " I mean it appears the premier is essentially just stacking the AER with people who think exactly like him , " Olszynski said . <p> Kenney told reporters he had nothing to do with Weissenberger 's hiring by the AER and only learned about it a few days ago . <p> " He was not appointed by the government , " Kenney said .
