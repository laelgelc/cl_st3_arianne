<h> Wet weather to continue in the Peace Region following wet week <p> What was a wet week in the Peace Country last week is expected to give way to some more showers this week , according to Environment and Climate Change Canada . <p> The national weather office says between 45 and 70 mm of rain soaked the Peace Region between Tuesday night and Saturday , including setting a Canada Day record for rainfall in Grande Prairie , when 37.1 mm fell on July 1 . <p> With more rain in the forecast , Meteorologist Alysa Pederson says July is setting up to be one of the area 's wetter months on record . <p> " We 're looking at an average for June being about 70 ( mm of rainfall ) , and an average for July being 69 mm . So , a five-day total , it does cross the month , but we 're pretty high up there for how wet it is this year , compared to what the average is .
