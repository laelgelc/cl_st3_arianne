For more information , visit the Sustainable Orillia website . <p> COVID-19 has curtailed many of the activities that we took for granted up until earlier this year : going out for dinner , taking in a movie , having friends over , going to a concert , or planning a vacation . <p> While some of the prohibited activities are opening up once again as we seek ways to return to some semblance of normal , other activities may remain out of reach for some time . <p> Given this reality , many of us may be looking at what we can do with the money we might otherwise have spent on concerts , restaurant meals , and travel . ( Pool companies , apparently , have more work than they can handle this summer . ) <p> This week 's tip from Sustainable Orillia is to consider a home renovation ( or retrofit ) which will save you money in reduced energy costs . <p> BF Nagy , author @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change , provides a " top 10 " for homeowners who might want to make their homes more energy-efficient . <p> He lists them in order of their impact on saving energy . Here they are : <p> Focus first on optimizing the building envelope ( insulation and double- or triple-pane windows ) <p> Invest in smart home technology , including a user-friendly thermostat . <p> Replace old lighting with LED lighting . <p> Replace old , inefficient appliances with Energy Star models <p> Use power strips ( to cut power to appliances when not using them ) . <p> Add drain water heat recovery to your shower pipe . <p> Choose modern low-flow plumbing ( toilets , faucets and showerheads ) <p> Nagy 's book is persuasive . While he acknowledges that some steps -- for example , " triple-pane argon windows " -- are expensive , he also shows that the savings that can result from taking the steps above will pay in energy-cost savings for the work you have had done , and often much more quickly than one might expect . <p> It may be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we enjoy , whether attending a concert , a Raptors game , or travelling . Why not turn this into a positive by both reducing your carbon footprint ( by reducing your energy use ) and then saving yourself some money ( also by reducing your energy use ) . That 's win-win for you and the planet . <p> Nagy 's book can be ordered from Manticore Books in Orillia .
