<h> DND fined $175k for having no ID number for petroleum storage tank system <p> Canada 's defence department has been fined $175,000 for operating a petroleum storage tank system without an identification number . <p> The Department of National Defence ( DND ) pled guilty Monday in an Alberta Provincial Court for committing an offence under the Canadian Environmental Protection Act . The $175,000 fine will be allocated to the federal government 's Environmental Damages Fund , to be used in support of environmental and conservation projects . <p> The fund works to direct court-awarded penalties for environmental offences to projects with positive environmental impacts . <p> In addition to the fine , the court ordered DND to complete a third-party environmental audit of the Canadian Forces Base Cold Lake and the Cold Lake Air Weapons Range , including a review of the department 's environmental-management systems . <p> Operators of petroleum storage tank systems that fall under federal jurisdiction must obtain an identification number from the environment minister to ensure compliance and reduce the risk of releases that can contaminate soil and groundwater . <p> Environment and Climate Change Canada said the charge against DND came after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Climate Change Canada enforces laws that protect Canada 's air , water , and natural environment , and we take this responsibility very seriously , " the department said in a statement announcing the conviction on Thursday .
