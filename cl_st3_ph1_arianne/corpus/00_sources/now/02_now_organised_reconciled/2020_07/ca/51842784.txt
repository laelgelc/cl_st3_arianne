C or warmer , together with minimum nighttime temperatures of 20 ? C or warmer , or when there is a forecast of two or more consecutive days with humidex values expected to reach 40 or higher . <p> Extreme heat is associated with negative health impacts ranging from heat stress to heat stroke and death . During periods of hot weather , the safety of all residents is the priority .
