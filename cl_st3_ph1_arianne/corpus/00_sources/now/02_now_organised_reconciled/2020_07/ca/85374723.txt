<h> Michael Liebreich appointed as Eavor Advisory Board Chair <p> CALGARY , Alberta and LONDON , July 09 , 2020 ( GLOBE NEWSWIRE ) -- Eavor Technologies Inc . ( " Eavor " ) , a global leader in the development of closed-loop geothermal power generation technology , has appointed Michael Liebreich to chair a new Advisory Board as it moves forward with its first commercial projects . <p> Founded in Alberta , Canada in late 2017 , Eavor has developed a proprietary , closed-loop approach to geothermal heat and power . Unlike Enhanced Geothermal Systems ( ESG ) , it involves no fracking , no GHG emissions , no earthquake risk , no water use , no produced brine or solids , and no aquifer contamination . Eavor systems are flexible and dispatchable , they can operate as standalone , supplement a grid , or be hybridized with wind and solar . <p> Michael Liebreich will assume the role of Chair of a new Advisory Board which will help guide the commercialization of Eavor 's technology .
