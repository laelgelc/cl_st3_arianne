<h> COVID-19 crisis outlines road map for climate action initiatives <p> A new CO2 pipeline commissioned last month in Alberta presents opportunity for industries and refineries to invest in carbon capture Submitted <p> If there 's a silver lining to the global COVID-19 pandemic , it 's that a self-induced global recession is expected to drive greenhouse gas emissions down by about 8% in 2020 , and there is now an opportunity to make that decline " structural , " according to the International Energy Agency ( IEA ) . <p> The IEA urges governments to focus post-pandemic economic stimulus spending and policies on a clean energy transition -- one that will create new jobs and stimulate economies , while holding greenhouse gases ( GHGs ) at or below pre-pandemic levels . <p> The plan 's goal is to prevent GHGs from spiking back up the way they did after the 2008-09 recession . <p> " After the financial crash in 2008 , there was a rapid rebound in the use of fossil fuels , " said Michael Bradshaw , professor of global energy at the University of Warwick 's Warwick Business School . It urges Ottawa to focus efforts on fuel-switching ( from coal power to natural gas ) and to consider nuclear power as " a serious option .
