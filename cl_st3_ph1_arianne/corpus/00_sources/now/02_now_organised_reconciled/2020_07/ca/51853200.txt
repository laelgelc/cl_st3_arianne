Hydro , constructed a hydroelectric dam in the upper portion of the Coquitlam River . <p> According to a Metro Vancouver staff report , this led to the destruction of coho salmon in the reservoir about 105 years ago . <p> " Returning coho salmon have not had access to waters upstream of Coquitlam Dam for spawning and rearing habitat since the first large dam on the Coquitlam River was completed by the Vancouver Power Company in 1915 , " the report recalled . <p> The report stated that coho salmon were " extirpated " in the upper reaches of the river " approximately 105 years " .
