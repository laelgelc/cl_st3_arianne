<h> Hotter-than-average summer possible <p> Hotter-than-average weather may persist throughout the summer in Hastings and Prince Edward counties , current Environment Canada forecasts show . <p> " There 's a better chance of above-normal temperatures than below ... for the rest of the summer , " federal meteorologist Gerald Cheng said Friday in a telephone interview . <p> He cautioned long-range forecasting is risky because conditions are so variable . A week ago , he said , it appeared the heat wave would extend beyond its ninth day and into the weekend . But instead , forecasts predicted lower -- but still hot -- conditions for the weekend . <p> Cheng said heat warnings could return by mid-week . <p> In terms of heat , he said , " It seems like this summer is the summer that keeps on giving .
