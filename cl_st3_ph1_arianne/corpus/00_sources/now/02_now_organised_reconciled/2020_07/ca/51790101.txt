<h> Market News <h> Bank of England gives banks 18 months to manage climate risks <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Banks and insurers in Britain must " fully embed " their approach to dealing with risks from climate change in their business by the end of 2021 , Bank of England Deputy Governor Sam Woods said on Wednesday . <p> " There are some areas of our expectations where few barriers exist to full implementation , but we recognise that challenges remain in others , " Woods said in a letter to heads of banks and insurers . <p> " Where challenges exist we will work closely with firms to understand how they are seeking to overcome them .
