<h> John Hartmann and William Onuwa elected as Directors of Boyd Group Services Inc . <p> /NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION TO U.S. NEWSWIRE SERVICES OR FOR DISSEMINATION IN THE UNITED STATES. / <p> WINNIPEG , MB , June 30 , 2020 /CNW/ - Boyd Group Services Inc . ( TSX : BYD ) ( " BGSI " , " the Boyd Group " or " Boyd " ) is pleased to announce the election of John Hartmann and William Onuwa to its Board of Directors at its Annual Meeting and Special Meeting held on Monday , June 29 , 2020 in Winnipeg . <p> Mr. John Hartmann was recently appointed to the role of COO of Bed Bath and Beyond , having previously held the position of CEO of True Value Company , and brings significant leadership experience to the Board . Dr. William Onuwa , who holds a Doctorate degree in Business Management , is currently the Chief Audit Executive at Royal Bank of Canada , having previously held the position of SVP &amp; Chief Risk Officer , Wealth Management at RBC Georgia and Insurance Group Canada . <p> " The Board has an ongoing commitment to renewal and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to support the growth of Boyd 's business .
