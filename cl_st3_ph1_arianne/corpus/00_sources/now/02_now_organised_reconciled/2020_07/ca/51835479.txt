<h> The Drilldown : Michigan 's Department of Natural Resources asks Enbridge to help pay for potential oil spill <p> Michigan 's Department of Natural Resources requested on Friday for Canadian company Enbridge Inc. to guarantee to pay for a certain amount of potential future oil spills . <p> Michigan is particularly concerned about a potential leak from Enbridge 's dual pipelines into the channel that crosses two Great Lakes . Dan Eichinger , the department 's director , requested the company have $900 million in liability insurance and have another $1.88 billion additional funds in case a spill occurs . <p> Although Enbridge has said the underwater section of their twin pipes built in 1953 is stable , the lines have been criticized as a hazard by environmental advocacy groups and Democrats including Governor Gretchen Whitmer . The company did agree in 2018 with former Republican Governor Rick Synder 's administration to decommission the twin pipeline following its replacement by another .
