Droplets in clouds can super-cool to below water 's freezing point , waiting for the appropriate nudge to convert into the solid state . That nudge can come from organic aerosols or even large molecules , which act as ice-nucleating particles . The chemistries and sources of those particles are many and varied : forest fires , paradoxically , create a broth of particulates that can drive freezing in clouds . Because clouds and snow can adjust the Earth 's reflectivity , or albedo , understanding that journey from fire to ice is critical to modelling the planet 's energy balance .
