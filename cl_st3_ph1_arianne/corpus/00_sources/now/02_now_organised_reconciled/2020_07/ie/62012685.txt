<h> Covid-19 Friday : no deaths , 20 new cases <p> THERE have been no new deaths reported to the Health Protection Surveillance Centre today . <p> There has now been a total of 1,763 Covid-19 related deaths in Ireland . <p> As of midnight Thursday 23rd July , the HPSC has been notified of 20 confirmed cases . There is now a total of 25,845* confirmed cases in Ireland . <p> The HSE is working to identify any contacts the patients may have had to provide them with information and advice to prevent further spread . <p> Dr Ronan Glynn , acting chief medical officer , Department of Health , said : ' Covid-19 is a highly infectious disease that is still circulating in our communities . It is a dangerous illness that no-one wants to catch . <p> ' While we have reason to be positive , we now need to continue to work together towards our collective goal of resuming healthcare services , reopening our children 's schools , and protecting the most vulnerable . <p> ' The past weeks have shown that when we maintain physical distance , wash our hands , wear a face @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ together we can interrupt the spread of Covid-19 .
