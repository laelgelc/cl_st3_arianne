The report highlighted that the Office of the Press Ombudsman received 261 complaints in 2016 , down from 278 in the previous year . The complaints related to articles published in national and local newspapers , magazines and online-only news publications . In 2016 the Press Ombudsman made 23 decisions , down from 34 the previous year . Nine complaints were upheld in 2016 .
