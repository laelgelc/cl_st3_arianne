<p> Rothamsted Research has appointed Professor Angela Karp as its new director and chief executive . <p> Karp had been acting as interim director since December last year , in addition to her role as director for science innovation , engagement and partnerships . She is the first female director of the institute since it was founded in 1843 . <p> She has more than 35 years ' experience in crop genetics and breeding for food and bio-renewables . She has more than 130 refereed publications , co-authored a textbook on genetics , and is co-editor of books on genetics and bioenergy . <p> In 2007 , she received the Royal Agricultural Society of England Research Medal and , in 2008 , the Alfred-Toepfer Prize for her research achievements . <p> More recently Karp has led the institute 's overall strategy on external engagement and partnerships .
