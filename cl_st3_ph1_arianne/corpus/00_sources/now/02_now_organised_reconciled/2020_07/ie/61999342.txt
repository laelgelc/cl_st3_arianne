<h> FRC seeks members , views for stakeholder reporting panel <p> The UK Financial Reporting Council has put out a call for interested parties to take part in a new project under the auspices of its Financial Reporting Lab . <p> The initiative will examine corporate disclosures in the UK about so-called company stakeholders . <p> In a statement , the FRC said the project will include corporate reporting obligations under section 172 of the Companies Act 2006 . <p> With effect from 1 January 2019 , UK companies have been required to report specifically on their compliance with that section of the Act . <p> It said that company directors must act in such a way as to " promote the success of the company for the benefit of its members as a whole " . <p> In doing so , the section goes on to explain that directors should consider a number of factors and stakeholders such as the firm 's workforce , its suppliers and even its impact on the wider environment . <p> Back in 2017 , Pensions &amp; Investment Research Consultants Limited and the FRC found themselves embroiled in a row involving the UK @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to report on their compliance with s172 . <p> Climate change campaigners ClientEarth have also argued that companies are not complying with the law and filed two regulatory complaints with the FRC . <p> The government subsequently issued The Companies ( Miscellaneous Reporting ) Regulations 2018 to force companies to explain how they have complied with s172 . <p> In addition to s172 , the new FRC Reporting Lab project will initially look at a range of other issues linked to stakeholder reporting such as : <p> how companies engage with stakeholders ; <p> which aspects of reporting it is that companies find most challenging ; and
