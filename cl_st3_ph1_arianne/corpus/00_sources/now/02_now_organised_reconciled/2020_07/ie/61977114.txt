<p> The Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) has today published its greenhouse gas emissions projections for the period 2019-2040 -- which expects to see an agricultural reduction of 16.5Mt of CO2 equivalent over the next decade . <p> According to the EPA , the projections show Ireland can meet its current EU target to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 30% by 2030 . <p> This would require full implementation of the measures in the 2019 Climate Action Plan and would result in 3% average annual emissions reductions from 2021 to 2030 . <p> The EPA projections show significant emission reductions across transport , the energy sector and households with emissions from agriculture also projected to decrease . <p> These emission reductions are to be achieved through a range of actions , committed to in the Climate Action Plan . <p> These measures overall are projected to contribute to emissions savings of 79Mt CO2 equivalent by 2030 , the agency notes . <p> Regarding transport , the EPA says " almost one million electric vehicles on our roads by 2030 , including 840,000 passenger EVs and 95,000 electric vans and trucks , will help achieve a projected decrease in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2030 " . <p> Advertisement <p> Meanwhile , with agriculture , the EPA says : " A reduction of at least 16.5Mt CO2 equivalent between 2021 and 2030 , by implementing the measures such as low emission slurry spreading ( LESS ) techniques and switching to stabilised urea fertilisers for crops and pasture . This , the EPA predicts , should deliver by 2030 an estimated 34% reduction in energy industries emissions , a 53% reduction in residential emissions and a 36% reduction in commercial and public services emissions . <p> Increased ambition at national and EU level to keep the global temperature increase to 1.5 ? will , however , necessitate a further step-up , additional to the Climate Action Plan , in the pace and scale of emission reductions , the EPA warns . <p> In addition , achievement of a low carbon pathway for Ireland and meeting future targets relies on maximising the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ management of , for example , forestry , grasslands and wetlands . <p> Commenting , Stephen Treacy , senior manager with the EPA , said : <p> Appropriate land management is a vital part of action on climate change , not just in Ireland but also across Europe and globally . <p> " Where land management is providing a store of carbon , this should be maintained or enhanced .
