<h> Air Liquide signs power purchase agreement for renewable electricity <p> Air Liquide has today signed a long-term power purchase agreement ( PPA ) to source renewable electricity to power some of its industrial and medical gas production assets in Spain . <p> The contract marks the first PPA for renewable energy in Europe , illustrating Air Liquide 's commitment to lower its carbon footprint , and supporting its climate objectives to increase purchase of renewable electricity by nearly 70% by 2050 . <p> Under the ten-year agreement , the French industrial gas giant said it will use renewable electricity equivalent to 15% of the Group 's current consumption for its Spanish activites . <p> To support the agreement , a wind farm is currently under development in Andalusia that will supply Air Liquide with renewable electricity , saving the company 250,000 tonnes of CO2 emissions over the term of the contract .
