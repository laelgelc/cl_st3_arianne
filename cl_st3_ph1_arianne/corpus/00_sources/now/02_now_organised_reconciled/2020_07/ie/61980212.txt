<h> Brazil ministers meet investors to discuss deforestation fears <p> Nordic pension funds AP2 and KLP and several asset managers came face to face with Brazilian government officials and the head of the central bank in an online meeting yesterday as the South American country took time to hear investor fears over deforestation in the Amazon . <p> The meeting took place as a follow-up to an open letter sent to Brazilian embassies by financial institutions last month . <p> Besides Swedish national pension buffer fund AP2 and Norwegian municipal pensions provider KLP , several European pension funds were among that letter 's signatories , including the Church of England Pensions Board , Northern LGPS , Surrey Pension Fund , AP4 and MP Pension . <p> Norway 's Storebrand Asset Management is leading and coordinating the " public policy dialogue " with Brazilian embassies in several European countries , as well as Japan , and the US . So far , 34 investors representing over $4.6trn ( ? 4trn ) have joined . <p> Commenting on this week 's meeting , Jan Erik Saugestad , chief executive officer of Storebrand , said : ? " <p> He said it was only through collaboration between governments , companies and investors that the necessary changes could be achieved , and that this marked a start in the process . <p> Saugestad said the investor group was convinced that sustainability and economic development went hand in hand . <p> " As financial institutions , we see deforestation and the associated impacts on biodiversity and climate change as systemic risks that have the potential to negatively impact returns , " he said . <p> Because of this , he said , it was in the interests of both companies and investors to achieve a regulatory framework that was consistent , long-term and protected the forest . <p> Storebrand said five issues were raised at the meeting that would be important for the financial institutions ' assessment of developments in the future . <p> These included a significant reduction in deforestation rates , the prevention of fires in or near forest area , and the enforcement of Brazil 's forest code . <p> Meanwhile , in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ minister Cristina addressed a land ownership regulation that is due to be voted on by Brazil 's congress and that investors had expressed concerns about . <p> She said the meeting had served to clarify to investors that the bill would be important for the environmental preservation of the Amazon . <p> According to Cristina , the investors had wanted to know whether the law could lead to increased deforestation in the Amazon , but the minister said that on the contrary , the new law would bring land under named ownership . <p> " They will have the title , and therefore they will have to comply with the government 's environmental rules , " she said . <p> According to Reuters , in the meeting yesterday Brazil 's government also announced it planned to ban setting fires in the Amazon for 120 days .
