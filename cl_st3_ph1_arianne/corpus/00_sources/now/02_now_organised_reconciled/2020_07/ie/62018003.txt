<h> Ireland produces greatest share of its electricity from onshore wind <p> Ireland is the number one ranking country in the world for producing the greatest share of its electricity from onshore wind , according to a new report . <p> The annual Renewables 2020 Global Status Report from multi-stakeholder governance group REN21 shows that Denmark produces the most amount of electricity using both on and offshore wind energy combined ; however , Ireland produces the most amount of energy onshore . <p> The report states that last year , 32.5% of Ireland 's electricity came from wind power . While this is second to Denmark overall , which met an estimated 47% of its energy demand from wind , the Danes have the " advantage of a large and developed offshore wind industry " . <p> According to the report , Ireland is also one of only four countries in the world -- the others are Denmark , Germany and Uruguay -- which met more than 30% of demand last year from variable renewable electricity like wind or solar power . <h> ' Every year we are cutting more CO2 emissions ' <p> CEO of the Irish Wind @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as an " incredible achievement for a country like Ireland " . <p> " Last year was a record-breaking year for the industry , " Dr. Connolly said . <p> " We are confident that our numbers will be even better for 2020 -- wind energy provided almost half of Ireland 's electricity during the first three months of this year . <p> Every year we are cutting more carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) emissions , reducing Ireland 's dependency on imported fossil fuels and driving down the wholesale price of electricity . <p> According to the REN21 report , total CO2 emissions in electricity generation fell by 11.7% in 2018 , as wind energy displaced fossil fuels .
