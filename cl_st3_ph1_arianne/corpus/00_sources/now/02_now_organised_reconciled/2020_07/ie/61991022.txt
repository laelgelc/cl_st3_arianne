<h> Future Human will be first major international tech event to go hybrid this October <p> Future Human reveals plans for a full live event experience for virtual attendees anywhere in the world . <p> Future Human , the successor event to the award-winning Inspirefest , will go ahead as planned on 29 and 30 October this year , but with a twist . It will be the first major international tech event in the world to go hybrid . <p> While most of its attendees will watch live from homes and offices around the world , the event itself will take place on a state-of-the-art , purpose-built broadcast stage in Dublin . Many speakers will appear on stage , with social distancing in place , while those overseas will be dialled in seamlessly to the stage . Discussions will be moderated by event MCs and chairs , including Future Human founder Ann O'Dea and BBC broadcaster Dearbhail McDonald . <p> " We knew with the insidious nature of this virus that hosting an event with thousands of attendees this October was unlikely to be a runner , but we did n't simply want to put it off to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ O'Dea . <p> " We were getting so many enquiries from our community as to whether we 'd go ahead .
