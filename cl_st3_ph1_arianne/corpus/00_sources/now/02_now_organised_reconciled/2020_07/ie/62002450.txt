<h> South Korea pledges $61 billion for ' net-zero society ' <p> As part of the South Korean New Deal , this " green " policy is the Asian country 's response to the rising tide of climate change , while also boosting its economy -- hit hard by the COVID-19 coronavirus -- in hopes of achieving what trade ministries have called a " net-zero society . " <p> " Now is the most appropriate time to speed up to become a carbon net-zero society , " said a South Korean government official , Arirang News Center reports . " The Green New Deal aims to overcome both the economic crisis caused by COVID-19 and the climate and environmental crises that caused the pandemic . " <p> The five-year , 61-billion-dollar ( U.S. ) initiative calls for the construction of zero-energy public facilities , many of which will be remodeled with eco-friendly materials .
