<h> Quality of peatlands ' cause of concern ' -- IPCC <p> The Irish Peatland Conservation Council ( IPCC ) is examining the quality of over 1,000 peatlands of conservation importance in Ireland . <p> According to Paula Farrell , IPCC 's campaign officer , the findings show that the quality of peatlands has " deteriorated substantially , with a lack of resilience to the impacts of climate change and disrupted weather patterns " playing a large contribution . <p> They have undertaken the review of Irish peatlands to inform a ' Peatlands and Climate Change Action Plan 2021-2030 ' , which is due to be released later this year . <h> Peatlands and climate go ' hand in hand ' <p> According to Farrell , peatlands and climate " go hand in hand " . <p> " Climate has influenced the formation of bogs for thousands of years , creating the perfect conditions for peat accumulation in the lake basins or poorly drained soils , " she explained . <p> " Likewise , peatlands can influence current and future climate .
