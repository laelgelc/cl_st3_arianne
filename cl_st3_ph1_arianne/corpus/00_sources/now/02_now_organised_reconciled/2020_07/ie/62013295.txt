<h> US plan for aircraft CO2 standards criticised by green groups <p> The US government has for the first time proposed regulating carbon-dioxide emissions from aviation by adopting ICAO 's aircraft CO2 standard . <p> But environmental campaigners say the regulations are " too weak " to make any meaningful difference to the airline industry 's impact on climate change , and are urging policymakers to " go back to the drawing board " and design more ambitious targets . <p> The US Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) on 22 July proposed adopting greenhouse-gas emissions legislation for commercial aircraft and large business jets that would " align US standards with the international CO2 emissions standards set by the ICAO " .
