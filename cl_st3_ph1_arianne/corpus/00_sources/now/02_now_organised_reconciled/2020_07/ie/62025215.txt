<h> Major funding boost for West Clare Greenway project <p> THERE has been a welcome boost for a long-cherished plan to create a greenway along the route of the old West Clare Railway , linking Ennis to the towns of Kilrush and Kilkee . <p> This week , Minister for Climate Change , Communications Networks and Transport , Eamon Ryan announced funding ? 255,000 for the project . The allocation will enable the start of an active consultation phase involving landowners along the route , with a view to creating another world-class visitor attraction in Clare . <p> The funding , which was allocated under the Carbon Tax Fund 2020 , represents a total allocation of ? 4.5m to 26 greenways and provides support for feasibility , planning and design . <p> The allocation to the West Clare Railway Greenway Project will progress the proposed amenity past concept and feasibility stage and allow Clare County Council to start actively engaging with landowners on route selection and preparation of the statutory consent phase of development -- namely planning permission , environmental impact assessment and preliminary design . <p> It is envisaged that the West Clare Railway Greenway will follow the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where feasible .
