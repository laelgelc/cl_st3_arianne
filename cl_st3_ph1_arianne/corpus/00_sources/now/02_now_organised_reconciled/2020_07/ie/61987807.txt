<p> The Port of Dover has unveiled plans for a ' virtual ' Freeport Zone that it says will help make Global Britain a reality while leading the fight against climate change . <p> The plans , which will form part of the port 's response to the government 's freeports consultation , see Dover working with a growing number of businesses , universities and public bodies to establish the UK 's first Global Trade and Innovation ( GTI ) Zone . The collaboration will see the creation of " secure and sustainable supply chain corridors designed to maximise the jobs and regeneration potential of the government 's emerging Freeports policy , " according to Dover authorities . <p> The port said the GTI Zone would strengthen the UK 's post-Covid economic recovery ; deliver economic growth , development and regeneration ; aim to create the UK 's first Zero Emission Logistics Corridors ; and enhance post-Brexit trade security and resilience . <p> The GTI Zone will connect the Port of Dover with key manufacturing hubs alongside regeneration sites identified by local authorities and Local Enterprise Partnerships . <p> Initial estimates by economic consultancy Oxera suggest that by enabling @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its potential , the GTI Zone could support almost 10,000 jobs .
