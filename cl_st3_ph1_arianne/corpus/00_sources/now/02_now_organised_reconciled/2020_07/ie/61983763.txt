<h> Net-zero asset owner club gains UNJSPF , nears targets framework <p> The UN Joint Staff Pension Fund ( UNJSPF ) has joined the UN-convened Net-Zero Asset Owner Alliance ( NZAOA ) as it looks to have its target-setting framework ready for external feedback by the end of this month . <p> In a statement , the UN pension fund 's investment management office said : " As a pension fund with long-term investment horizons and liabilities , UNJSPF is vulnerable to the financial impact resulting from both transition and physical risks caused by climate change . It is our fiduciary duty to protect the long-term financial interest of our beneficiaries . " <p> " As codified in the Paris Climate Agreement and United Nations Sustainable Development Goals , we have a key role to play in catalysing decarbonisation of the global economy and investing in climate resilience , " it added . <p> It also said that joining the alliance would give it " access to the best-in-class knowledge from peers on Paris-aligned climate finance change and will help enhance our sustainable investing approach to convert our assets to carbon neutrality by 2050 " . <p> Investors joining @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emissions associated with their investments to net-zero by 2050 , to be aligned with the Paris Agreement goal of limiting global warming to 1.5 ? There is a strong emphasis on engagement , with asset managers and policymakers as well as corporates , as the means of delivering on the group 's net-zero commitment . <p> As part of this , the group is very much seeking to advance the state-of-play with respect to net-zero portfolio target-setting . Members of the NZAOA commit to setting and publicly reporting on intermediate portfolio decarbonisation targets . <p> The first such targets have yet to be published , but according to Anqi Dang , sustainable investment analyst at Allianz , one of the NZAOA initiating organisations , the tentative timeline is for the group 's target-setting framework to be ready for external feedback by the end of this month . <p> The idea would be to be able to publish the finalised framework on the NZAOA website by the end of September , and for individual members of the group to have a bit more time to elaborate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or perhaps the first quarter of next year for some members . <p> Anqi was speaking during a webinar held last month by think tank 2 ?
