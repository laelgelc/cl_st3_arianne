Excessive fossil-fuel-age cleverness is threatening our extinction . It is imperative we behave towards Mother Nature and each other more kindly and gently than we do . My Facebook friend also said : " We need wisdom and courageous politicians who are prepared to face the truth in a Climate Emergency .
