" <p> The rain in the winter months softens the soil and that allows the roots to grow deep into the ground with relative ease . <p> The ongoing water shortage this year is a good reminder of how important it is to ensure the trees have the time to prepare for those dry conditions by getting their root systems deep in the soil . <p> " Established trees also reduce the need for regular watering .
