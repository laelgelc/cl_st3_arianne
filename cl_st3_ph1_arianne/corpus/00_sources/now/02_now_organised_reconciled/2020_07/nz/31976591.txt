<h> High hopes for future of our wool industry <p> Having been a wool buyer and exporter most of my working life , for the largest wool consumers and traders in the world , I have seen many projects come and go on how to make wool profitable . Nothing actually worked or was listened to . <p> We have had huge interference from the former NZ Wool Board , who in good faith held up prices , using taxpayers ' money , to no avail . At auctions such as at Invercargill , before sheep there were replaced by cows , they would mop up the whole clip to about 98 percent on offer . <p> Before the Soviet Union broke up , buyers there were our most important clients . This ended along with Communism , but the Wool Board continued to interfere regardless . Some wool was sent there and reshipped back to New Zealand !
