<p> EDS is broadly supportive of the report , released today , of the government 's independent resource management reform panel . The report offers wide-ranging recommendations for deep , fundamental reforms . <p> Key recommendations include : <p> Repealing the RMA <p> Replacing it with a Natural and Built Environments Act with a revised purpose and principles . <p> A major shift from managing environmental effects to achieving positive outcomes . <p> Creating a new Strategic Planning Act , requiring the preparation of regional spatial strategies encompassing both land and the coastal marine area . These strategies would align functions across other statutes , including the new Natural and Built Environments Act , the Local Government Act , the Land Transport Management Act and the Climate Change Response Act . <p> Enacting a dedicated Managed Retreat and Climate Change Adaptation Act , which would provide for managed retreat and for the establishment of a climate change adaptation fund . <p> Requiring decision-makers to give effect to the principles of Te Tiriti o Waitangi , and incorporating the overarching concept of Te Mana O Te Taiao in the purpose statement of the new Natural and Built Environments Act . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ performance of central and local government in giving effect to Te Tiriti and providing for an integrated partnership process between mana whenua and councils . <p> Requiring national direction to be made on a range of core matters and combining this into a coherent suite of instruments that clearly resolve conflicts and relationships between them . <p> Requiring the establishment of environmental bottom lines and targets . <p> Reformulating existing RMA plans into combined regional plans , reducing the 100 or so plans we have now to just 14 . <p> Expanding the role of the Parliamentary Commissioner for the Environment to provide a stronger auditing and oversight role of the resource management system . <p> Establishment of regional hubs for compliance , monitoring and enforcement . <p> Strengthening offences and penalties for non-compliance . <p> " It has been accepted for a long time now that the current system is n't working for the environment , business and communities , " said EDS CEO Gary Taylor . <p> " For several years , EDS and its business partners ( Infrastructure New Zealand , the Employers and Manufacturers Association , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have been pushing for its review .
