<h> Lockdown causes green electricity spike <p> The way people will pay for the transmission of their electricity from now on is being revealed later today . <p> With little fanfare , the country met a key climate change goal during lockdown : getting our renewable power generation over the 90 per cent mark -- for a week at least . In the midst of April 's lockdown , hydro , geothermal , wind and solar power plants generated 91 per cent of all electricity . <p> It 's a milestone the industry expects will become the norm in the next 15 years . <p> Across an average year , these renewable power sources supply 85 per cent of all electricity . But when conditions are right -- when there 's plenty of water , sun and wind or if demand is reduced -- this share can increase . <p> In late March and before the lockdown , fossil-fueled electricity generators provided 17 per cent of the electricity , Transpower data shows .
