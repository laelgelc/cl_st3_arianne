<h> Know your farm 's emissions profile <p> OPINION : As the world deals with the impact of the Covid-19 crisis , it 's important to remember climate change has n't gone away . <p> It remains a clear and present threat to New Zealand 's ability to produce sustainable and natural food . <p> Farmers in regions such as the Hawke 's Bay and Northland , who have been managing through crippling droughts and feed shortages , can only expect to have to deal with this more frequently as the effect of climate change increases . <p> The sector is already demonstrating its commitment to making positive changes . The Joint Action Plan on Primary Sector Emissions ( He Waka Eke Noa ) is an innovative partnership to reduce primary sector emissions . <p> It aims to equip farmers and growers with the knowledge and tools they need to reduce emissions , while continuing to sustainably produce quality food and fibre products for domestic and international markets . <p> Sheep and beef farmers have also already reduced their industry 's greenhouse gas emissions by 30% since the 1990s and worked to protect and restore native habitats on their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ second largest holding of native forest and native biodiversity in the country . <p> That is sequestering carbon and making a real difference . <p> But we can do more . As a country , we need to be positioning ourselves in the sustainable products market in a way that is robust and verifiable . That means connecting scientific tools to carbon certification programmes to give our customers confidence . <p> That starts on-farm .
