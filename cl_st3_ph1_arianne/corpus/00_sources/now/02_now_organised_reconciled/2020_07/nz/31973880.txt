<h> Talking trade and Maori <p> Yesterday was a chance for Maori in Tairawhiti to meet face to face with trade negotiators to share what is important to them and for Maori to gain an insight into why trade matters in the post-Covid-19 world . <p> More than 100 people came along to the one-day Te Taumata hui on trade at the Emerald Hotel . Some travelled in from East Coast communities like Tolaga Bay , Ruatoria and Whareponga . <p> Te Taumata chair Chris Karamea Insley started the day with a brief rundown of how trade creates jobs in New Zealand . <p> " One in four jobs is derived from trade , " he said . <p> " Maori work in forestry , fisheries . . . they create products and services that can be exported to other countries . Today is about learning the importance of trade agreements . " <p> Covid-19 had created an emerging opportunity space , said Nanaia Mahuta , Minister for Maori Development and Local Government . <p> " Domestically we have a lot of things to do , " she said .
