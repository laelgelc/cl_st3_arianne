<h> Community views central to planning in Albert-Eden <h> Community empowerment and sustainability top of agenda <p> Feedback from the community helped the Albert-Eden Local Board and Auckland Council staff in focusing on priorities that mattered to people in the local board area . <p> Priorities such as building resilient communities , a sustainable environment and places that meet a wide range of uses are now central to the draft Local Board Plan for 2020-2023 . <p> " We 've met lots of locals this year and I thank you for all your input so far . Now we want to hear from even more people , " say Albert-Eden Local Board Chair Margi Watson . <p> The local board has always supported community well-being and that 's now even more crucial post COVID-19 . <p> Our goals around valuing our diversity , protecting heritage and the environment , preparing for climate change , boosting our local economy and providing easy options for people to enjoy everything our area has to offer , will benefit many on a daily basis . <p> " We 've listened to the community from the start of our planning to ensure we choose @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with those who we hear from the least , including youth , children and minority groups . <p> We appreciate the support of local volunteer groups and key community stakeholders who have helped us shape our plan so far . Now it 's you turn to help us make it even more about your neighourhood and what you want your future to look like , " Margi says . <p> This is a great opportunity for Albert-Eden locals to have their voice heard through submitting their feedback to the local board 's plan , which sets the board 's priorities for the next three years . <h> Ways to have your say <p> Go here to read the plan and to make an online submission and different ways to give your feedback . <p> You can also find details on meetings to provide feedback directly to board members , including an online webinar on 23 July , for which you need to register .
