<h> Will Auckland 's drivers go electric ? <p> Analysis : Auckland 's new climate plan calls for two in five of the passenger cars in the city to be electric by 2030 . Is that possible ? Marc Daalder reports <p> As part of a bold plan to halve the entire city 's emissions by 2030 and reach net zero by 2050 , Auckland Council has committed to ensuring 40 percent of Aucklanders ' cars were electric by the end of the decade . <p> That 's a robust goal , particularly considering the Ministry of Transport ( MoT ) expects the nationwide fleet to be just 10 percent electric by the same date . Modelling by transport consultancy firm MRCagney and 1point5 , a non-profit policy shop dedicated to reducing emissions in line with halting warming at 1.5 degrees Celsius , estimates that without further intervention there will be 1.6 million cars on Auckland 's roads by 2030 . <p> That means some 640,000 vehicles will need to be electric . Of the cars that remain fossil fuel-powered , there will need to be an 18 percent increase in fuel efficiency . <p> The greatest challenge , however , will be replacing two in every five passenger cars in Auckland with an electric vehicle over the next decade .
