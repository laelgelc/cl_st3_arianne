Globalisation is a structural change , powered by revolutionary developments in communication technology since the early 1980s . This interconnectedness shows no sign of being reversed . <p> Indeed , Covid-19 has accelerated connectivity and the digital revolution in many states . <p> Related to this , the claim that two or three great powers will run the world in the 21st century is self-serving and wholly unrealistic . <p> Today , all states are confronted by security , economic , environmental and health challenges that do not respect territorial borders and can not be resolved unilaterally by great powers . <p> Covid-19 has only highlighted this .
