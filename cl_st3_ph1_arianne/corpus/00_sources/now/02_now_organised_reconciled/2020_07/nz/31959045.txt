<h> New model proposed to reduce carbon footprint - Otago University <p> Prior to the global pandemic , climate researchers identified an uncomfortable truth : the very meetings and events meant to support the fight against climate change were themselves causing vast greenhouse gas emissions through international air travel . For the American Geophysical Union 's conference held in San Francisco last December , it is estimated the 28,000 delegates travelled 285 million kilometres - almost twice the distance between Earth and the Sun . <p> However , Professor Higham says " there is evidence that shows that in actual fact very little is gained by traveling regularly to conferences in terms of career trajectory " . <p> The study found that intercontinental flights account for a high proportion of total emissions - far more than regional flights - meaning that promoting alternative regional modes of transport such as trains is limited in terms of reducing the overall carbon footprint of a conference .
