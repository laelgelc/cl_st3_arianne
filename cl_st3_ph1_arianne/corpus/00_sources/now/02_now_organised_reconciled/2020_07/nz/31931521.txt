<h> Number of natives under one billion trees anyone 's guess <p> How many of the one billion trees planted in the next decade will be native species ? Government tree planting agency Te Uru Rakau has clarified that it ca n't hazard an estimate . <p> The Government 's tree planting agency , Te Uru Rakau , says it ca n't estimate what proportion of the one billion trees programme will be native species , saying a previous figure it gave to Newsroom was meant to be purely " illustrative " . <p> The illustrative figure was used to calculate the estimated climate benefitfrom the tree scheme , which Te Uru Rakau has put at 384 million tonnes of carbon dioxide over the trees ' lifetimes . <p> The Government plans to foster planting of half a billion new trees over a decade , using a mixture of grants , education and co-funding forestry projects with other entities . <p> The one billion figure that gives the programme its catchy name was reached by counting another half a billion plantation pine trees in existing pine forests . As for the overall proportions of new trees , the agency says that 's largely up to landowners who will choose what trees they want . It expects people to continue voluntarily planting natives with no subsidies , but the agency says it 's impossible to forecast how many indigenous trees we will end up with . <p> Te Uru Rakau previously told Newsroom it had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( by assuming the total mix of species ) would be 70 percent exotic and 30 percent indigenous , equating to 384 million tonnes of CO2 sequestered over the forests ' lifetimes . <p> The mix of native and exotic species is important for working out the carbon sequestration , because pine absorbs more carbon , by forestry scientists ' measurements . <p> That 's despite pine usually being harvested and replanted about every 27 years , which allows native forest to slowly catch up with the pine over more than a century .
