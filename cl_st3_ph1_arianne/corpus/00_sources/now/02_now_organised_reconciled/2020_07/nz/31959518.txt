<p> We Are Here -- An Atlas of Aotearoa is the extraordinary product of years of work that offers an in-depth view of Aotearoa as a place and as a people . It asks you to step back from Google or Apple Maps and take a look at the world around you : your people , your places , your whenua . <p> Race Relations Commissioner Meng Foon talks with creator Chris McDowall and contributors Nadine Anne Hura and Veronika Meduna about this collision of data , design and heartfelt analysis .
