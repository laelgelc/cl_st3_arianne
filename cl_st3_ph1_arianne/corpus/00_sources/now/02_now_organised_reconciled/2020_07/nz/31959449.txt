Tracey McGill From Ara And Ngari Scartozzi Listen To Hon. James Shaw <p> The Hon. James Shaw , the New Zealand Government 's Minister for Climate Change and co-leader of the Green Party of Aotearoa initiated a spirited discussion of sustainability and entrepreneurship at Te Ohaka Centre for Growth and Innovation . <p> Te Ohaka is Otautahi Christchurch 's hub of innovation and start-ups and is a partnership founded by Ara Institute of Canterbury , ChristchurchNZ and Ministry of Awesome ( MOA ) , an organization that nurtures new business ventures and ideas through a combination of support , guidance and network linkages . <p> Mr. Shaw was participating in an afternoon korero with Ara Institute of Canterbury 's Masters of Sustainability students and staff from the Sustainability &amp; Outdoor Education programmes . Also present were some of the founders of Te Ohaka 's sustainability-focused start-ups , including Andy Nurse and Graeme Pile from Fertigation Systems , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Brothers Green , Caroline Thalund from Sustainability 360 and Fernando Gutierrez from Ananda Simply Wholefoods . <p> Jacob Varghese And Hon. She stated that Christchurch should not seek to be another Silicon Valley ; rather the opportunity exists for the city to set a new standard that the global innovation community could aspire towards . <p> Andy Nurse from Fertigation Systems and Ngarie Scartozzi from EClean followed Ms. Colbin 's comments with brief background speeches outlining the scope of their ventures and touching on the challenges that are ahead for them .
