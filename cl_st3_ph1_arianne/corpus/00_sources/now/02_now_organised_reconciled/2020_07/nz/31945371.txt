<h> A message to NZ party leaders on election 2020 , minorities and vilification <p> The race relations commissioner , Meng Foon , has just sent the following letter to leaders of New Zealand political parties . <p> Tena koe , Talofa , Fakaalofa lahi atu , Kia orana , ? ? , Namaste . <p> My role as Race Relations Commissioner is to promote and enhance harmonious communities in our diverse nation . As the leader of a political party who manages development of policy and campaigning in the 2020 general election , you will advocate a vision for Aotearoa . <p> For this reason , I am writing to share my advice for your party during the campaign period and beyond . <p> In addition to the Covid-19 pandemic we are still in , there are so many pressing issues facing Aotearoa New Zealand including lack of housing , rising unemployment , the need for training , climate change , family violence and much more . <p> The debate around racism globally and locally is important . But , I wish to remind you and your party to focus on issues rather than singling out minority communities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ play the ball and not the person ; and follow the rules . <p> I encourage you to debate and promote policies that will build a better Aotearoa . I call on you to present your position in a way that brings attention to your policies -- without scapegoating or inciting ill will towards minority groups . <p> The Human Rights Commission encourages and promotes the freedom of speech of all political parties in this and every election . With this right though comes the great responsibility to not vilify or dehumanise people . <p> As a leader of a political party you have an important role . I encourage your leadership team to promote an equal society , where all are acknowledged for their contributions . <p> My door is always open to all , but my role is non-partisan and I will maintain neutrality . I will ensure an even-handed approach to publicly calling matters to account should this be necessary . <p> I wish you all the best in your campaign , as serving our country is an honour . <p> The Spinoff is subject to NZ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in writing , within one month of publication , to info@thespinoff.co.nz .
