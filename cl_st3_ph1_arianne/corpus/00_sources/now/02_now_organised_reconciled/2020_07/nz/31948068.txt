Transpower 's report predicted that energy demand will jump by two-thirds over the next three decades . More than half of that growth is from the electrification of vehicles , while just under a quarter each will come from industrial heat needs and population growth . <p> Under that scenario , building new renewable energy generation is n't a choice - it 's a must if New Zealand is to avoid running out of power . <p> The scale of the investment needed is massive .
