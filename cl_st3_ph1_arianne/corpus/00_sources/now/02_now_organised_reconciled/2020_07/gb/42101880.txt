<h> Supporting walking and cycling will be a crucial part of our post-Covid recovery <p> Active transport like cycling and walking are vital to the nation 's coronavirus recovery -- but they need to be integrated across government policy making . <p> The public are being told not to use public transport unless their journey is essential and they seem to be doing just that : I 'm on the 08.30 train to London , in a carriage with 60 seats but only three passengers . <p> Social distancing measures mean buses , trams and trains operating at a fraction of normal capacity . Services are increasing to near pre-lockdown frequencies , but at maximum occupancy levels of 20% , public transport clearly ca n't accommodate everyone who needs to travel and the financial implications are dire . <p> Meanwhile the once eerily-quiet roads are filling up . Traffic has already returned to 70% of pre-lockdown levels . <p> One of the few positives of the crisis has been an explosion in the number of people taking to their bikes for both exercise and essential journeys .
