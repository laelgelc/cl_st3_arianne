We looked back on his first year in the role . <p> WHEN Boris Johnson became prime minister on July 24 , 2019 , his party was languishing in the polls and had not long suffered one of its worst ever performances at the ballot box , securing just nine per cent of the vote in elections to the European Parliament . <p> But almost immediately the Tories ' poll share began to rise , from an average of 29 per cent in the week he became PM to the mid-30s by the autumn , before hitting the early 40s at the start of the general election campaign . <p> After winning 45 per cent of votes in Great Britain on election day , Boris Johnson then led his party to even dizzier heights in the polls , until the Tories found themselves averaging as much as 52 per cent in the early weeks of the coronavirus crisis . <p> Then it all changed .
