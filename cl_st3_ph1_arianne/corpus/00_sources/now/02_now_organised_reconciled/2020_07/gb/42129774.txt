<h> The ' deeply unrepresentative ' lobbying of Scottish ministers <p> MOST lobbying of Scottish ministers has been carried out by big businesses and farmers , according to an analysis of Holyrood 's lobbying register by The Ferret . <p> Ten of the top 14 lobbyists over the last two years represented private vested interests . They included major companies , salmon farming multinationals , supermarkets , the whisky industry and landowners . <p> The most frequent ministerial lobbyist by far was the Confederation of British Industry ( CBI ) in Scotland , which says it speaks for 190,000 UK businesses . The second biggest was the National Farmers Union in Scotland ( NFUS ) , which represents over 8500 farmers . <p> Only four of the 14 top lobbyists could be said to represent the public interest . They were the Scottish Council for Voluntary Organisations , the Scottish Trades Union Congress ( STUC ) and charities for the blind and cancer research . <p> The analysis also revealed that the four most lobbied ministers since March 2018 were the former finance secretary , Derek Mackay ; the energy minister , Paul Wheelhouse ; the First Minister , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ewing . <p> Experts and campaigners said that lobbying at Holyrood had been exposed as " deeply unrepresentative " and " imbalanced " . They highlighted " loopholes " in the lobbying rules , and called for public interest groups to have more access to ministers . <p> Companies , farmers , retailers and landowners stressed the importance of engaging with ministers on behalf of those they represent . They said they complied with requirements to register their lobbying . <p> The Scottish Parliament has launched a review of the " effectiveness " of the lobbying register . MSPs on the post-legislative scrutiny committee have asked for comments by August 14 . <p> Scotland 's lobbying law was agreed by Holyrood in 2016 and came into force on March 12 , 2018 . It requires organisations which hold face-to-face meetings -- including online -- with MSPs , ministers and their special advisers to record whom they lobbied and why . <p> The law does n't cover lobbying by phone or email , by organisations with less than ten full time staff or by people who are unpaid .
