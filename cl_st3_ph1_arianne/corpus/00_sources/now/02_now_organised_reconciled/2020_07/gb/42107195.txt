They fell by 0.4 per cent in Cardiff and by 5 per cent across Wales . Industry is responsible for most of the emissions in the Vale , while road transport emits the most in Cardiff . <p> The Vale also emits more per person than in Cardiff and Wales . For every person in the Vale , 8.5 tonnes of carbon are emitted each year . For every person in Wales , 8.2 tonnes are emitted each year , and just 4.5 tonnes in Cardiff . <p> The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change reported in 2018 that net emissions must be reduced to zero in order to limit increases in global temperatures to 1.5 degrees . <p> The Welsh Government , Cardiff council and the Vale of Glamorgan council all declared a climate emergency last year , committing to net zero emissions in the public sector by 2030 .
