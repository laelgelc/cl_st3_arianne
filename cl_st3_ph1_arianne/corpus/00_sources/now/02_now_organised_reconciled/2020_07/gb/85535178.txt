<h> Garden city : the rapid growth of home farming in Singapore during lockdown <p> Singapore has long been known as a garden city , with lush tropical greenery lining her streets . So it 's only natural that during almost two months of lockdown -- known locally as the ' circuit breaker ' -- there was a growing desire to bring a piece of the outdoors , indoors . A surge in Singaporeans picked up their rusty gardening tools and started growing vegetables and fruits at home -- and more often than not , in small , high-rise apartments . <p> " People now want to grow edibles instead of just ornamental plants , " shares Joanna Chuah , the founder of WWEdibles , a small business selling her home-grown produce . " During ' circuit breaker ' , you could n't go out and buy ornamental plants like monsteras and alocasias because you need to get them from specific nurseries . But you can easily grow edible produce from seeds . " <p> Brian Thian is another local gardener who has been helping educate Singaporeans about tropical flora through his Instagram page , @briansgardenadventures .
