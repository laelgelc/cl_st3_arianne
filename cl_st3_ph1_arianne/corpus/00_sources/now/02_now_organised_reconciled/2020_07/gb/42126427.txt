<h> Dorset Council pledges to be carbon-neutral by 2040 in climate strategy <h> Dorset Council releases first draft of emergency climate strategy <p> DORSET Council has published the first draft if its climate and ecological emergency strategy online and set a target date for carbon neutrality of 2040 . <p> Dorset Council has published the strategy ahead of the document being discussed of the Place Scrutiny meeting on July 23 . The council declared a climate and ecological emergency back in May 2019 and , since then , teams of councillors and officers have been working to devise this strategy which will provide the direction , scope and ambition of the council 's action plan to address climate change across Dorset . <p> The strategy aims to deliver a realistic and achievable approach to ensuring Dorset Council is carbon-neutral by 2040 which is a decade ahead of the UK government 's 2050 target for the whole country . <p> Cllr Ray Bryan said : " I 'm confident that this strategy provides the foundation for an action plan that is realistic , ambitious and deliverable by 2040 and I 'd like to thank everyone who has been involved in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ recognise that the world has changed dramatically since we declared a climate emergency over a year ago . The COVID-19 pandemic has left Dorset Council with huge financial challenges that need to be urgently addressed . Balancing what we need to do to address climate change with our legal requirement to provide statutory services is key . <p> " The responsibilities of our climate and ecological emergency declaration will remain at the heart of Dorset Council 's plans and will continue to directly influence and inform future strategies , initiatives and policies . <p> " When our financial situation improves , when opportunities present themselves , or when new technology becomes available , I promise we will regularly return to this work to look for ways we can achieve our goals earlier . But in the meantime , I would ask everyone to read the strategy and let us know your views when we launch the consultation .
