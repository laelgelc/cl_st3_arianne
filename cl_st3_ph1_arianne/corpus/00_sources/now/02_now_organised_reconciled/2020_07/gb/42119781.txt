<p> With 50 percent of the cost supported by the Follow-up Committee of the Initiatives of the UAE President , the AED132 million plant will process the solid municipal waste generated in Ajman and Umm Al Qaiwain to produce energy to power cement plants that usually rely on charcoal for their operations . <p> Dr. Al Nuaimi received a briefing on the plant that will start operations in Q4 2020 . Spanning 40 hectares , the state-of-the-art facility will have two parallel operation lines -- one to receive non-recyclable waste from waste segregation units , turn it into alternative fuel , and transport this fuel to cement factories , while the second receives recyclable waste from segregation units and conveys it to recycling companies . It is expected to process 1,500 tonnes of municipal solid waste every day to produce 300,000 tonnes of alternative fuel annually . <p> The minister learnt about the centre 's activities , aimed at evaluating the state of the country 's marine environment . These include surveying water quality , tracking the concentrations of marine nutrients and plastic particles in seawater , identifying climate change-resistant coral species , monitoring the stocks of kingfish , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and inspecting samples in local fish markets . <p> He was also updated on the centre 's projects , such as establishing and observing artificial habitats and natural rock barriers in collaboration with the private sector , and setting up and running a fish hatchery . Besides , the centre has planted 3,000 mangrove saplings across the country 's coastal areas and 200 coral colonies along the coastline in Kalba , and continues to monitor them . <p> In conclusion , Dr. Al Nuaimi praised the exceptional efforts of the MOCCAE teams and stressed the importance of regularly updating work processes in line with the latest developments in environmental sustainability and biodiversity conservation .
