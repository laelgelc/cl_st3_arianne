This will also contribute to local economic regeneration and the ongoing work to tackle climate change . <p> In February 2020 , Calderdale was severely affected by its third major flood in just eight years . The Council has been lobbying Government to recognise the huge scale of risk that the borough continues to face due to its unique landscape .
