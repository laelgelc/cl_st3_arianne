The word was originally borrowed into English from French ; and for that reason it was sometimes pluralised as portmanteaux rather than portmanteaus . But it first arrived in English in about 1500 and , during the more than 500 years since then , it has been thoroughly anglicised in speech and is pronounced with the stress on the second syllable .
