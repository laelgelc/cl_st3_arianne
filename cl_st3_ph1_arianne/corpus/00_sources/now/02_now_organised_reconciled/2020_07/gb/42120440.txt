<h> LuxSE appoints Laetitia Hamon As Head Of Sustainable Finance <p> Date 15/07/2020 <p> The Luxembourg Stock Exchange ( LuxSE ) today announced the appointment of Laetitia Hamon as the company 's Head of Sustainable Finance . As from 15 July , the sustainability expert will take the reins of LuxSE 's sustainable finance team and the company 's renowned platform for sustainable securities , the Luxembourg Green Exchange ( LGX ) . <p> " We are very pleased to welcome Laetitia to our team . For the past 10 years , she has been developing a broad skill set and in-depth , practical expertise of sustainable finance matters . Thanks to her focus on climate change and sustainability in previous roles , Laetitia can play a key role in the future development of LGX . Her sound knowledge of the Luxembourg fund industry will also be an excellent addition to our fixed-income market expertise , and help us cater to the needs of a broad range of market participants , " commented Julie Becker , Deputy CEO of LuxSE and Founder of LGX . <p> Sustainability first <p> Laetitia Hamon brings a decade of experience in sustainability-related advisory @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ expert in sustainable finance with in-depth knowledge of Sustainable Investments , Climate Finance and Corporate Social Responsibility . For a number of years , she has actively participated in local and international working groups in this field , including the Association of the Luxembourg Fund Industry ( ALFI ) and the Climate Bonds Initiative ( CBI ) . Previous positions include ESG and Fund Analyst , as well as Business Development Officer for LuxFLAG . <p> " It is a privilege for me to join the Luxembourg Stock Exchange and contribute to its efforts to make capital markets more sustainable . Sustainable finance is at a turning point , and LGX is a frontrunner in the field , raising awareness and driving growth in Luxembourg and beyond . We now need to capitalise on this success to encourage all finance market participants to transition to a low-carbon and socially fair economy . This will be made possible by promoting transparency , contributing to education and giving access to relevant and reliable data , " said Laetitia Hamon , newly appointed Head of Sustainable Finance at LuxSE . <p> Mobilising private @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sustainable finance agenda and help reorient capital flows into sustainable investment projects . LGX is the world 's leading platform for sustainable securities and now counts more than 760 securities by 130 different issuers , totalling EUR 285 billion . In May this year , LuxSE established the LGX Academy to strengthen sustainable finance education and increase financial professionals and students ' knowledge of sustainable instruments , standards and labels as well as sustainable finance market practices . <p> LuxSE is also developing a centralised datahub for sustainability data points that will allow investors to better understand the environmental impact of different financial products and thereby make more informed investment decisions .
