<h> Climate change petition handed in to Dudley Council ahead of key vote <p> A PETITION signed by more than 400 people calling on Dudley Council to declare a climate emergency has been handed in ahead of a key vote later this week . <p> The petition , launched last October , calls on the local authority to take action on climate change by pledging to achieve a net-zero carbon footprint by 2030 and to put the fight against climate change at the heart of policy and budgetary decisions . <p> In February , councillors debated whether to pass a motion put forward by Councillor Chris Barnett which called for Dudley Council to declare a climate emergency and would have seen the local authority pledge to achieve a net-zero carbon footprint by 2030 . <p> The debate ran out of time before it could be put to the vote due to protests in the public gallery . <p> The motion is set to return to the council chamber this Thursday ( July 23 ) , where councillors will vote on proposals to declare a climate emergency in the borough and to set up a cross-party Climate Change Working Group @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for Dudley . <p> Unlike the petition , the proposal does not set a deadline to reduce carbon emissions , instead tasking the Working Group with setting a " realistic " carbon reduction target . <p> Andrew Tromans , who created the petition and is standing for the Labour Party for Belle Vale in next year 's local elections , handed the petition in to Dudley Council today ( July 21 ) . <p> Andrew said : " I am hugely grateful to hundreds of people who supported the petition . Their efforts have been part of the groundswell of public feeling which has led to Dudley Council having to countenance to the impending climate emergency . <p> " While I and many of those involved in environmental campaigning across the borough welcome the cross-party support for declaring a climate emergency in Dudley , we are awaiting further details .
