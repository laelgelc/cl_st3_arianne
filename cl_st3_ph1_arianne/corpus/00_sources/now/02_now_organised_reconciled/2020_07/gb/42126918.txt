More than 13,000 people have been evacuated . Eighty-six homes , most of them holiday cabins , have been destroyed . Some of the fires are predicted to burn at least until autumn , or even up to the first snows . The Anglican Church of Canada is providing financial aid for people displaced or affected by the fires . In consultation with the Bishop of Saskatchewan , the Rt Revd Michael Hawkins , the Primate 's World Relief and Development Fund ( PWRDF ) have granted $15,000 in relief funding to the Prince Albert Grand Council . This tribal council , representing 12 First Nations , is supplementing the Red Cross by providing meals , clothing , blankets and shelter . It is also transporting evacuees , giving medical care and helping firefighters battle the conflagrations , of which 57 cover more than 100 hectares .
