<h> Expansion plan promises new lease of life for ' hidden oasis ' <p> But Blawhorn Moss National Nature Reserve in West Lothian is one of our environmental crown jewels -- a hidden oasis where species such as the dragonfly-eating sundew , heath spotted orchid and curlew can be found . <p> It is also a rare survivor of the lowland raised bogs once common across central Scotland . <p> Now the reserve 's profile is set for a huge boost after plans were unveiled that will see the site expanded , a new boardwalk and circular paths put in , and work undertaken aimed at restoring the peatlands as part of efforts to preserve their climate changefighting properties . <p> It is also hoped the work announced by Scottish Natural Heritage ( SNH ) will put Blawhorn Moss on the eco-tourism map alongside locations such as the Flow Country , helping it become a visitor magnet and adding to the already marked increase in walkers since the coronavirus lockdown was introduced . <p> " Many of the National Nature Reserves we look after are the big iconic ones up in the Highlands and we have relatively few in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's reserve manager . <p> " But Blawhorn is only a half-hour drive from Edinburgh .
