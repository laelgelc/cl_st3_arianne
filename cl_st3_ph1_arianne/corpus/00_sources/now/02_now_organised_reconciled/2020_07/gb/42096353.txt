Not only has the United Kingdom 's departure left a giant hole in the EU 's future revenues , it has dispossessed the bloc of its most powerful champion of free trade , unfettered competition and -- crucially -- limited EU spending . <p> The second earthquake came when Germany , the largest net payer into EU coffers , abruptly crossed the floor last month .
