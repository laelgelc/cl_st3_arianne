<h> BOI approves perks for massive P4.15-B solar project in Pangasinan <p> The Board of Investments ( BOI ) approved the application of Pilipinas Newton Energy Corp . to develop a P4.15-billion solar energy project in Pangasinan . <p> In a statement on Wednesday , BOI said the company qualified for fiscal and nonfiscal incentives under the Investment Priorities Plan , including a four- to six-year income tax holiday . <p> BOI said the project would be constructed on a 70-hectare land in San Manuel , Pangasinan . The project , set for commercial operations in June next year , would use fixed photovoltaic modules for the generation of electricity with a peak capacity of up to 68.7 megawatts ( MW ) . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The power generated will then be sold to distribution utilities or National Grid Corp . of the Phils. via bilateral agreements or through the Wholesale Electricity Spot Market . <p> " The demand for power will continue to increase due to the ongoing industrialization in the country as it fuels the infrastructure expansion and the growth of the manufacturing industry .
