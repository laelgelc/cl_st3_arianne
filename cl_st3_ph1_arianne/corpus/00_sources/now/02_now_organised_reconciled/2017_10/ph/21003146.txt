<h> Pope Francis to call space station <h> Reuters <p> VATICAN CITY - Pope Francis will get closer to the heavens this month when he puts in a call to the International Space Station . <p> The Vatican said on Monday the pope would make the call at 5 p.m. ( 1500 GMT ) on October 26 . It gave no further details , but Francis has supported the work of the Pontifical Academy of Sciences , which regularly brings together scientists from around the world to exchange views on topics such as climate change . <p> The current six-member crew of the International Space Station is made up of three Americans , two Russians and one Italian . <p> It orbits the Earth at a height of about 220 miles , according to NASA 's website.
