<p> GREENPEACE has accused technology firms as the main cause of negative environmental impacts in the world , with some of the biggest such as Samsung Electronics , Amazon and Huawei gaining some of the lowest marks in a recently released report . <p> The Guide to Greener Electronics , released by Greenpeace US , assessed 17 of the top global tech companies in various areas including use of recyclable materials in their products , to the sustainability of the infrastructures that support these businesses . <p> Out of 17 companies , Amazon , Vivo , Oppo and Samsung has some of the poorest grades .
