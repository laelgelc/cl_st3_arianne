<h> EDC 's video production contest winners promote renewable energy <h> EDC 's video production contest winners promote renewable energy <p> Wednesday , October 25 , 2017 <p> THE young winners of the video production contest of Energy Development Corporation ( EDC ) dubbed " Sa Geothermal May Forever " and held recently focused on the importance of renewable energy in fighting climate change . <p> Negros Island Geothermal Business Unit head James Soriano said the competition has mutual benefit between EDC 's effort to promote sustainable energy or the use of geothermal energy 24/7 while helping the millennials who are interested in film production . <p> Of the five finalists , Travel Goals bagged the Best Film with the score of 80.91 percent . It received the P20,000 grand prize . <p> First runner-up was Power , with 74.18 percent score and a cash prize of P10,000 .
