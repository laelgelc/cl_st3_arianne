<h> Report : Agriculture can power transformation of African economies <p> According to a new report from Ghana-based African Centre for Economic Transformation ( ACET ) , backed by Kofi Annan and African finance ministers , agriculture can power the economic transformation of African economies . <h> Africa has resources to drive a boom in agriculture under the right economic conditions <p> Modernising African agriculture will require policies to boost farm productivity and strengthen the sector 's links with the rest of the economy . To achieve this , African countries must overhaul their land tenure systems and support farmers by making new technologies accessible , reducing business risk and educating them as businesspeople . These reforms could trigger an economic transformation with benefits far beyond the sector , stimulating growth in manufacturing and related services , whilst driving down food prices and raising disposable incomes . <p> " With a uniquely diverse climate and more than half the world 's uncultivated arable land , Africa has the natural resources to drive a boom in agriculture that can lift millions out of poverty . African countries currently spend $68bn a year on food imports , a figure likely to keep rising . Meanwhile , global food demand is forecast to double by 2050 , by which time the continent will be home to one billion young people . <p> " This is not just an economic argument . With more secure access to land , they can operate modern farms that produce more , and raise their incomes .
