<h> Opportunity for Investment in Ghana 's Hospitality Industry <p> One of the most frequently visited tourist destinations in English-speaking West Africa is Ghana , for many obvious reasons . The country is not in any way lacking in tourist attractions , especially for those interested in nature and West African slave trade era history . The Kakum National Park , Labadi Beach , Elmina Castle , the mighty Volta Lake , and the Akosombo Dam are just a few of the sites attracting foreign traffic to the country . <p> No wonder the country featured prominently on PWC 's Hospitality Outlook 2017-2027 report as a major emerging market in Africa . Big investors are seeing the light and responding to the stimulus . That is why hotels , both locally and internationally-branded , are springing up in the country . As of May 2017 , there were 2,723 hotels and lodges in Ghana . <p> The country 's government is not relaxing on the natural self-advertising qualities of its tourist sites . If properly adhered to , the growth contributed to the country 's economy by the hospitality sector will be consolidated consistently for a long time .
