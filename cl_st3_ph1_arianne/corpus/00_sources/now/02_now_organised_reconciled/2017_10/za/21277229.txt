<h> Msimanga elected to serve on Iclei Africa Committee <p> THE election of Tshwane mayor Solly Msimanga to serve on the regional executive committee for the Iclei Africa Committee would strengthen the City 's commitments to sustainability . <p> This was according to the city manager Dr Moeketsi Mosola , who applauded Msimanga for being internationally recognised by Iclei , a global network of cities that deals with sustainability and climate change intervention . <p> Msimanga 's term of office on the Iclei Africa Committee would run from 2018 to 2021 . <p> The global institution consists of more than 1 000 cities , towns and metropoles in 86 countries . <p> In Africa , Iclei has a presence in 22 countries , 97 full member cities , five associate partners and six Local Government Associations . <p> Mosola said the mayor was expected to be instrumental in the implementation of the Iclei Africa Strategic Plan . <p> " He will promote Iclei membership , activities , programmes and projects within the region and represent Iclei at key events locally , regionally and internationally . This is a major achievement for the City of Tshwane considering that the mayor is only just over a year in his term of office . This will further strengthen the City 's commitments to sustainability as he has proven to be an advocate for the environmental aspects of green projects to foster viable greening of the City , " he said . <p> Msimanga has also been elected to represent the committee on the Global Executive Committee , thus ensuring that sufficient attention is paid to sustainability issues affecting the African region and its local sphere of governance . <p> Kobie Brand , regional director of Iclei 's African Secretariat , said Msimanga and other executive committee members would ensure that Africa 's cities lead the exciting urban transformation . <p> " Worldwide Iclei @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more inclusive , resilient , greener and healthier cities for our fast-growing urban communities .
