<h> Pressure to save water mounts under New Normal plan <p> Due to the impact of climate change and reduced annual average rainfall as we have again seen this past winter , the City of Cape Town has adopted a scenario called the New Normal , where we are no longer relying only on rainwater to fill our dams for our water supplies . <p> The New Normal means that as a permanent drought region we have to change our relationship with water as a scarce resource and augment our supply with alternative non-surface sources . <p> Today , as part of our regular updates on the drought crisis , I am announcing key aspects of the City of Cape Town 's Critical Water Shortages Disaster Plan . <p> It essentially deals with the measures we are putting in place to avoid a time when water users do not have access to municipal drinking water .
