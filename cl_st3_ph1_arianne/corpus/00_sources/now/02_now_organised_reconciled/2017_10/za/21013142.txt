Thaler 's early embrace of research by psychologists Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky on how people actually think about probability and risk - and how that reality diverges from the models wielded by economists - was a risky , low-probability bet that economists would take such work seriously . In his 2015 book , Misbehaving : The Making of Behavioral Economics , Thaler told the story of how he came to make that bet and how it played out . <p> During a visit to Bloomberg headquarters in June 2015 , I asked him a few questions about how economists think .
