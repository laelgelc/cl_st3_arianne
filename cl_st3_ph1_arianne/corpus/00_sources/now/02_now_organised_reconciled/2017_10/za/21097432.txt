It was Monday , just before 4pm , and a colleague and I were driving to an appointment in Roodepoort when we were caught up in what has been described as one of the biggest storms to hit Gauteng in recent years . <p> It lasted a lifetime , or so it seemed , even though the clock indicated the first rush of hail , which transformed the landscape almost immediately , was over within 15 minutes .
