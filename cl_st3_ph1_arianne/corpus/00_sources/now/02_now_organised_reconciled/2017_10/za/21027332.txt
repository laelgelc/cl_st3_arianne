<h> Apply and answer call of UN 's Young Leaders programme <p> Young people today are not the leaders of tomorrow but the leaders of today . <p> These were the words of Jayathma Wickramanayake , UN secretary-general 's Envoy on Youth , as the organisation calls for young thought leaders to implement change . <p> The Young Leaders programme places a spotlight on 17 young people between the ages of 18-30 who are leading positive change towards a sustainable future . <p> They will work with the UN Envoy on Youth to engage young people on the Sustainable Development Goals , advocate for their achievement and support the UN 's advocacy efforts to mobilise young people . <p> The Sustainable Development Goals are a set of 17 internationally agreed goals to end poverty , fight inequalities and tackle climate change by 2030 . <p> The young leaders will work together to realise these goals by engaging their peers through strategic opportunities with the UN and through its existing initiatives , platforms and networks . <p> Wickramanayake said : " Never before has a generation been so well equipped - @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to put the planet and our societies on a sustainable path . Young people are the secret weapon to achieving the goals . " <p> Successful applicants will be expected to advocate actively for the Goals , in ways most accessible to young people across different contexts and to promote innovative ways of engaging their peers . <p> The role is not full-time but an advocacy function which should complement existing work . The function is honorary , non-remunerated and does not entail speaking on behalf of the UNs .
