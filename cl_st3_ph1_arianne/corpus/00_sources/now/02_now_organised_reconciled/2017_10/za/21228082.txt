<h> Gauteng highly likely to experience water crisis in near future <p> Another South African province could find itself suffering a water crisis according to a report commissioned by the Gauteng Department of Economic Development , Agriculture and Rural Development . <p> The report cautioned that drought , coupled with an ageing infrastructure could leave Gauteng with water shortages for a prolonged period of time . <p> " The possibility exists that prolonged drought could coincide with a major infrastructure failure resulting in potentially severe water shortages in the province , " the report states . <p> Estimates included in the report suggest that up to 37% of Gauteng 's water supply is lost through the deterioration of infrastructure . The water tariff also fails also fails to cover maintenance costs . <p> Climate change is expected to contribute strongly to the province 's challenges as drought is predicted to likely be more frequent and more intense . <h> The downside of urbanisation <p> Urbanisation and population growth have also had an adverse effect on Gauteng 's natural resources . Over half of the province has been altered by urbanisation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shows that although Gauteng has performed well economically , this has been achieved at the cost of the province 's natural resources , " the department says . <p> " Unacceptably high levels of pollution still characterise the region . Urgent action must be taken to correct activities and rehabilitate riparian systems . " <h> Deteriorating water quality <p> Also noted , in some areas of the province , is a water supply which is increasingly becoming unfit for human consumption . <p> " In the last several years there have been a growing number of examples where the water supplied is not fit for its intended purpose . For example , water sourced from Hartbeespoort Dam , which is supplied to the residents in the Hartbeespoort area , is now considered unsafe for human consumption " . <p> There are many similar examples , especially in informal settlements , where the " water quality is questionable or unsafe " . <p> The department says it is implementing strict water conservation and water demand management and working towards protecting natural ecosystems as a means to adapt to climate change .
