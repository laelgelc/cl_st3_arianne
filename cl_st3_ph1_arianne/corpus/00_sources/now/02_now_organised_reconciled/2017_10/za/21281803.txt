<h> UCT doing its bit to help Cape Town save water <p> The University of Cape Town ( UCT ) , with just close to 30 000 students enrolled on its campuses , has said it will do its bit to help Cape Town save water . <p> Cape Town has a daily water usage target of 500 million litres and the municipality has said it 's going to take the efforts of all residents and visitors to bring usage , currently at 585 million litres , down below the target . <p> " The water crisis is very serious not only because of the threat of running out of water , but because planned interventions are likely to be very costly , perhaps even unaffordable , " UCT said . <p> " Residents in the Western Cape need to be much better prepared to deal with the long-term consequences of being a warmer , drier region .
