<p> A sensitisation workshop was organised at the Peduase Valley Resort for customs officers to help them to be conversant with the use of a refrigerant identifier to improve the ODS qualitative monitoring and control capabilities . <p> Attended by senior customs officers at the various entry points in the country , the workshop also created a platform for interaction between officials from the EPA and the customs officers . <p> They were taken through topics such as the " Ozone depletion phenomenon , " " International and national response , " " Licensing and permission procedures " , " climate change , causes and effects and linkages to ozone depletion " , and the " role of customs officers and other key stakeholders .
