<p> Ghana and Cote d'Ivoire have agreed to set up a strategic co-operation framework , and have signed a Strategic Partnership Agreement ( SAP ) , to that end , which will give new momentum to Ivorian-Ghanaian co-operation . <p> This agreement covers the areas of Defence and Security ; Cocoa and cashew economy and other strategic crops ; Maritime cooperation ; Mining , energy and environment ; Transport ; and Economic policies . <p> Signing the agreement on behalf of the Government and people of Ghana , the President of the Republic , Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo noted that " this Agreement will bind our two countries in even closer intimacy , and go beyond the conventional tools of co-operation , and , I am confident , will yield results of a historic and enduring nature for our two peoples .
