Events organized across the globe aim to provide greater awareness and action for those experiencing food insecurity . It has become a global one problem , and today its solution is considered an essential part of achieving Sustainable Development Goal ( SDG ) 2 -- to achieve Zero Hunger by 2030 . <p> According to the United Nations , the world population is predicted to hit the 9 billion mark by the year 2050 .
