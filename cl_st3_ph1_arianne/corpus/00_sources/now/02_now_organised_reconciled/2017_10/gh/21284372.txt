Boahen Aidoo stated that the " long-term sustainability of the cocoa value chain is a shared global responsibility . There is a growing international cooperation and understanding to the effect that , the cocoa industry needs to maintain productivity and profitability at levels that are socially acceptable , economically viable and environmentally friendly . Thus , the attainment of a sustainable cocoa economy has become a predominant objective of all stakeholders . Meanwhile , perspectives on what it means , the best path to its attainment , and the level of commitment to achieving it differs among stakeholders .
