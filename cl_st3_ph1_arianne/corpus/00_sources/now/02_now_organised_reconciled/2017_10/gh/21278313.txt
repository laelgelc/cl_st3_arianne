<p> The Ghana Cotton Farmers Association has appealed to the government to revive the cotton industry to create job opportunities for unemployed people in the Northern Savanna Zone and the country . <p> Osofo Patrick Adingtingah Apullah , chief of Ghana Cotton Farmers Association , said the agricultural sector employs many people , hence the need for more attention to be given to the sector . <p> Osofo Apullah , who is also Chief of Nyankpala Zongo in the Tolon District of the Northern Region , was speaking in an interview with GNA at his palace at Nyankpala over the weekend . <p> He said government should include the cotton/fibre industry in the Planting for Food and Jobs programme for the employment of many people in the Northern Savannah Zone and also include textile and jute industries in Kumasi , Juapong and Tema . <p> He said there is the need for government to make the right investment as well as put in place strategies to empower cotton farmers , which would earn the country a substantial exchange in return . <p> Osofo Apullah urged the public and farmers to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ also plant more trees to combat climate change as it was greatly affecting the agricultural sector . <p> He appealed to all to embrace peace in the region to promote productivity saying " peace must be preached in homes , towns and cities across the country .
