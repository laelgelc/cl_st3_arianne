COP 23 , scheduled for November 6th to 17th , would be chaired by Fiji , with support from the German Government , and it would help discuss and strengthen commitments of countries towards climate change mitigation , adaptation and funding activities to relieve the world , especially , developing countries like Ghana , of the impact of climate change . <p> The COP is the next most significant UN global event after the UN General Assembly meeting , and Ghana is among many countries that have ratified the Paris Climate Agreement to implement policy actions towards greenhouse gas reduction
