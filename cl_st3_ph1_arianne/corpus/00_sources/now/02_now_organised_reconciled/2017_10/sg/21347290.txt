<p> An interactive guide by The Straits Times , giving readers an in-depth look into the anatomy of cancer , has won a prestigious global award . <p> The Anatomy of Cancer beat five other contenders , including entries from The Wall Street Journal and Bloomberg , to win best website infographic at the Editor &amp; Publisher EPPY 2017 Awards . <p> It shared the prize with an entry from The Texas Tribune and Pro-Publica that looks at the impact of climate change on the United States . <p> It is the first time ST has won at the annual awards , which were launched in 1996 and honour the best media-affiliated websites across 30 categories . The awards received more than 300 entries this year . <p> ST 's winning work features three-dimensional ( 3D ) human models that can be rotated .
