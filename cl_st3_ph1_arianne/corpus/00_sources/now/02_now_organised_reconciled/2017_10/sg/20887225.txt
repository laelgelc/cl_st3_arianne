<p> The World Economic Forum is holding its 33rd India Economic Summit in partnership with the Confederation of Indian Industry ( CII ) on 4-6 October in New Delhi . <p> The summit will host 650 leaders from 35 countries , including 370 business leaders , 20 senior public figures and leading representatives of civil society , arts and culture , science and academia . <p> The theme of the summit is Creating Indian Narratives on Global Challenges . Climate change , India 's cultural renaissance , infrastructure , gender parity , tourism , education and skills are prominently featured in the programme . The recent economic reforms , including demonetization and the goods and services tax , will be addressed in a special plenary session . <p> With the first-ever Open Forum India , more than 35 livestreamed sessions and over 130 journalists , the India Economic Summit offers unprecedented opportunities for the general public to follow the discussion and participate in the summit .
