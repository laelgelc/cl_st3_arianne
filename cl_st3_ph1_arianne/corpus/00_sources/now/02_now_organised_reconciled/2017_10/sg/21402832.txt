Between 1990 and 2016 , 2,843 weather-related disasters were recorded in Asia , affecting 4.8 billion people and causing more than 500,000 deaths . <p> Despite a rise in the number of natural disasters , there has been no discernable rise in the global number of deaths or in the number of people affected by natural disasters , when compared to data from 1990 to 1999 , the report finds . <p> This could indicate that countries are beginning to invest in adaptation strategies to cope with natural disasters , Cox says . " <h> Losses to the global workforce <p> Another set of indicators explored by the report look at the " human-mediated " impacts of climate change . These are impacts that are intrinsically linked to human society , but often exacerbated by climate change . <p> The first of these indicators explores how climate change has affected the productivity of the global workforce , particularly in the less economically-developed parts of the world . The report finds that the global productivity in rural labour capacity -- defined as those who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ excluding agricultural workers -- has fallen by 5.3% from 2000 to 2016 . <p> The chart below shows how this global loss in productivity is spread across the world , with red indicating a percentage loss in productivity and blue showing a percentage gain in labour capacity . <p> In 2016 , this drop in productivity effectively took more than 920,000 people globally out of the workforce , the report finds , with 418,000 of these workers being " lost " from India . <p> One way that higher temperatures threaten labour capacity is by making manual work more physically challenging , the report finds : <p> " Higher temperatures pose profound threats to occupational health and labour productivity , particularly for people undertaking manual , outdoor labour in hot areas . Loss of labour capacity has important implications for the livelihoods of individuals , families , and communities , especially those relying on subsistence farming . " <p> An additional " human-mediated " impact of climate change is undernutrition , the report finds . The report says : " Increasing temperatures have been shown to reduce global wheat production by 6% for each 1C increase .
