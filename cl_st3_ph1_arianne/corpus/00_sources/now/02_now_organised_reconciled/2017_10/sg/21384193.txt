<p> Almost three quarters ( 72 percent ) of large and mid-cap companies worldwide do not acknowledge the financial risks of climate change in their annual financial reports , according to the KPMG Survey of Corporate Responsibility Reporting 2017 . <p> Of the minority that do acknowledge climate-related risk , less than one in 20 ( 4 percent ) provides investors with analysis of the potential business value at risk . <p> KPMG 's survey studied annual financial reports and corporate responsibility reports from the top 100 companies by revenue in each of 49 countries : a total of 4,900 companies . <p> It found only five countries in the world where a majority of the top 100 companies mention climate-related financial risks in their financial reports : Taiwan ( 88 percent ) , France ( 76 percent ) , South Africa ( 61 percent ) , US ( 53 percent ) and Canada ( 52 percent ) . <p> In most cases , disclosure of climate-related risk is either mandated or encouraged in these countries by the government , stock exchange or financial regulator . <p> When looking specifically @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , public acknowledgment of climate-related financial risk is more common but still far from universal .
