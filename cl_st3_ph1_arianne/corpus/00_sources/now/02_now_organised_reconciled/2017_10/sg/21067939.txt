<h> This film relies heavily on special effects but pays scant attention to acting , dialogue or wardrobe <h> REVIEW / SCIENCE FICTION-ACTION <p> GEOSTORM ( PG13 ) <p> 109 minutes/Now showing/1.5 stars <p> The story : After extreme weather ravages the planet , nations unite to create Dutch Boy , a global satellite system that stops the problem with missiles and energy beams . The system 's architect , Jake Lawson ( Gerard Butler ) , is fired for insubordination and replaced by his brother Max ( Jim Sturgess ) . A malfunction 's fatal consequences leads to Jake 's return and a high-level conspiracy is uncovered . <p> The heavy-handed , old-timey feel of this disaster movie might have you thinking of Roland Emmerich , the German film-maker who directed Independence Day ( 1996 ) and its sequel , Independence Day : Resurgence ( 2006 ) . <p> You would be wrong , but only just . Geostorm is the directorial debut of writer Dean Devlin , who is a frequent collaborator with Emmerich . <p> The jingoism , sentimentality and bombast in Geostorm are in the same @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> As director , Devlin 's sappiness - everyone verbalises feelings as if reading a news report - is injected into a movie that recycles the climategeddon premise of Emmerich 's The Day After Tomorrow ( 2004 ) , but with the added moisture of the skyscraper-topping waves from the same director 's 2012 ( 2009 ) . <p> Is Devlin , as some say , being retro-ironic ? If so , the mask has become his face . He casts as leading man Butler , looking more like an angry shar pei than ever . He plays the rulebook-ripping loose cannon rarely seen outside of 1990s Bruce Willis movies or Comedy Central parody sketches . <p> His character and Sturgess ' Max have an intense sibling rivalry . We know this because it is mentioned every time they speak . <p> This is a movie where the special effects are relied on so heavily that acting , dialogue and wardrobe do not matter . <p> Butler 's beard stays the same scruffy length over days in space .
