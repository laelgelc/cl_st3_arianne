<h> Happy Mole Day ! Mayors Celebrate Scientific Date with Research Push <p> Move over Pi Day : Mayors across the U.S. are celebrating the day of the mole -- which began at 6:02 a.m. today ( 10/23 ) in honor of the vast number of atoms or molecules in a mole of matter ( 6.02 X 1023 ) -- by advocating unfettered scientific research . <p> So far , 20 mayors , whose cities , including Pittsburgh , Philadelphia , Denver and Nashville , have a population of almost 6 million people , have signed the pledge , which states that government scientists and those who conduct federally or state-funded research should be able to share their results " without political interference .
