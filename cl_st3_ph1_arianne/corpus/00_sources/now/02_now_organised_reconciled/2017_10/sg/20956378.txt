<h> Ong Ye Kung in Australia till Thursday <p> The Minister for Education ( Higher Education and Skills ) and Second Minister for Defence , Mr Ong Ye Kung , will visit Australia from today to Thursday . <p> Mr Ong will visit SAF troops training in Exercise Wallaby 2017 at the Shoalwater Bay Training Area in Queensland , the Ministry of Defence ( Mindef ) said yesterday . <p> His visit is at the invitation of Australian Minister for Foreign Affairs Julie Bishop . <p> He will also visit Canberra and Sydney to meet Australian leaders , including Ms Bishop and Minister for Defence Marise Payne , Mindef said in a statement . <p> The minister will also visit the Australian War Memorial , the Australian National University in Canberra , and the Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation 's digital and data innovation group Data61 in Sydney . <p> His itinerary also includes a visit to the Great Barrier Reef off Queensland , where he will receive a briefing from the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority on the impact of climate change on the Great Barrier Reef @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on October 07 , 2017 , with the headline ' Ong Ye Kung in Australia till Thursday ' .
