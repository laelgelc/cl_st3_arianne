Incheon boasts innovations like sensors that monitor the district 's temperature , energy use and traffic flow , as well as a vast network of underground tunnels that automatically sort household trash . Data Analytics <p> Second , data must underpin Mayors ' and City Leaders ' decisions . But staff must also be able to work with this data so they can stay focused on achieving key goals . In the aftermath of the recent hurricanes in the United States , for instance , cities and states have used data to organise their response .
