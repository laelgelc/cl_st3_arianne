<p> This effort to boost the electricity supply using Singapore 's reservoirs is pragmatic and productive . <p> Moreover , having the solar panels cover the water surface will not only reduce fresh water evaporation , but also help to cool the electronic units . <p> There are other factors in Singapore 's favour for renewable solar energy . <p> The Republic is just about one degree north of the equator and has , on average , 12 hours of sunlight a day . This translates to more than 4,000 hours of daylight a year . <p> The world 's largest floating solar farm is in the Chinese city of Huainan in Anhui province , where a tapestry of 120,000 solar panels covers an area of over 86ha , generating 40MW of power , enough for 15,000 homes . <p> From the crude data , it seems that each hectare could power over 174 homes . <p> The surface area of Singapore 's reservoirs is substantial . <p> If the reservoirs were deployed to host floating solar farms to produce renewable energy , it could power a large number of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' area were 1,500ha , the potential renewable energy could power over 260,000 homes . <p> This projection is based on the 15 per cent to 20 per cent efficiency of solar panels .
