<h> Email <p> A NEW study by the prestigious Lancet Commission on pollution and health carries important pointers for Pakistan . It states , amongst other things , that the incidence of pollution is changing : concerns about ambient air , soil and chemical pollution are now greater than apprehensions relating to impure household air and water . This type of pollution is produced by " industry , mining , electricity generation , mechanised agriculture , and petroleum-powered vehicles " , each of which is growing in Pakistan . The fastest-growing polluters in the world are developing countries with high rates of growth coming from sectors that rely on these activities . This poses serious health risks to the most vulnerable populations , the poor , minorities and children . Beyond health , the study points out that pollution has consequences for the economy as well , and in some cases can reduce the growth rate of an economy by up to two percentage points . <p> This is important to bear in mind for a number of reasons .
