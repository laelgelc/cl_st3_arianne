<p> Express News reported on Saturday that the former PM , who is the chief of the ruling Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz ( PML-N ) , has formed a inquiry committee chaired by party leader Senator Zafar Rajaul Haq and also including Climate Change Minister Mushahidullah Khan and Interior Minister Ahsan Iqbal . <p> The committee has been directed to identify those whose negligence led to the changes in the Khatm-e-Nabuwat declaration within 24 hours and present a report to the party president , Nawaz , who will take action against them as per party policy .
