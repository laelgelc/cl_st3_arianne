<h> BQPS-II becomes first Pakistani power plant certified under ISO 50001 <p> KARACHI : K-Electric 's ( KE ) flagship Bin Qasim Power Station ( BQPS ) -II became the first Pakistani power plant to achieve ISO 50001 energy management system certification . The certification was awarded by ISO-accredited body Bureau Veritas Pakistan to K-Electric after due process alignment and auditing . <p> The power utility is recognized as one of the industry leaders in energy management practices and this certification is yet another testament to the KE 's longstanding commitment to sustainability , including energy management initiatives to reduce energy consumption and greenhouse emissions , the company said . <p> According to K-Electric Chief Generation &amp; Transmission Officer Dale Sinkler , " with BQPS-II becoming the first Pakistani power plant to achieve ISO 50001 certification , KE is proud to have blazed a trail in energy performance and fuel efficiency . This certification not only ensures that we have a structured energy management system in place at BQPS-II but also allows us to replicate efficient practices to other power plants in our generation fleet . Moreover , 17 of KE integrated business centres ( IBCs ) are also certified with ISO 9001-2008 , 12 of which gained certification in FY 2016 , the company stated . <p> KE under its energy management policy actively promotes awareness for energy conservation , conducts energy audits per ISO 50001 global standard - aimed at reducing electricity costs , enhance productivity and help save the country 's natural resources for future generations both within the company and also to its consumers , KE said .
