<h> Hopes dashed for giant new Antarctic marine sanctuary <p> SYDNEY - Hopes for a vast new marinesanctuary in pristine East Antarctica were dashed Saturday after a key conservation summit failed to reach agreement , with advocates urging " greater vision and ambition " . Expectations were high ahead of the annual meeting of the Commission for the Conservation of AntarcticMarine Living Resources ( CCAMLR ) - a treaty tasked with overseeing protection and sustainable exploitation of the Southern Ocean . Last year 's summit in Hobart saw the establishment of a massive US and New Zealand-backed marine protected area ( MPA ) around the Ross Sea covering an area roughly the size of Britain , Germany and France combined . But an Australia and France-led push this year to create a second protected area in East Antarctica spanning another one million square kilometre zone failed . Officials told AFP that Russia and China were key stumbling blocks , worried about compliance issues and fishing rights . Consensus is needed from all 24 CCAMLR member countries and the European Union .
