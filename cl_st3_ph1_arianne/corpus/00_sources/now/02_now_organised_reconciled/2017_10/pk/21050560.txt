<h> Islamic Relief executes 300 projects for people 's welfare <p> A media familiarization session was held at Islamic Relief Pakistan 's headquarter . The objective behind the activity was to inform media and public about projects executed in different areas , and their importance in development of the country . A large number of journalists from different media houses attended the session . The participants were briefed on the catastrophic consequences of Climate Change in Pakistan and what steps are needed to be taken to minimize them . Sarmad Iqbal , Advocacy specialist of Islamic Relief Pakistan shared the details and impact of the projects carried out by IRP across Pakistan in the context of Climate Change . Mr. Adnan Cheema , Country Director Islamic Relief Pakistan in his address said " Islamic Relief has completed its 25 years journey in Pakistan . We reached over 6 million people in some of the remotest areas of Pakistan and executed more than 300 projects in different sectors like health and nutrition , livelihood and food security , Islamic Microfinance , Disaster Risk Reduction , Emergency Response , Water and Sanitation " .
