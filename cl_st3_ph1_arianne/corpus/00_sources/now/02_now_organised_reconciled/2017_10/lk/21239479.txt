After Xi Jinping 's Speech at the CCP Congress <h> Xi was also realistic or modest enough to announce a ' moderately prosperous society ' by 2025 , while our ' Vision 2025 ' promises absolutely ' prosperous ' or rather a ' rich ' economy by that time ! <p> by Laksiri Fernando <p> ( October 22 , 2017 , Sydney , Sri Lanka Guardian ) It is not completely an unanticipated speech by President Xi Jinping , nevertheless revealing new directions for the party , country and foreign policy . Thus it has implications for the world at large . This time , it was a marathon speech of three and half hours showing his seriousness , determination and epoch making character . In the Chinese system of politics , the party congress is not merely about the party , but also about the country . What is decided there is decisive . This is the 19th congress since 1921 , that means held on every five years . <p> When the country 's President , same as the General Secretary of the party @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the next five years , the congress goes on discussing them focusing on actions to be taken in working groups and again coming together to finalize them and to elect new leaders at various levels to implement them . The method is like the specialized workshop model in our countries . The congress goes on for a week . <p> Around 2,300 delegates attended the congress . 265 were on the stage as the present officials or political elite . With Xi Jinping , two past presidents , Hu Jintao and Jiang Zemin , were sitting beside him among other key officials . The congress will soon elect 370 full and alternate members to the central committee . Then the congress will elect 25 politburo members by secret ballot . Thereafter the standing committee of the politburo also will be announced . Altogether , the congress is composed of representatives of the provinces , the military and government agencies . The structure is perfectly pyramidal . <h> Background <p> The title of the speech was " Secure a decisive victory in building a moderately prosperous society in all respects and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for a new era . " ' Moderately prosperous society , ' ' socialism with Chinese characteristics ' and ' new era ' were the three concepts that he enunciated . Those were repeated several times throughout the speech , explaining the components . <p> Therefore , those who were predicting or speculating that China would turn completely capitalist , because of its adoption of a market economy and promotion of the private sector , may have to reassess their hopes in view of the present congress . After Mao Zedong and Deng Xiaoping , Xi Jinping might be the current trendsetter both in the economy and the social system . <p> Also those who were hoping that China would mechanically adopt a Western type democracy may have to wait at least for some time to assess the trends of democratization . China might not rush to extensively open up the political system while she has been taking some efforts to loosen the party or the state grip over the citizens and ensure some civil liberties while emphasizing citizen 's responsibilities and patriotism .
