<p> Global average carbon dioxide concentrations rose by 0.8% during 2016 , the largest annual increase ever observed . According to figures released overnight by the World Meteorological Organisation , atmospheric CO2 concentrations reached 403.3 parts per million . This is the highest level for at least 3 million years , having climbed by 3.3 ppm relative to the 2015 average . <p> The unprecedented rise is due to carbon dioxide emissions from fossil fuels ( coal , oil and gas ) and the strong 2015-16 El Ni ? o event , which reduced the capacity of forests , grasslands and oceans to absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere . <p> Greenhouse gas levels are unprecedented in modern times.WMO <p> The figures appear in the WMO 's annual Greenhouse Gas Bulletin .
