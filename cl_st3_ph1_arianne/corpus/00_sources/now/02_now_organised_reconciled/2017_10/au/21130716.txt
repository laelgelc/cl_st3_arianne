<h> Pope criticises US over Paris accord <p> Pope Francis has taken a subtle swipe at Donald Trump for leaving the Paris climate accord during an address to the UN . <p> Philip Pullella <p> ReutersOctober 16 , 20179:58pm <p> Pope Francis has implicitly criticised the United States for pulling out of the Paris agreement on climate change , praising it as a means to control the devastating effects of global warming . <p> The United States is the only country out of 195 signatories to have withdrawn from the accord , which aims to cut emissions blamed for the rise in temperatures . <p> US President Donald Trump announced the decision in June shortly after visiting the pope , a strong supporter of the deal . At the time a Vatican official said the move was a " slap in the face " for the pope and the Vatican . <p> " We see consequences of climate change every day , " the pope said in an address to the United Nations Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) at its headquarters in Rome . <p> " Thanks to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the problem and the international community has also worked out the legal methods , such as the Paris Accord , which sadly , some have abandoned , " he said . <p> Under the deal , US had committed to reducing its own emissions by 26 to 28 per cent , compared with 2005 levels , by 2025 . <p> In his Spanish-language address to the UN agency , Francis denounced " negligence toward the delicate equilibriums of the ecosystems , the presumption of manipulating and controlling the limited resources of the planet , and the greed for profit . " <p> Agriculture ministers and diplomats from the Group of Seven ( G7 ) world power nations attended the gathering , which marked FAO 's World Food Day .
