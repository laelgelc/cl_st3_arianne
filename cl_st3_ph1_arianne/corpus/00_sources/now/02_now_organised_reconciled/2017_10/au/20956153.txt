<h> Conference raises $9b for marine protection <p> Published : 7:53 am , Saturday , 7 October 2017 <p> A global conference to better protect marine life has raised more than $AU9 billion and won commitments to protect huge swathes of the earth 's oceans . <p> The European Union , which organised the Our Ocean conference in the Maltese capital of Valletta , its 28 member states and its EIB investment bank gave almost half those financial commitments , about $US3.4 billion . <p> Representatives from businesses , almost 100 countries and others pushed the total up to the unprecedented level . <p> The conference focused on funding and leading projects as varied as combating plastics pollution to countering illegal fishing and looking at the effects of climate change . <p> The Our Ocean conference has accumulated some 8.7 billion euros ( $A13 billion ) since it started in 2014 but the efforts in 2017 exceeded expectations . <p> ' We are beginning to see leaders in government , civil society and the private sector standing up to be counted to make tangible commitments to conservation , which is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the delegation for the WWF wildlife group . <p> On top of the financial commitments , nations also promised to add new Marine Protected Areas spanning more than 2.5 million square kilometres , which the EU said translates to over half its territory . <p> The efforts to better protect marine life came in in all shapes . Five top global insurance industry companies committed to refuse insurance to vessels internationally blacklisted for illegal or unregulated fishing . <p> ' Today is a major breakthrough , with leading insurers committing to deny a financial lifeline to pirate-fishing vessels , ' said Lasse Gustavsson , the executive director of Oceana Europe . <p> The mission to protect marine life is urgent , said the Vatican , pointing to the rapid decay in important sites like the Great Barrier Reef in Australia . <p> ' We witness a marvellous marine world being transformed into an underwater cemetery , bereft of colour and life , ' said Vatican Secretary of State Piero Cardinal Parolin.
