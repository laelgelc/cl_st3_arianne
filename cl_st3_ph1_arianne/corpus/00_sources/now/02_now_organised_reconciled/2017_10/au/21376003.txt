<p> Only the mudbricks were left after the flames had done their worst to Daryl Taylor 's home on Black Saturday . <h> Key points <p> Made of old tyres and everyday garbage such as aluminium cans and bottles <p> Its 45-degree angled slope allows a potential fire to " flow " over the house <p> A key element of the design is a concrete lined bunker , complete with oxygen and water <p> Why did the bricks survive the devastating 2009 blaze but nothing else did ? <p> It was a question that set Mr Taylor on a journey of discovery aimed at building a radical , new home that he could defend in the event of another fire . <p> Ten years on , Mr Taylor , of Kinglake in Victoria , is the owner of the state 's first officially approved Earthship -- a " radically sustainable building " made of retro-fitted mudbrick , beer cans , glass and old tyres . <p> The building is based on the work of Earthship Biotecture founder Mike Reynolds , who is also known as the Garbage Warrior . <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Mr Taylor believed " nuanced " design was necessary in houses , particularly in bushfire vulnerable areas like Kinglake because fire was unique in every set of circumstances . <p> " The design of the house has got to reflect the local environment , it 's got to be tied into the ecology and the topography and geography and none of these houses that are built by industry at scale take those kinds of things into consideration , " Mr Taylor said . <p> The Earthship provided an opportunity for less abrupt angles in the form of a berm , a 45-degree angled slope allowing a potential fire to " flow " over the house . <p> " It just provides a pathway for the fire rather than a fire smashing up against a 90-degree wall and just continuously hitting the wall , " Mr Taylor said . <p> With climate change and predictions of a longer bushfire season in Victoria , Mr Taylor said it was important to be able to stay and defend the house . <p> A key element of the design is a concrete lined bunker @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where people can survive for up to 45 minutes as the bushfire passes over . <p> " We wanted to build something that we could shelter in and actively defend , " Mr Taylor said . <p> Surrounded by four large water tanks , the Earthship is also partially underground helping it maintain its cool temperature of 21 degrees regardless of high temperatures outside . <p> " So the key idea is to have ample water at our disposal , plus generators that are off grid , " Mr Taylor said . <p> " The first thing that happened on Black Saturday was all the power went out
