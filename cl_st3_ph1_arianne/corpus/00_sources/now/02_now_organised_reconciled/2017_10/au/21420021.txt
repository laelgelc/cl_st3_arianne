<h> Peppermint Grove is Perth 's water consumption capital <p> HOMES in Perth 's most affluent suburb of Peppermint Grove are the city 's biggest water guzzlers , with average water use almost four times higher than some inner-city postcodes . <p> The Water Corporation 's latest snapshot of Perth 's water use by suburb shows the lush gardens and palatial homes of Peppermint Grove are soaking an average of 402,000 litres of drinking water a year . <p> Dalkeith had the second-highest rate of average consumption at 342,000 litres . <p> Outside the golden triangle , the Perth Hills suburbs of Gooseberry Hill , where customers used 340,000 litres on average in 2016-17 , Bedfordale ( 333,000 ) and Darlington ( 330,000 ) were the next biggest consumers . <p> By contrast , households in central Perth are typically using 118,000 litres a year , followed by neighbouring Highgate ( 123,000 ) , West Perth ( 124,000 ) and East Perth ( 126,000 ) . <p> The Water Corp said differences in use
