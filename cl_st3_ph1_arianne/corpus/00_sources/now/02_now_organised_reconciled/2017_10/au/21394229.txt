<h> Power prices split voters on Paris : poll <p> Updated : 6:59 am , Tuesday , 31 October 2017 <p> A new poll suggests Australians are split on the prospect of Australia quitting the Paris accord on climate change if it brings cheaper power prices . <p> A Newspoll survey published by The Australian found 45 per cent of surveyed voters would be in favour of quitting the international agreement for cheaper bills , compared with 40 per cent who did n't favour the move and 15 per cent who were n't sure . <p> US President Donald Trump pulled the his country out of the Paris accord , which commits Australia to a 28 per cent reduction on 2005 emissions in the next 13 years , claiming the cost outweighed the benefit . <p> Of the surveyed voters , those who support One Nation were the most in favour of pulling out , followed by Coalition voters , with Labor and Greens voters largely opposed to it .
