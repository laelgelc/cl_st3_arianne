<h> Italy 's high-rise forests take root around the world <p> Milan ( AFP ) - As balconies bristle with tree branches and sunshine dapples the leaves of thousands of plants , two apartment buildings in the heart of Milan have almost disappeared under lush forest . <p> The brainchild of Milanese architect Stefano Boeri , the Bosco Verticale ( Vertical Forest ) uses more than 20,000 trees and plants to adorn the high-rise buildings from top to bottom ? a project now being exported all over the world , from China to the Netherlands . <p> The two original leafy towers dominate the skyline in the northern Italian city , giving residents -- including celebrities like footballer Ivan Perisic -- an enviable view over the new district of Porta Nuova and beyond . <p> Cherry , apple and olive trees spill over balconies alongside beeches and larches , selected and positioned according to their resistance to wind and preference for sunlight or humidity . <p> Boeri said the idea came from his obsession with trees and determination to make them " an essential component of architecture , " particularly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ I was in Dubai in 2007 and I watched this city growing in the middle of the desert , with more than 200 glass towers multiplying the effect of heat , " he recalls . <p> He wanted instead to create something that " as well as welcoming life , can contribute to reducing pollution , because trees absorb microparticles and CO2 " . <p> " Cities now produce about 75 percent of the CO2 present in the atmosphere . Bringing more trees into the city means fighting the enemy on the spot , " he said . <p> - ' Best Tall Building Worldwide ' - <p> Opened in 2014 , the Vertical Forest won the prestigious Frankfurt International Highrise Award , and the Chicago Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat named it Best Tall Building Worldwide . <p> " It 's a unique thing to live here , we 're in direct contact with the plants while being in the city centre and in a super modern skyscraper , " says Simona Pizzi , who can see the mountains from her 14th floor apartment . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ years , and we see them changing with the seasons , " adds the proud owner of an apple tree , where the white flowers contrast magnificently with the green foliage . <p> Boeri worked closely with botanists to create a nursery of a thousand trees that have been trained to grow under specific conditions . <p> The team faced numerous challenges , from how the balconies should be structured to take the weight of the plants , to how to secure the tree roots and what needed to go into the soil . <p> They even carried out resistance tests at a hurricane centre in Miami . <p> " For every human being living in the building , there are about two trees , 10 shrubs and 40 plants , " Boeri said . <p> - The future of housing ?
