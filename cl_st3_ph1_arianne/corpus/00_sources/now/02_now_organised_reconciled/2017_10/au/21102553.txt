Picture : TONY LOMASSource:Supplied <p> FREE tours of a southern power station were booked solid yesterday , but there are still places available for power station tours in the North and North-West in coming weeks . <p> Hydro Tasmania yesterday opened its Meadowbank Power Station near New Norfolk . <p> Hydro Tasmania production and maintenance manager Jesse Clark said about 170 people took the chance to learn more about hydro electricity generation and the " Battery of the Nation " program , which aims to increase the amount of electricity Tasmania contributes to the national grid . <p> Using pumped hydro energy storage , the program has the potential to double Tasmania 's renewable energy capacity from 2500 megawatts to 5000 , Hydro says . <p> " Our tours are a chance to get an up-close look at some of the renewable energy assets that will play a role in that vision , " Mr Clark said . <p> " A lot of people can see that there needs to be a bit more energy come into the market ... they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Tasmania , " Mr Clark said . <p> He said Tasmanians were interested in their state 's electricity generation . <p> " There has been all the talk about climate change and renewable energy and knowing that in Tasmania we produce clean energy . <p> " The other side of it is Hydro Tasmania is Tasmanian owned and that connection the people have with the lakes and the power stations . " <p> Hydro Tasmania will open its Cethana Power Station in the North-West on Saturday and Poatina Power Station in the North on November 11 . <p> Tours are free of charge but bookings are essential .
