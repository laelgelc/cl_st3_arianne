<h> Amazon delivers supplies to hurricane-ravaged Puerto Rico <p> Amazon is lending a hand to residents of Puerto Rico following the devastating hurricane that hit the island almost two weeks ago . <p> The company sent a plane full of supplies , including baby formula , sheets , tents , tarps and other essential items , to San Juan on Monday , according to Instagram and Twitter posts from Jeff Bezos , founder and CEO of Amazon . <p> Much of Puerto Rico has been without food , water and electricity since Hurricane Maria , a category 4 storm , hit on Sept. 20 . Roads and buildings have been destroyed on the Caribbean island and communications networks are almost nonexistent . Local officials have complained that response efforts from the federal government have been slow . <p> Amazon is n't the only tech company to step up efforts to help victims . As soon as the storm passed , Tesla began sending hundreds of Powerwall battery systems , which are paired with solar panels to help restore electric power , according to news reports . <p> Phone and internet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Apple , Facebook and Uber have also donated to hurricane relief . Facebook has raised more than $10 million for victims of Harvey , Irma and Maria . <p> Tech companies have also rallied to help change policy and combat climate change , which scientists believe contributes to more powerful storms . In June , Apple , Facebook and Google as well as Tesla 's Elon Musk unsuccessfully urged President Donald Trump not to back out of the Paris accord to fight global warming . <p> Tech Enabled : CNET chronicles tech 's role in providing new kinds of accessibility . <p> CNET Magazine : Check out a sample of the stories in CNET 's newsstand edition .
