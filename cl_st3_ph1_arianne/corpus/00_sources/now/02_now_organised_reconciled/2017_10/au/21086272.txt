<h> Labor questions Turnbull energy policy <p> Labor says any energy policy presented to the coalition party room next week wo n't be based on industry or expert advice . <p> The Turnbull government is putting the finishing touches to its energy policy , which the prime minister says will address energy affordability , reliability and meet Australia 's emissions target . <p> It 's understood the policy could be discussed at Tuesday 's coalition party room meeting in Canberra . <p> Labor spokesman Pat Conroy said the government had ditched Chief Scientist Alan Finkel 's key recommendation of a cleanmh energy target , which had the backing of industry and experts and bipartisan support . <p> ' Whatever the prime minister presents to the party room , it has had no industry or expert consultation and will be the result of a shambolic process dictated by people who agree with former prime minister Abbott that policy to address climate change is akin to pagan goat sacrifice , ' Mr Conroy told AAP.
