<p> Pollution kills at least nine million people and costs trillions of dollars every year , according to the most comprehensive global analysis to date , which warns the crisis " threatens the continuing survival of human societies " . <p> Toxic air , water , soils and workplaces are responsible for the diseases that kill one in every six people around the world , the landmark report found , and the true total could be millions higher because the impact of many pollutants are poorly understood . The deaths attributed to pollution are triple those from Aids , malaria and tuberculosis combined . <p> The vast majority of the pollution deaths occur in poorer nations and in some , such as India , Chad and Madagascar , pollution causes a quarter of all deaths .
