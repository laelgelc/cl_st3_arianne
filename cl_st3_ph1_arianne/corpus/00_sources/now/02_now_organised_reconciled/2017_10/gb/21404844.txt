<p> The present-day effects of our changing climate mean more than just warmer days , cooler seasons , and more extreme storms . The impact of climate change is felt all across the globe -- affecting communities and economies from nations big and small , rich and poor , established and developing . However , it is the still-developing countries that struggle most with the negative consequences of climate change . <p> Fortunately , mayors and local governments are advancing policies and programs to boost equity and economic growth that help mitigate the toll of climate change on their communities . By pursuing " inclusive growth " , mayors worldwide are building not only a cleaner , more sustainable future , but one in which all people are able to succeed . <p> " Global Covenant cities are making great strides in advancing the fortunes of our most vulnerable people , while building sustainable cities .
