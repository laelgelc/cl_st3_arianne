<p> In the humanitarian horror that Libya has become , the migrant detention centre at Abu Salim is by no means the worst . <p> Migrant centres in this country , packed with thousands of people seized on the trafficking routes that criss-cross Libya , have become renowned for forced labour , beatings , torture and rape . <p> But in southern Tripoli , Abu Salim offers something close to respite for those who have been on the road for weeks if not months . Run by the interior ministry , it 's one of the few detention sites in Libya that journalists can safely visit . There 's a health clinic , a kitchen , dormitories and mattresses , spaces for prayer . <p> But there is little hope . The 150 or so migrants who are stuck here have made perilous journeys from Mali , Niger , Nigeria , Cote d'Ivoire , Burkina Faso , Gambia , Guinea , Senegal , but Abu Salim is the end of the road . It is likely to be the closest they will get to Europe .
