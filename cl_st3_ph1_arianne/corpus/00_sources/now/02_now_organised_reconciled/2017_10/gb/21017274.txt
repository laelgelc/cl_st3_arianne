<h> Get into film with host of top movies <p> YOUNG film fans in Southampton can enjoy a trip to the cinema at the Into Film Festival 2017 -- the world 's biggest youth film festival - which returns in November with its diverse programme of free screenings and events for five to 19-year-olds . <p> The festival , which runs from November 8 to 24 , uses the magic of film -- from exclusive preview screenings of new blockbusters to popular classics - to engage young minds in a broad range of topics . <p> Festival highlights in Hampshire include a special screening of Christopher Nolan 's Dunkirk , pictured , which brings to life the rescue mission involving hundreds of thousands of British Allied soldiers taking place on the beaches of Dunkirk at the height of WWII .
