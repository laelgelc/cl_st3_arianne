<p> The BBC has apologised for breaching its own editorial standards during an episode of the Today programme where a climate change sceptic was allowed to express a falsehood without being challenged . <p> During the breakfast show in August , Lord Lawson claimed that global temperatures had not risen in the last decade . In fact they have risen for the last three years . Although Lawson 's own think tank , the Global Warming Policy Forum , later acknowledges his inaccuracy , he was not pressed by presenter Justin Webb . <p> The interview sparked immediate controversy and the BBC received a number of complaints but initially stood its ground , arguing that " dissenting voices " were an important part of the organisation 's commitment to impartiality . <p> Some listeners redoubled their efforts and expressed dissatisfaction that the former Conservative Energy Secretary 's claim was not challenged in the slightest .
