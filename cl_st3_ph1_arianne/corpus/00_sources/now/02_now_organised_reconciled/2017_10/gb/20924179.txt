For over two decades , an ever more educated wealthy elite has trumpeted its belief in tolerance , diversity , and meritocracy -- even as it 's also helped usher in record levels of inequality that have left many Americans feeling economically excluded and increasingly angry . <p> Trump 's retrograde presidency has revealed the profound contradictions at the top of the US income ladder .
