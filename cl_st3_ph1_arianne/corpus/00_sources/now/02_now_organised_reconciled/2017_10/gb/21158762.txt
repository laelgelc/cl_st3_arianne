On the island of Borneo , three generations of Bornean elephants cross the terraces of an oil-palm plantation being cleared for replanting . In the Malaysian state of Sabah , where most rainforest has been logged , the palm-oil industry is still a major driver of deforestation , squeezing elephants into smaller pockets of forest .
