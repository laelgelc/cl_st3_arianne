The paper criticises those companies for their impact on climate change , such as the rise of sea levels and the increase in global temperatures . <p> The point of the paper is to assign responsibility -- and thus the potential job of clearing it all up -- to those who dug and pumped up those fuels . <p> The problem with this is that the basic contention is tosh .
