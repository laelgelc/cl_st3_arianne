Unheard of in 1987 , it is an important phenomenon produced by a strong jet of air in the tail of a cloud -- hence the sting -- wrapped round a low-pressure centre . Reaching 50km across , the jet starts at about 3km or 4km above the earth and descends over three or four hours , while snow and rain falling into it evaporate and cool as the system descends , which help it to accelerate to high speeds .
