<p> A brochure summarising the main outcomes will be presented to climate negotiators at their annual meeting , which this year is taking place in Bonn in November . <p> The Biological Impacts of Ocean Acidification report authors say some creatures may benefit directly from the chemical changes - but even these could still be adversely affected indirectly by shifts in the whole food web . <p> What is more , the research shows that changes through acidification will be made worse by climate change , pollution , coastal development , over-fishing and agricultural fertilisers . <p> Ocean acidification is happening because as CO2 from fossil fuels dissolves in seawater , it produces carbonic acid and this lowers the pH of the water . <p> Since the beginning of the Industrial Revolution , the average pH of global ocean surface waters have fallen from pH 8.2 to 8.1 . This represents an increase in acidity of about 26% . <p> The study 's lead author is Prof Ulf Riebesell from the GEOMAR Helmholtz Centre for Ocean Research in Kiel . <p> He is a world authority on the topic and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> He told BBC News : " Acidification affects marine life across all groups , although to different degrees . <p> " Warm-water corals are generally more sensitive than cold-water corals .
