<p> This year 's renewable energy report from the International Energy Agency confirms what many watching the industry already know : clean energy prices have taken a nosedive , upping competition and uptake of new technologies . <p> Clean energy will grow by about 1,000 gigawatts in the next five years , according to the International Energy Agency 's projection . That production will be equal to about half the globe 's coal power capacity , in a fraction of the time it took to build all that coal power . By 2022 , clean energy will account for a third of power generation at 8,000 terawatt-hours , or enough to meet the current electricity demands from China , India and Germany combined . <p> " The message is very clear : Competition is driving cost down at a pace which is much faster than it was in the past , " said Dr. Paolo Frankl , head of IEA 's renewable energy division , on a call with reporters .
