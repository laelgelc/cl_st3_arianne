The longer the wind blows , the more of its energy is transferred to the ocean , resulting in bigger waves . <p> The wind fetch is the area that winds blow over the ocean . If you blow over a hot cup of soup , the distance between the edges of the bowl equals the length of the wind fetches . Wind fetches in the Pacific can exceed 500 miles . <p> Air temperature is the final factor .
