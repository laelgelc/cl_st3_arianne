A recent study by researchers at Kyushu University in Japan , and published in Earth and Planetary Science Letters , now sheds new light on this stress build-up in tectonic plates . The focus was on the Nankai Trough , one of three major subduction zones offshore of Japan . <p> " Our understanding of the dynamic behavior of plate boundary faults has advanced , " lead author Takeshi Tsuji says .
