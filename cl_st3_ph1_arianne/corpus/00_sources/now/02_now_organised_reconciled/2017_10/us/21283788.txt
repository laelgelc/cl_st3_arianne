<h> High mountain archaeology topic of Cody talk <p> The melting snow and ice in mountainous areas is yielding some enlightening findings in the fields of archaeology and anthropology . <p> At the next Draper Natural History Museum Lunchtime Expedition talk , research scientist Craig M. Lee will explain how climate change is opening the cryosphere -- the frozen water part of the Earth 's system -- as a new research frontier . <p> The free lecture is titled " The Archaeology of Alpine Snow and Ice in the Mid-Latitude Mountains of the Greater Yellowstone and Beyond , " and takes place on Thursday , Nov. 2 , at 12:15 p.m. in the Buffalo Bill Center of the West . <p> " The discovery of rare , unique , and important artifacts and paleobiological specimens at melting ice patches holds the potential to revolutionize anthropological theories and concepts pertaining to human colonization and adaptations , " Lee said .
