And it 's become the primary tool both for entertaining people and addressing social problems . Violence that once seemed unthinkable has become central to Trump 's understanding of how American society now defines itself . <p> Language in the service of violence has a long history in the United States , and in this current historical moment , we now have the violence of organized forgetting . <h> Violence as a source of pleasure <p> As memory recedes , violence as a toxin morphs into entertainment , policy and world views . <p> What 's different about Trump is that he revels in the use of violence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . It means moving away from analyzing violence as an abstraction by showing how it actually manifests itself in everyday life to inflict massive human suffering and despair . <p> The American public needs a new understanding of how civic institutions collapse under the force of state violence , how language coarsens in the service of carnage , how a culture hardens in a market society so as to foster contempt for compassion while exalting a culture of cruelty . <p> How does neoliberal capitalism work to spread the celebration of violence through its commanding cultural apparatuses and social media ? <p> How does war culture come to dominate civic life and become the most honored ideal in American society ? <p> Unless Americans can begin to address these issues as part of a broader discourse committed to resisting the growing authoritarianism in the United States , the plague of mass violence will continue -- and the once-shining promise of American democracy will become nothing more than a relic of history .
