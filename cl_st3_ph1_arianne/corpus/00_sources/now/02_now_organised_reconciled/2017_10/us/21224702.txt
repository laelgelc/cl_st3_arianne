<p> The Baltimore Sun article regarding public awareness of hospital rates for surgical procedures ( The Capital , Oct. 19 ) was educational . The article observes the disparities in cost of care between hospitals in Maryland and begins a discussion of how hospital rates are determined . <p> In Maryland , hospital rates are set by the Health Services Cost Review Commission with the goal of improving access to care , reducing cost of care and improving quality of care . Frequently these goals are in competition . <p> The health care marketplace is extremely complicated , with multiple stakeholders , each having unique and competing interests . As national and state conversations continue regarding changes to our health care system , public policy needs to be illuminated by what is in the best interest of the citizens . <p> One type of facility offers a significantly reduced cost alternative for surgical care ; ambulatory surgery centers . Rates at ASCs are not determined by a state commission but must be negotiated with each insurance carrier .
