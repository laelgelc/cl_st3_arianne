<h> Difference Maker : Fried is builder of homes , and community <p> W hen Adam Fried arrived in the Fredericksburg area in 1988 and founded Atlantic Builders , it was the beginning of a fruitful career in home construction that would allow him to give back to the community . <p> " There has been an evolution over time for me to become involved in certain organizations I feel passionate about , " Fried said in a recent interview . " There is always need . " <p> Despite the heavy toll that the Great Recession took on the residential construction industry -- which Fried credits his management team at Atlantic for deftly navigating -- the company has enjoyed considerable success .
