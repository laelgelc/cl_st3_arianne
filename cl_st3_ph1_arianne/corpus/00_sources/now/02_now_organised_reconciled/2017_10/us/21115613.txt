<p> The 2017 hurricane season has already brought the U.S. three devastating storms , and the season does n't end for another two months . <p> As Puerto Rico and the U.S. Virgin Islands struggle to recover from Maria , Florida from Irma , and Texas from Harvey , New Jersey approaches the fifth anniversary of its own terrible hurricane . <p> Despite the lessons we should have learned from the $70 billion of damage wrought by Sandy , and the numerous storms that preceded it , our 130 linear miles of coastline and 239 coastal towns remain as vulnerable to another hurricane as they were in 2012 . <p> Preoccupied with a desire to return the coast to its pre-storm state , we have done very little to prepare for another Sandy -- which hit our shores with 80 mph winds and a storm surge that reached as high as nine feet -- let alone a Maria , which made landfall in Puerto Rico with winds at 165 mph .
