<h> Lord of the Rings and climate change <p> 22 hrs ago <p> I think about climate change a lot . Recently , I realized that thinking about climate change is something like reading the " Lord of the Rings " by J.R.R. Tolkien . <p> In LOTR , the rising power of the Dark Lord is becoming evident , but the quiet , gentle Hobbits , who love their country , their gardens and their way of life , do n't see it . The threats are gradual and have n't really become manifest in their country . Even though the powerful are beginning to focus on their country because it holds the key to destroying the dark power , the Hobbits do n't want to believe the warnings or change their way of life . <p> The hero , Frodo , also loves his home , his friends and his country . When the threat finally becomes explicit , he reluctantly accepts the challenge and , working with other races ( elves , dwarves , men and women , eagles ) , defeats the Dark Lord . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is plenty of that in other arenas ! ) , but through a sacrificial journey to destroy the Dark Lord 's ring of power . <p> I see our current circumstances in the U.S. as analogous to this . Although scientists ( and even Exxon , the fossil fuel company ) have recognized the threat of human-produced climate change for decades , changes have come slowly and have been easy to overlook . Until recently in the U.S. , the effects have been less dire than elsewhere in the world ( except in the West , with its decades-long drought ) . But more recently , we 've seen uncontrollable forest fires , and fantastically powerful storms , such as Katrina , Sandy and , now , Harvey , Irma and Maria , in the Gulf Coast , along with widespread flooding . In the island nations of Oceania , the seas are engulfing the land . In the Arctic and Antarctic , the ice caps are dramatically shrinking and huge ice shelves are detaching . In the Middle East , drought is exacerbating civil unrest and contributing to terrorism @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ n't seen these things here in the U.S. , it has perhaps been harder to take seriously the threats to us and future generations . Many people believe climate change is a hoax , and others hope that it is ! <p> Just as war finally came to Frodo and his friends , the dire effects of climate change are approaching and we need to take off the blinders and start dealing with them . Individuals can do their part -- there are vast amounts of information online for changes we can make that have a big impact . On a local , state and national level , however , we must urge our leadership to be leaders in addressing this threat . <p> It is inspiring to read stories of Pittsburgh , Philadelphia and other Pennsylvania cities and towns embracing renewable energy resources . It is hopeful to read of entrepreneurs developing technology to address these problems . Ultimately , though , the big changes come from congressional leadership . <p> In the U.S. House of Representatives , the Climate Solutions Caucus is working to effect change . This @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ including Pennsylvania Reps . Ryan Costello , R-6 , Patrick Meehan , R-7 , Brendan Boyle , D-13 , Brian Fitzpatrick , R-8 , and Matt Cartwright , D-17 . These leaders are champions for addressing climate issues . In July they voted to kill an amendment to the National Defense Authorization Act , proposed by Scott Perry , R-4 , that would have stripped the funding for studying the threats to national security through climate change . <p> The caucus now has 56 members . Reps . Tom Marino , R-10 , and Lou Barletta , R-11 , should join the caucus in addressing this threat . <p> The final outcome in LOTR was mixed : the earth was saved from the evil power of the Dark Lord , but much of beauty and wonder was lost in the struggle . I fear that will also happen on Earth . We will continue to lose amazing species of plants and animals , beautiful lands , people destroyed before we address this threat . Our own lives will change ; we will be called on to sacrifice and live more @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be very different from ours . We should take the prospect of this burden very seriously .
