<h> Lizards of Pennsylvania : Celebrate National Reptile Awareness Day <p> Although even the national websites and databases of national day observances ca n't tell us why , National Reptile Awareness Day is celebrated annually on October 21 , which is today . <p> According to Reptiles Magazine , on October 21 , " reptile fanatics have a day that they can celebrate and share their passion ; a chance to educate others who may not know about these amazing creatures called reptiles , and the habitat loss and threat of extinction that faces so many reptile species . " <p> In its report , " Reptiles and Amphibians - Threats and Concerns , " the National Park Service noted that " declines in amphibian and reptile populations have been and are being observed . Herpetofauna across the globe face threats from both known and unknown sources .
