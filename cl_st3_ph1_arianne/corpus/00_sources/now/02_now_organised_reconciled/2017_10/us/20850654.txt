<h> 30% of Europe 's Electricity Could Be Wind Powered by 2020 <h> In Brief <p> A pair of reports from WindEurope outlines how wind power alone could supply Europe with 30 percent of its electricity demand by 2030 . Many European countries are leading the way in reducing fossil fuel dependence . <p> Bloomberg points out that 716,000 jobs could be created with these efforts . The wind power industry could see 351 billion euros ( $417 billion ) of investment by 2030 . <p> For the next three years , Europe may see an average installation rate of an additional 12.6 GW per year . This would allow wind power alone to meet 16.5 percent of the continent 's energy demand , with a total of 204 GW of wind-generated power . The goal for 2030 would see that capacity to reach 323 GW . <p> Individual European countries are taking big steps to reduce their dependence on fossil fuels by investing in and building renewable energy generating installations .
