<h> This Surprising Sea Creature Is Causing Climate Change <p> Clams and worms at the bottom of the ocean may be releasing " ridiculous amounts of greenhouse gases " that contribute to climate change , according to a new study . <p> Researchers behind the study , published in the journal Scientific Reports , found that 10% of the methane emissions from the Baltic Sea come from clams and worms . That 's roughly equivalent to 20,000 cows . <p> Methane is one of the most potent greenhouse gases , roughly 30 times more effective at trapping heat in the atmosphere than carbon dioxide . The study evaluated the Baltic Sea specifically , but if that effect also occurs the globe the impact of ocean clams and worms to global warming would be significant . Study co-author Stefano Bonaglia of Stockholm University said the " small animals in the seafloor may act like cows in a stable . " <p> The researchers said the findings should receive consideration as some try to expand shellfish farming across the ocean .
