<h> Owners of big Montana coal mine due in court as layoffs loom <p> BILLINGS , Mont . - Attorneys for a large Montana coal mine are due in court this week to press a judge to reverse an order they say would prompt dozens of layoffs by blocking the mine 's expansion plans . <p> U.S. District Judge Donald Molloy has scheduled a Tuesday hearing in Missoula in the case involving Signal Peak Energy 's Bull Mountain Mine . It 's one of the largest underground mines in the U.S. <p> Molloy ruled in August that federal officials who approved the expansion overstated the mine 's economic benefits and failed to consider its climate change impacts . <p> Signal Peak attorneys filed an emergency motion Friday asking the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals to intervene . They warned about 30 layoffs were imminent with more likely in coming months .
