<h> A live performance of the song " Tutu " is the test case for using DNA as a non-degrading music storage device . <p> Three hundred years from now , if someone wants to listen to a classic recording of Miles Davis playing at the 1986 Montreux Jazz Festival , they can reach for a piece of DNA . After previous successful demos of storing other data on DNA -- including cat photos and an Ok Go video -- DNA has now been used for the first time to create an archival-quality file that can be stored for thousands of years . <p> advertisement <p> " We proved that the recording was perfect , " says Emily Leproust , CEO of Twist Bioscience , a DNA synthesis company that worked with researchers at Microsoft and the University of Washington to encode Miles Davis 's live performance of the song " Tutu .
