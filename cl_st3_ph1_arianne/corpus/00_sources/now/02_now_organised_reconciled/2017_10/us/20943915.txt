<p> A recent report from the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) on how weather affects economic performance will come into sharper focus Friday , as new payrolls data are set to be released in the United States . <p> The IMF research warned that weather impacts may have lasting effects on economies . The report , released at the end of September , points back to the unprecedented rise in global temperatures over the last 40 years and warns that the increasing global temperatures may end up lowering output , particularly in countries with warm climates . <p> It 's impossible to blame climate change for any individual storm , and much scientific modeling forecasts the effects of climate change to evolve over many years . The weather 's effect on the economy will vary from year to year and quarter to quarter . <p> But the IMF research predicts that warmer temperatures will eventually lead to agricultural output declines and a reduction in productivity .
