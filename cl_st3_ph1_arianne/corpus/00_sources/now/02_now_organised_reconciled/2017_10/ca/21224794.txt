How that oil would affect our fishing industry is difficult to predict . <h> Arctic cleanup difficult <p> Fears of an oil spill in the Arctic have always been high because of the lack of resources to clean it up . The remote location , plus ice cover and cold , dark arctic winters would hamper cleanup operations , possibly delaying them for months until daylight returns and ice clears . During that time , oil could accumulate under the ice , where it would remain invisible and may not be recoverable at all .
