<h> University of Alberta receives $24M grant for research equipment <p> The federal government is investing nearly $24 million towards research equipment at the University of Alberta . <p> On Thursday , the government announced $23.8 million in funding is going towards developing projects at the university that lead to ground-breaking results in areas such as adapting forests to climate change , advancing heart failure research and detecting and studying dark matter . <p> " It is an honour to have the opportunity to support such impressive research projects that address major problems in our society today such as heart failure and climate change , " Infrastructure and Communities Minister Amarjeet Sohi said . <p> " I have no doubt that science will push Canada to the forefront of future innovation and discovery . " <p> The university 's Centre for Nanofabrication will be one of the departments that benefits from the grant . <p> Dr. Jillian Buriak , Canada research chair in Nanomaterials for Energy at the U of A , said the grant will support the transition of laboratory scale discoveries into mass manufactured products at the centre @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on high-tech instrumentation allows them to go out into the field to start companies , to work with companies and really bring all this high tech infrastructure knowledge to the Canadian economy , " Buriak said . <p> The U of A funding is part of more than $554 million going towards 117 new infrastructure projects at 61 post-secondary institutions and research hospitals as part of the Canada foundation for innovation Fund . <p> " The funding challenges Canadian universities , colleges and research hospitals to tackle the biggest issues facing us today and take research to the next level , " Sohi said .
