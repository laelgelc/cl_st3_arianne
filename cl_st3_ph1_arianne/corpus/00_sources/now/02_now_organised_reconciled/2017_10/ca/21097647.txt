<h> Help ensure healthy environment is enshrined in charter of rights <p> There is a strong wave crossing our nation asking the Canadian government to accept the recommendations of the Standing Committee on Environment and Sustainable Development following its review of the Canadian Environmental Protection Agency ( CEPA ) . The movement is seeking to amend the Charter of Rights and Freedoms to ensure the right to a healthy environment for all Canadians . The Minister of the Environment and Climate Change , Catherine McKenna , has until Oct. 15 to decide whether to accept the recommendations . <p> Our basic human rights should include the right to breathe clean air , drink fresh water , consume safe food , access nature and know about the pollutants released into the local environment . 110 nations around the world have already recognized these rights for their citizens , and it is time that Canada , as a leading First World country , to adopt these rights as essential for us . <p> Historically the CEPA directs laws regarding toxins and pollutants , but the laws have been poorly enforced .
