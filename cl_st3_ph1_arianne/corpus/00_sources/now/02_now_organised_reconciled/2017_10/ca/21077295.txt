On the flip side , steel is essential to building the infrastructure required in a low-carbon economy -- everything from wind turbines and electric vehicles to mass transit systems . It aligns with Sweden 's overall goal to be the first fossil-free country by 2045 . The project is currently being studied , with research and pilot plant trials not scheduled until 2024 .
