<h> Letter of the Day : The argument of climate change <p> Kim Campbell 's references to " climate deniers " and " climate change " ( SP , Oct. 5 ) sound like something found in a time capsule from when she was Prime Minister . That was when people thought there would be no gasoline-powered cars by 2005 . It was before scientists in the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) unanimously rejected any link between climate change and man-made causes ( 1995 ) . It was before the " hockey stick " graph of global temperature was exposed as an outright fraud ( 2006 ) . It was before Climategate ( 2009 ) . <p> Now it 's different . Now nobody denies that we have a climate , and we know it 's something which has been changing since the world began . Nothing " ridiculous " or " criminal " here . Today 's politicians are kept informed by their own government-funded scientists . They know that none of the " extreme weather events happening in North America this summer " are caused @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the last 20 years : lots of climate change , but no net warming . And not much expectation of any , according to public statements by the IPCC 's leaders , whose description of the current era is " non-warming . " <p> There is a well-funded lobby which wants to keep the " global warming " delusion alive , but it 's on borrowed time .
