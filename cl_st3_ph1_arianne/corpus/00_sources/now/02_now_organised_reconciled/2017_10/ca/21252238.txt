This prevalence is said to be similar to other B.C. and Canadian communities . <p> But Kelowna 's rate of overdoses from illicit drugs , at more than 30 people per 100,000 of population , is higher than Vancouver 's . <p> " The rate of overdose deaths continues to be alarming and very concerning to everyone in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Dr. Trevor Corneil , IH 's chief medical health officer , said earlier this month . <p> In Kelowna , fentanyl was detected in 85 per cent of overdose deaths from January through August .
