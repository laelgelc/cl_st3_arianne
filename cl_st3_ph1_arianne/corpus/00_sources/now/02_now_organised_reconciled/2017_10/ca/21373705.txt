<p> The Polar Prince is a multi-purpose ship orginally built in 1959 in Lauzon , Quebec as a class 100A medium-duty icebreaker for service in the Canadian Arctic as the CCGS Sir Humphrey Gilbert . She was completely rebuilt in the 1980s and promptly retired and sold for scrap . The ship was purchased by a Newfoundland company and mostly sat idle until it was sold to an investor and then modernized in 2009 . In 2010 the Polar Prince was sold to GX Technology Canada , LTD ( division of Ion ) in Calgary , Alberta and finally put back to work in the Arctic . <p> Image Credit : WIKIMEDIA COMMONS/Herb Neufeld
