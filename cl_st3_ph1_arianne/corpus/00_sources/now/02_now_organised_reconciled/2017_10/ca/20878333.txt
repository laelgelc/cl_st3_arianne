<p> A renowned climate change research area near Whitehorse is marking 25 years , and looking to the future . <p> Scientists come from around the world to study and collect data from the Wolf Creek Research Basin . It started as a project focussed on water research , but now it also involves many scientists studying climate change . <p> " It 's a very powerful and important place , and it 's right here in Whitehorse , " said John Pomeroy , a professor of hydrology at the University of Saskatchewan . <p> Pomeroy has been involved with the Wolf Creek project since it began in 1992 . Back then , he said , the goal was to study how water flowed in northern climates . <p> Researchers gathered recently at Wolf Creek to mark 25 years of the research basin project .
