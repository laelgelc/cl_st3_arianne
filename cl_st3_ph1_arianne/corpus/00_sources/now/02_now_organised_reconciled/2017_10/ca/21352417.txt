<h> Carnduff receives federal support <p> The town of Carnduff has received support from the federal government through the new Municipal Asset Management Program ( MAMP ) . <p> Carnduff will receive $22,000 for its development of asset management plans . <p> The announcement was made on Oct. 19 by Amarjeet Sohi , the federal minister of Infrastructure and Communities , and Jenny Gerbasi , the president of the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) . <p> Carnduff is one of 103 communities from across the country to receive support through MAMP and the Municipalities for Climate Innovation Program ( MCIP ) . <p> In a press release for the announcement , it was noted that improving Canada 's infrastructure lays , in large part , in the hands of the municipalities . Communities across the country want to be sure they are investing their infrastructure money wisely , and that they are aware of and ready to adapt to the potential effects of climate change as they make local infrastructure investment decisions . <p> " These new programs will help municipalities to plan , build and maintain their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in infrastructure projects reducing greenhouse gas emissions and better preparing for the impacts of climate change , " said Sohi . " Innovative green infrastructure projects contribute to a clean growth economy and strengthen the middle class by ensuring communities are healthy and sustainable places to live . " <p> " Communities large and small are looking for ways to maximize their infrastructure dollars , plan for , and adapt to the impacts of climate change , and undertake programs that benefit the environment , " added Gerbasi . " Local solutions are helping tackle national challenges -- and are building a more livable , competitive Canada . We are proud of the great work communities are undertaking with the help of these federally funded programs . " <p> The projects announced demonstrate the work being done on these fronts in municipalities large and small . <p> For example , through MCIP , communities are studying how to adapt to coastal flooding and increase community resiliency , or they are working on a climate change adaptation and resilience strategy . <p> MAMP is helping Canadian municipalities make informed decisions on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ example , it could mean integrating new software that will make existing data more useful during decision making processes , or working towards a full-fledged asset management plan to ensure more strategic funding for a town 's basic infrastructure needs . <p> MCIP and MAMP are delivered by the Federation of Canadian Municipalities and funded by the federal government . <h> Comments <h> NOTE : To post a comment you must have an account with at least one of the following services : Disqus , Facebook , Twitter , Google+ You may then login using your account credentials for that service .
