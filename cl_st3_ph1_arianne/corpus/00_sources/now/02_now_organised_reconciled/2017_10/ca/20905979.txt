" It 's living in open areas like prairies and fallow land , agricultural fields , on the side of train railways , underneath power lines . Anywhere we find prairies and open areas , there is a possibility the brown snake is there . <p> " Unfortunately , those are the areas that are targeted for development , " he added . <p> While government conservation efforts often protect mature forests and wetlands , Bourgeois said open areas that are home to the brown snake are left unprotected . <p> " Open areas are disappearing at a very fast rate and so is the brown snake population , unfortunately , " he said .
