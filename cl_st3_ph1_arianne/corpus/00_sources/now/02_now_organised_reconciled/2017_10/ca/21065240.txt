<h> Ontario Repairing and Upgrading Social Housing in Niagara Region <p> Ontario is providing new funding to the Regional Municipality of Niagara to repair and retrofit social housing apartment buildings to be more comfortable and healthier places for people to live , while also making them more energy efficient . <p> Premier Kathleen Wynne was at the Fleming Centre in Beamsville to announce up to $6.9 million in funding over five years for the region 's social housing , contingent on carbon market proceeds . This investment is part of an Ontario-wide program to improve these homes for low-income and vulnerable tenants and to provide the support and opportunity that people need to live their best lives . This investment will also reduce energy bills for social housing providers and reduce greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions by making buildings more energy efficient .
