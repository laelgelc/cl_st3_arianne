In terms of per-capita emissions , we are in the middle of the provincial pack . Our total emissions have ticked slightly upward since 2005 and are up about 12 per cent from 1990 . <p> However , because we are largely hydro-powered , our biggest contributors to emissions are transportation ( about 40 per cent -- mostly stemming from a huge increase in SUV and light-duty truck use ) , agriculture ( about a third , stemming from livestock-related methane and nitrous oxide from soils ) and heating ( less than one-fifth ) . <p> A portion of revenue should be dedicated to building a clean and green economy targeting these sources .
