<p> As the NDP was coming together between 1958 and 1961 , it loosely existed as the New Party . Two candidates ran under the name in a pair of byelections in October 1960 . Walter Pitman , victorious in Peterborough , Ont. , became Parliament 's first and last New Party MP . <p> The following summer , at the party 's founding convention , the word " democratic " was inserted and the NDP was born . <p> Fifty-six years later , the " new " part can seem a bit anachronistic . But it is easy to imagine why someone might look at the world of politics and wish for something new . <p> In Jagmeet Singh , the NDP at least now has a leader who looks and sounds like something new . <p> At 38 , he is the youngest leader in the party 's history ( and 19 years younger than Tom Mulcair was when he became leader in 2012 ) . Though an MPP in Ontario for the last six years , he is a newcomer to Ottawa . <p> He 's comfortable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nice suits . His leadership raises the possibility of an NDP that can compete in the suburbs around Toronto . <p> And , as already well-noted , he is the first non-white leader of a major federal party . He wears a turban and a kirpan . That is history , but it also means a leader who will bring new experiences to the job and new stories to the stage . <p> But , of course , nothing stays new forever .
