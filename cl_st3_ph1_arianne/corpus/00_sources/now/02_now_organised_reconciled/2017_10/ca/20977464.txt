By his controversial and divisive actions , the erratic American leader has unwittingly become a warning for everyone , including Canadians , of the type of leader who should never be entrusted with power . <p> Trump has quickly polarized American society , undermining the stability of that nation . <p> Trump 's divisive policies have not been imposed solely on the American people , but also on other countries , negatively affecting important global trade and environmental matters , including the North American Free Trade agreement among Canada , Mexico and the United States , which Trump insists must be renegotiated in the United States ' favour . <p> Many Canadians would like to believe this country has resisted the siren call of those , like Trump , who have deliberately chosen to pursue policies that polarize society or stigmatize sectors of the population based on their race , religion or other characteristics . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's relatively friction-free environment to an evolving society with an increased openness that supports inclusive policies that advance and ultimately benefit every citizen . <p> Many see this tendency being advanced by a growing number of young people , including the election of younger members of Parliament , plus greater roles for women in governmental decision-making , as promoted by Prime Minister Justin Trudeau . <p> The country 's three major national parties are all headed by younger leaders : the New Democratic Party Leader Jagmeet Singh , 38 , elected days ago ; recently elected Conservative Party Leader Andrew Scheer , also 38 , along with a youngish 45-year-old Trudeau , already completing his first two years in power . <p> Canadian politics could become interesting in coming days as the Liberal government has to confront new leaders of the NDP and Conservatives , who have new or different policies , including on the dangers presented by global climate change . <p> This is especially relevant in British Columbia , where environmental issues are highly sensitive , especially to younger voters who supported Trudeau for his stated commitment to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ controversial pipeline projects . <p> Although Trudeau 's " sunny ways " approach to politics has been remarkably successful in maintaining his popularity , his failure to meet the pent-up expectations of Canada 's Aboriginal population , including younger people , is beginning to be a concern . Some chiefs have even boycotted meetings with the Trudeau government . <p> The Trudeau government 's proposed changes to income-tax legislation for self-employed individuals has been widely criticized , as has its lack of progress in controlling the massive government debt . While it 's too early to say what this will mean for the Conservatives and NDP , both hope to realize some gains . <p> Paradoxically , the Conservatives view Jagmeet Singh 's election as a useful development from their perspective , as it could help Conservative candidates in the 2019 election , since the NDP under its new charismatic leader might take away votes from the Liberals . <p> However , while the NDP could gain votes in the next election , it will have a tough time increasing support in vote-rich Quebec , where the party is weak @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in politics , and Singh is a turban-wearing Sikh . <p> Many view the remarkable political achievement of the photogenic Singh in rising to lead a major Canadian political party as a welcome development for this country 's democratic system . <p> However , the verdict on what he has accomplished for Canadian society remains to be demonstrated in coming days .
