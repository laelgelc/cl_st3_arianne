<h> A Parkway briefing for Premier Kathleen Wynne <p> In short order , you will be receiving a request to meet with Mayor Daryl Bennett and senior staff from the City of Peterborough . They want to advance the merits of a proposed arterial road in their city known as the Parkway . You apparently opened the door to such a meeting , Premier , with an extemporaneous suggestion made to the mayor this summer that he was welcome to bring his expressway concerns forward . <p> This file has a long history . Land was set aside for a cross-town Peterborough roadway as far back as 1947 , although only a small portion of it has been built . This ribbon of public land has since become a green recreational asset that is highly valued by bordering neighbours . In 2013 , city council approved an environmental assessment ( EA ) for the roadway by a vote of 8 to 3 . The plan includes a bridge through the 49-hectare Jackson Park and this bridge has been a flashpoint of continuing controversy . This has led the city to conclude that Minister Murray 's decision for more detailed EA work was political . That conclusion appears to be a key motivating factor for the meeting request , although it must be noted that Peterborough city council itself has taken decisions contrary to the advice of its own staff .
