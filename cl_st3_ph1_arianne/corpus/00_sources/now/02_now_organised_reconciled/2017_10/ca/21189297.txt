<p> Environmentalists who rejoiced at the death of Energy East , the pipeline intended to ship western oil to Atlantic Canada , seem to have nothing to say about Monday 's bombshell deal that saw Airbus acquire ( for no cash or debt ) a major stake in Bombardier 's C Series . It also seems that the anti-oil activists have yet to weigh in on the U.S. Commerce department 's decision to impose tariffs up to 300 per cent on Bombardier C Series jets that ostensibly prompted the sale , which allows the Quebec-based aerospace manufacturer to relocate manufacturing inside Airbus 's U.S. facilities and potentially elude those tariffs while pumping out more C Series planes for the American market . <p> Media reports in the wake of TransCanada 's decision to give up on its plans for a west-east pipeline showed opponents gleefully crossing Energy East off a sign listing various projects they 've been targeting for defeat .
