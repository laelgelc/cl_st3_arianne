<h> Extreme weather crisis to continue if no action taken to address climate change -- Mohammed <p> The UN Deputy Secretary-General , Amina Mohammed , on Tuesday warned that extreme weather crisis will continue to happen if actions are not taken to meet climate change challenges . <p> Mohammed gave the warning at the opening ceremony of the the 23rd Session of the Conference of the Parties to the UN Convention on Climate Change in Denarau , an island near Fiji 's third largest city of Nadi . <p> She said : " Extreme weather events show in graphic detail the risks we all face if ambition , action and commitment are not raised to meet the challenges of climate change . " <p> She said that they highlight the urgency of building resilience , investing in adaptation and bending the emissions curve by 2020 . <p> Mohammed stated : " For the UN , these events also underline the need to press forward , in line with the mandate from member states , on the UN secretary-general 's reforms to reposition the UN Development System to better support countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Development . <p> " These include the Sustainable Development Goals , the Paris Agreement on Climate Change , and the Sendai Framework for Disaster Risk Reduction , all landmark agreements reached in 2015 , she said , adding that implementation of the three must go together . <p> " She urged governments , businesses and citizens to work together and do their duties to fight climate change . " <p> She said that to deliver on its full potential , and assist governments and society to go further , faster together , it needs a complete and uncluttered set of guidelines that ensure collective responsibility for the achievement of the long term objective of the agreement . <p> The deputy secretary-general said these guidelines should ensure that the agreement fosters transparency on action and support , how parties will take stock of the global situation and how mechanism to facilitate implementation and promote compliance will operate . <p> She also stated : " We have the agreement . Now we need to raise ambition . 2020 should mark a turning point in the global economy , especially in key sectors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ states to bend the emissions curve and scale up efforts to build resilience so that this turning point can indeed be reached . <p> " As we look towards Bonn and beyond , this Pre-COP is a crucial planning opportunity . Besides the guidelines , the COP should agree on a package of modalities to convene Facilitative Dialogue aimed at assessing and ramping up ambition . " <p> She noted that there should be no doubt that " we are entering a new chapter on climate change " and " Our task is to ensure that it is adequate to the challenge " . <p> A total of 300 delegates from over 60 countries and regions , international institutions , civil society organisations and business leaders are gathering here to attend the two-day conference , namely Pre-COP 23 , which officially started on Tuesday .
