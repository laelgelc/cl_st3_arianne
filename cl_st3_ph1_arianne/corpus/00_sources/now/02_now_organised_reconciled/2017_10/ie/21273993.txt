<h> Kingspan Retains Position on CDP Climate ' A List ' <h> Kingspan has been named for the third year in a row on The Climate ' A List ' by CDP , the international not-for-profit that measures the environmental impact of thousands of companies around the world . Kingspan is one of only two Irish companies to make the list of 112 companies , which includes Unilever , Microsoft , and Toyota . <p> Featuring on the ' A List ' recognises Kingspan 's actions in the last reporting year to cut emissions , mitigate climate risks and develop the low-carbon economy . In 2016 Kingspan passed the half way mark along its path to Net Zero Energy ( NZE ) by 2020 , with renewable energy accounting for 57% of its total energy use .
