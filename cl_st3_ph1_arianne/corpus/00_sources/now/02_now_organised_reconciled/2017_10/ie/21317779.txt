<p> Labour Party Representative* in Cork Peter Horgan has urged private property owners to check the status of trees on their property following Storms Ophelia and Brian . <p> Peter Horgan ( Labour ) <p> " The work of the emergency services and City Council staff in responding and clearing up tree incidents was second to none , " said Mr Horgan . <p> " However , as we take stock of the aftermath and look at tree stocks on public land , private owners must be encouraged to assess their own land . Many private residences have large conurbations of trees that overlook properties and could cause untold damage of they were to fall . " <p> Mr Horgan suggested a once off tree topping amnesty be explored with Central Government to secure all tree stock in the city and County . <p> " Government has said that money will not be an issue . The aftermath of these storms is not Int to clear up the damage caused but to reinforce ourselves against future damage . Many homeowners are slow to tackle high trees in their gardens so @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ behalf of City Council would assess them ? It is worth having the conversation and the cost benefit would be minimal , compared to what would happen should some of these trees fall . " <p> " Climate Change is here to stay and storms like we experience in the last fortnight are going to become more regular . We need imaginative solutions and proposals to ensure that climate change in Cork is managed correctly and safely . " he concluded . <p> * A Party Representative is a ' Councillor in waiting ' . They are person appointed by a political party to represent an area . Most parties will put their Reps forward for election to County or City Council at the next opportunity .
