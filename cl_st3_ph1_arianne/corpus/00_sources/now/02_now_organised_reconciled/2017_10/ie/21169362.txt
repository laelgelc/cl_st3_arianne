<h> Years after the day after <p> On the night of November 20 , 1983 , US network ABC aired a made-for-TV film entitled ' The Day After ' . By some estimates , around 100 million people sat down to watch the drama about the build-up-to and the immediate aftermath of an all-out nuclear war between the US and the USSR . It must have made it to RT ? sometime shortly afterwards , as I vividly recall watching it and being transfixed by the terrifying transformation of the familiar into the ruined , order into chaos , and the overwhelming feeling of individual helplessness in the face of such an unspeakable calamity . <p> I was not alone .
