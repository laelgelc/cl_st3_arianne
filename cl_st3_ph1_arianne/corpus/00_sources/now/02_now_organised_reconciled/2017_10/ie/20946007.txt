<h> Renault aiming to make half of its cars electric or hybrid in five years <p> French car giant Renault has said half of its models will be electric or hybrid by 2022 and it is investing heavily in " robo-vehicles " with increasing degrees of autonomy . <p> A strategic plan released on Friday aims to boost Renault annual revenues to ? 70bn by 2022 from ? 51bn last year , in part through an effort to double sales outside its traditional markets in Europe - especially Russia and China . <p> The plans reflect the vision laid out last month by the Renault Nissan Mitsubishi alliance , the world 's leading car-maker by sales . Many of Renault 's new aims depend on saving money through sharing platforms and development with Nissan and Mitsubishi . <p> Chief executive Carlos Ghosn said Renault is aiming to sell more than five million vehicles annually by 2022 , up from 3.2 last year . <p> As regulators crack down on emissions from combustion engines and as drivers seek cars that can do more by themselves , Mr Ghosn wants to position @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cars . <p> " We are confident we can turn upcoming ... challenges into significant business opportunities for Renault , " he said . <p> The company pledges to offer eight purely electric vehicle models and 12 hybrid models by 2022 , compared with its 19 diesel or petrol models sold worldwide , he added . <p> The announcements come as the world 's major car-makers are rethinking their strategies to profit from pivotal changes in the industry : autonomous cars , connected cars that share data , car-sharing where people do not own a vehicle but order one by app , and low-emissions vehicles demanded by the European Union to fight climate change and by China , where many cities are fighting rampant pollution . <p> Renault is aiming to produce two million cars per year outside Europe compared with 750,000 cars in 2016 , with a heavy push in Russia . <p> Overall , Mr Ghosn said Renault would retrain 13,000 people over the next five years to adapt to changing markets .
