<p> BOB Geldof has lashed into a number of world leaders , saying he is " sick of them " . <p> At the opening ceremony of the One Young World Summit in Bogota , Colombia , the Dublin star turned his particular attention to State Counsellor Aung San Suu Kyi over her government 's treatment of the Rohingya minority in Burma , also known as Myanmar . <p> Referring to the Nobel Peace Laureates sitting on stage behind him , he said : " On this stage are some of the greatest people who have fought for justice and peace and equality in our world . <p> " And one of their number in Myanmar insults them -- insults them all . Who took the greatest prize that humans can give to another and then becomes one of the great ethnic cleansers of our planet . This is a disgrace . " <p> Ms Suu Kyi , who won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1991 , spent a total of 15 years under house arrest when in opposition . <p> However , she has drawn widespread criticism for her @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , which the UN human rights chief , Zeid Ra'ad Al Hussein , has called " a textbook example of ethnic cleansing " . <p> Bob was joined on stage by counsellors from the One Young World Conference , including former secretary-general of the UN Kofi Annan , and Colombia 's president Juan Manuel Santos , to open the three-day summit . <p> The summit , in its seventh year , brings together notable young people from 194 countries to discuss issues including climate change , conflict resolution and economic development . <p> Bob did give praise to President Santos and his efforts to bring peace to Colombia . <p> He said the nation has " found a man who could put away the ancient enmities , the endless killing , where people could put aside the past and say there is something better than death , than torture , than kidnapping . There is something better -- peace . "
