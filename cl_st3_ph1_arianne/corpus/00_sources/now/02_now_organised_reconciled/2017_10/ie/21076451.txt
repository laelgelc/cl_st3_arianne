<h> Legendary singer songwriter Luka Bloom to perform at The Dock <p> Reporter : <p> News Reporter <p> 13 Oct 2017 <p> 0 <p> Luke Bloom will perform at The Dock , Carrick-on-Shannon on October 19 . <p> With a poet 's heart and a rockstar 's soul , Luka Bloom is regarded as one of Ireland 's most respected contemporary folk artists , having produced over 20 albums since the 1970s , Bloom continues to push the boundaries of what his music can do . <p> A master interpreter , Bloom has continued to balance original songs with reinventions of tunes by a diverse range of songwriters . <p> Like many who write songs , Luka 's orchestra of choice is the guitar . What makes his career a little different is his constant search for a new voice within the guitar . <p> The guitar is the landscape on which the song is created , and he is forever probing that landscape for new inspiration .
