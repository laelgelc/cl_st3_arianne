No sooner had I started swanking around at work , casually dropping news of the impending trip to colleagues , when another email arrived from Tokyo . <p> It came from the conference organisers and contained two chilling words : " PowerPoint presentation " . Every speaker was expected to make one , it turned out , and the organisers were keen to see mine . <p> Happy thoughts of sashimi and sake instantly gave way to panic .
