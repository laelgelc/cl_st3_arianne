Mountaineer , patriot , architect and author , O'Brien was a pioneer of international ocean sailing . His voyage from 1923 to 1925 navigating south of the great headlands of Good Hope and Cape Horn in the 42ft ketch Saoirse , set new standards of seagoing competence for amateur sailors . He was the first to sail a small boat around the world carrying the tricolour . He smuggled guns for the 1916 Easter Rising in the Kelpie and was decorated in the British Navy in World War 2 .
