<h> Ophelia warns us to act on climate <p> Hurricane Ophelia represents the latest severe weather warning in Ireland in recent times , the impacts of which have been quite devastating in large parts of the country , notably along the western seaboard and in the flood plains along the River Shannon . At this stage , meteorological experts are uncertain as to when and how Ophelia will hit , but there is strong evidence from weather forecast models that its remnants will track close to or even over parts of Ireland tomorrow and Tuesday .
