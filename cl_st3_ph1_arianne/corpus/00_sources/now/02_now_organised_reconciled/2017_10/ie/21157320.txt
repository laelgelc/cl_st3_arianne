Cleopatra ruled during this time and cities like Alexandria prospered . This success , however , was linked to the river Nile and the flooding which supported the region 's agriculture . The study found that large volcanic eruptions interrupted African summer monsoons , reducing the flow of the Nile and leading to economic and political instability in Egypt . <p> The research into the period has provided better insight into climate change and its impact on society as a whole . The research shows how societies responded to climate shocks in the short term .
