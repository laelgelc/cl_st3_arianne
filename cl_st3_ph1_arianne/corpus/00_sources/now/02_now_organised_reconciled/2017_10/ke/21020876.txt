<p> Kenya sets aside U.S. $7.6 million dollars to mitigate drought in northern region <p> NAIROBI ( Xinhua ) --Kenya has set aside approximately 7.6 million U.S. dollars to mitigate the ongoing drought in the northern region . <p> Cabinet Secretary in the Ministry of Water and Irrigation Eugene Wamalwa said Monday the funds will be utilized from October to December to support 23 affected counties to improve their water supplies . <p> ? The government hopes to use the finance to procure and distribute collapsible tanks as well as drill and rehabilitate boreholes for increased water supplies , ? Wamalwa said when he flagged off 20 water browzers headed to Northern Kenya counties under the drought mitigation program . <p> The water browzers are aimed at enhancing access to clean and safe water for pastoralists during the current drought period . <p> Government data indicated that 27 million Kenyans can access safe , clean , drinking water within short distances as the current national access rate to water services stands at 60 percent with urban areas at 68.3 percent while rural areas at 51 percent . <p> Wamalwa noted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the current funding to the water sector is inadequate . <p> He said that in order to deal with the high deficit in access to water , the government and development partners are implementing projects all over the country to ensure access to clean water in line with the constitutional requirements . <p> Wamalwa added that the newly enacted Water Act 2016 has ushered in the much needed water sector reforms which will see the country have a National Water Policy . <p> He lamented that drought as well as climate change continues to have adverse socio-economic effects on the lives of Kenyans.
