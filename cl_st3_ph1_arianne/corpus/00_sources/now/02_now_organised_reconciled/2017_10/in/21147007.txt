<h> Rice pelleting ' technique to help tackle climate change <p> KOZHIKODE : An innovative rice farming method developed by a farmer in Wayanad is increasingly being adopted by farmers across the state , which has seen a steady decline in rice cultivation over the years , as an answer to tackle climate change concerns and production constraints . <p> The ' rice pellet ' farming developed by Aji Thomas Kunnel , a farmer hailing from Ambalavayal in Wayanad has already been implemented in over 200 acres in the state . Kerala State Council for Science , Technology and Environment ( KSCSTE ) is funding the process to mechanise the seed pellet production to meet the growing demand for pelletized rice seeds . <p> Experts at Kerala Agriculture University ( KAU ) has said that farmers who adopted the method have been able to double their rice yield to around five tonnes per hectare as opposed to the normal yield of 2.5 tonnes . <p> Aji Thomas came up with the innovative method after 10 years of experimentation . The 45-year-old farmer says rice seeds are first inserted into an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Panchagavya and Jeevamrutham in addition to extracts from leaves of various trees . Also , bio-agents like Pseudomonas and Azospirillum are used if needed . <p> The mixture along with rice seeds are filled in a rubber mould with 64 holes to make pellets . The mould is removed and the pellets are dried in sun for 12 hours . The pellets are then collected in a heap and sprinkled with water following which the seeds germinate in 72 hours . Around 64,000 pellets , each one inch in diameter , are needed for sowing in an acre of land . The pellets are sown manually in rows separated at a distance of 25 cm . <p> Aji said that the rice plants emerging from the pellet have substantially high number of shoots ( tillers ) when compared to the normal direct sowing or transplantation methods and thereby has the potential for more grain from the shoots .
