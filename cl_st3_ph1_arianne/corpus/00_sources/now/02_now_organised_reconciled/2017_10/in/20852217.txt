<h> National Conference on Climate Change and Sustainable Development : Perspectives and Transformational Challenges <p> Department of Law , University Of Jammu And CEERA , National Law School of India University , Bengaluru has jointly organized National Conference on Climate Change and Sustainable Development : Perspectives and Transformational Challenges . <p> The proposed Two-day National Conference expects threadbare deliberations on the cross-cutting theme of Climate Change and Sustainable development and the transformational challenges for the integration of climate change policy into national sustainable development strategy .
