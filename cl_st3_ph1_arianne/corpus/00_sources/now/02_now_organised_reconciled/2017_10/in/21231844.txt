<h> NZP hosts solo exhibition of Wildlife Photographs <h> Students during the solo exhibition of Wildlife Photographs by Onenjungshi Ao hosted by Nagaland Zoological Park on October 20 . <p> Dimapur , October 21 ( MExN ) : Nagaland Zoological Park ( NZP ) hosted a solo exhibition of Wildlife Photographs by Onenjungshi Ao , an upcoming Local Wildlife Photographer who is also working as a Photographer in the Chief Wildlife Warden 's Office under Department of Environment , Forest &amp; Climate Change , Government of Nagaland . <p> NZP , Officer-in-Charge and also Asst . Conservator of Forest Obed Bohovi Swu in a press release stated that the exhibition held on October 21 included many important bird species of our region . Till date Onenjungshi has documented about 20 species of free ranging birds within Nagaland Zoological Park . Photographs of captive wildlife of the park captured by Onenjungshi were also exhibited . <p> The main objective of the exhibition was to create awareness about India 's rich biodiversity , its importance to environment , its role in ecology and our role in conservation .
