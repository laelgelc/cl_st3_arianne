<h> President Kovind advises Indians in Ethiopia to connect with the youth of the host country <p> Addis Ababa/New Delhi , Oct 5 ( IBNS ) : President of India Ram Nath Kovind , who arrived in Ethiopia on Wednesday , addressed an Indian community reception in Addis Ababa hosted by the Ambassador of India to Ethiopia , Anurag Srivastava . <p> Speaking on the occasion , the President said Indian community in Ethiopia has been at the centre of India-Ethiopia relations . <p> The President said as teachers and educators , the Indian community have given a helping hand to nation building in their host country . As entrepreneurs , they have created economic opportunities and imparted skills to local people . As tech-professionals and workers they have added value to Ethiopian industry . <p> With hard work and dedication , the President said , the Indian community has built respect for itself within Ethiopian society . And yet it has retained and carried forward Indian values , family traditions and work ethic while adapting very well to this adopted home . <p> The President said that Ethiopia , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ multiple languages and varied cuisines ; of music , dance and drama . The Indian community here must take advantage of the local eclectic culture as well as showcase and share our own cultural diversity . <p> The President said both India and Ethiopia have large young populations . The youth are the future , and it is from them that new ideas would come . <p> He advised the Indian community to make special efforts to connect with Ethiopia 's youth . <p> He stated that this would help them germinate their ideas to provide solutions for a better world , be it tacking climate change or skilling people . <p> The President said the Government of India seeks sustained and proactive engagement with the overseas community . The purpose of this engagement is to provide an opportunity to familiarise the community with transformational changes taking place in India . <p> The President said the Government of India cares for the overseas Indian community and keeps its promise to stand by them .
