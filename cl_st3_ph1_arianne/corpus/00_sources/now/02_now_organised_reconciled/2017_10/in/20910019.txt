<h> Three ISRO satellites celebrated their birth anniversaries in the last week of September <p> In the last week of September , three ISRO satellites celebrated their birthdays . On 24 September , India 's first interplanetary spacecraft , the Mars Orbiter Mission celebrated three years in orbit , well beyond the planned mission duration of six months . On 26 September , the weather forecasting satellite ScatSat-1 completed a year in orbit . On 28 September , AstroSat , the first dedicated astronomy mission by India celebrated two years in orbit . <p> Mangalyaan . The ScatSat-1 was the primary payload on the PSLV-C35 mission which deployed 8 satellites into orbit in September 2016 . <p> The AstroSat mission was launched in September 2015 on board the PSLV-C30 mission , sharing the ride with six co-passengers .
