<p> For many years in the late 80s and early 90s , I used to work at our family textiles business . The factory was in Ankleshwar , an industrial estate about an hour 's train ride from my home in Surat . I would usually go to the factory in the afternoon , and return in the late evening . The business did not do that well and after the government liberalised India 's economy in 1991 , we could not compete and shut it down a few years later . <p> The factory put polyester yarn through a process called texturising which made the plasticky yarn more wearable .
