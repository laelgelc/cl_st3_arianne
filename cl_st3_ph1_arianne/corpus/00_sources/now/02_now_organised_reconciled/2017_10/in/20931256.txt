<h> Mizoram has highest number of clouded leopards in S E Asia <p> Aizawl , Oct 5 ( PTI ) Mizorams Dampa Tiger Reserve now holds the distinction of housing the highest number of clouded leopards in Southeast Asia , the state government claimed today . <p> The density of population of clouded leopards , locally knows as Kelral , is 5.14 per 100 sq km in the reserve , situated along the **26;761;TOOLONG , it said in a release . <p> The area of the reserve is around 80 sq km , the release said , adding , the density of population of marbled cats , also known as Sanghar , is 5.03 per 100 sq km . <p> The states Environment , Forests and Climate Change Department placed several camera traps at different places across the Dampa Tiger Reserve , which captured the clouded leopards 84 times and the marbled cats 36 times , it added .
