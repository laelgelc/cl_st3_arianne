<h> Appointments Committee of The Cabinet Reshuffles Bureaucrats In Ministries Ahead of Budget <h> Appointments Committee of The Cabinet Reshuffles Bureaucrats <p> Cabinet Committee on Appointments reshuffled the portfolios of many secretaries in the government . File Image <p> New Delhi , October 12 : The Cabinet Committee on Appointments on Wednesday made significant changes in the administration of various ministries . The committee appointed 11 Secretaries to government departments . The reshuffle comes ahead of Budget 2018 in February next year . <p> Senior IAS officer Ajay Narayan Jha will assume charge of the post of Secretary , Department of Expenditure on superannuation of Ashok Lavasa . Jha was serving as the Secretary of Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change as Officer on Special Duty . Similarly , Injeti Srinivas was appointed as Secretary of Ministry of Corporate Affairs .
