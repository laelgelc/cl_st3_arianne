<h> CHALLENGE TO POLITICS , FAITH <h> Rejuvenation of Rivers <h> By Dr S Saraswathi <p> The Union Government has plans to clean all rivers in the country -- a task urgently required , but most difficult to accomplish . Expectations are high from new Minister for Water Resources , River Development , and Ganga Rejuvenation Nitin Gadkari , who seems to be keen on taking up a challenging task . Adopting the Ganga Law and starting five river-linking projects that are in the pipeline are among his priorities . <p> Cleaning rivers is part of rejuvenation of rivers which has been going on all over the world in very small to long and deep rivers in all continents . Water scarcity and environmental degradation have drawn the attention of governments , NGOs , and general public to the urgency of restoring and cleaning water resources that nature has bestowed . <p> There are some popular leaders and voluntary organisations engaged in creating awareness and initiating popular schemes for restoring dwindling water resources in our country which are indeed our lifeline . The common man facing acute water problem in all States is inclined to believe these figures and does not look for verification . It is feared that very soon , perennial waters will become seasonal . <p> Remember , a recent verdict pronounced by the Nainital Court in Uttarakhand granted to the Ganges and Yamuna the same legal rights as enjoyed by human beings with all corresponding rights , duties , and liabilities . How they can be enforced and who is the custodian of the right to protect them are , however , still vague . <p> This verdict is doubtless one of far-reaching significance in many respects in the context of the pathetic condition of many rivers including the holiest of the holy . In 2011 , Bolivia enacted the Law of Mother Earth . <p> The situation in India definitely can not correct itself by natural cycles since human activities are day by day adding to global warming and climate change which will only worsen conditions . Faith-related polluting activities and political rivalry in sharing water add ammunition to people 's ignorance and pose a combined challenge to the very future of rivers . <p> Relevant in this connection is a silent movement against dam construction in some places . This is indeed unbelievable in India where dam wars are common between States fighting for share of water . <p> France and Canada have also removed dams . It has a highly centralised system of water governance to manage the needs of different fields of activity .
