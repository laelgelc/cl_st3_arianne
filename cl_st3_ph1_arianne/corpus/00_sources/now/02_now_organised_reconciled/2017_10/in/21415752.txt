<p> The Stone Age was a broad prehistoric period during which stone was widely used to make tools . Throughout the Paleolithic period ( old stone age ) that is believed to have occurred sometime before 3.3 million years ago , humans were food gatherers , depending for their survival on hunting wild animals and birds , fishing , and collecting wild fruits , nuts , and berries ; now being referred to as Hunter-Gatherer ( HG ) lifestyle . <p> Paleolithic people lived in small clans , or groups . They were nomadic hunters and gatherers , and sheltered in caves . They used fire and fashioned stone tools . There was food scarcity and adaption to starvation was essential for survival . Researchers conclude that among the several hundred hunter-gatherer societies that existed , the energy was obtained mostly from animal foods ( 70% ) and little from plant foods ( 30% ) . The HG diet consisted of mammals , birds , reptiles , insects , marine species ; everything edible on the animal carcass was eaten . Food was consumed immediately by gorging and wastage was rare . These hunter gatherers would guzzle 2 to 3 kg of animal after successful hunt . They would also gorge on honey , eggs , insects or fat . The plant food they consumed consisted of uncultivated plants and honey which was rich in fiber , calcium , magnesium and potassium , but low in sodium having low energy-density and low glycemic index . <p> Nutrient analysis of Paleolithic diet reveals it to be a low glycemic load diet containing lean protein , high fiber , high vitamins , high minerals , high anti-oxidants , low salt but no refined grains and sugars . An atherogenic diet is one that promotes atheromas , which are the hallmark of cardiovascular disease ( CVD ) called atherosclerosis ; a primary risk factor for heart attack and stroke . How could the meat in HG diets be cardioprotective and not atherogenic is because the meat would come from the flesh of wild game which contained about 2-4% fat only with high levels of mono and omega-3 fats . Omega-3 fatty acids , as we know are anti-inflammatory and reduce CVD risk by 30-50% . It may be pertinent to mention here that the meat that we consume nowadays comes from domesticated animals , containing 20-25% fat ( mostly saturated fat ) and low in omega-3 fat . <p> Now what about physical activity of Hunter-Gatherers . About 3 to 5 hours per day would be spent in sustained activity that included digging , walking briskly , and chopping with stone axe . The non-communicable diseases ( cancer , CVD , diabetes , obesity , hypertension ) were rare to non-existent . There was apparent absence of stroke and heart attacks . Life was short , death rates were high and main causes of death included accidents , food shortage , predation , and parasitic disease . <p> Then came the agriculture revolution that changed the human 's life . Agriculture is thought to have offered one principle advantage ; providing a greater amount of food per unit area of land which could feed a larger population . The origin of Agriculture is believed to have started with early river valley civilizations like the " fertile " lands of Mesopotamia , Egypt , and Phoenicia . Along with this man started domestication of animals possibly because of climate change , decline in big game population , and increase in human population . With Agricultural diet , there was relative abundance of food , but with cycles of feast and famine . The cultivated crops would be mainly cereals while as domesticated animals provided meat , and milk .
