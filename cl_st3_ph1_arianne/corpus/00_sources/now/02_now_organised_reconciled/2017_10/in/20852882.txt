Social wasps are essential pest-controllers and pollinators , but some species are declining while others are expanding their populations and range . Without basic data on the abundance and distribution of these wasps , we ca n't conserve ( or control ) them . <p> Yet we know relatively little about social wasps in Britain . So we asked the public to set out beer-filled traps for a short period of time when mostly old and soon-to-die worker wasps would be active . This approach would provide essential data that we need to manage social wasp populations .
