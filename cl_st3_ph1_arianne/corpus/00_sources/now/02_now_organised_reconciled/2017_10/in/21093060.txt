Some of these theories were -- ' Meat-eaters are more selfish and less social than vegetarians ' , Trash-filled environment tended to bring out racist tendencies ' , etc . The burning issue was the fate of doctoral and post-doctoral students who had taken such fabricated documents as reference materials . Such practices adversely affect not only the researchers but also the surrounding people . <p> Another global crisis which happens across disciplines is the concept of paid authorship . It encompasses open access , open data , open lab notes , open research tools and optimal usage of scientific resources .
