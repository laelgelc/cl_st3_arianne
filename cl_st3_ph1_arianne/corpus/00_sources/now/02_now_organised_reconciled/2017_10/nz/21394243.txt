We are meat and dairy food producers of some of the highest quality commodities in the world . We need to examine the root causes of the farm issues we face and make positive changes to the way we farm . <p> Traditional family farming with increasing and more costly chemical and energy inputs has failed to deliver for sheep , beef and dairy farmers .
