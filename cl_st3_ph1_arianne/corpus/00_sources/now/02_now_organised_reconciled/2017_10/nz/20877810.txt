<h> Support for climate change commission <p> The country needs to tackle climate change with a special body to advise and lead policy action , including a comprehensive emissions trading scheme , the head of a top power company says . <p> The chief executive of Contact Energy says a Climate Change Commission would help the transition to a low carbon economy . Photo : RNZ <p> Contact Energy chief executive Dennis Barnes said New Zealand needs to take the politics out of the climate change debate and move on beyond talking about technicalities . <p> He said action would be more effective with the establishment of a Climate Change Commission , which would provide expert , independent and objective analysis and advice , and help the transition to a low carbon economy . <p> The suggestions are part of the company 's submissions to the Productivity Commission 's inquiry into a low carbon economy . <p> " What we think would help this along would be an emissions trading scheme to cover all sectors , " Mr Barnes said . <p> " I think the transition to a lower @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in behaviour .
