<h> Steep rise in greenhouse gas emissions <p> The report details that New Zealand 's gross greenhouse gas emissions rose 24 per cent from 1990 to 2015 , with most of the increase having occurred by 2005 . The main activities responsible for the highest number and greatest growth in greenhouse gas emissions are agriculture , energy generation and transport . <p> Agriculture made up almost half our emissions ( 48 per cent ) in 2015 , reflecting the important role of the agriculture sector in New Zealand 's economy . Road transport , while responsible for 18 per cent of emissions , is increasing rapidly , with a 78 per cent increase in emissions since 1990 . <p> Hard questions were put to the panel about land use and transport and what they , as individuals , were prepared to sacrifice to reduce emissions .
