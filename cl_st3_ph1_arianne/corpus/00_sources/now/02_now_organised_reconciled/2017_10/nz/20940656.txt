<h> Niue marine protection area : New Zealand challenged to ' step up ' <p> New Zealand has been challenged to " step up " our marine protection , after the South Pacific country of Niue announced the creation of a large-scale marine protected area . <p> Once established , the new marine protected area will include 40 percent of its Exclusive Economic Zone and be the 28th largest in the world . <p> Niue will be one of only three nations that are fully protecting well over 30 percent of their ocean territories , which is what scientists recommend to keep oceans healthy and resilient to climate change . <p> Environmental groups WWF-New Zealand , Forest &amp; Bird and The Pew Charitable Trusts are welcoming the announcement , and call on New Zealand to " step up to the plate on marine protection " . <p> " We Kiwis love our oceans , and we want to protect them .
