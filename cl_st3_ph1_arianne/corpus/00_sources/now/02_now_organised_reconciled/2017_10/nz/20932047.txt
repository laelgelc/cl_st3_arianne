" <p> The Climate Commission would have an independent board and access to the best expertise we can muster . It would be an agency of Parliament , like the Commissioner for the Environment or the Audit Office . <p> It would prepare a low carbon plan , setting out the transition pathway to net zero . It would report to Parliament in an open , transparent fashion on clear milestones . <p> It would not have decision-making powers : those decisions must remain the prerogative of Ministers . But it would recommend - and Ministers would be obliged to explain any departure from those recommendations . <p> NZ First , Labour and the Greens have all explicitly endorsed this approach .
