<h> ACT leader offers advice on baubles <p> ACT Leader David Seymour is offering other parties some helpful advice on baubles : slash them . <p> " Winston Peters campaigned on reducing the number of MPs in parliament , but he has got ahead of himself . MPs are there to hold the Ministers and their departments to account . If we want more accountability we should start by slashing the number of Ministers , not the number of MPs holding them accountable , " says Mr Seymour . <p> " There 's 60-odd opposition MPs holding 28 Ministers , 40,000 bureaucrats , and a quarter-million government employees accountable . If you want to reduce Government , you 'd have to be gaga to start by cutting the opposition MPs . <p> " On the other hand , there are 28 Ministers with 76 portfolios and most of them are redundant . <p> " I have shown ( below ) how we could get down to only 35 portfolios held by 20 Ministers in Cabinet .
