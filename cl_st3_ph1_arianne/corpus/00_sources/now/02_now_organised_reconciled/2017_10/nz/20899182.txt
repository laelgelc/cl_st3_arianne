<h> Video captures massive pack of polar bears mauling dead whale <p> More than 200 polar bears were seen by a Kiwi-led expedition off the north-eastern coast of Russia . Credits : Supplied <p> Remarkable video has been captured of more than 200 polar bears eating a dead bowhead whale off the north-eastern coast of Russia during a Kiwi-led expedition . <p> Rodney Russ , the owner and founder of Heritage Expeditions , told Newshub the expedition had just completed the first-ever double transit of the northern sea route across the top of Russia , when they saw the bears in their natural habitat . <p> " As we were cruising down the coast , somebody said to me ' there is a flock of sheep ' , " Mr Russ said . " I said impossible , impossible - there are no sheep on this island . <p> " We got binoculars out and we realised then it was polar bear . " <p> Polar bears on Wrangel Island . " <p> The bears had been mistaken for a flock of sheep .
