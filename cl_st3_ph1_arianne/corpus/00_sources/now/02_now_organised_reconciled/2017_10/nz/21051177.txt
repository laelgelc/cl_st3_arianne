<h> American Samoa joins US climate change Alliance <p> American Samoa 's governor Lolo Moliga has pledged support for the intent of the Paris Agreement on climate change and is backing the United States Climate Alliance . <p> Lolo Matalasi Moliga at his State of the Territory address .
