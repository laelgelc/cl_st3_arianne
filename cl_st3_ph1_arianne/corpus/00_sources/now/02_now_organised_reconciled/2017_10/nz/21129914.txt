<h> Portugal wildfires kill dozens <p> At least 35 people have died as firefighters battle more than 145 fires in Portugal , and Spain . <p> Firefighters tackle a wildfire in Macao , a municipality in central Portugal . Photo : AFP <p> Portugal , still recovering from its deadliest forest fire on record in June , bore the brunt of the latest death toll - 32 , with the possibility of rising further , according to the authorities . <p> Authorities said they were still battling 145 blazes in Portugal while another 100 fires were raging in Spainish region Galicia . <p> The weekend 's fires had also injured 56 people in Portugal , civil protection service spokeswoman Patricia Gaspar said . The toll could still increase as seven people were unaccounted for . <p> Portugal 's government asked for international help and declared a state of emergency in territory north of the Tagus river - about half of its landmass . <p> " We are facing new ( weather ) conditions ... They could face up to 20 years in jail if convicted , police said . At least one person was arrested in Portugal for allegedly starting a fire .
