<h> Oil Free Wellington supporting blockade of Weapons Expo <p> Groups from all over the country are gathering to shut down the Expo , held this year at the Wellington Westpac Stadium . Members of Oil Free Wellington will join the protest . <p> Oil Free Wellington spokesperson Frances Mountier says , " we are supporting the blockade because peace issues and climate justice are intertwined . If we are to achieve a just and sustainable world there is no room for the weapons industry . You can not have a just world where people make money out of the deaths of others . " <p> She continues , " just as climate change is being fueled by oil and gas companies pursuing profit , war is fueled by weapons manufacturers and industries seeking to make money . Climate change and war destroy homes , displace people and effect the poor and vulnerable . " <p> " As climate change gets worse so too will conflicts around resources , land , migration and displacement .
