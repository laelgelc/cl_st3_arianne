<h> Return to subsidies will not solve climate change issues <p> It is important to keep agricultural biological emissions out of the ETS until cost-effective mitigation technologies are available , Andrew McGiven says . <p> OPINION : Well it 's finally happened . The sun is showing its face again and the ground is slowly starting to dry out . <p> The grass has been growing with the warmer than usual winter and spring , but pasture utilisation has been terrible and we have had heavy reliance on supplementary feeds to make up for the grass that has been constantly trodden into the mud . <p> According to the Waikato Regional Council ( WRC ) , the rainfall this year has been almost identical to 1924 , so it has been nearly four generations since we farmers have had to cope with these adverse conditions and for this length of time . <p> As farmers we have seen seasons shift and changes in the climate .
