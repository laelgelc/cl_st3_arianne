<h> Port Nelson cuts wine industry carbon footprint <p> Port Nelson recognised again for efforts to reduce carbon emissions in wine industry . <p> The success of a Port Nelson initiative to reduce the carbon footprint of New Zealand 's growing wine industry , has been recognised with another national award . <p> The company 's " QuayConnect " service has more than halved truck journeys between the port and the country 's largest wine producing region of Marlborough , since it began in February last year . <p> Under the scheme , four trucks are dedicated to transporting empty bottles to Marlborough and full bottles back to the port , meaning trucks no longer travel one leg of the journey empty or only partly full . <p> Port Nelson 's CEO Martin Byrne collected the latest award from the Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport ( CILT ) in Auckland on Wednesday night . <p> " Port Nelson is the main hub through which the vast majority of New Zealand wine travels around the country , and to the world , " said Byrne . <p> " Sustainability is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , so playing our part in reducing the carbon footprint of the country 's wine industry while dramatically improving logistics efficiency , is extremely important to us . " <p> New Zealand 's wine industry has overtaken seafood as the country 's fifth largest export commodity . <p> Wine was an increasing priority as a customer sector , the company 's general manager of business development , Eugene Beneke , said . <p> " As ' New Zealand 's Wine Port ' , it is important that we are close to the majority of New Zealand 's winemakers , while also offering fast and efficient shipping lines to their key markets in Australia , the US , Europe and Asia . " <p> Port Nelson 's wine-industry related volumes had hit a new high of 204,000 tonnes in the last financial year , breaking the previous year 's record of 178,000 tonnes . <p> Infrastructure like the Patterson Logistics Centre wine storage had meant the company had been able to handle the increased volumes , said Byrne . <p> " When the Patterson Logistics Centre II is completed in December @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " <p> In June , QuayConnect helped Port Nelson win the Resilience to Climate Change prize in the national 2017 Green Ribbon Awards . <p> The transport model was also a finalist in this year 's 2017 Deloitte Energy Excellence Awards .
