<h> Hope for last-minute trolley bus rescue <p> The electric buses are meant to be turned off tomorrow night . Photo/Wikimedia <p> There is a last-minute push to save Wellington 's trolley buses . <p> Power to the trolley network is set to be shut off tomorrow night , marking the end of an era for transport in the capital . <p> Electric diesel buses will run in their place from Wednesday . <p> However , a new government could mean a last minute rescue for the beloved buses . <p> Greater Wellington regional councillor Sue Kedgley said she 'll address the transport committee tomorrow , asking for the plan to be put on hold . <p> She says she 's not alone in wanting to find out what the new government thinks of the move . <p> " A number of councillors are very concerned that before any final decision is taken we should consult with the new government and clarify what the government 's position is .
