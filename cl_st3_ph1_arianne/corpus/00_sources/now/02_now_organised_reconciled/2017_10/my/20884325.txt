worldwide -- was formally launched in Stockholm on Tuesday . <p> Funded by Sweden , Norway and the Ford Foundation , a major US philanthropy , the Tenure Facility has already provided grants and guidance for pilot projects in Peru , Mali , and Indonesia , helping local communities leverage rarely enforced laws to protect their land and resources . <p> Disputes over land rights in tropical
