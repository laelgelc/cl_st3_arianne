<h> Reparations fight a just cause <p> I read your report on the speech of Professor Anthony Chen with mixed emotions . I agreed with everything he said about holding developed countries to account for their responsibility for climate change . <p> But why did he have to add " instead of seeking reparations for slavery " ? It is not an either/or issue . <p> Reparation is about obtaining justice for the damage caused by a deliberate and exploitative crime against humanity . It is a just cause that I have supported for years . <p> Action on climate change is about obtaining justice for the damage caused by the deliberate and exploitative emission of greenhouse gases and pollutants into our atmosphere . It is also a just cause . <p> Both campaigns are about making the exploiters take responsibility for the harm they have done by enriching themselves at the expense of our people . <p> Both involve reconciliation and partnership , so that in future , all of us can move forward in a spirit of total respect for all the diverse peoples and environments in our beautiful world .
