<h> Climate change effects vivid in Bangladesh : EU Ambassador <h> The EU head of the delegation has said effects of climate change can already be seen here in Bangladesh as elsewhere in the world . <p> " With the recent floods affecting over 45 million people in South Asia , including at least 6 million in Bangladesh , and three destructive hurricanes that hit the Americas this year , increasing resilience is a matter of priority , " Ambassador Rensje Teerink said on Tuesday . <p> " Climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction go hand in hand , " she said while speaking at a seminar in Dhaka . <p> The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Environment and Forests together with the Delegation of the European Union to Bangladesh organised the day-long seminar titled " Bangladesh and EU Dialogue on Climate Change : Global Perspective , Policies and Actions " . <p> Foreign Secretary Md Shahidul Haque , Secretary to Environment and Forests Istiaque Ahmad , and Chairman of the Palli Karma-Sahayak Foundation ( PKSF ) Qazi Kholiquzzaman Ahmed were also present , according to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on climate change , climate displacement , government of Bangladesh 's policies and initiatives and the EU and member states ' cooperation with Bangladesh in the area of climate change . <p> Presentations were made on EU climate change policy and policy priorities for CoP 23 , the UN climate change negotiations to be held in Germany from Nov 6 to 17 . <p> The ambassador stressed on taking action to prevent or minimise the impacts of climate change , which she said , " is a priority for many countries , and in particular those most vulnerable to its adverse effects .
