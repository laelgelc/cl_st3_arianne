<p> The proposed Framework is based on the findings of a well deliberated Demand Study in five districts Tharparkar , Poonch , Charsadda , Rawalpindi and Ziarat of Pakistan and AJK . <p> Speaking during the event , Secretary , Ministry of Climate Change Syed Abu Ahmad Akif said that the Government of Pakistan is committed to protect vulnerable and marginalized communities from economic losses due to natural calamities and man-made disasters . <p> ' The main purpose for developing such a framework is to set up a transparent mechanism with streamlined distribution channels and adequate funds , this will ensure that the money reaches the affected population in the shortest possible time ' he added . Chairman NDMA Major General Asghar Nawaz said that it is a unique and innovative initiative to mitigate impacts on vulnerable communities .
