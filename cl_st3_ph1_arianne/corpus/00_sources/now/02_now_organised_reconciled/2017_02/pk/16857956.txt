<h> Forest Dept to distribute 4.6m plants among public <p> The Forest Department has decided to distribute 4.6 million plants of different species among farmers , national building institutions and general public during ongoing spring season to offset the growing effects of global warming and climate change . This was disclosed by Conservator Southern Circle , Shafqat Munir while talking to media persons after distributing free plants among farmers of Peshawar here at Tarban Farm on Friday . He said distribution of plants free of cost among farmers and general public have been started and that 4.6million plants of different species including poplars would be distributed in Nowshera , Peshawar and Charsadda districts during the ongoing spring season .
