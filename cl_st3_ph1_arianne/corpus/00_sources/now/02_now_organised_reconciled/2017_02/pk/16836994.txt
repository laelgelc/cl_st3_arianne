<p> DERA ISMAIL KHAN : The National Green Day was observed as part of the Prime Minister 's Green Pakistan Programme here on Thursday . <p> Commissioner Dr Akhtar Nazir Warriach inaugurated the drive by planting a sapling along with the Divisional Forest Officer in his office . <p> Commissioner said that this programme would help tackle the problems being faced in wake of climate change and would lead to providing a healthy environment to the people . <p> He also urged the residents of the district to plant trees or saplings in their homes and consider them as their asset , adding that trees combat climate change , ensure clean air , provide oxygen and conserve energy . <p> He said it was not the responsibility of the government only , but joint efforts were needed to control pollution and prevent possible harm to the environment in the area .
