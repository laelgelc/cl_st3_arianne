The participation of foreign countries in Pakistan Navy 's Aman exercise actually shows their confidence in Pakistan Navy . The aim of the Aman exercise is to eradicate marine terrorism and smuggling . " <p> Zubair said that the professional expertise , honesty and preparedness have made Pakistan Navy prominent in the world and has protected our marine borders from dangers .
