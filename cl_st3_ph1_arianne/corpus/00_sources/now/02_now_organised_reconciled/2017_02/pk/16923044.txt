<h> Afforestation must to curb effects of climate change <p> PESHAWAR : The Governor Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , Engineer . Iqbal Zafar Jhagra has said that to ease the effects of climate change , there is a dire need to promote forestation and focus upon this sector of vital importance . " We may ensure safe future for the coming generations " the Governor said while talking while inaugurating the Spring Tree Plantation campaign at Governor 's House , Peshawar on Tuesday . Additional Chief Secretary , FATA , FidaWazir ; Secretary , FATA Production , Abdul Latif Khan ; Conservator , Forests , Abdul Basheer ; DFO , Khyber Fazal Elahi , Ejaz Khan , Tayyab and other concerned officials were present on this occasion . In FATA , during the ongoing campaign , the Governor was informed , plantation will be carried out over an area of almost 7500 acres which will also include 25,000 fruit plants . Apart from this , it was further told , broadcasting technique will also be applied to cultivate medicinal plants over an area of additional 1000 acres .
