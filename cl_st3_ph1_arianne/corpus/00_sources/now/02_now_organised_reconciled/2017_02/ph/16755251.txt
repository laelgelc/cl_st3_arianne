<h> Quick Links <h> The Standard <h> Carbon emission tax eyed <p> A congressman from Bicol region is batting for the passage of a climate tax on carbon dioxide emission from electricity under his proposed " Piso Para sa Kalikasan Act . " <p> Camarines Sur Rep. Luis Ray Villafuerte seeks to impose a tax of P1 for every kilo of CO2 emission on the monthly power bills of residential or household consumers . <p> Villafuerte , House committee on appropriations vice chairman , said the proceeds of House Bill No. 4939 , if passed , would be used exclusively for programs designed to help the most vulnerable communities better adapt to erratic weather patterns and other disastrous effects of global warming . <p> A consumer shall be exempt from the payment of the climate tax if the monthly consumption would not exceed 60 kilowatt hours or if the power is generated from renewable energy sources , he said . <p> " This bill 's purpose is two-pronged : Generating revenues and at the same time , protect and preserve the environment , " he said .
