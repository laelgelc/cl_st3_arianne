<h> Xavier Ateneo Aggies bag championship at 10th FASMin skills tilt <p> After seven years , Xavier University -- Ateneo de Cagayan was once again proclaimed as the overall champion of the Federation of Agriculture Students in Mindanao ( FASMin ) Congress and Skills Competition . <p> The tenth installment of the said event , bearing the theme " Food and Nutrition Security Amidst Challenges in Climate Change , " was held from January 30 to February 2 at the Mindanao State University ( MSU ) -- Naawan campus . <p> Students competed in 36 events under three categories : crop-based , animal-based , and literary-musical skills . <p> Animal-based skills category <p> XU was hailed as the champion in the animal-based skills category , winning 11 out of 16 events . <p> Xavier Aggies won first place in the four-player carambola , both in men and women categories .
