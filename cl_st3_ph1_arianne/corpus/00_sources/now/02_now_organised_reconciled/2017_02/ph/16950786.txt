<h> House panel OKs Salceda 's ' science for change ' measure <p> The House Committee on Science and Technology has approved House Bill ( HB ) 4581 , filed by Albay Rep. Joey S. Salceda , which aims to boost the country 's scientific innovations and inventions , research and development ( R&amp;D ) toward social progress and global competitiveness . <p> Titled Science for Change Program ( S4CP ) Act , with the theme of " Science for the People , " and a budget that could reach P672 billion by 2022 , HB 4581 is designed to help accelerate science , technology and innovation developments and enable the country to keep up with current global technology and innovation trends . <p> S4CP was launched by the House Committee on Science and Technology , chaired by Bohol Rep. Erico Aristotle Aumentado . When enacted , it will be implemented by the Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) . It was hailed by legislators as a key toward higher standards of STI and global competitiveness . <p> The total R&amp;D budget for 2017 is P5.8 billion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at P21 billion next year , more or less doubling yearly over the next five-year period , and could reach P672 billion in 2022 . <p> Salceda said it is his privilege to push legislation for the country 's strong science and technology program , one of the vital components for growth under the Duterte administration . S4CP focuses on four core concerns : 1 ) Program Expansion , 2 ) New Programs , 3 ) S&amp;T Human Resource Development and 4 ) Accelerated R&amp;D Program for Capacity Building of Research and Development Institutions and Industrial Competitiveness . PNA <p> Salceda said DOST will be guided over the next five years by the tagline " Science for the People " in its pursuit of R&amp;D and S&amp;T initiatives in 12 priority areas : R&amp;D to address pressing problems ; R&amp;D for productivity ; R&amp;D to tap , manage and store Renewable Energy Resources ; R&amp;D to apply new technologies across sectors ; <p> Disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation ; maximized utilization of R&amp;D results through technology transfer and/or commercialization ; accelerated R&amp;D program for capacity building of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and improvement of S&amp;T services ; <p> Human Resource Development for S&amp;T ; utilization of state universities and colleges in the regions which do R&amp;D and develop human resources in S&amp;T , and ; collaboration with industry , academe and international institutions . <p> For a country to be industrialized , the United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organization ( UNESCO ) recommends it should have 380 researchers , scientists and engineers ( RSE ) per million population doing R&amp;D studies , and one percent of GDP should be invested in R&amp;D . Based on a DOST 2013 R&amp;D survey , the Philippines has 270 RSEs per million population only , hence the need to increase the number by 110 in the coming years .
