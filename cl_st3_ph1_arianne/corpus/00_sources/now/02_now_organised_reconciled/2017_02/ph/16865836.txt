<h> Raising awareness for water security and sanitation <p> Over 15 million people in the Philippines have no access to clean and safe water . <p> Beyond this , the water supply for Filipinos is seen to be lesser in the coming years , as climate change and overconsumption have greatly affected the country 's water resources . <p> " We feel like we have a lot of source of water , so we take it for granted , " Elisea G. Gozun , Climate Resiliency team leader of the USAID Be Secure Project , said in an interview with the BusinessMirror at the sidelines of the Media Forum on Water Security and Climate Change at a hotel in Makati City . <p> " Well , we do n't have a lot of water , " said Gozun , a former environment secretary . <p> The Security for Resilient Economic Growth and Stability ( Be Secure ) Project is an initiative of the United States Agency for International Development ( USAID ) . " We ca n't have water security , unless we consider the climate hazards , " Gozun said . The new system has changed the Filipinos , she said , they forgot how to store and manage rainwater and save it for dry days . <p> " When we 're planning on water , we forget to include how we will deal with rainwater . It can actually be on our roof or on the runoff surface , " she said . " Instead of experiencing flood , you can have rainwater storage .
