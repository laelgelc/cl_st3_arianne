<h> The Standard <h> ' P653m foregone with mines closure ' <p> THE government stands to lose about P653 million in revenues from the closure and suspension of some 28 mining companies , the Finance department said Monday night . <p> Last week , the Department of the Environment and Natural Resurces ordered the closure of 23 mining companies and the suspension of five others for violating environmental laws . <p> Of the total revenue losses , P441.92 million would come from the affected mining firms while the remaining P211.72 million represented taxes lost . <p> But Finance Secretary Carlos Dominguez said it is the local governments in areas where the mining companies operate that would be the hardest hit . <p> " The national government is going to hurt , but it 's not going to be fatal .
