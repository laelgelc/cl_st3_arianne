<h> 2016 Miss Earth is back <p> MANILA , Philippines - After she was crowned 2016 Miss Earth in October last year , Katherine Espin ( photo ) of Ecuador returned to the Philippines to continue her work in advocating the preservation and protection of the environment , to address climate change and promote eco-tourism and good health . <p> Joining Katherine on this journey are the reigning environmental queens 2016 Miss Philippines-Earth and Eco-Tourism . Last week , the eco-warrior beauties visited Isabela where they attended the opening of the Agro-Ecotourism Exhibit , Bambanti Village , selection of 2016 Bambanti Kings and Queens , visited the Sta . Victoria Green Sanctuary in Ilagan and toured the Eco-Fuel Agro-Industrial Plant in San Mariano . Then , they traveled to Ilocos Sur to celebrate with the Ilocanos their environmental prosperity . Then , they will also conduct the " I Love My Planet Earth " school tours of the Miss Earth Foundation to educate youngsters about the preservation of the environment .
