<h> Pangan : By all means , explore sources of energy <h> Pangan : By all means , explore sources of energy <p> BY APRIL , we will be faced once again with the spectre of power plant breakdown or outright shutdown , reducing further our power supply in the country , foremost of which is the Malampaya-powered plant and the rest of coal plants now in operation . <p> We have identified several sources of energy ( which we desperately need ) such as hydro , geothermal , wind , solar and biomass but we do not exert sufficient efforts to maximize their usefulness nor conserve them in earnest . <p> And now , here 's a bright idea from the energy stakeholders : a first ocean power plant will soon be set up in San Bernardino Strait , between Sorsogon and Northern Samar , tapping the Pacific waters surrounding the Visayan islands . <p> This will be a joint project of a Philippine energy company and a French partner and it will utilize three to five turbines to generate additional mega watts of power . <p> We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of reducing our dependence on coal-powered plants which emit hazardous wastes harming our environment and the atmosphere . <p> We are a signatory nation to the Paris Agreement on Climate Change and yet we continue to use coal plants in the country . <p> To counterbalance our dependence on coal-fired power plants , we have tapped several alternative sources of clean energy such as solar and wind power plants and if the San Bernardino project will push through , tidal energy will be added to our list of power sources . <p> Meanwhile , we continue our search for more energy sources . <p> In a bid to conserve power supply , the Department of Energy ( DOE ) urged the public to adopt energy conservation measures to help ensure sufficient power supply especially in the electricity-hungry Luzon island grid ahead of the two-week maintenance shutdown of the Malampaya gas field . <p> Published in the Sun.Star Pampanga newspaper on February 03 ,
