The project is in line with the country 's efforts to combat climate change and its persistent problem with air pollution . <h> Here are five things you need to know about Asia 's first vertical forest in China : <h> 1 . The vertical forest will be built in Nanjing and will house more than 3,000 trees and plants <p> The living buildings are set to be built in the eastern Chinese city of Nanjing and will possibly house more than 1,000 trees and more than 2,000 other plants affixed to the facade . It will be helmed by the same group of architects behind other vertical forest projects in the world <p> The project will be headed by Stefano Boeri Architects , whose name is probably the most famous in green architecture . The team is the very same group of architects behind the successful Bosco Verticale , the vertical forest in Milan and another in Switzerland . They will bring their expertise in China , serving a practical purpose in the densely populated Pukou district of Nanjing. <h> 3 . It will be a mixed-used construction with a hotel , museum , school and private club <p> The buildings , which are expected to occupy about 6,000 square yards of land , are mixed-use in every sense . The country is facing a major problem as its smog levels continue to reach historic level . In fact , in January , 30 cities around the country were once again placed on red alert , the most severe form of air pollution warning . <p> Although the country may still be far from winning the battle , China is continuously making a concrete move that could eventually lead them to a cleaner future .
