Poverty rates have decreased dramatically from over 40 percent in the early 1980s to just below 10 percent in 2015 . Global life expectancy has risen from 65 years in 1990 to 72 years in 2013 . Almost every indicator of well being , from infant mortality to literacy and inoculation rates , has improved faster than at any other period in history . <p> This era of rapid growth and interconnectedness has brought tremendous benefits to billions of people around the world .
