<h> GAIP to commence pilot of livestock insurance <p> The Ghana Agricultural Insurance Pool ( GAIP ) is introducing a livestock insurance scheme to cushion farmers in the event of losses on their animals . <p> With effect tomorrow the pilot stage of the programme is expected to be rolled-out to provide security for smallholder farmers . <p> " In a bid to minimise the high risks associated with livestock farming in the country , the National Insurance Commission ( NIA ) had given the GAIP the permission to begin the pilot phase of the programme , " the General Manager of GAIP , Mr Ali Muhammad Katu , told the GRAPHIC BUSINESS in Accra . <p> " A smallholder farmer rearing about three cattle , for example loses his or her income in case their animal suffers accidental death . So , the livestock insurance scheme will provide security for such farmers in case of untimely death of their animal , " he said <p> According to him , stakeholders have already concluded discussions about the modalities of the programme .
