<h> Volta Delta under threat <p> GHANA 'S Volta Delta is yet to be recognised as critical and viable potential ecosystem that could spur sustainable development for communities , Mr. Ken Kinney Executive Director , Development Institute , has said . <p> According to him , communities currently situated at the countries Volta Delta wallowed in abject poverty . The Volta Delta is near Keta and Dzelukope in the Volta Region . <p> Mr. Kinney noted that the Volta Delta was under threat of resource exploitation such as salt mining , commercial agriculture , fragmentation of habitats , unplanned human settlement , salt water intrusion , siltation and destruction of mangroves and coastal erosion . <p> Mr. Kinney was speaking at a Volta Delta Stakeholders meeting in Accra organised by the Development Institution , an initiator of the creation of the Delta Alliance -- Ghana wing . <p> It also aimed at improving the resilience of the world 's deltas in the face of climate change as well as seeks the mandate of the Development Institute to continue to lead the process for a full membership of the Delta @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ country was waking up to the need for a long term development planning for the Volta Delta , which would be fed into a research agenda for the nation . <p> Mr. Fred Smiet , First Secretary , Water and Climate Change in the Netherlands Embassy in Ghana , noted that Ghana could not deal with Deltas without managing river basins . <p> However , Mr. Boateng-Gyimah said water governance had been saddled with challenges including conflict of use of water , inadequate legal and regulatory mechanisms , poor attitude towards handling of the environment and political interference .
