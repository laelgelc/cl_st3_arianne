<p> Peel is asking the provincial government to conduct toxicity tests on the additive used to fluoridate the region 's drinking water . <p> Regional council wants the Ontario government to provide clear evidence the additive is safe for human consumption . <p> After months of hearing scientific studies that have concluded water fluoridation has proven oral health benefits and contradictory arguments that the practice poses serious health risks , Peel councillors decided to drop the local controversy in the Ontario government 's lap . <p> For a year now , councillors on Peel Region 's Community Water Fluoridation Committee have been re-examining the benefits and potential health risks associated with adding fluoride to the municipal drinking water system . <p> The committee was established to form a recommendation for the regional position on continued use of water fluoridation in Peel . <p> However , it appears council members are no closer to forming that position than they were a year ago . <p> Committee Chair Carolyn Parrish admitted councillors have been " struggling " with the contradictory information presented on the benefits and dangers of fluoridation . <p>
