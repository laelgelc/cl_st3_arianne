<p> Prairie municipalities preparing for a hotter future , with increasing spring floods and summer drought , need to invest in " green infrastructure " the head of Winnipeg 's Prairie Climate Centre says . <p> " Green infrastructure is the ability of the landscape , if well managed , to absorb the shocks of climate change , both flood and drought ... Investment in green infrastructure is a critical form of climate adaptation , " said Hank Venema , an environmental system engineer who head the Climate Centre at the University of Winnipeg . <p> Governments can pay for the innovative infrastructure with same sort of " green bonds " now being used in Ontario , Venema said . <p> " We need to manage water better . Our ability to flourish will depend on our ability to manage a much more variable availability of water , " Venema told delegates to the Saskatchewan Urban Municipalities Association convention Sunday . <p> In recent years , many insurers have seen claims for flood and drought on the same section of land in the same year . <p> What 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wetland reservoirs that collect flood waters in the spring and hold them for release and use in the increasingly hot , dry summers , he said . <p> " It 's the other side of drainage . We settled the prairies by draining them and now we need to put features back on the landscape that allow us to retain and manage the water that drains off , " he said . <p> " If we do it well , we can create a whole stack of environmental and economic benefits , " he said . <p> The system would transform two per cent to three per cent of mostly low-lying , flood-prone farmland into temporary wetlands each spring , rather than simply draining them toward land downstream and causing problems elsewhere . <p> The water would be released as the need increases during the summer . <p> The water can be naturally cleaned in the process : Cattails that flourish in the wetlands are sponges for fertilizer nutrients that have washed off farmland and the plants can be harvested when the water is released . <p> They can @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on crops where the nutrients can be released or they can be formed into pellets to sell as fuel to burn and heat buildings .
