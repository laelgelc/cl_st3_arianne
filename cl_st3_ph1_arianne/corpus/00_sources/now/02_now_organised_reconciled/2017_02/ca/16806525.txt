<h> A climate change working group concludes the city needs more resources it if wants to get serious about keeping its green house gas emission targets . <p> If the City of Winnipeg wants to get serious about fighting the effects of climate change , it needs more resources . <p> That 's according to a report prepared by a council-led climate change working group , which will be up for discussion at Mayor Brian Bowman 's executive policy committee on Wednesday . <p> In April 2016 , Coun. Jenny Gerbasi was tasked with leading the climate change group , comprised of herself and councillors Matt Allard and Cindy Gilroy . <p> The trio reviewed OurWinnipeg -- the city 's long-term planning and development document -- and looked for ways to leverage money from the federal and provincial government , as well as other sources , for new green initiatives . <p> The report concludes that despite having approved emissions targets , the city is n't on track to meet any of them . <p> " Recent reports to Council show that only 0.2 per cent of the targeted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ operations , " the report reads , " and the community-wide target of reducing greenhouse gas emissions by 6 per cent below 1998 levels does not have a complementary action plan or strategy in place as to how to achieve this target . " <p> One of the biggest challenges the report identifies for the city meeting its emission targets is resources . <p> It recommends the city join other municipalities in establishing an " office of sustainability " this year and staff it with at least three people by 2018 . <p> " An Office of Sustainability should take the lead in researching and recommending climate change initiatives and targets , as well as coordinating implementation efforts within municipal departments , other levels of government , and community stakeholders , " the report says . <p> It also calls for the city to launch city-wide consultations to develop a " community-wide climate change action plan " for $94,000 , which the city can fund through a grant . <p> Input from those consultations should inform new emission targets for 2035 and 2050 , the report says .
