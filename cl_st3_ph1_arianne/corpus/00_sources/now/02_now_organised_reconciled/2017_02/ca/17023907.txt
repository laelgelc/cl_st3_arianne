<p> Quebec is providing $12.7 million over three years for climate change research through the Ouranos consortium . <p> The announcement was made Monday by three Couillard government ministers in the offices of Ouranos in downtown Montreal . <p> The funding is intended to finance applied research projects on how best to adapt to climate change . <p> Reducing greenhouse gas emissions will not be enough , Ouranos officials said . They added that we must also find ways to live with the impact of climate change on our infrastructure , agriculture , and economic development . <p> " Science makes it clear that greenhouse gas reduction must remain strong , but it will only partially reduce the magnitude of the impacts of climate change , " said Alain Bourque , general manager of Ouranos . <p> With the financial support received , Ouranos will be able to carry out some of the work that was already in the planning stage , such as mapping the risks of forest fires in mid-northern Quebec with forestry partners . <p> Ouranos also wants to develop other projects that relate to human @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bourque said . <p> During the holiday season , the Gasp ?
