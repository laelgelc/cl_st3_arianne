<h> Fraser Institute 's rankings show improvement at public schools <p> The Fraser Institute 's annual school rankings report released on Monday shows that nine out of 10 elementary schools in B.C. that have shown improvement over the last year are public , but the annual report continues to show that elite independent schools such as Crofton House , St. George 's and West Point Grey Academy hold the top spots . <p> The report is based on the annual Foundation Skills Assessment , a standardized test of reading , writing and numeracy skills of 4th and 7th graders in B.C. that is administered by the B.C. ministry of education . The use of its data by the Fraser Institute for the purpose of ranking and comparing individual schools in its annual Report Card has long generated controversy . <p> Glen Hansman , president of the B.C.
