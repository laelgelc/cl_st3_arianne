<p> Sting kicked off his 57th &amp; 9th tour with a surprise : Sting . Nobody expected when the lights went down at the Commodore Ballroom ( capacity : just under 1000 ) that the rock star headliner himself would walk out onto the stage . But that he did , catching the chattering , largely vintage crowd off-guard as they rushed to whip out their smartphones and capture the moment . <p> Sting , in a slim-fitting black blazer , was chatty , gracious , funny .
