<h> Climate Change Model Needs Missing Critical Components <p> COLLEGE PARK , Md. -- A new scientific paper by a University of Maryland-led international team of distinguished scientists , including five members of the National Academies , argues that there are critical two-way feedbacks missing from current climate models that are used to inform environmental , climate , and economic policies . The most important inadequately-modeled variables are inequality , consumption , and population . <p> In this research , the authors present extensive evidence of the need for a new paradigm of modeling that incorporates the feedbacks that the Earth System has on humans , and propose a framework for future modeling that would serve as a more realistic guide for policymaking and sustainable development . <p> Twelve of the interdisciplinary team of 20 coauthors are from the University of Maryland , with multiple other universities ( Northeastern University , Columbia University , George Mason University , Johns Hopkins University , and Brown University ) and other institutions ( Joint Global Change Research Institute , University Corporation for Atmospheric Research , the Institute
