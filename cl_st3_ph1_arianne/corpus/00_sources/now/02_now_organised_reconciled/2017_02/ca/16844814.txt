<h> Federal budget money earmarked to help Indigenous communities get off diesel <h> Communities relying on diesel to keep the power on in winter are seeking alternatives <p> The Inukjuak community , pictured here , voted to build a hydroelectric dam that would provide them with a clean renewable source of energy and help them replace diesel as a fuel source . ( Submitted by Chris Henderson ) <p> The upcoming federal budget will include up to $50 million to help remote communities develop renewable energy projects and get off diesel as a power source , CBC News has learned . <p> Sources say Ottawa is prepared to make " a sizable investment " to help mostly Indigenous communities develop renewable energy projects that could include hydro , solar , wind or biomass energy . <p> " I think it 's fair to say that the government will be making additional investments , " said a government official who was not authorized to speak publicly about the budget . <p> The federal government has been helping remote communities reduce their dependence on diesel power generators for more than a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ used to fund feasibility studies and community engagement . <p> In the 2016 budget , the Liberal government set aside $10.7 million over two years for the Northern Responsible Energy Approaches for Community Heat and Electricity Program , which is designed to help deploy renewable alternatives to diesel fuel . <p> Diesel is expensive because it has to be moved either by trucks over ice roads in winter or shipped by barge to remote communities . It 's also a dirty fuel , second only to coal in the amount of greenhouse gases it emits when burned . Yet 200 communities across the country rely on diesel fuel to heat homes and keep the lights on . <p> The Pan-Canadian Framework on Clean Growth and Climate Change signed in December 2016 by Prime Minister Justin Trudeau , along with eight provinces and three territories , promised to " intensify " efforts to move away from diesel as a way to reduce Canada 's greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Earmarking new money in the budget would be part of that effort . <h> Hydroelectric energy <p> The new budget could @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , located on the east side of Hudson Bay south of the Arctic Circle . <p> Inukjuak , with a population of 1,800 , is 300 kilometres away from the nearest road or transmission line . Every year the Inuit community uses about three million litres of diesel at a cost of about $2 a litre -- the cost approaching $6 million a year is subsidized by the Quebec government . <p> " It 's as reliable as it can be , " said Eric Atagotaaluk , president of the Pituvik Landholding Corporation , which manages the community 's land and economic development . " But every so often we have community blackouts . There was one time in the early 2000s that we had a community blackout that lasted two days for mechanical reasons .
