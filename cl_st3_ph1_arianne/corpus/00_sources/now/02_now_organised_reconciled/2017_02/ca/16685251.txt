<p> I tuned into the news last week as our prime minister toured the country to speak to ordinary , everyday Canadians to answer their questions . <p> What surprised me was the amount of protesters at the rallies opposing scientific-based fact . Facts are that pipelines are the safest , quickest , cheapest and thus most efficient means of getting petroleum resources to the markets they are needed in . <p> Justin Trudeau knows this , which is why he made the bold , unpopular to many , yet correct decision to approve the Trans Mountain and Line 3 pipelines himself . <p> So how did Albertans thank him ? Well , we sent our own band of anti-science extremists out , to not only ignorantly heckle during the town hall meeting , but to also protest against the scientifically proven fact that climate change is real , it is man-made and that the results of inaction could be catastrophic . <p> A carbon tax is a market-based approach to cleaning up after ourselves that does n't pick winners or losers and allows new and emerging forms of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Alberta has some of the best wind and solar resources in the country . Why not combine a mix of jobs in sunny , windy southern Alberta with those in oil heavy in northern Alberta ? <p> The politicians here who oppose such a tax have provided no alternative on how to fund such economic diversification , yet have no problem spewing much hot air themselves . It 's easy to criticize and organize poorly attended rallies to oppose such a tax , but in reality , it is just a distraction from a lack of clearly defined policy or even a shadow budget . <p> Opposition to pipelines is as fierce as anyone has ever seen and carbon emissions from the oilsands are one of the main contributors to resistance . The best solution is not to think we can build through indigenous territory to a pristine coastline and not have to give up something in return . <p> A carbon tax is a small price to pay to leverage the free market to satisfy the demands of our oil markets while maintaining a transition to cleaner energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> I was born and raised in Alberta . The name Trudeau was never popular in my house growing up , but guess what ? The National Energy Program happened before I was born , and I have moved on . It 's time the rest of the province does , too . <p> Justin Trudeau has given the oilsands the smart , intelligent , fact-based voice the industry was lacking under the former Conservative government . Albertans who benefit from the oil industry -- which is all of us -- need to wake up to this and realize we still have an ally in Ottawa and not create an enemy for no reason . <p> Now if that does n't wake you up , maybe some other fact-based numbers will . The two anti-pipeline mayors in Vancouver and Burnaby represent approximately 12 federal seats , 11 of which went Liberal . The anti-pipeline coalition of mayors in the Montreal region represents well over 20 seats in Parliament that went Liberal . Trudeau has a 26-seat majority in Parliament . <p> So we can either back the Liberal party @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we can risk federal seats in highly populated swing vote areas going to the federal anti-pipeline NDP . You might hate the Trudeau honeymoon , but your other option is waking up to another NDP nightmare . <p> It 's time to give credit where it 's due .
