<p> Claire Ruddy , executive director of AWARE , poses for a photo with the organization 's Zero Waste Station . <p> The Association of Whistler Area Residents for the Environment ( AWARE ) is hosting an Environmental Action Forum alongside their annual general meeting to ask residents what issues they would like to see the organization tackle in the coming year . <p> The forum and AGM are set for next Wednesday ( March 1 ) at the Squamish Lil'wat Cultural Centre . <p> " The board is updating our strategic plan at the end of the year , " said Claire Ruddy , AWARE 's executive director . " We really want feedback from the community as to where AWARE focuses time , energy and resources . This is a great opportunity to give feedback . " <p> The forum will be organized around AWARE 's three main mandates : safeguarding habitat , wildlife and biodiversity ; connecting people with nature and building a sustainable community , added Melanie Tardif , president of AWARE . <p> " The idea of this forum is to engage our membership @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ them to feel a part of what we 're doing . " <p> Ahead of the forum at the AGM , Tardif will offer an update on the organization 's last three years -- its accomplishments and finances -- and members will elect a new board . " This has been my first year as president , " she added . " It 's been eye opening with what we 've accomplished , but also learning the role ( and about ) governance . " <p> In total , the organization hosted 18 programs and projects . That ranged from hosting Elizabeth May 's talk on the COP21 UN Climate Change conference last January to taking over the community garden program -- dubbed GROW Whistler -- and launching two social enterprises . <p> " We 've also grown our volunteer list , " Tardif said . " A lot of people are really getting more involved . The clothes swap event is also becoming such a big annual event , we might want to make that into two events -- fall and spring .
