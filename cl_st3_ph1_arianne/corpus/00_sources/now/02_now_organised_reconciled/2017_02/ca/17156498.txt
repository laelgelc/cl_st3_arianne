<h> Who to believe on climate change ? <h> Poll <p> Premier Rachel Notley recently announced a budget of $600,000 to develop a program of educating Albertans on climate change . The question is what will be the science in this program . <p> Climate change plans in this country are based on the Paris Agreement , which used the report of the NOAA ( National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ) . It stated that temperatures continue to rise and that this phenomenon if unaddressed would be catastrophic for our planet . Governments including Canada and the provinces must act now . The data of the report , endorsed by the IPCC , supports this direction . <p> Except , was this report completely factual ? Dr. John Bates , a top scientist in the NOAA , just retired , and he has written a letter to the UK 's Daily Mail with quite the revelation . The data of this NOAA report was unverified , but the IPCC directed that the report must present the worst scenario of rising temperatures .
