The National and North American biathlon have been canceled due to lack of snow . <p> Call it climate change or just a stroke of bad luck , but there was no other choice . <p> Rather than run the risk of having 200 athletes come all the way to Prince George from various parts of the continent , only to be told there was n't enough snow to stage the 2017 National and North American biathlon championships at Otway Nordic Centre , organizers of the seven-day event had to pull the plug . <p> The competition , originally set for March 6-12 , has been moved to the Canmore Nordic Centre , which has the snowmaking capability Prince @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ done and we had a lot of great sponsors and 150-plus volunteers and with the community behind us it was really coming together , " said Vernon Neill , deputy chief of competition for the organizing committee . <p> " It was a very difficult decision - it 's been very emotional for our group as well and it 's a loss to the community and a lot of businesses we had standing up behind us .
