<p> The report reveals that there were 315 natural catastrophe events in 2016 that generated economic losses of $210 billion . For historical context , 2016 was the seventh highest year on record with the combined economic loss exceeding the USD200 billion threshold for the first time since 2013 . <p> The top three perils -- flooding , earthquake and severe weather -- combined for 70 percent of all economic losses in 2016 . While at least 72% of catastrophe losses occurred outside of the United States , it still accounted for 56% of global insured losses . <p> Overall , just 26% ( $54 billion ) of overall economic losses were covered by insurance in 2016 due to a higher percentage of damage occurring in areas with a lower insurance penetration .
