The Medupi power station and the massive coal mine next to it will keep South Africa from meeting its clean energy goals and send up a giant cloud of pollution to endanger the people and animals in the region , according to this report from South African environmental monitoring group Groundwork . <p> But if the South African government has its way this is just the beginning .
