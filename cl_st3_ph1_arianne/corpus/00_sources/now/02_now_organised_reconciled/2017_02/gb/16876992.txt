<p> I do n't know what bugs you , but I tend to get frustrated when people wo n't talk to me , but happily talk about me . I prefer to be up front , to say what I think and then , at the very least agree to disagree . Amicably ! I suppose it 's a family characteristic . <p> But it 's more than that . It 's an important Biblical principle too . This is how one New Testament writer puts it : " We will not be influenced when people try to trick us with lies so clever they sound like the truth . Instead , we will speak the truth in love , growing in every way more and more like Christ . " In other words , we simply reveal our immaturity ( or should I say childishness ? ) when we refuse to talk with those with whom we disagree . <p> Unity and harmony never flourish in a soil of silence or in a compost of backbiting .
