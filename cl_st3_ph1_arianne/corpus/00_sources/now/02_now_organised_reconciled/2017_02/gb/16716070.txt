<p> Ferens Art Gallery has launched a new exhibition and it is the first in partnership with the city 's Maritime Museum . <p> Offshore : Artists Explore The Sea is an exploration of the sea by some of the UK 's most innovative artists . The gallery says it has been inspired by Hull'r relationship with the sea . <p> Alice Sharp , curator and director of Invisible Dust , said : " We are delighted to announce ' Offshore : Artists Explore the Sea ' , our major new exhibition taking place at the Ferens Art Gallery and the Hull Maritime Museum which puts Hull 's role as a port central and will be an important part of the UK City of Culture year 's events . " <p> Offshore will run from April 1 to August 28 , and combines ten new commissions alongside existing works , including Artes Mundi 2017 winner John Akomfrah 's film Mnemosyne , John Smith 's film Horizon ( Five Pounds a Belgian ) , Tacita Dean 's celebrated The Roaring Forties : Seven Boards in Seven Days chalkboard series on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sculpture together with selections from Hull 's fine art and maritime Collections . <p> It is an exciting first joint exhibition for Ferens Art Gallery and Hull Maritime Museum .
