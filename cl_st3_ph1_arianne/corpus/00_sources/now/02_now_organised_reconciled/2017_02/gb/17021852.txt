Many in the crowd chanted " No ban , no wall . The Trump regime has got to fall . " They held aloft signs saying " Uphold the Constitution Now " and " Impeach the Liar . " <p> Nova Calise , one of the New York City organizers , said Presidents Day was " a perfect time to protest the person that 's currently holding the title of President of the United States , " adding Trump did n't share the values of those demonstrating Monday . <p> A rally in downtown Los Angeles also drew thousands .
