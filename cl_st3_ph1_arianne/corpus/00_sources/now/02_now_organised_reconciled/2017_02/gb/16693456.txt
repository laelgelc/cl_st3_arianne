<p> After just seven months in post , shadow Defra Secretary Rachael Maskell has resigned from the front bench to vote against the triggering of Article 50 . <p> Ms Maskell said she was voting against the legislation to ' represent the views of her constituents ' , who voted to remain in the EU by a margin of 17,634 . <p> A statement on her website read : " I have consulted with my constituents and local businesses and held a number of meetings for them to voice their opinions , read over 1,000 pieces of correspondence just this weekend and organised two packed emergency meetings on Monday night . <p> " I have listened carefully to my constituents who voted strongly to remain in the EU . <p> " In voting against the legislation , I am representing the will of my constituents . " <p> The statement also said Theresa May 's Brexit plan made ' no mention of climate change mitigation or environmental protection ' and raised concerns about the impact of leaving the single market and customs union on the agricultural sector .
