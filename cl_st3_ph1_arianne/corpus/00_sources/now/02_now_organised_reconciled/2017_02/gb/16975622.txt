<h> Family of incredibly rare snow leopards caught on camera <p> Ahead of Earth Hour on Saturday 25th March , WWF UK has released stunning new camera trap footage of a mother snow leopard and her cubs in the Khovd province in Mongolia . <p> This incredible species is rarely seen and one of the most at risk from the impacts of climate change . With this new footage , WWF hopes to inspire everyone to sign up to Earth Hour to show they want action on climate change . <p> Every year , Earth Hour inspires hundreds of millions of people and landmarks , including Big Ben , Times Square and the Hong Kong skyline to switch off their lights for one hour . <p> The symbolic hour helps put the spotlight on the issues facing the planet and for many is their first step to helping to protect its future .
