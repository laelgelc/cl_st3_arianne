<h> Former President Jimmy Carter Still A Strong Supporter Of Solar <p> A 1.3 MW solar energy project in Plains , Georgia has been constructed on 10 acres of land owned by former U.S. President Jimmy Carter . <p> The former President 's passion for the technology goes back decades . It was in June 1979 when President Jimmy Carter delivered a memorable speech on the potential of solar energy during the unveiling a solar hot water system installed on the White House . <p> He also created the Department of Energy and Federal Energy Regulatory Commission ( FERC ) , and signed the Public Utility Regulatory Act ( PURPA ) ; all crucial to the evolution of renewable energy in the USA . <p> Nearly 40 years on and President Carter is still a staunch supporter of solar , which he says is critical to meeting growing energy demand and addressing climate change . <p> " I am encouraged by the tremendous progress that solar and other clean energy solutions have made in recent years and expect those trends to continue , " he said . <p> Image @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ solar panels on the Carters ' property are projected to generate more than 55 million kilowatt hours of clean energy and will provide around half of the electricity requirements for Plains ; as well as bringing new jobs and revenue to the community . <p> The electricity generated by the facility is being sold under a Power Purchase Agreement ( PPA ) with Georgia Power . The solar farm was developed , engineered and installed by SolAmerica . <p> " This site will be as symbolically important as the 32 panels we put on the White House , " said Carter .
