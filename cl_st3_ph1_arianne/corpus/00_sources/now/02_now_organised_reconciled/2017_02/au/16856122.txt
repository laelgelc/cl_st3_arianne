<h> Cut the crap <p> Unless you 're giving street directions or teaching children how to tie shoelaces , it really is time to consider stopping using the terms " left " and " right " . They have all but lost their political meaning . They are so opaque and nebulous that they have become a barrier to worthwhile debate about public policy . <p> These days they seem to serve as flimsy insults launched between people who , when you dig into what they believe and value , actually agree about so much more than they disagree over .
