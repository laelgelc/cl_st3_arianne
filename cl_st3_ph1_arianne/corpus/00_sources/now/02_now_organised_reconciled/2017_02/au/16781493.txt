<h> This clip shows the grim reality of climate change <p> " Something that normally takes a number of years , we 're now seeing in a year or two , " says Rick Brown , owner of Adventure Sixty North adventure centre in Seward , Alaska . <p> Rick 's been a tour guide at Alaska 's Exit Glacier since the early ' 90s , and in ' Glacier Exit ' , a short documentary by filmmakers and travellers Raphael Rogers and Paul Rennick , he reveals the harrowing effect climate change is having on the place he 's called home since 2003 . <p> Raphael and Paul visited Exit Glacier because they wanted to witness the epic majesty of the place for themselves . But after meeting Rick , their reasons for being there changed , and ' Glacier Exit ' is the result . <p> Rick goes on to explain the how there have been changes in the wildlife , villages relocated and that the area 's 10 year floods are now happening every other year .
