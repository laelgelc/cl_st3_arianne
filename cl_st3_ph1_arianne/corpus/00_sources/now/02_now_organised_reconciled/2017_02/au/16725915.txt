<h> Our fight to the death with nature is not one we can win <p> In population biology a refugium , or simply fuge , is a protective place for a relict population that has become threatened in its native habitat . Paradoxically , refugiums often make things worse for individuals and populations remaining in nature . <p> The vast royal greenhouses at Laeken , near Brussels , are such a refugium . Built as a pirate showcase for the extraordinary biodiversity of the Congo rainforest that Leopold II had so brutally colonised , they now preserve these fast-disappearing species . Yet the paradox : the 800,000 litres of fuel oil burnt each year to keep these plants alive help drive the climate change that is destroying what natural populations remain . <p> Another refugium is the evangelical rapture .
