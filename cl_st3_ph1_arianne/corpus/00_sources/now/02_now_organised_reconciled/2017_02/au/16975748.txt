<h> Senate confirms Trump 's EPA pick <p> The US Senate has confirmed President Donald Trump 's pick to run the Environmental Protection Agency over the objections of Democrats and environmentalists worried he will gut the agency . <p> Senators voted 52-46 on Friday to approve Oklahoma Attorney General Scott Pruitt . Senator John Barrasso , a Republican and the head of the chamber 's environment committee , said Pruitt would improve the EPA by reforming and modernising it . <p> Only one Republican , Senator Susan Collins of Maine , voted against Pruitt . Two Democrats from energy-producing states , Joe Manchin of West Virginia and Heidi Heitkamp of North Dakota , voted for his confirmation . <p> The nomination of Pruitt , who sued the EPA more than a dozen times on behalf of his oil-producing state and has doubted the science of climate change , sparked widespread concern in progressive circles , and has upset many former and current agency employees . <p> Nearly 800 former EPA staff urged the Senate to reject him in a letter this week , saying Pruitt had " shown no @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , about 30 employees at an EPA regional office in Chicago joined a protest against Pruitt held by green groups . <p> Trump is likely to issue executive orders as soon as next week to reshape the EPA , sources said . <p> The Republican president has promised to kill former Democratic President Barack Obama 's Clean Power Plan , currently held up in the courts , that aims to slash carbon emissions from coal and natural gas fired power plants . <p> Trump also wants to give states more authority over environmental issues by striking down federal regulations on drilling technologies and getting rid of an Obama rule that sought to clarify the EPA 's jurisdiction over streams and rivers .
