<h> Changing streams on the Mekong River <p> The use of the Mekong River is changing rapidly . Aquaculture and fish farming are now among the high growth industries , offering employment and better standards of living , but at the expense of traditional fishermen who have plied these waters for centuries . <p> It 's a significant shift which fishermen say has resulted primarily from a sharp decline of natural fish stocks in the Lower Mekong Basin ( LMB ) -- a change welcomed by many who relish the prospect of an end to the back breaking work of fishing from rickety samphans under a harsh tropical sun . <p> Fish farms are growing in popularity with demand rising and traditional stocks in decline .
