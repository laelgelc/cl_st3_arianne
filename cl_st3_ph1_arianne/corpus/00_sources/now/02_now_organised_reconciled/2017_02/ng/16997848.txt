<h> Speaker pledges radio , TV as parliament week ends <p> On Feb.10 , events to mark parliament week ended with a ' public plenary ' where members of the public were engaged in discussions about the measures to mitigate adverse effects of climate change in parliament chambers . <p> According to speaker Rebecca Kadaga , this was meant to give the public a feel of what actually happens in parliament . Kadaga who pointed out the need to educate the public about functions of the legislature said parliament is considering starting a TV and Radio stations to rely information directly such that people can follow the deliberations . <p> Parliament week is a programme of events that involve exhibitions and meetings with different segments of society meant to enhance relations between parliament and the public just in line with the theme which was , ' Engaging the public in a people-centered parliament ' .
