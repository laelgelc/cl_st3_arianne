We want to continue with our work . In a short time , the rainy season would set in and the state is a place of torrential downpour and we can not wait for the rainy season as all the lofty projects of benefit to Cross River and Nigeria would suffer . <p> " We are appealing that by the middle of March , we would want to see our approvals granted . <p> But the Director of Health of Mother Earth Foundation ( HOMEF ) , Comrade Nnimmo Bassey retorted : " It is strange that the state government should issue such a threat .
