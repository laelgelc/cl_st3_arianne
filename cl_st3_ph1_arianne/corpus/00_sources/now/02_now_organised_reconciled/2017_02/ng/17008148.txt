<h> NCF partners Nigerian firm on renewable energy education <p> As a means of enhancing environmental education , the Nigerian Conservation Foundation ( NCF ) has entered into strategic partnership with a firm to promote awareness drive for renewable energy in schools . <p> The partnership with Skangix Development Limited involves a Renewable Energy Essay Competition " amongst secondary school pupils in Lagos State Education District III , which comprises Eti-Osa , Epe , Ibeju-Lekki and Lagos Island local councils in Lagos state is valued at N6 million . <p> Speaking shortly after the signing of the Memorandum of Understandinng ( MoU ) with the firm at NCF Secretariat Lekki on Friday , NCF Director General , Adeniyi Karunwi said engaging young minds believed to be the future is important as Nigeria is a signatory to the Paris Agreement within the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> According to him , the initiative underscores NCF/SDL 's commitment to the environment and to the Sustainable Development Goals while recognizing the role of children in the sustainability agenda , which includes Quality education , Gender @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Responsible consumption and production . Climate action and Partnerships for the goal . <p> He stressed that renewable energy such as sunlight , wind , waves and geothermal heat is naturally replenished and it never runs out . " Renewable energy is an alternative option to fossil fuel because renewable resources are available to meet the needs of the people if properly harnessed , " he added . <p> The Essay Competition , which will run from February 14 -- May 29 , 2017 with " Energy- Make it nature friendly as the theme " , is opened to secondary schools that have active NCF Conservation Clubs in full time education and is an on the spot essay writing and all participants must use the approved booklet .
