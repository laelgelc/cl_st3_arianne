<h> J$30m allocated for Portmore climate change park <p> A waterfall , as seen in September last year , under construction in the Portmore-Hagen Climate Change Park . <p> The Government has allocated J$30 million to the Portmore Hagen Climate Change Park project . <p> This is included in the 2017/2018 Estimates of Expenditure , now before the House of Representatives . <p> The project aims to raise public awareness about issues surrounding climate change among residents in Portmore , St Catherine and its environs . <p> The park will comprise a green recreational space for relaxation , which will also act as an educational tool to illustrate the generation and use of renewable energy , as well as reduction of the carbon footprint . <p> It is anticipated that this increased awareness will change how residents conduct their business and personal affairs . It is also expected to change how they maintain their homes , workplaces and public property in respect to climate change . <p> The project is being implemented by the Ministry of Local Government and Community Development and is expected to conclude in March 2018.
