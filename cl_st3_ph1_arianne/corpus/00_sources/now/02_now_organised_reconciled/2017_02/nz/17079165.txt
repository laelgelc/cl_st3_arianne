<h> Forester says Minister will give forest industry a greater voice <p> The Forest Owners Association says it 's looking forward to a higher profile in government with the appointment of Hon Louise Upston as holder of the forest portfolio in her role as Associate Minister for Primary Industries . <p> Ms Upston replaces Jo Goodhew . Jo Goodhew was a minister outside cabinet . Louise Upston is number 20 in the Cabinet . <p> Forest Owners Association Chair , Peter Clark , says the industry had a good relationship with Jo Goodhew , who he says always put a high priority on the forest and timber processing industry . <p> " But we were frustrated , as I 'm sure she was frustrated , that our issues did not get to be raised in cabinet by a minister directly concerned with forestry , " Peter Clark says . <p> " We are New Zealand 's number two primary industry , with forest product export returns running ahead of horticulture and meat , as well as being a large domestic industry .
