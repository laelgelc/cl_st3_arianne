<h> PM sets out business-as-usual agenda for the year <p> Prime Minister Bill English has set out a business-as-usual agenda for the year ahead as Parliament returns from the summer break . <p> Bill English . Photo : File <p> Mr English 's first Prime Minister 's Statement to Parliament is a roundup of the measures the Government intends to pursue before the September election , a vast range from an overhaul of tax rules to stop multinationals avoiding taxes to the Advanced Survey and Title Services Project . <p> The agenda is little changed from that of last year - apart from the impact on trade from Brexit and the election of US President Donald Trump in the United States . <p> The TPP has dropped down the list of priorities - but not off it completely . It notes that New Zealand will continue to push the line on free trade . <p> " The Government will continue to show leadership and make the case for open markets , and closer trade and economic ties . . . The Government will work with Trans Pacific Partnership countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , including through the TPP . <p> The Government will continue to look for opportunities to build closer economic ties with the United States and discussions with the United Kingdom about a post-Brexit trading relationship will also be progressed . " <p> Despite the difficulties in getting Ngapuhi to the settlement table , the Statement shows National is sticking to the goal of getting all " willing and able " iwi settlements through by the end of 2017 . <p> However , there was no mention of the Kermadecs Marine Sanctuary , which has been on ice since the proposal was challenged by Te Ohu Kaimoana - the Maori Fisheries Commission . <p> The statement also shows there has been no major rethink on immigration under Mr English - it states while the Government will continue to review the settings , it proposes to attract more foreign investment and introduce a new visa for young technology entrepreneurs . <p> While measures such as Mr English 's announcement for more police , an expansion of charter schools , and double bunking all get measures , there is no mention of climate change or the Paris Agreement .
