If we ignore that warning we 're putting people and their livelihoods at risk . <p> " People in the Hawke 's Bay and Canterbury are used to dealing with drought and fire risk , but no one wants to see this get worse . <p> " The Government has a moral responsibility to do its bit to eliminate climate pollution , to ensure the next generation does n't inherit a country where more frequent wild fires are the norm . <p> " The Green Party in government would make sure the biggest climate polluters pay for their emissions , prioritise transport investment in clean-energy rail and public transport , and transition New Zealand to 100 percent renewable electricity .
