<h> Coastal protection one step closer <p> Rising sea levels and other challenges posed by climate change are the focus of two new community-led panels . PHOTO/FILE . <p> The next step in protecting Hawke 's Bay 's coast has been taken - with the launch of a new phase of the Clifton to Tangoio Coastal Hazards Strategy 2120 . <p> Over the course of 2017 , two Coastal Hazard Assessment Panels containing 80 appointed members will be looking for long-term local solutions to the challenges posed by climate change . <p> Ahuriri 's East Pier hosted the launch earlier this week where the members - representing communities from Clifton to Tangoio - were joined by Hastings Mayor Lawrence Yule , Napier mayor Bill Dalton , and Hawke 's Bay Regional Council chair Rex Graham . <p> Coastal Hazards Joint Committee Chair Peter Beaven said with an expected 1.5 metre sea level rise over the next hundred years , " whatever measures we implement , we need to tackle this problem to reduce the angst for our coastal settlements and give certainty to our wider community " . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's Bay Regional - committed to the process , Mr Beaven said this would be " the best opportunity to date " for coastal communities to engage with councils on the issue . <p> " My sense of this whole thing tells me that whatever we come up with .. I know the three councils will take it very seriously . " <p> Those sitting on the panels include representatives from coastal communities , local businesses , those with mana whenua over the coast , or recreational interests will develop options for consideration by the Clifton to Tangoio Joint Committee and participating Councils . <p> These solutions would likely include a mix of coastal retreat measures , coastal defence or letting nature take its course . <p> ' Northern ' panel members - representing Tangoio , Whirinaki , Bay View , Westshore and Ahuriri - will focus on the coast between Clifton and the Port . <h> Related Content <p> The focus for ' Southern ' panel members - representing Marine Parade , Clive , East Clive , Haumoana , Te Awanga and Clifton - will be the area @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ develop recommendations to help the most at-risk communities plan how best to adapt to a coastline that is gradually changing shape . <p> During 2017 , the panels will learn about specific coastal hazard risks , develop options or ways to respond to those risks over 100 years , and make recommendations on applying these . <p> " It 's going to be a very interesting process , " Mr Beaven said .
