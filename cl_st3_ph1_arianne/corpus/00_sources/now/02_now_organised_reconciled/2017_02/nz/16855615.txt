<p> Although Taranaki iwi had constructed fortified villages called pa , there were other hidden refuges on the maunga ( mountains ) of Taranaki . These secret places were where the children , women and elderly were sent to keep them safe . <p> The refuges were hidden in difficult to reach places like Te Kohatu ( on the Kaitake Ranges ) , upon Te Umu Tao Manawa ( the Dome ) and in Te One Hahau ( the Pyramids ) . <p> These and other sites were a chain of sanctuaries that ringed the mountains and were only accessible by Taranaki iwi .
