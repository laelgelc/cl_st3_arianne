<p> Queer communities have long been aware that binary definitions of " male " and " female " do n't reflect all lived experiences of gender . For generations , transgender and gender-nonconforming people have supported each other in an often-hostile social climate . They built community at social functions and fought proudly for liberation . <p> The advent of the Internet brought gender self-determination and self- representation to new levels of possibility . AOL chat rooms evolved into Facebook groups , where isolated individuals found freedom and connection . These spaces empowered a multiplicity of politicized identities , rooted in intersecting experiences of race , ability , religion , geography , and class , in addition to gender . Access to stories previously shared through oral tradition helped combat historical erasure or misrepresentation . <p> In May 2014 , Time magazine proclaimed the arrival of " The Transgender Tipping Point " with a towering image of the actress Laverne Cox on the cover . The same year , Facebook declared fifty-six gender categories for users ' pro , honoring the spectrum of possibilities .
