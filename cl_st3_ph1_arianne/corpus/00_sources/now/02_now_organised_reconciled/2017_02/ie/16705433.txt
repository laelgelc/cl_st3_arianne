<h> Hogan launches consultation on the future of the CAP <p> A three-month consultation on the future of the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) was launched today by the European Commissioner for Agriculture , Phil Hogan , with farmers invited to have their say on its reform . <p> It is the first phase of the modernisation and simplification of the CAP . <p> Speaking at the launch of the CAP consultation , the Commissioner for Agriculture , Phil Hogan , said that the Commission is taking the next steps towards modernising and simplifying the CAP for the 21st Century . <p> " The CAP at the moment is far too complex and cumbersome for many of our farmers . " <p> By launching this public consultation , we are asking all stakeholders and those interested in the future of food and farming in Europe to participate in shaping a policy for all the people of Europe . <p> " This public consultation feeds directly into the roadmap for the Future CAP announced by President Juncker in December . <p> " The CAP is already delivering major benefits @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the vitality of rural areas , the rural environment and the contribution to the climate change challenge . <p> " By designing a roadmap for the future , I am confident it can deliver even more .
