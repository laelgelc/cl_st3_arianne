<h> Commission wants the public to have its say on the future of CAP <p> The European Commission wants the public to have its say on the future of the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) as part of the CAP 2020 reform . <p> The Commission has recently launched a consultation process , which includes a questionnaire , to contribute to the future impact assessment of the CAP . <p> This public consultation is open until May , 2 , 2017 . <p> According to the Commission , the goal is to summarise available evidence on the performance of CAP so far , draw lessons from the implementation of the latest reform , have a structured dialogue , confirm what the current difficulties are and anticipate needs for modernisation and simplification of CAP . <p> The consultation process will also consider emerging opportunities in the areas of health , trade , the bio-economy , the circular economy and the digital economy also need to be further considered . <p> It will also take forward work and consult widely on simplification and modernisation of the CAP to maximise its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> This will focus on specific policy priorities for the future , taking into account the opinion of the REFIT Platform and without prejudice to the next Multiannual Financial Framework . <p> The consultation process comes after the CAP 2013 reform prompted much debate . <p> Many questioning whether the reform went far enough to meet the broader challenges related to the balance of support , the economic prospects for agriculture and rural areas , care for the environment , action over climate change , sustainable and safe food production .
