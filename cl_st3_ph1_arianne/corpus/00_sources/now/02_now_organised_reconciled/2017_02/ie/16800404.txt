Why did you think about this theme ? <p> The purpose of the exhibition is to look at the new digital methods of image production , display , and distribution , which have largely displaced older forms of photographic practice . As a museum of photography and visual culture , we must find ways of exhibiting and discussing the photography , video , and other pictorial content that is on the Web .
