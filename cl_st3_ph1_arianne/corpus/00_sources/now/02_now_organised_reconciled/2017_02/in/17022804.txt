Many in the crowd chanted " No ban , no wall . The Trump regime has got to fall . " They held aloft signs saying " Uphold the Constitution Now " and " Impeach the Liar . " <p> In Chicago , several hundred rallied across the river from the Trump Tower , shouting " Hey , hey , ho , ho , Donald Trump has got to go . " <p> Rebecca Wolfram of Chicago , who 's in her 60s , said concerns about climate change and immigrant rights under Trump prompted her to start attending rallies . <p> " I 'm trying @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ else to do , " said Wolfram , who held a sign that said " Old white ladies are really displeased . " <p> The Salt Lake Tribune reports ( http : //bit.ly/2kFqWIG ) that the crowd marched to push back against Trump and his administration 's stance on such issues as the environment , immigration , free speech and Russia . <p> Some people raised signs that said " Not My President , " while others held up a large American flag . <p> Protester Reg Brookings warned the crowd that Trump is trying to divide the country by making such groups as immigrants the enemy .
