<h> Both Congress And BJP Ignore State of Environment in Dev Bhumi <p> Dehradun : With Uttarakhand going to the polls on February 15 , the biggest issue not being addressed by any party is the state of the environment . Strange , considering that in the past decade it has been ecological issues , ranging from flash floods to cloudbursts and forest fires to degradation of the hills that have impacted the people in this sylvan state the most . <p> Uttarakhand 's environment has been stretched to its breaking point by tourism , building of dams and sand mining along river banks . Environmental calamities , of which the 2013 floods is the most extreme example , have destroyed life and property . This is the elephant in the room that scarcely finds mention in party manifestos or candidates ' meetings . <p> The Congress and the BJP manifestos talk of development , irrigation and distributing of freebies like laptops and smartphones . Of these , no issue is more emotive than the Ganga . Revered by millions of Hindus as a sacred river , the Ganga has been reduced to a rivulet in Dev Bhumi . <p> The Bhagirathi and the Alaknanda , the two headstreams of the Ganga , have been dammed repeatedly . Tunnels have been blasted in the mountains to divert the water , and where the river bed stands exposed , sand mining , has ravaged the river banks . <p> All the rivers that originate in the glacial heights of the Uttarakhand Himalayas have been tapped for their hydro-electric potential as a nation hungry for economic growth taps every possible source that yields energy . The result is a flood of development along rivers , devastated mountains and an ecological balance increasingly under threat .
