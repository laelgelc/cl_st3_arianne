Photo : Hemant Mishra/Mint <p> New Delhi : Record low Indian solar tariffs tendered at a groundbreaking auction may catalyze green investments and help tip the balance of new power to renewables and away from fossil fuels . <p> Solar power in India fell to a record Rs3.30 a kilowatt-hour on 10 February after a World Bank-backed auction helped the central Madhya Pradesh state tender 750 megawatts ( MW ) of power . The auction format , which included payment guarantees and was run by the state and central governments , could become a template fueling growth in other regions and bringing energy security to the world 's second-most-populous country . <p> " The outcome for this tender has shown that the holy grail of grid parity has been discovered , " said Basant Jain , the director at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ build 250 MW of capacity in last week 's auction . If the auction can be replicated , it would unleash exponential growth in Indian solar and make it the cheapest source of energy available , he said . <p> India is among a growing list of countries stretching from Asia to Europe that have used auctions to make clean energy more affordable . The result should help Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's government meet pledges to install 175 gigawatts ( GW ) of renewable capacity by 2022 and spur discussion about whether solar can replace coal as India 's dominant source of energy . <p> The winning " bid brings the price of solar power in the same range as coal power , " said Ajay Mathur , a member of Modi 's Council on Climate Change , in an email . <p> Solar may pass coal and account for all new capacity on the grid in the next decade , according to a 13 February report issued by India 's Energy and Resource Institute . <p> Achieving parity <p> Under scenarios projecting high levels of green power @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ beyond 2027 , the price of electricity from renewable energy storage achieves parity with the price of electricity generated from domestic coal sources at prices below Rs5 a kilowatt-hour , according to the report , titled Transitions in Indian Electricity Sector . <p> In a sign the government wants to leverage momentum built from the tenders in Madhya Pradesh , it printed colourful newspaper advertisements featuring pictures of Modi and pledging to determine if the auction format could be applied in other parts of the country . <p> Some of the prominent auction features that could be replicated in other states include : <p> Template ready <p> " When you 're given a full template-ready project you can get rid of variability , " said Manoj Kumar Upadhyay , founder of domestic solar developer Acme Group , which also won 250 MW in the auction .
