The Timbuktu example shows how guerrilla archiving becomes at once a necessarily collective and distributed act . <h> The power of archives <p> Today 's data rescue efforts may be high-tech , but they have much in common with the Mazer 's collectors and the Timbuktu smugglers . In some ways , data rescues aim to do the opposite .
