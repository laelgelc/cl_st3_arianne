<h> India 's la-la land infrastructure moment <p> Chapter 5 of the Asian Development Bank 's report on infrastructure -- Meeting Asia 's Infrastructure Needs -- looks at the immediate investment required in infrastructure for 25 developing member countries ( DMCs ) between 2016 and 2020 . In 2015 , these countries invested $881 billion , which is well below the annual requirement of $1.2 trillion between 2016 and 2020 . If investments to mitigate climate change are considered , that gap increases to $1.34 trillion ( see Chart 1 ) . <p> Coming to India , the estimated investment in 2015 was $118 billion , or 5.5% of gross domestic product ( GDP ) . The annual gap in required investments is $112 billion or 4.1% of its GDP and if you consider climate change , then the gap increases to $144 billion or 5.3% of GDP . That 's the magnitude of the challenge which is faced by India and most DMCs in the list , except China .
