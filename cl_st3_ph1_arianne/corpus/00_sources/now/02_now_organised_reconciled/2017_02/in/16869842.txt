Therefore , the increase in numbers has come as a surprise , " said ecologist TK Roy , Asian Waterbird Census Delhi state coordinator , Wetlands International South Asia . <p> The census on the part of Yamuna between Wazirabad Barrage and Nizamuddin Bridge was carried out by a team led by Roy . Out of 2,641 birds spotted this year , the largest ( 2,010 ) has been that of mixed flock of brown-headed and black-headed gulls . Even red lapwing , which falls under the IUCN red-list of threatened species , was sighted . The Yamuna also provides feeding ground for these birds , " he said . <p> According to the ecologist , the presence of black-winged stilt shows the foul quality of the river in the city .
