<h> NASA taking stock of phytoplankton populations in the Pacific <h> February 6 , 2017 by Samson Reiny <p> The microscopic size of phytoplankton , the plant-like organisms that live in the sunlit upper ocean , belies their importance in the global environment . They provide the food source for the zooplankton that ultimately feed larger animals ranging from small fish to whales . And like plants on land , phytoplankton use carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to grow and thrive through photosynthesis , which ultimately releases oxygen into the ocean and atmosphere . <p> Phytoplankton also play a large role in reducing carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere : A recent study found that phytoplankton take in about 24 percent of this greenhouse gas . When they die and sink to great depths in the ocean , phytoplankton also move carbon dioxide out of contact with atmosphere . Among the most pressing questions scientists are investigating is how much of that carbon is being stored in the ocean over the long term .
