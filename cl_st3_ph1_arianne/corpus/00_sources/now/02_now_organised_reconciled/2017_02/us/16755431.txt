Cleaner air not only saves lives ( 160,000 last year alone , as reported by the American Public Health Association ) , it saves us treasure . <p> As part of the 1990 amendments to the Clean Air Act , Congress required the EPA to conduct " periodic , scientifically reviewed studies to assess the benefits and the costs of the Clean Air Act . " Is the law worth it ? they wanted to know . The answer was a very definite yes . The EPA concluded that the total monetized health benefits from the Clean Air Act between 1970 and 1990 ranged between $5.6 and $49.4 trillion . The costs to comply with the act during that period : $0.5 trillion . Thus the net direct benefits were between $5.1 and $48.9 trillion , with a central estimate of $21.7 trillion . The benefit-cost ratios were 43.4:1 for the central estimate and 11:1 for the low estimate .
