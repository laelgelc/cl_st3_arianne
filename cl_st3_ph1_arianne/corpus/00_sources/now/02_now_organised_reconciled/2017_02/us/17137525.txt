<p> Under the circumstances , it 's tempting to treat Trump as an anomaly and to view his mendacity as some sort of new development in conservative circles . But while Trump has introduced a new level of dishonesty into the world of White House communications , the grim reality is that his duplicity builds on years -- decades , really -- of movement conservatism honing lies as a major and , in some cases , central public relations tactic . <p> For journalists like myself who have covered the reproductive-rights struggle for a long time , Trump 's bottomless ability to lie feels very familiar . The anti-choice movement is the vanguard of the conservative movement generally . Tactics that anti-choicers develop and hone tend to spread out through conservatism , and nowhere is this more evident than when it comes to the use of shameless lying as a tactic . <p> What 's remarkable about the anti-choice movement is that it lies about pretty much everything . It 's impossible to argue with someone who wo n't concede even basic facts , making any attempt at reasoned discourse fall apart . Fifty-nine percent of Republicans in the House of Representatives and 70 percent of Republicans in the Senate , as of March 2016 , denied the scientific facts around climate change , according to Center for American Progress Action Fund polling . <p> In other words , the majority of congressional Republicans are liars . Instead , he 's just someone who correctly assessed that the Republican Party has no problem with lying as a strategy and has taken that observation to its logical conclusion . <p> So no one should be surprised to see Republican politicians falling in line with even some of Trump 's most obnoxious lies .
