<h> 30% of US buyers consider electric cars ; only 3% buy . Can this change ? <p> A recent report from consulting firm McKinsey &amp; Company took a hard look at the electric vehicle ( EV ) market and found that there are some considerable -- but not insurmountable -- roadblocks to its success . <p> The problem is summed up in one notable statistic from the report : in the US , 30 percent of the vehicle buyers McKinsey surveyed reported that they considered purchasing an EV , but only 3 percent actually did so . Numbers were similar for Germany ( 45 percent considered buying an EV , but only 4 percent did so ) , while in Norway approximately 22 percent of potential buyers ultimately bought an EV , " due in part to government subsidies , " the report states . The survey was conducted online and involved 3,500 respondents from the US , Germany , and Norway .
