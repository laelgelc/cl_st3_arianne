<h> We wish Ransdell luck in next phase of career <p> Western Kentucky University President Gary Ransdell has led an extraordinary life . <p> Not only was he a standout student while on the Hill , he has an impressive record of accomplishment as an administrator and as president of WKU . <p> Since being named WKU 's ninth president in September 1997 , Ransdell has transformed the campus and raised WKU 's profile from that a regional school to a nationally recognized and respected university . <p> During his nearly 20 years as president , he has worked tirelessly to secure funding to build new buildings , upgrade and renovate older buildings and oversee the movement of the athletic programs into the respected Conference USA .
