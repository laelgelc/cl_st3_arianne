<p> Nestled in the Svalbard archipelago lies a small unassuming-yet-sturdy building created to last forever . While it may look minimal , this building is one of the most important in the world because it holds the key to continual human survival . It houses the world 's largest -- and most secure -- collection of crop diversity .
