<h> World Bank lends Kenya Sh25 billion <h> share <p> World Bank has approved $250 million ( Sh25.75 billion ) to finance agriculture productivity and offer assistance to smallholder farmers and pastoral communities to enable them endure vagaries of climate change . <p> Under a project dubbed , Kenya Climate Smart Agriculture Project , World Bank board of directors approved the 38-year scheme in which the Kenya government will have a grace period of six years before it starts repaying the loan . <p> " The objective of the Climate Smart Agriculture Project is to increase agricultural productivity and build resilience to climate change risks in the targeted smallholder farming and pastoral communities , and in the event of an eligible crisis or emergency , to provide immediate and effective response , " said a statement sent to newsrooms by the bank 's Nairobi office . <p> " Supporting agro-weather , market , climate , and advisory services will have money released to finance the development of forecasting and marketing information system and their dissemination tools through three subcomponents , " said the statement . <p> Under the contingency @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ expenditures under the Immediate Response Mechanism in case of natural or man-made crises or disasters , severe economic shocks , or other crises and emergencies in Kenya .
