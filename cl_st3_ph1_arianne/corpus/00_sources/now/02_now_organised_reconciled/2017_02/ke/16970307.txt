<p> Peru 's National Association of Potato Growers and Derivatives ( APPAPA Peru ) expects to produce 40,000 tons of potatoes this year , a similar volume to the one achieved in 2016 . <p> This was stated by the president of Peru APPAPA , Victoriano Fernandez Morales , who said that climate change was affecting the potato production , which could decrease production , just as the drought caused a drop in the previous year . <p> He also said that 30% of the total production would be of native potatoes , 40% of yellow potatoes , and the remaining 30% of white potatoes . <p> Victoriano Fernandez said that the potato production was destined for the local markets of each region ; to the Great Wholesale Market in Lima ( GMML ) , and , to a lesser extent , to restaurants in Lima and to a well-known supermarket .
