<h> Human activity on wetlands raise alarm <p> Destruction of fragile ecosystems has raised concerns over the increase in human activities within and close to wetlands . <p> Speaking at Saiwa Swamp National Park on Wednesday , coordinator of the Water Towers Protection Programme Manyatta Wanjiku said the problem is exacerbated by climate change , putting people at risk of starvation in the near future . <p> Wanjiku said they have witnessed extensive soil erosion , overfishing , overgrazing and pollution on wetlands , all of which threaten the future of the ecosystems and multiply the impacts of climate change . <p> She said the problem has led to increase in invasive species of giant elephant grass in wetlands , which discourages the growth of papyrus reeds . <p> " The elephant grass blocks Sitatunga antelopes ' movement and unlike papyrus reeds , it absorbs more water .
