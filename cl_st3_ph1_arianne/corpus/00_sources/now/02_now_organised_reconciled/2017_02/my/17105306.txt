development and climate change . <p> Last year swathes of coral succumbed to devastating bleaching , due to warming sea temperatures , and the reef 's caretakers have warned it faces a fresh onslaught in the coming months . <p> Canberra updated the UN 's World Heritage committee on its " Reef 2050 " rescue plan in December , insisting the site was " not dying " and laying out a strategy for and southern areas escaped with less damage . <p> ' Imminent risk ' <p> The government has pledged more than Aus$2.0 billion ( US$1.5 billion ) to protect the reef over the next decade , but
