<h> Drowning in water woes <p> February is coming to an end and we have faced a number of water- related problems . <p> From water supply disruptions to floods , we either have too much or too little water , bringing disaster and frustration to Malaysians . <p> Unlike our usual hot and dry Chinese New Year , this year 's festive season was a little different . <p> Data from the Malaysian Meteorological Department showed that last month , 20 out of 34 rain monitoring stations showed " much above average " rainfall . This change in rainfall pattern is tied to the unusual number of floods this year . <p> We can blame climate change for this change , but we need to acknowledge that many water problems are caused by people too . <p> For example , the recent water supply disruptions in Kuala Lumpur and Selangor were caused by river pollution .
