<h> Letters <h> Much we can do over water <p> FEBRUARY is just coming to an end and we have already faced a significant number of water-related problems . From water supply disruptions to floods , we either have too much or too little water , bringing disaster and causing frustration to Malaysians . <p> Unlike our usual hot and dry Chinese New Year , this year 's festive season was a little different . Data from the Malaysian Meteorological Department shows that in January , 20 out of 34 rain monitoring stations showed " much above average " values for rainfall . <p> This change in rainfall pattern is then tied to the unusual number of floods we have seen this year . <p> We can easily blame climate change for this change in weather pattern but we still need to acknowledge that there are many water problems that are caused by people too . <p> One example is the recent water supply disruption in both Kuala Lumpur and Selangor which were caused by river pollution .
