In Southeast Asia , urbanisation is one of the major trends currently shaping its societies . <p> With a population of more than 630 million in 2015 , more than 40% of whom have already urbanised at a rate of 5.6% per year in Laos , 4.6% in Cambodia , 3.9% in Myanmar and 3.3% in Indonesia , Asean is home to some of the largest and fastest growing cities in the world . A few of those cities are already some of the most populated metropolises in the world . <p> Without doubt , the constant increase of Asean 's urban population will continuously put pressure on its sustainability .
