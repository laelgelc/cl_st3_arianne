<h> World <h> Germany 's Gabriel says Europe must spend more on defence , look at development <p> BONN , Germany ( Reuters ) - German Foreign Minister Sigmar Gabriel said on Friday there was no question that Europe must spend more on defence , but said the debate on military spending needed to include work on sustainable development . <p> " There is no question that Europe will have to take more responsibility for that ( military spending ) , but we can not reduce security and peace policies to just the extent of military spending . That will not allow us to fight climate change , drought or poverty , " Gabriel told a reporters at the end of a G20 foreign ministers meeting .
