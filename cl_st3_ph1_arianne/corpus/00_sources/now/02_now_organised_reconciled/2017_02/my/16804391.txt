<p> The Steering Committee for the Central Highlands and Dak Lak provincial People 's Committee held a press conference here to introduce the events with the theme " Converging quintessence - Promoting identity - Linking to Develop " . <p> Activities as part of the festivals include an elephant festival , a gong performance , traditional rituals of ethnic minority groups , and a wood statues carving contest . <p> Numerous trade and investment promotion activities will also be held , including a workshop on developing the Vietnamese coffee sector amid climate change and international integration .
