<h> Something 's fishy with prahok <p> This year 's prahok season has left a bad taste in the mouths of those involved in making the pungent dish that is a staple in Cambodian cuisine . <p> There are concerns that the vagaries in the mudfish catch , due to climate change and illegal exports to Vietnam , could seriously affect the small-scale home-based production of the fermented fish paste . <p> The fishing season for prahok that started in December just ended yesterday and there has been no shortage of the small mudfish used to make prahok paste -- the most distinctive ingredient in Khmer cooking . <p> However this year , fish prices have been unusually high despite Kandal province recording above average catches compared with previous years . <p> It is during this season that the local mudfish species , called trey riel , finds its way in large numbers into the Tonle Sap river and many fish buyers travel to the riverbank at Kompong Loung commune , in Kandal province 's Ponhea Leu district , to buy the vital commodity . <p> Sitting on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ purchased mudfish , Sum Soeun complained about the high prices . <p> " Last year , I paid 1,400 riel $0.35 for a kilogram of trey riel . This year , however , it seems I can only make 50 kilograms because I can not afford to buy more prahok fish , " she added . <p> Like Ms. Soeun , Roeung Lanch , too , expressed his disappointment over the high prices . <p> " At first , I was happy to know that the catch of prahok fish had increased this fishing season , " he said . <p> " But when I traveled here from Kampong Chhnang to buy trey riel to make prahok fish paste , I was shocked .
