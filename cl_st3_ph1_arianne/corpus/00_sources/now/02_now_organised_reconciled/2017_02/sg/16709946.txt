<h> Senate panel advances Trump EPA chief pick over Democrats ' boycott <p> Oklahoma Attorney General Scott Pruitt testifies before a Senate Environment and Public Works Committee confirmation hearing on his nomination to be administrator of the Environmental Protection Agency in Washington , US on Jan 18 , 2017 . PHOTO : REUTERS <p> WASHINGTON ( REUTERS ) - A Senate committee suspended rules on Thursday ( Feb 2 ) to approve US President Donald Trump 's controversial choice to lead the Environmental Protection Agency , amid a boycott of his nomination by the panel 's Democratic members . <p> John Barrasso , chair of the Senate 's environment and public works committee , said the panel would " suspend several rules " temporarily to approve the nomination of Oklahoma Attorney General Scott Pruitt as EPA administrator . <p> Democrats on the committee boycotted Wednesday 's meeting to approve Pruitt , saying that he doubts the science of climate change and has too many conflicts of interest with the companies he would be charged with regulating . <p> The full Senate will now vote on Pruitt 's nomination . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with Republicans holding a majority in the Senate , the nomination will likely be approved . <p> Barrasso justified the move by saying that Pruitt , who sued the EPA 14 times as Oklahoma 's top attorney , reflects the agenda of the president who won the 2016 election . <p> " Elections have consequences and a new president is entitled to put in place people who advance his agenda , " he said . <p> Environmental groups , which have strongly criticised the choice of Pruitt , raised concerns that the nomination was pushed through to the full Senate . <p> " If he is approved by the full Senate , he will start on day one as the worst EPA administrator in history , " said Ken Cook , president of the Environmental Working Group . <h> Follow ST <h> The Straits Times <p> We have been experiencing some problems with subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
