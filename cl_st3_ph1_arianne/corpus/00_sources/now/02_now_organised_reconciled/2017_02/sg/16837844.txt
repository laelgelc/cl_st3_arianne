<h> MOVE Systems and ALLCOT developing carbon credit generation project <p> Published by Allcot Group <p> Friday 10 February 2017 <h> Share <p> MOVE Systems International , LLC , a company which delivers safe and clean energy solutions to the mobile food industry , has begun a joint venture with ALLCOT Group to develop the first ever carbon credit generation project based in New York City . <p> The main objective is to replace traditional food carts powered by noisy , pollution spewing gasoline generators with MOVE 's new state of the art food carts powered by an eco-friendly combination of batteries , solar panels and CNG/propane . This switch will generate carbon credits and demonstrates specific actions being taken to fight climate change . <p> New York City has nearly 8,000 food carts and trucks operating on its streets resulting in an estimated 1.2 million mobile food transactions daily . <p> Traditional food carts are usually equipped with both gas generators for power and portable propane tanks for cooking appliances . <p> The presence of both fuel sources on board this confined space is a potentially dangerous scenario @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , including an incident in 2011 that caused a temporary shutdown of the West Side Highway . <p> MOVE 's new food carts , called the MRV100 , also include a restaurant-grade kitchen with refrigeration , sinks and water storage reservoirs .
