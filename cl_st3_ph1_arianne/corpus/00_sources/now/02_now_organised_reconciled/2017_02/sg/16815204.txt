<h> Masagos highlights importance of combating wastage even as prices are set to increase <p> Singapore is facing a major challenge in its water security in the next 50 years , and the impending rise in water prices - which sparked debate when it was announced on Tuesday - is only a small part of the solution . <p> Minister for the Environment and Water Resources Masagos Zulkifli said yesterday at a Pre-Committee of Supply consultation session - attended by 35 members of industry , academia , non-governmental organisations and the public - that having a culture of " revulsion " towards water wastage was much more critical to the country than worrying about the cost of water . <p> Industry will have a growing role in perpetuating this culture , as it is already using more than half ( 55 per cent ) of Singapore 's water .
