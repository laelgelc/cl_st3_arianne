<h> Video contest on Value of Forests <p> The United Nations General Assembly proclaimed March 21 as the International Day of Forests ( IDF ) in 2012 . <p> The day celebrates and raises awareness of the importance of all types of forests . On each International Day of Forests , countries are encouraged to undertake local , national and international efforts to organise activities involving forests and trees in the form of tree planting campaigns . <p> This year , the Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) in Sri Lanka is joining forces through the Sri Lanka UN-REDD Programme with the Forest Department and Climate Change Secretariat of Sri Lanka , as well as SLYCAN Trust , Thuru App Team and IUCN Sri Lanka to organise a one-minute video competition under the theme : Value of Forests as a part of IDF celebrations . <p> The competition aims to create awareness on the important role played by forests . This includes fighting climate change ; regulating weather patterns ; providing livelihoods ; preserving biodiversity ; ensuring water security ; preventing natural disasters ; and maintaining soil quality @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ warming and regulating climatic conditions . People all over the world are now uniting to protect existing forests and to increase forested areas in order to face this common threat , " states FAO representative in Sri Lanka Nina Brandstrup . <p> She further stressed the need for creating awareness on the issue on the need to protect forests and its benefits through creative means . <p> The competition focuses on developing an MP4 or AVI video of a maximum duration of one minute , which could be filmed using a video camera , a smartphone , computer-generated animation or a combination of these . It aims to raise awareness among the public on protecting forests through highlighting benefits in an engaging manner . <p> " Public needs to play a key role in protecting forests . The more there is awareness on the issue , there is more action taken to protect forests .
