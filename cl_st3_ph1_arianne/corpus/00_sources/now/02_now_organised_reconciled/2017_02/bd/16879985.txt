<h> IUB confers degrees to 1419 students <h> BRAC Founder and Chairman Sir Fazle Hasan Abed urged the graduating students of Independent University , Bangladesh on Sunday to engage in citizenry in a changing world . <p> He said this while delivering his written speech at the 18th Convocation , 2017 of Independent University , Bangladesh ( IUB ) . <p> IUB conferred degrees to 1419 graduating students at their Bashundhara campus in Dhaka . <p> Sir Abed said to the students that although building a career , chasing promotions and starting families would keep the students busy in the near future , they should still increase their engagement as citizens in a world that faces challenges like terrorism and climate change . <p> Abed said : " It is what you contribute to society that will be your true mark in the world , not what you have achieved for yourselves . " <p> He also said that many global leaders today speak of eradicating extreme poverty from the world , which is not a quixotic dream .
