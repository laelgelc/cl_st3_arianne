<h> Asia-Pacific Business Forum begins in city Feb 8 <p> on February 6 , 2017 <p> businessnews24bd.com <p> A two-day Asia-Pacific Business Forum ( APBF ) begins in the city on February 8 to provide the best platform for regional public-private dialogue on the role and needs of business in achieving inclusive , resilient and sustainable development . <p> President M Abdul Hamid is expected to inaugurate the two-day flagship regional business event on February 8 at the Sonargaon Hotel in the city , said a press release . <p> ICCB , United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Asia and the Pacific ( UNESCAP ) in Dhaka are jointly organising the forum . The forum will be held under the patronage of the Commerce Ministry . <p> The theme of the 2017 forum is " Regional Integration to Achieve Sustainable Development " . <p> Delegations from different countries including Bangladesh , Nepal , Sri Lanka and Bhutan , and organisations including the International Chamber of Commerce , UNESCAP , United Nations Conference on Trade and Development and Asian Development Bank will take part in the event . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of " Promoting Trade and Investment for the Successful Implementation of SDGs " on the following topics : Resilience : Role of Business in Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaption ; New Energy Realities : Building a Resilient and Low-carbon Future and Supporting Trade and Transport Facilities for Regional Integration . <p> Its mission is to provide the best platform for regional public-private sector dialogue on the role and needs of business in achieving inclusive , resilient and sustainable development . <p> Established in 2004 in Shanghai , the APBF has helped form a pan-Asian multi-stakeholder network , with hosting business forum in various locations , such as Almaty , Bangkok , Colombo , Jakarta , Kuala Lumpur and Kunming.
