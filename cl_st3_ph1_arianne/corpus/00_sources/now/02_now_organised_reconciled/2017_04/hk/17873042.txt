<p> CSR Asia and ELEVATE will be facilitating the next Global Compact Network Singapore ( GCNS ) Thought Leadership Series session to introduce responsible sourcing and what it means for companies in Singapore ( both large companies and SMEs ) . <p> Following global trends and international good practice , Singapore companies are increasingly expected to disclose more on their environment , social and governance ( ESG ) policies and practices . This greater demand for ESG disclosure and transparency also extends to companies understanding and implementing responsible supply chain management . Supply chains however are becoming increasingly complex . Companies need to better understand and address risks linked to unethical employment practices and environmental impacts in their supply chains . These risks have knock-on effects on business operations , reputation and brand . Consumers are also now more aware of potential human rights issues such as child labour and forced labour deep down supply chains . There is growing pressure for brands to move beyond tier-one auditing , towards having greater visibility and taking greater responsibility for their full supply chain . <p> Business are paying greater attention to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chain management . This strengthens the supply chain by making it more predictive and adaptive to changing market needs , helping the company develop resilience , mitigate risk , improve efficiency , and better manage supplier relationships . <p> This session will focus on equipping companies with a greater understanding of the supply chain landscape and showcase best practices in responsible sourcing and supply chain management . <p> Kevin Franklin , Senior Vice President of ELEVATE and Erin Lyon , Executive Director of CSR Asia and Europe Director of ELEVATE will lead and facilitate this leadership series , joined by guest speaker Nik Comito , Associate Director , Environmental Sustainability of Singtel . <p> This event is open to members and non-members of GCNS . As the local chapter of the United Nations Global Compact ( UNGC ) , GCNS encourages companies to align their operations and strategies with UNGC 's 10 universally accepted principles in the areas of human rights , labour , environment and anti-corruption . <p> Through various platforms for multi-stakeholder engagement and collective action , GCNS advances the stewardship of sustainable business practices , supporting Singapore 's national agenda of becoming a sustainable regional business hub . Since its inception in 2005 , GCNS ( then , the Singapore Compact for Corporate Social Responsibility ) has raised the level of awareness , and adoption , of Corporate Sustainability in businesses through collaboration between relevant stakeholders and practitioners in businesses , government and civil society . In addition , GCNS nurtures the next generation of sustainability business leaders through its youth initiatives . GCNS is a stakeholder of the Global Reporting Initiative .
