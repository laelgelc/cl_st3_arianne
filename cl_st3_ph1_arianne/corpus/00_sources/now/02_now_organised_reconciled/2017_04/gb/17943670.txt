<p> Disruption of the democratic process is within arm 's reach , according to MiVote , the democracy start-up that plans to use the internet and mobile devices for voters to set the direction of their country . <p> Its founder , Adam Jacoby , believes that offering a platform to educate voters and help them choose from a range of policy options will help break undemocratic binary options and deadlocks on issues as broad as refugee policy and the energy mix . <p> MiVote , which already claims several thousand members in Australia and plans to expand into the United States and Canada , also aims to shake up some of the least popular elements of the democratic system .
