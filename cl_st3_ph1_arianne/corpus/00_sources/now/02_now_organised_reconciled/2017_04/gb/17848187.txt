<h> Images of climate change ignite South Lakes festival <p> A HARD-HITTING collection of pictures showing climate change in action will be showcased at a South Lakes event . <p> Photographer Ashley Cooper 's book entitled Images From a Warming Planet will form part of this year 's Festival of the Fells . <p> Mr Cooper , who is based in Ambleside , will give a presentation on his work . <p> He said : " Those who attend the festival are mainly people who enjoy hill walking and climbing . Hopefully anyone who will have an interest in the outdoors will also have an interest in climate change , which is what I 'm talking about . " <p> Mr Cooper spent 13 years compiling the collection of more than 500 images which reflect the impact of global warming on every continent . <p> Mr Cooper was motivated to undertake the project after carrying out a photo shoot in Alaska in 2004 .
