<p> Prince Charles admitted it was " difficult " to know what to offer the Pope as a gift as the pair met for the first time in Vatican City . <p> The pontiff received Charles and Camilla for a papal audience in a hall close to his residence and following a private meeting the prince presented Pope Francis with a hamper of produce from his Highgrove estate . <p> As an aide lifted the lid of the wicker basket , the prince said : " They 're all homemade things I produce . " <p> Camilla added : " It 's all very good " . <p> It is the second time the pontiff has received a hamper of food from the British monarchy - in 2014 he was given a wicker box of produce that included honey from Buckingham Palace during a meeting with the Queen . <p> In return , Pope Francis gave Charles a bronze olive branch as a " symbol of peace " , as well as copies of his writings on climate change bound in red leather . <p> Image:Prince Charles and the Duchess of Cornwall visit the Vatican Apostolic Library
