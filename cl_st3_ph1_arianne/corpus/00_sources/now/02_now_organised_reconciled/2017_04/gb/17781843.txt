Back in 1990 , diesel cars were a niche product , accounting for just 6.4 per cent of new car sales in Britain . Efforts to reduce pollution were concentrated on the petrol-fuelled vehicles driven by the overwhelming majority of British motorists . <p> In the late Eighties , manufacturers were forced to switch cars to unleaded petrol .
