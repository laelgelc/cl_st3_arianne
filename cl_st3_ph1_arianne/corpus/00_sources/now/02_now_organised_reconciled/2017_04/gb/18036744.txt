<p> Millions of native oysters are to be put into the Solent , once the site of Europe 's largest oyster fishery . <p> The five-year project aims first to restore a thriving oyster population to the waters between the south coast and Isle of Wight . Oyster beds provide habitat for many other species and the shellfish filter vast volumes of water -- 200 litres per oyster -- helping to clean up pollution . Once re-established , significant oyster fishing could resume . <p> " We hope this programme will have a transformational effect on the Solent in the long-term , " said Tim Glover , at the Blue Marine Foundation , which is leading the project . A million young oysters will be put into the Solent in 2017 , in places where they can not be legally fished .
