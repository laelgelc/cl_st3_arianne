<p> A wildlife trust has launched a campaign to save prickly creatures which are disappearing at a faster rate than tigers ahead of National Hedgehog Awareness Week . <p> Hedgehogs are in trouble , Surrey Wildlife Trust has warned , and the trust is calling on all animal lovers to keep an eye out for them and report their findings on a new ' Hedgehog Hotspots ' web page . <p> The project is being launched to coincide with National Hedgehog Awareness Week 2017 , which runs from April 30 to May 6 , and is backed by the British Hedgehog Preservation Society . <p> Hedgehogs used to be a familiar and well-loved visitor to British gardens , but their numbers have fallen by nearly a third since 2002 -- they 're disappearing faster than tigers are worldwide .
