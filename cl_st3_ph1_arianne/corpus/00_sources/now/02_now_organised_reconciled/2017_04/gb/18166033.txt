<h> Terrorism and racism top list of most discussed global issues on Twitter <p> Terrorism and racism are the most discussed world issues on Twitter according to a study of 173 million of tweets . <p> The research , conducted by SOAS University of London focused on how the micro-blogging site was used as a barometer for global trends and mood swings . <p> This list of world issues that the UN feel are the most pressing was used for the study and the data was gathered by using tools to count the number of times each issue on the list was mentioned in a Tweet on a daily and yearly basis . <p> The words were then grouped into categories to give an easily digestible picture of the findings . <p> Terrorism was found to be the most discussed issue from the UN 's list of issues with 31 million tweets talking about the issue per year . <p> Racism was the second most talked about topic with 28 million tweets , followed by climate change in third and human rights in fourth . <p> In broader terms , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the most talked about on the social networking site with close to 60 per cent of tweets focusing on these issues . <p> Richard Hammond , a data researcher on the project , said : " We found in the course of our research that 88% of the tweets have negative connotations , which means that the subject is perceived by the individual as a problem that needs to be addressed or that can not be resolved .
