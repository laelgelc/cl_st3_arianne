<h> Final phase of Cairngorms pylon removal gets underway <h> More than 300 redundant pylons are to be removed from the Cairngorms National Park . Pic : PA <h> RUSSELL JACKSON <p> WORK to remove the last of around 300 redundant electricity pylons from the Cairngorms National Park is underway . <p> The final phase , which involves dismantling 84 towers between Aviemore and Kingussie , was ceremoniously marked on Friday with the toppling of the first pylon . <p> The removal of dud pylons from the Cairngorms is just one part of a ? 600m energy project created to upgrade the 137-mile Beauly-Denny power line . <p> 600 replacement towers have already been built on the line , which is 200 fewer than before . All in all , over 300 towers in the national park will be taken away . <p> New underground distribution cables have also been installed between Aviemore and Kingussie to replace some of the overhead cables . <p> The initial phase began in 2011 just east of Boat of Garten .
