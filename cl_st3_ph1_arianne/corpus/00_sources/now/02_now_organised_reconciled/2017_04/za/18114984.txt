<h> Zuma nephew declares Atha-Africa Yzermyn venture clean <p> AS if wanting to mine coal in a protected area was not controversial enough , Indian firm Atha-Africa Ventures is also fighting a rearguard action against allegations it may be corrupt . <p> The allegations mainly turn on the fact the company was able to win an integrated water use licence ( IWUL ) and environmental authorisation ( EA ) for its Yzermyn thermal coal project despite its location in Mabola near Wakkerstroom in Mpumalanga province . <p> Mabola is where the Pongola , Vaal and Tugela rivers converge . It was declared a protected area but only after Atha-Africa received its permits -- a development that has environmental groupings , such as Earthlife Africa , in a froth . <p> Then there 's also the fact that one of the project 's black economic empowerment beneficiaries is none other than Sizwe Zuma , the nephew of President Jacob Zuma . " We started this process in 2012 when the first scoping of the project was done . Our mining right application was granted in March 2013 . Then we only got the mining right two years later . <p> " The EA was put in around October in 2013 . It has taken four painstaking years of negotiations to get the EA and four-and-a-quarter years to get the IWUL . If we had political connections , then they are n't doing a good job " . <p> The plan is to build a 2.25 million tonne a year coal mine within about a year assuming it was approved today .
