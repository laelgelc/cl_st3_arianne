<h> Families adopting forests in Nkhotakota <p> Amid calls for Malawians to take care of trees , an initiative whereby families adopt forests has proven to be effective in saving trees in Nkhotakota . <p> As one way of sustaining forests in Nkhotakota district , families are adopting natural forests with the aim of protecting them from people who may want to be cutting down trees wantonly . <p> Some of the forests that are family owned are Mtunda , Boma , Msooya and Magaisa individual forests in NKhandwe village , Traditional Authority Malengachanzi in the district . <p> Speaking in an interview with Malawi24 , one of the family members of Mtunda Jackson said owning the forest is really helping to conserve trees since no one is allowed to cut the trees . <p> According to Jackson , with support from Total Land Care the exercise is made possible because they are equipped with skills on how to take care of the forests . <p> " The skills that Total Land Care equip us with are really helping to maintain the number of trees in the forest .
