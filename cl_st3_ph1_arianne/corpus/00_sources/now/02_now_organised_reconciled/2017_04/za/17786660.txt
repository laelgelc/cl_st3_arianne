<h> Increase in water tariff <p> WATER prices for uMgungundlovu District Municipality customers are set to soar . <p> Mayor Thobekile Maphumulo has announced an increase from R7 per kilolitre to R22 for the same amount of water . <p> She made the announcement while tabling a R923 million draft budget during full council yesterday . <p> Maphumulo said the district had been charging customers too little over the years , which has meant the district was operating on a loss . <p> " When we buy bulk water , we pay a lot of money and we have been charging our customers less than what we pay . That meant we were not getting our money back . <p> " The district has embarked on an exercise to review its water and sanitation pricing structure in a move to provide tariffs that are robust and offer sustainability in the provision of services .
