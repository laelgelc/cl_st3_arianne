" <p> The guano analysis found " no consistent relationships " between climate conditions and the penguin population in the Ardley Island colony , which currently has about 5,000 of the birds . <p> British Antarctic Survey penguin ecologist Claire Waluda said the innovative technique could be used to examine how volcanoes had affected other colonies . <p> " Changes in penguin populations on the Antarctic peninsula have been linked to climate variability and sea-ice changes , but the potentially devastating long-term impact of volcanic activity has not previously been considered , " she said .
