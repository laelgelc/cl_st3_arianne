Read More <p> COIMBATORE : That given the chance and proper guidance , a convicted offender can make a mark in life has been proved over and over again by this unique home that was started by the British government in 1938 . Many boys who have spent their days in the ' Observation Home ' have gone on to occupy posts in the judiciary , police and other departments . <p> Taken over by the government this year , the institution has come a long way since it was started as ' Reception Home ' in September 1938 at Lakshmi Mills junction in Coimbatore city by the British government . It was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ founded on June 1 , 1921 to reform juvenile offenders and prisoners who have served their sentences . <p> Freedom fighter V O Chidambaram Pillai , scientist G D Naidu and many other stalwarts have been part of the NGO . <p> In 1983 , the institution was renamed ' Observation Home ' . <p> This was the only home in the state run by an NGO . The government took over after the NGO was not able to generate enough funds to maintain the home . <p> " We brought experts to provide counselling to the inmates . Many boys have made it to the judiciary , police department and other departments .
