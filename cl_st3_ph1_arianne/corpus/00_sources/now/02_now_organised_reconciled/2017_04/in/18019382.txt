<h> In times of drought , millets may be a boon for Karnataka farmers <p> Sandeep Moudgal <p> TNN Apr 19 , 2017 , 09.38 AM IST <p> BENGALURU : The Karnataka government has decided to renew its push for lesser known crops such as millet in view of two consecutive droughts . Millets need 50% less input , including water and fertilizers , than conventional crops . <p> Agriculture minister Krishna Byre Gowda on Tuesday said his department was trying to drive the market towards millets , which will prove to be a boon during drought years . <p> " Let 's face what the recent trends show . Droughts and climate change will severely hurt the farming community in future . In such a scenario , where Karnataka is heavily dependent on rain-fed agriculture , millets can be a drought-proof crop , " said Gowda , adding that millets may yield a decent crop even if the state experiences 20% to 30% drought conditions in a year . <p> Gowda said the biggest challenge for the government , despite persisting with production incentives over the last four years @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ territory of millet production and consumers accepting it as a staple and healthy diet .
