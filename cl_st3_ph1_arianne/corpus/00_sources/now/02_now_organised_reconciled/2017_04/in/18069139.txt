<p> The 27 business leaders were convened by the World Economic Forum and include the chief executive officers of global banks , consumer goods and utility companies . <p> They are asking G20 leaders to act on the recommendations of the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures ( TCFD ) , an industry-led body chaired by the UN Special Envoy for Cities and Climate Change and former New York Mayor , Michael Bloomberg . <p> Together , the business leaders represent $4.9 trillion in assets under management and almost $700 billion in total revenue . <p> In an open message , they say that climate change is not only an environmental problem but also a business one .
