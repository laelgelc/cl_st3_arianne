<h> Water complaints lead SCB to many illegal connections <p> SECUNDERABAD : The officials of Secunderabad Cantonment Board ( SCB ) were shocked to find a consumer maintaining about half a dozen water connections while he was supposed to hold only one . <p> The incident came to light after a team of officials from the water wing of the SCB conducted a drive against illegal water connections in the cantonment on Friday . The SCB took up the drive following complaints of illegal water connections . Residents of Picket area lodged a complaint with the SCB chief executive officer and the ward member concerned that they have not been receiving sufficient water supplies . The water wing officials , who responded to the complaints , formed 20 special teams and conducted searches in all properties . <p> " We were really stunned during the searches after we found that one property owner has five or six water connections . We verified the records and found that the property owner had taken only one wa ter connection two decades ago.But he is possessing more than five water connections .
