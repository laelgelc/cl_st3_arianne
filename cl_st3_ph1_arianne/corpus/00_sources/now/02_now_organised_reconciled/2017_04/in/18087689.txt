The project would also look into diverse pollinator resources like bumblebees and other insects . <p> She said that AICRP 's study had found that there was 20-25% yield increase in cucumber and bitter gourd when stingless bees acted as pollinators . " Stingless bees have increased effectiveness as pollinators since they can enter smaller flowers .
