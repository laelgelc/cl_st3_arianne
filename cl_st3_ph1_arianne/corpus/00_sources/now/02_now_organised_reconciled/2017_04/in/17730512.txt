<p> A national-level workshop ' Plant Taxonomy : Principles and Practices ' concluded at University of Kashmir . It was organized by Centre for Biodiversity and Taxonomy ( CBT ) , Department of Botany , in collaboration with the Botanical Survey of India ( BSI ) , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) , Government of India . The workshop was attended by 40 participants from different universities , colleges , institutes of J&amp;K and other states . <p> In his keynote address , Dr Paramjit Singh , Director BSI said the CBT is one of the four Centres of Excellence recognized by the MoEFCC for imparting training in plant taxonomy under All India Coordinated Project on Capacity Building in Taxonomy ( AICOPTAX ) scheme . Prof. Azra N Kamili , Dean School of Biological Sciences highlighted that taxonomy forms the core of the ' big science ' of biodiversity . Prof. Z. A. Kaloo proposed the vote of thanks .
