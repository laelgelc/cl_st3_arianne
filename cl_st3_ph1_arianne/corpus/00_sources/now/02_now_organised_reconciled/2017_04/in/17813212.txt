It , however , was scuttled just a week before Singh 's trip by West Bengal chief minister Mamata Banerjee , to the great embarrassment of both New Delhi and Dhaka . This then raises a pertinent question : Will things be different this time around ? <p> Optimists believe that when the three Bengalis -- President Pranab Mukherjee , Hasina and Mamata -- meet over dinner ( hosted by the President ) along with Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 9 April , the elusive pact on River Teesta may finally be put back on course . As a result , many rivers became " shared rivers " , leading to differences among the countries in the region over the quantum of the shares . <p> The resulting " water conflicts " were further accentuated by the emerging political conflicts .
