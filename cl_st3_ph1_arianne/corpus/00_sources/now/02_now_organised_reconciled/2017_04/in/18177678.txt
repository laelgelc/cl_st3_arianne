<h> Manali-Leh highway to open by May-end <p> The Manali-Leh highway will be opened for vehicular traffic by May 25 while Gramphu-Kaza highway through Kunzum pass is expected to open by the end of June , an official said on Friday . <p> TNN Apr 29 , 2017 , 10.26 AM IST <p> ROHTANG ( MANALI ) : The Manali-Leh highway will be opened for vehicular traffic by May 25 while Gramphu-Kaza highway through Kunzum pass is expected to open by the end of June , an official said on Friday . Rohtang Pass was officially opened for both light and heavy vehicles on Friday after a gap of four months . <p> Chief engineer , Project Deepak of Border Roads Organization ( BRO ) , Mohan Lal , visited Rohtang pass on Friday to note the current progress of snow clearance on different roads . He said snow from Keylong-Tandi-Sansari road has been cleared and Manali-Rohtang-Keylong road has also been thrown open for vehicles .
