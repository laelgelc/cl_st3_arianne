<h> Saligao GTP approach roads littered with garbage <p> TNN Apr 30 , 2017 , 03.37 AM IST <p> Saligao : The garbage treatment plant on the Saligao-Calangute plateau may have found many admirers including Maharashtra chief minister Devendra Fadnavis lately , but , ironically , the approach road is littered with garbage on either side . <p> The road , which also leads to the Saligao seminary and the Pilerne industrial estate , is strewn with garbage and has become an eyesore to commuters as well as those working in the industrial estate . <p> A local resident , Mario D'Souza , said that the Saligao panchayat should take immediate action to get rid of the garbage dumped on the road . Locals further said that the Saligao panchayat has not fulfilled its responsibility in collecting the garbage . <p> When STOI contacted Saligao sarpanch Eknath Oraskar , he blamed the state government for not providing sufficient manpower and funds for undertaking the collection of garbage .
