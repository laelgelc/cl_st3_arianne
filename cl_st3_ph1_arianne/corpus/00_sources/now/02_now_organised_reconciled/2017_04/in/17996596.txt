<h> Nag river project yet to see light of the day <p> NAGPUR : The Nagpur Municipal Corporation 's ( NMC ) ambitious plan to beautify and develop the banks of Nag River along with pollution abatement measures is pending with the Maharashtra government . " The government is yet to take decision over providing its 25% share through loan or grants and inform the same to the central government , " said a senior NMC official . The state government is confused under which department 's head -- urban development or environment -- should contribute its share for the project . <p> Already , the ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) had provided the much-needed push to rejuvenate the Nag River and its tributary Pili by approving plan of Rs1,252.33 crore in June last year . The second plan 's estimated cost is Rs1,373.60 crore . <p> The project will have two different components -- Nag pollution abatement project of Rs1,252.33 crore and Nag River Front Development project of Rs1,373.60 crore . The NMC had already prepared the development plan report of the project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ got the nod of National River Conservation department as it forwarded the DPR to ministry of economic affairs ( MEA ) . <p> Initially , the MEA had designed the funding pattern of 85% aid from central government through grant-in-aid or providing the fund through loan , while NMC had to contribute 15% of the remaining cost of the project . But later the funding pattern was changed to 60% aid from central government , 25% loan or grant from state government and rest 15% of the remaining share should borne by urban local body ( i.e. NMC ) . <p> The MEA department sought a debt sustainable report from the state government that whether it would assist the NMC to complete the project . " Whether it will take the loan or provide its own grant to the NMC , the MEA has asked the state government , " the officer said . <p> The MEA has also sought the state government 's opinion whether the NMC will contribute its 15% share for the river abatement project or not .
