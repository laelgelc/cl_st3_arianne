<p> Water conservation in forests to be top priority , says new DG of forest <h> Water conservation in forests to be top priority , says new DG of forest <p> Seema Sharma <p> Water conservation in the forests of Uttarakhand will be the top priority of the Centre as it will not only curb the forest fire but also recharge ground water and revive the dead streams , new director general ( DG ) of forests in Ministry of Environment and Forests ( MoEF ) Siddhanta Das said . <p> TNN Updated : Apr 9 , 2017 , 10.18 PM IST <p> Dehradun : Water conservation in the forests of Uttarakhand will be the top priority of the Centre as it will not only curb the forest fire but also recharge ground water and revive the dead streams , new director general ( DG ) of forests in Ministry of Environment and Forests ( MoEF ) Siddhanta Das said . <p> " Water is the most important requirement for forests as well as for human being . Strangely , 90% of water conservation structures are created outside the forests @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the forests . Most of the rain water also washes away important soil nutrients , which badly hits growth of various species of vegetation , " Das said . <p> Structures such as small ponds and check dams will be built in the forests to store rain water , he said . <p> " The moisture , created by water bodies , will minimise the possibility of forest fire . The water will also help in growth and regeneration of trees and increase the forest cover . The wildlife will also benefit from this . The water seeped under the ground through these measures will also recharge ground water , which would also revive dry springs and create new springs as well . So people in the drought-prone areas or those suffering from lack of water will benefit from it , " Das said . <p> The officer further said that forestry is not only about forests but also about soil , micro-organism , air and water . <p> " Climate change is affecting all of them-- soil , water and air . But , in the past 15 years only , it has increased by 0.6 degrees . <p> " And , the way it is going , it will rise above 2 degrees by 2050 . The entire world is concerned that it should not go beyond 1.5 degrees as our knowledge to deal with a situation beyond that is limited , " Das said . <p> He expressed concern over destruction of forests in fire incidents also . " The trees work as carbon sink by absorbing the carbon dioxide from the air .
