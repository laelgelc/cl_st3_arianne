<h> Forest clearance to 19 projects revoked <p> Nagpur : The Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) has revoked in-principle approvals under Forest ( Conservation ) Act to 19 projects in Maharashtra pending due to non-compliance for more than five years . <p> The projects include controversial Human dam project in the buffer zone of Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve ( TATR ) , coal mining project in Lalpeth Colliery , Chandrapur , Surya irrigation project , among others . " The main reason for non-compliance is that several of these projects will affect wildlife areas , besides several of them also did not submit net present value ( NPV ) , " said forest sources . <p> The 19 projects involve forest land diversion of over 5,200 hectares . They are irrigation ( 13 ) , mining ( 2 ) , rehabilitation ( 1 ) , road ( 1 ) and other projects ( 2 ) . These projects have been hanging fire for more than a decade and some for over 16 years . In Stage I , the proposal is agreed in-principle in which usually the conditions relating to transfer , mutation , and declaration of reserve forest ( RF ) and protected forest ( PF ) under the Indian Forest Act 1927 of equivalent non-forest land for compensatory afforestation and funds for raising compensatory afforestation thereof are stipulated . In Stage II , after receipt of compliance report from the state government in respect of stipulated conditions , formal approval under the FCA is issued . <p> In case of these 19 projects compliance of conditions in the in-principle approval was awaited for more than five years and hence approval was revoked .
