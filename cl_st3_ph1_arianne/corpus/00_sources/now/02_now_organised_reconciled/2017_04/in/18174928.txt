<h> Another patient complains of being prescribed outside medical <p> Days after chief minister Aditya Nath Yogi cleared the government 's stand against private practice by government doctors and against commission nexus with private medical companies , a patient at Rani Laxmi Bai ( RLB ) combined hospital , Rajajipuram , complained to hospital authorities about the same . <p> TNN Updated : Apr 29 , 2017 , 10.14 AM IST <p> Representative image <p> LUCKNOW : Days after chief minister Aditya Nath Yogi cleared the government 's stand against private practice by government doctors and against commission nexus with private medical companies , a patient at Rani Laxmi Bai ( RLB ) combined hospital , Rajajipuram , complained to hospital authorities about the same . <p> Jwala Nagar resident Sonu Kumar Shukla informed the authorities through a written complaint about the orthopedic doctor at the hospital prescribing him medicines from outside . In his complaint , Shukla has alleged that the doctor first declined him service , asked him to go see a private practitioner and then allegedly prescribed him a costly medicine to be purchased form outside . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ patients to buy medicines from outside of the hospital . <p> Hospital chief medical superintendent Dr RK Chaudhary said an inquiry had been ordered to probe the matter in detail . The female patient had alleged that the skin doctor had informed her that the medicine required was not available at the hospital ' store . However , the hospital did have the medicine .
