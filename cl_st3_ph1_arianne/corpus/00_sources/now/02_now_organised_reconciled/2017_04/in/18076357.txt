<h> Students pledge to save earth , birds <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2017 , 02.00 AM IST <p> PATNA : The Environmental Science department of A N College-Patna organised several activities to mark the World Earth Day on Saturday . <p> Students enacted a drama on the theme of the celebrations , ' Save birds , save earth ' . They also pledged to save birds and placed water containers around the college premises for birds as a symbolic gesture . <p> A seminar was also held , at which speakers expressed their views concerning environmental issues . College principal S P Shahi spoke about the climate change and global warming that have led to higher temperature , dwindling water resources and extinction of birds , including sparrows . <p> Department head Preety Sinha and other faculty members also attended the programme . <p> Meanwhile , students of Indirapuram Public School sang ' Prithvi Gayatri Mantra ' at a function organised on their campus on the occasion of World Earth Day on Saturday.A herbal garden was also opened in the school on the occasion .
