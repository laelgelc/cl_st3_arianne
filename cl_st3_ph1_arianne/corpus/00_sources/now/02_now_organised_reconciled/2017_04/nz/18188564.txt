Regulations on fossil fuels were repealed , the Keystone XL pipeline was approved and if passed , Mr Trump 's budget will cut funding to the Environmental Protection Agency by a third . He has promised to leave the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> The climate demonstration is the latest in a series of protests against the new administration .
