<p> Drifts of floating plastic that humans have dumped into the world 's oceans are flowing into the pristine waters of the Arctic as a result of a powerful system of currents that deposits waste in the icy seas east of Greenland and north of Scandinavia . <p> In 2013 , as part of a seven-month circumnavigation of the Arctic Ocean , scientists aboard the research vessel Tara documented a profusion of tiny pieces of plastic in the Greenland and Barents seas , where the final limb of the Gulf Stream system delivers Atlantic waters northward .
