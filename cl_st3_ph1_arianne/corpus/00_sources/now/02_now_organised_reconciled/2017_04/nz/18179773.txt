<p> Organisers of the People 's Climate March held on Saturday said they wanted to keep the event " family-friendly " . <p> About 600 people gathered in Cathedral Square to watch the short play Earthy Earth and the Forces of Doom before marching to Cashel St with signs depicting , among other things , climate change minister Paula Bennet 's head emerging from a cow 's rear end . <p> In the play , protagonist Earthy succumbs to pressure from criminal brothers Carbon Dioxide and Methane to be " the hottest planet " despite wanting to " keep the coal in the hole and the oil in the soil " .
