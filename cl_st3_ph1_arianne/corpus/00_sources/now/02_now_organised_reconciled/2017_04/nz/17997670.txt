Some say he vanished without a trace . But that 's not possible when you hold a position of such power and authority for eight years . What you champion , what you block , basically what you stand for , all have an effect beyond -- even well beyond -- your time in office . <p> I remember the 2011 election when National campaigned on a partial sell-off of state assets . Surveys at the time showed nearly two-thirds of Kiwis opposed such sales . National received 47 percent of the party vote and required the support of minor parties to form a government . When questioned ( make that criticised ) on the latter appointment , the prime minister replied that all points of view were important . <p> The Government 's approach to forming a committee ( or choosing a mediator or investigator ) -- that is , pick people you like and you 'll get the result you want -- would be a recurring theme under John Key ( think David Bain compensation , think the Panama Papers ) . <p> Other approaches to get what you want have been used by the Government , including restrictions on what could be examined or proposed ( think the Green Growth Advisory Group ) or by cherry picking or ignoring recommendations ( think the Land and Water Forum ) . <p> Sustainability is about social justice , economic resilience @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ significant steps taken in any of these areas under a John Key Government . <p> To the contrary we have seen rising inequality , exemplified by policies favouring property investors ( even non-residents ) over the rightful needs of young Kiwis . <p> We have people living in cars , and on maraes and in motels as a stop-gap measure , but no housing ' crisis ' according to the Government .
