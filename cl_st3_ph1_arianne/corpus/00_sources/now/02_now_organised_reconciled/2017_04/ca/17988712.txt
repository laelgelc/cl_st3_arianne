<h> Where it 's Earth Day , every day <p> JEN DOERKSEN / WINNIPEG FREE PRESSJanice Martin , curator of the Assiniboine Park Zoo 's Animal Care and Natural Encounters , started as a keeper at the zoo . " I always knew I wanted to work with animals , but I thought that meant as a vet or in a lab . Then I started at the zoo . " She said she loves working with the animals . <p> Mother Nature called : she is very hot , and she 's sick of our garbage . And for Earth Day -- which is Saturday , April 22 -- she 'd like us to think how we can live greener , cleaner and more sustainably . <p> For the Assiniboine Park Conservancy , sustainability -- using the Earth 's resources without depleting or destroying them so that they can exist for future generations -- is n't just an Earth Day conversation . It 's top of mind 365 days a year , and informs educational programming , camps , workshops and guided tours , right through to the park @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ what we 're sustaining is urban , accessible green space where people have the ability to connect to the environment , " says Karen Lind , education and programs manager at Assiniboine Park . " We 're here to inspire people to take action and conserve nature for the future . ( Sustainability ) is integral for the health of our society . <p> " If our ecosystem ca n't function in a healthy way , then our economy ca n't function in a healthy way , our social system ca n't function in a healthy way . Everything is connected . " <p> Assiniboine Park offers lots of educational programming aimed at students in pre-K to Grade 12 . There are 16 different classes for elementary school students , teaching them about everything from polar bears to plant adaptation . <p> In the high-school years , the courses delve deeper into issues such as climate change and species diversity . The weekly summer Zoo Camps , and Zoo sleepovers -- which happen Friday nights from September through April -- are also popular ways for kids to engage with animals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ park 's educational programming is n't just for kids . Through the Sustainable Living and DIY Series , adult participants can reconnect with nature by learning how to harvest honey , make kombucha or forage for wild edibles . <p> Sustainability and conservation are also the cornerstones of a modern zoo that recognizes the public is no longer satisfied with the idea of keeping animals in captivity for the sake of entertainment . <p> " If we 're here to educate and promote species diversity and species conservation locally and globally , we actually really need to get involved with it , " Lind says . <p> The Leatherdale International Polar Bear Conservation Centre , located in the Journey to Churchill exhibit , is arguably the most high-profile example of that work . The centre houses and transitions orphaned cubs and other at-risk polar bears , and allows researchers to conduct field studies in a controlled environment . <p> The Assiniboine Park Zoo is also involved in a number of other conservation initiatives to protect a variety of species , including the red pandas , which are part of a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ species survival plans , accredited zoos work together to make sure there 's a viable genetic population of a species within zoos . <p> The Assiniboine Park Zoo 's zookeepers , and their popular Keeper Talks , are among the park 's most visible and valued educators . " The zookeepers are the rock stars of our organization , " Lind says . " They 're the ones who are the most connected with the animals , and have those intimate stories of the animals , and that 's why people are coming to the zoo . " <p> Janice Martin , the curator of animal care and natural encounters at the Assiniboine Park Zoo , would agree . " We have an amazing group of keepers who are passionate about their animals , and so to have someone who is passionate about an animal standing in front of you talking about an animal ? It just creates such an awareness and interest in the animal and the Zoo as a whole , that it 's really a win-win for everyone . " <p> Martin says this summer 's slate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ June 's talks will focus on water conservation ; July 's will focus on reducing , reusing and composting ; and August is shopping local . " Sustainable living is about changing something in your lifestyle to reduce your impact on the Earth , " Martin says . " I hope we send people home with something in their pocket that they can do differently . " <p> Both the park and zoo aim to lead by example by incorporating sustainability into their operations , whether that means recycling the water in the polar bear enclosure through a filtration system , using geothermal heating systems or using a BioVator to deal with organic waste . Martin has also challenged each of the keepers to identify five things they can do more sustainably . <p> The hope is that visitors to the park and zoo will be empowered to do the same .
