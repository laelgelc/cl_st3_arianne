<p> Prince Edward County mayor Robert Quaiff has lifted the water state of emergency for Picton and Bloomfield Tuesday morning . Quaiff declared the emergency on March 28 after a barge partially sunk four days earlier and released contaminants believed to be diesel fuel and hydraulic fluid into Picton Bay , threatening to breach the water intake for the Picton Drinking Water Plant . The move to lift the state of emergency came less than a week after the Hastings and Prince Edward Health Unit lifted a boil-water advisory . Quaiff said the municipality maintained its state of emergency until the water monitoring program performed by Pinchin Limited could be reviewed . Pinchin filed a report with the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change on Friday and the municipality was advised of the results early this week . The mayor said the municipality had access to a number of resources by maintaining its state of emergency .
