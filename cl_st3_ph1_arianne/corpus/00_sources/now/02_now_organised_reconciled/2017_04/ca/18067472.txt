<h> Earth Day sheds light on pipeline projects <p> Saturday marks the return of the annual Earth Day . It also marks the one-year anniversary of Prime Minister Justin Trudeau vowing to reduce Canada 's greenhouse gas emissions . And then , it also marks nearly five months since he gave two oil pipeline projects the green light . <p> These two things mean that Trudeau 's liberals " have a very difficult line to walk , " according to Warren Mabee , the Canada Research Chair in Renewable Energy Development and Implementation . <p> The green light for the Trans Mountain and the Line 3 pipeline projects going ahead , and then being used to pump barrels of oil from Alberta 's oilsands to global markets , drew criticism from many environmentalists across the country .
