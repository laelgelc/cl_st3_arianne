<p> Thousands of delegates from First Nations , M ? t is and Inuit groups will visit Edmonton when the city hosts the National Gathering of Elders this September . <p> Representatives from 623 indigenous groups , or an estimated crowd of up to 4,000 , are expected to attend the gathering Sept. 11-14 at the Edmonton Expo Centre . Discussion topics include reconciliation , treaties , climate change and education . <p> The Alberta government has given $100,000 to help host the event . <p> " There are real and pressing issues facing indigenous peoples in Alberta and throughout Canada and increased communication and co-operation -- between governments and between communities -- is essential when addressing these challenges head on , " said Indigenous Relations Minister Richard Feehan in a statement Wednesday . <p> Grand Chief Rupert Meneen from the Tallcree First Nation in Fort Vermilion said that the event will help advisers , teachers and leaders from across the country connect and " bring our voices to the forefront where we can make a real and lasting impact on the issues facing indigenous peoples .
