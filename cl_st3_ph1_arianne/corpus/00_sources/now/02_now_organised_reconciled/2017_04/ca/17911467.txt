The document 's language is designed to ease worries over austerity measures . <p> With the tabling of the second budget from Manitoba 's Progressive Conservative government , we can see clearly now there are two distinct personalities vying for control of Premier Brian Pallister 's brain . <p> There is " the end is near " Pallister , the premier who spent most of the last year sounding the alarm about a growing fiscal crisis that was threatening to swamp the province in a tidal wave of debt . Spending is going up , Finance Minister Cameron Friesen noted repeatedly on budget day , but in a way that is more sustainable . <p> That having been said , underlying Friesen 's optimism and assurances is a deep baseline of austerity . Overall spending , including in key departments such as health and education , is rising , but by less than the rate of inflation . <p> How did Pallister , a man given to alarmist rhetoric , achieve this delicately balanced state ?
