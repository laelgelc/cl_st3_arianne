Vancouver is to share expertise with those cities , as well as get access to resources from the foundation 's more than 100 corporate partners in fields such as finance and engineering . <p> Katie McPherson of Vancouver 's emergency planning office gets the new position of chief resilience officer , to be funded by a two-year grant from the foundation . <p> " It really helps us to think , not just about earthquakes in a silo , " McPherson told a news conference Tuesday .
