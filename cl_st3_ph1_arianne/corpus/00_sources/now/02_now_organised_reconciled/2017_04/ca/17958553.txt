It is for startups that are working with big data and its many applications in artificial intelligence , machine learning and the Internet of Things . <p> " I think it is an exciting opportunity , " Slifierz says . <p> He believes the Data Hub will be ideal for Skywatch , which is building an automated program that provides easy access to the enormous amounts of data collected by satellites . The technology is in private testing right now . <p> The startup , founded in 2014 , just hired its 14th employee . <p> " Considering we were just four people last year , we are doing well , growing very quickly , " says Slifierz . <p> The building at 14 Erb St. W. used to be a police station .
