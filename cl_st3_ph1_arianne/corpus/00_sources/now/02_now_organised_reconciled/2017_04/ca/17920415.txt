THE CANADIAN PRESS/Nathan Denette <p> Six provincial grants are part of $63 million earmarked in Budget 2017 to help Alberta communities and organizations adapt to severe weather events and a changing climate , Alberta Environment and Parks said in a press release . The projects will protect downtown Calgary and neighbouring communities during flooding and stormwater events . <p> Approximately $9.8 million will go to two pump station projects in Calgary 's Sunnyside neighbourhood and to the construction of a new flood-dedicated pump station , the ministry reported . <p> Another $1.7 million will be directed to flood @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ neighbourhood , while the West Eau Claire flood barrier will get another $740,000 .
