<p> This kind of passing back and forth is nothing new to the Town of Happy Valley-Goose Bay when dealing with the Muskrat Falls project . Since the inception of the project , this is normal , according to Town Councillor Tony Chubbs . <p> " In many years dealing with issues around Muskrat Falls , we find the buck is passed back-and-forth between Nalcor and the provincial government on who 's this responsibility it is on these issues , " he told TC . " I 'm not surprised at all ; it 's been like this since the Muskrat Falls project started . " <p> The issue Chubbs is referring to is the burning of construction and demolition waste material on the site , which started happening in the last year . Prior to that Nalcor would bring the material to the Town 's landfill for disposal .
