<p> A survey of more than 25,000 Albertans reviewing the K-12 school curriculum found " there exists a strong desire for the removal of Shakespeare as a required author . " <p> Respondents also questioned the need to keep cursive writing in the curriculum , and want students to be taught more about the environment and climate change . <p> " Albertans certainly agree that this work on curriculum is long overdue , and they want to play an active role in the process , " Education Minister David Eggen told reporters gathered in the wood shop at Edmonton 's Westmount Junior High School Thursday morning . <p> In the two-part survey posted online last October , respondents said they wanted an increased focus on basic math and literacy skills , more emphasis on financial literacy in earlier grades , and better preparation for careers and college and university . <p> Eggen said people also wanted fewer calculators in elementary school classes . <p> The first part posed 11 statements about the value of diversity , inclusion , skills and connections to careers in K-12 education , and asked respondents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kindergarten to Grade 12 curriculum should reflect the diversity of Alberta 's population .
