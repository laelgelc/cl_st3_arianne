For every barrel spent finding and extracting oil , society secured a hefty surplus or what the ecologist Charles Hall famously described as " EROI " : energy return on investment . Unprecedented net returns fuelled the unnatural scale of economic growth for 100 years . <p> But those bountiful energy returns are now falling off a cliff . Today , net energy returns average around 15 to one .
