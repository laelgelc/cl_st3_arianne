Coun. Robert Chambers won majority support last week for his resolution directing staff to prepare a report that analyses the county 's use of external consultants . " It is the responsibility of the county to manage finances in a fiscally responsible and sustainable manner , " Chambers told council . " The effective use of external consultants can enable the county to maximize the value of municipal services delivered to residents and businesses , but it would be prudent for council to examine routinely their use to ensure that we are maximizing the value received from them . " Chambers also wants the staff to do the analysis and report rather than hire a consultant . " We do n't want to pay for a consultant to evaluate the worth of consultants , " he said in an interview . Council has approved a recommendation to reduce the speed limits for Highway 2 to 60 kilometres per hour from 80 kilometres per hour between 1,300 metres west of Middle Townline road to 800 metres west of Middle Townline . The speed limit will also be reduced by the same amount on Highway 2 between 85 metres east of Middle Townline to 48 metres east of Middle Townline . Highway 2 is a boundary road between Brant and Oxford counties in that stretch . Oxford County council has approved an identical bylaw .
