<h> Nova Scotia author knows power of English language <p> Zack Metcalfe is a local environmental journalist and author . ( Contributed ) <p> Growing up , it was unlikely to anyone who knew Zack Metcalfe that he would have anything to do with writing when it came to choosing a career . <p> Throughout school , Metcalfe was illiterate -- needing special assistance to navigate the English language . <p> But , somehow , after hours of self-education , by his senior year in high school , he had completed writing his first book : A People 's Struggle . <p> " I remember the day I decided to be a writer very clearly because it seemed an absurd vocation for someone like myself , " he said . " But all the same , without the first clue how to spell properly I began writing . By my senior year I 'd completed my first book and was so inspired that before graduation I 'd written a second .
