<p> " I ca n't believe I 'm marching for facts " -- that 's what one of the protestor signs read at the March for Science held this past Saturday , on Earth Day . Marches were held around the world , in more than 600 cities across 68 countries , but the epicentre of the concept came from the U.S. , where the phrase " alternative facts " was coined by Kellyanne Conway , Counselor to the President . <p> As an adjective , " alternative " is defined as one or more things available as another possibility . Certainly , there are a lot of things " alternative " can describe . There 's alternative country music , for instance . There are alternative courses you can take in high school . Sometimes , there are alternative menus at restaurants for vegetarians . But there are no alternative facts , because facts are well ... facts . And how is a fact defined ? " A thing that is indisputably the case . " Facts are not disputable . There are no alternative facts -- but there are plenty of lies . The fact that we have to defend facts is dumbfounding , but it 's sorely needed . When we talk about things like truth and facts , we 're really talking about science , and the scientific method , which has been under orchestrated attack since climate change theory really penetrated the public consciousness in the 1990s . There was ( and is ) a lot of money already invested in the status quo , and people were justifiably concerned about how legislated changes could affect economic prosperity . <p> Unfortunately , rather than transparently addressing that concern , there was a tremendous corporate and political lobby against science and against the scientists around the world who were sounding the alarm about climate change . Public funding for science is notoriously spotty and hard to get , despite what many taxpayers have come to believe . <p> With the propaganda against climate change , an insidious trend started to develop among some of the most educated populations in the developed world . Out of these suspicions , conspiracies and fears , the alternative health market burgeoned into the fraudulent monster it has become . <p> Science and fact are not affected by faith , belief or emotion . How you " feel " about a pollen enema to treat bad breath is n't evidence or fact . Your belief fluoride is being used to control the masses does n't make it true . Truth is determined though trial , error , reproducible results and peer review . <p> The scientific process always works , regardless of the religion or nationality of the scientist using it . Simply put , the scientific method is a system with specific steps used to acquire new knowledge , as well as to correct or make sense of existing knowledge . The steps involve repeat testing and measurable evidence , and the measures and testing are governed by universal principles of reasoning . Science is n't about generating a theory that every scientist then works diligently to protect at any cost . There is no sacred cow in science . Evidence , not belief , drives scientific theory , and all theories are subject to change , as long as those changes stem from new evidence . <p> And a scientific theory is n't " just a theory " as the word might be defined by the general public . A scientific theory is an explanation that was generated using the scientific process , that can be repeatedly tested . Like everything else in science , it requires evidence , reason and demonstrable results . <p> Finally , though bruised , battered , misunderstood , and genuinely perplexed , the scientific community has started to fight back . It 's time the rest of us join the fight before we 're out of alternatives .
