This year , the 2017 Canadian International AutoShow in February had its highest attendance ever ! <p> When I speak to colleagues across Ontario , several common themes emerge : the ongoing challenge in finding and retaining skilled and unskilled employees , the importance of education and lifelong learning in all occupations and , of course , getting the message across that there are many jobs and careers available in new car dealerships . <p> If you are a student , or are looking to transition to a new industry , I strongly urge you to explore the diverse opportunities that exist in the retail automobile sector .
