<p> The B.C. Liberals are promising to overturn a City of Vancouver decision to " ban natural gas " from new buildings -- but the city says no such ban is in place . <p> " Today 's B.C. Liberals will ensure that natural gas remains an energy option for residents , restaurants and businesses in the City of Vancouver , " said Liberal candidate Andrew Wilkinson in a media release . <p> " While we all agree that climate change must be addressed , banning natural gas from the City of Vancouver at a huge cost to residents is not the way to go . " <p> But the city says there is no outright ban on natural gas in place . <p> Instead , after May 1 , developers will have various options to meet the city 's new Renewal City Strategy goals , which aim to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from all new homes and buildings by 2030 . <p> " How a developer meets those targets is up to them , " said the city in a written statement . <p> " It can involve a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ design , as well as opting for renewable energy .
