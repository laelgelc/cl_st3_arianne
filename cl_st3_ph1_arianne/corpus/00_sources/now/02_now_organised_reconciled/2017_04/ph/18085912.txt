<h> Valle : Home is gaining back its lushness <h> Valle : Home is gaining back its lushness <p> SEEMS like I 'm back to my past , where as a child playing along the reefs at low tide in my home town , time was of no essence and there was only fun with the sea and zillions of its living creatures . <p> It 's delightful to watch the children look into crevices and holes on stones , poking of slimy little starfishes and sea urchins . <p> It 's the world where I learned about the beauty of God 's amazing grace , it 's where nothing seemed to matter not even the sun beating down on our heads and skin until we turned into " anak-araw " or children of the sun with our golden brown skin and dark brown hair . <p> Looking at my hometown Baganga , Davao Oriental now regaining its lush greenery , and its farms getting busy as harvest time begins and trees are abundant with fruits , I can only heave a sigh of relief and wonder . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Mindanao Interfaith Services Foundation Inc . ( MISFI ) with its project on Enhancing Capacities in Disaster Risk Reduction Management for More Resilient Peoples and Communities which started early on in March 2016 is now on its final stage . <p> As part of the culmination activities in the project areas a three-day Leadership and documentation training seminar for at least 10 leaders of the Disaster Preparedness Committees ( DPC ) from the barangays Lambajon , Baculin and Saoquigue of the municipality was conducted . <p> The participants were barangay functionaries who are members holding different positions to equip them with the necessary tools to carry through each of their roles in the barangays particularly in preparation for any disaster that may strike in the future . <p> A community-based mitigation and adaptation measures was also introduced though the participants have begged off as they said they could no longer be absent from their individual tasks in the barangays . <p> Moreover , the leaders from Barangay Baculin said they already knew how to go about putting up a nursery for seedlings of fruit trees as they have already done @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fellow Bagangenos so focused in their learning process and discovering the real essence of being leaders in a formal way , I could n't help but be thankful that I am given this big opportunity to journey with them . <p> It could be said that really , nature has its own way of teaching us the hard lessons that we need to learn . <p> Typhoon Pablo not only ravaged by hometown and left much of my people destitute , but only for a while . <p> It also left us with a very important lesson about giving values , most particularly in heeding the warnings of nature and not be complacent . <p> In my Baganga , gone is the nonchalance and arrogance of a people who have not really known the wrath of nature for so long , such that when it was hit bulls eye , most of its inhabitants were awakened to the reality of climate change . <p> It 's good to know that not only are the barangay functionaries already attuned to current environmental changes , they are now fully aware about the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unheard of decades ago .
