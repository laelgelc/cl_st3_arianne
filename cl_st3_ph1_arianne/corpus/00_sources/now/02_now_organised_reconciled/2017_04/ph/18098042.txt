<h> 20,000 mangroves planted in Anilao , Iloilo on Earth day <h> 20,000 mangroves planted in Anilao , Iloilo on Earth day <p> ILOILO CITY -- About 20,000 mangrove seedlings were planted in a 20-hectare plantation in Barangay San Carlos , Anilao on Saturday to highlight the celebration of the 48th Earth Day in Iloilo province . <p> A coastal clean-up was also carried out simultaneously in the same plantation for the Action for Re-greening and Transformation for Climate Change Adaptation ( ART for CCA ) program of the Provincial Government . <p> Nearly 600 people from the Provincial Capitol , government agencies , non-government organizations , business sectors and other stakeholders took part during the Saturday 's " green " activities . <p> Iloilo Governor Arthur Defensor Sr. together with the regional directors of the different government agencies and local officials led the tree planting and clean-up activity . <p> In his speech , Defensor lauded the participants who gave their precious time to plant mangroves and clean the coastal plantation to save the environment . <p> He said that the country is currently facing a " very serious @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> He then encouraged the participants to continue to take part in a certain cause that further protect and preserve the environment . <p> Under the ART for CCA program of the provincial government , the province is annually aiming to plant 1.5 million trees in the different areas in the province .
