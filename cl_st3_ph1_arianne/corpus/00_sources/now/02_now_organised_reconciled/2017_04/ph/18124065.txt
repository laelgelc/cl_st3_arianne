<h> ABS-CBN 's Ging Reyes to present award in prestigious New York competition <h> ABS-CBN News <p> ABS-CBN Integrated News head Ging Reyes is in the 2017 New York Festivals presenters ' list , which includes other prominent industry executives and award winners .
