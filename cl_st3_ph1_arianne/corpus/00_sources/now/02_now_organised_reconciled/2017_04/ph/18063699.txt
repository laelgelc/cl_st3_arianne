<h> Warnings for Earth Day <p> Is climate change for real ? Donald Trump has always maintained that it is n't , and now that he 's president of the United States , among his first acts was to dismantle the policies his predecessor Barack Obama had put in place to help mitigate its effects . He clamped down on environmental research , ordered the easing of restrictions on carbon emissions as well as mining and drilling , pulled the plug on public funding for climate-change-related projects , and according to a report by Independent UK , " instructed federal officials to abandon the practice of factoring in the impact of climate change -- what is dubbed ' the social cost of carbon ' -- in their policymaking decisions . " <p> In countries like the Solomon Islands , however , the threat of rising seas is all too real , belying claims by Trump and other deniers that climate change is a " hoax .
