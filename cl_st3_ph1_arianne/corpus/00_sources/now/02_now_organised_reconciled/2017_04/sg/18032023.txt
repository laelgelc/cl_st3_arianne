<h> Climate change reroutes river in Canada ' abruptly ' <h> Melting glacier caused change in water flow that dried up river bed in four days last year <p> OTTAWA * The retreat of one of Canada 's largest glaciers has changed the flow of a northern river almost overnight in an extreme case of what researchers call climate change " river piracy " . <p> The discovery of the Slims River diversion away from the Bering Sea into another watershed that empties into the Pacific Ocean was published on Monday in the journal Nature Geoscience . <p> Canadian daily The Globe and Mail reported on Tuesday that the drainage of one watershed that was stolen by another appeared " to be a first for the scientific record books " . <p> Co-author of the study Gerard Roe , a professor at the University of Washington in Seattle in the US state of Washington , told AFP on Tuesday that the Slims River bed had dried up in only four days . <p> " The habitat was altered , " he said in a telephone interview .
