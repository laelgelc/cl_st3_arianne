<h> Thousands join global March for Science rallies <p> PARIS * Thousands of people rallied in support of science in Europe and Australasia ahead of a march in Washington , triggered by rising concern over populism and so-called alternative facts . <p> Yesterday 's March for Science demonstrations were organised in response to what is seen as mounting political pressure on facts-based evidence . <p> The casualties of this assault , say organisers , include efforts to fight climate change , the teaching of evolution and sexual health and budgets for vital research . <p> Protesters in Sydney wearing white lab coats called on politicians to support the scientific community , carrying banners reading " without science , it 's just fiction " and " we need thinkers not deniers " . <p> Demonstrators also turned out in Melbourne , Brisbane , Perth , Adelaide and other cities as well as Wellington and Auckland in New Zealand . <p> " In this day and age , there 's so much fake news and alternate facts going around that it 's important to remember that science is what has built the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was at the Sydney march with her molecular biologist mother , told Agence France-Presse . <p> Protesters in major university cities in Europe posted pictures on Twitter of marches in Bonn , Helsinki , Munich and Stockholm . <p> In Paris , a banner in French read : " We are the resistance against the orange menace in Washington !
