<h> 13 big businesses urge Trump to stay in Paris deal <p> Big businesses say they want to help the transition to clean energy . Pictured here is an unused coal mine in Black Mountain , Virginia <p> Thirteen big businesses , including oil giant Shell and retailer Walmart , on Wednesday urged US President Donald Trump to keep the United States in the Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> The deal signed by more than 190 countries in 2015 called for lower emissions to ease the threat of global warming . <p> " As businesses concerned with the well-being of our customers , our investors , our communities , and our suppliers , we are strengthening our climate resilience , and we are investing in renewables , efficiency , nuclear , biofuels , carbon capture , sequestration , and other innovative technologies that can help achieve a clean energy transition , " said the letter , published online by the Center for Climate and Energy Solutions . <p> " For this transition to succeed , however , governments must lead as well , " it added . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and practical framework facilitating an effective and balanced global response . We believe the Paris Agreement provides such a framework . " <p> The letter was signed by oil giants BP and Shell and technology powerhouses Google , Intel and Microsoft . <p> Other signatories included Dupont , General Mills , National Grid , Novartis , the utility giant PG and E , Schneider Electric , Unilever and Walmart . <p> Trump promised during his campaign to cancel US participation in the Paris climate agreement , but has since said he had " an open mind " on the matter . <p> Oil giant ExxonMobil has previously called for the United States to stay in the Paris accord . <p> ExxonMobil 's former chief executive , Rex Tillerson , now US Secretary of State , has also spoken in favor of the United States staying at the table . <p> On Tuesday , Energy Secretary Rick Perry said he believes Trump should remain part of the deal , but renegotiate it .
