<p> SINGAPORE - The supply of mud crabs to Singapore has fallen due to bad weather and increased demand , but restaurants have yet to raise prices . <p> Ocean Keris , a major supplier , has seen its supply of crabs from South-east Asia and Africa fall by about 30 per cent this year . <p> Their supply of about 50 to 100 tonnes a month has been hit by a trifecta of factors . While Singaporeans think of mud crabs as " Sri Lanka crabs " , the mangrove mud crabs now come from Indonesia , the Philippines , India and the east coast of Africa . <p> Ocean Keris director Lanson Lim said that the recent bad weather in South-east Asia has affected the catch . At the same time , higher temperatures resulted in 10 to 15 per cent more crabs dying in transit . <p> Tradeocean International has also seen a drop in the last few weeks . Manager Louis Tan cited climate change as another contributing factor .
