<h> Fairtrade sales pass one billion mark in anniversary year <p> At the annual press conference on the 22nd May in Berlin , TransFair CEO , Dieter Overath , will present the figures from the 2016 financial year . Just in time for the 25th anniversary of TransFair , sales of Fairtrade products in Germany are pushing through the 1 billion mark . A higher awareness of sustainable consumption , a growing commitment in trading and manufacturing , and an active general public is the foundation which keeps Fairtrade on a path of growth . <p> At the annual press conference on the 22nd of May in Berlin , TransFair CEO will present the figures of the 2016 financial year . <p> Heinz Fuchs , Chairman of the Supervisory Board , will consider TransFair 's strategic objectives of strengthening itself on a political and economic level in order to reduce poverty and inequality in world trade . <p> From Nicaragua , a Fairtrade partner country from the very beginning , Merling Preza Ramos will participate in the press conference . As Managing Director of the Prodecoop coffee organisation and member @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ network CLAC , she will report on the current challenges in the producing countries , such as climate change .
