The first event probably requires a little history lesson . When we think of genocide , most Kenyans think of the Rwandan genocide or the Nazi Holocaust . I guess very few have heard of the Armenian genocide . On the 100th anniversary commemorating the Armenian genocide , media reports emerged that a Turkish historian had uncovered new evidence that supported claims of an Armenian genocide . <p> Now why was this significant ? Turkey , the successor state of the Ottoman Empire has always denied that a genocide took place . That fact is amazing considering that 1.5 million people died . How can so many people die , and no one notices ? It is not just the state of Turkey that denies it , but even citizens are complicit in a cover up . It is a rare thing for a Turkish professor to admit the existence of the Armenian genocide .
