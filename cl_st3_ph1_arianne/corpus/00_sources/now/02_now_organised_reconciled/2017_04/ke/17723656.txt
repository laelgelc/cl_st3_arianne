<p> Kenya central bank governor Patrick Njoroge told a media briefing in Nairobi that the program will develop a legal and policy framework that will enable Kenya to issue green bonds . <p> " According to the program , the first green bond which will be used to gauge the appetite of investors will be floated by the end of the year , " Njoroge said . <p> The program is a joint effort of the Kenya Bankers Association ( KBA ) , Nairobi Securities Exchange ( NSE ) as well as the Financial Sector Deepening ( FSD ) Africa . <p> Njoroge said that Kenya is already a regional financial hub and so it hopes to lead the region in the adoption of green bonds . <p> During the launch , FSD Africa committed funding amounting to 600,000 U.S. dollars over a period of three years to fund the program with the objectives of aiding KBA to be in a position to tap the growing investor demand for green investments . <p> Njoroge added that the green bonds will help Kenya mitigate and adapt to the effects of climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the impact of the climate change that has caused the present drought in the arid and semi-arid parts of the country . <p> KBA CEO Habil Olaka said that the global financial community is already transiting towards green finance because it promotes sustainable development . <p> " We will therefore develop and sustain the best green bond practices that will inevitably strengthen financial structures in the country , " Olaka said . <p> NSE CEO Geoffrey Odundo said that the exchange is committed to developing a vibrant green market for the East African region . <p> " Through the NSE , issuers and investors will have a platform where they can come together and fulfill their green objectives , " Odundo said .
