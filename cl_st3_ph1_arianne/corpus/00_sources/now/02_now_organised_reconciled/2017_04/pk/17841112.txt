<h> Pakistan under strong influence of climate change <p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan continues to remain under strong influence of climate change this year as well . <p> " It is very rare that Pakistan has received heavy rainfall which continues for more than three days in the month of April , " said Pakistan Meteorological Department Director General Dr Ghulam Rasul . <p> While talking to The Express Tribune , he said the recent rain spell along with gusty winds continued for more than three to four days with high intensity over upper parts of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa , Gilgit-Baltistan , AJK and adjoining northern parts of Punjab . <p> " In April , rain spell of low to moderate intensity continues for hardly two days , " he said . <p> During last three days RawalaKot has received 150 millimeters of rainfall which is somewhat unusual in this month , he said . <p> Having heavy downpours this month is good for the country because it will help raise water level in depleting reservoirs , he added . <p> " It is expected that Pakistan will not face water shortage at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ required amount of water for wheat crops , " he said . <p> However after the rain spell in April , the coming week will remain hot and dry , he said . <p> " Though one or two more good rain spells are expected in April , meanwhile glaciers will also start melting by the end of this month which will also help fill the two major reservoirs of the country , " said Dr Rasul . <p> Talking about snowfall in Murree and upper parts of Pakistan , he said , " It is a normal thing . Sometimes these areas do receive rainfall in April . " <p> However because of the snowfall in Murree , the mercury level in Islamabad , Rawalpindi and in adjourning areas went down by 10 degrees Celsius , he said . <p> Moreover , Islamabad and Rawalpindi were hit by a strong windstorm Thursday night .
