His canvases have a unique impressionistic and realistic mix character painted with the attention to aesthetics , technique and style . He has adopted an appealing technique for topographical landscapes . <p> Ajab Khan has not attended any art school , but has gained popularity for his naturalistic landscape paintings of rural countryside . He appears to have strong and intimate observation of nature . His compositions usually have very pleasant scenes with aesthetic standard . Besides Pakistan , he has also exhibited in group shows at Canada , Germany , Italy and KSA . <p> Gul-e-Shazma is a graduate of 2015 from the Centre of Excellence in Arts and Design , Jamshoro . He takes inspirations from the beauty of colours and their chromatic effects in nature . " It is always the light that I remember most about any location , " he states . His medium is watercolor on paper and he loves to capture his compositions in the spontaneous movement of the brush on paper without any possibility of making corrections -- a challenge that he loves . <p> Mughees Riaz , a faculty member at Punjab University , is renowned for his aesthetically composed landscapes of the river Ravi and its surroundings that are popular among art collectors . He is among those few painters who opt to paint outdoors rather than in their studios . He derives great enjoyment witnessing the influence of changing light on colours in the surroundings . He then carefully captures the details in the visual field and creates the same life and spirit . <p> Sarfaraz Musawir graduated in 1998 and has held several solo exhibitions and group shows , including those in Bahrain , Thailand , Turkey , UAE and UK . His medium is watercolours and he loves to work with a watery brush . Hence , he noticeably maintains the special characteristics of transparency and fluidity of the medium with classy strokes . The subject of his two paintings are the streets of Lahore with interesting architecture on both sides . <p> Shahla Rafi has exhibited in France and UK since 1991 and occasionally in Pakistan at Gallery6 since 2010 . He has received much praise for his amazing landscapes done with thick layers of paints applied through the palette knife to capture the dramatic effects of light . His work creates an impression akin to carving . The gallery introduced him to art lovers last year through an exhibition on climate change . <p> Zulfiqar Ali Zulfi is also a graduate of NCA Lahore and a well-established landscape painter . One finds an element of spirituality on his canvasses , with gentle soft light pouring in the scene .
