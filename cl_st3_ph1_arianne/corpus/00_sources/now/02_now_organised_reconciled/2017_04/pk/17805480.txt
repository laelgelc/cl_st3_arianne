<p> Samar Khan is the first woman to have cycled on the 4,500 metre Biafo Glacier in the Karakoram Range in Gilgit-Baltistan <p> Samar Khan is first woman to have cycled on the 4,500 metre Biafo Glacier in the Karakoram Range in Gilgit-Baltistan . She set off for Biafo from Islamabad , cycling over 1000 kilometres in 15 days . In 2016 , Samar and fellow cyclist Gule Afshan Tariq cycled from Islamabad to Khunjerab , travelling a distance of 1,098km . <p> The 26 year old is also a trained mixed martial arts ( MMA ) fighter , blogger and motivational speaker . <p> Dawn spoke to Samar about her experience long-distance cycling in Pakistan . <h> How did you first become interested in long-distance cycling and what took you to Biafo Glacier ? <p> Samar Khan : I became a cyclist almost accidentally . Growing up in Rawalpindi , I never had many chances of playing sports beyond my teenage years . But in my final year of university I went through a difficult emotional experience . I was in a bad relationship and I felt rejected . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The course made me interested in adventure sports and I also made friends who were interested in adventure sports . Together , we decided to cycle from Islamabad to Abbottabad . <p> Later some friends from Hunza and Gilgit suggested that I cycle in the northern areas where people are more open-minded and used to seeing women tourists . So , for my next trip I decided to cycle from Islamabad to Khunjerab with my friend Gule Afshan Tariq . <p> Cycling along the Karakoram Highway over 10 days helped me overcome my fear of road cycling and once I had conquered my fears I was ready for a new challenge . After many months of research , I decided to cycle on the Biafo Glacier because I wanted to draw attention to Pakistan 's glaciers , which are threatened by climate change . <h> Not many women can be seen riding bicycles in Pakistan .
