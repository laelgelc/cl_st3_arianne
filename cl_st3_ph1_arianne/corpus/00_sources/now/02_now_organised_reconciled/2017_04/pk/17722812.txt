<p> DG ISPR Major General Asif Ghafoor in his tweet said Imran Khan congratulated General Qamar Javed Bajwa on his promotion and appointment as the Army Chief . <p> " I am happy that the Khyber Pukhtunkhawa government has taken up this momentous task of dealing with climate change and has taken a hands-on approach towards it , " he said . <p> " I know it is hard to trust our statistics after the government regularly lies to you , but I can assure you the project will end by the end of 2017 and one billion trees will have been pirated by that time , " he said about the tree project . <p> Imran Khan and General Bajwa also discussed various other matters at the meeting , DG ISPR added .
