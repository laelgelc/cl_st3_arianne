<p> Thousands of people across the US have marched to demand action on climate change on Donald Trump 's 100th day in office <p> In Washington DC , large crowds made their way down Pennsylvania Avenue in sweltering heat and planned to encircle the White House . <p> Organisers say about 300 other protest marches are expected around the country . <p> Participants in the Peoples Climate March said they 're objecting to Mr Trump 's roll-back of restrictions on mining , oil drilling and greenhouse gas emissions at coal-fired power plants , among other things . <p> In Augusta , Maine , protesters outside the statehouse said they wanted to draw attention to the damage climate change can cause marginalised communities . <p> A demonstration stretched for several blocks in the centre of Tampa , Florida , where marchers said they were concerned about the threat rising sea levels pose to the city . <p> In Boston , a crowd gathered in a public park and carried signs with slogans such as " Dump Trump " .
