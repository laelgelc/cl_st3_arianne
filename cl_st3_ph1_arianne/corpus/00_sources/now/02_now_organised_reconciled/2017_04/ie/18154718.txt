<h> Expert climate change team sets sail for Galway from Canada <p> Galway Bay fm newsroom -- An international team of scientists is setting sail for Galway from Canada today aboard the Celtic Explorer . The group is on a trans-Atlantic voyage to study the impact of climate change on the ocean . Departing from St. John 's Newfoundland , the team is due to arrive in Galway on the 23rd of May . The Marine Institute-led team of experts are surveying a transect of the Atlantic Ocean last surveyed 20 years ago to investigate carbon dioxide levels in the ocean . The survey is essential to understand and project how carbon dioxide emissions are accumulated in the oceans and the atmosphere , as well as its effects on the acidification of the ocean .
