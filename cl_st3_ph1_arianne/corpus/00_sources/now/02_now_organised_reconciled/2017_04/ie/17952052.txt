The two most dominant sectors ( outside the emissions trading schemes ) are agriculture and transport . With a growing economy , both are expected to increase . <p> The FoodWise 2025 policy is the main plank of Irish agricultural policy . Unless there is a dramatic breakthrough in technology it will lead to increased activity in the poultry and pig sectors , as well as an increased use of fertilisers . As yet , no " fix " has been found for that . <p> Equally , transport emissions are set to increase by almost 13 per cent between 2106 and 2020 .
