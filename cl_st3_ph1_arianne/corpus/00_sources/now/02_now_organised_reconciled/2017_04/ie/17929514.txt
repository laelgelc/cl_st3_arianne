<h> BDGP scheme to reopen for applications next week <p> It has been confirmed that the Beef Data and Genomics Programme ( BDGP ) is set to reopen for applications from beef suckler farmers , who are not already members of the scheme , next week . <p> The Minister for Agriculture , Food and the Marine , Michael Creed recently announced the re-opening of the programme . <p> The scheme will be known as BDGP II and will , like the original , commit to six years of payments to farmers for completion of actions -- aimed at delivering accelerated genetic improvements in the national herd and improvements in its environmental sustainability . <p> The success of the BDGP scheme over the course of its first two years has led to many calls for the programme to be re-opened , Minister Creed said . <p> Its benefits in driving both genetic improvement and sustainability of our beef farms are now well recognised . <p> " I have listened to these requests and examined the potential for re-opening the scheme in the context of the overall Rural Development Programme and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ scheme will be re-opened . <p> " This is a significant investment in the suckler sector and a real vote of confidence by this Government in its future , " he said . <h> Details of the scheme <p> The scheme is set to require farmers to complete the same actions as in the original BDGP over the course of six years , starting in 2017 and concluding in 2022 . <p> There will be a small change to the timing of the training and carbon navigator requirements , according to Minister Creed . <p> " From listening to the feedback from farmers in BDGP I , it became clear that the training and carbon navigator actions were found to be extremely helpful to farmers in understanding the actions that they would need to take in order to satisfy the requirements of the BDGP . <p> It is as a result of this feedback that I have decided to bring the training and carbon navigator actions forward to year one of this scheme . <p> " I am anxious to ensure as many farmers as possible avail of this opportunity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a world 's first in its use of technology and genomics to produce better ,
