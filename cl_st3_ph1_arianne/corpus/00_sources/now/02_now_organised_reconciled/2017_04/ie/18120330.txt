<p> Galway Bay fm newsroom -- Climate change and the threat it poses to the global food supply is up for discussion at a major conference in Galway this week . More than 150 of the world 's leading experts are meeting to debate everything from greenhouse gas emissions , increasing world populations , along with pressures on the agriculture industry . They say the answers will be found in multiple scientific disciplines , new technology and educating Government ministers . The event is hosted by the NUI Galway Plant and AgriBiosciences Research Centre . The body is a key strategic partner in the global climate change , agriculture and food security research program .
