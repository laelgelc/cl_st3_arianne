<h> 28 ' global citizens ' are being honoured in Dublin today . <p> IRELAND HAS A strong tradition of people volunteering abroad , something that stretches back decades . <p> Many changes have happened nationally and internationally in the 53 years that EIL Intercultural Learning has been in operation , but its goal has remained the same . <p> The non-profit organisation based in Cork aims to bring different cultures together through various volunteering and study abroad programmes . <p> In 2016 , EIL teamed up with similar organisations and charities , such as UCD Volunteers Overseas , Suas , Development Perspectives and Serve , to develop the Global Citizen Award ( GCA ) . <p> The GCA aims to recognise the work done by volunteers abroad and also encourages them to stay involved with social justice issues once they return to Ireland . <p> Kevin Hickey , CEO of EIL Ireland , says the awards " build on the very strong tradition of volunteering abroad that has been in Ireland for several generations " . <p> " In general , Irish people have a hunger to a make @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in EIL was that when people return to Ireland after volunteering abroad , they often come back quite deflated and feel like there is so much more that they should do but are not sure how . <p> " The GCA helps them to give a voice to the people they met when volunteering abroad . " <p> Some 28 people will receive awards at the GCA ceremony in Dublin city today -- gold , silver and bronze .
