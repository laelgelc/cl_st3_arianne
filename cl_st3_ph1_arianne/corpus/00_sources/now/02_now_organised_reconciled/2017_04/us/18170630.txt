<p> Super-high king tides hammered homes and roads in Marin County , California this past winter , and residents can expect worse as rising seas threaten to super-soak their lives . If nothing is done to adapt , highways and utility connections eight miles inland in the mostly rural Bay Area county could get doused within 15 years , on top of hundreds of buildings inundated closer to shore . Tens of thousands of residents could be affected . <p> So when Marin County wanted to invite the public into the process of planning for that hazardous future two years ago , officials decided to try something different .
