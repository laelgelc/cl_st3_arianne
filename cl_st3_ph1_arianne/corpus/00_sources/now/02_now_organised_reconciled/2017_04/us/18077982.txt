<p> The science of politics was under a microscope in downtown Los Angeles Saturday as tens of thousands of people took part in the nationwide March for Science . <p> Demonstrators packed Pershing Square in the morning for a pro-science rally and then marched through the streets to the steps of City Hall . <p> The march synched up with Earth Day and took place in cities around the world . Scientists and their work are on the defensive . <p> " There 's been an anti-science undercurrent for a long time , but it 's really come to a head . And it 's time to listen to the science , it 's time to listen to the facts . Facts are facts , there are no alternative facts . The truth is the truth and science is the best way to understand the truth , " organizer Philip Wheeler said . <p> Organizers highlighted what they call the growing trend to politicize science .
