<h> ' The Book Of Joan ' Recasts A Historic Heroine -- In Space <p> What does it mean to be human ? In Lidia Yuknavitch 's new novel The Book of Joan , what 's left of the human race is orbiting above the Earth , sexless and ageless , prisoners in a technological hell . Their lives are preserved through growing limbs and grafting skin . Presiding over it all is a one-time billionaire celebrity who evolved through media and technology into a despot . <p> His adversary is a girl called Joan ; Yuknavitch says she adapted the story of Joan of Arc to make her heroine " an eco-terrorist of sorts , although that name would depend on your point of view . She has allegiance to the planet , and diversity on the planet , including plants and animals and people . And as the story progresses , her allegiance turns into a question somewhat like " what 's the worth of humans , and what 's our relationship to the planet ?
