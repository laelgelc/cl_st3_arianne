<h> Malala named youngest ever UN Messenger of Peace <p> Nobel Peace Prize winner Malala Yousafzai has been named the youngest ever UN Messenger of Peace , with a special focus on girls ' education . <p> The 19-year-old Pakistani activist became known around the world after she was shot in the head and neck by the Taliban on her way home from school in 2012 . <p> She narrowly escaped death and , since her recovery , has continued campaigning for the rights of girls around the world to access education . <p> Yousafzai accepted the accolade -- the highest honor bestowed by the UN -- from UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres in New York Monday evening . <p> NEW YORK , NY -- APRIL 10 : ( L to R ) Malala Yousafzai listens as UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres speaks about her during a ceremony to name her as a United Nations Messenger of Peace at UN headquarters , April 10 , 2017 in New York City . " And I stand here again today and say the same thing . <p> " Once you educate girls , you change the whole community , you change the whole society . " <p> She also called on other girls from her home country to make their voices heard . <p> " You need to stand up ... you need to believe in yourselves , " she told them . " You are the real change-makers . If you do not stand up , change will not come ... It starts with us and it should start now . " <p> In a heartfelt speech , Guterres described Yousafzai as a " hero " and " the symbol of one of the most important causes in the world . Their role is to help focus global attention on the work and ideals of the United Nations . <p> Other Messengers of Peace include Leonardo DiCaprio , chosen for his work on climate change and other environmental issues , and Stevie Wonder , a committed campaigner for the rights of persons with disabilities .
