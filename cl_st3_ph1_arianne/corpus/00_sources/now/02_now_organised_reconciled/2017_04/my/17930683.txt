<p> German environmental aspirations are generally high , an annual survey shows . Turning them into reality , however , is often much more complicated . <p> Many Germans like the idea of fewer cars on the road , even if most are n't ready to give up their own . <p> That 's one takeaway from the latest survey of German attitudes toward the environment released on Wednesday .
