He died recently at 95 on Feb 21 , 2017 . <p> Leontief ( 1906-99 ) <p> Wassily Leontief was awarded the 1973 Nobel Prize for his analysis of the US production machinery . Using his ingenious input-output analysis ( I/OA ) , Leontief showed how changes in one sector of the economy can exact changes along the entire production chain . His method traces how all the parts are interconnected and how his theory can predict the consequences of changes in one part on another . <p> Leontief 's model has become a permanent part of planning and forecasting in all advanced nations and in private corporations around the world . What he developed was a mathematical description of a production system where some share of output from one industry will be used as input in another . He was able to track the inputs and outputs from every industry , the ultimate purpose being to produce goods and services for final demand , i.e. used for consumption or investment or export . No doubt , there is an elegant mathematical underpinning to I/OA but its success depends primarily on loads of detailed data . " Facts , " he said : " you have to have facts . Theories are n't good unless you have facts to back them . " <p> Leontief 's invention is unique . Unlike many others , he viewed the economy ( differently from Smith and Keynes ) as a woven fabric of complicated threads , connecting production units and geographical regions ( and later nations ) . He then devised a mathematical framework to pull everything together in a useful way to answer basic questions in everyday life . Indeed , Leontief 's I/OA even offered practical insights into understanding the effects of post-WWII demobilisation and re-armament for the Korean War &amp; other Cold War policy issues .
