<h> Vacancy at Federal University Of Technology , Minna , Nigeria <p> VACANCY FOR THE POST OF VICE- CHANCELLORPreamble It is hereby notified for the general information that the Post of Vice- Chancellor at the Federal University of Technology , Minna , Nigeria , will become vacant with effect from 3rd December , 2017 . Consequently , the Governing Council of the University , in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Universities ( Miscellaneous Provisions ) Act No 11 of 1993 , as amended by the Act No 25 of 1996 and the Universities ( Miscellaneous Provisions ) Amendment Act , 2003 , has approved that appropriate machinery be set in motion for the selection of a suitably qualified candidate to fill the vacancy.The University The Federal University of Technology , Minna , Nigeria , is one of the specialized third generation universities established in 1983 with the aim of creating high level manpower in the area of Science , Engineering and Technology . The University has over the years remained focused on its original mission and objectives . Today , the University has nine ( 9 ) Schools @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Centres . The University offers thirty- eight ( 38 ) programmes at Undergraduate , Masters and Doctorate levels . As at 2016/2017 session , it has a population of about 18,000 undergraduate and 2,000 postgraduate students with about 3,500 staff . The University in its quest to be globally renown has active collaboration with universities and specialized institutions in Nigeria , Africa , Asia , North America and Europe . It also has a Centre dedicated to the study of Climate Change funded as one of ten ( 10 ) Graduate Research Programmes spread among the West African Sub- Region by the German Ministry of Education and Research . The University in the recent past obtained a Five Million Dollar ( USD5.0 Million ) Grant from the World Bank with which it equipped a Centre for Genetic Engineering and Biotechnology and another One Million Dollars ( USD1.0 Million ) to improve its Fibre Optic Backbone for internet services .
