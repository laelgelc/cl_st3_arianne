<h> Canada continues efforts towards a clean economy <p> The Government of Canada continues to strive to be a global leader in clean technology by positioning Canada at the leading edge of a changing economy and ensuring that Canadians are able to access the jobs of the future . <p> The Honourable Navdeep Singh Bains , Minister of Innovation , Science and Economic Development , highlighted commitments from Budget 2017 that will boost the growth of Canada 's clean technology sector by making available more equity finance , working capital and project financing to promising clean technology firms . Nearly CAN$1.4 billion in new financing , on a cash basis , will be made available to help Canada 's clean technology firms grow and expand . <p> Budget 2017 also takes important steps to support the Pan-Canadian Framework on Clean Growth and Climate Change . Addressing climate change and protecting our environment represent an opportunity to support inclusive and sustainable economic growth . <p> Budget 2016 allocated CAN$16.4 million for the deployment of electric vehicle recharging and alternative fuel refuelling infrastructure .
