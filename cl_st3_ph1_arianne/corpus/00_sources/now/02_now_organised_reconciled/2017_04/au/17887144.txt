researchers sneaking up on the giant mammals to stick camera tags to their bodies by suction cups . <p> The Australian Antarctic Division ( AAD ) research is part of a long-term project into what and how humpback and minke whales eat , and how their patterns might be disturbed by issues including climate change and fishing . <p> AAD research scientist Dr Elanor Bell , who helps coordinate international non-lethal research into whales , said the research in the west Antarctic peninsula was part of a long-term ecological study into the foraging of humpback and minke whales . <p> At the
