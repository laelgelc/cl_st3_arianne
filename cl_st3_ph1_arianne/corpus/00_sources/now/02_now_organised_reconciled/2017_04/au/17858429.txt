<h> Change of climate among banks <p> The big four banks are reviewing their exposure to fossil fuels and considering new lending practices for mortgages and agriculture because of climate change impacts . <p> But some are leaving the door open to support next-generation coal-fired power . <p> The banks have provided written answers to questions put to them on climate policy by parliament 's economics committee . <p> While all four are committed to acting on climate change , some are keener than others to continue backing fossil fuels . <p> Asked about investing in new fossil fuel projects and coal-fired power , Westpac said it was " not practical " to stop financing certain activities or sectors , despite the bank seeing " climate change risk as a financial risk " . <p> This may change in the future but now a " balanced approach " was being taken , it said . <p> " We currently believe that our exposure to climate-related risks is appropriate but we will continue to review and refine our policies as the regulation , technology and science develop .
