<h> The week in finance - 29.04.17 <p> April 28 , 2017 <p> Published : Friday , April 28 , 2017 <p> Words by Paul Rickard <p> With stock markets in the US hitting new highs on the back of strong company earnings , and relief that centrist Emmanuel Macron is set to become the next President of France , the positive mood carried over into the Australian market . In a shortened trading week due to the Anzac Day holiday , the local share market added 70 points or 1.2% to close Friday at 5924 . <p> Banking stocks led the market higher , with each of the major banks adding around 2% . ANZ , which reports its half year profit on Tuesday , was the best , adding $0.95 to finish at $32.76 . <p> Resource stocks continued to ease , as metal and oil prices pulled back . BHP lost 1.3% , while Rio put on 0.3% . Impacting this sector was the Government 's announcement to intervene in the local gas market .
