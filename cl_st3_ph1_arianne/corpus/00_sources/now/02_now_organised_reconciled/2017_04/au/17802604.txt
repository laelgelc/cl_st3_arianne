<h> Qld dams to be upgraded to cope with floods <p> Dams across southeast Queensland will be upgraded over the next two decades to ensure they can cope with extreme rainfall and flooding events . <p> Wivenhoe and Somerset dams , northwest of Brisbane , are just a few of the bodies of water that will be upgraded over the next 18 years as part of Seqwater 's Dam Improvement Program . <p> Water Supply Minister Mark Bailey said some of the state 's dams were built in the middle of last century and needed upgrades to continue to meet safety standards . <p> ' As part of the program , a new study is underway to upgrade Wivenhoe and Somerset dams which includes considering options to improve the flood mitigation offered by both dams , ' he said in a statement on Wednesday . <p> ' Over the years we have seen advances in dam design and better understanding of the impacts of climate change , including extreme rainfall events and flooding , and this has meant that many dams across Australia need upgrades to address this .
