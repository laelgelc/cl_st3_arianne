<p> When the federal government embraced the concept of economic diplomacy back in 2014 the key pitch was that listening more to business would take foreign policy beyond traditional " peace and security " to " peace and prosperity " . <p> Well now the rhetoric faces its first big test as the country 's main business groups have replied with their ideas for the government 's foreign policy white paper . <p> The calls for a strong domestic economy to be the basis of a strong external economy might seem like the usual boilerplate . But dig deeper and the corporate lobbyists have produced eclectic ideas that do n't necessarily provide a clear path for the white paper team to take up the 2014 pitch . <p> New institutions can often be seeded in the fine print of an influential white paper .
