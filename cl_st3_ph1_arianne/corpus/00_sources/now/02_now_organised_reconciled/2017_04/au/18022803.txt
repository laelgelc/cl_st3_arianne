<p> NATIONAL Honey Month next month will showcase some sweet local events involving food and honey-based products . <p> Honey Month is a national bee and honey awareness campaign that promotes Australian beekeepers and their products to a broader public . <p> " One of the driving forces behind Honey Month is to educate the public about beekeeping and highlight the importance of honey to our environment and economy , " Maylands-based Honey I 'm Home Produce founder and Bees2Honey Industry Group secretary Blaine Campbell said . <p> The humble honeybee plays a vital role in the food security and food diversity for Australia . <p> " Surprising to many ; one in every three mouthfuls of food we eat relies on the honey bee for pollination of that food , " she said . <p> Ms Campbell said commercial beekeepers were declining in numbers and bees were under threat by loosening of bio-security measures , possible incursions of foreign diseases , climate change and through dwindling forest resources . <p> " One of the highlights in this year 's program will be the family-friendly sundowner at the Honey I @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by Don Hancey , WA Food Ambassador , " Ms Campbell said . <p> " Gelato champion three years running , Mimmo , will also be serving his fresh made waffles topped with a choice of more than 10 different types of honey gelato .
