<h> What Up With All The Tornadoes In The US So Far This Year ? <p> As of mid April , at least 570 tornadoes have been reported in the United States this year . So what 's going on there , America ? <p> Image : NOAA <p> As the NOAA notes in an agency release , the 2017 tornado season across the United States is off to an incredibly active start , at least compared to the last 25 years .
