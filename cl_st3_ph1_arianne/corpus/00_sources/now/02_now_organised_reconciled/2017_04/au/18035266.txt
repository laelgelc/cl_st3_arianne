<h> Farmers avoid loss with insured crabs , tea <p> Ma Rongda , a tea grower for nearly 30 years , knows how badly his business can be affected by extreme weather . But when heavy snow and cold hit his tea garden in early 2016 , Ma was not as worried as he used to be . <p> " I bought insurance for my tea plantations , " he explained . <p> For an annual premium of some 7,000 yuan ( $A1,347 ) , the 46-year-old received compensation of more than 220,000 yuan - enough to cover all of his production costs . <p> This is the second year that Ma and his fellow farmers in eastern China 's Anji County have had access to insurance . Before that , no policies were available for Chinese tea growers looking to secure their crops . <p> As erratic weather has become the new norm in China , insurance policies against losses from extreme weather have emerged in a wide range of agricultural businesses , from beekeeping to cattle ranching to seaweed farming .
