<h> ' Transition to green growth brings both merits , demerits ' <h> The Foreign Ministry hosted the conference in collaboration with the Danish Embassy in Dhaka <p> Foreign Minister AH Mahmood Ali said transition to green growth brings Bangladesh assorted benefits and at the same time it poses many challenges to the country . <p> " While pursuing green growth through green industries , it is a key question for the developing countries whether or not they will compromise the economic growth and prosperity they are seeking , " he told the inaugural session of a conference on green growth solutions , which was aimed at nurturing and creating atmosphere for the Bangladesh economy to keep growing in light of the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> The Foreign Ministry hosted the conference in collaboration with the Danish Embassy in Dhaka on Wednesday as the Nordic Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Metropolitan Chamber of Commerce and Industry joined hands with the ministry as its partners . <p> Mahmood said : " There is growing evidence that attaining cost-effective and sustained economic growth is possible through @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ combined with appropriate green technology and eco-innovation . " <p> He , however , added that the country 's economy was now at crossroads , which needed further growth acceleration to achieve a middle-income country status by 2021 . <p> The minister conceded that it was not possible overnight . " We have to go miles before we reach a satisfactory level of green growth . " <p> Bangladesh is pursuing a two-pronged strategy to deal with climate change : building resilience to effects of climate change and working towards achieving a low carbon , climate resilient development , he said .
