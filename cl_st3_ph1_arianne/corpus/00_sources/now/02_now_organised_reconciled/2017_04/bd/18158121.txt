<h> ' Mind the Earth : A New Perspective ' comes to Dhaka <h> The international photo exhibition , ' Mind the Earth : A New Perspective ' , has arrived in Dhaka with its founder and curator Kasper Brejnholt Bak , having reached out to global audiences in Copenhagen , Paris and Marrakech . <p> The Embassy of Denmark in Bangladesh , in partnership with Edge , The Foundation presents this thought-provoking journey , scheduled to open to the public on Friday at Edge Gallery and will be running till May 6 . <p> Admission is free for all and the exhibition will be open every day from 10am to 8pm at the Bay 's Edgewater Building on 12 North Avenue , Gulshan 2 . <p> The photos at the exhibition raise consciousness about planet Earth , how it has naturally come to be as it is today and how we , its inhabitants , continue to change its face , for the better and for worse . <p> ' Mind the Earth ' has been exhibited at COP21 in Paris and COP22 in Marrakech , the most important @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the exhibition is the intertwined subjects of sustainable development and climate change , explored through striking photos secured via Google Earth 's satellites . <p> Mind the Earth , a non-profit organisation , raises awareness about the challenges the world is facing in terms of climate change , population growth , urbanization and management of the limited resources of the planet in a broader sense through exhibitions and publications .
