<h> In the spirit of victory <h> Let us remember the strength and determination that brought us our victory , and apply it to the challenges we face today <p> Today we celebrate the 45th year of our victory , a day marked in the calendar to commemorate the martyrs who had made the ultimate sacrifice to build an independent Bangladesh . <p> As we remember our history and celebrate the unimaginable courage of our freedom fighters , let us not forget the milestones Bangladesh has achieved in the last 45 years -- we have excelled in an array of fields , from establishing our dynamic RMG sector , making great strides towards the Sustainable Development Goals , and increasing our per capita income . <p> Over the past years , Bangladesh has also moved to new heights in terms of organising and promoting festivals of literature and music , bringing people together , and showcasing our arts to the world . <p> While we celebrate the spirit of independence , we also need to gear up to face the challenges at hand . Climate change , migration policies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ though we have made great progress in these spheres , there is still a long way to go . <p> With a young population , and multiple youth leadership initiatives and programs in motion , Bangladesh has a great chance to move ahead and take advantage of the demographic dividend . <p> The history of our nine month-long Liberation War is a true testament to our people 's strength and determination to not bend or bow down to force . Let us remember the strength and determination that brought us our victory , and apply it to the challenges we face today . <p> As we look back on our various accomplishments from the last 45 years , let us vow to build a greater nation of diversity , potential , and resilience.
