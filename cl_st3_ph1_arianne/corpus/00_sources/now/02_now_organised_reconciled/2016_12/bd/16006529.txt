<h> Big projects taken to address rural development <h> Several development programs are aiming to transform rural areas some as soon as 2020 , said speakers at Communicating for Development : Rural Transformation ' held at Brac Centre , yesterday . <p> Economics Professor of Dhaka University Abul Barakat said that poverty is measured by 15 units such as food security , education , health and employment . <p> His assessment of rural and urban poverty rates were : " The poverty percentage is higher than the government run figure . " <p> Experts discussed 6 different poverty alleviation projects run by IFAD in Bangladesh . <p> Proffessor Abul also said : " In Bangladesh 75% of the total population live in villages where government and development organizations need to take 5 to 20 year long mega projects to develop them . " <p> Under the ' Promoting Agricultural Commercialization and Enterprises ' ( PACE ) project , the Bangladesh government and funded by International Fund for Agricultural Development ( IFAD ) are implementing 3 project to help the poorest and the most marginalized people across the country . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ total 4 , 52,000 people will receive financial and technical support and IFAD will fund USA $40 million . <p> Another government initiative called ' Rural Radio Initiative ' ( RRI ) , funded by IFAD is working in coastal areas to build awareness about climate change , agriculture and the environment through radio shows . <p> Ministry of Local Government , rural development and cooperatives and local government division are implementing another project with ADB , IFAD and KfW called ' Coastal Climate Resilience Infrastructure Project ' . <p> People from mainly 12 coastal areas will receive training and technical support and enhance their accessibility through development of climate resilience infrastructure and socio-economic opportunities , and people 's safety , as outlined by the project overview . <p> Rural poverty in the coastal areas is estimated to be 35% to 39% , is higher than the national average and the study read that by 2050 climate change could impact that percentage by 14% . <p> While 87% of the roads in the country would be substantially inundated due to climate change by 2050 . <p> Farhana Haque Rahman @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Shameem Reza of Dhaka University also addressed the programme .
