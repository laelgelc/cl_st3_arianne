<h> Pacific students encouraged to go for STEM scholarships <p> New Zealand 's Pacific Peoples Minister Peseta Sam Lotu-Iiga is calling for Pacific tertiary students to take up science , technology , engineering and mathematics . <p> Photo : RNZ / Alexander Robertson <p> He said the government introduced the Toloa Scholarships last year to encourage more of what are known as the STEM subjects to be studied by Pasifika students . <p> Mr Lotu-Iiga said by combining STEM skills with Pacific values and creativity , New Zealand 's Pacific youth can play key roles in addressing challenges such as climate change , digital connectivity and resource management . <p> He said higher qualifications tend to lead to higher earnings and better long term prospects for Pacific families and communities and there is $US570,000 dollars available over four years with a minimum of eight scholarships a year .
