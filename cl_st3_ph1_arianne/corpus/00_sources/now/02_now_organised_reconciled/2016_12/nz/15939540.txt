To satisfy this purpose , he can be expected to promote some ministers and challenge them with tasks that he might want undertaken with greater energy or better performance . <p> The other mission English will want to accomplish is to construct a team which he can confidently expect to lead into the next election , possibly towards the end of 2017 . <p> The new Prime Minister will not want to announce a lineup only to find sometime next year that his initial appointments were mistaken and that the job he assigned was beyond the capacity of his choice . <p> The odds of this outcome are probably low .
