Paris agreement enters into force faster than expected <p> The Paris Agreement officially entered into force on November 4 , less than a year after it was adopted in the French capital . To take effect , it needed 55 parties representing 55 per cent of global emissions to ratify the treaty -- that is , to formally consent to be bound by the agreement . This threshold was crossed on October 6 . <p> Small island states such as the Maldives , Marshall Islands , Fiji and Samoa , which are especially vulnerable to climate change , were among the first to ratify the agreement in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , China , and India did so in September and October .
