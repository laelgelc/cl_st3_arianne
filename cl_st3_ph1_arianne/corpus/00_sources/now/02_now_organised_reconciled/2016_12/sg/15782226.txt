( C40 photo ) <p> MEXICOCITY -- Embattled mayors from the United States , still reeling from the surprise election of Donald Trump , received advice last week from counterparts around the world who have weathered comparable political storms . <p> Gathered in the Mexican capital for an international mayors summit on climate change , leaders from Sydney , Vancouver and elsewhere offered lessons from their own experiences with national governments skeptical of coordinated action on climate change . <p> " We had a Trump-style leader , " said Sydney Mayor Clover Moore , referring to Tony Abbott , a vocal climate-change sceptic who was Australia 's prime minister from 2013 to 2015 . Although he has started to walk back some of those stances since the election , Trump 's pronouncements have raised deep concerns in U. S. cities , which had taken a lead on mitigating the causes of climate change even before President Barack Obama signed on to the Paris Agreement in September . <p> Counsel from Moore and others came during the C40 Mayors Summit , a biennial gathering .
