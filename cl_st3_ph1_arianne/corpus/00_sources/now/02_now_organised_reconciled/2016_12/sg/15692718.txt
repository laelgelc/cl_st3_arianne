A devastating drought in the Central Highlands -- the country 's primary coffee growing area -- earlier this year will likely hurt annual harvest figures . Laderach asserts that such severe weather events , known as climate variability , will become more common in the future . <p> " The dry season is predicted to get longer , so those dry spells are becoming increasingly normal , " he said in an interview with Mongabay .
