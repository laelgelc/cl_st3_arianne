SINGAPORE : Keppel Infrastructure Holdings will design , build , own and operate Singapore 's fourth desalination plant , national water agency PUB announced on Thursday ( Dec 22 ) . <p> The company was chosen as the preferred bidder after an open tender that attracted 16 bids from seven local and international bidders , PUB said in a statement . <p> The plant is expected to commence operations in 2020 and will add another 30 million gallons of water ( about 137,000 cubic metres ) per day to the nation 's water supply . <p> Under the arrangement with PUB , Keppel will supply water to PUB for a 25-year concession period at a first-year price of S$1.07867 per cubic metre . <p> " AN ICONIC PROJECT " <p> Located in Marina East , the plant will be the first in Singapore with the ability to plant an " iconic project " due to its ambitions of treating both reservoir and sea water . <p> PUB said the plant will help to strengthen Singapore 's drought resilience as well as enhance the reliability of water supply for the city and eastern Singapore areas by providing an alternative supply to these areas . <p> According to PUB 's Deputy Chief Executive of Policy and Development Chua Soon Guan , desalinated water is an important part of Singapore 's water supply portfolio . <p> " As a source independent of weather fluctuations , it is resilient against the vagaries of climate
