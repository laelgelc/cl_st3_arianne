Sustainably managed mangrove forests further provide firewood , food , as well as spawning grounds for fish . <h> Empowerment and community ownership <p> As we combat climate change , forgetting to engage local communities and empowering them in the process would be a major faux pas . These communities have , over centuries , developed practices that protect the natural resources on which they rely for their survival . <p> Using such knowledge will increase our chances of protecting ecosystems , which in turn will help us mitigate the impacts of climate change , and cope with its aftermath .
