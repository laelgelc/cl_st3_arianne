<h> Services <h> A multitude of problems threaten to swamp the middle class <p> RAYMOND Hall ( Letters , December 17 ) is right to be worried about the future of the middle class in Scotland . Taxation may be a problem in the short term but there are much greater threats waiting in the wings . American home-based manufacturing production has remained constant during the last decade whilst numbers working in the factories have declined by 25 per cent . It is machines , not Mexicans , which are the real cause of the rage that brought Donald Trump to the White House . <p> During the next 20 years automation , robots and new computing power will destroy thousands of jobs the middle class has made its own since the Second World War . The main victims will be in finance , law , accountancy and retail . People working in these sectors who live in the leafy suburbs of Edinburgh and Glasgow , with their shiny new cars in the drive , will not be replaced when they retire .
