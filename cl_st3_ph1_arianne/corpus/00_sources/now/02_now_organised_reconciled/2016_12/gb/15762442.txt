<p> When the skies open up and deluge an area , the results can be catastrophic , with roads washed out and homes destroyed by the resulting flash floods . Such extreme downpours are already occurring more often across the US , but a new study finds that as global temperatures rise , storms could dump considerably more rain and skyrocket in frequency . <p> The study , in the journal Nature Climate Change , suggests that storms that now occur about once a season could happen five times a season by the century 's end , a 400% increase . <p> And when such storms do occur , they could produce up to 70% more rain .
