<p> Polar bear numbers could drop by a third in the next 40 years because of melting Arctic sea ice , according to scientists . <p> Experts have calculated there is a 71% likelihood that polar bear numbers will be reduced by more than 30% in three generations . <p> This means the current population of some 26,000 bears could be cut to around 17,300 over 35 to 41 years . <p> The findings are consistent with the animals being listed as " vulnerable " on the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) red list of threatened and endangered species . <p> The loss of sea ice caused by climate change is having a direct impact on the ability of polar bears to feed and survive . <p> The bears need platforms of ice to reach their prey of ringed and bearded seals .
