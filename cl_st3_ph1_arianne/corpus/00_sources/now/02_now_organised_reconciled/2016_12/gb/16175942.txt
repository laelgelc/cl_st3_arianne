<p> After the events of 2016 , foreseeing the year ahead may be foolhardy . Best perhaps to abandon the task of objective prediction in favour of the search for hope . Is there any chance that those who accept complexity rather than demand false certainty , that those who search for solutions not for blame , could get their act together ? <p> Could 2017 be the year of the moderate insurgency ? <p> Such an insurgency needs help from its enemies . Expect " betrayal " to be on many lips as the year unfolds .
