<h> Boko Haram blamed for the death of at least 20,000 people and the displacement of millions . <p> At least 400,000 children are now at risk of starvation in Nigeria 's north-eastern states of Borno , Adamawa and Yobe , the United Nations has warned . North-eastern Nigeria has been ravaged by a seven-year-long insurgency by Boko Haram terror group . <p> The UN defined the ongoing humanitarian emergency as the biggest crisis facing Africa today , with 75,000 children at risk of imminent death . <p> Boko Haram started carrying out attacks in Borno in 2009 . However , the conflict soon spilled over into neighbouring states Cameroon , Chad and Niger . <p> The group is today blamed for the death of at least 20,000 people and the displacement of millions in the Lake Chad region . <p> The region , one of the poorest areas of the world , is witnessing a deep humanitarian crisis exacerbated by the negative effects of climate change .
