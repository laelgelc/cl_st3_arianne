<h> DA-OPAS , E. Samar continues to air University on the Air program <p> December 14 , 2016 <p> Samuel D. Candido <p> BORONGAN CITY , Eastern Samar , December 14 , 2016 ( PIA ) - Since the rice production in Eastern Samar is still inadequate , the University on the Air program of the Office of the Provincial Agricultural Services ( OPAS ) decided to focus its studies on rice production . This program is made possible through the support of Department of Agriculture- Regional Office 08 ( DA-RO 08 ) and DYES Radyo Ng Bayan Borongan . <p> According to OIC-Provincial Agriculturist Dr. Deogracias Paano , the rice sufficiency level of the province is approximately 68% only . That 's why it still needs to be augmented to meet the rice demand of Estehanons and that Eastern Samar will not import rice anymore from outside the province . <p> He also cited some reasons why the province 's rice production remains low.He said only 14% of farmland is irrigated where rice needs approximately 30% of water and 70% other inputs for its growth . His challenge to farmers then is to be open to modern studies on how to manage rice now that the effects of climate change are now being felt . <p> In the message of Mr. Francis Rosaroso of DA-RO 08 , he emphasized the importance of of the University on the Air as the study is discussed on air . The aired program is not just in Eastern Samar but is also aired in radio stations in Tacloban City , Catbalogan City , Catarman , Northern Samar ; and Sogod Southern Leyte . These programs are mostly heard on the radio at 5-6 A.M , three times a week . <p> Efforts were made to continue a study such as the University on the Air since based on studies , radio remains to be the most effective and the best channel of communication since information will be heard in the remotest area -- even though there @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The name of the program has levelled up as well -- it 's not just School on the Air but University on the Air already since the scope of this study has already widened , aside from the fact that they are following a module so that its purpose will be achieved by the listeners . <p> He also said that the DA-RO 08 led the implementation of the program to respond to the rice demand of Estehanons . A lot of work still needs to be done -- not just on the part of the farmers but to the ones who implements this as well . <p> This study on the air is an instrument where rice farmers learn knowledge and information within two months . <p> Some important topics discussed is on climate change and Association of South East Asia Nations ( ASEAN ) integration or the agreement of ASEAN nations that they will only have one market for their products -- no tariff will be collected by every government or free trade .
