<h> Nina is most devastating typhoon in Albay after Reming <p> December 29 , 2016 <p> LEGAZPI CITY , ALBAY , Dec. 29 -- Typhoon " Nina " left municipalities in Albay in a wrecked state after it hit the province last Christmas Day , December 25 , 2016 . <p> According to Tiwi , Albay Mayor Jaime C. Villanueva , " Nina " was the " strongest typhoon " to hit Albay in 10 years after " Reming " . Tiwi is one of the heavily damaged municipalities in Albay which is the nearest town to Catanduanes province where it made its first landfall . It also neighbours the town of Sangay , Camarines Sur where the typhoon made its second landfall . <p> Sangay which is neighbouring Tiwi , Albay was hit with maximum sustained winds of 175 kph near the center and gustiness of up to 290 kph . <p> Albay Gov .
