<p> If we go by the 2013 Annual Survey of Business and Industry , the average manufacturing establishment is small , with 48 employees . Less than 1% of manufacturing establishments are large according to the 2013 List of Establishments . SMEs comprise 12% . These mean that almost 9 out of 10 are micro manufacturing establishments that employ less than 10 people . <p> We have to recognize how we got into this position , so that we can frame our response to the constraint better . I would like to put it out there so we remember , that three decades of inappropriate policies resulted in the almost halving of our industrial sector . These policies , including aggressive trade liberalization early on , have shifted the structure of incentives , made capital lose markets , and adjust in such a way that they trooped to the sectors relatively insulated from liberalization -- real estate , utilities , and services . <p> The specific constraints are the same across economic sectors -- the inadequacy of economic infrastructure particularly in public works , energy and telecommunications ; the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the domestic market and the difficulty to access foreign markets ; and the constant need to upgrade skills and innovation . Addressing all this has even become more urgent by new challenges we face like climate change and the growing imperative for alternative energy sources and the need to reorient transportation priorities . <p> The ultimate question is how to restore a bigger share of manufacturing and industry in the national economy . Here the importance of industrial policy and planning need to be reiterated . <p> This involves multiple levels -- the large national scale and the much smaller local level . A related point is that while horizontal interventions are needed to address the binding constraints , industrial policy and planning necessarily imply industry- and sector-specific interventions . Action for Economic Reforms ( AER ) , through its Industrial Policy program , has been engaging both levels . <p> On the large national scale , the example of copper is instructive . In the country , the upstream ( mining and smelting ) and the downstream ( copper wires and electrical appliances ) thrive .
