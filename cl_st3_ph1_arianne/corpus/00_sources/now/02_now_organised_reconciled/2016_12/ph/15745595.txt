Besides , energy investments last for decades , and companies may well be loth to bet that future presidents will stick with Trump 's policies . <p> China takes air pollution in its cities at least as seriously as it does climate change : A recent study suggests that air pollution contributes to the deaths of 1.6 million people in China each year . India needs climate action as insurance against extreme weather , because it spends a fortune in the wake of storms , floods and other events . <p> Commercial self-interest also will keep other countries on the path toward decarbonization .
