<p> For more than an hour , the deep waters were a portrait of an efficient assembly line : one diver , using a steel brush , scrubbed the rocks , while another hammered on nails . A third diver planted a coral fragment beside the nails , aided by a fourth diver who fastened it to the nails using a zip tie . <p> Then the final touch : the diver glued the coral fragment to the rock to ensure it stayed in place , using Pioneer epoxy clay . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> This sequence followed coral restoration technology , said Virna Salac , national coordinator of the Department of Science and Technology 's coral restoration program . The detached coral fragments were gathered from the reef and grown in coral nurseries in shallow portions of the HINP . <p> ' Reef Fix ' <p> Corals are invertebrate animals which form into colonies we know as coral reefs . These reefs are vital marine ecosystems where water animals rely on food and shelter . The project was co-sponsored by the Alaminos City government , the DOST and the Pangasinan State University . <p> When the divers surfaced , they had transplanted 2,500 coral fragments off the Camantiles , Sison and the Mayor 's islands . <p> Retired police officer Manuel Velasco was able to transplant around 100 fragments and said he was happy to have contributed to their rehabilitation . <p> To cover a larger underwater area , the divers were grouped into five teams . Each team was assigned a quadrant spanning 90 square meters , said Martina Spakowski , executive director of the Pioneer Adhesives Foundation Inc . <p> Spakowski said they were supposed to transplant 5,000 coral fragments , but organizers decided to cut the target in half as it could be risky to send 50 divers under the sea at one time . <p> " But we will return in January to transplant the remaining 2,500 fragments to fulfill our promise to the city , " she said . <p> New plantation <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Acropora branching species that were planted two years ago through an earlier program sponsored by DOST , PSU and the city government . <p> Salac said the new plantation would be turned over to the city government in February next year . <p> On Dec. 14 , about a thousand volunteers planted mangrove seedlings at the coastal city 's mangrove areas as part of coral reef protection . The mangrove forests act as a shield that prevents sediment from flowing into the water where corals are propagating . <p> The volunteers were comprised of teachers , students , local government officials and employees , members of non-government organizations and church workers .
