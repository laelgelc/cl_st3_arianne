<h> Wish upon a Christmas star <p> Every year we all get tied up in the vibe , frenzy , and sometimes anxiety of Christmas . Everybody is so caught up with the season -- rushing around shopping , decorating , wrapping gifts , attending parties , getting stuck in traffic , complaining about the traffic -- we hardly find the time to stop and think how there are so many people out there still unable to do the same . ( Even the complaining , to be honest ) Spreading cheer at Christmastime is not the only means to celebrate the coming of Jesus Christ . A solemn wish , for those who seek and advocate for fairness , sympathy , respect , support , and recognition from society , can speak volumes about the true message of Christmas : peace , solidarity , salvation . No need for lavish presents and efforts , just the thought that Christmas is for everyone , everywhere .
