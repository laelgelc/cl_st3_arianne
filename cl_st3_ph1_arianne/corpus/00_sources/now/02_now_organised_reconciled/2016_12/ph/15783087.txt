<h> Environment bureau vows to sustain ' Green Schools ' drive <p> EMB 's Finance and Administrative Division Chief Maribel Munsayac emphasized the value of raising environmental awareness among school children and youth , engaging them in climate change adaptation activities . <p> " We want to raise awareness on the different environmental issues that has been confronting our country to young Filipinos , " Munsayac said . <p> The Green Schools campaign , launched two years ago , have been pushing for promotion of environmental protection , institutionalization of solid waste management programs , and recycling . <p> This is on top of tree planting program , campus gardening , and other greening initiatives . <p> In November , the Department of Environment and Natural Resources-EMB led three environmental events -- Environmental Awareness Month , Clean Air month , and National Climate Change Consciousness Week . <p> All activities were anchored on the theme : " Green schools ... building climate resilient and sustainable communities .
