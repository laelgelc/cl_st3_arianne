<h> Unrealized bamboo bonanza <p> Because only one third of one percent of the P30 billion spent by the National Greening Program over the last six years was on bamboo , the potential bonanza from bamboo was not realized . <p> Bonanza is defined by the Oxford Dictionary as " a situation or event that creates a sudden increase in wealth , good fortune , or profits . " Of our 5.7 million denuded hectares , only 15,000 hectares were planted to bamboo from 2010 to 2016 . Natural Resources Secretary Gina Lopez 's announcement that this would be increased to one million hectares for the next six years will result in a welcome bonanza .
