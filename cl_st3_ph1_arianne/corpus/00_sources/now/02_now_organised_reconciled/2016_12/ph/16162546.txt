<h> UN chief Ban Ki-moon bids colleagues , staff farewell <p> United Nations Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon , poses for a selfie photo with one of his staff members on his last day at the U.N . headquarters on Friday , Dec. 30 , 2016 . ( AP Photo/Mary Altaffer ) <p> UNITED NATIONS -- Ban Ki-moon has joked to hundreds of diplomats and UN staff as he left United Nations headquarters for the last time as secretary-general that he feels like Cinderella because everything changes for him at midnight on New Year 's Eve . <p> Flanked by the presidents of the General Assembly and the Security Council , the native South Korean thanked UN workers yesterday for their hard work and commitment during his 10-year tenure that ends at midnight Dec. 31 . <p> He told his colleagues he had two words for them : " Thank You . " <p> As the top UN official over the last decade , Ban fostered a global agreement to combat climate change and new UN goals to fight poverty and inequality .
