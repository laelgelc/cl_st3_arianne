<h> DENR reports expansion of PHL forests through greening program <p> The country 's forest cover expanded by approximately 250,000 hectares with the successful implementation of the National Greening Program ( NGP ) this year . <p> In the final year of implementation of NGP , some 367,684,457 seedlings were planted , covering a total of 242,507 hectares . This is 98 percent of the year 's target of 247,683 hectares . <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) , the lead implementing agency of the NGP , is still collating data from field offices of the DENR and other NGP partners . <p> A DENR accomplishment report as of October 2016 also said that a total of 555,794 jobs were generated through the NGP , employing a total of 90,912 individuals . <p> The NGP aims to plant 1.5 billion trees in 1.5 million hectares of open , degraded and denuded forest .
