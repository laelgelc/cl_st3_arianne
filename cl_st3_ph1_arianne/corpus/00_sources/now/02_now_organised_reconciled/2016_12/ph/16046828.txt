<h> Have a merry green Christmas <p> Enderun 's SustainabiliTREE is decorated with LED lights powered by solar and kinetic generators from ERA Solar and Pivot Edutainment <p> What can we do to give Mother Earth a present this Christmas ? We always take from her but what can we do to give back ? Enderun Colleges encourages everyone to help make the world a better place . The school 's Christmas tree for this year aptly named " Sustainabilitree , " is made up of bamboo poles re-used from last year 's decorations that were wrapped around with thin twigs while the ornaments are made up of recycled newspaper and barbeque sticks , which are handcrafted by the faculty and students of Enderun . The tree itself is powered by renewable energy coming from the eight solar panels that Buskowitz Development provided while at night , the tree is powered by McClellan 's Bambikes that are connected to generators provided by Pivot Edutainment . This year , De La Salle University ( DLSU ) encourages each and every one of us to speak and stand up for Mother Earth through its " Animo ! Christmas Tree , " a 20-meter-long Christmas tree , made up of reused to-dispose materials from the department stores . The environmental-friendly tree can be seen by LRT passengers and the passersby along Taft Avenue , Manila . With the theme " Berde -- Going Green this Christmas , " the tree reminds us that by " going green " this Yuletide season we secure the safety and health of the environment , we ensure hope for a better future for ourselves , and we save mother earth .
