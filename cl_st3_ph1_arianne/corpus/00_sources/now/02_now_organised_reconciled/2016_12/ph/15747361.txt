<h> Salceda seeks creation of Metrology Institute <p> TO effectively and efficiently adapt to the needs of contemporary times , PDP Laban Rep. Joey S. Salceda of Albay proposed the standardization and modernization of units of measurements to further enhance the " global competitiveness of Philippine products and services " and facilitate national economic progress . <p> Salceda recently filed House Bill 4368 ( HB 4368 ) in Congress that seeks to amend Republic Act 9236 , known as the National Metrology Act of 2003 , and strengthen the existing National Measurement Infrastructure System ( NMIS ) by creating a new agency , the National Measurement Institute ( NMI ) . <p> " What you ca n't measure is not important , " Salceda said , stressing the practical but vital requirements of being globally competitive , of which a standardized unit of measurement is among them . <p> HB 4368 , titled Enhanced National Measurement Infrastructure System , will accord greater consumer protection , transparency and confidence in measurements , which is a foundation of trade , science and technology , and quantitative research in many disciplines . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ practices in measurements in response to future development and requirements , and " provide support to Research and Development " in Metrology , Salceda said . <p> The bill covers research and development in the fields of " environment and climate change , public health and safety , transport , information and communications technology ICT , biotechnology-genomics , material science , Nanotechnology , photonics , metrology in chemistry , space technology applications and other emerging and enabling technologies .
