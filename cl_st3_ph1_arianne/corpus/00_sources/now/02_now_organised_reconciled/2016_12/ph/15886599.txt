<h> Coalition blocks P65-B Luna coal power plant <p> SAN FERNANDO CITY , La Union -- Groups concerned about the environmental and climate change joined religious organizations last Monday in opposing the proposed construction of a ww65-billion coal-fired power plant in Barangay Carisquis , Luna , this province . <p> The Philippine Movement for Climate Justice ( PMCJ ) , HELPS for Humanity ( H4H ) , Kanlungan Center Foundation , the United Church of Christ in the Philippines , the Catholic Church , and several private groups formed the Koalisyon Isalbar ti Pintas ti La Union ( Coalition to Save the Beauty of La Union ) . <p> Crisanto Palabay , H4H executive director , said that they formed the coalition for them to have a stronger voice and organization in blocking the 670-megawatt power plant of the Global Luzon Energy Development Corp. , the project proponent . The proponent 's mother company is the Global Business Power Corporation , a subsidiary of GT Holdings . <p> " We formed this coalition so that there will be an organization in the local level to help our groups in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We believe that the project in Luna is dangerous to our health and environment , " Palabay said . <p> Valentino de Guzman , PMCJ-Luzon coordinator , said they oppose the project because of the alleged ill-effects of burning coal to human health . <p> " We are pushing ( to the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ) for the moratorium on all new coal-fired power plants which covers the Luna project . No permit and no application should be signed or approved . All should be status quo because we have not yet resolved the issues on the bad effects of all those operating coal plants . Let us sit down and discuss everything first , " De Guzman said . <p> De Guzman said they will be seeking a meeting with Environment Secretary Gina Lopez to discuss the Luna project situated at a 40-hectare idle land in the coastal barangay of Carisquis and its stoppage .
