<h> Cocaine worth P90M found floating in Bicol <p> December 20 , 2016 <p> Julius B. Embile <p> LEGAZPI CITY , Dec. 20 ( PIA ) -- The Philippine National Police ( PNP ) in Bicol dealt a big blow against the illegal drug trade as cocaine worth P90 million was taken into its custody after being discovered floating at sea . <p> Police records showed that around 10:30 in the morning of Dec. 18 , Manuel Comota and Razel Bragais found a package floating at sea while they were fishing off the coast of Tiwi , Albay . The package was a net-full of brick-like items wrapped in packaging tape . <p> After salvaging the package , the two fishermen immediately informed the ' punong barangay ' of Barangay Sogod in Tiwi , Teresita Cericos , of the items they recovered . Initially suspecting the item as dynamite or other explosive compound , Cericos contacted the police in Tiwi . <p> After verifying the report , Police Senior Inspector Jan King Calipay responded to Cericos ' request for police chemist . Cericos turned the package over to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ then accompanied to Senior Supt. Antonio Cirujales for subsequent turnover of the recovered items to Albay Provincial Police Office ( Albay PPO ) . <p> Through the efforts of the PNP 's crime laboratory and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency , the items were identified as cocaine . The fishermen of Tiwi were able to salvage eighteen bricks of cocaine , with each weighing in about a kilo and worth about P5 million . <p> " We suspect that this was done by foreigners who are trying to smuggle drugs to other countries like Hong Kong . It is possible that they are using the Philippines as a drop off point for drugs being manufactured from South America , " said Chief Inspector Arthur Gomez , spokesperson of Albay PPO . <p> According to the PNP , this is the first time that PNP in Bicol was able to confiscate drugs as high-grade as cocaine . Due to the recent intensified effort against illegal drugs , the police suspected the drugs to have been dumped at sea by drug smugglers who were trying to evade arrest . <p> In @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ delivering and trading illegal drugs on land may have prompted drug runners to try conducting their business at sea . <p> He also added that the drugs recovered at Tiwi may be connected to the vessel the authorities recently chased near Masbate , which they suspected of transporting illegal drugs . <p> " It may have been easy for them the past years , but not today because of the actions being taken to eliminate illegal drugs in the country , " said Gomez . <p> On a press conference held yesterday , as the confiscated drugs were presented to the media , Police Regional Director Melvin Buenafe expressed his gratitude to the barangay head and the two fishermen for taking effort to turn over the drugs to the authorities . <p> He explained that their actions were of great help to the police 's on-going campaign against illegal drugs . Buenafe also encouraged the public to continue supporting the authorities on the said effort towards a clean and drug-free Philippines .
