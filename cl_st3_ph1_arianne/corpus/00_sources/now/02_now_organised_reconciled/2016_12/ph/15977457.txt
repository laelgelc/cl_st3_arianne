<h> PRO-Cor is best RLECC nationwide <p> LA TRINIDAD , Benguet , Dec. 19(PIA) -- The Police Regional Office -- Cordillera ( PRO-Cor ) was named as the Best Regional Law Enforcement Coordinating Committee ( RLECC ) in Luzon and in the country . <p> PRO-Cor Regional Director PCSupt . Elmo Francis Sarona received the plaque from National Law Enforcement Coordinating Council Chairman , Police Director General Roland Dela Rosa for being judged as the Best RLECC in Luzon and in the national level during the Command Conference at Camp Crame last week . <p> The award is a result of the concerted effort of all the members of the RLECC in the Cordillera region . The PRO-Cor RLECC work hard with dedication for the successful conduct of impact programs , projects and activities for the period July 15 , 2015 to July 15 , 2016 that contributed immensely to the attainment of the NALECC 's goals and objectives , the PRO-Cor stated . <p> The RLECC is composed of different government agencies and partners from the private sector with a role of formulating law enforcement policies for implementation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ legislations affecting the country 's peace and order , its economy and environment . <p> The PRO-COR also received plaque of commendation for surpassing target in " Double Barrel Alpha " . It garnered a high percentage accomplishment of 171 percent in accounting drug personalities during the implementation of Campaign Plan : Double Barrel Alpha for the period October 26 to December 7 , 2016 . This significantly contributed towards the goal of a drug-free Philippines . <p> Earlier this year , PRO-Cor also was awarded as the Best Police Strategy Management Unit in the country during the 115th Police Service Anniversary and the Outstanding Regional Advisory Council during the Center for Police Strategy Management 4th founding anniversary last August . <p> The advisory council was formed to help in transforming the Philippine National Police into a credible and effective police force and a requirement of the Police Transformation and Development program of the government in line with its Performance Governance System to pursue the PNP Peace and Order Agenda for Transformation and upholding the Rule of Law Plan 2030 .
