<h> 2017 IPP features modern incentives <p> The 2017 Investment Priorities Plan ( IPP ) will focus on the development and promotion of innovative , inclusive industries , enhance employment , entrepreneurship under a modern incentives regime . <p> Based on the draft 2017 IPP , which spans 2017-2019 period , the list of preferred areas of economic activities will set specific objects to make investments more relevant to the economy and the society . <p> The Board of Investments ( BOI ) , which spearheads the IPP formulation , did not explain what are these modern incentives . But these incentives may include perks to inclusive business models and the inclusion of micro businesses into their value chains .
