<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan 's average wheat yield rose to 2.8 tonnes/hectare during the past decade on usage of high-yielding seed varieties and an increased use of fertiliser and farm machinery , a minister said on Friday . <p> Sikandar Hayat Khan Bosan , Minister for National Food Security and Research said the maize yields also significantly increased with the coordinated efforts of the Pakistan Agricultural Research Council ( PARC ) and other international partners . <p> " The government is committed to ensure an adequate provision of food items through well-defined and targeted interventions to improve food security in the country , " the minister said while addressing a seminar . <p> Argentine embassy in Pakistan , in collaboration with the ministry , Pakistan Agricultural Research Council and the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations , hosted the seminar , titled ' new avenues of cooperation between Argentine and Pakistan in agriculture sector ' . <p> Nadeem Amjad , acting chairman of PARC said Pakistan and Argentine can partner in several areas , such as climate change , water resources , irrigation , water management , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ production of various crops . " Pakistan can also get significant benefits from the Argentinean expertise of establishing modern abattoirs , genetic improvement of livestock breeds , quality vaccine , fodder and feed production technologies , installation of milk processing units , fish farming , agricultural mechanisation , institutionalisation of disease surveillance , monitoring and reporting system , " Amjad said . <p> Argentine Ambassador in Pakistan Ivan Ivanissevich said the seminar would help in exploring new avenues of cooperation between both the countries in agriculture and livestock sectors .
