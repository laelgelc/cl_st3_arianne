<p> ATTOCK : Volunteers and rescue workers marched in Attock on Monday to mark World Volunteers Day . <p> " At the moment , around 1,200 volunteers are serving the people of Attock , while a further 2,300 volunteers listed as reserves are waiting for a chance to serve , " said Rescue 1122 District Emergency Officer Dr Mian Muhammad Ashfaq . <p> Speaking to participants of the rally , Dr Ashfaq said civilised communities work together to implement laws , rules and regulations . <p> The rally started from Liaqat Ali Khan Shaheed Stadium and concluded at Fawara Chowk . On the occasion In-charge Civil Defence , District Officer Social Affair Khalid Saba , In-charge NCHD Tahir Khattak , Anjuman Tajiran President Rashidur Rehman and peoples from different walk of life participated in the rally . <p> In light of rising natural , man-made disasters and acts of terrorism , the importance of volunteers serving people selflessly had increased manifold , he said . <p> The society should discourage those who defy rules and regulations , the district emergency officer said . <p> He added that accidents were @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and rickety public transport were used in violation of rules and regulations . <p> Dr Ashfaq said that Pakistan was facing both internal and external threats to security in addition to the vagaries of climate change .
