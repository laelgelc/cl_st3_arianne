<p> ISLAMABAD : An acute shortage of technical and secretarial staff at the Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) is hampering performance of the environment supervisory body . <p> Posts such as Environmental Impact Assessment ( EIA ) director and labs director have been vacant for six months , while Administration Director Fahim Riaz has gone on leave ahead of his retirement ( leave preparatory to retirement -- LPR ) . <p> Former EIA Director Ziaul Islam retired earlier in June this year and his replacement has not been appointed yet . <p> Meanwhile , Ziauddin Khattak , director of the Lab and National Environmental Quality Standards ( NEQS ) went on his LPR six months ago . He is expected to retire once his leave expires in another six months . <p> Intriguingly , the Labs and NEQS deputy director slot had fallen vacant after its incumbent , Farzana Altaf Shah , had been promoted as Pak-EPA director general a couple of months ago . <p> Similarly , the environmental watchdog is currently working without a legal director .
