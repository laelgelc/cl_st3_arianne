<h> Climate change : Pilot project to study agri data <p> LAHORE : The Punjab government will launch a pilot project for finding out the best ways and means to minimise the impact of climate change on agriculture sector . An amount of Rs99.96 million has been approved for the pilot project , an official of Punjab Planning and Development Department told APP on Sunday . Under the pilot project , modern techniques of rainwater management in cotton fields will be applied . He said that outcome of application of these modern techniques would enable experts to analyse their impact and compile data . The data will then be utilised for future planning , he added . The success of the pilot project would pave way for launching similar projects . The Punjab Provincial Development Working Party gave the go-ahead for the project in a meeting for current fiscal year .
