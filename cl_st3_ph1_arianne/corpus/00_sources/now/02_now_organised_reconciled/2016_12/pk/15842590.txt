The process began in 1976 with the First UN Conference on Human Settlements ( HABITAT-I ) in Vancouver . The conference recommended to the central/federal governments to develop spatial plans for cities to consolidate zoning and land use provisions . The event generated a lot of activity : Many governments prepared human settlement policies with a focus to support disadvantaged and vulnerable communities . <p> The second UN Conference on Human Settlements was held in Istanbul in 1996 . Due to decentralisation , emphasis was shifted towards local governments .
