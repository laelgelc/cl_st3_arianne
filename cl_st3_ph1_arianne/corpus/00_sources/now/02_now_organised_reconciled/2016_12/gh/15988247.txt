<h> Stakeholders Initiate Biogas Association of Ghana <p> A new body , Biogas Association of Ghana ( BAG ) , has been formed to promote the sustainable development of the biogas sector in Ghana . <p> Its objectives are to carry out , encourage and support research into the biogas technology and to ensure the development of quality standards by training actors in the industry based on modern approved practices . <p> The Association was initiated by the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) , the Ghana National Cleaner Production Centre ( GNCPC ) and the Energy Commission , at a meeting in Tema , to represent firms , institutions and individuals involved in biogas technology . <p> Dr Elias Delali Aklaku , the President of BAG , in an interview with the Ghana News Agency , said the Association was formed to influence policies that were aimed at improving sanitation , accessibility to energy , agriculture and the control of global warming . <p> He explained that biogas or anaerobic digestion technology provided sustainable solutions to most of the country 's sanitation , energy and agricultural challenges , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of its benefits . <p> Dr Aklaku gave the assurance that the Association would build the capacity of practitioners to collaborate with the Ghana Standard Authority ( GSA ) , EPA , Energy Commission and other institutions in setting standards for the industry and also solicit investment in the sector . <p> Mrs Lovas Owusu-Takyi , the Executive Member of BAG , expressed gratitude to the GNCPC , EPA , the Energy Commission and the United Nations Environmental Programme for their support to the Association . <p> " We will also support the government and development partners in addressing climate change issues and achieving the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) in Ghana , " he said . <p> Mr Daniel Digber of the GNCPC promised that they would work with the Association to enable them to operate effectively as a strong advocacy institution for biogas development . <p> Other members of the Association elected at the meeting to manage the affairs of the Association for the next two years are Mr Moses Pumpuni , Mr
