We are very committed to these challenges , but we need help , " Hidalgo told reporters . <p> Cities are looking to comply with the targets set by the Paris Agreement reached in December 2015 . <p> The Rio mayor said some of that money has already been earmarked by cities to fight climate change . <p> " When we say 375 billion dollars , it is not necessarily new funding .
