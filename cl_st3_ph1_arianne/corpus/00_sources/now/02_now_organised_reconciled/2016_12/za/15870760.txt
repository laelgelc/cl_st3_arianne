<h> Motsepe 's massive investment in Bill Gates ' energy fund <p> Patrice Motsepe is one of 20 of the world 's richest entrepreneurs to invest in a $1 billion energy fund started by Bill Gates <p> Microsoft founder Bill Gates is heading a $1 billion venture as part of his Breakthrough Energy Coalition , which he launched in December 2015 . <p> The fund is being set up to fight climate change by investing in clean energy innovation . Gates has recruited 20 of the world 's most successful entrepreneurs , venture capitalists and business leaders , including Alibaba founder Jack Ma , Amazon founder and CEO Jeff Bezos , Virgin 's Richard Branson and South Africa 's Patrice Motsepe , founder of African Rainbow Minerals , to name a few . <p> " I 'm honoured to work with these investors to build on the powerful foundation of public investment in basic research , " Gates said in a statement . " Our goal is to build companies that will help deliver the next generation of reliable , affordable and emission-free energy to the world . " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lifespan and will not only fund start-ups but will extend to companies that have reached a commercialisation stage , Fortune reports . <p> The fund will also consider a broad spectrum of areas related to energy not limited to electricity generation , that includes energy storage , industrial system use , energy system efficiency , agriculture and transportation . <p> It 's likely that energy storage will be considered in the first wave of the venture because cheaper and more efficient storage allows for greater dependence on clean energy , such as wind and solar power . <p> While the 20 investors contributing to the project could be the richest group of investors ever put together , Gates has emphasised that for this project to be successful , a lot more than $1 billion is needed . He still plans on getting more strategic partners , such as energy companies , to support and invest in the project .
