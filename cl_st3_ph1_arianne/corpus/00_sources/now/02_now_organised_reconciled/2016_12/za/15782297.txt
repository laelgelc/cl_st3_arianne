<h> University of Cape Town excels in environmental innovation at South Africa 's second Greenovate Awards <p> Young environmental visionaries from the University of Cape Town took first place in the Growthpoint Greenovate Awards for the second year running . <p> The awards recognise innovative solutions for the property industry to environmental challenges . <p> Now in its second year , the awards programme is an exciting initiative by Growthpoint Properties in association with the Green Building Council South Africa ( GBCSA ) . It inspires and encourages students of the built environment to discover , explore and invent ways to live more sustainably . <p> This year 's participants were from the University of Cape Town , University of the Witwatersrand and Nelson Mandela Metropolitan University . <p> Students were challenged to come up with ideas for any property-related project that makes the way we live greener and our environmental footprint lighter .
