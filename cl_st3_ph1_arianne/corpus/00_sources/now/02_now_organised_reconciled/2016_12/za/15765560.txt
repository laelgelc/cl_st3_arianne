<h> South African electric vehicle market is set to grow <h> South Africa takes giant leap by launching the Electric Vehicle Industry Association to accelerate the development of clean transport and reduce carbon emissions . <p> The Electric Vehicle Industry Association ( EVIA ) , endorsed by the department of trade and industry , was launched on Monday at the Industrial Development Corporation in Johannesburg . <p> Last year , during the climate change talks in Paris , it was agreed upon by the United Nations that all participating nations must pledge to reduce their carbon emissions contributions to limit global warming to 2 ? C above pre-industrial levels . <p> Among the biggest emitters of greenhouse gases is the transport industry and as such the pioneers of South Africa 's emerging electric vehicle ( EV ) industry have formed a consortium to accelerate the development of clean transport . <p> EVIA 's founding members include BMW SA , Gridcars , Nissan SA , the SA National Energy Development Institute ( SANEDI ) and Uyilo , a programme of the Technology Innovation Agency . <p> The association will serve @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government and researchers to stimulate the sector . <p> Participants will include government departments and agencies , other EV manufacturers , as well as electricity infrastructure and smart grid providers . <h> Innovative electric vehicle <p> Speaking at the launch , SANEDI chief executive , Kevin Nassiep , said : " EVIA is now a powerful industry body committed to working with government to develop electric mobility in South Africa . " <p> " We are accelerating the long overdue development of a sustainable and innovative electric vehicle industry in SA , " Nassiep added . <p> In correspondence with ESI Africa , SANEDI 's Senior Manager : Clean Energy Solutions , Carel Snyman , explained that there has not been any real growth in sales of EV because of the heavy tax in market . <p> " Not much growth is expected until we change the 43% tax on EVs . This is preventing more manufacturers from entering the market . <p> " Nissan and BMW took the bold step of bringing in EVs , hoping government would move faster to introduce incentives to create a favourable market @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> He stated that SANEDI and partners are now assisting government and cities to develop this favourable environment for cleaner mobility options in the country . <h> Charging facilities <p> With regards to addressing the lack of facilities for charging EV , Snyman told ESI Africa that the association brings together the suppliers of the infrastructure , the manufacturers of the vehicles , and the users of electric vehicles to plan for the positioning of more public chargers in those areas where needed . <p> According to Snyman , small amounts at this stage have been invested by various players as part of demonstration projects . <p> The investors include the founders , South African Cities Network , UNIDO , property developers , private companies and individuals .
