<p> Oregon Wild is promoting a 500,000-acre Crater Lake Wilderness , which would include Crater Lake National Park and surrounding lands . These lands are among the last remaining unprotected , roadless lands in the Cascades and are a keystone for the creation of viable wildlife corridors in the range . <p> Wilderness also protects clean water , scenic values and important ecological processes . A wilderness designation also contributes to significant carbon storage that can help ameliorate climate change . <p> There is some opposition to the proposal based on false assumptions . For instance , wilderness designations , rather than harming regional economies as some opponents argue , contribute to future economic viability . Because of automation , as well as ecological concerns , we will not see future growth in timber production . Rather , the future of Oregon 's economy is increasingly based upon quality-of-life attributes and amenities . <p> Protected landscapes such as parks and wilderness areas are a major draw for people who can live anyplace they choose , and for businesses that can operate in any location . The other major reason given for opposing wilderness is fear of wildfire . Yet wilderness designations reduce the occurrence of wildfire . <p> Research published just last month in Ecosphere has found that active logging increases the likelihood of severe fires . There are several reasons for this , including the fact that many fires are started by humans along roads , and logging slash creates the perfect bed for fire starts .
