<h> Obama invokes 1953 law to indefinitely block drilling in Arctic and Atlantic oceans <p> President Barack Obama on Tuesday moved to indefinitely block drilling in vast swaths of U.S. waters . <p> The president had been expected to take the action by invoking a provision in a 1953 law that governs offshore leases , as CNBC previously reported . <p> The law allows a president to withdraw any currently unleased lands in the Outer Continental Shelf from future lease sales . There is no provision in the law that allows the executive 's successor to repeal the decision , so President-elect Donald Trump would not be able to easily brush aside the action . <p> Trump has vowed to open more federal land to oil and natural gas production in a bid to boost U.S. output .
