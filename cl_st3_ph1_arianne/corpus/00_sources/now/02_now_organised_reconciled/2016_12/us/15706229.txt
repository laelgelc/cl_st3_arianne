<p> Climate change was the topic of a model United Nations program on Friday where nearly 1,000 high school students from around Connecticut got a sense how world leaders tackle the issue . <p> The program was held at the University of Hartford by the World Affairs Council of Connecticut , which runs community engagement and education programs on global issues . The council is one of 94 throughout the country and has run a model U.N . program since 1952 . <p> Different issues are explored each year , and the topic of climate change was chosen well in advance , organizers said . But Amanda Jolly , a spokeswoman for the council , the timing turned out well because the recent presidential election has given the issue new urgency . <p> " The issue of climate change has taken on a different tone with the upcoming change in administrations , " Jolly said . <p> Organizers said 33 high schools were represented .
