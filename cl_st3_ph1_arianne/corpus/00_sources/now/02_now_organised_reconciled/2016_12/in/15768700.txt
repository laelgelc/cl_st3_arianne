<h> More reports on observance of World Soil Day <p> Dimapur Dec. 6 : The department of Agriculture observed World Soil Day on theme " Soil a solid ground for life , " on December 5 at Directorate Conference Hall . Director of Agricultur , Helie R ? preo , speaking on importance of soil , asked the gathering to be aware of civil society and decision makers about the fundamental roles of soils for human life . He also emphasised the prominent contribution of soils to food security , climate change adaptation and mitigation , essential ecosystem services , poverty alleviation and sustainable development . Thungjamo Lotha , additional director of Agriculture in his keynote address highlighted on the facts of soil in Nagaland . He said soils of Nagaland were highly acidic and in some places the PH level is as high as 4-4.5 and high rainfall and leaching is the contributing factor for such acidity .
