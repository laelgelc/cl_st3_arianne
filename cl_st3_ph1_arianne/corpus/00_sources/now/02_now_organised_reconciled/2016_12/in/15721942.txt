<h> Climate change is not a myth <p> Our youth have to be redirected and investors have to be fostered through tax benefits on clean energy <p> Just five days before Donald Trump , a blatant denier of climate change , was elected as the 45th President of the United States , a rather promising treaty went into effect : the Paris Agreement . Developing nations play a key role in it . Western nations were oblivious ( and some still are ) of the carbon footprint they left behind in the last century . Thus , it is expected of us to grow wiser and not deteriorate the environment . It is time for us to realise what the agreement means for us as citizens and how we can contribute to ensure a future for our progeny . <p> Every nation that signed the treaty formulated its own Nationally Determined Contribution . A Rural Electrification Corporation Limited has been created to finance renewable power projects .
