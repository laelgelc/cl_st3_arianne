<h> State of art laboratory for Himalayan study at WII <p> A laboratory to map the ecology of the Himalayas was inaugurated by the secretary to the environment ministry Ajay Narayan Jha at Wildlife Institute of India on Saturday <p> TNN Dec 17 , 2016 , 10.01 PM IST <p> DEHRADUN : A laboratory to map the ecology of the Himalayas was inaugurated by the secretary to the environment ministry Ajay Narayan Jha at Wildlife Institute of India on Saturday . WII said that the landscape ecology and visualization laboratory ( LEVL ) will present two to three dimensional visualizations of present and futuristic projection of Himalayas and the outcome will be shared periodically to develop appropriate solutions . <p> LEVL , a part of National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan ecosystem ( NMSHE ) , is a program under the National Action Plan for Climate Change by the Prime Minister 's Office , which is being coordinated by the department of science and technology ( DST ) . <p> " The laboratory is a pioneering state-of-art-facility integrating various aspects such as field-based biological research , data centre , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all at one place . It would allow creation of inter-operable spatial database , linking field data and GIS data , detailed spatial analyses to understand various drivers of biodiversity responses , modelling and scenario building , two to three dimensional visualizations of present and futuristic projection of Himalayas , map production and capacity building for developing adaptations and policy planning , " said WII scientist K Ramesh . <p> He said that the working of the laboratory will be interactive with project personnel and stakeholders . The outcome will be shared periodically , so that local solutions and adaptation strategies can be developed simultaneously .
