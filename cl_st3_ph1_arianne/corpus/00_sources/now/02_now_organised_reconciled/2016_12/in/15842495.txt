<h> JAU to conduct week-long training course on water security <p> TNN Dec 11 , 2016 , 04.00 AM IST <p> Rajkot : A week-long model training course on ' Water security and challenges in the arena of climate change ' will be held at the Junagadh Agricultural University ( JAU ) starting December 14 . The course is being conducted by JAU 's directorate of extension and department of agriculture , cooperation and farmers ' welfare , New Delhi . <p> " Climate change is directly affecting the agriculture sector and availability of water . Rainy days are reducing and temperature is soaring .
