This is because carbon stores are greatest in places like the Arctic and sub Arctic where soil is cold and often frozen . Microbes are less active when it 's cold , so carbon can build up and up under the surface over many centuries . <p> As things warm up , the activities of microbes increases , and that 's when more carbon dioxide escapes .
