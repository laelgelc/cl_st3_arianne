<h> President Mukherjee asks for protection of child rights <p> President Pranab Mukherjee has asked people to commit themselves to protection of child rights . Addressing the opening session of the Laureates and Leaders for Children Summit in New Delhi , Mr Mukherjee said country has a moral obligation towards children , their development and security . <p> He said that programmes for children have to take centre-stage in national policy-making . The President called for addressing the disparities in education , health and poverty indicators . <p> The President said exploitation of resources and degradation of the environment is a serious challenge to sustainable development . He said developing economies , which are closely related to climate-sensitive sectors like agriculture and forestry , are more vulnerable to climate change .
