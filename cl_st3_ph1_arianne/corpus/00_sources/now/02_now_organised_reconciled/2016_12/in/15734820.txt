<h> Custom Navigation <h> KVK to celebrate World Soil Day <p> Port Blair <p> 4 Dec 2016 <p> The ICAR- Krishi Vigyan Kendra , Central Island Agricultural Research Institute here in association with the Natural Resource Management , Division , and CIARI will observe World Soil Day on December 5 at Collinpur Panchayat . Dr. Sibnarayan Dam Roy , Director , ICAR-CIARI , Port Blair will be the chief guest of the programme , Dr. B Gangaiah Head of Division , NRM and Dr. R K Gautam HoD , Horticulture and Forestry Division and Dr. Nageshram , Head , KVK , Port Blair will also deliver lectures about the importance of soil , climate change with special reference to World Soil Day . A drawing competition will also be held on the theme of " S ( Soul ) O ( Of ) I ( Infinite ) L ( Life ) for the School children of 7 - 9th Standard . A total of 50 farmers , farm women and 60 school children will participate the programme .
