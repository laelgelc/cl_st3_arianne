Synthetic biology can be used to create both living and non-living organisms , genetic materials and biological systems . <p> One example is the use of so-called gene drives , through which it is possible to force a genetic trait to spread through a population of plants or animals . Biodiverse countries such as Brazil , South Africa and India are concerned by projects that place commercially valuable DNA sequences online without following CBD rules requiring users of genetic resources to share benefits with source countries or communities . <p> Once such data is online , anyone can easily access and use it without needing physical access to genetic resources .
