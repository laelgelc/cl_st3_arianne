The world today is at an inflection point due to rapid technological advancement leading to economic , social and political turmoil . Developed countries have now seen almost two decades of stagnation in economic growth with outsourcing of jobs in manufacturing and the services sector to less expensive destinations in the developing countries like India , China , Philippines , Indonesia etc . These technological advancements threaten to decimate jobs and increase the rich and poor divide further .
