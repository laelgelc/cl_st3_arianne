<h> Antonio Guterres Wants to Meet Donald Trump ' As Soon As Possible ' <h> File image of Antonio Guterres . ( Image : PTI ) <p> Incoming UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said he wants to meet Donald Trump " as soon as possible " and is " determined to establish a constructive dialogue with the new US administration " . <p> Guterres , who met with Russian President Vladimir Putin at the end of last month , said he hoped for a similar outcome with the American President-elect . <p> The former Portuguese prime minister takes over from Ban Ki-moon on January 1 amid ongoing bloodshed in Syria and questions over the US role in the world under a Trump presidency . <p> " I had an excellent working meeting with President Putin and I hope this will also be the case with Donald Trump , " he told Portuguese television channel SIC in an interview on Wednesday . <p> " It is certainly in my interest to visit him as soon as possible , " he said . " <p> Trump on Friday said Washington 's policies at the UN will be different after he takes office . <p> " As to the UN , things will be different after Jan 20th , " he said on Twitter , referring to the date of his inauguration . <p> The tweet came after the United States refrained from vetoing the adoption of a Security Council measure calling on Israel -- its closest Middle East ally -- to halt settlement activities in Palestinian territory . <p> Guterres said he was " determined to establish a constructive dialogue with the future American administration " . <p> Trump has not hidden his scepticism about the fight against global warming but has modified that position since his November 8 election win saying he may support global accords on climate change , and that he had " an open mind " . <p> Asked about this , Guterres said the " new ( US ) government will certainly adopt a different position " than that of Barack Obama 's administration .
