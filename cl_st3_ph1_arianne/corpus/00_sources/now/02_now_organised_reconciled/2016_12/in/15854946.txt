<p> World Soil Day is observed to celebrate the importance of soil as a critical component of the natural system , considering its contribution to food , water , energy security , mitigator of biodiversity loss and climate change . Soil is the basis for food , feed , fuel and fibre production and for services to ecosystems and human well-being . The World Soil Day campaign aims to connect people with soil and raise awareness on their critical importance in our lives . <p> In keeping with the celebration , 250 soil health cards were distributed among farmers of the district . The soil health cards given to farmers is a printed report that will contain crucial information on macro nutrients in the soil , secondary nutrients , micro nutrients and physical parameters . The card will also be accompanied by recommendation on the analysis to improve soil health and obtain better yield . <p> A pre-Rabi campaign was also organized with an exhibition showcasing various agricultural and allied technologies by KVK , Dhemaji , and eight other developmental groups .
