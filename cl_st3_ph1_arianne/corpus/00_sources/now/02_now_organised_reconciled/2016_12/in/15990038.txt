The southern districts have not received much rainfall this season , as no system has passed through the Gulf of Mannar . <p> Overall , agricultural activity has been adversely affected . Paddy cultivation in Karur district has come down by 70 per cent while cultivation of millets has also reduced to 69 per cent .
