<p> The federal government on Thursday made public the official quarterly emissions data for the year to June showing an increase of 0.8 per cent on the previous year . <p> It also released projections showing emissions in 2030 will be just 0.5 per cent lower than 2005 levels , rather than the government 's own targets of 26-28 per cent lower . <p> Labor 's climate change spokesman Mark Butler seized on the timing of the release . <p> " Malcolm Turnbull has used the cover of Christmas to release damning data that confirms pollution levels are not only growing under his watch , but will continue to rise as far as the eye can see , " Mr Butler said in a statement
