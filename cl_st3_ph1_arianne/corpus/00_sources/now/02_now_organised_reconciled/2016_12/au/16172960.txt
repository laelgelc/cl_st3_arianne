<p> This week , Michigan became the fourth US state to place a ban on banning plastic bags . The new bill , signed into law Wednesday by Lt Gov Brian Calley , bans any bills " regulating the use , disposition , or sale of , prohibiting or restricting , or imposing any fee , charge , or tax on certain containers " , including plastic bags , bottles and so on .
