<p> Released on Friday , the report states participants in the ACT 's small and medium FiT scheme generated 34,910 megawatt hours ( MWh ) of electricity in 2015 -- 16 . This generation came from total installed capacity of around 26.3 megawatts , consisting of 10,304 solar power systems . <p> This scheme was open to applications from renewable energy generators with capacity below 200 kilowatts ( kW ) from 1 March 2009 and to 13 July 2011 . Only 15 medium scale generators ( 30kW -- 200kW capacity ) are participating , with the balance being small home and commercial solar power systems . <p> According to the Independent Competition and Regulatory Commission , the small and medium solar feed in tariff has cost a four-person household in the ACT around 88.9 cents per week . . <p> The Territory 's large-scale FiT program has been going gangbusters ; generating 57,423 MWh of electricity in 2015 -- 16 .
