<h> Key new appointment for Andrew Robb <p> Former Trade Minister Andrew Robb has been appointed Chairman of Asialink and Asialink Business . <p> Mr Robb replaces current Chairman Sid Myer , who will take up a new role as Patron of Asialink , and will continue to serve on the Asialink Business board .
