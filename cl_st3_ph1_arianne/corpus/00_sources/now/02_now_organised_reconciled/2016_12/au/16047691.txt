<p> These mouse-like creatures are unique to our country because they can only survive in sub alpine areas between 1200 -- 2228m above sea level . <p> But with only 2700 of them in Australia , the Mountain pygmy-possum is considered endangered -- thanks to the threat of feral cats , climate change and drought . <p> " These possums , which are about double the size of a common house mouse , are the only Australian marsupial to hibernate under the snow in winter and for long periods of time , " Saving Our Species senior threatened species officer Linda Broome told news.com.au . <p> " They are very unique to Australia , and while their numbers are still pretty dire -- their population is on the rise . " <p> And that is all thanks to an environmental program , called Saving Our Species . <p> The 30-year study , lead by research by a group of dedicated volunteers , has shown the endangered Mountain pygmy-possums are once again flourishing in the Snowy Mountains region . <p> A Mountain-pygmy Possum has a drink of water .
