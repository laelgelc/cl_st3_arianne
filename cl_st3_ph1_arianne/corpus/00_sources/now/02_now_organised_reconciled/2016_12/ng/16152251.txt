<h> For The First Time , China Has Implemented A Tax On Industrial Pollution <p> This is a first for China . As the country went into air pollution red alert last week for the second time in history , the government decided to start sanctioning industrial pollution . <p> Beginning in 2018 , Chinese factories will have to pay a tax on the pollution they produce , whether it 's in the air , water or soil . The tax will also have to be paid for sound pollution , calculated according to the decibel level emitted . <p> Industrial complex in Ganjiaxiang , China ( Source : Wikimedia Commons ) <p> Until now , there had been no environmental tax or legislation in place for industrial pollution . Instead , industries had to pay a range of fees , but according to experts , these were ineffective . <h> Pollution-related health problems in China <p> Along with the United States , China is the biggest greenhouse gas emitter in the world . After ratifying the Paris agreement on climate change along with the United States , China has already made a name for itself as the world 's biggest producer of renewable energies . <p> With this new tax on industrial pollution , they are sending a strong message . While they 've still got a long way to go , the country appears to be moving in the right direction .
