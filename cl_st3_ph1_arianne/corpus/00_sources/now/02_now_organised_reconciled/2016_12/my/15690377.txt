<p> VATICAN CITY : A cross-party group of MPs has tabled a motion calling for the House of Commons to congratulate Pope Francis on his 80th birthday . <p> The motion was tabled by Labour MP Robert Flello and has been signed by nine MPs so far . <p> The signatories include Christopher Stephens of the SNP , two Conservatives , Edward Leigh and Peter Bottomley , and six MPs from the Labour party . <p> The motion reads : " That this House congratulates His Holiness Pope Francis on his 80th birthday ; recognises his spiritual leadership of over 1.2 billion Catholics worldwide ; commends his leading contribution to tackling climate change , promoting sustainable development , welcoming refugees , building peace , encouraging prison reform , protecting religious freedom , and advancing global abolition of the death penalty ; and wishes him well for his continued pontificate . " <p> This week we begin the Church year with a call to stay awake .
