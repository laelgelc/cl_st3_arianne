<p> Tan Sri Prof Dr Zakri Abdul Hamid , co-chair of MIGHT and science advisor to the Prime Minister , said a broader approach is crucial to tackle inter-connected problems related to human well-being such as water , energy , climate change and education . <p> " For example , energy , water and food security are recognised as a highly inter-related trio of fundamental issues confronting policy-makers , who must weigh and balance choices , " he said . <p> He was briefng the media on the three-day Symposium on Broadening the Application of the Sustainability Science Approach which ended here today . <p> The meeting , hosted by MIGHT under the auspices of UNESCO and attended by experts from all over the world , was to come up with a proposal on ' Sustainability Science Policy Guidelines ' to be presented at the final symposium in Paris next year . <p> Zakri said sustainability science was essential for achieving the 2030 Agenda of Sustainable Development Goals , or SDGs . <p> He said the approach could be applied in the country 's research and development , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fields with multiple skills . <p> The United Nations University Institute for the Advanced Study of Sustainability senior visiting professor , Kazuhiko Takeuchi , said sustainability science was more needed now with humanity facing unprecedented challenges requiring researchers and policy makers to work together to develop integrated and transformative solutions . <p> UNESCO Senior Specialist for Sustainability Science Salvatore Arico said Malaysians must share knowledge and views to generate transdisciplinary research and education and tackle problems and challenges .
