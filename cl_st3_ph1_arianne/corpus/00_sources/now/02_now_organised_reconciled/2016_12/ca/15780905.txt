For us , this is a central question and one that touches directly on Canada 's well-being . <p> We believe a greater portion of resource revenues needs to be re-invested in creating the new economy Canada will need going forward . There are several ways we could do this , all of them well-tested . <p> Canada could start by following Norway 's lead and using a portion of resource revenues to create national or provincial wealth funds . Such funds could then be used to invest in the innovative Canadian companies that will create the jobs of the future . Norway 's wealth fund is worth about $860-billion ( U.S. ) .
