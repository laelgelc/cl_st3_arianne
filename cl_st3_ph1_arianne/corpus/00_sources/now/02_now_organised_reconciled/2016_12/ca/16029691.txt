<p> This is your last opportunity to check out the works . The exhibition showcases a wide array of large and medium-sized drawings by Montreal-based visual artist Ripley Whiteside . The creations that bear resemblance to menageries of colorful and exotic aquatic species endeavor to draw attention towards the alarming consequences of globalization and climate change , particularly in Lake Ontario . The use of rare , beautiful and foreign underwater species now inhabiting the Greater Toronto Area reveals the artist 's deep investigation of the aqua-cultural development . The artist sees the " aquariums " through downtown as a condensed form of the dislocation of aquatic life and highlights the menace of loss of habitat , pollution , overfishing and other environmental problems that have degraded biodiversity in the rapidly expanding global city , Toronto .
