<p> The fall session of the P.E.I. legislature was a lengthy one -- falling two days short of running into a 6th week . It was the longest fall sitting in years . Several things stand out over those often-tumultuous 19 days . <p> Contrary to Premier Wade MacLauchlan 's arguments , the highlight was not the new Municipal Government Act , which passed final reading Thursday night . Without question , the bill is important and will affect every municipal unit across the province . <p> It was the plebiscite on democratic renewal , which emerged as the defining issue . The plebiscite ended just a week before the house reconvened and the government 's refusal to recognize the result , which favoured mixed member proportional ( MMP ) , caused a furor . <p> To quell widespread anger , the government offered a ' binding referendum ' attached to the 2019 provincial election . That referendum will have MMP , plus another option , on the ballot .
