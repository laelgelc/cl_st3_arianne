<h> Canada must alter Constitution for indigenous leaders to play real role in first ministers conference : AFN <p> OTTAWA -- Canada needs to change the Constitution to ensure indigenous leaders can be in the room when the prime minister sits down to do serious business with the provinces and territories , the head of the Assembly of First Nations said Friday . <p> " Until that Constitution is fixed , we will continue to be excluded , " said National Chief Perry Bellegarde . <p> Indigenous leaders were frustrated at being invited to Friday 's meeting with the premiers , Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and U.S. Vice-President Joe Biden , but excluded from the afternoon session on climate change . <p> They argue that Aboriginal Peoples and their traditional way of life suffer some of
