<h> Guest Column : Honour for schoolyard bully <h> Poll <p> I just about spilled my coffee this morning while lying in bed and listening to the news . TIME Magazine 's choice for " Person of the Year " is Donald Trump ! My first impulse was to say " That 's like naming the school bully as this year 's valedictorian . " The school bully -- the person least likely to represent his class 's aspirations and accomplishments ; the person least likely to have read a book , to be able to put an original sentence together , to form and hold coherent thoughts in his head ; the person who lives by the law of the jungle , who is unfamiliar with anything resembling a moral code , a liar , a cheat , a manipulator , who fills any room he enters with dread . This does not sound like a good choice for valedictorian . It does , however , sound a lot like Donald Trump . <p> Admittedly , this announcement by TIME Magazine is little more than a marketing scheme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ honour " have often been outstanding individuals who have undoubtedly influenced events and have dominated the year 's news stories , individuals worthy of respect and honour . NBC 's " Today " program in its coverage of this announcement , says , " Every year , TIME editors select the person -- or idea -- who has most influenced the news and the world in the past year , for good or ill . " So this choice of the school bully begins to make some sense . Donald Trump has certainly been in the news this past year , despite of , and strangely , because of his numerous and unsubstantiated protestations of media bias . <p> But it 's also clear TIME has not always chosen praiseworthy people . Occasionally unsavoury and troubling newsmakers are chosen : Adolf Hitler in 1938 , Joseph Stalin in 1939 and 1941 , and the Ayatollah Khomeni in 1979 . " She goes on to refer to Donald Trump as the president-elect of The Divided States of America . <p> TIME 's justification for the choice of Donald Trump also " damns with faint praise . For reminding America that demagoguery feeds on despair and that truth is only as powerful as the trust in those who speak it , for empowering a hidden electorate by mainstreaming its furies and live-streaming its fears , and for framing tomorrow 's political culture by demolishing yesterday 's , Donald Trump is TIME 's 2016 Person of the Year .
