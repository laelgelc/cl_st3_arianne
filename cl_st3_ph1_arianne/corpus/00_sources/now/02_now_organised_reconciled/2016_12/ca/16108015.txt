<h> Government of Canada to require reports on asbestos products <p> On December 15 , 2016 , the federal government announced its intention to move forward with plans to manage asbestos . This will be accomplished through the development of additional regulatory measures . <p> To ensure that these additional regulatory measures are based on the best information available , some stakeholders , including those that manufacture , import and use asbestos , are required to submit reports to Environment and Climate Change Canada by 3pm EST on January 18 , 2017 . <p> The information collected from these mandatory reports will : <p> Inform government on the manufacture , import , export and use of asbestos and products containing asbestos for the 2013 , 2014 and 2015 calendar years . <p> Gather socio-economic information from companies , including the size of companies involved ( number of employees and revenue ) , the availability of alternatives to asbestos and whether a phase out strategy is in place or in consideration . <p> Help to inform the cost-benefit analysis of any future regulatory instrument . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
