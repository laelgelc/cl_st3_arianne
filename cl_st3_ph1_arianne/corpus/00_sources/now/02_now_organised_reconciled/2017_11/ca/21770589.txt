You 'll often see them reported starting in 1990 . Among other reasons , the defunct Kyoto Protocol called for reductions in our emissions using the year 1990 as a baseline . <p> So , how are those reductions going ? Not so well , it turns out . Ford , Heinz , Volvo , Hershey and many others have closed factories . <p> The problem is that we have not found a way to overcome the problem of growth . Today 's furnaces and car engines are more efficient than those of 25 years ago , but we are now a more populous country with more cars on the roads , and more buildings in our cities . <p> The gains in efficiency do not keep pace with the rate of growth . <p> In addition , we sidestep the meaning of words . Claims that a new technology takes tonnes of carbon dioxide out of the air are often untrue . Often it is more accurate to say that the new machines do raise carbon dioxide levels , but they do so at a slower rate than old technology .
