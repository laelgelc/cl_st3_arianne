<p> The snow geese are back , and their numbers continue to soar nearly out of control . <p> Sean Boyd , a federal bird biologist with Environment and Climate Change Canada , said Thursday that last year 's population in the Lower Fraser peaked at about 108,000 , the most since he has been involved in photo counts starting in 1987 . <p> There is every indication that this year 's numbers are as high or even higher , based on reports of good conditions on the birds ' breeding grounds at Wrangel Island in Russia . <p> " It 's good news for the geese , but not so good for the marshes , Vancouver airport and the farms , " Boyd said . " It 's probably too many geese . " <p> A more official count wo n't be known until December , based on photography of flocks from a small aircraft flying at an altitude of less than 400 metres , high enough not to disturb them . <p> The long-term management goal is to keep snow geese numbers at 50,000 to 70,000 through increased @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to three years to change the regulations in Canada . <p> " We 're way above that , " Boyd said .
