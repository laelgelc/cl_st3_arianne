For the proposed increase in biodiesel mandate , the carbon price that could be ascribed for the extra three per cent above the national requirement of two per cent appears to translate to an incremental increase of at least $5 per tonne . Thus , we are talking not $25 per tonne for Manitoba , but at least the equivalent of $36.50 per tonne or more . Within Manitoba , this is most important for natural gas , given both our cold climate that necessitates heating and the fact we will see natural gas-based heating bills increase by close to 25 per cent as a result of the levy . Alternatives can certainly help -- for example , heat pumps or biomass , the latter outlined by professor Nazim Cicek ( Free Press , Oct. 31 ) . These , however , are longer term to implement , while the levy will hit this January . <p> Consumption-based levies disproportionately affect limited-income families . Another good offset involves a simultaneous reduction in another consumption tax , with the obvious candidate being the provincial Retail Sales Tax ( RST ) . Although the numbers do not match perfectly , it is worthwhile considering a one per cent reduction in the RST to offset the levy .
