The epicentre of the 7.3 magnitude tremor was close to Ezgeleh , a town of 1,000 located 750 km southeast of Tehran . <p> The region remains without power and rescuers continue to sift through the rubble , sometimes using their cellphone flashlights . The death toll is expected to rise . <p> Iran sits atop multiple fault-lines , and in the past , far-less-powerful quakes have taken a deadly toll . In Dec. 2003 , a 6.6 magnitude tremor levelled the ancient city of Bam , killing upwards of 20,000 .
