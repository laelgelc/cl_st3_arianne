<p> BONN , Germany -- Microsoft says it aims to cut carbon emissions generated from its operations by 75 per cent by 2030 , from 2013 levels . <p> The software giant 's pledge Tuesday on the sidelines of global climate talks in Bonn , Germany , reflects the kinds of efforts large businesses are making to help curb climate change , which is largely caused by greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide . <p> Microsoft has already cut its emissions from 900,000 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent in 2013 to about 230,000 tons this year , taking it a long way toward the stated goal for 2030 . <p> But its electricity requirements will grow as the company expands its business , particularly in the field of cloud computing .
