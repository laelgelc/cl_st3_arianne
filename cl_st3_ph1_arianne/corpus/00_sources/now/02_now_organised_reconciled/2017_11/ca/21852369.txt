<p> The Trudeau government unveiled Wednesday its long-awaited national housing strategy , something the country has n't had in a generation or two . Lord knows , we need one . <p> At first blush , one might be impressed by the Liberals ' apparent commitment to housing from reading their announcement , including the claim that a whopping $40 billion will be spent to " reduce homelessness and improve the availability and quality of housing for Canadians in need . " <p> That 's until you read further . <p> In fact , in terms of actually creating or improving homes , the federal government is only ponying up $4.7 billion . Another $11.2 billion will be in the form of low-interest loans provided by Ottawa -- together with the $15.9-billion National Housing Co-Investment Fund that aims to build up to 60,000 new homes and repair 240,000 existing homes .
