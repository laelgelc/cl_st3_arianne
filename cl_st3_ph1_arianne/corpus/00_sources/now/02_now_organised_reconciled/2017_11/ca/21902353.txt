<h> News Local <h> City celebrates its housing program <p> The city used National Housing Day on Thursday to highlight some of the successes it has had providing affordable housing . <p> " Facilitating affordable housing is a top priority for the city , " Sheldon Laidman , director of housing and social services , said . " The cost of housing is the main component of household budgets . These city efforts are intended to make more housing more affordable for more people in Kingston 's tight housing market . " <p> In the first half of this year , 75 people found permanent homes through the city 's Housing First-Rapid Re-housing program for homeless people . <p> Work continues on the Rideau Heights Regeneration Strategy , and this year work included the design and initial construction of the new community centre and Shannon Park improvements . <p> The city provided funding for housing projects by Kingston Frontenac Housing Corporation , Dawn House Women 's Centre , and Addictions and Mental Health Service-Kingston , Frontenac , Lennox and Addington . <p> The city is participating in a two-year pilot @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ violence . In Kingston and Frontenac , 13 households received funding from the project . The project is set to conclude at the end of March 2018 . The Ontario government has announced a three-year , $30-million extension to the program . <p> The city is to receive more than $736,000 from the Ontario Ministry of Housing for repairs and retrofits to social housing units to improve living conditions and fight climate change . <p> The investment is part of Ontario 's Climate Change Action Plan and is funded by proceeds from the province 's carbon market . <p> The city and the County of Frontenac also support programs to help homeowners fix up their properties . <p> Kingston-Frontenac Renovates runs to March 2018 and to date has provided 10 households with more than $81,000 . <p> The Home Ownership Program provides access to funds to eligible people for a down payment for their first home . To date , five households have accessed close to $56,000 . <p> The Secondary Suites Program provides funds to help homeowners create affordable suites to rent out in their homes , helping them to pay off their mortgages .
