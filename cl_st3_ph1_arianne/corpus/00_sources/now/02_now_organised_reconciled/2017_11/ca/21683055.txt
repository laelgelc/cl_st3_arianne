<p> Premier Brian Pallister plans to introduce a $25 per tonne carbon tax starting in 2018 over the next four years , defying Ottawa 's directive . <p> The federal government has demanded that provinces establish a carbon tax that would start at $10 per tonne in 2018 and steadily increase to $50 per tonne by 2022 .
