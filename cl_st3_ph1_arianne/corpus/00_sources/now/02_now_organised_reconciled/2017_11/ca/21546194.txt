<h> Ontario is offering funding of up to $10 million for eco-projects that slash greenhouse gases <p> Oxford County will be asking the Ontario government for funds earmarked for local eco-projects that will help to reduce greenhouse gas pollution through a new provincial program . <p> On Wednesday county councillors are expected to approve an application for an initiative that is part of Ontario 's Climate Change Action Plan and offers up to $10 million for greenhouse gas reduction projects . <p> The fund supports projects such as renewable energy and deep energy efficiency retrofits to municipal facilities , as well as upgrades to drinking water or wastewater treatment plants . <p> " The primary goal of this challenge is to support municipalities who are trying to lead projects whether it 's transportation or buildings or what have you -- that will lead to a reduction in green houses gases and that will create a repeatable-type project , " said Jay Heaman , Oxford 's manager of strategic initiatives .
