<p> Designed to expand and enhance the University 's commitment to sustainability , the Action Plan lays out a number of specific initiatives to reduce McGill 's carbon footprint over the next three years . It also sets a long-term target of attaining by 2030 a Platinum sustainability rating -- the highest possible rating under the Association for the Advancement of Sustainability in Higher Education 's STARS program . <p> " We knew that if we were going to address climate change on campus , we needed to think beyond 2020 .
