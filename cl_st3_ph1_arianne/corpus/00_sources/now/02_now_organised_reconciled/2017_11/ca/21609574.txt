<h> Carleton awarded $4.7 million from Canada Foundation of Innovation <p> This past October , the Government of Canada , through the Canada Foundation for Innovation ( CFI ) , announced a multimillion-dollar investment at Carleton University for new research . <p> In a press release , Catherine McKenna , Minister of Environment and Climate Change , announced an investment of $4,746,499 through the CFI 's Innovation Fund to support research infrastructure at Carleton . The investment was made on behalf of the Minister of Science , Kirsty Duncan . <p> The CFI is an independent organization that invests in research facilities -- buildings , laboratories and equipment -- in Canada 's universities , colleges , research hospitals and non-profit research institutions . <p> According to their website , the Innovation Fund allows organizations to carry out research and technology developments that benefit Canadians , by covering part of the cost . <p> President and CEO of CFI , Roseann Runte , said in an email that the CFI periodically holds competitions in various categories for institutions to set their own research priorities . <p> Before taking on the role @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as Carleton 's president for nine years . <p> Runte also said every application is submitted by the researchers and these are reviewed by a committee which include researchers from across Canada and around the world .
