Jim Bridenstine 's assurances , however , failed to placate Senate Democrats at a contentious confirmation hearing on Wednesday , with several revisiting the Oklahoma lawmaker 's past statements on climate change , gays and immigrants and his brief management of a Tulsa museum that lost money during his leadership . <p> Florida Sen. Bill Nelson , the top Democrat on the Senate Commerce panel , called Bridenstine " divisive and extreme .
