<h> Do brokers do enough to highlight need for climate risk insurance ? <p> Imperial College London -- which has a delegation at the ongoing 23rd Conference of the Parties ( COP23 ) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) in Germany -- has reported on just how much of the world is not insured against climate risks . <p> At an official side-event organised by the Imperial College London delegation , along with the Munich Climate Insurance Initiative ( MCII ) and Munich Re Foundation , experts revealed that 70% of global damage brought about by climate change disasters are not covered by climate risk insurance . Particularly in less progressive countries , the percentage is even more troubling . <p> " In developing countries , 98% of the damage caused by climate change events does n't get any compensation unless the government steps in -- and of course , behind the damage there are people who lose their homes , workplaces , or worse , " said Simone Ruiz-Vergote , managing director of Allianz Climate Solutions . " <p> Christian Barthelt , project manager at the Munich Re Foundation , noted how insurance can cover a number of aspects of disaster risk management . The goal is to achieve improved resilience amid " a much greater " awareness of climate-related risk . <p> " An effective insurance policy depends on partnerships between the public and private sector , and the active participation of communities at risk , " said Barthelt . " You need to promote the project on the ground , develop a sustained dialogue with the different stakeholders , and ensure the long-term commitment of all those involved . " <p> Highlighted during the side-event was how insurance could be used as a tool in promoting sustainable development and adaptation to climate change . And while there 's a growing recognition of climate risk insurance , " there 's more to do , " said Imperial College London in its report about the discussions . <p> Separately Giles Crowley , head of commercial at Zurich , previously cited the important role of brokers within the realm of climate change risks . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ helping their clients scan for all forms of risk , climate risk included , " he said .
