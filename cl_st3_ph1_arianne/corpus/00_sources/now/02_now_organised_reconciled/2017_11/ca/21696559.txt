<h> Canada 's biggest national park among most threatened : international scientists <p> Canadian Press <p> Published : Nov. 15 , 2017 , 12:28 p.m.Updated : 6 hours ago <p> EDMONTON -- One of the world 's largest groups of conservation scientists says Canada 's biggest national park is among the most threatened World Heritage Sites in North America . <p> The International Union for the Conservation of Nature says Wood Buffalo National Park , which straddles the Alberta-Northwest Territories boundary , is significantly threatened by hydroelectric and oilsands development . <p> " This is quite embarrassing , " said Melody Lepine of the Mikisew Cree First Nation , many of whose members live alongside the park . <p> " It 's not looking good for Canada avoiding an endangered listing for Wood Buffalo . " <p> Wood Buffalo is a vast stretch of grassland , forest , wetland and lakes . Wood Buffalo is the only North American World Heritage Site to have deteriorated since 2014 . <p> " The big threats are from hydro dam development , " said Carolyn Campbell of the Alberta Wilderness Association . <p> Scientists have long warned about the slow drying of the Peace-Athabasca Delta from the Bennett Dam in British Columbia .
