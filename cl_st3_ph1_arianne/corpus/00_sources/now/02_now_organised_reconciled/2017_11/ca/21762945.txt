Wildlife warden Prodipta Baruah says the elephants were part of a herd of about 15 that had ventured into the area in search of food before dawn Sunday . He says the other elephants crossed the track and the final two were attempting to cross when the train struck them . Wildlife workers and veterinarians arrived to perform autopsies on the elephants before burying them in nearby pits .
