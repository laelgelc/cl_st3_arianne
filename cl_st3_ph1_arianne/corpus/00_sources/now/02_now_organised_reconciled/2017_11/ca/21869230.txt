TROY FLEECE / Regina Leader-Post <p> Federal Agriculture Minister Lawrence McAulay revealed details of the Canadian Agricultural Partnership ( CAP ) during an announcement in Regina at Agribition on Friday . <p> Aimed at strengthening the agriculture and agri-food sector , CAP is a $3-billion investment set to replace a similar policy framework known as Growing Forward 2 ( GF2 ) . <p> Like GF2 , CAP is comprised of $1 billion for federal programs and $2 billion in cost-shared programs between the federal , provincial and territorial governments , but the federal programs will be expanded and restructured . <p> Current federal programs under GF2 include AgriInnovation , AgriCompetitiveness and AgriMarketing . Under the new framework , AgriInnovation will be called AgriInnovate and three more programs will be added including AgriDiversity , AgriAssurance and AgriScience . <p> " These are the priorities that will help drive our industry forward .
