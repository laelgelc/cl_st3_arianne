<p> Growing up , Adam Arreak Lightstone lived like a migrating bird . The son of separated parents , Lightstone lived in Kingston with his father during the school year and then flew north in the summer to be with his mother in Rankin Inlet and Iqaluit , Nunavut . <p> Along with a solid platform , experiencing two very different ways of life helped 30-year-old Lightstone secure a spot in Nunavut 's legislative assembly last Monday . He upset the more experienced Monica Ell-Kanayuk . A former member of Nunavut cabinet for six years , she handled portfolios such as Economic Development and Transportation , Health and Tourism . <p> " It was a great surprise for people in Iqaluit and people paying attention across Nunavut , " Lightstone said . <p> While in Kingston , Lightstone attended Rideau Public School , Calvin Park Public School , Loyalist Collegiate and studied accounting and earned his bachelor of business administration at St. Lawrence College through Laurentian University .
