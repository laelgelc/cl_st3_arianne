<h> Firms line up to lobby for slice of feds ' Innovation Fund <p> Innovation was the name of the game in the latest batch of registrations posted to the federal lobby registry . <p> More and more companies are vying for a slice of the Strategic Innovation Fund , the Liberal government 's new $1.26 billion fund for players in the the industrial and technology sectors who want to invest more in research and development , attract more investments from abroad and grow their businesses . <p> According to new lobby registrations posted online last week , five more companies have registered to secure support from the Fund . BlackRock Metals is a materials and metals manufacturer headquartered in Montreal .
