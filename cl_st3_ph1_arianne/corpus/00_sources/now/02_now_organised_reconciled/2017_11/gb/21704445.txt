<h> Almost half of Dundee cars fail to meet emissions standards set to be enforced by 2020 <p> Almost half of privately-owned cars in Dundee will fail to meet strict emissions standards to be enforced by 2020 . <p> More than 14,000 diesel and 10,000 petrol vehicles in the city are not compliant with European emission standards , according to a council report . <p> The European emission standards monitor nitrogen dioxide levels from vehicle exhausts . Low Emission Zones , which are to be introduced in Dundee at the behest of the Scottish Government by the end of 2020 , use European emission standards to set a minimum mandatory acceptable level of nitrogen dioxide discharges . <p> Although Dundee has the fewest number of privately-owned cars of Scotland 's biggest four cities , 49.4% will not satisfy the requirement of acceptable emissions . <p> Dundee City Council has given its support for the emission zones ' implementation but said because national model framework outputs will not be available until early next year , no " definitive commitment " on introducing them in the city could be given . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of work which will need to be done before low emission zones can be finalised in Dundee . <p> He said : " Council officers have been clear they need more help in implementing these changes .
