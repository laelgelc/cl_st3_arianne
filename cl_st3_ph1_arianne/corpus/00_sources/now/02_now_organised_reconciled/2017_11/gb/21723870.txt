<h> Hundreds march in Edinburgh in the ' Battle for BiFab ' <p> HUNDREDS of workers from the troubled Burntisland Fabrication 's yards took to the streets of Edinburgh in what was one of the biggest demonstrations of its kind in Scotland for years . <p> First Minister Nicola Sturgeon returned from a UN climate change conference in Germany to make herself available for talks over the future of the crisis-hit yards , where bosses have filed a notice of intention to appoint administrators . <p> Her deputy , John Swinney , who was covering First Minister 's Questions at the parliament , said the Scottish Government was " doing absolutely everything " it could to protect the hundreds of jobs at risk on the firm 's sites in Burntisland and Methil in Fife , and in Arnish on the Isle of Lewis . <p> Around 1400 employees , contractors and subcontractors have work with contracts to manufacture equipment for renewables and oil and gas companies . <p> According to the GMB and Unite unions the problem stems from a dispute between Burntisland Fabrications , also known as BiFab , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Southern Energy ( SSE ) over unpaid bills . <p> The unions say SHL have yet to pay BiFab for contract work already completed on a Scottish windfarm , being put together as part of an SSE project . They say SHL are holding BiFab to ransom .
