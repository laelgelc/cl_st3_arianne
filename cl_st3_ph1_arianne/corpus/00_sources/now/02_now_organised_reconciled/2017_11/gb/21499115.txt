I have to admit that I have n't been there for some years but I was pleasantly surprised at how the Museum has evolved . It is a real jewel ! <p> It is great that our part of the world has done so much for the development of science through the work of Edward Jenner , just as we have affected theology through William Tyndall , and literature in the form of Laurie Lee . <p> Jenner 's empirical research not only found a treatment for the deadly disease of small pox but also was the father of vaccination .
