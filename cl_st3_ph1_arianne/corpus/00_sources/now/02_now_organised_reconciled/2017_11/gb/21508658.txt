<h> Citizens ' assembly in favour of a new pollution tax for farmers <p> Farmers would be forced to pay a new " polluter pays " tax on greenhouse gas emissions as part of a crackdown on climate change recommended by the citizens ' assembly . <p> The overwhelming majority of the advisory forum also called on the government to set up an independent body that could legally pursue the state if it failed to live up to its own climate change obligations . The assembly also said Ireland must scrap subsidies for peat extraction on a five-year phased basis and prioritise investing in public transport and cycling over private cars . <p> The group of 99 members of the public was tasked with examining how Ireland could become a " world leader " in tackling climate change and issuing a report to the government on its findings .
