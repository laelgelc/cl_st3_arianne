<p> A growing number of insurance companies increasingly affected by the consequences of climate change are selling holdings in coal companies and refusing to underwrite their operations . <p> About ? 15bn has been divested in the past two years , according to a new report that rates the world 's leading insurers ' efforts to distance themselves from the fossil fuel industry that is most responsible for carbon emissions . <p> Fifteen companies -- almost all based in Europe -- have fully or partially cut financial ties , says the study by the Unfriend Coal campaign , which represents a coalition of a dozen environmental groups including Greenpeace , 350. org and the Sierra Club . <p> Zurich , the world 's seventh biggest insurer , is the latest to shift away from coal , announcing this week that it is pulling out of coal to contribute to broader efforts to achieve the Paris accord goal of keeping global warming below 2C . <p> Allianz , Aviva and Axa have previously made similar moves .
