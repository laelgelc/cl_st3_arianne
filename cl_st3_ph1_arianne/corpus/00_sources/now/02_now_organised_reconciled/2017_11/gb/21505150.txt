<p> A green tax on farming and an end to state subsides on peat extraction are among measures to tackle climate changes recommended by the Citizens ' Assembly . <p> Prioritising investment in public transport services over new road developments and an expansion of bus and cycle lane networks have also been proposed by members of the Assembly . <p> A majority of citizens backed paying more tax on carbon intensive activities and urged the adoption of steps to encourage broader use of electric cars . <p> The proposals were among 13 recommendations on climate change made by the Assembly on Sunday . <p> An end to state subsidies for peat extraction would likely usher the end of peat fired electricity production in Ireland . The Assembly recommended that subsidies should be phased out over five years rather than axed immediately . <p> Members said the money should instead be spent on peat bog restoration and support for impacted workers . <p> Citizens also recommended a tax on green house gas emissions produced by the agriculture industry .
