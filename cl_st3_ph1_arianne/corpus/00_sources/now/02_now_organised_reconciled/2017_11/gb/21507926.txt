<h> Saint of the Day <h> Newsletter <h> Columban Missionaries Statement on opening of COP23 <p> On the opening of the United Nations ' COP23 presided by Fiji and hosted in Bonn , Germany , the Columban General Council have sent a message urging all leaders to take meaningful action on advancing the agreements made at COP21 in Paris and COP 22 in Marrakesh . <p> Columbans have been present in Fiji for more than 60 years and know intimately the impacts of climate change on small island states in the Pacific . <p> In a statement on the tragedy of Cyclone Winston in 2016 , Columban Superior General , Fr Kevin O'Neill said : " Our hearts ache for the suffering caused by Cyclone Winston . The images of human and environmental devastation urge responses of aid , mercy , compassion , love , and solidarity . On behalf of Columbans around the world , we extend our deepest condolences for all who have lost their lives , and for strength and courage in the rescue and recovery ahead . " <p> Columbans join with our sisters and brothers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ says : " We look forward to creating a global community of conscience and practice in which we learn to put belief into action in relation to our own lifestyles . We wish to be clear that we understand that systemic change is required to solve this crisis . We will continue to advocate for the policies that are so urgently needed . " <p> Pope Francis in his 2017 World Day of Prayer for Creation said : " The relationship between poverty and fragility of the planet requires another way of managing the economy and measuring progress , conceiving of a new way of life because we need a change that unites us all . " <p> On behalf of Columbans worldwide who see intimately the devastating impacts of climate change on both the human and non-human world , we offer our prayers especially for the people of Fiji and other small island nations as well the world leaders who have gathered in Bonn , that meaningful action will be taken to keep a commitment to the 1.5 degree threshold agreed to in Paris . <p> Columbans also commit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in keeping with reducing our harmful environmental impact and growing in our reconciliation with all of God 's Creation . <p> Fiji and Pacific Island are faced immediately with the climate refugee crisis . Therefore , in closing , we offer this prayer from Global Catholic Climate Movement , of which Columbans are founding members . <p> We pray that refugees from the climate crisis find peace and Fijian " Bula vinaka , " or a spirit of welcome , in their new homes . We pray that migration policies include climate refugees among those who need special consideration . <p> Rev Kevin O'Neill , SSCSuperior General5 November 2017 <h> Share this story <h> Related stories <h> Nov 5th , 2017 <h> Nov 4th , 2017 <h> We Need Your Support <p> ICN aims to provide speedy and accurate news coverage of all subjects of interest to Catholics and the wider Christian community .
