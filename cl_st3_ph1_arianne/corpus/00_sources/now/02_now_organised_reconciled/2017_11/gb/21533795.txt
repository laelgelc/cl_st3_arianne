<p> Archbishop Justin Welby , the leader of the Church of England who worked for a decade in the oil industry before becoming a priest , described climate change as a " moral crisis " on Saturday . " Climate change is pushing us toward disaster , " he wrote in the New York Times . " It is not a distant danger -- it is already with us . As we continue to burn fossil fuels , its effects will only grow . " <p> The latest UN climate change negotiations began in Bonn on Monday and Welby also sent a personal message to Fijian prime minister , Frank Bainimarama , who is overseeing the talks .
