In these documents , the university was found to have invested ? 1.3 million in Coller international , a tax-dodging private equity firm that primarily invests in Shell , alongside two deep-sea oil exploration companies . Ten colleges were also found to have pumped money into the fund , including ? 10m from hardly-cash-strapped Trinity College . <p> This news comes in the context of an ongoing campaign to make the University divest from fossil fuels .
