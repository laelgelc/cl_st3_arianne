<p> CRUCIAL talks are under way aimed at preserving the future of engineering firm BiFab . <p> First Minister Nicola Sturgeon -- who returned early from a UN climate change conference in order to help resolve the issue -- and Business Minister Paul Wheelhouse are in discussion with offshore contractor Seaway Heavy Lifting ( SHL ) . <p> Burntisland Fabrications , also known as BiFab , are in dispute with SHL , as well as Scottish and Southern Energy ( SSE ) , over unpaid bills .
