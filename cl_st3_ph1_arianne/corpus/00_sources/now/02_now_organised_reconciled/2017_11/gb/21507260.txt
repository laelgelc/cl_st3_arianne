Enough is enough . <p> Christian Aid has been actively campaigning for ten years to end the financial secrecy that facilitates tax avoidance , tax evasion and corruption . We have done this because it is poor people , in poor countries that are most vulnerable to the impact of these practices . <p> Developing countries lose many billions of dollars every year in this way , reducing their opportunity to fund their own economic and social development and thus increasing dependency on Aid .
