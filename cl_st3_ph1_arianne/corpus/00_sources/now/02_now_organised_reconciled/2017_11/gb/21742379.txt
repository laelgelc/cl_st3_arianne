<h> Best seabird breeding season for Outer Hebrides island since 2000 <p> The island of Mingulay in the Outer Hebrides has had its best seabird breeding season since 2000 , according to new figures . <p> The National Trust for Scotland ( NTS ) said 2017 has been a " bumper year " for razorbills and guillemots . <p> In fact , the population of guillemots reached an all-time high of more than 19,000 this year , the conservation charity said . <p> A guillemot in the Outer Hebrides . ( Laurie Campbell/The National Trust for Scotland/PA ) <p> The NTS has been monitoring seabird populations on Mingulay since it became responsible for the protection of the island . <p> It also cares for some of Scotland 's most significant seabird nesting sites , including St Kilda world heritage site , St Abb 's Head national nature reserve in Berwickshire and Fair Isle , Shetland , which are home to more than a million seabirds of all species every summer . <p> The charity said there is serious concern for the vulnerable Leach 's storm petrels on St Kilda , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ north-east Atlantic . <p> Some 47 nest boxes were installed at the archipelago but just three chicks hatched this summer . <p> Razorbills have had a good breeding season this year .
