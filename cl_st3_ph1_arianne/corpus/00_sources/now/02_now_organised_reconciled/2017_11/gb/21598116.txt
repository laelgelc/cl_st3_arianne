Contrastingly , EU trade accounts for only 7.2% in London . <p> Many northern regions were suggested to be 50% to 60% more dependent on trade with the European Union than London , making them more susceptible to the negative effects of Brexit . <p> Cumbria , East Riding and North Lancashire @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ would be the hardest affected . <p> With Brexit being one of the biggest challenges facing the North in the coming years , IPPR North urged millennials and generation Z to " unlock northern potential " in a much more effective manner than their predecessors . <p> In a decade , the younger generation will make up a majority of the North 's electorate and two-thirds of the working age population , but IPPR North senior research fellow Luke Raikes said they would inherit a huge number of challenges . <p> " Climate change is likely to have severe effects , robots and automation could threaten their job opportunities , and they will have to pay for the care of older people who will live much longer than ever before , " he said . <p> " They did n't cause these problems but they will have to find the solutions . " <p> Raikes added that while the North had the assets to tackle these challenges , these had been " wasted and run down " by the government over the past century .
