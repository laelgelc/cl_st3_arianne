<h> Denmark has signed a " political declaration " to support the local communities in Cox 's Bazar , which are suffering from hosting over a million refugees who fled Myanmar 's Rakhine State in the face of persecution . <p> The foreign ministry said the signing took place on Tuesday at the foreign office consultation between the countries in Dhaka . <p> Foreign Secretary Md Shahidul Haque and State Secretary of the Danish Development Cooperation Martin Bille Hermann signed the declaration during the discussion on the whole range of bilateral issues . <p> The foreign ministry said Denmark has decided to support the local people of Cox 's Bazar as the sudden Rohingya influx , repeating floods and cyclones during the monsoon have put severe pressure on their livelihoods . <p> Denmark was one of the first responding donors to the on-going crisis and has so far committed over $18.7 million for humanitarian relief of the Rohingya refugees through UN agencies and NGO 's . <p> It also announced allocating an additional $5.2 millionto the current Country Programme which includes agricultural education , climate change adaptation and human rights @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The State Secretary praised Bangladesh for hosting Rohingyas who have fled Myanmar since the influx began on Aug 25 . <p> They also discussed the mutual development cooperation . <p> The Danish state secretary congratulated Bangladesh for its results within poverty reduction , access to education and health as some of the most significant results , the foreign ministry said .
