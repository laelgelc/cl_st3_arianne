<h> Karachi <h> Share <h> ' Karachi will choose PPP if allowed to vote independently ' <p> The Pakistan Peoples Party will emerge as the largest political entity of Karachi if this city 's people are allowed to vote independently in the upcoming general elections . This assertion was made by PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto Zardari in a speech at the inauguration ceremony of the renovated Jahangir Park in Saddar on Sunday . The historic park , which has been renovated by the Sindh government at a cost of Rs200 million , was formally reopened after yesterday 's event . Bilawal said such a park was a need of the hour and was a much appreciated effort undertaken by the Sindh government for the province 's youth . Speaking of the renovation in terms of a step taken towards containing the threat of global warming , he said climate change has emerged as a problem in the entire world . He said the city 's old areas were being restored to their past glory and the people of this city being provided with the best civic facilities . Murad acknowledged efforts of all officers and agencies that were a part of the park 's renovation project . Spread over five acres , the park 's land was donated by Khan Bahadur Behramjee Jehangirjee Rajkotwala in the early 1880s . Named the Behramjee Park , the recreational facility was inaugurated on the eve of Nauroz in 1883 ; it later came to be popularly known as Jahangir Park . The facility comprises an amphitheatre , aviary as well as a library . Two RO plants and a CCTV surveillance system have also been installed at the park . The park 's maintenance will be looked after by the Sindh government for the next two years through the project directorate .
