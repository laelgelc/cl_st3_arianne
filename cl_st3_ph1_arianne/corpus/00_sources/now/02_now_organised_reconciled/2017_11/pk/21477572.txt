<h> Past in Perspective <p> " We have a single mission to protect and hand on the planet to the next generation " <p> -- Francois Hollande , President of France <p> The Climate Change agreement , also known as the Paris Agreement was signed in April of 2016 . However , the agreement came in to effect on 4th of November of the same year . It is revolutionary in the sense that the agreement is legally binding on all signatories . The accord is within the ambit of United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . The purpose is to keep the global temperature in check . Emission of green house gases will be monitored as well . The overarching purpose of the agreement is to mitigate the effects of global warming , whereby ensuring a safer future for coming generations . The agreement resonates with the needs of the world especially temperate zone countries like Pakistan which are experiencing the brunt climate change .
