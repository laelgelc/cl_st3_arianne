<h> Need for a flood master plan <p> THE Institution of Engineers Malaysia ( IEM ) is concerned and deeply saddened by the unprecedented floods in Penang and Kedah on Nov 4-5 . <p> According to the Department of Irrigation and Drainage ( DID ) and Malaysian Meteorological Department , many locations in both two states received rainfall that exceeded the 100-year Average Recurrence Interval ( ARI ) . <p> The prolonged flooding was aggravated by the high tide and strong winds which generated a storm surge . <p> This storm surge resulted in high tail water levels at all the affected river and drain outlets to the sea . <p> The high tail water at the sea not only prevented the effective drainage of floodwater run-off but also caused tidal flooding due to backflow of sea water into the catchment . <p> At the critical locations , the recorded rainfall depths in the 24-hour period was more than 300mm with a few sites exceeding 350mm . <p> IEM proposes that comprehensive flood mitigation measures need to be implemented to avoid similar floods . DID has implemented some of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ situation . <p> More allocations are needed in view of the dire situation so that the problems can be solved in an integrated and comprehensive manner . <p> Non-structural measures are also important to improve the situation such as flood forecasting and warning system for all major river basins , relocation of people from flood-prone areas and land use control . <p> Developers have a major role to play . <p> All their developments should comply with the Manual Mesra Alam Malaysia ( MSMA ) where it emphasises source control . <p> The increase of flood run-off from urban developments should be attenuated by having flood detention ponds or equivalent systems to ensure that there is no increase of flood run-off peak discharge before and after the development . <p> The MSMA also stress on soil embankment erosion and sediment control during the construction period . <p> If the control measure is not effective , large amounts of soil can be eroded from the sites into the river systems . <p> This can lower the flood carrying capacity due to reduction of the river flow section by sedimentation . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ where many locations in the country are experiencing extraordinary heavy rainfall and many have also exceeded the 100-year ARI . <p> The climate change factor should be considered in all the development and flood-mitigation measures by assessing its impact and how resilient we are in facing the changes . <p> The National Hydraulic Research Institute Malaysia Technical Guide on Estimation of Future Design Rainstorm under the Climate Change Scenario in Peninsular Malaysia is a good reference for adoption . <p> IEM proposes that the government implement a comprehensive and integrated flood-mitigation master plan to cater to the development planning . <p> IEM through its Water Resources Technical Division is willing to assist the authorities to provide the necessary technical advice .
