<p> KUALA Lumpur ranks 94th among the world 's most sustainable cities for transport , according to the 2017 Sustainable Cities Mobility Index . <p> The index was compiled for design and consultancy firm Arcadis by the Centre for Economic and Business Research . It explores mobility through three pillars of sustainability -- social ( people ) , environmental ( planet ) and economic ( profit ) -- to develop an indicative ranking of 100 of the world 's cities . <p> Kuala Lumpur scored an overall 31 per cent , an indication that the city should explore its mobility issues to benefit from an even greater economic future . <p> The report highlighted the untapped opportunity for Kuala Lumpur 's link with large-scale infrastructure and the ability to bring public transport to the masses to help the public utilise the transport system . <p> ( File pix ) Initiatives such as River of Life community development , especially those involving river cleaning and beautification , has enhanced Kuala Lumpur . <p> By 2022 , MRT Line 2 ( Sungai Buloh-Serdang-Putrajaya ) will begin operations .
