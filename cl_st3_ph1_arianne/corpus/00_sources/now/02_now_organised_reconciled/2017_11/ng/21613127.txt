<p> The Federal government on Friday signed the contract for the 3,050 megawatts ( mw ) Mambilla hydropower project valued at $5.792 billion ( close to N2.096 trillion ) in Taraba State . <p> Speaking yesterday while signing the contract agreement for the joint venture with three Chinese firms , the Minister of Power , Works and Housing , Mr. Babatunde Fashola said the development will mark a new dawn for Nigeria 's power sector . <p> While Fashola signed the contracts agreement on behalf of the Federal Government , the representatives of the Chinese firms who signed for their organisations include Yong Jun for China Gezhouba Corporation , Gan Yongskiv for Sinohydro Corporation Ltd and Ye Shijing for CGOC Group Ltd . <p> The minister further commended President Muhammadu Buhari for executing the project which has been on the drawing board for about 40 years . <p> He said , " The project is a new dawn for Nigeria 's power diversity and energy mix . This project will give us the opportunity for energy security , it will give the opportunity to comply with our Paris Climate Change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The $5.79bn project is expected to be completed in six years . The funding consist of $4.92bn from the Chinese Exim Bank and other Chinese lenders are expected to provide 85 percent of the contract sum amounting to $4.92 billion , the Federal Government will put forward 15 percent amounting to $868.87 million . <p> The scope of work for the project includes , four large dams ( Nya , Sumsum , Nghu and Api Weir ) , and two underground power house of 12 units of 250mw each .
