<h> AFC Signs Agreement with Green Climate Fund <p> The Africa Finance Corporation ( AFC ) has disclosed that it recently signed an Accreditation Master Agreement ( AMA ) with the Green Climate Fund ( GCF ) in Seoul , South Korea . Having received its accreditation in 2015 , AFC is one of the first African development finance institution to sign such an agreement . <p> Established in 2010 by the 194 countries founder members of the United National Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , the GCF launched its initial resource mobilisation in 2014 , which rapidly gathered pledges worth US$10.3 billion . <p> Following the signing of the AMA , AFC according to a statement , would have access to direct funding from GCF , rather than through intermediaries or third parties . AFC is also currently developing more than 500MW of renewable power projects across the continent , " the statement added . <p> Commenting on the partnership , the Chief Investment Officer of AFC , Oliver Andrews , said : " The consequences of climate change impact may have serious implications for the successful development of Africa 's economy . AFC is therefore highly committed to this partnership with GFC . Not only does AFC and the GCF have shared goals , we also have shared values . <p> " For example , AFC is committed to investing in post-conflict countries and those that face structural developmental challenges . Equally , the GCF also priorities societies that are highly vulnerable , in particular the Least Developed Countries ( LDCs ) .
