Mrs. Joke Jacobs , wife to another veteran actor , Olu Jacobs , experienced the power of adoption which Mrs. Dolapo Osinbajo , wife of Nigeria 's Vice-President , described as the power of love in the home of Mr. and Mrs. Silva , both of blessed memory . <p> " I received love ; I got the best of everything a daughter could ever wish for from her parents . People outside the immediate family could never tell that I was an adopted child . The bonding between me and my mother was indescribable . Available data reveals that about 25 per cent of married couples in the country are burdened by infertility issues . It 's an act of love , an act of concern and an act of willingness to look after other people and help them transform their lives .
