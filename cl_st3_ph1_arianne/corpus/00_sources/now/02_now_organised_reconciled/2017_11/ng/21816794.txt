<h> SPDC JV 's Afam VI power plant secures licence for another 10 years <p> The Nigeria Electricity Regulatory Commission has renewed the power generation licence for Afam VI Power Plant , a 650MW-capacity facility that has delivered over 24.16 million Megawatt-hour of electricity into the Nigerian grid between inception in 2008 and 2016 . <p> Afam VI is owned by the Shell Petroleum Development Company Joint Venture and located in Okoloma in Oyigbo Local Government Area of Rivers State . <p> Presenting the renewed licence to the leadership of SPDC at the Commission 's office in Abuja on Tuesday , the Commissioner , Legal , Licensing and Compliance , Dafe Akpeneye , described SPDC JV as a committed partner in the Nigerian power sector , adding that the company 's belief in the sector and its resolve to help it develop were remarkable . <p> Akpeneye said : " We hope for greater efficiency and improved operations from Afam VI in the next 10-year phase of your operations just as we look forward to working together to resolve some of the challenging issues in the power sector . " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of SPDC , Dr. Philip Mshelbila , described the Afam VI as a model worthy of emulation by government and other players in the power sector . <p> Mshelbila said : " Here 's a power plant with a dedicated gas plant operating with high uptime generating clean and efficient power from the combined cycle of three gas and one steam turbines . " <p> He lamented the challenges of debt , power evacuation and off-take which he said prevented the plant from delivering optimally at 15 per cent of the total national grid-connected electricity . <p> This also contributes significantly to the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions . <p> In 2016 , Afam VI power plant supplied approximately 12 per cent of the nation 's grid-electricity . <p> Built with the most efficient technology in the industry and utilising waste heat energy from the gas turbine exhaust , the plant generates an additional 200MW from the steam turbine without consuming any additional gas , thereby considerably reducing its carbon footprint . <p> As a Clean Development Mechanism project under the United Nations Executive Board for Climate Change , Afam @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ per year , while also maintaining excellent safety standards . <p> The operations at Afam VI have generated subcontract opportunities and employment for over 150 staff from the 16 host communities . <p> It also provided hands-on and offshore training for 15 youths in Electrical , Mechanical and Instrumentation engineering on Combined Cycle Power Plant operations and maintenance . <p> All the trainees are already employed in the Nigerian power industry . <p> Arrangements have been concluded for the training of another 15 community youths . <p> The power plant also won SPDC the Best Company in Climate Action Award in the 2016 edition of the Sustainability , Enterprise , Responsibility Awards for Corporate Social Responsibility , an annual event to celebrate organisations that invest resources to improve the socioeconomic living conditions of people in Nigeria and Africa .
