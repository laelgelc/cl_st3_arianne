<h> FG signs N2trn contract for 3050mw Mambilla hydropower project <p> The Federal government has signed the contract for the 3,050 megawatts ( mw ) Mambilla hydropower project valued at $5.792 billion ( about N2.096 trillion ) in Taraba State . <p> The Minister of Power , Works and Housing , Mr. Babatunde Fashola while signing the contract agreement for the joint venture with three Chinese firms on Friday said it marked a new dawn for Nigeria 's power sector . <p> Fashola lauded President Muhammadu Buhari for executing the project which has been on the drawing board for about 40 years . <p> He said , " The project is a new dawn for Nigeria 's power diversity and energy mix . This project will give us the opportunity for energy security , it will give the opportunity to comply with our Paris Climate Change Agreement because it will be delivering renewable energy . " <p> The $5.79bn project is expected to be completed in six years .
