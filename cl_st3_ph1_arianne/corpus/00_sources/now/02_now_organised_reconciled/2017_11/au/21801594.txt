<h> The Churchie National Emerging Art Prize winner revealed <p> The 2017 Churchie National Emerging Art Prize , aka ' the Churchie ' , has been presented to Pierre Mukeba , a Congolese-born artist based in South Australia . <p> Mukeba was awarded $15,000 for his series of six , large-scale figurative textile paintings .
