<h> Trump probably ca n't be swayed to stay in climate pact : Gore <p> BONN , Germany ( Reuters ) - Germany , Japan and other U.S. allies are unlikely ever to persuade President Donald Trump to drop his plan to quit the Paris climate agreement , former U.S. vice president Al Gore predicted on Saturday . Gore , speaking on the sidelines of a 200-nation meeting on global warming in Germany , dismissed Trump 's stated willingness to re-engage in the 2015 pact if he won better terms for U.S. taxpayers and businesses as a " smokescreen " . " It 's a classic political effort to have things both ways . He 's made his decision but he wants to give the other side a faint hope that ... that he might yet change his mind , " he told Reuters .
