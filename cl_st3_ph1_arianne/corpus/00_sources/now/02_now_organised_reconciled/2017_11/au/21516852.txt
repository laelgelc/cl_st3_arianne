<h> HSBC pledges $100 billion of finance by 2025 to combat climate change <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - HSBC has pledged to provide $100 billion in financing and investment by 2025 to help combat climate change , the bank said on Monday . <p> HSBC said it will facilitate financial flows to help boost support for clean energy and lower carbon technologies . " This will involve direct lending , bonds and project finance , alongside new products in asset management , " an HSBC spokesman said . Over recent years , HSBC has helped develop standards for issuers of green bonds and has issued its own 500 million euro ( $580 million ) green bond .
