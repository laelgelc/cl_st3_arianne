<h> Scientists call for ' reality check ' <p> Dr Wachenfeld said it had never been more urgent to tackle climate change . <p> " This is a moment for a reality check about the condition of the reef . But it 's also a call to action . This is a time for us to do more and act now to save the Great Barrier Reef , " he said . <p> Federal Environment Minister Josh Frydenberg announced $400,000 for Professor Harrison 's research , and said he was excited by its potential and would consider more funding if it was successful . <p> An additional $200,000 is being spent by the GBRMPA to identify the best sites to roll out the technology , which is also supported by the Queensland Parks and Wildlife Service ( QPWS ) and the Great Barrier Reef Foundation .
