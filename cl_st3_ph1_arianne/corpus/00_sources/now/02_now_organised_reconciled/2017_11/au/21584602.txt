<h> Bloomberg gives $50 million to aid shift from coal worldwide <p> OSLO ( Reuters ) - Former New York mayor and billionaire Michael Bloomberg is donating $50 million to help nations around the world shift from coal to combat pollution and climate change , expanding his funding outside the United States . <p> Bloomberg gives $50 million to aid shift from coal worldwide <p> The project would start in Europe and expand into other countries later on , his charity , Bloomberg Philanthropies , said in a statement on Thursday on the margins of U.N . climate negotiations among 200 nations in Bonn , Germany . The European Climate Foundation , a non-governmental group , will be the leading partner in Europe , it said . " Bloomberg 's announcement marks his first investment in efforts outside the U.S. to decrease reliance on coal and shift to renewable , cleaner energy sources , " Bloomberg 's charity said in a statement . In the United States , Bloomberg has given $110 million to a Beyond Coal campaign to close mines since 2011 .
