<h> Ask Fuzzy : When your cup runneth over <p> Drip ... drip . We 've all watched water drip into a glass until , like the final straw , the water spills over and runs into an ever-widening pool . This is a tipping point . <p> There are some fascinating things to observe about this scenario that reflect the nature of tipping points . The first is that it only takes a tiny drop to send the water over the edge . The second is that the amount of water that pools at the base of the glass is much more than the tiny drop that caused it all to happen . <p> Unless you stop the dripping , you 're going to have a whole lot more water to tidy up . You might ask what 's a little water ? It could be the difference between a standing wall and one that has been washed away . With climate change , incremental increases in greenhouse gases cause change in the whole system . The warming causes ice to melt , the ocean to warm ( and expand ) , increases the amount of water vapour in the atmosphere , and ultimately takes the entire climate system into a new , warmer state . <p> Like the water in our cup , the change to climate is larger ( or faster ) than the cause of it .
