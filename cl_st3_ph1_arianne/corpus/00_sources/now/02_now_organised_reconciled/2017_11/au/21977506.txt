<p> Fishing nets pose a serious risk to the survival of penguin species , according to a new global review of the toll taken by " bycatch " from commercial fishing . Fourteen of the world 's 18 penguin species have been recorded as fishing bycatch . <p> The review shows the level of bycatch is of greatest concern for three species : Humboldt and Magellanic penguins , both found in South America , and the endangered New Zealand yellow-eyed penguins . <p> On New Zealand 's South Island , yellow-eyed penguins are down to fewer than 250 nests . Previous population strongholds have declined by more than 75% . Conservative population models predict local extinction of yellow-eyed penguins by 2060 , if not earlier . <p> Penguins are among the world 's most iconic and loved birds , despite the fact that many people never get to see one in the wild . Indeed , the opportunities to do so are diminishing , with ten of the 18 penguin species threatened with extinction . After albatrosses , penguins are the most threatened group of seabirds .
