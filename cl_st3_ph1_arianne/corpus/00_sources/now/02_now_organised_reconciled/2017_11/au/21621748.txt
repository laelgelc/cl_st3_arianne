<h> Lessons From The Past , A Culture For The Future <p> The answer to many of the world 's challenges lies in the past , writes Teila Watson . <p> I was asked to talk about our culture and the planet in the last two years . I wanted to take the opportunity to not only look at what the last two years have been like , but also how things got this way and where we go from here . I 've realised in the last two years , how quickly two years can pass . How much can happen in two years and how important the next two years are . <p> So I will start at the beginning , beyond 60,000 years ago , because that is still how many of us locate ourselves . <p> When I say I am Kungalu , I am acknowledging that for tens and tens and tens of thousands of years , one parent of every child in my bloodline , has come from that country .
