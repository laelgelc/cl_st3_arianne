They are also taking a stand against the sheer expense of the project . The march takes place as the United Nations Climate negaotiations take place in Bonn , Germany . <p> The nuclear deal with Russian business is expected to cost South Africa up to R1.2trn , requiring the country to pay off their debts at R100bn a year .
