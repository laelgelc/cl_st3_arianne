<h> Wonders of our water beautifully captured <p> Simon Sephton has specialised in photographing water for nearly two decades . His work sets out to raise awareness of the beauty of this precious resource , but also to explore the interplay of life , light and water rather than the horrors of drought . His exhibition of new work , Water Light 2017 , takes place from December 6 to 21 at The Forge , 12 Windsor Road , Kalk Bay .
