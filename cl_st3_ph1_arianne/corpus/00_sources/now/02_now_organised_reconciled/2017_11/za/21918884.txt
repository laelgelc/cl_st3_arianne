Is there space for social investment ? Is there room for regard for socio-ecological issues ? What about shared value ? How relevant are Milton Friedman 's words given the complexity of modern society ? <p> The notion that business can concern itself with solely exploiting resources and maximising profits seems like a misguided fallacy that belongs to history . <p> I argue that the role of business has always been the same , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , business ought to make profit . Sustainability and long-term profitability could very well depend on it . <p> We often talk about business as if it is a living entity in its own right ; as if it exists in some type of vacuum .
