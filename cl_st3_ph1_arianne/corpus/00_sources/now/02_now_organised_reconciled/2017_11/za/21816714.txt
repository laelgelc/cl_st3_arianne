<h> New Sanlam company to focus on infrastructure and climate investments <p> Sanlam Investments and Phoenix InfraWorks have announced the establishment of Sanlam InfraWorks -- an investment and development company targeting infrastructure and climate related investments in global emerging markets . <p> The joint venture will bring together the capital raising track record and capability of Sanlam with the infrastructure and climate finance experience of Phoenix to produce a dynamic and scalable platform for investment and development , the company said in a statement . <p> As apart of the deal , Sanlam InfraWorks will acquire Phoenix InfraWorks ' existing 50% interest in Climate Fund Managers , the manager of Climate Investor One , a $412 million blended finance facility which provides capital for the development and execution of renewable energy projects in emerging markets . <p> " Energy , development and climate change are core issues for Africa , as well as the world , " said Nersan Naidoo , Chief Executive Officer of Sanlam Investments Core . <p> " I 'm particularly pleased that , through the establishment of Sanlam Infraworks , we 'll be able to deliver @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , promote positive change to the environment , " he said .
