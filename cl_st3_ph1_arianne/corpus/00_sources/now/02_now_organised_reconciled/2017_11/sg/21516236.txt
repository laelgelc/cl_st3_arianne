<h> PM Narendra Modi : Media should focus more on Indians than politicians <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi on 6 November urged the media to focus more on Indians rather than just the politicians . <p> Speaking at the 75th anniversary celebrations of Daily Thanthi newspaper at the Madras University , Prime Minister Modi said , " A lot of media discourse today revolves around politics . However , India is more than just us , the politicians . It 's the 125 crore Indians , who make India what it is . I would be happy to see that media focuses a lot more on their stories and achievements . " <p> He also asked the media to take a lead in the battle against climate change . <p> " Natural calamities seem to be occurring with increasing frequency across the world . Can media take a lead in the battle against climate change ? Can media devote just a little space to report or increase awareness about what we can do to combat climate change ?
