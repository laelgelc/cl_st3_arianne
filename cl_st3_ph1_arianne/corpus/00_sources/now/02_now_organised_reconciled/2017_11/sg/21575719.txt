<h> Delhi HC calls for emergency meeting on air pollution <p> The Delhi High Court on 9 November directed emergency meeting to be called by the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change Secretary with Chief Secretaries of NCR states and pollution control agencies within three days on pollution . <p> The court also directed to consider the possibility of cloud seeding as a measure to control air pollution . <p> The High Court raised questions on the decision of increasing parking fees by four times to control pollution . <p> The court also observed that the odd-even scheme last year had unclogged the city and asked the Delhi Government to consider implementing odd even vehicle movement scheme as a short term measure . <p> Delhiites woke up to another hazy day on Thursday , as a thick blanket of smog still engulfs the city . <p> The pollution level in Punjabi Bagh and R.K.
