<h> The two face common challenges and are keen to use technology to improve lives , says PM Lee <p> When Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong visited the Danish capital of Copenhagen in 2009 for a climate change conference , he was struck by the sight of people cycling after sunset in the depths of winter . <p> " I concluded that if the Danes could cycle in winter , Singaporeans must be able to do so in the tropics , " said PM Lee yesterday at a dinner he hosted for Danish Prime Minister Lars Lokke Rasmussen at the Istana . <p> The Danish PM is on his first official visit to Singapore . <p> Singapore is now working hard to promote cycling as a mode of transport and can learn from Denmark in this area , said Mr Lee . <p> Denmark and Singapore have plenty to learn from each other as they face common challenges , he added in his toast speech . <p> These challenges include an ageing population , a shrinking workforce , keeping development sustainable and preparing the economy for the future . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Denmark and it is my strong belief and hope that our two nations will continue to further develop our cooperation . <p> ' ' DANISH PRIME MINISTER LARS LOKKE RASMUSSEN <p> Both countries are also keen to use technology to improve their people 's lives , said Mr Lee , recounting discussions he had with Mr Rasmussen earlier in the day . <p> Mr Lee welcomed how Singaporean and Danish research institutes and officials are stepping up cooperation in the fields of sustainable development , healthcare and urban transport .
