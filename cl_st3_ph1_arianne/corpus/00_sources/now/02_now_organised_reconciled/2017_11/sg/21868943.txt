<h> Fight climate change by changing diet <p> To maximise Singapore 's contribution to the worldwide effort against climate change , our campaign needs to include a reduction in our consumption of animal-based foods , including fish , eggs and dairy ( Climate change campaign on the cards next year ; Nov 19 ) . <p> Fighting climate change by moving towards more plant-based diets is not a new idea . <p> In 2006 , a United Nations report , Livestock 's Long Shadow , noted that production of animal-based foods accounted for a greater percentage of human-produced greenhouse gases than all our cars , planes and other forms of transport . <p> There are two main reasons for this . <p> First , animal-based foods are inefficient . <p> Animals do not produce 1kg of meat , eggs or milk for each kilogram of food they eat . We have to feed many kilograms of plants to the animals to produce just 1kg of animal-based foods . <p> To grow this plant food , we have to cut down trees , which could have otherwise soaked up carbon dioxide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reason why our climate change campaign should include the reduction of animal-based foods lies in the fact that the digestion and excretion processes of cows , pigs , chickens , fish and other animals produce nitrous oxide and methane - greenhouse gases even more potent than carbon dioxide . <p> Making diet a cornerstone of the fight against climate change has at least three advantages . <p> One , when it comes to diet and climate change , every little bit helps . For example , it is a step in the right direction if we switch from ordering a meal of two meats and one vegetable , to a meal of two vegetables and one meat . <p> Two , all of us eat multiple times each day . Thus , we can do our little bit against climate change at every meal . <p> Three , because research suggests that increased consumption of plant-based foods helps prevent diseases such as diabetes and heart disease , we will be protecting not only the environment but also our health . <p> George Jacobs ( Dr ) <p> A version of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Times on November 25 , 2017 , with the headline ' Fight climate change by changing diet ' .
