<h> Syria signs up to Paris Agreement on climate change , leaving US as lone outsider <p> BONN ( AFP ) - Syria has officially signed up to the Paris Agreement , the UN said Tuesday ( Nov 14 ) , leaving the United States as the only country to reject the global climate-rescue pact . <p> " The Syrian Arab Republic deposited its instrument of accession of the Paris Agreement on 13 November 2017 , " said the UN climate secretariat ( UNFCCC ) hosting the annual round of global climate negotiations in Bonn , Germany . <p> War-torn Syria became the 169th of 196 countries that are members of the UN climate convention to take the legal step of ratification . <p> After Nicaragua signed up in October , Syria was the only country not to have adopted the pact .
