<h> Growing urban warmth helps city trees thrive <h> City trees are responding to climate change and urban growth , making the most of the heat island effect . <p> More than half of humanity now lives in cities and by 2050 the urban population will have grown to two thirds of all the people on Earth , which is why it is important that trees and green spaces offer city dwellers some relief from heat extremes . The species were Sakhalin fir , white spruce , small-leaved lime , horse chestnut , London plane , black locust , English oak , African mahogany , hoop pine and water oak . <p> Altogether the scientists worked with 73,685 observations of stem diameter growth over several decades .
