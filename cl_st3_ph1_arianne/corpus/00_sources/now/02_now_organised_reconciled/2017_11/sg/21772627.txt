DTSS Phase 2 is a S$6.5 billion project , with deep tunnels and link sewers alone costing about S$2.3 billion . <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <p> The DTSS Phase 2 conveyance system comprises the South Tunnel , which will transport domestic used water , the Industrial Tunnel for non-domestic used water and associated link sewers , which will largely run under the Ayer Rajah Expressway , as well as underwater . <p> The tunnels in phase two will connect to the existing ones in the first phase , which is currently serving @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2025 , the whole island will be served by the DTSS . <p> The DTSS is the nation 's way of collecting and transporting used water from the whole of Singapore to three water reclamation plants - Changi , Kranji and soon , Tuas -- for treatment . <p> The water is then treated and purified to become NEWater , or discharged into the sea . <p> INNOVATIVE FEATURES FOR A LONGER LIFESPAN <p> According to the plans , the deep tunnels will be lined with two layers to protect against corrosion , particularly from bacteria and other micro-organisms , as well as gaseous fumes in the harsh sewage environment . For underwater tunnels , there is an additional waterproof membrane to ensure water-tightness . <p> Fibre optic cables will also be embedded in the concrete lining so as to enable remote monitoring of the tunnels ' structural integrity without the need for physical inspection . The gas will then flow to Odour Control Facilities - which are located away from residential areas - treated , and then released .
