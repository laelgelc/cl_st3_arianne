REUTERS/Lucas Jackson <p> By Valerie Volcovici <p> WASHINGTON ( Reuters ) - U.S. voters picked climate change advocates in a handful of gubernatorial and state legislature races on Tuesday , providing a potential boost to state-level efforts to fight global warming through carbon trading schemes . <p> Democratic-led state and local governments have already promised to act as a counterweight to the administration of President Donald Trump , which is seeking to unwind climate change regulations it deems too costly , and remove the United States from a global pact to reduce emissions . <p> Democrat Phil Murphy won his race on Tuesday night to succeed New Jersey 's outgoing Republican Governor Chris Christie .
