<h> The Search for Oldest Ice on Earth Begins <p> Where in the world is the oldest ice on Earth ? A team of scientists is on a mission to find out . <p> A group of researchers from 10 European countries is traveling to East Antarctica this month to locate the oldest ice on the planet . Their mission is the first phase of Beyond EPICA ? Oldest Ice ( BE-OI ) , a project to collect more than 1 million years of Earth 's climate data . BE-OI research aims to answer questions about how Earth 's climate has shifted in the past , and what that means for the future . <p> Scientists will drill ice cores , penetrating deep into the layers of ice covering Antarctica , according to BE-OI scientists . The layers of ice in the cores can be read like the rings of a tree , and scientists can determine the age of the ice by counting the annual layers . Ice also traps air bubbles as it freezes , preserving the ancient atmosphere 's composition , the researchers said .
