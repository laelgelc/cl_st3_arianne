<h> Evidence Mounts Against So-Called Climate Change Hiatus <p> Evidence is mounting against the so-called climate change hiatus -- a period lasting from 1998 to 2012 -- when global temperatures allegedly stopped rising as sharply as they had before . This misconception can be explained , in part , by missing temperature data from the Arctic , a new study finds . <p> That seeming pause in rising global temperatures had been used as evidence by climate skeptics to suggest that the Earth was n't really warming at an unnatural pace . <p> To get around the data gap , researchers from the University of Alaska Fairbanks ( UAF ) and China created the first global data set of surface temperatures .
