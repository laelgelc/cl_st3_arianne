<h> First luxury Perigord truffle is cultivated in Britain <p> A specially trained dog named Bella pinpointed the location of a Perigord truffle cultivated in Wales , which scientists say is the farthest north the variety has ever been found <p> A black Perigord truffle has been cultivated in Britain for the first time , and the scientists who announced the breakthrough on Monday said climate change could make it a new British crop . <p> The 16-gramme ( 0.6-ounce ) specimen was cultivated in Wales in the roots of a Mediterranean oak tree that had been treated with truffle spores . <p> Scientists from Cambridge University and Mycorrhizal Systems Ltd ( MSL ) said they also added lime minerals in the surrounding soil to make it less acidic . <p> A Perigord truffle can be worth as much as ? 1,700 ( $2,200 , 1,900 euros ) per kilogramme in Britain . <p> It is currently found mainly in France , as well as Italy and Spain . <p> MSL said the truffles ' traditional Mediterranean habitat had been affected by drought due to long-term climate change , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the farthest north that the species has ever been found , " Cambridge University said . <p> Paul Thomas of MSL said : " This cultivation has shown that the climatic tolerance of truffles is much broader than previously thought , but it 's likely that it 's only possible because of climate change . " <p> " Some areas of the UK ... are now suitable for the cultivation of this species , " Thomas said , adding : " The potential for industry is huge . " <p> The truffle was recovered in March and found by a specially trained dog called Bella . It will be kept for posterity but the farmer can sell any future specimens to restaurants . <p> The results of the study have been published in the scientific journal Climate Research .
