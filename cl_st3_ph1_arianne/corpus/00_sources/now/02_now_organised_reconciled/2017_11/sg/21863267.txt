<p> A bizarre sight greeted people at Kanakakunnu Palace gate in Thiruvananthapuram on Friday . A woman played dead , dressed in a costume which showed her as a ' bloodied and charred ' as she was being ' barbecued ' . Under her was a faux charcoal grill . <p> The tableau ( installation ) was part of a campaign by animal rights NGO PETA . Their message : " Meat is murder , try vegan . " <p> " Most of us would never want to munch on a human , so there is no reason for us to chow down on a chicken , " 22-year-old Ayushi Sharma , a PETA campaigner , told the media . <p> This tableau protest comes days before International Meatless Day which is on November 25 . <p> Ayushi told TNM that they were planning similar installations in different places in India .
