<h> Seeking the Source of the Vanishing Great Salt Lake <p> The Great Salt Lake in Utah is roughly the same area as 75 Manhattans . It feeds and houses millions of birds of hundreds of species , provides the namesake of Utah 's capital city and some credit it for the state 's trademarked claim to " the greatest snow on earth . " <p> And it 's vanishing . <p> Since 1847 , the volume of water in the lake has dropped nearly 50 percent . More recently , the change has been so dramatic , you can see it from space . In 2016 , the Great Salt Lake reached its lowest levels in recorded history . <p> " Do we want to in 50 years change the name of our city to Salt City because the lake has gone away ? But development and water diversion since the mid-19th century have consistently reduced water entering the lake .
