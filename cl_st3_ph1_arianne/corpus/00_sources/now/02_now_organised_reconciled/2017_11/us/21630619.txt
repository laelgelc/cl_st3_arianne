<p> Life is full of risk . We protect ourselves from catastrophic events by paying for insurance policies that protect us from those events . <p> Although our homes are unlikely to burn , house and property fires do happen , and we want protection to cover our losses in the event there is a fire . <p> What if the climate scientists are right in saying irreversible sea level rise , repeated loss of mountain snowpack , more frequent and violent storms , and devastation of our forests will be unavoidable without effective action to reduce fossil fuel emissions ? <p> What if the Audubon Society is right , and our changing climate threatens the very existence of nearly half of North America 's birds ? <p> What if our military leaders are right , and global warming is , in fact , a threat multiplier ? <p> What if our sons and daughters , and their children , are left holding the bag to preserve our national security -- if millions of people around the world become displaced by rising sea levels , extreme temperatures and severe fresh @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ? <p> What if climate scientists are correct , and we are at a watershed moment with respect to taking decisive actions to conserve the natural environment we cherish -- from which we derive our livelihood -- the natural environment we have known as long as humans have walked the Earth ? <p> What if we could prevent the most dangerous global warming scenarios while continuing to grow our economy and create millions of good-paying new jobs ? <p> Even if we ca n't be absolutely certain 100 percent of climate scientists are correct 100 percent of the time , does n't it make sense to take out an insurance policy that would be effective in reducing emissions that would also improve our health , enhance our energy independence , create good-paying career jobs and preserve individual consumers ' liberty to choose what and how they consume , how they move around and how they make a living ? <p> An insurance policy such as this is readily available to us , and would be surprisingly straightforward to implement . <p> This policy will be the subject of a presentation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 1111 Abadie St. , Walla Walla . <p> The presentation will explore the climate change mitigation potential of a price on fossil fuels that returns the revenue to U.S. households through an equal dividend to every citizen . <p> The economic impact and the benefits to agriculture , including our critical drinking water and irrigation resources , will be given special attention . <p> The authors -- climate scientist Steve Ghan , Citizen Climate Lobby volunteer John Sandvig and Jennifer Syrowitz of Audubon Washington -- will present and lead the discussion .
