<p> Global carbon emissions in 2017 are expected to be around 2% higher than last year , hitting a new , unwanted record high . The news is a blow to hopes that emissions had peaked , and to efforts to keep global warming below 1.5 ? C , as agreed in the Paris climate accords . <p> New research by scientists at the University of East Anglia and the Global Carbon Project suggests that after three years of little to no growth in total global carbon emissions , 2017 will see a relatively major spike .
