<h> The Hidden Cost Of Electric Cars <p> EU electric car producers got a fresh lease on life last week as the European Commission proposed caps on vehicle emissions as a way to fight against global warming -- and to help close a technological gap with China . <p> The Asian nation is the world 's biggest supporter and producer of electric vehicles , and this year it overtook the U.S. in terms of the number of EVs on the road , with new registrations increasing 70 percent year-on-year to around 350,000 units , while Europe saw sales rise by only 7 percent . <p> In an effort to catch up with Beijing in a rapidly expanding market , EU lawmakers threatened financial penalties on carmakers that fail to cut tailpipe emissions by 30 percent between 2020 and 2030 . <p> On the face of it , this might sound like a smart move , and one supported by international institutions . Although they might have been promoted as ' zero emission , ' analysing the entire manufacturing process -- not simply the fumes they emit -- shows they have the capacity to create a far greater environmental impact than assumed .
