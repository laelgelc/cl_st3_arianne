Credit : NSF/Zina Deretsky <p> A new NASA study adds evidence that a geothermal heat source called a mantle plume lies deep below Antarctica 's Marie Byrd Land , explaining some of the melting that creates lakes and rivers under the ice sheet . Although the heat source is n't a new or increasing threat to the West Antarctic ice sheet , it may help explain why the ice sheet collapsed rapidly in an earlier era of rapid climate change , and why it is so unstable today . <p> The stability of an ice sheet is closely related to how much water lubricates it from below , allowing glaciers to slide more easily .
