He counts birds , assesses their health -- conjunctivitis remains a problem in house finches . He tallies insects and frogs . <p> He records the dates his plants bloom and wither . He plots temperatures . One bird feeder is an artful likeness of a creamy mansion ; another is a log slathered in smooth Skippy peanut butter . <p> Development can explain some of the changes Buse has noted . Buse surmises the development pushed deer out of the neighborhood , though the squirrels and chipmunks remain active .
