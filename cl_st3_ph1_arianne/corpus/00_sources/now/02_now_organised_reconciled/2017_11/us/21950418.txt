The number of schools with installed projects has grown by 46 percent , to a total of 5,500 buildings . <p> The increase in installations is directly correlated with falling costs for installations . In the past decade , the average cost of a school solar system fell 67 percent . <p> While school installations remain marginally more expensive than industrial , commercial and community solar systems , schools have been able to take advantage of economies of scale .
