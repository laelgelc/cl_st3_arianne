<h> Growth under new Govt expected to be slower than forecast - Westpac <p> Growth under the new Government is expected to be slower than previously forecast next year but should pick up momentum in 2019 and 2020 . <p> That 's the view of the Westpac Economics Team which has assessed the likely impact of the new government 's policies , as well recent signs of softening economic growth , in its November Economic Overview . <p> It has revised down its GDP forecast for 2018 , but upgraded its GDP forecasts for 2019 and 2020 . Chief Economist Dominick Stephens said the revised view is partly due to the change of government but added that New Zealand 's economic momentum was already slowing . <p> " Key drivers from recent years , such as high population growth and rising house prices , are dissipating .
