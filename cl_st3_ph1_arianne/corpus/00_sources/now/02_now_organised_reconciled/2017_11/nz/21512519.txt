<p> Rates bills are unlikely to shrink despite the second stage of a major efficiency drive getting underway at Auckland Council , a councillor is warning . <p> Photo : RNZ / Diego Opatowski <p> The finance and performance committee has called for a detailed breakdown of how savings of up to $37 million a year might be delivered , after an initial review found that potential . <p> The statutory review has so far targeted four of eventually 30 Auckland Council activities : its water and drainage operations , communications and engagement , waste , and international investment attraction . <p> Most of the potential savings so far could come by better integrating the council 's stormwater division with its separate water and sewerage company Watercare . <p> Councillor Chris Darby reminded the meeting that while better value for money might be found , so too might the need for higher spending to protect the environment and cope with climate change . <p> " I believe that we are up for some phenomenal expenditure in the decades to come , " he said . <p> " This decade - @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , but future plans I believe are going to be spending tremendously in that area . " <p> The $46 million annual spending on communications and engagement across the council and its agencies was the other major focus . <p> The opening review found potential for better co-ordination and groupwide spending , but councillors held off accepting a recommendation for an immediate 5 percent cut . <p> Some feared the cut would affect statutory consultation , or engagement with communities on projects . <p> The chief executives of the council and five agencies will report in February on the feasibility of making the cost cuts tha the review thought could be achieved . <p> International investment and global partnerships was found to be money well spent , but where some better co-ordination could occur . <p> The waste review found good work and significant savings had been made in the domestic waste area since the council was formed in 2010 , but much greater potential existed in trying to reduce commercial waste .
