<p> With only 246 breeding pairs of yellow-eyed penguins remaining in the South Island , researchers are now calling for governmental action on set nets . <p> A recent Ministry for Primary Industries investigation recommended introducing fisheries observers on boats , video monitoring of bycatch and managing set nets in important penguin foraging areas . <p> For penguin advocates , the changes to set net procedures are most important . <p> " We welcome the proposed introduction of video surveillance on fishing boats as an important step in monitoring and reducing bycatch , " said Forest and Bird seabird advocate Karen Baird . <p> " But for the yellow-eyed penguin , the situation is so urgent that we also need to immediately establish set net closures in important yellow-eyed penguin foraging areas . " <p> An MPI spokesperson said in a statement the Ministry is committed to managing the impact of fishing on all seabirds , including yellow-eyed penguins . <p> " We have already increased observation of set net fishing in the range of the Whenua Hou population , and most of the mainland yellow-eyed penguins .
