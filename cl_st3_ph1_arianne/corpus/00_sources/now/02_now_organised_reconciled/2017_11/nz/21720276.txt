<h> New Zealand ' high-risk ' for almost every natural disaster possible <p> New Zealand is on a fault line , covered in volcanoes and surrounded by the ocean . Credits : Video - Newshub ; Image - File <p> A new report has highlighted just how vulnerable New Zealand is to natural disasters . <p> The Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery - funded by the World Bank the EU and others - has looked at the risks posed by earthquakes , tsunamis , volcanoes and more across the world . <p> The latest data has been compiled into an online tool called ThinkHazard . Users can look at the overall risk factors for entire countries , or drill down to cities and regions . <p> New Zealand as a whole is ranked high-risk for almost every possible catastrophe , except extreme heat and water scarcity . <p> The disasters we need to prep for , according to ThinkHazard are : <p> Wildfires - Each year there 's a 50 percent chance or greater a wildfire will result in loss of life or property . Northlanders have a little less to worry about than the restt , while Wellingtonians and those in the South Island are unlikely to be in harm 's way . <p> Floods - " Potentially damaging and life-threatening urban floods are expected to occur at least once in the next 10 years , " with the most at-risk regions the West Coast and upper South Island . <p> Landslides - It 's hard to say where they might happen " as these depend on local geological conditions and other non-climatic factors " , but one look at State Highway 1 near Kaikoura shows how devastating they can be . <p> Tsunami - There 's a more than 20 percent chance of a potentially damaging tsunami in the next 50 years - perhaps even higher if climate change pushes up sea level . <p> Landslide following @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Earthquake - The South Island 's most at risk , as virtually every Kiwi would already have guessed by now . ThinkHazard says there 's a greater than 20 percent chance of a potentially damaging quake in New Zealand over the next 50 years . The further north you go past Wellington , the lower the risk . <p> River flood - A potentially life-threatening flood caused by a river overflowing is expected to happen at least once every 10 years , most likely in the Waikato - where the country 's longest river resides . <p> Cyclone - While climate change may reduce the number of cyclones that reach New Zealand , ThinkHazard says those that do are likely to be stronger than in the past . It gives a more than 20 percent chance of a cyclone with " potentially damaging wind speeds " in the next decade .
