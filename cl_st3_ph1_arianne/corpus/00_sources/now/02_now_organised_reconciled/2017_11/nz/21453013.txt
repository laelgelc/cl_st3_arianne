<h> Time to have your say on options for the Christchurch residential red zone <p> IVAN IAFETA <p> Last updated 05:00 , November 3 2017 <p> Peter Ball <p> Aerial view of the residential red zone , Christchurch . <p> OPINION : From amphitheatres to zorbing , more than 5000 ideas have shaped Regenerate Christchurch 's 10 possible combinations of uses for the Otakaro Avon River Corridor Regeneration Area . <p> We are seeking feedback on 10 possible combinations , the things we 've considered to get to this point and if there 's anything we 've missed to help inform the next stage of planning . More than 2000 pages of reports -- with summaries -- have been published to give the community as full a view as possible of the information that sits behind the potential land uses and explore how the area could make the biggest difference to Christchurch . <p> The 10 possible combinations -- none of them mutually exclusive -- have stimulated a wide range of views and it 's exciting to see people 's enthusiasm and passion for the area . <p> Iain McGregor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Te Tamariki - Children 's Day held in the residential red zone in March , is one of more than 5000 ideas suggested for how to use the red zone . <p> Many people have a strong connection with the area , including those that are still living there , former residents and neighbouring communities . The idea of more housing in or around this area or any form of development raises many questions , including some important ecological ones and questions around how we adapt to climate change . <p> There are 5442 houses that are no longer in east Christchurch and this has affected the area in many ways , including the local economy and the membership of various clubs and societies . Residential development is one way of increasing the local population and contributing to the local economy . <p> We welcome wide public debate on the merits of all possible land uses , including the land swap idea . It 's vital that we have this discussion about the future as a community . <p> Perhaps the most powerful idea is to use about half the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ open space corridor that 's almost twice the size of Hagley Park , spanning 10 kilometres from Barbadoes Street to Bexley and New Brighton . <p> Stretching up to 150 metres on either side of the river , the proposed open space corridor ( or " green spine " as it 's sometimes called ) reflects the community 's desire for a connection from the city to the sea that also respects the condition of the land next to the river . <p> This space , which is proposed on all 10 combinations , could feature many ideas suggested so far including permanent paths , trails , playgrounds , gardens , forest , wetlands and community initiatives . New transport links and wetland planting to help improve water quality could also feature alongside community spaces and places for people of all ages and abilities including tree-houses , forest schools and an outdoor amphitheatre . These uses are all explored in the community spaces and places report , available on our website . <p> Another idea that is considered in the reports that we have published is a new east -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Road . This could provide a valuable new connection between the commercial centres of Aranui and Wainoni with Shirley . <p> Other interesting possibilities considered in the published reports include a theme park , a wave surf park and a city-to-sea gondola -- the types of attractions that together could help create an amazing destination and generate up to four million visits to the area every year . <p> Once we 've heard from the community , the next step is for Regenerate Christchurch to refine the combinations into a shortlist and then hold a major exhibition early next year asking for public feedback on the shortlisted options . <p> The shortlisted options could be a mix and match of some , or all , of what 's out now . This is why your feedback on all aspects of the different combinations is so important to us and why we are n't asking people simply to pick their favourite . This step is n't a popularity contest for ideas . It is about asking if there 's anything else we should consider before shortlisting takes place . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ costs , benefits and potential trade-offs for the shortlisted options and people will be asked to give feedback in many ways over a longer timeframe . One of the big questions we still have to discuss is who pays for these ideas , and where we can find opportunities for investment and financial return to contribute to the city and to New Zealand . <p> When you look back on the history of Christchurch as a city , there have been some key decisions made over the years like creating Hagley Park or choosing a location for Cathedral Square and the airport . <p> We are approaching another decision that will shape the future of our city for hundreds of years , and influence how people experience life in Christchurch for generations to come . Lessons learnt from around the world show similar projects are best completed over generations , taking advantage of opportunities to change and adapt over time . This is an exciting time and we 'd encourage everyone who loves our city to stay informed and get involved .
