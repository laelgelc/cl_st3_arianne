<p> To help raise funds for Oxfam 's climate change and sustainability programmes in the Pacific , a brand new umbrella is being launched , featuring iconic Kiwi native ferns , birds and flowers <p> New Zealand artist Flox is hoping a new Blunt umbrella she 's designed will make a major difference to Oxfam in the Pacific . <p> The limited edition Blunt umbrella , which is covered with Flox 's trademark artwork of native birds , ferns and flowers , is spearheading a campaign to raise money to help Pacific communities affected by climate change . <p> For every Blunt brolly sold , $30 will go towards the Oxfam campaign . <p> SUPPLIED <p> Flox aka Hayley King is teaming up with Oxfam NZ to help launch a limited edition umbrella to raise cash for the Pacific . <p> Flox says her brief for the project was " selecting some New Zealand birds that had the most amount of colour " before using them in her designs .
