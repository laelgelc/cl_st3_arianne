We 'd swap dirty looks and smoldering passive aggressive comments , while our shared husband ( my ex , her current ) stood charmingly hapless on the sidelines . The fact that she is younger , blonder , and ( let 's just be honest ) hotter than me , would make our " catfight " even more entertaining . <p> But , I do n't hate my ex-husband 's new wife , stepmother of our two boys . Much to the contrary , in fact : I like her . <p> I like her because she convinced my 15-year-old , sports-crazy son to dance in a West Coast swing class .
