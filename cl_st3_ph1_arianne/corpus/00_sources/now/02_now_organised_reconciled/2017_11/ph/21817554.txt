<h> Eastern Visayas new dev't plan adopts climate change strategies <h> Eastern Visayas new dev't plan adopts climate change strategies <p> Wednesday , November 22 , 2017 <p> THE 2017-2022 Eastern Visayas Regional Development Plan ( RDP ) has included one chapter for Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation ( DRR-CCAM ) , making it unique from plans of other regions in the country . <p> National Economic and Development Authority ( Neda ) Regional Director Bonifacio Uy said the DRR-CCAM is important to support the attainment of the region 's twin goals of robust and sustained economic growth and reduced poverty and inequality in all dimensions . <p> " Given the region 's vulnerability to disasters due to its geographic location facing the Pacific Ocean in the eastern portion and within the Pacific Ring of Fire , we saw the need to highlight this aspect by taking the initiative of providing a separate chapter for DRR-CCAM , " Uy said . <p> Eastern Visayas is the only region in the country with separate chapter for disaster and climate change in the new RDP , said Uy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have worsened poverty situation in the region , as they destroy the people 's source of livelihood . <p> " Since natural disasters are expected to be aggravated by the effects of climate change , the strategies for the medium-term will be geared toward enhancing implementation of actions related to disaster prevention and mitigation , preparedness , response , and rehabilitation and recovery , " Uy said . <p> " These strategies should be highlighted after lessons on the importance of disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation and mitigation ( DRR-CCAM ) have been learned after super typhoon Yolanda and other past disasters , " he added . <p> The plan has presented 12 lessons learned from Super Typhoon Yolanda that ravaged many areas in the region in 2013 . These are vulnerability risk assessment and multi-hazard mapping , DRR-CCAM mainstreamed in various local policies and programs , environmental management , infrastructure system , risk financing and insurance . <p> Other post-Yolanda lessons learned are end-to-end early warning systems , local DRRM councils and offices , partnership and coordination among key players and stakeholders , disaster response operations , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ CCAM elements mainstreamed into human settlements , and comprehensive land use planning and zoning <p> The Eastern Visayas-RDP has twin regional goals of robust and sustained economic growth and reduced poverty and inequality in all dimensions by prioritizing on three sectors -- agriculture and fishery , manufacturing , and tourism -- as regional economic drivers . <p> The regional plan is considered a partner of the Philippines Development Plan for 2017 to 2022 under the term of President Rodrigo Duterte geared towards achievement of " Ambisyon Natin 2040 . " <p> " Eastern Visayas will follow a development trajectory that will ultimately lead to the country and region 's long-term vision . The region will also contribute to the vision of national societal goal of laying the foundation of inclusive growth , a high thrust society and a globally competitive economy by the year 2022 , " Uy said . <p> The new RDP seeks to attain a 6.8 percent to 7 percent economic growth by 2022 .
