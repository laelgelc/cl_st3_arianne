The Nepali delegation led by Minister for Population and Environment Mithila Chaudhary will take part in several events , sharing Nepal 's concerns on the effects of climate change that the country confronts . <p> The twenty-third session of the COP to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCC ) is a global event which brings together policy makers , delegates , scientists , lobbyists and private sectors . <p> Nepal has prepared a detailed status paper especially for the COP23 . The Legislature-Parliament ratified the accord in October the same year . <p> Global leaders converging at the COP23 are expected to push for implementation of the 2015 Paris Agreement by developing guidelines , incorporating various issues like adaptation , emission reduction , transparency of action and support , technology transfer and capacity-building of the poor and highly vulnerable countries like Nepal . <p> Besides this , the global event is also supposed to finalize the Paris Agreement rule book to be adopted at the COP24 in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the country has urged the international community to build momentum and trust among themselves for preparing the rule book guiding implementation of the Paris Accord . <p> Nepal , besides stressing timely adoption of the rule book and implementation of the accord , has asked for technical and financial support it deserves as a Least Developed Country ( LDC ) . <p> The status paper incorporates topics such as programs under the Paris Accord , mitigation measures , adaptation , means of implementation , and cross-cutting agendas like climate and gender .
