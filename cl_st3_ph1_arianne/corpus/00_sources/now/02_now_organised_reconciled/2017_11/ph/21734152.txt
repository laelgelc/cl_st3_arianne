File <p> MANILA , Philippines -- Vulnerable nations , including the Philippines , have pressed rich countries -- the biggest emitters of greenhouse gases -- to ensure " climate justice " by taking concrete actions to reduce global warming during a meeting on the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) in Bonn , Germany this week . <p> Sen. Loren Legarda , head of the Philippine delegation to the five-day 23rd Session of the Conference of the Parties ( COP23 ) , called on rich countries that are parties to the Paris Agreement to immediately fulfill their commitments to the pact . <p> " We come to COP23 with a strong call for climate justice : that those who are most vulnerable , who suffer the most from the adverse effects of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change , are empowered and enabled , in terms of capacity and finance , to fight back , " Legarda said in her address to the meeting on Thursday . <p> Under the Paris Agreement , she added , the industrialized nations are supposed to contribute heavily to financing major projects among vulnerable and developing nations most affected by global warming . <p> The vulnerable nations earlier formed the Climate Vulnerable Forum ( CVF ) , an international alliance of countries highly vulnerable to global warming . <p> It also created the Vulnerable 20 ( V20 ) , consisting of finance ministers from member-states of the CVF , which aims " to act collectively and decisively to promote the mobilization of public and private climate finance from wide-ranging sources .
