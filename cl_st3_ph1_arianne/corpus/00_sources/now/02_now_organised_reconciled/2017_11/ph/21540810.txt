<h> DOE asks agencies to be energy efficient <h> DOE asks agencies to be energy efficient <p> At the front . Energy and Utilization and Management Bureau Director Jesus Tamang ( center ) says the government should be first when it comes to energy efficiency and conservation . ( SunStar Foto/Ruel Rosello ) <p> AMID threats to climate change , the Department of Energy ( DOE ) pressed local government units to implement energy efficiency measures and ordinances . <p> Speaking to European Union delegates and local partners during the Energy Smart Visayas forum yesterday , DOE Energy Utilization and Management Bureau ( EUMB ) Director Jesus Tamang said the agency wants full cooperation from all branches of government , especially LGUs , in helping realize the DOE 's energy efficiency and conservation plans . <p> " We believe that if LGU can start mandating the use of energy efficient technologies , then we can have an accelerated adoptation , " Tamang said .
