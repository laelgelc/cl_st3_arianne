<h> Pangan : My assessment of the 31st Asean summit <h> Pangan : My assessment of the 31st Asean summit <p> THE Philippines has chalked up good plus points in its hosting of the regional Asean summit last week . It was a fine though herculean work as all hands were on deck , so to speak , to carry through the giant responsibility of being chairman of this year 's event . <p> The Association of Southeast Asian Nations or ASEAN which observed its 50th anniversary August this year , is credited for bringing in stability and economic development in the region . From the small group of Malaysia Philippines and Indonesia , it has blossomed into one cohesive association now compared of Brunei , Vietnam , Laos , Myanmar , Cambodia , Singapore and Thailand and it now compares with the European Union in terms of gross domestic products and investment . Today , ASEAN has established itself as an economic force with a combined GDP of $2.55 trillion as of 2016 and each member country has posted an average annual rate of 7.0 percent in economic growth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ before the Association 's founding . Dialogue partners led by the US contributed to the luster of the convergence . <p> Ramon J. Farolan , columnist of Philippine Daily Inquirer laments though : As we mark Asean 's 50th anniversary , the question we must ask ourselves is this : As one of the five founding Fathers of the Asean , why have we fallen behind Singapore , Malaysia , Thailand and Indonesia in terms of economic progress ? It is very likely that Vietnam has already overtaken us . That leaves us on top of Cambodia , Laos and Myanmar . And if we continue with our merry ways , pretty soon Cambodia may just pass us by . <p> Excellent observations by Mr. Farolan indeed . We have no one to blame but ourselves for our mediocre economic performance . We are so good at planning , but our resolve in implementing these plans remain wanting and dormant . That is why we become the doormat in the economic race . <p> *** <p> There are more issues in the Asean agenda like the North Korea nuclear threat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and climate change . <p> The Association needs to address these issues in other summits or conferences as we do nt have the luxury of time . <p> *** <p> The just-concluded Asean summit must have seen solutions to topics brought forth but it has more challenges to meet and solve .
