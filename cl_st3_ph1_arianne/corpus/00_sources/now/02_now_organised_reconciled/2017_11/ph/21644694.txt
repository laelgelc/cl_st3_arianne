<p> The Foundation for the Philippine Environment celebrates its 25 th year with the gathering of its officers and staff , members of its regional advisory committees and experts ' advisory panel , and partners from various groups all over the country on November 14 to 17 , at Nature 's Village in Talisay City , Negros Occidental . <p> The FPE is the first grant provider for civil society initiatives related to biodiversity conservation and sustainable development .
