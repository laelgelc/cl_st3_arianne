<h> Climate change imperils one in four natural heritage sites -- report <h> By Agence France Presse <p> Climate change imperils one in four natural World Heritage sites , including coral reefs , glaciers , and wetlands -- nearly double the number from just three years ago , a report said Monday . <p> Among the ecosystems most threatened by global warming are coral reefs which bleach as oceans heat up . ( AFP photo ) <p> The number of sites at risk has grown to 62 from 35 in 2014 , when one in seven were listed , according to the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) , which released the report at UN climate talks in Bonn , Germany . <p> Among the ecosystems most threatened by global warming are coral reefs which bleach as oceans heat up , and glaciers which melt . <p> " Climate change acts fast and is not sparing the finest treasures of our planet , " said IUCN director general Inger Andersen . <p> " Climate change now threatens the very fabric of our society , threatens our identity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ yet delicate patchwork of natural heritage , " she told journalists in Bonn . <p> The report found that 29 percent of World Heritage sites faced " significant " threats , and seven percent -- including the Everglades National Park in the United States and Lake Turkana in Kenya -- had a " critical " outlook . <p> " The scale and pace at which it ( climate change ) is damaging our natural heritage underline the need for urgent and ambitious national commitments and actions to implement the Paris Agreement , " said Andersen . <p> Negotiators are gathered in Bonn to work out a nuts-and-bolts rulebook for executing the pact adopted by nearly 200 countries in the French capital in 2015.
