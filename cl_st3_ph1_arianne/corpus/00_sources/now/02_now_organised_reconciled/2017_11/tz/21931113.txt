<h> Energy majors to cut methane emissions <p> BP , Eni , ExxonMobil , Repsol , Shell , Statoil , Total and Wintershall have committed to further reducing their methane emissions from the natural gas assets they operate around the world . The energy companies also agreed to encourage others across the natural gas value chain -- from production to the final consumer -- to do the same . <p> This commitment was made as part of wider efforts by the global energy industry to ensure that natural gas continues to play a critical role in helping meet future energy demand , while also addressing climate change . " The commitment by companies to the Guiding Principles is a very important step ; we look forward to seeing the results of their implementation and wider application .
