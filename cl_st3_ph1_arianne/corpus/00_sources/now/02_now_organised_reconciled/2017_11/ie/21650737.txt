<h> 29% of us believe ' supreme being ' guided evolution <p> A survey carried out for Science Week has revealed interesting attitudes towards science in Ireland . <p> In relation to climate change , 69% of Irish public surveyed ( 92% of the science community ) agreed that the earth is getting warmer as a result of climate change . <p> Almost the same number ( 68% ) of the Irish public surveyed ( 92% of the science community ) agreed that climate change was mostly due to human activity . <p> More than half of those surveyed said they believed it was not safe to eat food with pesticides . <p> 43% of those surveyed also thought that genetically modified foods were not safe to eat , with only 14% of the science community feeling the same way on that issue . <p> A disparity between the
