This , the WMO cautioned , is leading to ever more " severe ecological and economic disruptions " . Then , global average temperatures were 2-3 degrees warmer than today and sea level was 10m-20m higher . Climate system inertia means there is a delay before permanently high CO2 levels trigger these devastating effects . <p> Sea level rise of even a fraction of that , would create tens of millions of climate refugees , triggering ever-worsening crises that would likely cause many nation states to fail , while bringing the global economy to its knees .
