<p> Joella says she intends to raise the issue of racism in Ireland with the Leo Varadkar . <p> " I think it 's avoided in some cases because people might feel uncomfortable , " she said . <p> " But I think it should be something that 's taught in schools -- children should know that it 's not right to discriminate against someone because of the colour of their skin or where they 've come from . " <p> Joella also spoke to Mr Varadkar about issues of concern for young people in Ireland , as expressed to UNICEF Ireland , including disability rights , poverty , climate change and the Sustainable Development Goals .
