<h> Science celebration launches with a bang <p> This year 's Cork Science Festival promises to make science even more fun than ever before . A Celebration of Science , the festival runs from 10-19 November , and has something for all ages . <p> The Cork Science Festival line-up of over 100 events includes explosive workshops such as ' flames , flashes and frozen flowers ' to crime scene investigation , rainbows to laser shows , ' cool planet ' climate change workshops , ' down-under dinosaurs ' demos , building DNA games and the chemistry of cocktails and a special screening of The Matrix . <p> Supported by Science Foundation Ireland , and part of Science Week Ireland , the Cork Science Festival features two flagship events , which are free , open to all the family and will provide hours of educational entertainment . <p> ' Celebrate Science ' takes place at Gateway UCC on Sunday , 12 November from 12 Noon until 5pm and provides a great free day out for all the family . <p> The ' Community Festival of Science ' takes place on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 6pm and again provides an entirely free and fun engagement for every age with all things science . <p> Schoolgirl Emma Dunphy is surprised by a Tyndall cleanroom scientist at the launch of the Cork Science Festival .
