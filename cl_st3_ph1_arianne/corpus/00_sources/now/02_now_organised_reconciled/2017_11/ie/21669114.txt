<h> More from Mag <h> Japan 's Ambassador Addresses an Uncertain World <h> Talking to SOFIA , last night , the Japanese ambassador Mari Miyoshi discussed Japan 's place in a changing world . <p> Grace MeagherDeputy Radius Editor <p> Embassy of Japan <p> Tonight saw the Society for International Affairs ( SOFIA ) welcome another ambassador for an evening of political discourse . Her Excellency Ms Mari Miyoshi addressed the audience on issues regarding Japan 's activities with the UN , a contentious issue now that the country 's prime minister has decided to revise their pacifist constitution . <p> Before the talk began , reading material was distributed by the Japanese Embassy celebrating the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Japan and Ireland . Miyoshi began the talk outlining five major issues she believes are at the forefront of international politics : North Korea , China , terrorism , globalisation and cyber security . Illustrating how these " threats are creeping up " on us , she emphasised how the " increased sense of uncertainty " had necessitated a need to " secure global balance " . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ describing Japan and Ireland 's shared values and history within the UN . Back in Japan , she told us that there are 14 St Patrick 's Day parades around the country each year . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The ambassador herself began working in Japan 's foreign affairs ministry in 1980 , a ministry that " put emphasis on having good relations with our neighbours " .
