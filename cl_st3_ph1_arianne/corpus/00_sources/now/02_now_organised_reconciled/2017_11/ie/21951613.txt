highly-anticipated White Paper on ' The Future of Food and Farming ' . <p> The paper , presented by EU Commissioner for Agriculture Phil Hogan in Brussels , outlines guidelines on a new-look policy that will provide for " simpler rules and a more flexible approach " . <p> The paper confirms that a support system based on direct payments will continue . <p> However , future supports will be based more on results than compliance . <p> Commissioner Hogan also revealed that a new CAP implementation system will be introduced to give member states a much greater degree of subsidiarity ( more autonomy ) . <p> EU officials are confident that such changes will ensure that CAP " delivers real results in supporting farmers and leads the sustainable development of EU agriculture " . <p> A key
