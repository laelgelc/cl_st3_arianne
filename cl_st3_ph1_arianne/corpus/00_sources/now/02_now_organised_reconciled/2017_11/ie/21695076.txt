<h> Ireland ranked as Europe 's worst-performing country for action on climate change <p> A new report has singled out Ireland as the worst performing country in Europe for taking concrete action to tackle climate change . <p> The 2018 Climate Change Performance Index puts Ireland in 49th place out of a total of 56 countries identified in the Index . Together , the 56 nations account for over 90 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The report 's release comes as Ireland 's Minister for Climate Action Denis Naughten prepares to address the United Nations climate talks in Bonn , Germany . <p> The highly regarded Performance Index is produced annually based on a joint analysis by leading European think-tanks Germanwatch and the NewClimate Institute . <p> It ranks countries based on emissions levels , renewable energy take-up , energy use and climate policy , with Ireland performing poorly across all sections . <p> Minister Denis Naughten is set to address the COP23 talks in Bonn , Germany Photo : Environment Ireland <p> Ireland 's Results <p> Ireland 's low rating stems from the likelihood that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our 2020 emission reduction targets , the report states . <p> The Index report also points out that Ireland 's performance in terms of reducing greenhouse gas emissions is " very poor " . <p> " Ireland is nowhere close to being on track concerning its well-below-2 ? C compatible pathway with both its current level as well as its 2030 target , " the report states . <p> While the report points to a " very positive trend in the development of renewable energy " the current share of renewables in the overall energy mix is " insufficient " . <p> Source : Climate Change Performance Index 2018 <p> Reaction <p> In response to the findings , the Green Party issued a call for a radical new energy , transport , agriculture and industrial policy to restore Ireland 's " green reputation " . <p> Ireland 's ranking as the worst performing European country " brings shame to our country " , according to the party 's leader Eamon Ryan , TD . <p> He said that the government has to date failed to outline concrete plans for promoting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , or to rein in emissions from the agriculture industry . <p> " Taoiseach Leo Varadkar said at the start of his time in office that he wants to make climate action a priority but the truth is that his Government is doing nothing to position Ireland onto a more sustainable path , " Mr Ryan added . <p> " It is long past time for a radical change in direction before it is too late to restore our good name .
