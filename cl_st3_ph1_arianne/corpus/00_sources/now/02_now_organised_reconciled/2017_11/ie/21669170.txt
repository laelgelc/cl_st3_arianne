<h> Europe feeling the heat of our changing climate <p> The World Meteorological Organization ( WMO ) tells us that 2017 will be one of the warmest years on record , with many disastrous events including drought , floods , and heat waves taking place across the globe . <p> While it is abundantly clear that climate change is occurring on a global scale , we have often been told that everything will be okay in Ireland and Western Europe in the short term . <p> There have even been calls for Ireland to embrace the potential advantages that come with an additional few degrees Celsius , such as an extended tillage and horticultural growing season . <p> Relax and wait for the days when we can enjoy a glass of Cabranet Sauvignon , we are told . <p> However , as the WMO 's latest Statement on the State of the Climate tells us , both Ireland and our European neighbours are already feeling the reverberations of climate change . <p> This year has been regarded as one of the most devastating years for hurricanes , with three major @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Maria . <p> Both Harvey and Irma were category four hurricanes , marking the first time in history that two hurricanes of such force have made landfall in the United States in the same year . <p> Irma also holds the record for longest wind speeds on record , registering wind speeds of 300km/h for 37 hours .
