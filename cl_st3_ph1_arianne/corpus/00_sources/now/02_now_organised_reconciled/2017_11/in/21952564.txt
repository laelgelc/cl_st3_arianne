<p> The Supreme Court special bench of Justice J Chelameswar and Justice AM Sapre on Tuesday heard a writ petition filed by NGO Jheel Sanrakshan Samiti ( JSS ) regarding the construction of a five-star hotel by Mumbai-based developers Vardha Enterprises on the island situated in the middle of Udaisagar Lake in Udaipur . <p> Advocate Prashant Bhushan , appearing on behalf of JSS , submitted before the apex court that the petitioner 's grievance arose not from the construction of the hotel structure per se , but from the ancillary development works undertaken subsequently .
