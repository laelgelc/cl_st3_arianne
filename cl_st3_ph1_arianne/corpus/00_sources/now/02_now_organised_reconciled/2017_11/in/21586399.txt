<h> ISB to launch Clean Energy Lab in Hyd <p> TNN Nov 10 , 2017 , 01:30 IST <p> Hyderabad : Premier city-based B-School , the Indian School of Business ( ISB ) , on Thursday announced that it has joined hands with Shakti Sustainable Energy Foundation to launch the Clean Energy Lab , an incubation programme to support early stage non-profit startups working on creating policy impacts in the clean energy space . <p> The Clean Energy Lab has invited entries from non-profits working in the areas of renewable energy , climate change and energy efficiency and the deadline is November 15 , 2017 . <p> The selected participants will have access to funding of up to Rs 4.8 lakh , mentorship as well as access to infrastructure at the ISB campuses , strategic advisory support and access to networks and impact measurement support , among others . The year-long incubation programme will commence in January 2018 .
