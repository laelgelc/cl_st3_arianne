Increased triglyceride accumulation in the liver can also lead to inflammation , fibrosis , cirrhosis and liver failure , a serious medical condition known as NAFLD or NASH.Additionally , the company is currently evaluating Saroglitazar in several clinical trials for treating liver conditions like NASH and Primary Biliary Cholangitis ( PBC ) .
