<p> A three-day national workshop on ' Disaster Resilience of Kashmir in the Face of Climate Change ' will be organised by department of environmental sciences , Abdul Ahad Azad Memorial Degree College from 13 to 15th November , 2017 . <p> " Climate change has emerged as one of the serious challenges , mankind would face if not addressed well in time . According to the 2014 synthesis report of IPCC 's 5th assessment Report , climate change exposes people , societies , economic sectors and eco systems to risk , " said professor Mubashir Jeelani , coordinator of the event . <p> He said that encompassing an interdisciplinary approach , this workshop is going to be the first of its kind in the Kashmir , involving participants from diverse disciplines . <p> The Jammu and Kashmir government is on the brink of becoming a minority shareholder in a power generation company -- a joint venture with National Hydroelectric Power Corporation -- following its failure to clear equity share for starting construction work on hydropower projects
