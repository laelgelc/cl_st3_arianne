Guidelines on Environmental Management of Construction &amp; Demolition Wastes were brought out by Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) in March , 2017 . CPCB guidelines address the issues pertaining to abatement of adverse environmental impacts specifically arising from C&amp;D waste management activities . <p> BMTPC has also published guidelines on Utilisation of C&amp;D wastes as a useful resource for building materials and components . All these guidelines supplement each other and provide a technical and legal framework for effective utilisation and management of C&amp;D waste .
