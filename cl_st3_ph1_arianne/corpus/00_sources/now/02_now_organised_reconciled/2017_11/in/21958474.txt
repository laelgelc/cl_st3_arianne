<h> Trials of the Republic <p> Professor Kanti Bajpai , a liberal secular scholar , has written a series of essays on the issues confronting contemporary India in his thought provoking book , " India in the World : Trials of the Republic " . This collection is published by Times Group Books . <p> This volume reflects Bajpai 's keen eye , and is a blunt take on the challenges and complex realities that contemporary India faces and will continue to face if not solved in a timely manner . Karan Thapar , the eminent TV journalist , has rightly praised the author by saying that he has often learnt more from Bajpai 's one column than from reams of literature on the same subject . <p> The collection is divided into four key areas and chapters , which are-foreign policy , social policy , politics and the ideas of India . <p> The author has compiled his articles published in the Times of India over a span of seven years , which encompass the last four years of UPA 2ND and First 30 months of NDA Govt led @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other South Asian , South East Asian , African countries as well as other regional groupings in order to shake the Indian policy makers .
