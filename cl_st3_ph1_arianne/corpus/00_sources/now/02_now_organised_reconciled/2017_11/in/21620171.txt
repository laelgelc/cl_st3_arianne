<p> Of all the wild cats around the world , the snow leopard remains particularly enigmatic . <p> High up in the snowy heights of Central Asia and the Himalayas roams this gentle feline , which is rightfully called the " mountain ghost " . This is hostile terrain -- far from human habitat -- where only the toughest survive . Spotting a snow leopard here is a game of chance for those who 've braved the elements in order to study these shy creatures . <p> The International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) recently moved the snow leopard 's status from " endangered " to " vulnerable " on its Red List . The modification was based on field studies and estimates over the years . <p> According to the IUCN , a species whose numbers are fewer than 2,500 , and declining at a certain percentage over a period of time , falls under the " critically endangered " criterion .
