<h> Indian School of Business to launch incubation for green firms <p> The lab aims to identify individuals and thinkers with big , audacious ideas . <p> Indian School of Business ( ISB ) in collaboration with Shakti Foundation is going to launch a first of its kind incubation programme for non-profits in the clean energy space . <p> Hyderabad : Indian School of Business ( ISB ) in collaboration with Shakti Foundation is going to launch a first of its kind incubation programme for non-profits in the clean energy space . The lab aims to identify individuals and thinkers with big , audacious ideas to create research based policy impact in India and build their concepts into scalable institutions . <p> The Clean Energy Lab invites entries from non-profits , working in the areas of renewable energy , climate change and energy efficiency . The registerations for this program will be closed by November 15 , 2017 . <p> Prof. Rajendra Srivastava , Dean , ISB , said , " Clean energy is the need of the future and India is already making a mark globally for its initiatives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ambitious agenda to become self-reliant in generating renewable energy by 2022 . ' ' The yearlong incubation programme will be commenced in January 2018 and will include dedicated mentorship with assigned experts , capacity building and training sessions and a multitude of opportunities to network and partner with potential collaborators .
