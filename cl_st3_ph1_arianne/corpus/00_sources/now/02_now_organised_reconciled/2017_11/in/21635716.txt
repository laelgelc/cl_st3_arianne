The discussion was moderated by TOI 's coordinating editor , Amit Bhattacharya , and witnessed enthusiastic participation of Tricity residents , who fired a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ poisonous smog to the timing of Rahul Gandhi 's elevation as Congress president . <p> " I have endeavoured to remove the author 's bias in a biography and brought out not what I thought of Gandhi but how she thought of herself , " remarked Ramesh . " The book is not in a thematic form but in chronological order . This was aimed at establishing that Gandhi took out time for the environment even when she was facing grave political and economic crises . For example , in 1971 when there was so much tension over Bangladesh , Gandhi took two days off and spent it in a room with conservationists and experts to draft the Wildlife ( Protection ) Act , even though she continued receiving slips of paper from her defence advisors in that room amid mounting tensions with Pakistan , " recounted Ramesh . <p> November 12 also marks the 121st birth anniversary of India 's legendary birdman , Dr Salim Ali , whom Ramesh described as having " the most profound influence on Gandhi 's interventions in favour of wildlife conservation . " The bureaucracy could have otherwise hampered the power and positivity of Gandhi 's wildlife and environment decisions had not men like Dr Ali reached out to her , " Ramesh told TOI . <p> " The four major legislations that govern the protection of the environment and wildlife of India , and survive to this date , did not just come about in Gandhi 's tenures but they were directly a result of her decisions . She directed the bureaucracy in no uncertain terms to bring out the far-reaching Wildlife ( Protection ) Act , 1972 , the Forest Conservation Act , 1980 , Air ( Prevention &amp; Control of Pollution ) Act , 1981 and the Water ( Prevention &amp; Control of Pollution ) Act , 1974 . She also set up a separate ministry of environment and forests at the level of the government of India and remained its minister till her death in 1984 , " said Ramesh . <p> Ramesh 's book is regaled by charming anecdotes from the love of her @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gandhi with a tiger and her father , Jawaharlal Nehru feeding Red panda cubs at their Teen Murti residence in Delhi , which was a veritable mini-zoo . " In contrast to her authoritarian ways as a politician , Gandhi was remarkably benign when it came to getting state governments and her own party chief ministers to enforce laws in favour of environment and wildlife . For example , Madhya Pradesh CM SC Shukla refused to implement a ban on tiger shooting even though he had been appointed by Gandhi and his brother , VC Shukla , was a Union minister in her cabinet . Both the Shuklas were running a tiger hunting company for foreigners and were reluctant to obey Gandhi . So , Gandhi could have been tougher on enforcement of wildlife laws when her own party leaders did not fall in line . However , had she got too tough , she may have failed in environment protection as it was not a major idea of those times as India needed development to alleviate poverty , " explained Ramesh . <p> Ramesh did not spare his own @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ development over environment .
