<h> Fiji calls for urgency in talks to implement climate accord <p> Delegates make pictures in front of a photo of an island at the COP 23 Fiji UN Climate Change Conference in Bonn , Germany , Monday , Nov. 6 , 2017 . ( AP Photo/Martin Meissner ) <p> Fiji 's prime minister called for a sense of urgency in the fight against global warming Monday , telling negotiators " we must not fail our people , " as he opened two weeks of talks on implementing the Paris accord on combating climate change , which is already affecting his Pacific island nation . While diplomats and activists gathered in Bonn , the U.N . weather agency said 2017 is set to become the hottest year on record aside from those impacted by the El Nino phenomenon . The talks in Germany are the first major global climate conference since President Donald Trump announced that the U.S. will pull out of the 2015 Paris accord unless he can secure a better deal , and the first time that a small island nation is chairing such a conference .
