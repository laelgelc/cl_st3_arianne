Lokur also justified judicial activism due to absence of laws and poor implementation of the existing laws.The Compensatory Afforestation Funds has over Rs. 40,000 crore , with shares of the Centre and states , released in July last year after being passed by the Rajya Sabha.The fund is meant to aid both Centre and states in afforestation activities and is supposed to be controlled by the Compensatory Afforestation Management and Planning Authority ( CAMPA ) .
