<p> He convened a meeting with officials concerned over progress of Forest Department in past 10 years , seeking suggestions and guidance , here on Thursday . He was accompanied by Field Director , Project Tiger of Kawal Tiger Reserve , Saravanan . <p> Karnan said teak and mango saplings were being planted under Haritha Haram programme . So , he wanted officials to create awareness over merits of planting of various trees including fruit , flower bearing ones and medicinal plants among farmers . He suggested that it would provide alternative source of income to farmers . <p> The IAS officer instructed officials of Forest Department to persuade farmers to use organic fertilizers for crops . He asked them to enforce relevant provisions of Law for protecting forest cover . <p> He told them to book non-bailable cases against those who chop trees illegally . The Collector advised authorities to create awareness over usage of LP gas and conservation of forests among rural folks who depend on vegetation for firewood . <p> The Collector asked teachers to sensitise students on afforestation by conducting tours to deer parks , picnic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conservation of green cover , massive plantation drive , advantages of forests , pollution , climate change , should be incorporated in school textbooks . <p> Karnan said environment could be protected by planting saplings in every household . He asked the authorities to check the menace of wild pigs and raise trees as part of bio-fencing around forests .
