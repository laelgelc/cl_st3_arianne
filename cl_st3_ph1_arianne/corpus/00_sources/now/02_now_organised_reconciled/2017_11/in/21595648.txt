<h> Yashoda Group of Hospitals Bags National Health Care Award <p> Hyderabad , Nov 10 ( UNI ) Yashoda Group of Hospitals has bagged HEAL foundation 's National Health Care Award for its excellence in innovative hospital administration . Group Chairman G Ravinder Rao received the award from Telangana Minister for Health Dr C Laxma Reddy at 5th National Health Writers and Editors Convention held here today . HEAL Foundation is the largest network of Health Writers across India and SAARC countries . In an endeavour to present to the nation its finest healthcare providers , the foundation has initiated the Healthcare Awards at the National level for the healthcare industry . The Convention is attended by health and medical science writers from across the country . The objective of the convention is to initiate a dialogue among stakeholders who play an imperative role in shaping the opinion and perception among general masses .
