<h> Chanda industrial area AQI worse than Delhi <p> Nagpur : On Monday when the apex court observed Delhi 's situation to be emergency-like , the air quality recorded in the industrial area of Chandrapur was even worse than the Capital . The average air quality index ( AQI ) of Chandrapur was recorded to be 500 while at most places in Delhi it was between 400-490 on Monday . <p> Pollution control board officials blamed it on " an error in the monitoring equipment " . Experts TOI spoke to said there could be other possibilities . <p> As per the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) data , the AQI at MIDC 's Khutala monitoring station was at a consistent high till 9pm . An AQI of 500 , which falls under the ' severe ' category , can affect healthy people and have serious impact on those with existing diseases . In the second station , the AQI is 269 , which is again categorized ' unhealthy ' . <p> The ' prominent pollutant ' at the MIDC station was stated to be nitrogen dioxide ( NO2 ) . While its permissible limit for 24 hours is 80 micrograms per metre cube ( mpcm ) , the NO2 levels from 12am to 8pm remained constant at 500 mpcm . Particulate matter PM10 and PM2.5 kept varying between ' poor ' and ' moderate ' zones . <p> According to officials from the Chandrapur office of the Maharashtra Pollution Control Board ( MPCB ) , " A fault has been detected in the monitoring equipment and this could be a reason .
