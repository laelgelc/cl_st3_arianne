<h> Earth Today Clarendon students to get water security <p> ACCESS TO water has always been a challenge for students of Mount Airy Primary and Infant School in Mocho , Clarendon . <p> The tank at the school is insufficient to meet the needs of the 121 students and 13 members of staff , not to mention community members . <p> But in six months ' time , that will change when the school completes construction of a 20,000-gallon Ferro-cement tank and the upgrade of its guttering system , and installs a solar-powered water pump . <p> This is thanks to grant funding from the Environmental Foundation of Jamaica ( EFJ ) , which administers the Special Climate Change Adaptation Fund on behalf of the Adaptation Programme and Finance Mechanism . <p> The school was one of 51 community and civil society grantees who received funds from the EFJ . <p> " We are very happy to have received the grant . It will make us better prepared for the dry season .
