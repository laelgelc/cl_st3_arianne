<h> Ground Broken for 100,000-Gallon Water Tank at Mason River <p> Environment <p> August 17 , 2015 <p> Written by : Garfield L. Angus <p> Photo : Melroy Sterling <p> Minister of Water , Land , Environment and Climate Change , Hon. Robert Pickersgill ( left ) , and Member of Parliament for North Clarendon , Hon. Horace Dalley , break ground for construction a $35.7 million water storage facility at Mason River in the parish on Friday ( August 15 ) . The undertaking is part of a $202.2 million upgrading of the Mason River Water Supply System . <p> The project , on which work got underway in August 2013 , is being undertaken by the National Water Commission ( NWC ) . <p> It entails replacement of existing ageing pipelines , and installation and rehabilitation of pumps , reservoirs and tanks , to enable the storage and distribution of water to householders . <p> Minister Pickersgill informed that two contracts associated with the project have been completed , one for an intake facility at a cost of $21.3 million , and the other for construction of a pumping station and sump for $14 million . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ materials has been approved by Cabinet and is to be signed by the NWC , while the pipe laying contract has been approved by the National Contracts Commission and is to be submitted to Cabinet for approval . <p> The final contract , for the supply and installation of pumping plant equipment , has been submitted to the National Contracts Commission and is awaiting its approval . <p> Minister Pickersgill urged the residents , who will benefit from the improved water supply to pay their bills , and to conserve on the use of the commodity . <p> " We need to adopt the mindset that we should conserve on the use of water , whether or not we receive rain , " he said , noting that climate change continues to make rainfall unpredictable . <p> Member of Parliament for North Clarendon , Hon. Horace Dalley , for his part , told the residents that as soon as the project is completed , rehabilitation work will be done on the Mason River main road .
