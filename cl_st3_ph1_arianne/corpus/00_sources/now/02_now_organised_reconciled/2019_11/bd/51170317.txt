How difficult is it to translate Tagore ? <p> " It has been quite courageous , maybe crazily courageous , of me to try to translate Tagore 's poetry from Bangla into German . It has to be understood that Tagore did n't really translate his poetry from Bangla into English . It was rather a paraphrase -- lyrical prose -- an approximation of the content , not the words .
