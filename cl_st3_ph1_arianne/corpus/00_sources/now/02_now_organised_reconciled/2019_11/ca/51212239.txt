<h> Costs of Kingston 's climate emergency declaration begin to show <p> KINGSTON -- Skeptics of city council 's climate emergency declaration pointed to the budget increase for a planned community centre as an example of things to come . <p> On Tuesday , council approved a $1.95-million increase to the construction costs of the East Kingston Community Centre , about half of that amount is to fund features of the building that would lower its greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The proposed building would feature geothermal heating and cooling , rooftop solar panels , a high-performance building envelope , LED lighting throughout and the potential for additional green technology . <p> With the exception of a diesel-fuelled emergency generator , which accounted for about $500,000 of the cost increase , the building would not directly rely on fossil fuels . <p> The green energy additions promise to reduce the building 's greenhouse gas emissions by 86 per cent , almost halving the
