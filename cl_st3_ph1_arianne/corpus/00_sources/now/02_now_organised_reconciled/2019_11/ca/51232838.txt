Over 90% of climate scientists , and most Canadians , recognize that human activity has triggered a massive release of greenhouse gases ( GHG ) , leading to temperature increases , changes in rainfall , and raising the frequency and magnitude of extreme events such as flooding , wildfires , and droughts . <p> Canadians recognize that climate change is a serious problem and 60% feel that it either is , or will soon be , an emergency . However , the facts speak otherwise . <p> It is time to recognize the obvious and declare that Kelowna , like the rest of the world , is facing a climate emergency .
