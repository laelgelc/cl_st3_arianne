November 21 , 2019 . Steve Russell/Toronto Star <p> Ontario 's energy minister is facing heat for the second day in a row , after calling a website that rejects " consensus science " on climate change one of his " favourite periodicals . " <p> Greg Rickford cited the site -- called Climate Change Dispatch -- in Monday 's question period , when asked about the PCs cancellation of more than 750 renewable energy projects . <p> He read an excerpt from one particular piece that claimed the " subsidized feed-in " of wind energy caused German electricity prices to soar . " Well , I 'll be a chicken fried in goose fat , Mr. Speaker . It turns out that there 's another jurisdiction that 's having the same challenge as we are , " Rickford told the House . " <p> When faced with questions from reporters on Tuesday , Rickford evaded giving a yes-or-no answer on whether he believes human activity is a significant contributor to climate change . Rickford did not speak with reporters after question period on Wednesday , with his spokesperson saying his " comments from yesterday stand . " <p> Rickford was unapologetic on Tuesday , when quizzed about his choice of citation . " Those quotes are compelling , in my respectful view , so I 'll not explain or defend my position . As a man well-studied , I 've relied on literature sources that oppose my views and my opinions . " <p> He offered a similar explanation during Wednesday question period , when the issue was raised by NDP MPP Ian Arthur -- who told the legislature that Rickford 's source had never been cited in Parliament , nor the U.S. Congress or Senate .
