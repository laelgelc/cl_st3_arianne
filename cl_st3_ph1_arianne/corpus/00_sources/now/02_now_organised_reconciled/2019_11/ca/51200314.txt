We define resilience thinking as an approach that recognizes the complex interactions between society and our ecosystems , embraces the idea of change , and acknowledges uncertainty . <p> Resilience thinking requires acknowledgement from municipal governments that climate-related changes may be unanticipated and sometimes catastrophic , as in the flooding in Alberta in 2013 . With resilience thinking , however , we can move forward with solutions that allow municipalities to continue to flourish despite both changes we anticipate and changes that surprise us . <p> The Netherlands , for example , approaches flood mitigation in ways that provide " room for the river " -- increasing the river 's depth , storing water , and relocating dykes .
