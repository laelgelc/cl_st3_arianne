<h> Traffic on Algonquin Boulevard East to be disrupted on Friday <p> NEWS RELEASE CITY OF TIMMINS ****************** The City of Timmins wished to notify the public of an expected traffic disruption on Algonquin Blvd East between Spruce Street and Balsam Street on Friday , Nov. 29 , 2019 between 11:30 a.m. and 1:30 p.m. as a result of the planned Climate Change Demonstration . <p> Police will be on scene to redirect motorists .
