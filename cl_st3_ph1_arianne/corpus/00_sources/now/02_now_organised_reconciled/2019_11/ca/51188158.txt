<p> Some quick facts about Climate Transparency 's 2019 report card on Canada 's climate plan as part of its annual report card on G20 climate action . <p> Canada 's emissions picture within the G20 : <p> -- Canada 's per-capita emissions in 2016 were 18.9 tonnes per person , compared with a G20 average of 7.5 tonnes per person . ( For comparison purposes , the average passenger car produces about 4.5 tonnes of emissions each year ) . <p> -- The lowest per-capita emissions in the G20 were in India , at 1.9 tonnes , and the highest in Australia , at 21.8 tonnes . <p> -- Almost one-third of Canada 's emissions come from transportation , and per-capita emissions from transport are the second-highest in the G20 . Canada 's emissions from transportation are also rising while they are falling in the G20 as a whole . <p> -- Buildings produced 13 per cent of Canada 's emissions , and Canada has no national strategy to reduce emissions from existing buildings .
