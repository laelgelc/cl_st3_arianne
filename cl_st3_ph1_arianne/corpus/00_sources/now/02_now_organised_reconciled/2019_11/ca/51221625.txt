Because regionalism remains such a major issue , the composition of cabinet is a matter Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and his team must take very seriously . This is true partly because they need to " show love " to parts of the country where they hope to win more seats next time around . <p> This love must extend to all the regions of the country , including Quebec , where the Liberals performed worse than expected on Oct. 21 , winning only 35 seats out of 78 , compared to 40 in 2015 . Although losing five seats might not look so bad in absolute terms , this figure must have been greatly disappointing to the Liberals .
