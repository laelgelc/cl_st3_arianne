Small , divisive agendas make for a small , divided country . It is not enough to simply please ' the base , ' " he said . <p> " As difficult as the process may be to arrest and to mitigate the effects of global warming , the work can not be left to the next fellow . The stakes are too high , the risks to our planet and the human species too grave .
