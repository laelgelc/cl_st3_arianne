" -- Nicolas Boulerice , Le Vent du Nord <p> Le Vent du Nord ( or North Wind ) is getting territorial -- in the broadest sense of the word . <p> The fabulous Quebecois folk band addresses our sense of place in their latest release Territoires , from the particular land mass they call home to more of an internal , emotional map . <p> It 's a big place . <p> " We put an ' s ' to the Territoires because we did n't want to just imply Quebec , " explains veteran musician-songwriter Nicolas Boulerice . " Of course Quebec is our territoire but so is North America and the world . My house is a kind of territory . Childhood is a territory by itself . " <p> The songs on Territoires -- roughly half originals , half traditional -- take inspiration from both history and the heart , from a look at French explorer Samuel de Champlain 's dream of bridging Indigenous and European peoples in a united nation ( Le Pays du Samuel ) , to a beautiful love song ( Le Soir Arrive ) , to a tune celebrating gardening ( Le Jardinier ) . There 's even a song about the mythical monster of Lake Champlain , known as Tatoskok or Champ . <p> That last number was a natural since Lake Champlain is tied to the Richelieu River Valley where three members of the band were born and still live today , about a half-hour outside of Montreal . It 's an ongoing thing . <p> Some of their numbers are borrowed directly from the folk legacy , while others are brand new but written in a traditional style . Then there are those hybrids that borrow poetry or music and add new music or poetry to complete the song . <p> " It 's important to us to keep the link with the oral tradition and the very old stuff , but we have a lot of discussion about what a traditional tune is , and what is folklore . Their most unusual , archaic instrument has to be Boulerice 's vielle a roue , or hurdy gurdy , that bizarre hybrid of bowed strings and keys with origins dating back to 11th century Europe with its own unique sound . <p> Boulerice grew up with music in his blood going back a couple of generations .
