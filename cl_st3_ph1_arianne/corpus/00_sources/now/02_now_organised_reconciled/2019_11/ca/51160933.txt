<p> The province 's new environmental targets need to guarantee an end to Nova Scotia 's outdated reliance on coal-burning power and commit to 100 per cent renewable-based electricity by 2030 , says Green Party leader Elizabeth May . <p> The Sustainable Development Goals Act should also include a commitment to shut down Donkin coal mine as early as possible , she said . <p> " Nobody should be mining coal anymore , " said May , arguing it 's the province 's shared responsibility to help transition those workers into more sustainable green jobs . <p> " The goal should be on decarbonizing the electricity grid by 2030 . The act should make clear that Nova Scotia should shut down its coal firepower plants as quickly as possible . " <p> The act , which was introduced by Environment Minister Gordon Wilson last Thursday and passed into law on Wednesday , aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 53 per cent below 2005 levels by 2030 and move the province to a net-zero carbon footprint by 2050 . <p> The province 's emission goals fall short of what the science says is needed to keep global heating under @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ per cent below 2005 levels , but May credits the province for legislating hard caps . <p> " This is breaking out of the pack and much closer to what needs to be done , " May said , adding that the federal government has yet to enact emission cutting legislation .
