<h> B.C. zooms past rest of Canada in sales of zero emission vehicles <p> B.C. continues to be the national leader by a long shot when it come to the adoption of zero emission vehicles including electric , plug-in hybrids and fuel cell cars . <p> New numbers released by Electric Mobility Canada show that a full 10 per cent of all new passenger vehicles sold in the province fall into the the ZEV category , well above the national average of 3.5 per cent . Quebec is the next closest province at seven per cent . <p> Al Cormier , founding president of Electric Mobility Canada , said B.C. 's unique combination of incentive programs , policies and legislation around ZEVs has been effective . <p> " All that has provided incentives and encouragements to buyers of vehicles in B.C. , to researchers , to companies . So generally there are great conditions to promote electric vehicle sales and it 's working , " said Cormier . <p> The province currently offers a rebate of up to $3,000 on the purchase of a new electric vehicle , which can be stacked with the $5,000 federal government rebate that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ maximum $6,000 rebate available through the B.C.
