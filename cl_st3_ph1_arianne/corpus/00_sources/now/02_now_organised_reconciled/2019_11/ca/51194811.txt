<p> Businesses are catching on to the benefits of sustainable practices , and local companies have a role to play in the big picture . <p> That was one of the messages that came out of Thursday 's presentation , called The ROI ( return on investment ) of Sustainability , by Bob Willard . <p> Willard , a former IBM executive who worked with the company for 34 years , is the founder of Sustainability Advantage , the goal of which is to equip people with the resources they need to make positive changes as champions of the environment . <p> There 's long been a perception that ' going green ' comes with a hefty price tag . Those days are already on the way out , Willard said , noting solar and wind power , for example , is now cheaper than coal . <p> " The technology costs for solar and wind have dropped like a stone . The need to charge extra for products is also going down , " he said . " People are surprised -- almost embarrassed -- that these things are not as costly as they thought .
