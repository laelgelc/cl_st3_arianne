<p> A calculation of Branford 's greenhouse gas emissions will be used to set and measure emission reduction targets . <p> The emissions inventory was presented to councillors in two categories -- for the Corporation of the City of Brantford and for the general community -- at this week 's operation and administration meeting by Rochelle Rumney , who was hired this year as the city 's climate change officer . <p> The inventory is the first step toward the city creating a climate change action plan . Next year , city staff will report to council with recommended targets and actions to reduce emissions . <p> Data presented in the report show that the community 's greenhouse gas emissions total based on 2018 data was 700,080 tonnes . <p> Emissions from residential , commercial/institutional and industrial buildings are fairly evenly split across the three sectors , said Rumney in her report , but , combined , emissions from buildings make up 57 per cent of community emissions . Personal transportation makes up 41 per cent of community emissions . <p> Corporate greenhouse gas emissions totalled 15,001 tonnes .
