<h> Martin : As budget crunch looms , London councillors must show taxpayers respect <p> London treasurer Anna Lisa Barbon sets out the reasons for the higher than expected budget , citing increasing costs from provincial cuts during a special council budget meeting . ( Mike Hensen/The London Free Press ) <p> It 's time London councillors really earn their $52,000 and respect the taxpayers who pay them . <p> Crunch time lies ahead for the city budget and storm clouds suggest we face significant tax hikes unless they curb city spending . <p> There was a time when councils were loathe to raise property taxes at all , fearing taxpayers ' wrath . No more . Currently , they began with a target increase of 2.7 per cent , well above the rate of inflation . So much for " leadership " on the tax front , with most outside boards and commissions submitting spending plans exceeding that . <p> That target translates into an extra $82 in taxes on the average residential property -- for each of the next four years , or more than $328 in all , once compounded .
