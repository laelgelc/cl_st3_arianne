<p> In ancient times , theories of justice often were discussed among philosophers and religious thinkers . Justice was frequently thought of as a synonym for fairness . Included also were ideas on what the just person owed to God and what was owed to the state in which the person was born and raised . <p> In Plato 's Republic , 380 BCE , he described a dialogue between Crito and Socrates . Socrates knows that if he does not escape from Athens he will be condemned to death because his teachings were judged to have corrupted Athenian youth . Socrates ' friends offered to help Socrates to escape from the Athenian justice system . Socrates argued that because his father raised him in Athens that he , Socrates , was obligated by law and by principles of justice to suffer death either by his own hands or by Athenian laws . <p> Approximately 700 years later Saint Augustine in The City of God described justice as " love serving God and ruling well all else .
