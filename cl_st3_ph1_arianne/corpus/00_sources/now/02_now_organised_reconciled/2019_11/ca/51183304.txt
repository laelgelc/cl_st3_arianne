<p> Carrie Cussons is excited . The transition to the new Halifax Convention Centre that began 18 months ago is paying off in ways even she did n't expect . " I think what 's been most amazing for me , our shareholders , the city , the province , the business community and the tourism sector is how the Centre is really living up to and exceeding expectations . Cussons joined the organization in 2009 and has led its transition to the new downtown Halifax Convention Centre , including operational preparations , corporate restructuring and human resources strategy .
