The UN expects the urban population to rise to 6.7 billion -- 68 per cent of humanity -- by 2050 . There will be 43 mega-cities with more than 10 million inhabitants each as early as 2030 , mostly in China and other Asian countries . <p> Building out these and hundreds more large cities will require much of the remaining allowable carbon budget .
