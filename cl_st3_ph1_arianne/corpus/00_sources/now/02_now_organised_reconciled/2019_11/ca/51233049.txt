<h> Green schools recognized <p> A number of Hastings and Prince Edward District School board elementary schools have received recognition for their efforts in the Ontario EcoSchools program . Kente Public School ( gold ) ; Bayside Public School ( gold ) ; Hermon Public School , ( silver ) ; Madoc Public School ( silver ) ; Stirling Public School ( silver ) ; York River Public School ( silver ) ; and Harry J. Clarke Public School , ( bronze ) have all been recognized for their efforts . Ontario EcoSchools is a provincial program that addresses environmental issues in the school system . It was developed in 2002 by the Toronto and District School Board in an effort to nurture environmental leaders , reduce the ecological impact of schools , and build environmentally responsible school communities and in 2005 it was expanded to include schools across the province . Each school receives an EcoSchools plaque in recognition of the achievement . It marks the second year HPEDSB schools have participated in the program with Deseronto Public School , Madoc Public School , Stirling Public School and York River Public School receiving certification last year . Their efforts connect with our goals to be leaders in public education , and to support all students to be globally-minded learners and leaders . " Schools must submit game plans for the academic year in the fall , including action plans for six categories including teamwork and leadership ; energy conservation ; waste minimization ; school ground greening ; curriculum and environmental stewardship . Four certification levels can be completed , helping schools work toward common environmental goals and student engagement . Certification is based on a provincial standard of excellence and reaches 1,000,000 students across Ontario . Students Noah Roy , Kallie McCormack , Kyle Bartlett , Sadie Rosatte-Copeland and Judd Charbonneau appeared in front of the trustees with teacher Cassie Lyon , who oversaw the program .
