<p> No less than 21 people , businesses and groups that are all working to improve the local environment were feted last night at the Lake Simcoe Region Conservation Authority 's 38th annual Conservation Awards ceremony at Madsen 's Greenhouse Banquet Hall . <p> Nari Hwang , a Grade 6 student at Shanty Bay Public School , won the Ernie Crossland Young Conservationist Award . <p> The honour is given to individuals and groups that are 30 years of age or younger and are involved in a significant leadership role in a conservation project or with a conservation-based group or organization . <p> Living on the north shore of Lake Simcoe 's Kempenfelt Bay , Hwang has made art out of garbage and plastics to bring awareness to the amount of plastic garbage found in rivers , oceans , lakes and streams , and the negative effect these items have on the environment throughout the world . <p> Hwang is passionate about protecting Lake Simcoe from pollutants and plastics and keeping the lake healthy .
