<h> Patty Hajdu Sworn in as Minister of Health <p> OTTAWA -- Patty Hajdu is Canada 's new Minister of Health . The appointment of the new Liberal cabinet was made in Ottawa yesterday by Prime Minister Trudeau . " Today , I can introduce the strong , diverse , and experienced team that will work together to tackle the big issues that matter to people from coast to coast to coast . Whether it 's making life more affordable for the middle class , taking action on climate change , or keeping our communities safe we will continue working tirelessly for all Canadians , " stated the Prime Minister . <p> Minister Hajdu shared , " A huge honour to be appointed the next Minister of Health . I 'm looking forward to continuing the incredible work of Ginette Petitpas Taylor to promote and advance the health and well-being of all Canadians .
