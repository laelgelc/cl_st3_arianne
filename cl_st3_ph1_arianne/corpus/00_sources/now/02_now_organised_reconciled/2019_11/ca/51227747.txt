<p> There is no evidence pointing to human error in the flooding that devastated communities in the province this spring , according to the special advisor on flooding . <p> In a report presented to John Yakabuski , minister of Natural Resources and Forestry Oct. 31 , Doug McNeil confirmed that the record-setting flooding across the province was caused by a combination of weather conditions . <p> McNeil also said in the 157-page report that there was no negligent operation of water control structures . <p> " The sheer amount of water ( snow and rainfall ) on the landscape directly contributed to the flooding , " McNeil said in the executive summary of the report . <p> " Measures taken by water managers everywhere were effective in reducing the magnitude of flooding and associated damages throughout the drainage basins .
