<h> Climate march planned to disrupt Black Friday shopping in Vancouver <p> Protesters march through the intersection of Georgia and Granville streets , disrupting traffic to draw attention to the threat of climate change . Oct. 18 , 2019 . <p> SHARE <p> VANCOUVER --Climate protesters are planning another march through downtown Vancouver , and this time they intend to slow down the shopping frenzy of Black Friday . <p> The group Extinction Rebellion Vancouver , which previously shut down traffic on the Burrard Street Bridge , said the march will begin around 1 p.m. at the park behind Burrard SkyTrain Station . <p> Participants will be dressed in black for what they 're calling a " funeral service for the future of our planet , our ecosystems , and for the lives lost in the climate crisis . " Extinction Rebellion said it expects hundreds of people to take part . <p> " This solemn demonstration of grief will disrupt the business as usual on consumerism 's holiest day , " spokesperson Grace Grignon said in a statement . <p> " Our goal is to send a strong message to our local and provincial government that they need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crisis , or prepare for mass non-violent civil disobedience . " <p> For a previous Extinction Rebellion march that zig-zagged through the downtown core in October , the group declined to provide a route in advance so commuters would plan around them . <p> Protesters have not provided a route for Friday 's march either , but said it will begin at Art Phillips Park and end at Robson and Thurlow . <p> Other chapters of the Extinction Rebellion are planning similar protests around the world on Black Friday . The group said 3,000 different communities are taking part .
