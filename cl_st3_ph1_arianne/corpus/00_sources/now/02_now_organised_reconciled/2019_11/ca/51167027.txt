Khelsilem spoke to the Courier a few hours after Frances Bula broke news in the Globe and Mail that the Nation 's proposal for its 11-acre property now involves producing 6,000 housing units in 11 towers -- double the number of units initially proposed for the project last spring . <h> related <p> " We 've followed the city 's existing guidelines around spacing between the towers , in terms of the number of square feet .
