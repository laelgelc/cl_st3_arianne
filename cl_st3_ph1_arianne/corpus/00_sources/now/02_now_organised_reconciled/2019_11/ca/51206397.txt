In our system of government , cabinet ministers mostly play the role of Walmart greeters at their government department stores . It 's called the back-to-square-one gambit . It works almost as well as calling a royal commission . <p> For those reasons , crafting cabinets is a little like a parlour trick . The idea is to give everyone the impression that something magical has happened , and that the group that will govern the country for the foreseeable future is fair , balanced , representative and oh-so-talented . <p> The prime minister exhibited the usual political legerdemain in coming up with his new 36-member team . It is a politically correct blend of familiar and new faces and gender balance , with a tacit nod to the results of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of winners , losers , stay-putters and those shuffled sideways . <p> And it is the result of Trudeau losing his majority government and the popular vote to that charismatic fellow , Conservative Leader Andrew Scheer . It is in that sense a " listening " cabinet . <p> Quebec was bound to do well in landing jammy posts around the big table . Though the Liberals lost five seats in the province , they won just enough to keep them in power . With the Bloc Qu ? b ? cois nipping at their heels , they must show Quebecers they are the party with the province 's best interests at heart . <p> So it was not surprising to see Francois-Philippe Champagne make the political quantum leap from international trade to foreign affairs . <p> Nor was it a shock to see Montreal MP Pablo Rodriguez replace Ontario 's Bardish Chagger as House Leader . <p> One of the new kids on the cabinet block , also from Quebec , is Steven Guilbeault . You can bet the farm that Trudeau wo n't miss an opportunity to show Quebec how important it is to his government -- no doubt creating spikes in the political blood pressure of the Wexit set in the process . <p> It remains to be seen if appointing two Manitobans will make the West feel any more included . MP Dan Vandal becomes minister of northern affairs , and Jim Carr , battling cancer , is now special representative for the Prairies . Conservative MPs may not greet him with open arms . <p> After four years of Catherine McKenna 's largely empty rhetoric on the environment file , Trudeau had little choice but to shuffle her out . The Green party moved heaven and earth to recruit him as a candidate in the recent election , possibly seeing him as Elizabeth May 's successor . Back in 2001 , Guilbeault was one of two Greenpeace members who scaled the CN Tower in Toronto and unfurled a sign that read " Canada and Bush Climate Killers . " He was a natural pick for environment minister . <p> But instead Trudeau chose Jonathan Wilkinson , former fisheries minister , as his new environment minister . The MP from North Vancouver has an NDP pedigree , having worked as an advisor to former Saskatchewan premier Roy Romanow . But it was probably his later work in the green technology industry that originally caught Trudeau 's eye . <p> Wilkinson was a CEO of a gas purification company in Vancouver before @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . With no support in Alberta and Saskatchewan , Trudeau is probably hoping that Westerners will remember that Wilkinson grew up in Saskatoon . <p> The main reason Trudeau tapped Wilkinson for environment ? He supports a project that could be the first major controversy of the new government -- the Trans Mountain pipeline purchase and expansion . <p> Guilbeault , on the other hand , is dead against pipelines . That will make him a frustrated man in this new cabinet , and maybe Trudeau 's next Jody Wilson-Raybould -- depending on how serious he is about his principles . <p> The PM may also be hoping that Westerners will remember that his one true star from the last government , foreign affairs minister Chrystia Freeland , was raised in Alberta .
