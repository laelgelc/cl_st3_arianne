<p> Renewable energy startup Powershop has partnered with a Great Barrier Reef restoration project , as it rides the wave of climate change sentiment and strives to make small change add up to big effects . <p> First launched in 2016 , Powershop 's Your Community Energy program is intended to " help people be more engaged in energy " , interim chief Catherine Anderson tells StartupSmart , and to give them a way to bring renewable energy to community projects . <p> " What we realised is there is a real demand to help different organisations , and also a cohort of customers that were very willing and able to do that , " she says . <p> Customers opt into the project , adding a premium of $0.66 for each kWh of energy they use . That surplus cash is pooled and distributed to charitable organisations , whether that 's putting solar panels on the roof of a charity 's building , or supporting environmental restoration groups . <p> To date , the project has raised more than $500,000 for 33 projects , with more than 15,000 customers signing up to the scheme . <p> " We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that everyone feels a part of and cares about , " Anderson says . <p> The project is striving to protect the Great Barrier Reef by identifying the most climate change-resilient corals , and growing them in a specialised nursery .
