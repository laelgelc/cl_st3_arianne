It is hard to get a clear picture of Russia and China , who appear to be happy putting developing nations into debt , as they market their nuclear reactors overseas with very generous loans -- it helps to have state-owned companies funding this effort . <p> But when it comes to Western democracies , where the industry is supposed to be commercially viable , there 's trouble . The latest news from S&amp;P Global Ratings has made it plain : nuclear power can survive only with massive tax-payer support .
