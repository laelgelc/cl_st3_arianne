Love him or hate him , he 'll get you blogging . <p> AS A bunch , be they rural volunteers or urban professionals , firefighters are a very special breed of human beings . <p> They are generally the easiest and most pleasant to speak with and interview of all our emergency services . <p> Unafflicted by the jargon and bureaucratic restraints and protocols that bind many service sectors and seemingly all levels of government , they tell it as it is , or was , depending on the moment . <p> Rural volunteers will tell you if the situation has become scary , nasty or downright terrifying as easily as an urban professional will explain how , at the point of exhaustion , they had lain face down on a bitumen road @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Since the September 10 , 11 and 12 Peregian Beach fires that now seem a very long time ago , firefighters have become a constant in news stories on all platforms . <p> In truth , they have been going hard since August , called out either multiple times in a day or rostered on for long days and nights on a fire ground that shifts , twists and turns with each wind change . <p> If not here on the Sunshine Coast , they have been posted to the South Burnett , Gympie or when needed , into the fray through northern NSW . <p> Blokes who knock off at midnight tired , drained and deplete , constantly rehydrating to replace sweated body weight before returning to the fray at 8am ready to go again . <p> They all talk about the need for fatigue management and how they look out for each other . <p> They take risk seriously and rely on their strong training base and chains of command . <p> It 's all very matter of fact .
