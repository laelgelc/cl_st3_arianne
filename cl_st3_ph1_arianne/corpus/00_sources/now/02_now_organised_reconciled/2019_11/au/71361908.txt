<h> Climate change fingerprints ' all over ' Peregian fires <p> WHEN I found out I had been placed at the Sunshine Coast for my junior doctor rotations , I could n't believe my luck . This is one of the most beautiful parts of the country , with a stunning coastline , lush forests , and friendly , laid-back people . <p> I love living and working in this slice of paradise , but over the past few months , I 've grown increasingly worried about how worsening bushfire conditions are posing a growing health risk to this community . <p> More than 100 bushfires burnt across Queensland and New South Wales last month , with some truly terrifying scenes on Peregian Beach , here on the Sunshine Coast . People were still recovering from that when fresh fires started up again a few weeks ago . <p> After a brief respite , the fires have started up again , and it 's likely that we 'll see more fires in the coming hot months . <p> Bushfires have always been part of life in Australia , but the fingerprints of climate change are all over the recent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fires that spread very fast and are hard to put out , and fires that burn beyond the usual end of the season . <p> Obviously , bushfires are a dangerous time for those directly affected . Losing your home -- or a narrow miss -- can be traumatic . In these recent Peregian Beach fires , a local school was nerve-rackingly close to the flames , highlighting the severity of the threat . <p> But these prolonged bushfire conditions pose significant health risks to our wider community too , because the pollutants from smoke can travel to towns and cities thousands of kilometres away from the fires . These pollutants can worsen lung diseases like asthma and chronic obstructive lung disease . <p> As bushfire seasons lengthen and intensify , my colleagues and I worry about the likely increase in the number of people seeking emergency services because they are experiencing breathing issues , heart problems , burns , heat stress , and trauma ; and we 're all concerned about the long-term mental health impacts of living with prolonged danger too . <p> Sunshine Coast locals with pre-existing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , along with children , pregnant women , and the elderly .
