<p> Climate change is bringing longer and hotter summers , meaning Australians are faced with a new bushfire reality - and we are already feeling the effects . Around the world bushfires are burning and destroying landscapes and people 's lives , for example in California , while there have also been recent devastating fires in Queensland and New South Wales . Last year dozens of fires broke out in the Ballarat region , including four major fires , with the Mt Clear fire igniting in April . Climate modelling reveals that in south east Australia the severity and extent of the fire season is likely to increase , with less rainfall and hotter and drier conditions creating ideal fire conditions . In the past climate shifts have occurred over thousands of years , but with increasing greenhouse gas emissions , the change we are currently experiencing is occurring within about 100 years - so rapidly that wildlife and natural vegetation are struggling to adapt .
