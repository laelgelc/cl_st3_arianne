C. <p> The bill was passed with bipartisan support , including from the centre right Nationals , in contrast to Australia where climate and energy policy has provoked toxic debate and scare campaigns from the far right factions that dominates the Coalition government . <p> " This is a historic piece of legislation and is the centrepiece for meaningful climate change action in New Zealand " , New Zealand climate minister James Shaw said following the passage of the bill . <p> " Climate change is the defining long-term issue of our generation that successive Governments have failed to address .
