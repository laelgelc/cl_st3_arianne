<h> WA Greens Senator Jordon Steele-John slams major party politicians ' arsonists ' as bushfires tear through country <h> Topics <p> A West Australian senator has labelled major party politicians " arsonists " during an incendiary debate in Canberra . <p> With bushfires raging on both sides of the country , Greens Senator Jordon Steele-John laid into opponents as discussions over the link between climate change and fire risks continue to heat up . <p> " How dare any of you suggest that in this moment at this time it is appropriate to be prosecuting a piece of legislation with the aim of propping up coal , " he said during a debate on the federal government 's proposed laws cracking down on energy companies . <p> " You are no better than a bunch of arsonists -- borderline arsonists -- and you should be ashamed . " <p> The remarks sparked objections from Labor 's Murray Watt but the West Australian refused to withdraw them , with Mr Steele-John insisting they were true . <p> " For Senator Steele-John to refer to members of this chamber as arsonists on the very day that we are told by fire @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ never seen before is beyond offensive , " Mr Watt said . <p> Firefighters in NSW and Queensland are battling scores of life-threatening fires , with the " catastrophic " conditions that have forced many people to abandon their homes expected to cause further problems as the day went on .
