Hundreds of ACT people felt that exact same disquiet of those suddenly dispossessed by the threat of bushfire ; the feeling of numbness , concern and uncertainty . Canberra emerged from the 2003 conflagration emotionally and physically scarred with four people dead and numerous others badly injured .
