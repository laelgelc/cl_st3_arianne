<h> Coal power to drop by record 3% in 2019 <p> Emily BeamentAAP <p> November 25 , 2019 11:24AM <p> The amount of electricity generated from coal plants around the world is on track to drop by a record three per cent in 2019 , analysis suggests . <p> Assessment of monthly electricity data from countries worldwide indicates a fall of about 300 terawatt hours from coal in 2019 - more than the combined output from coal power in Germany , Spain and the UK in 2018 . <p> The drop in coal power - a key source of greenhouse gas pollution - in 2019 compared with 2018 raises the prospect the world could see a slowing-down in the growth of global carbon emissions in 2019 . <p> But global coal use and carbon emissions remain far higher than the level needed to meet international targets under the Paris Agreement to limit global temperature rises and prevent the worst impacts of climate change , the analysis published on climate and energy website Carbon Brief warns . <p> The global fall in
