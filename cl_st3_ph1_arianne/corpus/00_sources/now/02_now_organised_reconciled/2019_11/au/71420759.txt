<p> The world-first project is expected to generate strong interest from numerous airports in hot regions , especially those with busy long-haul schedules through the heat of the day . <p> Trials at Adelaide Airport in conjunction with SA Water have shown that planting lucerne around runways can drop air temperatures by more than 3C on warm days and therefore improve the performance of departing airplanes . <p> In warmer , less dense air , planes must travel faster down the runway to produce the lift needed for take-off . When a runway lacks the distance required to reach these speeds , a plane 's weight must be reduced by removing passengers , luggage and cargo .
