That information would then have to be put into a database carefully to ensure it matched up with the particular part used in a particular car . Finetuning that process with pen , paper and guesswork from the executive suite can take months , if not years . The screwdriver tightens the screw to the required 70 NM , quietly sends that information to the database and the worker can quickly move on to the next task . <p> I did n't see many pens behind ears or in top pockets at E.GO 's Aachen factory . <p> Flipping the car factory on its head <p> E.GO is a start-up making four-seat electric-only ' Life ' cars for a cost of ? 16,000 to ? 20,000 ( NZ$27,000 to NZ$34,000 ) . This era is seen as the successor to the first three industrial revolutions : steam-driven factories and transport ( 1780-1850 ) , mass production in mechanised and electrically-powered factories ( 1870 to 1920 ) and then the computerisation of factories and supply chains ( 1970-2010 ) . <p> Also called ' smart factories ' , there 's a lot more information on how the Industry 4.0 movement is progressing in New Zealand via Callaghan Innovation 's Industry 4.0 Hub .
