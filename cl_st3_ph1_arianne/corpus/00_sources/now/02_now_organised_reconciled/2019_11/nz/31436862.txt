I used to habitually roll out a quote attributed to Einstein : " Nothing will benefit human health and increase chances for survival of life on Earth as much as the evolution to a vegetarian diet " ( unfortunately , the quote is unverified and highly unlikely to be something he actually said ) . <p> Coming into the summer of my second year abstaining from meat , I started to lapse .
