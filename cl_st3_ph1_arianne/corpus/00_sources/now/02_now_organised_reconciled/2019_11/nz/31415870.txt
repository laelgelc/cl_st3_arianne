<p> Partly because it was a widely unexpected result , the opening days of Wellington mayor Andy Foster 's tenure have been gripping . He has inherited a Council which roughly leans left , and faces a difficult three years if he wants to get his campaign agenda through . The next three years could be a reminder to Wellingtonians about the drama and importance of local government . <p> Picking a deputy will be an early test of the balance of power . It was reported last week by the NZ Herald 's Georgina Campbell ( paywalled ) that there were moves to get the ( relatively speaking ) right leaning Diane Calvert or Nicola Young in the mix . But now Stuff 's Tom Hunt reports an ultimatum has been made , and that neither of those choices are acceptable to the bulk of the Council . Fleur Fitzsimons from Labour or Sarah Free from the Greens are considered the frontrunners .
