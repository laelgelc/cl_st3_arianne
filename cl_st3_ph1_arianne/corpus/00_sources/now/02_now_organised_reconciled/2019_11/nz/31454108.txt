<h> A potential point-of-sale method to check fish oil capsules <p> Fish oil capsules are a popular health supplement with many health benefits , but concerns have been raised over the quality because omega-3 fatty acids are very prone to oxidation and deterioration on exposure to heat and light . Plant &amp; Food Research and University of Otago scientists have found a rapid way to analyse the concentrations of these fatty acids throughout the production process , including point-of-sale , to ensure that consumers are getting their money 's worth . <p> Researchers have for the first time used a portable handheld device based on laser technology to analyse the concentrations of omega-3 fatty acids eicosapentaenoic acid ( EPA ) and docosahexaenoic acid ( DHA ) in intact fish oil capsules . The relatively low-cost Raman spectroscopy device can analyse the contents of soft gel capsules within seconds without destroying them .
