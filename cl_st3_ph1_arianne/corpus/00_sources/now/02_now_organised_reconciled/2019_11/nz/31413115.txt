New Zealand 's productivity has effectively stagnated since 2010 . These technologies , which were themselves evolutions of metallurgy and insights into the properties of electricity , combined to create the industrial revolution from the early 1800s . Suddenly , factories powered by steam engines could pump out countless garments , widgets and machines . <p> Almost as quickly , these new products and their masters could be transported to and around new markets via train and ship . Output per hour worked exploded . Prices often fell and some people made astonishing fortunes . The wages of others whose skills were replaced by machines fell . Often , with the help of governments and unions , they and their children retrained and got better jobs with better wages . Some businesses and workers never recovered . Most eventually thrived . <p> All this activity and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , documented and often transacted by the telegraph . They drove new social movements , political transformations , revolutions and the first major wave of globalisation . But it did n't all happen at once or in a straight line .
