<p> Mahara Gallery Arts Review premier award winner Ben Caldwell has described his achievement as both an honour and a home-coming . <p> The John Mowbray Open Award , which comes with a $1,000 prize , was announced along with five others at a Gallery function on Saturday coinciding with the opening of the Kapiti Arts Trail . <p> The event also marked 10 years of Mahara Arts Reviews , launched originally to give Kapiti and Horowhenua artists the opportunity to showcase the diversity and quality of art being produced across the district . <p> " It was the idea of a review not a competition that intrigued me , " said Ben Caldwell . " I 'm not one for competitions -- I like the idea of art bringing people together . <p> " It is an honour and privilege to be acknowledged alongside some amazing work and artists . It feels like coming home to Wellington -- Kapiti Coast is where I 'm meant to be . " <p> Ben won the Open Award with his sculpture , My Burning House , which draws inspiration from the whare tapa wha model for Maori health and well-being developed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from a proverb from Ghana . <p> " Proverbs , metaphors and humour are important aspects to my practice , " he says . " The relationship between the art object and the viewer is where the magic happens . I try to play on these . " <p> Mahara Gallery Director Janet Bayly describes the work as something of the reverse of Noah 's Ark . <p> " The burning house sits precariously atop its classic Kiwi wooden post like a washed up relic from a shipwreck . But it is also taking a raft of birds , plants and animals down with it . <p> " It 's visualisation of climate change is presented in a way that viewers of all ages will be able to engage with and understand . " <p> Ben Caldwell was born in Palmerston North , grew up in Titahi Bay and now lives between Paraparaumu and Aotea . <p> He is in the process of setting up what he calls his " Artspace " -- which includes a workshop and small gallery -- in Paraparaumu . <p> He is working as a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . He is also working as a tutor to share his knowledge and is considering Doctoral studies in the future . Ben Caldwell 's first exhibition was as an 18-year-old at Pataka . He went on to complete a Visual Arts Diploma at Whitirea , a Batchelor 's Degree at Weltec in 2007 and a Masters in Fine Arts ( Honours ) at the University of Auckland . <p> " Each year the Review attracts new artists , some of whom return for a solo show , as well as seasoned well-established and senior artists , " says Janet Bayly . <p> " It 's always a thrill to have both ends of the wide and vital spectrum of the visual arts in our district represented in such a show . " <p> All of the 78 entries in the Review are currently being exhibited in Mahara Gallery .
