<h> Campaigner welcomes removal of racist refugee restrictions <p> The founder of the Double the Quota campaign has applauded the coalition government for Friday 's announcement that a discriminatory policy would be removed . <p> The policy , while not affecting the total number of refugees , had slashed the number of refugees coming to New Zealand from Africa and the Middle East . <p> Under the restriction , only refugees from these regions who already had existing ties to New Zealand would be eligible to be bought in under the quota . The same restrictions did not apply to refugees based in Asia-Pacific and the Americas . <p> Murdoch Stephens , founder of the successful Double the Quota campaign added , " I am pleased that the leaders of the country have heeded the call to scrap this racist policy . The Immigration Minister has made a strong statement that our humanitarian refugee policy must fulfill the goal of helping those most at risk .
