<h> Migrant exploitation review : Govt " chasing its tail " <p> You ca n't look at migrant exploitation without looking at what this country sells the exploited , an immigration lawyer says <p> Migrant advocates say the country looks set to keep continuing to sell migrants the " pipe-dream " that keeps them coming to New Zealand , despite a Government review . <p> The migrant exploitation review is one of the most tangible outcomes of a commitment in the Labour and New Zealand First coalition agreement - one of only two on immigration - to take " serious action " on migrant exploitation . <p> " There does n't seem to be any desire to investigate why we have this problem in New Zealand . And if there 's something fundamentally wrong in our immigration policies which creates vulnerability . " <p> There are ten proposals including : a new bridging visa ; a new offence ; new hurdles for would-be employers ; and new penalties for migrant exploitation . And if there 's something fundamentally wrong in our immigration policies which creates vulnerability . " <p> That hole is what people like McClymont think is the root cause of these scams : migrants who are sold a residency dream that does n't exist - then get into debt chasing it . <p> The debt <p> Digging themselves out of debt is what keeps most exploited migrants in New Zealand . <p> How they ended up with that debt goes back to the time before they landed here . <p> " They 're sold this pipe-dream and then they arrive here and it 's very different .
