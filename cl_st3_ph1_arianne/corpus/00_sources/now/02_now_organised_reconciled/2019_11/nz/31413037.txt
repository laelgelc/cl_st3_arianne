<h> APEC Santiago - all is not lost <p> Leaders ' Week in Chile has been cancelled and there has been growing unrest in several member countries , but APEC can still prove its value , Dr Alan Bollard writes <p> Comment : A few years ago , Peru hosted the APEC leaders meeting in Lima . It was the year of the Brexit referendum and Donald Trump 's unexpected election . <p> In the big concrete conference centre in the heart of Lima , I found a number of APEC leaders reviewing the anti-globalisation and populist mood of that year , and pondering why the political mood was changing . <p> Did n't people recognise that APEC-style reform had helped bring so many out of poverty ? Did this mark a mood throughout the region ? <p> Was APEC-trade reform no longer delivering dividends for most people ? Or was there something inadequate in the way we were communicating the benefits ? <p> There was no agreement on these questions . We did not envisage difficulties with a growing economy looking for improved economic connectivity in the Asia-Pacific , and a history of successfully hosting major international events . <p> However , the past few weeks have seen violent street protests in Santiago and other Chilean cities , resulting in nearly two dozen deaths .
