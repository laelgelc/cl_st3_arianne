Jackson St has a fantastic collection of funky cafes , friendly pubs and quirky shops in an eclectic mix of historic architecture . At the weekend , it 's a brunch destination for the wider region ; during the week it is busy with locals . The library is a vital community hub but the booksellers had all but gone , apart from a small selection at the local PaperPlus . A quick sketch map to locate all of the other independent bookshops in the area showed there were none close by , and that Petone would be an ideal place to set up a new bookshop . <p> We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hawkes Bay to find out more about the realities of being a bookseller in New Zealand .
