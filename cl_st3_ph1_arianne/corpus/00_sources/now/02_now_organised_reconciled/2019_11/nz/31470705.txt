<h> Gisborne marches for climate action <p> CLOSE TO 100 people marched in Gisborne today to demand action from the world 's leaders on climate change . <p> They joined tens of thousands of other people around New Zealand in the People 's Climate March , in support of climate change action at the Paris climate talks next week . <p> People waved banners and signs with messages such as " solutions not pollution " and " stop talking , start planting " along Gladstone Road and at Heipipi Park . <p> They called for the Government to set a more ambitious target on reducing carbon emissions . A number of people spoke to the crowd at Heipipi Park , including Green Party MP Gareth Hughes .
