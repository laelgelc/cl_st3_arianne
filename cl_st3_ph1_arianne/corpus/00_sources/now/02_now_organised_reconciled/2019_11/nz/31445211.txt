' All the dishes were made with rescued food donated by a local supermarket , which would otherwise have been sent to landfill . <p> The winning team , the Fabulous Foodies prepared and cooked a selection of delicious dishes . They were happy to walk away with a hamper full of organic wine , local cheeses , Ecostore products and much more . When food scraps break down in landfill , harmful methane gas is released into the atmosphere , leading to climate change .
