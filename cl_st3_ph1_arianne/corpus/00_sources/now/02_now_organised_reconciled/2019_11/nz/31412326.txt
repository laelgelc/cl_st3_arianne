<h> Saving us from ourselves <p> OPINION : The Government 's policy to reduce agricultural greenhouse gas emissions in New Zealand is working directly against the goals of the Paris Accord . <p> NZ 's pastoral farming is a low emissions process . Studies published in the NZ Crown Research Institute ( CRI ) assessment of agricultural production systems the world over show NZ is " head and shoulders " above its competitors . <p> This goes well beyond our on farm production . With regard to NZ lamb sold in the UK , ocean shipping made up 5% of the final product 's carbon cost -- voiding the belief that meat produced on this side of the world is environmentally unsustainable . <p> I am told that Ireland is the world 's next most efficient producer of dairy product . However , NZ can produce dairy products here and ship there and it still has lower emissions than local Irish produce . Incredible ! <p> Our traditional beef systems are towards the lower end of the emissions scale and why would n't they be ? According to the Paris Accord , this ' national circumstance ' should factor into the way we go about our business in addressing climate change . <p> Back to the Paris Accord , which New Zealand is a signatory to . The article goes on to say : " However , that is not to say that other countries are not directly or indirectly regulating emissions for their agricultural sectors " . <p> Not only this , but MfE explains -- as if this is now the overriding objective -- " ... developed countries should continue taking the lead by undertaking economy-wide absolute emission reduction targets and developing countries should move to economy-wide targets over time .
