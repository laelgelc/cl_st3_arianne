<h> Australia Braces as Bushfire Warning Reaches ' Catastrophic ' <p> ( Bloomberg ) -- Australia is bracing for another week of devastating bushfires , with swaths of the eastern seaboard and even areas of greater Sydney facing a " catastrophic " threat that 's unprecedented at this time of year . <p> It 's the first time authorities have set the highest warning level for Australia 's largest city since the six-step fire-danger rating system was introduced a decade ago . <p> Three people have been killed and more than 150 homes destroyed in the state of New South Wales in recent days as fires ripped through areas rendered tinderbox dry by a two-year drought . The devastation is exceptional , considering summer has n't even begun . <p> Authorities expect the situation to deteriorate Tuesday as hot and windy conditions sweep through New South Wales and neighboring Queensland , where states of emergency have been declared . <p> " Everyone has to assume the worst , " New South Wales Premier Gladys Berejiklian said at a press conference Monday .
