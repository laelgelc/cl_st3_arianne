<h> The killing of New Zealand Books <p> Ten questions for Creative New Zealand to consider following its decision to kill the distinguished literary journal , New Zealand Books . <p> Creative New Zealand probably ought not look like a pack of vandals and philistines , but that 's the optics and the perception and the reality of the situation since its decision to reject the application of the distinguished literary journal New Zealand Books , forcing its closure after 29 years . <p> The journal is - was - an intelligent and in-depth print quarterly . It could be infuriating ; reviews often appeared months after the book was published . It could be a little bit toothless ; there was a general reluctance to maim or kill New Zealand authors . But it published many or most of the finest minds in New Zealand writing , and above all it covered the waterfront : here was a print journal exclusively devoted to New Zealand literature , and it wrote with authority on fiction , history , natural history , memoir , children 's books , everything .
