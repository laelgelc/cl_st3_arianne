<h> Sustainable architectural approach earns distinguished award <p> A Wanaka architect who has promoted sustainability in buildings for decades has been given one of the highest New Zealand Institute of Architects awards . <p> At the annual New Zealand architecture awards ceremony in Queenstown on Saturday night , Anne Salmond was made a Distinguished Fellow of the institute in recognition of her exceptional influence and contribution to New Zealand architecture over the course of her career . <p> In announcing the award the institute said Ms Salmond had been " a trailblazer " for women in architecture for more than 30 years . <p> " She has led a successful practice producing high-quality architecture from a base in a small provincial centre , and has championed important concerns such as sustainable design , prefabricated construction and the study of post-occupancy building performance . " <p> Ms Salmond said when she graduated in 1980 there were only eight other female students in her year and only one registered female architect . <p> " In those days people would often express surprise when you said you were an architect but most of those eight women are still practising architecture now , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
