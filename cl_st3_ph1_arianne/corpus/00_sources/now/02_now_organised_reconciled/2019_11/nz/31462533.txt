<h> Awards for the best and worst communicators <p> *Watch Newsroom 's James Elliott announce the winners in the video player above* <p> Newsroom is pleased to announce the winners in the 14th annual People 's Choice Plain English Awards . These 2019 Awards recognise the best and worst in government and corporate communications as nominated anonymously by members of the public . <p> The award for the People 's Choice -- Best Plain English Communication goes to Accuro Health Insurance for their website www.accuro.co.nz . <p> The person who nominated this website said : <p> It 's a beautifully clear , simple website . I found it clear and logical . <p> The international panel of judges who reviewed this nomination said : <p> This website is a very good example of plain English used to support a business to communicate clearly and in an engaging way with as many New Zealand residents and citizens as possible . I 'd expect it to have a positive impact on sales to new customers and existing customer retention and engagement . <p> This is a very strong site . It gives a great first impression , drawing the reader in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ concise and engaging content and a simple-to-navigate layout that makes it easy for readers to find and do what they need to . <p> Accuro has done a good job creating a user experience that is clear and helpful , which is refreshing for an insurance organisation ! <p> The award for the People 's Choice Worst ' Brainstrain ' Communication goes to Auckland Council for its Proposed Plan Change letter -- clarifying the relationship between the Special Character Areas Overlay and underlying zone provisions within the Auckland Unitary Plan ( see the letter below ) . <p> The person who nominated this document said : <p> A good friend of mine came in waving her ( copy of this letter ) at me saying she had a master 's degree in English and could n't understand a word of it . <p> The international panel of judges who reviewed this nomination said : <p> This document is very difficult to understand , which disturbs me because it seems very important for landowners . Also , the writer portrays the content as unimportant , but I do n't think it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The council is trying to bring a planning change to the attention of its ratepayers and residents . But it has failed miserably to think about how to get that message across in plain language . The letter appears to be written for people who understand council processes , not for those who will be most affected by the change . <p> The third component of this communication is already written in plain language , so the skills to write the rest in plain language exist . This is poor performance for a very important matter , and a huge opportunity missed . Many residents were frustrated by this communication . <p> The People 's Choice Awards are held each year and form part of the Plain English Awards , which , as of this year , are held every second year .
