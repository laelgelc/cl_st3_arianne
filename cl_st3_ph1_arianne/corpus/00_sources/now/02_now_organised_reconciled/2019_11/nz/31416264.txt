<h> ' It 's time for men to shut up . ' Is it ? <p> " It 's time for men to shut up , " a novelist said today at the Man Booker ceremony in London . New Zealand writers respond . <p> Oh look , a teacup -- let 's put a storm in it ! A small , contained , but passionate storm blew up this morning ( New Zealand time ) at a finalists ceremony for the Man Booker at the Southbank Centre in London . <p> Edinburgh-based novelist Lucy Ellmann , when asked about the length of her book , Ducks , Newburyport , an uninterrupted female monologue lasting 1000 pages , said jokingly or semi-jokingly : " I think it 's time for men to shut up . " <p> Jokes often illuminate good reason . Is it , in fact , time for men to shut up ?
