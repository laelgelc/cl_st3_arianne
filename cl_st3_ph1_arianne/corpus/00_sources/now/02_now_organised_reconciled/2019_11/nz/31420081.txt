<h> Research shows Kiwis want more public media -- Better Public Media <p> Research commissioned by Better Public Media Trust has revealed an overwhelming response to suggested government funding options for public media . <p> The research was conducted by Research New Zealand as part of an online omnibus survey in late October with a nationally representative sample of 1000 New Zealanders , 18 years and over . <p> Almost two thirds of respondents support ad-free weekends on TV One , with only 9% opposed . And a significant majority , 60% support making TV One completely non-commercial , with just 11% opposed . <p> The research offered a range of improvements for public media funding and policy . It found respondents were more likely to approve than disapprove , with disapprovals to various options never rising above 25% . <p> The most popular options are to turn TV One into an ad-free channel and website , remove advertising , and to tax big tech companies like Facebook and Google to support New Zealand programmes and media . <p> TDB Recommends NewzEngine.com <p> Less popular were options to expand RNZ to include an ad-free television channel ( RNZ+ ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Maori Television into a large ad-free broadcaster and media outlet .
