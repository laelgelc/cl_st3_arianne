<p> News from WCC Concrete from earthquake-prone social housing flats being demolished by the Wellington City Council will be recycled , instead of going to landfill . <p> The earthquake-prone flats at Nairn St have been empty since April 2017 , when tenants moved to other housing in the city . <p> In a first for the Council , the demolished concrete will be taken to CentrePort where it will be crushed and made into gravel and used for regeneration work at the port , and by roading contractors for footpath renewal work in the city . <p> Trial runs have been done to ensure the concrete is suitable for recycling , and around 50 truckloads ( 320 cubic metres ) have already been taken to CentrePort . In addition to concrete from the site , metal and window frames from the 15 former units are also being recycled . <p> Mayor Andy Foster says the concrete recycling initiative lines-up with the Council 's Te Atakura -- First to Zero blueprint for the city to become carbon zero . <p> " This concrete will be diverted from landfill and will contribute to lowering of carbon emissions by reducing concrete @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with CentrePort to walk the talk and factor in protection of our environment and climate change in our decision-making . " <p> The Council 's Waste Minimisation portfolio lead Councillor Laurie Foon says it 's exciting to see the Council taking a lead in reducing its waste footprint . <p> " Saving concrete from the landfill and turning it into a valuable resource is just the beginning of Wellington 's circular economy journey . Regionally we 've committed to drop our waste output by a third before 2026 , and clever ideas are the only way we are going to get there . Stepping up to partner with CentrePort is a great example of this . " <p> CentrePort General Manager Regeneration Anthony Delaney says their award-winning waste minimisation project has been running for over two years , recycling 35,800 cubic metres of concrete to date . <p> " Sustainability is a fundamental principle of CentrePort 's regeneration , and in addition to recycling our own waste , we 've started partnering this year with external organisations to help them recycle . <p> " We 're delighted that the Wellington @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us on this partnership , and we welcome their commitment to environmental sustainability in this way .
