It 's typically used to dismiss what are perceived to be outdated views on issues such as climate and gender . <p> And while this intergenerational strife has a strong cultural dimension , economics is important too . <p> It would not surprise me if some of today 's students whisper ' OK boomer ' under their breath during lessons about the strong link between a country 's unemployment rate and its rate of inflation .
