I caught up with Haylee Koroi who is a member of Te Ara Whatu , a group of Maori and Pasifika youth from Aotearoa taking action on Climate Change . As in previous years , Te Ara Whatu are planning to send a group of eight rangatahi to the COP25 UN climate conference next month ( with help from Pledge Me ) .
