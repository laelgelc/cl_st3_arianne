<h> U.K. Parliament to Elect Replacement to Speaker John Bercow <p> The U.K. House of Commons will elect a new speaker on Monday to replace John Bercow before it dissolves for the country 's general election in just over five weeks . <p> The vote will probably be the last major decision of this Parliament , which Prime Minister Boris Johnson has called " dead " and repeatedly attacked for stymieing his Brexit plan . <p> When Parliament dissolves on Wednesday , it will mark the official start of the campaign . Not all current members of parliament who vote for the new speaker will return .
