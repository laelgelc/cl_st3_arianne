<h> Popular live broadcast of Tribunal hearing <p> The live broadcast of the Whanganui District Inquiry by iwi radio station AWA FM means more New Zealanders can be part of what is happening . Judge Wainwright , Presiding Officer on the Whanganui District Inquiry says " Apparently wh a nau are tuning in both locally and overseas , and that 's great " . <p> The live broadcast means that people can hear first hand from the claimants about important issues such as the early ' purchase ' of Whanganui lands by the New Zealand company ; the Native Land Court and Crown purchasing of M a ori land in the nineteenth and early twentieth centuries . <p> Iwi radio is a hit with M ? ori youth . Listeners have already used U-Tube , texts and Bebo to chat about the hearing with friends and wh a nau in Aotearoa and overseas . Local students have joined the hearing to support teachers , parents , aunties and uncles . <p> Everyone is welcome to attend the Whanganui hearings . Week 3 of 14 runs from 10-14 September 2007 at Parikino Marae , Parikino . <p> On Thursday , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ visit to see places of significance to claimant groups . The site visit includes a jet boat journey on the Whanganui River , and a bus tour to Koriniti and Hiruharama . The Whanganui Tribunal panel for the Whanganui District Inquiry ( Wai 903 ) are Judge Carrie Wainwright , Dr Angela Ballara , Dr Ranginui Walker and Professor Wharehuia Milroy . <p> Claimant groups to give evidence the week 10-14 September 2007 are Ng ? ti Hinearo and Ng ? ti Tuera , and the Whanganui River M ? ori Trust Board . Issues to be raised include Crown purchasing policy , land alienation , local government , education services , health services , and dealings with Crown and government agencies , both in the past and present . <p> Tangata whenua groups from all over the Whanganui inquiry district will present evidence for the Whanganui District Inquiry during 2007 and 2008 .
