<h> Questions raised over Westport rat 1080 results <p> Results released over the weekend of independent testing of some of the carcasses found in Westport have done little to solve the mystery of what killed over 600 rats <p> Test results from an unnamed mysterious laboratory on rats and other wildlife that washed up on a Westport beach have puzzled toxicologists , with one describing the results as " very unusual " and in direct conflict with published studies . <p> The secret laboratory 's results show levels of 1080 and related substances present in rats , birds and a starfish . This differs from results reported by the Department of Conservation ( DoC ) which found no trace of 1080 present in rats . <p> Numerous theories have been put forward as the reason more than 680 rats washed up in Westport . These include 1080 poisoning , a flash flood , mass suicide , poor swimming ability or a farmer using rat bait . Bad weather and a spike in rat numbers caused by a mast year have been suggested as a possible factor in the unusual incident .
