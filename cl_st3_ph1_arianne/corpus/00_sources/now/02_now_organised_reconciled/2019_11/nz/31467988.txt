<h> Australian State Touts Tech in Bid to Fend Off Climate Criticism <h> Australian State Touts Tech in Bid to Fend Off Climate Criticism <p> ( Bloomberg ) -- A laggard state in the transition to cleaner energy in Australia , one of the world 's most-polluting rich nations , is hoping that highlighting technological progress will help it fend off some of the criticism of its climate policies . <p> Western Australia will use a conference showcasing innovation in the resources sector this week to demonstrate how the industry can help to tackle climate change . The nation 's top mining state by revenue is underlining its position as a major producer of the lithium used in batteries for electric vehicles or power storage and the iron ore needed to make the steel to build wind turbines to deflect its poor record on renewable installations . <p> " The mining industry is critical to a renewable energy future , " Bill Johnston , Western Australia 's minister for mines , petroleum and energy , said in an interview before a resources technology conference that starts in Perth on Wednesday . That echoes the policies of Scott Morrison , Australia 's Liberal premier , who has consistently said that the nation will meet its 2030 targets to reduce carbon emissions under the Paris Agreement , but has no clear policy agenda to reach them and has rebuffed calls by leaders from its island nation neighbors for a commitment to phase out coal , the most polluting fossil fuel . <p> Johnston 's comments come after a report last week from The Climate Council , an environmental lobby group , that put the state near the bottom of the pile in terms of renewables takeup , at just 8.2% of the generation mix in 2018 .
