It is republished here as part of Newsroom 's partnership with Covering Climate Now , a global collaboration of more than 250 news outlets to strengthen coverage of the climate story . <p> It makes good business sense to contain planetary warming to 1.5 ? C. Passing the Paris target spells disaster , with more extremes of global heat , writes Tim Radford . <p> Submarine heatwaves happen three times more often that they did in 1980 . Ocean warming events can devastate coral reefs and trigger even more damage from more intense acidification and oxygen loss in the seas , with disastrous consequences for fishery and seafood . <p> The ecosystems on which all living things -- including humans -- depend are shifting away from the tropics at up to 40km a year . Extremes of torrential rainfall , drought and tropical cyclones are becoming measurably more intense . <p> And all this has happened because global mean surface temperatures have risen in the last century by about 1 ? Right now , the planet is on track to hit or surpass 3 ? C by 2100 . The case for drastic reductions in greenhouse gas emissions is now more compelling and urgent than ever . <p> " First , we have under-estimated the sensitivity of natural and human systems to climate change and the speed at which these things are happening .
