<h> Farm animals suffering due to poor winter grazing practices <p> Farm animals are suffering in muddy , confined spaces , with many people ignoring the problem and officials unsure of what to do about it , a hard- hitting report has found . <p> The comments came in a report by a special taskforce on winter grazing . <p> The report was ordered by the government after animal rights activists released pictures of large numbers of cows wallowing in mud after being confined in a restricted area , which was soon trampled into a quagmire . <p> The activists said this was cruel and cited cases of cows giving birth to calves while standing knee deep in mud . <p> Farm groups said the pictures were taken out of context and most winter grazing was not like this . <p> Intensive winter grazing is used so that grass on the main part of the farm can recover at a time when pasture growth is limited by cool temperatures and short daylight hours . <p> The animals are then fed crops or other food in a confined area . <p> The report said this could cause serious harm to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ how extensive the problem was . <p> " Reports to the Taskforce varied , with some reporting as low as 5 percent of farmers are doing badly ... while other reports suggest 20 percent or more are doing badly with a further 30 percent of farmers not following best practice . <p> " However there is no objective measurement . " <p> The taskforce added getting rid of winter grazing was not a sure fix either , since animals kept on their usual paddocks could be exposed to winter storms and suffer a lack of nutrition from slow-growing grass . <p> It wanted an urgent study to be undertaken to understand winter grazing better . <p> People needed to become more sensitive to the problem . <p> " The widespread prevalence of poor intensive winter grazing practices leads to people ' walking past ' or being desensitized to poor practice and considering it as normal , " the report said . <p> " This is unacceptable . " <p> The report was even more critical of how officials were dealing with this , saying not all compliance tools available were being used and enforcement activity was hindered by a lack of clear rules . <p> Further enforcement tools were needed , and above all , codes of welfare needed to be aligned with emerging scientific understandings of animals ' sensitivity . <p> The taskforce said animals themselves were being taken for granted . <p> " Cows can handle a lot , but in some ways this has n't done them any favours , " the report said . <p> " Just because they are stoic does n't mean their welfare is n't affected .
