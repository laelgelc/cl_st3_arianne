<h> Plugging in to the future of transport <p> Two state-of-the-art electric vehicle charging stations will soon be available at the Athol Street carpark in the Queenstown town centre . <p> The fast charging stations can charge electric vehicles ( EV ) in 20-40 minutes , meaning drivers and their passengers can have a coffee , get their shopping done and be on their way with a fully charged battery . <p> Queenstown Lakes District Council General Manager Property and Infrastructure Peter Hansby said the number of people owning and hiring electric vehicles is growing and providing the infrastructure to support this shift is vital . <p> " If you 're choosing to drive , an EV is a much more environmentally friendly option and a very effective way to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , " Mr Hansby said <p> " Adapting to emerging technologies and promoting behaviour change is a big part of our transport strategy for the district . We 've already successfully introduced EV charging stations in Wanaka and we hope they 'll be a welcome addition to our parking offering in the Queenstown town centre too , " he said . <p> QLDC approved a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the fast charging station back in November 2017 . <p> Initially two fast charging stations will be installed with two car parking spaces marked for EV charging . <p> " While only two spaces will be available in the first instance , we 'll be able to provide for further charging stations in the future if there is demand , " Mr Hansby said . <p> Mr Hansby said work to install the charging stations will start on 8 October and take approximately ten days . <p> " We need to close a portion of the Athol Street carpark to allow for the charging stations to be installed . We acknowledge the temporary loss of carparking in the town centre may be frustrating for some but we hope people can see the bigger picture and benefits to the community once the chargers are installed , " he said . <p> When complete , only electric vehicles will be allowed to use the marked spaces .
