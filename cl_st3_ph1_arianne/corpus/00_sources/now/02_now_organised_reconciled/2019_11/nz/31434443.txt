<h> Peters accepts National ministers did n't leak <p> Winston Peters ' has accepted in the High Court that two former National ministers he had been suing for $450,000 for breaching his privacy were not the source of the leak or responsible for it . <p> In his closing submission today , Peters ' lawyer Brian Henry said both Anne Tolley and Paula Bennett denied in their evidence leaking information on Peters ' seven-year overpayment of superannuation - and the lawyer for the Ministry of Social Development and public servants did not challenge those denials . <p> " That left the MSD in the position that they now can not avoid a finding that the breach was on MSD , " Henry said . " The plaintiff was expecting a challenge from MSD to the ministers , but the MSD has not challenged the evidence that they the ministers did not leak . <p> " That dual denial removed two of the options that the plaintiff , when it opened its case , was expecting to have examined in the court .
