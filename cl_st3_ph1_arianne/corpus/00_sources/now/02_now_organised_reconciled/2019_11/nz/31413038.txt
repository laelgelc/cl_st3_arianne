<h> Havelock North 's legacy : a new regulator <p> A new water regulator is the next step to address a major public health problem , writes Dileepa Fonseka <p> New Zealand looks set to get a new water regulator that will have the job of making sure the water we drink is safe . <p> New legislation will be introduced to Parliament in the coming months establishing an authority dedicated to regulating drinking water . It is expected to become law next year . <p> Reports on drinking water quality have flooded out of the Government since the Havelock North water crisis in 2016 left four people dead and thousands seriously ill , and exposed a series of flaws in the way our water supply is managed . <p> Within the pages of those reports positive observations about the state of New Zealand 's water regulations were few and far between . <p> The Ministry of Health - the main body enforcing compliance with drinking water regulations - had n't enforced a single breach of drinking quality standards since 2007 .
