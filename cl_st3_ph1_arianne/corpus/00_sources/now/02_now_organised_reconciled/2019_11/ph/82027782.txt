<h> Pakistan reports record high in dengue cases in 2019 <p> Health officials with the National Institute of Health ( NIH ) in Pakistan say the number of dengue fever cases reported in 2019 is a record . According to Pakistan NIH official , Dr Rana Safdar , a record-breaking 44,000 people have been infected with mosquito-borne dengue in Pakistan this year . <p> Image/CIA <p> The previous record was 27,000 in 2011 .
