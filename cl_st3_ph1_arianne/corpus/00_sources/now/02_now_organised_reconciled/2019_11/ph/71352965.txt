UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres says countries are still losing to climate change despite calls for interventions , in a press briefing after the Asean-UN Summit in Nonthaburi , Thailand on Sunday ( Nov. 3 , 2019 ) . The UN chief appealed to governments worldwide to help in by limiting temperature growth to 1.5 degree at the end of the century , to be carbon neutral by 2050 , and to reduce emissions by 45 percent in the next decade .
