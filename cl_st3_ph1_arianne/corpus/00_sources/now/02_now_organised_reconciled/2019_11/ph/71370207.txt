<h> Wise Use of Water <p> Water , and the lack of it , has been a nagging issue for Cebuanos for several months now . Coupled with a seemingly insensitive ( or incompetent ) water supply agency , people have been squeezing their brains on how to further manage their use of whatever water supply made available to them . It is true that with climate change and overpopulation of our cities ( yes , there are way too many of us now ) , resources such as water could be volatile . However these issues could have been averted if authorities involved in water supply had laid out long-term plans to make sure our faucets would not run dry .
