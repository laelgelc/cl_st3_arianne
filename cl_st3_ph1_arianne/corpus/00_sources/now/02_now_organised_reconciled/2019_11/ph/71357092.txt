<h> BSP invests US$150-M in green bonds <p> MANILA -- The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas ( BSP ) has placed USD150 million in the green bond fund launched by the Bank of International Settlements ( BIS ) . <p> In a statement Tuesday , the central bank said it is " proud to be a member of the advisory committee created by the BIS to give guidance on the objectives of the initiative and the features of the fund , which is designed to help central banks incorporate environmental sustainability objectives in reserve management . " <p> " With its participation in the open-ended fund , the BSP takes a step further in recognizing the role of sustainable investing in reserve management , " it said . <p> " Investing in green bonds will also provide diversification benefits to the Bank 's total reserves , " it added . <p> BSP Governor Benjamin Diokno said the country 's investment in BIS ' green bond fund " is now part of RP 's ( Republic of the Philippines ) GIR ( gross international reserves ) . " <p> " It 's part of the diversification of the Bank 's reserves @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The BSP statement cited that " climate change appears to be inevitable , and the financial sector has a significant role to play in pursuing sustainable and inclusive growth in the global economy , the environment , and society . " <p> " The BSP is one with the BIS in its broader commitment to support environmentally responsible finance and investment practices , " it added . ( PNA )
