<h> PH marine resources dev't ' largely being neglected ' : NCWC <p> MANILA -- The development of the Philippines ' " tremendous " marine resources is " largely being neglected , " an official of the National Coast Watch Council ( NCWC ) said on Tuesday . <p> During the National Marine Summit 2019 , NCWC executive director , Undersecretary Jose Luis Alano was saddened that the Philippine waters have not been fully exploited , despite the country 's rich natural resources . <p> " Indeed , the Philippines is blessed with beautiful islands , " Alano told his audience , which include maritime experts , in a speech delivered at the Manila Hotel . <p> " However , ironically , the development of these sustainable use and management of the country 's marine assets are largely being neglected , " he added . <p> Alano also urged the stakeholders from the academe and think-tanks , community-based and non-government organizations , environmentalists and scientists who attended the national maritime summit to help the government deal with the current marine issues in the country . <p> The first National Marine Summit was held this year to emphasize the policy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ interests of the Philippines rest on sustainable development . " <p> Tap marine resources <p> Rhodora Azanza , president of National Academy of Science and Technology , stressed the need to tap the country 's aquatic resources , saying such move would benefit Filipinos . <p> " The Philippines has a lot of properties , natural resources in our territories valued very well in the past because we were concentrating on our man-based natural resources , " Azanza said . <p> " We are rich in marine resource and we have to tap them for the good of the Filipinos , " she added . <p> Despite being an archipelagic nation , the Philippines had lesser ocean-based economic activities last year . <p> A data from the Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) released on Oct. 25 showed that the country 's ocean-based industries accounted for 3.6 percent of gross domestic product in 2018 , same figure registered in 2016 but lower than the 3.7 percent posted in 2017 and 2015 ; 3.9 percent in 2014 ; 4 percent in 2013 ; and 4.3 percent in 2012 . <p> Deputy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Executive Secretary Salvador Medialdea , said the country should develop in " sooner " time its marine resources that have huge economic potential . <p> " We are an archipelagic nation and this geographical fact brings us vast of advantages that we have yet to fully exploit . The sooner all of us embrace our archipelagic nature , the faster we can use our seas to our own advantage , " he said . <p> Expect hazards , risks <p> Azanza noted that the country may yield a potential net annual benefit of USD6.436 billion as a net income from its marine ecosystem . <p> She , however , acknowledged the " hazards and risks " that will be faced by the country in boosting its ocean-based economic activities . <p> She said these include " primary threats to the sustainability of marine ecosystems ; climate change impacts ; unsustainable coastal development ; excessive nutrient input and pollution ; sedimentation ; overfishing ; and unreported and unregulated fishing . " <p> " We have to take care of the management of the opportunities and also the risks attributed to these @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opportunities ( We should not only focus on the opportunities ) , " Azanza said . <p> " We have to consider also the risks associated with these opportunities , " she added . <p> Updated national maritime policy needed <p> Alano said the NCWC would soon come up with an updated national maritime policy ( NMP ) , which he said " has languished in obscurity . " <p> He said there would amendments to the NMP , which include " additional policies . " <p> The NMP was formulated in 1994 to emphasize the Philippines ' archipelagic state and enable the sustainable management of the country 's coastal and marine environment and resources . <p> National Security Hermogenes Esperon Jr . earlier said the updated NMP reaffirms the Duterte administration 's commitment to harmonize economic progress with sustainable development ; develop effective maritime law enforcement systems ; and ensure peaceful settlement of disputes through the rule of law .
