<h> 4Ps beneficiaries trained as teachers <p> TO SERVE AS TEACHERS . Parent-leaders ( PL ) of Pantawid Pamilya Pilipino Program ( 4Ps ) undergo a series of training to equip them with skills in handling the family development sessions ( FDS ) . The West Visayas State University ( WVSU ) -- Lambunao campus under its ' WVSU-Helps for 4Ps ' project develops the ' PL na , Teacher Pa ' module where parent-leaders are trained to become teachers to handle their FDS. ( Photo courtesy of Dr. Jaime G. Latoza ) <p> ILOILO CITY -- They are not just parent-leaders ( PL ) but they will also serve as teachers handling and facilitating family development sessions ( FDS ) for members of the government 's Pantawid Pamilya Pilipino Program ( 4Ps ) . <p> This was the idea behind the " PL na , Teacher Pa " module conceptualized by the West Visayas State University ( WVSU ) -- Lambunao campus under its " WVSU-Helps for 4Ps " project . <p> " Helps for 4Ps is an engagement of WVSU with the DSWD ( Department of Social Welfare and Development ) in response specifically to the conduct @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Latoza , head of the Planning and Development Office , in an interview on Thursday . <p> Latoza , also the program leader of the WVSU-Helps , said the university through its extension and training office has been involved in the delivery of the FDS. <p> However , they noticed that one of the challenges was that the town has only seven municipal links ( ML ) and three local government unit links who handle the FDS for the more than 3,000 members enrolled with the 4Ps in Lambunao 's 73 barangays . There are also a limited number of civil society organizations ( CSOs ) that get involved in the FDS. <p> " We come up with an idea to have a training to enhance the capability of parent leaders because we see the potential in them and found it useful in the delivery of family development sessions , " Latoza added . <p> As of March this year , the WVSU-Helps for 4Ps trained 120 parent leaders and 20 CSOs . They had their first FDS with 4Ps members in April . <p> They will be closely monitored @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ seven months , after which they will be considered graduates . <p> " This is the first in Western Visayas and in the Philippines . I was told that DSWD is actually targeting to develop a module to train parent leaders to facilitate the FDS this year . They still have no mechanism to do that , " Latoza said . <p> Along with the " PL na , Teacher pa " module , the WVSU has developed topics for the FDS that the parents have crafted based on their needs assessment . <p> " The topics were approved by the region ( DSWD ) . This is what is unique in Lambunao because the topics that we are using are prepared by the parent leaders , " Latoza said . <p> He said the topics focus on elections , disaster preparedness , education , community involvement , climate change , facing life 's challenges and tips on strengthening families . <p> " The effect is good because the approach is not highly technical , it is more community-based such that parents understand each other . There is no gap @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Latoza hopes that the initiative will be sustained and the parent-leaders on their own can already handle the sessions themselves . <p> He said the next plan is to have the parent leaders handle the FDS of other barangays and not just limit their exposure in barangays where they reside . <p> The initiative is very timely especially that the 4Ps has been institutionalized , Latoza said . <p> " It is important that we deliver the FDS well because it is one important tool of the 4Ps to achieve its objectives . If we strengthen families , then 4Ps will have a good result , " he said . <p> FDS is one of the requirements for 4Ps members to continue receiving their grant . <p> The other requirements should be for school-age children to be in school and availment of health services in the government 's health facility .
