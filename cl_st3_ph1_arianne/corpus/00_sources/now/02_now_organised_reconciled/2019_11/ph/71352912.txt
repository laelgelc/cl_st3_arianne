<p> It 's becoming a challenge to find open areas in a highly-urbanized metropolis like Surigao City where one can plant trees . But the Rotarians in the City led by the Rotary Club of Metro Surigao Foundation , Inc. , committed to plant 1M trees and their initial experience had them wanting for more . <p> Together with family members and friends -- with the theme " Rotary Connects the World " -- Rotarians from Surigao City , travelled recently to the adjacent Dinagat Islands to a tree-planting adventure in a mined-out area at Cagdianao Mining Corporation ( CMC ) . <p> " In one day alone , all 139 of us , including our children , relatives and friends , planted 2200 trees under an hour . We felt we could do more . We felt we connected with the world indeed .
