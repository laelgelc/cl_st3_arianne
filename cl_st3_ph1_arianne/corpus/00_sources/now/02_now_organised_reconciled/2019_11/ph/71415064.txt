Joey Salceda also exempts foreign assistance or international donations , and goods and services donated from abroad , from taxes , import duties , and donor 's tax . <p> It was Nueva Ecija Rep. Estrellita Suansing , who moved for the approval of the eight tax provisions of the substitute bill . <p> The Salceda panel gave in to the proposed " special exemption to facilitate prompt and efficient response to and/or recovery from disasters " as provided for under Section 67 of the proposed DDR Act . <p> Section 67 of the substitute bill seeks to grant exemption from taxes and import duties for foreign disaster assistance or international donations coursed through the DDR . <p> It also provides exemption from donor 's tax and allows the treatment of donations as a deductible expense for local disaster assistance . <p> Under the bill , goods and services donated from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Section 67 ( d ) which grants " tax incentives to encourage members of the private sector to render aid or provide disaster assistance , and/or to invest in disaster resilience and climate change adaptation measures for their residences , communities or businesses " was referred by the Salceda panel to the House Committee on Local Government which has jurisdiction over local taxation . <p> Under the bill , the local government unit ( LGU ) may also implement local tax rules which would grant disaster victims reasonable reduction , exemption or deferment of local taxes or other types of tax assessments ; or take other necessary action at the local levels to provide tax relief to disaster victims . <p> The Salceda panel also approved Section 68 of the substitute bill which provides that the Bureau of Customs ( BOC ) shall create rules which would among others hasten the processing , release of donated goods and equipment to disaster victims and/or affected areas . <p> It also passed Sections 54 , 64 , 69 and 88 of the proposed DDR Act . <p> Under Section 54 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ allow the immediate implementation of any or all of the following remedial measures : c grant of tax credits or exemptions by the BIR , upon the recommendation of the President or the Department .
