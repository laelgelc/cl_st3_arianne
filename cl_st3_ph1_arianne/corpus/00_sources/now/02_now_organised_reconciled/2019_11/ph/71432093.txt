<p> The alarm about looming environmental disasters has been sounded off a couple of decades ago . But , the discourse on the topic has not changed much . <p> People are still debating the best garbage disposal system that will not hurt air , land or water . And , while people are doing that , the garbage situation is slowly creeping to crisis proportions . Politics and money seem to be the culprits nonetheless . <p> According to the Department of Environment and Natural Resources , the Philippines produces some 9.2 million kilograms per day . In Metro Manila alone , residents generate about 12,500 tons per day , earning us a spot in the top five cities of the world with the most waste . <p> Of the LGUs in Metro Manila , Quezon City gets the top spot by generating about 3,600 tons a day , followed by Manila with 1,174 tons per day , Caloocan , 912 ; Para ? aque , 634 and Makati , 474 .
