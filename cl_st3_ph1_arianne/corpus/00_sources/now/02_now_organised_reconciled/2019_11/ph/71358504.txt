<p> LOS BANOS , Laguna , Nov. 6 -- The ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity ( ACB ) commended the renewed commitment of the leaders of the ASEAN Member States ( AMS ) to environmental protection and climate action during the 35th ASEAN Summit and related summits held in Bangkok , Thailand , on 3 November 2019 . <p> In the Chairman 's Statement at the 10th ASEAN-United Nations ( UN ) Summit , the ASEAN leaders spotlighted the UN 's support for the ACB as part of its global action on climate change . <p> " As part of efforts to further build resilience and strengthen support for climate change adaptation and mitigation strategies , we welcomed the UN 's support for the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity in enhancing coordination among ASEAN Member States in the conservation of ecosystems , " the Chairman 's statement read . <p> ACB Executive Director Theresa Mundita Lim emphasised the importance of intensifying cooperation among AMS in conserving and sustainably managing biodiversity in the region . <p> " Conserving biodiversity and protecting the remaining intact ecosystems in the region greatly matters to the world , given that the ASEAN is a major contributor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Lim said . <p> The ACB also supported the call of Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte to fellow ASEAN leaders not to sacrifice the environment and the region 's rich biodiversity , particularly in the maritime domain , in achieving economic progress . Duterte likewise appealed for regional cooperation in combating marine debris . <p> " We laud this affirmation of the ASEAN Member States ' commitment to biodiversity conservation during the 35th ASEAN summit . This further bolsters our efforts to fulfill our mandate , " Lim said . <p> The ACB , headquartered in the Philippines , was established by the ASEAN Member States in 2005 to facilitate intergovernmental and multi-stakeholder cooperation and coordination on the conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity . <p> Lim noted the ASEAN leaders ' concerted actions , particularly in preparing for the the 15th Conference of the Parties ( COP15 ) to the Convention on Biological Diversity ( CBD ) to be held in Kunming , China in October 2020 . <p> A post-2020 global biodiversity framework , a new paradigm to safeguard nature and its contributions to human well-being is expected @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that this cooperation will result in a strong unified ASEAN voice in the global framework , " added Lim . <p> In the Chairman 's Statement at the 34th ASEAN summit held in Bangkok , Thailand , on 23 June 2019 , AMS were encouraged to join the preparation of the post-2020 biodiversity framework under the CBD , taking into account the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and Sustainable Development Goals . <p> " We recognised the progress made in implementing conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity of the region to achieve the Aichi Biodiversity Targets of the Strategic Plan for Biodiversity 2011-2020 , with the support of the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity , " the statement read . <p> The ACB in collaboration with AMS will hold the Third ASEAN Conference on Biodiversity in Malaysia next year . It is one of the biggest biodiversity events in the world , where the progress made by the AMS in achieving the 2020 Aichi Biodiversity Targets and the region 's long-term direction will be tackled .
