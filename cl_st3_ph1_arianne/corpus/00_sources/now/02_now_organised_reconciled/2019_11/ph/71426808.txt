<h> Pops Fernandez joins campaign to protect Mother Earth <p> Actress-singer Pops Fernandez has joined the bandwagon in the campaign to protect Mother Earth as she made an appeal to do our share in preserving and protecting the environment . <p> ( Photo courtesy of Pops Fernandez Facebook / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> " Let 's be aware of the issues related to the environment . So let 's help protect and preserve it , " said Fernandez at the launch of PLDT-Smart Sustainability Group Sustainability Program , dubbed " Gabay Kalikasan , " at the opening ceremony of its three-day Sustainability Fair at the Legazpi Street entrance of PLDT Makati General Office recently . It coincided with the National Climate Change Awareness Week and the 91st anniversary of PLDT . <p> Fernandez also lauded the program which she describes as " a good project that would benefit the people . " <p> Asked if she would love to do a show in support of the campaign , Fernandez said : " Yes ! Why not ? I would love to do that . " <p> Other artists who lent their support for the same program were Unique Salonga @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " We as Filipinos have a powerful motivation to be active when it comes to taking care of our planet . We are at the frontlines of climate change , " said PLDT Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Manuel V. Pangilinan said . <p> Pangilinan added : " Several recent international studies have warned that the Philippines is among the countries that are being most adversely affected by the changing climate . " <p> PLDT Senior Vice President and Smart Chief Financial Officer Chaye Cabal-Revilla has been appointed as the company 's Chief Sustainability Officer . <p> " PLDT and Smart go beyond business . Caring for our environment is just as important as taking care of our people and hitting our profit targets . The impact of climate change affects everyone pervasively . This includes our safety , our health , our existence , " Cabal-Revilla said .
