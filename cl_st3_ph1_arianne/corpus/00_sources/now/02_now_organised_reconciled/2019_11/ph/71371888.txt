<h> MGB touts merits of responsible mining in new campaign <p> TEAM EFFORT . Officials from the Mines and Geosciences Bureau , together with leaders in the mining industry , mark their cooperation on the information campaign showing the positive contributions of mines in the country during its launch on Friday ( Nov. 8 , 2019 ) . The campaign aims to change public perception of the mining sector which has long been associated with environmental destruction and corrupt practices .
