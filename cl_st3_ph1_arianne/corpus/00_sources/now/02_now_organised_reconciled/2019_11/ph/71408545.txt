<h> ' Ramon ' leaves Cagayan with zero casualty <p> TUGUEGARAO CITY , Cagayan , Nov. 21 ( PIA ) - - According to the Provincial Climate Change and Disaster Risk Reduction Management Council ( PDRRMC ) , the province has attained zero casualty amidst the threat such as massive flooding and landslides brought about by Ramon . <p> Governor Manuel Mamba said the implementation of the pre-emptive evacuation days before the expected landfall of the typhoon contributed a lot in the attainment of zero casualty . <p> " I suppose that those living in the landslide and flood prone areas are now in the evacuation centers that is why we do n't have any casualty , " Governor Mamba said . <p> He also said that the ' adopt-a-neighbor ' strategy is also one of the best practices of the province which really reduces risks and damages to lives and limbs . <p> The adopt-a-neighbor strategy is a scheme wherein those families living in a safe and sturdy structures adopt their neighbors who are living in high risks areas temporarily before the onslaught of a certain calamity such as typhoons . <p> As of now , there are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the province . <p> " We are still assessing the cost
