With the age of social media , increasing levels of public awareness exert more pressure on companies to pursue a larger social and environmental purposes , other than just augmenting profits . <p> Terms such as " People , Planet , Profit , " " Environmental Social and Governance ( ESG ) standards , " " Impact Financing , " " Green Investing , " " Sustainable Business , " " Social Entrepreneurship , " and " Conscious Capitalism " have proliferated in private , public , and societal spheres in order to overturn the traditional way of doing business . <p> While these terms seem novel , the concept of sustainability was already in existence decades ago . In fact , sustainable development was described as early as 1987 in the United Nations Bruntland Commission Report as " development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs .
