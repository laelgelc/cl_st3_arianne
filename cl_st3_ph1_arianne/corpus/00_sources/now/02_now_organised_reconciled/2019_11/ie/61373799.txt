The beautiful chapels with their soaring ceilings and stained-glass windows are perfect for performance and cinema screenings -- a soon-to-be repurposed precinct in a new and exciting format as part of the emerging Kells cultural hub . Coming into Kells has brought Guth Gafa right into the heart of the town . David Rane , Guth Gafa co-director explains the move . <p> " We believe that by being in the centre of Kells we can be part of a dynamic town buzz for this action-packed weekend .
