<h> Prof John Sweeney to give talk on climate change in Dundalk next week <p> Professor John Sweeney will be speaking on the Climate Emergency and Ireland 's Response at a free event in The Spirit Store next week . <p> The event takes place on Tuesday December 3rd at 8pm and is open to all those interested to attend . <p> John is Ireland 's leading authority on climate change and the science behind it . <p> A lecturer in Maynooth University 's geography department since 1978 , John is also director of its Irish Climate Analysis and Research Units ( ICARUS ) . <p> He contributed to the reports of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , which was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2007 . John is also currently President of An Taisce , the National Trust of Ireland . <p> The night is presented by the Louth Nature Trust and Birdwatch Ireland in association with the Louth Environmental Network and admission is free .
