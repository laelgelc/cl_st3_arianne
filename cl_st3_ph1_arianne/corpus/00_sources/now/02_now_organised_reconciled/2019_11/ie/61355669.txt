<h> Get Laois wired for electric cars <h> Environment : More local charging points on the cards <p> 6 Nov 2019 <p> 0 <p> As part of efforts to tackle climate change and encourage a more environmentally sound approach , Laois could soon have more charging points for electric cars . <p> At the recent meeting of the Portlaoise Municipal District in County Hall , Cllr John Joe Fennelly proposed a motion calling on Laois County Council to contact ESB in connection with the charger for electric cars in Temperance Street , Abbeyleix , as this charger unit has not been working for the last four months and also to outline where extra charging points are to be installed going forward . <p> He said that two or three people in Abbeyleix use these and they had to drive elsewhere to charge their cars . <p> He said it was important to have the chargers if the council wanted to lead with climate change measures . <p> Cllr Willie Aird said he had seen two people charging up one Sunday morning , but added : " They were n't Laois cars . " <p> Mr Wes Wilkinson , senior executive engineer , said that as part of their upgrade strategy ESB have indicated that they have undertaken a full review of the charging network and are currently upgrading or replacing AC chargers . <p> The charger at Temperance Street is included in these plans and will be replaced/upgraded in the coming months . <p> Mr Simon Walton , director of services , added that there is a new government initiative to support the roll out of electric vehicle charging points .
