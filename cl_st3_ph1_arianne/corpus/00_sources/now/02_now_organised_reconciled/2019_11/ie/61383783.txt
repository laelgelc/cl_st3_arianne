' <p> Housing Agency conference told that Ireland is facing a climate emergency and housing has a major role to play in addressing climate change and in ensuring Ireland meets its targets . <p> This was the message from the Housing Agency as it hosted its annual conference which explored the theme of ' Housing and Sustainability ' . <h> Creating sustainable communities into the future <p> Ensuring existing homes are more energy efficient , and that new homes produce less emissions , both during construction and over their lifetime , is critical to achieving climate targets . " <p> Compact growth minimises car usage , reduces associated costs and frees up space for other uses such as public parks , playgrounds and community facilities . <h> ' Key pillars ' <p> Michael Carey , chairperson of the Housing Agency said : " Careful planning , good design , quality construction , and good density are the key pillars to meeting current and future housing needs in Ireland . <p> " One of the main objectives of the Housing Agency is to ensure that sustainable communities are at the heart of housing policy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ places that are well planned , with good quality housing , where people have the opportunity to work , learn and play , and where people want to live for future generations .
