<h> 11 Waterford projects qualify for 2020 Young Scientist Exhibition <p> 11 projects from Waterford schools have been named as qualifiers for the 2020 BT Young Scientist Exhibition . <p> BT , the organiser of the event , delivered the good news to the over 1,100 students involved in the 550 finalist projects this week . In total , over 1,800 projects were submitted by students in schools all across the island of Ireland . <p> The students will now begin preparing to exhibit their projects and meet the judges at the Exhibition in January 2020 . <p> A significant number of the projects this year delve into pressing social issues such as mental health , transgender rights and the effects that social media has on young people .
