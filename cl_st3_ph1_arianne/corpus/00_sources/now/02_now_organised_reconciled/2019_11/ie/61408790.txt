<h> Tuam girl wins national Soapbox championship <h> A student from Presentation College Tuam is the winner of the grand final of the Europe Direct National Soapbox Competition 2019 . <p> Johanna Lane , from Bishop Street in Tuam , was the winner of the 18 and Under section of the Soapbox competition national finals which took place at the Royal College of Physicians in Dublin . <p> The Europe Direct National Soapbox Competition is organised by libraries around the country with competitors given three minutes to make a speech for of against the motion . <p> The theme for this year 's competition was ' Climate Change is Not My Problem ' . <p> Galway East TD Sean Canney congratulated Johanna on her win and praised the competition itself , saying that it " provides citizens from every European country an opportunity to have their say about European Union affairs " . <p> Eight libraries around Ireland host Europe Direct Information Centres , which offer citizens a direct link to the activities of the European Commission .
