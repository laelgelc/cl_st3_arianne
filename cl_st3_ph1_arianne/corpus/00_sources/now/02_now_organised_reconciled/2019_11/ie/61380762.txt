<h> Early exit from peat wo n't curb Ireland 's 2030 carbon target <p> The Government-supported closure of the country 's peat industry over the next 13 months will have " no direct impact " in offsetting Ireland 's carbon emission reduction target by 2030 , it has been confirmed . <p> Clarification on this is likely to perplex the thousands of midlands families and small rural businesses still reeling from last week 's sudden announcement that two ESB peat-firing power stations ( supplied with peat from Bord na M ? na ) will shutdown at the end of 2020 -- 15 years earlier than promised under BNM 's initial decarbonisation plan outlined in 2015 . <p> The loss of the large-scale Lough Ree Power Station located in Lanesboro , Co . Longford and the West Offaly Power Station located in Shannonbridge is expected to put more than 4,000 jobs -- both direct and indirect -- in the firing line throughout the region . <p> The decision was reached in line with Government goals to transition away from dependence on fossil-fuel energy and to accelerate moves towards a green , low-carbon energy economy . <p> Whilst acknowledging the frustrations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the peat-harvesting industry for generations -- the Government says it will create hundreds of new jobs in the midlands in the areas of retrofitting homes , bog rehabilitation , recycling and others . <h> Carbon market <p> However , it is worth pointing out that the action will not directly curb Ireland 's efforts to reduce its greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions targets -- set by the European Union under its Effort Sharing Regulation -- for 2030 . <p> And the reason is as follows : <p> Since 2005 , more than 11,000 power stations and industrial plants across the EU -- including Bord na M ? na -- have operated under what 's called the EU 's Emissions Trading System -- referred to as ' EU ETS ' . <p> The ETS feeds into EU policy to combat climate change and is considered " a key tool " for reducing greenhouse gas emissions in a cost-effective manner . <p> The ETS was established as the world 's first major carbon market and accounts for over three-quarters of all international carbon trading . <p> It operates under a ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cap ' side , the overall volume of GHG that can be emitted by the power plants , factories and other fixed installations covered by the EU ETS is limited by a cap on the number of emission allowances . After each year the company must surrender enough allowances to cover all its emissions , otherwise it will be hit with heavy fines . <p> According to the European Commission , the EU ETS has proved that putting a price on carbon and trading in it can work , as emissions from installations in the system are said to be " falling as intended " . <p> According to the commission , in 2020 , emissions from sectors covered by the system will be 21% lower than in 2005 ; in 2030 it projects that they will be 43% lower . <p> However , this system is completely separate from the binding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ireland -- for the next decade which covers all sectors that fall outside the scope of the EU ETS . <p> EU leaders have specified that all sectors of the economy not covered by the EU ETS must reduce emissions by 30% by 2030 compared to 2005 levels -- based on " principles of fairness , cost-effectiveness and environmental integrity " . <h> Department response <p> In response to a query on how the closure of the peat industry will impact on Ireland 's carbon emission reduction targets for 2030 , a spokesperson for the Department of Communications , Climate Action and Environment stated : <p> " The accelerated exit from peat will mean that at least 1.25 million tonnes of carbon will be saved each year and emissions will reduce by up to 9 million tonnes up to 2027 . <p> These figures relate to electricity ( ETS ) sector so there is no direct impact on targets . <p> " But it is still important for long-term decarbonisation and objectives set out in the climate action plan , " the statement concludes .
