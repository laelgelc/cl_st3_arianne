I use the faculty constantly for my own benefit -- for counsel and guidence when I 'm thinking about issues . The university is just a jewel in the West of Ireland and we need to do everything we can to raise the profile of the capabilities which the University of Limerick has , " he told the Limerick Leader adding that he supports UL 's plans to establish a city centre campus . <p> " I think its great , I think the university needs to continue to grow . Darwin said ' it 's not the smartest of the fittest or the fastest that survive -- it 's those who adapt best to the changing environment ' and I think that 's what the University of Limerick is doing by outreaching directly into the city centre and I think it 's a great move and I complement them for their boldness and vision to go and do that , " he said . <p> Ahead of the Shannon International Leasing Conference , MBA students at the Kemmy Business School as well as students of Aeronautical Engineering @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ E195-E2 . <p> Addressing the students , John Slattery said there are extraordinary opportunities in the commercial aviation sector at present but that climate change and embracing the planet are significant challenges . <p> He stated that Embraer is committed to becoming a carbon neutral organisation and that the development of the E195-E2 is part of that process but that more needs to be done . <p> " The aircraft is the most fuel efficient aircraft in the world in its class -- we spent $750m dollars just to design the wings -- not even to produce one pair of wings -- just to design them .
