<h> Series of free events taking place in Dundalk Library this Saturday <p> A number of events associated with the Louth Science Festival are taking place in Dundalk Library this weekend . <p> On Saturday morning at 11am there will be a Crann na Beatha-Tree of Life Workshop . <p> This involves using a variety of recyclable materials to create a tree sculpture while learning how growing more trees can counter-act climate change . The workshop is aimed at children from 6-plus and is a lovely opportunity for parents and guardians to create a sculpture together . <p> The event is free and all are welcome to attend . Saturday <p> Also on Saturday morning at 11.30am the library will host Let 's Go Wild : Jungle tales and art activities for the very young . <p> This is suitable for children aged 3-7 . <p> Finally at 2.30pm on Saturday , the library will host Walking ( Slowly ) Towards Space . <p> In 2014 Dundalk local , Dr Niamh Shaw announced to the world that she wanted to go to space ; and then she made a show about it . She went on a simulated Mars mission and spent time at the European Space Agency 's Astronaut Centre as artist and engineer . <p> ' Walking ( Slowly ) Towards Space ' will see acclaimed communicator Niamh sharing her journey , illuminating the relevance of space in our society , her progress , her struggles and challenges , the importance of sharing the story of space to the wider population , and explaining why she has devoted the remainder of her life to fulfilling a 40 year old dream . Her talk takes place on Saturday at 2.30pm as part of Louth Science Festival . <p> All the events are free and all are welcome to attend .
