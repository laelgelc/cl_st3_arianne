Capitalism is a voracious system forever in search of somewhere to put its money so that it can turn that money into more money . <p> Asking it to do anything else is like suggesting your pet cat become vegan . Henry Ford is alleged to have said : " I do n't make cars ; I make money . " Left to its own devices , in the end , capitalism turns everything into a saleable product and then into money .
