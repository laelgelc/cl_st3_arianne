<h> ' Do or die ' - young climate activists take over Dail and demand action <h> Young climate activists have presented politicians with 10 simple steps to help save the planet . <p> The Dail was taken over by 157 delegates aged 10 to 17 from 26 counties in a special youth assembly to formulate a list of priority actions . <p> The gathering was jointly organised by the Oireachtas and RTE as part of the broadcaster 's special Climate Week series of events . <p> Ceann Comhairle Sean O Fearghail welcomed the group to the Dail , saying : " There is something monumentally important about this gathering . <p> " Many of you have been outside the gate waving your banners on the subject of climate change and now you 're inside in the place where the decisions are made .
