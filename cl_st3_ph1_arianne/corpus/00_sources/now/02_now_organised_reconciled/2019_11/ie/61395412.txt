<p> GCI highlighted the major forces behind intelligent connectivity ; artificial intelligence ( AI ) , broadband , cloud , and the Internet of Things ( IoT ) . It said that these key enablers show significant potential for economic growth . <p> AI-infused intelligent connectivity has begun to have an impact on the digital economy . GCI found that countries who scored upwards of 65 and continuously invested in intelligent connectivity should expect to see a GDP boost of 1% . However , it found that success in the era of intelligence depends on the ability to collaborate on a global scale . <p> Not only developed economies can benefit from intelligent connectivity . When AI is deployed by industries or organisations , any nations can experience the upside potential of AI , irrespective of digital development level .
