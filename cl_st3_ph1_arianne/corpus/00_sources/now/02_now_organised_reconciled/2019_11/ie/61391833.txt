<h> No bus for Kilkenny this Christmas as NTA confirms yet another delay <h> Councillor : ' Service has now lost any shred of public goodwill ' <p> ' It is most unfortunate that the service will not be up and running for the Christmas period ; many town centre shops are struggling and would benefit from this service ' <p> Hopes that locals and visitors would be able to use Kilkenny 's new bus service to do their Christmas shopping have evaporated , with news that the project has yet again been delayed . <p> The National Transport Authority ( NTA ) has informed the council of the latest delay to the long-awaited service , which was originally to be in place by September of 2017 . After several delays , the public was told it would be ready for Christmas . Now , the NTA says it will be February 2020 . <p> Following this latest setback , one local councillor has described the handling of the rollout of the scheme as ' utter incompetence ' , and says the NTA has now lost any public goodwill that had existed . <p> In a letter dated November @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Chief Executive of Kilkenny County Council Colette Byrne that the bus service will now not commence until 2020 .
