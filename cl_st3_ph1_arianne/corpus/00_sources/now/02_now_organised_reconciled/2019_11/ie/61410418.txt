<h> Svante partners with Cross River on carbon capture projects <p> Svante Inc. , formerly Inventys , has signed a collaboration agreement with Cross River Infrastructure Partners LCC to develop industrial scale carbon capture projects in North America . <p> Connecticut-based Cross River will be responsible for project origination and financing the carbon capture and storage projects using Svante 's technology , as well as operating facilities that deliver captured carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) to end-users . <p> " We provide capital and commercialisation solutions to technology providers such as Svante to develop pipelines of small to mid-size projects with similar risk profiles , " said Andrew Wilder CEO of Cross River . <p> " Our goal is to help unlock significant investment capital to accelerate the deployment of low-carbon technologies such as Svante 's . " <p> The partnership will focus on the Permian Basin in the US and in Canada 's western provinces , both of which have large CO2 storage potential , a wide range of commercial CO2 users , and a significant number of industrial sources of CO2 , such as cement and hydrogen production . <p> " With the development of new sustainable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the US ' 45Q tax credit to incentivise industry and transitional project financing , the financial sector is poised to support industrial scale carbon capture that will have a meaningful impact on climate change , " said Claude Letourneau , President and CEO of Svante . <p> " An integrated project delivery model is fundamental to ensuring carbon capture and storage projects can be financed and delivered with both cost and scheduling certainty .
