<h> Learn more about the heritage of Laois at a Portlaoise conference <p> Portlaoise is the place for a special free conference on the varied heritage of Laois this month . <p> The Heritage Office of Laois County Council will hold a conference called Laois Heritage Matters on Saturday November 23 , at the Midlands Park Hotel . <p> It aims to bring together all interested people to explore and celebrate the rich built , natural and cultural heritage in Laois . <p> Catherine Casey is Heritage Officer with Laois County Council . <p> " This conference is a great opportunity for the people of Laois to come together and learn more about our heritage , to share views and discuss issues with a range of specialists , " she said . <p> The conference will also provide the opportunity to discuss the next Heritage Plan for County Laois , which will be written later this year and published in early 2020 . <p> Topics will include a sample of surveys and awareness-raising work undertaken in recent years . <p> The morning will focus on natural heritage , the recent BirdWatch Ireland survey of swifts in Laois , community @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Plan actions in Laois , and adaptation plans for climate change . <p> In the afternoon the spotlight is on the built and cultural heritage , with talks on the archaeology of the Great Heath , maps of Laois the Portlaoise Plane , recent work at Fort Protector in Portlaoise and conservation at three medieval churches . <p> The advice is to book early , on Eventbrite or email ccasey@laoiscoco.ie <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Derry Now editorial team on 028 7129 6600 for Derry City stories Or 028 7774 3970 for County Derry stories .
