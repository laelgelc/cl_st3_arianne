Tipperary schoolkids learn how to save the planet at science workshops <p> 23 Nov 2019 <p> 0 <p> Dr Ken Farquhar demonstrating a science experiment to students of Carrick-on-Suir 's Edmund Rice Secondary School <p> A science show about saving the planet at Carrick-on-Suir 's Edmund Rice Secondary School and Carrick-on-Suir Library was enjoyed by children from local schools this month . <p> Dr Ken Farquhar 's interactive show How to Save the Planet in 45 minutes was run in association with Calmast WIT Stem Outreach Centre took place at both venues on Friday , November 8 just before the start of Science Week . <p> Groups from local primary schools attended the fun science sessions . <p> The show demonstrated ways to reduce , recycle , use green transport and preserve water and essential resources . <p> Dr Farquhar explored the cause and effect of global warming and climate change and conducted practical experiments to back up the theory . <p> Students gave their views and spoke of how their own schools were working towards a more sustainable planet for the future through the Schools Green Flag initiative focusing on energy conservation , recycling , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ school and at home . <p> A series of related workshops took place in the Library over four days during Science Week under the guidance of four Edmund Rice Secondary School Transition Year students .
