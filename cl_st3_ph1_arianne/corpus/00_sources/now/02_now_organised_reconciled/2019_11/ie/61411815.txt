<h> Hutchinson snaps up Carys Bray 's climate change novel <p> Hutchinson has signed the new novel from award-winning author Carys Bray about the impact of climate change on mental health . <p> Hutchinson has signed the new novel from award-winning author Carys Bray about the impact of climate change on mental health . <p> Jocasta Hamilton , publishing director at Hutchinson , acquired world English rights in When the Lights Go Out in a " strong five-figure " deal from Veronique Baxter at David Higham . Hutchinson will publish in hardback , e-book and audio on 7th May 2020 . <p> Bray 's first novel , A Song for Issy Bradley , was published by Hutchinson in 2014 , going on to be shortlisted for the Costa Awards the following year and winning the Authors ' Club Best First Novel Award 2015 . Her most recent novel , The Museum of You ( Hutchinson ) , was published in 2016 . <p> Hutchinson said of her forthcoming novel : " Brilliantly exploring the impact climate change is having on our mental health , When the Lights Go Out centres on the relationship between Emma , who 's preparing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for societal collapse . In his mind , desperate times call for desperate measures . Chris has turned off the heating . He treks his sons across the Moss in the drubbing rain . He has filled the garage with rice and beans and wants the family to practise suturing on the pigs trotters he 's put in the freezer . And he has other plans that , if voiced , Emma would surely veto . But what if , while preparing for disaster , he unwittingly precipitates it ? " <p> Hamilton said : " Carys is such an incredible writer . Here she 's writing about hope , fear and the climate of a marriage changing . The novel asks how you live if you believe your world is going to end and how you rescue someone who does n't want to be rescued . " <p> Bray , who is based in Lancashire , said : " I started writing this novel almost four years ago . During those years , discussion of the issues I was exploring became more diffuse and urgent .
