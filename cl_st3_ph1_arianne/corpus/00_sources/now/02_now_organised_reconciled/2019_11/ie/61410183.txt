UK startup Econic Technologies is developing catalysts that could take some of that CO2 and lock it up in high-performance polymer materials . <p> Econic 's catalysts allow carbon dioxide to react with a range of epoxides to make polycarbonate materials in which up to 50% of the polymer 's molecular weight ( depending on the epoxide structure ) is derived from incorporated CO2 . The company spun off from Charlotte Williams ' group at Imperial College London in 2011 . <p> While Econic is not the only company developing ways to make such materials , its catalysts are some of the most active and selective available , claims commercial director Rulande Henderson .
