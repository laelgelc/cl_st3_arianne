One of my personal goals is to promote engineering to all , and specifically to improve gender balance . <p> I believe that the only way to solve the ' world 's big problems ' is through allowing all diverse groups to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ marginalised communities in the profession of engineering .
