<p> Festival lift-off in Kilworth took place last weekend and the mood is understandably upbeat with a full and varied programme to look forward to until the end of the week . <p> The centrepiece of the Bank Holiday weekend was the fancy dress parade . Yes , it did threaten to rain on this particular parade , however , the Gods were smiling on the north Cork village and the mainly youthful participants were able to proceed in comfort . <p> With plenty of imagination evident , the adjudication panel had plenty of food for thought . Some of the themes included , space travel , climate change and the management of plastic . <p> John Ahern is a native of Araglin and has been associated with The Avondhu since 1997 . A great lover of sport , John is particularly interested in GAA and is often called upon to don the referee 's jersey . He has a keen interest in local issues and current affairs and his knowledge of the general locale sees him in much demand for Avondhu assignments .
