<h> Floods in Italy 's Venice blamed on climate change <p> MILAN ( AP ) Flooding in the canal city of Venice , Italy has reached the second-highest level ever , after the infamous 1966 floods . <p> The high-water mark hit 187 cm late on Tuesday , meaning more than 85% of the city was flooded . The highest level ever recorded was 198 cm in 1966 . <p> Venice 's mayor blamed climate change for the " dramatic situation " and called for a speedy completion of a long-delayed project to construct off-shore barriers . Called " Moses , " the moveable under-sea barriers are meant to limit flooding of the city , caused by southerly winds that push the tide into Venice . <p> Mayor Luigi Brugnaro said that the flood levels represent " a wound that will leave indelible signs .
