" <p> Fires in the state 's northeast have claimed three lives , destroyed more than 150 homes and razed more than 850,000 hectares ( 3,300 square miles ) of forest and farmland since Friday . <p> Fire conditions are forecast to be worse on Tuesday than they were at the peak of the current fire emergency on Friday . <p> Premier Gladys Berejiklian said the last time a state of emergency was declared in New South Wales was 2013 when there were extensive fires in the Blue Mountains west of Sydney . <p> " The catastrophic weather conditions mean that things can change very quickly , " she told reporters in Sydney . <p> Catastrophic fire danger has been declared for Sydney @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with severe and extreme danger across vast tracts of the rest of the state . <p> The week-long declaration of a state of emergency gives the Rural Fire Service sweeping powers . <p> The annual Australian fire season , which peaks during the Southern Hemisphere summer , has started early after an unusually warm and dry winter .
