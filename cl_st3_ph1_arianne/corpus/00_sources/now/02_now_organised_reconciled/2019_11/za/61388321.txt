<h> Constitutional Court dismisses mine company bid to mine in protected area <p> By ERNEST MABUZA - 18 November 2019 <p> The Constitutional Court has had the final say on the approvals for a coal mine inside an Mpumalanga Protected Area and Strategic Water Source Area . <p> Earlier this month the Constitutional Court refused mining company Atha Africa 's final challenge of a 2018 high court decision to set aside ministerial approvals for the proposed coal mine inside a declared protected environment . <p> In November 2018 , the high court in Pretoria set aside 2016 decisions by former mineral resources minister Mosebenzi Zwane and the late environmental affairs minister Edna Molewa to permit a new coal mine to be developed inside the Mabola Protected Environment . <p> In February , the high court refused Atha Africa 's leave to appeal against its decision . <p> In April , the Supreme Court of Appeal also refused Atha 's application for leave to appeal .
