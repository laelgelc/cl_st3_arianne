The 2019 report explored the theme of South Africa : A society in transition and what this means for those engaged in entrepreneurial ventures . <p> Donna Rachelson , director of Seed Academy , says : " The positivity of entrepreneurs is encouraging , and we have seen that when they are supported , they acquire skills that give them a much better chance of succeeding . The granularity of our survey results combined with our in-depth understanding of the ecosystem allows for this study to be one of the most highly quoted reports with respect to findings on entrepreneurship in South Africa .
