<p> The UK 's two-party political system is fragile during this election following the rise of the Liberal Democrats and the Brexit Party . However , it still looks like Labour is the only one capable of unseating the Conservatives . At the heart of any partnership would be a second referendum on Brexit , which the Lib Dems have said they would support if they ca n't win a majority .
