<h> More than a third of South Africans without water -- R90bn needed to fix this <p> Nearly 500 000 residents of Sebokeng and Evaton in the Emfuleni local municipality were left without water for the past three days . The municipality said that the abrupt stop in water supply was due to a power failure at the Langerand reservoir on Sunday . This then led to low levels of water in the area . <p> Water tanks had to be brought in by the authorities so residents would have a supply of water while municipal technicians were sent to the reservoir for repairs . <p> The water interruption is the latest in a series of challenges facing Emfuleni and other municipal water systems in the country .
