<h> Political Analysis South Africa <h> Ivory Coast 's president says 54 percent of the population lives in cities <h> Ivory Coast 's president says 54 percent of the population lives in cities <p> Published on <p> 21 November , 2019 <h> Ivory Coast has 54 percent of its population living in cities , with a high concentration in Abidjan , which hosts 5.5 million inhabitants , President Alassane Ouattara said on Tuesday , 19 November 2019 . <p> Ouattara , who took part alongside German Chancellor , Angela Merkel , and several of his peers at the G20 Conference on German Direct Investment in Africa , said : " Fifty-four percent of the Ivorian population lives in cities , especially in Abidjan , which has 5.5 million inhabitants . " <p> He referred to a flagship project for water purification and drainage implemented by the German firm , Gauff GmbH &amp; Co Engineering KG , and the German bank , KFW , with the support of the Ivorian government , a note from the presidency in Abidjan said . <p> The Ivorian president noted the importance of this project , considering the challenges of sanitation and rainwater drainage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " that this sanitation and drainage project is of paramount importance for his country . <p> He went on to say that the project also allows Cote d'Ivoire to be well equipped to face up to the challenges of climate change with all its disasters , including floods . <p> Ouattara has also encouraged the German government to join France in securing the United States ' signature on the COP 21 Climate Agreement . <p> German Chancellor and Egyptian President , Abdel Fattah Al Sissi , and the current Chair of the African Union , have both welcomed positive results . These have been achieved since the implementation of direct cooperation between Germany and the African member states of the " Compact With Africa . " <p> They stressed , however , that challenges remain , including the issue of security and the fight against terrorism , climate change and youth employment . <p> Agreements on , among others , sanitation and military engineering and the establishment of a German commercial representation in Cote d'Ivoire were signed during this ceremony .
