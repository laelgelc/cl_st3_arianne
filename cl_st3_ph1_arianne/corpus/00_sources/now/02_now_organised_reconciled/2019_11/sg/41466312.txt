<h> Popular <h> SIA going green in and out of the air <p> Plastic is out and paper straws and reusable cups are in for Singapore Airlines as it seeks to shift to more sustainable practices , both in and outside the aircraft . <p> In a push to go green , Singapore Airlines has completely swopped out single-use plastics , for both the " swizzle stick " ( drink stirrer ) and straws , replacing them with bamboo and paper substitutes respectively . <p> The paper cups that pilots traditionally get their drinks in have also been switched to reusable ones to cut down on wastage . <p> The moves are just one part of the national carrier 's push to increase sustainability across its operations and lower its carbon footprint against a backdrop of worsening and unpredictable climate change . <p> Even the in-flight catering gets a rethink in the form of the " farm-to-plane " concept . Locally sourced food is prepared and delivered to the plane where it is , reducing the need for transportation of that food to the aircraft from elsewhere . <p> To save on utilities , some 18 per cent of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be generated by solar panels , while rainwater will make up 12 per cent of water consumption .
