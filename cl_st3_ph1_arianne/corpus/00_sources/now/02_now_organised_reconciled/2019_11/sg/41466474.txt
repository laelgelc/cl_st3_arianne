<p> Having expressed a tremendous amount of dissatisfaction with the Kyoto Protocol , it was not surprising that many countries were determined to create a new agreement that would hopefully address in a meaningful way some of the more demanding aspects of climate change and the consequences for countries , large and small . Alas , a deal has been reached . <p> The New York Times sums up the recent climate change agreement gratifyingly : " With the sudden bang of a gavel on Saturday night , representatives of 195 nations reached a landmark accord that will , for the first time , commit nearly every country to lowering planet-warming greenhouse gas emissions to help stave off the most drastic effects of climate change . <p> The deal , which was met with an eruption of cheers and ovations from thousands of delegates gathered from around the world , represents a historic breakthrough on an issue that has foiled decades of international efforts to adddress climate change .
