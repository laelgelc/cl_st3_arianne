<h> Emerging brands that swear by sustainable fashion <p> The fashion industry has had a devastating impact on the environment . It remains the second-largest polluting industry in the world . As per a 2018 report published in the Nature Climate Change journal , " The total greenhouse gas emission from textile production is 1.2 billion tonnes per year . By some estimates , sector emissions are expected to rise by more than 60% by 2030 . " It is thus impossible to tackle climate change without creating a sustainable fashion industry . <p> But we seem to be headed in the wrong direction . According to a survey conducted by Ellen MacArthur Foundation , " Global clothing production has doubled in the past 15 years , and this has happened because of the quicker turnaround of styles , decreasing price , and more production , which is called fast-fashion . " <p> The damage wrought on the environment by this industry has only been exacerbated , and its rate accelerated , by the proponents of fast fashion . <p> Yet the general awareness levels have increased around the world .
