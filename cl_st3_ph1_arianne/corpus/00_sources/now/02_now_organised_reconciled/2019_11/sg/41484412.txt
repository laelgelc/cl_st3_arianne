<p> With endeavours on an international scale to mitigate climate change , advances to push for energy conservation on an individual level are also currently under way in Singapore . <p> Initiatives such as equipping homes with smart meters to keep track of electricity consumption , customising tips to save energy for selected homes and giving incentives to go green have been introduced . <p> Such initiatives will enable individuals to make better lifestyle decisions that reduce energy consumption in their homes . <p> These initiatives have yet to have an impact on the majority of the population so , in the meantime , it would be helpful to make available the estimated energy consumption for common household appliances .
