The state government has estimated the loss and damage caused by the cyclone to the tune of Rs 50,000 crore . <p> Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change and BJP leader Babul Supriyo was sent to Bengal 's cyclone-affected areas by the Central government to assess the situation on the ground and report back . On Wednesday , Supriyo visited the affected areas of Namkhana , Bakkhali and Diamond Harbour and following his visit to the affected areas , he spoke to The Sunday Guardian about his experience , and also about the politics of Bengal and the byelections that are due in the state .
