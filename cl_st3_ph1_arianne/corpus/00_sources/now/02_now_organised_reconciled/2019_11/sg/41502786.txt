" is a question I 'm invariably asked after I give a talk on climate change and health . Even as wildfires incinerate communities in California ; hurricanes decimate islands , taking thousands of lives ; and Qatar starts to air condition its outdoors from scorching heat , some continue to " not believe " in climate change . <p> But I know none of this would work . Research shows us that presenting scientific facts , the " information deficit model " of communication , is often not effective in changing deeply held beliefs about climate change . <p> So instead of asking myself how I should convince someone of climate change , I started asking why instead . The answer is simple , is n't it ? If they " believe " in climate change , they will want to take action .
