The researchers also found that the very young and the very old were the most vulnerable . <p> Undernutrition is a global public health concern , especially in the low- and middle-income nations . In 2016 , around 420 million adults of 20 years and more , and 192 million children and adolescents between 5 and 19 , were underweight . <p> Of children under 5 years of age , 150 million were stunted , and 52 million were wasted .
