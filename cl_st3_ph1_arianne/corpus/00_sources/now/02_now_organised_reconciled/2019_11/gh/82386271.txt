<h> Help climate change campaign -- Bawumia to Banking sector <p> Vice President Dr. Mahammudu Bawumia wants Ghana 's banking sector to be in step with the rest of the world in addressing the challenges arising from climate change . <p> The Vice President made the call when he launched Ghana 's Sustainable Banking Principles and Sector Guidance Notes in Accra . <p> The five Sector-specific Guidance Notes on priority areas of the economy cover the Agriculture &amp; Forestry , Mining and Oil &amp; Gas , Construction and Real Estate , Power and Energy , and Manufacturing activities as related to the banking portfolios . <p> Dr. Bawumia said the government will implement measures to undertake climate change and green economy programmes and projects , which will promote a clean environment , increase job creation and accelerate poverty reduction .
