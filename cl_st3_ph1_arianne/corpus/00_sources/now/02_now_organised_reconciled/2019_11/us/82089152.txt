There are nearly 400 species of birds that could be in grave danger of extinction in less than 100 years . <p> Painted Bunting on approach ( Photo : CONTRIBUTED PHOTO BY JOHN NELSON ) <p> Climate change , however , is not the only reason why birds are disappearing . <p> Habitat loss is a major factor that is often overlooked . With approximately 1,000 people moving into our state every day , natural habitat for our birds and other species is quickly being turned into residential communities . If you are looking for an example of this , drive down Becker Road . <p> Obviously , we ca n't prevent people from moving into the Treasure Coast , but there are some things we can do to make our yards more bird friendly . <p> Landscaping with native plants and trees is one of those remedies -- it will create a backyard habitat for our birds in peril . Many species of birds depend heavily on insects in their diet , and those insects can be found in abundance in native plants and trees . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the use of pesticides , when in fact , our birds are the best pest control there is . <p> John Nelson ( Photo : CONTRIBUTED PHOTO FROM JILL GLEICHMAN ) <p> Making your property bird friendly is simple . Start by looking at your landscaping and talk to a nursery employee that specializes in native plants and trees -- or consult with our local Native Plant Society . The National Audubon Society also has created a great database that can help you identify native plants specific to our area : **25;326;TOOLONG . <p> Bringing native plants like American Beauty-Berry or Fakahatchee Grass and native trees onto your property not only helps birds with food resources , but it may also provide nesting habitat for some species . <p> If you 're happy with your landscaping , consider putting up some bird feeders . While most bird species eat insects , they also supplement their diet with seeds . A bird feeder in your back yard can almost immediately attract birds . <p> As habitat for birds continues to be lost due to development , bird-friendly backyards are increasingly becoming @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in our urban areas . This has become so successful that skyscrapers are often designed with green spaces on top of roofs or terraces . <p> Not every bird species is going to be attracted to your back yard , so how we respect the rest of our environment is equally important . Plastic garbage is rearing its ugly head nearly everywhere . We often think of plastic as being an environmental issue for our marine life , and it is , yet there are several species of birds that live on the open ocean . <p> Northern Cardinal ( female ) ( Photo : CONTRIBUTED PHOTO BY JOHN NELSON ) <p> Scientists are finding large numbers of these birds are ingesting plastic scooped up from the ocean surface . The results are tragic . <p> Back at home , we can reduce the number of bird kills in our neighborhoods by keeping our cats indoors . <p> Cats are great house pets , but they have no place in our natural environment .
