<p> The highest tide in decades has submerged more than 85% of Venice in floodwaters , causing catastrophic damage to the historic Italian lagoon city and its monuments . <p> " Venice is on its knees , " Mayor Luigi Brugnaro said on Twitter on Wednesday . " The Basilica of St. Mark 's has suffered serious damage like the entire city and the islands . " <p> Brugnaro , who has blamed climate change for Venice 's second-worst flooding on record , declared a state of emergency and said the city will " need everyone 's help to overcome these days that are putting us to the test . " <p> The flooding , prompted by strong winds that pushed a high tide into the city , has caused at least one death -- an elderly man was electrocuted while trying to repair a hydraulic pump on the island of Pellestrina -- closed schools , theaters , and conservatories ; submerged the crypt at St. Mark 's Basilica ; and sunk motorboats and ferries , a critical mode of transportation . <p> Venice was built on a series of small islands in a shallow lagoon and has slowly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a result of climate change .
