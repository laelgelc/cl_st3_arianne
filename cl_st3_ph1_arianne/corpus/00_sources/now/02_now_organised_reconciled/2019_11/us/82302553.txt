<h> Harvard-Yale game delayed by on-field protest <p> A climate change protest got in the way of Harvard and Yale 's historic Ivy League football rivalry game on Saturday , as activists delayed the start of the second half by running onto the field in New Haven , Conn . <p> With the Crimson taking a 15-3 lead into the locker room at halftime , protesters from both schools went to midfield at around 1:40 p.m . ET following the performance by the Yale band . The group eventually grew from a few dozen into the hundreds , and after the field was cleared , the game was resumed at 2:48 p.m . ET . <p> Written on one of the several banners carried onto the field was , " " Nobody wins . Yale &amp; Harvard are complicit in climate injustice . " <p> Players who were warming up for the second half returned to their locker rooms until order was restored . <p> ESPN reported that a number of protesters tied themselves together and asked to be arrested . Some of the spectators were taken off the field by police . A few remaining protesters who stayed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ told by Yale Police Chief Ronnell Higgins they would be arrested . <p> According to Harvard student Caleb Schwartz , who is a spokesman for the group Divest Harvard , the protest has been planned for months . <p> " This is a very deliberate choice of targeting this specific to get our action out there , " Schwartz told ESPN 's Paul Kix.
