<p> Tracey Henderson is a rare woman at the top ranks of an energy company who calls the shots on where to drill for oil and gas -- and in a world where big energy outfits have cut back on massive exploration projects , she is responsible for one of this year 's biggest discoveries . <p> As chief exploration officer for Kosmos Energy Ltd . KOS 0.18% , Ms. Henderson led the effort that located a big natural-gas field off the coast of West Africa . <p> Kosmos , a $2.6 billion market-cap company focused on exploration , discovered the field in partnership with BP PLC , and announced the Orca-1 well find in late October . The field , offshore Mauritania , holds around 1.3 billion barrels of oil equivalent , according to the consulting firm Rystad Energy . <p> When Kosmos first began working in the area , " the region was underexplored and overlooked by the industry .
