<h> " So Heavy I Fell Through the Earth " <p> Grimes ' new music this year has often resembled roguish , pessimistic concept art . With stomping n ? -metal transhumanism and sinister synthpop about abusive power dynamics , the dystopian conceit of her upcoming climate change-themed Miss Anthropocene has so far hinged on audacious genre-hopping and overly affected lyrics , for better and for worse . Grimes ' latest offering , the six-minute downtempo odyssey " So Heavy I Fell Through the Earth , " feels like an inevitable comedown from the rave-ups , with the forceful theatrics supplanted by a languid pace and insular frame of mind . <p> Grimes seems even more emotionally fraught than we last heard on the slick , breathless " Violence . " She coos of feeling so " weighed down " by love that she fears the ground beneath her could give way , extending another dark , complicated notion of violence as comfort .
