Or a smart factory floor where thousands of sensors stream data to the cloud , boosting manufacturing efficiency while machines perform preventive self-maintenance that keeps workers safe . Or dozens of autonomous vehicles that platoon on the highway , communicating directly in real-time . <p> They 're all the direct benefit of an emerging silicon renaissance when the furthest flights of human imagination will be reached through the power of software and bold new chip designs .
