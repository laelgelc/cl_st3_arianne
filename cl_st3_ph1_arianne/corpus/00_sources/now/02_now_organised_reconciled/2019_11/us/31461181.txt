Pew Research Center recently polled 3,627 US adults to gauge their opinion on climate change , and the results show a generational divide in the GOP getting wider with every generation . <p> While only 31% of boomers ( 1946-64 ) say the federal government does too little to reduce the effects of climate change , 52% of millennials ( born after 1980 ) say the same thing . Overall , two-thirds of US adults ( 67% ) agree the US government does too little . <p> Do n't expect change anytime soon .
