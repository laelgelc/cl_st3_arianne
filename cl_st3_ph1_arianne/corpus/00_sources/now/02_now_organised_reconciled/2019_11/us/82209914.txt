<p> ANKARA , Nov 19 ( Reuters ) - Turkey has ordered the detention of 133 military personnel over suspected links to a network Ankara accuses of organising an attempted coup in 2016 , the state-run Anadolu news agency said on Tuesday . <p> The suspects are being sought in an operation centred in the western coastal province of Izmir , Anadolu said , adding that 82 of them were serving members in the military . <p> Ankara blames U.S.-based Muslim cleric Fethullah Gulen , who has lived in self-imposed exile in Pennsylvania since 1999 , of masterminding the failed putsch on July 15 , 2016 . He has denied any involvement . <p> In the three-year purge since the coup attempt , more than 77,000 people have been jailed pending trial and about 150,000 civil servants , military personnel and others have been sacked or suspended from their jobs . Widespread arrests are still carried out routinely . <p> Turkey 's Western allies and rights groups have criticised the massive crackdown , saying President Tayyip Erdogan has used the abortive coup as a pretext to quash dissent . <p> Ankara has defended the measures as a necessary response @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and has vowed to eradicate Gulen 's network .
