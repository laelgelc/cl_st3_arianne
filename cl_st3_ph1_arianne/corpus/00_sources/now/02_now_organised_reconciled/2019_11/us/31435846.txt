<h> The Science of Stupid : Galileo is Rolling Over in His Grave <p> So here 's a fine howdy-do for Galileo Galilei : Exactly one day -- one flipping day -- after the great man 's 450th birthday , on Feb. 15 , 2014 , a study by the National Science Foundation ( NSF ) revealed that one in four Americans does not know that the Earth orbits the Sun . That 's roughly 78 million people , or six times greater than the entire population of Galileo 's native Italy in 1632 -- the year he was sentenced to life under house arrest for advancing that heretical belief . Yet somehow , four centuries later later , 25% of us still have n't gotten the word .
