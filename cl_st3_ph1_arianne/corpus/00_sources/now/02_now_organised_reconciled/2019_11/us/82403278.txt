" It has to be done to prevent catastrophic climate change . The cost of business-as-usual as the Arctic ice cap melts , sea levels rise , deadly floods and droughts become commonplace , and animal species go extinct en masse is far worse than making the bold decision to put the green evolution into overdrive .
