<p> BEIRUT , Nov 1 ( Reuters ) - Lebanese banks opened to customers on Friday for the first time in two weeks following an unprecedented wave of protests that led the prime minister to resign , with small numbers of customers queuing as the doors opened . <p> At a branch of Blom Bank , one of Lebanon 's biggest banks , in Hamra Street , around 10 customers entered the bank as its doors opened after 8 a.m. , a Reuters witness said . The number then grew to 20 . <p> In the Sodeco district of the capital , around 20 people were queuing outside a branch of Frasnsabank and around 15 were waiting outside a branch of Bank Audi , a Reuters witness said .
