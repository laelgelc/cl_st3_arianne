<p> In a sweeping new report on the state of the oceans and the cryosphere -- the ecological realm of sea ice , snow , and glaciers -- the United Nations climate change body does n't pull any punches . Even if one looks only at oceans and ice , failure to dramatically reshape " all aspects of society " in order to keep warming in check -- less than two degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels -- will mean devastation for coastal cities , Arctic communities , small island states , anyone who relies on glaciers for water , and virtually all sea creatures . <p> In other words , it would be the end of life on Earth as we know it . <p> By contrast , holding warming to 1.5 ? C would still produce devastating effects -- but they would be substantially less severe , and still leave room for humanity to adapt . <p> The report , written by more than 100 climate and marine scientists from more than 36 countries , details how a rise in sea levels is slated to become more extreme .
