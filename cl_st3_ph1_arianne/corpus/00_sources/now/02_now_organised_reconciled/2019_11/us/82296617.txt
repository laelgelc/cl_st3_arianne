<p> NAGOYA , Japan , Nov 23 ( Reuters ) - South Korea and Japan will put discussions about their contentious wartime past back on the table , Seoul 's foreign minister said on Saturday , warning that a recent thaw in relations had bought them a little , but not much , time . <p> Kang Kyung-wha made the comments to a small group of reporters on the sidelines of a Group of 20 ( G20 ) ministers ' meeting in the Japanese city of Nagoya . Kang spoke after meeting with her Japanese counterpart , Toshimitsu Motegi . <p> " We bought time for intense discussions , but there 's not much time left for us , " Kang told reporters . <p> On Friday South Korea reversed at the last minute a decision to let an intelligence-sharing pact with Japan expire . Kang hailed that as a breakthrough in relations .
