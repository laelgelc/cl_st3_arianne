<h> Farmers can play a role in reducing atmospheric carbon Letter <p> I am increasingly impressed by the potential of land-based methods of carbon drawdown to mitigate climate change . Many of these practices could be applied on farms large or small , conventional or organic . <p> Interspersing trees with food crops or livestock animals adds diversity of nutrients to the soil . Planting cover crops such as clover , hairy vetch and rye carry nitrogen from the atmosphere into the soil . Rotational grazing ( allowing cows or other ruminant animals to intensively graze a pasture , then moving them to adjacent fresh pasture to allow the first one to regrow ) adds significant fertility to soil . Planting trees in the pasture increases soil carbon even more . <p> No-till farming and permaculture ( working with nature 's systems to create multi-layered , compact food gardens with minimal machinery or other disturbance ) are among many ecological agriculture practices that partner with Earth and soak up atmospheric carbon . <p> Plants are carbon storehouses .
