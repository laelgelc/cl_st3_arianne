<p> Beth Rose Middleton , professor and chair of the Native American studies department and Fellow at the John Muir Institute of the Environment at UC Davis , will speak on " Tribal Leadership in Climate Change Adaptation " from 9:45 to 10:50 a.m . Sunday , Nov. 10 , in the Walker Room at Davis United Methodist Church , 1620 Anderson Road in Davis . <p> Middleton is the author of " Trust in the Land : New Directions in Tribal Conservation " ( University of Arizona Press 2011 ) and " Upstream : Trust Lands and Power on the Feather River " ( University of Arizona Press 2018 ) . <p> From traditional land stewardship to interventions in state , national and international policy , California Indian nations are leaders in environmental policy and planning . Middleton will reflect on her collaborative research and curriculum development with tribes on topics including Native land trusts , Native-led conservation land acquisitions , tribal participation in the carbon credit market and the importance of reintroducing traditional fire management . <p> For more information , contact the church office at email protected or 530-756-7530.
