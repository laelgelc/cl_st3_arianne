<p> This image features the intersection of 1st Street and Broadway in downtown Los Angeles . In the background is the historic Los Angeles Times building , opened in 1935 . At right is the 10-story Mirror Building , opened in 1948 . <p> A branch of Citizens National Bank occupies the opposite corner . Next door is the Redwood Restaurant . In 1973 , the William Pereira-designed Times-Mirror headquarters replaced those buildings . <p> Turns out , this unpublished image may have been taken for a business story . The Nov. 2 , 1963 , Los Angeles Times business section reported the merger of Crocker Bank and Citizens National Bank . <p> Current development plans call for the removal of the former Times-Mirror headquarters building .
