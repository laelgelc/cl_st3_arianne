Instead of riding into the sunset , Gov . Eric Holcomb tapped him to head the state 's effort to get a handle on the opioid/heroin epidemic that has been hammering the state , a disaster that has claimed thousands of lives . This became the most challenging job in Indiana . I personally know of a half dozen families who have either lost a son or daughter , or who have struggled with recovery with this insidious scourge .
