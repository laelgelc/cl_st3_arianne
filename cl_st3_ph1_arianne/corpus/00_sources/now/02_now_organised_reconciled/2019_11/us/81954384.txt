<h> Jane Fonda spends night in jail after demonstration arrest <p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- Actress and activist Jane Fonda spent a night in a local jail after her fourth arrest in as many weeks while participating in a climate change demonstration on Capitol Hill . <h> Actress and activist Jane Fonda is arrested by U.S. Capitol police at Hart Senate Office Building as she and other demonstrators called on Congress for action to address climate change , on Capitol Hill in Washington , Friday , Nov. 1 , 2019 . ( AP Photo/Jose Luis Magana ) <p> The 81-year-old Oscar winner was among more than 40 people arrested Friday while sitting in the atrium of the Hart Senate Office Building . A spokesman for Fire Drill Fridays , Ira Arlook , says Fonda was the only one who spent the night in jail , her first as part of the ongoing demonstration . <p> Arlook says Fonda appeared in Superior Court about 1 p.m . Saturday and was released . <p> Fonda has said she plans to get arrested every Friday as she advocates for reducing the use of fossil fuels .
