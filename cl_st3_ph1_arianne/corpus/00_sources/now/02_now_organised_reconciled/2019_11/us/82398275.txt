Kenya 's interior minister says dozens of people have been killed in mudslides , after heavy rains unleashed overnight floods in western Kenya . ( AP Photo ) <p> NAIROBI , Kenya ( AP ) -- Flash flooding has hit the small but strategic East African nation of Djibouti , where the government and United Nations said the equivalent of two years ' rain fell in a single day . Several regional countries including Kenya are struggling after such downpours , with more to come . <p> Rainfall from October to mid-November has been up to 300% above average in the greater Horn of Africa region , according to the Famine Early Warning Systems Network . <p> On Thursday , a joint Djibouti-U.N. statement said up to a quarter-million people have been affected in recent days in the country on the Red Sea that 's home to military bases for the United States @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the end of the month that number could grow . <p> Djibouti has been called one of the world 's most vulnerable non-island nations in the face of climate change as sea levels rise . Neighboring Somalia has been hit hard by recent flooding as well . <p> In Kenya , East Africa 's economic hub , the government said 120 people have been killed in flooding and mudslides during an unusually severe rainy season . More than 60 died over the weekend in West Pokot county . <p> More than 18,000 people across Kenya are displaced , according to the Kenya Red Cross Society . Infrastructure has been damaged , making aid delivery more difficult . <p> " We have health issues , and is it wounds , is it children who are coming up with pneumonia , is it diarrheal illnesses , " said Dr. Taabu Simiu at the West Pokot County Referral Hospital . <p> Some survivors are struggling . <p> " Life here is terrible because we do n't have money , because if someone had their money in the house it was all swept away @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , said . The official spoke on condition of anonymity as he was not authorized to speak to the media . <p> Two villages with almost 5,000 residents in all remain cut off from the rest of the world several days after the flooding , said West Pokot county disaster management official Monicah Kalinyong'ar . <p> Gov .
