<p> LONDON , Nov 29 ( Reuters ) - Elizabeth I , one of England 's best-loved monarchs , has been revealed to be the translator behind an English version of an ancient text by Tacitus who described the high politics , treachery and debauchery of the Roman elite . <p> A 16th Century translation of the first book of Tacitus 's Annals - written in elegant italic hand on ruled paper - has been shown to be Elizabeth 's after an analysis of handwriting , her style of writing and the type of paper used . <p> " The manuscript translation of Tacitus Annales now preserved at Lambeth Palace Library is the work of Elizabeth I , " John-Mark Philo wrote in The Review of English Studies . <p> " Elizabeth goes to some lengths to retain the density of Tacitus 's prose and his celebrated brevity , " Philo wrote . " She follows the contours of the Latin syntax with remarkable commitment , even at the risk of obscuring the sense in English .
