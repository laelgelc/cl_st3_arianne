<h> Study looks at potential for massive flooding in Flagstaff <p> November 15 , 2019 GMT <p> FLAGSTAFF , Ariz . ( AP ) -- New modeling shows that a rush of water nearing the flow of the Colorado River could run off potential fire-scarred areas and flood parts of Flagstaff . <p> The chance of massive flooding in the Rio de Flag , a normally dry riverbed , is rare -- about 1% each year . But the modeling helps target areas of the surrounding forest that need to be thinned to lessen the risk of catastrophic wildfires , Coconino County officials said . <p> The county recently released a draft of the study that looks at the potential impact of a 100-year storm following a 14-square-mile ( 36-square-kilometer ) hypothetical wildfire on the southwestern side of the San Francisco Peaks , the Arizona Daily Sun reported . <p> Water would hit the closest neighborhoods and fan out through downtown Flagstaff before being contained in a natural channel , said county flood district director Lucinda Andreani . <p> Flooding would be worse if a wildfire stripped the land of vegetation , she said .
