<p> October 2019 saw the extent of Arctic sea ice -- the surface of the ocean that is covered by ice -- reach at its lowest on record . <p> According to climate scientist Zack Labe , Arctic sea ice extent was more than 2.6 million square kilometres lower than the average between 1981 and 2010 and the lowest on record for a month of October . <p> Sea ice extent typically falls during the first half of September before growing again in the second half of the month . September extent increased by over 130,000 square kilometres on average during the 1981-2010 period , but this year it actually decreased by 80,000 square kilometres , according to the US National Snow and Ice Data Centre ( NSDIC ) . <p> That 's because of unfavourable weather conditions and because water and surface temperatures in the Arctic Ocean have been higher than usual since late summer .
