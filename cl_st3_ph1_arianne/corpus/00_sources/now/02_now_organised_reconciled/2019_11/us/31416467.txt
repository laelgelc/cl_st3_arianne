<h> Michael D Higgins warns of ' negative forces ' ahead of elections <h> President calls on voters in the European elections to rediscover founding values of EU <p> President Michael D Higgins said the future of the EU needs a " vibrant political debate " as to the best path forward . <p> President Michael D Higgins has warned of " negative social forces " sowing uncertainty across the Continent , and called on voters in the European elections to seize the opportunity to rediscover the founding values of the European Union . <p> Voters will go to the polls to elect the Republic 's next MEPs on May 24th . Mr Higgins , in a statement issued to the media on Wednesday , said the EU was facing serious challenges due to Brexit and other matters . <p> " Our European Union today faces new , serious challenges and we must seize the opportunities that exist -- including the European elections -- to debate with energy , and with respect , the future shape of the European Union , " he said . <p> " In the shadow of Brexit and of some negative social forces which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ created across Europe . <p> " We Europeans are now invited in this election year to define , through deliberation and a values-driven debate , the forms of union and the outlines of the European Union that we seek . " <p> Mr Higgins said the future of the EU needs a " vibrant political debate " as to the best path forward . <p> " In particular , the voices of young people , and of all those who feel marginalised or excluded , must be heard , " he said . <p> " Europe can use its potential to address the major challenges of our world , including achieving sustainability , making a real and urgent commitment to addressing the issues of climate change and global poverty . <p> " Today , as they are deciding on how to vote , I call on all Europeans to engage actively in respectful debate in the pursuit of the great idea of a peaceful , integrated and inclusive Europe ; one that will seek to be an exemplary region in the world , for other regions to follow . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rediscover , deepen , re-define and build on the founding values of the European Union . Let us use our voices and our votes to reconnect the union to the hopes of millions of Europeans . " <p> The intervention is Mr Higgins ' second in as many months on the EU . During a speech at UCD at the end of March , he called for changes in the European institutions and said the " narrow version of an economic union " had left " European unity still reeling " . <p> Mr Higgins made frequent criticisms of the EU during his first term . He also criticised it for an economic model which had undermined the social cohesion in Europe and between member states .
