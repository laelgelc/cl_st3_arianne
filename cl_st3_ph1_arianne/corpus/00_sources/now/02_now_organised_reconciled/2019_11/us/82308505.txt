<h> Save the Whales <h> Report : Whales Are " Nature 's Solution to Climate Change " <p> If we want to halt or even reverse global warming , we need to find a way to get carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere -- and increasing the number of whales on Earth might be our best option . <p> For a new International Monetary Fund report titled " Nature 's Solution to Climate Change , " researchers did the math and found that a single whale absorbs an average of 33 tons of carbon dioxide over the course of its lifetime , trapping that carbon far away from Earth 's atmosphere when it dies and sinks to the bottom of the ocean . <p> " Nature has had millions of years to perfect her whale-based carbon sink technology , " the authors wrote . " All we need to do is let the whales live . " <p> But according to the authors of the IMF report , the carbon-sucking potential of whales far outweighs that of trees . They found that a tree can only absorb upwards of 48 pounds of CO2 per year , meaning even if @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it 'd still absorb just 2.4 tons of carbon compared to a whale 's 33 tons . <p> Whales do more than just trap carbon in their bodies . They also produce waste that phytoplankton need to grow -- and researchers estimate that those microscopic creatures capture 40 percent of all carbon dioxide produced . <p> For that reason , the authors of the IMF report believe whale preservation should be at the top of the global climate agenda . <p> " When it comes to saving the planet , " they conclude , " one whale is worth thousands of trees .
