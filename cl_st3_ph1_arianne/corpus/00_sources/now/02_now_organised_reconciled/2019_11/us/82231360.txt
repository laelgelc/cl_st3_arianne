<h> Sanders official predicts health care , climate change will be top issues in fifth Democratic debate <p> A national policy director for Sen. Bernie Sanders 's ( I-Vt. ) 2020 campaign predicted that health care and climate change will be among the top issues raised during Wednesday 's Democratic presidential primary debate . " Obviously , health care is going to be near or at the top of the list , " Josh Orton said Tuesday during an appearance on " Rising . " " I also think that one of the things we 're seeing more and more young people engage with -- and you saw this with some of the Sunrise Movement that he and Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez talk to in Iowa -- is global climate change and how are we actually going to address this existential crisis , " he added .
