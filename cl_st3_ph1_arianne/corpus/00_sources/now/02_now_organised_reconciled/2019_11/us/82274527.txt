He found the old tree was dying and needed to come down eventually . It had become a hazard , weakened by age and drought , and posed a risk that a strong wind would knock more branches down . The town trimmed back branches and did what it could to stabilize the tree . <p> But earlier this year it began leaning precariously toward La Fonda , said town of Taos manager Rick Bellis . Some people did take pieces . " <p> Some people used it for firewood .
