<p> BEIRUT , Nov 12 ( Reuters ) - A top U.N . official in Lebanon called on Tuesday for the urgent formation of a new government made up of people known for their competence , which he said would be in a better position to appeal for international support . <p> " The financial and economic situation is critical , and the government and other authorities can not wait any longer to start addressing it , " the U.N . Special Coordinator for Lebanon , Jan Kubis , said in a statement after meeting President Michel Aoun . <p> Kubis urged authorities to prioritize maintaining monetary and financial stability , including measures to give people confidence and protect their savings .
