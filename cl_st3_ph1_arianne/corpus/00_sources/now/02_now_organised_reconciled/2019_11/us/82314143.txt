<p> WASHINGTON , Nov 24 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Secretary of Defense Mark Esper asked for Navy Secretary Richard Spencer 's resignation after losing confidence in him over his handling of the case of a Navy SEAL accused of war crimes , the Defense Department said on Sunday . <p> " I am deeply troubled by this conduct shown by a senior DOD official . " Esper said in a statement . " Unfortunately , as a result I have determined that Secretary Spencer no longer has my confidence to continue in his position . I wish Richard well .
