<p> BERLIN , Nov 8 ( Reuters ) - Around 35 mine staff were trapped about 700 metres below ground , two of them injured , after an unexplained blast in a potash mine in eastern Germany , the Mitteldeutsche Zeitung news website reported , citing a spokesman with the state mining authority . <p> The local broadcaster MDR reported that a rescue operation had begun . <p> The staff were located in a safe sector of the mine in Teutschenthal , about 150 km ( 95 miles ) southwest of Berlin , and were being supplied with oxygen . <p> The mining authority said one of the injured was in a serious condition , the other only lightly injured , and that there was a possibility that they could be brought to the surface through a shaft that was intact . <p> The mine is no longer active but is being used to deposit mining waste from elsewhere , the website said .
