<p> KABUL , Nov 19 ( Reuters ) - Taliban insurgents released two Western hostages - Kevin King from the United States and Timothy Weeks from Australia - on Tuesday in a prisoner exchange deal with the Afghan government , two Afghan officials said . <p> " The two professors are safely freed and are being taken care of now , " one of the officials said , speaking on condition of anonymity . <p> The American and Australian were exchanged with three insurgent leaders , including a key militant figure , Anas Haqqani .
