<p> SANTIAGO , Nov 4 ( Reuters ) - A Magnitude 6.0 quake struck near the coastal city of Coquimbo in central Chile , the U.S. Geological Survey ( USGS ) said on Monday , causing building to shake in capital city Santiago but no damage . <p> The quake , reported earlier by the European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre ( EMSC ) as a magnitude of 6.0 , struck near the coast some 89 miles ( 143 km ) northeast of port city Valparaiso , at a depth of 30.5 miles ( 49 km ) , the USGS said . <p> Chile 's national emergency office said there were no preliminary reports of injuries to people or damage to property or infrastructure . <p> The quake , which struck on land , was not strong enough to produce a tsunami along Chile 's coast , the nation 's navy said . <p> Mining firm Antofagasta Plc , whose Pelambres copper mine is near the epicenter of the quake , said the mine was operating normally .
