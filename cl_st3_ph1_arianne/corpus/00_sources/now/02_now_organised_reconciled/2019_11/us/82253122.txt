Kitchen Theatre to stage award-winning ' Proof ' <p> The Kitchen Theatre Company continues its season with David Auburn 's " Proof , " the Pulitzer Prize- and Tony Award-winning play that explores what we inherit from our parents and what it takes to believe a woman when she does n't have the physical proof to back up her words . Following the death of her father , a famous mathematician , Catherine must grapple with what she may or may not have inherited from him -- from brilliance to madness . With the appearance of Catherine 's estranged sister , an unexpected suitor and a notebook containing a groundbreaking proof , Catherine 's life and the future of the field change forever . Directed by KTC producing artistic director M. Bevin O'Gara , the cast features Aila Peck as Catherine , Rom Barkhordar as Robert , Joseph Marrella as Hal and Turna Mete as Claire .
