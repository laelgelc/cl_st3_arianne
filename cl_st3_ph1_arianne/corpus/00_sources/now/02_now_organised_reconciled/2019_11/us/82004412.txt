<p> BRUSSELS , Nov 5 ( Reuters ) - The U.S. decision to begin its withdrawal from the Paris Agreement is a disappointment but work against climate change will go on and Washington may one day want to rejoin the deal , the European Commission said on Tuesday . <p> " We regret the U.S. notification to withdraw from the Paris Agreement , " a spokeswoman told a news briefing , referring to the global pact to fight climate change . " The Paris Agreement ... is here to stay , its door remains open and we hope the U.S. will join it again one day , " she said . <p> The Trump administration said on Monday it had filed paperwork to withdraw the United States from the Paris Agreement , the first formal step in a one-year process to exit .
