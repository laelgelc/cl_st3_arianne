<h> Transcript : Media Q&amp;A With Atlanta Fed 's Raphael Bostic in New York <p> Nov. 8 , 2019 3:40 pm ET WSJ Pro <p> Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta President Raphael Bostic sat down with reporters after a speech in New York on Thursday , Nov. 7 . He discussed why he would have dissented from last week 's rate cut if he had a vote on the Federal Open Market Committee and how his regional bank 's board of directors helped him reach that conclusion . He also laid out a case for keeping rates steady well into the future and talked about how his district is on the front lines of dealing with climate change .
