<h> France , Denmark call for more unified asylum rules in Europe <p> Denmark 's Prime Minister Mette Fredriksen , left , is welcomed by French President Emmanuel Macron for a lunch at the Elysee Palace in Paris , Monday , Nov. 18 , 2019 . Denmark 's Prime Minister Mette Fredriksen is in Paris for bilateral talks . ( AP Photo/Michel Euler ) <p> PARIS ( AP ) -- France 's President Emmanuel Macron and Denmark 's Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen have called for more unified European Union rules on granting asylum to better handle uncontrolled immigration . <p> In a joint statement in Paris Monday , the two pushed for a " reinforced protection " of European external borders . Macron said he hoped the EU will be able to make progress on the issue in coming months . <p> Frederiksen said the present asylum system is " inhumane . " <p> Europe has for years seen a major influx of migrants fleeing war and poverty . <p> Both leaders said they also have shared views on fighting climate change and protecting the European social model .
