<p> BEIJING , Nov 26 ( Reuters ) - China 's top diplomat Yang Jiechi said China strongly condemns the U.S. legislation supporting protesters in Hong Kong , in an interview with the official Xinhua . <p> " China resolutely opposes and strongly condemns the bill , and has expressed our severe position to the American side , " said Yang , according to the Xinhua report . He urged the United States to stop the bill from becoming law . <p> The U.S. House of Representatives sent the legislation to the White House last week after voting 417 to 1 for the " Hong Kong Human Rights and Democracy Act " , which the Senate had passed unanimously the day before .
