The decision to migrate in response to sea-level change sits along a continuum between forced and free migration4 . Models should therefore be able to evaluate changes in migration as sea-level migrants arrive in safer locations , congesting housing and labour markets , and diminishing the expected quality-of-life of other migrants who may then revise their location preferences .
