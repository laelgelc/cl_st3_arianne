<p> ( Changes slug , corrects reference in first paragraph to embassy worker , not diplomat ) <p> BEIRUT , Nov 1 ( Reuters ) - A Lebanese court sentenced Uber driver Tariq Houshieh to death on Friday for the murder of British embassy worker Rebecca Dykes in December 2017 , state news agency NNA reported . <p> Houshieh confessed to raping and strangling the 30-year-old Dykes , who worked at the embassy for Britain 's Department for International Development . <p> Lebanese judges routinely call for death sentences in cases of murder . But the country has an unofficial moratorium and has not carried out an execution since 2004 , according to the monitoring group Human Rights Watch .
