<p> WASHINGTON , Nov 13 ( Reuters ) - The United States needs to resolve issues involving Turkey 's purchase of Russian S-400 air defense system to achieve progress on other fronts , the White House said on Wednesday during a visit by Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan . <p> " In order to achieve progress on other fronts , it is vital that we resolve the issues involving Turkey 's purchase of the Russian S-400 air defense system , strengthening our defense partnership , " the White House said in a statement .
