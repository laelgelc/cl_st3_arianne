<p> Nov 15 ( Reuters ) - Alibaba Group Chairman Daniel Zhang said Hong Kong 's " future is bright " as the company presses on with its secondary listing in the city gripped by increasingly violent protests and recession . <p> " Over the last few years , there have been many encouraging reforms in Hong Kong 's capital market . During this time of ongoing change , we continue to believe that the future of Hong Kong remains bright , " Zhang wrote in the chairman 's letter included in the company 's supplementary prospectus . <p> Four thousand people have been arrested in Hong Kong since June and the territory 's economy has sunk into recession for the first time in a decade as the anti-government demonstrations disrupt business and deter tourists . <p> Alibaba is hoping to raise up to $13.4 billion in its Hong Kong listing and the shares are due to start trading on Nov. 26 . The retail price of the shares will be capped at HK$188 each . <p> The share sale is set to be Hong Kong 's largest in more than nine years , and comes as Beijing seeks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ maintain a sense of business-as-usual in the face of more than five months of unrest . <p> Alibaba had originally considered a Hong Kong IPO in 2013 , but ultimately chose New York after failing to gain approval from Hong Kong regulators for its unusual governance structure .
