<h> Housing vs. trees : SF backs 744 homes at UCSF Laurel Heights campus <p> S.F. supervisors approved a 744-unit housing and retail complex to replace this UCSF building at 3333 California St. <p> Photo : Paul Kuroda / Special to The Chronicle <p> Champions of housing beat out urban tree advocates as the San Francisco Board of Supervisors unanimously approved a plan to transform the UCSF Laurel Heights campus into a 744-unit housing and retail complex . <p> Despite protests from many neighbors -- who said the project at 3333 California St. is out of scale with the residential enclave and would destroy too many mature trees -- the board on Tuesday night backed the project . More than five years in the making , the proposal includes 186 affordable units for seniors , a child care facility , 5 acres of open space and 35,000 square feet of retail . <p> The project would be one of the largest built in that area of the city , which includes wealthy neighborhoods that have resisted both affordable and market-rate housing in recent decades . <p> Though opponents can still file a lawsuit over the project , the developer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a state law that would speed up any court challenge to 270 days .
