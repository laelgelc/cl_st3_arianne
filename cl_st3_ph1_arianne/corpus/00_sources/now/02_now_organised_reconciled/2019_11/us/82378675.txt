<p> European Commission President-elect von der Leyen addresses the European Parliament in Strasbourg Reuters <p> STRASBOURG ( Reuters ) - German conservative Ursula von der Leyen told the European Parliament on Wednesday that fighting climate change was an " existential " challenge and she would ensure money was provided to help adapt economies to lower fossil fuel consumption and greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Speaking ahead of a final vote in the chamber to confirm her as European Commission president from Dec. 1 , and also confirm the other Commission members , von der Leyen said : " We do n't have a moment to waste any more on fighting climate change . " <p> She said that transition towards " climate neutrality " by mid-century had to be achieved in a " sustainable way " , meaning it must keep on board workers , regions and governments that still rely on coal for jobs , energy needs and economic growth .
