<p> KIEV , Nov 15 ( Reuters ) - French President Emmanuel Macron assured Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy of support during a phone call ahead of a peace summit on eastern Ukraine taking place on Dec. 9 in Paris , Zelenskiy 's office said on Friday . <p> The two leaders were coordinating on preparation for the summit between Ukraine , Russia , France and Germany , the statement said , adding that Macron would also visit Ukraine . <p> " Emmanuel Macron emphasised that Ukraine could further count on France 's support in all matters , " the statement said .
