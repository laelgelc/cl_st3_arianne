<p> LUXEMBOURG , Nov 11 ( Reuters ) - It is too early to say the European Investment Bank ( EIB ) has won its battle to end funding of fossil fuel projects , the bank 's president said on Monday , after European Union finance ministers backed such a move on Friday . <p> Werner Hoyer said EU governments remained divided on the issue and a final decision was still to be made . <p> On Friday , EU finance ministers in an unprecedented move backed the EIB 's call to phase out its funding of gas , oil and coal projects to help combat climate change . <p> The EIB has sought approval to end from 2021 its financing of fossil fuel projects , which has amounted to about 2 billion euros ( $2.2 billion ) a year since 2013 - mostly spent on gas infrastructure . <p> At Friday 's meeting , discussions on climate finance were " very , very short , " Hoyer told a news conference in Luxembourg .
