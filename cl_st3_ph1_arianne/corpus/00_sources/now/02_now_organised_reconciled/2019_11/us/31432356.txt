<h> With more Santa Ana winds on the way , most of California is abnormally dry <p> Most of California is either abnormally dry or has slipped into moderate drought , according to U.S. Drought Monitor data from Nov. 12 that was released Thursday . <p> Meanwhile , moderate Santa Ana winds are on tap for Southern California this weekend , with relative humidity readings expected to be in the teens and single digits . <p> The portion of California considered to be abnormally dry expanded to 81.4% , covering much of the state from Shasta County southward , according to the new data . The abnormally dry area covered just 17.8% of California one week earlier . <p> A slice of land along the Colorado River and continuing northwestward along the Nevada border is in moderate drought .
