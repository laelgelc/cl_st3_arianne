The goal was to strengthen civil society , including local and national police forces , and increasing economic opportunities to decrease migration . Since Trump took office , the funding has gradually decreased , with the White House requesting $436 million in fiscal 2019 . At the end of March , Trump announced that he would be " ending " previously appropriated aid to the three countries because of another surge in migration from the region .
