<h> We mapped every wine country fire . They 're larger and more destructive than ever <p> Recent wildfires in California 's wine country are among the largest and most destructive in state history . October 's massive Kincade fire is the latest in a wave of fires that are dramatically reshaping life in the region . <p> To investigate the trend , The Times analyzed every wildfire since 1950 , when the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection began reliably tracking the size and spread of fires . In the last 20 years , more acres have burned in the region than in the entire previous half-century . <p> Why is this happening ? Scientists point to rising temperatures and the effects of Diablo winds on increasingly dry terrain . <p> " In a way , climate change is priming the landscape to ignitions , " said Max Moritz , a wildfire specialist at UC Santa Barbara . <p> Acres burned in wine country region per year <p> 1950-1999 : 1,011,750 acres 2000-2019 : 1,101,322 acres <p> 1950-1999 : 1,011,750 acres <p> 2000-2019 : 1,101,322 acres <p> Seven of wine country 's 10 biggest fires since 1950 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Fire in 2018 , made up of the Ranch and River fires , was the largest recorded fire in California history . <p> Biggest fires in wine country <p> After 2015 Before 2015 <h> New areas are burning <p> The fires are n't just bigger -- they 're also reaching territory that has n't burned in many years .
