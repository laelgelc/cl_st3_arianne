<p> Vermont Sen. Bernie Sanders appeared on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on Tuesday night ( November 26 ) to slow jam the news ! <p> The Democratic presidential candidate slow-jammed some of his campaign 's main points with host Jimmy , and ended by channeling hit-making singer Lizzo 's current hit " Good As Hell " by the end of it . <p> " We can no longer tolerate three people owning more wealth than the bottom half of America , " Bernie expressed at one point . " I have the experience to fight for that bottom half and to create an economy that works for all of us , not just those on top . " <p> Elsewhere on the show , Sanders threw free-shots with Fallon , reacted to getting and endorsement from Ariana Grande , opened up about growing up in Brooklyn , the Trump impeachment hearings and the dangerous realities of climate change .
