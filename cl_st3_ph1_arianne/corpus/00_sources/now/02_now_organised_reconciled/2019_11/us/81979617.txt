<h> UPDATE 4-Macron says Europe-China climate cooperation ' decisive ' <p> * Franco-Chinese partnership on climate now more necessary - French official ( Adds Chinese Foreign Ministry comment on U.S. move ) <p> By Marine Pennetier <p> SHANGHAI , Nov 5 ( Reuters ) - Cooperation between Europe and China on reducing climate-warming emissions will be " decisive " , French President Emmanuel Macron said on Tuesday , after the Trump administration filed paperwork to withdraw the United States from the Paris Agreement . <p> The U.S. move is the first formal step in a one-year process to exit the global pact to fight climate change , part of a broader strategy by President Donald Trump to reduce red tape on American industry . <p> But it comes at a time scientists and many world governments are urging rapid action to avoid the worst impacts of global warming . <p> China and France pledged at this year 's G20 summit to " update " their contributions against climate change beyond their current ones to reflect " their highest possible ambition " . <p> The 2015 Paris climate agreement encourages countries to make stronger pledges if they are able to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trade fair , just after a keynote address by Chinese President Xi Jinping , Macron said commitments will need to be enhanced . <p> " If we want to be in compliance with the Paris agreement , we will need next year to enhance our commitments to reduce emissions , and we must confirm new commitments for 2030 and 2050 , " he said . <p> " The cooperation between China and the European Union in this respect is decisive , " Macron added . " Next year , we need , in the agenda of enhancement , to be collectively up to the task .
