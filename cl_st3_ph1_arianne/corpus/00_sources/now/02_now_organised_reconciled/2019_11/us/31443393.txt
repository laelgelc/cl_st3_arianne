<h> Trump supporters and rival demonstrators clash near site of state Democratic convention in Long Beach <p> A small group of President Trump 's supporters and rival demonstrators clashed with one another Saturday in Long Beach , where the state Democratic Party is holding its annual convention . <p> Chanting " four more years , " supporters of Trump had taken to the streets with bullhorns and placards , said Arantxa Chavarria , a spokeswoman for the Long Beach Police Department , and a fight among three men ensued . Officers intervened and " separated the combatants . " <p> Speaking to witnesses , the Long Beach Post reported that two men approached the Trump supporters and started arguing . While it was unclear who started the fight , an anti-Trump protester hit a Trump supporter " in the face with a manual scooter . " <p> The three individuals will be charged with assault with a deadly weapon that is not a firearm , Chavarria said .
