<h> Berkeley Asks Residents to Reduce Waste on Thanksgiving <h> By Bay City News *Published at 12:54 pm on November 24 , 2019 <p> In advance of Thanksgiving , city of Berkeley officials are asking residents to reduce waste that goes to the landfill and adapt to a revised pickup schedule for some people . <p> The city said residents with garbage pickups on Thursdays or Fridays should plan to put their carts out a day later on Thanksgiving week . <p> Residents with usual Thursday collections should place their carts curbside by 7 a.m. on Friday , November 29 , and residents with regular Friday pickups should place their carts curbside by 7 a.m. on Saturday , November 30 . <h> Local <p> Berkeley officials also said garbage collections may occur later in the day than usual . <p> People who have missed pickups should report them on Monday , December 2 , by calling Customer Service at ( 510 ) 981-7270 . <p> Berkeley officials advise residents to reduce what the city sends to landfills by planning ahead to use what can be re-used and sort out their recyclables and compost . <p> The city said cutting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only helps residents and Berkeley be more sustainable , it boosts a national trend toward reducing waste during the holidays . <p> Berkeley officials said residents can prevent wasted food by making small shifts in how they plan , store and eat their food . <p> They say when people waste food they also waste all the energy , water , and resources that went into producing that food and getting it into their kitchens . <p> City officials also said Thanksgiving food scraps make great compost . <p> They said residents should place their food scraps and food-soiled paper in their green compost carts because food that 's sent to landfills generates greenhouse gasses such as methane that contribute to climate change .
