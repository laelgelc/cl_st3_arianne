<h> Earth System Alert <p> In response to growing public demand , policymakers and business leaders are increasingly uniting around shared commitments to reduce planet-warming greenhouse-gas emissions . But while phasing out fossil fuels is necessary , ensuring humanity 's long-term survival will also require far-reaching protections for the Earth 's natural systems . <p> POTSDAM -- More than 600 cities have announced measures to tackle climate change , and more than 680 of the world 's largest corporations have committed to cut greenhouse-gas emissions in line with the 2015 Paris climate agreement . These policymakers and business leaders are responding to growing public demands for climate action , which have been vividly exemplified by school strikes and other mass demonstrations over the past year . But global warming is just one of multiple urgent , interlocking crises that are now imperiling the very foundations of human wellbeing . <p> While few dispute the need to combat climate change , the Earth 's biodiversity , soil , air , oceans , and freshwater systems are also increasingly threatened . Together , these natural systems interact with the climate and determine the environmental conditions for the entire planet .
