<p> " We have so far confirmation of 18 deaths in our district , " Abdulah Ahmed , the police chief of the region -- which is located in southern Sindh province and stretches over 22,000 square kilometers ( 8,500 square miles ) -- told AFP . <p> He said that officers in each village had verified that each death was caused by lightning strike . <p> In contrast , lightning kills an average of three people each year in the UK , according to the Royal Society for the Prevention of Accidents . <p> The Thar desert , straddling the Indian border and one of Pakistan 's poorest areas , is dependent on monsoon rains . <p> But such a heavy rainfall in November is " unusual " , while " lightning of that intensity is unprecedented , " said Dr Syed Sarfraz , a senior meteorological officer in Karachi . <p> He said the causes were still being investigated but suggested hot air over the desert had met with a cold air mass entering from Iran , fuelling the storms .
