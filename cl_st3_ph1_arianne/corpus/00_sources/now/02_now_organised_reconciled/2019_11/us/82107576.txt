<h> Georgia university warns of layoffs after budget shortfall <p> CARROLLTON , Ga . ( AP ) -- A Georgia university is warning some professors that they may be laid off because of a budget shortfall . <p> The University of West Georgia says it sent non-renewal notices to an unspecified number of faculty members , telling them they may not have jobs next year . <p> The Times-Georgian of Carrollton reports the 13,000-student university told faculty members that it 's facing a $3.1 million shortfall , mainly because 500 fewer freshmen than expected enrolled this fall . Tuition is the largest share of West Georgia 's $212 million budget . <p> Faculty senators were told the $3.1 million cut would remain in place for five years . <p> Interim President Micheal Crafton says the university is trying to limit the impact of the shortfall . After protests last week , the university plans an open forum Monday to discuss the cuts . <p> The Associated Press is an American multinational nonprofit news agency headquartered in New York City .
