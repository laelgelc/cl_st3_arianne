<p> SOUTH BEND , Ind . - A group of protesters organized a sit-in at South Bend Mayor Pete Buttigieg 's office on Friday to emphasize the importance of a climate action plan . <p> The group , who are part of the Sunrise Movement , entered the Mayor 's downtown South Bend office single-file and placed letters on the front desk asking for the Buttigieg to improve his plan for climate action . <p> " We have a national platform that 's not available to everyone around the world , " said one protestor . " So I 'm here today to take a stand to pressure to Pete , to pressure our common council to pass a more aggressive , a more ambitious common council plan . " <p> The protesters say that they can help Buttigieg in implementing his plan , but they believe that the Mayor should treat it as an emergency . <p> The group is hoping that Buttigieg will change his plans so that there 's accountability when it comes to pollution . <p> Mayor Buttigieg 's Office released a statement on the sit-in , saying : <p> " Under Mayor Pete Buttigieg @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sustainability and action to address the climate change crisis . From constructing the first LEED-certified South Bend city government buildings , to implementing green infrastructure in neighborhoods throughout the area , to responding to historic flooding caused by climate change , the Mayor has led from the front on climate . On Monday , the Common Council will vote on a climate action plan that sets aggressive goals to reduce greenhouse gas emissions in South Bend . The plan will be a living document responsive to changing circumstances , but includes near-term benchmarks to drive early action by local stakeholders . "
