" <p> Buffelgrass is native to Africa and Asia , but it was introduced in Arizona in the 1930s for cattle forage and erosion control because of its high seed production and ability to tolerate drought . <p> The species reduces the biodiversity of the desert by competing with native plants for nutrients , water and sun , and provides continuous fuel for wildfires . <p> A team of researchers from the UA 's College of Social and Behavioral Sciences and the College of Agriculture and Life @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ laws and policy decisions , which are often missing from plans to eradicate buffelgrass . <p> Funded by a $1.58 million grant from the National Science Foundation 's CNH2 : Dynamics of Integrated Socio-Environmental Systems program , the team hopes to build a model that could contribute to changes in how communities work together to deal with the invasive species . <p> According to Aaron Lien , an assistant research scientist in the School of Natural Resources and the Environment , researchers already know how to get rid of buffelgrass .
