<h> KUA and 11 Other Florida Utilities Break Ground on One of the Largest Municipal Solar Projects in the Nation <p> Kissimmee Utility Authority today participated in a groundbreaking event for the Florida Municipal Solar Project , a large-scale solar energy project that will enable KUA to provide renewable energy to its customers , beginning next summer . <p> One of the largest municipally-backed solar projects in the United States , the Florida Municipal Solar Project is a joint effort between KUA , 11 other Florida municipal electric utilities , the Florida Municipal Power Agency ( FMPA ) and Florida Renewable Partners , LLC . <p> A total of 900,000 solar panels will be installed at two sites in Osceola County and at one site in Orange County -- enough solar panels to fill 900 football fields or stretch from Jacksonville Beach to Key West two and a half times . The total generating capability will be 223.5 megawatts of zero-emissions energy , which is enough to power 45,000 typical Florida homes . <p> KUA has agreed to purchase 30 megawatts of power from the project . <p> " Through this partnership , we are able to build a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and enables us to offer our customers affordable , emissions-free electricity , " said Brian Horton , KUA president and general manager . " We look forward to the completion of the first phase of this project and adding more renewable energy to our power generation mix . " <p> Construction on phase one of the project will continue through mid-2020 . When complete , the power output from the project will be equal to 37,250 average-size rooftop solar systems . <p> FMPA is serving as the project coordinator . The builder , owner and operator of the solar farms is Florida Renewable Partners . KUA and the other participating municipal electric utilities will purchase power from the project .
