<p> WARSAW , Poland ( AP ) -- Poland 's prime minister on Thursday was given the task of forming a new government after the ruling right-wing party won a second term in power . <p> President Andrzej Duda appointed Mateusz Morawiecki to return as prime minister and Morawiecki said his next four-year term will focus on greater economic growth as Poles strive to catch up with the West . <p> His new Cabinet is expected to include most of the same ministers linked to the ruling Law and Justice party but Morawiecki is paying special attention to the European Union and climate issues . <p> EU matters are being moved from the Foreign Ministry to Morawiecki 's office .
