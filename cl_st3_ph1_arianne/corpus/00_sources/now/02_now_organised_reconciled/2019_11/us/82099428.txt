Enter the Green New Deal , a proposal sponsored by U.S. Congress members Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez ( D-NY ) and Sen. Ed Markey ( D-MA ) earlier this year offering a slate of recommendations aiming to address both climate change and the nation 's economic inequality .
