<h> Diving Into The Science of Cave Diving <p> Cavediver Jill Heinerth is in studio talking about her new book and speaking engagement at the Greensboro Science Center . <p> Author : Nya Coleman <p> Published : 9:40 AM EDT November 2 , 2019 <p> Updated : 8:52 AM EST November 3 , 2019 <p> GREENSBORO , N.C. -- A sport once tagged as the domain of adrenaline junkies , cave divers have become an important asset to scientists exploring global climate change , archaeology , water issues , and unique biology . <p> Jill Heinerth is a cavediver , explorer , photographer , and film maker . Heinerth has been diving all over the world including in Egypt , Antarctica , Bermuda and The Bahamas . <p> Jill Heinerth <p> She will share her experiences and talk about her new book , Into the Planet : My Life as a Cavediver , at the Greensboro Science Center Sunday , November 3 . Light refreshments will be provided at 6:00 p.m . The event is free and begins at 6:30 p.m.
