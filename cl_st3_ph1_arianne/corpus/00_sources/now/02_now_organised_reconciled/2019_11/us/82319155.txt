<p> GOMA , Democratic Republic of Congo , Nov 24 ( Reuters ) - The bodies of at least 18 people killed in a plane crash on Sunday in eastern Democratic Republic of Congo have been pulled from the wreckage , rescue workers said . <p> The passenger jet crashed into a residential neighbourhood in the city of Goma while taking off from the nearby airport . The airline , local company Busy Bee , said there were 16 passengers and two crew members on board .
