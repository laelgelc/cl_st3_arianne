<h> Polar passage : Voyage offers close-up view of bears , habitat <p> On my final and favorite day in the desolate , frigid beauty of the Canadian Arctic , early this November , I watched a solitary polar bear lumber gracefully over the tundra and head out onto the ice as the afternoon sun lit up a frozen Hudson Bay . <p> The call of the polar bears had enticed us north to Churchill , " polar bear capital of the world , " a tiny town poised in northern Manitoba on the barren , western shore of Hudson Bay .
