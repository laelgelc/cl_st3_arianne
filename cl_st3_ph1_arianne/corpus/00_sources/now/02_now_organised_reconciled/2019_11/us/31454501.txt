<p> The youth wing of the Quebec Liberal Party is taking a hard line against the oil and gas industry , urging candidates for the party 's leadership to pledge they will end any and all investment in those sectors within two years of forming a government . <p> In a communiqu ? issued Friday , on the eve of a meeting of the party 's general council in Sherbrooke , the youth wing contends that investing in fossil fuel energy is to place public funds at risk and that the industry is not viable in the long term . <p> The youth wing wants Quebec to continue to lead the way in dealing with climate change by turning talk into action . <p> The wing holds 33 per cent of the votes in the election of the next leader . <p> Dominique Anglade , Liberal MNA for Saint-Henri -- Sainte-Anne in Montreal , was the only declared candidate for the leadership as of Friday , but Drummondville Mayor Alexandre Cusson may well throw his hat into the ring when the council meets on Saturday . <p> The leadership selection process will begin when the council meets .
