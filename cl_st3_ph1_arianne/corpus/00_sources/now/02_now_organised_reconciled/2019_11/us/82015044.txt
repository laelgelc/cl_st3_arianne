<p> Swimme wondered : Where did Emily 's personality come from ? His imagination took him back in time . He considered traits that Emily inherited from wolf ancestors , reptiles , amphibians and fish . He even wondered about that moment when unicellular beings came together for the first time to form the first ever multicellular beings . <p> It was at that point that Swimme realized that his dog 's personality , unique and distinct , was constructed by this process of creation -- a series of interactions among organisms that stem all the way back to the birth of life .
