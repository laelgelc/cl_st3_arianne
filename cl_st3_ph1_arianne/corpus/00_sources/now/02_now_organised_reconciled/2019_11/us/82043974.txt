<h> Reader Opinion : Climate change <p> Written By : Pat Scott Nov 7th 2019 - 2am . <p> In the past 50 years , have you noticed changes in precipitation patterns ? For instance , more heavy rainstorms ? Land too soggy to plant in the spring ? Have you noticed migration of animal and insect species farther north ? Have you noticed warmer lake temperatures , on average ? Have you noticed later ice-in in the winter , and earlier ice-out ? Have you noticed fewer extremely cold nights in winter , and generally decreased snow cover ? Maybe you have also noticed an increase in ticks spreading disease . You might be aware of fewer birds and more difficulty fishing for brook trout , walleye and whitefish . Aspen and birch seem to be dying in large numbers , and wild rice is less plentiful . <p> The above are statistics that have been observed by the 1854 Treaty Authority and the Great Lakes Indian Fish &amp; Wildlife Commission ( GLIFWC ) , and reported at the Climate Summit on Saturday , Nov. 2 , at the Mille Lacs Indian Museum .
