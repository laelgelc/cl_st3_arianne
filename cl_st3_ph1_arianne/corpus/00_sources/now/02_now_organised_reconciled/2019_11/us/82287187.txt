<p> Kia 's new K3 midsize EV sedan is making its premiere at the 17th China Guangzhou International Automobile Exhibition . A model released exclusively for the Chinese market , the all-new K3 EV has been sold in the country as a gasoline and a plug-in hybrid model , and will be released as an EV model to coincide with the nation 's efforts to fight air pollution and adhere to global climate change targets . <p> Kia also used the show as a platform to unveil the new KX3 at the show . Specifically targeting China 's competitive SUV market , the new B-segment compact SUV , known as Seltos in other markets , goes on sale at the same time as its reveal at the motor show . <p> Aside from the all-new KX3 and K3 EV , other Kia models are also on display , highlighting the brand 's latest design and technology innovations . These include the Futuron all-wheel drive concept SUV coupe and the Soul Booster EV concept . <p> The Futuron concept SUV coupe publicly debuted earlier in November during the 2019 China International Import Expo ( CIIE ) in Shanghai .
