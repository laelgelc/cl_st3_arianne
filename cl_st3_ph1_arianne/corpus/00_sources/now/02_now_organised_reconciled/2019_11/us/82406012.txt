<p> LONDON , Nov 30 ( Reuters ) - British police named the man who stabbed two people to death in London on Friday in what the authorities called a terrorist attack as 28-year-old Usman Khan , who had been previously convicted of terrorism offences and released from prison last year . <p> " This individual was known to authorities , having been convicted in 2012 for terrorism offences , " said Britain 's top counter-terrorism police officer Neil Basu in a statement . <p> " He was released from prison in December 2018 on licence and clearly , a key line of enquiry now is to establish how he came to carry out this attack .
