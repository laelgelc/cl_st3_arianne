<p> In the American imagination , California carries two fundamental meanings : hope and disaster . From its spirited beginnings , the state has always signaled possibility , but more and more , as the planet 's temperatures rise , as earthquakes continue to rattle with unpredictable fury , and bigger and deadlier fires ravage the state , the thought of disaster chokes the mind . We are reminded of our own mortality . Blood-orange skies . Monstrous plumes of smoke . Scissoring Santa Ana winds . Seesawing temperatures . Erratic changes in weather . A new normal -- and not just for California , but for everyone . <p> In July , the writer Jon Mooallem reported on the historic 2018 wildfires that gripped the northern California township of Paradise last November . Within eight hours , an entire municipality vanished into ash . The fires were of such immense and unforgiving wrath that they would end up being the most catastrophic blazes in the state 's history .
