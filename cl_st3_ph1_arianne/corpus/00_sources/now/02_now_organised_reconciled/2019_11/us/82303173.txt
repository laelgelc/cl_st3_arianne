<p> TOKYO , Nov 23 ( Reuters ) - Pope Francis arrived on Saturday in Japan , the second leg of a week-long Asian trip whose main aim is to bring an anti-nuclear message to Nagasaki and Hiroshima , the world 's only cities to suffer atomic bombing in wartime . <p> Francis , a determined anti-nuclear campaigner who will read a message on nuclear weapons in Nagasaki , will also meet survivors of the March 11 , 2011 nuclear meltdown at Fukushima , the world 's worst nuclear disaster since Chernobyl in 1986 . <p> The pope touched down in Tokyo after flying from Thailand for a four-day visit that will be the first in 38 years and only the second in history . <p> Another goal of his visit is to encourage the Catholic community in Japan , where just about 1 percent of the population identifies as Christian , about half of them Catholic . <p> He will say two Masses , one in Nagasaki and one in Tokyo , as well as meeting Japanese officials and Emperor Naruhito .
