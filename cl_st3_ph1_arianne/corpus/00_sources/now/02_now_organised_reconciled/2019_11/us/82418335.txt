<h> NYK Granted Japan 's First Sustainability Linked Loan <p> Yesterday , MUFG Bank Ltd. granted NYK a syndicated sustainability-linked loan , Japan 's first advanced loan that allows for conditions such as the loan 's interest rate to be adjusted according to the borrower 's CSR performance . <p> This loan encourages sustainable economic activities and growth by linking loan pricing to company performance , which is determined by the score provided by CDP* annually . As long as high CDP ratings are maintained , the company 's CDP score will not cause the loan 's interest rate to change prior to the repayment deadline . <p> In accordance with the new medium-term management plan " Staying Ahead 2022 with Digitalization and Green " announced in 2018 , NYK integrated ESG principles into management strategies to drive the sustainable development of society and enrichment of the company 's corporate value . In fact , NYK achieved a place on the Climate Change A List in 2018 .
