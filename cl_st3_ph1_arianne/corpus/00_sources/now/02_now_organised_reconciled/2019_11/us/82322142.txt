<p> SYDNEY , Nov 25 ( Reuters ) - Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison said on Monday that allegations in a media report that China had tried to install an agent in a parliamentary seat in Canberra were " deeply disturbing and troubling " . <p> The Australian Security Intelligence Organisation , in a rare public statement , said it was already investigating the allegations reported by Channel 9 television . <p> " I find the allegations deeply disturbing and troubling , " Morrison said at a media conference , noting that ASIO 's investigation was ongoing .
