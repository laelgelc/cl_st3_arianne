<p> PARIS , Nov 29 ( Reuters ) - Vivendi 's Canal+ and Qatar-based beIN Sports have won the rights to broadcast Champions League matches in France between 2021 and 2024 for a record 375 million euros ( $413 million ) a year , French sports daily l'Equipe reported . <p> BeIN confirmed in a statement on Friday that it had won the rights . It did not say how much it paid but described the price as " reasoned and thought-through . " Canal+ declined to comment . <p> L'Equipe also reported that TF1 , France 's biggest TV broadcaster , obtained the right to show the final of the sport 's most-watched club competition . <p> The sum paid by Canal+ and BeIN is higher than the 315 million euros a year RMC Sport paid for the rights up to 2021 . RMC is part of SFR , the telecoms group owned by Altice Europe . <p> Before that , the Champions League rights had been held by Canal+ and BeIN.
