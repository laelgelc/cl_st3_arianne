<p> At this year 's Venice Biennale , Wadsworth Atheneum contemporary art curator Patricia Hickson was impressed with Kahlil Joseph 's two-channel video installation " BLKNWS . " She also was impressed by those watching the installation : They would n't get up to leave . <p> Visitors to Joseph 's exhibit at the Hartford art museum will understand . The temptation is strong to sit in the darkened gallery much longer than originally planned , wondering where Joseph will go next . The progressive collection of images about black American lives is magnetic and thought-provoking . <p> Advertisement <p> The images include viral videos , reproductions of newspaper stories , historical footage or stills and segments filmed specifically for BLKNWs .
