<h> We Are Running Out of Air <p> Officials have implored the people of New Delhi to stay inside , indefinitely . Five million children in India 's capital have been handed face masks . Everyone is to keep windows closed . Contrary to the most fundamental medical advice , the city 's chief minister urged residents this week to " avoid outdoor physical activities . " <p> News images seem cut from an apocalyptic outbreak film . One of India 's holiest rivers is covered in toxic foam that looks like white cotton candy . Midday visibility is like a foggy dusk . The air reportedly causes people 's eyes to burn . <p> At the root is not some panic-inducing virus , though . The cause is simply pollution from agriculture and transportation .
