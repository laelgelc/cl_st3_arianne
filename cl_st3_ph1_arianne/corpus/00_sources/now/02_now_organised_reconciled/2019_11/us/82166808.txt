REUTERS/Christian Mang <p> The package , agreed after months of haggling between the Chancellor Angela Merkel 's conservatives and her Social Democrat coalition partners , has already been criticized as inadequate in view of the urgent challenges posed by climate change . <p> Environment minister Svenja Schulze said the measures , which compel ministries to take additional steps to cut emissions if Germany falls behind on its climate goals , plugged a major gap in Germany 's legislative framework . <p> Germany , Europe 's largest economy , means to cut greenhouse gas emissions to 55% of its 1990 level by 2030 . Merkel in September acknowledged her coalition 's credibility @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2020 emissions cut target . <p> " If too much carbon dioxide is emitted we have to take immediate measures to reach our 2030 goals , " she told reporters after the vote . <p> The package also includes a domestic carbon pricing scheme , bigger incentives for buying electric cars , higher road tolls for trucks from 2023 , surcharges on domestic flights and plans to pump money into rail operator Deutsche Bahn . <p> An initial price of 10 euros ( $11.02 ) a tonne for carbon dioxide emissions from heating and transport , was attacked by the opposition Greens as far too low . <p> " You have failed in humanity 's task of protecting the climate , " the Greens ' floor leader Anton Hofreiter told the Bundestag 's legislators ahead of the vote enacting the law . <p> The Greens have vowed to use their strong position in the Bundesrat , the upper house of parliament , and in state governments to tighten aspects of the law that are in their purview .
