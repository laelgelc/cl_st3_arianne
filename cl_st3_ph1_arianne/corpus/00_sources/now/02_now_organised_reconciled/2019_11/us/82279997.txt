<p> ANKARA , Nov 22 ( Reuters ) - Germany 's ambassador to Turkey said the arrest of a Turkish lawyer hired by the German embassy is " incomprehensible " and he is pressing Ankara to clarify the charges , according to a statement seen by Reuters . <p> The lawyer was arrested in mid-September , a German official said on Wednesday . <p> " Our legal consultant has provided our Embassy with support that is ordinary from an international perspective and undoubtedly not forbidden . Such cooperation should be possible without any obstacles , " ambassador Martin Erdmann said in a statement . <p> " This arrest is incomprehensible for us . This is why we are making intense efforts for the charges being mentioned to be made clear , and we have brought this up repeatedly with Turkey , " Erdmann added . <p> Relations between the two countries have been strained over German criticism of President Tayyip Erdogan 's crackdown on suspected opponents after a failed coup in 2016 , and Turkey 's military offensive last month against a Kurdish Syrian militia . <p> The lawyer had been hired by the German Embassy to provide documents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Germany , German media reported . <p> " Legal consultants are important counterparts at our Embassy in Ankara , as they are at all our missions , in clearing up and better evaluating issues related to the regulations of the country we are in , " Erdmann said . <p> The number of Turks seeking asylum in Germany has risen since the 2016 failed coup that Erdogan says was planned by supporters of U.S.-based cleric Fethullah Gulen who were deeply embedded in Turkey 's institutions . <p> Between January and October this year , some 9,500 Turks applied for asylum in Germany , up 8% from the same period last year , official figures show , making Turks the third-largest group of asylum seekers after Syrians and Iraqis .
