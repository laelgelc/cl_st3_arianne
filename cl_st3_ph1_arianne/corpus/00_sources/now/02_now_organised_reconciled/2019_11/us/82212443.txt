<p> BEIJING , Nov 18 ( Reuters ) - China 's agriculture ministry said on Monday it will launch an investigation into the illegal production , sale and use of African swine fever vaccines in the country , citing online reports of their use by some farmers . <p> No country has yet approved a vaccine for the usually deadly hog disease , although several institutes around the world are trying to develop one . <p> The ministry has previously warned against the use of such products , after reports of both imported and homemade products being used in China .
