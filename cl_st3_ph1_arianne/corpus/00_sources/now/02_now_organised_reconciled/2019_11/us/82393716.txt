<p> Nov 28 ( Reuters ) - Vancouver voted late on Wednesday to ban the use of plastic straws and bags from April next year , making it the first major Canadian city to enact such a wide-reaching ban , according to the city . <p> The move , aimed at cutting the use of plastic bags and certain other single-use plastic products , is part of a trend in many countries aimed at reducing plastic pollution . <p> Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau in June announced plans to ban by early 2021 some single-use plastics such as straws , bags and cutlery . <p> Montreal enacted a ban on certain types of plastic bags in 2018 . <p> Vancouver said plastic bags and straws each make up 3% of shoreline litter on its beaches each year .
