<p> JERUSALEM/GAZA , Nov 13 ( Reuters ) - Palestinian militants fired rockets into Israel on Wednesday , the second day of a spike in cross-border violence , sending people rushing to shelters as sirens sounded , although there were no reports of casualties . <p> The worst fighting in months began on Tuesday , after Israel killed in Gaza a top commander of the Islamic Jihad , a Palestinian militant group backed by Iran , accusing him of masterminding recent attacks against Israel . <p> In response , Islamic Jihad gunmen fired about 200 rockets into Israel throughout the day . Further Israeli air strikes on the militant group 's targets in Gaza killed eight more Palestinians . <p> It appeared that Hamas , the main militant group that rules Gaza , was staying out of the fighting for now . A diplomatic source told Reuters the U.N . Middle East envoy was on his way to Cairo to begin mediation to end the violence .
