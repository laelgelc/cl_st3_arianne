It 's the onset of the industrial revolution . Because if we just take clothing , for example ... the idea 200 years ago that you would have a piece of clothing that you wear one to five times -- which is what happens with most fast fashion -- is absurd . I mean , somebody in your household would make that clothing . There would be a lot of labor that goes into it . There would be the necessity to purchase the fabric . It was a measure of wealth . There 's a reason why people gave things like linens and towels and other things as wedding gifts , because it was wealth .
