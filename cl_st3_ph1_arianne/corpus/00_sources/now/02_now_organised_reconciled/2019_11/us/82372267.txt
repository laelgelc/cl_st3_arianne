<p> **45;1141;TOOLONG ... to Content <h> In Florida and elsewhere , GOP pressured over climate change <p> TALLAHASSEE , Fla . ( AP ) -- While President Donald Trump and his administration reject the urgency of the threat , leading Republicans in Florida and other states find themselves under political pressure to address the immediate impacts of climate change . <p> These leaders are increasingly changing their message , and in some cases their policies , to acknowledge climate science and discuss mitigation , even as the Trump administration dismisses both . <p> Florida 's new Republican governor has appointed a science officer , established a climate change czar and pledged to spend billions of dollars to restore the Everglades and combat the pollutants that spawn blue-green algae and red tides . <p> In the Southeast , where Republicans in South Carolina and Georgia control the legislature or occupy the governor 's mansion , the GOP is acknowledging , even if begrudgingly , sea level rise and the threat from intensifying hurricanes.
