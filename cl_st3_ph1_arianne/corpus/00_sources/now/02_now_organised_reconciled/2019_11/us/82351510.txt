<p> GENEVA , Nov 26 ( Reuters ) - The head of the World Meteorological Organization ( WMO ) said on Tuesday that global temperatures could rise by 3-5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels this century if nothing is done to stop rising heat-trapping emissions . <p> " If we do n't do anything , the range will be 3-5 degrees Celsius by the end of this century , " said WMO Secretary-General Petteri Taalas , in response to a Reuters question about climate scenarios . <p> He was speaking at a news conference in Geneva where another U.N . agency presented a study showing that global emissions surged to a record level in 2018 .
