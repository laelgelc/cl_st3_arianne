Funny you should mention it , I have just the thing : <h> Mama Stamberg 's Cranberry Relish <p> The relish has a tangy taste @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . It 's also good on next-day turkey sandwiches , and with roast beef . <p> 2 cups whole raw cranberries , washed <p> 1 small onion <p> 3/4 cup sour cream <p> 1/2 cup sugar <p> 2 tbs horseradish from a jar ( red is a bit milder than white ) <p> Grind the raw berries and onion together . ( I use an old-fashioned meat grinder . I 'm sure there 's a setting on the food processor that will give you a chunky grind -- not a puree . ) <p> Add everything else and mix . <p> Put in a plastic container and freeze . <p> Several hours before serving , move it from the freezer to the refrigerator compartment to thaw . ( It should still have some icy slivers left . ) <p> The relish will be thick , creamy and shocking pink . ( OK , Pepto-Bismol pink. )
