<h> Governor Newsom endorses John Laird for senate <p> -- John Laird , a candidate for California State Senate representing District 17 , which includes Paso Robles , recently announced the endorsement of California Governor Gavin Newsom . <p> " John Laird is a very talented leader who has a strong track record of fighting for a better future for California . We need him in the State Senate , " said Governor Newsom . " From tackling climate change to working on housing affordability , John has a deep understanding of the issues and he knows how to get things done . For me , it 's a no-brainer to endorse him for State Senate , representing the Central Coast .
