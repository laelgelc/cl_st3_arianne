<h> LETTER : Much more revenue needed for public transportation <h> Monday <p> Nov 25 , 2019 at 12:56 PM <p> To the editor : <p> Last month ? along with 86 other Cambridge residents ? I signed a petition to the governor , legislators and other officials regarding public transportation . We highlighted the crucial role public transit plays in addressing climate change , traffic congestion , social inequity and public health . <p> We commended the Baker-Polito administration for the improvements they have made to the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority . However , we also said that much more needed to be done . We pointed out that green , affordable , reliable and accessible transit is an essential public good . <p> We called for transformative changes in public transit , including the following . <p> First , for the MBTA , major investments must be made , including : a tunnel connecting North and South Stations ; linking the Red and Blue lines ; doubling service on the 20 most popular bus routes ; electrifying the whole commuter rail system and transforming it into a regional rail system as advocated by Transit Matters . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ electric by 2030 . <p> We also urged the legislature to immediately approve major new funding sources . <p> Examples are : the Transportation Climate Initiative , or ? cap and invest ? for Northeast U.S. states ; doubling the gas tax ; charging tolls on all interstates , etc . <p> Revenues from these sources should be dedicated to public transit and bike/pedestrian facilities . If these taxes/fees disproportionately affect low-income people and rural communities , there must be compensating benefits for these populations . <p> I urge all readers of the Cambridge Chronicle to contact their state representatives and senators , about the urgent need for new revenue sources to pay for desperately-needed improvements to our public transportation .
