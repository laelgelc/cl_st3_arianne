<h> Reader 's View : Senator 's sponsorship needed for ANWR <p> As a student of political science with a passion for the environment , I am very concerned about certain policies surrounding our great wild places in the United States . The Trump Administration 's 2017 tax bill created a severe threat of oil and gas drilling in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge ( ANWR ) in Alaska . The ANWR is over 19 million acres of habitat for species already threatened by climate change . The potential destruction of this habitat is very deserving of more public attention . <p> Thankfully , the Arctic Refuge Protection Act of 2019 could help the ANWR . The bill would designate around 1.5 million acres of the ANWR as part of the National Wilderness Preservation System . Those 1.5 million acres then would be protected by the highest existing level of government environmental protection . <p> Minnesota 's U.S. Sen. Amy Klobuchar is a cosponsor of the act , but our state 's other U.S. senator , Tina Smith , has yet to become a cosponsor . Please join me in calling on Sen.
