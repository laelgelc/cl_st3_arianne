<h> Mitch McConnell named Person of the Year by WholeFoods Magazine <p> WholeFoods Magazine has named Sen. Mitch McConnell its Person of the Year , and , as you might expect , Twitter has some thoughts . <p> In a post announcing the award last week , the magazine wrote , " From cannabis to climate change , Mitch McConnell 's name is linked to some of the most pressing topics of discussion in the industry this year . " <p> The magazine is not affiliated with the Austin , Texas-based chain of grocery stores , Whole Foods Market . <p> McConnell announced the achievement in a tweet Wednesday , saying , " I was recognized as the most influential person in the natural products industry , specifically because of my work to legalize industrial #hemp for farmers in Kentucky and around the country . " <p> In 2018 , McConnell was able to legalize the plant in the Farm Bill . Since that time , he has successfully gotten hemp insurance provisions through disaster aid legislation and even approved a resolution that created a Hemp History Week .
