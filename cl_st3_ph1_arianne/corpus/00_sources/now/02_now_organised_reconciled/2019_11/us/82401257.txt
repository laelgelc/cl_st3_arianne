<p> BEIRUT , Nov 28 ( Reuters ) - The Lebanese parliament 's budget and finance committee will approve the 2020 budget by the end of the year and the next government must adopt it , committee head Ibrahim Kanaan said on Thursday . <p> " Before the end of next month , the holiday season , ... we will have finished the debate and approval of the 2020 budget , " Kanaan said in a televised news conference . " It is not possible for the new government not to adopt this budget because when this budget is approved it will become a law .
