<h> City Commissioner Strand hosts climate action town hall Nov. 27 <p> Written By : Forum staff Nov 26th 2019 - 10am . <p> FARGO -- Fargo City Commissioner John Strand will host an interactive town hall climate action conversation from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m . Wednesday , Nov. 27 , in the Commission Chambers of Fargo City Hall , 225 4th St. N. <p> Commissioner Strand will offer opening remarks and serve as an informal moderator in the solicitation of opinions from audience members . The town hall will be an interactive listening session intended to gather concerns on climate change and ideas for local action . <p> As a public meeting being held in the Commission Chambers , the town hall conversation will be broadcast live on TV Fargo 56 and across the city 's social media channels .
