Democracy can disappear quickly under the autocratic rule that Barr envisions . <p> After World War I , the world 's functioning democracies soared from a handful to some 29 nations . Then , by 1942 , the number had dwindled to only 12 . It took a world war with more than 60 million deaths to restore democracy to nations of Europe and Asia .
