The clues of landscape evolution in mountainous terrain are often preserved in elevated low relief landscape of abandoned channel , which develops in response to drainage capture and divide migration , leading to in-situ development of low relief upland in a transient system9,10,11,12 . The upstream drainage area loss of abandoned channel would reduce the drainage discharge leading to its in-situ evolution as parched low relief landscape with decreased erosion potential of the channel leading to a net surface uplift viz. erosion E &lt;rock uplift U9,13,14 .
