<p> BEIJING , Nov 28 ( Reuters ) - China 's Foreign Ministry on Thursday said it will take " firm counter measures " if the United States continues to interfere in Hong Kong . <p> It said legislation signed by U.S. President Donald Trump on Wednesday backing protesters in Hong Kong was a serious interference in Chinese affairs and U.S. efforts were " doomed to fail " . <p> It warned that the United States will shoulder the consequences of China 's countermeasures if it continues to " act arbitrarily " in regards to Hong Kong . <p> The legislation signed by Trump was approved unanimously by the U.S. Senate and by all but one lawmaker in the House of Representatives last week .
