<h> How Experimental Musicians Are Soundtracking the End of the World <p> It does n't take a genius to figure out that plastics are destroying the planet , since a bunch of geniuses already did . Nevertheless , the experimental electronic duo Matmos wanted to remind us of this fact earlier this year when they released Plastic Anniversary , an album where every sound is sourced from plastic materials : a Plexiglass riot shield , a discarded breast implant , a broken vinyl record . It 's all very clever musically , as Matmos ' work always is , but also slightly superfluous as far as agitprop goes . Perhaps that gratuitousness accounted for the desperately madcap air of the duo 's recent performance at Krakow , Poland 's Unsound festival , where they thwacked away at objects molded from " dead dinosaur juice " in the wholly incongruous setting of a 19th-century opera house .
