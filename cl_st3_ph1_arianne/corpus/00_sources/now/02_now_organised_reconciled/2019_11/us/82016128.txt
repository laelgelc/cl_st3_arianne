<p> WASHINGTON , Nov 6 ( Reuters ) - The House committees leading the impeachment probe of Republican President Donald Trump released a transcript on Wednesday of the deposition of Ambassador William Taylor , the top U.S. diplomat in Ukraine . <p> Taylor 's testimony , delivered behind closed doors on Oct. 22 , was considered some of the most damaging to the Trump administration to date , as he outlined how it was leveraging nearly $400 million in U.S. aid to Ukraine in exchange for an investigation of the Republican president 's political rival .
