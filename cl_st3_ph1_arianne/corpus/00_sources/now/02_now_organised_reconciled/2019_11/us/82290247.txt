<p> The year is 2119 . New Orleans is 60 percent underwater . People regularly receive 3D organ transplants . Denver is the site of a violent protest movement -- and artists have reached new musical frontiers . <p> Evans recruited four notable local musicians ( Venus Cruz , CRL CRRL , Dameion Hines and Felix Fast4ward ) to ride shotgun as a fictional band from the future .
