<p> Just to the north of the runways of Montreal 's Trudeau International Airport , a gaggle of birders squeeze their way between towering cattails and leafy green reeds on an autumn Sunday to emerge upon an unexpected oasis , the Canadian equivalent of a waterhole in the African savanna . <p> Mallards and Canada geese by the dozen paddle through a vast pond in the Ipex Wetland as great egrets draped in frocks of glaring white spear ill-fated frogs at the shoreline .
