<p> Alongside perennial concerns like " work " and " money , " sociocultural issues including mass shootings , access to health care and the 2020 presidential election now cause the most stress for American citizens , according to the annual " Stress in America " survey from the American Psychological Association ( APA ) . <p> The survey was conducted between Aug. 1 and Sept. 3 and polled 3,617 adults across the U.S. , according to an APA press release .
