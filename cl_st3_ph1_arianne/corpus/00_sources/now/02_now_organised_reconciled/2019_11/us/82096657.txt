<p> NUR-SULTAN , Nov 12 ( Reuters ) - Kazakhstan 's former president , Nursultan Nazarbayev , is trying to arrange a one-on-one meeting between Russian President Vladimir Putin and his Ukrainian counterpart , Volodymyr Zelenskiy , Nazarbayev said on Tuesday . <p> Such a meeting , if it took place , would represent a breakthrough in two-way ties strained for years by armed conflict between the Kiev government and pro-Russian separatists in eastern Ukraine . <p> " Zelenskiy has already agreed to a tete-a-tete , and ( I have ) informed the president of Russia about that . We shall see now ( if Putin agrees ) , " Nazarbayev told a conference on international politics in Nur-Sultan , the Kazakh capital . <p> Nazarbayev , who said he was ready to host such a meeting in the Central Asian nation , spoke to Putin by telephone on Monday , his office has said .
