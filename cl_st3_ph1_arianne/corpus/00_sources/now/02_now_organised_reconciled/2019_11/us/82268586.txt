<h> SAVE selected for street tree planting grant <h> Thursday <p> Nov 21 , 2019 at 8:00 AM <p> Saugus Action Volunteers for the Environment ( SAVE ) President Ann Devlin is pleased and excited to announce that SAVE has been selected by The Foundation Trust to receive an award of $20,000 to plant at least 30 street trees in the Town of Saugus . <p> SAVE , whose mission for more than 45 years has been to provide a better quality of life in Saugus through environmental concern and action , applied for the grant because of various factors that have contributed to a steady decline in street trees . <p> " With these trees , SAVE is making a lasting difference in Saugus . People in town will be able to enjoy their shade and beauty for generations to come , " said Anthony Spinazzola , trustee of the Foundation Trust . " It means a great deal to the Foundation Trust to partner with SAVE on this important project . " <p> The trees , which are expected to be planted in April 2020 , will be a much-appreciated and much-needed addition to neighborhoods and to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the easiest and most effective ways to combat climate change , SAVE is most grateful for this generous gift from the Foundation Trust .
