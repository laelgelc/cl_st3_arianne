<p> WASHINGTON , Nov 1 ( Reuters ) - The United States is looking into the new leader of the Islamic State to determine his role in the organization and where he came from , Nathan Sales , the U.S. counter-terrorism coordinator , said on Friday , after a U.S. raid last month that killed its former leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi . <p> " Any time there is a leadership transition in the terrorist organization , we want to make sure that we have the latest information that we need to have to confront the threat , " he told a briefing .
