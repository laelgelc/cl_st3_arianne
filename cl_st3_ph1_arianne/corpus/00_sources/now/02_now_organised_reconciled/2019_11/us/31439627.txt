I think they need to make room in the club for me , because I used to sell a product that has become almost as reviled : digital political ads . <p> While political campaigns have used digital ads for more than a decade , the practice came under intense scrutiny after the 2016 election , after Facebook revealed that Russians had bought $100,000 worth of targeted ads that 10 million Americans had seen . In any form of advertising , marketers try to match their message to their target audience . To ban digital ads because they 're better at achieving that goal is to draw a distinction without a coherent line . If a newspaper promotes an op-ed by a columnist arguing that President Donald Trump is unfit for office , is that a political ad ?
