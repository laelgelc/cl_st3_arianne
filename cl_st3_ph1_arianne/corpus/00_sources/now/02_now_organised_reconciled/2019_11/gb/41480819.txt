<h> Venice flooding hits second highest level ever <h> Venice flooding hits second highest level ever <p> The mayor of Venice has blamed climate change as flooding of the historic lagoon city hit the second-highest level ever , as the city braced for another wave on Wednesday . <p> The high-water mark hit 74in late on Tuesday , meaning more than 85% of the city was flooded . The highest level recorded was 76in during infamous flooding in 1966 . <p> The governor of the Veneto region , Luca Zaia , said one person died , although the cause was not immediately clear . <p> A man in his 70s died on the barrier island of Pellestrina , apparently of electrocution , said Danny Carrella , an official on the island of 3,500 inhabitants . <p> Photos on social media showed a city ferry and taxi boats grounded on walkways flanking canals .
