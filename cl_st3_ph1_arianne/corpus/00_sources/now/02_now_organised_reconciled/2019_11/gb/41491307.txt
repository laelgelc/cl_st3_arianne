In the same election , the current Derbyshire Police and Crime Commissioner Hardyal Dhindsa was beaten into second place for Labour by Conservative Pauline Latham . <p> History : Mid Derbyshire existed as a constituency as far back as 1885 though to 1910 before it disappeared from the county political constituencies . During that time , the seat was held continually until 1909 by Nottingham lace manufacturer and Liberal James Alfred Jacoby . He lost in that year to Lib-Lab pact candidate John Hancock , sponsored by the Derbyshire Miners Association . Hancock was a Liberal but took the Labour party whip in the Commons and then won the seat for Labour in the following year . <p> The seat was not contested again because the First World War intervened before the next election was due to take place in 1915 and it ceased to exist until it was resurrected in 2010.
