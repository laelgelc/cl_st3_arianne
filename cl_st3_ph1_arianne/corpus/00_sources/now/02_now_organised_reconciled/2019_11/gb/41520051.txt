Historically , we 've focused on financial performance and corporate governance to mitigate risk and assess potential for long-term value creation . That is no longer enough . Today , we owe a fiduciary duty to the community at large . Today , social and environmental concerns are at least as important as financial performance and governance . And our twin fiduciary duties -- to our investors and to the larger community -- are best served not by maximising short-term profitability but by concentrating on long-term returns and risks . <p> At SBI Funds Management , we believe that companies that focus @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ profits -- deliver sustained returns over an extended period . Adopting this logic creates a virtuous cycle : as investors focus on ESG , companies respond by integrating such considerations into their business practices . <p> As investors , we need to answer the climate change challenge and pull up our values-based socks .
