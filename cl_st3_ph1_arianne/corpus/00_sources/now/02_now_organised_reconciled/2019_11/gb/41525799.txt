<h> Yorkshire power station Drax reveals plans to supply 80 per cent of biomass from its own sources <p> The Yorkshire-based power station Drax plans to supply more of its own biomass , to help the UK reach its targets for reducing carbon emissions . <p> In a trading update , Drax said it was targeting biomass self-supply capacity of five million tonnes by 2027 . The company said it was evaluating options for a further three million tonnes over the next seven years . <p> Drax said it is also targeting returns significantly in excess of the group 's cost of capital . Drax said it was trading in line with expectations and its acquired assets were performing strongly . <p> " We believe sustainable biomass has a long-term critical role to play . That 's why we plan to supply 80 per cent of our biomass from our own sources - a significant increase on the 20 percent we currently self-supply . Supplying more of our own biomass will cut costs and reduce supply chain risks , ensuring our biomass power generation remains viable in the long term .
