<h> Arcadis appoints Anusha Shah as Director of Resilient Cities <p> 21 November 2019Consultancy.uk <p> The UK wing of engineering consultancy Arcadis has strengthened its global cities programme with the appointment of Anusha Shah , who arrives at the firm as Director of Resilient Cities . The newly created role will see Shah focus on working with city leaders to better understand short and long-term resiliency planning challenges . <p> International professional services firm Arcadis specialises in design and consultancy services . The firm 's 27,000 people are active in over 70 countries , generating annual revenues of ? 3.2 billion . While globally , Arcadis reported a multi-million pound loss in its annual results earlier in the year , one region of the company to buck its downward trend was the UK . There , Arcadis enjoyed growth of 13% in 2018 , well ahead of the broader UK consulting market . <p> In a bid to maintain the positive momentum of its UK wing , Arcadis recently sought to emphasise the role of entrepreneurialism in its firm with a new appointment . Nilesh Parmar , who has been with Arcadis since 2004 , has joined the business @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Chief Entrepreneur . Now , the firm has continued its drive to innovate its British business model , with the appointment of its first Director of Resilient Cities . <p> Anusha Shah joins Arcadis for the newly created role , in which she will work with city leaders to better understand short and long-term resiliency planning challenges . She will also support organisations in meeting their net zero targets , and maintain sustainability as they respond to the pressures of mass urbanisation and climate change . A chartered engineer , Shah specialises in water and environmental engineering , and brings almost 20 years of experience in designing , managing and leading projects and programmes to the firm . <p> Speaking on her appointment , Shah said , " With mass urbanisation on the increase , creating resilient cities that can withstand , respond to and thrive in the face of some of the world 's most pressing issues around climate change and water scarcity is more important than ever . Earlier in her career , she was also a Director at Jacobs , and a Senior Consultant with TRIOSS . <p> Peter Hogg , UK Cities Director at Arcadis , remarked , " Anusha 's expertise in climate change and sustainability will bring a real depth and focus that supports our work with cities and city leaders around the UK . In creating places where people want to live and work , we need to be planning long-term , and creating resilient , future-proofed communities is a key part of the picture . Understanding the impacts of climate change and how we can respond to its challenges will be crucial to how we shape our cities , and Anusha will play an integral role in driving forward this agenda .
