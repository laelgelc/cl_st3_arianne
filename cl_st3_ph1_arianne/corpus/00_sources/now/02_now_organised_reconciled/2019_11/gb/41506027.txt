<h> GENERAL ELECTION 2019 : Tom Franklin , York Central Green candidate <h> GENERAL ELECTION 2019 : Tom Franklin , York Central Green candidate <p> The Press has offered prospective parliamentary candidates the chance to have their say ahead of the General Election . Here Tom Franklin , York Central Green candidate , sets his stall out <p> IN the four years , I have lived in York , I have seen the effects of austerity on schools and communities leading to the rise in homelessness , especially because of cuts to local government and the freezing of benefits while rents rise . <p> I also see the vulnerability of York to more frequent extreme floods because of climate change . <p> I have a strong campaigning record and can help to ensure that York gets services it needs including improved local rail and bus services , better funding for education in York Central and real action on climate change . <p> The Green Party and I are absolutely clear that the greatest issue we face is the climate emergency , and only by getting a strong green vote , whether or not we get many MPs , can @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ suffering the fate of areas like Fishlake village in recent weeks . <p> Action to cut burning of fossil fuels will help reduce the risk of climate change and mitigate the effects of it , it will also create jobs through a Green New Deal . <p> Amongst other things , this will improve the quality of housing reduce the need for heating , help strengthen local communities by ensuring services are delivered locally reducing the unnecessary travel and improve air quality by reducing traffic . <p> It will also provide better bus services and provision for cyclists and pedestrians which will reduce congestion on the roads . Waste is a huge problem and York has low rates for recycling . <p> We need to reduce waste at source by banning the use of non-recyclable plastics for packaging , and ensuring that it is easier to recycle . This will take work at both the national and local level . <p> Perhaps the next most urgent issue is Brexit . <p> I support remaining in the EU . <p> A majority of people of York voted Remain in the referendum @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ most to lose from leaving the EU . However , I also believe that we should have a " People 's Vote " to choose between leaving and remaining . <p> The original referendum did n't make clear what leaving might look like , and was severely affected by fraud . <p> We need a People 's Vote to know what people really want now . <p> The issue is too important for a three-year-old referendum to decide it . <p> The health service is grossly overstretched through ten years of cuts and focuses too much on curing once the illness has occurred rather than prevention . <p> We need to increase funding , especially in mental health which has seen massive cuts in York including the closure of Bootham Park and several outpatient facilities . <p> We need greater efforts in health promotion , and we need more staff providing care , including staff from overseas , from the EU and beyond . <p> Having worked in education I know that the current system with its focus on exams and measuring is preventing children from learning anything beyond what @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ levels of child mental illness . <p> Education needs to focus on learning instead of tests and to be for everything we do , not just work . <p> We have shown we are willing to work with other parties to achieve the best possible outcomes by our work on the City of York Council and in Parliament . <p> We do not believe that any single party has all the answers , and a mixture of MPs is better for the country . <p> By voting Green you could double the representation of the party in Westminster . <p> A vote for me is a vote for a greener , better York with happier healthier people .
