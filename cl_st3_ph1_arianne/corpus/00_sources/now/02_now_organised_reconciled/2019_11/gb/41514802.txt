<h> NFU President outlines her vision for future of British farming <h> NFU President comes to Dorset to outline her vision for future of British farming <p> Farmers flocked to Kingston Maurward College from all over the county to hear NFU President Minette Batters present her vision for the future of the agricultural sector . <p> The NFU President came to Dorset for the first of the union 's annual open meetings for 2019 . She spoke about the future of farming against the backdrop of the country 's political crisis involving Brexit and the upcoming general election . <p> Mrs Batters laid out three " seismic planks " for agricultural policy going forward : trade , labour and the contents of the Agriculture Bill . <p> Emphasising the need to maintain the highest of food production standards , she said : " We must make sure that , whatever the colour of a future Government , our production standards -- which are some of the highest in the world -- will not be sacrificed for trade deals .
