<h> Time for us all to get motoring on combatting climate change <p> The polling firm YouGov has been tracking views on the environment for nearly a decade . <p> For most of that time , the subject ranked pretty low in terms of voter priorities . <p> But this election marks a big change . Figures from July show that more than one in four voters ( 27 per cent ) rank the environment as one of their three top issues -- behind Brexit and health , and on a par with crime and the economy . <p> For the younger generation , the sense of urgency is even more profound . Some 45 per cent of 18 -- 24 year olds put it as their second biggest concern after Brexit . <p> So the first televised election debate tonight focusing on this defining issue is hugely welcome . <p> The challenge of reaching net-zero emissions by 2050 can not be underestimated .
