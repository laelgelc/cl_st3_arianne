<h> Are Extinction Rebellion activists criminals ? Abertay students to study ' green criminology ' as part of new course <p> Students at Abertay University will explore whether protests by climate change activists Extinction Rebellion ever break the law as part of a new criminology course . <p> The course , which will begin next year , will see third year students from the Division of Sociology take on a new ' Green Criminology ' module designed to analyse a wide range of environmental issues . <p> The State Environment and Crime course will study the climate emergency and the perceptions surrounding the actions of environmental activists -- and whether the actions from protesters ever stray into criminality . <p> A wide range of issues around climate change activism , as well as other ' green ' themes such as animal abuse and the use of fossil fuels , will be discussed as part of the topic . <p> In addition to on-campus study , students will be taken to relevant sites across the country to investigate the blurred societal perceptions
