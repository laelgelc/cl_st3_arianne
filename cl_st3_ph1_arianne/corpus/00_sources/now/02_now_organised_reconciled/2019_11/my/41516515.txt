<h> UK to take back 42 containers of plastic waste illegally shipped to Penang <p> The UK has agreed to repatriate 42 containers of plastic waste illegally shipped to the Penang Port over a year since March 2018 , in accordance with the Basel Convention . <p> The decision comes after UK 's Environment Agency visited the site in response to news of the illegal shipment of plastic waste from the UK , according to a joint statement by the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry ( Mestecc ) and the British High Commission . <p> " The UK Environment Agency held a series of meetings with Mestecc , Royal Malaysian Customs Department , relevant port authorities and agencies in Malaysia . <p> " These meetings have resulted in a greater mutual understanding of the regulatory framework and policies related to trade in plastic waste , as well as an exchange of knowledge in sharing intelligence , inspection procedures , identification and repatriation of plastic waste , " it said . <p> Mestecc Minister Yeo Bee Yin ( above ) praised the UK for its willingness to take back the illegal plastic waste . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ working closely with Mestecc in repatriating 42 plastic import containers in Penang Port is highly commendable . <p> " This co-operation signifies a recognition that plastic pollution is a global issue which requires commitment from various countries to address the problem . <p> " We hope the co-operation and understanding between Malaysia and the UK will set an example for other countries with companies exporting contaminated plastic waste to other developing nations , " she said in the statement . <p> Meanwhile , British High Commissioner to Malaysia Charles Hay said the UK looks forward to working with Malaysia on the broader agenda of environment conservation and climate change , particularly with the UK set to chair the 26th Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change next year . <p> " The UK government shares the same concerns as the Malaysian government on the issue of plastic waste . <p> " The repatriation of these 42 containers reflects our commitment to fighting the illegal plastic waste trade , " he said .
