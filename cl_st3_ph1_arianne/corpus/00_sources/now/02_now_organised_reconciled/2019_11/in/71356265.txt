It sought and was promptly granted permission by Pune Municipal Corporation ( PMC ) to do this . In a city fighting to cling to what is left of its green cover , the move has inevitably sparked an outrage and the green brigade has taken up the issue with both PMC and the district collector , urging that the decision be revoked . <p> The club , which is in the process of setting up a new facility adjacent to its administrative building near the outgate , has sought permission to fell the trees as they are inhibiting the construction .
