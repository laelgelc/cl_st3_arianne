<p> Himachal 's House of Horrors : Explosions Night and Day Scare Kids , Crack Walls , All for a Tunnel <p> The development catastrophe <p> While attending a winter school in Himachal Pradesh recently , I witnessed many developments in the infrastructure of roads , in particular , where a tunnel had been cut through the hillside to shorten the earlier route , and also broaden the passage to four lanes . <p> As a commuter I was told such development would aggrandise the economy of the state and enhance the inhabitants ' standard of living . <p> However , on a field survey to understand recent developments in the socioeconomic status of some villages in Kullu , I encountered ' under-wrapped realities and the other way around ' . <p> On our first field visit my fellow participant Umesh Yadav , a graduate student at the Jawaharlal Nehru University , interacted with a family on the uphill . <p> Irrigation crisis <p> Like every other family here , agriculture is their economic mainstay . A respondent from the family told him they grow all the major crops adaptable to the topography of the region . They used @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ livelihood . But the past year 's asymmetric agricultural growth , and its overall stagnation , had forced them to think beyond agriculture <p> Climate change has overwhelmingly affected crops here : there is reduced rainfall . Some crops had even failed because rainfall is the only source of irrigation in high altitude lands . <p> The whole agrarian economy is in crisis here . Some members of the family would go to Chandigarh or Delhi for seasonal employment . The state government is n't supportive of farmer 's grievances . A minor exception was a tank built in Jwalapur , a nearby village , from where drinking water is being circulated to families nearby . <p> Under this stark crisis , for irrigating their cultivation , families are either relying upon rainfall or trying to store water from nearby flowing streams . <p> Road-and-tunnel developers <p> Meanwhile , both the central and the majority of the state governments are in full swing to usher in a certain kind of development , even at the cost of the environmental destruction , and against the wishes of concerned stakeholders at the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The incumbent corporate-friendly state government plans to build eight new tunnels in Himachal Pradesh . <p> In our field survey we saw that the government has allowed a private construction company , AFCON , to build all eight tunnels , each 16 kilometres long , which will shorten the passage from Chandigarh to Manali by a mere 80 to 60 kilometres , cutting travel time by only 2 to 3 hours . <p> Unsurprisingly , the state government is heedless of the ecological degradation which has an overarching impact on the whole of Himalayan society . <p> Speaking with workers engaged in the construction projects , we found some problems in AFCON 's progress . The workers asked not to be named for fear of losing their jobs . <p> One worker figured to us that 1,200 people are employed in the project overall , of whom around 700 are local inhabitants and the rest hail from Nepal , Assam or Bihar . <p> We were told an unskilled worker gets 390 rupees per day , semi-skilled workers get 451 , and skilled workers get 551 rupees per day . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ per month , along with 7,000 more in overtime , averaging to 18,000 per month . <p> In order to sustain the livelihood of fellow family members , as most of them live in joint families , some local workers also do farming off season as they do n't get full-time employment . <p> We found that one worker had lost his life while working , and the company has provided 7 lakh rupees as compensation , besides 50,000 from the trade union amongst them . <p> How long will these 7.5 lakh rupees sustain the whole family ? All the other members were dependent upon that one male person , because of the strict patriarchal nature of the family structure here . <p> Working conditions <p> Fellow participant Nikhil Malik , a research scholar at Ambedkar University Delhi , found from a worker that as far as health precautions are concerned , the company claims to have an ambulance on standby , but that it does n't usually come on time . This could have serious repercussions on the health of any labourer in case of an accident . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ profit of the company owners , the latter should arrange clinical checkups for them on a regular basis . But the workers told us there 's nothing like that here , there is no sensitisation about workers ' health , there are always health hazards . <p> In fact , some workers had experienced problems with their eyes and lungs . But they could n't bother about themselves because they had to work for their families . <p> We were told that AFCON provides the short-term mechanism of jaggery to deal with health problems . Even that is being provided in very few instances . <p> Workers get four vacations per month : two Sundays and two personal leaves . But actually , workers under pressure to earn work on those days as well , because if they do n't go their overtime will not be counted . <p> Their constant worry does n't let them enjoy their leisure time for a single day . Even when they go to have their meal , they have to purchase the ingredients on their own . Asked about their grievances , residents explicitly articulated the horror right from the advent of this development catastrophe . <p> They told us that sometimes " the company " uses 50 tonnes of explosives in the hills , which have direct repercussions in the form of cracks in the houses of families nearby . Mirrors and interior tiles regularly break from the force of smaller explosions . <p> Villagers , young children in particular , were undergoing mental stress and anxiety from the repeated vibrations and horrifying sounds of explosions , which happen both day and night . <p> The groundwater level is also rapidly going down . Villagers alleged that the tunnel development was supposed to be done over a year , but the company ruthlessly completed it in just six months . <p> Along the way we were told that more than one lakh trees had been cut down in just the area surrounding the tunnel , let alone the passage and route . <p> A victim respondent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ affected by this project protested and filed a complaint against the company . <p> However , the corporate-government nexus came into play and people started threatening the villagers who had complained .
