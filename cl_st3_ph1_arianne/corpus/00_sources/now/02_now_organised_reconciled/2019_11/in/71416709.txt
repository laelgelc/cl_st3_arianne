<h> Mission Compassion <p> The film ' A Prayer for Compassion ' ( top , right ) draws on the idea of compassion , by exploring the beliefs of different religions from across the world <p> Watch a film that advocates compassion towards animals as a way out of the climate crisis <p> By Navjyoti Dalal <p> At the beginning of 2019 , Dr Sailesh Rao , an advocate of veganism , was compiling his ' vegan goals ' for the year . " I asked my granddaughter , Kimaya , what my goals should be , and she answered , ' Your goal should be to make the impossible possible ! ' , " says Rao , a resident of the United States , who is currently in the country to meet that goal . Rao is the founder of Climate Healers , a nonprofit organisation working to alleviate the climate crisis . For the past month , he has been delivering lectures across the country , in Chennai , Mumbai , Delhi , Bengaluru , and Surat , about veganism and how it could save the planet . On Friday , Rao will speak in Pune , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the American filmmaker Thomas Wade Jackson , and British actor Jerome Flynn ( of Game of Thrones fame ) . <p> The event will comprise a talk , a film screening and a panel discussion on the environment and veganism . The event will begin with Rao 's talk , ' Healing the planet , solving the climate crisis ' . It is one of many talks he will deliver in the city , at educational institutions , and at a conference on health and sustainability . <p> " My talks are centered on solutions to our environmental crises . I will be addressing the specificities of India within the environmental crises and how to handle the crisis today , " he shares . As one championing a compassionate way of life through veganism , Rao believes that India has the key to being the change . " I felt that the vegan movement will be catching fire in India , just in time to quench the climate change fires that are devastating the planet today , " he says . <p> The talk will be followed by the screening @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Jackson . It captures Jackson 's quest for compassion . The film 's underlining theme explores if compassion can grow to include all beings . The filmmaker seeks answers from religions , asking if people who identified themselves as religious or spiritual could include all human and non-human beings in their circle of respect , care and love . Drawing from the breadth of religions such as Christianity , Judaism , Islam , Hinduism , Buddhism , Jainism , the Native American tradition , Unitarian Universalism , Zoroastrianism ( and also the spiritual-not-religious point of view ) , Jackson 's film begins in America , goes to the UN Climate Conference in Morocco , and traverses through the Indian subcontinent in search of answers . <p> Rao worked as a systems engineer for 20 years , developing internet communication infrastructure . In 2005 , his perspective underwent a shift after watching a presentation by former US vice president Al Gore on TV . " It changed my life . I realised that I was wasting my time working on making the internet infrastructure 10 times faster than it was already @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Three years later , after studying the myriad environmental crises facing humanity , I went vegan and adopted the guiding principle of ' Compassion for all life is infinitely sustainable ' , " he says . Citing that the animal agriculture industry is one of the leading causes for gas emissions , Rao likens the vegan movement to the Khadi movement in the 21st century , only on a global scale . <p> A panel discussion featuring Rao , Jackson , and Flynn will be the concluding part of the evening . Flynn is one of the executive producers of A Prayer for Compassion . The actor has been raising awareness in various ways , about human abuse of animals . " He has , time and again , spoken up for the innocent animals , " says Rao . <p> The event in Pune has been organised by Climate Healers , and Direct Action Everywhere .
