In a study published in Nature -- the British multi-disciplinary scientific journal -- the Pune-based scientists have found a common trigger to the California fire , East Asian floods , Brazil flood and also the East African droughts . " We started with a quest to find if such weather events experienced across the globe have any common thread . The answer we found was the warming of Indo-Pacific Ocean , " said Roxy Koll , a climate scientist at IITM . <p> The study drew attention to a staggering two-fold expansion of the Indo-Pacific warm pool . The expansion of the ocean is attributed to the warmest ocean temperatures which have gone up to 28 degree Celsius . This has impacted global rainfall patterns which , in turn , set off the global weather events being witnessed .
