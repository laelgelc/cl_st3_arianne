<h> NYK Line Wins First Sustainability Loan in Japan <p> Maritime Activity Reports , Inc . <p> Shailaja A. LakshmiNovember 29 , 2019 <p> Pic : NYK Line <p> The Japanese shipping giant NYK Line has secured a syndicated sustainability-linked loan from MUFG Bank Ltd . This is Japan 's first advanced loan that allows for conditions such as the loan 's interest rate to be adjusted according to the borrower 's CSR performance . <p> This loan encourages sustainable economic activities and growth by linking loan pricing to company performance , which is determined by the score provided by CDP ( Carbon Disclosure Project ) annually . <p> As long as high CDP ratings are maintained , the company 's CDP score will not cause the loan 's interest rate to change prior to the repayment deadline . <p> In accordance with the new medium-term management plan " Staying Ahead 2022 with Digitalization and Green " announced in 2018 , NYK integrated ESG principles into management strategies to drive the sustainable development of society and enrichment of the company 's corporate value . <p> In fact , NYK achieved a place on the Climate Change A List in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ CDP yearly , and 139 companies are on its A List , including 20 Japanese companies and NYK . <p> The purpose of this loan is for NYK to promote its ESG management and motivate the company to maintain a high CDP score by responding to climate change through business activities . <p> This loan has obtained third-party opinions from Japan Credit Rating Agency Ltd . ( JCR ) for the loan 's rationality and compliance with the Sustainability Linked Loan Principles published by the Loan Market Association ( LMA ) . <p> Maritime Reporter and Engineering News ' first edition was published in New York City in 1883 and became our flagship publication in 1939 .
