<h> ' Untold suffering ' <p> The chorus against climate change seems to be getting louder than ever , but as schoolchildren , too , are now asking : Is anybody really listening ? Over 11,000 scientists from 153 countries joined their voices this week to call for greater and more urgent action to limit the deepening , and more accelerated , the threat of climate change humans face today . Pointing out that the first alarm on global warming was sounded 40 years ago , these scientists say that very little has been done to fully mitigate the impact the entire range of human activities has been having on the planet . They go on to suggest changes on key indicators that can pull the planet back from the brink .
