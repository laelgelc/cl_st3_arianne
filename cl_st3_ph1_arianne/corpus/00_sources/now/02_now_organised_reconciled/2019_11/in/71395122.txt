<p> Apex court orders MCZMA to look into the controversial society 's application to be regularised under amended CRZ rules of 2018 . <p> The Supreme Court has ordered the Maharashtra Coastal Zone Management Authority ( MCZMA ) to consider an application by the controversial Adarsh Housing Society in Colaba to be regularised under the amended Costal Regulatory Zone ( CRZ ) rules of 2018 . An order to this effect was passed by a division bench of Justice L Nageswara Rao and Hemant Gupta early last week . <p> The amended CRZ rules increase the maximum floor space index ( FSI ) from 1.33 to 3 for buildings in the island city , and from 1 to 2.7 for those in the suburbs , depending on the width of the road .
