<h> Cyclonic storm Maha to make landfall in Gujarat <p> A yagna underway in Ahmedabad to ward off detrimental impact of cyclone Maha <p> Ahmedabad is likely to receive moderate to heavy rainfall at isolated places on Nov 6 and 7 <p> Brace for heavy rains , Ahmedabad . Severe cyclonic storm ' Maha ' is likely to make a landfall along Gujarat coast between Dwarka and Diu , bringing in widespread rainfall on November 6 and 7 . According to the Met Department , Ahmedabad is also likely to see moderate to heavy rainfall on those two days . <p> As per the IMD forecast sent out at 5 pm , " The severe cyclonic storm ' Maha ' over eastcentral Arabian Sea moved further westwards with a speed of 9 kmph during past 6 hours , intensified into a very severe cyclonic storm and lay centered over eastcentral Arabian sea at 2.30 pm , about 590 km west-sotuhwest of Veraval ( Gujarat ) , 630 km westsouthwest of Diu and 590 km southwest of Porbandar . " <p> Jayanta Sarkar , Regional Director , IMD said , " Severe cyclonic storm Maha lay centered at 580 km southwest of Diu and 550 kms southwest of Veraval . From November 5 onwards , it will re-curve towards Gujarat and is likely to make a landfall between Diu and Dwarka during November 6 midnight and early morning of November 7 . During that time , heavy to very heavy and widespread rainfall is expected across Gujarat region and Saurashtra . The wind speed of Cyclone Maha during crossing is expected to reach 100 to 110 kmph gusting to 120 kmph . Sarkar also stated , " It is likely to enter Gujarat , however things may change .
