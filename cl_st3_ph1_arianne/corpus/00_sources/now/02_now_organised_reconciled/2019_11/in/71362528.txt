<p> Cyclonic storm Maha which is likely to cross Gujarat is weakening and will have a maximum sustained speed of 70-80 kmph , according to Met department forecast . However , precautions need to be taken in coastal areas , said IMD . <p> The press release of IMD mentioned , ' The Very Severe Cyclonic storm ' Maha ' over west central and adjoining east central Arabian Sea moved east-northeastwards in the past six hours and lay centered over west-central and adjoining east central Arabian Sea , about 640 km west-southwest of Porbandar , 690 km west-southwest of Veraval and 750 km west-southwest of Diu . <p> It is very likely to move east-northeastwards with rapid weakening . It is very likely to cross Gujarat coast around Diu as a cyclonic storm with a maximum sustained wind speed of 70-80 kmph gusting to 90 kmph around morning hours of November 7 . <p> Heavy to very heavy rains very likely at a few places in the districts of Gujarat region namely Ahmedabad , Anand , Surat , Bharuch ; in the districts of Saurashtra namely Junagadh , Gir Somnath , Amreli , Bhavnagar , Botad , Porbandar , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Mahesh Palawat , Vice President of Meteorological and Climate Change in Skymet weather said , " Cyclone Maha has re-curved and has become a severe cyclonic storm now . By the time it will reach Gujarat coast , between Diu and Porbandar , it is likely to turn into a depression or it may fizzle out . Ahmedabad may only see light rain .
