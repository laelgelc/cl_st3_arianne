Italian city hit by highest tide in over 50 years -- see pictures <h> Flooding in the canal city of Venice has reached the second-highest level ever , touching nearly the level of the infamous 1966 flooding . The mayor of Venice is blaming climate change for flooding . The high-water mark hit 187 centimeters ( 74 inches ) , meaning more than 85% of the city was flooded . The highest level ever recorded was 194 centimeters ( 76 inches ) during the infamous flood of 1966 .
