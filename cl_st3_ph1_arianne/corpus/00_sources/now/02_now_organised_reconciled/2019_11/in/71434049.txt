<h> Uttar Pradesh <h> Uttarakhand <h> West Bengal <h> NYOOOZ <h> My City My News <h> Around 1 24 MT of plastic waste used to construct a road in Narangi Military Station in Guwahati <p> Friday 29th November , 2019 <p> Guwahati : In line with the Government 's mission against single-use plastic and initiative to reduce , reuse and recycle plastic waste , around 1.24 MT of waste plastic has been used to construct a road in Narangi Military Station which is a pilot project of Military Engineer Services ( MES ) for road construction . Union Minister of Environment , Forest and Climate Change Prakash Javadekar recently told the Lok Sabha that around 40 percent of plastic waste collected in India remains uncollected and littered on the streets . A whopping 25,940 tonnes per day of plastic waste is generated in India . Therefore , to deal with the growing menace of plastic pollution , the Centre has made it mandatory for all road developers in the country to use plastic waste for road construction which is indeed a very good step towards a greener environment .
