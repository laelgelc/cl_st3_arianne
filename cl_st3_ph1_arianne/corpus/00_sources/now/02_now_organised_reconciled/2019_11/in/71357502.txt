<p> Berlin : Sea levels will continue to rise long after the carbon emission pledges made through the historic Paris climate agreement are met , and global temperatures stabilise , according to a study . <p> The Paris agreement on climate change mitigation , adopted in 2015 , calls for limiting global average temperatures to well below 2 degrees Celsius compared to pre-industrial levels , and to pursue efforts to keep the increase to 1.5 degrees Celsius . The agreement took effect in 2016 . <p> A team led by scientists from the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research in Germany found that emissions released during the initial 15-year period of the agreement would cause sea levels to rise by about 20 centimeters by the year 2300 . <p> They noted that the estimates did not take into account the potential impact of an already irreversible melting of parts of the Antarctic ice sheet .
