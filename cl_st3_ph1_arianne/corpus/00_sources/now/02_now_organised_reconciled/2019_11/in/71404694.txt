<h> Parliament debates Delhi air , but what about earlier panel reports ? <h> Last Updated : Thursday 21 November 2019 <p> Amid increasing air pollution in Delhi , Parliament on November 21 , 2019 debated on the poisonous air outside . A day earlier , the parliamentary standing committee on urban development met to discuss the role of various agencies to combat air pollution . <p> Rajya Sabha members raised an array of issues , from stubble burning to plantations , and made several suggestions . <p> National Congress Party 's ( NCP ) Vandana Chavan proposed that every city should have an urban forest to sequester carbon . Vir Singh of Bahujan Samaj Party suggested planting trees along highways . <p> The Supreme Court has already asked the government to explore options such as anti-smog towers to purify air in a 10 kilometre radius . <p> Important and simpler recommendations to tackle causes of critical air pollution , especially in the winter , have been ignored in the past . <p> A report by a parliamentary panel on science and technology and environment and forests ( tabled August 7 , 2018 ) on air pollution in Delhi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of forests as a main cause . <p> The absence of green belts around Delhi and surrounding areas let dust storms from neighbouring states enter Delhi , it said . The report recommended a massive , time-bound tree plantation exercise on the borders of Delhi with Haryana , Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh to absorb pollution-causing particulate matter ( PM ) . <p> Something like this can absorb ultra-fine PM2.5 from stubble burning in Haryana and Punjab that travel to Delhi via north westerly winds . No concrete step has been taken yet . <p> It also proposed that the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) , in consultation with state governments , plant trees along the western and eastern peripheral expressways . <p> Talking of stubble burning , it had noted that weak enforcement of the statutory ban by Punjab , Haryana and UP governments , coupled with lax sensitivity , adversely affected ongoing efforts . <p> The panel acknowledged that subsidy schemes to promote disposal of agricultural residue in a scientific manner did not help much ; it expressed surprise that no scientific study @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ascertain the level of damage caused by burning stubble . <p> Mentioning dust pollution in Delhi , the committee took note of the number of mechanised road-sweeping machines to curb road dust in Delhi-NCR .
