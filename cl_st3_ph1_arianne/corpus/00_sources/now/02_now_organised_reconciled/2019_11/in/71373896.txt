I 'm not saying it 's the Booker , but to my mind it is one of the most competitive , keenly contested prizes in the subcontinent since it is open to writers of all genres and nationalities published within India . <p> For the first time in the history of literary prizes in India a posthumously published novel has been nominated for a prize . <h> Numair was first introduced to me by his childhood friend , fellow writer , and , in his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ November 2017 . <p> From our very first conversation it was clear that he was deeply suspicious of editors and agents .
