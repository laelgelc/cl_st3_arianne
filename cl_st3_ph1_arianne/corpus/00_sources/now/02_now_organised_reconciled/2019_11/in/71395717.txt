<h> Making air pollution an issue of governance <p> The first week of November 2019 saw the worst smog and pollution levels in Delhi in three years ; flights were turned away and schools were kept closed . These recorded levels of pollution , however , fit a pattern and are not totally unexpected . While the Delhi government showed some signs of being forewarned -- announcing the rationing of vehicles on the road according to the odd and even scheme , and promising to distribute over five million masks -- there was very little done across the entire region by the governments concerned . Even the Delhi government began to distribute the promised masks only on the 1st of November , a week after the surge of pollution levels . This brief makes the case for making air pollution a political issue in India . <h> Introduction <p> The traditional Bijli-Sadak-Paani ( electricity-roads-water ) campaign issues have expanded in recent elections to include national security , sanitation , and the armed forces .
