<h> What Mumbai needs to do to safeguard itself as rising sea levels put the city at risk of being submerged by 2050 <p> Just as we begin to mull the effects of climate change , Mumbai is experiencing unusual November rain , thunder and lightning . This may be an anomaly , but anomalies are increasingly becoming the norm today , and it would be foolish to ignore them . <p> The Mumbai monsoon has broken several records this year , including a 65-year-old one for the highest seasonal rainfall . While temperature variations and heavy rainfall are short-term reflections on the full effects of climate change , the rising sea level is far from being so . While more than 50 per cent of the population affected will be the urban poor , those living in the city 's high-rises will undoubtedly face the severe brunt , too . <p> With most infrastructure likely to be submerged , and no power , mobility or safe sanitation to rely on , the deluge will bear serious implications on public health and survival .
