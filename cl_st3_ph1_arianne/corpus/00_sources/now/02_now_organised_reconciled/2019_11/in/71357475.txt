<h> Last Updated : Monday 09 September 2019 <p> The National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) on September 6 , 2019 heard the matter of remedial action against cheap waste paper and road sweep waste import for firing of brick kilns . <p> According to one Amit Jain , 900,000 tonnes of waste was imported , which is hazardous and is leading to air pollution . <p> The Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) report of June 28 , 2019 , acknowledged that there was no proper mechanism for plastic waste management , which was being dumped in open or burnt in brick kilns resulting in pollution . <p> The NGT said that mere issuance of directions can not be interpreted as adequate action and cited the orders passed by the tribunal in the past with respect to management of plastic waste .
