But Yeung is not deterred by the intensifying competition from Chinese peers . <p> " Consumers in mainland China have doubts about domestic food safety as there are too many food scandals , " he said . " For food , trust is the most important thing , and that can not simply be built by marketing . The brand image of OmniPork and Green Monday has been built because we have been doing it seven and a half years ago , when there was not any business opportunity but still we were willing to do it out of our beliefs . " <p> " As for OmniPork , we are a Hong Kong brand , the products are produced in Thailand , and developed by a team based in Canada , OmniPork is equivalent to an international brand , and the confidence on our brand will be different from that of other mainland peers , " he added . <p> Soybeans and peas , the main source of protein used in plant-based meats like Omnipork , are in plentiful supply worldwide . However , they , among other farm products , have been on the bargaining table amidst the escalating tensions between the United States and China . <p> Chinese importers have been recently purchasing soybeans from Brazil , the nation 's largest soybean supplier , despite the White House announcement that China had agreed to buy up to US$50 billion of US farm products annually during trade talks this month . <p> Yeung told EJ Insight that the Sino-US trade war has an impact on his business . " We are importing the Beyond Meat products , made in the United States , into the mainland China market , and that incurs tariff . " <p> However , OmniPork imports into China do not incur tariffs because they are produced in Thailand . <p> OmniPork 's raw materials , including peas and soybeans , are from North America , according to Yeung . <p> " Earlier , China imposed tariffs on the US soybean imports , which resulted in a large oversupply of soybeans in the US . That may benefit us as prices of our raw materials could fall , " he said . <p> That said , Yueng admitted that given the nature of the trade war , with its quick-changing dynamics , there is no telling how long his firm will see the added benefits . <p> OmniPork , made by proteins coming from shiitake mushroom , peas , non-GMO soy , and rice , is designed to be an all-purpose plant-based pork analogue .
