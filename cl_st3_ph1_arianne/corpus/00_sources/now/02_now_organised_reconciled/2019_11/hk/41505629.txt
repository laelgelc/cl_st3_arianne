<h> Common issues of international concerns will be deliberated on Sagarmatha Sambaad <p> KATHMANDU : The ' Sagarmatha Sambaad ' ( Sagarmatha Dialogue ) to be held on April 3-4 next year in Kathmandu will lay emphasis on ' Climate Change , Mountains and Future of the Humanity ' in its first edition . Minister for Foreign Affairs Pradeep Kumar Gyawali said so during the inauguration of the Secretariat for Sagarmatha Sambaad here today . The Secretariat is located inside the Narayanhiti Museum and towards the Department of Passport under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs . Minister Gyawali , talking to media persons following the inauguration , said the forum will serve Nepal as a platform to shed light on the achievements and developments Nepal made in advancing the cause of the women and marginalized communities and Nepal 's role in promoting regional cooperation . Furthermore , Gyawali shared that the event , the flagship global forum , aims to generate discourse on matters of common concerns to Nepal as well as to the world such as climate change , ecology , terrorism , immigration , intellectual property , economy among others . The Minister , who is also coordinator of the organizing committee of the event , said that the deliberations would contribute in forging a common viewpoint on the issues , seeking ways to resolve them . Invitations for participation to the event have been extended to Heads of the State and government of 20 mountainous and tropical countries that were bearing the brunt of climate change , shared the Minister . Participants of the event would also include high-ranking political leaderships from various countries as well as the representatives from the member of public hardest hit by the climate change as well as concerned government officials , researchers , scholars among others .
