We are all too aware of the battles fought on a daily basis , especially on the African continent , to keep our people fed . <p> But there are other concerns as well . Apparently if your diet consists mainly of meat , you are more likely to be affected by lack of something to eat than if it does n't , and you may want to blame climate change for that .
