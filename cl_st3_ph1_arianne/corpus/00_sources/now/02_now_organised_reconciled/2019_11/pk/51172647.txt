<h> Govt to Construct Vertical Building in Cities <p> A three-day Asia Regional Conference on Climate Change was inaugurated by Prime Minister Imran Khan . It was revealed in the conference that the government wants to construct vertical buildings in cities . <p> While addressing the 7th Asian Regional Conservative Forum of International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) , PM Khan said that the protection of Pakistan 's environment is the top priority of the government . <p> Prime Minister Khan said , " Pakistan has twelve ecological zones from desert to Himalayan forests which are rare in the world , " however , the population growth and less interest in protection of the environment has negatively affected wildlife and forests . <p> Also , PM said that the protection of the environment will be made part of the syllabus in Pakistan . <p> PM Khan informed about yet another campaign under which vertical buildings will be built in cities . He said it is important for town planners to be conscious about the protection of the environment . <p> IUCN President Zhang Xinsheng appreciated the government for launching the 10 billion tree tsunami project . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to address the issue of climate change .
