<h> Islamabad artist reimagines Pakistani singers as military generals <p> Meme culture has become the go-to coping mechanism for millennials , who spend hours on their mobile phones looking for quality humorous content . <p> A young illustrator from Islamabad creates amusing mashup art , which is the highlight of his Instagram account @ digink . <p> His new series imagines Pakistani maestros , such as Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan , Abida Parveen , Noor Jehan and Arif Lohar stepping into the role of military generals . <p> The 21-year-old spoke to SAMAA Digital about his inspiration for working on the unique concept . <p> " It 's honestly just relatable , " he says . " We all saw these legends while we were growing up . " <p> " I think we get that feeling of nostalgia every time we think of these icons portrayed in a way that we have n't really seen . So , I think it just takes us back to that good time where everything seemed alright , " he added . <p> Speaking about his brainstorming process , he remarked that the " ideas just come , sometimes I 'll see @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but sometimes I need to sit down and focus on the idea to make . " I was studying at a college in Australia and needed to kill time , " he said . " That was the first time I heard about the software that I use now , " he added . <p> Although it may seem like a bizarre combination , most fans would agree that the art pieces appear more like contemporary masterpieces you would definitely want to frame at your home . <p> His unusual art pieces have attracted the attention of more than 25,000 followers on Instagram . <p> With 300 different masterpieces , the young artist finds it hard to pick his favourite , but tells us one that is closer to his heart than the rest . <p> " The one with the samosas behind Faisal mosque , " he said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ story behind it .
