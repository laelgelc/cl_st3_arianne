<h> Daraz set to plant 11,000 trees in Karachi to fight effects of climate change in Sindh <p> Daraz , the leading online marketplace in Pakistan , is spearheading a plantation drive in Karachi and aims to plant 11,000 trees across the metropolis to help counter the effects of climate change in Sindh . Joining hands with the United Nations Association of Pakistan ( UNAP ) and Shan Foods , the e-commerce platform has planted more than 70 trees in the past month and will continue its efforts towards improving environmental health in the upcoming monsoon season . <p> The plantation drive was launched prior to the mega Gyara Gyara sale which drove unprecedented traffic , presenting a unique opportunity for Daraz to engage sellers , partner brands and customers to create change and build awareness regarding the impacts of global warming in the country .
