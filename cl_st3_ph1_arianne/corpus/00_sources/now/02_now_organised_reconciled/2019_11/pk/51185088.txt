<h> ' Air pollution causes 50% of environmental degradation cost ' <h> Islamabad <p> Islamabad : Advisor to Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam has said that air pollution is a silent killer as the economic cost of environmental degradation over the last decade went up from 6 per cent to 9 per cent in which air pollution contributes 50 per cent of the total cost . <p> Mr Aslam was speaking here at a seminar on ' Combating air pollution for improved public health , agriculture and economy : Time to act now ' organised by Sustainable Development Policy Institute ( SDPI ) . <p> Mr Aslam said that the major contributing factor to worsening air quality and causing smog issue in the country , especially in Lahore , is transport sector followed by crop burning and waste burning and emissions from brick kilns and steel furnaces . He said that we also have cross boundary issue with India , especially related to crop burning , which requires regional approach for redressal .
