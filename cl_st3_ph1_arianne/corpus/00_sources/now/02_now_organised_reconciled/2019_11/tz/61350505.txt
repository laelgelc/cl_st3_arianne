<h> ADFNS 2019:Enhanced efforts to fight hunger and malnutrition in Africa <p> ADFNS 2019:Enhanced efforts to fight hunger and malnutrition in Africa <p> The commemoration of the 10th Africa Day for Food and Nutrition Security ( ADFNS ) concluded with a call for enhanced efforts to fight hunger and malnutrition in Africa , through " Fostering New Agricultural Technologies for Improved African Diets , " which is the theme for this year . <p> This year 's celebration was organized by the African Union Commission in collaboration with the Government of the Arab Republic of Egypt , United Nations food agencies and other implementing partners , to cultivate collective responsibility toward increasing sustainable access to safe , nutritious and healthy foods amongst Africa 's countries . <p> The ADFNS serves as a rallying point to intensify political and financial commitments at all levels in order to address current challenges of food and nutrition insecurity in Africa . <p> The main objective for commemorating this year 's ADFNS was to discuss ways and modalities to advance new agricultural technologies to tackle hunger and malnutrition .
