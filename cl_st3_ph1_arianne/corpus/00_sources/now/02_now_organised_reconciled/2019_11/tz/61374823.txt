<h> National Assembly endorses SADC protocol on environmental management <p> THE National Assembly yesterday endorsed the Southern African Development Community ( SADC ) protocol on environmental management for sustainable development to enhance protection and trans-boundary management of the environment . <p> Prof Makame Mbarawa <p> Water Minister Prof Makame Mbarawa said the protocol will contribute towards sustainable development and regional integration of SADC , ensure economic growth as well as poverty reduction . <p> " The main objective of the ratified protocol is to improve the welfare of citizens within SADC and ensure sustainability of natural resources and environment conservations " said Prof Mbarawa . <p> He insisted that concerted efforts are required to address the trans-boundary impacts and challenges of climate change , widespread desertification , drought and loss of biodiversity . <p> The minister asserted that ratification of the protocol was crucial to control declining water resources , air pollution , deforestation , waste disposal and the spread of invasive alien organisms . <p> The SADC protocol on environmental management for sustainable development was signed by member states on August 18 , 2014 in Gaborone , Botswana . <p> Tanzania 's decision to ratify the protocol increases @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ three inducing Tanzania , Eswatini , Namibia and South Africa . <p> In their opinion , industries , trade and environment committee said ratification of the protocol will enable Tanzania to collaborate with SADC member countries in fighting against the negative impacts of climate change . <p> According to the committee , Tanzania shares a number of its resources with various countries . The resources include the Indian Ocean , Lake Tanganyika , Lake Nyasa , Zambezi river , river Ruvuma , River Save/Sabi , River Congo , Limpopo and Orange . Others are the Kalahari Desert and part of Congo forest . <p> Presenting opposition opinions , Special Seats MP , Latifa Chande urged the government to put more emphasis on environment conservation activities as well as including the matter in the nation 's five-year development plan 2016/17 -- 2020/21 . <p> Chande suggested for the government to allocate at least 1 per cent of its GDP for science , technology and research activities which would help in the fight against climate change . <p> She added there should be a system to share information on climate change and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the impacts of environmental destruction . <p> Ileje MP , Janeth Mbene said : " Ratification of the protocol adds to government efforts in protecting the health of Tanzanians since emissions of greenhouse gases pose a number of health risks " . <p> The Parliament also ratified the Kigali Amendment on Phasing Down Hydrofluorocarbons ( HFCs ) of the Montreal Protocol on ozone depleting substances ) and the Protocol amending the Marrakesh Agreement establishing the World Trade Organisation ( WTO )
