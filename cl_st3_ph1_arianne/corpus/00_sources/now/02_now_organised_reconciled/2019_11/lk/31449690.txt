<h> Sampath Bank marks 25 years of commitment to Wildlife Conservation <p> November , 21 , 2019 <p> Adding yet another iconic milestone to its outstanding service to the nation , Sampath Bank recently marked 25 years of commitment towards the conservation of wildlife in Sri Lanka . <p> Sampath Bank has extended its support towards environmental sustainability by donating Rs. 5/- for each new issuance of a Sampath Debit Card every year since 1994 to the Wildlife and Nature Protection Society of Sri Lanka ( WNPS ) . Through Sampath Bank 's 25 years of commitment to the cause , every user of a Sampath Debit Card can take pride in making an active contribution towards the conservation of wildlife and nature in the country . <p> Expressing his views on this momentous occasion , Aruna Jayasekera -- Group Chief Human Resource Officer of Sampath Bank PLC noted , " We are elated to celebrate this historic moment signifying Sampath Bank 's 25 years of commitment towards supporting nature and wildlife conservation . In line with our core values to uphold social and environmental sustainability , Sampath Bank launched this initiative 25 years ago to champion environmental stewardship @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's flora and fauna . We wish to take this opportunity to thank all our stakeholders , and most importantly our customers for their continuous patronage and support to make this mission a success . " <p> Initiated as Ceylon Game Protection Society in 1894 , the society was later established under its current title ' Wildlife and Nature Protection Society of Sri Lanka ' in 1970 . The WNPS has immensely contributed towards the protection of fauna and flora and plays a key role as a policy advocate , environmental educator and conservator . The society has also notably contributed towards the enactment of wildlife protection legislation in Sri Lanka . WNPS further intervenes to prevent the destruction and harmful commercial exploitation of fauna and flora and provide assistance to conserve nature in all forms including landscape , water , soil , fauna and flora as well as marine habitats . <p> Sharing his thoughts on the commendable contribution made by Sampath Bank , Jehan CanagaRetna -- General Secretary of WNPS noted , " This is truly a significant moment where a leading bank in the country has continuously @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contributors like Sampath Bank have given us the strength to continue our mission in protecting nature and wildlife for the benefit of future generations .
