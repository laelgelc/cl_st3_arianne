<h> What expert says on FG 's refinery deal with Russia <p> An oil industry expert and senatorial aspirant in the last general election in Abia state , Mr Chinedu Onyeizu , has commended the Federal Government for signing a government backed partnership with Russia to revamp the nation 's refinery . <p> Onyeizu gave the commendation in an interview with News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Abuja on Sunday . <p> The Group Managing Director of the Nigerian National Petroleum Corporation , Mr Mele Kyari , at the Russia-African summit said the corporation had signed a Memorandum of Understanding for the Russian firms and the state company to rehabilitate Nigeria 's four refineries . <p> He also stated that gas infrastructure development was part of the discussion with the Russian investors . <p>
