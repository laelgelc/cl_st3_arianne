. <p> Recipients of the loans and grants from the Asian Bank are classified under groups A , B , and C , according to their per capita income levels and creditworthiness . <p> The bank said only members in the three groups have access to loans and grants . <p> " Some of them will not be affected because they are vulnerable countries such as small island developing states . <p> " The list of countries under each category will be updated before the effectivity of the diversification with the latest Gross National Income ( GNI ) per capita data , ' ' the ADB said . <p> The financing terms
