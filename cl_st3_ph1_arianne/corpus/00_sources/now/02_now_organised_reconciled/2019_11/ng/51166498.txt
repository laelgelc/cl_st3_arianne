<p> Minister of Humanitarian Affairs , Disaster Management and Social Development ( MHADMSD ) , Hajiya Sadiya Umar Farouq , has assured victims of recent floods in Fufore local government area of Adamawa state of federal government 's assistance . <p> In a statement made available to Blueprint in Abuja Friday , the minister gave the assurance on Thursday , October 31 , during a visit to the local government . <p> She was accompanied by the Director , Relief and Rehabilitation of the National Emergency Management Agency ( NEMA ) , Senator Aisha Dahiru Ahmed , <p> and staff of the Ministry . <p> According to the statement , on arrival , the minister paid a courtesy call on the Lamido of Adamawa , HRH Alhaji Barkido Aliyu Mustapha , where she commiserated with him and the people of the local government area . <p> The statement further stated that the minister then proceeded to Fufore town , where she called on the District Head- Hakimin Fufore , Alhaji Ahmed Gidado and went on to distribute relief materials to the flood victims .
