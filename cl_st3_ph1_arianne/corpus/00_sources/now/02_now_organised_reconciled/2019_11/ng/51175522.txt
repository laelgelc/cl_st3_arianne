<h> European Union holds Climate Sustainability Event to Advocate Renewable Energy in Kano State <p> After two successful and impactful events in Lagos and Abuja , the European Union took its message of green energy and sustainable development to the Centre of Commerce , Kano State , on September 25th . <p> The event , themed ' Renewable Energy for a Sustainable Future ' , took place at the Dangote Business School , Bayero University and was well attended by foreign dignitaries , academics , industry experts , political leaders and students . <p> Like the preceding editions in Lagos and Abuja , the Kano event sought to bring to the forefront of national discourse , key climate change issues while advocating renewable energy as a viable solution . <p> Ambassador Ketil Karlsen , the Head of the European Union Delegation to Nigeria and ECOWAS , in his speech , underlined the effects of climate change in Nigeria and the contribution of the European Union towards combating it .
