to protect against sea level rise or improving the quality of road surfaces to withstand hotter temperatures , building flood defences and raising the levels of dykes as well as behavioural shifts such as using less water , adopting renewal energy , good waste disposal practice and planting more trees . <p> Others are using scarce water resources more efficiently , developing drought --
