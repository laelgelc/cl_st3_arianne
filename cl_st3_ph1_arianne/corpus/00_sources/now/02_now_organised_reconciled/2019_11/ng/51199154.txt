<h> Herder/farmer clash : Buhari proposes tree planting as part of solutions <p> President Muhammadu Buhari has proposed tree planting as part of the solutions to the frequent clashes between herdsmen and farmers in the country , according to the Presidency . <p> In an opinion article published in the Scientific American , Buhari stated that drylands forced herders to migrate from the North/Sahel region to the South during the dry season in search of wetlands to graze cattle . <p> He argued that to restore the ecology of the North , tree planting would help and in the process , keep the herders in their locality , as against the seasonal migration . <p> The President wrote that Nigeria , which had already planted 1million trees as part of efforts to combat climate change , would plant 25 million more trees . <p> " Today , President Muhammadu Buhari writes in an opinion article in Scientific American , in which more than 150 Nobel laureates have been published , that the Nigerian government is taking action against the ecological breakdown that drives conflicts . <p> " He explains that planting trees is part of the solution , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ top of the 1 million hectares already reforested . <p> " The article comes ahead of the project launch later this month by the National Youth Climate Innovation Hub , the organisation mobilising youth in the tree planting exercise .
