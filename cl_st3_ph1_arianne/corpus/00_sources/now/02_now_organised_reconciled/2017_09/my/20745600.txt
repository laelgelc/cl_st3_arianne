<h> Coffee supply may soon drop due to farms ' low earnings - ICO chief <p> YAMOUSSOUKRO : Farmers earn so little from coffee that many are likely to abandon their trees , endangering future supplies at a time of surging demand , the head of the International Coffee Organisation ( ICO ) said on Monday . <p> Jose Sette , executive director of the intergovernmental body , said farmers ' low earnings in many countries were depressing supply even as demand grows around 2% annually on the back of rising consumption in emerging markets . <p> " If farmers are not well remunerated and encouraged to plant coffee , then at some point in the future we may have difficulties in getting the amount of coffee we need , because demand is growing steadily , " Sette told Reuters in an interview on the sidelines of ICO 's council meeting in Ivory Coast 's capital , Yamoussoukro . <p> " We do n't have the same confidence that we will have the supply to keep up , " Sette said , pointing out that coffee trees require a long @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it 's worth their patience .
