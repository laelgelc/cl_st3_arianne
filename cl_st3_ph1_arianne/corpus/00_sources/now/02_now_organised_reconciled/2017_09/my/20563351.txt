<p> Sahabat Alam Malaysia and the Consumers ' Association of Penang are alarmed by the massive flash floods that hit Penang both on the island and the mainland following intense downpours throughout the early morning of Friday , 15 September . <p> Apart from many areas in Penang under water , we also received news of landslides and mudslides in several areas such as in Tanjung Bungah and Paya Terubong , where hill cutting is going on for more high-rise projects . <p> Clearly , despite previous incidences of floods and landslides in Penang following intense rains , we do not seem to have learned any lessons . <p> Heavy tropical storms are to be expected in Penang and in the country . <p> But what is most disconcerting however is that the state government , instead of taking preventive measures such as in protecting hill lands and planting more trees to prevent soil erosion and surface water run-off , is approving more hill-cutting and removal of trees along roads for all kinds of development projects . <p> All the flood mitigation measures will prove ineffective and inadequate if we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which stem from increased water run-off from removal of trees and
