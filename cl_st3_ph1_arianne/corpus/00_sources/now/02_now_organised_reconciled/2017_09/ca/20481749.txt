<h> Fourth annual Science Literacy Week returning with 700 events <p> Science Literacy Week , a nationwide celebration of science-based literature , is set to take place September 18 -- 24 for its fourth consecutive year . Its founder , Jesse Hildebrand , is a U of T alumnus whose dual passion for science and literature inspired the event 's creation . <p> The event began as an attempt to get libraries to showcase their science book collections , said Hildebrand . When he graduated from U of T with a degree in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology , he noticed a certain lack of science books on display in libraries . He voiced his concern , encouraging libraries to make their collections more prominent . Three years later , in 2014 , he created Science Literacy Week . <p> " I just wanted to reach as many people as possible ... Science is increasingly in the news -- if you look at the eclipse , millions of people have looked up all across Canada and the United States -- everywhere !
