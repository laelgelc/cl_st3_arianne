Here is where the candidates stand on the issues . <h> Economic justice <p> With some differences on the specifics , there are a few common threads in the candidates ' economic policies . These include ending corporate bailouts , increasing the minimum wage in federally regulated industries to $15 per hour , making access to employment insurance easier and increasing taxes on the wealthy . <p> Niki Ashton 's leadership showcase speech10:21 <p> Additionally , the four candidates want more effort made to go after tax cheats and to close loopholes , would increase taxes on certain kinds of investments , bump up the corporate tax rate ( to 19 per cent for Caron , 19.5 per cent for Singh and 21 per cent for Ashton ) and tax the assets and inheritance of the wealthiest Canadians . <p> But @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's signature policy plank is his basic income proposal , which would supplement the income of Canadians living below the low-income cut-off . This would top-up their incomes to roughly $18,000 to $25,000 , depending on where the recipient lives . <p> Caron would also institute a seven-hour work day , without any drop in wages , to compensate for increasing automation in the workplace . <p> Angus wants to give workers the right of first refusal to purchase a closing business and restructure it as a worker co-operative . His platform includes incentives toward the creation of more co-operatives in Canada . He would also put in place measures to recognize the " new working class , " namely those in temporary , contract and freelance employment . For example , he would exempt them from paying the employer portion of CPP . <p> Ashton would create a national post office bank to increase banking access , particularly in rural communities , and reduce banking costs . <p> Jagmeet Singh 's leadership showcase speech14:32 <h> Combating climate change <p> Climate change is listed as a priority for all four @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , phasing out gas and diesel vehicles and replacing them with electric cars , eliminating subsidies to the fossil fuel industry , investing in the retrofitting of buildings to make them more energy efficient and opposition to pipelines like Trans Mountain , Energy East and Keystone XL -- and the National Energy Board approval process that greenlights these projects . <p> Caron 's platform includes some big investments in a green economy , including $32 billion for green renewable energy to help with the transition from oil ; $10 billion over 10 years for electric high-speed rail and $18 billion for public transit .
