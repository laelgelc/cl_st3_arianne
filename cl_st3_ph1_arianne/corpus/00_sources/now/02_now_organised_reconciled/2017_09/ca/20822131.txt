<h> Letter of the Day : Collective solution to climate change <p> When we discuss the effect of increasing carbon emissions , we are looking at the results of our collective consumption of fossil fuels . Putting a price on those carbon emissions has largely been accepted to be the easiest first step in changing our collective behaviour and to encourage innovation from industry . <p> In their Sept. 6 report for the Globe and Mail 's The Energy Mix , Shawn McCarthy and Jeffrey Lewis note a different response to our collective problem . Calgary-based Encana , along with several other fossil fuel companies want to lobby the federal and provincial governments for streamlined regulations and breaks on carbon pricing so they can still make a profit . <p> We may recall the 2016 Paris Accord , were the global community , including Canada , set GHG emission reduction targets . Globally , the fossil fuel industry has known that transition away from carbon based energy sources is happening now .
