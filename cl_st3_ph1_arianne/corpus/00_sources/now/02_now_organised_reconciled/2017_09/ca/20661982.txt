" <p> As of Wednesday afternoon the water was still too dirty to see if any fish had died , he said . <p> Article Continued Below <p> The Ministry of Environment and Climate Change was alerted to the spill and staff collected samples both upstream and downstream , said Gary Wheeler , a ministry spokesperson in a statement . <p> " The Ministry , the Region of Peel and the City of Brampton are working to assess any environmental impacts and identify the source of the substance by investigating upstream catch basins and surrounding industry . " <p> Meanwhile , the city has hired a contractor to clean up the spill , which has now stopped , using a vacuum truck , and the Region of Peel has deployed absorbent booms at the drainage pipe and downstream , he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was discovered spilling into Fletcher 's Creek in Brampton on Sept. 20 2017 . ( Lake Ontario Waterkeeper ) <p> While restoration work in other sections of Fletcher 's Creek has been ongoing for years , the work undertaken this summer is part of a Lake Ontario Waterkeeper project to save the Redside Dace , an endangered minnow that in Canada is found mostly in the Greater Toronto Area in streams flowing into Lake Ontario . <p> " The things that make it endangered are mostly caused by humans -- unsustainable development , runoff , spills like this , are what are wiping it out , " said Rebecca Harrison , the waterkeeper 's programs manager . <p> " All this really great restoration work that 's happening to extend the habitat can all be lost if we do n't pay attention to point source pollution , " she said . <p> The Redside Dace may be the first species to feel the effects of that pollution . <p> The minnow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , is an indicator species , the equivalent of a canary in the coal mine , she said . <p> " It represents the overall health of the body of water . " <p> Fletcher 's Creek , which is located near residential neighbourhoods and has recreational trails alongside it , is home to other fish and wildlife as well . <p> Forder said he 's seen signs of deer , coyotes , and on Tuesday he and Harrison both said they saw a mink . <p> Tuesday was also the first time Harrison saw a Redside Dace -- it jumped out of the water right in front of her , something they do to feed . <p> " To walk upstream today and see a jet-black stream from a spill that quite likely could kill that same fish that I saw yesterday , it 's heart-wrenching , " she said . <p> Delivered dailyThe Morning Headlines Newsletter <p> The Toronto Star and thestar.com , each property of Toronto Star Newspapers Limited , One Yonge Street , 4th Floor , Toronto , ON , M5E 1E6 .
