<p> Regarding Kent Peacock 's " Climate : politics or perception ? " in Saturday 's Herald ( Sept. 9 ) . In addition to your obvious views on the subject , your demeaning comments such as " The Great Leader in the White House " and " the U.S. senate in a rare moment of clarity " leave readers little doubt as to your personal political stripe and thus your belief in Mann-made climate change . Perhaps the title of your article should have been " Climate : politics is everything . " <h> One Response to " Climate all about politics " <p> Yes , imagine if climate science had not been politicized ... we would have developed science-informed policies that would have seen the closure of the last coal-fired plant in the world many years ago ; the development and installation of new energy technologies ; and a re-visioning of our idea of the good life for ourselves and our children and our grandchildren well into the future . <p> But , alas , instead we have the reality that Dr. Peacock expressed in his Opinion -- the manufacture of public opinion for partisan gains .
