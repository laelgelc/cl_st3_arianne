Out where I live , near Fergus , Ont. , we 've never had so many . Even now , I 'm still hearing that dreaded , high-pitched nyee . . . nyee when I venture outside on some evenings . So I slap and curse like a stevedore . Then I slap some more -- until finally , screaming with rage , I abandon my garden and flee back into the house for a consolatory glass of vino . <p> The trigger for this invasion has , of course , been our soggy summer -- perfect for mosquitoes to breed in . My house is surrounded by a thicket of cedar and spruce , where they love to lurk after hatching out .
