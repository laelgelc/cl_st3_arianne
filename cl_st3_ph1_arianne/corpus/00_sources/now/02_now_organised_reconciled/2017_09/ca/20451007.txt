Or are they just weather ? After all , droughts , floods , storms and fires have been coming and going for a very long time . Climate scientists such as Michael Mann ( in a recent op-ed in the Guardian ) have been careful to explain that one can not attribute any particular event ( such as a wildfire or a storm ) to climate change . This question rests on a deep misunderstanding of the nature of climate science , and indeed all science . Because the science of climate has policy implications , some of which are very obvious ( such as that humanity must abandon the use of fossil fuels as soon as possible ) , people make the mistake of thinking that climate science is a political position . They think that it is advanced mainly or entirely for rhetorical reasons .
