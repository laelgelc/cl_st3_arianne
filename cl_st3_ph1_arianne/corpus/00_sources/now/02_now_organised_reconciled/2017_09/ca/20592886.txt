<h> Exhibits explore relationship , spirituality of water with us <h> Poll <p> Artist Ed Pien has two exhibits currently running together in the University of Lethbridge main gallery exploring different realities and the relationship between water and the spiritual world . Herald photo by J.W. Schnarr @JWSchnarrHerald <p> Water , its relationship with us , and with spirituality is explored through several exhibits by renowned artist Ed Pien at the University of Lethbridge art gallery . <p> Pien 's exhibits kick off the " You Are Here " series of exhibitions , workshops , performances and presentations running from September to March 2018 . <p> " You Are Here " invites people to find new ways to think about the future of their environment . In the main gallery , " Liquid Being " explores the sentience and spirituality of water . <p> " Recently , I was inspired by Ecuadorial Amazon Indigenous people , who were able to fight with the government and foresters by saying they could n't cut certain forests down because they were sentient , " said Pien . <p> " Because they are treated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to win the case . <p> " I thought I could explore the sentience of water in this work . The water projects a shadow through the structure and evaporates over time . The shadows are part of a larger image projected on the floor of the gallery . <p> Pien uses 150 litres of water in the exhibit , which is about the amount an average adult would drink over the course of the show . <p> " The sentience of water is really hard to comprehend , " he said . <p> " It takes a leap of faith and imagination , in a way . It 's hard to make a connection between ourselves and water because we are so different . <p> " Water occupies time and space in a totally different way than we do .
