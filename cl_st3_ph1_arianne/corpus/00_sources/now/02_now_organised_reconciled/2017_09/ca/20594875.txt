" We 've talked about it for some time , but when we had that second big storm -- There 's an urgency now that we needed to step up . " <p> It will not be easy . It could mean building new wet lands , new rules for developers , changing infrastructure , and changing the way we farm . <p> " I think we 're talking about how things function as part of a system , " explains Wyma saying that it is all part of a puzzle to adapt to changing weather patterns . <p> " One-in-100 year storm events , which used to be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ happening with increasing frequency , " he says .
