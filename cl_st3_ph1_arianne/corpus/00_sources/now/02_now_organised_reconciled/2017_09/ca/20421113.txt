The transparency framework takes the form of national reporting through National Communications ( NCs ) from all Parties , BURs from non-Annex I ( developing country ) Parties , as well as Biennial Reports ( BRs ) and annual greenhouse gas ( GHG ) inventory submissions from Annex I ( developed country ) Parties . <p> Under the UNFCCC transparency framework , each Annex I Party must submit an annual GHG inventory covering emissions and removals of GHG emissions for all years from the base year or period to two years before the inventory due date .
