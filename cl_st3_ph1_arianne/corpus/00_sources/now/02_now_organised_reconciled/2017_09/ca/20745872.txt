<h> The 2017 SFI conference brings Forests . A Way of Life . <p> September 25 , 2017 <p> By Sustainable Forestry Initiative Inc . <p> Sept. 25 , 2017 - The Sustainable Forestry Initiative Inc . ( SFI ) is hosting its 2017 Annual Conference September 27-29 in Ottawa . A Way of Life . -- will highlight how we all depend on forests whether we are harvesting professionals , landowners , producers , customers , Indigenous peoples , or individuals using forests for recreation .
