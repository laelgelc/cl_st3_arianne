She was referring to the fact that both the B.C. New Democrats and the Green Party had campaigned on platforms focused on building the new energy economy and opposing new pipelines . Many who live in B.C. reject Notley 's specious logic and instead believe that one province can not trample First Nations ' rights and ruin the earth for the rest of the country . <p> A significant majority of Canadians know that climate change is real and is already happening . Polling shows that most want Canada to meet its commitments under the Paris climate agreement and start taking meaningful action against climate change . <p> Yet the Trudeau and Notley governments speak the lines given to them by big oil .
