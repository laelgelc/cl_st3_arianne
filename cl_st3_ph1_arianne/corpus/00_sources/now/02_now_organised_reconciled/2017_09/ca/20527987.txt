There are parts of the world with very even weather patterns and that can be very predictable , " said author of Wild Weather on the Prairies , Monica Zurowski last week . " But in the Prairies , you can go from the highest of highs to the lowest of lows , and I think those of us who live on the Prairies know there 's something unique about Prairie weather , and what we endure or enjoy , depending on the time of year .
