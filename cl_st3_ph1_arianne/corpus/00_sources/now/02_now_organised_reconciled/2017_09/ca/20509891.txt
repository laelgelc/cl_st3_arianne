<h> Letter of the Day : Climate change is ' idiotic ' obsession <p> Too bad that , despite the assault on biological diversity that the Nature Conservancy Council says we human beings are visiting upon the Earth ( Nature losing ground despite conservation , SP , Aug. 25 ) , Chicken Little has n't gone extinct but , rather , seems to be thriving ! Biologist Dan Kraus covers much ground that I agree with . But then he says " our natural species are also facing new threats such as ... climate change that unknown 20,000 days ago . " He did mean days , not years . Still , this is just so silly . Was Saskatchewan 's climate " different " in the 1960s ? No , of course not .
