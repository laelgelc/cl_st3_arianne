<h> Related Stories <p> Some of Canada 's leading climate change scientists are fighting to keep the country 's northernmost research station in operation . <p> The Polar Environment Atmospheric Research Laboratory ( PEARL ) in Eureka , Nunavut , tracks atmospheric data that no other research station can , given its High Arctic latitude , only 1,110 kilometres from the North Pole . <p> There , researchers study ozone depletion , and how the thawing Arctic is contributing to climate change , as well as other polar science . <p> The PEARL research station on Ellesmere Island has been operating continuously since 2005 .
