<h> UK Unions Call for Energy to be Returned to Public Ownership <h> Trade Unions for Energy Democracy <p> The annual congress of the UK Trades Union Congress ( TUC ) has passed a historic composite resolution on climate change that supports the energy sector being returned to public ownership and democratic control . <p> The resolution -- carried unanimously -- calls upon the 5.7-million-member national federation to work with the Labour Party to achieve this goal , as well as to : implement a mass program for energy conservation and efficiency ; lobby for the establishment of a " just transition " strategy for affected workers ; and , investigate the long-term risks to pension funds from investments in fossil fuels . <p> The Labour Party 's 2017 election manifesto , For the Many , Not the Few , pointed to the failures of electricity privatization , energy poverty , the need to honor the UK 's climate commitments , and to put the UK on course for 60% of its energy to be met by zero carbon or renewable sources by 2030 . <p> The Manifesto also committed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ renewable energy , affordability for consumers , and democratic control .
