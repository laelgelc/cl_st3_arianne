<h> Setting the stage for election <p> To take part in the celebration of the agricultural community in Ontario , several local , municipal and provincial politicians attended the opening ceremonies of the 2017 International Plowing Match in Walton . <p> Seated on stage amongst other notable dignitaries such as IPM Chair Jacquie Bishop , the first woman Chair in 100 years , the Minister of Tourism , and the Minister of Agriculture , the politicians were each given a chance to speak to the crowd . <p> Even though certain points made by each politician remained divided based on which party they belong to , there is one point that crossed all party lines and united them - that the agricultural industry is an important part of not only the Ontario economy but for Canada as well . <p> Despite the unifying aspect of how important farmers and the work they do every day , regardless of the weather , it appeared each politician was setting the stage for the upcoming provincial election . <p> Election day in Ontario is on June 7 , 2018 and each party leader @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ made sure to hammer home the underlying message of their platform . <p> NDP leader Andrea Horwath focused on the issue of hydro rates and supported the rise of minimum wage . <p> Acknowledging and reminding the crowd the land is Saugeen First Nations , she also spoke on how farming is the backbone of Ontario . Horwath stated the NDP believe it is time for a change in Ontario . <p> Mike Schreiner , leader of the Green Party , spoke on climate change and how it affects farming , the threat of trade negotiations and stressed that farmers will continue to be job and wealth creators . <p> Schreiner publicly made a commitment that they will work with any party to stand up and defend farmers and farmland in this province . <p> Leader of the PC Party , Patrick Brown , stressed that one can be a proud Progressive-Conservative and want to watch every dollar , while at the same time care about the environment . <p> Brown stated that there needs to be commitments from the government on risk management , hydro rates relief , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a strong agricultural sector and to protect the farming industry in Ontario . <p> Current Premier of Ontario , Kathleen Wynne spoke on conversations regarding NAFTA and how the Liberal Party believes that agriculture in Ontario is an important aspect to our economy . <p> Wynne stressed that the Liberal Party will stand up for the farmers of Ontario by standing up for supply management , and making sure the Federal Government understands that . <p> Premier Wynne added that the Liberal Party and the Provincial Government are working very hard , and will continue to work hard , for a fairer Ontario , where everyone has opportunity . <p> Perhaps it was not the right venue for the politicians to set the stage for their political platform for the upcoming provincial elections ; perhaps it was the perfect setting . <p> It is important to research what each party 's platform is in the lead up to the provincial election . <p> It is also important to listen to what the politicians are speaking about and are making promises on . <p> Regardless of what was deemed important by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ media scrums , it was clear that each leader was unified by wanting one thing - a better and more successful Ontario for all .
