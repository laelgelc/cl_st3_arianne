<p> Canada 's new chief science adviser is worried . Our country spends billions of dollars on research , and many people believe that what our scientists discover is just debatable opinion . <p> Now Mona Nemer , an Ottawa heart researcher , has the job of sorting out the best evidence on public science issues -- from climate to pandemics to astronomy -- and telling the prime minister and cabinet . <p> She says she 'll make her advice to the government public , as long as this does n't violate national security . <p> " As it stands now , the idea is to provide an annual report on everything that we have done , " she said in an interview Thursday . Where the government asks for particular advice -- for instance , it might ask how to prepare for a pandemic -- " if we have looked at it and we have advice then we will make it public before the year end . " <p> Science is a broad domain , and she sees herself consulting experts in each field that comes up .
