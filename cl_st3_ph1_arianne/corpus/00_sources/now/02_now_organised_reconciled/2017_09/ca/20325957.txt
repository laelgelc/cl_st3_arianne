We 're living in an age when we get constant updates on people we know thanks to Twitter , Facebook , and Instagram . Ironically , the digital world has made actual face-to-face contact seem unnecessary ; there 's little need to catch up over coffee when you 've got an Internet connection . And that makes music -- especially in a live setting -- more powerful than ever .
