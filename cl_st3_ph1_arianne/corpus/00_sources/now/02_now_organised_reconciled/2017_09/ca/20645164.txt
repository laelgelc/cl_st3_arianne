<p> Ontario MPP Jagmeet Singh has undeniably added some life to what had been a sleepy NDP leadership race . Speculation that the fashionable , image-conscious criminal defence lawyer from Brampton , Ont. might throw his hat in the ring swirled for months before he officially announced his leadership bid . But his profile has increased dramatically in recent weeks , after a video went viral of him being heckled by a woman in Brampton who accused him of having ties to Muslim extremists . <p> Singh , a turban-wearing Sikh , has also attracted controversy . Questions remain about his odds in Quebec , where debate swirls around the place of religious symbols in public life .
