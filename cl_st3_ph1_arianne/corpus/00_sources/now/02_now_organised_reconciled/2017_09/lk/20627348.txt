<h> Sri Lanka 's President attends UN Economic &amp; Social Council sessions <p> Sep 19 , 2017 ( LBO ) -- The Sessions of the UN Economic and Social Council ( ECOSOC ) was commenced on September 18 at the UN Headquarters in New York . <p> President Maithripala Sirisena also participated at this Sessions which is only participated by the Heads of States . <p> During the meeting it was discussed in detail regarding how the UN must work in the future and paid their special attention in further improving the efficiency of the UN , developing cooperation as well as reducing the administrative expenditure and to provide maximum benefits to the member states . <p> A large number of states leaders including US President Donald Trump participated in this event . <p> The General Debate of the 72nd Session of the United Nations General Assembly is scheduled to commence today , on the theme " Focusing on people : striving for peace and a decent life for all on a sustainable planet . <p>
