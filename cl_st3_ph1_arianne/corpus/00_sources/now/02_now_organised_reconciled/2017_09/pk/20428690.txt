<h> EPA central lab made functional : LHC told <p> The Punjab government and Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) apprised the Lahore High Court ( LHC ) on Thursday that EPA 's central laboratory was made functional two weeks back in compliance with orders of the court . In its report , Punjab government also said that provincial environmental quality standards had also been notified few weeks ago . The EPA would therefore enforce industrial pollution charge rules 2001 in collaboration with Lahore Chamber of Commerce and Industry . LHC Chief Justice Syed Mansoor Ali Shah then disposed of the petition based on this statement and directed the Punjab government to implement environmental laws against the pollution emitting factories in Punjab in pith and substance . It is pertinent to mention here that the chief justice had directed Punjab chief secretary in July 2016 to make the laboratory functional within three months . He said that after the Paris Agreement , signed in 2015 , to curb pollution and environmental degradation , it was necessary for the government to implement the pollution charge rules and adhere to United Nations Climate Change Convention of which Pakistan was also a signatory from 1992. -- APP
