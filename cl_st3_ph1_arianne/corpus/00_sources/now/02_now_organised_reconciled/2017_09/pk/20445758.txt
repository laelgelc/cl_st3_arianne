<h> FAO to assist in planting Chilgoza Pine to improve environment <p> Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations with the collaboration of Ministry of Climate Change and provincial Forest Departments will contribute to improving environment , enhancing resilience and improving livelihoods in Pakistan over the next 5 years through its project " Reversing deforestation and forest degradation in high conservation value Chilgoza Pine in Pakistan . " Global Environment Facility ( GEF ) which is the largest public donor for projects aimed at improving the global environment will provide financial support for the project .
