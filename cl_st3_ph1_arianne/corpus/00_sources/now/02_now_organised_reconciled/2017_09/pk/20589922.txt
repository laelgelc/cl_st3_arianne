<h> MOL Pakistan launches tree plantation campaign <p> ISLAMABAD : MOL Pakistan Oil and Gas Co BV launched a campaign for the beautification of a select area in the Fatima Jinnah Park , F-9 Islamabad . The campaign was inaugurated by Dr Berislav Gaso , the executive vice president , Upstream of MOL Group . <p> Dr Gaso launched the beautification campaign by planting a tree sapling of Magnolia in the selected area of the park . The area has been allocated to MOL Pakistan by the Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) . MOL Pakistan has taken up this project as part of its contribution to the city that hosts its branch office in Pakistan . The occasion was attended by Hungarian Ambassador Istvan Szabo , a select group of high officials from CDA and MOL Pakistan Oil and Gas Company . <p> Dr Gaso said that for MOL Group and its subsidiaries , the protection of environment and safe guarding biodiversity had been a cornerstone of their commitment to the world .
