<h> Forests are biggest safeguard against climate change : minister <p> ISLAMABAD : Federal Minister for Climate Change Mushahidullah Khan on Friday said that forests were the biggest safeguard against climate change . <p> " Pakistani forest cover is less than 12 percent . This is far less than the requirement , " he said , while addressing the National Assembly 's Standing Committee on Climate Change . The meeting was held at the Parliament House with Dr Hafeezur Rehman Drishak in the chair . <p> The committee also discussed the pollution Rawal Lake and deforestation in Punjab . <p> The minister further said that international law prevented the states from settling population very close to wetlands . " Diseases like dengue are products of climate change . The increase in temperature causes growth of dengue mosquitoes , " he said . <p> Previously , the Punjab Forest secretary briefed the participants about the seven climate change issues in the province , including loss of indigenous flora , decrease in fodder production for livestock and low productivity of riverine forests .
