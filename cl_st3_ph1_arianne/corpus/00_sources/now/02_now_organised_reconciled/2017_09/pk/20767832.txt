<p> The PPP 's parliamentary leader in the Senate , Taj Haider said that the lawmakers had been asked to clarify their position within seven days . <p> He said that disciplinary action could be taken against the senators for remaining absent on an important day . <p> On September 22 , the senate had passed the amended Elections Bill 2017 . <p> The passage of the bill paved way for ousted premier Nawaz Sharif to lead the ruling Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz ( PML-N ) again . <p> He had earlier been removed from the post after the Supreme Court disqualified him as the prime minister in July . <p> The new law allows any citizen to form and lead a party . <p> The Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) and the Muttahida Qaumi Movement ( MQM ) had taken strict action against party senators , who voted in favour of the bill in violation of party discipline . <p> The MQM revoked the basic party membership of its Senator Mian Attique , who voted for the bill . <p> The PTI has issued show cause notices to its senators @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abstaining from voting . <p> The Election Commission of Pakistan had in July de-notified Nawaz Sharif as a member of the National Assembly in the light of the Supreme Court 's July 28 verdict , also rendering him ineligible to lead his party . <p> The bill , approved by the National Assembly in August , gives every citizen the right to be part of a political party or create one , except those who are in government service . <p> After the passage of the bill , Minister for Climate Change Mushahidullah Khan said that with the approval of Clause 203 , Nawaz Sharif could become the party president again . <p> " In fact he has already become the party chief , " he added . <p> This news was published in The Nation newspaper .
