The federal climate change ministry was set up in 2012 followed by approval of the National Climate Change Policy and National Disaster Risk Reduction Policy ( 2012 ) which provide a comprehensive framework for policy goals and actions for mainstreaming climate . Given the technical and procedural complexity involved in the implementation of the REDD+ activities , Parties ( UNFCCC signatories ) agreed that REDD+ should be implemented in three phases : readiness , implementation and results-based actions . Pakistan is currently into its readiness phase under which it is developing its vision . Once Pakistan will meet the pre-requisite , it will enter into implementation and verification phase that will result into the payments in dollars .
