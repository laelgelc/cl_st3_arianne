<p> Niaz Ali Haji , a farmer from Gul Bela village in Charsadda district , considers Farm Forestry concept a boon for him and his colleagues because of the positive turn around in their lives due to economic benefits of the practice . " For the last 12 years when I decided to switch over from crops farming to growing poplar and eucalyptus trees on my 120 kanal of land , it proved to be a favour of lady luck on me , " remarks Niaz Ali while sharing his experience of Farm Forestry with APP during a visit to his fields . " The return of planting tree is slow , but huge , much beyond the expectation based on experience of crops farming , " discloses Niaz . Earlier I used to grow wheat and sugarcane on my land which in return provided me financial benefits though it was a ' hand to mouth ' living . Whereas financial benefits of fast growing trees like poplar , eucalyptus and tamarix are much more from growing ordinary crops , Niaz claimed . " High returns of commercial forestry are attracting land owners towards growing trees and this practice is not only proving to be economically beneficial for growers , but also a big source of overcoming accelerated rate of deforestation , " said Bashir Hussain Shah , renowned Forester and former Director of PFI ( Pakistan Forest Institute ) . The burden of wood based industry has been shifted from natural forest to farm forests because of low price and availability of wood in abundance , he told APP . " Promotion of commercial forestry dispels the impression that wood based industries are dependent on natural forests , " Syed Muhammad Abubakar said , a Lahore based international award winning environmental journalist . Talking to APP , Abubakar said it is simple to understand that how farm forestry is sustaining wood based industries . The price of wood obtained from trees of natural forest including Deodar or Sheesham is in hundreds of rupees per cubic feet . Whereas , the price of wood obtained from farm forests stands around Rs 6 to Rs 9 per kg depending on quality and size of grown tree . So how industrialists would opt for expensive produce instead when a much cheaper is available in the market , he asked . " The concept of farm forestry needs to be promoted and communities be involved in this practice if we want to protect and increase our natural forest besides combating the challenges posed by climate change and its impact on environment of the country , " Abubakar suggests . -- APP
