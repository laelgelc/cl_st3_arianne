<p> The Anlo Beach Community near Shama needs to be relocated as soon as possible to save lives of the over 3,000 people living in the fishing community . <p> The community is being seriously threatened by tidal waves with the ocean continually flooding the community , destroying buildings , schools , mangroves , coconut vegetation , and the beautiful sandy beach as well . <p> A tour by journalists to the Anlo Beach revealed that most homes were gone , and a number of peoples ' livelihoods destroyed . During the tour , it was observed that River Prah , which initially was not entering the sea at the beach , had now overflowed its banks and was flowing into the sea , creating artificial estuary . <p> The water current also continues to eat into the remaining sand bar that is left to protect the land . <p> " The sea is invading the community so fast and this will require some form of adaptation . <p> " So right now the people are under threat .
