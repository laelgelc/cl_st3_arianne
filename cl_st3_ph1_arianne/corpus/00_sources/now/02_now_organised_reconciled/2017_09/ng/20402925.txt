<h> Environmentalist Decries Sand Filling Of Lagos Lagoons <p> An environmentalist , Mr. John Ekoko , on Wednesday decried the ongoing sand filling of Lagoons in some parts of Lagos State by the state government , warning against its consequences . <p> Ekoko , a former Chairman of the Nigerian Environmental Society , Lagos Island Chapter , spoke in an interview with newsmen in Lagos . <p> Newsmen report that massive sand filling is currently ongoing at the lagoon end of Iyana Oworo , Ogudu and Ilubirin areas of the state . According to him , the state government is sand filling the lagoons without due consideration for the surrounding coastal areas . <p> He said that its consequences were already noticeable on the surrounding coastal areas as well as on water transportation . <p> Ekoko said a comprehensive Environmental Impact Assessment ( EIA ) should have been carried out on the areas before any dredging and sand filling work was carried out . <p> The environmentalist said that his concern and that of others was that they have yet to see the EIA documents on any of the ongoing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ filling and dredging of Lagoons at the environment and non-governmental levels , but government does not want to listen . <p> " You see what is happening at the western part of the bar-beach because of the Eko Atlantic City project ; the coastal areas are now shadows of what they used to be . <p> " I want to use this opportunity to remind the state government that climate change is real , the world over . <p> " The sea level is increasing as a result of the melting ice and that is why the issue of flooding is becoming very , very dangerous , " he said . <p> The environmentalist said that coastal lines had been tampered with as a result of unregulated sand filling and dredging . <p> Ekoko urged the state Ministry of Physical Planning and Infrastructure to rise up to the occasion and nip the foreseeable disasters in the bud . <p> He said that the recent experiences along the Lekki axis would be child 's play if government should continue to ignore their warnings.Lagos Lagoon Punch Newspaper <p> SaharaReporters.com is an outstanding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ongoing corruption and government malfeasance in Africa .
