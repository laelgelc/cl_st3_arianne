<h> Start with volunteering if you lack funds -- Ayodele <p> The Chief Executive Officer of L'Afrika Integrated Farms and Country Coordinator of African Youth Employment Initiatives Nigeria , Sipasi Ayodele , 29 , tells ANNA OKON , about his life as an agricultural entrepreneur <p> What have you been up to ? <p> I have Bachelor of Technology ( Agriculture ) degree from the Federal University of Agriculture , Akure , Ondo State , and master 's degree in Animal Production and Health and Animal Science simultaneously from the University of Ibadan . I attended the Rural Future Laboratory and the G20 Summit on ' ONE World without Hunger is Possible ' in Germany . <p> Before setting up ProtectOzone , I volunteered and I am still volunteering with several local and international Non-Governmental Organisations among which are ONE in Africa , Voluntary Service Overseas Nigeria , Education Concern for Hunger Organisation ( East and West Africa ) , Food4All Ghana and others . <p> How did L'Afrika Integrated Farms Limited start ? <p> I started agriculture as a child , at the age of nine . My grandparents were excited and gave me the necessary support to expand by building more cages as the chicken were multiplying into hundreds . <p> When I left the village to join my parents in the city of Lagos , I tried raising birds but the house owners resisted me . During my secondary school years , offenders were always sent to piggery / poultry to serve punishment . Because of my love for agriculture , I committed more offence in order to be sent to the piggery / poultry always . This led to my poor academic performance . <p> Upon graduation from the high school , when it was time to proceed to the university , because of my poor performance , I decided to choose animal production and health as it was the only course I passed without struggle . <p> On getting to the university , I had low grades from my first year to the third year but during my Industrial Training and practical years I did exceptionally well to the surprise of my @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ working my fingers to the bone . <p> During my final year in 2011 , I started my poultry farm using herbal amino acids alternatives which my final year project centred around . I saw the international community interested in what I do at my backyard locally . So I decided to give my life to it . After that , I went further to acquire a Master 's degree in animal science at the University of Ibadan . <p> What are the challenges of the business ? <p> Many times , customers become price takers after I have spent a lot on production .
