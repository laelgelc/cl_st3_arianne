They underscore the reality that only a global partnership supporting home grown and inclusive solutions stands a chance of achieving the goals . <p> Building on the millennium development goals , the SDGS open up new priorities such as climate change , economic inequality , innovation , sustainable consumption , peace and justice . The goals are interconnected .
