<h> Garlands for EnviroNews at 8th environment lecture <p> EnviroNews Nigeria , an online publication , will today be conferred with the " 2017 Environmental Media Excellence Award " , at the 8th Environment Outreach Magazine Public Lecture and Environmental Awards holding in Calabar , the Cross River State capital . <p> The 2017 Environmental Awards is to honour individuals and corporate bodies that have distinguished themselves in various fields of environmental management . <p> According to the host and Publisher of the Environment Outreach Magazine , Chief Noble Akenge , the theme of this year 's lecture is : " Nigeria 's Depleting Forests and Its Implication for Forest Resources and Climate Change " . <p> The lecture will be delivered by an expert , Professor Hilary Inyang , a former Vice Chancellor , Botswana University of Science and Technology , Papalye .
