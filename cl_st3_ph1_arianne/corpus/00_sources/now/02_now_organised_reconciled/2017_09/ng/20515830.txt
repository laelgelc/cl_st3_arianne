<h> FG engaging foreign partners to tackle flooding -- Minister <p> The Minister for Environment , Ibrahim Jibril , has said the Federal Government is working in partnership with international agencies and foreign countries to tackle flooding in the country . <p> The minister made this known at the opening session of the 5th General Assembly of the African Network of Man and Biosphere Reserves at the International Institute of Tropical Agriculture , Ibadan on Tuesday . <p> The five-day event has representatives of the United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organisation ; Germany and Belgium governments , experts on environmental issues from international agencies and many African countries . <p> The minister said many did not appreciate the effort of the government in tackling environmental problems in the country , especially the recent flooding in Benue , Lagos , Oyo , Sokoto and other states in Nigeria , stressing that the FG had engaged international partners to open up major rivers and blocked waterways in the country . <p> He said , " The problems of climate change can not be overlooked . We have seen the flooding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The consequence of this problem is devastating . The River Benue needs a long-term plan to be dredged in order to open up the canals . It will then be able to contain much water instead of spilling it into the communities and causing devastating flooding . <p> " The Vice President was in Benue to see the suffering of the people . We have the short term , medium term and long term plans to deal with the environmental problem . <p> " The Lake Chad had shrunk and today , it is only 10 per cent of what it used to be in the 1960s . This has devastating consequences on the means of livelihood of the people and the country is also feeling the consequences . " <p> He added , " The FG is working with international partners to see that Lake Chad basin is recharged . The Federal Government does a lot of things that people do not seem to realise their importance . " <p> The minister said it was vital to seek funds outside government budget to tackle environmental problems as the FG @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can not rely on government budgetary provision to tackle environmental challenges facing us . Ordinary appropriation is not all we need to solve our environmental problems . We have to engage development partners as we can see here at the assembly . There are representatives from UNESCO , Germany and Belgium governments and several African countries . These are strong development partners who are willing to assist and we will continue to collaborate and engage them positively so that we will be able to take care of the environmental issues facing Nigeria , " Jibril . <p> Copyright PUNCH.All rights reserved .
