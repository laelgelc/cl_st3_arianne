Around 25 % of the overall ESI Fund budget goes to climate-related actions , paving the way for a shift towards a low-carbon economy and meeting the EU 's long-term 2050 greenhouse gas reductions target . <p> RegioStars encouraged nominations that actively contribute to the EU 's 2030 Climate and Energy Framework , especially those projects which have adopted a holistic approach and that help Europe move towards a low-carbon economy . <p> A geothermal energy network in Montieri , Tuscany cuts heat and hot water costs by a third for homes , schools and health centres . It has led to drops in traditional fuel consumption and greenhouse gas emissions .
