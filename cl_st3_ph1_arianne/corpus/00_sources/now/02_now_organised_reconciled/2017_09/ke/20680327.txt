<p> A story is told about a poor mad woman who lived in a shopping centre in the countryside . <p> She was remarkable , not just in the extreme degree of insanity and unkemptness of appearance but also another curious fact : With the infallible regularity of the African seasons ( before climate change ) , she was pregnant every long rains season . The imponderable to many right-thinking members of the community was the filthy person responsible for the sexual exploitation of this unfortunate woman . <p> It came to pass that in the shopping centre there lived a shopkeeper , who , on top of being a pillar of the community and a ranking church elder , was also guilty of the sidelong gaze of the pervert . She was " Wazimu " during the day but " Mary " amid the demented squeals and the rustle of soiled rags in the dead of the night . <p> SICK INDIVIDUAL <p> While the shopkeeper , he of the lazy eye , was a sick individual , if in his illness he acknowledged that he was unwell , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was not out of love or his charms as the village Lothario but a mental illness every inch as bad as the poor woman he preyed upon , then he would find not just redemption but the hope of treatment and restoration of health . <p> Kenya is in the grip of ethnic mobilisation worse than in 2007 . The tribal divisions amongst Kenyans of all walks of life have become so deep that , if this continues for any length of time , relations between folks will be irreparably damaged . <p> If you ask why the people are on the verge of picking up sticks ( or worse ) against one another , you will be told that it is because of " politics " . But what is politics ? Is it ideological differences ? Is it mobilisation for the purposes of taking power ? <p> In other societies , the desire to take power is so that one can organise society in accordance to one 's beliefs . Democrats in America want to take power to create a gentler society where the poor can access medical @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it for the direct opposite . <p> STEAL PUBLIC FUNDS <p> Why do Kenyan politicians want to take power ? To steal public funds and be ridiculously wealthy ? To cook tenders for their friends ? To satisfy a deep desire to dominate others and be venerated ? To change society and create a more prosperous and safer place for all of us and our children ? <p> Dangerous ailment <p> Suppose , just suppose for a minute , that like the pillar of the community with the funny eye , we had a secret , dangerous ailment that was always bringing us to the brink of war . Suppose that ailment was congenital tribalism , one that we are unable to shed or overcome and one which dominates every aspect of our political life , so much so that we are helpless in its face and it drives us into dark places every five years , renders us as insane as Mary . <p> Suppose , like the shopkeeper playing his church elder part , we just mouth these high fallutin ' concepts of democracy , equity , inclusion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ actual fact , we are asking , why is n't the snout of our tribe deep in the trough ? <p> KILLING <p> In those circumstances , then I would recommend the following treatment . There is no point of killing each other , destroying our lives and tearing our children 's future apart . Let us confront our sickness ; there is no shame in illness or in seeking health . <p> So , rather than saying our counties are administrative , political or economic units , let us recognise them as tribal homelands . And rather than talking about coalitions and parties , let us talk about tribal alliances . Let us convert the Senate into the Tribal Council of Kenya , a national institution where ethnic interests are negotiated . The National Assembly can remain just that -- an institution that deals with issues cutting across all homelands . <p> In this theoretical approach , the tribe becomes the basic unit of identity , administration and economy . Politics becomes a process of negotiating not individual interests and desires but ethnic rights . <p> SUPER ROTATION <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the tribes take turns at the presidency , we share parastatal jobs between the tribes , we share public service jobs by tribe and each tribe gets a fair share of the tax kitty . <p> With time , we will do away with national elections . Elections will only happen within the tribe which is producing the candidate for that job . If , for example , it is the turn of the Maasai to rule , the rest of us need not be involved . The Maasai elect the best among their candidates to fill the vacancy . If it is the turn for the Kuria to produce the managing director of Kenya Ports Authority , the interview is handled in the homeland ; no need for the rest for us to be involved . <p> Would n't this free us from living a lie ? <p> That is , if we had a problem in the first place ( but I am sure we have none ) , everything is hunky dory , we are church elders and pillars of the community .
