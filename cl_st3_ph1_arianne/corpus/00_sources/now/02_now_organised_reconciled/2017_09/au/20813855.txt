<h> Could Oz Be The Next Country Hit By A Massive Cyclone ? <p> National Geographic talks with Jeff Keppert , head of High Impact Weather research at the Bureau of Meteorology about the likelihood of cyclones hitting Australia and how we , as a nation can prepare for such an event . <h> The recent events in the gulf and across the pacific have caused mass devastation , how likely is it that events like this will happen in Australia ? <p> Australia can , and has , seen cyclones of broadly similar wind speed intensity to Irma , and that have produced major flooding like Hurricane Harvey . However , major disasters occur when a severe event impacts a vulnerable population . Australia 's coastal areas are relatively sparsely populated , especially in WA and the NT , so it is less likely that a large population would be impacted . We have quite stringent building standards , and a good level of conformity to them , so most structures will only experience light to moderate wind damage , and even a partially damaged structure can still provide good shelter from the wind for its occupants . <p> Like the US and Caribbean , we have a highly effective warning system , so populations are well prepared for an event , which demonstrably saves lives and property . <h> What kind of damage would Australia be looking at if we did have similar weather ? <p> Flooding , both riverine and storm surge , would be likely . Many houses would be partially damaged , some with roofs removed . However , the total destruction which we saw in Tropical Cyclone Tracy in 1974 would not recur , because building standards , and our forecast and warning services , have improved enormously since then .
