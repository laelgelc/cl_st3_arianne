<h> Irma Is But The Latest Disaster To Strike The Everglades <p> As Hurricane Irma rakes the west coast of Florida , all eyes are on the cities and human lives in the storm 's path . But Irma is also hammering the Florida Everglades , and the scientists familiar with this fragile ecosystem are concerned the storm could deliver a devastating blow . <p> " They 're losing out , " said Hal Wanless , a geologist at the University of Miami , when asked what 's been happening to the Everglades ' coastal marshes and mangrove swamps in the wake of recent , powerful hurricanes . According to Wanless , a trifecta of urban development , extreme weather , and climate change has eroded the Everglades ' resilience .
