<h> China trip seeks new source of power <p> A TRIP to inspect the latest technology being used in China to generate electricity from rubbish and lessen the amount going to landfill is part of Mornington Peninsula Shire 's bid to attain carbon neutrality by 2020 . <p> Cr Hugh Fraser said moving to a waste-to-energy system could lead to the shire being able to close its Rye tip and avoid a $2 million a year state tax to keep it open . <p> " The Rye landfill is the shire 's largest contributor to its greenhouse gas and carbon footprint , " Cr Fraser said . <p> He said the only question remaining was when the tip could be closed , which would also save money on opening costly new " waste cells " . <p> " Council has resolved to transition to disposal of its kerbside municipal waste to a waste-to-energy facility when economically available , " Cr Fraser said . " The waste market and the access to such technology is moving quickly .
