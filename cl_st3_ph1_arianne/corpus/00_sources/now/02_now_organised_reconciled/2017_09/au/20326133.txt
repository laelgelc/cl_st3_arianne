<h> The ' Oscars ' of Australian science awarded <p> The nation 's most outstanding scientists have been honored at the 2017 Australian Museum Eureka Prizes Award Dinner , the " Oscars of Australian science " . <p> Australia 's premier science awards celebrated scientific breakthroughs from around the country , ranging from robots that are making Australia a world leader in farm automation , to technology that can reliably produce life-saving oxygen for newborns in hospitals . <p> Fifteen awards -- worth $150,000 in prize money -- were presented on Wednesday night ( August 30 ) , recognising excellence in science , research and innovation , scientific leadership and engagement , and school science across a broad spectrum -- from environmental and innovative technologies , to national security , citizen science and for the first time , data science . <p> Eureka Prizes winners included scientists and researchers who have : <p> * discovered ways to help protect the Great Barrier Reef from the significant environmental damage caused by sediment runoff ;
