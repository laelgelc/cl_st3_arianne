Comparatively weaker sunlight , large continental ice sheets , and very low amounts of carbon dioxide in the air created the right conditions for an ice age . During the next 15,000 years the global climate shifted from glacial to warm , interglacial conditions as sunlight strengthened . <p> But the Southern Hemisphere 's climate changed abruptly roughly 18,000 years ago . But these unique volcanic eruptions occurred just before ( or possibly during ) the most significant period of rapid climate change in the Southern Hemisphere , and global greenhouse gas concentrations increased during this time . <p> The fundamental question is : " did these two events occur at the same time by coincidence or were they causally linked ?
