<p> Australia 's building and land-use policy settings fall well short of what 's needed to make meaningful progress toward creating sustainable cities . <p> You will find environmental sustainability goals and objectives in government strategy documents . But our newly released review of building and land-use planning policies around Australia has found New South Wales is the only state without serious gaps in legislation and enforcement . <p> Research shows a large percentage of new dwellings in Australia fail to meet even minimum building requirements when checked after construction . <p> There is little legislation and enforcement , with the notable exception of NSW 's Building and Sustainability Index ( BASIX ) . This means neither building codes nor state planning systems are achieving sustainability goals required for a low-carbon future for cities and buildings . <h> Stepping into the breach locally <p> As the national building code is failing to improve sustainability , efforts to do this through local planning systems are emerging . <p> The Council Alliance for Sustainable Built Environments ( CASBE ) in Victoria is one example .
