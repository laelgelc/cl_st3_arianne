Alex Ganotis , the Councillor for Stockport , is leading the agenda . <p> The landmark summit will be held in March 2018 . It aims to set a pioneering deadline for Greater Manchester to achieve carbon neutrality -- a net zero carbon footprint . <p> Manchester is among 50 Labour-run city councils across the UK to have already pledged to carbon neutrality by 2050 .
