<h> Port exonerated over dredging link to Montrose coastal erosion crisis <p> A study has exonerated Montrose Port Authority of contributing to the erosion crisis in the town . <p> A Coastal Process Assessment refuted the link between the port 's dredging activity and coastal erosion in Montrose . <p> Montrose Golf Links -- battling to save the 455-year-old course from a watery grave -- has pointed the finger at the port for " wasting " the dredged material by dumping it at Lunan Bay . <p> Links director Chris Curnin claimed if the material was put onto Montrose beach in the " correct " position it would have a " significant impact and slow down the erosion " . <p> The port has made public the findings of the assessment undertaken by leading marine consultancy ABPmer , whose key objective was to " provide a comprehensive understanding of the coastal processes operating in Montrose Bay " . <p> Montrose Port Authority chief executive Nik Scott-Gray said the study has disproved the previously-reported link between the dredging of MPA 's navigation channel , subsequent material dumping to the south of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Golf Links Limited ( MGLL ) to the north . <p> He said : " The information provided on the coast process and morphology of Montrose Bay illustrates that there have been phases of both accretion and erosion of the beach and dunes since 1865 . <p> " The cycle of change witnessed at Montrose is common along the east coast of Scotland , as patterns of wave activity in this area are multi-directional , and can result in changes in direction of longshore sediment transport . <p> " In other words , any link to port dredging and coastal erosion was made on classification of Montrose Bay as a self-contained sedimentation system , whilst in fact , coastal change within Montrose Bay is not unique and is controlled by far wider influences than those within the bay itself .
