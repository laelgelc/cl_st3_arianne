<p> The Scottish Natural Heritage has identified hundreds of areas that could be at risk in the future as the sea level could rise by more than half a meter over the next seven decades . <p> According to their report , the sea levels in the Firth of the Clyde could be 47cm higher in 2080 than in 2008 . <p> It 's not just the Naval Base that is at risk , other local infrastructure such as : A roads , railway lines , the rail station at Prestwick International Airport , Mudflats and areas of saltmarsh which are used by birds for nesting and feeding in a protected area of the inner Clyde . <p> Mike Cantlay , Chairman of Scottish Natural Heritage , said : <p> " We conduct regular research into the long-term future of Scotland 's natural environment ... having this advance notice allows partners to work together to address potential issues and plan ahead for ways to mitigate these risks .
