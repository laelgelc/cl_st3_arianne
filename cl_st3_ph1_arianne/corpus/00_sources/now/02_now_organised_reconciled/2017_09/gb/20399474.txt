Doing so stops 25 -- yes , 25 -- times more emissions than the next most effective measure , going without a car . <p> It 's likely that a new Royal flown and driven around the world will have a larger carbon footprint than most of us -- but as the average Briton produces 70 times more carbon than someone from Niger , the difference between another British prince and another British subject is marginal in the grand scheme of things <h> There could be four billion less of us <p> Population growth and its devastating consequences @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The UN issued its latest , and sobering , population projections in July.It expects nearly a third more of us on this creaking planet by 2050 , and by 2100 -- within the lifetime of many children born today -- more than 11 billion .
