<p> I have always been fascinated by apocalyptic visions , as a writer and as a reader . <p> I wrote my first novel , set after an apocalypse , at 14 . I wanted to find out if I could believe that humans would evolve morally and ethically after an ultimate lesson . But , at its most profound level , the novel expressed and explored my confusion and grief at my father 's death and the drunk driver who killed him . The fictional apocalypse I imagined is effectively my father 's death . What could be more like an apocalypse for a child , after all , than the death of a beloved parent ? <p> For me , the best post-apocalypse novel is personal as well as political .
