<h> Do n't give up on the Great Barrier Reef . It is not ' dead ' , environmentalist urges <p> The Great Barrier Reef is not " dead " , the leader of a new initiative to salvage the natural wonder has urged . Andy Ridley , the founder of Earth Hour and man behind a new campaign called Citizens of the Great Barrier Reef , has warned against speaking in such dire terms about the colourful coral world off the coast of Australia , claiming it remains " one of the healthiest reefs in the world " . <p> " To say the Great Barrier Reef is dead implies that we have given up on what is , to me , the most amazing natural icon on our planet and that is a red line we should not cross , " he said . " The state of the Great Barrier Reef is appalling but , in reference to its size , it 's still one of the healthiest in the world .
