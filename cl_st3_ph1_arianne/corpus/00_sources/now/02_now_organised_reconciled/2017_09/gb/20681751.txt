<h> Mars hails basmati milestone in pursuit of sustainable rice supply <p> Mars Food has said that all of the basmati rice sold under its Uncle Ben 's and Seeds of Change brands will now be sourced from farmers who are working towards the Sustainable Rice Platform 's ( SRP ) standard for sustainable rice . <p> SRP is an initiative co-convened by UN Environment and the International Rice Research Institute , aimed at developing a global standard for sustainable rice cultivation . <p> Mars will work with its basmati rice growers in India and Pakistan to ensure that the company can trace its rice from field to fork .
