<h> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has conveyed her felicitations to German Chancellor Angela Merkel on her re-election for the fourth term in office . <p> In a message , she expressed that the relations between the two countries would be strengthened and widened further in the field of economic cooperation , migration , climate change and combating terrorism and violent extremism , among others . <p> She also invited her to visit Bangladesh , according to a foreign ministry statement .
