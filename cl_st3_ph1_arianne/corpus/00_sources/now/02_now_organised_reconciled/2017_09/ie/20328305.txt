At the ISSA , these root vegetables are grown outdoors and then replanted in polytunnels for a year to allow the plants to go to seed naturally . <p> " We have to imitate the rotting process to get the best seeds . Anyone who buys open-pollinated seeds can then continue the seed-saving process by letting some of their crops go to seed and using these seeds to plant further crops .
