<p> US PRESIDENT BARACK Obama had a " pen and phone " strategy . His successor Donald Trump has an eraser . <p> Since his first days in office , Trump has set out on a wholesale reversal of a long list of accomplishments that Obama achieved through executive action -- a less enduring means than the hard-and-fast language of legislation . <p> The latest Obama-era policy to fall is the programme shielding from deportation hundreds of thousands of young people brought into the country illegally as children . <p> The Trump administration said on Tuesday that the government would stop issuing new work permits while lawmakers debate whether to pass another solution . <p> In explaining his decision , Trump accused Obama of making " an end-run meaning to evade or circumvent around Congress " to protect the so-called " dreamers " . In effect , this time it 's Trump making an end-run around Obama . <p> Obama , coming out of semi-retirement , retorted that Trump 's action was a " cruel " and " self-defeating " decision tinged with politics . <p> It was yet another @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ attained through unilateral action : What one president does by executive fiat , the next can just as quickly overturn . <p> And it 's not just a Trump-Obama dynamic .
