<h> Ireland fares poorly on alcohol use , child sex abuse and suicide rate <h> We come 20th out of 188 countries evaluated in a new international health study . <p> Ireland scored very poorly when it came to alcohol consumption Source : Shutterstock/komokvm <p> IRELAND IS RANKED 20th in a new study looking at how much progress countries need to make in order to reach health-related Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> The Global Burden of Disease study , published in The Lancet , is the first comprehensive analysis of trends from 1990-2014 and projections to 2030 for 188 countries . <p> The topics evaluated include children being overweight , maternal mortality , harmful alcohol use and smoking prevalence . <p> Singapore tops the list , followed by Iceland , Sweden , Norway and the Netherlands . Afghanistan is the lowest placed country , with the Central African Republic , Somalia , South Sudan and Chad making up the bottom five . <p> Ireland rates highly in many of the categories , which are marked out of 100 , but gets one red mark for harmful alcohol @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lower than any other country in the top 20 . <p> We scored 90 in terms of both road injury mortality ( the death rate per 100,000 of the population ) and vaccine coverage .
