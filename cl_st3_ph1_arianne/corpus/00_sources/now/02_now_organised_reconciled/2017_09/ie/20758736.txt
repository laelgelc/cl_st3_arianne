<p> The Irish Film Institute today launches its exciting 2017/2018 Schools Programme with highlights that include a screening of Dunkirk on 70mm , Emer Reynolds 's compelling documentary The Farthest , a new Short Course in Film for Junior Cycle students , and a range of special events that will allow students to meet and engage with Irish filmmakers . <p> The Irish Film Institute 's 2017/2018 schools education programme is its most comprehensive to date . Once again the programme has been designed to encompass the full academic year , making it easier for schools and teachers to plan their visits and related materials well in advance . As in previous years , the programme has been designed to align closely with subject curricula and to offer opportunities for students to access a wide range of interesting and exciting cinema . <p> Last year over 18,450 students attended IFI Education screenings , and this year the programme will visit 26 locations nationwide , including Carlow , Ballina , Thurles , Kilkenny , Newbridge and Bantry . <p> Our biggest programme yet has been a joy and a challenge to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sheer potential for film in education and we are confident teachers and schools will find something of interest and relevance.Alicia McGivern , Head of IFI Education <p> Senior Cycle films offered this year include Brooklyn , An Inconvenient Sequel , with an introduction by climate change lecturer , writer and activist Dr Cara Augustenborg , and an encore screening of Shakespeare 's Globe : King Lear .
