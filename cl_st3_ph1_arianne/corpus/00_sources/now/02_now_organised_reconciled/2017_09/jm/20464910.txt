<h> Renewable energy boost - Clinton Foundation confident that Jamaica will tap into more natural resources for power grid <p> A section of the Wigton Windfarm in Manchester , which was expanded with the help of the Clinton Foundation . <p> Around the world , the Clinton Foundation works to promote clean energy projects in island nations . <p> These projects - power plants that harness the sun , wind , and heat from beneath the earth 's surface to generate renewable energy - can be a significant boon to the communities where they exist , with cleaner air , lower cost to produce electricity , and more jobs in an emerging sector . <p> There is a good reason why our focus is specifically on island nations like Jamaica . As climate change threatens our ecosystems , causing more severe weather events and rising oceans , island nations will be the first to be impacted by the devastating effects . <p> In addition , with the high cost of importing and transporting fossil fuels , island nations are able to cut costs by switching to renewable energy . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ invest in renewable energy projects that can reduce the cost of energy production and one of the reasons why ... the Jamaica Public Service Company ( JPS ) announced it would be investing US$100 million in energy facilities that can store the electricity generated by renewable plants . <p> One project getting off the ground in 2017 is the 37 MW Paradise Park Solar Farm in Westmoreland , being developed by Eight Rivers Energy Company . The site will provide electricity to Jamaica 's national energy grid at a lower cost than fossil fuels , and reduce carbon dioxide emissions by an estimated 30,000 tonnes per year . <h> Numerous steps <p> However , there are numerous logistical steps that must be taken to responsibly construct renewable energy plants like Paradise Park .
