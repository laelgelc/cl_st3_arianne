Any high-school science student knows that the increase in ambient temperature leads to more moisture in the air . These two things fuel hurricanes and typhoons and make them more severe . Therefore , try as you may , there is , indeed , a direct relationship . <p> While the naysayers of the former Bush and the present Trump administrations have downplayed the impact of human-caused global warming , perhaps because of their economic interests , Mother Nature has reacted like the female canine and her colloquial namesake among humans . <p> In 2005 , Hurricane Katrina , the third most powerful in the history of the USA , ripped through the Southern states , affecting the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at least 1,245 people , with a further 135 still missing .
