<p> The Port Antonio Police Station , which is in a dire state of disrepair , is to undergo major repairs through an initiative to be undertaken by the National Housing Trust ( NHT ) . <p> The unsightly look of the station 's physical structure , which was allowed to decay rapidly over the past decade , was vastly becoming a breeding ground for rats and other pests , forcing the Ministry of Health to order that facility closed on at least two occasions . <p> Minister without Portfolio in the Office of the Prime Minister with Responsibility for Economic Growth , Job Creation , and Climate Change Daryl Vaz told The Gleaner yesterday that the actual repairs to the station will commence shortly . <p> " The NHT will be carrying out a complete rehabilitation of the Port Antonio Police Station , " commented Vaz . <p> " A temporary location has been identified to house the police during the period of the rehabilitation . We are going to provide the hard-working men and women at the Port Antonio station with a <p> well-needed and ungraded rehabilitated building @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ comfort and good health . " <p> The police will be housed at the Juci Beef building at Bryan 's Bay , which is in close proximity to the Port Antonio courthouse . <h> LONG OVERDUE <p> And Mayor of Port Antonio Paul Thompson , who , having learnt about the repairs , is lauding the NHT for what he described " as long overdue " . <p> " The unsightly look of the administrative building and officers ' quarters is rather painful to look at , " said Thompson . <p> " There is no way that law-enforcement officers could perform at their best under such deplorable conditions . It is long overdue , but nevertheless , it is a welcome sight for all residents . As a matter of fact , it would come as no surprise to hear that someone fell through the board flooring at that facility . I am sure that all the officers are breathing a sigh of relief as their very lives were in danger , and it is not from gunmen or other criminal elements . "
