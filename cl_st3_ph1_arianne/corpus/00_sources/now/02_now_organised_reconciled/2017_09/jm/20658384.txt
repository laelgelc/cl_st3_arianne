<h> ' PE ' to become compulsory in schools locally <p> Education , Youth and Information Minister , Senator Ruel Reid , has indicated that schools across the island will be required to have physical activity programmes involving students at all levels . <p> Speaking at Cumberland High School in Portmore , St Catherine on Tuesday as the United Nations Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) in Jamaica observed the World 's Largest Lesson ( WLL ) 2017 , Reid announced the pending , new requirement of the Education Ministry . <p> " They must have ... a programme for exercise that promotes good , healthy lifestyles for all grade levels . From grades seven right through to 13 , and certainly at the early-childhood institutions , we will be promoting good health , " the minister said . <p> He noted that recently , Health Minister , Dr Christopher Tufton , indicated that there is a " very high level of obesity " locally , with close to " 42 per cent of the population considered obese " .
