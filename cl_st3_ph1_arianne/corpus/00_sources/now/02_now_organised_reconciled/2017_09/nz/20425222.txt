<h> ' This stardust wo n't settle ' - Jacinda Ardern <p> Jacinda Ardern is leading in the polls . Credits : Video - The AM Show ; Image - Getty <p> Labour leader Jacinda Ardern has hit back at her opponent Bill English 's latest line of attack on her . <p> Earlier this week , the Prime Minister said once the " stardust " had settled and Ms Ardern 's honeymoon as new leader was over , the focus of the election campaign would shift away from personality and back to policy . <p> Ms Ardern ran with the line during the Stuff Leaders ' Debate on Thursday night . Each of the leaders was given three minutes on the mic to kick off the debate - Ms Ardern said she did n't need three minutes because the crowd would be hearing enough from her over the next 90 . <p> But she did want to share " one simple message " . <p> " This stardust wo n't settle , because none of us should settle , " she told the raucous Christchurch crowd . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . New Zealand should n't settle for anything less than taking on head-on the challenges that we face this election . " <p> She said sticking with the status quo - National and its allies - was a bigger risk to the future of New Zealand than taking a punt on Labour and its 37-year-old leader , who 's only been in the party 's top job for just over a month . <p> " There is risk we 'll continue to have home ownership out of reach for the next generation . It 's already the worst it 's been in 60 years , and if we continue down this track it 'll keep getting worse . " <p> Later in the debate , she asked Mr English what he meant by " stardust " . <p> " I just mean the whole focus on a person who comes in as a new leader , " Mr English said . <p> " I think people really took notice of that , handled it well early on . But now the issue is about what makes a difference to people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ instance that climate change is the nuclear-free issue of our generation , then what are your plans for climate change ? " <p> Ms Ardern used that opportunity to outline Labour 's plans to strengthen the Emissions Trading Scheme , then explain why it was important for New Zealand to get to know her as a person , as well as her party 's agenda . <p> " People should know who they 're voting for . They should know what our character traits are , they should know how we 're going to react in a test of leadership , what our values are , " she explained . <p> " When you 're in leadership , you 're going to be thrown tests along the way and you need to know how we 'll respond to them . So yes , people should know us . That should never be at the expense of us talking about our specific policies , our specific plans and our intent over the next three years . There 's a balance there to be struck . "
