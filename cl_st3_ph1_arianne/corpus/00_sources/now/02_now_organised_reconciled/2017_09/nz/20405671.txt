<p> The group is among civil society groups which have gathered in Apia for the Pacific Islands Forum leaders meeting . <p> The network 's Maina Talia said , as Pacific countries head to COP23 in November , they can not turn a blind eye to Australia 's status as the world 's largest exporter of the dirtiest form of fossil fuel . <p> Mr Talia said Australia was the largest and richest of the Forum countries and its position on coal weakens the Pacific 's ambitions for a strong stance on climate change . <p> " Because when we talk about climate change we can not turn a blind eye to justice . And it is a matter of justice when we are facing this very critical moment for the Pacific and especially low-lying atolls , " he said .
