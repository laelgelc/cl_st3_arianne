It was singularly unprepared for the job . It had spent the previous nine years in opposition micro-managing issues and shuffling leaders seeking to regain power . It had failed to develop its policies to respond to the fast-changing world . <p> A year later , its economic strategy still consisted of only a dozen or so A3 sheets of paper , reported Colin James , the veteran political journalist . <p> As it promised in the election , National established a tax working party , which later proposed many remedies to the distortions in our tax system .
