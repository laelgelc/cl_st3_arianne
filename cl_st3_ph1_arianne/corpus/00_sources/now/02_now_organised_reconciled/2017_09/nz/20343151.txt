<p> A leaked report on climate change warns $19 billion worth of property is now at a greater risk of flooding and coastal erosion . <p> Green Party leader James Shaw says they 're calling on the government to release the report so people can begin planning for rising sea levels . Source : 1 NEWS <p> The draft document , which was commissioned by the Environment Ministry and given to the Green Party , says planning for rising sea levels needs to start now , and New Zealand can not afford to wait to take action . <p> It says 43,000 homes , 130,000 people and two kilometres of roads will feel the impact . <p> " We 're calling on the government to release the report so that business and families and councils can get ahead of this challenge and start to put in place plans to adapt to sea level rise and coastal erosion -- with some urgency , " says Green Party leader James Shaw . <p> The report states that homes in Canterbury and the Hawke 's Bay face the greatest impact from climate change .
