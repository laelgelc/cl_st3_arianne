<h> Climate change underpins all policy platforms <p> A third of a million Kiwis believe the many promises being made in the lead-up to the election all depend on restabilising the climate . After key elements of the Climate Consensus Coalition Aotearoa ( CCCA ) Action Plan were presented to Parliament by Wise Response in mid-August , its support has mushroomed and now represents some 80 organisations with 328,000 members plus 600 individual supporters . <p> The CCCA Plan was developed largely because of no real action by New Zealand following the Paris COP21 agreement . Carbon emissions continue to rise .
