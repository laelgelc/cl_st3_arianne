<h> Social issues top agenda for Local Government New Zealand <p> Local Government New Zealand is beginning to look further into social issues affecting the country . <p> It 's something it wants to address alongside climate change and water policy with the Government , after the election . <p> Local Government New Zealand President Dave Cull says they 're not usually responsible for social issues like housing , but it 's become a major issue . <p> " I think it would be safe to say that pretty much every community in the country has housing issues , they 're expressed differently in different places in some places it 's availability , in others it 's affordability in others it 's quality . " <p> He says they want to see councils gain more control over rental property standards . <p> It 's one of the priorities highlighted in its election manifesto for the next Government alongside climate change , water and bio-diversity . <p> President Dave Cull says stronger legislative tools need to be introduced to deal with housing issues . <p> " It 's very difficult for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ local housing , you can basically let housing in pretty much any condition unless there 's sewerage running underneath the floor . " <p> Climate change is also a top priority the organisation wants to see addressed after the election . <p> It believes its position is made clear in a Local Government Declaration that was signed by 44 mayors and council chairs around the country . <p> President Dave Cull says part of the declaration is wanting the Government to lead a national campaign to raise awareness on future impacts of climate change . <p> He says it 's a prevalent issue . <p> " We know and we are witnessing more frequent and more extreme weather events not only here but all over the world .
