<h> Election choices in close race <p> Only a foolhardy person would predict the result of the election tomorrow . The polls have been all over the place . <p> What began as a potential borefest , with as much colour as a Rotorua mud pool , turned vivid . Andrew Little 's resignation from Labour brought bright star Jacinda Ardern to the fore , and new energy and purpose from the party and supporters . <p> Abruptly , too , the young took an interest and many flocked to hear her " vision " . <p> National 's leader Bill English responded with vigour and the election was all on . His assurance and assertiveness contrasts with his previous tilt for power in 2002 as National leader . That ended in a pounding defeat to Helen Clark . <p> Mr English is emphasising there is a clear choice this election . While neither party whatever the rhetoric , in essence is straying from the neoliberal path , differences in emphasis are distinct .
