" <p> It wants to replace the scheme with a Kiwi Climate Fund that would put a charge on all climate pollution , including agriculture . <p> The proceeds would pay for a massive tree-planting programme , and whatever was left over would be returned as a dividend to New Zealanders aged over 18 .
