<h> Fiji leader calls for prayer ahead of COP23 <p> Fiji 's prime minister , Frank Bainimarama , has called on all Fijians to participate in a weekend of prayer this month to help guide the country 's efforts at the COP 23 climate change conference in Germany in November . <p> Frank Bainimarama Photo : Supplied <p> Fiji is the co-host of the meeting . <p> Mr Bainimarama said the government was holding a weekend of prayer for its COP23 Presidency through the churches , mosques and temples . <p> The FBC reported him saying it was a chance to come together as a nation to pray for Fiji and pray that it brings the attention the issue deserves .
