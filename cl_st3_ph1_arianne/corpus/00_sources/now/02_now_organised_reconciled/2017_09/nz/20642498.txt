<p> Enele Sopoaga was speaking at a Small Island Developing States event in New York where he and other leaders have gathered for the United Nations General Assembly . <p> Describing climate change as a security issue , he said there was a need to ensure that implementation of the Paris Agreement was verifiable . <p> The Agreement , which aims to limit global temperature increase to well below 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels , was designed to protect communities most vulnerable to climate change impacts . <p> Mr Sopoaga said Tuvalu would not vote for any country which did not support climate change as a security issue and a permanent item on the UN Security Council agenda . <p> He called for scaled-up financing for climate change , saying new climate finance must be additional to existing development assistance . <p> " Existing development assistance is not climate change funding and should not be counted as such .
