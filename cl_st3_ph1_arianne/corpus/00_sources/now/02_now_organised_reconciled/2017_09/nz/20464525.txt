<p> As part of its climate change policy announced in Auckland today , the party says the Kiwi Climate Fund would put a charge on all climate pollution , including from agriculture , and pay people for planting trees . <p> The Greens leader James Shaw said the charge on agriculture emissions , starting in 2020 , would reduce an average dairy farm 's profitability by less than 2 percent . <p> " No farmer I 've talked to wants their child to inherit a world with longer droughts and drier rivers . Agriculture can no longer be exempt from reducing climate pollution ; farmers need to be part of the solution , " he said . <p> The fund would charge $40 per tonne of carbon dioxide emissions and $6 per tonne of nitrous oxide and $3 per tonne of methane emissions from agriculture . <p> " We say tax pollution more , and peoples ' incomes less , " Mr Shaw said . <p> The Green Party would drop the bottom tax rate from 10.5 percent to 9 percent . <p> The party has also reiterated its pledge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reaching zero emissions by 2050 . <p> It would do this by passing a Zero Carbon Act in its first 100 days in government . <p> The party would also establish an independent Climate Commission , and public investment funds to divest from companies directly involved in exploration , mining , and production of fossil fuels . <p> Other initiatives include creating a new humanitarian visa category for people displaced by climate change in the Pacific , and a fund to help farmers transition to sustainable agriculture . <p> " It hit me that climate change is n't about generations unborn any more , " Mr Shaw said after watching online coverage of monsoon floods in India and Bangladesh , and the hurricanes that hit Houston and Caribbean islands . <p> " We are living in the climate changed world .
