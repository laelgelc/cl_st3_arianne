<p> Mrs Dalziel described the cathedral devastated by the 2011 quake that killed 185 people , as the heart and soul of the city . <p> The Government and Christchurch City Council made an offer of $35 million and promised to fast-track the legislation if restoration was chosen . <p> Debate on whether the cathedral should be restored to its former glory , partly reinstated , or demolished and replaced with a modern new building have raged between the church , heritage campaigners and the public for the past six-and-a-half years . <p> Head of the Canterbury Anglican Church Bishop Victoria Matthews previously said child poverty and climate change were bigger concerns for the church . <p> A recent church-commissioned survey found the Christchurch community was evenly-split on whether the cathedral should be rebuilt or demolished .
