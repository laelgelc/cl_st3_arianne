<h> Greens announce Kiwi Climate Fund to tackle climate change <p> The Green Party today announced a comprehensive plan to tackle climate change including replacing the Emissions Trading Scheme with a Kiwi Climate Fund . <p> " We 're the first generation that will feel the effects of climate change , and the last that can stop it . We have a responsibility to act , and the Green Party has a plan to do it , " said Green Party leader James Shaw . <p> " The Green Party 's first priority in government will be passing the Zero Carbon Act and to set up an independent Climate Commission , to reduce climate pollution to net zero by 2050 . <p> " The Kiwi Climate Fund will put a simple , transparent charge on all climate pollution and use the revenue to fund tree planting around the country . <p> " The Fund 's remaining surplus will be returned to every New Zealander , aged 18 and over , every year as a dividend . We estimate this could be $250 per person in 2020 . <p> " Farmers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We will introduce a charge on agricultural emissions , starting in 2020 , which is estimated to reduce the average dairy farm 's profitability by less than 2 percent , or less than 6 percent when combined with the Green Party 's proposed clean rivers nitrate levy .
