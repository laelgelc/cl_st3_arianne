<p> UC San Diego announced Thursday that Mark Merrifield will be the inaugural director of its Center for Climate Change Impacts and Adaptation . <p> Merrifield , an alumnus of UC San Diego 's Scripps Institution of Oceanography , returns to California after having served for two decades as the head of the University of Hawaii Sea Level Center . <p> The appointment is a major milestone for the center , which was founded in in 2015 with a $5 million gift from longtime Scripps supporters Richard and Carol Hertzberg . <p> The stated goal of the project is to come up with practical solutions for living with the fall out of climate change . Many scientists predict that impacts , such as sea-level rise , drought and heat waves , are now inevitable at some level . <p> The following is a condensed transcript of an interview with the center 's incoming director : <p> Q : What do you hope to accomplish in your first year as director of the Center for Climate Change Impacts and Adaptation ? <p> A : The center has a fairly broad mandate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and adaptation over a whole spectrum of different problems and aspects ... so I 'm hoping within the first year , we can set up the framework and infrastructure within the center to branch out into these different areas .
