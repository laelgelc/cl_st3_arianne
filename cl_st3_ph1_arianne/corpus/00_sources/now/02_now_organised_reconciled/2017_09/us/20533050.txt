Political leaders on the right who slashed safety nets for the poor , denied climate change , and made life harder for pregnant women rarely felt any real heat from bishops , never mind a pope , as long as they were viewed as sufficiently anti-abortion . <p> Pope Francis is making those once comfortable politicians look over their shoulders . <p> Since his election almost five years ago , the pope has rejected a narrow , single-issue framing of what it means to be " prolife " in ways that offer a refreshing , long overdue challenge to a binary U.S. political narrative .
