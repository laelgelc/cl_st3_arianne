<h> Most of the world 's best places for coffee will be gone by 2050 <p> According to a new study in the journal Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences , the world 's largest coffee-producing regions could shrink by as much as 88% by 2050 as a result of climate change . The study is the first of its kind to look at how bees -- key coffee crop pollinators -- will be impacted by a warmer planet . <p> Bees are the unsung heroes of our global food system , responsible for pollinating as many as two-thirds of the crops we eat . Without them , our farms would falter . <p> As the planet warms , both bees and coffee crops will respond by moving to more suitable climates when they can . Often , those more suitable areas are uphill , where elevation protects them from excessive heat . <p> But in countries like Nicaragua , Honduras , and Venezuela , that is n't an option .
