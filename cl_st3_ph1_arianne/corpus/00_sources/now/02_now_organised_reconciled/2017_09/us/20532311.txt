Changing the rules on how $3 billion of cap-and-trade funds will be spent is a gift to the United Auto Workers , which is struggling to organize workers at Tesla . <p> The Legislature approved a perfectly good Assembly Bill 398 two months ago . Designed in part to help the state reach Gov . Jerry Brown 's goal of 1.5 million zero-emission vehicles in California by 2025 , its clear intent was to give a portion of the cap-and-trade funds to auto manufacturers , including Tesla , for rebates big enough to entice buyers to purchase their cars .
