<h> Letter : Dangers of reckless behavior <p> I saw a man driving east directly into the morning sunshine , wearing no sunglasses , no windshield visor down . A child stood behind the driver 's seat looking over the man 's shoulder . Both were gazing at the driver 's handheld iPhone . <p> Climate deniers , like the man in the car , deliberately put others in danger . The guy driving could have killed the boy or pedestrians . Climate deniers driven by greed and power put everyone in harm 's way . Houston 's unprecedented flooding or the West 's hellish wildfires may have been averted if extremists in Congress had put the brakes on America 's addiction to coal and oil . <p> Advertisement <p> Scientists for more than 35 years have predicted these extreme environmental disasters . More than 2,000 scientists from 80 Academies of Science around the world have held a 97 percent consensus that human activity is largely the cause of accelerated global warming .
