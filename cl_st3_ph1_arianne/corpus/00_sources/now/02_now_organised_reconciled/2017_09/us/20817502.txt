<h> Stay on target <p> What do David Attenborough , Barack and Michelle Obama , Leonardo DiCaprio , and David Bowie have in common ? <p> They are the namesakes of 15 new species of " smiley-faced " spiders . <p> Primarily found basking in the Caribbean sun , the arachnids were discovered by a University of Vermont scientist ( and four of his students ) . <p> " This was an undergraduate research project , " spider expert Ingi Agnarsson , a professor of biology at UVM , said in a statement . <p> Named for the smiley face pattern on their abdomens , the Spintharus spider has long been thought to have one widespread species -- from northern North America down to northern Brazil . <p> But when a research team from the Caribbean Biogeography Project ( CarBio ) -- spearheaded by Agnarsson of UVM 's Biology Department and Greta Binford at Lewis &amp; Clark College -- looked closer , they found more than 15 regional breeds . <p> " And if we keep looking , we 're sure there are more , " Agnarsson said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) <p> On top of the glory of their discovery , participating students got to christen the insects -- some named after beloved family members , others for revered politicians , actors , and musicians . <p> " We all named the Bernie Sanders spider , " according to Lily Sargeant , a project contributor who graduated from UVM last year .
