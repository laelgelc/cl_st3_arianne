<h> Women 's Contribution to the Development of Science and Green Innovations Dicussed at Expo 2017 <p> PR Newswire <p> Sep . 9 , 2017 , 10:25 AM <p> ASTANA , Kazakhstan , September 9 , 2017 /PRNewswire/ -- <p> Delegates from 25 countries gathered at the International Forum " Women for the Future Energy " in the capital of Kazakhstan . <p> The main objective of the event was to discuss issues such as the contribution of women to promoting innovative ideas in the sphere of alternative energy and the green economy , ensuring sustainable and inclusive growth in the context of transitioning to clean energy , and effective partnership of the state , the business sector , and civil society . <p> In her opening speech addressed to the participants of the forum , Gulshara Abdykalikova , the State Secretary of the Republic of Kazakhstan , stressed the need to boost the participation of women in establishing global sustainable development . Among them were : Galina Karelova , Deputy Chairperson of the Federation Council of the Federal Assembly of Russia ; Natalya Kochanova , Head of the Belarus President Administration ; Cholpon Sultanbekova , Deputy Prime Minister of the Kyrgyz Republic ; Betsy Nelson , Vice President Risk and Compliance and Chief Risk Officer at EBRD ; Irene Natividad , President of the Global Summit of Women ; and women who are world leaders in the sphere of the green economy and alternative energy or representatives of state structures . <p> The forum resulted in the adoption of a Resolution encouraging the world community to follow the principles of achieving a reduced environmental impact , as well as containing climate change and the deterioration of natural resources . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2017 dedicated to " Future Energy " - is an exhibition and entertainment event that is held from June 10 to September 10 , 2017 in the capital of Kazakhstan - Astana .
