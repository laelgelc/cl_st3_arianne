It 's actually good for you . The microbe Mycobacterium vaccae found in dirt improves cognitive functions like learning , and seems to trigger serotonin production , making us happier and relaxed . <p> A strong body and mind . Studies have found that children at Sweden 's forest schools -- where most of the day is spent outside , regardless of weather -- have fewer sick days than children at traditional @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , boosted motor skills , and improvements in children 's abilities to delay gratification , show self-control and set and reach their own goals . <p> Better physical and social health through risky play . Engaging in risky play -- that is , at great heights , at high speed , with dangerous tools , with dangerous elements or without supervision -- in nature leads to greater physical and social health . Risky play helps kids test their limits , increase their perceptual-motor capacity , and teaches them to avoid and adjust to dangerous environments and activities . <p> **25;316;TOOLONG children . Struggling to fit in and to decide what was best for her children , Linda turned to her own childhood for answers , and embarked on a six-month journey to Sweden with her daughters in tow . Could the Scandinavian philosophy of " there is no such thing as bad weather , only bad clothes " be the key to better lives for her children ?
