<p> If a huge iceberg breaks off from Antarctica and no one 's around to hear it , did it even make a sound ? <p> That 's the question Chicago-based performance art duo , Luftwerk , answered when they took seismic readings of Antarctica 's disintegrating ice and turned it into a public art installation , called White Wanderer . And the sound is a harrowing , distressing echo of groans that could be mistaken for a chorus of forewarning whales . <p> Petra Bachmaier and Sean Gallero were inspired for the project after one of the largest-ever recorded icebergs broke off from Antarctica 's Larsen C Ice Shelf in July . Experts are at odds over what role climate change played in the trillion-ton iceberg 's detachment but agree on the opportunity it provides to better understand what will happen to Antarctica , our oceans and us , as the world warms .
