<h> New campaign seeks to require half of Maryland energy to come from renewable sources <p> A coalition of environmentalists , clergy and solar and wind energy companies launched a campaign Wednesday calling for half of Maryland 's electricity to come from renewable sources . <p> That would double a policy adopted last year requiring that renewable energy account for 25 percent of the state 's electricity portfolio by 2020 . The new campaign is setting a target of 2030 . <p> " We can not wait another moment to begin bringing about the clean-energy future we need , " said Brooke Harper , Maryland and District of Columbia policy director for the Chesapeake Climate Action Network . <p> The campaign is casting the state 's renewable energy mandate as a tool to create " green " jobs , particularly in economically depressed communities and among women and people of color . Utilities like Baltimore Gas and Electric Co. and retail electricity sellers are required to buy enough of the credits to equal a set and growing percentage of their power supply -- 15.6 percent in 2017 -- or pay a penalty . <p> The certificates cost Maryland electricity ratepayers $126.7 million in 2015 , according to the state Public Service Commission . <p> Karla Raettig , executive director of the Maryland League of Conservation Voters , said the coalition is prepared for that . <p> " We have a lot of momentum , " she said .
