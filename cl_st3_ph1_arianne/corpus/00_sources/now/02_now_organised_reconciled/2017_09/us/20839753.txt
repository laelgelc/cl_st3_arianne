K-129 held a crew of nearly 100 sailors , as well as a full payload of nuclear missiles . Following its loss , the US Navy noted the flurry of Soviet activity dispatched to locate the ship and saw an opportunity to gain access to their rival 's military secrets . They decided to locate and then steal the sub .
