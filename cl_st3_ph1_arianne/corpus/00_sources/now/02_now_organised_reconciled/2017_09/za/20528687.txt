<p> The South African Wind Energy Association ( SAWEA ) will partner with the Durban University of Technology and the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research ( CSIR ) and embark on an educational road trip with the EnergyDRIVE . <p> This custom-built truck , said organisers in a statement , was designed to teach rural learners about the benefits and uses of renewable energy technologies and was expected to engage with over 2 500 rural high school learners located close to wind farms . <p> A first of its kind in Africa , the initiative aims to raise awareness of renewable power resources and climate change . <p> " Participating wind farms will each fund a portion of the road show as well as host field trips for participating learners . " <p> Beyond wind power , the vehicle features a solar roof structure , biogas digester , photovoltaic panel display unit as well as a solar hot water display unit . <p> The walls of the container are made up of a battery bank , photovoltaic components , a TV and display cupboards , making it an inspirational @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this custom-built vehicle makes it ideal to reach high schools situated within wind farm beneficiary communities . These communities are often located in areas that are not easily accessible and learners often do not have access to information about these technologies , " said Brenda Martin , chief executive of the South African Wind Energy Association . <p> " The container features attractive interactive demonstration models which assist with understanding a range of renewable technologies . " <p> The mobile unit is expected to depart Durban on November 3 and arrive in Cape Town on November 13 , having spent time in host communities along the way .
