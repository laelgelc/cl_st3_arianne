<h> New factories to ramp up eThekwini 's recycling efforts <p> The two new factories will feature a large recycling park which will specialise in recycling old vehicles , electronic scrap and rechargeable batteries . <p> Two new factories are set to open in the south of Durban and create 1 000 employment opportunities for the local community in the recycling sector . <p> The factories , which are being developed by Chinese company Jingmen Hubei , will feature large recycling parks which will specialise in recycling old vehicles , electronic scrap and rechargeable batteries . <p> The development comes as a result of the relations forged between KwaZulu-Natal and Hubei Province , from the People 's Republic of China and has been welcomed by Premier Willies Mchunu and eThekwini Mayor Zandile Gumede . <h> Partnering for change <p> Premier Mchunu said the development will help strengthen socio-economic relations between the two provinces .
