This week 's calving event also appears to have started in the middle of the glacier , Jeong said . <p> This " inside out " melting may be happening because human-caused climate change is warming the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bottom of the ice shelf where the glacier rests , Jeong said . <p> These calving events have consequences , Jeong added . However , the edges of glaciers help keep the rest of the glacier from melting , like " debris clogging the drain , " Jeong said . As these edges wear away , the rest of the glacier is more likely to melt into the ocean , he said . <p> " It 's generally accepted that it 's no longer a question of whether the West Antarctic Ice Sheet will melt , it 's a question of when , " Ian Howat , an associate professor of earth sciences at Ohio State , who wrote the 2015 study with Jeong , said in a statement last year .
