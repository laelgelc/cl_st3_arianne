<p> ABOARD THE PAPAL PLANE ( WASHINGTON POST ) Pope Francis said Monday ( Sept 11 ) that US President Donald Trump should allow young immigrants to stay in the United States if he is truly " pro-life " . <p> " I have heard it said that the president of the United States presents himself as a man who is pro-life , and if he is a good pro-life ( man ) then he will understand that the family is the cradle of life , and that it must be defended as a unit , " Francis said , in response to a question about Trump 's decision to discontinue the Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals program ( DACA ) that allowed immigrants brought to the United States as children to live and work or study in the US .
