<p> The sharp fluctuations of the past two years have brought into focus the unreliability and climate variability that could become the new normal in the state in the coming decades with a changing climate . Even though the state received heavy showers for three days starting September 15 , the unreliability is affecting every aspect of the life of the state 's residents . <p> The uncertainty finds expression in the activities of village and town panchayats ( local self-governance bodies ) .
