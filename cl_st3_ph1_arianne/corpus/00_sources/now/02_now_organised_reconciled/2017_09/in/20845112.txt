<h> Dhyani takes charge as VC of SGRR varsity <p> Summary : DEHRADUN : Pitambar Prasad Dhyani officially took charge as vice-chancellor of Shri Guru Ram Rai University ( SGRR ) on Saturday.Dr Dhyani has a long experience in the administrative works . Elaborating his mission and vision for SGRR he added that by utilizing human resources in the best possible manner , he aims to make the university a role-model in the field of higher education , health and environment .
