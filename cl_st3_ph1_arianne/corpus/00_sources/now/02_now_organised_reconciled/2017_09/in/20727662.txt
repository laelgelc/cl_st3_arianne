<h> Rejuvenation of Devika river <p> UDHAMPUR : The modalities for the preparation of Detailed Project Report ( DPR ) for ' rejuvenation of Holy River Devika ' was discussed here on Sunday at meeting held under the chairmanship of Commissioner Secretary Housing and Urban Development Department , Hirdesh Kumar . Pertinently , Rs 170. 54 cr has been approved for this project under National River Conservation Plan by the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , Government Of India ( GOI ) . The meeting was attended by District Development Commissioner , Ravinder Kumar , Chief Engineer UEED Mohammad Haneef , Joint Director Planning H&amp;UDD Hari Hari Dutt , Chief Town Planner Jammu Joginder Singh , SE Hydraulic Circle Joginder Kumar , CPO Ashok Khajuria , ACR Subhash Chander Dogra and other district officers . The Commissioner /Secretary directed the consultant agency to take all the stakeholders on board while preparing the DPR for the project . He also stressed upon for preparation of the DPR within stipulated time frame so that the work on the holy project could be undertaken at the earliest .
