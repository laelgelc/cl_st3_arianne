<h> ' Submit report on Aravali tree-felling ' <p> Shilpy Arora <p> TNN Sep 5 , 2017 , 02:08 IST <p> GURUGRAM : The National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) on Monday directed the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) to submit the report prepared by a committee on alleged tree-felling in the Aravalis . <p> The tribunal was hearing a plea filed in June by environment activist Col Sarvadaman Singh Oberoi , who had alleged that the government of Haryana illegally granted permission to a real estate company to fell trees in Faridabad . <p> Around 6,000 trees were allegedly felled in a deemed forest to clear 52 acres of land for a housing project by the realty firm . <p> In reply to a show-cause issued by the NGT , the Haryana government had earlier told the tribunal that no forest was cleared in the Aravalis by any developer . <p> Later , acting on the instruction of the green tribunal , the MoEF had appointed a high-level committee in July to inspect the land and verify the veracity of the allegations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ But , the committee 's report has not been filed in the green tribunal till now . <p> According to environmentalists , the report is expected give a clearer picture of the site where the trees were cut . " The inspection can easily identify traces of the trees that had been felled .
