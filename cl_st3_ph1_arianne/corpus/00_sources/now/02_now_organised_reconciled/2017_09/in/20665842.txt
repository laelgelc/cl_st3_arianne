In recent years , instead of the monsoon being more or less evenly distributed across four months , the city saw a few days of intense rain that contributed to almost half the city 's annual rainfall , according to experts . This , they said , could be the monsoon pattern from now on , which means Mumbai may become prone to floods . <p> This monsoon , just six days of ' very heavy ' to ' extremely heavy ' rain contributed to 40% of the city 's rainfall so far . <p> Mumbai received 303.7 mm -- the highest 24-hour average for a September day in ten years -- between 8.30am on September 19 and 8.30am on September 20 . Monsoon in Mumbai is generally from June to September . <p> While the city has been surpassing its annual average in recent years , extreme rainfall events have increased only recently , said experts .
