<h> Why Industry 4.0 Is The Future Of Manufacturing <p> Disruptive technologies and megatrends are shaping the future of manufacturing . The fourth industrial revolution -- Industry 4.0 -- has been gathering momentum in recent years and is set to be transformative in 2017 . Industry 4.0 is the digitisation of the manufacturing industry and a realisation of the potential of the Internet of Things ( IoT ) combined with artificial intelligence and data science . Done right , Industry 4.0 will enable manufacturers to improve efficiencies whilst reducing costs . <p> The revolution is far from surprising . By 2020 , there will be 30 billion connected devices on earth , as per a recent Cisco report . This is leading to the Big Data mountain growing beyond recognition . Machines communicating with people -- and other machines -- are creating so much data that we have generated more in the past two years than in the previous 5,000 years of human history . <p> It is now up to manufacturers to unlock this data .
