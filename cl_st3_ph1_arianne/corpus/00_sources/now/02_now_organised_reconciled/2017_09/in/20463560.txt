<p> At New York 's prestigious Museum of Modern Art ( MoMA ) today , ZeeJLF will try , in the space of one evening , to give the audience a whiff of the Jaipur magic with a key element of the festival 's attraction -- a provocatively-titled , free-wheeling discussion on a subject of topical interest by a carefully curated panel from across countries , cultures , academic disciplines , professions and languages . This evening 's debate at MoMA is titled ' Patriot Games : contextualising Nationalism ' -- an issue that resonates today all the way from the US to India -- with a panel of disputants that is as diverse as it is distinguished .
