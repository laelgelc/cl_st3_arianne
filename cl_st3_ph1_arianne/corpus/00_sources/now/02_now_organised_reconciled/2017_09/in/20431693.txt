<h> Algae Dome is a prototype for a form of food-producing architecture <p> Furniture giant IKEA is on a mission to explore and design new ways of living , not just stylishly , but sustainably . Presenting Exhibit A : The SPACE10 -- IKEA 's external future-living lab . <p> One of the many areas the brand plans to explore is the future of food , and ways to improve the food system . And IKEA believes algae -- that humble chlorophyll-containing organism -- could provide the answer to some of the world 's biggest problems -- from malnutrition to climate change . <p> To further this mission , IKEA has set out to create the Algae Dome ( which was presented at the CHART art fair in Copenhagen earlier in September ) . <p> The dome is a four-metre-high , food-producing architecture pavilion that houses a photo-bioreactor -- a closed system that enables the high productivity of microalgae , a potential superfood of the future . <p> In fact , thanks to some early-autumn sunshine in Denmark , the dome was able to produce 450 litres of microalgae during the three days of CHART .
