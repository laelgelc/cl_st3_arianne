<h> Basai does n't make it to govt 's list of wetlands , greens stunned <p> Shilpy Arora <p> TNN Sep 4 , 2017 , 07:16 IST <p> GURUGRAM : The Basai wetland does n't find any mention in a document submitted by the Haryana government to the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) regarding identification and notification of wetlands in the state . <p> The irrigation and water resources department of Haryana has submitted a list of 51 wetlands in the state , of which , Gurgaon district has only three -- 28.82 hectares in Harsaru , 120.80 hectares in Kherki Majra Dhankot ( Najafgarh lake ) , and 153.96 hectares of Sultanpur lake ( a bird sanctuary ) . <p> " As per directions of the Hon'ble National Green Tribunal , the government of Haryana has initiated work for identification of water bodies and notification of wetlands in the state . HARSAC ( Haryana Space Application Centre ) has given a list of 51 water bodies with area over 10 hectares , as per the latest study of Haryana Wetland Atlas . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the irrigation and water resources department has taken up the task of notification of wetlands and their restoration thereupon , " states the document ( a copy is with TOI ) submitted by the state government . <p> The government also submitted a ' brief document ' requesting the Union ministry to notify Najafgarh jheel as a wetland . " Najafgarh jheel is one such body having area over 50 hectares . The ' brief document ' ( for identification and notification of Najafgarh jheel as a wetland ) has been prepared following guidelines of the Wetland ( Conservation &amp; Management ) Rules , 2010 . You are requested to kindly consider the details pertaining to the document and arrange to notify Najafgarh jheel as a wetland , over an area of 120.80 hectares , as identified by HARSAC , " the document states . <p> According to MCG officials , there has never been any wetland in Basai . <p> " As per the Master Plan , land in Basai is marked as Sector 100 and 110 for public utility , and as agricultural land in revenue records @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the city get flooded every year during monsoon , but we ca n't call them rivers , " said Sudhir Chauhan , senior town planner , MCG . <p> " Similarly , we ca n't call Basai a wetland . Haryana has already submitted a document to the Union ministry with a list of 51 wetlands , as per a study carried out by HARSAC . We 've also submitted a ' brief document ' ( as per rules ) for notification of Najafgarh jheel as a wetland , " Chauhan added . <p> Birders expressed shock over the move . " It 's shocking the state has listed only 51 wetlands . As per Wetland Inland Atlas of the MoEF&amp;CC , there are 44 wetlands in Gurgaon alone . How can the entire state have just 51 ?
