<p> After steadily declining for over a decade , global hunger is on the rise again , affecting 815 million people in 2016 , or 11 per cent of the global population . <p> This was stated by the recently-released new edition of the annual United Nations report on world food security and nutrition . <p> At the same time , multiple forms of malnutrition were threatening the health of millions worldwide . <p> " The increase - 38 million more people than the previous year - was largely attributable to the proliferation of violent conflicts and climate-related shocks , " according to the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World 2017 . <p> " Some 155 million children aged under five are stunted ( too short for their age ) , " stated the report . <p> " About 52 million suffer from wasting , meaning their weight is too low for their height . An estimated 41 million children are now overweight , " it added . <p> " Anaemia among women and adult obesity are also cause for concern .
