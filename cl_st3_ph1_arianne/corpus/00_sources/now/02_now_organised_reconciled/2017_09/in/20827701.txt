<p> The Punjab and Haryana High Court has sought a response from the Centre and the Panjab University on a petition stating the annual PU Campus Students ' Council elections should be held without degrading the environment by opting for paperless campaigning during the polls . <p> A Chandigarh lawyer and an alumnus of University Institute of Legal Studies , PU , Pardhuman Garg , has filed a public interest litigation stating that the authorities had violated various guidelines recommended by the Lyngdoh Committee on conducting the campus polls during the elections earlier this month . <p> " The quantity of paper wasted in canvassing and campaigning is huge and the damage therein to the environment is irreparable . On every election , tons of paper is wasted for canvassing by the candidates and their supporters . There is no accountability for usage of paper and neither is any norm or procedure for re-cycling of this waste paper , " Garg has said in his plea before the High Court . <p> Suggesting that the students instead of campaigning through paper posters , paper pamphlets , paper flyers and paper @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the candidates , Garg has said the use of Twitter , Facebook and other such websites for the polls will also create a " level playing field " for the contestants . <p> Besides the university administration and the Ministry of Environment and Forest for Climate Change , the campus students council has also been made a party to the case . <p> The PIL will come up for hearing on October 7 before the division bench of Justice Ajay Kumar Mittal and Justice Amit Rawal.
