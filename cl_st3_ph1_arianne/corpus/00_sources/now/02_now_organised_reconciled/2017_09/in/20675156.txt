<p> New Delhi : The Centre has directed states to submit strategies for tackling health challenges posed by climate change and extreme weather conditions in their respective regions as part of a comprehensive national-level strategy which is in the works . <p> The plan is aimed at protecting people against climate-sensitive illnesses including cardio-respiratory diseases , certain types of cancer and allergies . <p> Representational image . AFP <p> The ' National Action Plan on Climate Change and Human Health ' has been drafted by an expert group constituted under the Health Mission of the Prime Minister 's Council on Climate Change . <p> At a regional consultation organised on Wednesday by the health ministry , the health and environment disaster departments , and the meteorological divisions of the north Indian states were asked to frame ' State Action Plans ' . <p> Dr Jagdish Prasad , Director General of Health Services of the health ministry , said every state has specific health issues depending on the weather . <p> " Some states are prone to drought and floods , while desert and coastal regions have specific illnesses .
