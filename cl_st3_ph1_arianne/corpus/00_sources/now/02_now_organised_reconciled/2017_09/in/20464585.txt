Authorities elsewhere , whether in the United States , or here in India , would do well to borrow from practices there . Having being buffeted by regular hurricanes , Cuba is now constantly , through the year , prepared for the next one . Since 1986 , every single Cuban has participated each year in a two-day hurricane exercise . Hurricane preparedness is part of school curricula . Citizens are engaged in this process at all levels , each person knows where to go and there is an extraordinary level of cooperation .
