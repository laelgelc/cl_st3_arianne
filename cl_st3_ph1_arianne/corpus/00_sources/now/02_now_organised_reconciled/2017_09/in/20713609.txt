<h> Committee constituted to clear confusion over ' minor minerals ' <p> Kohima , September 23 ( MExN ) : A confusion of jurisdiction has risen over who controls " minor minerals , " including " forest royalty items , " in Nagaland State . The Government of Nagaland has set up a committee to resolve the matter . <p> In January this year , the Department of Geology &amp; Mining ( DGM ) notified that as per the Nagaland Minor Mineral Concession Rules 2005 ( NMMCR ) , it will regulate ( and charge royalty for ) the mining of minor minerals in the State . However , a " confusion of jurisdiction " has cropped up between the DGM and the Department of Environment , Forest &amp; Climate Change ( DEFCC ) . <p> An Office Memorandum signed by Lhoubeilatuo Kire , Principal Secretary to the Government of Nagaland , DEFCC , on September 22 informed that the " status quo as of today shall remain in force till the confusion over jurisdiction between the statutory acts " of the DGM and DEFCC is " resolved by the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ &amp; Development Commissioner , Nagaland . " <p> Thus , the DEFCC will " continue to supervise the collection of revenue and regulation of Forest Transit Passes issued over these items by Government of Assam and Manipur , " noted the memorandum . <p> This was issued in clarification after the DGM issued a notice on January 24 , 2017 , as per the NMMCR , " for regulation of all minor minerals including stones , sand etc. " but which " include forest royalty items in Rules framed under the Nagaland Forest Act 1968 , Nagaland Jhumland Act 1970 and notified in compliance to the Supreme Court interim Orders in the WP ( 202 ) of 1995. "
