<h> The Government of Canada Recognizes the National Historic Importance of the Wrecks of HMS Erebus and HMS Terror <p> 23 hours ago <p> 03-09-2017 <p> PRN <p> GJOA HAVEN , NU , Sept. 3 , 2017 : Over one hundred and fifty years ago two British Royal Navy ships went missing in the icy waters of Canada 's Arctic , only to be recently discovered resting on the ocean floor near King William Island , also known as Qikiqtaq . <p> Today the two vessels - the HMS Erebus and HMS Terror - are being commemorated as national historic sites with a plaque unveiling ceremony held at the Umiyaqtutt Festival in Gjoa Haven , Nunavut , by Parks Canada and members of the Franklin Interim Advisory Committee . <p> By unveiling these plaques , we are commemorating not only historic sites , but the people and places of the North , and the Inuit collaboration that helped us understand the history of the Franklin expedition . <p> The locations of the vessels had been a mystery for over 150 years , after Sir John Franklin and his crew went @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Over time , Inuit traditional stories helped European searchers better understand the fate of the Franklin ships ; and that same traditional knowledge - or Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit - combined with the technology of modern searchers , finally uncovered the sunken vessels in 2014 and 2016 . <p> Both boats were found underwater largely intact . In the HMS Erebus , a stunning array of artifacts was found - like brass cannons , a cast-bronze bell , and even the handle of a sword . <p> It 's important to protect and commemorate these sites of history , and Parks Canada is working with the Franklin Interim Advisory Committee to develop an Inuit Guardians Program for the two vessels . As of September 1st 2017 , Inuit Guardians are posted at both wreck sites during periods with little ice to monitor the sites , report any unauthorized vessel traffic , and help Parks Canada ensure their protection . <p> But the landscape around the vessels also needs protection . <p> Today , the impacts of climate change are increasingly visible in the North . Eventually , the Guardians will play a key role in hosting visitors to the wreck sites to not only share the Franklin story , but to tell the story of climate change and the threat it poses to Inuit communities and their traditional way of life . <p> The discoveries of the HMS Erebus and HMS Terror showed the world that modern science and Inuit Qaujimajatuqangit can solve enormous challenges . Now , with the threat of climate change advancing , they will again have to be marshalled to an even greater degree . <p> From September 2-10 , the first annual Umiyaqtutt ( Shipwreck ) Festival will take place in Gjoa Haven , Nunavut . Focused on the theme of " Encounters along the Northwest Passage " , the week long celebration will showcase anniversary milestones of the discoveries of the Franklin expedition research missions , as well as the important role Inuit knowledge and community involvement played in the finding of the wrecks of HMS Erebus and HMS Terror . <p> Quotes <p> " Today 's designation commemorates @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and HMS Terror .
