<h> ' Effort is to only conserve ecosystem of our lakes ' <p> Q. By forcing devotees to immerse idols in artificial tanks , do n't you think you are interfering with their faith ? <p> A. We do not force or insist citizens to immerse idols in plastic tanks . We humbly request them to understand the consequences of immersion in lakes thereby creating awareness and a collaborative environment where citizens themselves come forward to use artificial tanks . In case an idol bears a red mark indicating PoP we insist that immersion should be in artificial tank according to NMC guidelines . Our effort is solely to conserve our lake ecosystems and ensure that we do not pollute the lakes to such an extent that the water body dies . <p> Q. Do n't you think clay idols can be immersed in a natural water body as it dissolves within minutes ? <p> A. Clay idols are painted and paints contain heavy metals like cadmium , chromium , lead , mercury etc which is injurious to the health of aquatic species . Many Hindu religious organizations and mandals feel that organizations like yours get active only during Hindu festivals . <p> A. Ganesh Visarjan due to sheer numbers involved is a major festival where lake water quality is adversely impacted year on year . Mankind is facing an imminent water crisis such that has not been witnessed before given the synergistic harmful effects of lake pollution , climate change and lack of adequate mitigation measures . We require more sustained efforts to ensure water sustainability , lake and river conservation and citizens have a major contribution by way of adopting measures as immersion in artificial tanks . <p> Q.
