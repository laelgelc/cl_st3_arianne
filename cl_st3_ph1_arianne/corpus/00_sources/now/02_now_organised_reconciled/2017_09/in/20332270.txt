<h> Swiss President reaffirms support for India 's fight against black money <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi ( right ) with Swiss president Doris Leuthard before the start of their meeting in New Delhi on Thursday . Photo : AP <p> New Delhi , September 2 ( IANS ) President of Switzerland Dorsi Leuthard has reaffirmed her country 's support for India 's fight against black money . <p> " The Swiss government , the federal council , is committed to support India 's fight against black money also like automatic exchange of information of fiscal matters , " Leuthard said while addressing the launch event of " 70 Years of Swiss Indian Friendship " here on Friday night . <p> " And we hope this year to have cleared the approval of that agreement , " she said . <p> India and Switzerland signed an agreement for automatic exchange of financial information in November 2016 . <p> On Thursday , following delegation-level talks headed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Leuthard , both countries agreed to enhance cooperation in the fight against tax fraud while acknowledging the need for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ standards on tax transparency . <p> A joint statement issued said that " both leaders emphasised their willingness to further enhance cooperation in the fight against tax fraud and tax evasion " . <p> Leuthard also said that trade and investment between the two countries have been growing and high-level bilateral visits have become more frequent . <p> Modi visited Switzerland in June 2016 in what was the first prime ministerial visit from India to that country in several decades . Swiss Presidents have earlier visited India in 1998 , 2003 , and 2007 . <p> " Over 200 Swiss companies are present here in India having created more than 100,000 jobs , " Leuthard said . <p> " Switzerland is number seven when it comes to trade and 11th biggest foreign investor in India , " she stated . <p> " Vice-versa , India has around 140 companies with offices in Switzerland , making Switzerland the seventh largest recipient of Indian investment . " <p> Stressing that the protection of these mutual investments is important , she said that Switzerland was working to renew the investment protection agreement with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with its European Free Trade Area ( EFTA ) partner countries for a free trade agreement with India . <p> The Swiss President also said that there were many subjects of common interest between Switzerland and India " like skilling our workforce , railways , building environment-friendly cities and communicating high-tech research to everyone " . <p> While 94-year-old Swaminathan is a close collaborator with the Swiss Development Cooperation on food security and climate change for over 10 years , Premchand is a former president of the Swiss-Indian Chamber of Commerce and is considered a strong pillar of the Indo-Swiss business community . <p> Maim , a Swiss citizen based in Bengaluru , is a co-founder of the Swiss start-up Minsh , a community messaging system and private social network . <p> Echanove and Srivastava are co-founders of the Urbz platform in Geneva and Mumbai and are engaged in projects involving architecture , design , planning , anthropology , pedagogy and technology . <p> India and Switzerland signed their friendship agreement on August 14 , 1948.
