Developing countries want the developed world to pay for their carbon emission-fuelled industrial growth , while the US and Europe want emerging economies like India to commit to sustainable growth . <p> Central to this debate is how the developed countries should " pay " and one way is for them to provide funds to middle-income and low-income nations to mitigate and adapt to climate change . That , in a nutshell , is climate finance . <p> This may sound simple , but the reality is anything but .
