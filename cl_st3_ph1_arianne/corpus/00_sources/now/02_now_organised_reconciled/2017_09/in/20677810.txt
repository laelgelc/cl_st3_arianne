<p> Billionaire and Microsoft co-founder Bill Gates feels sorry for making the Control-Alt-Delete function , used to start up Windows on computers . Gates , while revealing his dislike of the command , said if he could go back and change anything about the software that made him a multi-billionaire it would be to add the ability to perform the command using only a single key , according to International Business Times . " The IBM PC hardware keyboard only had one way that it could get a guaranteed interrupt generated . Clearly the people involved should have put another key on in order to make that work , " Gates told CNN . " If I can make one small edit I 'd make that a single key operation , " he added . Gates was at a panel discussion of Bloomberg Global Business Forum in New York City . He blamed IBM for this issue in the year 2013 .
