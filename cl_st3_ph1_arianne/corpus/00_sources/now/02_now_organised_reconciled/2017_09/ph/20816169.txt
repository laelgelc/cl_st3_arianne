Many of Metro Manila 's existing pumping stations were built in the 1970s and have become inefficient and underperforming , " the World Bank said . <p> The project aimed for completion in 2024 will be jointly implemented by the Department of Public Works and Highways and the Metro Manila Development Authority , with support from the National Housing Authority , the Social Housing Finance Corp . as well as local government units . <p> " Recurrent flooding has made life more difficult for the poorest populations who live in low-lying areas , on riverbanks , and in other danger zones . When floods occur , the capacity of people to earn a living is constrained , and many can fall back into poverty .
