<h> Long-endangered snow leopard upgraded to ' vulnerable ' status <p> The elusive snow leopard -- long considered an endangered species -- has been upgraded to " vulnerable , " international conservationists said Thursday . But experts warned the new classification does not mean they are safe . <p> The animals still face serious challenges including poaching and loss of prey in their high Himalayan habitat . <p> In this file photo , Georgina , an endangered snow leopard , watches as her new twin cubs play in their enclosure during their public debut at the Los Angeles Zoo , in California .
