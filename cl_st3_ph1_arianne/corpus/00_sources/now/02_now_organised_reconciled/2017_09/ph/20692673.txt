<h> Araullo now Kapuso <h> Araullo now Kapuso <p> BROADCAST journalist Atom Araullo returns to GMA 7 to be part of the Network 's News and Public Affairs group . <p> Araullo , who used to be part of the Kapuso Network 's primetime newscast " 24 Oras " via the segment ATOMic Sports , expressed his eagerness to be a Kapuso once again . <p> " I 'm very excited . Ang dami kong gustong gawin ( there are so many things I want to do ) , particularly in the realm of documentary production . I think this is a great place to do all of that , " he said . <p> GMA Network chairman and chief executive officer Felipe L. Gozon welcomed Araullo on his homecoming : " Because GMA 7 is known for world-class and award-winning documentaries and public affairs programs , we are excited with Atom Araullo 's transfer especially since he is known as a great broadcast journalist and documentarist , among others .
