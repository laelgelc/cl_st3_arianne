<h> Trudeau tells UN Canada has failed its indigenous people <p> Declaring that Canada is " no land of wonders , " Prime Minister Justin Trudeau told the United Nations on Thursday that his country was working to address failures in the treatment of its indigenous people . <p> Justin Trudeau , Prime Minister of Canada speaks during the 72nd session of the General Assembly at the United Nations in New York on September 21,2017 . ( AFP PHOTO / TIMOTHY A. CLARY / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> Trudeau told the UN General Assembly that Canada 's 1.2 million indigenous people had mostly known " humiliation , neglect and abuse " in a country often held up as shining example of diversity . <p> " We know that the world expects Canada to strictly adhere to international human rights standards , " said Trudeau , who has put reconciliation with first nations at the top of his reform agenda . <p> " That is what we expect of ourselves , too .
