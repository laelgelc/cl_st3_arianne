<p> GOVERNMENT offices are encouraged to lead in implementing energy efficiency and conservation measures and help the energy department in its campaign to mitigate adverse effects of climate change in the environment . <p> Artemio Habitan , Energy Efficiency and Conservation Division ( EECD ) chief of the Department of Energy , challenged government representatives during a seminar to implement government energy management program ( GEMP ) in their respective offices . <p> The GEMP was a reiteration of the Administrative Order ( AO ) No. 110 of former pres . Gloria Macapagal- Arroyo last 2004 , said Habitan during the " Seminar On Energy Efficiency and Conservation in Government Buildings in Cebu City recently . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Stipulated under AO No. 110 was for the government to undertake a program promoting the sensible use of energy resources through intensified conservation efforts and efficient utilization . <p> Under GEMP , government offices would undergo spot checking conducted by DOE and evaluated whether or not an office had energy efficiency and conservation practices .
