<h> Even past presidents <p> But Duterte is hardly the first president to use task forces . His predecessor , Benigno Aquino III , had also created separate task forces for the aftermaths of Super Typhoons Pablo and Yolanda . <p> At least two of Aquino 's task forces are active to this day , the IATF-EID ( yes , that was Aquino 's 2014 creation ) and the National Task Force on the West Philippine Sea . <p> Gloria Macapagal Arroyo also created task forces . Her Presidential Task Force on Climate Change , formed in February 2007 through an administrative order , was led by her environment secretary . <p> In 2005 , Arroyo created the National Anti-Crime Task Force to " streamline " other government efforts against criminality . <p> In the same year , she created a special task force to protect Filipino entertainers in Japan from exploitation by traffickers . <p> Presidents have turned to task forces for a reason , said University of the Philippines poltical science department chairperson Herman Kraft . <p> " Task forces are good , especially for multifaceted issues . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he told Rappler . <p> Task forces , based on how past Philippine presidents have used them , are typically comprised of various departments , like the Department of Health or the Department of Education , which should now be able to more efficiently coordinate with one another on a specific issue or goal -- say , the pandemic and protocols for schools given the health crisis . <p> They usually implement a policy crafted by another entity -- say , the Cabinet or a council . <p> " A council is a stand-alone policy-making body , like the National Security Council , for example , responsible for an entire area of concern like security , housing , finance , " said Kraft . <p> In the Duterte government 's pandemic efforts , the IATF-EID But their frequent creation is a bad sign for any government . <p> " It does not reflect well on government if task forces are constantly being set up to respond to a problem , " said Kraft . <p> This is because it 's a symptom that existing institutions , like executive agencies , commissions , maybe even entire systems like the judiciary , ca n't adequately respond to these problems . <p> The need to constantly form task forces shows an inclination towards " ad hocism " and a lack of trust or interest in institutions , said Kraft . <p> What ends up happening is that existing agencies , like the departments , have to funnel their funds into the task forces they are part of . So the department programs that the budget was intended for get defunded . <p> In the case of Duterte 's planned typhoon task force , it 's a repeat of many other typhoon task forces that could indicate shortcomings of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) , the highest coordinating body tasked to prepare for , and respond to , calamities . <p> The NDRRMC was created during the Arroyo administration and is led by the defense secretary .
