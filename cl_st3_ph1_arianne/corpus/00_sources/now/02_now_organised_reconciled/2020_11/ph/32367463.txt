<h> CCC seeks faster accreditation of DAEs <p> The Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) is seeking a faster accreditation of direct access entities ( DAEs ) to allow access to financing from the Green Climate Fund ( GCF ) that will help implement crucial climate change adaptation and mitigation interventions in the country . <p> Following the 27th Meeting of the GCF Board held virtually from Nov. 9 to Nov. 13 , the CCC welcomed the accreditation of four new accredited entities ( AEs ) or partner organizations , ranging from country organizations called DAEs , to regional and multi-national bodies . <p> AEs submit funding proposals and act as a conduit of GCF funds as they implement climate projects approved and funded by the GCF . <p> CCC Commissioner Rachel Herrera , who sits as Alternate Member in the GCF Board representing the Asia-Pacific constituency , appreciated the Board 's accreditation of the new AEs -- three of which are DAEs -- but also noted that it is still too low in enabling stronger country ownership for developing countries . <p> In the case of the Philippines , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ process of approval as DAE for GCF . <p> " We continue to express our concern that AEs spend an average of 21.1 months from application towards approval -- with one DAE taking up to 30 months , which are 30 months ' worth of time and resources lost , " Herrera said . <p> " The direct access modality is designed to help developing countries exercise ownership of finance , align these with national climate action plans , and not the least , build the needed capacity within our national institutions for climate projects .
