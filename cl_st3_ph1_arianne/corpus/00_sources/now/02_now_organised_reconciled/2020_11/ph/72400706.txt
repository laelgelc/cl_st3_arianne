<p> An official of the House of Representatives has called for the allocation of additional funds for the creation of permanent , disaster-resilient evacuation centers in the wake of the recent catastrophic devastation of Super Typhoon Rolly in Bicol region . <p> House Assistant Majority Floor Leader Fidel Nograles ( Rizal , 2nd district ) said given that the country experiences strong typhoons regularly , having permanent evacuation centers rather than make-shift ones should be top priority . <p> Nograles stressed with the effects of climate change now manifesting , there is no choice now butto deal with the reality that at least once a year , and the country " faces a disaster of a scale that requires evacuation . " <p> In the past , education has had to take a backseat since many evacuation centers are set up in schools , according to Nograles . <p> The lawmaker from Rizal province added that the Covid-19 pandemic has emphasized the need for permanent evacuation centers , with quarantine facilities taking up the space usually occupied by the former . <p> He noted that even the Department of Education @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with the latter calling on government not to use schools as quarantine facilities and evacuation centers at the same time . <p> " If there are quarantine areas for COVID in schools , then it will be used as an evacuation center , I do n't think that should be encouraged , " Education Secretary Leonor Brinones said during a recent ' Laging Handa ' Public Briefing . <p> Nograles also said the pandemic increases the pressure to discard the usual and de-humanizing practice of cramming a large number of people in evacuation centers . <p> The permanent facilities , besides being disaster-resilient , should have access to running water , and should adopt engineering solutions that would prevent the spread of disease while maximizing space , according to the neophyte solon .
