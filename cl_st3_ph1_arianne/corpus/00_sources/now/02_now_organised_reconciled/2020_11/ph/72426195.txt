Stella Quimbo filed House Bill No. 8031 or the Bayanihan to Arise as One Act , which provides some ? 400 billion to help the country bounce back . It is broken down as follows : <p> - ? 100 billion for worker subsidies <p> - ? 100 billion for capacity building of impacted sectors <p> - ? 90 billion for additional social amelioration to affected households ( P20 billion of which is targeted to households in typhoon-devasted areas ) <p> - ? 50 billion for the rehabilitation of typhoon-affected areas <p> - ? 30 billion for assistance to displaced workers <p> - ? 25 billion for procurement of COVID-19 vaccines and medications <p> - ? 3 billion for internet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> - ? 2 billion for internet allowances of college instructors and students <p> The bill also proposes to extend the effectivity of Bayanihan to Recover as One Act or Bayanihan 2 until June 4 next year to ensure its full implementation . <p> " Government must also anticipate the need for additional response measures , given that Bayanihan II shall cease to be in force and effect by 19 December of the current year while the effects of the pandemic , and the virus itself are expected to prevail well into 2021 , " read the bill 's explanatory note . <p> Moreover , the bill mandates the National Economic Development Authority to submit to Congress a long-term economic resilience plan . <p> The plan shall include the required investments in the health sector in the next 10 years , the required investment in digital infrastructure to improve connectivity , the required database to ensure responsiveness of social protection programs of the government for vulnerable households , and the required key climate change adaptation and mitigation investments . <p> " Uncertainty is the primary effect this pandemic has on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
