<p> The Philippine Commission on Human Rights ( CHR ) will soon release an exhaustive report on companies it has found to be morally liable for the ill-effects of climate change . <p> A first case of its kind in the world to frame climate justice as a human rights issue , the CHR worked within the limited parameters of trying to impose exact accountability from global companies and even the governments that enable them . <p> The CHR report would not be legally binding on anyone , but the CHR hopes it can give birth to a string of new cases all over the world . <p> There are many difficulties in attaining climate justice within the existing frameworks -- just some of them involving the doctrine of political issue , and a global consensus that many of our economies still rely on fossil fuel . <p> How do we use domestic and international law mechanisms to save the world ? <p> Justice reporter Lian Buan spoke with CHR Commissioner Roberto Cadiz for this episode .
