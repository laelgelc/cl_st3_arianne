<h> Manila bats for revival of ' nilad ' plant population <p> MANILA -- The " nilad " plant where the name " Manila " came from will soon repopulate the city through a partnership between the city government and the Department of Environment and Natural Resources and Ecosystems Research and Development Bureau ( DENR-ERDB ) . <p> Manila Mayor Francisco ' Isko Moreno ' Domagoso in a meeting with DENR-ERDB on Tuesday agreed to launch the " Nilad for Maynila " Project . <p> The project that will bring back the nilad plant in Manila aims to re-green a part of the Manila Bay . <p> The toponym or place name of Manila is said to be coined from the abundance of the stalky-rice plant that features flowers of bright white and yellow . <p> The nilad plant is said to be abundant in the city before and people say it is from the phrase " may nilad " meaning " where nilad is " . <p> A quick survey identified four potential sites where the nilad plant will be initially planted . <p> These are the areas of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , an area adjacent to the Manila Yacht Club , and on the islands along the Manila Baywalk . <p> Domagoso ordered Manila Department of Public Service director Kenneth Amurao to deploy Team Mandaragat , Baseco Beach Warriors , and Estero Rangers to assist the city government in planting and protecting the nilad population in the identified locations . <p> The nilad plant , with scientific name Scyphiphora Hydrophyllacea , is a mangrove species that grew abundantly along Manila Bay and Pasig River during the early times . <p> Mangroves help people weather the impacts of climate change and help mitigate its causes . <p> They have the capacity to take more carbon out of the atmosphere than terrestrial forests .
