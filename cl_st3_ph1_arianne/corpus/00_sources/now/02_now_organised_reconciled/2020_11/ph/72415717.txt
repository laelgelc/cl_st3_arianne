<p> Sen. Grace Poe organized a team of volunteers to pack and send relief goods to survivors in Cagayan Valley , one of the hardest-hit regions by Typhoon Ulysses . <p> " We need to extend as much help as we can now because this is the most critical time for survivors of the calamity . Every donation that reaches them sends the message that they have not been forgotten and that help is on its way , " said Poe . <p> Cagayan Valley , home of the 4,100-sq-km Magat Dam , became a catch basin as water from provinces affected by Typhoon Ulysses flowed into the dam and left dam operators in a bind . Aerial photos of Cagayan Valley left the area unrecognizable in what has been described as the worst flood to hit the region in 40 years . <p> Volunteers from Poe 's office distributed bags filled with rice , sardines , vitamins , and calamansi juice in Ilagan City and the Municipality of Tumauini in Isabela , as well as the far reaches of Tuguegarao City .
