Sea-level increase reached 5 mm per year in several oceanic areas surrounding the continent and exceeded 5 mm per year in the south-western Indian Ocean from Madagascar eastward towards and beyond Mauritius . This is more than the average global sea-level rise of 3-4 mm per year . <p> Coastal degradation and erosion is also a major challenge , especially in West Africa . About 56% of the coastlines in Benin , Cote d'Ivoire , Senegal and Togo are eroding and this is expected to worsen in the future .
