However , if the life of the land teaches anything , it is the need to look at the long term . " <p> He went on to praise " the strength of our society : its resourcefulness , its compassion and its stoical determination " and how they can be engaged to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , such as global warming , climate change and the heartbreaking loss of biodiversity . " <p> And Charles warned there is a " short window of opportunity " to " rebuild " the planet for future generations , after praising new initiatives and technological advancements such as wool insulation for houses and the National Garden Scheme , which raised money with online videos . <p> He wrote : " Like the threatened red squirrels collecting their hazelnuts or jays their seeds and acorns , we must be wise and far-sighted . <p> " We have a very short window of opportunity , which must not be squandered , in which to seize something good from this crisis and , as we rebuild , to put Nature , our planet and our children and grandchildren first .
