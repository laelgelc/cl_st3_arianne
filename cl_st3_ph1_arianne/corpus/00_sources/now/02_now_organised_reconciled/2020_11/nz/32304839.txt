<p> A competition where budding financial analysts decide whether to buy , sell or hold stocks in a publicly traded company has been won by University of Otago students . <p> The Otago team won the New Zealand edition of the 2020 CFA ( Chartered Financial Analysts ) Institute Research Challenge and will compete in the Asia Pacific regional competition next year . <p> All eight of the country 's universities competed . It is the second time Otago has won , the first being in 2016 . <p> The Otago students , coached by the head of the university 's accounting and finance department , Dr Helen Roberts , spent 12 weeks studying NZX-listed agribusiness Scales before valuing it and then defending that evaluation . <p> They recommended a hold on the apple orchardist 's stocks with a target price of $5.01 a share . <p> The team presented on their valuation case for 10 minutes and then faced 10 minutes of questions from the judging panel . <p> Rosie Jerram was one of the team members and won an additional prize for her contest speech delivery . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ There are some pretty significant risks on the horizon for Scales with climate change risk as well as RSE workers not being let into the country , so that is pretty huge for the potential implications of reducing their crop . " <p> Ms Jerram said she enjoyed learning about Scales and the agribusiness industry . <p> " We were learning about how some of those industry dynamics work , particularly trade with China , how Covid has affected markets ... and how businesses can go about being resilient to shocks like Covid . <p> " It 's a pretty unique experience to be able to value a company at this point in your studies . It 's quite different to what we get to learn in class . " <p> The University of Otago team also received support from Contact Energy 's senior corporate finance analyst , Tim Armour.
