<h> Experience Arjuna Oakes ' Live Session For ' Watch The World Burn ' <p> Chris Cudby / Wednesday 4th November , 2020 11:27AM <p> While the world remains mesmerised by today 's election in the US , Arjuna Oakes unveils a literally scorching live session for the Poneke-based singer and multi-instrumentalist 's new single ' Watch The World Burn ' . Tackling the theme of overcoming complacency via being able to recognise issues in our everyday lives instead of simply ignoring / dismissing them , specifically the urgent topic of climate change , Oakes ' song is as on-point as it gets for today 's turbulent times . The soulful and groove-packed tune is drawn from his forthcoming Recovery Pt.1 EP releasing on the 20th of this month , featuring live in-studio performances by Oakes and band -- delving into " the process of healing in relation to the planet , ourselves , and our society in the current moment " .
