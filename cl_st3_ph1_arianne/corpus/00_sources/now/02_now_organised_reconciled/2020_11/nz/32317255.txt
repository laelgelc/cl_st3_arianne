<h> CHANGING CLIMATE <p> Droughts , fires , floods , farmers facing more pests and diseases , livestock stressed by heatwaves and a tide which keeps creeping up against the coast . <p> This is how experts anticipate climate change will look in Tairawhiti and Hawke 's Bay over the next 80 years . <p> A new report by the National Institute for Water and Atmospheric Research ( Niwa ) explores possible impacts of climate change for the two east coast regions , outlining the future with a scenario of continuing high emissions , and alternatively what it could look like if the world took effective action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Tairawhiti and Hawke 's Bay are likely to see more " extreme hot days " , less frost days and a shift to more extreme rainfall events . <p>
