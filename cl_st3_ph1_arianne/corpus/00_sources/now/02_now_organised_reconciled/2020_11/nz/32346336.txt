<h> $1m extra a year for flood scheme <p> A decade-long construction programme to upgrade the Waipaoa Flood Control Scheme received a $7.5 million boost from the Government 's Covid recovery fund in August and next year the council will seek to increase its budgets for this project by about $1m a year , to be able to deliver the enhanced protection scheme by 2031 . <p> An update report for councillors last week stated that $25m was sought from Crown Infrastructure Partners for this project , and the $7.5m granted allows " for an accelerated delivery programme of stopbank construction works over the next 2.5 years " ; " This will ensure that the benefits related to flood protection are realised earlier and will also provide additional jobs and local economic stimulus .
