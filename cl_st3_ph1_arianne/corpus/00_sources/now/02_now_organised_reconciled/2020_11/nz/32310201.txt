<h> Waiting for a meaningful plan <p> In the lead-up to this by-election l have tried to present myself as a candidate of choice as opposed to any of the other options available to voters because l believe that this election or any other for that matter , should be a battle of ideas . <p> In order to do that l have felt the need to talk clearly and factually about the economic and social problems this region faces in the immediate and long term . <p> In the process , I have been very critical of some of Council 's actions and/or lack of action in a number of key areas of their responsibility . <p> While identifying specific things that could be done a lot better , l have recognised my own responsibility to offer alternatives to Council 's way of doing things . <p> I suppose we are talking about a " VISION " of where we want this region to be in 10-20 years time and providing the detail of how we can achieve those goals using our limited resources . <p> The Council is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that we should follow . <p> I dispute that mainly because their vision is a Clayton 's variety -- it does n't exist ! <p> Most people would expect to find evidence of Council 's long term planning in the LTP which is supposed to be a document of " intent " -- full of ideas about how our leaders propose to negotiate the problems associated with climate change and the likely economic straitjacket we will be forced to operate under for the foreseeable future -- mainly due to the ramifications of Government 's Covid strategy .
