<h> Council seeks feedback on rules on housing choice <p> As Tauranga City Council looks at ways to cater for a continuously growing population , calls are being made for feedback on proposed City Plan rule changes . <p> A statement from council says some of the City Plan rules need to change to provide more housing capacity and to enable development and redevelopment to be managed in a manner that reduces the risk to property and lives . <p> Tauranga City Council is asking the community for feedback on three proposed plan changes by 18 December . <p> " Our city 's population is not only growing fast , it is also changing overtime -- we are getting older , and families are getting smaller , " says Urban Form and Transport Committee chair Larry Baldock . <p> " So we do n't all need a three or four bedroom house , which is generally what is being built in Tauranga right now .
