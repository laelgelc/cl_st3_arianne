<p> Press Release -- Kapiti Coast District Council Starting this week , through to Wednesday 9 December , Kapiti Coast District Council is asking our communities what matters most to them ahead of developing its Long-term Plan 2021-2041 . <p> Mayor K Gurunathan said hearing what 's most important to our communities in the early stages of developing the Council 's Long-term Plan 2021-2041 is of critical importance . <p> " Responses will help inform decisions about where to focus Council activity over the next twenty years . " <p> The Council 's Long-term Plan sets out the mahi the Council intends to do over the next twenty years , how much things are likely to cost , and how the Council plans to pay for it . The Council is required to review its Plan every three years . <p> " The Council has worked hard over the past three years to get its finances in order so it can progress the development people want to see in the district , " said the Mayor . <p> " It 's an interesting time to be planning ahead .
