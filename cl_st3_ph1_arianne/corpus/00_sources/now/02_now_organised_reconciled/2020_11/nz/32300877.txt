<p> Ratepayers have forked out around $1 billion in the last decade for local councils to settle building disputes , according to a study commissioned by the Ministry of Business , Innovation and Employment ( MBIE ) . <p> The leaky home saga in particular has seen building companies go bust , leaving local councils to settle the tab with property owners . <p> Fear of being left out of pocket has prompted councils to be risk-adverse when issuing building consents , slowing much-needed development . <p> Ratepayers have been lumped with this bill because New Zealand uses ' joint and several liability ' to allocate liability when multiple parties are responsible for the same loss . This means a claimant can recover damages from any or all of the parties that caused the loss .
