<h> Best NZ kids ' books of 2020 <p> Right now in Aotearoa , children 's books are following three distinct trends : anxiety , climate change and Maori stories/books in te reo Maori . <p> Thefirst two -- anxiety and climate change -- are international trends and , while they began prior to 2020 , this year has seen a huge number of books released along these themes . <p> While the third trend is unique to Aotearoa , it is one that has been growing for some time .
