<h> Should electric cars be taxed like petrol cars ? <p> Words : Matthew Hansen <p> 11 Nov 2020 <p> It 's fast becoming an age-old question of sorts , particularly in countries like New Zealand that are somewhat ahead of the EV curve and in countries like Australia that are in danger of lagging behind . <p> Most countries aiming to push electrification in order to shrink the environmental impact of their respective fleet decide to at least relax the taxes surrounding electric cars , in order to incentivise the models and make them more enticing for the public to adopt . In New Zealand , both the Labour Government and the National Party opposition are in support of incentivising EVs , although the two parties tend to do things differently . In other nations , EVs can be heavily subsidised via significant discounts . <p> A storm of parliamentary disagreement appears to be brewing in Australia , however , following the South Australian Government 's decision to draft in a new road user charge tax for low and zero-emission vehicles .
