<h> Climate emergency being recognised <p> The Government will declare a climate emergency next week , it was confirmed during the opening day of our 53rd Parliament yesterday . <p> A bid to make this declaration last year went nowhere as it was opposed by NZ First . It will sail through now with Labour 's majority and support from the Greens -- and hopefully other parties too .
