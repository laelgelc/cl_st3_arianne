Boss who spoke on the theme ' Reclaiming Nigeria 's Laggard Livestock Subsector : A Modern Dairy Value Chain In Perspectives ' , noted that available statistics indicate that Nigeria produces 526,000 litres of Dairy products as against the demand for about 1.2 million litres per annum . <p> The solution to this he said is to mount a sustainable campaign for the production of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
