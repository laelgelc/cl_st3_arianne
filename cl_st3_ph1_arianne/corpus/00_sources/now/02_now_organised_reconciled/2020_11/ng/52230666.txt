<h> Fake News Capable Of Destroying Nigeria 's Democracy , NPC Boss Warns <p> ABUJA- Mr. Francis Nwosu , Executive Secretary of Nigerian Press Council ( NPC ) has warned that fake news is capable of destroying Nigeria 's democracy , if left unchecked . <p> Nwosu who stated this at the weekend in Abuja at the 4th Edition of the Open Forum on Agricultural Biotechnology ( OFAB ) Nigeria Chapter , Media Award , urged journalists to be " perpetually involved in the establishment and promotion of a culture of factual reporting on issues . " <p> He cautioned that fake news , if left unchecked , is capable of threatening the foundations of the country 's democracy , fostering distrust in institutions , undermining individuals ' right to truth and potentially increasing social polarisation , among other negative impacts . <p> In a paper entitled , " Fake News : Why Journalists Must Stay With Science , " he pointed out that , " Fake news is rustled up by individuals or groups to deceive the public , cause ill feelings , influence people 's views , push @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ policy , or disparage or malign a person 's character . <p> " It is indeed written and published with the intent to mislead in order to damage an entity , agency , person , and/or gain financially or politically , using sensationalist , dishonest , or outright fabricated headlines to increase readership . " <p> He noted that fake news is both anti-ethical to journalism profession and injurious to the polity . <p> " A cursory look at our polity will portray one at the mercy of fake news . <p> " Like in some other climes , the issue of fake news has become a serious challenge in our country . <p> " A school of thought had postulated that there has always been fake news . <p> " The difference today is that social media platforms have enabled fake news to spread widely and more quickly than ever before . <p> " While social media is the biggest source of fake news , the media give prominence to it through publication of fictitious stories .
