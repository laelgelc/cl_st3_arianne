There is evidence that the process of coastal urbanization is accelerating the increase in flood risk . With safe areas already occupied , new settlements and developments are occurring increasingly in high-risk areas . As spatial planning and infrastructure investments ( such as drainage systems ) struggle to keep up with the pace of urbanization , risks build up and are locked in . <p> In the coming years , land subsidence , rapid coastal urbanization and climate change could further increase flood risks .
