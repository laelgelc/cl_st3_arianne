<h> Climate crisis will be like a permanent pandemic : Deloitte <p> The economic effects of climate change will be more severe than those of the pandemic by 2070 and will continue indefinitely unless decisive action is taken to decarbonise the Australian economy , modelling by Deloitte Access Economics has found . <p> The modelling finds a business-as-usual approach to climate change could result in an economy that is 6 per cent smaller in 2070 than baseline assumptions predict it will be . <p> In that world , which assumes global temperatures will have risen by more than 3 degrees , there will be 880,000 fewer jobs , and annual gross domestic product will be $3.4 billion less than it would be in a world without climate change . <p> The Deloitte report warns unchecked climate change could end the Australian way of life .
