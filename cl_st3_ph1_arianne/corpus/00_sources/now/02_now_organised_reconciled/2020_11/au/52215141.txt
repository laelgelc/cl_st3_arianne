<h> LETTERS : The hidden cost of climate change <p> THE EFFECTS of climate change are being seen and felt across Australia ; bush fires , droughts , severe storm events , floods and coral bleaching are now the new norm . <p> But there is another impact that has yet to garner much media attention ; the escalating costs of insurance . <p> Insurance companies are not in the business to lose money . But as more properties are damaged by severe storms , floods and bush fires , they are paying out more and more money . <p> At this year 's AGM , Suncorp 's CEO mentioned that in 2006 , Suncorp 's " Natural Hazards " allowance was $68 million . This year , 2020 , it was $950 million . <p> Are you still wondering why your insurance premiums are going up ? <p> Perhaps a more insidious problem is known as " risk avoidance " , a polite way of insurance companies saying they will not insure you at any cost . <p> It is because of " risk avoidance " , that we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of " red zones " , areas where buildings are uninsurable due to the high risk of a natural disaster . <p> Needless to say , uninsurable properties have little to no market value . <p> This should not come as a surprise . For decades scientists have been telling us that climate change will bring on an increase in the number and severity of natural disasters . <p> While insurance companies have been listening , our government has chosen to ignore the science , continue to support the fossil fuel industry and refuse to develop any plan to reduce our carbon emissions . <p> And now we are all paying the price through huge insurance premiums and uninsurable properties . <p> There are no easy solutions . No doubt , the way forward will be complicated and involve a collaboration between government , insurance companies , the building industry , developers and property owners . <p> In the meantime , there is one thing we know for certain ; if we continue with business as usual , the impacts of climate change will only increase in the near future @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We must work to immediately reduce our carbon emissions . <p> The cost of mitigating climate change now is far , far cheaper than paying for it in the future . <p> LPMC .
