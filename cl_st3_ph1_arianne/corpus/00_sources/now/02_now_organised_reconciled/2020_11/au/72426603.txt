<p> UNSW Sydney will lead an international coalition of top climate research universities when it issues its first declaration today ahead of the G20 Summit on 21 and 22 November 2020 . <p> The International Universities Climate Alliance member universities span all populated continents , representing one-third of the 100 highest performing climate research universities and a quarter of the top 100 environmental research universities worldwide . <p> Its Declaration implores world leaders to use the post-COVID recovery to implement measures to counteract climate change , warning that failure to do so will lock in catastrophic consequences for generations to come .
