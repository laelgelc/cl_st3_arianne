<h> Pitch Wrap : Foxtel , Dulux , Great Barrier Reef Foundation <p> It 's hard to keep track of the account wins and losses across the industry . The new appointment sees Foxtel expand its existing relationship with Mindshare who has been its media agency of record since 2014 . <p> DuluxDulux has appointed DDB Melbourne as its sole creative and strategy agency following a competitive pitch .
