<p> MAGNA and Twitter have teamed up to release the Impact of Culture : What it Means for Brands Today study , which seeks to understand consumer perception of a brand 's involvement with culture . <p> The study found that Aussies not only want but expect brands to be involved in culture and those that do benefit in a big way . The study showed a brand 's involvement in culture plays an important role in shaping consumer 's purchase decisions .
