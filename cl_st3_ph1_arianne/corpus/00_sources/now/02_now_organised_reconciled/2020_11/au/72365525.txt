<p> A new report from Deloitte Access Economics suggests that the Australian economy could be up to $3.4 trillion worse off by 2070 if it and other countries fail to act on global warming , and could miss out on more than 250,000 new jobs that could be created through a transition to zero net emissions . <p> In its new report , Deloitte Access Economics warns that the impacts of global warming on the Australian economy , based on the world 's current trajectory , could dwarf the impacts currently being experienced due to the Covid-19 pandemic , and that industries that have been hit hardest by Covid-19 lockdowns are also some of the most vulnerable to the impacts of climate change . <p> Deloitte 's analysis is based on a scenario where the world warms by more than 3 degrees by 2070 , a trajectory that would see a reduction in Australia 's economic growth of around 3 per cent per year , and create 880,000 fewer jobs . <p> The warning is a potent one for Australia , which has sought to hide under the coat-tails of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for its go-slow response to climate change .
