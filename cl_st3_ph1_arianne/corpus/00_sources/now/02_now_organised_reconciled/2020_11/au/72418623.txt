<p> Last week San Francisco became the latest city to ban natural gas in new buildings . <p> The legislation will see all new construction , other than restaurants , use electric power only from June 2021 , to cut greenhouse gas emissions . <p> San Francisco has now joined other US cities in banning natural gas in new homes . The move is in stark contrast to the direction of energy policy in Australia , where the Morrison government seems stuck in reverse : spruiking a gas-led economic recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> Natural gas provides about 26% of energy consumed in Australia -- but it 's clearly on the way out . It 's time for a serious rethink on the way many of us cook and heat our homes . <h> Cutting out gas <p> San Francisco is rapidly increasing renewable-powered electricity to meet its target of 100% clean energy by 2030 .
