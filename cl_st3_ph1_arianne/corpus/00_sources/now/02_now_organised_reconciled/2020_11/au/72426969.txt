<p> None of us has a memory of internal European borders . Instead of collecting national stamps as children , we collected the first euros with different symbols of European capitals . Our generation has been thoroughly shaped by a united Europe . <p> But many young Europeans today are disappointed and frustrated by the European Union 's failure to fulfill its implied promises . The alarmingly high youth unemployment rates of 25-40% in countries such as Italy , Greece , Spain and even Sweden provide more than sufficient reason for many to question the EU 's worth . Moreover , despite living in an era of peace and relative prosperity , our generation has grown up amid a multitude of crises : financial , migration , climate and now the health and economic crises caused by Covid-19 . <p> While the coronavirus was claiming its first lives in Bergamo , Italy , in late February 2020 , people were still singing at apr ? s-ski parties in the Austrian resort of Ischgl and celebrating Carnival in Cologne .
