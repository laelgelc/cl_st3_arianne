<h> City of Wanneroo reviews special rural rule on solar panels <h> Topics <p> A QUIRK in planning rules that required some homeowners to get permission for solar panels is under review . <p> A couple hoping to install solar panels contacted the City of Wanneroo and were told they needed development approval in the special rural zone , despite it not being required in other zones . <p> They were told that under District Planning Scheme 2 , there were no exemptions for single house additions in the special rural zone , so residents had to lodge development applications with full architectural plans . <p> Several neighbouring properties had already installed solar panels without being aware of the requirement . <p> The City 's website identified what approvals were required for a list of additions and structures such as carports , patios , shade sails and retaining walls , but did not mention solar panels so few people would have thought permission was needed . <p> After the couple raised " red tape " concerns with the Times and a Wanneroo councillor , the City determined that development approval @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ been cancelled and fees are being refunded , " planning and sustainability director Mark Dickson said . <p> " The City 's procedures and information regarding residential rooftop solar panels have been reviewed in the light of this enquiry . <p> " In line with the City 's approach to the environment , the City remains highly supportive of residents installing solar systems in appropriate locations . " <p> The couple was pleased with the outcome and looked forward to having lower power bills after their system was installed this month . <p> The City of Joondalup does not require development approval or building permits for solar panels on single houses and grouped dwellings . <p> Approval and permits are necessary however to install solar panels on multiple dwellings and commercial buildings . <p> Wanneroo councillors are due to consider a report on the City 's draft climate change strategy at their November 17 meeting , which highlights investment in solar panel systems on council buildings to reduce reliance on non-renewable electricity .
