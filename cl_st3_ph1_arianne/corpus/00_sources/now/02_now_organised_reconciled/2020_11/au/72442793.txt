<h> Churchlands PS busy with environmental projects with $2000 grant <h> Topics <p> CHURCHLANDS Primary School students have been busy with their environmental sustainability projects , with City of Stirling Mayor Mark Irwin visiting them last week . <p> The school received a $2000 grant as part of the City Living Green program , to buy native trees and shrubs . <p> Year 5 students who are the school 's environmental officers planted the native trees and shrubs during an overnight camp . <p> Camera IconChurchlands PS Credit : Supplied <p> The students also learnt about wildlife and had a visit from WA naturalist Eric McCrum . <p> " I love being a school environmental officer because I get to help the planet and learn how to care for animals and nature , " Year 5 student Sam Wall said . <p> The grant was also used to establish a program for the school to audit its utilities and set goals to reduce water , energy and waste consumption . <p> Churchlands PS sustainability coordinator Sheree Samsa said future projects include displaying QR codes next to the plants in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ipads and establishing a frog habitat . <p> " Kids really do want to make a difference and do their bit to help the environment , " she said . <p> " Giving them a voice and an opportunity to make a difference is really important and a way to negate all the doom and gloom they hear about climate change " .
