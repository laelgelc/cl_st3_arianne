<p> You 're invited to join us here on Tuesday evening to watch live as the recipients of Queensland 's 2021 Australian of the Year Awards are announced . The awards presentation , in the presence of Premier Annastacia Palaszczuk , is scheduled to begin at 6.30pm AEDT and run for about an hour . The virtual ceremony will be hosted by ABC Radio Brisbane breakfast presenters Craig Zonca and Loretta Ryan and streamed live on this website . Publisher ACM , official media partner of the 2021 Australian of the Year Awards , will host the livestream on the websites listed below .
