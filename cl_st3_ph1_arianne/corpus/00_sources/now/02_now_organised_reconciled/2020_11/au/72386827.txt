<h> Societal implications of COVID-19 : Free webinar <p> The COVID-19 pandemic has resulted in a stressful 2020 for many Australians , but there are lessons we can learn from these experiences to address future challenges , says USC psychology lecturer Dr Jacob Keech . <p> Dr Keech will share early insights from his current study in a free public webinar ' Societal Implications of COVID-19 ' on Thursday 12 November at 6pm , as part of USC 's ' Research Unmasked ' series . <p> " We are conducting a longitudinal study into the stress and coping behaviours of people in the United States and Australia over four months during the pandemic , " Dr Keech said . <p> " Experiences of the COVID-19 pandemic have differed between Australia and the United States and examining trends in stress and coping between countries can provide valuable information . " <p> Dr Keech will also share strategies for managing longer-term stress , and the benefits of people consciously changing their own mindset , based on his previous research . <p> Also on the panel will be Professor of Urban Design and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from how we coped with the COVID-19 pandemic could inform how we face climate change challenges , and Dr Natalie Watson-Brown , who will discuss her research into changed driver behaviour during the pandemic lockdown period . <p> Event organiser Dr Gemma Read said that university researchers were playing a key role in using information gathered during the pandemic across multiple fields -- from psychology to town planning -- to prepare for the future . <p> " We do n't want to miss learning these lessons , " Dr Read said . <p> " The pandemic is likely to have far-reaching effects on our behaviour and the way we live , but what we can take from it is how we can be more prepared in future to deal with major stressors in future .
