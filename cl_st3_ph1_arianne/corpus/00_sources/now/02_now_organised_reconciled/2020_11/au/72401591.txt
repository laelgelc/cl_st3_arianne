<p> In an upside down year like 2020 , it does n't take much to get Larry Fink excited . The chief executive and co-founder of the $US10 trillion ( $13.7 trillion ) fund manager BlackRock has returned to the office two or three days a week , and is thrilled to have that human contact again . <p> " I find it exhilarating , " he said in a video interview for the Financial Services Council 's inaugural investment conference that started on Monday morning . <p> Like many business leaders , Fink ca n't shake the lingering questions about whether working from home in a time of pandemic might hurt corporate culture and innovation . But at the same time , he says BlackRock has n't missed a beat , both in terms of how employees have managed the transition to working from home , and its performance . <p> " We 've never had more alpha generation for our clients , " Fink says . <p> The firm 's third quarter earnings released last month showed a 24 per cent increase in earnings , and 18 per cent increase in revenue . Net inflows for the September quarter also soared 6 per cent to $US129 billion , the best rate of growth in two years . <p> Fink says that while he is an environmentalist , his view on where BlackRock needs to shift comes from his position as a capitalist . <p> While he was moved by a series of trips he took last year -- including one to the Great Barrier Reef , where he was shocked by the extent of coral bleaching -- it was the prevalence of climate-related concerns in client meetings that really led him to push BlackRock towards being a leader on climate . <p> " There is no question in my mind the frequency of events is giving us real evidence that climate change is investment risk , " Fink says . <p> " No company should be surprised about how this is evolving and changing . We are going to see a seismic reallocation of capital . " <p> Indeed , while there has been justifiable criticism of the way BlackRock 's views on ESG translate into its action , the reallocation seems to be under way among the firm 's clients . <p> Fink says allocations to strategies with a climate focus have doubled this year and asset owners , particularly with a long-term focus , place more importance on environmental , social and governance matters . <p> " We 've been rewarded with more share of wallet because of our voice .
