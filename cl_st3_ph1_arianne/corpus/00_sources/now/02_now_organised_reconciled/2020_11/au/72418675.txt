<p> An international coalition of climate research universities has formed to implore G20 countries to prioritise climate action as part of their post-COVID recoveries . The International Universities Climate Alliance , founded and led by University of NSW , is comprised of almost 50 of the world 's top-rated universities for climate and environment research . The universities , which span every liveable continent , have banded together ahead of the annual G20 summit on Friday and Saturday to create a resource that aims to support world leaders and industry in planning for and responding to climate change . As one of the countries most exposed to the worst impacts of climate change , Australia should be at the forefront of the fight to reduce emissions , UNSW Scientia Professor Matthew England said . " With G20 member countries representing nearly 80 per cent of global CO2 emissions and 85 per cent of global GDP , they have the obligation , the opportunity , and the means to lead global systemic change , " he said .
