<p> Australia 's political class is a mess on energy policy . The Greens argue it should be illegal to burn coal while the Nationals insist climate change is a hoax , and anyone who tries to do something about it is a communist . In the middle are the Liberals , who 've stopped hugging coal and started sucking up to gas , and the Labor Party , who do n't know where they stand . There 's one technology that could break through the noise , with Tassie set to capitalise : green hydrogen . Water 's two parts hydrogen , one part oxygen . You send an electric current through water , you can separate out the hydrogen . If the electricity you 're sending through is renewable , you 're using sun and wind to turn water into fuel . It can replace coal , oil and gas , and do it with zero emissions . Japan and Korea are all for it and independent experts predict the market 's set to grow 1,200 per cent in the next five years .
