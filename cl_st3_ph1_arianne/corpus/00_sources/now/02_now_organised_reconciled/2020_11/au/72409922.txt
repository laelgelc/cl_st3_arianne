<p> " Boards have been challenged in a way they have not been for decades , " says Australian Institute of Company Directors CEO Angus Armour . " If you compound a recession with a pandemic and a catastrophic natural event , it 's quite a list of challenges . On top of that , whether companies are in financial services or resources , we have seen outcomes that have fallen short of what the community expected , " Armour says . <p> In many ways , boards have responded " heroically " to some of the challenges , argues Armour .
