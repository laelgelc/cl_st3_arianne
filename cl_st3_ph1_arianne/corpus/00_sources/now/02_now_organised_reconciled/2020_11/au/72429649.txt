Colin Sutton , Newtown <p> When three or more economists are gathered together and support with wild enthusiasm a brave new tax revision , it pays the community to look closely . Under present arrangements , most people effectively borrow an amount that covers the purchase price of their property , including stamp duty . This relatively small amount , given the overall price , is then paid back over a particular period , usually 25 years , at one of the lowest retail interest rates available . A land tax , by way of contrast , would involve an annual amount paid by the purchaser for as long as they own the property , determined at the will of the government of the day , out of after-tax earnings .
