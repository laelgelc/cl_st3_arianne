<h> The soap opera that sends Jess Scully to sleep <h> JESS SCULLY <p> Author , deputy lord mayor of the City of Sydney <p> What I 'm watching : A TV series called The Comedy Store . It 's on Stan . It 's a documentary about the Comedy Store in LA , which is like the birthplace of comedy from the ' 70s , ' 80s , ' 90s , and today . One of my ways to decompress when meetings and the bathtime/bedtime routine is over is to do the washing-up and watch stand-up comedy . What you learn from this documentary is how hard comedians work . It 's a mastery of language and timing and connecting with the audience and really honing it down to the essence of the joke and the moment . <p> Jess Scully has been listening to the Teeny Tiny Stevies with her young daughter , watching The Comedy Club and reading graphic novel The Grot .
