<h> Climate Warnings on Gas Pumps Will Soon Appear in the First U.S. City <h> Dharna Noor <p> Climate scientists have been warning about the dangers of using oil and gas for decades . Now , one U.S. city is slapping those warnings where they 'll be seen all the time . <p> Cambridge , Massachusetts is placing cigarette-style cautionary labels onto gas station pumps , per an ordinance the city passed in January . Officials hope the move will help consumers better understand the risks associated with the continued use of fossil-based fuels like diesel and gasoline . <p> The city 's Climate Action Plan includes a goal of reaching citywide carbon neutrality by 2050 by lowering emissions by 80% and offsetting the remainder . This one small piece which could help ensure it gets there . <p> " The fight to reverse climate change requires that everyone take action to change their behaviour , and the City must underscore the fact that each individual 's behaviour can make an impact on the environment and on public health , " the ordinance says . <p> The ordinance is the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the country .
