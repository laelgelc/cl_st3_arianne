Authority to rule comes from God . As I read the last words in the last chapter of Ian Bersten 's book , I could not but see so clearly the theme he has woven in his publication : the Liberals rule by divine right . They can do no wrong . <p> Bersten makes clear his message in the book : Australia is Liberal , for the Liberals , by the Liberals and so we all shout God Save the Queen .
