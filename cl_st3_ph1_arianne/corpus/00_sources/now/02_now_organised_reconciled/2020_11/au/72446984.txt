<h> American Utopia is so good I wanted to turn around and watch it all over again <p> American Utopia is more like a barefoot tent-show than a concert film . David Byrne gives us a revival meeting dressed up as avant-garde theatre , on a bare cube of stage , hemmed in on three sides by a fine chain-link curtain . The politics may be modern , with mentions of Black Lives Matter and climate change and voter registration , but the method is olde-time American razzle-dazzle .
