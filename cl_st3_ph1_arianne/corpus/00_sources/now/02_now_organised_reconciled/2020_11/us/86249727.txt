Over the next 30 years deliveries of the zero-emission fuel are expected to ramp up exponentially as the world 's fifth-biggest polluter seeks ways to replace its heavy fossil fuel use and meet a pledge to become greenhouse gas neutral by 2050 . <p> The monumental shift would require Japan to import the fuel using an armada of specialized tankers , according to Kawasaki Heavy Industries Ltd. , the tank owner and the country 's only developer of a hydrogen supply chain .
