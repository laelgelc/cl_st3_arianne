Lundin Gold is supporting the affected communities through assistance with transportation of people and supplies and has reaffirmed its commitment to fund the replacement of the public bridge to be constructed under the authority of the provincial government , estimated at $3.0 million . Following the collapse of the bridge , a group of local residents erected a blockade on the public road used to access Fruta del Norte .
