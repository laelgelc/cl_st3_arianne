<h> The Politics of Climate Change <p> **30;6782;TOOLONG : //images.c-spa ... Theda Skocpol , a former president of the American Political Science Association , spoke to students at the University of Tulsa about the politics of climate change . She presented highlights from her January 2013 report , Naming the Problem : What It Will Take to Counter Extremism and Engage Americans in the Fight Against Global Warming , about why the Obama administration was able to get a health care bill passed but not environmental legislation . She also spoke about the media 's effect on public opinion about climate science . She used slides during her presentation and responded to questions from members of the audience . This lecture , title " Naming the Problem : Extremism and the Fight Against Climate Change , " was given at The University of Tulsa 's Allen Chapman Activity Center . <p> Professor Theda Skocpol , a former president of the American Political Science Association , spoke to students at the University of Tulsa about the politics of climate change .
