TEBORG , Sweden , Nov. 5 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- The Volvo Group is taking the next step in adjusting and future-proofing the company in line with the ambitions of the Paris Climate Agreement . The ambition is that Volvo Group will be a net-zero emissions company by 2050 , at the very latest . In order to be transparent on its progress , the company is now committing to the Science Based Targets initiative . <p> " Our commitment to future generations is well-known and the Volvo Group is doing its part in achieving the goals of the Paris Agreement . Being the leader in sustainable transport and infrastructure solutions is good for the company and its stakeholders , but even better for the world .
