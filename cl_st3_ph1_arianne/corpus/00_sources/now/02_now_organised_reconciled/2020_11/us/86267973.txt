<p> In the late first century after the birth of Christ , the Roman poet Juvenal asked a simple question : " Quis custodes ipsos custodiet ? " Who will guard the guardians themselves ? <p> Juvenal was talking about sexual fidelity , but in our intellectually desiccated era , the question is most often applied to governments . <p> However , it should most reasonably apply to those who identify themselves as the guardians of the public good in our society -- the free media . Is the media the " enemy of the people " as some have claimed ? They are not . <p> But neither are they the tribunes of civic virtue and steady , unbiased tellers of truth . In many instances , they are , unfortunately , the unwatched watchmen . <p> Let me offer three examples , each drawn from The New York Times , which styles itself as the paper of record ( whatever that means ) in the United States . <p> First , we come to the 1619 Project , which posed the hypothesis that 1619 , the year that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " real " founding date of the United States .
