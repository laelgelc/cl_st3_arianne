The Global South is confronting the world 's biggest science challenges : sustainable <p> ... <p> I am an Australian science journalist based in Medellin , Colombia since 2014 . The Global South is confronting the world 's biggest science challenges : sustainable development , good public policy and climate change adaption , on top of fighting tropical diseases , corruption and poverty . Vital work is being done by scientists in the Global South , that is , the mostly tropical , developing nations in Africa , Asia and Latin America . But these achievements are often under-reported and I aim to shine a light on these scientists and their #GlobalSouthScience milestones . My previous work on how science and technology can help countries in the Global South to develop in a rapid yet sustainable way has appeared in Science , Nature , NBC , DW and various newspapers in the UK and Australia . I am also one of the organizers of the 2021 World Conference of Science Journalists in Medellin , the first in the Global South for 20 years .
