In particular , rock-based , Earth-size planets that orbit a star like our sun and orbit at nearly the same distance . Kepler 's tour revealed potentially billions of planets in the Milky Way outside of the nine in our solar system . It also delivered images of other blue-green planets with the potential to support life . <p> Now , an updated analysis of the retired telescope 's findings , published in the Astronomical Journal , provides a more accurate snapshot of habitable worlds . In a " conservative " estimate , NASA researchers believe that at least 50% of all sun-like stars have terrestrial planets with the potential for water .
