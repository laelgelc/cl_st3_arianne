<p> Google is sticking to its plan to have all of its products include recycled materials by 2022 , this year designing its new Pixel and Nest products to incorporate the re-use of otherwise wasted resources . <p> It is only part of the devices that are comprised of re-used materials , however . In the case of the Pixel , the back cover is made with 100% recycled aluminium . <p> " This is our first phone to incorporate recycled aluminium , which not only eliminates the use of mined aluminium in the enclosure and reduces waste , it also lowers the carbon footprint of manufacturing the enclosure by 35% compared to using virgin aluminium , " Google wrote in a blog post . <p> The new Nest Audio contains 70% recycled plastic across its enclosure parts , including the acoustic fabric , and is covered in the same sustainable fabric the search giant introduced with the Nest Mini last year . For the latest Nest Thermostat , Google said it made the trim plate from 75% " post-consumer recycled plastic " . <p> " Google 's focus on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our sustainability commitments , but also enables our supply chain partners to confidently invest in and develop these types of materials so that the wider consumer electronics industry can use them too , " the blog continues . <p> Google has also updated a handful of other climate change goals that it set last month , including committing to using recycled or renewable material in at least 50% of all plastic used across its hardware products by 2025 . <p> " Our new 50% commitment raises the bar well beyond industry standards . We hope this is a milestone on the path to a future where we design out waste and pollution and keep materials in use longer , " Google said . <p> The company is also committing to achieving UL 2799 Zero Waste to Landfill certification at all final assembly manufacturing sites by 2022 and by 2025 , it is also hoping to make its product packaging 100% plastic free and 100% recyclable . <p> " We 've already made a reduction in plastic use in our packaging since 2016 , but we have a lot of hard @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " the blog says .
