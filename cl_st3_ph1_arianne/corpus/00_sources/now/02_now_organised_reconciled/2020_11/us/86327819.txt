<p> We 're killing our oceans . That 's not hyperbole . For years , scientists have been telling us that climate change and our addiction to plastic are directly responsible for the decline in ocean health . That 's possibly most evident at Australia 's famed Great Barrier Reef . <p> A study published in October found that about half the reef has died since the 1990s , largely because of record-breaking temperatures . Key to the survival of the reef ( and others like it ) is data . That 's where the Schmidt Ocean Institute comes in , a nonprofit using advanced technology to map the ocean floors . We spoke with the Institute 's Dr. Carlie Wiener .
