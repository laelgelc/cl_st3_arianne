<h> Olivia 's Trees : From 6-year-old 's vision , a tree fund grows <p> " A society grows great when old men plant trees in whose shade they shall never sit . " - Ancient Greek Proverb <p> One of the core tenets of Sustainable Tallahassee 's vision is to empower all our residents to help create a world-class sustainable community that meets the needs of present and future generations . <p> When we think of a sustainable future , the first images that come to mind are often high-tech devices like solar panels or autonomous electric vehicles . As important as these advanced technologies are in our quest to build a more just and sustainable society , one of the most important things we can do is also relatively simple : plant trees . <p> Trees play an important role in increasing urban biodiversity , and providing plants and animals with a favorable habitat , food , and protection . Further , living in close proximity to urban green spaces and having access to them can improve our physical and mental health , for example by decreasing high @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in cities can help cool the air up to 3-14 degrees Fahrenheit , thus reducing the urban " heat island " effect and helping urban communities adapt to the effects of climate change . Trees also help regulate water runoff and can play a key role in reducing flooding . A mature evergreen tree , for example , can capture more than 4,000 gallons of water per year . <p> Trees improve air quality as fine particulates of dust , dirt , and smoke are trapped on leaves and bark . They also absorb pollutant gases such as carbon monoxide , nitrogen oxides , ozone , and sulfur oxides . In fact , trees can play an important role in climate change mitigation as a mature tree can absorb up to 330 lbs . of CO2 per year . <p> Trees are also used to symbolize life , wisdom , prosperity , and resilience -- all qualities that we strive for in a community , and ourselves -- and planting a tree shows a commitment to growth and represents a connection to the Earth as a whole and an area @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a tree is a way for a community to show that it cares about the place it calls home . <p> As we continue to explore ways to help make our community a great place , both for today and tomorrow , Sustainable Tallahassee is excited to launch Olivia 's Tree Fund -- a new program that will provide funding , supplies , and volunteer opportunities to plant trees in the capital city region . <p> Olivia 's Tree Fund honors the inspiring generosity of local 6-year-old Olivia Ingram . Celebrating through giving is a tradition in Olivia 's family , and when they recently moved into a new home , she was saddened that some trees had been cut down to clear the land . With the help of her parents Janeia and Spencer , Olivia held a " Llamanade " stand to raise money for a cause that would help the environment and the animals that had been displaced . <p> Olivia was able to raise $500 , which she chose to donate to Sustainable Tallahassee to help our efforts to accelerate the transition to a more sustainable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to match this amount , which will allow us to plant about 3,000 trees by the end of 2021 . <p> 3,000 trees are a great start , but we believe that with your help we can do even more ! As the program ramps up , each additional $100 donation will allow us to plant another 1,000 trees . This not only helps us to help Olivia support the environment and animals but will also establish Olivia 's Tree Fund as an ongoing opportunity for people to come together and build a sense of community through shared service to the future . <p> As we approach #GivingTuesday on Dec. 1 , we hope that you will consider supporting Olivia 's Tree Fund by donating and sharing our vision with your friends and loved ones . Sustainable Tallahassee will be beginning tree plantings in early 2021 , and there will be lots of opportunities to come out and help !
