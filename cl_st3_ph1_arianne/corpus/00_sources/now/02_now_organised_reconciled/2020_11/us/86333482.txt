<h> In ' I Am Greta , ' an unlikely celebrity grapples with fame <p> It begins on a sailboat . A girl , dressed all in black , sits astern , motionless . Beside her , the boat 's tiller swings wildly from side to side , and behind her , ocean swells rise far above her head . " I feel that everything I 've experienced over these last few months is like being in a dream , " she says . " Or in a movie . " <p> It 's a fitting opening for I Am Greta , the new documentary -- airing now on Hulu -- about climate activist Greta Thunberg . In August of 2018 , the Swedish filmmaker Nathan Grossman heard about a 15-year-old girl staging a strange and quiet protest against climate change in Stockholm . He packed up his gear and went to see . " I thought it would be a three-week shoot , " Grossman later told the Hollywood Reporter .
