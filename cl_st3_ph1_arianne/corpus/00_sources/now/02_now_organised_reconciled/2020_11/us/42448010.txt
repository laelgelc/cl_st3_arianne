<h> 18 new energy deals around the world will help build infrastructure worth more than $2 billion . <p> Two years ago , Google became the first company of its size to buy as much renewable electricity as the electricity it used . But as the company grows , so does its demand for power . To stay ahead of that demand , Google just made the largest corporate renewable energy purchase in history , with 18 new energy deals around the world that will help build infrastructure worth more than $2 billion . <p> advertisement <p> Photo : courtesy GoogleThe projects include massive new solar farms in places like Texas and North Carolina where the company has data centers . " Bringing incremental renewable energy to the grids where we consume energy is a critical component of pursuing 24 ? 7 carbon-free energy for all of our operations , " Google CEO Sundar Pichai wrote in a blog post today .
