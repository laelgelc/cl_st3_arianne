<h> Portland Museum of Art selects artists for exhibition about 2020 <p> Twenty-four artists and one artist collective have been chosen for the Portland Museum of Art 's exhibition about the turmoil and upheaval of 2020 . The juried exhibition , " Untitled , 2020 : Art From Maine In A Time , " will open in February . <p> Artists living and working in Maine were encouraged to fill in the blank in the exhibition 's title by submitting art for consideration that speaks to the uncertain and unsettled nature of this historic moment , and they responded en masse . More than 900 artists submitted work , said Jaime DeSimone , curator of contemporary art at the PMA and curatorial liaison for this exhibition . <p> The five jurors settled on 25 artists after many long digital meetings . " They were given the impossible task to survey 900 submissions and come out on the other side , " she said . As we have been doing since March , we will have a robust digital presence of the show online , and we will do everything we can to highlight the artists ' work and provide visibility that way , " she said .
