<h> The Biden administration needs to reimagine the foreign service and revitalize America 's influence abroad . <p> The US State Department today looks like one befitting of a nation down on its luck and with its back to the world . <p> Decimated by departures of foreign service officers around the world , beset by budget cuts , and disparaged by a president whose idea of diplomacy is to berate allies and coddle dictators , this nation 's oldest cabinet-level department is badly in need of renovation . Not just a rebuilding , although that is essential , but also a rethinking by the incoming Biden administration of who should serve as a diplomat and what that means . <p> " The foreign service today faces one of the greatest crises in its history , " said former ambassador to NATO Nicholas Burns at a recent remote conference sponsored by the Belfer Center at Harvard 's Kennedy School . " It faces a crisis in lack of support , it faces a crisis in lack of leadership in some cases .
