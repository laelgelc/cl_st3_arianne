<h> Round the world on an electric boat : Briton commemorates Magellan <p> By Reuters Staff <p> 1 Min Read <p> Slideshow ( 5 images ) <p> SEVILLE , Spain ( Reuters ) - British navigator Jimmy Cornell set sail from Seville in Spain on Thursday to commemorate the 500th anniversary of the first voyage around the world - this time on an electric boat . <p> Cornell plans to follow the route taken by explorers Ferdinand Magellan and Juan Sebastian Elcano . <p> The trip " has two dimensions , a historical dimension , because I want to travel the original and historic route , Cornell told reporters . <p> " But it has another very important dimension for me , which is to protect the environment . This is why it is a fully electric boat with zero emissions . " <p> Cornell completed his first world voyage between 1975 and 1981 . After returning to some of the places he had visited , Cornell said he was shocked to witness the destruction caused by climate change , prompting him to take on the zero carbon challenge . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; Editing by Ingrid Melander and Janet Lawrence
