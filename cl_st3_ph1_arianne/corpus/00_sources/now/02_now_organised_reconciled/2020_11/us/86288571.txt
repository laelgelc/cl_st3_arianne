Under Georgia law , the top two finishers in each race will compete in a runoff on Jan. 5 . Both of the incumbents running for reelection are Republicans , and the outcome will determine which party controls the Senate for the next two years . Republicans currently have a two-seat advantage , which means they only need to win one of the two Georgia races to retain control of the Senate .
