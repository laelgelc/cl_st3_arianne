<h> Here is some of the most interesting research to have appeared in top science journals last week <h> Glowing Europa <p> Jupiter 's moon Europa glows -- it can look slightly green , blue or even white . New lab experiments which recreated the environment of Europa shows that the various salty compounds on it react to radiation and emit the shimmer . " If Europa were n't under this radiation , it would look the way our moon looks to us -- dark on the shadowed side , " lead author Murthy Gudipati said in a release . " But because it 's bombarded by the radiation from Jupiter , it glows in the dark . " <h> Polypill and heart diseases <p> Heard of polypills ? It is a single pill which contains many medications .
