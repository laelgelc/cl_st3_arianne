The 2020 Hyundai Ioniq Plug-In Hybrid can go an estimated 30 miles on a full charge , and gets you the increased fuel economy of a hybrid once battery-only power is depleted . It also achieves an impressive 52 mpg EPA-estimated combined fuel economy , has useful and straightforward tech , and comes with an abundance of standard features , making it a stronger buy than the Toyota Prius Prime , and our new Editors ' Choice winner for the segment . <h> Pricing and Design <p> The 2020 Hyundai Ioniq PHEV comes in three trim levels , all with a 1.6-liter , 4-cylinder engine that puts out a paltry 104-horsepower . That said , like the Prius Prime , the Ioniq PHEV feels sporty in EV-only mode , except with more wobbly handling . <p> We also like the smooth shifting of the six-speed automatic transmission , which is a refreshing change to the ubiquitous fuel-saving but droning CVT found in most hybrids .
