Erik Paulsen , who succeeded Ramstad in Minnesota 's Third Congressional District . <p> " A lot of folks who run for office today get involved in politics because they 're more about an ideology rather than offering solutions to problems facing their community , the state and the country , " Paulsen said Friday morning .
