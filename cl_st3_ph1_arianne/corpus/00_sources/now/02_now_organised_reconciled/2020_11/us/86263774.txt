<h> Biden 's oil comments at the final debate did n't tank his favorability <p> " Would you close down the oil industry ? " President Trump asked his opponent at the final presidential debate in Nashville , Tennessee , last Thursday . " I would transition the oil industry , yes , " Joe Biden said . " Basically what he is saying is he is going to destroy the oil industry , " Trump said . " Will you remember that , Texas ? Will you remember that , Pennsylvania ? Oklahoma ? Ohio ? " <p> Biden tried to walk it back after the debate that night , even though his own climate plan shows that he 's serious about weaning the country off of polluting fuels . But the damage , at least in the eyes of the Trump campaign , had already been done . <p> " This probably will put the nail in the coffin for Joe Biden in Pennsylvania , " Trump campaign senior adviser Jason Miller told reporters a day after the debate . " This is really devastating . " <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ representative poll conducted by Ipsos for the poll analysis group FiveThirtyEight showed that Americans were just as likely to vote for Biden or Trump after the debate as they were before the event , an indication that Biden 's comments on transitioning from fossil fuels did n't unnerve a meaningful percentage of respondents . ( It 's also more proof that election debates do n't tend to sway viewers ) . The same respondents were contacted before the debate and after . <p> And when it came to the candidates ' debate performances and policies , a higher percentage of respondents said they approved of Biden than Trump : 68.7 percent of respondents said Biden 's debate performance was " good " and 60.2 percent thought the same of his policies . By comparison , 51.8 percent of respondents approved of Trump 's performance and 43.8 considered his policies to be " good .
