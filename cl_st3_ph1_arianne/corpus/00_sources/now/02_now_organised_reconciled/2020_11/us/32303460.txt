He claims that we have so many cases because we do so much testing . Apart from the ontological problems , his implication that doing less testing would make us look better is a flat illogical fantasy . Testing does not cause deaths , so testing less would not reduce the number of deaths . <p> Fewer cases with the same number of deaths would make the calculated lethality of COVID-19 worse than with more testing ! Detecting fewer cases is a bad thing and could lead to unwarranted mitigation actions .
