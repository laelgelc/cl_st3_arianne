<h> The 2020 Atlantic hurricane season keeps raging with Hurricane Eta <p> The 28th named storm of an epic Atlantic hurricane season formed yesterday and is barreling toward Central America as Hurricane Eta today . That ties the record with 2005 for the most storms strong enough to earn a name in a single season . <p> This is also the 12th hurricane to form this season , which has only happened three times before , according to Phil Klotzbach , a research scientist in the Department of Atmospheric Science at Colorado State University . <p> 2020 has been exceptionally active <p> The World Meteorological Organization ran out of names on its alphabetical list in mid-September , with more than two months left in the 2020 season . That forced the agency to turn to Greek letters for storm names for just the second time in its history . But this is the first time Eta has been used since one storm went unnamed in 2005 .
