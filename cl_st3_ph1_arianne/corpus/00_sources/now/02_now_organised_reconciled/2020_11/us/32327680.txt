<p> **45;3009;TOOLONG ... to Content <h> EU eyes reboot of ties with US , hopes to meet Biden soon <p> BRUSSELS ( AP ) -- Top European Union officials are calling for a reboot of relations with the United States under President-elect Joe Biden . They want to set aside the tensions and divisions of the last four years under President Donald Trump over trade , security and climate change . European Council President Charles Michel is expected to invite Biden soon to videoconference talks and then an in-person meeting in Brussels . Michel told EU ambassadors Tuesday that " we must revive , and renew , our trans-Atlantic alliance . " He says he expects a Biden-led administration to " adopt a calm style . That will be a good thing . " But Michel counseled against " euphoria " and for remaining lucid , saying " The United States are changing , Europe is changing . "
