<p> All hope is lost . The villain is gloating . Defeat seems assured . Then in the distance , backup finally arrives . It 's not too late . A spark of hope ignites . The good guys are gon na win this ... <p> That 's the structure of countless Hollywood endings . It 's also something editor and filmmakerJohn Handem Piette was thinking about last week as he obsessed over the presidential-election returns . As mail-in votes were counted , gradually shifting the balance away from President Donald Trump and delivering victory to now President-elect Joe Biden , Piette considered how many people had worked together to bring about this historic moment . <p> To him , it felt a lot like the finale of Avengers : Endgame . <p> " I 'm a big Marvel nerd . I love that scene . I 've watched that scene a hundred times , and I just kind of felt like it met the moment . It matched the moment , " he told Vanity Fair in an interview . " I just could n't watch the TV anymore , and my brain could n't shut off . And I just thought , Let me edit this fun thing for my friends . " <p> The result is a remix of that iconic scene , mapping the faces of 2020 's most visible political players over the heads of familiar heroes . He called it America : Endgame , and after uploading the clip on Friday morning , it did n't just go viral . It went supernova -- with about 33 million views on Twitter so far and more than 110,000 shares on Facebook , not counting the other countless other shares and copies . <p> LeBron Jamesposted it , along with many other celebrities . Even the Hulk passed it on , with Mark Ruffalo writing : " Amazing . Lol ! ! " <p> Piette pulled an all-nighter last Thursday , spending about 16 hours on the project . He posted it Friday morning , a day before networks called the race for Biden . <p> The result , he acknowledged , was a bit rough , but it struck at the right moment -- just before national jubilation began . " My editor brain wanted to finesse it and perfect it . I could have spent another day on it -- but I just thought time is of the essence , " he said . " I think that 's part of the charm , to be honest . I can only see the imperfections , but I think people like that . It 's like a South Park-y kind of thing , where it makes it funny . " <p> He walked Vanity Fair through the video 's key moments : <h> Joe Biden as Captain America <p> " That 's the biggest movie of all time . You knew they were going to beat Thanos , but you were n't sure . You knew maybe those heroes were going to come back , but you were n't sure . That moment , when Captain America seems beaten down , hope seems lost -- when are those other guys going to show up ? " Piette said . " It 's like , we knew the mail-in ballots were coming in , but the numbers still looked bad .... Then of course Trump gives his ' victory speech . ' There was some PTSD for me . I had flashbacks from 2016 , like , Oh God , could this happen again ? " <p> Advertisement <p> This scene resonates so deeply because it 's not one single hero who saves the day -- it 's the work of all of them together . " I think that 's the point of the video too , " he said . " It 's a team effort . There 's so many people , with so many different political views . We land on different places on the political spectrum , but we all think we 're on the right side of history . So we should all work together . We all are banding together against what 's clearly wrong , what 's clearly misaligned with our morals , our values . " <p> Then it became a matter of figuring out which real-life figure correlated to which fictional superhero . <h> Kamala Harris as Falcon <p> Cap 's close friend was the perfect choice for the vice president-elect , particularly because Anthony Mackie 's soaring hero is the one who first alerts Chris Evans that help is on the way . " I could have matched Kamala with someone else , but I think the timing of that moment , and the fact that Falcon is the one that gets in his ear and says , ' on your left , ' I knew Kamala 's got to be the one , " Piette said . " She 's going to be his wingman , so to speak . " <p> He even spliced together audio to roughly recreate Falcon 's line . " Finding the audio of her saying ' on your left ' was probably the biggest nightmare of the whole thing , " Piette said . " I probably spent an hour just looking through interviews and speeches she 's given , just to find the word ' left . ' " It finally turned up in a Wolf Blitzer interview . " She said something like , Yeah , it 's quid pro quo ; there is n't much left to the imagination . I was like , Fuck , she said ' left ! ' There it is ! I was just crazy and delirious at 5 a.m. when I finally found it . " <h> Georgia as Wakanda <p> The influence of Black leaders and African American voters gets a nod as former president Barack Obama appears as Black Panther , flanked by voting rights activist Stacey Abrams as Okoye and Atlanta mayor Keisha Lance Bottoms as Shuri . <p> Abrams was the pivotal force organizing disenfranchised voters after alleged suppression efforts that supporters believe cost her the 2018 gubernatorial election . Bottoms rallied her city to turn out for Biden . <p> " That was just sort of , they 're delivering Atlanta . They 're delivering Georgia for Biden , " Piette said . " Obama gave a speech there . He did one of his rallies there in Atlanta to help turn out that vote , and thank God he did . " <h> Guardians of the Galaxy as Primary Opponents <p> Primary rivals can be wild cards for any politician , as hard feelings from the early part of the election carry over into the general . In this case , the Guardians of the Galaxy seemed like a good fit for Biden 's former opponents as they became fighters for his cause . <p> Piette assigned Cory Booker to Drax the Destroyer , Elizabeth Warren as Mantis , and Beto O'Rourke as Star-Lord , with Bernie Sanders turning up as Doctor Strange . " He was so important , " Piette said . " I wanted Bernie to say , ' Medicare for All , ' and Dr. Strange would probably want that , as a doctor . " <h> The Squad as the Heroines <p> Endgame gave its female superheroes a shared moment in the spotlight as they positively strutted into battle . Piette thought this would be a good showcase for the congressional progressive powerhouses known as the Squad : Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez as Captain Marvel , Rashida Tlaib as Mantis this time , Ayanna Pressley as Pepper Potts in her Rescue Armor , and Ilhan Omar as Shuri . <p> Advertisement <p> As the scene progresses , Hillary Clinton rides a pegasus as Valkyrie , Nancy Pelosi pops up as Wasp , and Michelle Obama makes an entrance as Gamora . <p> From there , Andrew Yang appears as the mystical warrior librarian Wong . " The thing is I could have gone all night . Honestly , I wanted to , if I could have spent more time on it , " Piette said . <h> Hunter Biden as Thor <p> The bearded man with a cigarette is Biden 's son Hunter , which is one of the references that has raised the most questions . " There are certain ones that pop up a lot more than others in the comments . Like , Why are they there ? Who 's that ? " Piette said . " So Hunter Biden , when he shows up as Thor , a lot of people did n't know who that was . " <p> Piette used a photo from the dubious " found laptop " story that Trump and his surrogates tried to use to smear Hunter and his father in the final weeks of the election . " That was the photo that the right was using to try and peddle the laptop conspiracy theory , " Piette said . The lack of awareness among viewers shows just how little resonance that dubious attack has had . <p> The Thor reference was n't entirely a dig at Hunter , whose personal issues Trump tried to drag into the campaign , Piette said . " I thought , You know what ? Thor was in a bad place in this movie , he was suffering from addiction , he was struggling with his worthiness to wield his weapons , but eventually , he does find his worthiness . He does feel worthy and he overcomes his addiction , and I thought Hunter was really the perfect choice for that . " <h> The Lost Heroes <p> When Ant-Man makes himself gigantic and crashes onto the scene , Piette thought this would be a good place to pay tribute to those who did n't live to see the end of the fight but had a profound impact on it nonetheless . Georgia congressman and civil rights icon John Lewis became Giant-Man , while Arizona senator and war hero John McCain was mapped onto the Hulk . <p> Then Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg floats in as War Machine , accompanied by one of the other controversial references in the video : Sean Connery . <h> Why ... Sean Connery ? <p> The actor died just days before the election and did n't have any public comments or apparent influence upon the contest . The actor 's appearance on the body of Rocket Raccoon was just a joke , Piette said , a moment of absurdity to cut through all that earnestness . <p> Advertisement <p> " I 've gotten messages like , ' Can you edit out Sean Connery ? ' " he said . " I could n't figure out for the life of me who to put as Rocket . I saved that for last . Everyone I was thinking of , I did n't really want to put them as the raccoon , kind of hanging off of RBG 's shoulder . It 's disrespectful a little bit . " <p> Then he realized the way Rocket held his blaster aloft reminded him of 007 . " Honestly , I am a big Sean Connery fan and a big James Bond fan , and From Russia With Love is my favorite Bond movie , " Piette said . " He 's known for fighting Russian agents , and that 's kind of an undercurrent of both of these elections -- the Russian influence . So who better to fend off the Russian threat , than Bond himself ? " <p> There are a few other randoms in the video as well , like Elon Musk as Iron Man . " People forget this stuff , but he did leave the president 's advisory councils early on . When Trump left the Paris climate accords , Musk was pissed off and he tweeted , ' am departing the presidential councils ... Climate change is real , ' " Piette said . " Honestly , Tony Stark was no angel . He 's a flawed hero . It took a while for Tony Stark to even be more of a hero than an antihero . In Civil War , he 's basically the villain . So I thought it matched . Not everyone likes them . " <h> Trump as Thanos <p> " That look on Trump 's face there , it 's one of the most cathartic parts for me , because Trump never admits defeat . He never wants to show his anguish or show fear or anything , even though everything he says and does seems to point to severe insecurity , " Piette said . " That moment where he just looks shook is so cathartic for me , because inside , that 's what he 's feeling . That 's what he what he would look like , if we got a peek into his soul . He 's just afraid . " <p> He hopes America : Endgame also serves a serious purpose . Laughter can defuse and diminish a tyrant , and he knows how much time Trump spends on Twitter . " A part of me really hopes that it did get up the ladder to the Oval Office , " the filmmaker said . " I think it 's an emperor-with-no-clothes moment , right ? It 's that moment where the kid says , ' Hey , he 's naked . ' Then everybody kind of drops the facade and starts laughing . " <p> Piette is anticipating more trouble from Trump as he contests legitimate voting results and further damages trust in the American electoral system . " Right now , it seems very dangerous because I think honestly , with Trump as a lame-duck president , I 'm more nervous than I was when he was empowered , " he said . " Who knows what he 's going to do the country . I do n't feel like he wants to hand the country over in good shape to Joe Biden . I feel like he would n't mind letting the country set itself ablaze and hand Biden the scraps . " <p> It has already inspired another video for Piette . " I might have to do one where the Snap happens , " he said . " I may have to do a part two where they 're like , You got to get out of there . "
