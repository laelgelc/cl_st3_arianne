When it 's washed , synthetic fabric tends to release huge numbers of microfibers ; some are captured at wastewater treatment plants , while others escape into the environment . One recent study estimated that around 4,000 metric tons of plastic microfibers , or 13.3 quadrillion fibers , were released into the environment in California in 2019 . More of the fibers can escape as people wear the clothing outside . In response , some companies , like Patagonia , are researching ways to design fabric differently or turn to other materials , like wool . Startups are working on new materials that can biodegrade , like fabric made from methane emissions . <p> " There are a lot of promising developments in industry at the moment , " says Napper .
