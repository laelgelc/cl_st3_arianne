AERAP , the Africa-Europe science platform , will organise a briefing on EU support for cooperation with Africa on science , R&amp;D innovation and education for the period 2021 to 2027 . The briefing will take place online on Wednesday , 18 November 2020 . <p> In March 2020 European Commission published a Communication entitled Towards a comprehensive Strategy with Africa , which sets out a new comprehensive EU strategy with Africa that will be built on five partnerships : <p> 1 . A partnership for green transition and energy access ; <p> 2 . A partnership for digital transformation ; <p> 3 . A partnership for sustainable growth and jobs ; <p> 4 . A partnership for peace and governance ; <p> 5 . A partnership on migration and mobility . <p> Science and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ partnerships .
