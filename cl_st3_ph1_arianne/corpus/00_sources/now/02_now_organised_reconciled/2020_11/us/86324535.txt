<h> EU drafts deal to upgrade 2030 climate change target next month <p> BRUSSELS ( Reuters ) - The European Union has drafted a deal to curb greenhouse gas emissions by at least 55% by 2030 , against 1990 levels , a target it hopes its 27 members will endorse at a summit next month , the document showed . <p> Slideshow ( 5 images ) <p> If the Dec 10-11 EU leaders ' meeting approves the draft conclusions seen by Reuters , it could make the EU the first major economy to submit a new climate pledge under the 2015 U.N . Paris climate agreement , before a year-end deadline to do so . <p> The current 2030 target for EU members , who together make up the European Council , is a 40% cut in emissions from 1990 levels , already higher than the amount pledged by other big emitters such as China and the United States . <p> " The European Council endorses a binding EU target of a net reduction of at least 55% in domestic greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 compared to 1990 , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the European Commission , proposed the target in September , saying anything less would fail to put the EU on track to reach net zero emissions by 2050 - a target it has already signed up to . <p> The U.N . climate science panel has said the world needs net zero carbon dioxide emissions by 2050 to keep global temperature rises this century to below 1.5 degrees above pre-industrial times - the level required to avoid catastrophic climate change . <p> The document says the new 2030 target would be delivered " collectively " by EU countries - an assurance to countries concerned by the economic transformation that deeper emissions cuts will require . <p> That could soothe the Czech Republic , which has said it could support a 55% emissions cut if it is EU-wide , but not a national requirement . <p> The draft also says the target will reflect countries ' different national circumstances , and that upcoming reforms to the EU carbon market will make funds available to help poorer states invest in cleaner energy . <p> Coal dependent Poland has said it needs more @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tougher climate target , and last month called for EU carbon market reforms to redirect revenues to poorer countries . <p> EU ambassadors will discuss the draft later on Wednesday .
