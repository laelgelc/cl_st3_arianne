<h> Divestment by Sasol Chemicals North America LLC of its 50% Equity Interest in Gemini HDPE LLC <p> JOHANNESBURG , Nov. 24 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- The board of directors of Sasol is pleased to announce that Sasol Chemicals North America LLC ( " SCNA " ) , a wholly owned subsidiary of Sasol , has agreed principle terms with INEOS Gemini HDPE Holding Company LLC ( " INEOS " ) and a new entity to be formed by INEOS ( " Newco " ) to sell its 50% membership interest in Gemini HDPE LLC ( " Gemini " " ) to Newco ( the " Sale " ) for USD404 million ( subject to adjustment for cash , debt , working capital and other items ) . Gemini produces and sells bimodal high-density polyethylene based in La Porte , Texas , United States of America . <p> The Sale represents a further step in achieving Sasol 's strategic and financial objectives by accelerating the focus on specialty chemicals and reducing net debt .
