<p> Recently , scientists discovered a mysterious fossil in a century-old archive - a new species of pterosaur . The remnants of the new species were found amongst fossils collected from phosphate mines in the English Fens in the latter half of the 19th century . <p> While Ph.D . student Roy Smith was examining fossilized fin spines of sharks , Smith came across fractured pieces of pterosaurs ' toothless jaws . While these do look similar to shark spines , there are several subtle differences . <p> For example , " one such feature are tiny little holes where nerves come to the surface and are used for sensitive feeding by the pterosaurs . Shark fin spines do not have these , " said Smith . <p> Pterosaurs are closely related to dinosaurs and were among the first organisms to evolve the ability to fly .
