You 've never even heard of Valencia County ? <p> Well , I suppose that 's not altogether surprising . Tucked into the sublime , mountain-ringed deserts south of Albuquerque , Valencia County has a population of 76,688 people . It 's the sixth-most populous county in New Mexico , a state not exactly known for its populous counties . It 's probably not a place you 've visited . It 's surely not a place you 'd assign any political significance . <p> Except that Valencia County has the longest streak of picking presidents of any county in the United States . <p> It started in 1952 , when Dwight D.
