The forests here have furthered empires , providing Phoenicians with timber for their merchant ships , and early Egyptians with wood for elaborately carved sarcophagi . <p> But now the very survival of these ancient giants is in question . Scientists say rising temperatures and worsening drought conditions brought about by climate change are driving wildfires in this Middle Eastern country to ever higher altitudes , encroaching upon the mountains where the cedars grow . <p> Changing weather patterns in Lebanon , defined by its long Mediterranean coastline and mountain ranges , are also upsetting the ecology of the cedar forests .
