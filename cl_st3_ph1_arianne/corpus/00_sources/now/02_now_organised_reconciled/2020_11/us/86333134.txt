And a better world can benefit from the right kind of public development finance . <p> But public banks , governments , and businesses need to make changes if we want to move away from a world of self-inflicted existential threats . The existing models of debt and financial aid , focused on states and corporations , only serve to replicate colonial power imbalances , and support the prevailing top-down paradigm . <p> Instead , people who are the purported beneficiaries of development finance have to be the key decision-makers . <p> Community-led development and human rights must be front and center on the summit agenda . Indigenous Peoples , grassroots communities and social movements must be invited to share their vision of development . <p> If governments and their public banks are serious about transformation , and leaving behind old patterns of crises , human rights in common have to come before finance in common .
