<h> Future Tense Newsletter : Welcome to March 2020 : The Sequel <p> Cases of COVID-19 are skyrocketing . Hospitals are struggling to keep up with patients . It feels so much like the early days of the pandemic that I 'm half-expecting a new season of Tiger King and a run on toilet paper . <p> Some things have changed since March , of course . In Downsizing , some well-intentioned scientists create a way to miniaturize humans , with the goal of combating climate change : Small humans , as some academics wrote in a troll-y paper I covered for Future Tense in 2012 , would have a much smaller footprint on the planet . It 's a story of how technology can be coopted and the confusing questions that can arise from a massive social change : Should downsized people pay the same amount of taxes ?
