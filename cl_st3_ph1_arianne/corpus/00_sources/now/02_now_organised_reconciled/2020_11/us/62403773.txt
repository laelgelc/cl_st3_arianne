<p> House Democrats elected to keep their leaders in place on Wednesday , marking a win for the party 's moderate wing . <p> The party confirmed Wednesday that Rep. Nancy Pelosi , D-California , would continue to serve as Speaker of the House . Rep. Steny Hoyer , D-Maryland , will again serve as the House Majority Leader , Rep. James Clyburn , D-South Carolina , will continue to serve as the House Majority Whip , and Rep. Hakeem Jeffries will keep his role as the chairman of the House Democratic caucus . <p> No other Democrats mounted serious challenges to current party leadership positions . <p> Pelosi 's upcoming term will mark her fourth as Speaker of the House . She previously served as speaking when Democrats held the majority in the House from 2007 to 2011 , and she was elected as Speaker again when Dems won back control following the 2016 midterms . <p> Though House Democrats will be working with a Democrat president in Joe Biden next term , serious questions remain about the future of the party . <p> Democrats went into the 2020 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ White House -- and plenty of polling suggested that they would do so .
