<h> Species more likely to die out with rapid climate change <p> Great tits have to adapt to the behaviour of their prey quickly . Otherwise things can go really wrong . Credit : Shutterstock , NTB scanpix <p> An ever warmer climate could be bad news for species that depend on stable and abundant access to food at certain times of the year . <p> " If changes happen too fast , species can become extinct , " says Emily Simmonds , an associate professor at NTNU 's Department of Biology . <p> She is the first author of an article in Ecology Letters that addresses how great tits can be affected if the supply of larvae changes in the spring . <p> Spring , plants and larvae arriving earlier <p> Several bird species depend on the abundance of larvae while their young are small . If the larvae supply peaks earlier in the spring than normal , there may simply be too little food for the hatchlings . <p> A warming climate can bring about changes like this .
