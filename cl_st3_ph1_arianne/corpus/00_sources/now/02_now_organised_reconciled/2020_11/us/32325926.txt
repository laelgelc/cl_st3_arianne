I expect that it will be granted , " Quarles said . " I suspect we could probably join before the spring . " <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> According to its website , the NGFS seeks to help the world meet the goals of the Paris climate agreement and " enhance the role of the financial system to manage risks and to mobilize capital for green and low-carbon investments in the broader context of environmentally sustainable development .
