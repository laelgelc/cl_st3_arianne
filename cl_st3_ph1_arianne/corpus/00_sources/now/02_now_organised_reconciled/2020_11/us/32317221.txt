<p> On the wall of a residential street in Charlton , south London , Louis Masai puts the finishing touches to a striking large-scale mural of an orangutan . <p> The British artist , known for his signature patchwork style , travels around the United Kingdom to paint colourful murals of animal species in decline or on the brink of extinction , to issue a warning about the devastating effects of climate change and biodiversity collapse . <p> According to the International Union of Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) there are currently 23,250 species listed as threatened . This means : critically endangered , endangered or vulnerable . Adding extinct and extinct in the wild species the figure is 24,153 . <p> It 's widely predicted that as many as two-thirds of all species could be near extinction by the end of this century . But , some are now rising in population due to increasing concern about the extinction crisis .
