We North Carolinians cherish our bountiful state and place great value in its protection . Our children play and explore in creeks , we savor mountain air that 's clear and clean , and we marvel as the fall colors come to our forests and again as the dogwoods bloom in the spring . <p> For decades North Carolina has been a leader in the Southeast in protecting our environment and public health and in conserving our lands and waters .
