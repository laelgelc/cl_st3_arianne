<p> A historic shift in U.S. economic policy is taking shape regardless of who sits in the White House or controls Congress : an increasingly muscular role for state power to build up industries U.S. leaders deem critical to America 's national security and place in the world . <p> Whether President Donald Trump 's " America first " manufacturing approach and his mandates to make medical equipment in the U.S. , or former Vice President Joe Biden 's " build back better " plan to fund and incentivize a green energy sector , free-market fundamentalism is receding into the rear view mirror -- along with any notion that the triumph of western-style liberal democracy and laissez-faire capitalism will grab hold of U.S. competitors around the world . <p> It 's a bipartisan shift away from a decadeslong allegiance to freer markets and toward a new economic era in which government intervenes more often .
