<h> How cities of the future are shaping up to be smarter and more sustainable <p> Today 's cities are grappling with problems ranging from gridlocked traffic and overflowing landfills , to unaffordable housing and growing inequality . Without action , those problems will only get worse . <p> The United Nations predicts that by 2050 , the world 's population will reach 9.7 billion .
