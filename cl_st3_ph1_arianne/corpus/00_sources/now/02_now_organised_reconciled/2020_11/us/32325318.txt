As a result , stronger storms could wreak havoc on communities farther inland , the study finds . It 's the latest warning that hurricanes could cause even more devastation in a warming world . <p> Hurricanes feed off heat energy from the sea . The more heat , the more energy -- and the more intense hurricanes can become . There 's already evidence that the strongest hurricanes have become more common . But once a hurricane reaches land , it 's cut off from its source of energy and typically begins to rapidly lose strength .
