the UK 's only land border with the EU . <p> The 1998 Good Friday peace deal that effectively ended Northern Ireland 's 30 years of sectarian violence created new institutions for cross-border cooperation on the island of Ireland . <p> But Johnson has put forward legislation that would break the Northern Ireland protocol of the Brexit divorce treaty that seeks to avoid a physical customs border between the British province and EU-member Ireland . <p> That prompted a warning two months ago from Biden , who has talked about the importance of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1998 agreement as it withdraws from the bloc or there can be no separate U.S. trade deal . <p> " They talked about the importance of implementing Brexit in such a way that upholds the Good Friday Agreement , " a British official said after Tuesday 's Biden-Johnson call . <p> Johnson has predicted close ties with the United States under Biden , seeing common ground on issues like climate change . <p> " Among the shared priorities they discussed were containing COVID-19 and ... combating climate change , " Biden 's transition team said in a statement after the call . <p> " The President-elect expressed his interest in cooperating with the UK , NATO , and the EU on shared transAtlantic priorities , and reaffirmed his support for the Good Friday Agreement in Northern Ireland , " they added . <p> Johnson has never met Biden and commentators have suggested he will have to work hard to foster the so-called " special relationship " between the historical allies . <p> Additional reporting by Elizabeth Piper , William James and by Padraic Halpin in Dublin ; editing by Stephen Addison
