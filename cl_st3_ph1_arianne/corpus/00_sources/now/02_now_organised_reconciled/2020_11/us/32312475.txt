<h> Westneat : Endorsement for a bully <h> Spokane paper 's plug reveals moral bargaining of Trumpers <p> By Danny Westneat <p> Published : November 1 , 2020 , 6:01am <p> Share : <p> Danny Westneat is a columnist for the Seattle Times . Photo <p> With much of the state and nation having already voted , it feels like Election 2020 is all over but the shouting . The shouting , though , can be highly revealing . And these past few days the uproar has been loudest in an unlikely place : Spokane . <p> The Lilac City is a light-blue town smack in a deep-red county . The red parts outmuscle the blue at the ballot box , such that in the 2016 presidential vote , Donald Trump won Spokane County by about 9 percentage points . <p> So you 'd think it would n't be that big of a deal that the area 's daily newspaper , The Spokesman-Review , endorsed Trump for reelection last Sunday . <p> But the editorial stance was roundly booed , both locally and nationally , and it led to scores @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ editorial page editor weighed in to say he 's " ashamed to say I worked there , " while a current news columnist called it " an utter mind-twister . " <p> The problem was n't just that the paper endorsed Trump . It 's the argument the editorial made . <p> " Donald Trump is a bully and a bigot , " it started out . Yes , it went on , it 's true , Trump panders to racists . He endlessly tweets conspiracy theories . He denies climate change . And he 's been " cavalier about COVID-19 and has led poorly through the pandemic . " He 's also " a wretched human being , " the editorial remarkably concluded , adding : " We recommend voting for him anyway . " <p> You have to admit that 'd be a great bumper sticker : Wretched Human Being for President ! <p> Some Spokanians were disgusted that a candidate could be a bigot and endorsed in the same breath . Considering our history , should n't bigotry be disqualifying ? Some journalists at the paper , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the publisher , Stacey Cowles , were angry because of something not mentioned -- that Trump has launched a war of sorts on journalism itself . <p> All strong critiques , but I want to rise in defense of the editorial anyway . Not because I agree with it . But because I learned something from it . <p> It 's easy to see why Trump has such a loyal following with the masses , from his outsider brashness to his middle-finger style with the liberals to his tough-on-immigration stances . I 've been curious for years though about how it is that so many party elites and establishment types seem so compliant to his worst excesses . <p> Why does no one in the Republican Party ever speak up ? I 've interviewed a number of Trump backers over the years to try to understand this disconnect . The rationale is that Trump 's wild statements and even his bigotry are a sort of performance art , an act not to be taken literally . <p> The Spokesman editorial refreshingly cuts through all the performance art baloney . Trump @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a bigot and a know-nothing charlatan . But we back him anyway .... why ? Because we think he 'll be better for business . <p> Seriously , that 's pretty much it . Joe Biden wants more spending on things like health care and education ( true ) , so he 'll have to " impose unprecedented tax increases , " ( not true , especially the " unprecedented " part -- for the record what Biden is actually proposing are higher taxes on people making more than $400,000 a year ) . <p> Given the choice between a wretched human being and " a doddering , doting uncle who would hand out gifts the nation ca n't afford , " the editorial closes , " economic policy and principle should prevail . Vote Donald Trump . " <p> This may be the most revealing thing written about the moral bargaining that 's at the root of the Trump era . It goes to how it is that the elite classes , from D.C. down to the Chamber of Commerce level , all could tut in public how " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ behavior , while for the most part enabling it . Money is n't the only thing that matters , as any fantastically rich person will tell you . But if to get some tax cuts or goose the business cycle all you have to do is tolerate some rank bigotry , crazed conspiracy theorizing , polarizing name-calling and autocratic demagoguery , is the choice even difficult ? <p> The real story is that the publisher in Spokane is far from the only one to take this deal . Much of the business class and the rank-and-file of an entire political party is in for it with the rationalizing of a cult . <p> I feel for my reporter brethren at The Spokesman-Review . But I 'm also glad the paper printed this . It says the quiet parts out loud . At least now , when people look back at this time in bafflement , how it ever came to pass will be right there in writing .
