<h> Job well done , America Opinion <p> Updated Nov 10 , 10:37 AM ; Posted Nov 10 , 10:37 AM <p> By Zach Alger <p> America is imperfect . We get things wrong . We depend on immigrants , but often deride them . Our treatment of Black people over the centuries has been inexcusable . Our poor and our sick are not as well cared for as they should be in a country as rich as ours . And we are occasionally too eager to use our military in situations where we should not . But there has always been a critical mass who have seen these injustices and striven to make the country better , and along the way America has done a lot of good . <p> The last four years have been an exception . Four years ago just enough people , not a majority but just enough , in just enough states sent the message ( whether they intended to or not ) that progress did n't matter anymore . <p> The world shuddered . This was America . This was the home of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the Kennedys , of Martin Luther King junior , of Twain , Hemingway , and Melville . This was the country who instituted and paid for the Marshall plan which rebuilt western Europe , and who put pressure on the Soviets for 50 years until democracy could finally return to the rest of that continent . <p> How could this country elect someone who exhibited all the warning signs of everything we had spent the last century trying to defeat ? <p> Those of us living here who knew that this new direction was wrong and fought against it have felt horribly alone as we watched this behavior become normal , if not accepted . Allies were mocked and degraded as they were asked " what have you done for me lately " , and then told that a favor to the president is the same as a favor to the country . Internationally treaties were ripped up while domestically subpoenas were ignored . The party in power was always right and the people that questioned this were the enemy of the people . Slowly other countries began to accept that this was apparently the new America , and though they generally knew to keep their opinions about it to themselves , privately they looked at us with sadness and apprehension , the way people do with a friend who was always a good person but has become an angry drunk , all the while hoping that they would n't head down that same road into darkness . <p> Eventually a global pandemic began to spread , and while other nations did indeed have bad outbreaks , most quickly got them under control . America , with its leadership of Alternative Facts , did not . The man behind the desk told us it was no big deal . A hoax . A quarter million of us died and many more are left with lasting side-effects that may affect them for the rest of their lives . While areas across the country burned , some from unmitigated climate change and others from civil unrest that , rather than quelling , the government instead intentionally exacerbated into yet another attempt to fear-monger , businesses in small towns shuttered as what should have been a quick remedy turned into almost a year of drawn-out uncertainty and the working class slowly began to realize that these tax cuts to the rich failed to trickle down while debt continued to accumulate for them and their children to repay . <p> But another four years had elapsed , and it was time for another election . And this time a clear majority of just enough people in just enough states made a decision . The awesome responsibility America shoulders in the world has been reclaimed by people who will again attempt to use it for good , and the relief is palpable . <p> The work is n't over , but for the moment at least take some time and know that the world appreciates a job well done . <p> Zach Alger , Annville , Pa . <p> Note to readers : if you purchase something through one of our affiliate links we may earn a commission .
