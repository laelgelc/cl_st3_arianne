<p> When Father John Grace , pastor of Immaculate Conception Catholic Church in Hampton , talks about the hundreds of solar panels that now cover the roof of the church sanctuary , he talks not just about saving money on electricity , but protecting God 's creation . <p> " That is adding something to the quality of life for the future , " Grace said of the solar panel array that was installed in July 2019 . <p> Grace said it was a project his parishioners embraced and now look at with pride . <p> " I have got a lot of retirees , " in the parish , he said . When the idea of installing solar panels was first proposed , " they said , ' Let 's go for it , ' which you might not expect from an older parish , but they did . " <p> " We are saving about 10% or 11% " on energy costs , Grace said . " We have also avoided producing about 175 tons of greenhouse gases .
