<p> WILMINGTON , Del. -- Acknowledging that the nation is in a " dark " place , President-elect Joe Biden on Monday said his administration would strengthen the country 's vulnerable economy despite the exploding pandemic as he pushed forward with the business of preparing to assume the presidency . <p> Biden , who will take office on Jan. 20 , offered an optimistic assessment of the next four years during a meeting with several business and labor leaders ahead of an afternoon economic address from his makeshift headquarters in Delaware . <p> " To state the obvious , we seem to be turning a pretty dark corner now , " Biden told the group , their faces projected on video screens , as the meeting began . <p> " I do n't want to be falsely optimistic , " he continued . But " I think we can do this . " <p> Biden , a Democrat , has vowed to spend trillions of dollars to reinvigorate U.S. manufacturing , expand health care coverage and combat climate change , among other priorities . Since defeating Trump , Biden has devoted most of his public remarks to encouraging Americans to wear masks and embrace social distancing measures . <p> But members of his coronavirus advisory board have been more specific .
