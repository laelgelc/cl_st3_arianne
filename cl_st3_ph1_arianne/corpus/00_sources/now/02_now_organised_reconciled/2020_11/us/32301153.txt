File photograph : Getty <p> The Department of Agriculture has denied that Ireland 's forests are a net emitter of greenhouse gasses and insisted they remain " a substantial and growing store for carbon dioxide " . <p> In a detailed response to a report it commissioned the department said strong reactions to the findings " are misleading insofar as they are looking only at one subset of the estate " . The paper concluded that Ireland 's forest estate -- classified as " managed forest land " -- had changed from a sink capturing CO2 during the 2012 to 2017 period . <p> The report entitled FRL 2021-2015 : Ireland , a National Forestry Accounting Plan was prepared with the objective of informing Ireland 's inventory of national greenhouse gases for the next five years , as required by the European Union .
