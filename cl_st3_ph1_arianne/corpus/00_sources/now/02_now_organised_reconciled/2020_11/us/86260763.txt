<p> Last month JPMorgan Chase &amp; Co. announced a commitment to partner with clients to reduce emissions by 2030 in voluntary alignment with the Paris Climate Accord . The firm 's new Center for Carbon Transition will provide " centralized access to sustainability-focused financing , research and advisory solutions , " part of a focus on developing new metrics to measure clients ' environmental impact such as " carbon intensity , " and a commitment to making internal operations carbon neutral this year . <p> Rather than focusing on philanthropy or volunteerism alone , leaders like JPMorgan 's Jamie Dimon are developing strategies to drive social and environmental change through core business operations . By doing so she 's setting expectations for clients and suppliers to do the same . <p> JPMorgan 's announcement reflects a slow but momentous shift in how top business leaders are conceptualizing modern corporate responsibility . That shift that is here to stay . <p> Other major financial players have made similar moves in the past year , demonstrating an embrace of stakeholder capitalism , the concept mainstreamed by the Business Roundtable 's 2019 letter @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " committing to deliver value to all stakeholders , " including customers , employees , suppliers , communities , and shareholders . <p> UBS announced in September 2020 that its preferred solution for clients will now be sustainable investments -- a first-of-its-kind recommendation from a major global financial institution ( sustainable investments include , for example , screening out certain industries like tobacco or selecting in to impact-focused investments ) . Blackrock 's CEO Larry Fink has leveraged his popular annual CEO letters over the past three years to focus on investment risk and climate change , the relationship between purpose and profit , and the importance of corporate social purpose . And Goldman Sachs announced in February 2020 that moving forward , the firm would only underwrite IPOs for companies with at least one diverse board member . <h> More than lip service <p> Critics assert these " announcements " may only pay lip service to social and environmental issues , rather than fundamentally realigning to a more inclusive and equitable economy . The JPMorgan Chase announcement , for example , does not provide many specifics on how the firm will guide clients toward more climate-friendly products and practices , lacks punitive measures , and stops short of completely divorcing from oil and gas companies . Other firms have been criticized for expecting praise in their overdue efforts to address problems they have helped historically create , such as the racial wealth gap . <p> Despite criticisms , the combined potential of corporate commitments to the environment and social impact is not insignificant or insubstantial . As a group , JPMorgan Chase , Blackrock , UBS , and Goldman Sachs hold a combined $13 trillion in assets -- literally 4% of global total wealth ; a sum that is larger than any single country 's GDP , with the exception of China and the United States . In the financial world , the Financial Accounting Standards Board creates and enforces the Generally Accepted Accounting Principles ( GAAP ) . The GAAP are widely understood and used by investors and business leaders , providing a uniform set of metrics used for business comparison and analysis . Impact-weighted accounts take this a step further , integrating a line item directly onto a financial statement that reflects a company 's positive and negative impacts on stakeholders . Individual companies can contribute to these efforts by participating in the frameworks as they are created . <p> Third , we can conceptualize and communicate the value of stakeholder capitalism even more broadly . In today 's digital economy , brand reputation is king : McKinsey estimates that two thirds of the touch points that lead to purchase are reputation factors , such as past experience , word of mouth recommendation , and online reviews . Brands who ignore stakeholder capitalism and exploit employees , suppliers , or communities today do so at the expense of their own reputation and success . On the flip side , businesses who build authentic stakeholder partnerships create greater shared value that can be communicated to consumers and investors .
