While it 's hard to predict with any reliability months out from now , the upward trajectory of cases over the past couple of weeks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all but certain to be followed by an increase in deaths . We already have more deaths per day than we did in July . This time around , the upper Midwest and Great Plains states are being slammed by the virus , hitting new records for their local epidemics . <p> And we 're all about to go inside , windows will be shut as the temperatures drop and we 'll all begin to lower our guard , lower our masks . In fact , that is already happening : It 's small gatherings now that are driving increases in cases in many places . I 'm tired ; you 're tired ; we 're all tired . We crave human contact , to see our friends and family . The holidays will simply amp up this desire to get together to celebrate the fall and winter holidays as we do every year . <p> As much as Trump has botched the response to the virus , it 's not only in the United States that we 're seeing a resurgence in cases . In Europe , countries that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are seeing the virus roar back and instituting new measures to contain the latest outbreaks . <p> As Julia Belluz reports in Vox , most of these countries did n't use the summer wisely to scale up testing and contact tracing , ensure humane isolation and quarantine ( e.g. , paid isolation " leave " ) , institute mandatory mask-wearing , and retro-fit ventilation in places like schools . The countries that did , such as Germany and in Asia-Pacific -- New Zealand , South Korea , and China -- are thus far managing this next phase of the pandemic far better than the rest of us . <p> This suggests that a possible Biden presidency does n't offer a quick fix to our plight , even if we have models of how to proceed in the " success stories " I mentioned above . We 'll see data over the next few months , and even if one or more vaccines is shown to be safe and efficacious , we will need to get it to the people who need it and ensure they are willing to take it . No small feat . Meanwhile , which of the new vaccines might be best ? Right now , there is no plan to test them head-to-head , to answer this most basic question . <p> We can grieve for the past , but going back to normal does n't offer solace to many , particularly when it 's " normal " that got us into this mess . But I also wonder if this is an opportunity to move to new ways of thinking , new ways of being in the world with each other . " <p> What will help us survive this long winter is not sinking into anger and resentment , looking behind us to make America great or " normal " again . And this is n't meant to be a moment of spiritual uplift but an invitation to work . Components of the public health response are there to use to help us through the pandemic , but they are only one set of tools we need right now .
