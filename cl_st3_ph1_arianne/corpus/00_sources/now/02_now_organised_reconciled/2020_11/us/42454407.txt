The subject of that email , " It 's closer than you think -- Please Vote . " Inexplicably , the numbers were trending toward the xenophobia , misogyny , systemic racism and vitriol ticket in the middle of a pandemic that has taken the lives of almost 240,000 Americans and counting . Those numbers indicated that there was a very real possibility that the ticket of death and destruction might win -- again . Thankfully , disaster was diverted . <p> But we should all keep in mind that more than 70 million Americans , including more than 400,000 people in the state of New Mexico do not have a problem with the status quo .
