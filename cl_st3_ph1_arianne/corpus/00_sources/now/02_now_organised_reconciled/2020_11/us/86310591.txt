What 's needed now is cross-partisan cooperation to ensure a smooth and orderly transfer of power . Our national security depends on it . <p> Twenty years ago , the presidential transition was truncated , to disastrous results . Bush , was ultimately declared the winner cut in half the normal transition period , " the report detailed , " hampering the new administration in identifying , recruiting , clearing , and obtaining Senate confirmation of key appointees , " which left our country more vulnerable to attack . <p> Together , along with eight others in a bipartisan group of commissioners , we worked to understand the circumstances that led to the attacks , and to provide recommendations to guard against future ones .
