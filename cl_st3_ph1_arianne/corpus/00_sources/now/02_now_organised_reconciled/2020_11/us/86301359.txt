<p> Here 's the thing about sustainable shopping : There are very few things you can purchase that are actively beneficial for the climate . Unless you 're buying a tree that will suck carbon from the air , most products require land , water and fossil fuels to produce and use . New stuff -- clothes , appliances , bath products , toys , etc. -- inherently comes at some environmental cost . <p> Still , we all need new stuff sometimes ; even I have had to replace the pair of yoga pants I 've lived in since the start of the pandemic . You may be surprised to find that most clothing contains plastic , which is a petroleum product . It requires 32,000 gallons of water to produce the steel for an average passenger car . Unless your purchase represents a significant upgrade from what you already own -- say , swapping out your old gas-guzzling car for an electric vehicle -- you are better off trying to refurbish or repurpose existing items than acquiring more stuff .
