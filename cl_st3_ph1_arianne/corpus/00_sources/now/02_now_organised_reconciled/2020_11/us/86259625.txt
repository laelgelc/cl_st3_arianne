Both are on the ballot this year . <p> Cities and states use markets to encourage renewables by buying clean energy credits for every electron they use . The extra money provides an incentive for businesses to build more clean-electricity generators . Many states have also passed a clean energy mandate : an order to utilities to get more energy from solar panels , wind turbines , hydroelectric dams , or other sources of clean energy .
