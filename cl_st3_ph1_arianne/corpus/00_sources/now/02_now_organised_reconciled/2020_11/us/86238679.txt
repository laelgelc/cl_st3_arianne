<p> Asian Americans are the fastest-growing group of eligible voters in the United States , increasing from 4.6 million in 2000 to 11.1 million in 2020 , according to the Pew Research Center . And in Pennsylvania , one of the states that 's most likely to decide the presidential election , some 4 percent of the voting-age population is Asian American or Pacific Islander . Yet much mainstream political coverage ignores the importance of this crucial demographic . <p> Last month , we traveled across Pennsylvania , profiling 12 Asian American voters in the hopes of rectifying that problem .
