REUTERS/Jonathan Ernst <p> WASHINGTON ( Reuters ) - U.S. President-elect Joe Biden has held separate congratulatory phone calls with the leaders of Australia , Japan and South Korea , his transition office said on Wednesday . <p> All sides expressed their determination to strengthen bilateral ties as well as tackle global issues such as the coronavirus pandemic and climate change , the office said . <p> After defeating President Donald Trump last week , Biden has focused on laying the foundation of his administration , while the Republican incumbent Trump has been pressing ahead with his long-shot strategy to upend his election defeat with lawsuits .
