<h> In Our View : Cheers &amp; Jeers : Tree shines bright ; stow signs <p> The Columbian <p> Published : November 28 , 2020 , 6:03am <p> Share : <p> Cheers : To holiday spirit . Although traditional public gatherings have been canceled during the holiday season because of the coronavirus pandemic , a small sense of normalcy is visible in Esther Short Park . A large tree there , near the bell tower , has been decorated with the festive lights we are accustomed to seeing this time of year . <p> Because of COVID-19 concerns , the Rotary Club of Vancouver canceled the large public gathering that typically accompanies the lighting of the tree , and a holiday market in the nearby Hilton Vancouver Washington also has been canceled . While those cancellations are a reminder of the pandemic that has altered daily life , the tree will light up the night for the next several weeks and provide a bit of much-needed Christmas cheer . <p> Jeers : To leftover signs . Campaign signs still dot the roadside in many parts of Clark County , weeks after the election has come and gone . <p> For signs on public property , candidates should quickly form a team to remove them . For signs on private property , homeowners should properly dispose of the signs ; most signs are plastic and therefore can not be recycled .
