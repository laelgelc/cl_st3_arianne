<p> The United Kingdom will ban the sale of new combustion-engine vehicles by 2030 , Prime Minister Boris Johnson announced today . It will also ban the sale of new hybrid cars by 2035 . Johnson made the announcement tonight as part of a new ten-point plan for a " green industrial revolution . " <p> This is the second time Johnson has moved up the deadline . The original plan was to stop sales of petrol and diesel-powered vehicles by 2040 . Back in February , Johnson moved the target to 2035 . He 's come under increasing pressure to crack down on gas-guzzling cars in order to meet the UK 's broader goal of eliminating emissions contributing to climate change by 2050 . <p> " Although this year has taken a very different path to the one we expected , the UK is looking to the future and seizing the opportunity to build back greener , " Johnson said in an emailed statement released to the press . " The recovery of our planet and of our economies can and must go hand-in-hand .
