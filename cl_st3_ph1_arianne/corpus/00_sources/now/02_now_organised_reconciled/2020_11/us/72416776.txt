<h> The two young Jews behind a Biden PAC <p> Just three months before the presidential election , a pair young Jewish political activists formed the Jewish Unity Political Action Committee . It raised all of $31,000 , and every cent of it was spent supporting Joe Biden and Kamala Harris . <p> Led by the co-founders , Isabel Baer of Westchester , N.Y . and Ethan Wolf of Deerfield , Ill. , the group micro-targeted pockets of young Jews in the battleground states of Michigan , Pennsylvania and Florida , encouraging them to register and vote for the Biden ticket . <p> While it 's impossible to say how much of a difference the Jews for Unity PAC made , Biden did carry Michigan and Pennsylvania , and Wolf said videos the PAC produced and streamed were seen over one million times . <p> " We had a great candidate in Joe Biden , and we wanted to prevent Donald Trump from winning another election , " said Wolf , who is 21 and a student at Ohio State University . Typically , most Jews favor Democratic candidates , and three post-election surveys this month indicated as much although the accuracy of any poll is now under scrutiny , given how so many missed the mark in the two most recent presidential elections . <p> J Street , a liberal-leaning lobbying group , found Biden had a big edge among Jewish voters , 77 percent to 21 percent , while the Republic Jewish Coalition had it closer , 60.6 percent for Biden to 30.5 percent for Trump . A survey by The Associated Press , a nonpolitical voice in tracking election results , fell in between , with 68 percent for Biden and 30 percent for Trump . <p> Young people of all religions this year voted in substantially higher numbers than in 2016 .
