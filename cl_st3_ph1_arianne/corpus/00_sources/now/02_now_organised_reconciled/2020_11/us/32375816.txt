Hawaii went from having the lowest unemployment in the country almost overnight to the highest . State GDP saw a 40% drop in the second quarter alone . <p> Climate change is on fast-forward , exacerbating the impact and frequency of natural disasters -- at a huge human cost . Flooding of biblical proportions . <p> Dumpster fire on top of dumpster fire . <p> Our daily lives have been turned upside down with parents trying to juggle working from home and overseeing their children 's remote class lessons in stressful days that seem endless . We know that isolation and lack of social interaction combined with constant worry and tension are creating a mental health crisis for many of our islanders . <p> As the year draws to a close , if you have n't lost your job or been hospitalized with COVID-19 , you may want to gather with your downsized immediate family over a smaller turkey spread and talk about how to turn compassion into action and help so many in need . <p> Whether you give financially or find ways to volunteer remotely to help your favorite cause , this season it is more important @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ means spending less money , a meaningful gift can be made , with the whole family joining in the discussion about paying it forward .
