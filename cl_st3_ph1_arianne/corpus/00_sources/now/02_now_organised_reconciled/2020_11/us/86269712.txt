<h> Wildfire Disaster Sparks Emergency Strategy Rethink in Australia <p> ( Bloomberg ) -- Australia will introduce new laws to allow the federal government to declare a state of emergency as part of its new strategy to avoid a repeat of last summer 's unprecedented wildfire season . <p> The government will also enhance its emergency management capability , ensure data about disasters including wildfires from the eight states and territories are better collated and accessible , and create a national disaster recovery agency . <p> " It 's important to leave a legacy from this tragic event over the summer , to make sure we learn the lessons and to build on that , to build a more resilient Australia , " the minister for emergency management , David Littleproud , told reporters in Canberra on Friday . <p> Littleproud announced the government would be supporting all the recommendations contained in the report into the bushfires .
