But , in other South-East Asian countries like Singapore and Malaysia , there was no increase in the rates of reported cases . <p> The dengue virus is a growing threat in particularly in some Asian and Latin American countries , all thanks to climate change . The Aedes mosquito is responsible for transmitting dengue . Symptoms include fever , nausea and vomiting , headache , and muscle and joint pain . <p> Tropical and sub-tropical climates allow the dengue virus to thrive mostly in urban and semi-urban areas . The World Health Organization ( WHO ) reported that the number of cases increased 8-fold globally over the last 20 years . Slowly but steadily , the dengue virus is also starting to spread to Mediterranean countries in Europe . <p> What makes dengue a potentially lethal disease for which there is no cure or effective vaccine is that there are four serotypes of the dengue virus . This means it is possible to be infected four times . The only way to effectively tackle the dengue virus is to detect it at an early stage before dengue fever progresses to the dreaded dengue hemorrhagic fever . <p> A patient with dengue hemorrhagic fever will suffer from severe internal bleeding and goes into shock or experiences a sudden drop in blood pressure . Without timely platelet transfusions and intensive medical treatment , the disease can be fatal . <p> In Thailand , Singapore , and Malaysia , government agencies enforced total shutdowns for three months . Lead author Jue Tao Lim of the National University of Singapore and his colleagues analyzed dengue case counts using national surveillance data available through mid-2020 . They also obtained further information on climate , COVID-19 interventions , and overall population census data . <p> Data was included from 2010 for Thailand and Malaysia and from 2012 for Singapore until mid-2020 . The team also referred to local and national government websites to factor in the exact duration of physical distancing measures . <p> They found that in 2019 , the average number of reported dengue cases for 10,000 individuals was 1.5 , 2.15 , and 0.31 in Thailand , Singapore , and Malaysia , respectively . <p> In Thailand , the researchers further highlighted that social distancing is expected to lead to 4.32 additional cases per 100,000 individuals every single month . A few examples of places where water accumulates are from plumbing leaks , bathrooms , gardens with plant pots , and even in AC ducts that are not regularly cleaned . <p> Previous research has found that residential areas are a focal point of transmission . Around 60% of dengue cases live less than 200 meters apart from each other and come from the same transmission chain .
