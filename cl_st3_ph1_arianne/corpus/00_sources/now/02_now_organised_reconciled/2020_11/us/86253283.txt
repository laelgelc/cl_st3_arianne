They chug along quietly and uneventfully until , one day , they unexpectedly turn our world upside down . Hurricanes and pandemics , elections and market crashes - all inevitable products of complex systems - ceaselessly remind us of our limited understanding of the world . What 's missing is the ability to notice and comprehend the counterintuitive nature of complex systems .
