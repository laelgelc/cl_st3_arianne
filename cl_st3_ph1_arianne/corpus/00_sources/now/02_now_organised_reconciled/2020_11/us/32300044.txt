It 's the most aggressive challenge the DOJ has made against a tech giant since it accused Microsoft of anti-competitive practices in 1997 . The lawsuit homes in on Google 's search dominance in the United States ( Google represents 80 percent , according to the complaint ) and the practices and deals that have solidified that claim .
