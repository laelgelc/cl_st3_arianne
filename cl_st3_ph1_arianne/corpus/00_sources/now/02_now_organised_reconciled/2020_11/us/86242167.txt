Among these are achieving " technological self-sufficiency " and becoming a " middle income country . " By most standards this implies a target of at least doubling and even tripling per capita Gross Domestic Product ( GDP ) by 2035 . The CCP also reiterated its announced goals under the Paris climate accord to reach " peak CO2 emissions " by 2020 while reducing the overall " energy intensity " of its economy . In September President Xi had set a goal of China attaining " carbon neutrality " by 2060 . In 2000 China already accounted for 13.6 percent of global CO2 emissions , but by 2017 its share had reached 27 percent . Total carbon emissions by China expanded slightly in 2018 and 2019 , reaching a total figure of about 10 billion metric tons ( BT ) per year . In contrast , China is the world leader in building new coal-fired generation plants and is on track to grant more permits for new coal facilities in 2020 than in 2018 or 2019 .
