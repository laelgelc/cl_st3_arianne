<h> Piedmont students make NC Latinx 20 Under 20 <p> Surry Community College 's early college high school programs have a lot to be proud of . Nancy Garcia Villa , Mariana Vazquez Gallardo and graduate Leslie Hernandez are a part of the North Carolina Latinx 20 Under 20 . <p> Hernandez , a Yadkin Early College High School graduate and current UNC Greensboro student , explained what the recognition meant to her . <p> " I did it not only to highlight my honors and achievements , but also to represent my family , " said Hernandez . <p> The other part of making the Latinx 20 Under 20 list is giving back to the community . Hernandez was busy during her time at Yadkin Early College High School . She was the indoor girls soccer team captain and leader of the Juntos 4-H organization . Hernandez spoke highly of her 4-H experience . <p> " To me it was 4-H. 4-H gave me a lot .
