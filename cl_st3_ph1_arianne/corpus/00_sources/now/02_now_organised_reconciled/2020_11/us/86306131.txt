<h> Opinions The four-year nightmare that awaits Joe Biden <p> Take a deep breath . Savor the fact that President Trump has only a couple of months left to harm the country -- with the power of the federal government , anyway . Then replace the relief with realism . All the huge problems Trump made worse require the nation to make the kind of progress the political system has not managed in a decade , or longer , and Joe Biden will face higher obstacles to getting anything done . <p> Once inaugurated , President Biden will face either a Republican Senate or one that is barely Democratic -- and most likely the former . Sen. Mitch McConnell ( R-Ky. ) , a master of obstruction , once again leads the opposition . But Biden faces an added pressure : Failure is not an option . The consequences of more delay would be human misery on a vast scale . And Trump will have left Biden a mountain of new federal debt that makes any big policy push harder . <p> To make any headway , Biden will have to build congressional coalitions that span the gap between Sen. Biden will have to satisfy Democrats with half-a-loaf politics , even as he persuades enough Republicans that it is in their interest to give him anything at all . And , when he fails , he will have to use his executive powers in a way that does not lay the groundwork for the next Donald Trump to abuse those authorities . As he does so , Biden will also face a hostile Supreme Court . Staying home next time , or the time after that , or the time after that because you do n't have Trump to vote against will only drag the country backward again .
