<p> ( Bloomberg ) -- U.K. Prime Minister Boris Johnson announced a 12 billion-pound ( $15.9 billion ) plan to boost green industries and tackle climate change , in a blueprint he says will create or support as many as 250,000 jobs . <p> As he seeks to relaunch his premiership and revive the economy , Johnson aims to use his plan for a " green industrial revolution " to deliver on his pledge to invest in former industrial heartlands that voted for him at last year 's election . <p> Under the wide-ranging proposals , sales of new petrol and diesel cars will be banned from 2030 , the government will back investment in electric vehicles , hydrogen , wind and nuclear power , and measures to make homes more energy efficient . <p> " Although this year has taken a very different path to the one we expected , I have n't lost sight of our ambitious plans to level up across the country , " Johnson said in a statement released by his office .
