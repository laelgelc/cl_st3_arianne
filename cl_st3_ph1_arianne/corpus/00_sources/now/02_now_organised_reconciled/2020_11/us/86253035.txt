<h> As African penguin population dwindles , researchers plan new colony <p> CAPE TOWN ( Reuters ) - South African researchers plan to release scores of abandoned , hand-reared African penguin chicks at the Western Cape 's De Hoop nature reserve , boosting efforts to start a new breeding colony of the seabirds at risk of extinction . <p> The only penguin that breeds in Africa , it was once South Africa 's most abundant seabird . But the population plunged to around 13,000 breeding pairs last year , from more than 1 million pairs in the 1920s , when their eggs started to be harvested for human consumption , government data shows . <p> Researchers have since January 2019 deployed dummy penguins that emit the distinctive call of the birds to try to entice them onshore at De Hoop , 230 km ( 140 miles ) southeast of Cape Town .
