<p> " If you vote for Biden , he 'll listen to the scientists , " Donald Trump told a crowd of thousands at a recent campaign rally in Carson City , Nevada . On both topics , his contention is the same : that the sorts of policies they might recommend -- from measures to control the spread of Covid to participation in international climate accords -- would only hamper economic growth .
