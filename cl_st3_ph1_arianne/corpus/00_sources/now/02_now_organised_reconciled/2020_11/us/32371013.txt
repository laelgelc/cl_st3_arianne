<h> Today 's letters : Keeping kids outdoors can be good for learningBack to video <p> I live near two elementary schools that share a large field and I have noticed that the kids are outside a lot more than they used to be . In part , it may be that their lunch hours and recesses are staggered but it is also because classes are being conducted outside , even music . <p> This would be a marvellous opportunity for Ontario schools to look more closely at Udeskole , the Scandinavian concept of outdoor school . They use outdoor activity for more than phys-ed as they incorporate many aspects of their curriculum into outdoor and nature activities . It 's obvious that children would be more active , have a greater sense of well-being , be more connected to the outdoor world and probably enjoy school more . <p> Ontario keeps implementing education reform and swinging back and forth between new ideas and a return to basics .
