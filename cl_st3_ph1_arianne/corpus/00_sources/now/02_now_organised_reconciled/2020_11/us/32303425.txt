According to a report released on Thursday ( October 29 ) by the Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform for Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services ( IPBES ) , an independent intergovernmental body based in Germany that focuses on biodiversity conservation , based on around 700 scientific studies , has highlighted the link between degrading of nature and increasing pandemic risks .
