<h> Record number of named Atlantic storms <p> This year 's Atlantic hurricane season has broken the record for the number of named storms , the US National Hurricane Center says . <p> Subtropical storm Theta in the north-east Atlantic is the 29th , breaking the previous record of 28 set in 2005 . <p> Forecasters say another system is forming in the Caribbean which could be named in the near future . <p> Climate change is being blamed by many scientists for the increasing intensity of storms . <p> Particularly dangerous storms are given names to raise public awareness before they strike . <p> The hurricane season , which runs from June to November , has produced several powerful storms including Eta , which struck Florida at the weekend after causing destruction and killing dozens in parts of Central America . <p> Zeta hit Louisiana at the end of October , becoming the fifth named storm to make landfall in the state this season . <p> Theta , not currently a hurricane , is moving north-eastwards towards southern Europe . <p> Forecasters are watching the formation of another possible @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This season is also the first since 2005 during which the 21 alphabetical names set by the World Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) have run out because of the number of storms .
