<h> UPDATE 1-BoE 's Bailey urges banks to commit to post-COVID recovery <p> LONDON , Nov 17 ( Reuters ) - Britain 's economy is undergoing lasting changes as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic and the financial services industry must make a " major commitment " to support the investment that will be needed , Bank of England Governor Andrew Bailey said . <p> While the pandemic was unlikely to prompt the huge shifts in Britain 's industrial makeup that took place in the 1980s and 1990s , Bailey said there was no room for complacency . <p> " None of us have good answers yet to how much these changes will persist , or even increase . But my best guess is that there will be lasting changes , " Bailey said in a speech to TheCityUk , a financial services industry group . <p> The changes brought by COVID , and the challenge of addressing climate change , would require a " major commitment " from the financial services industry , Bailey said . <p> They also underlined the importance behind a drive at the BoE to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ investment . <p> Bailey warned that authorities would keep an eye on risks from higher debt levels . <p> " While the current low level of interest rates supports the sustainability of UK corporate debt , higher leverage would make the corporate sector more vulnerable to interest rate or earnings shocks , " he said . <p> Britain 's economy on course for its worst year in a century and Bailey pointed to a muted outlook for investment next year because of the pandemic but also uncertainty about Brexit . <p> But the recent news about positive COVID-19 vaccine trials represented " some light at the end of the tunnel , " he said .
