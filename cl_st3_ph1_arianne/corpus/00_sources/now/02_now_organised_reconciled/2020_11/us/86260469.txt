<p> When I last wrote for Forbes Finance Council , I took care to show how , in the absence of U.S. regulatory action on climate change , institutional investors are nevertheless taking it upon themselves to adapt the metrics and methodologies needed to decarbonize their portfolios and disclose the material impacts of these efforts . Unsurprisingly , I 've found that I am not alone in the conclusions I drew . Only financial regulators , equipped with the necessary data intelligence , can develop standardized and consistent frameworks for classifying physical and transition risks , exposures and the impacts of financial sector climate-alignment efforts . <p> And even if I 'm mistaken , as the U.S. continues struggling with the Covid-19 pandemic , it 's unlikely the country will jump into regulatory action anytime soon .
