<h> As private space starts booming , the footprint of the industry could become massive . Making the rocket fuel from CO2 could help . <p> When SpaceX launches its Starship rocket , designed to send crew members to the International Space Station , deliver satellites to space , and travel to Mars as early as 2024 , each launch will emit an estimated 715 tons of CO2 , roughly as much as 10 cross-country flights on a 747 . If spaceflight becomes common -- and if the rocket is also used for quick trips in place of jets on Earth -- it could become a meaningful source of emissions .
