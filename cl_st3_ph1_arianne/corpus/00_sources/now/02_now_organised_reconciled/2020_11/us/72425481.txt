" We started to do outreach to others and then it snowballed really quickly . The 29 founding members employ hundreds of thousands of people across all 50 states , according to the organization . <p> Notably absent from the coalition are the " Big Three " American car manufacturers : General Motors , Ford , and Fiat/Chrysler . <p> " I think our interest is a little different , " said Britton . " We have a goal to accelerate and reach this 2030 EV sales goal and I think we bring together this kind of squarely EV group to make progress on those goals . " <p> ZETA hopes to push the United States to 100% electric vehicles through national policy . They have five actions that it says will lead to its goal , including consumer incentives , upgraded infrastructure , and American manufacturing .
