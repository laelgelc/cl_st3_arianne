<h> Biden signals sharp shift from Trump with Cabinet picks <p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- President-elect Joe Biden is tapping Obama-era officials for top national security positions . He 's signaling a shift from the Trump administration 's " America First " policies that disparaged international alliances . The six picks announced Monday mark a return to a more traditional approach to America 's relations with the rest of the world . Biden will nominate longtime adviser Antony Blinken to be secretary of state , lawyer Alejandro Mayorkas to be homeland security secretary and Linda Thomas-Greenfield to be ambassador to the United Nations . Former Secretary of State John Kerry will be Biden 's climate change envoy .
