<p> U.K. Prime Minister Boris Johnson is preparing to announce a 10-point climate plan next week , according to reports , in order to have an ambitious policy to showcase at next year 's COP26 UN climate summit in Glasgow , Scotland . <p> The U.K. has already set a goal of reducing emissions to net zero by 2050 , mirroring the European Union 's plan . But so far Johnson has n't produced a detailed plan for how the U.K. will reach this target .
