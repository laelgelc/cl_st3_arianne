<h> Greening Our Community : Tallahassee -- A Green Industry Frontier <p> I spent the last 10 years running businesses for a New York-based global food and agriculture company . One of the businesses sold biodigesters to Brazilian pig farmers . Biodigesters are systems that cover pig waste lagoons ( they are exactly what they sound like ) , flare the trapped methane , and generate carbon credits under the United Nations-backed trading markets . These were complex deals that involved all sorts of specialists : financiers , engineers , attorneys , logistic managers , and commodity traders . <p> This is fine for one deal , but to do many deals it would be helpful to be able to select and target knowledge to reach the widest audience . For instance , teaching basic economics and engineering elements to our sales team allows them to screen good biodigester candidates among their farming customers . This reduces the number of initial visits the specialists have to make , thereby lowering the costs of each transaction . We can reinvest these savings by hiring more relationship managers and reaching more geographies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Scale is taking a successful technology and making it available to a large number of users . Not only does the technology have to work , but users have to be convinced that they need it , manufacturers that they can produce it , and business owners that they can make money from it . <p> If you can accomplish these goals , then climate change technologies can achieve the upper end of their greenhouse gas ( GHG ) reduction targets . Teaching interdisciplinary skills and concentrating them where they have the most impact is the next big frontier for generating climate change solutions . The best place to teach those skills , I think , is at a university where interdisciplinary knowledge can take root . <p> This is where Tallahassee comes in . The city can become a hub for producing climate change problem solvers . It 's a field of employment that does n't really exist yet and Tallahassee can invent it . FSU and FAMU are rich in the climate-and-society ingredients needed to develop pragmatic climate change solutions programs . <p> FSU has a deep bench @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and regional planning . The School of Communication is doing innovative work in sustainability through filmmaking , and teaching students how to think critically about climate change information and recognize greenwashing . <p> FAMU has a rich land grant mission to serve Florida communities through education and research in agriculture , bioenergy , and land use . So much opportunity for reducing GHGs in the atmosphere runs through these three sectors . <p> The two universities already work together through the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering to produce promising climate change technology ( e.g. , chemical-free fertilizer , biodegradable slow-release pods to hydrate crop roots ) . Both have sustainable campus groups for engaging the local community in green initiatives . Both have robust undergraduate programs in environment and sustainability . A graduate level program that teaches students how to bring all these disciplines together to scale greenhouse gas reductions is the next logical step . <p> Forming public/private partnerships will be key for interdisciplinary climate programs to take root in Tallahassee . Companies teach students their businesses and shape the challenges they face when they de-carbonize . Students come to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ allowing them to move beyond advocacy . Research benefits from lessons learned from this interaction . The FSU Jim Moran College of Entrepreneurship , the Domi Station at Railroad Avenue , and the new startup incubator at Innovation Park can be springboards for graduates who wish to start their own climate change solutions businesses here in Tallahassee . <p> I 'm collaborating with colleagues at both universities and the FSU Office of Research to explore building a graduate education program in climate change solutions . The goal is to teach students climate science , quantitative analysis , solutions scaffolding , business plans , and social context so they know how to slice through complex situations to reduce GHGs for organizations . Our goal is to make it available to students from all backgrounds . <p> I witnessed a real demand for these skills in my business and think it extends well beyond agriculture . It 's early days but we hope to roll out surveys and focus groups to quantify student demand for this type of program .
