<h> State Democratic leadership needs ' drastic overhaul ' Opinion <p> On Nov. 3 , Florida Democrats experienced devastating losses when we were expecting to make gains . After so much work from organizers throughout the state , especially from young organizers , this is an awful outcome for Florida 's future . This gives a mandate to Gov . DeSantis and Republican leadership in the House and Senate to enact far-right policies next session and beyond . That is squarely the fault of state Democratic leadership . <p> We call on Florida Democratic Party state chair Terrie Rizzo and Florida Democratic Party executive director Juan Pe ? alosa to resign . We need a drastic overhaul of state party leadership to make the gains we need to flip Florida blue -- and more importantly , to serve Floridians in the ways they need , not at the whim of corporate interests and favoritism at the state level . <p> We 're standing in solidarity with calls from state Sen. Shevrin Jones and Rep. Anna Eskamani . We desperately need new leadership in Florida that organizes communities more than once @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ part of the state , especially in Miami-Dade , while progressive policies passed with an overwhelming majority . Amendment 2 increased wages for more than a quarter of Florida 's workers . The fact that numerous high-ranking state Democrats had reservations on raising the minimum wage , which should be a top legislative priority , demonstrates how out of touch the Florida Democratic Party is with Florida voters . <p> The fear of being " too left-wing " for voters is partly unfounded , especially when Miami-Dade was one of four counties in the state that passed Amendment 2 with more than 70% of the vote . The young organizers in Florida know what it takes to connect with voters and serve our communities in a way that party leadership is wildly unfamiliar with . <p> We must connect with and listen to Floridians , building community coalitions continuously , not just when Democrats need votes in a presidential or midterm election . The solution is clear : The Florida Democratic Party needs to give voters a reason to vote for them , not just reasons to vote against Republicans @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organizing strategies , because the future of Florida depends on it . We need elected officials who will take swift and meaningful action to combat climate change ; support the interests of workers , unions , and students ; expand health-care access ; protect immigrant and LGBTQ+ rights ; fight for equity for BIPOC Floridians ; stop corporate handouts on both sides of the aisle ; call for community control of law enforcement ; and be uncompromising in opposition to the rising tides of far-right policy . <p> Especially , we need leaders who respect activists on the ground who have been working to address these issues for decades .
