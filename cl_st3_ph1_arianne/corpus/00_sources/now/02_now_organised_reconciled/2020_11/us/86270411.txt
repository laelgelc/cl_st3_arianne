<h> Fed moves closer to joining global peers in climate-change fight <p> ( Reuters ) - The Federal Reserve is moving closer to joining an international group of central bankers working to understand and reduce climate change risk , paving the way for the world 's most influential central bank to help shape global financial markets ' response to a warming world . <p> Fed Chair Jerome Powell on Thursday said the U.S. central bank had been working with the Network of Central Banks and Supervisors for Greening the Financial System ( NGFS ) , and had been attending meetings . " I think we are in the process of applying for membership there now , " he said . <p> Fed Governor Randal Quarles told lawmakers in testimony on Tuesday , " We have requested membership , " adding , " I expect that it will be granted .
