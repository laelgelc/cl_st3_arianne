<h> Accelerating These 3 Trends Would Reshape The Energy Industry <p> Decarbonization , decentralization and digitalization could facilitate the industry 's shift to zero-carbon energy . <p> Shutterstock <p> For years , interest in the energy transition , the sector 's transformation from fossil-based to zero-carbon systems , has been building . Such an enormous shift takes time , of course , but the pandemic has given the world an opportunity to accelerate it -- if we make the right choices . <p> According to estimates from the World Meteorological Organization , CO2 emissions worldwide are projected to be 4 to 7 percent lower for 2020 compared to a year earlier , due to restrictions in travel and other activities put in place to stave off the coronavirus . In April , during the peak of COVID lockdowns , CO2 emissions plunged a record 17 percent . <p> The risk is that once we move beyond the recent resurgence of infections and get COVID-19 under control , energy demand and emissions could simply return to pre-pandemic levels .
