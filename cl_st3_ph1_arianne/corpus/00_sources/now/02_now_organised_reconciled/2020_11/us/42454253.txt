Recently , our skies have been hazy and our air quality has been poor from fires around us that we were not responsible for . That is why it is so important to educate our friends , neighbors and children on the importance of fire safety . <p> Did you know there is a pollutant in wildfire smoke called particle pollution ? It 's a mix of solid and liquid particles in the air that are no bigger than one-third the diameter of a hair strand . When these particles are inhaled , they can lodge in the lungs and possibly trigger asthma attacks , strokes , etc . A few studies of children in California showed that those who breathed in smoky air had more coughing spells , wheezing , bronchitis , and were more likely to be admitted to the hospital for respiratory issues . <p> Wildfires are essential for the change and renewal of ecosystems but it can become dangerous when out of control .
