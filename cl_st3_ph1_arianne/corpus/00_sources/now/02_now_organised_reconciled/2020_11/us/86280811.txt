The Rhodes Scholarships are postgraduate awards providing transformative educational opportunities . Established in 1903 , they are the oldest and perhaps the most prestigious international graduate scholarship program in the world . Nearly 8,000 Rhodes Scholars have gone on to serve at the forefront of government , education , the arts , NGOs , commerce , research and other sectors .
