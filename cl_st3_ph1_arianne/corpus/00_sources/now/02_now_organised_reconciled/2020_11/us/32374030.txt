<p> When the first automobiles were being sold at the beginning of the twentieth century , motorists brought their own containers to purchase gasoline at hardware stores and pharmacies . It was n't long before entrepreneurs were selling gas on the side of the road . In 1905 , America was buying 25,000 vehicles per year , and the first commercial curbside pumps began operating in St. Louis , Missouri . Five years later , there were over half a million vehicles travelling U.S. roads . In 2013 , the first full service filling station was built in Pittsburg , Pennsylvania . There was good money to be made selling gasoline and performing routine maintenance , and by 1930 there were over 120,000 service stations operating in 48 states , about the same number as there are today . <p> According to Statista , the U.S. currently has about 25,000 electric vehicle charging stations .
