<p> ( Reuters ) - A federal court judge on Friday for the second time in two years blocked drilling on more than 300,000 acres in Wyoming because the government failed to adequately consider its impact on climate change . <p> According to court papers , Judge Ralph Contreras of the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia ordered that the environmental analyses for 282 oil and gas leases be sent back to the U.S. Bureau of Land Management ( BLM ) for additional study . <p> It was the second time Judge Contreras issued such a ruling on the same set of leases .
