The inquest is set to begin on Monday , November 30 and a decision could take a minimum of 10 days . If successful , Ella will be the first person in the U.K. , and potentially in the world , for who air pollution is listed as a cause of death . <p> Air pollution kills an estimated seven million people worldwide , according to the World Health Organization ( WHO ) data .
