<h> Letters to the Editor <p> Share <p> Many small shops make towns unique <p> To the editor , <p> A big thank you to Tom Murphy for his columns in the Kennebunk Post as well as many community leaders who have stepped up during these very trying times . His writings brought back childhood memories , made me think of life way back then . <p> There were many kids in the neighborhood , games of Frisbee , kickball , softball . There was a strong sense of community . There was a variety of shops and talkative owners on Main Street . There still are . <p> As we approach the holidays , I think of those many small shops and businesses that make the Kennebunks so unique . I have traveled in northern New England extensively , and I think of how some towns have kept their character ; others have been overdeveloped and become another copy of Anytown , USA . <p> We are still unique here , but many have taken a hit . As we approach the holidays I think of the shopping options @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like we have here in our towns . <p> I understand your deep concerns about health and preference for online shopping . If however , you are comfortable being in public , please consider gifts or gift certificates . <p> I have often found very unusual gifts , artwork and products made by Maine craftspeople in these shops . When I walk in , I am often greeted by name ; no feeling lost in a warehouse or put in a phone tree . <p> Many of our local businesspeople also serve on local boards , committees and volunteer to make the Kennebunks a better place . <p> Please support them this holiday season . <p> Ward Hansen <p> Kennebunk <p> Flag flying position questioned <p> To the editor , <p> As a veteran , I know that when two flags are displayed at the same level , the American flag should be flown to the flag 's own right of other flags , which is the position of prominence .
