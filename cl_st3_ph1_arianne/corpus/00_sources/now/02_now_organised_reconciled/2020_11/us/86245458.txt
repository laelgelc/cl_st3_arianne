<p> As California reels from this year 's record wildfires , the state announced on Thursday that it would prevent insurance companies from dropping homeowners for one year in many parts of the state , a sign of the growing financial turmoil caused by climate change . <p> The measure , which applies to almost one-fifth of the state 's residential insurance market , prohibits companies from canceling or refusing to renew insurance policies for 2.1 million households in or near areas hit by this year 's wildfires . <p> The announcement reflects the increasing strain that climate change has placed on California , which had imposed a similar moratorium once before , at a smaller scale .
