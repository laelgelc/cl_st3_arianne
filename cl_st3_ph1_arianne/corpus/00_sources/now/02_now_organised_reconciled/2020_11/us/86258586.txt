Countries across the world have started to consider a hydrogen-based economy as the answer to the growing concerns over increasing carbon emissions , energy security , and climate change . Global hydrogen production is forecast to more than double , reaching 168 million tons by 2030 from 71 million tons in 2020 , with revenue expected to reach $420 billionin 2030 from $177.3 billion in 2020 . <p> " For the hydrogen economy to become a reality , decisive government actions are required in four key areas , " said Swagath Navin Manohar , Industry Analyst , Industrial Practice at Frost &amp; Sullivan .
