Smoking bees is a technique that has been used for millennia by beekeepers from around the world . The smoke triggers a feeding response in bees as they gorge in preparation for fleeing a burning hive . Feeding bees are less likely to sting . Smoke also dulls and confuses the bees ' sensitive chemical communication signals -- including the alarm pheromone released when a bee stings . This helps keep the mood of the hive calm . <p> But growing tourism industries can also pose a danger to the animals .
