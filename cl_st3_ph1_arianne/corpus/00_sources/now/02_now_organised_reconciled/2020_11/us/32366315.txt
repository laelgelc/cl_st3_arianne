" Col. Karl Jansen , commander of the St. Paul District , said the decision followed " an exhaustive review " and extensive work with federal and state regulators , Native American tribes , environmental groups and Enbridge . <p> " I believe our decision is based on sound science and strikes the balance between protecting natural resources and allowing reasonable development , " Jansen said . <p> All that remains in the six-year-old process now is for the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency to issue a storm water construction permit to protect surface waters from runoff while it 's being built , and then for the independent Public Utilities Commission to give a final green light .
