After a standard " Hi , nice to meet you , " they walk up and put their arms around a person . It 's a built-in familiarity that has almost been lost during the COVID-19 era . <p> " They 're just like that , " Burns said about his two children after they ran off during a Halloween party in Fourth Ward . " They take to people really fast . " <p> Burns and his wife , Bianca , started Boxing for Bullies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after he saw the impact of bullying on the youth within his own family . He was inspired to help after watching a video of his younger cousin being attacked and bullied at school . <p> By teaching children the basics of boxing and self-esteem , he wants to prevent school shootings and deaths by suicide . He calls it boxing therapy . <p> The pandemic has pushed the workouts outdoors . The groups is typically made up of children and adults , including some divorcees and , at times , a two-time cancer survivor . <p> The program is part of the Fourth Ward Health and Educational Center for Youth , a nonprofit organization in Historic Freedmen 's Town . The center 's mission is to create a better way of life for inner-city youth through health and educational programs . <p> Charonda Johnson , the center 's president and vice president of the Freedman 's Town Association , said the boxing program was borne from a need to instill confidence and self-esteem in the community 's youth without having to leave the neighborhood . <p> There are no @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . She remembers when it was n't safe to gather for an outdoor workout . New neighbors , homegrown community leaders and programs like Boxing for Bullies has made the neighborhood a vastly different place , she said . <p> " Gentrification has affected us , but we have n't had to relocate to a new place . We have been fortunate to own properties that we can rent out as affordable housing . " <p> Burns will train anywhere in Houston , but the Boxing for Bullies program is central to Fourth Ward . That was done by design , Johnson said . <p> The classes are Mondays , Wednesdays and Fridays at various outdoor locations , like the sandlot at Carnegie Vanguard High School on Taft Street .
