However , seven out of the last 12 years has seen less than average rainfall . <p> With more than two-thirds of the area under rain-fed irrigation , this is a critical risk for the farmers -- more than 80 per cent of whom belong to the small and marginal category having limited resources to recover from crisis situations . <p> Munni Bai , a farmer in Haldi village while speaking of the changes in climate , said : " It is important for us to look at solutions and actions that help us cope with such changes . When we sow our kharif crop in June , we are not sure how the weather patterns will behave and we are trying to address the scenario as it develops over the entire cropping season . " <p> Cotton is an input intensive crop that uses large amounts of water , fertilisers and pesticides . This leads to increased cultivation costs . At a time when the cotton crop was facing pest attacks , farmers had to rely and use organic pesticides to address them . " <p> Farmers are slowly realising that it is possible to control pest attacks by using organic inputs in place of chemical pesticides .
