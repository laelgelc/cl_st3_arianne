<h> A cooperative presidency <p> According to a survey of international relations scholars , foreign governments will be more willing to cooperate with the United States if Democratic Presidential nominee Joe Biden wins the 2020 election instead of Donald Trump . Results from a Teaching , Research , &amp; International Policy ( TRIP ) survey of 708 international relations scholars at US universities overwhelmingly agree Biden will be better able to achieve his foreign policy agenda . <p> NYT News Service <p> 2/5 <h> Voting for Biden <p> By a significant margin , 95 per cent of the experts agreed that Biden will be a more effective foreign policy president than Trump ( 5 percent ) . Further , if the election were held today , more than 94 per cent of IR faculty who are eligible to vote would cast a vote for Biden , and 5 percent would vote for Trump , the survey found . " Despite concerns about foreign election interference , most scholars are confident that Russia , Iran , and China will not significantly impact the outcome of the election . <p> PTI <p> 3/5 <h> China 's long term threat <p> They do believe , however , that foreign efforts to interfere will undermine American citizens ' confidence in election results , " the survey said , adding that the experts see Russia as the " most pernicious " of the three states when it comes to election meddling , " even though they see the rise of Chinese power and influence as a greater long-term threat to the United States than the rise of Russian power and influence . " <p> Getty Images <p> 4/5 <h> Some similarities too <p> The experts also believe that Trump and Biden 's foreign policies would differ most markedly in the areas of climate change , engagement with multilateral organisations , and global public health . The scholars surveyed see the most similarities between the two candidates ' policies on exchange rate policy , counterterrorism , and the use of military force abroad . <p> AFP <p> 5/5 <h> Remembering Obama 's foreign policy successes <p> The survey found that 88.5 percent of scholars believe climate change is a major global threat , followed by the spread of infectious diseases , with 76 per cent agreeing that it is a major threat to society . Over 70 per cent of international relation scholars believe that former President Barack Obama outperformed Trump in foreign policy areas .
