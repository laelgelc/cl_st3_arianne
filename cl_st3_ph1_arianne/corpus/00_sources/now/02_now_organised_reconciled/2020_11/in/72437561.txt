<p> Washington : The US space agency along with the European Space Agency ( ESA ) has successfully launched a satellite to monitor rising global sea level . <p> The joint US-European satellite built to monitor global sea levels lifted off on a SpaceX Falcon 9 rocket from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California late on Saturday . <p> About the size of a small pickup truck , the Sentinel-6 Michael Freilich satellite will extend a nearly 30-year continuous dataset on sea level collected by an ongoing collaboration of US and European satellites , while enhancing weather forecasts and providing detailed information on large-scale ocean currents to support ship navigation near coastlines . <p> " The Earth is changing , and this satellite will help deepen our understanding of how , " said Karen St. Germain , director of NASA 's Earth Science Division . <p> " The changing Earth processes are affecting sea level globally , but the impact on local communities varies widely . International collaboration is critical to both understanding these changes and informing coastal communities around the world .
