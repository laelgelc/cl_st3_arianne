<h> ' China will secure economic recovery with right mix of policies ' <p> Washington , Nov 25 : International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva said that China will secure economic recovery and ensure balanced and high-quality growth with the right mix of supportive macroeconomic policies . <p> " China 's fast recovery continues thanks to resolute measures in combatting the virus , mitigating its impact , and supporting growth , " Georgieva said in a statement on Tuesday at the conclusion of the fifth " 1+6 " Roundtable convened by the Chinese authorities , Xinhua news agency reported on Wednesday . <p> " With the right mix of supportive macroeconomic policies focused on strengthening social safety nets and further key reforms , China will secure the recovery and ensure balanced and high-quality growth , which will benefit China and the world , " said the IMF chief . <p> Chinese Premier Li Keqiang held the fifth " 1+6 " Roundtable with leaders of six major international economic institutions in Beijing via video conference . <p> Noting that the roundtable took place against the backdrop of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the COVID-19 pandemic , Georgieva said while China 's recovery continues , there are other economies that " may be losing momentum " as they contend with further waves of the pandemic . <p> " To end the crisis as quickly as possible , there is no alternative to working together , first and foremost by ensuring effective vaccines and treatments are available for everyone , " the IMF chief said . <p> Georgieva highlighted the policies of providing debt relief to the most vulnerable countries , reforming the World Trade Organization ( WTO ) and tackling climate change , and welcomed China 's efforts in these areas .
