<h> Daily Court Digest : Major environment orders ( November 19 , 2020 ) <h> Published : Thursday 19 November 2020 <p> Banadag railway siding <p> Appropriate action for pollution prevention was being taken at Banadag railway siding in Jharkhand : High-capacity field rain guns and wind screens over and above the boundary wall were installed , along with an effluent treatment plant . This was according to the joint inspection report filed before the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) . <p> In pursuance to the July 17 , 2020 NGT order , a committee was constituted comprising members from the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) , Jharkhand State Pollution Control Board ( JSPCB ) and the district magistrate of Hazaribagh , to inspect the Banadag railway siding . <p> It is a public railway siding owned by the Indian Railways and comes under East Central Railway . The railway siding was in the revenue village of Banadag and extends to the villages of Katkamdag and Banka . The habitation of these villages was more than 500 metres away from the railway siding . <p> There was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the southern side , which was about a kilometre away from the Banadag railway siding . Gonda dam was about 2.5 km in the north of Banadag railway siding . <p> At Banadag railway siding , coal from Pakri Barwadh coal mining project of National Thermal Power Corporation Ltd ( NTPC-PBCMP ) was being loaded and transported to its various thermal power plants . <p> NTPC-PBCMP had valid consents to operate under Air Act 1981 and Water Act 1974 . Both consents were valid till December 31 , 2023 . The company also had valid authorisation under the Hazardous and Others Wastes Rules , 2016 and was valid till May 16 , 2023 . <p> Agricultural fields were found on the northern and south eastern side of the railway siding . About seven to eight feet of green agro net was installed to further increase the effective height of the wall . <p> Construction and installation of 20-feet height windscreen ( over and above the boundary wall ) was under progress .
