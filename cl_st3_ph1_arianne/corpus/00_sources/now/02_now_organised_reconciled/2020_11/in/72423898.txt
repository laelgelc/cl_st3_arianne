<h> EU conference tomorrow to give voice to indigenous people on protected areas <h> Published : Wednesday 18 November 2020 <p> Indigenous people and activists will , for the first time , get a chance to speak with European parliamentarians on protected areas and the ways in which it affects their livelihood . <p> The virtual conference will take place November 19 , 2020 and comprise representatives of indigenous peoples and local communities in Africa , Asia and Latin America , non-profits and European Commission officials . <p> The conference comes against the backdrop of a push by the European Union , the United Nations and others to turn 30 per cent of the world 's surface into protected areas by 2030 . <p> The European Commission wants the same target to be adopted around the world at the Convention on Biological Diversity summit in 2021 . If that happens , millions of indigenous and local people will be reduced to landless poverty . <p> Activists and experts have claimed that protected places wo n't save the planet . They have no effect on climate change either . <p> Experts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ indigenous communities will deteriorate the space further . The indigenous people in protected areas are not allowed to live or use the natural environment to feed their families or gather medicinal plants . <p> In several cases , local people are pushed out of forests through coercion and bribery and are harassed and beaten up as well , according to Survival International . It is a non-profit that works for tribal people to give them a platform to voice their concerns . <p> The doubling of protected areas to cover 30 per cent of the globe would ensure these problems become worse , for the most biodiverse regions are those where indigenous peoples still live , according to the non-profit . These will be the first areas targeted by the conservation industry , it added . <p> Social media hashtag #BigGreenLie was also floated by Survival International to protest against the proposal . <p> We are a voice to you ; you have been a support to us .
