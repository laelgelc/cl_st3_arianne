<h> Dawn of the age of Revival <p> A joint initiative by Sensible Earth and the Corporation of the City of Panaji ( CCP ) , Revival 2020 , a festival that celebrates stories of sustainable coexistence , will begin on November 6 in Panaji . NT BUZZ details <p> CHRISTINE MACHADO NT BUZZ <p> From cycling tours and repair workshops to curated walks , music jams , storytelling and more , Revival 2020 , a joint initiative by Sensible Earth and CCP promises to be three days of fun , but more importantly of learning . <p> " I think there is a huge lesson that we have to learn from the COVID outbreak and climate change . It is important that we take a moment to think about sustainability from a new perspective . And this is the main inspiration behind organising this festival , " says founder of Sensible Earth , Sanjiv Khandelwal . <p> The three-day festival which begins on November 6 at Garcia de Orta Garden , Panaji celebrates stories of sustainable coexistence and is also an opportunity to economically support local businesses , professionals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> At the festival , guests can visit the sustainable bazaar featuring an array of eco-friendly fashion , beauty and food products . Several chefs will also be displaying their culinary creations , while guests can also shop for wild foods -- local produce , seeds and saplings . At the Repair Cafe one can avail of free cycle check-ups , test drive upcycled bikes , learn how to mend clothes with Sale Swap Takeaway ( SST Goa ) , and mochi repairs for your broken footwear or bags . At The Karma Cafe , you can pay as you wish or barter your services in exchange for delicious food by home cooks . Any funds received will be directed to make sweets this Diwali and will be shared amongst people who suffered a lot in this pandemic . The festival will also include The Place Of Possibilities , which will be corners for conversations for festival goers . <p> " The Place Of Possibilities is amazing , interactive and thought-provoking concept where people will be greeted by people from Karma Cafe and there will be conversation .
