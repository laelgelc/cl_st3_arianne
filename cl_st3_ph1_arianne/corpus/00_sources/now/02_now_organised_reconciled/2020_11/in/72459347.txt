<h> Published : Monday 23 November 2020 <p> Boris Johnson 's " 10-point plan " for a post-pandemic green industrial revolution in the United Kingdom to tackle climate change is unprecedented : It has been brought in almost a decade earlier than the previous commitment of 2040 . <p> One of the key points is to ban on the sale of diesel- and petrol-powered cars and vans from 2030 . <p> The move , announced November 17 , 2020 , would affect all cars running on diesel and gasoline . The sale of plug-in hybrid vehicles , however , would be allowed for five more years , till 2035 . <p> These targets , being touted " highly ambitious " by both industry experts and policy makers , present numerous logistical challenges . They are , however , a step in the direction towards cleaner transport . <p> What is the plan ? <p> The government is expected to launch a series of consultations to create a roadmap to phase out the diesel run Heavy Goods Vehicles . <p> The same would create a surge in demand for electric vehicles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cars in the United Kingdom .
