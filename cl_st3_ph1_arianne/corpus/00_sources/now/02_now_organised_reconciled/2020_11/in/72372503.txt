<h> Highlights <p> From Nov 7 , no traffic during weekends till Feb 2021 Cafes can expand seating to pavements Companies from the UK and the IISc will monitor quality of air <p> With its all-year-round good weather , except scattered rains in the monsoon , it 's always a wonder why Bengaluru did not have outdoor dining options . Now more than ever , with social distancing becoming mandatory , Bangalore Mirror had written in May this year about how the street as an outlet could work to support local enterprises and bring back people on the street . <p> Looks like someone heard us . From November 7 , Church Street is set to become a pedestrian-friendly thoroughfare ( closed for traffic ) during the weekends , with restaurants and cafeterias situated here allowed to extend seating on pavements on Saturday and Sunday . This pilot project will run till February 2021 .
