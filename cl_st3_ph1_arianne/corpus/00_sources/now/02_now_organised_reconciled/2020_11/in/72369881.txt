The US is the largest emitter of greenhouse gases , but the president has repeatedly undermined sustained efforts by climate change researchers on the subject . <p> In November 2018 , for instance , when a group of government scientists and officials from 13 federal agencies brought out a volume of the National Climate Assessment , Trump denied its findings outright . The report had stated : <p> " Based on extensive evidence , it is extremely likely that human activities , especially emissions of greenhouse gases , are the dominant cause of the observed warming since the mid-20th century .
