<h> Wetlands at risk as the incidences of droughts rise <p> In a major study , researchers have shown how droughts are threatening the health of wetlands globally . <p> November 03 , 2020 <p> In a major study , researchers have shown how droughts are threatening the health of wetlands globally . <p> Published in the journal Earth-Science Reviews , the study highlights the many physical and chemical changes occurring during droughts that lead to severe , and sometimes irreversible , drying of wetland soils . <p> " Wetlands around the world are incredibly important for maintaining our planet 's biodiversity and they store vast amounts of carbon that can help fight climate change , " said study author Luke Mosley from the University of Adelaide in Australia . <p> According to the researchers , wetlands can suffer " water droughts " both from the effects of a drier climate , and also when excessive water is extracted or diverted that would normally flow into them . <p> In some cases droughts can lead to very long-term ( less than 10 years ) and irreversible soil changes , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the drought ends . <p> " We have seen many examples of how drought in the Murray-Darling Basin in Australia has caused major issues including acidification of soil and water due to acid sulfate soils exposure in wetlands , " Mosley said . <p> " This review highlights substantial gaps in our global understanding of the effects of drought on wet soils and how they will respond to increasing drought , " Mosley added . <p> The researchers revealed that effects can be different in different soil types and different regions of the world . <p> The spatial distribution of drought studies shows there has been limited assessment in a large number of regions , including south and central America , Africa , the Middle East , Asia and Oceania .
