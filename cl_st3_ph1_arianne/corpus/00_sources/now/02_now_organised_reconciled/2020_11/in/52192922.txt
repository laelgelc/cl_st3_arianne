<h> Air pollution , Covid top list of concerns this Diwali : Survey <p> New Delhi : In the top 10 pandemic-hit states , air pollution , Covid-19 and economy have topped the list of concerns this Diwali , a public survey said on Wednesday . <p> From a sample size of 2,218 from 25-60 years of age and equally divided as male and female , 42.9 per cent of the respondents said that coronavirus is the biggest concern this Diwali . <p> The second highest concern is air pollution that received 23.2 per cent votes , followed by the economy at 19.5 per cent , jobs at 7.1 per cent , farmers ' issue at 3.8 per cent , while China is the least of their concerns at 3.4 per cent . <p> Aarti Khosla , publisher of CarbonCopy , told IANS , " It is not surprising that the pandemic has assumed the largest attention across all the 10 states surveyed . " <p> CarbonCopy tracks industry development in the climate and energy sector . <p> Among the 10 states , respondents from West Bengal particularly feel that the government is not doing enough to deal with Covid-19 . A majority of the respondents from all the other states feel that their government is doing enough to deal with the pandemic . <p> Apart from Uttar Pradesh , which remains undecided on whether the government is doing enough on air pollution , a majority of the respondents from the other states are not satisfied with the efforts of their governments in addressing air pollution . <p> More than 70 per cent of the respondents who selected air @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bengal , Kerala , Delhi and Karnataka , who are not satisfied with the government 's response in tackling air pollution . <p> In Karnataka , the percentage is significantly high at 88 per cent of the total respondents who picked air pollution as their top concern . <p> A majority of the respondents in all the 10 states surveyed are not satisfied with their government 's efforts to address the problems related to the economy . <p> Respondents from West Bengal and Tamil Nadu , who are not satisfied with their government 's response towards the economy , ranged between 75-88 per cent . <p> " Having environmental issues on an equal footing to concerns about the economy shows a perception change , large part of which can be attributed to the pandemic .
