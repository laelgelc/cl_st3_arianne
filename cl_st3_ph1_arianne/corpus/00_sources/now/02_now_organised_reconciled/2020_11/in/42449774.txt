Since farm productivity is not showing desirable growth there is an urgent need to focus on research as well as better agricultural practices to ensure that productivity levels are increased in the shortest time possible . Special attention may be required for States with relatively low productivity . <p> Statistics show that the production and productivity in pulses and oilseeds are of growing concern . A sizeable proportion of these items is met through imports . The scope for import of pulses is limited due to the limited number of countries producing it .
