<h> Boris Johnson tweet to Joe Biden reveals traces of message for Trump <p> British Prime Minister Boris Johnson has risked being left red-faced after his message of congratulations to US president-elect Joe Biden was found to have retained traces of Donald Trump 's name , Sky News reports . <p> On Saturday , the prime minister offered his congratulations to Biden and his vice-presidential running mate Kamala Harris after they were declared the winners of the US election . <p> In a graphic attached to his Twitter post , Mr Johnson said in a statement : " Congratulations to Joe Biden on his election as President of the United States and to Kamala Harris on her historic achievement . <p> " The US is our most important ally and I look forward to working closely together on our shared priorities , from climate change to trade and security . "
