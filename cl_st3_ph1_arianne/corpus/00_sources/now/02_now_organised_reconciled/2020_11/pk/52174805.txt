<p> Zartaj Gul ( Minister of State for Climate Change ) : Pakistan and Bosnia have been enjoying strong bilateral relations . Pakistan was among the countries that extended support to the Bosnia during the war of Bosnia and Herzegovina . In today 's joint press conference , Prime Minister Imran Khan and Chairman of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina Sefik Dzaferovic discussed Islamophobia , Kashmir issue and bilateral trade . Chairman of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina has endorsed the narrative of Pakistan on Islamophobia and Kashmir dispute . Both the countries also agreed to increase the trade volume . The present government is determined and focusing on to boost the economy through trade with other countries . Prime Minister Imran Khan announced a massive package for SMEs in the countries that is part of efforts to improve industrialization . Chairman of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina Sefik Dzaferovic has also invited Prime Minister Imran Khan to visit Bosnia . Sefik Dzaferovic has stressed for the resolution of the dispute in line with the UN Security Council Resolutions .
