Now is the time for Green Politics , a political ideology based on the creation of an ecologically sustainable society pillared on the values of environmentalism and social responsibility . The new political landscape requires an updated green slogan promising environmental sustainability for the current and future generations which will resultantly ensure an equal and steady supply of food , clothing , and shelter . <p> Many of the world leaders are practicing green politics by promising the voters concrete steps for attaining environmental sustainability and supporting various green initiatives .
