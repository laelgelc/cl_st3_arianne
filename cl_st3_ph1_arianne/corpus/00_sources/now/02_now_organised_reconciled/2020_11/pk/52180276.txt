<h> Bosnian president calls for peaceful resolution of Kashmir dispute <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Chairman of the Presidency of Bosnia &amp; Herzegovina Sefik Dzaferovic has called for the peaceful resolution of the Kashmir dispute in the light of the UN Security Council ( UNSC ) resolutions . <p> In a meeting with the Foreign Minister Shah Mahmood Qureshi at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Islamabad on Wednesday , the Bosnian president said that human rights should be respected in occupied Jammu and Kashmir . <p> Sefik Dzaferovic said that Pakistan and Bosnia enjoy close relations and his visit will further strengthen bilateral relations . <p> Shah Mahmood Qureshi briefed the Bosnian president about the situation in Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) and appreciated his statement on the occupied territory . <p> The foreign minister said that both Pakistan and Bosnia &amp; Herzegovina have always stood by each other in difficult times . <p> Qureshi said that Pakistan desires to expand relations with Bosnia in different fields including bilateral trade , education , culture , and people to people contacts . <p> The foreign @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ between Pakistan and Bosnia &amp; Herzegovina . <p> Earlier , the Chairman of the Presidency of Bosnia and Herzegovina Sefik Dzaferovic has arrived in Islamabad on a two-day official visit to Pakistan . <p> The Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul along with other officials received the Bosnian president . <p> Sefik Dzaferovic is accompanied by a high-level delegation including the Minister for Security as well as Presidential Advisers .
