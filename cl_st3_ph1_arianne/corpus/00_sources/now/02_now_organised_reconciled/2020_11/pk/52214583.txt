<h> Global warming <p> A gradual increase in the overall temperature of the earth 's atmosphere is generally attributed to the greenhouse effect , caused by the increasing level of carbon dioxide , CCGC and other pollutants . The influence of human activities , since at least the beginning of the industrial revolution , has deeply woven into the very fabric of climate change . Human activities have been responsible for a worldwide average temperature increase between 0.8 and 1.2 . C ( 1.4 and 2.2'f ) of global warming since preindustrial time , and most of the warming observed over the second half of the 20th century could be ascribed to human activities . <p> Furthermore , humans emit a large quantity of these heat-trapping gases , including carbon dioxide by burning fossil fuel and the release of the resulting emission to the atmosphere . As people have added more and more of heat-trapping gases to the atmosphere , the planet has warmed more than it could naturally become . As the level of carbon dioxide and other gases increase , the average global temperature also increases .
