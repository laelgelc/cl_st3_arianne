<h> Pakistan urges G20 to extend suspension of debt payments <p> Pakistan has called on the Group of 20 nations , representing the world 's biggest economies , to extend the suspension of debt payments until the coronavirus-hit developing countries have recovered from the economic devastation wrought by the pandemic . <p> " The COVID pandemic has devastated the economies of many developed countries and it has added to the multi-dimensional challenges that we are facing from the climate change and other endemic problems facing us , " Ambassador Munir Akram told the annual ministerial meeting of the Group of 77 and China . <p> Established in 1964 by 77 developing countries , the group is designed to promote its members ' collective economic interests and create an enhanced joint negotiating capacity in the UN . It 's membership now stand at 134 . <p> The meeting was convened by Dr Irfan Ali , the president of Guyana , which holds the presidency of G-77 . <p> In his remarks , the Pakistani envoy stressed the need for unity to help G-77 achieve its objectives , and said that the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economies to recover from the crisis . <p> Highlighting Pakistan Prime Minister Imran Khan 's initiative for debt relief , Ambassador Akram said , " We believe that the debt suspension by the G-20 should be extended until the period that we have recovered from the pandemic . " <p> He called for the cancellation of least developed countries ' debt ; implementation of the debt restructuring for other developing countries , including through efficient debt buybacks and debt swaps ; invigoration of sizable net inflows from the multilateral development banks as well as expanded concessional finance through the Official Development Assistance ( ODA ) and other sources . <p> In addition , the Pakistani envoy proposed the issuance of new Special Drawing Rights ( SDRs ) , and the repurposing of existing and utilized SDRs for developing countries . An additional $1.5. trillion annually was needed for investment in sustainable infrastructure , he said .
