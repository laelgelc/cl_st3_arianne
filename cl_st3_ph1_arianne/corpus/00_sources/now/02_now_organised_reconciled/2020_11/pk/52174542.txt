<h> Pakistan 's Highest-Level Participation in 75th Session of UN General Assembly <p> Pakistan participated at the highest level in the Opening Segment of the UN General Assembly 's Annual Session from September 21 to October 1 , 2020 . <p> This year 's session was of enhanced significance due to the United Nations ' 75th anniversary celebrations . <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan reaffirmed Pakistan 's commitment to multilateralism ; exposed egregious violations of human rights and international law in Indian Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir ( IIOJK ) ; called for the realization of the Kashmiris ' right to self-determination ; and articulated Pakistan 's perspective on a host of contemporary global and regional challenges . <p> The limitations imposed by COVID-19 necessitated the conduct of meetings in a virtual format . The world leaders participated through video statements in the various events . <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan addressed the High-Level Segment of the General Assembly on September 25 , 2020 . <p>
