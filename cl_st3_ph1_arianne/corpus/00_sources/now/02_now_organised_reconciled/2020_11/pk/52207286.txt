It has been in gestation for over four decades . It began in the 1980s , with Reagan , when the US made the explicit policy decision to transfer massive shares of national income and wealth from the working and middle classes to those who were already the most-wealthy . This was the notorious ' Supply Side Economics ' . <p> Supply Side Economics lowered tax rates on the highest income earners from 71 percent to 35 percent .
