<h> MYSay : Time to revisit the worldview in our economic thought <p> In perhaps one of the greatest ironies , Klaus Schwab , the founder of the high priest of neoliberalism , the World Economic Forum , wrote recently about the need to move away from the neoliberal ideology that underpins our modern economy . <p> In his Great Reset vision , a theme emphasised in the recent edition of Time magazine , he called for a re-evaluation of " capitalism " as we know it today and a reset of our economics , politics and societies as a response to the savage impact of Covid-19 . This would entail an entirely new thinking to address inequality in its many forms , ensure economic and social security and tackle climate change . <p> For the uninitiated , this would have been transformative , if not revolutionary .
