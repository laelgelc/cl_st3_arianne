<h> Biden 's transition teams sideline industries Trump put in charge <p> ( Nov 12 ) : President-elect Joe Biden has stocked his transition team with policy experts , academics and former Obama administration officials , a contrast with the industry-friendly figures President Donald Trump sent into the government upon winning office . <p> The roughly 500-person group Biden 's campaign announced on Monday , will prepare federal agencies for the incoming administration and draft plans to overhaul their operations after four years under Trump . The president-elect 's team includes people who favour stronger government regulation than Trump -- particularly for the financial and energy sectors -- and greater consumer protections . <p> Four years ago , Trump stocked his so-called agency " landing teams " with conservatives from think tanks and advocacy groups -- including people who had been openly hostile to the agencies they were charged to review . They can not access office space or begin closely working with government officials until the administrator of the General Services Administration , Emily Murphy , issues a finding that Biden is the incoming president .
