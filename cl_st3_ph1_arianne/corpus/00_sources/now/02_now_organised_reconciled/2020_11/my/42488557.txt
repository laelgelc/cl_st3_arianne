<p> The country hopes that the United States will further broaden and deepen its presence in this region , says Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong , highlighting political security and economic ties as two areas of cooperation . <p> Speaking at the eighth Asean-US Summit yesterday , Lee said the United States had been a vital partner for Asean since the end of World War II . <p> He also thanked the United States for its support to the region during the Covid-19 crisis . <p> The virtual summit was attended by US National Security Adviser Robert O'Brien , who represented President Donald Trump . <p> During the session , Lee welcomed the United States ' continued security presence in Asia . <p> He said Singapore appreciated American support for the Asean Outlook on the Indo-Pacific , which is the bloc 's plan to develop the Indo-Pacific region . <p> He also noted that operations of the United States ' Seventh Fleet help strengthen regional security and safeguard the rights of regional countries under international law . <p> " We also hope to continue to work with the United States @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ multilateral institutions , and finding multilateral solutions to global challenges like climate change and pandemics , " he added . <p> Lee also highlighted the strengthening of Asean-US economic ties by protecting supply chains , maintaining open and unimpeded trade flows and safely resuming international travel . <p> The primie minister also called for the United States to continue supporting Asean 's efforts to deepen economic integration with partners , such as through the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership , which will be signed today , as well as the Comprehensive and Progressive Agreement for Trans-Pacific Partnership which was signed almost three years back . -- The Straits Times/ANN
