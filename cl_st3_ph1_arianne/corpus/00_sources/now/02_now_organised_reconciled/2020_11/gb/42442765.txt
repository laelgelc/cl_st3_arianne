<h> ' Proposed housing development will cause irreversible destruction ' <h> ' Proposed housing development will cause irreversible destruction ' <p> ON A windy autumn day I joined the Thomas Hardy Society for a walk that tracked the perimeter of the proposed housing development across land to the north of the culturally renowned , county-town of Dorchester . <p> It is beyond the compression of any person to even contemplate such irreversible destruction of such an extensive area of farmland , which contributes to the bread-basket of the nation and is a green lung ( evident while hiking with magnificent far-reaching views across productive agricultural land ) for this market town . <p> Even more so having watched " Extinction - the facts " and heard Sir David Attenborough 's clarion call " We have one final chance to create the perfect home for ourselves and restore the wonderful world we inherited " and " we need immediate action to stop this crisis " . <p> That includes putting a stop to this development - especially as it will ' sandwich ' the River Frome flood plain through excessive run-off @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its natural ecology . It is also a landscape featured by Thomas Hardy and the artist Gordon Beningfield alike , attracting cultural tourism to Dorchester . <p> It may , indeed , be proved that housing needs to be found for a substantial number of people in Dorchester , in which case councillors are charged with the responsibility to be creative about using areas within the town 's already extended boundary , such as redevelopment to breathe new life into the town centre where a wide range of community facilities are readily to hand , and can be expanded and improved for new uses without the need to increase pollution by an obliged use of vehicles to reach the town centre . <p> If coronavirus and climate change have taught us anything it is that what happens on one side of Mother Earth - China and the Amazon , obvious big examples - the ripple effects are felt by all corners of this , now fragile , planet . <p> What happens to the tract of land to the north of Dorchester will have an effect on the entire county @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Nothing or nowhere is immune from affecting the precarious nature of our planet . <p> This is my plea to each and every one of us , and this case especially the land owners , proposed developers and current councillors and planning officers , to heed Sir David 's warning - You each have one final chance to ensure that your actions do not contribute any further to the demise of the wonderful world we have inherited . <p> Stop , think and examine your conscience . Think globally , act locally . Be mindful that your decision will contribute to the global crisis . What happens here in Dorset will add to increases in carbon , the cause of so much destruction .
