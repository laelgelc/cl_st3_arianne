<h> These are the young creative talents you need to know about in 2020 <p> With 2020 being one of the most unpredictable years in history , largely thanks to the global pandemic and the rapid rate at which climate change is affecting our planet , creatives across the globe have had to rethink how they work . <p> And to coincide with the Fashion Awards which take place on Thursday 3 December , the British Fashion Council has recognised those who have made the most out of the bad situation . The 2020 New Wave : Creatives list celebrates the 50 young talents from across a multitude of disciplines , such as artists , activists , casting agents , hair and makeup artists , image makers , nail artists , set designers , stylists and writers , who have been innovative in their work and have been key composers of change within the fashion industry . <p> This year 's Creatives were chosen by a panel of the industry 's key figures , including GQ Style Deputy Editor and Fashion Director Elgar Johnson , designer Charles Jeffrey and make-up @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ among 2019 's New Wave , such as model Adut Akech and drag queen and journalist Tom Rasmussen . <p> The 2020 New Wave : Creatives line-up will benefit from direct , ongoing access to the steering committee , which is a dedicated communications project that offers international PR , year-round social media support across the BFC 's channels , and a place on a purpose-built portal on the BFC website , which acts as a go-to resource for those looking for exceptional talent . <p> And this year 's 50 international creatives are the most exciting yet . The likes of self-taught director Fenn O'Meally , who has worked with Nike and Prada , stylist Betsy Johnson , hair stylist Latisha Chong , creative director Louis Burguet , multi-disciplinary artist Furmaan Ahmed , London-based dancer , artist and trans activist Sakeema Peng Crook and visual artist Marlou Fernanda have all secured a place on the coveted list and will be recognised globally for their contributions .
