<h> Campaign launched to protect path from climate change - as the cliffs crumble <h> Campaign launched to protect South West Coast Path <p> AS THE Jurassic Coast continues to crumble with more sections in danger of collapse a campaign has been launched to protect the South West Coast Path against climate change . <p> Businesses are showing their support for the famous 630-mile National Trail , which runs from Poole Harbour to Minehead , in Somerset , ahead of the winter season . <p> It comes after it was revealed this week that a 90-metre long crack had appeared in the cliff at West Bay , prompting a 200m section of the path to be closed . In August a huge section of cliff collapsed near Burton Bradstock . <p> The South West Coast Path Association ( SWCPA ) , the charity that looks after the path , is appealing for donations to its Every Mile Matters campaign which aims to protect the path and ensure it is there for future generations to enjoy . <p> Some businesses have already pledged their support for the campaign which has already @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 100,000 target . <p> The funds raised will be spent on increasing the path 's resilience against the onslaught of extreme weather , conserving the biodiversity of the trail 's wildlife , and championing the positive impact it has on people 's health and wellbeing . <p> In the past eight years , the charity has recorded 180 exceptional events , including cliff falls , landslips , or collapses in infrastructure such as bridges , caused by heavy rainfall , flooding or storm changes . The charity predicts that climate change will increase the frequency and severity of these events in the coming years . <p> Julian Gray , director of the SWCPA , said : " The lockdown has shown how important the path is for people 's health and wellbeing and the local economy . It 's great to see businesses stepping up to support the Coast Path to ensure it 's there for future generations . " <p> Since 2013 , the SWCPA has been tracking ' exceptional coastal erosion events ' with the help of its volunteers .
