<p> Britain is being exposed to " an economic blackout of frightening proportions " because of its dependence on foreign satellites , a space industry chief has warned . <p> Space industry body UKspace is concerned that firms have very little ability to defend data if hostile powers decide to attack satellites owned and operated by other countries . <p> According to analysis by the association , at least 90% of the satellite communications and data that UK businesses rely on is owned and operated by other nations , such as the US and states within the EU . <p> While a national cyber force was recently announced to improve Britain 's military satellite defences , a report by UKspace to be published next week will express fears over civilian dependency on foreign satellite assets . <p> It claims even a temporary disruption , caused by a hostile cyber or space-based attack , or simply the future withdrawal or change of services , would " wreak havoc " on the economy . <p> " Data from satellites has become so critical to our everyday lives that even a temporary disruption @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said Nick Shave , chairman of UKspace . <p> " Investing in our own national capabilities is essential for our future security , but also an opportunity to create new jobs in the UK , driving a stronger recovery across the country .
