<h> CIOB responds to Government 's Ten Point Plan <p> The Chartered Institute of Building ( CIOB ) has responded to the government 's ' Ten Point Plan for a Green Industrial Revolution ' . The plan seeks to create and support up to 250,000 jobs covering clean energy , transport , nature and innovative technologies across the UK . <p> The CIOB welcomes the extension of the Green Homes Grant by a further year as it will give industry and consumers further time to make use of the scheme and ensure that all work is carried out to the highest standards . <p> However , the Institute is disappointed to see that the Government has not included a national retrofit strategy as part of its plan for green buildings . <p> While it is a positive step that the Government recognises the importance of making homes , schools and hospitals more energy efficient , a long-term roadmap is needed to give the built environment sector the certainty it needs to invest in green technology and low carbon skills . <p> Ultimately , the ? 12 billion allocated in this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ amount that will be needed to ensure that our built environment is fit to meet the net zero target . <p> The CIOB urges the Government to make a national retrofit plan a core part of its Industrial Strategy and key infrastructure priority , to support jobs and help to eradicate the UK 's contribution to climate change . <h> Build Back Better : The role of Inclusive Capitalism in Britain 's housing and cities <p> In this online webinar , we bring together key parliamentarians to explore how we use the power of Inclusive Capitalism to ' Build Back Better ' , in the 2020s .
