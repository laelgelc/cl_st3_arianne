<p> This week 's lecture provided students with a unique insight into China 's involvement in African Development . This is a highly charged topic as many believe China to be guilty of " debt trap diplomacy " -- a method of strategically entrapping nations into enormous debt and using this to gain leverage and obtain resources . On the other hand , some praise China for being the main actor in helping Africa to economically develop into a continent full of MEDCs . The answer is probably somewhere in between " . <p> While many in the West look at China 's involvement in Africa as exploitive and manipulative , many in Africa appreciate China 's help in developing African countries . For example in Senegal , many are positive about Chinese lending and what it means for Senegal 's infrastructure . Therefore , could the Chinese " dept trap " just be a Western conspiracy ?
