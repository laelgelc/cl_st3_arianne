<h> Antarctic : Stirling High School pupils help find colony of emperor penguins <p> SCOTTISH high school pupils have received praise for helping to find a new colony of emperor penguins in the Antarctic . <p> Pupils from Stirling High School developed and coded an algorithm to identify penguin colonies from satellite pictures , which helped British Antarctic Survey researchers confirm the location of the colony . <p> The students launched the project , aiming to find out if colonies could be spotted using satellite images and software available to them , after being inspired by a David Attenborough documentary . <p> The work formed part of an Institute for Research in Schools project titled Earth Observation . <p> The pupils won praise for their work in the report from British Antarctic Survey geographers and researchers Dr Peter Fretwell and Philip Trathan . <p> The pair confirmed they had found 11 new colonies , of which a number were situated far offshore on sea ice formed around icebergs . <p> Trathan said : " Whilst it 's good news that we 've found these new colonies , the breeding sites @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will decline . <p> " We need to watch these sites carefully as climate change will affect this region .
