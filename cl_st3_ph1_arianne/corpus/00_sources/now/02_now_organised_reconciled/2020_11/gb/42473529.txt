<p> Capitalism is broken . Inequality has reached levels not seen since the Gilded Age . Social mobility is falling . The novel coronavirus pandemic has highlighted the fact that far too many " essential " workers have no sick leave , no health care and no savings . Climate change remains a clear and present danger . <p> All too often these are seen as problems that only the government can fix .
