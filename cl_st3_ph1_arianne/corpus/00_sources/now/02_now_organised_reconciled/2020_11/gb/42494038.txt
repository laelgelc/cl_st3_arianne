<h> Chance to have your say on future of Brighton 's downland <p> RESIDENTS are being offered a " once-in-a-lifetime " opportunity to help shape the future of downland . <p> Brighton and Hove City Council is putting together the City Downland Estate Plan , which will set out a vision for how downland could be managed over the next 100 years .
