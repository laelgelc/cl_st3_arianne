<h> Loretta Minghella to step down as First Church Estates Commissioner <p> THE First Church Estates Commissioner , Loretta Minghella , is to step down next summer before taking up the post of Master of Clare College , Cambridge , in October 2021 . Her successor has yet to be announced . <p> Ms Minghella joined the Church Commissioners from Christian Aid in 2017 ( News , 30 June 2017 ) . The Archbishop of Canterbury said last week : " In the three years since she joined the Commissioners she has worked to increase the Commissioners ' funding available for mission , strengthened measures to steward the fund in perpetuity , and responded to the pandemic with a liquidity package for struggling dioceses and cathedrals " ( News , 3 April ) . <p> He continued : " During her tenure she has overseen the development of a more sustainable infrastructure for our investment activities and commenced implementation of a new phase of climate engagement and divestment . The Commissioners will continue to benefit from her leadership and expertise until the summer of next year .
