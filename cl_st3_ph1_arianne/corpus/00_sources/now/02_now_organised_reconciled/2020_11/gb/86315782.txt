Native millet is also gluten free , she added . <p> The study , which involved researchers in ecology , food science , social science , marketing and business , found that native millet was the most economically viable of all the grains they tested . Native grains are called dhunbarr in the Gamilaraay language . <p> Researchers also found that native grasses had environmental benefits .
