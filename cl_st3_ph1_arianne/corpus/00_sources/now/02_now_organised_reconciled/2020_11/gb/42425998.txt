<h> Consultation launched into future of Caithness and Sutherland peatlands <p> A new draft strategy for the Flow Country peatlands of Caithness and Sutherland could help the case for making the internationally important area a World Heritage Site . <p> A public consultation has been launched to seek local views on the strategy that envisages making a submission for UNESCO world heritage status by early 2023 . <p> It also proposes promoting the area as a green tourism destination , as well as greater community involvement in managing the peatlands , actions to contribute to climate change projects and to minimise impact on the area from development including wind farms . <p> The document has been drafted by NatureScot on behalf of the multi-agency Peatlands Partnership , formed in 2006 shortly after the first management strategy in 2005 . <p> Caithness and Sutherland are home to the largest and most intact area of blanket bog in Europe , with 4% of the world 's resource . The area supports a wide range of wildlife and is a vitally important store for carbon . <p> Professor Stuart Gibb , of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Partnership , said the new strategy follows previous initiatives , including the five-year ? 11 million Flows to the Future Project which helped restore blanket bog habitat . <p> " Our new draft strategy seeks to build on this success , and includes the aspiration to secure inscription of The Flow Country as a UNESCO World Heritage Site . <p> " It also recognises recent changes in public sector policy including the Scottish Government 's declaration of a climate emergency and biodiversity crisis , and ' green recovery ' opportunities emerging from the global Covid-19 pandemic . However , as with the original strategy we aim to embed the relationship between people and peatlands at the heart of our new approach . " <p> Graham Neville , NatureScot 's Northern Isles and North Highland area manager , added : " This important area of expansive blanket bog requires careful management at the landscape scale to ensure that it remains healthy . <p> " The communities and businesses that depend on the peatlands all have a stake in this , and the strategy aims to co-ordinate action to enhance and promote @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Since 2005 , we have seen major advances in peatland restoration and related research .
