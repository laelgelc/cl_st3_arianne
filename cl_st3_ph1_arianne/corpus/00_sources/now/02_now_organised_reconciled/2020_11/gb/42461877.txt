<h> Farming method protects endangered species of bird <h> Farming method protects endangered species of bird <p> A pioneering England-wide initiative has been launched , to try and recover nature across the length and breadth of the country , and help everybody access and enjoy it . <p> The Nature Recovery Network Delivery Partnership , led by Natural England , is bringing together representatives from more than 600 organisations to push ahead the restoration of protected sites and landscapes , helping to provide at least 500,000 hectares of new wildlife-rich habitat across England . <p> The network links together the country 's best nature-rich places , restores landscapes in towns and the countryside and creates new habitats for everybody to enjoy . <p> It is the biggest initiative to restore nature ever to be launched in England . <p> The partners , including the Council for Sustainable Business , Wildlife and Countryside Link , National Parks England , RSPB and the Country Land and Business Association , alongside Defra , the Environment Agency and the Forestry Commission , will be providing a wide range of support including funding and land @ @ @ @ @ @ sure existing protected sites are in the best possible condition , the Nature Recovery Network programme will recover threatened animal and plant species and create and connect new green and blue spaces such as wetlands , ponds , meadows , woodlands , and peatlands . <p> It will engage conservation rangers and environmentally focused community-based projects and put lost features like hedgerows and trees back into our landscapes . <p> These restored habitats will help address climate change through capturing carbon , while improving the quality of our air , water , and soil , and provide natural flood protection . <p> They will also provide more places to enjoy and connect with nature and help to improve health and wellbeing . <p> It is hoped that the initiative will restore
