<h> Majority of Scots opposed to grouse shooting as row over sport continues <p> Almost three quarters of Scots are in opposition to grouse shooting , according to a new survey . <p> The survey , commissioned by the League Against Cruel Sports Scotland , found 71 per cent were opposed to grouse shooting , with only a " pitiful " 12 per cent in favour of the sport . <p> It comes after a review recommended introducing a licensing scheme for grouse moor shooting , prompted by poor breeding raptor populations . <p> Last December , Professor Alan Werritty 's review said the licensing scheme should be introduced if there is no " marked improvement " in the ecological sustainability of grouse moor management within five years . <p> A response to the review from The Scottish Government is expected to be published later this month . <p> The Diffley Partnership surveyed 1,364 people via the ScotPulse online panel in early October . <p> Robbie Marsland , director of the League Against Cruel Sports Scotland , said : " Almost a year ago , Professor Werritty said wider societal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ made , well here are those views . <p> " Seven in 10 people do not support grouse shooting in Scotland , with only a pitifully low number in favour . <p> " We are urging the Scottish Government to take these views into account when it responds to Werritty later this month . " <p> He added : " There is a circle of destruction that surrounds grouse moors . <p> " These moors account for enormous swathes of Scotland which deplete biodiversity , add to climate change pressures , employ rigorous predator control which causes untold suffering to tens of thousands of animals each year and make a woeful economic contribution . <p> " The time has come to move on from this archaic use of land and look at reviving grouse moors in a way which does n't revolve around a minority bloodsport opposed by the vast majority of Scottish people .
