<h> Applications open for $50m fund that supports sustainability projects <p> Individuals , groups , and businesses with sustainability ideas such as reducing waste , and coming up with ways to mitigate climate change , can now apply for funding for their projects . <p> The first grant call for the SG Eco Fund was announced by the Ministry of Sustainability and the Environment ( MSE ) yesterday . Applications will close on Jan 31 next year . <p> The $50 million fund was initially announced by MSE during the Committee of Supply Debate in March . It will support ground-up projects led by the community that aim to build a more sustainable future .
