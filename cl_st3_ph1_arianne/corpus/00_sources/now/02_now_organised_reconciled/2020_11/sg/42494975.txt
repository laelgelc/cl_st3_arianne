One of the guiding principles for the SBTN is the nine planetary boundaries , a concept developed by 28 international researchers , including Johan Rockstr ? m of the Stockholm Resilience Centre and Will Steffen from the Australian National University , to describe certain thresholds that have the potential to destabilise the world . The concept was introduced in an article in Nature in 2009 . <p> The nine boundaries -- climate change , biosphere integrity , land-system change , freshwater use , biogeochemical use , ocean acidification , atmospheric aerosol loading , stratospheric ozone depletion , and chemical pollution and the release of novel entities -- identify various Earth system processes , each containing thresholds for safe operating limits .
