Working at a wine importer back then , she quickly found herself enjoying the dynamic pace of the job and the vibrant epicurean lifestyle that comes with it . As her passion for wines and the business grew over the years , she made every effort to develop her understanding and deepen her knowledge of the industry across its full spectrum from procurement to logistics , sales and marketing and more . <p> Today , 16 years later , Linda is the assistant general manager of Grand Vin , a distributor of some of the finest wines in the world .
