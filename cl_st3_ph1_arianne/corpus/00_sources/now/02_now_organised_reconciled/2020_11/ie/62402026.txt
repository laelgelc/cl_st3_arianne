<h> ClientEarth calls for climate change reporting improvement <p> Campaign group ClientEarth has called on businesses to treat the latest Financial Reporting Council letter to chief executives , chief finance officers and audit committee chairs as a chance to make much needed improvements to their climate-change reporting . <p> The letter sets out the FRC 's supervisory priorities for the coming year . They include reporting on the impact of climate change , the COVID-19 pandemic and the completion of the UK 's departure from the European Union . <p> ClientEarth climate finance lawyer Daniel Wiseman told IPE now is the time for both companies and regulators to face up to their responsibilities . <p> " The continued failure by companies to disclose material climate-related financial information to investors means they are likely breaching the law , " he said , adding that " global policies are rapidly realigning around reaching ' net zero ' greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 .
