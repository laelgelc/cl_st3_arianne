<h> FTSE Russell <h> Blog The US green economy : a bipartisan growth story <p> In the wake of the US presidential election , and as the composition of the US Senate remains in the balance , investors and analysts are struggling to understand what the US climate , energy and environmental policy landscape will look like over the next four years . <p> The President-elect has made clear that the US will rejoin the Paris Agreement on Climate Change , but unfortunately , we do n't have a crystal ball on other announcements . But we can look back over the recent past at the evolution of the US green economy to try to draw some conclusions about the sector 's prospects -- despite uncertainties around the direction and detail of federal policy . <p> Central banks have welcomed the news about a recent COVID-19 vaccine breakthrough . However , implementation risks remain on vaccines , and a more flexible policy armory may still be required because monetary policy initiatives , since the COVID-19 crisis , have closely followed the GFC playbook.
