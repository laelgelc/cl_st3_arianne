<h> Emissions from agriculture decreased by 3.9% in 2019 <p> The Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) has today ( Friday , November 20 ) published its provisional greenhouse gas emissions for Ireland for 2019 . <p> The figures show a reduction of 4.5% compared to 2018 , which is the largest decrease since 2011 . Significant emission reductions are recorded for the agriculture , energy industries and residential sectors . These decreases come despite modest growth in the domestic economy of 1.7% over the year . <p> Table below : Provisional greenhouse gas emissions for 2018 and 2019 for Ireland .
