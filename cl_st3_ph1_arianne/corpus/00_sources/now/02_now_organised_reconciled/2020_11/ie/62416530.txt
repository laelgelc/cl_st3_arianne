" <p> " For the 4.9 million people currently living in the Republic of Ireland this proposal will he continued " provide recognition of their involvement in our national story , " Mr Roddy said . <p> " In addition to this , it provides families with the opportunity to remember and acknowledge family members that are now deceased . By recognising both the living and the dead this can be a commemoration of all our people who otherwise might not be acknowledged .
