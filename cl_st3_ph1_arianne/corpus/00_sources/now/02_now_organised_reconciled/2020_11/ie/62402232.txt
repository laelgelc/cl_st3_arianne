When they break , they create turbulence that drives an upward flux of nutrients from the deep ocean ( which is light-limited but nutrient-rich ) to the photic zone above ( which is nutrient-poor ) . <p> One of the best places for studying internal tides is the Atlantic coastline of the Caribbean islands , especially the southern Bahamas and the Turks and Caicos Islands . Traditionally , observing internal waves required multiple instruments , which caused problems when matching biochemical responses to the physical processes .
