This is possibly the simplest explanation anyone can offer to justify the 20-year-long project to map the seabed surrounding Ireland . <p> Fury is the joint manager -- with Sean Cullen from the Geological Survey Ireland ( GSI ) -- of the integrated mapping for the sustainable development of Ireland 's marine resources ( Infomar ) , a project funded by the Department of Environment , Climate and Communications . <p> Its aim is to map Ireland 's entire seabed by 2026 , becoming the first country in the world to have systematically mapped its marine territory . Scientists calculate the depth of the ocean by measuring the time it takes for the sound beam to come back to the surface . <p> So-called sound velocity profilers measure the temperature , salinity and changes in the density of the water .
