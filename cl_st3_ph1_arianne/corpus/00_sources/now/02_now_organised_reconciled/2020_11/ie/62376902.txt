<p> Making the announcement , Minister Heather Humphreys said : " The Western Development Commission has played an important role in promoting economic and social development in the region since it was established in 1999 . <p> " Over the past three years in particular , the WDC has established itself as an important contributor to the development of the region , supporting the objectives of Project Ireland 2040 to make Ireland a better country for all . <p> " The WDC has also made important contributions to the development of policy at national level in areas such as remote working , climate change and rural development . <p> " I am delighted to appoint a new board to guide and direct the work of the Commission for the next three years . <p> " There were many expression of interest in appointment to the new board and I want to thank everyone who took the time to apply through the State Boards process . <p> " The composition of the new board reflects a mix of new members and previous board members . <p> " They possess an excellent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the public sector , the community sector and the education sector . <p> " I have asked Mr. Gerry Finn , who has been a member of the WDC Board for the last three years , to take up the position of chair of the board , and I wish him well in that role . " <p> The minister added : " I want to thank the previous board for their work in supporting the development of the western region and their careful stewardship of the WDC over the last three years . <p> " The WDC 's five-year Strategy , ' Work Smarter , Live Better ' , which was published in 2019 , will provide a strong framework for the incoming Board to continue the WDC 's work over the coming period . <p> " I have no doubt that the incoming board will continue to make a significant contribution to economic and social development in the western region , in collaboration with other key stakeholders .
