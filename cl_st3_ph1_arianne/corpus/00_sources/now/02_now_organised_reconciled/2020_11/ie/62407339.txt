<p> Asked how he would describe India-EU relations , recently appointed Indian Ambassador to the European Union , Santosh Jha , said they were ' excellent ' . According to the official , " A new impetus has enabled us to focus on our strategic partnership with a renewed sense of vigour , " since the July summit meeting between the EU and India . <p> The diplomat , appointed in the summer , believes the key to the relationship is having a common world view , saying , " As the two largest democracies in the world , it is in our interest to cooperate , in order to strengthen the rules-based international order and multilateralism .
