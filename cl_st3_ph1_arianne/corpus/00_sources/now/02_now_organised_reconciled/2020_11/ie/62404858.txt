<h> Linde included in DJSI for 18th consecutive year <p> Linde has been listed on the Dow Jones Sustainability World Index ( DJSI World ) for the 18th consecutive year . <p> Also included on the DJSI North America , the DJSI World is a trusted benchmark for investors incorporating ESG factors into their decision-making . It identifies the top 10% of companies in each sector among the largest 2,500 companies in the S&amp;P Global Broad Market Index . <p> Companies are selected based on a detailed analysis of long-term economic , environmental and social criteria .
