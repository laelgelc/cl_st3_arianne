<p> The lack of medicinal products and medical devices , of which the general public has been so aware of over the past few months , is nothing new . The COVID-19 health crisis and the sudden and greatly increased global demand for medicines has merely shone a public light on a phenomenon that healthcare professionals and some patients were already well aware of . <p> Unfortunately , this situation is at risk of creating headlines again , as we enter flu season and are hit in full force by the second wave of COVID-19 . The disruption and pressures affecting supply were multiplied twenty times between 2000 and 2018 , resulting in considerable risks to patient safety and weakening the healthcare system in the Member States . <p> The COVID-19 health crisis has also highlighted the increasing dependence of the European Union on third countries , mainly China and India .
