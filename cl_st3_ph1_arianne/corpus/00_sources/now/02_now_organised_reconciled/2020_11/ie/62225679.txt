<p> Former Unilever CEO Paul Polman , round-the-world sailor Dame Ellen MacArthur and UN Principles for Responsible Investment CEO Fiona Reynolds are among the leading figures on climate action and finance who will be taking part in Climate Finance Week Ireland from 2-6 November . <p> The AIB-sponsored event will focus on finding sustainable ways to repair the economic damage caused by Covid-19 and explore how Ireland can best finance its transition to a low-carbon economy . <p> More than 20 virtual events will take place throughout the week .
