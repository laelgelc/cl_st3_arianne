<p> With pressure on land and resources on the rise and the effects of climate change increasing , the fresh produce industry has been developing innovative solutions so it can continue to deliver a quality product . <p> These solutions took centre stage during Green Future : New Production Models , the second session of Asiafruit Congress ON , Asia 's leading conference event for fresh produce decision-makers . <p> To begin the session , viewers from around the world were introduced to Ernst van den Ende , managing director of the plant sciences group at Wageningen University &amp; Research , one of the world 's leading agricultural research institutes . <p> Van den Ende provided an overview of Wageningen University &amp; Research 's approach to developing production innovations . <p> " Most of the research focusing on agricultural production in Wageningen is under the umbrella of more , with less and better , " said van den Ende . <p> " We need to produce more per square metre in order to feed the world but at the same time we have to do that with less inputs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and healthier . " <p> To achieve this Wageningen has looked to deliver innovation in a range of areas such as vertical farming , the use of robotics and drones , data science and artificial intelligence , strip farming and greenhouses . <p> " We first look at the ecological principles and then we try to think , what kind of technology fits into the system to use the ecological principles ? That is really changing the production systems a lot , " said van den Ende . <p> " Technology plays an important role because if we are able to modernise agriculture maybe that 's a more appealing job for youngsters , than it is today . " <p> The focus then turned to vertical farming and Jan Doldersum , manager of chain and retail of Rijk Zwaan and Jarno Mooren , plant specialist of City Farming , Signify , shared the advances being made in different areas of the sector . <p> Doldersum spoke of the role of vertical farming in meeting the pertinent challenges of food safety and product availability and Rijk Zwaan 's efforts to provide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and variety is crucial , its best quality and maximum yield , " Doldersum . <p> " What is the exact role of Rijk Zwaan in these kinds of projects ?
