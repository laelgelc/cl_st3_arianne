Therefore to understand drought we have to understand it as both a natural and social phenomenon . " <p> Agricultural drought is defined by a lack of soil water to support the growth of crops , caused by too little rainfall , whether it meets the requirement of a meteorological drought or not . <p> Hydrological drought is caused by the low availability of surface water , such as low water levels in dams , rivers , lakes and other @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or high water use , for instance . <p> Socioeconomic drought occurs when human activity is affected by any type of drought . I do not think anyone has undertaken such a study yet " . <h> How has the drought affected agriculture ? <p> The head of the Agricultural Business Chamber , John Purchase , told Africa Check that currently effects on agriculture can be seen in the Free State , KwaZulu-Natal and the North West " as summer crops , especially maize , could not be planted in many areas , or plantings were severely damaged by the drought and heat " . <p> Soya , sorghum , groundnuts and sunflower crops have also been affected , which has negatively impacted South Africa 's food security .
