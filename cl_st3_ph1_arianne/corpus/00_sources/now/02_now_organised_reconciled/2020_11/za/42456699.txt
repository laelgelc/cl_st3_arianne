<h> Impact of climate change on the economy <h> Climate change affects the state of the economy . How does this happen and what 's the solution ? <p> Apart from its serious impact on the environment and people , climate change is one of the biggest threats to economic stability . <p> Over the last two years , mounting public awareness , fanned by widespread perception that extreme weather events are becoming more frequent , as well as the growing weight of scientific evidence on changing weather patterns , has added further urgency to the debate . <p> A study by Deloitte focusing on the impact of climate change on the state of the economy has highlighted the immense cost and implications in this regard .
