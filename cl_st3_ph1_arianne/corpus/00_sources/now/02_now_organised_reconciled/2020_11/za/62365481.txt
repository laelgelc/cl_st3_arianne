<p> While we have come a long way from those days , scientists and authorities are still cautioning residents to be wary as another ' Day Zero ' could easily strike at any moment . <p> According to a study by Stanford University and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ( NOAA ) , Cape Town has a high chance of facing another Day Zero within the next decade . <p> The study , published in the journal Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences , made use of new high-resolution simulations to conclude that human-caused climate change made the Day Zero drought in southwestern South Africa five to six times more likely . Furthermore , such extreme events could go from being rare to common events by the end of the century . <p> " In a way , the ' Day Zero ' drought might have been a sort of taste of what the future may be , " said lead author Salvatore Pascale , a research scientist at Stanford 's School of Earth , Energy &amp; Environmental Sciences ( Stanford Earth ) .
