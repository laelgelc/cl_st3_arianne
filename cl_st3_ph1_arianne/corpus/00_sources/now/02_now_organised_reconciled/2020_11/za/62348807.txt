These are positions espoused by new DA leader John Steenhuisen and his supporters during the 2020 DA Federal Congress . <p> These are positions that are closer to the ( US ) Republicans , not the Democrats ; the ( UK ) Tories , not Labour . <p> Yet within the party led by Steenhuisen are younger people pushing for the " wokest " of policy positions . <p> At the congress , younger delegates put forward resolutions calling for the special prevention of LGBTQIA+ hate crimes , making gay conversion therapy illegal , the implementation of rent control in DA metros , the establishment of " ecocide " as a crime against @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> These proposed resolutions sit rather uneasily next to those calling for the weakening of collective bargaining and the reduction of taxation . <p> By the end of the weekend , the impression was that the young wokes within the party are indulged -- up to a point . That point is where these progressive proposals touch on the free market economy , at which point they must be smacked down . <p> This was very evident from a look at the resolutions which passed and failed at the congress . In particular , to hear DA representatives pushing for such leftie policies -- even if they were shot down by the majority -- is an indication of internal ideological contestation that seldom reaches public eyes unless it involves race and identity . <p> But it also makes one wonder how a leader goes about uniting party delegates among whom differences of opinion seem profound . It has often been said of the DA that , like many liberal parties , its positions are economically conservative but socially liberal . <p> Yet a party that sees resolutions raised at the same congress calling simultaneously for a crackdown on unions and the implementation of rent control is , arguably , not just healthily diverse . It may also be fundamentally confused . <p> Steenhuisen effectively acknowledged this in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : " For a while , we lost sight of who we are and what we offer : clear , principled and decisive leadership . " <p> This new decisiveness was evident even before the foregone conclusion of the election results , with the issue of farm murders placed firmly in the spotlight by the congress . <p> Indeed , in his opening address to the congress , party chairperson Ivan Meyer placed the issue at the very top of South Africa 's social ills by calling for a moment of silence for " those who have fallen " in farm attacks . <p> That farm attacks are a genuine problem in South Africa is unquestionable . The resolution passed . <p> The direction being embarked upon by Steenhuisen 's party is indeed clear , and seems to have as its priority the winning back of the famous votes lost to the Freedom Front Plus in the last election . The question is , what comparable special effort is the party making in policy and messaging to win back the township votes shed ? <p> What effort , too , will Steenhuisen 's DA make to ensure that the likes of Ntuli and other young progressives have a home @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 20% of delegates ' votes at the congress , that figure still amounts to a few hundred of the party 's most important figures . <p> That is not negligible , particularly given the " clear blue water " -- to borrow a favourite DA phrase -- that would seem to exist in ideological terms between the Steenhuisen camp and that of Ntuli . <p> To run any organisation in a democratic fashion is not easy . To do so in the context of a political party with local government elections less than a year away is even less so , especially as the party led by Steenhuisen 's predecessor managed to shoot the lights out -- relatively speaking -- in the 2016 polls . <p> But the most difficult task of all for Steenhuisen may be to hold a course that is acceptable to the highest number possible of his own party 's representatives -- avoiding the damage of further high-profile resignations .
