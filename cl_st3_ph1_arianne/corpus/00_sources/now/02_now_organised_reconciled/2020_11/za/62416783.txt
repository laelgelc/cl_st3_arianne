As a result , the City lifted drought-level water restrictions and reduced the tariff level as of November 1 . <p> However , it would be a mistake to " put our water situation to rest " as suggested by Mr Wydeman . <p> Climate change modelling undertaken by the City indicates that less rain will fall , and that droughts will be more common , last longer and be more severe . <p> We know that dams provide only two years of security .
