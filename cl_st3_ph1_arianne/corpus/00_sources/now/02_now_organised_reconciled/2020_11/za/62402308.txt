<p> An international coalition of leading climate research universities urges world leaders to prioritise climate change and will issue its first declaration ahead of the G20 summit . <p> The declaration , representing one-third of the 100 high-performing climate research universities and a quarter of the top 100 environmental research universities worldwide , wants world leaders to use the post-Covid recovery to implement measures to counteract climate change . <p> They also warn that failure to do so will result in catastrophic consequences for generations to come . <p> The climate alliance is led by the International Universities Climate Alliance , which includes UCT . <p> Climate change is an enormous challenge to overcome but presents the world an opportunity to unite against climate change and invest in a shared-future . <p> The climate alliance seeks to support global leaders to meet the challenge of the current climate crisis through sharing evidence-based information on climate research .
