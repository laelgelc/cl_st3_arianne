It is expected to be one of the first Ford plants in the world to achieve this status . <p> Ford Motor Company of Southern Africa ( FMCSA ) has embarked on a pioneering solar energy project for its Silverton Assembly Plant in Pretoria , as part of its vision to develop an integrated renewable energy solution that aims to have the Silverton Assembly Plant entirely ' green ' and energy self-sufficient by 2024 -- making it one of the very first Ford plants in the world to achieve this status . <p> The company has kickstarted ' Project Blue Oval ' through a partnership with SolarAfrica to provide a 13.5MW solar installation for the facility , valued at R135-million , which will deliver approximately 30-percent of the Silverton plant 's annual power requirements .
