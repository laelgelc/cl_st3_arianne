fisheries is the appeal authority for any appeals against such authorisations . <p> In late September , the centre launched its three-part high court review and rescind application against the current Minister of Environment Barbara Creecy , and Minister of Mineral Resources Gwede Mantashe and his department 's Western Cape regional office . <p> The centre is seeking to : <p> set aside Creecy 's rejection of its appeal against the DMRE 's environmental authorisation for Tormin 's expansion plans ;
