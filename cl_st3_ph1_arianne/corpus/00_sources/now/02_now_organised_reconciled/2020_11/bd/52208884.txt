<h> 30 organisations win Joy Bangla Youth Award 2020 <h> Thirty organisations have won the Joy Bangla Youth Award 2020 for their work to develop the country and society . <p> Sajeeb Wazed Joy , the prime minister 's son an ICT adviser , announced the winners at a virtual programme organised by Young Bangla , the youth platform of the Awami League 's Centre for Research and Information , on Tuesday . <p> The winners include 16 under social inclusion category and 14 under social development . <p> Young Bangla picked 47 organisations for the final from 600 groups of youths aged between 18 and 35 . <p> Describing their work as " outstanding " , Joy said they are working to serve the common people in an inspiring manner . <p> He thanked them for working for the poor children , people with disabilities and other marginalised people . <p> Joy said the winners were trying to resolve the problems they found while some other people always prefer to complain . <p> " They ( winners ) are not big organisations , maybe a single youth working in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their own ideas instead of complaining .
