<h> India 's Modi calls Biden to advance strategic ties <h> Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi has spoken to US President-elect Joe Biden by telephone to congratulate him on his election victory , which he said showed the strength of US democracy , and he vowed to deepen strategic ties . <p> Modi initially sent his congratulations to Biden in a social media message soon after US television networks projected him to be the winner of the Nov. 3 election even though President Donald Trump has refused to concede defeat . <p> Modi followed that up with a phone call to congratulate Biden and reaffirm his commitment to a strategic partnership between the two big democracies , Modi said on Twitter late on Tuesday . <p> " Prime Minister Modi warmly congratulated President-elect Biden on his election , describing it as a testament to the strength and resilience of democratic traditions in the United States , " the Indian foreign ministry said . <p> Modi had built close ties with Trump . The two leaders held a joint rally in the US city of Houston last year and again in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a visit to India by Trump . <p> Modi 's critics said the prime minister had come dangerously close to endorsing Trump 's candidature , which they said could hurt India 's prospects under a Democratic administration . <p> India and the United States drew closer during Trump 's presidency as both countries aimed to counter China 's expanding military and economic influence in the region . <p> During the telephone call , Modi also extended his best wishes to Vice President-elect Kamala Harris , the daughter of an Indian immigrant whose election to the second highest public office in the United States has been cheered in India . <p> The two leaders also discussed their priorities , including containing the novel coronavirus pandemic , promoting access to affordable vaccines and tackling climate change , the foreign ministry said .
