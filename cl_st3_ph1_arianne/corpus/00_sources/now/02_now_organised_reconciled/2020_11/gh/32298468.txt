<h> Technology For All-Year-Round Cocoa Production Unveiled <p> A new technology designed to supply continuous water to cocoa farmers for all-year-round production has been introduced by Mondelez International , the implementer of the Cocoa Life Programme . <p> The programme is meant to mitigate the effect of climate change on cocoa production in Ghana and help triple the country 's annual yield . <p> The technology , known as the Solar-powered Irrigation System , is designed to supply a year-round consistent water supply to over 70,000 cocoa farmers under the Cocoa Life Programme . <p> It will help cocoa farmers to produce throughout the season and will not have to wait for rainfall to determine their annual yields . <p> Speaking at the launch of the first pilot project of the new technology at Otwebediedua in the Eastern Region , the Head of Cocoa Life Programme in Ghana , Mrs. Yaa Peprah Amekudzi , said the new technology would ensure that cocoa would get the required liters of water for high yields throughout the year . <p> She explained that the technology had been introduced to solve the perennial complaint @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which continued to affect cocoa production in the country . <p> " As an organization committed to helping cocoa farmers benefit from their work and also ensure sustainability of cocoa production , we decided to undertake research on how other countries were able to generate enough water supplies for their cocoa production , " Mrs. Amekudzi said . <p> The research , she emphasized , led to the discovery of the solar-powered irrigation system , which has the capacity to supply more than 5000 liters of water hourly , depending on its size . <p> Mrs. Amekudzi said the pilot programme of the irrigation technology would cover 10 farms in the six regions Cocoa Life operates within . <p> " In all , we operate in 700 communities spread across 18 districts within six regions of Ghana . More than 70,000 cocoa farmers are under the Cocoa Life Programme , " she said . <p> Mrs. Amekudzi added that " with all the policies that Ghana COCOBOD is coming out with , supported by the private sector and cocoa farmers , Ghana can achieve one million metric tonnes of cocoa @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ multi-million dollar factory to process cocoa waste would be opened , adding that " this will further boost the cocoa sector " . <p> Technical aid and member of the research team at Mondelez International , Edward Kumah , said the introduction of solar irrigation technology would help mitigate the risks associated with climate change . <p> He said cocoa needed about 30 liters of water per day and if the water requirement was achieved , it would bear fruits throughout the year .
