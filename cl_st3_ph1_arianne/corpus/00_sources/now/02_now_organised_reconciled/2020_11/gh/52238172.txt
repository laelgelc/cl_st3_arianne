But the presumption of net benefits from fossil fuels is eroding as widening circles of people , rich and poor experience the downside . No ECA support for wind projects in the four African countries has been found . <p> Although many industrialized nations are advocating a switch to renewables at home , such support is not seen in their cooperation with developing countries . While they commit to phasing out fossil fuel energy domestically , these commitments are abandoned as soon as their companies move across international borders , where they continue to push dirty energy , and as such contribute to climate change . <p> Not in line with the Paris Climate Agreement <p> Export credit agencies provide insurances and guarantees to companies doing business abroad .
