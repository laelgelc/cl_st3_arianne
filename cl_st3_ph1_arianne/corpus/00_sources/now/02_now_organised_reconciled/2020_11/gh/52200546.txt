<h> Commonwealth Mourns Former President Of Ghana Jerry John Rawlings <p> Commonwealth Secretary-General Rt Hon Patricia Scotland Q.C has expressed her sadness at the passing today of Flt . Lt. Jerry John Rawlings , former President of Ghana . <p> " I am saddened to hear of the passing of Flt Lt Jerry John Rawlings , the first President of the Fourth Republic of Ghana . <p> " Former President Rawlings will be remembered in Ghana , Africa and beyond for spearheading Ghana 's return to multi-party democracy in the early 1990s and the smooth transition of power in 2001 . <p> During his tenure , Ghana was one of the first eight member countries to serve on the newly formed Commonwealth Ministerial Action Group ( CMAG ) . In 1998 , he supported CMAG 's Ministerial Mission to Sierra Leone with the provision of a base and logistical assistance in Accra . <p> " On behalf of the Commonwealth family , I would like to convey our deepest condolences to the family of Flt Lt Rawlings , and the Government and people of Ghana .
