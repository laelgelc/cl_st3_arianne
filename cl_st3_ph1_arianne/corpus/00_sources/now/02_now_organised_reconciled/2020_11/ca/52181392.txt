For Our Kids was formed to provide a national platform for parents and grandparents to choose how to get involved . <p> Just over a year later , with chapters in 16 Canadian cities , For Our Kids is already making a big difference . They 've hosted town halls with parents and politicians . Their neighbourhoods are dotted with green hearts thanking climate champions .
