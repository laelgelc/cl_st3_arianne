<h> Linde Included in Dow Jones Sustainability World Index for 18th Consecutive Year <p> ACCESSWIRE <p> Linde Included in Dow Jones SustainabilityTM World Index for 18th Consecutive Year <p> GUILDFORD , UK / ACCESSWIRE / November 18 , 2020 / Linde ( NYSE:LIN ) ( FWB:LIN ) has once again been recognized as a global leader in sustainability through its inclusion in the Dow Jones SustainabilityTM World Index ( DJSI World ) and DJSI North America . Linde is the only company in the chemical sector to be admitted in the DJSI World for eighteen consecutive years . <p> The DJSI World is a trusted benchmark for investors incorporating ESG factors into their decision-making . It identifies the top 10 percent of companies in each sector among the largest 2,500 companies in the S&amp;P Global Broad Market Index . Companies are selected based on a detailed analysis of long-term economic , environmental and social criteria . Linde received a perfect score in the sector for several criteria including risk governance , codes of conduct , climate change strategy , water-related risks , employee development programs and philanthropic activities . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are integral to everything we do on behalf of our stakeholders , " said Steve Angel , Chief Executive Officer of Linde . <p> Linde helps its customers improve their environmental performance and reduce their carbon footprint .
