<p> On October 19 , 2020 , Breakwater Resources Limited , which operates the Langlois Mine , pleaded guilty in the Val -- d'Or courthouse to one count of violating the Fisheries Act . The company was fined $350,000 , which will be directed to the Government of Canada 's Environmental Damages Fund . <p> On February 28 , 2018 , a 500-litre spill of flocculent from the Langlois mining site in Lebel -- sur -- Qu ? villon resulted in a discharge of acutely lethal effluent into the Wedding River .
