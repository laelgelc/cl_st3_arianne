We welcome new bloggers -- contact us for details . <p> In the early weeks of the COVID-19 pandemic , a video went viral portraying a Cuban medical team of doctors and infectious disease specialists arriving in Italy , just at the peak of its first wave . <p> The video was moving -- a team of medical personnel from a small island country putting their lives at risk to help in Europe . But that effort was n't unusual for Cuba . Before the start of the pandemic , Cuba already had 28,000 medical personnel working in 60 countries -- more than all the G7 countries combined . <p> Cuba has had a longstanding commitment to both domestic and international medical innovation and cooperation , dating back to 1960 . Since that time , an estimated 400,000 Cuban doctors , nurses and technicians have provided medical assistance throughout @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cuba is now helping countries in four continents in the struggle against COVID-19 . Since March 2020 , Cuba has sent 52 medical teams to 39 countries to buttress often shaky healthcare systems where the poor and marginalized are at the most risk of contracting the disease . <p> Cuba 's work in fighting COVID-19 around the world is part of a decades-long process of solidarity , supporting the medical needs of those most at risk , wherever they may be . Who else has made such a contribution to international health ? <p> In 2005 , the Cuban leadership took the pulse of a world increasingly beset by natural disasters such as floods and hurricanes , and outbreaks of transmissible diseases , mainly attributable to climate change . Their conclusion was that the cycles of pandemics and other disasters was liable to speed up in the 21st century . They therefore decided to establish the Henry Reeve Brigade . <p> Canada has excellent medical personnel and top-notch infectious disease specialists , and yet the country seems to have been caught as unprepared for a second wave of COVID-19 as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Canada , we faced a startling scarcity of personal protective equipment and a preventable crisis in long-term care homes across the country . <p> The Canadian government was the only one in the world to refuse a citizen request for help in the case of the Manitoba First Nations ' ask for Cuban health personnel to buttress the frail health care structures found in isolated communities . <p> As is the case for many wealthy countries , Canada has mostly been inward-looking , not considering that an uncontrolled outbreak elsewhere outside of our borders can become a future threat to all nations , including our own . While promoting public health is tied to peace and security , uncontrolled disease can only lead to the opposite , through humanitarian crises , economic and social instability . <p> Cuban health teams have participated in dozens of missions including sending medical staff to Haiti in 2010 to provide care after a devastating earthquake and then staying on to fight cholera , ultimately vaccinating 400,000 patients . This nomination has been endorsed by many eminent Canadians such as Bruce Cockburn , Michaelle Jean , Senator Pierrette Ringuette and David Suzuki as well as several distinguished Canadian organizations , including the Council of Canadians . <p> Around the world , every country is currently dealing with the massive challenge posed by COVID-19 .
