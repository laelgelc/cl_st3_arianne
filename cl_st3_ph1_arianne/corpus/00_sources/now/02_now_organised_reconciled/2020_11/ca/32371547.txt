Today 's weather is expected to start out cloudy with a 40 per cent chance of showers or drizzle this morning , then switch to rain in the afternoon , with a high of 7 C , according to Environment and Climate Change Canada . Overnight , it looks like a chance of showers and the temperature remaining at around 7 C. Thursday 's weather is similar to today 's with a chance of drizzle in the morning and afternoon rain , though it looks a little warmer , with a high of 10 C. Then there should be some respite from the wet weather on Friday , as the forecast calls for a mix of sun and cloud , and 9 C. There 's more rain overnight Friday but then Saturday looks pleasant , again with a mix of sun and cloud and 9 C.
