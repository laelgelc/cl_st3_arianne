<p> Each year , about 196,000 tonnes of seafood -- everything from salmon to scallops -- is harvested off the B.C. coast . <p> But unlike its East Coast counterparts , the province does n't have a fisheries minister . <p> In the past several decades , the province has seen tumbling salmon populations , an increasingly inequitable distribution of the fisheries ' economic benefits and a drop in local processing capacity . All have eaten away at coastal communities , and the province 's ability to feed itself from the sea , a situation that advocates say calls for a minister dedicated to the portfolio . <p> " Right now ( we ) have no ministry that has the resources or capacity to focus on that plethora of environmental , social and economic issues , " said Tasha Sutcliffe , senior fisheries policy adviser at Ecotrust Canada . <p> Those issues are broad .
