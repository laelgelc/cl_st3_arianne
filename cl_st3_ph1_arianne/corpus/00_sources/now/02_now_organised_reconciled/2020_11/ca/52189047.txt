<h> The Government of Canada finalizes regulations to improve air quality and protect human health <p> OTTAWA , ON , Nov. 10 , 2020 /CNW/ - The Government of Canada 's number one priority remains keeping Canadians safe and supporting families and businesses through the COVID-19 pandemic . Protecting our health also means taking steps to keep our air clean . <p> New national regulations to reduce air pollution from the petroleum and petrochemical sectors will improve air quality for nearby communities and workers . ( CNW Group/Environment and Climate Change Canada ) <p> Today , the Minister of Environment and Climate Change , the Honourable Jonathan Wilkinson , announced that the Government of Canada has finalized national regulations that will reduce pollution from petroleum and petrochemical facilities across the country , including in Sarnia , Mississauga , Montr ? al , Burnaby , Prince George , Saint John , and many communities in Alberta and Saskatchewan . <p> Harmful airborne substances -- known as volatile organic compounds -- emitted from these facilities contribute to premature deaths and more frequent and severe asthma symptoms , and they force workers and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Government of Canada worked closely with industry to provide many compliance options to minimize costs .
