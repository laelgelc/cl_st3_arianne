<p> OTTAWA , Nov 17 ( Reuters ) - Climate change will have a profound impact on Canada 's economy and the country must mobilize quickly to mitigate the threat as well as capitalize on opportunities , the Governor of the Bank of Canada said on Tuesday . <p> Part of that effort includes ensuring the financial system is well positioned to help businesses and households manage risks , both in terms of extreme weather and the transition to low-carbon growth , Tiff Macklem said in remarks to a public policy group . <p> " Our financial system proved to be resilient during the global financial crisis and has been a key shock absorber so far through the COVID-19 pandemic , " Macklem said . " We need to ensure the financial system is just as resilient in the face of climate change . " <p> A stable financial system is resilient to the physical and transition risks , he added , noting that those risks are often mispriced and underappreciated . <p> " We need to position Canada to seize the climate-smart opportunities that consumers , workers and investors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to mitigate the threat and capitalize on the opportunity , we all need to mobilize .
