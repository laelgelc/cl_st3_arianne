The objective was to be more agile in responding to changing market conditions and customer requirements while containing manufacturing costs through more efficient maintenance planning and execution . This strategy has proven to have significant merit and has been adopted as the Company 's standard operating approach . At the same time , it became clear that the 100 Mile House OSB mill was unlikely to have a role to play in the future . As the Company 's highest cost operation , this mill had been indefinitely curtailed since August 2019 in response to a wood supply shortage and rising fibre costs .
