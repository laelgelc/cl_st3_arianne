<h> Hard-hit Central America in crosshairs of another hurricane <p> Honduras and Guatemala announced evacuations Friday as a second major hurricane in days closed in on Central America with the region still reeling from deadly storm Eta last week . <p> Eta killed more than 200 people across Central America , with heavy rain bursting river banks and triggering landslides as far north as Chiapas , Mexico . <p> The US National Hurricane Center ( NHC ) in Miami has now confirmed that another major hurricane is approaching Honduras , Nicaragua and Guatemala , whose populations total more than 30 million . <p> The NHC forecasts Tropical Storm Iota to become a Category 2 or 3 hurricane as it moves into the same shell-shocked countries , hitting Nicaragua and Honduras by late Sunday or early Monday -- less than two weeks after Eta hit . <p> Authorities in Honduras on Friday ordered the evacuation by police and the army of people in the area of San Pedro Sula -- the country 's second city and industrial capital , located 180 kilometers ( 110 miles ) north of Tegucigalpa . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ seen flooded following the passage of storm Eta on November 7 , 2020 <p> The San Pedro Sula valley was hit hard by Eta and about 40,000 people are still in shelters across the country . <p> Guatemala 's disaster management agency CONRED meanwhile called on residents in the country 's most threatened areas in the north and northeast to voluntarily evacuate to shelters .
