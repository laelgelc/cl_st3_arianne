<p> Following the collapse of a public bridge over the Zamora River in Los Encuentros two weeks ago , a small number of local community members erected an illegal blockade on the public road leading to FDN . Although the Company had agreed to fund $3.0 million for the construction of a new public bridge , the blockade organizers attempted to capitalize on the bridge collapse . Following extensive efforts by the Company , a resolution was reached , and the blockade was lifted . <p> Throughout the 15-day blockade of a public road to FDN , operations continued .
