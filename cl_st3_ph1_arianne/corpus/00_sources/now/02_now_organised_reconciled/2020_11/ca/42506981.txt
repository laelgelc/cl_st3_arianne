The Plague of Justinian which hit the Mediterranean area in the 500s not only killed millions but it crucially weakened the Byzantine Empire and helped ruin its plans to reconquer western Europe from the barbarians . The Black Death of the 1300s wiped out over a hundred million people in Europe and Asia . It sped the decline of the feudal system , engendered persecution of Jews , lepers , and gypsies , and created a better economic life for the peasantry whose labour was now much more valuable .
