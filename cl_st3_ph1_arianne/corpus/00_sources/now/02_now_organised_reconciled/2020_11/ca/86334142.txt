<h> Why the Future of Nuclear Power Is Tiny and Factory-Made <p> In the 1950s , few things seemed more futuristic and utopian than harnessing nuclear energy to power your home . Towering nuclear reactors popped up across the U.S. with the promise of harvesting energy from smashed atoms of Uranium to power everything from lights in an office to an oven cooking a pot roast . With clean and efficient nuclear power , anything seemed possible . <p> But as the years went on , doubt about the safety of these reactors began to poison the bright future they 'd once promised . Stories of nuclear waste polluting waterways downstream of power plants began to stir alarm , and in the 1980s the Chernobyl nuclear power plant explosion sent radiation billowing across Europe and into the tissues of an estimated 4,000 Ukrainians who died from radiation poisoning .
