<h> Food integrity important to Canadians <p> According to new research from the Canadian Centre for Food Integrity ( CCFI ) , Canadians are optimistic about their food supply , even as Canada 's food system faces exceptional pressure and public scrutiny due to the global pandemic of COVID-19 in 2020 . <p> The recently-unveiled 5th edition of the 2020 Public Trust Research Report , Trends in Trust &amp; The Path Forward indicates that of the 2,903 Canadians sampled , the proportion of Canadians who feel that Canada 's food system is headed in the right direction has reached a five-year high . <p> 47% of Canadians are confident about the overall direction of the food system , after a significant 12-point increase compared to 2019 . <p> John Jamieson is President and CEO of the Canadian Centre for Food Integrity . <p> He says Canada 's food system stakeholders should be proud of their efforts throughout the pandemic , that has ensured Canadians uninterrupted access to healthy , Canadian-grown food . <p> As the pandemic continues to take its toll on the economy and unemployment rates , Canadians are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ circumstances have highlighted this struggle , with 51% of Canadians indicating they have less money to spend on food , as a direct result of the pandemic . <p> And the 2020 research also concluded that the topic of sustainability in food is not just a trend but a requirement to be a trusted and successful food system player . Sustainable food means many things to many people , but for almost half of Canadians , 45% , it refers to food options and production practices that address climate change and have a positive impact on the environment .
