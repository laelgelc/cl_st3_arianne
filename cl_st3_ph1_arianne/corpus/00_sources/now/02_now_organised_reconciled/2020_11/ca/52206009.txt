<h> New student residence being built at UTSC will double residence capacity <p> The design of the new proposed residence at UTSC . COURTESY OF HANDEL ARCHITECTS <p> Construction has begun on a new 750-student residence building at UTSC that is expected to be completed by fall 2023 . The new space will be on the site of a current campus parking lot located at 3300 -- 3316 Ellesmere Road . <p> The structure will be the first residence built at UTSC since Joan Foley Hall in 2003 . Joan Foley Hall is UTSC 's sole apartment-style residence , alongside 13 townhouse residences , which serve as another option for students . <p> Facilities <p> Residential floors will be arranged into three sections , or ' communities , ' that will vary in colour and design . The building will include a mix of single- and double-occupancy bedrooms , fully accessible suites , and kitchen , lounge , and study spaces . <p> The building will also feature a rooftop garden and terrace , an event space , and rooms dedicated to workshop-style learning . <p> The residence 's dining @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ days per week for the first time , as campus food services are currently open only from Monday through Friday . <p> The residence will also house the Office of Student Experience &amp; Wellbeing and Student Housing &amp; Residence Life , which currently operate out of UTSC 's Arts and Administration Building and the Residence Centre , respectively . <p> In an email toThe Varsity , Andrew Arifuzzaman , UTSC 's Chief Administrative Officer , wrote that " Expanding our residence spaces has been a priority for the University , to meet a major need at UTSC , and provide a hub of community that meets the needs of our students . " <p> " The project showcases U of T 's commitment to providing dynamic approaches to learning and living , and investing in infrastructure that makes our campus an environment for excellence .
