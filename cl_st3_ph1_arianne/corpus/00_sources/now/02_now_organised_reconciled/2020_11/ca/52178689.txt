<h> Muneesh Sharma and Madi Kennedy : Time for B.C. to pick up the pace on building retrofits <p> With election day in the rearview mirror , many British Columbians are looking to the provincial government to take strong leadership on urgent economic and social priorities in concert with bold action on climate change . <p> Upgrading the buildings where we live and work ticks all of these boxes by reducing carbon pollution ; increasing safety , public health , and housing affordability ; and aiding in economic recovery by creating thousands of local jobs using mostly local materials . <h> What is PACE ? <p> PACE financing is an innovative " green " loan instrument developed in California in 2008 that overcomes the capital constraints that many buildings owners face when contemplating upgrades . The concept is simple : building owners borrow funds to complete retrofits , such as replacing gas furnaces with heat pumps , upgrading windows , adding solar panels , or even making seismic improvements . <p> What makes these loans different is they are registered on the property title , not to the owner , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are repaid in installments added to property taxes over the retrofit 's lifespan . PACE programs are now active in 37 U.S. states , resulting in almost 300,000 retrofit projects and US$16.5 billion in economic impacts . <p> PACE , which enjoys cross-sector support , is attractive to commercial and residential property owners because they can fund projects with no out-of-pocket costs . PACE 's transferability from one owner to the next with the sale of the property means that building owners are only on the hook for the cost of a retrofit during the period they are benefitting from it . <p> With flexible financing terms and repayment periods up to 30 years , it 's possible to take on deep , whole-building retrofits with substantial energy and carbon savings . The annual utility-bill savings for a PACE project sometimes surpass the yearly repayments ( levied on property-tax bills ) so that property owners can be cash-flow positive immediately . That means building owners have more money to spend on other capital projects , expenses , or business expansion . <h> Can PACE work in B.C. ? <p> We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ growing interest via Union of B.C. Adopting a provincewide program would help cities meet climate goals without saddling cities with debt . <p> In September , the government committed to investing $2 million in a PACE roadmap and pilot program through its Economic Recovery Plan . This investment is welcome , but sound policies can fail through poor execution . Without scale and continuity , a pilot program would likely not achieve the desired outcome . The U.S. experience shows that it takes time and scale to create a PACE ecosystem where property owners , underwriters , appraisers , financiers , and contractors understand PACE and are comfortable with its promotion and use . <h> How can we get to implementation ? <p> PACE BC -- an alliance of city councillors , business owners , and environmental organizations -- is asking the provincial government to unlock PACE for British Columbians through enabling legislation .
