<p> SAINT JOHN , NB , Nov. 5 , 2020 /CNW/ - Wild salmon is an iconic Canadian species that has faced challenges and a rapid decline in recent decades , particularly in the Atlantic Ocean . Atlantic salmon plays an important role in the culture and livelihoods of many on the East Coast , including Indigenous peoples . <p> Dr. Kurt Samways loading a fish into a tank to be lifted to the river by helicopter .
