<p> To bring forward effective solutions to the drastic climate change the world is undergoing , the youngsters from more than hundred and fifty major cities around the world are joining hands on November 13 , 14 and 15 to participate in Climathon 2020 . <p> Contestants representing different cities in South Asia including Dhakka , Karachi , Hydrabath , Mumbai , and Jaffna are participating in the event this year.Climathon is an initiative started in Jaffna in 2019 and following its success , continuing for the 2nd year this time . Due to the COVID 19 pandemic situation around the world , we are conducting it online this time but we are sure this will never limit us from providing the best this year too . <p> The main organizer of this event this year is OMNE ( Organic Movement of North and East ) .
