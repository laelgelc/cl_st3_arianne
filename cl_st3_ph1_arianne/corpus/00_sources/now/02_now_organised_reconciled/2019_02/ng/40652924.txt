<h> NLNG Calls For 2019 Science , Literature Entries <p> It is that time of the year again when the Advisory Boards for The Nigeria Prize for Science , The Nigeria Prize for Literature and The Literary Criticism Prize , sponsored by Nigeria LNG ( NLNG ) Limited , publish the call for entries for the 2019 edition of prizes , thereby flagging off this year 's competitions . <p> The Science and Literature prizes , which are now in their 15th year , each come with a cash prize of $100,000 while the Literary Criticism Prize has a prize money of N1million . <p> The science competition , which recognises outstanding scientific achievements by Nigerians and non-Nigerians , will this year focus on Climate Change : Erosion , Drought , and Desertification . <p> The Literature competition , on the other hand , will focus on Children 's Literature . The prize , which honours the author of the best book by a Nigerian rotates among four literary genres , namely prose fiction , poetry , drama and Children 's Literature . The Literary Criticism Prize , which also aims to promote Nigerian Literature , will receive entries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ critical essays on new writings in Nigerian Literature . <p> The call for entries for the Literature prize and Literary Criticism opened on February 15 , 2019 , and will close on April 5 , 2019 , while that of the science prize also opened on February 15 , 2019 , but will close on May 3 , 2019 . <p> For this year 's Literature and the Literary Criticism competition , Professor Obododinma Oha will chair the panel of judges . Oha , a poet , editor , and translator , is a professor of Semiotics , Stylistics , and Creative Writing at the Department of English , University of Ibadan . He also writes poems in English and Igbo . <p> Other members of the panel include Professor Asabe Usman Kabir , a professor of Oral and African Literature at Usmanu Danfodiyo University Sokoto ; and Dr. Patrick Okolo , a Senior Lecturer at the University of Lagos Nigeria with specialisation in African postcolonial literature , gender , and cultural studies . <p> The winners of the Literature and Literary Criticism prizes will be announced at an award @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the first LNG export from the NLNG 's Plant on October 9 , 1999 . The Science Prize winner , as usual , will be revealed earlier in the year . <p> Some of the more notable past winners of the prize include Soji Cole who clinched the Literature Prize award with his play , ' Embers ' ; Dr. Peter Ngene who was awarded the Science Prize , for his work in ' Innovation in Electric Power ' ; Professor Isidore Diala , a professor of African Literature at Imo State University , Owerri , who took home the Literary Criticism Prize and the late Ikeogu Oke ( 2017 , Poetry ) with ' The Heresiad ' . <p> The Nigeria Prize for Literature and The Nigeria Prize for Science are part of Nigeria LNG Limited 's numerous contributions towards building a better Nigeria . <p> Yemi Adebisi is the Life Editor , Independent Newspapers .
