<p> The establishment of a world class agricultural research outfit , Centre of Excellence in Agriculture and Sustainable Development , CEADESE , at the Federal University of Abeokuta , FUNAAB , is primarily to combat the dangers posed by climate change and strengthen human and material capacity for agricultural development . <p> This was the submission of the director of the Centre , Professor Olukayode Akinyemi while speaking with newsmen while conducting them round a new set of equipment at the centre headquarters in Abeokuta . <p> According to the university don , the Centre since it 's establishment in 2013 as one of the two of its kind on the continent , has been striving to ensure excellence in Agricultural research and quality student output remained on the front burner of the World Bank facility . <p> Akinyemi , a professor of Applied Physics further explained to correspondents that , " the capacity building programme of CEADESE involves introducing new postgraduate curricula leading to Master and Doctoral Degrees in Agricultural Development and Sustainable Environment ( M.AgSE/PhD.AgSE ) , in response to specific productivity challenges , short-term skill acquisition for industry stakeholders , specialized workshops and to introduce internships @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Akinyemi stressed the importance of partnership and collaboration with relevant bodies both locally and internationally . In this regard , CEADESE has agreements of partnership with a number of institutions including International Institute of Tropical Agriculture , National Centre for Agricultural Mechanisation , Aborny Calavy , INRAM , Cotonou , Benin Republic and CERSA , University of Lome , Togo . <p> Akinyemi revealed that due to the continental outlook of CEADESE international students are taking advantage of the opportunity to acquire innovative research experience that will ultimately lead to sustainable agricultural practices to combat the menace of climate change and improve agricultural output in Africa . <p> This he maintained is bringing back the good old days of international students getting quality education in Nigerian university saying that would go a long way in changing the perspective and often stereotype nagative classification of the Nigerian educational system . <p> He said , " the research facility is not for Nigeria and Nigerians alone .
