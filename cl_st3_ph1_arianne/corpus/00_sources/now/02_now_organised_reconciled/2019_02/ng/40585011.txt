<h> Experts Berate Apathy Towards Wetlands In Nigeria <p> Lagos -- Some environmental experts have frowned at the level of apathy displayed by various stakeholders , including the government on the preservation of wetlands in the country . <p> February 2 , is the United Nations designated date for the celebration of World Wetland Day . It is a day that is set-aside for people all over the world to reflect on how best they can harness the values of wetlands in mitigating global warming . <p> As is the tradition of choosing a topic for each year , the theme for the 2019 wetlands day is ' Wetlands and Climate Change ' . The topic raises awareness on the importance of wetlands ( swamps , marshes , mangroves , estuaries ) in coping and mitigating with climate change . <p> Though wetlands are known to be the carbon sink of the world in the sense that it absorbs the carbon components in the environment and creates a balance in the atmospheric condition , majority of the wetlands in Nigeria , just like a good part of its ecosystem , is threatened by human activities and negligence on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some of Nigeria 's coastlines , one will begin to wonder if those coastlines are not a liability and a curse to its surrounding environment , all due to years of negligence and recklessness and on the part of its inhabitants . <p> Michael Uwagbae of Wetlands International ( Port Harcourt ) remarked that in Tanzania , Senegal and Kenya , the wetlands have been utilized to these countries advantage unlike in Nigeria where there is limited awareness of what wetlands does coupled with the fact that government is not doing enough . <p> Bayelsa , he said , has the largest mangrove wetland in Nigeria , with almost 80 per cent of it domiciled in the state , yet not much is being done about it . Besides , being turned to a tourist 's attraction site , Uwagbae said that there are several types of marine lives that breed and survive only in the mangroves such as periwinkles and various species of fishes . <p> " Apart from the marine lives , neglecting the wetlands exposes an environment to the effects of climate change because there will be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Most of the fish in the marine come to the estuaries ( the point where the fresh water meets the salts water ) to breed . Without wetlands , you can not have a proper recycling of the marine lives . " <p> Achike Chude , an environmentalist , though acknowledging that wetlands in well organized societies are huge source of income , said that if critical issues like education are being neglected by the government , that it is not wetlands that they will be interested in . <p> Chude explained that there are wetlands in various places in Nigeria , especially in the Niger Delta , but that a good number of them are polluted by environmental degradation . He then advised that it is incumbent on government to ensure that the wetlands are protected for the singular purpose of preserving the ecosystem . <p> On his part , Babatunde Durosinmi-Etti , Lagos State Commissioner for the Environment , called on Lagosians to view wetlands protection as a veritable solution to climate change and a means for conserving the environment for posterity . <p> " The coastal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enormous wetlands resources that can be relied upon to conserve nature and fight climate change , has for so long contended with the pressure of development which inexorably drained this vital natural gift ' . <p> " Lagos , like other coastal states , is susceptible to the effects of climate change such as sea level rise , choral bleaching and change in water temperature while its wetlands play important roles in coping with these challenges by being useful in absorbing and storing carbon , reducing floods , relieving droughts , reducing storm surges and protecting coastlines .
