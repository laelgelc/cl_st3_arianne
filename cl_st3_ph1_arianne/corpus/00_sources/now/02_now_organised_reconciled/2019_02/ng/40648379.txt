<h> Access Bank to issue corporate green bond in Africa <p> Access Bank Plc says it is set to issue the first corporate green bond in Africa . <p> The bank said the Securities and Exchange Commission had given it approval for its book build . <p> In a statement on Thursday , it said the book build which commenced on Thursday would be concluded in exactly one week , after which the funding of commitment would begin on Friday , March 1 , 2019 . <p> The bank said , " The bond , a five-year fixed rate senior unsecured green bond of up to N15bn is the first ever climate bonds standard certified corporate green bond to be issued in Africa . It has been awarded a B2 rating by Moody 's and verified by PwC ( UK ) . " <p> It added that this followed the certification by the Climate Bond Initiative as having met the Climate Bond Standards . <p> The statement added that the management of Access Bank had in anticipation of the approval , launched the Nigerian Green Bond Market Development Programme in June 2018 , in partnership with FMDQ OTC Securities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ approval , the Group Managing Director , Herbert Wigwe , described it as a step in the right direction adding that it lent credence to the process and gave hope of a favourable outcome . <p> According to Wigwe , the final approval of the green bond would mean another feat that was commendable and spoke to the vision of being a key player in the country 's finance sector . <p> He stated , " With over a decade 's experience leading sustainability in the Nigerian financial sector , we believe that the issuance of this bond will create a path to financing Nigeria 's climate change objectives and also unlock the country 's economic growth potential .
