<h> Methyl bromide : Ignore NAFDAC 's warning , NAQS tells farmers <p> Farmers and exporters who rely on Methyl bromide for the control of certain agricultural pests should ignore the recent warning of the National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control on the use of the agro-chemical . <p> According to the Nigeria Agricultural Quarantine Service , the use of Methyl bromide for crop pest control is still permissible in Nigeria . <p> In a document made available to our correspondent in Abuja on Tuesday by the Head of Media , Communications and Strategies , NAQS , Dr Chigozie Nwodo , the agency provided explanations on why the agro-chemical was still permissible . <p> NAQS is the statutory agricultural quarantine authority in Nigeria with the mandate to promote and regulate sanitary and phytosanitary measures in order to minimise the risk to the agricultural economy , food safety and the environment . <p> The agency argued that it was within the sole purview of NAQS to make pronouncements on the permissibility or otherwise of the use of Methyl bromide and other agro-chemicals for the phytosanitary treatment of agro-commodities . <p> It said , " The attention of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the effect that the agro-chemical , Methyl bromide , has been phased out and its use in Nigeria is now prohibited because of the impact of the substance on climate change , particularly the depletion of the ozone layer . <p> " The said announcement has caused concern and panic among farmers and exporters who rely on Methyl bromide as a potent option for the control of certain agricultural pests . " <p> The NAQS added , " In the interest of clarity , we wish to state that Methyl bromide is a widely used gas fumigant for the control of insect pests , termites , rodents , weeds and some soil-borne diseases . <p> " Whereas the use of Methyl bromide is known to have some deleterious effect on the environment , it is acceptable for controlled quarantine use because of the lack of comparably efficacious alternatives .
