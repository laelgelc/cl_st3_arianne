<p> EDINNBURGH , UK -- 24th March 2018 : Edinburgh Castle and landmarks across Scotland switch off their lights in support of Earth Hour . Hundreds of millions of people around the planet will be taking part in WWF 's Earth Hour as a symbolic show of solidarity for the planet . Pictured animal silhouettes stand at Edinburgh Castle to symbolise that climate change is effecting wildlife across the globe .
