Hammed Ali ( Rtd ) . <p> Asaolu said the signing of the Free Trade Agreement would place Nigeria on a higher pedestal to negotiate her position properly in the Continent . <p> He said ECOSOCC was ready to work with all stakeholders in the economy to ensure that Nigeria takes advantage of the agreement to boost trade in the region . <p> He pointed out that the organisation would embark on advocacy visits to strategic stakeholders to alley their fear on the consequences of signing the agreement , adding that Nigeria remains one of the biggest economies in Africa . <p> According to him , " As it is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ three more countries append their signature , it becomes mandatory for Nigeria to key in as it will become effective once 22 countries signed , then the door of negotiation will be closed against Nigeria . <p> " Now we can sign the agreement and join in the negotiation to protect our interest , or else Nigeria risk being participants only . <p> " Now , Intra-African trade is low , just 14.2% .
