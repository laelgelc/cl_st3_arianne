The company states that it considers education as a key development factor with unique potentials to stimulating development and growth . <p> Under its Corporate Social Responsibility , ( CSR ) , the company administers a scholarship programme that covers post primary , undergraduate , overseas postgraduate and maritime studies . The post primary scholarship supports very high performing basic pupils in host communities to gain access to tip of the rage education in top rate secondary schools in Nigeria . <p> On the other hand , over N500 million has been spent on undergraduate scholarship for Nigerian students in various universities .
