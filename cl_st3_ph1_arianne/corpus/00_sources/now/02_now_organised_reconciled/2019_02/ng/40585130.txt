<h> Transport Biggest Polluter As UK Greenhouse Gases Fall <p> Transport was the largest sector for UK greenhouse gas pollution in 2017 , as emissions driving climate change fell 3% overall , official statistics show . <p> Greenhouse gases were down 3% on 2016 to 460 million tonnes , and the main pollutant , carbon dioxide , was also down 3% , to 373 million tonnes in 2017 , the most recent year for which figures are available . <p> The UK 's domestic greenhouse gas emissions have fallen by more than two-fifths ( 42% ) since 1990 and carbon dioxide is down 37% over the same period , according to the data from the Business Department ( Beis ) . But transport emissions have fallen just 2% since 1990 and now account for more than a quarter ( 27% ) of the total greenhouse gas output -- a bigger share than energy supplies , businesses , homes , agriculture or other sectors . <p> Pollution from energy supplies , which was the largest emitting sector until 2016 when it was overtaken by transport , has dropped 60% since 1990 . <p> The sector recorded a fall of 8% @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ coal and towards renewables and now accounts for just under a quarter of emissions ( 24% ) . <p> Much of the pollution from transport , which includes road , railways , domestic aviation , shipping , fishing and aircraft support vehicles , is from road traffic , in particular passenger cars . <p> The Government has outlined plans to phase out the sale of new conventional petrol and diesel cars and vans by 2040 , as part of efforts to curb urban air pollution and tackle emissions of the gases which drive up global temperatures . <p> UK greenhouse gas emissions in 2017 were 42% below their 1990 level .
