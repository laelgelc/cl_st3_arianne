<p> The National Association for Scrap Artisans and Craft said it had trained over 3,000 youth in entrepreneurship and vocational skills in Kaduna . <p> Its Chairman , Alhaji Yahaya Sarki made the disclosure in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) on Friday in Kaduna . <p> Sarki said " our mission is to see that young graduates and those that did not go to school or even delinquents are trained to be able to engage in decent living , so that they can be off the street . <p> " So far , we have trained over 3000 youths and they are spread all over Nigeria doing the best they can to earn a living , " he said . <p> According to him , the training was mostly in fabrication , so that those trained are able to fabricate cooking utensils , rakes , cooking stoves , fish driers , ovens and the like . <p> He added that some of the beneficiaries were also trained as auto mechanics , welders and vulcanisers . <p> Sarki disclosed that the organisation is working with Kaduna State Forestry Ministry , to train some of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protection . <p> The chairman added that the organization is trying to promote the use of coal , to reduce the indiscriminate felling of trees which exposed the environment to desertification and negative impacts of climate change . <p> He said that they have met with officials of the UNDP and the state environment ministry on how to promote the campaign . <p> Sarki also said that the group had signed a memorandum of understanding with Kaduna State University and Federal Ministry of Works on training of youths under it .
