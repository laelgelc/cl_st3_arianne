<h> FAO warns food supply threatened by declining biodiversity <p> In a report , the first of its kind by the Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) , it said there was " mounting evidence that the biodiversity that underpins our food systems , at all levels , is declining around the world . " <p> That is putting food production and the environment " under severe threat , " the FAO warned . <p> " Once lost , plant , animal and micro-organisms species that are critical to our food systems , can not be recovered . " <p> Biodiversity enables agriculture systems to be more resilient to shocks such as disease and pest outbreaks , as well as coping with climate change . <p> The report cited as examples the dramatic fall in food production from infestations such as the potato blight in Ireland in the 1840s and the losses of the tropical taro plant in Samoa in the 1990s . <p> The FAO says the drivers for biodiversity loss include changes in land and water use and management , pollution and overharvesting . <p> Geographically , Latin America and the Caribbean , rich in biodiversity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ species , such as crustaceans , fish and insects . <p> The Rome-based agency says countries are adopting biodiversity-friendly practices from sustainable forest management to an ecosystem approach to fisheries and organic agriculture -- but more needs to be done .
