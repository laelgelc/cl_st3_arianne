<h> Corporate Green Bond : Access Bank to Issue First in Africa <p> Access Bank Plc has announced it will issue the first corporate green bond in Africa . <p> The lender said the Securities and Exchange Commission had given it approval for its bookbuild . <p> In a statement released on Thursday , the bank said the bookbuild which commenced on Thursday would be concluded in exactly one week , after which the funding of commitment would begin on March 1 , 2019 . <p> " The bond , a five-year fixed rate senior unsecured green bond of up to N15bn is the first ever climate bonds standard certified corporate green bond to be issued in Africa . It has been awarded a B2 rating by Moody 's and verified by PwC ( UK ) , " the bank said . <p> Reacting to the approval , Herbert Wigwe , the Group Managing Director , said it is a step in the right direction , saying it lent credence to the process . <p> He stated , " With over a decade 's experience leading sustainability in the Nigerian financial sector , we believe that the issuance of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change objectives and also unlock the country 's economic growth potential .
