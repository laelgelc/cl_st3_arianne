<h> What is best practice for on-farm waste management ? <p> Waste management has become an increasingly important issue in farming throughout Ireland . <p> Everyday issues encountered by farmers include the disposal of waste generated from routine practices to dealing with consequences from the unauthorised activities of others and the increasing blight of illegal dumping . <p> It is important for everyone to understand their responsibilities in respect of correct waste management practices in order to protect the environment , and to protect themselves from potential prosecution . <h> Illegal Dumping <p> Construction and Demolition ( C&amp;D ) waste is waste generated from construction and demolition activities ( including excavated soil from contaminated sites ) .
