<h> Innovation paves way for next stage of global energy transformation <p> Countries at the forefront of the energy transformation are getting more than a third of their energy from variable renewables like solar and wind , and they 're doing it in a cost-effective manner . By making use of innovative solutions that allow to integrate a higher share of renewables into power systems , innovation holds the key to a cost-effective global energy transformation . <p> These findings come from a first-of-a-kind mapping and analysis of innovations that will transform the power sector , launched by the International Renewable Energy Agency ( IRENA ) today in Brussels . By then , variable renewables would account for 60% of the total power generated globally . Moving to a new phase where the massive but cost-effective scale-up of renewables power is crucial , the power sector transformation is strongly accelerated by innovation trends in digitalization , decentralization and electrification of the end-use sectors . Understanding and learning from the experiences from leading countries in VRE integration is crucial to replicate and enhance innovation that can accelerate this transformation . <p> With close to 15% of VRE share in annual electricity generation today , the EU has the highest levels of variable renewables in power systems globally .
