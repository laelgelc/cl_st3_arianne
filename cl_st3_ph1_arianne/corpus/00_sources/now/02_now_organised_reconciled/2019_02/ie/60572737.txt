<h> Dear parents of planet earth ... <p> Lorna Gold calls for parents to get behind our young people as they continue to strike on Fridays for a climate-proofed future <p> TO : Parents of Planet Earth <p> SUBJECT : Climate Strike <p> Dear fellow parents , <p> Over the past few weeks we have witnessed an unprecedented rising of our children across the world . They have skipped school on Fridays to protest about climate change , following the example of a Swedish teenager , Greta Thunberg . <p> Their example has drawn both praise -- especially from scientists -- and criticism . For many people their action may seem quite bewildering . Why are they taking such extreme action ? Are they being unduly politically influenced ? Should we be concerned ? <p> I shared many of these questions until a few years ago I had a rude awakening to the urgency of climate change and what it means for our children . Like these children on the streets , I became anguished by the silence and the fear to face this mammoth challenge facing us all . <p> Like these children , I have studied the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ clear : we are out of time . Not " nearly " out of time -- but actually out of time . <p> Our planet is already warming fast and the next generation will be in serious trouble . Unless we take radical action and change our ways , we risk bequeathing them an unlivable planet . Surely none of us want that ? <p> This realization , which shook me to my very foundations , led me to give up my full-time job and to focus on waking people up to this stark reality . I wrote a book about my experiences , Climate Generation , with a wish that others could understand what the scientists are telling us . <p> I wanted to share my story with other parents -- not as some kind of guilt trip , but as a sincere plea to wake up and take our kids future seriously . I shared my concerns on the Ryan Tubridy Show . <p> " We realise that we need each other . That we are all the climate generation now ... " <p> I also started to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for change . Last December a friend who read my book , Jim , introduced me ( on social media ) to Greta Thunberg . <p> Together Jim and I started following her . She was striking for climate action . Many school children across Australia , and then the world , started to follow her example . <p> Then , in December , Greta made her speech to the UN climate talks in Poland . In one minute she managed to capture the sheer urgency of what the world is facing -- and put a call out to everyone to raise their voices by coming out on Fridays . <p> That following Friday , Jim and I decided we would answer her call . It seemed the most natural thing in the world to go on climate strike . We did n't think for a minute we were " too old " . <p> As parents who care we wanted to be the answer to that call . For me , with young boys , I felt it only right that I should stand on their behalf . After @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and my generation that has caused this mess . <p> Sure , my boys will join when they can , but why should they stand in the cold , miss school and shoulder such a heavy burden ? When they ca n't , I will . <p> It is a wonderful eclectic mix of humanity . We greet each other in a spirit of inter-generational embrace . We realise that we need each other . That we are all the climate generation now . <p> So my call to ALL parents , grandparents , aunts and uncles -- and everyone else in the young at heart group -- is to get behind our young people , especially our youngest humans . <p> They need us now more than ever . There is a golden moment now to show you care and you are sorry for what our generation has done . It does n't take much -- perhaps one hour each Friday : stand in a public place with your placard . <p> Post on social media and log it on www.fridaysforfuture.org map . And watch it grow .
