Their weight gain and performance has impressed me . <p> We are told that lighter , more efficient cows will be needed to combat climate change and , from what we have seen of the Stabilisers to date , we hope that they will fit the bill . <p> A lot of our suckler cows are just too big , with a mature weight of 750-800kg .
