<p> The class boycott , led by young people , is part of a global " #FridaysForFuture " school strike campaign . It encourages pupils to skip school in order to urge governments to take action against climate change . <p> Students are set to march to government headquarters on March 15 from Chater Garden , after police told them that their initial chosen location of Victoria Park was unavailable . <p> Having to ' fight the adults ' <p> Greta Thunberg , the 16-year-old Swedish environmental activist who sparked the global student movement , responded to the news of the Hong Kong class boycott on Thursday . She tweeted : " We fight for our future in many places and in many ways . It does n't help if we have to fight the adults too , " in response to a story about the Hong Kong Education Bureau warning students against the strike . <p> Over 175 people had clicked " attending " on the Facebook event by Friday morning , with almost 1,000 more clicking " interested .
