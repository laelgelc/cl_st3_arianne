<p> According to data from NASA , 2018 recorded the fourth highest global average surface temperature . It is expected that 2019 will be the second warmest year on record . In the first two programmes of this year 's Swire Classic Insights , the Hong Kong Philharmonic Orchestra ( HK Phil ) cordially invites you to share our concerns about Global Warming through music on 1 March ( Fri ) and 2 March ( Sat ) in the Grand Hall , Lee Shau Kee Lecture Centre , Centennial Campus , The University of Hong Kong . <p> In celebration of the 75th birthday of Michael Nyman , the HK Phil dedicates the last programme of this year 's Swire Classic Insights to a man who is one of Britain 's most innovative and celebrated composers .
