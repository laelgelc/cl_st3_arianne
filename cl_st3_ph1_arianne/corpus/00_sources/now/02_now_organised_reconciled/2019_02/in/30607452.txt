<h> Forest department tables its GOs after two years <p> HYDERABAD : The Telangana government has been talking tough on forest smugglers and encroachments , but ironically forgot to get approval of the assembly on the amendment to the Forest Rules issued twoand-half-years ago . Now , the department has placed three GOs ( No. 55 , 31 and 10 issued in September 2016 , September 2017 and February 2018 respectively ) before the assembly and council for approval two days after the validity of the GOs was questioned by the Federation of Telangana Timber Merchants , Saw Millers and Allied Industries . <p> The GO No. 55 , Telangana Wood Based Industries ( Regulation ) , Rules 2016 , repealing the TS Saw Mill ( Regulation ) Rules , 1969 was issued with an intention to curb indiscriminate tree felling and sale of timber in the forest areas of the state . But the environment , forests , science and technology ( EFS&amp;T ) department did not bother to send them to the assembly for approval . Realising the mistake , the special chief secretary wrote a letter to secretary of the Legislature Secretariat on November 27 , 2018 , informing that a case was filed by timber association and that need to be approved by both assembly and council on priority basis soon after formation of the new government .
