The area is rich in forestland and most of the coal blocks are in moderately to densely forested areas . Given the ecology , concerns of biodiversity , and wildlife management , coal mining in this area has been controversial for years . <p> However , in the past the Union environment ministry had granted forest clearance ( FC ) to coal mines in the area , such as the PEKB and Tara mines . This was in fact done going against observations of the FAC that the area is too sensitive for mining .
