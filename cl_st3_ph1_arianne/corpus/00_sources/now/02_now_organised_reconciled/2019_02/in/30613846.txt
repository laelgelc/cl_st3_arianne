<h> PLPA bill : Punjab ' not to follow ' Haryana <p> CHANDIGARH : The speculations that passage of Punjab Land Preservation ( Haryana Amendment ) Bill 2019 passed by Haryana government on Wednesday will put pressure on Punjab to amend PLP Act on its land as well , has been rejected by prominent people from different walks of life . <p> Head of forest force in Punjab , Jatinder Sharma said , " All credit goes to Punjab CM Capt Amrinder Singh for issuance of PLP notification in respect of 15 villages falling in Punjab and situated on the periphery of Chandigarh last year . The bureaucrats have to comply the orders of the government . So it all depends on the government 's decision making . In case of Punjab , CM has been quite perceptive to the forest and wildlife conservation , well being of people , maintaining its groundwater table and pollution combating . " <p> The notification was issued last year under section 4 of the PLPA 1900 covering around 21 , 944 acre in 15 villages . These villages- Parol , Majrian , Seonk , Parchh , Nada , Karoran , Tarapur , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Dulwan , Siswan and Choti Bari Nagal- fall in ecologically sensitive area of the Shivalik foothills on the Chandigarh periphery . The term of 15-year validity of notification had come to an end on February 2 last year . <p> The decision of extension of notification was taken on the report of Soil and Land Use Survey of India and Indian Institute of Soil and Water Conservation . Both these institutes under union ministry of agriculture had termed the soil erosion risk in these villages as ' very high ' after conducting an extensive study and consultation with locals and experts , and recommended to keep the villages under forest cover to protect them from further degradation . <p> Kanwar Sandhu , MLA under whose jurisdiction these villages fall said that he was unaware about the contents of Haryana amendment bill but acknowledged that Punjab had very small portion of forest under its territory which need to be protected . <p> " Punjab also complied with the Supreme Court order , of December 12 , 1996 and July 6 , 2011 known as Lafarge case directing all the states and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ whether they stand degraded , denuded and cleared .
