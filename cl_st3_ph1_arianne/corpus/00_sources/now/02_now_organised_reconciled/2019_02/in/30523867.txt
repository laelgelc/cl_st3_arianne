A direct conversation with girls across all 30 Indian states and six major cities , the survey sought to understand their everyday realities and aspirations . There are about 80 million teenage girls in India today , and most of them are going to be first-time voters this year . An all-women team of 1,000 surveyors visited a representative sample of 74,000 teenage girls in their homes , in over 600 districts across all 30 states of India .
