<p> New Delhi , Feb 11 ( UNI ) Considering the urgent need of sharing the agro-meteorological knowledge among researchers , policy makers , industry and farmers to learn from each other , identify the technologies or practices and develop a road map for risk management , a three day international workshop began here on Monday . <p> This is being organised by the ' Association of Agro meteorologists ' ( AAM ) with a focus on " Advances in Agro meteorology for Managing Climatic Risks of Farmers " ( INAGMET-2019 ) , is jointly organised by India Meteorological Department , Indian Centre of Agricultural Research ( ICAR ) , Indian Agricultural Research Institute and Jawaharlal Nehru University . <p> Inaugurating the symposium Earth Sciences Ministry Secretary Dr Madhavan N Rajeevan said that the symposium would bring together the global experience on the advances in weather and climate predictions , understanding the impacts of climate change and variability on agriculture and sharing and cataloguing the experiences of global meteorological and agricultural communities on effective interventions and promising technologies for adaptation and mitigation as well as identifying areas for future research and collaboration as per the requirements of farming community @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weather and climate services for agriculture , monsoon variability and predictability , climate variability and change , agro meteorological information and geospatial decision support systems , risk transfer with weather and crop insurance and other related issues . <p> INAGMET-2019 is expected to provide a platform for interactions among the professionals , scientists , policy makers and other social groups to share knowledge and experience on various aspects related to thrust areas of agro meteorology . <p> " Around 450 delegates in different field of Agriculture especially Agro meteorology from nearly 100 institutions across the country and also from abroad are participating " , the Ministry release said . <p> While the weather and agricultural scientists would roll out the recent advances in their respective fields , session for the Industry will be special
