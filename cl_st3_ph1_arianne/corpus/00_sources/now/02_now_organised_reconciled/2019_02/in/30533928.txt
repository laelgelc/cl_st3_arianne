<h> Forest dept sends eco-zone proposals to Centre <p> Ranchi : The state forest department on Tuesday sent draft proposals to ministry of environment , forest and climate change for declaring an eco-sensitive zone around four protected wildlife abodes -- Palkot sanctuary ( Gumla ) , Lavalong sanctuary ( Chatra ) , Gautam Buddha sanctuary ( Koderma-Hazaribagh-Gaya ) and Udhawa bird sanctuary ( Sahebganj ) . This will end all non-forestry activities detrimental to the health of these places once it comes into force . Principal chief conservator of forests ( PCCF-wildlife ) P K Verma told TOI that similar draft proposals for remaining places such as Palamau Tiger Reserve , Topchanchi wildlife sanctuary ( Dhanbad ) , Parasnath wildlife sanctuary ( Giridih ) among others are pending with the state government level for its consideration . <p> " We have proposed an area up to 5km around these places to be declared as eco-zone . A similar format is being adopted for remaining sanctuaries too , " said Verma , adding that recently the state government finally gave its approval for sending eco-zone proposals after which they started acting in this regard . <p> Notably , similar proposals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ could n't get through due to lack of clarity and consensus between different stakeholders . " A couple of years back , similar proposals for a few sanctuaries were sent , but there were several loopholes in the draft plans and the Centre returned them raising several objections . Thereafter , things kept lingering , " said another departmental source . <p> Verma however hoped that they will succeed in getting eco-zones declared around all the sanctuaries this time . " I do n't think there should be any issue as the four sanctuaries as we have followed due process . We had also made our draft notifications public in the recent past to seek objections , if any , but we received none . So , the state government finally gave its approval to send the proposal to Union government , " said Verma . <p> Jharkhand has 12 sanctuaries and protected parks .
