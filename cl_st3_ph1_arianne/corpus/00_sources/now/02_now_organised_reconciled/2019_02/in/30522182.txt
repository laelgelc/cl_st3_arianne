<p> The students took part in the rally enthusiastically by raising slogans along the way urging the people to preserve the natural resources . <p> Warangal Rural : More than 90 people including nature lovers and members of the Nature Club of the National Institute of Technology ( NIT ) Warangal participated in a cycle rally from Hanamkonda to the Pakhal Lake on Saturday . The Forest Department has organised the rally on the occasion of world wetlands day . <p> Chief Conservator of Forests ( CCF ) , Warangal , MJ Akbar , who flagged off the rally near the Warangal Collector 's office in Hanamkonda said the people should have knowledge of global warming , climate change , and its consequences . " With a view to protect and raise the forest , the State government is taking several steps including the execution of the Telanganaku Haritha Haram ( THH ) programme , " he said and appreciated the students for taking part in the awareness rally . District Forest Officer ( DFO ) K Purushotham said that the awareness rallies like this would awaken the people to the fact that several dark forces had become detrimental @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ duty to conserve the environment against such forces , " he added . <p> The students took part in the rally enthusiastically by raising slogans along the way urging the people to preserve the natural resources . The group reached Pakhal in the afternoon following which they were addressed by a former professor of Kakatiya University ( KU ) Vatsayana Raju who explained to them what exactly are wetlands , where they are found and how they are part of the ecology . <p> " A wetland is a distinct ecosystem that is inundated by water , either permanently or seasonally , where oxygen-free processes prevail . The unique hydric soil in the wetlands gives birth to characteristic vegetation of aquatic plants , " he added . The students were taken on a walk to the wetland part of the Pakhal Lake . They were shown the various species of trees in the forest and their uses .
