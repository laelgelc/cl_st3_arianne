<h> Death in a deccan trap ? <p> It was not the first extinction that Earth witnessed . Mass extinctions have been replayed five times on the planet . Yet , the event that removed ( non-avian ) dinosaurs 66 million years ago is the one that has generated most public interest . Despite tall scientific claims and preciseness of geological data , what triggered the cataclysmic event is a matter of informed conjecture . <p> The dominant hypothesis is that a large asteroid or comet hit Earth near the Chicxulub peninsula in Mexico around 66 million years ago resulting in a sudden climate change that lasted long enough to end the dinosaurs ' long reign on the planet . The ' Chicxulub event ' is certainly the most well studied and has generated hundreds of scientific papers , a Hollywood blockbuster and appears as T-shirt art . The notion that there is more to that extinction story than what meets the proverbial eye has largely been erased from the public imagination . Until last week ... <p> ... when two scientific studies rekindled the ' extinction debate ' . Or , was it the double impact of a death asteroid comet andDeccan trap volcanic eruptions that created an inhabitable home for dinosaurs et al ? <p> Studying events that ravaged the planet millions of years ago may appear to be dodgy science . Some have , in the past wrongly likened it to astrology . Yet , the area of earth sciences is one of the most advanced in science . Which is why despite having different conclusions on the same event , both papers generated so much interest among the scientific community . As has been the case many times before , this time , too , the two apparently contradictory outcomes can been reconciled to arrive at a plausible theory via new data sets . <p> The two papers -- ' U-Pb constraints on pulsed eruption of the Deccan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ eruptive tempo of Deccan volcanism in relation to the Cretaceous-Paleogene boundary ' -- both published in latest February edition of Science , studied the radioactive markers in the ancient lava flows from around the area close to Mahabaleshwar , Maharashtra . The data generated by the dating methods used by the two research groups implicated volcanic eruptions in the K-Pg mass extinctions in one of the cases , and placed the beginning of the eruptions nearly 60,000 years after the asteroid/comet impact in the other . <p> The Princeton University-Amravati University et al team used uranium-lead ( U-Pb ) dating to map a precise timeline of the Deccan Traps volcanic eruption . The results indicate that there had been four very-high-volume eruptions in the Deccan . Each of these eruptions lasted roughly 100,000 years , belching out large amounts of magma and climate-altering greenhouse gases into the environment . The results indicate that the onset of these mega eruptions began tens of thousands of years before the Chicxulub impact in Mexico . <p> According to the paper , " ... the amounts of methane , carbon dioxide and sulfur dioxide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have dramatically altered the planet 's climate and environments , triggering mass extinctions long before the asteroid 's arrival " . <p> The other University of California , Berkeley-Indian Institute of Technology Bombay et al team used a different technique -- argon-argon dating -- to study the eruptions . While less precise , the technique can address some of the limitations of U-Pb dating . This team concluded that over 75% of the Deccan Traps lava volumes were spewed out " nearly 600 thousand years after the K-Pg extinction event , which limits the responsibility of magmatism ( volcanic activity ) as a primary trigger of the extinction " . <p> The first study is a huge advance in the dating of the Deccan Traps eruptions . But science is not sure about what the volcanoes precisely did to the dinosaurs . Soon , more precise dates for the eruptions will help us understand their role in the K-Pg extinctions .
