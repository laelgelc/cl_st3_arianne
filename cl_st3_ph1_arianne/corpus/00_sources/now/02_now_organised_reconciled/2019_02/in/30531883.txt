Photo : Ashis Senapati <p> A three-year-old leopard died after getting caught in a trap laid by poachers to catch a wild boar in Badmal forest range in Odisha 's Bolangir district on February 5 , 2019 . <p> The leopard was stuck in the iron trap unattended for hours together and in the struggle to get out its left leg had been dismembered . <p> " The leopard had also sustained deep wounds in other parts of its body while trying to escape from the trap laid by poachers to catch wild boar , " said Rashmi Ranjan Nayak , divisional forest officer , Bolangir . " Forest officials have launched an operation against poachers . Also , an autopsy would be conducted and we have registered a case under provisions of the Wildlife Protection Act , " added Nayak . <p> This is the second incident of unnatural leopard death in Odisha within three weeks .
