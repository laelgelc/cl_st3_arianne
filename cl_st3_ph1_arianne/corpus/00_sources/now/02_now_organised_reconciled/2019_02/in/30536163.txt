More than one-third , or 36 per cent of the glaciers in Hindu Kush Himalaya ( HKH ) will disappear by the year 2100 , and that 's if global warming continues at the current rate of 1.5 degrees Celsius . But it 's more likely that carbon emissions will increase and Earth will warm faster , and if that happens , two-thirds of the area will be in danger . These findings are part of the Hindu Kush Himalaya Assessment , released on Monday . The report took five years to research and publish , and involved more than 350 researchers and policy experts , 185 organisations , 210 authors , 20 review editors and 125 external reviewers . <h> What is the Hindu Kush Himalaya region ? <p> Hindu Kush Himalaya ( HKH ) is an area of over 4.2 million sq km across eight countries . It spans parts of Afghanistan , Pakistan , China , Tibet , Myanmar , Bangladesh , all of Bhutan and Nepal . In India , it includes the entire territory of 11 states -- Jammu &amp; Kashmir , Himachal Pradesh , Assam , Arunachal Pradesh , Sikkim , Uttarakhand , Manipur , Meghalaya , Mizoram , Nagaland and Tripura , as well as Darjeeling and Kalimpong in West Bengal . It includes mountains such as Everest , K2 , Machhapuchhre and hundreds of six-thousanders beloved by mountaineers . <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ question of losing your favourite winter trekking route . Together with China 's Tien Shan mountains , HKH is called the third pole , as it holds the highest amount of ice outside the North and South Poles . This ice is crucial for the 240 million people who live in HKH , as water from these glaciers feeds some of the most important rivers that originate from these mountains , including the Ganga , Brahmaputra , Indus , Irrawaddy , Yangtze , Mekong , Amu Darya and more . As the glaciers melt , the water levels will rise , which could initially lead to floods . Eventually , though , the water will simply dry up , leaving hundreds of millions drought-stricken and severely impacting all of these countries ' agricultural output . Also , a decrease in glacial cover will mean rising sea levels , threatening the habitat of a number of animals and wiping out the biodiversity hotspots that the mountains are famous for . <h> Women bear the brunt <p> The report also highlights how gender inequity poses a threat to HKH .
