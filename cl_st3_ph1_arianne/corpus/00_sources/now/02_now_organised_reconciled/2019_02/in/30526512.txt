<h> Stop pigging out and save the planet , Chinese told ahead of Lunar New Year <p> KUALA LUMPUR , Feb 4 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - As Chinese people celebrate the new Year of the Pig this week , environmental campaigners are urging them to eat less pork and help save the planet . <p> China consumes more meat than any other country and accounts for half the world 's consumption of pork , which is used in everything from dumplings and stir-fries to hotpots . <p> That has helped make it the world 's biggest emitter of climate-warming greenhouse gases - according to the U.N . Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) , livestock are responsible for about 14.5 percent of global emissions . <p> " Chinese emissions can be reduced by almost 10 percent in the next decade if Chinese people just ate half as much meat , " said Jen Leung , China climate director at the U.S.-based charity WildAid . <p> " So just try eating a little less pork in honour of a healthy Year of the Pig , " she told the Thomson Reuters Foundation . <p> Twelve animals make @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ transition from year of the dog to year of the pig . <p> The week-long holiday starts on starts on Monday , the eve of the new year , and is the most important in the Chinese calendar , when millions of people travel home . <p> Many restaurants have special menus to mark the festival , which is celebrated by Chinese communities around the world . <p> In Hong Kong , famous for its dim sum and barbeque pork , one company is partnering with restaurants to promote Lunar New Year dishes cooked with its " vegan pork " made with ingredients including soybeans and peas . <p> " Traditional belief dictates that we should not eat the same animal during their year in order to bring good luck -
