<p> Kollam : The Vice President of India , Shri M. Venkaiah Naidu has said that the time has come for media bodies to come out with a code of conduct for journalists in view of the critical role journalism plays in protecting democracy and in serving the larger good of the society . <p> Addressing the gathering after inaugurating the Golden Jubilee celebrations of Kollam Press Club , in Kollam , Kerala today , he observed that instead of giving expression to popular feeling , the newspapers these days were giving expression to coloured and partisan views . <p> The Vice President said that Journalists must never shirk from the core principles of journalism like independence and objectivity and shun unhealthy practices such as sensationalism . <p> Shri Naidu advocated for prescribing a minimum educational qualification for aspiring journalists and said that it was absolutely necessary to ensure that the standards and ethics of journalism are maintained and not compromised .
