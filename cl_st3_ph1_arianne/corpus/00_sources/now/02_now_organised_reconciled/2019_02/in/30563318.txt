<h> NSCCC approves project for rejuvenation of Papum-Poma River <p> NEW DELHI , Feb 13 : The 14th meeting of the National Steering Committee on Climate Change ( NSCCC ) was held on 7 February last here at the Kaveri Meeting Hall , Indira Paryavaran Bhawan , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change under the chair of Secretary EF&amp;CC and Chairman NSCCC , CK Mishra . Representative from various states and union territories of the country took part in the meeting and deliberations were made on the presentations made by them . The Arunachal Pradesh government also presented the project titled ' Addressing Climate Change Vulnerability of Papum-Poma River ' at a proposed cost of Rs 24.70 crore for three years , which was approved by the national steering committee . The state was represented by PCCF ( Env &amp; CC ) AK Shukla and Deputy Director ( Environment &amp; CC ) and Project Coordinator D Dohu Robin .
