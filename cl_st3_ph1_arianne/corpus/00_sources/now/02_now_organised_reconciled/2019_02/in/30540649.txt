<h> Celebrating World Wetland Day <p> TNN Updated : Feb 14 , 2019 , 21:51 IST <h> Related Videos <p> Celebrating World Wetland Day ... <p> Around 500 students from various schools across Chandigarh visited Chhatbir Zoo and Sukhna Lake as a part of activities being planned to commemorate World Wetland Day on Thursday . While they had a learning session at zoo as they learnt more about animal species , a trip to Sukhna gave them an opportunity to explore the living way of migratory birds . The event was done by UT 's department of environment along with an NGO , Yuvsatta . At the zoo , environmentalist Kulbhushan Kanwar elaborated on the variety of animals , birds and reptiles there . The inquisitive students asked the experts about the life of animals . Later , they went to Sukhna Lake 's regulator end area and spotted birds , including bar-headed goose , ruddy shell duck , tufted duck , common coot , etc .
