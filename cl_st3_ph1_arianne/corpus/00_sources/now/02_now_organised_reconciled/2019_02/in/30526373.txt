<h> ' No. of scientists advising NGT reduced to 2 from 20 ' <p> CHANDIGARH : Environmental laws and policies in India had been diluted and there was no law on renewable energy so people still relied on the old Electricity Act , said Supreme Court advocate Sanjay Upadhyaya , who has fought several legal battles on these issues . Groundwater extraction had been made easy with payment of small fee for anybody by the National Green Tribunal in recent past , he claimed , adding that in place of a battery of 20 scientists and forest officials working as advisory group to the judges of NGT , only two scientists and foresters were doing all the work . <p> Upadhyaya was speaking on the concluding day of the 4th International Dialogue on Himalayan Ecology on Sunday . Participating environmentalists and experts expressed concern over deterioration of biodiversity , especially springs . They emphasized on the need of preparing an economic model to mitigate the impact of climate change . <p> Dialogue Highway managing trustee Devinder Sharma said , " Unless we have economic model for climate change , we will not be able to control global temperature . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the US is controlling the wealth of 36 trillion dollars . As per Niti Ayog , 5% of GDP goes in giving tax concession to corporates , " he said . <p> " Forests are cleared and diversified for commercial ventures like industries which create pollution and eliminate carbon sequestration .
