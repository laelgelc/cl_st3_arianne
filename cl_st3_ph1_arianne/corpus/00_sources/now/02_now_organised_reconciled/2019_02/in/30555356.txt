<h> International conference on dryland development begins in Jodhpur <p> TNN Updated : Feb 12 , 2019 , 12:11 IST <p> JODHPUR : With concerns over shrinking wetlands in the country , deliberations began on Monday to explore avenues for conversion of dryland areas from grey to green at the 13th International Conference on Dryland Development . Experts from across the globe are participating in this four-day conference which will go on till Thursday . Conference is being organized under the joint aegis of the International Dryland Development Commissioner and Arid Zone Research Association of India .
