<h> Chhattisgarh forest department expense avoidable : CAG <h> Last Updated : Monday 04 February 2019 <p> Representational photo . Credit : Getty Images <p> The Comptroller and Auditor General of India 's ( CAG ) Report No.4 of 2018 on the Revenue Sector , Government of Chhattisgarh dated January 10 , 2019 , found that the Chhattisgarh forest department incurred excess and avoidable expenditure on unirrigated mixed plantations set up under the State Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority ( State CAMPA ) in 2014-2015 . <p> This was found in four forest divisions of Chhattisgarh namely Balodabazar , Balrampur , Jashpur and Korba . <p> The audit compared the Chhattisgarh forest department 's norms of site preparation -- work like site cleaning , pit digging , fencing work etc. carried out in advance to plantation work -- with the norms under State CAMPA . <p> The audit found that under departmental norms , the pit size prescribed for plantation of 1,100 plants per hectare was 45 centimetre ( cm ) x 45 cm x 45 cm as against the size of 30 cm x 30 cm x 30 cm under State CAMPA .
