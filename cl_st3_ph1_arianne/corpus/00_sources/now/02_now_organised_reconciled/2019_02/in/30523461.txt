<h> ' Agrarian crisis , deforestation behind climate change ' <p> CHANDIGARH : The two-day fourth international dialogue on Himalayan ecology was held here on Saturday , where experts discussed the paradigm shift of climate change that has been evident for the past few years and its influence on agriculture . <p> Devinder Sharma , managing trustee , Dialogue Highway , said , " Climate change is impacting agriculture in a big way by way of deforestation , changing land use of forest area and overuse of fertilisers . We can certainly re-orient these emissions as well as temperature rise with correct measures . " <p> Echoing the same views , Suresh Kumar , chief principal secretary , Punjab , said , " Today , we are facing an agrarian crisis , which is ultimately causing climate change . Farmers are not giving even breathing space of even a day to farm land and use overdose of agro-chemicals , so much so that there is almost no house left in south-west Punjab without a cancer patient .
