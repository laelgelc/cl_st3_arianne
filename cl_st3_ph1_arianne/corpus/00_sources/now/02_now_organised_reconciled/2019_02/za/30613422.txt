AFP/ERIC CABANIS <h> The programme consists of a credit support mechanism that will develop a model for funding embedded generation renewable energy projects . <p> The Development Bank of Southern Africa said on Wednesday it had been granted US$100 million funding from the Green Climate Fund to establish the Embedded Generation Investment Programme ( EGIP ) . <p> Embedded generation under EGIP is defined as the production of electricity from generation facilities that are connected to the national grid with or without wheeling arrangements . <p> The investment programme consists of a credit support mechanism that will develop a model for funding embedded generation renewable energy projects in South Africa .
