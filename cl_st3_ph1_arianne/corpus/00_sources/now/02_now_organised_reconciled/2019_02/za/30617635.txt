<p> The North Gauteng High Court has been petitioned by the City to allow electricity purchases from independent producers , alleviating reliance on Eskom and moving Cape Town towards more renewable power resources . <p> Mayoral Committee member for Energy and Climate Change , Phindile Maxiti , says the City wants Cape Town to become less dependent on Eskom . <p> " We are doing everything possible to move away from sole reliance on Eskom for our energy needs , while at the same time trying to become more resilient and sustainable through the use of cleaner energy such as renewable energy and transitional fuels such as natural gas . " <p> The City is fighting for its right to purchase cleaner energy directly from independent power producers ( IPPs ) . <p> Maxiti explains why the city needs to move towards a more sustainable source of electricity . <p> " As a responsible local government that is increasingly becoming internationally recognised for our climate change programmes , we must move toward more sustainable , greener energy resources to meet our carbon-neutral commitments .
