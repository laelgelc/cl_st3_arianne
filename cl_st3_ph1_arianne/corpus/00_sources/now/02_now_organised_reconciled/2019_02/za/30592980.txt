<p> Good Work Foundation ( GWF ) has won three awards at the 2018 Reimagine Education Conference and Awards , known as the " Oscars of education " . <p> This event in San Francisco featured 1 184 projects from 39 countries . With 160 international judges evaluating the initiatives , GWF is honoured to have represented South African and the African continent so well , said Ryan James , head of development . <p> GWF won bronze in the sustainability , silver in the regional ( Africa ) , and gold in the engineering and technology categories . <p> James said what stood out most last year was the sense of urgency . With issues like climate change , migration , nationalism and wealth disparity becoming more evident to many more people , guests repeatedly asked how education could be reimagined so that a shared future based on collaboration could become an achievable target . <p> GWF 's major showcase was its ecosystem of learning and working that has been developed on the Hazyview Digital Learning Campus . <p> The model has become well known thanks to a start-up IT service desk and call centre on campus that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ marketplace , but also delivers double-digit cost savings to its customers , including the South African subsidiary of Deutsche Telekom . <p> What 's most exciting about that campus start-up is that the profits generated are being used to reimagine education . The profits fund access to inquiry-based learning , using education technology , for 4 300 pupils from the surrounding villages . They are learning in new ways , posting huge improvements in basic literacy and numeracy . <p> " This was our third consecutive year attending the conference and we are proud to stand side by side with organisations and their leaders who are urgently addressing humanity 's approaches to learning , " James continued .
