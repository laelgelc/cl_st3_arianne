As an answer to climate change , the Green New Deal is the best humanity has right now . Which means that South Africa could reap the benefits too . <p> " If everyone is guilty , then no one is to blame , " said Swedish schoolgirl Greta Thunberg at Davos two weeks ago . " And someone is to blame . " <p> Was this a veiled reference to Nathaniel Rich 's novella-length article " Losing Earth , " in which TheNew York Times Magazine took the unprecedented step of devoting an entire issue to climate change , ultimately blaming our collective failure to avert its worst effects on " human nature " ? <p> Perhaps . A much-cited mid-January poll found that 59% of registered voters support a 70% tax on every dollar above the 10 millionth a person earns in one year , with 71% of Democrats , 60% of independents and 45% of Republicans saying they 're in favour . <p> Meanwhile , notwithstanding the gulf between the elite and the US public on tax policy , centrist news brands like TheWashington Post and TheFinancial Times are giving voice to academics who take seriously the idea of the Green New Deal . " <p> Indeed , which brings us back to the hard facts of climate change . It 's no coincidence that the movement has arisen in the wake of October 2018 's IPCC special report , which gave humanity all of 12 years to cut global carbon emissions by a colossal 45% . Ocasio-Cortez , like Thunberg and the enlightened youth they both represent , appears to have taken seriously the injunction in the report 's opening line that what humanity urgently needs rights now is " rapid , far-reaching and unprecedented changes in all aspects of society " . <p> To this end , the Green New Deal has been variously compared not just @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Plan , which rebuilt Western Europe after World War II , and to President Kennedy 's promise to put a man on the moon , which in the early 1960s was a pipe-dream motivated by geopolitical necessity . Of course , as today 's youth would be quick to point out , the necessity is n't beating the Russians in a Cold War space-race -- it 's ensuring that come the end of the century , their own children would inhabit a planet that supports human life . <p> Hence the title of Professor Hockett 's piece in The Financial Times : " Pay for Green New Deal now or spend even more later " . <p> But pay for what , exactly ? <p> The details of the Green New Deal are yet to be worked out . It did n't help that the US Midwest recently experienced an Arctic freeze directly linked to a climate change-induced polar vortex -- and it wo n't help when more " once in a century " hurricanes batter the Gulf of Mexico , or more unprecedented wildfires rage through California , as they undoubtedly will . <p> For those of us in the rest of the world , this counts as morbid good news .
