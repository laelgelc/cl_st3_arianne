<h> No immediate threat of #loadshedding , says Eskom <p> Cape Town - The energy grid remains under pressure , but Eskom said there was no immediate threat of load shedding this week . <p> Spokesperson Andrew Etzinger said power would be constrained " but we 're not anticipating load shedding " . <p> " If we do , however , see a number of our generators fail , the scenario will change and we will come back to the public if we do see a change to the outlook " . <p> Last week , the power utility was under enormous strain with load shedding gripping most parts of the country for six days . It also emerged in Parliament that Eskom was technically insolvent and at its current trajectory would not survive past April . <p> Eskom said the emergency reserves of diesel and water remained very low . <p> The utility said it had lost six additional generating units , which had placed additional strain on the system . <p> Meanwhile , the City of Cape Town urged residents to reduce the risk of nuisance tripping . <p> " This is when the power is restored @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some parts . Residents are encouraged to switch off appliances , including geysers , air conditioners and pool pumps prior to load shedding and leaving one light on to indicate the return of the supply , " said Phindile Maxiti , mayco member for energy and climate change . <p> " We are also continuing our endeavours to become less reliant on Eskom for electricity . " <p> He said that due to spare generation capacity from the Steenbras Hydro-electric Power Scheme , the City could keep its customers on a lower stage of load shedding . q! q! q! q! q! q!
