<h> Care for trees they care for you <p> ARE you complaining about the heat and drought ? How many trees have you planted in your life ? Or , have you replaced those trees that you have removed from earth ? <p> Humans urgently need to reconsider their footprint on earth and start to care and give back and not just take or demolish land . <p> Putting it simple : by removing a tree , you remove a complete ecosystem . <p> It is a scientific fact that trees , especially in forests , play an important role in climate change and cooling the earth surface . As trees grow they absorb carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) , the main greenhouse gas responsible for global warming .
