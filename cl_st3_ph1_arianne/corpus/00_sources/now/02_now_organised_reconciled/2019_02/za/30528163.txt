<h> Wetlands : the world 's water wonder <p> As South Africa 's water sources face increasing pressure , the country has been urged to acknowledge the importance of wetlands as a valuable water system with the potential to help counter climate related challenges . <p> On 2 February 2019 the world once again turned its attention to the protection and conservation of wetlands for the annual World Wetlands Day event . It was under the 2019 theme Wetlands and Climate Change that South Africans were reminded of the important role these distinct ecosystems play in combatting climate related issues like droughts . <p> " In light of the current severe drought , there 's no better time to ensure we have healthy and functioning ecological infrastructure , " noted CapeNature CEO , Dr Razeena Omar . <p> " This includes not only our rivers and streams , but importantly also wetlands because these ecological systems act as water-holding and water-producing devices which provide clean , safe water to our dams and ultimately the end consumer , " she continued . <h> Climate adaptation and building resilience <p> In addition to playing a significant role in stabilising greenhouse gas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ extreme weather and helping to reduce floods , wetlands are also able to help relieve droughts . <p> During the dry season in arid climates , wetlands release stored water , delaying the onset of droughts and minimising water shortages . <p> The message is clear , wetlands are critical for climate adaptation and building resilience however , they remain in trouble around the world . <p> According to Martha Rojas Urrego , Secretary General , Ramsar Convention on Wetlands , more than a third of all the world 's wetlands were lost in just 45 years . <p> " Today , wetlands are disappearing three times faster than forests .
