<h> FirstRand joins Nedbank in coal-fired power exodus <p> JOHANNESBURG -- Unsurprisingly , yet another local bank has opted out of providing financing for new coal power projects in South Africa . The only bank left standing , according to this report below from Chris Yelland , is Absa . It will be interesting to see what Absa 's stance is on new coal IPPs in the country . But judging by global trends with regard to the banks ' shunning of dirty energy projects , these two coal power projects may have to start finding different sources of capital to get off the ground . -- Gareth van Zyl <p> By Chris Yelland* <p> FirstRand Bank has joined the exodus of local banks that were funding new coal-fired power plants in South Africa , and in particular the Thabametsi and Khanyisa independent power producer ( IPP ) projects . <p> Chris Yelland , EE Publishers <p> These two projects are included in the draft integrated resource plan for electricity , Draft IRP 2018 , for completion in 2023 and 2024 .
