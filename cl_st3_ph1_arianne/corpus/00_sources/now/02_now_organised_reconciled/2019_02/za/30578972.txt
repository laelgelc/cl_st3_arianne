<h> How to reduce the risk of ' nuisance ' tripping from load shedding <p> Cape Town -- The City of Cape Town encourages residents to reduce the risk of nuisance tripping , by switching off all appliances which use electricity before load shedding is scheduled to take place . <p> When power is restored after load shedding , nuisance tripping sometimes occurs , which happens when the power does not come back on immediately after the occurrence of load shedding , the City said . <p> This is when the power is restored to an area and fails to come back on in some parts . The power outage often goes unreported because residents assume that it is due to load shedding . <p> Residents are encouraged to reduce the risk and occurrence of nuisance tripping by switching off appliances , including geysers , air conditioners and pool pumps prior to load shedding and leaving one light on to indicate the return of the supply .
