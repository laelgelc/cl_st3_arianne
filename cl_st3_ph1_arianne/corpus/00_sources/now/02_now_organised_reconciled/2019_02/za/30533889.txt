But at the party 's manifesto launch in Cape Town on Tuesday , De Lille expressed confidence that Good has what it takes to ' fix SA ' -- with a focus on tackling the problem of South Africa 's racially divided cities . <p> The issue of spatial justice , De Lille continued , " has not been addressed by any political party in the last 25 years " . <p> It makes sense that De Lille would choose this matter as a policy centrepiece . Good has laid out a fair justification for this emphasis : The fact that more than 65% of the South African population is already urbanised . But it is also probably a reflection of a small party 's need to focus limited campaign resources and manpower in constrained areas where it can achieve the greatest bang for its buck . <p> The
