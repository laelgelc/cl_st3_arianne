He 's the founder and CEO of OOM Energy , an Oakville , Ont. -based company that has developed a new way of providing electricity to customers . <p> " You 're looking at the next big thing , " Clydesdale said . What he 's referring to is his company 's Integrated Energy Platform ( IEP ) , an on-site power system that aims to take buildings off the sometimes unstable electrical grid while also reducing their carbon footprint .
