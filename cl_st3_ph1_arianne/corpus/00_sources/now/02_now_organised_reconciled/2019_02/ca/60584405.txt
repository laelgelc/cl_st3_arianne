<p> This past month , Divest McGill has protested outside of the Arts Building every Friday as part of its new ' Fossil-free Friday ' initiative . The target of their demonstrations is McGill 's ongoing investments in fossil fuel companies . <p> " Bringing Fossil-Free Fridays back up again is a strategy that will engage the community and have a lot of visibility , especially with what is happening worldwide right now , " Divest McGill member Morgen Bertheussen said . <p> The weekly protests are inspired by Swedish teenage activist Greta Thunberg 's strikes for climate action . Thunberg skips school every Friday to perform sit-ins at the Swedish parliament , a demonstration which has garnered significant international attention .
