<h> Canadian wing of Credit Suisse Securities promotes two senior executives <p> Credit Suisse Securities ( Canada ) Inc. has promoted two of its most senior Canadian investment bankers as the Swiss bank looks to consolidate its clout in the country 's energy sector . <p> Tom Greenberg , who has led Credit Suisse 's Canadian investment banking and capital markets operations from Toronto , has been promoted to a new role as global co-head of the bank 's oil and gas group .
