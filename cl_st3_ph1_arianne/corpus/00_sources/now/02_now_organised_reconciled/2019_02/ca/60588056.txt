<h> 31 dead ducks discovered in TFN pond <h> Residents of Tsawwassen First Nation condo complex worried what 's ending up in their area <p> Tsatsu Shores resident Jim Northey in front of the pond where dozens of waterfowl were discovered dead . <p> Photograph By Sandor Gyarmati <p> Wildlife officials have begun trying to solve a deadly mystery at the Tsawwassen First Nation . <p> Environment and Climate Change Canada officers retrieved 31 dead ducks discovered floating in a man-made pond on the shore facing the Tsatsu Shores condominium complex this afternoon . Concerned residents of the complex , literally a few metres from the pond , called to report the disturbing sight in what appears to be mass death due to some kind of poisoning or contamination . <p> An officer told a resident after the animals had been retrieved and bagged that water samples would be taken and necropsies performed , but it could be difficult to ascertain the cause unless there 's something obvious still in the water . He asked her to call if there 's any more dead waterfowl seen .
