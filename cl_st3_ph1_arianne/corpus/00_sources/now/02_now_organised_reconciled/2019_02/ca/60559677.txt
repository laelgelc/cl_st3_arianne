<h> Insects could disappear ' all at the same time ' : scientist <p> A new research paper has come to the startling conclusion that without changes to the way we produce our food , " insects as a whole will go down the path of extinction in a few decades . " <p> The review published in the journal Biological Conservation looked at 73 studies that measured the prevalence of insects , mostly in North America and Europe . They calculated that populations are declining at an average rate of 2.5 per cent annually . <p> Examples of the studies included are a 27-year-long study in Germany that found a 76 per cent decline in flying insect biomass and a 36-year-long study in Puerto Rican rainforests that found losses of 98 per cent of ground-foraging and 78 per cent of canopy-dwelling arthropods . <p> Study co-author Francisco Sanchez-Bayo , an ecologist at the University of Sydney in Australia , told CTV News Channel on Monday that we could lose insects " all at the same time . " <p> " They 're all going down in parallel , " he said . <p> Researchers identified four major causes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ first one obviously is the habitat loss , " he said . " That 's due to agricultural practices , urbanization and deforestation . " <p> The second factor , he said , is " pollution by pesticides and industrial chemicals of different kinds . " <p> " A small contribution of biological factors like invasive species and pathogens " is the third factor , according to Sanchez-Bayo . <p> The fourth major cause -- and least significant of the group , the researchers say -- is climate change . " Not only the warming we 've experienced in the last century but also the cyclones ( are ) devastating . " <p> The researchers say that " rethinking of current agricultural practices , in particular a serious reduction in pesticide usage and its substitution with more sustainable , ecologically-based practices , is urgently needed to slow or reverse current trends . " <p> " In addition , effective remediation technologies should be applied to clean polluted waters in both agricultural and urban environments , " they write . <p> Sanchez-Bayo said that losing insects altogether would have dramatic impacts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lose all of the " ecosystem services they provide . " <p> " For example , pollination in the case of the bees or butterflies ( and ) some of the beetles as well , " he said . <p> " We have to bear in mind ( that ) insects provide the main food source for innumerable species of vertebrates , " Sanchez-Bayo added , pointing to birds , bats , frogs , reptiles and freshwater fish as examples . " All these other species of vertebrates will go down with the insects at the same time .
