<h> From Gucci to Prada , fashion fails evoke racist imagery <p> MILAN -- Luxury fashion is all about breaking codes , creating a new , irresistible message that captivates consumers . But some of the globe 's top brands have raised eyebrows with designs that have racist connotations . <p> The latest instance of that was Italian fashion designer Gucci , which produced a black wool balaclava sweater with an oversized collar that pulls over the chin and nose . It includes a slit where the mouth is , ringed with what look like giant red lips . Its similarity to blackface prompted an instant backlash from the public and forced the company to apologize publicly late Wednesday . <p> Gucci also withdrew the offending garment from sale on websites and stores . It said the incident would be " a powerful learning moment for the Gucci team and beyond .
