And it 's also useful to look at politicians ' recent attempts to use memes for good and ill . <p> What is a political meme ? <p> A political meme is a purposefully designed visual framing of a position . Memes are a new genre of political communication , and they generally have at least one of two characteristics -- they are inside jokes and they trigger an emotional reaction . <p> Memes work politically if they are widely -- or virally -- shared , if they help cultivate a sense of belonging to an " in-group " and if they make a compelling normative statement about a public figure or political issue . <p> Memes can spread rapidly online and into popular culture due to their shareability -- they are easily created , consumed , altered and disseminated . They can quickly communicate the creator 's stance on the subject . " That audience tends to have self-referential language , cultivating an in-group that can decipher the memes and get the " in joke " while those who are n't in on the joke can not . ( For an excellent display of this , listen to one of the " Yes Yes No " segments on the Reply All podcast , in which the hosts explain complex , multi-layered memes to a confused non-digital native . ) <p> A person who wants to successfully create or repurpose a meme therefore needs to have sufficient understanding of that shared sphere and its digital norms . <p> By drawing on shared meanings , meme creators can compress complex ideas into simple visual packages . For instance , feminist memes both critiqued and lampooned the 2012 claim by presidential candidate Mitt Romney that he had " binders full of women " that he could bring into his administration . <p> ' I have a drone ' <p> Memes about former president Barack Obama 's administration ranged @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ discourse , such as the critique of his targeted drone program through the " I have a drone " meme . <p> Similarly , Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau has been the focus of many positive memes , particularly when he is compared to U.S. President Donald Trump , but also negative memes , including those referencing Canada 's arms deal with Saudi Arabia . <p> The dark side of memes <p> While memes can communicate nuanced ideas , generate a sense of cultural belonging and evoke strong emotions , they also have serious downsides as forms of political discourse . Political memes can offer deeper reflections on social issues , with a persuasive effect that risks creating " a self-convincing audience " that is motivated to engage with the messaging . <p> There is serious concern that memes are replacing the nuanced debate necessary in a healthy democracy . <p> It is still debated whether racist , " alt-right " memes , many originating on Reddit and 4Chan with an increasingly transnational reach , were a deciding factor in Trump 's 2016 election win . <p> Authoritarian regimes are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially memes , both domestically and abroad to advance their interests . <p> Furthermore , seemingly neutral memes can be " weaponized " . An infamous example is Pepe the Frog , which evolved from a sad frog cartoon into a symbol for the politically charged , racist and anti-Semitic messages of the so-called alt-right , a U.S.-based white nationalist movement . <p> Another means of weaponization is to link politically charged issues like immigration or climate change to unrelated but highly provocative issues like the spread of the Zika virus . <p> It can be difficult to counter these negative effects of memes . One problem is that they can rarely be fact-checked . Rather than making textual claims , they rely on a visual grammar . After all , it would be silly to fact-check an electoral system equated to Oprah 's gift giveaways . <p> Because memes are ambiguous and require interpretation of visual grammar , it is hard to hold their creators to account . They risk being perceived as making lame attempts to cater to a community to which they do not belong . Hillary Clinton 's attempt to engage young voters backfired when she tweeted out " How does your student loan debt make you feel ? Tell us in 3 emojis or less . " It was criticized as condescending and a distraction from her actual policy proposal to make college more affordable . <p> Politicians can face ridicule and accusations of " inauthenticity " when they engage in this new form of political performance . <p> When politicians use memes , they must also surrender control over how they will be repurposed and interpreted over time . <p> Premier Horgan did not say " pro rep is lit " because it was a convincing argument , but because he knew it would get the internet 's attention . <p> Politicians need to connect with young voters , and use memes to signal to this particular @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contest because they recognized connecting with young voters is a top priority as youth are more likely to be in favour of proportional representation . <p> More research is needed to understand the effect that political memes have on the quality of political debate .
