<p> A northwestern New Brunswick city council has declared a climate emergency , a move the mayor says is a call to action . <p> Edmundston council on Tuesday joined Halifax and Vancouver in making a declaration to fight climate change . <p> Mayor Cyrille Simard said the declaration , which calls for accelerating the shift to greener forms of energy , reflects the impact climate change is already having on the community . <p> " We see the impact on the ground , we see the cost of that , " he said , pointing to torrential rains , flooding that 's worse and more frequent , and invasive species . <p> Edmundston 's mayor says climate change has resulted in weather changes like more rain in the winter , increasing the amount of ice that builds up and maintenance costs .
