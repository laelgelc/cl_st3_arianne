The Sault applies sand and sweeps during the winter , which , he said , has been very effective . <p> The government 's rationale for introducing GRF is that it follows a new harmonized standard under the International Civil Aviation Organization . <p> The new methodology goes into effect during the winter of 2020-2021 , giving the Sault airport management one more winter to prepare . <p> The Sault airport does n't rely on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of an increase to users fees charged to airline tickets , and landing and terminal fees charged to the airlines . <p> " We do n't have a large operating budget to increase , so for us to switch from sand to potassium acetate is going to be very costly , " said Bos . <p> He estimates one application of potassium acetate could cost $4,000 for its 6,000-foot main runway , and considerably more if the airport has to open its 6,000-foot crosswind runway . <p> When new safety regulations come down from Transport Canada , Bos said it 's always difficult to recover the extra costs to implement them since federal funding has been stagnant . <p> When Ottawa divested itself of most of Canada 's airports in the 1990s in favour of private authorities , the Airports Capital Assistance Program ( ACAP ) was put in place as a funding vehicle for airports handling under 525,000 passengers annually . <p> It was to be funded by the ground rent charged to larger airports , like Toronto Pearson , and deposited back into the ACAP program .
