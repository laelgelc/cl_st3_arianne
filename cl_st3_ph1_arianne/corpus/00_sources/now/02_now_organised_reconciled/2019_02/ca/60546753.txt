<h> Talk of the Week : Re-imagining capitalism as a sustainable system <p> FOR ALL the wealth it has created , capitalism has unleashed a litany of evils . Climate change . Wars . Poverty . Inequality . Alienation . <p> Is capitalism doomed ? Can it be saved ? <p> Former Wall Street executive John Fullerton believes capitalism is fundamentally sound , save for some hiccups . Fullerton laid out his ideas in " Regenerative Capitalism : How Universal Principles and Patterns Will Shape our New Economy " , a 2015 paper published by his Connecticut-based think-tank Capital Institute . <p> Fullerton will deliver a talk presented by the Vancity credit union . The event , tilted " Reimagining Economics : How living systems can fix a broken one " , is on Tuesday ( February 19 ) at the Vancouver Convention Centre West ( 1055 Canada Place ) .
