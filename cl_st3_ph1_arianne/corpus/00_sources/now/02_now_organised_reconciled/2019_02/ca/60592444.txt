<h> Political Science Talk <p> Thursday , Feb. 28 , 9-10:30 a.m . <p> A-1049 <p> Dr. Elizabeth Schwartz , University of Saskatchewan , will speak on , " Local Solutions : Climate Change Policy in Canadian Cities " What are Canadian cities doing to combat climate change ? How can we make those actions more effective ? Responding to climate change and preventing catastrophic and irreversible changes to our environment is going to require large-scale transformation of our societies and economic systems over the next few decades . This will require more than technological innovation . The majority of greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions are created in urban areas , and although a large proportion can be controlled by municipalities , responding to climate change is n't a typical responsibility of local governments . In this presentation , Dr. Elizabeth Schwartz examines local contributions to global efforts to reduce GHG emissions . She presents a new theory of how municipal administrative structure can make cities ' climate change policy more effective . <p> Presented by Department of Political Science <p> Event Listing2019-02-28 9:00:002019-02-28 **32;184;TOOLONG Science TalkDr .
