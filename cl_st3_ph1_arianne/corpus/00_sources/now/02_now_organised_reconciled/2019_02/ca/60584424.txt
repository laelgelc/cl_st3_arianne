<h> As it has done in previous years , the university 's board of governors is implementing the maximum two per cent hike allowed by the provincial government <p> Thompson Rivers University will increase the cost of domestic undergraduate tuition by two per cent ahead of the 2019-2020 school year -- an annual practise administration said is common among every B.C. university . <p> The board of governors has unanimously approved a notice of motion to increase tuition . The matter will be up for approval at its March 29 meeting . <p> A two per cent increase is the maximum allowed for domestic post-secondary students by the provincial government . <p> According to the university , the increase is needed to offset inflationary costs , but wo n't cover the entire amount . <p> In December , the board approved a three per cent tuition increase every year for the next three years for international students attending TRU . There is no provincial cap on increases for international students . <p> Tuition and fees for first-year students at TRU are estimated at $5,300 .
