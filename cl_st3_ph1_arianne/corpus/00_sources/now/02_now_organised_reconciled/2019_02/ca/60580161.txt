<h> Consumer goods companies preparing for climate change impact <p> BERLIN -- Companies behind some of the best-known consumer products -- from soaps to sodas -- are beginning to factor climate change into their business equation , according to a report published Monday . <p> The survey of 16 major corporations by non-profit group CDP found that many are working to lower their carbon emissions , prepare for the effects of global warming on their supply chain and respond to growing environmental consciousness among customers . <p> Examples include brewer AB InBev 's efforts to develop a variety of barley that needs less water and Unilever adjusting its detergent formulas so they work at the lower " eco " temperature settings on modern washing machines , the London-based group said . <p> " We were surprised how much these companies were aligning themselves with changes in consumer preferences , " said Carole Ferguson , the report 's lead author . <p> This includes chasing trends such as veganism , a small but growing factor in the market that 's driven by people who shun animal products for ethical or health reasons , but also because they have larger carbon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a maker of plant-based nutrition bars , is a typical example where a large company has snapped up a small brand to fill a niche it did n't yet cover . <p> Such purchases help companies bolster their green credentials at a time when they 're beginning to feel the heat of climate activism .
