( File photos ) <p> Some of the easiest ways to think about diversity focus on questions of " who . " Who is present within a space ? Who has a seat at the table ? Who is given the opportunity to speak -- and who is listened to ? Questions of " who " can also be documented and reported on , turned into numbers and figures , with relative ease . <p> But if diversity stops at " who , " it misses other questions every bit as crucial , like " what " and " how . " <p> " It 's about getting beyond the numbers , " says Dal Provost Teri Balser . " Numbers are easier to monitor , but metrics and targets do n't get at the heart of what diversity is , and its value .
