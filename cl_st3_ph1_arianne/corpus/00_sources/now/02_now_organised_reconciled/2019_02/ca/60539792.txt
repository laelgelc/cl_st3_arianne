<p> SEELEY 'S BAY -- The expansion of a quarry mining a " little-known but extremely useful " mineral on the edge of the city limits is to go before city 's planning committee later this month . <p> Canadian Wollastonite is seeking to have a portion of land rezoned to allow the mine expansion . <p> The company 's existing open pit mine is entirely in the Township of Leeds and the Thousand Islands . <p> But the company has plans to expand its quarry west , into the city , and to do so it needs city council to rezone the land from agricultural to permit mining . <p> Wollastonite is a calcium-silicate mineral and is added to other materials , such as cement , ceramics , paint and plastics , to increase their strength . <p> Canadian Wollastonite owns more than 200 hectares , with about two-thirds of that land being in Leeds and the Thousand Islands Township . <p> In October , the city and the township agreed to realign an unopened road allowance to accommodate expanded mining operations in the portion of land within the city limits . <p> " We officially began shipping @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ years have been introducing the mineral into a number of new markets in both Canada
