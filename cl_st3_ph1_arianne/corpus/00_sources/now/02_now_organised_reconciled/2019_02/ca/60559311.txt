( CBC ) <p> Montreal runs one of the largest snow-removal operations in the world . It takes an army of 3,000 workers to remove snow from 10,000 kilometres of Montreal streets each winter . That adds up to $165 million a year . <p> After a major storm like the one the city had last week , crews go to work around the clock . Streets are cleared of cars ; snow is plowed and dropped into trucks and hauled away to one of the city 's 29 snow dumps . <p> About 300,000 truckloads of snow are taken off the street over the course of one winter . <p> " It 's a huge operation , " says city spokesperson Philippe Sabourin . <p> Sabourin gave CBC Montreal a behind-the-scenes tour of the snow-removal process . <p> The downtown snow-removal operation is run out of a city garage east of the Jacques-Cartier Bridge . <p> At the garage , a bulldozer mixes salt and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> This front-end loader mixessalt and gravel at the city 's garage on Bercy Street .
