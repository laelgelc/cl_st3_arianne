By raising the price of goods and services that are sources of carbon emissions , Ottawa 's proposed carbon tax would work just as cigarette taxes have worked in discouraging people from smoking -- but with one , very big difference . <p> The higher cigarette prices from tobacco taxes hit smokers in two ways .
