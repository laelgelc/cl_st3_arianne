<h> No more plastic drinking straws starting Jan 1 , 2020 <p> SIBU : All eateries within the Sibu Municipal Council ( SMC ) area are prohibited from giving plastic drinking straws to customers beginning January 1 , 2020 . <p> During a meeting of the council today , chairman Datuk Tiong Thai King said the council will make it mandatory for eateries to support its " Say No to Plastic Straw " campaign which will be a condition for licence renewals and applications starting next year . <p> The campaign , he said , will be launched during SMC 's annual green event , the " Love Earth Day Campaign " , on April 29 this year . <p> These campaigns , according to him , are part of the local efforts to reduce plastic waste to help mitigate climate change . <p> The council is giving all eateries until December this year to clear their stocks of plastic straws . <p> On the Love Earth Day Campaign 2019 , Tiong said this year the council and co-organiser , JCI Sibu , will continue to spearhead it in collaboration with the Education Department . <p> " Under the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ council is taking the lead to educate locals through a variety of awareness programmes about the importance of protecting nature 's gifts by actively participating in global environmental protection movements , " he said . <p> On the smoking ban , he said the council will enforce it at all eateries starting March 1 as directed by the Local Government and Housing Ministry . <h> About New Sarawak Tribune <p> ' The New Sarawak Tribune ' is an effort by a group of businessmen to retrieve the glory days of the now defunct ' Sarawak Tribune ' ( Sarawak 's oldest English daily set up in 1945 ) , which was discontinued due to its publication of an Islamic caricature in 2006. q! q! q! q! q! q!
