Nonetheless , the majority of people tend to assume that it only involves disposing of their waste into the dustbin . <p> This is indirectly reflected when the majority of people , especially in the developing countries , commonly do not even practise waste separation and waste recycling , although it is an integral element under the solid waste management system . <p> In addition , the definition also suggests that , a country that plans to achieve a sustainable solid waste management system should take the entire elements which fall under the solid waste management system into consideration . <p> For example , if the local government plans to improve their solid waste management system , they should not only focus on one element , such as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all elements under the solid waste management starting from managing waste generation until the final element which is waste disposal . <p> In other words , to achieve a sustainable solid waste management system , it requires a more holistic or comprehensive approach . Similarly , in order to reduce body fat effectively , one should not only focus on exercise , but also closely monitor one 's diet , as both play important roles in reducing body fat effectively . <p> Ideally , if a country has a sustainable solid waste management system , it will experience less negative environmental impact such as air pollution , harmful diseases , climate change and waste generation . <p> However , the reality is that a majority of countries , especially developing countries , are experiencing many challenges in managing their solid waste management system . This is mainly contributed by the growing number of population and rapid economic growth . <p> A similar context is also experienced in Malaysia . Although for many years , KKCH has frequently organised programmes like Ops Kutu Sampah , gotong-royong , litter-free run , litter-free premises , and litter-free restaurant , issues of solid waste management still persist . <p> The programmes and campaigns conducted by KKCH show that KKCH mainly focuses on the aspect of cleanliness , public health and sanitation towards addressing the issues of solid waste management . However , too much focus on this aspect suggests that Kota Kinabalu is still trapped in addressing the basic issues of solid waste management . <p> Developed countries , on the other hand , are no longer focusing on the basic issues of solid waste management , but instead are more focused towards improving the aspect of environmental conservation . <p> Although the aspects of cleanliness , public health and sanitation are crucial towards maintaining the cleanliness of the city , they may not comprehensively address the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cleanliness alone may not resolve the issues of increasing waste generation in Kota Kinabalu . <p> The lack of comprehensive or holistic approaches towards addressing the issue of solid waste management in Kota Kinabalu has indirectly affected the state government to effectively address the solid waste issue . On January 13 , 2016 , KKCH worked with General Incorporated Association Conference of Earth from Akita in Japan and Japan International Cooperation Agency to develop guidelines for waste separation in Sabah . Through this project , three residential areas , namely Taman Greenview , Taman Jindo and Alam Damai Condominium in Kota Kinabalu , were selected as part of a pilot study to assist the development of waste separation guideline .
