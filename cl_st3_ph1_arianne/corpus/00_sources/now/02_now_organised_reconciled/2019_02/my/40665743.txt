<h> M'sia has to step up grain corn production : Sim <p> SEBERANG JAYA : Malaysia has to step up the production of corn as the nation spends RM3.09 billion a year to import grain corn , Deputy Agriculture and Agro-based Industry Minister Sim Tze Zin said today . <p> He said the amount spent annually to import grain corn , which served as the main source of animal feed formulation , had been increasing by 10 per cent every year . <p> " The dependency on the imported corn has reached almost 100% , creating risk and instability to the national livestock industry . The demand for grain corn alone is increasing globally and is expected to double by the year 2050 . <p> " Our challenge to increase the corn production to meet future demand lies on research and development ( R&amp;D ) , governance and policy implementation , " he said when opening the Asean Regional Corn Conference organised by the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute ( Mardi ) here . <p> He said the increased prices of chickens and eggs lately were closely related to the cost of buying grain corn for animal feed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ million metric tonnes of grain corn , from 80,000 hectares , by the year 2032 despite the challenges of the global situation that are increasingly competitive , such as free trade , climate change and sustainable development goals . <p> He said that according to a Mardi report , which shared experience in the potential for grain corn in Malaysia , the quality of the grain corn planted in Seberang Perai was better compared to the imported grain corn . <p> " Studies on its nutrient composition and the growth performance of local broilers has shown no significant difference compared to the imported ones .
