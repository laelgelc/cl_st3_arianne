<h> Malaysia opens tender for RM2b large-scale solar power projects <p> PUTRAJAYA ( Feb 15 ) : The Malaysian government had today called for bids for an estimated RM2 billion worth of projects under the third round of the Large-Scale Solar ( LSS3 ) scheme to increase electricity generation from renewable energy , Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin said today . <p> Yeo said the competitive bidding process will be opened for a six-month period from February to August 2019 . She said the outcome of the exercise is expected by year-end . <p> " This is an open tender free for all . Whoever that can give us the lowest price for the first 500 megawatts ( MW ) -- having passed all the required financial and technical qualifications -- will be the winner of this bidding exercise . <p> " Ownership can be up to 49% foreign , but the contractor for engineering , construction and commissioning must be 100% local as there are enough competitive local players in Malaysia , who however may not have enough financial muscles .
