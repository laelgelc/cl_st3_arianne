in addition to the global warming that comes from enhanced greenhouse concentrations alone , " according to the study . <p> A temperature increase of that magnitude would melt polar ice and lift sea levels tens of metres . <p> The last time the planet was that hot , some 50 million years ago during the Eocene Epoch , crocodiles roamed the Arctic . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ would overwhelm humanity 's capacity to adapt , scientists say . <p> A barely one-degree increase since the mid-19th century -
