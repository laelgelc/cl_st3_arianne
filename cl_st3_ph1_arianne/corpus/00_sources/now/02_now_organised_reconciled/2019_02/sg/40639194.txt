Yet hairdressing , both as a sector and as an individual activity , can have a massive carbon footprint . <p> Hairdressing uses high levels of hot water , energy and chemicals . Similarly , in our homes , heating hot water is typically the most energy intensive activity . For the cost of a 10-minute shower that uses an electric immersion heater , you could leave a typical television on for 20 hours . <p> Advertisement <p> So while it helps to turn lights and appliances off , the real gains in terms of reducing energy usage are in slashing our use of hot water . <p> In the UK , a quarter of emissions are residential and , of those , the vast majority come from running hot water . Not only is the hair sector a high user of resources , but hairdressers probably talk to more people than any other occupation -- and are in a great position to pass on advice about lower resource hair care . <p> From speaking with hairdressers , it seems that ever since the episode of Blue Planet II in which David Attenborough explained how a whale mother was still carrying her dead baby which , it was claimed , had been poisoned by plastics ( though scientists working on the show have confirmed there was no actual evidence to prove this ) salons have been seeing a massive increase in clients wanting to know that their hairdresser is doing their bit . <p> Our research has found that many hairdressers are keen to make changes that are better for the environment . There are many products out there that are better for the environment , not because they have " organic " or " eco " on the label , but because they reduce the need for hot water . <p> Dry shampoo is a great example . It is fast , convenient , and great at festivals and on the move . It also makes hair easier to style , is cheap and avoids the need for any hot water . <p> Similarly , leave-in conditioner avoids the need for an extra rinse and again makes hair easier to style . It is also fantastic at giving body to fine hair , and saves water , energy , money and time . <p> The salon owner can also obtain a sustainable salon certificate to let customers know these things are important @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ salon has numerous benefits , and not just in terms of reputation .
