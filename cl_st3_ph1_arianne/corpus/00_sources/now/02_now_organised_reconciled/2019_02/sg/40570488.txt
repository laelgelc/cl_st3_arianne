<h> US navy wants to build a wall to stave off climate change <p> WASHINGTON ( BLOOMBERG ) - The Navy is considering erecting a 4m flood wall around the Washington Navy Yard to protect the historic complex along the Anacostia River from rising sea levels , internal Department of Defence documents show . <p> Military engineers proposed the wall in a study , obtained by Bloomberg News through a public-records request , that describes a structure as long as 2.4km , to protect three dozen buildings at a cost of as much as US$20 million . <p> It 's the latest example of a federal agency getting ready for climate change , despite the Trump administration 's public dismissal of the threat . <p> The department has been considering a wall at the sprawling complex - which sits on a revitalised waterfront in the nation 's capital - since at least September 2016 .
