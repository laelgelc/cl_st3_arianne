<h> Norway gives go-ahead to disputed Arctic copper mine <p> OSLO ( REUTERS ) - Norway 's government approved on Thursday ( Feb 14 ) the building of a copper mine near Europe 's northernmost point despite years of opposition from indigenous Sami herders and fishermen . <p> Norway 's decision on the copper mine has been viewed as a litmus test for the Arctic , where climate change and technology are enabling mineral and energy extraction , shipping and tourism , but threatening traditional ways of life . <p> " The mining project will strengthen the industrial base in the north , " Industry Minister Torbjoern Roe Isaksen of the centre-right coalition government said in a statement . <p> " It will contribute positively to the local community , with new jobs and skills . " <p> The Nussir ASA project is expected to bring jobs and investment to the Kvalsund municipality , but the digging could damage summer reindeer pastures , and a plan to dump tailings in the fjord would destroy spawning grounds for the coastal cod . <p> " I am shocked by the government 's decision .
