<h> Report identifies key trends for sovereign wealth funds to watch <p> Shifting demographics , the rise of technology and increasing focus on sustainability are key trends that sovereign wealth funds should incorporate in their investment frameworks , according to a new report . <p> The report said ageing could result in a redistribution of wealth across generations and change consumption patterns , creating the need for new products and services . <p> This would need new investment in infrastructure , the healthcare and wellness industries , and housing . <p> The report also said shifting demographics will impact other key economic variables . <p> Academic research , for example , suggests that equity values in the United States correlate with demographic trends . As baby boomers age , they will likely move from investing in equities to selling equities to fund their retirement . <p> " All else being equal , this would exert pressure on equity multiples , " said the report by UBS bank and Swiss business university IMD . <p> Technology was also highlighted as a key area of consideration because of its disruptive nature . <p> Sovereign wealth funds must be able to quickly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opportunities for extra returns or avoid permanent losses , the report added . <p> Companies and whole industries can also be put at risk by non-competitive dynamics coming from challenges outside the market , for example , from the area of cybercrime . <p> Climate change will likely be an important driver of sustainable strategies for companies , which may have different effects across economies and sectors , and therefore across asset returns . Commodity-based sovereign wealth funds are the most exposed investors as their source of wealth is oil , which is being disrupted by climate change . <p> " We believe that diversifying climate change risk in the assets that they invest in global markets should become a key driver of their investment strategy in the future . But all investors should incorporate climate change in their investment framework , " said the report . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on February 23 , 2019 , with the headline ' Report identifies key trends for sovereign wealth funds to watch ' .
