<p> WASHINGTON ( WASHINGTON POST ) - Some 115,000 years ago , homo sapiens were still living in bands of hunter gatherers , largely confined to Africa . It was one that 's quite close to our current climate , but with one major discrepancy - seas at the time were 20 to 30 feet higher . <p> During this ancient period , sometimes called the Eemian , the oceans were about as warm as they are today .
