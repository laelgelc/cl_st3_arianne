It will draw on the country 's strengths and the Singaporean DNA - of openness , multiculturalism and self-determination - forged from its roots as an open port . <p> The Government also picked up on three lessons from the country 's history . The first is that as long as the Republic stays relevant and useful , Singapore and Singaporeans will have a place in the world . The country , he said , must develop deep capabilities , stay open and connected , and draw ideas and talents from around the globe . <p> " Singaporean talents have been making their mark in various fields , and connecting with other highly skilled individuals from around the world will make our team even stronger , " he added . <p> Mr Heng also noted that external @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . It can also take advantage of its strategic location and " serve as a neutral , trusted node in key spheres of global activity " . <p> Mr Heng added : " We strive to be a place where people and ideas congregate , at the frontier of global developments .
