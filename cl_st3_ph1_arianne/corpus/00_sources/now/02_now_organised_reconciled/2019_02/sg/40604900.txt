<h> The main threats to the international order <p> The majority of respondents from 26 countries of the world considers climate change " a major international threat . " This is evidenced by published on Monday , February 11 , a report of the American research center Pew Research Center ( PRC ) . Terrorist group " Islamic state " ( IG ) took the second place among the factors of greatest risk . Moreover , the representatives of eight countries , including Russia , France , Indonesia and Nigeria , put IG in the first place . <p> In the number of threats also included " the influence and impact of the Russian Federation " . However , only in one country -- Poland -- the respondents brought this answer to a leading position in the list ( 65 percent ) . In the United States a similar opinion was shared by half of the respondents ( 50 percent ) . <p> Meanwhile , the number of those who consider the United States as a threat , has increased in comparison with survey data for 2013 and 2017 .
