<p> As state elections in Andhra Pradesh draw closer , the YSRCP is on a promise-making spree . The party 's chief Jagan Mohan Reddy soon after announcing that he would hike pensions to Rs 3000 made a promise to bring in legislation that would give 75% reservation to locals in jobs at existing and future industries in the state . Political analysts viewing the promises from YSRCP and even TDP wonder if political parties are even considering other development models than ones based on welfare . <p> On Thursday , Jagan at a party event in Kadapa announced that if voted to power in the upcoming state assembly elections , his party would bring in legislation that would provide 75% reservation in jobs to locals in existing industries and those that will be set up . He also promised to fill the 2.4 lakh vacant posts in the state This is the YSRCPs answer to the 5.2% unemployment rate in the state . <p> " The government did not care to fill the 2.4 lakh government posts lying vacant .
