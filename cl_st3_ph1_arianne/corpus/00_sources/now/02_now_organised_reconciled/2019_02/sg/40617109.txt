Such an inclusive approach will require sharing best practices and investing in what is proven to work . Meanwhile , development partners must provide long-term support that emphasizes capacity-building and institutions , and balances humanitarian , economic , and security imperatives . <p> For education systems and services to be truly inclusive , however , they must also leave no one behind , such as refugees . Closing this gap will require not just increased domestic financing , but also a renewed commitment from international donors . <p> Everyone has the right to an education .
