The study covered plantations established between 2002 and 2016 , and over those years , the researchers found an increase in the conversion of peat soils . <p> Their projections point to peat conversion rates rising for the foreseeable future , with the majority of smallholder oil palm expansion happening on peat soils by 2030 . " The agricultural land that people are prepared to convert to palm oil has already been converted -- and a lot of the existing farmland is becoming exhausted , so farmers are venturing further away and going into more marginal areas , " Schoneveld says . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ islands are responsible for the worst environmental effects of palm oil .
