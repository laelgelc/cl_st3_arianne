<p> The Government will be investing in infrastructure in a big way and developing long-term plans to protect the country against climate change , Finance Minister Heng Swee Keat said yesterday . <p> But individuals must also change their ways and work towards a more sustainable future . <p> " As a low-lying island nation , there is nowhere to hide when sea levels rise , " he said . " To protect ourselves against climate change and rising sea levels , we will have to invest more . Together with existing infrastructure needs , our total bill for infrastructure will increase significantly , " he said . <p> Mr Heng pointed out that it is very difficult to project spending needs so far ahead , although the different ministries have done some preliminary estimates . <p> " We will continue to do our best to look forward , develop fiscal plans well in advance , and put in place the right approach to finance such long-lived major infrastructure .
