Using the environment and new technology to stay cool as it gets hotter <p> The Government is aware of the need to plan for and mitigate rising temperatures , which is caused by a combination of climate change and the urban heat island ( UHI ) effect from urbanisation , said Environment and Water Resources Minister Masagos Zulkifli . <p> Mr Masagos was responding to a question from Nominated Member of Parliament ( NMP ) Mohamed Irshad on what steps the authorities are taking to " cool our island " . <p> The measures are : <p> Adopting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Development Board flats to maximise wind flow and shade in estates . <p> Incorporating green open spaces into urban landscape and distributed greenery to cool the environment through shade and evapotranspiration . <p> Encouraging the use of coatings and building materials to limit the absorption and retention of solar radiation . <p> Continued investment in research and development is also ongoing to better understand the effects of rising temperatures in Singapore . <p> Mr Masagos said a key contributor to the UHI effect is heat generated by human activities requiring energy consumption . Working on this , the ministry has amended and introduced policies to mitigate these effects . <p> With the amendment of the Energy Conservation Act in 2017 , Singapore was able to put into force a carbon tax earlier this year and this sends " an important price signal to incentivise emissions reductions " , noted Mr Masagos . <p> The Mandatory Energy Labelling Scheme and the Minimum Energy Performance Standards were also introduced in 2008 and 2011 respectively .
