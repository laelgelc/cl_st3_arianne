They 're even ready to pay up to 50 per cent more than the price of a conventional car to own an EV , according to a Frost &amp; Sullivan survey commissioned by Nissan last year . <p> And yet , demand for EVs is taking off at an uneven pace , even as the region 's concerns around climate change , pollution and congestion have continued to grow . <p> Advertisement <p> We asked consumers in Singapore , Thailand , Indonesia , Philippines , Malaysia and Vietnam to tell us what is stopping them from switching to an electric vehicle . The results were striking and challenge all of us to address car buyers ' fears -- or risk EV demand not taking off at all . <p> " Range anxiety " was consumers ' number one concern , with 98 per cent worried they will run out of power in an EV . This is despite the fact that some EVs can travel more than 250km on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ twice around the perimeter of Singapore on a single charge . <p> In fact , the average annual mileage in Singapore for a private passenger car is a little over 45km per day according to the Land Transport Authority . Even in Malaysia , the average driver travels 66km per day , according to the Malaysian Institute of Road Safety Research . <p> Regional car buyers shared that infrastructure is also a major concern . 97 per cent said that a lack of public and private infrastructure was a barrier to buying electric cars . <p> They feared having to travel far away from their homes or offices to charge their EVs -- or that it would take too long to charge . Private companies and service providers must take the lead to deal with infrastructural challenges . <p> Drivers in Singapore can look forward to at least 3,000 charging stations by 2020 . In Malaysia , various organisations are working together to increase the number of charging stations nationwide from 400 to over 3,000 by this year . Strategically-located charging points that complement consumer behaviours are critical to allaying consumer fears . <p> Drivers want to be able to charge their cars while shopping for groceries or at work , where idle time in a carpark means there is a full charge where they return . <p> We see governments around the region implementing policies that make it more affordable for consumers to switch to EVs . Thailand 's Energy Policy and Planning Office is studying further tax deductions for car buyers who purchase EVs . <p> The Indonesian government is promoting the purchase of environmentally-friendly cars like EVs by introducing larger tax-breaks for buyers .
