<h> China tycoon loses Aussie PR status , barred from entry <p> SYDNEY * A prominent Chinese billionaire political donor has been stripped of his Australian residency and barred from returning to the country after scrutiny of his Communist Party ties , local media reported yesterday . <p> Mr Huang Xiangmo - a property developer who has donated millions to Australia 's two main political parties - has been at the centre of a spate of political interference concerns . <p> He was yesterday left stranded outside the country after Australia 's Home Affairs office cancelled his permanent residency and turned down his application for Australian citizenship , the Sydney Morning Herald reported . <p> Quoting unnamed sources , the Sydney Morning Herald said Mr Huang had his application for an Australian passport blocked for a range of reasons , including what the paper said were " character grounds " and concerns over the " reliability " of information he had supplied in interviews . <p> Australia 's spy agency has long voiced concerns that China was interfering in Australian institutions and using the political donations system to gain access .
