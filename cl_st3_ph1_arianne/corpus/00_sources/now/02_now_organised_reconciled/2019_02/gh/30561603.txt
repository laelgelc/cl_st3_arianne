<h> First Lady to champion ban on plastics in West Africa <p> Mrs Rebecca Akufo-Addo ( left ) , with her colleague first ladies in a discussion at the old AU building VIP holding area <h> The First Lady , Mrs Rebecca Akufo-Addo , has been selected as ' Co-Champion ' of a continental campaign aimed at getting African countries to ban plastic products which generate waste . <p> The campaign , which is on the theme : " Banning plastics towards pollution-free Africa " , was initiated by the Department of Rural Economy and Agriculture ( DREA ) of the African Union ( AU ) Commission to rid African cities of plastic waste . <p> One of its objectives is to advocate and create awareness of the impact of plastic in Africa . <p> It is also to help share experiences , strategies and measures being undertaken at the various national and regional levels . <p> Mrs Akufo-Addo and the First Lady of The Gambia , Mrs Fatoumata Bah Barrow , who is the campaign ' Champion ' in West Africa , are to advocate a plastic pollution free West Africa . <p> Other champions <p> The First @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Co-Champion , respectively , for Central Africa ; the First Ladies of Rwanda and Kenya are the
