<h> Jubilee House to be powered by Solar energy from August <p> The seat of government , Jubilee House , will from August this year be powered by solar energy , as an example to other public institutions , the President has disclosed . <p> Delivering the 2019 State of the Nation Address to Parliament Thursday , President Akufo-Addo said government 's target is to install up to 200 megawatts of distributed solar power by 2030 in both residential and non-residential facilities . <p> This , he believes , will reduce government 's liabilities to the Electricity Company of Ghana ( ECG ) . <p> According to him , in order to reduce government 's expenditure on utilities , and also promote the use of solar power for government and public buildings , the Energy Ministry initiated the Solar Rooftop Programme . <p> The Ministry is leading by example with the installation of a 65-kilowatt solar rooftop system at its premises . <p> Renewable energy has also become a necessary addition to the country 's energy sector because it has increasingly become cheaper , and is key to the implementation of Ghana 's international obligations under Sustainable Development Goal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , reliable , and sustainable energy , as well as Sustainable Development Goal 13 , on urgent action , to combat climate change . <p> " Mr. Speaker , affordable and reliable energy is absolutely critical to realising our vision of economic transformation . I am happy to announce that gas production tripled during the year , from 100 to 300 million cubic feet per day . <p> The seat of government , Jubilee House <p> " The Ministry of Energy is undertaking steps to remove the transmission bottlenecks , to ensure that Ghanaian gas can reach power plants located in the eastern part of the country , and I am confident that , by August this year , the situation would have been fully remedied to ensure Ghana uses locally produced gas for the bulk of its thermal power generation , saving substantial amounts of foreign exchange on imported fuels , " he said . <p> According to the President , government is committed to achieving an electricity generation mix that ensures diversity and security of energy supply . <p> He said , for this reason , government will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with our policy target of 10% renewables in the energy mix from the current 1% . <p> President Akufo-Addo noted that another justification for renewable energy is that , " in spite of Ghana 's excess electricity generation capacity , we can still not achieve our universal access target because there are many Ghanaian communities , especially those on islands and lakesides , that can not be reached through the national grid . <p> " For example , there are currently 200 island and 2,000 lakeside communities that require mini-grids from renewable sources to meet their energy needs .
