and Buff Bay and Orange Bay in Portland . <p> The major problems to be addressed are retreat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ these towns ; coastal and riverine flooding ; and deforestation . <p> The project will address the issues identified , by implementing shoreline protection and ecosystems restoration ( reforestation ) , drainage system outfall upgrade and capacity building . <p> Activities in Annotto Bay include the installation of 600 metres of rock revetment ;
