<p> How long do humans have until a serious global warming event similar to the one 56 million years ago ? A new study says that at the rate of carbon emissions today , it could come as early as in 140 years.Carbon EmissionsAccording to a new study , the concentration of carbon dioxide in the Earth 's atmosphere is rising faster than it did at any point during the time of the dinosaurs .
