<h> UNHCR ambassador urges Filipinos to help refugees <p> Broadcast journalist and the newest national Goodwill Ambassador of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees ( UNHCR ) Atom Araullo called on Filipinos to take a step in helping and empowering refugees across the globe . <p> " We can never move forward by leaving the most vulnerable behind , " Araullo said . <p> ( UNHCR/Kervin Luy / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> He added that lending a hand to refugees all over the world is more than an act of charity or sympathy . <p> " It is our collective desire to be able to move forward as a society . Their ( refugees ) problems are also our problems , " said Araullo . <p> The UNHCR goodwill ambassador , during his title conferment on February 4 in Makati , also underscored the importance of showing solidarity , understanding , and compassion with the refugees and how they can turn stories of loss and despair to stories of " hope , rebuilding , and healing . " <p> " Empowering refugees by helping them rebuild their lives means recognizing that they are not threats , they are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ society 's greatest contributors , " Araullo stated . <p> According to UNHCR , the number of refugees has reached a record-high of 68.5 million , and about 87 , 505 of the displaced persons are in Mindanao . <p> In his speech , Araullo mentioned that there is a greater need of support and care for refugees , especially now when one person is displaced every two seconds . <p> " There are so many displaced people around the world . It 's growing each and every year not just because of war , conflict , or persecution ; it 's also because of problems like climate change . People living in areas where there was n't any problem before are now being forced to move just because they 're looking for a better place to raise their families , to get away from the stress of finding something to sustain their everyday needs , " said Araullo . <p> Araullo also shared that talking to refugees across the globe has made him realize that anybody could experience being displaced , which made him even more eager to spread @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it is globally . <p> " At any given day this ( what refugees went through ) could happen to us , and we want to make sure that we are working together so that we can help each other at our time of need . Sometimes it 's difficult to imagine especially if you 're not really living in a place that you can assume , will be under threat or under siege anytime soon but it 's based on my experience talking to all these refugees . It can happen to anyone , " he told The Manila Bulletin . <p> " We have to show that we have this compassion for other people because at the end of the day , it 's just one goal , we all live in one global village and if today a certain set of people is in need , the following day we might find ourselves in their situation so we should be able to help each other , " Araullo added . <p> #StepWithRefugees <p> The Philippines has long been a " source of outpouring generosity for families on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Shinji Kubo , UNHCR representative to the Philippines . <p> Kubo said the Philippines , on a number of occasions , would open wide its heart and would be the only country to welcome refugees when other nations have " shut their eyes and closed their doors .
