<h> Lorenzana , Villar lead groundbreaking of OCD admin building <p> Defense Secretary and National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council ( NDRRMC ) Chairperson Delfin N. Lorenzana and Department of Public Works and Highways ( DPWH ) Secretary Mark A. Villar led the groundbreaking ceremony for the new multi-million peso Office of Civil Defense ( OCD ) administration building at Camp Aguinaldo , Quezon City on Tuesday , February 26 . <p> The groundbreaking ceremony signaled the start of the construction of the seven-storey OCD admin building that will house the different services of the OCD . <p> A total amount of P275 million was allotted by the DPWH for the construction of the building which is expected to be completed by April 2020 . <p> The thanksgiving ceremony for the NDRRMC Operations Center was held after the groundbreaking ceremony . Secretaries Lorenzana , Villar , together with NDRRMC Executive Director and OCD Administrator Undersecretary Ricardo B. Jalad and the director of the SMS Global Technologies Inc. , Ricardo B. Rivera , led the thanksgiving ceremony . <p> The thanksgiving ceremony was conducted to express the gratitude of the NDRRMC Operations Center which was a product of collaborative @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provided technical assistance , donated equipment , and other materials . <p> In his speech , Lorenzana said that the NDRRMC Operations Center could not have a more suitable location other than Camp Aguinaldo which has been the home and nerve center of the national disaster and risk and rescue management effort . <p> " We have a reached the point where the unprecedented impacts of climate change are apparently peaking . Our country has witnessed destructive calamities of all sorts in the recent years and we need to toughen up and put our best disaster risk reduction and management assets to work , " he added . <p> Lorenzana said the adverse effects of the changing climate patterns and extreme weather conditions are critical points in the nation 's security . <p> " Recall what happened to our country during the times of Ondoy , Yolanda , and Usman . These are just some of the lethal and destructive events in our country , and these are more than enough to stimulate us to be disaster resilient , " Lorenzana said . <p> " We have been advocating for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Department of Disaster Resilience , and we are happy that it continues to gain ground , " he said , adding that House Bill No. 8165 was already approved on third and final reading . <p> Lorenzana said since it started its operations , the new operations center has been facilitating various disaster operations , including the typhoons and landslides that ravaged the country . <p> " Last year , this operations center served as the rallying point and coordination center of all efforts to assist our countrymen imperiled by the hazards of natural disasters , " he added . <p> During the thanksgiving ceremony , Lorenzana and Jalad presented a plaque of appreciation to the representatives from different private donors and government agencies which provided support in the establishment of the NDRRMC Operations Center . q! q! q! q! q! q!
