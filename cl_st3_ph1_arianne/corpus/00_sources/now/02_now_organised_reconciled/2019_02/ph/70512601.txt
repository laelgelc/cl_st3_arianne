<p> Environment and Natural Resources Secretary Roy Cimatu urged governors from climate vulnerable provinces to fully implement a program that would help protect communities from climate-induced disasters in the future . <p> " Climate change is already here , and it will be here for the next generations . It is now , therefore , our obligation to take this program for implementation , so that the next generations will have a guide , " Cimatu said . <p> DENR Secretary Roy Cimatu ( DENR / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> He made the appeal during the planning and convergence budgeting on the Risk Resiliency Program ( RRP ) for the eight priority climate vulnerable provinces held at the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) Central Office in Quezon City . <p> These provinces are vulnerable on their high susceptibility to climate hazards such as flooding , rainfall-induced landslide , storm surge and drought . <p> The DENR chief , who chairs the Cabinet Cluster on Climate Change Adaptation , Mitigation and Disaster Risk Reduction ( CCAM-DRR ) , told governors that the decisions they make today will have lasting consequences for future generations . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This will be the template of the younger generations to achieve what we intend to do , what the national government intends to do with this program , " Cimatu said . <p> The RRP is a convergence program of the Cabinet Cluster on CCAM-DRR . It envisions strengthening the resilience of natural systems and the adaptive capacities of vulnerable groups and communities to curb future risks and disasters . <p> Aside from Cimatu and the governors of eight priority climate vulnerable provinces , the meeting was attended by representatives from the Department of Agriculture , National Economic Development Authority , Department of Public Works and Highways , and Department of the Interior and Local Government . <p> " We need to increase our efforts in resiliency and strongly urge renewed cooperation from key national government agencies with our concerned provincial governments . It should be a collective undertaking , and in doing so , we can create great solutions together , " Cimatu said . <p> At the meeting , provincial governors presented the current situation of climate vulnerabilities in their respective jurisdictions . <p> The meeting was intended @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and reinforce government thrust for a more prudent and targeted spending by fostering collaboration among agencies in planning , budgeting and implementing priority resilience programs and projects in the said provinces . <p> Also discussed during the meeting was the CCAM-DRR Roadmap for 2018 to 2022 , which serves as " an important guide for the implementing agencies in the programs and projects to mainstream climate change and disaster risk reduction in a vision to build adaptive and resilient communities . " <p> The Roadmap also aims to establish climate-resilient communities and their enhancement in the 17 climate vulnerable provinces , and major urban centers such as Metro Manila , Cebu , Iloilo and Davao. q! q! q! q! q! q!
