<h> ' PHL must spend more for social protection ' <p> THE Philippines needs to spend an average of 3.48 to 5.58 percent of its GDP to fill its social protection gaps up to 2030 , according to estimates by the Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) . <p> In a report , titled " Asia 's Fiscal Challenge : Financing the Social Protection Agenda of the Sustainable Development Goals SDGs , " the ADB said addressing the fiscal challenges in providing social protection is key to achieving Agenda 2030 . <p> " Many countries still face considerable challenges in creating the sustainable financing needed for their social-welfare program , the bedrock for the success of the social protection agenda under the SDGs , " said Woochong Um , director general of the ADB 's Sustainable Development and Climate Change Department . <p> The ADB made estimates based on low and high assumptions .
