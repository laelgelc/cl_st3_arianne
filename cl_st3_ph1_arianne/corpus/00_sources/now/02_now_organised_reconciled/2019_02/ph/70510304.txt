<h> Morningstar unveils indices to track carbon footprint of firms <p> Morningstar , Inc. , a provider of independent investment research , has introduced a set of indices that track the carbon footprint of companies , aimed at helping investors gauge how the firms are addressing concerns about climate change . <p> The new Morningstar Low Carbon Risk Index Family reflect not only climate-related risks but also exhibit attractive investment attributes , Morningstar says in a statement on January 31 . <p> Carbon footprint refers to the amount of carbon dioxide released into the atmosphere as a result of the activities of an individual , organisation or community . <p> According to Sanjay Arya , Morningstar 's head of indexes , the new benchmarks will " empower investors to evaluate and invest in companies that are adapting to the low-carbon economy and managing their businesses strategically for the long term " . <p> " Whether motivated by environmental concerns , fiduciary obligations or investment outcomes , I believe the new indices offer more options to lower carbon exposure without compromising returns , " he says in the statement . <p> The indices are carbon research on more than 4,000 companies across 130 industry groups . <p> " These indices go beyond the common approach of carbon footprinting , which reflects current emissions and is just a starting point for analysis of carbon risk , " Dan Lefkovitz , a strategist for Morningstar Indexes , says in the statement . <p> The indices are based on the 12-month trailing averages of both the
