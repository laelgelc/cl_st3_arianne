<h> DAR , partners plant 2,000 mangroves in E.B. Magalona <p> NEGROS . Personnel of Department of Agrarian Reform-Negros Occidental with Department of Environment and Natural Resources personnel , barangay officials and staff , and student-volunteers during the mangrove planting activity held at the coastline of Barangay Tomongtong in E.B. Magalona town on Tuesday , February 26 . ( Contributed photo ) <p> + <p> AA <p> - <p> February 27 , 2019 <p> THE Department of Agrarian Reform ( DAR ) in Negros Occidental-North , in partnership with the City Environment and Natural Resources ( Cenro ) -Bago City , Municipal Government of E.B. Magalona and Tomongtong Elementary School , planted at least 2,000 mangrove propagules at the coastline of Barangay Tomongtong in the said town on Tuesday , February 26 . <p> The 120 participants are composed of DAR and Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) employees , barangay officials , and students-volunteers , among others . <p> The event was in support to the Expanded National Greening Program of the government . <p> Mangrove is among the commodities that was given prominence in the country 's campaign to combat climate change . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ awareness on the preservation and conservation of the environment because " we only have one planet earth -- one world . It is our earth . Our home . " <p> Punong Barangay Haydiginia Bendicion , for her part , thanked all who participated in the activity . <p> Some of the mangrove saplings planted previously were swept by the water thus , there 's a need for rehabilitation , she said . <p> Cenro-Bago Forest Extension Officer Dave Labinghisa , meanwhile , mentioned the commendable strong partnership between the DAR and DENR in the province . <p> Labinghisa said the DENR lacks funding for activities like planting of trees . But in partnership with other agencies , such initiatives will continue to thrive .
