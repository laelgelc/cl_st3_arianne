<h> No measles outbreak , says Davao mayor <p> DAVAO City Mayor Sara Duterte-Carpio said there is no measles outbreak in the city after the city government was relentless in its effort to vaccinate the children during last year 's outbreak . <p> " We have cases but it 's not outbreak level because if you can remember we have outbreak last year so what the City health Office ( CHO ) did was they strengthened their vaccination on children who were not yet vaccinated , " Duterte-Carpio said Friday , February 8 . <p> The Davao City Epidemiological Surveillance Unit ( CESU ) recorded 22 deaths because of measles , an infection locally known as tigdas , from January to September 2018 . <p> Measles is a virus that can thrive in a country where climate change is evident , and that it infects most when temperature levels are low . <p> One of the problems that are seen is the low turnout of children being vaccinated against measles because of the Dengvaxia scare . <p> " We can not force parents to have their children vaccinated . Their fear has basis because there were children who were vaccinated by Dengvaxia who died because of this ) , " she said . <p> What they do now is they continue their information dissemination to inform the public that the vaccine for measles and other common vaccines are safe for children and also to raise awareness about the importance of immunization . <p> In a statement , CHO head Josephine Villafuerte also urged the public to be vaccinated for their own protection . <p> " When one in the family has measles , everyone will get measles so we are urging the public , especially those who have not been immunized , to go to their health centers to get vaccinated , " Villafuerte said . <p> She said that the vaccine is free-of-charge and there are enough vaccines for everyone . <p> " All of the health centers in Davao are offering measles immunization for children , even those out of the target age . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " she added . <p> Villafuerte also revealed that cases of measles in Davao City went down in January this year compared to the same period last year . <p> " As of February 4 this year , we have recorded only 45 cases of measles in Davao City . It has significantly dropped considering that January last year , we had 141 cases , " she said . <p> The CHO head also emphasized that the vaccines are proven and tested to be safe . <p> " We have been using the measles vaccine for a long time , so it 's already been proven safe and effective , " Villafuerte said .
