Agenda of the SDGs , " surveyed the fiscal situation of 16 countries in the region and identified the challenges they face in building and expanding their social welfare programs . <p> ADB said the Philippines , have around 20 percent of their population living below the national poverty line , thus necessitating greater spending power for social welfare programs . <p> Other than the
