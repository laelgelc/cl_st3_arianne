These devastating flood events 2015 caused a huge loss to lives , cultivated lands and other property of people of Pakistan along with wasting precious water into sea every year . <p> As per basis to store flood water which will not only control damage but also help meet needs during dry years ( droughts ) by providing stored water . <p> This will also help to manage energy crises by
