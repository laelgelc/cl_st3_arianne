<h> German govt calls for stronger nature protection in energy transition <p> BERLIN : The German Federal Agency for Nature Conservation ( BfN ) called for a stronger consideration of nature protection in regard to the expansion of renewable energies in Germany , a report published by the BfN on Wednesday shows . <p> " The energy transition must take place in a way that is compatible with nature and the environment , " said Beate Jessel , president of the German nature conservation agency BfN . <p> The expansion of renewable energies was " absolutely necessary " to achieve Germany 's climate protection goals as well as to protect species and habitats from the " consequences of climate change " , Jessel added . <p> However , the high number and " wide geographical distribution " of renewable energy plants would contribute to changes in land use and landscape as well as creating risks for certain species and their habitats , according to BfN .
