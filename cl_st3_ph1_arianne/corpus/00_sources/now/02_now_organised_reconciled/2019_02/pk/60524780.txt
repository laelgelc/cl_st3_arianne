<h> Govt to plant 150m trees in three months , says PM 's adviser <p> ISLAMABAD : Prime Minister 's Adviser on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam on Friday said the government would plant 150 million trees in the next three months . <p> " We are fulfilling the promise of making Pakistan clean and green . We request people to take full part in the tree plantation campaign , " Malik Amin Aslam said during a visit to a land in Lahore where the trees would be planted . <p> The federal government has completed preparations for ' Plant for Pakistan ' campaign . Prime Minister Imran Khan will inaugurate the campaign at Balloki area near Lahore . <p> He said that 2,500 acres of land had been retrieved from land mafia in the city . " For the first time in the history of Pakistan a land is reclaimed to grow it into a jungle by planting trees , " he said . <p> The jungle would eradicate the issue of smog-like weather in Lahore in the future , he said . <p> The Lahore High Court ( LHC ) on January 31 sought details of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hearing of a petition on non-plantation of trees in Punjab , Lahore Development Authority ( LDA ) director general , Punjab Highway Authority ( PHA ) director general and officials of other relevant institutions turned up before the court . <p> The PHA DG had informed the court that Rs 27 billion were required for plantation of trees .
