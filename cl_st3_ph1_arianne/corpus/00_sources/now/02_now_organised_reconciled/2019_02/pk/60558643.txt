<h> FAO Resource Centre inaugurated at Sindh Agriculture University <h> Pakistan <p> TANDO JAM : With an aim to engage and support Pakistani youth associated with agriculture , the FAO Resource Centre was inaugurated at Sindh Agriculture University Tando Jam . <p> Mina Dowlatchahi FAO Representative in Pakistan and Professor Dr Mujeeb-uddin Memon Sahrai unveiled the plaque in the presence of senior faculty members of the university , students and FAO delegation . <p> As a result of collaboration with the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations ( FAO ) , this center will promote the use of latest technology through development of strong relationship with farmer groups , researchers and academia . <p> Speaking at the ceremony , Mina Dowlatchahi FAO Representative in Pakistan said " FAO looks forward to a fruitful and long collaboration to support youth development and unlocking the potential of the agriculture sector in Sindh . This center will improve delivery to agri-sector and help encourage combined investment and strategies for sustainable agriculture development in the province " . <p> Graduates will be facilitated at
