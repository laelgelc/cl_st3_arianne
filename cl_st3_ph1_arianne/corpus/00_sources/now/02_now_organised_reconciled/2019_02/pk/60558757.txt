<h> Rs319.3bn released under PSDP 2018-19 <p> ISLAMABAD : The federal government has released Rs319.3 billion against the total allocation of Rs675 billion under its Public Sector Development Programme ( PSDP ) 2018-19 for various ongoing and new schemes . <p> The released funds include Rs136.9 billion for federal ministries , Rs137.231 billion for corporations , and Rs25.963 billion for special areas , according to latest data released by Ministry of Planning , Development and Reform . <p> Out of these allocations , the government released Rs123.618 billion for National Highway Authority out of total allocation of Rs185.2 billion , whereas Rs13.613 billion have been released for National Transmission and Despatch Company ( NTDC ) and PEPCO for which an amount of Rs33.36 billion was allocated under PSDP 2018-19 . <p> Similarly , Rs6.33 billion have been released for Communication Division ( other than National Highway Authority ) for which the government has earmarked Rs13.97 billion under PSDP 2018-19 . <p> Railways Division received Rs16.99 billion out of its total allocation of Rs28.06 billion , whereas Aviation Division received Rs0.742 billion out of total allocation of Rs3.65 billion . <p> The government also released an amount of Rs18.53 billion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ total allocation of Rs30.9 billion , while Rs18.22 billion have been released for the Pakistan Nuclear Regulatory Authority out of its total allocations of Rs0.285 billion . <p> An amount of Rs2.228 billion has been released for Finance Division out of its total allocations of Rs12.34
