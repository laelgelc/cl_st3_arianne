<p> Federal Minister for Communications and Postal Services , Murad Saeed said on Tuesday that the government was initiating pragmatic steps for plantation of saplings along national highways and motorways under Clean and Green Pakistan project . <p> He said this while addressing a signing ceremony of memorandum of understanding ( MoU ) between Ministry of Communications and Ministry of Climate Change . <p> The MoU will help implement the project " Apni Shahrah " to establish plantation in the Right of Way of motorways and national highways in the country . <p> The minister said that as per vision of Prime Minister Imran Khan , 10 billion trees would be planted under Clean and Green Pakistan campaign and that signing of the MoU between Ministry of Communications and Ministry of Climate Change was a step forward to that direction , under which plantation would be done along motorways and national highways throughout the country . <p> Besides completing ongoing schemes , he desired to undertake new road building projects on build-and-operate basis .
