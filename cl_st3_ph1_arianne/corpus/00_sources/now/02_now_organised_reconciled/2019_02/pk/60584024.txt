<h> Climate crossroads <h> Opinion <p> Sixty-one years ago , Dr Martin Luther King declared , " Today the choice is no longer between violence and nonviolence . It is either nonviolence or nonexistence " . Emboldened by the success of the Montgomery Bus Boycott two years earlier , King saw nonviolence not only as a powerful strategy for achieving social change ; he viewed it as a philosophy and way of life that gave the world its only genuine alternative to the doomsday scenarios posed by the cold war arms race . As he said , " In a day when Sputniks and Explorers dash through outer space and guided ballistic missiles are carving highways of death through the stratosphere , nobody can win a war " . <p> Today , as efforts to control nuclear proliferation appear to be unravelling or failing , and as countries like the US and Russia are engaging in a newly intensified arms race , Dr King 's words carry new urgency .
