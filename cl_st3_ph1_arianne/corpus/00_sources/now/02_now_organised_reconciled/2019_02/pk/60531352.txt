<h> PTI govt working for human investment , tourism promotion : State Minister <p> LAHORE : Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul has said Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) government was working for betterment of various sectors including human investment , tourism , youth and culture . <p> She was talking to the media after inaugurating an exhibition , held to pay tributes to Syed Sadequain Ahmad Naqvi , renowned calligrapher and painter , on his 32nd death anniversary . <p> She said , " Sadequain do not need any recognition but when we talk about him , we get recognized . " Sadequain 's painting , Arz-o-Samawat , his murals , his illustrations of classical Urdu literature , especially the poetry of Ghalib , Iqbal and Faiz on canvas needed to be projected at international level , she added . <p> Lahore Museum is best place for today 's event as it would help revive museum as well , she added . <p> The state minister said that youngsters should visit museum as it was the best way to get familiar with art and history of the country . <p> " A nation , which forgets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , it becomes tongueless " , she added . <p> Zartaj further said that artists were our face , our identity as they project Pakistan through their work .
