<p> A peaceful , secure and tension-free Indian Ocean region is in Pakistan 's lingering strategic interest , as it was in the 1980s when India acted as a proxy of the then Soviet Union . And , now as the United States returns to the Indian Ocean region with a view to checkmating China - which is said to be turning into a soft superpower - India acts as a proxy of the United States . In today 's Indian Ocean there is the big power rivalry to acquire commercial ports and military facilities . Pakistan wo n't like to act as anybody 's proxy , though it does have substantial stakes in a secure and tension-free Indian Ocean . As Admiral Amjad Khan Niazi said on the eve of five-day multinational naval exercise Aman-2019 , Pakistan 's extraordinary dependence on the seas for trade , the CPEC project and its strategic location astride the global energy highway warrant that this body of water remains safe and crime-free . Rightly then Pakistan played host to the Aman-2019 , which is sixth in sequence and attended by 45 countries .
