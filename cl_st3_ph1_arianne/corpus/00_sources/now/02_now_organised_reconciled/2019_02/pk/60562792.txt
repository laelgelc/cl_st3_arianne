<h> Civil Service Reforms : the CSS examination and initial training <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan emphasized the " depoliticization of bureaucracy " in his very first address to the nation . Since then he has twice addressed the civil servants assuring them of political non-interference in their working . There is no doubt that the bureaucracy in Pakistan is largely functioning as an arm of the political masters rather than as a pillar of the state . Hence , the high priority accorded by the PM to an improvement in affairs has been welcomed by all sane quarters . PTI has often reiterated the KP Police " depoliticization model " as its signature achievement . The model revolves around the vigilance by a strong and efficient Police Inspector General and ensuring that police officers are not moved from their postings before fixed tenures . This may have improved the working of the police in the short-term .
