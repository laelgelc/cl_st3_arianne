The two organizations have issues , which need to be redressed , " he said . <p> He said the statutory notification ( SRO ) for non-biodegradable plastic products had been imposed in the federal capital , which was not fully implemented . <p> A ministry official @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ committee that the plastic industry was the fifth largest revenue generating source of the country .
