<p> ISLAMABAD : National Assembly Standing Committee on Climate change in a meeting held here on Tuesday unanimously elected MNA Munaza Hassan as its Chairperson . The Special Secretary , National Assembly Secretariat welcomed the Members and briefed about the procedure for election of the Chairman/Chairperson . <p> MNA Rubina Irfan proposed the name of MNA Munaza Hassan as Chairperson of the Committee while MNA Shaista Pervaiz seconded her candidature . <p> The Special Secretary , National Assembly Secretariat , congratulated the newly elected Chairman on behalf of the Speaker , Secretary National Assembly and assured her full support from the Secretariat for effective and efficient functioning of the Committee , a press release said . <p> The Chairperson expressed her gratitude to the members of the committee for posing confidence in her and assured them to run the business of the Committee with consensus . <p> The Members while appreciating views of the Chairperson assured her their fullest support in the process of legislation . q! q! q! q! q! q!
