The phenomenon led to many farmers either cultivating cotton and rice late , or not cultivating at all due to water uncertainty . This affected livelihoods in almost all the districts . Leading grower bodies have demanded the provincial government of Sindh to declare Badin district as a calamity-hit area . They want the government to announce package for farmers and people , as the communities dependent on agriculture-related occupations were facing hardships .
