This year 's theme , ' Wetlands and Climate Change ' , aimed at making people , especially youngsters , aware of the effects of climate change on wetlands and coastal communities . <p> Different organisations , including the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) , the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) and other non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) and community-based organisations ( CBOs ) organised different events . <p> These events included awareness activities , seminars , talks and walks , in which the fisherfolk community and their leaders , as well as teachers and students of the coastal community schools participated in large numbers .
