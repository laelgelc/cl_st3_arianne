<p> UPDATED -- Sunday , February 3rd Nissan of Japan has confirmed that the new X-Trail SUV will now not be built at its U.K. plant in Washington , Tyne and Wear . On Saturday , Sky News reported that Brexit was one of the reasons for the switch . <p> A Nissan spokesperson said the company would not comment on rumor and speculation . However , today a statement has been issued by Nissan Europe , and this confirmed that production of the X-Trail will remain in Japan and will not also be shared at the Washington plant . <p> Nissan Europe chairman Gianluca de Ficchy said : " While we have taken this decision for business reasons , the continued uncertainty around the UK 's future relationship with the EU is not helping companies like ours to plan for the future . " <p> Washington is close to Sunderland , the first city to announce voting results for the 2016 referendum on Brexit -- Sunderland voted to Leave by a large margin despite the many warnings both before and after the referendum that car manufacturing in the region would be affected . <p> Nissan employs 7,755 workers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 30,000 supply chain jobs in the region and elsewhere in the U.K. The company generated ? 6.4 billion from sales of cars from its U.K. plant in 2017 . <p> While no jobs losses have been announced today the loss of X-Trail production puts the long-term future of the Washington plant in doubt and the extra jobs that would have been created by building the SUV will now not arrive on Wearside . <p> More than half of the cars made in Washington are exported to the E.U. <p> Ironically , the E.U. recently signed a free trade agreement with Japan , but the U.K. will only be able to benefit from this FTA while it is still in the European Union .
