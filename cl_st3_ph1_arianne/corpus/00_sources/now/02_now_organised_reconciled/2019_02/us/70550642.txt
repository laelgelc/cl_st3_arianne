<h> HARP SEAL FACTS <p> *THE HARP SEAL is found in waters of the Arctic and far north Atlantic Ocean , although some migrate south along the northeast U.S. coast in winter. * THE SEAL derives its name from the horseshoe- or harp-shaped pattern on the back and sides of adults of the species. *HARP SEALS are carnivores , eating fish and crustaceans . They can weigh as much as 400 pounds and are agile swimmers . Their average life span in the wild is 20 years . The creatures spend most of their life at sea and around snow and ice. * HARP SEALS rely on the availability of sea ice as a haul-out platform for giving birth , nursing pups , and molting and , therefore , are sensitive to changes in the environment that affect the timing and extent of sea ice formation and breakup . They also eat snow and ice to stay hydrated. * HARP SEALS have historically been hunted in Canada .
