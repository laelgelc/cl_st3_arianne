My librarian mother taught us to love learning . My father , a shipyard worker , put in overtime and extra shifts ; and they made sure we volunteered to help others . When we asked why he 'd given away his only jacket , Dad turned to us and said , " I knew when I left that man , he 'd still be alone . But I could give him my coat , because I knew you were coming for me . " <p> Our power and strength as Americans lives in our hard work and our belief in more .
