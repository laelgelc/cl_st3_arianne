This long-awaited service fulfills the company 's self-imposed deadline to start a commercial ride-share service by the end of 2018 . Waymo , Google 's self-driving car project , has been operating in the Phoenix metropolitan area since 2016 and is an industry leader in autonomous vehicles , with over 10 million miles driven on public roads , and nearly 7 billion miles driven in simulations . The launch of Waymo One does come with limitations . <p> It is only available to existing Waymo early-riders , a program which has allowed about 400 people to use Waymo ride-share since 2017 while the company continued to test its technology . The only thing that really has changed is that these early-riders must pay for the ride-sharing service .
