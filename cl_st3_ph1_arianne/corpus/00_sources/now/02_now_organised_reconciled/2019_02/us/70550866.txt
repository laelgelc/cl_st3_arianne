<h> Millions of fish have been dying in Australia 's major rivers <p> Fish have been struggling to breathe and dying by the millions on the banks of Australia 's largest river system . Experts say that without serious change , it will continue to happen . <p> Poor management , excess upstream irrigation and drought led to three mass deaths of endangered fish species during December and January in the Murray-Darling Basin . These deaths included Murray cod fish that were decades old , according to an investigation by the Australian Academy of Science that was published last week . <p> Craig Moritz at the Australian National University in Canberra , who chaired the investigation , says the sight of millions of dead fish should be a wake-up call .
