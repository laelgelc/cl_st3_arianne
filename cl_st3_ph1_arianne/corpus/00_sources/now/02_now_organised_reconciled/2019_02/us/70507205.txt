If emissions continue unabated throughout the 21st century , the climate of North American urban areas will become , on average , most like the contemporary climate of locations about 500 miles away and mainly to the south . In the eastern U.S. , nearly all urban areas , including Boston , New York , and Philadelphia , will become most similar to contemporary climates to the south and southwest .
