<p> Four protesters were charged Wednesday with tampering with an Enbridge pipeline site in Itasca County earlier this week . <p> Appearing in Itasca County District Court on the charges were Brenna Anglada , 40 , of Cuba City , Wis. ; Michele Naar , 62 , of Duluth ; Allyson Polman , 26 , of Denton , Texas ; and Daniel Yildirim , 32 , of Viroqua , Wis . They were all released from jail and are scheduled to return to court on Feb. 19 . Each of them face counts of felony aiding and abetting attempted damage to pipeline property and misdemeanor aiding and abetting attempted criminal damage to property . <p> A spokeswoman for the group said it was her understanding that the protesters contacted Enbridge before turning the valves for safety reasons and to give the company the opportunity to turn the valves off . The protesters , wearing hard hats and safety vests , initially claimed on their Facebook page that Enbridge 's Line 4 had been off for 45 minutes .
