Her crusade includes changing consumer behavior -- beginning with her own -- in an effort to not only reduce waste today , but also to get companies to offer more products in reusable containers in order to slow the waste of the future . <p> No statistics exist to show how widespread the individual fight against single-use plastics has become . A 25-cent fee on disposable cups is intended to encourage customers to bring their own . <p> If the world is changing , it 's not yet changed .
