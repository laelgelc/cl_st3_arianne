<h> Rising Temperatures Could Melt Most Himalayan Glaciers by 2100 <p> NEW DELHI -- Rising temperatures in the Himalayas , home to most of the world 's tallest mountains , will melt at least one-third of the region 's glaciers by the end of the century even if the world 's most ambitious climate change targets are met , according to a report released Monday . <p> If those goals are not achieved , and global warming and greenhouse gas emissions continue at their current rates , the Himalayas could lose two-thirds of its glaciers by 2100 , according to the report , the Hindu Kush Himalaya Assessment . <p> Under those more dire circumstances , the Himalayas could heat up by 8 degrees Fahrenheit ( 4.4 degrees Celsius ) by century 's end , bringing radical disruptions to food and water supplies , and mass population displacement . <p> Glaciers in the Hindu Kush Himalayan Region , which spans over 2,000 miles of Asia , provide water resources to around a quarter of the world 's population . <p> " This is a climate crisis you have not heard of , " said Philippus Wester , a lead @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the region , already one of the world 's most fragile and hazard-prone mountain regions , will range from worsened air pollution to an increase in extreme weather events .
