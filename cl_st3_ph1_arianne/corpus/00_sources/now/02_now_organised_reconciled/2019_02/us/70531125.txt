<h> Editorial : Signs of life , struggle in region 's economy <p> ACCORDING TO Old Dominion University economists , the Hampton Roads economy crossed an important threshold in the last year when the number of jobs in the region finally surpassed employment prior to the Great Recession . <p> The fact that such a milestone is notable for our region speaks volumes about the grim economic landscape here in recent years . <p> After all , that nationwide downturn began in late 2007 and accelerated throughout 2008 , when the global economy appeared on the brink of collapse . Recovery began shortly after , and economists point to its official end in 2009 . <p> The effects of that calamity lingered for considerably longer . In many places -- Hampton Roads , for instance -- recovery progressed in fits and starts , and the economy of today is in many ways considerably different from that before the financial system crashed . <p> Nationwide , employment reached its pre-recession threshold in the summer of 2014 , so the Hampton Roads figures offer evidence that recovery has been uneven and region-specific . That should set off alarm bells in board rooms , government meeting rooms and living rooms across the area , as it underscores the need to transform Hampton Roads to broaden the employment base and develop a healthier , more dynamic economy . <p> That 's easier said than done , of course .
