These adaptations , however , help the fish survive , said Postlethwait , professor emeritus of biology and member of the UO 's Institute of Neuroscience . <p> The icefishes , he said , are examples of what Charles Darwin called the " wrecks of ancient life . Among genes that disappeared amid the months of night and months of sunlight in the polar region were those tied to circadian rhythms .
