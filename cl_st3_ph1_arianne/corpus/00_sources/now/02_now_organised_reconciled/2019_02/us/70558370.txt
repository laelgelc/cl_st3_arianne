<h> 200 block of Cayuga Street will be closed for months after ' significant damage ' <p> ITHACA , N.Y . -- After a water main break this weekend , the 200 block of North Cayuga Street will need to be completely rebuilt and the work will likely take until May , the City of Ithaca says . <p> The water main break occurred Saturday on the 200 block of North Cayuga Street between Buffalo and Seneca streets and caused " significant damage to the road , " the city said in an update Wednesday . When the 89-year-old pipe broke , it washed out support materials for the water and gas lines . <p> " Unfortunately , it has become clear that to avoid further damage to the road and utility lines , and to prevent damage to vehicles , a complete rebuild is necessary before traffic can be allowed back on that section of road , " the city said . <p> The city has more than 90 miles of underground water pipes , and a third of them are more than 100 years old , and some are approaching 150 years old .
