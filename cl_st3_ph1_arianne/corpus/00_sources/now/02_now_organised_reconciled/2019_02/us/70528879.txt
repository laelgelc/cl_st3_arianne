<h> Will Durkan 's ' city of the future ' vision include a more affordable Seattle ? -- UPDATE <p> Seattle Mayor Jenny Durkan will make her second State of the City address Tuesday . Among respondents who identified themselves as homeowners , around 48% disapproved or strongly disapproved of her performance in the first year of her administration . The number jumped to 62% among those who identified as renters . <p> The city 's homelessness crisis , high rents , nearly impossible real estate market , and spiraling cost of living likely will remain an issue for Durkan throughout her term .
