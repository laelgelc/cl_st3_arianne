The Caribbean Climate Fund was established by Harding 's Hyperion Equity after completing his contract as CEO of the Caribbean Climate Smart Accelerator , which is now run by their recently appointed CEO , Racquel Moses from Trinidad . <p> Harding , in his former post with the Accelerator , facilitated the implementation of a water harvesting technology that has effectively taken a Jamaican children 's hospital off the water grid .
