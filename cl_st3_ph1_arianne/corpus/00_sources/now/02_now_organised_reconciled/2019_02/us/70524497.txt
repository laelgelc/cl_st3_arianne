We believe that communities in the modern West have more in common than not , and that a deeper understanding of the myriad issues confronting the region will help us strive for something better . <p> The West is facing a number of unique ( and not-so-unique ) challenges . Hate crimes in the West rose 20 percent from 2016 to 2017 , and a bewildering number of plants and animals face mass extinction .
