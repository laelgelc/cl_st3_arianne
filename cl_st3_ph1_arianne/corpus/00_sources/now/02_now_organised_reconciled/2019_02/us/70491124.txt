<h> Anderson : Recognize the potential of natural gas <h> Thursday <p> The City of Eugene 's idea to increase the NW Natural franchise fee in hope " to regulate natural gas to curb the emission of heat-trapping greenhouse gases " is an ill-conceived money grab . <p> NW Natural customers ' use of natural gas in homes and buildings account for approximately 5 percent of total greenhouse gases emitted in the state . That 's nothing to sneeze at , but what do you really gain if you " provide some relief for low- and middle-income households impacted by any new regulation , " as Councilor Claire Syrett stated ? Do n't low- and middle-income households make up the majority of the homes in our community ? On top of that , what is the cost to administer " relief ? " Again , what is the gain ? <p> If the City of Eugene wanted to address greenhouse gases , they might want to look at the elephant in the room . According to the Eugene Community Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory Report , dated July 2007 , " Over half of the community 's current GHG emissions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of total emissions ) and diesel fuel ( 11 percent ) . " The same inventory in February 2017 reports that the largest single source of emissions in our community is still transportation , at 32 percent of total emissions . These are derived primarily from burning gas and diesel fuel . <p> Here , natural gas ( methane ) can help reduce our creation of greenhouse gases . Natural gas in the form of compressed natural gas creates significantly less greenhouse gases than gasoline and diesel when burned in an internal combustion engine . CNG can be utilized in such an engine with little adaptation . <p> Current technologies provide adequate engines to power the family car or the largest 18-wheeler down the road . CNG as a transportation fuel has made substantial gains in the light , medium and heavy-duty fleets as opposed to electric vehicles , which have limited cargo capacities due to the immense weight of their batteries . <p> The best candidates to initially benefit from CNG are fleets that stay relatively close to home and achieve low miles-per-gallon fuel consumption . Prime candidates include @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ less-than-full-load trucks ( UPS , Federal Express ) ; airport shuttles ; taxis ; telecommunication vans etc . These are proven uses : the Los Angeles Metro has over 2,250 natural gas buses in use . Refuse hauler Waste Management has more than 6,500 natural gas garbage trucks in use . UPS has 3,775 CNG vehicles in its fleet . AT&amp;T has more than 8,000 . <p> When we start using renewable natural gas in our system -- methane byproducts created at sewage treatment facilities and landfills -- the reduction in greenhouse gas emissions will get much larger . On top of that , natural gas costs about 30 percent less than gasoline and diesel as a transportation fuel . Even though Eugene is one of a few communities in the state where natural gas is available for transportation purposes , the City has done nothing to take advantage of this opportunity . <p> Climate change is real and a significant threat to the way of life for our children and grandchildren to come . The time to act is now . Every day we delay reducing our use of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ already done to our atmosphere . <p> NW Natural 's network of pipes in our community are an incredible asset we should appreciate . Whether it is heating our homes or powering vehicles on the road , natural gas is and will be a valuable energy resource for decades to come . As renewable gases become viable and available , NW Natural 's network of pipes will be invaluable to the utilization of those energies . <p> Our city leaders should view NW Natural as a partner in addressing greenhouse gases , not an adversary .
