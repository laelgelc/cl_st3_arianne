<h> Canada 's clean tech industry on the rise after decades of investment <p> Canada has emerged as a clean technology leader , with 12 firms on the Global Cleantech 100 list . The list , published by industry monitor Cleantech Group , identifies firms most likely to make a significant environmental and economic impact in the next 5 -- 10 years . <p> Details : Canada 's clean tech sector has grown through investment from both industry and government . Sustainable Development Technology Canada , an arm's-length fund of the federal government that finances clean technology demonstration projects , has seeded the sector with $1.36 billion in funding since 2001 . <p> Support for the sector was maintained even as other developing technologies were attracting more investment . <p> Private venture capital is also expressing renewed interest : $365 million was invested in Canadian clean tech companies in the first half of 2018 , double the amount in the same period of 2017 . <p> Yes , but : Even as last year 's UN report highlighted the urgency and scale of climate change , carbon emissions rose 3% in 2018 .
