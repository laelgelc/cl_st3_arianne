<h> World Wetlands Day 2019 : Importance and Theme of the Day For Nature Conservation and Solution For Climate Change <p> World Wetlands Day ( WWD ) is observed on February 2nd every year to raise awareness about the importance of wetlands on the planet . February 2nd marks the date of adoption of the Convention on Wetlands at 1971 Ramsar Convention in Iran . Ever since , the day is annually celebrated on February 2nd based on a new theme on the importance of loss of wetlands and the need to conserve it . The theme for World Wetlands Day 2019 is ' Wetlands and climate change ' highlighting the role of wetlands as a natural solution to reduce the impact of climate change . <p> A wetland is a water ecosystem either permanently or seasonally . What distinguishes wetlands from other land forms or water bodies is the vegetation of aquatic plants that are adapted to the unique hydric soil .
