Photograph : The Siberian Times <p> Russia is battling an unprecedented invasion of polar bears on a remote Arctic archipelago that is home to the country 's most northerly military base . Known locally as " Lords of the Arctic " , the wild animals have been terrorising the population on Novaya Zemlya , a group of mountainous islands off Russia 's northern coast once used as a testing ground for atomic bombs . <p> " People are scared , afraid to leave their homes , daily routines are being broken , and parents are unwilling to let their children go to school and kindergartens , " according to Alexander Minaev , the deputy head of the local government in Novaya Zemlya .
