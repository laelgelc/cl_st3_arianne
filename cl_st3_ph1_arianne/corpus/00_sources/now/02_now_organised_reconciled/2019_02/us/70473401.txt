<p> The Thwaites Glacier on Antarctica 's western coast has long been considered one of the most unstable on the continent . Now , scientists are worried about the discovery of an enormous underwater cavity that will probably speed up the glacier 's decay . <p> The cavity is about two-thirds the area of Manhattan and nearly 1,000 feet tall , according to a study released Wednesday by NASA 's Jet Propulsion Laboratory .
