<h> Letter : Address terror of climate change <p> By Diane Meisenhelter , Portland <p> Published : February 24 , 2019 , 6:00 AM <p> Finally , there is a resolution to address the terrors of climate change that at the same time will create millions of green jobs . Polls by Data for Progress and Yale and George Mason University Climate Change Communication programs show vast majorities of Democrats of all ages and a majority of Republicans nationwide support the concept . <p> This has unnerved Republican leaders who are trying to stop this momentum by pushing for a quick vote within the month , possibly without expert testimony or even letting the bill be fully developed . Americans know that the U.N . Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change report last October said we have only 12 years to transform our economy to prevent catastrophic changes and that now is the time to mobilize for a clean energy , nonfossil fuel and non-nuclear economy .
