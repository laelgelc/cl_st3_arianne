The article contains several completely unchallenged claims against the private certification industry that reflect a failure to understand the legal role and functions of certifiers . Private certifiers are public officials , and often local small businesses , who pride themselves on acting in the best interests of the public . Their responsibilities and obligations are the same as for certifiers employed by local government .
