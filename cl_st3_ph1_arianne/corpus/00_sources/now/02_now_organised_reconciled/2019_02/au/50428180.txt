<p> What are your dinner table conversations about the wild weather we are experiencing ? The metres of rain in Townsville ? The unstoppable bushfires in Tasmania ? The record temperatures across Australia ? Or the fish kills in the drying Murray Darling River system ? Talking with family , I find opinion is divided . Some remember the drought of ' 45 , the Cygna grounding of ' 75 , the Pasha Bulker of ' 07 and say nothing has changed . But for me , the climate is rapidly changing . The unique rainforest , button grass plains and heathlands of the southwestern Tasmania wildlands have begun to vanish . Where they can , birds and other animals are moving to new locations - spare a thought for the alpine pygmy possum of the Snowy Mountains .
