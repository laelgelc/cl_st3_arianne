We 've seen multiple enquiries around the world -- from the United Kingdom probes and reforms travelling in different directions internationally , to the global rethink on the entire audit standards model . There 's been more attention on oversight and inspections than ever before . <p> What does all this heightened interest truly mean ? While some are quick to call this a crisis of how auditors are performing , there is a much deeper context here .
