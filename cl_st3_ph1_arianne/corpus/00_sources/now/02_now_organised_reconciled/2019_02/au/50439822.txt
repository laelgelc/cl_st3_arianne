The question is , can the same be done in March this year for the seats of Lismore and Tweed ? <p> Your final day to enrol to vote for the next state election is Monday , March 4 with polling day set for Saturday March 23 -- and your vote could make all the difference . <p> After the 2015 election the Nationals led in the Lismore electorate by 53 per cent to 47 per cent , a 2,345 vote advantage , and in Tweed the Nationals also led 53 per cent to 47 per cent but with a 2,577 vote advantage .
