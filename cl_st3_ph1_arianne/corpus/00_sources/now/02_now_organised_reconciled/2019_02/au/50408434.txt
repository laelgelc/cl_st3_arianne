<p> So we thought January 2019 was hot . Well we had best believe it . According to the statistics that I have been able to research , January 2019 's mean maximum temperature ( 31.2 ) and mean minimum temperature ( 20.3 ) well and truly exceeded recent overall mean recordings ( 27.7 and 18.4 respectively ) . This is for records since 1995 at Port Macquarie Airport and mean temperatures for records dating back to 1907 ( 25.7 and 18.3 ) . It also exceeded January 2018 ( 28.4 and 18.4 ) in both categories . Personally I do not believe I have ever felt the effects of heat as much as I have this January . As a teenager I would spend all day at the beach in school holidays and , in later days , enjoy a beach dip whenever possible . To me there is nothing more exhilarating than cracking a good body wave and riding it for 20 metres or so . But not this year . I can only suggest that those sceptics regarding climate change take a good look at these figures and perhaps be persuaded that climate change is a reality . What is causing climate change ? Whilst I do not claim to have the experience to say I am very inclined to believe the vast majority of learned researchers that blame it on human activity , especially the burning of coal . This letter is therefore a plea from the heart to our politicians to open their eyes and minds to what is happening to our planet . They need to take whatever action that needs to be done is done to ensure that we leave a liveable planet for our children , grandchildren and beyond .
