<p> THE latest offer by one of the supermarket chains of a 10-cents-per-litre increase in the cost of milk is an insult to our dairy farmers , although I would suggest that they would welcome any additional income regardless of the size . Let 's get real about this , some people are prepared to spend between $6 and $8 on a schooner of beer and be content to do so , but ask them to spend $1.10 on a litre of milk and they complain . We will wake up when Australia does n't have a single dairy farm left and we have to import our milk , and then look back when you had the opportunity to support our local farmers as you pay $6 or $8 for your litre of milk . The supermarkets are killing this industry off . Increase the price per litre by at least $1 and maybe a few dairies might survive . Up to you , supermarket bosses .
