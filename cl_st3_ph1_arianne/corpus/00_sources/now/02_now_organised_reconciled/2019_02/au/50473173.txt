<h> Kungullanji program highlights value of Indigenous research <p> Griffith University 's Kungullanji Indigenous Student Research Conference marked the end of a successful summer research program for participants , highlighting some of Griffith 's talented , up-and-coming researchers this month . <p> The Kungullanji Summer Research Program aims to create research opportunities and pathways for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students while improving their skills , enhancing their student experience and improving support for Indigenous researchers . <p> Scholars attend research training workshops and cultural support sessions while undertaking their research project under the guidance of an academic supervisor . <p> Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students showcased the results of their summer research project , presenting a seminar and research poster at the conference on 12 February . <p> " This program gives students a taste of what it 's like to be a researcher and the impact their research could have on the community . <p> " We are already seeing past graduates from the Kungullanji program transitioning to higher degree by research programs at
