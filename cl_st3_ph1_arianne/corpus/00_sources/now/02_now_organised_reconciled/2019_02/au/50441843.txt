<h> Coal a matter of practical policy trade-offs <p> What happens to a nation that bludgeons its biggest export ? The export industry that provides the foreign exchange to finance its imported motor vehicles , computer and electronic equipment and our overseas holidays . The one that helps pay for the increased spending built into Canberra 's budget projections , including on disability services . The one critical to creating the essential material of steel . The one that supports the wages that are n't growing fast enough . The one that helps power hundreds of millions of people out of poverty , creating as Asian middle class that will pay for other things that Australia sells , such as education , health services and tourism . <p> Coal should not be worshipped . But nor should the fossil fuels that powered the rise of the industrial age be demonised . The serious risk of global warming means that Australia should help lead international efforts to reduce the release of carbon emission and other greenhouse gases into the atmosphere .
