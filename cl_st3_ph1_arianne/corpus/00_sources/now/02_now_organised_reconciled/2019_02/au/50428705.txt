<p> Similar to Labor 's 2007 strategy , Ms Phelps , Ms McGowan and Ms Sharkie have offered the tribally conservative voter a reduced-risk alternative to the status quo . Or , as some have coined it , " continuity through change " . <p> But there are also key differences . While Mr Rudd promised measured economic modernisation in a socially-conservative manifesto -- opposing same-sex marriage , for example -- the new breed of once-were-Liberals flip that around . <p> They tend to emphasise the low tax , pro-business instincts of conservatives , but are more left-leaning on social policy and the environment .
