<p> BHP 's trial of zero-emissions vehicles at the Olympic Dam operation in South Australia in an attempt to reduce exposure to particulate matter is progressing . <p> The company , which launched the first light electric vehicle last June , said it was committed to halving the number of people exposed to particulate matters ( such as diesel ) , while also replacing fuel with lithium-ion batteries as an energy source for vehicles . <p> The company stated that the switch to the electric vehicles could provide not just environmental benefits , but economical ones as well . <p> " Because we rely on vehicles like this for day-to-day operations , we expect the shift to battery powered , eco-friendly cars will have a significant impact once deployed on a wide scale across our other operations , " BHP announced . <p> " It 's about more than just shrinking our carbon footprint . We 're banking on emerging technologies , such as light electric vehicles , to be more efficient with lower maintenance costs .
