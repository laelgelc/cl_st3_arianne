<h> Soil scientists share knowledge <p> Landowners in the district can gain a better understanding on soil management by attending a free workshop hosted by the Upper Campaspe Landcare Network on an olive farm in Edgecombe . Soil scientists Christian Bannan from South East Soil and Water , and Melissa Cann and Martin Hamilton from Agriculture Victoria will bring their wealth of knowledge to help landowners assess and improve their soil types . " This workshop will allow attendees to observe soil issues in the field and discuss any issues they may have on their land , " Christian said . " We will cover soil physical and chemical constraints at the site , and those that exist in the local region . " Landowners will gain an understanding that will help them take action to improve production and soil condition on their properties . " At the workshop participants will learn in the field around an open soil pit . Some of the topics that will be covered are : Soil structure , water infiltration , plant roots and root-zone depth , acidity , drainage , water holding capability , impeding layers and plant nutrition .
