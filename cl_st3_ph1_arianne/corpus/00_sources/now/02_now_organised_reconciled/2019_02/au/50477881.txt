<h> And the winner of the political broken promises award goes to ... <p> On the long list of broken promises in Australian politics , none compete with the pledge to voters three years ago when the Liberal Party promised stable government and then delivered civil war . One of the biggest questions at the coming election is whether Labor can be any better . It was not that long ago that Labor MPs were just as divided as the Liberals and almost as brutal with their leaders . ( Yes , almost . The removal of Turnbull set a new benchmark for mayhem . ) <p> The incredible thing about the government 's leadership spill last August was that the Liberals did not learn the lessons of the Labor experience . <p> So why should voters expect the next crop of political leaders to be any different ?
