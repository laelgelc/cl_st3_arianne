<h> World Bank VP arrives in Sri Lanka to support reform plans <p> February 13 , 2019 02:30 pm <p> - <p> The World Bank Vice President for the South Asia Region , Hartwig Schafer arrived today on his first visit to Sri Lanka to gain a better understanding of the country 's development priorities and how the Bank can continue to support its critical reform agenda . <p> " Sri Lanka has made good progress in reducing poverty and ranks the highest in South Asia in terms of human capital development " said Schafer . <p> " To continue its journey towards a high middle-income country and create more quality jobs for its people , Sri Lanka needs to continue focusing on better fiscal health , boosting its competitiveness as a tourism , logistics and IT hub and managing climate change risks . The World Bank will continue to provide its best expertise to support Sri Lanka 's future growth " . <p> During his three-day visit , Schafer will meet President Maithripala Sirisena , Prime Minister , Ranil Wickremasinghe and senior government officials including the Minister of Finance , Mangala Samaraweera , State Minister of Finance , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka , Dr. Indrajith Coomaraswamy . <p> Schafer will hold discussions on Sri Lanka 's low female labor force participation , visit project sites to meet communities engaged in development work and launch the World Bank 's new Sri Lanka Development Update . He will also meet with leading representatives of the private sector and the civil society , the statement said . <p> Before joining the South Asia Region , Schafer served as the Vice President for Global Themes and as Vice President for Operations Policy and Country Services . He led several key reforms , including the rollout of the World Bank 's new policies on procurement , environmental and social safeguards , and innovation of the Bank 's lending and knowledge instruments .
