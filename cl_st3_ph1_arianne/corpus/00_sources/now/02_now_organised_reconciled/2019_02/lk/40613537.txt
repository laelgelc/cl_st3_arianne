<h> World Bank South Asia Vice President in town <p> The World Bank Vice President for the South Asia region , Hartwig Schafer is currently in town on his first visit to Sri Lanka to gain a better understanding of the country 's development priorities and as to how the bank can continue to support its critical reform agenda . <p> " Sri Lanka has made good progress in reducing poverty and ranks the highest in South Asia in terms of human capital development " said Schafer . <p> " To continue its journey towards a high middle-income country and create more quality jobs for its people , Sri Lanka needs to continue focusing on better fiscal health , boosting its competitiveness as a tourism , logistics and IT hub and managing climate change risks . The World Bank will continue to provide its best expertise to support Sri Lanka 's future growth . " During his three-day visit , Schafer will meet President Maithripala Sirisena , Prime Minister Ranil Wickremesinghe and senior government officials including the Finance and Mass Media Minister Mangala Samaraweera , State Finance Minister Eran Wickremaratne , Colombo Mayor Rosy Senanayake and Central Bank Governor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ discussions on Sri Lanka 's low female labour force participation , visit project sites to meet communities engaged in development work and launch the World Bank 's new Sri Lanka Development Update . <p> He will also meet with leading representatives of the private sector and the civil society . <p> Before joining the South Asia Region , Schafer served as the Vice President for Global Themes and as Vice President for Operations Policy and Country Services . He led several key reforms , including the rollout of the World Bank 's new policies on procurement , environmental and social safeguards , and innovation of the bank 's lending and knowledge instruments .
