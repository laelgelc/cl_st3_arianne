<h> SL 's social indicators among highest in South Asia - WB report <p> World Bank Vice President for the South Asia region , Dr. Hartwig Schafer was in Sri Lanka last week to gain a better understanding of the country 's development priorities and how the Bank could continue to support its critical reform agenda . The World Bank also launched the Sri Lanka Development Update with a focus on ' Demographic Change in Sri Lanka ' in Colombo , where Dr. Hartwig Schafer made the opening remarks . During his visit he also visited a future project ( flood control ) site along the Kelani River .
