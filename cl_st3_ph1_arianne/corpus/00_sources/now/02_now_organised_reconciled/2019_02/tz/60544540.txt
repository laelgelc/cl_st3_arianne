<h> EDITORIAL : Take urgent steps to insure smalllholders <h> Thursday February 14 2019 <p> In the light of the seemingly relentless climate change effects that adversely impact agriculture , urgent steps are needed to promote agricultural insurance for smallholder farmers . <p> Farmers are prone to climate change effects like drought and floods that affect farm yields . There are also other forms of damage caused by animals , insects , crop diseases and any number of other perils which may impact crop yields in terms of quantities and quality . <p> Tanzania had 31 registered insurance companies as of December 2017 , out of which only four -- including a recently-registered firm -- provide insurance products and services to farmers , focusing on large-scale farmers , not smallholders . <p> All-embracing agricultural insurance is important -- if only because smallholder farmers constitute the largest part of the working population , with over 60 per cent of the adults engaged in agriculture . <p> These are the producers of some 95 per cent of the food consumed in Tanzania , as well as most raw materials for our factories . <p> It is , therefore , important to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the country 's economy . <p> The government needs to effectively address soonest challenges impacting the business as identified by the insurance companies . For instance , the insurers want state-of-the-art rain and other weather monitoring facilities in place at all times . <p> Also , the government can learn from the Kenya government which supports agricultural crops insurance by subsidizing premiums . <p> On the other hand , farmers can access insurance by forming themselves into groups , and apply modern farming techniques as well as good agricultural practices . Most smallholders still go about farming using traditional tools and methods , continue to depend on the rains even in areas with enough alternative water sources . <p> Indeed , there is a real need for spreading public awareness on insurance benefits in general , and for agriculture stakeholders in particular . q! q! q! q! q! q!
