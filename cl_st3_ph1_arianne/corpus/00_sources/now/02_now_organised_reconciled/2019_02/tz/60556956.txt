<h> Firms sign contract to protect forests in Tabora Region <p> THE Tanzania Forest Services ( TFS ) has signed contract with a UK based firm -- RedCop and Tanzania 's Livingway URT Ltd both dealing with carbon trade for protection of forests in Tabora Region . <p> The project which will cost over 90bn/- ( Euros 38 million ) will be implemented between 20 and 30 years with purposes of addressing the negative impacts of climate change . The funds will be sourced from different international donors and institutions . <p> TFS executive officer , Prof Dos Santos Silayo said recently during the signing ceremony between the agency and the companies , held at the Tanzania Forestry Research Institute ( TAFORI ) in Morogoro Region . <p> He said the programme to be implemented in Tabora Region is part of the government effort to reduce the causes which leads to climate change .
