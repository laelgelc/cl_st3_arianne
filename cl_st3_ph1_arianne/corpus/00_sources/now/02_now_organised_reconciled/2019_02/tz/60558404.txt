<h> Rice farming in Zanzibar set to receive a boost <p> Zanzibar needs at least 80,000 tonnes of rice annually to meet the needs of its population , but the struggle to increase production has so far resulted in production of less than 50,000 tonnes last year . <p> That means the country has to continue relying heavily on imports to feed its more than 1.3 million people ( 2012 census ) as its efforts to increase or produce enough continues with support from foreign countries particularly South Korea , Indonesia , and China . <p> The government has been making intensive efforts to improve the agricultural sector , particularly since 2011 when Ali Mohamed Shein , shortly after taking over as President introduced , " The Agriculture **25;1902;TOOLONG Program " with aim to double or triple rice production to reduce dependence on imported rice . <p> Despite existing and emerging challenges in farming which include insufficient or lack of modern skills and technology and impacts of climate change , the government has maintained its efforts for the last eight years , supporting rice farmers by offering agricultural subsidies to rice farmers by covering 75 per cent of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prices for example Hansunil herbicide from China at Tshs.6,000 ( $2.6 ) instead of Ths. 26,000 ( $11.3 ) per liter and fertilizers at Tshs.10,000 ( $4.4 ) for the 50-kilogram bag instead of Tshs. 60,000 ( $26 ) . <p> In addition a total of 600 farmers received training on good farming practices through Expanding Rice Production Process ( ERPP ) . These cumulative efforts have substantially increased the rice yields from 39,000 tonnes in 2017 to 48,118 tonnes in 2018 , equivalent to an increase of 23 per cent . Likewise , last year also saw a noticeable increase in the availability of various fruits and vegetables , particularly tomatoes . <p> On 13th December 2018 , the Ministry of Agriculture , Natural Resources , Livestock and Fisheries , on behalf of the government , signed an agreement with a contractor from the Republic of Korea for the construction of irrigation infrastructure that will cover a total of 1524 hectares .
