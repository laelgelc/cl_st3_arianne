<h> National turns new leaf on green attitude <p> National turns new leaf on green attitude but still lost on agriculture and climate - Greenpeace <p> Saturday , 23 February : Greenpeace has welcomed some of the National Party 's new environment policy proposals announced at the annual Bluegreens Forum today , but is critical of the party 's failure to address climate change and agriculture . <p> Speaking from the Bluegreens conference in Raglan , Greenpeace senior campaign advisor Steve Abel says National 's proposals for new marine reserves and a new national park in the Catlins as well as a countrywide bottle deposit scheme to address plastic pollution are good to see , but National is lost on the greatest challenges of our time - climate and agriculture . <p> Greenpeace also says it 's vital that national park and marine reserve proposals are developed closely with iwi . " Waste to energy schemes are a false solution backed by the likes of Exxon and Dow Chemical because they allow production of plastic to continue .
