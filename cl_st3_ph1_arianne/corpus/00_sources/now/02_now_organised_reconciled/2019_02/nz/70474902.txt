The rate and extent of wetland destruction in New Zealand has been recognised as among the highest in the world , " says Annabeth Cohen , Forest &amp; Bird 's Freshwater Advocate . <p> Ms Cohen says wetlands on private land are most in danger of being drained and destroyed .
