<h> Huntly gets creative with its first ' Arts in the Park ' <p> Huntly 's first ' Arts in the Park ' family day at Fraser Park on Saturday next week ( 16 Feb ) will offer people the chance to try out different arts and crafts for themselves , and children will be able to share their work with their favourite dog from the Waikato District Council animal control team . <p> A network of enthusiastic and talented people and groups , brought together through a growing Huntly arts development programme , will run stalls offering the opportunity to take part in activities ranging from clay and mosaics to beading , flax weaving and painting . Food stalls , and music and dance performances will round off a day of family entertainment . <p> Waikato District Council 's animal control team are joining in the fun with one of the dogs that is a regular favourite in the Council 's ' Dogs in Libraries ' reading programme for young children .
