<h> QSO recipient working to liberate us all from ageism <p> Eleanor Bodger , Director and Founder of Eldernet and Care Publications was appointed a Companion of the Queen 's Service Order in the 2019 New Year Honours List for her services to seniors and her community . <p> After working as a nurse , a social worker , a community development worker , and working in the public health system Eleanor saw the need for one place that older people could go to find information about the services they needed .
