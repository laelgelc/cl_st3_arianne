<h> A new water tax would affect everyone <p> IrrigationNZ says a proposed nationwide water tax would affect all Kiwis , and there needs to be more discussion about how this would impact households , farmers and businesses . <p> " The Tax Working Party has recommended the government consider introducing a water tax on all types of water use including hydro-generation , household use and commercial water use , " says Nicky Hyslop , IrrigationNZ Chair . <p> " This would result in higher power and food prices for households and businesses and higher rates bills to pay for the irrigation of parks and reserves as well as a direct water tax on household and business water use . " <p> The working party is proposing that the water tax could be used to fund the restoration of waterways . <p> " While we all want to see cleaner rivers , often the solutions to improving rivers require people to change their existing practices both on farm and to prevent urban wastewater discharges into rivers .
