<h> Reminder to control dogs to protect penguins <p> The death of a juvenile little blue penguin/korora , killed by a dog near the Whangawehi stream at Mahia Peninsula , has prompted the Department of Conservation ( DOC ) to remind owners to control their dogs . <p> DOC East Coast Operations Manager John Lucas said little blue penguins are vulnerable as they leave the nest . This is a reminder to owners to keep dogs under control around coastal areas . <p> " Little blue penguins are at risk of being attacked by dogs and to keep penguins safe , dogs should be on a lead at all times . <p> " Penguins have a strong smell which is quite attractive to dogs .
