<h> Regional Council to consult on establishing a new CCO <p> Hawke 's Bay Regional Council voted today to ask the community for feedback on creating a new Council Controlled Organisation or CCO as the appropriate structure to potentially offer shares in Napier Port . <p> Feedback on establishing the CCO can be given to the Regional Council from Friday 15 February until Monday 11 March 2019 . <p> The Regional Council is considering a minority Initial Public Offering or IPO on Napier Port to provide funding for significant investment in the Port . An IPO would release funds for the Port to significantly reduce its debt and begin construction of a new wharf in 2020 , without the need for financial contribution from ratepayers . <p> Regional Council Chair Rex Graham says the creation of a CCO is the simplest and most efficient structure to offer shares to the public . <p> " The establishment of a CCO is a process step that incurs very little cost but we welcome the views of the public on the creation of this entity " , says Mr Graham . <p> The Statement of Proposal will be on the Regional Council @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from Friday 15 February . The submission form will also be online at hbrc.govt.nz . Printed copies of the Statement of Proposal and the Submission Form can be posted on request by calling 06 835 9200 or 0800 108 838 or emailing ourplan@hbrc.govt.nz . This public consultation period runs for three weeks until 5pm on Monday 11 March 2019 . <p> Mr Graham stressed that no shares were currently being offered and any decision to proceed with an IPO would be made by the Regional Council over the coming months .
