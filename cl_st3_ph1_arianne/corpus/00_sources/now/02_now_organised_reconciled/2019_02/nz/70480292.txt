<h> Standard Drink Knowledge Lacking in New Zealand <p> New research commissioned by Lion 's Alcohol&amp;Me* programme has identified that most Kiwis do n't know what a standard drink is , and even those that think they do , ca n't accurately communicate what it means . <p> Understanding standard drinks is the key to people drinking responsibly , and the research of 800 Kiwis found most people do n't understand it well enough . Respondents could n't accurately say how many standard drinks are in the most common alcohol beverages and three quarters were unfamiliar with the nation 's recommended healthy drinking guidelines . <p> There was also a lot of confusion around the drink driving limit in NZ , with only 17% of respondents getting it right . Most respondents had a number of drinks they thought they could consume and still be safe to drive with only 11% correctly pointed out that there is no set number -- a variety of factors impact whether you 're safe to get behind a wheel . <p> Jude Walter from Alcohol&amp;Me comments , " This survey highlights there is a real opportunity to educate Kiwis in a way that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ process one standard drink an hour , regardless of your age , size or how much you 've eaten and there 's no way to speed that up .
