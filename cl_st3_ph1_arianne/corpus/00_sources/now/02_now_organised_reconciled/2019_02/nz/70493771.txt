<h> Draconian laws push Responsible Freedom Camping underground <p> For over 25 years now , the " New Zealand Standard for the self containment for Motor Caravans " ( NZS:5465 ) has been in place . During this time it has failed to solve any Freedom Camping problems , and has become little more than a method of infringement for Councils and others to enact , while discriminating against the majority of New Zealand 's Freedom Campers . <p> Any attempt to control Freedom Campers has to provide education at the very least , and any mechanism to restrict Freedom Camping must place the emphasis on the people doing the camping , NOT the mode of camping as the NZ standard does . <p> Recent attempts to " raise the bar " on the Standard by some organizations , has only achieved to further drive Responsible Freedom Campers underground and lead to little changes in the behaviour of some Freedom Campers . Raising the bar does nothing to educate the offenders which is where the real need lays . <p> Any changes to the Standard would only be effective if it lead to positive behaviour changes .
