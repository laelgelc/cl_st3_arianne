<h> Report calls for action to address harassment online <p> A comprehensive new report from ActionStation launched Monday examines the harm that unmoderated and under-regulated online platforms are causing New Zealanders . <p> The report , comprising of new research , individual experiences , and expert opinions highlights a damaging side of the internet , and makes the case for more scrutiny of the role tech giants play in our society . <p> ActionStation are calling for government action to address gaps in current legislation . The report includes recommendations and a petition calling for the government to : <p> 1 . Remove : Ensure platforms are active in removing harmful content quickly . An investigation into the most effective method to do this would be required , but the responsibility should be placed on the platform , not the users.2 . Reduce : Limit the reach of harmful content . Neither the platforms nor the users who create hateful and harmful content should benefit from algorithms that promote divisive and polarising messages.3 .
