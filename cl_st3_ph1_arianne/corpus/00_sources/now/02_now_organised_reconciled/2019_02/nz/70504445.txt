<h> Providing a Maori perspective on wellbeing <p> Te Puni Kokiri , in collaboration with the Treasury , is proud to release a discussion paper that provides a Maori perspective on the Living Standards Framework . <p> Te Puni Kokiri Chief Executive Michelle Hippolite believes " a radical shift in the conversation about wellbeing is needed if we are truly to achieve intergenerational wellbeing for Maori and all New Zealanders in the future " . <p> An Indigenous Approach to the Living Standards Framework is part of a suite of discussion papers published on the Treasury website to stimulate conversations about how to better support intergenerational wellbeing and raise living standards . <p> " Maori have their own understanding of intergenerational wellbeing that draws on cultural values , beliefs , social norms and indigenous knowledge " , says Mrs Hippolite . <p> The paper contends that Te Tiriti o Waitangi , Te Ao Maori and a whanau-centred approach need to drive Maori wellbeing .
