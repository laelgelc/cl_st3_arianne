<h> Tuvalu dismisses citizenship for fisheries idea <p> Tuvalu 's leader Enele Sopoaga has lambasted a former Australian leader 's suggestion to swap Australian citizenship for maritime resources . <p> Tuvalu 's Prime Minister Enele Sopoaga Photo : RNZI / Jamie Tahana <p> Mr Sopoaga has described Kevin Rudd 's proposals as " imperial thinking " . <p> Mr Rudd wrote in a recent essay that Australia should offer citizenship to residents of the small Pacific nations of Tuvalu , Kiribati and Nauru in exchange for control of their seas , Exclusive Economic Zones , and fisheries . <p> Mr Sopoaga warned , in an interview with the ABC , it amounted to neo colonialism . <p> He said Tuvalu was a fully independent country and there was no way he was going to compromise its rights to fisheries resources . <p> He said what was needed was urgent action on climate change . <p> Mr Sopoaga has called for the establishment of a Pacific supra-state , along the lines of the European Union , that is " based on cooperation and integration , perhaps into some sort of United States of the Pacific " . <p> Mr Sopoaga @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pacific Island nations to amplify their concerns about climate change on the global stage . q! q! q! q! q! q!
