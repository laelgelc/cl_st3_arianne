<h> PM warns slowing global growth will weigh on economy <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern said New Zealand 's economy remains " relatively strong " but warned that slowing global growth will have an impact . <p> " We have strong fundamentals and are well prepared , but we need to be realistic that if the global economy slows , it will affect our economic growth , " she told Business New Zealand in a speech . <p> " The finger of blame for the slowdown in global trade growth is generally pointed at countries pursuing increasingly protectionist policies , which are naturally affecting confidence and investment plans , " she said . Specifically , " trade tensions in the wake of tariffs imposed by the US on Chinese imports dented the strong growth seen in 2017 .
