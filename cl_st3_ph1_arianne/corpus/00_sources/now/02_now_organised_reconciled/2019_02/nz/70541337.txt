<h> Frasertown couple up for sustainable farm awards <p> THE finalists have been announced for the 2019 East Coast Ballance Farm Environment Awards and among them are Frasertown sheep and beef farmers Dave Read and Judy Bogaard . <p> Run by the New Zealand Farm Environment Trust , the awards champion sustainable farming and growing across 11 regions . <p> The East Coast includes the areas covered by Hawke 's Bay Regional Council and Gisborne District Council . <p> Mr Read and Ms Bogaard have been farming Waiau Station , near Frasertown in the Wairoa district , since 1996 . <p> The 1213-hectare property is on steep hill country and run in conjunction with the 592-hectare Allington Farm at Kotemaori . Together , the business is a breeding and finishing operation , carrying 12,604 stock units . <p> Mr Read is breeding his own bare-breech , low-input sheep ( no docking , fewer dags ) and his cows are bred for parasite resilience .
