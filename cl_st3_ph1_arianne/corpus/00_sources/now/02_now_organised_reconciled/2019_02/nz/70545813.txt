<h> Marshall Islands plans to raise islands to escape sea level rise <p> Most islands in atoll nations , such as in this aerial view of Jaluit in the Marshall Islands , are as little as two meters above sea level and threatened by climate change . " We need the political will , and especially traditional leaders ' commitment , to see this through . That is why a national dialogue is planned to bring all the parties together . " <p> The outlook for the Marshall Islands and other atoll nations is not good , according to government officials involved in climate change and disaster response work .
