<h> Construction to start on Renwick reservoir <p> Construction of Renwick 's new water reservoir will begin soon , with Fulton Hogan awarded the tender for the work . <p> Councillor Cynthia Brooks said it was a great day for Renwick . <p> " The new reservoir and treatment plant will be a game changer for Renwick . The reservoir , which is three times bigger than the town 's existing capacity , will help alleviate summer demand pressure on our aquifer . " <p> " This is a very significant investment in long-term water management for our community and it will help us get through Marlborough 's dry summers . " <p> The construction contract includes ground improvement works and building the 1500m3 reinforced , pre-stressed concrete reservoir at 24 River Terrace . Completion is expected in July 2020 . The contract was awarded based on Fulton Hogan 's previous reservoir construction experience and price . <p> Due to the constrained size of the site , construction of a new water treatment plant can only start after the reservoir is finished .
