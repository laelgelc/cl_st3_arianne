It was unjustlyconfiscated in 1863 and given to settlers who farmed it until theirdescendants sold it for around $20m to Fletcher Building Limited in2016 . Fletcher wants to build 480 houses . <p> According to Pania Newton , SOUL Spokesperson , " Our people can not thrivewithout this land .
