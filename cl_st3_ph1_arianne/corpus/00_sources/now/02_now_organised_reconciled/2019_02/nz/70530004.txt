Uniting Palestinian factions was not the main objective of the Moscow conference , in the first place . <p> Instead , the nature of the event , the host country and the clear messages sent to Washington and Tel Aviv were all meant to communicate something else entirely . And they did . <p> The head of the Fatah delegation to the conference , Azzam al-Ahmed , apologized to his hosts on behalf of Palestinians for failing to achieve political reconciliation . <p> But that apology could have been prepared in advance . It would not have been rational to expect that a conference organized in such haste , with few preliminary meetings or intense prior consultations , could have achieved the coveted unity . <p> If one is to also consider the various unity agreements , signed between Fatah and Hamas in the past - but never honored - and bearing in mind @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gaza recently , a unity deal in Russia would be nothing less than a miracle . <p> So why did the Russians hold the conference in the first place and why did the Palestinians agree to attend , if its failure was a foretold conclusion ? <p> Around the same time that Palestinians met in Moscow under the auspices of the Russian Foreign Minister , Sergey Lavrov , the US was holding its own conference in Warsaw , Poland . <p> The Warsaw meet was the US ' attempt at drawing a new political paradigm to replace the defunct ' peace process ' , which , itself , was an American political invention . <p> While the ' peace process ' , thanks to US blind support of Israel , has failed terribly , Warsaw , too , is unlikely to deliver any meaningful or long-term political vision in the Middle East .
