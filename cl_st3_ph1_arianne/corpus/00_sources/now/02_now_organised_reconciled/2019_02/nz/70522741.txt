<h> Opening announced for Te Papa 's ground-breaking nature zone <p> The biggest change to Te Papa since the museum opened will be unveiled to the public on 11 May 2019 . <p> Te Taiao Nature , Te Papa 's brand new $12 million nature zone , is a bold and immersive journey through the natural world of Aotearoa New Zealand , combining cutting-edge science with matauranga Maori . <p> The 1,400-square-metre zone is a permanent new addition to the museum . Entry is free . <p> Te Papa revealed yesterday Sunday 17 February that a priceless moa egg , one of only 36 in the world , will be at the heart of the spectacular new space . <p> Te Taiao Nature will feature over 1,200 collection items from New Zealand 's natural world , along with dozens of brand-new interactive experiences , from creating your own tsunami to weighing in against a giant moa . It replaces Te Papa 's previous nature area , which closed in April 2018 .
