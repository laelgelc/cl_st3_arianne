<h> Extra services to cope with peak transport demand <p> Auckland Transport is gearing up for the busiest time of the year on public transport . <p> Late February and March see an annual peak transport demand as tertiary students return for the academic year and fewer workers are on annual leave . <p> AT is putting on extra services on key routes to add to the additional capacity created by the New Network for bus services . The New Network across Auckland has simplified bus services and added more than 1700 additional trips a day in the past year . <p> Bus Services Manager , Darek Koper says AT is now running 12,937 trips on a weekday compared to 11,200 this time last year .
