<p> The exhibition was hosted at the On The Spot Artspace in Nuku'alofa and was arranged by the Seleka International Art Society Initiative ( SIASI ) . <p> It was a tribute to their fallen fale which was destroyed by Cyclone Gita last February . <p> " We 've lost our land and our studio but cyclones can not take our spirits and we will move forward , " says Taniela Patelo . <p> The exhibition featured more than 20 paintings from Tongan artists Tevita Latu , Taniela Petelo and Virginie Dourlet . <p> Seleka International Art Society was founded by Tevita Latu in 2008 aimed at promoting art and encouraging young artists from Tonga . <p> -Partners- <p> " I had the chance to study art in Australia . I always promised myself that when I would be back I would teach Tongans about art , " says Latu -- and the Seleka initiative was the result . <p> The fale has been a home for more than 100 young artists and was situated in the Fanga ' Uta Lagoon in the main island of Tongatapu . <p> Taniela Patelo ...
