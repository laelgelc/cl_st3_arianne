<h> Whangarei Theatresports Troupe Calls For New Members <p> Whangarei performance troupe The Improv Mob is calling for new members following a year of successful shows . <p> Improvisational theatre , also known as theatresports , is the art of creating scenes with minimal preparation and maximum laughs . Scenes , characters , dialogue and plot are made up on the spot . <p> Northland 's only improv group , the Improv Mob was founded at Northland Youth Theatre in 2016 and moved to OneOneSix at the start of 2018 . After a year featuring sold-out shows and fundraisers at OneOneSix , the troupe is open for new members . Jam sessions can be joined casually or regularly . <p> " We 're a very welcoming group and always looking for new personalities , " organiser Michael Botur said .
