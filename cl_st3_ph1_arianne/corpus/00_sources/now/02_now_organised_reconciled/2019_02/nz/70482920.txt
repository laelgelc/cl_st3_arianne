The New Zealand Anti-Vivisection Society ( NZAVS ) says that any attempt to test recreational drugs on animals will be met with fierce resistance . <p> " We are reminding the government that if they start testing party pills on innocent animals , they will be met with public outrage . Collectively New Zealand defeated the last government 's attempts to use animal experiments for party pills , and we will do so again if we need to , " says NZAVS Executive Director , Tara Jackson . <p> " The very idea of testing party pills on animals is absurd .
