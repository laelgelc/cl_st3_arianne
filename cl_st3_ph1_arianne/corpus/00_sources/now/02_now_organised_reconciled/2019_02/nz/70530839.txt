<h> Ask an expert : Los Angeles for families <p> Q. My husband and I would like to take the kids to Los Angeles to visit Disneyland or Universal Studios . What other family-friendly activities would you suggest ? <p> A. Los Angeles is a very family-friendly city . I always recommend beginning with a city tour , especially if it 's your first visit . <p> Los Angeles is a very large , spread out city and a city tour enables you to see a fair chunk of it at leisure , ticking off a number of highlights that may otherwise be considered just a photo opportunity . Places of interest usually include the Hollywood Walk of Fame where you can stroll along in search of the star of your favourite celebrity . <p> Santa Monica Pier is one of my favourites to visit . This marks the end of iconic Route 66 , is open year-round and provides plenty of fun for all the family . Entertainment includes arcade games , buskers , a Ferris wheel , rollercoaster and a beautiful 100-year-old antique merry-go-round .
