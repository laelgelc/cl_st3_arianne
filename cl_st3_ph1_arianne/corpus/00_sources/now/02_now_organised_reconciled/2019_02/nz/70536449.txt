<h> Speed up , cow gas researchers urged <p> A $50m taxpayer-funded body seeking answers to farming emissions needs to move much quicker -- and merge with a farmer-led organisation , a review tells MPI . The review is likely to bolster calls for more farm-gas research money in the Budget , as the Government grapples with how to bring agriculture under the Zero Carbon Bill . <p> A multi-million dollar Kiwi effort to find answers to animals ' greenhouse gases has been told it is doing a good job , but it needs to hit the accelerator . <p> A review out yesterday from the Ministry for Primary Industries says the New Zealand Agricultural Greenhouse Gas Research Centre ( NZAGRC ) should expand its work and also get a wriggle on if it is to get solutions to farmers in time to help New Zealand meet its Paris agreement targets .
