<h> ' Broken ' ratings system a burden , Timaru District Council <p> A report prepared by Timaru District Council expresses concern about the increasing burden councils face due to central Government requirements . <p> The rating system is fundamentally " broken " and councils are being burdened with rising costs , according to the Timaru District Council . <p> A six-page submission to the Productivity Commission , written by Timaru District Council corporate and planning manager Mark Low on behalf of the council , outlines a number of concerns with the system . <p> In the report , Low warns that councils are being burdened with an expectation of " doing more , to a higher standard , with less " , as central Government delegates more tasks to councils . <p> Councils obtain most of their funding through rates , but the submission expresses concern about whether this is sustainable . <p> " Ultimately these challenges lead council to the conclusion that the financing system for local government is fundamentally broken , and unsustainable heading into the future .
