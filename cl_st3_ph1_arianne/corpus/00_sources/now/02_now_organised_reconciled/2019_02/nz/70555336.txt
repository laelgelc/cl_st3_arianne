<h> Councils plan a huge hike in spending , debt <p> A financial check on councils reveals a huge hike in planned capital spending and debt . David Williams reports . <p> Queenstown 's council is being hit with a double whammy . <p> Mayor Jim Boult says it has underspent on infrastructure in the past , so it 's being forced to spend more to catch up . That 's millions of dollars being spent maintaining roads and footpaths , and on infrastructure to continue to provide clean drinking water , get rid of wastewater and deal with stormwater . <p> " Then lay on top of that a rapidly increasing permanent population and rapidly increasing visitor numbers , " Boult says . The forecasts are startling . By 2028 , the Queenstown-Lakes is expected to be home to almost 50,000 people , up 29.5 percent , with a " peak day population " , boosted by tourists , of 149,000 people . <p> " Not only have we got to spend money on existing infrastructure , we 've got to create new infrastructure as well -- which clearly gives us a challenge .
