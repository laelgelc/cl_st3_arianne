<h> Pacific leaders urged to do more to eliminate violence against women <p> A leading European Union official has urged Pacific Island leaders to do more to ensure the elimination of violence against women and girls in the region . <p> The European Union 's Commissioner for International Cooperation and Development , Neven Mimica Photo : EU <p> Neven Mimica , the EU commissioner for International Co-operation and Development , is in the region this week . <p> He oversaw the launching of the EU 's Spotlight Initiative To Eliminate Violence Against Women and Girls at the Taumeasina Island Resort in Apia . <p> According to Mr Mimica there have been some gains in countering domestic violence , but everyone can do more . <p> " Collectively all of us here today are here to help break this cycle , because the scourge of domestric violence will not be stopped by one person , one organisation , or one country alone , " Neven Mimica said . <p> In Samoa 's capital , Mr Mimica met with a number of Pacific Islands leaders for negotiations on relations between the EU and 79 countries in the Africa , the Caribbean @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pacific Islands leaders in the Africa , the Caribbean and the Pacific group , Apia , 26 February 2019 Photo : EU <p> " The EU 's commitment to and partnership with the Pacific region is strong . We are natural partners in the tackling of global challenges such as climate change , and we continue to work together , " Mr Mimica added . <p> The Apia event was part of discussions among " high level " delegates including the commissioner and local leaders which have begun on how to implement a European Union Pacific Partnership initiative in the region . <p> Launched last November , it aims to prevent all forms of violence against women and girls by addressing their root causes . q! q! q! q! q! q!
