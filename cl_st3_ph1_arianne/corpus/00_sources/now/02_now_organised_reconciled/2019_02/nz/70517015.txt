The desk is clear , the phone stops ringing and you can say whatever you like . Dr Rod Carr was not about to confess his secrets or admit his critics were right all along but he did seem unusually perky on his third to last day as vice-chancellor of the University of Canterbury . <p> Carr is an unusual creature . A former Deputy Governor of the Reserve Bank who has an astonishing facility with numbers and an incredible recall of detail , he is not the dour technocrat you might expect .
