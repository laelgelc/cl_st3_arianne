<p> Maori Climate Commissioner , Donna Awatere Huata , says now is the time for Children to shame adults into climate change action . <p> " On March 15th , the movement of children striking from school to protest the inaction by adults on climate change finally reaches the shores of Aotearoa . " <p> " Following in the footsteps of 15 year old Swedish climate campaigner Greta Thunberg and 13-year-old American Alexandria Villasenor , children around the planet are striking to protest against the failure by adults on a problem they will inherit as a poisoned legacy . " <p> " As adults , we should be ashamed at our inaction and wilful delusion as every climate change alarm screams at us that we must make immediate change .
