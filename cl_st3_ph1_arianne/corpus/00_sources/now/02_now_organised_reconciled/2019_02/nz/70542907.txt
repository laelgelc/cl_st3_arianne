<h> Standards to make homes warm and dry released <p> The new healthy homes standards to make rental properties warmer and drier were today announced by Housing and Urban Development Minister Phil Twyford . <p> The standards set minimum requirements for heating , insulation , ventilation , moisture and drainage , and draught stopping in residential rental properties . They reflect feedback from a wide range of public health experts , stakeholders including landlords , tenants and building experts . <p> Phil Twyford said making sure all New Zealanders had warm , dry homes was one of the most important public health changes the Government could make . <p> " Nearly 600,000 households rent in New Zealand , and our rental stock is of poorer quality than owner-occupied homes . It 's estimated about 200,000 families live in rental homes that do not have ceiling or underfloor insulation . <p> " The Ministry of Health says 6,000 children are admitted each year for ' housing-sensitive hospitalisations ' . These children have been found to be nearly four times more likely to be re-hospitalised and 10 times more likely to die in the following 10 years .
