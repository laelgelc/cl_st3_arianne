<h> A glimpse into the future for Wanaka Town Centre <p> Wanaka residents will be given a glimpse of the future when town centre activation trials kick off in March . <p> The trials will test some of the possibilities being investigated through the Wanaka Town Centre Masterplan project , which started back in July last year . <p> Queenstown Lakes District Deputy Mayor Calum Macleod is thrilled to see some action start to happen on the ground and is looking forward to hearing what people think . <p> " We 're listening to what the community is telling us and have designed a short programme of activities that will better link our stunning lakefront to the town centre , allow for active travel and bring our people together to talk about how things might be in the future , " he said . <p> " Over the course of three days , we 'll be closing part of Ardmore and Helwick Streets to test out how it feels to create more car-free , open space on the lakefront , " Mr Macleod said . <p> The trials are scheduled to run from 12.00pm on Friday 15 March until @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ardmore and Helwick Streets . <p> They will involve road closures on Ardmore Street ( between Dungarvon Street and Lakeside Road , and Helwick Street ( between Ardmore Street and Dunmore Street ) from 3.00pm on Thursday 14 March until 3.00pm on Tuesday 19 March time corrected to allow for set up and removal . <p> The trials will also include some changes to the way people move around town , including the removal of carparking on the foreshore , new one way streets ( Dungarvon from Dunmore to Ardmore , and Ardmore from Dungarvon to McDougal ) , and a dual access bus entry/turning point near the log cabin .
