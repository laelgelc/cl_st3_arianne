<h> Fixing Auckland 's Housing Crisis <p> Auckland is the ninth most unaffordable city in the world . How can that be ? <p> Auckland is a beautiful and vibrant city with many highly desirable qualities , however , its housing costs are out of control . This is particularly true for first home buyers , people who are renting and those who are on fixed incomes . <p> Such a large amount of people 's disposable income is being spent on housing they are struggling to get ahead . As a city we need to recognise this is a moral issue that should be addressed . <p> The Crown , and the building industry , can not fix this issue on their own .
