<h> Path closure at Mangaiti Gully <p> A walking path through Hamilton 's Mangaiti Gully will be closed from 25 February to 1 March to allow for the removal of a group of willow trees . <p> The path , which can be accessed from Wairere Dr in Queenwood/Hukanui , is a popular walking route for residents , but staff in Hamilton City Council 's Parks and Recreation Unit have concerns the trees will continue to crowd out native plantings also lining the path . <p> The willow trees have been slowly removed throughout the gully as native tree and plant species have been replanted , these removals are a continuation of that . The closure is from 8am to 4pm each day and is to ensure a safe site for arborists carrying out the job . <p> Path users will need to detour via adjacent streets while the work is being done .
