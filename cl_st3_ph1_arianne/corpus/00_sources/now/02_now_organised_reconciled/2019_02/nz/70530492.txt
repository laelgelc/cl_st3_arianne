<h> Fish &amp; Game reminds hunters of ' special game bird hunt ' <p> Eastern Region Fish &amp; Game staff are reminding hunters of a special game bird season for black swan in Management Area A1 , and for pukeko throughout the region over this coming weekend . <p> Management area A1 includes the Rotorua Lakes , coastal estuaries in the Bay of Plenty and that part of the Waikato River just above Rawhiti Lagoon downstream to Lake Atiamuri . <p> Hunters should check their Game Bird Hunting Guide booklet if they are unsure of the A1 boundaries . <p> The special season is for Saturday and Sunday , the 23rd and 24th of February only , and available for hunters who hold a 2018 licence . <p> There is no limit on the number of black swan which can harvested but a daily limit of 10 birds applies to pukeko . <p> Senior Fish &amp; Game Officer Matt McDougall says that members of the public should n't be alarmed if they hear shots fired during the weekend , starting from around 6.30am on Saturday . <p> " Both species are numerous in this region and in some areas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> Mr McDougall says that Fish &amp; Game officers will be out ranging and bag limits and other regulations will be strictly enforced . <p> He says that hunters are reminded that the rules which apply in the main game bird hunting season , which of course , ended some time ago , apply equally to the special season . <p> The regulations state that non-toxic shot is strictly required around waterways ( not lead shot ) , and shotgun magazines must be restricted to three shots in total with no exceptions , he says .
