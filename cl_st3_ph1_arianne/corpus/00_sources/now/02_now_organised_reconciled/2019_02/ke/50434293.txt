<h> OKECH KENDO : Failed development by chopper <p> Feb. 13 , 2019 , 12:15 am <p> By OKECH KENDO <p> Chopper <p> The more things change the more they remain the same . But the appropriate expression for a community stranded about 400km southwest of Nairobi , is the more the times change the more things remain the same . <p> The passage of time has made no difference for the transformation of this community , surviving on the leeward side of national development . <p> On a dry , dusty , day in early February of 1973 , development arrived in their dusty land . It came in a parcel , with glossy yellow and red ribbons wrapped around the prized package . <p> A helicopter had delivered then Vice President Daniel Moi among a people who rarely hosted leaders of such eminence . Moi came to launch development for the community between Otaro , Ngeta and Lieta primary schools in Karachuonyo constituency . <p> Moi 's grey immaculate suit flapped as he alighted from the helicopter . Standing erect , the future president was wielding his trademark rungu . He also brought greetings from President Jomo Kenyatta . Moi was from the seat of power , from where development was dished out to communities without consulting them . This was 37 years before devolution . Children poured from the villages to welcome development . The old , from Kanjira location , hosting the airstrip , and Kamser location , the neighbour to the east , arrived . They sang songs of hope . <p> Then Karachuonyo MP , Okiki Amayo , should have been a comedian had he lived in a different era . He was hawkish , rowdy and abrasive -- a n archetypal party politician . Amayo was then an agent of development . The MP was hosting the VP during the launch of Otaro Airstrip . <p> Villagers called the murramed ground , in the middle of nowhere , an airport . They could not tell the difference .
