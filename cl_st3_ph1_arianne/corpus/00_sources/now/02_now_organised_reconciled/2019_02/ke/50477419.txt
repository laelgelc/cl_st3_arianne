<p> At this point , it is a ritual that must be observed : The complaint about the weather . <p> In its own quiet way , the weather is going a little bit crazy here on the coast of East Africa ; none of the drama of polar vortices or the coverage , but it is there . Dar es Salaam has been experiencing heat that is inhumane and summer is supposedly winding down . <p> To add insult to injury , extreme weather does something to the national grids of most places experiencing it .
