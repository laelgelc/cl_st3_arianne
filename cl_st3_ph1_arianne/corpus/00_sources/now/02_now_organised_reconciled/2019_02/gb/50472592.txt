<h> ' Wetter weather ahead ' <p> The BBC Weather Centre said it was likely to be one of the warmest Februaries since records began in 1878 . <p> Sunny , warm conditions are expected to last into Wednesday , when maximum temperatures at Kew Gardens and Porthmadog are forecast to be slightly cooler at 19C and 17C respectively . <p> On Thursday , a high pressure system is expected to break down as wetter , windier weather moves in across Wales and into England . <p> Dr Friedericke Otto , acting director of the Environmental Change Institute at Oxford University , said people were right to ask themselves whether the record temperatures were being driven by climate change . <p> " I am very confident to say that there 's an element of climate change in these warm temperatures , " she said . <p> " But climate change alone is not causing it .
