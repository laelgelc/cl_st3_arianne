She said she wanted to return to this country to have her baby and has now given birth to a boy . <p> Then there were the teenagers who joined the international Youth Strike 4 Climate protest about climate change on Friday instead of going to school . <p> Many people have argued that Shamima was still a child when she and two of her friends went to Gatwick four @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and therefore her choice was n't valid , so she should be regarded as a victim who was groomed . <p> She said , in an interview with The Times from the Syrian refugee camp where she currently is , that she " does n't regret coming here " and that her life in Raqqa with her Dutch Muslim convert husband had been " normal ... the one I wanted " . <p> She was not " fazed " by seeing a severed head in a bin because it had belonged to an enemy of Isis .
