<h> Churches in the Poppyland area of north Norfolk are hoping for a big turn-out for their ' Big Brew ' event at Northrepps village hall . <p> The Fairtrade Fortnight event is part of the nationwide celebration of 40 years of Traidcraft , the trading company that buys directly from small farming cooperatives and the charity which teaches farmers more efficient methods and techniques to overcome the local effects of climate change . <p> People attending will be able to learn skills they need to turn their potential into profit . <p> Spokesman Mike Terry said : " We are hosting a ' Big Brew ' with plenty of coffee , tea and chat , cakes galore , and a superb range of delicious delights of fantastic quality . Eat in and take a selection box home . <p> " We will also have many Fairtrade products for sale . Sainsbury 's has kindly donated some of the ingredients in support of this . " q! q! q! q! q! q!
