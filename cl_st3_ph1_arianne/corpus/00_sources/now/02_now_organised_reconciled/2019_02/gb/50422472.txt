<p> At first sight , the verdict of the last referendum appeared to be straightforward . On June 23 , 2016 , the people of Britain voted , if by a narrow margin , to tell the Government it must find a way to leave the European Union . <p> In practice , unravelling almost half a century 's worth of multi-layered co-operation was always going to be a complex task . I am in awe of the ingenuity , dedication and patience shown by Olly Robins and many other former colleagues in Whitehall for the way they have tried to solve this riddle . <p> Their job has not been made any easier by the myriad of different -- and sometimes contradictory -- promises made for Brexit . At various times , the British people have been assured that the UK can control immigration from the EU , have the exact same economic benefits as we currently enjoy in the single market and negotiate the easiest trade deals in history .
