<p> Defra Ministers have reappointed Liam Dolan and Sue Hartley to the Board of Trustees of the Royal Botanic Gardens , Kew , for second terms of three years . Both appointments will run from 1 March 2019 until 28 February 2022 . <p> RBG Kew became an Executive Non-Departmental Public Body under the National Heritage Act 1983 and is sponsored by Defra . The Board of Trustees comprises a Chairman and 11 other members . <p> The appointments have been made in accordance with the Governance Code on Public Appointments published by the Cabinet Office . All appointments are made on merit and political activity plays no part in the selection process . <p> Trustees do not receive any direct remuneration for their services , although reasonable travel and subsistence expenses can be reimbursed . <p> There is a requirement for appointees ' significant political activity to be made public .
