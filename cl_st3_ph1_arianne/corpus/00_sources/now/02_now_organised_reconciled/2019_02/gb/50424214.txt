<h> ' Day of rebellion ' : Anger as environmental activists urge youngsters to skip school <h> ' Day of rebellion ' : Anger as environmental activists urge youngsters to skip school <p> COUNCIL chiefs have slammed climate campaigners who are calling on Southampton youngsters to take time off of school to join their protest . <p> The activists from Extinction Rebellion have urged pupils and students to " show local government they are concerned " about climate change and join them next Friday for a " day of rebellion . " <p> The group recently staged a " die-in " at the Bargate to show the number of people estimated to die from pollution in Southampton every year . <p> They also took to Westquay with other green groups in a mass flash-mob to protest against Southampton City Council 's decision not to bring in charges for vehicles entering the city . <p> Now they have organised a day of protests in Guildhall Square . <p> Organisers said : " On February 15 , young people from all over the UK and Sweden will be striking from school to take a stand for their own future . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ concerned ; we will not sit by as our world is blindly destroyed . <p> " Take the day off school , college , or university , and join us at 11am for a student-focused demonstration . <p> " Use your voice ! Explain to your head teachers why you are striking . Engage them in conversation . They will appreciate your passion and your knowledge . They will listen . <p> " Bring banners , food , friends and musical instruments . It will be a fun day with music , dancing , singing , and even the limbo . " <p> But Cllr James Baillie , a maths teacher at Swanmore College , told Echo it can take pupils a long time to catch up on any missed classes . <p> He said : " I think it 's completely irresponsible and I 'm really uncomfortable with them encouraging pupils to miss school . There are many ways in which their voices can be heard , but I do n't think them missing some of their education is the best way of achieving their goal . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , nationally and globally , but it 's not just the day you are off of school , it 's the ripple effect .
