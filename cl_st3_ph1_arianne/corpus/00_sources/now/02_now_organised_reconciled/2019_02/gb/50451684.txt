<h> The Watercolour World : preserving images of vanishing nature <h> Duke and Duchess of Cornwall among backs of new project to digitise pre-1900 watercolours <p> We 're used to seeing watercolours hanging on our walls at home or in galleries . We 're perhaps less used to seeing them as historical records of a world before film . But for many of those painted before the 20th century , that is what they are . Yet watercolour paintings are liable to fade over time and for that reason many are shut away out of sight . A new online initiative launched last Thursday with the support of the Duke and Duchess of Cornwall has set out to change that . <h> A colourful past <p> The Watercolour World project invites museums , galleries and the public to identify watercolours painted before 1900 . Using a special portable scanner , the paintings are then digitalised and stored online for the future .
