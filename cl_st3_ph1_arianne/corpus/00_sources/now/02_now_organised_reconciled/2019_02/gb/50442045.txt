<h> Malaria milestone and using light to hear : News from the College <p> From an important step towards malaria elimination , to experimenting with light to help us hear better , here is some quick-read news from across the College . <h> Testing modified mosquitos <p> Genetically engineering certain species of mosquito is one potential new technique to boost malaria elimination efforts . However , scientists want to make sure they know how any modified mosquitos might behave in the wild . <p> Now , researchers from Imperial , LSTM , the University of Perugia , the University of Oxford and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention have followed groups of modified mosquitos in large cages that mimic real environments . <p> The mosquitos were modified to pass on a trait that makes more of the next generation male .
