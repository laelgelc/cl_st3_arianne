<h> Solving the mystery of sea snakes that can not drink seawater <h> Sea snakes are surrounded by seawater . While previously scientists believed they could drink seawater , new research has shown they need to access fresh water . <p> The leader of the study , Harvey Lillywhite , a professor of biology of the University of Florida , said : " This study contributes to a fuller understanding of how pelagic sea snakes , and possibly other marine animals , avoid desiccation following seasonal drought at sea . " <p> The new study has been published in PLOS one . It shows that sea snakes living in drought areas relieve their dehydration as soon as the wet season hits . <h> What is the yellow-bellied sea snake ? <p> The yellow-bellied sea snake , or hydrophis platurus , is the only reptile in the order Squamater that lives on the open sea ; <p> It has one of the largest geographic ranges of any vertebrate ; and <p> It has no access to fresh water during the dry season . <p> So how does the yellow-bellied sea snake survive in drought regions ?
