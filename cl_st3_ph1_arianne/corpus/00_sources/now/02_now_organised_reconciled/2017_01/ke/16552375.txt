<h> We need to end the dry season of ideas <h> share <p> The drought ravaging many parts of the country has most farmers worried . Livestock are already dying due to lack of water and pasture while short season crops for the October-December period have all withered , except for those millionaire farmers who can afford irrigation and greenhouses . <p> As you read this someone could be breathing his last because he is unable to have food or water . It is not a joke . The dry spell has run for the last three months and even before it started , rains were very erratic . <p> Interestingly , though , rain is no longer an issue for farmers alone , it affects everybody . We are all suffering water shortages ; power rationing ; high cost of food especially vegetables , and the prevailing unhealthy high temperatures . <p> When residents of Western , one of the greenest regions in Kenya , begin to complain about drought , you know it has gone to the extreme .
