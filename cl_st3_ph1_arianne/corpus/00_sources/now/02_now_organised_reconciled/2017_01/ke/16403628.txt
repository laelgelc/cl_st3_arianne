<p> A dirt road branches from Kisumu-Usenge highway at Kolenyo towards Reru village , Kisumu county , some six kilometres from the shores of the Lake Victoria . <p> Once a fishing community , Reru residents no longer enjoy large catches of Tilapia and Nile Perch . <p> Fish stocks in the second largest fresh water lake in the world have dwindled , leaving a trail of destruction on trees mostly used for making canoes and paddles . <p> The lake region also experiences massive erosion from surface run-offs that have rendered land bare and cut the harvest of staple foods , leaving the area poorer , amid the high prevalence of the HIV/Aids scourge . <p> It is under these circumstances that a group of women widowed by HIV have resolved not to be left behind as the world makes concerted efforts to reduce the effects of climate change . <p> Inspired by world leaders ' Paris meeting <p> Aniga Women Initiative , which has more than 50 members , makes energy-saving cooking stoves , an idea that struck when world leaders met in Paris , France , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> When the Star met secretary Merine Akoth in December last year , she said they wanted to be part of the team that would restore the environment around Lake Victoria . <p> " We no longer get enough fish from the lake as a result of hyacinth and poor practices which have swept it clean .
