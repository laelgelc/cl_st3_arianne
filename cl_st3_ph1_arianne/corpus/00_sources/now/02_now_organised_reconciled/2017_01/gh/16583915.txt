<h> Climate change produces severe risks in Europe <p> Europe is facing rising sea levels and more extreme weather due to climate change , the European Environment Agency ( EEA ) said in a report on Wednesday . <p> Among extreme weather were more frequent and more intense heat waves , flooding , droughts and storms , according to the report . <p> It reviewed the latest trends and projections on climate change and its impacts across Europe . <p> Better and more flexible adaptation strategies , policies and measures will be crucial to lessen these impacts , the report said . <p> According to the report , nearly all European regions are vulnerable to climate change . <p> Among them , southern and south-eastern Europe is projected to be a climate change hotspot , as it is expected to face the highest number of adverse impacts . <p> At the same time , in western parts of Europe , coastal areas and floodplains are also seen as hotspots as they face an increased risk of flooding from rising sea levels and a possible increase in storm surges . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , changes in the distribution of climate-sensitive diseases , and changes in environmental and social conditions , it said . <p> In terms of economic costs , it could be very high , since climate-related extreme events in EEA member countries account for more than 400 billion euros ( 429.82 billion U.S. dollars ) of economic losses since 1980 . <p> The report also said that mainstreaming of climate change adaptation into other policies is progressing but can be further enhanced . <p> Other further actions include improving policy coherence across different policy areas and governance levels and more flexible adaptive management approaches , it said .
