<h> Shortsighted energy policy to benefit none <p> Science has proved that climate change is largely caused by human activities . Any energy policy that goes against the science is shortsighted and disastrous to the whole world . <p> As Donald Trump was sworn in as U.S. president on Friday , the White House website published the new president 's America First Energy Plan , saying he will eliminate " harmful and unnecessary " policies such as the Climate Action Plan and embrace the shale oil and gas revolution . <p> The energy plan of Trump , who has called climate change a " hoax , " has a wrong starting point -- the denial of human activities as the main factor behind climate change . <p> Shale oil may benefit the United States by bringing down the price of oil , but Washington 's policy carries another price -- the disorientation of world efforts against climate change . <p> The world consensus is that clean energy , instead of fossil fuels , is the only option . <p> Breakthroughs in such areas as second-generation biofuels , photovoltaic and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rapid development of related industries and thus promoted the transformation of economic growth models . <p> Renewable energy , led by wind and solar power , has surpassed coal in 2015 to become the largest source of installed power capacity in the world , according to the International Energy Agency . <p> Some may be concerned that greenhouse gas mitigation and turning to clean energy may compromise economic growth . But as Trump 's predecessor Barack Obama indicated , greenhouse gas mitigation can boost efficiency , productivity and innovation . <p> For instance , carbon dioxide emissions from the energy sector in the United States fell by 9.5 percent from 2008 to 2015 , while the economy grew by more than 10 percent . <p> It is hoped that the Trump administration will come back to the right track of international cooperation in the face of perhaps one of the most pressing global challenges for mankind .
