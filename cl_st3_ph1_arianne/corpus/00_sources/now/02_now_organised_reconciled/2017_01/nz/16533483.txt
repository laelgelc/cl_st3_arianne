<h> Auckland Transport to trial electric buses <p> The government has announced funding for the e-bus trial and related infrastructure as part of the EECA ( Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority ) Low Emission Vehicles Contestable Fund . <p> Auckland Transport ( AT ) has been awarded up to $500,000 to part fund an electric bus trial and close to $300,000 for electric vehicle charging infrastructure . AT will top up the funding so two buses can be used in the trial . <h> ' Great news for Auckland ' <p> Auckland Mayor Phil Goff says , " Great news for Auckland as we do our part to reduce emissions and combat climate change . <p> " Whether it is trains , buses or cars , electrification of our transport network is key to making Auckland a cleaner more sustainable city , and providing a better customer experience for our public transport users .
