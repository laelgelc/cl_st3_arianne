<h> Grandmothers and farmers block Fonterra plant <p> Three grandmothers , a student and a farmer have this morning chained themselves to a gate to prevent coal being delivered to Fonterra 's Clandeboye dairy factory in South Canterbury . <p> At 7.30 am , the five locked themselves to the gate at the entrance to the factory 's coal plant , as a pile of woodchips was dumped in front of them , with the message " FONTERRA QUIT COAL , " while others were dressed as cows pointing to the woodchips as an alternative .
