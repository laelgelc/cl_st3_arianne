<h> Trump administration is giving us a good lesson on Twitter security <p> Several recent incidents involving U.S. President Donald Trump 's administration can teach users something about IT security -- particularly about Twitter and what not to do with it . <p> It turns out that several White House-related Twitter accounts -- including the president 's official account , @POTUS -- until recently were revealing sensitive information that hackers might be able to exploit . <p> The problem revolves around the service 's password reset function . If the account holder does n't take certain steps to secure it , Twitter exposes information that anyone with the right skills can use to uncover what email address -- in redacted form -- was used to secure a Twitter account . <p> A hacker who goes by the name WauchulaGhost noticed the problem and began tweeting about it . He found that the @POTUS account was secured to a Gmail address that , although partially redacted , could be guessed as belonging to a Trump aide in charge of social media . <p> Twitter <p> The hacker found the same @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ first lady and Trump 's press secretary , all of which were also secured with Gmail addresses . <p> " It 's not hard to figure the emails out from there , " WauchulaGhost tweeted . But for government officials or business executives , it can be asking for trouble . <p> That 's what happened in last year 's election .
