<h> Chile is battling the worst wildfire in its history <p> Chile orders extra funds to be spent on tackling the country 's worst forest fires ever , which have destroyed 155,000 hectares of land in the centre of the country and led to the evacuation <p> Chile is battling the worst wildfire in its history , and is now pleading for international assistance after months of trying to bring deadly fires under control . <p> Drought , strong winds and high temperatures have stoked a series of fires in Central Chile since November ( environmentalists blame Chile 's unusually dry weather on climate change ) . But things went from bad to worse when smaller brush fires converged into a massive wildfire that destroyed the town of Santa Olga Thursday evening , killing 10 people and reducing over 1,000 buildings to smoldering rubble . <p> Central Chile is no stranger to natural disasters ; it regularly endures floods and earthquakes . But this this different . Chilean President Michelle Bachelet called the fires " the greatest forest disaster " in the country 's history .
