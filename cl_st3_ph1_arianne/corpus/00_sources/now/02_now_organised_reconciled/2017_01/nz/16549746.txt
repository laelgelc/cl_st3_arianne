<h> Coastal communities present complex issues for councils <p> ON THE AGENDA : The impact of rising sea levels on coastal communities such as Haumoana are being considered in a LGNZ position paper on climate change . PHOTO/DUNCAN BROWN <p> The sea level is expected to rise by up to one metre in the next 100 years due to climate change , and that means some hard decisions ahead for central and local government , as well as the people living in coastal communities , says Local Government New Zealand ( LGNZ ) president Lawrence Yule . <p> A LGNZ paper on managing the risks in coastal areas is close to being finalised and looks at the implications of water quantity , infrastructure investment , civil defence and land use planning in light of climate change . <p> " Councils and their communities will need to consider how rising sea levels and more frequent storms will impact them and the infrastructure they use , " Mr Yule said . <p> " This is a very difficult , complex , long-running issue to deal with , and the politics are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we need to be prepared to make brave decisions - if we do n't the generations to come wo n't thank us .
