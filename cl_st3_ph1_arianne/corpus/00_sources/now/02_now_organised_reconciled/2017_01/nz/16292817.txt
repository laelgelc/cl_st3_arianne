At 18 years of age he graduated from university ; in his celebrated career at the University of Otago he has uncovered the massive inter-generational IQ gains now known worldwide as the Flynn Effect ; and , at 82 he is still running several kilometres a week , publishing books and being invited to speak overseas . <p> Q Why do you do what you do ? <p> A I have always wanted to be a free man -- an autonomous human being and that means a mind free of falsehood about the most important questions . I was a very devout Catholic as a child , and God ( and heaven and hell ) seemed to be the most important things in the universe . In a few years , I decided I was an atheist but now had to confront the question : if no commandments came from God , were my humane ethical principles just a matter of opinion . My greatest satisfaction has been clarifying these questions , and related questions like free will and the status of science , which took a long time . In How to Defend Humane Ideals ( 2000 ) and Fate and Philosophy ( 2012 , written to help non-specialists to see their way ) , I reached conclusions I could live with . <p> Along the way , I decided to try to live up to my humane ideals , whatever their status , and this meant opposing racial bias as the most obvious evil existent .
