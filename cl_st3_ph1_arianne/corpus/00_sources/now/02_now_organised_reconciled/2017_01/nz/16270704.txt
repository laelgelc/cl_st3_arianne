Source : Associated Press <p> A long existing rift in the Larsen C ice shelf suddenly grew by 18 kilometres in the second half of December , and now only a thread of 20 kilometres of ice is keeping a 5,000 square kilometre piece from breaking away . <p> Lead researcher at Swansea University Professor Adrian Luckman , says " I would think the end of this particular rift and this iceberg carving is imminent , within the next few months I would have thought . " <p> Larsen C ice shelf is losing its top layer as a result of warmer air temperatures , and its bottom layer because of warmer ocean currents and circulation . <p> " Climate warming must be playing a part in the overall picture of ice shelf loss , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ C , we can not attribute directly to climate change " , Professor Luckman says . <p> It 's the most northern major ice shelf in Antarctica and Professor Luckman says that it holds back the flow of glaciers that feed into it . <p> " A loss of 10 per cent of the area of this ice shelf , we have shown from modelling studies , will make the remaining ice shelf more vulnerable , will make it less stable . So we might expect to see more such carving events in the future and someway down the line , " he adds .
