<p> Solar power will roll out across Auckland after a multimillion dollar partnership received the green light . <p> Mayor Phil Goff announced the sustainable energy partnership , between Auckland Council and energy company Vector , on Tuesday . <p> Goff said the partnership comes at a time when Auckland Council is looking to demonstrate its leadership in helping New Zealand fulfil its climate change commitments made at last year 's COP21 summit in Paris . <p> " As New Zealand 's largest city , we must play a leading role in shifting to a low carbon economy . Vector shares our vision and we are pleased to be able to partner with them to achieve this goal . " <p> The first stage of the partnership will see a pilot energy efficiency programme developed for Auckland homes , followed by renewable lighting for the Auckland Harbour Bridge . <p> Goff said one of the challenges Auckland faced was safeguarding its future energy needs . <p> " With about $2 billion infrastructure investment in energy solutions needed for our growth over the next ten years , it 's important we 're developing innovative , cost-effective and future-proofed solutions . " <p> Vector chief executive Simon Mackenzie said there was a lot of work to be done , but Vector was excited to be on board . <p> The energy efficiency project is led by Vector 's majority shareholder , Entrust , and will focus on assisting and educating residents on energy-saving solutions . <p> Goff said the partnership is the first of many strategic projects to be announced in 2017 designed to " make Auckland better " .
