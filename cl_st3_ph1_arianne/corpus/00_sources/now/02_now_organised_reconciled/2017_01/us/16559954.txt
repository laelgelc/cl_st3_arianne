<p> Sundance Film Festival 's lineup is full of films that do not back down from the tough racial , political and environmental issues challenging today 's world . <p> From Jan. 19 to 29 , Park City , Utah transforms into the epicenter of the indie film industry . Around 40,000 people flock to the festival , instagramming at every possible stop before they settle in to watch the 181 showcased films . <p> These 181 films are chosen out of more than 13,000 submissions , which for those doing the math means that any film has about a 1.4% chance of being selected .
