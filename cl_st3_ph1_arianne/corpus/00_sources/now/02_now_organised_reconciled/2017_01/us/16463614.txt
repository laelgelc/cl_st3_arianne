For both good and bad reasons . <p> Let 's start with the good : I do n't think even foreign policy folks remember just how bleak America 's position in the world appeared to be in January 2009 . The outgoing president was spectacularly unpopular . In the preceding few months , the worst financial crisis in a century had hit the acute phase . The U.S. economy was shrinking by about 8 percent . Lots of foreign observers , and lots of American observers , too , were writing the obituary for American leadership in the world . The German finance minister was predicting that the United States would lose its status as the financial superpower .
