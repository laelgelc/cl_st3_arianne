---- 3 Is the Soundtrack of a Revolution <p> Killer Mike and El-P are back , with their most compelling and aggressive album to date . <p> Just before 2016 finally caved to the only thing more relentless than its darkness -- that is , the passage of time -- rap duo Run the Jewels released its third album . While it was originally scheduled for a January 2017 release , the group , consisting of Killer Mike and El-P , instead decided to surprise fans with what is essentially the opposite of a holiday album . Unless , of course , your idea of holiday is a revolution against oppression , rampant capitalism , and The Man . <p> The album opens with one of its most pleasant-sounding tracks -- but even " Down " is a chest-thumping anthem for the disenfranchised . " Even birds without wings want to fly , y'all , " Mike and guest Joi sing on the hook , which sets the stage for what is without question an unapologetically politically charged album . " And the album 's last song is literally titled " A Report to Shareholders/Kill Your Masters . " In years past , all of this might have felt too on the nose or brash for casual hip-hop fans , and a little too self-righteously Robin Hood in spirit . But these days , it feels bold and entirely appropriate . <p> Despite being a community-minded and frankly lovable man ( as well as one of Bernie Sanders ' most vocal supporters ) , Killer Mike leans into the " scary black man " persona hip-hop artists have long been branded ( or burdened ) with , using it to his advantage as a way to intimidate the opposition .
