<p> Attempts to assess Barack Obama 's eight years in the White House bring to mind Chinese leader Zhou Enlai 's verdict on the significance of the French Revolution : " It 's too soon to say . " Supporters ' delirious hopes have been unfulfilled and opponents ' fevered fears have gone unrealized , as they were bound to be . But one solid judgment is possible : Obama has been a model of dignity , and appreciation of his cool composure will grow in his absence . <p> Obama 's signature achievement is something that did n't happen : The United States did n't enter a depression as a result of the financial crisis of 2008 . After a painful recession , the nation entered its longest period of job growth in history . Obama 's decisions to stimulate the economy , rescue key financial institutions and bail out the auto industry were controversial at the time , and might not have been made by a president more wedded to free-market ideology . <p> President-elect Donald Trump and others can plausibly argue that the recovery should be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , faster growth and higher incomes . Such claims will be validated or disproved in time . But no one can doubt that Trump inherits a healthier , more resilient economy than Obama did . A president who was often accused of being a socialist kick-started the engines of capitalism . <p> Obama 's biggest legislative achievement was the Affordable Care Act , which Trump and the Republican Congress have promised to repeal . Obamacare is modest in its ambitions : It is not the single-payer system sought by progressive health-care reformers , and does not even include a government-run insurance option to compete with private providers . Because it aims to keep private insurance at the center of the health-care system , Obamacare relies on a politically vulnerable Rube Goldberg system of mandates and subsidies . <p> But like his economic program , Obama 's health care plan came in response to a crisis .
