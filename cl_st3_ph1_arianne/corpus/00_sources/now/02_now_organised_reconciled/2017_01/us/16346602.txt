<p> A vast swamp forest in Central Africa contains enough carbon to equal two decades ' worth of U.S. fossil fuel emissions , scientists have found . <p> The Congo Basin peatland -- an area larger than New York State -- has accumulated around 30 billion metric tons of carbon over the last 11,000 years , according to a study published Wednesday in the journal Nature . <p> The discovery makes the fate of this vast carbon sink an important consideration for climate projections and land conservationists . <p> A team of U.K. and Congolese researchers spent three years roaming the Cuvette Centrale peatland to find samples of peat to test in their labs .
