---- Michael Greenstone , an environmental economist who worked in the Obama administration , will be the new head of the Becker-Friedman Institute , the University announced over winter break . <p> Greenstone is the Milton Friedman Professor of Economics and the Director of the Energy Policy Institute of at Chicago ( EPIC ) . Much of Greenstone 's recent research has focused on energy and growing markets . He recently published a paper demonstrating the benefits fracking has on the communities surrounding fracking sites . Greenstone is a public advocate of some forms of environmental regulation . Last month , he co-wrote an op-ed in The New York Times titled " Donald Trump Should Know : This Is What Climate Change Costs Us . " <p> Greenstone also served as chief economist for President Obama 's Council of Economic Advisors from 2009 -- 10 . From 2010 -- 13 he was director of the Hamilton Project at the Brookings Institution , and he remains on its advisory board . <p> Greenstone will maintain his title as director of EPIC after he assumes his new role on July 1 .
