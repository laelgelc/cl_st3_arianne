<p> New evidence indicates the primary cause of megafaunal extinction in Australia 45,000 years ago was likely a result of humans , not climate change . A paper on the subject is published in the journal Nature Communications . <p> A range of now extinct megafauna that was present when humans first arrived in Australia .
