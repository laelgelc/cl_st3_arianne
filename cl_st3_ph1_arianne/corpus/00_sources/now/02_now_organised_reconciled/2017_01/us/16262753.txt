( Image : NASA/John Sonntag ) <p> Scientists have been watching a giant rift in Antarctica 's Larsen C ice shelf for many years , but the crack grew substantially this past December , prompting concerns that it 's about to break free . The resulting iceberg is expected to be one of largest ever recorded . <p> " This event will fundamentally change the landscape of the Antarctic Peninsula . " <p> According to geologists with the MIDAS project , a mere 12 miles ( 20 km ) now separates the western most point of the crack to the ocean . The rift has been advancing steadily over the past few months , but it grew by a whopping 11 miles ( 18 km ) during the second half of December .
