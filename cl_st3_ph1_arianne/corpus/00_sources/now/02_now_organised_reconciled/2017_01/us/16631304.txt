<h> Changing of the seasons <p> Normally when I write under a topic heading like this it is to wax philosophically on the coming of the cool air of autumn and the fall , blood sports . This year it is to consider whether ( shiver ) Al Gore might be right about climate change vs. him just making a good living while espousing the warming of the earth . I believe we are in a natural warming trend that may last 30 years or so based on historical data and the study of growth rings on ancient trees ; however , there is no questioning that this has been a strange winter . <p> I was sitting with a friend on his patio last week the day after a 25-degree morning and just a couple of days after the sleet storm . We were trying to sip a nice glass of wine and discuss recent electoral events when I finally threw up the white flag and begged to go inside to get away from the torrent of mosquitoes that enveloped us .
