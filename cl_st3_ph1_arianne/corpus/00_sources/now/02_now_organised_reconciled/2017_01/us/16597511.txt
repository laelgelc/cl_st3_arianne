" <p> Arctic coastlines have some of the highest rates of erosion in the world , and those rates are increasing as the climate warms . <p> That 's partly because sea ice is forming later and melting earlier , exposing shorelines to heavy waves for longer . As permafrost melts , coastline that once was rock-solid is now starting to soften and wash away . <p> The permafrost " is really as hard as concrete , but when you thaw it , when you melt out the ice , then it immediately loses its cohesion , " explains Michael Fritz , a German researcher who has studied coastal erosion on Yukon 's Herschel Island for close to a decade .
