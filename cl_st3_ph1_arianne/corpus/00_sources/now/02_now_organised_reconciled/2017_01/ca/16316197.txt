<p> The City of Vancouver has hired an analyst from an environmental think-tank to help steer the city 's renewable energy strategy . <p> Matt Horne leaves his post as the Pembina Institute 's B.C. associate director Nov. 16 to join the city as climate change policy manager , where he 'll be responsible for setting and maintaining interim targets in the city 's plan to use 100 per cent renewable energy by 2050 . <p> " His first job is going to be doing the detailed work of , where do we need to be by 2020 , where do we need to be by 2025 ? " said Doug Smith , the city 's director of sustainability . " We have to lay out the path and then determine the gaps and what policies we need to put in place .
