<h> Beijing Creates Anti-Smog Police To Tackle Air Polluters <p> We do n't know if you 've noticed , but Beijing has a bit of a smog problem . Videos of clouds of particulate matter-filled doom are constantly cropping up online . The most recent airpocalypse looked like a moving wall of dark magic encroaching on the metropolis , a witchcraft conjuring of respiratory ridiculousness . <p> Beijing 's authorities already have a weird and arbitrary alert system in place , but there are n't really any solid measures to sort out this smog , most of which comes from construction , coal burning and traffic . Now it appears that they have assembled a crack team of anti-smog police who will go around shaking their fists at anyone barbecuing too aggressively . <p> According to Reuters , open-air cookery , garbage incineration , biomass burning and dust kicked up by traffic on the roads -- but not the traffic itself -- will be monitored in person . <p> It 's not particularly clear what anyone can do about dusty roads , except perhaps tarmac over them of course @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take the smog-making particles out of the air , sort of like a cheaper and less special effects-laden version of the Ghostbusters . <p> Fines or prosecution could be placed on individuals or companies that are excessively pumping too much nastiness into the air .
