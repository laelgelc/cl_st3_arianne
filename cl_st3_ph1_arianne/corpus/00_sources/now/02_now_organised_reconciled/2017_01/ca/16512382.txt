<h> Women 's March on Washington Edmonton Solidarity Event to take place at legislature <p> Protesters will gather around the world today in support of women 's rights , including here in Edmonton . <p> Sister events to the Women 's March on Washington have been organized all over Canada , and the world . The march is in support of women 's rights as well as other progressive causes such as Gay rights , gun control , immigrant rights , equal pay , reproductive freedom , racial justice , worker rights , climate change , and support for vaccinations . <p> Paula Kirman helped organize the Women 's March on Washington Edmonton Solidarity Event . When she heard about the event being held the day after Donald Trump 's inauguration , she decided to bring the conversation here to Alberta . <p> " The reason why I think this important is because we need to raise the level of civil discourse in our society , among ourselves and also in politics , " said Kirman . " <p> Kirman added that while the original March on Washington is specifically aimed at sending a message to the new American administration , many of the issues being raised are relevant to Canadians and Albertans as well . <p> " There are very uniquely Canadian and Albertan reasons for wanting to take part in this . Women experience gender based violence all over the world , " she explained . " Equity in the work place has always been an issue . And in the political arena and even just in day-to-day life for a lot of women , bullying and harassment is something that happens on a regular basis . " <p> The Women 's March on Washington Edmonton Solidarity Event will begin at 1 o'clock this afternoon at the north side of the Alberta Legislature , lasting for roughly an hour . Speakers include Edmonton Strathcona MP Linda Duncan and Indigenous Rights Activist Roxanne Blood , among others .
