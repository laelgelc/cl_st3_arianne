<h> 2017 campsite reservations open for national parks <h> Riding Mountain , majority of other national parks taking reservations until end of March <p> Melanie Robinson trudged through the snow for this shot in Riding Mountain National Park over the holidays . Campsite reservations for 2017 for most national parks opened Monday .
