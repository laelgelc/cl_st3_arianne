<h> Tourists invited to glimpse Franklin wrecks this summer in Nunavut <h> Parks Canada , Adventure Canada team up ; IIBA expected by summer <p> If you 're a thrill seeker with thousands of dollars burning a hole through your pocket and a wild desire to visit a shipwreck from Sir John Franklin 's doomed expedition , you 're now in luck . <p> An Ontario-based tour company -- in partnership with Parks Canada and the Interim Franklin Advisory Committee -- announced one of its cruise ships will stop at the site of the sunken HMS Erebus during its " Out of the Northwest Passage " voyage , scheduled for this coming summer . <p> According to Adventure Canada 's travel itinerary , the voyage will consist of a two-week trip from Kugluktuk , Nunavut , to Kangerlussuaq , Greenland . <p> During the trip , passengers " may have a chance " to snorkel over the shallow waters of the Erebus wreck , marking the first time tourists will get a glimpse of the ship since it was discovered in 2014 . <p> Parks Canada will also offer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ vehicle , or ROV , for those who remain on the ship , one of two lost in the ill-fated Franklin Expedition of 1845-46 . <p> The trip costs between $8,995 US for the ship 's most modest cabins , up to $22,795 US for a luxury suite , the website says . <p> The location of Erebus , in Queen Maud Gulf near the Adelaide Peninsula , has never been publicly disclosed by Parks Canada , to protect it from looting . <p> " It 's also a great way for visitors to learn more about Inuit history and culture and the significant contributions of Inuit knowledge to the story of the lost Franklin Expedition , " Catherine McKenna , the minister of environment and climate change , said in a Parks Canada news release , Jan. 20 . <p> " I hope to see more partnerships like this which will support northern communities through employment opportunities and community development , and showcase the wonders of the Canadian North . " <p> The Interim Franklin Advisory Committee , established in March 2016 , consists of Inuit and Nunavut @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of that committee that allowed Parks Canada to do that with Adventure Canada , " the Kitikmeot Inuit Association 's director of planning and communication , Fred Pedersen , told Nunatsiaq News by phone from Cambridge Bay , Jan. 23 . <p> The Parks Canada news release says " local Inuit guides " will work on the cruise ship during the Northwest Passage cruise to help provide an Inuit perspective on the Franklin discoveries and other Arctic attractions . <p> The Ocean Endeavour will also make a scheduled stop in Gjoa Haven the day after the ship visits the Erebus shipwreck site . <p> Pederson chairs the interim committee , which is scheduled to meet again in Yellowknife Jan. 24 and Jan. 25 for talks on a future Gjoa Haven heritage facility capable of housing Franklin relics and research . <p> " We wanted to start getting the interest there for other companies to start planning tours that benefit Gjoa Haven and increase tourism opportunities related to the two Franklin ships , " Pedersen said .
