<p> I agree with ( Windsor Minor Hockey Association president ) Dean Lapierre and what he posted about the march on Washington with the exception of the name-calling . <p> Why , when they call themselves sisters , go to another country to protest God knows what and forget about their sisters here -- one receiving a $1,000 hydro bill ? <p> Why go to another country to protest and forget their sisters here in Canada ? What must it have cost them to organize this jaunt ? <p> Why not concentrate on the plight of their sisters here and protest : high hydro bills , the carbon tax , licence fee increases , property taxes and such . Most of these women have to work two jobs just to keep food on the table . <p> What did these protesters accomplish ? Their picture on the front page . <p> Wake up . Take care of your own in this country . They are hurting . It would be great to have a leader who says he wants to make Canada great instead of concentrating on climate change . <p> What good will a good climate be in the future if the country is a trillion dollars in debt and has no jobs ? <p> PATRICIA BIRCH , Emeryville <p> Share Your Views <p> Send letters to the editor to letters@windsorstar.com .
