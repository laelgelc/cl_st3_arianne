<p> I found Walter Brown 's opinion on our new carbon tax attention grabbing . He uses facts , which I appreciate , to support his position . <p> However , I am not sure it is logical to let Canadians off the hook for a manmade problem just because we are lucky enough to live in a country with an abundance of forests and wetlands . <p> After all , man had nothing to do with the geological makeup of the country but he does contribute to its greenhouse gas emissions . <p> There are other facts one might want to consider when thinking about this complicated issue . For example , Canada has less than half of one per cent of the world 's population . <p> Yet , as Mr. Brown points out , we produce 1.7 per cent of the world 's emissions . In other words , much more than our share . <p> In fact , we are the eighth largest producer of greenhouse gases in the world .
