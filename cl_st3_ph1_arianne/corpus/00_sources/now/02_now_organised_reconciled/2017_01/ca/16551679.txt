<h> 200,000 litres of oil spilled after pipeline leak in southern Sask . <p> Cleanup is underway after an oil spill from a pipeline near Stoughton , Sask . <p> The government was notified about the spill on Jan. 20 . According to officials , the leak happened on a low-lying area where there is a frozen slough . <p> Approximately 200,000 litres of crude oil was released onto agricultural land , but no oil entered any creeks or streams . Officials say the spill affects an area as much as 20 metres in radius . <p> The pipeline , owned by Tundra Energy Marketing Inc. , was shut down when the leak was discovered . <p> Cleanup began on Jan. 21 . Surface oil was removed with vacuum trucks . The government believes 170,000 litres of oil has already been cleaned up . <p> Assistant deputy minister of Economy Doug MacKnight says the pipeline will be excavated and further cleanup will take place . Ocean Man First Nation and Environment and Climate Change Canada were both notified of the spill the day it happened , as the spill is on First Nation Land . <p> The government says air quality and wildlife have not been affected . <p> Stoughton is approximately 60 kilometres east of Weyburn . <h> Photos <p> Crews working to clean up an oil spill near Stoughton , Sask .
