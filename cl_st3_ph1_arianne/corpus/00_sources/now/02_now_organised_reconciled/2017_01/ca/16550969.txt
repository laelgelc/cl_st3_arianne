<p> One of the leading figures in the development of Vancouver 's film exhibition industry is devoting himself to addressing climate change . <p> The Pembina Institute , a local clean-energy organization , announced today ( January 23 ) that Leonard Schein is joining their board of directors . <p> Schein founded the Vancouver International Film Festival in 1982 as well as Festival Cinemas , which operated the Ridge , the Park Theatre , and Fifth Avenue Cinemas ( before they were sold to Cineplex Entertainment ) .
