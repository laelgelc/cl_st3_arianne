<h> Government Releases Results of Public Climate Change Consultations <p> The provincial government has released the results of public consultations on climate change which took place last year . <p> Over 200 people took part in 11 in-person and online consultation sessions took place between July and September of last year . <p> The provincial government will develop an action plan over the coming months that considers input from the consultation sessions as well as through received through discussions with the federal and other provincial and territorial governments .
