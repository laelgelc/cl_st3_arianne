<h> Pipeline breach southeast of Regina leaks 200,000 litres of crude oil <p> A pipeline has leaked about 200,000 litres of crude oil onto agricultural land in southeastern Saskatchewan . <p> Government spokeswoman Kathy Young says the spill site 10 kilometres north of Stoughton is in a low-lying area with a frozen slough . About 170,000 litres of the spill have been recovered and the oil has not entered any creeks or streams , she said . <p> Young says environmental consultants are at the site and say air quality and wildlife have not been affected . <p> Young said the Environment Ministry was informed on Friday night when the spill was detected .
