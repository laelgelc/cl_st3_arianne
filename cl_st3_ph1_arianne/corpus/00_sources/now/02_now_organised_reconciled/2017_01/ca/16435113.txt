<h> Celebrating corporate sustainability leadership <p> Despite U.S. change in direction , the private sector is proving its commitment to sustainable development . <p> A sombre mood descended on the COP22 climate change conference in Marrakech in mid-November , as the prospect of a hostile Trump administration began to dawn on attendees . But within the week , over 360 companies and investors made clear the position of the U.S. business community with a joint letter to the U.S. president-elect , members of Congress and global leaders : " We , the undersigned members in the business and investor community of the United States , reaffirm our deep commitment to addressing climate change through the implementation of the historic Paris climate agreement . " <p> The disparate group , which included DuPont , General Mills , Hewlett Packard and Nike , called on U.S. legislators to continue on the low-carbon path established in recent years , invest in solutions both at home and abroad , and remain in the Paris Agreement .
