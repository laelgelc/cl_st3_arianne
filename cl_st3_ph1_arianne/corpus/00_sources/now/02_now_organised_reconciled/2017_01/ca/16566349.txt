<h> Cohen : Women 's March marks the start of the next U.S. campaign <p> Demonstrators protest near the White House in Washington , DC , for the Women 's March on January 21 , 2017 . Hundreds of thousands of protesters spearheaded by women 's rights groups demonstrated across the US to send a defiant message to US President Donald Trump . /ANDREW CABALLERO-REYNOLDS / AFP/Getty Images <p> WASHINGTON -- The Women 's March on Washington of 2017 did not have the impact of other great mass protests in the capital . It did not have the poetry of the March on Washington for Jobs and Freedom in 1963 or the power of the Bonus March of 1932 . <p> As mass demonstrations go , it lacked focus . There was a stage and speakers but it was hard to hear them . There was no Martin Luther King , Jr . to inspire and no Douglas MacArthur to provoke . In different ways , those historic gatherings pricked a nation 's conscience . <p> The Women 's March was more subtle . Throngs of women , men @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ flooded the streets . But they were n't marching for one purpose , such as civil rights or military pensions . <p> They were casting a broader net . In name , this was supposed to be about women 's rights -- access to abortion , protection from sexual harassment . <p> They were joined by gays and lesbians , Black Lives Matter , environmentalists . The mood was joyous . There were toddlers in strollers , college students and families . <p> In early afternoon we took refuge from the dense crowds in the National Museum of Natural History on the Mall , finding relief among the whales . Others had the same idea . <p> But it was the sheer number and passion that was the statement . As my friend Scott Gilmore argues , Donald Trump has done for the left what Hillary Clinton and Barack Obama could not . He gave this coalition a sense of purpose and direction . <p> Here 's a prediction : Saturday inaugurated a seismic season of protest in the United States . They are developing a grassroots network of activists amplified by social media . <p> Trump is a perfect lightning rod . His language and manner are incendiary . He will annoy , antagonize and overstep , and he will draw people into the streets . <p> His dissembling acolytes , such as Kellyanne Conway , will continue to spin falsehood . After a disastrous four days , she sounds like Cruella de Vil of 101 Dalmatians . <p> What is the risk to Trump of mass protests ? Obamacare is an example .
