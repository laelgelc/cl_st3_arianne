---- Climate plan has three benefits <h> Poll <p> Albertans often ask me how the Climate Leadership Plan will affect them and their families . <p> It 's a good question . And with all the fear-mongering out there about the plan , it can be hard to get to the truth . <p> Three benefits stand out : <p> First , the Climate Leadership Plan paved the way for the approval of the Trans Mountain Pipeline , a historic decision that will open up new markets for our energy products , creating tens of thousands of jobs and billions of dollars in economic activity . <p> Second , the Climate Leadership Plan protects Albertans ' pocketbooks . Sixty per cent of Alberta families will get full carbon levy rebates . Those rebate cheques start arriving this month . And every penny raised by the levy will be invested back to diversify Alberta 's economy . <p> And third , all Albertans will benefit from energy efficiency programs that will save families money on their home heating bills . <p> These benefits from climate action are possible because the Climate Leadership @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ It 's designed by Albertans , for Albertans . To protect family budgets , households will receive cash rebates to offset costs . <p> Here 's how it works : <p> Sixty per cent of households will get a full rebate of $200 for an adult , $100 for a spouse , and $30 for each child under 18 ( up to four children ) . Those cheques start arriving this month . <p> Full rebates will be provided to single Albertans who earn $47,500 or less , and couples and families who earn $95,000 or less . An additional 6 per cent of households will receive a partial rebate . <p> In addition , the small business tax is being cut by 33 per cent , saving small business owners about $185 million a year . With this cut , Alberta now has the second lowest small business tax rate in the country . As an energy leader , that just does n't make sense . <p> So starting early this year , Albertans will get help to make our homes and businesses more energy efficient . Programs will include free installation and rebates to improve energy efficiency and save money on our heating bills .
