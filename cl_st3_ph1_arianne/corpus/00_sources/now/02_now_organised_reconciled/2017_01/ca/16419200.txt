<h> Letter : The ongoing search for Truth <p> Poor old Pontius Pilate , a public servant of the Roman Empire , was understandably a bit perplexed when faced with somebody who was offering him the Truth with an upper-case T. <p> " What is truth ? " is a question that must surely puzzle many a public servant , wherever he or she may be . Truth , in the hands of those in power , has an uncomfortable way of changing , or shape-shifting , to suit the needs of the government or the corporation . We have heard too many half-truths lately , or almost-truths , or could-be-truths . Even " truths " that are demonstrably lies , but are expected to be swallowed whole anyway , because it suits the liar , and because the liar has dozens of supporters who will swear to the " truth " of the lie . We 're calling it the " Post-Truth Era , " I think ; something absurd like that , anyway . Scientists like their truths to be demonstrable , and demonstrable over and over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and come halfway to meet the Truth with our gift of Faith . We do n't demand proof , like the scientific community does , but we do like to see the Truth being demonstrated now and then in human interactions . You might say we 're predisposed to accept some truths , but the days of blind unquestioning acceptance are long gone . So , what is Truth ? Well , the scientists tell us that climate change is true , but corporations and governments assure us it is a lie -- quite possibly a Chinese conspiracy ! The Acts of the Apostles tells us that amicable co-operation is better than cutthroat competition , and that sharing our resources is better than hoarding . Governments , and the corporations that support them , will deny these truths , because they depend for their continued existence on the envy and greed of consumers/voters . That does n't help us much with discovering what Truth is , nor even what is true and what is a lie , but I think most people prefer to be told the truth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us seem to have a " nose for lies " that warns when we hear a lie . Clearly , if we start accepting the obvious lie as truth , and if we fail to reject this form of public discourse , then we 're in more trouble than I thought we were . <p> Ed Healy Marystown <p> Your comment has been submitted .
