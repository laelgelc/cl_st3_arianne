<h> Minister of Energy should debate in scientific terms <p> I read your comment on the Fraser Institute report on Jan. 18 in the Toronto Sun . <p> I was unpleasantly surprised to see that you , the Ontario Minister of Energy , called someone a " climate denier " because this person does not agree with you . You ask for input , but you do n't want to be criticized . <p> A response from the Minister of Energy should be based on science and not on the Liberal party dogmas . To call the Fraser Institute right-wing is further polarizing , it does not contribute to the discussion , and it is a discredit to you and your ministry . <p> Please explain to me how replacing 25 per cent base load of coal powered electricity with four per cent intermittent wind and solar is possible . From where do you get the missing 21 per cent ? <p> If you had consulted with the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change , you would have known that over 50 per cent of Ontario 's smog comes from the American Midwest . <p> Your government can not take credit for cleaner air without taking into consideration that the closure of industries and the increase of natural gas use for electricity generation in the U.S. has a huge , and perhaps the biggest impact on the air quality in Ontario . <p> I do n't dispute that closing coal generation helps improve air quality in Ontario , but I agree with the Fraser Institute that the effect was negligible , if scrubbers would have been added to coal plants . <p> In Europe ( new ) coal plants are still being used for electricity generating but , with air cleaners in place , they are producing cheap energy , and there are no smog days . <p> You mention 53 smog days in Ontario in 2005 because of coal . How could we still have 30 smog days in 2012 when generating from coal was less than per cent of the electricity capacity ? <p> Windsor had 24 smog days in 2012 . Did the smog travel 600 kilometres from the only Ontario coal plant in Thunder Bay to Windsor , or did it come from just across the border ? <p> Many families in Ontario can not afford to heat their house comfortably . This is caused by your disastrous energy policy , the Green Energy Act . Families have to resort to alternatives like woodstoves and waste oil burners . I think you will agree that the pollution these wood and oil burners create , inside and outside the building , is not improving air quality . <p> Kids ( even kids with health issues ) like the young boy named Mathew , whom you are so compassionate about , are forced to live in poorly heated and moist homes , in great risk of getting ill , hospitalizations or even premature death . And because of your energy policy ! <p> The Ontario government has indeed made a dramatic change , taking money from wind companies , taking away democratic rights from municipalities , and forcing wind projects on unwilling communities . <p> The Ministry of Energy , and especially you , the minister , should be ashamed for the thousands of Ontarians , especially the children and elderly , living in energy poverty .
