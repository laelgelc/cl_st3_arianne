<h> U of C student 's glacier research on ice due to U.S. travel ban <p> PhD Candidate Samira Samimi holds an ice core sample taken from the Greenland Ice Shelf during fieldwork she did in 2016 . Her planned trip back to Greenland may be disrupted due to President Donald Trump 's travel ban affecting seven countries . <p> A PhD candidate working out of the University of Calgary says she never expected her life 's work to be overturned by the actions of a foreign government . <p> Samira Samimi is an Iranian citizen and permanent Canadian resident . She 's also a member of a team that 's funded by NASA , studying meltwater retention in Greenland . <p> She said the study pertains to climate change and glaciology . <p> " I was supposed to join my team on April 17 in Albany , NY to get aboard an C-130 Hercules aircraft with the Air National Guard to fly to Greenland , " she said . <p> However , an executive order from U.S. President Donald Trump is keeping anyone with Iranian passports out of the country . <p> " I do n't even have a visa , " she said . " I had n't applied yet . It was a few months away . I only need to be in Albany for one night . " <p> She said even if she flies commercial to Greenland , she wo n't be able to carry on with the team in the U.S. plane . <p> " That requires me to have a U.S. visa because it 's a U.S. army plane , " she said . <p> Samimi said she ca n't even count on other scientists to collect her data for her . <p> " Each one of us is working on a specific thing and we 're experts in what we 're doing , " she said . <p> She left her equipment in Greenland last year , with many items set up in glaciers collecting data . <p> Her project leader , Mike MacFrerrin at the University of Colorado , said not having Samimi 's data will leave a big hole in the work of the entire team . <p> " The thing that gets me about all this is the utter arbitrariness of it , " he said , " She is a valuable team member who now fears being left behind simply because of her nationality of origin . There is no legitimate excuse for it . " <p> The University of Calgary is offering up help to potentially affected students . <p> In a statement , university president Elizabeth Cannon said any restriction that prevents students and scholars from going abroad for study , teaching or research is deeply concerning .
