More than 150 food-processing firms have closed or left Canada since 2008 , affecting almost 30,000 jobs <p> Every bite we take has carbon tax implications . <p> From farm to fork , food is responsible for about 25% of the world 's greenhouse gas emissions . And every decision consumers make when selecting food impacts the environment . <p> Many want to do something about it . <p> In Canada , the federal government has decided that means a tax on carbon emissions . The program starts in 2018 with a $10-per-tonne carbon price plan and ramps up to $50 a tonne by 2022 . <p> Many have pointed out that energy costs will rise because of a carbon tax . But little attention has been given to what will happen to food costs .
