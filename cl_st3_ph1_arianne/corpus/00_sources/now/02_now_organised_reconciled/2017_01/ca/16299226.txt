<p> Half of Canadians are within $300 a month of not being able to pay their bills . This is a serious problem . <p> Climate change is a threat to the environment and to economy . The problem is how to balance both . We need an incrementally rising carbon price in Canada so that it is at least $150 tonne by 2030 , but we ca n't burden already overburdened Canadian taxpayers . <p> Here in Sudbury , we host the national headquarters for the Citizens ' Climate Lobby . Its members have diligently researched and educated us . The evidence is clear . The solution is a revenue-neutral carbon price . My favourite policy is a carbon fee and dividend .
