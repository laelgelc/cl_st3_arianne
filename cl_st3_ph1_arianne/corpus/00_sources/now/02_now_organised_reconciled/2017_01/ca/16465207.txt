They all know what he 's talking about . <p> This year 's Davos gathering is actually focused on the rise of populism and simple-minded attacks on globalization ( Trump , Brexit et al . ) . That 's only to be expected , since the world 's ultra-rich are potentially threatened by that sort of thing .
