<h> Shahrul Malaysia 's new permanent rep to UN <p> Shahrul , a senior career diplomat , formerly served as director-general of the Asean-Malaysia National Secretariat . <p> PUTRAJAYA : Muhammad Shahrul Ikram Yaakob has been appointed as the new Permanent Representative of Malaysia to the United Nations ( UN ) in New York , Wisma Putra announced today . <p> Shahrul , 55 , a senior career diplomat , who has served in the foreign ministry since 1988 , is expected to assume his new post in the first week of February . <p> He received his letter of appointment from Foreign Minister Anifah Aman in a ceremony at the ministry here today . <p> Prior to his new appointment , Shahrul served as director-general of the Asean-Malaysia National Secretariat from 2013 to 2016 . <p> He has held several key positions at the ministry , including as Malaysia 's ambassador to Austria from 2010 to 2013 , ambassador to Qatar from 2007 to 2010 and counsellor at the Malaysian embassy in Vienna , Austria , from 2002 to 2005 . <p> Wisma Putra , in the statement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the UN was vital to promote and uphold Malaysia 's interests and foreign policy objectives on issues deliberated incorporated in the 11th Malaysia Plan , it said . <p> Additionally , it said , the Permanent Representative was responsible for continuing the initiatives undertaken by Malaysia at the United Nations Security Council during its non-permanent membership for the term 2015-2016 . <p> This included promoting the five priorities pursued at the Security Council , namely on moderation , mediation , peacekeeping , peace-building and UNSC reform , it said . <p> " Malaysia will continue to support the work of the United Nations , the pillars of which are international peace and security , social
