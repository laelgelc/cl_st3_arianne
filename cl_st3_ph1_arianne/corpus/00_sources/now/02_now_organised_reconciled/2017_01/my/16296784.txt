<h> Letters <h> Forget the fireworks for celebrations <p> EVERY year fireworks displays attract thousands of revellers to usher in the New Year . But is it worth it at the end of the day ? <p> Take the Pandamaran Complex fireworks display in Klang recently . It attracted 15,000 people . At the stroke of midnight the cheering took a dramatic turn into pandemonium when the thousands ran helter-skelter when the explosions occurred . <p> For starters , the event was unlicensed . Fourteen people , including children , were injured . An effusion of profuse apologies from the organiser MPK ( Klang Municipal Council ) followed . The council even volunteered to foot the injured 's medical expenses . <p> Let us pause for a moment and consider the moral dilemma of the New Year 's Eve fireworks . <p> Fireworks display may be awesome to behold but the money that is burnt could be used to help those in need such as the homeless or abused victims or single mothers .
