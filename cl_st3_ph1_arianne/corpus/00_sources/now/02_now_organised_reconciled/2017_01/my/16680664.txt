<h> Malaysia 's first carbon credit firm to offset CO2 emissions <p> Climate Change Protector , specialising in renewable and sustainable energy , is tasked to preserve the flora and fauna of 396,000ha of forest in the region . <p> KUALA LUMPUR : Malaysia 's first carbon credit rating company hopes to see other states follow Kelantan 's move after the state gave the company 25% of its land mass for forest preservation and to reduce carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) emissions . <p> Under the United Nations ( UN ) collaborative programme -- Reducing Environment from Deforestation and Degradation ( REDD ) -- Climate Change Protector ( CCP ) Sdn Bhd was given 396,000 hectares of forest in a move to preserve the flora and fauna of the region . <p> The company , specialising in renewable and sustainable energy , received the project under the UN 's REDD initiative for a 30-year concession period with the Kelantan government . <p> CCP chief executive officer Tang Too Siah said the recent memorandum of agreement ( MoA ) signed on Jan 9 was the Kelantan state government 's initiative to protect the region from illegal and legal mining activities . <p> " The size ( of the area ) is enormous , this is the first time the government has given 25% of its land mass to such a commitment , " he told Bernama . <p> The overall development goal of the programme is to reduce forest emissions and to enhance carbon stocks in the forest while contributing to national sustainable development , Tang added . <p> In the context of forest , carbon stock refers to the amount of carbon stored in the world 's forest ecosystem , mainly in living biomass and soil . <p> A dossier released by Greenpeace International in March 2013 on " Identifying High Carbon Stock Forest for Protection " states that tropical forests hold large stores of carbon , harbours important biodiversity and are critical for the livelihoods of many local communities . <p> Tang highlighted that the conversion of these forests to agriculture or plantations , made it one of the largest emitters of greenhouse gases . <p> " CO2 is one of the main causes of the greenhouse effect ... it is mostly produced by manufacturing industries . <p> " Since CCP is a carbon credit trading company , our goal is to offset the emission of CO2 via our preservation , " he said . <p> Currently , industries are allocated with a certain amount of pollution in the form of a tradable certificate or permit that allows the right to emit one tonne of CO2 or another greenhouse gas . <p> Tang said once the industries reached their quota , they must buy more carbon credits to exceed their current limit . <p> " Right now , the company is targeting the European market , " he said . <p> On the prospect of this initiative , he said there would be positive cash flow between the state government and the company , which was expected at 55% and 45% of earnings , respectively . <p> " The returns would be seen after the scientific evaluation and verification on how much carbon stock we can get . This will be done by certified UN auditors within 24 months from the date of the MoA signing , " he said . <p> In addition to profit returns , the state will receive the latest drone , thermal scanning and surveillance technology , derived from the project for free . <p> " A joint task force would be formed to monitor the area for any illegal activities . The company will engage the latest technology , which will then be handed over to the government for free , including hardware and software , " Tang said . <p> Malaysia 's commitment towards the conservation of its natural tropical rainforests would be seen as a positive initiative , given that the country is one of the leaders in producing crude palm oil , he added . <p> Tang hoped other states would follow suit as the initiative would shed a positive light on the international community , in the country 's bid to do its part in offsetting inevitable climate change . <h> Comments <p> Readers are required to have a valid Facebook account to comment on this story .
