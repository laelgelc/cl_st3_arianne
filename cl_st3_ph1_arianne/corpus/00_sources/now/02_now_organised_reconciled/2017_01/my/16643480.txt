<h> Environment <h> More than 40 detained in Chile for spreading forest fires <p> SANTIAGO : Authorities have detained 43 people suspected of stoking some of the deadly Chilean forest fires that have killed 11 people and destroyed large swaths of land , President Michelle Bachelet said Sunday . <p> For nearly two weeks , fires have raged across seven regions in south and central Chile , devastating more than 400,000 hectares ( 988,000 acres ) , Bachelet said in her latest update on the tragedy . <p> She added that 43 people had been detained " for their possible responsibility in the forest fires . " <p> Most of the suspects were apprehended in the hardest-hit regions of O'Higgins , Maule and Biobio . They could face a penalty of up to 20 years in prison . <p> Of the 130 active forest fires , 50 are under control while 66 others are still being battled , officials said . <p> The Chilean government is distributing aid to those affected by the blaze , most of whom are farmers and ranchers whose homes , animals and land has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ extinguish the fires , including members of the military , police , public officials and residents . <p> Of these , more than a half have come from abroad to help with what Chile has called the largest emergency operation in its history . <p> Fires are common in Chile 's parched forests during the southern hemisphere 's summer . Most are caused by human activity . More than 35 people have been arrested and accused of playing a role in starting or propagation the fires . <p> Fire activity this year was worse than usual because of a drought attributed by environmentalists to climate change . -AFP
