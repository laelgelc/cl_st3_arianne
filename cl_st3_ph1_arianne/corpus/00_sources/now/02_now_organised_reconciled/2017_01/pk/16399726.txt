<p> Notwithstanding the fact that Pakistan is vigorously pursuing a policy of peaceful co-existence with its neighbours , relations with India and Afghanistan continue to remain under ever-growing stress . Apart from some issues on the bilateral level , the nosedive in relations with both of them owes much to our relations with the US ; difficulties have persisted since 2010 due to the Raymond incident , Salala attack and the Abbottabad operation . Though relations have improved to some extent as a result of the revival of dialogue with the US and the visits of Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif and former COAS General Raheel Sharif to the US , there are still some kinks to be removed . <p> Pakistan has made several overtures towards India to restart the suspended dialogue and build bonhomie with the purpose of starting a new era of relations dictated by the new emerging regional and global realities but the response unfortunately has been very discouraging . India has not reciprocated Pakistan 's desire for peaceful co-existence . It wants relations on its own terms .
