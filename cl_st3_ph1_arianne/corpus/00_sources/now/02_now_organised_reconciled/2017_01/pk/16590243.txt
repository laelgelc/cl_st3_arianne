<p> ISLAMABAD : The Executive Committee of the National Economic Council ( ECNEC ) allowed the Finance Division to release development funds for approved 2453 MW ( Gross ) Combined Cycle Power Plant at Haveli Bahadur Shah and Balloki . <p> Finance Minister , Mohammad Ishaq Dar , chaired the ECNEC meeting on Wednesday where the Committee considered and approved various projects relating to climate change , energy , health , and higher education sectors . On a proposal of the Ministry of Planning , Development &amp; Reform , the ECNEC approved the project regarding Green Pakistan Programme - Revival of Forestry Resources in Pakistan at an estimated cost of Rs. 3652.142 million . <p> Projects of Expanded Programme of Immunization ( EPI ) Balochistan at a total cost of Rs. 4,998.91 million with foreign exchange component of Rs. 713.93 million were approved . <p> The project aims to plant indigenous and fast growing local tree species along canal and roadsides in Punjab , KPK , and Sindh with Olive and Kikar Phulai forests in Kala Chitta , Pabbi Rasul and Fort Monroe in Punjab , a Mangrove forest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prevent erosion in Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu &amp; Kashmir , etc . <p> ECNEC approved a Higher Education Commission proposal of PhD Scholarship Programme under US-Pakistan Knowledge Corridor ( phase-1 ) at a rationalised cost of Rs. 18million including foreign exchange component of Rs. 17 million . Under this , a segment of the US-Pakistan Knowledge Corridor Higher Education Committee ( HEC ) will send 1500 PhD students to top ranking US universities . The HEC will also provide counseling and guidance facilities to candidates taking GRE and TOFEL tests and proposal writing to assist students in seeking admission in US universities . ECNEC directed HEC to ensure participation of students or scholars from all over the country on merit . ECNEC also approved the HEC proposal for establishment of Sub Campuses of Public Sector Universities at district level at a rationalised cost of Rs. 5.2 million .
