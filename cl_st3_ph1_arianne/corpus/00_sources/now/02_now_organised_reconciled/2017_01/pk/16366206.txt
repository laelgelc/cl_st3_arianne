<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan 's vulnerability to the impacts of climate change has been increasing with the passage of time despite that its contribution to the global warming is very much negligible . It is currently on the seventh position among the world top 10 countries vulnerable to the vagaries of climate change and global warming . <p> Pakistan 's greenhouse gas emission is less than a percent of global emission , but still it is most vulnerable to its impacts . " In 2015 , Pakistan was at the 8th position and now it is on the 7th place among top 10 countries of the world which are most vulnerable to the climate change , " Zahid Hamid , Minister for Climate Change , said this while presiding over the UNIDO 's Cleantech Awards distribution ceremony here . The awards were distributed under the Global Cleantech Innovation Programme ( GCIP ) of UNIDO . <p> GCIP is a global programme , aimed at supporting and converting innovations in the field of clean technologies into successful businesses .
