Obama , America 's first black president , was bowing out with a message of hope . But it was not an honest assessment of the health of American democracy and the world after eight years under his administration . Obama has always been a man who had a way with words . But the ' yes we did ' could only be read as a clich ? , not the reality of an eight-year presidency .
