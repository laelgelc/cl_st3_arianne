<h> School attendance in Karachi falls to 40% because of cold wave <p> Though attendance at the private schools fell to 40 percent after the cold wave at the end of winter vacations , the Sindh government has rejected proposals calling for an extension of the vacations , it has been reliably learnt . <p> The provincial government , according to the sources , has called an emergency meeting of the education department 's steering committee between January 18 and 20 to discuss the issue , but the committee will only discuss the dates for next year 's winter vacations . The committee called officials of the meteorology office to review summer and winter vacations for the next academic year due to climate change . <p> According to the sources , the provincial government is considering to issue instructions for increasing school hours by one hour to cope up with the emergent situation . <p> The meteorological office has predicted an increase in the intensity of the cold wave , along with rainfall in the next week .
