<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan 's 55 most prestigious companies with significant contribution for social development through different remarkable projects and activities will be given awards at the ninth Corporate Social Responsibility ( CSR ) summit to be held here on January 19 . <p> Federal Minister for Planning and Development Ahsan Iqbal will be the chief guest at the ceremony , said a press release here on Saturday . Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce &amp; Industries ( FPCCI ) President Zubair Tufail will open the inaugural session while Ministry of Climate Change Secretary Syed Abu Akif , Benazir Income Support Programme ( BISP ) Chairperson Marvi Memon and others will chair the second session . <p> This event is being organised by the National Forum for Environment &amp; Health . According to NFEH President Naeem Quershi , all the award winning companies have played a vital role in empowerment of society in the fields of education , health , environment , socio-economic development , welfare projects for under privileged and under-served masses of the country .
