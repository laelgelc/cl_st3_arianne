<h> Water shortage in Absor <p> It is known to everyone that problems of Turabt are mounting but no one seems to be serious to think about their redressal . In fact , Absor is the biggest part of Turbat populated by a large number of people . Unfortunately , the area has been without water since 1st January and residents are in hot waters without water . ( forcing the residents of the area to suffer a lot on this account . ) Moreover , the tanker mafia is selling water for a very extortionate price . We , the residents of Absor , are enduring a lot because the concerned authorities are keeping a mum about the issue of water . I request the government of Balochistan to prioritize the issue since Turbat is the second largest city of Balochistan . The Government is , perhaps , well verse of the fact that climate change has worsened conditions in the area as Turbat has not received rain for the last five years .
