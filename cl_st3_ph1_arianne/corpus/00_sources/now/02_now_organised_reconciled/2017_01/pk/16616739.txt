<p> Islamabad : Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid has proposed the holding of pre-summit meeting of Bishkek summit in Pakistan . <p> He made the proposal during a meeting of the Global Snow Leopard Steering Committee held in Nepal , said a statement issued here . President of the Kyrgyz Republic Almazbek Atambayev proposed to host a Global Snow Leopard Summit and Green Investment Forum in Bishkek on Sept 7 and 8 . <p> The purpose of the summit is to energise the progress of the GSLEP Programme at its mid-point ( 2013 -- 2020 ) , tackle the growing threats to snow leopards and their habitats , and raise financial resources for GSLEP Implementation . <p> The event is expected to help raise resources to not only support implementation of national and global priorities identified by the programme , but also to foster integrated development of local economies and conservation of wildlife in Asia 's mountain areas . <p> Founded on the principle that ' to conserve our natural ecosystems and species , we must gain the support of local people ' , the Global Snow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the year 2013 , is championed by the governments of 12 snow leopard range countries , and supported by national and international organizations from across the world . <p> The Steering Committee of the GSLEP Programme , comprising of Forest and Environment Ministers ( or their designates ) of the 12 snow leopard range countries , met on the 20th of January 2017 in Kathmandu Nepal . A two-day long stocktaking workshop on the status of management plans of the GSLEP landscapes , and overall progress of national snow leopard and ecosystem priorities preceded the meeting .
