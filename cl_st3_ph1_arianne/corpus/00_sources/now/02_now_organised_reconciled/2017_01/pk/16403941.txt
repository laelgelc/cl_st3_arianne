<p> KARACHI : Climate change is playing an important role in the ecosystem dynamic of the Arabian Sea as fishermen have reported an unusual increase in the population of Purpleback flying squid in Pakistani waters during November 2016 to January 2017 . <p> The fishermen have been sighting increase in the occurrence of oceanic ( Sthenoteuthis oualaniensis ) , sometimes caught in their gillnets . In the beginning of this year , they located unprecedented concentration of this squid in offshore waters between 20o and 24o N both along Sindh and Balochistan coasts especially in the Khori Great Bank along Sindh coast and in off Malan along Balochistan coast . <p> This squid lives in open waters from the surface down to depths of around 1,000 m of ocean . It exhibits diurnal vertical migration and moves from deeper layers to surface or shallower waters during night . <p> Commenting on the development , Rab Nawaz , Senior Director Programmes , WWF-Pakistan stressed the need for studying long term changes in the oceanographic factors that may result in increase of certain species .
