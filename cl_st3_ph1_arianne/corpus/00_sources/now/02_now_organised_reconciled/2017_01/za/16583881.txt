When it put its power behind spreading a liberal brand of democracy , it became a global trend . <p> The US is not an ordinary state . It is not just another global power . It is not a typical nation-state , in fact , but is a state with a wide international character that millions in the world feel through the force of US commerce , the influence of the dollar and Coca-Cola , the violence of regime change designs and the war on terror excesses . <p> Every twist and turn in US conduct and foreign policy affects us all everywhere in the world . The US has the burden to act responsibly and with consideration for what ripple effects its actions have on the globalised world under its care . <p> It is a responsibility that obligates Washington to understand and consider the interests of the international system by which the world system operates , the UN to regional organisations to international finance institutions . He has promised to focus on building the US while maintaining relations with others in the world . <p> This can imply that the US will want to act like a normal nation state .
