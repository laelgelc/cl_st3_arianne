The African Growth and Opportunity Act ( AGOA ) is a United States trade agreement , enacted on 18 May 2000 . It seeks to provide assistance to African economies and to facilitate bilateral trade between Africa and the US through the provision of , for example , tax free exports from African countries to the US . <p> As one of South Africa 's largest trading partners , trade with the US amounted to $12,7bn in income for South Africa in 2015 . South African exports to the US were valued at $896m for the year to date , in June 2016 . The AGOA has also since its inception created 60,000 jobs for South Africans in the labour-intensive agriculture and automotive industries . The agreement was recently extended till 2025 . However , negotiations to renew the Act are set to take place during Trump 's presidency .
