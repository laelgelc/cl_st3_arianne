<h> Late Indigo Shire Councillor Don Chambers has been recognised , posthumously , in the Australia Day Honours Awards with an Order of Australia Medal ( OAM ) . <p> The late Indigo Shire Councillor Don Chambers received a posthumous Medal of the Order of Australia for his services to local government and to the Victorian community on Australia Day.Cr Chambers , who died suddenly in October 2015 , was not only a fierce local community advocate , but a three-time Indigo Shire Councillor , active sportsman , environmentalist , devoted family man and a much loved member of the Rutherglen community.Indigo Shire Mayor Cr Jenny O'Connor said the council was extremely pleased Cr Chambers had been recognised posthumously in the Australia Day Honours Awards . " This is such a fitting award for Don , " she said . " It recognises a unique person whose drive and commitment for the greater good of his community is well known . " It would be true to say Don was ' a legend ' -- for many reasons . " Cr O'Connor said Cr Chambers served Indigo Shire Council and the community with great determination over two decades and his vision was for a fairer and more sharing community . " While Don spoke out loud and clear for his Rutherglen community he always had an eye for the bigger picture and where Rutherglen and the Indigo Shire fitted in the overall scheme of things , " she said . " Indigo Shire benefitted from his strong connections at both a regional level and at State and Federal Government level . " He was also known for his commitment and passionate advocacy for recognition and development of aboriginal cultural heritage and was the prime driver of our council flying the Aboriginal flag alongside the state and Commonwealth flags . " Cr O'Connor said Don believed the most pressing environmental issues in Victoria ( and the world ) were climate change , with water supply and our soil condition ( salinity , acid soil and carbon content ) needing urgent attention in Australia . " His passions on waste and resource recovery , planning and development , equity in rating and the promotion of agriculture and economic development across the shire were a highlight of his council life , " she said.Don Chambers advocated strongly for his beliefs , particularly in relation to the environment and waste.He was on the board of the North East Regional Waste Management Group ( chair for 4 years ) , Eco Recycle Victoria , Sustainability Victoria , North East Water and a committee member of the Water and Land Care Advisory Committees for North East Catchment Management Authority.He was Chairman of Keep Australia Beautiful National Association 10 years , and was Chairman of Keep Australia Beautiful Victoria . On his return to Indigo Shire Council in 2012 , he represented Indigo Shire on the North East Waste and Resource Recovery Forum , and was Chair of the North East Waste Resource Recovery Group Board . " In his leisure time , Don was a member of Masters Athletics Victoria and his local track and field club at Wodonga and competed regularly in local , interstate , national and World Masters track and field championships right up to the year he died and he brought home more than a few ribbons , " Cr O'Connor said . " Indigo Shire Council remembers him and honours him as a fine example of civic pride and leadership , and as a special friend . " Don fought hard for what he believed in , saw every effort through to the end and treated everyone as equals and deserving of respect . " He was , and is , a true legend . "
