<h> State under siege <h> Victoria the most bushfire prone State . <p> A new Climate Council report believes Victoria is at risk of facing longer and increasingly dangerous bushfire seasons as the climate warms . <p> The report found Victoria was the state most affected by bushfires , despite it comprising only three per cent of Australia 's land mass . <p> The Climate Council 's Professor Tim Flannery said the increasing severity , frequency and the lengthening fire seasons would strain Victoria 's existing resources for fighting and managing fires . <p> ' ' Victoria , as the state worst-affected by bushfires in Australia , is on the frontline of increasing bushfire risk due to climate change , ' ' Prof Flannery said . <p> ' ' Reducing greenhouse gas emissions , in addition to preparing communities , emergency services and the health sector , is an absolute priority if we are to protect Victorians from worsening fire danger conditions in the future .
