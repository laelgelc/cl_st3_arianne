<h> Andrew Masterson <p> " Trump is just a magic pill , " said Helen Czerski , English physicist , BBC science television presenter and , most recently , successful author . <p> As descriptions of the new US President go , it 's pretty mild , but it serves Czerski 's point . Life -- stuff like climate change and pandemics and antibiotic resistance -- is complicated , and even the most agile science communicators ca n't make it simple . <p> And this explains the rise of populist demagogues . Scientists , burdened by actual evidence , equivocate and talk in probabilities . Trump and his appointees , dismissing evidence , talk in certainties , however wrong .
