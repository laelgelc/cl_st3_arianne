<h> How Barack Obama rebuilt the United States economy <p> Barack Obama 's measured administration has set up the US economy for recovery . AP <p> by Martin Wolf <p> How should we assess the economic success or failure of Barack Obama 's presidency ? This is a difficult question to answer . After all , the incumbent of the White House can not determine the performance of the huge and complex US economy . Indeed , policy initiatives usually have a modest impact . But the story of Obama 's presidency is a little different from the usual , since it began amid the worst financial crisis since the 1930s . <p> If we consider the disaster he inherited and the determination of the Republicans in Congress to ensure he would fail , his record is clearly successful . This does not mean it is perfect . Nor does it mean the US confronts few economic challenges . Neither statement would be close to correct . Household net worth has also reached 50 per cent above its 2008 level . <p> Yet Obama was interested in more than economic recovery . He tried to move the US closer to the universal health insurance taken for granted in other high-income countries . The Affordable Care Act ( " Obamacare " ) has added an estimated 20 million adults and 3 million children to the insurance rolls . Healthcare costs have also grown exceptionally slowly since the law was enacted , relative to past US performance . <p> These are all genuine achievements .
