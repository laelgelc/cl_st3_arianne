<h> Climate change now driving New Zealand 's Battle for the Birds <p> The rifleman , robins , keas , wekas , kekapos -- and of course the kiwi -- they 're all part of the remarkable diversity of birds in New Zealand . <p> The bad news is that an estimated 25 million of those native birds are killed every year by introduced predators , mainly mammals such as rats , stoats , the feral cat and even Australia 's common brushtail possum . <p> To deal with the problem , the country has unveiled an ambitious plan for a predator-free New Zealand by 2050 . <p> And now experts say that climate change is adding a new imperative for a long term solution , with massive plagues of rats , mice and stoats invading the famous New Zealand beech forests in recent years .
