<p> Scientists say the world is closer to catastrophe than at any time since the height of the Cold War . <p> The Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists has moved the hands of its symbolic Doomsday Clock forward by 30 seconds overnight . <p> It cited nuclear weapons , climate change and the election of US President Donald Trump as reasons for the move . It 's the closest the clock has come to midnight in 64 years . <p> Featured : Rachel Bronson , executive director and publisher of the Bulletin of the Atomic ScientistsThomas Pickering , retired US ambassador , member of the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists ' board of sponsorsDavid Titley , former chief operating officer of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration
