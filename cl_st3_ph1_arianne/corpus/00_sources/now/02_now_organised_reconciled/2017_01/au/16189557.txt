You know , where head-hunting was a thing up until a couple of decades ago . We just drove through it on the most challenging off-road trail down under . <p> I had been to Cape York once before , when a few friends and I managed to take a WWII-era Jeep across the Old Telegraph Track , considered to be Australia 's most-technical off-road trail . After I moved to Australia , and subsequently quit my job , we decided we needed to go back on our off-road lap of the country . Why ? Because you can jump off pristine waterfalls , camp on deserted beaches a few hundred kilometres long , and then end your day in an ancient rainforest . <p> For the adventure traveller , there 's two main goals for Cape York : first to tackle the Old Telegraph Track , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ vehicles are tall , and then reach the tip , Australia 's northernmost point .
