<p> The Clean Energy Regulator has increased pressure on ERM Power , Alinta Energy and other electricity retailers to fulfil their renewable energy obligations and abide by the " spirit of the law " and help meet the 2020 target . <p> Retailers that elect to pay penalties instead of submitting renewable energy certificates should " make concrete plans " to make good the shortfall , the regulator said amid heightening concerns about the viability of the scheme . <p> A surge in the cost of Large-scale Generation Certificates ( LGCs ) , each of which represent 1 megawatt-hour of renewable energy , has meant that for some retailers it makes economic sense to pay the " shortfall charge " rather than source expensive certificates . <h> Related Quotes <h> Company Profile <p> The actions have also drawn criticism from some parts of the broader business and wider community . <p> But the companies have been puzzled with the reaction , particularly from the regulator . ERM is " disappointed with the emotive and unbalanced response " , a spokeswoman said , adding the critics " have not taken time to understand the design of the scheme and the benefits that can arise from its flexible design " . <p> Alinta chief executive Jeff Dimery said the company 's strategy actually worked in favour of the scheme 's objectives . <p> " Alinta 's strategy supports the aim of the scheme , minimises the cost of meeting its RET liability which is beneficial for both customers and shareholders and promotes greater competition in the energy market on a go-forward basis , " Mr Dimery said . <p> Under the rules of the RET , retailers that pay a penalty have three years to surrender LGCs and get the charge refunded . But in the meantime , their customers are paying premiums for green power but the money is going straight into government coffers rather than on supporting wind and solar projects . <p> Clean Energy Regulator chair Chloe Munro has accused those intentionally defaulting on their LGC obligations as " undermining " the RET scheme - which legislates 23.5 per cent of power from renewable sources by 2020 and which some industry source say is already unlikely to be met . <p> " We have been transparent and clear that we view the intentional failure to surrender certificates as a failure to comply with the spirit of the law and an undermining of the objectives of the scheme , " the regulator said . <p> " For the 2016 compliance year there are sufficient certificates in the market to enable all liable entities to meet their obligations . " <p> Energy Minister Josh Frydenberg said ERM and Alinta 's commercial decisions was not reflective of industry experience to date with the RET , saying sufficient renewable certificates in the market and a pipeline of renewable projects demonstrated investor confidence . <p> But Opposition Leader Bill Shorten - whose party has proposed 50 per cent renewables by 2030 - said Prime Minister Malcolm Turnbull 's " weak " position on climate change was now affecting the RET . <p> " The whole Renewable Energy Target is under threat which throws the whole market for renewable investment into doubt . They are providing no certainty whatsoever , " Mr Shorten said on Tuesday . <p> Clean Energy Council chief executive Kane Thornton said the RET was working properly and was set to deliver deals worth $2 billion of large-scale solar alone , but he said there should be some concern about ERM and Alinta 's behaviour . <p> " Customers should be justifiably concerned by any decision by an energy retailer to deliberately breach the RET legislation to prop up their revenue , " Mr Thornton said . <p> " Many of ERM 's customers who have chosen electricity products such as Green Power would naturally expect that it is meeting its commitments to support renewable energy products . " <p> ERM said it had purchased " millions " of LGCs from renewables projects in recent years and will be " fully compliant " with the rules . <p> " We will be paying the Clean Energy Regulator $123 million in the coming weeks , and reserving the right to surrender LGCs in the future or the 2016 year , a spokeswoman said , adding that the scheme was " designed with flexibility in mind . " <p> " This is a matter of timing , " she said . " In 2016 we did buy a substantial number of LGCs from renewable sources . " <p> Mr Dimery said Alinta had supported the development of several large renewables projects such as the Bald Hills wind farm in Victoria and the Walkaway wind farm in Western Australia . More recently Alinta bought the rights to develop a 500 MW renewable energy project pipeline , including the Yandin wind farm in WA . <p> He said Alinta had also committed to purchasing LGCs from other developers of wind and solar projects to be commissioned over the next two years , " at rates significantly cheaper than the current spot market " . <p> Prices for LGCs surged last year as the race to meet the 2020 target became evident , and currently are trading at about $88 . Under the RET scheme , the shortfall charge is set at $65 per certificate , which is not tax deductible , making its effective cost $93 . ERM said it paid the penalty rather than source 1.9 million LGCs at a cost of about $86.
