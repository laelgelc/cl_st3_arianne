<h> The deepening crisis of capitalism <p> In the six months since the federal election we have seen an acceleration of the ruling class 's neo-liberal agenda . The continuing cuts and privatisations are rationalised by Turnbull 's three-word slogan , " Jobs and Growth " , but the effect seems to be quite the opposite . <p> In the September quarter last year , Australia 's GDP shrank by 0.5% , the biggest drop since the 2008 Global Financial Crisis . <p> The gap between rich and poor continues to widen , with the top 20% of households receiving half the nation 's income , while the bottom 20% gets just 4% . The generational wealth gap is widening too -- Baby Boomers own 53% of the wealth in Australia . These figures point to a disappearing middle class . <p> While the average household income is $107,000 , the top two million households earn more than double that -- $260,000 . That is 12 times more than the $22,000 earned by the bottom 20% of households . <p> The net wealth figures in each group reveal an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an average of $2.5 million , and own 62% of Australia 's private wealth -- 71 times that of the lowest 20% . <p> Housing stress , defined as spending 30% or more of household income on rent , continues to worsen , particularly for low income earners . At a time when more than 100,000 people are homeless , crisis accommodation services are turning away more than half of all those seeking new accommodation and public housing stock has declined . <h> Attacks on workers <p> Each month last year , fewer people were recorded as being in full-time work while part-time work continues to rise .
