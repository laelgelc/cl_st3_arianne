<p> This study was carried out prior to the flood defence works carried out at the river . <p> With climate change bringing about more frequent flooding episodes , how flood risks are perceived is important , according to the study . <p> It is known that that people respond differently to various environmental , technological and health risks , with people often worrying more about less likely catastrophic events like a plane crash or nuclear accident , rather than about risks that impact over time or those which they 've become accustomed to , and sometimes even decided to ignore . <p> A research team in UCD Earth Institute recently published some risk perception findings in the journal Risk Analysis . <p> Their findings emphasised the important role that people 's individual perception of flood risk can play in the implementation of flood policy . <p> This research , conducted in residential areas close to the River Dargle in Bray that flooded during Hurricane Charley , found that significant numbers of people living there judge their homes to be further away from the area at risk of flooding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , project investigator , ' This is important , as people who do not perceive themselves to be exposed to flood risk are less likely to undertake preventive actions that will reduce the harmful impacts of flooding on them . ' <p> Whilst risk can be measured scientifically , there is a very human element in how people perceive it with worry about flooding an important factor . <p> Dr Finbarr Brereton , project investigator added that ' it was of interest when we found that the real distance that people live away from the flood zone is not actually a critical influence on their level of worry . ' <p> In fact , the study found that the main driver was perception of distance , i.e. individuals ' own personal judgement about how close they live to the area prone to flooding . <p> This Bray-based study is also important as the household surveys were undertaken prior to the commencement of the flood relief scheme on the River Dargle , ' so it will be very interesting to see how people 's perception of flood risk changes when @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Professor Peter Clinch , Chair of Public Policy at UCD , who also sits on the government-appointed National Expert Advisory Council on Climate Change added , ' crucially , a significant level of misperception was found amongst householders in the study . Hence , future public-authority led campaigns will need to make a special effort to target those individuals who may be misjudging their exposure to flooding or other climate impacts to try and make sure that they are ready in the event of future flooding or storm impacts . ' <p> The scientists said that harmful climate-related impacts on households and wider communities are of growing concern and it is important that these complex issues are understood as future awareness-raising activities will be required to help people adapt to the inevitable impacts of climate change .
