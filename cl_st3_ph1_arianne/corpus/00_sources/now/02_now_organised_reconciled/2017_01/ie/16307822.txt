They deliver key ecosystem services in relation to biodiversity , climate change mitigation , flood control , habitat linkage , water and soil protection , and landscape enhancement . <p> Many of our native woodlands also provide for outdoor recreation and are an ideal place for young and old to learn about nature and the environment . <p> With careful ' close-to nature ' management , native woodlands can provide valuable timber and non-timber products and services .
