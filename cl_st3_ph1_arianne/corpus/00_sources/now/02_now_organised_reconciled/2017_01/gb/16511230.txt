<p> So much of what 's happening today in the world mirrors the 1930s , the period that gave rise to fascism , the second world war and the Holocaust . At the time , there was rising inequality , failing economies and far-right populism , as nation states retreated from the global stage in an effort to shore-up support at home . Sound familiar ? <p> These are not the ideal conditions to embark on the ambitious UN 's sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) , with targets including ending poverty and achieving gender equality by 2030 . <p> We need the optimistic conditions of the late 1940s , not the 1930s , to take on the goals . The post-war period ushered in global cooperation never before seen : the Universal Declaration of Human Rights , the sympathetic Marshall Plan , the UN charter , the Geneva conventions . All of these recognised that our wellbeing relied on minimum standards , shared ambition and interdependency . <p> Tragically , the dark clouds of the 1930s hover ominously above us .
