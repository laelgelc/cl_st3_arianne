<p> The last 12 months were a landmark year in many ways , but one of its less heralded moments came from Britain 's power industry . Because for the first time in our history this country is now fuelled more by wind energy than by coal . In fact , there was even one day - May 10 - when no British electricity was produced by coal whatsoever , a situation which has not occurred since coal power stations first came online in 1882 . <p> This is a remarkable and rapid decline . Only a year earlier coal accounted for nearly a quarter of our energy needs . But the Government has now moved aggressively against coal .
