The first Chinese President to attend the annual gathering of the global elite , he surprised delegates with the vehemence of his defence of free markets . Meanwhile , the imminent inauguration of a potentially protectionist US President hung heavily over proceeding . <p> But there was a more subtle shift at a corporate level . The banks , which have been the whipping boys for a decade of meetings , appeared to have made a collective decision to play the political game with a touch more nous .
