<h> Forest fires ravaging southern Chile have now killed 11 people , President Michelle Bachelet said Saturday , as firefighters waged an all-out battle to extinguish the raging infernos . <p> Bachelet told reporters that more than 9,000 firefighters and other responders have been enlisted to battle the fires , which have destroyed almost 1.2 million acres ( 480,000 hectares ) in seven regions of the South American nation . <p> " We are fighting the fire without pause and without precedent , " Bachelet declared . <p> More than 4,500 volunteer firefighters and members of the country 's national forest brigades plus some 4,600 police officers and other public officials are battling the blaze . <p> The fires have forced more than two thousand people into shelters , and have damaged or destroyed more than 4,000 dwellings . <p> Among the countries that have dispatched aid to help put out the infernos are Argentina , Spain , France , Portugal and Mexico . <p> A Russian aircraft with a 42-ton water-carrying capacity is due to arrive on Sunday , while Brazil and Peru were also expected to send @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's national forest service , Conaf , there are a total of 110 separate fires , 50 of which are under control , while 60 are still being fought . <p> Fires are common in Chile 's parched forests during the southern hemisphere 's summer .
