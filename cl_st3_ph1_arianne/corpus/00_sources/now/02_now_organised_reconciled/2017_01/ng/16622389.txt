And they 're deadly serious . <p> Each shift takes into account major threats to civilization , including climate change . However , it assesses -- first and foremost -- the threat of nuclear war . <p> This year the Bulletin is especially concerned with the rise of totalitarian and authoritarian regimes , and North Korea 's recent weapons tests .
