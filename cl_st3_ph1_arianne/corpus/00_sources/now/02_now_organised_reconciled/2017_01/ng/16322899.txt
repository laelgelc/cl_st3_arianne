" <p> For Samuel Jackson , the Affordable Care Act was a pivotal accomplishment in Obama presidency . <p> " The moment that we got the Affordable Care Act passed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , so it was really a great thing to know that if something happened to them , they could get cared for . " <p> Meanwhile , John Legend reveals the 2008 election made him cry . <p> " I never cried before , from an election result . " <p> While Ellen DeGeneres recalled the funnier times she shared with Obama and The First Lady . <p> " The CVS experience with First Lady Michelle Obama was pretty cool and dancing with Barack at his birthday party .
