<h> Pope commends Islamist terror as ' Homicidal Madness ' <p> He wants leaders to improve social conditions that serve as fertile ground for the spread of fundamentalism and radicalisation . <p> In his annual address to diplomats , he also forcefully restated a call to ban nuclear weapons , saying experiments by North Korea to build long-range missiles , risked setting off a new nuclear arms race . <p> He spoke to diplomats from more than 180 countries . In his , " state of the world " address , the Pope spoke of the need to defend European unification and for greater unity in facing climate change . <p> He reserved his toughest words of condemnation for the wave of " fundamentalist-inspired terrorism " in 2016 , listing attacks by Islamists in Europe , Africa , Asia and the United States .
