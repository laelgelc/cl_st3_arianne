<h> Cardinal Quevedo calls for aid to Mindanao flood-affected communities <p> MANILA - Cotabato Archbishop Orlando Cardinal Quevedo on Tuesday urged parishes all over the Philippines to collaborate with each other for sending relief aid and donations to communities affected by flash floods that hit Mindanao a day earlier . <p> During a press conference at the University of Santo Tomas , where the second day of the fourth World Apostolic Congress on Mercy ( WACOM4 ) was being held Tuesday , Quevedo asked for donations in the form of clothes , blankets , medicine , and materials for shelter , among others . <p> Meanwhile , Fr . Nick Lalog reported that at least two people were confirmed dead by the Archdiocese of Cagayan de Oro . <p> He cited Fr . Satur Lumba , the director of social action for the Archdiocese of Cagayan de Oro , as reporting that one of the fatalities was from Cagayan de Oro City , the other from Agay-ayan in Gingoog City . <p> By Tuesday morning , the flood had started to recede in some parts of the archdiocese , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had sought temporary shelter at the vacant lot of the church , Lalog said . <p> Quevedo , speaking before some 5,000 delegates , said the degradation of the environment , which lead to such calamities as flash floods , marked the lack of " communion in love " all over the world . <p> Typhoons had become stronger and more frequent , with places that did not experience them in the past now being affected increasingly , such as southern Mindanao . Tornadoes , droughts , and severe winters were also hitting other parts of the globe . <p> " In love , God dwells in us , " Quevedo elaborated , calling Christians to love their neighbor , which included their enemies . <p> He urged the participants to examine themselves : " Do I love as I should ? Do I love God above all else ? Do I love my neighbor as myself ? " <p> Christians , he said , were different parts of one body , with Christ being the head of it . Each person had different gifts and played distinct roles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , we build back together the whole body . When one suffers , everyone in the body of Christ suffers , " Quevedo said . <p> He asked Filipinos to be more aware about the little things that they could do to combat global warming and climate change . They could use their cars less often , and turn off lights and appliances when these were not in use . <p> Church leaders in the Philippines , said Quevedo , had previously protested irresponsible mining and illegal logging , and the Catholic Bishops ' Conference of the Philippines was the first bishops ' conference in the world to issue a pastoral statement on the environment , in 1986.
