<h> Pangasinan to implement irrigation projects <p> LINGAYEN , Pangasinan January 27 ( PIA ) -- To intensify the agricultural productivity of farmers and to bring basic services at the grassroots level , the provincial government here will soon implement the rehabilitation and construction of irrigation systems and small farm reservoir in Pangasinan . <p> On Monday , the Sangguniang Panlalawigan passed a resolution authorizing Governor Amado Espino III to sign a memorandum of agreement ( MOA ) with project beneficiaries for the implementation of rehabilitation and construction of communal irrigation system , pump irrigation system , installation of shallow tubewell irrigation project , small water impounding project , and small farm reservoir in the province . <p> Board Member Noel Bince , author of the resolution , said the provincial government is implementing the construction and repair of abovementioned irrigation systems in order to generate additional irrigated areas and serve as mitigating measures to the effects of climate change . <p> " The provincial government 's major concern under its development thrust is to increase agricultural productivity and to bring basic services at the grassroots level to effect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " said Bince . <p> Bince disclosed that the MOA will serve as a vehicle for collaboration efforts between the provincial government and irrigators association , farmers association and fisherfolks project beneficiaries purposely to promote and develop the sustainability of the irrigation project of the province . <p> Under the agreement , the provincial government is tasked to administer , manage and disburse fund in accordance with existing government auditing rules and regulations ; conduct periodic monitoring and evaluation to ensure the progress and accomplishment of the project in accordance with the specification , the proper utilization of the fund and compliance with provision of the agreement ; supply the materials needed and provide labor requirements relative to the construction and rehabilitation of the project ; and assign resident engineer to supervise and monitor the progress of the project . <p> It is also the provincial government 's responsibility to assign institutional development officers to cater the association 's need on organizational development and implement rice , corn high value crops production program and provide extension support and agricultural technology to increase agricultural productivity and food security on its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the operation and maintenance of the irrigation facilities after the construction and repair is completed ; create project management staff to oversee the operation and maintenance of system ; submit periodic report to the Provincial Agriculture Office on the operation and maintenance of the system ; and support rice , corn , high value crops production program and other agricultural technology and extension support provided by the agriculture office .
