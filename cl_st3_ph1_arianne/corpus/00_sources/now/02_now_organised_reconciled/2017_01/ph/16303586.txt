<h> ADB operations reach record high of $31.5 billion in 2016 <h> ADB operations reach record high of $31.5 billion in 2016 <p> ASIAN Development Bank ( ADB ) operations for Asia and the Pacific rose by 17 percent year-on-year to an all-time high of $31.5 billion in 2016 , the Manila-based multilateral lender said Monday . <p> ADB 's operations in 2015 amounted to $26.9 billion . <p> In the preliminary figures it released , ADB said its approvals of loans , grants , technical assistance and cofinancing have been growing steadily over the years as development needs in the region continue to rise . <p> " The increase in our development financing to Asia and the Pacific is reflective of our strong commitment to reducing poverty and improving people 's lives in the region , " said ADB President Takehiko Nakao . " As ADB celebrates 50 years of development partnership with its member countries , we will strive to remain the region 's premiere development bank by providing financing , knowledge , and partnership . " <p> " Asia is growing at a steady pace , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ both sustainable and inclusive , " Nakao added . " ADB will continue to improve by becoming a stronger , better , and faster bank to help the region achieve its development objectives . " <p> He acknowledged that the region faces many challenges such as implementing the Sustainable Development Goals , climate change , increasing inequality , rapid urbanization , aging , and disaster risk management . <p> Approvals of loans and grants for sovereign ( government ) and nonsovereign ( primarily the private sector ) operations by ADB itself reached a record $17.5 billion , a 9 percent increase from $16.0 billion in 2015 . <p> Non-concessional loans from ADB 's Ordinary Capital Resources ( OCR ) amounted to $14.4 billion . Concessional loans and grants from the Asian Development Fund ( ADF ) reached $3.1 billion , with $2.6 billion going to loans and $518 million to grants . Technical assistance , meanwhile , increased by around 20% to $170 million from 2015 's $141 million figure . <p> ADB said its strong showing of operations was based on the larger financing capacity generated by the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ADF and OCR -- which formally took effect on January 1 , 2017 .
