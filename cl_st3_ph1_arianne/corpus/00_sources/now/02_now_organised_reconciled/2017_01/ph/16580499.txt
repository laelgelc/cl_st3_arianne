<h> ASCC seeks to increase ordinary citizens ' awareness of ASEAN <p> QUEZON CITY , Jan. 25 - One of the goals of the ASEAN Socio-Cultural Community Pillar is to mainstream the Association of Southeast Asian Nations and to make it an institution that ordinary citizens are familiar with . <p> This was underscored by DSWD Secretary Judy M. Taguiwalo , chairperson of the ASCC as she led the launching of the Philippines ' Chairmanship of the 2017 ASEAN Socio-Cultural Community Pillar at the Marquee Mall at Angeles City , Pampanga . <p> " We want to mainstream the Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) and make it an institution that ordinary citizens are familiar with . The agreements , projects and programs of the ASEAN should be explained to citizens of its Member-States so they can make use of them as they go about their daily lives as members of their respective societies .
