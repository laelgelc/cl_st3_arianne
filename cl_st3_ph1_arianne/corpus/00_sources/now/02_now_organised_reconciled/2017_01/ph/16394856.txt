<h> Bill Gates ' New Energy Fund <p> Bill Gates , the billionaire co-founder of Microsoft , announced the week before Christmas that he is going to set up a billion dollar investment fund to tackle climate change . Observers see this as an energetic response to U.S. President-elect Donald Trump 's well-known skepticism that climate change exists . The Gates fund will invest in transforming energy research and development on reducing the carbon emissions into atmosphere that cause climate change .
