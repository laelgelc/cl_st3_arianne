---- Ring out the false , ring in the true <p> Antonio Gramsci once wrote why he hated celebrating the New Year . He said : " I want every morning to be a new year for me . Every day I want to reckon with myself , and every day I want to renew myself ... no day of celebration with its mandatory collective rhythms , to share with all the strangers I do n't care about . Because our grandfathers ' grandfathers , and so on , celebrated , we too should feel the urge to celebrate . That is nauseating . " <p> Gramsci then praised socialism , explained his desire for it , " Because it will hurl into the trash all of these dates which have no resonance in our spirit and , if it creates others , they will at least be our own , and not the ones we have to accept without reservations from our silly ancestors .
