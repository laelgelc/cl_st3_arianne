<h> Opinion <h> The lady from Batanes <h> Yellow Pad Filomeno S. Sta . Ana III <p> Posted on January 31 , 2017 <p> In Congress , she is addressed as the lady from Batanes . But she is not Ivatan . She is Kapampangan , hailing from the town of Lubao , Pampanga , the hometown of two former presidents , namely Diosdado Macapagal who won fair and square in the 1961 elections and daughter Gloria whose 2004 victory was tainted by the " Hello Garci " cheating . <p> The lady from Batanes has won many congressional elections , sometimes with a narrow margin , but always clean and convincing victories . In a House that has been called the house of thieves , being addressed the honorable lady from Batanes befits her . <p> By chance , I saw Dina recently , accompanied by her husband Butch . To those who ask about the husband and former Budget Secretary , he is busy nowadays doing carpentry , fixing furniture at home and at Fundacion Pacita , dreaming of walking the Camino de Santiago , and of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ suspicion that Butch is part of a Liberal Party conspiracy to remove Rodrigo Duterte from the Presidency . <p> Dina looked tired , yet she emitted a glow . It was a glow that we all wish to see and feel especially during these times of anxiety . <p> Dina has been in and out of the hospital lately . Probably , the depressing situation in Congress has aggravated her condition . A humanist like her gets affected by such bills that restore the death penalty and lower the age of criminal liability from fifteen years old to nine years old . <p> Dina , too , has a personal reason to oppose a weak tobacco tax bill . The recently passed House bill on tobacco taxation allows two tax rates for cigarettes ( thus undermining the current law of having one rate for all cigarettes regardless of price ) and has a minimal tax rate increase ( small if compared to what the health advocates and the Department of Finance would like to have ) . Dina was the original author of the successful tobacco tax reform that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the sin tax law . <p> The story of how that law was passed must be told sooner than later . The struggle was most difficult and challenging in the face of the lobby and money of vested interests . The collective action to pass the sin tax reform was made up by a cast of thousands . A whole sweep of civil society , particularly the organizations of the doctors , nurses , and the youth , mobilized for this effort . <p> In Congress , two stars shone brightly , namely Dina and Sid Ungab . Sid then was the chair of the House 's Ways and Means Committee . He was the master of the art of war , and he outfoxed the most veteran of politicians who were captured by vested interests . <p> Dina was the political and moral compass . She initiated the filing of a tax bill at a time that then President Noynoy Aquino expressed reluctance on new taxes . Even though outnumbered by politicians who took advantage of Aquino 's inaction on tax legislation , she with a few @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bad bill . Dina was patient , and eventually her President recognized the importance of reforming the taxes on tobacco and alcohol . <p> Dina combined her intensity and passion with motherly affection and warmth to win over many politicians . Ultimately , the sin tax bill became a law of historic significance . <p> Today , the Department of Finance is proposing a comprehensive tax reform package , which will have greater significance than the dramatic 2012 sin tax reform . It is a good proposal and deserves multi-partisan support . It upholds fairness , equity , efficiency and progressivity . Although the legislators easily support some parts like the lowering of the personal income tax rate and higher income tax exemption , the populists and vested interests oppose the other features . <p> The essential components of the tax reform package are being undermined . One example already mentioned is the passage by the Lower House of an undesirable tobacco tax bill . In addition , many congressmen -- including some who were Dina 's party-mates in the previous Congress -- want to dilute the excise tax @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) . <p> Increasing the excise tax on gasoline is necessary because the current tax rate has been severely eroded by inflation for having not been adjusted since 1997 . Besides , the main consumers of gasoline are the richest 10% of Filipinos , and their consumption contributes to pollution and climate change . Narrowing the list of goods exempted from VAT is fair because many of the exempted items benefit the rich more than the poor . To illustrate , the rich senior citizens can avail themselves of the VAT exemption from eating in fancy restaurants , while the poor elderly can not even afford to have a Jollibee meal . <p> Dina supports what is fair , equitable , efficient , and progressive . And she is against bad compromises . <p> Dina 's circumstances , however , have changed , affecting how she will be involved in the big battles . In the first place , she does not belong to the House 's inner circle ; she is outside the kulambo ( mosquito net ) as the Filipino saying goes . Further , her health @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ intensity , her will and passion for real change remain . <p> Dina has been fighting all her life -- fighting the fascist dictatorship , fighting corruption and vested interests , fighting for democracy , good governance and human rights . <p> Dina knows how to choose her battles . <p> I recall the time she chaired the House 's Committee on Energy and co-chaired the Joint Congressional Power Commission . She had a good grasp of the issues that had to be tackled . At the same time , she knew that her counterpart in the Senate was not receptive to , and would have even obstructed , the key proposals on energy or power , which she could pass in the House of Representatives . She thus took the prudent approach of avoiding confrontation with a more powerful Senator . At any rate , the first-order issues pertaining to energy could have been resolved even without legislative intervention . <p> Dina will be again choosing her battles . And for her biggest battle in life , she will shout the cheer that she first heard from Butch : One Big Fight !
