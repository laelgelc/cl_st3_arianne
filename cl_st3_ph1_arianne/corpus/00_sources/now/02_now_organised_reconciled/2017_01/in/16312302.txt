<h> Heritage French consulate building in Puducherry leads in going green <p> Puducherry : The iconic heritage building that is over two-and-a-half centuries old and houses the French consulate general at Puducherry , is leading the way in tapping solar energy . The building that received five-star rating ( highest level of certification for being energy efficient ) from Bureau of Energy Efficiency ( BEE ) , following an energy audit in 2015 , has installed a rooftop on-grid solar system that produces 5,700 units per month on an average . <p> " The solar plant has produced more than 33,000 units of power since mid-July last year , saving more than Rs 2.3 lakh , " said French consul general Phillipe Janvier Kamiyama in an official communication . <p> Successive French consul generals ' decade-long efforts efforts in improving the technical aspects and changing the habits of the staff members have finally started to yield results . The consulate has replaced 49 old air-conditioners with new ones that use inverter technology . This will substantially lower the power consumption .
