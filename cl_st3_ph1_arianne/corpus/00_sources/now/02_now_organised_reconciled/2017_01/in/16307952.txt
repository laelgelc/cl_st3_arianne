It was launched in June 2016 and was implemented in Devanakonda Mandal of Kurnool district with only groundnut crop . <p> " Following the launch @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sowing Application for farmers combined with a Personalized Village Advisory Dashboard for the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh , the results show a 30% higher average in yield per hectare , " said a joint media release by ICRISAT and Microsoft on Monday . <p> ICRISAT adopted Microsoft 's Cortana Intelligence Suite including Machine Learning ( ability of computers to learn without being specifically programmed ) and Power BI ( Business Intelligence ) , to disseminate information to the farmers and government officials involved with APPSM to promote digital farming practices in the state . <p> Using a Sowing Application combined with a Personalized Village Advisory Dashboard for AP , the dashboard provided important insights around soil health , fertilizer recommendations , and seven-day weather forecasts derived from the world 's best available weather observations systems and global forecast models .
