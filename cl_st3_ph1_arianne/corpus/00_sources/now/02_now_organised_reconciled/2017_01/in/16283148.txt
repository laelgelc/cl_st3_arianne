---- CPREEC conducts sustainability education prog. in SA School <p> Port Blair <p> 7 Jan 2017 <p> The CPR Environmental Education Centre ( CPREEC ) . a Centre of Excellence in Environmental Education of the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) , Government of India , in collaboration with WIPRO -- Earthian and the Science Wing , Directorate of Education , has taken up a Sustainability Education Programme in South Andaman schools . The programme involves innovative project submissions on sustainability of water and biodiversity by groups of students guided by a teacher . <p> CPREEC with its outreach in South Andaman oriented 100 teachers from 50 schools through 2 workshops . 54 project submissions were received from the participating schools . Among the 54 projects , 12 projects were selected by a competent committee for state level and recommended to national level . The 12 winning projects teams from schools are Vitala Public School Hope Town , GSSS . Haddo ( H&amp;T ) , Kamaraj English Medium School Brookshabad , Navy Children School Minnie Bay , Carmel Senior Secondary School and Ummat Public School , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ students and guide teachers . <p> C.P.R.Environmental Education Centre , Chennai , in association with the WIPRO Earthian has also awarded the best project submissions at the regional level and confers Regional Winners-2016 in Andaman &amp; Nicobar Islands through a Regional Recognition Event . All awarded teachers and students were given a Regional Winner Certificate and the memento for the school .
