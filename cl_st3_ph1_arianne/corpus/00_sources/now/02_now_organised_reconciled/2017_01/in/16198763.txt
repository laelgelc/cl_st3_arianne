---- CM to stage dharna on Jan 5 against ' non-federal nature ' of Centre <p> Seemingly dejected with National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) order , rejecting the state government-envisaged Bhagirathi Zone Master Plan project , purportedly meant to pump development in the riparian areas , Chief Minister Harish Rawat would stage a dharna in Delhi on January 5 . The venue of the sit-in is , however , yet to decided , though the probable site is either Jantar Mantar or the headquarters of the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change . This aside , the CM would raise the issues of the ban imposed by the Centre on the supply of wheat for the APL category people and the proposed sell-off of IDPL by the Centre .
