<h> Maharashtra was once a dinosaur habitat : Expert <p> " We have discovered evidence of existence of dinosaurs in Chandrapur district of Maharashtra , " said Vijay Mugal , director of paleontology division , central region , Geological Survey of India , Nagpur , on Friday . <p> TNN Updated : Jan 21 , 2017 , 09.58 AM IST <p> ( Representative image ) <p> NAGPUR : " We have discovered evidence of existence of dinosaurs in Chandrapur district of Maharashtra , " said Vijay Mugal , director of paleontology division , central region , Geological Survey of India , Nagpur , on Friday . Mugal , who has an extensive research experience in Central India , was speaking at ' Popular Science Lecture ' organized by Raman Science Centre on its premises . Skeletons , eggs and nests of dinosaurs were discovered at Raholi in Gujarat and Dhar-Jhabua in Madhya Pradesh , he said .
