<h> Fishermen seek practical solutions from state government <p> Traditional fishermen community has asked the state government to address their issues while amending the Kerala Marine Fisheries Regulation Act ( KMFRA ) . <p> TNN Updated : Jan 11 , 2017 , 09.16 AM IST <p> Representative image . <p> KOCHI : Traditional fishermen community has asked the state government to address their issues while amending the Kerala Marine Fisheries Regulation Act ( KMFRA ) . Speaking at a meeting of fisheries department officials , scientists , researchers and a large number of traditional fishermen to discuss the proposed amendments of the KMFRA , they sought for practical and meaningful changes that could help their livelihood . The meeting was held at the Central marine fisheries research institute ( CMFRI ) in Kochi on Tuesday . <p> Speaking at the meeting , PV Janardhanan , state president of the traditional fishermen association expressed concerns over the move to stretch the annual trawling ban period to two phases . " CMFRI had prepared MLS in order to curb practice of juvenile fishing . Karnataka state government has also approached the CMFRI to prepare the MLS for the state , " he said . " We will examine the demands raised by the fishermen community in connection with the amendment of KMFRA , " he added . <p> Ernakulam MLA Hibi Eden said the authorities should consult with the fishermen community before amending the Act . <p> " The fisheries sector in the state is moving towards a chaotic situation due to many reasons .
