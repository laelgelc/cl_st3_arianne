<h> Unicef launches appeal of $3.3 bn to children affected by conflict , disasters <p> United Nations , Feb 1 ( IANS ) The UN Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) launched an appeal of $3.3 billion to provide emergency assistance in 48 countries around the globe , against the backdrop of children driven from their homes due to conflict , disasters and climate change . <p> A total of 48 million children living through some of the world 's worst conflicts and other humanitarian emergencies will benefit from Unicef 's 2017 appeal , which was launched on January 31 , Xinhua news agency cited a UN press release . <p> " From Syria to Yemen and Iraq , from South Sudan to Nigeria , children are under direct attack , their homes , schools and communities in ruins , their hopes and futures hanging in the balance , " the press release noted . <p> " In total , almost one in four of the world 's children lives in a country affected by conflict or disaster , " it added . <p> The UN agency fears that an estimated 7.5 million children will face severe acute malnutrition across the majority of appeal countries . <p> Unicef 's Humanitarian Action for Children sets out the agency 's 2017 appeal and its goals to provide children with access to safe water , nutrition , education , health and protection in some of the world 's worst conflicts and humanitarian emergencies . <p> The largest single component of the appeal , or $1.4 billion , is for children and families caught up in the conflict in Syria , which will soon enter its seventh year . This also includes Syrian refugees in neighbouring countries . <p> With enough funding , Unicef hopes to reach 81 million people , including 48 million children with access to safe water , nutrition , education , health and protection . <p> Unicef is particularly concerned about another slow-burning threat -- malnutrition . <p> " Malnutrition is a silent threat to millions of children , " Fontaine said .
