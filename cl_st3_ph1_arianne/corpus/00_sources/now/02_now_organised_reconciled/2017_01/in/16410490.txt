As a result , an increased number of standard buses would be made available for busy routes at all times , including peak hours , PMPML officials told TOI . <p> " Over 200 routes have been identified and we plan to start the scheme soon . Preparations regarding the same have been initiated . " <p> According to Waghmare , there are many routes in Pune where passenger inflow remains heavy halfway through but by the end of the journey , buses are literally empty . For instance , on the Vishrantwadi route there are very few passengers en route to Alandi . Similarly , areas located beyond Katraj , Bhosari , Hadapsar , Kothrud , Malwani , etc , see buses travelling with very few passengers . <p> " We will ply mini buses on these routes instead of standard buses .
