Under pressure from politicians and locals , forest department put up three cages in the area in which a male leopard was trapped on December 30 . <p> Even as cages were in place , another six-month-old leopard cub got trapped , indicating there was mother leopard in the same area with another cub . Hence , it could not be determined which leopard had actually killed the girl .
