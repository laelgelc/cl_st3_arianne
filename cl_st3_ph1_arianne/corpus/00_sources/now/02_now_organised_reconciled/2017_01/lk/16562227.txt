<h> President receives award for commitment to mitigate climate change <p> A workshop on ' Climate Change : Opportunities and Challenges for Business , ' organized by the American Chamber of Commerce in Sri Lanka ( AmCham ) held today at the Ballroom of Galle Face Hotel , under the patronage of President Maithripala Sirisena . <p> The President was presented an award for his commitment towards mitigating climate change . The award was presented to President Sirisena by RavinBasnayake , President of the AmCham on behalf of its Board of Directors . <p> The keynote address of the event was delivered by Professor Sir Robert Tony Watson , the Chairman of the Intergovernmental Platform for Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services . <p> Minister Faizer Mustafa and Prof. Mohan Munasinghe were among those participated in this event .
