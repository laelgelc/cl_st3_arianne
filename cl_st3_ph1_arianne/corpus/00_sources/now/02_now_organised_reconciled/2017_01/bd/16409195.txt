<h> Climate change influencing migration flows in Bangladesh : IOM <h> Climate change and natural hazards are influencing migration flows in South Asian countries , the International Organization for Migration ( IOM ) says after conducting a study in Bangladesh , Nepal and the Maldives . <p> It says natural hazards , aggravated by climate change like floods , storm surges , droughts , cyclones and heavy precipitation , have played " a crucial role " behind the increasing migration flows of South Asia over the past years . <p> It has brought to light the fact that climate change and environmental degradation will further contribute to the movement of people living in the region . <p> The study also indicates that the countries have experienced variations in temperature and rainfall in recent decades . Sudden and slow onset climatic events often affect the lives and livelihoods of vulnerable people across the three countries . <p> The countries have been selected for the research project as within change and environmental degradation severely affect South Asia , with its tangible impact on human mobility being apparent over the last few decades . <p> The Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre ( IDMC ) estimates that 7.9 million people were displaced in 2015 due to sudden onset disasters in the region , accounting for 36 percent of the estimated total global displacement . <p> In order to
