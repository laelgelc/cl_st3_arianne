<p> It is crucial that conferences such as the Gobeshona Conference are allowed to take place , and the issues discussed are brought to light . There is constant research and progress in the long-standing fight against climate change , and it is up to the Bangladeshi government to recognise these elements and embrace them . <p> In terms of policy , Bangladesh already lags behind , with a recent report showing that the air in Dhaka has dangerous levels of carcinogenic pollutants . Our water bodies continue to be a disgraceful sight , having become a common deposit for waste . To say nothing of the way we have treated the Sundarbans . <p> The government has taken certain initiatives when it comes to fighting climate change , but these small steps are not enough . We need to take long strides in this matter , and ensure that Bangladesh does not fall victim to its own economic progress . <p> All sectors of the government should set aside petty squabbles and come together to tackle climate change . We are all in this together .
