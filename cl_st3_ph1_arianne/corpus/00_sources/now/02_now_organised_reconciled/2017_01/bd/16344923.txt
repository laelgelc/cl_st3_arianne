these issues , at Independent University ( IUB ) yesterday . <p> " Only good research can fill the gap between the realities on the ground and the policy making process , " said Saber Hossain Chowdhury , president of Inter-parliamentary Union . <p> " The importance of quality research is crucial for Bangladesh since we have been facing the negative impacts of global warming and as it moves towards becoming a developed country it has to keep climate change in mind with taking on development initiatives , " he added when addressing the program as a special guest . <p> According to the 5th
