<h> Climate change will cost Bangladesh $121 billion <h> Climate experts and researchers warn that it may cost the country up to USD 121 billion or 5% of the national GDP from 2005-2050 as it is not capable of dealing with the adverse effects of climate change through mitigation and adaptation , at Gobeshona conference yesterday . <p> Climate change is a big threat to Bangladesh 's sustainable development goals as well , undermining possible poverty eradication and food security as they country is yet to develop an effective mechanism of addressing disaster related Loss and Damage . <p> Dr Saleemul Huq , the director of International Centre for Climate Change &amp; Development ( ICCCAD ) said : " Although people are not aware of the relationship between their loss and climate change , they are indeed interconnected with each other while we need more research regarding our mechanisms for loss and damage .
