However , with rising global temperatures , the incidents of climate change are evidenced more severely than ever . <p> According to the Fifth Assessment Report of the Inter-governmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , the frequency of heat waves has increased in large parts of Europe , Asia , and Australia . So , it is evident that climate change is happening and it affects everyone . <p> As Dr Saleemul Huq , director of the International Centre for Climate Change and Development , rightly explains : " It is true that climate change will hit the world 's marginalised communities first , the rest of the world will succumb to its impacts as well .
