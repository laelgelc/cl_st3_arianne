<h> Readymade clothing industry shining Bangladesh 's image : Shahriar <h> The state minister for foreign affairs has said the apparel industry is " inextricably linked with Bangladesh 's success story - not only in economic terms but also in projecting the country 's image " . <p> " Made in Bangladesh labels are now commonplace in American and European stores . McKinsey has called Bangladesh the " next China " and predicted that Bangladeshi garment exports , now about $28 billion a year , could be doubled by 2020 , " Md Shahriar Alam said on Tuesday . <p> He was speaking at the closing ceremony of the Institute of Business Administration Communication Club 's ( IBACC ) ' Brandwitz ' . <p> The state minister said young entrepreneurs with their " new ideas could take this sector forward through their innovation and creativity " . <p> " It is not just business , it is in a way branding of a country to the wider world . " <p> Shahriar added that Bangladesh 's branding " is not merely limited to economic fronts , our achievements in social sectors have also captured global attention " . <p> " Bangladesh has earned global applause and recognition in its role and contributions for women empowerment , poverty alleviation , climate change , water security , ICT and even in migration , " he said .
