<h> PM Hasina leaves Dhaka for Switzerland to attend World Economic Forum <h> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has started for Davos , Switzerland to take part in the 47th World Economic Forum . <p> An aircraft of Etihad Airways left Dhaka 's Shahjalal International Airport at 10:45pm on Sunday with the prime minister and her entourage on board . <p> They are scheduled to reach Zurich on a four-day visit via Abu Dhabi on Monday morning local time . <p> Foreign Minister AH Mahmood Ali and State Minister for ICT Zunaid Ahmed Palak , among others , are in her tour party . <p> Ali at a press conference on Saturday said Hasina will be the first " elected " Bangladesh leader to attend the prestigious forum in Davos . <p> She will speak at different sessions on water economy , sustainable development , regional cooperation , climate change , ICT and women 's leadership and empowerment in Davos-Klosters , he said . <p> He also said the prime minister will highlight Bangladesh 's achievements and try to woo investors . <p> The World Economic Forum annual meeting in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leaders in collaborative activities to shape the global , regional and industrial agenda at the beginning of each year . <p> Leaders share their insights and innovations on how to best navigate the future in this Forum . Newly elected UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres will also attend the meeting . <p> Executive Chairman Prof Klaus Schwab invited the prime minister . <p> Ali said the Bangladesh officials were also ready for bilateral meetings on the sidelines of the forum . <p> Heads of government and state of 45 nations will be among the 3,000 participants .
