<h> Al Gore to speak at ECOCITY World Summit 2017 <p> Published by Waldron Smith Management <p> Wednesday 1 February 2017 <h> Share <p> We are pleased to announce the Hon Al Gore ( former US Vice President and current Chair of The Climate Reality Project ) will be the Principal Speaker at the ECOCITY World Summit 2017 which will be held in Melbourne from 12-14 July 2017 . Mr Gore will deliver his famous slide presentation including his insights on the role of cities in meeting the global climate challenge . <p> The involvement of Mr. Gore will further strengthen the potential for this ECOCITY World Summit to play a crucial role in accelerating global climate change action by cities and subnational governments . <p> Further program updates are anticipated within the coming weeks . <p> Call for contributions are now open and early bird registration savings are on offer .
