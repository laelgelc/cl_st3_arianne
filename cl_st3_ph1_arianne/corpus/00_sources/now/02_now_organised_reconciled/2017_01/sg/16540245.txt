<p> Handout photo of NASA 's DC-8 flies over the Brunt Ice Shelf in AntarcticaReuters <p> With every passing year we witness some drastic effects of global warming but we still choose to turn a blind eye towards them . 2017 is nothing new . Probably the first wake-up call of the year is the fast growing crack in Antarctic ice shelf called Larsen C. <p> According to experts , drastic climate change has made the crack grow by more than six miles in the past few weeks and the cleft now measures more than 100 miles . It is also said that once the crack is completed , it will form one of the largest icebergs on record . <p> However , the problem does not end here . According to Project MIDAS , a British Antarctic research project that is closely studying the crack , the break " will fundamentally change the landscape of the Antarctic Peninsula , " reported USA Today . <p> Andrew Fleming , remote sensing manager at the British Antarctic Survey , when asked about what caused the drastic melting of Larsen C @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ thawed the ice shelf . <p> To understand the situation better , we have to know the difference between an ice shelf and an iceberg . Ice shelves are permanent floating bodies of ice that connect to a land mass whereas icebergs are large free-floating masses detached from ice shelves and carried out to sea . In the case of Larsen C , only a final 12 miles of ice is keeping the parent ice shelf to the future iceberg connected .
