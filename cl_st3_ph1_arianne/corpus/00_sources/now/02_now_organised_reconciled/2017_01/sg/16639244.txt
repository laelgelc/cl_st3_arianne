<h> Power battle between Pope and traditionalists escalates <p> The Knights of Malta announced that Grand Master Matthew Festing ( far left ) had resigned from his position at the request of Pope Francis . The two are pictured here during a private audience at the Vatican in June last year.PHOTO : EUROPEAN PRESSPHOTO AGENCY <p> ROME * It began as a fight over staffing . Then came a dispute about condoms , followed by papal concerns about Freemasons . <p> Now it has become a full-scale proxy war between Pope Francis and the Vatican traditionalists who oppose him , with the battleground being a Renaissance palace in Via dei Condotti , Rome 's most exclusive street . <p> The palace is the headquarters of the Knights of Malta , the mediaeval Roman Catholic order . For months , an ugly , if quiet , spat over staffing simmered behind the order 's walls before spilling across the Tiber River to the Vatican . <p> Pope Francis took the extraordinary steps of demanding the resignation of the order 's leader - a decision the Knights officially accepted on Saturday @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Until last week , the Knights order was led by its conservative Grand Master Matthew Festing of Britain . <p> Conservatives promptly denounced what they called an illegal annexation and ideological purging by a power-obsessed pontiff , while liberal observers saw the whole episode as resulting from an act of subterfuge by the Pope 's most public critic within the Vatican hierarchy , US Cardinal Raymond Burke . <p> " The Vatican is a thing built of tradition , " said Mr John Thavis , author of The Vatican Diaries and a veteran church analyst , " and once those traditional parts start feuding with each other , that is a dangerous sign . " <p> The Pope remains one of the world 's most popular figures , but the spat with the Knights is a small indicator of how the political tensions rippling across the globe are alive in the Vatican , too . <p> Only a year ago , his calls to fight climate change and help migrants seemed to place him in the lead of a progressive global vanguard , in keeping with his push @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is more politically isolated . <p> The election of US President Donald Trump and the rise of far-right populists in Europe have ushered in an angrier era - and emboldened those traditionalists inside the Vatican who sense that the once-impregnable Pope could be vulnerable . <p> Supporters of Cardinal Burke have complained that the Pope , for all his talk of fostering debate , was intolerant of opposing views , especially more orthodox ones . <p> " It sends a message to the rest of the Catholic world that if you try to stand for orthodoxy in the Church , you are going to be sent away , " said Mr Michael Hichborn , president of the Lepanto Institute , a conservative Catholic organisation in Virginia . " And the people pushing for heterodoxy will be put in power .
