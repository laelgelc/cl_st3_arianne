<h> Chinese President Xi Jinping says to build new relations with United States <p> GENEVA ( REUTERS ) -- China will build a new model of relations with the United States as part of its creation of a " circle of friends " around the world , President Xi Jinping said in a speech at the United Nations in Geneva on Wednesday ( Jan 18 ) . <p> His speech , the finale of a Swiss trip that included a state visit and topping the bill at the World Economic Forum in Davos , was intended to highlight a Chinese interest in playing a cooperative role in international affairs . <p> " We always put people 's rights and interests above everything else and we have worked hard to develop and uphold human rights , " he said . " China will never seek expansion , hegemony or sphere of influence . " <p> Human rights groups and Western governments have accused China of widespread human rights abuses .
