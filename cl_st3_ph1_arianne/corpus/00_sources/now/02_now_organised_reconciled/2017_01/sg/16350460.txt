<p> Climate change and demographic trends are the challenges facing ski tourism but delegates attending the first European Mountain Travel Summit held in Crans-Montana , Switzerland , remained undaunted about the industry 's potential . Plus there 's the new market of Asia to tap . Arnie Weissmann , Editor In Chief , Travel Weekly , reports from the ski slopes . <p> Shorter seasons , higher snow lines , rising operating costs and key demographic slowdowns would suggest that skiing is an industry in decline . But even as these topics were being discussed by ski executives from over 20 countries at the inaugural European Mountain Travel Summit here , the audience 's mood remained elevated , bolstered by speakers ' passion for winter activities and stirred by Alpine views of glowing mountaintops , seen through the window of the Crans-Montana conference hall . <p> Attendees ' optimism was sorely tested .
