<h> 2016 was warmest yet , UN confirms <p> GENEVA ( AFP ) - Last year was Earth 's warmest on record , according to data compiled by the UN , confirming US findings that heat records were broken for a third year in 2016 . <p> The study by the World Meteorological Organization found that global average temperatures were about 1.1 degree Celsius above the pre-industrial period , and about 0.07 degrees Celsius above the record set in 2015 . <p> " 2016 was an extreme year for the global climae and stands out as the hottest year on record , " said Petteri Taalas , the agency 's secretary general . <p> " Long-term indicators of human-caused climate change reached new heights in 2016 , " he said in a statement . <p> " We have also broken sea ice minimum records in the Arctic and Antarctic , " he said , adding that the Arctic was warming twice as fast as the global average . <p> He also said the analysis found that atmospheric concentrations of both carbon dioxide and methane reached record levels . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration , Nasa 's Goddard Institute for Space Studies , the UK 's Met Office Hadley Centre , and the University of East Anglia 's Climatic Research Unit . <h> Follow ST <h> The Straits Times <p> We have been experiencing some problems with subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
