<p> Rainfed rice yields decrease under climate change alone due to increases in temperature that shorten the growing season for rice . <p> In a bid to reduce global warming , pollution and transition to a clean energy economy , Ebonyi State government has move to enact a law on climate change . <p> The Special Assistant to the Governor on Climate Change , Dr. ( Mrs. ) Obinuju Aloh presented the draft bill to the State Executive Council for approval . <p> She explained that the main objective of the bill is to use legislation to reduce exposure or sensitivity to climate change hazards . <p> Aloh noted that the law will focus on but not limited to adaptation , building frameworks for promoting enabling green growth as well as building a climate resilient society through attainment of set targets . <p> She noted that the law would ensure that issues of climate change are taken into consideration in carrying out development and planning in virtually all sectors in the state . <p> " Since climate change is linked to many other Issues like agriculture , food production , health infrastructure and others , the law when passed will equally mainstream climate change into all aspects of development and planning in the state " , she explained . <p> She also noted that the law provides for funding disaster management and climate change adaptation . <p> " Ebonyi is the first State to have a draft Bill on Climate change and this shows that the Governor and the state government is serious and committed to climate change issues , " she added .
