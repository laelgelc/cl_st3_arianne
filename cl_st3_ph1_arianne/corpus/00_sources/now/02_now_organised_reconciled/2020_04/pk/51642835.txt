<h> Pakistani filmmaker wins big at New York Festival 2020 <h> Share <p> Filmmaker Shehzad Hameed Ahmad has outdone himself ever since his documentary Flight of the Falcons won big at the New York Festival in 2016 . <p> Ahmad is keeping up with his winner title as he just bagged three awards at this year 's New York Festival . After his recent win , the filmmaker has added six New York Festival awards to his name . <p> This year , he won the silver medal in the Human Rights category for ' White Shade of Terror . ' The movie revolves around white supremacist terrorism based on the Christchurch shootings last year . <p> ' Caught in the Crossfire ' , a film that sheds light upon the plight of Afghans suffering in the war between Allied Forces and the Taliban , won a Bronze medal in the Human Concerns category . <p> His third award was a finalist position for ' The Battle for Kabul ' - a film that explores the power and control of Taliban . <p> Shehzad is a talented filmmaker who has travelled to some of the most faraway and wild parts of the world , including the Amazon Rainforests in Brazil , the camps of stranded Pakistanis in Bangladesh and the white hate groups in Norway . <p> Recently , he shot underwater in the Great Barrier Reef in Australia for his recent documentary series ' The Politics of Climate Change . '
