<h> Balochistan Has Turned Into A Barren Land With No Water , Here 's How It Happened <h> You guys all know about Balochistan , right ? <h> We know it 's our largest province <p> Source : tribune.com.pk/INP <h> And we know it 's rich in culture <p> Source : dailytimes.pk <h> I mean , we may not focus a lot on how it 's holding up , but hey -- it 's there , right ? <p> Source : thenation.pk <p> Well , I 'm not sure if not focusing on Balochistan is pretty wise , tbh . Why ? Because climatically , it 's not doing too well . According to a recent report in Dawn.com , Balochistan is pretty much a barren desert now . <h> In fact , Dr. Ainuddin , the chairperson of the Disaster Management Department at the University of Balochistan , clearly went on to state that half of Balochistan is gripped by a drought and famine . <p> Source : Asim Khan <h> Both , the drought and the famine have a few causative factors . Here are a few of them : <p> There has n't been a lot of rainfall ( 176 mm annually . ) <p> Deep well pumping has led to decreased groundwater levels . <p> The Western areas of the province sometimes receive less than 50 mm of rainfall . <p> Water shortage has increased to 60 percent in the province . <p> The water level is decreasing in Quetta by three-and-a-half feet every year . <p> The decreasing water levels are due to deep-dug tube wells . <p> Source : Asim Khan <h> Moreover , Balochistan 's ancient water supply has also either dried up or been destroyed . <p> The water supply system was based on the karezaat . Out of the 1384 karezaat that were present , only 100 remain functional now . <p> Source : Asim Khan <h> The construction of dams , however , might help save the 8.57 billion cubic metres of water out of around 10.693 billion cubic metres that are being wasted every year . <p> Very few dams have been built by the government . This leads to unnecessary wastage of water we desperately need to be saving . <p> Source : Daily Balochistan Express Quetta/ Nadir Jailani <h> Tariq Zehri , the director general of the Environment Protection Authority in Balochistan , agrees with the fact that climate change has a role to play in this . <p> Moreover , Bashir Agha , the superintending engineer of the Agriculture Department fears that if the situation continues , a large-scale migration will be inevitable . <p> Source : **39;3418;TOOLONG <p> The warning signs are all there .
