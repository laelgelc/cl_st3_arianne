<h> UN secretary general called the coronavirus ' the most challenging crisis we have faced since the Second World War ' <p> United Nations Secretary General Antonio Guterres said on Tuesday that the novel coronavirus was " the most challenging crisis we have faced since the Second World War . " <p> Guterres said the world needed to coordinate on a two-pronged approach that both stops the spread of the virus and addresses the dire economic fallout from the pandemic . <p> Economic relief must prioritize the world 's vulnerable populations , including women , low-wage workers , and people living in conflict zones , he said , adding : The response must focus on " building more equal , inclusive and sustainable economies and societies . " <p> The UN released a 26-page report calling for coordinated health and economic measures to stop the spread and fallout of the coronavirus . <p> United Nation Secretary General Antonio Guterres warned that the novel coronavirus was " the most challenging crisis we have faced since the Second World War " and that aggressive medical and economic efforts were needed to thwart it . <p> " It is a combination , on one hand , of a disease that represents a threat to everybody in the world , " Guterres said at a press briefing Tuesday night . " And , second , because it has an economic impact that will bring a recession that probably has no parallel in the recent past . " <p> As of Wednesday morning , there were more than 880,000 confirmed cases of COVID-19 worldwide and 44,000 deaths caused by the illness , according to Johns Hopkins University 's database . <p> On March 11 , the World Health Organization had declared the coronavirus a pandemic . At the time , WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus warned " this is not just a public health crisis -- it is a crisis that will touch every sector . " <p> In his opening remarks Tuesday , Guterres said the world 's response must be twofold . First , the world needed coordinated health response that included " testing , tracing , quarantine and treatment " and " measures to restrict movement and contact . " <p> He also called for " universal access to treatment and vaccines " when they were ready . <p> But Guterres also warned of a related economic disaster , noting that the International Monetary Fund anticipated a recession worse than the 2008 financial crisis . <p> An accompanying 26-page report released by the UN called on its member nations to conduct aggressive members to restrict transmission and a " large-scale , coordinated and comprehensive multilateral response amounting to at least 10 percent of global GDP . " <p> Guterres said that any economic relief must also address the world 's most vulnerable populations , including women , low-wage workers , and people in conflict zones . <p> " Everything we do during and after this crisis must be with a strong focus on building more equal , inclusive and sustainable economies and societies that are more resilient in the face of pandemics , climate change , and the many other global challenges we face , " he said .
