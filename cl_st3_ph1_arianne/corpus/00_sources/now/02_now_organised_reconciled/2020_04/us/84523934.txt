Scott Applewhite/AP ) <p> Government has a role to play <p> Despite some politicians ' recent calls for less government , it 's instructive to note that federal government-funded innovation has led to the Internet , GPS , smartphones , MRIs , Barcodes , and more . <p> And now , at this perilous time , we need government to come through for us more than ever to help overcome the public health crisis and heal the economy . Fortunately , our governor and local officials anticipated the severity of the pandemic and made decisive moves to slow the disease . <p> But at the federal level , we have witnessed a pervasive disdain for government and the resultant hollowing out of experienced officials . This has rendered the national response to the coronavirus dangerously less effective than our local response . <p> Collapsing a key National Security Council department , slow response to the first appearance of the virus , lack of centralized logistics management of medical equipment and supplies , and unclear and contradictory communications still characterize the federal response a full two months since the first cases appeared in the US . <p> Simplistic , short-sighted political ideology has compromised our government 's ability to perform its most important role : keeping Americans safe . <h> Posted ! <p> A link has been posted to your Facebook feed . <p> Sisters Amber Goss , 7 , second grade and Makayla Gomez , 6 , kindegarden at Andrew Jackson Elementary in Indio wave to their school staff on April 3 , 2020 during a teacher " parade " to show support from a distance to their students .
