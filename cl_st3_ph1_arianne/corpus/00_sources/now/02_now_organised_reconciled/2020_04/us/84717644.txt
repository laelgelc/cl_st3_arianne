<h> Readers Write : Earth Day can be a new beginning <p> On April 22 , 1970 Earth Day was born , a tangible statement of the growing support for protection of our environment . Today it is celebrated every year in 193 countries around the world . <p> Fifty years ago today , the golden anniversary of that first celebration , I often wonder why people fight so hard to ignore the lessons it has to teach . Patrick Moynihan the senator from New York once said : " Everyone is entitled to his own opinion , but not his own facts . " <p> Many in our country have the ability to " adjust " to the results of climate change . They fly away from an approaching storm , have insurance to fix its damage , but clearly you ca n't distance yourself forever . <p> No amount of denial or money will insulate you from the forces of nature as evidenced by the Pandemic caused by Covid-19 <p> I created this image of a virus 1,000 times smaller than a spec of sand engulfing our world . The smallest bit of nature overrunning our whole planet . <p> It is hard to deny how dependent we are on one another , on scientists and on the world community working together .
