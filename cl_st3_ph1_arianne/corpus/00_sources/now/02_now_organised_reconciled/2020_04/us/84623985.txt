<h> News <h> Surrey County Council plans to build solar farm to power its own buildings <p> Surrey County Council is going to start generating its own electricity with a solar farm that will power council buildings . <p> This is just one of the projects listed on its programme of green schemes to meet 2030 and 2050 carbon reduction targets . <p> The panels will be installed on a landfill site somewhere in the county and will generate five megawatts 5MW to feed into the local electricity grid . <p> On average , 5MW can power over 1,500 homes and save around 2,150 tonnes in carbon dioxide CO2 . However the council will use it to power its own buildings and make its operations greener . <p> It is hoped this amount will then offset the electricity used by the council 's corporate estate , according to council papers . <p> The scheme is one of a number listed in the Greener Futures Investment Programme . <p> Surrey has committed to spending nearly ? 300 million over five years on projects to help it adapt to climate change . <p> SCC has set targets to be zero carbon in the county by 2050 and to be zero carbon across the organisation by 2030 . <p> A study by Leeds University commissioned by the council found that it currently emits around 6.4m tonnes of carbon a year from its buildings , transport , commercial and industrial operations . <p> This has actually dropped by 35 per cent since 2005 but the report noted if the council carries on with business as usual it will only achieve a carbon reduction of 44 per cent and not meet the zero emissions targets . <p> Only nine per cent of the council 's corporate electricity use is from renewable energy sources . <p> Other schemes will look at changes to transport networks , waste management and environmental protection . <p> Other schemes in the Greener Futures Investment Programme : <p> Automatic number plate recognition cameras at Community Recycling Centres -- to prevent unauthorised waste Converting 89,000 street lights to LED bulbs Install 80 on-street electric vehicle charging points Bus lane improvements Improve footpaths and bridleways Flood alleviation schemes Construction of two new CRCs and a new waste transfer station Electric or ultra low emission vehicles into community transport SEND and public transport Initiatives aimed at encouraging residents to reduce , reuse and recycle their waste Match funding for grant funding scheme for SME businesses for investment in energy efficient technologies .
