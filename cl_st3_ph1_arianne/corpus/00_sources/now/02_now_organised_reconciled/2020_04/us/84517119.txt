<h> Beat switch for E&amp;E reporter Hiar <p> Corbin Hiar , an energy and environment reporter for E&amp;E News , will be joining the company 's Climatewire publication where he will be covering how businesses are and are not responding to climate change . <p> Hiar won the National Press Club analytical reporting award as " sole author of a series on the dangers that lax regulation of oil and gas production in national wildlife refuges poses to animals and surrounding communities . " <p> Before joining E&amp;E News , Hiar was with SNL Financial working as a power reporter . He has also worked as a researcher at Harvard University 's Safra Center for Ethics . Before that , he was a reporter with The Center for Public Integrity . <p> Hiar has also served as a news editor at The Huffington Post and as an associate editor at PBS MediaShift . He was also an editorial contributor at The Economist Group . <p> Additionally , Hiar has worked at UN Dispatch as an environmental reporter and blogger . He also served as a researcher/blogger and as a web intern at Mother Jones and The Nation , respectively . He also has experience working as a cardiology fellowship coordinator at New York Methodist Hospital . <p> Hiar is a B.A. in international relations and environmental studies from Bowdoin College .
