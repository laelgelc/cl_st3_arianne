For the Los Angeles Times , Matthew Fleischer writes very specifically to address the cars polluting effect : <p> So how did we get here ? Coronavirus lockdowns , sure . While we obsess about what it may do to our bodies and communities , as far as oil is concerned , the virus is an economic abstraction . Even when we do notice the material aspects of this process , these are usually downstream : the clearing of air pollution over Wuhan or the reduction of greenhouse gas emission resulting from economic slowdown .
