( Photo : MIKE LAWRENCE / THE GLEANER ) <p> Many years ago I commanded an Army basic training unit at Ft . Knox , Kentucky . My First Sergeant , older and wiser than I , once remarked , " I do n't care who I learn from . " <p> His real name - no joke - was Allen B. Smart . <p> As the COVID-19 virus spreads across the globe it is clear that we Americans are not smarter than Asians , Africans or Europeans . <p> Is this a " teaching moment " ? <p> It is ironic that , as our individual liberties are being restricted in the form of stay-at-home orders , we are also being guaranteed that anyone having symptoms linked to this virus will receive testing , care and treatment . <p> Is this a " teaching moment " ? <p> The U.S. Debt Clock at usdebtclock.org informs that the 2019 federal corporate and individual tax cuts have added more than $1 trillion to our current federal debt of $23.6 trillion . And now the COVID-19 Stimulus package - justified by need , with more to follow - will add another $2 trillion . <p> It is gratifying that our national expression of moral decency and compassion is " trumping " concerns like ... Can you pay ? ... Do you have health insurance ? ... Are you a citizen ? <p> Is this a " teaching moment " ? <p> Our federal government has used taxpayer money to support wars in Afghanistan and Iraq , to pay for the 2008 Great Recession bailout of banks , insurance companies and automakers . Our defense budget is larger than the combined total of the next 10 largest defense budgets in the world , including China and Russia . The wealthiest nation in the world seems to be able to pay for anything we choose to support . <p> Is this a " teaching moment " ? <p> Our time demands that we , as a nation , make no small plans . We have it within our power to awaken our democracy from a timid , caretaker sleep . To awaken the greatness of the decent , caring , gentle , giant within us . President Lincoln , at Gettysburg , spoke of a " new nation , conceived in Liberty , and dedicated to the proposition that all men are created equal " ... " that this nation , under GOD , shall have a new birth of freedom " . <p> This is the time to make our society , our democracy , and our economy work better for all Americans . <p> Yes , COVID-19 must be defeated . But , we must look beyond the pandemic and move boldly into our " New Birth " as a nation , knowing that large purpose often requires large sacrifice . <p> Our elected representatives must plan now to step up to the tasks of dealing boldly with : <p> -- Climate change , knowing that the U.S. remains responsible for 15 percent of the world 's greenhouse gas emissions . In the U.S. federal government 's absence , 40 of our country 's mayors , plus the mayors of Paris , Montreal , Mexico City , and others have signed an agreement to uphold the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> -- Re-work our tax code , which has created the largest inequality of incomes and wealth in the world . <p> -- Dealing with healthcare issues which leave millions of poor among us without affordable access . <p> -- Insuring the viability of Social Security , Medicare , and Medicaid programs , which have " unfunded liabilities " of $132 trillion and have been left for future generations to pay . <p> -- Upgrading a decaying infrastructure which has been with us since the 1950s . I 've ridden the " magnetic levitation train " in China from Pudong Airport to Shanghai . We traveled 30 miles in 7 minutes , reaching speeds of 428 kilometers per hour . <p> -- And finally , if the city of Chicago can provide public-school kids the opportunity for a free path from pre-K through four years of college , certainly our federal and state representatives can imagine and support education programs which prepare our nation 's youth for the workforce of the future , break the cycle of poverty among the poor , and deal with a student loan debt crisis that is larger than the credit card debt of all Americans . <p> If these issues are too large for our current leadership , and require sacrifices they are not willing to ask all to make , then maybe those leaders should be in a different line of work .
