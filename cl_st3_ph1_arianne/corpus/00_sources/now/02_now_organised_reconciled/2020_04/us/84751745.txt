<h> France wants fossil fuel price floor for EU green goals <p> BRUSSELS ( Reuters ) - The European Union should impose policies to underpin fossil fuel prices to protect the bloc 's climate ambitions during its recovery from the coronavirus pandemic , the French government said . <p> FILE PHOTO : Activists protest against the carbon dioxide emissions trading in front of the World Congress Centre Bonn , the site of the COP23 U.N . Climate Change Conference , in Bonn , Germany , November 17 , 2017 . REUTERS/Wolfgang Rattay/File Photo <p> EU ministers met on Tuesday to discuss the role of energy markets when the health crisis ends . Most EU countries believe the bloc 's recovery plan must uphold its Green Deal policy to decarbonise by 2050 . <p> In a document sent to ministers ahead of the virtual meeting and seen by Reuters , France called for the EU to use taxes or its carbon market to prevent " extremely low prices " seen during the pandemic from thwarting climate ambitions . <p> " The cost of fossil energies should be proportionate to their true environmental impact , " the document said . <p> Some countries including the Netherlands and Austria voiced support for the French price floor plan , according to three EU officials . <p> Countries such as Poland backed other parts of the proposal , including a call to quickly impose carbon charges for imports into the bloc . <p> The Polish government warned last month that the economic hit from the pandemic will make EU climate policies harder to achieve . <p> The European Commission plans next year to review EU energy taxation rules and the emissions trading system ( ETS ) , a carbon market policy that forces industry to pay for the carbon dioxide emissions . <p> But France called on the EU to take steps " without delay " to bolster the ETS market stability reserve , which removes surplus carbon permits from the system to avoid a build-up of supply . <p> With Europe headed for a steep recession , the fear is that the ETS could suffer the same fate after the 2008-2009 financial crisis when lower prices for carbon permits provided little incentive for firms to curb their pollution . <p> Energy prices in Europe have plummeted because of lockdowns during the pandemic . <p> The benchmark European API2 coal futures price hit $52.00 a tonne on Tuesday , its lowest level since June 2016 . <p> Prompt benchmark European gas prices in Britain and the Netherlands have also hit record lows . <p> So far , lower fossil fuel prices have not boosted coal-fuelled generation because of low power demand . <p> But those supporting a strong carbon price want to ensure there is no rapid increase in fossil fuel power production when Europe 's economy recovers . <p> Commission president Ursula von der Leyen on Tuesday said her Green Deal plan to decarbonise Europe 's economy by 2050 should serve as the bloc 's " motor for the recovery " .
