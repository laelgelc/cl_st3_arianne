<h> Letter : Use your time to help resurrect democracy <p> If your locked-down status is leaving you with time on your hands , put it to good use . How about helping to resurrect democracy ? <p> Democracy thrives when an informed citizenry is actively and publicly engaged in the issues of the day . So why are n't you getting informed and raising your voice on the issues that concern you most ? <p> Our struggles with COVID-19 tell us that committed and well-informed leadership is necessary to protect the American people . And we need to hear less " Me " and more " We " in coming together as a country . <p> This is a good context in which to discuss climate change . First of all , we have neither informed leadership nor an informed public on this issue . Both our president and governor are willfully ignorant . For three decades the fossil fuel industry has actively undermined the peer-reviewed science while offering no science that has survived peer review . <p> Meanwhile , Australia has experienced drought , heat waves and forest fires . The permafrost and the ice caps are melting faster than scientists have predicted . <p> So , inform yourself ! Google the Summary for Policymakers of the IPCC 's Report on 1.5 degrees Celsius , which calls for " rapid , far-reaching , and unprecedented changes in all aspects of society . " Read Falter by Bill McKibben if you want a sobering account of how we got to this point and why we must immediately act together . If you want to understand the fossil fuel industry 's role in misinforming the American people , google America Misled : How the Fossil Fuel Industry Deliberately Misled Americans About Climate Change . If you want to understand the many things our government could be doing to address climate change , read Drawdown : The Most Comprehensive Plan Ever Proposed to Reverse Global Warming , edited by Paul Hawken.
