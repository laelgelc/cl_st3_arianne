Bernie Sanders , the progressive whose two campaigns for president pulled the Democratic Party to the left , ended his White House bid Wednesday , leaving former Vice President Joe Biden as the party 's presumptive nominee for the 2020 election . <p> The Vermont senator 's announcement created fresh urgency for Mr. Biden to unite the factions within the party and win over Mr. Sanders 's supporters . It also capped a remarkable turnaround for the former vice president and a stark fall for Mr. Sanders , who was the front-runner after the first three nominating contests , before Mr. Biden 's decisive victory in South Carolina vaulted him into a series of triumphs in early March and a nearly insurmountable delegate lead . <p> Mr. Sanders , 78 years old , ended his bid following Tuesday 's Wisconsin primary , which was marred by logistical and political challenges that could face voters in November if the pandemic lingers . During a live stream address , Mr. Sanders acknowledged that his " path toward victory is virtually impossible , " adding that if he " had a feasible path to the nomination , I would certainly continue the campaign . But it 's just not there . " <p> Mr. Sanders said the coronavirus pandemic , which kept him from holding his signature massive rallies , was a critical factor in his decision to leave the race . And while Wisconsin will not report results until next week , polling indicated he was likely to suffer another significant loss . <p> Bernie Sanders said he would end his run for the Democratic nomination , leaving former Vice President Joe Biden as the party 's presumptive nominee for the 2020 election . Photo : Etienne Laurent/EPA/Shutterstock <p> A senior aide to Mr. Sanders said his decision was the culmination of weeks of discussions and evaluation of different scenarios . His campaign had expected Wisconsin , like many other states , to postpone its primary , the aide said , giving him more time for consideration . But after the Supreme Court ruled the election would proceed , the urgency for Mr. Sanders to make a decision increased . <p> In his remarks Wednesday , Mr. Sanders congratulated Mr. Biden , calling him " a very decent man , " and said he would work with him to move progressive policy ideas forward . <p> However , Mr. Sanders said he would remain on the ballot in the states set to vote in order to continue accumulating delegates , which would give him more leverage to shape the party platform at the convention in August . <p> Mr. Biden 's campaign has held multiple calls with Mr. Sanders 's team in recent weeks to discuss ways of coalescing the party around Mr. Biden 's candidacy . The pair spoke by phone on Wednesday , according to people familiar with the call . <p> In a lengthy statement , Mr. Biden praised Mr. Sanders 's contributions to the party . The self-described democratic socialist campaigned on establishing single-payer health care , eliminating student and medical debt , making public colleges and universities free , and overhauling the economy to combat climate change . His two presidential bids popularized many of his ideas ; a Wall Street Journal/NBC News poll from March found 67% of Democratic primary voters supported a Medicare for All system . <p> With coronavirus taking center stage , Mr. Biden 's campaign framed the general election as a choice of whether he or the president best offers experience and competence along with compassion and empathy . With that in mind , Mr. Biden 's campaign has been reaching out to the Vermont senator 's backers in hopes of preventing the kind of tension that lingered in the party after Mr. Sanders 's loss to Hillary Clinton in the 2016 nominating contest . <p> " I see you , I hear you , and I understand the urgency of what it is we have to get done in this country . I hope you will join us . You are more than welcome . You 're needed , " Mr. Biden said to Mr. Sanders 's supporters . <p> President Trump made clear he would try to woo some of Mr. Sanders 's supporters too , suggesting in tweets that establishment Democrats had prevented Mr. Sanders from capturing the nomination . " The Bernie people should come to the Republican Party , TRADE ! " Mr. Trump tweeted . <h> Sen. Bernie Sanders and his wife Jane Sanders arrive at a campaign rally at Morehouse College in Atlanta in November 2019 . <p> Photo : Nicole Craine for The Wall Street Journal <p> Mr. Sanders 's exit also could bring former President Obama , perhaps the Democratic Party 's biggest name , off the sidelines . Mr. Obama remained neutral in the race and made clear he would help unite the party once the primaries concluded . Mr. Obama did not immediately offer his endorsement , but his support of Mr. Biden is widely expected . <h> More <p> Mr. Obama has held multiple conversations with 2020 candidates , including Mr. Sanders , over the last few weeks about how to best position the party to win in November , according to a person familiar with the conversations . <p> Mr. Biden could also appeal to a wider range of Democrats with his choice of running mate . He has pledged to choose a woman for the slot . Some of the potential candidates include a trio of Mr. Biden 's former presidential rivals -- Sens . Kamala Harris of California , Amy Klobuchar of Minnesota and Elizabeth Warren of Massachusetts -- plus Michigan Gov . Gretchen Whitmer and Sen. Catherine Cortez Masto of Nevada . Ms. Harris , who joined Mr. Biden at a virtual fundraiser Wednesday , formed a joint fundraising agreement earlier in the day with the Democratic National Committee to support the nominee and Democrats up and down the ballot . <p> Mr. Biden also faces pressure to adopt more liberal policies . Several liberal advocacy groups issued a letter to Mr. Biden Wednesday urging him to adopt policy positions in line with Mr. Sanders on issues such as the Green New Deal , Medicare for All and free undergraduate tuition . They also requested that he decline to appoint any current or former Wall Street executives , corporate lobbyists or people affiliated with the fossil fuel , health insurance or private prison industries to his transition team or administration if he wins the White House . <p> Stef Feldman , Mr. Biden 's policy director , said the campaign had been engaging with progressive leaders and was " considering additional policies that build on " Mr. Biden 's proposals . <p> Mr. Sanders 's fortunes have changed dramatically since the end of February , when he appeared likely to cruise to the nomination . He had overcome a heart attack in the fall to become the Democratic front-runner , winning Nevada by 27 points , narrowly capturing New Hampshire and effectively tying for the lead in Iowa . <p> But the party 's moderate wing began to consolidate around Mr. Biden after he won South Carolina by 28 points with the help of black voters , a key bloc of Democratic primary voters . Four former candidates backed Mr. Biden over the course of a week . Ms. Warren , the candidate closest to Mr. Sanders ideologically , also exited the race but did n't endorse anyone . <h> Sen. Bernie Sanders and former Vice President Joe Biden during the Democratic Presidential Debate in Atlanta in November . <p> Photo : Maddie McGarvey for The Wall Street Journal <p> Mr. Sanders suffered several disappointing weeks in March . A 17-point loss in Michigan that month cemented his dire position . The Vermont senator had long said that in a general election , he would be able to carry Midwestern states like Michigan that narrowly voted for President Trump in 2016 . Mr. Sanders had won the state in a surprise victory against Hillary Clinton in the Democratic primary that year . <p> The Vermont senator also did not expand the electorate , as he had promised . Though some contests showed an increase in turnout , it was generally in Mr. Biden 's favor . In many states , Mr. Sanders 's strong hold on the youngest and most-liberal voters was more than offset by Mr. Biden 's strength among less-liberal and older voters . <p> While versions of Mr. Sanders 's liberal policies were broadly popular with the Democratic electorate , Mr. Sanders faced voters who prioritized getting Mr. Trump out of office and voted on what they viewed as electability rather than policy . Many of them worried that a democratic socialist would be easier for the president to beat in a general election . <h> Bernie Sanders held a large campaign rally in Long Island City , N.Y. , in October after recovering from a heart attack . <p> Photo : Natalie Keyssar for the Wall Street Journal <p> Mr. Sanders 's political career began in 1981 when he became mayor of Burlington , Vt. , winning by just 10 votes . He was elected to the House of Representatives in 1990 and to the Senate in 2006 . Mr. Sanders has always identified as an independent but has caucused with Democrats while in Washington . <h> Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez endorsed Mr. Sanders and became a draw at his campaign events . <p> Mr. Sanders became a prominent national figure during his run for the Democratic nomination against Mrs. Clinton , the former secretary of state , in the 2016 presidential election . Mr. Sanders started in that race as a long shot , but rose to be considered a real threat to Mrs. Clinton . <p> She ultimately secured the nomination , but his 2016 run gave him a national profile that helped him launch his 2020 campaign as a front-runner . And many voters cited his consistency in pushing for liberal priorities , including a single-payer health system , as a reason to back his second White House bid . <h> Despite being the oldest candidate in the 2020 Democratic field , Mr. Sanders had a fervent base of young supporters .
