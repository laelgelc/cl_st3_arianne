<h> Monmouth College to have online graduation in May , live ceremony in September <p> Nick Vlahos Journal Star @vlahosnick <h> Wednesday <p> Apr 15 , 2020 at 3:20 PMApr 15 , 2020 at 3:34 PM <p> MONMOUTH -- This year 's spring graduates of Monmouth College can consider themselves special . <p> Because of the coronavirus pandemic , they 'll get to graduate twice . <p> Monmouth is to conduct an online commencement next month , followed by an in-person ceremony Sept. 19 , according to information the college released Wednesday . <p> The online portion is scheduled for May 16-17 . <p> Various school commencement traditions are to be posted the first day and earlier that week at the Monmouth website and on the college Facebook page . <p> A combined baccalaureate and commencement ceremony is to be posted May 17 . Monmouth President Clarence Wyatt and faculty dean Mark Willhardt are to read graduates ' names and confer degrees . <p> " It breaks our hearts that we wo n't be together in person on that day this year , but we will gather virtually to celebrate our seniors , their families and their faculty and staff mentors , " Wyatt stated in a news release . " And we will come together in person in the fall to share and celebrate again . " <p> About 200 graduates are likely , according to Monmouth spokesman Duane Bonifer . He expects most of them to return for the September ceremony . <p> Bonifer cited the close-knit nature of the college . The September ceremony is expected to be the first opportunity for the graduates to convene since in-person classes were canceled last month . <p> Unique aspects of the Monmouth commencement ceremony , including a bagpiper-led procession , also might lead to sizable September participation , according to Bonifer . He compared it to a high-church ceremony . <p> " Assuming things are under control vis- ? -vis the COVID-19 pandemic , I expect the weekend to be a pretty memorable one , " Bonifer stated in an email . <p> The commencement speaker , Boston-based Sam Vaghar , is to offer an address during the virtual ceremony and at the in-person ceremony . <p> Vaghar is co-founder and executive director of the Millennium Campus Network .
