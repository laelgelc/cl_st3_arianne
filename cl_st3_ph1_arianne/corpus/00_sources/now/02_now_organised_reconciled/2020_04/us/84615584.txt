<h> Letter : The link between climate change and diseases <p> Published : 4/17/2020 12:01:17 AM <p> Modified : 4/17/2020 12:01:06 AM <p> From James Dodson 's letter on Easter : " At this time of fear and danger , I can think of no more inappropriate thing to push than the global warming/climate change narrative . Modern agriculture and transportation depend almost entirely on petroleum , and farmers , truckers and the fuel industry deserve thanks . " <p> Really ? Science has shown a direct relationship between climate change and infectious diseases . From the World Health Organization : " There are three categories of research into the linkages between climatic conditions and infectious disease transmission . The first examines evidence from the recent past of associations between climate variability and infectious disease occurrence . The second looks at early indicators of already-emerging infectious disease impacts of long-term climate change . The third uses the above evidence to create predictive models to estimate the future burden of infectious disease under projected climate change scenarios . " <p> There is indisputable evidence that shows that the fossil fuel industry is a major contributor to climate change . If you look at cause and effect , one could conclude that the fossil fuel industry has a link to the current pandemic crisis we are in , and you think that the fuel industry deserve thanks ?
