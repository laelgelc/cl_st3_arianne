<h> Reader 's View : Duluth stepping up has been inspiring <p> Written By : Brett Cease , Duluth Apr 19th 2020 - 8am . <p> It has made me so proud to read of our north-country business leaders from Duluth Pack , Frost River , Loll Designs , Wintergreen Wear , and so many other places all rethinking their supply chains and workforces to help support our medical personnel in making made-in-the-USA hospital gowns and other personal protective equipment . I went online to support their work . <p> Every story I 've heard of people helping those in need as we take precautions has filled me with hope that humanity can overcome fear with love and can face hard things and do what 's right in a crisis . Our coming together to prevent the worst of this pandemic has given us practice for doing so in the future as we face other essential challenges such as the impacts coming from climate change . <p> The News Tribune 's excellent coverage has inspired hope and highlighted how our community is supporting each other during the coronavirus outbreak .
