Progressives ( 59% of whom said the economy was not working well for them personally ) and those who are liberal or very liberal ( 55% of whom said the same ) . In other words , Sanders supporters . Sanders is campaigning in a socialist bubble . His message resonates with the far left but not with the working class . <p> Of course , everything could change if the coronavirus pushes the economy into recession .
