<h> SNEC 2020 solar conference postponed due to COVID-19 <p> April 10 ( Renewables Now ) - The SNEC PV Power Expo , the world 's largest solar trade show , has been postponed to October due to the global spread of the coronavirus COVID-19 , the organisers of the event said on Thursday . <p> This year 's edition of the solar conference and exhibition will be held from October 9 to October 12 , 2020 , according to an update by the organising committee and the Shanghai New Energy Industry Association . The venue of the trade show will be the National Exhibition Convention Center in Shanghai . <p> The trade show was originally planned to take place between May 24 and May 27 , 2020 , with the Shanghai New International Expo Center hosting the exhibition . <p> This is yet another one of a series of events that were cancelled or delayed due to the global COVID-19 pandemic . This year 's UN climate change summit COP26 , scheduled to take place in Glasgow in November , was put off earlier this month , while plans for Intersolar Europe 2020 were scrapped as the continent continues to fight against the crisis . <p> Veselina Petrova is one of Renewables Now 's most experienced green energy writers . For several years she has been keeping track of game-changing events both large and small projects and across the globe .
