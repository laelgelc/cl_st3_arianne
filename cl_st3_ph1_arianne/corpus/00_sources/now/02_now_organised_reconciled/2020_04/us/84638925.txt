If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Unlike other species , wood frogs can be found in winter climates <p> Jerry Carlson , Special to the Times Published 8:14 a.m . CT April 18 , 2020 <p> I was taking a sit-down break from my wood splitting operation when I noticed some movement on the far side of the driveway . At first , I thought it was a leaf doing cartwheels in the wind , but I soon realized it was more than that . I had to look . <p> Upon closer examination , I discovered that the movement I had observed was a frog hip hopping its way across the yard . Between the fact that it was an odd-looking frog and also a very cool day for frogs to be out and about , I became curious . <p> Buy Photo <p> Jerry Carlson ( Photo : Dave Schwarz , dschwarz@stcloudti ) <p> It did n't take long for me to find information on reddish-colored frogs in Minnesota . The internet is a wonderful resource for that sort of thing . In a few moments , I had learned that the unusual-looking amphibian I had seen was a wood frog . <p> Because wood frogs are common and widespread across North America , I am sure I have seen them before . However , I do n't remember seeing a frog that was this rusty red . <p> According to Wikipedia , wood frogs are known for their unusual tolerance to cold . Because of this , their range extends farther north than most other frog species . <p> Unlike many other frogs that migrate to the water for winter , wood frogs spend their winters in forested areas . Their fall migration takes them away from water into wooded regions where they dig a shallow hole under the leaves . <p> It is here they spend the winter , allowing their bodies to freeze . Their heart stops beating and their lungs stop breathing . Their bodies secrete special fluids prior to freezing that allow them to survive . <p> In contrast to the wood frog , the common leopard frog spends its winters in the water under the ice . These frogs have the ability to breathe through their skin in the winter months as they lay dormant on the bottom . <p> Although frogs are found throughout the world and have thousands of different species , their numbers are dwindling . This is somewhat alarming as they have been around for millions of years and are considered to be a barometer for the health of the Earth . <p> Loss of habitat , climate change , fungal diseases , pollution and pesticides are all thought to be part of the problem . <p> This is the opinion of outdoors columnist Jerry Carlson .
