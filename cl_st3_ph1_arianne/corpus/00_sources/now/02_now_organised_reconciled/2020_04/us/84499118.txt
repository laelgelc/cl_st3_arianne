<h> Harvard Book Store offers ' Quarantine Reading ' and new service to advise customers individually <p> Harvard Book Store has compiled a list of book recommendations dubbed " Quarantine Reading " that can be ordered online during the closing of the store 's physical location . This collection of 19 books is sure to provide weeks of entertainment for adult readers suffering from lockdown-induced boredom . The list contains staff favorites compiled originally for Boston.com and Boston magazine , and may get more additions over coming weeks , general manager Alex Meriwether said . It spans an impressively wide and eclectic range of literary genres , including titles such as Thomas Mann 's classic novella " Death in Venice , " Michael Finkel 's " The Stranger in the Woods : The Extraordinary Tale of the Last True Hermit , " Neil deGrasse Tyson 's " Astrophysics for People in a Hurry , " the Backyard Guide to the Birds of North America and all three installations of Hilary Mantel 's recently completed Cromwell-focused " Wolf Hall " trilogy .
