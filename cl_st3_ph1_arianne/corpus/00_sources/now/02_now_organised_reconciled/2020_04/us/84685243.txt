<p> Heatwaves across Europe last summer led to record-breaking temperatures in countries including France and Germany , often peaking above 40 degrees Celsius . <p> The report shows 11 of the 12 warmest years in a dataset running back to 1981 have taken place since 2000 ; unusually warm weather in February , June and July last year pushed 2019 ahead of previous highs in 2014 , 2015 and 2018 . <p> While annual rainfall was roughly at average for the full year due to downpours in November , the data shows soil moisture was the second lowest for 40 years . There were summer droughts across much of Central Europe . <p> The report said Greenland saw record summer ice melt , blamed on below-average snowfall and an earlier than expected spring . <p> The EU-funded Copernicus program uses satellites to monitor atmospheric and climate readings from space . A new mission , Sentinel-6 , will launch in November with two satellites to monitor rising ocean levels caused by climate change .
