John Arthur Smith , chair of New Mexico 's Senate Finance Committee and a state senator for over 30 years , formally announced Monday his bid for a ninth four-year term representing the 35th senate district . <p> In a news release , Smith , a Democrat , cited his " deep experience crafting equitable budgets that maximize every dollar " as critical and stated : " We must preserve our social safety net , lift displaced workers back into stability , and preserve gains we have made in early education , K-12 funding , and addressing climate change . " <p> Although former Democratic governor Bill Richardson famously described Smith as " Dr. No " over his fiscal conservatism , the senator has also helped the chamber say " yes " to initiatives such as all-day Kindergarten and early childhood education , as well as the creation in the most recent legislative session of an Early Childhood Trust Fund to be supported by in-state oil and gas revenues . <p> New Mexico Gov .
