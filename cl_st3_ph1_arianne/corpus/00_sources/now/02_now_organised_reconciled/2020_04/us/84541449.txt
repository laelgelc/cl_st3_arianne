<h> Court rolls back rollback <p> It 's Thursday , April 9 , and a federal court struck down another one of Trump 's environmental rollbacks . <p> The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Washington D.C. Circuit overruled the Trump administration 's 2018 decision to suspend an Obama-era regulation that restricted the use of hydrofluorocarbons ( HFCs ) , which are potent greenhouse gases . <p> The federal court found on Tuesday that the EPA inappropriately overhauled the rule without public notice or a comment period . The 2-1 ruling is a win for environmental groups , particularly the Natural Resources Defense Council ( NRDC ) , which had challenged the agency 's action . <p> The Obama-era rule limits the use of HFCs , which are found in air conditioners , refrigerators , aerosol propellants , and insulating foams . The chemical 's impact on climate change can be hundreds to thousands times worse than carbon dioxide , pound for pound . Without regulation , HFC use is projected to grow over the next few decades as a substitute for ozone-depleting substances that are being phased out under the Montreal Protocol . <p> " This is an important victory for our climate , " NRDC attorney Peter DeMarco said in a statement . " EPA must ensure that as companies complete their transition away from ozone-depleting substances , they switch to alternatives safer than climate-polluting HFCs .
