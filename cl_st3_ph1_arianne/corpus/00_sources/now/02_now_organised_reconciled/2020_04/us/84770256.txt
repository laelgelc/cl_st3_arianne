<h> Total Solar DG installs rooftop PV array for Bollore Logistics <p> April 30 ( Renewables Now ) - Total Solar Distributed Generation said today it has concluded work on a 1-MW rooftop solar system for Bollore Logistics ' Green Hub in Pioneer Turn , Singapore . <p> The company , which is wholly owned by French energy major Total SA , has installed more than 2,400 photovoltaic ( PV ) panels which will be able to produce about 1.3 GWh per year , or enough to meet about 30% of the building 's power needs . <p> Bollore Logistics , part of French transportation group Bollore , has pledged to reduce 43% of its greenhouse gas emissions by 2027 . <p> " In Singapore , we have over 200,000 sq m of warehouse space and 1,600 employees . It is vital for us to tap into sustainable energy to support our electricity consumption in the fight against climate change , " commented Frederic Marcerou , managing director of Bollore Logistics Singapore . He added that Green Hub is the company 's first building equipped with solar panels .
