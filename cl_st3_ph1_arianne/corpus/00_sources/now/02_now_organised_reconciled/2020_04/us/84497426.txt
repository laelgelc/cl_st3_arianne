Tom Carper deserves recognition for standing up for cleaner cars . As the ranking member of the Senate Environment and Public Works Committee , our senator has blasted the Environmental Protection Agency 's March 31 rollback of auto emissions standards . <p> Automakers had already agreed to improve fuel efficiency by 5% per year over the next few years , but the administration 's new rule will put the brakes on those plans . The result will be that drivers will not be able to go as far on a tank of gasoline , the air will be dirtier than it would have been , and America will continue to relinquish our leadership role in addressing climate change and sea level rise . <p> This is a particularly bad policy choice during the COVID-19 crisis , since increased air pollution causes or exacerbates asthma and other underlying respiratory conditions that make people more susceptible to the virus . Gas prices may be low now but they are notoriously volatile , and the new rule will result in drivers paying more to fuel their inefficient cars over the long term . <p> Other countries from Asia to Europe are mandating or encouraging cleaner vehicles , including electric vehicles . Electric vehicles can deliver even cheaper fuel costs . Electricity costs about one-half to one-third the cost of gasoline , mile for mile . EVs also have lower maintenance costs , requiring no oil changes or tune-ups . America must not be left behind in the race to the future by this short-sighted policy change .
