<h> Letter : Pandemic shutdown should answer some climate questions <h> Wednesday <p> Apr 8 , 2020 at 12:05 AM <p> During this time when many are working from home , driving less , not traveling and not using one-time use plates and utensils , I hope our climate change scientists are gathering all the data they can . We should be able to look back at this period and clearly tell whether we humans can really affect the level of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere . <p> If the data shows a significant drop , we can have a science-based discussion about permanently adopting the lifestyle the corona virus has temporarily imposed on us . <p> If there has not been a scientifically significant drop , I think we need to relook at our hypothesis . <p> Tom P.
