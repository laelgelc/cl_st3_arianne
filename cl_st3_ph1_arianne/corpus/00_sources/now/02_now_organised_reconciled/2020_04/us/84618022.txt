<h> Mark Longley : Like virus , climate change was labeled a hoax <p> For years , health experts have been warning us about an impending pandemic of the current magnitude . It 's clear we were n't listening and as a consequence we did n't adequately prepare for what was forecast . <p> Our late start in addressing the problem contributed to the acute nature of this calamity . So , as we emerge from the current existential threat of the coronavirus , it might be wise to consider that we have been forewarned for many years by the world 's top scientists of another rapidly approaching existential threat to mankind : global warming/climate change . Are we listening ? <p> Early on , the virus was labeled a hoax ; climate change has also been called a hoax . Is there a lesson here ? <p> On the positive side , we know now what steps we need to take to reverse the effects of burning fossil fuels and releasing greenhouse gases . But are we taking those steps ? <p> This may be a good time to reflect on how we can invest in solutions at the local , state and national level and join forces with diverse , enlightened leaders to chart a new course for a sustainable future . <p> If you would like to learn more about global warming and what you as an individual can do to help turn the tide , check out the " Get Involved " tab at sandwichclimate.com .
