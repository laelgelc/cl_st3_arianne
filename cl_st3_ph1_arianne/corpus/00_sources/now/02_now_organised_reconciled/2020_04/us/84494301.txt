( Photo : Adam Berry , Getty Images ) <p> Everybody deserves to live a healthy , prosperous life in a safe setting regardless of race , gender , religion , age , or socioeconomic status . This is what community health is about : ensuring that there is access and resources to health care regardless of one 's upbringing . <p> As public health nurses , we try to educate those who do not have access to health care information such as those with no insurance or who are unable to visit doctors due to disabilities . National Public Health week is April 6-12 , with each day highlighting a specific aspect of health care . <p> On Monday , we are advocating for mental health . One in five Americans experience some type of mental illness . Suicide rates are up in California , and many with mental health issues are likely to develop drug dependence to cope with their feelings . As public health nurses , we want to promote getting help through rehabilitation or mental health clinics , so people can live optimal , proactive lives . Promoting telemedicine options that are free of charge and easily accessible , such as suicide and help hotlines , can help people work through their problems . <p> Tuesday focuses on maternal and child health . Some 31% of women will face pregnancy complications in the United States . Moreover , ensuring access to financial aid programs such as WIC and SNAP are essential to make sure both the mother and baby have adequate food and their needs are met . If we promote education to all women before birth , fewer complications will occur and families will be better prepared to care for their child . <p> Next , Public Health shifts to violence prevention . About one in four men and one in three women have had a form of physical intimate partner violence , and we had 39,773 gun-related deaths in 2017 . By educating the community and advocating gun safety laws and trauma-informed services , we can interrupt cycles of abuse and promote healthy gender norms and relationships . <p> On Thursday , we emphasize environmental health to ensure our planet stays healthy and we try to prevent climate change . Millions of Americans are at risk due to unsafe drinking water , with air pollution causing health issues such as asthma . With more natural disasters expected , it is necessary to prepare the community for disaster plans so their loved ones can be safe . We are also promoting reducing greenhouse gases by recycling , carpooling and relying on reusable items to reduce their carbon footprint . <p> Access to education is the primary focus for Friday . If one graduates high school , their average lifespan automatically increases up to nine years . We are campaigning for eliminating school lunch debt completely , ensuring medical and support services , and integrating families into their children 's education . We want to make sure school is accessible and welcoming to all children . <p> The next day , we are promoting safe and healthy housing . Lead pipes , pest infestations and residential crowding places a community at a higher risk for health problems . We want to enforce maintenance schedules to ensure housing does n't become substandard and stronger housing regulations . <p> Lastly , Sunday addresses economic inequality . There are 6.8 million children living in poverty , which harms their ability to succeed and thrive in their health and development . We encourage tax policies that will help distribute resources to support low-income families . <p> To sum up , public health addresses so many aspects necessary for a prosperous life . Spread the word about National Public Health Week to help educate those in need .
