A standard drought can last for several months to more than a decade . <p> CNN reports that the study confirms a very wet period occurred from about 1980 to 1998 , right before the onset of the latest megadrought . This sudden shift in environmental conditions from wet to arid " was hastened by the background drying forced from anthropogenic warming , " according to the study . <p> This conclusion implies a second megadrought caused by climate change rather than natural meteorological conditions . The Washington Post writes that historically , megadroughts are caused by fluctuations in tropical oceanic conditions , such as the cyclic cooling of tropical Pacific waters known as La Ni ? a . <p> " The megadrought era seems to be reemerging , but for a different reason than the past megadroughts , " Park Williams , the study 's lead author and a researcher at the Lamont-Doherty Earth Observatory at Columbia University , told the Post . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> CNN notes that instances of megadroughts are likely to worsen ; the study reportedly notes that anthropogenic global warming -- or human-caused climate change -- is likely to bring more changes in the environment . <p> " The magnitude of future droughts in North America and elsewhere will depend greatly on future rates of anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions globally , " the study notes . <p> The effects of a parched landscape obviously include a shortage of water flow in rivers and reservoirs , resulting in less groundwater available for human use , like drinking and irrigation . <p> " I think the important lesson that comes out of this is that climate change is not a future problem , " Benjamin I.
