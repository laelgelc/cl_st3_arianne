<h> Vermont 's The Global Warming Solutions Act ( GWSA ) <p> The global warming solutions act will raise energy costs and increase litigation in the state while doing nothing to prevent climate change . <p> Making Act 168 's Greenhouse Gas Reduction aspirational Goals of reducing statewide greenhouse gas ( to 25% below the 1990 level by 2012 , 50% below by 2028 , and " if practicable " , 75% below by 2050 ( 10 VSA 578 ) into mandatory requirements by allowing regulators , unchecked by elected representatives to enforce even steeper emission cuts would be bad for the economy , bad as a matter of law and principle , and do nothing to prevent climate change . Regarding the latter point , contrary to the claims of advocates for this change , the planet does not face a climate emergency , meriting decarbonizing every human activity -- an impossible goal in any case . <p> This paper concludes : <p> Unilateral mandatory action to reduce emissions will cripple the competitiveness of Vermont in the national economy . It will force Vermonters to rely on uncertain and inevitably more expensive energy sources in every aspect of their daily lives . <p> Enforcing a vast array of mandatory carbon emissions reduction rules will require an army of regulators and enforcers , leading to a Green Police State . <p> Most damning , the GWSA 's vast expansion of government power will be commenced without the record vote of a single member of the legislature . This is a shocking violation of the Vermont Constitution ( Ch . I Art . 6th ) and an assault on democratic government itself .
