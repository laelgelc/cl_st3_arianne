If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Pandemic could delay first trials in Thomas , Woolsey fire lawsuits against Edison <p> The coronavirus outbreak could delay the first trials in the Thomas and Woolsey Fire lawsuits against Southern California Edison . <p> Jury selection in the first Thomas Fire trial was set to start June 1 , with arguments beginning June 15 , said Westlake Village-based attorney Alexander Robertson . The unredacted report on the cause of the Woolsey Fire , as well as its backup evidence , was set to be released to attorneys this month , Robertson said . His firm represents plaintiffs affected by both fires . <p> But the current pandemic has delayed preparations for the summer trial and apparently pushed back the release of the Woolsey Fire investigative report , Robertson said . <p> " Unfortunately , COVID-19 has completely shut down the Woolsey case and its 90% shut down the Thomas case , " Robertson said . <p> Plaintiffs allege negligence on Edison 's part led to the December 2017 Thomas Fire that started in Ventura County and spread to Santa Barbara County . The November 2018 Woolsey Fire also originated in Ventura County and stretched into Los Angeles County . <p> Residents from all three counties affected by the fires have sued the Rosemead-based utility company . <p> According to the Ventura County Fire Department , there were two ignition points for the Thomas Fire and both blazes eventually merged together as one . The department 's investigative report shows Edison power lines were to blame for the ignition on Koenigstein Road and in Anlauf Canyon . <p> In Los Angeles County Superior Court , where the wildfire litigation is being handled , the Spring Street courthouse was shut down as part of its efforts , according to a March 23 news release from Presiding Judge Kevin Brazile . That 's the building where hearings are held in the fire lawsuits , Robertson said . <p> Edison officials said its legal team is doing what it can under the circumstances . <p> " Despite the pandemic and stay-at-home restrictions designed to keep everyone safe , SCE has been working diligently with the other parties on issues that can be addressed during this brief period when the courts are closed , " according to a statement from Robert Laffoon-Villegas , an SCE spokesman . <p> " When possible , we have continued to engage in the discovery process and work on pre-trial issues with plaintiffs in both matters . " <p> Much of the preparations left over in the Thomas Fire case deal with depositions , Robertson said . With the state , local and federal guidelines about social distancing that would be difficult to manage . Robertson and other plaintiffs ' attorneys suggested trying depositions using video technology , which other states have permitted during this time , Robertson said . <h> Posted ! <p> A link has been posted to your Facebook feed . <p> Firefighters run for their lives Saturday at De La Garrigue Road and Highway 150 near Ojai on Dec. 9 .
