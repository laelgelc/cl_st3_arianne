If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> The 50th anniversary of Earth Day , April 22 was supposed to be a big deal . Organizers of annual large events in Ojai , Ventura , Oxnard and Thousand Oaks expected bigger crowds than ever . <p> Although large gatherings are canceled , " It 's not a day ; it 's a movement , " and commemoration goes on , according to website of The Earth Day Network , a nonprofit that consolidates information from event organizers . <p> The Earth Day Network provides " isolutions " for coronavirus self-isolation activities benefitting the earth . " Feeling powerless ? " they ask in one of their daily postings . " Switch to green power ... Swapping out fossil fuels like natural gas and coal for renewables is one of the easiest ways to reduce your carbon footprint . " <p> People can contact a solar installation company , many of which are still taking appointments for estimates during the pandemic . In Ventura County , where community choice aggregated power is available , many people have the option of switching to fully renewable power sources through their existing energy provider . <p> Every day this month , the Earth Day Network has been providing a daily " environmental challenge " online . Promoted activities include suggestions for eating a plant-based diet , fixing and doing-it-yourself instead of buying , conducting a plastic audit in your home and composting . <p> Day 14 was particularly interesting , as the network provided participants an opportunity to " Skype a scientist , " making scientific experts available on topics that include plastic pollution , climate change , biodiversity and Earth Day . <p> Each business will offer something specific to their audience , like a certain take-out meal , yoga pose , online purchase offer or fitness challenge , and they will share it on social media with the hashtag #VLTEarthDaySpecial . <p> For other local Earth Day business activities , consider the list , provided by the non-profit Surfrider Foundation of Ventura County , of " Ocean Friendly " certified restaurants offering takeout-curbside service during the pandemic : https : **28;327;TOOLONG . Ocean Friendly restaurants " take proactive steps to eliminate single-use plastics , " according to the Surfrider website . <p> Earth Day is also a good day to consider buying an electric car . Local car dealerships ' physical locations are not open , but many are offering off-site test drives and delivery . <p> " To provide for social distancing , we bring a car to your house and let you test drive it unaccompanied by a salesperson , " said Tony Anaya , sales manager for Simi Valley Chevrolet . <p> All it takes is a copy of your driver 's license , insurance card , a signature on a " borrow a car " form and an appointment . <p> For Earth Day , Ayala is featuring the electric Bolt 's loaded LT version for $29,170 , with discounts . The Manufacturer 's Suggested Retail Price is $38,670 , and although earlier this month , Bolts reached high sales levels making them ineligible for state and federal incentives , a dealer discount of $1,000 and a factory rebate of $8,500 compensate . <p> Similarly , Tesla is offering a " touchless delivery " option for new car buyers .
