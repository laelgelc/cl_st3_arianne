<h> ' Great Global Cleanup ' How to watch Zac Efron , Zach King and more in this Earth Day special <p> The Great Global Cleanup , with hosts Zac Efron ( The Greatest Showman ) and filmmaker and social media star Zach King , airs tonight , April 22 , at 9 p.m . ET on the Discovery Channel . You can also live stream it on Sling Blue or fuboTV ( 7-day free trial ) . <p> The special spotlights the next generation of climate activists and what they are doing to clean up the planet . The special features different locations around the globe , including the beach in Efron 's hometown , where groups of individuals and volunteers try to clean up damage from climate change and humans . <p> In addition to the clean-up , Efron will interview Earth Day founder Denis Hayes and other environmental experts . <p> Celebrity personalities such as Lil Dicky , Liza Koshy and Cody Simpson also appear in the special .
