<h> Industry releases updated global sustainability reporting guide <p> APPEA recently joined with the International Petroleum Industry Environmental Conservation Association ( IPIECA ) , the American Petroleum Institute ( API ) and the International Association of Oil &amp; Gas Producers ( IOGP ) to endorse the release of the new edition of the Sustainability reporting guidance for the oil and gas industry . <p> The fourth edition of the Sustainability Reporting Guidance provides oil and gas companies with a clear framework to demonstrate their important role by reporting on how they manage climate and sustainability impacts and opportunities . <p> Updates to the guidance have been applied to six focus areas : <p> Reporting process <p> Governance and business ethics <p> Climate change and energy <p> Environment <p> Safety , health and security <p> Social impacts . <p> Notably , modifications were made to improve reporting of performance indicators related to climate change and energy , as these areas are of real interest to an investment community increasingly committed to financing innovative , lower-carbon , energy paths . <p> The updated guidance represents the work of more than 80 representatives from 28 oil and gas companies from six continents . <p> The udpate was also supported by an independent external stakeholder panel comprised of experts representing NGOs , investors and investor groups , banks and expert consultants .
