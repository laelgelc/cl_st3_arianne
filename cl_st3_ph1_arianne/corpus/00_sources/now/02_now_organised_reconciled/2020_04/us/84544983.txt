If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> OPINION <h> Where will the U.S. go after the pandemic ? <p> As one reads , researches the novel coronavirus ( COVID-19 ) and continues observing the unfolding worldwide public health crisis , one ponders the aforementioned question . Clearly , the more testing is done , the more we discover the extent of this public health crisis and the impact on those infected , sick and dying . It will be sobering , frightening and physically , mentally and economically challenging to all . I submit we will persevere and get through this as we continue to act responsibly and adjust to the changed paradigm this public health crisis is manifesting nationwide , if not worldwide . <p> However , there must be a strong , coordinated central approach to this crisis by our federal government that is collaborative , sensible , transparent and honest . Our state , local governments and industry must be in sync with a particularly difficult approach . Political posturing , grandstanding , greed and lying by our governmental and political leaders have no place . <p> Individuals , organizations , businesses , corporations and public entities must serve all together through physical distancing , aspirational social communicating , taking care of themselves and others by exercising safe and sanitary practices . After this crisis ebbs , we must ensure we are never so grossly unprepared to effectively address the reality of infectious viruses , nor are we lax about prevention . <p> History shows us , and should have taught us , what is and what is not real . Let us learn from those lessons and upgrade our testing abilities so we can accurately track the spread of viruses ; develop the ability to produce and stockpile necessary equipment for rapid comprehensive deployment ; and create clear and strategic coordination of physical-distancing practices , while promoting social connecting to enable people to emotionally and psychologically cope with such public health challenges . <p> Our nation and the infectious disease scientists have known about this type of potential outbreak , certainly since 2018 more recently , as well as from the history of such outbreaks for years before ( e.g. , smallpox , flu , Ebola , bird flu , swine flu , etc . ) . The record shows that our governments and citizens consciously and unconsciously fail to proactively address the research and the reality of world viruses and contagions although warned about them time and time again . <p> We can make movies about the public health challenges , but we refuse to be responsible for adequately and effectively preparing for such , and mitigating their current and future public health and economic impacts of viruses and/or pandemics . The world 's most powerful nation must be better in leading , researching and preparing for the public health challenges facing not only our communities , our nation , but also facing the world because we live in a global society supported by a global economy . <p> Perhaps , as author Ed Yong recently stated in The Atlantic , " ... one could also envisage a future in which America learns ... a communal spirit , ironically born through social distancing , causes people to turn outward , to neighbors both foreign and domestic ... The nation pivots , as it did after World War II , from isolationism to international cooperation . Buoyed by steady investments and an influx of the brightest minds , the health care workforce surges ... and public health become the centerpiece of foreign policy . The U.S. leads a new global partnership focused on solving challenges like pandemics and climate change . " <p> I , as one American living in my hometown of Springfield , Missouri on this planet Earth , am down with that vision ! <p> Read or Share this story : https : **39;325;TOOLONG ...
