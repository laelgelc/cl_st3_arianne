<h> Earth Day to be Celebrated Virtually <p> Today is Earth Day . Earth Day is celebrated April 22nd each year with this year being the 50th anniversary of Earth Day . This year 's theme for Earth Day is climate action . As COVID-19 continues to impact life throughout the world , Earth Day advocates are turning to technology to celebrate the day as well as plan future events . Advocates are asking residents to help drive action associated with Earth Day and the fight against climate change by flooding the digital landscape with global conversations , calls to action , performances , video teach-ins and more . The first Earth Day sparked the passage in the U.S. of the Clean Air , Clean Water and Endangered Species Acts , and the creation of the Environmental Protection Agency and the hope is more changes can be sparked by people around the world celebrating the 50th anniversary of Earth Day .
