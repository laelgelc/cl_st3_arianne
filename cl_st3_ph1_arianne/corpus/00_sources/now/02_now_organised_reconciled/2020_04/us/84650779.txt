As an agency whose mission is ostensibly , among its other functions , to safeguard natural resources ; it has followed a policy of exploitation of those very resources it is tasked with protecting . <p> Recall the extensive oil and gas exploration of a few years ago , utilizing large quantities of water from the District 's reservoirs , all for the purpose of enhancing its bottom line . With land holdings in 18 counties , the sky 's the limit . While commercial logging has undoubtedly been carried out by MWCD in the past , as a purportedly responsible land use policy amidst the fact of rapidly accelerating climate destabilization , it is untenable and inexcusable . <p> Our forests are a crucial element in storing large amounts of carbon . They provide ecosystem services for mitigating climate change at no cost to tax payers , along with other life enhancing benefits . At this this critical time , the Muskingum Watershed Conservancy District should be setting a much-needed example of thoughtful stewardship , and for actively addressing climate change in Ohio . Nobody else is . <p> David A. Lipstreu , Newark <h> Coronavirus : Be cautious but do not surrender your rights <p> The coronavirus outbreak has impacted our country in a way that is unprecedented in our history . But for the time being , let 's not talk about the disease -- whether it is as bad as predicted or not . Let 's talk about the impact on our Republic . <p> Governors in all the states have placed restrictions on the population to stay at home in hopes of containing infection . Businesses have closed down -- restaurants , shopping malls , even churches . Understandably precautions need to be taken to avoid becoming ill or spreading illness . But has it gone too far ? In some states it most definitely has . <p> In Kentucky , police were sent to a church parking lot to take the license numbers of people who dared to listen to a worship service . In Michigan , the governor has said people may not visit other homes , go to garden centers , even to go out to buy a baby car seat . In Virginia , the governor used this time to pass draconian gun control laws in clear violation of the Second Amendment . Speaker Nancy Pelosi has used her power to derail stimulus checks meant for helping businesses and individuals unless her private political agenda was included -- such as taxpayer funding of abortion . Really ? We have shut down our country to save lives , but should be obligated to fund the taking of baby 's lives ? <p> This should be the biggest wake-up call we have ever had in our country . That the liberty that we hold so dear , and take for granted , can be so easily revoked ; this should sound the alarm in every citizen in this nation . Yes , be careful and cautious when dealing with illness , but do not surrender your Constitutional rights and liberties in the face of mass induced hysteria . Once gone , they are exceedingly difficult , if not impossible , to regain . <p> Maureen Collins , Mansfield <h> Why does the media care about coronavirus and not abortion ? <p> Do you find it remarkable that our media and leaders are getting their shorts in a wad over the Chinese virus when America routinely has between 40,000 and 80,000 deaths yearly -- 83,000 the winter of 2018-19 -- from our usual winter influenza ? The Swine Flu in 2009-10 killed many of our children . More importantly , over 1 million unborn people lose their lives yearly in the United States through abortion and we barely hear a peep from them .
