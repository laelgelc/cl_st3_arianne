If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <p> COVID-19 is first and foremost a public health crisis , but as it has unfolded , it has rippled out into an economic crisis , too . The stock market has been volatile , lurching in ways we have n't seen since the 2008 recession . Businesses are closing their doors as millions stay home to try to limit the spread of the virus . In our community , we 've seen schools , businesses and places of worship shutting their doors to people to limit exposure . <p> All of this means families in York and across America are feeling unprecedented economic pain . There is likely more to come . It 's heartbreaking and frightening for so many . <p> To support people suffering from the economic impacts of the pandemic , our elected leaders approved making direct cash payments to the American people . <p> " We need cash in the hands of affected families , " said Republican Senator Tom Cotton from Arkansas , one of the first conservatives to call for this type of relief . <p> " So many people in this country are worried about what happens when their mortgage/rent/car payments/bills are due , " Democratic Congresswoman Maxine Waters posted on Twitter . <p> In the early stages of this discussion , there were no fewer than nine proposals for direct cash payments . We 're encouraged to see the broad agreement that during a crisis , it helps to put cash in people 's pockets and let them spend it how they see fit . <p> When Congress is able to turn its attention to climate change -- another looming crisis -- it should not forget this lesson : Direct cash payments are a simple , transparent , and fair way to support Americans when economic winds are shifting . <p> Climate change demands that we stop emitting greenhouse gases , which are trapping excess heat in our atmosphere and upsetting our planet 's delicate balance . Over the next 10 years , America needs to move from a fossil fuel-based economy to a clean energy economy . That will be a major change , but it should not be an acute crisis like we 're in now . By planning to give cash payments to Americans , we can ensure the health of our economy while making a gentle transition to a clean energy future . <p> Here 's how . Congress could put a price on carbon pollution , driving our economy away from fossil fuels and toward clean energy sources , and it could rebate that money as an equal cash payment , or " dividend , " to all Americans each month . <p> Cash payments put Americans themselves in the driver 's seat with their own finances . Individuals like us are empowered to make our own decisions about how we spend that money . We can pay bills , buy groceries , save , invest in a more energy efficient car , spend it at a local business , or anything else . <p> This is especially important for low- and middle-income Americans , who might otherwise struggle with the cost of energy and other price increases as we shift to a clean energy economy . When dividends are given to everyone on a regular basis , regardless of their income status , low- and middle-income Americans benefit dramatically . For anyone concerned about the " least of these " in our society -- who are uniquely vulnerable to both economic shocks and climate change impacts -- direct cash payments are an effective tool to help meet their needs . <p> Finally , cash dividends are transparent and easy to track , unlike tax offsets . That visibility helps people and our elected officials stay focused on the problem at hand : right now , the pandemic . Soon , climate change . <p> It 's clear that money in the hands of Americans helps keep our economy running . That 's why Congress and the President are about to use that tool in the current crisis . When we 've dealt with COVID-19 , let 's use that same tool to combat climate change .
