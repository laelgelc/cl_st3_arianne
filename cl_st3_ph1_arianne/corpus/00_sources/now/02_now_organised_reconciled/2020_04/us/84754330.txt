<h> Letter : Feltes has a strong record on climate crisis <p> When we vote this fall , there is no question that the climate crisis should be on the forefront of our minds . Electing a governor who is committed to combating climate change is absolutely essential . <p> Gov . Sununu 's two terms in office have made it clear he is the wrong man for such a job . That leaves the two candidates in the Democratic Primary : Dan Feltes and Andru Volinsky . We need a governor who has a strong record on dealing with the climate crisis , and Dan Feltes is that candidate . Andru Volinsky talks a lot about the environment , but Dan has actually done the work , including sponsoring 40 bills to advance clean energy jobs , combat the impacts of climate change and impose tough drinking water standards . <p> Furthermore , as a partner and shareholder in Bernstein Shur , Volinsky personally profited from his firm lobbying for projects like the Portland-Montreal pipeline . That was a mere five years ago and Volinsky has not distanced himself from the firm since . <p> On the other hand , Dan is the only candidate in the race whose concrete actions speak as loudly as his words about the climate crisis . I hope that you will all join me in voting for Dan Feltes because it is essential we elect a governor who cares about the environment and who we are certain can do what is necessary to protect it .
