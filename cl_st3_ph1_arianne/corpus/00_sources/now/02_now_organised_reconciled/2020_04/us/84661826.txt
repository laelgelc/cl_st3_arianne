<p> On site emergency responders were deployed and immediately initiated response procedures , working closely with the local fire department on arrival . The fire has been fully extinguished . <p> Our priority is the welfare of our employees and contractors who have been directly affected by the incident . All personnel are accounted for and there are no reported injuries . There were 58 people underground who were evacuated in accordance with procedures and use of rescue chambers . The majority of the personnel were evacuated without delay and those who could not safely evacuate were directed to refuge chambers . Once the fire was extinguished and situation determined safe , remaining personnel were transported to surface . <p> While an investigation to determine the cause will be initiated , an underground haulage truck was on fire around the 1140 m level of the mine . Relevant authorities and government officials have been notified . <p> It is too early to determine the impact on the underground mine and when operations can be restarted in the affected area of the mine . Surface operations continue , with the concentrating facilities presently undergoing scheduled maintenance .
