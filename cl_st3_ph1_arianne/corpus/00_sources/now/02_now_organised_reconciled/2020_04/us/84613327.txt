<h> Extreme floods to hit US cities ' almost daily ' by 2100 <p> by Patrick Galey <p> With oceans predicted to rise by one to two metres by 2100 , researchers in the US looked at the frequency of extreme water levels measured by 202 tide gauges along the US coastline <p> Coastal cities in the United States could experience " once in a lifetime " extreme flood events almost daily by the end of the century if sea levels continue to rise at current rates , new research published in Scientific Reports showed on Thursday . <p> With oceans predicted to rise by one to two metres by 2100 , researchers in the US looked at the frequency of extreme water levels measured by 202 tide gauges along the US coastline . <p> They combined this data with various modelled pathways of sea-level rise to predict the rate at which flooding events may increase in future . <p> The results were stark : at nearly three-quarters of the gauge locations the difference between average high tide and a once-in-50-years flooding event was less than a metre . <p> Most temperature scenarios predict sea-level rises higher than that . <p> The team also found that the risk of extreme flood events will double every five years on average as seas get ever higher . <p> This likely means profound impacts on major US cities including Miami , Atlantic City and Charleston in the decades to come , the authors concluded . <p> " In the absence of adaptation measures , the rate of coastal hazard impacts will likely double every five years , and this is indeed quite problematic , " lead study author Sean Vitousek , from the United States Geological Survey , told AFP . <p> " Miami , Honolulu , Charleston , Atlantic City , and many other US cities currently experience minor flood levels during tides exacerbated by any storm conditions . <p> " If sea-level projections hold , and in the absence of adaptation by 2050 , this flooding will become much more widespread , frequent , and severe . " <p> A special report from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change last year concluded that oceans could rise 1.1 metres by century 's end . <p> But Vitousek said rising oceans would cause " widespread disruption of transportation due to road closures during spring tides " much sooner , perhaps by 2050 . <p> On current rising trends , by 2100 some areas of coastal cities would become " practically uninhabitable " , he warned . <p> Citation : Extreme floods to hit US cities ' almost daily ' by 2100 ( 2020 , April 16 ) retrieved 17 April 2020 from https : **39;134;TOOLONG ...
