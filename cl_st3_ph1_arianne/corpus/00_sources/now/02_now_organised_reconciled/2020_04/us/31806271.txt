<p> Contra Costa County saw a jump in the number of new COVID-19 cases over the past day , but there were no new fatalities . <p> According to information released Saturday by the Contra Costa County Health Services Department , 34 new cases were reported in the county over the 24 hours ending at 11:30 a.m . Saturday , bringing the county total to 685 confirmed cases . <p> The increase was noteworthy -- up 15 from the previous day . It also was the first time in three days that the number of cases increased from the previous day .. <p> However , in perhaps the more significant finding , the number of deaths remained the same in Contra Costa County from the day before , holding steady at 19 . It was the first time since April 12 that a 24-hour period elapsed with no new deaths . <p> There has been other good news in Contra Costa County . The total number of people hospitalized countywide with COVID-19 peaked on Monday at 44 , and has fallen to 37 . <p> Broken down by city , Richmond has the most cases , with 75 , followed by Concord with 63 , Antioch with 51 , Pittsburg with 50 , Brentwood with 44 , Orinda with 43 and Walnut Creek with 37 . <p> Orinda has the most confirmed cases per capita , with a rate 238 per 100,000 people . <p> Countywide , 54% of the cases are women , and people age 41 to 60 make up the age group with the most cases , according to county health statistics . <p> Paul Rogers has covered a wide range of issues for The Mercury News since 1989 , including water , oceans , energy , logging , parks , endangered species , toxics and climate change .
