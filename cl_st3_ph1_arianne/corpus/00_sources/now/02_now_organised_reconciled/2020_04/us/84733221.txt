<h> Letter : Environmental rollbacks should not go unchecked <h> Monday <p> Apr 27 , 2020 at 10:57 PMApr 27 , 2020 at 10:57 PM <p> With the COVID-19 outbreak shutting down or limiting production , manufacturing and travel across the globe , there has never been a better time to think about the impact human intervention has on the environment . Because of the outbreak , pollution levels are plummeting to record lows in cities and countries across the globe . <p> The stall in pollution led to an increase in air quality , but the Trump administration still wants to continue to roll back regulations despite these positive outcomes . We need to take a look at our actions as a country to recognize that a cleaner , safer and better world is possible . <p> As a soon-to-be college graduate , I worry greatly about the future implications of climate inaction and deregulation . The 2019 California wildfire season was of particular worry , as the fires got dangerously close to my family that lives near the Bay Area . Yet , wildfires are just one of many possible weather extremes that could become more common because of climate change . <p> Across the state , MassPIRG chapters of student activists are fighting for the Decker/Garballey Bill H. 2836 to be passed here in Massachusetts so that our state will be powered by 100% renewable energy by the year 2045 . <p> We ask that you show your support for the planet and use your voice to ensure these unchecked environmental rollbacks do not go unnoticed . <p> Trevor Wilson <p> Hopedale <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
