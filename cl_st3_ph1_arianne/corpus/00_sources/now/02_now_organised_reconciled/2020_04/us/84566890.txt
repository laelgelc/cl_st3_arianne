Thank you for your support of the Monitor . <h> Letter : The vital role of petroleum <p> Published : 4/12/2020 12:01:08 AM <p> As I sit here writing this letter and gazing at a small corner of the kitchen , it amazes me to see the number of things in my immediate environment that owe their existence in whole or part to petroleum : a family member 's coat , a coffee machine , a microwave , a cell phone , a dish drainer , the flooring , utensils and so forth . <p> Also , there are two large bowls of fruit , all of it requiring transport from places very remote from here -- again , by petroleum . <p> At stores all over the map , vehicles that are either diesel- or gas-powered bring food , medicines and all sorts of vitally important items . At this time of fear and danger , I can think of no more inappropriate thing to push than the global warming/climate change narrative . <p> Modern agriculture and transportation depend almost entirely on petroleum , and farmers , truckers and the fuel industry deserve thanks .
