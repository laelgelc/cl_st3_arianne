<h> Letter : Apocalypse of climate change a self-fulfilling prophecy <h> Tuesday <p> Apr 28 , 2020 at 12:01 AM <p> Some Donald Trump supporters believe that he is the harbinger of the end times . Jerusalem is now the historic capital of Israel . Pestilence and locusts and strife are at the center of the world stage . Perhaps they are right . Trump does appear to embody many of the characteristics of an apocalyptic figure . <p> For starters , Trump is like a cult leader for millions and millions of devout followers . These folks will follow him anywhere , forgive him anything , and trust him for everything . His messaging is apocalyptic in tone and he seems to see himself as a man on a holy mission from God . In short , Trump and Republicans are playing their parts in a story that will end in a world ecological meltdown . <p> As I see things , the apocalypse of climate change is a self-fulfilling prophecy which certainly justifies being judged as having Biblical significance . But , as always , we humans can choose between the future of Trump and his Republican Party followers and a completely different future , one that tries to arrest the meltdown of our planet . <p> Rob Armour , Winter Haven <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
