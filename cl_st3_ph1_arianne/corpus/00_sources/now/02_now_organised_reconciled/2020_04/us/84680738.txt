<h> Elrich : ' We Can not Forget the Urgency of Climate Change ' <p> County Executive Marc Elrich released the following statement commemorating the 50th anniversary of Earth Day . <p> As we mark the 50th anniversary of Earth Day in Montgomery County , we have a lot to be proud of , but we also understand that there is so much more to do . Montgomery County embraces environmental stewardship as a fundamental responsibility to preserve and sustain our planet and has been a longtime leader in sustainability . <p> Even while we are in the midst of a pandemic , we can not forget the urgency of climate change . I am committed to leading the way to make our County greener , to make progress toward zero waste and to combat the climate emergency . And I look forward to doing that with our many partners throughout the County . <p> On this Earth Day and beyond , let us come together as a community and unleash our creativity and find innovative ways to preserve and sustain our County , our State , the country and the planet . We owe it to our children because they deserve to inherit a cleaner and more sustainable world .
