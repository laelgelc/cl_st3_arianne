If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Message from the Dalai Lama <p> Q : What does the Dalai Lama have to say about the coronavirus ? -- Fish from Colorado <p> A : Thank you Fish . We in the West often slip into thinking that the only resources for healing and hope we can access are those from Judaism and Christianity . As this plague continues , I hope periodically to include spiritual messages from Eastern traditions , as well . <p> Here is a recent posting sent to me on the internet from His Holiness the Dalai Lama . I hope his message reminds us all that there is no such thing as Jewish hope or Christian hope or Buddhist hope . There is just hope . Stay safe . <p> " Sometimes friends ask me to help with some problem in the world using some " magical powers . " I always tell them that the Dalai Lama has no magical powers . If I did , I would not feel pain in my legs or a sore throat . We are all the same as human beings , and we experience the same fears , the same hopes , the same uncertainties . <p> From the Buddhist perspective , every sentient being is acquainted with suffering and the truths of sickness , old age and death . But as human beings we have the capacity to use our minds to conquer anger and panic and greed . In recent years I have been stressing " emotional disarmament " to try to see things realistically and clearly , without the confusion of fear or rage . If a problem has a solution , we must work to find it ; if it does not , we need not waste time thinking about it . <p> We Buddhists believe that the entire world is interdependent . That is why I often speak about universal responsibility . The outbreak of this terrible coronavirus has shown that what happens to one person can soon affect every other being . But it also reminds us that a compassionate or constructive act -- whether working in hospitals or just observing social distancing -- has the potential to help many . <p> Ever since the news emerged about the coronavirus in Wuhan , I have been praying for my brothers and sisters in China and everywhere else . Now we can see that nobody is immune to this virus . We are all worried about loved ones and the future , of both the global economy and our own individual homes . But prayer is not enough . <p> The crisis shows that we must all take responsibility where we can . We must combine the courage doctors and nurses are showing with empirical science to begin to turn this situation around an protect our future from more such threats . <p> In this time of great fear , it is important that we think of long-term challenges -- and possibilities -- of the entire globe . Photographs of our world from space clearly show that there are no real boundaries on our blue planet . Therefore , all of us must take care of it and work to prevent climate change and other destructive forces . This pandemic serves as a warning that only by coming together with a coordinated , global response will we meet the unprecedented magnitude of the challenges we face . <p> We must also remember that nobody is free of suffering , and extend our hands to others who lack homes , resources , or family to protect them . This crisis shows us that we are not separate from one another -- even when we are living apart . Therefore , we all have a responsibility to exercise compassion and help . <p> As a Buddhist , I believe in the principle of impermanence . Eventually this virus will pass , as I have seen wars and other terrible threats pass in my lifetime , and we will have the opportunity to rebuild our global community as we have done many times before . In sincerely hope that everyone can stay safe and stay calm . At this time of uncertainty , it is important that we do not lose hope and confidence in the constructive efforts so many are making . " <p> -- His Holiness the Dalai Lama <p> ( BTTW : I will be appearing on Saturday morning ( prerecorded ) May 2 on the worldwide event organized by Oprah Winfrey and Tim Shriver and others called " A Call to Unite " check it out on your favorite internet platform .
