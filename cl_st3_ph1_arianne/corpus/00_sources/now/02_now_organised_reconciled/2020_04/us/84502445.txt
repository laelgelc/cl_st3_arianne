<h> Letter : The local impact of climate change <p> Published : 4/6/2020 12:01:18 AM <p> I 'd like to bring up an issue of concern for all of our futures . <p> As many of us Concord Monitor readers are aware , the climate is changing across the globe , with this past decade being the hottest ever recorded , and 2019 the second hottest year on record . This is not slowing down . As we continue to burn fossil fuels and emit greenhouse gases into the air , the effects of climate change will only worsen . <p> There are large effects , such as ocean acidification , flooding , intensifying storms , etc. , but as things worsen , there will be more local impacts too . Only 50% of ski resorts in New England are going to be economically viable by 2050 , and this will impact not only our economy but also New Hampshire overall , as many people choose to live here due to our beautiful mountains , long foliage season and temperate summers . This will hurt local businesses and our tourist season . <p> We need to take action to reduce these impacts . Right now only 21% of our energy comes from renewable sources . That 's not enough . We have the solutions . With everyone 's help calling our representatives to tell them these issues matter to us , we can help our futures .
