<h> PACJA calls on African states to think green in Covid-19 fight <p> ACJA Executive Director Mithika Mwenda said it would prove difficult in the long run for states to spend so much on mitigation of the Covid-19 pandemic but forget climate change , which has claimed many lives in Africa . <p> The Pan African Climate Justice Alliance ( PACJA ) has called on African states to give the Covid-19 pandemic a greener look and attempt to juggle the two crises with sincerity . <p> Speaking on Tuesday to mark the Earth Day , the PACJA Executive Director Mithika Mwenda said it would prove difficult in the long run for states to spend so much on mitigation of the Covid-19 pandemic but forget climate change , which has claimed many lives in Africa . <p> He referred to news that at least 48 people had been killed in the Democratic Republic of Congo , after it rained only once . In Kenya , where Dr Mithika spoke from , landlides claimed at least 12 lives overnight after flash floods . Many parts of the country have suffered flooding , and thousands displaced . <p> As the world marked the 50th anniversary of Earth Day on Tuesday , Mithika said it would be key for governments to help their people , including the indigenous communities that interact with nature more closely , to conserve them by ensuring the green climate fund reaches the grassroots . <p> Get breaking news on your Mobile as-it-happens . At the end of the day both will have killed huge numbers and messed our economies , " he said . <p> He regretted that the UNFCCC 's COP25 did not achieve much on climate action in Africa , and urged that the African Group of Negotiators prepares well and employs better tacks to ensure UN climate summit COP26 , which has been pushed to 2021 , achieves more on tangible issues . <p> The COP26 was scheduled to take place in Glasgow , Scotland in November . <p> " Climate change is foreseeable and we can do something to avert much . If we did the right thing in good time , there would be enough space for us to focus on emergencies like Covid-19 , " said Mithika . <p> The situation , Mithika said , was scary for developing countries as a lot of donor countries were busy grappling with the Covid-19 pandemic in their own countries . <p> " Right now everyone is inward looking and we can only beg .
