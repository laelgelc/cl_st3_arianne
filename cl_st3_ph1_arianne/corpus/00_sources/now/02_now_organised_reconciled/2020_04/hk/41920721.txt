<h> Why conservation focus is good for business <p> There is no doubt that these are tough times to be in business . Apart from the challenges of today 's fragile economy , we are facing the mother of all crisis with the rapid loss of the world 's biodiversity . Now more than ever , our impact and actions as individuals and organizations count in the fight for our planet and the preservation of our lives and businesses . <p> Our fast-growing human population explosion is causing significant extinction levels . We have already lost half of our forests and coral reefs and we have seen wildlife populations decrease on average by 60 percent in recent decades . Scientists estimate that we are losing a dozen species each day and forecasts suggest that up to half of all living species could be extinct by the middle of the century . Global temperatures are also rising , with 2016 being the warmest year on record for planet earth . The cold hard truth is that we are also running out of time to turn things around . <p> But there are also signs that change is coming . 2019 marked the year of the ' Greta Thunberg Effect ' and global climate change strikes which drove corporations around the globe to assess what changes they could make to reduce their impact . For example , retail giant Amazon unveiled a ' climate pledge ' promising that the company would transition to zero emissions by 2030 - and it 's a move we can all take inspiration from . <p> If a business with such a deep , large physical infrastructure can make this kind of change , then anyone can do it with the right vision , strategy and implementation . As business leaders in one of Asia 's hub cities , we have a role to play in changing things for the better . It 's in our hands to lead the charge and take action to implement conservation programs and instill greener and more sustainable practices across our organizations . Something that is very important for our city where sadly , one in five Hongkongers are simply not interested in environmental issues . <p> The business community has the influence , resources and capital , and the heat is on the private sector to make strides in conservation and lead the world in a more sustainable direction . Luckily , good environmental practices are also good for business , with HSBC reporting that 84 percent of companies want to make sustainable changes to improve revenues or financial performance . While the shift to sustainable , conservation-oriented business practices may seem daunting , the fact is that companies that have enacted sustainability initiatives are actually achieving new levels of success . <p> In fact , there are many reasons why sustainability and conservation practices also produce strategic and financial rewards . Beyond being more ethical , there are also bottom-line benefits of things like eco-friendly manufacturing and socially-conscious business strategies and numerous other examples point to the power of sustainability . For example , recent studies show that sustainable initiatives can increase sales revenue by up to 20 percent , increase market value by nearly 10 percent , lead to lower investment risk and cut employee turnover rates in half . <p> Companies that actively enforce corporate social responsibility ( CSR ) programs have a higher chance of attracting the best talent . Younger generations are more conscious of the impact they have on the environment . A firm 's CSR initiatives , business ethics and environmental practices are often deciding factors in an employer of choice for today 's workforce . According to some research , 62 percent of millennials prefer to work for a company that makes a positive impact on the planet . It can give them a reason to stay and to work harder , beyond earning a paycheck . <p> Conservation is also important to future-proofing your business -- after all , if the planet does n't make it , neither will your company . Apart from implementation of greener practices and education , looking at how your company can make investments towards securing the planet will change the game significantly . For example , companies looking at green investments can explore eco-land banks to secure animal habitats and natural resources which are needed across the globe from Africa to the US and Europe -- but of course ensuring these land banks are organically run and sustainably managed is a key consideration . <p> Apart from greener investments and business practices , if you 're interested in making a bigger impact why not start your own initiative to support the NGOs and issues you 're passionate about ? Last year we took action and started Auction4Wildlife , a global initiative to conserve the world 's wildlife by raising funds , via charity auctions . <p> Our first beneficiary , African Parks takes a holistic sustainable approach to wildlife conservation . They also actively work with local communities to find economic solutions for them , either through park employment or developing eco-tourism opportunities , to bring the local communities on-board so they are aligned with the Park in protecting the wild areas and wildlife . Their work is dependent on much needed funding and donations and we hope others in the global business community are inspired to get involved in conservation responsibility and believe in the impact they can make in support of the movement . <p> As recent world events have demonstrated , we can no longer sit on the sidelines of environmental change . If you want your business to be sustainable -- for the benefit of your shareholders , employees and the planet -- you must take action today . If you want to take action but are unsure where to start , my suggestion would be to look at the bigger picture as detailed , curated conservation programs take time , resources and energy to develop , but will play out to be beneficial for conservation and your businesses bottom line . <p> Every action counts towards securing our planet 's future and there are significant returns for businesses courageous enough to go down the route of being responsible . I encourage you to believe in the change you and your business can make in influencing the world around you . Be brave , and let your actions set the standard for other organizations in creating a world where wildlife thrives again .
