<h> The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet , headed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi has appointed Tarun Bajaj , Additional Secretary in the Prime Minister 's Office , as the new Economic Affairs Secretary <p> As many as 23 senior IAS officers have been appointed as secretaries in different central government departments as part of the bureaucratic rejig amid the coronavirus pandemic . <p> Under the bureaucratic reshuffle , Tarun Bajaj , Additional Secretary in the Prime Minister 's Office , has been appoiunted as the new Department of Economic Affairs Secretary . <p> Union Health Secretary Preeti Sudan , who was due to superannuate on April 30 , has been given a three-month extension as part of a major top-level bureaucratic reshuffle effected by the central government on Sunday amid the coronavirus crisis . <p> The decision assumes significance as the Health Ministry is at the forefront of checking the spread of COVID-19 in the country . <p> The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet , headed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi , has approved the three-month extension in Sudan 's service , beyond the date of her superannuation , i.e. April 30 , a Personnel Ministry order said . <p> She will continue to function as the Secretary , Department of Health and Family Welfare during the period , it said . <p> Rural Development Secretary Rajesh Bhushan has been appointed as the Officer on Special Duty ( OSD ) in the Health and Family Welfare Department in the rank of secretary . <p> He is likely to take over as the secretary of the department after the expiry of Sudan 's extended tenure of three months . <p> Nagendra Nath Sinha will be the new Secretary of Rural Development Department in place of Bhushan . He is at present Secretary , the Department of Border Management in the Home Ministry . <p> Information and Broadcasting Secretary Ravi Mittal has been moved out as Secretary , Department of Sports , the order said . <p> Amit Khare , the Secretary of the Department of Higher Education , has been given additional charge as the Secretary , Ministry of Information &amp; Broadcasting . <p> Khare has earlier served in the I&amp;B Ministry . <p> In a surprising change , Consumer Affairs Secretary Pawan Kumar Agarwal has been appointed as Special Secretary ( Logistics ) in the Department of Commerce . <p> Leena Nandan , the Special Secretary in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways will be the Consumer Affairs Secretary in place of Agarwal . <p> Food and Public Distribution Secretary Ravi Kant has been moved out as the Secretary , Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare . <p> Senior bureaucrat Sudhanshu Panday will be the Food and Public Distribution Secretary in place of Kant . Panday is currently Additional Secretary , Department of Commerce . <p> Road Transport and Highways Secretary Sanjeev Ranjan has been moved out as the Shipping Secretary upon the superannuation of incumbent Gopal Krishna on April 30 . <p> Aramane Giridhar , the Additional Secretary in the Cabinet Secretariat will be the Secretary in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways in place of Ranjan . <p> CBSE chairperson Anita Karwal has been appointed as the Secretary , Department of Education and Literacy . She is a 1988-batch IAS officer of Gujarat cadre . <p> Her colleague from the same cadre , Rameshwar Prasad Gupta has been appointed as the Secretary in the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . Gupta is currently Special Secretary , NITI Aayog . <p> Pradip Kumar Tripathi , the Special Secretary and Establishment Officer in the Department of Personnel and Training , will be the Secretary , the Ministry of Steel . He is a 1987-batch IAS officer of Jammu and Kashmir cadre . <p> Tarun Kapoor , the Vice Chairman of Delhi Development Authority ( DDA ) , has been appointed as the Secretary of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas in place of M M Kutty , who superannuates this month-end . <p> Rajesh Verma , Special Secretary in the Department of Agriculture will be the new Secretary , Ministry of Corporate Affairs . <p> Anand Kumar , a 1984-batch IAS officer of Kerala cadre , will be new Culture Secretary . He is at present Secretary in the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy . <p> Indu Shekhar Chaturvedi will succeed him . <p> Arvind Kumar Sharma , Additional Secretary in the Prime Minister 's Office will be the Secretary in the Ministry of Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises upon superannuation of incumbent Arun Kumar Panda on April 30 . <p> Ram Mohan Mishra has been appointed as the Secretary in the National Commission for Scheduled Castes . He is currently Special Secretary and Development Commissioner in the Ministry of Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises . <p> Rajesh Kumar Chaturvedi who is currently Special Secretary &amp; Financial Advisor in the Culture Ministry , will now be the Secretary in the Department of Chemicals and Petrochemicals . <p> Shri Ajay Tirkey , will be the Secretary in the Ministry of Women and Child Development , while Niten Chandra has been appointed as the Additional Secretary , Central Agency Section , Department of Legal Affairs . <p> Chandra is at present Secretary , Central Information Commission ( CIC ) where he will be succeeded by Satbir Bedi , the chairperson of National Council for Teacher Education .
