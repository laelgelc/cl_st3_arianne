<p> Cry over saving the pristine glory of Dibang valley in Arunachal Pradesh is getting louder with every passing day . <p> On Wednesday , nature conservationists sent a petition to the Environment ministry to reject the 3,097 megawatt Etalin hydropower project in the valley . <p> They were of the view that the hydropower project will entail the diversion of luxuriant forest land , cutting down lakhs of trees and pose a threat to the region 's rich biodiversity . <p> According to reports , Conservation India , a non-profit website on wildlife and nature conservation , sent a memorandum with 4,305 signatures to the Ministry , asking it to deny environmental clearance for the project . <p> The signatures were received only in the past three days , the portal claimed . <p> Wildlife biologists and scientists including Nandini Velho ; Umesh Sreenivasan , assistant professor at the Indian Institute of Science , Bangalore ; Aparajita Datta , senior scientist at the Nature Conservation Foundation ( NCF ) ; Anindya Sinha , a professor at the National Institute of Advanced Studies , and others wrote at least four letters last week to the ministry 's forest advisory committee ( FAC ) flagging massive biodiversity loss if large hydropower projects were allowed to go ahead in Dibang Valley . <p> FAC has considered granting forest clearance to the Etalin hydropower project at a meeting last Thursday . <p> Hoever , the minutes of the meeting are yet to be released . <p> The project will involve diversion of 1,150.08 hectres of forest land and clearing of 2.7 lakh trees in subtropical , evergreen and broad-leaved rainforests . <p> Dibang Valley is home to a genetically distinct population of tigers , over 75 species of other mammals , and 300+ species of birds , including many endangered ones , Conservation India said in its memorandum . <p> The valley is part of the Eastern Himalayan Global Biodiversity Hotspot , which is one of only 36 such hotspots in the world . In addition to tigers , the greater region of Dibang Valley harbours mammals including the clouded leopard , Asiatic golden cat , Asiatic wild dog , red panda , Mishmi Takin , red goral , and Gongshan Muntjac . <p> Rapid bird assessments in the region have found endangered species such as the Blyth 's tragopan , Sclater 's monal , rufous-necked hornbill , Ward 's trogon , Hodgson 's frogmouth , beautiful nuthatch , wedge-billed babbler and the recently discovered Mishmi wren-babbler . <p> " There is consensus amongst scientists that the rise in zoonotic diseases ( such as Covid 19 ) and acceleration of climate change is linked to biodiversity and forest destruction , " the memorandum said , adding that the proposal should be scrapped . <p> According to FAC 's own factsheet , the project falls under the richest biogeographical province of the Himalayan zone and one of the mega biodiversity hotspots of the world . <p> The proposed project location falls at the junction of the Paleoarctic , Indo-Chinese , and Indo-Malayan biogeographic regions with luxuriant forests and rich flora and fauna . <p> It is also a vital tiger area . <p> The project is proposed to be developed by Etalin Hydro Electric Power Company Limited , a joint venture of Hydro Power Development Corporation of Arunachal Pradesh Limited and Jindal Power Limited ( JPL ) . <p> The total cost of the project is about Rs 25,296.95 crore , according to the factsheet . <p> Northeast Now is a multi-app based hyper-regional bilingual news portal .
