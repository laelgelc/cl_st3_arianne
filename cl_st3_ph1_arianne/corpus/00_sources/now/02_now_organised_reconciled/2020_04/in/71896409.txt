<h> Modi govt effects big bureaucratic rejig <p> In a major bureaucratic reshuffle effected on Sunday , Health Secretary Preeti Sudan was given a three-month extension to maintain continuity in the government 's coronavirus fight , while Higher Education Secretary Amit Khare was given additional charge of the I&amp;B Ministry . <p> The Appointments Committee of the Cabinet headed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi approved Sudan 's extension beyond the date of her superannuation on April 30 , according to a Personnel Ministry order . <p> Tarun Bajaj , Additional Secretary in the PMO , will be the new Secretary in the Economic Affairs Department . Higher Education Secretary Khare was given additional charge of the Information and Broadcasting Ministry , while incumbent Ravi Mittal was shifted to the Department of Sports . <p> Government appointed Rural Development Secretary Rajesh Bhushan as the Officer on Special Duty ( OSD ) in the Health and Family Welfare Department , indicating he may take over from Sudan after her extended tenure ends . The period as OSD will help Bhushan understand the work of the ministry , which has become central to the country 's fight against Covid-19 . As many as 23 senior IAS officers have been appointed as secretaries in different central government departments as part of the rejig . <p> Nagendra Nath Sinha will be the new Secretary of the Rural Development Department in place of Bhushan . He is at present Secretary in the Department of Border Management in the Home Ministry . Another additional secretary in the PMO , Arvind Kumar Sharma , has been appointed as Secretary in the Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises Ministry . In a surprising change , Consumer Affairs Secretary Pawan Kumar Agarwal has been appointed as Special Secretary ( Logistics ) in the Department of Commerce . He has been succeeded by Leena Nandan , a Special Secretary in the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways . <p> Food and Public Distribution Secretary Ravi Kant has been shifted out as the Secretary , in the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare and has been replaced by Sudhanshu Panday , currently Additional Secretary in the Department of Commerce . <p> Road Transport and Highways Secretary Sanjeev Ranjan has been shifted as the Shipping Secretary . Aramane Giridhar , Additional Secretary in the Cabinet Secretariat will replace Ranjan . <p> CBSE chairperson Anita Karwal has been appointed as the Secretary in the Department of Education and Literacy . She is a 1988-batch IAS officer of Gujarat cadre . Her colleague from the same cadre , Rameshwar Prasad Gupta has been appointed as the Secretary in the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . Gupta is currently Special Secretary , NITI Aayog . <p> Pradip Kumar Tripathi , Special Secretary and Establishment Officer in the Department of Personnel and Training , will be Secretary , Ministry of Steel . He is a 1987-batch IAS officer of Jammu and Kashmir cadre . <p> Delhi Development Authority ( DDA ) Vice Chairman Tarun Kapoor has been appointed as the Secretary in the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas in place of M M Kutty . Rajesh Verma , the Special Secretary in the Department of Agriculture , will be the new Secretary in the Ministry of Corporate Affairs . Anand Kumarwill be the new Culture Secretary . A total of 10 IAS officers have been given in-situ upgradation to the level of Special Secretary in the rank and pay of Secretary . These Bidyut Bihari Swain , Apurva Chandra , Jyoti Arora , B. Anand , Neel Kamal Darbari , Rajiv Bansal , Ali Raza Rizvi , Praveen Kr . Srivastava , Arvind Singh and Indevar Pandey .
