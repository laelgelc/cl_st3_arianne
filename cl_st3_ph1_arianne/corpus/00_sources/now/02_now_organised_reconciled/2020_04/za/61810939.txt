He started the future farming campaign in his community , but has had to put the project on hold owing to the national coronavirus lockdown . <p> He believes future farming is the way forward in terms of climate change and other factors . He also believes it can help to address the food shortage in South Africa . <p> Future farming is a smarter way of farming and is believed to be faster and more efficient than conventional farming , using methods such as aeroponics and hydroponics . <p> Redaa says he started farming as a solution to problems the country was facing at the time . <h> Other All4Women readers liked ... <h> WIN a R 2,000 Woolworths Voucher <p> Subscribe to our Free Daily All4Women Newsletter to enter <p> " I am the community forum chairperson and I was the safety forum chairman , and during this time I realised the problems we were facing , with how the water and electricity crisis was affecting the farming in the area . <p> " I was looking for solutions to this problem and came across the best solution : future farming . It works and it 's the way forward , " Redaa says . <p> " I planted these vegetables and it took about a month to grow . With aeroponics , a nutrient-rich mist is used to spray and nourish the crops , while with hydroponics the plants or crops are fed with their own nutrients . <p> " We want to implement future farming to pre-empt the situation in the world and ensure food security for people . <p> " Future farming will not just be a method of quick growth and a quick turnaround time , but to create jobs and long-term farming for any condition we may find ourselves in , " Redaa says . <p> Redaa has also joined forces with Brian Keets , who is currently leasing land in the Schaapkraal area , to open it up to the community , especially the youth , as a safe space for learning and skills development . <p> Brian says he hopes the area they have designated for this programme becomes a safe haven for children . <p> " The idea is that the 400 to 500 children living in the nearby informal settlement will have a programme here to upskill them . <p> " Many of the children here are at school but lack the essential items for school , such as uniforms or stationery . The other almost 40% do not attend school at all . <p> " I am also the chairperson of the NGO Assist Trust and , partnering with many different NGOs , we 're going to start reading , computer , farming and other programmes on this property as soon as the lockdown ends . <p> " We want to ensure that this 400-square-metre property is used not only as a safe space for children to study , but also as a getaway and safe space , " Brian says . <p> All food grown in the future farming programme will be sold to businesses at market value , while the profit will go towards a feeding scheme for children from the surrounding informal settlements . <p> Redaa says the only thing lacking is government funding . <p> " The president spoke about the importance of food security , but now we need government on board . <p> " It is in progress , but the current lockdown has restricted us in many areas . <p> " Future farming is something that should be looked into . Part of our strategy is to also assist others to duplicate this set-up , and with our expertise and final end-products we will be able to help others overcome many obstacles in a shorter period of time than what we 're going through . <p> " If we could get hundreds of these farms up and running , we could create thousands of jobs . It might not be as big as the commercial farms , but rather have 1,000 small farmers together as one that produces much more than a commercial farm produces , not only jobs but also produce , " he says . <p> Schaapkraal forms part of the Philippi Horticulture Area ( PHA ) farmlands , which provide about 30,000 jobs to most locals in the area and its surroundings .
