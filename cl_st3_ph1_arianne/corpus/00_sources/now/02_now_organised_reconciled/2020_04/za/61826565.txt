<h> ANALYSIS <h> South Africa 's 24-hour trend report -- 26 April 2020 <p> By Centre for Analytics and Behavioural Change* 27 April 2020 <p> A group of doctors participate in an event before leaving to South Africa at Jose Marti International Airport in Havana , Cuba , 25 April 2020 . A group of 216 Cuban health workers left Cuba to go to South Africa and help in the fight against COVID-19 , following a request for aid . ( Photo : EPA-EFE/ERNESTO MASTRASCUSA ) <h> This is a summary of the trending , highest impact , and most active themes and narratives related to social cohesion in South African public-domain social media conversations on 26 April 2020 . <p> The arrival of Cuban doctors to assist South Africa was the top story by volume , trending with a burst of 98% . <p> The second biggest trending topic was the project by the Lamiez Holworthy and Khuli Chana Foundations . <p> Cuban doctors became the top trending topic for the day after @JacksonMthembu tweeted a picture of uniformed Cuban doctors with their national flag on their way to South Africa . The post was retweeted over 1,000 times and about 1,500 people talked about the doctors yesterday . <p> Responders showed mixed emotions . Some asked how much the Cubans would be paid , while others explained SA would need more hands when restrictions are eased . South Africans seem divided : " We do n't need them now " vs " We need to be prepared for when this gets worse " are the main sentiments of opposing camps . <p> In a stunning display of solidarity , Switzerland has projected the national flags of several countries , including South Africa , onto the Matterhorn . Conversation peaked at 08:00 as people shared the image of the SA flag on the mountain . Sentiment toward topics like Swiss Alps , Matterhorn and solidarity with South Africans was very positive . <p> Between 10:00 and 15:00 there was a spike in conversation about an outreach by Lamiez Holworthy and Khuli Chana , who tweeted pictures of themselves distributing essentials to needy families . <p> Photos of the masked performers were retweeted over 1,000 times , with the hashtag #planetofthehavenots . <p> Responses included " God bless you " ; " are they a couple " ; " I love your boots " and " Do we really have to know ? " Most people reacted with big ups and gratitude for the outreach . <p> Lockdown levels <p> Conversations about the new industry classifications and the Level 4 lockdown regulations permeated South African social media over the weekend . <p> The EFF 's Mbuyiseni Ndlozi , 23 July 2019 . Photo : Chanel Retief <p> @MbuyesiniNdlozi 's post questioning the easing of restrictions was the top post by impact and reach . <p> The SAfm post regarding Ethekwini Metro was the fourth highest post by reach , and the eighth by impact . <p> Industry Classifications trended as the second biggest topic by volume on Twitter after Ministers Nkosazana Dlamini Zuma and Ebrahim Patel released the classification of industries as part of the risk-adjusted strategy to reopen the economy . <p> Most lockdown rules would remain in place . People leaving home must wear cloth masks , curfews would be imposed and social gatherings remained banned . <p> Dlamini Zuma 's address received significant backlash on Twitter for being in English and Zulu but not in other South African languages . Others said her speech was confusing and difficult to follow , with many requesting infographics . <p> " Level 4 " trended as the fifth biggest topic by volume as South Africans debated its rollout . <p> The DA supported a phased lifting of lockdown restrictions . The EFF strongly advised against the premature lifting of restrictions , saying it would put South African lives at risk . <p> The EFF 's Mbuyiseni Ndlozi : " Guys we are under lockdown , and the curve is on the rise ... what is going to happen when we end or relax the lockdown ? The average number of new infections this week is above 150 . Relaxing the lockdown is going to achieve only 1 thing : tripling the infection ! We are regressing ! " The tweet was widely acknowledged and shared . <p> Others supported news that Ethekwini Metro could remain in Level 5 longer because of the high number of infections . <p> Life in lockdown <p> ' Love ' and ' Life ' were the fourth and sixth top trending words on Sunday . <p> The post by @pamelamtanga asking , " What have you learnt about yourself " was the third biggest post by reach . <p> Comedic posts by @JuksAfrika and @ThulaniTeeTee were fifth by reach . <p> The lockdown has given many a chance to pause and reflect on life and love . <p> One person said she loved the lockdown because her father was sober and no longer beat her mother . More than 1,300 people liked the tweet and over 400 talked about it . <p> Another tweeted : " We fell in love over texts during the lockdown " , pointing to the ways young people are trying to search for love in the isolation of lockdown . <p> @ammaarah05 tweeted about how her friends helped to celebrate her birthday with an online scavenger hunt and a Zoom party . <p> These stories of hope reflect the resilience of the South African spirit , as people strive to find happiness in the darkest moments . <p> @MyLifeAsSne tweeted that online learning during lockdown was cruel . The 22-year-old 's post was retweeted about 400 times , reflecting how many students are struggling without the familiarity of attending classes with classmates . <p> In an article in The Krugersdorp News and other publications , Dr Jaco Putter explained that humans do n't like the feeling of being out of control , and this feeling can become unbearable right now . <p> @Kenziekay 's tweet that she never wanted to experience a pandemic like this again was retweeted over 18,000 times . <p> South Africans are not only coming to terms with the lockdown , but are embracing it with a stream of jokes , memes and comedic content on Twitter . <p> @TheyloveNdiswa lightened the mood with a little coronavirus romance : " You are my sanitiser , the quarantine of my heart . My one and only covid , when you are not there , I feel like I 'm in lockdown . The mask of my life , you will always be my virus , you know why ? Cause my love for you keeps on spreading . " <p> @JuksAfrika thought he would conquer 2020 , but 2020 has caught him with his pants down . His pictures had Twitter in stitches . <p> Organic ' remedy ' <p> President of the Republic of Madagascar Andry Rajoelina ( C ) attends his swearing-in ceremony in Antananarivo , Madagascar , 19 January 2019 . EPA-EFE/HENITSOA RAFALIA <p> Several African leaders , most recently the President of Guinea Bissau Umaro Embalo , are supporting an African organic " remedy " launched by the President of Madagascar , Andry Rajoelina . <p> Rajoelina thanked Embalo for his offer to supply his country and neighbours in West Africa with TAMBAVY CVO/Covid-Organics . <p> Twitter users have had an extremely positive reaction to the news of African solidarity . <p> @RebonaPico states , " Covid-Organics is derived from Artemisia , an indigenous plant widely known to us as Umhlonyane in Zulu and Lengana in Sotho and Tswana . " <p> Mosque arrests <p> A few men in Pretoria disregarded lockdown regulations and met for Friday prayers as Muslims headed into Ramadan . <p> A video of SAPS forcing the praying men to lie on the floor during the raid has gone viral . A video posted by @Abramjee has gone viral with more than 600,000 views and 8,000 people talking about the post . <p> Conversation around the mosque arrests continued throughout the weekend . The narrative of abuse of power by SAPS resurfaced. @MphahleleRidge tweeted that the police must not behave like criminals to enforce the law . <p> The Muslim Judicial Council said they understood the need for the arrests , but were appalled by the heavy-handed way the police responded to the men , who were not resisting arrest .
