<h> News feed <h> Great Barrier Reef suffers worst-ever coral bleaching : scientists <p> Australia 's Great Barrier Reef has suffered its most widespread coral bleaching on record , scientists said Tuesday in a dire warning about the threat posed by climate change to the world 's largest living organism . <p> James Cook University professor Terry Hughes said a comprehensive survey last month found record sea temperatures had caused the third mass bleaching of the 2,300-kilometre ( 1,400-mile ) reef system in just five years . <p> Bleaching occurs when healthy corals become stressed by changes in ocean temperatures , causing them to expel algae living in their tissues which drains them of their vibrant colours . <p> " We surveyed 1,036 reefs from the air during the last two weeks in March to measure the extent and severity of coral bleaching throughout the Barrier Reef region , " Hughes said . <p> " For the first time , severe bleaching has struck all three regions of the Great Barrier Reef -- - the northern , central and now large parts of the southern sectors . " <p> The damage came as February brought the highest monthly sea temperatures on the Great Barrier Reef since Australia began keeping records in 1900 . <p> The reef is worth an estimated $4 billion a year in tourism revenue for the Australian economy , but is at risk of losing its coveted world heritage status because warmer oceans brought about by climate change have damaged its health . <p> Back-to-back bleaching events in 2016 and 2017 prompted the government agency overseeing the reef to downgrade its long-term outlook to " very poor " .
