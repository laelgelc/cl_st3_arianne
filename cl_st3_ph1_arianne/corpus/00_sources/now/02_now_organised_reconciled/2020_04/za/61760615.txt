<h> Political Analysis South Africa <h> Should all tycoons be obliged to contribute towards charitable causes ? <h> Should all tycoons be obliged to contribute towards charitable causes ? <p> Published on <p> 31 March , 2020 <h> Business tycoon and founder of Amazon , Jeff Bezos , has donated ten billion dollars towards fighting climate change , which makes me wonder if it is a reputational obligation for billionaires to do such grandeur gestures . <p> News broke today , 18 February 2020 , that billionaire , Jeff Bezos , has donated ten billion dollars to help fight climate change . The magnificent philanthropic gesture is one of the many donations Jeff has made in recent years , which has positively contributed towards various causes . I have always wondered if the charitable contributions made by Jeff and other billionaires have helped promote their businesses , subsequently leading to them maintaining their financial status , or if it is in fact their genuine and heartfelt efforts to contribute towards causes close to their hearts . <p> Business tycoons , such as Bill Gates and Mark Zuckerberg , are some of the other billionaires who have been known to contribute their billions to charitable organisations . This has positively affected their reputations , as the public feels more inclined to support their businesses , ultimately generating more income for themselves and helping them maintain their financial status . <p> On the other hand , what about media billionaires ? Kylie Jenner , founder of Kylie Cosmetics and Kylie Skin , is one of the youngest billionaires in the world . Rarely does anyone hear of Kylie contributing large amounts of money to charities , on the contrary , we often see her spending exorbitant amounts of money on parties , car purchases and houses . <p> Does this type of spending affect businesses as a whole , and are businesses more profitable if they are affiliated with philanthropic donations ? Only time will tell .
