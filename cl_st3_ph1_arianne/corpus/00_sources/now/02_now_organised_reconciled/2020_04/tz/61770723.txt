<h> The 1970 rainfall records were broken in 2019 says TMA <h> Thursday April 9 2020 <h> In Summary <p> TMA director general Ms Agnes Kijazi said since 1970 , the country had not recorded such a high amount of rainfall like what was recorded in 2019 . <p> By Hellen Nachilongo @musanachi60 **30;46216;TOOLONG <p> Dar es Salaam . The Tanzania Meteorological Authority ( TMA ) has said 2019 received the highest rainfall ever since 1970 . <p> The statement was made at the official launch of the status of Tanzania climate for 2019 . <p> TMA director general Ms Agnes Kijazi said since 1970 , the country had not recorded such a high amount of rainfall like what was recorded in 2019 . <p> " The country 's annual mean was 1283.5mm , which is equivalent to 125 per cent of the long term ( 1981-2010 ) average and higher than long term mean rainfall by 256.5mm . October to December 2019 rainy season received above normal rainfall and is the second wettest on record since 1970 and October 2019 was the wettest October ever since 1970 , " she said . <p> Moreover , the report highlighted an increase in temperature during the night and more warming during daytime over the past two decades since 1995 . <p> The country annual mean temperature was 23.8 ? C which is 0.8 ? C warmer than long-term average . This warming in 2019 is ranked the fourth highest on record since 1970 . <p> Ms Kijazi said March and April 2019 were the warmest months of the year with country mean temperature higher than long term average by 1.1 ? C and 1.6 ? C respectively . <p> On the case of extreme rainfall events , she said since 1970 there were thirteen ( 13 ) events of above normal rainfall events with 2003 being the driest year . <p> She urged stakeholders from various sectors to act upon and use the information available in the report for socio-economic benefits of various sectors such as agriculture , construction and industrialization . <p> Earlier while giving general overview of the occasion , Dr. Ladislaus Chang'a , Director of Research and Applied Meteorology said the statement on the status of Tanzania climate provides information on the current status of climate , extreme climatic events and their associated impacts with the goal of enhancing awareness and understanding of climate variability and climate change among stakeholders . <p> " The current statement provides analysis of weather and climate events that occurred over different parts of the country by placing them into historical perspectives , " he said .
