<p> The United Nations World Food Programme ( WFP ) has donated two mobile storage units to support Bhutan 's Covid-19 preparedness and response . The units , worth a total of US$45,000 , will be used to help authorities store food to meet the needs of half the tiny Himalayan nation 's population of 772,000 for three months . Although Bhutan has reported only five coronavirus cases and no deaths , there are fears it faces an imminent emergency due to the pandemic . " The government has taken a proactive approach to address the emergency , which is putting increased pressure on the country 's health system as well as food security , " said Svante Helms , head of WFP Bhutan . " WFP is pleased to extend our support at this critical time to protect the population , especially the most vulnerable , against potential shocks such as decreased access to affordable and nutritious food amid the growing unemployment rate and lost/reduced wages . " <p> WFP 's technical assistance package for Bhutan includes regional food price monitoring , positioning of food , targeting food to the most vulnerable , establishing a National Food Security Reserve and ensuring food safety and quality WFP Bhutan is also preparing a nutrition technical assistance package for schoolchildren to ensure their nutritional needs are met and is in discussions with Bhutan 's agriculture ministry to explore support of farmer organizations , production and market linkages . The WFP is the world 's largest humanitarian organization , saving lives in emergencies , building prosperity and supporting a sustainable future for people recovering from conflict , disasters and the impact of climate change . The Buddhist kingdom of Bhutan on March 28 confirmed its first two cases of Covid-19 detected in Bhutanese nationals -- both students in capital Thimphu who had recently returned from studying overseas . Its first confirmed infection was a 76-year-old American tourist on March 5 . He was evacuated to the United States by air ambulance . The Bhutanese government has arranged to house all overseas arrivals at a designated quarantine facility . The health ministry has also called back to Bhutan doctors studying overseas to bolster the country 's ranks of 3,000 healthcare staff , while 600 volunteers have been trained to carry out health screening , with plans to train 5,000 more .
