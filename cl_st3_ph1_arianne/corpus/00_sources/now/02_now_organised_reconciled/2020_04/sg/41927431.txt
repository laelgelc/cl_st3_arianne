<h> 2nd International Conference on Urban Agriculture and City Sustainability ( Urban Agriculture 2020 ) <h> Share <h> Date <h> Location <h> More information <h> Event Type <h> Event Industry/Topic <p> Following the success of the first conference in the series which took place in the New Forest , UK ( 2018 ) , the conference has been reconvened in 2020 . The aim of the meeting is to review and discuss ways in which urban agriculture can contribute to achieving sustainable cities . <p> As urban populations continue to increase it is essential to consider ways of reducing their impact in terms of use of natural resources , waste production and climate change . <p> The increasing number of people in cities requires new strategies to supply the necessary food with limited provision of land and decreasing resources . This will become more challenging unless innovative solutions for growing and distributing food in urban environments are considered . <p> The scale of modern food production has created and exacerbated many vulnerabilities and the feeding of cities is now infinitely more complex . As such , the food system can not be considered secure , ethical or sustainable . <p> In the last few years , there has been a rapid expansion in initiatives and projects exploring innovative methods and processes for sustainable food production . <p> The majority of these projects are focused on providing alternative models that shift the power back from the global food system to communities and farmers improving social cohesion , health and wellbeing . It is therefore not surprising that more people are looking towards urban farming initiatives as a potential solution . <p> These initiatives have demonstrated that urban agriculture has the potential to transform our living environment towards ecologically sustainable and healthy cities . Urban agriculture can also contribute to energy , natural resources , land and water savings , ecological diversity and urban management cost reductions . <p> The impact urban agriculture can have on the shape and form of our cities has never been fully addressed . How cities embed these new approaches and initiatives , as part of new urban developments and a city regeneration strategy is critical . <p> The Urban Agriculture conference will address these challenges and search for new solutions .
