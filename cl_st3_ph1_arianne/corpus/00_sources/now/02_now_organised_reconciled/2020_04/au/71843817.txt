<h> Council signs climate change declaration in response to teens ' calls for action <h> Topics <p> A GROUP of teenagers took their concerns about climate change to council chambers , appealing for the City of Wanneroo to declare a climate emergency . <p> Butler College students Aiden Campbell , Lily Fox and Chloe Rooker gave passionate deputations about their environmental concerns at the March council meeting . <p> The 16-year-olds , supported by several of their peers in the public gallery , appealed to councillors to make the declaration when it considered a report on Aiden 's petition , submitted last year . <p> Aiden focused his deputation on recent events , including the Amazon rain forest fires and last summer 's Australian bushfires , as well as rising temperatures , reduced rainfall and rising sea levels . <p> " We are currently living in a climate crisis and denying this state of emergency is to be insupportable , " he said . <p> " This administration needs to reassure the youth of this City that you 'll be passing down to us a future that is safe , secure and stable with climate change not proposing any major threats to these factors of wellbeing . " <p> Chloe made a plea for decision makers to take young people 's concerns seriously and to take immediate action . <p> Lily asked them to review priorities so future generations could enjoy the beauty of Australia . <p> Councillors moved to strengthen the City 's commitment to combat climate change , voting to become a signatory of the WA Local Government Association ( WALGA ) Climate Change Declaration . <p> Mayor Tracey Roberts said the move demonstrated an understanding and acknowledgement of the impacts of climate change , and clarified the City 's stance on the issue . <p> " This decision underscores council 's support for appropriate mitigation and adaptation actions at a local government level , " she said . <p> " The City 's approach to addressing climate change covers a wide range of actions , including but not limited to protecting local biodiversity , extensive coastal erosion management works , water and energy reduction , shaping a sustainable transport future and community education on waste reduction and recycling . <p> " The City has a proven track record in this area , and by aligning itself more closely with WALGA policy on the matter , Wanneroo Council will be able to better advocate for and influence action at state and federal levels . " <p> The council also agreed to develop a youth climate forum for residents to provide feedback and have input into the City 's commitments . <p> Quinns Rocks Environmental Group spokesman David Wake said it was great to see young people taking the initiative and raising the issue of climate change . <p> " It 's obvious to all of us and it 's time that we took action , " he said . <p> Mr Wake said according to the UN , people needed to significantly reduce emissions by 2030 and eliminate them by 2050 . <p> He said the WALGA statement " does n't go far enough " and " could be more forthright " . <p> Councillor Frank Cvitan referred to the impact of the coronavirus on humanity and the environment , particularly in China . <p> " Everything shut down -- within a week , pollution had dissipated , " he said . <p> Councillors praised the youngsters for their passion and courage to show up , stand up and speak out at the council meeting . <p> Camera IconYanchep bushfire emergency . " <p> Camera IconStock image . Credit : Supplied/Getty Images/iStockphoto <p> Young people plea to be taken seriously <p> Chloe asked whether the pleas and protests of young people were not being taken seriously because they were seen as " young , impressionable or immature " . <p> " We are in the midst of a climate emergency , and we need to take action now , " she said . <p> " We need to come together as a council and community and work actively to prevent the roaring effects of climate change and stop the growing threat it has on all of our lives before it 's too late . <p> " As a resident of the City of Wanneroo , myself and many others , have recognised the damage and growing threat that our environment has faced and continues to face every single day as a result of climate change due to our careless past actions . <p> " We are in a state of crisis , with very little time to reverse the damage done . " <p> She referred to climate projections from the Department of Primary Industries and Regional Development website , including that average annual temperature increases and the likelihood of more days in Perth with temperatures over 35 degrees Celsius . <p> " Our country is beautiful and the local councils and communities work hard in keeping Australia a safe and economically efficient place , " she said . <p> " However , there is an aspect of our region , our home that is being overlooked on both a local and national scale , that aspect is our environment . <p> " We need to think about our environment like it 's a house -- we do n't litter and ignore and trash our homes ; we nurture and clean and look after our homes . <p> " We should encourage and educate the community about putting rubbish in the correct bins and also display what would happen if this is n't followed through . <p> " We should show how rubbish pollution in our oceans and streets have negative impacts on our environment and surrounding wildlife and this could be shown through more local advertising . " <p> " Australia has amazing landscapes that make up our special environment and country , with features that are unique to Australia only , " she said . <p> " I refuse to deny this state of beauty to future generations . <p> " We are right now in the beginning of a climate and ecological crisis . <p> " Collectively , we must acknowledge that the situation is not under control and that we do n't have all the solutions yet . <p> " We need to create more awareness of this climate crisis , in order to secure my kids , your kids , nieces , nephews : the future generations chance to experience this wonderful place we call home . <p> " My favourite beach -- where I learned to swim -- Quinns , has collapsed multiple times and sand bags had to be put in place . <p> " However , this has n't resolved the problem -- the right side of the beach still suffers from ( erosion ) and has now formed more rips that are unsafe . <p> " What 's more disturbing is the purposeful disregard people make when littering at and around our beaches . <p> " Last year when having a quick swim at my local beach in Eden beach Jindalee , I discovered 27-plus unused McDonalds straws which were deliberately buried in the sand , and with more scattered across the shore . <p> " Where would those straws have ended up if I had n't found them ? " <p> Lily said she had been involved in the bushrangers cadet program doing coastal cleanups , planting trees and weeding , and called on the City to increase funding for the program . <p> " City of Wanneroo , I am asking that you declare a climate emergency to raise awareness , put stronger enforcements on those who litter and to take action on this emergency , " she said .
