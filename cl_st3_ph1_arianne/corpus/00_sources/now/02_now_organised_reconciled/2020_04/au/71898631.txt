<h> The complexity of Mother 's Day when you 're uncertain about having children <p> Big hugs to all the mums out there as your special day approaches -- and bigger hugs to the women out there who have lost their mums , lost children or desperately wanted to be a mum but circumstances prevented them from having babies . <p> Mother 's Day can be emotionally tricky for some , but I feel if it 's approached with kindness everyone can feel the love . <p> I 'm child-free by choice . It was my decision to not have children , so Mother 's Day is not sad for me ; it 's a day I celebrate my mum and all the mums out there ( especially those home schooling . Seriously , you all deserve medals ! ) . <p> Mother 's Day is interesting for women who have n't ruled out motherhood , putting the decision on pause . ( iStock ) <p> It 's an interesting day for women who have n't ruled out motherhood but have instead put the decision on pause , like Rose Hartley . She 's the author of Maggie 's Going Nowhere , and last year she decided to freeze her eggs . <p> " I 'd just left a marriage . I just wanted to have that extra time -- apparently 34 is the ideal age to freeze your eggs because you 've still got high quality eggs , but it 's not so young that it 'd be a waste of money , " she says . <p> Hartley had tried to fall pregnant in her early thirties , but due to endometriosis was not successful . <p> " I think all the way along when I was trying , every time it failed , I would actually feel just a little bit of relief , and I kept ignoring that feeling . I kept thinking that , ' Oh , I 'm just getting nervous about the responsibility ' or ' I 'm worried about climate change and you know , what kind of world I 'll give to my kids ' , " she says . <p> " When I thought about it more , I realised I 'd never actually considered why I would have children , because it 's just a thing that you do , you know , it 's the thing that 's expected of you . I think most people know that they want them or they have them by accident . But no one teaches you how to make a decision of whether it 's actually the right thing . <p> " For me , there are other things in my life that give my life meaning , writing fiction really gives my life meaning , and so I just do n't feel that there 's something missing . I 'm curious about what it would be like to have kids , but I do n't actually feel any pangs . I do n't have any sore spots about it . " <p> So on Mother 's Day , will Hartley expect that she might be a little bit more reflective on that day or think about her eggs that are sitting in the freezer ? <p> " I might consider my eggs , but honestly , I normally just think about my own mum and my sister-in-law . I do n't really think so much about me , I think because it 's hard for me to imagine being a mother . It 's never really been a life dream of mine . You know , I only tried because it 's what you do , " she says . <p> " It 's hard for me to imagine being a mother . It 's never really been a life dream of mine , " Rose Hartley tells Shelly Horton . ( Supplied ) <p> Personally , I get messages from people saying , " But you have dogs , you are a mum to your fur kids ! " I think that is a lovely sentiment , and I love my dogs to bits , but I would never , ever , put myself in the ' mother ' category . <p> Hartley agrees . " I 'm exactly the same . It 's never crossed my mind , although I do sometimes refer to Doris her cat as my daughter . I 'm well aware that I do nothing for her except feed her and pat her . It is so different . " <p> So let 's not dilute Mother 's Day . Mothers have the hardest job in the world . We know the sacrifices they 've made , so let 's just celebrate them for one incredible day and make sure they are spoiled rotten rather than trying to slice off bits and pieces for different parts of society . <p> " Yeah , I completely agree . I think mothers get such short shrift , you know , they 're judged if they do anything wrong . They 're not appreciated for the things that they do , which is , everything . I 'm just very appreciative of the mothers in my life , " Hartley says .
