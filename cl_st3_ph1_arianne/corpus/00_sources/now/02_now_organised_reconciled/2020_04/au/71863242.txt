It has forced politicians to behave more maturely , to tell the truth and admit that the greater good can not be achieved without sacrifice and pain by some or all , and to co-operate in aiming for the best possible outcome . How petty and puerile the behaviour in question time , the lies and obfuscations , the calculated scare campaigns against worthy policies , and ignoring experts seem now , and how good is placing the welfare of society above that of the individual , or vested interest lobby group ?
