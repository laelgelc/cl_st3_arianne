<h> Head On Photo Festival <p> Explore more than 110 exhibitions from Aussie and international photo-artists . Plus , there 'll be 80 live-streamed artist talks , panel discussions and workshops . ( It 's all online . ) <p> This year Australia 's leading photography festival is turning 11 -- and going completely virtual . It 's been rechristened the Head On(line) Photo Festival . <p> You can explore more than 110 exhibitions and a daily program of 80 online events from May 1 to 17 . Artists from five continents are involved in the festival , which has previously toured America , China , India , Europe and New Zealand . <p> The topics explored include the urgency of climate change , the precious ( but fleeting ) nature of childhood , the beauty of old age , the rise of anti-immigration groups , the devastation of war , identity politics , and the value -- inherent and ascribed -- of the selfie . <p> Highlights include Australian photographer Matt Smith 's Untitled , which uses underwater photography to showcase the diverse marine life from Bondi Beach and the surrounding coastline ; Paper Tigers , an homage to contemporary Australian photojournalism ( featuring 30 of the country 's best ) ; and a powerful series by Pulitzer Prize-winning photojournalist Rene'e C Byer , which captures the faces of people living on one US dollar a day . <p> The winners of Head On 's annual awards , plus more than $70,000 in prizes , will be announced at a free online launch party on May 1 . <p> There 'll also be a diverse program of more than 80 live-streamed artist talks and panel discussions with top international photographers , plus free workshops ( including a street-photography workshop where you 'll use Google Street View ) .
