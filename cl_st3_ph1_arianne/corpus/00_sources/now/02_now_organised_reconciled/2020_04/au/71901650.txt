US-allied powers are also finding it increasingly difficult to reconcile their strategic affinity with the US and their economic ties with China . This is why , in my earlier piece , I argued that the attractiveness of the American-led system wo n't be as decisive as Mazarr hopes . Sheer economic weight will be more important in deciding the question . &lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;None of this is to deny Mazarr 's argument about the importance of legitimacy in building a sustainable order .
