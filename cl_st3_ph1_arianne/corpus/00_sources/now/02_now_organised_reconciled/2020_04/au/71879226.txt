<h> ' There 's a lot more that we can do with diversity ' in games and gaming <p> Comfortably ensconced in a Teneriffe workspace roughly 16,000 kilometres from her hometown , Mel Taylor strives to make gamers emotionally connect with her work . <p> In the latest creation , young Blueberry ages from a girl into a woman before the player 's eyes in a game that deals with growing up , growing old and the trauma and responsibilities of parenting , from a female perspective . <p> Brisbane resident Mel Taylor has taken up residence in the sqhub to create a game called Blueberry . <p> The Mellow Games designer is one of 10 creatives to have taken up residence in Screen Queensland 's working space , sqhub . <p> The new wave of screen industry professionals across games , film , TV and online platforms is using the space to nurture talent and launch businesses and projects . <p> Advertisement <p> The hub , supported by the state government , aims to provide local creatives professional development , industry networks and the business knowledge needed to grow successful screen start-ups in Queensland . <p> Blueberry will be Ms Taylor 's third game , following Orwell , which focuses on online privacy and data surveillance in partnership with Osmotic Studios in Germany . <p> Ms Taylor is using sqhub to launch the new game , having swapped her hometown of Hamburg , Germany , for Brisbane . <p> " The gamer will have to climb a tower , which is a representation of what 's going on in the mind of the main character , Blueberry , who begins as a girl and ages into an old woman , " she said . <p> " The higher you climb , the older you age and you are faced with different scenes in Blueberry 's life . <p> " An example is when a player has to reach the cookie jar in the kitchen or rescues a toy animal in the bathroom . " <p> Blueberry rebels through high school and fights with her mother before becoming a mother herself . <p> " There will also be memories that are hidden that she never wanted to think about for years . " <p> The 34-year-old said she strived to make games that emotionally resonated with players and showed how people dealt with societal and personal issues . <p> " It 's good to be in the hub because there are a lot of people in the screen industry and it 's great to work with them and exchange knowledge , " she said . <p> " I love that it evokes emotions apart from just being fun . " <p> The game will follow a character named Blueberry who begins as a girl and will grow into an old woman , learning life lessons along the way . <p> Ms Taylor is interested in making gamers empathise with her characters . <p> " Historically , games have been very one-sided but I think there 's a lot more that we can do with diversity and add more characters in a game , " she said . <p> " It 's interesting we 're seeing this and messages behind stories . " <p> Other projects to be launched through sqhub include an LGBTI+ web series by <p> 8 Slice Media and Nikki Organ and Bobbi-Lea Dionysiu of Disruptor Studios are using the space to make an LGBTI+ web series while games industry veteran Alex Butterfield has Lifeboat Earth , a game about hope and human agency for climate change . <p> Early-career producer Caden Pearson is creating a documentary in development with NITV and developing a crime web series based on his feature screenplay , Walter 's Ghost . <p> Blueberry is expected to be released next year on Nintendo Switch and Steam .
