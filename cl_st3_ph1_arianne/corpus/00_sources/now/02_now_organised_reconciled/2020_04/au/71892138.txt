<p> In the 11 months since he became Minister for Government Services , Stuart Robert 's most visible contribution has been to attempt to blame a non-existent cyber attack for his failure to prepare the Centrelink website for the demands resulting from the government 's coronavirus response . He recently said that Barnaby Joyce does n't understand the proposed coronavirus app ( " Barnaby does n't grasp virus app : minister " , canberratimes.com.au , April 19 ) . Does Mr Robert understand that the Centrelink website still refuses to upload some documents that applicants must provide ? Does he understand that he could arrange for the Centrelink website to upload those documents , as the Prime Minister 's website already does ? Failing that , does he understand that he could arrange for the Centrelink website to provide information on how to convert documents into formats that the website will accept ? Does Mr Robert understand that he could arrange for the Centrelink website to provide a more informative response than , " Something 's gone wrong . Please try again . " Does the Prime Minister understand that something 's gone wrong with his choice of Minister for Government Services , and that it 's time to try again ? Thanks for the welcome observations about the original plans for Canberra by Walter and Marion Griffin ( " Griffin 's plan pays off during pandemic " , April 19 , p7 ) . The many positive references to their design for low-density planning and green open spaces remind us of the Griffins ' original principles at great risk of being abandoned . The hallmark of Canberra has always been its design as the national capital in the national interest . Not to be re-fashioned as just another high-rise town . That should also have been " Griffins ' Plan " ( plural ) , reminding us it was both Walter and Marion together . The sesquicentenary of Marion Mahony Griffin 's birth will be on 14 February 2021 . It 's cause for celebration here in their celebration of Australia . My Facebook feed keeps showing a sponsored ad from the ACT 's Evoenergy promoting " Gas Rewards " for installing gas equipment . I suggest that readers would be mad to take up the offer for the following reasons . Firstly , the Australian Energy Market Operator 's published predictions show that they do n't know where we will source almost of half of the demand for gas during the winter of 2025 . Secondly , the ACT has stated policy to phase out gas by 2045 and the world has agreed to global warming limits that mean we must cut back quickly on fossil fuels . Thirdly , electric heat pumps use the ACT 's 100 per cent renewable electricity very efficiently to extract ambient heat for space and water heating . Electric heat pump heating is cheaper to operate . Finally , fully electric homes avoid the gas supply charge . Saving several hundred dollars every year is better than the one-off cash-back being offered as an enticement to stay hooked on gas . The federal government says it is committed to lower taxes and aggressive deregulation after COVID-19 . Instead , now with direct experience of the health and social impacts of climate change and pandemic risks , it could consider redistributive fairness for everyone , increased sustained funding of public health and education , and strengthened regulation to mitigate risks . As a cyclist , I give a hearty " Thank you ! " to any pedestrian , and a big wave to any driver , who extends any deliberate ( or accidental ) courtesy to me on the shared paths or the roads . I call it positive reinforcement therapy .
