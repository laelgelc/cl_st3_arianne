<h> The Royals : A creative response to the coronavirus crisis <p> Independent agency The Royals wanted a creative reponse to the corponvirus crisis . <p> So a challenge went to the team : " Creatively respond to the statement ' coronavirus is like ... ' . " <p> The result was put together in the agency 's own publication , Everybody Knows . <p> A brief selection : <p> Belinda Cecchini wrote : " Coronavirus is a reminder that we are all connected . It 's pretty confronting being in lockdown . I miss my friends , family , colleagues , the daily commute , having somewhere to rush to , a sense of purpose . I miss feeling connected to my fellow humans . The iso blues are real , so I 've been meditating -- a lot . " <p> Kitty Turpin : " I do n't know if I have 250 words in me for this , but it 's sure making me think about building a tiny house and GTFO of the city . " <p> Anthea Wright : " Coronavirus is house arrest for climate crime . " <p> Lewis Farrar looked at conspiracy theories , including : <p> The coronavirus is part of an American plot to ruin the Chinese economy . <p> The coronavirus is part of a Chinese plot to ruin America 's economy . <p> Cocaine cures COVIS-19 . <p> Drinking cow urine protects against COVID-19 . <p> Greta Thunberg caused COVID-19 to help with climate change . <p> Andrea Sophocleous : " ... I remember that this is only the beginning . This virus is going to change us forever . I just hope it 's not a superficial , short-lived change . I hope we learn from the ' coronapocalypse ' and change the way we treat each other and the planet . "
