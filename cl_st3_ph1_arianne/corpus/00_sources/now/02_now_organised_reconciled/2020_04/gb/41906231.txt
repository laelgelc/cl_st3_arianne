<h> Chairman Tatters confirms Elgin voted in favour of SPFL proposal <p> Elgin City chairman Graham Tatters confirmed the Black and Whites backed the SPFL 's resolution on ending the Championship , League 1 and League 2 season . <p> City were among 16 clubs from the third and fourth tiers who backed the proposal , which was one more than was required in order for the motion to pass . <p> Three clubs from the bottom two divisions voted against , with one vote still to be received by the league governing body . <p> It remains unclear whether the motion will pass , with only seven of the required eight supporting votes in the Championship having been received . <p> Although two second-tier clubs have rejected the proposal , a further club has yet to make its stance clear to the SPFL board . <p> Although Elgin are third in League 2 at present , and on course to finish in the play-offs should the season be played to a finish , Tatters says a desire to press ahead with a solution was behind the supporting vote from Borough Briggs . <p> Tatters said : " We voted for it . Once they decided there was no way forward in the play-offs , we looked at it from the point of view that we wanted to get something done so we can start preparing for next season . <p> " We were hoping it would go to the play-offs , because we were playing very well at the time . We were one of the form teams . <p> " In the end we just felt that somewhere along the line we had to make a decision because we can not move at the moment . <p> " Being third in the league , and getting the league sorted out so we can all progress , was one way of dealing with it . " <p> Calling an end to the season would allow the early distribution of prize money for clubs , however Tatters insists that was not City 's prime motivation for supporting the motion . <p> Tatters highlighted player contracts as an example of why he supported the campaign being ended . <p> He added : " We are going to get the money anyway , so that was irrelevant in the great scheme of things . <p> " The Rangers resolution failed at the first hurdle , I think that was down to the legal aspect . <p> " One of the main things was to get it all sorted out so we can start progressing . <p> " At the moment , we do n't know where we stand with players ' contracts . <p> " A lot of players are going to be out of contract in June , and no club in their right mind is going to start signing people without knowing what 's going to happen . <p> " It 's going to be a nightmare for any players released in June . It 's OK if you are in furlough can sign another contract , but if you have n't you will be in the deep muck . <p> " I ca n't see many clubs wanting to sign players until they find out what 's going to happen . " <p> Tatters has been part of numerous meetings between League 2 clubs in recent weeks , and he says the concept of league reconstruction has been high on the agenda further up the league setup . <p> He added : " A lot of people wanted the whole question to be tied in with reconstruction , but the SPFL said they would n't do that . They wanted to separate reconstruction from what is going on at the moment . <p> " We have started a League 2 chatroom on WhatsApp . <p> " There has been no animosity or aggravation in our league . When we have had our meetings it has all been amicable . <p> " We are not at the top end of the food chain though . We have a say in it all , the vote still counts , but the League 2 clubs are n't really going to change the world .
