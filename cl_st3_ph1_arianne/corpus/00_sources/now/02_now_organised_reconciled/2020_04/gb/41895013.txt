<h> Starmer pledges to draw upon Christians in the Labour Party <p> The newly elected Labour leader , Sir Keir Starmer , arrives at BBC Broadcasting House on Sunday <p> The newly elected Labour leader , Sir Keir Starmer , arrives at BBC Broadcasting House on Sunday <p> THE new leader of the Labour Party , Sir Keir Starmer , has said that he intends to draw upon the work of Christian activists in his party . <p> In a statement issued on Monday by Christians on the Left , the Labour Party 's Christian affiliate group , Sir Keir , who was elected leader on Saturday , said : " I want to ensure that Christians on the Left 's activism is drawn upon in Labour 's work in opposition . <p> " In particular , Christians on the Left 's recent campaigns on climate change and poverty , ethical banking , tax evasion or arms sales must also be priority issues for Labour to tackle . " <p> During the leadership race , members of Christians on the Left nominated Sir Keir to be leader , and Angela Rayner to be deputy leader ( News , 14 February ) . Sir Keir has appointed Ms Rayner as his deputy . <p> The Christians on the Left statement welcomed Sir Keir 's election as leader . " Labour can now get on with the task of providing a credible Opposition to the government , working with it to help the fight against the coronavirus but fulfilling its constitutional duty to scrutinise and propose alternative ideas , " it said . <p> " Keir expressed strong support for Christians on the Left in his statement to our members . He recognised that Christian Socialism is a fundamental part of the Labour Party 's history . He welcomed our ' Love your CLP ' campaign as an example of the type of culture he wants to encourage in the Party and committed to working with Christians on the Left and other faith groups . We welcome his commitment to root out anti-Semitism in the Party . " <p> On Monday , the Christian MP David Lammy was appointed Shadow Justice Secretary , and the chair of Christians on the Left , Jonathan Reynolds , was appointed Shadow Work and Pensions Secretary . <p> Under the leadership of Jeremy Corbyn , Sir Keir 's predecessor , the Labour Party was strongly criticised for the anti-Semitism shown by some members . In November , the Chief Rabbi , Ephraim Mirvis , wrote in The Times that a " new poison " had taken hold of the party ( News , 29 November 2019 ) . <p> Advertisement <p> On the day that he was elected , Sir Keir wrote to the President of the Board of Deputies of British Jews , Marie van der Zyl , " to reiterate my commitment to stamping out antisemitism within the Party and to extend an invitation to meet me to discuss this further " . <p> Ms van der Zyl said on Saturday : " We were very pleased that he Sir Keir and all the other leadership contenders signed up to the Board of Deputies ' Ten Pledges on anti-Semitism . The new deputy leader Angela Rayner also signed up to these pledges . . . Now they must act to rid the party of the awful disease of anti-Jewish racism . " <p> The chief executive of the pressure group Campaign Against Anti-Semitism , Gideon Falter , said : " As the new leader of the Labour Party , Sir Keir has no time to lose in making good on his pledge seek out anti-Semitism and ' tear out this poison by its roots ' and rebuild relations with the Jewish community . " <p> Sir Keir 's wife , Victoria Alexander , is from a Jewish background , and has family in Tel Aviv . He said that he has attended many events in London synagogues with her family . <p> On Monday , Sir Keir appointed the Christian MP David Lammy as Shadow Justice Secretary .
