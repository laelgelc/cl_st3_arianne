<p> The COP26 climate talks have been postponed until 2021 amid the " unprecedented global challenge " of the coronavirus outbreak , ministers have announced . <p> Business Secretary Alok Sharma , who had been picked by the Government to head up the United Nations summit in Glasgow , confirmed the move as he promised to reschedule the gathering as soon as possible . <p> The COP26 event had been expected to attract 30,000 delegates from around the world , including heads of state , climate experts and campaigners . <p> It was billed as the biggest international summit ever hosted by the UK . <p> But the decision to pause the talks comes amid plans for the event 's venue , the Scottish Events Campus in Glasgow , to be turned into a temporary hospital to treat Covid-19 sufferers . <p> " The world is currently facing an unprecedented global challenge and countries are rightly focusing their efforts on saving lives and fighting Covid-19 , " Mr Sharma said in a statement . <p> " That is why we have decided to reschedule COP26 . <p> " We will continue working tirelessly with our partners to deliver the ambition needed to tackle the climate crisis and I look forward to agreeing a new date for the conference . " <p> UN climate change chief Patricia Espinosa said : " Covid-19 is the most urgent threat facing humanity today , but we can not forget that climate change is the biggest threat facing humanity over the long term . <p> " Soon , economies will restart . This is a chance for nations to recover better , to include the most vulnerable in those plans , and a chance to shape the 21st century economy in ways that are clean , green , healthy , just , safe and more resilient . <p> " In the meantime , we continue to support and to urge nations to significantly boost climate ambition in line with the Paris Agreement . " <p> The COP26 postponement follows a wave of cancellations and delays to major world events , including the 2020 Tokyo Olympics and the Euro 2020 football tournament .
