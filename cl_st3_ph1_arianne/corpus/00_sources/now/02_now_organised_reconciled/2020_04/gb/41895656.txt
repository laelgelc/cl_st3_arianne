<p> Launched on March 30 , the awards recognise people from all walks of life that inspire people with their acts of bravery , determination and selflessness . Categories target contributions throughout the community from good neighbours , volunteers and people integral to our wellbeing in our day to day lives . <p> The event is being headline sponsored by Leicestershire 's Police and Crime Commissioner , with individual categories being sponsored by local businesses from a wealth of sectors . <p> De Montfort University is an institution committed to the public good and last year its students carried out a total of 20,465 hours of volunteering throughout Leicester , making the university , which this celebrates its 150th anniversary , the ideal sponsor for the Eco Warrior category . <p> Last year , De Montfort students worked on a total of 101 projects , including volunteering at food banks , holiday food projects to feed school children during the summer , pop-up cafes to alleviate social isolation among the elderly , Saturday art clubs for children and community cinema events across the city . <h> LeicestershireLive Heroes 2020 Award categories : <p> Good Neighbour <p> Healthcare Hero <p> Volunteer of the Year <p> Community Action <p> Bravery and Courage <p> Young Heroes <p> Emergency Services <p> Lifetime Achievement <p> Hero of Leicestershire 2020 <p> Eco Warrior <p> A spokesman for the university said : " Students also volunteered across the world as the university encourages everyone to become global citizens , as well as working for the public good in Leicester . This has seen students working with refugees across a series of European cities , teaching English to children in Thailand and carrying out work on many projects in India . <p> " All of this work has seen the university build up a relationship with the United Nations and DMU is now the global academic hub for one of the Sustainable Development Goals -- SDG 16 to promote peace , justice and strong institutions . It is the only university to be granted such an honour in the United Kingdom . " <p> De Montfort University Leicester ( DMU ) responded with gusto to the Winter Warmer appeal run all month by the Students Union and the #DMUlocal team <p> The spokesman added : " DMU may be the world leader for SDG 16 but is committed to working towards all the goals and is supporting the city 's declaration of a climate change emergency , which has also been signed by Leicester City Council , DMU , the University of Leicester and the students ' unions at both institutions . <p> " That is why DMU is delighted to be sponsoring the Eco Warrior award , which recognises environmental action and learning and the unsung heroes who have gone the extra mile . " <p> After the nomination closing date on Wednesday , July 22 , three finalists will be shortlisted within each category by a judging panel . <p> The winners will then be announced at the awards , which takes place on Tuesday , September 8 , 2020 , at the King Power Stadium .
