Because football is the most popular sport on earth , thus in the forefront of a lot of minds . Also , and importantly , the money they earn is guaranteed wages as opposed to winnings . Golfers , tennis players and boxers earn most of their income from prize money , or from endorsements which are a result of being a winner .
