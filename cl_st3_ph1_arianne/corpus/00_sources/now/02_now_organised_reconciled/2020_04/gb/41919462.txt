That 's why we need a public enquiry now . " <p> Galsworthy said the UK was on track to become one of the worst affected nations in Europe by the virus , pointing to the rate of deaths per million people . He argued the government had been slow to enforce a lockdown and provide frontline healthcare staff with enough PPE . He also labelled Number 10 's attempts to ramp up testing for the virus a " scandal " . <p> He said : " Never again can we see our frontline NHS staff trying to take care of themselves and their patients wearing only bin liners and thin surgical masks that do not provide adequate protection when they are in a high risk environment .
