The court concluded provisions for foreign investors have " no adverse effect on the autonomy " of EU law -- as long as democratic choices , food safety , consumer protections or fundamental rights do n't fall under the scope of the any dispute settlement schemes . Chalk that up as a win for the European Commission and free traders , for three reasons ... <p> 1 . No change : The Commission can now trumpet the fact " no changes have to be made to the text " of the agreement and ratification can now proceed -- and Commission spokesman Margaritis Schinas was quick to do so Tuesday .
