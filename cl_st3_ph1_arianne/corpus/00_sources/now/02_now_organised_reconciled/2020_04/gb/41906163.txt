<h> Aberdeen youngster quizzes First Minister on coronavirus <p> An Aberdeen youngster was among children picked to ask the First Minister questions about coronavirus . <p> Elsie Woodward-Nutt from Aberdeen was one of twelve from across the country to put her question about Covid-19 directly to Nicola Sturgeon as she took part in a special Q&amp;A designed specifically for kids . <p> With many children in Scotland feeling overwhelmed by the pandemic , Elsie asked when the play parks would be open again . <p> Speaking after the Q&amp;A , the seven-year-old said : " She said they will be open as soon as they can . I was very excited to get picked . <p> " I have n't told my friends yet because I have n't been able to contact them . <p> " I 'm missing my friends , but like being at home more . I am missing school and doing work . " <p> Father , Ian Woodward-Nutt , added : " Mum and dad were very pleased for Elsie 's moment of fame . <p> " A friend of ours had seen the Parent Club was inviting kids to submit questions and Elsie has always been very inquisitive about coronavirus , so we submitted one . " <p> Parent Club is an online information and support hub for parents in Scotland and they invited children to submit questions via video link . <p> Some of the other children asked when they would be able to see their friends again , when schools would reopen and why they ca n't see their grandparents . <p> Nicola Sturgeon said it was a " very important " session for her as she did her best to give them all the answers they needed in " simple terms " . <p> Since measures to limit the spread of coronavirus were introduced , children across the country are having to accept a different way of life , without much time to understand why . <p> The First Minister added : " I know that some will be thinking it 's scary and I understand that . <p> " We want to help them at this difficult time by reassuring them that it 's okay to be feeling this way and to answer some of the key questions they have . <p> " We want children and their parents to feel as reassured as possible regarding the current situation . <p> " We know they need up to date information , clear guidance and continuous reassurance as they navigate this change to their lives . <p> " Parent Club offers helpful guidance and support at this challenging time . <p> " We 're encouraging parents to use it as a trusted source of information and to connect with other parents while staying at home to protect themselves and their families from the virus .
