<p> The stark warning came as the FCA unveiled its latest business plan , which set out its key priorities for the next one to three years . <p> The regulator said it would attempt to progress with its work where possible without undermining the focus on the coronavirus response , but warned it could be " weeks or months " before it could concentrate on the plan . <p> " This shock is not like previous economic downturns , but nor will it follow the pattern of a natural catastrophe , where the damage can be sized relatively quickly , " it said . <p> " Here , there is enormous uncertainty about the size and nature of potential damage . " <p> The FCA has already rolled out measures to tackle the economic turmoil wreaked by the pandemic and social distancing measures , including emergency guidance for government schemes such as rent holidays and support for small businesses . <p> The watchdog today said it will also crack down on scams relating to the crisis amid fears criminals could prey on vulnerable consumers . <p> " There may be some who see these times as an opportunity for poor behaviour -- including market abuse , capitalising on investors ' concerns or reneging on commitments to consumers , " it said . <p> " Where we find poor practice , we will clamp down with all relevant force . " <p> Aside from Covid-19 , the FCA warned that pension freedoms had created a " significant risk of harm " for consumers and outlined a new campaign to protect consumers from high-risk investments . <p> The watchdog also said it will transform its own approach to regulation in response to the pandemic , with a focus on making faster and more effective decisions . <p> " At a time when many people and businesses are suddenly finding themselves in acute financial difficulty , and investors are finding it ever more challenging to make investment decisions , it is right that the core priorities of the regulator 's business plan go to the heart of some of the most immediate issues , " said Simon Turner , a partner in EY 's financial services regulation practice . <p> " The message to firms is clear : act now in a way you will be proud of when we come out of this crisis . " <p> Sarah Isted , partner at PwC , said : " The priorities announced in the FCA 's annual business plan underscore the extent to which the Covid-19 pandemic has fundamentally changed the financial landscape in the short and medium term . <p> " Beyond Covid-19 , the FCA continues to prioritise climate change , technology , operational resilience , financial crime and culture . As the immediate implications of the pandemic become clearer , these areas will all remain important .
