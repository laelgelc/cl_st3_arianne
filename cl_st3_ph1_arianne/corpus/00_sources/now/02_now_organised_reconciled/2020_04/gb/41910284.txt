But when the Flow threatens to disappear and isolate these systems from one another , it 's up to Emperox Grayland II ( a.k.a . Cardenia Wu-Patrick ) , scientist Marce Claremont , and guild merchant Kiva Lagos to convince the Interdependency to get past their denial to acknowledge the threat and figure out how to reform their entire culture ... all while fending off aspiring emperox Nadashe and the rest of her Lannister-esque Nohamapetan family . <p> Scalzi swears that , when he pitched this series in 2014 , he could in no way anticipate how relevant it would feel to the current moment .
