<h> Peace Museum provides online activities based on influential figures <h> Peace Museum in Bradford provides online activities for kids <p> A BRADFORD museum is helping to support students who are having to study at home due to the coronavirus pandemic . <p> The Peace Museum , which is dedicated to the history of peace , has created educational resources which can be accessed online and worked through at home . <p> The museum occupies three small galleries in a Victorian building in the centre of Bradford and it usually delivers educational workshops in schools across the city and the North . <p> But , all that was put to a stop when the COVID-19 virus came to England and schools were closed . <p> A spokesperson for The Peace Museum said : " As schools have closed down for most students amid the coronavirus pandemic , our education team has been busy creating educational resources to do at home . <p> " We have created activity sheets inspired by iconic figures from the peace movement and some based on education sessions we offer for schools . <p> " The activities are aimed at children aged 7-11 , but could be suitable for anyone . " <p> The topics and figures range from those who are well established , to more modern influential characters . <p> On that note , Greta Thunberg is one of those the activities focus on . <p> The Swedish teenager skipped school and inspired an international movement to fight climate change . <p> Find out more about her and her cause in this activity . <p> One activity focuses on primatologist ( the study of the behaviour , biology , evolution , and taxonomy of non-human primates ) , Jane Goodall . <p> She is most known for her long-term study of wild chimpanzees in Tanzania . <p> Find out more about the animal rights icon and her trailblazing work in this activity . <p> A name many people may be familiar with is , Rosa Parks . <p> She was an American activist described as " mother of the civil rights movement " . <p> Learn how this remarkable woman helped change the lives of millions of people with just one simple act of defiance . <p> The next activity focuses on Sadako Sasaki . <p> She was a Japanese girl who became a victim of the atomic bombing of Hiroshima when she was two-years-old . <p> Learn about a courageous girl whose life , which ended through the effects of war , inspired a worldwide call for peace . <p> The final figure is Wangari Maathai . <p> She was a renowned Kenyan social , environmental and political activist and the first African woman to win the Nobel Prize . <p> Find out more about her and her inspiring legacy in this activity . <p> The museum also has a number of other downloadable resources to keep you busy during lockdown . <p> The Art of Peace section allows you to create your own postcard , using an activity sheet based around Bradford artist , David Hockney . <p> Mr Hockney has created many pieces in the past focusing on peace-making , including a postcard called " Peace On Earth " . <p> You can use this as inspiration for your own postcard . <p> The " Caring for Our World " activity sheet meanwhile gives ideas for how we can all work together to look after the enviroment . <p> You will be given facts about our planet and the animals that live here , as well as being asked questions - such as , is there anything you can do in your garden to help look after the animals that visit , like Bees and Hedgehogs ? <p> The museum has also uploaded activity sheets that are usually available in the building itself . <p> This includes a template to create your own " Peace Symbol Dreamcatcher " . <p> The final product could look something like the main image above . <p> Other templates you can print off and colour in include : "
