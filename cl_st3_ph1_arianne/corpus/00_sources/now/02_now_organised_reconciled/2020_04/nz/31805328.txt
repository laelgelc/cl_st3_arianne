<p> In a speech highlighting the values of globalisation and international cooperation , Ardern made the announcement at the University of the South Pacific this evening . <p> " Climate change continues to have a significant impact on the people of the region , as coasts erode , sea levels rise , and fish stocks move . I know this is not hypothetical here , but a reality as it is in the places I have seen with my own eyes - Tuvalu , Tokelau , Samoa , " she said . <p> " The artificial lines between what we call domestic policy and international policy are just that - artificial . When we 're trying to address issues such as climate change , or infectious diseases , or trans-national crime , borders do not matter - these are borderless challenges and they demand a collective response . <p> " Today , I am pleased to announce that New Zealand is making under this programme a $2 million dedicated investment in supporting Fiji to manage one of the most confronting issues that climate change has already brought - the displacement of communities . <p> " We are contributing to the establishment of Fiji 's Climate Relocation and Displaced Peoples Trust Fund for Communities and Infrastructure , launched in September - a world first initiative that aims to sustainably and sensitively manage the relocation of Fiji 's communities . " <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern at an event at the University of the South Pacific in Suva . Photo : RNZ / Jo Moir <p> Five communities have already been moved and a further 42 have applied for government support to move . <p> New Zealand is the first country to contribute to the fund , after a request from Fijian Prime Minister Frank Bainimarama . <p> Ardern said $98m had already been approved for projects under the package , which was part of a wider commitment to invest at least $300m globally in climate-related assistance in the next three years . <p> It was hoped the funding would give confidence to other countries to contribute also . <p> " Fiji is on the front line of the battle against climate change , ' ' Ardern said . <p> " It 's important we support those who contributed the least to rising sea levels and extreme weather but who are experiencing it the most , resettle their communities in safer places . <p> " This is a reality we have a collective responsibility to manage and support . " <p> The Fiji Trust Fund is a world-first designed to " sustainably and sensitively manage " the relocation of Fiji 's communities . <p> Ardern is in Fiji for the first official visit by a Prime Minister to the country since 2016 . <p> Ardern today visited a settlement in Suva where Australian and New Zealand funding is helping improve sanitation and water supply . <p> She said Fiji had a different measure of poverty , and New Zealanders appreciated that a small amount of money could make a big difference in the Pacific . <p> " With the small contribution we 've made here , we 're making sure that people have toilets , that they can protect themselves from flooding that could wipe their villages out . " <p> Earlier , she hosted a lunch with women MPs , youth leaders and women in business and civil society roles in Fiji . <p> She said New Zealand was not free of the problems that come from gender inequality . <p> She told a group of Fijian women leaders that while New Zealand had female Prime Ministers , Governors-General and Chief Justices there was still work to be done and barriers to be broken down . <p> Ardern was put on a stage at the RISE informal settlement event , but following her speech and saying she preferred to sit with her friends - the Fijian people - she joined the women on the floor after her speech .
