<h> Netflix Demand Share Increasing In US And Globally Ahead Of Earnings <p> Ahead of Netflix 's Tuesday earnings call , Parrot Analytics has found that the streaming giant has experienced steady growth in digital original demand share in both the US and globally since December 2019 . <p> In the United States , Netflix 's share of digital original demand grew from 50.0% for the month of December 2019 to 53.9% for the month of March 2020 . Early signs for April show further growth , as the service has 54.7% of US demand share as of April 13th . <p> Globally , the numbers are more impressive . The service grew its global share of demand for digital original content from 52.3% in December 2019 to 57.8% in March of 2020 . <p> These numbers reversed a recent downward trend , when Netflix lost 11.2% in global demand share for digital originals from the year 2018 to 2019 .
