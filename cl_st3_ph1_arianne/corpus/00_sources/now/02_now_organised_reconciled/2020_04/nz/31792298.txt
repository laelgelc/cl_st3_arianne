<h> Game changer <p> A new research winery in the heart of New Zealand 's vine country will be a " drawcard " for international researchers , says MJ Loza . <p> Speaking at the opening of the Bragato Research Institute ( BRI ) facility - with its bespoke-tank technology and sustainable , future-proofed design - the BRI Chief Executive said it would enable valuable collaborations around the world , as well as throughout New Zealand . <p> " While based regionally in Marlborough we are here for New Zealand 's entire winegrowing industry , operating nationally , with global reach . " <p> The BRI and its research winery received start-up funding from the Ministry of Business , Innovation and Employment ( MBIE ) and Marlborough District Council , and are intended to add to the industry 's research capability , not duplicate it , he told the audience . <p> " We need to be expert in our chosen fields and partner with the very best in New Zealand and globally . " <p> That meant expanding on research to benefit New Zealand 's wine industry , while developing new research capability ; promising science excellence , while " connected and collaborating with the world 's best " , MJ said . <p> The new winery would ensure research to a standard of excellence that the industry - and its wines - deserve , he added . <p> " We 'll conduct winemaking trials and we 'll take vineyard trials through to finished wines , under levels of experimental control not possible in New Zealand before . " <p> Aside from the winery , BRI has research priorities around sustainability , climate change , grapevine improvement , industry research data and technology transfer - " making sure the industry 's research is taken up and applied for real benefit " . <p> MJ said the winery 's location at the Marlborough Research Centre in Blenheim - alongside Plant &amp; Food Research , the Nelson Marlborough Institute of Technology ( NMIT ) , Wine Marlborough , and New Zealand Winegrowers - was strategic , enhancing the ability to " build connections between research , industry and education " . <p> Winery Establishment Manager Tracy Atkin acknowledged the commitment of the Marlborough-based project team that worked to design and refine the winery and its specialist trial tanks , which have inserts to enable four-in-one fermentation and automated functions for remote control . <p> She also applauded the efforts and initiative of the contractors behind it , who delivered the facility and its fittings in time for vintage 2020 , just nine months after it received the go-ahead . <p> " Not only did we not need to go outside of the Marlborough/Tasman region for anything , I do n't believe we would have the result we have today if we were anywhere else , " she told guests at the opening . <p> " The team have worked as a community on this , continually coming up with new ideas and inspiration , working together for what you see today . " <p> Andy Frost was one of the industry representatives on the working group , and helped design the 200-litre tanks , then refine them after replicated trials were conducted in the first tranche in the 2019 vintage . <p> Seeing the completed winery , its floor lined with rows of world-first tanks , was " exciting " and a real step up for the wine industry , which increasingly recognised the value of research , Andy said . He was struck by the " tidiness " of the winery layout , with few fittings extruding from the pedestals , despite the winery having all its bells and whistles . <p> " The overall design is really exciting - it will be an absolute breeze to operate . " Andy , who was involved in a lot of research while at Pernod Ricard , said industry members were eager to help make the research winery the best it could be , reflecting the information sharing that is typical of the industry . <p> " There was no holding back because they knew it was for the good of all . "
