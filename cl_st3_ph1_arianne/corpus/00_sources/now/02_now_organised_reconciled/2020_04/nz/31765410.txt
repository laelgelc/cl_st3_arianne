<h> Time soon to rescue the country <p> If there is one salutary lesson for our politicians to learn from this mini-pandemic , it is that you must never let the " experts " dictate the narrative . <p> Join the council and you rapidly learn that " expert " planners want unlimited infill housing so everyone can be forced to live within the Gisborne city sewage/water reticulation area , because that is more efficient . Hmm , true , so it is , if efficiency is your only priority . <p> Let economists run the economy , let the defence force decide our spending priorities , oh dear no , recipe for disaster . Let educationists decide how our schools should be run and soon there will be two teachers , one teacher aide , one psychologist , one social worker and 14 administrators per student . Let climate change " experts " decide public policy and they would pretty soon institute rules that would see about 2 billion people die from starvation . <p> Because " experts " , by definition , have tunnel vision . <p> Somehow our politicians have abdicated their governance responsibility to health " experts " in the current situation . And they have wasted no time in taking advantage of their moment in the spotlight . <p> In fact , I 'll go out on a limb here ( not for the first time ) and predict that in the 12 months from March 1 , 2020 to February 28 , 2021 there will be fewer overall deaths in New Zealand than in the average year . There will be hardly any from Covid-19 , and fewer than usual from influenza and other age-related risks because of social distancing and greater awareness of care for the elderly . Probably some extra suicides from people whose financial futures have been devastated , though . <p> Around the country surgeons are sitting on their backsides because elective surgery has been cancelled , hospitals are mooching along wondering what to do with themselves as they await the hordes of dying patients . <p> How many as of today ? Oh yes , 12 in hospital throughout the country , four in ICU . And 1000 or so sitting around at home coughing and sneezing a bit . <p> Meanwhile the health experts are dancing in the streets . Whoopee ! They have cancelled the sale of hard liquor , they have cancelled rough sports , they have cancelled gambling , they have cancelled unhealthy fast food , they have n't yet figured out that they can cancel smoking entirely by declaring the sale of tobacco " non-essential " , but give them time . <p> One day , when Mother Earth finally gets fed up with us pesky humans , there will be a real pandemic , a serious one , a virus that kills 5 percent or 10 percent or 20 percent of the people who get it . When that happens the shutdown procedures currently in place will be vital . So maybe we should view this as a good practice run ? No . It is over the top . <p> I really do n't want to sound too critical . Leaders have to be brave enough to make tough decisions , and Jacinda Ardern has done that . She has grown in her role and might well end up as one of our truly great Prime Ministers . I do n't fault her swift and decisive actions here . I may not totally agree , but I do n't have her responsibilities ; she carries the burden and she is carrying it with grace and determination . I just hope that she will soon recognise that we have gone a bit overboard , that it is time to rescue the country . And that she will act decisively to bring things back to normal . She looks pretty smart to me , I have confidence that she will .
