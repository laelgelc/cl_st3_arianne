Swarbrick moved up several spots between lists at the last election , ensuring her entry to Parliament . <p> Tuiono is a veteran activist and education consultant who has worked at the United Nations and Massey University . He mounted an unsuccessful mayoral campaign in Palmerston North in 2019 , finishing a distance second to incumbent Grant Smith . <p> Tuiono was also in the 2017 list but at the unwinnable spot of 16 . <p> The minimum number of seats the party could win while still being in Parliament is six - barring the unlikely possibility it wins an electorate seat . At the last election it won eight . <p> If it won eight again all current MPs would be returned as well as Tuiono , bar Gareth Hughes who is not standing again . <p> School climate strikers Lourdes Vano and Luke Wijohn feature on the list , but at likely unwinnable positions of 12 and 19 . <p> The Green Party would have to win roughly 10 per cent of the party to get 12 MPs into Parliament , a number they have not polled very close to since Jacinda Ardern became leader of the Labour Party and pulled a lot votes away from the Green Party . <p> A party spokesperson said the list would now go to a nationwide vote . <p> " We can confirm the Green Party has announced our interim list that was decided by delegates who attended the Party 's conference in February . Using the most democratic list selection process in the country , the list will now go to members for a nationwide vote .
