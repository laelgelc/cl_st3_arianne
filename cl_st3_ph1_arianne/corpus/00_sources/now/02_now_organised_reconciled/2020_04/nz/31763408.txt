<p> KASAM spokesperson Cindy Baxter said Friday 's decision was vindication for years of hard work . <p> " This is a victory for the oceans . The Court of Appeal has ruled that protecting the environment is the bottomline and that 's what we 've argued all the way through . <p> " They ca n't just go and dig up 50 million tonnes of the seabed every year for 35 years and claim there 's no impact on the environment . So we 're really , really pleased , it 's fantastic for the people , for the whales and all the creatures in the Taranaki Bight . " <p> The Court of Appeal , in its decision , found the Environmental Protection Authority 's decision making committee , which required the casting vote of its chair to grant the original consents , focused on too much on managing the effects of the proposed project instead of its primary objective of protecting the environment . <p> " The DMC erred in focusing on the sustainable management objective that applies to all marine consents under the Exclusive Economic Zone Act , and failing to give separate and explicit consideration to the environmental bottom line of protecting the environment from pollution caused by discharges of harmful substances . " <p> The court also found interests of Treaty of Waitangi principles were not adhered to . <p> " The kaitiakitanga relationship between tangata whenua and the marine environment and its resources is a relevant ' existing interest ' . That kaitiakitanga relationship includes , but is not limited to , the stewardship and use of natural resources such as kai moana . The cultural and spiritual elements of kaitiakitanga must also be considered . " <p> The Maori Party also welcomed the decision and congratulated Ngati Ruanui and Te Kaahui o Rauru for their perseverance . <p> " Here in Aotearoa , from Raukumara to Te Hiku to Kaikoura to Patea , we have seen that Maori are on the frontline of resisting extractive industries that pollute our environment and pushing for transformative climate action , " Maori Party president Che Wilson said . <p> " Ngati Ruanui and ( Maori Party candidate ) Debbie Ngarewa-Packer have led the fight against seabed mining in Aotearoa . If elected as the MP for Te Tai Hauauru this year , Debbie will take that fight into Parliament . " <p> Wilson said the Maori Party position was to ban all seabed mining permits across Aotearoa . <p> " We would also ban new onshore oil and gas permits , fracking , and new coal mines . A vote for the Maori Party is a vote for strong action on climate change and protecting our natural environment . " <p> If Trans-Tasman Resources resource consents application had been successful it wanted mine for iron ore in a 66sq km area of seabed off the Taranaki Bight . <p> The company intended to dig up to 50 million tonnes of sand each year , extract the iron ore from it , and dump the residue on the sea floor . <p> It has said the scheme would create 300 jobs and add $160 million to New Zealand 's GDP . <p> In a statement yesterday , chairman Alan J Eggers said Trans-Tasman Resources was awaiting its legal team 's analysis of , and advice on , the Court of Appeal 's rulings and conclusions . <p> Eggers said further comment on the Court of Appeal Judgement would be made once its board had received that advice .
