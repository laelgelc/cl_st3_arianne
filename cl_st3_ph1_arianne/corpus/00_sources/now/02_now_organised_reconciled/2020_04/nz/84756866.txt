<h> Long wait for construction of Northland 's water storage facility <p> Drought-stricken Northland will have to wait until next summer for construction to begin on a water storage facility . <p> A parched paddock in Northland during the summer . Photo : RNZ / Simon Rogers <p> Up to $30 million has been provided through the Provincial Growth Fund for the Far North , Mid-North and Kaipara to investigate potential water storage sites in the region . <p> Regional Economic Development Minister Shane Jones says the studies have identified some promising sites to build a series of small-scale reservoirs and create a distribution pipeline . <p> " An initial site near Kaikohe has been identified and , depending on post-Covi-19 timeframes , construction is expected to be underway there over next summer . Further sites will be explored as the project evolves , " Jones said . <p> " There is strong interest in this project from the wider primary sector , and belief that there is significant potential to grow high-value horticulture in the region if more water was available . " <p> Shane Jones . Photo : RNZ / Richard Tindiller <p> The frequency and severity of droughts are expected to increase due to climate change and having a reliable water supply would be increasingly important , Jones said . <p> " Water is critical for Northland 's economy and wellbeing . It will be even more so now that our regional economies are in recovery mode from the fallout of the Covid-19 pandemic . <p> " Security of water supply will open up new markets , create jobs and provide new opportunities for Northland . " <p> Jones said further engagement with potential water users will be done when lockdown restrictions allow .
