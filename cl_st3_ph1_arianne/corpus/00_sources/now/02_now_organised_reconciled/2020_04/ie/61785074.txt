<h> London CIV hires permanent CIO from LGPS Central <p> London CIV has just announced Jason Fletcher as the pooling vehicle 's new permanent chief investment officer . <p> It also announced Jacqueline Jackson as its head of responsible investment , a new role , and Cameron McMullen as its new client relations director . <p> Fletcher will be joining London CIV following three years as CIO for another local authority pension asset pool , LGPS Central , and West Midlands Pension Fund before that . He has also worked for the Universities Superannuation Scheme as its deputy head of equities and co-deputy CIO . <p> Fletcher 's appointment at London CIV follows the recent promotion of Rob Hall to deputy CIO . <p> Jackson will be joining the London pension collaboration from Trucost , S&amp;P Global , where she directed business development and responsible investment , assessing investment risks relating to climate change , natural resource constraints and broader environmental , social and governance factors . <p> She is due to start working at London CIV by July . London CIV also announced that Chris Bilsland , a non-executive director , had been appointed to the board as the " responsible investment champion " . <p> McMullen will be joining the London pool next week , taking over from Kevin Cullen , who is retiring . McMullen was most recently at MJ Hudson Allenbridge . <p> In a statement , London CIV said the appointments were a " key milestone " for the pool , which had been " working to further bolster the senior management team and have the necessary resources to be more active in their commitment to responsible investment and climate change , working in partnership with their pool members to do so " .
