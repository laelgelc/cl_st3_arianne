<h> Earth Day 2020 : Universities in the US went virtual for Earth Day at 50 <p> Earth Day is celebrated each year on April 22 , but this year 's observation of the event -- which marks the 50th anniversary of the environmental movement -- has been extremely low key , thanks to COVID-19 . <p> This year 's theme is climate action . <p> Universities are usually big observers of the movement , but just because many ca n't organise on-campus events to mark the occasion , some institutions have decided to go ahead with a virtual Earth Day -- or week -- instead . <h> University of Southern California <p> USC 's virtual Earth Day involved a series of online activities , including virtual interactive forums , panel discussions , a career fair and even a sustainability-themed meditation session using Zoom and other video technology , said the university . <p> Some of the topics covered in their forum included topics that covered the causes of the climate change crisis , solutions to the disruption and USC 's sustainability efforts , talks about the university 's sustainability goals , progress and challenges , and even featured a career fair with " green " employers . <h> University of Colorado Boulder <p> For #EarthDay , we are resharing our story about @EcenterCU 's compost efforts in Norlin Library .
