That 's a concept that today underpins our vital image as we market our beef , lamb , milk , pigs and poultry across the world . <p> Surely now is the time that we need to protect and harness this image more than ever before . <p> While on the Teagasc board , the topic of nitrates was a major issue . Nevertheless , Teagasc stuck resolutely to pursuing a science-based solution and , backed by solid research , this view held sway in Europe . <p> I am confident that a similar science-based , research-backed approach will deliver a workable solution to climate change for Irish and European farmers . <p> Advertisement <p> But let 's be clear ; emotion has no place in these negotiations . <h> Petty squabbles <p> As a poultry producer and cattle farmer I have , without doubt , benefited from what can be achieved by good , reliable genetic information . <p> The ICBF has collected this information and presented it in a workable format for farmers and breeders to enable them to take maximum advantage ... and more of this is needed . <p> As new money is introduced over the coming Covid-19 recovery period our Government must ensure that small businesses -- including farming -- can avail of finance to rebuild at reasonable rates . <p> The EU must address food security seriously ; short-term market supports must be put in place . <p> But , to be frank , the mocking or knocking of our institutions needs to stop . <p> We need to encourage and acknowledge their successes -- and deal with their failings in-house in order to build strength at all levels . Do not undermine them . <p> Petty squabbles over a misprinted label or objecting to EU ( Polish ) beef being imported into the UK has a hollow ring when we are the enemy in UK farmers ' eyes . <p> Remember , when EU support is needed we will turn to our Polish compatriots in negotiations at European Commission level . <p> And , it is my hope , when the Beef Taskforce returns to the table to deal with the fallout it must work in a spirit of openness and common purpose ( as the Beef Forum did in 2014 ) to protect our markets and keep our products moving .
