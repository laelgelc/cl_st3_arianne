<h> Online library memberships soar by more than 300% <p> New online library memberships have soared by more than 300% across Ireland since the outbreak of Covid-19 . <p> New book worms have turned to online library services as strict measures are keeping the nation at home . <p> E-books and audio books have become increasingly popular as the Government reported a dramatic increase in the use of online services in recent weeks . <p> Senior Government official Liz Canavan said : " There 's been a 106% increase in e-book loans and a 66% increase in eAudiobook loans since the beginning of March . <p> " In addition , there has been over a 300% increase in new library users throughout March . <p> " It 's heartening to know that people are taking advantage of community services that are made available to support them at this difficult time and reading is a great way , not only to entertain ourselves during this crisis , but also to look after our mental health . " <p> It was also confirmed during the government 's daily briefing that farmers will be able to access funding from an agri-environment scheme a month earlier than planned . <p> Ms Canavan said : " As part of the response to the impacts that Covid-19 is having on Irish farming incomes , payments for the Green , Low-Carbon , Agri-Environment Scheme -- GLAS -- will commence a month ahead of schedule . <p> Bringing forward this payment should significantly improve cash flow on Irish farms in these challenging times <p> " The GLAS scheme incentivises farmers to promote biodiversity , protect water quality and also to combat climate change on their farms . <p> " Over ? 26 million in GLAS balancing payments will be issued to 42,300 farmers who are participating in the scheme and will reach bank accounts early next week . " <p> The Department of Employment has also warned of a bogus email which is seeking a refund of unemployment payments made to customers to a nominated bank account . <p> Ms Canavan said the emails generally came from a Gmail or Hotmail address . <p> " The department has asked us to warn you categorically that it does not use Gmail or Hotmail addresses when issuing notifications to customers , " she said . <p> " These incidents are treated with the utmost seriousness and the garda ? have been notified . <p> " Should a person receive such an email and be in any doubt as to its authenticity , please contact the department at 01704308 . " <p> She also said that over 1,000 calls were made to the Community Call Forum on Thursday . <p> The HSE have developed an information pack on how to protect yourself and others from coronavirus .
