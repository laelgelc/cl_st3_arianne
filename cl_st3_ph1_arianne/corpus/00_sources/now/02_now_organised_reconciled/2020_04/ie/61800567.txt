<h> Fun home-schooling challenges launched by Concern <p> INSECT hotels and hand-washing songs are among the many new fun home-schooling projects for children launched today by Irish charity Concern Worldwide . <p> New projects will be posted online daily as part of the 17-day challenge on an education phone app called Makematic to teach children and their families about the United Nations 17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . The goals include ending poverty , climate change and hunger . <p> " This is a very valuable tool for parents trying to home-school their children , " said Concern 's Schools and Youth Programme Officer , Lauren Wright . <p> " We want to bring the world into people 's homes during these challenging times with positive activities for families to get involved with . <p> " These are fun and very educational activities for children from ages 7 to 17 , but anyone can join in . <p> " We hope that children can watch the videos that will be posted each day to learn about the development goals and then partake in one or two of the challenges listed . <p> " We are using #17daystolearn for anyone sharing what they have done online and we think it would be great for children to keep a scrapbook of their work too to show teachers when they are back at school . " <p> The first challenge , which centres around the ' no poverty ' development goal , is to create a " blessing bag " -- which is a small bag or box of essential items that people living in poverty might need , such as food , snacks and or toiletries . <p> Other challenges that will be posted on the Makematic website and app to promote awareness and understanding of SDGs over the coming weeks include : <p> Making an insect hotel in a garden or balcony ( SDG : Life on land ) ; <p> Make a fun 20-second video to show people how to wash their hands . ( SDG : Good health and well-being ) ; <p> Track how much water your household uses each day ( SDG : Clean water and sanitation ) ; <p> Make a paper windmill and take it outside to see it move ( SDG : Affordable and clean energy ) ; <p> Find a quote you like about peace , write it out on paper and display it in your window ( SDG : Peace Justice and Strong Institutions ) ; <p> Create a " jar of hope ' by filling it with notes of wishes , hopes and ideas for the world ( SDG : Reduced Inequalities ) ; <p> Make a cartoon strip to show how climate change is affecting someone your age in a different place in the world ( SDG : Climate action ) . <p> Limerick Post News is your local news , entertainment , business , fashion website .
