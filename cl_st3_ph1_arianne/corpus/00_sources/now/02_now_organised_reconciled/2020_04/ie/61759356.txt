<h> GOAL calls on young people in Limerick to join its " NextGen Global Youth Programme " <p> HUMANITARIAN aid agency , GOAL , is calling on young people in Limerick to join its innovative youth programme aimed at bringing young people in Ireland together with their peers in Africa to contribute to creating a better world . <p> The programme launch comes at a time when the world is facing one of the biggest crises of this generation as it battles the spread of the coronavirus . <p> GOAL is appealing to those aged between 18 and 25 with volunteering experience and an understanding of global issues , to apply for the GOAL NextGen Global Youth Programme 2020 . <p> The programme will involve participants from Ireland and African countries to create , trial , and share a development education resource to guide passionate young people on contributing to a sustainable world for all . <p> The Programme intends to help young people better understand the tools and processes of effective advocacy through global citizenship . <p> Selected participants will join 12 online seminars between May and October 2020 introducing them to concepts and providing them with discussion space . <p> The seminars will have input from expert guest speakers . Participants from Ireland and Africa will also facilitate online workshops with young people outside the programme to test the resource they are creating jointly . <p> GOAL Global Citizenship manager , Nina Sachau , said : " We are living in very worrying times . There is renewed urgency for action in response to global crises which include Covid-19 . <p> " The current health crisis shows us that people living on this planet are really interconnected . What happens in one part of the world affects every other part of the world .
