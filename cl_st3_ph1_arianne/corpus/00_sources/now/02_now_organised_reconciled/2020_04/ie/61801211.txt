<h> Cappabue 's viral heroes honoured in recycling awards <p> Cappabue NS students Chloe and Kayla McCarthy with Tallulah Little and Rufus Pullen with their recycled whale ( Photo : Andy Gibson ) <p> Share this article <p> SCHOOL may be out for the moment , but that has n't stopped the talented students of Cappabue National School from making the news . <p> Not only have they officially released their viral rap song ' One Small Change ' across all digital platforms , but they were also announced as one of 10 global winners in the 2020 Global Recycling Day #RecyclingHeroes competition . The school will now pick up $1,000 for their efforts to combat climate change . <p> Speaking to The Southern Star , principal of the two-teacher school near Kealkil , Norma Healy said the pupils are absolutely delighted with this award . <p> ' What is great is that someone nominated us for this award and we had no idea who that was . What is also great , too , is thatthese awards are in their inaugural year and we 're the first ever Irish winner and the only European country in the Top 10 , ' said Norma . <p> ' We really appreciate the person who did nominate us and we only got official notice from the organisers two weeks ago about our win . We definitely plan to get two new ipads with the money we have won from this . It 's a pity that schools are closed , but we 've told all our students the great news . ' <p> The #RecyclingHeroes competition , which was launched in February , encouraged people from across the globe to nominate individuals , businesses or communities that set examples and pushed boundaries with their recycling initiatives . <p> The school 's rap video ' One Small Change ' went viral in recent months and with the help of Gary McCarthy of GMC Beats and past pupils who helped make the video , they decided to release the song as a single . <p> ' Our single is now available to download on any music platform you want , so we will see how it goes down with the public , ' said Norma . <p> They school were also junior finalists in this year 's Fresh Film Festival which was due to take place in Limericklast month , but had to be cancelled due to the Covid-19 crisis . <p> ' So organisers have instead made all the films available on YouTube and there 's a facility to click on your favourite movie , ' added Norma . <p> The single ' One Small Change ' is currently on release on all digital platforms . <p> Subscribe to the Southern Star'sYouTube channel , like us onFacebook and follow us onTwitter and Instagram for all the latest news and sport from West Cork .
