<h> PHL must invest more in nursing or face 250,000 gap in 2030 -- WHO <p> UNLESS the Philippines makes a greater investment in nursing , it is projected to lack almost 250,000 nurses by 2030 . <p> The warning is contained in a report of the World Health Organization ( WHO ) , which projected the shortfall of nurses at 249,843 , unless greater investment is made now to retain them . <p> As the number of Covid-19 cases keeps rising worldwide , the WHO released on World Health Day a report which highlighted the global shortage of nurses worldwide . <p> By 2030 , the WHO 's State of the World 's Nursing 2020 report projects that , without action , there will be a shortfall of 4.6 million nurses worldwide . <p> State of the World 's Nursing 2020 paints a picture of a complex health labor market challenges . <p> For the Western Pacific , WHO Regional Director Dr. Takeshi Kasai said the case for investing in the nursing workforce has never been " clearer . " <p> " Right now , nurses are on the front lines of the Covid-19 fight , working tirelessly to save lives and protect others in their community , " Kasai said . <p> " Governments across the Western Pacific Region must invest in strengthening their nursing workforce as an essential part of preparedness for health challenges such as emerging infectious diseases , but also the health challenges brought about by climate change , ageing populations and a growing burden of noncommunicable diseases , " he added . <p> In the case of the Philippines , only 50 percent to 60 percent of nursing graduates become professional nurses who are eligible to practice as nurses in the country . <p> " The Philippine government , as well as the private sector , will need to address issues on uncoordinated production and inequitable distribution by promoting decent jobs and local opportunities for career development , if they are to retain an adequate number of appropriately trained nurses for the needs of the health system , " <p> said Drs . Tauhidul Islam and Socorro Escalante , current acting WHO Representatives for the Philippines . <p> They stressed that investments in quality nursing training and ensuring adequate pay and decent working conditions will help improve health outcomes , promote gender equality and support economic growth in the Philippines . <p> For this year 's World Health Day , the State of the World 's Nursing 2020 was produced by WHO in partnership with the International Council of Nurses and the global Nursing Now campaign , with support from governments and other partners . <p> The report provides a global picture of the nursing workforce , using data and standardized indicators from 193 countries and areas , including the Philippines . <p> At the World Health Assembly in May 2019 , ministers of health from across the globe designated 2020 as the Year of the Nurse and Midwife . <p> It marks 200 years since the birth of Florence Nightingale , the founder of modern nursing , and recognizes the critical contribution these professions make to global health . <p> Claudeth Mocon-Ciriaco is a grant recipient and fellow of 2017 media fellowship program by Probe Media Foundation Inc .
