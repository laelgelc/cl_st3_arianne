<p> Air quality in Metro Manila continues to improve amid the ongoing implementation of the enhanced community quarantine in Luzon , which limited the movement of motor vehicles since March 17 , the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) noted . <p> Based on the DENR 's latest Air Quality Monitoring Data released , the air breathed in the National Capital Region ( NCR ) is much safer today than a month ago , or before the entire country was ordered placed under a State of Public Health Emergency due the coronavirus disease 2019 ( Covid-19 ) pandemic . <p> This was attributed by the DENR-NCR air quality monitoring to the fact that public transportation was suspended immediately , while the movement of private vehicles was also drastically cut down . <p> Much of the air pollution in urban areas is carbon monoxide or half-burned smoke emitted by motor vehicles , which is being monitored by quality monitoring stations put up in strategic areas in the NCR . <p> The Marikina City Hall Air Quality Monitoring Station noted that air pollution in the city decreased in daily particulate matter , or PM-10 concentration from 13 percent to 72 percent , with an average of 43 percent from March 15 to April 10 . <p> In Para ? aque City , Don Bosco Barangay Hall Air Quality Monitoring Station reported the decrease in daily PM-10 concentration ranges from 4 percent to 54 percent or an average of 22.6 percent . <p> The significant drop in air pollution in Metro Manila was observed as early as a week after the quarantine was implemented . <p> The acceptable threshold standard level of PM-10 is 60 mass per unit volume ( expressed as ug/Ncm ) . <p> Based on DENR Administrative Order 2013-13 , the acceptable limit for PM-2.5 is 50 ug/Ncm for the short term 24-hour average level , and 35 ? g/Ncm for the long term one-year average level . <p> " We can see the drastic drop in numbers of PM-2.5 in Muntinlupa and Para ? aque , and the drop in numbers of PM-10 in Las Pi ? as and Marikina . This explains why our sky now is clear and looks clean , " DENR Undersecretary Benjamin D. Antiporda said . <p> " The sudden stop of activities in many countries allows the Earth to heal from environmental degradation , " Environment Secretary Roy A. Cimatu said underscoring the upside of the lockdown . " The major cause of climate change , which is air pollution due mainly by mass transport energy emissions , is being abated . <p> The number 2.5 in PM-2.5 refers to the diameter in micrometers or micron of the fine mixture of minute solid particles and liquid droplets present in the atmosphere . When inhaled , these can affect the heart and lungs . <p> PM-2.5 particles come from emissions of motor vehicles , fossil fuel power plants , and wood burnings , among others .
