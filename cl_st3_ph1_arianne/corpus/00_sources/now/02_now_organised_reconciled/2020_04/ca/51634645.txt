Yes , I want to press that we will , in this place and before too long , see new climate targets that meet the imperative of a looming disaster far greater than COVID-19 . ... But right now what I want to say is ' thank you ' for the spirit of collaboration . ' ... As we recognize that for the first time in my life , governments all around the world have decided , without hesitation , life is more important than money . " <p> Climate action requires global collaboration . <p> Even if mostly shut in at home due to COVID-19 , Barrie and area residents can participate in Earth Day in any number of ways . <p> Here is a list of top 10 actions for Earth Day , compiled by local Green Party members . <p> COMMUNITY : 1 . Donate to plant a tree ! Living Green Barrie is hoping to plant 10,000 trees around Simcoe County and you can help them by donating $25. 2 . Join Urban Pantry 3 . Grab a bag and fill it with garbage while on a walk around your neighbourhood ( with gloves on ! ) . 4 . Looking for a gift online ? Instead of buying from Amazon , why not support a local business downtown Barrie and purchase an e-gift card from a Downtown Barrie business . 5 . Send a letter or email to your local MPP , calling for stronger action against climate change and improved policies for the environment . <p> AROUND HOME : 1 . Start composting either in your backyard or even indoors . 2 . Start a pollinator garden and consider using native plants if possible . If you do n't have much outdoor space , you can even do this by planting wildflowers in a planter . 3 . Eat plant-based for the day . If you 're new to vegetarian eating , check out local nutritionist Pam Rocca 's blog for recipe ideas . 4 . Build a birdhouse . Visit nestwatch.org for birdhouse designs . 5 . Unplug your electronics and go for a walk with a goal to listen to sounds , smells and sights of nature . <p> We welcome your feedback and encourage you to share your thoughts on this story .
