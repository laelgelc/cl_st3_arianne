<p> MARKHAM , ON , April 15 , 2020 /CNW/ - Pond Technologies Holdings Inc. ( the " Corporation " or " Pond " ) ( TSX.V : POND ) , ( OTCQB : PNDHF ) an ESG company addressing global sustainability challenges of climate change and nutrition , today is providing a corporate update on recent business activities , including the appointment of Grant Smith as Chief Executive Officer of Pond Technologies Holdings Inc . Mr. Smith will succeed Steven Martin , who has retired from his position as Chief Executive Officer , effective immediately . Mr. Martin will remain involved with the company as a technology advisor , assisting with the continued advancement of Pond 's proprietary growth platform . In addition , Kevin Andrade will be stepping down from his role as Chief Operational Officer but will remain as a strategic advisor focusing on partnerships and major projects . <p> As part of a comprehensive corporate succession review and planning process conducted by the Board of Directors , the decision was made to name Mr. Smith , Chief Executive Officer . Mr. Smith has served as President of the Corporation 's subsdiary , Pond Naturals , since December , 2018 . Mr. Smith is a 25-year veteran of the North American nutraceutical industry and has a strong track record of building strategic relationships and driving sustainable growth within an organization . Mr. Smith will be responsible for shepherding a company-wide effort to increase sales across all divisions as well as supporting Pond 's technology licensing efforts through the sourcing of off-take contracts for the high-value algae products that Pond 's technology produces . <p> " The Corporation wishes to thank both Steven and Kevin for their service , " said Cameron Mingay , Chairman of the Board . " We look forward to calling upon their valued skillsets as Pond aggressively pursues the licensing of its technology to projects around the world . Pond remains fully focused on monetizing its extensive intellectual property assets through a high-margin licensing and royalty revenue model that we believe will benefit all stakeholders . We have full confidence in Grant to lead the company forward with its strategic plan . " <p> " Our team has been working tirelessly to implement Pond 's strategic vision , " said Mr. Smith , Chief Executive Officer . " As incoming CEO , I look forward to building on Pond 's foundation of achievement , and leading the team in its focus on sustainable revenue growth . I want to thank the Board of Directors for their vote of confidence and look forward to ever greater successes for Pond . " <p> Pond is also pleased to announce that its Agassiz , British Columbia plant has been awarded a COVID-19 site license from Health Canada and has been awarded a contract to manufacture hand sanitizer . Production is expected to begin the week of April 20th and the Corporation has already received an order for 3,200L of hand santitizer with a request for additional volumes . <p> About Pond Technologies Holdings Inc. : <p> Located in Markham , Ontario , Pond is a technology company that provides profitable solutions to the global health and wellness challenges of climate change and nutrition . Pond 's proprietary growth platform , including patented advanced photonics , optimizes key growth inputs in order to provide a controlled environment that maximizes the growth of algae and other organisms . This enables industrial emitters to generate new revenue streams from the transformation of underutilized CO2 to valuable algae-based products , such as protein for animal feed and nutraceutical products like Chlorella , Spirulina , and Astaxanthin for human consumption .
