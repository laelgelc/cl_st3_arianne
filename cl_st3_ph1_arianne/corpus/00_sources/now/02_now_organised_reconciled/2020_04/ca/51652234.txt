From an early age he developed a passion for art , environment and social action . In the 1990s , his work focused on acoustic ecology and soundscape composition , notably as a founder and first administrator of the World Forum for Acoustic Ecology . That focus has continued throughout the past few decades . Earlier this year , he attended a conference on Creative Climate Change Leadership with artists from around the world in Arizona called Crisis : Principles for Just and Creative Responses . <p> " In May 2019 , my climate denial bubble burst . It was a terrifying and disorienting experience that made me question everything about my life .
