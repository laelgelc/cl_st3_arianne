It outlines concerns about Alberta 's role in the federal government and what the signees call a push for " equality " for the province . The authors definition of equality has a number of different pillars , from repealing carbon taxes to changing the size of Parliament . <p> The declaration outlines the " challenges " faced by Alberta and possible solutions . It contends that Alberta is " physically and economically isolated " from Canada 's power structures . <h> RELATED <p> It 's structurally quite similar to the 2001 " Firewall Letter , " written by future-prime minister Stephen Harper and other prominent Albertans to then-premier Ralph Klein urging him to to fully exercise Alberta 's constitutional powers by creating a provincial police force and withdrawing from the Canada Pension Plan . <p> The Buffalo Declaration , however , is targeted at Justin Trudeau 's federal government rather than the provincial one .
