Basic Reproduction Number is usually written as R0 ( pronounced R naught , the zero should be a subscript , but babble ignores the &lt;sub&gt; tag ) , Herd Immunity Threshold as HIT . <p> R0 is the average number of other people who will be infected by one person who has the disease , assuming that all other members of the society are susceptible , that is , none are immune . If this number is greater than 1 , the disease will spread , if it is smaller than 1 , the disease will die out . R0 depends on many factors , including the disease itself and the social customs of a given group . R0 can also be lowered by intentional actions such as social distancing . <p> If some of the individuals in a society are immune to the disease , this will reduce the number of people infected by a single person who has the disease . For example if 90% of the population is immune , a carrier will only encounter 10% as many potential new infectees . If there are enough immune indivduals that each carrier will infect fewer than 1 other person on average , then the disease will die out . This is the HIT . <p> It is possible to calculate the HIT from R0 . As I said before , R0 is hard to determine , but the estimate for covid-19 is 1.4 to 3.9 . This results in a HIT of 29% to 74% .
