<p> Melanie Joly , Canada 's Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages Photo : Government of Canada Flickr <p> M ? lanie Joly , Canada 's Minister of Economic Development and Official Languages , said Wednesday three sectors of the economy are on the federal government 's radar at the moment to receive further pandemic-related subsidies : oil and gas , tourism and the airline industry . <p> " We are looking at specific aid for these sectors , " Joly told the Surrey Board of Trade by video conference . <p> The three sectors she identified stand out as carbon intensive , but Joly did not address how the Canadian economy may pivot to address either climate change reduction or even mitigation . <p> Last week the think-tank Pembina Institute called for governments to prioritize " investments that support the development of industries and businesses producing low- and zero-carbon goods and services to strengthen the foundation of B.C. 's clean economy and secure our domestic supply chain . " <p> Joly was asked to address economic resilience during the pandemic response and the post-pandemic recovery plan . <p> Joly said to expect further federal government support for fixed business costs beyond wages during the pandemic response . <p> Following the wage subsidy program , " we knew the next steps would be other fixed costs " such as property and equipment leases , said Joly . <p> An announcement is forthcoming , she said . <p> Surrey Board of Trade CEO Anita Huberman asked Joly about economic development following the pandemic ; however , Joly had little to say , stating the focus right now is the emergency financial recovery efforts . <p> " I think there is a pre-pandemic world and a post-pandemic world . We are looking at how we deal with the real impact of the pandemic . " <p> Joly said during the recovery it is important to maintain Canadian-based businesses . <p> " We need to keep them Canadian and we need to make sure we can have the best companies in Canada , " she said . <p> She also said she expects banks and other financial institutions to be offering low interest loans to businesses in order to maintain liquidity . <p> " If that 's not the case we have a real problem " and " I want to hear about it , " she said . <p> Joly said it will be important to maintain consumer confidence as well . <p> " We need to cross the bridge to the other side of shore , " said Joly . <p> Joly noted the 75% wage subsidy up to $58,700 for those companies showing income loss of 30% or greater . For the unemployed and those earning up to $1,000 per month , the emergency response benefit is $2,000 per month . <p> " We know the wage subsidy can be better . We 're getting there , " said Joly.
