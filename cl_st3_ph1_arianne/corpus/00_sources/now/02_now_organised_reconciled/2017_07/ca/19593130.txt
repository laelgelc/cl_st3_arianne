<p> Four months after seizing more than 25,000 Husky Energy Inc. documents as part of an investigation into a major pipeline spill in Saskatchewan last summer , federal officials want a Regina judge to rule on whether additional files the Calgary-based company says are protected by attorney-client privilege should be disclosed . <p> Federal and provincial authorities are investigating Husky 's role in the spill , which dumped 225,000 litres of crude near and into the North Saskatchewan River on July 20 and 21 , 2016 . It could face millions of dollars in fines under Saskatchewan legislation , plus a penalty of up to $500,000 under the federal Fisheries Act . <p> Environment and Climate Change Canada ( ECCC ) launched its probe of the spill , which Husky has claimed was caused by ground movement , hours after the first reports of oil slicks on the vital waterway .
