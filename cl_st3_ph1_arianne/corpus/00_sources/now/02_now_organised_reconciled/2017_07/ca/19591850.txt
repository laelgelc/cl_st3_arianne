<h> Conserving Wetlands to Help Fight Climate Change <h> News Release <h> Conserving Wetlands to Help Fight Climate Change <h> Protecting Diverse Habitats Across Ontario <p> Ontario is strengthening the protection and increasing the rate of wetland recovery across the province . <p> Wetlands are an essential component of Ontario 's biodiversity and are vital in mitigating the impacts of climate change by lowering the risk of flooding and drought , preventing erosion and moderating climate extremes . These diverse and productive habitats have many economic and health benefits , including improving water quality and offering excellent recreation and tourism destinations . <p> Ontario is also working with Ducks Unlimited Canada in a new partnership . This partnership will see Ducks Unlimited Canada chair a new committee comprised of environmental groups , industry , conservation authorities and indigenous representatives . As part of this partnership Ducks Unlimited Canada will also rehabilitate and improve wetland systems throughout southern Ontario , including important coastal wetlands . <p> Conserving Ontario 's wetlands and fighting climate change is part of our plan to create jobs , grow our economy and help people in their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ providing $1.9 million to Ducks Unlimited Canada to restore wetlands in southern Ontario . <p> Wetlands act as natural infrastructure , producing at least $14 billion in economic benefits each year for people in Ontario . <p> Wetlands are areas that have been saturated with water long enough for the soil to become waterlogged . This allows water-loving or water-tolerant plants to grow and thrive , thus contributing to the health of our biodiversity . <h> Additional Resources <h> Quotes <p> " Halting wetland loss requires coordinated efforts and a clear plan of action and that 's exactly what this strategy provides .
