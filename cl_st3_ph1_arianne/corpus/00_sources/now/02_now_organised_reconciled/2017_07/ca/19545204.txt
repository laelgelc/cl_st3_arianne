It forced the evacuation of 90,000 people , destroyed thousands of homes and buildings and forced the shutdown of oil sands operations . <p> The fire spread across 590,000 hectares -- an area larger than Prince Edward Island -- before it was brought under control . Man likely started the fire but the conditions were directly related to climate change . An unusually hot , dry air mass was in place over Northern Alberta in early May 2016 , which brought record-setting temperatures to Fort McMurray , hitting over 32 C for days , along with high winds and low humidity . It was a recipe for disaster . <p> B.C. declared a state of emergency this month as more than 200 wildfires burn across the province . Newfoundland and Labrador have unveiled a new tool will help stop forest fires before they start , with an online hazard map that provides real-time information about the risk of wildfires throughout the province .
