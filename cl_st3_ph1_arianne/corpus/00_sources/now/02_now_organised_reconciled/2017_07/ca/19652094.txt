<p> A transport truck rollover yesterday south of Hearst sent the driver to hospital and closed Highway 11 north . <p> The accident happened around 8 a.m . Saturday . <p> Officers of the Hearst Detachment of the OPP were called to the accident on Highway 11 , in Studholme Township west of the Town of Hearst . <p> Police investigation revealed that a single a tractor-trailer overturned , which resulted in a male driver being taken to hospital via air-ambulance for serious injuries . The Hearst Fire Department was also called to assist with extricating the driver . <p> Highway 11 was closed for several hours while the OPP Technical Collision Investigators ( TCI ) assisted with the investigation . The Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change of Ontario ( MOECC ) also attended due to a fuel spillage . <p> One lane of the Highway has now been re-opened . The cause of the accident is still under investigation .
