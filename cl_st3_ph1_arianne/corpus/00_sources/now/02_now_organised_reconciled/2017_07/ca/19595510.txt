The operation processed and packaged mined materials used in the abrasives industry . <p> From June 21 , 2014 , until to July 21 , 2015 , the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change received complaints about dust that was being emitted from the company 's plant . <p> A July 20 ministry news release said residents along the road complained that dust emanating from the plant was covering their land , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ inhaling the dust . <p> Plant visits by ministry staff identified the fugitive dust sources . Samples were taken from two residential properties on Kukagami Lake Road and were determined to be ground minerals consistent with the product produced at Mohawk Garnet . <p> The matter was turned over to the ministry 's investigation and enforcement branch and charges were laid . The company later declared bankruptcy . <p> Along with the fine , the company has to pay a victim fine surcharge of $62,500 . The company was given 15 days to pay .
