<h> G20 's financing of fossil fuels outweighs renewables , study says <h> G20 's financing of fossil fuels outweighs renewables , study says <p> Fossil fuels received four times more than clean energy in public financing from Group of 20 member countries , according to a report by environmental groups that urged more financing be directed to renewables to address climate change . <p> Financing for oil , gas and coal projects from G20 public finance institutions and multilateral development banks averaged $71.8 billion per year between 2013 and 2015 , according to a report released by Oil Change International , Friends of the Earth U.S. , the Sierra Club and WWF European Policy Office . That compares with $18.7 billion annually provided to support clean energy . <p> The report came after U.S. President Donald Trump vowed to usher in an era of " energy dominance " last week with plans such as lifting barriers to finance overseas coal energy plants and pulling the U.S. out of the Paris accord . Germany will host world leaders at a G-20 conference in Hamburg this week . <p> " Our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ money where its mouth is when it comes to the clean-energy transition , " Alex Doukas , senior campaigner at Oil Change International and one of the report 's authors , said in a statement . " If other G20 governments are serious about standing up to Trump 's climate denial and meeting their commitments under the Paris Agreement , they need to stop propping up the outdated fossil fuel industry with public money . " <p> Of the $71.8 billion in public finance , $13.5 billion went to exploration for new reserves of oil , gas and coal , according to the report . Japan provided the most exploration finance at $3.4 billion a year , followed by South Korea 's $1.6 billion . The U.S. and China provided $1.4 billion each . <p> The groups analyzed public support for energy projects from G20 public finance institutions such as overseas development aid agencies and export credit agencies , as well as multilateral development banks such as the World Bank . <p> " If G20 leaders are serious about meeting climate goals , they must undertake rapid and ambitious efforts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ green ' activities , " authors of the report said . " This is a significant step they can take even without the cooperation of Donald Trump . " <p> Other findings by the groups included : <p> - Japan , China and South Korea were the top providers of public financing among G20 for fossil fuels followed by the U.S. and Germany . <p> - Top five recipients of public finance for coal included Vietnam , Russia and Australia . <p> - Brazil , the U.S. and Saudi Arabia were top recipients of public finance for oil and gas .
