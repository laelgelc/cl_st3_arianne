<h> Increased Water Quality Testing At Area Schools <p> The Lambton Kent District School Board is working on ways to conserve water and further safeguard its quality at area schools . <p> Since 2007 , the province has required school boards to flush the plumbing , sample and test drinking water for lead content annually , from one location in each school , then report results to the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change . <p> Health and Safety officer Connie Ogilvie says as of July 1 , amendments to the regulation make it necessary to collect samples at every drinking water fountain and every tap that 's used to prepare food or drink . <p> " Now we have a timeline where we 'll be getting every drinking tap sampled by a certain date , " says Ogilvie . " So , we 'll be doing a third each year , for three years , and then every tap will be sampled . " <p> She says testing is n't required at fixtures not used for drinking water , like bathroom taps and sinks in classrooms . <p> Instead @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water is for handwashing only . <p> Ogilvie says the board is also planning to install bottle filling stations at 25 of its schools by this September -- with the rest to receive one by September 2018 . <p> Melanie has been a broadcast and multimedia journalist in Sarnia-Lambton since 2005 .
