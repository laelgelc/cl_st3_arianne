<h> Chris Ballard Ontario 's New Minister of the Environment and Climate Change <h> News Release <h> Chris Ballard Ontario 's New Minister of the Environment and Climate Change <h> Peter Milczyn Promoted to Cabinet as Minister of Housing <p> Premier Kathleen Wynne announced changes today to her cabinet , following the resignation of Glen Murray as Minister of the Environment and Climate Change . <p> Chris Ballard moves from Minister of Housing and Minister Responsible for the Poverty Reduction Strategy to become the new Minister of the Environment and Climate Change , leading Ontario 's fight against climate change by reducing greenhouse gas emissions and overseeing the province 's transition to a low-carbon economy . <p> Peter Milczyn , MPP for Etobicoke-Lakeshore , is promoted to cabinet , appointed as the new Minister of Housing . He will also take over as Minister Responsible for the Poverty Reduction Strategy . In these roles , Milczyn will oversee and build on Ontario 's Fair Housing Plan , making sure that people across Ontario are able to find a safe , affordable home . He will also work alongside Helena Jaczek @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ontario 's Basic Income Pilot and supporting people in Ontario to get ahead , find income security and break the cycle of poverty , particularly for children and youth . <p> In addition , the Office of Francophone Affairs for Ontario will become a standalone ministry , reflecting the importance of Ontario 's Francophone population to the province 's economic prosperity and its diverse , vibrant cultural fabric . Marie-France Lalonde will continue in her role as Minister of Francophone Affairs and Minister of Community Safety and Correctional Services . <p> Glen Murray will remain as MPP for Toronto Centre . <p> The changes announced today maintain the size of Ontario 's cabinet at 29 ministers .
