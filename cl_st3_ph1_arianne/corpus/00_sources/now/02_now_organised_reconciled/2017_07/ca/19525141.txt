Even at today 's " lacklustre " three-per-cent global growth rate , incomes/consumption would double in just 20 years and produce -- in this century -- dramatic climate change , widespread extinctions , the collapse of major biophysical systems , global strife and diminished prospects for continued civilized existence . <p> But even this threat is n't enough to move the world community to act sensibly to save itself . Like a mind-altering drug , the compound myth of perpetual growth and continuous technological progress obscures reality .
