<p> The provincial government has released the report card for the province 's students . <p> The results are fairly even across the board for the 2017 Transcripts of the provinces high-school students following their public exams . <p> The final grades , calculated based on a blend of school marks for the year and marks in the public exams , shows that final grades remained consistent for students throughout the province , with a slight increase from last year in Math , making it the highest ranked average at 79% . <p> Earth Systems continues to be the subject with the lowest provincial average , staying consistent from last year at 62% . That covers everything from geology and ecology to the study of climate change . <p> Overall , the province 's graduation rate is 93.3 per cent , the highest in recent years , and is expected to increase slightly more once all supplementary exams have been completed by August and November .
