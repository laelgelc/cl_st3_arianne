<h> Use power of the market to solve climate change <p> I agree that current government policies are not nearly enough to tackle the serious problem of climate change . The government is fearful of enacting effective policies . It seems to be easier to promote recycling or light bulb replacement . <p> Carbon fee-and-dividend is an elegant solution that the government can implement if it is serious about tackling climate change . A steadily-rising carbon pollution fee is assessed at the well-head , mine or port of entry . All the revenues collected are then returned to citizens in the form of a monthly dividend cheque . This system is a solution because the increasing carbon pollution fee means that low-carbon sources of energy become more competitive . The fact that the dividends are paid to citizens means that the revenues are recycled into the economy , giving people the means and opportunity to buy low-carbon alternatives .
