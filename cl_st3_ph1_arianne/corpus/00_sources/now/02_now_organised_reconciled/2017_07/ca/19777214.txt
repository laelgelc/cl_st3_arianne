<h> Small odds of reaching climate goal : researchers <p> There is a five-per cent chance of limiting average global warming to under 2 C , the target set in the 2015 climate-rescue Paris Agreement , researchers said on Monday . <p> And chances of meeting the lower , aspirational 1.5 C goal , also listed in the 196-nation pact , were a mere one percent , they wrote in the journal Nature Climate Change . <p> A U.S.-based expert team used projections for population growth to estimate future production , and related carbon emissions from burning fossil fuels . <p> Based on these data , " the likely range of global temperature increase is 2 C to 4.9 C , with median 3.2 C and a five percent chance that it will be less than 2 C , " they wrote . <p> Their calculations were not based on worst-case-scenario predictions of unabated energy use , the team said , and provided for ongoing provision for efforts to curb fossil fuel use . <p> However , it does not provide for the possibility of a sudden , massive shift @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ less than 1.5 C warming will require carbon intensity to decline much faster than in the recent past , " wrote the team . <p> The world 's nations concluded years of acrimonious negotiations two years ago to conclude the Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> They undertook to hold global warming to " well below " 2 C over pre-Industrial Revolution levels , and to strive for 1.5 C in a bid to stave off disastrous sea level rise , droughts , storms and other climate effects .
