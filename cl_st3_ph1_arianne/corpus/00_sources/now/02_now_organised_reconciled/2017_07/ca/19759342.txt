<h> Jason Kenney makes bid for leadership of United Conservative Party <p> Coming at no surprise , Jason Kenney announced his leadership bid for the United Conservative Party Saturday in both Calgary and Edmonton . <p> Just six and half hours before his event in Calgary he delivered an almost identical speech , with the same jokes , and even entered and exited the room -- just like earlier -- with country song ' Alberta Bound ' by Paul Brandt playing in the background . <p> Kenney said he 's the right person to lead the newly formed UCP government many times during his almost 40-minute speech , in between knocks at Justin Trudeau and the Liberals and the NDP provincial government . <p> " I offer you my passion , energy , work ethic and proven leadership , " he said . " I really believe this province needs not just somebody who can be Premier on day one , but somebody who can stand up to Justin Trudeau 's damaging policies that are hurting the province . " <p> He was met with a boisterous applause by about @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Markin MacPhail Centre . <p> He said Alberta 's hard working identity is " under assault " by the current government . <p> " It is being damaged every day by an ideological government that takes its inspiration from the failed theories of socialism , by a resentment of success , a distrust of enterprise , a mistaken belief that a powerful state is a greater force for good than strong families and robust civil society , " said Kenney . <p> He blamed the NDP for the economic downturn in the province , saying they " poured gasoline onto the fire of a recession " and cited the carbon tax as the " single biggest lie in Alberta political history . " <p> Later in the speech , he criticized the provincial curriculum as a means of bringing political agendas -- specifically that of the Liberals -- into the classroom .
