<p> It 's going to be a scorching week across the Lower Mainland and the Fraser Valley . <p> This morning Environment Canada issued a special weather statement warning of an " extended stretch of hot weather " . <p> Temperatures are expected to reach the mid to upper 30s by midweek in the Fraser Valley , the Howe Sound-Whister area , and on inland Vancouver Island . <p> It will be a few degrees lower in areas closer to Georgia Strait and Juan de Fuca Strait . <p> " Daily temperatures records from Tuesday to Thursday will probably be broken in many communities , " Environment Canada says in the statement . " And the all time records for the month of August may be threatened , too . " <p> Prolonged hot weather can elevate the risk of heat-related illness , according to Environment Canada , and everybody is at risk . <p> The alert recommends drinking plenty of water even before feeling thirsty .
