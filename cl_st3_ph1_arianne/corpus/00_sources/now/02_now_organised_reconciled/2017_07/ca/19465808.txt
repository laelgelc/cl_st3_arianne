<p> Britain will need to curb public spending further or raise taxes if leaving the European Union does long-term damage to economic growth , underscoring the importance of the country striking new trade deals , the government 's budget watchdog said on Thursday . <p> The Office for Budget Responsibility said ensuring robust trade agreements was more significant for the long-run health of Britain 's public finances than the size of any " divorce bill " to settle one-off liabilities with the EU . <p> " While some numbers mooted for it are very large , a one-off hit of this sort would not pose a big threat to fiscal sustainability .
