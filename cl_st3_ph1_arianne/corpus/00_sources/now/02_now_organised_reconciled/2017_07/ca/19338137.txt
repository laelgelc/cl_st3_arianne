The Butterflyway Project is an effort to re-imagine neighbourhoods as " highways of habitat " for local pollinating critters . <p> One of those canoes sits at the corner of Barton Ave . and Crawford St. , in Christie Pits Park . It holds about two dozen native plant species , and it 's one of about 40 canoes across the city . <p> " The intent of butterflyway project is to build highways of habitats for butterflies , " Butterflyway Project manager Jode Roberts said . " A corridor for bees and butterflies to move through any neighborhoods .
