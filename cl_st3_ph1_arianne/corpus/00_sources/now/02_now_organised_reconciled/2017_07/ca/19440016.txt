<h> UBC forest researcher posts video of harrowing wildfire escape <p> After filming her harrowing escape from the B.C. wildfires , a Vancouver forestry researcher worries that with ongoing climate change such devastation is now " the new normal " for the province . <p> Dr. Sally Aitken , associate dean for research and innovation at the University of B.C. 's Department of Forest and Conservation Sciences , said a two-week vacation to her recreational property in the west Chilcotin region was cut short Monday when smoke nearby grew and she and her husband decided to flee . <p> But around 8 p.m. , as they drove through the community of Hanceville , the flames grew larger and the smoke became black . <p> Aitken 's 45-second video , shot on Highway 20 just west of Hanceville , captures some of the most intense flames as they raced through the destruction for 40 minutes , she said . <p> In her short video , flames burn on both sides of the highway as Aitken urges her husband to manoeuvre away from them and speed up .
