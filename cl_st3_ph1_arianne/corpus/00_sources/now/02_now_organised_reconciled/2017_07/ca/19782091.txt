<h> NWT First Nations partner up to map out millions of hectares of wetlands <p> Members of First Nation communities in the Northwest Territories are partnering up with a conservation group to map out 31 million hectares of wetlands . <p> The data will be used to understand vegetation , animal habitat and to plan for climate change . <p> Marshes and swamps make up nearly half of the wetlands . <p> The project is costing approximately $500,000 to complete in partnership with Ducks Unlimited . <p> " The wetlands here are definitely in change , " said Kevin Smith , Boreal program manager for Ducks Unlimited . " Some of them have been impacted in the NWT by anthropogenic industry but primarily it has changed with climate change and all of the things that are going on -- we wanted to get a baseline data on what is going on now . As things change in the future we can have more information about that . " <p> The project will also be used to show elders the territory , and map out older communities .
