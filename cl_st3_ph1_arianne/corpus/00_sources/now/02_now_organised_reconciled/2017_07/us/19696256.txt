It 's the scallop capital of the world . <p> The city was a once a leader in the whaling industry . But even if you 've been there , you might not know New Bedford is home to the longest hurricane barrier on the East Coast -- an enormous feat of civil engineering . The barrier rises 20 feet above the surface of the water , totals 3.5 miles in length , and crosses New Bedford and Fairhaven harbors . <p> The sea wall was built back in the 1960s , after New Bedford was slammed by hurricanes several decades in a row .
