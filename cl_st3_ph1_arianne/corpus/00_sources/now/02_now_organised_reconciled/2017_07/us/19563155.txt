<p> Plum Island is mostly a national wildlife refuge . It 's a long , narrow spit of sand 50 miles north of Boston . <p> You can see evidence of climate change past and present there , and get a glimpse of the future . <p> The island was created by climate change . Over millions of years , natural forces made the planet a lot colder and the ocean sea level a lot lower . <p> Nowadays , it 's hard to believe Plum Island was once buried under half a mile of ice . Eleven thousand years ago , the glacier began receding , leaving a scarred landscape that was sculptured by wind , waves and currents into today 's barrier island beach . <p> Parker Jones , a frequent visitor to Plum Island , lives in nearby Newburyport .
