<h> An iceberg the size of Delaware has broken off Antarctica <h> It 's one of the largest icebergs ever recorded <p> A large crack seen in the Larsen C shelf photographed in August 2016 . Image : NASA <p> An iceberg roughly the size of Delaware and weighing more than a trillion tons has broken off an ice shelf on the Antarctic Peninsula . <p> The calving off of the 2,500-square-mile iceberg from the Larsen C ice shelf was detected and confirmed in data from NASA , but scientists say the iceberg was already floating before it separated and therefore has no immediate impact on sea level . The iceberg had been closely monitored by scientists for months , as a deep crack slowly extended for over 120 miles . On July 6th , satellite data showed that only 2.8 miles of ice kept the iceberg attached to the larger ice shelf , called Larsen C. <p> Larsen C is the fourth largest ice shelf in Antarctica with an area of about 20,000 square miles , according to NASA .
