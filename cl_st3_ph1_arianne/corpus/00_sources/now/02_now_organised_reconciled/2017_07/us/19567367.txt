<h> Lassoing the super power of geek chic , Comic-Con sees revenue growth <p> The annual Comic-Con International kicks off this week in San Diego . Comic-Con makes money selling tickets , renting floor space to vendors and exhibitors , and getting sponsors . GuideStar , which tracks nonprofits , says the convention has seen steady year-over-year revenue growth for the last 17 years , as comic culture has become central to pop culture . The most recent report puts convention revenue at $19 million , which exceeds its expenses .
