<h> Sucking Up CO2 Will Cost Hundreds of Trillions <h> Study finds that if the world does n't begin cutting emissions soon , the price of capturing and storing carbon dioxide will soar . <p> Unless we start cutting carbon dioxide emissions soon , it 's going to cost today 's young people as much as $535 trillion to clean up the atmosphere by 2100 , according to a study published on Tuesday evening . By way of context , that 's around seven times the size of the entire global economy . <p> In contrast , if the world starts reducing emissions 6 percent a year by 2021 , it will only cost $8 to $18.5 trillion to extract enough carbon dioxide to avoid the worst dangers of climate change , or $100 billion per year on the low end . <p> It is n't news that lowering greenhouse-gas emissions by switching to clean energy sources will ultimately be far less expensive and risky than any unproven technological means of sucking up carbon dioxide .
