<p> In February 1980 , a series of storms pounded Southern California . During storm surge and waves on Feb. 19th , the Lady Alexandra , a floating restaurant in Redondo Beach , " developed a 45-degree list and was in danger of capsizing , " according to the Los Angeles Times . <p> Large waves from storms a week earlier had already forced closure of the Lady Alexandra . The vessel was scuttled to prevent further damage . <p> Built in 1924 , the Lady Alexandra served as a steamship in British Columbia until 1952 . It was converted to a restaurant in 1960 . <p> Following the scuttling , the Lady Alexandra was declared a total loss .
