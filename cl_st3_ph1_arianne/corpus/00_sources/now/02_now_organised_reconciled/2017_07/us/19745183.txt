<h> Village of Elk Rapids gets " greener " with new resolution <p> The Watershed Grand Traverse Bay is applauding the Village of Elk Rapids for unanimously approving a Green Infrastructure Resolution . <p> That means , whenever possible , the village will be using green practices for future projects such as redoing roads , storm water systems and adding in some rain gardens . <p> It 's all in an effort to make water runoff cleaner before it enters the bay . <p> " This shows an example to other communities , to the citizens , that live there and the folks that come and visit that green infrastructure really something we think is going to help mitigate climate change , it 's going to help reduce the amount of storm water that goes into our bays , which is the reason people live , work , and play here , " said Christine Crissman with the Watershed Grand Traverse Bay . <p> Elk Rapids is the first community in the watershed to approve this resolution .
