It would force a one-time vote in the Legislature before 2024 , a referendum on spending future proceeds collected from the state 's cap-and-trade program . <p> The proposal is relatively small -- it 's only 253 words -- but has the potential to spark a very big debate on a notable part of Gov . Jerry Brown 's legacy : the climate cash that 's been a lifeline to high-speed rail . <p> About $1.5 billion of that has never been spent , and remains the focus of testy debates about using it to help shrink the state 's carbon emissions footprint .
