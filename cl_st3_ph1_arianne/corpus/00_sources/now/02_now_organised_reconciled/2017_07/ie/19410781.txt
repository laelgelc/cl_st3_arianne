<h> WATCH : Trailer for An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power <p> An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power is the follow-up to the 2006 environmental film The Inconvenient Truth , fronted by former U.S. Vice-President , Al Gore . The sequel will again target popular audiences to highlight the current status of global climate change and the energy revolution . <p> Producer T Bone Burnett and Gore found a collaborator in One Republic 's Ryan Tedder who wrote the title song for the film , ' Truth to Power . ' Tedder wrote the song from the perspective of Earth . He describes the origins of his inspiration , stating , " I wanted to take a break from writing songs about myself . So I took the point-of-view of mother earth turning the tables on those who 'd betray her . " <p> The film comes at a particularly appropriate time , following Trump 's recent decision to bow out of the Paris Agreement -- one of only three countries in the world ( Nicaragua , Syria , USA ) refusing to comply with efforts to limit global temperature increases .
