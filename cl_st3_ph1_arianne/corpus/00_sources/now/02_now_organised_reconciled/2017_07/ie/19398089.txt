<h> Citizens ' Assembly votes to end mandatory retirement age <h> The forum also recommends forcing all workers to join a pension scheme <p> Members of the Citizens ' Assembly vote on the wording of the ballots that were to be subsequently voted on , in the Grand Hotel , Malahide , Co Dublin . Photograph : Maxwell 's <p> There should be no mandatory retirement age and all workers should be made to join a pension scheme , the Citizens ' Assembly has recommended . <p> On Sunday , the 99-person forum voted overwhelmingly in favour of abolishing the practice of employers setting an age at which workers must retire , with 86 per cent of members opposing such measures . <p> There was even more support for ending the current anomaly whereby workers who retire at 65 can not access the State pension until they are 66 , with 96 per cent of members voting in favour of finding a remedy to this situation . <p> The assembly gathered in Malahide , Co Dublin , on Saturday and Sunday for a second weekend of discussions on how best @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ State 's ageing population . <p> On Sunday , 87 per cent of delegates voted in favour of introducing some form of mandatory pension scheme to supplement the State pension , given the poor levels of coverage among many workers . <p> The assembly did not make any recommendations as to the exact nature of such a scheme , which may take the form of an auto-enrolment regime as in the UK , where workers are able to opt out or change their contributions , or a compulsory scheme with no opt-out option . <p> The assembly also decided that there should be a " rationalisation " of private pension schemes , after delegates heard on Saturday that 160,000 such policies are currently available in Ireland . <h> State pensions <p> Members also voted for the State pension of ? The report is expected to be submitted to the Oireachtas in September for consideration . <p> The next sitting of the Citizens ' Assembly is scheduled for the weekend of September 30th , when members will commence deliberations on the State 's approach to climate change .
