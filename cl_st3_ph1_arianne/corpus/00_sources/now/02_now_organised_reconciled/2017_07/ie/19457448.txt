<h> A chunk of ice quarter the size of Wales breaks from the Antarctic <p> A VAST iceberg more than a quarter the size of Wales has broken away from an ice shelf in Antarctica . <p> The 2,200 sq mile chunk of ice , one of the largest ever recorded , has reduced the size of the Larsen C Ice Shelf by 12 per cent and changed the landscape of the Antarctic Peninsula forever , scientists say . <p> Although the breakaway of the trillion-tonne iceberg was a natural event , researchers on the Midas research project will be watching for signs of instability in the rest of the shelf . Lead investigator Prof Adrian Luckman of Swansea university said : ' Although we 're not aware of any link to human-induced climate change , this puts the ice shelf in a very vulnerable position . '
