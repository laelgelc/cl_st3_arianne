<h> Galway TD claims GLAS scheme is failing farmers <p> Galway Bay fm newsroom -- A Galway-Roscommon T.D has criticised the GLAS Scheme for failing farmers and planners . The Green Low-Carbon Agri-Environment Scheme focuses on the preservation of various habitats and species , mitigating climate change and improving water quality . However , Independent Deputy Michael Fitzmaurice says its implementation has been a fiasco . Deputy Fitzmaurice says that even when the technical issues surrounding the submissions were rectified , farmers were still not happy with the way it was handled .
