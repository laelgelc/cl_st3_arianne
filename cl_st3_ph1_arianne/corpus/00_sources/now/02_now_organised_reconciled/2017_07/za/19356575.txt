<h> Campaign afoot to clean-up Kuils River in W Cape <h> Tags : <p> The Western Cape Department of Water and Sanitation has embarked on a river cleaning campaign to mitigate the drought in the province . <p> Director , Debbie Hene , says they 'll be using volunteers to clean seven big rivers which have been polluted through human actions . Hene says cleaner rivers will help with the free flow of water to catchments areas . <p> A river clean-up campaign has been organised to educate people about the importance of protecting water sources . The campaign is part of Mandela Month commemorations . <p> Kuils River is one of eight rivers in the province earmarked for cleaning . It runs through a number of communities , therefore it is prone to pollution . <p> The Department of Water and Sanitation says water security , particularly in the face of global climate change , calls for a unified approach to protect rivers and wetlands . <p> Authorities says South Africa is the 38th driest country in the world .
