found -- well short of the 10-year average of 47,000 for the period or the 5,100 deaths in the first half of 2016 . <p> April floods and landslides in Colombia that claimed 329 lives were the deadliest single event . <p> Disasters inflicted a financial cost of around $41 billion in the first six months , Munich Re reported . <p> That was less than half of the $111 billion toll in the same
