<h> We 've bookmarked the best of Melbourne Writers Festival 2017 <p> August is a heavy month for culture lovers . No sooner have we recovered from the darkroom thrills of MIFF than we 're queuing up to have authors sign our best-loved paperbacks . <p> This year 's Melbourne Writers Festival program has launched and , as usual , offers a dizzying array of literary delights . More than 300 local and international authors are appearing at countless events , but we 've gone through and bookmarked some of our favourites . <h> Melbourne Writers Festival <h> The headliner <p> This year 's program seems to focus on interesting themes and issues , rather than big-name imports . There 's no Lionel Shriver , PJ Harvey or Salman Rushdie . There 's not even a Boris Johnson ( small mercies and all that ) . That said , American writer Joyce Carol Oates has a reputation for powerful -- and best-selling -- literary fiction including We Were The Mulvaneys , The Accursed and The Falls . US writer Megan Abbott is the award-winning author of eight crime novels , including last year 's thrilling You Will Know Me . She 's also a staff writer on The Deuce , the latest show from The Wire 's David Simon . Melbourne writers Sarah Schmidt and Sarah Bailey will also appear , discussing true crime novel See What I Have Done and addictive thriller The Dark Lake respectively . <p> Sarah Bailey .
