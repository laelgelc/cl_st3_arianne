<h> Great Barrier Reef not ' out of the woods ' <h> UNESCO has not listed the Great Barrier Reef as " in danger " . <p> The Great Barrier Reef has been left off UNESCO 's " in danger " list following a meeting of its World Heritage Committee . <p> It endorsed the federal government 's Reef 2050 Plan , which lists a series of measures designed to protect and manage the ecosystem . <p> But Greenpeace campaigner Alix Foster Vander Elst hit out at the decision and said while the government had been spared the embarrassment of having the reef listed as in danger , it 's strategy would not work because it did not address climate change .
