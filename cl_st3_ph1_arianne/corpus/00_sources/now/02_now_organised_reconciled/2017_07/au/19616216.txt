The rate of poverty is also higher if you live outside a metropolitan city : rural and regional poverty is a reality overlooked at the left 's peril . <p> Underemployment -- where people want to work more hours @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ year 12% of women , 7% of men and 18% of young workers will be struggling to find enough paid work hours to make ends meet . <p> Housing has never been so unaffordable . Tenants experience high rates of rental stress , spending more than one-third of their income on rent . Three-quarters of a million households are in mortgage stress and the number is rising dramatically . About 52,000 households are at risk of defaulting on their mortgage within the next 12 months . There is a crisis in housing availability and the waiting list for public housing in NSW alone is 60,000 . <p> Add to this the attacks on education , on welfare entitlements , the cuts to penalty rates and the slowest wage growth since the last recession and you can understand why young people are more and more distrustful of politicians and politics as usual . <p> Neoliberal capitalism is creating the preconditions for resistance , and while we have yet to see mass popular outbreaks , that is not to say that people are not resisting -- look at the response across regional @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , or the resistance by Aboriginal communities to state violence , stolen generations and land theft .
