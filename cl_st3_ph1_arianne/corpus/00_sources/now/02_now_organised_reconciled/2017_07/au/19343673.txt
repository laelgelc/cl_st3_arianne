<h> New model reconciles climate history with forecasts <p> Understanding the history of carbon emissions and the climate is vital to forecast their future . <p> Jochen Tack / Getty <p> How much will burning fossil fuels warm the planet ? It depends on two basic factors : the direct contribution the emissions make to higher temperatures through the greenhouse effect -- known as radiative forcing -- and the response of the complex interactions of the global climate system to that forcing , known as climate sensitivity . <p> Climate sensitivity , which predicts the total change in temperature in response to a given change in carbon dioxide levels , is the crucial and more controversial one . This week Harvard University scientists Cristian Proistosescu and Peter Huybers have made an important contribution to our understanding of it .
