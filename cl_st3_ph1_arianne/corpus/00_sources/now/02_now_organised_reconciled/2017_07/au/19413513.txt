<p> The pressure on companies to report on climate change risk may be intensifying but only 130 of the top 200 companies listed on the ASX have a climate change policy or emissions reduction target , according to the Australian Council of Superannuation Investors . <p> ACSI , which represents 37 industry superannuation funds , said in its tenth annual sustainability report that 70 ASX200 companies did not make any climate-related disclosures in 2016 . <p> " It 's disappointing that such a large number of companies did n't make any climate disclosure , " said Louise Davidson , chief executive of ACSI . " It 's unlikely that 70 would have no climate change exposure . " <p> ACSI measured climate change reporting across three realms : greenhouse gas emissions , a policy statement or other statement acknowledging climate change as a risk , and a climate change related or energy efficiency target .
