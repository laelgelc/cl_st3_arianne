<h> The Rapture theatre review ( Fortyfivedownstairs , Melbourne ) <p> There in only one true ' creator ' in the program for The Rapture . There are five performers involved in the production and countless other artists and contributors credited , only one of them will speak to us on stage . When she does , she informs us with no lack of certainty -- that the ' creator ' is she . <p> Moira Finucane 's gothic appropriation of a Catholic Mass , is a surreal and spectacular series of monologues , most of them addressing issues of climate change , feminism , and surprisingly , given the shape of the vehicle , only a little of religion . The high priest(ess)-creator is a role she 's made for herself , and she takes it with all the confidence you 'd expect of one of Melbourne 's most iconic performers . The action is delivered with an intensity that shifts at times between the charming and the vicious , the histrionic and the droll , the pathetic and the passionate . <p> Whether centre stage in stunning seven-inch @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Finucane 's celebrant is standing on the edge of a world in economic , environmental and erotic crisis . Whether ghoulish or girlish , she takes the audience through increasingly deconstructed elucidations of our modern , consumerist existence . <p> As per the Mass structure , this dramatic cabaret is led by an Old Testament-like speech of fury and wrath followed by a contemporary New Testament-like allegory ( in this case an amusing and pointed story of gender bias from her own professional artistic life ) , hymns , a great deal of incense and so on . It ends with a homily , a dark rye Eucharist , drinks at the bar , and Dusty Springfield 's pop hit I Only Want to Be With You pumping from the sound system as the audience danced the aftermath away . <p> The space has been transformed into a stark approximation of a Catholic church . Moira 's repeated nudity is very real . Two dominant statues -- crowns of thorns around their heads and donation boxes at their feet -- are giant auks ( an endangered species of penguin ) . <p> The theatrical trappings and the comfortable structure of Mass are particularly satisfying . I 've only been to a handful of such ceremonies and not of any particular religious persuasion , so the situation does n't necessarily add up to anything more than the sum of its parts for me . I 've found myself every bit as taken by the astounding design , presentation and ideas of actual religious rituals as I was with The Rapture . <p> The structure of the Mass , while not deconstructed in the work , is subverted by a gorgeous and at times comical design and a text that consistently provides surprises . The audience is jammed in cabaret style around the cross-shaped catwalk stage thrust as Finucane pulls out all the stops with her possessed and capricious tour-de-force performance . <p> I 'm not sure the work delivers anything I 'm not already @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ my Facebook wall and the muddied connections to the history of the Church do not make it easy to follow its episodic structure . The atmosphere is palpably one of excitement , the setting gorgeous , the performance and text clearly hard wrought and the choir , pianist , jewelry and sound are at times ethereal , but together they put our Creator 's plight and that of the planet just out of reach . <p> Still there is elevation , not least when Finucane sits at the altar as though by a pool in the sun , basking in only a bikini while her hair blows in the winds created by an ordinary house fan that we 've just watched her turn on . Then seconds later an unexpected cold transforms her into a freezing wreck unable even to switch off the fan . Through the pomp and ceremony it 's moments like these that cement The Rapture as a compelling dramatisation of deteriorating politics , a rallying cry to those who will suffer as the planet chokes and an incendiary study of our dystopian world .
