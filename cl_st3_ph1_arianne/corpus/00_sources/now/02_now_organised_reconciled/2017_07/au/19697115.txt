<p> We have no time to lose . Climate change is here and now , intensifying extreme weather . Rural and regional communities are on the front line . Our days are getting hotter , risking our access to water . <p> I have witnessed first-hand the dreadful anxiety communities go through when it 's unclear they 'll get the water they need to prosper . It 's not pretty . <p> Allegations that billions of litres of water from the Murray-Darling are being sucked out of the river without permission is a devastating kick in the guts for downstream farmers and their communities . <p> Decades of challenging negotiations appear to be have been totally disregarded by some .
