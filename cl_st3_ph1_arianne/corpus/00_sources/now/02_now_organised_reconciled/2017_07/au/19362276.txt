<p> New guidelines being presented to the G20 this weekend will change the way individuals , companies , investors and regulators manage the financial risks of climate change . These risks include physical events , such as changing weather patterns and natural disasters , as well as new technologies and regulations . <p> As big investors adopt the guidelines , the companies in their portfolios will be pressured to report on climate change . This will make it easier for investors of all kinds to understand the impacts of climate change on their portfolios , and to assess new opportunities , such as new products and services that will be required and developed . <p> Companies like BHP have already begun reporting on how climate change will affect their businesses . But , until now , corporate disclosure on climate change has been patchy , shallow and not always financially relevant . <p> The new guidelines will create a common language for talking about climate change as a financial risk .
