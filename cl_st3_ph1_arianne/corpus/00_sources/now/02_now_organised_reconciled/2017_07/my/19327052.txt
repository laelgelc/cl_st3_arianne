Ahmad Yakob said today the state government never authorised a carbon credit trading company red-flagged by Bank Negara Malaysia ( BNM ) to collect money from the public for a project to reduce the " carbon footprint " . <p> He said anyone who made payments to the company should lodge a report with the police . <p> " The state government has been accused of collecting money illegally under the
