<h> Early warnings may blunt trauma of displacement due to flooding <p> ( Reuters Health ) - Early flood warnings reduce the mental health impacts of being forced to flee home because of rising waters , a UK study suggests . <p> " Since people are often displaced from home and live in different places for many months , services in areas not directly affected by flooding should be alert to this increased risk and offer support as necessary , " Dr. Angie Bone , the head of Public Health England ( PHE ) ' s Extreme Events team told Reuters Health . <p> " A warning of at least 12 hours prior to flooding may reduce risks and is why we encourage everyone to sign up to the Environment Agency 's free flood warning service " ( bit.ly/1sL3t67 ) , Bone said by email . <p> Heavy rainfall across southern England in the winter of 2013-2014 washed away railroad tracks , wiped out power in many areas and forced thousands out of their homes , Bone and her colleagues write in Lancet Planetary Health .
