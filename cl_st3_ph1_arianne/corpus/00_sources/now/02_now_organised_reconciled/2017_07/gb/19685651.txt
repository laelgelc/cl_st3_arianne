<h> Dream of exploring Greenland ? Here 's how to make sure you travel there responsibly <h> Tourism is growing in some of the world 's increasingly fragile places , Greenland included . <h> Share this <p> There 's a travellers ' saying that " when you 've seen the world , there 's always Greenland " . For tourists , the Arctic island can feel like the kind of place only a few people go , but now it 's becoming an increasingly popular destination . <p> It 's no surprise really . Whether you 're exploring the fjords by boat , hiking to the edge of the ice sheet , or stopping off at isolated fishing villages , the remote wilderness of Greenland feels unlike anywhere else on earth . And depending on the time of year you visit , it can feel like a vastly different place . In summer tourists can experience 24 hour sunlight , and it 's much greener in the south , while it 's possible to catch a glimpse of the Northern Lights during winter , when temperatures can be harsh . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . With a popular of 56,000 -- around the same as Hereford -- but a land mass of 836,330 square miles , the world 's largest island is vast and mostly unoccupied , except for the ice . <p> The largest mass of ice in the northern hemisphere ( which in places is an incredible two miles thick , and makes up 85% of Greenland ) is -- at its current rate of melting -- adding 1mm a year to ocean levels globally . Scientists are concerned this could speed up further , as warmer conditions are apparently encouraging algae to grow and darken the ice 's surface . <p> While tourism is still comparatively low ( which adds to its charm ) , in 2015 there were 23.8% more tourists in Greenland than the previous year , and 9.9% more in 2016 . The tourist board is predicting continued growth in coming years . <p> So , if you do want to experience the great wilderness of Greenland , how can you make sure you do so responsibly ? Bear in mind you probably ca n't fly direct <p> It goes without saying for any destination that you should fly direct when possible to mininise carbon emissions , but for Greenland it is n't easy . You can fly directly from Copenhagen ( Greenland is a self-governing administrative division of Denmark ) and Iceland . So look at greener ways to get to Copenhagen , or combine your trip with a holiday in Iceland . Air Greenland covers virtually every inhabited corner of the island , but be sure to minimise the amount of internal flights you take -- iceberg spotting from the sea is much more picturesque anyway . <h> 3 .
