<p> The first ever African director general of the World Health Organisation assumes office on Saturday . The election of Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus , or as he is popularly known , Tedros , by an overwhelming majority of this year 's World Health Assembly is rightly hailed as historic . This was the first time that each of WHO 's 194 member states got a direct say in selecting the world 's top health leader .
