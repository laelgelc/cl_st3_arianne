<p> Poland 's rightwing government is pulling out all the stops for what it sees as its greatest foreign policy achievement to date : a visit to Warsaw today by US president Donald Trump . The idea is to make the big man feel as good about himself as possible , which will hopefully benefit Poland in some way , such as a more categorical assertion that Nato would -- under US leadership -- protect Poland from any aggression from Moscow . <p> PiS is working hard to tickle Trump 's ego .
