<h> ' Total lack of clarity ' over what will happen to UK 's relationship with European nuclear body Euratom post-Brexit <p> The Government says Britain will leave Eurotom because it is under the jurisdiction of the European Court of Justice . <p> There is a total lack of clarity about the implications of our relationship with the European nuclear body Euratom as we prepare for Brexit . <p> The Government says Britain will leave the agency -- which governs the movement of radioactive material in Europe -- because although not part of the EU , it is under the jurisdiction of the European Court of Justice . <p> The NHS depends increasingly on nuclear materials . Radioactive isotopes are crucial for many clinical investigations , for example , diagnostic scanners , as well as for research . <p> Cancer patients in the UK will soon be faced with inadequate treatments . Many others will face increasingly inadequate diagnostic tests . <p> But it is not just British healthcare which is threatened .
