The fungus fetches up to Rs 2,800,000 ( ? 20,000 ) per kg in raw form . During the peak season of yarsha collection , locals drop everything to pursue fungus hunting , including their usual profession . Even schools remain closed during yarsha collecting seasons . <p> The fungus grows on the head of the larvae of caterpillars and locals crawl on hands and knees to find it .
