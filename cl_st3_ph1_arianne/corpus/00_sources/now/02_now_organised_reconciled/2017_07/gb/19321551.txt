<h> Investigation after buzzard found in trap in Inverness-shire <p> A buzzard has been freed after being found in an illegal trap in Inverness-shire . <p> The RSPB called the incident a " flagrant incident of raptor persecution " -- while police are now investigating the incident . <p> The inspector leading the investigation said there had been " nothing accidental " about the trap which the raptor was found in . <p> The buzzard was found on the south slopes of Beinn Bhrac , above Strathdearn and the River Findhorn , in early June . <p> It was found by a member of the public , who released the bird -- suffering a minor injury in the process . <p> Inspector Mike Middlehurst said : " It is very disappointing to have an incident like this reported , especially when there is a great deal of positive work going on in the Highlands to tackle wildlife crime . <p> " Unfortunately , there are some who continue to deliberately target birds of prey ; there is nothing accidental in the setup of these traps . <p> " I am @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the bird and for their assistance in trying to free it . <p> " They were slightly injured in the process of releasing the bird and had the knowledge to photograph it .
