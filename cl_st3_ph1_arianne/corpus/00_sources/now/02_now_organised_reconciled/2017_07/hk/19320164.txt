<h> Navigating future forward change <p> Schroders believes sustainable investing is all about allocating capital for the future . <p> As Environmental , Social and Corporate Governance ( ESG ) moves up investors ' agenda , there is a growing trend in the industry towards products that track a sustainability index . <p> But Jessica Ground , Global Head of Stewardship at Schroders , thinks an active approach is needed to capture the benefits of ESG investing . <p> She explains that ESG investing is incredibly complex , for example , incorporating climate change into an investment strategy has many different aspects ranging from governments ' responses to climate change , to how consumers may modify their own behaviour . <p> " It is very hard to do this passively , " she says . <p> Ms. Ground thinks another issue with ESG indexes is that they tend to be quite backwards looking , based on historic data . <p> " ESG is really about navigating future forward change .
