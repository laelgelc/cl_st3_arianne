<p> In her final report as Parliamentary Commissioner for the Environment , Jan Wright proposes New Zealand follow the lead of the UK , which introduced its Climate Change Act with overwhelming support in the House of Commons in 2008 . <h> Recommendations <p> Dr Wright 's report is critical of a lack of a process to get New Zealand to its climate change reduction target - 11 percent below 1990 levels by 2030 under the Paris Agreement . <p> She said putting climate change targets into law would make current and future governments accountable for actually reducing emissions . <p> Other recommendations include : <p> A Climate Change Transition Bill including at least one greenhouse gas emissions reduction target and a requirement that it be met <p> The Bill introducing a limit on carbon credits purchased from overseas <p> Setting up a new body , the Climate Change Commission <p> Five-yearly carbon budgets , recommend by the Commission and set by the Climate Change Minister , consistent with climate change targets .
