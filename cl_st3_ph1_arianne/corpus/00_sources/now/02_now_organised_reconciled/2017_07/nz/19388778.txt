<p> International leaders are trying to keep their feet on the pedal to reduce global warming after President Donald Trump withdrew the United States from the Paris Agreement , the global accord to counter climate change . <p> At a federal level , the United States may feel it is being disadvantaged by this international deal - but individual states are now taking up the fight themselves , including Hawaii . <p> The beautiful island state relies on its natural assets to attract visitors . Tourism is the state 's top industry . On the island of Oahu , steep sharp-edged vegetation-covered hills drop to flat green pastures and a postcard coastline . Plenty of reefs , golden sandy beaches and surfing . <p> And these prized natural features are among those threatened by the effects of climate change . <p> In the past month , Hawaii became the first US state to introduce laws to bring its environmental standards in line with the Paris climate accord . <p> " We are the testing grounds , " Hawaii Governor David Ige said before signing the document . <p> " Tides @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bleaching , coastlines are eroding , weather is becoming more extreme .
