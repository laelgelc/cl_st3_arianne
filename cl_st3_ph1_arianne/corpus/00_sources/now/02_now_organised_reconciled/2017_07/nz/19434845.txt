<h> Fiji plastic bag surcharge welcomed <p> An environmentalist has commended the Fuel Retailers Association in Fiji for implementing a nationwide surcharge on plastic bags at all convenience stores and service stations . <p> The Ministry of Fisheries and Forests said the association would introduce a 10 cent surcharge for single use plastic bags at 75 service stations from next month . <p> The Fiji based environmentalist , Adrian Midwood , said Fiji had had a plastic problem for decades . <p> He said he was pleased to see a surcharge being implemented as a deterrent . <p> " By directly hitting the pocket of the consumer I think we actually have a chance of minimising the plastic bags waste that 's going onto the beach which ends up in our oceans and affects our marine wildlife .
