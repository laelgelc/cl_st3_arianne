It will help Trees That Count towards its goal of planting 4.7 million native trees in 2017 - one for every New Zealander . <p> " Our new partnership with Z goes deeper than the climate change agenda though - it 's about the vast array of benefits environmental conservation provides our country and about safeguarding it for future generations , " Tanya said . <p> " We encourage New Zealanders to help us boost the live native tree count being recorded at http : **27;864;TOOLONG by fuelling up at Z on July 13.
