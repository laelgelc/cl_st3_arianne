<p> A group of beefy lads , in posh-looking school uniforms featuring brightly striped blazers , trooped into the food court at Dunedin Airport . I guess they were a visiting sports team . <p> They settled themselves at the small tables around us , four to a table . After a few minutes they 'd stopped talking to each other , their forearms were on the tables , and their heads were lowered so they were almost touching . <p> Were they in prayer ?
