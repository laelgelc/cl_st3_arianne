Yet today there is a stark , fundamental disconnect and erosion of trust between wider society and governments , big business and public institutions . There is a view that the latter groups are unaccountable , ignore impacts and failures and , in some cases , are even corrupt . <p> Many see a complacency in these organisations and a failure to make the case for their beneficial contribution , beyond shareholder returns . Business thought it was enough to pay taxes , pay employees and issue dividends . My fear is that , as a result , the world is in danger of reverting to a closed and short-term mindset , driven by an undercurrent of protectionism . It is time for a fresh period of renewal . <p> To rebuild trust , we need a new covenant between business , policymakers and the rest of society . <p> For business , we must do more to engender trust . This starts with transparency on the taxes we pay , the supply chains we use , the people we employ and the structures under which we operate . It is not enough to adhere to global regulations , we must strive to go further and advocate for more . <p> Only then can we be accountable , and legitimately demand that of others - including governments . While this should be true for all businesses , in all sectors , it is especially true for natural resources .
