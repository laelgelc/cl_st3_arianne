<h> Stu Kneebone : Strategy looks at water issues <p> It is council 's response to the challenges we as a region face with how we manage our freshwater resources over the coming decades . is top of mind for all of us . <p> A key part of the strategy is about working with others and advocating to central government for a broader range of tools to assist us in making better decisions when it comes to allocating the rights to take and use fresh water . <p> Regional councils are currently very limited with what they can and ca n't do when granting a consent to take water . <p> The
