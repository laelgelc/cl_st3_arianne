<h> Councils ' climate change message for the Government <p> Mayors from around the country are meeting in Auckland where they have laid out a pre-election wish-list and a commitment to tackling climate change . <p> More than 600 delegates are at the Local Government New Zealand conference for two days in Auckland , which is followed by an annual meeting on Tuesday . <p> Dunedin mayor Dave Cull and Nelson mayor Rachel Reese are seeking to be the new LGNZ president to replace Lawrence Yule , who steps down after nine years in the role . <p> As the conference opened on Sunday Mr Yule unveiled the LGNZ election manifesto . <p> Hot topics in the manifesto include how to the fund infrastructure , improvements to risk and resilience and new ways of protecting water . <p> Mr Yule also launched a position statement on climate change , and a 2017 climate change declaration signed by 39 mayors . LGNZ represents about 78 councils . <p> " Local government is committed to playing its role by utilising the full range of skills and capabilities it holds to better understand @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to consider climate change in its decision making , " Mr Yule said . <p> " But this is a problem of national scale , in need of a joint , national response . In our view we need to see more from central Government on climate change . " <p> At the conference there is a focus on litter legislation , local government funding , cat management and health in this year 's remits . Remits are voted on in a secret ballot and if passed will become official policy and be actioned by LGNZ.
