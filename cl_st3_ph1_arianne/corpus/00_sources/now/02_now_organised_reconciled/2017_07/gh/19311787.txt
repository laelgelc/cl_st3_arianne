<p> Ghana risks missing the opportunity to access an estimated US$320 million Green Climate Fund ( GCF ) , meant to mitigate effects of climate change , an Economic Officer of the Finance Ministry , Robert Mensah , has said . <p> This is due to the inability of organizations to submit workable proposals to access the fund and manage it . <p> The GCF , is an international financing mechanism under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , designed to assist developing countries to support low emission and climate-resilient programmes and projects . <p> The fund also aims to enhance the resilience of people , enterprises and ecosystems in developing countries to the adverse impacts of climate change . <p> Speaking to the Goldstreet Business at a Climate Finance and Integrity Seminar organized by the Ghana Integrity Initiative , Mensah explained that the major challenge for many institutions , that applied to manage the fund , was their inability to meet the proposal criteria . <p> Out of 50 institutions , which applied to manage the fund in 2015 , only two , Ecobank @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ accreditation to manage it .
