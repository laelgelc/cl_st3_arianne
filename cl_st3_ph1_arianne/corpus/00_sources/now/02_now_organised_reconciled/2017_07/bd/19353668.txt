Summit may not be China 's time to fill US shoes - but the moment is comingGetty Images <h> This contest for world leadership could not come at a worse time <p> The world 's 20 biggest economic powers are about to gather in Hamburg for a two-day summit pondering whether they should still look to the US for global leadership . <p> The summit , ostensibly about financial stability , could mark the moment of the US 's formal abdication as the world 's pre-eminent power . The task of leadership will be seen to be passed not to a single successor , a reluctant China is not ready , but to a new unstable quartet of Donald Trump , Xi Jinping , Vladimir Putin , and Angela Merkel . <p> This contest for world leadership could not come at a worse time . Too many lights are flashing red on the global dashboard : climate change , North Korea , world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gulf . Yet the quartet comes to the summit , starting on Thursday night , with very different aims . <p> Merkel , the serene host , had planned to use it to lift the world 's gaze to the long-term challenge posed by migration from an increasingly populated and youthful Africa but instead finds herself firefighting . <p> ' Terrible error ' <p> Merkel is not keen on being leader of the liberal west , describing such labels as " grotesque " and choosing to be seen in the constant company of the French president , Emmanuel Macron , to dilute the impression of German dominance of Europe . <p> Trump in turn continues to attack the German trade surplus , ridicule climate change , mull curbs on German steel imports and insist global politics is a Darwinian battle in which each leader must think first and only of their national interest . <p> Trump will be unconcerned if he is outnumbered since it will only deepen his narrative of America as a victim , a story that works with his political base . His focus will be on his meeting with Vladimir Putin on Friday .
