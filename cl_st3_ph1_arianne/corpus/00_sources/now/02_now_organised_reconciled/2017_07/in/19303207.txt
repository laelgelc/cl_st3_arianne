Estimates suggest that cities , which account for over 70 percent of energy-related greenhouse gas emissions , grow by about one million inhabitants every other week . Increasingly recognized as the place for " opportunities " , cities now apart from trying to keep pace with the mass influx witnessed , also have to deal with the climate implications this urban migration brings along . <p> Having said that , cities have the potential to be major incubators for climate change solutions .
