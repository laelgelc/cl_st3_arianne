Jain , chairman of the Indian Green Building Council ( IGBC ) , congratulated the capital 's Metro network by saying , " Delhi Metro is the first to become a green Metro . The platinum ratings that they have got are hard-earned and a lot of toil has gone into the process . " <p> The Delhi Metro Railway Corporation ( DMRC ) has become the world 's first ever Metro system to get a green certification for all its major buildings and installations .
