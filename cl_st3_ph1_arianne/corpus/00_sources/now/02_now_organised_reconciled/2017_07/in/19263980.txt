<h> PCMC invites bids for energy project at Moshi <p> Pimpri Chinchwad : The Pimpri Chinchwad Municipal Corporation has failed to initiate the waste-to-energy project at the Moshi garbage depot even one-and-half years after it was proposed . <p> Nearly 800 tonnes of garbage is dumped every day at the depot . Over the past 15 years , the civic body planned two waste-to-energy projects . But none of them has materialized . <p> Hoping to be successful in its third attempt , the PCMC , which plans to provide five acres of land at the Moshi garbage depot , has invited fresh bids for implementing the project for a period of 20 years on the design , finance , build , operate and transfer basis . <p> The contracted private agency will have to implement the project as per solid waste management rules , 2016 , published by the ministry of environment , forests and climate change . <p> According to pollution control rules , power generation projects should have no atmospheric emission except combustion gases , with pollutant level within the limits mentioned by the Maharashtra Pollution Control @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to officials of the environment department of the PCMC , the best conversion technology will be used .
