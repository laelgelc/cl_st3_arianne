<h> The melting of Gangotri glacier has been studied by the Indian Space Research Organisation . <p> Gangotri glacier retreated by 0.15 sq km between 2007 and 2016 , the Rajya Sabha was informed on Monday . <p> Environment Minister Harsh Vardhan in a written reply said the melting of Gangotri glacier has been studied by the Indian Space Research Organisation ( ISRO ) by taking Survey of India maps of 1962 as the base . <p> He said that the area of Gangotri glacier was estimated as 224.42 sq km from the maps of 1962 , while from 1990 onwards satellite data was used to monitor the Himalayan glaciers . <p> " The study by the ISRO during last four decades has revealed that loss in area of Gangotri glacier during the years : ( i ) 1962 and 2005 was 3.19 sq km ; ( ii ) 1990 and 2007 was 0.13 sq km and ( iii ) 2007 and 2016 was 0.15 sq km , " he said . <p> Major initiatives taken under the National Mission for Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem ( NMSHE ) under the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ establishment of a Centre for Himalayan Glaciology at the Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology in Dehradun and setting up of six thematic task forces , he said . <p> They include natural and geological wealth , water , ice , snow including glaciers , micro flora and fauna and wildlife and animal population , Himalayan agriculture , traditional knowledge system and forest resources and plant diversity . <p> He said state climate change centers have been set up in Jammu and Kashmir , Himachal Pradesh , Uttarakhand , Manipur , Mizoram , Tripura , Sikkim , Arunachal Pradesh , Nagaland , Meghalaya and West Bengal . <p> He added that as per the fifth assessment report ( AR5 ) of Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) published in 2014 , globally land and ocean surface temperature has risen by 0.85 degrees Celsius ( 0.65 to 1.06 degrees ) over the period of 1880 to 2012 . <p> Many extreme weather and climate events like heat waves , heavy precipitation and severe weather phenomena have been observed since about 1950 , he said . <p> According to the India @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ temperatures across the globe , all India mean temperature has risen by nearly 0.60 degrees Celsius over the last 110 years .
