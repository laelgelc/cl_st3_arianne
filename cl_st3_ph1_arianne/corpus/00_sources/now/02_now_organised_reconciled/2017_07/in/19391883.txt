Police said that they have got leads in the cases . <p> In the last week , desecration of 10 religious structures has been reported in the state with the maximum being from the Salcete belt . <p> A senior police officer said that as most of the acts of vandalism were carried out in the wee hours it has been difficult to identify the culprits . " We have got leads and are working on them , " the officer said and added that the leads have come from CCTV footage .
