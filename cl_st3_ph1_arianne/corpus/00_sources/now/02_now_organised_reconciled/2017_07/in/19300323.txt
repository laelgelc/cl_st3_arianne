<h> Tadoba buffer areas open for tourism <p> TNN Jul 4 , 2017 , 05.52 AM IST <p> Nagpur : With Tadoba core remaining closed for tourists this monsoon , there is some silver lining by visiting the buffer areas of the park . <p> From Tuesday , the deputy director ( buffer ) has thrown open tourism from all gates of Agarzari-Devada and Junona . Other areas like Navegaon ( Ramdegi ) and Kolara will also remain open depending on the motorability of vehicles . As of now , sources said roads are not motorable at these two gates . <p> The entry will be on first-come-first-serve basis with no reduction in the carrying capacity . Tourists will be allowed to move in tourism zones on motorable routes . <p> Officials said private vehicles will not be allowed and they will have to hire Gypsies available at the gates . Even as NTCA has asked to shut monsoon tourism in the core , there are no restrictions in the buffer . <p> " Tourism will be allowed depending on the local weather conditions , " said officials . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not likely to follow suit stating that buffer zone of Pench i.e. Mansinghdeo Wildlife Sanctuary has been notified as critical tiger habitat ( CTH ) and its status is no more a buffer zone now . <p> Under the provisions of Wildlife ( Protection ) Act 1972 , as amended in 2006 , a tiger reserve consists of core or critical tiger habitat and the buffer/peripheral area .
