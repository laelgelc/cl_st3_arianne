<p> Meenaben used to dread the onset of summer every year . Her small two-room home in a slum in Ahmedabad would get so hot that she could not sit indoors for several hours in the day , even with a ceiling fan running . <p> Two months ago , however , she agreed to an experiment : a non-profit organisation that works in the Ramesh Dutt Colony where she lives , on the outskirts of the city , painted her tin roof with white reflective paint , to try to reduce the heat . <p> The simple change worked .
