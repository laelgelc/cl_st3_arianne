<h> Move to ensure Madayipara 's conservation raises concern <p> Thiruvarkkattu Devaswom , controlled by Chirakkal family fear that the land would be vested with the government . <p> KANNUR : While the campaign to protect Madayippara , the laterite plateau in the district known for its biodiversity , is gaining momentum , the move to notify the area as a biodiversity protection centre has raised concerns for the Thiruvarkkattu Devaswom , controlled by the Chirakkal family here , as they fear the land would be vested with the government.However , the protection would be carried out without taking over the land , said the people who mooted the idea .
