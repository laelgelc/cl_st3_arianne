<p> Minister of State for Cooperatives Mir Zahoor Ahmad today chaired a meeting of Jammu and Kashmir State Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development ( SCARD ) Bank to review its overall functioning . <p> He also inspected the proposed site for Head Office of SCARD Bank , Srinagar .
