These essentially include methods that , among other things , protect and enhance the crops and the soil , improve water absorption and use efficient seed treatments . While Indian farmers have traditionally followed these principles , new technology now makes them more effective . <p> For example , for soil enhancement , certified biodegradable mulch films are now available . A mulch film is a layer of protective material applied to soil to conserve moisture and fertility . Most mulch films used in agriculture today are made of polyethylene ( PE ) , which has the unwanted overhead of disposal . It is a labour intensive and time-consuming process to remove the PE mulch film after usage . If not done , it affects soil quality and hence , crop yield . An independently certified biodegradable mulch film , on the other hand , is directly absorbed by the microorganisms in the soil . It conserves the soil properties , eliminates soil contamination , and saves the labor cost that comes with PE mulch films . <p> The other perpetual challenge for India @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crops like rice and sugarcane have a high-water requirement . Again , Indian farmers have long experience in water conservation that can now be enhanced through technology . <p> Seeds can now be treated with enhancements that help them improve their root systems . This leads to more efficient water absorption . <p> In addition to soil and water management , the third big factor , better seed treatment , can also significantly improve crop health and boost productivity .
