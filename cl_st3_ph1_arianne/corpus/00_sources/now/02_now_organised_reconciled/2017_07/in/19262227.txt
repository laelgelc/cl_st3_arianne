The opening ceremony was held at Maquinez palace , ESG Complex , in the city at the hands of Dr Cesare Onestini , Charge d'Affaires , Delegation of European Union to India , ESG Vice Chairman Rajendra Talak , and ESG General Manager Mrunal Niket Walke . Speaking on the occasion , Dr Cesare Onestini , Charge d'Affaires , Delegation of European Union to India , said the relation is becoming stronger between India and the European Union in the field of Arts , Literature and movies too . He thanked Directorate of Film Festival for having such type of union and event . He also thanked Club members and the audience present .
