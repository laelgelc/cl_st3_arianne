<h> Green panel gives nod to runway extension <p> PTI Jul 20,2017 , 05:08 AM IST <p> Hyderabad : A committee under the ministry of environment , forests and climate change accorded permission for the extension of runway at the Vijayawada Airport to meet the current demand and facilitate landing and take-off of bigger aircrafts . <p> A proposal submitted by the Airport Authority of India ( AAI ) said , the existing airport is currently handling about 40 operations per day ( 20 landings and 20 take-offs ) and operating capacity of the airport is about 300 passengers . In annualised capacity , the airport capacity is 3.60 lakh passengers per annum . <p> After expansion , the airport capacity will be 7.50 lakh .
