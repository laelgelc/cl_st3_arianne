They ( poachers ) are foiling all our techniques . They develop techniques to overpower our systems . This is a big fight , " Vardhan said . <p> " We had over 1 lakh tigers a century ago , now are happy with just 2,226 , " the minister said on the occasion of World Tiger Day . <p> India is a part of
