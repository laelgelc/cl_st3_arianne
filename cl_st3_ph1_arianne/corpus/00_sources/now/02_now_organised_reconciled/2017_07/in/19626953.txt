<h> Over 1 lakh footfalls in four days of Science Express exhibition at CSTM <p> -- By karishma ravindran Jul 23 , 2017 12:13 am <p> FOLLOW US : <p> Mumbai : The brain behind the 16 air-conditioned coaches of Science Express train is a group of 17 Nobel laureates from Germany . They transformed a Garib Rath express train into a science exhibition show on wheels using the concept of tunnel . <p> Till the last day of the exhibition on Monday , the train has attracted more than one lakh visitors . <p> " The group of 17 laureates derived the idea of a mobile science exhibition after they constructed a stationed science tunnel . This science tunnel was showcased in 17 countries , " said Nitin Tiwane , the manager ( operation ) of Science Express . <p> The concept of tunnel help the visitors view the exhibition by giving attention to details . It took three months for the group to re-module the train into science exhibition coaches . <p> Science Express mobile exhibition was made possible through the vast network of Indian Railways . " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ exhibition in every state of India . Hence , the railway route was the best way to spread awareness of science , " Tiwane said . <p> The theme of the exhibition is climate action and the train is stationed at Chhtrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus ( CSTM ) . The four-day exhibition saw people from all walks of life including teachers , students , nursery students and parents . <p> " I have been able to understand the concept of climate change in a better way after I visited this exhibition . I wish to visit more of such exhibitions in future , " said Renika Rebello , a student of Class X at a school in Thane . <p> The train was divided into eight phases consisting of different subjects ranging from science and technology to bio-diversity . <p> Around 50 personnel are deployed inside the coaches and are known as science communicators . They help visitors in understanding the displays better and travel for 206 days of the year visiting different states of India . <p> " They explain the concepts of science to students and others who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is the ninth year of the Science Express exhibition at CSTM . The train is a joint initiative of Department of Science and Technology , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change and other science institutes . <p> The train first chugged into Delhi junction railway station on October 30 , 2007 . The first theme for the exhibition was based on universe and the Big Bang theory . The later years saw the changing themes ranging from bio diversity , conservation of environment and global issues . <p> In India , the first train featuring science exhibition called ' Vigyan Rail ' was flagged off by the Department of Science and Technology in 2003-2004.
