<h> Interest of Goa first , wo n't allow Vasco to be a coal hub : Parrikar <p> TNN Jul 27 , 2017 , 08:55 AM IST <p> Porvorim : Chief minister Manohar Parrikar , on Thursday , told the Goa legislative assembly that his government would not allow Vasco to become a coal hub . " The interest of Goa is of paramount importance to me . I will not allow pollution to happen , " said Parrikar in response to the question tabled by Curtorim MLA Aleixo Reginaldo Lourcenco . <p> The Curtorim MLA said that the state government must intervene at the highest level to stop Vasco from turning into a coal hub , to which Parrikar said he would write to the ministry of environment forest and climate change and place before it the government 's reservations . <p> " I 'm confident that it will not overrule my observations , " Parrikar said .
