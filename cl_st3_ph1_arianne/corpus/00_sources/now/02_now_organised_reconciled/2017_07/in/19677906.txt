<h> SC to K'taka : Submit details of Kalsa work by July 31 <p> Panaji : The Supreme Court has directed Karnataka to submit chronological details of the Kalsa project work carried out so far by July 31 on a petition filed by Mhadei Bachao Abhiyan ( MBA ) , Goa , regarding damming and diversion of the Kalsa Bhandura Project . <p> On July 24 , the petition filed by MBA in 2007 came for hearing before the Supreme Court bench of Justice Madan Lokur and Justice Deepak Gupta . <p> Counsel for Karnataka state has agreed to submit details of the work completed by the Karnataka Neeravari Nigam Limited in Kankumbi , from 2006 . <p> MBA has filed a petition seeking direction to restrain Karnataka from proceeding with the construction of the Kalsa Bhandura Project in the Western Ghats . <p> The MBA alleged that work has been undertaken without approval under the Forests Conservation Act , 1980 , and other related clearances .
