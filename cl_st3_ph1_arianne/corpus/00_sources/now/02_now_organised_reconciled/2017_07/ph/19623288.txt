<h> Creation of Architect Office in LGUs sought <h> Creation of Architect Office in LGUs sought <p> CLARK FREEPORT -- Officials of the United Architects of the Philippines are calling for the creation of Architect 's Office in local government units ( LGUs ) . <p> The UAP officials , headed by National President Architect Guillermo Hisancha , disclosed this during the Joint Induction Ceremonies of the UAP District A2B at the Widus Convention Center here recently . <p> Hisancha said that the UAP has sent communications to the Department of Interior and Local Government ( DILG ) for the full implementation of the Republic Act 9266 or the Architecture Act of 2004 . <p> UAP National Executive Vice President Benjamin Panganiban , Jr . said that the Local Government Code provides for the establishment of city and municipal architect that can work hand in hand with city local government engineers and building officers . <p> Hisancha meanwhile revealed his intention to unite all Filipino architects all over the world . <p> The UAP head said his vision is anchored on ideals of UAP -- Unity , Accountability , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ UAP for the organization 's accomplishments . <p> " We made another history which will make us motivated to achieve better performance and follows with rendered service .
