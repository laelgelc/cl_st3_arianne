<h> Farmers to insure crops amid pest infestation <h> Farmers to insure crops amid pest infestation <p> Saturday , July 29 , 2017 <p> The Office of the Provincial Agriculturist ( OPA ) reminded farmers in Negros Occidental to insure their farms to lessen possible crop losses amid pest infestation affecting some rice fields in the province . <p> Provincial Agriculturist Japhet Masculino said Friday the provincial government , in partnership with the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation , has been providing indemnity claims to farmers covered by the Negros First Universal Crop Insurance ( NFUCIP ) . <p> " They can ask the help of their respective City and Municipal Agriculture Offices or inquire directly at the OPA , " Masculino said , adding that pest infestation is one of the pressing threats in the province 's agriculture sector along with calamities and climate change . <p> The OPA had earlier reported that almost 60 hectares of rice farms in cities of Kabankalan , La Carlota and Talisay , and Candoni town were infested by rice black bug this month . <p> The initial damage and production losses were pegged @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ accounted for the affected farms in La Carlota City and Candoni , at eight and 6.2 hectares , respectively . <p> The said rice farms are already in maturity stage thus , the damage is higher than the latest 44 hectares at Barangay Magballo in Kabankalan City , which are still in vegetative stage . <p> Measures like application of metarhizium and light trapping operations were already conducted to contain the infestation . Masculino said that under the NFUCIP , enrolled rice farmers may avail of the P17,000 claims per hectare of farms damaged and affected by pest infestation . <p> The enrollment premium per cropping season is P840 . Of which , only P340 is the counterpart of farmer-enrollees while the remaining P500 is shouldered by the provincial government as loan , he added . <p> Early prevention <p> Joyce Wendam , regional director of Department of Agriculture-Negros Island Region ( DA-NIR ) called on local government units ( LGUs ) to immediately implement necessary measures to prevent the spread of infestation . <p> DA-NIR monitoring showed that another small portion of farm in Valladolid was infested by rice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Wendam said that ideally , the light trapping operation should be conducted five days before and after full moon which " LGUs are not properly observing . " <p> She also urged cities and municipalities to strengthen their information dissemination and advocacy on how to manage rice black bug and other pests that can infest agricultural farms , especially rice . <p> " We should not underestimate the damage and losses that can be brought by pest infestation as according to PhilRice 10 pieces of black bug represent 38 percent of the total losses per hectare , " Wendam added .
