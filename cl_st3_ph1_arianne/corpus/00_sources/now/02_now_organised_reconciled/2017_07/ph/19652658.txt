<h> Duterte warns mining companies : pay higher taxes or shut down <p> DAVAO CITY , Philippines -- President Rodrigo Duterte gave a stern warning to mining companies that they will have to take responsibility to rehabilitate mining areas they destroyed or he will put an end to mining . <p> During his second State of the Nation Address on Monday , July 24 , Duterte veered away from his written speech to explain more about his plan to run after mining companies . <p> " I 'm warning all mining operations and contractors to refrain from unbridled destruction of water resources I am warning all mining operations and contractors to refrain from the unbridled and irresponsible destruction of our watersheds , forests , and aquatic resources , " Duterte said . <p> Duterte said : " You have to come up with a substitute , either spend to restore the virginity of their source or I will tax you to death .
