The decision to ban non-electric vehicles by 2040 is part of an ambitious plan under Emmanuel Macron 's government , which hopes to reach its targets under the Paris climate accord . <p> According to Nicolas Hulot , France 's new ecology minister , the decision was made mainly due to public health concerns and as a way to fight against air pollution , which several French cities such as Paris struggle with on a high level .
