Manila , after all , floods every time a torrential downpour happens . And people have long learned to shrug off the danger and inconvenience arising from this perennial problem , as well as the plain government ineptitude to do something about it . <p> But the ADB warning is not about the ordinary flooding endured by the metro due to seasonal rains .
