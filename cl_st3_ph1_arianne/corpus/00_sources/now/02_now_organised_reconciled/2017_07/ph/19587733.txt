We jumped up because we believed that old superstition that we would grow taller . <p> On New Year 's Eve , we were transported by the booming cannon sounds of firecrackers . The sky was lot with thousands of Roman candles and fountains of iridescent ribbons . <p> On quiet nights , we would trace our favorite constellations and search for shooting stars . One special star outshone the rest . Our secret wish on the brightest star was that time would stand still -- that everything would remain the same . <p> Life seemed so perfect and so simple then . We were innocent and carefree .
