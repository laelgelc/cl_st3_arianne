( AP ) <p> TOKYO -- " Pen-Pineapple-Apple-Pen " and the UN are rhyming . <p> JAPANESE comedian Pikotaro has adapted his catchy song to promote the United Nations ' sustainable development goals . The original went viral last year after pop star Justin Bieber tweeted that it was his favorite video . <p> Pikotaro , in his trademark leopard-lizard design outfit , was a bit reserved at an appearance Wednesday alongside the more conservatively dressed Foreign Minister Fumio Kishida . <p> The PPAP star beamed at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pledged to do his utmost for the awareness campaign . Kishida reassured him that it is a Japan-hosted reception at the UN headquarters where he will be performing . <p> The UN action plan sets goals in fighting poverty , climate change and other global challenges .
