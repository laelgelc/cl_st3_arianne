<h> Lagos Assembly tasks commissioner on floods <p> The Lagos State of House of Assembly on Tuesday urged the state Commissioner for Environment , Dr. Babatunde Adejare , to check recurrent flooding in parts of the state . <p> The call followed a motion by the Chairman of the House Committee on Waterfronts Infrastructure , Mr. Gbolahan Yishawu and seconded by Mr. Segun Olulade , the Chairman of House Committee on Health . <p> The House also urged Governor Akinwunmi Ambode to direct the ministry and other relevant agencies to remove all unapproved structure in the state . <p> In his motion , Yishawu said the government must ensure that all canals and other drainage infrastructures are cleared frequently . <p> He said : " This House calls on the Ministry of Environment and all relevant agencies to be more proactive in removing all encumbrances to free flow of water . <p> " The ministry should create a rescue team and ensure cooperation with local governments as well as collaboration with other ministries to checkmate floods . <p> " There is a need for proper information dissemination , sensitization of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ who appeared before the lawmakers , briefed the lawmakers on measures to curb flooding in the state . <p> Adejare blamed the massive flood that ravaged some parts of the state on natural and human causes . <p> " The flood affected places such as Lekki , Victoria Island and Lekki- Epe Expressway . But the water receded within 12 hours . We have been embarking on tree planting to reduce effect of climate change in the state . <p> " Man-made is caused by people who build houses on drainage .
