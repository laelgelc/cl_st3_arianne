<p> The Federal Government has vowed to tackle the problems posed by climate change in every part of the country even as it inaugurated ecological fund intervention projects executed at the Federal College of Education Eha-Amufu ( FCEE ) in Isiuzo Local Government Area of Enugu State . <p> The projects , which include internal roads covering four kilometres , soil erosion and flood control was inaugurated by the Minister of Foreign Affairs , Mr. Geoffrey Onyeama . <p> The minister said the intervention was part of the quest to deliver on the promises of the Federal Government . " The origin of these projects was an appeal made by the school authorities and the federal government made an immediate response . This is evident of the importance this administration attaches to development in our educational institutions , " he said . <p> The minister said colleges of education were one of the most important pillars the current administration had to support in order to make the country great .
