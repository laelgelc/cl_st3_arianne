<p> The earth 's sixth mass extinction is now taking place . In other words , we 're losing species at record rates ( 100 times faster than in the previous century ) , making this the worst catastrophe since the dinosaurs went extinct 65 million years ago . <p> The same scientists who were ringing alarm bells two years ago have just published a new study in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences ( PNAS ) journal that concludes we are experiencing a much worse " biological annihilation " than we previously thought . <p> The researchers have based their findings on a much larger data set than what was used in the previous study conducted by the WWF ( 27,600 species examined versus 3,700 in the previous study ) , in addition to data from geographical analysis . <p> They have quantified the decline in vertebrate animals not according to species , but according to population ; in other words , how many groups of animals are present in any given area . <h> We 've lost 50% of all vertebrate animals in the last 40 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ animals have gone extinct in the last forty years , according to estimates . Focusing in on 177 mammal species , the researchers found that animal groups had lost at least 30% of their habitat and that nearly 40% of the animal groups had lost 80% of their natural habitat between 1900 and 2015 . <p> They concluded that vertebrates were going extinct at a massive rate , both in number of animals and in land area . And this is happening throughout the world , with catastrophic consequences for our ecosystems . <p> For example , in 2016 , there were only 7,000 leopards and 35,000 African lions on earth ( down 43% since 1993 ) . Bornean orangutan populations fell by 25% in ten years ( the equivalent of 80,000 animals ) and giraffe populations went from 115,000 specimens in 1985 to only 97,000 in 2015 . <p> And that 's only the most well-known animal species ... <p> " The full extent of the mass animal extinction has been underestimated : it is catastrophic , " says the report . We have degraded and destroyed animal habitats with intensive agriculture , deforestation and urbanization . <p> And when animal populations are not exploited or eliminated due to hunting , fishing and poaching , they are victims of pollution and climate change . <p> " The primary forces driving the sixth mass extinction are human overpopulation due to continued population growth , and excessive consumption of resources by the rich .
