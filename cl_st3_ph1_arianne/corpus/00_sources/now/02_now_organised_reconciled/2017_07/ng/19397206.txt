<h> THIS WEEK : Makerere to start climate change studies <p> Makerere University is planning to introduce climate change studies in its various courses as the country and region battle a devastating drought . Outgoing Vice Chancellor Professor Dumba Sentamu made the remarks at the opening of a two day 11th international conference on Community Based Adaptation conference in Kampala recently . <p> Dumba said the programs would be bolstered under the College of Agricultural and Environmental Science and integrated in other courses like Land Economics . He added that the higher institutions should take the lead in imparting knowledge on climate change . <p> Prof Bernard Bashaasha , the Principal of the college said the new programs will be covered at both undergraduate and graduate level . The conference was organized by the International Centre for Climate Change and Development .
