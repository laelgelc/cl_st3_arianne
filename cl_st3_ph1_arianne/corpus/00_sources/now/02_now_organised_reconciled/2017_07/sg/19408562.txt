<h> TN cow trade body moves SC against Centre 's cattle sale order <p> A Tamil Nadu-based cow trade organization going by the name of Maattu Vyapaarigal Matrum Tholilalargal Sangam moved the Supreme Court on Monday against the Centre 's new cattle trade rules . <p> The organization has filed a petition in the apex court challenging the new cattle trade rules . <p> On May 25 , the Centre , through an order , imposed a ban on the sale of cattle , including cows for slaughter and restricted cattle trade solely to farm owners . <p> Minister for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Harsh Vardhan had ordered that the ministry has notified the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals ( Regulation of Livestock Markets ) Rules , 2017 to ensure that the sale of cattle is not meant for slaughter purposes . <p> Regulating animal trade is a state business , but animal welfare is a central subject , thereby providing the window for the ministry to notify the rule . <p> In lieu of this , there was widespread opposition of the order , with many states openly denying accepting the notification .
