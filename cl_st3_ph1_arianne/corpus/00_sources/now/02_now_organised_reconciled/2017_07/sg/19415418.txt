<h> German minister compares G20 rioters to ' neo-Nazis , terrorists ' <p> " These were n't demonstrators . These were criminal anarchists , " fumed de Maiziere <p> Germany 's Interior Minister Thomas de Maiziere on Monday compared the far-left and anarchist radicals who rampaged through the G20 summit host city of Hamburg to " neo-Nazis and Islamist terrorists " . <p> A hardcore of militants among mass demonstrations against the Group of 20 meeting had burnt cars , looted shops , erected street barricades and hurled rocks and bottles at riot police during pitched street battles . <p> In the worst unrest , Friday night in Hamburg 's Sternschanze district next to the G20 venue , police commandoes with automatic weapons detained radicals who hurled rocks from rooftops and fired metal balls with powerful slingshots . <p> " These were n't demonstrators . These were criminal anarchists , " fumed de Maiziere , pointing to the " completely uninhibited violence " that he said in many cases amounted to attempted homicide . <p> He charged that these militants could " not claim any political motives " but were @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ terrorists " . <p> The clashes erupted as leaders from the world 's 20 biggest developed and emerging economies held a two-day meeting focusing on trade , terrorism , climate change and other key global issues . <p> Authorities said nearly 500 police officers were injured in a deployment of more than 20,000 , and 186 people were detained . <p> No accurate number for protesters injured was available . <p> The explosion of violence has sparked pointed questions over how Hamburg could descend into " mob rule " and why Chancellor Angela Merkel chose a city known as a hotbed of anti-capitalist radicals as the venue . <p> De Maiziere rejected such talk and said Germany would continue to hold large events in major cities because " anything else would amount to a capitulation of the state " . <p> The German police officers ' union GdP on Friday hit out at so-called black bloc anarchists , accusing them of " hijacking peaceful demonstrations by tens of thousands of people to deliberately attack " police .
