<h> US isolated over climate : G20 host Merkel <p> President Donald Trump met German Chancellor Angela Merkel on the eve of the G20 summit in Hamburg , which is expected to be testy for the tycoon following his outbursts on global trade , NATO and climate change <p> US President Donald Trump faces isolation at Friday 's G20 summit over his climate sceptic stance , Chancellor Angela Merkel said , noting that many countries are determined to honour the landmark Paris accord . <p> Trump may have vowed to pull the US out of the 2015 climate protection deal , but " many many other countries want to go on implementing " it , Merkel told journalists before a meeting with the US president . <p> Efforts to halt climate change is a key issue that world leaders gathering from Friday in the German port city of Hamburg are struggling to find consensus on . <p> Merkel has said that the talks on the issue would " not be easy " with Trump , who has once called climate change a Chinese hoax . <p> " We are not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will call discord discord . Because there are also different opinions on some important questions , " she said . <p> In the run up to the summit , negotiators from the 20 world powers have already been battling for days to lock down an agreement for a final communique . <p> Sources close to negotiations say the G20 final document -- to be finished by Saturday morning -- would reflect the 19-versus-US situation , underlining that the Paris accord is " irreversible " . <p> It would also reaffirm that other G20 nations are committed to the Paris accord while " taking note " of Washington 's decision . <p> Earlier fears that some G20 members such as Saudi Arabia could be tempted to side with the world 's biggest economic power have not materialised , said a source speaking on condition of anonymity . <p> German Foreign Minister Sigmar Gabriel said after Merkel 's talks with Trump said that Germany and the US remained " significantly at odds " on climate as well as over trade . <p> " The question is whether the Americans remain convinced that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ America is the winner or not , " Gabriel told public broadcaster ARD . <p> " Or can we manage to convince the Americans that if everyone plays by the same rules , then this will be best for everyone , " he said . <p> Signing up to an anti-protectionist pledge used to be routine at G20 meetings , but not this time , with Trump reportedly eyeing tariffs on steel imports as he seeks to put " America first " . <p> Sources close to the negotiations on the final communique said that the US was unlikely to agree to a strong condemnation of protectionism . <p> Instead , they said that the statement might stress the importance of free trade but also recognise the right of countries to defend itself against uncompetitive practices .
