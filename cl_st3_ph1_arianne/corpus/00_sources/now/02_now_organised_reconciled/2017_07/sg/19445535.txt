<p> The Philippine startup scene saw 20 promising ideas from groups and individuals chosen as finalists in two separate competitions and top winners gain a chance to compete globally to win money and mentorship . <p> ServeHappy Jobs , a niche job platform catering to the hospitality industry , won the championship round of Seedstars Manila 2017 , the local leg of global seed-stage startup competition Seedstars World . <p> ServeHappy outperformed seven other finalists who were chosen from among 80 teams for the competition organised by Switzerland-based incubator Seedstars . <p> The Philippine champion will now participate at the regional summit in Bangkok as well as the Seedstars Summit in Switzerland in April 2018 . It stands to gain opportunity to win up to $1 million equity investment in the Seedstars World global finals . <p> Founded in August 2016 by its CEO Audrey Tanco Uy , ServeHappy helps businesses mostly in the hospitality , events and travel industries in the Philippines to find their crew through its platform that leverages on social media and messaging technologies . <p> Bloom with its blockchain solution to cut fees for remittances @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which grabbed the last spot in the top three . The five other finalists at Seedstars Manila were Stash , Haraya Labs , Fetch , Retailgate , and Senti . <p> The finalists that came from different parts of the country were chosen for displaying unique and innovative views on several social issues that contribute to poverty in the Philippines such as climate change and displacement , peace and security , health and wellness , and education and learning through the use of technology .
