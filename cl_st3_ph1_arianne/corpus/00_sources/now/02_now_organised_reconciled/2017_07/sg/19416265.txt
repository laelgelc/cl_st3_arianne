<h> Bhagnani and Siddiqui to unveil first look of ' Carbon ' at IIFA 2017 <p> MUMBAI : Actor and producer Jackky Bhagnani along with co-star Nawazuddin Siddiqui will be unveiling the first look of their upcoming short film ' Carbon ' on 13 July at IIFA 2017 in New York . <p> The film will be showcased as part of a special event titled , ' Shuruaat : The global education and leadership foundation gala ' with a host of Indian and International celebrities , and delegates of the Global Citizen Foundation and the UN in attendance . <p> " As an actor and producer , the only way I know how to communicate with audiences is through film . ' Carbon ' deals with serious environmental issues like global warming and climate change and their impact on our world . As a citizen of this planet , I think it 's time we stop talking and start doing . Carbon is my attempt at creating awareness and I 'm thankful to IIFA for giving me this platform " , says Bhagnani . <p> A futuristic film set in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bajpai and Rameez Khan , co-produced by Deepshikha Deshmukh and stars Siddiqui , Prachi Desai , Yashpal Sharma and Bhagnani in key roles . <p> While Jackky plays the protagonist with an artificial heart , Siddiqui portrays the role of a man from mars .
