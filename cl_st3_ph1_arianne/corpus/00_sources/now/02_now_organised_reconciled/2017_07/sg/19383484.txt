<h> PM Modi discusses bilateral relations with S. Korea , Italy , Norway <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Saturday held discussion with South Korean President Moon Jae-in on the sidelines of the G-20 Summit in Hamburg , Germany . <p> During the meeting , both leaders expressed their commitment to further develop the special strategic partnership between India and South Korea , particularly through participation in programmes such as ' Make in India ' , ' Digital India ' and ' Start up India ' . <p> Prime Minister Modi also extended an invitation to President Moon to visit India , which the latter accepted . <p> The Indian and Italian leaders focussed on promoting bilateral relations , particularly trade and investment and people-to-people ties . <p> Prime Minister Modi invited Italy 's participation in World Food India - the food processing exhibition to be held in India in November this year . <p> The two leaders stressed on the significance of promoting interaction between medium enterprises of the two countries for strengthening bilateral economic cooperation . The Indian leader invited participation of Norwegian pension funds in the National Investment and Infrastructure Fund . <p> Prime Minister Solberg on his part invited India to participate in the Oceans ' Conference on the sidelines of the UNGA . <p> In a gesture symbolising cooperation for attainment of Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) , Prime Minister Solberg presented to Prime Minister Modi , at the end of the meeting , a football with SDGs inscribed on it .
