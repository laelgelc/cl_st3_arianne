At the end of 2016 , HNA Group initiated the Green Tour program , a public benefit program in the form of carbon footprint offset . As the first innovative public interest program launched by a Chinese airline , Green Tour aims to become a benchmark brand in the green aviation sector through enhancing public awareness by inviting travelers to become the advocates of environmental protection . Passengers of HNA Group 's flights are invited to offset their carbon footprint through voluntary donations or Fortune Wings Club points . The passenger can calculate the extent of their carbon footprint using the Carbon Footprint calculator which could be found on Hainan Airlines ' official website , their mobile phone or at the airport self-check-in terminals .
