<h> King calls for forest protection <p> King Norodom Sihamoni yesterday oversaw a tree planting ceremony in Kandal province to mark the 27th Arbor Day , pleading for all citizens to protect the country 's forests . <p> At the ceremony in Tuol Prech commune , King Sihamoni urged citizens to participate in planting their own trees , while also fighting to protect the country 's forests by stopping clear-felling , deforestation and wildlife hunting . <p> " All the people in the country , please join in protecting forests , in stopping illegal forest clearing , and stopping illegal logging , hunting , and the planting of illegal wildlife traps , " he said . <p> " Join in the tree planting to increase the forest cover . " <p> King Sihamoni added that healthy forests have economical and social value , as well as environmental .
