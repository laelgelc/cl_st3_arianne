<h> China 's Xi says will fulfil Paris climate deal obligations <p> French President Emmanuel Macron ( L ) shakes hands with Chinese President Xi Jinping ( R ) during a bilateral meeting on the second day of the G20 summit in Hamburg , Germany , 08 July 2017 . REUTERS/Ian Langsdon/POOL <p> BEIJING ( Reuters ) - Chinese President Xi Jinping told his French counterpart Emmanuel Macron on Saturday that China will fulfil its obligations under the Paris climate change agreement , China 's Foreign Ministry said in a statement . <p> The two were meeting on the sidelines of the G20 summit in the German city of Hamburg .
