<h> Tech chiefs speak up for transgender people <p> WASHINGTON * Technology chiefs have taken to Twitter in opposition to President Donald Trump 's surprise decision to ban transgender people from serving in the military . <p> Many of the CEOs included a hashtag calling for the President to #LetThemServe , in opposition to Mr Trump 's tweets , which , if they became policy , would be a reversal of an Obama administration decision to let transgender armed forces members serve openly . <p> First out of the gate on Twitter appeared to be Google CEO Sundar Pichai , who expressed his gratitude for transgender members who serve . Twitter CEO Jack Dorsey wrote that " discrimination in any form is wrong for all of us " , and Apple CEO Tim Cook , who is gay , wrote that " we are indebted to all who serve .
