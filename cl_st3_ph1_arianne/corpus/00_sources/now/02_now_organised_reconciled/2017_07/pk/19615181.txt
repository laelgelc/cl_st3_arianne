<h> ' Everyone needs to help protect biodiversity ' <p> PESHAWAR : There is a need for a pragmatic approach by communities for sustainable conservation of biodiversity and wildlife resources to boost the productivity of the ecosystem in the country . <p> This was stated by experts during a three-day conference on biodiversity by the Centre of Plant Biodiversity University at Bhara Gali in Abbottabad . The conference was organised with assistance from the Higher Education Commission . <p> Dr Asad Ali , the chief coordinator of the conference and the director of the Botanical Garden in Nowshera , highlighted the significance of biodiversity and stressed on the active role of communities for conserving vegetation through collective initiatives . This , he said , was key for sustainable biodiversity conservation . <p> " Biodiversity boosts ecosystem productivity where each species , no matter how small , has an important role to play in our lives , " he maintained . He pointed out how a large number of plant species means a greater variety of crops . Moreover , greater species diversity ensures natural sustainability for all forms of life @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protect biodiversity for our future generations by adopting practical measures . In this regards , the media , religious scholars and local government representatives have an important role in creating awareness amongst the public regarding the significance of biodiversity , wildlife and forests in our daily life . <p> The sustainable use of biological resources , he explained , was still a challenge for the society as a whole and an integrated approach was required to tackle this challenge . <p> Other speakers said the youth have an immense potential but require direction to play a constructive role . For this , academicians , parents and the society need to shoulder responsibility . <p> The conservation of biological diversity ensures economic prosperity and ecological sustainability and is a tool to fight climate change , desertification and global warming , they said .
