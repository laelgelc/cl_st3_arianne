They suggest that the findings should help us to better understand the biology of depression and improve its diagnosis and treatment . <p> Depression is not the same as the mood changes and short-lived emotional reactions that we have to everyday challenges . It is a common illness that can endure , cause great suffering , and damage people 's lives at school , at work , and at home .
