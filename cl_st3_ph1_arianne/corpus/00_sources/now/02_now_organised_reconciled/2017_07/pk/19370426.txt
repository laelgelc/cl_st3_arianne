<h> G20 draft statement shows 19-against-US split on climate <p> HAMBURG : World leaders at the G20 summit acknowledged US President Donald Trump 's divergent stance on climate change , according to a draft communique seen by AFP on Friday . <p> The draft underlines that the 2015 Paris climate accord is " irreversible " and affirms that other G20 nations are committed to the deal while taking note of Washington 's decision to quit the agreement . <p> Ahead of the summit , a key concern among Western allies was whether they would be able to convince G20 members to stand firm on the Paris agreement on combating global warming . <p> If the draft is confirmed in the final statement due to be published Saturday afternoon , it would reflect the 19-versus-the-US split over the issue . <p> British Prime Minister Theresa May meanwhile said world leaders will redouble efforts to persuade Trump to rejoin the Paris deal . <p> " I believe the collective message that will be given to President Trump around this table will be the importance of America coming back into that agreement , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensure that can happen , " she told the BBC.
