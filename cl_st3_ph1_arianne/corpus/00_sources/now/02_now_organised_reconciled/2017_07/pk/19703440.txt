<p> My last visit to Swat , once known idyllically as the emerald valley , was after massive floods had struck the region around this time in 2010 . I remember sitting on the banks of the picturesque Swat River on the roadside , and being told : " See that pile of bricks on the other side ? That 's all that remains of Mullah Fazlullah 's madrassa . It was swept away instantly by the floods . " <p> For years the former princely state of Swat , famous for its fruit orchards , snow-clad mountains , Buddhist stupas and trout-filled rivers , was a popular tourist destination -- its hotels were clean and its people well-educated , at least in the main towns . <p> In 2007 , the Taliban began taking control of the region , shutting down hotels and even destroying them along with schools .
