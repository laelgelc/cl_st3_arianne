<h> Delayed civil service reforms <p> Though reforms of all sorts have become a refrain in political manifestoes of all the political parties , the fundamental civil service reforms are yet to be undertaken . The federal government initiated a process with much fanfare , yet the same has been halted with some superficial changes in the course of CSS exams . <p> The subject article pertains to two cadres only that is PAS ( DMG ) and PMS cadres recruited by the federal and provincial governments respectively . The federal government boasts of recruiting the best graduates through the process of competitive exams and invests them with the administrative powers in the centre as well as provinces . The system , however , is inefficient , corrupt and the basis of much that ails the governance in Pakistan .
