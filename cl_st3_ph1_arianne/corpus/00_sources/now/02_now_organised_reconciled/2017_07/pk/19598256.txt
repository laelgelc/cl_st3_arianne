<p> ISLAMABAD : With the specter of drought looming over the country in view of the erratic weather patterns , the Council of Common Interests ( CCI ) is scheduled to meet on July 31 and is likely to approve the National Water Policy aimed at ensuring effective use of water by households , farmers and industries . <p> The policy says the impact of climate change -- intensification of floods , erratic monsoon rains and droughts -- is already evident in Pakistan ; and rising temperatures , resulting in enhanced heat and water-stress condition , particularly in arid and semi-arid regions , will lead to reduced agriculture productivity . <p> In this scenario , the proposed policy calls for making water delivery services economically and financially sustainable and puts emphasis on the social and economic value of water .
