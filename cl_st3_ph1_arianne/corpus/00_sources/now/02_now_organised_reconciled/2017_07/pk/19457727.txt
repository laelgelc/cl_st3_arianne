<h> add The News to homescreen <h> Editorial <p> July 13 , 2017 <h> Share <p> Advertisement <h> The G20 summit <p> G20 summits are always a source of global controversy . The meeting of the world 's biggest economies brings out the outrage of many who champion for fair trade and a fair global economic system . This is why thousands of protesters gathered in Hamburg last week as major world leaders , including Russian President Putin , US President Trump , German Chancellor Angela Merkel and Chinese President Xi Jinping arrived in the city for talks on global trade . The hostile atmosphere was intended to serve as a reminder to these leaders that the decisions they take do not benefit the world 's most vulnerable populations , both in their home countries and globally . The 20 countries represented in the G20 represent 85 percent of the world 's GDP as well as two-thirds of its population . For example , the joint statement on climate change -- issued by all of the leaders minus Trump -- symbolises how the US is now marking itself out as an international pariah . <p> The other 19 leaders issued a direct rebuke to the US for abandoning the Paris climate accord , a situation unimaginable even a year ago . The US president 's most important meeting was with Russian President Putin , where the issue of election hacking was brought up in the background of new reports of Trump administration backchannel contacts with Russia . Presented as a success for the US , the meeting was anything but . It is clear that Russia has the upper hand . Trump and the Chinese president met like long lost best friends while Trump had a similarly cordial meeting with UK PM Theresa May over a trade deal with the US . Trump 's enthusiasm for a quick deal belied his promises of a more closed US economy going forward . The slightly good news @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the US and Russia seems to be holding for now . If anything , it seems the traditional international powerhouses of the US and the UK are running out of steam after choosing paths to isolation . Both were given a tough treatment . Instead , the summit seemed to be about winning favour with Russia and China , who could soon be the world 's biggest hegemons . The G20 summit signals the emergence of a new world order . The protests confirm that this one too will not be able to address the real challenges faced by the world .
