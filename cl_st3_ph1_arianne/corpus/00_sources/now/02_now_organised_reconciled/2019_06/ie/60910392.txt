During the event , musicians and artists from Extinction Rebellion Lambeth will present a performance titled ' Petroleo and Fueliet ' , drawing attention to the fact that BP is expected to spend $70bn on new oil and gas extraction in the next decade . <p> In a letter to the Trustees of the Royal Opera House , Extinction Rebellion Lambeth said : <p> The national acclaim of the Royal Opera House , its integrity and values are transferred by association to the BP brand .
