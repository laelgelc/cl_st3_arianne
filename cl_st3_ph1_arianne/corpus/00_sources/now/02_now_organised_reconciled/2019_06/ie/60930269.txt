Local authorities will also be rolling out about 200 charge points nationwide . <p> While there 's no specific plan to encourage take up of EVs in the plan , Varadkar said the government will monitor market trends over next decade and introduce measures to increase EV sales . <p> Before the report was launched today , there had been speculation about the introduction of congestion charges for towns and cities , but that is not contained in today 's report . <p> When asked about it today , Transport Minister Shane Ross said : <p> " This is a rolling document -- anything like that will be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ruled out . About 40% of new builds currently have heat pumps installed . <p> As reported earlier this month , consumers will be able to sell excess electricity from their home solar panels back to the grid in the near future . <p> A microgeneration scheme will be put in place by the end of this year , with a view to commence an enduring support scheme by 2021 so that all people with solar panels can sell their electricity back to the grid . <p> The minister wants to raise the amount of electricity generated from renewable sources from 30% now to 70% by 2030 , so there will be a big focus on foreshore wind @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ foreshore and offshore wind energy , and new legislation coming down the pipeline this year to streamline development . <p> Coal and peat energy will be phased out , with today 's report giving a timeline for the closure of such plants around the country . <p> The burning of coal in ESB 's Moneypoint generation plant will cease by 2025 , states the plan , and Bord na M ? na will transition away from peat by 2028 . <p> The dates to phase out fossil fuels will " undergo an ongoing review , " the report states . <p> Carbon tax on the cards <p> There has been a lot of talk about the carbon tax , and it appears the government will sign up to the Joint Oireachtas Committee on Climate Change 's recommendation that it should be increased by ? 20 to ? 80 on a phased basis to 2030 .
