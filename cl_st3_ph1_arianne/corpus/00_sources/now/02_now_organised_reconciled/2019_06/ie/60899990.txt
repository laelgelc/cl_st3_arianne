<h> HubSpot 's Barbara McCarthy reveals how to hire Generation Z <p> HubSpot EMEA director of engineering , Barbara McCarthy , says Generation Z promises to be vastly different . To hire them , you need to appeal to their passions and values . <p> They are passionate , they are fiery , they are idealistic and they are digital natives . And if employers do n't measure up , Generation Z will not work for them . <p> That 's the stark message that HubSpot 's EMEA director of engineering , Barbara McCarthy , had for Inspirefest 2019 about workers who do not only know their worth but are going to be far more numerous and influential than their millennial predecessors . <p> ' They 're a different type of generation . They 're fiery , they 're determined and they want to pursue their passions ' -- BARBARA MCCARTHY <p> It is said that Generation Z , mostly born after the mid-1990s , will account for 40pc of consumers in the US by 2020 . <h> A different type of generation <p> " We know more about them than we did about millennials when they entered the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cultures within companies that they want to work for right now , " urged McCarthy . <p> Generation Z workers have a huge interest and aspiration to work in tech , and they are beginning to come into the workforce in their droves , just as competition to hire them increases . <p> " Unlike their predecessors , the traditional career values do n't really resonate with them . They 're a different type of generation . They 're fiery , they 're determined and they want to pursue their passions . " <p> They have never known a world without the internet , said McCarthy . " They 've never known a world where information was n't at their fingertips 24/7 . " <p> Not only that , but 40pc of Generation Z is connected to the internet for more than 10 hours a day and this access of technology will be expected in the workplace . <p> McCarthy gave the example of a recruitment firm called TextRecruit , which has already honed its methodology to appeal to a new generation that automatically sifts through information noise at lightning @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ developed an eight-second gauge or filter to determine if something is interesting or not . <p> " One of the things we learned at HubSpot is that we used to do a lot of classroom-based learning and that does n't appeal to them at all . If you want to learn how to code or change a tyre in a car or how to get those amazing GHD curls , you actually go to YouTube . This is the type of learning that appeals to them . " <h> Diversity is in their DNA <p> McCarthy also explained that for Generation Z , diversity is very important . " It is estimated that by 2020 , 50pc of all kids in the US will come from an ethnic or minority group . So , diversity in the traditional sense of the word is actually going to become the norm . <p> " So that means that they really , really , value diversity , belonging and inclusion within companies , and they want to see that companies actually value it . " <p> This means diversity has to be baked @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and not some side thing or form of virtue signalling . " So , whether it 's events or whether it 's employee resource groups , companies have to show that it is as important to them as it is to the candidates that they are trying to recruit . " <p> Generation Z is also highly entrepreneurial and employers will be challenged to appeal to the future founder within these workers . <p> " 72pc of second-level students say that they want to start their own business and 61pc of college students would prefer to be entrepreneurs rather than employees . How should we think of that from a company perspective as employers ? We should lean into that and actually encourage it and embrace it . Give them the autonomy and challenge to think like a CEO . " <p> McCarthy said that at HubSpot , engineers follow a set of principles or a shared belief system that enables them to take ownership of problem-solving . <p> " They take responsibility and ownership like nothing I 've ever seen because they own the problem definition , they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ work . So , they really , really take ownership and feel , I suppose , passion about the work that they 're doing . " <p> As McCarthy pointed out , those in Generation Z are a passionate lot who have the courage of their convictions . She pointed to 16-year-old Greta Thunberg , the Swedish schoolgirl who , at age 15 , began protesting outside the Swedish parliament about the need for immediate action to combat climate change and has since become an outspoken climate activist . <p> " She started what I would call a global movement among students around climate . So this is what motivates her . We have to allow the next generation to bring those passions into work because we know that if somebody is allowed to be motivated by passion , they 're going to have a huge sense of pride in what they do . In order to generate pride , you have to have a higher organisational purpose . " <p> HubSpot recognised this early in the company 's history by codifying its culture . <p> " It 's about @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ customers , how we deal with failure . All the things that are important to you and what you stand for as a company , write them down . " <p> Generation Z will value professional and personal development . Also , in terms of an increased focus on anxiety levels , McCarthy said companies will need to be able to able to support their employees on not just their physical wellbeing , but their mental wellbeing as well . " Companies need to talk about mental health more openly . " <p> Generation Z , McCarthy concluded , is a determined , fiery and dedicated generation . " They 're going to outnumber all the previous generation . They 're going to be really dedicated to hard work and their careers . But we need to remember that 60pc of the jobs in 2034 , they do n't even exist yet . <p> " So , the way we think about recruitment and building effective teams , all that has to change .
