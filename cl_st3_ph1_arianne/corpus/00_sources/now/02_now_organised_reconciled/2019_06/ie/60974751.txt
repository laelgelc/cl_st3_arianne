<h> 60 schemes contaminated : Water may be an election issue <p> Monday , July 01 , 2019 - 12:00 AM <p> As expectations around a pre-Christmas election gather momentum , especially if Brexit is resolved without chaos , it is unlikely that the idea of water charges will be revisited in the short-term . Despite that understandable , if unwise , evasion , issues around a secure water supply remain critical . <p> The Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) has warned that more than 550,000 Irish Water customers use water at risk of contamination by e.coli or cryptosporidium . <p> Cork City 's main supply is just one of 60 " at-risk " schemes dotted around the country .
