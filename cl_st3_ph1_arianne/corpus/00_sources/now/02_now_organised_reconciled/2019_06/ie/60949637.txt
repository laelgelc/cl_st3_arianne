<h> Carlow-built Keenan ' green ' machine to help farmers in fight against climate change <h> This is cool ! <p> Reporter : <p> Darren Hassett <p> 23 Jun 2019 <p> Email : <p> news@carlowlive.ie <p> 0 <p> Carlow-based machinery brand , Keenan , has been certified by the Carbon Trust as a more sustainable diet feeder , proven to reduce methane emissions on-farm <p> Borris based machinery brand , Keenan , has been certified by the Carbon Trust as a more sustainable diet feeder , proven to reduce methane emissions on farms . <p> The well-known company - which has a manufacturing site in Carlow -- has become the " first manufacturer of diet feeders to be accredited by the Carbon Trust " . <p> The announement comes in light of the Government 's announcement regarding The Climate Action Plan , which aims to push Ireland to reach its 2030 targets , and to achieve net zero carbon emissions by 2050 . <p> Keenan say they are offering farmers a " tangible means of reducing their carbon footprint on-farm , while enhancing production and profitability , " said Matt Higgins , General Manager , Keenan . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ enhanced rumen performance ( per liter milk and daily liveweight gain in beef animals ) can come as a result of improved feed conversion efficiency ( FCE ) in livestock . <p> Having investigated the output from the Keenan diet feeder and reviewing data from the Keenan controller , the Carbon Trust has established that the Keenan machine can increase rumen efficiency by delivering feed that is more easily digestible for cattle , meaning less energy is required and produced by each animal . <p> " The animal food supply chain is faced with growing pressures to reduce methane and carbon dioxide emissions . As concern for our planet 's finite resources escalates , we have a responsibility to be proactive in sourcing solutions and partnerships that can relieve environmental strain and prioritize sustainability on-farm , " said Higgins . <p> Carlow Live brings you all the latest news and events from Carlow as it happens !
