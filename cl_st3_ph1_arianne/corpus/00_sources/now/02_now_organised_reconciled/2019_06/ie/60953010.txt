<h> BY Rob Gill <h> Iata makes plea against EU-wide aviation tax <p> Airline organisation Iata has called on European governments not to introduce a proposed EU-wide tax on flights . <p> Currently there is a proposal from the Netherlands for a new " environmental " tax on tickets across the EU 's nation states . <p> Iata cited its own consumer research suggesting that passengers were more supportive of the development of sustainable aviation fuels than the introduction of a new green tax on flights . <p> The association said 64% of consumers wanted governments to back the development of sustainable fuels as the best way of managing aviation 's impact on emissions and climate change . <p> The second most popular option was supporting the research and development of new technology and better airline operations at 62% . While introducing environmental taxes was backed by just 22% of those surveyed . <p> Alexandre de Juniac , Iata 's chief executive , said : " Aviation takes the climate change challenge very seriously . For more than a decade we have set and exceeded tough targets for carbon emissions , and we plan to achieve much more . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : work with aviation to encourage investment in clean fuels , and new hybrid and electric technology . This will help airlines cut emissions in half by 2050. "
