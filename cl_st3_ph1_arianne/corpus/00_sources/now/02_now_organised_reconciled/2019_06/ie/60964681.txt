<h> Importing Brazilian beef would be ' utterly bizarre ' -- ICSA <p> Implementing climate action policies in Ireland while the EU imports meat products from South America is " utterly bizarre " , according to the Irish Cattle and Sheep Farmers ' Association ( ICSA ) . <p> Eddie Punch , the association 's general secretary , said that , while farmers were " up for the challenge " of mitigating climate change , they would be " all the more committed " if it was n't for the contradictions in the proposed trade deal between the EU and the Mercosur trading bloc in South America . <p> " Asking farmers to plant more trees and reduce livestock seems utterly bizarre when we 're planning to import more meat from a country ( Brazil ) that is cutting down rainforests , " said Punch . <p> The Mercosur deal , potentially involving an extra 99,000t of beef , 200,000t of chicken/turkey and 600,000t of ethanol , will totally undermine farmers in Europe , particularly in the red meat sector .
