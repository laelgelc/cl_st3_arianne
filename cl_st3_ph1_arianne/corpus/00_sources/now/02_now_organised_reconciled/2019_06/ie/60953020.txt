<h> A new European Parliament : what can we expect ? <p> The European elections in May left a scenario in which , for the first time , the Social and Christian-democratic parties do not have an absolute majority in the European Parliament . This fact will have a decisive influence on parliamentary arithmetics , since the forces that have dominated European politics so far ( European People 's Party and the Party of European Socialists ) will require a third pro-European parties ( Liberal or Ecologists ) to be able to take forward the legislative agenda marked by the new European Commission . Report by Federico Bonaudi . <p> The European Parliament asserts its institutional role in European policy-making by exercising these various functions : participation in the legislative process , budgetary and control powers , involvement in treaty revision and its right to intervene before the European Court of Justice .
