First line : " The first thing I got wrong was the title . " <p> Throughout the play Finig returns to the theme -- how much trouble the title was causing him . He has predicted the rage of Conservatives to this title and hoped it would inspire terrorists to use the play as a manual . <p> It 's a brave play on the hugest of subjects and sometimes because of its enormous complexity it 's hard to follow .
