<h> Bahrain to ban plastic bags in July <h> MANAMA , 16 days ago <p> Bahrain 's Supreme Council for Environment ( SCE ) said that the kingdom will start phasing out the use of plastic products in July , Bahrain News Agency ( BNA ) reported on Wednesday . <p> Dr Mohammed Mubarak Bin Daina , chief executive of the SCE , explained that Ministerial Order Number ( 11 ) for the year 2019 with respect to the technical regulations for plastic products will come into effect on July 21 , 2019 . <p> The order will regulate and phase out the use of plastic bags . The first phase will focus on single-use plastic bags as well as banning the import of non-biodegradable plastic bags . Later phases will witness a permanent ban on the use of plastic bags at certain malls and supermarkets . <p> The SCE and the Ministry of Industry , Commerce and Tourism are currently working on setting the implementation phase for the remaining plastic products . <p> Dr Bin Daina noted that the SCE will issue guidelines for manufacturers and suppliers on reducing plastic waste to ensure a smooth transition . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ research on Bahrain 's plastic production reaffirmed that switching to the production of degradable plastic will not affect production lines . <p> He went on to highlight that the SCE has also conducted a market study on the consumption of biodegradable and compostable plastic products in Bahrain in order to measure the impact of finding viable alternative solutions to plastic . The study focused on the consumptions of plastic use in the foodservice industry . <p> Bahrain is joining a number of leading countries in banning the import of plastic waste , following the United Nation 's call to mitigate ocean pollution and climate change , the BNA report added .
