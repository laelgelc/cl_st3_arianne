<h> Mercosur letter : Varadkar outlines Irish farmer fears to Juncker <p> Taoiseach Leo Varadkar has outlined his " deep concerns " over a possible trade deal with the Mercosur trading block in South America in a letter to European Commission President Jean-Claude Juncker . <p> The letter , seen by AgriLand , is co-signed by four European leaders , including the Taoiseach , and outlines the particular exposure of the European agricultural sector if a deal goes ahead . <p> " The question of import into the union of beef , poultry , sugar and ethanol is particularly sensitive to us , " the letter reads . <p> The import quotas currently negotiated for beef imports could threaten this fragile sector in our countries , particularly against the background of the potentially dramatic and negative impact of a disorderly Brexit on EU markets . <p> " The cumulative effects of quotas negotiated in various trade agreements signed by the union can ultimately destabilise production and the agricultural sector , " the four European leaders warned . <p> Advertisement <p> They continued : " In order to preserve the activity of our farmers and protect our rural economy on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ products on the other hand , several elements need to be taken into consideration in the negotiation with Mercosur .
