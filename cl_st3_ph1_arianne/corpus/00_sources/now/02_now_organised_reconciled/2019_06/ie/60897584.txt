<h> EPA : Agriculture emissions ' to increase steadily ' in coming years <p> Greenhouse gas emissions from agriculture are predicted to " increase steadily " over the coming years , according to the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) . <p> The newly updated greenhouse gas projections for 2040 show there is a " significant gap " in Ireland meeting its emissions targets , according to the agency . <p> The EPA 's Greenhouse Gas Projections for the period 2018-2040 show that , while there will be a decrease in emissions overall , Ireland will need " full implementation " of its mitigation measures if it 's going to meet the objectives set out under the EU 's ' Effort Sharing Targets ' . <p> These targets refer specifically to the years 2020 and 2030 . <p> In terms of agriculture , the EPA said that the projected rise in emissions from the sector is down to an increase in animal numbers , " particularly for the dairy herd " . <p> The agency argues that emissions mitigation policies , that have been identified by Teagasc , will be important for agriculture going forward . <p> Advertisement <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ says that emissions will increase in this sector until at least 2022 .
