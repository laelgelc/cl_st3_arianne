<p> Plans have been lodged for an " iconic " greenway that will link Malahide and Donabate . <p> Fingal County Council has lodged the application with An Bord Pleanala for the Broadmeadow Way , a proposed new 6km walking and cycling greenway route that will run between Malahide Demesne and Newbridge Demesne in Donabate . <p> The proposed development will run across the Malahide Estuary adjacent to the mainline railway viaduct . <h> Read More <p> If permission is granted , it is expected the greenway will eventually connect with the proposed Fingal Coastal Way , a 32km high quality , recreational stretch of greenway connecting Donabate , Rush , Skerries , Ardgillan Castle , Balbriggan and Bremore Castle . <p> Mayor of Fingal Anthony Lavin said he was delighted to see the project progressing to the planning stage . <p> He said : " This piece of greenway infrastructure will provide considerable benefits not just for tourists visiting the area but also immeasurable quality of life benefits for residents .
