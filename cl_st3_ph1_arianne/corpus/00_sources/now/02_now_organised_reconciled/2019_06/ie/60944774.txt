<h> Climate plan : Governance proposals strongest asset <p> June 21st , 2019 <p> The governance section of the newly launched climate plan has been referred to as the plan 's " strong point " by experts in the days following its publication . <p> Under the Climate Action Plan to Tackle Climate Breakdown , the Government states its intention to establish a Climate Action Delivery Board within the Department of An Taoiseach and to found a Climate Action Council as a successor to the current Climate Change Advisory Council . <p> Delivering quarterly progress reports , the Delivery Board will hold all Departments and public bodies accountable for the delivery of actions outlined in the plan . <p> The Climate Action Council will see an expansion of powers from its predecessor , such as making five-year carbon budget recommendations , monitoring state progress in reducing emissions , and providing policy evaluation advice to the Government . <p> Additionally , all major Government investment decisions will be subject to a carbon impact and mitigation evaluation under the plan . <p> The Government will also propose a new Climate Action ( Amendment ) Bill according to the report , which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ budgets for three five-year periods . <p> Assistant Professor of Law and Government at DCU , Dr Diarmuid Torney said that " the Governance arrangements are among the stronger parts of the report " . <p> Dr Diarmuid Torney ( L ) as members of the Expert Advisory Panel , speaking at the meeting of the Citizens Assembly on climate action in 2017 Photo : : MAXWELL 'S <p> The governance section of the report , Dr Torney told The Green News , is a " U-Turn " from the 2015 Climate Act . " I think it 's a fairly radical overhaul of the existing arrangement .
