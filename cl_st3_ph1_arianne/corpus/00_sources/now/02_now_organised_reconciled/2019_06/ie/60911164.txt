It beggars belief . <p> " We 're talking about climate change . It 's a precedent as well . A dangerous precedent . We should be opening them throughout the city ; not closing them , " the Independent councillor told The Leader . <p> In a statement to the Leader , the council said : " The facility is closing due to health and safety concerns of the movements of vehicle and vehicle/pedestrian interface in the area , there is insufficient room at the location to implement a traffic management plan to address these concerns . Staff working at the facility will be reassigned within the cleansing department .
