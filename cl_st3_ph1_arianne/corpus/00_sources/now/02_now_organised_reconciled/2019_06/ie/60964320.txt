<h> FuelCell Energy partners with Drax <p> FuelCell Energy , Inc. has entered into a contract with UK-based Drax Power Station to supply its carbon capture solutions . <p> FuelCell Energy will support Drax with a Front End Engineering and Design ( FEED ) study evaluating the use of its carbonate fuel cells to capture carbon dioxide emissions from Drax 's biomass boilers . <p> The Drax Power Station is the largest single-site renewable power generator in the UK , with a capacity of over 3,900 megawatts of renewable generation , primarily from sustainable wood pellets sourced from responsibly managed forests . <p> FuelCell Energy 's carbon capture technology is based on the company 's carbonate fuel cell power plants , which are able to concentrate CO2 from dilute flue gas streams as a side reaction during power generation . <p> The FEED study will focus on a system that captures up to 85 tonnes of CO2 per day while generating additional power for the station . <p> Drax plans to use the CO2 captured within a greenhouse nearby the power station , any excess CO2 could be transported to other greenhouse locations . <p> " Carbon capture using @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and efficiency of concentrating and capturing carbon dioxide from emitters , " said Tony Leo , Executive Vice-President and Chief Technology Officer at Fuel Cell Energy . <p> " We are pleased to have the opportunity to partner with Drax and the BEIS ( United Kingdom , Department for Business , Energy and Industrial Strategy ) for such an innovative and critically important subject such as cleaner energy .
