<p> Climate Change has been on the world 's mind for some time now , with governments and organisations doing their best to highlight the need to improve the world we 'll pass down to our children . <p> Locally , a picture circulating social media has really hit the message home . Cathal Nolan of Midland Weather Channel today shared an image showing how temperatures have changed since 1901 . <p> " This image helps us to visualise just how significant the temperature increases per year have been since the start of the twentieth century , with the past decade simply being remarkable in the frequency and strength of warmer annual temperatures , " he said . <p> " Heatwaves such as that observed in 2018 are made twice as likely due to anthropogenic climate change , with our current warm spell also being linked with climate change due to a weakening temperature gradient between the Arctic and Mid-Latitudes resulting in a much weaker and meandering jet-stream .
