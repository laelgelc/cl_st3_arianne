The European Green Capital 2021 title brings a lot of responsibility to be a strong role model for cities across Europe , as we all work together to preserve and restore our planet and tackle climate change . <p> " Lahti has the environmental credibility and passion to embrace that responsibility and be an inspiring international leader . I also congratulate Lille and Strasbourg , for getting this far in the competition -- I wish you well in your continuous sustainable journey to make your cities fit for life .
