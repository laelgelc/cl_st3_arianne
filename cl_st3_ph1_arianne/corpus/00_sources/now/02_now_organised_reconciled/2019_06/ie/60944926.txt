<h> Gallery Prestigious speakers address Sean MacDiarmada Summer School <p> The annual Sean Mac Diarmada Summer School took place at Kiltyclogher on June 7 and 8 where large audiences heard a variety of speakers discussing a broad agenda of topics . <p> Included for discussion was the role of women in the Irish Revolution , education , the unifying power of sport in society , the challenges of climate change and the need for the promotion of social decency in modern Ireland to ensure that the dreams of Mac Diarmada and his comrades of 1916 , are realised . <p> The weekend commenced on Friday evening with the opening of the Summer School by actor , commentator and current Leitrim Person of the Year , Seamus O'Rourke . <p> Former Senator Paschal Mooney gave the opening presentation on the role of women in Irish politics over the past 100 years , commenting on their role in past politics and describing the landscape of Irish politics with greater female participation in the democratic process .
