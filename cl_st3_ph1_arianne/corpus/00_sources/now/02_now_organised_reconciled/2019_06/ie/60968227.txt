<h> EU and Mercosur group agree draft free trade deal <p> Trade negotiators for the European Union and the South American Mercosur trading block have reached agreement on a new free-trade deal . <p> It paves the way for a significant increase of imports of cheaper beef and other goods from South America . <p> The Mercosur trade deal has been almost 20 years in the making and involves Brazil , Argentina , Paraguay and Uruguay . <p> The region has 260 million consumers , making it the fifth largest market outside of the EU . <p> It is the largest free trade agreement negotiated by the EU to date and follows on from the completion of recent European trade agreements with Canada , Japan , Mexico , and Vietnam . <p> It comes at a critical time in global trade with the US engaged in trade wars on a number of fronts , including with China , and threatening more . <p> The Mercosur trade agreement still has to be fully ratified by the European Commission and by the European Parliament in
