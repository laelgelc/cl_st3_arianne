<h> EU research project RESCCUE focuses on resilient cities in face of climate change <p> 13 June 2019 <p> Climate change has big and long-term impacts on cities . EU-funded research aims to increase resilience , improving cities ' capacity to anticipate , prepare for , respond to and recover from related events such as droughts and heavy rain - protecting key infrastructure and lives <p> Research takes place in three cities -- Barcelona , Bristol and Lisbon -- each of which faces a variety of challenges . These include coastal , river or pluvial flooding , sewer overflow during storms , and drought . <p> Extreme events resulting from climate change present serious problems for urban services including water and energy supply , transport , telecommunications and waste disposal .
