<h> Climate Change Could Make Siberia Habitable <p> When it comes to climate change there is little good news but a new study is revealing an unintended possible positive outcome of the environmental disaster . It turns out that climate change may make large parts of Siberia habitable . <p> This could happen as early as the late 21st century . The study team from the Krasnoyarsk Federal Research Center , Russia , and the National Institute of Aerospace , USA , explored current and predicted climate scenarios to come up with their theory . <h> Becoming more habitable <p> " Previous human migrations have been associated with climate change . As civilizations developed technology that enabled them to adapt , humans became less reliant on the environment , particularly in terms of climate , " said the study 's lead author Dr Elena Parfenova , from the Krasnoyarsk Federal Research Center . <p> " We wanted to learn if future changes in climate may lead to the less-hospitable parts of Asian Russia becoming more habitable for humans . " <p> For their analysis , the team explored January and July temperatures and annual precipitation .
