<p> The demonstration , under the ' Galway Together Against Trump ' banner , took place at Eyre Square in the city centre . <p> The event was organised by a coalition of groups , opposed to US foreign policy as well as the Trump administration 's stance on climate change , abortion and a number of other issues . <p> Organisers said they wanted to send a message to President Trump that he is not wanted nor welcome in Ireland . <p> They objected to what they termed the Irish Government 's " complacency and compliance " with the current US administration . <p> The gathering was addressed by a number of speakers , including US military veterans , Ken Mayers and Tarak Kauff , who are on bail after being arrested at Shannon Airport earlier this year on suspicion of an alleged security breach there . <p> Mr Kauff said the actions of Mr Trump were a threat to humankind . <p> Poet Rita Ann Higgins also spoke at the protest , along with a number of other Galway anti-war campaigners and former US military personnel . <p> RT ? . ie is the website of Raidi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Public Service Broadcaster .
