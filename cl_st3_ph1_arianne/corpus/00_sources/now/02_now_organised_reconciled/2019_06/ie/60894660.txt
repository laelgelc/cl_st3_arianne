The Leshan great Buddha and acid rain <p> This statue is located in China 's Sichuan Province where construction started in 713 AD . It is being renovated at great expense after many years of damage from acid rain . <p> The continuing economic growth in China has led to increasing amounts of air pollution from transport and coal-burning . As a result , their emissions of sulfuric and nitric acids have continued to attack the calcite ( calcium carbonate ) mineral of the natural cliff stone that the monument is carved from . The cracks and visual degradation appear because the acids and related acidic ions dissolve the carbonate content . <p> The term acid rain was first coined in 1872 .
