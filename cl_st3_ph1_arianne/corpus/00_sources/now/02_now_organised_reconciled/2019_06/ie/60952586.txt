<h> IFA to protest on ' sell-out trade deal ' with Mercosur <p> A protest will be held by the Irish Farmers ' Association ( IFA ) at the European Commission offices in Dublin over the " double standards of the commission " in their reckless pursuit of a sell-out trade deal " with the Mercosur trade bloc . <p> The protest is scheduled to get started at 8:00am this morning , Monday , June 24 . <p> Commenting ahead of the demonstration , IFA president Joe Healy said : " We will be there to oppose EU Commission plans to sell out Irish farming in a deal with the devil that is Brazil and its new President Bolsonaro . <p> It is totally unacceptable that the commission is prepared to sacrifice Irish and European farmers , but they are also giving the green light to the further destruction of rainforests . <p> " Farmers are sick of the double speak from the EU Commission which lectures us on climate change , but is prepared to do a deal with a country with a climate destruction agenda , " he said . <p> The president claimed that numerous reports @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ meet EU standards on animal welfare , traceability , food safety and the environment . <p> Advertisement <p> " We hear a lot about the size of the Irish cattle herd , which is under seven million .
