<h> Cork County Council to be first local authority to publish a Climate Adaptation Strategy <p> Cork County Council is to become one of the first local authorities in the country to publish a Climate Adaptation Strategy , which will form a blueprint for more environmentally-friendly planning and services into the future . <p> The council will launch the draft strategy at a meeting in County Hall on July 8 and it will then go out for public consultation . <p> Louis Duffy , the council 's environment director , said following this the final document will be completed for adoption in September . <p> In a report to councillors , Mr Duffy said the draft strategy will contain a proper understanding of the key risks and vulnerabilities of climate change . <p> He said it will advance the implementation of climate resilient adaptation actions in a planned and proactive manner and " ensure that climate adaptation considerations are mainstreamed into all plans and policies and integrated into all operations and functions of the local authority .
