<h> Reminder : ICSA to select new president tomorrow <p> The Irish Cattle and Sheep Farmers ' Association ( ICSA ) will select a new president tomorrow evening , Thursday , June 27 , in an election to be held at the Midlands Park Hotel in Portlaoise , Co . Laois . <p> Three candidates have put their names forward for the role : Hugh Farrell ( the current chairperson of the association 's Animal Health and Welfare Committee ) ; Dermot Kelleher ( the current regional vice-president for Munster ) ; and Edmond Phelan ( former chairperson of the Beef Committee ) . <p> The victorious candidate will lead the organisation for a two-year term up to 2021 . <p> The election and preceding discussion will begin at 7:30pm . The discussion portion of the evening will be chaired by AgriLand news editor Claire Mc Cormack . <p> Each candidate will make a presentation to the association 's National Executive , which will then be followed by a panel discussion and a question and answer session with the candidates . <p> Following that , the vote will take place , with the result expected by 9:00pm . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to AgriLand to give their views on the main issues facing agriculture today . <p> All three candidates agreed that , where climate change is concerned , there was too much of an emphasis on Irish agriculture compared to other industries , as well as agriculture in other parts of the world . <p> The candidates said that a possible Mercosur trade deal would undercut efforts to mitigate for climate change among Irish farmers . <p> Regarding the ? 100 million beef fund , they agreed that the focus must be on beef prices . <p> The election has been organised following the resignation of former president Patrick Kent , who stepped down in April to take up the role of agricultural advisor to then European Parliament candidate Mick Wallace .
