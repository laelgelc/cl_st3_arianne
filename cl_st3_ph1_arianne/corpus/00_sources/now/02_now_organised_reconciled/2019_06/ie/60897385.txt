<p> The group aims to formulate and deliver a coordinated approach to the deployment of hydrogen transport in Ireland . <p> Hydrogen fuel , which can be harnessed to produce emissions zero emissions electrical power for cars and heavy-duty vehicles , offers a sustainable alternative to traditional fuel sources . <p> The use of sustainable hydrogen-based fuel can reduce carbon emissions in the transport sector , currently the second highest contributor to greenhouse gas emissions in Ireland , and do so in a cost-effective manner , according to HMI . <p> HMI hopes to achieve its goals through the mass market implementation of Fuel Cell Electric Vehicles . <p> FCEVs consume a mixture of hydrogen fuel and air to produce electrical power and emit water vapour as their only byproduct . <p> Complementing progress already made on electric vehicles , FCEVs are expected to be the preferred zero emission technology for longer range drivers and a pathway to the decarbonisation of other vehicle segments such as heavy goods vehicles and buses , HMI predicts . <p> They will also publish a report on the potential role of hydrogen technology in the decarbonisation of the national transport fleet in Q3 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ - hydrogen production , refuelling stations and FCEV rollout - will work closely with the Hydrogen Ireland Association , an organisation that reviews the use of hydrogen in various sectors such as the built environment , integration with renewables and energy supply chains . <p> " Hydrogen Mobility Ireland is addressing one of the most pressing questions of our time - how to effectively and efficiently decarbonise transport in Ireland , " Mark Teevan , Director of Toyota Ireland and Chairperson of HMI , said .
