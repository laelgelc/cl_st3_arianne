" The plan provides for the government 's ambition to ban the sale of petrol and diesel cars by 2030 , bringing almost a million electric vehicles into circulation . <p> Further to this , they hope to have fully phased out petrol and diesel-powered cars completely by 2040 . People have long lamented the inadequate charging network but the government has announced further investments to this end in this plan . <p> It proposes to stop granting NCT certificates for petrol and diesel cars by 2045 , effectively revealing a cut-off point for them on our roads . <p> A car scrappage scheme will also be introduced @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This could come on stream as early as next year . <p> Public transport is a big driver of this plan with Minister Ross saying the ultimate goal is to get people out of private cars and into buses through the Bus Connects scheme . <p> The photo opportunity getting Twitter talking on Monday was the Cabinet being ferried to Monday Climate Action plan in a hybrid Dublin bus .
