Underpinning this plan , 17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) were adopted , which are a call to action for the public and private sectors to end poverty and tackle climate change . <p> But how do we get to a world where there is zero hunger , good health , and economic growth . <p> The United Nations Development Programme estimates that $6trn of annual investment is required to achieve the SDGs . Yet , by the end of last year , the amount of money invested in impact investments stood at only $228bn .
