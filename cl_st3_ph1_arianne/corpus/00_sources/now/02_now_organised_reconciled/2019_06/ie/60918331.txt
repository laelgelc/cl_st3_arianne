<h> Rats , whippet , King Kong and long noses <p> IT 'S the open exhibition of the year , this time co-ordinated by painter Jock McFadyen , and featuring some 1,581 artworks from an entry of no fewer than 16,250 . It 's the Royal Academy summer exhibition , of course , and as always with much to inspire and impress ; and much to the contrary . <p> McFadyen says at the outset : " I do n't really care what anyone thinks of the hang . " Curating such a menagerie of works is , he says , " a really big juggling act " which he also likens to making an artwork in its gestation . <p> In fact , in the first of the traditional rooms , the Wohl Central Hall , there is a menagerie . Beasts feature , from guillemot to whippet , a polar bear , and an elephant in the room , to carp , swan , budgie , even King Kong , and a colobus .
