<h> Mercosur deal a ' betrayal of Irish cattle farmers ' -- ICSA <p> The EU Mercosur trade deal has been labelled " an absolute disgrace , which completely undermines the EU 's moral authority to lead on climate change " by the Irish Cattle and Sheep Farmers ' Association ( ICSA ) . <p> Slamming the deal , which was announced earlier this evening , Friday , June 28 , newly elected ICSA president Edmond Phelan said : <p> " Let there be no doubt : A calculated decision has been made by European leaders to increase car sales , mainly petrol and diesel , at the price of sacrificing the EU beef farmer . " <p> The president said that the Irish beef sector , with 90% exported to EU markets , will " take the full brunt of this outrageous decision " . <p> It beggars belief that more rain forest will be cut down to facilitate Brazilian beef expansion while sustainable EU beef systems will be made completely unviable . <p> " At a time when Brexit is already damaging the Irish beef sector , this deal is a complete betrayal of Irish cattle farmers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ farmers planting trees here when trees are being cut down in South America and massive quantities of fuel will be burnt to ship cheap , lower standard Brazilian beef to a market which is already fully supplied ? " <p> Phelan outlined that the ICSA will be demanding that the Oireachtas is given its say on this and that every single Irish politician stands up for our interests . <p> This deal has nothing in it for Ireland . Consumers will see less and less Irish beef as we abandon any pretence that local , sustainable food matters and we become more and more dependent on South America . <p> " It 's not just beef ; the deal also will see more chicken from South America . <p> " The Government must stand up for Irish farmers ; there is no room for equivocation on this , " Phelan concluded .
