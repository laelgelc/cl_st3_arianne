<h> How to take climate action through repair , reuse and recycling <h> Tips for individuals , communities , businesses and the public sector <p> Now that the government 's Climate Action Plan has been released , many are wondering what they can do to take climate action . <p> One area often overlooked is reducing consumption of goods . <p> Recent studies have found that up to 40% of global emissions are due to extraction , manufacturing and distribution of goods . <p> Textile production , for example , contributes more to climate change than international aviation and shipping combined . <p> Currently in Ireland it is estimated only about 3% of goods are reused compared with 45% recycled and the rest recovered or disposed . <p> It is critical to move away from a disposable culture ( take-make-waste ) towards sustainable reuse of goods we have already -- through repair , reuse and recycling . <p> Now is the time for everyone to act to reduce the climate impact of our resource consumption . <p> Community Reuse Network Ireland ( CRNI ) has developed climate actions individuals , communities , businesses and the public sector can take @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reuse and recycling . leisure time . <p> These actions will contribute to the Climate Action Plan , which will strengthen waste collection and management systems to support consumers to reduce , reuse and recycle . <p> Climate Action for Communities : <p> Sharing repair , reuse and recycling information and resources among community groups , schools , clubs and parishes can encourage others to pursue a sustainable lifestyle . <p> Community-based initiatives , such as repair cafes , tool libraries , book swap box , repair and upskilling workshops , and swap events for tools , toys and clothes are some of the ways communities can get involved . <p> These actions will contribute to the Climate Action Plan , which will promote more innovation in reduction , reuse and recycling in communities . <p> Climate
