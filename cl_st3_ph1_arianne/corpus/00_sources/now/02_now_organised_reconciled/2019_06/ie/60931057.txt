<h> Q&amp;A : All you need to know about the Climate Action Plan <p> A. It is the document that sets out what the country must do to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Q. Sounds familiar -- do n't we already have one ? <p> A. We 've had several of sorts , most notably the 2017 National Mitigation Plan which was to have been the definitive action plan , but they lacked detail , legal status or both -- and not a lot of action was taken on foot of them anyway . <p> Q. What 's the point in a new one ? <p> A. The same reason we had the previous attempts . We 've signed up to EU and international agreements that require a joint effort by all countries to stop global temperatures rising any further and hopefully stop the climate chaos already in evidence from getting worse . <p> Q. Why should that be of interest to me ? <p> A. Well , you might care about saving the natural world and humanity from the worst effects of climate change . Nudged is ok but what 's this about being forced ? <p> A. There will be rules that say you simply can not , for example , build a new house with an oil or gas central heating system after a certain date or can not buy a new petrol or diesel car . Some of the encouragement will be quite forceful too -- an increased carbon tax , for example , is going to hit petrol , diesel , oil and gas users hard in the pocket . <p> Q.
