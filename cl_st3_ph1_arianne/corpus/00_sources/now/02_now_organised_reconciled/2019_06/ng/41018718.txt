<h> Buhari sends delegation to US to back Nigeria 's candidate for UN General Assembly presidency <p> Ahead of election for the Presidency of the United Nations General Assembly scheduled to hold in New York on June 4 , 2019 , President Muhammadu Buhari on Sunday sent a Federal Government delegation to support Nigeria 's candidate , Prof. Tijjani Muhammad- Bande . Prof. Bande , who is the Permanent Representative of Nigeria to the UN , will become the second Nigerian to be elected President of UNGA after Major General Joseph Nanven Garba ( retd ) , who held the position between 1989 and 1990 . The Permanent Secretary , Ministry of Foreign Affairs , Ambassador Mustapha Sulaiman , is leading the delegation , which also includes , the Director-General , National Intelligence Agency , Ambassador Ahmed Rufai Abubakar ; Executive Secretary , Petroleum Technology Development Fund , Dr. Bello Aliyu Gusau ; and Senior Special Assistant to the President on Media and Publicity , Malam Garba Shehu .
