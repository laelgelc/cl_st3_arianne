<h> NIHSA predicts flooding in 30 states <p> No fewer than 74 Local Government Areas in 30 states in Nigeria would experience severe flooding in June , July , August and September , the nation 's hydrological agency said . Another 279 local government areas would experience minimum flooding across the country in the period , it added . Clement Nze , Director-General , Nigeria Hydrological Services Agency , under the Federal Ministry of Water Resources , gave the prediction at a Stakeholders ' Validation Workshop in Port Harcourt on Wednesday . The workshop had the theme : Review and Validation of the Project Report for Collection of Baseline Data , Study of Flood Prone Areas and Production of Flood **29;2029;TOOLONG Maps for Hydrological Area five ( Niger South ) . Nze said : " In our 2019 prediction , we mentioned that about 74 LGA in 30 states would be heavily impacted by flooding while another 279 LGAs will not experience severe flooding . " The implication is that we should begin to take measures to avert this disaster . We are predicting that this would occur between June , July , August and September .
