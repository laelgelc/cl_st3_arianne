<h> Buhari sends delegation to support Muhammad-Bande for UNGA presidency <p> Ahead of the election for the Presidency of the United Nations General Assembly scheduled to hold in New York on June 4 , President Muhammadu Buhari on Sunday sent a delegation to support Nigeria 's candidate , Prof. Tijjani Muhammad-Bande . <p> According to Presidency in a series of tweets via the official Twitter handle @NGRPresident , Ambassador Mustapha Sulaiman is leading the delegation , which includes the DG , National Intelligence Agency , Ahmed Rufai Abubakar ; Executive Secretary , Petroleum Technology Development Fund , Bello Aliyu Gusau ; and SSA to the President on Media and Publicity , Garba Shehu . <p> The statement quoted Buhari as expressing confidence that Nigeria 's Presidency of the 74th session of the UNGA will provide a unique opportunity for Nigeria to lead the global call to tackle the political , social , economic and environmental challenges facing the world . <p> " Nigeria looks forward to working with UN member states to promote international peace and security , prevent conflict , strengthen global action to tackle climate change , ensure inclusion , human rights , and empowerment of youth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and violent extremism , and establishing functional anti-corruption mechanisms to assist countries like Nigeria recover and repatriate stolen funds , " the statement added . <p> " ( If elected ) , Prof. Bande , who is the Permanent Representative of Nigeria to the UN , will become the second Nigerian to be elected President of UNGA after Maj. Gen. Joseph Nanven Garba ( rtd ) , who held the position between 1989 and 1990 , " it added .
