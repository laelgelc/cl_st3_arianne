<h> NEMA embarks on grassroots sensitisation on flood management <p> Enugu -- The National Emergency Management Agency ( NEMA ) has embarked on grassroots sensitisation in the South East to contain impending flood in some parts of the zone . <p> The South East Zonal Coordinator of the Agency , Mr Fred Anusim made the disclosure in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Enugu on Monday . <p> Anusim , who is in charge of Enugu , Anambra and Ebonyi states , noted that preventing disaster through information dissemination was cheaper and better than solving it . <p> The coordinator mentioned Anambra , Ebonyi and Enugu where flood forecast would affect this year . <p> Anusim listed some of the council areas prone to flood in Enugu State to include Nkanu East , Enugu North , Enugu South , Oji-River and Uzo-Uwani local government areas . <p> He said that in Anambra , Ogbaru , Onitsha South , Ayamelum and Anambra East would be affected while in Ebonyi ; Ivo , Ikwo , Abakaliki and some parts of Ishielu would be affected . <p> He said that NEMA had commenced sensitization in local @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ flood prone areas noting that land disputes could cause disaster that led to poverty and lack of food . <p> " These days , due to development , most of the things we do in the environment contribute to global warming , that is why we are having climate change . <p> " In Nigeria , we are lucky that the only disaster we always face is flooding , but in most cases we are the ones that create such problems for ourselves , " the coordinator said . <p> Anusim appealed to governments of the zone to take disaster management seriously by taking proactive actions in preventing disasters such as flood and communal clashes . <p> " Disaster management is everybody 's business ;
