<h> Nigerian ambassador is next UN General Assembly president <p> UNITED NATIONS ( AP ) -- Members of the United Nations General Assembly have chosen the Nigerian ambassador to the world body as its next president . <p> They elected Tijjani Muhammad-Bande ( teeh-JAH ' -neeh moo-HAH ' -mahd BAN-deeh ) by acclamation on Tuesday to preside over the 74th U.N . session for one year , starting in September . <p> He 's the second Nigerian president of the 193-member policy-making General Assembly . Joseph Nanven Garba was president during the 1989-1990 session . <p> Muhammad-Bande succeeds Maria Fernanda Espinosa of Ecuador . <p> The incoming president told General Assembly members that when the United Nations convenes in September , its highest priorities will include climate change , universal health coverage , gender equality and the eradication of poverty and hunger . <p> The 61-year-old career diplomat is a native of Zagga in northwest Nigeria and has a master 's degree in political science from Boston University .
