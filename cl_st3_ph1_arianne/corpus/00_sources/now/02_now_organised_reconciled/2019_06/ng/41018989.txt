<h> Ogun distributes cooking stoves to residents <p> The Ogun State Government has said that as part of efforts towards reducing the emission of greenhouse gases into the environment , it has commenced the distribution of clean cooking stoves . <p> The Permanent Secretary Ministry of Environment , Mrs Yetunde Dina , said the distribution of the stoves was part of the Federal Government 's initiative to mitigate the effect of emission , which had also been embraced by the Ogun State Government for the benefit of residents of the state . <p> Dina said the aim of the distribution was to create awareness on the negative impact of cooking with firewood and to promote a change in the present cooking practice . <p> " Global warming is caused by the presence of greenhouse gases in quantity above normal and the major consequence of global warming is climate change , which is the derailment in the patterns of weather conditions over time , " she said . <p> Explaining how to use the cooking stoves to the beneficiaries , an environmentalist , Mr Olusegun Oguntoke , said with the stoves , carbons being released into the air would be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ little firewood or charcoal . <p> The Director of Special Duties and Climate change , Mr Bola Orekoya , said the stoves would make cooking safer and efficient , adding that it would go a long way at preserving the trees and cleaning up the atmosphere of dangerous gases . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
