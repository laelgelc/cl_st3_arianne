<p> * ' It 's a demonstration of confidence ' The Debt Management Office ( DMO ) , yesterday , said its second Sovereign Green Bond offering , where it sought to raise about N15 billion to support the Federal Government 's climate change project resulted in oversubscription of over 220 per cent . <p> According to the DMO Director-General , Ms. Patience Oniha , the Offer , which opened to the general public on June 3 , and closed on June 10 , was preceded by road show meetings with investors and other stakeholders in Lagos , and Abuja . <p> " The results of the second Sovereign Green Bond issuance revealed increased knowledge and awareness of Green Bonds by subscribers , and perhaps also demonstrated a greater level of commitment from the general public towards protecting the environment , " she was quoted in a statement . <p> She revealed that the total value of subscriptions received was N32.93 billion , representing 220% of the N15 billion offered .
