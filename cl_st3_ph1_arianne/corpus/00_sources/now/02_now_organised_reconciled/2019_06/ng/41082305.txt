While experts say fighting climate change will require bigger and bolder actions by governments around the world , some people are doing what they can to help , including changing long-held travel habits . <p> The trend is most prominent in Sweden , where the likes of teen climate activist Greta Thunberg have challenged travelers to confront the huge carbon cost of flying . <p> " Even I , who was climate aware 10 years ago , did n't think about flying in the way that I think now , " said Bjorstrand as she waits on the platform of Nykoping station in eastern Sweden .
