Like I said when I began , I went into farming some years ago , and over that time , I 've recorded many ups and downs , and mostly downs . However , the ups make up the most recent records , and I can chalk it all up to the Nigerian Meteorological Agency ( NiMet ) . <p> As you can imagine , weather is extremely important to the production of crops and the workload of the farmer .
