Dr. Samson Ayokunle : CAN President <p> The Christian Association of Nigeria ( CAN ) , on Monday promised to work with the leadership of the Miyetti-Allah Cattle Breeders Association of Nigeria ( MACBAN ) in the quest for peace and stability of the country . <p> The President , CAN Dr Samson Ayokunle , made the promised in a statement signed by his Special Assistant , Pastor Adebayo Oladeji on Monday in Abuja . <p> Ayokunle , who commended MACBAN for its peace initiative , promised to work with the leadership of the association in the quest for peace and stability of Nigeria . <p> The National Secretary of MACBAN , Othman Ngadzarna had led the delegation of the association to congratulate CAN president on his victory for the second term . <p> CAN president urged the leadership of the Miyetti-Allah Cattle Breeders Association of Nigeria ( MACBAN ) to protect the image of President Muhammadu Buhari by exposing the criminal Fulani herdsmen in the country . <p> According to him @
