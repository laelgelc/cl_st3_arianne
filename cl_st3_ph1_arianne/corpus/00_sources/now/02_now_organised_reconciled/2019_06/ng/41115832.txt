<h> Why UAE , others are interested in Nigerian market -- Mairo Usman <p> From being a delegate in 2014 , Mairo MT Usman is the representative of the Annual Investment Meeting ( AIM ) of United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) in Nigeria . President Muhammadu Buhari was a guest of honour at this year 's edition of AIM held in Dubai . In this interview , she speaks on the benefits of the president 's visit , Nigeria 's relationship with the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) , among others .
