<h> Osinbajo Reels out Investment Opportunities in Nigeria , Africa <p> Vice-President Yemi Osinbajo has said the huge development challenges facing Nigeria and the rest of Africa are " excellent opportunities for groundbreaking investments and innovation " . <p> He stated this as a guest at an interactive session hosted by the Council on Foreign Relations ( CFR ) , a think-tank , in New York . <p> In his opening statement on the topic " Nigeria 's Economic Prospects " , the vice-president hinged his discussion on four areas including population and environment with respect to climate change . <p> He identified the other two areas as production especially agriculture , manufacturing and technology as well as security , and the challenges of terrorism , violent Islamic extremism , social exclusion and resource conflicts . <p> Osinbajo noted that what happened in the four areas in the continent in the coming years would impact positively or negatively on the fortunes of the world . <p> He said : " The doomsday scenario is as follows : Africa 's population grows exponentially , food production is not able to match population growth , a rise in transhuman conflicts due @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ jobs and opportunity for a large poorly educated youth population , leading to aggressive illegal migration , vulnerability to extremism and the creation of a convenient breeding ground for extremist groups and a terrorist launch pad to the rest of the world . <p> " A horrifying situation indeed and possible if in the next three decades Africa drops the ball on these four indicators . <p> " Neither Africa nor the rest of the world can afford to have these scenarios playing out . " <p> Osinbajo stated that being the most populous nation in the continent , Nigeria has a critical role to play in those four areas . <p> According to him , it is not an exaggeration to say that as " Nigeria goes , so goes Africa " . <p> The vice-president said although the challenges were huge , they were excellent opportunities for " ground-breaking " investments and innovation by both local and foreign players . <p> " So take population , the mere fact that we will become the third most populous nation in the world by 2050 and the 14th largest economy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opened up our telecoms sector for private investment 18 years ago , many thought , ' yes , they have a large population but not a large enough middle class to buy and use mobile phones ' . <p> " They were wrong .
