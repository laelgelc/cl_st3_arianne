<h> LASG to sanction firms , individuals for tree cutting , others <p> The Lagos State Government says it has commenced a crackdown on companies and private individuals who continuously violate the provision of the Greening Law on setbacks and perimeter fences in the state . <p> The General Manager , Lagos State Parks and Gardens Agency , Mrs Bilikiss Adeniyi-Abiola , who made this known on Tuesday , stated that the action became necessary in the face of flagrant violation of the law by banks , telecoms companies , petrol stations , shopping malls , car parks as well as private individuals , even after several warnings to that effect . <p> She said , " It is pertinent to state here that residents and organisations must respect the trees , parks , gardens , railings , medians , lawns and all other properties of government . <p> " These facilities belong to all of us because they were provided with taxes that you and I pay ; we must guard them jealously . <p> " I want to emphasise here that the agency has zero tolerance for cutting down of trees anywhere in the state without the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has commenced constant patrol across the state to curtail acts that are damaging to the greening ambition of the state government and ensure total compliance with the Greening Law by the people . <p> She also stated that to enhance the aesthetics of the state and mitigate the effects of climate change , LASPARK would be partnering motorcycle ride-hailing firm , Gokada , to plant about 100,000 trees in the next five years in a campaign tagged , ' 100 X 25 Campaign .
