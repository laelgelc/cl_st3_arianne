<h> Oregon Senate Republicans end walkout over carbon bill <p> SALEM , Ore . ( AP ) -- Republican lawmakers returned to the Oregon Senate on Saturday , ending a walkout that began on June 20 over a carbon emissions bill they said would harm their rural constituents . <p> The minority Republicans returned after Senate President Peter Courtney said the majority Democrats lacked the votes to pass the controversial legislation . The House had previously passed the bill , one of the centerpieces of Oregon 's 2019 legislative session , which is scheduled to end on Sunday . <p> The departure of the 11 Republicans had prevented the Senate from reaching a quorum to vote on the bill that was aimed at countering climate change .
