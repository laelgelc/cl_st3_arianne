C daily or city-dwellers would experience this heat for four months of the year if swift action is not taken to restrict urbanisation and improve the quality of rural life . <p> Published in the journal Earth 's Future , the research assessed a range of possible scenarios regarding the rate of climate change and socio-economic development in 173 African cities for 2030 , 2060 , and 2090 . <p> It corroborated investigations with data from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , on the basis of climate projections and future urban demographic data .
