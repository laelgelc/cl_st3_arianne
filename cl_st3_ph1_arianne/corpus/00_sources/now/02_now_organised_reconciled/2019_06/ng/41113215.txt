We are here to do that , to see where the pain itches them most . " <p> He decried the flooding at the Ugbor area in Benin , saying that it was caused by a building erected on the natural flow path of the drains and urged residents to seek approval from the appropriate authority before they construct buildings . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Also speaking , the Commissioner for Environment and Sustainability , Dame Omua Oni-Okpaku , said a permanent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the state . <p> According to her , the drains will be de-silted while the water will be channeled to the moat while a permanent solution would be provided very soon . <p> The Commissioner tasked residents on proper waste disposal , regular clearing of drains and adjusting to new behaviours to mitigate climate change . <p> " Sensitisation is ongoing against indiscriminate building and improper waste disposal .
