<h> Yar'Adua Centre raises team for 20th anniversary <p> The Board of Trustees of the Shehu Musa Yar'Adua Foundation has set up a committee for the 20th anniversary of the commissioning of the Shehu Musa Yar'Adua Centre . In a statement signed by the Director-General of the Foundation on Friday , Jacqueline Farris , the Committee will be headed by Mr Akin Kekere-Ekun . <p> Farris also disclosed that the board also met yesterday , June 13 , to review activities for the year 2018 and programmes/projects for 2019/2020 , under the chairmanship of ex-president Olusegun Obasanjo . <p> Farris said the board reviewed the progress of the Foundation 's activities for the year 2018 and approved programmes and projects for 2019/2020 which include Climate Change Education and Awareness , Promoting Good Governance and Responsible Management of Public Resources and Confronting Gender Based Violence . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Farris also said : " The position of the 24 Yar'Adua Merit Scholars in Nigerian universities supported by the Foundation was received and commendations were sent to those who have creditably concluded their studies .
