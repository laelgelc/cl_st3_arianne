<h> Expose killers in your midst , CAN tells Miyetti Allah <p> The President , Christian Association of Nigeria , Rev. Samson Ayokunle , has asked the leadership of the Miyetti Allah Cattle Breeders Association of Nigeria to protect the image of President Muhammadu Buhari by exposing killer herdsmen in the country . <p> Ayokunle gave the counsel in Abuja on Monday while hosting a MACBAN delegation , led by its National Secretary , Baba Ngadzarna , and Alhaji Sidia Metankari , who came to congratulate him on his re-election and to seek a date for dialogue with the leadership of CAN on the ongoing national security challenges . <p> The Christian leader said CAN was not against the President , noting that all the association was demanding was for the government to be responsive to the yearnings of Nigerians . <p> He attributed the challenges facing the country to lack of good leadership , adding that good governance would change the negative narratives in the country . <p> Ayokunle said , " The leadership of CAN is not attacking President Muhammadu Buhari on religious grounds .
