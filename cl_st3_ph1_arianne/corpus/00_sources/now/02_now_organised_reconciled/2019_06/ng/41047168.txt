<p> The United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) has designated Dave Matthews Band as a Goodwill Ambassador for their powerful environmental advocacy and large-scale engagement of their fan base to take action for the planet , . <p> According to a statement by UN Environment , this is the first time a full band has received this recognition . <p> The band and nonprofit partner REVERB have been working together since 2005 on the BamaGreen Project -- an ambitious set of environmental efforts undertaken by the band on the road , in the studio , and at home , as well as the education , mobilisation and resulting actions of DMB fans around the globe , the statement said . <p> REVERB is a nonprofit organisation dedicated to empowering millions of individuals to take action toward a better future for people and the planet through direct action at concerts , the statement said . REVERB partners with artists , festivals , and venues to reduce the environmental footprint of live music and touring while engaging the music community -- music-makers and music-lovers -- to create real , positive change . <p> Entering the 15th year of the DMB/REVERB @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impact of their efforts and released a comprehensive report detailing the results of their work . Celebrated annually on June 5th , the theme for this year 's commemoration is air pollution , an issue leading to around seven million deaths each year globally .
