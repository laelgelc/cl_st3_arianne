<h> Global warning <h> Exploring humanity 's many misconceptions about life on Earth <p> Most people are fairly confident about the validity of the way they see life , the universe and everything . <p> This may be a mistake , according to award-winning Toronto-based broadcaster Ziya Tong . Her thought-provoking first book sets out to prove " that even our most stable conceptions of the world can be overturned . " <p> Tong is accomplished and popular , having hosted a variety of culture- and science-themed shows such as CBC 's Zed , Wired Science on PBS and Daily Planet on Discovery . <p> She hopes to stretch the " reality bubble " in which so many people exist . Fascinating anecdotes about bizarre occurrences such as being hit by a meteorite or the Apollo 11 astronauts ' customs forms on returning from the moon are interspersed with wide-ranging historical and scientific vignettes which illustrate and illuminate humanity 's " blind spots .
