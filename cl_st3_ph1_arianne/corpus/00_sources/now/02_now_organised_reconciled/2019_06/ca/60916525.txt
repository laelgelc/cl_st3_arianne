<h> Federal government announces $10.8-million investment in Shediac <p> SHEDIAC , N.B. -- <p> Combating climate change requires new and innovative approaches to electricity distribution . Upgrading infrastructure with new technologies will create jobs and lower energy costs while also protecting the environment by lowering carbon emissions . <p> The federal government announced a $10.8-million investment in a smart grid project that will see New Brunswick Power and Nova Scotia Power pilot new digital technologies that will create 150 jobs and reduce emissions by 2,800 tonnes by 2030 , while lowering costs for consumers . <p> The Collaborative Grid Innovation for Atlantic Smart Energy Communities project involves piloting smart grid infrastructure and will make it easier to incorporate renewable energy onto the electricity grid in Shediac , N.B. and Amherst . <p> In Shediac , 500 homes will be selected to test technologies such as smart meters , time-varying rates , smart thermostats , solar rooftops and in-home battery storage . The utility will also focus on potential cybersecurity issues posed by these connected devices . <p> " These smart investments will not only ensure that families have lower energy costs , they will also create a stronger grid @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ same time , incorporating renewables , smart meters and battery storage will significantly reduce emissions -- something all families want to do , " Fundy Royal MP and Parliamentary Secretary to the minister of Tourism , Official Languages and La Francophonie Alaina Lockhart said . <p> In Amherst , the focus will be on commercial applications , including solar panels with battery storage on commercial buildings to help manage customer demand and reliability needs , and smart electric vehicle charging trials for optimal charging . The construction of two solar farms will also help power homes and businesses in the region . <p> " We look forward to participating in this Smart Grid project as we in the Town of Amherst recognize new and innovate approaches are needed for electricity distribution , " Amherst Mayor David Kogon said in an email to the Amherst News . " One of the town 's priorities is becoming a greener community and this project will not only help Amherst become greener , but will help the entire province of Nova Scotia achieve the same goal .
