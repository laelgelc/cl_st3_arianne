Calgary was narrowly selected over Baku , Azerbaijan by a vote of 21-20 on the fourth ballot . <p> Kazakhstan , Dubai and Buenos Aires were knocked off the list in previous rounds of voting . <p> Mayor Naheed Nenshi appeared with Denis Painchaud , chair of World Petroleum Council Canada to announce the win in a video posted to social media . <p> We are proud to announce Canada and Calgary will host the 24th World Petroleum Congress in 2023 after a vote in St. Petersburg , Russia .
