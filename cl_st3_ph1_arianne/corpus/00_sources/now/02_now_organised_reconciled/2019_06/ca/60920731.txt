In the span of minutes , these black soldier fly larvae have become nutritious , high-protein food that chickens , dogs , cats and farmed Atlantic salmon love to eat . <p> " This is the future of food , " says Bruce Jowett , director of marketing for Enterra , a private B.C. company that sells farmed fly larvae products directly to commercial feed companies . " We are diverting food waste from the landfill , and black soldier fly larvae are converting it into protein .
