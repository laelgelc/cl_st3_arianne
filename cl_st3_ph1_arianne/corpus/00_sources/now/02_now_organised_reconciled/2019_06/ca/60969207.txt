Albert Gazette <p> St. Albertans are in for more wet weather for the July long weekend . <p> Kyle Fougere , meteorologist for Environment and Climate Change Canada said the stormy , rainy weather St. Albert has seen over the past two weeks is set to continue into this weekend . <p> Fougere said thunderstorm season starts mid-June and begins to taper off in the second week of August . <p> " We 're really just getting into the peak of a thunderstorm season now , " Fougere said , adding that thunderstorms and wet weather are typical for this time of year . <p> Although June is known to bring showers , Fougere said this year the month has been wetter than the average . <p> On average , it rains an average of 78 mm @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ region got 102 mm so far with a few more wet days left in the month to bring the numbers even higher . <p> But the rain is good news , Fougere said , because St. Albert and the region are coming off of the driest spring on record . <p> The meteorologist said that this year the region had less than 25 per cent of the average precipitation for the months of March , April and May . <p> The average rainfall for spring is 91 mm but this year the region only had 23 mm of precipitation fall . <p> " Most of northern Alberta was that dry , which is why we saw so many fires in northern Alberta , " Fougere said . <p> " Thankfully we have had above normal precipitation for June , which is good .
