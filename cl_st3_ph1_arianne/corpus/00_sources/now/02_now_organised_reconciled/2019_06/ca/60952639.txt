<h> What Will it Take to Win a Green New Deal ? <p> The push for a Green New Deal ( GND ) that 's become a big topic of political discussion in the US has come north . Behind the scenes , staff from a number of major NGOs including Greenpeace and Leadnow are playing key roles in the initiative . <p> The Pact calls the GND " a vision of rapid , inclusive and far-reaching transition , to slash emissions , protect critical biodiversity , meet the demands of the multiple crises we face , and create over a million jobs in the process .
