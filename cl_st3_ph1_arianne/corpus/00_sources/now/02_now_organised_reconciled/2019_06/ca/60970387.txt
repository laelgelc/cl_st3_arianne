These are caused by inflammation of the brain or spinal cord and can be fatal . <p> Only one in 150 people who get the virus develop neuroinvasive disease , however . The elderly and anyone with immuno-compromised conditions are most at risk . <p> Dr. Denise Werker , Saskatchewan 's deputy chief medical health officer , said there is a specific type of mosquito , culex tarsalis , that carries West Nile virus .
