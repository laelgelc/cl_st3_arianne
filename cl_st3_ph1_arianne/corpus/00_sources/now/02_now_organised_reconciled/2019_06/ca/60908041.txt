<h> Killing of Whooping Crane <p> Crime Stoppers in partnership with The Wildlife Enforcement Division of Environment &amp; Climate Change Canada are seeking the public 's assistance in identifying suspects with relation to the senseless killing of a Whooping Crane . <p> It is alleged that in the evening hours of May 5th unknown culprit(s) attended on private property in the area of N Line and 10th Side Road on the north end of Barrie Island and shot a Whooping Crane . <p> The Whooping Crane is a rare and endangered species . This crane belonged to a small population of 100 raised and released cranes that are being monitored with telemetry bands . <p> Any information leading to the identity of the persons or vehicles involved would be appreciated . <p> Help us solve crime by calling Crime Stoppers at ( 705 ) 942-7867 or 1-800-222-8477 .
