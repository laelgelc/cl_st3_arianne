<h> ' Close all Slaughterhouses ' march today at the Sails <p> The Okanagan March to Close All Slaughterhouses starts today at 11:45 a.m. at the Sails sculpture in downtown Kelowna . <p> The local march is part of a network of similar events in 32 cities in 12 countries . <p> The group of concerned citizens will march a round-trip of three kilometres back to the Sails for a vigil that will last until 2:30 p.m . <p> As the name of the march indicates , the goal is the closure of all slaughterhouses worldwide to stop the death of 70 billion land animals ( including chickens ) and 1,000 billion aquatic animals ( all kinds of fish and seafood ) annually for food . <p> The movement wants all slaughterhouses shuttered because animals are feeling beings , meat production inevitably leads to the death of animals , living conditions and slaughter causes suffering and because eating animals is not necessary . <p> " Robust scientific data has proven that diet-related illnesses , including many types of cancers , obesity , diabetes and heart disease are directly linked to the consumption of animal products , " reads the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ RCMP officer , Dan Moskaluk . <p> Moskaluk switched to a plant-based diet after being diagnosed with cancer and credits the switch with helping to save his life . <p> " In addition to causing us to suffer and die from preventable illnesses , animal agriculture is the leading cause of our current environmental degradation and rapid climate change , " added Moskaluk.
