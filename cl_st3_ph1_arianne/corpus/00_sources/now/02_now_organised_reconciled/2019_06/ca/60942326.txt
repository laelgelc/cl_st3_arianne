<h> MFDA issues Notice of Settlement Hearing in respect of Mubarak Baksh <p> TORONTO , June 20 , 2019 /CNW/ - The Mutual Fund Dealers Association of Canada ( " MFDA " ) announced that it has issued a Notice of Settlement Hearing dated May 27 , 2019 regarding the presentation , review and consideration of a proposed settlement agreement by a Hearing Panel of the MFDA 's Central Regional Council . <p> The settlement agreement will be between Staff of the MFDA and Mubarak Baksh ( " Respondent " ) and involves matters for which the Respondent may be disciplined by a Hearing Panel pursuant to MFDA By-laws . <p> The proposed settlement agreement concerns the allegation that between December 2014 and January 2017 , the Respondent obtained and possessed , 31 pre-signed account forms in respect of 13 clients , contrary to MFDA Rule 2.1.1 . <p> The settlement hearing will take place on August 29 , 2019 , commencing at 10:00 a.m .
