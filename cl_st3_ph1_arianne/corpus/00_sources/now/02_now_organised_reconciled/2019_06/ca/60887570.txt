<p> Construction could begin later this year on Diageo 's planned revival of the legendary Port Ellen Distillery on Islay , which was closed in 1983 . Only the seaside warehouses and the old kiln building are left , and they 'll play a key role in the distillery 's future when it opens in 2021 -- assuming local officials sign off on planning approval for the project . That application was submitted in mid-May , with a decision expected later this summer .
