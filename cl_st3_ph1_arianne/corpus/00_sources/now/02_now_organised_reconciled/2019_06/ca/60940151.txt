Nazi Germany 's treatment of Jewish people is an example of mass destruction of human life which fits as an aspect of the definition of genocide set out in the UN convention . <p> However , certain aspects of genocide do not necessarily result in the destruction of human life . Human dignity is attacked by actions of people in authority . Usually , it is government policy and its subsequent implementation which results in this kind of genocide . Intention can be inferred from the results , including the length of time of said implementation . <p> Canada 's stated intention of stripping away the " Indian " from the First Nations people did , and continues to do , exactly this . Its reserve system , its education policy , its apparent commitment not to honour treaties or Aboriginal title and thus keep First Nations people in a state of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ treatment of First Nations people at all levels of the legal system and the deliberate removal of children from the family setting in different policy ways , are all aspects of the results of genocide , which meets its definition . <p> The MMIWG issue is related to the above-mentioned government policy initiatives .
