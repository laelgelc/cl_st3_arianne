<h> Financial firms are facing climate reality <h> Financial firms are facing climate reality <p> Recognized by financial-market participants as well as policy-setting bodies , climate change has taken centre stage as an economic and financial risk . That shift in perspective has catalyzed greater awareness in a range of industries -- and notably , in the case of financial services , more action . <p> In a global survey of 27 major financial institutions -- including , banks , asset managers , and insurers -- which collectively represent around US$20 trillion in assets , the Global Association of Risk Professionals ( GARP ) found more than 80% of participants have already identified climate-related risks and opportunities . That includes efforts to facilitate a shift toward a low-carbon economy through new offerings like green bonds ( 60% ) and modifications of existing products ( 40% ) . <p> " Financial institutions ' treatment of climate risk has changed dramatically over the past five years , " said Jo Paisley , Co-President of the GARP Research Institute .
