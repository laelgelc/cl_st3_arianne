<h> How our use of electricity affects climate change <p> Susan Koswan highlights the urgency of continuing to tackle climate change . While there is still plenty of room for improvement in our overall energy use in Ontario , electricity generation is an area where significant progress has been made . Data from the Independent Electricity System Operator shows that transmission-connected generator output in 2018 was nuclear , 61 per cent ; hydroelectric , 25 per cent ; wind , seven per cent ; gas/oil , six per cent ; and solar , less than one per cent . These percentages are for electrical energy actually generated , typically measured in TWh . The breakdown in Koswan 's column described peak generating capacity , typically measured in GW . Peak capacity refers to the maximum amount of electricity that could be generated , if needed . Because electricity demand varies throughout the year , not all that capacity is utilized at all times . Nuclear generating plants operate at full output most of the time .
