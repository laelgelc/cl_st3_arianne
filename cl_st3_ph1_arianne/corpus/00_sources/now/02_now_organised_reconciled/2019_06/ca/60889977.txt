<p> March 4 : Alberta has suffered yet another blow in its efforts to build a pipeline to carry crude out of the province . As Tom Vernon reports , this comes from a U.S. decision delaying Enbridge Line 3 by a year . <p> The Minnesota Court of Appeals on Monday said state regulators must conduct a further review for Enbridge Energy 's plan to replace its deteriorating Line 3 crude oil pipeline because the project 's environmental impact statement does n't address the possibility of an oil spill into the Lake Superior watershed . <p> The Public Utilities Commission last year signed off on what was intended to be the Minnesota Department of Commerce 's final environmental review of the project .
