<h> The environment is becoming the hot button issue of the federal election : Nanos survey <p> The environment is emerging as " one of the defining battle grounds " for the upcoming federal election , according to Nanos Research analyst Nik Nanos . <p> " For the first time in 15 years , the last four weeks have had the environment as the top national issue of concern , " Nanos told CTV News . " More important than jobs , more important than health care , more important than immigration . " <p> Concerns about the environment have been rising in the public consciousness ever since a landmark UN report was put out last fall , which laid out a grim picture of the future if world leaders across the globe ca n't slow down human-caused warming of the planet . <p> In the political sphere , the issue of how best to tackle climate change has been a source of contention , with federal Conservative leader Andrew Scheer and several premiers opposing the Liberal government 's federal carbon tax . <p> An Ontario court ruled Friday that the federal government 's carbon tax is important to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This ruling means the " the battle lines have been drawn , " Nanos said . <p> " This recent court decision is a direct body blow to the government of Ontario , the government of Alberta , the government of Saskatchewan , because what it says is that the feds can intervene and take leadership on environmental issues and on the carbon tax , " Nanos said .
