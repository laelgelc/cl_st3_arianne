<p> In the next generation , the fact of climate change caused by human behaviour will become a dominant theme , if not the dominant theme , in our politics . Climate scientists are already telling us that humanity faces a global emergency . According to a 2018 United Nations report , the most likely increase in average temperature -- of 2.7 degrees above pre-industrial levels by 2040 -- will have disastrous consequences and displace tens of millions of people . Mainstream scientists believe the opportunity to avoid serious change has now passed ; the question is how much ecological destruction and human suffering climate change will wreak . We have an obligation to mitigate that change . <p> That means changing how we live and where we live . These are local questions . The only ethical imperative for urban planning in the 21st century will be to mitigate the effects of climate change . If we want to substantially reduce our society 's carbon emissions , we need to end sprawl and put more people 's homes within walking distance of their workplaces , schools and amenities .
