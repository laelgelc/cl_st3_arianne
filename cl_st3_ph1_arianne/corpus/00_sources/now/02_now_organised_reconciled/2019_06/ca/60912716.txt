This work is expected to be complete by the fall of 2019 . " <p> Nancy Anningson , the Ecology Action Centre 's coastal adaptation senior co-ordinator , said the EAC 's preferred approach is not to " hard armour " areas , meaning build a new seawall with concrete abutments or structures because of the potential to divert wave energy and increase erosion of neighbouring land . <p> " Our preferred approach is something like @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hard element but then lots of more natural approaches or , in a perfect world , a completely natural approach like a living shoreline , they call it , " Anningson said in a telephone interview on Tuesday . <p> But in this case , there already was a seawall in place . <p> " So I would say , initially my thought is it 's good that they are taking sea level rise into account and we see a lot of things along the Halifax waterfront that you sometimes scratch your head and wonder , " she said . <p> Anningson said Nova Scotia is experiencing greater sea level rise than other areas .
