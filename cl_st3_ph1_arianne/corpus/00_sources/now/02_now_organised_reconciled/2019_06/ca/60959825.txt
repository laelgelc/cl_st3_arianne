( Ken Linton/CBC ) <p> Prince Edward Island businesses and consumers are getting set for the ban on plastic checkout bags that takes effect on July 1 . <p> P.E.I. will become the first province in Canada to ban the single-use bags , under the Plastic Bag Reduction Act . <p> " I was up along the North Shore in the resort area last week and I was pleasantly surprised with how the business community up there has been making the adjustment , " said John Hughes , with the Department of Environment , Water and Climate Change . <p> Hughes has been travelling the Island , speaking to residents and businesses , in anticipation of the new rules . <p> John Hughes , from P.E.I. 's Environment Department , prepares to show a group from the Greater Charlottetown Area Chamber of Commerce what kinds of paper and reusable bags will be acceptable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> He said he has heard some concerns from the business community . <p> " They 're concerned with how long is it going to take them to use up their existing supply of bags , " Hughes said . <p> " Are they able to find a satisfactory supply of paper bags and reusable alternatives ? Because this is really about promoting the reusable bags . " <h> Education not enforcement <p> Hughes said the intent of the legislation is not to have 30 million paper bags replacing 30 million plastic bags , but to encourage Islanders to find ways to reuse bags , or eliminate them entirely . <p> " It can be a bit of a challenge because we 've been using plastic for so long now , " Hughes said . <p> " The infrastructure to supply paper and reusable bags has to be built up again .
