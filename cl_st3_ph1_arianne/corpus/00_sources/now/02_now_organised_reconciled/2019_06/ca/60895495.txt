<h> On World Environment Day , Government of Canada to help Forests Ontario plant 50 million trees Fran ? ais <p> OTTAWA , June 5 , 2019 /CNW/ - Canadians know that forests are vital to our future . Forests are " the lungs of the world , " producing oxygen we need to breathe and cleaning the air and water . They are also home to an abundance of plants , animals and other species . Investing in Canada 's forests creates a natural legacy for generations of Canadians . <p> Keeping forests healthy is also one of the best ways to fight climate change , because they absorb carbon pollution and protect against erosion in flood zones .
