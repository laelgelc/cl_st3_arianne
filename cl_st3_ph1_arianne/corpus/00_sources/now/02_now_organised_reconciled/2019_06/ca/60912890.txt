The definition might also be extended to include the idea of separating oneself from the concerns of others within a country . In recent times we have seen this attitude or idea expressed by the President of the United States . <p> We might also take a look at the happenings in England . With the resignation of Theresa May , and the possibility of Boris Johnson taking her place , isolationism might become the order of the day in that part of the world . Just prior to his visit to England Donald Trump gave Johnson his support , saying that he would be ' very good ' as the next UK Prime Minister .
