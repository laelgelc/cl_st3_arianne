The UN says family farming produces about 80 per cent of the world 's food , on 70-80 per cent of the available farmland . That figure is significantly higher in Canada , where family farms are said to account for as much as 97 per cent of all farms . This is not a widely understood statistic in Canada , where activists have convinced the public " factory farms " are taking over the country . <p> Put it in perspective . UN membership consists of many countries where family farmers are " smallholders " and the main producers . Accordingly , you see a great deal of significance put on farmers who , by Canadian standards , have very small operations . Putting family farms in perspective for Canadian consumers is vital for promoting a greater understanding of agriculture 's real needs .
