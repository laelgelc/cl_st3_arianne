<h> Renfrew Community Association has received a grant to study the idea <p> A northeast Calgary neighbourhood has received a $194,000 grant to study the feasibility of building a community solar garden . <p> Much like a regular community garden , it would provide benefits for local residents . But they 'd come in the form of power profits , not hearty greens . <p> " They have their own little piece of it and their own investment that they have over the life of the system , " says Paul Gill , who first pitched the project to the Renfrew Community Association and applied for the grant . <p> " We are kind of the guinea pig for this . " <p> Gill is an electrical engineer and a big advocate for renewable energy . He has solar panels installed on his own home , which also uses geothermal power , and drives an electric car . <p> " It 's always striving toward a sustainable future .
