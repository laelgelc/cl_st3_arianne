<h> Have your say on changes to city 's main planning document <p> Greater Sudbury residents are invited to share their comments at a special planning committee meeting June 26 , where changes to the city 's main planning document will be discussed . <p> Phase 2 of the Official Plan review focuses on water/wastewater , economic development and transportation needs throughout Greater Sudbury . <p> A news release from the city said comments in each of these areas , as well as general comments on the planning process , will be considered prior to the completion of the OP . <p> Anyone interested in making a presentation is asked to attend in person June 26 at 1 p.m. , in council chambers of Tom Davies Square , 200 Brady St. <p> Phase 1 of the review focused on growth and settlement , lake water quality , source water protection , climate change , planning for an aging population , local food systems and natural and built heritage . That part of the review was approved by the Ministry of Municipal Affairs and Housing on April 26 .
