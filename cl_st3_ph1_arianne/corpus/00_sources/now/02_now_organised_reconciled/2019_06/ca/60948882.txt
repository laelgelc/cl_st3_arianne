<p> Throughout the first half of the year , Prime Minister Justin Trudeau was against the ropes . He was hammered almost daily by first the China mess and then Lavscam and it even seemed -- for a few days -- that his political demise was imminent . <p> A lot of that is gone now . Yes , Trudeau 's numbers dropped massively in the polls and remain that way . Yes , he could still lose the upcoming election . But he 's no longer in a daily defensive posture . In some ways he 's even back on top framing the debate again . <p> Case in point : Did Andrew Scheer actually want to release his climate change plan last week ? Sure , he 's long wanted to ax the carbon tax . But was drafting this 60-page plan about putting a cap on large emitters and reducing global emissions one of his driving issues , one of the things that spurred him to run for leader in the first place ? That 's doubtful . <p> More likely , the Conservatives released it because the Liberals pushed them to do it . Even if that 's true though , there 's only so much time you can spend placating your critics and enemies . They 'll gladly keep you playing the game according to their rules if you let them keep you busy like that . There 's a risk this could become a trend with Scheer . <p> With Ontario Premier Doug Ford , it 's worse .
