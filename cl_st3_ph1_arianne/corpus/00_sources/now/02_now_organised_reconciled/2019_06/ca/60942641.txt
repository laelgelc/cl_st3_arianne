( CBC ) <p> A task force is recommending that a risk management strategy be developed for the Bearspaw reservoir just northwest of Calgary . <p> The reservoir is a key source of drinking water for Calgary but it 's also important to residents in Rocky View County and to TransAlta , which uses the water for hydro generation . <p> The reservoir was created in the 1950s following the construction of the Bearspaw dam on the Bow River . Located on the city 's western boundary , it provides 60 to 65 per cent of Calgary 's drinking water . <p> However , a lack of regulation means that there are no restrictions on recreational use of the water body . <p> With increasing development around the reservoir , there are concerns about the growing risks associated with human activity that could result in pollution , affecting water quality . <p> Last year , the city , the county and TransAlta put together a task force to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Recommendations are in <p> The committee 's vice-chair , Coun.
