How badly had he plagiarized certain sections of his platform from environmental groups ? <p> But a central feature of Biden 's plan is receiving much less attention , despite having potentially massive global implications . In addition to promising to make the U.S. carbon-neutral by 2050 and spending $1.7 trillion of federal money on green infrastructure -- elements that have so far dominated headlines -- Biden is also vowing to throw America 's weight behind a diplomatic effort to block Chinese investment and support for climate-destroying coal plants . <p> His plan accuses China of " financing billions of dollars of dirty fossil fuel energy projects across Asia and beyond .
