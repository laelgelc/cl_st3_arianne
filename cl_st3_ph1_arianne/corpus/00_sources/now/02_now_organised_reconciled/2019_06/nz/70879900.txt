<h> Pacific ministers debate fisheries issues <p> Pacific Islands Forum fisheries ministers are meeting this week to tackle a broad range of issues around the protection and security of the region 's valuable tuna resources . <p> Photo : RNZI / Johnny Blades <p> The Forum Fisheries Agency meeting is in Pohnpei in the Federated States of Micronesia . <p> The FFA director general , Manu Tupou-Roosen , said ministers recognise they must ensure the major offshore resource - tuna - is not only managed sustainably , but that there are increased social and economic benefits for Pacific people . <p> The ministers will look at the impact of climate change on tuna fisheries , finalise a new regional long-line strategy , address human rights abuses and labour conditions for crew on fishing vessels <p> There are also calls for them to consider the diminishing coastal fisheries , which are source of much of the food consumed by Pacific Islanders .
