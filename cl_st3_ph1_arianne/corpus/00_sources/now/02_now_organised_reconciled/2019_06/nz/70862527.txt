<h> Wellington library building more than just bricks and mortar <p> Wellington Central Library -- knocking down the library would be like ripping out a chapter in a book , says Angela Foster . <p> Six weeks ago , Wellington Mayor Justin Lester announced the immediate closure of Wellington 's Central Library . After receiving a report on the building 's seismic vulnerability , the mayor said the library would be shut indefinitely while options for its future were considered . <p> The mayor , rightly , is mindful of the safety of everyone who works in or visits the library . However , it is concerning that the option that already seems to be favoured is the one that 's the most extreme : demolition . <p> We do n't know who is advising the mayor and council . It 's an outstanding example of the architecture of its era , a postmodern milestone in the continuum of Wellington architecture that stretches from the mid-19th century to the present day . <p> Knocking down the library would be like ripping out a chapter in a book .
