<h> Rural communities do n't ask for a lot <p> June is always a busy time in council as we get ready to sign off our Annual Plan for the next 12 months and also set the rates . It is a complex process where every activity that we do has to be accounted for and budgeted for . We also need to make sure it is consistent with what we said we would do in the Long Term Plan . <p> This year there were no material changes to our Long Term Plan , and if that is the case , councils are not required to consult with their communities . We decided that it was good to catch up anyway , and we had 16 Council Cuppas around the region to touch base and listen to residents ' concerns and ideas . <p> One thing that struck me when we visited some of our rural communities up the Coast , as well as to the west , is that they do not ask for a lot .
