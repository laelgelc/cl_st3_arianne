<h> Granular approach needed for cost-effective emissions reduction <p> Cost-effective emissions reduction will require a granular approach and a willingness to rethink traditional ways energy is used in the economy , a visiting climate and sustainability expert says . <p> Michael Liebreich says the interactions between energy , transport , industry and new technologies can be very complicated . <p> That complexity sometimes allows people to promote " a lot of stupid things " which are hard to disprove . People , and sometimes governments , also tend to look for simple answers when the best options may vary by industry and even within industries . <p> Liebreich , visiting New Zealand as a guest of Z Energy , says policymakers and firms need to focus on the tasks that need to be performed in the economy and how they could be delivered in a carbon-free way . <p> That could show that batteries can power coastal shipping , but that port-side hydrogen fuel-cells are needed to recharge occasional visits by large cruise liners .
