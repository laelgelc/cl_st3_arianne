<p> Hurunui College students have been translocating kiwi , trapping stoats and monitoring whio in the Nina Valley . <p> Relocating kiwi , trapping possums , monitoring whio ( blue ducks ) and writing submissions is all in a day 's work for a North Canterbury 's restoration group . <p> The Nina Valley Restoration Group comprises the older students of Hurunui College , an area school in Hawarden , and celebrates its 10th anniversary this year . <p> Teacher Tim Kelly said they had forged some valuable relationships over the decade , particularly with the Department of Conservation ( DOC ) . <p> supplied <p> The students learned how to examine the stomach contents of stoats they had trapped . <p> " DOC have been really good about the students being hands on . They have trained the kids to use the telemetry gear to track kiwi , they show them how to change transmitters and how to monitor the welfare of the kiwi . We 've taken part in both Operation Nest Egg and wild-to-wild transfers . " <p> The year 7 to 13 students have been active in translocation projects , working alongside DOC rangers to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pass for release in the Nina Valley , and they all know how to handle the birds . <p> supplied <p> The project gives students hands on experience in the conservation field . <p> Weekend trips are held monthly to check the school 's 20-kilometre network of trap lines . The work is extra-curricular , but related activities are regularly incorporated into class work . A group of students recently visited Lincoln University to learn how to analyse the stomach contents of stoats they had trapped . <p> " We 're always looking for research opportunities to test things for people , like carrying out lure tests or trialling DOC-approved traps . We 're also keen to incorporate technologies into what we do -- new trapping technology and new telemetry , " Kelly said . <p> The school 's Electronic Possum Lures project is one example of investigating new technologies and involves the students designing and trialling electronic lures . <p> " The project has been developed during school time . Students are learning about the design thinking process : they start with a problem -- we 're not catching @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to solve the problem . They then make prototypes , the best of which are reproduced and tested in a scientific manner in the bush . " <p> Kelly said Hurunui College students were also becoming more involved in environmentalism and sustainability , and had made submissions to Environment Canterbury on public policy and climate change . <p> The group is also aligned with the Kea Conservation Trust , which has fitted transmitters to four kea in the Nina Valley . The students are charged with monitoring the kea , along with the whio already being monitored through their river surveys . <p> More kiwi translocations are likely once bird numbers build up in Arthur 's Pass . In the meantime , students are continuing to monitor the success of the kiwi already translocated to Nina Valley , and are hopeful there will soon be kiwi chicks growing up in their backyard .
