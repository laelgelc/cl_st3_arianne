<h> New Zealand tourism operators stepping up to help the environment <p> In the fourth of a 1 NEWS series on being a more sustainable consumer , Katie Bradford looks at what Kiwi tourism operators are doing to protect the environment . <p> As the increasing numbers of visitors to our shores bring with them some big challenges , many of New Zealand 's tourism operators are doing their best to try and limit negative impacts on the environment . <p> Tourists spent $39 billion here in 2018 , with over 3.7 million visitors arriving every year , a number that 's climbing annually . <p> Now , tourism operators around the country are hoping to try and preserve the environment so New Zealand 's natural wonders can be enjoyed by future generations . <p> " We have a vision of New Zealand being the most sustainable tourism industry , we 're not there yet but we think we can get there , " Chief Executive of Tourism Industry Aotearoa Chris Roberts says . <p> In just over a year , more than 1100 tourism operators have signed up to the sustainable tourism initiative . <p> " They need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ business takes that responsibility and together as an industry we 're addressing that big question of carbon , " Mr Roberts told 1 NEWS . <p> The Tourism Industry Association says it will monitor just how sustainable businesses really are . <p> " You need to be thinking long term as a business owner about being here and being a good citizen and leaving the world , and leaving New Zealand better than you found it , " Mr Roberts says . <p> The hope is tourists are also thinking about the impact of climate change , especially given the distance many travel . <p> The combined effort is all work that needs to be done if New Zealand really is to be clean , green and beautiful .
