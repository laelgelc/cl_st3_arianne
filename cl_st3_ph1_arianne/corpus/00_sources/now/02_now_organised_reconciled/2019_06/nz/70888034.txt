He wanders around looking for food . He almost does n't pay attention to people and cars , " Oleg Krashevsky , a local wildlife expert who filmed the animal close up , told CNN . " He is quite young and possibly lost his mother . " <p> " He probably lost orientation and went south , " Krashevsky added .
