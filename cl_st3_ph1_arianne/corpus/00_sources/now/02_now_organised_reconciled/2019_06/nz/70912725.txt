<h> Porirua 's Annual Plan signals significant growth <p> Porirua City Council last night adopted its Annual Plan while flagging some challenging and exciting times ahead for the city . <p> Transmission Gully is scheduled for completion in 2020 and will bring with it the first significant changes to our roading network in decades . It will affect the way the city operates as the existing state highway network becomes local roads that <p> reconnect the east and west of our urban area . Alongside this change Porirua is facing significant residential growth - far outpacing our previous best estimates . <p> To help manage this , a Growth Strategy was finalised in March 2019 to capture these changes and allow us to sensibly develop a new District Plan to lay out new rules for the way our city develops . <p> Mayor Mike Tana says these challenges are stretching the Council 's financial and staffing resources and causing us to look hard at the way we operate . <p> " We have heard the message from our community clearly that you think our rates are already high and you do n't want to pay more from your household @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this we have committed to two things for the year ahead . <p> " Firstly we have kept to the commitment we made in our Long-term Plan to contain our rates increases to an average of 4.98% . We have improved the salaries of our lowest paid workers by adopting the Living Wage as the minimum wage for our staff . <p> " None of this is easy .
