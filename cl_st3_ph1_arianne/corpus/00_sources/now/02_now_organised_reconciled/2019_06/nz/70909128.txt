<p> News from NZ Government Minister of State Services Chris Hipkins today announced the most significant changes to the New Zealand Public Service since the State Sector Act of 1988 . <p> The State Sector Act 1988 will be repealed and replaced with a new Public Service Act . This will underpin a modernising of the Public Service for the good of New Zealanders and make it easier to tackle the biggest challenges facing the Government of the day , Chris Hipkins said . <p> Under the changes , boards made up of chief executives from relevant government agencies will be established to tackle the most pressing issues . These boards , or joint ventures , would be accountable to a single minister and receive direct budget appropriations . Public servants from across the system will be deployed as required . <p> " The public service is operating in a fast changing and unpredictable context where major social , demographic and technology driven changes are reshaping the world as we know it , " Chris Hipkins said . <p> " When it comes to the really big and complex challenges it does n't work anymore to put a single @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of chief executives jointly accountable for delivering on complex government priorities . This ca n't happen under the current Act . Chris Hipkins said the shift to a single , unified public service approach would be complemented by cultural change .
