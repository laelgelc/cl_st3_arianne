<h> Blowing in the wind <p> Is New Zealand the Saudi Arabia of wind ? Bruce Munro talks to those gearing up for a decarbonised economy that will be largely powered by wind . <p> For decades , Saudi Arabia has been synonymous with oil and wealth . <p> From 1985 to 2014 , petrol in the oil-rich Arabian kingdom cost 28c a litre , there was no personal income tax and the government books were constantly in the black -- oily pitch-black .
