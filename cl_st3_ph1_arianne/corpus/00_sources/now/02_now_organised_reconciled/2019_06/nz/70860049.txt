<p> The centre 's acting director , Bill Hamblin , said luckily these quakes did n't match the devastation of last year 's 7.5 quake in the Highlands . But he said they were reminders of the need to be prepared . <p> " One of the things we 're now working on feverishly is to improve our disaster capacity , for response and being prepared ; putting in regional stores and supplies ; pre-positioning non-perishable supplies ; and having the equipment to move those supplies . " <p> Dr Hamblin said that over the years the national disaster response capacity had diminished , for various reasons . <p> " So , it 's now that we have a look at this , and we see the effects of climate change , we see more of these issues happening with landslips etc in the country , " he said . <p> Dr Hamblin cited last month 's landslide in Tsak Valley of Enga province , which killed eight people as a village was wiped out . <p> He also cited recent floods in Vanimo-Green which decimated food crops .
