But as things played out , we learned a lot . <p> Former Representative Beto O'Rourke 's glitzy entrance into the race has worked against him , as many voters came to see the Texas Democrat as a political lightweight with an outsize sense of entitlement . At times he sounded halting and appeared ill at ease ; at one juncture he allowed New York Mayor Bill de Blasio to talk over him .
