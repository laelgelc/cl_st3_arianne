<h> Climate change could increase risk of conflict , says study <p> Climate change could have an increasing impact on the risk of armed conflict in the future , according to a new study . <p> The study , published in the journal Nature and led by Stanford University in the US , analyses the results of past research on the issue . It concludes that if the earth warmed by 4 degrees Celsius - the path we might be on with no intervention - there would be a 26 per cent chance of a substantial increase in conflict risk . At 2 degrees , the chance was 13 per cent . <p> The risk stems from extreme weather and related disasters , which can damage food production and farming as well as impacting on economic stability and raising inequality , the researchers said . <p> Andrew Renneisen <p> Nurses measure Ammar Khalid , ten months , while undergoing treatment for severe acute malnutrition at a health clinic in Yemen in 2018 .
