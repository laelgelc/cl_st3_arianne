<p> Osaka Japan , June 27 ( ANI ) : India and Japan on Thursday agreed to hold a ' two-plus-two ' dialogue of their foreign and defence ministers before Prime Minister Shinzo Abe visits India later this year.The top-level talks to discuss issues related to policy and security was decided at a meeting between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Abe during their meeting on the sidelines of G-20 Summit in Osaka earlier today . " Before the Abe meeting later this year there would be a number of important meetings and the most important would be the first 2+2 ministerial meeting . Foreign ministers and defence ministers of the two countries will meet and discuss issues related to policies and security , " Foreign Secretary Vijay Gokhale , told the media briefing on the meeting between Modi and Abe.However , the date of the meeting was not announced .
