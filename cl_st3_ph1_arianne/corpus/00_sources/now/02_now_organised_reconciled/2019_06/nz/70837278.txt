<h> Rising sea levels due to earthquakes may impact insurers <p> An expert has warned that increased seismic activity could potentially cause sea levels to rise faster than climate change -- a concern that may impact insurance policies in active earthquake zones like New Zealand . <p> Shin-Chan Han , a professor at the University of Newcastle in Australia , told CGTN that sea levels in American Samoa rose at five times the global average due to land subsidence -- a sinking effect in the Earth 's crust that was triggered by the Samoa-Tongan earthquakes in 2009 . <p> " This could be even more dangerous to the region than the impact of climate change , " Han told CGTN . <p> " The predicted sea level rise from land subsidence in American Samoa is just as much , if not more , than the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change projected a rise in the Samoan Islands due to climate change under the highest CO2 emission scenario . " <p> According to Han , governments in earthquake zones -- including New Zealand - should assess how this finding will affect their levels of preparedness .
