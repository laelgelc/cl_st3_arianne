<h> Top of the south strategy instigators start series of community conversations <p> Nelson , as well as towns in Tasman district and Marlborough , are scheduled to host a series of hui to help with the development of a top of the south intergenerational strategy . <p> Instigators of a planned top of the south strategy begin a scheduled series of hui on Monday to hear from the wider community . <p> Six hui are planned across Nelson , Tasman and Marlborough throughout July and August in the series of community conversations called Te Tauihu Talks . <p> The information gathered at the hui will then be fed into the development of the Wakatu Incorporation-led Te Tauihu Intergenerational Strategy , which is scheduled to be launched at the end of September . <p> Resilience across the economy , environment and people is the topic of the first hui , to be held at the Suter Gallery in Nelson on Monday evening from 6pm to 8pm .
