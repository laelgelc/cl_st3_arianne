<p> What does the shift in risk pricing by insurance companies mean for Kiwis ? Do insurers have a social responsibility ? <p> According to CoreLogic NZ insurance lead Richard Deakin , there is no doubt insurance premiums will rise again . While New Zealand is unique with state insurer the Earthquake Commission ( EQC ) holding a socially responsible position , private insurers are in a different boat , he said in a commentary . <p> Deakin 's comments follow the changing face of insurance cover in Wellington . This include IAG 's announcement that it would roll out a risk-based pricing model to all its brands starting July , which came a year after Tower 's decision to shift into risk-based pricing .
