<h> $154m earmarked for development assistance in Fiji <p> Fiji expects a total value of Official Development Assistance ( ODA ) in 2019-2020 to be F$154.4 million , reports FBC News . <p> According to FBC 's northern editor Elenoa Turagaiviu , this will consist of $13.8 million to be disbursed as cash grants and $140.6 million of aid-in-kind contributions . <p> According to the 2019-2020 Budget Supplement , the priority areas for the ODA support in 2019-2020 include education , health , agriculture , financial inclusion , poverty alleviation , social mitigation and renewable energy . <p> The Economic Services sector is expected to receive the majority of the cash grants at $6.5 million , of which $4.0 million will be provided by the European Union ( EU ) for the Sustainable Rural Livelihood programme , reports FBC News . <p> In addition , $1.5 million will be provided by the World Bank for the REDD+ project , while the Government of India has committed $1.0 million towards the Micro Small Business Grant initiative . <p> About $0.3 million will be provided by the UNDP for the Fiji Ridge to Reef Project , while a grant of $0.5 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Energy Financing Project .
