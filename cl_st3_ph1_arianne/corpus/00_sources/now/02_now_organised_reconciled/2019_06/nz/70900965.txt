In The New Photography , Athol McCredie traces the memories and modus operandi of eight New Zealand photographers who , in the 1960s and 70s , pushed our photography into the realm of art . <p> We dithered for days over which to feature here : John Fields , and his images of East Cape and Oneroa ?
