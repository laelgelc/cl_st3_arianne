" <p> " We also address important areas neglected by the coalition Government , such as water storage , biotechnology and food safety . " <p> Bridges claims the document is part of the biggest policy development process by a parliamentary opposition in over a decade . <p> " National holds every rural seat in Parliament except one and we 're proud to represent rural New Zealand .
