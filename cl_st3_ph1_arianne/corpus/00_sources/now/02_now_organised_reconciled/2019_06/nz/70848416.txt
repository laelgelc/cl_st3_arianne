<h> Local authors shortlisted for awards <p> Debbie McCauley ( centre back , holding book ) promotes her book Legends of Mauao across from the landmark itself . <p> Several local authors and illustrators have been shortlisted for the New Zealand Book Awards for Children and Young Adults . <p> The awards celebrate the contribution that children 's authors and illustrators make to ' build national identity and cultural heritage ' . <p> A total of 164 entries were received for the awards this year , with 29 books making the shortlist . <p> Ko Mauao te Maunga : Legend of Mauao , written by Debbie McCauley and illustrated by Debbie Tipuna is shortlisted for the Elsie Lock Award for Non-Fiction . Slice of Heaven , written by Des O ' Leary is shortlisted for Best First Book . <p> Ko Mauao te Maunga retells the traditional legend of Mauao and the bilingual book is suitable for people of all ages . <p> Debbie says she is over the moon with the book being shortlisted for the non-fiction category . <p> " It 's wonderful to have a uniquely Tauranga story in the awards . " <p> She thinks the book was shortlisted because it fills a gap in the market through sharing Tauranga legends . <p> " Tauranga does not have many of its stories out there for children and we deserve our own stories . " <p> " If children who live here have a book about something they can walk on , and take care of , that is so much more relevant for them than books set overseas . " <p> Slice of Heaven is a young adult novel which tells the story of a group of teenagers who attend a multi-cultural high school in South Auckland . <p> Des says he was inspired to write the novel after his experience teaching at Aorere College in South Auckland . <p> " I really loved the multicultural environment there . It was a real eye-opener , there was no single dominant culture which meant that everyone was a minority . " <p> NZCYA Convenor of Judges Crissi Blair praises the depth of the subject matter amongst the finalist titles , exploring subjects of climate change to representations of diversity . <p> The winners of each of the main six categories are Picture Book , Junior Fiction , Young Adult Fiction , Non-Fiction , Illustration and te reo Maori will be in the running to be named Margaret Mahy Book of the Year .
