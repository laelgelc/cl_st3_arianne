<h> Melting glaciers create new market for tour operators in Alaska , US <p> The rapid melting of glaciers because of climate change has created a new market for tour operators in the US state of Alaska , officials said . <p> Operators of several tour companies are experiencing an increase in customers booking trips to see the receding glaciers of the nation 's only Arctic state before they lose access to them , The Anchorage Daily News has reported . <p> A new review of glacier research data published in the Journal of Glaciology predicts Alaska 's 25,000 glaciers will lose between 30 per cent and 50 per cent of their mass by the end of this century . <p> There is " more awareness " of the receding glaciers among tourists , said Paul Roderick , operations director for Talkeetna Air Taxi , which flies customers over the ice fields of Denali and the Alaska Range .
