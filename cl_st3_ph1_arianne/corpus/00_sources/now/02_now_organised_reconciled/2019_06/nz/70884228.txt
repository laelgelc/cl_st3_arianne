Hon Aupito William Sio <p> Minister for Pacific Peoples Aupito William Sio will travel to New Caledonia tomorrow to represent New Zealand at the Pacific Community 's ( SPC ) biennial Conference on 21 June in Noumea , along with Ministers and representatives from other SPC member countries . <p> " As the oldest and largest Pacific regional organisation , SPC has a critical role in providing scientific and technical services to its Pacific country members , " Aupito William Sio says . <p> " SPC 's important work strengthens the ability of Pacific countries and territories to sustainably manage their natural resources , respond to climate change and disasters , improve education quality , promote human rights , gender equality , cultural diversity and opportunities for young people , respond to non-communicable diseases , and access and use development statistics . <p> " The biennial SPC Conference is a key part of SPC 's governance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This year it will select the next Director-General of SPC and discuss the importance of a science-based approach to management and governance of the Pacific Ocean , " Aupito William Sio says . <p> New Zealand has been a member and donor of SPC since its establishment in 1947.
