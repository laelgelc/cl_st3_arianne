<h> Plenty of head-scratching over carbon zero bill <p> The challenges are many for the rural sector over the methane targets set in the Zero Carbon Bill . <p> OPINION : When facing so much change I sometimes wonder whether ignorance could be bliss . <p> Unfortunately ignorance is not a good option when operating any business so I try to get my head around stuff . But it 's either brain capacity decrease or velocity of change increase making me feel that the list of things I do n't understand is growing longer . The carbon zero issue adds plenty of perplexions . <p> Standing in the paddock observing how well the grass has grown all autumn is great . Good winter feed stored and with it much carbon stored in all those busy photosynthesising blades . But while emissions from the stock eating the pasture are counted , it 's an anomaly that the carbon in the plant is not taken into account on the other side of the ledger . Only part of the natural cycle is considered . <p> This time of the year the clear air holds lines of clouds lingering in the sky @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in many directions on blue as evidence of folk flying overhead . With feet firmly on green tera firma I do n't understand how the Paris Accord can include methane from stock and yet omit emissions , large quantities of more serious carbon dioxide , released from planes . <p> Back home for a cuppa and peruse of paper I notice in this one edition advertisements for overseas holidays totalling five full pages . I do n't understand how we can be focussing on reducing our footprint and yet heavily promoting fossil fuel consuming travels . <p> Next page I read with confusion an article about a well known " conservationist " reported as " continually circling the globe " on her tours to " educate " the world with her climate change concerns . She obviously does n't walk , bike or go by electric car . Surely her credibility would be higher if she stayed home and vented her views via available technology . <p> Tourism is touted as New Zealand 's big income earner .
