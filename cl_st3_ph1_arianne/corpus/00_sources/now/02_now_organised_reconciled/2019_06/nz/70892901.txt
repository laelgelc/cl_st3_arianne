<h> Fiji and Marshall Islands call for EU to up climate change targets <p> EU member states have blocked the grouping from committing to a net-zero carbon emissions target for 2050 . <p> Photo : AFP <p> It comes after the leaders of Fiji and the Marshall Islands urged the EU to join them in making the commitment . <p> Leaders from the 28-nation bloc are entering the second day of the European Summit talks in Brussels today . <p> But the Guardian reported Poland , the Czech Republic and Hungary yesterday refused to sign a text which set out the target . <p> Writing in the online policy news site EURACTIV on Thursday , Marshall Islands President Hilda Heine and Fijian Prime Minister Frank Bainimarama said the summit was an opportunity that should not be wasted . <p> " As small island states , we have been doing our part , and for this momentum not to be lost we need major economies to come in behind us and do the same , " they said .
