<p> News from Wellington City Council On Thursday next week , Wellington City Councillors will be asked to agree on a preferred direction for intensification of the city centre and suburban centres to provide for population growth over the next 30 years . <p> This follows the Planning for Growth city-wide engagement on four growth scenarios , alongside a survey on the Council 's blueprint to become a Zero Carbon Capital by 2050 . <p> In addition , the Regional Council and the City Council will make decisions by the end of June about funding and next steps for the ' Let 's Get Wellington Moving ' programme of work . <p> Wellington Mayor Justin Lester says this is an exciting time for Wellington . " Never before have we had an opportunity of this scale to bring about transformational change . How we grow our city , how we move around our city and how we deal with seismic risk and the causes and impacts of climate change will impact generations to come . " <p> The City Council expects 50,000 to 80,000 more people to call Wellington home over the next 30 years .
