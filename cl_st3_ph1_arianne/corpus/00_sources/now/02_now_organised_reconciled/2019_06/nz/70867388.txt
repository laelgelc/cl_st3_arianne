<h> Waitaki District Council CEO encourages greater representativeness <p> Do you have a passion for the Waitaki ? Are you someone with great ideas you 'd like to share , do you want to be a voice for your community ? Then the Waitaki District Council wants you to stand in this year 's local body elections . <p> " The emphasis this year is on diversity " , said Chief Executive Fergus Power . " Diversity in the range of ethnicity , age , gender and life experience of candidates . In March of this year , Parliament sent a clear signal to councils via an amendment to the Local Government Act that means that Chief Executives are required to encourage representative participation in the electoral process . The intention is to encourage elected representation on councils that better reflects the nature of each district 's changing demographics . The best way to do this is for people from different communities , genders and age groups to get actively involved in local politics .
