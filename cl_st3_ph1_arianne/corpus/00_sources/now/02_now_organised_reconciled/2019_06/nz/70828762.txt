<h> The Kiwi entrepreneur doing his bit for climate change in Africa <p> In the heart of Africa , Josh Whale is helping tackle climate change one moto at a time . <p> Motorbike taxis -- commonly known as motos or boda bodas -- are the most common form of transport for East Africa 's 443 million people . Each day , the region 's 2.5 million motos clock up , between them , journeys equivalent to a return trip from Earth to the Sun , and then back again to the Sun . New Zealand **26;1678;TOOLONG Josh Whale offers this startling statistic to explain the importance of motos to the region and the significance of his new venture in Rwanda 's capital , Kigali . <p> Although affordable for passengers , motos return less than $2.50 a day to their drivers , who work an average of 12 hours . Whale and his start-up partners are trialling electric motos . Unlike most battery-powered motorbike taxis elsewhere in the world , these are powerful enough to tackle Kigali 's hilly terrain .
