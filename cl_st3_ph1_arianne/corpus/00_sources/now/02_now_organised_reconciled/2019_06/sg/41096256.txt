<p> Singapore -- The National Environment Agency has fined around 900 households for " breeding mosquitoes " due to failure to clean up stagnant water and unintentionally leaving water containers uncovered . The households were fined at least S$200 . <p> The Woodlands cluster area -- consisting of Woodlands Avenue 6 , Circle , Crescent and Drives 60 , 70 and 7 -- showed the highest proportion of mosquito breeding grounds at 85 percent . The cluster also had the highest number of dengue cases since the beginning of the year . <p> Most households during the inspection have perhaps carelessly set up breeding grounds for mosquito larvae by failing to cover water containers or clean up stagnant water . <p> The NEA is alarmed by the continuing increase in dengue infections . In a statement , the NEA said that " The dengue transmission is therefore not localised , and everyone has to be alert to the threat .
