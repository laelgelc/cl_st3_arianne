<p> The sea is engulfing a community of 40 households in the northern Philippines where people rely on whatever the ocean can offer for a living . <p> People have already started losing their homes , their school and their chapel to the sea . The water is taking over land that is home to the poorest of the poor in the region . <p> Rolando Yunson , a 70-year-old resident of Bisagu , a village near the town of Aparri in Cagayan province , said the sea " is leaving nothing but electricity posts . " <p> " People were forced to leave and look for other land to resettle , " said Yunson . Bisagu is located along an estuary where the Cagayan River pours into the Philippine Sea . <p> But even before the recent rise in sea level , Yunson said the village had experienced storm surges and huge waves . " Gradually , the village became uninhabitable , " said the old fisherman . " Typhoons became more destructive .
