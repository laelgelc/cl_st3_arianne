Green Pulse analyses the beat of the changing environment , from biodiversity conservation to climate change . <p> This month , we chat with Mr Rodney Martinez , international director for the Ecuador-based International Research Centre on El Nino , or CIIFEN , on a climate phenomenon known to wreak havoc all across the Pacific Ocean - El Nino . <p> El Nino is a natural climate cycle that can significantly influence weather patterns across the world . In places such as Ecuador and Peru , El Nino brings rain to the usually arid coast of South America , causing flash floods . And as the waters warm along the coast during El Nino events , the supply of nutrient-rich cool water to the surface is cut off , affecting fisheries and ecosystems there . <p> El Nino is a natural climate cycle that would occur regardless of human-caused climate change .
