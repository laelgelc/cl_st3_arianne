<h> Everest has installed a weather station <p> Scientific expedition of the National geographic society and the Nepalese Tribhuvan University was established on Everest five weather stations , one of which is at a record height , 8430 meters . The expedition also explored the ecosystems of mountains in order to understand how they are affected by climate change . <p> This writes the Chronicle.info with reference to zn.ua . <p> In 2019 , the National geographic society launched a project Perpetual Planet Extreme Expeditions . In its framework will be held several expeditions , whose purpose is to study the impact of climate change on mountains , rainforests , and oceans . In April of 2019 started the first of them who went to Everest . <p> The expedition reached base camp on the mountain on April 16 . Then for nearly two months , the researchers explored the ecosystem of mount Everest to understand how they are influenced by climate change . Biologists conducted monitoring animals and plants , and also collected soil samples and lake water and examined the presence of living organisms . The station will record temperature , pressure , relative humidity , speed and wind direction .
