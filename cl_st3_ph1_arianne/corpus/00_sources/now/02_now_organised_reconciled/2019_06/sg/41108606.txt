Energy demand has grown by 80 per cent since 2000 and , over the next 20 years , is projected to grow by two-thirds again , as people leave the countryside for the city , swapping kampongs for condos , bicycles for cars , and wet markets for airconditioned shopping malls . <p> Powering Southeast Asia 's growth story is the single biggest contributor to man-made greenhouse gas emissions -- coal .
