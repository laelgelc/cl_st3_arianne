<p> SINGAPORE - Ten land parcels for food farming in Lim Chu Kang were put up for sale by public tender on Wednesday ( June 19 ) , the Singapore Food Agency ( SFA ) said . <p> These include six land parcels for beansprout farming , three land parcels for general agriculture ( food ) farming and a land parcel for vegetable farming , the agency said in a statement . <p> The land parcels for general agriculture ( food ) farming may be used to farm food crops , seafood , quail eggs , cattle or goats for dairy milk , and/or frogs reared for food . <p> The plots , which come with a 20-year lease term , have a land area of at least 6,208 sq m each , which is slightly smaller than a football field . <p> The largest land parcel has a land area of around 20,000 sq m . <p> They are located in Sungei Tengah Road , Neo Tiew Crescent and Neo Tiew Harvest Place . <p> SFA said that Singapore farms have to be efficient in their use of scarce resources , such as land , water and energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of meeting 30 per cent of its nutritional needs locally by 2030 . <p> " Farms should leverage technology to increase productivity and be sustainable and resilient against climate change , " the statement said . <p> SFA added that proposals that can achieve high production levels in a sustainable manner will have a higher chance of being awarded the new land parcels . <p> Proposals will also be assessed on their production capability , track record , relevant experience and qualifications as well as innovation and business sustainability including waste management . <h> The Straits Times <p> We have been experiencing some problems with subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
