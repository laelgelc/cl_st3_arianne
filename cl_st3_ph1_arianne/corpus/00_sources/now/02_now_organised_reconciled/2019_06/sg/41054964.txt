<h> Popular <h> MAS developing market for insurance-linked securities <p> Singapore can contribute to sustainable growth in Asia by helping to strengthen the region 's resilience to natural disasters , said Acting Prime Minister Heng Swee Keat yesterday . <p> To this end , the Monetary Authority of Singapore ( MAS ) is developing the market for insurance-linked securities as an alternative risk financing solution , he said . These can help provide financing quickly in the wake of regional disasters or catastrophic losses . <p> Mr Heng , who is Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister , said only about 5 per cent of economic losses in developing Asia are currently insured , despite the fact that natural catastrophes have increased in intensity and frequency due to climate change . <p> " This puts tremendous strain on governments of developing nations in the event of a natural catastrophe , which can set back economic progress in the affected areas for years to come , " he added . <p> He was speaking at the Official Monetary and Financial Institutions Forum 's Global Public Investor report launch . <p> Global public investors are central banks , sovereign funds and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gives insights into their performance and practices . <h> Related Stories <p> To develop the market for insurance-linked securities , MAS has set up a Natural Catastrophe Data Analytics Exchange , Mr Heng said .
