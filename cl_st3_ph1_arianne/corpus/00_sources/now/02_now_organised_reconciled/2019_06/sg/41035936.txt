<p> SINGAPORE - A Singapore-registered company has won the annual Liveability Challenge , a global call for companies to come up with innovative solutions for clean energy and sustainability in tropical cities . <p> The competition saw an intense battle with over 300 entries from 51 countries fighting for the award sponsored by local non-profit organisation Temasek Foundation . <p> From hydrogen-powered engines to edible protein made from food waste , the contest pitted solutions to make cities in the tropics greener . <p> Sophie 's Kitchen was named the winner on Friday ( June 7 ) at the Sands Expo and Convention Centre at Marina Bay Sands . <p> The team beat five other finalists to walk away with $1 million in funding for their technology to produce food-grade protein using microalgae as an alternative to animal- and plant-based protein . <p> The innovation is expected to save large amounts of arable farmland that is required in agriculture .
