Driven by needs that are sometimes not provided by a permanent placement , for example , flexible working arrangements or gaining experience without a need to commit to a company , more people today seek greater control over the way they work . <p> As a result , the contingent workforce or gig economy is accelerating . In Singapore , 82% of free agents do so by choice , a much higher proportion relative to other developed countries such as Sweden , Spain and the United Kingdom .
