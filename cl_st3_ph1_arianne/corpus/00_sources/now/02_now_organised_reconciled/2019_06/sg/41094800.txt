The latest UN projections , released this week , indicate that we will add another 2 billion people to the planet by 2050 and 3 billion by the end of the century . While population growth is ebbing in many countries -- and even starting to contract in a few -- population growth in some countries remains brisk , if not breakneck . <p> India , for example , is projected to add 273 million people by 2050 under the scenario UN demographers consider most likely , overtaking China as the most populous country in the world . The population of sub-Saharan Africa is projected to double from about 1 billion to just over 2 billion by mid-century . <p> In releasing the projections , UN demographers point out that such rapid growth can pose challenges for efforts to eradicate poverty , end hunger , provide health care and education to all and arrest environmental degradation . <p> And indeed , one need only look to the news of the day to get a snapshot of how continued population pressure could perilously collide with other global concerns . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ heat wave that is gripping India , where authorities have ordered police to escort water tankers as growing numbers of people compete for limited water supplies . <p> An Ebola outbreak in Uganda has renewed concerns over the possibility of another pandemic , like HIV-AIDs , erupting on a continent with a rapidly urbanising population and poorly prepared public health systems . <p> Last month , researchers in Australia analysed global climate change data to develop long-term risk scenarios , emphasising that without more serious action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , civilisation as we know it could collapse by mid-century . <p> The apocalypse may be not imminent , but with population projected to grow from 7.7 billion today to 10.9 billion by 2100 -- and a global middle class that is also rapidly expanding -- struggles over land , water and other resources could easily intensify . <p> There are steps that can be taken to feed and accommodate more people on the planet .
