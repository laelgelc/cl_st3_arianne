<h> Share <p> Greenergy Solutions Inc. held a ceremonial groundbreaking on May 28 , 2019 for a 200-megawatt ( MW ) solar power plant and storage facility in Cordon , Isabela , Philippines . <p> Ruth P. Briones , Chair and CEO , Greenergy Solutions , announced during the groundbreaking that the Cordon Solar project will be the first of four large-scale utility solar plus storage projects the company will construct in Isabela province , Philippines . <p> Briones said the project will provide 24-hour uninterrupted power that will be initially supplied to consumers under the Wholesale Electricity Spot Market ( WESM ) while negotiating for a long-term Power Purchase Agreements ( PPAs ) with distribution utilities . <p> She noted that the project will first construct a 50-MW facility and will be expanded to 200 MW in five years . Briones added that the company was able to secure about 300 hectares of land for the project . <p> The solar facility will be constructed within an area of 75-hectares covered by a Special Land Use Permit to be converted into a 25-year Forestland Lease Agreement ( FLAG ) issued by the Department of Environment and Natural Resources @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be online early in 2020 and the expansions will subsequently proceed after the commissioning of the initial capacity . <p> Briones unveiled another similarly significant project : The plan to construct a co-located Biomass/Waste-to-Energy ( WTE ) Project in the same project site . <p> Briones said the Biomass-WTE project will provide livelihood opportunities to the people living in the adjacent communities . Neighbouring local government units will feed the solid wastes to the biomass/WTE facility , therefore getting rid of their waste problems and at the same time supporting clean energy generation . The Biomass Facility will establish energy crops plantation in the timberland areas adjacent to the project site in order to secure the supply of feedstock for the project . <p> Lynn Zuniega , town mayor of Cordon , Isabela , welcomed the project with great expectation . <p> Zuniega considers the project as being instrumental to address the impacts of climate change in the province of Isabela .
