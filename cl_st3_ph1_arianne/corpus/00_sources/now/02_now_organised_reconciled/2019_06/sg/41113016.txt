We know that climate risk is impacting the design and future of electricity systems all over the world , with or without policy measures . " <p> As the cost of energy storage solutions like batteries slides down the cost curve , Australia has a chance to lead globally in the creation of large-scale electricity grids that can balance a high volume of renewable energy , Franklin said at the 2019 Australian Energy Storage Conference and Exhibition . <p> The share of renewable in Australia 's energy mix has leapt from about 8.5 per cent to about 21 per cent since the early 2000s . Batteries can charge up during the middle of the day , when the sun is out ; and then discharge in the early evening , when solar panels are no longer generating but household demand for energy is at a peak . This is known as time-shifting in the energy industry . <p> With storage becoming cheaper , a combined renewable-storage source of generation is a more attractive option to replace a coal or gas plant .
