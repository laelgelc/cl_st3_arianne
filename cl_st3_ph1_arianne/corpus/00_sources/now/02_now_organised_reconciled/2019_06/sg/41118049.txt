America sign historic trade deal <h> Hailed as largest trade agreement EU has ever concluded , treaty concludes 20 years of talks <p> BRUSSELS/OSAKA * The European Union and South American bloc Mercosur have agreed on a free trade treaty , concluding two decades of talks and committing to more open markets in the face of a rising tide of protectionism . <p> Hailing it as the largest trade agreement the EU has ever concluded , European Commission president Jean-Claude Juncker said in Osaka on Friday that the two sides " are sending a strong signal that we stand for rules-based trade " . <p> Mr Juncker was among European leaders , as well as Argentine President Mauricio Macri , gathered for the Group of 20 ( G-20 ) summit in Osaka , who shared a podium at the event . The four Mercosur countries are Argentina , Brazil , Paraguay and Uruguay . <p> His remarks came as some G-20 leaders signalled difficulty in efforts to draft a summit communique , with disagreements ranging from trade to climate change . <p> Agricultural exports to Europe have been a key point of contention , while @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ undermined to ease Mercosur 's access to European markets . <p> Ms Christiane Lambert , head of France 's biggest agricultural union FNSEA tweeted : " Unacceptable signature of a Mercosur-EU accord , which will expose European farmers to unfair competition and consumers to total deception . " <p> Mr Joachim Rukwied , president of the German Farmers ' Association , said he feared a fallout from the deal , though other industries see a huge potential for a big boost . <p> " It is unacceptable that the European Commission signed this completely unbalanced agreement , " he said . He said that agriculture must not be sacrificed in favour of the automotive industry , referring to the large auto industry in Europe . <p> The Association of German Chambers of Industry and Commerce welcomed the deal , which creates a market that covers nearly 800 million people .
