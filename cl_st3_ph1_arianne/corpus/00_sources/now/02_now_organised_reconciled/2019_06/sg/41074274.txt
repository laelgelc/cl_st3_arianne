<h> Democratic presidential hopeful Klobuchar details top policy goals <h> Democratic presidential hopeful Klobuchar details top policy goals <p> FILE PHOTO : U.S. Democratic presidential candidate Amy Klobuchar participates in the She the People Presidential Forum in Houston <p> By Ginger Gibson <p> WASHINGTON ( Reuters ) - U.S. Senator Amy Klobuchar unveiled on Tuesday a 137-point list of priorities she would address in her first 100 days in office if elected president , a sweeping set of policies she is highlighting as part of her bid for the Democratic nomination . <p> The policies touch on matters ranging from high-profile topics like climate change , healthcare and gun regulations to the classification of federal tax offices . <p> Klobuchar is one of more than 20 candidates vying for the Democratic nomination to challenge Republican President Donald Trump in November 2020 . The Minnesota lawmaker has so far struggled to gain traction in opinion polls . <p> She will face off on June 26 against nine other candidates in the first debate of the nominating contest . An additional 10 candidates will debate the next night .
