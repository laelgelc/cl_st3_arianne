<h> Watch a SpaceX rocket cut through fog to deliver trio of Canadian satellites into orbit <p> Bad weather can be a death sentence for a scheduled rocket launch , but SpaceX did n't mind a bit of fog getting in the way on Wednesday as it launched its Falcon 9 rocket from Vandenberg Air Force Base in California . The vehicle successfully delivered three Canadian radar satellites into orbit . <p> As Spaceflight Now reports , the satellites are part of a $900 million mission by the Canadian government to actively monitor forests and coastlines , among other things . The launch itself was quite a sight , with the rocket piercing the thick coastal fog and soaring into the sky . <p> The launch begins at around the 12-minute mark . You 'll want to stay tuned until at least the 20-minute mark so you can enjoy the Falcon 9 booster returning to Earth and landing in the middle of a blanket of fog . It 's all pretty cool . <p> The trio of identical satellites deployed as planned , then stretched their instrument arrays in preparation for conducting their observation mission .
