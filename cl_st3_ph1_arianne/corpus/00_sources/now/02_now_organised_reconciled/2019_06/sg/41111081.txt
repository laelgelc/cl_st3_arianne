<p> Prime Minister Mia Mottley announced today the island would host the 15th United Nations Conference on Trade and Development ( UNCTAD ) quadrennial meeting in October next year . <p> Mottley , who was speaking to the media at the Grantley Adams International Airport after arriving from London , England , said the United Arab Emirates , which had put in a late bid , would host the World Economic Forum and electronic forum the following month after the two countries engaged in discussions and decided to co-chair the events . <p> Barbados will be the first small country to host the conference . <p> About 3 500 visitors and delegates are slated to attend . <p> " It is a massive undertaking , but it is one that we feel we are more than capable of doing , " Mottley said . <p> The Prime Minister said the real benefit was not just in hosting the conference , but would put Barbados at the centre of the world in discussion on trade and development issues .
