<h> France welcomes PM Modi 's call for global conference on terrorism <p> France on Monday welcomed Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's proposal for a global conference to tackle the threat of terrorism , saying the fight against terror is at the heart of its priorities . <p> Minister of State for Europe and Foreign Affairs of France Jean Baptiste Lemoyne made the remarks on the sidelines of an interaction with Indian alumni of French institutions in the fields of business administration , engineering and design here . <p> " Every single initiative to fight terrorism is welcome because it is a threat to every country in the world ... So , everything that can be done to unite efforts is welcome . It 's ( terrorism ) a global challenge like climate change . We will be closely looking at this initiative , " he said . <p> " Fight against terrorism is at the heart of our priorities ...
