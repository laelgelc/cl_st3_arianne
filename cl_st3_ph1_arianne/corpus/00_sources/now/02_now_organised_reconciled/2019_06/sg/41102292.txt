<h> Tax us more , billionaires urge US presidential candidates <p> NEW YORK * " Tax us more ! " is the message from about 20 super-wealthy Americans who have urged presidential candidates to back higher taxes on the wealthiest to confront climate change and other priorities . <p> " America has a moral , ethical and economic responsibility to tax our wealth more , " said the group , which included investor George Soros , Facebook co-founder Chris Hughes , descendants of Walt Disney and the owners of the Hyatt hotel chain , in a letter on Monday . <p> The signers pointed out that fellow billionaire Warren Buffett has said he is taxed at a lower rate than his secretary . <p> The letter alluded to support among Democratic presidential candidates for higher taxes on the super wealthy , including Mr Pete Buttigieg and Mr Beto O'Rourke . <p> But it noted broad bipartisan support for taxing the super wealthy , saying " some ideas are too important for America to be part of only a few candidates ' platforms " . <p> It praised a proposal by Senator Elizabeth Warren that would raise taxes on those @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ taxes , a measure expected to affect the 75,000 wealthiest families . <p> The letter was signed by 18 people representing 11 families , plus an anonymous person .
