<h> Singapore welcomes ASEAN declaration on tackling marine waste : PM Lee <h> Singapore welcomes ASEAN declaration on tackling marine waste : PM Lee <p> Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong has flagged the urgency for Singapore and ASEAN to step up efforts to tackle the growing waste issue and pursue sustainable development . This comes as ASEAN leaders adopted the Bangkok Declaration on combating Marine Debris at their summit on Saturday ( Jun 22 ) . This includes plastic , untreated liquid and semi-solid waste , he said . <p> " With technology breakthrough , we can even turn waste into resources , " he added . <p> During an ASEAN meeting in March , environment leaders discussed the draft declaration , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ acknowledged the " urgent need " to work together in addressing these issues . <p> The meeting also produced the ASEAN Framework of Action on Marine Debris , which covers four priority areas : Policy support and planning ; research , innovation and capacity building ; public awareness , education and outreach ; and private sector engagement . <p> " The transboundary nature of many of these environmental challenges makes it impossible for any single country to solve the issue alone , " Mr Lee said .
