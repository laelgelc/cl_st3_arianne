Deforestation of the Amazon rainforest in Brazil sped up in May ( 2019 ) to the fastest rate in a decade , according to data from an early-warning satellite system , as experts pointed to activity by illegal loggers encouraged by the easing of environmental protections under President Jair Bolsonaro . <p> According to the Brazilian space research institute INPE , the Deter alerting system registered deforestation of 739 sq km in May , the first of three months in which logging tends to surge following the region 's rainy season . <p> That is up from 550 sq km in May 2018 and more than double the deforestation detected two years earlier . <p> " If this upward curve continues , we could have a bad year for the Amazon forest , " Claudio Almeida , head of INPE 's satellite monitoring programme , said on Tuesday ( June 4 ) . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is in the next two critical months , " he added . <p> The data adds to concerns from environmentalists who warn that Bolsonaro 's five-month-old government has dismantled conservation agencies , shown scepticism about fighting climate change and cut the budget to enforce environmental laws . <p> Brazil 's environmental protection agency IBAMA , which has been starved of funds in recent years , lost authority when he took office in January , and the forestry commission was moved to the Agriculture Ministry , which is run by farm industry allies . <p> For Marcio Astrini , Greenpeace Brazil 's public policy coordinator , Bolsonaro 's government is " anti-environmental " and has pushed to reduce forest protections without presenting a plan to fight deforestation . <p> Bolsonaro 's Environment Minister Ricardo Salles said in an interview with Reuters that Brazil remains as committed as ever to protecting the forests . <p> " We are not going to remove anything .
