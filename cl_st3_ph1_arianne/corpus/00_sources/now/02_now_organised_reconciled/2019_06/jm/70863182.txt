<h> Jamaica joins Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action <p> Jamaica is the first country in the English Caribbean to join the ' Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action ' . <p> The Coalition of Finance Ministers for Climate Action which was launched in April 2019 , at the Spring Meetings of the World Bank Group and International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) , aims at driving stronger collective action on climate change and its impacts . <p> The Coalition abides by seven principles called the Helsinki Principles which promote national climate action , especially through fiscal policy and the use of public finance . <p> These principles are designed to support finance ministers to share best practices and experiences on macro , fiscal , and public financial management policies for low-carbon transformation . <p> Jamaica , like other small island developing states , is highly exposed to extreme weather events and climate risks in the face of climate change and there is no doubt about the urgency of accessing more climate financing from public and private sources to assist the country 's path to climate-resilient growth .
