<h> MP calls for increased investment in renewable energy <p> Member of Parliament for Nsawam-Adoagyiri Frank Annor-Dompreh is calling for increased investment in renewable energy . <p> He says renewable energy is the surest means to reduce Ghana 's energy generation bill and ensure electricity is made available to all . <p> The chairman of parliament 's Foreign Affairs Committee in a statement on the floor said there are numerous health and environmental benefits associated with Ghana increasing its dependence on renewable energy like solar and wind . <p> Below is the full statement read by the MP on the floor on the future of renewable energy in Ghana . <p> 1 . Mr Speaker , Ghana , like several African countries have made commendable actions in the direction of Renewable Energy .
