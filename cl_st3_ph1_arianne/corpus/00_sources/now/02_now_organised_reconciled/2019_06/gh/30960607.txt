<h> IMF Managing Director calls for cooperation to support global growth <p> The Managing Director of the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) has called for cooperation to support global growth . <p> Speaking at the Group of 20 ( G20 ) meeting of Finance Ministers and Central Bank Governors in Fukuoka , Japan , Christine Lagarde said : " We met at a time when the global economy is showing tentative signs of stabilizing and growth is projected to strengthen . While this is good news , the road ahead remains precarious and subject to several downside risks . <p> " The principal threat stems from continuing trade tensions . The IMF estimates that US-China tariffs , including those implemented last year , could reduce the level of global GDP by 0.5 percent in 2020 or about $455 billion which could make a significant dent in the projected pick-up in economic activity . " <p> She said a second risk is that , with interest rates very low , debt levels are rising in many advanced economies , and emerging markets remain vulnerable to a sudden shift in financial conditions .
