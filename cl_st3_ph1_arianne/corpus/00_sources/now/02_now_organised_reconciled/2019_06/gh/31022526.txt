And the weather pattern has become unpredictable . <p> A century ago , Ghana had more than 8.2 million hectares of forests but that has been depleted to about 1.6 million now . Blewusi Aziedorme , a farmer lamented cassava they had planted 14 months ago was still in the fields despite the fact that it 's matured and was ready for harvest about five months ago . <p> " The ground is too hard . It 's not raining and that is why we ca n't uproot the cassava . This traps a lot of heat in the air , thus interrupting the development of rainfall clouds . And when eventually the rain comes , it 's heavy , causing floods . This also results in an unpredictable rainfall pattern . " Whatever you do , is a cause and effect relationship . So the more damage you cause , the more impact we face from climate change , " Glen Asomaning who is Director of Operations at Nature and Development Foundation ( NDF ) explained . <p> The impact of the act of deforestation and illegal logging is bigger than just climate change . Kennedy Akroma who is a traditional leader in the area says it 's sad that " they are destroying the river just to make money . The projection is that rainfall in a lot of cocoa growing areas will decline by 2% in the year 2020 and 11% in 2050 and result in a 14% and 28% decline in cocoa yield respectively . By 2080 , moisture is predicted to be inadequate for profitable cocoa production in Ghana if the current trend continues . <p> The ideas on possible solutions are several .
