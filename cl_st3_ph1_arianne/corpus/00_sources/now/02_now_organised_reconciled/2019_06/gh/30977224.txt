<p> The government says it will used proceed to invest in off-grid solar , wind farm , irrigation , afforestation and reforestation in line with the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> The Nigerian government will invest in off-grid solar , wind farm , irrigation , afforestation and reforestation as investors oversubscribed country 's second green bond . <p> The green bond which was offered for N15 billion was oversubscribed by 220% , yielded a total subscription value of N32.93 billion , the Debt Management Office ( DMO ) said . <p> Green bonds or climate bonds channelled towards environmental sustainability activities and to support climate-related or other types of special environmental projects . <p> According to the DMO , the government bond was an auction on June 3 , 2019 , and closed on Monday , June 10 , 2019 , was offered for a tenor of 7 years , at a coupon of 14.50% p.a . <p> " Retail investors were not left out , as the number of individuals who subscribed for the second Sovereign Green Bond more than doubled . <p> " The number of subscriptions grew by almost 201% with the share of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2017 Sovereign Green Bond . <p> " The stronger participation of retail investors shows that financial inclusion and deepening of the domestic financial market , which are some of the key objectives of the DMO in its issuance activities , are being achieved , " the DMO said . <p> A green bond is an effective investment tool to finance the transition to a low-carbon economy in line with the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> But the government noted that part of the proceeds will be used to finance projects in the 2018 Appropriation Act .
