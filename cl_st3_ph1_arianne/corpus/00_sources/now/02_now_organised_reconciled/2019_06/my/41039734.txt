<h> 62 M'sian companies hold APs to import plastic waste <p> KUALA LUMPUR : A total of 62 Malaysian companies currently hold approved permits ( AP ) to import and process plastic waste . <p> The permits are issued by the National Solid Waste Management Department ( NSWMD ) within the Housing and Local Government Ministry . <p> In July last year , the Ministry announced a three-month suspension for the issuance of permits following the incident of contamination in Kuala Langat , Selangor , believed to have been caused by a factory processing plastic waste illegally . <p> NSWMD deputy director ( Facilities and Import Permit Unit ) Wemi Kalsuna Katerun said all the 62 companies were being continuously monitored to ensure they abide by stipulated regulations . <p> Stern actions , including the revocation of licences , will be taken if any of those companies were found to have violated the regulations pertaining to the permits . <p> " If any of the companies were
