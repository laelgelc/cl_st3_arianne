<h> No action to be taken on students protesting against climate change <p> GEORGE TOWN : Penang 's Education Department has no plans to initiate action against a primary school here after it was revealed that 11 of its students were part of a global protest to raise awareness about climate change . <p> The department had just asked the school to submit a report for administrative purposes and not for any action to be meted out , said its deputy director Abdul Rashid Abdul Samad . <p> " There is no issue here . Essentially , the educators here are pleased to allow students to be exposed to the green movement , as it can help inculcate the need to conserve the environment and to recycle as well as to live a healthy life . " <p> A report had emerged quoting members of the Parent-Teacher Association of the school , who had feared that action would be taken against them for allowing the young children to be part of the protest . <p> The protest was organized by a loosely organised civil society called Klimate Utara Malaysia ( Kaum ) and Klima Utara Malaysia ( Kamy ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in conjunction with a series of global protests attended by the youth . <p> The brief protest was part of the Global Climate Strike , a movement initiated by Swedish teenager Greta Thunberg to raise awareness about climate change . <p> But in Penang , a Penang Island City councilor Vino Dini Chandragason was upset that the gathering had become an occasion to protest against the proposed Penang Transport Master Plan . <p> She had apparently expressed her grievances by accusing the organisers of exploiting the young to protest against the mega state transport project . <p> Her remarks have since gone viral on social media . <p> The issue has drawn responses from a varied of parties here including from state executive councilor Phee Boon Poh , who sent a message via social media that he hopes that there would not be any alleged retaliation against the school . <p> He said that Penang encourages the young to be part of the green movement to curb pollution and to raise awareness on the need to fight climate change . <p> State PKR vice-chairman , Jason Ong Khan Lee , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ freedom to assemble was part of the new Malaysia 's parameters . <p> " Unless the gathering was to sow hatred or with an agenda to cause discord , I think in the new Malaysia , we have to tolerate protests .
