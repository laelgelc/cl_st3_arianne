<h> Aussie diplomat is defending ' corporate malfeasance ' <p> MP SPEAKS The Australian High Commissioner 's defence of Lynas in the face of compelling evidence the company has contravened the terms of its operating licence is an unprecedented breach in diplomatic protocol in Malaysia . <p> Instead of working to ease tensions between the Australian company and the country that is host to its business , as diplomats normally would , Andrew Goledzinowski 's statement of June 10 , 2019 , regarding Lynas encourages adversarial behaviour and corporate malfeasance . <p> Most importantly , Australia 's most senior diplomat in the country is making light of Malaysian law . <p> Goledzinowski challenged my statement that removing all waste from Malaysia is part of a " legally binding agreement signed by Lynas " . <p> From the time of Lynas 's entry into Malaysia in 2012 , approval of its operating licence was clearly conditional upon the Australian miner shipping out all wastes generated from its processing plant in Pahang . <p> The following points , encapsulated from various official documents attached here , clearly show Lynas 's contractual obligations . <p> On Feb 22 , 2012 , former @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issued a joint statement which said Lynas must undertake to return any residue generated by its factory in Gebeng to its original source . <p> The next day , former executive chairman of Lynas , Nick Curtis signed a letter of undertaking that commits to removing from Malaysia all waste generated by the Lynas plant in Gebeng , Kuantan . <p> On March 6 , 2012 , a similar undertaking was given by Mashal Ahmad , Managing Director of Lynas Malaysia . <p> Six days after the issuance of the TOL , on Sept 8 , 2012 , AELB informed Lynas the issue of " removal of residue ... is legally binding and AELB will enforce it " . <p> On Dec 19 , 2012 , a second joint-ministerial statement endorsed by the then cabinet reiterated that the TOL granted to Lynas required as a specific condition that it removes all the residue generated by the Lynas Advanced Materials Plant ( LAMP ) out of Malaysia . This includes all products made from the residue .
