<h> Swinburne team placed second in hackathon <p> KUCHING : Swinburne University of Technology students finished second in the recent Ericsson Hack for Good 2.0 hackathon , a competition that provided participants the opportunity to build Internet of Things ( IoT ) -based solutions . <p> The competition required participants to upload a video pitch and abstract of the proposed idea of their chosen theme , which included IoT in Climate Change , IoT in Water Quality Management , IoT in Mangrove Ecology and Diversity , and IoT in Fisheries Production . <p> Only the top 10 best ideas were shortlisted to proceed with real hacking . <p> The team , supervised by School of Engineering deputy head Dr Chua Hong Siang , competed under the IoT in Climate Change theme . <p> The trio came up with a low-power , cost effective , and non-invasive solution towards climate change monitoring . <p> It consists of low-power sensing devices with a lifespan of more than 10 years without the need to recharge or have a battery replacement , a drone equipped with sensors known as Hummingbird that can be used to periodically fly to the monitoring sites for the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be automatically switched on to shine on plants when light intensity is low and carbon dioxide concentration is high . <p> Utilising the Narrowband ( NB ) -IoT technology , the entire development took more than a month to complete . <p> With the system in place , it eliminates the need to constantly enter into hard-to-access sites to check on the status and health of the sensing devices . <p> Maintenance is only needed when the system detects a significant deviation between the readings from ground devices and the Hummingbird . <p> Additionally , the ground devices can be programmed remotely . <p> The team members said the award not only recognised their efforts towards environmental monitoring but also served to motivate them to work harder . <p> Hack for Good 2.0 was open to tertiary students from all private and public institutions in Malaysia ( Category 1 ) , as well as professional developers , programmers , designers , engineers , and inspiring technopreneurs in Malaysia ( Category 2 )
