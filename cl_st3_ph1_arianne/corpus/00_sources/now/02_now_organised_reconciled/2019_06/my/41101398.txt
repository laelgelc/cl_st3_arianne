<h> M'sia on right trajectory to achieve renewable energy targets <p> ADUN SPEAKS In reference to the statement made by prominent economist Jomo Kwame Sundaram today , there is actually no issues of political will by the Pakatan Harapan government in setting the right tone via policies to promote the uptake of renewable energy . <p> The government is committed to fulfilling its promise in the Harapan manifesto to increase the installed capacity of renewable energy ( excluding large hydro ) from two percent in 2018 to 20 percent by the year 2025 . It must be noted that this target is significantly higher than the one set by the previous administration ( 13 percent by 2030 ) . <p> In October 2018 , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin announced the new Net Energy Metering policy , where excess electricity generated by rooftop solar installations injected back to the grid will offset the intake from TNB on one-to-one basis , making the proposition of installing rooftop solar far more financially attractive . This is arguably the most progressive solar policy in South East Asia . <p> Also liberalised was the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ install solar panels , but also to just sell solar energy directly to the consumer . This enables commercial and industrial premises to avoid the heavy capex of buying the solar system , and instead enjoy green renewable energy with zero upfront cost . <p> To date , 35 companies have been registered to provide these services , including TNB 's own subsidiary . With this groundbreaking policy , customers are no longer bound to TNB as their only supplier of electricity but have 34 other options . <p> These policies have not only been attractive to the private sector , but even local governments in Johor , Penang and Selangor have actively pursued solar installations . <p> In terms of making Renewable Energy Financing more attractive , the government has also expanded the list of assets available for the Green Investment Tax Allowance from nine to 40 items , and many of these are renewable energy items for the Biogas , Biomass and Small Hydro installations . <p> The government has also introduced the Green Technology Financing Scheme ( GTFS ) 2.0 where qualified applicants are given a two-percent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ guarantee , making renewable energy projects more bankable and attractive to banks . <p> In addition to opening the spaces for private/individual investment in solar , the Energy Commission has also expanded the Large Scale Solar scheme by another 500MW , and as a testament to the openness of this tender process , over 700 companies have purchased the tender documents and are in the midst of preparing their submissions . <p> RAJIV RISHYAKARAN is the state assemblyperson for Bukit Gasing and board member of Sustainable Energy Development Authority ( Seda ) .
