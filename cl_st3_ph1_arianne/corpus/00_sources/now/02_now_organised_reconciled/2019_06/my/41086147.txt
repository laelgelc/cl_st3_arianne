<p> KUALA LUMPUR ( June 21 ) : Air passengers want governments to encourage the development of new technologies and sustainable aviation fuels to reduce aviation carbon emissions , rather than impose ineffective " environmental " taxes , according to the International Air Transport Association ( IATA ) . <p> In a statement on its website June 19 , IATA said a survey it commissioned showed that most passengers ' preferred actions for governments to prioritise for managing aviation 's climate change impacts are to : <p> support the development of sustainable aviation fuels ( 64% ) <p> support research and development of new technology and better operations ( 62% ) . <p> IATA said that in contrast , environmental taxes were one of the least popular options , with just 22% support . <p> IATA director general and CEO Alexandre de Juniac said aviation takes the climate change challenge very seriously . <p> " For more than a decade we have set and exceeded tough targets for carbon emissions , and we plan to achieve much more . <p> " Public opinion has a clear message to governments : work with aviation to encourage investment in clean fuels @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ help airlines cut emissions in half by 2050 , " he said . <p> de Juniac said commercial aviation is responsible for around 2% of annual global carbon emissions . <p> He said the industry has a target to cap carbon dioxide through carbon-neutral growth from 2020 , and to cut emissions in half by 2050 , compared to a 2005 benchmark . <p> Achievement of this target ensures aviation 's compatibility with the goals of the Paris climate agreement , to limit global warming to between 1.5-2 degrees , said de Juniac . <p> " Airlines have spent billions on new planes that have helped to cut emissions per passenger in half since 1990 . From next year we will cap emissions in a global offsetting scheme that will generate US$40 billion of climate financing . <p> " And airlines have bought up all the sustainable aviation fuel that is available . Governments should listen to their citizens . The way forward for aviation and the environment is sustainable aviation fuels .
