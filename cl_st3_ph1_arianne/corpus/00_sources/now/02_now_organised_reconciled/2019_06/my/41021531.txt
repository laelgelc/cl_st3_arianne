We shared his passion about education , community welfare and helping others . The family will carry on his legacy , either personally or through Yayasan Tan Sri Lee Shin Cheng , " he said . <p> Yeow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ father held at the family 's residence in Diamond Hill , IOI Resort City , here today . <p> The late Lee , the grand patriarch and founder of local conglomerate IOI Group , was well-known for his contribution to Malaysia 's oil palm industry and as a philanthropist . <p> Holding his tears back , Yeow Chor said his late father would have reached 80 years old today and had planned to retire at the age of 80 . <p> Lee reportedly breathed his last at 8.30pm on Saturday after a period of illness .
