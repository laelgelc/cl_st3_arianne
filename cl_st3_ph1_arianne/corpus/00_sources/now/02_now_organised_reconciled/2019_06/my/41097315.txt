<h> Cause of Pasir Gudang air pollution to be known in two days <p> JOHOR BARU : The cause of the pollution in Pasir Gudang which resulted in breathing difficulty and vomiting among residents particularly students on Thursday will be known in two days ' time . <p> Johor Mentri Besar Datuk Dr Sahruddin Jamal disclosed that the relevant agency was in the final stage of establishing the chemical concerned . <p> The result would be announced by the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry or the Chemistry Department , he told reporters at Pasir Gudang Municipal Council Stadium here today . <p> According to him , 30 chemical factories in Pasir Gudang had been shortlisted for investigation into whether they were related to the latest pollution incident . <p> " The Environment Department will investigate and if the chemical is similar to that which had caused the breathing difficulties and vomiting in the students , we will order the factories to cease their operations , " he said . <p> According to him , there are currently 265 such factories in Pasir Gudang . <p> On the blood samples taken from the 15 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the reported pollution three months ago of Sungai Kim Kim , Sahruddin said the results were negative . <p> Meanwhile , he said 75 individuals were referred to Sultan Ismail Hospital today and of the number , four had been warded . Three were aged below 12 years while one was 30 years old . <p> The mentri besar said the state government had proposed for all educational institutions in Pasir Gudang to be closed from tomorrow until Thursday as a precautionary measure . <p> As of yesterday , 16 schools , one polytechnic , and 69 kindergartens under the Education ministry had been ordered to close until Thursday . -- Bernama
