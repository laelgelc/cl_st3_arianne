<h> Stricter checks at ports for plastic waste <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Bernama ) : More stringent checks will be carried out on containers at all ports to ensure that no prohibited plastic waste enters the country . <p> Customs director-general Datuk Paddy Abdul Halim said they were working with the National Solid Waste Management Department and the Department of Environment to combat the smuggling of plastic waste into Malaysia . <p> " We are carrying out tighter checks on containers entering into the country , especially at Port Klang . <p> " The Customs has joined a special team formed by the government , which views seriously the dumping of banned plastic waste in the country , to combat the illegal activities , " he told Bernama . <p> The plastic waste issue came to light when authorities uncovered hundreds of containers in Port Klang found to contain illegal waste brought into the country . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the 60 containers of plastic waste brought into the country illegally on May 28 , would be sent back to their countries of origin . <p> She @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tonnes was plastic waste from
