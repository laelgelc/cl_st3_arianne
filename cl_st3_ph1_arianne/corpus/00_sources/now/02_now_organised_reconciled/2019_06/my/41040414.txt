<h> 62 companies hold permits to import and process plastic waste <p> KUALA LUMPUR : A total of 62 Malaysian companies currently hold approved permits ( AP ) to import and process plastic waste . <p> The permits were issued by the National Solid Waste Management Department ( NSWMD ) within the Housing and Local Government Ministry . <p> In July last year , the ministry announced a three-month suspension for the issuance of permits following the incident of contamination in Kuala Langat , Selangor , believed to have been caused by a factory processing plastic waste illegally . <p> Department deputy director ( Facilities and Import Permit Unit ) Wemi Kalsuna Katerun said all the 62 companies were being continuously monitored to ensure they abide by stipulated regulations . <p> Stern actions , including the revocation of licences , will be taken if any of these companies were found to have violated the regulations pertaining to the permits . <p> " If any of the companies authorities . <p> Following the recent incidents of environmental contamination and the related public outcry , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin declared war on contaminated plastic waste being dumped by other countries in Malaysia . <p> Last month , the minister ordered 60 containers laden with contaminated plastic waste and stranded at Port Klang to be sent back to the respective countries of origin in stages . <p> The media also reported that 265 containers of plastic waste have been stranded at the North Butterworth Container Terminal
