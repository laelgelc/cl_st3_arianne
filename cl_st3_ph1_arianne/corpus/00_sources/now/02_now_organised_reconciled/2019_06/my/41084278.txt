<h> Group lodges police report on alleged water contamination by Lynas <p> KUANTAN : A group that has been engaged in a years-long battle against Lynas Malaysia Sdn Bhd has lodged a police report against the rare earths processing company over allegations of water contamination . <p> The group that calls itself " Save Malaysia Stop Lynas " ( SMSL ) claimed that there was serious groundwater contamination from toxic heavy metals including nickel , lead , chromium and mercury , among others . <p> They based these allegations on a review of Department of Environment reports on water sampling tests carried out by SMSL since 2016 . The documents were part of an executive review committee report on Lynas that was released in December last year . <p> SMSL chairman Tan Bun Teet said in a statement issued today that polluting the environment , especially the groundwater , is a crime . <p> " Since our regulators have not taken any action and have flouted their own regulations despite the data clearly showing otherwise , we have no choice but to act in the interest of our community and for our country , " he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Water Services Industry Act any person found guilty of contamination that results in death can be sent to the gallows . <p> Otherwise , the person convicted of such a crime faces a RM500,000 fine or 10 years in jail or both . <p> Tan explained that lead is particularly harmful to babies and children . " The World Health Organisation has warned that there is no safe exposure level of lead , " he said . <p> He said SMSL decided to lodge a police report because the new government has not taken any action since it came into power more than a year ago . <p> In another development , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin has postponed her visit to Australia to discuss the issue of the storage of Lynas ' radioactive waste in Malaysia . <p> A spokeswoman from her ministry said on Wednesday the trip , initially set for this week , has been postponed " pending further developments " . <p> Yeo was supposed to meet Bill Johnston , the Minister of Mines in Western Australia @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , according to a Reuters report . <p> Lynas ' operating licence is due for renewal in September and Malaysia wants the company to remove its stockpile of wastes as a condition for the renewal .
