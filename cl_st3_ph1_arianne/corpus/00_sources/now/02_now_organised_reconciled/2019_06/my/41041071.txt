<h> Customs to crack down on illegal plastic waste entry at ports <p> More stringent checks will be carried out on containers at all ports to ensure that no prohibited plastic waste enters the country . <p> Customs director-general Paddy Abdul Halim said his department is working with the National Solid Waste Management Department and the Environment Department to combat the smuggling of plastic waste into Malaysia . <p> " We are carrying out tighter checks on containers entering into the country , especially at Port Klang . <p> " The Customs have joined a special team formed by the government , which views seriously the dumping of banned plastic waste in the country , to combat the illegal activities , " he told Bernama . <p> The plastic waste issue came to light when it was reported that hundreds of containers in Port Klang were found to contain illegal waste in 10 operations carried out by authorities . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the 60 containers of plastic waste brought into the country illegally on May 28 would be sent back to their countries of origin . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weighing 450 metric strict conditions are imposed on those importing and processing plastic waste . <p> Among these is that importers should have storage premises and facilities to carry out the processing and recycling
