<h> Cause of toxic air in Pasir Gudang yet to be identified : Yeo <p> JOHOR BAHRU ( June 27 ) : The exact cause of the air pollution causing vomiting and breathing difficulties among students in Pasir Gudang since last Thursday has not been ascertained until now . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the results of laboratory tests found that samples from eight patients taken to test for cyanide , blood cyanide and volatile organic compound ( VOC ) have turned out negative . <p> " The chemical department is keeping on looking for chemical substances in the urine and blood samples of patients . <p> " But we have failed to detect any chemical substances in the urine and blood samples which is causing breathing difficulties and vomiting in the victims . This is very different from the Sungai Kim Kim pollution case where we had physiological evidence of the presence of chemical substances in the urine and blood , which enabled us to identify the exact cause , " she told reporters at Menara Aqabah here yesterday . <p> Yeo said to ensure the safety @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ permanent air pollutant monitoring station would be set up in Pasir Gudang , the details and design of which would be studied . <p> She said the government had also proposed the creation of a Prediction of Chemical Dispersion System that would be the standard reference to ensure that all substances in Pasir Gudang were within the normal range . <p> " I have also discussed with YB Tan ( Tan Chen Choon ) to create a buffer zone between the industrial and residential areas , " said Yeo . <p> Meanwhile , Khuzzan said that as of noon yesterday , there was no new admission .
