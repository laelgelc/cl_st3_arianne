<h> Sharing the legacy of Senator Hubert Humphrey <p> IN 1985 , I was fortunate to be among a group of mid-career professionals to join the Fulbright Hubert Humphrey Fellowship programme in the United States of America . Three were selected from Malaysia , one of the more than 75 countries who participate in the fellowship . <p> A selected number of universities in the US hosted us , and I went to Cornell University in Ithaca , upstate New York . My stint at Cornell , which was spent at the faculties dealing with the environment , did a lot to shape my interest on environmental issues . <p> More than 40 Malaysians have graduated from the programme and are now all members of the Malaysian chapter of the Humphrey Alumni , a mixed group that includes scientists , doctors , journalists and public administrators . <p> The annual programme is managed by the Malaysian-American Commission On Educational Exchange ( Macee ) . <p> We learnt many things about the contributions of Senator Hubert Humphrey to humanity and the world .
