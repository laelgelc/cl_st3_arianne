<p> KOTA KINABALU : A coalition of conservationists and community-based entities have called for mangrove-preservation measures in future development along Sabah 's coast . <p> Coalition 3H , which is made of nine organisations , recommended that any tourism development and associated roads planned must consider future effects of climate change and preserve the natural coastal defence system that mangrove provides . <p> Coalition 3H said that despite their immense value , mangrove forests are in constant danger of being damaged and disrupted by infrastructure like roads , hotels and housing developments , shrimp farms and fish farms , and to a lesser extent from being cut for firewood , charcoal and posts , the coalition said in a statement . <p> WWF-Malaysia Sabah conservation head Dr Robecca Jumin said Sabah has the largest mangrove area of any state in Malaysia with over 232,000 hectares , mainly along Sabah 's east and southeast coast . <p> " However some critical areas , for example on Sabah 's west coast , remain unprotected . <p> " While Sabah should be proud to host two wetlands recognised as being of global importance and designated as Ramsar sites -- the Lower sea has been reclaimed . <p> " This natural coastal protection and stabilisation from mangrove forests is going to become more and more important as sea levels rise and more severe storms hit Sabah .
