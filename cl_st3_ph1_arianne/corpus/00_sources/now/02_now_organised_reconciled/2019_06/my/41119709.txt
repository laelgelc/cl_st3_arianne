<h> Gas detected in Pasir Gudang could cause cancer - chemistry expert <p> The cause of the air pollution resulting in respiratory problems especially among school students in Pasir Gudang is still unknown . <p> However , the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin recently announced three types of gas , namely acrylonitrile , acrolein and methyl mercaptan were detected in the area . <p> Following that , a handful of residents in Pasir Gudang who were met by Bernama raised various questions regarding the side-effects on health over the next five to 10 years if they were exposed to the material . <p> Zulkifli Abdul Rashid , a senior lecturer at UiTM 's chemical engineering faculty , said based on his analysis , the acrylonitrile gas had a carcinogenic nature that could cause cancer if one was exposed to the gas daily or for too long . <p> This is evident from scientific studies as reported in the toxicology database by the Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry ( ATSDR ) for acrylonitrile , he said when contacted by Bernama . <p> " If they ( the residents ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ too long , it not only has short-term effects on health , but can harm the internal organs causing cancer . <p> " The results of epidemiological studies show that acrylonitrile can be carcinogenic to humans . Through experiments of chemicals or gas on laboratory mice , it has been found to cause tumours in mice . Even some scientific studies also show that acrylonitrile is also said to cause lung cancer in humans , " he said . <p> However , the probability of the population at risk of cancer depends on several factors including their medical history whether they have asthma , age factor , duration and concentration of exposure to the gas . <p> " In addition , the geographical aspect or position of residents living close to the current air pollution area are at a higher risk of developing cancer in a shorter time than those living far away from the source of these gaseous pollutants , " he explained . <p> The latest pollution incident causing respiratory problems in Pasir Gudang since June 20 is the second such incident after the chemical waste pollution crisis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than 4,000 people sick and 111 schools closed . <p> Meanwhile , Zulkifli also anticipates the presence of five toxic gases including the three that have been detected so far . Methyl mercaptan and acrylonitrile react with water elements to produce two other types of toxic gas such as hydrogen sulfide and hydrogen cyanide which results in a rotten and rancid odour . <p> The " mysterious " smell the people around Pasir Gudang have been getting was probably one of the two gases , he said . <p> Earlier , Bernama reported that most of the residents around Pasir Gudang who were affected complained the strange odour got worse at night and after it rains . <p> Zulkifli explained the acrid smell of hydrogen sulphide smells like rotten eggs , while hydrogen cyanide has an odour of " bitter almonds " but not everyone can detect the smell . <p> The presence of hydrogen cyanide gas was reported highest in Taman Tanjung Puteri Resort during the toxic waste disposal at Sungai Kim Kim , Pasir Gudang on March 13.
