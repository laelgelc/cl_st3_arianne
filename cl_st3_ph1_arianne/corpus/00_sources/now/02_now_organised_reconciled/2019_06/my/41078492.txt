<h> As ecological problems mount , listen to voices of the young <p> Given the mess adults have created , youths may have better ideas on how to build a sustainable and happier world , says Mustafa K Anuar . <p> The importance of safeguarding the environment could not have been stressed more in recent weeks . <p> Reports emerged about imports of plastic waste , legal or otherwise , polluted rivers and idle rare earth waste at Lynas in Pahang -- all of which raised deep concern among Malaysians . <p> The use of plastics in particular has become so overwhelming in our daily life that there are already moves domestically and internationally to reduce single-use plastics . For Malaysia to be known as the world 's dumping ground for plastic waste is a reputation we can do without . <p> Some of these damaging environmental incidents are often the result of an irresponsible and lackadaisical attitude among some civil servants , business people and ordinary Malaysians . These incidents have an impact in varying degrees on the health and general wellbeing of ordinary Malaysians . <p> Such environmental issues have also raised a red flag on food @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ factories , for instance , have a serious impact on vegetables and fruit grown in the vicinity . <p> Similarly , polluted rivers and coastlines will poison marine life such as the fish and prawns that end up on our dining tables . <p> Our domestic food supply will dry up over time if little is done to tackle land , air and water pollution . <p> Meanwhile , unregulated logging and deforestation will hit the Orang Asli hard . These communities are actually custodians of the environment . Often , they find themselves fighting against rampant logging , which destroys their livelihoods , their lifestyles , their culture -- and degrades Mother Nature . <p> That 's not all . Intrusion from so-called modernisation projects eg the construction of highways and other forms of human intervention disturbs animal life . These animals then become vulnerable to predatory poachers and end up as endangered species . <p> The impact of all this adds up , contributing to climate change and ecological degradation . <p> It was thus heartening and refreshing to see young people , inspired by Swedish teenager Greta @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ worldwide campaign to tackle climate change . They are demanding that adults in positions of power behave more responsibly towards the environment , which will be inherited by future generations . <p> Certainly , environmental degradation is not a legacy that the adults of today should bequeath to the young . <p> In this regard , we applaud and commend the relentless efforts of the Sungai Ara Tamil Primary School for having raised environmental consciousness among its pupils . The school is an excellent model for other schools in the country to emulate -- rather than being turned into an easy and unnecessary target of vilification in political circles . <p> Environmental awareness in Malaysia can be traced back to the pioneering work of the late consumer advocate SM Mohamed Idris , who founded and helmed the Consumers ' Association of Penang , Sahabat Alam Malaysia and Third World Network for a long time . <p> Idris and his colleagues helped to raise awareness especially among schoolchildren about the rights of consumers .
