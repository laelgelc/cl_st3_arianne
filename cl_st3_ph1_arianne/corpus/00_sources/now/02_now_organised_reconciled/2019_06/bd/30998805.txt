<p> The European Union ( EU ) has announced a fund of ? 18 million ( nearly Tk171.5 crore ) to help improve resilience among both the Rohingya refugees and the Bangladeshi host communities in Cox 's Bazar . <p> Over 288,000 children and families of both the communities will be benefited as a part of the EU-supported United Nations Children 's Fund ( Unicef ) program , titled " Building Rohingya Refugee and Host Community Resilience .
