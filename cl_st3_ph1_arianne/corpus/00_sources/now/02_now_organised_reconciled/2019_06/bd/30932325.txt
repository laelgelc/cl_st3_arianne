<h> TIB calls for Tk1,000 crore to combat climate change impact <p> Bangladesh has formed Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund , and has been allocating funds from the budget since 2009-10 <p> Transparency International Bangladesh ( TIB ) has urged the government to allocate Tk1,000 crore , in the upcoming fiscal year 2019-20 budget , to combat climate change impact . <p> The rights and anti-corruption watchdog also demanded transparency , accountability , and proper utilization of the fund for fighting climate change related issues . <p> Bangladesh has received praise from the global as well as domestic community for its timely , and multifaceted steps to combat impacts of climate change , said a statement issued by TIB on Saturday . <p> The statement also said , Bangladesh has formed Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund , and has been allocating funds to it from the budget since 2009-10 . <p> " Since the formation of Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund , the government allocated Tk700 crore per year during the first three years , " said Iftekharuzzaman , executive director of Transparency International Bangladesh ( TIB ) . <p> He said : " However , the budget allocation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change related risks , it rather decreased gradually . <p> " The decline in the amount of fund allocation against the increased risk of climate change was a great concern . " <p> " In meeting deficit of funds to mitigate the risk of climate change impacts , we are urging the government to allocate Tk1000 crore for the fund in the next budget , " he added . <p> In addition , Iftekharuzzaman also demanded proper steps , and guidelines to ensure accountability , and transparency in the spending of the allocated funds .
