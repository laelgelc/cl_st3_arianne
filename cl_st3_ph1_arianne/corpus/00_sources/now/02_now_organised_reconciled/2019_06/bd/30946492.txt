<h> Bangladesh seeks climate justice , compensation in Berlin meet <h> Bangladesh has asked for climate justice to be established and demanded compensation and adequate financial and other assistance for adaptation measures . <p> State Minister for Foreign Affairs Md Shahriar Alam made the demand at the ' Berlin Climate and Security Conference 2019 ' on Tuesday . <p> He said migration and relocation are options to consider for the persons displaced due to climate change . <p> He called for more concrete global coordination and " insisted that the developed countries and the countries responsible for producing huge amount of greenhouse gases must triple their nationally determined commitments ( NDCc ) if we want to limit the rise of global temperature below 2 degree centigrade and preferably up to 1.5 degree centigrade " . <p> To deal with climate change related displacement , Shahriar urged new binding legal instrument as the existing instruments are not adequate and are mostly voluntary . <p> The state minister described the grave threat of climate change to Bangladesh and explained how , if continued in the same fashion , this will cause extreme devastation to every sector of Bangladesh endangering all the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Agreement , 2030 Development Agenda ( SDGs ) and the Sendai Framework and continuation of existing facilities for LDCs after graduation as effective measures to fight climate change . <p> This annual conference is organised jointly by the German foreign ministry and two German independent think tanks namely Adelphi and Potsdam Institute of Climate Research . <p> The conference discussed the existential threat to human civilization as a whole due to ever worsening climate change and the grave danger it presents to the physical and political security of different countries and even individuals .
