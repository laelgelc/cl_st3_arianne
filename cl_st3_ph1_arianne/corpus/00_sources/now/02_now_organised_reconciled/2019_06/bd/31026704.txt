<h> SDGs to be missed without rational wealth distribution : Speakers <p> UNB News <p> Publish Date - June 28 , 2019 , 12:34 AM <p> 336 Views <p> Dhaka , Jun 27 ( UNB ) -- Speakers at a seminar on the sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) , the successor to the Millenium Development Goals , said that SDGs would not be achieved unless rational distribution of wealth is ensured through democratic practices . <p> " In our perspective , now the democracy and wealth are held only by few people . Our economy is of the 5 percent , by the 5 percent and for the 5 percent , " said Rashed Khan Mennon , president of Workers Party and also former civil aviation and tourism minister , while addressing the seminar organised by Centre for Policy Dialogue ( CPD ) in the city on Thursday . <p> The seminar titled , " What type of democracies are sustainable for achieving the SDGs ? " was also addressed by Saber Hossain Chowdhury , chairman of the parliamentary standing committee on Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Dr Rene Holestein , ambassador of Switzerland @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kendra , and Dr Tufail Ahmed , vice chancellor of Britannia University , Cumilla and local government expert . <p> Rashed Khan Menon said that although there was a promise made by the political parties during the fall of military rural Ershad that they would reform the local government system , they failed to do so . <p> " Again the 14-party alliance made the similar promise , but that promise was not implemented and the local government system is still not functioning in the country , " he said , of the alliance which he served over two terms as minister . <p> He said there are strong cells set up in the Prime Minister 's Office and other ministries to achieve the SDGs but the progress is not satisfactory . <p> " SDGs would not work unless we harmonise our democratic system , " he told the seminar . <p> Saber Hossain Chowdhuruy said democracy is one of the important aspects for achieving SDGs . But the other issues , specially the No-16 of SDGs which emphasizes peace , justice and strong institutions are not ensured in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bodies , the other goals of the SDGs would not be achieved , " he said , adding that it would have to be ensured nobody is left behind . <p> DrTufail Ahmed said that the goals of the SDGs have to be localised through the local government institutions . <p> " But unfortunately , local government bodies are not getting that importance in implementing the SDGs , " he said . <p> Dr Zafrullah Chowdhury said democratic practices and rights are absent in the country . " Unless we ensure people can talk freely and openly , we would not be able to achieve the SDGs . " <p> Presenting her experience Prof Rounaq Jahan said this has been a reality that international agencies do not finance any research on political issue while obstacles are faced when any data is sought from any government agencies to do research on any political issue . <p> " Even I had to face police harassment in seeking any data on political issues , " she said .
