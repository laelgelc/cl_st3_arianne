<h> CDSP Project , a ray of hope for the poor <p> Syed Nasir Hossain : Over 2.70 lakh poor families who have lost their homes due to natural disasters like cyclone , river erosion and landslides have got homes under the Char Development and Settlement Project IV ( CDSP IV ) of Bangladesh Water Development Board ( BWDB ) , said a government official . The main objective of the project is to make the homeless , landless and shelter less poor people capable of running their own income-generating initiatives having interest-free loan and training on various trades to make them economically independent . Engr Md Mahfuzur Rahman , Director General of BWDB told Daily Industry that the project would complete by December 2021 . The project would facilitate the poor and underprivileged people to meet their demand of housing . Commenting on the said project , Rahman said , the CDSP project is the role model for women empowerment too . Such activities of the project will be accelerated in future to alleviate poverty for achieving the Vision-2021 of the present government as well as attaining Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) by 2030 , said Shamsusdoha @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leadership in sheltering families displaced by climate related calamities , " he observed . The Project Purpose is to improve and more secure rural livelihoods for thousand of households who comprise the population of Nangulia , Noler char , Ziauddin and Urir Chars , Char Bagardona , Boyarchar , in the coastal areas of Noakhali , Laxmipur , Feni and Chattrogram districts in southeast Bangladesh . Under the project , a total of 44,410 acres of land have so far been distributed among 34,162 ultra poor families , said the project officials . The Project , one of the priority projects of the government , was designed to provide the distressed people with homes and other supports to be self-reliant . The Char Development and Settlement Project IV ( CDSP IV ) is jointly financed by the International Fund for Agricultural Development ( IFAD ) , the Government of Netherlands ( GoN ) and the Government of the People 's Republic of Bangladesh ( GoB ) , sources informed . Freedom fighters , widows and divorcee women are given priority in getting homes under the project . In the meantime , 543.98 kilometers of village roads , 341 bridge and culverts , 124 cyclone shelter center have been built in the project area besides 2,628 deep tube-wells and 15,834 numbers of sanitary latrine and public toilet have been set up . CDSP-IV is a very successful project that has already achieved a visible transformation in the target area and the lives of more than 28,000 beneficiary households ( 155,000 individuals ) . CDSP has delivered improvements of arable land with 60km of embankments to prevent saline intrusion , The combined effect of these interventions has been a major impact on natural resource management , climate change adaption , livelihoods and agriculture production . As the project now targets the impoverished people of the whole country , by aiding them to build their shelter with a means of income generation can also check the displacement of people from one place to another . Natural calamities like flood , erosion and drought uproot many people in the country almost every year . The victims find no other alternative but to migrate to cities and towns for survival . These rootless people add to the population of cities and towns that are already overburdened with millions of people . Majority of them do not have arable lands but they have patches of land where they are living after making shacks or shanties . For want of financial support they can not build sustainable dwelling houses there . These people are now availing easy loan facilities to make their homes .
