<h> Protecting the Sundarbans <p> There is no denying that the Sundarbans is a crucial part of Bangladeshi heritage <p> The fact that the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) , the official advisor on natural World Heritage , has recommended that the Sundarbans be classified as " World Heritage in danger " should worry us all . <p> There is no denying that the Sundarbans is a crucial part of Bangladeshi heritage : Not only is it infused with Bangladesh 's identity as a nation , being home to the Bengal tiger , it is also instrumental in preserving the region 's natural ecosystem , to say nothing of the thousands of people whose livelihoods are connected to its existence . <p> The fact of the matter is that we as a nation of have been negligent in ensuring that the Sundarbans are preserved as a natural heritage site . <p> With severe threats from coal-fired power plants and other industrial activity -- over 150 active industrial projects -- close to the Sundarbans , the world 's largest mangrove forest is in danger . <p> This should come as a wake-up call for the authorities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conservation of the Sundarbans a priority . <p> With Bangladesh being one of the countries most vulnerable to the detrimental effects of climate change , never has the natural ecosystem of our nation been more important . <p> It is up to the authorities now to ensure that governmental policy prioritizes the natural landscape of the Sundarbans , which has given us so much in terms of flora and fauna , and continues to contribute so much to Bangladesh 's identity , economy , and hydrological and ecological dynamics .
