<h> Bangladesh needs to detect , treat over 1.5 million new TB cases in next 5yrs <p> Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and USAID have signed a Statement of Partnership ( SP ) on Tuesday , June 25 , 2019 Courtesy <p> Health Ministry , USAID sign document to combat TB in Bangladesh <p> The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare and USAID have pledged to deepen their partnership to end tuberculosis ( TB ) in Bangladesh . <p> The newly signed new Statement of Partnership ( SP ) establishes a shared framework between the ministry and the United States Agency for International Development ( USAID ) to reduce the incidence of tuberculosis by 2022 through increasing case detection and treatment of TB in its all forms . <p> The two partners noted that Bangladesh needs to detect and treat more than 1.5 million new TB cases over the next five years . <p> This partnership marks the latest example of USAID 's new model of partnership , the Global Accelerator to End Tuberculosis , which aims to catalyze investments and aid countries across the world and meet the UN target of treating 40 million people by 2022 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Family Welfare reaffirmed Bangladesh 's commitment to significantly reduce cases of TB by 2022 . <p> USAID Mission Director Derrick Brown said : " By signing the Statement of Partnership , we at USAID recommit ourselves as a close partner of the Bangladesh government , as the country accelerates its efforts to achieve their targets by 2022 . " <p> He said USAID Bangladesh will strongly collaborate with the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in their mission of identifying , preventing , and treating all undetected forms of TB cases . <p> Bangladesh treated more than 2.5 million TB cases in 2018 . However , almost 26% of cases remain undetected and the rate is even higher for the multi-drug resistant TB cases and child TB cases . <p> Through the signing of the partnership statement , USAID Bangladesh will be a technical partner of the Bangladesh government in their journey towards ending TB in Bangladesh . <p> USAID Bangladesh 's TB investments will focus on strengthening the laboratory and diagnostic services , detection and treatment of all forms of Tuberculosis , and preventive therapy . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ $7 billion in development assistance to Bangladesh since 1971 . <p> In 2018 , USAID provided nearly $219 million to improve the lives of people in Bangladesh through programs that expand food security and economic opportunity , improve health and education , promote democratic institutions and practices , protect the environment , and increase resiliency to climate change .
