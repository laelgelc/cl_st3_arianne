<h> ' Clash of civilisation ' or crash : Environmental doomsday ? <p> What do the following civilisations have in common : Mesopotamia four millennia ago ; the 8th-century Viking Greenland settlement ; Mayas from the 10th century ; and the Khmer empire in the 15th century ? All were victims of climate change , caused by multiple factors , indicating the scourge 's outreaching tentacles . Respectively , these were drought and desertification ; a deep-freeze prohibiting farming ; over-population producing drought ; and drought alternating with quick but violent monsoon rains ( Sean Fleming , World Economic Forum , Newsletter , March 29 , 2019 ) . <p> Whether the 21st-century civilisations follow the same pathways will depend as much on what we do about reducing emissions , adjusting to rising ocean water consequences ( like coastal erosion , social dislocations , and economic disruptions ) , and effectively waging the green campaign of planting more trees , as it will on population controls , resource distribution , and ending conflicts .
