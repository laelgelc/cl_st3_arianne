<h> Barriers to environmental justice <p> The world is facing an environmental problem -- the increase in air pollution , deforestation , climate change , and other problems are threatening the existence of the world as we know it . <p> The problem is so crucial that a country alone can not solve it and , as a result , they are having to work together . <p> They are trying to pool together their resources in any way possible so that the environment is harmed less , or not at all . <p> Knowingly or unknowingly , people are damaging the environment and their consciousness in this regard will become increasingly important in the future . <p> Countries are introducing laws specific to the environment to empower ordinary people so that they too can play a role in the environment 's protection . <p> Bangladesh has a special court to deal with environmental issues by the Environment Court Act 2010 -- to ensure efficient environmental justice .
