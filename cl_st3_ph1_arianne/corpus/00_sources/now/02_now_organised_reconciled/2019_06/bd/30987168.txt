<h> Updating our waste management <p> Garbage disposal and waste management in the city have historically been sub-par <p> It is commendable that the government is working towards expanding the landfill stations of Dhaka -- there are plans currently in place to build several modern landfills , including one on 118 acres of land in Nasirabad -- in order to tackle the ever-present problem of waste management in the capital . <p> There is little doubt that garbage disposal and waste management in the city have historically been sub-par and that city corporations , past and present , have never truly tackled the challenge successfully and there is hope that these new initiatives could provide the building blocks for a more liveable Dhaka city . <p> What is cause for further encouragement is the government 's plans to implement more environmentally friendly projects for future waste disposal . <p> Bangladesh 's population density makes waste disposal even more challenging for the authorities , and with the threat of climate change looming large along with Bangladesh 's confirmed susceptibility to it , the authorities must be applauded for their foresight . <p> However , what is important now is for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are completed efficiently . <p> Far too often , there have been initiatives proposed but then were mired by inefficiency , poor management , and unnecessary bottlenecks -- this continues to derail the country 's progress . <p> Dhaka is recognized as one of the fastest growing cities in the world , and it is at the forefront of the country 's overall economic achievements . <p> However , our considerable economic milestones would be wasted if we can not raise the quality of life in our capital and largest city .
