<h> TIB calls for Tk1,000 crore to combat climate change impact <p> The rights and anti-corruption watchdog also demanded transparency , accountability and proper utilization of the fund to be allocated for fighting the climate change related issues <p> Transparency International Bangladesh ( TIB ) on Saturday urged the government to allocate Tk1,000 crore in the upcoming budget for the fiscal year 2019-20 to combat growing impact of climate change . <p> The rights and anti-corruption watchdog also demanded transparency , accountability and proper utilization of the fund to be allocated for fighting the climate change related issues . <p> Bangladesh has received laud from the global as well as domestic community for its timely and multipurpose steps to combat climate change impact , says a statement issued yesterday . <p> Bangladesh has formed Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund and has been allocating fund from the budget since 2009-10 . <p> " Since the formation of Bangladesh Climate Change Trust Fund , the government allocated Tk700 crore per year during the first three years , " said Iftekharuzzaman , executive director of Transparency International Bangladesh ( TIB ) . <p> However , the budget allocation did not increase in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ decreased gradually , he said . <p> The decline in amount of fund allocation against the increased risk of climate change was a great concern , said Iftekharuzzaman . <p> " In meeting deficit of fund to mitigate the risk climate change impact , we are urging the government to allocate Tk1000 crore for the fund in the next budget , " he added . <p> In addition , Iftekharuzzaman also demanded proper steps and guideline to ensure accountability and transparency in the spending of the allocated funds .
