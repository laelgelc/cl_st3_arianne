<h> EU to donate 18 million euros for Rohingyas <p> Staff Correspondent <p> European Union has announced a fund of 18 million euros to help build resilience among Rohingya refugees and Bangladeshi host communities . <p> Over 288,000 children and families will benefit as part of a three-year long EU-supported Unicef programme , building synergy across four key areas of nutrition ; water , sanitation and hygiene ; education ; and child protection , said a joint statement of European Union ( EU ) and Unicef yesterday . <p> " The support from the European Union allows us to address the issue of resilience in a holistic manner , integrating and harmonising services for the delivery of better results , " says Tomoo Hozumi , Unicef Representative in Bangladesh . <p> " We have substantially scaled up our humanitarian response over the past year and a half thanks to strong support from partners like the European Union . At the same time , the situation remains critical with 1.2 million people requiring humanitarian assistance in one of Bangladesh 's poorest and most vulnerable districts . Over half of the girls marry before the age of 18 years and close to 50,000 children are engaged in child labour . <p> The district records very high rates of malnutrition . One in two children suffer from stunted growth due to poor maternal nutrition and lack of adequate infant feeding and care practices . <p> Further , Cox 's Bazar is one of the most vulnerable districts to natural disasters in the country including cyclones , flooding and landslides caused by monsoon rains , and the impact of climate change . <p> " We need to act decisively to secure a better future for all those affected by the refugee crisis , " said Rensje Teerink , EU Ambassador to Bangladesh . <p> To date , the European Union has contributed 24.8 million euros to Unicef to support the Rohingya refugees and host communities in Bangladesh .
