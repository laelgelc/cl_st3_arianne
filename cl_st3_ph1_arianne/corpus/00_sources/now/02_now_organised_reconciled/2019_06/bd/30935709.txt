<h> Govt set to formulate standards to monitor workplaces , protect workers <p> The government is formulating a set of standards , which will impose limits on indoor air pollution , especially in workplaces , and empower itself to punish violators . <p> This is the first time there will be a legal instrument on maintaining indoor air quality . A set of standards is already there to combat outdoor pollution . <p> Officials said the new standards will enable the government to monitor air pollution inside workplaces and deal with workers ' health issues . <p> Because of the absence of a legal framework on indoor air quality , the government earlier could not assess the level of air pollution at garment or cement factories , rerolling mills , medical facilities , and shopping malls . <p> According to the Occupational Safety and Health Administration ( OSHA ) of the US Department of Labour , poor indoor air quality can cause headache , fatigue , trouble concentrating , and irritation of the eyes , nose , throat and lungs . <p> Also , some specific diseases have been linked to specific air contaminants or indoor environments like asthma @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , there are some exposures , such as asbestos and radon , which do not cause immediate symptoms but can lead to cancer after many years , it adds . <p> The Standards for Indoor Air Quality , the legal instrument which deals with air pollution inside homes and workplaces , is part of the government 's effort to ensure clean air amid concerns over poor air quality in major cities of Bangladesh . <p> In a recent survey , Dhaka has been ranked the second most polluted capital city in the world after New Delhi . <p> SM Munjurul Hannan Khan , director of the Clean Air and Sustainable Environment ( CASE ) project on curbing outdoor pollution , said , " As we do n't have any indoor standards , we ca n't say to what extent air pollution is happening inside the four walls . The new standards will help us understand this pollution . " <p> The new set of standards complements the National Ambient Air Quality Standards , which deals with outdoor air pollution .
