<h> There 's more than one side to any investment story <p> In the second quarter of an already eventful year , investors are faced with a range of stark choices . The strange part to note here , though , is that despite reporting sturdy economic growth , there is no underlying inflation -- or at least not in the way that the Fed measures it . <p> Why does this matter ? This apparent lack of inflation has changed the story we are seeing in the United States .
