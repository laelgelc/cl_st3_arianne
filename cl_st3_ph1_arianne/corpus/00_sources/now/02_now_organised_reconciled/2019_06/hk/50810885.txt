<h> NSO celebrates 13th Statistics Day in Srinagar <p> Srinagar , Jun 29 ( KNS ) : The National Statistical Office ( NSO ) under Union Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation , Government of India celebrated 13th Statistics Day , 2019 in collaboration with Directorate of School Education , Jammu and Kashmir government in auditorium of S.P Higher Secondary School M.A. Road Srinagar on Saturday . <p> The theme of 13th Statistics Day was " Sustainable Development Goals " ( SDG ) . <p> In the inaugural session , Altaf Hussain Haji said that the development and wellbeing are the two burning issues of each and every country of the world and that they can be viewed through Sustainable Development Goals with " targets " and " indicators " . <p> He also said that the success of these surveys depends to great extent on the quality of the response from public in general for providing correct information and also requested to all the stakeholders to extend their cooperation and spare valuable time to provide correct and complete information . <p> S N Pradhan , ADG PIB said in his speech that the agreement committed by UN members @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) which are ( 1 ) End poverty ( 2 ) Zero hunger .
