" <p> The expert noted that despite global alarm bells ringing over the threat of climate change , the issue remains a " marginal concern " within the human rights community . <p> He specifically criticised the UN Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights for not devoting enough attention and resources to the issue . <p> " As a full-blown crisis that threatens the human rights of vast numbers of people bears down , the usual piecemeal , issue-by-issue human rights methodology is woefully insufficient , " he said . <p> All special rapporteurs are independent experts who do not speak for the UN but report their finding to the world body .
