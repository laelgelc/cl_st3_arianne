<p> The economic cost of the Knysna wildfire came to at least R3.3 billion , the biggest portion impacting the insurance industry . <p> This is according to Richard Walls from the Fire Engineering Research Unit ( FireSUN ) at Stellenbosch University . <p> He was speaking at the release of an in-depth authoritative and independent report , commissioned by short-term insurer Santam , into the devastating fires that struck the Knysna area in June 2017 . <p> The Knysna fires were the worst wildfire disaster in South African history and the report found that their severity was caused by a cocktail of factors including drought , low atmospheric humidity , strong winds and abundant fuel . <p> Walls said during his presentation that the economic cost of the Knysna fire was at least R3.3bn . <p> " This is a minimum figure .
