<h> Political Analysis South Africa <h> Ethiopia to launch earth observatory satellite into space by November 2019 <h> Ethiopia to launch earth observatory satellite into space by November 2019 <p> Published on <p> 24 June , 2019 <h> Ethiopia has finalized preparations to launch an earth observatory satellite into space by November 2019 , according to Ethiopian Space Science and Technology Institute . <p> Institute Director-General Solomon Belay said " we have completed about 95 percent of the satellite , and checking and launching will be conducted after September . " <p> The satellite will help provide agricultural information , water and irrigation shade amount , rural development as well as climate change in the country , Solomon said . <p> The satellite will also scientifically report data of the forest coverage in the country . For instance , the ongoing four billion tree planting initiative progress of the seedlings can be supervised through the satellite . <p> Professionals in the area have already received trailing to control and maintain the satellite , Solomon pointed out . <p> Deputy Director-General of the institute Abdisa Yilma said that the final work left is to check the satellite before launching it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " During the implementation stage it will be operated and maintained by our professional . Currently we have the capacity to partially design , operate and maintain the satellite as an institute and as a country . " Abdissa added . <p> The satellite has been manufactured in collaboration with Chinese government . Ethiopian engineers and scientists have been participating from the design to the last process , Abdisa continued . <p> Ethiopia 's earth observatory satellite is expected to be launched from China .
