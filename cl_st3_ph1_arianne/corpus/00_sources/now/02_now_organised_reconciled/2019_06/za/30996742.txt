<h> Kouga to host workshop <p> 2019-06-20 06:00 <p> MORE than 100 delegates from nine countries will gather in Jeffreys Bay this October for an internatio-nal climate change workshop . <p> Set to take place at Mentorskraal Country Estate from October 9 to 11 , the workshop will bring together representatives from Germany , South Africa , Namibia , Zimbabwe , Rwanda , Burkina Faso , Peru , Ecuador and Nicaragua for discussions on how municipalities can safeguard their communities from the potentially devastating impact of climate change . <p> Executive Mayor Horatio Hendricks said Kouga Municipality was honoured to have been selected to host the workshop . <p> " We were one of three African and South American municipalities under consideration and are thrilled to have been chosen , " he said . <p> The workshop is a continuation of Kouga 's climate change partnership with the German municipality Ilsfeld . <p> Twelve German municipalities and their respective partners from Africa and South America will be in attendance .
