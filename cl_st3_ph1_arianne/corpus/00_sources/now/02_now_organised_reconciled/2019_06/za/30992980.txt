<h> SA 37th most dangerous country in the world - much worse than El Salvador <p> By Unathi Nkanjeni - 19 June 2019 <p> South Africa has slipped to 127 on the Global Peace Index , an annual survey of the world 's safest countries . Image : Supplied <p> South Africa is the 37th most dangerous country on earth , according to this year 's Global Peace Index ( GPI ) , which was released on June 12 . <p> Mzansi dropped two places compared to the 2018 report . In the global ranking , SA is at 127th . Two countries notorious for gang violence are ranked more peaceful : El Salvador at 113th and Honduras at 123rd . <p> The index is released annually by the Institute for Economics and Peace , a Sydney-based think tank that analyses the economic costs of violence . <p> The survey is based on statistics from various United Nations organs , the Economist Intelligence Unit and numerous other international bodies . <p> It measures a wide range of weighted factors including violent crime , political instability , policing and external conflict .
