REUTERS/PETER NICHOLLS <p> London -- British MPs will hold a Citizens ' Assembly to gather views on how to meet its newly strengthened emissions targets , parliament announced on Thursday , in response to growing public unease over the climate crisis . <p> Although the assembly , to be held over several weekends later in 2019 , would have no independent powers , MPs said its deliberations would inform broader discussion over how Britain can deliver on a commitment to reach net zero carbon by 2050 . <p> " Ending our contribution to climate change can be the defining decision of our generation in fulfilling our responsibility to the next , " Greg Clark , secretary of state for business , energy and industrial strategy , said . " But it will require the effort @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countries have started to make increased use of Citizens ' Assemblies , to serve both as pressure valves on contentious issues and to mobilise support for decisions that legislatures driven by election cycles might struggle to pass . <p> Participants are often chosen to reflect the wider population in terms of demographics such as age , gender , ethnicity , class and sometimes political attitudes . They have been used in Ireland , Canada , Australia and the US . <p> Britain became the first major economy to enshrine a net zero carbon target into law last week , a move that would imply profound and rapid transformation in sectors from energy and transport to food , farming and housing . <p> Although government decisions to support fracking , back a third runway at Heathrow and cut subsidies to the solar industry , have raised questions about its commitment to a low-carbon world , some industries see big gains from energy transition . <p> " I hope the Citizens ' Assembly will demonstrate that , when all is considered , there is strong public support -- even demand -- for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ benefits of net zero by 2050 , " said Rachel Reeves , chair of parliament 's business , energy and industrial strategy committee . <p> Climate activist group Extinction Rebellion has demanded the government convene a Citizens ' Assembly to address the climate emergency , but wants it to be chosen by lot and given real decision-making power . The group said the announcement was a " first step " towards giving people a voice . <p> " However , we can not pretend that this is a legitimate assembly with real or legislative power , " it said . " A half-arsed attempt at a Citizens ' Assembly will doom the process and the results . "
