<h> National Environment month is celebrated every year in June , with the South African government focused on raising awareness about climate change . <p> The international cousin of National Environment Month -- World Environment Day -- forms part of the United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) . It is celebrated annually on 5 June . The focus this year is on climate change . <p> The day was adopted in the early seventies with a view to deepening environmental awareness . In addition , governments address concerns about the depletion of the ozone layers , global warming , desertification , etc . <p> In South Africa , we dedicate the entire month of June to environmental concerns , with the Department of Environmental Affairs leading the way .
