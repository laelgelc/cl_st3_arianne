<p> Just four years ago , in Paris , nearly 200 countries agreed to tackle climate change . The Paris Agreement was supposed to be the defining moment in the fight against carbon emissions , which trap heat in the atmosphere and warm up the planet , causing changes in the climate . <p> Just last week , the amount of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere jumped to its highest level in three million years -- 414.8 parts per million -- and to the highest level in human history . <p> The higher the concentration of carbon in the atmosphere , the more effective it is at trapping heat . That heat is driving climate collapse around the world , resulting in ever-more damaging droughts , floods and other natural disasters . <p> The data on carbon concentrations comes from the Scripps Institution of Oceanography , situated on top of a mountain on an island in Hawaii . It has been recording for more than six decades .
