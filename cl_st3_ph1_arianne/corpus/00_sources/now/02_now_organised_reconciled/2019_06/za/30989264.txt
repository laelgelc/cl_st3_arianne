<h> Seychelles floating solar project a first for Africa <p> Implementation of Africa 's first independent power producer floating solar photovoltaic ( FPV ) project is continuing in the Seychelles . The project , launched by the Seychelles Ministry of Environment , Energy and Climate Change and the Seychelles Energy Commission , will be the first utility-scale , private-sector funded floating solar project in Africa , and aims to support the country 's transition to renewable energy . <p> The project is being implemented by the Government of Seychelles and the Public Utilities Corporation with the support of the African Legal Support Facility and the Clinton Foundation , with Trinity International LLP and Multiconsult Norge AS serving as the transaction and tender advisers .
