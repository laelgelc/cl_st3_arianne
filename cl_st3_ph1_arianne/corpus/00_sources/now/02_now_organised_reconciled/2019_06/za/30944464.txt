<h> World Environment Day : Eleven ways to get involved <p> Here is how you can reduce air pollution and save the environment , while also benefiting the health of those around you . <p> Female commuter wearing mask/ iStock <p> June 5 is World Environment Day . This year 's theme is ' Air Pollution ' . <p> Air pollution is a serious global problem . Inhaling polluted air over a long period of time can result in lung diseases , asthma , bronchitis , and cancer . According to **26;219;TOOLONG , air pollution causes 1 in 9 deaths globally , and an estimated seven-million premature deaths every year . <p> Air pollution is also bad for the environment . It causes climate change , decreases the oxygen supply in oceans , and makes it harder for plants to grow . <p> There are several gases and things that cause air pollution . The burning of fossil fuels ( coal , oil , natural gas , and gasoline ) to produce electricity and power vehicles causes air pollution . <p> Cleanerandgreener.org reports that humans cause air pollution through the use of electricity , fuels , and transportation , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in their production and delivery .
