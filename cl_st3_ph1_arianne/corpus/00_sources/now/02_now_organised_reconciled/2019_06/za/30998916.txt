<h> Some of the key topics being discussed during Sustainability Week <p> Early next week the 5th annual Sustainability Week 2019 will take place at the CSIR International Convention Centre ( ICC ) in the City of Tshwane from the 24th to the 26th June . <p> The gathering will see Africa 's local government leaders and related stakeholders discuss sustainable city growth and dive into the various facets of sustainable service delivery and the green economy in on the continent . <p> According to organisers , there will be a number of topics up for discussion , with leaders on the continent holding the following seminars that we think are particularly noteworthy . <h> Sustainable Cities Africa Conference <p> This seminar will take place on the second day of the conference , where it will see regional leaders discuss critical topics which will introduce potential solutions and also provide a channel for actual deal making to take place . <h> Sustainable water resource seminar <p> Currently South Africa is facing a water crisis , and this seminar will see experts present their findings on climate change .
