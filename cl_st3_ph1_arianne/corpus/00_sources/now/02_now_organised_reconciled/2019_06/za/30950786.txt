The sixth Parliament has not yet sat , the relevant committee chairpersons have not yet been appointed , and already this committee faces a problem that brings together many of the challenges that lie ahead for this new ministry . <p> Fortunately , Didiza is an old hand , respected by most stakeholders and described as professional and calm , which is exactly what she is going to have to be . <p> Adding the government responsibilities of agriculture to the vexing challenges of land reform can @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Didiza was a masterful choice by President Cyril Ramaphosa . Kriek reportedly said he was " cautiously optimistic " . <p> Didiza was also welcomed at the other end of the spectrum by the activist NGO , the Institute for Poverty , Land and Agrarian Studies ( Plaas ) , based at the University of the Western Cape , which has been closely engaged in the land expropriation process . Land reform concerns political and social issues that extend well beyond simply redistribution of land , and faces obstacles in the form of incomplete legislation that is still grappling with land ownership in former Bantustans . Particularly troublesome is the Traditional Courts Bill , which is very likely to be revived by the sixth Parliament as it goes to the heart of traditional leadership and the extent of its control over a large portion of South Africa 's rural territory . <p> Agriculture , on the other hand , is itself a broad area requiring expertise ranging from marketing to disease control . It currently faces specific challenges with the ongoing drought in many parts of the country that has crippled the sector . Agriculture , along with mining and manufacturing , is especially in need of urgent attention in the light of the latest shock drop in GDP figures . <p> Land reform is further complicated by the conflicting needs of Minister Barbara Creecy 's enormous environmental and climate change challenges , as head of Environment , Forestry and Fisheries , as well as those of mining @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ how the land is to be put to use .
