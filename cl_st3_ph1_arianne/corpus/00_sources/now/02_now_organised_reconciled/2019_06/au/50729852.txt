Three more years in which carbon emissions will continue to grow , as they have for the past four years , three more years of torturing refugees , three more years of screwing workers and welfare beneficiaries . But that is no cause for despair . We ca n't wait three years ; we have to fight now . <p> There is every indication that the new Morrison government will continue where it left off before the election . <p> Even before parliament has resumed , we 've seen the same nasty , reactionary and racist approach .
