<p> For the first time since my family began farming on this property in the 1950s , we did not receive enough rain last year to sow winter crops . I am a third generation farmer and throughout my family 's farming history , there has always been good times and bad times on the land . Unless we receive good rain soon , we face the very real prospect of having no winter crops for the second successive year . We consider ourselves lucky though as , despite reduced yields , we did get summer crops . Some farmers have missed both summer and winter crops for the past couple of years . Sydney will face water restrictions for the first time in a decade . The drought that has plagued farmers and rural communities is starting to be felt in metropolitan areas .
