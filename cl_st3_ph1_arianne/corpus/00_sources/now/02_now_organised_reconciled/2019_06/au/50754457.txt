<p> While the WA EPA has taken a step back from the " carbon neutral " Rubicon it planned to leap across back in March , the revised guidance that will shape the next 12 weeks of industry consultation evinces the same determination to fill the breach left by a failure of national climate urgency . <p> The effect of the plan was that WA would take national leadership in climate change management . Future projects would be required to be carbon neutral and mitigation strategies would need to account for emissions generated by any particular project and those subsequently manufactured by their customers . <p> As the EPA noted in its latest consultation framework , the release of the original assessment guidelines " was met with diverse public reaction with serious concerns raised by some companies with potential exposure to these increased requirements " . <p> This is a Yes Minister sort of way of recalling that some very big investors in future energy and minerals projects in WA went ballistic over both the nature of the impositions planned for future development approvals and the fact that this profound reform platform had landed without any effective consultation . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chief whip for opposition to the new EPA guidelines was Woodside Petroleum managing director , Peter Coleman .
