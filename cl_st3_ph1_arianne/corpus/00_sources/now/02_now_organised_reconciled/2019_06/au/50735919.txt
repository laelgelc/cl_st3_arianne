<p> To mark World Environment Day on June 5 , a new series of brochures titled Threatened Fauna of the Hunter and Mid Coast has been released . The serious has been released by Hunter Local Land Services ( HLLS ) to help build community awareness about endangered species in our region , and the roles they play in maintaining biodiversity and habitats . The new brochures are on the regent honeyeater , swift parrot , Eastern curlew , and Australasian bittern . These are all priority species under the Australian government 's National Landcare program . Related : Community invited to Manning River Turtle Conservation Workshop Brochures have also been developed for the Manning River helmeted turtle ( found only in the upper and middle catchments of the Manning River ) , brush-tailed rock wallaby , spotted-tail quoll , and koala . HLLS Natural Resource Management team leader , Lyndel Wilson said the brochures are helpful guides on how to identify and support local threatened species .
