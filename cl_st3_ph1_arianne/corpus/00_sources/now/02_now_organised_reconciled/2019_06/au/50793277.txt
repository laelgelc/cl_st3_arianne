<p> The State Government has rejected reports it is planning a carbon tax as Prime Minister Morrison today warned that carbon emissions reduction with the wrong target or an unclear mechanism could cost jobs . <p> " We 've got no plan for a carbon tax in Western Australia , " Environment Minister Stephen Dawson said today in response to media reports based on documents tabled in State Parliament earlier this month . <p> The documents revealed that Mr Dawson 's office was told the Environmental Protection Authority would propose " requirements to offset all direct emissions " for large projects two weeks before the EPA issued its controversial guidance on March 7 . <p> The February 21 memo to Mr Dawson from the Department of Water and Environment Regulation said the EPA 's recommendations would be " significantly more rigorous " than standard industry practise and place " significant burdens " on LNG producers . <p> DWER said the cost would be " broadly consistent with the internal carbon price " that oil and gas companies used when assessing possible costs of addressing climate change that could be imposed after they decide to invest in a project . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on 28 February , estimated the cost of offsets to the Chevron-operated Wheatstone LNG Project was between $30 and $130 million a year at full production . <p> The maximum offset cost is equivalent to 1.7 per cent of the $7.8 billion annual revenue the Wheatstone project would earn from LNG sales , based on the prices received by 13 per cent owner Woodside in the first quarter of 2019 .
