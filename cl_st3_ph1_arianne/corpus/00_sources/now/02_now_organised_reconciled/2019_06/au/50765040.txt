<h> Wolfe offers ag fixes to ' complex , severe ' climate change <p> David Wolfe , professor in the School of Integrative Plant Science ( SIPS ) , told a congressional committee in a hearing on agricultural resiliency that climate change impacts have been more complex and severe than scientists had forecast three decades ago . He called for more technology solutions . <p> U.S. House Agriculture Committee/Provided <p> David Wolfe appears before the U.S. House Subcommittee on Biotechnology , Horticulture and Research June 12 in Washington .
