<h> Highlights of G20 agreements <p> AP Photo XLP105 , XLP106 <p> AAP <p> Sunday , 30 June 2019 9:36 am <p> The two-day Group of 20 summit in Osaka concluded with a slew of agreements.Image : AP <p> Highlights of the agreements from the Osaka summit of the leaders of the Group of 20 major economies , spanning a slew of issues including global warming , trade and international finance , the environment and sustainable development : <p> TRADE AND THE GLOBAL ECONOMY <p> * The leaders agreed to address inequality and use smart policies to protect against threats to global growth from tensions over trade and geopolitical issues . <p> * They pledged to build up financial " buffers " to ensure government debts are manageable and to ensure monetary policies keep prices stable and economies growing . <p> * Leaders discussed urgent problems with pollution , loss of biodiversity and climate change , noting the need for a shift in thinking about financing and the involvement of businesses in innovation and investment in sustainable growth . <p> * But their declaration included a recognition of the US decision to withdraw from the Paris Agreement on reducing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ progress from 2005 to 2017 in reducing such pollution . <p> --- <p> CYBERSECURITY AND TERRORISM <p> * The G-20 is committed to doing more to prevent use of the internet to fund and facilitate terrorism and extremism . <p> * While the internet must be open , free and secure , it also can not serve as a " safe haven for terrorists , " the leaders said . <p> * The leaders also agreed that such efforts must respect human rights and " fundamental freedoms such as freedom of expression and access to information " . <p> SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT <p> * The leaders say they will work to ensure " no one is left behind " by alleviating poverty , investing in good quality infrastructure , promoting gender equality and providing access to health , education and training .
