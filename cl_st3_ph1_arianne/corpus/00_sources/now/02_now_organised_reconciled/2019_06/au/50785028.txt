<h> 2019 National Prizes : BlueScope Glenn Murcutt Student Prize <h> Jury citation <p> The jury was unanimous in awarding the 2019 BlueScope Steel Glenn Murcutt Student Prize to Sobi Slingsby of Griffith University for her project on Lady Elliot Island . <p> Sobi 's scheme is a beautifully considered architectural response to the anticipated impact of climate change and sea-level rise on a coral cay at the southernmost tip of the Great Barrier Reef . The proposal is for a group of shelters , both on land and over the water , that provide the essential needs of human habitation . The land-based structures are kept below the treetops , while those on the water are raised on sturdy foundations and imagined to foster new coral growth as the coral cay adapts to climate change . <p> The natural world is not static -- the shelters can be tuned to suit different climatic conditions and adverse weather to capture breezes , offer shade and withstand the extremes of tropical cyclones . The technologies are simple and direct , providing an experience akin to camping .
