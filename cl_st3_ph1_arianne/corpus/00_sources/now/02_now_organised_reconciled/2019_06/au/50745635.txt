The meadow garden has become a very popular theme in many of the big National Trust gardens . The main purpose of meadows is to provide a habitat for insects to feed and breed all year . <p> Inside the pavilion were the most beautiful displays of all the plants we could only dream of growing in WA . Many of them are commonly seen in English borders -- alliums , peonies , delphiniums , lupin , clematis and of course roses . <p> Some display gardens focused on climate change and the need to select more drought-hardy plants . Mind you , a drought in England equates to going three weeks without rain . <p> Trees were also a major focus to provide shade in summer and nesting areas for birds . <p> It was interesting to see that in England there are also major concerns about loss of habitat and decreasing opportunities for kids to get outdoors and explore nature .
