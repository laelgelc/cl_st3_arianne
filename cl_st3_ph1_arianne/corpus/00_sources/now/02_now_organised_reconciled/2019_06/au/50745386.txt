<h> Canada supports Inuit-led climate change strategy <p> Comments Off on Canada supports Inuit-led climate change strategy <p> From : Environment and Climate Change Canada <p> Inuit have lived off the land for millennia and the wildlife and environment around them are central to their culture , well-being , and economy . Climate change is causing permafrost to thaw , sea ice to disappear , and threatening the animals Inuit rely on . The North is warming at three times the global rate and as the impacts of climate change continue to threaten ways of life , it is increasingly essential to mitigate climate change and build resilience in the North . <p> Today , the Minister of Environment and Climate Change , Catherine McKenna , the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations , Carolyn Bennett , and the Minister of Indigenous Services , Seamus O'Regan , announced federal support of $1 million to implement the National Inuit Climate Change Strategy ( NICCS ) . Inuit observations have provided useful information at different time scales and levels of detail that have significantly contributed to our understanding of climate change in Nunavut . Some examples : <p> Sea ice conditions have changed ; the ice is thinner , freezes up later and melts earlier . Similar observations have been made for lake ice . <p> Aniuvat ( permanent snow patches ) are decreasing in size .
