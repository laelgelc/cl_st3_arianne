<h> Leading Australian artists are taking on the political class in this free exhibition <p> It 's not every art gallery that consults ABC election analyst Antony Green 's predictions when setting the opening of an exhibition . But Campbelltown Arts Centre 's OK Democracy , We Need to Talk is all about our democratic process . The team took Green 's predictions of three dates when the election might fall and scheduled their opening for the middle weekend , May 18 and 19 . Luckily for them , that was the weekend prime minister Scott Morrison picked . <p> The ensuing exhibition is made up of all new artworks about how we vote , how we receive information and how we can make our voices heard . <p> " There 's a lot of talk about capitalism , there 's a lot of talk about journalism , there 's a lot of talk about protest , " Campbelltown Arts Centre director Michael Dagostino says . " All these big conversations are happening in the public at the moment . As artists are part of communities , they 're having those same conversations .
