A mere three weeks after the election and the urgency of the task has become clear : the Reserve Bank cut its cash rate on Tuesday to a new record low of 1.25 per cent , with more to come . That came with a warning from governor Philip Lowe that monetary policy has its limits , particularly without structural reform agenda for growth . That was underlined the next day by Wednesday 's news that annual economic growth has softened to only 1.8 per cent . <p> In turn , that 's brought to the surface the central problem with the election campaign .
