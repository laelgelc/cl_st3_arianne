<p> Half of NSW 's coastal community thinks rising sea levels will not impact them directly , new data released today by UNSW scientists has shown - and 25 per cent of surveyed accommodation businesses situated close to the coast are unsure if sea level rise is even occurring . The report - released on the anniversary of the 2016 East Coast Low ' superstorm ' that saw widespread damage along Australia 's east coast , including the collapse of a Collaroy swimming pool - describes what the NSW community understands about coastal erosion and inundation , as well as the driving forces behind these hazards : sea level rise and severe coastal storms .
