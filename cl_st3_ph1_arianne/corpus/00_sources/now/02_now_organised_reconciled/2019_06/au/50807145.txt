<p> A theatre production supported by local mental healthcare providers is set to be held at the Lithgow State Mine on Saturday , October 12 . The show will be based on real stories from real people living with a mental illness within the community . It will highlight the challenges faced by people with mental health conditions and use these as the basis for songs , poetry , comedy and spoken word . The show was funded under one of four Nepean Blue Mountains Primary Health Network ( NBMPHN ) Well-Being Grants to Support Farming Communities . Lead Creative Developer for Trybal Productions James Daley said the aim of the show was to enhance the social and emotional well-being of both the participants and those who came to view it . " The project has the potential to help reduce the feeling of isolation and provides opportunities for informal and formal support . It will be fun and nurturing , and will help to build the resilience needed to sustain people through hard times , " he said .
