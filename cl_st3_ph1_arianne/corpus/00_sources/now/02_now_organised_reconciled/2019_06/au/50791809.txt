<h> Labor must now pass the full set of tax cuts <p> Labor lost the political argument at the election . But its tax stance shows that the party does not understand the policy reasons why either . <p> Jun 24 , 2019 -- 12.00am <p> Labor lost a federal election after pitching its well-worn ' fairness ' mantra against the Coalition 's cause of aspiration for ordinary workers . To his credit , new Labor leader Anthony Albanese now says that people earning $200,000 a year are not " the big end of town " . That 's the term former leader Bill Shorten first used in 2016 to describe multinational companies . By election day last month , Mr Shorten 's list of class enemies had expanded to small business owners , retirees on modest incomes , successful tradies -- or the blue-collar electrical engineer at the port of Gladstone who embarrassed him with an awkward tax question on high-earning resources workers . <p> In the end , the only Australians who swung to Labor were already well-off voters more concerned with post-material issues such as climate change and refugees .
