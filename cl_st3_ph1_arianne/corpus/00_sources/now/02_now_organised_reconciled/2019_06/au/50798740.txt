<h> What the future of wine will be like in the region <p> A UNIVERSITY of Southern Queensland wine expert is part of an international research project looking at how millennials are adapting to a new world of wine . <p> Professor Jeff Gow said a new generation of drinkers was seeking alternative options to traditional wines , such as organic or earth-friendly wine . <p> " Climate change is the canary in the coal mine , driving changes in varieties , which combined with new consumer tastes will change business models , " he said . " Non-traditional wine production is increasing at 30-40 per cent a year , and although it 's currently a small part of the Australian market it 's expected to grow significantly over the next few decades as seen in the large USA and Europe markets .
