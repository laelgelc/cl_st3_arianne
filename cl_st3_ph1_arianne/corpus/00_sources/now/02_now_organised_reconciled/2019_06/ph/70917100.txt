<h> PH co-organized UN forum proves Manila active vs. climate change <p> Ambassador Evan Garcia , Permanent Representative to the United Nations in Geneva <p> MANILA -- Manila continues to take part in conferences tackling ways to address the growing threat of climate change as manifested in an event co-organized by the Philippines on the sidelines of the 41st Human Rights Council session in Geneva early this week . <p> On June 26 , the Philippine government , together with civil society members , co-organized the panel discussion titled " Fighting Climate Change through International Solidarity " . <p> Representing the Philippine delegation was Permanent Representative to the United Nations in Geneva Evan Garcia , who underscored that Manila continues to recognize the need for " climate action . " <p> " As a country that is one of the most vulnerable to the adverse impacts of climate change , the Philippines has long recognized the dangers of inaction , " Garcia said in his opening remarks during the event . <p> " Yet action without vision or a wide base of support is equally perilous : well-meaning response , if sporadic or isolated to a few sectors , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ into complacency , " he said . <p> For climate action to be sustainable and effective , he called for the involvement of all segments of the population and sectors of society . <p> " It has to be deliberate , relentless , strategic , and inclusive .
