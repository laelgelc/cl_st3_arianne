( Photo courtesy of BFAR ) <p> TACLOBAN CITY -- The Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources ( BFAR ) will finally have its new building , nearly six years after super typhoon Yolanda ( Haiyan ) wrecked the regional office . <p> The fisheries bureau is eyeing the completion of its five-story disaster-resilient building by mid of 2020 after it was delayed for several years due to bureaucratic hurdles . <p> BFAR Regional Director Juan Albaladejo told the Philippine News Agency ( PNA ) on Friday the PHP189-million project has its groundbreaking on May 29 with key officials from BFAR , Philippine Fisheries Development Authority ( PFDA ) , and National Economic and Development Authority . <p> " It 's like a dream come true for us . We painstakingly went through the process , but it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is storm surge and climate change proof , " said Albaladejo in a phone interview . <p> Funds for the building 's construction were sourced from the national government 's 2013 savings . It was not utilized within the one-year period as it took time to come up with the building 's disaster-resilient design . <p> " We placed the budget to PFDA as their trust fund so it would not be reverted to the National Treasury . Its timely disbursement has been affected by several changes of leadership in PFDA . We are happy that the new general manager pursued this project , " Albaladejo added . <p> Drawing lessons from the monster typhoon , the BFAR official said they will place all their sensitive equipment , including laboratory in the fourth and fifth floor . The second and third floors will serve as office spaces of different sections . The ground floor 's space will be used by a canteen operator and some for parking . <p> " We lost a lot of investments when ' Yolanda ' struck . We have to make sure that our equipment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disrupted during typhoons , " the official added . <p> Since late 2013 , BFAR has been renting office spaces in the city 's Marasbaras district after their two-story building was destroyed and facilities were swept by big waves . <p> The new building will rise in the same property in Diit village . The five-story building has a floor area of 5,000 square meters at 1,000 square meters per floor . ( PNA )
