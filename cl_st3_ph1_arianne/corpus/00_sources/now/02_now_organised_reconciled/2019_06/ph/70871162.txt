<h> Globe : Building more cell sites to lower energy consumption <p> MANILA -- Globe Telecom has urged the government to implement policies to ease restrictions for building cell sites across the country and lower the energy consumption of telecommunication firms and consumers . <p> " We understand that site density is a hard problem to solve and is not entirely within our control . But this is a message from a public policy perspective that we are constantly giving our government to allow us to build more cell sites faster to the same degree as Vietnam who has 70,000 towers already as compared to the Philippines ' barely 23,000 , " Globe chief technology and information officer Gil Genio said during the Globe Power Summit 2019 held in Bonifacio Global City , Taguig on Friday . <p> Genio said building more cell sites would enable telco firms , as well as their customers to save on power consumption . <p> " The reality in the Philippines is that the more dense site networks we have , the less power our cell sites will consume .
