<h> Change diet and help protect environment -- group <p> DAVAO CITY , Philippines -- A local group here has pushed for a change in diet as a means of protecting the environment and living a healthier life . <p> During the World Meat Free Week event at People 's Park , Davao City last week , Harold Banggay , a youth leader from #IAmHampaslupa and Mindanaoan Youth Development Center said the ever high demand for meat consumption is responsible for a quarter of all greenhouse gas emissions that cause climate change . <p> " Many of us still have not looked into what we are eating and how it contributes to damaging our planet . We need to challenge ourselves to ditch our fast-food culture , eat less meat and more locally and ecologically grown fruits , vegetables , and root crops , " he said . <p> In a report by Greenpeace , which is also a partner of the said event , " our food system is currently responsible for a quarter of all greenhouse gas emissions that cause climate change .
