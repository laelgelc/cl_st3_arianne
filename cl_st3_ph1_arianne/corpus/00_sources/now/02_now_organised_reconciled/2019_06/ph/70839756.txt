We 'll just vote Yes to radical proposals . No more talk , " Locsin said in a tweet . <p> Locsin 's statement came in an apparent response to UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres ' warning that countries are not living up to their commitments under the 2016 Paris Agreement to keep the global temperature rise to below two degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels , he said . <p> " Climate change is running faster than what we are ...
