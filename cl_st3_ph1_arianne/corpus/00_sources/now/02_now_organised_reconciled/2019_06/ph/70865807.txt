<h> Ilocos farmers learn adaptation strategies to beat climate change <p> LAOAG CITY -- The provincial government of Ilocos Norte , in collaboration with various national government agencies and the extension directorate of the state-run Mariano Marcos State University , continues to empower farmers and teach them practical ways to sustain crop productivity in climate vulnerable areas . <p> Following a recent farmers training in Batac City attended by some 40 farmers representing at least four organizations in the towns of Solsona , Marcos , Vintar , and Piddig , MMSU president Dr. Shirley Agrupis urged farmers to adapt to the current challenges posed by climate change . <p> " You are the backbone of the economy . Without you , no one will feed the nation . I hope that you are ready to prevent if not mitigate the impact of climate change , " Agrupis said in an interview Wednesday . <p> Through the establishment of Science and Technology Community-Based Farm ( STCBF ) on Climate Resilient Technologies , farmers groups are being trained at the MMSU-University Training Center to improve their farming practices across the province . <p> Personnel of the MMSU extension directorate are popularizing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to farmers . <p> A carrageenan plant growth regulator ( CPGR ) has recently been found to increase rice yield up at an average of 25 percent to 30 percent . <p> Carrageenan is an indigestible polysaccharide ( carbohydrate ) from red seaweeds , and is further degraded through modified irradiation technology that elucidates growth promotion in plants . <p> Studies conducted in the different areas of the country show enhanced yield even at low concentrations , making it a very effective organic fertilizer . <p> According to farmers who tried the technology , it is a great help especially to those who have limited financial capability . <p> " Immadu ti apitko idi nagusarak iti organiko nga abuno ken ti panangyaplayko kadagiti nasursurok iti panagsanay .
