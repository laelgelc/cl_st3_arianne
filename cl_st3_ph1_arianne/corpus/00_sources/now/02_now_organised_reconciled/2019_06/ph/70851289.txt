<h> Youth groups launch ' Climate Shorts ' <p> ENVIRONMENTAL youth groups in Negros Occidental led by Youth for Climate Hope have launched a program geared towards raising consciousness and public action on the effects of climate change . <p> Dubbed " Climate Shorts , " the program launched last week is composed of various activities like short talks , short films and short performances , among others . <p> Also forming part of the activities is the commemoration of the World Environment Day at the Provincial Capitol 's Social Hall in Bacolod City on Thursday , June 6 . <p> Themed " Beat Air Pollution , " the celebration is a means of calling on today 's generation to be mindful of their everyday actions and to explore renewable energy . <p> Considered as the receiving end of the climate breakdown , this generation is also urged to adhere and strengthen the implementation of Republic Act 8749 or the Clean Air Act especially in the local level . <p> Provincial Environment Management
