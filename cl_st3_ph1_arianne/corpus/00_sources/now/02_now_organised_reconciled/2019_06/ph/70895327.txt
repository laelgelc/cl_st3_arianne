<h> Lost polar bear taken to Siberian zoo for treatment <h> Reuters <p> A stray polar bear is seen in the industrial city of Norilsk , Russia on June 17 , 2019 . Irina Yarinskaya , Zapolyarnaya Pravda/Reuters <p> MOSCOW , Russia -- A famished polar bear picked up this week after it wandered lost into a northern Russian city hundreds of kilometers from its Arctic habitat was taken on Friday to a zoo in the Siberian city of Krasnoyarsk for treatment . <p> The 2-year-old female polar had appeared , starving and exhausted , in the northern nickel-producing city of Norilsk on Sunday . <p> It then became ill and almost died after scavenging for food on a rubbish heap in Norilsk and urgently needed treatment , oil company Rosneft which works in the region said in a statement . <p> The company said it had taken part in the operation to transport the bear from Norilsk to Krasnoyarsk . <p> " The bear is receiving all necessary treatment and healthy food , " it said . <p> Climate change has been damaging polar bears ' sea-ice habitats and forced them to scavenge more for food on land @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> It was unclear how the animal had ended up in Norilsk but Oleg Krashevsky , a local wildlife expert who saw the polar bear in Norilsk , said it was possible it had simply got lost . He said the bear 's eyes were watery and that evidently it could not see well . <p> Krashevsky said it was unclear what would be done with the polar bear as it looked too weak to be taken back to its natural habitat .
