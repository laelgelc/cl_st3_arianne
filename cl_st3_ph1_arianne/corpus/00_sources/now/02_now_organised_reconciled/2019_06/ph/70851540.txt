<h> Rethinking water management to address the water crisis <p> TODAY , more than half the world 's population live in cities , and in Asia we are expecting unprecedented growth . By 2030 , megacities of 10 million inhabitants or more will be located primarily in Asia . Manila , in particular , will be the fourth largest megacity in the world in terms of population . <p> As epicenters of human activity , cities see intense production and consumption , depleting huge quantities of our limited natural resources while contributing to climate change . One of the most critical resources under mounting stress in urban cities is water . <p> Recently , Manila experienced its worst water crisis in nearly a decade . More than six million people were affected when water supply by service provider Manila Water was cut off suddenly , aggravated by limited infrastructure as well as shrinking dam supplies following the El Ni ? o dry spell . <p> The crisis comes as a stark reminder that we need to treat water security as a top priority .
