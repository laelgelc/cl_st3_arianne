<p> Geothermal leader Energy Development Corporation ( EDC ) engaged more than 700 Negrense youth once again on what it takes to have a greener future through the rerun of its GeoSkwela in Luce Auditorium in Silliman University last Monday , June 24 , 2019 . <p> GeoSkwela is a pioneering campus event that aims to bring the youth closer to experts , academicians and inspirational figures who share the advocacy of cleaner energy and sustainability . It aims to continuously stimulate awareness , dialogue , and exchange of ideas on this topic in line with Negros Oriental 's commitment to cleaner energy sources , primarily geothermal . <p> These individuals served as mentors whose life and career experiences proved that sustainability is not only feasible or possible , but a way of life that is truly rewarding -- not just for one 's self , but for others and the entire community . <p> The event kicked off Silliman University 's academic calendar and included Rhyn Esolana , a third year Foreign Affairs student from Silliman University , as one of the speakers . He has served as the Vice Chairperson for the Environment of the Silliman University @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and biodiversity protection . He is cofounder of the student-led Project Balod marine conservation workshop . <p> EDC was represented by Miguel Lorenzo De Vera , head of its Strategic Initiatives , Legal , and Regulatory Office ( SILRO ) , who imparted valuable information on geothermal and the benefits of this renewable energy source , which is touted as the cleanest , most stable , and the energy of the future . <p> Dr. Jorge Emmanuel , an adjunct professor in the Institute of Environmental and Marine Sciences and in the College of Engineering and Design at Silliman University and a " Balik Scientist " of the Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) shared the wealth of information and knowledge that he had gained as a globally educated and experienced expert in the fields of climate change mitigation and disaster risk reduction , renewable energy , ecological solid waste management , and plastic pollution in the environment . <p> GeoSkwela 's special guest was Miss Earth 2014 Jamie Herrell who related her personal and professional journey during her reign as an international beauty title holder representing pro-environment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ influence and available platforms for communication to continuously engage the public for environmental causes . <p> The four speakers also shared what the youth can do as individuals to be able to contribute to the protection of the environment and how they can take baby steps in living a sustainable lifestyle . <p> " With initiatives such as GeoSkwela , sustainability and environmental advocacy are more effectively entrenched in the grassroots level of academic communities and student bodies -- ensuring that a greener and more stable energy future is a brighter prospect today more than ever , " said Norreen G. Bautista , head of EDC 's Corporate Social Responsibility Group in Negros Island . <p> EDC is the country 's premier renewable energy company and one of the world 's largest geothermal producers . Its geothermal facilities in Valencia , Negros Oriental has been providing clean , renewable , reliable power 24/7 to Negros Island and other parts of the Visayas region for over 36 years .
