<p> CITY OF SAN FERNANDO , Pampanga -- The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) 3 ( Central Luzon ) has called on the public to join its " carless days " campaign in observance of Environment Month this June . <p> " We encourage the public to join us in going carless on June 6 , 19 and 26 . This small sacrifice on our part equals a big impact on the environment as it will reduce emissions and at the same time , help us save on fuel , " DENR-3 Deputy Director Arthur Salazar said during the kickoff of the Environment Month at the Government Center here Monday . <p> Salazar said the DENR has conceptualized the " carless days " campaign to raise public awareness on the adverse effects of climate change . <p> He said harmful gas emissions from vehicles are among the biggest contributors to climate change . <p> " Clean air is our shared and collected responsibility and with over 1.3 million registered motor vehicles in Region 3 alone , you can just imagine how much we contribute to air pollution and how much it affects our health and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ personal vehicles , Salazar asked the public to instead use public transportation , bicycles and other eco-friendly means , or carpool . <p> Meanwhile , the event was also marked by the setting up of a one-stop shop for the processing of environmental requirements by the Environmental Management Bureau . <p> In 1988 , then president Corazon Aquino signed Presidential Proclamation 237 , declaring the month of June as Philippine Environment Month .
