<h> EU organizes stargazing , telescope viewing at SM Aura <p> The European Union ( EU ) Delegation to the Philippines will organize on Monday ( June 10 ) a stargazing and public telescope viewing event as part of its year-long celebration of the Viva Europa 2019 cultural showcase . <p> Telescopes and other astronomy gadgets will be assembled to illustrate and identify the most prominent constellations , and to observe the Moon and the brightest celestial objects that can be seen in the heavily light-polluted evening sky of Manila . <p> Astronomers from both Europe and the Philippines , such as Giovanni Seritella of the EU Delegation to the Philippines , Professor Jun Cajigal of the Philippine Astronomical Society , and Christopher Lu and Ralph Tiburdo of the Manila Street Astronomers Society , will lead the discussion about the wonders of the Universe .
