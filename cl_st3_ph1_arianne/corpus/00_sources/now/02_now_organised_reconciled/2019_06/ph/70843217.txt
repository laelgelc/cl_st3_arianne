( Photo from PCAARD website ) <p> TACLOBAN CITY -- The Leyte provincial government has encouraged farmers to plant bamboo , citing its huge demand in the global market and its role in mitigating climate change impact . <p> Leyte Governor Leopoldo Dominico Petilla told reporters on Thursday that there are vast idle lands in Leyte that can be tapped for bamboo production to enhance poor farmers ' income . <p> Petilla said that about 7,000 hectares along the Binahaan River alone are suitable for bamboo planting . The river , which traverses the towns of Pastrana , Dagami , Tanauan , and Palo is a major source of water for Tacloban City and nearby towns . <p> " Aside from economic benefits , bamboos are good to prevent soil erosions along rivers and deterrent to landslides especially in hilly areas , " Petilla added . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bamboo-planting and processing to be able to produce internationally-accepted products . <p> " We are heading in that direction , but we have to produce first before we market our province .
