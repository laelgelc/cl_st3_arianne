<p> MANILA , Philippines -- The Philippines has topped the list of countries facing the highest risk of climate change hazards , according to the latest Global Peace Index ( GPI ) released by Australia-based independent think tank Institute for Economics and Peace ( IEP ) . <p> Citing data from the Index for Risk Management , the IEP said the Philippines scored the highest in terms of risk of multiple climate change hazards such as floods , cyclones and droughts . <p> It noted that 47 percent of the population live in areas with high exposure to climate hazards . <p> Following the Philippines in terms of risk due to multiple hazards are Japan , Bangladesh , Myanmar , China , Indonesia , India , Vietnam and Pakistan . <p> Meanwhile , the Philippines ranked seventh in terms of risk to single natural hazard , after Vietnam , Somalia , Japan , Bangladesh , Myanmar and Indonesia . <p> For the first time , the IEP assessed the impact of climate change to overall peace worldwide
