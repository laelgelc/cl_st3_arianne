<h> Pre-SONA forums make comeback next month <p> President Rodrigo R. Duterte delivers his third state of the nation address ( SONA ) on July 23 , 2018 . ( File photo ) <p> MANILA -- After its kickoff last year , the Office of the Cabinet Secretary and the Presidential Communications Operations Office ( PCOO ) are set to bring back a series of forums to discuss the achievements during President Rodrigo Duterte 's third year in office , prior to his State of the Nation Address ( SONA ) on July 22 . <p> Presidential Spokesperson Salvador Panelo said Cabinet Secretary Karlo Nograles updated the Cabinet with the pre-SONA activities of the Cabinet clusters . <p> Nograles presented these pre-SONA activities during Monday night 's Cabinet meeting which was presided by Duterte , according to Panelo . <p> He said there will be three Pre-SONA forums to be spearheaded by the PCOO . <p> The first forum entitled " Patuloy na Pag-unlad " will be led by the Economic Development and Infrastructure Cluster in Manila on July 1 . <p> The second one , " Patuloy na Malasakit at Pag-kakaisa " will be led by the Human @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Governance Cluster , in Cebu City on July 10 . <p> Finally , the " Patuloy na Katatagan " will be presented by the Climate Change Adaptation , Mitigation and Disaster Risk Reduction Cluster and the Security , Justice and Peace Cluster in Davao City on July 17 . <p> The SONA , which is delivered by the President of the Philippines every year , is a constitutional obligation , required by Article VII , Section 23 of the 1987 Constitution . <p> It is a venue that allows the President to report on the state of the country , unveil the government 's agenda for the coming year , and also propose to Congress certain legislative measures .
