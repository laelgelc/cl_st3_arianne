Without doubt , the planet is heating up at an alarming rate . <p> With this comes a louder and more urgent call for sustainable mobility -- especially from the transport sector , which has been estimated by the Paris Agreement of 2016 to contribute to one-fifth of the world 's carbon emissions . <p> The same is true in the Philippines .
