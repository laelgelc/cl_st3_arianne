Read More <p> AHMEDABAD : Even as scientists today link the severity of cyclones or hurricanes to climate change , Gujarat has had an unusual tryst with hurricanes . Between 1970s and now , Gujarat has been hit by 11 cyclonic storms . Two of these tropical cyclones -- in 1981 and 1998 -- wrecked the state . In both cases , landfall was in Saurashtra ports : Rajpara port in Junagadh ( and also in Veraval town ) in 1981 ; and Porbandar and Kandla ports in 1998 . The storms that matched the intensity of the 1981 and 1998 cataclysms rammed the state in 1976 and 1978 with wind speeds of 167kmph , according to the Union government 's directorate of economics and statistics . Cyclones start when frenzied high-speed winds and moisture swirl around low-pressure air pockets above hot ocean waters .
