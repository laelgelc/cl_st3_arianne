<h> Adani gets final nod to start construction of controversial coal mine in Australia <h> First acquired by Adani in 2010 , the project is slated to produce 8-10 million tonnes of thermal coal a year and cost up to $1.5 billion , but has been mired in court battles and opposition from green groups <p> India 's Adani Enterprises on Thursday received the go-ahead to start construction of a controversial coal mine in outback Australia , after a state government approved a final permit on ground water management . The Carmichael mine has been a lightning rod for climate change concerns in Australia , and was seen as a factor in the surprise return to power of the conservative Liberal/National coalition in a national election in May . <p> First acquired by Adani in 2010 , the project is slated to produce 8-10 million tonnes of thermal coal a year and cost up to $1.5 billion , but has been mired in court battles and opposition from green groups .
