<h> Residents of U.S. pig farming state kick up a stink over property rights <p> WASHINGTON , June 19 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - From not being able to hang laundry outside or have friends over for a barbeque to health problems , residents of the U.S. state of North Carolina are having their lives disrupted by large industrial pig farms , said a lawsuit filed on Wednesday . <p> Communities in eastern North Carolina , the United States ' second-largest pork producer and one of the densest hog farming areas in the world , have for years complained about the smell and pollution caused by the animals ' faecal waste . <p> On Wednesday , environmentalists and community groups filed a lawsuit seeking to overturn those laws by arguing they violated property rights and the state 's constitution . <p> " How is it that the state can take away my community 's ability to protect our homes and health ? " said Devon Hall , co-founder of the Rural Empowerment Association for Community Help , one of the plaintiffs . <p> A spokeswoman for the North Carolina attorney general declined to comment on the lawsuit , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hog producer in the area . <p> Residents say the stench can be so bad they ca n't hang their laundry outside or have friends over for barbecues , advocates told the Thomson Reuters Foundation . <p> " All of these things ... really result in severely diminished enjoyment of one 's property , " said Will Hendrick , a staff attorney with Waterkeeper Alliance , an advocacy group , and party to the suit . <p> " It 's long been considered that a man 's home is his castle , but this waste is breaching the castle .
