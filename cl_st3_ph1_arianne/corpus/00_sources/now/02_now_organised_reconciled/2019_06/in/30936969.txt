Whether the houses are laid vertically or horizontally , these neighbourhood communities are registered under cooperative laws or as civil associations . <p> RWAs are typically registered under the Societies Registration Act , 1860 . RWAs are the finest expression of civil society consensus ; an answer to urban problems ; a tool for community building and for self-management and direct democracy . <p> An urban resident is endowed with intensive communication skills , fast transport and uninhibited relations with other human beings .
