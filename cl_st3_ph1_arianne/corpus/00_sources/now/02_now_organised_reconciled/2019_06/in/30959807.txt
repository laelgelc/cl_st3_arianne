The spring , dedicated to Goddess Kheer Bhawani , has an irregular septagonal shape with its apex called Pad ( feet ) to the East . The northern and the southern sides are longer than the western side which is called Shir ( Head ) . In the centre of the holy spring where once stood a mulberry tree , there is one marble temple which enshrines some idols found at the time of cleansing the spring . <p> There are many interesting stories related to this festival . The pandit was guided by a snake through the swampy and marshy land , until he reached the hollow trunk of a mulberry tree . <p> The pandit took the clue and after performing Puja poured milk which he had brought for this purpose .
