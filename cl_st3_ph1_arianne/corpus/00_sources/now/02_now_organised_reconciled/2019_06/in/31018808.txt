<h> Patna HC : Felling of trees is equivalent to murder <p> PATNA : The Patna high court on Tuesday observed that felling of trees for widening of roads was equivalent to murder . A bench of Justices Jyoti Saran and Partha Sarthy was hearing a PIL filed by one Gaurav Kumar Singh , who had urged the court to direct the authorities concerned to take up plantation and not to fell trees , especially for widening roads . The petitioner has annexed a TOI report , which referred to a study that roadside trees help reduce 40% pollution and heat and if the trees are more than ten years old , their effectiveness increases up to 70% . A few reports published in vernacular dailies over recent felling of trees in Patna for development work have also been annexed . Justice Saran observed that felling of trees was like committing a murder and the contractors were pouring chemicals on the roots to kill the trees overnight and fell them with due permission on the plea that they were dead . The court now has asked the government to submit details of the action plan for plantation with count of trees on or before the next hearing on Friday .
