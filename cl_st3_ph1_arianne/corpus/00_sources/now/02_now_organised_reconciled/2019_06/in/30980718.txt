<h> MP CM for early clearance of projects <p> Bhopal , June 15 ( UNI ) Madhya Pradesh Chief Minister Kamal Nath on Saturday suggested to the NITI Aayog that , for public utility projects , approval may be granted by the state dispensation under the Forest ( Conservation ) Act . Citing the reason , he averred , " As a result of Madhya Pradesh 's untiring endeavours ; the state boasts approximately 95,000 sq km of jungles -- the highest figure in the country . Looking at the vastness , it is challenging to execute developmental activities ; which require multiple permissions and sanctions that either delay or halt the evolution processes . Every year about 125 applications are submitted online under the Act . These include projects from key sectors such as irrigation , roads , mining , transmission , potable water , etc .
