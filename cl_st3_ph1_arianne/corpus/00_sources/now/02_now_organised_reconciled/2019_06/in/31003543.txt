<h> Mizoram : Forest area constitutes 86.27 % of total geographical area <p> The total forest cover of Mizoram is 18,186 sq.km , which is 86.27 per cent of the state 's geographical area , according to Mizoram Environment , Forest and Climate Change minister T. J. Lalnuntluanga . He was quoting the Forest Survey of India . <p> Replying to a question during assembly session here on Friday , Lalnuntluanga said that the state covers an area of 21,081 sq.km , constituting 0.64 per cent of geographical area of the country . He also said that , as per Forest Survey of India , which is conducted after every two years , the percentage of forest cover or densely forest area is decreasing gradually . <p> In terms of forest canopy density classes , Mizoram has 131 sq.km under very dense forest , 5,861 sq.km under moderately dense forest and 12,194 sq.km under open forest . <p> The minister them informed the assembly that bamboo plantation is currently being undertaken in 38,601 hectare of lands in 458 locations within the jurisdiction of 11 forest divisions and three autonomous district councils . He also said that the government does @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plantation near Aizawl . He added that a meager of Rs 24,000 revenue has been collected from the bamboo plantation . Lalnuntluanga also said that the state government would look into bamboo research centre at Aizawl 's Bethlehem locality . <p> Intervening in the discussion , Chief Minister Zoramthanga said the state government is taking massive effort to cultivate as much bamboo as possible under the National Bamboo Mission . He also said that bamboo plantation is one of the flagship programmes of the state government . <p> Zoramthanga then said that the state government focuses on plantation of two selected bamboo species and asked a company to send at least 10 lakh saplings in a month . The Chief Minister then expressed hope that bamboo plantation will generate employment opportunities for many people .
