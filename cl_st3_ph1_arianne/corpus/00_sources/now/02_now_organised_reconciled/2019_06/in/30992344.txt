<h> Services <h> Over 11-quintal polythene bags seized from wholesalers <p> TNN Updated : Jun 19 , 2019 , 7:07 IST <p> Ludhiana : Tightening the noose around those selling polythene bags , the municipal corporation along with the Punjab Pollution Control Board ( PPCB ) conducted a joint raid in three markets of the city on Tuesday . Around 11 quintals of polythene bags were seized from wholesalers in Field Ganj , vegetable market near Jalandhar Bypass and Kesar Ganj Mandi . From Field Ganj alone , 8.5 quintals of the banned bags were seized . The raiding teams got together at MC Zone D office at 10am . A team led by MC sanitary inspector Ashwani Sahota went to the vegetable market near Jalandhar Bypass . Another team led by MC medical officer Dr Jasvir Kaur visited Kesar Ganj Mandi . Both the officers jointly recovered over 2 quintals of polybags . The third team , under the leadership of MC medical officer Dr Vipul Malhotra , visited kutcha number 16 in Field Ganj from where 8.5 quintals of polythene bags were seized .
