For years now we have lived on the fringes of this issue ; India Premier League ( IPL ) matches have been moved because of drought , heatwaves in Australia have hospitalised Test cricketers , and last year , two hurricanes ravaged through the Caribbean , destroying cricket grounds in Anguilla and Dominica besides much else . Playing a fundraiser at Lord 's is fine , but does top flight cricket owe the environment more ? <p> After an IPL match in Pune last year , I saw piles of unsegregated but recyclable rubbish left behind .
