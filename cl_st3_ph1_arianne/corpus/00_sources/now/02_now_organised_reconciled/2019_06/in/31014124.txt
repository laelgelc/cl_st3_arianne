<h> Odisha signed a note of Technical cooperation with Government of Germany for implementation of Sustainable Urban Development -- Smart City <p> Bhubaneswar : Government of Odisha has signed a note of Technical cooperation with Government of Germany for implementation of Sustainable Urban Development -- Smart City , Climate Smart Cities and Adoption for Climate change in cities . <p> The objectives of the above projects are to improvise the process of planning and implementing sustainable and integrated urban development solutions , to develop programmes and policies for climate resilient turban systems and to develop ICT based solutions for ease of governance . <p> The projects will be developed and apply concepts for sustainable urban development regarding the provisioning of urban basic services and housing in three selected smart cities i.e Bhubaneswar ( Odisha ) . Kochi ( Kerala ) and Coimbatore ( Tamil Nadu ) . <p> The German contribution through all three projects combined is 16.4 million EUR ( INR 127 Crores approximately ) and the projects are expected to run until December 2021 . <p> Under the Indo-German Development Cooperation , the projects aim to support mission JAAGA and BASUDHA in capacity building of various stakeholders @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ aims to develop ICT based tool for adaptation to urban flooding scenario in Bhubaneswar by January 2020 .
