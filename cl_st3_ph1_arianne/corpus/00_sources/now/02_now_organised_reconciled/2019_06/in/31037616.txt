<h> Heavy rain showers likely in Mumbai today <p> MUMBAI : Mumbaikars are likely to witness heavy rain showers in the city on Monday , predicts IMD . With heavy rains for the third consecutive day on Sunday , Mumbai surpassed its required June rainfall by recording 515.1mm of rain -- 10.1mm above normal . According to India Meteorological Department ( IMD ) data , nearly 64.9% ( 327.7mm ) of rain required in the month of June ( 505mm ) was received in the last three days . But , experts warned , averaging monthly rainfall by using data of only two to three downpour days could be misleading . <p> " Any crop pattern used by farmers in our country is not dependent on one or two days of rain , but rainfall received over a period of time . So , such averaging done sometimes could be very misleading , " said Dr Rakesh Kumar , scientist and director , National Environment Engineering Research Institute ( NEERI ) . " Akshay Deoras , meteorologist and PhD researcher at department of Meteorology , University of Reading , UK , concurred that the total rainfall received figures only look good statistically . " Prior to Friday 's showers , the rain deficit for Mumbai was too high ( over 200mm ) , " he said . " But then owing to the kind of rain the city received , the rainfall accumulation was high and this is not good in terms of impact these events create . Every city has its tolerance limit and even the ground has a certain capacity it can hold . This kind of rainfall is also very dangerous for agriculture as everything gets washed of . Instead we need proper rain distribution . " Since monsoon onset on Tuesday , Mumbai received its first heavy showers on Friday .
