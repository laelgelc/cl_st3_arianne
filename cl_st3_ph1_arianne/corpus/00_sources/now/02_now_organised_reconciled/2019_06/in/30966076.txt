Appropriate to the diffused geopolitical landscape is a policy which enables promotion of India 's interests through multiple circles of engagement . The old East-West , North-South divides have become progressively less and less relevant though they still influence our foreign policy perspective . We will have to actively seek partnerships which are more issue based and hold the promise of advancing India 's economic and security interests . In this sense , some partnerships will be more valued than others but need not be exclusive . <p> India 's relationship with the US will carry greater weight than relations with China which remains an adversarial power and is unwilling to change this equation .
