<h> On a maiden run <p> After practising parkour for six months , Bharatanatyam dancer Deekshita Shetty aspires to amalgamate parkour moves with the classical dance style that she has been learning for 13 years . She is especially interested in incorporating the " flip " into Bharatanatyam , but is yet to master the acrobatic manoeuvre from parkour . The sport , which is also known as freerunning , is famous for such gravitydefying stunts . <p> In her endeavour to merge the two disciplines , Shetty , 19 , is fortunate to have found a mentor in Deepti Sinha , who at 30 , is one of the country 's first female parkour athletes . Sinha got into parkour four years ago . She was introduced to the sport by the Mumbaibased freerunning pioneer , Deepak Mali . " At the time , I was the only girl in Deepak 's parkour group , Make Your Own Way ( MYOW ) , " says the IT professional , who has since been featured in commercials starring Bollywood celebs such as Deepika Padukone . <p> Sinha would accompany the MYOW boys on their weekly freerunning sessions at Bund @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but she did miss having a girl around in the group . " To be honest , I was not expecting a great turnout , " says Sinha . <p> On the day of the parkour jam , though , Sinha was delighted to observe that prominent female athletes such as the boxer Vaishnavi Bhujbal and the diver Hrutika Shriram had turned up .
