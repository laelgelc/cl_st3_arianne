Illegal constructions and dumping of waste imperiling East Kolkata Wetlands <p> On May 27 , 2019 , the NGT directed the East Kolkata Wetland Authority and the Bidhannagar Municipal Corporation to fence the area around the Mollar Bheri , where the corporation has been dumping its solid waste , to prevent leachate and slurry . <p> The tribunal was hearing a petition filed by environmental activist Subhas Datta , highlighting the exploitation of the East Kolkata Wetlands . A survey conducted by Datta has revealed that a large number of constructions have sprung up within the area for commercial/ industrial and other activities . These constructions are deleterious to the water body . <p> A large number of plastic reprocessing units have also been set up on the Bantala-Basanti highway .
