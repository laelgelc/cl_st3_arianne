<h> No care from government , this wetland and its avifauna seek a saviour <p> A black-necked stork was found here with a rubber ring around its beak last year <p> Just over a year ago , the rescue of a black-necked stork left an Indian audience , and the international media , absorbed . But little has changed at the spot where the bird was found , amid a mound of garbage in the neglected Basai wetland . On June 9 last year , TOI carried a report , ' Plastic bomb in Gurugram wetland ' , highlighting dumping of plastic trash in the wetland , and how waste material got entangled around the beak of the stork . The state environment minister had to intervene to rid the wetland of the plastic scrapheap . However , far from being saved , this habitat and its fauna continue to face threats -- not just from illegal dumping , but from a construction and demolition ( C&amp;D ) plant coming up in the area . TOI visited the spot recently and found that mixed waste , including plastic , rubber and C&amp;D debris , is being dumped in this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , exposing the government 's ineffectiveness . As per the 2031 Master Plan and revenue records , land in Basai is marked for public utility ( sectors 100 and 110 ) and for agricultural purposes . But although over 300 species of birds visit Basai every year , the state government has refused to give this marshy expanse the status of wetland . Birders blame the powers-that-be for this sorry state of affairs . " Despite that waste dumping proved hazardous for the black-necked stork last year , the authorities have n't learnt any lesson , " lamented Pankaj Gupta , a birder from the Delhi Bird Foundation , an NGO . " Fisheries have already destroyed the area , now illegal garbage dumping has made the situation worse , " he added . In 2016 , this NGO filed a petition in the National Green Tribunal , seeking a stay on the setting up of a C&amp;D treatment plant by the MCG . The case , however , was disposed of last year . But the court directed MCG to ensure that construction work start only after requisite permissions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Construction and Demolition Waste Management Rules , 2016 . Others , meanwhile , despair of the lack of protection given to such ecologically sensitive zones . " Unfortunately , most wetlands in the city have turned into wastelands , and the poor birds are becoming victims , " rues birdwatcher Anandita . " The only way forward is to notify wetlands , to prevent illegal dumping of waste . " Further , despite the Coimbatore-based Salim Ali Centre for Ornithology and Natural History ( SACON ) advising that the wetland be declared a Ramsar site , no action has been taken by the state in this regard . SACON , in fact , had conducted a study , ' Inland Wetlands of India : Conservation Priorities ' , for the ministry of environment , forests and climate change , in collaboration with UNDP India . The ensuing report stated that the wetland in Basai has high biodiversity value and thus can be declared as a ' conservation area ' or ' community reserve ' . A Ramsar site , incidentally , is a wetland deemed to be of international importance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , an intergovernmental environmental treaty instituted by UNESCO in 1971 , which came into effect in 1975 . Sources in the forest department , meanwhile , say the authorities had come under tremendous pressure to safeguard the wetland , following the episode involving the black-necked stork . " There was a lot of talk ( around ) protection of Basai , as only the Haryana government can give status of wetland to Basai . And , as the land belongs to MCG , the corporation has to give green signal for the wetland status , " said a source , adding , " However , it seems that the authorities have forgotten about it , despite that senior officials in the department having taken it up with the higher authorities and MCG . " <p> Basai wetland is home to around 280 species of birds , including several species covered under the Convention of Migratory Species , among them the greater spotted eagle , eastern imperial eagle , egyptian vulture , ferruginous duck and marbled duck . Before it 's too late .
