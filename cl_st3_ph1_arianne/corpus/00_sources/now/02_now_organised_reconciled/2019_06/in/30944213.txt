Long-term exposure to polluted air is known to cause lung cancer , heart disease , stroke and diabetes . Breathing problems like asthma have surged in recent years , and about 350,000 new cases of asthma are reported among children each year in the country . It is no surprise that on average , a child born today in South Asia will lose two and a half years of its lifespan because of the impact of air pollution on health . <p> The 5th of June each year is observed as the World Environment Day for " encouraging worldwide awareness and action to protect our environment " .
