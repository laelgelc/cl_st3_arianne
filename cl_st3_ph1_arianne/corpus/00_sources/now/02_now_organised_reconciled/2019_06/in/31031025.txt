<h> Services <h> Modi , Xi and Putin also discuss climate <p> TNN &amp; Agencies Updated : Jun 29 , 2019 , 4:53 IST <p> DELHI/OSAKA : Prime Minister Narendra Modi sought to keep the focus on terrorism when he met met Chinese President Xi Jinping and his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin . The there leaders discussed key issues including counter-terrorism and climate change . Modi sought help from Xi and Putin in organising a global conference on terrorism . The three countries , in a joint communique of the 16th Meeting of the foreign ministers of Russia , India and China , strongly condemned terrorism in all its forms and manifestations . In his opening remarks here , Prime Minister Modi said the trilateral meeting in Osaka is a useful medium of discussion and coordination on major global issues . The three leaders met in Argentina at the summit level last year . " As the world 's leading economies , the exchange of views between us on the economic , political and security situation of the world is important . Our trilateral meeting today is a useful medium of discussion and coordination on major global issues . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in February , views were discussed on many issues . These include cooperation on issues of counter-terrorism , international hot-spot , reformed multi-lateralism , climate change under RIC , " Modi said . <p> " Together addressing global challenges . PM Narendra Modi hosted Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping for an informal ' RIC ' meeting in Osaka . Discussed counter-terrorism , international hot-spot issues , reformed multi-lateralism and climate change , " MEA spokesperson Raveesh Kumar tweeted . In February , China and Russia agreed for a closer policy coordination to eradicate the " breeding grounds of terrorism " , in a significant gain for India in its push to isolate Pakistan internationally on the issue of terrorism post-Pulwama attack in which 40 CRPF personnel were killed by a suicide bomber of Pakistan-based Jaish-e-Mohammed.
