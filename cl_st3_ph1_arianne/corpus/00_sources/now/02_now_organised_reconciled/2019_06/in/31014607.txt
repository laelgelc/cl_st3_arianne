<h> Services <h> Thakur invites Dubai-based group to invest <p> TNN Updated : Jun 25 , 2019 , 13:25 IST <p> Representative image <p> SHIMLA : Chief minister Jai Ram Thakur has invited Dubai-based Lulu group to invest in shopping malls and hypermarkets in tourist destinations of Himachal Pradesh like Shimla , Manali , Dharamshala and other industrial hubs of the state . <p> Thakur-led delegation held a meeting with M A Yusuf Ali , chairman and managing director of LuLu International on Sunday evening and discussed investment opportunities in tourism and hospitality sectors . Delegation also called on minister of climate change and environment of UAE , Dr Thani Al Zeyoudi , on Monday and discussed possible areas of collaboration , particularly in tourism and real estate . The team had also invited Ali and Al Zeyoudi to the global investors meet scheduled for November this year . Industries minister Bikram Singh assured help and support from the government . Ali saidhis group would explore possibilities in fruit processing and procurement and small shopping malls according to the demand of the state .
