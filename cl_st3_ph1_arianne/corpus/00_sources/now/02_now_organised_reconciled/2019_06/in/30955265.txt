<h> Importance of green environment highlighted by Sonitpur district administration and organizations <p> June 8 , 2019 <p> A Correspondent <p> Tezpur : In a move to raise the global awareness about the importance of the healthy and green environment in the human lives and mark the World Environment Day , Sonitpurdistrict administration along with different organizations and nature loving individual , Pollution Control Board , Tezpur in association with a hotel organized different programmes including distribution of saplings of tree plant , cleanliness drive , rally etc under Swachh Bharat Mission . <p> On the other hand , a day long programme on World Environment Day with the theme of ' Air Pollution ' was also organized by KVK , Sonitpur , Napam , at Napam Chapori village . The programme was started with an inaugural speech of Sanjukta Saikia , Subject Matter Specialist ( Plant Protection ) , who welcomed the participants and aware the farmers about the importance of plants in minimizing air pollution and purification . Saikia also emphasised the role of farming community in mitigating the effects of climate change .
