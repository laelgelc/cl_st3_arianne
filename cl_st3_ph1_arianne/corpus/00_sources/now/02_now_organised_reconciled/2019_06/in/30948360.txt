<p> Rapid industrialisation and urbanisation in the relentless pursuit of growth are causing irrevocable damage to mother earth and environment . Challenges posed by the evils of global warming and climate change are threatening the very existence of mankind . <p> India is battling a major environmental crisis of air pollution . This is because of an alarming rise in greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions due to consumption of fossil fuels in transportation sector and also brown clouding due to existing practice of burning crop residues following the harvest season . <p> Burning of gasoline and agricultural waste results in emission of harmful gases , toxic materials as well as airborne particulate matter -- all known to have ill effects on human health . Farm waste burning causes Acute Respiratory Infection ( ARI ) , especially among children below the age of five and senior citizens . <p> The National Capital Region is one such case in discussion ! According to the research , levels of airborne particulate matter in Delhi spikes 20 times over the safely threshold defined by the World Health Organization , especially during winter . <p> The impact of increasing pollution is not just @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economic health , something that citizens , economists , businessmen , politicians and bureaucrats are deeply concerned about . According to a recent study published in the International Journal of Epidemiology , the effect of exposure to burning agricultural residue has an economic cost of a whopping $30 billion annually . This cost jumps manifolds to $190 billion , which is a staggering 1.7 per cent of India 's GDP , when we add impact of burning of firecrackers , industrial and vehicular pollution . <p> World Environment Day is a shot in the arm reminder to combat issues of vehicular pollution and agricultural residue burning , on war footing so as to arrest the huge social and economic costs it entails . <p> Technology has a crucial role to play in combating seemingly intractable problems of environmental pollution . Today 's advance technologies have capabilities to convert waste into wealth in the form of bioenergy and bio-chemicals . Instead of burning agri waste , it can be used as a raw material or feedstock for the production of biofuel such as ethanol and compressed biogas ( CBG ) . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the compressed natural gas ( CNG ) . Biofuels are carbon neutral unlike fossil fuels and therefore do not contribute to the GHG emissions . <p> Smart integrated bio refineries based on the 2nd Generation ( 2G ) ethanol technology are capable of converting agricultural waste into biofuel and variety of renewable chemicals . Resultant is an overarching positive impact on society , energy , environment , and economy . <p> The movement of using agricultural waste to bioenergy and renewable chemicals that are gaining industry wide acceptance resonates well with the development of the ecosystem for sustainable circular bio-economy . <p> Circular bio-economy implies processing biological resources for value creation by way of renewable energy and products , and whilst in use , recover and regenerate products as well as materials at the end of service life . <p> The entire value chain , beginning with agricultural residue collection to biofuel conversion has the potential to create huge employment opportunities in rural areas . Thus , it can create a sustainable alternate revenue stream for farmers and rural entrepreneurship for building up requisite ecosystem . <p> Biofuels offer significant @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ savings of carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) and local pollutants from the use of blended fuel . It is projected that CO2 emissions can be reduced up to 10.41 million tonne CO2 by 2021-2022 at a 20 per cent blending rate for ethanol . This is significantly important from the point of view of India 's commitments made at the COP21 Climate Change Summit in Paris . India has committed to a target of CO2 emissions reduction by 33 per cent by 2030 with respect to the 2005 levels . <p> Successful implementation of the government 's Ethanol blending programme will not only be a major step in the direction of energy self-reliance but will also result in considerable foreign exchange savings . It may be noted that India is amongst the largest energy importers in the world and with 20 per cent ethanol blending , it can save forex of the tunes of Rs 26,000 crore . <p> New age technologies such as 2G ethanol and CBG have the potential to effectively deal with environmental challenges and make the world a better place . <p> DISCLAIMER : The views @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ necessarily subscribe to it .
