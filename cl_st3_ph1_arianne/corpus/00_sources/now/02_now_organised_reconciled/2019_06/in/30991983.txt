<h> Services <h> Mango yield in Valsad , Navsari plummets <p> TNN Updated : Jun 19 , 2019 , 11:34 IST <p> Picture used for representational purpose only <p> SURAT : Mango season is likely to get over within a fortnight . Majority of farmers are an unhappy lot because mango production this year in south Gujarat has been the poorest in the last five seasons . Mango yield in Valsad and Navsari this year is estimated to be around 55 per cent to last year 's total output in these two districts . Darshan Desai has an orchard at Talavchora village of Chikhli taluka in Navsari district . It has 5,000 mango trees . He had a yield of 500 tonnes of mangoes from his orchard last year , but this season it has been around 150 tonnes only . Sanjay Nayak , a farmer of Gandeva in Navsari district , has 2,000 mango trees in his orchard . He had a yield of 200 tonnes last year and this season , its around 85 tonnes only . " This has been one the worst seasons for mango farmers in the past five years . We know it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ few years we are not getting good yield due to climate change , " Nayak said . Dr DK Sharma , in-charge of Pariya farm of Navsari Agriculture University ( NAU ) in Valsad district , who manages a mango orchard on 60 hectare area , said , " Against last year 's yield of 12 tonnes per hectare , we had just seven tonnes this year . Alphonso variety has been the greatest disappointment . " <p> Dr Sharma said , " This is happening mainly because of climate change . Prolonged winter had delayed flowering and then the temperature required for setting of fruits remained elusive . When small fruits were set , it did n't grow due to the sudden increase in temperature . There was a situation when it looked like we will have a bumper yield , but soon the flowers withered away . " Some local farmers attributed poor mango yield largely to pollution .
