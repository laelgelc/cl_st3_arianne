<h> Thousands join IDY celebration in S Africa <p> The two big events were held at the iconic Wanderers Cricket Stadium and a university campus in the sprawling mainly Black African township of Soweto , respectively . Image Credit : ANI <p> Thousands of South Africans gathered at two separate venues here and joined in a mass yoga session , marking the International Yoga Day celebrations . Although IDY is officially celebrated on June 21 , the event here was hosted on the weekend to make it convenient for people to attend . <p> The two big events were held at the iconic Wanderers Cricket Stadium and a university campus in the sprawling mainly Black Africantownship of Soweto , respectively . At the Wanderers , Consul-General Dr K J Srinivasa said yoga has become a global event and people were adopting the ancient Indian spiritual practice with great enthusiasm . <p> " In South Africa , IDY has turned into an International Month of Yoga rather than just a day of yoga , " he said . " Since the beginning of June , schools and community organisations across the country have been hosting yoga events , most of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and this will continue over the next week at various venues , " the diplomat added . <p> Srinivasa said they are also focussing on environmental awareness and climate change this year as part of IDY . In Soweto , IDY has been celebrated since 2016 by the Art of Living Foundation and its sister organisation , the International Association for Human Values , founded by Sri Sri Ravi Shankar . <p> With the focus being on youth this year , the University of Johannesburg partnered with these organisations . IDY in Soweto included interactive drumming , praise singing and a performance by the Alpha Ministries Choir . <p> Gauteng Provincial Minister for Sport , Arts , Culture and Recreation , Mbali Hlophe , who joined IDY in Soweto , said yoga is not just an exercise but a mental experience to calm the mind .
