The disease is caused by parasites that are transmitted to people by the bite of an Anopheles mosquito . These mosquitoes thrive in regions with warm temperatures and humid conditions like high rainfall . A temperature range above 20 degrees Celsius is ideal for the growth of the parasite , below which they are not able to complete the growth cycle . <p> It is due to this reason that countries which have a humid and hot climate see more cases of malaria .
