<p> NEW DELHI : As the slow progress of the southwest monsoon continues to deepen the drought situation , President Ram Nath Kovind on Thursday urged Indians to focus on water conservation and management to tackle the crisis . <p> " Over time , the traditional and effective practices of water conservation are disappearing . Houses have come up over ponds and lakes , and the vanishing water sources have accentuated the water crisis for the poor , " said Kovind , while addressing the joint sitting of both Houses of Parliament on Thursday . <p> Highlighting that the growing water crisis is the biggest challenge in the 21st century , he added that the situation is likely to get worse due to the increasing impact of climate change and global warming . <p> Human-induced warming had already reached 1 ?
