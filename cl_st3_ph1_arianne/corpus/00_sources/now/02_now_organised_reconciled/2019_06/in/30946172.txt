<h> Committed to make Gujarat pollution-free : Chief minister Vijay Rupani <p> AHMEDABAD : Gujarat chief minister Vijay Rupani on Wednesday expressed commitment to make clean and green Gujarat free from pollution . Gujarat is the only state in the country which has created competition for environmental challenges through different climate change departments . He said that along with industries and society , the government is also committed to fight against water pollution by raising the green cover through planting 10 crore trees and by cleaning the rivers . Rupani launched the pilot project of Emission Trading Scheme to solve the problem of air-pollution in Gujarat in a programme of World Environment Day Celebration being organised at Ahmedabad . Rupani said that the state government is in compliance with the orders and instructions of the National Green Tribunal , closer notices have been issued to industries in Vapi and penal action has been taken . For the ceramic industries of Morbi , PNG has also been made available to the industries . He also added that due to the serious threat due to air pollution , the state government has also started the Deep-Sea Discharge pipeline scheme for Jetpur @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ treat contaminated water . Rupani added that Indian culture worships fire , air and water as God . They even worship trees as god and river as their mother . World 's developed countries have exploited the natural wealth and now they come to teach the lesson of pollution emancipation . On this occasion the chief minister also released the book ' National Heritage of Gujarat ' , ' Pictorial Guide of Kutch ' and ' Gaganvhiri Safari Kamdaro ' books published by Geer Foundation . Gujarat is at the forefront in industrial sector and will also lead in measures of environmental purification , he added . " Polluted water will be disposed deep into ocean through pipelines . When China 's chemical industry is moving towards Gujarat , we have many opportunities for development in this sector . It will also improve the standard of living of the people , but the state government wants to move forward by making strict laws to reduce the depletion of forest and water pollution , " he said .
