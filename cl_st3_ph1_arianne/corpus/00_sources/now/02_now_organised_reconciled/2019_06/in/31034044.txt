It is regarded as a forum comprises of the central bank and head of government from 19 countries and the European Union ( EU ) . The EU is represented by the European Central Bank and by the European Commission . <p> G20 summit was first held in Berlin in 1999 with the aim to discuss and take policies pertaining to the promotion of international financial stability .
