" It will also be sent to decision makers in governments and leading corporates across the world . The planet is beyond politics . Hundreds of star performers and students of different genres of music from the USA , UK , Brazil , India , China , Slovenia , Switzerland , Ukraine , among others have recorded.The performers who will feature in the anthem include Glen Velez , Sacramento Youth Symphony , Pete Lockett , TV Gopalakrishnan , Royal Hartigan , Chitravina N Ravikiran , Karaikkudi Mani , Kadri Gopalnath , Eduardo Pirozzi , Kala Ramnath , Tarun Bhattacharya , Kadri Gopalnath and Emilija Kercan .
