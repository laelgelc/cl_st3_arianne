During his visit to Jaisalmer on Monday , the CM visited the hatchery that was constructed in Jaisalmer for the purpose . He also spotted around 13 Great India Bustards ( GIBs ) in the Desert National Park . <p> Gehlot said , " This project will be a milestone in conserving this critically endangered bird . Out of the total population of 150 GIBs in the world , 120 birds are found in the Jaisalmer district of Rajasthan .
