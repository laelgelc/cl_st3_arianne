In one powerful moment , it did not go well . <p> Kamala Harris of California , a former prosecutor , invoked race and identity , challenging Biden to apologize for working with segregationist senators and for opposing aspects of school busing in the 1970s . <p> Biden did not apologize , and Harris then made it personal , saying she benefited from busing as a young girl in California . <p> She did tell Biden , " I do not believe you are a racist . " <p> Throughout the debate , Biden had a mostly subdued presence and did little to draw bright distinctions between his policies and those of top-tier challengers like Harris and Sen.
