Read More <p> PILIBHIT : A full-fledged programme for conservation of crane species in the country will not be possible until studies on the impact of threats to this vulnerable bird are carried out , said Asad R Rahmani , former director of the Bombay Natural History Society . Making a presentation at a workshop on crane conservation in Pilibhit Tiger Reserve headquarters on Tuesday , the avian scientist said without a study of the threat to its habitat , health and reproductive system , it is not possible to devise ways to protect the crane . " India has six of the total 15 crane species found globally . These primarily include sarus and blacked necked cranes which breed mostly in northern and central parts of the country , and two migratory species -- demoiselle and common crane -- which have a characteristic of migrating to other countries especially @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ estimated national population of around 18,000 cranes , around 60% of these birds are present in UP alone . This long legged bird has been enlisted in schedule I of the Wildlife Protection Act in view of the many threats to its habitats and survival , " Rahmani said . As cranes are deemed to be sacred birds in many countries like Magnolia , Japan , China and Germany , people avoid hunting them . The danger comes from elsewhere . The crane species usually stay in wheat and paddy fields apart from places near wetland with shallow water as food is plentiful there . But now , the excessive use of chemical fertilizers in crops and water pollution are adversely affecting them , Rahmani said . " The increase in area under sugarcane cultivation , especially in UP , is causing shrinkage of the territorial area of cranes as these birds never stay or make habitats amid long and dense crops , " the avian expert said . According to avian experts , these factors pose a threat to the survival of the crane species and called for evolving an integrated plan to carry out a scientific study to precisely measure the degree of adverse impact of these threats on the life span of cranes . " Unfortunately such a crucial study has never been conducted in India , " said Rahmani.
