The theme for the year 2019 , set by the United Nations Environment Programme is ' Air Pollution ' . <p> Reportedly , the Bhamla Foundation , a non-governmental organisation which works across several themes like health , child rehabilitation , women 's empowerment and environmental awareness has collaborated with the ministry for the song . <p> The theme song was launched by Secretary MoEFCC CK Mishra in New Delhi . The song is a call-to-action for Indian citizens to combat the environmental issues of the time .
