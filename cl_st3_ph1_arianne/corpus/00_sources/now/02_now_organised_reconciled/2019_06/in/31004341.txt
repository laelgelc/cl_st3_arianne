<p> One of the trees earmarked for a transplant on May 13 this year ( L ) and the same spot on June 15 ( R ) , displaying the callous attitude at play here <p> In 2015 , the Maharashtra State Road Development Corporation ( MSRDC ) was pinned by the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) for the criminal offence of first denying and later admitting to chopping down two fully grown trees on Nigdi-Dehu Road , for widening National Highway 4 ( NH4 ) . Four years later , the project -- being executed by the National Highways Authority of India ( NHAI ) -- has once again claimed three more trees earmarked for transplant , thanks to a " miscommunication " between the authorities and the axe-happy contractor . <p> In the present phase of the roadwidening initiative , 87 trees are scheduled to be felled and another 18 to be transplanted . Despite the disrepute Pune Municipal Corporation ( PMC ) earned with its botched-up transplant efforts at the Salim Ali Bird Sanctuary , its officials managed to allow a contractor to chop down trees that were to be transplanted .
