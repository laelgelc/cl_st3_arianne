The award recognises local and indigenous community initiatives . Sunandamma used to walk more than 5-6 kilometers everyday from the village as there was no transport facility as there was no live hood in the village outskirts . Being a fearless woman , Sunandamma all alone used to walk for longer distances to gathers people and explained in the village how land gets back its fertility . <p> " I have no fear to work , I can walk many more miles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fields , I was rich with unworthy land before but am rich now with a lot of trees and crops , " says Sunandamma . She transformed land of rocks , hard soil and waste into the agricultural fields . " Challenges comes in life , I had a very worst part , you would have understood by listening that , the land for which i worked is a rocky land filled with stones , nothing can stop us if we are confident to face life , " said Sunandamma . <p> Recognised for community seed collection <p> Anjamma a nationally honoured community seed keeper from Gangwar is well-known for saving and conserving nearly 100 varieties of traditional seeds and has been a community seed keeper for over 25 years and a sterling example of how an ecologically conscious woman can transcend her marginalization , gender and economic deprivations to become a role model for this modern generations . <p> " We had no land , used to work for daily wages and we lived in a hut . With extreme hard work we bought 2 acres of fallow land @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ millets , overcoming all difficulties . We also went for daily work , " says Anjammma . With the Help of Deccan Development Society ( DDS ) Anjamma mastered the skill of choosing the right seed during harvesting , and to treasure it for future use . She now has an ample store of these seeds , which she supplies to the Sangham members . " While harvesting we collected some good seeds checking grain by grain and stored . <p> Slowly I bought a bullock , a cow , a goat , a buffalo and a cart . I used the one bullock of ours and one of my brothers , and ploughed the land . With the grace of God and good rain , our hard work paid off . This was made possible by the DDS Sangham , it helped in doing this easily , and today we are able to bring back the lost seeds . Kanakamma has been living in Machnoor village for over 25 years . " Though hailing from a poor family , I managed to be stable in life by adopting changes every day , with the guidance of the DDS . <p> My life turned in a good way and from the stage of nothing to stage of something with a positive and good recognition in the villages , my life has seen a lot of transformation .
