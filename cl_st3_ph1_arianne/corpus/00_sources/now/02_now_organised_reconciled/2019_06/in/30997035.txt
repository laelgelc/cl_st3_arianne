The people of India gave a clear mandate . The government is working for ' Sabka Saath , Sabka Vikas ' . " <p> President Kovind said the public has given a clear mandate in the 2019 Lok Sabha elections . " In the last 5 years , citizens were convinced the government is for them , " he said . <p> He also said that the Narendra Modi government is moving ahead to create a strong , secure and inclusive India .
