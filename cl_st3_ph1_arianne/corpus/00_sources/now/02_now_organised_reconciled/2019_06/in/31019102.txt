<h> Virar-Alibag corridor to eat up 424 hectares of forest <p> Among the four alternatives shortlisted , the MMRDA has chosen the third one ( yellow ) , which it says will cause ' least ' damage <p> Forest cover in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region ( MMR ) is once again set to become the victim of infrastructure development . Construction of the much-awaited Virar Alibag Multimodal Corridor road ( MMC ) will eat into forest cover admeasuring 424 hectares and a proposal in this regard is under consideration with the Ministry of Environment and Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) . <p> On March 24 , the MMRDA submitted two proposals related to the MMC road project between Navghar in Palghar district to Balavali in Raigad district to the MoEF&amp;CC for securing a wildlife clearance . <p> The proposal has stated that 424.68 hectares of forest cover will have to make way for the MMC alignment project .
