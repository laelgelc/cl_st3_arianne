<p> Nagarkurnool : As there are no minor fruit-bearing trees left in their habitat , monkeys are setting themselves onto agricultural and horticultural crops in villages , giving farmers sleepless nights . While driving them away has become a mammoth task , scientists see one way out to mitigate the problem -- to domesticate minor fruit-bearing plants . <p> There used to be a time when tribals living in these forests used to make a living by collecting and selling them in the nearest towns . However , due to climate change and its impact on rainfall in the forests , some of the varieties have gone extinct . Due to cultural changes and change in lifestyle of tribals , the practice of collecting fruits has gradually become a thing of the past over the last couple of decades . Even now , some of them collect and sell these minor fruits , but the price is really high , which is unaffordable for general customers . <p> In the past , some of the minor fruits such as Neeredu ( syzgium cuminii ) , Usirikaya ( phyllanthus emblica ) , Sethaphalam ( annona squamosa ) , Velakkaya ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Morli/Chironji ( buchanania lanzan ) were domesticated and various varieties of the minor fruits were also developed . <p> In Rajasthan , four varieties of ' Ber ' , which were named Seb , Mundia , Ilachi and Gola , were developed and even in Karnataka , two varieties were developed and domesticated . However , whether it was lack of interest on the part of previous rulers in erstwhile AP or lack of will among administrators , no effort was put in to protect , develop and promote certain minor fruit varieties which are indigenous to the Telugu-speaking States . <p> " There is an urgent need to do research on the nutritive value of all these fruits and to develop new varieties to improve their yield potential , making them adaptable to field-level cultivation and using them for processed foods such as jams , jellies and juices , " opined Dr M Padmaiah , former principal scientist and head , Social Sciences , ICAR- Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research , who continues to dedicate his post-retirement life to make farming better for mankind . <p> Speaking to ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ could fund this research and development projects and ICAR 's Fruit Research Institutes , agricultural and horticultural universities and even the Forest Department could play a major role in research on domesticating minor fruit varieties .
