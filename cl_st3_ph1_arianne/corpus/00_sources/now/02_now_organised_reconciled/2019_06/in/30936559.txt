During the first term of the Modi government , Harsh Vardhan was appointed Minister of Health and Family Welfare in 2014 . He was later given the charge of the Ministry of Earth Sciences and Ministry of Science and Technology . <p> In 2017 , he was appointed the Minister of Environment , Forest and Climate Change following the demise of Union minister Anil Madhav Dave . In the recently-concluded Lok Sabha elections , Harsh Vardhan retained his Chandni Chowk Lok Sabha constituency , winning it by a margin of over two lakh votes .
