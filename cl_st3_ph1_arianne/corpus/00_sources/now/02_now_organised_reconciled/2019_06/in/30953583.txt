<h> Estonia gains a seat at the UN Security Council <h> Estonia has , for the first time , been elected a non-permanent member of the United Nations Security Council . <p> The vote took place on 7 June at the United Nations ( UN ) headquarters in New York City . All 193 UN member states were in present to cast their votes . Estonia was elected a non-permanent member of the United Nations Security Council for 2020-2021 with 132 votes . <p> The non-permanent seats on the Security Council are distributed on a regional basis . Estonia belongs to the Eastern European group and its main regional competitor for the seat was Romania , a country of almost 20 million inhabitants . Estonia has not been a member of the Security Council before , while Romania has been a member on four occasions . <p> In the first voting round , Estonia received 111 votes and Romania 78 -- both countries short of a majority of two thirds , 129 votes , needed to get elected .
