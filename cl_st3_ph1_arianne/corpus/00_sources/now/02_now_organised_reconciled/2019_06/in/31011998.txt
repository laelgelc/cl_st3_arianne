Apart from a large number of houses being merely livable or even dilapidated , there are millions of people in the country who are either homeless or live in unauthorised colonies . And more notably , a large chunk of this population is based out of urban areas . <p> India has experienced a significant uptick in the trend of people migrating from rural areas to urban cities in search of better living in the past couple of decades since the full-fledged advent of globalisation . In spite of all the positivity and possibilities within in such a scenario , this trend and the concomitant uptick also contributes to the inherent socio-economic disparity prevalent in India since times immemorial .
