<h> Lions Club of Imphal Valley continues tree plantation <p> 24-Jun-2019 <p> By Our Staff ReporterIMPHAL , Jun 23 : As part of its continuous endeavour to conserve the environment , Lions Club of Imphal Valley in collaboration with Citizens ' Club , Soibam Leikai , Imphal East observed " Environment Month cum Tree Plantation Project " at the premise of the later today.Around 50 tree saplings were planted in and round the premises of Citizens ' Club.Speaking to reporters on the sideline of the programme , Lions ' Club of Imphal Valley president Dr N Romi Singh conveyed that the programme was conducted with the firm determination to alleviate the growing impact of global warming and climate change in the State . Romi said today 's programme was the second edition of observing Environment Month cum Tree Plantation Project after conducting the first edition at Nongpok Sekmai.He lauded the volunteers of Citizens ' Club for actively supporting the project and appreciated Forest and Environment Minister Th Shyamkumar for providing tree saplings to Lions Club of Imphal Valley .
