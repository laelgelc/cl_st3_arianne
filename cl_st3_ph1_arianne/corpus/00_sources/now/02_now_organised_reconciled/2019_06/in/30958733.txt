Mr Javadekar conducted a review of the ' Project for Pollution Abatement of River Mula Mutha in Pune ' on Sunday . <p> The first package of the river cleaning project includes construction of branch sewers in Baner . It was also highlighted that the Rs 990 crore worth project that aims to reduce the pollution of Mula-Mutha River in Pune has been fast-tracked . <p> Officials of the Pune Municipal Corporation ( PMC ) , forest department and consultants related to the project were present for the review meeting .
