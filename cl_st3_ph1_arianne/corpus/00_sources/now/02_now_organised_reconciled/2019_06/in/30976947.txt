<h> Summer school workshop at PU highlights interesting facts <p> TNN Updated : Jun 14 , 2019 , 18:17 IST <p> The ongoing summer school of the National Academy of Sciences ( NAS ) at the department of physics , PU , saw various talks on subjects like Physics , Chemistry and Environmental Science being delivered . In the first session , Dr PK Ahluwalia from HPU delivered two interesting talks , with first being on discovery of electron and second on ' what can I do after schooling ' . Prof D Mehta from the physics department introduced various basic concepts on measurements and also corrected various misconceptions regarding solar eclipse and use of microwave radiations . Later , during the afternoon session , Prof AS Ahluwalia from botany department talked about the effect of effluents on the climate change . He discussed various modern life styles being adopted by us which are causing more carbon dioxide footprint on mother earth .
