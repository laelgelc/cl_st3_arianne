<h> Services <h> Mizoram launches climate change awareness campaigns <p> Agencies Updated : Jun 27 , 2019 , 12:32 IST <p> Representative image <p> AIZAWL/AGARTALA : The Mizoram government has initiated an awareness campaign on climate change across the state , officials said on Wednesday . The Mizoram Remote Sensing Application Centre ( MIRSAC ) and Deputy Commissioners of various districts are organising these campaigns involving government officials , NGOs , media persons , academicians , village council leaders and students , an official of state Directorate of Information and Public Relations ( DIPR ) said . The government took action after a study named Assam , Mizoram , and Jammu and Kashmir , among others as the 12 Himalayan states in India that are extremely vulnerable to climate change . The study , entitled ' Climate Vulnerability Assessment for the Indian Himalayan Region Using a Common Framework ' was conducted in 2018 by the Indian Institute of Technology , Mandi , IIT-Guwahati and Indian Institute of Science , Bengaluru along with the governments of all 12 states . The study also prepared a vulnerability index for the 12 states .
