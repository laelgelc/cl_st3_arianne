<h> ESIL to file affidavit over land dispute <p> Cuttack : The Essar Steel India Ltd ( ESIL ) will soon file an affidavit before the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) in response to a notice issued to it in connection with a dispute over a plot of land close to Sankari village under Banspal tehsil in Keonjhar district sanctioned to it for industrial purpose . A senior ESIL official said the company 's application for environmental clearance to start any construction activity on the sanctioned land is already pending with the Union ministry of environment and forest and climate change . The company 's application has been supported as required by confirmation and certificate of non-forest land in connection with the sanctioned land , which the offices concerned of the tehsildar and the forest range officer at Banspal , had issued on March 7 and May 11 in 2018 , respectively . The ESIL official , who did not wish to be named , refused to comment on the dispute over the sanctioned land at this stage . The official , however , added that the company would soon file an affidavit in response to NGT 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to ESIL to file a reply . The NGT had issued the notice after a committee constituted by it recommended the cancellation of the sanction of 306.670 acres of forest land in favour of ESIL on the ground that the sanctioned land " has the characteristics of forest containing about 20,003 numbers of trees of different varieties " . On February 19 , 2019 , NGT had decided to first constitute a committee for a ground survey to ascertain the veracity of the allegation before issuing notice on the matter . The committee comprised the collector of Keonjhar district , the divisional forest officer ( Keonjhar ) , member secretary of the State Pollution Control Board and a representative from the regional office of the ministry of environment , forest and climate change . <p> Subsequently , the committee had submitted its report . The case was taken up on May 28 . Taking note of the committee 's report , the Tribunal posted the matter to August 19 for hearing along with the ESIL 's response .
