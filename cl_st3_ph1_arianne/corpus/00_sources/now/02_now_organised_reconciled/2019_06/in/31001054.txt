<h> Ireland Government to Ban Sale of New Petrol and Diesel Cars by 2030 <h> The decision to stop petrol and diesel vehicles by 2030 is one of the 180 proposals of the Ireland government that covers business , construction , transport , agriculture and waste management , all aimed at zero carbon emissions . <p> Ireland has announced it will ban the sale of new petrol and diesel vehicles by 2030 as part of its new climate change plan . The government hopes to have 950,000 electric vehicles on Irish roads by then , supported by a network of charging stations . The measure is one of 180 proposals covering business , construction , transport , agriculture and waste management intended to put Ireland on a path to achieve net zero carbon emissions by 2050 . <p> " Our approach will be to nudge people and businesses to change behaviour and adopt new technologies through incentives , disincentives , regulations and information , " said Prime Minister Leo Varadkar . <p> " Our objective ... is to transition to a low-carbon and climate-resilient society .
