<h> Navi Mumbai to lose another six acres of mangroves for upcoming airport <p> The CIDCO had earlier sought permission to cut nearly 500 acres of mangroves for the airport <p> Navi Mumbai will lose another six acres of mangroves , and this time it is for developing infrastructure for the Navi Mumbai airport . Earlier , the project proponent , City Industrial Development Corporation , had sought permission to cut nearly 500 acres of mangroves for the airport . <p> Last year , the Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) of the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change had agreed to cut the mangroves in larger public interest so that people could reach the airport fast . The CIDCO wants to build three bridge and remove the bund on Moha river . Two existing bridges will be dismantled . <p> The clearances were recently granted by the Maharashtra Coastal Zone Management Authority ( MCZMA ) . These bridges will help passengers access the airport speedily . But while giving permission , the MCZMA has also asked that five times more mangroves must be planted for what was destroyed .
