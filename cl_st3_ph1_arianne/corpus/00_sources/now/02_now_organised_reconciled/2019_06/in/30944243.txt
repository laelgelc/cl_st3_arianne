<h> World Environment Day : ' Ploggers ' of Bengaluru combine jogging with plastic clean-up to do their bit <h> Residents of Bengaluru have taken up ' plogging ' , an activity which combines jogging with plastic clean-up . On the occasion of World Environment Day , we talk to these ' ploggers ' who are doing their bit . <p> Bengaluru : The fifth day of June each year is observed as World Environment Day , an initiative by the United Nations ( UN ) to spread awareness about the importance of protecting our natural ecosystems and saving our planet from man-made destruction . It is no surprise that one of the most harmful waste products introduced by humans into God 's green Earth is plastic . <p> Owing to the composition of plastic substances , they take almost a thousand years to decompose inside a landfill which makes it a major threat to the environment . N Raj , also known as the ' plogman ' of Bengaluru , started the initiative in 2018 and now enjoys the support of hundreds of locals who share his concern for cleanliness in the city which is known as India 's information technology ( IT ) capital . <p> Speaking to Mirror Now during a clean-up drive in the city 's Cubbon Park area , Raj says , " A city like Bengaluru , which is cosmopolitan by nature , always accepts fresh ideas and we thought that plogging which started in Sweden in 2016 would be a good fit for the city . Our waste is our responsibility . " A young girl who was part of the clean-up drive added , " My message for all of Bengaluru is to keep the city clean .
