Experts point to rising temperature <h> Last Updated : Monday 03 June 2019 <p> Image : Getty Images <p> On June 2 , 2019 a cloudburst over Uttarakhand 's Lambagad village in Chamoli district killed one person . Soon after , an alert was issued in the state 's Almora region owing to rising water level in Ramganga River . <p> While this is the first cloudburst of 2019 , the state faced around a dozen of these extreme weather events last year . Experts say not only has the frequency of such events increased , but their timing has also changed . <p> " The number of cloudbursts is definitely increasing and their time pattern has also changed . We are hardly in June right now and a cloud burst has happened . Earlier , cloudbursts were common during monsoon or post-monsoon period , which is September-October . Cloud bursts are happening sooner every year , " said Kireet Kumar , scientist at GB Pant National Institute of Himalayan Environment and Sustainable Development . <p> Kumar added that the main reason for this increase is rising temperatures due to climate change . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cloud formation .
