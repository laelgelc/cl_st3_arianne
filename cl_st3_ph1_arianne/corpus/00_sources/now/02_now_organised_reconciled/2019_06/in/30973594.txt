<h> Plastic ban in J&amp;K opens door for revival of wicker crafts <p> Updated : June 13 , 2019 09:17 PM IST <p> Single-use plastics have been banned in several states of India , including Jammu and Kashmir . A decade ago , these baskets made from locally produced kaani ( wicker ) by Ganie and his fellow craftsmen , were a familiar sight in villages here , used in carrying and storing farm-produce and other commodities . But now , artisans like Ganie are compelled to shore up their income by working as manual labour at construction sites . <p> The ubiquitous plastic bag has made deep inroads into rural Kashmir , as in the rest of India . While the use of these bags has been banned through legislation in several states , including in Jammu and Kashmir , the ban has not always been effective . As a case in point , plastic bags and containers have steadily taken the place of traditional kranjul wicker baskets that women in Kashmir used to carry vegetables in . And people are now reluctant to give up the " plastic habit " , even in the face of a ban .
