<h> Opinion Left or Right , genetic science unites all luddites <p> Earlier this month , in Maharashtra , the farmers ' organisation Shetkari Sanghatana ( SS ) announced that its members would be planting genetically modified ( GM ) herbicide-tolerant ( HT ) Bt cotton . HT Bt cotton is not allowed in India , and growing any GM crop illegally attracts five-year imprisonment and a ? 1-lakh fine . But the government itself estimates that HT Bt cotton is being planted on over 15% of the country 's total cotton cultivation area . Lakhs of farmers have gone rogue , because they see much more value in this than in obeying laws that have left Indian agriculture in a parlous state . Can the government arrest lakhs of farmers ? Apparently , one SS farmer has been detained . <p> The GM debate has been going on in India for two decades now , and is one issue that has brought the leftists and the far-right together . So we find Communists and the Swadeshi Jagran Manch ( SJM ) both peddling bad science ( using every rabble-rousing tactic and making sure that the Indian government keeps @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> To begin with , two basic points for the Luddites to consider . One , ever since man began farming thousands of years ago , we have been genetically modifying plants , through methods like saving and re-planting the best seeds , cross-pollination and hybridization . Genetic engineering ( GE ) is simply the latest technique , the difference being that it involves transferring genetic material from one organism to an unrelated organism -- for example , DNA from bacteria to corn . It is just another leap forward in a long journey of innovation , like from chullahs to gas ovens . Two , I call upon every diabetic leftist and SJM anti-GM activist to forsake her insulin injections -- your daily dosage is genetically engineered . In fact , the development of synthetic insulin faced years of intense anti-GE activism , exactly what we see today against GM crops . Time has proved the agitators hilariously wrong . <p> There are three classic Indian GM cases . Meanwhile , India imported about ? 80,000 crore worth of edible oils last year , and the bill keeps growing . <p> Bt brinjal was approved by the GEAC in 2009 , but under pressure from activists , the government disallowed its planting . Bangladesh , using the same GEAC report , went ahead . Today , about 17% of the country 's brinjal farmers grow Bt brinjal , which has reduced the GM crop farmers ' pesticide costs by 61% . The Bangladeshi Bt brinjal farmer 's net returns per hectare are six times as much as his non-Bt counterpart per year . <p> The only GM crop allowed in India is Bt cotton , a historic decision taken by Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee in 2002 . A study by Professor Ashok Gulati and Kavery Ganguly estimates that Bt cotton has earned India about $67 billion from exports and savings in imports , from 2002-03 to 2016-17 . <p> In 2017 , 189.8 million hectares of biotech crops were planted in 24 countries ( and consumed in 67 ) . To achieve the same yield standards , more than 300 million acres of conventional crops would have been needed . According to UK consultancy firm PG Economics , the first 20 years of biotech crops ( 1996-2016 ) have seen a reduction of pesticide spraying by 671.2 million kg and the environmental footprint associated with pesticide use by 18.4% . The figures for 2016 : the drop in release of greenhouse gas emissions was equivalent to removing 16.75 million cars from the roads ; direct global farm income benefit from GM crops was $18.2 billion , equal to an average increase in income of $102/hectare . So what are these Luddites fighting against ? <p> Agriculture is possibly the most environmentally destructive human activity . So high yields coupled with lower greenhouse gas emission ( organic and conventional farming emit about the same amount ) are absolutely essential for a sustainable future . In India , farmers are stuck with low-yielding breeds , and climate change will only bring more insect attacks . Yet , the Prime Minister still talks of doubling farmers ' 2015 income by 2022 . More than anyone else , he knows the benefits of GM crops , because it was the farmers of Gujarat who gained the most from Bt cotton . How long will he allow himself to be held hostage by some obscurantist fanatics ?
