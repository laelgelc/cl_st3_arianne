<h> World Environment Day : 25,000 saplings planted in Trichy <p> A sapling is being planted by residents in Srirangam on Wednesday ( TOI photo by R Baskar ) <p> TRICHY : The Trichy Corporation with the help of residents , students and NGOs planted 25,000 saplings here on Wednesday , on the occasion of the World Environment Day . The corporation has formed 20 task committees comprising officials to make the initiative a success . The committees identified suitable location and custodians who would take care of planted saplings . " Residents welfare associations , NGOs , and students who are willing to take care of the saplings in their vicinity were identified in Srirangam , K Abishekapuram , Ponmalai and Ariyamanagalam zones . The custodians have been requested to periodically water and maintain the native tree saplings , " corporation commissioner N Ravichandran said . <p> The civic body provided bamboo tree guards free of cost to the custodians for protecting the saplings from stray cattle . Sowing seed ballsWhen it comes to ' contributing ' to the nature , joining a tree plantation drive seems to be the most common and easiest resort for many people . But a group of students studying in Visveswaraya National Institute of Technology ( VNIT ) has come up with a plantation method with a twist . Instead of planting saplings or sowing seeds directly , they have been preparing seed balls ( a mixture of cow dung and soil with a seed inside ) to counter the depleting green cover . One of the students , Aniruddha Kane shares , " Firstly seed balls are cost-effective and secondly they increase the life expectancy ; germination rate is faster as well . With the onset of monsoon seed balls will be sown or dispersed on the barren land . We have chosen seeds of jackfruit , mango , jamun etc .
