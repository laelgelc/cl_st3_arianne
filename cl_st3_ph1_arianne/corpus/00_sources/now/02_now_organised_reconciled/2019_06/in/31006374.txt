<p> In an order issued by the Telangana State Power Generation Corporation Limited , the CMD said all the 1 to 8 units ( 4x60MW+4 ? 120 MW ) of the plant with 720 MW capacity have to be retired following the directions from the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . <p> The Union Ministry had granted environmental clearance to Kothagudem Thermal Power Station ( KTPS ) 7th stage with a precondition that the KTPS ( O&amp;M ) units must be phased out latest by 2019 . The GENCO Board had accorded permission for the retirement of the power plant on environmental and techno-economic reasons , he noted . <p> In view of this , the existing employees working in all cadres ( including artisans ) would have to be redeployed to other power stations and a skeleton staff should be retained so as to look after dismantling and switchyard maintenance , Prabhakar Rao said . <p> The GENCO Board approved the constitution of a committee to frame guidelines for the redeployment of employees .
