<h> People-centric tech targets disaster displacement <p> AS weather-related disasters force millions from their homes each year , new technologies are transforming the humanitarian response . <p> By Gareth Willmer <p> When disaster strikes , the immediate death toll is first to hit the headlines . But a much longer-term problem is the potentially huge number of people forced to move -- a figure set to surge amid the rising impact of climate change . <p> Over 17 million people worldwide were displaced by disasters last year , the vast majority of which were weather-related , according to a new report by the Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre ( IDMC ) . <p> At 41.3 million , the estimated number of people living in internal displacement from disasters or conflict is at its highest-ever level , the report states , adding : " Despite policy progress in several countries , the root causes of internal displacement are still not being adequately addressed . " <p> One of the biggest issues in the coming years , the report highlights , will be rising rural-urban migration , with 95 per cent of urban growth expected to happen in developing countries .
