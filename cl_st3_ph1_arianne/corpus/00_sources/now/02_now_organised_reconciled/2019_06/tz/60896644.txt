<h> Dar team win Mazingira Cup soccer tourney <p> A soccer team owned by a sanitary company , Abby Environment and Agriculture Company Limited , emerged as winners of the Forum on Climate Change ( FCC ) Mazingira Cup tournament that climaxed in Dar es Salaam yesterday . <p> The competition geared towards commemorating the World Environment Day . <p> Abby team cruised to a 2-1 win over Kajenjele Company Limited team in the final that was played at the Jakaya Kikwete Youth Park . <p> Mazingira Cup tournament started earlier last month and involved eight teams that were made up of the sanitary companies ' workers . <p> Abby team shall serve as an environmental ambassador for one year following the victory . <p> Rebecca Muna , FCC Executive Director , said that the tournament was organized to influence the community to switch to renewable energy sources consumption . <p> " We encourage the government , environment stakeholders , academicians and policy makers to spread the word on the importance of renewable energy sources that could preserve the world 's beauty " she added . <p> World Environment Day 2019 is hosted by China with a theme of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we can do something about the quality of air that we breathe ' . <p> According to the United Nations Environment Agency , approximately seven million people worldwide die prematurely each year from air pollution . <p> About four million of these deaths are occurring in Asia-Pacific . <p> World Environment Day 2019 will urge governments , industry , communities , and individuals to come together to explore renewable energy and green technologies , and improve air quality in cities and regions across the world .
