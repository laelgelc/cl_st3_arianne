<h> Britain to become first G7 country with net zero emissions target <p> Britain will toughen its climate targets and commit to reaching net zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 , the government said late on Tuesday , becoming the first G7 nation to set such a goal . <p> The country currently aims to cut greenhouse gas emissions ( GHGs ) by 80 percent from 1990 levels by 2050 . However , campaigners say this does not go far enough to meet pledges made under the 2015 Paris climate agreement to try to limit a rise in global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> " Now is the time to go further and faster to safeguard the environment for our children , " Prime Minister Theresa May said in a statement . <p> " Reaching net zero by 2050 is an ambitious target , but it is crucial that we achieve it to ensure we protect our planet for future generations .
