<h> IUCN launched Nature &amp; Biodiversity Safeguarding Actions <p> International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) Pakistan formally launched its project titled " Assessment of the State of Nature and Biodiversity Safeguarding Actions in Northern Pakistan " in collaboration with the line departments , private sector and local communities at Forest Complex , Gilgit-Baltistan Forest , Wildlife and Environment Department ( GBFWED ) , Government of Gilgit-Baltistan ( GoGB ) . Shahid Zaman , Secretary Forest , Wildlife and Environment , GoGB graced the occasion as chief guest . <p> The program started with welcome address by Mahmood Akthar Cheema , Country Representative IUCN Pakistan . He said that IUCN is a membership Union uniquely composed of both government and civil society organizations . <p> It provides public , private and non-governmental organizations with the knowledge and tools that enable human progress , economic development and nature conservation to take place together .
