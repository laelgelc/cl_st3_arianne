<h> Lahore 's pigeons are vanishing in plain sight <p> The number of wild pigeons in Lahore , which were once spotted hovering over buildings , is slowly and steadily declining . <p> The birds frequently flock near Istanbul Chowk , Data Darbar , Mall Road , Jain Temple , and Shahi Qila , where many people feed them too . <p> " I love these birds , but I have noticed that their number is decreasing by the day , " said a resident . " The wildlife department should do something about it . " <p> Naeem Bhatti , who is the deputy director of the wildlife department , remarked that the birds have been disappearing because of fatal diseases . " One of the most common diseases that affect them is the Newcastle Disease , " he said . The Newcastle disease is a contagious viral bird disease that affects many wild and domestic birds and it can even be transmitted to humans . <p> " To stop the spread of the disease , the birds can be given vaccines and antibiotics , " he explained .
