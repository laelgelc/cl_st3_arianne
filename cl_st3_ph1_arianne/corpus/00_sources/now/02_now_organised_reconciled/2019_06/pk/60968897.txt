<h> Senate body calls for a cleaner , greener Islamabad <p> The Sub-committee of the Senate Standing Committee for Climate Change , convened by Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed , took up various issues posing a threat to Islamabad 's environment . <p> The meeting was attended by Senator Samina Saeed , Green Peace activists and senior officers from the Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) , Ministry of Climate Change and all departments concerned . Minister for Climate Change Zartaj Gul was also present in the meeting . <p> Issues taken up in the meeting were billboards that have turned into an eyesore for Islamabad 's residents , sewerage near F9 Park for which a compliance report will be submitted to the committee within 15 days and finalisation of a landfill site for solid waste management . <p> While discussing the landfill site , Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed asserted that the site must not be just a garbage dumping ground but must follow international standards and give priority to recycling waste , which was a modern industry . <p> Raising the issue of billboards , Senator Samina Saeed was of the view that these must be minimised since , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ threatening as well . The Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) informed the committee that as per the direction of the Supreme Court , 80 percent of billboards in Islamabad had already been removed . It was recommended that the ratio of billboards ' size be taken into consideration as well . <p> While taking up the issue of nullah management in Islamabad , Dr Dushka Sayed was of the opinion that to deal with this issue it was imperative that the issue of slums be separated from that of high rises . As per the recommendation in the previous meeting to link corporate social responsibilities ( CSR ) with the responsibility for environment degradation and pollution caused by high rise construction , marquee operation and other corporate activity , the committee recommended that legislation must be passed to make CSR contribution mandatory . <p> Meanwhile , in a bid to make the federal capital plastic-free , Zartaj Gul said a fine of Rs 500,000 would be imposed on those violating the ban after August 14 . <p> Zartaj Gul informed the sub-committee that anyone who was seen using plastic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and also urged for the use of cloth bags instead . <p> Pakistan is ranked 7th in the list of countries affected due to natural disasters . The state minister had already announced a blanket ban on use of plastic bags in Islamabad from August 14 . The sub-committee was mainly focusing on the report on environmental issues pertaining to the federal capital . <p> CDA officials informed the sub-committee that the nullah at the F-9 Park would be cleared in 15 days . <p> Metropolitan Cooperation Islamabad ( MCI ) officials also informed the committee that the managements of the private housing societies of E-11 were directed to establish sewerage treatment plants of appropriate technology to give a sigh of relief to the residents and improve environment . <p> In the meeting , Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) Director General Farzana Altaf informed the committee that there was no air pollution in I-19 and I-10 industrial area , as it was being monitored round the clock .
