If it were just that it would be a much less difficult problem to beat . Rising temperatures , or heatwaves , is just one manifestation -- one amongst many -- of the great challenge of climate change . Climate change is a complex of multiple manifestations of changes in the greater climate system -- not just changing weather . It is about temperatures rising in many places , sometimes in heatwaves , sometimes in erratic temperature swings , sometimes even in temperatures falling . It is about erratically changing rainfall systems ; cyclones , tsunamis , monsoon bursts ... or just the disappearance of expected rains . It is about drought .
