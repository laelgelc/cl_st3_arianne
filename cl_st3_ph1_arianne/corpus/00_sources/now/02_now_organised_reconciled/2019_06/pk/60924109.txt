<p> The biotechnology research scientists believed that the commercialization of biotech corn would support the growth of maize production in Pakistan . Experts told Business Recorder here on Friday that globally , biotech crops have assisted in increasing the productivity of farmers through efficient agricultural practices . <p> The increased productivity means that farmers can produce more from same acreage with same or less operational/input and labor costs . Additionally , this has also helped in improving/protecting the biodiversity and environment . <p> The single and stacked biotech maize with insect resistance and herbicide tolerance traits were officially approved for commercial cultivation by the National Bio-safety Committee ( NBC ) of the Ministry of Climate Change ( MOCC ) . Thereafter , the field performance/seed registration trials for biotech maize hybrids were conducted as part of the regulatory requirement for varietal registration under the auspices of the Ministry of National Food Security and Research during 2017-18 . <p> It is learnt that these biotech maize trials exhibited excellent performance/yields . Field demonstrations of biotech maize have also been conducted by the companies in past to exhibit their performance to maize farmers who have always been appreciative of the technology @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ experts referred to the biotechnology research scientists . <p> In 2017 , Pakistan achieved an all-time high maize production of 6.13 million tons which was up by 16.3% from last year 's 5.27 million tons . Current maize production is almost at par with the maize grain demand which is primarily triggered by poultry , dairy and wet milling industries . Significant growth of poultry requires increased corn grain production to sustain future demands . Similarly , for a sustainable growth of dairy industry demand of maize silage is also on increasing trend in the future . With little possibility of adding more acres to maize cultivation , there is a need to produce more grain from existing acreage to meet all future corn grain demands . <p> The commercialization of biotech maize will be a significant milestone in the Pakistan as this will not only help our farmers to increase their productivity but also meet our national food and feed demands from existing resources , experts added .
