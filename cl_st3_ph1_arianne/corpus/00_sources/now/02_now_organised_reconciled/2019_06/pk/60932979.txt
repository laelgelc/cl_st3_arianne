Departments of Agriculture , Food and Livestock , Food Industry , CropLife Pakistan Association , UN agencies ( FAO , WFP , UNDP ) and the World Bank . <p> The representatives from all stakeholders participated in the consultative meeting to discuss the themes and curriculum of the program . <p> Food security encompasses food availability , affordability and accessibility .
