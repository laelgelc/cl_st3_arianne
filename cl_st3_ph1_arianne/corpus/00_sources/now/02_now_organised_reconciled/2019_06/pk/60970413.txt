<h> Europe 's heatwave consistent with climate change , more to come : U.N <p> GENEVA : This year is on track to be among the hottest ever and that would make 2015-2019 the world 's hottest five-year period on record , the World Meteorological Organization said on Friday . <p> It is too soon to definitely attribute Europe 's current blistering heatwave to climate change but it is " absolutely consistent " with extremes linked to the impact of greenhouse gas emissions , the United Nations agency said . <p> " Heatwaves will become more intense , they will become more drawn out , they will become more extreme , they will start earlier and they will finish later , " WMO spokeswoman Clare Nullis told a briefing in Geneva . <p> " We are still only the end of June , but it seems like the earth is set to experience its five warmest years on record , so that is 2015 to 2019 inclusive , " she said .
