<h> Email <p> PESHAWAR : The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa government has directed irrigation department and district administration across the province to remove encroachments from waterways to avoid damage during the upcoming monsoon season . <p> This was decided in a provincial monsoon conference held here on June 20 . The conference was chaired by Chief Secretary Mohammad Saleem Khan and attended by senior officials of government departments . The conference discussed Provincial Disaster Management Authority ( PDMA ) ' s monsoon contingency plan 2019-20 . <p> The minutes of the meeting available with Dawn state that the participants were informed that encroachments in riverbeds and other waterways tended to increase velocity of water and cause floods in rivers and streams . <p> The chief secretary directed the district administration and irrigation department to take urgent measures against encroachments to minimise impact of floods . He also stressed the need for installation of hydro-metrological sensors on rivers and streams to cope with freak weather caused by climate change . <p> The director general of PDMA told the meeting that work on monsoon contingency plan was started in January . He said that the planning involved extensive deliberations with other government departments @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ partners were also taken on board about the process . He also briefed the meeting about the coordination mechanism of PDMA for managing disaster situation . He also explained the vulnerability profile of each district prepared during the planning that included threats like riverine , flash , urban and glacial lake outburst floods , heavy concentrated rainfall , heavy winds/cyclone and land sliding incidents . <p> The PDMA chief said that the province was highly affected by climate change as well as hydrological hazards . The meeting was informed that 12 districts were vulnerable to flash floods and GLOFs ; seven to riverine floods ; nine to urban flooding ; eight to heavy rainfall/storms ; and 13 to landslide . <p> The chief secretary directed the irrigation secretary to review and if required update the data regarding nulllahs and canals so that the current position was known to all the departments concerned .
