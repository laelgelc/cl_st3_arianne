<h> Policy on electric vehicles soon : Aslam <p> ISLAMABAD : Adviser to Prime Minister on Climate Change , Malik Amin Aslam said emissions from the vehicles remained one of the largest contributors to air pollution and the government is moving towards the direction of materialising the policy on electric vehicles to tackle air pollution . The Prime Minister Imran Khan has approved the initial concept of the proposal and policy consultation with the key stakeholders is in the process , which will be presenting in the cabinet in a couple of weeks , he added . <p> During a seminar titled " World Environment Day 2019 : Air Pollution " , organised by Sustainable Development Policy Institute ( SDPI ) here at Islamabad , Malik Amin Aslam said electric vehicles will solve many of our issues including air pollution , issues of unmet capacity charges , reduce fuel import bill and save two third cost of the transportation .
