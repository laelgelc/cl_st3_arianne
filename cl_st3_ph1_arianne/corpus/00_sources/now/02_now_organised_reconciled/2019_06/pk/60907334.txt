<h> Pakistan rejects any trade war or new cold war with China , says Senator Mushahid Hussain <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Chairman of the Senate Foreign Affairs Committee , who also chairs the Pakistan-China Institute ( PCI ) , Senator Mushahid Hussain has said during his speech at launch of book on the leadership of President Xi Jinping that Pakistan rejects any trade war or cold war with China . <p> The function which was organized by the Pakistan-China Institute was packed to capacity with a large gathering of scholars and students , parliamentarians from different political parties , business and opinion leaders , think tanks and retired senior military officers and ambassadors . <p> During his speech , Senator Mushahid Hussain lauded the leadership of President Xi Jinping where he has placed China at the centre stage of global politics leading the struggle against climate change , supporting globalization as well as launching the Belt &amp; Road Initiative ( BRI ) of which China Pakistan Economic Corridor ( CPEC ) is the flagship project . <p> Senator Mushahid Hussain termed BRI as the most important diplomatic and developmental initiative of the 21st century .
