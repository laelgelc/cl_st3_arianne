<p> Advisor to Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam has said that over one million health cards will be distributed in Attock district and this is an ample proof that this govt is fulfilling its promise regarding health facilities . <p> He said this while addressing a Health cards distribution ceremony in Jinnah Hall Attock . <p> Malik Amin said that people will get medical treatment up to the 700,000 to 1,400,000 rupees through this card while the card will be renewed on annual basis . <p> He said that a fresh survey will be made in this regard and number of health cards will be increased as per the result of the survey . <p> About Clean and Green Pakistan program , he said the programme is in full swing which will improve the environment while encroachments on government lands specially forest department lands are also being removed .
