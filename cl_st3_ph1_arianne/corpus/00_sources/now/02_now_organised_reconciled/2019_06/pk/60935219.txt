<h> Collaborations for environmental protection , water management <p> US-Pakistan Center for Advanced Studies in Water ( USPCAS-W ) , Mehran University of Engineering and Technology ( MUET ) Jamshoro and Sindh Environmental Protection Agency ( SEPA ) have agreed to collaborate for environmental protection in the province through capacity building of public and private sectors and research in the field of the water &amp; wastewater management . <p> A delegation of SEPA led by Director General Naeem Ahmad Mughal visited USPCAS-W and held a detailed meeting with management of the center . The delegation of the Center led by Project Director Dr. Bakhshal Lashari and . Deputy Director Academics and Research Dr. Rasool Bux Mahar gave presentation regarding the performance , achievements and available facilities and services of the Center . Dr. Bakhshal Lashari , Project Director of the center said that USPCAS-W will welcome every organization to provide its expertise in various sectors related to environment and water . He said that Center is already working with various organizations and industries to resolve their water related issues with the help of innovative research of its faculty and scholars . <p> Briefing the delegation , DG SEPA Naeem @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and agreed to collaborate in future He sought the list of research projects , their titles and recommendations as he may discuss those with government , non-government and private organizations on priority basis for implementation . Mr. Mughal said that Center should conduct trainings , workshops , awareness campaigns for general public and workers of various industries and SEPA will collaborate in this regard . He further said that USPCAS-W and SEPA will also work for air quality monitoring , indoor air pollution , to make mechanism for climate change in future and other sectors also . Both the organizations agreed to sign a MoU and develop an action plan to address the priority issues in the field of water and environment .
