<h> Plastic ban violators to face Rs0.5m fine after Aug 14 , says Zartaj <p> ISLAMABAD : In a bid to make the federal capital plastic-free , State Minister for Climate Change Zartaj Gul Friday said a fine of Rs500,000 would be imposed on those violating the ban after August 14 . <p> Zartaj Gul informed the Sub-Committee of the Senate on Climate Change , which met here at the Parliament House under the chairmanship of Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed , said that anyone who is seen using plastic bags in the capital after August 14 will be fined and also urged for the use of cloth bags instead . <p> Pakistan is ranked 7th in the list of countries affected due to natural disasters .
