In the first five years of its existence ( 2002-2007 ) , the HEC brought about a major increase in the funds invested in universities across the country . During this first half decade , the HEC touted that it had overseen a purported increase of 5,000 percent in the budget allocated to the higher education sector . This was hailed unequivocally as a great success . <p> When the funds-starved higher education sector began to receive much needed inflow of cash , things certainly appeared to look better .
