focus on underdeveloped areas for balanced regional development " . <p> The finance bill for the FY2019-20 was presented by State Minister for Revenue Hammad Azhar , who said the funds were allocated in line with the government 's policy to promote harmony and national integration . <p> Of the total outlay , Rs 951 billion was set aside for the federal ministries and divisions , whereas Rs912 billion was earmarked for the provinces , according to a budgetary document , which further noted that the National Economic Council ( NEC ) had approved the PSDP allocations on May 29 , with foreign assistance worth Rs127 billion . <p> According to the budgetary document finalised by the Ministry of Planning , Development , and Reforms , the budget for PSDP , through an extensive consultative and
