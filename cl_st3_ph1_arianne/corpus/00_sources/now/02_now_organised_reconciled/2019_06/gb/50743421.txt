<h> Celebrating satellites ' contribution to polar science <p> Seven areas of fast-flowing ice on the Antarctic Peninsula have been formally named after Earth observation satellites , following a request by a University of Leeds scientist . <p> Dramatic changes in the shape of the Antarctic ice sheet have become emblematic of the climate crisis . Reports of iceberg calving , changes in ice-sheet speed , thickness and mass have informed the climate change debate , and are largely thanks to routine monitoring by an international fleet of Earth observation satellites . <p> Recognising the importance of observations from space , the UK Antarctic Place-names Committee has approved seven new names for international use . <p> The decision follows a request by Dr Anna Hogg from the Centre for Polar Observation and Modelling at the University of Leeds , who identified that the major glaciers flowing westwards from the Dyer Plateau on the Antarctic Peninsula are thinning and flowing at rates of more than 1.5 metres a day . <p> ?
