Or so the press release reads . <p> But while the group consist of environmentalists , scientists and farmers from different sectors of the industry , I suspect the discussions might be a bit more down to earth , with former NFUS president , Nigel Miller , appointed as one of the co-chairs . <p> The aim of the committee is to find the best way for farming to help Scotland get to its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) target by 2045 while still producing the sort of quality produce for which the country is known . <p> There is a danger here . It would also be the sort of short-sighted approach often latched on to by governments in a hurry . <p> The trouble is that it might also lead to a situation where we rely heavily on imports from parts of the world which are likely to be much more heavily impacted by global warming than the UK . <p> If our own farming industry was left to decline and wither even in the short term -- with the loss of the key skills and knowledge which currently support it -- it would be all but impossible to revive it at a later date . <p> On the topic of government decision-making processes , it was confirmed last week that Westminster 's failure to push @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ result in a spectacular blow to the country 's arable sector . <p> For the government inaction which has stalled the move to E10 -- a blend of petrol containing 10% bioethanol -- is putting the future of the country 's two big bioethanol plants in the North of England , which are capable of utilising 2m tonnes of home-grown wheat a year , at risk . <p> Many other countries , including many European nations as well as the United States , have already pushed through moves which make the use of such a blend obligatory .
