<h> Bishop welcomes government promise to reach ' net zero ' <h> Bishop of Salisbury welcomes government promise to reach ' net zero ' by 2050 <p> The Bishop of Salisbury has welcomed a promise from the government to reduce greenhouse gas emissions to net zero . <p> The Church of England 's lead on environmental affairs - the Right Reverend Nicholas Holtam - has welcomed the news that the government will set itself a stricter target on climate change . <p> The government has promised to cut greenhouse gas emissions to almost zero by 2050 . <p> He said : " Christians and people of all faiths have long called for action on climate change to both preserve the natural world on which we all rely , and to protect God 's creation for generations to come . " <p> The new aim - known as " net zero " - means emissions from homes , farming , industry and transport will have to be cut to nothing , or offset by planting more trees or sucking CO2 from the atmosphere . <p> Bishop Nicholas said : " The impacts of climate change affect all of us , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weather events and the least able to cope with these impacts , making it imperative for us as Christians to push to action to deal with these threats .
