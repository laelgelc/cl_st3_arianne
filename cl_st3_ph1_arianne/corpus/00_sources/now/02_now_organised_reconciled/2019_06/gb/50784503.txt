<h> Letters : First Minister is guilty of rank hypocrisy when she advocates restoring our peatlands <p> THE First Minister advocates " restoring peatlands " as a component of tackling climate change ( " Scots told to speak up on tackling ' climate emergency ' " , The Herald , June 20 ) . The most significant damage to peatlands has been from wind farms ( also advocated by the First Minister ) which require bases and access roads . The First Minister has declined to acknowledge this , either through ignorance ( incompetence ) or deviousness ( deception by economy with truth ) . Hypocrisy is not a robust policy ; people notice . <p> The SNP is currently chanting in unison that Boris Johnson is not fit to be Prime Minister .
