<h> LETTER : 70 years of peace because of the EU <p> Anyone who 's watched Simon Reeve 's travelogue series about Vietnam will realise how hugely lucky we , in the UK , are to have experienced 70 years of peace since the end of the Second World War . <p> Some people may try to convince you that over 70 years ' peace in Europe is due to our membership of NATO . <p> EU ( 13132530 ) <p> However , do n't believe them . I can tell you , from personal experience , that NATO would never have held together had the EU not been created , knitting together , as it does , the economies of Europe and encouraging the major powers in Europe to work together on supranational issues , like climate change and global tax avoidance , which can not be solved by any one nation on its own . <p> The EU has already proven to be the greatest ever contribution to peace in the world , and we need to work collectively to preserve and improve it .
