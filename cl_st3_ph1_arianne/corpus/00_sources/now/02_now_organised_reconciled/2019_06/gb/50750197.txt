<p> Almost a third of Scots are unaware of the damage flying does to the environment , according to new research . <p> A survey found 31% did not know travelling by plane was contributing to climate change . <p> Meanwhile , a third ( 33% ) were unaware of the impact of taking the car for short trips , and of single-use plastic . <p> Censuswide Scotland published details of the survey in the wake of Holyrood 's Environment Committee meeting , insisting ministers must put in place clearer plans for action across the board to tackle the " catastrophic harm " being done to the environment . <p>
