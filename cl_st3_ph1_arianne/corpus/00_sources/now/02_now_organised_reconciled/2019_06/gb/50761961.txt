<p> The occupation of an oil rig by Greenpeace protesters has been brought to an end . <p> The environmental campaigners had first boarded the Transocean rig in the Cromarty Firth on Sunday evening . <p> The rig , under contract to BP , had been due to leave from near Invergordon , heading for the Vorlich oil field east of Aberdeen . <p> Police said nine people in total had been arrested in connection with the demonstration . <p> The last two men to be removed from the rig were aged 40 and 50 .
