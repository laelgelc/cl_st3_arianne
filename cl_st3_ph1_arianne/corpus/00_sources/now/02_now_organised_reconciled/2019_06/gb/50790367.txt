<h> Emily Eavis hopes Glastonbury will inspire fans to live more sustainably <p> Emily Eavis hopes measures at Glastonbury Festival such as banning single-use plastic bottles and encouraging the use of public transport will inspire fans to live more sustainably . <p> The co-organiser of the event , headlined by The Cure , The Killers and Stormzy this year , urged those attending to bring tents that could be reused for " a lifetime " instead of being dumped at the Somerset site . <p> Festival-goers are asked to use public transport to travel to Worthy Farm and to unplug any non-essential appliances at their homes before setting off . <p> They are discouraged from using disposable wipes , even those labelled biodegradable , and to bring reusable water bottles they can re-fill for free at taps and WaterAid kiosks . <p> There are only a small number of showers available to preserve water , with festival organisers suggesting visitors take a wash cloth and bar of soap for strip-washes instead . <p> " Sustainability and the need to live in harmony with the land , has always been vital to Glastonbury Festival , " Eavis said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to a rich natural habitat and it
