Online defamation and hate speech are illegal like their offline counterparts . Most YouTube videos are classified as " tele-media " and have to follow a number of broadcasting laws . In addition , they are often interpreted as being similar to television which obliges YouTubers to label paid content and abstain from paid political advertising ( none of which apply to the case at hand ) .
