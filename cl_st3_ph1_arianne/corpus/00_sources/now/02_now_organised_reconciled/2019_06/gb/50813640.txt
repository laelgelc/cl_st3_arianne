<h> Bishop of Oxford joins call for action on climate change <p> The Bishop of Oxford , Rt Revd Stephen Croft , joined thousands in Westminster on Wednesday to demand action on climate change . <p> ' Time Is Now ' saw 12,000 people gather to put pressure on politicians to act on the need for unprecedented action to tackle climate change . <p> Organisers from the Climate Coalition and Greener UK reported that people from 99 per cent of the UK 's constituencies signed up for the march . <p> In his latest blog , the Rt Revd Croft called for action to prevent " apocalyptic " damage , saying : " The time is now to arrest the emission of greenhouse gases which are causing such lasting damage to the Earth . " <p> " Life on Earth is about to change in apocalyptic ways during the remainder of this century if we continue to do next to nothing . " <p> " Setting the care for the earth again at the front and centre of our politics and our lives must be the priority if there is a fair and rich future for life on earth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after months of mounting demonstrations , including several in Oxford . <p> The demands are not going unnoticed : last month , the House of Commons approved a motion to declare an environmental emergency , while this week , the House of Lords debated a target to decrease greenhouse gas emissions to zero by 2050 . <p> Despite these advancements , activists continue to call for an Environment Bill to clean up polluted air and tackle plastic pollution . Greener UK published a draft of the bill in December . <p> The Environmental Bill would also include legally binding targets and an independent watchdog to enforce environmental laws .
