<h> People in Wales are more satisfied with the quality of GP care and education , but are increasingly concerned about climate change , according to the latest results from the National Survey published recently . <p> The National Survey for Wales is a face-to-face survey of 12,000 people across Wales . The study gathers information on many topics including health , schools , sports , art and culture . The 2018-19 survey also included a number of new questions on environmental issues and the Welsh language . <p> Results are used by the Welsh Government and other organisations across Wales to help inform decision and policy making to make Wales a better place to live . <h> Key results from the National Survey in 2018-19 include : <p> 83% of people in Wales are highly or very highly satisfied with their lives <p> 93% of people surveyed are satisfied with their GP care ( +7% from last year ) and 93% ( +3% ) are satisfied with the care they received at their last NHS hospital appointment <p> 93% of people think the world 's climate is changing .
