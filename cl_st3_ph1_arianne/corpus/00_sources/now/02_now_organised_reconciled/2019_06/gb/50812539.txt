<h> Unprecedented moves by universities to tackle UN SDGs <p> The University of Southern Denmark has made the United Nations ' Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) the overarching focus of its strategic plan , while in Sweden 36 universities have signed a climate framework in support of the Paris 2016 climate agreement , and in Norway the Bergen Summer Research School is addressing how research might impact on policies for a sustainable future . <p> The executive board of the University of Southern Denmark ( SDU ) on 17 June announced the largest transformation in the history of the university , deciding that the 17 UN SDGs were to become the focal point for SDU 's work as a university . <p> Niels Thorborg , chairman of the SDU board , said that all 17 SDGs are to be addressed : " Extreme poverty and hunger at global levels and reducing inequality and combating climate change are all clearly linked and require an integrated effort to achieve lasting results . " <p> SDU will launch 17 new one-year masters programmes , one for each of the 17 SDGs . SDU has 4,000 staff and 28,000 students and provides 170 different education programmes . <p> The aim of the SDGs are : <p> * Abolishing poverty and hunger in the world <p> * Reducing inequalities <p> * Ensuring good education and better health for all <p> * Slowing down climate change <p> * Providing more sustainable economic growth <p> * Promoting peace , security and strong international partnership . <p> SDU is now promoting these goals actively in research and educational programmes and in the public outreach of the university . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the SDGs are research focusing on vaccines in industrialised and developing countries ; drone technology ; reduction of endocrine disrupting chemicals ; ' CoC -playful minds ' composing guides for philosophical dialogues related to each of the 17 SDGs ; recycling and re-extraction of electrical equipment . <p> From the summer of 2019 SDU will invite students and employees to participate in an open forum exchanging ideas and initiatives , where 12 such ideas have been proposed .
