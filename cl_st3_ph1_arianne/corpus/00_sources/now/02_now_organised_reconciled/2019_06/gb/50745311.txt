<p> With climate change and pollution getting worse , and new threats like seabed mining on the horizon , our oceans urgently need help . <p> As part of our work to protect the oceans , in April we launched a 10-month Pole to Pole expedition from the Arctic to the Antarctic . As part of the expedition Greenpeace is sailing to the Arctic ice edge in Fram Strait , between Svalbard and Greenland , to collect various samples and measure the snow and ice thickness .
