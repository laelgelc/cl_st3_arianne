<p> Temperatures rose to record levels yesterday in one of the worst heatwaves to hit Europe , with several deaths reported and experts warning millions of people to expect worse because of climate change . <p> The Met Office said today was the hottest day of the year so far , with Charlwood in Surrey recording a temperature of 30.8C . The day is expected to get hotter , with highs of 34C forecast in southeast England . The record for the hottest June day was 35.6C , in 1976 . Bristol and Yorkshire are forecast to enjoy temperatures of about 30C , while the Met Office warned that thunderstorms could break out in southeast Scotland and northeast England this evening . <p> Temperatures have rocketed across Europe , to a new high in France <p> Yesterday the temperature reached more than 45C in France , the hottest ever recorded there .
