With me were my son and two good friends , and the Pilatus felt like a Messerschmitt 109 cutting through the clouds and landing on a dime . The Pilatus is a great airplane . It can cruise for seven hours at 280 knots , and land at less than 500 metres . It seats six people very comfortably . The only man who has complained about this aircraft is my old friend Charlie Glass , who like a true lefty whined about the lavatory 's headroom .
