<h> Teenagers will take the day off school to fight for future of the planet <h> Teenagers to take the day off school for climate protest in Dorchester <p> Youngsters will be taking the day off school to fight for the future of the planet . <p> A ' strike ' will be taking place in Dorchester tomorrow as part of a climate change protest . <p> Organisers say some schools are behind the initiative and allow pupils time off but say it is down to the individual whether they want to attend . <p> The protest is being called Youth Strike for Climate and is being organised by the groups Fridays For Future , Dorchester and the UK Student Climate Network . <p> The group was set up after Greta Thunberg , a 16-year-old Swedish activist , stood outside the Swedish parliament to protest against climate change . She has since gone on to become known worldwide for her activism , appearing on the front cover of Time magazine . <p> A spokesman for Fridays for Future , Dorchester said : " There 's a small group of us that organise these strikes every month , this month @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that we 're a part of , and students of all ages take part . We march through the town and either finish or start at the County Hall , where we speak to councillors . <p> " We have three demands -- for everywhere to declare a climate emergency and to act upon this , to implement this area into our education and make it compulsory for us to learn about how we can change our actions and the scary facts upon the climate and environmental issues . The third demand is to bring the voting age down to 16 , so that us , the young people , have a voice in our future . These demands are always the heart of the strike . " <p> One of the organisers is encouraging young people to take the day off for the strike . <p> He said : " The idea is to create a powerful image , where children choose their future and their home over their education . This is a small way of doing it , but so far it has created a great start @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ others wo n't . It 's all up to the child to decide whether they want to come or not . " <p> Fridays for Future aims to help battle climate change and is working towards tackling environmental issues , including plastic pollution .
