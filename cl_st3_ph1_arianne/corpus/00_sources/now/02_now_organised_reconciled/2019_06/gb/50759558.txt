<h> Is the future of meat animal-free ? <h> Report claims majority of meat people eat by 2040 will not come from slaughtered livestock <p> More than half the " meat " consumed around the world in 2040 will not come from slaughtered animals , amid growing unease at the environmental and ethical impact of the global food industry , a major report has predicted . <p> Based on interviews with industry experts , global consultancy firm AT Kearney estimates that in 20 years around 60% of all meat will either be cultured alternatives grown in vats or plant-based products that look and taste like meat . <p> It predicts that 35% of meat will be cultured , created " through exponential cell growth in bioreactors " and not involving animal slaughter .
