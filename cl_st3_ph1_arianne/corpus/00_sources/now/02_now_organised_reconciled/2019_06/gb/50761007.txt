This is one of the most difficult shocks we central bankers have to deal with . " <p> Central banks should include climate change risk in their economic models used in monetary policy , notably by pushing out their forecast horizons , he said . <p> However , they need to go even farther by taking climate risks into account when they assess the financial risks of collateral that banks post with them to get liquidity . Villeroy said this would represent " a very powerful change of our monetary policy " . <p> A network of some 40 central banks and financial sector regulators issued a warning about the financial risks relate to climate change at an event held at the Bank of France .
