<h> Main road to close to traffic for 24 hours as part of Clean Air Day efforts <p> A main road through Waltham Forest will close as part of Clean Air Day plans . <p> Hoe Street will be closed off to traffic all day on Sunday , September 22 as part of World Car Free Day events running across London which will see 20km of roads pedestrianised for 24 hours . <p> Waltham Forest Council hopes the event will challenge reliance on cars and highlight the need to improve air quality in the area , which is one of the authority 's long-term ambitions . <p> The council expects the event will boost the local economy and hopes to build on the previous successes of this year 's Welcome to The Forest event and the annual Leytonstone Car Free Day Festival . <p> The council also wants more local residents to arrange Street Parties , Play Streets and even Jumble Trails on the day , building on the regular events which already take place . <p> Cllr Clyde Loakes , deputy council leader and cabinet member for the environment , said : " We have seen already how @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ greater community interaction -- helping to achieve improved health and wellbeing , create opportunities for informal play , as well as attracting thousands of people to events like the London Borough of Culture launch event , Welcome to The Forest , in January . <p> " They also really support our local high streets and shopping parades .
