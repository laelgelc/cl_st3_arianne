<h> What Will World Happiness Look Like in 2050 ? <h> The world could be much happier or much less happy in three decades . <p> It seems as though people these days are increasingly ashamed about the legacy that current generations are leaving for the future . In the face of climate change and other challenges , parents seem to question whether life will be any good for their children and grandchildren . <p> Some say this will be a century of conflict , of desperate migration , and of increasing scarcity . <p> As we devote more attention to all of these concerns , and work to ensure that other species and natural systems can flourish ( or just survive ) , I also think that it 's of utmost importance to think about how life will be for humans . <p> So , how good will life be in 2050 ? Even without the huge uncertainties in environmental changes that will beset us , the scope of possible changes in biotechnology , artificial intelligence , and politics makes for an impossibly complex prediction task .
