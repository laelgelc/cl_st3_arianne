<h> UK becomes first major economy to pass net zero emissions law <p> Chris Skidmore signs legislation to commit the UK to a legally binding target of net zero emissions by 2050 <p> The UK today became the first major economy in the world to pass laws to end its contribution to global warming by 2050 . <p> The target will require the UK to bring all greenhouse gas emissions to net zero by 2050 , compared with the previous target of at least 80% reduction from 1990 levels . <p> The UK has already reduced emissions by 42% while growing the economy by 72% and has put clean growth at the heart of our modern Industrial Strategy . This could see the number of " green collar jobs " grow to 2 million and the value of exports from the low carbon economy grow to ? 170 billion a year by 2030 . <p> Energy and Clean Growth Minister Chris Skidmore said : <p> The UK kick-started the Industrial Revolution , which was responsible for economic growth across the globe but also for increasing emissions . <p> Today we 're leading the world yet again in becoming the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to net zero by 2050 while remaining committed to growing the economy - putting clean growth at the heart of our modern Industrial Strategy . <p> We 're pioneering the way for other countries to follow in our footsteps driving prosperity by seizing the economic opportunities of becoming a greener economy . <p> The UK 's 2050 net zero target -- one of the most ambitious in the world -- was recommended by the Committee on Climate Change , the UK 's independent climate advisory body .
