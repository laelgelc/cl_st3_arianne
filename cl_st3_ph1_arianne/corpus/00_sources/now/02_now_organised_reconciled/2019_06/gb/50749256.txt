<h> Could this much-loved green space be a new home for major firework display ? ( Possibly , but there are many concerns ) <p> A MUCH-loved green space has been suggested as a possible new location for a major firework display - but town councillors are wary about the idea . <p> Bridport Round Table has approached Bridport Town Council for a second time over the possibility of using Asker Meadows for its annual firework display - though the organisation has stressed the idea is in its infancy and wo n't happen any time soon . <p> The round table first asked the town council to consider the idea back in January , when councillors raised concerns about highway safety , the environmental impact , the proximity to residential properties and the availability of parking . <p> At that time , councillors said they were worried about smoke from fireworks causing visibility issues on the A35 .
