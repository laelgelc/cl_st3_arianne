He has worked for the Green party in Northern Ireland and Scotland -- as well as being an organiser for the Divest Parliament campaign which aims to get MPs at Westminster to divest their pension funds from fossil fuels . <p> " To me , Extinction Rebellion is another means of bringing about the change that is needed to tackle the climate emergency , " he said . " Only , it is n't just about changing politics or changing finance , its about system change .
