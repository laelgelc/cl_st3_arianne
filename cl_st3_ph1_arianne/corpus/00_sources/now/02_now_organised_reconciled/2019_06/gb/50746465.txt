<h> LETTER : We must halt the march toward destruction of our home planet <h> LETTER : We must halt the march toward destruction of our home planet <p> THE cynicism contained within comments made following the various articles run by The Westmorland Gazette regarding the student strikes for climate change is saddening . <p> We have known about climate change for a generation and yet a generation of politicians has failed to do anything of any consequence to halt the march toward destruction of our home planet . <p> It is these very students who will feel the initial impact of our lack of action . It is their children who could well find themselves in unimaginable circumstances due to our fence sitting . <p> It seems to me that many of the comments may have been made by the ill-informed ; by those who , if not climate change deniers , then those who have not digested the science and fail to realise the fact that catastrophic changes may come within a generation . <p> These changes , if left unfettered , would take 20,000 years to reverse . That 's not exaggeration , that 's not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a fact . We must take note and we must act . <p> Instead of making judgemental comments about the students ' use of mobile phones , their diets or their parents ' foreign holidays ( all based on absolute supposition by the way ) perhaps these ne'er sayers should use their energy in a more constructive way . <p> May I suggest that they look at their own lives and their own habits , seeking ways that they might make a difference rather than doing down those who are at least making an effort to secure a viable future ? <p> Well done to these well-intentioned and well-informed students who have taken time to show us that they must be listened to . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
