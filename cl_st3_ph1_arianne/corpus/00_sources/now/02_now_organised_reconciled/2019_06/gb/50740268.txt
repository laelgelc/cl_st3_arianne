In order to succeed , it is necessary to either stimulate nature to absorb more CO2 , or to create technology that does the same thing . <p> Going further , a country will become carbon negative when it regularly absorbs even more CO2 than it gives out . By 2050 , there will be two billion . <p> Based on 2018 figures , experts have worked out that for those two billion cars to be electric , annual production of neodymium and dysprosium would have to increase by 70% , copper output would need to more than double and cobalt output would need to increase at least three and a half times for the entire period from now until 2050 to satisfy the demand . <h> The other energy costs of electric cars <p> Electric vehicles come with an energy cost too .
