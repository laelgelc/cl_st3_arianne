<h> Flood resilience lessons for 400 schoolchildren from across the Lune Valley <p> Hundreds of pupils will learn about the risks and dangers of flooding and how they can help build up resilience for the future this week . <p> The University of Cumbria 's campus in Bowerham Road , Lancaster is hosting an event in partnership with the Lune Valley Flood Forum , welcoming up to 400 pupils from 11 primary schools . <p> Statutory agencies , charities , and nature and wildlife bodies including the National Flood Forum , Environment Agency , Met Office , Lancashire Fire and Rescue Service and Cumbria Wildlife Trust are playing major roles in this multi-agency event . <p> Six ' learning zones ' are being created for children to explore . They include a ' Flood Hub ' where children will discover how flooding affects people 's lives , with an opportunity to talk to residents whose homes have been affected and flood emergency groups from the local area . <p> Environmental organisations will be in the ' Systems Zone ' where young visitors will discover more about how river systems work .
