<h> Revealed : The 2019 Edinburgh Festival Fringe , with new venues , stars - and a record number of shows <p> This year 's Edinburgh Festival Fringe is once again even larger in size and scale than the year before : the 2019 programme , launched today , includes 3,841 shows , up from 3,548 in 2018 , and 59,600 performances , up from 56,796 . <p> The programme has a record 63 countries represented , and more than 700 free shows , with more than 400 ' pay what you want ' shows , an increase from 260 last year . <p> In the Fringe programme there are also 963 Scottish shows , with 744 coming from Edinburgh . <p> Once again the Fringe , which will run from 2 to 26 August , will be a perhaps overwhelming festival of theatre , dance , circus , physical theatre , comedy , music , musicals and opera , cabaret and variety , children 's shows , spoken word , free shows , exhibitions and other events . <p> The sheer scale of the festival is one again sure to spark debate over the size of the festival @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is based . <p> Last year , the Fringe issued 2.8 million tickets , a record , 5% higher than 2017 . <p> This year , it is on course , at the current rate of growth , to sell or distribute three million tickets . <p> This year the Fringe Society , which runs the annual festival in August , has introduced a new feature , the Inspiration Machine , " an interactive , arcade-style machine that will randomly display videos from Fringe artists at the push of a button .
