<p> The order was started by Queen Victoria in 1896 so that she could personally reward people who had helped her as a monarch . <p> During the reign of Edward VIII , in 1936 , the order was amended to admit women . <p> The highest class of the order is to be a Knight or Dame Grand Cross ( GCVO ) , which is what Kate Middleton has now been awarded . <p> The Royal Victorian Order , one pictured , is a Maltese cross surrounded by a blue ring <p> Other classes include Knight or Dame Commander ( KCVO or DCVO ) , Commander ( CVO ) , Lieutenant ( LVO ) , and Member ( MVO ) . <p> The two highest classes come with admission into knighthood if the awardee is not already a knight or a dame . This means they can be called sir or dame . <p> Once part of the Order , members are given a badge which is a Maltese cross surrounded by a blue ring and featuring a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ RVO and they are awarded for services to the monarch . Pictured is Elizabeth II 's hairdresser John Carmichael receiving his medal in 2017 <p> There are variations on the badge for each grade of the order . For example Knights and Dames Grand Cross wear the badge on a sash passing from the right shoulder to the left hip . <p> Their badge also has white enamel with gold edging , setting it apart from the other ranks . <p> Foreigners can also be part of the Royal Victorian Order , however their ribbon will differ from others and have an additional central white stripe . <p> The Order 's motto is ' Victoria , ' in reference to the founder and its chapel is the Savoy Chapel in London .
