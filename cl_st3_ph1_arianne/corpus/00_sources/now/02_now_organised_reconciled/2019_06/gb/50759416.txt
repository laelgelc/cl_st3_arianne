In case you missed it ... Boris is " not aiming for a no-deal outcome . " <p> You could try to read further and find out what that really means . It hides failings and malicious intent in plain sight . <p> Thus , the tousled , unkempt , blustering , old Etonian is their perfect candidate . Nothing like the miserable old guys normally associated with the elite . A social liberal , do n't you know , who supports gay marriage . This is not democracy . So dinnae sup . Dinnae even hurl your anger . <p> Instead strategise . <p> Because big change is coming beyond the currently stalled constitutional debate . And resolving it will be the making of our new nation . <p> Over recent weeks , I 've found myself explaining I ca n't write about truly interesting and planet-shaping issues because Brexit , Tory leadership elections and the like are hogging the limelight . The Greens want the roads cash spent on dualling the rail line north instead . <p> It 's a perfectly valid suggestion that demands a reply . So far none has been forthcoming .
