<h> A taste of the future if this bonkers climate change policy is n't scrapped <p> WHEN you commit yourself to meeting a target , you commit yourself to taking the necessary actions to reach that target . <p> Last Monday , our politicians unanimously waved through changes to the Climate Change Act ( 2008 ) which , if passed through the Lords , will commit us to cutting greenhouse emissions to net zero by 2050 . In doing so they committed us , up and down the country , to making changes in how we live which are ' unprecedented in their overall scale ' . These are the words of the Committee on Climate Change ( CCC ) which recommended the 2050 target . <p> The CCC has welcomed Parliament 's action with delight and will begin the busy work of ' providing advice on the detailed path to net zero ' . However , in recommending the 2050 target , the CCC has already done a great deal of this work . This is set out in the very report that recommends net zero and is used to justify why the CCC considers the target achievable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 277 pages and so they know full well what they have signed us up to . For comparison , we spend ? 40billion on schools per year . <p> The CCC is aware that the target is tough and therefore that the changes outlined in its report will mostly be necessary , not optional .
