<p> Honourable ladies and gentlemen , I am proud to be representing Pitcairn here today , and it is a privilege to listen to such expert and thought-provoking interventions . <p> Pitcairn is the smallest and most remote of the members of the Pacific Community . Situated in the middle of the Pacific Ocean , halfway between New Zealand and Chile , and two days away by boat from the nearest other human settlement . It has just 50 inhabitants . It is a community that has endured , against the odds , since 1790 , and is by necessity a community closely connected to the sea . <p> And despite its size and location , it is deeply committed to playing its part on these critical global issues under discussion today - and in particular in protecting our oceans , our sea life , and tackling the scourge of plastic pollution . <p> Pitcairn is proud to be home to an MPA of 835,000km2 .
