Life is hard for the city 's 600,000 residents -- much of the local economy is based on energy revenues , meaning it was hit particularly hard by the 2014 oil crash . Suffering food shortages , unemployment and a bootleg alcohol tragedy that has killed at least 76 , residents likely felt that there were few prospects left in this isolated , frozen wasteland . <p> In 2017 , however , they found a glimmer of hope in an unlikely place : the nascent bitcoin market . The process of ' mining ' for cryptocurrencies relies upon the one thing that Irkutsk has in spades -- access to cheap energy .
