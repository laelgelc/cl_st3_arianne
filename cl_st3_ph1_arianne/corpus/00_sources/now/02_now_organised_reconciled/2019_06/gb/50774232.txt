<h> Let London take back control of its finances <p> As many a City A.M. reader will know , London is different from the rest of the UK in a variety of ways -- and not just in terms of how it votes , its cultural mix , or its size and complexity . <p> There is also the amount of tax that London 's citizens and businesses pay . Recent figures put together by the Office for National Statistics highlight that the capital produces that rarest of economic entities : a fiscal surplus . <p> For 2017/18 , some ? 34bn of revenue raised in London was spent elsewhere in the country . That 's around ? 4,000 for every man , woman and child in the city . <p> Without that transfer , public expenditure in London could have been as much as a third higher . <p> London is one of only three regions where revenues raised locally are greater than public spending received , along with the South East and the East of England . London and the South East have recorded surpluses in 18 out of the last 19 years .
