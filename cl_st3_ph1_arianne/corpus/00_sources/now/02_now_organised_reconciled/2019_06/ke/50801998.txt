<p> The National Environment Tribunal on Wednesday revoked a licence issued by Nema for the Sh200 billion Lamu coal power plant . <p> Nema issued the impugned environmental and social impact assessment licence to Amu Power Company Ltd . The firm was expected to build the proposed 1050 MW coal plant in Kwasasi , Lamu . <p> The tribunal ruled that National Environmental Management Authority failed to consider its own regulations when issuing the license to AMU . It said it was unreasonable and the environmental agency ignored the set procedure . <p> Nema failed to inspect the engineering systems , adding that climate change was not considered while the study was being conducted . <p> " There was a casual approach by Nema in such a serious project , they , therefore , failed to carry out their mandate as required by law , " the tribunal ruled . <p> The tribunal said the only option was to stop the project and validate the environmental impact assessment report . <p> NET also ruled that there was no proper public participation before Nema gave AMU Power the go ahead .
