<p> As Kenya celebrated Madaraka Day on June 1 , the world was remembering ecotheologian Thomas Berry . Four days later , on June 5 , we marked World Environment Day , whose theme wasAir Pollution . <p> On Madaraka Day we commemorate the attainment of self-governance in 1963 after decades of British colonial rule . Madaraka is a Kiswahili word for freedom or independence . <p> Self-governance meant that we now had the powers to exercise control over our own affairs . But the control over own affairs is being tested by widespread corruption , the breakdown of our social web , ailing environment , and drought and famine , which are now threatening close to 23 counties . <p> This is an indication that drastic societal changes are needed to deliver the Kenya we want . Redefining our relationship with our natural resources is paramount . This forms the foundation for our survival on the planet .
