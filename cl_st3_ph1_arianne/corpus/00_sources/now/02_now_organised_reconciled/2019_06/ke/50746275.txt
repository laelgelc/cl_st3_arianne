One of the most interesting discoveries is the influence biases wield on our thinking and decision-making . For instance , the confirmation bias states that humans tend to listen more to information that supports a belief they already hold , rather than to information that contradicts existing beliefs . Another interesting bias -- the projection bias -- explains why humans often think that another person has the same behaviour , attitude or belief as themselves , even if there is no indication of such . These biases explain why we remember those who shout loudest rather than those calmly making rational arguments .
