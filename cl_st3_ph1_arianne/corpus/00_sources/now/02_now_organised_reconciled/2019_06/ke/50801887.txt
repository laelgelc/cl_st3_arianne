<h> Environment Tribunal stops Lamu coal project <h> June 26 , 2019 ( 3 days ago ) 4:51 pm <p> National Environment Tribunal observed that public participation and EIA study are the oxygen in any project . And that they ca n't excuse non-compliance/COURTESY <p> By JEMIMAH MUENI , NAIROBI , Kenya Jun 26 -- The controversial Lamu coal project has been stopped by the National Environment Tribunal ( NET ) , a week after activists held protests against its construction . <p> The ruling was issued Wednesday by bench of five judges of the Tribunal Court sitting in Nairobi . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Justice Mohammed Balala who read out the ruling said they had observed that environmental concerns raised by locals are valid because there was no environmental impact assessment conducted . <p> They also faulted the National Environmental Management Authority ( NEMA ) and AMU Power for failing to conduct public participation . <p> " The second respondent ( Amu power ) is hereby ordered to cease the construction of the coal plant in Lamu until a fresh Environment Assessment Impact and public participation is undertaken , and the outcome of the report be published publicly , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( NEMA ) acted in violation in issuing the first respondent ( Amu power ) with an operating license without having adequately involved Lamu residents in public participation as required by the law , " Balala ruled . <p> The tribunal also set aside the NEMA issued license . <p> " Conditions on license were generalized with no links to issues identified . Shows casual approach by NEMA to a serious issue , " the court found . <p> This ruling has come as a win not only for Lamu residents but also to activists and Environmentalists who have been opposing the construction of the coal power plant , arguing that Kenya does not need coal power plants , as it has plenty of renewable energy which is cheaper , cleaner and sustainable for future generations . <p> Environmentalists have cited coal as being dirty , highly polluting , contributing to climate change and an environmental hazard . <p> Katiba Institute also made submission before NET , highlighting five key issues which include air quality , climate change , health , public participation and the role and responsibility of NEMA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ project that will have a negative impact on the country 's air quality , with accompanying adverse impacts on human health and biodiversity . <p> They further contended that the project will additionally contribute to adverse climate change impacts and that it was inconsistent with Kenya 's low carbon development goals and commitments . <p> Mark Odanga , a lawyer from a non-governmental organization which has been supporting Katiba Institute in this case , said that Wednesday 's win is not just for Lamu but for Kenyans because of its environmental concerns . <p> Odanga said that failure to have sufficient public participation , denied Lamu residents who will be highly impacted by the coal project an opportunity to fully understand the potential impact of the project . <p> " This was a great victory in so many levels in terms of upholding certain values of sustainable development under the constitution , amplifying and recognizing the voices and concerns of people of Lamu , so that this is no longer just seen as a crusade against development , but need to open a public debate about weighing the costs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are concerns sufficient protections are put in place so that development is
