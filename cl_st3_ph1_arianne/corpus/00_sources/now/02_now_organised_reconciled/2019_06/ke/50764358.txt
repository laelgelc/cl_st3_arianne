PHOTO FILE NATION MEDIA GROUP <h> In Summary <p> The scientists will study different trends with the aim of strengthening climate knowledge gaps on the continent in relation to sensitive socio-economic sectors . <p> Seven East African scientists are among 21 researchers who will receive up to Ksh12.8 million ( $128,000 ) each from a UN-supported initiative in the next two years to conduct different studies that will prepare the continent to deal with the impacts of climate change . <p> Through a programme known as Climate Research for Development ( CR4D ) , the scientists will study different trends with the aim of strengthening climate knowledge gaps on the continent in relation to sensitive socio-economic sectors . These include agriculture and food security , health , disaster risk reduction , energy , and natural resources management ( water , forests and others ) , gender , migration , urbanisation ,
