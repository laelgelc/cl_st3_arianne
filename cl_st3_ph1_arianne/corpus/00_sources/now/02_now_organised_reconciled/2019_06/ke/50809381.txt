Instead , farmers are likely to be offered expensive GM ( Bt ) cotton seeds produced by giant multinational companies . <p> It is important to note that the abbreviation " Bt " was corrupted at the ceremony to mean biotechnology cotton . This is incorrect . <p> MOLECULAR LEVEL <p> Bt stands for Bacillus thuringiensis , which is a naturally occurring bacteria that produces a toxin that kills pests , including cotton leaf worm . <p> Genes from the bacteria are infused into the cotton genome in the lab at molecular level . <p> This process is called genetic modification and falls within the broader family of biotechnology sciences but has a clear distinction from other biotechnology practices and technologies . It is also falsely argued that it is a form of breeding . <p> The biggest mistake Kenya could ever make is to believe that the commercialisation of Bt Cotton is a " direct intervention " to solve the problems of farmers and their livelihoods . <p> GM cotton , which has been commercialised in some African countries , has failed to meet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a right to know and understand more about this technology . They have a right to decide on whether to allow GMOs on our farms and plates . <p> CLIMATE CHANGE <p> GMO cotton will end up on our plates , because it is used to make cottonseed oil and cotton seed cake ( used for animal feeds ) . <p> We can not talk about sustainable livelihoods and at the same time undermine the ability of farmers to own , use and freely breed indigenous seeds . <p> Locally produced seeds , which are better adapted to our environment , allow farmers to mitigate against the effects of climate change and increasingly unpredictable market trends . <p> Pest management can be easily controlled through functional biodiversity , and integrated organic practices . <p> Despite ongoing research and without certainty on the outcomes , the best actions the government can take are to give farmers access to conventional seeds ; provide reliable extension services and ensure proper management of buying agreements with manufacturers .
