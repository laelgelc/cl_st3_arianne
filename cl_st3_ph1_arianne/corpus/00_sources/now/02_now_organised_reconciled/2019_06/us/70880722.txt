<p> Democratic lawmakers have called the bill the " Clean Energy Jobs Bill , " in an effort to emphasize the potential creation of new jobs from a transition to green energy . <p> Republicans have focused criticism on the impact of that transition : in effect forcing fossil fuel prices higher to reduce consumption .
