<p> Serious Radiohead fans just got a gift -- one that they never expected and that the band never meant to give them . Eighteen hours of previously unreleased Radiohead tracks began circulating on the web last week -- only because someone stole a treasure trove of music that Radiohead frontman Thom Yorke kept on 1990s-era MiniDiscs . <p> Whoever stole the recordings from Yorke first demanded a $150,000 ransom in exchange for returning the music and not releasing it online . On Tuesday , the band went ahead and officially released all 18 MiniDisc recordings on Bandcamp , where fans can stream the music for free or pay about $22 for digital copies . The proceeds will benefit a U.K.-based climate advocacy group , Extinction Rebellion . The tracks will only be up for 18 days . <p> " We got hacked last week -- someone stole Thom 's minidisk archive from around the time of OK Computer , " Radiohead guitarist Jonny Greenwood said in a statement on Twitter .
