<h> US military is world 's ' single largest producer ' of greenhouse gases -- report <p> The Pentagon is the " single largest producer of greenhouse gases in the world , " according to a new study about climate change that accuses the Trump administration of being in " various modes of denial " about it . <p> The report , from Brown University 's ' Costs of War ' project , focuses specifically on " post-9/11 wars " and their impact on emissions . It estimates the US military has been responsible for 1,212 million metric tons of greenhouse gases between 2001 and 2017 . Emissions from " overseas contingency operations " in Afghanistan , Pakistan , Iraq and Syria accounted for more than 400 million metric tons of CO2 . In 2017 alone , the report says , " the Pentagon 's emissions were greater than all emissions from Sweden or Denmark .
