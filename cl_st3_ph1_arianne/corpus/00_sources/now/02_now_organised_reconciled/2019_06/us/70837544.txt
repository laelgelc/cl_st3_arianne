The Great Dying saw a warming increase of four or five times that . <p> But it happened gradually . So Deutsch thought , Why not re-create the hothouse of the Great Dying in a computer , a model that simulates the warming , and see how present-day species in the ocean would fare ? He could crank up the carbon dioxide -- that would in turn raise temperatures and lower oxygen in the oceans . Then he could watch as parts of the ocean started to become deadly . <p> " The first thing that happens is that you start to see a local loss of species as they begin to move in response to the climate heating up , " he says . <p> But some parts of the planet were more forgiving .
