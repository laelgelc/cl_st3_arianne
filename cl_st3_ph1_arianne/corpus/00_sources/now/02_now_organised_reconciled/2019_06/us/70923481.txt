<h> Sterling Democrats to hear resolution asking for support of right whale conservation <h> Sunday <p> Jun 30 , 2019 at 5:07 AM <p> Just recently , a North Atlantic right whale was found dead in the Gulf of Saint Lawrence . Named Wolverine for three parallel scars slashed across his back by a ship strike , he was the first death this year of this critically endangered species . Tragically , he likely will not be the last . <p> The North Atlantic right whale is the official marine mammal of Massachusetts , and is one of the most endangered large whale species . Centuries of hunting have been replaced by the modern dangers of rope entanglement and propeller strikes . Human activity remains the greatest threat to the whales , particularly climate change , underwater noise pollution ( like the seismic blasts used to test for offshore oil drilling ) , ship strikes and entanglement in fishing gear . <p> The right whale population continues to drop , dwindling to a mere 410 . Last year , not a single calf was born . Jim McGovern , the SAVE Act is a bipartisan effort that codifies innovative and essential standards for shipping speed and whale monitoring . <p> The SAVE act would provide critical funding for whale-safe gear that would ensure an ocean that sustains both the right whale and the fishing industry for the hardworking men and women who make a living on the water .
