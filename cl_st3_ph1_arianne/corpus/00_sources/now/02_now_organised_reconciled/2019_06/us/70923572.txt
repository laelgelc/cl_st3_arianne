The same with demanding divestment in fossil fuel company stocks . <p> Some of the new proposals to address climate change put me mind of the debate between faith and works , especially when they seem more for demonstration purpose than actually reducing emissions . Numerous governments have suggested phasing out all carbon-based electricity generation or all petroleum-fueled vehicles by a point decades into the future , and these tend to be hailed by activists as representing , if not solutions , then great strides forward .
