<h> The fierce urgency of now : Toward a new multilateralism <p> In his " I Have a Dream " and " Beyond Vietnam " speeches , the Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King spoke of a " fierce urgency of the now " for " vigorous and positive action " on civil rights , peace , and social justice . Today , such action is just as urgent to tackle polarizing inequality , financial instability , and a breakdown of the climate system . <p> The 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and the Paris Agreement on climate change can serve as a 21st century mission for vigorous and positive action . A strong coalition will be needed to compensate for the U.S. and others that appear hostile to building a multilateral compact for financial stability , social inclusion , and carbon neutrality . <p> But some G-20 countries have shown they can lead . As the U.S. has signaled its intent to withdraw from the Paris Agreement , others such as China , Germany , and the U.K. are leading by phasing out fossil fuels , investing in clean technologies and advancing green industrial policies ; but actions at home must be globalized .
