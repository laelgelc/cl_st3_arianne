<h> Birthright Trips , a Rite of Passage for Many Jews , Are Now a Target of Protests <p> Halfway through a 10-day tour in Israel , Risa Nagel had a decision to make . <p> The 25-year-old grant writer from Seattle had hiked the hills of Galilee and wandered the ancient market in Jerusalem . " Do you want to come ? " <p> Ms. Nagel agonized . The next day , after the group held a moment of silence at the Western Wall , her friends announced that they were walking off .
