This sets the stage for daily showers and thunderstorms timed to the late afternoon and evening . <p> A clear focus for storms is lacking , with the nearest front retreating northward into northern Pennsylvania through Thursday . The jet stream aloft has weakened , but small ripples of energy ( often hard to identify ) will stream through , triggering off batches of storms now and then . <p> The overall flow is progressive , meaning storms will be on the move ( as opposed to stalling out ) , but the very deep , moist atmospheric layer means locations that receive one or more cells in succession could pick up locally heavy rainfall , as much as two to three inches . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and modest levels of wind shear ( the increase in winds with altitude , which helps to organize longer-lived , stronger storm cells ) raise once again the specter of isolated strong to severe thunderstorms . <p> According to the Weather Service 's Storm Prediction Center ( SPC ) , Tuesday 's threat is greatest in the direction of Baltimore and northeastern Maryland , which is in the slight risk zone ( level 2 out of 5 ) .
