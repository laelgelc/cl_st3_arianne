<h> India to set up RE capacity of 500 GW by 2030 <h> Our Bureau New Delhi Updated on June 25 , 2019 Published on June 25 , 2019 <h> India aims to set up 500 GW of renewable energy generation capacity by 2030 , Aanand Kumar , Secretary , Ministry of New and Renewable Energy , said . <h> " India would have installed 175 GW of RE capacity by 2022 without taking into account large hydro and 225 GW including large hydro . By 2030 , India plans to establish 500 GW of renewable energy capacity . " Kumar said at the 17th meeting of the International Renewable Energy Agency ( IRENA ) Council held in Abu Dhabi . <h> Kumar said fighting against climate change and adoption of renewable energy is a matter of faith and commitment for India .
