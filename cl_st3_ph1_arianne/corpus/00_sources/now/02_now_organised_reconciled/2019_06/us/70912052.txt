<h> City of Kamloops aligning with global climate change goal <p> City council set a goal for staff to align Kamloops efforts on climate change with the global effort to limit the temperature rise to 1.5 C. <p> Coun. Arjun Singh brought the motion forward at Tuesday 's ( June 26 ) council meeting that a strategic goal be set , along with mandating staff to outline actions to reduce Kamloops ' portion of greenhouse gasses ( GHG ) . During the debate on the motion , Singh argued waiting for other governments is going is n't acceptable . <p> " Sometimes I hear people say , if China does n't do it , or India does n't do it or if Alberta does n't do it , what 's the point of us doing it , " he says . " I think that logic , if you go through the whole thought process , no one does anything .
