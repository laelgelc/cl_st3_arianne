<p> Jay Inslee entered the presidential race as the climate candidate and on Monday , he released his most aggressive plan to deal with the crisis yet . <p> His so-called Freedom from Fossil Fuel plan shows how the U.S. government could go toe-to-toe with the fossil fuel industry and start to wind it down . The plan includes ending subsidies for the industry , banning oil exports and phasing out fracking , and putting a fee on greenhouse gas emissions as well as supporting states and cities suing Big Oil for damages . It also includes a strong commitment to support fossil fuel workers and frontline communities .
