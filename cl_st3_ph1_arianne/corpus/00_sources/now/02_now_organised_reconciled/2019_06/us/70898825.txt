<h> The Nordic Biennale Of Contemporary Arts Celebrates Its 10th Edition <p> " Honest . Pure . Raw . " This slogan in the arrivals hall of Oslo Airport welcomes everyone to Norway . Just an hour away , the 300-year old town of Moss is hosting The Emotional Exhibition : the 10th edition of the Nordic Biennale of Contemporary Arts now through October 2 , 2019 . Curators Marti Manen and Anne Klontz had a challenging task of inviting new names and perspectives while honoring the anniversary occasion for a best-of retrospective . Momentum 10 gathered twenty-nine artists from across Scandinavia and beyond . Given the Nordic reputation for stoicism , the emotional theme may seem counter-intuitive . However , Edvard Munch 's iconic painting The Scream still best reflects our anxious times and his solo exhibition has just opened in Saudi Arabia , the first in the Middle East for this Norwegian master . Munch had spent several productive years in Moss and the local Refsnes Gods Hotel even displays eight original works in its dinning hall .
