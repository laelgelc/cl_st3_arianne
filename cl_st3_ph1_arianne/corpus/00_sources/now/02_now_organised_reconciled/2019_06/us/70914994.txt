This August , he will become a professor in the University of Maine Presque Isle 's agriculture program as the Robert Vinton Akeley Chair of Agricultural Science and Agribusiness . The professorship is an endowed position named after the late Presque Isle potato breeder and made possible with a $1 million donation from his daughter , Mary Barton Akeley Smith . <p> " It 's a relatively new program .
