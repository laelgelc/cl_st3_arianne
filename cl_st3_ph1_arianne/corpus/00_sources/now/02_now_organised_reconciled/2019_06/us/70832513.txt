In order to honor Indigenous Peoples ' rights to make decisions about their traditional territories , stabilize the global climate , and protect threatened species , safeguards around forestry in Canada are urgently needed . The industrial footprint in Canada must stop expanding into intact forests in order to prevent ongoing boreal caribou declines and significant ecosystem carbon loss . To avoid significant harm to their brands , major tissue companies including Procter &amp; Gamble , Kimberly-Clark , and Georgia-Pacific will need to reduce their reliance on pulp from intact forests , commit to increasing recycled content in their products , and ensure that where they do obtain Canadian wood and pulp , forestry operations are FSC-certified with strong implementation and robust audits .
