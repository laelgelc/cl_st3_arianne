These should gradually coalesce into larger storm complexes that will then drift eastward toward the metro area . <p> The atmosphere is destabilizing , although some mid-level clouds may have slowed that process a bit . There is sufficient moisture in the lower atmosphere to support heavy convective showers and storms , but the atmosphere overall is drier than in the severe thunderstorm events of the past week . <p> Wind shear , the increase in wind speed with altitude , is also considerably weaker today than in the past week .
