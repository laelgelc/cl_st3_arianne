Fortunately , for the wider ecosystem , other single-celled organisms are quick to gobble up most of these compounds before they reach the surface . <p> These hydrocarbons , called alkanes , are chemically similar to diesel . If the other bacteria did n't consume the compounds , the cyanobacteria would effectively create oil spills . Precisely what these alkanes are doing in the bodies of cyanobacteria -- and why every known species of cyanobacteria produces them -- is a mystery .
