Photograph : Universal History Archive/Universal Images Group via Getty Images <p> The new film Apollo 11 about the first spaceflight to the moon is due for release in Ireland this coming weekend . It consists solely of high-quality archival footage , without narration nor interviews . Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin stepped onto the moon on July 21st , 1969 , and so we are just a few weeks away from the 50th anniversary . The film trailer shows in particular the intensity of the crew as they " suit-up " to prepare for the launch . The immensity of the challenge ahead is written all over their expressions . <p> As a young engineering undergraduate , I had the extraordinary privilege of meeting Neil Armstrong in person , having a short conversation with him in 1976 . Astronomical events , space technology and research are actively followed and debated . Eirsat-1 , Ireland 's first satellite , has been developed at UCD with support from the European Space Agency ( ESA ) . It should be launched next year . <p> The National Space Strategy report notes that 67 Irish companies and organisations collaborated with ESA last year . The space sector in Ireland employed 2,000 people in 2014 , and this is expected to rise to over 4,500 by next year . This should then reduce the very lengthy development and engineering cycles hitherto in the space industry . Shorter development timescales would in turn make it more commercially feasible for smaller companies and venture-backed start-ups to increase their participation in the sector . <h> Opportunities <p> The opportunities in the space industry reinforce our own focus on world-class technology , and high-skilled and sustainable employment . They also further strengthen opportunities for international collaboration , and thus to showcase the quality of Irish engineering and scientific research . <p> One of the most significant agreements with ESA is Irish access to data about our planet collected by Earth observation sensors , both space and terrestrial , via the EU 's Copernicus programme .
