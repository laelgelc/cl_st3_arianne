<p> Washington , D.C. -- Today , the Trump administration released its final version of the so-called Affordable Clean Energy ( ACE ) rule , a repeal of the Obama-era Clean Power Plan ( CPP ) . While the CPP would have fought climate change by reducing carbon pollution from power plants , the ACE rule fails spectacularly in achieving that goal . In response , Christy Goldfuss , senior vice president for Energy and Environment at the Center for American Progress , issued the following statement : <p> The dirty power scam finalized today fails to take action on climate change and would instead increase the pollution that endangers millions of Americans with asthma and other lung conditions . After a year of record-breaking hurricanes , extreme wildfires , and devastating floods , it is clearer every day that we can not delay action on climate change . Yet President Donald Trump and his EPA administrator , Andrew Wheeler , have done everything they can to reverse climate action and remain more focused on doing favors for their coal company friends than protecting the public . Just this year , Trump and Wheeler plan to undo protections for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate commitments under the Paris agreement , and roll back the commonsense clean car standards . It 's time for this administration to listen to the voices of the future demanding action on this crisis and moving our country toward a 100 percent clean energy economy instead of siding with polluters and the voices of the past .
