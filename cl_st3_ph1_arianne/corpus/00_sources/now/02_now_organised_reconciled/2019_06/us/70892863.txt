<h> City of Seattle : How would you upgrade the Ballard Bridge ? <p> SEATTLE - A new survey is asking Seattle residents how they would like to prioritize updates to the 102-year-old Ballard Bridge : Biking , walking , or driving . <p> The Ballard Bridge was built back in 1917 , and now , is due for what the city calls " significant rehabilitation . " The bridge itself is described as being in " good condition , " with the planned updates largely for ensuring it 's up to modern standards for every mode of transportation . <p> " We 've launched the Ballard Bridge Planning Study to evaluate how to bring the bridge up to current transportation , functional , and structural standards including improved bicycle and pedestrian facilities and keeping buses and freight moving , " the project 's page reads . <p> Funded by the Move Seattle Levy , the city will weigh " rehabilitation and replacement options for the long-term future of the bridge .
