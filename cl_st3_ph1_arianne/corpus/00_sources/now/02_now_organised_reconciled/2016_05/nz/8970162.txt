<h> NZ seeds arrive at ' doomsday ' vault <p> The first ever samples of New Zealand seeds to be stored in the Arctic 's underground global seed vault are arriving on the Svalbard archipelago today . <p> The entrance of the Svalbard Global Seed Vault .
