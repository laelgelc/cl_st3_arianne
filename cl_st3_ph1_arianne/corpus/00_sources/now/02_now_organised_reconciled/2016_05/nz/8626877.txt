<p> Imagine this : alongside the nutritional details listing sugar , fat , calorie and salt content , the labelling on your food shows the volume of greenhouse gases released in its production . <p> Or this : Fonterra , rather than being just a dairy company , is reinvented as a protein company , with an array of plant-based products like oat milk and nut burgers . Or that the supermarket snack-food aisle offers high-protein insect bars , roasted fava beans and other products developed by clever entrepreneurs alert to the demands of the carbon-conscious consumer . <p> It sounds fanciful , but the point is that when it comes to greenhouse gas emissions , food matters . Around half of New Zealand 's emissions are from agriculture -- mainly methane from ruminants such as dairy cows , beef cattle , sheep and deer .
