<h> Industry 's ETS subsidy axed ; $25 a tonne carbon cap remains <p> Electricity producers , transport fuel providers and industries with high carbon emissions will lose access to the subsidy that requires them to offset only half their emissions , Climate Change Minister Paula Bennett announced in the Budget . <p> Major emitters who do n't face export competition are subject to a ' one-for-two ' subsidy scheme over the next three years , with a benefit to the government in avoided costs of $356 million over the next four years , assuming carbon valued at $12 a tonne . <p> The subsidy elimination was widely anticipated . Mrs Bennett also announced there will be no change to the upper limit for the price of New Zealand units of carbon of $25 a tonne . <p> " We looked at it closely but think that $25 is fair and reasonable , " Mrs Bennett told BusinessDesk . " Certainly , at around that $14-15 mark we 're going to be growing a lot of trees and forestry starts stepping in .
