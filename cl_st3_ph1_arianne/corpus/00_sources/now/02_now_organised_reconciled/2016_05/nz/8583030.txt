<h> Editorial : Govt made the right call by keeping Kiwirail <p> Kiwirail floated the possibility of closing the national rail network . <p> OPINION : Kiwirail backed the idea of shutting down the railways . The turkey voted for Christmas ? . <p> There was no way for the national rail network to spin a profit in the next ten years , and so the " highest value " commercial option would be to shut it all down , except for Auckland and Wellington passenger services , the Kiwirail board concluded . <p> This offer followed an exhaustive nine-month review of possible futures for the rail business , conducted in 2014 but released last week . <p> Perhaps it was a sober assessment of the economics of a challenging industry . Or perhaps Kiwirail bosses were sick of the Government 's indecisive , short-term funding of the state-owned enterprise , and wanted to force a decision on its future . <p> If it was the latter , it worked , at least partially .
