<h> The growing influence of ESG on Asian indices and investments <p> Nowadays , the consideration of environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) factors weigh heavily on investment decisions and the creation of indices . <p> A few years ago , a commonly expressed view in Asia seemed to be that " if we find evidence that ESG contributes positively to investment returns , we will embrace the approach further " . <p> Jessie Pak , managing director ( Asia ) at FTSE Russell , says that more recently , this trend has slowly evolved to consider ESG more holistically as part of the investment process . " One big catalyst is the changing interpretation of fiduciary duty , most notably in the updated interpretation of how environmental and social factors are considered in relation to stewardship responsibilities , " she explains . <p> Ms. Pak points out that FTSE Russell has launched a number of ESG-focussed indices with various exchange partners in Asia , such as the FTSE4Good Bursa Malaysia Index , as well as supporting some of the continent 's large institutional investors such @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is an issue that investors are taking increasingly seriously , and we are particularly starting to see increased appetite in the Asian market , " she remarks . <p> According to Shirley Low , head of Asia Pacific at STOXX Ltd , her firm is also seeing more demand for sustainable investment-related products in Asia ; she says Australia remains as the forerunner for sustainable investment in the Asia-Pacific region . <p> " ESG investing is also in the development process with many large investors moving from pure negative screening to active engagement . This will increase the ' activism ' and influence how a company acts globally , not only focussing on its own profitability but also on how their decisions will impact the world environment or social issues . Over the long term , the company 's ESG score may have an impact on its share price , " she notes . <p> At S&amp;P Dow Jones Indices ( S&amp;P DJI ) , Tim Edward , senior director of index investment strategy , notes that especially on the topic of climate change , politicians are evaluating a wide range @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to laws clarifying the precise role of a " fiduciary " .
