<h> Letters <h> Impact of food wastage <p> THE United Nations Food and Agriculture Organisation estimates that about 795 million of the world 's 7.3 billion population suffer from chronic undernourishment now . Yet , about a third of the food worldwide goes to waste . <p> Food waste or food loss is food that is discarded or lost or left uneaten . The causes of food waste or loss are numerous , and occur at the stages of production , processing , retailing and consumption . <p> In developing countries , most losses occur during production while in developed countries food is wasted mainly at the consumption stage . Most Malaysians waste food at the consumption stage , especially during festive occasions . <p> According to the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute ( Mardi ) , Malaysians waste about 15,000 metric tonnes of food daily . Of this , 3,000 metric tonnes are untouched leftovers that are still edible . This amount is equivalent to three decent meals a day for 5.3 million people . <p> Meanwhile , according to the National Solid Waste Management Department @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 36,000 tonnes of garbage Malaysians generate annually . <p> It is estimated that the volume of food waste in peninsular Malaysia by 2020 would fill up the entire Petronas Twin Towers 16 times . <p> Surprisingly , the largest contributor of solid waste are households . <p> Wasting food is not only an ethical and economic issue but also an environmental concern . On the economic side , it threatens food security through depletion of the limited natural resources . The agriculture sector uses a vast amount of fresh water for growing crops . <p> According to the World Water Assessment Programme , agriculture accounts for 70% of all water use around the world . Food preparation also requires a plentiful supply of water . <p> Furthermore , farm equipment used to feed and maintain livestock and to plant and harvest crops use diesel and other fuel sources . When people waste meat and vegetables , the resources used in their production will also be wasted . Thus , wasting food also means wasting energy . <p> Food waste also needs to be transported to treatment plants or incinerators , hence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ food wasted , the more fuel is needed for transportation and for processing . <p> According to the Waste &amp; Resources Action Programme ( WRAP ) , a charity based in England , the embedded energy costs of producing , packaging and transporting food is equivalent to at least 15 million tonnes of carbon dioxide every year . <p> Rotting food releases methane gas , one of the most harmful greenhouse gases(GHG) that has been found to contribute to climate change . Methane is 23 times more potent than CO2 as a greenhouse gas . <p> Surprisingly , according to our Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) , less than 3% of food waste was recovered and recycled in 2010 , leaving 97% in the landfills to produce methane . <p> Reducing food waste has clear benefits for climate change mitigation . According to a WRAP 2015 report , an astonishing 7% of all global greenhouse gas emissions , or 3.3 billion tonnes per year , are due to food waste . <p> WRAP estimates that by 2030 , GHGs could be reduced by at least 0.2 and possibly as much @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ waste . <p> Everyone must be involved in food waste reduction . Start at home since the majority of food waste occurs here . For example , make a list of meals weekly before shopping and cook the exact quantity required . <p> Government and business entities must also plan the best strategies to address the problem of food wastage . Educational programmes and awareness campaigns should be implemented more actively to remind consumers of the impact of food wastage to the domestic economy .
