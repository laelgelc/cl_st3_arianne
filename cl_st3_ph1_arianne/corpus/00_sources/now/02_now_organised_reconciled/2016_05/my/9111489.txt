<h> Wetlands help slow down global warming <p> I WAS surprised to read that 90 per cent of our freshwater swamps have been drained for rice cultivation . Experts know that the destruction of wetland ecosystems will generate massive greenhouse gas emissions in future . <p> Experts at an international wetlands conference in Brazil in 2008 issued this warning . <p> While wetlands cover only six per cent of the world 's surface , they -- marshes , peat bogs , swamps , river deltas , mangroves , tundra , lagoons and river floodplains -- are estimated to hold 771 megatonnes of greenhouse gases , or 10 to 20 per cent of the globe 's terrestrial carbon . <p> Wetlands provide many environmental services , including water filtration and storage , erosion control , a buffer against flooding , nutrient recycling , biodiversity maintenance and a nursery for fisheries . <p> Drainage and destruction of these ecosystems is responsible for large amounts of carbon emissions .
