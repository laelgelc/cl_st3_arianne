<h> Tech News <h> Electric bikes blaze a trail <p> The next time you get a delivery from Kentucky Fried Chicken ( KFC ) , the rider carrying your meal might just be on an environmentally friendly electric scooter . <p> Eclimo Sdn Bhd is designing and assembling the scooters in Bayan Lepas , Penang , and has leased 300 to KFC . <p> Despite a hefty price tag of RM18,800 ( excluding GST ) , the company claims it has sold 450 electric scooters , called the ES-11s , to the police in Kuala Lumpur , the Municipal Council of Penang Island and individuals . <p> " They are concerned about the environment and willing to contribute to fight climate change , " said Eclimo director Datuk Dennis Chuah . <p> KFC Delivery staff posing on the eco-friendly electric scooter . <p> The ES-11 has even garnered overseas interest . Eclimo plans to sell 100 units of the ES-11 to Cambodia this year to ferry tourists around the Angkor Wat area . <p> The ES-11 offers fuss-free charging . Chuah claims that the electric bikes do not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ any AC wall outlet . <p> " It takes four hours to fully charge an ES-11 and costs just RM1 for a full charge . A fully charged ES11 electric scooter can reach a maximum speed of around 100kph , " he said . <p> " The lithium-ion battery ( made in Seremban ) lasts about three to five years , " said Chuah who has previously worked on lithium batteries for radio controlled toys . <p> Eclimo recycles these battery cells which can then be used by Pasar Malam operators , rural area residences ( as it can be charged by solar panels ) and disaster areas . <p> " The used battery packs can also be used to replace petrol generators , " he said . <p> But Eclimo is not going to stop at just selling the ES-11 models , as it 's working on a " more sports-style electric motorcycle " .
