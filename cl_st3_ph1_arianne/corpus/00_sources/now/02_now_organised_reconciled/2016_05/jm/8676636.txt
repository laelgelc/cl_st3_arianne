<h> News <h> Regional countries developing sanitary and phytosanitary measures <p> BELMOPAN , Belize ( CMC ) -- Caribbean countries have a living bank of marine resources from which they collectively cash out hundreds of millions of dollars a year to support emerging national economies by providing good jobs , food and foreign exchange , among other benefits . <p> However , in order to remain active and competitive in the global marketplace , countries have had to find ways to surmount the challenges posed by stringent international standards , called sanitary and phytosanitary ( SPS ) measures , for food safety and for protection against diseases carried by animals and plants . <p> Under a European Union-funded SPS Measures Project , the ability of Caribbean countries to effectively address those challenges is being strengthened through initiatives such as specialised training for those gatekeepers who help to ensure the safety of both imported and exported foods .
