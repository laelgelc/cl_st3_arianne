They focused on two subjective factors that influence consumers : the feeling of regret that stems from either failing to cash in on a discount or missing out on a potential purchase , on the one hand , and the misperception of the products ' future availability , on the other . The JCPenney switch to consistently lower prices did not yield the revenue bump its management had anticipated .
