<h> New maps shed light on the secret lives of clouds <p> Clouds might seem like a nuisance if you 're headed on a Sunday afternoon picnic . But put aside your personal biases for a second and consider this : Clouds can also tell the story of life on Earth . <p> That story has become a lot clearer thanks to new maps created by scientists that document a global year in the clouds in more intimate detail than ever before . The maps -- a cloud atlas if you will -- provide a fine-grained view of how clouds move around in our atmosphere and represent an important link between climate and ecological research .
