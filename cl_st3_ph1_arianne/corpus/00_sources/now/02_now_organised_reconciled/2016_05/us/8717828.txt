Photo by Sean Whaley/Review-Journal <p> By SEAN WHALEYLAS VEGAS REVIEW-JOURNAL CAPITAL BUREAU <p> MINDEN -- The three Democratic candidates seeking a primary win in June to take on Rep. Mark Amodei in the 2nd Congressional District in November agree on one point : The GOP incumbent from Carson City is out of touch with many of his constituents . <p> At a candidate forum here last week , Vance Alm , Rick Shepherd and Chip Evans spoke to the Douglas County Democratic Women about why they are the best choice to face off against Amodei , who has represented the district since 2011 . <p> But any of the candidates who win the June 14 Democratic primary will face an uphill battle . <p> The congressional district , created in 1981 , has always been held by a Republican . The district originally reached into Clark County but now stretches across the northern third of Nevada .
