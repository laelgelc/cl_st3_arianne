<h> At high-level forum , UN spotlights power of information technology in advancing development <p> 3 May 2016 ? Information and communication technologies ( ICTs ) have the power to help people around the world bridge the digital divide and accelerate innovation as world leaders continue to work towards implementing the United Nations development agenda , Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon underlined today at the opening of a high-level forum of the ICT for development community . <p> " Information and communication technologies are the drivers of the global economy and have revolutionized how we communicate , interact socially and do business , " Mr. Ban stressed in a video message to the opening of the annual UN World Summit on the Information Society ( WSIS ) Forum in Geneva , which is hosted by the UN International Telecommunications Union ( ITU ) . <p> " ICTs also provide innovative solutions to combat climate change , improve health care and education , and empower women and the vulnerable , " the Secretary-General said . <p> " People are better connected than ever , but we still have to close the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have to ensure that everyone has freedom of access to information , " the UN chief said . <p> He urged participants to continue working to ensure universal access to a more open information society . <p> " Let us empower people everywhere with these transformative technologies so that they can help build a better future for all , " Mr. Ban said . <p> Video Message from Secretary General Ban Ki-moon for WSIS Forum 2016 <p> Along those lines , ITU Secretary-general Houlin Zhao noted in a separate video message that putting ICTs at the heart of global development will be critical to meeting the SDGs . <p> " The WSIS process is all about helping to bridge the digital divide so that people everywhere can reap the tremendous benefits of connectivity , " Mr. Zhao said . <p> " Let us strive to empower individuals with transformative technologies that can change their lives , the lives of their families and their communities , so that we can all share in a more prosperous and more equitable future , " he added . <p> The forum -- whose over-arching theme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the SDGs ' -- is co-organized by the UN Educational , <p> This year 's event is being chaired by Daniel A. Sepulveda , Deputy Assistant Secretary of State and Coordinator for International Communications and Information Policy in the United States State Department 's Bureau of Economic and Business Affairs . <p> The forum , which will conclude on Friday , 6 May , is expected to include 1,800 participants , including more than 85 government ministers and 250 high-level representatives . Sessions will focus on , among other things , cybersecurity , promoting peaceful and inclusive knowledge societies , and implementing best practices and addressing challenges .
