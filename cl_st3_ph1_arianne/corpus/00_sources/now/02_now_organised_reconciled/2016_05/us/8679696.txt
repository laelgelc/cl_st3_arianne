<p> New Jersey got hit by only one major snowstorm last winter -- the historic January blizzard that dumped as much as 30 inches of snow across the region . But the Garden State , and other states along the East Coast , could face more frequent winter storms as a result of changing weather patterns that have been documented in Greenland , according to a report by the Washington Post . <p> The report said two recent climate studies have found that warmer temperatures and melting sea ice in the Arctic region have contributed to the frequency of a weather pattern known as the Greenland block , a pattern that often results in colder air and snowstorms in the eastern United States . <p> A Greenland block occurs when a large high-pressure system in the upper atmosphere remains centered near Greenland and stays in place for a long period , according to the National Weather Service .
