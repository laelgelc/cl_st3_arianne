<h> Kufuor calls for donor support in fighting climate change <p> Former President John Agyekum Kufour has said donors and the public should play a major role in assisting businesses fight climate change and environmental degradation . <p> The ex-president , who was speaking at the World Bank Group launch of the Climate Innovation Centre to support Ghana 's green economy in Accra on Wednesday May 17 , indicated that : " If we are seeking green solutions to fight the impact of climate change which is global , I believe public policy , donor community support , as well as private venturists should share the risk of investment to transition from fossil fuels to green energy . " <p> The centre is a technology hub designed to help over 100 local clean technology businesses develop and commercialise innovative solutions to climate change . <p> The World Bank believes climate change will have serious and adverse consequences for many development sectors in Africa , and threatens the economies and livelihoods of many African countries . <p> To support African countries as they deal with the challenges of climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of action aimed at adaptation to climate change in Africa , seizing mitigation opportunities on the continent , building knowledge and capacity , and making more financing available . <p> For ex-president Kufour , he held the view that : " We should not put the burden all together on the entrepreneurs who dare to go into say bamboo ...
