<h> Campaign mobilises masses to march against bread price increase <h> Tags : <p> High bread prices in the country have become a source of concern and protest as the Food Sovereignty Campaign mobilises masses to march against an increase in bread prices . <p> This week , the campaign kicked off , with a smaller march , as an example of what will happen on a larger scale , if bread prices go any higher . <p> Food activist , Xolisa Bangani , says they demand that the government assist small scale farmers . <p> " The agricultural sector must support agri-ecology way of whereby we farming not against nature but we farming in line with nature so we can try to bring a solution to this climate change and this drought situation that we are in today .
