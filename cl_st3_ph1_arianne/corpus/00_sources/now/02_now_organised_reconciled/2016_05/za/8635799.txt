<p> By the middle of the century , African , Middle Eastern and central Asian economies will be severely hit by water shortages . The World Bank have warned that water deficits will take double digits of these regions ' GDP . <p> Their report shows that by 2050 , increased demand for agriculture and cities would worsen shortages across large parts of Africa and Asia . <p> The combination of climate change and increasing demand threatens water supply . It is also concerning global governments - the report speculates that these shortages could spur conflict and migration . <p> Water shortages wo n't be exclusive to Africa and Asia . Barack Obama held a business summit last March in order to protect California from the next drought . However , the World Bank found that water deficits will have practically no impact on the economies of North Africa and Western Europe .
