As the ADB turns another year , we take stock of what we can do better to serve our members better . <p> Much has been said about how we are entering the Asian Century , with the continent reclaiming its dominant role in the global economy . In 1990 Asia contributed only a little more than 20 percent in the world 's GDP growth . In 2014 Asia 's share shot up to around 40 percent , the biggest slice of the pie and far larger than the contributions of the United States and the European Union .
