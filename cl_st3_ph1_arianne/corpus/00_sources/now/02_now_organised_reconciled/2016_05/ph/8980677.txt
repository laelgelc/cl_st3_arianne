<h> Baguio LGU cuts down power expense with led conversion project <p> BAGUIO CITY , May 23- - The city government has cut down its power consumption with the pocket implementation of the Light Emitting Diode ( LED ) conversion project in selected priority areas . <p> Engr . Ric Molina of the Electrical Division of the City Engineer 's Office reported a 50 percent actual savings realized in pilot areas covered by the conversion project which include the city 's main thoroughfares and major parks . <p> Pursued by phase starting 2014 , the project was initiated as the city 's plan for the conversion of all existing streetlights , park lights and government-owned building and facilities into LED technology and for the repair and maintenance of the same on a Private-Public Partnership ( PPP ) and in a cost-saving share scheme remained shelved , according to City Budget Officer Leticia Clemente . <p> The project is one of the several economic cost-cutting measures implemented by the city government as part of its continuing effort to make the city " a climate change-resilient or green city . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the city sourced out from its own funds . <p> The first phase of the project covered the city 's main streets Session Road , Harrison Road , Magsaysay Avenue and Abanao St. while the second comprised Rose Garden , City Hall park , Dominican Hill and Retreat House , Wright Park and just recently Burnham Park . <p> Molina said the project involves the replacement of streetlights from high pressure sodium lights to LED lights with a five-year warranty . It also covers the installation of additional fixtures . <p> Clemente said the city aims to include the barangays in the project but the barangays need to give their counterpart from their own revenues . <p> In the meantime , the city is encouraging the barangays to adopt the city 's LED bulb specifications in replacing their fixtures to contribute to the city 's cost-cutting efforts until a policy adopting a standard fixture is issued by Mayor Mauricio Domogan . <p> The city government 's power costs had run up to P42.32 million per year for a total of 8,200 lighting fixtures lighting streets , alleys , parks , overpasses @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ offices and schools .
