Now they have more to run on , " Smith said . <p> The study also was the first to tie a specific layer of Martian ice with a specific period of time . " Eventually we 'd like to be able to do this for every layer , " Smith said . <p> From the perspective of an Earthling , every day on Mars may feel like an ice age .
