<h> PH calls for bigger emission reductions to achieve Paris goal <p> May 18 , 2016 <p> MANILA , May 18 -- The Philippines on Monday called on member-countries to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) to eye bigger reductions in gas emissions in order to achieve a below 1.5 degrees Celsius global temperature goal . <p> At the opening of the UN Climate Change Conference in Bonn , Germany , Secretary Emmanuel De Guzman , vice chairperson of the Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) , said governments across the globe must set more ambitious targets in their Intended Nationally Determined Contributions ( INDCs ) to preserve the gains of the historic Paris Agreement . <p> The Paris pact , which was unanimously adopted in France last December , is the first agreement requiring all countries to join the fight against climate change . <p> " ( But ) we are all aware that our current INDCs translate to an estimated 3 degrees of warming , or double the long-term goal . The success of the Paris Agreement is seen to largely depend on the goals of these pledges , which will eventually determine the amount of action required to deal with climate change beyond 2020 . <p> Last month , nearly all parties to the UNFCCC , including the Philippines , have signed the Paris Agreement at the UN Headquarters in New York , signifying that efforts towards the entry into force of the climate deal is underway . <p> The Philippine government is expected to ratify the Paris Agreement this month . <p> According to De Guzman , while nations work to realize current pledges , all parties must likewise begin to update and set more ambitious Nationally Determined Contributions ( NDCs ) , taking into account " the concept of equity and the principle of common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities in light of different national circumstances . It is at the forefront of an advocacy for global efforts towards low carbon economy to limit global warming to well below 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> De Guzman noted that CVF member-countries , though which contribution to global GHG emissions is " marginal , " have shown strong leadership to take on mitigation . <p> He said that the Philippines , for instance , has submitted an initial and conditional INDC with 70 percent emission reduction to the UN body , which can be attained through stronger international partnerships . <p> To achieve the goal , he said , all parties must pitch in to do more , particularly the developed countries .
