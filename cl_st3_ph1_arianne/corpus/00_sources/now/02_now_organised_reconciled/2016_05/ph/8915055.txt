<p> MANILA -- Incoming President Rodrigo Duterte and members of the incoming 17th Congress should prioritize ending hunger and malnutrition . <p> The Philippine Legislators ' Committee on Population and Development ( PLCPD ) made the challenge on Thursday ( May 19 ) at the weekly Pandesal Forum at the Kamuning Bakery Cafe in Quezon City . <p> The PLCPD , which was established in September 1989 , is a an advocacy group of legislators from the Senate and the House of Representatives . <p> " We have a lot of things yet to do to achieve food and nutrition security , " PLCPD Executive Director Romeo Dongeto said . " The next government should look into a wide range of issues , from agriculture to trade , from infrastructure to financing , from healthcare to clean water and sanitation . " <p> Dongeto also reminded the incoming government officials of the new Sustainable Development Goals , the second of which is ending hunger , achieving food security , improving nutrition , and promoting sustainable agriculture . <p> The government should start by reviewing the implementation of laws @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ many good laws on food and nutrition , " he said . " There are at least 21 government agencies with mandates that directly or indirectly affect the many facets of food and nutrition security . Yet millions of Filipinos still experience hunger , chronic malnutrition , and micronutrient deficiency . " <p> Citing figures for 2015 from the Social Weather Stations ( SwS ) , Dongeto pointed out that 13.4 percent of Filipinos said they experienced hunger . <p> He also cited 2015 figures from the Food and Nutrition Research Institute showing that 33 in 100 children aged below five were stunted or " bansot " -- that is , too short for their age -- and seven in 100 children in the same age bracket were wasted or too thin for their height . <p> He noted that in 2013 the National Nutrition Survey found that one in every 10 Filipino adults suffer from chronic energy deficiency . <p> " President-elect Duterte should deliver on his promise of eradicating poverty , especially in the rural areas , by empowering small farmers and fisherfolk , " Dongeto said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countryside , create sustainable livelihood , and help farmers and fisherfolk withstand the impact of climate change and natural disasters . <p> He also urged Congress to pass a law that would provide healthcare , clean water , sanitation , and nutrition in the first 1,000 days of a child 's development -- that is from the child 's first nine months in the mother 's womb up to its second birthday , a critical period in its physical and cognitive development .
