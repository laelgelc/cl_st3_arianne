<h> Albay election switch <p> The Albay leg of the May 9 elections easily qualifies as one of the most interesting episodes of the 2016 electoral exercise . Pushed by Albay Gov . Joey Salceda , administration bet Mar Roxas comfortably paced the presidential race in the province , trailed by Senator Grace Poe , Davao City Mayor Rodrigo Duterte and the rest , until shortly before election day . <p> For reasons deemed completely valid , Salceda switched his support to Poe less than two weeks before voting day , propelling Poe to dramatically dislodge Roxas from the lead . Poe , who conceded to Duterte two days after the polls , scored landslide wins in the second and third districts of the province . Salceda himself , who completes his nine-year term as governor , won a 92% vote mandate as Congressman of Albay 's second congressional district . <p> * * * <p> Filipino youths will likely rally behind Salceda 's education agenda in Congress . His priority bills include the National Higher Education Contribution System ( NHECS ) for Private Tertiary schools ; free @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Trust Funds of R20 billion each for business incubation , arts and culture , research and development , technology diffusion and extension ; international tourism marketing , and the cityhood of Daraga town , site of the Bicol International Airport in his district , among others . <p> NHECS is an expanded national version of his Albay Higher Education Contribution System ( AHECS ) , which has enabled nearly 40,000 poor Albayano youths to earn college degrees and vocational education in just eight years . The national version will benefit poor Filipino youths nationwide . Salceda is credited for transforming Albay from a prostrate province ravaged often by disasters , to a vibrant local economy and a global standard model in disaster risk reduction ( DRR ) and climate change adaptation ( CCA ) . <p> * * * <p> My friend who visited northern Catanduanes shortly before the election , noted how rampant was vote buying in his hometown .
