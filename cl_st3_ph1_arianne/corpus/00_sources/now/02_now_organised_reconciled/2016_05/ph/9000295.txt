<p> Fresh from her recent victory for a fresh second mandate , la Alcadeza de Zamboanga Maria Isabelle " Beng " Climaco-Salazar , presented her plans for the city in her Message during the Ateneo de Zamboanga University ( ADZU ) ' s Strategic Planning Conference Held May 11-13 , 2016 at the Garden Orchid Hotel.Putting forth her battle cry , " I love Zamboanga " the Mayor said that elected officials should set aside differences and work towards re-building the city and becoming more responsive to the needs of the citizenry .
