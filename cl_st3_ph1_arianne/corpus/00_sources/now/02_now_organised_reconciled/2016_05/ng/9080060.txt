<h> One Year Of Buhari 's Foreign Policy : Between Change and Continuity <p> His foreign policy was not formally unveiled on assumption of office . This made analysts to wonder at the beginning of his administration what shape his foreign policy would take . <p> Buoyed by the goodwill of winning the presidential election as an opposition candidate , President Muhammadu Buhari entered the international system with his " Change " mantra , which encapsulates the ideology of his administration . <p> Soon on assumption of office , Buhari embarked on a tour of the neighbouring countries-Niger , Chad , Benin Republic and much later Cameroun , to seek understanding , cooperation and assistance in the fight against the Boko Haram insurgents in the North -- Eastern part of the country . <p> Within the first month in office , the President visited Germany where he met with Chancellor Angela Merkel and also attended a meeting of the G.7 countries to brief members on the security situation in the country and the plans to rebuild the war-torn region .
