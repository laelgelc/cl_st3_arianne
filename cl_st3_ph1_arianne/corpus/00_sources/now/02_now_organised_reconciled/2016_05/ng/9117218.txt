<h> Niger Basin Authority pledges to preserve , boost potential of region <p> The Niger Basin Authority has pledged to implement an Operational Plan that would preserve the region and enable it meet its full potential . <p> This was the thrust of the presentation by the Executive Secretary of the Authority , Mrs Toupta Boguena , at the ongoing Extraordinary Session of the Council of Ministers in Abuja on Tuesday . <p> She listed drought , population increase , as part of the causes of depletion in the river basin , saying efforts are on to preserve the region to enable it meet its full potential . <p> According to her , the authority is implementing an Operational Plan from the just concluded 11th Summit of the Heads of States and Governments with active participation of the nine member countries . <p> " For implementation of our operational plan , the authority will provide jobs for youths in the area of agriculture , livestock , fishery and conservation of the basin 's natural resources . <p> " In line with the shared vision of the member states @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the use of water resources based on the Water Charter . <p> " This framework , subsequently ratified , contains binding principles , shared strategies and investment plans would provide a solid foundation for the cooperative development of cross-border water resources .
