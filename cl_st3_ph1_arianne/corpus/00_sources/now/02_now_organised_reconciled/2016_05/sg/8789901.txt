<h> Animals Vital For Preserving Carbon-Storing Forests <p> AsianScientist ( May 12 , 2016 ) - By Aathira Perinchery - Losing animals that disperse seeds of large tree species could decrease the amount of carbon stored in a majority of the world 's tropical forests , says a new study . <p> " We found that large-seeded , animal-dispersed tree species attain greater adult sizes , and thus store more carbon than small-seeded animal-dispersed ones , " says Mr. Anand Osuri , researcher at the National Centre for Biological Sciences , Bangalore , and lead author of a paper on the study published April in Nature Communications . <p> Forests are important carbon sinks , known to mitigate human-caused climate change . One process that boosts carbon sequestration is seed dispersal , which can be abiotic ( wind or water-driven ) or biotic ( aided by animals ) . <p> Over-hunting of fauna and subsequent decline in
