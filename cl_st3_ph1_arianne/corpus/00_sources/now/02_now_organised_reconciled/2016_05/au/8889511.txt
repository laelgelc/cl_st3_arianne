<p> As the two main parties prepared to enter a debate about climate change and clean energy policies , the Australian Solar Council , the industry 's principal lobby group , released a report card on the Coalition 's efforts on renewable energy to date . <p> The Coalition did not fare well , receiving an F in all subjects , apart from the Clean Energy Finance Corporation , where it got a D after deciding to keep the institution it had spent three years trying to dismantle , but allocated $1 billion of its funds elsewhere . <p> " ( Tony and Malcolm 's ) joint project gets an overall F because it continues to smash renewables jobs , reduce electricity market competition , stop regional investment in large renewables projects , and keep all consumers locked into ever increasing power bills , " the council concluded . <p> The council has launched a Vote Solar campaign to target marginal seats in a push for stronger solar and renewable energy policies .
