<h> The government is spending $171 million to save The Great Barrier Reef <p> The Great Barrier Reef , currently hit by widespread bleaching believed to have been caused by warmer sea water , gets $171 million extra in the budget . <p> Environment minister Greg Hunt says the money will fund projects to help make the reef more resilient . <p> He says this is part of a projected $2 billion to be spent on the reef over the next decade . <p> " The Reef 2050 Plan and the Reef Trust are already addressing key threats to the reef by improving water quality and coastal habitats , mitigating outbreaks of crown-of-thorn starfish and protecting threatened and migratory species such as dugongs and turtles , " he says . <p> " It is these projects which will make the reef more resilient s we address the challenges of climate change and the current coral bleaching event which has affected reefs around the world . "
