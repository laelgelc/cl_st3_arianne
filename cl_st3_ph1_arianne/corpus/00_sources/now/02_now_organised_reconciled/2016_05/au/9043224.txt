<h> Activists on track for change <p> A GROUP of activists will visit Armidale to rally against climate change next Sunday . <p> COLOURFUL CHARACTERS : New England activists take to the streets helping to raise awareness about climate change . <p> A GROUP of climate change activists from Armidale will travel by train to Tamworth to take part in an election climate rally next Sunday . <p> The event is being held to coincide with World Environment Day . Organisers want to raise global awareness and promote positive environmental action to protect the environment . <p> World Environment Day is run by the United Nations . <p> Armidale spokeswoman Vee Ness said it was important for local residents to be vocal about the issues they cared about . <p> " We need to be engaging the politicians to act on these issues for tomorrow , for our children and for our region 's future , " Ms Ness said . " We hope to fill the train from Armidale to Tamworth , and anticipate to see many there . "
