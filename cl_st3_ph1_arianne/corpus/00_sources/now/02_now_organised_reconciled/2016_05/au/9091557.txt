<h> Labor 's $500m plan to protect the reef <p> Pro Reef and anti coal , protesters gather outside Queensland 's parliament in April to hit out at the state Labor government granting a mining lease for Adani 's $22 billion mine , rail and port project for the state 's Galilee basin and Abbot Point . AAP Image/Nathan Paull <p> Elise Scott <p> Canberra AAP <p> Federal Labor has declared protecting the Great Barrier Reef one of its highest priorities , pledging $377 million in new funding for better management and research . <p> Opposition Leader Bill Shorten flew to Cairns on Monday to reveal a $500 million fund to boost research , improve water quality and eradicate pests like the crown of thorns starfish . <p> The new money adds to the $123 million already pledged by the federal government . <p> It includes a $50 million cash injection for the CSIRO for reef-specific research , which will include a focus on the impacts of climate change .
