<p> Malcolm Turnbull made his first serious investment in his early 20s . He bought an investment flat in Sydney . <p> His parents did n't help him to pay for it , though his dad worked with his son on repairs and renovations . <p> The tax system , however , did lend the young Turnbull a hand . His tenants paid him rent , but the cost of paying the mortgage was greater than the income he received . So when he filed his tax return each year , he was able to claim the loss against his taxable income , reducing his tax bill . <p> It 's the magic of negative gearing -- the tax system helps pay off the property , even as the value of the real estate grows .
