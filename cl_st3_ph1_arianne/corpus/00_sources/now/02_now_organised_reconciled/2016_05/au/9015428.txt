<h> Changing landscapes <p> The only constant in landscapes is change . Ecosystems are always changing in response to natural drivers such as fire and flood . <p> Humans have complicated things . Indigenous Australians manipulated the Australian landscape and climate through burning for millennia , sustaining a population of around 750,000 and underpinning a culture . <p> European colonisation brought a different and more pervasive change , clearing land , building cities , damming rivers and establishing an increasingly mechanised and industrialised agriculture . <p> These iconic but changed landscapes inspired the romantic art of Arthur Streeton and poetry of Banjo Paterson among many others -- and helped forge a young nation 's identity . <p> Change can happen surprisingly quickly . <p> Often before we know it we have gone too far and need to scramble for fixes that are so often costly , slow and ultimately inadequate . <p> Demand for more secure sources of energy has generated rapid expansion of coal seam gas and wind power generation , and the development of northern Australia remains a bipartisan priority <p> Brett Anthony Bryan <p> For @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1960s raised the alarm that the feverish post-war period of soldier resettlement , land clearance and agricultural development threatened entire native plant and animal communities with extinction . <p> The Government 's response over the following 30 years was to expand greatly the conservation reserve network and eventually prohibit land clearing . <h> History repeating ? <p> Agricultural lands produce a range of goods and services .
