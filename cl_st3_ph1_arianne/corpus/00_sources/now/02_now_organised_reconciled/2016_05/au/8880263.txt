<h> Why China will lead green innovation ( and is worth investing in ) <p> Economic and financial incentives will drive Beijing to curtail its pollution problem . <p> by Aniket Shah <p> ? The Paris Climate Conference came and went in December last year . Although there was considerable excitement about the outcome , it was simply the beginning of a multi-decade process of decarbonising the global economy . <p> This transition will require tens of trillions of dollars of public and private investment in low carbon energy , infrastructure , transportation systems , energy efficiency projects and more . However , a major question remains : where will this investment come from ? <p> Private investors should take note of China 's leading role in tackling climate change . This may come as a surprise to many observers . After all , China is the world 's largest carbon dioxide emitter . The environmental challenges in the country are leading to significant health problems and premature deaths for hundreds of thousands of people annually . <p> China is taking climate change seriously for two broad reasons . The first is the significant challenges that climate change presents for the country , as discussed above . The second is a broad recognition that sustainable development is a major business and investment opportunity for new technologies to be developed , new sectors to be created , and the nation 's growing savings to be invested . <p> That is why the recently released 13th Five Year Plan ( for 2016-2020 ) , a road map for the country 's economy and investment , puts the environment front and centre as a national priority .
