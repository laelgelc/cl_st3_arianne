<h> Wood first policy in Tasmanian the first of its kind in Australia <p> Tasmania is developing a new policy that will require wood to be considered as the first-choice construction material during the initial stages of public building projects . <p> Making this announcement , State Treasurer Peter Gutwein said that Tasmania will be the first state in Australia to develop a wood encouragement policy , following recent changes to the National Construction Code that allow for timber products such as CLT to be utilised in more construction projects .
