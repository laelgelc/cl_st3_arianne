<p> As far back as 1981 a proposal for an international agency dedicated towards renewable energy was made at a UN conference in Nairobi . It took until 2009 for the founding conference to be held and in 2011 the International Renewable Energy Agency was born . <p> IRENA now has 147 members and serves " as a platform for international co-operation , a centre for excellence , and a repository of policy , technology , resource and financial knowledge on renewable energy . Australia is a member as is most of Europe , the USA and much of Asia . Canada is not a member . <p> IRENA 's HQ is in Masdar City , in the UAE . It has about 90 people and a 2016 budget of US$30.4 m plus it expects/hopes to " mobilise " additional voluntary resources for US$15 m .
