<h> Infosys to launch of its household biogas digester project <p> Sreemoyee Chatterjee <p> Ramanagara district in Karnataka would soon see the launch of a household biogas project wherein 7,620 household biogas units would be built , providing an equal number of biogas stoves to as many families in the district . <p> TNN May 30 , 2016 , 06.49 PM IST <p> BENGALURU : Ramanagara district in Karnataka would soon see the launch of a household biogas project wherein 7,620 household biogas units would be built , providing an equal number of biogas stoves to as many families in the district . <p> An initiative of Infosys , 22 crore would be spent on the project and over a period of ten years , the project is expected to generate biogas equivalent to about one million cylinders of domestic LPG . Infosys would be training the beneficiaries to maintain the biogas digesters and monitor their performance in partnership with SKG Sangha , a Karnataka-based NGO with experience in implementing biogas projects . <p> The project was inaugurated by Sudha Murthy , chairperson of Infosys Foundation in Channapatna @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bangalore Rural and Ramadas Kamath , executive vice president , head at infrastructure , facilities , administration , security and sustainability at Infosys were present at the inaugural program along with KP Raju , president of the local panchayat . <p> This biogas project will give the beneficiary families a better and healthier lifestyle through clean and fast cooking for many years .
