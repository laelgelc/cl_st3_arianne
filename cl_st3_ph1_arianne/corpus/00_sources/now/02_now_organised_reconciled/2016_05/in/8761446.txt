<h> NBWL nod to relocate villagers from the jaws of Bay of Bengal <p> BHUBANESWAR : The National Board for Wildlife ( NBWL ) on Tuesday approved relocation and rehabilitation of inhabitants of Satabhaya and Kanupur , two sea-side villages located inside the Bhitarkanika Wildlife Sanctuary . <p> With coastal erosion accelerating over the years , the two revenue villages are gradually being consumed by the Bay of Bengal prompting the residents to shift to safer places . <p> Sources said the standing committee of NBWL , presided by Union Minister of State ( Independent ) for Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) Prakash Javadekar , cleared the proposal of the State Government which had sought relocation of 571 families from Satabhaya and Kanupur to Bagapatia village which is also located within Bhitarkanika Wildlife Sanctuary . <p> This apart , another 71 families of Chargharia , a hamlet located inside Bhitarkanika National Park , would also be relocated to Bagapatia . <p> Interestingly , Bagapatia village , which was notified by the Revenue Department in 1965 , has at least 473 tenant encroachers . The 642 villagers would be eligible only for 0.1 acre homestead land . <p> Last year , the State Government had decided that villagers would be allowed to continue with their age old vocation like farming in their land even after relocation . <p> There are at least 410 villages within Bhitarkanika sanctuary limits . In 1998 , the district administration had proposed that land of 47 villages would be acquired under Land Acquisition Act and inhabitants would be shifted outside where the oustees would be given a rehabilitation and resettlement package .
