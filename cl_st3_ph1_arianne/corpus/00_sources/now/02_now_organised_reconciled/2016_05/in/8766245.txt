<h> Unknown MP village to host ' ideological mahakumbh ' <p> NINAURA : While BJP president Amit Shah plans a dalit mahakumbh at Ujjain on Wednesday , three days later , PM Modi will hold a brainstorming session on Indian spiritual ethos , climate change and agriculture in the backdrop of Simhastha , just 11km from the holy city , but wo n't take a holy dip in the Kshipra . <p> " PM Modi wo n't visit Kshipra ghats as this would offset movement of lakhs of pilgrims at the Simhastha site . He will take off from conclave meet at Ninaura village after four hours , " said BJP Rajya Sabha member , Anil Dave , who is overseeing preparations . <p> Ninaura , a nondescript village on outskirts of Ujjain district , bordering Indore has suddenly catapulted into prominence to play host to an international conference , a village which for more than a decade has never seen a chief minister on its soil , leave alone a PM . Babulal Gaur during his short stint as chief minister had visited Ninaura for a few @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Right Way of Living , the grand conclave is being held on a sprawling 90acre agricultural land acquired from 61 farmers , flattened and decked up for a three-day Vichar Mahakumbh from May 12 . Five helipads have been built along with three centrally air-conditioned auditoriums and a workstation , which will function like a PMO . Farmers who had harvested their crop a few months ago , were paid a compensation of Rs 20,000 per hectare for the three-day extravaganza . <p> On May 14 , after Prime Minister Modi touches down at one of the five helipads , he 's scheduled to hold a high-level diplomatic meet with Sri Lankan president Maithripala Sirisena , a special invitee to Simhastha , said Anil Dave . Diplomats and dignitaries from 41 nations will be hosted at Ninaura conclave , who will be put up at Indore hotels , but will be driven down to Ujjain for a visit to Simhastha mela site . <p> The cynosure of all eyes at the Ninaura conclave grounds are two mud huts , which have been named -- Chitrakoot and Panchavati . With thatched roofs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ these huts have hi-tech facilities inside , including air-conditioned drawing , living and bedrooms with furniture and attached toilets . <p> While Dave has been camping at Chitrakoot hut , he scotched speculation this was specifically built for Modi . However , chief minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan during his whistle-stop tours of Ninaura does stop over at Panchavati for a few hours . <p> " These mud huts provide a rural touch and bring out the fragrance of Bharat , " said Dave . These dung-plastered huts are the only sylvan speck in the lavish spread of temporary modernity at the sprawling Ninaura conclave . The land will be returned to the farmers after the meet , but as a local farmer told TOI , " We do n't know how to make the land ready for another harvest . There 's not a speck of grass on the levelled fields . It needs to be ploughed and irrigated again at a huge cost to grow crops .
