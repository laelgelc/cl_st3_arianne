<h> Science Express returns home , sets more records <p> The current run of the train , which will get over on Saturday , has taken forward most of the existing records on its name . <p> TNN May 6 , 2016 , 06.14 AM IST <p> Ahmedabad : When one enters the air-conditioned coaches of Science Express : Climate Action Special ( SECAS ) , it 's not just one of its kind exhibition on the wheels , but a moving behemoth that holds seven national and international records . <p> The current run of the train , which will get over on Saturday , has taken forward most of the existing records on its name . <p> SECAS will remain at Gandhinagar railway station from May 5 to 7 . It is the train 's third avatar and eighth run . Earlier , the same train had been Science Express for four runs and Biodiversity Special for three runs . <p> Dilip Surkar , director of Vikram A Sarabhai Community Science Centre ( VASCSC ) , said that the train has a special connection with Gujarat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . The SECAS was flagged off from Delhi on October 15 . <p> " This year , we surpassed many records , including the one of the most expensive single railway ticket . When the train had started off , it had set a record at Rs 4.76 crore which during this run escalated to Rs 12.64 crore . During its run for seven months , it covered 19,800 kms in 20 states , adding to its record of longest running moving exhibition . So far , over 23 lakh citizens have visited the train , taking total tally to 1.55 crore , another record , " said Surkar . <p> He said that the train , which had also got a mention in COP 21 at Paris as an innovative project , has also been the largest and most visited exhibition or awareness initiative about climate change in the world . <p> " But the train is not about the records .
