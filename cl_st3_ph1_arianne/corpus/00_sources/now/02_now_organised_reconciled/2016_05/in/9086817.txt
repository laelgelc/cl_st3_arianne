<h> Moratorium under CEPI in Chandrapur area of Maharashtra lifted <p> The Ministry of Environment , Forests &amp; Climate Change has lifted the moratorium under the Comprehensive Environmental Pollution Index ( CEPI ) in respect of the industrial cluster of Chandrapur in Maharashtra . <p> This will enable new investments in the region , which was stalled for last more than five years , an official press release said . <p> The Ministry had imposed moratorium in 43 CEPI areas on 13.01.2010 . It has been lifted in respect of the industrial cluster/area of Chandrapur ( MIDC Chandrapur , Tadali , Ghuggus , Ballapur ) in Maharashtra vide vide Office Memorandum dated 20.05.2016 , the release said . <p> The release said the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) conducted monitoring in Critically Polluted Areas ( CPA ) of Chandrapur during February-March , 2016 and re-assessed the CEPI score . <p> It said the CPCB , vide its communication , dated April 18 , 2016 informed the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change about the revised CEPI score based on the monitoring . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Maharashtra ) is 54.42 , as compared to the CEPI score assessed by CPCB in 2013 ( 81.90 ) .
