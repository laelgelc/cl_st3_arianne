KERALA <h> A transformational model for eco conservation <p> In an initiative to conserve biodiversity , the Forest Department has put in place a multi-species inventory and monitoring mechanism . <p> Touted to be the first of its kind to be undertaken in the country , the endeavour has been aimed at ensuring the sustainability of the ecosystem and the flow of ecosystem services to the forest community . The department also expects the mechanism to address the threats of biopiracy and poaching . <p> Official sources point out that the system is intended to usher in a transformational model of forest management . <p> One of the crucial goals of the system will be to enhance the availability of water in the prevailing circumstance of rising temperature . It will also benefit the public during drought situations , the sources said . <p> " The newly developed protocol is also targeted to bring together managerial sciences , advances in technology and forestry practices to helpforest personnelimprove their efficiency . To enable self-monitoring , a Forestry Planner has been designed for individual @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ formulated for institutionalising the change , " an official said . <p> According to Jawaharlal Nehru Tropical Botanic Garden and Research Institute Director P.G . Latha , who is also the Director in-charge of the Kerala Forest Research Institute ( KFRI ) , teams of scientists representing the institute will be sent to various sample spots spread across the State to monitor the natural resources . <p> " Barring some animals which face the threat of extinction , there has not been much focus on the preservation of plants . With the effects of climate change being felt , an endeavour of such scale has become the need of the hour , " she pointed out . <p> KFRI scientist E.A. Jayson , who was part of the panel which developed the protocol , said the monitoring system will enable assessment of the effectiveness of management . " Undertaking regular monitoring is necessary for proper utilisation of natural resources and to implement remedial measures , wherever necessary .
