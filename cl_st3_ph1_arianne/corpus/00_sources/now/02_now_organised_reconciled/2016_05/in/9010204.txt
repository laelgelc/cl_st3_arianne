IN SCHOOL <h> Our favourite Green champions <h> A look at the animation characters that inspired the powerful environment movement and persuaded children and adults alike to think green <p> The world woke up to the threats of global warming and the related environment issues in the last ten years . Climate change and carbon footprint slowly became household words . Thanks to awareness created by media , government , activists and various art forms . <p> Films , especially animated ones , took on a good share of the responsibility . They created loveable characters and heart-warming tales that lobbied for climate action . They helped children develop a critical awareness of the environmental problems that surround them . <p> But there were messages in earlier films too . The film features well-known actors such as Robin Williams , Hugh Jackman , Brittany Murphy and Elijah Wood . It won the BAFTA and an Oscar for best animation .
