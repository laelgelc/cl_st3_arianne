It has restricted the lead content up to 90 ppm ( internationally accepted standards for all kinds of paints ) , saving children and women who are mostly affected by lead poisoning that damages the central nervous system , causes anaemia and lowers Intelligence Quotient ( IQ ) among children . <p> While the Regulations on Lead Contents in Households and Decorative Paints Rules , 2016 that would be implemented from June 8 , has limited the lead content only in household and decorative paints , National Referral Centre for Lead Projects in India ( NRCLPI ) Bengaluru has made a strong recommendation to Gazette of India ( GOI ) to fix lead content below 90 ppm for all kinds of paints and not just domestic ones . <p> Dr Thuppil Venkatesh , retired professor at St John 's Medical College , also popularly known as the lead man of India , said : " After Quality Council of India ( QCI ) , Pollution Control Board Authorities , NRCLPI and several NGOs brought to the notice of the government about elevated blood lead level , especially @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ health , it is commendable that GOI has brought out the draft notification to fix the lead content in all decorative paints . But that is not enough . All kinds of paints , paint pigments and solvents should be brought under the rule to prevent lead poisoning that affects 6 , 00,000 people every year as per WHO records . Mandatory 90 ppm lead content rule should be fixed for all imported paints , pigments and finished products as well . " <p> " Industrial paints , paints used on toys , vehicles , fences and pavement railings contain more than 20,000 ppm of lead content that is dangerous for human health . It took almost 8 years for Indian government to issue such a notification regulating Lead content in paints . Now that they have done it , they should not limit the rule only to domestic paints but should apply it to all paints , " said Partha Sarthi KS , advisor to NRCLPI . <p> He added : " Since 60% of paints are manufactured by unorganised sector in India , this new regulation will have limited @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ kinds of pigments or solvents used as paints that are having high level of Lead concentration , on which the government should act immediately .
