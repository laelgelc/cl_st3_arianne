Much like in other times of disaster , social media has erupted with comments and tweets on the Uttarakhand forest fires as well . A Facebook page , ' Uttarakhand Burning Silently ' has garnered thousands of likes , especially , a post by the page admin , Ela Smetacek who is of Indo-Czech origin citing , " This is a National Emergency . The forests of Uttarakhand have been burning for over a week now " has been shared over 55,000 times and still counting .
