<h> Obama to host Modi at White House on June 7 <p> New Delhi/ Washington , May 21 : Prime Minister Narendra Modi will meet US President Barack Obama at the White House on June 7 during his fourth visit to the country and discuss a wide range of issues , including climate change , clean energy partnership , security and defense cooperation , and economic issues . <p> The two-day visit to the US will also see him enjoy the honor of being the first foreign leader this year to address the joint meeting of the US Congress . Modi 's visit is taking place at the invitation of President Barack Obama , Ministry of External Affairs announced here on May 21 . Obama is completing his second and the last term in office this year . <p> In Washington , the White House issued a statement which said the visit will highlight the deepening of the US-India relationship in key areas since the President 's visit to New Delhi in January 2015 .
