Once so wealthy from oil sales that they could finance wars , mega-projects , and domestic social peace simultaneously , some of them are now beset by internal strife or are on the brink of collapse as oil prices remain at ruinously low levels . Unlike other countries , which largely finance their governments through taxation , petro-states rely on their oil and natural gas revenues . Russia , for example , obtains about 50% of government income that way ; Nigeria , 60% ; and Saudi Arabia , a whopping 90% . When oil was selling at $100 per barrel or above , as was the case until 2014 , these countries could finance lavish government projects and social welfare operations , ensuring widespread popular support .
