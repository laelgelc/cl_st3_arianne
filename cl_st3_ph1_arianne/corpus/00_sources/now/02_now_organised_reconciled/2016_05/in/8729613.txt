<h> Slowdown affecting millions of people moving from farming to construction sector <p> THE HANS INDIA May 09,2016 , 04:37 PM IST <p> The construction sector in India , which employed more than 44 million people at the end of 2010 ( the last date for which official data are available ) , is witnessing a slowdown , affecting millions of people moving from farming to the construction sector . <p> Constituting 7.8 percent of India 's gross domestic product ( GDP ) in 2013-14 , the real-estate sector was buffeted by domestic and global slowdowns , with growth decelerating from 7.6 percent in 2012-13 to six percent in 2013-14 . <p> As India tries to move its people away from agriculture -- which contributes 15 percent of the GDP but employs 263.2 million or 54.6 percent of the working populationc -- a majority of those leaving are finding employment in construction . <p> While agricultural employment declined five percent between 2005 and 2010 , construction saw a growth of nearly 70 percent . <p> The construction sector is now India 's second-largest employer after agriculture , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ poverty levels . <p> India 's poverty rate declined from 37.2 percentin 2004-05 to 21.9 percent in 2011-12 ; 269.7 million Indians now live below the official poverty line , down from 407.2 million in 2004-05 . Construction has played a major part , both in rural areas ( through the 10-year-old Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act , which provides jobs building ponds , roads and other infrastructure ) and urban areas ( through real estate and infrastructure ) . <p> Construction is a $126 billion ( Rs.8.39 lakh crore ) industry -- larger than pharmaceuticals and gems and jewellery sectors , for example -- attributed to the infrastructure sector , industrial activities , residential and commercial development . <p> India 's urbanisation might also explain the expansion of rural infrastructure and non-farm jobs in rural India . India 's urban population rose from 286 million in 2001 to 377 million in 2011 , a growth of about 32 percent , according to Census 2011 .
