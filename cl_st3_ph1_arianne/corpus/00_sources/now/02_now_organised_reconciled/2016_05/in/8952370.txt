In many ways the growth debate is purely GDP oriented when much of the world is looking for answers beyond GDP . Sometimes , economic and market analysts become cheerleaders for any rise in consumption that can raise average economic indicators without going into the details . <p> The discussion on climate change is as much about nature of growth as about sustainable development .
