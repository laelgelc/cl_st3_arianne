<h> New rules mandate NMC to recycle construction waste <p> Nagpur : Construction and demolition materials can be seen dumped by builders or individuals by the roadside , open places , open plots etc . Nagpur Municipal Corporation ( NMC ) lifts , transports and dumps this material at Bhandewadi dump yard by paying private operator Kanak Resources Management Ltd . <p> New rules framed by Central government would now require the civic body ensure there was no littering/dumping of materials by builders or individuals and also it should collect , process and reuse the material . Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) has framed Construction and Demolition Waste Management Rules , 2016 , that state construction and demolition materials were not a waste but a resource . Under these , the state government will have to formulate a policy . " MoEFCC minister Prakash Javadekar revised all rules pertaining to waste management and this will benefit the local bodies and citizens . I have asked the civic chief to table all rules before general body . We will discuss and approve them followed by their implementation , " he said . <p> NMC can also now fix penalty charges and levy on builders/individuals not segregating the construction and demolition materials and not handing it over to the NMC or its appointed agency . NMC can also fix charges for collection , transportation and processing the material . Interestingly , MoEFCC has also made mandatory for the local bodies to utilize 10-20% of total quantum of construction and demolition materials collected from the city in municipal and government contracts . <p> Manager of Kanak Resources Kamlesh Sharma said NMC could manufacture useful products or raw materials from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Delhi where sand and tiles are manufactured from these materials , " he said . <p> RTI activist TH Naidu said rules also made it mandatory for the local bodies to take waste management plan from builders before permitting construction . " The norm for segregating construction and demolition waste and deposit it at collection centre or hand it over to the authorized processing facilities will help in many ways . I had lodged complaints with the NMC many times about construction materials dumped by the side of Sahakar Nagar nullah . Huge amount of debris has been dumped there . NMC engaged Kanak Resources and lifted it but no action was taken against the persons who dumped it , " he said . <p> Naidu added new rules will also give relief to the citizens in interior areas . " Construction and demolition materials can be seen dumped by the roadside obstructing traffic and creating problems .
