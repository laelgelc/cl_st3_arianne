" <p> The concept of van panchayats came into existence in the pre-independence era in the year 1931 with the Forest Panchayat Act ( Forest Councils Act ) of 1931 . Van panchayats have since then been incorporated under Section 28 of the Indian Forest Act 1927 . At present , 12,064 van panchyats with more than 50% women as their representatives and volunteers manage about 5,23289 hectares of forest land in the hill state which comprises about 14% area of the state . <p> In fact , most of these village bodies have women in majority especially within the various hill districts like Nainital , Chamoli , Champawat , Pauri , Tehri , Uttarkashi , Dehradun etc . They are involved in the enhancement of forest cover , sustainable management and protection of existing forests .
