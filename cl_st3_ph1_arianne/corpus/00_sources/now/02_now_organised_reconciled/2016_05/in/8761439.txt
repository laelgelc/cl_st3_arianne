<h> India may burn its ivory stockpile as poaching deterrent <p> Close on the heels of Kenya , which recently set on fire over a 100 ton of its ivory stockpile , experts in India are mulling a similar provision to destroy the " white gold " to send a strong message against poaching . <p> " We ask the State forest departments to burn it ( ivory ) publicly or in the presence of the media , " Project Elephant Director R. K. Srivastava told PTI from Delhi . <p> However , he admitted that few States in the country had so far come forward to make an ivory bonfire . <p> Part of the Union Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , Project Elephant provides financial and technical support to all the 16 major elephant-bearing States of the country . <p> Karnataka 's Chief Wildlife Warden M. B. Hosmat said they had mooted a proposal to burn its ivory stockpile but the proposal is waiting the government 's approval . <p> " It will be for the first time that we 'll burn it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ preliminary stage . It will take some time for us to implement it , " he said . <p> Karnataka is estimated to have the largest quantity of ivory in India followed by Tamil Nadu , Kerala and Odisha as they have the largest number of tusker population . <p> Prof R. Sukumar , well-known expert on Asian elephants , estimates that India must be having around 30 tons of ivory which is growing every year . <p> " Most of it comes from natural death of tuskers . Some is also seized from poachers through the forest department , customs and police , " said the ecologist who works at the Centre for Ecological Sciences in the Indian Institute of Science , Bengaluru . <p> There are chances of pilferage from the stock in custody of various State or central authorities as there is a heavy demand for ivory which is used for ornamental and decorative purposes . <p> In the largest-ever pile of ivory set alight anywhere in the world , Kenya had recently destroyed 105 tons of ivory including those confiscated from poachers and accruing naturally from mortality @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trade and poaching claiming 30,000 elephants every year . <p> Under the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species ( CITES ) , to which India is a signatory , ivory trading is banned .
