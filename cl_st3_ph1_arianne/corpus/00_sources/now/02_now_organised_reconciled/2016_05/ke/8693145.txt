<p> GROOTFONTEIN Namibia ( Xinhua ) -- Tourists visit the Hoba Meteorite near Grootfontein of northern Namibia . Discovered in 1920 at a farm near Grootfontein of Namibia , the Hoba Meteorite , which mainly consists of iron and nickel , is the largest single meteorite known in the world today . It weighs about 60 tons and measures about 2.95 meters by 2.84 meters . Its thickness varies between 0.75 meter and 1.22 meters . The Namibian government opened a tourist center at the site in 1987 , effectively preventing vandalism to the meteorite ever since .
