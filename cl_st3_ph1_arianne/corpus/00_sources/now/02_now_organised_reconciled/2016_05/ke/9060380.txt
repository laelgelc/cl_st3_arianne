<h> Repatriation of Somali refugees not reversible , Uhuru says <p> May . 27 , 2016 , 3:00 pm <p> By PSCU <p> President Uhuru Kenyatta with UN Deputy Secretary General Jan Eliasson , and Foreign Affairs CS Amina Mohamed . The Deputy Secretary General paid Uhuru a courtesy call in State House on Friday May 27 . Photo/PSCU . <h> Pages <p> Kenya 's decision to repatriate Somali refugees back to their country is not reversible , President Uhuru Kenyatta has said . <p> The President said the process of repatriation has already began and called on the global community to partner with Kenya to ensure its success . <p> " The train has already left the station . It 's now up to those who are interested at seeing the success of the journey to come on board , " he said . <p> Uhuru was speaking during talks with the UN Deputy Secretary-General Jan Eliasson , who paid him a courtesy call on Friday at State House , Nairobi . <p> He said Kenya 's action should not be misinterpreted as a move to abandon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ refugees from neighbouring nations for decades . <p> He emphasised that Kenya viewed the repatriation of the refugees as one way of stabilising Somalia . <p> The President assured the international community that Kenya will handle the repatriation process in accordance to the international standards , and in the most humane way . <p> He also called on all UN member states to support the move . <p> Uhuru said because of the large population of refugees , natural resources such as water and pasture were becoming scarce in the areas they inhabited , accelerating the threat of conflicts in the region . <p> He added that the refugee issue is a shared responsibility and hence the international community had a duty to support Kenya in ensuring that Somali refugees are repatriated in the best way possible . <p> The President said if the challenge of Somali refugees is not dealt with now , it could pose a major conflict in the future . <p> " We want Somalia helped to take back its citizens and be able to continue with their lives back home , " he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stabilising the region , especially Somalia , and South Sudan . <p> He said he will communicate Kenya 's position on repatriation to the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugee Filippo Grand once he gets back to New York . <p> Eliasson expressed gratitude to the President and the people of Kenya for their hospitality which has helped citizens of neighbouring countries in times of great need . <p> The UN Deputy Secretary General said , the United Nations shares Kenya 's concerns and it will look for ways and means of partnering on the issue of refugee . <p> On environment , Uhuru said Kenya is committed to conservation efforts because Africa is more affected by global warming even though it is the least contributor . <p> Uhuru said Africa is a big stakeholder on environmental conservation and will continue taking lead in prioritising programmes aimed at dealing with climate change .
