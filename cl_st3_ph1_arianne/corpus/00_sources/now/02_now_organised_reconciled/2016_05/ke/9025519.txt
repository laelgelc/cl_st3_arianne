<p> Nairobi is set to benefit from a two-year Sh18.9 billion disaster resilient grant after it was picked to join the 100 Resilient Cities network worldwide . <p> The grant is through a partnership between the Rockefeller Foundation and the 100 Resilient Cities Network which will fund cities to adapt to emergencies and other rapid urbanisation crises . <p> Through the network , Nairobi will be linked to the best development practices of other cities in the network through partners in the public , private , academia and in different sectors in technology , infrastructure , land planning , innovative finance as well as community engagements . <p> The 100 Resilient Cities Network president Michael Berkowitz said Nairobi is set to benefit from approximately Sh1.05 billion per annum to cater for sustainable development programmes and mitigate climate change , rapid urbanisation , insecurity , infrastructure development and other modern challenges . <p> The county government is also to pick a chief resilience officer who will be paid directly for two years by Rockefeller Foundation . <p> Acknowledging the naming of the city in the network yesterday , Governor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from other model cities mechanisms of addressing acute and chronic challenges . <p> " This new platform will enable Nairobi City develop a sound strategy to avert and bounce back from stresses and shocks that are part of a growing city .
