<h> DP Ruto , Nkaissery in Turkey for summit <h> share <h> DP Ruto in Turkey for summit . <p> DPPS @PeopleDailyKe <p> Deputy President William Ruto is in Istanbul , Turkey for the World Humanitarian Summit . Accompanied by Interior Cabinet secretary Joseph Nkaissery , PSs Karanja Kibicho ( Interior ) and Monica Juma ( Foreign Affairs ) , the DP is expected to state Kenya 's position on the refugee crisis at the summit hosted by United Nations Secretary General Ban Ki-moon and Turkish Prime Minister . <p> The two-day meeting which kicks off today will discuss the humanitarian crises in the world including , the refugee crisis in Kenya , Turkey and other countries , natural disasters and climate change . The summit will also deliberate on the need to commit nations to address forcible displacement of persons . <p> The meeting comes in the wake of Kenya 's decision to close Daadab refugee camp on security grounds . The move has elicited mixed reactions , with the UN and developed countries urging Kenya to rescind its decision .
