<p> HO CHI MINH CITY - AFP May 25 , 2016 - Barack Obama fielded questions Wednesday on everything from rap and weed smoking to his good looks at a lively meeting with young Vietnamese , who see the US leader as a far cry from their staid Communist rulers . The president received a huge cheer as he took to the stage in a 1-party authoritarian State where politics is opaque and young people 's voices are rarely heard .
