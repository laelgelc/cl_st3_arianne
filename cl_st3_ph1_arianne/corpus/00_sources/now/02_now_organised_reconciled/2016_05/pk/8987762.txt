PHOTO : INP <p> KARACHI : Last year , Karachi lost over 1,200 of its residents to the deadly heatwave that coincided with the start of Ramazan . This year too , the mercury seems to be climbing at similar levels and , unless preparations are made in advance , the consequences can be as fatal as last year . <p> Even though other cities of Sindh experience temperatures much higher than Karachi , such as Larkana , Jacobabad and Mithi , the mortality rates were lower as locals in other cities were well adapted to cope with the rising heat . Figures provided by the Provincial Disaster Management Authority Sindh revealed that 60% of the deaths took place at homes , while 40% of them occurred due to direct sun exposure . <p> Lack of awareness was the major issue last year , leading to the high number of deaths in Karachi , according to Strengthening Participatory Organisation 's chief executive Naseer Memon .
