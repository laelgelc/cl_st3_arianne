<p> NIAMEY : Some 100,000 people across the arid West African country of Niger will likely be hit by massive flooding this year , the United Nations warned on Tuesday . <p> Heavy rains are set to hit multiple regions in the poor country and may affect about 105,000 people , the UN 's Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs ( OCHA ) said in a statement . <p> " A comprehensive contingency plan " will be put in place as soon as about a tenth of the total population of 18 million is affected , the UN agency added . <p> OCHA said it has begun to gather resources to help possible victims . Local authorities are meanwhile building levees along the Niger River 's embankment to protect the thousands living in the capital Niamey , state media reported . <p> The river , Africa 's third largest , has a flood basin of over two million square kilometres ( 770,000 square miles ) which is home to more than 100 million people from Guinea to Nigeria . <p> Climate change has wreaked havoc in Niger @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ food shortages - buffeting the lives and livelihoods of millions of the country 's farmers . <p> Flooding in 2012 killed more than 100 people , affected more than half a million Nigeriens and caused at least 135 million euros ( $145 million ) worth of damage , according to the disaster prevention office . <p> Floods similarly killed dozens of people and affected hundreds of thousands in 2014 and 2015 . <p> Niger 's inhabitants are no strangers to hardship and the landlocked former French colony is already one of the world 's least developed countries , ranking 187th on the UN Human Development Index . <p> Global warming is only worsening the problems , with the steadily encroaching desert now covering three-quarters of Niger . The sands are encroaching on the Niger River , threatening the survival of several millions living in its basin .
