<p> ISLAMABAD : The federal government is fully committed to replace mercury-based technologies and products with better technology . <p> This was stated by Ministry of Climate Change Joint Secretary Iftikharul Hassan at the national inception workshop , which discussed the project on the development of Minamata Initial Assessment in Pakistan . <p> The workshop aimed at drawing the attention of national and international community to control and eliminate the widespread exposure of mercury due to human-generated sources . <p> " The Government of Pakistan is fully committed to replace mercury-based technologies and products in-line with decisions being taken by opting for friendly technologies and products in Pakistan , " he said . <p> Ministry of Climate Change Secretary Syed Abu Ahmad Akif said sound procedures and management techniques should be devised to handle the hazardous effects of mercury and other chemicals . <p> Minamata Convention on Mercury addresses to phase out mercury-added products like thermometres , compact florescent lamp ( CFLs ) , batteries and dental fillings by 2020 , including elimination of Chlor-alkali ( caustic soda ) production by 2025.
