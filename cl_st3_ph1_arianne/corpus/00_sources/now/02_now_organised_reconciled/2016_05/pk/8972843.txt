<p> ISLAMABAD : The Climate Change secretary has pledged to stem biodiversity loss in the country in collaboration with all provincial forest and wildlife departments and non-governmental organisations . <p> " We must realise that loss of biodiversity in all parts of the country that has speeded up alarmingly and many wildlife and plant species are on the verge of extinction , " the Secretary Syed Abu Ahmad Akif stressed while addressing a concluding ceremony of the three-day national awareness-raising and training workshop on ' Biodiversity conservation , Access to Genetic Resources and Fair and Equitable Sharing of the their Benefits under the United Nations ' Nagoya Protocol ' on Sunday , said a press release . <p> The International Day for Biological Diversity was also celebrated by the Climate Change Ministry to highlight the role of biodiversity conservation for poverty reduction and achieving the United Nations ' 17 Sustainable Development Goals . The goals were adopted last year on September 25 and are to be achieved by year 2030 . <p> The Biological Diversity Day is celebrated every year on May 22nd . It underpins peoples ' livelihoods and sustainable development in all realms of socio-economical activity , including agriculture , forestry , fisheries and tourism , among others . By halting biodiversity loss , we are investing in people , their lives and their well-being . " <p> Pakistan provides habitats for 188 mammal species , out of which six are endemic and 20 are threatened with extinction . There are 666 bird species , out of which 25 are endangered .
