<p> Rory Mulcahy SC for Apple Distribution Ltd told the hearing that the company was happy to address all the concerns raised by locals . These included flooding fears , energy usage , environmental and climate change impact and wildlife issues . <p> Mr Mulcahy said a range of experts would address the concerns . He said while the company was committed to using renewable energy , it was not proposing any generating facilities on site but would be connected to the national grid . <p> He said the development would not require increased generation capacity and the company would enter into a contract with a renewable energy supplier . Apple has claimed that it will run the centre on 100pc renewable energy . The 24,500 sq m centre in Athenry was granted planning permission by Galway County Council .
