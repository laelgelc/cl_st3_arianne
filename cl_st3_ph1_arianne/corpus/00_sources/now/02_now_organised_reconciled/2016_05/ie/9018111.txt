<h> Apple accused of ' jumping on global warming gravy-train ' at city oral hearing <p> Galway Bay fm newsroom -- Apple has been accused of ' jumping on the global warming gravy-train ' as day two of the oral hearing on the company 's Athenry data centre gets underway . <p> ' Energy and climate change impacts ' is the first of three major topics being discussed at the hearing , which is expected to run until the end of the week . <p> Around 40 people have gathered at the Connacht Hotel for this morning 's proceedings . <p> The first submission of the day , from a member of the public , outlined concerns over Apple 's energy policies , saying the company has jumped on the ' global warming , renewable energy gravy-train . ' The appellant says the public will end up subsidising Apple 's significant energy usage , resulting in higher costs for the consumer .
