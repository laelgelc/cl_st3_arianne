<h> Minister for Climate Change urged set up citizens convention <p> The Minister for Climate Change is being urged to set up a citizens convention on how Ireland will handle the issue . <p> Denis Naughten is the first member of an Irish Government to be given the portfolio . <p> Following his appointment a group of academics have written to him to outline the extent of the task ahead and asking that action be taken to inform citizens of the seriousness of it . <p> Barry Mullins , dean of engineering at DCU , said that the population needs to agree on how we will give up our dependence on fossil fuels . <p> " It would reach into every highway and byway , every local community , every town , every city in the country , and offer citizens and community groups the opportunity to learn what the nature of the choices are , and that would feed into essentially a 20 or 30-year vision for how we 're going to escape our addiction to fossil fuels , " he said .
