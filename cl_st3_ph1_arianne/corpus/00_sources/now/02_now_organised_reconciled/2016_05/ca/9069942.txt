" <p> During the morning convocation , grads from the faculties of arts , science and healthy studies received their degrees . <p> Canada 's 21st prime minister , Paul Martin , was also in attendance to receive an honorary doctorate of law degree from BU . <p> " It really is exciting , especially from Brandon University , " Martin told media following the ceremony .
