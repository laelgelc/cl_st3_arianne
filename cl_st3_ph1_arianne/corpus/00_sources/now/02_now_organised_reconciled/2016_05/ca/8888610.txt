<h> Ottawa names review panel for Trans Mountain pipeline expansion <h> Felicity Stone May 17 , 2016 <h> Panel 's report due November 2016 with final decision on pipeline in December <p> A federal decision is in sight for Kinder Morgan 's proposed Trans Mountain pipeline expansion . With reports from the National Energy Board 's recommendation and Environment and Climate Change Canada both due by May 20 , on Tuesday federal Minister of Natural Resources Jim Carr announced the members of the Trans Mountain pipeline expansion project ministerial panel : former Tsawwassen First Nation chief Kim Baird , former Yukon premier Tony Penikett and University of Winnipeg president Annette Trimbee . <p> Kim Baird says the panel will meet for the first time this week to scope out the process and will provide details as they become available . They will start gathering feedback from local communities and groups along the proposed Trans Mountain expansion project route in June and will submit a report to Minister Carr by November 1 .
