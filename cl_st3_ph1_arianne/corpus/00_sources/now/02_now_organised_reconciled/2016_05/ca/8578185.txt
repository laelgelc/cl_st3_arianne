<p> Sun is a source of energy for living beings . Today , it is an important source of renewable energy that is experiencing a global boom . <p> On the one hand , solar energy is clean , relatively easy to install , and provides electricity at peak times when we need it most . On the other hand , solar is expensive and requires storage to provide energy when the sun does n't shine ( for off-grid systems ) . These disadvantages are changing rapidly with dropping panel and battery prices . <p> How does the solar resource in Northwestern Ontario compare to other areas using solar energy ? <p> Ontario receives more sunlight than Germany , a leading nation in per-capita photovoltaic ( PV ) use , and Northwestern Ontario has more sunshine hours than other parts of the province . <p> Since solar PV panels perform better in cooler temperatures , our region has an added performance advantage .
