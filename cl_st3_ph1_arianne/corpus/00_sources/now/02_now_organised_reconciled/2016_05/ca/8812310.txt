The resultant IoT Platform for Chinese Smart Cities will be application compatible with the current Sensity IoT Smart City Platform , thereby providing application developers access to a global market for cities . Outside of China , the joint venture products will be sold under the Sensity platform program . <p> As manufacturers and governments worldwide seek solutions to combat climate change , China is focused on taking the global lead in the market for smart city installations .
