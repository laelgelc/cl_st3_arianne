<h> Comment : It 's time to move beyond the sewage-treatment debate <p> Over the past several years , there has been significant debate regarding sewage treatment for the Capital Regional District . The lack of effective sewage treatment in the CRD has been a topic of significant discussion well beyond Vancouver Island . <p> As an MP from British Columbia and as the parliamentary secretary for Environment and Climate Change ( the department responsible for relevant wastewater treatment regulations ) , I believe it is time to move beyond this debate . It is time for the CRD to move forward in implementing effective sewage treatment . <p> The requirement for new secondary-treatment facilities that must be built by 2020 applies to the Lion 's Gate Treatment plant in North Vancouver and to 14 other cities across Canada ( note that the regulation itself applies to 2,546 wastewater treatment systems across Canada ) . <p> This 2020 secondary treatment requirement represents a minimum level . It certainly will . <p> Federal wastewater systems effluent regulations that require a minimum of secondary treatment specifically take into account local conditions .
