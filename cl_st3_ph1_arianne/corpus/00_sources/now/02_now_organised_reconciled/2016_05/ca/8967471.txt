<p> Canada 's female politicians have been speaking out a great deal lately , in ways that are n't overtly partisan , about their experiences as women and the gendered policy gaps they see . This is heartening , but it is -- and should be -- a careful calculation . <p> On a personal level , no woman wants to be consigned to the " women 's issues " pigeonhole . This worry is itself influenced by sexism , by the idea that the concerns of roughly half the population amount to a fringe interest .
