<p> The advanced farming market will witness a CAGR of 13.44% during the forecast period 2016-2022 . The use of advanced farming will improve monitoring of crop health and animal , quality of the filed , improvement in soil ( moisture and fertility ) , and yield production . It also helps in reducing wastage on the field and improves the efficiency of land and the crop . <p> The usage of technologies improves decision making process in agriculture . Escalating the demand of the food supply chain has mandated the utilization of the agriculture resources . With this , the regulatory bodies have also started developing rural areas by setting up new infrastructure to ensure the benefits of the development in these areas . <p> Over the last few years , with the growing food requirement and climate change concerns , farmers across the globe have started utilizing the technology- and environment-friendly farming practices . Although this transition is mostly in the developed economies , it is expected that in the coming years , even the underdeveloped markets will adopt it .
