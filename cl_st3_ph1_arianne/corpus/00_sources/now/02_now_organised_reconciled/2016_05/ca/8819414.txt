<p> There was a wave of information flowing through the grounds of Ball 's Falls Conservation Area this week . <p> Nearly 5,000 students visited the green space Tuesday to Friday for the annual Niagara Children 's Water Festival , intended to promote water protection and conservation among local youth . <p> The festival , in its 13th year , began as a small-scale event under a single tent on the conservation area property . <p> It has since expanded to include 54 activities that this year drew 90 schools from within the Niagara Peninsula Conservation Authority 's watershed to participate . <p> Students moved from station to station , collecting a drop of knowledge at each regarding topics such as water conservation , hydroelectricity , climate change and loss of wetlands . <p> " The activities all relate to water science , water engineering , water conservation , water protection and water technology , " NPCA events coordinator Brianne Wilson said . <p> Each stop included an interactive element to keep the Grade 2 to Grade 4 students engaged .
