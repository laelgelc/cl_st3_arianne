<p> More than eight in 10 Republicans who support Donald Trump regard refugees from Iraq and Syria as a major threat to the US , according to a survey that lays bare the deep political polarisation in America . <p> " America 's Place in the World " is a survey of foreign policy attitudes conducted periodically by the Pew Research Center . The latest poll was carried out among 2,008 US adults from 12 to 19 April , before Ted Cruz and John Kasich pulled out of the race for the Republican nomination . <p> For those who back the New York billionaire in the primary campaign , the large number of refugees fleeing the conflict in Iraq and Syria is especially worrisome , according to Pew . Some 85% of Republican voters who support Trump say the refugees are a major threat to the US , compared with 74% of those who prefer Cruz and 59% who prefer Kasich .
