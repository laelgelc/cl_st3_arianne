<h> Kew to release State of World 's Plants report <p> On May 10 , The Royal Botanic Gardens , Kew will release a new report on the State of the World 's Plants , as part of an international science and policy symposium . <p> This report , the first output of the Kew 2020 Science strategy , will highlight the global status of plants , providing an annual review of the issues affecting their diversity and abundance . The report will also provide new baseline data against which we can evaluate how plants are faring and how this is changing over time . <p> Kew said : " Plants represent one of the most important constituents of biodiversity , the foundation of most of the world 's ecosystems and hold the potential to tackle many of the world 's present and future challenges . As well being a snapshot of the present status , the report will be forward-looking and will include horizon scanning to identify important and emerging issues , research and knowledge gaps around plants . <p> " The report centres around three main @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to plants , policies and international trade .
