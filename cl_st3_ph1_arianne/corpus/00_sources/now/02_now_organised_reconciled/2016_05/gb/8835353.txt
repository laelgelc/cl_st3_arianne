<h> UK emission cut may exceed the rest of EU <p> Ministers should delay setting stringent new climate change targets so the UK is not left taking more radical action than the rest of Europe , a group of MPs have warned . <p> The Government is obliged under the Climate Change Act to set a target by the end of June for cutting UK carbon emissions in the period 2028-2032 . <p> The Committee on Climate Change ( CCC ) , its official advisors , have recommended it commit to slashing emissions to 57 per cent below 1990 levels -- or about a third below current levels . <p> It says the plan would require a radical shift toward electric cars , green energy and away from using gas for heating and cooking . <p> " Our extra effort would result in no extra reduction in CO2 emissions across Europe as a whole -- just a higher burden on British business and a lower burden on our competitors .
