<h> The day coal power dropped out of the national grid for the first time in more than 100 years <p> The Government is planning to phase out coal energy completely by 2025 PA <p> At midnight on 10 May 2016 , the UK hit an energy milestone . For the first time in over 100 years , the amount of coal being used by the national grid to power Britain 's kettles , computer and televisions fell to zero . And then it stayed at zero for four hours . <p> Two days later , this time for five hours , coal usage fell to zero again . Nuclear , wind , hydro and solar energy powered the national grid in coal 's place . By 13 May , the needle had hit zero four times , for a total of around 25 hours . <p> This historic turning point came on the eve of a Government consultation on phasing out coal energy completely by 2025 . <p> " As part of our plans for a cleaner energy future , we are one of the first countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by 2025 , " a spokesperson for the Department for Energy and Climate Change told The Independent .
