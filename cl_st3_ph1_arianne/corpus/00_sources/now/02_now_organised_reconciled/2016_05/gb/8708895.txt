<h> Attenborough at 90 : his greatest journeys and how to do them <p> The debate about the most recognisable voice on television is , frankly , a short one . David Attenborough 's gentle tones have been a soothing element of the British tapestry for more than six decades , bringing insight and authority to a near-endless array of BBC documentaries on the natural world and the many bestial inhabitants of our planet .
