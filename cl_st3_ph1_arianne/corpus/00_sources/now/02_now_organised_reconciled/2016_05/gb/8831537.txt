<p> A court in Delhi has ruled that Rajendra Pachauri , the former chairman of a Nobel prize-winning UN panel on climate change , will stand trial on charges of stalking and sexual harassment of a former employee . <p> A 29-year-old former employee of The Energy and Resources Institute ( Teri ) , based in the Indian capital , filed a police report against Pachauri last year . She said Pachauri , who led the organisation , had made inappropriate advances soon after she joined in 2013 . <p> Earlier this year police filed 1,400 pages of evidence , including text messages and emails from Pachauri , and testimony from 23 witnesses , many of them Teri employees . Judge Shivani Chauhan said the evidence was sufficient to proceed with the trial . <p> " There are allegations against the accused that he made sexually coloured remarks upon the complainant on several occasions , " she said .
