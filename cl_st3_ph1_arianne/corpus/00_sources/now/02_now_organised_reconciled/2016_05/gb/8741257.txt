<h> Power from footsteps : London trials intelligent streets <p> The UK capital 's public transport operator Transport for London ( TfL ) plans to run a trial of its ' intelligent street ' concept in a traffic-free area just off Oxford Street . The project will use solar and innovative kinetic power techniques to meet the street 's energy needs , with the long-term goal of using it as a model for more pedestrianised areas in the capital . <p> TfL has partnered with the New West End Company ( NWEC ) , a business that is working on 25 different streets in London to power them renewably and transform them into more pedestrian-friendly retail destinations . The planned makeover is of Bird Street , which lies just off Oxford Street in central London . <p> Bird Street is already a traffic-free space and will now be turned into an ' intelligent street ' that can generate its own renewable electricity from solar and kinetic energy technology , thus creating a model for deployment elsewhere in the city .
