<h> Environmental groups demand end to logging of Australia 's native forests <p> An area of the Tasmania 's Styx forest after logging . A report produced last week by the National Parks Association of NSW concluded Australia 's regional forestry agreements resulted in an increase in threatened species . Photograph : Hancock/EPA <p> More than 30 environmental groups have signed a statement demanding that agreements allowing the logging of Australian native forests not be renewed . <p> Australia 's 10 regional forestry agreements ( RFAs ) were signed between 1997 and 2001 , each running for 20 years , with the first two expiring in 2017 . <p> The agreements between state and federal governments mean proposals to log in designated native forests are n't required to be approved through the usual federal process , under the Environmental Protection and Biodiversity Conservation Act ( EPBC ) . <p> The agreements were trumpeted as establishing the " conservation and sustainable management of Australia 's native forests " with the aim of providing " certainty for forest-based industries , forest-dependent communities and conservation " . <p> The report 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as well as the logging industry 's own data , and found the RFAs cost the states huge sums , did n't decrease disputes over logging and worsened the environmental outcomes . <p> The report also noted the agreements , which were designed in the 1990s , did n't take climate change into account , and logging of native forests is a significant source of greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The federal government 's current policy is to extend the RFAs when they expire . The report concluded that " would constitute an irrational decision on environmental , economic and social grounds " . <p> The statement , signed by more than 30 environment groups , was sent to the environment minister , Greg Hunt , Labor 's environment spokesperson , Mark Butler , and the Greens ' environment spokesperson , Janet Rice . <p> " The groups endorsing this statement will not accept extension , rollover or renewal of regional forest agreements .
