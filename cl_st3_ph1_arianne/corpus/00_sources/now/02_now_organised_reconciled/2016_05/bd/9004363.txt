<h> $176.06mn deal signed with WB to boost agri productivity <p> on May 24 , 2016 <p> businessnews24bd.com <p> The government of Bangladesh on Tuesday signed a $176.06 million financing agreement with the World Bank to increase the agricultural productivity and have access to markets for over one million poor farmers , particularly women.Economic Relations Division ( ERD ) Senior Secretary Mohammad Mejbahuddin and World Bank Country Director Qimiao Fan signed the deal on behalf of the government and the World Bank respectively at the ERD conference room in the afternoon to implement the National Agricultural Technology Programme Phase II Project.The overall objective of the project is to improve the national agricultural productivity , market linkage and farm income , with a particular focus on small , marginal and female farmers . <p> The World Bank is acting as administrator of USAID grant ( Trust Fund ) for the project . <p> Apart from the World Bank fund , the International Fund for Agricultural Development ( IFAD ) will provide $ 23.86 million as loan while USAID will provide $ 7.43 million as grant for the project . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Association , which provides grants or zero to low interest loans , has a 38-year term , including a six-year grace period , and a service charge of 0.75 percent and commitment fee of 0.50 percent of 1 percent on the withdrawn financing balance . <p> The project will be implemented by September 2021 by Bangladesh Agricultural Research Council ( BARC ) , Department of Agricultural Extension ( DAE ) , Project Management Unit under the Ministry of Agriculture and by the Department of Fisheries and Department of Livestock Services under the Ministry of Fisheries and Livestock . <p> The Second National Agricultural Technology Programme ( NATP II ) is expected to increase and diversify the productivity of crops , livestock and fisheries , and enhance poor farmers ' access to markets . <p> The project will help enhance nutrition by ensuring food safety and more diversified food consumption . Through emphasising demand-driven research and modern agricultural technology , the project will help increase farm yields and adaptation to climate change . <p> " Building on the success of earlier World Bank support , this project will help achieve food security @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ through safer and more diversified food , " said Qimiao Fan , World Bank Country Director for Bangladesh , Bhutan and Nepal . <p> He said Bangladesh has done remarkably well in improving agricultural productivity and food security . " With over 40 percent of the total workforce engaged in agriculture , the sector plays an important role in the country 's growth and development and we hope with this support to ensure that poor farmers , particularly women , share in the productivity gains of the sector . " <p> The project will be implemented in 57 districts where it will benefit small-scale farmers through stronger linkages with research , agricultural extension services , farmer groups , and on-farm demonstrations to promote improved agricultural technologies . <p> The project will focus on training for farmers and agricultural extension field staff to ensure knowledge sharing and technology transfer .
