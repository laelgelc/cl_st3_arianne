<h> Youth delegate at Climate Change conference Briar Wyatt <p> DELWYN DICKEY <p> Last updated 13:49 , October 25 2016 <p> Delwyn Dickey <p> Briar Wyatt of Stanmore Bay is off to Morocco to the 22 Conference of the Parties ( COP22 ) of the United Nations Framework for the Convention on Climate Change as part of the New Zealand Youth Delegation . <p> As an ecology and environmental science student at the University of Auckland , Briar Wyatt of Stanmore Bay is acutely aware of the impacts climate change will have on her future . <p> When the next United Nations Convention on Climate Change kicks off in Morocco next month , the 21 year old will be among a small group of young adults flying the flag for the youth of New Zealand . <p> As part of the New Zealand Youth delegation , Wyatt is critical of the government 's plans to reduce green house gas emissions after ratifying the Paris Agreement in October . <p> New Zealand was a big purchaser of carbon credits from Russia and the Ukraine that produced millions of tonnes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had they met their emissions targets domestically . <p> " The biggest change needed is the government 's attitude , " she says . " They 're the only ones who think we 're doing enough and are standing in the way of real action . " <p> " There 's little point signing the Paris agreement if all we 're doing is buying fake carbon credits from overseas . " <p> Also currently campaigning against the use of plastic microbeads , Wyatt will be putting her qualifications to good use when she starts in Auckland Councils Waste Solutions Department .
