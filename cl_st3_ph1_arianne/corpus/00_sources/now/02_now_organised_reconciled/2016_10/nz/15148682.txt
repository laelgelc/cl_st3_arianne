<h> Morocco protesters take to streets again over fishmonger 's death <p> Protesters marched through the centre of Al-Hoceima to demonstrate against the death of a fish seller who was killed by the crushing mechanism of a rubbish truck . <p> Thousands of Moroccans have filled the streets , protesting for a fourth day over the death of a fishmonger crushed in a garbage truck , after a confrontation with police who confiscated his produce . <p> The death of Mouhcine Fikri on Friday ( local time ) has triggered one of the largest protests nationwide since 2011 when the February 20 movement organised demonstrations for democratic reform inspired by the Arab Spring uprisings across the region . <p> Large-scale protests are rare in Morocco , where the king still holds ultimate sway . Morocco calmed Arab Spring-style protests in 2011 with reforms , spending and tougher security while leaders in Tunisia , Egypt and Libya were swept from power . <p> STRINGER/REUTERS <p> The protest is among the largest seen in Morocco in recent years . <p> A frenzy of angry postings on social media and protest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ describe the royal establishment . <p> " Protests will continue until all the responsible for that crime are punished , " said Al-Houssine Lmrabet , one of the protests organisers . " We also want guarantees that this will not happen again . This should start by cleaning the public administration of corrupted people . " <p> STRINGER/REUTERS <p> Mourners carry the coffin of fishmonger Mouhcine Fikri who was crushed to death inside a rubbish truck as he tried to retrieve fish confiscated by police in the northern town of Al-Hoceima . <p> Fikri 's death parallels how Tunisia 's uprising began in 2011 , triggering revolts across the region , after a young man set himself on fire in desperation because police confiscated fruit and vegetables he was selling . <p> Morocco 's protests erupted at a sensitive moment as the kingdom prepares to host the 2016 United Nations climate change conference in November and the prime minister begins to form a coalition government after elections this month . <p> Smaller protests were also held on Monday ( local time ) evening in several other cities and towns including the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ central town of Settat . High school students protested earlier in the northern town , activists said . <p> In an effort to calm tensions , King Mohamed , currently on a tour of Africa , ordered the interior minister to visit the victim 's family and present royal condolences . The government also promised an investigation . <p> Mouhcine Fikri had fish confiscated by police after he bought it at the port . Local authorities have banned swordfish fishing and sales in this season . <p> According to local media and authorities , Fikri jumped inside the trash truck that police used to destroy the confiscated fish in a desperate attempt to recover it and he was caught inside the crusher . <p> Activists accused police officers of ordering garbage men to crush Fikri , but the Moroccan police ( DGSN ) denied those accusations in a statement on Sunday ( local time ) . <p> Moroccan authorities heavily police protests , nervous over popular unrest since the 2011 protests . During those protests the king devolved some of his authority to an elected government in a constitutional reform .
