<h> Calls for Super Fund to stop investing in fossil fuels <p> The New Zealand Super Fund is being asked to stop investing in the industry that 's killing our planet . <p> Eleven organisations , including the likes of Oxfam New Zealand and the Public Service Association , have written a letter to the Super Fund asking it to stop investing in the fossil fuel industry . <p> Executive director of 350 Aotearoa Niamh O'Flynn said it 's time the industry is seen for what it is . <p> " The fossil fuel industry is basically the heart of the beast of climate change ... they 're the industry that 's blocked all progress of renewable 's , they 're the industry that 's basically melting the planet with their emissions . " <p> O'Flynn said it 's a message the public would easily support . <p> " Once people understand that people like Super Fund are invested in the industries that are literally killing our planet , people are quite concerned . "
