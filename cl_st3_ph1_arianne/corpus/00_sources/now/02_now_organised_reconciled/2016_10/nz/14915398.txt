<p> Veganism . Throw that one word into a news article , and suddenly World War III erupts . The comment section turns into a minefield of vitriol . <p> It 's unclear who fired the first shots , but there 's plenty flying from both side . For a diet based on compassion and non-violence , it sure does stir up some rough conversations . <p> Take the recent sheep video . A woman saw some sheep , cried , and posted it to her Youtube page . Suddenly the whole country was talking about it . Everyone had to get their opinion in , and many of them were less than pleasant . <p> She received an avalanche of threats . Meat-eaters also got some vitriol thrown their way . <p> The mere fact that she was vegan sparked another flurry of hateful comments , and the fight raged on . <p> Most conversations around veganism devolve into mud-slinging rather than actual discussions . <p> It 's a shame , really .
