The Gardens are already the home of another giant : the Great telescope . Known as the Leviathan , it was built in front of the Castle in the 1840s and was the world 's largest telescope for 70 years . The 3rd Earl who constructed this also planted our first redwoods , the largest of which is now over 30m tall . Both types of sequoia flourish happily at Birr and with their rapid growth rate are already , producing some very tall trees . As many children have experienced , Birr Castle Gardens added another giant to its attractions in 2013 with a giant tree house . Birr Castle is well known for its world-famous gardens .
