<h> Halloween brought to Leitrim classrooms <p> Halloween is being brought to Leitrim classrooms with a new campaign that gives students the chance to go to school dressed up while helping people living the horrors of poverty overseas . <p> Primary and secondary schools are signing up for the Hunger Heroes campaign , run by Irish aid agency Concern Worldwide , which teaches students about world hunger and food waste . <p> The campaign involves fun-filled awareness and fundraising activities , like a Hunger Heroes Day where students dress up for one day . <p> Concern 's school programme officer , Claire Marshall , said : " While Halloween is a fun holiday for us here in Ireland , a real horror exists for millions of people living in hunger and suffering the effects of climate change . <p> " People living in countries like Malawi , Mozambique and Ethiopia live in what would to us be a nightmare with their livelihoods and food sources destroyed by droughts and floods due to the effects of climate change , exacerbated by the El Nino and La Nina weather events @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with conflicts and other issues , is that every night , 795 million people go to bed hungry and every morning 66 million children go to school hungry .
