<h> World Bank to fight climate change through sports <p> The World Bank has revealed that they will engage more than hundred ( 100 ) Olympic and Paralympic athletes globally as part of the " Connect4Climate " programme to launch a social media campaign against climate change , ahead of the upcoming climate conference in Marrakesh , Morocco from ( 7-18 ) November , 2016 . <p> The motivation for the campaign stems from the recent proclamation of a set of new ' federal and non-federal actions ' against the climate by President , Obama at the inaugural national ' Green Sports Day ' on October , 6 at the White House . <p> Under the non-federal actions , President , Obama launched the ' Sports4Climate ' programme to help combat the climate through sports . <p> Thus , based on Obama 's ' Sports4Climate ' programme- " The World Bank 's ' Connet4Climate ' program will engage athletes globally under the Sport4Climate Initiative , to challenge the international community to accelerate negotiations in order to exceed the 1.5degree Celsius target for average global temperature rise .
