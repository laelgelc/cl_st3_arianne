<h> We need to link our education to farming - Amidu Sulemana <p> Alhaji Amin Amidu Sulemana , Upper West Regional Minister , has said there is the need to develop an appropriate curriculum to link education to farming for children to appreciate the importance of farming . <p> He said there is a gap between education and farming ; and educated youth tend to shy away from farming simply because they have gone to school . <p> " Farming pays more than any other job but today , the youth shy away from it because they have not properly understood the concept of farming very well , " he said . <p> Alhaji Sulemana said this at the First Upper West Food Day held at Jirapa in the Jirapa District of the Upper West Region . <p> The Ministry of Food and Agriculture ( MoFA ) and Yara , an agro input organization jointly organized the Day to coincide with the 71st World Food Day Celebration . <p> Alhaji Sulemana called on stakeholders to pool resources to support farmers and livestock producers to help increase their scales @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ meat . <p> He said the designation of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 13 which was aimed at taking urgent action to combat climate change and its impact was a clear demonstration of the global commitment to dealing with climate change and its impact . <p> He said government had developed and launched a National Climate Change Policy ( NCCP ) and had started its implementation across the entire country to address the teething challenges of climate change in all sectors of the economy . <p> Mr Kwaku Minka Fordjour , the Upper West Regional Director of MoFA , said the Day was an event meant to drum home the need to increase food productivity . <p> He said globally , about 800 million people did not have access to food , and this needed concerted efforts from all stakeholders to address the challenge . <p> Mr Fordjour said the Upper West Region was faced with a double edge challenge which included low soil fertility and a single farming season which hindered its quest to increase food production in the region . <p> Dr Abebe Hankore , Head of Sub/Area @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said research reveals that a significant percentage of the food produced often got lost at the dining table . <p> He urged all to share their food with others if they genuinely believed they could not finish what was served them to prevent wastage . <p> The global theme for this year 's World Food Day celebration is : " Climate is Changing , Food and Agriculture must too " while the sub theme for the regional celebration is : " Building Resilience in Food Security , a Prerequisite for Social Development " .
