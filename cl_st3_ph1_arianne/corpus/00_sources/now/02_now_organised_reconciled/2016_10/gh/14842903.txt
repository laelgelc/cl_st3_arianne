<p> Britain under its Climate Change Act committed to cutting emissions by 80 percent of the benchmark level from 1990 by 2050 . The British Committee on Climate Change said emissions are now 38 percent below 1990 levels even as the economy grew by 60 percent since then . <p> John Gummer , the chairman of the committee , said the British government 's support for last year 's Paris climate agreement and steadfast support for low-carbon initiatives deserves credit , though ignoring things like drafty homes and heating systems means the targets could be out of reach . <p> " Action is needed now to ensure the U.K. can deliver its climate obligations at least cost , " he said in a statement . <p> British Prime Minister Theresa May in September pledged to ratify the Paris climate agreement . European ministers signed off on the arrangement last month and , since British voters decided to leave the European Union , the committee 's report said the government needed to at least replicate standing climate arrangements in place with its EU partners . <p> " As they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ half of the required emissions reduction to 2030 , " the report said . <p> The warning follows a report Wednesday from the U.S. Energy Information Administration that emissions of carbon dioxide , a potent greenhouse gas , declined in the U.S. power sector to their lowest levels in a quarter century . <p> The EIA attributed the decline to a move in the power sector away from coal and toward natural gas , along with a gradual embrace of renewable resources like wind and solar power .
