<h> Ghana seeks international support to curb galamsey <p> The Minister of Environment , Science , Technology and Innovation , Mahama Ayariga , is urging for collaboration with international agencies to deal with the menace of illegal mining activities in Ghana . <p> According to the Minister , illegal mining persists as a critical challenge to Ghana 's ability in addressing climate change issues . <p> The unregulated activities of the informal miners have led to undesirable effects including destruction of vegetation , pollution of water bodies as well as a marked reduction in agricultural production , among others . <p> The lack of implementation of interventions has been cited as one of the key factors that have led to the perpetration of the act . <p> But the sector Minister admits Ghana could win the fight if it adopts strategies through collaboration with other developed societies . <p> " We have that issue that we are working on and I believe that it is one area where we can forge stronger partnership to learn from one another and to build a capacity as an EPA to be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be involved to deal with the issues , " Mr. Ayariga stated . <p> He was speaking at an engagement session involving media practitioners , with the Environmental Protection Agency of the US , in Accra . <p> According to Mr. Ayariga , the impact of the illegitimate activities on agriculture and food production threatens human lives as the quality of life may be compromised . <p> " Agricultural activities taking place in locations where the waters are polluted , producing yields that are transported to market centers in urban communities that take their soul remotely related to the sites of these illegal activities , " he explained . <p> Galamsey activities have been more pronounced in areas such as Obuasi in the Ashanti region , Wassa Amenfi West , East and Central districts in the Western region as well as Dunkwa in the Central region . <p> Citi FM 's Obrempong
