<p> The risks posed by climate change are not limited to coffee . In developing countries , nearly two thirds of the extremely poor earn a living from agriculture . They are on the frontline in the fight against climate change , yet they often lack the finance and knowledge to withstand its effects . This can prove devastating to their livelihoods . <p> So what can be done to address this poverty imbalance and empower smallholder farmers ?
