<p> THE world has entered a new era of " climate change reality " after pollution levels broke through a symbolic threshold , scientists claim . <p> Carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere reached 400 parts per million ( PPM ) globally for the first time in history , according to annual results from the World Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) . <p> Experts say the results mark the start of an era , with C02 concentrations predicted to remain above the benchmark for generations . <p> According to the WMO , carbon dioxide in the atmosphere was at around 278PPM -- a level said to represent a natural balance -- before the industrial revolution . <p> However , agriculture , industry and other aspects of human activity are said to have caused a 40 per cent increase in the warming effect on the planet from 1990 to 2015 . <p> The findings come despite reasonably static human-generated carbon dioxide emissions in 2014-15 .
