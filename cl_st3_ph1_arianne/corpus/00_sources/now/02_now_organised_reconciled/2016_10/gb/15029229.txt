REUTERS/Kym Yano , National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration/Handout File Photo <p> Scientists raised the alert level for a remote Aleutian volcano on Monday after an explosion was detected on the mountain and heard by residents of a tiny village some 45 miles ( 72 km ) away , a monitoring website said . <p> Cleveland Volcano , a 5,676-foot ( 1,730-metre ) peak on the uninhabited Chuginadak Island , about 940 miles ( 1,504 km ) southwest of Anchorage , was raised to orange from yellow by the Alaska Volcano Observatory . <p> The orange code , the second-highest on the scale , is issued when a volcano is " exhibiting heightened or escalating unrest with increased potential of eruption , " according to the observatory .
