Buying a tanker of water privately costs 5,000 rupees ( $34 ) , more than most people can pay . <p> Instead the village 's farmers and traders buy their water in one-liter bottles from small-scale vendors who charge four to 10 rupees a liter ( less than one US cent ) for drinking water brought from about 30 kilometers away . <p> " We buy drinking water , and try to use other sources for cooking and washing , " said Sarath Mahanama , one villager .
