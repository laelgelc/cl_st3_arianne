" <p> Genetic modification may produce excellent results in the laboratory , which " may be advantageous for some , but have ruinous effects for others , " he said . <p> " Genetic selection of a quality of plant may produce impressive results in terms of yield , but have we considered the terrain that loses its productive capacity , farmers who no longer have pasture for their livestock , and water resources that become unusable ?
