<h> Swiss fund wants ' hypocrite ' DiCaprio to return US$25m <p> Film star was in London for the premiere of a climate change documentary and was invited to press conference about 1MDB held by Bruno Manser Fund . <p> by Raevathi Supramaniam in London <p> LONDON : Swiss-based activist group Bruno Manser Fund has urged actor Leonardo DiCaprio to return to the Malaysian people his earnings of about US$25 million from the movie " Wolf of Wall Street " . <p> The fund also urged DiCaprio to stand down as the United Nations Messenger of Peace for climate change . <p> " DiCaprio lacks the credibility for such a public role , " said the fund 's executive director , Lukas Straumann , who alleged that DiCaprio and his foundation had benefited from his involvement with Jho Low and Riza Aziz , with over US$2 million in donations received by his foundation . <p> " He owes the Malaysian people accountability on his financial ties to Jho Low and Riza Aziz , " Straumann said . <p> The Bruno Manser Fund is named after a Swiss activist @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Penan tribe and has been active on issues concerning the Sarawak rainforest and native rights . <p> The London press conference was also attended by Centre to Combat Corruption and Cronyism representative Sudhagaran Stanley , and Sarawak Report editor Clare Rewcastle . <p> DiCaprio was in London for the premiere of his climate change documentary " Before the Flood " .
