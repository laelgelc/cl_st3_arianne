<h> UNDP allocates USD 38mn to support dry zone communities in Sri Lanka <p> Oct 20 , 2016 ( LBO ) -- The United Nations Development Programme announced the commencement of a 38.1 million dollar project under the Green Climate Fund to improve agriculture-based livelihoods of farmers in the dry zone . <p> " The project will allow assistance to be provided to 770,000 people in three river basins -- Malwatu Oya , Yan Oya and Mi Oya -- who are vulnerable to climate change , " UNDP said in a statement . <p> " Around 1.2 million people living in the same areas will indirectly benefit from the project . " <p> UNDP will work with a number of government institutions and it will be implemented from 2017 to 2024 . <p> Further to the grant from the GCF , the Government of Sri Lanka will leverage Government co-financing amounting to US 14 million dollars for this project to address several financial , technical , and institutional barriers related to achieving integrated water management to improve agriculture-based livelihoods of smallholder farmers in the Dry Zone . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ approved 8 other proposals .
