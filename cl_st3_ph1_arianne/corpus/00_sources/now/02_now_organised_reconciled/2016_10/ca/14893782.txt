<p> Jim Prentice loved his family , he loved Canada and he inspired tremendous loyalty amongst his friends . <p> Today , many people are sad over Jim 's passing , but first and foremost , our condolences go out to Jim 's family and very close friends . Jim 's friends were not only personal friends , but also people that loved him because they believed in what he was trying to do for Canada . In every aspect of Jim 's life , he was a leader -- a man who inspired people to do their best . <p> As a man who was involved in so many aspects of public life , he will surely be remembered by many people in very different ways . That speaks to his compassion , his respect for people and his commitment to making people 's lives better . <p> I first met Jim almost 30 year ago , when I was his articling student .
