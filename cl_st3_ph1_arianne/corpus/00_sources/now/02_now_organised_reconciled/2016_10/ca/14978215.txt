<p> SAN ANTONIO-- ( Business Wire ) --CST Brands , Inc . ( NYSE : CST ) today announced that the company will release its third quarter results after the market closes on Monday , November 7 . In light of the pending merger with Circle K , a subsidiary of Couche-Tard , CST does not intend to issue financial guidance regarding the company 's projected financial performance or host a third quarter earnings conference call . <p> As previously reported , on August 21 , 2016 , CST Brands entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Circle K Stores Inc. , a Texas corporation ( " Circle K " ) . Under the terms of the merger agreement , CST will be merged with a subsidiary of Circle K. Circle K is a wholly owned subsidiary of Alimentation Couche-Tard Inc .
