<p> The federal government is purposely limiting funding for First Nations schools in a continued attempt to use education as a tool of assimilation , according to the director of education in Kitchenuhmaykoosib Inninuwug , a remote First Nation 600 kilometres north of Thunder Bay , Ont . <p> " I do n't think this is an accident . I think it 's planned that way , " said Bill Sainnawap . " I used to call it a failure system , a built in failure system .
