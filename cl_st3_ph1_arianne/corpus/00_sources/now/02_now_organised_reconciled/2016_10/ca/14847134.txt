<p> The Alberta government has appointed an advisory panel to give advice on how to diversify the oil and gas sector . <p> The seven-person panel will be chaired by Jeanette Patell , government affairs and policy leader with GE Canada , and Gil McGowan , president of the Alberta Federation of Labour .
