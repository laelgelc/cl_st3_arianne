We commend our government for making the right choice and finding a balance between our commitment to the environment and energy prosperity . <p> So what now ? <p> Almost 200 countries signed the COP21 declaration last year in Paris . But many of these countries , particularly developing countries , continue to build coal-fired generation to provide electricity to their growing economies .
