" Triovest identified that there was so much devastation and that the tenants of the property were sadden by the loss of so many trees . " <p> He said Triovest took action by planting trees as part of a tenant engagement program and then contracted Sawmill Sid to re-purpose the fallen ash . <p> " Not only are they helping fight climate change , but if some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ families in need , then it 's a amazing thing , " said Gendron . <p> Sawmill Sid is a Canadian firm , which specializes in urban wood . It recovers wood that is grinded into wood chips and sends it to dumps or land fills . <p> At the event , Sawmill Sid team captured 5.5 tons of carbon ( CO2 ) and prevented it from being emitted into the atmosphere . <p> " Every time you pick up a wood from a forest and you make it into a wood product like a table or bench , you are actually saving that wood until the life of that product , " said Sheila Storey , CEO of Sawmill Sid .
