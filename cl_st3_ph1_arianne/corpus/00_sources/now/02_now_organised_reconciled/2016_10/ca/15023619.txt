Sea gates are large , complex , and often-expensive , barriers that can be closed as needed to hold back surging seas and protect valuable , low-lying land . The best-known examples are in the Netherlands , including its Oosterscheldekering , a three-kilometre-long structure that some herald as a wonder of the world . <p> Doug Smith , Vancouver 's acting director of sustainability , said a sea gate at the narrowest point of False Creek could form part of a larger effort to prevent the estimated billions of dollars of damage that a future flood could cause .
