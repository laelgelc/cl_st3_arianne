<p> Islamabad -- Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid , Wednesday , urged for a stronger emphasis on risk prevention and identification of role of all stakeholders from national to local level . Today , the nature of the common challenges that our region faces are complex , multi-dimensional and trans-national . Addressing the concluding session of the 13th Regional Consultative Committee ( RCC ) on Disaster Management here , he said that the multi hazards threats posed by natural calamities outlines a dire need as well as great scope for extensive and intense mutual cooperation at the regional level . The participants of this session discussed the road map and action plan of HOA and looked for the opportunities for creating synergies with other regional mechanisms .
