<h> Zahid discusses climate matters with Farooq <p> Islamabad -- Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid called on AJK Prime Minister Raja Farooq Haider Khan here at Kashmir House on Tuesday and discussed climatic matters with him . While talking to the Federal Minister the Prime Minister said it is a need to accelerate the efforts to address the challenges of climate change effectively.Raja Farooq Haider said the AJK government was making efforts to tackle climate change by devising a effective policy on the matter along with formulating a framework for its implementation . He said that in AJK , government was focussing on growing &amp; protect more forests and spend sufficient resources for seasonal plantation . The federal minister told the Prime Minister that federal government has allocated 2 billion of rupees for the plantation and other environmental friendly projects in Pakistan including AJK.
