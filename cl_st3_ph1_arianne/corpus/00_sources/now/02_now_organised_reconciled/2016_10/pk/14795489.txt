<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan on Monday acknowledged the role of Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) of the United Nations ( UN ) in Pakistan for improvement of agriculture sector and poverty reduction . <p> This was stated by Federal Minister for National Food Security and Research ( NFSR ) , Sikandar Hayat Khan Bosan , in a meeting with FAO-Rome Director General Jose Graziano da Silva during his visit to Pakistan , a statement said . <p> Bosan appreciated the major contributions made by FAO in Pakistan over few years such as achieving Avian Influenza free status since 2008 and establishing National Poultry Lab , which is as Hub Lab for SAARC countries , control of FMD of cattle and buffalo , implementing community based development initiatives for alleviating poverty and ensuring food security and supporting relief and rehabilitation activities in emergencies were especially mentioned . <p> The minister requested further technical support from FAO DG in strengthening the National Agricultural Research System ( NARS ) of Pakistan ; value addition and reducing post harvest losses in fruits and vegetables ; because unavailability of handling , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 20-30 percent post harvest losses in these crops ; exploiting genetic potential of local fish species Rohu , Thaila , Mori and their value addition ; developing thermo-stable vaccines for control of trans-boundary animal diseases in Pakistan ; conservation and improvement of farm animal genetic resources ; and research on climate change impacts and mitigation , the statement said . <p> The FAO-Rome DG was also briefed on several success stories from Pakistan under the current government 's agro-friendly policies , it added . One of the examples shared was of livestock uplift and eradicating various deadly animal diseases . While appreciating the efforts for solving core issues in agro and livestock sectors , FAO DG also highlighted the benefits and importance of having a unified national policy . He said that it is a big challenge for the country to develop , implement and make it a success story through efficient coordination among the provinces . <p> The director general was of the view that zero hunger programme for the poor faction of the country is multifaceted . <p> While looking forward to establish national policy , we also need @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , he said . The FAO DG stressed that food production is not only one factor to be emphasised but promoting balanced diet among masses is also very important . It is pertinent to mention here that meat-eating habits have lead to higher cancer rate in people , especially women , so it is very important that we deal with this issue on priority basis . <p> He also suggested bringing all stakeholders , private sector and civil society on board for uplift of agriculture , livestock and fisheries sectors . Later , the FAO DG met Ahsan Iqbal , Federal Minister Planning , Development and Reforms , who emphasised on the nutritional value of the agro-products . He insisted on providing for national and international donors to support agriculture and livestock-related research and production of better nutritious crops .
