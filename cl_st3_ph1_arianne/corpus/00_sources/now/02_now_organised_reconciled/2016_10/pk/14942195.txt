This year 's theme of WMBD is ' Stop the illegal killing , taking and trade of migratory Birds ' . <p> UN chief Ban Ki-moon in his message for WMBD has termed migratory birds a symbols of inter-connectedness of life on earth and said he fully supported the global campaign dedicated to raising awareness @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to pollution and climate change . <p> Extreme weather in Russia forces many precious species of birds to leave their home in search of moderate weather to South . On their way , they make a stopover at different lakes and water reservoirs in Pakistan , including Tanda Dam ( Kohat ) and Haleji , Keenjhar and Lungsee lakes . <p> According to data available , about one million birds migrate from Siberia every year , including houbara bustards , cranes , teals , pintails , mallards , geese , spoon bills , waders and pelicans , using the Indus Flyway , also known as the International Migratory Bird Route Number 4 , covering a distance of about 4,500 kilometres .
