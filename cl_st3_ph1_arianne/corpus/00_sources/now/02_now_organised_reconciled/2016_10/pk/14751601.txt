<h> Book on environmental challenges of Pakistan launched <p> Islamabad -- Urdu version of the book titled ' Pakistan Environmental Challenges and Measures for Pollution , ' written by Dr Mahmood A Khawaja was launched here at a ceremony on Friday . The ceremony was organized by Sustainable Development Policy Institute ( SDPI ) . Secretary Ministry of Climate Change Syed Abu Ahmad Akif said Urdu translation of a book on environment was a great endeavour for the promotion of national language and ensuring outreach to the general public . He said English language is one of the gifts of colonialism , which has subdued the local language Urdu . He said this book is an important source of knowledge which requires more dissemination . It is important to have a book in Urdu language on scientific subjects , for a better outreach to the general public .
