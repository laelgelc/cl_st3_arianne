<p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistan and Kyrgyzstan have agreed to strengthen collaboration in higher education and consented to all possible support in the future . <p> In a recent visit of Higher Education Commission ( HEC ) officers , vice chancellors and Pakistan Medical and Dental Council ( PMDC ) to Bishkek , Kyrgyzstan , all rectors of Kyrgyz universities , academics and delegates agreed for having collaborations in future . <p> An HEC official told APP on Monday that the visit was arranged to explore avenues of collaboration , assess facilities and strength of Kyrgyz educational system , and see where Pakistan and Kyrgyzstan can cooperate for enhancing quality of education , research and technology transfer . During the visit , he said that HEC officials gave a detailed overview of higher education sector of Pakistan and initiatives which are being undertaken for promotion of higher education . <p> The official said that launching of University of Central Asia Pakistan ( UCAP ) in Islamabad also came under discussion with officials of the Ministry of Education and Science of Kyrgyzstan and various higher education institutions . The Islamia College Peshawar also signed a DoU with International University of Kyrgyzstan . HEC also singed a DoU with the IK Akhunbaev Kyrgyz State Medical Academy .
