<h> Journalist Cyril Almeida refuses to record statement <p> ISLAMABAD : A prominent newspaper journalist Cyril Almeida refused to record his statement before a government 's committee set up to hold an inquiry into his story about a purported confrontation between civilian government and military . <p> Prime Minister House and Punjab Chief Minister Shehbaz Sharif called the report as fabricated . <p> Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif directed the interior ministry to hold an inquiry to determine who leaked the report . <p> A source told SAMAA that Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid , who is head of the inquiry committee , twice contacted Mr Almeida and asked him to record his statement . <p> The journalist , who was barred from traveling abroad until the completion of inquiry , refused to appear before the committee . <p> " I have invited APNS , PFUJ and CPNE representatives for a meeting on Friday to discuss the issue , " he said , adding that journalist would be questioned if he was still standing by his story , which was inaccurate in the first place .
