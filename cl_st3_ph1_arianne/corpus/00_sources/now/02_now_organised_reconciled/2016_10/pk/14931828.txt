<p> ISLAMABAD : The Ministry of Science and Technology has shown strong support towards initiatives related to renewable energy technologies ( RETs ) with emphasising upon academia , industry and R&amp;D organisations to make extraordinary efforts to enhance the share of RETs in total energy mix of Pakistan . <p> This was said by Rana Tanveer Hussain , Minister for Science and Technology , while speaking at the inaugural ceremony of international conference and exhibition on " Renewable Energy Technologies " held in Islamabad on Tuesday . <p> Hussain urged the investors to opt for Pakistan 's renewable energy sector , which has immense potential . <p> Opening a three-day international conference and exhibition , he said Pakistan was facing power shortage for the last so many years , adding that incumbent government , since its inception , was taking strides to overcome the menace of load shedding . <p> " Pakistan is endowed with a lot of solar energy throughout the year , which is a great investment opportunity for foreign and local business enterprises " , he said . <p> The event was organised by Pakistan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ COMSATS in collaboration with ISESCO . The event will conclude on October 20 . <p> While speaking to a gathering of over 120 participants , the minister considered it important to fully exploit the RETs for successful implementation of the government 's energy policies , especially amidst the huge concerns related to energy scarcity . <p> He considered RETs a solution to their energy security issues , while noting the abundance of solar energy available with the developing countries . <p> The minister said there was a dire need to promote the environment-friendly energy for survival of mankind . <p> He considered the presence of eminent Pakistani and foreign scientists , academicians and experts as an encouraging sign and hoped for the best outcomes of the event . <p> While speaking on the occasion , Science and Technology Secretary Fazal Abbas Maken highlighted the importance of RETs , especially in view of the rising environmental concerns due to rapid climate change . <p> He called for more efforts to include the share of renewable energy in the total energy mix of Pakistan . Dr Basharat Mehmood , Director PCRET , said that the vast renewable energy resources available in Pakistan were among the highest in the world .
