<h> Beaconhouse students participate in tree plantation drive <p> Islamabad -- As many as 100 students of the Beaconhouse School Potohar Campus planted over 100 plants of Kachnar in a tree plantation event jointly organized by the Islamabad Devcom Centennial club ( IDCLC ) and Development Communications Network ( Devcom-Pakistan ) Thursday morning . The event was held at the Lake View Park in collaboration with the Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) . It was the second event of the campaign " Grow a plat , own a tree " , launched last month by the partners including IDCLC , Devcom-Pakistan and the CDA to mark the Lions Clubs International ( LCI ) Centennial Celebrations . The LCI 305N2 District Governor Sitara Satwat was the chief guest on the occasion . The LCI District Chief Coordinator Tariq Mughal and District Secretary Amna Haider , the Creative Director Devcom-Pakistan Riffat Ara Baig , IDCLC President Shaaref Munir , and the Beaconhouse Global Perspective Program Coordinator Mahparah Raza were also present on the occasion to guide the students working in groups . They sacrificed their studies this day to prove their love for the nature and trees . This is indeed commendable . " The president IDCLC Shaaref Munir , an A level student , briefing about the campaign said , " Millions saplings are planted in every season of tree plantation , but hardly a few per cent survive due to lack of ownership by the people . We wish to inculcate a spirit in the youth that the trees are as valuable assets as having money or a house " . Shaaref Munir said that the IDCLC team is a teenage leadership for the teenage youth to engage them in variety of activities to combat the challenges facing the marginalised communities , environment and climate change , food security , health and education for all children .
