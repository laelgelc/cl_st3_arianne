<h> Winter to formally set-in from December <p> Islamabad -- Director General Pakistan Meteorological Department ( PMD ) , Dr. Ghulam Rasool Tuesday said this year , Winter season would formally start by the month of December with less than average rains . ' Prolonged dry weather prevailing in most parts of the country , insignificant rains and delay in advent of Winter season were all the result of Climatic Change impacts in the region ' , Dr. Ghulam Rasool said . He said the weather patterns usually keep on changing due to overall climate change impacts . -- APP
