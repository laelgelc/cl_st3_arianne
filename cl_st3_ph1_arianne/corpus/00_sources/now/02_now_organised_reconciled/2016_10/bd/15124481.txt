<h> Incentives to boost renewable energy on cards <h> Regulator and The Daily Star organise discussion on energy efficiency <p> The government plans to introduce feed-in-tariff shortly to boost production of electricity from renewable sources in a bid to ensure energy security and cut carbon emission . <p> Feed-in-tariff is a policy designed to accelerate investment in renewable energy technologies such as solar , biomass , hydropower and wind by providing tariff above the retail rate of electricity to producers . Under FIT , the state offers long-term contracts to producers . <p> " We will go for feed-in-tariff , " said State Minister for Power and Energy Nasrul Hamid at a discussion on renewable energy and energy efficiency at The Daily Star Centre in Dhaka yesterday . <p> The Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority and The Daily Star jointly organised the discussion with support from the United Nations Development Programme . <p> The minister , however , did not mention the timeframe , but officials said a draft law on FIT has been sent to the Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission . <p> The government is pushing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ introducing energy auditing for all industries , Hamid said . <p> Bangladesh 's installed power generation capacity is about 15,000 megawatts , while the actual generation is about 9,000MW , according to official data . <p> The government has set a plan to generate 20,000MW of electricity by 2020 and 40,000MW by 2030 as part of its objective to bring all citizens under electricity coverage . <p> Bangladesh is lagging behind most countries in deploying renewable energy . <p> In terms of energy , the contribution of renewable energy , excluding hydro , is less than 1 percent , said Ijaz Hossain , a professor at the department of chemical engineering of the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology . <p> The government has set a target to produce 10 percent of the country 's electricity from renewable sources by 2020 . <p> At present , individual households have solar panels for consumption , he said . Experts said achieving the goal of 10 percent power from renewable resources by 2020 is nearly impossible given the current circumstances : the sales of solar home systems have slowed in recent times . <p> Bangladesh 's economic growth , which was 7.11 percent last fiscal year , would require huge power generation , said SREDA Member Siddique Zobair . <p> The country 's power generation is mostly dependent on local gas that is dwindling fast and imported oil . <p> For that reason , renewable energy and energy efficiency are the major areas that the country needs to improve on to minimise the vulnerability of the energy sector . <p> The use of energy-efficient bulbs , controlling air-conditioners , maintaining boilers in industries , minor retrofitting of machinery and training people on such @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Khan , a former chairman of the BERC , suggested energy auditing and introduction of prepaid meters to improve governance in the power management . <p> Rezwan Khan , vice-chancellor of United International University , called for taxation and policies that encourage the use of energy-efficient appliances . <p> Renewable energy actually turns out to be cheaper than fossil fuel-based energy if environmental and social costs are considered , said UNDP Programme Specialist Arif M Faisal .
