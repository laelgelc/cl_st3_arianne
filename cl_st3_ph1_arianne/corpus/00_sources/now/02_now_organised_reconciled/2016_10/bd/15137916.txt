<h> Importance of studying Environmental Science and Management for a better world <h> Climate change is currently one of the most widely discussed issues around the world . Bangladesh is one of the countries worst affected by this anthropogenic cataclysm . <p> Other major factors also heavily contributing to this is the rise of water and air pollution and the overall degradation of soil and other elements of the environment . It is not only Bangladesh , but also the rest of the world , that is affected by the insidious grip of climate change . This has resulted in an increased number of natural calamities which is only exacerbating with time . It is high time for us to take up initiatives against this global obstacle for the sake of the present and future generations and the sustenance of all other organisms we share the Earth with . This is why we need to have a clear concept about climate change and natural disasters , and come up with a proper development plan , engage skilled youth and build suitable infrastructure . <p> With this notion , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , North South University , inaugurated Bangladesh 's very first Department of Environmental Science and Management -- ESM . Currently , North South University provides their students with the facility of doing Bachelors and Masters in two programs of Environmental Science and Management . The courses offered by the ESM department have been designed following the standards of renowned universities in North America as well as taking into consideration the needs of the locals students . The Bachelor programs consists of 125 credits . The two programs offered for Bachelors degree are , BSc in Environmental Science ( this is for students who studied Science at HSC or A levels ) and BSc in Environment Management ( students with any HSC or A levels background can take part in this program ) .
