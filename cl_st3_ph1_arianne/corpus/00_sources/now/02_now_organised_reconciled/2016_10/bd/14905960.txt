It became BRICS through the joining of South Africa in 2011 . The association has formed a bank , New Development Bank , with focus on lending for infrastructure projects . <p> Before BRICS , an association called ' BISTEC ' was formed with Bangladesh , India , Sri Lanka and Thailand in June 1997 . Myanmar joined the group later the same year and the name was changed to BIMSTEC .
