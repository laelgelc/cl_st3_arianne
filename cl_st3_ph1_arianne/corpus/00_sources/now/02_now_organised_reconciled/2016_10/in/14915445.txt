<h> Revealed : The 10 countries that are worst at feeding their people <p> A year after the international community set the target of eliminating hunger by 2030 , 50 countries around the world are failing to provide their people with enough food . <p> According to the Global Hunger Index for 2016 , close to half of all developing countries received " serious " or even " alarming " ratings based on levels of malnutrition , growth stunting and child mortality . <p> Overall , the world is getting better at addressing the issue of extreme poverty-driven hunger . But in some regions , particularly central Africa , there is still a long , long way to go to eradicating the problem . <p> " Simply put , countries must accelerate the pace at which they are reducing hunger or we will fail to achieve the second Sustainable Development Goal of ending global hunger by 2030 , said Shenggen Fan , the director general of the <p> International Food Policy Research Institute ( IFPRI ) which has been producing the index annually for the last 11 years @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ four key parameters : shares of undernourished population , wasted and stunted children aged under 5 , and infant mortality rate of the same age group . Countries worse than India include extremely poor African countries besides Afghanistan and Pakistan . <p> Africa accounts for six of the worst 10 countries in the ranking this year , with three - Central African Republic , Chad and Zambia - coming in the last three places . <p> The rest of the top 10 is completed by Haiti , Madagascar , Yemen , Sierra Leone , Afghanistan , Timor-Leste , and Niger . <p> Among the 118 countries ranked , only two have seen their scores get worse in the years since 2008 - Sri Lanka and Jamaica . <p> But there are 13 other countries for which a lack of data means it was n't possible to calculate an index score - and they include some of the most concerning malnutrition crises in the world . <p> The IFPRI said that even without a complete score , partial indicators for things like child stunting , wasting and mortality were cause for concern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Armed conflict is a leading cause of hunger and undernutrition in many of these countries , " said Barbel Dieckmann , President of Welthungerhilfe , one of the organisations involved in the report . " Zero hunger will only be possible if we significantly increase political commitments to conflict resolution and prevention . " <p> This year , no single country scored so badly as to fall in the " extremely alarming " category - but the nationwide figures mask the fact that within some states there can be acute malnutrition crises . <p> Mexico , for instance , has a low level of overall hunger , but contains areas where child stunting - an indicator of malnutrition - is relatively high . <p> Dominic MacSorley , CEO of Concern Worldwide , called it " unacceptable , it is immoral and shameful " that there were still 795 million people " condemned to facing hunger every day of their lives " . <p> " Agenda 2030 provides us with the ambition and commitment to reach zero hunger , " he said .
