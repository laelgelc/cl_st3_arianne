<h> Murma Jatra Mela to get the status of state fair : Raghubar Das <p> Ranchi : Chief Minister Raghubar Das today said the Murma Jatra Mela ( fair ) of the state will be accorded the status of a state fair . He said the venue of the Mela will be developed into a tourist spot as it was a religious and cultural heritage of Jharkhand . <p> Das made the announcement at the concluding day ceremony of the Jatra Mela . He said he had prayed to the Almighty to rid Jharkhand of poverty and make it a developed state . He said the tribal society should be made educated and a job-oriented community be developed . <p> The CM said it was necessary to fight collectively against the curse of poverty .
