<h> Sommelier Judith Sciarone suggests pairing wine with organic food for a healthier life <p> In an era where more and more of us aim at being mindful about our consumption and health , including wine into a balanced diet appears essential . Indeed , moderate consumption of one or two glasses of wine per day has proven time and again to be beneficial for our health , both physically and mentally . <p> Various vitamins and essential minerals present in both white and red wine are more easily absorbed into the bloodstream thanks to the presence of alcohol . <p> The key to leading a healthy life as to so many other things , as we know , is balance , diversity and moderation . By all means , consume whatever you wish , but make sure you strike a balance , that you diversify your menu and that you do n't go overboard on one food group .
