<h> Farmer organisations across the country protest against GM mustard <h> A government-conducted biosafety report had declared a variety of genetically modified mustard safe for humans and animals . <p> Farmer groups across the country on Sunday protested against the government 's conditional approval for the commercial development of genetically modified mustard . Groups from Karnataka , Kerala , Maharashtra , Odisha , Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh held dharnas , fasted and adopted resolutions not to use genetically modified crops . <p> Kirankumar Vissa , an engineer with the Rythu Swarajya Vedike , a farmers ' rights organisation based in Andhra Pradesh , said , " Farmers organisations have strong concerns about seed production being taken over by single companies . " The group was also a part of Sunday 's protests . <p> The Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee , an apex body under the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , had in September declared that a variety of GM Mustard developed at Delhi University was safe for human and animal health .
