<p> For those who are not familiar with the concept of coral bleaching , it is a phenomenon where extraordinary conditions such as rising temperatures release small photosynthetic algae - called zooxanthellae - which turn the coral white . <p> Australia <p> More than six months have passed since the bleaching crisis of the Great Barrier Reef was announced and since then , little improvement has been made to that end . <p> Scientists with the Climate Council - a non-profit outfit fighting climate change - visited the reef in September and what they found was not very heartening . <p> Of the 900 coral reefs that were examined this year , nearly 93% were found affected by coral bleaching . If this occurrence is not brought under control and soon , the scientists believe that the extent of damage will be catastrophic . <p> What is really required to tackle this problem is a global effort dedicated towards one and only one goal - to prevent coral bleaching by addressing issues like water pollution , etc.
