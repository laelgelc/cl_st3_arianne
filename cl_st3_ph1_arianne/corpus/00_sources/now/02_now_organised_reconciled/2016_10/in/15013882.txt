<h> Tendu labourers ' protest march today <p> Tendu labourers and farmers cultivating forest land will take out a protest march to the office of chief conservator of forest ( CCF ) in the city under the banner of Vidarbha Kisan Majdoor Congress ( VKMC ) on Monday . <p> TNN Updated : Oct 24 , 2016 , 09:36 IST <p> ( Representative image ) <p> Chandrapur : Tendu labourers and farmers cultivating forest land will take out a protest march to the office of chief conservator of forest ( CCF ) in the city under the banner of Vidarbha Kisan Majdoor Congress ( VKMC ) on Monday . <p> VKMC has charged that forest department has stopped paying bonus to tendu labourers for the last two years and it has also started a drive to evict those who are cultivating the forest land . <p> According to VKMC , thousands of people from every district in east Vidarbha go to jungle for tendu leaves collection every year . As many as 82% of the tendu leaves in the state is collected from six Vidarbha districts -- Chandrapur @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Thus the tendu collectors earn good income during the season which lasts around two months and bonus helps them meet necessary expenses like livelihood and education of their children throughout the year . However after the new government assumed power in the state , forest department has allegedly stopped payment of the tendu bonus . <p> Similarly , the department has issued orders to remove people who are cultivating crops in small patches of forest land to earn their livelihood . Forest department is keen on reclaiming its 5.50 lakh hectare land , which will further escalate the agrarian crisis among marginal farmers , the organization has claimed . <p> Hence , to press the demand of restoration of tendu bonus and stop the campaign to remove the forest cultivators , the protest march and sit-in has been organized .
