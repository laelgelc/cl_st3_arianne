<h> MP bans stock , sale of glass-coated kite thread <p> HT Correspondent , Bhopal <p> <p> Updated : Oct 21 , 2016 20:49 IST <p> Taking cognizance of a plea filed by People for Ethical Treatment of Animals , the MP government has imposed a ban on stocking and sale of glass-coated thread used to fly kites . The order will go a long way in saving birds from getting injured by the razor-sharp threads , popularly called manja . <p> Peta had written to the senior officials in the state , including environment minister Lal Singh Arya , requesting a ban on manja as it had led to several injuries and loss of life in the recent past . <p> " Most people care about wildlife and would choose plain cotton kite strings over the glass-coated ones if they knew about the risk it poses to birds " , said government affairs liaison of Peta , Nikunj Sharma .
