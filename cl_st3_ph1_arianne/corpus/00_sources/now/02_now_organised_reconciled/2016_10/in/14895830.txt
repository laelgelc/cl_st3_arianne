<h> 2,000 MW Mandvi power plant gets green nod <p> Infrastructure Leasing and Financial Services Ltd ( IL&amp;FS ) group has received environmental clearance for its proposed 2000 MW gas-based power project in Mandvi taluka of Kutch district . <p> TNN Oct 17 , 2016 , 06:42 IST <p> ( Representative image ) <p> AHMEDABAD : Infrastructure Leasing and Financial Services Ltd ( IL&amp;FS ) group has received environmental clearance for its proposed 2000 MW gas-based power project in Mandvi taluka of Kutch district . The expert appraisal committee under the union ministry of environment , forest and climate change recently gave its approval to Nana Layja Power Company Ltd , a special purpose vehicle created to develop the power plant . <p> The gas-fired power project will be developed within a multi-product special economic zone ( SEZ ) by IL&amp;FS group at Mandvi . Details provided by the company say that in its environmental impact assessment report ( EIA ) , the cost of the project for setting up the gas-based power plant is Rs 7,187 crore . <p> " The environment clearance accorded shall be valid @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issue of this letter for the start of production operations , " the ministry said in its notification approving the project . <p> Apart from the gas-fired power plant , a coal-based power plant with a capacity of 4000 MW has also been planned by the group at the same location . The investment for the same is around Rs 28,000 crore . <p> The proposed power plant is conveniently located and well-connected to the sea which gives it the advantage of bringing imported coal along with the indigenous coal and gas required for the power plants.Considering these aspects , both the plants are proposed to be developed in the multiproduct SEZ and free trade and warehousing zone ( FTWZ ) at Mandvi in Kutch . Estimated project cost for development of SEZ , including proposed power plants , is around Rs. 38,741crore . <p> Overall , IL&amp;FS is developing an integrated maritime complex comprising a state-of-the art shipyard , a marine and energy SEZ and FTWZ .
