<p> The Mahagenco started trial run of second unit with installed capacity of 660MW on Friday . The trial run will be done for 72 hours . During the trial run , the unit will generate power at its installed capacity . The unit will be declared as commissioned , technically called as commercial operation declaration , if runs at full load for the entire test run period . <p> The pollution level during trial run is always high as compared to normal run . More quantity of oil is utilized instead of coal during trial run . <p> To prevent from high level of pollution , the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) had made it mandatory for the Koradi thermal power station expansion project to install flue gas desulphuriser ( FGD ) at one of the three units in initial stage in its environment clearance issued in January 2010 . The Koradi thermal power station was supposed to go for commissioning of remaining two units after monitoring the pollution level of first unit . <p> High sulphur dioxide ( SO2 ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the area around the power plant . Prolonged exposure to high SO2 level can cause diseases like cancer . Therefore , FGD was made mandatory . FGD is a pollution control equipment installed in thermal power plants to reduce the sulphur dioxide ( SO2 ) content in emissions . <p> Koradi 's three new units will have total installed capacity of 1,980MW which is considered as very high quantum . <p> The state government 's consent appraisal committee headed by Satish Gavai , principal secretary of environment department , in the meeting held on September 16 has approved consent for the second unit 's operation . The minutes of the meeting have been released couple of days ago . <p> " Environmental clearance stipulated condition to install FGD in one of the unit initially and further depend upon the prevalent ambient levels of SO2 . Provision of FGD to all the remaining units shall be made .
