These stations were classified into four groups -major , medium , small cities and hill stations . <p> " Less than 50% stations from each group showed a significant increasing trend in annual mean temperature from 1901-1970 . However , in the 1971-2013 period , more than 80% stations from all the groups , except the small city group , showed a significant increasing trend .
