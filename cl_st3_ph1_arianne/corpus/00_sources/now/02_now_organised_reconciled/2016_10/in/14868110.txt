( Source : AP ) <p> Environmental groups early Saturday said nations have reached a deal to limit the use of greenhouse gases far more powerful than carbon dioxide as part of efforts to fight climate change . At issue are hydrofluorocarbons , or HFCs , which are used in air conditioners and refrigerators and have been described as the world 's fastest-growing climate pollutants . <p> Observers said the agreement , set to be announced shortly , would cap the use of HFCs beginning in 2019 , led by developed countries including the United States , the world 's second worst polluter . More than 100 developing countries including China , the world 's top carbon emitter , would start taking action in 2024 .
