<h> NGT fines local bodies for illegal dumping of solid waste <p> PUDUCHERRY : The National Green Tribunal , southern bench , has slapped a penalty of 9 lakh on two municipalities and two commune panchayats for illegal dumping of solid waste materials in the Union territory of Puducherry . <p> Tribunal judicial member Justice P Jyothimani and expert member P S Rao slapped a penalty of 5 lakh on Puducherry municipality , 2 lakh on Oulgaret municipality and 1 lakh each on Ariyankuppam and Villianur commune panchayats . The tribunal passed the orders following a petition filed by Perambai panchayat president K Lakshmanasamy . The tribunal posted the case to November 11 for further hearing . <p> Lakshmanasamy in his petition charged that Puducherry municipality , Oulgaret municipality , Ariyankuppam and Villianur commune panchayats have been illegally dumping solid waste materials in a 9-hectare site at Kurumampet in Puducherry bordering his village Perambai without obtaining the mandatory environmental clearance from the Union environment , forest and climate change ministry . The civic bodies have not obtained authorization from the Puducherry pollution control committee , he pointed out @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has been completely contaminated due to the illegal dumping . Moreover burning of waste at the dump yard has been causing severe air pollution . The dump yard is located adjacent to the petitioner 's village , Puducherry veterinary college and Puducherry dairy farms . " The dump yard has rendered the entire area uninhabitable . But the dalit communities and economically weaker sections living in the area are unable to relocate themselves . The entire area is infested with flies and mosquitoes . More than 7,000 people in my panchayat are effected by it , " said Lakshmanasamy . <p> He urged the tribunal to issue an order preventing the civic bodies from dumping waste materials at the site and directing the PPCC to ensure strict compliance of its order .
