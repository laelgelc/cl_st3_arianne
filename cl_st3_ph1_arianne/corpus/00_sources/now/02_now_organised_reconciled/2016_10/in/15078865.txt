<h> World may lose 2/3rd of its wildlife by 2020 : WWF <p> October 27 , 2016 <p> New Delhi , October 27 ( IANS ) : The future of many living organisms is under question as the world may loss 68 percent of its wildlife by 2020 -- a possible prelude for the sixth mass extinction , a major WWF report has said . According to the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) -Living Plant Report 2016 , 58 per cent of the global population of fish , birds , mammals , amphibians and reptiles has already been lost between 1970 and 2012 . These patterns are directly attributed to human induced climate change . <p> The report says that about 41 percent , mammals , 46 per cent reptiles , 57 per cent amphibians and 70 per cent freshwater fishes are " threatened with extinction " in India . Four of the 385 species of mammals are already extinct in India . <p> Seven per cent of birds may also extinct in the world . " Habitat loss and degradation and over exploitation of wildlife are the most common threats to the terrestrial population , " the report says . <p> As per the report , by 2000 , 48.5 per cent of the tropical and subtropical dry broadleaf forest habitat had been converted for human use . This has led to a 41 per cent overall decline in tropical forest species . The report held food production to meet the complex demands of an expanding human population as primary reason of the destruction of habitats and over-exploitation of wildlife . <p> The world 's population has grown from about 1.6 billion people in 1900 to today 's 7.3 billion . All these changes may lead to the world 's sixth extinction , following the extinction of reptiles , mammals ( twice ) and dinosaurs ( twice ) . <p> As per IUCN , the total threatened animal species has increased from 5,205 to 8,462 since 1996 .
