<h> Telangana , Andhra Pradesh top in tree cover destruction <p> According to the data , 10,80,101 trees were illegally pulled down in 15 states and two Union Territories , of which 2,04,164 trees were axed in the state of Telangana and 3,95,489 in the state of Andhra Pradesh . <p> TNN Updated : Oct 21 , 2016 , 08:39 IST <p> ( Representative image ) <p> HYDERABAD : Close to six lakh trees were illegally axed between April 2013 and March 2016 in the states of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh , accounting for over 55 per cent of the total destruction of tree cover reported from 15 states and two union territories , a new report by the Union ministry of environment , forests and climate change ( MoEFCC ) says . <p> According to the data , 10,80,101 trees were illegally pulled down in 15 states and two Union Territories , of which 2,04,164 trees were axed in the state of Telangana and 3,95,489 in the state of Andhra Pradesh . <p> Telangana recorded the maximum number in 2015-16 , when 76,670 tress were chopped as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ offices and buildings . <p> While the state of Andhra Pradesh saw a drop in the number of trees illegally axed , from 1,63,649 in 2013-14 to 97,244 in 2015-16 , the state of Telangana witnessed a rise in the number of trees being pulled down . The numbers in the state rose from 66,554 in the 2013-14 period to 76,670 in the 2015-16 period . <p> " Smuggling of red sanders is rampant in the East Godavari area , " said SK Chottray , additional principal chief conservator of forest ( vigilance ) in Andhra Pradesh , while elaborating on one of the reasons for the loss of tree cover in the state . <p> In Telangana , smuggling of teak and timber along the Godavari in Karimnagar and Warangal is a major reason for illegal axing of trees . Experts say despite the public outcry , the situation has worsened .
