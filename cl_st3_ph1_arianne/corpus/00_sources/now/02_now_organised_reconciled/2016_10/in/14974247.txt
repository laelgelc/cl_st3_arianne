Here are few other places too that are nearing extinction . <p> TNN Oct 21 , 2016 , 12:02 IST <p> Every one of us is aware of the effects of global warming and how it has been wrecking havoc on mother nature . Climate changes , urbanisation and gross negligence by humans have altered many of our natural treasures . <p> Case in point -- the 25 million-year-old Great Barrier Reef . An article by Rowan Jacobsen , paying obituary to the iconic coral reef system , has taken the internet by storm and has brought to everyone 's notice that the earth 's largest living structure is dead and gone . <p> " The Great Barrier Reef of Australia passed away in 2016 after a long illness " read the article , making everyone sit up and take notice .
