<p> A new species from the Pika family , a mammal belonging to the hare family has been discovered in the higher altitudes of the Himalayas in Sikkim . <p> To identify the species , Pika pellets were collected to get its DNA . On comparing the DNA sequences from the pellets with that of all known Pika species in the world , it was found that these were quite different . <p> To prove that this is indeed a new species , the Sikkim Pika was compared to its close relatives . It took two years for collaborations with researchers from the Chinese Academy of Sciences , Zoological Museum of Moscow and Stanford University to get detailed data on these possible sister species . <p> The study has been published in the journal Molecular Phylogenetics and Evolution . <p> Here 's all you need to know about this new species of Pika : <p> Scientists have identified it as ' Ochotona sikimaria ' , it was discovered by the study based on genetic data and skull measurements <p> They resemble tailless rats <p> They have been in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change , like increasing temperature , which has caused several of the populations in Pika series go extinct <p> Unlike other mammalian species inhabiting such harsh environments , Pikas do not hibernate .
