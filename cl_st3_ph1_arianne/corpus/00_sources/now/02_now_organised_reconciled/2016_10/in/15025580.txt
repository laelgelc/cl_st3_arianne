<h> Forests nearly thrice Bengaluru 's size encroached in Karnataka <p> TNN Updated : Oct 24 , 2016 , 23:26 IST <p> Bengaluru : Forest land close to thrice the size of Bengaluru city has been encroached upon in Karnataka as of October 2016 , up 129% from August 2014 . <p> According to data from the ministry of environment , forests and climate change ( MoEF ) , a total of 1.87 lakh hectares of forests are encroached in Karnataka ; Bengaluru 's total land area is 74,100 hectares . <p> Karnataka shows the highest encroachment of forests in south India , and third , nationally . Nationally , encroachments are under control and have reduced by 3 lakh hectares . Madhya Pradesh , with 5.34 lakh hectares , is at the top of the list of forest encroachment , followed by Assam ( 3.17 lakh hectares ) . <p> Karnataka is among the three states that reported an increase in encroachment of forest land during the period . " There is just one person to guard 8,000-10,000 acres of forest . While we are enhancing the use of technology and putting in place other systems , the problem persists , " he said . <p> Karnataka has been facing challenges in removal of encroachments by people claiming to be ' forest dwellers ' . " Not everybody can be considered a traditional user of the forest , who has certain rights .
