<h> Ansari pushes for water conservation <p> TNN Oct 5 , 2016 , 05.49 AM IST <p> PUNE : Considering the growing water scarcity , the planning and management of water resources and their use in an optimal , economical and equitable manner has become a matter of utmost urgency , said vice-president Hamid Ansari on Tuesday . <p> Speaking at the centenary celebrations of the Central Water and Power Research Station ( CWPRS ) here , the vice-president said that in the coming years , the impact of climate change on the hydrological cycle , and the water availability in our country will likely become central questions before national planning agencies , and that the CWPRS would have to play a major role in developing the knowledge , insight and skills to meet those challenges . <p> Ansari said that with the increasingly uncertain global climate change scenario and the long-term impact of management actions , decision-making will become even more difficult .
