<h> India must ensure 40 per cent of its power generate from non-fossil fuel sources by 2030 <p> New Delhi , October 1 : As India prepares to ratify the terms of the Paris Agreement on climate change on October 2 , Gandhi Jayanti , it must prepare to fulfil a global commitment by ensuring 40 per cent of its power capacity comes from non-fossil fuel sources by 2030 , up from 30 per cent today as per a power ministry assessment in August 2016 . <p> The countries who will sign the accord must reduce the focus on fossil fuels in their energy mix to 30-35 per cent below 2005 levels by 2030 .
