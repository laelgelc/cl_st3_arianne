The zoo was shut for the third consecutive day even though no new deaths were report on Saturday . <p> TNN Oct 23 , 2016 , 07:44 IST <p> Representative image <p> NEW DELHI : The ministry of environment , forest and climate change has constituted a three-member committee to monitor the spread of a new strain of avian flu ( H5N8 ) , which has killed 24 birds across Delhi since October 12 . The zoo was shut for the third consecutive day even though no new deaths were report on Saturday . <p> MoS environment Anil Madhav Dave announced that the three-member committee has issued advisories to all state departments and would monitor them on precautionary measures . <p> Amitabh Agnihotri , director of National Zoological Park , said the issue was also being discussed at the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ On Saturday , Agnihotri expressed cautious optimism on containment of the virus , claiming that every measure had been taken to separate the infected and migratory birds from the residents . <p> " Migratory birds suspected of being infected have been put in isolation and barriers have been created between them and the resident birds . We have also been fumigating the area with disinfectants , and minimizing human contact with these birds . But we will have to monitor the situation for the next few days before any date can be decided for the reopening of the zoo , " he said . <p> The zoo entry and exit have been covered by lime , while staff and tyres of vehicles entering the premises are being regularly sprayed with disinfectants .
