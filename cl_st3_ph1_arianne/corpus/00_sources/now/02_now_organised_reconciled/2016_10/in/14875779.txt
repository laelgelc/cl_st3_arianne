<h> India to soon allocate site for second Russian-designed nuclear power plant <p> NK India , Panaji ( Goa ) India , Oct. 15 : India has said it is working towards early allocation of the second site for the construction of the Russian-designed nuclear power plant as Moscow assisted Kudankulam nuclear power plant has already started generating power . <p> A joint statement issued by both sides after the bilateral meeting between Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Russian President Vladimir Putin said , " The Indian side stated it is working towards early allocation of the second site for the construction of the Russian-designed NPP . public and private sectors of India , " said the statement . <p> Both sides also noted with satisfaction that robust civil nuclear cooperation , collaboration in hydrocarbon sphere , long term LNG
