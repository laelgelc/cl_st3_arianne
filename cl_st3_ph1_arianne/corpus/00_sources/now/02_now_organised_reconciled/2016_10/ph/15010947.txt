<h> The Standard <h> Victims need food , aid ASAP -- senator <p> Senator Juan Miguel F. Zubiri on Sunday called for the release of food aid , farm inputs like seeds , farm tools and machines as well as financial grants to victims of Typhoon " Karen " and " Lawin . " <p> He also called on the Philippine Crop Insurance Corp. , the Department of Science and Technology and Department of Agriculture to adopt weather-triggered insurance . <p> " Victims need food and non-food aid . Food aid must be for a long enough time to tide them over this cropping cycle and up to the next . Many farmers can only plant when the weather is right for their land preparation , repair of irrigation canals , replacement of farm equipments and farm animals and many more important tasks . " <p> Zubiri warned that " food aid can not stop abruptly . Too little food aid will mean we leave them to hang or drown in hunger .
