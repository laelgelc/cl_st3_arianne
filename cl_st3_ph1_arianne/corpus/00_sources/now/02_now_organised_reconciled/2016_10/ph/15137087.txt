<h> Palace to consider Ramos ' advice on Paris climate pact <h> Palace to consider Ramos ' advice on Paris climate pact <p> THE Duterte administration will moot the possibility of ratifying the Paris agreement on climate change , as " guided by the wisdom " of former President Fidel Ramos , a Palace official said Monday . <p> " We appreciate former President FVR 's ( Ramos ) advice . We will be guided by the wisdom of former President FVR , " Communications Secretary Martin Andanar said in a text message . <p> Andanar made the statement in response to the former president 's column , entitled " Climate Change -- Attention : P. Digong , Cabinet and Congress " and published by newspaper Manila Bulletin over the weekend . <p> Ramos said Duterte is " unwittingly shooting himself in the mouth , and also all of us , 101.5 million Filipinos " when he said he would not honor the climate deal , which was crafted by some 195 countries , including the Philippines . <p> The Philippines , one of the top five countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Paris agreement on climate change . <p> The climate pact aims to hold the increase in the global temperature to " well below " two degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels and pursue efforts to limit the temperature increase to 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels . <p> Ramos believes that Duterte needs to approve the Paris agreement and certify it for Senate ratification " to promote our country 's interests and fight for climate change .
