<h> Tax rebates await pastel roofed homes <h> Tax rebates await pastel roofed homes <p> TAX rebates await Baguio residents who will paint their home roofs in cool and light colours . <p> Vice-Mayor Edison Bilog said if the ordinance mandates residents and establishments to paint their rooftops with light colors is passed , there will be discounts when paying real property tax for those who will comply . <p> Bilogs said the rebate on taxes will serve as an incentive for everyone to follow the law . <p> " It is a little reward for them and an incentive , " the vice - mayor said . <p> Bilog in his proposed law is pushing to mandate all government owned buildings and structures , barangay halls , multipurpose halls , barangay covered courts , day care centres and existing residences , buildings to undergo roof repainting and renovations as well as new residences to be painted with cool colors to help protect the environment . <p> The ordinance aims to present a simple yet effective solution to help protect the environment and manage the effects of climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of greenhouse gas concentration in the atmosphere at a level that would prevent dangerous anthropogenic interference with climate change . <p> Bilog said numerous studies have found out roofs with cool colored paint can be a mitigation strategy against climate change and could even bring many benefits including lower energy use , reduced air pollution and greenhouses gas emissions and improved human health and comfort . <p> The Vice-Mayor said the idea of the DOT to paint the entire Quirino Hill with white is a welcome project and will compliment the anticipated law when passed . <p> Published in the Sun.Star Baguio newspaper on October 06 , 2016 . <p> Latest issues of Sun.Star Baguio also available on your mobile phones , laptops , and tablets .
