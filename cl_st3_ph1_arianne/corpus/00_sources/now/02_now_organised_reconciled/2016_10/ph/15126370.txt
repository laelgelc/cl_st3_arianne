Ramos on Sunday urged President Rodrigo Duterte 's administration to approve and ratify the Paris Agreement on Climate Change , saying that any further delays would make Filipinos more vulnerable to the effects of typhoons . <p> " With the Philippines not having yet ratified the Paris Agreement , we can only be observers in the Marrakech Meeting . Therefore , President Duterte should forthwith approve the Paris Agreement and certify it for Senate ratification to promote our country 's interests and fight for climate justice , " Ramos said . <p> " Any further delay will increase Filipinos ' vulnerability to super-typhoons that are forecast to come soon ... , " Ramos said in a column for a national newspaper . <p> Former President Fidel V.
