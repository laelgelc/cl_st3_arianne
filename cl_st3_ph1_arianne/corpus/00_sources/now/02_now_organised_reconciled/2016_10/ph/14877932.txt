<h> Deal on greenhouse gases OK 'd <p> KIGALI , Rwanda ( AP ) -- Nations have reached a deal to limit the use of greenhouse gases far more powerful than carbon dioxide in a major effort to fight climate change . <p> The talks on hydrofluorocarbons , or HFCs , have been called the first test of global will since the historic Paris Agreement to cut carbon emissions was reached last year . HFCs are described as the world 's fastest-growing climate pollutant and are used in air conditioners and refrigerators . <p> The agreement announced Saturday morning , after all-night negotiations , caps and reduces the use of HFCs in a gradual process beginning in 2019 with action by developed countries including the United States , the world 's second worst polluter .
