The difference with this type of debt is that if a loss greater than a pre-specified amount occurs as a result of a hurricane or earthquake , the issuer is allowed to delay or skip interest and/or principal payments , while the bondholders incur losses that can be substantial . <p> Yet , in a near-zero interest rate environment , investors have largely benefited from the higher yields associated with catastrophe bonds .
