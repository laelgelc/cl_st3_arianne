<p> Teenager Jade Hameister has been named the Australian Geographic Society 's Young Adventurer of the Year after she trekked through one of the world 's most extreme environments to get to the North Pole . <p> Jade completed the 11-day journey in April , aged just 14 years . <p> Accompanied by her father Paul , guide Eric Philips and camera man Petter Nyquist , she withstood temperatures of -35 degrees Celsius , dragging a sled that was as heavy as she was . <p> It is the latest achievement for a young adventurer who climbed Mount Kosciuszko when she was six and trekked to Mount Everest Base Camp when she was 12 . <p> Jade spoke to Lateline about how she managed the latest gruelling adventure and how it opened her eyes to the threat posed by climate change . <p> Our trip took 11 days to ski 150 kilometres to the pole .
