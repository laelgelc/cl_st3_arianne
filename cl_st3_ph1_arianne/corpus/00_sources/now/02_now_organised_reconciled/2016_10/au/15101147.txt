<p> The Green Climate Fund will this year approve hundreds of billions of dollars towards projects that help poorer nations mitigate the effects of climate change and transition to low-emission technologies . <p> Howard Barmsey , a former Australian climate change envoy and former head of the Global Green Growth Institute , will lead the GCF which will play a role in trying to fulfil the Paris Agreement to limit the world 's temperature increase to below 2 degrees Celsius.
