<p> Fifteen years had elapsed since we last explored the Gardens of the Queen . In the necklace of keys , mangrove islets , and reefs about 50 miles off Cuba , we had discovered a marine wilderness that astonished us with its vibrant life . <p> We returned to Cuba anxious about what we would see in the wake of time and climate change in this national park now covering about 850 square miles . On our first dive we descended into a large stand of elkhorn coral , a critically endangered species diminished throughout the Caribbean . We found ourselves in a dense thicket , amazed as we watched grunts and snappers jostle for space among the broad branches , as if in a game of musical chairs .
