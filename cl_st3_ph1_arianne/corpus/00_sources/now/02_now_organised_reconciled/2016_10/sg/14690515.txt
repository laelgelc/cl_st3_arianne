<p> The Paris Agreement on climate change is all but sealed . India ratified the global deal on 2 October . The European Union also ratified it on 4 October , taking the total emissions covered by the deal to more than 55% of the world total . This ticks the box for the Agreement to come into force . The deal will be effective a month from now . <p> When the Agreement text was first concluded at the COP21 climate summit at Paris , loud cheers and teary eyes gave the impression that humanity had already saved itself , forgiven itself for raising average temperatures above 1 degree Celsius at a pace not seen in thousands of years . We need to ideally keep this limited to 1.5 degrees Celsius , and at most to 2 degrees Celsius , which is also what the Agreement stated in its goal . <p> Can we ?
