<h> OpenGov speaks to Commissioner David Watts about leading the United Nation Special Rapporteur on the Right to Privacy 's Big Data and Open Data study . <p> Earlier this year , OpenGov spoke to Adjunct Professor David Watts , Commissioner for Privacy and Data Protection for the State of Victoria in Australia to learn about the Victorian Protective Data Security Framework . <p> In July , Commissioner Watts was appointed by United Nations ( UN ) Special Rapporteur on the Right to Privacy 's lead on a Big Data and Open Data study . OpenGov caught up with him to discuss his new role , and the objectives and scope of the study . Commissioner Watts talked about the importance of defining the terms , studying the risks and mitigation strategies . <p> Can you tell us about your new role as the lead on the UN global study on Big Data and Open Data ? <p> Let me start with some background . One of those being Big Data and Open Data . The aim of the project I am leading is to produce a report on Big Data and Open Data for the Special Rapporteur for presentation to the UN Human Rights Council and General Assembly in the latter part of 2017 . <p> We have divided the Big Data/ Open Data theme into a number of areas of inquiry . The first area of inquiry relates to how best to frame the issues . The starting point is to define both Big Data and Open Data . <p> Interestingly , there is no agreed definition of Big Data .
