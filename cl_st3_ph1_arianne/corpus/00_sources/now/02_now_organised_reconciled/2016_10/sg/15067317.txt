<h> Rethinking production and consumption for a zero-waste Singapore <h> As a country with no natural resources , Singapore 's throw-away culture is not just ironic but also bad for the climate . It is time to close the production loop and move to a sustainable economy , say participants at a roundtable on a zero-waste Singapore . <p> Great Taste , Less Waste campaign at supermarket NTUC FairPrice . Its national recycling rate of 61 per cent obscures the fact that more can be done to encourage Singaporeans to recycle with the domestic household recycling rate standing at 19 per cent . <p> Looking beyond our borders , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trash .
