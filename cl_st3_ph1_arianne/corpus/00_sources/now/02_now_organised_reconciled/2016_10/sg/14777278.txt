<h> ' The most impossible job on this earth ' <p> UNITED NATIONS * It 's a job that was described by its first incumbent , Mr Trygve Lie , as " the most impossible job on this earth " . <p> When Mr Antonio Guterres takes over the reins of the United Nations as secretary-general next year , he will inherit an organisation with 193 member states and a mammoth bureaucracy that has haphazardly evolved . <p> The UN Charter says little about the top job , which is defined by the incumbent and circumstance , notes The Guardian . No one can meet everyone 's expectations or standard and people often seek the qualities lacking in the last holder , as well as those that seem the most needed at the time . <p> Mr Guterres , a former Portuguese prime minister , will step into the secretary-general 's role at a time when 24 people worldwide are fleeing their homes every minute due to war or persecution .
