<h> ITU standardises universal charger for laptops <p> One million tonnes of external power supplies are manufactured each year , says the ITU . <p> The International Telecommunication Union ( ITU ) has approved a new environmentally-friendly standard for a universal charger for laptops and other portable devices . <p> The new standard provides for improved energy-efficiency and reduced greenhouse gas emissions , and is expected to lead to significant reductions in e-waste . <p> The new international standard -- officially known as Recommendation ITU-T L.1002 " External universal power adapter solutions for portable ICT devices " -- was developed by the ITU standardisation expert group for ICTs , the environment and climate change , ITU-T Study Group 5 . <p> According to the ITU , one million tonnes of external power supplies are manufactured each year . ITU-T L.1002 specifies principles for the eco-design of laptop chargers to reduce no-load power consumption five times lower than the norm . <p> When multiplied by the millions of such chargers in use , this will greatly reduce the greenhouse gas emissions produced by these devices , says the ITU @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ devices , as well as design principles for the efficient use of raw materials , will greatly increase their lifetime and reduce the e-waste resulting from their disposal . <p> ITU-T L.1002 builds on ITU standards providing universal chargers for handheld devices such as mobile phones ( ITU-T L.1000 ) and stationary devices such as xDSL modems ( ITU-T L.1001 ) first adopted by ITU members in 2009 and 2012 , respectively . <p> " E-waste has grown into a significant challenge to environmental sustainability , " says ITU secretary-general Houlin Zhao . " The L.1000 series of standards will contribute to the achievement of the targets set out by Goal 12 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals to ensure sustainable production and consumption patterns .
