<h> African farmers demand investment in ecological farming <p> Thirty small holder farmers in some rural areas of Kenya will embark on a four-day resilience journey to sensitise their leaders on ideal agricultural system for the country and the continent . <p> According to a statement made available to the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) , on Tuesday in Abuja by Greenpeace Africa , an international NGO on environment , the farmers are demanding investment in Ecological Farming . ' <p> Ecological Farming combines local farmers ' knowledge with the most recent scientific knowledge to create new technologies and practices that increase yields without negatively impacting the environment . <p> The farmers ' journey which is ahead of the Food Day billed for Oct.16 of every year would enable them to exhibit produce , share knowledge and share seeds to their counterparts . <p> " We , as farmers and consumers from around Kenya , call upon the Government of Kenya and international aid donors to listen to our demands , to move away from conventional agriculture and support ecological farming . <p> " Conventional agriculture @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change worsens , " they said . <p> They said that they had decided to support each other because they had not received sufficient support from authorities and donors . <p> According to the Greenpeace , the farmers ' appeal comes at a very critical time , " the current food system is broken , the environment is damaged . <p> " The current industrial agricultural model has left thousands hungry and dependant on technologies that are unable to withstand weather shocks and lined the pockets of a few corporate organisations .
