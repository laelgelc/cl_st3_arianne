<h> NOA Says Youths Are Key To Climate Change Mitigation <p> Fidel Agu , Abuja ; Nigerian youths have been admonished to enlist massively in the campaign to mitigate the effects of climate change , describing them as key to success in the quest for a sustainable environment , given their vast youthful energies . <p> The Director General , National Orientation Agency ( NOA ) , Garba Abari , represented by the Director of Special Duties , Mrs. Mette Edekobi , gave the admonition at a One-day Sensitization Workshop on Environmental Sustainability and Civic Education at the Government Secondary School ( GSS ) , Nyanya , Abuja . Donating library materials to the school , Abari charged the students to read wide and imbibe the national core values of Discipline , Integrity , Dignity of Labour , Social Justice , Religious Tolerance , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the " Change Begins with Me " reorientation programme of government . <p> On her part , the Principal , GSS Nyanya , Mrs. Blessing Ezendu observed that the workshop was apt and timely considering the far-reaching impacts of climate change , adding that everyone has a part to play in ensuring a sustainable environment . <p> Resource persons at the workshop stressed the need for the youth to take environmental sustainability awareness to their homes and neighborhoods in order to discourage greenhouse emissions , deforestation , use of non-biodegradable materials and other climate change inducing practices in the community .
