<h> NCF warns on extinction of wildlife , forests <p> President Board of Trustees Nigerian Conservation Foundation ( NCF ) , Izoma Asiodu , has urged Nigerians to desist from illegal trade in wildlife and destruction of forests to prevent extinction of wildlife and desert encroachment . <p> According to him , these illegal activities are not only responsible for the reduction in the population of wildlife but also desert encroachment in some parts of the nation . <p> Asiodu spoke at the 27th Annual General Meeting of the foundation at its headquarters in Lagos at the weekend . <p> He said : " Illegal trade in wildlife is a major driver of species loss . This callous trade is really depleting the population of wildlife that renders valuable benefits to the ecosystem . <p> " It has been reported that between 2010 and 2012 , approximately 100,000 African elephants were killed out of a population estimated to be less than 500,000 . <p> " Climate change is the biggest global environmental threat .
