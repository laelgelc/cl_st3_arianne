<h> These Aerial Photos Reveal the Devastating Cost of Industrial Pollution <h> From photographer J. Henry Fair 's new book , Industrial Scars <p> When photographer J. Henry Fair first started photographing industrial sites from the air , he did n't know exactly what he was looking at -- so , he learned . This is what making phosphate fertilizer looks like , this is leeching copper ore , this is waste from an aluminum refinery . Now , the images were more than " a beautiful green thing " or " a beautiful red thing , " they were fitting into a story , what Fair calls , " the big story . " <p> Industrial Scars , his new book , attempts to show the vast and incredible marks that humans have left on the planet , from fracking to copper mining to the production of food . " Climate change is so big that people do n't really get it " , he says , " A picture does tell a story that words ca n't tell .
