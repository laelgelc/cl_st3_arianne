Credit : NASA <p> Significant changes in the distribution of plants on Earth can be a reality by 2050 . The prediction is made by scientists from Center for Macroecology , Evolution and Climate at the University of Copenhagen , based on fossilized pollen . The pollen stems from plants that existed during previous periods with climate changes - similar to those expected in this century . <p> We still can not physically travel in time , but ancient fossils can - like a time machine - give us knowledge about the past and the future . <p> In a study published today in Nature Climate Change , scientists use fossilized pollen to examine the future of biodiversity on Earth under climate change . The scientists predict profound changes in the distribution of plants globally .
