<h> The Coming Public Health Disaster <p> NASA scientists recently confirmed that August was the hottest month in recorded history . It tied this past July for that dubious honor . Meteorologists are linking this heat to the recent spate of extreme flooding worldwide -- everywhere from Louisiana to China . <p> However , global warming is n't just an environmental issue . According to the Lancet , a British medical journal , " Climate change could be the biggest global health threat of the 21st century . " <p> High temperatures could set the stage for a public health disaster . They 're disrupting global ecosystems and food production , causing more extreme weather events and wildfires , threatening coastal communities with accelerated sea level rise and creating the perfect conditions for deadly diseases to spread . <p> Reversing the increase in global temperatures is therefore an imperative not just for the environment but for human health , too . <p> This past August is not an anomaly . Higher levels of heat unleash greater concentrations of this chemical , which is the key ingredient in smog . Ozone irritates eyes and diminishes lung function , especially among the young and the elderly . <p> Excess ozone can lead to asthma attacks , bronchitis and other allergic reactions . Over the past 30 years , as the Earth has warmed , the number of Americans suffering from asthma has doubled . <p> Climate change is also increasing the number of animals that carry disease , including fleas , ticks , mosquitos and other insects that thrive in hot weather . Mosquitoes are incubating faster , living longer and spreading to more places . <p> The increased insect population elevates the danger of contracting illnesses like Lyme disease , which is carried by ticks . Debilitating abdominal illness could be the result . <p> For example , the " brain-eating amoeba , " which enters the human body through the nose and then devours the brain , is usually only found in warmer states like Florida or Texas .
