This has prompted the IEA to significantly boost its five-year forecast for renewable energy growth . <p> The announcement challenges Prime Minister Malcolm Turnbull 's claims that coal will remain a strong part of Australia 's energy mix for decades to come . <p> The latest edition of the IEA 's Medium-term Renewable Market Report predicts renewables will remain the fastest-growing source of electricity generation over the next five years , with their share growing to 28% in 2021 from 23% in 2015 . <p> Last year marked a turning point for renewables . Led by wind and solar , renewables represented more than 60% of the new power capacity around the world , rapidly closing the gap with coal .
