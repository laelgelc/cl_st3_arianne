This put the first nail in the coffin , especially as many felt uneasy by the social Darwinist legacy of Malthus as well as several coercive ( and highly publicised ) attempts at population control in the Developing World by the West in the sixties and seventies . <p> Later , in 2002 , John Howard won an election campaign over the Tampa ' crisis ' and the slogan ' we will decide who comes into this country ' .
