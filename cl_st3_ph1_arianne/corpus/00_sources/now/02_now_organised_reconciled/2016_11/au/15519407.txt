<p> Scientific reports released for a recent conference on disaster risk reduction warn that people are already dying and economies being hit by climate change -- and that the dangers are growing . <p> Paul Brown* <p> The massive economic and health losses that climate change is already causing across the world are detailed in six scientific papers published in July . <p> " Current climate conditions in tropical and subtropical parts of the world are already so hot during the hot seasons that occupational health effects occur and work capacity for many people is affected . " <p> Perhaps most striking is the warning about large productivity losses already being experienced due to heat stress , which can already be calculated for 43 countries . One of the papers estimates that in South-East Asia alone , " as much as 15% to 20% of annual work hours may already be lost in heat-exposed jobs " .
