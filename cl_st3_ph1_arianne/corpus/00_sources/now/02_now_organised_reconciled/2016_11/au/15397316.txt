<h> WA premier heckled at Western Power rally <p> WA Premier Colin Barnett has been abused and heckled while speaking to a hostile crowd of hundreds of people rallying against plans to privatise utility Western Power . <p> Mr Barnett tried to explain the government 's plans , saying it would be good for West Australians and all but guaranteed that their electricity bills would not go up because the company would be regulated . <p> The debt-laden company could ' pay interest to banks or dividends to Australian super funds ' , he said outside Parliament House , again flagging a desire to sell the poles and wires utility to superannuation funds . <p> He was booed and shouted down by a crowd of mostly union members chanting ' Western Power not for sale ' and ' it 's our power Colin ' . <p> Unions WA member Tim Dymond insisted the overseas experience showed privatisation led to a worse service for higher prices as private providers extracted profit . <p> ' It 's not that governments ca n't take bad decisions but they are also accountable through @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ companies are not , ' he told AAP . <p> University of WA English lecturer Stephen Dedman attended the rally and said privatising Western Power would mean the public lost control of an essential service including its maintenance . <p> ' The environmental impact of cutting services or maintenance would be disastrous - climate change is going to mean we have more bushfires , ' he said . <p> WA Labor leader Mark McGowan was cheered at the rally when he guaranteed a government with him as premier would never privatise the company . <p> Treasurer Mike Nahan , who has driven the privatisation push , accused Labor last week of running a ' Trump-like propaganda campaign ' in saying it would threaten energy supplies .
