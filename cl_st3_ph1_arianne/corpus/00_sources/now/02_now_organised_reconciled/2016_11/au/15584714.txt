<p> The Carmichael coal mine planned for Queensland 's Galilee Basin has cleared another legal hurdle , with the state 's Supreme Court dismissing a legal challenge to the validity of the Queensland government 's decision to approve the project . <p> The court found in favour of the Queensland Department of Environment and Heritage Protection , ruling that its approval of Indian firm Adani 's proposal was within the rules . <p> The decision is another setback for environmentalists ' bid to stop the controversial project .
