<p> Generation Y has grown up in a rapidly warming world . According to the US National Climate Data Centre , every month since February 1985 has seen above average global temperatures , compared with the twentieth century . I have no memories of a " normal " month . <p> The question now is what the future will look like . At some point in the decades to come , these record-breaking temperatures will not be rare ; they will become normal . But when exactly ? <p> In a new study just released in the Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society , I ( together with co-authors Andrew King and Sarah Perkins-Kirkpatrick ) find that on the current greenhouse gas emissions trajectory , global temperatures like 2015 will by normal by 2030 , and Australia 's record-breaking 2013 summer will likely be an average summer by 2035 . <p> While we still have time to delay some of these changes , others are already locked in - cutting emissions will make no difference - so we must also adapt to a warmer world . <p> What is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ used broadly in the media and in scientific literature to make sense of climate change .
