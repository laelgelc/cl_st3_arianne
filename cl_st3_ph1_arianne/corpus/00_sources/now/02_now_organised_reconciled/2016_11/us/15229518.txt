In 2010 , the Supreme Court opened the door to unlimited electoral spending by corporations and unions so long as they remain ostensibly independent from the candidates they support . A subsequent lower-court ruling extended this decision to individuals , and afterward super PACs were created to serve as vehicles for these unlimited contributions . <p> A system of barely independent unlimited-money groups soon emerged to process giant contributions from billionaires , corporations and unions aiming to influence elections .
