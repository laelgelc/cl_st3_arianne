<h> Study Predicts More Days of Record Heat in the Future <p> The United States is already setting twice as many daily heat records as cold records , but a new study predicts that will get a lot more lopsided as man-made climate change worsens . <p> Under normal conditions , without extra heat-trapping gases from human activity , the nation should set about the same number of hot and cold records over the course of several years . But that 's not happening and it 's steadily getting worse , scientists said . <p> If and when the nation warms another 4.5 degrees ( 2.5 degrees Celsius ) , expect there to be around 15 heat records for every cold one , the new study in Monday 's Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences predicts .
