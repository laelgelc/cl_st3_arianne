<p> The 21 men and women who stood awaiting the nation 's highest civilian honor Tuesday in the White House East Room represented Barack Obama 's particular vision of the United States : one where pioneering scientists , groundbreaking performers , crusading activists and unconventional artists chart America 's destiny . <p> President Obama has not stinted on handing out the Presidential Medal of Freedom during his time in office : He has bestowed it on at least 114 individuals , more than any of his predecessors . Nearly half of the latest honorees were artists , actors or musicians , including singer Bruce Springsteen , actor Robert Redford and " Saturday Night Live " producer Lorne Michaels . <p> But there were also computer engineers and famous architects , as well as what the president called " rabble rousers " and philanthropists . <p> Striking a nostalgic tone at the end of the ceremony , the president said : " So , just on a personal note , part of the reason why these events are so special to me is because everybody on this stage has touched @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that they probably could n't imagine .
