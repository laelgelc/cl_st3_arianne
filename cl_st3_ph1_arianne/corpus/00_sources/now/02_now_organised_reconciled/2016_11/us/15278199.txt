<h> Shallow reefs off Singapore survive in the face of adversity <h> November 8 , 2016 <p> An analysis of the health of highly impacted coral reefs off Singapore during a 27-year long period has shown they are more resilient to the impacts of human activity and warming than expected . <p> A UNSW-led international team found that shallower reefs rebounded rapidly from a major bleaching episode in 1998 , despite experiencing such high levels of sedimentation that underwater visibility was typically less than 2 metres . <p> " It is remarkable that diverse shallow coral communities can persist in such adverse conditions , " says study first author Dr James Guest , formerly of UNSW and now at the Hawai'i Institute of Marine Biology . <p> " Undoubtedly , Singapore 's reefs have suffered as a result of human activities , but the recovery of corals at shallow sites is really surprising given how impacted this environment is . It really shows how tough corals can be .
