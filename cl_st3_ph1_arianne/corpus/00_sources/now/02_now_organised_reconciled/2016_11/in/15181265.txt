<h> Monitoring Committee reviews control of avian influenza : Report <p> Except for the death of a Muniya bird in the National Zoological Park , New Delhi , no other avian influenza casualties were reported during the past 24 hours to the Monitoring Committee set up by the Government . <p> An official press release said the committee , constituted by the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change to oversee the outbreak of H5N8 avian influenza , reviewed the control and containment of the influenza situation . <p> No deaths were reported from other affected areas including Deer Park at Hauz Khas , New Delhi and Gwalior Zoo . <p> The control and containment measures of avian influenza in the affected zoos were being continued , which include continuing surveillance and the zoo being screened regularly for any dead bird , the release said . <p> Bio-security measures were being strictly enforced . Disinfection was being regularly done by spraying anti-virus and other solutions . <p> Delhi Zoo remains closed for the safety of visitors and control of the disease .
