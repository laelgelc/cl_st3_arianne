<h> U'khand to set aside 1% budget of all govt depts for climate change <p> Seema Sharma <p> In a crucial move , the Uttarakhand government has decided to allocate one per cent of the total budget of each of its 66 departments towards fighting the climate change which is threatening to assume alarming proportions <p> TNN Nov 23 , 2016 , 10.38 PM IST <p> DEHRADUN : In a crucial move , the Uttarakhand government has decided to allocate one per cent of the total budget of each of its 66 departments towards fighting the climate change which is threatening to assume alarming proportions . <p> State council on climate change has given the go ahead for the move . Experts said that the move , if implemented properly , will generate sensitivity among departments towards dealing with the problem . <p> RN Jha , chief conservator of forest and nodal head on climate change in the state , said : " Uttarakhand Council on Climate Change has approved the proposal for allocating one per cent budget of all state government department for climate change adaptation programme . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " Jha said . <p> Amit Negi , principal secretary of Disaster Management and secretary in Planning and Finance department , said : " Since the state was currently undergoing budgetary planning , this was a good time for departments to think about climate specific funds . It is important for three or four key departments , like forest , water and energy , to integrate climate action and disaster resilience in their planning . " Negi said that the move was a " milestone " . <p> John Firth , chief executive officer of United Kingdom-based organisaiton Acclimatise , said : " We should also ensure that the 99% of the budget allocation was also focused on eco-friendly development . " <p> He was speaking at a conference here . Chief secretary S Ramaswamy inaugurated the conference . <p> Jai Raj , principal chief conservator of forests , said : " With right approach on development , we will save our efforts , resources and money which otherwise go waste when disaster happens due to faulty development actions . PWD should construct roads keeping the slope in such a way which do not trigger landslides .
