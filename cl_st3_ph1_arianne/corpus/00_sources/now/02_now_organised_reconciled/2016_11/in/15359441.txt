( Source : File ) <p> The Centre has tweaked service rules to allow online filing of officers ' performance reports from the next fiscal . The move , which comes after Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's directive in this regard , will help check delayed submission of employees ' confidential report which often comes as a hindrance for promotion . <p> Watch what else is making news : <p> It has also been decided that an officer can be assessed solely on the basis of his/her overall record and self-assessment for a year if his Annual Performance Appraisal Report ( APAR ) is not recorded by December 31 of the assessment year . It is expected that by now sufficient progress would have been made towards the compliance with the earlier directive and the departments would be in a position to roll out mandatory online filing of APARs/Annual Confidential Reports ( ACRs ) from the financial year 2017-18 , said the letter written by Rajiv Kumar , Establishment Officer . <p> A meeting of representatives of the ministries concerned has been called on Thursday to review the progress made in this regard " so as to ensure implementation of mandatory online filing of APARs from financial year 2017-18 onwards " , it said .
