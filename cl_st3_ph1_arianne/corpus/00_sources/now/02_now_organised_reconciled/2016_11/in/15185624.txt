<p> To develop Chandigarh as a green city , UT Administrator V P Singh Badnore on Wednesday asked officials of the Chandigarh administration to visit Bristol -- the winner of 2015 Green Capital of Europe Award . UT home secretary Anurag Agarwal and director tourism Jatinder Yadav have been given the responsibility to visit Bristol and prepare a report . <p> Badnore , who was at the UT Secretariat , said both the officers were going to attend World Travel Market , scheduled to be held in London from November 7 to November 9 . <p> Thereafter , the officers will visit Bristol , which is located in South West of England , from November 10 to November 12 . <p> Agarwal said they will study the climate change plan , public transport and energy . Since 2005 , carbon emissions in Bristol have been reducing . They aim to become the hub for low-carbon industry in Europe . <p> In contrary , the quality of air in Chandigarh has deteriorated beyond permissible . The Greening Chandigarh Task Group has prepared the Greening Chandigarh Action Plan 2016-17 . The plan comprises plantation targets of various departments and agencies and the strategies to achieve the desired result . <p> During this year , 2,20,000 saplings are being planted in a phased manner on government and private land and 50,000 saplings are being distributed by the forest department for free to the citizens of Chandigarh .
