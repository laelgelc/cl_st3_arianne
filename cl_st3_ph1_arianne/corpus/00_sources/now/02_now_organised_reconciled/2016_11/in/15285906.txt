<h> UN 's 22nd Conference of Parties , COP 22 begins <p> Marrakech , Nov 8 : Minister of States ( Independent Charge ) , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Government of India , inaugurated the Indian pavilion in the UN 's 22nd conference of parties ( COP 22 ) which began at Marrakech , Morocco , Africa on November 7 . Representatives from fourteen States of India including Shambhu Singh , Additional Chief Secretary , Forest and Environment , Government of Manipur and Dr Brajakumar , Deputy Director , Directorate of Environment , Manipur , has been nominated as Indian delegates.The conference is organised by the United Nation Framework Convention for Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) participated by over 192 countries.Many international negotiations on climate change mitigation and adaptations amongst the countries will be debated during these 12 days ( November 7-18 ) . UNDP is hosting a session on " Innovation to enhance climate actions at sub national level " in the Indian pavilion of COP22 Marrakech .
