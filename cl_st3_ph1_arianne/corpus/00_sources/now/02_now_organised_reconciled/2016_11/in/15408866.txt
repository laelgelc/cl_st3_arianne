<h> Ahmedabad teen 's green power film wins int'l award <p> TNN Updated : Nov 16 , 2016 , 11.37 AM IST <p> AHMEDABAD : An Amdavadi teen 's short film on climate change has won the first prize in Film4Climate Global Video Competition , organized as part of ' Connect4Climate ' event for youth . The winners were announced at the United Nations Climate Conference ( COP22 ) in Marrakesh , Morocco , on Sunday . <p> Rameshwar Bhatt , 15 , a class XI student , had participated in the event with his film " A Sun at Night " that shows how a locality in Shahpur had switched to solar energy to fulfil its power requirements . The film is told from the perspective of a local woman and how the switch has changed her life . <p> The film has won first prize in ' Young Award , ' a category for participants in the 13-17 year age bracket . The event had received 860 videos from 155 countries . <p> " It was almost a last-minute entry for the competition after I shot the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ theme of alternative energy is close to my heart and I knew about the successful experiment in Ahmedabad .
