<p> Tomorrow the Ocean Energy Forum will release a report that charts a path to 10% of Europe 's energy needs being met by tidal and wave energy . The roadmap outlines five main phases for success . These phases would be underpinned by a 250 million euro investment fund and a 70 million euro insurance fund . Research and Development , Prototype , Demonstration , Pre-Commercial and Industrial roll out could then be delivered along the envisioned timetable . Commissioner for the Environment , Maritime Affairs and Fisheries , Karmenu Vella , who will attend the Ocean Energy Conference in Brussels for the launch of the roadmap , said : " The roadmap estimates that ocean energy could meet up to 10% of the EU 's power demand by 2050 , equivalent to cutting 276 million tonnes of CO2 every year " .
