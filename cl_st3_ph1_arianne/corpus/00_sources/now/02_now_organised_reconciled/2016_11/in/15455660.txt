<p> Exploring how the construction industry can reduce the carbon footprint of infrastructure projects and drive sustainability throughout the value chain , while also significantly reducing business costs , was the focus of the latest CCC seminar . The event , which was held on November 10th in Birmingham , England , was hosted by Volvo CE and endorsed by the Green Construction Board ( GCB ) . <p> 100 business leaders from major infrastructure projects , contractors and research bodies , including Skanska , Crossrail , Anglian Water and Cambridge and Leeds universities , as well as Government agencies , attended the seminar to listen to distinguished speakers share best practices on how to reduce the industry 's carbon footprint . The event covered an array of topics such as , the challenges of reducing carbon in infrastructure projects , tools and guidance to measure and achieve carbon reduction , client 's expectations of their supply chains and how new technology and low carbon materials can help deliver substantial carbon and cost reductions , while at the same time delivering higher performance . <p> Part of the solution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emissions from its products and facilities , but climate change is too big of an issue to be dealt with through the resources of one company alone , " said Volvo CE senior vice president , Bill Law ( right ) , who introduced the seminar .
