<h> Plea in HC against AWBI removal from animal testing panel <p> New Delhi , Nov 20 ( PTI ) Delhi High Court has sought the Centres response on a plea claiming that the Animal Welfare Board of India ( AWBI ) was being excluded from a panel on animal testing , as it had opposed a government notification allegedly paving way for bulls to be used for " performances " like Jallikattu . <p> Jallikattu , also known Eruthazhuvuthal , is a bull-taming sport played in Tamil Nadu as part of the Pongal harvest festival . <p> Justice Sanjeev Sachdeva issued notice to the Ministry of Environment Forests and Climate Change and the Control and Supervision of Experimentation on Animals ( CPCSEA ) seeking their replies on the petition which alleges that the Boards removal from the panel violates the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act , apart from being also " arbitrary and high handed " . <p> The plea by a member of the Board claims that the government by way of a notification issued on January 7 had excluded bulls from the list of animals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the Supreme Court which had stayed the circular . <p> Subsequently , the Board was excluded from CPCSEA that was reconstituted on February 17 , without informing AWBI which has been part of the panel since 1991 , the petition has alleged . <p> The court listed the matter , which seeks setting aside of the February 17 decision reconstituting the CPCSEA , for next hearing on January 23 next . <p> The petition also seeks reconstitution of the panel with adequate representation of AWBI . <p> Central government standing counsel Jasmeet Singh contended during the brief hearing that under the Act the Board was not required to be made a member of the CPCSEA . <p> The petition by Chinny Krishna , Vice Chairman of the Board , claims that CPCSEA was set up on AWBIs advice which was done as per the provisions of the Act .
