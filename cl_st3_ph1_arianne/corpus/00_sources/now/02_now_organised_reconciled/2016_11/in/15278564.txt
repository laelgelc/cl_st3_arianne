<h> India and UK released Joint Statement <h> sangeeta Krishnan <p> India and the United Kingdom released a Joint Statement on 7 November 2015 to strengthen their strategic partnership . The joint statement was released after meeting of Prime Minister Narendra Modi and UK Prime Minister Theresa May in Delhi . <p> PM Theresa May was on a three-day visit to India from 6 November to 8 November 2016 at the invitation of the PM Modi . <p> This was May 's first bilateral visit outside Europe after taking over as the Prime Minister of the UK .
