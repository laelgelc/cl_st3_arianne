Yet wind and hydropower projects have been linked to forced displacements , threats , intimidation and killings of local community leaders , while Amnesty International 's latest research has exposed child labour in electric car supply chains . <p> Although 34 out of the 50 companies have some community consultation commitments in place , only five of them commit to respecting indigenous peoples ' free , prior &amp; informed consent ( FPIC ) . Three of those five have allegations against them about this commitment . <p> Undoubtedly , renewable energy done right could have enormous benefits to local communities .
