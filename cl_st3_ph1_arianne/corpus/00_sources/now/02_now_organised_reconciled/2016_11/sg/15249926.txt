<h> Focus on renewable energy innovations to reduce carbon emissions <p> It was a relief to read that Indonesia has finally agreed to sign on the dotted line as a sign of its commitment to fight its extensive deforestation and forest fires that have contributed greatly to its carbon emissions ( " Indonesia ratifies Paris climate change pact " ; Oct 20 ) . <p> However , certain green groups have described that goal of Indonesia as " ambitious " , as the country 's climate woes are deeply rooted and intertwined such that there are no easy answers . <p> Now , buildings being built are required to be more green and energy efficient . There are even plans for future buildings to produce more energy than they consume . <p> The Republic has pledged to reach its peak in emissions by 2030 ; which is also the year in which our population is projected to reach 6.9 million by the Population White Paper in 2013 . <p> The Paris climate agreement may look good on paper , like the Kyoto Protocol , but the gist of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in terms of the actual reduction in greenhouse gas emissions . <p> So , what does that mean to the man in the street ? <p> It has often been said that our people are enslaved by the 3Cs , namely comfort , convenience and consumerism .
