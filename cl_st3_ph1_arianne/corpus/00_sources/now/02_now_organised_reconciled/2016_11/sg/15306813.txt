<h> India pitches for cultivation of medicinal plants for climate change adaptation in India <p> The Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education ( ICFRE ) has pitched for conservation and cultivation of medicinal plants for climate change adaptation in India at the Conference of Parties ( COP22 ) United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> The ICFREE underlined the importance of medicinal plants in India , which has about 6,000 plants species codified under the natural and herbal medical system . <p> Dr G.S . Goraya , a senior official from ICFREE , said that a research in 18 Indian states , including north-east has listed about 350 medicinal plants under the threat listed as " red " . <p> Of which , 100 are in active threat and the wild population has reached a level of " no return " . <p> The official said that the situation has forced the industry to use " substitutes " of such plants , which are less efficacious . <p> " The herbal plants are needed in high quantities but they are not available in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ harvesting and increasing biotic pressure in the harvested area , " Goraya said , adding " diminishing habitats " is an area of concern for the country . <p> He stated the factors that have triggered changing climate are weakening resilience of the rootstocks .
