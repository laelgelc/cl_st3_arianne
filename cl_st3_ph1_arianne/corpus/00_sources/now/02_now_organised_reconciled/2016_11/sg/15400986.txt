" <p> Scientists say oil and other fossil fuels are the biggest contributors to man-made warming . Trump , however , has called global warming a " hoax " and pledged during his campaign to " cancel " the Paris deal . <p> " The agreement was historic , " Hollande said . " But what we must say here is that this agreement is irreversible . " <p> Earlier , U.N . Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon said he hopes Trump will shift his course on global warming and " understand the seriousness and urgency " of addressing the problem . <p> " As president of the United States , I 'm sure that he will understand this , he will listen and he will evaluate his campaign remarks , " Ban told reporters in Marrakech . <p> The Paris Agreement was signed by more than 190 countries and has been formally approved by more than 100 of them , including the United States and even oil-rich Saudi Arabia . <p> Ban called international climate action " unstoppable " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " is immune from the impacts of global warming . <p> " We have no right to gamble with the fate of future generations -- or imperil the survival of other species that share our planet , " Ban told the conference . <p> Trump 's election has created uncertainty about the U.S. role in the Paris deal , which calls on all countries to reduce or curb their greenhouse gas emissions and encourages rich countries to help poor ones deal with climate change . <p> King Mohammed VI of Morocco urged delegates at the conference to translate their commitments into actions . <p> " What 's at stake is the very existence of man , " the king said . " It is therefore our joint duty to work hand in hand to protect humanity . "
