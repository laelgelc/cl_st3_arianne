Oil palm plantations at industrial and smallholder scale have displaced large areas of tropical forest and their increasingly threatened wildlife . As was shown in a recent study on Borneo , the rate at which this happens is still increasing . So what to do ? <p> There are several possible strategies for reducing the impact of the oil palm sector on nature .
