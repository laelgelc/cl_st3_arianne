<h> Maritime and Port Authority names new chairman <p> THE Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore ( MPA ) said on Monday its deputy chairman , Niam Chiang Meng , will be appointed chairman of MPA with effect from Dec 16 , 2016 . <p> Mr Niam will take over from Lucien Wong , who will relinquish his post as MPA chairman on Dec 15 as he assumes office as Deputy Attorney-General of Singapore from Dec 19 before taking over as Attorney-General . <p> Mr Niam has been deputy chairman of MPA since July 2016 . He retired from the administrative service on Aug 1 , 2016 , after 33 years of service in the public sector . <p> Before his retirement as Permanent Secretary to the National Population and Talent Division and National Climate Change Secretariat , Mr Niam was chief executive of the Housing &amp; Development Board , and Permanent Secretary for the Ministry of Communications and Information , the Ministry of Community Development , Youth and Sports , and the Ministry of Law .
