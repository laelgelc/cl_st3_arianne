Or car owners to take public transport more often . <p> Messages emphasising the impact of climate change and the importance of individual action have been told and retold over the years for decades . Yet , behavioural changes are perhaps the hardest to cultivate . <p> At the Ministry of the Environment and Water Resources ( MEWR ) , its marketing communications team has been working to ramp up awareness of the Sustainable Singapore Blueprint . The policy paper , which maps out the Government 's effort to build a more liveable and sustainable future , was first put out in 2009 . <p> Efforts to brand it and give it an identity started last year , says Ms Andrea Chua , senior assistant director of MEWR 's five-person marketing communications department . <p> Two of its officers - senior executives Ian Kong and Chee Kang Xiong - were hired in 2015 and 2013 respectively as they were trained in film and video .
