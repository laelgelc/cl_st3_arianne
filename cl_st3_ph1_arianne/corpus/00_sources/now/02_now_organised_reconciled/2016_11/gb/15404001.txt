<p> When I was a child , I felt a bit sad that I did not get to see most of history . I was too young for 9/11 and not born at the right time for flying cars or the teleportation machine . I had the impression that I was born when history decided to slow down for a decade or two . <p> And I guess that is good . A major challenge for Western countries and the world general at the time was terrorism . The next one , I thought , would be climate change .
