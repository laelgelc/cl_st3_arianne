All these risks are reflected in global reactions . So the news of the election outcome was greeted with a round of applause in the Russian parliament . The Kremlin will no doubt be hoping that Trump 's frequent overtures to Putin , including his expressions of admiration for the strongman , will offer areas of negotiation to Russia 's benefit .
