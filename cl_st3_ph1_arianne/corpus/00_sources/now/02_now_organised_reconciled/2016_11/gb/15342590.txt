<p> JOHN Kerry has become the highest-ranking US official to visit Antarctica when he landed there yesterday ahead of a two-day trip to hear about the impact of climate change on the frozen continent . <p> The US Secretary of State flew from New Zealand , where his aides had earlier described the trip as a learning opportunity . Kerry planned to receive briefings from scientists working to understand the effects of climate change on Antarctica . <p> Upon arriving in Antarctica , his plans called for his entourage to transfer immediately at the small McMurdo airstrip to a smaller military transport plane for a three-hour flight to the research station the US government operates near the South Pole . <p> Kerry planned to visit that station for about two hours before returning to McMurdo for the night . <p> He has made climate change an intensive focus of American diplomacy during his term in office , and had previously spent decades working on the issue as a US senator .
