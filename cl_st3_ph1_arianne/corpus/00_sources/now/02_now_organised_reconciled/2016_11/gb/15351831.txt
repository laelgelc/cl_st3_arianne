At the level of policy , the legislative achievements of Obama over the last eight years could be erased in a matter of months . With Republican control of both the House and Senate , little stands in the way ( except for perhaps a legislative filibuster in the Senate ) of Trump pushing his agenda through Congress . Ironically , we know little of what this policy agenda might actually be , given his predilection for contradiction and ambiguity . There is no disguising the hammer blow that Trump 's victory is to the Democratic Party . <p> Perhaps most significantly of all , this election has enormous implications for American society . History is replete with countless examples of failed retreats to nationalism and protectionism , and the calamitous risks involved .
