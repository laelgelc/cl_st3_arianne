<p> Prince Albert of Monaco has sent a congratulatory letter to President-elect of the United States , Donald Trump . <p> In his message , he solidified the commitment the Principality of Monaco has with the United States on promoting their shared values . However , he also requested that the President-elect preserve the United States ' commitment to end climate change .
