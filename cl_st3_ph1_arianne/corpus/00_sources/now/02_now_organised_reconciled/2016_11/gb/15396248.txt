<h> Jonathan Pershing said the Paris agreement is ' durable , it is inclusive it is ambitious ' . <p> The US special envoy on climate change has said that the Paris climate deal will continue to exist even under Donald Trump , amid reports that Trump could withdraw the country from the pact . <p> Jonathan Pershing , the lead negotiator for Washington , told a packed press briefing that the efforts put in by the countries that have ratified the Paris Climate Change Agreement are good enough to withstand the impact of the Trump presidency . <p> " Heads of state can and will change but I am confident that we can and we will sustain a durable international effort to counter climate change , " the BBC quoted him as saying . <p> Pershing 's comments come as global leaders are gathering in Marrakech in Morocco to take the deal forward .
