<p> ISLAMABAD : Speakers at an awareness session on Monday highlighted impacts of climate change on rural population of Pakistan , especially small farmers . <p> They said lives of millions of people had been jeopardised due to climate change effects . " The real challenge lies in ensuring the implementation of the Paris Agreement to avert catastrophic climate change and enhance climate resilience , " they added . <p> The session was organised by Oxfam and Indus Consortium here on Monday . Oxfam urged developed countries to fulfil their pledges to allocate resources that will help vulnerable people to adapt and build climate resilience . <p> " There is a need for taking concrete actions to cut greenhouse emissions and provide climate finance to support climate adaptation in developing countries - like Pakistan - which is 8th most affected country in the world .
