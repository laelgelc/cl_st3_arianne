<p> KARACHI : The Consul General of Morocco and Chairman Pak-Morocco Joint Business Council Ishtiaq Baig left for Morocco to attend 22nd session of the Global Climate Change Conference ( COP22 ) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) in Marrakech as part of Pakistan 's delegation headed by the Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid , on invitation of the Government of Morocco . The historic conference on climate change will be attended by the King of Morocco H.M. King Mohammed VI , including many heads of the states , delegates and experts from the world community , to discuss the issue of global warming . Ishtiaq Baig will also discuss industrial pollution in Pakistan with the visiting delegates .
