The nature of the disasters in South Asian countries including floods and draughts exist across the region and thus it can be responded to through collaborative mechanisms . <p> Dr. Imran Khalid of SDPI presented details of water related complexities in relations to climate change and said that we need a trans-border climate change adaptation strategy .
