<p> Sir : Food wastage has become a serious economic and environmental issue in Pakistan . Be it is a five-star hotel or a school cafeteria , a large quantity of food is wasted each day . <p> Producing , storing and cooking food requires water , fuel and energy . Each of these processes emits various greenhouse gases that ultimately lead to the climate change . Climate change is the major issue these days and the whole world is affected by it . The most evident result of climate change today is smog that has made lives miserable . Food wastage is also one of the reasons that we are losing biodiversity . Also , wasting food is disrespect to the blessings of God . <p> If we are lucky enough to get food , we should not waste it . There are millions of people in the world who do not get food twice a day . If the food wastage continued with the same pace , we could face food shortage and drought in not so distant future . Food should be cooked according to the requirement . <p> Still , if it is in excess , it can be frozen and used some other time .
