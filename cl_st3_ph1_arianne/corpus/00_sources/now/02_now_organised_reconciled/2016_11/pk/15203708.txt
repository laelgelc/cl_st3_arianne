<p> LAHORE : The motorway from Lahore to Pindi Bhattian was closed for traffic on Thursday night , owing to the fog which had made lives difficult for citizens . <p> In Lahore , citizens felt difficulties to set out of homes and travel due to heavy smog . People complained of sore eyes due to the heavy spell which was continued since yesterday . <p> A 20-member special team of the Punjab government submitted its initial report on smog to Chief Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Thursday . <p> The report states that due to the climate change many areas of the city are under heavy fog , advising people to travel carefully . <p> Health experts have warned citizens to take care and in case of soreness in the eyes , wash them with cold and fresh water .
