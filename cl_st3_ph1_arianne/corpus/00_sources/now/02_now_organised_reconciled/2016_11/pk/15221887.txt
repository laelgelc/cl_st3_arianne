<h> Climate change : Paris Agreement comes into force <p> The Paris Agreement on Climate Change will come into force on Friday , less than a year after its adoption in Paris . The development takes place a few days ahead of the start of COP-22 in Marrakesh . <p> A press release stated that the swiftness of this process was unprecedented . It added no other multilateral treaty of such prominence had been ratified by as many countries in a short span of time . The agreement was ratified by more than 80 states , accounting jointly for more than 60 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions . <p> On the occasion , Ambassador of France to Pakistan Martine Dorance welcomed Pakistan 's ratification of the Paris Agreement on November 2 . " It is an extremely positive sign as it reflects Pakistan 's commitment to the fight against climate change one year after the Paris COP21 and just a few days before the next international Conference , COP22 , starts in Marrakesh . <p> I also welcome the announcements of the Federal Minister for Climate Change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pakistan 's climate change bill . " <p> She continued that France will stay committed as it is time to implement the agreement . " Marrakesh will be the first follow-up conference to the Paris Agreement and will be devoted to climate action . "
