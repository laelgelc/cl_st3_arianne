<h> Heavy fog engulfs Punjab , 12 dead in various accidents <p> LAHORE , Pakistan : Heavy fog on Thursday engulfed the Punjab Province which not only caused disruption in traffic flow on motorways but also left 12 persons dead in various accidents . <p> Sources said that 12 vehicles struck each other on motorway near Sukheki interchange in Punjab 's Hafizabad district because of dense Smog , resulting in deaths of nine persons and 70 injuries . <p> Two persons including a woman and her son lost their lives when two vehicles collided at Charagh Abad Interchange on motorway . Three other persons were also injured in the accident and were shifted to Allied Hospital Faisalabad . <p> Likewise , an accident occurred due to dense fog in Khushab in which one person was killed . <p> Meanwhile , several sections of the motorway were closed after the spell of dense fog disrupted movement of traffic in Punjab . <p> The M2 motorway , connecting Rawalpindi and Islamabad to Lahore , was closed from Sial Mor to Lahore in the wake of zero visibility due to heavy fog @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ M3 motorway , leading to Faisalabad from Pindi Bhattian , was also closed completely due to zero visibility , he added . <p> From Islamabad to Sial Mor , light fog was observed with visibility 80-150 meters and the section remained open . <p> On GT Road , the visibility was 80-140 meters as light fog engulfed Kamonki , yet , it too remained open for traffic . <p> In Lahore , citizens felt difficulties to set out of homes and travel due to heavy smog . People complained of sore eyes due to the heavy spell which was continued since yesterday . <p> A 20-member special team of the Punjab government submitted its initial report on smog to Chief Minister Muhammad Shahbaz Sharif on Thursday . <p> The report states that due to the climate change many areas of the city are under heavy fog , advising people to travel carefully . <p> Health experts in the committee recommended that children and elderly citizens should be protected from smog and covered their faces with masks while going out . In case of sore eyes , wash them with fresh and cold water .
