<h> Pakistan may go dry by 2025 <p> ISLAMABAD : Owing to climate change , Pakistan 's nascent water resources may be depleted by 2025 , warned experts on Wednesday . <p> During a seminar " Climate Change : Challenges and Strategies " at the National University of Modern Languages ( NUML ) on Wednesday , Strengthening Participatory Organization CEO Nasser Memon said that unless solid measures are taken to protect our water resources , we could face devastating effects . <p> He said that while climate change was a global phenomenon , Pakistan is among the top five countries which are badly affected by the rapid environmental changes . He added that in the 20th century , the sea levels globally increased at an average of 10 to 20cm while snow fall had reduced by at least one to two days per year . <p> International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) Global Vice-President Malik Amin Aslam urged the government to create awareness among masses about the importance of trees and to make planting more trees mandatory for every Pakistani . <p> Aslam lauded Khyber- Pakhtunkhwa @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that other provinces would follow in their footsteps to launch their own campaigns to help combat onset of climate change .
