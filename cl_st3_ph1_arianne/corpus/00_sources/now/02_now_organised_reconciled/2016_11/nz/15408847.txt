<h> Resilient home winner shows how we may cope with climate change in the future <p> COLLEEN HAWKES <p> Last updated 11:09 , November 16 2016 <p> A Home for All Seasons by JTP and Ed Barsley is the winning entry in the Sunday Times British Home Resilient Home of the Future Award . <p> If you ca n't beat it , plan to live with it . That 's the philosophy behind the design of this development , which has just won the Sunday Times British Homes Resilient Home of the Future Award . <p> And it 's all about climate change and extreme weather events . <p> The winning design by JTP and Ed Barsley is called A Home for All Seasons . It features three-level terrace houses where the living is on the upper two floors . In a flood , the ground floor , accommodating the garaging , can be sacrificed without damage to the house and residents . <p> SUPPLIED <p> The terrace housing is designed to accommodate flooding by having the living areas on the upper two levels . <p> ?
