<h> Scientists study seabird distributions <p> Knowing where an albatross prefers to dine is vital information when it comes to undertaking any marine activity . <p> Whether it 's setting up an aquaculture business , looking for minerals or fishing , understanding the effect any ocean-based activity may have on our sea birds is crucial to their future . <p> That 's why NIWA scientists are undertaking research that will help us better understand what several of our seabird species are up to . <p> The research is being funded by the Sustainable Seas National Science Challenge , which aims to enhance use of the New Zealand 's marine resources while ensuring the marine environment is cared for and used wisely . <p> The Sustainable Seas challenge is working to address many of the issues raised in a report on the state of our marine environment , released last week by the Ministry for the Environment ( MfE ) and Statistics New Zealand . <p> The report said New Zealand 's marine environment is under increasing pressure from climate change , pollution and pests .
