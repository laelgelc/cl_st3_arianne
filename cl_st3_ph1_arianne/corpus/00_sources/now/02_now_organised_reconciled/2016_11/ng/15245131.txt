<h> 17 motorcycles have been donated to the Enugu State Government for the supervision of Neglected Tropical Diseases ' ( NTDs ) projects . <p> The donation was made possible by the U.S -- based Research Triangular Institute ( RTI ) and The Carter Center ( TCC ) Nigeria . <p> The Project Administrator in charge of Enugu , Anambra and Ebonyi States ' Projects of the Carter Centre Nigeria , Mr Emmanuel Alor , presented the motorcycles to the state government on behalf of the RTI and TCC on Saturday in Enugu . <p> It was done during the 2016 strategic planning meeting organised by Enugu State Ministry of Health . <p> Speaking at the occasion , Alor said the donation was to assist the NTDs to overcome logistic challenges encountered by its coordinators during treatment data collection . <p> He was optimistic that the motorcycles would facilitate the flow of data from the local government councils to the state and warned against their conversion to other usages . <p> The Enugu State Commissioner for Health , Dr Samuel Ngwu , who received the motorcycles , said the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at the local government councils . <p> The commissioner said that the state would continue to mobilize rural dwellers in the 17 local government councils to embrace the NTDs programme . <p> Ngwu , therefore , commended the Institute and the TCC for their efforts in supporting the state government toward the elimination of tropical diseases in the state within the framework of the WHO guidelines . <p> Also speaking , the Permanent Secretary in the ministry , Mr Sylvester Ugwuagbo , commended the donor agencies for the gesture and pledged that the motorcycles would be put into good use . <p> He promised to secure official permission for their use in the state capital following the ban on riding of motorcycles in Enugu State by the state government . <p> President Muhamadu Buhari ( L ) , and Gov .
