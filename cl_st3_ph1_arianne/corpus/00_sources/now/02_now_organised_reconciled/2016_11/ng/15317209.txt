<h> What a Trump presidency means for U.S. and global climate policy <p> At the international climate negotiations in Morocco today , the mood after the election is deeply uneasy as the United States ' role in such efforts is now uncertain . <p> The U.S. has over recent years forged a role as a global leader on clean energy and climate change . At the federal level , the Obama administration oversaw a number of regulatory actions that collectively have driven down overall U.S. emissions and have been on track to a roughly 17 percent reduction from 2005 levels by 2020 . <p> Longstanding high investment in energy research and development has paid off in technological advances . States and local jurisdictions have been expanding their own renewable energy goals and improving their preparedness for climate-driven extreme weather events .
