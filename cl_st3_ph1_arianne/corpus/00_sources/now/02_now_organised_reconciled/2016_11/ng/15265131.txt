<h> Marrakech conference opens as Africa tackles climate change <p> The UN Climate Change Conference in Marrakech kicked off today Monday , just three days after the Paris Climate Change Agreement entered into force . <p> At the opening , Morocco 's Foreign Minister and newly-elected COP22 President Salaheddine Mezouar underscored his country 's willingness to host the conference as a demonstration of Africa 's commitment as a whole to contribute to global efforts to tackle climate change . " It emphasizes Africa 's desire to take its destiny in hand , to reduce its vulnerability and strengthen its resilience , " he said . <p> President Mezouar pointed to the groundswell of momentum building around the world .
