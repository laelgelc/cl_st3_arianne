<p> Researchers from the Institute of Agricultural Research &amp; Training Ibadan , Oyo State , in collaboration with the Raw Material Research &amp; Development Council , University of Ibadan , have produced bio-gel , a cooking fuel , from Moringa stem . <p> The process of the production was exhibited on Wednesday at the End-of-project workshop on climate change &amp; fossil fuel reduction and environmental conservation using Moringa Oleifera plant at IAR&amp;T . <p> The workshop was organised by Lifebuilders organisation , an NGO , in collaboration with IAR&amp;T and Raw Material Research &amp; Development Council , Oyo State . <p> Speaking at the event , the IAR&amp;T Director , Prof. James Adediran , noted that the bio-gel production was consistent with one of the institute 's objectives of mitigating effects of climate change . <p> He said the use of Biochar for soil fertility management , botanical for plant protection and planting of improved varieties of crops adapted to Southern ecology received attention in the institute 's programmes . <p> Adediran said : " All our efforts in these areas contribute to the reduction of greenhouse gases . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for an environment-friendly source of energy and all hands must be on deck to make a success .
