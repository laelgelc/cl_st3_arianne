<p> Ten global oil and gas companies on Friday announced they will invest ? 1 billion in low-carbon-emission technologies to help combat climate change . <p> BG Group , BP , Eni , Pemex , Repsol , Saudi Aramco , Reliance Industries , Royal Dutch Shell , Statoil and Total -- who account for almost a fifth of the world 's oil production -- will fund the project through the Oil and Gas Climate Initiative , they said in a statement . <p> " We are on the look-out for potentially game-changing technologies that could have a long-term impact on greenhouse gas reduction , " said the group . <p> Advertisement <p> " To help us meet our ambition , we are launching OGCI Climate Investments , a partnership that will enable us to invest $1 billion over the coming years to support start-ups and help develop and demonstrate innovative technologies that have the potential to reduce greenhouse gas emissions significantly " . <p> The fund will focus on reducing methane emissions , carbon capture and improving industrial and transport efficiency .
