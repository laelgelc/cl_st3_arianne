<p> Chinese President Xi Jinping sent a congratulatory letter Friday to UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon on the taking effect of Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> In the letter , Xi said that thanks to joint efforts , the Paris Agreement became one of the fastest international accords in history to enter into force . <p> Xi stressed that international society has been devoted to boosting the agreement to become effective since it was signed in December 2015 . China signed the agreement on April 22 , the first day it was open to signature , and ratified it on Sept. 3 . <p> Under the Chinese presidency , the first Presidency Statement on Climate Change throughout G20 's history was issued , providing political support for the signing of the agreement . <p> Xi stressed that the Paris Agreement ushers in a new phase of international cooperation on fighting global warming .
