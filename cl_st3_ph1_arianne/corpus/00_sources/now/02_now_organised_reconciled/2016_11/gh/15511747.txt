<h> Rwanda to ratify Montreal protocol amendment by 2017 <p> Rwanda will ratify the ambitious Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol by early next year , according to the ministry of natural resources on Monday . <p> Vincent Biruta , Rwanda minister of natural resources , told reporters that Rwanda intends to ratify the Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol by early 2017 . <p> " We recognize the climate imperative of the amendment . The Kigali amendment is an example of what we can achieve by coming together with a focus on action . Parties to the protocol should speed up ratification processes at the national level . We must support each other in this effort if we are to achieve the ambition set before us , " he said . <p> Last month in the Rwandan capital Kigali , the world witnessed the amendment of Montreal Protocol to curb global warming hydrofluorocarbons ( HFCs ) in largest climate breakthrough since Paris . <p> HFCs substances which are used mainly in refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment , have a global warming effect up to 15,000 times greater than carbon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , according to climate change experts . <p> The small central African country has already ratified the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> Biruta said that Rwanda is committed to any effort geared towards addressing climate change disastrous effects . <p> The ratification of amendment to the protocol will signify the international community 's commitment to practical action to achieve the goals of the Paris Agreement -- limiting global warming to 2 ? C , and the more ambitious target of 1.5 ? C. <p> The Kigali Amendment is the result of seven years of negotiation . Under the amendment , developed countries will start to phase down HFCs by 2019 while developing countries will freeze their levels of consumption in 2024 , with some starting the freeze in 2028 . <p> According to the United Nations Environment Program ( UNEP ) , HFC emissions are growing at a rate of about 7 percent annually . If the current mix of HFCs is unchanged , increasing demand could result in HFC emissions of up to 8.8 gigatons of CO2 equivalent per year by 2050 . <p> Montreal Protocol @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> It was first signed on September 16 , 1987 , and is widely considered to be one of the most effective multilateral environment treaties ever negotiated .
