<h> TechnoServe to revive fortunes of agric sector <p> For decades , Ghana 's agricultural sector has suffered low investments , minimal technological infusion and general neglect , resulting in marginal growth and contribution to national output . <p> From 30.4 per cent in 2006 , the sector 's contribution to total economic output , measured by gross domestic product ( GDP ) , has declined to 20.2 per cent in 2015 . The drop in the sector 's contribution to GDP can be traced to the steady reduction in its growth rates over the years . <p> After ending 2008 at 7.4 per cent , growth in agriculture resumed a downward trend ; dropping to 5.3 per cent in 2010 before peaking at 5.7 per cent in 2013 . <p> Last year , growth in the sector dropped from 4.6 per cent in 2014 to 2.5 per cent , raising questions over the impact of various interventions aimed at lifting it . <p> To help reverse the trend , various institutions and stakeholders have come out with a number of initiatives that are aimed at inspiring farmers and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of those is TechnoServe Ghana 's dialogue on agricultural mechanisation .
