<h> Paying the price of over-development ? <p> 9 November 2016 @ 11:00 AM <p> FLOODS are occurring in Penang in areas not known to be flood prone before and the past 10 days or so have witnessed floods come and go , leaving residents cleaning up the mud left behind in their homes every other day . Waist-high flash floods have meant that some children are going to school for the Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia examinations in boats . People are late for work as floodwaters and landslides close off roads . These are the people 's grouses . The once peaceful " Pearl of the Orient " may yet drown in the combined effects of over-development and climate change . A DAP-led state , Penang is where politicians are finger-pointing at the expense of a population suffering massive disruptions . <p> The state government has been slow to use its funds for flood mitigation , but has been forced to do so recently . Some RM650,000 has been allocated for immediate works , but its looking more like Band-Aid solutions than long term .
