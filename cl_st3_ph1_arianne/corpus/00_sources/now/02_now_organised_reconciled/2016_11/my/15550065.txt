<h> NGOs Raise Climate Change Risk <p> At a conference on the topic of the environment yesterday , civil society groups repeatedly raised the risk of natural disasters and hazards that Cambodia will face if the effects of climate change are not adequately addressed . <p> The one-day conference in Phnom Penh sought to find solutions to the issues that climate change will bring to Cambodia 's cities , such as urban migration and disaster relief . <p> Tep Sokha , program manager for Czech NGO People in Need , warned that Phnom Penh will face increasing challenges in the future , including floods , fires , pollution and lightning strikes , and plans to mitigate against these must be drawn up now . <p> Acknowledging that a national strategic plan on disaster risk reduction exists , he said the growing hazards had not received enough attention and planning . <p> Fiona Ramsey , charge d'affaires at the EU embassy , spoke of the need to address urban development concerns that often ignored the needs and rights of the poorest citizens . She called for better land usage planning @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " We know that it 's complicated , but I would like to encourage national authorities , not only in the cities but in other place , to be responsible in facilitating development and creating partnerships to address the challenges of urban development , " she told attendees . <p> Phnom Penh Deputy Governor Eang Ony acknowledged that Phnom Penh faces a number of problems that must be solved urgently , blaming the forced evacuation of the city under the Khmer Rouge in 1975 , and subsequent four years of near abandonment , on a lack of trained urban planners . <p> He stressed that City Hall was aware of the problems and was taking action to ensure the best possible development of Phnom Penh in a sustainable way .
