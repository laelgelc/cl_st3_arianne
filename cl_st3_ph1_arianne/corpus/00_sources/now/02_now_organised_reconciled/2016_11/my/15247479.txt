<h> Climate Summit Starts in Marrakesh <p> Almost one year after the adoption of the UN Paris Agreement on Climate Change , the 22nd Conference of the Parties ( COP ) to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change will meet in Morocco today to craft policies that will slow the rise of the world 's average temperature and implement a plan that will lower greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions globally . <p> Cambodia is one of more than 180 countries to sign the historic climate change agreement since December 2015 -- although the Kingdom has not yet ratified it . <p> The effects of climate change around the world have become more severe over time , wreaking havoc on communities and economies .
