<h> Subscribe to industry newsletters <h> Access to trade and information is of great interest to the agricultural community <p> An interesting upcoming development to hit the African audience is the Continental Free Trade Area ( CFTA ) to be launched in 2017 . The main objectives of the CFTA are to create a single continental market for goods and services with free movement of business persons and investments , and thus pave the way for accelerating the establishment of the Customs Union . It will also expand intra-African trade through better harmonisation and coordination of trade liberalisation and facilitation instruments across the RECs and across Africa in general . The CFTA is also expected to enhance competitiveness at the industry and enterprise level through the exploitation of opportunities for scale production , continental market access and better reallocation of resources . <p> Peter Draper , managing director at Tutwa Consulting Group says the group is working with the Southern African Customs Union on negotiations for the CFTA .
