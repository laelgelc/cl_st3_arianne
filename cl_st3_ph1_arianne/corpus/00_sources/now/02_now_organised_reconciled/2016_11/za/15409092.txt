<h> SA movie receives special mention at Film4Climate award ceremony <p> Connect4Climate has announced the winners of the Film4Climate Global Video Competition . <p> The winners were selected by renowned filmmakers , climate influencers , world leaders , and communication professionals , headed by the Italian Film Director , Bernardo Bertolluci . <p> The were announced at the United Nations Climate Conference ( COP22 ) in Marrakech , in a Ceremony that celebrated the power of youth , cinema and the creative industries in generating climate action and tackling climate change . <p> Winners are from USA , Kiribati , Finland , France , India , Brazil , Morocco and Greece . Videos from Laos , Mexico , Kenya , India and South Africa 's " Beatrice " by Sydelle Willow Smith received a special mentions . <p> Program Manager of Connect4Climate , World Bank Group , Lucia Grenna says , " We are amazed by the quality and emotions of the videos . There are climate stories from every corner of the world , highlighting solutions and calling for action .
