<h> Orange signifies hope for VAW <p> NEGROS OCCIDENTAL , Nov. 22 ( PIA6 ) -- The Provincial Council for Women ( PCW ) here will be using the color orange to signify " hope " during the 18-day Campaign to End Violence Against Women ( VAW ) <p> Marie June Pavillar-Castro , current adviser and former chair of PCW -- Neg . Occ. , said they chose orange because it symbolizes dawn , which represents hope . <p> " The PCW , with the women councils of the different local government units ( LGU ) of the province , will be using orange ribbons and we will be adopting trees which will have signs promoting the end of VAW , " she said in a radio interview with PIA . <p> The signs will also contain the theme for this year 's campaign : " VAW-free Community Starts with Me . " <p> The campaign will start on November 25 and will last until December 12 . <p> On the national level , the Philippine Commission on Women has partnered with the Department of Tourism ( DOT ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ end VAW , according to Pavillar-Castro . <p> Famous landmarks all over the country will be filled with orange objects to make people curious and for them to be aware of the 18-day campaign . <p> According to Pavillar-Castro , some of the major cities have already made a commitment , and some of the landmarks include the Miag-ao Church in Iloilo , the Hundred Islands in Pangasinan , the San Juanico Bridge and the Cebu Provincial Capitol . <p> She also urged restaurants and establishments here to use orange as a sign of support to the campaign . <p> " Establishments and restaurants can also join the advocacy to ensure the safety of clients from sexual and other forms of harassment , " she said .
