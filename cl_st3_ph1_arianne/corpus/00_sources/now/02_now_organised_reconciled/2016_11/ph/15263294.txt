<h> Lift the cloak of invisibility <p> Soon after super-typhoon Yolanda ( international name : Haiyan ) struck the country , the good people of Eastern Visayas were excluded from the government 's official counts of employment , underemployment , and unemployment . <p> It was one of the less-glaring mistakes of the past administration , but with no less profound results . For by refusing to include Eastern Visayas from official job-related surveys , it deprived the nation of an insight into the region 's pace of recovery . And the the people of Eastern Visayas themselves a measurement of how they are faring and of what still needed to be done . <p> If we remember , results of official job-related surveys were a favorite of the past administration as proof of vaunted economic growth . Too bad for all of us and the people of Eastern Visayas , the statistics were fixed to exclude what we presumed were included .
