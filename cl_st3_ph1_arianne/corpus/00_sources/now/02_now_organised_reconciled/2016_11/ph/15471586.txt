Council ( ABAC ) Dialogue with Leaders between the grouping 's 21 leaders and 63 CEOs from the world 's top companies at the Lima Convention Center . <p> This year 's APEC Chair , Peruvian President Pedro Pablo Kuczynski , is expected to deliver the welcome remarks . <p> The ABAC dialogue will be followed by the breakout sessions where leaders will discuss a wide range of topics under various themes , such as taking Asia Pacific to the next level of economic integration ; growing global Micro , Small and Medium Enterprises ( MSMEs ) ; building larger , robust inclusive financial markets ;
