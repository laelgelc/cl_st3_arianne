<h> Duterte changes mind , to sign climate change pact <h> Kimberly Jane Tan , ABS-CBN News <p> President Rodrigo Duterte changed his mind Monday , saying he will now allow the country 's ratification of the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> Duterte said he made the decision after consulting his Cabinet , who were mostly in favor of the ratification . <p> " Ako naman it 's a Cabinet decision . I will go along with it , I will sign it , " he said at a Palace press conference . <p> It 's a Cabinet decision so I will go along with it , I will sign it . <p> But the President said that if the country ratifies the agreement , there should be an assurance of the clauses being enforced by all countries . <p> In Paris last December , nearly 200 countries -- including the Philippines under then President Benigno Aquino III -- agreed on a binding global compact to slash greenhouse gases and keep global temperature increases to " well below " 2 degrees Celsius . <p> Manila promised to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ if the country is not a major emitter . <p> Wealthy countries were also asked to set aside at least $100 billion yearly as financial assistance to developing countries to enable all countries to actualize renewable energy sources starting 2020 . <p> Duterte had expressed disdain for the pact , saying cutting carbon emissions at a point when the Philippines ' economic rise is just starting will be unfair since industrialized countries have been giving off such emissions for decades .
