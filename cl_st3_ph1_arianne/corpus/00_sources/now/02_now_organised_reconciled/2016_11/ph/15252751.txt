<h> Quirino eyes IADP expansion <p> CABARROGUIS , Quirino , Nov. 7 ( PIA ) -- The provincial government here has expressed intention to expand the Quirino Integrated Agricultural Development Program ( QIADP ) to serve the remaining 25 per cent farmers who are living below the poverty line . <p> The QIADP has been carried out here from 2013-2016 by the Korea Institute for Rural Development ( KIRD ) through a grant of US$5 million from the Korean government 's Korea International Cooperation Agency ( KOICA ) and the province 's counterpart of P3 million . <p> In his presentation of the results and lessons learned during the implementation of the project , Gov . Junie E. Cua said there is a need to lay the foundation of further intervention so that the gains made in the activities will be maximized . <p> He said of the 40,000 farming households in the province , 10,000 are living below the poverty line and only 95 of them were served by QIADP Phase 1 and 3,500 by the Q-Life . <p> " We will replicate it in the remaining 6,400 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Cua added . <p> Furthermore , Cua stressed the need to sustain the environmental protection under the agro-forestry component through expansion of integrated farming system ( rice , corn , cattle , fishpond , vegetable , fruit and forest trees production ) . <p> " The province is located very strategically as a head source of a long river system , the Cagayan River that is why it is necessary to protect the environment . Anything wrong will affect the whole valley , " Cua said . <p> To sustain the QIADP Phase 2 , the project beneficiaries will be organized into cooperatives so that they will have access to bank loans .
