<h> Opinion <h> Safeguarding our democratic space <h> Grassroots &amp; Governance Teresa S. Abesamis <p> Posted on November 09 , 2016 <h> President Rodrigo Roa Duterte has openly announced that his idol is the dictator and former KGB head Vladimir Putin of Russia . He has also said that Ferdinand Marcos was a great leader . He is also making moves toward the People 's Republic of China , a largely totalitarian state , which has disallowed duly elected representatives of the people of Hong Kong from taking their seats in Parliament . It is obvious that if left unopposed by a loud enough public opinion , and enough assertive opposition leaders , and an adequately free and open media , our country is in danger of losing much of the democratic space it has taken much sacrifice and many heroic lives to recover , following years of the Marcos totalitarianism . <p> During the first months of the Duterte Administration , there have been alarming manifestations of the new President 's dictatorial tendencies . <p> Well , it seems this is how he governed Davao City ; and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ problem in the city then was peace and order ; and his draconian measures seem to have succeeded . However , he can not expect to apply the same paradigm to the country , which has to contend with its dynamic global context , and a multicultural , archipelagic constituency with complex challenges . We can take the longer route of open and participative governance ; or allow this President to take authoritarian and bloody shortcuts . <p> At the outset of the current administration , I was very impressed with many of the President 's choices for his Cabinet : notably Economic Planning Secretary Ernesto Pernia , Finance Secretary Carlos Dominguez III , Peace Process Facilitator Jesus Dureza , Transportation Secretary Arturo Tugade , and Gina Lopez . <p> However , there seems to be a streak of cruelty and even sadism in our newly installed national leader given his public statements , and some of his public behavior . <p> Whether he denies it or not , he does encourage our police forces to " kill , kill , kill . " In fact , he has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the law . He has furthermore announced generous bounties for executions of drug lords , pushers , and users . I hear stories about media reporters being threatened on their phones for stories they have written about some of the killings . <p> We have witnessed on television the incredible extent to which the President goes , with the cooperation of some of his minions , to destroy the reputation of Senator Leila de Lima for her work years ago as Chairman of the Human Rights Commission when she investigated and criticized then Mayor Rodrigo Duterte for the alleged Davao Death Squat summary killings . <p> Thank God , Senator Leila de Lima has taken the bold step of filing a landmark case for habeas data against Rodrigo Roa Duterte as a private citizen as a test case in the Supreme Court . Bless your heart Dean Jose Manuel Diokno of De La Salle for taking on the challenge . Your father , the great Senator Jose Diokno , who could have been a great president , is one of our truly brave heroes who along with the late @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ dictatorship and never gave in . <p> Thank you , former President Noynoy Aquino , for appearing at the rally opposing the Marcos burial at the Libingan ng mga Bayani , and for making a statement that such an act would be a desecration of that hallowed ground . <p> Little by little , public opinion is mobilizing and asserting itself , despite the huge popularity based on surveys , of the current President . <p> The administration has launched a movement called Kilusang Pagbabago which reminds me of Kilusang Bagong Lipunan . It seems to be the brainchild of Cabinet Secretary Leoncio Evasco , Jr. , a former priest and communist who became the campaign manager of Du30 in the presidential election . It has been demonstrated that Evasco is an effective mobilize ; so this development bears watching . <p> Meanwhile , let us not underestimate this initiative . <p> It is a good thing that the order for powerful guns to be supplied to the police has been canceled . President Duterte has announced that the money intended for the purchase will be redirected to development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ democratic activists must not let their guards down . <p> As in the case of the Marcos dictatorship , it takes a lot of work , and years of perseverance to strengthen and safeguard our democratic space . Let us mobilize public opinion to help the good guys in the current government get their voices heard ; so that their leader will lean toward their point of view , rather than just his own . <p> It is not impossible . The President has backtracked on his hard line versus signing the Climate Change Pact , because the majority in his Cabinet advocated the signing , even though he says he does not agree with it . <p> The President has changed his mind many times ( Kilusang Pabagobago , as a friend calls it ) . Public opinion , if asserted loudly and broadly enough , can pressure his Cabinet , many of whom thank heavens , are finally asserting themselves . <p> Media which has to be the public 's ally must continue to protect its freedom . We can not , and must not let our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ complacency .
