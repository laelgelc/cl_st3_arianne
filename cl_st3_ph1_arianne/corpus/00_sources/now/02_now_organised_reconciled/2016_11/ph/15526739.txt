<h> 2 Benguet towns formulate climate change action plan <h> 2 Benguet towns formulate climate change action plan <p> Wednesday , November 23 , 2016 <p> LA TRINIDAD , Benguet -- The towns of Kabayan and Bokod are pilot areas in the formulation of their own Local Climate Change Action Plan ( LCCAP ) that will harmonize indigenous peoples ' ( IPs ) practices in the existing Ancestral Domain Sustainable Development and Protection Plan ( ADSPP ) . <p> The ADSDPP will serve as the main reference material of the LCCAP for IPs ' concerns . LCCAP is the blueprint in the climate change mitigation and adaptation of said towns , said Green Philippines National chair Elias Labro Jr . <p> During the 2nd Benguet Cultural Communities Conference recently , Labro suggested that aside from harmonization with the ADSDPP , a resiliency framework should serve as guide in the formulation of the LCCAP embracing IP concerns . <p> These concerns include respect to some bundle of rights of IP ; respect for culture and tradition ; economic activity for the IP in connection with ecological preservations , among others @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Green PH Chair for Luzon , said their group is extending technical assistance to the piloted LGUs in the formulation of LCCAP that will likewise incorporate gender advocacy programs . <p> Kabayan Mayor Gideon Todiano said his municipality together with the LGU of Bokod committed to formulate its own LCCAP as guide in carrying out programs to lessen the impact of disaster and come up with climate change adaptation and mitigation measures . <p> The mayor bared they have allotted P1 million as counterpart in the ongoing preparations for plan formulation which involves the community through series of consultations and meetings adding they have to work double time for its completion within the year . <p> Both towns can access budget under the P2 billion People 's Survival Fund after complying with the plan .
