But when popular leaders who are able to consolidate power -- like the late President Marcos and now President Duterte -- emerge , the dynamics change . Such is happening today when as early as the first few months of his administration , we are already seeing a Congress dominated by a super-majority . <p> Recently , this concern was expressed by Ifugao Rep. Teddy Baguilat in an interview with Lynda Jumilla when he noted that he and his seven fellow Liberals had decided to become the " critical voices " in a government with a " lean opposition .
