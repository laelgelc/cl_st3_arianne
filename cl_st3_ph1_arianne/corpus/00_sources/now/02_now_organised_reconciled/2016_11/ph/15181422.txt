<h> New Miss PH Earth ready to replace Imelda Schweighart <h> ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA - There 's a new Miss Philippines Earth following the resignation of this year 's title-holder . <p> Imelda Schweighart stepped down a day after she was caught on video accusing newly crowned Miss Earth Katherine Espin of undergoing cosmetic surgery . She has since apologized for her comments , but claimed that she was only telling the truth . <p> She was replaced by Loren Mar Artajos , who is set to fulfill her remaining obligations . <p> " It is with great honor that I accept this new opportunity given to me by Carousel Productions , " said Artajos in a statement . " I am deeply humbled by the overwhelming support that my family , friends , and supporters is giving me right now . " <p> " This new endeavor is truly challenging but with all the support that I am getting . I am sure that this will be easy and worthwhile . I am ready to take on the responsibilities of being the new Miss Philippines Earth with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ love for Mother Earth , we will make a difference , we will make a change . Thank you everyone for all your love and genuine support , " she added . <p> Artajos , a nursing graduate from Laoag City , was named Miss Philippines Water in June . <p> According to her Miss Philippines Earth profile , she wants to be an " advocate of the environment , spread awareness of the current climate change issue the world is facing today and implement measures to mitigate global warming . " <p> A controversial reign <p> There were several controversial incidents during the reign of Schweighart . She likened President Rodrigo Duterte to Nazi leader Adolf Hitler in front of fellow contestant Miss Austria ; got into a heated exchange with a former beauty queen ; and lamented publicly that the gown intended for her was given to Espin . <p> But the final straw that pushed her to tender her resignation appeared to be the backlash that Schweighart , who failed to qualify for the top 16 of the Miss Earth 2016 pageant , received after she was caught on video publicly bashing the newly crowned Espin.
