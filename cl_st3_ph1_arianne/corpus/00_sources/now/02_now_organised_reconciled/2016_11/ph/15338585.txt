<h> Economy <h> BoI eyes to complete new IPP by yearend <p> Posted on November 12 , 2016 <h> THE Board of Investments ( BoI ) is slated to complete by yearend the Investments Priorities Plan ( IPP ) 2017-2019 , which will expand priority areas in line with the Duterte administration 's 10-point economic agenda . <p> " The target date of submission is considered as milestone undertaking for the agency , with the list already with the President before the end of the year , " read the statement of the country 's lead investment promotion agency in an e-mail to reporters on Friday , referring to President Rodrigo R. Duterte . <p> The three-year IPP , which will be implemented starting next year , is a list of priority investment activities geared towards providing fiscal support to inclusive business projects that benefit the micro , small , and medium enterprises ( MSMEs ) . <p> The priorities will also be guided by the Comprehensive National Industrial Strategy . <p> The preferred activities for 2017 may include manufacturing , agribusiness , and services as major areas for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ infrastructure and logistics , the BoI said . <p> Environment or climate change-related projects are also being proposed for inclusion . <p> The IPP 2014-2016 focuses on eight preferred activities : manufacturing , agribusiness and fisheries , services , economic and low-cost housing , hospitals , energy , public infrastructure and logistics , and projects under the public-private partnership program . <p> In March this year , the BoI started crafting the new IPP , with a tecnical technical working group working on the plan 's policy directions , priorities and timelines . <p> Upon clearance by the BoI Board , the draft IPP will be presented in multi-sectoral consultations in Metro Manila , La Union , Laguna , Cebu , Iloilo , Zamboanga , Davao , and Cagayan de Oro to gather comments and feedback from stakeholders , according to the statement . <p> All comments and inputs gathered will be processed and presented to the BoI Board for decision and finalization of the IPP list for endorsement by the Trade Secretary to the president . <p> Earlier , the BoI revealed plans to use the IPP to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agriculture . <p> The IPP currently in place provides incentives only to areas in the Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao , Mindoro , and Palawan for businesses engaged in the production of poultry and livestock . <p> Apart from spreading out development , BoI is also considering to increase the capacity of establishments under the tourism sector . -- Janina C.
