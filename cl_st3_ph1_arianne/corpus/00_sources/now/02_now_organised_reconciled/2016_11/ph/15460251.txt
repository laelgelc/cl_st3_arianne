<h> Villar : 2017 agri budget to level the playing field <p> The P50.5-billion proposed budget of the Department of Agriculture ( DA ) for year 2017 was crafted to address the barriers that hinder the competitiveness of local farmers and fisherfolk , Senator Cynthia Villar said yesterday . <p> Villar , vice chairperson of the Senate Committee on Agriculture and Food , presided over the budget hearing of DA and its attached agencies under Finance Subcommittee " E " , and was tasked to defend the budget in plenary . <p> " I am pleased to say that we have high expectations that the budget of DA next year will address the barriers that hinder Filipino farmers and fisherfolks from being more competitive and to increase their income , " Villar said . <p> " We have taken into consideration that the agriculture sector has had to contend with tougher competition with the opening or reintegration to the ASEAN Economic Community ( AEC ) since last year and on top of that , climate change issues , " she added . <p> Villar said studies have identified the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ technical expertise , inadequate access to socialized credit , lack of mechanization and financial literacy . <p> " Thus , the objective of the 2017 budget of the DA is to increase competitiveness of farmers and fisherfolks by bringing down production cost and mechanization , " Villar said . <p> The lady senator said the proposed budget allocated P2 billion for free irrigation fees , provisions for farm mechanization through the shared facilities program , and improvement of farmers ' technology , among others . <p> The Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation budget nearly doubled from this year 's P1.6 billion to P2.5 billion to fund the subsidy for insurance premium of subsistence farmers and fisherfolks . <p> Villar also stressed that the proposed budget for next year includes funding for recently enacted laws , which she authored . This will enable the laws to deliver on its promised benefits to the intended beneficiaries in the agriculture sector .
