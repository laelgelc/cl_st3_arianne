Codamon <p> Feature Story : <p> LAGAWE , Ifugao , Nov.16 ( PIA ) - - The Alternative Learning System ( ALS ) is an important component of the Philippine Educational system to achieve quality and access to education as envisioned in the Education For All ( EFA ) 2015 Philippine Plan of Action specifically on the reduction of non-literacy rate . <p> The Bureau of Alternative Learning System ( BALS ) of the Department of Education ( DEPED ) is mandated to address the basic education needs of the Out-of-School Children ( OSC ) , Out-of-School Youth ( OSY ) and adults in the far-flung and isolated communities of the country as provided under Republic Act 9155 otherwise known as the Governance of Basic Education . <p> The Schools Division of Ifugao implements two major programs of the ALS under the DepEd namely the Basic Literacy Program ( BLP ) and the Accreditation and Equivalency Program ( A&amp;E ) ' It also includes special or pilot programs like the eSkwela @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ LGU ) funded classes in the different municipalities or Districts of the province . <p> Implementation of these programs is guided by the vision to be the best producer of lifelong learners among the Ifugaos and the mission of developing exemplary programs and open creative learning opportunities to achieve multiple literacy for all in partnership with other producers of learning . <p> ALS-Ifugao Coordinator Arsenio Yongoyong reported that for this first semester of 2016 , there are 2,969 total number of learners enrolled under the regular programs of which 2,182 of them registered for the examinations to be conducted this 2016 . <p> Presently , there are 15 ALS District in the entire province with a total number of 55 implementers of the various programs and 108 Learning Centers distributed among the different districts in the 11 municipalities of the province . The municipalities of Aguinaldo , Alfonso Lista , Banaue and Lagawe have two ALS districts each . <p> Learning Centers are being conducted in barangay halls , churches , training centers , private houses and even under the trees . <p> In the implementation of the ALS @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ relative to the successful implementation of their programs and projects in certain districts brought by the collaborative efforts between the implementers , stakeholders and the local government units . <p> They reported that Alfonso Lista allotted the amount of P480,000.00 for honorarium and supplies of their mobile teachers . Municipality of Aguinaldo submitted project proposal to the municipal government also for fund allocation in support of the ALS . <p> Asipulo provided information , communication technology gadgets like laptops and computers to the ALS . Banaue allocated funds and materials for ALS activities including the preparation of project proposals and coordination with TESDA for funding while Hingyon allocated the amount of P40,000.00 yearly for honoraria of facilitators . <p> Hungduan also allotted P20 , 000.00 for the transportation of test takers and P40 , 000.00 for honoraria of facilitators , Kiangan rendered technical assistance in sports and paper related activities , Tinoc funded 2 facilitators for Curriculum Year 2016-2017 while Lagawe and Lamut continue to make linkage to partners for ALS funding . <p> The ALS implementers of Ifugao also related and listed the issues and concerns they encountered @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to test postponement by the Central Office that affect the percentage of test takers and passers , absenteeism and lack of commitment among the learners due to varied reasons , most learners are discouraged since the ALS implementers can not give valid reasons for the test postponement , climate change affected the attendance of learners , lack of facilities and learning materials in some learning centers , no permanent learning center to safe keep records and insufficient modules from the Central Office . <p> ALS Provincial Coordinator Yongoyong informed that they already crafted their future plans for the next quarter of this year that involve the conduct of more linkaging to non-supportive stakeholders , submission of proposals and requests for the institutionalization of ALS fund in the local budget , conduct of literacy mapping to identify ALS learners or beneficiaries , conduct livelihood skills training for personal and community development . <p> ALS implementers will also attend trainings for personal and professional upliftment and the development of localized supplementary learning materials suited to the learner 's needs .
