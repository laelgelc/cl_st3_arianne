<h> White Quirino Hill in the works <h> White Quirino Hill in the works <p> CONSENT of barangays within the Quirino Hill project is now being consulted for the planned painting of homes in a bid to fight climate change . <p> The Department of Tourism and the local government have embarked on the Qurino Hill project aiming to paint over 6,000 homes white with splashes of green . <p> The local government is leading talks with residents of the area and awaiting public approval . <p> In partnership with Boysen Paints , the project is seen to be a response to climate change saying studies show an all-white paint motif has been found to have a cooling effect and thus an effective strategy to combat the changes in weather . <p> DOT regional director Venus Tan said the effect of the white paint will make the mountainside homes look cooler as well as have a cooling effect on those living and looking at the masterpiece . <p> The project is under the DOT 's REV Campaign launched months ago geared towards a re-bloom , re-green and rehabilitation which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ program is a collaboration and synergy of government agencies , local government units and academic institutions , private sector groups and members of the community . <p> Dubbed as " Project Puraw ( White ) " , Mayor Mauricio Domogan said the proposed endeavor entails the painting of the roofs and walls of thousands of houses in the area that encompasses four barangays . " I was informed that the kind of paints and colors chosen will help mitigate climate change as these will not absorb sunlight but reflect it back to the atmosphere , " Domogan said . <p> The mayor is also hoping the DOT regional office will be able to get the signed approval of the Quirino Hill homeowners so the project can commence as soon as possible .
