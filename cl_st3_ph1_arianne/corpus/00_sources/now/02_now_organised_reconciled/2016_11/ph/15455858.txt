<h> BRM launches radio program on City Environment Code <p> November 18 , 2016 <p> BAGUIO CITY , Nov. 18 ( PIA ) - - The Baguio Regreening Movement ( BRM ) in partnership with Radyo ng Bayan-Baguio launched on Thursday ( Nov. 17 ) a radio program to tackle the provisions of the recently passed City Environment Code . <p> Dubbed " Talakayan sa Environment Code " , the program is aired over DZEQ Radyo ng Bayan from 8am-9am every Thursdas and hosted by Station Manager Mr. Andre Amadeo . <p> The City Environment Code , which had long been pending at the city council , was finally approved on August 10 , 2016 . <p> For effective implementation of the law , the public should know and understand its contents that protect the city 's environment " because whether we like it or not , we need the environment ; environment is Life " and without the environment , nothing can exist on earth " , BRM Chairperson Atty . Erdolfo Balajadia said . <p> The program will be a venue for invited guests , the public @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Environment Code . Listeners can also phone in their questions or comments or send their questions thru SMS to the Radyo ng Bayan hotline which will be read by the anchor during the program . <p> Balajadia narrated how the environment code came about in the program 's initial airing . The draft environment code has been pending before the city council since 2005 and has undergone several revisions in order to reflect current realities and standards . Until he finished his third term as city councilor in 2013 , the code was passed . With the assumption of new set of officers , he urged councilor Fred Bagbagen , the committee chair on environment , to enroll it and it underwent many discussions . The BRM members also talked with members of the city council . It was finally endorsed to the Office of the City Mayor and was approved on August 10 , 2016 . <p> Other panelist during the launching include City Budget Officer Atty .
