<h> Hope springs for Ryan Reynolds <p> As Deadpool , he fights villains and megalomaniacs on the big screen . But Ryan Reynolds is fighting a different kind of baddie -- the environmental kind , like climate change -- when he is n't performing in front of the cameras . On Instagram , the 40-year-old actor posted his picture with the tree he planted last year . He wrote , " A year ago , this little sprout was the 50th millionth tree @EddieBauer and @AmericanForests planted . <p> " This year , we embarked on another partnership to work toward the next 50 million , planting another 280,000 through generous customer donations . Happy birthday , buddy ! #onetree . " <p> Ryan was later quoted , " The outdoors have always been a big part of my life , and I hope to help preserve our forests for future generations , one tree at a time . " -- Rito Asilo
