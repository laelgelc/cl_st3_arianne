<h> Peru advances food security agenda as APEC meet host <p> LIMA , Peru -- Food security has been selected as one of the priority areas in this year 's APEC Meetings here to address a global population explosion that is projected to hit 9.6 billion by 2050 requiring a 60 percent increase in the current food that that the world currently produces . <p> " One of our priorities this year is strengthening the regional food market , " said Peru 's Minister of Agriculture and Irrigation Jose Manuel Hernandez , who is chair of the 2016 APEC Food Security Ministerial Meeting . <p> " In addition to stimulating long-term and inclusive economic growth in the Asia Pacific , enhancing APEC food systems and achieving our food security objectives and the Bogor Goals will be APEC 's contribution to the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development , " Hernandez said . <p> In September this year , 21 APEC Ministers recognized the key role that food security plays in achieving quality growth and prosperity in the region as they issued a statement that sets clear steps forward to enhance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to production . <p> The Piura Declaration on Food Security gives policy recommendations for the APEC Economic Leaders to consider during their annual meeting this week here . <p> Specifically , APEC Ministers described collaboration points to enhance regional food markets and trade ; develop sustainable and resilient food systems ; foster cooperation for the sustainable use and integrated management of water resources ; encourage innovation and technology ; fast-track rural-urban development and establish infrastructure , investment and services for food security . <p> In a statement posted on APEC website , Hernandez cited the increasingly harsh effects of climate change , diminishing natural resources , as well as gaps in access to services between rural and urban populations are hampering APEC economies ' efforts to achieve food security . <p> " This puts us in a crucial time to address the specific problems of the food production sector , " Hernandez said . <p> Across the region , agriculture is undergoing changes that impact its ability to produce food as it is demanded by the population .
