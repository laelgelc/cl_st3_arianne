<h> 200 companies committed to reduce emission <h> The Science Based Targets initiative announced that 200 companies have committed themselves to setting emission reduction targets consistent with the global effort to keep temperatures well below the 2-degree threshold , just 18 months after its launch <p> In the past year , the initiative saw a growth rate of over 2 companies per week committing to setting science-based targets , far exceeding the project 's time line and signaling that climate action had gained tremendous momentum in the private sector . <p> These 200 businesses represent an estimated USD $4.8 trillion in market value , comparable to the total estimated value of the Tokyo stock exchange , and are responsible for 627 million metric tonnes of CO2 equivalent emissions per year , roughly equal to the annual emissions of South Korea . <p> The Science Based Targets initiative is a partnership between CDP , UN Global Compact , WRI and WWF , which helps companies determine how much they must cut emissions to prevent the worst impacts of climate change . <p> Of the 200 companies , 26 have had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cola Enterprises , Dell , General Mills , Kellogg , NRG Energy , Procter &amp; Gamble , Sony , Thalys and other leading companies . <p> " Walmart is the first retailer with an emissions-reduction plan approved by the Science Based Targets initiative , in alignment with the Paris Agreement in December 2015 , " said Doug McMillon , president and CEO of Walmart . <p> " We want to make sure Walmart is a company that our associates and customers are proud of -- and that we are always doing right by them and by the communities they live in .
