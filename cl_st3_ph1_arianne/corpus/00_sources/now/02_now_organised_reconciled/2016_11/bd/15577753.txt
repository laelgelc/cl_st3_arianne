<h> Call to save rivers of Bangladesh <p> on November 25 , 2016 <p> businessnews24bd.com <p> The government must take effective steps to save the country 's rivers from grabbing and polluting for the sake of the environment and people 's livelihood , said speakers at a national convention on saving the rivers today . <p> It is the government 's prime duty to save the rivers by taking legal actions against the grabbers and polluters , they added . <p> The discussants also laid emphasis on including the people in a massive awareness campaign for saving the rivers . <p> They made these remarks at National River Convention-2016 organised by Bangladesh Nodi Bachao Andolan ( Save the River Movement , Bangladesh ) ; a green group which brought their representatives from 45 districts at Supreme Court Bar Association in the city . <p> " It is really beyond our thinking that people have to call for a movement to save a country 's rivers . Now , the influential are grabbing our rivers by constructing industrial buildings and housing illegally .
