<h> Seabird Poop May Be Fighting Global Warming : Audubon Society <p> November 27 , 2016 6:56 amLast Updated:November 27 , 2016 12:18 pm <p> Newfoundland and Labrador seabirds could be playing an interesting and unexpected role in keeping polar temperatures cool . <p> The Audubon Society has published an article outlining research in Nature Communications about the role ammonia in seabird poop plays in creating low-lying clouds that reflect sunlight and keep Arctic waters cool . <p> According to the article , researchers found what is being called " strong evidence " that ammonia bursts in the Arctic were tied to tens of millions of seabirds that migrate north in the summer . <p> The researchers caution however that the effects are not nearly enough to counter the results of climate change , but they are gaining a better understanding of the relationship between seabirds and the atmosphere .
