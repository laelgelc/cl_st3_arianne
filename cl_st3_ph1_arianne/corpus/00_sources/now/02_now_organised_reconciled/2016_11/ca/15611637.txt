<p> The Nanos Research survey found hydro was the most important issue for 20.5 per cent of voters , eclipsing perennial priorities health care ( 15.1 per cent ) , jobs and the economy ( 9.6 per cent ) and high taxes ( 7.3 per cent ) . Respondents were asked for their top issues without being given a list of options . <p> Such a result is " unusual , " Nanos Research chair Nik Nanos said . Typically , pocketbook matters such as electricity are " second-tier " concerns behind larger issues , such as the economy . <p> " Hydro rates right now are the political lightning rod , " Mr. Nanos said in an interview . " They are on the radar for everyone . " <p> The poll of 500 Ontarians was conducted from Nov. 15 to 19 , using random-digit dialling of both mobile phones and land lines , with live interviews .
