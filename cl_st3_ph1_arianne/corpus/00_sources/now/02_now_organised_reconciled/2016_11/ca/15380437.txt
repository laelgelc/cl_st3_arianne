<p> In her more than 25 years of working with the Regional Health Authority ( RHA ) , Dr. Shelley Buchan has seen a lot of change . <p> Buchan recently retired from her position as Medical Officer of Health for Southern Health . She says the biggest change she 's noticed over the years in the region is that we 're living longer . Buchan says children born today compared to those born 25 years ago will live 2-3 years longer . <p> " That 's an improvement in health when you can see that life expectancy increasing , " Dr. Buchan says . <p> Community health assessments also began over Buchan 's tenure , allowing the RHA to gather more information about public health in smaller areas in the region . <p> " Although there is a general increase in health , living longer , we do see pockets within our region that are n't " Buchan explains .
