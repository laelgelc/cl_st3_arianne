<p> With the distraction of Brexit and Donald Trump , you might have missed that a country of more than 93 million people nearly went bankrupt this year . The International Monetary Fund stopped that from happening , announcing on Nov. 11 that it would lend Egypt the equivalent of about $16 billion . Brexiteers and Trump 's army of angry old white men might disagree , but the rescue of Egypt was evidence that globalization works . The IMF was created at the end of the Second World War to help make the planet a less chaotic place . The fund in 2016 prevented a dangerous region from becoming even more so . <p> But there is something else about that loan .
