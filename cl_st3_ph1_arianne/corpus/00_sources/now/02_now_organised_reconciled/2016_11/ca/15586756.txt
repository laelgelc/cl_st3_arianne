<p> OTTAWA -- Canada will shutter its coal-fired power plants by 2030 as part of its strategy to cut greenhouse gas emission under the Paris climate accord , Environment Minister Catherine McKenna announced Monday . <p> The plants , located in four provinces , produce about 10 percent of Canada 's total CO2 emissions , and closing them will remove the equivalent in emissions of 1.3 million cars from roads , or five megatons of greenhouse gas emissions , she told a press conference . <p> " As part of our government 's vision for a clean growth economy , we will be accelerating the transition from traditional coal power to clean energy by 2030 , " she said . <p> With an abundance of hydroelectric power , as well as nuclear , solar and wind power , 80 percent of Canada 's electricity production emits no air pollution . <p> McKenna said she aims to ramp that up to 90 percent by 2030 .
