<p> Documentary filmmaker , former CBC television host and social activist Avi Lewis , co-author of the controversial Leap Manifesto , is one of the guest speakers who will address the Economics Society of Northern Alberta 's upcoming 2017 economic outlook conference . <p> Lewis , the son of politician and diplomat Stephen Lewis and the husband of high-profile activist and bestselling author Naomi Klein , will deliver the afternoon keynote address at the daylong conference , which is slated for Dec. 8 at Edmonton 's Chateau Lacombe Hotel . <p> Lewis created a furor in April at the federal New Democratic Party 's national convention in Edmonton , where party members agreed to consider the anti-capitalist manifesto as policy . The document , which calls for an accelerated end to all fossil fuel development , drew harsh criticism from Alberta Premier Rachel Notley and Alberta Federation of Labour president Gil McGowan . <p> " New Democrats in Alberta are not people like Avi Lewis and Naomi Klein and other Toronto dilettantes who authored the Leap Manifesto , " McGowan told the National Post .
