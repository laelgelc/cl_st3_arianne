<h> Hundreds gather in front of McDougall Centre for rally against NDP <p> A crowd of hundreds protest the provincial NDP and the carbon tax in front of the McDougall Centre . Saturday , November 5th , 2016 . ( Tom Ross - 660 NEWS ) <p> You could cut through the vitriol and frustration , but it remained civil . <p> Hundreds of people , closer to 1,000 at the beginning , took part in a large demonstration in front of the McDougall Centre in downtown Calgary Saturday afternoon . They were there to protest Rachel Notley 's NDP government and its carbon tax as part of the Alberta Wide Rally Against the NDP , which took place in multiple areas across the province . <p> Organizer Dwayne Croteau said the carbon tax is being put forward without proper consultation , and those against it want to be heard . <p> " We 're opposed to the way in which they 're doing business and that is , ramming this carbon tax down our throat . At least 1,000 people had signed it halfway through the rally . <p> Wildrose MLA for Calgary-Foothills , Prasad Panda , was also in attendance to show his support , but needed to be sure the demonstrators would stay calm . <p> " They had to do it in a democratic way and a peaceful way .
