It has become a fact of life and is unfortunately putting extraordinary pressure not only on agriculture , but also on environmentally sensitive areas . <p> The Port of Vancouver 's Terminal 2 project ( T2 ) , at Deltaport , represents yet another chapter in this ongoing saga . <p> Recently , my office submitted 35 pages of comments on the port 's Terminal 2 proposal to the Canadian Environmental Assessment Agency ( CEAA ) as part of the latest stage of environmental review . Essentially , the federal government is determining whether the thousands of pages of information and analysis , provided by the port , are " sufficient .
