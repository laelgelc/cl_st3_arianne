<h> Alberta introduces bill to cap oilsands emissions at 100 megatonnes a year <p> EDMONTON -- The legal rubber has hit the road on Alberta 's promise to cap greenhouse gas emissions from the oilsands -- the engine of the province 's economy . <p> Environment Minister Shannon Phillips introduced Bill 25 in the legislature Tuesday that , if passed and enacted , will forbid oilsands industries from collectively emitting more than 100 megatonnes of greenhouse gases a year . <p> Phillips said the legislation would help green the industry while spurring innovation and growth . <p> " Alberta got the oil out of the sand . We can now get the carbon out of the barrel , " Phillips said at a legislature news conference . <p> " Alberta can be an energy producer and a world leader on climate action , and Alberta must get the most value for our resources and find new markets for our products . <p> " But we can not do it unless we are taking credible action . This bill is a critical element of that story .
