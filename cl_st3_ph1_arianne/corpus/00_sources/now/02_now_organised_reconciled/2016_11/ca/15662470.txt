<p> The month of November is a time to reflect on our veteran 's bravery and sacrifice . <p> This Remembrance Day my staff and I were honoured to lay wreaths at ceremonies across the riding . We were glad to have the opportunity to take the time to pause and pay tribute to those who served and continue to serve to make our world a safer place . <p> I had the opportunity to attend the ceremony held at the Fort William Gardens and my staff paid tribute to veterans at the Fort William First Nation ceremony on Mount McKay . Both ceremonies were beautiful and well-attended . <p> I would like to thank all those who came out to remember the men and women who have courageously served our country , and would like to extend a special thank you to the organizers of the ceremonies . <p> As we all know there is much work to be done to increase the level of services Canadian veterans receive and I am proud to highlight this government 's commitment the reopening of the Veteran 's Affair office in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ closed by the previous government . The Thunder Bay office will reopen in January and will be located at 130 Syndicate Ave . South . I truly believe this will be an important step in increasing the services for Veterans across our entire region . <p> November is also a time to celebrate our children . In honour of National Child Day I was pleased to participate in UNICEF 's Bring your MP to School Day . I had the chance to visit both Kingsway and Hyde Park Public schools , where students were discussing reconciliation and treaties as part of treaty recognition week . <p> I was extremely impressed to see the reconciliation birch bark canoe they made by hand . It was some awesome work that even caught the attention of Prime Minister Trudeau on Twitter ! <p> I also had the chance to stop by St. Patrick 's High School and speak with the grade 10 Civics classes .
