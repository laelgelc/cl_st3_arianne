<h> Halton Region taxes could increase 1.9 per cent in 2017 <h> Halton Regional council <p> Halton residents could see a tax hike of 1.9 per cent on the regional portion of their property tax bill in the new year . <p> The proposed increase to maintain and improve services for 2017 translates into a $24 increase for a home worth $400,000 . <p> Commissioner of Finance and Regional Treasurer Mark Scinocca told Regional council Wednesday ( Nov. 9 ) , during an overview of the 2017 Budget and Business Plan , the increase is consistent with council 's direction to keep the tax rate at or below the rate of inflation . <p> Maintaining service levels <p> " The 2017 budget continues to maintain service levels in the region 's core service areas and ensures delivery of the priorities identified by Regional council , " he said . <p> Regional taxes pay for a variety of Halton services , such as regional roads and planning , paramedic services , public health and social assistance , children 's Nov. 29 and Dec. 1 before it heads to Regional council for approval on Dec. 14 , includes a 3.7 per cent increase in the police budget approved last month , Scinocca noted . <p> Does not include Town or school taxes <p> It does not include municipal and education taxes . <p> Utility rates set to rise <p> Utility rates are also projected to rise by 5.1 per cent or $45 next year for a typical household that uses about 250 cubic metres annually . <p> " This increase is based on a negative consumption growth of 3.5 per cent and a customer growth of 1.6 per cent , " he explained . <p> The
