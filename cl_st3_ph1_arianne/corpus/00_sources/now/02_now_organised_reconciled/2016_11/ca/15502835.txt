<h> Fight Against Global Warming Gaining Momentum <p> Momentum is building in the international fight against global warming . <p> The United Nations Conference on Climate Change wrapped up in Marrakech , Morocco on Friday . <p> Newfoundlander , Carl Mercer , works with the United Nations Development Program and says the wheels are in motion for bigger and better policy . He says it 's ambitious , but the Paris Agreement is just a piece of paper unless things happen on the ground . <p> The United Nations is worked on a global plan of action following the historic Paris Agreement , which was put into effect November 4 . <p> There are worries that some countries are n't committed to the global shift away from fossil fuels , including newly elected American President Donald Trump , who threatened to withdraw the United States from the Paris agreement . <p> Mercer says the movement has too much momentum to be stopped . He says most people realize things need to change so regardless if a few countries do n't sign up , this initiative is moving forward .
