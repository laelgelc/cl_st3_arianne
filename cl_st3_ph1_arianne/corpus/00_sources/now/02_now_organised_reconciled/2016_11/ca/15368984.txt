<p> An Alberta environmental group says a recent survey shows strong public support for creating a new provincial park in the Bighorn area west of Rocky Mountain House . <p> A poll done last month for the Yellowstone to Yukon Conservation Initiative found 83 per cent of Edmonton respondents back the idea , as do 68 per cent of people living near the area . <p> There 's already about 5,000 square kilometres of provincial public land in the Bighorn Backcountry along the eastern borders of Jasper and Banff national parks , but Yellowstone to Yukon wants to roughly double the district 's protected space . <p> " What we 're actually proposing to government is something significantly larger than what 's in the public land use zoning , " says Stephen Legault , a program director for the Canmore-based non-profit group . <p> Other organizations such as the Alberta Wilderness Association also support a Bighorn park . <p> The expanded region would give a major boost to Environment and
