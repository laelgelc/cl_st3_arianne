<h> In 2015 alterations were made to the culvert plan dimensions and alignment <h> Uxbridge Town Hall <p> UXBRIDGE -- The Township of Uxbridge will be seeking comments from residents on changes to the downtown Brock Street Culvert plan between Nov. 24 and Dec. 24 . <p> Residents first got a chance to see the updated concept at a public information centre back in July . According to a report from the township 's director of public works , 15 residents attended and four comments were received . <p> The Brock Street culvert project aims to replace the current deteriorated culvert that carries the Uxbridge Brook under Brock Street . The culvert in its current condition poses a flooding threat .
