<p> I am interested in taking part in climate change in our community . After watching the documentary , Before the Flood , I feel somewhat responsible for making my contribution to build a clean economy for future generations . <p> We are fortunate enough to have many resources such as wind , solar and geothermal renewable sources , and should not take this for granted . Not only do we have access to these tools , we have the technology and education to implement these innovations to make a positive contribution to climate change . <p> The increasing frequency of extreme weather conditions , burning of forestry , not only have an effect on us , but affect the survival of animals . Personally , I would not want to leave behind an earth for my children and grandchildren where they are unable to experience the beauty of our earth . <p> We have the world in our hands and have the power to push the governments to act on climate change .
