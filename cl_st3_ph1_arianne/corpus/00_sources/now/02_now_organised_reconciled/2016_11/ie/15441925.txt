<h> PICS : We love Leonardo DiCaprio even more after he visits a restaurant which helps the homeless <p> When Leo finally got his Oscar , he used his acceptance speech to highlight global warming . <p> Since then , he 's made a documentary on climate change around the globe called Before the Flood , which he made publicly available for free . <p> Now the Oscar winner continues his activist work by recently visiting a restaurant in Queensferry Street , Edinburgh which helps the homeless . <p> The Revenant star met the staff and had lunch at Home , Josh Littlejohn 's latest Social Bite venture . It was set up to provide training and employment for members of the Social Bite Academy , a four-year paid course for homeless people . <p> Social Bite has cafes in Edinburgh , Glasgow and Aberdeen which encourage customers to ' pay forward ' meals for the homeless .
