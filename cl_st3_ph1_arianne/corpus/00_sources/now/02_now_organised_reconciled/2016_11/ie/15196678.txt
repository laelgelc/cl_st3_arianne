<h> Three Cavan schools seek BTYS glory <p> Sixteen students from Cavan will be making their way to the RDS in January in a bid to secure a coveted BT Young Scientist &amp; Technology Exhibition award . A total of nine project entries from schools across the county have been accepted to contest in a number of categories in the 2017 ceremony . <p> Cavan will have a larger contingent travelling to Ballsbridge from the 11 to 14 January compared to the previous year . The competition saw 2,091 project ideas submitted by secondary students from 375 schools across the island . Of the 550 projects shortlisted to compete at the exhibition 16 students from three local schools will be carrying Cavan 's hopes . <p> This year 31 counties are represented , and girls out-numbered the boys with a gender split of 61% female to 39% male entrants . Students submitted ideas and innovations on subjects ranging from migration to climate change to concussion injuries , with Social &amp; Behavioural Sciences the most popular category . <p> Speaking at the announcement , Minister for Education and Skills , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Exhibition is a fantastic platform for many students in Ireland " It is a testament to all involved , that in its 53rd year , the event still continues to grow in such strength and numbers . " <p> Managing Director of BT Ireland , Shay Walsh , said that for BT the exhibition is an investment in young people and the ideas that they have for improving the world we live in " These students continue to amaze us , and we are delighted that they have entered the upcoming exhibition in record numbers . BT Young Scientist &amp; Technology Exhibition is making a difference . We are excited to see what the 2017 entrants have to show us in January . " <p> This year there are 1,622 group entries and 469 individual entries . Commanding 41% of the entries Social &amp; Behavioural Sciences is the most popular category , followed by Biological &amp; Ecological Sciences which has 29% of the students seeking awards , Technology makes up 16% of the cohorts and Chemical , Physical &amp; Mathematical Sciences has 14% of entrants . <p> Projects from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will examine a range of diverse topics including brestfeeding , wine consumption and Troponin as an indicator for a heart attack . <p> There are over 140 teacher and pupil awards to be won , including cash prizes , international trips and the overall title of BT Young Scientist &amp; Technologist of the Year .
