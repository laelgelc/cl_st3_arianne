<h> Citizens ' Assembly receives 600 submissions on abortion <h> Body will discuss Eighth Amendment during its first full-day meeting on Saturday <p> The Citizens ' Assembly has received 600 public first four weekends of presentations and deliberations between November and March will focus solely on the topic of the Eighth Amendment to the Constitution , which recognises " the right to life of the unborn , with due regard to the equal right to life of the mother " . <p> The opening weekend will involve presentations from experts across the areas of law , clinical practice , ethics and
