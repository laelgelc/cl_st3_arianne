<h> ? 14,400 granted for Rock Armour Works in Lordship <p> A total of ? 14,400 has been granted for Rock Armour Works to the revetment in Rampark in Lordship . <p> Rock Armour Works is a method of preventing coastal erosion along the shoreline . <p> The news was revealed today by local TD Fergus O'Dowd , who said : " I have been informed by Minister Sean Canney that ? 14,400 has been made available for Rock Armour Works in the Lordship area that will be delivered through the Office of Public Works . This will be warmly welcomed by all the residents in the area . " <p> A number of areas of the county have been affected by flooding and erosion in recent years . Indeed , in 2014 the main Whitestown to Ballagan coast road on the Cooley Peninsula ( pictured above ) was heavily damaged by a storm . <p> Last November climate change expert Prof John Sweeney of NUI Maynooth warned that Louth was at serious risk of flooding and erosion in the years ahead .
