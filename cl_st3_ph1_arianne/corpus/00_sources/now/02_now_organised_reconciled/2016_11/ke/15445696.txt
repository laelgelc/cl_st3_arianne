<h> Uhuru returns from Morocco climate change conference <p> President Uhuru Kenyatta jetted back into the country Thursday evening from the Mediterranean city of Marrakech , Morocco , where he addressed the United Nations climate change summit . <p> The plane carrying the President and his delegation touched down at the Jomo Kenyatta International Airport shortly before 9pm . <p> On arrival , Uhuru was received by senior government officials led by Majority Leader in Parliament Aden Duale , Foreign Affairs PS Monica Juma and Chief of Defence Forces , General Samson Mwathethe . <p> At the 22nd session of the Conference of the Parties ( COP22 ) , Uhuru urged world leaders to remain committed to the Paris Agreement on climate change that gave hope to poorest countries . <p> In support of the agreement , he said his cabinet has approved its ratification and now awaits the final ratification by Parliament . <p> Uhuru welcomed the coming into force of the Paris Agreement , saying it will provide the much-needed impetus to address climate change for a safer future . <p> He emphasised the need to synchronise @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Sustainable Development Goals . <p> He said the development of the rule book to guide the implementation of the Agreement must keep in focus its objective , principles and provisions of the convention , including equity and common but differentiated responsibilities and respective capabilities . <p> While in Morocco , Uhuru also met 30 African leaders on the margins of the COP22 to drum up support for Foreign Affairs CS Amina Mohamed , Kenya 's candidate for the post of chairperson of the African Union Commission . <p> Among the leaders were those from Nigeria , Gabon , Rwanda , Seychelles , the Comoros , Sudan , Senegal and Chad . <p> Thank you for participating in discussions on The Star , Kenya .
