<p> The Energy Forum is a collaboration of British Columbian power producers , industry associations , and non-government organizations that are working together to address the challenges and opportunities presented by the nexus of energy , climate and ecosystems . Our members continue to support an ambitious Climate Leadership Plan that transitions B.C. 's buildings , vehicles and industries away from fossil fuels to become increasingly energy efficient and reliant on renewable energy , while minimizing impact on the environment.1 <p> With the successful Paris climate talks behind us , the Climate Leadership Plan is timely .
