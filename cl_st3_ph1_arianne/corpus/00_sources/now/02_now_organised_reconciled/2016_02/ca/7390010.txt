The capital funding gap is at least $9.6 million a year and could be as high as $23.7 million . It has resulted in an unfunded accumulated capital backlog of about $120 million , the department says . <p> If anything , the problem is even more acute when it comes to program funding . <p> In actual dollars , the museums receive the same amount in program funding today as they did a decade ago , the document says .
