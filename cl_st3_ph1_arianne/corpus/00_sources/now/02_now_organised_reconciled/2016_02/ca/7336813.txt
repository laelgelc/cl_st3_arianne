's oil and gas sector up six percent in 2014 <p> A new provincial report cites oil and natural gas as the driving force behind an increase in industrial greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions in B.C. <p> Industrial air pollution increased 2.1 percent from 20 million tonnes in 2013 to 20.3 million tonnes in 2014 , according to the report . <p> Including GHG emissions from electricity imports , the increase was 5.7 percent , to 22.4 million tonnes . <p> The increase in industrial emissions was not observed across the board . <p> The mining and smelting sector , for example , saw an 8.4-percent reduction in GHG emissions , and waste treatment pollution was reduced by 6.8 percent . <p> Meanwhile , GHG emissions from oil and gas operations in B.C. increased six percent , to 11.3 million tonnes in 2014 . <p> Though accounting for a much smaller amount of total industrial emissions , the most significant increase came from electricity imports . They were up 60 percent , to 2.2 million tonnes .
