<h> Sustainable development of cities of the future <p> The rapid urbanization of the Indian cities has brought forward a plethora of issues and challenges . It has become inevitable that the sound principles of sustainable development as mentioned in the Brunt land report i.e. social , economic and environmental must become the essence of any future growth . <p> The United Nations Habitat report on climate change noted that cities are not investing adequately in tackling climate change , and unplanned urbanization has led to environmental degradation . In Indian context , the Smart Cities initiative is a step towards habitations where the man kind can not only survive but also thrive through improved quality of life and wellbeing . <p> With waste , water , energy and social factors being given substantial emphasis , green rating systems inherently ensure that the cities provide sufficient amenities , have innovative urban planning systems , and achieve better land use , spatial concentration and infrastructure design . There is no singular solution to all challenges . Every city is different with its own history , culture and policies . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contextual solutions to challenges faced by the Indian cities . <p> The Government is being proactive and visionary to develop smart cities and exponential development is happening in services and construction technologies . <p> However , the design approach to the sustainable cities of the future is an iterative thought process from macro to micro level . An integrated design process with involvement of urban planners , architects , engineers , environmentalists and the residents of the city must be the foundation of such development . <p> With few proposed capital cities , India has a chance to evolve comprehensive design strategies based on minimum land and resource use . Instead of imitating the glossy western cities , the emphasis must be on developing a city which is weaved into the Indian fabric and caters to the regional , climatic and temporal idiosyncrasies of our country . <p> Presently , the cities cover less than 2% of the planets ' surface but consume 78% of the world 's energy . On one hand , we are running out of land and at the same time landfills are increasing at a fast pace . Cradle to cradle system loops should be envisioned and devised at city level for each and every element such as waste and water . The change has to start from within so a parallel drive to change the mind-sets of the citizens is crucial . A city can only be green when its citizens lead a sustainable lifestyle . <p> The design of the city should evolve around the movement of its residents . <p> Transport linkages are the arteries of any city .
