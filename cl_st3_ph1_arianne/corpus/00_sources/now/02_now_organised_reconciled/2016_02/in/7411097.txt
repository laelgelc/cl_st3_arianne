<h> Hottest January smashes global temperature record <p> The average temperature across global land and ocean surfaces was 1.87 degrees Fahrenheit <p> WASHINGTON : Last month was the hottest January in modern times , US data showed on Wednesday , the latest in a string of heat records fuelling concerns over the pace of climate change . <p> The average temperature across global land and ocean surfaces was 1.87 degrees Fahrenheit ( 1.04 Celsius ) above the 20th century average for January , the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration said . <p> This was the highest January average since records began in 1880 , NOAA said in a statement , surpassing the previous record of 2007 by 0.29 degrees Fahrenheit . <p> January marked the ninth consecutive month to break a monthly global temperature record , NOAA said , after the blistering temperatures of 2015 set a heat record for the year . <p> Last year was the hottest by far since 1880 , beating the previous record set in
