<h> Mitigation cost must be part of infra plans <p> Nagpur:Setting up green infrastructure and including mitigation costs in cost of projects intruding into forested areas are some of the key recommendations made by Wildlife Institute of India ( WII ) , Dehradun , to Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) . <p> Taking a cue from battle over mitigation measures for widening of NH-6 &amp; NH-7 , MoEFCC asked WII to prepare technical document suggesting measures to mitigate impact of linear projects like canals , roads , and rail and power transmission lines going through forested areas . <p> However , even as January 31 was the last date to submit comments by stakeholders on the draft guidelines prepared by WII , not many know about it as MoEFCC did not publicize them . " It seeks to serve as a tool to enable road and rail agencies to work together with conservation agencies to maximize opportunities presented by transportation and power line projects , to minimize environmental impacts and enhance biodiversity and ecosystem services by promoting development of green infrastructure , " WII officials said . <p> According to National Transport Development Policy Committee ( NTDPC ) , demand for road-based transport services had dramatically accelerated following liberalization and has provided for as much as 90% of total passenger traffic , leaving a meagre 10% for rail . <p> Rail and road freight traffic is expected to grow at about 12% and 8% per annum respectively , to achieve a 50% share each in the total freight traffic at the end of 15th Plan . <p> Hence , green infrastructure holds key . It offers a blueprint for ' smart conservation ' in the same way that engineering plans provide a blueprint for future roads , railway lines , canals and power lines .
