<h> Awareness campaign on ' conservation of wildlife and environment ' in Dimapur <p> Awareness campaign for " conservation of wildlife and environment " was launched by Fingerprint-Design &amp; Events in collaboration with the department of Environment , Forest and Climate Change at Immanuel College on Tuesday . <p> A press release by Fingerprint-Design &amp; Events head , Hongba Phom stated that the team would be visiting 25 colleges and 20 schools in Dimapur in the coming months . <p> It stated that the campaign was focussed more on one point of " why should we conserve wildlife and environment when we are so embroiled in our daily social struggle for survival ? " <p> The release stated that schools and colleges being the epicentre for any change in the future , the campaign believes that more than environmental books , students needed to understand the weightage of conserving wildlife and environment in the present context .
