<h> Campaign on conservation of wildlife and environment launched <p> February 24 , 2016 <p> Students of Immanuel College , Dimapur read the brochure on ' conservation of wildlife and environment ' distributed by Fingerprint on February 23 . <p> Dimapur , February 23 ( MExN ) : Fingerprint- Design &amp; Events in collaboration with the Department of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Government of Nagaland have launched an awareness campaign for ' conservation of wildlife and environment . ' <p> The Fingerprint team , starting with Immanuel College from February 23 will be visiting 25 Colleges and 20 Schools in Dimapur in the coming months to spread the awareness campaign among the young masters of the future . <p> " The Campaign is not about the educative theories about wildlife and environment rather we are focusing on one point of Why should we conserve Wildlife and Environment when we are so embroiled in our daily social struggle for survival ? , " Fingerprint -- Design &amp; Events , Head Hongba Phom stated in a press release . <p> " We feel that school and college @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ see in future ; Environmental books have made them understand the importance of conservation of Wildlife and Environment . But more importantly they need to understand the weightage of Conserving Wildlife and Environment with that of our present social needs . Through this Campaign , we believe that the ideas implanted at present should reflect the sow in years to come , " he said . <p> He also added that , " a small and humble start for a Giant change but we truly do not want to protect the environment but create a world where the environment does n't need any protection .
