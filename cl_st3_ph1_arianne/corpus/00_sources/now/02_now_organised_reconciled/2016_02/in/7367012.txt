<h> Devotees still throng the 600-yr-old Uran temple for Maghi Ganeshotsav <p> The Ridhi-Siddhi Vinayaka temple , in Vinayak village near Uran , is one of the oldest structures in the city , dating back to 1365 <p> TNN Feb 11 , 2016 , 12.50 AM IST <p> NAVI MUMBAI : The Ridhi-Siddhi Vinayaka temple , in Vinayak village near Uran , is one of the oldest structures in the city , dating back to 1365 . The temple draws thousands of devotees every year during the Maghi Ganeshotsav . The current supervisor of the temple , Sadanand Gharat from Gharat ' Phajandar ' family , has been doing his best to protect this ancient structure and the idol , which was gifted to his forefather , Yesu Ganu Gharat by the Peshwa in 1753 . <p> Gharat , a retired Central excise officer said that his forefather was the revenue collector of this area . " The Peshwa was very satisfied with his sincerity and gifted him the temple and adjacent area . I took over the maintenance of this temple from my father , Raghunath Gharat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of India and , as per their advice , lacquered the idol . He has been keeping the walls clean and dry , so that climate change does not affect the stones . The maintenance now assumes more importance as the pollution levels in the village have been increasing slowly . <p> The east-facing two-and-half-foot idol , made out of a single stone , has Riddhi and Siddhi statues on each side , is a ' swyambhu ' or self-manifested idol . The mandir was built with big squares of rocks and the entrance is less than 5-foot high and 2-foot wide . The inscription on the back of the idol is the main source of its information .
