<p> The failure of the successive governments to take measures for conservation of wetlands in Kashmir has pushed them on the brink of extinction . <p> While authorities in Kashmir would commemorate the World Wetlands Day on Tuesday by holding seminars and debates , the wetlands in Valley continue to face the official neglect . <p> The World Wetlands Day marks the signing of convention on wetlands , called the Ramsar Convention , on this day in 1971 for conservation and sustainable utilization of wetlands . <p> In absence of conservation measures , wetlands in Kashmir including Wullar , Hokersar , Haigam , Mirgund , Shalabugh have lost their carrying capacity due to extensive siltation and pollution . <p> Wullarlake and its associated wetlands comprise an important habitat for migratory water birds within the Central Asian Flyway and supports rich biodiversity .
