<h> Overlapping provisions lag passage of Environment Code <h> Overlapping provisions lag passage of Environment Code <p> THE proposed environment code was found to have several overlapping provisions stalling its supposed approval for second reading and for publication . <p> The 135-page draft provides measures on the regulation of fireworks and pyrotechnics use , mining regulation , tourism management and even the operation of livelihood centers . <p> Members of the Baguio City Council found these provisions were deemed in conflict with existing laws of the city including the City Land Use Plan ( Clup ) . <p> Discussions were terminated and City Council members agreed the item will remain in the agenda for discussion in the next sessions . <p> The proposed code states the city 's " unique ecological , historical and socio-cultural characteristic " shall be maintained through the implementation of sustainable development , conservation , preservation , possible remedial action , continuing information and education campaign as to relevant environmental legislation , policies , and avenues for active involvement of the general public against air , land , water , visual and noise nuisance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
