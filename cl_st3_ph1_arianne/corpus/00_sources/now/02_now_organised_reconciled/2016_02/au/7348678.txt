<p> Forests play a critical role in the global carbon cycle , helping to mitigate climate change by storing carbon that would otherwise end up in the atmosphere . <p> It turns out that conifer trees , like evergreens , may actually cause temperature increases where they 've taken root in place of broad-leafed trees . <p> For example , look at Europe where the study was focused . Once upon a time , its forests were largely leafy deciduous trees . Now , a majority of Europe 's trees are managed by humans , and we 've been planting trees like pines and spruces because they grow faster . <p> But by replacing older forests with ones that are newer and faster-growing , Europe has gone into " carbon debt . " Harvesting those older trees and replacing them with conifers has released 3.1 billion metric tons of carbon . <p> Researchers say this change caused a temperature increase equal to 6 percent of warming attributed to fossil fuels . When less of that radiation is reflected into space , the planet can heat up . <p> Some countries are already planting trees to help combat climate change .
