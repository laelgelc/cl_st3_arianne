<h> MFI Summit to Mark Decade of Growth <p> Khmer Times/May Kunmakara <p> Thursday , 25 February 2016 <p> 312 views <p> The National Bank of Cambodia is hosting the National Summit on the Development of the Microfinance Sector in Cambodia next month , marking a decade since the government announced that 2006 was the " Year of Microfinance . " <p> The summit will be presided over by Prime Minister Hun Sen and will take place on March 14 and 15 , according to the press release from the National Bank of Cambodia ( NBC ) . <p> " The objective of the National Summit is to promote public awareness on the development of the microfinance sector in Cambodia and its important role in the development of the economy and in poverty alleviation -- one of the government 's national strategies -- by attracting more attention from local and international stakeholders , " the NBC said . <p> Sim Senacheert , president and CEO of Prasac Microfinance , one of the largest microfinance institutions ( MFIs ) in Cambodia , said the sector had developed rapidly since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the sector has been increasing sharply approximately between 30 to 35 percent annually while the number of customers has also grown into the millions , " he said . <p> Mr. Senacheert added that the rapid development of MFIs also helped Cambodians economic values such as responsible budgeting and borrowing , saying that Cambodians are learning quickly . " The rate of non-performing loans ( NPLs ) is pretty low , between 0.5-0.6 per cent . This means that customers use loans efficiently , " he said . <p> According to Mr. Senacheert , the expansion of MFIs not only raised people 's living standards but also reduced informal lending and the crippling interest rates that come with it . <p> " What is important is that we give people more access to formal lending , If not , they will receive loans from the informal sector , which charge 5 to 10 percent per month , " he added . <p> Over the past decade , MFIs have been expanding not only in size but also by upgrading their services from traditional lending to offering deposits , ATMs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in 25 cities and provinces across Cambodia last year , with 1,354 offices and total outstanding lending of $2.9 billion as of December , according to the Cambodia Microfinance Association ( CMA ) , which said lending rose by 45 percent year on year . <p> The number of borrowers reached 2 million by the end of last year , the CMA said . Eight MFIs were granted deposit-taking licenses , and their combined deposits totaled $1.3 billion , a rise of 45 percent from the year-before period , the CMA said . <p> MFIs have also expanded their network to rural areas . Prasac has more than 180 offices nationwide , Mr. Senacheert said . <p> He said the sector still faces challenges . " We do not have the adequate funds to meet the high demand of loans , we have tough competition , and factors like climate change are affecting the agricultural sector , which is one of the main absorbing industries of loans , " he explained .
