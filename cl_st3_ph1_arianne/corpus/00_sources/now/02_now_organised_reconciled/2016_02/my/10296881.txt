<h> Antarctica expedition returns <p> SEPANG : Scientists from six local universities have completed a 33-day expedition to the Antarctica and brought home more than 200 samples for research . <p> Gathered from seven sampling checkpoints , these include water , algae , soil , ice , marine invertebrates , spores , air , and etc , which will be used to study the effects of climate change . <p> Deputy Science , Technology , and Innovation Minister Datuk Abu Bakar Mohamad Diah said the mission was a success and expected to contribute to climate change research globally . <p> " Climate change involves many scientific fields and the data we 've collected will be beneficial to future research in related fields . <p> " Our scientists will also be able to share their knowledge gained from this expedition with their international peers , " Abu Bakar told a press conference at the Kuala Lumpur International Airport ( KLIA ) today when welcoming the eight researchers home . <p> The Antarctic was chosen as the effects of climate change is more apparent due to its location . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Argentina , the world 's southern most port , where they travelled more than 1,000km by ship to Antarctica . <p> Their ship left the port on Jan 18 but had to turn back due to high winds and rough seas with waves going up to 6m , forcing the team to delay departure for 36 hours . <p> The mission resumed on Jan 22 and the team managed to reach their destination in slightly better weather .
