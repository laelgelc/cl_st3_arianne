<h> Asian institutions slow on the uptake of ESG investment strategies <p> Sustainable investment specialist Saturna Capital anticipates that environmental , sustainable , and governance-themed ( ESG ) investments will gain traction among Asian institutional investors , especially through sharia-compliant vehicles in Malaysia due to the parallels they share with ESG investing . <p> Patrick T Drum , a portfolio manager at Saturna Capital , tells Asia Asset Management that although ESG investment may be behind the curve in Asia , he believes more local institutions will incorporate ESG strategies into their portfolios as they bid to seek lower risk exposures offering returns in line with conventional strategies . <p> " Comparatively , the US market is at the vanguard of ESG investment , which is predominately due to its government placing clear regulatory guidance on matters relating to national security , resource allocations and population migration , " he notes . As an example , he singles out the California Public Employees ' Retirement System ( CalPERS ) , which was one of the first pension funds to employ ESG strategies . <p> According to the USSIF @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and responsible investment ( SRI ) in the US amounted to US$6.57 trillion in 2014 , up from around $3 trillion in 2010 .
