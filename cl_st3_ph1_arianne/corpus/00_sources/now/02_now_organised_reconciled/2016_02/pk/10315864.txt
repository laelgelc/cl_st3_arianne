<h> Spring trees plantation campaign starts in KP <p> Thursday , February 18 , 2016 - Peshawar ? The spring trees plantation campaign was kicked off in Khyber Pakthunkhwa wherein 123.279 saplings of different species would be sown with the help of different stakeholders to offset the effects of global warming and climate change . <p> Officials in Forest Department told APP on Wednesday that spring trees plantation campaign has been launched across the province and stocks of fit plants were sent to the concerned stakeholders to meet the said target . <p> As many as 47.907 million saplings would be planted in southern Circle comprising Peshawar , Mardan , DI Khan , Kohat and Bannu divisions while 37.734million would be sown in Abbottabad Region-11 including Lower Hazara with a target of 9.592 million , Upper Hazara 8.122 million and Watershed Management Circle 20.02 million respectively . <p> In Malakand Circle , 37.638 million saplings would be sown including 24.330 million in Malakand East and 13.308 million in Malakand West circles respectively . To meet the said target , 108.352 million would be planted with the help of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by Village Development Committees ( VDCs ) , 1.376 million with assistance of defence forces and 1.3331 million by educational institutes respectively . <p> The official said local communities are being encouraged through their respective VDCs to help carry out most of the planting , while individual farmers will be facilitated on their farms and fields . The official said Rs5 per plant has been fixed for farmers , NGOs , provincial departments and autonomous bodies while free plants would be provided to defence forces , adding 11.304 million saplings were planted during last monsoon campaign . <p> Under the Green Growth Initiative ( GGI ) strategy for forestry ? s improvement in KP announced on February 9 , 2014 , the official said special enrichment plantations would be carried out over 22,239 acres depleted forest areas annually that would improve stocking density of forests and improve ratio of well stocked forests in the province .
