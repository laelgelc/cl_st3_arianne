<h> WWF Pakistan 's Best Story Award for three journalists <p> KARACHI : WWF Pakistan awarded Best Story Awards to three journalists for their stories on climate change on Tuesday at WWF Pakistan office in Karachi . <p> The first award was given to a freelance journalist Rabab Ibrahim for her story on the rise in sea level , second award went to Associated Press of Pakistan ( APP ) Reporter Rashid Ali Panhwer and third award was given to Geo News Reporter Nizam Samoon for his story on the disappearance of indigenous seeds from Thar Desert . <p> These environmental journalists produced stories which were published on a website called www.thenaturenews.com followed by a recent training ' Reporting impacts of climate change on communities : Building network and media capacity to cover climate change ' organised by the National Council of Environmental Journalists ( NCEJ ) in collaboration with the Earth Journalism Network ( EJN ) . <p> Around 40 journalists were trained during these sessions that were held at different locations across the nation . <p> Speaking on the occasion , WWF Pakistan Biodiversity Senior Director @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially the rise in temperature , frequent cyclones , uncertain rainfalls , super floods and severe droughts are caused by these climate change . The events brought about drastic changes in the socio-economic and environmental conditions of the country . The situation was quite visible in recent floods across the nation , the heat waves in Sindh particularly Karachi and the current drought situation in Tharparkar district . <p> The director also shared that coastal communities of Pakistan were more vulnerable to these climate change 's impacts , hence they require adaptation practices to address the issue . He also urged that there is a great need for building the capacity of journalists so that they highlight and document the impacts of these climate changes on local communities in a proper way . <p> The president of NCEJ Amar Guriro shared that climate changes were not a local or national issue but has become a global issue .
