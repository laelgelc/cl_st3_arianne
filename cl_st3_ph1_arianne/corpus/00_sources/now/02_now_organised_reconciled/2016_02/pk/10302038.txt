<h> UNIDO organizes event to celebrate success of program promoting innovation in clean technologies <p> UNITED NATIONS : United Nations Industrial Development Organization ( UNIDO ) organized an event to celebrate remarkable success of the ' Global Cleantech Innovation Programme for SMEs and Startups ' ( GCIP ) , a competition and accelerator program promoting innovations in the area of clean technologies . <p> According to a press release , the event aimed at creating awareness on the need of clean technology innovations and connecting winners with venture capitalists from Pakistan by showcasing their technologies through media . <p> " GCIP-Pakistan has supported fifty-five SMEs and Startups in Pakistan through a programme of extensive mentoring , training , access to investors and showcasing opportunities in 2015 alone " , the release read . <p> The program culminated in five winners being awarded prize money ranging from US$ 15,000 to US$ 20,000 , and being given the opportunity to participate in the Cleantech Open Global Forum held in Silicon Valley , USA . <p> The release stated that the visit was of prime significance for the participants in terms @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ capital from international investors and venture capital firms . One of the national runner-up teams , TAWANAI which has developed an agricultural dry bio-waste gasifier was additionally recognized as the best 2015 GCIP Runner-up in the Waste-to-Energy category at the global level . <p> " GCIP for SMEs and startups , simultaneously implemented in Armenia , India , Malaysia , Pakistan , Turkey and South Africa , aims to enhance emerging clean technology startups and strengthen policy frameworks for innovation . The program takes a competition and accelerator approach to select the best cleantech entrepreneurs and support them to develop their innovative technologies into full-fledged market-ready products " , the release read . <p> The chief guest of the ceremony , Mr. Shoaib Mir , Additional Secretary of the Ministry of Climate Change ( MoCC ) , appreciated the efforts of UNIDO and highlighted the importance of innovation in rapidly changing world . He stressed the need to develop a culture of innovation in Pakistan for economic development and compared the classical concepts with the modern version of innovation . He reiterated to all the innovators that there is no limit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can not predict which innovations will be thriving in the coming years , the release stated . <p> Atif Ikram Sheikh , President of the Islamabad Chamber of Commerce and Industry ( ICCI ) highlighted various initiatives of Islamabad Chamber , undertaken jointly with UNIDO , to support entrepreneurship , with a particular focus on youth and women . He was optimistic that such initiatives would promote clean technologies in SMEs and enable them to compete more effectively in the market . He expressed keen interest to work with UNIDO on such initiatives in the long run . <p> UNIDO Representative , Esam Alqararah , addressing the ceremony , elaborated uniqueness of the program to develop an entrepreneurial ecosystem for fostering innovations in clean technologies and its impact on overall economy of the country . He highlighted importance of the programme in creating new industries , that bring about new jobs , prosperity , but simultaneously saving the environment . <p> At the end , ICCI distributed certificates among the winners , runner ups and the Most Promising Woman-Lead Team .
