<h> Government to hold census next month , NA told <p> ISLAMABAD : The National Assembly was informed on Thursday that the government was implementing different projects under the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor ( CPEC ) that would benefit all the provinces . <p> Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs Shaikh Aftab Ahmad told the house that western and eastern routes of the CPEC would connect with each other . The minister said the government had decided to take census next month . He said that exercise for delimitation of constituencies would begin after the census , if required .
