<h> Dr Jim King assures support to Balochistan <p> Friday , February 12 , 2016 - Quetta ? Chief Minister Balochistan , Nawab Sanaullah Khan Zehri has said that provincial government is utilizing all available resources to improve health and education sectors as both sectors are among the top priority of the government . <p> ? The educational budget has been increased to 24 per cent while the budget of health was raised to 12 per cent , ? he said . He was talking to a delegation of World Bank led by its president Dr. Jim Yong Kim who called on him in Karachi , the other day . <p> Member of Provincial Assembly Mir Asim Kurd Gailo , Senator Shahbaz Durrani , Chief Secretary Balochistan Saifullah Chattah , Additional Chief Secretary Naseebullah Bazai and other concerned officials were also present on the occasion . Expressing satisfaction over the reforms made by the provincial government in education and health departments , and on ongoing social development process , Dr. Jim Yong Kim assured provincial government to review the support of World Bank in different sectors . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ World Bank would visit Balochistan to assess the additional support for the province . In the meeting , reforms in education and health sectors , minerals , water sector , environment , provision of technical education to the youth and other social development issues were thoroughly discussed . <p> Health Authorities informed World Bank delegation about the proposed projects , including that of Mother and Child Health Project , Capacity Building of teachers , Quetta-Pishin Development and Review of Impact of Climate Change and Prevention Programme. ?
