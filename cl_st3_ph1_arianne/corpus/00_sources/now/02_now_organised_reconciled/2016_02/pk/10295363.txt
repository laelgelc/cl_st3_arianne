<h> Clogged sewerage system stirs up protest <p> A number of people including women and children staged a protest demonstration against Pasrur Tehsil Municipal Administration ( TMA ) over the clogging of sewerage system for months.The perturbed people chanted slogans against the TMA officials and blocked traffic on various inter-city roads in Pasrur by burning tyres on roads.They told newsmen that all the streets and roads in Pasrur city including congested Rehmanpura , Buhadarpura , Tajpura , Hanifpura , Muslim Colony , Taimur Colony , Inayatpura , Umeedpura , Shah Malook Road , Kutchery Road , Kalaaswala Road , Kutchery Road , Civil Hospital Road and Godown Road were inundated due to stagnant sewerage water and the TMA officials have allegedly turned a blind eye to the situation . The people added that rains aggravate the situation . They said that the people especially the children had been suffering great ordeal while passing through these streets and roads inundated with sewerage water while going to their schools and colleges . They said there was no road and street safe from sewerage water . When contacted , Assistant Commissioner Tauqeer Ilyas Cheema confirmed that the entire sewerage system of Pasrur city has become choked . He said that early cleanliness and clearance of the clogged sewerage system has also been ordered and TMA sanitary workers were busy rectifying the system on emergency grounds .
