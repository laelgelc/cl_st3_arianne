<h> Stock food for two weeks , MQM asks people <p> KARACHI - Muttahida Qaumi Movement 's ( MQM ) coordination committee yesterday appealed to the citizens to be very careful for the next two weeks advising them to preserve food and medicines for any untoward situation.It was observed in a joint meeting of Karachi and London coordination committees following the predictions of MQM chief Altaf Hussain . During the meeting overall situation including the climate change , earthquake , flood and other natural calamities came under discussion . It was discussed that the MQM chief not only unfolded the conspiracies against MQM but also informed the nation about the menace of terrorism like Talbanisation and ISIS . It said that the authorities and media persons took his concern non-seriously . The meeting unanimously decided to pile up at least a fortnightly stock of medicines and foods for the nation to fight against any such untoward situation .
