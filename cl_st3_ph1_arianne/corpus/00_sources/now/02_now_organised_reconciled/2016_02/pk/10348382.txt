<p> KARACHI : The health experts have warned against Zika virus in Pakistan , saying that there is serious threat of spreading of the epidemic in the country . <p> Dr Muhammad Khalid , who heads the Washington chapter of Physicians for Social Responsibility , said climate change and poor sanitation could spread Zika epidemic in Pakistan . <p> The PSR group has won the Nobel Prize for its efforts to promote peace and also works to help raise global awareness on various issues . <p> Dr. Muhammad Khalid said there is no vaccine for Zika virus , therefore , creating awareness among the masses is essential . <p> The World Health Organization has declared the outbreak a global public health emergency , noting its association with two neurological disorders - microcephaly in babies and Guillain-Barre syndrome that can cause paralysis . <p> They also called upon the government to take necessary measures against Dengue virus immediately .
