<h> Amendments in NAB 's law imperative for early disposal of cases : minister <p> Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid said on Friday that amendments to the National Accountability ( NAB ) law are imperative for timely disposal of old cases <p> Speaking in the National Assembly on Friday , the minister said that a total of 2,593 cases of the government 's employees across the country had been referred to the NAB but that only 229 had been disposed of during the last five years . Hamid said that around 233 cases in Rawalpindi , 332 in KP , 366 in Lahore , one in Multan , 700 in Quetta , 545 in Karachi and 416 in Sukkur were referred to the NAB .
