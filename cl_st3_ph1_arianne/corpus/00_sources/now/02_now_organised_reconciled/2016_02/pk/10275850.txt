<h> LEAD Pakistan signs MoU to design Punjab climate change policy <p> LAHORE : Leadership for Environment and Development ( LEAD ) Pakistan Thursday signed an MoU with Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) and Planning and Development Department ( P&amp;DD ) , Government of Punjab , to formulate Punjab climate change policy and providing strategic benefits of research , management , training , capacity building and development programs on low-carbon development and economic growth , read a press statement . <p> According to the MoU , LEAD Pakistan will help in the capacity building of EPA and P&amp;DD Punjab in different aspects of climate compatible development , provide technical expertise to the policy development process , and help coordinate , consult and engage relevant stakeholders in the preparation to draft provincial climate change policy and multilateral environmental agreements ( MEAs ) action plans ; and assess their capacity for implementation and recommending policy measures for capacity building of stakeholders . <p> Information will also be collected from various government departments regarding climatic variations in Punjab and their impact .
