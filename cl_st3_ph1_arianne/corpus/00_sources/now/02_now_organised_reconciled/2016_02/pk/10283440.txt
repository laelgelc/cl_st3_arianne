<h> Pakistan urges integrated approach to implement 2030 agenda <p> NEW YORK - Pakistan has called for an integrated approach to implement the United Nations 2030 development agenda by placing sustainable energy at its heart.Chairing a meeting of an informal group of countries that seek to promote what is called ' SE4All ' or Sustainable Energy for All , Pakistan 's Ambassador to the UN , Maleeha Lodhi , said that energy is pivotal to achieve economic growth and development . " It 's cross cutting nature means that it is central to securing almost all the other Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , " she said , adding , that energy is linked to virtually all the objectives associated with poverty eradication , climate change , employment , education , food and water security.She told the participants of the meeting that with the adoption of SDG 7 , which seeks to ensure access to clean and affordable energy , the UN has for the first time , focused specifically on energy as a universal goal . What would be critical , she said , is enhanced technological support to exploit indigenous resources including advanced and cleaner fossil-fuel technology together with scaled up investments for energy .
