<p> Under the dual factor impact of both weather and holiday , the vegetable prices in Guangzhou soared . It is expected that the overall vegetable price in Guangzhou will continue to stay high level and will not return to normal until the spring festival season passes . <p> The phenomenon of vegetable prices soaring not only occurred in Guangzhou recently , but also has appeared in many other cities in China because of the stepwise increase of logistic and labor costs . <p> Moreover , the abnormal climate change during the spring festival season also attributed to the significant price increase of vegetables . In January , the cold wave hit the southern region and the vegetable growing areas are severely damaged . In conjunction with the tight vegetable supply situation , the vegetable prices soared in the majority part of the region in China .
