<h> Life is n't always planned <p> Change in mindset of couples here may be needed if Singapore is to reverse its dismal birth rate . <p> Chinese New Year is a time for kinship , overeating and endearingly stilted conversations with people you know you really should see more often . <p> But there is also nothing like a gathering of the extended family to serve as a reminder of the impending demographic crisis facing Singapore . <p> My mother , who at 67 is old enough to have sung three different national anthems during her time in school , is the third of seven siblings . <p> Between them , they produced 11 children , with my second uncle having the biggest brood . He fathered three during the 1960s and 1970s , when concerns about overpopulation led to the Stop At Two policy . <p> At 30 , I am the second youngest in my generation of 11 .
