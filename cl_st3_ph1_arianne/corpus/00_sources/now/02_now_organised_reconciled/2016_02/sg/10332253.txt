<h> China 's most beautiful wetlands <p> Wetlands are vital to human life , as they host a huge variety of life , protect coastlines and provide natural sponges against river flooding and store carbon dioxide to regulate climate change . <p> But such important and beautiful lungs of the planet are disappearing every year due to human activities . More than 64 per cent of the world 's wetlands have vanished since 1900 and China too is seeing its wetlands evaporating . <p> For example , in the past 50 years , the northern province of Hebei has lost 90 per cent of its wetlands . In Shaanxi province the 30 counties that make up the Guanzhong area have seen up to 10,000 ponds disappear , says chinadialogue.net . <p> With World Wetlands Day being marked on Feb 2 , we have selected China 's most beautiful areas . The area is known as China 's swan nature reserve . <p> Ruoergai Wetland Nature Reserve <p> China 's largest peat moorland area lies in Southwest China 's Sichuan province . The nature reserve is home to many animal species such as the black-neck crane ; it is also China 's important natural reservoir . <p> Zhalong Nature Reserve <p> Zhalong in Mongolian means sheep or cattle pen . The wetland lies at Songnen plain in Northeast China 's Heilongjiang province .
