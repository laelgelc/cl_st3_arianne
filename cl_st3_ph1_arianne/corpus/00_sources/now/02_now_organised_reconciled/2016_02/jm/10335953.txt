<h> Auto weather station at Portmore school <p> The Meteorological Service of Jamaica ( Met Service ) has installed an automatic weather station at the Greater Portmore Primary School in St Catherine . <p> The weather station was placed at the school last December after the institution won the 2015 Weather and Climate Change Schools ' Quiz Competition put on by the Met Service . <p> The facility , which is operated at World Meteorological Organization standard , measures atmospheric conditions to provide information for weather forecasts and to study the weather and climate . The measurements taken include temperature , barometric pressure , humidity , wind speed , wind direction , and precipitation amounts . <p> The automatic station at the school replaces an old manual-gauge system and allows for measurements from remote areas . <p> Acting director of the Met Service Jacqueline Spence said that the setting up of the station was beneficial to the Met Service . <p> " We are now able to monitor rainfall activity , temperature , and so on .
