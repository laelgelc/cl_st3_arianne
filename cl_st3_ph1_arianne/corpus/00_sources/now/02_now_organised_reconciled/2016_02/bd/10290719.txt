<h> ' Revolutionary success ' achieved in agriculture sector : Experts <p> RANGPUR , Feb 13 : Experts at a post-rally discussion Saturday said the nation has achieved revolutionary success in agriculture , fishery and livestock sectors ensuring food security for the growing population . <p> The pro-farmer policy of the present government led by Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has helped the nation in achieving the success to increase food production substantially despite various adverse situations , including climate change impacts , they said . <p> Rangpur unit of the Krishibid Institution Bangladesh ( KIB ) organised the rally and discussion in observance the Krishibid Day-2016 with the theme of " Bangabandhu 's Contribution Agriculturists Class One . " <p> Acting President of Rangpur district unit of KIB Agriculturist ASM Nasir Uddin Khan presided over the discussion at the conference room of the Seed Certification Agency ( SCA ) office in the city . <p> A large number of the agriculturists of different government and non-government departments , organisations , institutes and agencies participated in the discussion . <p> Earlier , the Agriculturists brought out a huge colourful @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the Town Hall premises before gathering at the discussion at SCA office . <p> General Secretary of Rangpur unit of KIB and Regional Farm Broadcasting Officer of the Agriculture Information Service Abu Sayem delivered welcome speech narrating contributions of the Agriculturists in developing the country 's agriculture sector .
