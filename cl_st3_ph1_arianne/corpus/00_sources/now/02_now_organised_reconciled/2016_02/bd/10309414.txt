<h> BCSIR supporting Jessore housewives to use bio-gas <p> Our Correspondent <p> JESSORE , Feb 16 : Rubia Khatun is a housewife of village Bahirampur under Jhikargachha Upazila . Instead of burning woods or branches of trees , she now cooks food for her four-member family by using bio-gas . <p> " Using two baskets of cow dung , I get sufficient gas for everyday cooking " , Rubia added . <p> She set up the bio-gas plant with a cost of Tk 19,000 beside her house with the help of the Bangladesh Council of Scientific and Industrial Research ( BCSIR ) , she added . <p> In addition , Rubia said , she got Tk 12,000 as non-refundable grant from the Institute of Fuel Research and Development ( IFRD ) of BCSIR . <p> Like Rubia , Parvin Akhter also uses bio-gas for cooking . <p> Mohammad Shah Jamal , project director of Mitigation of Carbon Emission and Extension of Alternative Energy Usage through Dissemination of Biogas Plant and Improved Cook Stove said by using 80 kilograms of cow-waste every user can produce some 105cft biogas in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plants in Jessore " , he added . <p> Nazrul Islam , chairman of BSCIR , while visiting biogas plants in the district said BSCIR is the lone research and testing institute of the country which is trying to change the socio-economic condition of the country . <p> " Research is underway to bring such a technology in which people can cook food by using solar energy . " We have already succeeded in cooking rice on the rooftops by using the technology " , the chairman added . <p> SM Asaduzzaman Sujan , senior scientific officer , Institute of Fuel Research and development ( IFRD ) and also deputy project director of the project said being funded by Climate Change Fund of the Climate Change Unit the Ministry of Environment and Forest is implementing the project in Jessore , Bagura , Bagerhat , Jamalpur , Sherpur , Madaripur , Shariatpur , Barishal , Natore , Rajshahi , Cha-painawabgonj , Naogaon , Jaipurhat , Gaibandha and Nilphamari . <p> " A total of 5,000 families are being giving supports with biogas plants " , he added . <p> Abdur Razzak , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of cow dung are available everyday .
