<p> Bangladesh-born engineer Sayeef Salahuddin has been named as recipient of the US Presidential Early Career Awards for Scientists and Engineers . <p> The award is the highest honour bestowed by the US government on science and engineering professionals in the early stages of their independent research careers . <p> A total of 105 researchers have been honored this year , says a White House press release . <p> " These early-career scientists are leading the way in our efforts to confront and understand challenges from climate change to our health and wellness , " Obama said . <p> " We congratulate these accomplished individuals and encourage them to continue to serve as an example of the incredible promise and ingenuity of the American people . " <p> Sayeef , an associate professor of Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences at University of California , received his B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET in 2003 and PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University in 2007 . <p> Established by US President Clinton in 1996 , the awards are coordinated by the Office of Science and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Awardees are selected for their pursuit of innovative research at the frontiers of science and technology and their commitment to community service as demonstrated through scientific leadership , public education , or community outreach.
