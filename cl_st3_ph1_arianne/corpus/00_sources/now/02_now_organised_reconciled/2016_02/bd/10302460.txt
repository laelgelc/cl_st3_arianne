<h> 3 UK MPs to help restore UK visa centre in BD <p> DHAKA , Feb 15 : Visiting three MPs of British Labour Party on Monday said they will work for the restoration of UK visa centre in Bangladesh . <p> The three British MPs -- Sir Keir Starmer , Stephen Timms and Steve Reed -- made the commitment when they met Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina at her Sangsad Bhaban office with other eight members under the banner of Labour Friends of Bangladesh . <p> Labour Friends of Bangladesh are now in Bangladesh as part of their programme to ' Meet , Listen and Learn ' from Bangladesh . During the meeting , Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina sought more British investment in the country , especially in the Special Economic Zones ( SEZs ) , emphasised establishing food processing industries in the SEZs . <p> Sheikh Hasina said the relations between Bangladesh and Britain are traditional and excellent , and mentioned that there are similarities between the Labour Party and Bangladesh Awami League on various policies . <p> About the large number of British Bangladeshis and Bangladeshi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the economies of both the countries . On climate change issues , she mentioned that Bangladesh is the most vulnerable to climate change . " Bangladesh has got many pledges for climate fund , but received a very negligible amount , " she said adding that the country has taken various programmes to face the adverse impacts of climate change with its own resources . <p> The three British MPs said there are huge Bangladeshis living in their constituencies . About the three Bangladesh-origin British MPs , including Prime Minister 's niece Tulip Siddiq , they highly appreciated them and said they are respected in Britain as well .
