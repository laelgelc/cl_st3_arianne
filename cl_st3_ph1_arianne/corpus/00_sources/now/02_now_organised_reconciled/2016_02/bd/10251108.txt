<h> ' Adoption of tech necessary to mitigate emerging adversities ' <p> FE Report <p> Adoption and transformation of technology is necessary to mitigate the emerging adversities of climate change effect on the country 's agricultural production , speakers at a seminar said on Sunday . They also stressed the need for achieving the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) through changing the existing food habit and application of new technologies in the context of the climate change . Their observations came at the seminar on sharing of final evaluation report of the project " Promoting Climate Adaptive Technology ( PCAT ) " , implemented by Centre for Climate Change and Environmental Research ( C3ER ) , which was held at BRAC Centre in the city . Professor Emeritus Dr Ainun Nishat presented the research findings at the seminar while Director General , Seed Wing , and Additional Secretary , Ministry of Agriculture Anwar Faruque , Director and Head of News Channel i Shykh Seraj , Senior Director , Strategy , Communication and Empowerment of BRAC Asif Saleh and Program Head , Agriculture and Food Security of BRAC @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the discussion . In his research paper Dr Nishat said people of southern part of the country suffer social , environmental and economic vulnerabilities because of climate change . The research paper suggested a number of strategies including introduction of proper weather monitoring system , adoption of alternative cropping pattern , prevention of illegal entry of seeds from neighbouring countries , implementation of water saving and irrigation technologies and monitoring of the price of the crops etc to maintain food production . Mr Anwar Faruque said along with government , all non-governmental organisations have to come forward to invest , implement , advocate and communicate the climate adaptation technologies among the farmers . He emphasised the need for two-way communication between policymakers and farmers to ensure sustainable food security in the country .
