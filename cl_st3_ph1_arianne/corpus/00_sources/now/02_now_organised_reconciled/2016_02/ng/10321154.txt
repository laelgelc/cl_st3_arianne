<h> Nigeria , Others Gear Up for 2nd UN Environment Assembly in Kenya <p> Governmental consultations in preparation for the second UN Environment Assembly ( UNEA ) - due to take place in Nairobi , Kenya next May - kicked off on Monday , February 15 with the convening of the Open Ended Committee of Permanent Representatives to the UN Environment Programme ( UNEP ) . <p> The committee , which is made up of 300 delegates representing all UN Member States and major groups , will consider key items on the UNEA-2 agenda with focus on furthering action on delivering the environmental dimension of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development . <p> The committee will also review a global thematic report on ' Healthy Environment , Healthy People ' according to a UNEP release . Nigeria , alongside many other UN member states , are currently battling environmental and climate change issues relating to erosion , desertification , flooding and deforestation . <p> For many , the creation of UNEA is the coming of age of the environment as a world issue , as it places environmental @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ security , finance , health and trade for the first time . The first UNEA meeting took place in Nairobi in June 2014 . <p> UN Under-Secretary-General and Executive Director of UNEP Achim Steiner said , " 2016 has been called the year for implementation and delivery . UNEA-2 is our chance to show the world UNEP 's readiness and commitment to achieving the Sustainable Development Goals through our programme of work . " <p> " This OECPR is a vital forum in itself - a landmark in UNEP 's journey , and one that will help determine the organisation 's direction and place in the global arena . <p> " The resolutions , decisions and outcomes that you prepare here will put new and emerging issues on environment firmly on the global radar , sending important signals to the global community about how to fully address this major pillar of an integrated sustainable development agenda . The work you do this week will position UNEA to as the International Environment Rendez-vous for all , and lay the groundwork for UNEP 's contribution to the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ first major global conferences taking place since the adoption of the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda and the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> The meeting will review UNEP 's Medium Term Strategy , its Programme of Work and budget which constitute UNEP 's capacity , through its strategy , business model and financial resources , to contribute to delivering the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda and the Paris climate agreement . <p> A package of draft resolutions will also be reviewed on topics that range from the illegal trade in wildlife to chemicals and waste and marine plastic debris . Other key topics include mobilising resources for sustainable investments and forced displacement and the environment . <p> UNEA will also consider the new stakeholder engagement policy , which will help to determine how civil society will be able to contribute in solving urgent environmental challenges and take advantage of environmental opportunities . <p> Steiner said , " UNEP 's stakeholders are critical to its work , and you will see that in action at UNEA-2 . UNEA-2 will be preparing critical outputs for ECOSOC on the delivery of the 2030 Agenda . <p> A new report entitled ' Healthy Environment , Healthy People ' will also be reviewed prior to submission to ministers at UNEA 2 . The report summarises the links between environmental quality and human health and wellbeing . <p> The theme also addresses many Sustainable Development Goals , emphasising UNEP 's integrated approach to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment links , including inequality , migration , unplanned urbanisation , unhealthy lifestyles , and unsustainable patterns of consumption and production .
