<h> Kiribati 's Tong considering international climate change job <p> The outgoing president of Kiribati Anote Tong says he has not finished his advocacy work on climate change and he is considering taking up an international role on the issue . <p> Photo : RNZI / Koro Vaka'uta <p> Mr Tong 's presidency comes to an end next month as he has run the maximum three terms allowed in his country . <p> He has made his mark telling the world how his low-lying country is at the forefront of nations confronted by rising seas and changing weather patterns . <p> He said he has had some job offers and was considering a role directly involving the region . <p> " I have no intention of being so fully engaged again . I think I 've had that . But I 'm very willing to use the momentum that I 've been able to gather . I think the mistake is to think we 've done it . We have n't by any means . "
