<h> And the award for sanctimonious acceptance speech of the year goes to ... <p> OSCARS night is upon us : a night for listening to the profound utterings of those Oracles known as Academy Award nominees . Some may recall that last year 's awards were the target of a red carpet campaign launched by Amy Poehler , with the hashtag #askhermore . Unlike male actors , said the campaigners , female stars tend to get asked frivolous , demeaning questions about their sparkly frocks , rather than being quizzed about politics or acting technique . It was time for Hollywood 's goddesses to be taken as seriously as the gods . So , at the awards , we got Reese Witherspoon telling us how hard it is being a woman in Hollywood , Patricia Arquette saying she had n't got round to doing her nails because she 'd been too busy working on her eco-sanitation project and Julianne Moore being asked questions about her role as an Alzheimer 's sufferer in Still Alice . <p> This Oscar night , I would like to propose an alternative @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ them " I mean the male gods as well as the goddesses -- less about the environment , the war in Syria , the Paris terrorist attacks , LGBTI rights , or their pet cause .
