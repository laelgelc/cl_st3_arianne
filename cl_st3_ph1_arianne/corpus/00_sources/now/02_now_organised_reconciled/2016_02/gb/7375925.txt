<p> What an interesting article about the many nations represented by shop owners in Narborough Road ( " The Nations of Shopkeepers " , Mercury , February 3 ) . <p> People learning from and helping each other . Success resulting from these qualities as well as determination , endurance and a sense of " all being in it together " . No regeneration money , just " people making it work " . <p> What a credit to Narborough Road and to Leicester . <p> In May 2015 , Leicester CND ran a People 's Ballot in Narborough Road . We asked people to vote yes or no to one question : Do you think the UK should replace its nuclear weapons system ? <p> Fifty-nine per cent of people said No , only 25 per cent said Yes , while 16 per cent were n't sure . <p> We , too , found Narborough Road a vibrant and interesting area . We did reflect on how many of those we spoke to would actually have a vote in UK elections . <p> Based on our ballot @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ MP , the MP for Narborough Road should be voting against the replacement of Trident , at a cost of about ? 100 billion , when parliament votes in 2016 . <p> Just think of the jobs , health and social care , housing , education , and climate change mitigation you could buy for that . <p> The Government 's National Security Strategy identifies international terrorism , cyber-attacks and natural hazards as greater threats than nuclear war .
