<p> MORE Scots support the renewables industry than any other part of the energy sector , a study has found . <p> A new poll found seven in ten people want the next Scottish Government to prioritise green power generation . <p> Nearly three-quarters of those quizzed , 70 per cent , said they want to see more energy produced from renewables such as wind , solar , wave and tidal power . <p> Some 42 per cent of those questioned said the next government should not prioritise building new fossil fuel stations or extending the life of existing facilities . <p> Industry body Scottish Renewables published the poll today on the first day of its annual conference in Edinburgh . <p> Chief executive Niall Stuart said : " The poll suggests that the people of Scotland continue to be strongly behind the growth of renewable energy , with support for the sector way ahead of any other . <p> " Just months after the Paris climate change agreement , the poll also shows clear support for Scotland 's next government to prioritise policies to reduce greenhouse gas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy needs , most people said they would prefer to install solar photovoltaic panels , followed by solar thermal panels and wind turbines . <p> Of those who indicated they already had a renewable energy device installed , solar panels were most commonly featured along with biomass boilers and heat pumps . <p> Stuart added : " While only four per cent of the sample has small-scale renewables at home , there is clear appetite amongst householders to generate their own renewable energy . <p> " More than a third of those surveyed said they would consider solar panels to power or to heat their homes , and 20 per cent were interested in installing a wind turbine or biomass boiler . <p> " We will be continuing our work on the Feed-in Tariff and Renewable Heat Incentive to ensure that smaller-scale renewables remain a viable investment for homeowners across Scotland .
