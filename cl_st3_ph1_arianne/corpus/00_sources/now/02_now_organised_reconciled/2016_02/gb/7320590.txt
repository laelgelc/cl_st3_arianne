<p> ECONOMY minister Edwina Hart has chaired a second meeting of the Tata Steel Task Force , just weeks after the company announced job losses . <p> She has outlined discussions held and actions agreed in a letter to members with an update of the first meeting . <p> " The purpose of my Statement today is to provide Members with an update following the second meeting of the Task Force , which took place yesterday afternoon , " she said . <p> " Once again , there was a strong attendance from Tata , Unite and Community Unions as well as organisations able to provide support for individuals and the business community . <p> " I was pleased to welcome Lord Bourne , Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Climate Change and Parliamentary Under Secretary of State for Wales , to the Task Force who confirmed his commitment to working closely with us to address issues as quickly as possible . <p> " The Vice Chancellor of Swansea University also joined the Task Force to ensure that higher education considerations are factored in to the planning . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ both Tata and the Trade Unions who were quick to acknowledge the positive and open working relationship between them . They also commended the speedy response of the work steams and the comprehensive approach being adopted . <p> " All four of the work streams have been quick to respond and have developed a clear view on their focus and priorities . They have defined the key initial actions that need to be taken forward . <p> " The Skills and Training work stream has focussed on the support that will be provided to the employees of Tata Steel directly impacted by the announcement . Of central importance is how to maintain as much of the workforce as possible in the area . Timing is a key consideration as the formal consultation is underway but the work stream has planned to work closely with Tata on a number of key actions . These include the provision of a one stop shop for support and advice to employees and the development of a single coordinated route for vacancies to be communicated to interested employees . <p> " The work stream has confirmed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ announcement and will include agency staff . There will be close working with the Business and Supply Chain work stream on the support needed for employees of affected supply chain companies . <p> " The Business and Supply Chain work stream has quickly established a small team to begin the work of contacting the supply chain and offering support . This team comprises members of Industry Wales , Neath Port Talbot County Borough Council and Welsh Government Officials . <p> " As this is a live commercial situation , I am limited in what I can say about the supply chain companies but I can assure Members that this work is commencing on a confidential basis . Contact is being made with other Local Authorities across South Wales , given the breadth of the supply chain . <p> " The work stream has completed the first draft of an Enterprise Zone submission and my Officials will be working with them to develop the detail of this . I have received a reply from the Chancellor in regard to the potential for enhanced capital allowances . They will be looking at how the support needs to be aligned so that it is easily accessible and meets people 's needs .
