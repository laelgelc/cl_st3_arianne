<h> Kids get on average a single hour of ( often wrong ) instruction on the subject . <p> Though roughly 95 percent of scientists agree that climate change is caused by humans , you might not know it if you were learning about the environment in middle school or high school . In a recent randomized study of thousands of science teachers , a group of US researchers found that nearly a third of teachers tell students that the current observed trends in global climate change are " natural . " <p> Published today in the journal Science , the results of the study reveal that science education on the subject is unevenly distributed . Teachers are all over the map when it comes to what they 're teaching about climate change , with 30 percent telling students that " recent global warming ' is likely due to natural causes , ' " and another 12 percent not emphasizing potential human causes of climate change .
