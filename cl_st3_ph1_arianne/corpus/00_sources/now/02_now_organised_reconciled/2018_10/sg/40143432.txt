<h> New World Bank fund to insure against climate disasters <p> Heavy monsoon rains predicted for the months ahead threaten to make a bad situation worse , and spread mosquito-borne diseases such as malaria and dengue <p> The World Bank on Friday launched a new $150 million fund intended to help insure vulnerable countries against natural disasters and the increasing risk of climate change-linked crises . <p> The announcement follows a string of recent natural disasters across the world including record storms and a series of deadly quakes in Indonesia , where the Bank and International Monetary Fund are currently meeting . <p> The so-called Global Risk Financing Facility ( GRiF ) , set up with money from Germany and Britain , will finance the creation of disaster insurance and other risk mechanisms so funds are available immediately when crises hit . <p> " What we are seeking to do ... is to change the whole system so countries can rely on funding when necessary that 's reliable , that 's adequate , that 's rapid , " said Matthew Rycroft , permanent secretary for Britain 's Department for International Development . <p> " If they combine that with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ very significant benefit , " he added at a panel announcing the fund . <p> The World Bank and other international institutions already have several mechanisms in place to help nations adapt to the risk of a changing environment . <p> But the new fund is intended to bolster those programmes as the effects of climate change intensify . <p> " It 's too little , but it 's not too late , we really need to build the momentum ... change fundamentally the way we do our investment , " said Laura Tuck , the World Bank 's vice president for sustainable development .
