<h> ISRO successfully tests engine for Chandrayaan-2 , opens new center <p> The E6 engine ( the fifth hardware of CE-20 integrated engine ) was successfully hot tested for 25 seconds at High Altitude Test facility , ISRO Propulsion Complex ( IPRC ) , Mahendragiri on October 11 , 2018 . The test demonstrated steady state operation of engine and performance of all engine subsystems were observed to be normal during the hot test , said an ISRO press release . <p> The upper stage of GSLV MK-III vehicle is powered by Cryogenic Engine ( CE ) -20 which develops a nominal thrust of 186.36 kN with a specific impulse of 442 seconds in vacuum . The engine operates on gas generator cycle using LOX / LH2 propellants combination . <p> The major subsystems of the engine are thrust chamber , gas generator , LOX and LH2 turbo pumps , igniters , thrust &amp; mixture ratio control systems , start-up system , control components and pyro valves .
