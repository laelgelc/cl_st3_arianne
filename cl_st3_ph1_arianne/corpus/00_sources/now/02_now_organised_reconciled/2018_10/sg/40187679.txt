CO2 is by far the largest factor , accounting for roughly 50 per cent of the increase in " radiative forcing " since the year 1750 . CH4 accounts for 29 per cent , while N2O accounts for around 5 per cent . The remaining 16 per cent comes from other factors including carbon monoxide , black carbon and halocarbons such as CFCs . <p> Human emissions of greenhouse gases have increased atmospheric concentrations of CO2 , CH4 and N2O to their highest levels in at least a few million years -- if not longer .
