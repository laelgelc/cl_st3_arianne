<h> Cambodia construction boom built on ' blood bricks ' and slavery : Report <p> PHNOM PENH ( THOMSON REUTERS FOUNDATION ) - Cambodia 's construction boom is built in part with " blood bricks " manufactured by modern-day slaves , including children , researchers said on Tuesday ( Oct 16 ) . <p> Poverty fuelled partly by climate change has pushed tens of thousands of Cambodian families into bonded labour , making bricks for buildings in the capital , Phnom Penh , according to a report by researchers from London 's Royal Holloway University . <p> " Kiln owners repay farmers ' debts and offer a consolidated loan . In return , farmers and their families are compelled to enter into debt bondage with the kiln owner until the loan is repaid . " <p> Families surveyed had agreed to pay back loans of between US$100 ( S$137 ) and US$4,000 .
