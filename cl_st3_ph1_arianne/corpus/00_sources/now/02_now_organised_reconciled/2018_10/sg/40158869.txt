With strains growing in transatlantic relations under Donald Trump 's presidency , Asia-Pacific is assuming significantly higher prominence across the EU . <p> Both sides have strong common political and economic interests , and the summit will see Brussels showcasing a major new initiative , shadowing China 's Belt and Road project , to improve connectivity with Asia-Pacific .
