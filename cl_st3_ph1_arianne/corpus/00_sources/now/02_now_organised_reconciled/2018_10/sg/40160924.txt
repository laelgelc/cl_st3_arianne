Also more people live in vulnerable mobile homes and tornadoes are more likely to happen at night in those places , he said . <p> Even though Texas , Kansas and Oklahoma get many more tornadoes , the four deadliest states for tornadoes are Alabama , Missouri , Tennessee and Arkansas , according to the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration . <p> " More folks are generally at risk because of that eastward shift , " Gensini said . <p> Because tornadoes sometimes go undercounted , especially in the past and in less populous areas , scientists do n't like to study trends by using counts of tornadoes .
