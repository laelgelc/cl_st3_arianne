<p> GEORGE TOWN : The Penang government today signed a memorandum of understanding ( MOU ) with the United Nation 's Human Settlements Programme ( UN HABITAT ) . <p> The MoU is to develop a common monitoring framework for projects implemented in the state to be in line with the latter 's sustainable development goals ( SDG ) . <p> The five-year MOU is the first of its kind between a Malaysian state government and the UN HABITAT . <p> It involves different phases of cooperation based on both current and future needs . <p> The state government has committed USD$250,000 ( about RM1 million ) towards the said initiative . <p> Chief Minister Chow Kon Yeow said , in line with the state 's aspiration to achieve its Vision 2030 to transform Penang into a " Family Focused Green and Smart State that Inspires the Nation " , both parties had agreed to collaborate as both share in the common objectives to improve livability , upgrading the economy and empower communities . <p> He said the state
