It 's an agreement with Mexico and Canada that has generous protections for Republican-aligned industries , including Big Pharma and fossil fuel polluters ; no meaningful environmental protections ; weak consumer safeguards ; and no mechanism to enforce the moderately improved labor standards contemplated by the deal . <p> The global trading order built over the past generation has been a comprehensive effort to emancipate global corporations from the laws and regulations devised by the world 's democracies to protect citizens from corporate abuse .
