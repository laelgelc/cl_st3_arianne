<h> You are here <h> Electricity use down at MAS Building <p> Thu , Oct 11 , 2018 - 5:50 AM <p> Green and smart designs , energy saving features were incorporated into the 33-year-old building 's infrastructure renewal plans . <p> THE Monetary Authority of Singapore ( MAS ) is another recipient of the Best Energy Efficiency Practices Award in the public sector category this year . Its building , completed in 1985 , is an early adopter of energy efficient practices , being among the first to implement electronic ballast for its lightings . <p> Through concerted efforts by staff and support from management , MAS recorded a 23.5 per cent saving in electricity in the financial year 2017/2018 from the financial year 2013/2014 baseline . <p> MAS embarked on a journey , starting in 2012 , to rejuvenate its 33-year-old building to meet Green Mark Platinum rating . Green and smart designs and energy saving features were incorporated in the building infrastructure renewal plans eg lift &amp; power upgrading , office renovations and central air conditioning system .
