<p> Sikkim beat 51 other nominations from across the world ; Brazil , Denmark and Quito in Ecuador shared the second spot . <p> Sikkim has earned the badge of being the world 's first 100 per cent organic state . The state , which walked away with the Future Policy Award , 2018 , at a UN ceremony in Rome , also received an " Oscar " for best policies at the ceremony after bolstering its efforts to attain sustainable development goals . <p> The award was presented at the headquarters of the Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) on Monday . The state competed against 51 other nominees from 25 countries . The award , Future Policy Award 2018 , is given to a state with world 's best laws and policies promoting agro-ecology . <p> Chief Minister Kumar Chamling , Sikkim Democratic Front MP Prem Das Rai , and Indian Ambassador Reenat Sandhu received the award on the behalf of the state . <p> " Let us build an organic world together , " he said while accepting the award from FAO 's Deputy Director Maria Helena Semedo , who praised the state for setting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said to have benefited over 66 000 farming families and boosted incoming tourism .
