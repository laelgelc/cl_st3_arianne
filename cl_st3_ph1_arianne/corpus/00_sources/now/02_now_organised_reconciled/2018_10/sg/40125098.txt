<h> Poland expects U.S.to be " very active " at global climate talks <p> WARSAW ( Reuters ) - The United States will be " very active " at December 's U.N . climate discussions even though Donald Trump pulled out of the global accord , the incoming Polish chairman of the talks said . <p> " The U.S. delegation will appear in Katowice and for sure will be very active , that was confirmed during the talks I had on this subject in Washington , " Michal Kurtyka told Reuters . <p> Kurtyka , a former Polish deputy energy minister , said that although the United States had said it would quit the 2015 Paris Agreement , this would " formally only be possible in 2020 " . <p> While the accord set a sweeping goal of ending the fossil fuel era this century , its text was vague on details . <p> A U.N . report on Monday warned that temperatures are likely to rise by 1.5 degrees Celsius between 2030 and 2052 if global warming continues at its current pace and if the world fails to take rapid and unprecedented measures to stem the increase . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ IPCC ) met last week in Incheon , South Korea to finalise the report , which is seen as the main scientific guide for government policymakers on how to implement the Paris Agreement . <p> This will form a key part of the agenda for environment ministers from almost 200 governments when they meet in Katowice , the heart of Poland 's coal-producing region , Silesia . <p> TURNING POINT <p> Kurtyka said that a U.N . meeting in Bangkok in September was a " turning point " when it comes to how the negotiating parties will try to reach a " good result " in Katowice , but a number of sticking points still has to be resolved , including how to finance reducing climate change . <p> A surge in carbon emission prices had influenced the Bangkok talks , Kurtyka said .
