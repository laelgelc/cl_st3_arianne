<p> Good news and bad news arrived this week from the world 's top climate change experts . Good news : they can tell us in agonizing detail why the world should really , really keep the rise in global warming to less than 1.5 degrees Celsius . Bad news : the 132 authors of the 700-page report offer many ideas but no feasible plan for how to do that . As the International Panel on Climate Change 's co-chair put it , " One thing the report did not aspire to do is answer the question of feasibility . " So we can call it the Beach Boys Report ? " Would n't it be nice ... " <p> The 2015 Paris Agreement set an overall goal of staying below 2 degrees Celsius of global warming . However , the combination of the deal 's country-by-country goals would not accomplish that , and no major country is on track to meet its goals anyway .
