The Penang government should increase its budget to strengthen hill slopes , a local civil group said today after a landslide at Bukit Kukus in Paya Terubong last Friday killed nine workers and injured four others . <p> Calling itself AnakPinang , the group suggested the RM10 million budgeted this year to reinforce slopes may be insufficient . <p> " This year , RM10 million was allocated for hill strengthening . The state should allocate more for next year 's budget , " it said it a statement . <p> AnakPinang wants the state government and the relevant authorities to introduce better mitigation measures for developments near hilly terrain . <p> Group member Joshua Woo also mooted a dedicated state department in charge of strengthening slopes , adding that better planning is needed to tackle climate change effects on Penang . <p> " There should be better monitoring and enforcement of these projects @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ AnakPinang also welcomed Penang Chief Minister Chow Kon Yeow 's instructions for all state agencies to evaluate every ongoing and upcoming infrastructure projects in the state . <p> " Penang must continue to progress through development to become a more economically established and socially resilient state , " it said . <p> The group stressed that the state should not cancel all of its planned infrastructure projects but instead , take critical steps to ensure safety measures are adhered to . <p> " Since Penang island has limited flat land , we can not rule out necessary development that may involve hillslope , " it said . <p> They also urged the state government to conduct thorough investigation on the Bukit Kukus incident . <p> " The state needs to give the public an answer as to what went wrong in the project , " the group said . <p> Last Friday 's landslide occurred at the construction site of the Bukit Kukus paired roads project that is part of the Penang Transport Master Plan , just a week after 11 beams fell onto a slope at another site for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a landslide at a housing project in Tanjung Bungah killed 11 workers .
