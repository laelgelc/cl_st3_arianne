<h> Photographs that imagine a brighter future <h> Seaweed harvesting , carbon capture technology , and a hut made of plastic bottles -- these were the winning entries from a global photo competition to highlight inspiring ways to fight climate change . <p> Re-imagining a brighter future : entries of finalists of the photo challenge on display at the World Business Council for Sustainable Development Council Meeting gala dinner at Gardens by the Bay in Singapore .
