We wo n't have any more fights over this , " Bolsonaro , who vows to boost Brazil 's fragile economy , told a media conference four days after winning the October 7 first-round in the presidential race . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ beginning of the end for the Amazon , " his leftist rival in Sunday 's run-off , Fernando Haddad , said last week . <p> Geraldo Monteiro , a political science professor at Rio de Janeiro State University , said : " As he 's backed by the agrobusiness lobby in congress , which is really strong , Bolsonaro virtually wants to make the environment serve the interests of agrobusiness .
