This follows Mr Kurz 's three-day official visit to Singapore in August . <p> Advertisement <p> In Vienna , Mr Lee will meet and be hosted to lunch by Mr Kurz , after which both leaders will witness the signing of a memorandum of understanding ( MOU ) on Cooperation in the Area of Digitalisation and Information and Communications Technology . <p> This MOU covers the exchange of information and best practices in several areas , including digitalisation strategies , e-commerce and digital trade , as well as emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence . <p> Later in the day , Mr Lee will attend a dinner with Austrian business leaders . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Brussels to attend the 12th ASEM Summit from Thursday to Friday . <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <p> With an aim to strengthen links between Asia and Europe , the biennial summit will see leaders of 51 European and Asian countries discuss a wide range of global issues , such as trade and investment , climate change and security challenges . <p> When in Brussels , Mr Lee will also attend a leaders ' meeting between the EU and the Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) . Singapore is the ASEAN chair this year and the coordinator for ASEAN-EU dialogue relations . <p> On the sidelines of the ASEM summit , Mr Lee will sign the EU-Singapore Free Trade Agreement ( EUSFTA ) with European Council president Donald Tusk and European Commission president Jean-Claude Juncker . <p> Together , they will also witness the signing of the EU-Singapore Investment Protection Agreement ( EUSIPA ) and the EU-Singapore Partnership and Cooperation Agreement ( ESPCA ) .
