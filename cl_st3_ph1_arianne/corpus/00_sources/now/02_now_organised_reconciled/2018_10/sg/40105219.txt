<h> October 03 , 2018 <p> Friday , November 23 , 2018New Delhi <p> It has been a year of calamities . Fearsome floods , cataclysmic cyclones , and heat waves that had half the world with its backs to the wall . Against this backdrop of extreme weather incidents , the world will be meeting -- as it does every year -- to discuss and debate the next steps on how to save itself . The forthcoming 24th Conference of Parties ( CoP ) is important in that sense -- in it , nations would try to reach some decisions on how to implement the Paris Agreement fully . <p> Centre for Science and Environment ( CSE ) invites journalists working in India to a one-day National Media Briefing on current science , negotiations , issues and concerns in climate change . The Briefing will aim to explain , among other things , the 2018 IPCC report , the phenomenon of extreme weather events , the path that climate negotiations have taken till this year , and the expectations from the 24thCoP .
