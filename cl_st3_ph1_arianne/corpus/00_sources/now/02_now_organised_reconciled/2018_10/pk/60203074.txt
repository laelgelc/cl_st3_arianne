<h> Rs100.65b released under PSDP 2018-19 <p> ISLAMABAD - The government has released over Rs 100.65 billion under its Public Sector Development Programme ( PSDP ) 2018-19 for various ongoing and new schemes against the total allocations of Rs.675 billion . <p> The released funds include Rs 47.14 billion for federal ministries and Rs 18.455 billion for special areas , according to a latest data released by Ministry of Planning , Development and Reform .
