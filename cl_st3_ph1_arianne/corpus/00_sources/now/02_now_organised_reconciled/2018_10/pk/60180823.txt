<p> LAHORE -- The Punjab government has decided to shut down those brick-kilns , which have failed to convert their designs to new prescribed technology and are causing intensive air pollution due to huge emissions , from October 27 . <p> The decision has been taken in a meeting presided over by Smog Commission Chairman Dr Pervaiz Hasan . The meeting was attended by representatives of Brick-Kilns Association and Environment Protection Department ( EPD ) . <p> Previously , it was decided to shut down the brick-kilns from October 20 , but the date had been extended till Oct 27 after a request of brick-kilns association and consultations with Advisor to Prime Minister on Climate Change Amin Akram Khan . <p> The owners have been requested to convert their brick-kilns to new zig-zag technology during this period . The brick-kilns will have to be shut down in case of any abrupt weather change which could cause Smog , the owners were told . <p> The government has also imposed Section 144 from Oct 1st , banning the burning of substandard fuel including plastic , garbage , polythene bags in brick-kilns . <p> The factory owners have been warned that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not install smoke controlling equipment in their factories .
