<p> Zartaj emphasizes for public awareness on tree plantation , cleanliness <p> G+ <p> October 21 , 2018 <p> Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul has emphasized the need to raise public awareness on tree plantation and cleanliness . <p> She expressed these views while addressing a ceremony in connection with clean and green Pakistan campaign at Shakar Parian in Islamabad on Sunday . <p> The Minister said the country is facing environmental degradation , decreasing space of forests , pollution and other issues due to injudicious use of natural resources . <p> Zartaj Gul said federal government has started working on different projects to improve and conserve natural resources , including ten billion tree tsunami drive , improving underground water level and to overcome water and air pollution . <p> She said international community should provide financial and technical assistance to help Pakistan launch clean energy projects .
