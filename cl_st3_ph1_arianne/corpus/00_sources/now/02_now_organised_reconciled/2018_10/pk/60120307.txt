<h> Federal Cabinet : Six new ministers took the oath today <h> Islamabad : Prime Minister ( PM ) Imran Khan gave approval and six new ministers took the oath on Friday ( today ) . <p> Six new ministers took the oath today and will be included in the federal cabinet . Faisal Vawda , Ali Ameen Gandapur , Mohammad Mian Soomro , Azam Swati , Sahibzada Mehboob Sultan , and Zartaj Gul are six new ministers . <p> It is the 5th time that the federal cabinet is expanded in the last two months . <p> Earlier , it is said that Ali Ameen Gandapur will get portfolio of Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan Affairs , Mohammad Mian Soomro will get Privatization Commission , Azam Swati to be given Ministry of Science and Technology and Zartaj Gul to be Minister of State for Climate Change . <p> It is pertinent to mention that currently the federal cabinet is comprising of 38 members -- 24 federal ministers , 6 ministers of state and 4 advisers to Prime Minister . <p> Syed Faizanullah Hussainy is a senior content writer with writing expertise in different niches .
