<h> ' Pakistan facing major challenges in terms of harsh temperature owing to climate change ' <p> A delegation of World Bank led by its country director Patchamuthu Illangovan called on the Adviser to Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam Khan on Tuesday . <p> World Bank Operations Officer Amena Raja , World Bank Programme Leader Lixin Gu and World Bank Disaster Risk Management Senior Specialists Kamran Akbar and Ahsan Tehsin were the part of the delegation . <p> Both sides discussed the areas of mutual interest including cooperation in different plans to deal with climate change hazards . <p> The delegates said that quality of life in a number of areas in South Asia including Pakistan is dropping due to harsh temperature and other climate changes and the World Bank is especially focusing to mitigate these growing environmental issues . <p> The minister apprised the delegates that environment is the foremost priority agenda of our government and we are rigorously pursuing it since the day one . <p> " We have already initiated the 10 Billion Tsunami Tree Plantation Drive a month ago and now we have launched a Clean Green Pakistan initiative to bring behavioural @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Imran Khan . Growing seasons have been shifted , " they informed . <p> The minister further said that our Clean Green Pakistan initiative lays special focus on five major areas which include plantation , solid waste management , liquid waste management , availability of clean drinking water as 42 percent people of Pakistanis do not have access to clean water and lastly , hygiene . <p> The delegates informed the minister that World Bank was running a Green Development Programme in collaboration with the Punjab government to deal with pollution issues . The delegates expressed their willingness to cooperate with the Ministry of Climate Change to deal with solid waste management , agriculture and water related issues . <p> The adviser to PM on climate change said that water wastage is another gigantic problem which Pakistan is facing . <p> " We had no proper strategy to store flooded waters but our government in accordance with the vision of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ aptly use wet lands for this purpose .
