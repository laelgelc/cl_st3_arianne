<p> Advisor to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam Khan said in a statement that Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) has planted 4160 trees so far , adding that Ministry of Climate Change would continue close monitoring of the plantation sites till completion of commitment of 2.5 million trees in federal capital . He said that the civic body is bound to submit its quarterly report to the ministry . <p> He said that the Ministry of Climate Change is devising comprehensive , short , medium and long-term plan for rescuing capital city environment from degradation and necessary instructions have been passed to the Pakistan Environmental protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) and other concerned departments of the ministry to prepare working papers in this regard . <p> He said that provision of clean environment to the people is not only a responsibility but also a religious obligation and we are taking up these issues with relevant ministries .
