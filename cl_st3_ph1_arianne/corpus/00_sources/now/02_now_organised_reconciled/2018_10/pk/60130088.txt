<p> ISLAMABAD -- Prime Minister Imran Khan announced Monday a countrywide cleanliness campaign will be launched on October 13 as part of the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) government 's policy for a clean and green country . <p> While speaking in Islamabad , the premier said the countrywide five-year campaign includes " changing mindsets " towards cleanliness , creating dumping sites for waste , provision of public toilets , and starting a volunteer scouts programme to help support the campaign . <p> PM Khan said that the nationwide call to action would be launched on the coming Saturday and that the chief ministers , ministers , president , as well as governors from around Pakistan will take part .
