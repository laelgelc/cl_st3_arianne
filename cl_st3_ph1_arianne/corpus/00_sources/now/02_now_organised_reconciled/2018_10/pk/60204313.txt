<p> Prime Minster 's Advisor on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam has said environmental protection and human rights are top priority of the government . <p> Talking to a delegation of the European Parliamentarians in Islamabad on Monday , he said the government is pursuing 10 billion tree plantation in next five years under the program of Clean Green Pakistan . <p> Malik Amin Aslam said the government is also seriously concerned about the menace of smog coming from India and we will raise the issue with New Delhi to prevent the burning of crop waste . <p> He said we are looking for new methods to alleviate environmental degradation and cooperation from NGOs and Civil Society is very imperative in the implementation of protection and preservation of environment . <p> Highlighting the potential of renewable energy in Pakistan , Malik Amin Aslam emphasized that the government is focused in advancing the production of renewable energy as the geographical location and ecological environment are very conducive to solar and wind energy production in Pakistan .
