<p> Asian Parliamentary Assembly Standing Committee on Political Affairs has underlined the need for synergizing efforts and resources for peace , development and prosperity in Asia . <p> The Gwadar Statement issued after two-day meeting of the Committee at Gwadar on Tuesday said the members of the Assembly should take their cooperation forward . <p> It said that while our Asian vision is focused on trade , economic and energy markets , it also carries a much-needed heed to help adapt to climate change and preserve the environment . <p> The statement recognized that through bilateral as well as multilateral synergized efforts , we can check the challenges of climate change and give a better future to our coming generations . <p> It said that to mark this historic gathering , the distinguished delegates also lent a hand in Pakistan 's national drive towards tree plantation campaign . <p> Earlier addressing the concluding session , Acting President of Pakistan Sadiq Sanjrani said Asian countries should accord top priority to address issues of human rights violations and hurdles to peace , prosperity and inclusive development . <p> Leader of the House in Senate Shibli Faraz said that extensive deliberations and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ build upon in future APA meetings and realizing an Asian Parliament . <p> The Committee approved the draft resolutions on good governance , role of law and judicial empowerment , good parliamentary practices and building prosperity in Asia through friendship and cooperation . The Committee also discussed in detail and approved the draft resolution on Asian Parliaments ' unwavering support for the Palestinian people . <p> Over one hundred parliamentarians from twenty-six Asian countries , including China , Saudi Arabia and Iran , participated in the conference .
