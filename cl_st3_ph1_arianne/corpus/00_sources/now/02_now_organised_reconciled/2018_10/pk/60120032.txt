<h> 6 more ministers to be inducted in Federal Cabinet today <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan has once again given approval to expansion in the federal cabinet and six more ministers will be inducted the cabinet today ( Friday ) . This is the fifth time that the cabinet has been expanded in the last two months . <p> President Dr Arif Alvi will administer the oath to the newly-appointed ministers at 11:00am today . Ali Ameen Gandapur , Faisal Vawda , Azam Swati , Mohammad Mian Soomro and Sahibzada Mehboob Sultan will be sworn-in as federal ministers while Zartaj Gul will take oath as minister of state . <p> Sources said that Mohammad Mian Soomro will be given portfolio of Privatisation Commission and Ali Ameen Gandapur will get portfolio of Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan Affairs . Azam Swati will be given Ministry of Science and Technology and Zartaj Gul will be Minister of State for Climate Change . <p> Currently the federal cabinet is consist of 32 members and with the inclusion of six new ministers , the strength of federal cabinet will reach 38 , including 24 federal ministers , six state minister , four advisers to PM @ @ @ @ @
