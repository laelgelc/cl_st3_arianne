<p> KARACHI : Careem , an App-based transportation network company has joined hands with World Wildlife Fund-Pakistan ( WWF ) to plant 180,000 mangrove plants along Sindh and Balochistan coast to mitigate the impacts of climate change . <p> With this initiative , Careem became part of WWF-Pakistan 's recently launched ' Rang Do Pakistan ' campaign . Officials of Careem and WWF jointly planted 50 mangrove saplings at WWF-Pakistan 's wetland centre near the Sandspit beach . According to WWF-Pakistan officials , 180,000 plants would be planted in next one year at Sandspit beach and Sonmyani of Balochistan . <p> WWF-Pakistan 's Sindh and Balochistan regional head told Daily Times that as a part of this collaboration , Careem will pay Rs 20 for each plant . This means that the company would pay Rs 3.6 million ( m ) for 180,000 mangrove saplings . <p> " This initiative will not only help restore the lost mangrove forest cover but will also build resilience of the local communities to tackle climate change and other natural disasters particularly soil erosion and coastal flooding in the area , " said Khan .
