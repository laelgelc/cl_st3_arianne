When the quality of land is deteriorated , 25% of additional fertilizers should be applied which is economically not feasible . This problem can be easily sort out by the use of nanofertilizers . <h> Applications of nanomaterials <p> Nanomaterials are applied in small quantities , so the amount of toxic active ingredients can be reduced , nutrient losses after fertilization can be minimized and water and nutrient use efficiency can be enhanced on sustainable basis .
