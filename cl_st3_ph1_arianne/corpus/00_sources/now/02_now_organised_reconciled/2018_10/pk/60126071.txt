<p> Federal government 's Green Pakistan programme for offsetting impact of climate change is going along quite steadily and the private sector 's contribution in its implementation is quite appreciably increasing gradually . <p> Besides federal government institutions , more and more private sector organisations as well as educational institutions are joining this programme and with every passing day , more and more plants are being planted throughout the country to ensure a greener environment under the Green Pakistan programme which aims to plant 10 billion trees in the next five years . <p> Subway has also taken a commendable initiative in this regard under which 10 thousand saplings have already been planted , while more are still being planted in schools of Faisalabad every day on the basis of planting one plant for every one sub sold . <p> Subway Business Development Agent Mubashar Chohan said on Saturday that this is only a beginning and with its successful completion , it will be extended to more and more educational institutions in other districts in due time .
