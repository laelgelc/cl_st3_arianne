<h> Americans Nordhaus , Romer win 2018 Nobel Economics Prize <p> Americans William Nordhaus and Paul Romer won the 2018 Nobel Economics Prize for work in integrating climate change and technological innovation into economic analysis , the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences said on Monday . <p> Nordhaus , of Yale University , was the first person to create a quantitative model that described the interplay between the economy and the climate , the academy said . Romer , of New York University 's Stern School of Business , has shown how economic forces govern the willingness of firms to produce new ideas and innovations , laying the foundations for a new model for development , known as endogenous growth theory . <p> " Their findings have significantly broadened the scope of economic analysis by constructing models that explain how the market economy interacts with nature and knowledge , " the academy said in statement .
