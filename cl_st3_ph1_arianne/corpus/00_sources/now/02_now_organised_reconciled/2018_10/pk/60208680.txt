<p> The report presents a sobering picture of the impact of human activity on the world 's wildlife , forests , oceans , rivers and climate , underlining the rapidly closing window for action and the urgent need for the global community to collectively rethink and redefine how we value , protect and restore nature . <p> The report highlights that over recent decades , human activity has also severely impacted the habitats and natural resources wildlife and humanity depend on such as oceans , forests , coral reefs , wetlands and mangroves . <p> While shedding light on the extent and impact
