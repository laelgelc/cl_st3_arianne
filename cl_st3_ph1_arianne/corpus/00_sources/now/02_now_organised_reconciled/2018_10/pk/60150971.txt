Today , around 50% of all graduates through the UoL 's distance and flexible learning programmes , across 180 countries , are women . In celebration of 150 years of educating women , both in the UK and worldwide , the UoL organised a month of ' conversations ' around the world on the subject of women , higher education and equality in the workplace . Overall , there are more females than males in higher education in almost all regions .
