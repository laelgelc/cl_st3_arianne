<p> LAHORE : The provincial capital , Lahore is among the most polluted cities in the world and urgent action needs to be taken to tackle this issue . The report , which was presented in the presence of environmental journalists and researchers , presented a sobering picture of the impact of human activities on the world 's wildlife , forests , oceans , rivers and climate .
