<h> Four more ministers to be inducted in federal cabinet : reports <p> ISLAMABAD : The federal cabinet is expected to induct four more ministers , according to media reports . <p> According to details , Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) leader Faisal Vawda and Muhammad Mian Soomro are expected to be inducted in the federal cabinet . <p> Soomro had earlier declined an offer of becoming a state minister for privatisation when the summary of the cabinet division was revealed to him in this regard only a few hours prior to the ceremony , which was scheduled on September 11 . <p> Moreover , Zartaj Gul is being considered for the post of state minister for climate change , while the name of Ali Muhammad Khan is also being considered for inclusion in the federal cabinet . <p> The final list of ministers will be passed under Prime Minister Imran Khan 's review on it . <p> Out of the four , two people are will be inducted as federal ministers while the other two will be inducted as state ministers .
