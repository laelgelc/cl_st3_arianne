<h> National Bank endeavours to make Pakistan greener <p> MARDAN : National Bank of Pakistan ( NBP ) , in a bid to mitigate the impact of climate change and global warming , had planted saplings at Mardan as part of national tree plantation drive of the government . <p> Service Quality Group Head Shaukat Mahmood was welcomed by Abdul Wali Khan University VC Dr Khurshid Ahmed at the varsity , where he formally inaugurated ' NBP Green Pakistan Drive ' by planting a tree at the premises and donating 5,000 fruit and other trees . <p> A proposal of establishment of Research Data Centre and sponsorship of NBP was also discussed at the forum . The planned centre access would be given to research students and all major universities of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa . The research centre will be the first and only RDC where students could access real-time data of international financial institutions .
