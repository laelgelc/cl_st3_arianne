<h> Why loneliness needs its own portfolio <h> By Fiona Patten <p> 22 October 2018 -- 6:47pm <p> Aloneness is a state of mind that feels good . It 's a place where you feel complete in yourself and no number of friends , family or well-wishers is really going to add to that . <p> Loneliness is almost the opposite . It 's a state of mind where you feel anything but complete and where a yawning lack of everything , including people and human companionship , opens up like a huge black hole inside you . <p> Loneliness should be taken far more seriously as a societal health issue .
