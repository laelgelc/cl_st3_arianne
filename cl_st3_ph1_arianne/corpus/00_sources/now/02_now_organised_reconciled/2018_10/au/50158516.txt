Design alumni are thrilled that retrospectives of their work will be held in Sydney next year , at the Museum of Contemporary Art ( MCA ) . <p> The MCA recently announced the major solo exhibitions by Laurence and Gladwell as part of its 2019 program . <p> Both artists graduated from UNSW Art &amp;
