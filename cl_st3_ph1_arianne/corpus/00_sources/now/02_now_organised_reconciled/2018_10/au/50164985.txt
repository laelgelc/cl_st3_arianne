<h> Pacific central to Labor 's foreign policy <p> Opposition Bill Shorten is set to lay out Labor 's foreign policy in a speech at the Lowy Institute . <p> Australia 's relationship with its Pacific neighbours should be driven by a genuine desire to help the region , Labor says . <p> Opposition Leader Bill Shorten will outline Labor 's approach to foreign policy on Monday at an address at the Lowy Institute in Sydney . <p> " Our goal will not be the strategic denial of others but rather the economic betterment of the 10 million people of the Pacific islands themselves , " he will say . <p> China 's presence is growing in the Pacific , yet the Morrison government remains tight-lipped over whether recent initiatives , such as a pact between Australia , the US and Japan to invest in the region , are to challenge China . <p> If elected at the next federal election , a Labor government would not only boost foreign aid , but encourage private firms to invest in infrastructure projects to boost development in the region . <p> " My vision is for Australia to actively facilitate concessional @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ projects through a government-backed infrastructure investment bank , " Mr Shorten will say . <p> " Our neighbours in the Pacific are looking for partners to help them build infrastructure . And as prime minister , I intend to make sure they look to Australia first . " <p> Labor would also reinstate a minister for pacific affairs and international development . <p> The position was held by Senator Concetta Fierravanti-Wells under the Turnbull government , but was made an assistant minister role under Prime Minister Scott Morrison . <p> Australia should put more effort into recognising and understanding the diversity of languages , culture , laws and stages of development in the region , Mr Shorten will say . <p> " We will not define our Pacific neighbours by their smallness in size and population . <p> " But by the greatness of the ocean they are custodians of - and that we share with them . " <p> A Labor government would help the region address climate change and the threat of rising oceans to their existence , he will add .
