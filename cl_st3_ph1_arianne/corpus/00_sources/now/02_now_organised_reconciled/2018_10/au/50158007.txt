<h> The truth about Wentworth noone 's willing to admit <p> I watched weekend visitors to Parramatta Park sharing the public barbecue between their shwarmas , chicken Tikkas , and good old Aussie snags . <p> While keeping an eye on their kids on the playground , folks generously exchanged food across backgrounds , and politely complimented each other 's cooking . As things wrapped up , a lady in a sari , another in a hijab , and red-bearded lad with Eels stubby shorts and full-sleeve tatts cleaned up the hotplate for the next lot of punters . <p> Here in West-worth , rather than Wentworth , we 're generally nice and normal . We 're about getting along and getting ahead . <p> The way we live shows how out of touch much of the commentary about the recent by-election was and why our pollies should ignore it . <p> Indeed , new records were set for oxygen-free ascents to the highest heights of hyperbole . Competitor commentators poured out paragraph after paragraph about winners and losers , factions , and this interest versus that intrigue . <p> Parramatta Park is the site of many cultural events @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and has public barbecues that visitors regularly use . Another announces that the certain defeat of the Government at the next election - even though he has no access to any marginal seat polling and may not have noticed that Rose Bay is different to Rockhampton . <p> Others say Kerryn Phelps ' victory shows the need for " more progressive " ( whatever that means ) policies about Nauru or climate change - even though neither of those issues is now or has been in the top 10 of polled community concerns for more than 10 years . <p> It 's unaccountable . It distorts for convenience rather than distils for clarity . It inaccurately generalises from the specific . It 's utter bollocks and it 's dangerously skewing our politics . <p> When a senior ABC journo sings political satire on The Insiders , we 're hearing the death @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The rotten replacement is a new political entertainment industry with its own Kim Kardashian 's of the left and Lady Gaga 's of the right . As far as by-elections go , Wentworth was part of the trend of recent times . People tend to strongly vote against sitting governments . Ho hum . But that 's too dull a yarn . <p> And , the truth is that there is no empirical evidence of a situation where a single issue has substantially changed people 's votes in several generations of Australian politics . They take the job interview approach . Is the job applicant competent , stable and decent ? Do they have a track-record of getting the job done ? Will they stick around ? Will they be honest and treat their co-workers respectfully ? <p> It 's our default setting - pragmatism and resilience - that defines life in western Sydney and otherwise , and helps us peaceably live and work with each other across vast cultural differences . <p> Our everyday experience is totally different from much of the confected conflict we read and hear about in media terms - be it about politics , gender or religion . They 're looking for a nice present for the niece 's wedding and whether the groom is a d*ckhead or not . They 're studying mortgage rates and Sydney property prices , and whether it 's good or bad for them . <p> They 're building cool small businesses , saving money by shopping on e-bay and Gumtree , and achieving the highest degree of education in the world 's history - while all the time debating whether the Honey Badger did or did n't do the right thing . <p> Unlike the loudest of the shouting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ideological warriors , but just that those they voted for turn up for work and do their best .
