<p> " As Wentworth goes , so goes Australia " may not have a familiar ring , particularly when applied to an electorate where there is so much spectacular wealth , real estate values remain stratospheric , and you are just as likely to be competing for parking space with a doggie pedicure van as a tradie 's ute . <p> But Saturday 's byelection in a Wentworth electorate dominated by Sydney 's eastern suburbs has assumed monumental national significance , reflected in a last-minute intervention by former Liberal prime minister John Howard , and accusations of " dirty tricks " in its final days . The prospect of the Liberal Party losing a jewel in its electoral crown , plus its lower house majority , has turned Australian politics on its head . <p> This is not just because a win by independent candidate Kerryn Phelps , or -- less likely -- Labor 's Tim Murray , would result in the Coalition holding just 75 seats in a 150-member House of Representatives .
