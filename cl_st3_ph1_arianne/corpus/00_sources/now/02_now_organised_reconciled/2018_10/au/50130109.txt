<h> Farmers facing drought are on the front line of climate change <h> By Charlie Prell <p> 16 October 2018 -- 10:00pm <p> When you live on the land , droughts can be soul destroying . They creep up silently , and slowly but surely tighten like a vice . They get into your bones . There 's no knowing when the drought will break , or just how far you 'll be pushed . <p> I am a fourth generation sheep farmer from Crookwell , about an hour north of Canberra . When the autumn rains failed this year I reduced my stock by almost half . It looks as though the spring rains are going to be light , so I 'm making plans to tighten my belt and sell more of my stock if I have to . We 're now down to our lowest sheep levels since the millennium drought ten years ago . <p> Charlie Prell is a fourth generation family farmer from Crookwell , NSW . <p> And I 'm not alone .
