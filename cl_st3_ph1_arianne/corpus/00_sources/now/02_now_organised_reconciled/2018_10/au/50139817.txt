The evening of 31 December , 2009 , would be the third time we hosted this small gathering of the people who were closest to us . <p> Julia and her partner Tim Mathieson , as well as Wayne Swan and his wife Kim , were regular guests . I remember Therese saying just before the 2009 party how unusual it was that both Julia and Wayne had suddenly cancelled . My chief of staff at the time , Alister Jordan , also thought it unusual . Alister believes that it is from about this time Julia began to subtly withdraw her support for the government 's program .
