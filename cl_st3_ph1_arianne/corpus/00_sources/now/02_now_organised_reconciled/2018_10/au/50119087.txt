<h> Water Act should not discriminate , Minister says <p> Victorian Water Minister Lisa Neville said the Water Act should not be able to discriminate on the basis of proposed use , despite many calling for the act to be changed . <p> " Decisions on the extraction of groundwater take into account any potential impact on the groundwater resource or environment , " she said . <p> " These decisions consider the effect it will have on the resource , and the extraction near Stanley is only 0.5 per cent of the overall water resource available for extraction . <p> " The Government has also made sure Victorian water corporations have the most up-to-date and accurate modelling on the impacts of climate change , so they can factor that into their planning and assessment of permits for water extraction. "
