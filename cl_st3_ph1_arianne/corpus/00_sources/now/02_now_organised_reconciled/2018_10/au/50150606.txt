<h> Government must deal with the real heart of our power problem <p> The Wentworth byelection was not quite the referendum on energy and climate policy that some claim . Yet it was energy and climate policy that brought down Malcolm Turnbull in the Liberal Party room on August 24 and which generated most of the backlash in his former seat on Saturday . It showed that climate change has the potential to shake the Liberal grip on other heartland seats in the leafy and professional suburbs of Australia 's big cities . As reported in The Australian Financial Review , Liberal moderates plan to press -- ironically -- for a return of Tony Abbott 's direct action plan to demonstrate the government 's credential on climate change . Any move on that front should be qualified by a strict accounting of the cost-benefit -- or abatement costs -- of the policy in the first place .
