Tomohiro Ohsumi <p> by Francois Kunc <p> There is no set of issues that could be said to be more " current " , both in Australia and around the world , than the nature and consequences of climate change . <p> It is generally accepted that in the course of what Jacob Bronowski memorably called " The Ascent of Man " , one area where hunting-gathering gave way to agrarian civilisation some 8000 to 10,000 years ago was the " Fertile Crescent " , which stretched from the Nile in the west to the Tigris and Euphrates in the east . <p> Climate change has been suggested as one reason for that development . There is no doubt that the civilisations that grew up around those mighty rivers depended on a predictable climate and made laws which today we would describe as environmental . <p> Sumeria 's ancient , pre-biblical literature records the first climate catastrophe in an account of a world-destroying deluge .
