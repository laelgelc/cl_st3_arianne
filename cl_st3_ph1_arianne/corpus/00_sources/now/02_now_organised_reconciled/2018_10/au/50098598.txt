<h> Study shows climate change shaped rapid evolution of modern kangaroos <p> Camera IconResearchers from Flinders University have found modern kangaroos evolved rapidly to develop their current features about three million years ago.Picture : Tourism WA <p> Canberra Scientists have been able to establish a timeline for the evolution of the modern kangaroo , showing ancestors of today 's kangaroos appeared in Australia at least 25 million years ago . <p> According to researchers from Flinders University , modern kangaroos rapidly evolved and diversified to develop their current features about three million years ago . <p> It was largely due to environmental change -- the spread of grasslands across the continent -- according to the study published on Friday in the journal Science . <p> The marsupials are Australia 's national animal , with an estimated population of about 45 million -- almost double the continent 's human population . <h> It 's the backyard brawl you 'll only see in Australia -
