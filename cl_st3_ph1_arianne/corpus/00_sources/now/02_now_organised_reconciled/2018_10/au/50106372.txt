<p> The Sydney Opera House was not intended to be an ivory tower , but a home to the business of cultural activities for all Australians . <p> It was largely funded by ordinary people gambling on lottery tickets . The first performances were for the construction workers labouring on this complex architectural structure . <p> So the debate about using its sails to communicate commercial messages raises some interesting ideas -- if you put aside the name-calling and personal attacks that are typically used to avoid an informed discussion of real issues . <p> The Opera House does promote a wonderful range of activities for children of all sociodemographic profiles . <p> However , the bulk of the entertainment the Opera House promotes seems to be beyond the means and tastes of the average person . <p> Even with large taxpayer subsidies being paid to Australian opera and the performing arts , tickets to most of the events at the Sydney Opera House seem beyond the means of most Australians . <p> Tickets to Spartacus , assuming the average Australian likes ballet , start at about $70 .
