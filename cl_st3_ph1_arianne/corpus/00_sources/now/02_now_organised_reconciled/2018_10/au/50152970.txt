<h> The day before everything changed <h> By Nick Galvin <p> At dusk on January 25 -- the day before Australia Day -- thousands of people are expected to gather on the harbour foreshore at Barangaroo Reserve . <p> Wesley Enoch , who is directing his third Sydney Festival , believes one of the issues with Australia Day is that there is little or no prescribed ritual around the event.Credit:James Brickwood <p> There will be a fire , music , performance and stories . <p> The purpose of the vigil will be to imagine what life would have been like at that moment in 1788 , just before Arthur Phillip planted the Union Flag in the soil of Sydney Cove - that moment when Australia stood briefly poised between two unimaginably different worlds . <p> The event is part of the 2019 Sydney Festival , which launched its program this week . " he said . " There 's a sense of commemoration of sacrifice and loss of life . Australia Day was only legislated to happen on January 26 1994 -- we have n't had time to think about what it means . <p> " Now we 're being asked to think about it we are uncomfortable with it . We do n't know what rituals we need . I think the role of the festival is to shape some of that thinking .
