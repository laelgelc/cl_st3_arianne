<h> $850m WB-ADB loans for education , health <p> Bangladesh yesterday signed loan agreements worth $850 million with the two largest multilateral development partners -- Asian Development Bank and World Bank -- for primary education , health and fishing sectors . <p> A $500 million loan deal was signed with the ADB to support Bangladesh 's Fourth Primary Education Development Programme ( PEDP 4 ) , which aims to provide quality education to all children from pre-primary to grade 5 . <p> Another $240 million financing agreement was signed with the World Bank to help improve management and production in marine and coastal fisheries and aquaculture . <p> The Sustainable and Marine Fisheries Project will help improve fisheries management systems , infrastructure , value chain investments and encourage private sector investment to increase the availability and quality of sea fish . <p> Kazi Shofiqul Azam , senior secretary of the Economic Relations Division , and Manmohan Parkash , country director of ADB , signed the deal at a ceremony held at the ERD conference room in Dhaka . <p> Azam and Zahid Hussain , acting country director of the World Bank , signed the deal for the $240 million @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the loan is a challenge , " Azam said . <p> Yesterday was the last working day for Azam , as he is retiring on October 27 . <p> He went on to request the officials concerned to be more careful for proper implementation of the projects . <p> The PEDP 4 is expected to directly benefit 18.6 million students , and about 340,000 teachers and more than 65,000 schools that are under the management of the primary and mass education ministry . <p> The programme aims to : reduce double-shift operations at schools by recruiting more teachers and building more classrooms ; step up teacher education and provide needs-based training for teachers and teacher educators ; reform examinations and assessments as well as enrich teaching and learning resources such as with digital materials , according to an ADB statement . <p> The total cost of the PEDP 4 programme is $14.7 billion , of which the government will provide $13.2 billion .
