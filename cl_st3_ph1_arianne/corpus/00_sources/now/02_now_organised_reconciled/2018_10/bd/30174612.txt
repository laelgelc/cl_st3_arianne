<h> The first summit of the global initiative P4G or Partnering for Green Growth and the Global Goals 2030 has opened a new horizon for green growth in Bangladesh , the foreign minister says . <p> AH Mahmood Ali spoke at the first summit in Copenhagen which ended on Oct 20 , the Bangladesh embassy said . <p> The foreign minister said Bangladesh , being one of the most vulnerable counties to the adverse impact of climate change , finds " strong attachment " with the core values of the newly formed alliance of P4G . <p> It is a new Danish-led international green initiative , formally launched in New York in September 2017 during the 72nd UNGA , with the ambition of becoming the world 's leading forum for developing concrete public-private partnerships to deliver on , inter alia , SDGs and the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> It aims to bring together businesses , governments , and civil society organizations in innovative public-private partnerships to advance solutions that help meet humanity 's greatest needs in five key areas : food and agriculture , water , energy , cities and circular economy , aligning with SDGs with special @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 13 , 15 and 17 , among others . <p> The first P4G Summit held in Copenhagen on Oct 19 and 20 . <p> Danish Prime Minister Lars Lokke Rusmussen inaugurated the summit which was attended by heads of state from South Korea , Vietnam , Ethiopia , the Netherlands and ministers from China , Japan and other countries , CEOs , academicians , international organisations and entrepreneurs . <p> The Bangladesh foreign minister led a 13-member delegation , both from public and private sector , to the summit . <p> He said P4G would " promote new and innovative business opportunities , including transfer of technology between the partner countries " . <p> Mahmood Ali also met his Danish counterpart Anders Samuelsen on the sidelines of the summit . <p> They signed the framework of government to government or G2G MoU in relation
