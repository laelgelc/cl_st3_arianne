<h> Mahatma Gandhi 's popular quotes and bhajans and hymn resonated on Tuesday in Dhaka and some other districts as India began the two-year long celebrations of the 150th birth anniversary of its founding father . <p> High Commissioner Harsh Vardhan Shringla inaugurated the programme at the chancery in Dhaka , recalling the relevance of Gandhi 's values and his teachings . <p> He also told the gathering about the initiatives being undertaken by the government of India to commemorate the 150th birth anniversary of Gandhi who is fondly called ' Bapu ' . <p> Around 300 admirers of Gandhi from all walks of life and various corners of Bangladesh , including Gandhi Ashrams in Barishal , Naogaon and Jamalpur , joined in celebrating the Indian independence hero . <p> Similar programmes were also organised in the assistant high commissions in Chittagong , Rajshahi , Khulna and Sylhet . <p> Gandhi is revered as one of history 's most transformative and inspirational figures . His birthday , Oct 2 , is also the International Day of Non-Violence which was adopted by the UN through a unanimous resolution in 2007 . <p> " Gandhi proved that non-violence can change history @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as we continue our work to advance peace , sustainable development and human rights for all of the peoples of the world , " UN Secretary-General Ant ? nio Guterres said in a message . <p> The high commissioner quoted Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's write-up in newspaper : " He remains a shining beacon of hope for millions of people across the world who seek a life of equality , dignity , inclusion and empowerment . The impact he left on human society has few parallels . <p> " At a time when inequalities are not uncommon , Bapu 's emphasis on equal and inclusive growth can herald an era of prosperity for the millions on the margins . In an era where climate change and environmental degradation have become central issues of discussion , the world can refer to the thoughts of Gandhiji . More than a century ago , in 1909 he differentiated between human wants and human greed . <p> " He urged both restraint and compassion while utilising natural resources and , he himself led by example in doing this . " <p> He said there would be number of events in commemorating Bapu 's 150th birth anniversary . <p> Tuesday 's commemorative event was jointly organised by the Indian High Commission and the Gandhi Ashram Trust , Dhaka . <p> The establishment of Gandhi Ashram Trust is rooted with the ' Peace Mission ' of Gandhi immediately after the communal riots in 1946 at Noakhali . <p> Gandhi stayed for about four months in the riot-stricken areas .
