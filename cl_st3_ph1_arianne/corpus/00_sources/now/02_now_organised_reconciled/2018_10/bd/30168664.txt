<h> Reducing Dhaka 's population <p> Dhaka has recently been branded as the second worst city in the world to live in by the Economist Intelligence Unit ( EIU ) . The ranking simply confirms what we have always known . <p> This is mainly because Dhaka is an extremely overpopulated city . It is perhaps one of the reasons why Dhaka has not done well when it comes to urban development and planning . <p> Yet , Dhaka still provides the most opportunities for jobseekers . In addition , those who lose everything due to climate change in the coastal regions come to Dhaka to take shelter and build a new life . Therefore , the city 's population will only increase should things continue the way they are . <p> On top of it , successive governments have made Dhaka the centre of literally everything . This needs to change . The government should adopt a long-term decentralisation plan , under which , major government offices would be distributed around the country . If the government begins decentralising itself , large businesses will follow suit .
