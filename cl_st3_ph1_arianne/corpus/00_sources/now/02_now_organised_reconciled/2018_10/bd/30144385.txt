<h> Bangladesh joining global initiative P4G for green growth <h> Bangladesh has decided to join the global initiative P4G or Partnering for Green Growth and the Global Goals 2030 . <p> The government is engaging with the ground-breaking global initiative as one of the few pioneering countries that will boost Bangladesh 's efforts to combat climate change as well as to attain the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , the Bangladesh embassy in Denmark said . <p> This has been conveyed by Bangladesh Ambassador to Denmark Muhamamd A Muhith during the P4G ambassadors ' meeting hosted at the chancery on Thursday evening . <p> Ambassadors from P4G member countries , senior officials from the Danish foreign ministry , senior advisor from Danish Prime Minister 's Office and CEO of the State of Green , the pioneer organization in Denmark dealing the green economy , among others , attended the meeting . <p> P4G is a new Danish-led international green initiative , formally launched in New York in September 2017 during the 72nd UNGA , with the ambition of becoming the world 's leading forum for developing concrete public-private partnerships to deliver on , inter alia , SDGs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ together businesses , governments , and civil society organizations in innovative public-private partnerships to advance solutions that help meet humanity 's greatest needs in five key areas : food and agriculture , water , energy , cities and circular economy , aligning with SDGs with special emphasis on Goals 2 , 6 , 11 , 12 , 13 , 15 &amp; 17 , among others . <p> During the launch , Chile , Ethiopia , Kenya , South Korea , Mexico and Vietnam along with the leaders from businesses and international organisations including C40 , Global Growth Institute , World Economic Forum , and Denmark 's State of Green formally partnered with the initiative . <p> The new initiative formally started its operation from January 1 this year . <p> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has approved Bangladesh 's position to become a formal partner of the P4G initiative . <p> The first P4G Summit is going to the held in Copenhagen on Oct 19-20 . <p> Foreign Minister Abul Hassan Mahmood Ali will lead the Bangladesh delegation to summit . <p> The summit is expected to be attended by the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of international organisations , businesses , academia , and the civil society from around the world to form a powerful coalition on the sustainable development goals through innovative partnerships , the embassy said in a statement .
