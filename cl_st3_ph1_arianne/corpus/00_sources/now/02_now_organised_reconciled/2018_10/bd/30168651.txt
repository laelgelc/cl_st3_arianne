<h> Bangladesh Delta Plan 2100 <h> Green activists call for discussion among all stakeholders <p> Underscoring the need for a holistic strategy for the " Bangladesh Delta Plan 2100 " , green activists yesterday said a thorough discussion among all stakeholders is needed before implementation of the plan . <p> It is a wide-ranging and far-reaching plan , aimed apparently at formulating a holistic strategy for better utilisation of Bangladesh 's land and water resources -- in light of the adverse effects of climate change , said Dr Abdul Matin , general secretary of Bangladesh Poribesh Andolon ( Bapa ) . <p> Bapa and Bangladesh Environment Network will jointly hold a two-day special conference titled " Delta Plan 2100 and Sustainable Development in Bangladesh " on January 4 and 5 next year to inform people and get their opinion on the strength and weakness of the plan . <p> Bapa yesterday held a press conference at Bangladesh Shishu Kalyan Parishad in Dhaka to reveal details of the proposed conference . <p> The Executive Committee of the National Economic Council ( Ecnec ) last month approved the Bangladesh <p> Delta Plan 2100 .
