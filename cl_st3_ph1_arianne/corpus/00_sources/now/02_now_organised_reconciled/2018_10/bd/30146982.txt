<p> Pointing towards the possibility of a prolonged repatriation , Deutsche Welle Editor-in-Chief Ines Pohl yesterday said the ethnic minorities from Myanmar should be given a chance to get integrated in Bangladesh 's society . <p> " We all have to find a way to get out of the ' state of denial ' and face the fact that we have to give them a chance to get integrated , to start with education , having a chance to find a job , and start a life here in Bangladesh , " she said . <p> She was addressing a roundtable on " Migration -- Challenges and Approaches in the East and the West " , arranged by the global German broadcaster at a Dhaka hotel . <p> A DW team headed by Ines recently visited the refugee camps in Cox 's Bazar .
