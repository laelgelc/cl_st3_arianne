<h> Germany joins other European donors in securing safe drinking water in city <p> UNB News <p> Publish Date - October 17 , 2018 , 08:02 PM <p> UNB NEWS - UNB NEWS <p> 153 Views <p> Dhaka , Oct 17 ( UNB ) -- The Economic Relations Division of the Ministry of Finance and the KfW Development Bank acting on behalf of the German Federal Government have signed a loan agreement of EUR 90 million to finance safe drinking water supply for the city dwellers . <p> Germany has teamed up with other major European Development Partners - the French AFD , the Danish DANIDA , the European Investment Bank and the EU . <p> This joint European -- Bangladeshi programme is called in short " Saidabad III " . <p> The project will be implemented by the Dhaka Water Supply and Sewerage Company ( DWASA ) . <p> It will ensure that in future , the water for 8 million people in Dhaka will be safe and reliable . <p> Once this project is implemented , the present sources of water ( groundwater and the highly polluted Sitalakhya River ) will be replaced by water taken from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 26 km in the East of Dhaka . <p> From Haria , the raw water will be pumped to the Saidabad Water Treatment Plant , where an additional , third water treatment plant will be constructed , increasing the water production by 450,000 m ? per day to daily production of 900,000 m ? . <p> " This will enable the Dhaka Water Supply and Sewerage Company to serve more people in Bangladesh 's capital city , " said German Ambassador in Dhaka Peter Fahrenholtz . <p> " And it will make it services less dependent from the use of groundwater and therefore more resistant to climate change , especially during dry seasons and in view of the increasing salinization of its aquifers , " he added . <p> The overall project costs are expected to be about EUR 570 out of which the Government of Bangladesh will provide EUR 140 million .
