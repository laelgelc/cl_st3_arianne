<p> Gallery Tart in Zurich , Switzerland has organised Materials for Exhibition Only , a solo exhibition by Kashef Chowdhury . Born in Bangladesh , Kashef Chowdhury works in architecture , photography and art and has led lectures and exhibitions in these areas . He was awarded the Aga Khan Award for Architecture in 2016 . In 2018 , he was a speaker at the Engadin Art Talks . Materials for Exhibition Only is his first solo show as an artist in Switzerland . The exhibition , which started on September 22 , will run till October 27 . <p> The exhibition constitutes four sculptures which are , in an abstract way , borne out of a certain global political consciousness and the current issues facing the world today . Each of the four pieces highlights global inequality , refugee crises , wars and conflicts that peril peace in this day and age as well as the threatening issues of climate change .
