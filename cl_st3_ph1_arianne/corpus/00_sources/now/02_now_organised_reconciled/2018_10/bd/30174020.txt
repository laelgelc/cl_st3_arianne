<h> 81 state-owned mills , factories privatised : Matia <p> Sangsad Bhaban , Oct 21 ( UNB ) - A total of 81 state-owned mills and factories in the country have so far been privatised after the formation of the Privatisation Commission . <p> Agriculture Minister Matia Chowdhury , who is in-charge of the Prime Minister Office in Parliament , made the disclosure in Parliament on Sunday replying to a starred question from treasury bench MP AFM Bahauddin Nasim ( Madaripur-3 ) . <p> Of the mills and factories , the highest 30 were under the Textile and Jute Ministry , 19 under the Industries Ministries , five under the Environment , Forest and Climate Change Ministry , four under the Fisheries and Livestock Ministry , said Matia . <p> Besides , 20 other state-owned entities under the Industries Ministry and three entities under the Finance Ministry were given to the private sector through handing over government shares , she said .
