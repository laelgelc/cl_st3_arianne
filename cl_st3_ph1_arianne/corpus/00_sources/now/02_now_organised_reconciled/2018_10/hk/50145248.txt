<h> PRI warns of ' inevitable ' climate policy threats to global portfolios <h> Principles of Responsible Investment is warning of an " inevitable policy response " to climate change that will place portfolios at risk <p> By Jonathan Rogers <p> The United Nations-supported Principles of Responsible Investment ( PRI ) is warning of an " inevitable policy response " to climate change that will place portfolios at risk for investment managers who have high exposure to environment-polluting assets . <p> The London-based PRI 's warning is timely given the grave predictions of the effects of a rise in the pre-industrial global temperature to 1.5 degrees celsius recently released by the UN 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) . PRI is a non-government organization that fosters the growth of investment practices aligned to environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) considerations . <p> In a report published in early October the IPCC warned that there is just a dozen years left in which to contain global warming to a maximum 1.5C .
