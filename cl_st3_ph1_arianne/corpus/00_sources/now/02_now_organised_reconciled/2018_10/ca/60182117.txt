<p> Frontier is a $20.6-billion mega mine that 's undergoing public hearings in Fort McMurray before a joint review panel established by the Alberta Energy Regulator and the Canadian Environmental Assessment Agency . <p> Frontier , which was first proposed as a four-phase project in 2011 , would be built in two phases of 170,000 barrels-per-day ( bpd ) and 90,000 bpd with a planned mine life of 41 years . <p> Environment Canada joined the region 's First Nations in raising concerns about the impact of the mine on the health of the Ronald Lake Bison herd . The herd 's current range overlaps with the Frontier lease area . <p> Susanne Forbrich , a regional director and the team lead for Environment Canada at the hearing , told the panel there is concern the mine would cause significant habitat loss for the wood bison herd . <p> Forbrich warned a loss of habitat would result in the depletion of the herd and facilitate disease transmission , as seen in other herds in Alberta . <p> " It is Environment and Climate Change Canada 's view that the project represents a high risk to the Ronald Lake herd @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some of Teck 's mitigation measures are potentially uncertain or ineffective .
