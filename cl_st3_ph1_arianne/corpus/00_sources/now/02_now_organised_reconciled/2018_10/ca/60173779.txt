<h> Epic 4,200-km run from Peterborough to Mexico to raise awareness of threatened monarch butterfly <p> Peterborough residents Carlotta James and Rodney Fuentes are part of a team planning the Monarch Ultra , a 4,200-kilometre relay run through Canada , the United States , and Mexico to raise awareness of the decline of the monarch butterfly , which makes the same journey every fall . The project will also include a documentary film about the run , and the group has launched a crowdfunding campaign to support an initial trip to ensure the planned route is safe . ( Photo : Rodney Fuentes ) <p> Forty-two runners , each running a 100-kilometre ultra-marathon , all the way from Peterborough in Canada to the Sierra Madre mountains in central Mexico -- the same 4,200-kilometre journey monarch butterflies make each fall . <p> That 's the dream of Peterborough residents Carlotta James and Rodney Fuentes who , along with Elmira 's Clay Williams and Toronto 's Galen Brown , have launched a Kickstarter campaign to fund the first stage of what they are calling the Monarch Ultra relay . <p> Planned for the fall of 2019 , the Monarch Ultra would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ America ( Canada , the United States , and Mexico ) , with the objective being to raise awareness of the monarch butterflies who make the same journey and the threats to these resilient insects as well as other pollinators . <p> Carlotta James , Rodney Fuentes , and Clay Williams are on a mission to build a 4,200-kilometre relay run through three countries , make a documentary about it , and save the monarch butterfly from extinction along the way .
