<h> Website highlights effects of climate change on Canada 's forests <p> October 23 , 2018 <p> Written by University of Winnipeg <p> Oct. 23 , 2018 - The Prairie Climate Centre at the University of Winnipeg has launched a new section of their groundbreaking Climate Atlas of Canada that explains the effects of climate change on Canada 's forests : **34;5088;TOOLONG . <p> The recent release of the IPCC Special Report Global Warming of 1.5 C underscores the fact that climate change threatens our irreplaceable forest ecosystems and economies , and that preserving Canada 's forests is an essential strategy to help fight climate change . <p> Canada 's forests are some of the largest in the world . They have enormous economic , cultural , environmental , and recreational value for Canadians of all walks of life . And they are already showing the impact of our changing climate .
