<h> What to look for in Doug Ford 's next 100 days as premier <p> Premier Doug Ford is n't being humble in describing his first 100 days in office . He says his new government has taken " action the likes of which Canada has never seen before . " So what will he do for an encore ? <p> It 's unlikely the Ford government can keep up the breakneck pace of its first 100 days , when it moved quickly to scrap pretty much anything the Liberals left behind that was n't firmly set into political concrete . Tackling other parts of the 15-year Liberal legacy -- and starting to build his own -- will take Ford more time . <p> Here are the key things you can expect to see in the next 100 days . <h> Employment laws <p> Ford clearly has Bill 148 in his sights .
