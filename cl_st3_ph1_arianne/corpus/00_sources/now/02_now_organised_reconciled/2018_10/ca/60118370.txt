<p> The Ottawa Wolves RFC are the first Canadian inclusive rugby club to host the Bingham Cup and follow other inclusive rugby clubs that have successfully held the biennial tournament . <p> The Bingham Cup is an international gay rugby union tournament that promotes inclusivity , equality and competition . <p> Seventy rugby teams from five continents are expected to participate in the 2020 Bingham Cup . <p> " Having the opportunity to host the Bingham Cup in Ottawa is an honour for our city , " said Mayor Jim Watson , who was on hand for Thursday 's announcement . " This event will bring excitement , spirit of competition and economic benefits to Ottawa , and allow our residents to enjoy world-class rugby matches . " <p> Administered by International Gay Rugby ( IGR ) , the Bingham Cup is named in memory of Mark Kendall Bingham , a former University of California , Berkeley rugby star and co-founder of the Gotham Knights RFC , a gay-inclusive rugby club in New York City . <p> Mark Kendall Bingham lost his life in the September 11 , 2001 terrorist attacks onboard United Airlines Flight 93 . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Cup will be held in Canada for first time -- a country with a growing rugby community and a proud reputation of openness toward the LGBT+ community , " said Ben Owen , IGR Chair . <p> First held in 2002 , the Bingham Cup is today the largest international amateur rugby union tournament in the world . The 2018 tournament hosted by the Amsterdam Lowlanders RFC had more than 2,300 registered participants from 63 countries . The 2020 tournament in Ottawa will include week-long cultural , educational and sporting events and is forecast to generate economic activity in excess of $8.5 million . The 2020 tournament will also be carbon-neutral , feature a wheelchair rugby exhibition game and celebrate Indigenous culture . <p> " The Bingham Cup promotes the values of inclusivity and equality regardless of sexual orientation , and those are values that Canadians support wholeheartedly , " said The Honourable Catherine McKenna , federal Minister of Environment and Climate Change and Member of Parliament for the riding of Ottawa Centre . <p> One of only three gay-inclusive rugby clubs in Canada , the Ottawa Wolves RFC was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to host the 2020 Bingham Cup in the nation 's capital .
