Using a combination of 12 international climate models , they made their best projection as to how each of those grid squares would be changed . <p> The changes were calculated for high- and low-emissions futures . As well , they were projected out to the end of the century . <p> Their method allows them to be quite specific . <p> Edmonton , for example , currently averages three or four days every summer in which the temperature climbs above 30 C , Blair said .
