How has the online battlefield for a better world changed ? <p> In some ways , social media -- including the more dynamic and participatory mediums of Facebook and Twitter -- have partially displaced websites as sources of information , or at least may now function as the primary outreach tool that alerts people to information on websites . <p> Twitter hashtags like #BlackLivesMatter and #MeToo have gone viral and have been a potent way of sharing lived experiences both quickly and widely . Cell phone videos of police shootings and racist incidents have also brought into sharper relief for the broader public this reality .
