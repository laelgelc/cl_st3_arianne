Homeowners , tenants , small businesses and agriculture operations will be allowed to apply for the funding . <p> The Municipal District of Taber will receive $2.3 million , the County of 40 Mile will receive $933,000 , the Town of Taber will receive $146,000 and the Village of Foremost will receive $53,000 . <p> For fires , $1.9 million has been set aside for Cardston County , Municipal District of Acadia , Cypress County and Wheatland County under the Municipal Wildfire Assistance Program . <p> Shaye Anderson , Minister of Municipal Affairs , says the government is also focused on ensuring @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ weather patterns . <p> " It 's really apparent that climate change has become a very big deal for us , " he said over the phone from Edmonton .
