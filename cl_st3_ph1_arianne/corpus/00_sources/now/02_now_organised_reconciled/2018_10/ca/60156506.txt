<h> Climate Change Likely to Ding Beer Supply <h> The average price of a pint could double by the end of this century because of declines in barley yields , a study predicts . <h> Oct 15 , 2018 <h> Shawna Williams <p> In a collaboration that came about over drinks , an international team of climate researchers has modelled the effects of climate change on barley and beer production . Their predictions , published today ( October 15 ) in Nature Plants , include a drop of 3 percent to 17 percent in barley yields by 2099 , a decrease in the supply of beer , and sharp increases in prices . <p> " Climate change will affect all of us , not only people who are in India or African countries , " coauthor Dabo Guan of the University of East Anglia in the UK tells Reuters . <p> CNN reports that Guan and others hatched the idea for the study when they went out for beers in China after a series of lectures . They used climate change data to model the projected effects on barley yields and the economic response to those changes . " That makes sense . This is a luxury commodity and it 's more important to have food on the table .
