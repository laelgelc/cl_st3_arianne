<p> Wednesday , October 31 , 2018 , 11:07 AM - More than half of the world 's wildlife species are in decline , according to a dire new report from the World Wildlife Fund . <p> The WWF 's Living Planet Index , released Monday , shows some 60 per cent of species have declined over the last four decades . That 's based on long-term studies of 16,704 populations representing 4,005 species worldwide . <p> This does not mean that 60 per cent of world 's wildlife has vanished , only that of the studied species , 60 per cent of them saw a drop in their numbers due to a variety of factors , among them habitat loss and overexploitation .
