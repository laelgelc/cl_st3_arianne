<h> Virtual tree to nurture real empathy <h> Team shot VR test footage near Mount Seymour <p> With stereoscopic cameras and extremely sensitive microphones , the Wild Empathy team is attempting to make a virtual reality environment as lovely as a tree in the hopes that greater empathy will lead to increased environmental stewardship .
