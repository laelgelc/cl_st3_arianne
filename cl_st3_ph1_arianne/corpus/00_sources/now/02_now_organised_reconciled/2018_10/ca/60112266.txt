<h> Kelowna <h> A voice for the future <p> A new group has been formed to help inform Kelowna voters leading up to the municipal election . <p> Called the Future of Kelowna Consortium , the group has a decidedly green bent . <p> The group " is united by a desire to elect city councillors who will support the livability , equity , and sustainability of Kelowna for generations , " the consortium says . <p> " Name recognition is often said to win municipal elections , " says organizer Robert Stupka , one of the group organizers and a K.L.O Neighbourhood Association council member . " What really should matter is the positions and the qualifications of candidates .
