<h> Nova Scotia approves tire burning just in time for the end of the world <p> Urgent changes at an unprecedented level never before seen in human history are needed to save the planet from environmental collapse . Nova Scotia has responded to this clarion call from the world 's leading scientists by paying a company to burn old tires . <p> The Department of Environment announced today it 's awarded industrial approval for Lafarge Canada to burn 350,000 scrap tires as fuel for the company 's Brookfield cement plant . <p> Lafarge will receive $367,5000 in public money to subsidize its efforts . <p> The license is only for a 12-month period . At least to start .
