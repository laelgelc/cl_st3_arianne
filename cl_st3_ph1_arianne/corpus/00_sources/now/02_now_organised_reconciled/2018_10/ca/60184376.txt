<h> Ottawa to return 90% of money it collects from carbon tax to the Canadians who pay it <p> OTTAWA -- The federal government will return 90 per cent of all the money it collects from a carbon price directly to the Canadians . <p> But it has pushed back the start date of its new carbon tax another four months to allow the affected provinces to prepare . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau unveiled the details of the carbon tax rebates at a Toronto college on Tuesday , in an attempt to sell Canadians on the need to pay for pollution without breaking their pocketbooks . <p> " Starting next year , it will no longer be free to pollute anywhere in Canada , " Trudeau said at Humber College . <p> Unlike some political leaders , both in Canada and elsewhere , the prime minister said he is not willing to pass the burden of climate change on to our children and grandchildren . <p> Ottawa required all provinces to put a minimum price on pollution of $20 a tonne of emissions by Jan. 1 .
