<h> State-of-the-art research equipment <p> Cambrian College is getting $2.5 million from Ottawa to buy some state-of- the-art equipment for the school 's Applied Research department . <p> Sudbury MP Paul Lefebvre says the investment will make Cambrian more competitive and will allow local businesses , through the college , access to specialized equipment they would not otherwise have . <p> " A lot of the times they have to go down south to commercialize this , or to test it before they can commercialize their equipment . So , this investment of $2.5 million , this is actually helping students , Cambrian College , as well as private businesses in the area to scale up . And we need that because those are great jobs for northern Ontario . " said Lefebvre . <p> Ottawa is providing $180,000 to re-map the Junction Creek watershed , a major waterway in Sudbury . <p> It 's designed to help protect the area from future flooding caused by changes to the environment . <p> " It has happened in the past and we know with climate change , this is a serious problem that we have . So we want to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ mitigate and eventually negate any effect that flooding may have in the area . But to do that , we need the data . " said Lefebvre . <p> The funding for Cambrian College is from FedNor , while the watershed money comes from the Ministry of Public Safety . <h> Photos <p> Cambrian College is suspending the women 's basketball program after the school was unable to find a head coach .
