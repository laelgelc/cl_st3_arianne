<h> Two U of T experts to lead new federal research network for Lyme disease <p> ( photo by iStock ) <p> Federal Minister of Health Ginette Petitpas Taylor has announced a $4-million investment in the first-ever Pan-Canadian Lyme Disease Research Network , which will bring together researchers from more than 40 universities across the country , as well as patients , public health experts and representatives from communities living with Lyme disease to improve health outcomes for Canadians with the disease . <p> Taylor has chosen two University of Toronto experts to be part of a three-person team to lead the network , which will also address gaps in prevention , treatment and diagnosis of the tick-borne infection . <p> Lyme disease is caused by the bacteria Borrelia burgdorferi and is transmitted to humans through the bite of an infected , black-legged tick . A warming climate has coincided with the spread of this insect population across the country and has led to an increase in the incidence rate of infections . <p> " One of the major challenges with Lyme disease is that it presents unspecific symptoms , making it difficult for us to recognize them as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tests for Lyme disease may not detect early stages of infection .
