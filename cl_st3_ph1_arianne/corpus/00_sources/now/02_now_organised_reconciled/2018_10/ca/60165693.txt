( Jeff McIntosh/Canadian Press ) <p> From devastating wildfires to massive storms , the extreme weather this year has been a reminder that climate change is already happening -- and we think that 's worth talking about . <p> With that in mind , CBC News is launching a weekly environmental newsletter called What on Earth ? -- delivered to your inbox every Thursday afternoon . <p> Here 's what readers will get every week @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ state of the environment . We will highlight trends and solutions that are moving us to a more sustainable world , as well as what each of us can do , individually , to be more green . <p> The newsletter will explore topics such as green energy , ethical eating , plastic packaging , electric vehicles and the " circular economy " ( an economic model that aims to reduce waste and encourage the reuse of materials in industry and manufacturing ) . <p> While last week 's gloomy report from the UN 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change illustrated the risks of damage to the environment in the coming decades , many countries , companies and individuals are meeting this challenge head-on -- with positive results . <p> Without minimizing the scale of the problem , we 'd like to keep you informed about the ways in which people are solving some of the most pressing concerns , especially in Canada .
