<p> WASHINGTON , Oct. 9 , 2018 /CNW/ -- New Orb Media Inc. reporting illuminates the catastrophic impact rising sea levels and more extreme rainfall are having globally . New research suggests that the human and financial costs of flooding are already much higher and longer-lasting than previously suspected . <p> Worldwide , floods are the most frequent form of natural disaster . As economists strive to calculate the long-term effects on global supply chains , national GDPs , household income , and inequality , the consequences of climate change related flooding are becoming increasingly clear . <p> Today , 40 percent of the world 's population lives within 100 kilometers ( 63 miles ) of a coastline -- and that number is increasing . This urbanization , combined with climate change and outdated infrastructure , compound to make floods more intense and damaging . <p> " If we 're only adding up the direct cost of a flood on the houses that were inundated , and the price it took to bail those out , and the price it takes to repair infrastructure , and other things like that , then we 're potentially missing large hidden costs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , University of Chicago . <p> The biggest loss from any catastrophic flood , to both businesses and people , is opportunity cost .
