Some caribou units are moving into higher threat levels faster than scientists can assess them . <p> While the threats caribou face are complex and vary by region , the common denominator is human activity , primarily through resource development and , increasingly , climate change . <p> Wherever they are found , caribou play the role of umbrella species , meaning their presence is of central importance to their ecosystems . In the North , they are also a food source for Indigenous communities . <p> All caribou in Canada belong to one species . Scientists regard the 11 units as distinct because of a combination of their habitat , ecology and genetic differences . That task is compounded by the fact that caribou populations are in constant flux , which makes it difficult to separate normal variations from a sustained downturn . <p> Yet , even in the far north , the emerging prognosis for the caribou is no longer in doubt .
