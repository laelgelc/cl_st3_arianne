<h> Negligible benefit of carbon tax <p> The letter writer 's reference to employment rates is somewhat misleading . According to a study by the Fraser Institute . the period from 2001 to 2012 saw Ontario with the lowest rate of growth in real per capita GDP in the country . During that time , public sector employment grew by 27.6 per cent , while private sector employment rate grew by a mere 5.6 per cent . This public sector growth rate has crowded out private sector employment leaving unemployment rates either unchanged or possibly higher . Clearly , public sector wealth has but one source : private sector taxation . The burden of additional public sector employment is driving a blow to the economic well-being of the Ontario economy . Additional taxation in the form of an unnecessary carbon tax further deteriorates the economic well-being with negligible benefit other than to increase our virtue signalling to the rest of the world .
