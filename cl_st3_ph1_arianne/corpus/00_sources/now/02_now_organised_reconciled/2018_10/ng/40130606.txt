<h> Innovation in energy storage wins NLNG 's $100,000 science prize <p> THE advisory board of The Nigeria Science Prize has announced " All Solid State Liquid Sulphur Battery , " a work by Dr Peter Ngene as the winning work for this year 's The Nigeria Prize for Science , worth a $100 , 000 . <p> The Chairman of the Advisory Board for the Science Prize and a science prize laureate , Prof. Akpoveta Susu , disclosed this to journalists at a press conference in Lagos on Tuesday . <p> The " All Solid State Liquid Sulphur Battery " is a new type of battery for energy storage with implications on renewable energy development . The work also contributes to surmounting challenges in Nigeria around power transformers explosions due to degradation of insulators in the transformers . <p> According to Professor Susu : " This winning work is evolutionary .
