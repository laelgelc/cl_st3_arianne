as well as drought and a decline in agribusiness , added to the miseries of the war-weary Afghans . <p> Figures released by the UN mission in the country on Monday , said over 2,790 civilians were killed while over 5,250 others injured in conflict-related incidents in the first nine months of the year in the country . <p> Almost 54 per cent of the country 's population of over 30 million
