<h> FG seeks Europe , America 's support in recharging Lake Chad <p> President Muhammadu Buhari on Friday said the greatest investment Europe and America could make in Africa now was to help the continent to accomplish inter-basin water transfer to recharge the Lake Chad . <p> According to a statement by his Special Adviser on Media and Publicity , Mr. Femi Adesina , the President spoke while receiving the Chairman of the African Union Commission , Mr. Moussa Mahamat , at the Presidential Villa , Abuja . <p> Buhari noted that Lake Chad , which provided a means of livelihood to millions of people in four countries -- Chad , Cameroon , Niger and Nigeria -- had now been reduced to 10 per cent of its original size due to the impact of climate change . <p> He said , " People who depended on the Lake for fishing , farming , animal husbandry , and many others have been thrown into dire straits . <p> " That is one of the reasons youths now dare the Sahara Desert and the Mediterranean Sea to seek greener pastures in Europe .
