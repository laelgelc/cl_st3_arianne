<h> Institute Cautions Farmers Against Using Waste Water For Irrigation <p> Dr Martin Eduvie , the Head of Training , National Water Resources Institute ( NWRI ) Kaduna , has cautioned farmers against the use of waste water for irrigation . <p> He told News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) on Saturday in Kaduna that some farmers rely on water from drains , lavatories , septic tanks or soakaway , among others , for irrigation , which are not ideal for agriculture . <p> He said that in most cases , waste water from industries move into the river , and from there , farmers used the water directly , not knowing that most water from drains and industries contain chemicals that are not suitable for plants . <p> Eduvie advised consumers to be careful about the source of their vegetables , its smell , as well as how long vegetables last . <p> He , however , added that the issue of how long vegetables last might not be directly related to the type of water used in its irrigation until research is
