<h> 181 Countries Tackle Tobacco Coys ' Meddling In Treaty Talks <p> No fewer than 181 countries will converge in Geneva , Switzerland for the Eighth Conference of the Parties ( COP8 ) of the Global Tobacco Treaty to shut off one of the last avenues of influence the tobacco industry has to interfere with public health policymaking at the talks . <p> At the conference holding between October 1-7 , countries will advance a provision that will prohibit the tobacco industry from exploiting public badges , which are primarily used by tobacco industry representatives to delay , block and weaken the treaty . <p> LEADERSHIP recalls that in the past year , in advance of COP8 , Big Tobacco has escalated its attempts to re-normalize the deadly industry ; doubling down on promoting heat-not-born products and launching a billion dollar foundation in hopes of regaining lost footing in policymaking spaces . This year , governments will also advance policies to eliminate the trade . <p> The policy to kick Big Tobacco out stems from a broader treaty directive called Article 5.3 that prevents industry interference in the halls of government .
