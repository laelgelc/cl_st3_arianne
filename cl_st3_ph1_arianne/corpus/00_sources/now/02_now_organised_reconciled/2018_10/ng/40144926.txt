<h> Flood : EU supports states with N400m , as FG assists victims with N3bn <p> The European Union Ambassador to Nigeria and ECOWAS , Mr. Ketil Karlsen , says the Union has approved one million euros ( N400m ) to assist three of the nine states affected by flood in Nigeria . <p> Karlsen said this on Friday in Abuja at a joint news conference by the European Commission in Nigeria and the National Emergency Management Agency . <p> This is even as Director General NEMA , Mr. Mustapha Maihaja , disclosed that the Federal Government had released N3bn for flood victims across the country . <p> Karlsen said the funding was to provide shelter , medicine and other basic items for an estimated 60,000 people in Anambra , Kogi and Delta states . <p> " We have responded decisively to the calls of Nigerian government to seek help from the international partners in a situation where the ongoing flooding in the country has affected around two million people . <p> " The European Union has He said the EU support was not limited to humanitarian aid but also included efforts to mitigate the effect of flooding through early preparation . <p> The envoy assured that the Commission would follow up on a clear supervision and monitoring to ensure that the materials are being delivered as urgently as possible to the affected people . <p> He also disclosed that the European Civil and
