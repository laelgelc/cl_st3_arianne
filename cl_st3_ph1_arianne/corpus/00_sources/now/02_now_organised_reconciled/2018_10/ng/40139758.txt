<h> Nigeria requires $1trn to modernise energy infrastructure -- Osinbajo <h> Related News <p> Mr Osinbajo made this known while declaring open a two-day National Energy and Climate Change Summit at the State House Banquet Hall on Thursday in Abuja . <p> The summit was organised by the Energy Commission of Nigeria ( ECN ) in collaboration with the International Energy Charter ( IECh ) and the European Union . <p> Represented by the Minister of Science and Technology , Ogbonnaya Onu , the vice president said the summit would provide a high level forum to discuss energy and climate change in relation to economic development and environmental protection . <p> He expressed optimism that the deliberations would provide solutions toward improving sustainable energy supply and access in Nigeria . <p> The vice president said he was happy that IECh , a reputable energy organisation that promotes multilateral framework for cross-border cooperation in the field of energy development , was part of the summit . <p> " I recall the ceremony last year , about 18 months ago , in this same hall , when Nigeria signed the International Energy Charter Political Declaration after obtaining the approval of President @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ observer member state . <p> " I also recall , that the ECN immediately signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with IECh secretariat in order to promote such activities that will be beneficial to our dear Nation . <p> " Following this , a study of the Energy Investment Risk Assessment for our country has been completed ; the report which has just being presented to us here today . <p> " I am happy that the assessment which featured four performance indicators has scored our energy investment risk as moderate . This is challenging in view of the huge investment of about 1 trillion dollars required to modernise our energy infrastructure in 29 years from 2014 to 2043 , ' ' he said . <p> The vice president said that Nigeria 's energy related policies were not only geared toward energy supply security but also to mitigate global warming . <p> Mr Osinbajo said that Nigeria 's determined contributions to the Paris Climate Protocol were robust . <p> He said that to further support private investment , the federal government had increased transparency and openness in governance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in business registrations and permit . <p> On his part , Eli Bala , the Director-General , ECN , said that the commission had the mandate and responsibility for strategic planning and co-ordination of national policies in the field of energy . <p> " The importance of energy as an instrument for national development as well as international cooperation and diplomacy can not be over emphasised . <p> " Modern societies , however , demand that development therefrom must be sustainable as defined by the Brundtland Commission in 1987 in its report , on Our Common Future . <p> " This year 's summit has the objective of assessing the investment environment in the energy sector for sustainable energy development , ' ' he said . <p> In a remark , Urban Rusnak , IECh 's Secretary-General , advised Nigeria to improve its investment in climate and decrease the risk perceived in investment in the country . <p> Mr Rusnak said there was need to highlight the importance of a rigorous impact assessment of existing and proposed policies . <p> " Without understanding how the policy works and what are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ existing policies , there will be no progress ; no possible development . <p> " Second and very important finding is that the level of transparency and public engagement needs to be increased . We have seen all over the world of issues of acceptance of energy investments . <p> " The most important finding is the streamline and clarification of judiciary procedures to give investment community security and feelings of the responsibility of the host nation over investments , ' ' he said . <p> The National Energy Summit was instituted by ECN in 2010 as an annual event to provide a platform for discussion on energy security , economic development and environmental protection . <h> NEVER MISS A THING AGAIN !
