appeal while speaking at the inaugural lecture of the Guild of Professional Bloggers of Nigeria ( GPBN ) in Lagos . <p> The News Agency of the Nigeria ( NAN ) reports that the theme of the lecture was " Combating Fake News in the Cyberspace " . <p> Mohammed said the collective efforts of all Nigerians were needed in view of the threats fake news posed to peace and unity of the country . <p> He said the spread of fake news has also promoted ethnic and religious suspicions among Nigerians . <p> He said the media , especially , had a huge responsibility to check the problem in view of their
