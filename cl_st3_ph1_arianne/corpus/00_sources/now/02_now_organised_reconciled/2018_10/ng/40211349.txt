<h> Bringing Fresh Impetus To Federal Character Commission <p> Perhaps a sincere way to assess the impact of the Change Agenda of the Buhari administration is to hold the mirror of change across ministries , departments and agencies . <p> The Federal Character Commission , ( FCC ) , hitherto plagued by lopsided placement in its senior cadre , has embraced equity with the appointment of Honourable Abayomi Sheba as the executive chairman . <p> Up until last month , officers from one geo-political zone occupied both the positions of executive chairman and secretary . Now with the appointment of Sheba , a new impetus has arrived at the Federal Character Commission . <p> The FCC was established by Act No 34 of 1996 , now cited as FCC Act . Cap F7 Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004 , which mandates it to implement and enforce the federal character principle and formulae to ensure equitable distribution of public posts , socio-economic amenities and infrastructural facilities across the country . <p> When the federal character principle appears in national discourse , many are wont to limit this mandate to mere distribution of positions and offices . To be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ each state of the federation and the Federal Capital Territory shall be equitably represented in all national institutions and in public enterprises and organizations . " <p> The principle of equity and fairness , in the Nigerian context , also applies to provision of physical facilities such as roads , bridges , hospitals and even provision of potable water . This is more so , where the federal government has increasingly become engaged in providing basic infrastructures that ought to be the responsibility of states and local governments . <p> Indeed , the commission is yet to fully sensitize Nigerians on its core mandate which transcends distribution of posts and offices . This mandate includes providing an equitable formula , subject to the approval of the President , for distribution of socio-economic services , amenities and infrastructural facilities ; formulate modalities and schemes subject to the approval of the President , for redressing the problems of imbalances and reducing the fear of relative deprivation and marginalization in the Nigerian system of federalism as it obtains in the public and private sectors . <p> So what magic is Sheba bringing to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ politician parades enviable credentials in service to the people , having represented the interest of his constituency since the return of democratic rule in1999 when he was elected to the House of Representatives . <p> He distinguished himself not just as a member but was appointed the chairman , committee on poverty alleviation and community development for the four years he served at the National Assembly . That was when he led a team of his committee to the Peoples ' Republic of China to understudy poverty reduction mechanism of the Chinese Government . He also served as a distinguished member of the Presidential National Core Team that drafted Nigeria 's Poverty Reduction Strategy Paper . <p> After the sojourn at the National Assembly , Sheba briefly represented Ondo State as a director on the board of Nigeria Wire and Cable Plc and subsequently supervised the State Liaison office in Abuja . In 2009 , he was first appointed a federal commissioner at FCC . After nine years of meritorious service , he was last month elevated as executive chairman of the commission . <p> The task of the Commission @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ complexity ; a vast territory of diverse cultures , orientation , religion and opportunities . All these should compel the Federal Government to pay more than a casual attention to the operations and efficiency of the commission . <p> Staffers at the commission are enthused on Sheba 's appointment .
