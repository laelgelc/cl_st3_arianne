<h> Charcoal use detrimental to environment -- Don <p> The Focal Officer , Regional Centre of Expertise on Education for Sustainable Development , Prof. Albert Olayemi , has said the use of charcoal is detrimental to the environment <p> He stated that charcoal was not only polluting the environment , but was also increasing deforestation , desertification and flooding . <p> Olayemi spoke during a sensitisation outreach titled : ' Charcoal production , burning for sustainability , ' in Ilorin , the Kwara State capital . <p> The summit was organised by the Agricultural and Rural Management Training Institution and the RCE . <p> The don raised the alarm that Nigeria had the highest loss of forest in the world from charcoal production . <p> He explained that the huge amount of charcoal produced annually was contributing to the loss of over 11 per cent of the nation 's forest cover . <p> Olayemi warned that at that rate , Nigeria would completely lose its forest cover in less than 30 years . <p> " Already , about 35 per cent of Nigeria is turning into desert like with the Sahara making incursion down south .
