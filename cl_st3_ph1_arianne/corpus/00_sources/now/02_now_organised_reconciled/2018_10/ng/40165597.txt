<h> SWOFON Wants FG 's Support For Farmers To Attain Zero Hunger By 2030 <p> Small-Scale Women Farmers Organisation in Nigeria ( SWOFON ) says the Zero Hunger by 2030 project can only be achieved if the Federal Government supports small-scale farmers to boost their productivity . <p> Mrs Olaleye James , the National Public Relations Officer of SWOFON , said this on Thursday in an interview with NIGERIA NEWS on the sidelines of the 11th National Agricultural Show in Abuja . <p> She said that SWOFON was ready to support Federal Government 's efforts to end hunger in the country by 2030 , adding , however , that the government had to put in place certain strategies to facilitate the fulfilment of the project . <p> James said that the government should introduce a budgetary process that would enable smallholder farmers to make input into budget planning processes , particularly in the area of agriculture . <p> She also underscored the need to review the land tenure system of the country . <p> " There should be allocation of 10 per cent of the annual budget to the agriculture sector at the national and state levels in order @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Besides , the government needs to review customary land laws to enable women to inherit the land for the sustainability of their farming ventures , while allocating land to smallholder women farmers ' cooperatives . <p> " We need easy access to credit at single-digit interest rate and the removal of those loan conditions that require collateral and guarantors . There is a compelling need to make credit schemes gender-friendly . <p> " We need to be included in decision-making processes in policy formulation and implementation ; we need access to markets and guaranteed minimum produce prices so as to avoid post-harvest losses and boost our income , ' ' she said <p> James said that the government should provide adequate security across the country , initiate trauma healing programmes for women farmers in the crisis areas and put an end to the incessant herdsmen-farmers conflicts . <p> " To promote good agricultural practices , the government should recruit more female extension workers to educate women farmers in modern farming techniques , while research outcomes on agricultural production should be simplified for women farmers and all smallholder farmers <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and improved seeds ; capacity building activities in new climate change and mitigation methods should also be organised for women farmers in particular . <p> " Government should provide silos , crop dryers and other facilities to control post-harvest losses for smallholder farmers , in addition to putting in place produce processing and marketing facilities , ' ' she said . <p> James noted that women farmers constituted more than 60 per cent of the entire population of Nigerian farmers , adding that any effort to empower women would certainly facilitate the country 's plans to achieve zero hunger by 2023 . <p> She , nonetheless , stressed the need for the government to deal with farmers directly , instead of " political farmers ' ' , adding that the Anchor Borrowers Programme should also be restructured to make it more farmers friendly .
