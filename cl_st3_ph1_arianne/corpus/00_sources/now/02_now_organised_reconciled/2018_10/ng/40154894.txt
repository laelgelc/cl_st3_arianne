<h> Ensuring Safe Application Of Biotech In Nigeria <p> The signing of the Biosafety Act into law in 2015 and the subsequent approval for the commercialization of the two-cotton species in 2018 flagged off Nigeria 's readiness to play in the biotechnology league . The Federal Government laid up all the necessary groundwork in relation to the Cartagena Protocol with the establishment of a regulatory body , the National Biosafety Management Agency ( NBMA ) to oversee all issues that relate to biotechnology and ensure safe application of modern biotechnology in the country.Recall that NBMA was established in 2015 . The agency is saddled with the responsibility of ensuring that the activities of modern biotechnology and the use of genetically modified organisms do not have any adverse impact on the conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity . As the nation prepares to finally commence the commercialisation of the cotton-species , the agency has begun intense preparation to streamline its activities and mainstream the nation 's biosafety framework into the national system for effective regulation . <p> Pursuant to this , the agency with the support of the Convention of Biological Diversity Secretariat convened a meeting with key stakeholders @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's ministries , departments and agencies like the ministries of environment , trade and investment , agriculture and rural development , Nigeria Agricultural Quarantine Service ( NAQS ) , National Agricultural Seed Council ( NASC ) , National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control ( NAFDAC ) , Nigeria Customs Service ( NCS ) , and the Consumer Protection Council ( CPC ) with a view to streamline and firm up the nation 's biosafety regulation . Speaking during the awareness seminar for key policy and decision makers for the validation of national biosafety mainstreaming strategy in Abuja , the director-general of NBMA , Dr Rufus Ebegba said the agency believed that mainstreaming biosafety into national systems would further strengthen its network of regulation for a functional , holistic biosafety system in Nigeria and also strengthen awareness on biosafety systems in the country . <p> " It is therefore not out of context that this move is a right step in the right direction . In our drive to mainstream biosafety , we have so far signed MoU with your institutions , we have also identified your legislations/policies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ areas for mainstreaming identified during the draft desk study , which was a product of your inputs . It is from your inputs that the mainstreaming strategy has been developed , " he said . Ebegba averred that the draft desk study with the MDAs was to find out possible areas of convergence with the possibility of mainstreaming and also coming together to ensure that the mandate of the agency was delivered to the people of Nigeria . This is to enable the country have a holistic biosafety system so that the products of modern biotechnology will be safe to the environment and human health , he pointed out . <p> He further said , " We are here to review the strategy which we have developed with a view of mainstreaming biosafety into the national system .
