<h> Flood , others threatening Nigeria 's zero hunger target -- FAO <p> The severe flood recorded in about 14 states in Nigeria as well as conflicts and climate change may derail efforts targeted at achieving zero hunger by 2030 , the Food and Agriculture Organisation of the United Nations has said . <p> According to the FAO Country Representative in Nigeria , Suffyan Koroma , this developments has posed great concern to not just the organisation , but to other countries of the world . <p> Speaking at a rally to celebrate the 2018 World Food Day in Abuja on Tuesday , Koroma stated that if the challenges were not addressed , Nigeria would have to put in more efforts to achieve its food sufficiency target . <p> He urged the Federal Government to improve its drive in managing flood , conflicts , diseases and pest control in order to meet the country 's food needs . <p> Koroma said , " In view of what we have been doing in the area of crop and livestock production that contribute to zero hunger , Nigeria should be free of hunger by 2030 . " <p> Commenting on food prices , Koroma noted that factors such as the efficiency of value added products , energy , effective legislation , transportation and legal framework , among others , were very important for the prices of food to drop . <p> This is coming as the Minister of Agriculture and Rural Development , Chief Audu Ogbeh , expressed hope that a zero-hunger world by 2030 was still possible . <p> He , however , decried the devastating impact of climate change on food production and food security all over the world . <p> Ogbeh stressed that the goal could be achieved if all stakeholders , including the government , the private sector and development partners work in collaboration to fight hunger , extreme poverty and malnutrition . <p> The minister , who was represented by the ministry 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said Nigeria was moving towards achieving self-sufficiency in rice production , adding that with that in view , the country could be a model for the world . <p> Ogbeh stated , " Permit me to state that policies of this administration on the agriculture sector have started yielding results , especially in the area of rice production .
