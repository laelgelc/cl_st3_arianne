<p> On the heels of one of the deadliest climate-related weather disasters affecting countries all over the world , an unprecedented gathering of global leaders yesterday launched the new global commission on adaptation to catalyse a global movement to bring scale and speed to climate adaptation solutions . <p> The Global Commission on Adaptation is led by Ban Ki-moon , the eighth Secretary-General of the United Nations ( UN ) , Bill Gates , co-chair of the Bill &amp; Melinda Gates Foundation , and Kristalina Georgieva , Chief Executive Officer ( CEO ) , World Bank . It includes 17 convening countries and 28 commissioners , including the leaders , representing all regions of the globe and all sectors of development and industry . <p> As indicated in the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) Special Report on Global Warming of 1.5 ? C , damaging climate change impacts are being felt now , much sooner and more powerfully than previously projected . Adaptation is about managing the risks associated with climate change -- from floods and droughts to sea level rise and storms . <p> The work of the Commission will elevate the visibility and political @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ investments , new technologies and better planning to become more resilient to climate-related threats . <p> According to the group , there are four major roadblocks slowing adaptation that the commission will work to address : <p> * Decision makers and the wider public are not yet aware of all the opportunities to be gained from becoming more resilient and less vulnerable to climate impacts and natural hazards ; among others . <p> * Governments and businesses fail to incorporate climate change risks into their social and economic development plans and investments ; <p> * Adaptation efforts fall short of those who need them most , the world 's poorest and most vulnerable people ; and <p> * Although adaptation is a global challenge , global leadership on the issue is scarce . In short , the world is falling short of the transformation required to adapt to a changing climate . <p> In its first year , the Commission will oversee preparation of a flagship report and present its findings and recommendations at the 2019 UN Secretary-General 's ( UNSG ) climate summit .
