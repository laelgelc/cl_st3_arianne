suspended services until further notice , the weatherman says the winds are likely to continue until tomorrow at the earliest <p> THOUSANDS of potential travelers to and from Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar were left stranded yesterday following the cancellation of marine transport services due to strong winds and boisterous waves in the Indian Ocean . <p> The leading service provider , Azam Marine and Kilimanjaro Fast Ferries company , announced the suspension of the services until further notice to the consternation of the many would-be passengers , including tourists . <p> The company said in a statement that it would resume the services only after being given the green light by the Tanzania Meteorological Agency ( TMA ) and marine transport regulatory authorities . <p> Speaking to The Guardian in Dar es Salaam , Tanzania Shipping Agencies Corporation ( TASAC ) director general Japhet Loisimaye said an average of 4,500 passengers travel by boat between Dar es Salaam and Zanzibar
