Tanzania Forest Conservation Group ( TFCG ) in collaboration with the district council and other partners . <p> The project which is supported by the European Union under the Global Climate Change Alliance , aims to promote the use of energy saving cooking stoves to reduce deforestation . <p> It also aims at coming up with an effective strategy to support poor and rural households on ways to fight climate change impact . <p> The project which has provided modern cooking stoves to more than one hundred households in the village to reduce firewood use is being implemented in Shambangeda ,
