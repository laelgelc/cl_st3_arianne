<h> 5,000 Kigoma farmers to benefit from the joint United Nations prgm <p> OVER 5,000 small scale farmers in Kakonko , Kibondo and Kasulu districts in Kigoma Region are benefiting from the agriculture component of an initiative by the United Nations in Tanzania dubbed ' Kigoma Joint Programme ' or in short ' KJP ' aimed to support host communities affected by the influx . <p> Speaking over the weekend during the launch of the farmers inputs market meeting at Katahana village in Kibondo District , the representative of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations ( FAO ) , Fred Kafeero , said that the KJP 's agriculture theme objective is to address the various bottlenecks in the maize , cassava , beans value chains and livestock production in the project areas . <p> " The aim is to address the challenges faced by the farmers in the value chain of their farming activities with special focus on maize , beans and cassava . Besides , we also look at the ways we could help them boost their production in livestock and related products , " he said . <p> FAO , the World Food Programme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ UNCDF ) and International Trading Centre ( ITC ) jointly implement the agriculture component of the KJP in collaboration with the government of Tanzania with funding from the government of Norway . <p> Kafeero said that the programme will enhance the small scale farmers ' capacity through training on good agricultural practices , and integrating climate-smart agriculture through the farmers field schools and junior farmers field learning school approach . <p> " They will also be provided with quality maize , bean seeds and cassava plating materials in order to boost their production , " he added . <p> So far a total of 135 farmers facilitators ( training of trainers ) and 42 district and ward level staff including crops , livestock , community development and cooperatives officers have been trained and it is expected that the " training of trainers " model will expand the knowledge and skills to the over 5000 smallholder farmers targeted . <p> speaking at the launch of the farmers inputs market meeting at the Katahana village , Kibondo District Administrative Secretary , Ayubu Sebabili , expressed gratitude to the entire UN family in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is a great relief to the host communities . <p> " It 's good to see development partners joining hands to support our communities . So far we 've seen activities around capacity building among trainer farmers and today we witness the launch of this inputs market that will enable farmers to access quality seeds , plating materials and fertilizer in time . We look forward with so much hope , " he said . <p> According to him , besides the effects of the influx of refugees in their areas , the host communities were also faced with a number of other challenges including poor agriculture practices , unreliable supply of quality seeds and planting materials , effects of climate change , among others . <p> " We see that many of these will be addressed withing this programme . As the goverment , we 'll offer all our support to ensure that we realise the targeted objectives , " Sebabili pointed out . <p> The Kigoma joint programme is an area-based cross-sectoral United Nations joint programme to improve human security in Kigoma Region . the joint programme builds @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the region . <p> KJP consists of 16 UN agencies cooperating in six themes of sustainable energy and environment ; youth and women 's economic empowerment ; violence against women and children ; education , with a focus on girls and adolescent girls ; water , sanitation and hygiene ( WASH ) and agriculture with the focus on developing local markets . <p> It focuses on three districts in Kigoma , mainly Kasulu , Kibondo and Kakonko.
