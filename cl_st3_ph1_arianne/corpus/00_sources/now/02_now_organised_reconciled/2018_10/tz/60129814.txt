The member raised concern of the imminent disappearance of forest . <p> " There is concern that if these practices ( deforestation ) continue , the Community will lose approximately 12 million hectares of forest by 2030 " , Kasamba told an attentive House . <p> The Assembly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the many benefits from forests and to ensure livelihoods for the people that live in and around forests , the Community needs to adopt programmes on Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and Forest Degradation ( REDD+ ) " plus " conservation and the sustainable management of forests and enhancement of forest carbon stocks and adopt programmes aimed at halting or preventing the destruction of forests . <p> The resolution which was seconded by Dr Woda Jeremiah Odok , calls for the promotion and use of alternative technologies that minimize tree cutting especially for firewood and home consumption purposes . <p> At the same time , the Assembly recognises that the EAC Climate Change Policy provides for several strategies which include promotion of sustainable management of forests and wetlands in the region , promoting reforestation , afforestation and improvement of agricultural productivity . <p> Kasamba called for more action in conservation matters saying EAC had already lost 6 million hectares of forest cover . <p> According to Kasamba , in Uganda , a government report has revealed that the country 's forest cover has dropped to 9% , representing a 3%drop in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ area 945,687sq km is home to one of the largest trees ' cover in the world , but it is similarly at risk .
