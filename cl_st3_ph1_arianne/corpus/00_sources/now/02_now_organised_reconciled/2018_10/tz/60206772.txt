<h> Arusha-based forestry training institute hatches new course <p> URBANISATION is rapidly increasing in Tanzania , but so is the concern that towns and cities contribute to destroying the environment and in view of this , a new training course to greenly the cities will start from 2019 at the Arusha-based Forestry Training Institute . <p> The government , through the Arusha-based , Forestry Training Institute at Olmotonyi will be introducing two new courses namely of ' geo-informatics , ' and ' urban forestry and landscaping , ' effective from early next year . <p> Geo-informatics has been described as the science and the technology which develops and uses information science infrastructure to address the problems of geography , cartography , geosciences and related branches of science and engineering . <p> That was revealed over the weekend , by the Rector for the Olmotonyi Forestry Training Institute , Christognus Haule , during the 78th graduation ceremony at the institution 's premises in the upper parts of Ngaramtoni area , Arumeru District the occasion which marked the 81st year since the formation of the forestry and environment oriented public college . <p> " We are also glad that more people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " said Haule adding that last year there were 331 graduands but the number had doubled to reach 664 graduates this year , which is more than 50 per cent of increase . <p> According to the FTI principle , the other new course to be added at Olmotonyi portfolio will be ' urban forestry and landscaping , ' which will facilitate efforts to make cities green in the backdrop of global warming and worldwide climate change concerns . <p> Out of the 664 graduates , there were 273 female students . Courses in which the 2018 batch graduated in included ; Certificate in Forestry , Ordinary Diploma in Forestry and Technician Certificate in Forestry . <p> The Permanent Secretary in the Ministry of Natural Resources and Tourism , Prof Adolf Mkenda said his office will ensure that the project on " Empowering Communities through Training on Participatory Forest Management , REDD+ and Climate Change Initiatives " ( ECOPRC ) , funded by Norway will be sustained through the second phase . <p> The Royal Norwegian government and the government of Tanzania signed a bilateral agreement of a five @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the sustainable management of Tanzania 's forest resources in ways that maximise social , economic , environmental and governance benefits for communities engaged in Participatory Forest Management , REDD+ and climate change initiatives .
