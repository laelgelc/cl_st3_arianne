<h> Waste management in cities need long term solutions -- report <p> LACK of enforcement of environmental laws and regulations , as well as unwillingness among urban dwellers to pay for waste management services , are key challenges facing waste management in the country , a new report has shown <p> The National Environment Statistics Report ( NESR , 2017 ) -Tanzania Mainland launched here on Thursday states that while cities and towns are generating an ever-increasing volume of waste , effectiveness of their solid waste collection , transportation and disposal systems was declining . <p> According to the report published by the National Bureau of Statistics ( NBS ) , urban local authorities ' waste collection problems need long-term solutions , which can not be achieved in the short run . <p> " Currently , waste management is a growing problem in Dar es Salaam City due to increasing urbanization via rural-urban migration , rising standards of living and rapid development associated with population growth . These have resulted in increased solid and liquid waste generation by industrial , domestic and other activities . " Therefore , this means that every person in Dar es Salaam produces a daily average of 0.9 kg/day per capita of household waste , " says the report . <p> It further states that improper solid waste management contributes to air pollution , surface and groundwater contamination and public health challenges . <p> NESR also shows that municipal solid waste ( MSW ) management is commonly the largest single budget item for communities , and this sector is often one of the largest employers as well . <p> It recommends that it is imperative to move towards a green economy in the solid waste sector by prioritizing waste avoidance , minimization of waste by reusing materials @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " ( Reduce , Reuse and Recycle ) . <p> In addition , moving this important sector towards responsible stewardship will contribute to the main targets of the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , the report noted , highlighting the latter as end poverty , promote prosperity and well-being for all , protect the environment and address climate change . <p> In developing countries , solid waste management is considered to be one of the most serious environment problems confronting most urban areas . <p> Waste management is gradually becoming a serious concern in Tanzania due to limited sorting at source and improper storage , collection , transportation , treatment and final disposal . <p> This implies that significant proportions of the waste generated end up in the environment in unacceptable ways of disposal , which accentuate environment and public health risks . <p> The waste management problem is more pronounced in squatter settlements , where 70-80 percent of the urban population resides without the necessary infrastructure and waste collection service <p> Speaking during the launch of the report , the deputy minister of State , Vice President 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ findings will help the government and other stakeholders put renewed efforts to improve the situation .
