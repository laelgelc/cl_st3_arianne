Poor education <p> Not every person without an education is living in extreme poverty . But most of the extremely poor do n't have an education . And why is that ? There 's a lot of barriers stopping children from going to school . Many families ca n't afford to send their children to school and need them to work . More still do n't see a benefit in educating girls . Education is often referred to as the great equalizer , and that 's because education can open the door to jobs and other resources and skills that a family needs to not just survive , but thrive . UNESCO estimates that 171 million people could be lifted out of extreme poverty if they left school with basic reading skills .
