<h> Yes , we can have a world free of hunger by 2030 <p> Hunger is a reality that challenges millions of people 's lives across the globe , especially among the poorest and most vulnerable . New evidence continues to signal an increase in world hunger and a reversal of trends after a prolonged decline . <p> In 2017 , the number of undernourished people is estimated to have hit the 821 million mark - around one out of every nine people in the world . While some progress continues to be made in reducing child stunting , levels still remain unacceptably high . Nearly 151 million children aged below five ( over 22 percent of the total of kids in this age group ) were affected by stunting in 2017 . <p> The theme of this year 's World Food Day ( ' A world free of hunger by 2030 is possible - our actions are our future ' ) reminds us that investing in the comprehensive vision of SDG2 - End hunger , achieve food security , improve nutrition , and promote sustainable agriculture - can catalyse achievement right across the 2030 Agenda . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to access sufficiently nourishing food , sustaining our natural resources and safeguarding biodiversity , and transforming food systems in the rural sector while increasing the productivity and income of smallholder farmers , fishers , foresters , and pastoralists . <p> The causes of hunger are many and complex . For instance , poverty is one of the main ones . If you are poor , you can not afford nutritious food . This makes it difficult to work or earn a living , and often means that people remain in a poverty trap . <p> Conflicts disrupt farming and food production . Millions of people are often forced to flee their homes , and find themselves deprived of access to sufficient food . In addition , climate variability and change - manifested in extreme weather events such as floods , tropical storms , and long periods of drought - causes suffering to poor farmers , fishermen , pastoralists , and forest dwellers . They do n't have enough to eat , and they are often forced to leave their homes . <p> World Food Day provides a platform for the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ civil society , and academia to interact and affirm further actions for ensuring Zero Hunger in Tanzania by 2030 . It is also an opportunity to renew our commitment to invest in agriculture and rural development , since most of the poor people live in rural areas and depend on agriculture , fisheries or forestry as their main source of income and food . They are always the first to be affected by climate change and other shocks . <p> Achieving Zero Hunger by 2030 is possible , but we need to redouble our efforts to get back on track . It requires a mix of public and private investments , and risk-informed policies and interventions , to ensure that the poor and most vulnerable in rural areas benefit from the growth and employment opportunities generated . There is need to address malnutrition by devising national strategies that promote synergies between nutrition and food security , rural development , social protection , sustainable agriculture , climate change , biodiversity , health , and education . <p> Everyone has a role to play in achieving Zero Hunger . It is everyone 's duty to adopt a new mindset by using the Earth 's resources sustainably , following nutritious diets , and changing their day-to-day actions with the aim of reducing food waste and taking on a more sustainable lifestyle . <p> With a Zero Hunger way of living comes the responsibility to treat food with respect , including the people that give us that food - the farmers - and the resources that go into producing it . Vulnerable rural producers need to empower themselves by uniting and forming local cooperatives , to realise their right to adequate food and decent employment and share knowledge about sustainable agriculture . <p> Let our actions be transformative , embracing the principles of sustainability and tackling root causes to leave no one behind . We can achieve Zero Hunger if we join forces across nations , sectors and professions , and act on evidence .
