<h> List of top Kenyan gospel artists <p> The Kenyan music industry has continued to grow in the recent past as new and vibrant artists emerge to challenge those who have long been in the scene . Kenyan gospel artists have not been left out as they remain the most popular musicians in and outside the country . With gospel music , they influence the lives of most Kenyans morally . The influence they command is formidable . The styles that the artists employ include African rhythms , classical and choirs . The industry has been modernized with powerful instruments that make it easy to reach the young people of this generation . <p> There are several benefits of gospel music in the society as it helps to shape the values and character of those who love it . In fact , gospel music has been used for corrective and educative purposes where it positively influences the behaviour of every member of the society regardless of their background .
