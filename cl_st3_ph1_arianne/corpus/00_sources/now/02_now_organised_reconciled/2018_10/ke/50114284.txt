<h> Roba banks on free seedlings and poor women to increase forest cover <p> Mandera county has embarked on a programme to increase its forest cover to 10 per cent to minimise the effects of climate change . <p> Governor Ali Roba said the environment department will distribute over 60,000 seedlings to households and public institutions such as schools and health centers . It will also create awareness on the importance of environmental conservation . <p> He said his administration has engaged 270 vulnerable and needy casuals , mostly women , who will each be allocated 15 trees to water and look for a modest monthly wage . <p> Speaking on Wednesday in Mandera town while launching the project , Roba asked residents for support . <p> The move is inline with the Kenya government initiative to increase the country 's forest cover to 10 percent . <p> " We have initiated a multi-sector aproach to greening Mandera county and protecting our biodiversity , as well as improving living standards , " Roba said . <p> The programme also aims to support vulnerable persons and improve their living standards . <p> " It 's our duty to protect and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future generations . I appeal to residents to plant more trees , " Roba said .
