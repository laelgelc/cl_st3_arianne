<h> County to develop bill and policy to manage disasters <p> Trans Nzoia County government is in the process of developing a disaster management policy that will force the government to set aside emergency funds . <p> The County Director of Special programmes and disaster Management Lilian Milimo said the policy will enable own budget , that in the past we have been directly under the office of the governor . <p> Milimo was speaking in Endebess Sub County during a Civic education and public participation sensitisation workshop for Sub County stakeholders on Thursday . <p> " Once we develop a policy , then the department shall perform its duty fully without relying on the governor since we will have the funds to cater for the specific disasters that occur across the county , " she noted . <p> She encouraged residents to seek disaster services in the special programme offices for further action . <p> Endebess Sub County administrator Scholastica Nalovile told them to forward any complaints they have through the Citizen 's complaints and grievances desk especially on the destruction of public utilities such as dams which can cause disaster . <p> This comes as the Devolution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disaster risk management policies to enable them to manage disasters at the county level . <p> He said disasters in the country have become common due to climate change which calls for preparedness both at the county level and at the national government to cushion citizens from being affected . <p> He also advised county governments to have enough fire engines that will enable them to respond quickly and extinguish fires whenever they occur . <p> Disasters such as fires and floods have been common in the country and thus the need for prudent planning .
