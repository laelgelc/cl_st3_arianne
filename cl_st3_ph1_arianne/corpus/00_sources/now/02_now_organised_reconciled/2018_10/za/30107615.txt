<h> Egypt to bring together international youth in 2nd Annual World Youth Forum <p> JOHANNESBURG - For the second consecutive year , Egypt is bringing together youth of all nations at the 2nd Annual World Youth Forum ( WYF ) . <p> This year the Forum will be held from 3 t0 6 November , in the city of Sharm El Sheikh , Egypt , under the auspices of President Abdel-Fatah El-Sisi . <p> The WYF is a platform by youth for youth to discuss different topics that occupy the world today and will shape the future . It aims to send a message of peace , prosperity , harmony , and progress to the entire world . <p> WYF engages youth from around the globe in an enriching set-up , allowing them to exchange views and recommend initiatives to decision-makers and influential figures . <p> The forum is a chance for youth to engage with top policymakers , network with promising youth from the region and the world that are determined to make the world a better place for everyone . <p> Last year the conference was attended by more than 3,000 participants from all over the world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ online . This year , the WYF will concentrate on three main themes ; Peace , Development and Creativity . <p> The theme of peace will discuss sub-topics such as the Role of World Leaders in Building and Sustaining Peace and Water Security in the Wake of Climate Change ( Day Zero ) . <p> The African Agenda 2063 , " the Africa we want " , will be discussed under the theme of development . <p> Employment opportunities in the artificial intelligence era , as well as entrepreneurs and innovative startup roles in economic growth will also be discussed under the theme of creativity . <p> Youth from around the world between the ages of 18 - 40 years old can apply as attendees or speakers through the linkhttps : **32;1489;TOOLONG . **155;1523; The Embassy of the Arab Republic of Egypt in South Africa encourages all South African Youth to register and participate in the 2nd annual WYF.
