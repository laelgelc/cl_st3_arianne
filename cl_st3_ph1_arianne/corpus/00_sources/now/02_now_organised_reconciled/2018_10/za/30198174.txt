<h> Land Care awards for farmers and learners <p> News - Date : 28 October 2018 <p> Written by : Silas Nduvheni / Viewed : 343 <p> The Provincial Land Care Coordinator in the Department of Agriculture and Rural Development , Ms Thivhavhudzi Nekhavhambe , urged learners , farmers and the community to take care of natural resources , because resources are vulnerable and can be lost . <p> " We find that natural resources such rivers , soil care and veld care are not taken into consideration by our communities . People are just throwing used nappies and plastic containers in our rivers . Every day we are losing the top soil and people are unnecessarily cutting down trees , " said Nekhavhambe . <p> She was speaking last Tuesday during the handing over of the awards won by some farmers and young learners during the eighth land-care conference , which was held in Mangaung . <p> The ceremony for Vhembe region 's winner was held in the department 's western boardroom in Makwarela .
