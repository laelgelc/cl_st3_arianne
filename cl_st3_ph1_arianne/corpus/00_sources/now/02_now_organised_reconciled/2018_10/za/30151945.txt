<h> Why we should have more marine protected areas in KZN <p> Beach clean-ups are one way of contributing to marine conservation to prevent rubbish that has washed up from the sea from being blown back into the sea . At the Durban harbour mouth , rubbish is often washed on to the banks . Sibusiso Ndlovu African News Agency ( ANA ) <p> Durban - As National Marine Week ended on Sunday , there was still hope that a proposal submitted to the Department of Environmental Affairs a year ago would be considered in an effort to conserve the country 's threatened marine environment . <p> " The best method of ensuring marine conservation is to have protected areas , " said Bruce Mann , a Durban-based senior researcher with the South African Association for Marine Biological Research . <p> The proposal cited 22 marine networks to be added as Protected Marine Areas ( PMAs ) and included four from KwaZulu-Natal , including the Protea Banks off Port Shepstone and the Tugela Banks . <p> Part of the proposal was to extend the Isimangaliso Wetlands Park and Aliwal Shoal , already PMAs , further offshore . <p> Mann @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensuring effective marine conservation .
