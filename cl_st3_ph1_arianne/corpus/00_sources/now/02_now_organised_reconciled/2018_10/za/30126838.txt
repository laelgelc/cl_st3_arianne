While the intention by the government to plug existing gaps in public finance is commendable , it should not be at the expense of growing the economy . <p> Although SA has to honour its pledge in the 2015 Paris Agreement on climate change to support the global effort to stabilise greenhouse gas concentrations , the country is not compelled to do so under the current national circumstances .
