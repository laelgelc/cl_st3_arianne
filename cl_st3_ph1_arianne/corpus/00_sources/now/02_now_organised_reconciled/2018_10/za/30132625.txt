<h> VC 's first 100 days in office <p> One hundred days into her five-year term of office as vice-chancellor ( VC ) of Africa 's leading university , Professor Mamokgethi Phakeng has experienced the highs and lows that reflect the challenges of a transforming , research-intensive institution in the 21st century . <p> In her first weeks in July , Phakeng held scores of meetings with key campus constituencies , opening discussions on a shared vision for the university . <p> VC Prof Mamokgethi Phakeng 's first 100 days in office have seen her engage with leaders , students , staff and community .
