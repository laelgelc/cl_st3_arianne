<h> Beer lovers face price spikes , shortages as climate changes <p> INTERNATIONAL -- Climate change will brew trouble for beer lovers in coming decades as it shrinks yields of barley , the top grain used to make the world 's most popular alcoholic drink , a study published on Monday said . <p> Extreme weather events featuring both heat waves and droughts will occur as often as every two or three years in the second half of the century if temperatures rise at current rates , the study said . <p> Average global barley yields during extreme events are expected to drop between 3 percent and 17 percent , depending on the conditions , said the study , published in the journal Nature Plants . <p> Less barley will mean beer shortages and price spikes . Under the hottest scenario , China will suffer the most shortages this century , followed by the US , Germany , and Russia , it said . <p> Dabo Guan , a professor of climate change economics at the University of East Anglia and the study 's lead author , said beer issues pale in comparison to other climate induced problems , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
