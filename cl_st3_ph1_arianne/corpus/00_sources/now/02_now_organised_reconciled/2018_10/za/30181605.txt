<h> Continued roll-out of water pressure management in city <p> The City of Cape Town is continuing the roll-out of pressure management technology to various parts of the water supply network . <p> The City said on Tuesday this is to ensure that Cape Town uses water in the most efficient way possible . <p> Pressure-related work will be taking place as follows -- Tuesday : Goedemoed , Country Place , Sonstraal Heights , The Crest , Wellway Park East and Klein Nederberg from 6pm to midnight ; Tuesday and Wednesday : CBD , Gardens and Zonnebloem from 6pm to 3am ; Wednesday and Thursday : Protea Valley , Van Riebeekshof and Door de Kraal from 4pm to midnight . <p> " Pressure management is helping with the metro 's water conservation effort and to adapt to the new normal of water management . It also reduces leaks and pipe bursts by better ensuring that pressure remains within levels that the pipework can tolerate , and reduces the rate of loss from leaks and bursts , " the City said . <p> Meanwhile , average dam levels in the Western Cape have declined slightly for the first time since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The latest figures show average dam levels in the province stand at 65.7% .
