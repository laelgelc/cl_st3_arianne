<h> New system to warn of freak waves and storm surges in Tuvalu <p> A new warning system in Tuvalu is hoped to save people from freak waves and storm surges . <p> It was developed after Cyclone Pam in 2015 , which sent waves rolling across the country 's atolls , forcing the evacuation of some 300 people . <p> The Pacific Community 's Mia Ramon said the system is vital for survival on low-lying atolls , with such events forecast to increase with climate change . <p> " Tuvalu people want to stay home , " she said . " And the Tuvaluan Prime Minister is very famous for saying ' If you save Tuvalu , you can save the world ' . " <p> " Because Tuvalu represents really where the hardest hit people will be . The lowest lying atolls . And the ones who are the most affected . And we 're expecting that these severe weather patterns are just going to increase and multiply over the next few years . "
