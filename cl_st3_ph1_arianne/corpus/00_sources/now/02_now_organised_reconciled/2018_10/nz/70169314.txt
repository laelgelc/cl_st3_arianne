<h> Bay planning project takes top honours <p> Photo : File . <p> A Bay of Plenty planning project has landed top honours in an international planning award . <p> Innovative risk-based planning and engagement for natural hazards , by the Bay of Plenty Regional Council , was named overall winner in The Commonwealth Association of Planners inaugural Awards for Outstanding Planning Achievement in the Commonwealth . <p> While CAP has existed for more than 40 years and represents more than 40,000 planners and allied professionals , the awards themselves are new . <p> The CAP Awards for Outstanding Planning Achievement in the Commonwealth champion the very best examples of planning practice in the Commonwealth . <p> It aims to share outstanding planning practice , demonstrate the role of planners and planning in creating resilient and sustainable communities and promote planning as a tool to improve societal well-being . <p> The inaugural awards attracted entries from across the Commonwealth , which were then whittled down to a shortlist of eight entries ( five of these from New Zealand ) .
