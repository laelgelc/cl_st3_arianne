<h> Clarion call from climate scientists <p> The scientific call for global action on climate change stepped up several levels with a report out yesterday warning that " urgent and unprecedented changes are needed " and we have just 12 years to limit climate change catastrophe . <p> A key line in the sand in this new report by the UN Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) is a maximum 1.5 degrees Celsius of warming -- at the ambitious end of the historic Paris agreement of 2015 to keep global temperatures between 1.5C and 2C above pre-industrial levels ( with the average already 1C higher ) . However , on current emissions rates 1.5C is now seen as being breached around 2030 to 2050 rather than in the early 2020s , as was assessed in the last IPCC report in 2014 , following revisions to the amount of warming that has already occurred . <p> The politics of achieving even a 2C maximum rise is challenging , and the research carried out since 2014 shows that it is too dangerous a situation to aim for .
