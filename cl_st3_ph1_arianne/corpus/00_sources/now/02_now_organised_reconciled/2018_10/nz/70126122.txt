<h> Opinion : We need to go to war on climate change <p> OPINION : My generation may not go to war against a hostile foreign nation but it needs to go to war against climate change . <p> When our men went to war to fight for the allies we banded together as a country , committed to a common cause . Fighting climate change is no different . <p> On Monday , the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) released its ' life altering ' special report ' Global Warming of 1.5degC ' . It warns that , unless ' heroic ' action is taken in the next 10 years , the damage will be irreversible . <p> We need to be the heroes . <p> We may not have a hostile nation advancing on our border , but we do have the threat of sea level rise licking at our coastline . <p> If we do nothing , the global temperature will rise to two degrees above pre-industrial levels . But if we can limit warming to 1.5degC , then we can reduce sea level rise by 10 centimeters . <p> For low lying @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this is crucial to avoid an increased number of devastating floods and storm surges . <p> We may not have fighter jets threatening us with bombs from the sky but we do have excessive carbon emissions . <p> If we want to limit global warming to 1.5 degrees , we need to make " deep reductions " in methane emissions worldwide . Methane hangs around in the atmosphere , trapping in heat , contributing to the 0.2 degree temperature rise every decade . <p> The Environmental Defense Fund says methane is the principal contributor to man-made climate change . Eighty six percent of New Zealand 's methane emissions comes from our farming sector . While it is a huge part of our economy , if we do n't make major changes now , it will a non-existent economy for our children and grandchildren . <p> One of the lead authors , and the only New Zealander on the IPCC panel , Bronwyn Hayward told me , " the Government needs to bite the bullet and tackle the issue of methane emissions with farmers . " <p> Carbon zero by 2050 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ methane . Considered a " short lived gas " , the government proposes " emissions of methane ... must be significantly reduced to sustainable levels " . <p> Based on 2010 levels , the IPCC reports the world needs to cut methane emissions by more than one third . Should n't we write that in to legislation as well ? Rather than aiming for a flimsy " significant " . <p> We may not be losing people on the battlefield but we could use 400,000 species if we fail to cap the temperature at 1.5degC . <p> If global warming continues to increase at the current rate , it 's predicted to reach 1.5degC between 2030 and 2052 . <p> At that temperature , many species are at risk of extinction - but if the global average temperature rises by another half a degree , about 400,000 species could die out . <p> At 1.5degC , coral reefs would decline by 70 to 90 percent , whereas nearly all would be lost with 2C . <p> So what can you do ? <p> Arm yourself ! <p> With knowledge , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , where you are being wasteful and where your greatest emissions come from . <p> Have a plan of attack ! <p> Make small , sustainable changes to your life rather than trying to change too much at once . Use reusable plastic bags , catch a bus to work , introduce meat-free Monday or reduce your plastic consumption with bamboo toothbrushes . <p> Enlist your friends ! <p> Everyone needs to do their part and a good word from you could help change someone 's perspective . Think little picture . If we do n't change our habits now , holidays in the Pacific could be a thing of the past , you could be clearing up from a wild storm a little more often , and that morning coffee could get a whole lot more expensive . <p> We are fighting for our country , not against our enemies but against ourselves .
