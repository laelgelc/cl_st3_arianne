<h> Tukuitonga goes into battle on behalf of Pacific for WHO position <p> Dr Colin Tukuitonga , a New Zealander of Niuean descent and proposed by New Zealand , was given resounding support for his nomination from Pacific countries . Image : AUT <p> By Sri Krishnamurthi <p> Health challenges in the Pacific Islands require acute and immediate attention from the World Health Organisation , says Dr Colin Tukuitonga , a New Zealander of Niuean descent whose nomination was proposed by New Zealand . <p> Dr Tukuitonga goes into battle this week for the position of WHO regional director for the Western Pacific , in a struggle which takes place on October 8-13 in Manila , Philippines . <p> He is up against three others -- Dr Narimah Awin , proposed by Malaysia ; Dr Takeshi Kasai , proposed by Japan ; Dr Susan Mercado , proposed by the Philippines -- at the nomination which will take place during the 69th session of the Regional Committee for the Western Pacific . <p> " Without a doubt it is our turn , not just for climate change but other health challenges such as Non-communicable diseases ( NCD ) ( diabetes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ New Guinea , and the list goes on . The candidate is a long-term staff member of WHO , " says Dr Tukuitonga . <p> Dr Tukuitonga is guarded against talk of the money-game buying votes in the process . <p> " Only in so far as offers made by Japan to small islands , such as a new airport extension in Solomon Islands , " he says , and quickly adds " New Zealand is meeting most of the costs of my campaign " . <p> His expectation is that all the Pacific Island countries will back him -- at least when it comes to voting from the second round onwards .
