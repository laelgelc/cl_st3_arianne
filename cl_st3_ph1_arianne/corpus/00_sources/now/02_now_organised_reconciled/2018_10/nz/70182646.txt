Someone once tried to explain to me that Shakespeare is meant to be spoken , and heard aloud . I tend to think Bradbury is the opposite , his " prosetry " swirls up out of the printed page and straight into your imagination . The only author I ever plagiarised , I copied a whole passage from one of his exquisite short-stories into a school essay .
