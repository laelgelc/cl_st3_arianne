<h> Fellowship for research into the impact of climate change <p> Scientist awarded fellowship for research into the impact of climate change on New Zealand land <p> Manaaki Whenua Landcare Research scientist Dr Kendon Bell has been awarded a Rutherford Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship to continue his research into measuring the impact of climate change on New Zealand land . <p> The research , Empirical measurement of the impact of climate change : correcting for measurement error in precipitation and understanding the incidence of impacts , looks at how rainfall impacts agriculture and land . <p> Climate econometrics is an emerging field that combines the history of connections between weather , and economic and social outcomes with climate forecasts to estimate future damages from climate change . <p> Dr Bell is thrilled to have the opportunity to undertake this work full time . <p> " This work gives me the chance to combine economics and big data to tackle a key question for society , " he says . " I 'm really excited to be doing rigorous , data-driven research that will help us learn about how climate change will affect people .
