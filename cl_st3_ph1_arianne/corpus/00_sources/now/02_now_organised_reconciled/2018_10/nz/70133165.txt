Various stories and comments out of the Police Association 's conference have been incredibly illuminating about recent trends , and where things might be going next . <p> First of all , there has been a lot of anecdotal evidence that police have started decriminalising cannabis by stealth , and now there 's some statistical evidence that backs that up . Radio NZ reports that in the 20 years to 2014 , the number of arrests for cannabis possession plummeted by 70% , and in that same time the number of warnings being given out doubled . One of the things being considered by the police is whether they themselves would be able to smoke , reports the Dominion Post . And the Police Association are concerned about what the implications for officers would be if they were involved in an incident -- a shooting , for example -- and were to subsequently test positive .
