<p> Southland has set itself the ambitious goal of becoming a carbon neutral region which the Government is backing with a $190,000 grant from the Government 's Community Environment Fund . <p> Associate Environment Minister Eugenie Sage announced the funding today for the Carbon Neutral Advantage project which will see local business , industry and communities work together to transition Southland to a carbon neutral region . <p> " Venture Southland 's ambitious project to take a regional approach to reducing carbon emissions shows great leadership and is something the Government is proud to support , " Eugenie Sage said . <p> " Communities are often best placed to find solutions to problems and I look forward to seeing what Southland can do . " <p> Over the next three years Venture Southland aims to measure , assess , and take steps to reduce the region 's emissions .
