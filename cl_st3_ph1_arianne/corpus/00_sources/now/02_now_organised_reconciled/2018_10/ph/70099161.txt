This will be more significant to the beneficiaries belonging to the indigent and underprivileged sector that rely on public transport services , " said Angara , who is one of the authors of the Free College Law . <p> The bill primarily seeks to institutionalize the grant of student fare discount currently being enforced by the Land Transportation Franchising and Regulatory Board ( LTFRB ) even
