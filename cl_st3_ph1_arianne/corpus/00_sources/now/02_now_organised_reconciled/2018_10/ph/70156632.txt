<h> Toward a ' Green , Green , Green ' Philippines <p> Several studies have shown that there are real mental- health benefits to having greener cities . One published in the Journal of the American Medical Associationdetailed a case of how the conversion of abandoned lots in Philadelphia , Pennsylvania , into community parks and gardens actually led to a 68-percent decrease in residents ' feelings of depression , particularly among those living below the poverty line . <p> Interestingly , a recent Aarhus University , Denmark study related satellite maps of Denmark 's greenery with incidences of schizophrenia and found that those who did not have access to green spaces within 210 square meters ( sq m ) of their homes were 50 percent more likely to succumb to the mental illness . <p> The Greek philosopher Socrates once said : " By far , the greatest and most admirable form of wisdom is that needed to plan and to beautify cities and human communities . " Yet , data would show that even if we have our own share of " wise " urban planners and designers , their wisdom has n't been really put to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> For instance , in the 2012 Asian Green City Index -- a study of 22 major , often high-density Asian cities across certain criteria -- Metro Manila registered very low in terms of the amount of green spaces available per inhabitant . Where residents in Singapore had access to 66 sq m of green space ; 50 in Taipei ; 44 in Kuala Lumpur ; 27 in London ; 23 in New York City ; 19 in Shanghai ; and 17 in Hanoi ; those in Metro Manila had only 4.5 sq m . Only those in Bangkok , Jakarta and Kolkata had access to fewer green spaces . <p> Congestion due to poor planning has also led to an urban landscape that favors cars and other motor vehicles , making it inconvenient -- sometimes dangerous -- for people to travel around as pedestrians . <p> It 's no wonder then that a 2017 Stanford University survey of 111 countries determined that Filipinos were the fourth most lethargic or physically inactive people in the world , walking only 4,008 steps or 4.6 kilometers a day . But clearly , we have not done such a good job with building up our cityscapes and ensuring that our urban spaces truly nurture life and promote productivity . <p> This is why the Department of Budget and Management 's new program called " Green , Green , Green " is auspicious , as it marks a decisive step toward unleashing the wisdom of our urban design and planning experts across the country in tandem with our local government units , particularly cities . <p> Under this year 's budget , up to P2.6 billion has been set aside -- as the assistance to cities component of the Local Government Support Fund ) -- for grants and support for projects that involve enriching open spaces ; establishing forest parks and botanical gardens ; installing benches and proper shading to streetscapes ; setting up bikeways and walking lanes ; and constructing other " green " infrastructure . <p> While such projects are definitely about making our cities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more resilient , especially in the face of the devastating effects of climate change . <p> Out of the country 's 145 cities , 143 expressed interest when the DBM launched the program earlier this year . And out of that number , 65 cities recently comprised the first batch of recipients of assistance of various amounts depending on the project scope , population size and land area covered . <p> Ours is such a beautiful country .
