<p> In Photo : Prof. Janeth Morata-Fuentes , program leader , presents the Biodiversity and Vulnerable Ecosystems Research program overview . <p> PALO , Leyte -- The Philippine Science High School ( PSHS ) in Eastern Visayas Campus emphasized the importance of preserving biodiversity in the region through the Biodiversity and Vulnerable Ecosystems Research ( BiVER ) Program funded by the Philippine Council for Agriculture , Aquatic and Natural Resources Research and Development of the Department of Science and Technology ( DOST-PCAARRD ) . <p> According to Prof. Janeth Morata-Fuentes , program leader , aims to document biodiversity in Eastern Visayas through ecological assessment and systematics study . She added there is a need to become more adaptable to the environment with the effects of climate change . <p> With the rich marine and terrestrial resources of Eastern Visayas , the whole region has also become more vulnerable to stronger typhoons and heavier rainfalls due to climate change . The BiVER program , through the data it can provide , intends to help agencies like the Department of Environment and Natural Resources and local government units in their policy-making endeavors and in the creation of evidence-based resource-management programs . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ground to develop their research skills , as well as allow them to have community-immersion experience . <p> Expected outputs of the program include inventory of flora and fauna , data on environmental quality of ecosystems , computational models of economically important river systems and a BiVER database system and web site which will store research data and serve as accessible source of information for the public . <p> This project is the first collaboration of PSHS and PCAARRD , which Acting Executive Director Dr. Reynaldo V.
