<h> Duterte to attend IMF , WB meetings in Bali <h> Dharel Placido , ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA - President Rodrigo Duterte will fly to Bali , Indonesia on Wednesday to attend the annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) and World Bank ( WB ) . <p> The President will also join his fellow Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) leaders at the sidelines of the meetings on Oct. 11 . The meeting will be attended by IMF Managing Director Christine Lagarde , WB President Jim Yong Kim , and UN Secretary General Ant ? nio Guterres . <p> Foreign Affairs Assistant Secretary Junever Mahilum-West said Duterte and his fellow Southeast Asian leaders will discuss how " to create better prosperity and equity as well as deliver meaningful progress in achieving sustainable development goals . " <p> " The ASEAN leaders gathering will serve as a platform at the highest levels for further engagement of ASEAN leaders with the IMF managing director , WB President , and UN Secretary General , " West said in a Palace briefing . <p> " The gathering will discuss key issues related to building synergy in strengthening @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ regional integration . " <p> West said Duterte , for his part , will discuss the Philippine government 's efforts in achieving development goals , and supporting micro , small and medium enterprises . Duterte will also call for economic resilience amid prevailing global economic uncertainties . <p> The President will also seek to encourage deeper cooperation between the ASEAN , IMF , and WB to implement the ASEAN Vision 2025 , spells out the goals of the regional bloc as a political security and economic community , and the UN 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development . <p> West said the topics to be covered by the 2.5-hour meeting include minimizing the adverse impact of climate change , alleviating poverty , tackling inequalities and promoting women 's empowerment , energy connectivity and technical solutions for energy efficiency , promoting greening and MSMEs , agriculture , education , health , food and nutrition , enhancing social security , and promoting decent work for all .
