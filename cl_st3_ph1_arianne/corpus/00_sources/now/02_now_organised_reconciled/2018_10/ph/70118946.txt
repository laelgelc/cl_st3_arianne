Whole cultures , economies , and industries have been built around rice -- from research , to planting , to consumption . Songs about planting rice as well as idyllic paintings of rice farms and farmers have defined a good part of our own art history . Those paintings symbolize our connection with the land -- as if the land only had a singular role , and that was to feed humans . <p> If you are a prolific rice eater as most Asians are , then it 's likely that when you go to other places in the world , you would take great pains to make sure rice is part of your diet . But there is another threat that has not been in the news : rice is being stripped of its nutritional value , no thanks to the air . <p> A study that came out last May 2018 studied , for the first time , the effect of increasing concentrations of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere on the nutrients that rice contains .
