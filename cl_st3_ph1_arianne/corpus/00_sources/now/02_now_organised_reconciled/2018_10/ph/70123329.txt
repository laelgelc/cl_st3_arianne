<p> STOCKHOLM - Americans William Nordhaus and Paul Romer won the 2018 Nobel Economics Prize for work in integrating climate change and technological innovation into economic analysis , the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences said on Monday . <p> Nordhaus , of Yale University , was the first person to create a quantitative model that described the interplay between the economy and the climate , the academy said . <p> Romer , of New York University 's Stern School of Business , has shown how economic forces govern the willingness of firms to produce new ideas and innovations , laying the foundations for a new model for development , known as endogenous growth theory . <p> " Their findings have significantly broadened the scope of economic analysis by constructing models that explain how the market economy interacts with nature and knowledge , " the academy said in statement . <p> Worth 9 million Swedish crowns ( $1 million ) , the economics prize was established in 1968 . It was not part of the original group of five awards set out in Swedish industrialist Alfred Nobel 's 1895 will . <p> " This year 's Laureates do not deliver @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ closer to answering the question of how we can achieve sustained and sustainable global economic growth , " the Academy said . <p> The Nobel prizes for physiology or medicine , physics , chemistry and peace were awarded last week . <p> This year 's awards have stood out for two reasons . <p> Proceedings have been overshadowed by the absence of the literature prize , postponed to give the Swedish Academy time to restore public trust after a sexual assault scandal . <p> Three women have been awarded Nobel prizes , an unusually large number for a single year .
