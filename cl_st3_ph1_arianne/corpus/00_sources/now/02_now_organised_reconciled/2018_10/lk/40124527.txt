<h> SL retains 16th place in UNCTAD 's Liner Shipping Connectivity Index <p> 2018-10-08 10:06:49 <p> 0 <p> 574 <p> Sri Lanka held on to the 16th best connected trading nation to others by sea status in 2018 , according to the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development ( UNCTAD ) ' s latest Liner Shipping Connectivity Index ( LSCI ) , released alongside the Review of Maritime Transport 2018 . <p> The country 's LSCI number has increased by 108.9 percent to 72.46 in 2018 since UNCTAD first compiled the index in 2004 . <p> The best-connected territories for seaborne trade were all in Asia led by China ( 1 ) , Singapore ( 2 ) , Republic of Korea ( 3 ) , Hong Kong ( China ) ( 4 ) , and Malaysia ( 5 ) rounding out the top 5 , each with a score of more than 100 according to the index 's metrics . <p> The LSCI is an indicator of a country 's position within the global liner shipping networks . It is calculated from data on the world 's container ship deployment -- the number of ships , their container @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the size of the largest ship . <p> Seaborne trade expanded by a healthy 4 percent in 2017 , the fastest growth in five years , while UNCTAD forecasts similar growth this year . <p> According to its Review of Maritime Transport 2018 , volumes across all segments are set to grow in 2018 , with containerized and dry bulk commodities expected to record the fastest growth at the expense of tanker volumes . <p> The 2018 edition of the UNCTAD Review of Maritime Transport , marking its 50th year of publication , was launched at the Global Maritime Forum 's Annual Summit that took place in Hong Kong on 3 -- 4 October 2018 . <p> " While the prospects for seaborne trade are positive , these are threatened by the outbreak of trade wars and increased inward-looking policies . Escalating protectionism and tit-for-tat tariff battles will potentially disrupt the global trading system which underpins demand for maritime transport , " UNCTAD Secretary General Mukhisa Kituyi said . <p> UNCTAD 's Review of Maritime Transport 2018 has identified seven key trends that are currently redefining the maritime transport @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are : protectionism , digitalization , e-commerce and the implementation of the Belt and Road Initiative , excessive new capacity , consolidation , relationship between ports and container shipping lines , scale and climate change . <p> The authors of the report stressed that these trends entail challenges and opportunities which require continued monitoring and assessment for effective and sound policymaking . They highlighted that Belt and Road Initiative would lead China to achieve higher growth rates surpassing the traditional global operators . <p> " Port development and refurbishment projects under the Belt and Road Initiative , for example in Pakistan ( Gwadar ) , Djibouti , Myanmar ( Kyaukpyu ) , Greece ( Piraeus ) , and Sri Lanka ( Hambantota and Colombo ) are contributing to the upgrading and up scaling of port infrastructure in Africa , Asia and Europe . <p> " Chinese investment in container ports is expected to grow as port operators in China continue to expand internationally , ultimately surpassing the growth of traditional global operators , " the report stated .
