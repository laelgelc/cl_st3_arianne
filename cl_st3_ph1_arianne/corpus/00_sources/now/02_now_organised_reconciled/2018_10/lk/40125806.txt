<h> Landmark UN climate report warns a risk of crisis as early as 2040 <p> October 8 , 2018 05:01 pm <p> - <p> Temperatures could rise by 1.5 degrees Celsius as early as 2030 if global warming continues at its current pace and the world fails to take rapid and unprecedented measures to stem the increase , experts warned in a landmark UN report on Monday . <p> The report is seen as the main scientific guide for government policymakers on how to implement the 2015 Paris Agreement , which aims to limit the rise in global average temperatures to " well below " 2C above pre-industrial levels , while seeking to tighten the goal to 1.5C . The report was finalised at a special meeting in South Korea last week . <p> Meeting the 1.5C limit would demand " rapid , far-reaching and unprecedented change in all aspects of society " , the panel said .
