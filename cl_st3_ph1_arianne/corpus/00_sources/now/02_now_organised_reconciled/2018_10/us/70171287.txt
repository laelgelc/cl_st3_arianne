" Mostly the reason that we are revising the standards is that they have n't been looked at in about 15 years . " <p> Swiat said that the standards were different from those supported by outgoing Douglas , who preferred standards developed by Hillsdale College , a conservative college in Michigan . <p> The board instead adopted standards set by the Department of Education alongside a working group of both teachers and members of the general public , which provided input . <p> " There was contention behind a few different issues . With the science standards , the superintendent did @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were frightened that evolution as well as climate change were n't included at the level that they though it should have been , " Swiat said . <p> It will be up to local districts to interpret the standards , according to Swiat . <p> " The standards of education are what kids need to know . From there , school districts are able to develop curriculum , " he said . <p> Douglas finished third in the GOP primary in August . She will be replaced by either Republican Frank Riggs or Democrat Kathy Hoffman , who square off in November 's general election .
