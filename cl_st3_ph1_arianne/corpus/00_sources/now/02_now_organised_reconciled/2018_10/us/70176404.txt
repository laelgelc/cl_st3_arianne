<p> Super Typhoon Yutu rampaged through the U.S. Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands on Thursday , leaving behind storm damage that residents are calling the worst they have ever experienced . <p> Tied for the strongest storm anywhere in the world this year , Yutu packed sustained winds of 180 mph , and its gigantic eye enveloped much of the island of Saipan and all of Tinian , leaving the Pacific islands " mangled , " as one local official described it to The Washington Post .
