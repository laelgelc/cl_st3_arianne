Such investors played a huge part in bringing down the racial segregation practice of apartheid in Africa by choosing not to invest in certain African companies at the time . But early on , such investors also knowingly paid the price of lower returns on their investments : After all , lots of extra work is involved with evaluating and screening companies and how they conduct business .
