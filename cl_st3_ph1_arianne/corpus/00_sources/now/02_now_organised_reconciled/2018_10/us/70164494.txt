<h> Vinis : Measure 105 does n't belong in our ' culture of yes ' <p> By Lucy Vinis <h> Sunday <p> Oct 21 , 2018 at 12:01 AM <p> One of my top priorities as your mayor is to bring people together to collectively address the opportunities and challenges that face us and to do so with a spirit of optimism , generosity , collaboration and open-mindedness . I call it a culture of " yes , " and we are making great progress . <p> Our economy is growing , and we continue to invest in our roads and parks . Our downtown is expanding toward the river . We recently broke ground on a new Kidsports facility at the Civic Stadium site . On the climate change front , a new Equity Panel has been formed to ensure that everyone has a voice at the table . The City is finalizing a Spanish-language version of its website , and we are planning ways to recognize the many cultures in our community in anticipation of being on the world stage in 2021 . <p> Into this culture of " yes " comes the dark cloud of Measure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a 1987 Oregon law that ensures that state and local police resources ca n't be commandeered by federal agents to enforce immigration violations unless a person has already been detained for committing a crime . Our local police resources are already stretched to the limit without having to worry about doing the federal government 's job . <p> One of my first actions as Mayor was to preside over an ad hoc committee to advise city council on protecting individuals from profiling . In March , 2017 , I signed an ordinance ensuring that our local resources would not be used to detect or apprehend people whose only violation of the law is an immigration violation . <p> This September , City Manager Jon Ruiz issued an administrative order clarifying that the ordinance also prohibits all city departments from collecting or retaining individually identifiable information about immigration status unless that information is necessary to deliver a city service or is voluntarily offered . Let 's continue to nurture a culture of " yes . " Please join me in voting No on Measure 105 . <p> Lucy Vinis is the mayor of Eugene .
