<h> University of Minnesota exhibit focuses on water <p> MINNEAPOLIS ( AP ) -- A new , traveling exhibit hosted by the University of Minnesota showcases the stories and relationships Minnesota residents have with water . <p> The Minnesota Daily reports that the We Are Water MN exhibit recently opened at the university 's Institute on the Environment on the St. Paul campus . <p> University researchers say the exhibit underscores the importance of connecting water research with community movements . It was organized by local nonprofit Minnesota Humanities Center in collaboration with several state agencies . <p> River Life program coordinator Patrick Nunnally says the collaboration comes as the state 's waters are facing stresses , such as aging water infrastructure , increasing pollution and climate change . <p> The exhibit will be at the university until late November before it moves to other locations in Minnesota .
