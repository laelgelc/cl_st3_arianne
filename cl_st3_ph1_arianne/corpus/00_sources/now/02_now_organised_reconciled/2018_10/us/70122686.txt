<h> ' Rise of the Superstorms ' featured Oct. 15 <p> Oct 8 , 2018 <p> NEWBURYPORT -- In a continuing effort to make local communities aware of how climate change is energizing storm systems and affecting coastlines , Storm Surge of the Merrimack Valley is offering a public event at the Parker River National Wildlife Refuge on Oct. 15 at 7 p.m . <p> The event , at 6 Plum Island Turnpike , will be the first of the Storm Surge 2018-19 program , designed to inform , present new research and discuss how increasing , severe weather events are affecting inhabited areas at great human and monetary costs to society , according to a press release . <p> In a Daily News column earlier this year , Storm Surge 's Michael Morris cited the escalating expenses that the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration reported since 1980 , growing from $1 billion in damage to more than $306 billion in 2017 . <p> The event Oct. 15 will include a viewing of the PBS film " Rise of the Superstorms , " which covers the three major storms hitting Houston , Florida and the Caribbean in summer 2017 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ question-and-answer session to discuss how climate change is increasing the exposure to these types of storms in New England and why Greater Newburyport must continue to take these risks seriously and develop a comprehensive resiliency plan . <p> Last year , Storm Surge presented Chris Raia , a police officer from Toms River , New Jersey , who walked the audience through the damage Hurricane Sandy did to his community and cited the parallels of Toms River and Plum Island . Both are barrier islands susceptible to storm-related flooding , rising tides and , ultimately , a breach that can seriously affect Greater Newburyport . <p> The discussion will focus on how these superstorms are the new normal and on how well they can be predicted . It will also focus on the increased need for accommodating climate refugees as hurricanes become more frequent and severe . <p> On Nov. 13 , Storm Surge will feature Cameron Wake , a research professor and Sustainability Institute Program chairman at the University of New Hampshire . <p> First Amendment : Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion , or @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of speech , or of the press ; or the right of the people peaceably to assemble , and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances .
