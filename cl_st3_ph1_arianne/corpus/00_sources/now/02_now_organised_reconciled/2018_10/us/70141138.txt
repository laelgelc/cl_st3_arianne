<p> Photographer and Freeport resident Kerry Michaels made her first " vote " image in 2008 . She shot her second in 2010 , or thereabouts . <p> " The idea started as a way to just say ' vote the environment , ' " she explained . Her kids were coming of age . She wanted them to understand " how desperately important it is for their future to vote . " <p> She 'd walk around her yard ( Michaels is an accomplished gardener ) searching out leaves , flowers , twigs , berries that she could use to spell out the word " vote , " the natural materials underscoring her message . <p> " Then , of course , in 2016 I went kind of berserk , " Michaels said , making and shooting multiple vote images , then posting the images to Facebook , Instagram ( kerrymi ) and Twitter , often with a link to an organization , like Vote.org . <p> This past month , Michaels has picked up the pace again .
