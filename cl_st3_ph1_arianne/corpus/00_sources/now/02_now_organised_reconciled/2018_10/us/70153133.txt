<p> But those seeking a more lasting -- and environmentally friendly -- symbol might instead consider planting a tree . It worked for a romantic young man from Leeds a century ago , whose tree has just been voted the UK 's favourite . <p> Vic Stead grafted three beech saplings together in Aberford , where he would walk along the colliery railway every day to see his beloved . Bent into an N shape , the trees grew to form a lasting tribute to Nellie , and evidently worked , as the pair ended up married .
