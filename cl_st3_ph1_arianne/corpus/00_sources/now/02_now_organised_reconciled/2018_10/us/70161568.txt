Out beachcombing once in October , I was drawn on by a white blob at the tideline . It was a baby grey seal washed in from one of the islands -- Caher , perhaps , or Frehil , where nobody goes . <p> Scarcely a metre long and still in its silky birth coat , it had enough energy to mount threatening little charges on its flippers , hissing like a wildcat if I leaned too closely in . <p> What , if anything , was expected of me ?
