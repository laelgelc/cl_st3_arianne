<h> Using Steel ' Spiders , ' We Can Revive Large Swathes Of Coral Reefs <p> Science I write about the ocean , climate change , and the future of our planet . <p> Coral spiders deployed in the reef off the coast of Pulau Badi.Jordan Hollarsmith <p> The Coral Triangle exists in the southwest Pacific Ocean , surrounded by Indonesia , Malaysia , Papua New Guinea , the Philippines , the Solomon Islands , and Timor-Leste . Considered a " biodiversity hotspot " , this 2,200,000 square-mile expanse of coral reefs is directly linked to the livelihoods of nearly 120 million people and is threatened by poor management , destructive fishing practices , pollution , and climate change . <p> Off the shores of the tiny Indonesian island of Pulau Badi - part of the Spermonde Archipelago in South Sulawesi - exists a coral reef that supports the largest fishery in Indonesia . A two-hectare portion of this reef has been beleaguered by human disturbances over the past half-century . From March 2013 to September 2015 , 11,000 coral spiders were anchored in coral rubble across a 75,000 square-foot stretch of the degraded reef , and scientists surveyed coral growth on the spiders and nearby rubble from 2014 to 2017 . <p> Local Indonesians work with Mars Inc. to deploy coral spiders made from rebar.Jordan Hollarsmith <p> Although coral growth on the spiders rarely reached the amount seen at a nearby undamaged site , the amount of corals on and around the spiders increased from 6-8% to over 60% over the course of the surveys . Additionally , these corals did not appear to bleach after intense temperature spikes occurred in the seawater ( over 86 ? F ) . And , as coral continued to grow , researchers observed an influx of reef fishes including , rabbitfish , parrotfish , and surgeonfish . <p> A fish embedded in coral.Gabriel Ng <p> The corals themselves may also be capable of recovering from severe human disturbances . In late 2014 , illegal cyanide fishing activities occurred in one of the rehabilitated areas . After being reported to authorities , vindictive fishermen removed and destroyed several spiders , causing the amount of live coral to drop from 50% to 19% . However , a February 2016 survey showed that the remaining coral had rebounded to 55% . <p> Coral reef restoration is a novel endeavor with myriad approaches - from gardening corals in a controlled laboratory setting to using electricity to stimulate coral growth . While these techniques have been effective to different degrees , they are generally not feasible at large scales .
