<h> Redefining Healthcare with Design of the Green Hospital <p> The design of eco-friendly , energy-efficient , green construction projects has been gaining attention in all quarters , from the construction of private homes , office buildings , to large buildings such as hospital complexes . In the health sector , the so-called " Green Hospital " is a concept that is beginning to redefine how healthcare facilities are built to protect the environment while saving human lives . <p> The greater the amount of energy consumed in a hospital , the greater the release of toxic wastes to the environment , causing damage which may put human lives at risk of other diseases and death . <p> The shift to constructing sustainable healthcare facilities is largely centered on reducing the carbon burden in hospitals while ensuring that the occupants -- staff and patients -- are kept safe . <p> More and more hospital administrators are beginning to involve architects in incorporating green concepts into hospital design .
