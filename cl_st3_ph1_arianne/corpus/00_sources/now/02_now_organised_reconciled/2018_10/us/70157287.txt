<h> Help Foreign Policy Pick Its Top 100 Global Thinkers of 2018 <h> We 're asking for readers ' help in building our list of the year 's most influential names . <p> Every year , Foreign Policy puts out a list of the world 's top 100 Global Thinkers . <p> 2018 marks the 10th year of this special edition , and to mark it , we are holding 10 spots on the list for readers to decide . This is your chance to help us pick the most important Global Thinkers of this year . <p> Enter the name of your nominee , your email address , and a brief explanation justifying your selection . Keep in mind that your choice should be based on contributions made in the last 12 months in foreign policy , international security , global economics , technology , business , health care , climate change , art , activism , or science .
