<h> Foretelling the Future of Oil <p> It 's hard if not impossible to say for sure , but there are few more important questions , since the answer will be a key guide to the fate of humanity over the next century . <p> It 's worth speculating about because the climate will depend on it , with the burning of gasoline and diesel key drivers for global warming . The balance of international power is also in play , with the future fortunes of such foes as Russia and Iran tied to the commodity . Huge fortunes could be made or lost from any shifts in the energy economy , which is currently controlled by some of the largest private and state corporations in the world . <p> Predicting the energy sources of the future has always been a fool 's game . In the 1950s , nuclear energy was supposed to promise cheap power for all but is now in eclipse . Biofuels seemed to hold great promise a decade ago , but now are little more than an afterthought .
