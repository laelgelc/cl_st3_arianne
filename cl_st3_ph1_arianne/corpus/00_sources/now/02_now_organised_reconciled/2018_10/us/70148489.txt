<h> Judge clears way for trial of kids ' climate change case <p> Ruling that a group of teenagers is entitled to a court test of their claim that their constitutional right to a safe environment has been violated by years of federal government neglect , a federal trial judge in Oregon moved on Monday to start that trial in just two weeks . <p> Unless the Trump Administration persuades a higher court to step in , and it is almost certainly going to attempt that , U.S. District Judge Ann Aiken plans to get started on that case in her Eugene courtroom on October 29 . She rejected an Administration plea to put the case on hold . <p> While Judge Aiken also refused to give the government permission to file a pre-trial appeal in the case , there are alternative ways that federal attorneys can use to ask higher courts to postpone or even to dismiss the entire case outright .
