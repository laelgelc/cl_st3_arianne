Stevenson , who used the alias Evanna Lopez in the scheme , did not return the money , police say . <p> The man sold his home several years ago and transferred the money to Stevenson , police say . Stevenson later requested more money , purportedly to burn in an effort to " ward off the spirits , " police say .
