And those rules made rape cases especially difficult to report , prosecute , and punish . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and rules mandating that juries be given special cautionary instructions before deliberating rape cases , were premised on the idea ( to paraphrase Matthew Hale ) that rape charges were easy to bring but difficult to defend , " though the defendant be never so innocent . This gets the truth exactly backwards . <p> To be sure , the presumption of innocence finds one important expression in the criminal setting . But its basic idea , that the party who accuses must bear the burden of proving the charge , is rooted not in law but in morality . It is exceptionally difficult to prove a negative .
