<p> As you may have heard , the world is in a state of crisis , and climate change is putting our very future on the line . On the new show , Clark looks at the state of humanity , its interaction with technology , and what existential risks the human race faces from the universe out there -- or more frighteningly , from right here on Earth . Over the course of 10 episodes , Clark will chat with those most likely to know what humanity is up against and whether we are too clever for our own good , including astrobiologists , philosophers , economists , and transhumanists . <p> Hate to say it , but it 's not looking good for us humans .
