Photo by Glenn Puit . <p> ASHLAND The city 's Board of Commissioners conducted its first reading of a new firework ordinance on Thursday . <p> The document states fireworks may only be discharged or used between the hours of noon and 11 p.m. from June 30th through July 4th , on December 31st and any day which Paul G. Blazer High School football team plays a game at Putnam Stadium . <p> Outside of the approved dates , it would be unlawful to set off fireworks without an approved permit by the City Fire Chief . There will be no fee for a permit . Any person convicted of violating the ordinance would be guilty of a violation set out by state regulations and result in a $250 fine . <p> The discussion of an ordinance began in July after extensive complaints from residents about fireworks going off at all hours of the night , scaring pets , presenting a fire danger and putting those with fragile medical conditions in jeopardy . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fireworks with such public displays handled by a competent display operator , such as Summer Motion . <p> Discussion at the meeting Thursday focused on the proposed times of noon through 10 p.m . <p> Commissioner Amanda Clark asked that the time be moved from 10 p.m. to 11 p.m. considering annual city fireworks can sometimes take place after 10 p.m . <p> " If we 're going to set them off at 10:15 , why ca n't the public ?
