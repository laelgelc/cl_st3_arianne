<h> Dixie Sokolik : Always look on the bright side of life <p> As I wake up today , it 's looking dreary outside our windows -- wet and highly windy . Whether it is a dreary rainy day , the political scene across the country , or the climate change warnings that go unheeded by many , it can cause a person to feel despondent . We may complain and grouse about the weather here , but when I think of the horrific destruction from the recent hurricanes in Florida and the Carolinas , I feel guilty complaining about a little wet and cold weather event . <p> Here in Winona , we rarely witness severe destruction from storms , even though it is possible and has happened with torrential rains and an occasional tornado . But none of it compares to the hurricanes that are happening more frequently and with more devastation . <p> I realize that with storms like this and other disasters around the world , whether weather related or manmade , occur so often that it can cause depression even in those who are n't already suffering with the painful disease . I do n't mean a Pollyanna attitude , but a sincere caring attitude that can help others through their pain . <p> Even as I write this on a rather gloomy day , I know that the air will become crisp and fresh shortly ; we will enjoy the autumn scenes and upcoming holidays ; we will be able to change into cuddly winter garments ; and we all have a chance to vote soon ( if you have n't already ) , and hopefully , we will see and feel a sweeping change in the political direction in the country . And if the USA becomes better , our allies across the globe will relax a bit more . <p> Many times the world news is filled with gloom and doom , but on most networks , at the end of the broadcast , there will be an upbeat story about something that has recently happened that just has to make us smile . So it behooves one to stay tuned till the end -- perhaps literally ! <p> If you ever saw the play or film " South Pacific " you will remember the very joyful song " Happy Talk . " The song can be found on a computer or your phone . I recently downloaded it because I needed the uplifting tune and lyrics . Some of which are : Happy talk , keep talking happy talk . Talk about things you like to do . You got to have a dream , if you do n't have a dream how you gon na have a dream come true ? <p> The lyrics go on to describe many little overlooked special moments in everyday life and nature . It is sung by a wonderful musical theatre and film actress , Juanita Hall . <p> We need to hang on to happy thoughts . And I do n't mean to hide our heads in the sand and ignore the tragedies taking place here @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of us can do , whether it is donating goods and services to areas undergoing devastating weather events ; physically assisting others who have lost homes or undergone other tragedies in their lives ; finding reputable organizations that are giving assistance throughout the United States and around the world and contributing resources to them as we are able ; and of course , any one of us can lift up prayers to whatever higher power we worship . <p> And throughout all of this , we have to do our best to keep our own attitude as positive as possible . If you 've ever been in a real downer mood ( and we all have ) do n't you feel better being around someone who is optimistic and cheerful ? I do n't mean artificially and mechanically happy -- it 's easy to tell the difference . But as they recognize the troubles and problems that surround us , they maintain a " can do " attitude . No , they ca n't cure the world of its woes , but they can do more by being a positive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ time . <p> For today , I am going to find out what type of trustworthy organizations are helping people who are suffering from these latest weather disasters and do what I can to contribute . If I were physically able , I would go to some of those places and do what I could . And it would n't be to just throw some rolls of paper towels to them ! <p> And that reminds me , once again , whatever you do or do n't do , be certain to vote in the upcoming election . Too many people give up and feel it does n't do any good . But ( and I know you hear this more than you believe ) every vote counts ! Not only do we need to cast our individual ballots , but also we must remind all of our friends and family members to get out there on or before Nov. 6 . Our country and the world is in need of a big change -- and it can start with our own government taking responsibility for trying to help , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its people . <p> In the meantime , try to maintain positivity and think about the words and melody in that song " Happy Talk .
