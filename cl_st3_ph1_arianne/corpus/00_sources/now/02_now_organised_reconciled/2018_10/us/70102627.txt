<p> The Sahara Desert used to be home to a thriving ecosystem . About 8,000 years ago , the region had wetter conditions that supported plants , animals , and the people who lived there . <p> Northern Africa was wetter than it is today during the African Humid Period , colloquially known as the Green Sahara , because of a series of monsoons . As the Earth 's orbit slowly changed , however , rains became less and the vegetation started to die off . <p> About 5,500 years ago , the Sahara underwent a terminal decline that led to it becoming the desert that we know today . <p> Some experts think that humans played a major role in the desertification of the Sahara .
