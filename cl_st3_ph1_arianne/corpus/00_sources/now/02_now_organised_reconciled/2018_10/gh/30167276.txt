<p> The European Union ( EU ) has welcomed the ' Ghana Beyond Aid ' agenda with a pledge to offer the needed financial and technical support to help translate the initiative into reality . <p> This was disclosed by the Ambassador and Head of Delegation of the European Union ( EU ) to Ghana , Diana Acconcia , when she met the Minister for Foreign Affairs and Regional Integration , Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey on Wednesday , October 17 , 2018 . <p> Ambassador Diana Acconcia said the EU is committed to a strengthened multilateral system between the two sides , assuring of continued efforts to help grow Ghana 's economy at all levels . <p> Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey , on her part , lauded the European Union for the recent signing of 175 million Euro financial agreement with Ghana . <p> The gesture was in support of the implementation of specific developmental projects in the areas of Public Financial Management ( PFM ) , Economic Partnership Agreement ( EPA ) , sustainable Agricultural Development in the Savannah Ecological Zone , decentralization and climate change for a period of seven years . <p> The minister was , however , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( IEPA ) , which will enable Ghana
