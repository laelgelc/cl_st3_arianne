<h> Staff of the Youth in Afforestation programme have rejected government 's decision to slash their allowances . <p> According to them , such a decision contradicts the initial contract they signed with the Forestry Commission and the Youth Employment Agency ( YEA ) . <p> Earlier this month , the Forestry Commission communicated to all beneficiaries , explaining that their allowances have been slashed in order to accommodate the cost involved in paying workers of the newly introduced Nation Builders Corps ( NABCo ) . <p> Supervisors will now receive a monthly allowance of GHc 700 , instead of the GHc 1,200 which they previously took home . <p> The allowance of HND holders , who were initially earning GHc 800 as deputy supervisors , has also been slashed to GHc 700 . <p> However , there was no change in the salaries of workers with WASSCE certificates -- such personnel will continue to receive GHc 400 . <p> The decision to slash the allowances has not gone down well with some staff of the Youth in Afforestation programme , who are currently threatening a protest . <p> " The decision by the management is in total breach @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Youth Employment Agency made an offer and we , beneficiaries , accepted it and appended our signatures , hence , it becomes binding under the law of contract , " sections of the letter read . <p> " We , therefore , entreat the management , Board and the government , to rescind their decision of reducing our meagre allowances and respect the original contract to avert putting the much-applauded project , which is also aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals -- SDG 13 on Climate Change , into jeopardy .
