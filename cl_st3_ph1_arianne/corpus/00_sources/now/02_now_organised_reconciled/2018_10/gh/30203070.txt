<p> The new ambassadorial role for the musician comes after a couple of others . <p> He has been a climate change ambassador as well as one for the eradication of hepatitis B. <p> Okyeame Kwame 's new made-in-Ghana song features Kidi , which was produced by Dan Grahl and arranged by Ebo Taylor . <p> Kwame released his first solo album ' Boshe Ba ' ( Promised Child ) in 2004 , just after the break-up of the Akyeame Band . <p> He followed in 2008 with ' Manwesem ' ( My Poetry ) and in 2011 with ' The Clinic ' , launched at the Aphrodisiac Night Club in Accra . <p> Okyeame Kwame has helped to discover native African talents by working as a judge on music reality shows in Ghana and has also acted in Ghanaian films which include ' Ties That Bind ' , ' The Comforter ' and ' Amsterdam Diary ' .
