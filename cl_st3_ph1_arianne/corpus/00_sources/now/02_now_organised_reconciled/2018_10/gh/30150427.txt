<p> The Agona West Municipal Directorate of the National Disaster Management Organisation ( NADMO ) has marked the World Disaster Day with a call on the citizenry to stop felling of trees to avoid climate change . <p> It also cautioned the public , especially estate developers to desist from putting up buildings on waterways , to prevent flooding and disasters . <p> The week-long celebrations , took officials of the Directorate to churches , mosques , market places , schools , keep fit clubs , civic organisations , driver 's unions and students bodies to sensitise them on the need to prevent disasters . <p> Mrs Justina Marigold Assan , the Agona West Municipal Chief Executive expressed regret over the alarming rate at which trees were being fell illegally and affecting the environment .
