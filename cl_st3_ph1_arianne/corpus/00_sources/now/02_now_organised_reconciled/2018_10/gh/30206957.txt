<p> We , the members of the Civil Society Organisations Platform on Sustainable DevelopmentGoal 13 ( CSOs SDG 13 ) Sub-Platform , in furtherance of our firm commitment to support national development processes led by the National Development Planning Commission ( NDPC ) , resolved at our 1st General Meeting held at Ampomaah hotel in Accra which discussed issues bothering on SDG 13 including ways of supporting its achievement .
