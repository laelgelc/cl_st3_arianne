<h> Registration opens for 2019 Greenway Cycling competition <p> Registration for the 2019 edition of Greenway National Cycling Competition has been launched in Accra . <p> The competition , which is being organised by Greenway International Foundation , an environmental protection organisation working for a carbon-free environment and oceans free of single-use plastics . <p> This historic event which will take place on January 15 through to July 21 , 2019 , is open to the public from who are between 19 - 45 years . <p> About 5,000 participants are expected to take part in the five-laps competition in the various regions and cover a distance of about 300 kilometres . <p> Executive Director of the Foundation , Michael Olusegun Olusanya who performed the official launch on Monday , said the Greenway National Cycling Competition is in active partnership with the Greater Accra Cycling Association chaired Kofi Boakye-Yiadom . <p> Mr. Olusanya said the ultimate aim is to create awareness among people to mitigate the effect of climate change and progressively , to reduce greenhouse gases in all 10 regions of Ghana . <p> He urged those who wish to participate in the event to log on their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take exciting prizes worth over ? 100,000 to the potential winners and other amazing prizes . <p> He said the registration continues till December 31 , 2018 . <p> Prizes <p> The overall winner will take home a cash prize of ? 15,000 , two pure gold medals , a brand new bicycle , National Cycling for Climate Honor and extras . <p> The runner-up will receive ? 10,000 , one pure gold medal , a brand new bicycle , National Cycling for Climate Honor and extras . <p> Third Prize winner will get ? 7,000 , one pure gold medal , a brand new bicycle , National Cycling for Climate Honor and extras . <p> Fourth to 10th winners will take home silver medals , trophies , a brand new bicycle , National Cycling for Climate Honor and extras .
