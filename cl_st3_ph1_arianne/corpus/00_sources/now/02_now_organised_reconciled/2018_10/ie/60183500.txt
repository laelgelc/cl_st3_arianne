<h> Signal Group : Monitoring is essential for VOC emissions reduction <p> UK-based Signal Group develops and manufactures gas analysers for the professional testing of volatile organic compounds ( VOCs ) , vehicle and engine emissions , tobacco smoke , impurities in special gases , power station emissions , catalyst efficiency , and boiler/burner combustion emissions . <p> John Clements , Managing Director of Signal Group , explained to gasworld why VOC emissions reduction is important , how monitoring performs an essential role and what the latest developments are in monitoring technology . <p> Can you tell us a bit of background about VOCs ? <p> In general , VOCs are harmful to both health and the environment , so management of the risks associated with VOCs necessitates accurate and reliable monitoring . However , it is first necessary to distinguish between the two main areas of concern -- indoor and outdoor VOCs , not least because the definition of VOCs is different for these two applications . <p> Indoors , VOCs are organic chemical compounds which evaporate under normal indoor atmospheric conditions of temperature and pressure . Some organic chemicals are harmful to the skin and can be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be breathed in and cause a wide variety of negative health effects ranging from minor irritation to death . <p> In the outdoor environment , the main concern with VOCs is their role in the formation of ozone , a constituent of photochemical smog . At ground level , ozone is generated when VOCs react with sources of oxygen molecules such as nitrogen oxides , and carbon monoxide in the atmosphere in the presence of sunlight . Ozone can be harmful to health , particularly in children , the elderly , and people of all ages who have cardiovascular problems such as asthma or COPD . Ground level ozone can also have harmful effects on sensitive vegetation and ecosystems . <p> In the outdoor environment , VOCs are commonly defined as any organic compounds which are emitted from non-natural processes and have photochemical ozone creation potential . This is generally interpreted as any organic compound released to the atmosphere from an operator 's plant or process , excluding releases of naturally produced VOCs from within the plant boundary and methane . <p> In most countries , the emissions of regulated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ permits that include an emission limit for total organic carbon ( TOC ) . The Standard Reference Method for the measurement of TOC is with a Flame Ionisation Detector ( FID ) . In some cases , where there is a potential for the emission of particularly toxic VOCs , the site permit may include a requirement for the monitoring of individual organic compounds , which means that a monitoring technology capable of speciation would be necessary . Alternatively , it may be necessary for the monitoring activity to distinguish between methane and non-methane VOCs . <p> What are the advantages of monitoring VOCs ? <p> In addition to regulatory compliance , there are a host of other reasons for monitoring VOC emissions . For example , monitoring is often employed to measure and check abatement plant efficiency . Monitoring can also help to optimise process flows and can help to identify solvent reduction opportunities , which lower costs , improve environmental performance and reduce risks to workers . Hydrogen or a hydrogen/helium mixture is used as the fuel for the flame which burns in hydrocarbon free combustion air . An electric field is generated by a polarisation voltage between two electrodes , and the ions generated by the combustion result in a charge which is directly proportional to the quantity of Carbon atoms derived from organic compounds in the sample . <p> Globally , there is a significant number of manufacturers developing portable VOC detectors for fugitive VOC emissions , most of which employ a photoionisation detector ( PID ) . However , this technology is not suitable for TOC emissions monitoring because of the enormous variation in response factor between different VOCs . Solvent destruction methods include thermal or catalytic oxidation , bio-filtration , activated carbon or a sacrificial liquid , or concentration systems followed by oxidation . TOC measurement is important in emissions monitoring as an increase in TOC can indicate poor combustion efficiency , resulting in the release to air of partially combusted materials containing organic carbon , which may increase the emissions of particulates .
