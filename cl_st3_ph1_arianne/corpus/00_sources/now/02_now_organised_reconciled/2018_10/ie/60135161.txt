<h> Reigate Headmaster leads the UK in Education for Social Responsibility <p> The Headmaster of Reigate St Mary 's School ( RSM ) has partnered with local primary resource producing company , Planbee to create an exciting range of resources to enable junior schools to deliver Education for Social Responsibility ( ESR ) lessons Planbee resources could benefit all junior schools <p> Education for Social Responsibility teaches children what it means to be fulfilled as a human being . It focuses on ethical trade , ecosystems , biodiversity , climate change , finite resources and water scarcity so children are well versed in some of the most important and pressing issues of our time . The aim is to help them not only live contented , sustainable lives themselves but to mould better , fairer and more sustainable economic practices in the decades to come . <p> Marcus Culverwell , Headmaster of Reigate St Mary 's spent a sabbatical researching and writing about sustainability in education and has been working with colleagues at the Independent Association of Prep Schools ( IAPS ) to develop ESR for the past eight years . He recently teamed up with Planbee to produce @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and fun ESR lessons . <p> Surrey-based primary resource provider , PlanBee was founded in 2008 by former teacher Becky Cranham . Since its foundation , the provider has become a leading resource service for educators across the UK , which now employs a local team consisting predominantly of former primary teachers who know what it takes to produce outstanding lessons . <p> Planbee developed ESR resources in conjunction with Marcus Culverwell and these have been purchased by the Independent Association of Preps Schools so they can be made available to all IAPS schools in the UK . It is hoped that maintained schools will follow suit by using these resources within their curriculum . <p> Marcus Culverwell said , ' I am absolutely delighted that the IAPS has purchased these new resources for every IAPS school in the country as this important area of education is absolutely vital , yet historically it has been sadly neglected .
