So it has proved for Maurice Gilbert , co-owner and public face of Ballyhoura Apple Farm who has steadily been building his food business with Gerry Murphy out of the former Shannon Development site in the town of Kilfinane . <p> Derelict and overgrown only three years ago - " it cost us ? 8,000 just to clear the trees and get in the gate " - the site is now abuzz with activity , a huge Centra truck laden with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ back out as we arrive , workers are packing bottles into crates . Following on from that Gerry bought the field across the road and we had planning permission for 70 houses and I built the houses . <p> " He would sell them the site and I would sell them the house .
