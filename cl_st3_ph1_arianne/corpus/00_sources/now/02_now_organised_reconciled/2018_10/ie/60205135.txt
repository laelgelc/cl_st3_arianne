<h> Fijian appointed top job at IUCN <p> Fiji national Radhika Murti has been appointed to a top role at the world 's largest environment network , the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) . Picture : SUPPLIED <p> A FIJIAN national has been appointed to a top role at the world 's largest environment network , the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) . <p> Radhika Murti has been appointed to director role of IUCN 's global program on ecosystem management , making her one the network 's youngest directors . <p> As the new director of the global program on ecosystem management , Ms Murti is in charge of IUCN 's work covering ecosystem assessments , drylands management , disaster risk reduction , climate change adaptation and conservation on islands . <p> " I am extremely pleased with the appointment of Radhika Murti as Director of the Global Programme on Ecosystem Management , " says IUCN Director General Inger Andersen in a statement . <p> " We are grateful to Ms Murti for the valuable work she has contributed to IUCN over the past decade , and for her commitment to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and climate change . I hope that this appointment will inspire other women to continue reaching for leadership positions within the conservation community . " <p> According to the press statement , Ms Murti joined IUCN in 2008 as a Project Officer , relocating from Suva , Fiji to IUCN 's headquarters in Gland , Switzerland . <p> Prior to joining IUCN , she worked with the Ministry for Fisheries and Forest of Fiji , The University of the South Pacific and the United Nations Development Programme Multi-Country Office in Suva . <p> Nazhat Shameem Khan , Fiji 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations in Geneva also expressed pride at this important achievement by a Fijian living in Switzerland . <p> Ambassador Khan highlighted the importance of IUCN 's work protecting the natural environment in Fiji , and that Ms Murti 's appointment is of special significance to the country . <p> She also reflected on the advancement of capable women in the field of conservation , and commended Ms Murti as a role model for other Fijian women working across the world in this field . <p> Born @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a bachelor 's degree in Forestry Science from the Australian National University , a Master 's degree in Land Management from The University of the South Pacific , and will shortly be defending her PhD on nature-based solution approaches to climate change adaptation in Burkina Faso , Chile and Senegal at the University of Bern . <p> " Growing up in Fiji and experiencing disasters and , more recently , climate impacts inspired me to develop my knowledge and skills in this area of work , and be engaged , particularly through my work at IUCN , in efforts to reduce peoples ' vulnerabilities in Pacific Island countries , " Ms Murti said . <p> " There is a lot to be done and I hope to inspire more young professionals from the Pacific region to choose topics such as climate change , conservation and sustainable development for studies and career prospects .
