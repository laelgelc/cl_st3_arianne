It also called for government efforts in strengthening the monitoring of protected areas and raising public awareness of the animal . <p> The snow leopard is said to be the indicator of the health and sustainability of the mountain ecosystem . It is listed as Vulnerable on the IUCN Red List of Threatened Species because the global population is estimated to number less than 10,000 mature individuals . <p> Currently , 60 percent of the snow leopard 's habitats are located in China . The animals are mainly distributed in provinces and regions such as Qinghai , Tibet , Xinjiang , Gansu , Sichuan , Yunnan and Inner Mongolia .
