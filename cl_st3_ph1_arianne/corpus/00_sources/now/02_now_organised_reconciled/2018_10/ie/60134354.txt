<h> How solar power is changing lives in Africa <h> The unrivaled , life-changing power of solar energy : battling climate change and accelerating socio-economic development <h> The unrivaled , life-changing power of solar energy : battling climate change and accelerating socio-economic development <h> KIGALI / ROTTERDAM , 31 AUGUST 2018 - In June 2018 , Bouake , the second largest city of Cote d'Ivoire , suffered its first-ever shutdown of domestic water supply . The country 's dammed lake , which supplies 70 percent of the city 's water supply , completely ran dry . According to many experts , this was yet-again another consequence of climate change . <p> As a result of the drought , the people of Cote d'Ivoire went through extraordinary hardships affecting everything in their daily life , from acquiring clean drinking water and cooking to basic hygiene .
