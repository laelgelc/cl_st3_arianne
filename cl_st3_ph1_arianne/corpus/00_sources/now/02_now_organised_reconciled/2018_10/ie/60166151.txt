<p> The Swiss based helicopter training and services agency Next-Generation Flight Training has called on the helicopter industry to make a concerted effort to collaborate with local communities , in order to mitigate the impact of natural disasters stemming from climate change . <p> During a talk on ' Effects of Climate Events on the Helicopter Industry ' at Helitech International Christian Muller , CEO at the company said that as ' tools of catastrophic intervention ' helicopters have a unique power to carry out ' almost limitless missions ' , but that now is the time for industry to ' figure out new roles ' for their aircraft and also ' think about what new materials might be needed for future missions . ' <p> Additionally , Muller suggested that weather associated risks and hazards on local communities must be better planned and managed , as the impact of climate change , such as ' more extreme weather patterns ' become more common . <p> Addressing the impact of a summer water shortage in mountainous regions throughout Switzerland , Muller said that Swiss Army helicopters had to transport water in bambi buckets to allow cows to drink , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ country 's cheese supply . <p> ' If cows do n't have water , or they ca n't be brought off a mountain , they have to be slaughtered , ' he said . <p> In terms of joined up planned and future management of extreme weather operations , Muller suggested that local communities should be thinking of adding helicopter support guidelines to their emergency planning protocols and emphasised the speed with which rotorcraft can make timely interventions to help relief efforts . <p> ' We must work with local communities on flash flooding , and think what can we do in 30 minutes ? Can we make observations on damage from the air , ask what is coming next ? Within an hour we could have a second helicopter on site and add additional resources . We must look at capabilities and timeframe for delivery , ' he explained . <p> Muller also suggested that despite economic realities having an impact on local infrastructure , ' small communities should not fear the use of civil helicopter because of expense .
