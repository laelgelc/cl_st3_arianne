<h> ' CO2 reductions can not be borne by agriculture alone ' <p> The European Parliament has voted in favour of a 40% reduction in carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) emissions from passenger cars and vans by 2030 . <p> MEP Mairead McGuinness has welcomed the result saying : " This vote benefits the agriculture sector as the significant CO2 reductions necessary to meet our climate targets can not be borne by agriculture alone and ambitious reductions in transport are crucial . <p> There is already a strong focus to reduce emissions coming from the agriculture sector and other sectors will have to make efforts to reduce their climate impact as well . <p> The First Vice-President of the European Parliament stressed that CO2 emissions coming from the transport and agriculture sectors are counted together under effort sharing regulation -- along with emissions from the buildings and waste sectors . <p> However , transport and agriculture together account for 75% of the country 's emissions . <p> Advertisement <p> The EU Parliament has set an interim target for a 20% emissions cut by 2025 .
