<h> Trade agreement negotiations between the EU and Vietnam conclude <p> The European Union and Vietnam have recently finished negotiating a Trade Agreement and an Investment Protection Agreement . <p> Once in force , it is hoped that the agreements will provide opportunities to increase trade and support jobs and growth on both sides . <p> The European Commission has reportedly submitted the proposals for signature and conclusion of both agreements to the European Council . Once authorised by the council , the agreements will be signed and presented to the European Parliament for consent . <p> After the Parliament 's consent , the council can conclude the trade agreement and it can enter into force . The Investment Protection Agreement will be ratified by member states according to their national procedures . <p> The trade agreement is expected to eliminate virtually all tariffs on goods traded between the two sides . <p> The agreement also includes a strong , legally binding commitment to sustainable development , including the respect of human rights , labour rights , environmental protection and the fight against climate change -- with an explicit reference to the Paris Agreement , according to the commission @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ #EUtrade deal with Vietnam will help exporting EU farmers &amp; dairy producers by slashing all tariffs on their products . ? ? ? ? ? ? https : //t.co/80IqaI9Dv6 <p> Meanwhile , the president of the European Commission Jean-Claude Juncker outlined that the trade and investment agreements with Vietnam are " exemplary " of Europe 's trade policy . <p> " They bring unprecedented advantages and benefits for European and Vietnamese companies , workers and consumers . <p> " They take fully into account the economic differences between the two sides .
