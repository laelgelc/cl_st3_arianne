<p> VTT and St1 have launched a unique experiment in Finland aiming to produce hydrocarbons from industrial carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) , which can be refined to fossil-free petrol , diesel and chemicals . <p> VTT and St1 will be carrying out pilot-scale experiments at St1 's biorefinery in Jokioinen , Finland this autumn to demonstrate how hydrocarbons can be produced in a climate-friendly way from CO2 extracted from the bioethanol production and from hydrogen ( H2 ) produced with water electrolysis . The pilot is due to begin in October and run for approximately five weeks . <p> The pilot plant consists of integrated mobile containers : a unit for processing CO2 , a H2 production unit and a synthesis unit for turning the CO2 and the H2 into liquid and solid hydrocarbons . <p> " Our goal is to create new business opportunities for example to fuel and chemical producers .
