<h> The Hitchin Automated External Defibrillation Scheme is still going strong -- 16 years after purchasing its first public access defibrillator for the town centre . <p> The scheme was set up with the support of health and beauty chain Boots and raised money within the town to buy the life-saving equipment to be placed outside the Boots store . <p> Now , the town has eight defibrillators which can be access by members of the public should somebody go into cardiac arrest . <p> The scheme was initially linked with the East of England Ambulance Service to provide a pager system which alerted 12 trained volunteers in the event of a cardiac arrest . <p> This continued for three years until the Community First Responders Scheme became a nationwide initiative .
