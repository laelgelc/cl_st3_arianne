<h> Diesel , Petrol , Coal And Cigarettes Set For Budget Price Hike <p> Finance Minister Paschal Donohoe 's Budget 2019 is set to increase the prices of diesel , petrol , coal , and cigarettes , according to the Irish Independent . <p> The Minister is looking to raise money to fund social welfare hikes and income tax reductions , and he has suggested introducing a series of ' stealth taxes ' on workers and businesses . <p> At the top of this list , as is understood by the Irish Independent , is the Carbon Tax , which will drive up the cost of home heating and motoring . <p> In addition , the excise duty on cigarettes will also rise , however , it is not yet known if it will replicate last year 's 50c increase . <h> ' A Definite ' <p> " Carbon tax is a definite but we need to be careful . It could backfire because workers and people in rural Ireland will be hit worst , " one Cabinet source told the Irish Independent . <p> Discussions are believed to be already underway in mitigating the effect of a carbon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with a weekly fuel allowance increase being considered . <p> One positive push from the carbon tax is as an answer to Ireland 's lack of progress on tackling climate change and high energy consumption . <p> Last week , in its pre-budget submission , The Solid Fuel Trade Group , whose members supply the majority of coal , briquettes and other regulated solid fuels nationally , warned the Minister for Finance against a further increase in the carbon tax . <p> The fuel group said that the increased tax would cause unintended consequences on several levels including public health , the environment and the exchequer , as any further increase in tax will drive demand for more polluting fuels such as sod turf , wet wood and untaxed coal products as ' fuel-poor ' people would look for a quick and easy alternative , the group said .
