<h> Former Macra president appointed to top job for bioenergy <p> Former Macra na Feirme President Sean Finan has been appointed as the new chief executive officer of the Irish Bioenergy Association ( IrBEA ) . <p> A chartered engineer and part-time farmer from Ballinlough , Castlerea , Co Roscommon , Finan holds an engineering degree from NUI Galway as well as a Certificate in Agriculture . <p> He brings to the role a " wealth of experience gleaned in various positions within many community , voluntary , representative and statutory organisations at a local , national and European level over the years " , according to the IrBEA . <p> Prior to joining IrBEA , Finan worked in engineering and management positions with John Sisk &amp; Son over a 12-year period . <p> He took a two-year secondment from Sisk in 2015 to take up the role of 35th national president of Macra na Feirme . He also currently holds the voluntary post of vice president of the European Council of Young Farmers , CEJA . <p> Founded in May 1999 , IrBEA 's role is to promote the bioenergy industry and to develop the sector .
