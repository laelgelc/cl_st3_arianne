Before that , the existence of stone tools as far back as 22.5 million years ago could be considered antecedents , or even examples of the earliest engineers . <p> From levers , wheels and pulleys , innovations grew more sophisticated , requiring the technicalities of civil engineering . The results of this can be evidenced in humanity 's earliest shelters , buildings and bridges . <p> Things have expanded leaps and bounds since then .
