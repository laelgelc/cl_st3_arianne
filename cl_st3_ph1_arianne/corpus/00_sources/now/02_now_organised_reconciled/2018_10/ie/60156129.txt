<h> The price of beer could rise due to global warming , new research shows <h> Climate change could cause the price of beer to rise , according to new research . <p> Scientists have predicted that the effects of global warming will lead to a fall in the crop yields of barley which is an essential component of beer . <p> Droughts are expected to cause barley to decline up to 17 per cent in the US , Europe , Australia and Asia where the crop is gown most . <p> This could result in the price of beer to rise . <p> The price of a six-pack of beer could rise to ? 17 in Ireland , according to the US scientists ' economic model . <h> Advertisement <p> Dr Steven Davis , from the University of California at Irvine , who is a co-author of this research said : " The world is facing many life-threatening impacts of climate change , so people having to spend a bit more to drink beer may seem trivial by comparison . <p> " But there is definitely a cross-cultural appeal to beer , and not having a cool pint @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ adds insult to injury . " <p> Only 17 per cent of barley in the world is actually used to make the drink while the rest is used to feed livestock . <p> If there is a shortage of the crop the first thing that it would be used for is to feed hungry animals and not thirsty humans , researchers predicted . <p> The study also said that barley grown in the northern Great Plains of the US , the Canadian prairies , Europe , Australia , and the Asian steppe were more likely to be affected . <p> Dr Nathan Mueller another co-author from the University of California at Irvine , said : " Current levels of fossil fuel consumption and CO2 ( carbon dioxide ) pollution - business as usual - will result in this worst-case scenario , with more weather extremes negatively impacting the world 's beer basket . <p> " Our study showed that even modest warming will lead to increases in drought and excessive heat events in barley-growing areas , " Dr Mueller continued .
