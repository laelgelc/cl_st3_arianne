<p> The effects of global warming are likely to lead to sharp falls in crop yields of barley , an essential beer ingredient , scientists are predicting . <p> Droughts and heat waves were expected to cause barley declines of up to 17% in parts of the US , Europe , Australia and Asia where the grain is grown most . <p> As a result , the cost of beer could soar .
