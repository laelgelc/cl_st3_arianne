<h> Ireland 's brand value one of the fastest-growing in the world , says new report <p> Ireland 's nation brand continues to be one of the fastest-growing in the world , rising 26% in value year on year to US$538 billion , according to the latest Brand Finance Nation Brands report . Jumping six places in the ranking , Ireland has broken into the top 30 , growing faster than the USA , China or the UK . This can be explained by a combination of factors including the continued growth of the Irish economy over the last five years and a renewed appeal as a location for domestic and inbound investment , given that Ireland will be the only English-speaking nation in the EU following Brexit . <p> Simon Haigh , Managing Director of Brand Finance Ireland , commented : <p> " Despite US tax reductions , discussions on EU corporate tax harmonisation , and the vagaries of Brexit deal negotiations , Brand Ireland proves ever more appealing .
