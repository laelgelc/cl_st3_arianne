<h> Israel aims for zero new gasoline , diesel-powered vehicles by 2030 <p> Reuters Reuters <p> 10 October , 2018 , 1:35 pm <p> FILE PHOTO : Members of the media drive electric vehicles during the inauguration of Better Place 's new vehicle demonstration center in the Israeli city of Tel Aviv February 7 , 2010 . REUTERS/Gil Cohen Magen/File Photo <p> JERUSALEM ( Reuters ) -- Israelis will no longer be able to buy new gasoline or diesel-powered vehicles after 2030 , the Energy Ministry said on Tuesday , unveiling a plan to replace them with electric cars and trucks that run on natural gas . <p> The challenge will be creating an initial " critical mass " of cars that will move the local industry away from gasoline and diesel engines , Energy Minister Yuval Steinitz said . <p> " We are already encouraging by funding charging stations , more than 2,000 new charging stations around the country , " Steinitz told Reuters . <p> The government , he said , will also " reduce taxation on electric cars to almost zero , so they are going to be much cheaper " . <p> The electric vehicle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Israel off gasoline , diesel and coal . Israel in recent years discovered huge deposits of natural gas , a cleaner-burning fossil fuel , and it is converting its power stations accordingly . <p> The plan was released a day after a U.N . report on climate change called for major changes in the way humankind uses energy . <p> The tipping point is expected around 2025 , when , according to the ministry 's target , there will be about 177,000 electric cars on the road in Israel . Today there are just a few dozen . <p> After that , it will become easier and cheaper to own electric cars , so the ministry expects the number should jump to nearly 1.5 million by 2030.
