<p> As part of the draft rules , some single-use plastic products are to be banned where alternatives are readily available and affordable . MEPs added very lightweight plastic bags , products made of oxo-degradable plastics and fast-food containers made of expanded polystyrene to the list . <p> The consumption of several other items , for which no alternative exists , will have to be reduced by member states in an " ambitious and sustained " manner by 2025 . Other plastics , such as beverage bottles , will have to be collected separately and recycled at a rate of 90% by 2025 . <p> MEPs also agreed that reduction measures should cover waste from tobacco products too , in particular cigarette filters containing plastic which will have to be reduced by 50% by 2025 and 80% by 2030 . <p> It is claimed that one cigarette butt can pollute between 500 and 1,000 litres of water , can take up to 12 years to disintegrate .
