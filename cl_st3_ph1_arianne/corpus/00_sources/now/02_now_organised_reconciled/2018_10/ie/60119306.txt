Picture : JOVESA NAISUA <p> THE head of the Roman Catholic Church in Fiji said as a bishop he does not intend to inform Catholics who they should vote for or vote against . <p> Archbishop Peter Loy Chong said this month the church would use the Sunday Homilies to assist people in making informed choices when they go to the polls . <p> The above sentiment that had been echoed by Archbishop Chong has been posted on the Archdiocese of Suva Facebook page . Archbishop Chong said the Catholic social teaching taught that fundamental moral measure of any economy was how the poor and vulnerable were faring . <p> He also outlined the changes in poverty in Fiji . <p> In his summary , he claimed that the current economic trend showed an increase in poverty and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rich richer . <p> " As Catholics , we are called to work for greater economic justice in the face of persistent poverty and growing economic gaps , " Archbishop Chong said . <p> " We urge Catholics to use the following ethical framework for economic life as principles for reflection , criteria for judgment , directions for action and criteria for election , " he added . <p> Archbishop Chong said a fundamental moral measure of any economy would be on how the poor and vulnerable were faring and that the economy existed for the person , not the person for the economy . <p> " The global economy has moral dimensions and human consequences . " Decisions on investment , trade , aid and development should protect human life , our mother Earth and promote human rights especially for those most in need wherever they might live on this globe . " <p> He stressed that the economic life should recognise the fact that everyone was God 's children and members of one human family , called to exercise a clear priority for the poor .
