The exact number of this critically endangered species is unknown . However , many claims to have sighted it as recently as 2002 . Part of the Most Wanted Lost Species -- a global project to find and protect rare animals -- it remains one of the most endangered species that is endemic to India . Landslides , flooding and deforestation , as well as hunting for food , has made this mammal nearly extinct . Its habitat is believed to be reduced to less than 100 sq km .
