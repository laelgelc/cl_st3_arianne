<h> Share Via Email <p> NEW DELHI : While stressing on the need to work " unitedly " to address issues like terrorism , climate change , economic development and social justice , Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Sunday said that peace does not only mean " no war " . <p> " Development of the poorest of the poor is the real indicator of peace , " Modi said in the 49th episode of his monthly radio address " Mann Ki Baat " , adding that wherever there will be a talk of world peace , India 's name and contribution will be written in golden letters . <p> The Prime Minister said although India had no direct connection with the First World War it was an important event because Indian soldiers fought bravely and played a very big role by sacrificing their lives . <p> " Indian soldiers showed it to the world that they are second to none if it comes to war . Our soldiers have displayed great valour guarantees areas and adverse conditions . There has just been a single objective behind it- Restoration of peace .
