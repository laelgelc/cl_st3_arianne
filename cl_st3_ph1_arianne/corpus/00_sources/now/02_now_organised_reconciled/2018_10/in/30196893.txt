Led by Kenyan native Dave Ojay , who is pursuing social entrepreneurship course at Kanthari International here , the cleanliness campaign continued till late evening . Dave is working on a project christened Naam Festival that aims to protect and conserve Lake Victoria in Kenya and proposed the cleansing of Vellayani during an assignment . <p> " I am quite aware of the pathetic condition here . The lake stinks , thanks to sewage , plastic and other waste discharged into it .
