<p> Proposals made by Brazil 's far-right presidential front-runner Jair Bolsonaro present a serious threat to the country 's environment that could lead deforestation " to explode , " a group of non-government organizations said on Friday . <p> More than 20 NGOs , including Greenpeace and WWF-Brasil , signed the open letter taking aim at several proposals , calling for Brazilians to take action to protect the environment as a runoff vote approaches on Oct. 28 . <p> Among a long list of complaints , the NGOs criticized " the announcement of a possible exit of Brazil from the Paris Agreement . " <p> Bolsonaro has said he could take Brazil out of the Paris Agreement to combat climate change , following in the footsteps of U.S. President Donald Trump . <p> The NGO letter did not mention Bolsonaro by name . <p> Brazil is home to roughly 60 per cent of the Amazon , the world 's largest tropical rainforest that is considered by many as nature 's best defence against global warming , with its trees absorbing huge amounts of carbon dioxide . <p> The letter also condemned plans to combine the environment and agriculture ministry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ week . <p> " The subordination of these functions to agriculture , in addition to demonstrating a lack of knowledge of the subject , would generate insurmountable conflicts of interest and jeopardize four decades of progress in protecting the environment , " the letter said .
