<h> Govt constitutes committee to construct Nallar dam <p> TNN Oct 15 , 2018 , 00:26 IST <p> Coimbatore : The Tamil Nadu government has constituted a technical expert committee to conduct a feasibility study for building the Nallar reservoir . According to the public works department ( PWD ) , 7 tmc ft of water can be stored in the proposed new reservoir . <p> A PWD official said water can be diverted from Upper Nirar weir through a straight tunnel for 17km and stored in the proposed Nallar reservoir . <p> From there , the PWD can release water to the Thirumurthy dam whenever needed . The newly proposed reservoir will come up in the reserve forest area and the state government should get permission from the ministry of environment , forest and climate change to construct the dam . <p> According to the PWD , water has been diverted from Periyar river basin ( Nirar-Idamalaiyar -- Periyar rivers ) to Upper Nirar weir . From there , water is being supplied to Lower Nirar dam , Sholayar reservoir , Parambikulam reservoir , Thunakkadavu reservoir and Peruvarippallam reservoir .
