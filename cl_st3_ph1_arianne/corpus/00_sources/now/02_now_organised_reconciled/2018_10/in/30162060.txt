<h> ' When you have failures , your mind is more stimulated than with success ' <p> Sonam Wangchuk is a technology outlier in the true sense of the descriptor . An engineer by training , he turned his anguish at fellow Ladakhis leaving his home region for greener pastures into solving real-life problems of the citizenry there . In doing so , not only is Wangchuk , 52 , overhauling education at his alternative school but is also forging new technologies such as ice stupas to address water problems in the Himalayan desert and temperature control solutions powered by solar energy and combined with the use of local building materials . We revisit this 21st episode of Outliers podcast in which he talks about his ambitions to build the Himalayan Institute of Alternatives , Ladakh -- an alternative university , as he calls it .
