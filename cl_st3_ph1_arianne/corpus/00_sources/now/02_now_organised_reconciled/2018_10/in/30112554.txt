<p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi received the ' United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) Champions of the Earth ' award from UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres at an award ceremony here on Wednesday . He said that the award recognises the Adivasis of India who " love their jungles more than their lives " , the fisherman of India who only take as much is required for sustenance , the farmers whose lives depend on the changing seasons and the women who are mindful about " reuse " and " recycle " and have lived by it every single day . <p> " Climate and calamity are directly related to culture ; if climate is not the focus of culture , calamity can not be prevented , " Prime Minister Modi said while accepting the award .
