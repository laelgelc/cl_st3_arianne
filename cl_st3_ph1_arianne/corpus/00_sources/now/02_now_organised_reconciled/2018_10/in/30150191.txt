<h> Loutolim missing link road to open by Mar 2019 <p> TNN Oct 15 , 2018 , 03:41 IST <p> Panaji : The much-awaited missing link of the national highway through Loutolim is finally expected to be open for traffic by early next year . Officials of the public works department ( PWD ) said that March 2019 has been set as the deadline for completion of the work . The four-lane road will link NH 17B at Loutolim with Verna on NH 17 , reducing travel distance by around six kilometres for locals . <p> Due to this missing link , normal traffic and trucks coming to the industrial estate follow the long route from Loutolim , Rasaim , Cortalim and Verna . On a larger scale , the new road once complete , will connect Vasco to Ponda , via the Borim bridge . The new road will benefit all traffic coming from Ponda and Borim towards Vasco by almost 6km . <p> Until construction of the missing link was taken up , the road tapered off into a dead-end on the cliff at the end of the Verna industrial estate . <p> The missing link road @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment , forest and climate change had okayed diversion of 51,800sqm of forest land for construction of the road .
