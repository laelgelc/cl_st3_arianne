<h> Top Searches : <h> 44 govt teams deployed to find green violators <p> tnn Oct 30 , 2018 , 23:40 IST <p> New Delhi : Keeping in mind the grim pollution situation , 44 teams of government officials will be deployed in the capital from Thursday . The teams include sub-divisional magistrates/tehsildars , officers of municipal corporations , Delhi Pollution Control Committee , environment department , the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change and the Central Pollution Control Board . They have been mandated to take strict punitive action against violators . <p> Delhi environment minister Imran Hussain on Tuesday said the teams would be mandated to take action on garbage burning , stubble burning , vehicular emission , dug-up and dusty roads , industrial emission , construction and demolition waste and use of power generators or DG sets . <p> " The joint campaign for clean air has been initially scheduled for a week and the campaign will be flagged off at the Indira Paryavaran Bhawan , Jor Bagh , Aliganj , on November 1 by the Union minister of environment , forest and climate change , and the Delhi environment minister , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ corporations , the Public Works Department , New Delhi Municipal Corporation , Delhi Development Authority , Delhi Cantonment Board , Delhi Metro Rail Corporation and other agencies have been asked to ensure that water is regularly sprinkled on building material , that they remain covered and the construction site be effectively covered for preventing dust .
