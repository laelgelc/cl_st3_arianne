<p> The ' Kachhua ' or Turtle Wildlife Sanctuary ( TWS ) , the world 's only protected area dedicated to freshwater turtles , situated in Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's constituency Varanasi , is set to be wiped off the conservation map of India . <p> Authorities have cited the sanctuary as a " threat " to the famous ghats on the bank of the Ganga and say that the sanctuary has failed to fulfil its objectives .
