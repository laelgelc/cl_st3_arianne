<h> Ahmed Patel asks PM to extend ESZ <p> TNN Oct 7 , 2018 , 04:51 IST <p> Gandhinagar : Ahmed Patel , the Rajya Sabha MP from Gujarat and Congress treasurer , wrote to Prime Minister Narendra Modi over the disproportionate number of lion deaths in Gujarat in recent weeks and asked him to act quickly . <p> Patel wrote , " I am writing regarding the large number of lion deaths at Gir National Park in Gujarat . More than 23 lions have died in the last three weeks and some 180 have died since 2016 . <p> " The Gir lion is the pride of Gujarat but unfortunately these large scale deaths are a manifestation of negligence . The reasons for these deaths have not emerged overnight but are the result of the state government 's prolonged mismanagement and poor oversight . " <p> Patel suggested that the PM direct the Union ministry of environment , forests and climate change to extend the eco-sensitive zone ( ESZ ) around Gir to 10km from the protected area . " In Gujarat , the ESZ extends just 0.5km from the protected zone .
