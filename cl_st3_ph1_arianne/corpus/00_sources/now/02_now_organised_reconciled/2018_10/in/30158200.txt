<h> Graded plan to combat pollution kicks in <p> GURUGRAM : MCG has started implementing Graded Response Action Plan ( GRAP ) measures to combat the rising levels of pollution in the city , <p> Following a meeting in September with ministry of environment and forest and climate change officials , the MCG had drawn up a list of measures that will implemented as the pollution levels in the city go up during the winter . The measures include regular mechanised sweeping of roads , stopping construction activities , and stopping the use of coal and fire wood in hotels and restaurants . <p> In the first leg , the corporation has started identifying stretches that have high level dust generation . Once these stretches have been identified , mechanised sweeping will be carried out . <p> MCG commissioner Yashpal Yadav said that while some of the measures are being implemented now , others will also kick in if the pollution levels deteriorate further .
