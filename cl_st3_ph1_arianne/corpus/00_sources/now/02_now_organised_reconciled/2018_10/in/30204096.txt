<h> 13-year-old Aanya Soni of Jaipur is a climate change crusader <p> Oct 30 , 2018 , 06:00 ISTSource : TNN <p> 13-year-old Aanya Soni of Jaipur is a climate change crusader . She has been working towards raising awareness about climate change . In February this year , she visited Antarctica for 2041 Expedition with Robert Swan . That makes her the youngest Indian to do so . She co-founded ' Kids4aCause ' during 2014 Kashmir floods . That helped the people of Kashmir to recycle things and create new products . Narrating more on the expedition , she added that she has learnt so much within that fortnight , since there were 80 others from across the globe who were part of it . More importantly , she said that she has learnt how mankind is harming the planet and she got to see first-hand , what so long she was was reading in textbooks .
