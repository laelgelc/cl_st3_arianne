<p> SHIMLA : Himachal governor Acharya Devvrat on Friday suggested formation of a separate ministry at the Centre to resolve common issues of the Himalayan states . He said the issues and challenges of the Himalayan region were totally different from the plains . <p> Addressing a conclave of Himalayan states organised in Shimla on Friday , he said for the first time ever , serious efforts have been made in this direction . <p> He said , " Today , we talk about global warming , while its main reason is chemical farming . " He expressed concern over the fact that various departments and organizations were promoting chemical farming , though they were very much familiar of the fact that companies producing chemical products were responsible for pollution and global warming . <p> He said he had himself experienced that only natural farming was conducive which had also been confirmed by the scientific data taken from Gurukul ( Haryana ) fields .
