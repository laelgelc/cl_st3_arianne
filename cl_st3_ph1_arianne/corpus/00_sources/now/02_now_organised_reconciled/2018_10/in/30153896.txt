<h> End of an era : On the bankruptcy of Sears , the Amazon of the 20th century <p> My friends and I learned to drive in the basement of a two-story Sears in suburban Seattle . They taught driver 's ed to teenagers in a dingy , lemon-scented room tucked away in the back of the store , behind the power tools and the luggage and the lawnmowers . During breaks in class , we 'd wander down fluorescently lit halls , through the mattresses and the couches , up the escalator to the enormous clothing section , past the perfumes and the electronics and the refrigerators , trying our best not to get lost in a maze of things and stuff . And I remember thinking : Who on earth is buying all this ? <p> Not enough people , as it turns out .
