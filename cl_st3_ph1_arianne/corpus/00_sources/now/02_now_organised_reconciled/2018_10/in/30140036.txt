<p> NEW DELHI : The Schwab Foundation for Social Entrepreneurship , the sister organisation of the World Economic Forum and the Jubilant Bhartia Foundation of Jubilant Bhartia Group today announced Prema Gopalan of Swayam Shikshan Prayog as the winner of the Social Entrepreneur of the Year India 2018 Award . The Award was presented by Dr. Rajiv Kumar , Vice Chairman , National Institution for Transforming India , NITI Aayog , Government of India in the presence of over 150 participants in New Delhi . <p> The SEOY India Award 2018 winner , is revitalizing rural economies by putting women in charge . Working in areas affected by global climate change , it has unleashed women farmers , entrepreneurs and grassroots business leaders who are solving the problems of their communities through small businesses . <p> Prema Gopalan and her team have built vibrant eco-systems to enable 145,000 women to succeed in remote and ailing markets . The SSP model comprises four ventures : a federated network of 5000 SHGs ; a resilience fund for women-led businesses ; a rural school of entrepreneurship and leadership for women ; and a market aggregator that provides warehousing , branding , marketing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , it has catalyzed the government , investors , financial institutions and Indian and global corporations to partner directly with grassroots women business leaders . <p> The SEOY India Award promotes social entrepreneurs in India by recognising organisations that work to promote inclusive growth and contribute to bring change in the society through their entrepreneurial vision . The winner of SEOY India Award participates in the annual and regional meetings of the World Economic Forum ( WEF ) . These meetings offer unprecedented opportunities to engage with global decision makers from the public , corporate , media , academic , and civil society sectors . The winner will be invited to join the <p> Schwab Foundation 's global community of over 300 social innovators . The finalists are evaluated on key parameters of Innovation , Sustainability , Scalability , Reach and Direct Social Impact . <p> Applauding the work of the two finalists of this year SEOY India award , Dr. Rajiv Kumar , Vice Chairman , National Institution for Transforming India , NITI Aayog , Government of India said , " India is at the forefront of social entrepreneurship @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ view , is the platform where real transformation is taking place .
