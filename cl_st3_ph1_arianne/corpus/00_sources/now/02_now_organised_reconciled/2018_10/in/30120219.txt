<h> This puja , take a tour of Petronas Towers in Noida <p> NOIDA : The city is gearing up for its annual Durga Puja celebrations with interesting pandal themes such as a replica of the Dakshineshwar Kali temple of Kolkata , Jaipur 's Hawa Mahal and Petronas Twin Towers of Malaysia . Most of the Puja organisers have also planned schedules . The Kali Bari in Sector 26 , the oldest puja committee in the city , is making its pandal themed on the temple in Kolkata . It will begin celebrations from Sashthi on October 15 and end with the immersion of idols on October 19 . In Sector 61 , the Balaka Bengali Cultural Association 's Puja pandal will be a thematic replica of Jaipur 's Hawa Mahal along with the Petronas twin towers of Malaysia , which will be lit with LED lights . Also , in Sector 50 , Saptarshi Sangha Trust will make a pandal themed on a traditional temple structure . <p> " Although Durga Puja is still a week away , preparations for making the five-day festival successful are going on in full swing at the Kali Bari , where the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ October 15 and end with the immersion of the idols on Vijaya Dashami on October 19 .
