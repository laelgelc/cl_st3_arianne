<h> Cotton as source of bare necessities <p> ET Bureau <p> Oct 18 , 2018 , 11.16 PM IST <p> 0Comments <p> The next logical step is to genetically modify the cotton plant to increase and strengthen its fibre content . <p> In the era of synthetic meat , made from plant material or cloned meat cells , it should not come as a total surprise that scientists have developed a strain of cotton , whose seeds are edible for human beings . Cattle , of course , already merrily chomp on cotton seeds . Cotton seed oil is consumed by people , whether they know it or not , and whether the cotton plant from which these are derived has been genetically modified or not . The oil cake , the stuff that remains after oil has been pressed out of the seeds , is a protein-rich component of cattle feed , too . So , it was not far-fetched to imagine that cotton seeds could be made into human food , too .
