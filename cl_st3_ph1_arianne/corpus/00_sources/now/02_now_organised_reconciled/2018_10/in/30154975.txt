<p> In a major victory to the efforts to protect UNESCO World Natural Heritage Site the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF ) has notified 56,825 sq km of the Western Ghats spread along a 15,000km in six states as ecologically sensitive area ( ESA ) . <p> The draft notification , if it gets final clearance , will ban activities including constructions , mining , quarrying and sand mining . <p> BCCL <p> The final notification will be issued after 60 days following the submission of objections or suggestions .
