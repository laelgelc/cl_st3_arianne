The issue of AMR in India is alarming with about 58,000 neonatal deaths every year due to sepsis by resistant pathogens . <p> " AMR is rampant due to poor sanitation conditions , relaxed regulation on waste discharge from drug manufacturers and indiscriminate usage of antibiotics in humans , livestock and poultry . There is a lack of large-scale study on AMR in India , but available research has shown the presence of many multiple drug-resistant bacteria and genes in the country . Many studies have reported high load of drug resistance in isolates from clinical poultry , fish , meat and milk samples , environmental samples including ' Sewage ' , " Dr Shouche told The Indian Express . <p> " The availability of such repository will be valuable in more than one ways , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ involved in the validation of the AMR status of the isolates , develop new antibiotics and antifungals , conduct studies on the effectiveness of new drugs and understand the emergence and spread of AMR not only in clinical set up but also in poultry and environment . <p> NCMR is functioning as the International Depository Authority under the Budapest Treaty and is also designated as Repository by the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change under the Biodiversity Act , 2002 .
