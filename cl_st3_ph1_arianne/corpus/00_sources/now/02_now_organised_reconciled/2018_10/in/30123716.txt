<h> ' Himalayan states should join hands on common issues ' <p> TNN Updated : Oct 7 , 2018 , 09:19 IST <p> CM Jai Ram Thakur <p> SHIMLA : Chief minister Jai Ram Thakur said that all Himalayan states should come on a common platform for raising the issues of common interest before the Union government for overall development of these states . He said that Prime Minister Narendra Modi was aware of the developmental needs of these states and so several schemes aimed at development had been launched by the Centre . <p> Thakur said this while addressing the ' Conclave of Himalayan States ' organized by the HP State Council for Science , Technology and Environment in close partnership with Integrated Mountain Initiative and Shoolini University , Solan , on the theme ' Well Being of the Next Generation of Himalayan People ' on Friday . As many as 11 Himalayan states of the country and Nepal participated in this conclave . <p> He said that this conclave would go a long way in coining a joint strategy for development of three key sectors -- agriculture , tourism and power generation .
