<h> PM Narendra Modi and French President to receive ' Champions of the Earth ' award <p> Oct 04 , 2018 , 11:03 ISTSource : TNN <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron would jointly receive the United Nations 's highest environment honour ' Champions of the Earth ' award . They are being awarded for showing exemplary leadership and advocate action on sustainable development and climate change . United Nations announced the award on 26 September during a high level segment of the UN General Assembly . The award is being given to them for their leadership and action on sustainable development and climate change . " Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Emmanuel Macron will jointly receive Champions of The Earth Award on October 3 at the function which will take place at Pravasi Bharatiya Kendra , " ministry of external affairs spokesperson Raveesh Kumar said at a press conference .
