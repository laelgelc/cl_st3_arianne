<h> Birds of different feathers flock together <p> CHANDIGARH : Guess who 's come visiting at the Ropar Wetland Conservation Reserve ? The European bird Eurasian Hobby , which had given the reserve a miss for the last couple years , is back again , making it a rare sighting . With winters setting in , families of migratory birds have begun flocking at the Ramsar site and , by last count , 13 species had arrived at the Ropar wetland , which has been notified as a conservation reserve last year . <p> Ecologist T K Roy , who conducted a survey cum monitoring exercise for autumn 2018 in mid-October said so far 127 species of birds have been sighted in Ropar . " More than 10 species of larger flocks of winter migratory water birds are expected to arrive in October-end or November , depending on winter climatic condition , " he said . <p> Ropar wetland is echoing with loud honking calls of orange-hued distinctive waterfowl , ruddy shelduck , also known in India as the Brahminy duck , a member of the family Anatidae .
