<h> Hurricane Willa ravages fishing village on Mexico 's Pacific coast <p> TEACAPAN , Mexico ( Reuters ) - When Amadeo Ramos went to bed Tuesday night , nine sturdy metal tables were fixed to the concrete floor of his seafood restaurant just off the boardwalk of the Mexican fishing village of Teacapan . <p> By the time Hurricane Willa had torn through the village the next morning , they were nearly all gone . <p> " There were nine tables here , " said Ramos , pointing to an empty space in his El Bucanero restaurant where customers would eat shrimp ceviche washed down with beer . " Only two are still here . The others were broken to pieces and flew away . " <p> Willa ripped into Teacapan as a Category 3 hurricane , one of the strongest to hit Mexico 's Pacific coast in years , prying off roofs with winds of up to 120 miles per hour ( 193 km per hour ) . <p> No deaths were reported thanks to mass evacuations , but the storm left the streets of Teacapan a muddy mess . <p> Trees still standing were littered with debris @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the boardwalk was blasted apart , with large chunks of concrete scattered on an adjacent road by a wrecked fishing boat that the hurricane hurled out of the water . <p> Teacapan lies about 70 miles ( 110 km ) south of the beach resort of Mazatlan , and the village 's few thousand local residents were moved to shelters inland a day before Willa struck . <p> A general view shows the area by the damaged boardwalk in the town of Teacapan near the southern tip of Sinaloa state after Hurricane Willa hit Mexico October 24 , 2018 .
