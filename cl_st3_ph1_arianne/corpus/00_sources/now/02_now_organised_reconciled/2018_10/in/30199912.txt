" I went to Antarctica in February this year for two weeks , to be part of the expedition . The biggest challenge was that in a matter of 90 days , I and my family had to raise Rs 28 lakh to be part of the expedition , but it could n't be our own money . In fact , we got to know that a certain type of penguin is migrating from there because Antarctica is getting warmer . If it starts melting , then the sea levels will rise by one metre which is catastrophic . There are small ways in which we can avoid that like carpooling , stopping use of plastic and composting amongst others . Small changes can go a long way .
