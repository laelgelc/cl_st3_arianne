<h> ' Second population of Asiatic lions must be considered ' <p> AHMEDABAD : A leading expert on lion conservation , Dr Craig Packer , believes that a second population of Asiatic lions , " at a safe distance from Gir " , must be considered , in the wake of the current combined canine distemper virus ( CDV ) -Babseia infection outbreak , which has claimed 23 lions . In an email to TOI , Dr Packer said this shift was important , " to ensure the perpetuation of the sub-species ( Asiatic lion ) in the face of increased disease risks with climate change and a growing human population . " Dr Packer was part of a team of scientists involved in mitigating the CDV outbreak in the African lion populations at the Serengeti sanctuary ( 1994 ) and the Ngorongoro Crater ( 2001 ) in Tanzania .
