<p> NEW DELHI : Deputy Chairman of the Rajya Sabha Harivansh on Wednesday emphasized on the need for carrying forward dialogue on the issues of shared interest of the BRICS countries . <p> Presenting India 's views on ' Fostering an inclusive and just world through Inter-parliamentary Co-operation ' during the Fourth BRICS Forum Parliamentary meeting , Mr Harivansh called upon other nations to work together constructively in the wake of the 2030 Agenda , which provides a useful framework for sustainable development with general focus on major economic , social and environmental issues . <p> He especially remembered the year 2018 being the 150th Birth Anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi and the birth centenary of Nelson Mandela whose values of peace , tolerance and democracy continue to inspire and guide us till date . <p> The Rajya Sabha Deputy Chairman also emphasized the urgent need to combat climate change by BRICS guided by the common but differentiated responsibilities . <p> He said that in Gandhi 's scheme of ' Sarvodaya through Antyodaya ' , the last man was the centre for development . " This approach remains relevant even today , " he said . ( AGENCIES )
