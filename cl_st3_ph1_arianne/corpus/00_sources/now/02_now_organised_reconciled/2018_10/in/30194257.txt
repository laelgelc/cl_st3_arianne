<h> ' Revive water bodies to make Madurai smart ' <p> TNN Oct 27 , 2018 , 00:32 IST <p> Madurai : Madurai was smarter in terms of water management in the past and the best way to bring back the good old days was by reviving river Vaigai and the water bodies , said Dr S Chandran of the civil engineering department of Thiagarajar College of Engineering . <p> He was speaking at an interaction on building an agenda for the smart city programme organised by the South-Central India Network for Development Alternatives and Laya-Indian Network on Ethics and Climate Change here on Friday . Taking ownership of the waterbodies would help revive them and recycling water and commercializing it would help in many ways , he said . <p> Professor of architecture , Thiagarajar College of Engineering , Dr G Balaji , said there was a need to redefine smart that did not just mean an outward appearance but also sustaining and integrating development with nature .
