Plans in Japan <p> IT has been widely reported that Prime Minister Narendra Modi is in Japan on a two-day visit as part of the annual India-Japan summit . Modi will be hosted by his good friend Shinzo Abe at the Japanese prime minister 's holiday home in Yamanashi , near Mount Fuji . Last year , Modi had invited Abe to Ahmedabad to visit the Sabarmati Ashram . But there has also been much discussion on whether Modi should visit the Renkoji temple in Tokyo , where Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose 's ashes are preserved . The Japanese government would like India to take the ashes back , as the temple is a bit of a white elephant for the country -- it only has Indians visiting it . Moreover , no major Indian politician has visited the temple , barring Atal Bihari Vajpayee and Jaswant Singh . Dreaming of the big blue <p> MITA Vohra , a British Kenyan of Indian origin , was invited to design a piece of jewellery for an exceptional auction held at the second Monte Carlo gala , on the opening day of the Monaco Yacht Show that took place on October 25 . Her jewellery raised Euros 1,50,000 through the event , which was presided over by Prince Albert II of Monaco . Funds raised from the auction will go towards saving the world 's oceans from plastic pollution , climate change and development of marine-protected areas . Mita , who hails from a prestigious family of hoteliers in Kenya and the UK , says she was honoured to have been invited . Her concern for nature and sustainability came about as a child growing up in Kenya . <p> ?
