Himalayan states as the time has come to move from thermal power to clean energy . <p> Underlying the need for exploitation of hydropower potential in Himalayan states , the Himachal Pradesh government on Wednesday called for more projects to improve the economy of these states . <p> RD Dhiman , principal secretary , Environment , Science and Technology said it was the need of hour to
