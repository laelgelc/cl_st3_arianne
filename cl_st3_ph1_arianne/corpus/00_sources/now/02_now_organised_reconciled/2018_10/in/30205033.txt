<h> French prof push for South Asia network of researchers <p> KOLKATA : South Asia needs a strong network of researchers in regional science to evolve solutions for problems like climate change , urban-rural development and linkages , governance , poverty , innovation and other pressing issues of global importance , a leading French academic told researchers and scientists in Kolkata on Monday . <p> Andre Torre , first-class research professor at the National Institute for Agronomic Research in Paris , and president elect of the Regional Science Association , Europe , is in Kolkata to catalyse a South Asia network comprising sociologists , economists , statisticians , goegraphers and political scientists from India , Bangladesh , Sri Lanka , Paksitan , Nepal and Bhutan . The Thai chapter of the Regional Science Association will also be part of the network . <p> Regional science is a field of study that analyses development problems and formulates policies to solve them . Regional science emerged as a discipline in the 1950s .
