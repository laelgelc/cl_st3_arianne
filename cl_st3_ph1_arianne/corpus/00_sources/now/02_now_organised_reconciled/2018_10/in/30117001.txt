<h> Punjab Cabinet regularizes 19-month service of former top bureaucrat <p> CHANDIGARH : The Punjab cabinet on Wednesday decided to provide full retirement benefits to former IAS officer Himmat Singh by regularizing around 19-month service as ' compulsory waiting period ' . During the regularized period of August 1 , 2015 , to March 19 , 2017 , the ex-bureaucrat was posted by the previous SAD-BJP government at a much junior position despite being the senior most officer of the Punjab IAS cadre . He had protested against the appointment and had also gone on a long medical leave at the time . <p> The 1980-batch retired IAS officer Himmat Singh , considered close to present chief minister Amarinder Singh , had retired from service on August 31 , 2017 . Then in December 2017 , he was appointed as adviser for agriculture diversification , climate change and wildlife preservation on " honorary basis " for three years . <p> During the SAD-BJP regime , he was posted as member secretary of the Punjab State Women Commission on March 16 , 2012 .
