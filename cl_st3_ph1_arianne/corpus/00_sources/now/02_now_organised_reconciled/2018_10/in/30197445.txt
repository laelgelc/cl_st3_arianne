<h> Greens oppose Rs4 crore windfall to FDCM staff <p> Nagpur : The Forest Development Corporation of Maharashtra ( FDCM ) , a state-owned company engaged in commercial extraction of timber , for the first time has decided to release Rs4 crore towards performance-linked incentive to its employees . However , the FDCM decision has come in for sharp criticism from conservationists and greens who say the money towards incentive , running into lakhs of rupees , will come from cutting rich forest . <p> On October 23 , FDCM managing director N Rambabu directed all divisional managers ( DMs ) that all officials , employees and van majoors should be paid one-month salary ( basic pay and grade pay and dearness allowance ) with a ceiling of Rs50,000 as incentive . <p> " FDCM is a profitmaking company and the decision was taken by board of directors at its 187th meeting on September 27 . " FDCM owns rich reserve forest areas in tiger corridors and adjoining national parks and sanctuaries . The incentive money will come from felling these forests , which is not advisable , " says Eco-Pro president Bandu Dhotre . <p> Dhotre felt at a time when man-animal conflict has flared up in the region , there is no option but to close down FDCM . In Pandharkawda , 90% of the conflict is in FDCM area . " Besides , many alternatives to wood are available in the market , " he said . <p> Gadchiroli honorary wildlife warden Uday Patel too felt that the employees ' demand for incentives may be justified but ethically it is wrong to pay crores by putting pressure on forests .
