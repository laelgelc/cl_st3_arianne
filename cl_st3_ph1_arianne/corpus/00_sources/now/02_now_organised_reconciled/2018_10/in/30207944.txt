<h> Sabarmati gets 143 million litres per day of sewage <p> AHMEDABAD : Sabarmati river , which gets polluted in the city limits , on an average receives 15% of the 960 million litres per day ( MLD ) of untreated sewage which the city generates every day . The city has the capacity to treat 817MLD of sewage . A report released recently by the Gujarat Pollution Control Board ( GPCB ) states that the Sabarmati had the biological oxygen demand ( BOD ) of 8.6 and the chemical oxygen demand ( COD ) of 29 at the entry point of the city but the levels rise to 80 and 224 respectively at Vasna-Narol Bridge . The permissible limit of BOD is 30 . <p> Sabarmati , which is considered a model in giving facelift to an ailing river , is among Gujarat 's most 20 polluted river .
