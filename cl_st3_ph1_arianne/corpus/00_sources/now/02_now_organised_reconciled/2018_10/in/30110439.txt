<h> PM Modi receives UN 's Champions of the Earth award <h> Sangeeta Nair <p> Oct 3 , 2018 15:19 IST <p> PM Modi receives UN Champions of the Earth award <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi received the UN Champions of the Earth award , the highest environmental honour of the United Nations on October 3 , 2018 . The award was presented by UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres at a special ceremony in New Delhi . <p> Speaking on the occasion , UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said that Prime Minister Modi is a leader who realises the issue of climate change and understands the benefits of climate action . He said that PM Modi knows the problems and also works to solve them . He said that a green economy is a good economy and the technology is on the side of those who believe in a green economy . <p> The award was announced on September 26 , 2018 , on the sidelines of 73rd UN General Assembly in New York . <p> Significance <p> Prime Minister Modi has been selected in the leadership category for his pioneering work in championing the International Solar Alliance , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the award , the UN Environment is recognising Modi for his bold environmental leadership on the global stage . <p> The award is also in recognition of Modi 's unprecedented pledge to eliminate all single-use plastic in India by 2022 . <p> Other Awardees <p> The prestigious awards programme recognised six of the world 's most outstanding environmental changemakers this year for their combination of bold , innovative and tireless efforts to tackle some of the most urgent environmental issues . <p> The winners include world 's first fully solar-powered airport Cochin International Airport in Kerala , Filipino environmental activist Joan Carling , Plant-based food alternative providers Beyond Meat and Impossible Foods and China 's Zhejiang 's Green Rural Revival Programme , which was awarded for the transformation of a once heavily polluted area of rivers and streams in East China 's Zhejiang province . <p> French President Emmanuel Macron was jointly awarded with PM Modi in the Policy Leadership category for their efforts regarding the creation of the International Solar Alliance . <p> About the award <p> The annual ' Champions of the Earth ' prize is awarded to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sector whose actions have had a positive impact on the environment . <p> The award is the UN 's highest environmental recognition celebrating exceptional figures from the public and private sectors and from civil society . <p> The awards were established in 2005 under the United Nations Environment Programme . Since then , they have recognized 84 laureates ranging from leaders of nations to grassroots activists in the categories of policy , science , business and civil society . <p> The awards recognise 5-7 laureates annually and they are invited to an award ceremony to receive a trophy , give an acceptance speech and take part in a press conference .
