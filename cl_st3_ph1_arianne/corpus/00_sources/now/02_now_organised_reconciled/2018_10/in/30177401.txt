In order to unequivocally attribute the changing distribution of mosquito and tick vectors of emerging VBDs to climate change , scientific principles of causality must be established under randomized experimental conditions . <p> For scientists to say definitively that climate change is directly boosting the population of disease-carrying mosquitoes and ticks , they would first need to demonstrate that the changes in distribution of these disease vectors were not due to other factors . <p> Why should we care about the association between climate change and the spread of emerging VBDs ?
