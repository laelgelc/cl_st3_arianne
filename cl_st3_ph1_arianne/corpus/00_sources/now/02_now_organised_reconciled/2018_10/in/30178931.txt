<h> Tokamak fusion reactor 57% complete : ITER chief <p> TNN Oct 23 , 2018 , 04:49 IST <p> Picture for representational purpose only <p> AHMEDABAD : Nearly 40% of the shipload that has reached Cadrache in France for realizing the electricity-generating Tokamak fusion power plant -- the 500MW International Thermonuclear Experimental Reactor ( ITER ) , the world 's largest fusion reactor -- has been delivered by India as on October . The load mainly includes components related to the cryostat ( especially the bottom and middle parts ) , cooling towers , plate type heat exchangers , and 2,500 spools of electric lines . <p> These are among the nine major procurement packages that have been committed by India , which are worth over Rs 10,000 crore . Of this , Rs 3,500 crore worth of equipment has already been delivered , says ITER India director S P Deshpande . The fusion reactor is firmly " on track and is 57% complete " , said director general of ITER , Bernard Bigot . Chakraborty and Deshpande highlighted how Indian industry has gained from the project . Beginning with the 4,000 tonnes of stainless steel cryostat developed by L&amp;T facility in Hazira , India is also contributing a large network of cooling and chilled water systems to the ITER site .
