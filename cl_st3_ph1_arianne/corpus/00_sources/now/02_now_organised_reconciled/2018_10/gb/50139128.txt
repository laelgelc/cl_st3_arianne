<h> Government names TrustMark as new mark of quality for customers <h> Energy efficiency and clean growth are a key part of the new TrustMark <p> In support of the Government 's Green GB Week , TrustMark announces that it has expanded its remit to include repair , maintenance and improvement ( RMI ) , retrofit and energy efficiency sectors . This is in order to cover any work a consumer chooses to have carried out in or around their home . <p> TrustMark has extended its responsibilities following the industry led Government-commissioned Each Home Counts review in 2016 . This identified a need for an all-encompassing mark of quality for consumers to recognise and trust . TrustMark was chosen to be the brand that the quality mark has now become with clean growth and energy efficiency being a key focus of the scheme .
