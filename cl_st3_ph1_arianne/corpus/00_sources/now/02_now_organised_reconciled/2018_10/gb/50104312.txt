Over the howl of the wind I could hear trees crashing down outside . I did n't know how long the storm was going to last . I did n't know where the next tree would fall . <p> When the wind finally eased , I ventured outside to see if my neighbours were OK , to see if their houses were still standing . I 'll never forget that feeling . <p> Cyclone Winston was the biggest cyclone ever to make landfall in the Southern Hemisphere . The devastating storm left 44 people dead , and 350,000 people , almost 40% of Fiji 's population , were affected . Kiribati is a large ocean state comprised of 32 atolls and one raised coral island , spread across more than a million square miles of the central Pacific Ocean , and with a population of approximately 110,000 . <p> Almost the entire land area of Kiribati , including the whole of the main population centre of South Tarawa , lies less than three metres above sea level . Kiribati is considered one of the most vulnerable countries on earth to the impacts of climate change . <p> The nation 's people , the I-Kiribati , fear not only the loss of their livelihoods and security but also the impact of displacement on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their land and sea . <p> Tinaai Teaua , a member of Kiribati Climate Action Network , told Oxfam : " Land is very important . What our culture is like . How we are so proud of being I-Kiribati . The main message is to limit warming to 1.5 degrees . That was already agreed , but now they have to live up to their words . " <p> The case of Kiribati highlights the need for stronger international action to minimise the impacts of climate change and to provide greater support to vulnerable communities .
