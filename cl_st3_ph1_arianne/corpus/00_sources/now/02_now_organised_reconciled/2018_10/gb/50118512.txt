<h> Line-up of speakers announced for Northern Farming Conference <h> Line-up of speakers announced for Northern Farming Conference <p> THE line-up of speakers at next month 's Northern Farming Conference has been announced . <p> Emeritus Professor Allan Buckwell , Senior Fellow at the Institute for European Environmental Policy , and Upper Nisbet farmer Robert Neill , chairman of AgriScot will join a range of farmers and industry experts . <p> The event takes place at Hexham mart on Wednesday , November 7 , under the theme " Your farm , your future " . <p> Prof Buckwell , a former Country Land and Business Association policy director , has spent two thirds of his career as an academic agricultural economist , specialising in agriculture and rural policy .
