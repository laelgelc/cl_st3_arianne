<h> Council is shining example of energy saving <h> Council is shining example of energy saving <p> HALTON Borough Council is leading by example by using more energy-saving lighting . <p> In the past three years , the council has upgraded or replaced more than 7,000 of the borough 's street lanterns to LED , resulting in an annual energy saving of over two million kWh , which is equivalent to ? 250,000 . <p> LED street lighting provides better light quality for road users , as LED lanterns produce a whiter light , which improves night time vision and colour definition . <p> In 2015 , following a bid to the department of energy and climate change ( DECC ) , the council received a grant contribution towards the installation of about 2,000 LED street lights . <p> From 2016 , the executive board agreed a programme to roll out the installation of LEDs in the rest of the borough at the rate of 2,500 lanterns per year . <p> To date , the project has resulted in an energy reduction of 70% . <p> Procurement of the lanterns meeting the council 's specification is continually being monitored @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ residential roads is due to be completed by the end of March 2021 . <p> Cllr Stan Hill , the council 's executive board member for highways and transport , said : " It is essential that we spend money on modernising our street lights to guard against rising energy prices and maintenance costs , and the savings that have been made , both in cost and energy , by installing these LED lights are tremendous . <p> " We have received some very positive comments from residents about the new lights as they are being installed around the borough .
