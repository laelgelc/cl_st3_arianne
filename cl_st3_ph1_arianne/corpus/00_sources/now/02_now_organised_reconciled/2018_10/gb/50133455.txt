<h> Amazon to install solar on 10 of its fulfilment centres <p> The company plans to initially deploy large-scale rooftop solar systems on 10 of its fulfilment centres in the UK over the coming 18 months , subject to planning and landlord approval . <p> Amazon says they expect the solar panels , which will have a capacity of 20 megawatts , to generate the equivalent amount of electricity required to power over 4,500 UK homes and will reduce its carbon footprint by 6,000 tonnes of CO2 annually . <p> Amazon has also signed a deal for 100% renewable electricity to power its UK buildings . <p> ' As our fulfilment network continues to expand , we want to help generate more renewable energy at both existing and new facilities around the world in partnership with community and business leaders , ' said Stefano Perego , operations director for Amazon UK . <p> ' We are putting our scale and inventive culture to work on sustainability , which is good for the environment , our business , our customers , and the communities in which we operate .
