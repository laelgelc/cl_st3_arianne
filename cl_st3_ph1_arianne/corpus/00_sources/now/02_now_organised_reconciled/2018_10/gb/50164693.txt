<h> Campaign launched to secure a wilder future for the Isle of Wight <h> Campaign launched to secure a wilder future for the Isle of Wight <p> HAMPSHIRE and Isle of Wight Wildlife Trust has launched a new campaign to secure a wilder future for the county . <p> Over the past 50 years , 56 per cent of species have declined across the UK , with 15 per cent now at risk of disappearing altogether . <p> Locally , there have been dramatic reductions in species such as water voles , nightingales and even common species like the large white butterfly <p> This loss is being driven by intensive agriculture , climate change , development , recreation pressure and unsustainable use of natural resources . <p> Research has demonstrated that nature plays a vital role in improving physical and mental wellbeing and yet many people are disconnected from their natural surroundings and rarely spend time in nature . <p> Debbie Tann , chief executive , said : " There 's complacency here when it comes to the state of the natural environment . <p> " Although our county looks green and pleasant , our wildlife is in freefall @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the incredible plants and animals that we are losing , but because we all depend on the natural world for our own survival and for the health of our society . <p> " We have to act now to tip the balance in favour of nature 's recovery . <p> " While the Wildlife Trust has achieved a great deal in the past few decades -- creating fantastic safe havens for wildlife on our nature reserves , working with others to improve areas for wildlife and offering hundreds of thousands of opportunities for people to connect with nature -- we can not hope to turn the tide by ourselves . <p> " We need many more people on nature 's side and much , much more space for wildlife .
