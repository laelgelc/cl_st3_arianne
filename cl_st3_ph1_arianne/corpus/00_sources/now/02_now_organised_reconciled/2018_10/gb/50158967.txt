<h> Breaking News <h> Expressway to ruin countryside the size of Birmingham <h> Countryside the size of Birmingham is at risk of housing developments in the Oxford-Cambridge Arc . <p> 27,000 hectares of farmland and woodland will be threatened , according to analysis from Campaign to Protect Rural England . <p> The Government is set to accept a recommendation from the National Infrastructure Commission ( NIC ) , this Monday ( 29th Oct ) , to build 1 million new homes between Oxford , Milton Keynes and Cambridge , including the new Expressway . <p> This would result in an area of countryside greater than the size of Birmingham being lost to development . <p> The NIC , who are backing the scheme , recommend building 1 million new houses in the Arc by 2050 , in order to ' boost economic growth ' . <h> What happens next ?
