<p> Named in honour of the first Hydrographer of the Navy , the Alexander Dalrymple Award has been presented by the UKHO each year since 2006 in recognition of an individual 's outstanding contribution to hydrography : the science of surveying and charting our oceans . Mr Bryant is the thirteenth recipient of the award ( and the fourth recipient from the UK ) nominated for the global impact he has made within the international hydrographic community . <p> Over his career spanning 45 years , Mr Bryant has coordinated the training of hundreds of students . This has included securing a long-term partnership with the NIPPON Foundation , under which the UKHO has trained 64 international students in marine cartography . <p> Presenting the award at this year 's World Hydrography Day reception , Rear Admiral Tim Lowe congratulated Mr Bryant on his achievements and his commitment to enhancing the cartographic skills of individuals and nations across the globe . These skills are essential to supporting maritime trade and the safety of ships navigating our oceans . <p> During his speech at the event , RAdm Tim Lowe also commented on the need to support the use of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ marine geospatial data in developing sustainable marine economies : <p> As experts in seabed bathymetry and marine geospatial data leaders , it is incumbent on us to support the data revolution .
