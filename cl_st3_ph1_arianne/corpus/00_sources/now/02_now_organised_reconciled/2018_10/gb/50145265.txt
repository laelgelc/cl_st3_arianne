Until this is achieved we will support calls for subsidised travel for all those who require it , such as but not limited to who are elderly , disabled , carers , unemployed , children &amp; young people , those in full time education or on low incomes . This subsidy must be at least a 50 per cent reduction on standard fares . " <p> Commenting on the passing of the motion , Transport spokesperson for the Scottish Greens John Finnie MSP stated : " Greens are already campaigning for better buses and more reliable rail services , and our MSP group will make the most of the Transport Bill coming to Parliament .
