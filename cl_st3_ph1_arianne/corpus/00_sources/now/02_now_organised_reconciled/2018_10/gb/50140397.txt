<h> Green GB Week : Supporting Clean Growth in Peru <p> This week is highlighting UK-funded activities that support clean growth in Peru <p> The British Government 's first annual Green GB Week is highlighting the opportunities clean growth offers and raising understanding of how we can contribute to tackling climate change . The UK has led the world to date in cutting emissions while creating wealth . Between 1990 and 2016 , the UK reduced its emissions by over 40% while growing the economy by more than two-thirds -- the best performance in the G7 on a per person basis . <p> The global move to cleaner economic growth -- through low carbon technologies and the more efficient use of resource -- is one of the greatest industrial opportunities of our time .
