<p> Could sci-fi become reality ? The Terminator was a cyborg assassin played by Arnold Schwarzenegger . <p> Cyber warriors or specially enhanced human soldiers could be a potential threat in the future , according to a new report from a Ministry of Defence ( MOD ) think tank . <p> The ' Future Starts Today ' report is an attempt to identify the kinds of challenges facing the UK in the decades ahead . <p> It warns the world is becoming more volatile , with increasing threats from terrorism , climate change and the rapid advance of technology . <p> The document , worked on with experts from allies around the world , examines potential developments including wars fought by robots or " enhanced " humans and the need for the Government and military to respond . <p> It warns of an increasing risk of the use of nuclear or chemical weapons and the potential for " new areas of conflict " including in space and cyberspace . Defence Secretary Gavin Williamson said : <p> " This report makes clear that we are living in a world becoming rapidly more dangerous , with intensifying challenges from state aggressors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our way of life and the technological race with our adversaries <p> " Identifying these threats means we can continue to build an armed forces that can stay ahead of them . " <p> Remotely piloted drones are already used by the military , but what about remotely piloted soldiers ? <p> The document , the sixth edition of the Global Strategic Trends report , has been developed by the MOD'sthink tank , known as the Development Concepts and Doctrine Centre . It said : <p> " Whilst it is envisaged that humans will continue to be central to the decision-making process , conflicts fought increasingly by robots or autonomous systems could change the very nature of warfare , as there will be less emphasis on emotions , passion and chance . " <p> The report , compiled with partners in Sweden , Australia , Finland , Germany , France and the Netherlands , examined the possibility of " human enhancement " , including " gene editing , physical and cognitive prosthesis , and pharmaceutical enhancement " . <p> Their development over the next 30 years is likely to offer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " and " the application of these technologies and the integration of human and machine on the battlefield present opportunities to enhance military capability " . <p> The willingness to adopt these technologies could confer a competitive advantage over adversaries , but " moral , ethical and legal thresholds " would need to be defined . <p> The report also suggested a " hybrid " approach could go beyond military or economic attacks and open up " new arenas of conflict , including in space , cyberspace , sub-oceanic and , potentially , augmented and virtual reality " .
