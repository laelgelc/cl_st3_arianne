<h> Plans for ? 18million of flood defence work in East Rhyl will go on show to the public <p> Denbighshire County Council , supported by the Welsh Government 's Coastal Risk Management Programme , has drawn up plans to install rock armouring and increase the height of the sea wall along the promenade between Splash Point and the slipway adjacent to Rhyl Golf Club . <p> Construction is expected to take 18 months and start during 2019 , subject to planning permission , other necessary consents being granted and funding agreement . <p> Residents will have the chance to view the plans and comment on them at a drop-in session on October 11 between 10am to 8pm at the Elwy Room , Rhyl Community Fire Station . <p> Cllr Brian Jones , Denbighshire County Council 's lead member for Highways , Planning and Sustainable Travel , said : <p> " Due to the low-lying nature of East Rhyl , wave overtopping remains the most serious coastal flood risk . <p> " Short-term resilience measures were undertaken by Denbighshire County Council following the 2013 storms and this project will provide substantial and durable protection in the long term . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the Council under our Corporate Plan and once completed this work will offer a huge comfort to the community .
