Hunger , pestilence and violent death have brooded over their desiccated land ever since the first clans reached the Horn of Africa more than a millennium ago . <p> It is not in the Somali national character , enamelled by suffering , to complain . Setbacks are shrugged off and mortality contemplated with disdain .
