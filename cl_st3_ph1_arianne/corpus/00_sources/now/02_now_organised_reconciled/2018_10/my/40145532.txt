<h> Fuziah to step aside if Lynas wants her out of review team <p> KUANTAN : Lynas review committee chairman Fuziah Salleh ( pic ) is prepared to step down in light of objections from the rare earth materials producer . <p> The Kuantan MP said she had proposed to the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin that the committee be revamped without her participation . <p> Fuziah said she refused to be used as ammunition by Lynas seeing that any outcome of the review would be criticised with her in the committee . <p> " No matter how impartial the review , Lynas will not accept it due to my involvement . <p> " My concern is for Malaysia , the people and our environment , " she said , adding that she was prepared to step aside if Lynas wanted her out of the review team . <p> " I have no doubt that there will be highly qualified personnel who can fill the void . <p> " I prefer not to be used by Lynas to criticise the review when it has a very important function to perform @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Fuziah 's appointment as chairman of the executive review committee had been heavily questioned due to her anti-Lynas stance . <p> Lynas employees recently held a rally here to call for fair treatment from the government . <p> Fuziah , who is Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department , said she would still continue to speak out on issues surrounding the Lynas Advanced Materials Plant ( LAMP ) in Gebeng here . <p> She said a fresh review was necessary following the mandate given by the people to the Pakatan Harapan government . <p> " Of course we have to review Lynas again . <p> " Malaysians have placed their trust and hope on Pakatan Harapan and the Cabinet is obliged to set up an executive review to look into the approval process and safety issues related to the LAMP , " she said .
