<h> Ambiga receives UN award for contribution to sustainable development <p> Former Bersih chief Ambiga Sreenevasan has received the 2018 United Nations Award in recognition of her contribution to the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> UN Malaysia , in a statement , said that other two recipients who received the same award were non-profit charity MyKasih Foundation and the National Ozone Unit of the Environment Department . <p> The award was presented in conjunction with the 73rd anniversary of the ratification of the United Nations charter in an event officiated by Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department Liew Vui Keong today . <p> It said that the award honours the outstanding contributions of individuals and organisations that embody the underlying principles of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development in three categories , namely human rights and governance ; leaving no one behind ; and environmental sustainability . <p> A new UN logo , which includes 17 colours representing the SDGs , was also revealed at the ceremony . <p> " Human rights and the SDGs are two sides of the same coin .
