<h> Lynas accountable to employees if operations shut down -- Fuziah <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Oct 16 ) : Lynas Corporation Ltd ( Lynas ) should take full responsibility for its employees who face the risk of losing their jobs if the company 's operations are shut down , Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department Fuziah Salleh said . <p> Fuziah , who is Kuantan Member of Parliament , said this was because the rare earth company had repeatedly assured the community and its own employees that the company 's operations located in Gebeng , Kuantan were safe . <p> She added that the Government was concerned about the welfare of Lynas employees but the bigger aspect should be seen especially concerning the safety of Kuantan residents . <p> " According to my views , we have to look at the larger aspect , namely the welfare and safety of Kuantan 's overall population of 600,000 people . <p> " Many among them work in Gebeng and are exposed to air pollution in terms of dust and radiation coming from the factory , this is also important , " she told Bernama after appearing as a guest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at Wisma Bernama , here tonight . <p> Today , Lynas took out advertisements in several newspapers with testimonies of its workers who expressed their confidence in its operations . <p> Prior to this , they had voiced their fears about job security should the plant be shut down . <p> Yesterday , Energy , Technology , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin announced the setting up of an executive committee to evaluate the operations of the Lynas Advance Materials Plant ( LAMP ) in Gebeng , Pahang to ensure that Malaysia does not become a dumping ground for hazardous waste from other countries . <p> According to Yeo , the committee is made up of individuals who had not openly stated their stand on the plant , whether they were in favour of it or not . <p> The committee has been given six weeks to do the evaluation and give its report and recommendations to the Government .
