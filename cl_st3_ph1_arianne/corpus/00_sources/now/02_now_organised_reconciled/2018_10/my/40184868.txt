<h> No decision on coal power plants in Sabah yet , says Yeo <p> KOTA KINABALU : The state and federal governments have yet to decide on developing coal power plants in Sabah as the focus is to strengthen the transmission and distribution of supply first . <p> Energy , Green Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin ( pic ) said the power reserve in Sabah is at 32% , which means there is enough supply for now . <p> " The main reason there is so much disruption is due to the transmission and distribution networks , which is why the focus is to get this up and running properly first , " she said after a meeting with Chief Minister Datuk Seri Mohd Shafie Apdal here on Tuesday ( Oct 23 ) . <p> She said the issue of coal power plants was discussed but it did not mean anything concrete at the moment . <p> " We are looking at many different suggestions . We will have a better picture of what Sabah needs and how the state and federal government can come to an agreement on solving power supply @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said the focus is to provide reliable and sustainable power in Sabah and make sure the problem of disruptions does n't reoccur . <p> " We will come up with a plan by year-end hopefully , and then we will make an announcement once we have it , " she said . <p> Yeo also pointed out that the ongoing federal project to improve power supply in Sabah would most probably be completed by 2021 . <p> " By then , Sabah can expect a 50% reduction in disruptions but then , it would still be double the rate faced by consumers in Peninsular Malaysia , " she said , <p> Sabah currently experiences four times higher disruptions compared to the peninsula , " she said . <p> Meanwhile , Shafie said they also spoke about dams and gas among other issues during the meeting . <p> " We want to ensure that supply and distribution of power reaches all consumers and industries , " he said .
