<h> ' Himalayan Viagra ' under threat from climate change <p> WASHINGTON : A prized caterpillar fungus that is more valuable than gold and is nicknamed " Himalayan Viagra " in Asia , where it is seen as a wonder drug , is becoming harder to find due to climate change , researchers said Monday . <p> People in China and Nepal have been killed in clashes over the years over the elusive fungus " yarchagumba , " known formally as Ophiocordyceps sinensis . <p> Although it has no scientifically proven benefits , people who boil yarchagumba in water to make tea or add it to soups and stews believe it cures everything from impotence to cancer . <p> It is " one of the world 's most valuable biological commodities , providing a crucial source of income for hundreds of thousands of collectors , " said the report in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences , a peer-reviewed US journal . <p> In recent decades , the fungus has skyrocketed in popularity and prices have soared -- it can fetch up to three times the price of gold in Beijing , researchers say . <p> While @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , researchers wanted to find out more . <p> So they interviewed around four
