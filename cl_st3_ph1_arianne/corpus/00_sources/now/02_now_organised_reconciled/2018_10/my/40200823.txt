<h> Import of plastic waste banned <p> PUTRAJAYA : Malaysia has permanently banned the import of plastic scrap , following revelations the country has become a top dumping ground for plastic waste , said Housing and Local Government Minister Zuraida Kamaruddin . <p> However , companies can apply for APs to import quality , clean plastic . <p> " We have agreed to stop the issuance of APs for the import of plastic scrap , the types that are contaminated . <p> " Those who wish to import quality , clean plastic can apply for new APs but there is strict criteria and we have added more conditions . <p> " At present , only eight companies in Malaysia fully comply with this criteria , " Zuraida told reporters after a multi-ministry meeting at her ministry here . <p> The additional conditions for companies include obtaining approval from the Department of Occupational Safety and Health , having their own factories and submitting their list of end product buyers . <p> " Some AP owners previously did n't even have their own factories , so they sent their plastic waste to illegal factories , " said Zuraida . <p> Zuraida @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the next three years , to protect the local plastic industry . <p> The plastic recycling and manufacturing industry in Malaysia has an economic value of RM30bil while globally , it is worth RM600bil . <p> " So we will phase out the import of plastic waste in three years and then we go fully local , " said Zuraida . <p> Zuraida said Malaysia imported a total of 1.8 million tonnes of plastic from 33 countries since 2015 . <p> " After this , we will limit import to only developed countries such as the United States , Korea , Japan and European countries .
