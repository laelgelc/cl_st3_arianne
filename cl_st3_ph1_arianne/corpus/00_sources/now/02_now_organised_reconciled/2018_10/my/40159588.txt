<h> Govt freezes licence issuance for plastic waste recycling factories <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The government has frozen the issuance of licences to set up plastic waste recycling factories in Malaysia and banned the import of such materials to ensure existing illegal factories do not continue their operations . <p> Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin told Parliament yesterday that the government had established a joint ministerial committee to tackle plastic waste in the country . <p> Yeo said the committee comprises housing and local government ; water , land and natural resources ; and international trade and industry ministries , as well as her ministry . It will meet for the first time on Oct 26 to deliberate on plans to resolve issues concerning the import of plastic waste . <p> " Since China banned the import of plastic waste , developed countries have diverted their waste export destination to Southeast Asia , including Malaysia , and the situation is getting worse , especially with more and more illegal plastic recycling factories found locally . <p> " The Malaysian government has conducted raids on these factories , particularly in Kuala @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
