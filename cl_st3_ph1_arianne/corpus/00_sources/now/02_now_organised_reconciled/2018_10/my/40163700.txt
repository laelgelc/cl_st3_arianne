<p> KUALA LUMPUR : Sustainable Energy Development Authority ( Seda ) Malaysia has revoked non-performing Feed-in Tariff ( FiT ) projects in total of 155.7256 MW last month and will release a total quota of 114.5682 MW for eligible applicants to apply . <p> The small hydro category will have a quota of 74.5682 MW for applications expected to achieve commercial operations by 2020 and 2021 . The quota allocated for biogas is 30 MW whereas for biomass is 10 MW ; these quota are for applications achieving commercial operations by 2021 . <p> This announcement was made by , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin at the opening of the International Greentech and Eco Products Exhibition ( IGEM ) 2018 yesterday morning . <p> According to Yeo , Seda will also conduct the inaugural exercise of e-bidding for their biogas quota . The aim of the e-bidding process is to create a better pricing efficiency for electricity generated from the biogas resources through healthy competition . <p> Seda Malaysia will first conduct its gamma testing of the e-bidding system together with the biogas industry on 25th October 2018 and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ applicants can submit their applications through the e-FiT online system ( efit.Seda.gov.my ) during Q4 of 2018 . <p> Seda will announce the dates of applications one month before the opening period to ensure fairness in the process . -- Bernama
