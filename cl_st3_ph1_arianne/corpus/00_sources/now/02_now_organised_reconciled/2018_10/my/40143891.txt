<h> Fuziah Salleh withdraws from Lynas review committee <p> KUANTAN ( Bernama ) : Kuantan MP Fuziah Salleh ( pic ) has withdrawn as the head of the committee to review the conditions of operation for Lynas Corporation Ltd 's rare earth plant . <p> She said she conveyed her decision to the Cabinet last Tuesday after considering the matter and also to prevent Lynas from shifting the focus from the real issue . <p> Fuziah , who is also a Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department , said her withdrawal is to prevent questions over her role and that she would be more comfortable voicing issues as an elected representative . <p> " If I am in the committee , I can not give any comments to the media and in public , " she said to reporters after attending the Pahang Hardcore Poor Entrepreneur Carnival here on Friday ( Oct 12 ) . <p> Asked who would replace her in the committee , Fuziah said Energy , Green Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin would make the announcement soon . <p> Commenting on the peaceful demonstration by over 1,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Lynas management does not talk about the plight of the 600,000 residents in the district . <p> She said the Australian management only said that the plant is safe and operating at low radioactive levels but does not talk about radioactive waste . <p> " Now , we are talking about radioactive waste , not low radiation levels . If so , the waste should not be in the Gebeng Industrial Site . If they say it 's safe , I challenge them to send the waste to Australia , " she said . -- Bernama
