<p> KUALA LUMPUR : Three laws regarding environment and wildlife will be amended to improve its governance , management and enforcement , the Dewan Rakyat was told yesterday . <p> Tengku Zulpuri Shah Raja Puji <p> Deputy Water , Land and Natural Resources Minister Tengku Zulpuri Shah Raja Puji said the three acts were the National Forestry Act 1984 , Wildlife Conservation Act 2010 and Environmental Quality Act 1974 which is under the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry . <p> " Most of the enforcement is within the jurisdiction of the state government . <p> So , the amendments to the acts will involve the enforcement at the federal government level , " he said . <p> He was replying to Ahmad Nazlan Idris ( BN-Jerantut ) who had wanted to know about the amendment to combat exploitation of flora and fauna in the country . <p> Replying to a supplementary question from Datuk Seri Dr Wan Junaidi Tuanku Jaafar ( PBB-Santubong ) on the current status of the proposed amendments , Tengku Zulpuri said it was in the discussion stage involving the ministry , Attorney-General 's Chambers and Forestry Department Peninsular Malaysia @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ six months , he added . -- Bernama
