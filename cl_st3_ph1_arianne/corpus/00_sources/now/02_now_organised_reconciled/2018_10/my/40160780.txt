<h> Yeo Bee Yin : ' Good news ' for renewable energy players <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Oct 17 ) : The Malaysian government will announce policies to fast-track its plan to increase the portion of renewable energy ( RE ) in the country 's total energy generation mix to grow the RE sector , said Energy , Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister , Yeo Bee Yin . <p> Yeo said today that she will announce the policies tomorrow . She said RE constitutes 2% of the country 's total energy generation mix as at 2016 . <p> Today , Yeo said : " I will announce some good news for RE players tomorrow ( Oct 18 ) . I 'm looking forward to the activities to grow the sector ; that will come after this announcement . <p> " These policies would be the result of a lot of engagement with stakeholders and industry players , " she said without elaborating . <p> She said Malaysia plans to increase its RE portion of total energy generation mix to 20% by 2030 .
