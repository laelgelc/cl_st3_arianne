<h> Solar power users to get cheaper electricity bills <p> TWO new policies will be introduced to help domestic and commercial users of solar power to enjoy cheaper electricity bills from Jan 1 , 2019 , says Energy , Science , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> The breakthrough follows the implementation of a " solar leasing " concept and better profits for those producing excess solar power . <p> " We have decided to improve and expand the Supply Agreement for the Renewable Energy ( SARE ) programme to allow consumers to install solar panels for their homes under a solar leasing concept . <p> " Under solar leasing , there will be zero upfront cost , where individuals need not come out with any initial costs for installing the solar panels , " Yeo said when answering a question raised by Ahmad Fadhli Shaari ( PAS-Pasir Mas ) . <p> This model , she said , was similar to purchasing handphones but was now applicable to solar panels . <p> " The monthly payments for solar power or repayments to the solar power company can be done through the Tenaga Nasional Bhd ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the size of the area fitted with solar panels , she said consumers would see a reduction in their monthly electricity bills . <p> Also to be implemented on Jan 1 is an improved Net Energy Metering ( NEM ) mechanism , to allow consumers producing solar power to enjoy better rebates from TNB . <p> Yeo said the current NEM system was not well received . <p> As of October this year , only 17MW or 3% out of the 500MW quota was taken up . <p> She said this was because consumers were selling excess solar power at a displaced cost to TNB.
