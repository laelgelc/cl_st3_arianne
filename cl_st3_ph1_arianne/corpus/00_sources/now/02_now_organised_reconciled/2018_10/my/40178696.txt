<p> PENANG has signed a memorandum of understanding ( MoU ) with the United Nations Human Settlement Programme ( UN-Habitat ) to establish a strategic partnership to advocate and promote the New Urban Agenda ( NUA ) and the United Nation 's Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) here . <p> Chief Minister Chow Kon Yeow said the MoU was the first of its kind between a state government and the UN-Habitat , which is the main proponent of SDGs adopted by the United Nations General Assembly in September 2015 , which Malaysia is committed to fulfilling by 2030 . <p> NUA is a framework that guides efforts around urbanisation and development such as local fiscal systems , urban planning and basic services and infrastructure to ensure sustainable development . <p> " It is in line with the aspiration of the state government to achieve Vision 2030 to transform Penang into a family-focused green and smart state that inspires the nation , " Chow said during the recent signing ceremony in Komtar . <p> He signed the five-year MoU on behalf of the Chief Minister Incorporated ( CMI ) while UN-Habitat executive director Datuk Seri Maimunah Mohd Sharif signed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and George Town Conservation and Development Corporation ( GTCDC ) would be among the first to align theirprojects to support the implementation of the SDGs . <p> " The state would fund the programme with an initial amount of $250,000 ( RM1mil ) and hopes that in due course all state governments and state related agencies would adopt the SDGs in the planning , implementation and monitoring of their projects , " said Chow . <p> Maimunah said that although the SDGs and the NUA were discussed and agreed at global level , they were implemented locally . <p> " Therefore , only with activeparticipation of both state and local governments , progress could be achieved . <p> " We would develop a common framework for Penang projects through the implementation of a baseline study to establish an information base to monitor and assess progress under NUA on projects focused on climate change , urban resilience , urban regeneration , land use planning and city extension , " she added .
