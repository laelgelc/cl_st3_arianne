<h> Twelve years too long for zero plastic <p> ENERGY , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin has recently done a lot to break up the nation 's love affair with single-use plastic . With the upcoming unveiling of the " Roadmap towards zero single-use plastic 2018-2030 " , Malaysia now has a targeted timeline to wean its people off single -- use plastic . <p> But 12 years would appear to be much too long to get to that elusive zero . The reason given for this is that time is needed by the government to find affordable and accessible alternatives , and for plastic manufacturers to switch to producing more eco-friendly products . <p> Alternatives for single-use plastic bags -- the reusable bags which are estimated to be equivalent to 170 plastic bags -- have already been around for more than 10 years , as have reusable water bottles . <p> Where manufacturers are concerned , they should have known by now that the single-use plastic industry would come to an end eventually due to the constant negative press on their products over many years . Other priorities on the environment may put this down on the list of things to achieve or the people driving and implementing this roadmap might be transferred out , and this would reduce the effectiveness of the plan .
