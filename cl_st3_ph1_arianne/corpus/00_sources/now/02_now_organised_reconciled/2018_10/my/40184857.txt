<h> Govt developing SMS alert system on tsunami , says Yeo <p> KOTA KINABALU : The government is developing an SMS tsunami alert system , with the hope of allowing people time to flee to safer grounds if disaster strikes , says Yeo Bee Yin ( pic ) . <p> The Energy , Green Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister said this is in view of the climate change and geologists ' warning that some areas in the country , specifically Tawau off Sabah , could be hit with tsunami . <p> After a meeting with Chief Minister Datuk Seri Shafie Apdal here Tuesday ( Oct 23 ) , Yeo said the government has decided to prepare itself better to face disasters . <p> " A person will receive a text message on the phone at the area where the tsunami alert or warning is issued , " she said . <p> She said once the system is developed , the government will work towards creating awareness and conducting drills to ensure people know what to do when disaster strikes . <p> " All our 56 tsunami warning sirens nationwide at hotspots are working , but @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ if the sirens are sounded , people may not know what to do , " she said . <p> She said the ministry , together with other relevant agencies , are working on a plan on how to handle the matter . <p> She also said that the SMS alert system could be expanded for other disasters in future . <p> On the recurring landslides in Cameron Highlands , Yeo said a thorough study is needed to find the exact causes . <p> " We will need to study what could have caused these landslides so regularly , and after we have accurate details , then we can find solutions to the problem , " she said . <p> On another development , Shafie said the state government will follow the directive of Prime Minister Tun Dr Mahathir Mohamad to raise the minimum marriage age to 18 . <p> He also said the Sabah government does not mind having the Chief Minister 's post limited to two terms , as suggested by Dr Mahathir . <p> " But if there is a need
