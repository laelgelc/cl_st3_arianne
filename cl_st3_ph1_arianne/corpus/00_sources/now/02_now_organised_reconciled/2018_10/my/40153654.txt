<h> Executive Committee formed to review Lynas ' operations in Pahang <p> PUTRAJAYA ( Oct 15 ) : An executive committee has been set up to review the operations of the Lynas Advance Materials Plant ( LAMP ) in Gebeng , Pahang to ensure that Malaysia does not become a toxic waste dumping ground for other countries . <p> Minister of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Yeo Bee Yin said the Cabinet on Oct 10 had decided that members of the committee would comprise those who had not openly stated their stand either to support or protest against Lynas 's operations . <p> " This is to prevent any parties from questioning the credibility of the committee , due to the stand of some of its members ( over the issue ) , " she said in a statement today . <p> She said the Ministry had examined the list of committee members and found them all to be experts in their relevant fields .
