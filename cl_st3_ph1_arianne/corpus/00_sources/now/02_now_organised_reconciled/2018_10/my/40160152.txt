<h> Yeo Bee Yin : All states to charge for plastic bags by 2021 <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The federal government wants all states to begin imposing a pollution charge on plastic bags used in hypermarkets , chain stores and restaurants within three years from 2019 . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said there would also be a " no-straw default " position for eateries as the government was looking to cut down on single-usage plastic waste . <p> She said this during the launch of the ministry 's Roadmap Towards Zero Single-use Plastics 2018-2030 here on Wednesday ( Oct 17 ) . <p> The pollution charge , to be imposed during Phase 1 of the roadmap from 2019 to 2021 , would be collected via local councils in the states . <p> The roadmap will have three phases and will run until 2030 . <p> " The implementation of the charge , as well as the no-straw default , will be done according to the ( readiness of ) various states , as each has a different public awareness level , " she said . <p> Yeo also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government would be launching standards drawn up by Sirim for biodegradable and compostable bags .
