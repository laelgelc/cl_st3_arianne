<h> Trending Now <h> Fuziah withdraws from Lynas review committee <p> KUANTAN ( Oct 12 ) : Kuantan MP Fuziah Salleh today withdrew as the head of the committee to review the conditions of operation of the rare earth plant of Lynas Corporation Ltd . <p> She said she conveyed her decision to the Cabinet last Tuesday after making the appropriate consideration and on the basis of not wanting Lynas to shift the focus from the real issue . <p> Fuziah , who is a Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department , said her withdrawal is also to prevent her role to be questioned and she will be more comfortable voicing issues as an elected representative . <p> " If I am in the committee , I can not give any comment to the media and in public because I will be questioned , " she said to reporters after attending the Pahang Hardcore Poor Entrepreneur Carnival here . <p> Asked who would replace her in the committee , Fuziah said Energy , Green Technology , Science , Climate Change and Environment Minister Yeo Bee Yin will make an announcement on the matter soon . <p> Commenting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their families last Saturday , Fuziah said the Lynas management does not talk about the plight of the 600,000 residents in the district . <p> She said the Australian management only said that the plant is safe and operating at low radioactive levels but does not talk about radioactive waste . <p> " Now , we are talking about radioactive waste , not low radioactive levels . By right , the waste should not be in the Gebeng Industrial Site . If they say it 's safe , I challenge them to send the waste to Australia , " she said .
