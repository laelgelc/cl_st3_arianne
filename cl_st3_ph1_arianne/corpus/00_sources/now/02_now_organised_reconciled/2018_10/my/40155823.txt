<h> Joint ministerial committee to be set up : M'sia wo n't be plastic dumping ground <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The Cabinet has decided to set up a joint ministerial committee to look into ways of dealing with the import of plastic waste in the country . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the committee comprises her ministry , the Housing and Local Government Ministry , International Trade and Industry Ministry , as well as the Water , Land and Natural Resources Ministry . <p> " The Housing and Local Government Ministry will be the main coordinator of this committee . <p> " I would like to stress once again that we will do our best , and that the government is committed to ensure that Malaysia will not be a dumping ground of plastic waste for advanced nations , " she said in reply to Ma'mun Sulaiman ( PH-Kalabakan ) during the Ministerial question time on Tuesday ( Oct 16 ) . <p> Yeo added the issue of imported plastic waste had become more serious due to China 's ban on such material . <p> Following this , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Asia , including Malaysia , Indonesia , Thailand and Vietnam . <p> She said the ministry , through joint enforcement operations with other agencies , had conducted raids on illegal factories processing plastic waste . <p> Yeo said some 30 illegal factories had been closed . <p> She added that the enforcement team had ensured that apart from the factories being sealed off , their electricity and water supplies were also cut off . <p> " I have also told the agencies to nab the operators of these factories -- to investigate and bring them to court to ensure they are punished in accordance to the law , " she added . <p> To another question by Siti Zailah Mohd Yusoff ( PAS-Rantau Panjang ) on the measures to get rid of plastic waste in the country , Yeo said her ministry would ensure that all plastic material that could not be recycled would be banned . <p> She also said that her ministry was looking into reviewing Acts related to the environment .
