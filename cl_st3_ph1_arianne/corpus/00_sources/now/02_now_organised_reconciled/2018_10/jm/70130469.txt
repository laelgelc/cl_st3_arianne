<h> MGI partners with Dutch firm to strengthen coastal resilience <p> Jamaica 's Mona GeoInformatics Institute ( MGI ) has partnered with Dutch research firm , Deltares to strengthen coastal resilience in the Caribbean Region . <p> The Deltares-MGI partnership is expected to add to the economic investment potential of the region through research that enhances tourism , shipping , fisheries , and deep-sea exploration . <p> The Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) for co-operation was officially entered into on last week during Deltares 10-year anniversary celebration in Delft , Netherlands . <p> Deltares , an independent institute for applied research in the field of water and subsurface with their main focus being on deltas , coastal regions and river basins .
