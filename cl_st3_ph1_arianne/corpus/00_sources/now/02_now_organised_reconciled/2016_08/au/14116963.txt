<h> PNG PM O'Neill to meet Barack Obama <p> PNG Prime Minister Peter O'Neill will sit down with US President Barack Obama at the conclusion of the Pacific Islands Conference of Leaders now under way in Hawaii . <p> Mr O'Neill , the conference chairman , said Mr Obama was nearing the end of his term but was still the president , with a great deal of influence in the global community . <p> Climate change was the number one issue on the minds of Pacific leaders , the prime minister said , and their people were in grave danger from the effects of extreme weather brought about by the changing climate .
