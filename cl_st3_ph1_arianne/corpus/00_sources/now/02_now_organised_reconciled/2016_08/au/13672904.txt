<p> There is a strange dichotomy going on in the upper ranks of Suncorp , one of the country 's biggest general insurers . <p> Its CEO , Michael Cameron , is not convinced humans cause climate change but he has radically changed the company 's reinsurance policies to protect shareholders against catastrophes that he believes are caused by climate change . <p> Cameron told Chanticleer on Thursday that there is no doubt Australia 's climate is changing .
