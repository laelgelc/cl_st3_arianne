<p> Christians around the world are joining in the annual World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation on 1st September . <p> The date also marks the beginning of the Season of Creation , which runs until 4th October - commemorated by Roman Catholics and other churches from the Western tradition as the feast day of St Francis of Assisi , known for his affinity with nature and in particular as the writer of the prayer , the Canticle of the Creatures . <p> The 1st September was proclaimed as a day of prayer for the environment by the Orthodox Church in 1989 with many other Christian churches since following suit , most recently the Roman Catholic Church following a declaration by Pope Francis last year . <p> In a video message for the day , Rev Dr Olav Fykse Tveit , general secretary of the World Council of Churches , said Christians believe God did not abandon creation and " that we ourselves can become beacons of hope by sowing the seeds of a future filled with justice and peace " . <p> Noting that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a price tag " , he said it was time for Christians to affirm once again that " creation is not for sale " . <p> " This means that we need to change drastically , the way we produce goods , the way we organise trade and operate in the financial system .
