<p> Do you think your data is safe because your computer is n't connected to the internet or a network ? Wrong . The DiskFiltration hack , demonstrated in this video by security researcher Mordechai Guri of Israel 's Ben-Gurion University , works by controlling the actuator in a hard drive which moves back and forth across the drive 's platters to read and write data .
