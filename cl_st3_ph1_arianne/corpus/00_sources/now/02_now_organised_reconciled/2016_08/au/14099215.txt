<h> Insurers push G20 on climate commitments <p> Insurers with $US1.2 trillion under management have called for the Group of 20 to set a timetable to phase out subsidies for fossil fuels by 2020 when they meet at a summit in China this weekend . <p> Aviva , Aegon NV and MS Amlin said fossil fuel subsidies were at odds with commitments by G20 nations to combat global warming agreed by almost 200 countries last year at a Paris summit . <p> ' Climate change in particular represents the mother of all risks , ' Aviva CEO Mark Wilson said in a statement . <p> The companies called on the G20 leaders , who meet in the Chinese city of Hangzhou on September 4-5 , to set ' a clear timeline for the full and equitable phase-out by all G20 members of all fossil fuel subsidies by 2020 ' . <p> A phase-out should start with the elimination of all subsidies for fossil fuel exploration and coal production , they said . <p> Their statement was also signed by the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries and UK-based energy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to phase out fossil fuels , the main man-made source of greenhouse gases blamed for climate change , since a meeting in 2009 in Pittsburgh . <p> The British-based Overseas Development Institute think-tank estimated that average annual subsidies for fossil fuel production were $US444 billion in 2013 and 2014 , roughly four times the subsidies for renewable energy in 2013 . <p> Last week , investors managing more than $13 trillion of assets urged the G20 to ratify the Paris climate deal by the end of 2016 to help avert droughts , floods , mudslides and rising sea levels . <p> No G20 nations have yet completed the ratification process , according to a UN . tracker .
