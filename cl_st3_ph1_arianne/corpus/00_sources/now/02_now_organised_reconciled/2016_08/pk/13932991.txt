<p> KARACHI : World Humanitarian day was observed under the auspices of National Humanitarian Network ( NHN ) Sindh chapter . The theme of the day was " one humanity " . Speaking at the occasion Prof. Mushtaque Mirani of Sindh Rural Support Program said that aid workers often wok in harsh conditions without any support . He called on development sector to introduce different mechanisms of transparency to ensure accountability and promote humanitarian principles . He urged that the aid workers should be recognized through giving them awards , which may promote dedication and passion among the people to contribute more towards the cause of humanity . <p> Nasir Ali Panhwar of Centre for Environment &amp; Development said that development strategies must be focused on reducing the risk of humanitarian crisis and their impact when they occur , through better preparedness and capacity building . He said that support for sustainable recovery in post crisis and transition settings is crucial , while humanitarian assistance should be considered an investment in development .
