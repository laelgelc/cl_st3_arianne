<p> An official in the ministry told The Express Tribune that the EU has also expressed serious concern over capital punishment in Pakistan . The current government had ended the moratorium on death sentences following the Army Public School carnage in Peshawar back in December 2014 . However , Pakistan managed to satisfy the union to some extent on the ground that since it is in a state of turmoil fighting terrorism , death sentence in certain cases is the last resort to control the situation . <p> However , sources said that the latest concern of the EU is the rising trend of honour killing in Pakistan , which is at an all-time high .
