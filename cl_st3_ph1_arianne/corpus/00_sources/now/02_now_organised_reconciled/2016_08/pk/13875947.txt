<p> BAHAWALPUR : Commissioner Saqib Zafar requested district officials on Tuesday to mobilise all available resources to plant a million saplings and plants during the division-wide Monsoon Tree Plantation Drive . <p> Zafar inaugurated the tree plantation campaign by planting a shrub in his office lawn . <p> " Tree Plantation campaigns are necessary to meet the challenges of climate change . " The commissioner urged forest officials to be vigilant and to extend their support in making the Monsoon Tree Plantation Drive a success . <p> Faisal Haroon gave a detailed briefing regarding the campaign . <p> He said 428,000 saplings would be planted in Bahawalpur district ; 286,000 would be planted at Bahawalnagar district ; and 336,000 saplings would be planted in Rahim Yar Khan district . <p> He said they had also set up 24 sale points where plants were being sold for Rs3 and saplings for Rs1.
