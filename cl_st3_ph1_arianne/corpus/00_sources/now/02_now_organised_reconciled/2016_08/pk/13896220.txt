The politicians of KP think that if the dam is built at Kalabagh a large area of the province including Nowshera will come under water causing huge loss to the residents , while Sindhi politicians have their own apprehensions about unfair distribution of water and Punjab denying them their due share . <p> I fully endorse the Wapda chairman 's views . We need the dam not only for power generation but also for enhanced storage of water which we must have because of climate change .
