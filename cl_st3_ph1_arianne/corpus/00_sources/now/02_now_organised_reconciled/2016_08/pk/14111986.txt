<p> ISLAMABAD : Plastic shopping bags are still in use in the markets of Islamabad despite a ban by Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) on their manufacturing and sale . <p> According to Ministry of Climate Change , Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) have taken a number of measures for replacing the use of plastic bags but still it is being sold without any restriction . <p> It is an established fact the plastic shopping bags are harmful for human health . In 2013 , Pak-EPA had notified SRO titled " Prohibition of non degradable plastic products ( manufacturing , sale and usage ) regulation " in 2013 . <p> Four companies have so far been registered with Pak-EPA to import , manufacture , stockpile , trade , supply and distribute , sell or use any scheduled plastic products which are bio-degradable . <p> Due to this reason a number of major stores of Islamabad like Utility Stores , Metro , Tehzib , Rahat , McDonald and Shaheen Chemist etc are using environment friendly plastic shopping bags on the directions of Pakistan Environmental Protection @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Capital Territory ( ICT ) to use only bio-degradable shopping bags . <p> Pak-EPA officials claim that they are making efforts to implement regulations in letter and spirit . However , according to information submitted to National Assembly this month , Pak-EPA is still not aware about the exact number of plastic bag manufacturing units in Islamabad . It has requested the Islamabad administration and CDA to update it about the manufacturers of the bags .
