Cadogan was elected unopposed . <p> Forty-three people have put their names forward for 14 Dunedin City Council spots , and 11 for the mayoralty . <p> That compared with 35 for council and nine for mayor in 2013 . <p> Every community board in the city will be contested , for the first time in years . <p> That is a remarkable turnaround since Monday this week , when it looked as if there might
