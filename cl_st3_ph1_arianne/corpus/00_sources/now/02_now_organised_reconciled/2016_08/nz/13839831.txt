<h> Awarded Mt Cook pilot witnesses climate change effects <p> Mt Cook ski plane pilot Wayne McMillan has received an important industry award . <p> A notable career in aviation has been crowned with an unexpected honour , and a unique view of the effects of climate change on Aoraki/Mt Cook . <p> Mt Cook skiplane pilot Wayne McMillan said he was in disbelief when he heard he had won this year 's Greg Vujcich Memorial Award , as he had no idea he had even been nominated . <p> The prestigious national award is given by the New Zealand Air Line Pilots Association ( NZALPA ) to accomplished long-serving pilots who have also taught and mentored other pilots . <p> McMillan , who had flown for what was formerly the Mt Cook Airline since 1979 , and who had taught about 20 people to fly , said he had also watched the effects of climate change creep up the mountain over the decades .
