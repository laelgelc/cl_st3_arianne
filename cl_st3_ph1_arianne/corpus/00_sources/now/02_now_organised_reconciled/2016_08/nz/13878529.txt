<h> Govt will ratify Paris climate agreement by end of 2016 <p> UPDATED 5.27PM The Government says it will ratify the Paris Agreement on climate change it signed up to last year . <p> The international agreement commits countries to reducing their greenhouse gas emissions to 30 percent below 2005 levels by 2030 . <p> Climate Change Minister Paula Bennett said they plan to ratify the agreement by the end of the year . <p> " Certainly that 2030 target of being 30 percent below 2005 levels is ambitious , and we wo n't get there by doing the status quo , so there has to be more action , " she said . <p> Ms Bennett said this could see the current emissions trading scheme ( ETS ) being tweaked . <p> " Yes it will . So we 've done phase one of it , and now we 're working on phase two , and we 'll be going out for submissions on that and looking at some of the lessons we can learn from the past , and making sure that we 've got that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> Oxfam New Zealand has welcomed the Government 's plans to ratify the agreement by the end of the year . <p> Executive Director Rachael Le Mesurier said it is really important the Government takes action on climate change . <p> She said climate change is here and the Government needs to make sure we do all we can to honour the agreement and reduce gas emissions . <p> But Greenpeace says the Government 's announcement wo n't be worth anything if it does n't make serious changes . <p> Greenpeace Climate Campaigner Kate Simcock said to meet the 2030 targets , a major change in Government policy will be needed . <p> " They 're just doing absolutely nothing at the moment to reduce emissions , and our emissions are soaring . So they urgently need to stop building new roads , exploring oil and gas , putting taxing on solar , and intensive dairying . " <p> So far 22 countries other than New Zealand have ratified the agreement .
