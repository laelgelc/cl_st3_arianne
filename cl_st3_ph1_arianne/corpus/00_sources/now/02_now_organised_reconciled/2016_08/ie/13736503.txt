<h> Earth overshoot day : Earth 's resources for year used in less than eight months <p> Monday , August 08 , 2016 <p> Emily Beament <p> Humans will have used up the Earth 's budget of natural resources for the year in less than eight months , environmental campaigners warn . <p> People are putting more carbon dioxide emissions into the atmosphere than the forests and oceans can absorb , and catching fish and cutting down forests more quickly than nature can replenish them . <p> As a result , the world will reach " Earth overshoot day " today , the point in the year when humans have exhausted annual supplies such as land , trees and fish , and outstripped Earth 's capacity to absorb greenhouse gases . <p> The problem is worsening , with the planet sliding into " ecological debt " earlier and earlier . <p> " It will stimulate emerging sectors like renewable energy , while reducing risks and costs associated with the impact of climate change on inadequate infrastructure . <p> " The only thing we need more of is political @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ already embracing the challenge , pointing to Costa Rica which generated 97% of its electricity from renewable sources in the first three months of this year . <p> The UK , Germany and Portugal are also setting new records for renewables , while China 's government has outlined a plan to reduce its citizens ' meat consumption by 50% , which could cut the emissions from the livestock industry by a billion tonnes by 2030 . <p> In the UK , solar outperformed coal over the course of a month for the second month on record in July , while overall renewables contributed a quarter of the country 's electricity generation in 2015 . <p> The Global Footprint Network is also urging individuals to take action to live more sustainable lives .
