<h> Business <h> Electronic Concepts celebrates 35th anniversary in Oughterard <p> Wednesday , 31st August , 2016 1:00am <p> Electronic Concepts Europe ( ECL ) is celebrating 35 years in business in Oughterard , Co . Galway . ECL employs over 65 people , with an average service period of 16 years . Some employees have been with the company since its inception . <p> The company manufactures high reliability film capacitors for the electronics industry . Originally established with support from IDA Ireland as an off-shore manufacturing facility for its parent company , Electronic Concepts Inc. of Eatontown , New Jersey in the US , ECL has , over the years , evolved into a complete business unit with design , engineering , R&amp;D , sales and marketing functions . <p> The company initially exported 100% of its products back to the US , however today US exports represent less than 5% , with the bulk of exports now going to new markets in Europe and Asia . <p> ECL 's products have changed significantly over its 35-year history , moving from producing miniature devices to more @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opportunity and growth potential for film capacitors in the power electronics industry at the turn of the millennium , in particular in the renewable energy sector . To meet the needs of these new markets ECL changed its manufacturing operation to accommodate these larger power capacitors . <p> This re-invention of the company has been a success and ECL 's customer base has diversified and grown significantly . Current customers include some of the world 's leading companies in the power electronics industry . 95% Of ECL 's products are supplied to the green energy industry . <p> The company 's Technical Director , Conor Gibney says the potential for future growth in the renewable energy sector is strong . " Global commitments towards offsetting climate change and the need for Green House Gas Emission reductions will provide for continued growth within the Renewable Energy sector for many years to come .
