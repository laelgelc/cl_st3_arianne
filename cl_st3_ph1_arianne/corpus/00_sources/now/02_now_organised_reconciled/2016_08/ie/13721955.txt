What kinds of opportunities might arise as we begin to leave fossil fuels behind ? <p> The agriculture sector is responsible for 40pc of affected emissions and will be increasingly under the spotlight . Government must find ways to mobilise farmers as investors , and there is a need for new business models to promote partnerships between rural communities and professional project developers . These may include equity partnerships , lease and power purchase agreements , and co-operatives , all common in other countries but not yet in Ireland . <p> There will also be greater pressure to pursue carbon-neutral farming , particularly within the context of an expanding dairy sector .
