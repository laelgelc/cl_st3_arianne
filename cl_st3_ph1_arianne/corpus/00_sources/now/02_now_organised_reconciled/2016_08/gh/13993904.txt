<h> AASU Accord Of 5th African Students And Youth Summit <p> The All Africa Students Union Accord on Climate change and food security was agreed upon and promulgated by a team of student 's representatives from the regional blocks of Africa during the 5th summit of the All Africa Students union held on Accra Ghana from 10th to 14th August 13 , 2016 . <p> We discussed the trending issues on climate change as well as the effects it has on agriculture and food security . The summit hopes that this accord and the recommendations herein would inform stakeholders and other policy agencies on specifically how the students and youths of Africa would want the issues of climate change in relation to food security be tackled for results to be seen and how they can be engaged as the largest body in achieving the goals of building good adaptive capabilities and ensuring food security in the future . <p> Since the Maputo Declaration in 2003 , the position of agriculture on national and regional political agenda on the Continent has been reassessed . However , the rising menace of Climate Change within the global sphere especially in the early stages of the Millennium is beginning to threaten the fruits of these efforts . Unfavorable weather conditions and unpredictably harsh climatic factors have considerably impacted on agriculture globally and more especially in Africa where more than 50% of the populace depend on rain-fed and other natural sources for production of farm produce . The above has therefore occasioned global discussions on this creeping menace especially when its effect is proving to threaten the very existence of human lives . <p> The Copenhagen Consensus in 2009 was therefore the lead forum that began galvanizing governments across the globe towards sustainable efforts at combating Climate Change and its attendant problems . Since then scientists , agronomists , politicians and other relevant professionals have made the issue of Climate Change and food security , a better part of regional and national discourses and there have been several engagements on ways to handle and successfully negotiate this canker as a continent . <p> Armed with the above knowledge , we the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and under AASU decided to spotlight this issue and deliberate responses which is reflective of the views of the Students and Youth across the Continent .
