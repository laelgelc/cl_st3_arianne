<p> The New Forest national park is being destroyed by overgrazing by its ponies , driven in part by taxpayer subsidies , according to Chris Packham . <p> The naturalist and broadcaster claims that overgrazing by the ponies , as well as cattle and deer , is causing loss of woodland and threatening species in one of the UK 's most important biodiversity hotspots . <p> " In simplistic terms , we have a New Forest now but we wo n't have a New Forest tomorrow because there is simply no regeneration taking place , " said Packham , who has lived in the forest for 12 years . <p> Packham and other campaigners blame the biodiversity losses on pony numbers which have increased steeply in the last 50 years from around 2,000 in 1960 to around 5,000 today .
