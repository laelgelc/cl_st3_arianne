The Louvre Museum of Art in Paris was recently forced to defend its collection from abnormal flooding . Last week , alongside the Royal Opera House , National Portrait Gallery and Royal Shakespeare Company , it was announced that the British Museum would be renewing its sponsorship deal with BP for a further five years .
