<p> A 12-year-old boy in the far north of Russia has died in an outbreak of anthrax that experts believe was triggered when unusually warm weather caused the release of the bacteria . <p> The boy was one of 72 nomadic herders , including 41 children , hospitalised in the town of Salekhard in the Arctic Circle , after reindeer began dying en masse from anthrax . <p> Five adults and two other children have been diagnosed with the disease , which is known as " Siberian plague " in Russian and was last seen in the region in 1941 . <p> More than 2,300 reindeer have died , and at least 63 people have been evacuated from a quarantine area around the site of the outbreak . <p> " We literally fought for the life of each person , but the infection showed its cunning , " the Yamal governor , Dmitry Kobylkin , told the Interfax news agency .
