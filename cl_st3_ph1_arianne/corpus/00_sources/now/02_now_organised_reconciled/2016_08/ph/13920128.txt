<h> Indigents get glasses during sight saving month <p> SAN JOSE , Antique Aug.19 ( PIA ) - - The Samahan ng mga Optometrists sa Antique identified some 200 indigents as recipients of the reading glasses in two municipalities of the province . <p> Dr. Ligaya Sarmiento-Onanad , head of the Samahan ng mga Optometrists sa Antique said that the recipients in San Remigio and Sibalom will receive their reading glasses this month in line with the observance of the Sight Saving Month with the aims to increase the public 's awareness of the of the importance of proper eye care and the prevention of avoidable blindness and eye diseases . <p> " Eating vegetables and fruits , practice healthy lifestyle , and regular appointment with the optometrist or ophthalmologist are important in taking care of our eyes , Onanad said during PIA Radio program . <p> She said that " our eyes are our windows to the world , therefore the month-long Sight Saving celebration must not only be observed in the month of August but all throughout the year .
