this year . <p> One of which was the forum held at Planta Hotel in Bacolod City on Wednesday participated by more than 100 member-representatives from different sectors . <p> " This is a in combating climate change , " Gonzales said . <p> The forum , also held in partnership with the Department of Trade and Industry-Negros Occidental , was participated by farmers from small barangays
