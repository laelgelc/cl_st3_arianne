<h> Friends and neighbors <p> Because nobody knows a country better than the young , 20 Taiwan university students are on a one-week visit to the Philippines ( August 29-September 4 ) under the International Youth Ambassadors Program , now on its 9th year ( since 2009 ) , one hundred Youth Ambassadors are visiting 17 cities in 16 countries in the Asia-Pacific , North America , Latin America , Europe , Africa , and the Middle East . <p> Yesterday at the University of Santo Tomas , Taiwan 's de facto envoy Dr. Gary Son-Huann Lin welcomed the youth ambassadors led by Minister OdiShri-ren Sun . In a one-and-half program in six acts , " Dynamic Youth , Friendly Taiwan " gave a snapshot of scenic Taiwan , timeline of its economic and democratic transition , culture and arts , history ; indigenous traditions ( one showing marked similarity to our Banaue ) ; Chinese opera , martial arts , tai chi , tea ceremony , calligraphy ; cultural creativity ( energetic dance performance depicting achievements in sports and high-tech ) ; and culminating in the release @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( Onscreen : Taiwan youth in humanitarian assistance abroad , and images of Taiwan 's culture ) . <p> While projecting Taiwan 's soft power abroad , it also broadens the youth participants ' orientation in international affairs . The Taiwan Economic and Cultural Office ( TECO ) arranged a program for the youth delegation to visit the Philippine national and local politicians , academes , major dailies , Rotary Club members , and historical and cultural institutions for a cross-pollination experience . " Most of all , the human touch , to know each other better and widen and deepen genuine friendship , " Dr Lin added . <p> For the Philippines , the visit is especially timely with the launch of the outreach program of newly elected President Tsai-Ing-wen The " New Southbound Policy " seeks wide-ranging two-way and multi-dimensional collaborations and partnerships at all levels between Taiwan and ASEAN .
