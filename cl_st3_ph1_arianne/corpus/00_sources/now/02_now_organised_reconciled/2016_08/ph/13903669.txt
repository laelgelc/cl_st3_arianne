<p> The Infanta-Real Watershed Forest Reserve is fast regaining its natural beauty 10 years after the 2004 landslide . <p> CALAMBA CITY , Laguna -- Mayor Jun Ynares of Antipolo City thanked the PTT Philippines Corp . ( PTTPC ) , a Thai oil firm based in the country , for supporting the Sibol Re-greening Marikina Watershed Project in a 100-hectare site at the sub-village Ysiro , San Jose Village , Antipolo City . <p> The city 's information office informed the Philippines News Agency ( PNA ) today on the compliment and expression of gratitude of Mayor Ynares to those , including PTTPC who supported the programs of the local government in preserving the environment and mitigating the effects of climate change . <p> It added that the mayor 's office is open to non-government organizations ( NGOs ) and private sectors promoting the preservation of the environment . <p> " We thank the PTTPC for their support . Our office is always open to NGOs and the private sectors promoting programs that take care our Mother Nature , " Ynares said in Filipino . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ changes of weather conditions due to climate change , rest assured our city government will not stop to provide support and assistance to projects that could help reduce global warming , " he added . <p> The Sibol Re-greening Marikina Watershed Project enjoins everyone especially the youth to protect the Marikina Watershed through tree planting and prevent flooding and landslides shallowing Laguna de Bay as a result . <p> PTTPC as part of its Corporate Social Responsibility ( CSR ) provided fruit bearing trees for the natives to plant and to maintain as source of their livelihood . <p> For the third time , PTTPC last August 12 provided 50,000 fruit bearing trees planted at Sitio Ysiro , San Jose Village joined by personnel from the Department of Education ( DepEd ) -Antipolo , Rizal Provincial Government , City Environment and Waste Management Office ( CEWMO ) , Philippine National Police ( PNP ) , Laguna Lake Development Authority ( LLDA ) , diplomatic communities and PTTPC volunteers .
