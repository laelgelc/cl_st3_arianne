<h> Tabuk City farmer is 2016 Nat'l . Gawad Saka finalist <p> TABUK CITY , Kalinga , Aug. 25 ( PIA ) -- A rice farmer from this city has qualified to the 2016 search for National Gawad Saka Awards . <p> Honorio Clemencia of Barangay Balong is vying for the Most Outstanding Integrated Rice-based Farmer in the Gawad Saka Awards of the Department of Agriculture ( DA ) for the year . <p> City Agriculturist Julibert Aquino informed that Clemencia cultivates a two-hectare riceland in their barangay which he integrates with the tropical fruits like mango , lanzones , lime and rambutan . He also raises pigs , ducks and fish . <p> Through his integrated farming , Clemencia is able to provide for the education of his five children , with three already finished with the college education and are now employed . <p> In 2012 , Teodoro Kub-ao of Barangay Bulo , here , won the same award .
