<h> PSA pushes for responsive and efficient statistical system <p> August 10 , 2016 <p> CARLITO C. DAR <p> BAGUIO CITY , Aug. 10 ( PIA ) - - The Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) is now pushing for a responsive , effective and efficient statistical system that will help the country achieve its commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals primarily aimed to end extreme poverty , fight inequality and injustice as well as to fix the concern on climate change . <p> PSA officials shared this to Cordillera stakeholders during the regional dissemination forum on the Philippine Statistical Development Plan ( 2011 to 2017 ) Update and the Sustainable Development Goals or SDGs on August 8 in this city . <p> The SDGs , which was signed by 193 UN member countries , is the next global development plan that follows the Millennium Development Goals ( MGDs ) that ended in 2015 . It is a 15 year plan with 17 goals , 169 targets and 230 indicators that focus on poverty , hunger , health , quality education , gender equality , clean water @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ growth , innovation and infrastructure , reduced inequalities , sustainable communities , responsible consumption , protection of the planet and life on land and water , peace and justice , and more importantly global partnership for the attainment of the goals . <p> National Statistician and Civil Registrar General Lisa Grace Bersales , in her keynote message , called call for a stronger partnership in the development , generation and dissemination of the needed statistics and indicators that will provide high -- quality , evidence - based policies plans and decisions . <p> With the country 's experience in the attainment of the MDGs where the country failed to achieve the given target in some areas , stronger partnership between the government , private sector and civil society organizations/groups as well as for more active participation of all other stakeholders are needed in bringing in relevant , timely , disaggregated and better quality statistics , primarily for the achievement of the SDGs . <p> " Through this forum , I hope that we can formulate more effective and efficient policies that will steer us towards the equitable development in our country @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the development of statistics on our hands . Let us spread further the significance of statistics and its value on the progress and change that we are always aiming , Bersales said . <p> PSA affirmed the need to improve admin-based data , optimal use of information technology , more technical and financial support in monitoring the SDG indicators and the need for more advocacies on the use of SDG indicators for evidence-based decision making , among others . <p> During the regional dissemination forum , PSA Asst . Nat'l Statistician Candido Astrologo Jr . provided the highlights and updates on PSDP ; Gean Cefidoza gave a regionalize outlook of the SDG ; Lotis Ira gave a briefing on the formulation of a localized SDP and core regional indicators while Bernadette Balamban gave an overview on SDG and other updates . <p> Said forum is part of the on- going commitment of PSA to achieve their vision of solid , responsive and world class authority on quality statistics and civil registration .
