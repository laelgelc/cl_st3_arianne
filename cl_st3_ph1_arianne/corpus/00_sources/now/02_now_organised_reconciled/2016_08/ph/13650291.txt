Padilla submits formal complaint vs. mining firm to Lopez <p> BAYOMBONG , Nueva Vizcaya , August 2 ( PIA ) -- Governor Carlos Padilla here has formally submitted the complaint of mining-affected villagers and anti-mining groups in the province in a bid to block the exploration expansion of OceanaGold Philippines , Inc . ( OGPI ) . <p> OGPI is currently conducting expansion of its exploration for gold-copper deposits in barangay Didipio in Kasibu town after it earned a permit early this year from the Aquino administration . <p> In the complaint , Padilla has called on the withdrawal and suspension of OGPI 's exploration permit which he added was considered a ' midnight deal ' . <p> He said Nueva Vizcaya is a critical watershed of major dams such as the Magat , San Roque , Pantabangan , Casecnan and Ambuklao dams which irrigate thousands of agricultural lands in Cagayan Valley and region 3 aside from the power they generate . <p> " We are a watershed haven and we promote the development of our province as an agroforestry area and eco-tourism because of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ natural wonders , " Padilla said . <p> He added that within the Financial and Technical Assistance Agreement ( FTAA ) of OGPI lies the Capissaan Cave system , considered as the fourth longest cave system in the country including the vast plantations of citrus and high value crops among indigenous people . <p> Padilla said most of the communities covered by the on-going exploration is being opposed through the establishment of barricades by anti-mining groups and villagers . <p> " They oppose mining because it would disrupt their way of life , destroy the environment and source of income , it will provide suffering , social divisiveness and negative peace and order condition , " he said . <p> Padilla also said that the Provincial Development and Physical Framework Plan(PDPFP) and Comprehensive Land Use Plan ( CLUP ) did not prioritized mining in its development and the province is prone to major disaster and is a priority key biodiversity conservation research area being part of the Caraballo-Palali Mountain Ranges . <p> " Mining will only provide suffering to the people and it must not be destructive to the environment as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he said .
