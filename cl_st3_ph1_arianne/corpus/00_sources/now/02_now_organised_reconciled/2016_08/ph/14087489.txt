<h> Southern Leyte aims to be E. Visayas ' greenest province <p> Maasin City , Southern Leyte -- Southern Leyte could become the greenest province in Eastern Visayas because of its massive tree planting campaign . <p> Southern Leyte Rep. Roger G. Mercado said close to six million trees have been planted in various parts of Southern Leyte and more are still being planted . <p> Southern leyte Map ( Courtesy of Google Maps ) <p> Mercado said on Monday 10,000 seedlings were planted in Barangay Laboon , the birthplace of President Rodrigo Duterte , as part of a reforestation drive . <p> " We have to be known as the greenest province in Eastern Visayas as we have planted almost six million trees and more are being planted . We have to care for our mother earth by planting trees and this helps fight global warming and climate change , " Mercado told Manila Bulletin . <p> He said they started planting trees in Southern Leyte in 1987 . <p> Joined the tree planting in the mountain of Laboon were Mrs. Luz Mercado , Southern Leyte Governor @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ officials , provincial officials , and officials and employees of various national government agencies , students , women 's groups and civic organizations . <p> Mercado , who marked his 65th birthday , said trees were always planted in Maasin during his birthday and other celebrations of the city and provincial governments . <p> He said that after Southern Leyte was struck by the deadly Guinsaugon landslide and Typhoon Yolanda , the residents have grown more concerned about the environment .
