<h> Opinion <h> How to beat bad air days <h> M. A. P. Insights By Chit U. Juan <h> For Health and Wellness , what we can not live without is air . Clean air . We can last a few days without food , even without water , but can not last an hour without air . But is our air breathable ? <p> I remember playing golf many years ago in Fort Bonifacio and right around the 13th hole , you could see Ayala Avenue and the smog that gives it a brownish orange color , which you know meant bad air being trapped in our atmosphere . And bad air that everyone else on Ayala Avenue was breathing . That must have been 1995 . <p> Fast forward to 2016 and my nephew Raffy tells me he sees the same situation from Forest Hills in Antipolo -- the whole cities of Makati and Mandaluyong covered in a thick dark smog that just lingers . <p> I never thought I would develop asthma but now almost everyone I know has adult-acquired asthma . Apparently , not very many people do . But there are laws for this -- like the Clean Air Act authored by entrepreneur , now real estate magnate , Senator Manny Villar . Is it being implemented properly ? If so , we should be enjoying cleaner air by now . <p> Old automobiles Dated inefficient diesel , and even gasoline , engines which are older than 10 years are made with less efficient engines that also have bad emissions . But why are we still able to drive them around ? Because all it takes is a smog test , a few hundred pesos and they are off running on the streets again . <p> Cheap used imports From what I gathered , about 20,000 used vehicles enter our ports every year and are able to get registration even if they are second hand or used and maybe not efficient .
