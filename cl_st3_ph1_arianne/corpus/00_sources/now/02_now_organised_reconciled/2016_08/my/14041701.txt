" It 's a massive opportunity to start getting ' Laudato Si " deeply imbedded in our Catholic mindset and the life of the Catholic family . " <p> The day opens what numerous Christian communities are calling the Season of Creation that runs through the feast of St. Francis of Assisi Oct. 4 .
