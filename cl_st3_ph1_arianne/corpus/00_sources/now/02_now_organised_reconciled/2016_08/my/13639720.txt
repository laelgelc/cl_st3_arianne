<p> KLANG : The 45-day National Scientific Cruise Expedition in the South China Sea costing almost RM1 million will provide crucial information such as research data and analysis on the impact of El-Nino on marine life . <p> Science , Technology and Innovation Minister Datuk Seri Madius Tangau said the data will be used specifically to build an ocean model as an adaptation to climate change and identify marine resources , flora and fauna , as well as rocks and sediments . <p> " This is the second expedition and from the first expedition in 2009 , four new species of marine fish from among 213 species were recorded , " he said at the launch of the expedition here yesterday . <p> Madius said the data is also important to studies on various species of seaweeds and macro algae , which opens the door to new research and development in the pharmaceutical aquaculture sectors . <p> " The information provided would also help the government to come up with decisions and plans , especially on the management of fishery , coral reefs , food security , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " he said . <p> Madius also noted that this expedition will empower local scientists in competing with other maritime countries . <p> " All the discoveries and outcome from the expedition can also help awareness programmes on the importance of preserving the sea ecosystem . <p> " All the data and analysis will be deposited into the Malaysia National Oceanographic Data Centre , under the Science , Technology and Innovation Ministry . It is a hub for marine science and oceanography data , " he said .
