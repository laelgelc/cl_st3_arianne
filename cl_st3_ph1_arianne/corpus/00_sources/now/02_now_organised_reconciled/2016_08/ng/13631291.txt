This is the opportunity that the Paris Agreement represents , " Beyai said . <p> He gave assurance of " UNDP 's continuing commitment to partnering the government of Nigeria not only on the implementation of its climate change agenda but also in its broader aspiration of improving the quality of life of Nigerians and the delivering the dividends of democracy to Nigerians .
