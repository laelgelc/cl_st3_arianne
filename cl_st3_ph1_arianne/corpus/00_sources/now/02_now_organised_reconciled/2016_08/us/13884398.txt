<h> Study finds that views of swing voters do not matter much to presidential candidates <h> August 17 , 2016 <p> Despite the familiar belief that candidates must appeal to the " moderate middle " of the voting public in order to win elections , U.S. presidential candidates routinely take less-than moderate positions on a variety of issues . Are they catering to the extreme views of their respective core supporters ? A new study out today finds that Republican presidential candidates are generally more responsive to the views of their base voters than Democratic candidates , but neither party 's candidates are more than minimally responsive to the preferences of the swing voters whose views define the center of the political spectrum . The study was published as part of a special issue of The ANNALS of the American Academy of Political and Social Science ( a journal from SAGE Publishing ) titled " Elections in America .
