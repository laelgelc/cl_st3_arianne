In the recent drought , farmers like Nguyen received no early warning that could have avoided losses . <p> The recently approved Mekong Delta Integrated Climate Resilience and Sustainable Livelihoods Project , backed by a $310 million IDA credit to Vietnam , will help upgrade the monitoring , analyzing and sharing of information at the level of the Mekong Delta to support decision-making under uncertainty .
