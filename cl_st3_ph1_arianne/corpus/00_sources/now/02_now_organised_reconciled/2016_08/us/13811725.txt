Scott Applewhite , File ) <p> HONOLULU ( AP ) -- U.S. Sen. Brian Schatz is seeking his first full term in the Senate after being appointed in 2012 , and he starts that journey in Saturday 's primary . <p> Hawaii 's former governor chose Schatz to fill the seat that became vacant with the death of Sen. Daniel Inouye . Schatz was then elected to fill out the remainder of Inouye 's unexpired term in 2014 . <p> Schatz faces four Democrats in the primary and is widely expected to advance to the general election . Four Republicans are also campaigning for the nomination . <p> The other Democratic candidates are Makani Christensen , Tutz Honeychurch , Artuto Reyes and Miles Shiratori . The Republican candidates are John Carroll , Karla Gottschalk , Eddie Pirkowski and John Roco . Libertarian Michael Koloski also put his name in for the seat . <p> Schatz had an unlikely road to the Senate . When Inouye died in December 2012 , it was left to then-Gov . Neil Abercrombie to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have U.S. Rep. Colleen Hanabusa take the job , but Abercrombie instead chose Schatz , his lieutenant governor . <p> Hanabusa challenged but lost to Schatz two years later when a special election was held for someone to fill the last two years of Inouye 's unexpired term . <p> Schatz serves on the Appropriations , Commerce Science and Transportation and Indian Affairs committees . He also serves on the Select Committee on Ethics . <p> Schatz has introduced legislation on reducing the effects of climate change and promoting clean energy . Hawaii leads the nation in its initiatives to become completely energy independent by 2045 . <p> Schatz , who was raised in Hawaii , was a member of the Hawaii House from 1998 to 2006 . He is married and has two children .
