<h> Satellite images reveal full extent of destruction following Italy 's earthquake <h> August 31 , 2016 <p> Over the weekend , the European Commission has published accurate maps of the damage caused by the catastrophic earthquake that hit central Italy on 24 August . The JRC is responsible for the technical coordination of the EMS . <p> The service can be activated in case of an emergency .
