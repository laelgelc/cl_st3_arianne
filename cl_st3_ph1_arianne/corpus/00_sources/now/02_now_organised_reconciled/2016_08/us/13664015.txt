The energy of the irradiation causes an abrupt expansion of the target 's molecules and the inertia of this expansion generates a shock wave . The energy from the shock wave can create heat and light that melts and reflects off of the forsterite . <p> Previous studies without the laser shock technique only measured the properties of forsterite at shock pressures below 200 Giga Pascals ( GPa ) . The new experiments put forsterite crystals under pressures between approximately 250 and 970 GPa . For comparison , the pressure at the center of Earth is estimated to be 360 GPa . <p> Researchers measured the pressure , temperature , density , and reflectivity of laser-shocked forsterite .
