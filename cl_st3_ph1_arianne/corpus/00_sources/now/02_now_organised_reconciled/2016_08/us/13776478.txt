<h> Time Running Short For Extension Of California Climate Law <p> ( AP ) -- With three weeks left in **30;1231;TOOLONG , Gov . Jerry Brown and other advocates of extending the state 's ambitious climate change efforts are struggling to persuade enough lawmakers to go along . <p> Environmentalists want to protect the litany of emissions-targeting programs before their legal authorization comes into doubt in 2020 , but they face stiff opposition from oil companies , Republicans and moderate Democrats . <p> Brown wants to extend and strengthen a 2006 law known as AB32 , which set a goal of reducing greenhouse-gas emissions to 1990 levels by 2020 . <p> In a sense of growing frustration and apparent pessimism , officials in the governor 's office and Legislature last week lowered expectations for imminent progress .
