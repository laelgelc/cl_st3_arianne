<p> Justin Trudeau 's Liberal government has enjoyed a relatively stress-free honeymoon since winning office last October . The feel-good vibe is threatened , however , by revelations of wasteful spending by two cabinet ministers , a practise that has the same effect on political honeymoons as food poisoning has on the real variety . <p> Health Minister Jane Philpott 's hiring of a high-end car service owned by a Liberal supporter is being examined by ethics commissioner Mary Dawson , who will focus on the perception of preferential treatment . Company owner Reza Shirani , who canvassed for Philpott during the last election , has admitted he was in the driver 's seat on July 12 , when the cabinet minister billed taxpayers $1,994 for transportation during a speaking engagement at the Assembly of First Nations ' annual meeting in Niagara Falls , Ont . <p> Philpott -- who billed the public $1,700 for car service on a different day and $520 for use of Air Canada lounges during her travels -- has admitted these were " excessive costs " and has promised to reimburse taxpayers . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ontario home to Toronto 's Pearson International Airport totalling $3,815 . <p> " The costs were too high , " said Philpott .
