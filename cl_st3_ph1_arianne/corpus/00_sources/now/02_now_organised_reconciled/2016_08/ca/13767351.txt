( Mary Altaffer / AP FILE PHOTO ) <p> Actor-environmental activist Leonardo DiCaprio 's documentary call to save an imperiled planet , a hotly anticipated sequel to a horror classic and movies about musicians from The Beatles to The Stooges are among the latest titles announced for the 41st Toronto International Film Festival . <p> TIFF unveiled the movies in popular genre series Midnight Madness , TIFF Docs and the boundary-pushing Vanguard program , along with cinema classics worth a second look on the Cinematheque bill and another 41 short films from around the world on Tuesday . <p> TIFF runs from Sept. 8 to 18 . <p> " Revelations abound in this year 's crop of documentaries , " said TIFF Docs programmer Thom Powers , pointing to works @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Negro ) and a re-examination of student Amanda Knox 's two murder trials ( Amanda Knox ) . <p> In addition to DiCaprio and Fisher Stevens 's The Turning Point , the doc bill includes the premiere of Matt Tyrnauer 's Citizen Jane : Battle for the City , marking what would have been the 100th birthday of brilliant writer , urbanist and long-time Toronto resident , Jane Jacobs . Also premiering is Canadian director Richie Mehta 's India in a Day , the result of an open invitation to people across India to video moments in their lives during the same 24-hour period . <p> Among the offerings on the Cinematheque program is a 30th-anniversary screening of Jonathan Demme 's Something Wild ; western One-Eyed Jacks , the only film directed by Marlon Brando ; and a 10th-anniversary screening of Guillermo del Toro 's Pan 's Labyrinth.
