It centres on the fear that a spill could contaminate the drinking water in the Montreal area . <p> That 's why those wondering about the future routes for exporting Alberta oil should cast an eye to the politics around a river crossing somewhere near Pointe-Fortune , Que . <p> Though National Energy Board hearings on Energy East open Monday in Saint John and move into Quebec by the end of the month , we still do n't know exactly where that crossing , the likely political choke point for the proposed pipeline , will be .
