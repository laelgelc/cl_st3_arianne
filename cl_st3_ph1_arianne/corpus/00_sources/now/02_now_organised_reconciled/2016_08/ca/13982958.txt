<h> U of A officials visit the Hat <h> Poll <p> The connections between University of Alberta and southern Alberta were highlighted as officials from the Edmonton-based post-secondary institution toured Medicine Hat Monday and met with local representatives . <p> " This is very much a place that matters to the University of Alberta , " said U of A president and vice-chancellor David Turpin . <p> " The University of Alberta is the university for all people of Alberta , " he said , and something everyone should be " extremely proud of . " <p> Meeting with the school divisions included talking about teacher education and what advice needs to be given to high school students , said Turpin , while the topic with local politicians was on how the university could work with the city on various sustainable and alternative power projects that are underway here . <p> Meetings with MHC often centre on how the two post-secondary institutions can more effectively collaborate -- MHC has numerous areas of study which students can start in , and then transfer to the U of A to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in this region . <p> " Water management is critical , " said Turpin , not just for drinking but industrial and agricultural use . U of A research in the region is looking at the influence of climate change on water , modelling surface flows and groundwater , and long-term sustainability of hydro sources . <p> As well , 25 kilometres north of Duchess , Alta. , is the U of A Rangeland Research Institute , studying areas like improving crop yields , grasslands management and cattle health . <p> Overall , there are 180 students from Medicine Hat enrolled in the university , and 50 offers have been accepted so students from MHC can transfer to the university . There 's also 1,200 alumni from Medicine Hat . <p> " I think one of the things we 're seeing more and more of is more students coming to the University of Alberta , " said Turpin .
