<h> New federal funding secured for restoration of North Delta 's Cougar Creek <p> Ongoing efforts to clean up and restore salmon habitat within Cougar Creek are getting a boost thanks to an influx of cash from the federal government . <p> On Aug. 15 the Corporation of Delta announced the municipality had received $70,000 to fund the rehabilitation of the salmon-bearing creek . <p> The money was distributed from the Federal Environmental Damages Fund , a Government of Canada program , administered by Environment and Climate Change Canada , that ensures that court-awarded fines are used for projects with positive environmental impacts . <p> " This funding provides Delta with the opportunity to make substantial reparations to an iconic waterway in the North Delta community , " said Delta Mayor Lois Jackson . " Improving fish habitat in Cougar Creek demonstrates our prioritization of preserving and enhancing our natural areas .
