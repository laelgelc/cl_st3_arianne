<p> The use of irrigation in Ethiopia is growing . Ministry of Farming and Natural Resource Irrigation Drainage Researcher , Tilahun Hordofa , said that as irrigation could assist in withstanding drought and food insecurity caused by climate change and related disasters , the development of irrigation has become the prominent policy direction of the government . <p> Ministry Irrigation and Drainage Mechanic Amerga Kersie , for his part said , though irrigation is mainly used to harvest maize , onion , tomato , green pepper , salad , and lettuce , it could also be used to harvest fruit . <p> Amerga also said that by giving prime attention to irrigation , the government has been working aggressively to produce fruit and major crops , to enable the sector to get out of dependency on rain water . In doing so , several irrigated farm lands are becoming glutted with water due to a lack of adequate understanding among farmers , he added . <p> This could bring negative consequences , such as the increment of water scarcity , the drying up of crops , the growth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ destruction of fertile lands , among others . <p> Oromia Irrigation Development Authority Vegetables and Fruits Irrigation Professional , Roman Negeri , said that the state administration has been working on providing practical training to farmers along with continuous support and follow up to bring radical change in irrigation schemes . <p> According to the Ministry of Water , Irrigation and Electric , about 5.3 million hectares of land could be developed through irrigation . Hence , the exploitation of such an untapped resource , if done effectively , would increase production in both quantity and quality . <p> So , proper implementation of irrigation schemes would help to withstand natural phenomena and ensure food security at household and national level as well .
