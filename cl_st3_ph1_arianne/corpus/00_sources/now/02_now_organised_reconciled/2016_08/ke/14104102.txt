<p> The potato is an important crop for Latin America and especially for South America , where it originated . Genetically improving potatoes can be a way to produce varieties that are resistant to pests and climate change , said Arnulfo Gutierrez , an expert on potatoes and a Panamanian official . <p> There are no modified potato varieties in the region yet , said the specialist who chaired the organising committee of the XXVII Congress of the Latin American Potato Association ( ALAP ) , which was held this week in Panama City . <p> Gutierrez said genetically modified potatoes were a major advance from the scientific point of view , but stressed that societies would only be able to profit from these developments after having accepted these products and the technology which produces them . <p> " If the society does not accept this product we 'll definitely have to find another way of improving them , " he said . <p> The researcher from the Institute of Agricultural Research of Panama ( IDIAP ) recognised that transgenic potato cultivation was a very controversial issue because @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment , human health , and biodiversity .
