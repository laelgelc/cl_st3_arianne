And the supreme law is poised to shape the legacy of President Uhuru Kenyatta after next year 's General Election . <p> In his ambition to " make Kenya great " among world nations , Kenyatta will have to hue his legacy against two extremes . On the one end , he has to dismantle the colonial legacy that divided the country into high-potential and low-potential areas . Indeed , the failure of the post-colonial state to reverse this historical injustice stoked a slew of grievances that segments of the political class whipped to ignite the 2008 post-election violence . <p> On the other extreme , Kenyatta has to build his legacy in a 21st century world where the dominant capitalist system on which the new Constitution is embedded is going through one of its worst crises . It proves that the Constitution 's " county magic " is manifestly the sharpest tool of rolling back the colonial legacy of underdevelopment . <p> COUNTY GOVERNMENTS <p> Since 2013 , the Jubilee Government has implemented 47 county governments , ushering in Kenya 's homegrown " Marshall Plan " . From $210 million allocated in 2013 , funds devolved to the counties have increased to $280 million in 2016 . Moyale has a referral hospital , the largest in the region , serving Kenyans as well as Ethiopians . In June , Marsabit County had its first-ever caesarean section ! <p> But a winner-takes-all logic in the Constitution can be a recipe for perpetual instability . But the lesson is clear : building trust , and improving conditions for shared development are key to peace and sustainable development . <p> At the conceptual level , Kenya has to balance between the imperatives of capitalism and democracy in order to create a shared wealth and prosperity . In a sense , Kenya 's 2010 Constitution signifies the inherent tension between markets and democratic politics . <p> THROUGH VOTE <p> While capitalism allocates resources through markets , democracy allocates power through the vote .
