<h> Water restrictions imposed on Mpumalanga villages <h> Tags : <p> Water restrictions are to be imposed on villages in and around the Nkomazi Municipality in Mpumalanga . <p> The two main rivers that supply the municipality are drying up . It is so dry water engines have been pumping mud . <p> There are critically low water levels . <p> The Nkomazi and Crocodile rivers have to be above 60% full to ensure supply . <p> If the rain does not come in the next few days 54 villages and four towns will be without water . <p> " Water shortages are a problem especially because of the drought and you can not blame anyone . It is concerning , " says a resident . <p> Another resident adds : " The water supply is sometimes cut and when water comes back it is muddy due to low levels because of the drought ; we can not even water our gardens . " <p> Water tankers have been dispatched and residents are urged to use water sparingly . <p> Nkomazi Municipality spokesperson Cyril Ripinga says , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for other villages to have water as soon as this week . We appeal to residents to bear with us as our water sources are dry . We are calling on residents to use water with caution . " <p> The water woes are not unique to Nkomazi , some provinces face the same challenge among them , Gauteng . <p> Speaking to SABC NEWS from a Water conference in Sweden - the Water and Sanitation Minister is adamant water restrictions in Gauteng will not be lifted . <p> Water and Sanitation Minister Nomvula Mokonyane says , " Particularly in agriculture we need to find alternative crops to plant as we have missed harvest due to drought . If you pray , we need a miracle . It is time to adapt to the climate change . " <p> The shortage has been aggravated by poor maintenance . Residents are urged to report leaks .
