The first was a political crossroads where the choice was between a genuine democracy offering universal franchise versus the co-option of tame representatives into an artificially constructed parliament . The right road was taken . But then a second crossroads might well be encountered some twenty years later where the choice was between growing an inclusive free-market economy to go alongside the political democracy ; or undoing all the political progress made during the transition on account of failure of economic growth .
