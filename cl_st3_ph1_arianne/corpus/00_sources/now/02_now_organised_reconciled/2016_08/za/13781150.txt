<p> Today , across the globe , wildlife enthusiasts and conservationists are celebrating World Lion Day , 10 August , but it is also an international day of mourning as we reflect over the loss and plight of Africa 's iconic land predator . <p> The strength and bravery of our big cats is deeply symbolic of the courage of the human heart and strength of the human spirit . We see mirror images of ourselves and social reality when we admire lions . Big cats remind us of the deep connection we have with nature . This perhaps explains why we treat issues affecting our big cats with so much passion . ( Remember Sylvester and Cecil ? ) . Encroachment on lions is an intrusion on humanity . <p> Not only are big cats scientifically important for ecosystems , but they are economic pillars . That is , while the importance of lions can not be overstressed for natural order , these animals are also sources of socio-economic wealth . Lions create jobs , and reduce unemployment and income inequality .
