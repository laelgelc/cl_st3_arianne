<h> Govt boosts research development in SA <p> The Square Kilometre Array will see SA require considerable data centre facilities and an increased supply of data scientists . <p> Over the next three years , the Department of Science and Technology ( DST ) will invest R60 million towards the establishment of a regional data centre and a national e-science teaching and training platform . <p> According to the DST , in partnership with the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research ( CSIR ) , it decided to establish the two cyber infrastructure projects . <p> The initiatives are aimed at addressing the growing demand for big data facilities in research and business , as well as boost the local universities that will take the lead in these initiatives , says the department . <p> The DST also notes the investment will further the implementation of the National Integrated Cyberinfrastructure System ( NICIS ) .
