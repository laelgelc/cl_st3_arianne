industry , ministry of power and animal welfare board of India as parties in the case . <p> The matter is likely to be listed before a bench headed by NGT Chairperson Justice Swatanter Kumar . <p> The petition said that ' manja ' poses a huge threat when it comes into contact with live overhead electric wires leading to grid failure . <p> " Due to manja being coated with glass , metals and other sharp material , these strings act as good conductors of electricity , increasing the probability of detached manja strings stuck in power lines electrocuting kite flyers and passersby coming into contact with such strings , " it said . <p> PETA claimed that minor children @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' manja ' which causes respiratory problems in them as they inhale harmful substances which are extremely detrimental to their health . <p> The plea has also referred to various orders including the November 2015 order of the Allahabad High Court which banned the use of Chinese manja in the entire State of Uttar Pradesh and sought ban on " manufacture , import , sale and use " of manja <p> Stay updated on the go with Times of India News App .
