<h> India ahead of 2012 climate change target , says Anil Dave <p> Source : The Hitavada Date : 21 Aug 2016 11:49:18 <p> Staff Reporter , <p> Union Minister for Environment Anil Madhav Dave said as far as climate change targets are concerned , India is ahead of targets of 2012 . He stressed that in India , development and environment will go in a parallel manner . <p> Anil Dave said above things while addressing a press conference in Bhopal on Saturday . Answering to a question about translocation of Gir lions to Madhya Pradesh , he said process is underway in this regard . He added that illegal mining shall be stopped and only limited mining shall be done in rivers . <p> He said recently concluded Monsoon Session of Parliament was good in many terms . More than 100 bills , including Goods and Services Tax ( GST ) and Compensatory Afforestation Management and Planning Authority ( CAMPA ) Bills , were passed . He also stressed that there is no animal as " Neelgai " rather it is a blue animal . Talking about his recently concluded ' Tiranga Yatra ' , he said he was overwhelmed by the response he had received and now he is planning couple of more Yatras . He assured that State is going to benefit through his ministry .
