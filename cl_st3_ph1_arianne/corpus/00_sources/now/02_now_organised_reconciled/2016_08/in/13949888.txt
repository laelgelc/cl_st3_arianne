<h> Urjit Patel 's energy connection <p> From power distribution reforms to the economics of climate change and the contentious energy pricing issues to the intricacies of upstream oil and gas production -- Raghuram Rajan 's lieutenant has dabbled in all <p> New Delhi : India 's newly appointed Reserve Bank of India ( RBI ) Governor Urjit R Patel is deft not just at monetary policy formulation and the larger economic policy reforms , the 52 year-old inflation fighter is an expert at handling energy sector 's issues too.Patel 's experience in the economic sphere boasts of an impressive connection with the country 's energy sector . <p> From power distribution reforms to the economics of climate change and the contentious energy pricing issues to the intricacies of upstream oil and gas production - Raghuram Rajan 's lieutenant has dabbled in all . <p> A product of the London School of Economics ( LSE ) , Patel was part of several high level committees between 2000 and 2004 in the previous National Democratic Alliance ( NDA ) regime including the Prime Minister 's Task Force on Infrastructure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) . <p> Prior to his appointment as the deputy governor at the RBI in January 2013 , Patel was advisor ( energy &amp; infrastructure ) at The Boston Consulting Group . His other assignments include , president ( business development ) at Reliance Industries ( RIL ) ; member of the Integrated Energy Policy Committee of the Government of India ; and member of the Board , Gujarat State Petroleum Corporation Ltd . <p> As part of the core team of experts selected to frame the country 's energy policy ( IEP ) in 2005 -- along with the likes of the then planning commission member Kirit Parikh , Administrative Staff College of India 's T L Sankar , and Department of Atomic Energy Secretary Anil Kakodkar - Patel played a key role in working out the blueprint of the policy that has guided India 's energy sector reforms over more than a decade . <p> The policy formulation turned around the performance of the energy sector through multiple ambitious reform initiatives including market-linked resource allocation and pricing of fossil fuel-led energy generation , introduction of competitive energy markets for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the role of independent regulators for tariff setting . The Modi government now plans to replace that policy docket with a new National Energy Policy being framed in the Niti Aayog . <p> As part of the expert group on State Electricity Boards ( SEBs ) in 2001 , Patel was part of the government 's maiden attempt to nail and correct the issues plaguing the power distribution sector reforms and prescribed the first-ever financial restructuring scheme for distribution utilities , then called One Time Settlement ( OTS ) . The scheme was later re-introduced in different forms as Financial Restructuring Package ( FRP ) by the UPA government and most recently as UDAY by the Modi government . <p> Among the most seminal works by Patel on India 's energy sector is a paper titled " The Power Sector in India : An Inquiry into the Efficacy of the Reform Process " , co-authored with Saugata Bhattacharya and published in 2007 . The paper highlighted for the first time the humongous dissimilarity and variation that existed between different state distribution utilities ' performance post the reforms of 2001 . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ how the 2001 reforms may not have worked as desired and how the situation on financial front for discoms might worsen again . One , despite five years of reforms , because of the resource crunch in the state-owned system to add capacity , the performance gains were only incremental and not enough to meet the soaring energy demand in future . <p> And two , the Accelerated Power Development and Reforms Programme ( APDRP ) , a pet reform initiative of the then UPA government , has " patently had limited success in attaining the objectives for which it was initially intended " . Not surprisingly , the centre had to announce a new discom restructuring scheme called Financial Restructuring Package or FRP in 2012.
