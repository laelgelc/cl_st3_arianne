This is the second time this year that the FAC has turned down the proposal of the AP government to convert forest land into concrete jungle . <p> The FAC , which deals with clearances for new projects , has asked the AP government to furnish details of the land use @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of greenery . Under environment norms , the AP government will have to take up compensatory social forestry on an equal extent of land . But the government failed to submit details of the project . <p> The government last year sought clearance for diversion of 13,267.12 hectares . It submitted a revised proposal on April 25 this year . The FAC took up the case on July 12 and turned down the proposal . The government has sought exemption from submission of land use pattern . However , the FAC insisted that submission of land use pattern was important for taking decision on whether or not to grant permission to take over the forest land . <p> Sources said the FAC wanted to know how much of the 13,000 and odd hectares of land would be utilised for the greenfield capital city of Amaravati . The government has proposed a number of development projects apart from Amaravati .
