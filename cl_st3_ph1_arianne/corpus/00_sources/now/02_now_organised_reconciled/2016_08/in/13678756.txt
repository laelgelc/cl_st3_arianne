Experts working in the water sector told HT that it was not enough to just conduct structural audits of the old , colonial bridges to ensure its safety . They said that hydrological study of river basins is a must for any new construction on the rivers as well as audit of old structures . <p> " An overall hydrological study of a river basin has to be carried out before planning new structures on the river like barrages and bridges .
