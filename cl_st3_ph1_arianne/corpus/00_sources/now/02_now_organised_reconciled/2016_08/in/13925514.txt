<h> A Quarter Of India 's Land Turning To Desert , Farmlands Shrink <p> In states like Jharkhand , Rajasthan , Delhi , Gujarat and Goa , more than 50 per cent of land is turning to desert . <p> Chennai : An analysis of satellite images have revealed that more than a quarter of Indian land is turning to desert and agricultural areas are shrinking . <p> A report from the Indian Space Research Organisation says the loss of land productivity is estimated at 96 million hectares , or nearly 30 per cent of land . <p> Analysis of satellite mapping shows new areas in Jammu and Kashmir , Orissa and Jharkhand turning arid with nine states together accounting for nearly 24 per cent of land turning to desert . <p> In states like Jharkhand , Rajasthan , Delhi , Gujarat and Goa , more than 50 per cent of land is turning to desert . <p> " Population pressure has resulted in over exploitation of land for cultivation , grazing , water resources and deforestation leading to degradation of drylands , " Minsiter of State Jitendra @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ country we should be more than alarmed by this data , " said S Janakarajan , chairman of the South Asia Consortium for Inter-disciplinary Water Resources Studies . <p> " There is no plan to reverse this process or its impact , " he added . <p> The ongoing study , initiated by the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , was led by the Indian Space Research Organisation and involved 19 other institutes . <p> It analysed satellite images over an eight year period to compile the atlas .
