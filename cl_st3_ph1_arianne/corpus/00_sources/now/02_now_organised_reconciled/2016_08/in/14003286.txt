<h> BRICS nations to set up disaster management task force <p> A two-day meeting of BRICS Parliamentarians on disaster management culminated here on Tuesday with the adoption of Udaipur Declaration . <p> TNN Aug 24 , 2016 , 02.32 PM IST <p> UDAIPUR : A two-day meeting of BRICS Parliamentarians on disaster management culminated here on Tuesday with the adoption of Udaipur Declaration . The meeting laid bare the common thread of challenges on disaster issues faced by all the BRICS nations . <p> The member nations agreed to move away from relief-centric to a holistic approach to disasters with a greater emphasis on prevention , mitigation and preparedness . This highlights the importance of forecasting and early warning systems to help authorities in alerting the communities as well as responding swiftly to a situation . <p> The meeting discussed the advances in technology and knowledge sharing amongst member countries to enable them leverage it to reduce disaster risk . At the conclusion ceremony , minister of state , home affairs , Kiren Rijiju , termed the meeting as a new milestone in collaboration and cooperation among BRICS @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The Udaipur meeting has successfully adopted the Udaipur Declaration whereby we have resolved to set up a dedicated joint task force for disaster risk management for regular dialogue , exchange , mutual support and collaboration among BRICS countries , " Rijiju said . <p> The roadmap for implementation of three-year joint action plan ( JAP ) for BRICS emergency services ( 2016-18 ) was also finalized . The JAP was agreed upon at the first meeting of BRICS ministers for disaster management at St. Petersburg in Russia earlier this year . <p> " This meeting has agreed on a road map for implementation of the joint action plan where all of us have resolved to work together on exchange of information/ experiences on disaster management , research &amp; technology exchange on forecasting and early warning for floods and extreme weather events and capacity building of stakeholders for disaster management , " Rijiju said . <p> A technical session on disaster risk in a changing climate was held on Tuesday . The session focused on emerging disaster risks as a result of climate change and evolving practices in the area of disaster @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ discussions on the implications of changing climate on disaster risk . <p> Summing up the takeaways from the meeting , R K Jain , member , National Disaster Management Authority , said , " It came out clearly during the meeting that all member countries face similar challenges . Mainstreaming of disaster risk reduction , advance use of technology in providing early warning , need for adequate funding to deal with rehabilitation and reconstruction after a disaster and changing the impact of climate on disasters are common challenges faced by all of us . <p> The deliberations have been very useful and we got an opportunity to learn about the disaster management structure , system and processes followed in other BRICS countries .
