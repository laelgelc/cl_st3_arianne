<p> Plan to replace forests over thousands of acres in Kodagu , Shivamogga and Chikkamagaluru districts with plantations is a ticking time bomb ; it will lead to reduced inflow into rivers that feed Bengaluru and Mysuru , say experts <p> It was predicted that , this year , the state would witness a bumper monsoon . Following years of drought , everyone was pinning their hopes on this year 's rains to wash away the state 's agrarian and drinking water woes . <p> However , to everyone 's surprise , this year 's monsoon has been dismal , at least as far as reservoirs and hydel power are concerned . Major reservoirs like KRS and Linganamakki are still far from brimming , and with only a couple of months of monsoon left , the rest of the year could well be arid . So what 's got wrong with the weather ? <p> The Western Ghats are catchment regions for two major rivers , Cauvery and Sharavathi : Both supply nearly half of the state 's population .
