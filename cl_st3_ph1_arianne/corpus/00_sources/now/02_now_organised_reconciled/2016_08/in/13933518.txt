<h> BRICS delegates to mull over devpt goals in Jaipur <p> Jaipur : Women parliamentarians from Brazil , Russia , India , China and South Africa ( BRICS ) arrived in Jaipur on Friday , ahead of a two-day meet on sustainable development goals . <p> The first delegation of parliamentarians arrived in the morning and was received by Rajpal Singh Shekhawat , minister for urban development and housing . Russia is represented by a group of six while Brazil has five members . From India , more than 25 women MPs will be attending the event . <p> The main event would kick off on August 20 at the legislative assembly with an inaugural ceremony . The delegates would be welcomed by Lok Sabha Speaker Sumitra Mahajan . The event will also be attended by chief minister Vasundhara Raje and speaker of Rajasthan assembly Kailash Meghwal . <p> After group photo session and address of the dignitaries , a plenary session on ' Perspective on implementation of sustainable development goals ' will be held at the assembly . The annual conference of BRICS is to be held in Goa in October this year .
