<h> CEE to host International ESD conference for educators in September <p> AHMEDABAD : City-based Centre of Environment Education ( CEE ) would host international conference for transforming education for children and youth from September 16 to 18 as part of UNESCO 's Education for Sustainable Development ( ESD ) goal . <p> " ESD has got a wide recognition during ' the Decade of Education for Sustainable Development ( DESD ) , ' generating rich experiences of transforming education for children and youth . The Global Action Programme ( GAP ) , an outcome from the DESD has a large emphasis on children and youth as key drivers for change . The conference at CEE that was held in January this year in cooperation with UNESCO , UNEP and the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , Government of India , highlighted the role of education in achieving the Sustainable Development Goals , bringing together the achievements and learnings from the DESD , " said a CEE official . <p> The conference would discuss the outcomes of Goal 4 from the ESDG Conference specifically focusing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pedagogy and curriculum in both formal and non-formal settings to engage children and youth .
