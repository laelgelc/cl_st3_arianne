<h> Columns <h> The inherent dangers of political expediency driving economic policy <p> On April 22 , 2016 , Jamaica joined with 170 other countries and affixed its signature to the Paris Accord on Climate Change , which in simple terms represents a commitment by the signatories to reduce emissions as part of the method for reducing greenhouse gases . In signing the 12-page agreement , the members promised to reduce their carbon output " as soon as possible " and to do their best to keep global warming " to well below 2 degrees C " ( 3.6 degrees F ) . <p> Jamaica 's signing of this treaty is consistent with the country 's National Energy Policy ( 2009-2030 ) which is spelt out on the Ministry of Science , Energy and Technology 's website . To this end , former energy minister , James Robertson 's message stated thus : " A cornerstone of this policy is diversifying our energy base . We will find new ways to power our economy and to reduce the amount of energy we use .
