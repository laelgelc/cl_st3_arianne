The world needs certainty . Eliminating fossil fuel subsidies and creating a multi-decade rising price curve for carbon will enable clearer discounting of future investment . <p> For their part , fossil fuel subsidies can be reduced on a straight line basis . Achieving predictability in carbon levies can be achieved through carbon trading applied to an ever greater proportion of carbon emissions coupled by a minimum floor price below which carbon prices ca n't fall . <p> This has been the current fatal flaw of carbon markets to date . Already , forward looking jurisdictions like Alberta , Canada are considering such hybrid systems . Australia implemented one before a change of government abolished it .
