However , under the leadership of its current executive director , Joan Clos , UN-Habitat has pivoted to take a larger focus on the issue of " sustainable urbanization " , with a focus on the growth of cities on a majority-urban planet . <p> Meanwhile , the world is now preparing for only the third global conference on issues of housing and urbanization -- Habitat III , taking place in October in Quito .
