<h> Kuala Lumpur ' living library ' bridges generation gap in aging Asia <h> While Asia faces a possible demographic " tax " brought on by a rapidly growing elderly population , aging is still taboo in many of its cities . Here 's what students in the Malaysian capital are doing to change this . <h> Share <p> A " living library " project in Malaysia 's capital is lending out older people 's knowledge to the young , to improve the lives of pensioners and bridge Kuala Lumpur 's generation gap . <p> It links groups of students with retirement homes so older people can discuss the challenges they face living in the city . <p> The project is run by the World Economic Forum 's Global Shapers Community , an international network of young professionals from business , academia and civil society , seeking to make a positive impact in local communities . <p> " We rotate the students around the elderly , " said Renard Siew , a " curator " with Global Shapers , which has more than 375 hubs around the world .
