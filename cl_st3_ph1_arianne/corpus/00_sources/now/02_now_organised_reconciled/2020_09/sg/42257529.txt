<p> Not many people are aware that there is a direct relation between air pollution and Gross Domestic Product ( GDP ) , which is an important indicator of the economic performance of a nation . According to a study by the Centre for Research on Environment and Clean Air ( CREA ) , IQAir Air Visual and Greenpeace Southeast Asia , health damage from air pollution caused between 0.4% and 6% of annual GDP in the world 's leading cities for the first half of 2020 . <p> The same research also found that the estimated losses were between 4.1% and 6.6% for Indian cities from the beginning of this year until September first week .
