in case of future pandemic outbreaks , its chief architect said . <p> Barcelona-based Guallart Architects last month won a contest to design a community in Xiong'an , a new city promoted by Chinese President Xi Jinping as " a new standard in the post-Covid era " that can also be applied elsewhere . <p> The proposal , that includes wooden buildings with large balconies and shared 3-D printers , will allow residents to produce resources locally , and provide all amenities " even in moments of confinement " , according to a press statement . <p> " We can not continue designing cities and buildings as if nothing had happened , " said founder Vicente Guallart . <p> " Our proposal stems from the need to provide solutions to the various crises that are taking place , in order to create a new urban life based in the circular bio-economy , " he said . <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as if nothing had happened . <p> Vicente Guallart , founder , Guallart Architects <p> In China , tech giant
