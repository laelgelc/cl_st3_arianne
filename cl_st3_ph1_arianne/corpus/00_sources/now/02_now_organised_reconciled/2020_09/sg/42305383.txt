If not , the argument goes , the SEC will come after them . <p> As a former SEC enforcement lawyer who spent over a decade spotting failed and misleading disclosures , I can attest that enforcement of broad-based materiality requirements does not work with this kind of near-magical efficiency . <p> That is why securities laws sometimes require very specific data and metrics on certain important matters like executive compensation . <p> But , so far , not for climate risk .
