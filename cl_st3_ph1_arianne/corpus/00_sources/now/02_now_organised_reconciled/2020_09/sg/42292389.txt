<h> " Patterson shares views on CARICOM " <p> Port of Spain -- Former Jamaica prime minister P.J. Patterson has warned against a fragmentation of the regional integration grouping , CARICOM , and is urging regional leaders to ensure that the Caribbean is present " at the table " as negotiations continue for a new world order . <p> Patterson , was part of a virtual panel discussion on Sunday as part of the Bocas Lit Fest that examined the various crises facing the region from the coronavirus ( COVID-19 ) pandemic to the effects of climate change . The discussions also centred on what the present political leaders from the Caribbean have learned from the past to navigate their citizens into the future . <p> Apart from Patterson , 85 , who served as Jamaica 's sixth prime minister from 1992 to 2006 , the other members of the panel included former Belize attorney general Godfrey Smith and Alissa Trotz , a professor of Caribbean Studies at the University of Toronto .
