A highly connected smart city infrastructure represents a major opportunity for achieving this change . Extensively embedded Internet of Things ( IoT ) sensors can gather a wider variety of data . IoT refers to a system of connected devices and sensors that can communicate with one another without human-to-human or human-to-machine interaction . <p> From real-time traffic information and detailed vehicle emissions to temperature and wind direction , fusing data from various sources can reveal valuable insights .
