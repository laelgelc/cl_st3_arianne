Most of the 111,000 hectares of irrigable land in the Teesta basin in Bangladesh is uncultivable during this period due to lack of water . In 2013-14 only 35 per cent of the total irrigable area was cultivated . <p> In 1997 water flow of Teesta in Bangladesh was nearly 6,500 cusecs during dry season .
