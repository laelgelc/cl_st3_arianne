<h> Nations must be wary of escalating geopolitical rifts --Duterte cautions <p> CALOOCAN CITY , Sept. 24 ( PIA ) -- President Rodrigo Roa Duterte on Tuesday cautioned that countries must also be wary of the rising geopolitical tensions across the globe amid nations grappling with the coronavirus pandemic . <p> " Escalating tensions benefit no one . New flashpoints heighten fears and tend to tear peoples apart , " the President said in his address during the 75th session of the United Nations General Assembly . " When elephants fight , it is the grass that gets trampled flat . " <p> Given the size and military might of the contenders , there will be terrible toll on human life and property if real conflict occurs , he added . <p> " I therefore call on the stakeholders in the South China Sea , the Korean Peninsula , the Middle East and Africa : if we can not be friends as yet , then in God 's name , let us not hate each other too much .
