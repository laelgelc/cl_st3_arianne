<h> House passes Dept. of Disaster Resilience bill on final reading <p> Metro Manila ( CNN Philippines , September 22 ) -- The House of Representatives on Tuesday approved on third and final reading House Bill No. 5989 , or the Disaster Resilience Act . <p> Voting 241-7-1 , lawmakers passed the bill that seeks to establish the Department of Disaster Resilience ( DDR ) which will deal with emergencies related to calamities and disasters . <p> The bill proposes a " unified command system " where the department will plan out disaster risk reduction management and response projects to mitigate the impact of natural and man-made disasters . <p> The mandate of the department covers natural hazards and geological phenomena such as earthquakes and volcanic activities ; hydrological , oceanographic and meteorological phenomena such as tropical cyclones , floods , and erosions ; climate change such as El Nino or La Nina , and extreme rainfall and temperature . <p> The bill stated that the department will also be in charge of the implementation of national , local , and community-based disaster resilience and management programs , in collaboration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The passing of the bill had been given top priority by the lower chamber early this year after the eruption of Taal Volcano .
