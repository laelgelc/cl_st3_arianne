<h> This Filipina engineer was recently cited as one of Asia 's rising scientists <p> It 's not often we get to consider mathematical equations as tools to help curb the effects of climate change , or maybe formulate strategies to deal with the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> This field is called process systems engineering , which is not so popular in the Philippines but is a well-established discipline internationally . It is the nature of Prof. Kathleen Aviso 's work . The engineer , who is also the current Assistant Dean for Research and Advanced Studies at the Gokongwei College of Engineering at DLSU , was recently cited by asianscientist.com as one of Asia 's Rising Scientists , for her relevant contribution to environmental research . <p> Aviso has received awards for her increasingly important work . In 2008 , she won the Outstanding Scientific Paper Award from the Philippines ' National Academy of Science and Technology .
