<h> Editorial : Lack of support for R&amp;D <p> ONE of the things that the coronavirus disease 2019 ( Covid-19 ) made us realize is the importance of a strong foundation in science and technology . <p> This strong foundation has allowed some countries to respond quickly and effectively to the pandemic . <p> It is also quite obvious that as we enter a more digitally integrated society , science and technology will play a crucial role in allowing our country to move forward and reach new heights . <p> Sadly , the science and technology sector in the country is not getting as much support as it should be . This is evident with the meager budget the Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) is set to receive for 2021 . <p> On September 9 , the House of Representatives committee on appropriations approved on Wednesday , September 9 , 2020 , the proposed P23.89-billion budget for 2021 .
