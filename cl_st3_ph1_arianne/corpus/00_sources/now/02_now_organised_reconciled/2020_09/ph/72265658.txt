<h> By 2035 , all new cars sold in California will be zero-emission vehicles <p> Some big news for those of you residing in California . The state has just announced that it 's moving to eliminate the sale of gasoline-powered cars and trucks . This follows Governor Gavin Newsom 's issuance of an executive order requiring all new passenger vehicle sales to be zero-emission by the year 2035 . <p> All this comes in an effort to reduce reliance on fossil fuels , fight climate change , and spur economic growth in the process .
