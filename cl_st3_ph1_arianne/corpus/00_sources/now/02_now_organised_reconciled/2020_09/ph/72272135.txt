With the signing of the Universal Health Care ( UHC ) law , as well as the acceleration of digital across industries , the healthcare industry is expected to thrive and further meet the needs of many Filipinos . <p> Reports have spotted the main issues that the entire healthcare sector encounters . A report last year from the Oxford Business Group ( OBG ) , for instance , pointed out what it regards as the " triple burden " on the local health system . " Policy makers , healthcare providers , and pharmaceutical firms are compelled to scale up their research and development efforts to meet these changing needs . " <p> The report also notes the rising costs of healthcare . Its consolidated research noted the steady growth in health expenditure , the latest of which indicated an increase of 20% from 2015 to 2017 . <p> " This growth trend has been evident since 2012 , in which per capita health expenditure growth surpassed per capita GDP growth . The gap continued to widen over the five years thereafter , " the report stressed . <p> It also noted the consistently large share of household out-of-pocket payments ( OOP ) in the total health expenditure in the country . <p> " In 2017 , OOP spending was at 54.5% , amounting to PHP 372.8 billion . These hospitals are not enough to attend to the country 's population of about 108 million people , " the report pointed out . <p> In spite of these challenges and risks , steps have already been taken to address them . The hallmark among these actions is the Republic Act No. 11223 , more known as the UHC Law .
