<p> Ticks are among nature 's most hardy survivors . They 've been around for at least 100 million years and used to feast on dinosaur blood . Their bodies contain anti-freeze to help them survive cold weather and their two front legs have carbon dioxide and infrared sensors to help detect when a warm-blooded mammal is approaching . Tiny hairs on their legs increases friction and allows ticks to latch onto animals that brush by .
