<h> House approves Dept. of Disaster Resilience bill on second reading <h> Lawmakers approved on second reading a bill that seeks to create a new department that would solely focus on addressing emergencies related to calamities and disasters . ( FILE PHOTO ) <p> Metro Manila ( CNN Philippines , September 15 ) -- Lawmakers approved on second reading a bill that seeks to create a new department that would solely focus on addressing emergencies related to calamities and disasters . <p> The House of Representatives on Tuesday approved House Bill No. 5989 or the Disaster Resilience Act which will establish the Department of Disaster Resilience ( DDR ) . <p> Under the bill , the department will plan out disaster risk reduction , management and response projects through a " unified command system . " This covers events categorized as geological phenomena such as earthquakes , volcanic activities ; hydrological , oceanographic and meteorological phenomena such as tropical cyclones , floods , and erosions ; climate change such as El Nino or La Nina , extreme rainfall and temperature . <p> The department shall also take charge in vulnerability @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ responsibilities . It will be headed by a Secretary with undersecretaries and regional offices , the bill stated . <p> Congress considered the measure as one of its top priorities since earlier this year when the Taal Volcano in Batangas erupted , covering nearby provinces in ash and prompting the evacuation of its residents . The House will now need to pass the bill on third and final reading before sending it to the Senate . <p> At present , there is the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council ( NDRRMC ) but it is under the Office of Civil Defense headed by Defense Secretary Delfin Lorenzana . This arrangement resulted in slow release of aid to disaster-stricken areas in the past since the NDRRMC had to wait for the OCD 's approval first before taking action . <p> The creation of the new agency will ensure more efficient emergency responses , Senate Majority Leader Migz Zubiri said in January . <p> President Rodrigo Duterte had also urged the swift passage of a similar bill during his 2018 State of the Nation Address .
