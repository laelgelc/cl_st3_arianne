<p> As the human race continues to battle against the pandemic , we never fail to thank our health care workers who provide their services in the frontline . Some send them gifts , while some write them poems and songs , and some stand on the balcony of their high-rise condominiums and claps and yells to praise doctors and nurses . <p> But do you think we could survive the pandemic only with the aid of medical care alone ? No . We need to boost our immunity by eating nutritious food to be able to fight the virus . <p> Again , do you think food just suddenly appears on grocery stores and markets ? The kilos of rice grains , bulk of vegetables , pieces of corn , and packs of monggo beans ? The nutritious meals you find atop dining tables ? Do you think these ' battle gears ' we use to fight the deadly virus , since the vaccine is still out of sight , just pop out of somewhere ? <p> Definitely no .
