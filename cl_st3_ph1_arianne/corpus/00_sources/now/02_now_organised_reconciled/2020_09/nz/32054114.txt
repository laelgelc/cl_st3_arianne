<h> WWF-New Zealand Is Taking A Stand For Our Ocean <p> This election , WWF is amplifying the voices of New Zealanders as they call for a better protected ocean . <p> On Monday 31 August , WWF-New Zealand launched a new digital campaign , demanding our political leaders finally make ocean health an election issue -- a joint call with the voices of marine and climate scientists , Maori leaders , young ocean activists , and many other New Zealanders . Less than 1% of Aotearoa 's coastal and marine area is fully protected . 80% of New Zealanders -- including 91% of our youth , and 88% of Maori -- agree that we must do more . <p> Every single life on our planet is dependent on the ocean . It 's one of the world 's greatest resources .
