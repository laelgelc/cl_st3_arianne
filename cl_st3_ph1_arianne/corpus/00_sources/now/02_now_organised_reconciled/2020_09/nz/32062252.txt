In addition to the increased minimum wage for all , employers are obliged to provide workers with adequate housing and food or pay allowances to cover these expenses . <p> " We are very encouraged by the bold steps Qatar is taking to increase the protection of the rights of migrant workers and ensure adequate living and working conditions for all , " the experts said .
