<h> Govt open to advice on 100% renewable target - Shaw <p> The government may be willing to shift ground on its 2035 renewable electricity target if the Interim Climate Change Committee feels there are better targets to pursue , Climate Change Minister James Shaw says . <p> The committee , established last year to examine ways of delivering the 100 percent renewable electricity target and bring agriculture into the emissions trading scheme , is due to report back to the government in April . <p> In December , committee chair David Prentice said that while the government 's 100 percent renewable target was technically achievable , it would be expensive and made little sense given the industry only accounted for about 5 percent of the country 's emissions . The country was better to focus on electrification of industry and transport , he said . <p> Shaw told delegates at the Downstream energy conference in Auckland today that the electricity sector has a " great opportunity " to meet a potential doubling of electricity demand projected out to 2050 and to meet that without using fossil fuels . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not fixed on its view on the 100 percent renewables target and will consider any advice the ICCC delivers . <p> He said the committee had protected its independence " fiercely " .
