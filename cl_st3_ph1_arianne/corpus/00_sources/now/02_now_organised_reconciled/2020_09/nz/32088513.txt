<h> Infrastructure updates needed , new cycleway not <p> I write to commend Bob Hughes for his letter of September 18 which in my view provides a very sensible opinion on priorities for council 's expenditure of our hard-earned money . <p> I am a ratepayer and like many others in our city , also a keen cyclist . My favourite ride is from home , down Stout Street to join the shared walkway/cycleway from Peel Street , weaving around our picturesque rivers to our world-class oceanside ride up to the Midway Surf Club , on to Centennial Marine Drive along to Pacific Street , Awapuni Rd , Lytton Rd then home . This route provides a blend of shared cycleways , streets and roads which I feel perfectly safe on , although I do take extra care along Awapuni Rd due to the heavy traffic . <p> I used to own a Taruheru riverside property in the 1990s which often had high water during winter , and frequent flooding . Climate change , escalating higher seas/rivers and increasing weather events will pose real concerns for a cycleway built in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ city 's ageing infrastructure is a major barrier to progressing a number of initiatives including , amongst other priorities , the development of housing in Gisborne . I understand that we can not pump enough drinking water from the dams at Waingake because there are kilometres of old cement asbestos pipes that blow under pressure . There have been several events in Mangapapa in the past few years . This is just one example of how critical it is for the council to spend our ratepayer dollars on repairing/replacing our ageing infrastructure where needed . <p> Covid-19 has seen Gisborne/East Coast families who have been living elsewhere returning home . This , amongst other factors , has pushed our house values up and put extra pressure on the need for new housing . <p> The need to update our infrastructure is only going to increase as time goes on . By its nature , infrastructure is out of sight and , for many , out of mind until it fails . <p> So with a limited budget available for the council to work with , I would suggest that an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ new cycleway is a nice to have .
