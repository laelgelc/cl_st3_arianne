<h> With Act polling at 7% , who is on course to join David Seymour in parliament ? <p> Yesterday 's Colmar Brunton poll for TVNZ had Act inching toward its best-ever election result , on 7% . With that , the one-man Act band would become a Seymour-led club of nine . So who the hell are they ? <p> By now , David Seymour must be among our most recognisable political figures . Entering parliament fresh-faced in 2014 as the MP for Epsom , the Act Party leader has been the total caucus of the party for six years now . <p> If current polling is to be believed , that 's about to change .
