<p> An urban farm sounds like a contradiction in terms but actually it may well be a way of the future , a new economic , ecological and social sector . <p> Charmian Smith <p> The model for urban farms uses small plots of city land , provides food security for locals by growing food intensively while improving the soil , benefiting the environment , and providing jobs , rather than relying on volunteers as the current model of community gardens does . <p> " We are really looking at transitioning a volunteer sector into a sustainable economic sector , " Sarah Smuts-Kennedy , vision holder and one of the founders of OMG ( organic market garden ) , an urban farm in Symonds St , Auckland , says . <p> OMG is under the umbrella of **26;747;TOOLONG which aims to regenerate the ecosystem via agriculture and the production of food . <p> " We want to break the myth that industrialised food production is the only way to feed the world , " she said . <p> Now in its third season , OMG employs almost two FTE staff @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ boxes each week to local subscribers . <p> It also trains interns in regenerative horticulture , an intensive growing system based on the health of the soil and its biology -- the microbes , fungi and worms -- which , in turn , grows healthy crops and encourages beneficial insects such as pollinators . <p> The farm was set up on a derelict inner-city section that had been acquired but not used for a city rail link . Occupying about 330sq m , it turns over about $1400 a week selling vegetables , but this season plans to increase that to around $2000 with the sale of seedlings , compost and education , she said . <p> A project like this needs seeding finance , but in three to 12 months it becomes self-sustaining , providing jobs , healthy food and repairing the soil system . It recycles local food scraps and organic waste into compost , acts as a heat sink to mitigate climate change and helps councils to meet their biodiversity responses , prevents runoff and generates food security within the local community , she said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ use various organic sprays that , although less harmful than conventional sprays , affect all insects or fungi both beneficial and detrimental . <p> " When we 've repaired the soil system we have plants that no longer need any pesticide or fungicide .
