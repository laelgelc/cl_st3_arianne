<h> 100 percent renewable energy is a red herring <p> Opinion : A promise to move to fully renewable electricity by 2030 distracts from Labour 's unwillingness to act on the sectors that contribute measurably to our greenhouse gas emissions , like transport and agriculture , Marc Daalder argues <p> On the face of it , the promise from Labour that it would make New Zealand 's electricity system fully renewable by 2030 seems a bold climate pledge . <p> In reality , it is little more than a red herring to distract from the woeful lack of policy to reduce emissions in sectors that pollute far more than the energy industry . <p> The pledge advances an earlier target of a 100 percent renewable system by 2035 and would involve a review of the plan when the Government evaluates its five-year emissions budget in 2025 . Because New Zealand 's electricity system is so reliant on hydroelectric generators - which produce nearly two-thirds of our power - a dry year with less rain could mean we do n't create enough electricity to power everything that needs power .
