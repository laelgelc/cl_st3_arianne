<h> Research reveals shocking detail on how Australia 's environmental scientists are being silenced <p> Ecologists and conservation experts in government , industry and universities are routinely constrained in communicating scientific evidence on threatened species , mining , logging and other threats to the environment , our new research has found . <p> Our study , just published , shows how important scientific information about environmental threats often does not reach the public or decision-makers , including government ministers . <p> In some cases , scientists self-censor information for fear of damaging their careers , losing funding or being misrepresented in the media . In others , senior managers or ministers ' officers prevented researchers from speaking truthfully on scientific matters . <p> This information blackout , termed " science suppression " , can hide environmentally damaging practices and policies from public scrutiny . The practice is detrimental to both nature and democracy . <p> When scientists are free to communicate their knowledge , the public is kept informed.University of Queensland/AAP <h> Code of silence <p> Our online survey ran from October 25 , 2018 , to February 11 , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Australian ecologists , conservation scientists , conservation policy makers and environmental consultants .
