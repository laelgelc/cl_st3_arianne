<h> There are worse things than lowering the voting age <p> In last week 's leaders ' game show styled election debate , the two candidates were asked for their views on lowering the voting age . As expected , National 's conservative Judith Collins ruled out extending the franchise to those aged 16 and over , from the current 18 years . But it was perhaps also predictable that Jacinda Ardern did not support the proposition either . Liam Hehir says she has correctly read general public opinion , " She understands that a cautious , even conservative approach will keep her in the Beehive " . Surveys reveal that most respondents believe 18 is the correct age to be able to vote . So there 's no widespread mandate for change , especially from those who already have the power .
