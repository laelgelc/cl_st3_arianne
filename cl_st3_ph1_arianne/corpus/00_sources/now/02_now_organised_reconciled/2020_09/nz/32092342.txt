This included rapid emergency grants and technical assistance to help governments meet urgent medical needs ; a new COVID-19 Pandemic Response Option ( CPRO ) , which is supporting countercyclical expenditure programs ; and assistance for the private sector . <p> ADB has so far committed about $11.2 billion in financial and technical assistance to fight the pandemic .
