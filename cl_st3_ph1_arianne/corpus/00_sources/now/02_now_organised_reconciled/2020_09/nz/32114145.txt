<h> Business leaders keen to ensure climate action stays on agenda <p> The economic disruption caused by the coronavirus pandemic must not be allowed to derail efforts over climate change , business leaders say . <p> Businesses are encouraged to reduce their carbon emissions as part of their commitment on climate action . Photo : 123RF <p> The Sustainable Business Council ( SBC ) has teamed up with the Climate Leaders Coalition ( CLC ) , to press action on reaching a zero-carbon economy . <p> Both organisations have launched new multi-year strategies to achieve this goal , which includes climate advocacy , collaborating with central government on climate initiatives , and encouraging businesses to reduce their emissions . <p> Sustainable Business Council 's executive director Mike Burrell said Covid-19 had shown businesses how they could unite against the existential threat of climate change . <p> " I suppose the only good side of Covid-19 is that it 's shown us that we can mobilise very quickly , both nationally and internationally , to confront a crisis when it 's on a massive scale like Covid-19 has been . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ when it comes to mobilising against climate change , " Burrell said . <p> Mike Bennetts Photo : RNZ / Alexander Robertson <p> CLC includes more than 100 of the country 's leading chief executives . <p> Each member pledges to take steps to reduce their individual emissions and commits to annual reporting on how they are adapting to climate change . <p> " Over the past two years , CLC has created the momentum for CEOs to lead the way by signing up to ambitious climate change commitments . The next phase for us will be demonstrating progress against these commitments and making even more aspirational moves to accelerate further action , " said CLC convenor Mike Bennetts . <p> Under the new partnership , the CLC will come under the umbrella of the SBC , which would prioritise peer-to-peer learning among companies , partnering with similarly minded initiatives internationally and public relations . <p> Actor , model , producer , activist and former fitness guru Jane Fonda reflects on her relationship with climate change activism in a new book:What Can I Do ?
