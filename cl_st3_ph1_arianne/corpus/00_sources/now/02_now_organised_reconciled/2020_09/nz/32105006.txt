Smaller government and fiscal discipline are out , big public objectives like addressing poverty , tackling climate change and growing the regions are in . <h> How do you achieve outcomes ? <p> Achieving the new outcomes will require a different approach . Most obviously , we 're going to need to work out what we want to achieve in the first place . <p> Set the vision and identify the outcomes <p> This process can be as hard as we want to make it . We then match what we 're already doing to the outcomes , satisfy ourselves that what we 're doing aligns with what we want to achieve and then pat ourselves on the back for promoting outcomes . <p> This is not outcomes achievement . It is reverse engineering policies to make ourselves feel like we 're on the right track and it is contributing to the problem . <h> What we still have to do <p> If New Zealand is serious about achieving outcomes different to those which we have been achieving , we must change three things . <p> We must change the way we plan .
