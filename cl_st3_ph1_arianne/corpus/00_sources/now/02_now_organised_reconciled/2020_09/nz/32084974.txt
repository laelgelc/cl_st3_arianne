I congratulate the 100 Parties that have been leading by example , " added Mr. Guterres . <h> What is ozone ? <p> Ozone is a special form of oxygen with the chemical formula O3 . The oxygen we breathe and that is so vital to life on earth is O2 . <p> Most ozone resides high up in the atmosphere , between 10-40 kilometres ( about 6-25 miles ) above Earth 's surface . This region is called the stratosphere and it contains about 90 per cent of all the ozone in the atmosphere . <p> Though it constitutes a very small part of the atmosphere , ozone 's presence is vital for life on Earth , as it shields the planet from Sun 's biologically harmful ultraviolet UV-B radiation .
