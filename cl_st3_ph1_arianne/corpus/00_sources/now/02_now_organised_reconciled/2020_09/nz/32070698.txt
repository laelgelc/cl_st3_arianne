<p> News from Wellington City Council The capital 's car share schemes are proving to be popular in Wellington -- and with the planet . <p> The Wellington City Council 's car share policies are designed to encourage the use of car sharing as a flexible , low carbon option that sits alongside public transport modes . With more than 7200 Wellingtonians belonging to car sharing schemes , car sharing is fast becoming the new normal for Wellington . <p> Data from 2019/20 provided by Wellington 's two car sharing providers , MEVO and Cityhop , shows that across the two schemes , one car share vehicle now replaces 11 private vehicles , there has been a 170% increase in membership , almost 50,000 trips taken , and 70,000kg of CO2 from car share trips has been offset . <p> These results show there is a lot of public support for this kind of operation in our city , says Mayor Andy Foster . <p> " We know from recent consultations that alternatives to private vehicles are a top priority for Wellingtonians , and the uptake in usage of car @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opportunity provided to them . <p> " Car sharing also supports inner-city residents and businesses by giving people greater travel choices , and it means they can get a car when they need one while avoiding the high cost of car ownership and sometimes congested parking that comes with owning a car . <p> " These car share operators also contribute to the Let 's Get Wellington Moving and Te Atakura -- First to Zero work programmes , supporting the city 's growth and climate change response . " <p> Car sharing is a type of car rental but is geared toward people who want to use a car for brief periods of time and only pay for their usage . The cars are generally newer model , low-emissions vehicles , and some companies even offset all fuel emissions produced , a trend pioneered by MEVO . <p> As electric chargers become more integrated into the city , it 's expected that Wellington 's two car share providers will employ more electric cars too . <p> Global data shows car share schemes help reduce parking congestion , support the use @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with Council 's aims to make Wellington a sustainable eco-city , says Sustainability Manager Tom Pettit . <p> " We encourage this way of getting around the city by providing dedicated on-street car parks for car share vehicles or allowing vehicles to ' free float ' among any of our parking bays for extended periods provided they pay for parking . <p> " The Council has around 30 car parks across the city specifically for car share vehicles which helps reduce the need to own a car -- or at the very least , to not bring their car into the CBD .
