<p> The horror of June 2013 is etched in Rajendra Singh Negi 's mind . On June 12 , 2013 , he travelled a few kilometres from his village to the town of Rudraprayag in Uttarakhand on some work . <p> Heavy , incessant rainfall started the next day and water levels rose alarmingly in the Garhwal Himalayas at the confluence of the Alaknanda and Mandakini rivers , headwaters of the Ganga . <p> " On the morning of June 17 , 2013 , around 7.30 am , I saw a wall of water more than 20 feet high rush down the Mandakini , " Negi said . " It looked like the end of the world . " <p> Days of extreme rainfall had dumped billions of litres of water over the western Himalayan region . Between June 13 , 2013 , and June 19 , 2013 , the mountain state of Uttarakhand received excess rainfall of nearly 850% .
