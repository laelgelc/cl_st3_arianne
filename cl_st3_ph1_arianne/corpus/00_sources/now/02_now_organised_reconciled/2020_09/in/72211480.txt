<h> Govt hopeful of resuming mining soon : Governor <p> Panaji : Stating that the development journey of Goa so far , was not easy and it is only due to the determination , self respect and self confidence that the state has become a role model in certain areas , Governor Satya Pal Malik on Tuesday said that the government is doing everything possible for finding solution to mining and Mhadei issues . <p> Delivering the annual customary gubernatorial address in the state legislative assembly , Malik said that the state is exploring all possible lawful options to resume mining operations and has also filed review petition before the Supreme Court to review its order dated February 7 , 2018 , wherein it had cancelled 88 mining leases in the state . <p> " The government is very optimistic about resuming the mining operations in Goa , during the ensuing season , thereby rejuvenating state 's economy and lives of citizens , " he added . <p> Speaking further , Malik said that the government is also making efforts to prevent Mhadei water diversion outside the river basin by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Mahadayi Water Disputes Tribunal , as also has filed a Special Leave Petition in the Supreme Court praying for review of the tribunal award , besides filing necessary contempt proceedings before the Supreme Court , " he noted . <p> The Governor informed the members of the state legislative assembly that the revenue collection of the state through Goods and Services Tax was Rs 2,558.40 crore till December 2019 , while the excise revenue collection of the state has touched Rs 351.55 crore up to December 2019 , showing a growth of 7.6 per cent as compared to the same period of the previous year .
