Despite the Union government 's electrification spree , the availability of quality electricity is a far-fetched dream in rural areas . Supply of electricity through centralised grid becomes increasingly difficult and technically and economically challenging for more remote places . Local , self-controlled electric systems capable of producing power are , thus , a way out . <p> " Electricity is one of the greatest gifts to mankind . But the supply in India has its shortcomings in terms of equity -- economic , ecological , qualitative and quantitative -- which are much desired , " said Pavan Choudary , managing director of Vygon India Pvt Ltd .
