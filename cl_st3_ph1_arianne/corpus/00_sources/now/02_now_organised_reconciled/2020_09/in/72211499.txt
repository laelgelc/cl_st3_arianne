<p> Faculty members , students and staff with the help of professionals have been working hands-on to promote coexistence , which is often fraught with conservation challenges . Campuses range from having designated on-site biodiversity-rich areas that have formal conservation plans ( like in IIT-Mandi ) , to sporting patches of green within urban spaces ( Indian Institute of Science , Bengaluru ) .
