<h> Letters to the editor ( 30 Sept 2020 ) <p> Dengue-demic ahoy ? <p> We are still in the middle of the worst pandemic known to man but it appears that the miseries of the common man are going to be compounded by dengue knocking at our doors . Cases till August this year in Goa have been pegged at 248 , double that of the numbers recorded for the same period last year . With similar symptoms being shared by dengue , malaria and COVID , their confirmatory diagnosis becomes doubly difficult to arrive at . We are witnessing a sharp upswing in dengue cases in Goa and given the creaking health infrastructure , the onus lies with individuals to keep themselves safe by maintaining cleanliness of their surroundings and going in for testing at the slightest symptoms of fever . <p> With people forced to stay indoors this pandemic has facilitated breeding of the Aedes Aegypti mosquito , also regular cleaning and sanitisation measures could not be undertaken in hotels and other commercial establishments due to lockdown .
