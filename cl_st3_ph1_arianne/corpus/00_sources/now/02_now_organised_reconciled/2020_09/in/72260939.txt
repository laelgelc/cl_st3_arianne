<p> Chief Minister Vijay Rupani announced assistance for students to buy battery-operated two-wheelers . The announcement took place on Thursday as part of the celebration of Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's birthday in Gandhinagar where the Climate Change department also signed 10 E-MOUs . <p> Focusing on reducing pollution in the cities , the government is now emphasising on the purchase and use of electric vehicles . <p> The CM made several announcements on Thursday evening . Students from Class 9 and college students will get assistance to buy battery-operated two-wheelers . The government aims to give a subsidy for 10,000 vehicles . <p> The government has also decided to give subsidy for 5000 e-rickshaws with Rs 48,000 each for individual or institutional users . Along with the subsidy , the government will also implement a Rs 50-lakh scheme to install charging facilities in the coming days . <p> Further , CM Rupani spoke of energy generation through solar energy .
