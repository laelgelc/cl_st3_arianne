<h> Believe it or not ! Indus water mixes with Nal Sarovar <p> The Indus is back in Gujarat , not just teasing and tempting as it did until about 200 years ago , but with its elixir of life that about 5,000 years ago gave rise to a civilisation . <p> Boon indeed it may prove , specially for water-deficient Kutch and parts of Bhal region , where the scientists say water table may get a boost . The romance of having the Indus , or the Sindhu , water right at the doorstep of Ahmedabad is an added bounty . <p> The thin blue line from Little Rann to Nal Sarovar near Ahmedabad shows the flow of Indus water .
