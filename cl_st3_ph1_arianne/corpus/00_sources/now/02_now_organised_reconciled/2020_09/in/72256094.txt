environment ministry informed Parliament on Saturday . <p> In a written reply to a query by CPI MP Binoy Viswam , Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Babul Supriyo said 313 alerts and advisories were issued by the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau ( WCCB ) between 2017 and 2020 for preventive action against poaching and illegal trade of wildlife . <p> He said the management of forests and wildlife is primarily the responsibility of states and union territories . <p> Financial assistance is provided to them under the Centrally Sponsored Schemes of Integrated Development of Wildlife Habitats for providing better protection to wildlife including endangered species , improvement of their habitat and to ensure harmonious existence of human settlements with wild animals , he said . <p> Referring to data available with the WCCB and provided by states and UTs , the minister said 478 endangered animals were killed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The animals were
