Coinciding with the 75th anniversary of the global body , it reflected the directness and candour with which India can make itself heard in the comity of nations . Coming as it did in the midst of a raging pandemic , and a serious border situation with China in Ladakh , the speech heralds a tectonic shift in India 's rules of engagement with the world . I would say even for the Prime Minister it was an evolutionary curve , compared to his first UNGA address in 2014 .
