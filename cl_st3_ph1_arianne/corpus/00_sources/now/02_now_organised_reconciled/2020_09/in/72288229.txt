<h> Last Updated : Tuesday 22 September 2020 <p> Floods have ravaged Sudan : At least 100 people have died and more than 110,000 homes destroyed since mid-July 2020 . The Nile has breached its banks to the highest levels for the first time in a century . <p> The Sudanese government recently declared a state of emergency for three months and designated the country a natural disaster zone . It warned that heavy rains could continue for the coming weeks . <p> The United States National Aeronautics and Space Administration ( NASA ) Earth Observatory recently captured two rainy seasons in Sudan -- four years apart . <p> The tributaries of the Nile -- white Nile and Blue Nile -- were flooded by September 2 , 2020 .
