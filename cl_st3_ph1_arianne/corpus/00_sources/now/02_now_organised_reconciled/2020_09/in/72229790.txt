<h> Share Via Email <p> BHUBANESWAR : Beach tourism in Odisha is all set to get a major boost with the State Government developing the golden beach at Puri as a Blue Flag beach on a pilot basis . <p> The 870-metre stretch from Digabareni square to the back of Mayfair Hotel has been developed as Blue Flag beach under the World Bank-funded Integrated Coastal Zone Management Project ( ICZMP ) of the State Forest and Environment Department and Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . <p> The Blue Flag certification is a highly recognised eco-label in which a beach has to meet 33 criteria fixed by the Foundation for Environment Education ( FEE ) , Denmark . <p> As part of the initiative , core infrastructure such as public toilets , changing and shower room , watch towers , solar lighting system , sitting arrangements , demarcation of safe swimming zone besides mechanised pollution abetment services , beach safety and security measures have been developed on the stretch at an investment of Rs 7.88 crore . <p> Peripheral activities such as approach road , fencing with plantation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ track , installation of CCTV cameras and public address system have been executed by Odisha Government through the State Project Management Unit of ICZMP at a cost Rs 3.34 crore . <p> The national jury has recommended eight such applications from India , including the Golden Beach of Puri , to the international jury of FEE for final certification .
