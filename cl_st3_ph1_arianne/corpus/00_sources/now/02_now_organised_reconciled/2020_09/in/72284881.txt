<h> NGT raps Centre for delay in finalising ESZ in Western Ghats <p> The principal bench of National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) has once again come down heavily on the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) for the inordinate delay in finalising notification on eco-sensitive zone ( ESZ ) for the protection of the Western Ghats . <p> It has given the central ministry three months ' time till December 31 to finalise the notification . <p> The NGT strongly objected to the reason of ' lockdown ' given by the MoEF&amp;CC for the delay in complying with its order . The NGT , however , said the direction to stop the salary to the advisor , ESZ Division , MoEF&amp;CC , will become operative if the ministry fails to finalise the notification by December 31 , 2020 . It has ordered the MoEF&amp;CC to file a compliance report on February 11 , 2021 . <p> The NGT , in an earlier direction , had allowed time to the MoEF&amp;CC <p> till March 31 , 2020 , for finalisation of the notification and warned @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ advisor , ESZ Division , if there was a further default and delay beyond March 2020 . <p> However , during the hearing on Monday , the MoEF&amp;CC produced a notification dated September 22 , 2020 , issued by it , for excluding the time for publication from March 25 , 2020 to May 31 , 2020 on account of the COVID-induced lockdown . <p> " Even if lockdown period is to be excluded , there is no justification for continued delay merely because the states have sought exclusion of area from eco-sensitive zone . Let the needful be done now before December 31 , 2020 . For any default thereafter , direction to stop salary of the advisor , ESZ Division , MoEF&amp;CC , will be operative , " the NGT order reads . <p> The Tribunal , headed by its chairperson Adarsh Kumar Goel , was hearing a plea alleging that notification of eco-sensitive zone in the Western Ghats has not been finalised despite NGT 's direction to complete the exercise by March 31 , 2020 . <p> The Tribunal noted that the consideration of Dr K Kasturirangan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ principle by the MoEF&amp;CC , has been pending for the last around eight years for more and more exclusion of area from the eco-sensitive area for ' development ' . <p> The NGT observed that the states have called for either alteration in the draft notification or its complete withdrawal . It said in its previous order of August 28 , 2018 , that no changes be made to reduce the area of eco-sensitive zone in terms of notification dated February 27 , 2017 , without the same being considered by this Tribunal . <p> The NGT said submissions of all the states have been examined keeping in view the recommendations of the HLWG ( high level working group ) . The analysis of the response from the states reveals that there is a concern of the states about prohibition of certain activities having implications on medical care , healthcare and education . <p> The states are concerned about certain areas within the proposed notification that have since the time of submission of Gadgil Committee report and HLWG report , undergone demographic and infrastructural changes .
