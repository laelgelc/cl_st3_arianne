<p> New Delhi : How can India clean up its environment , get rid of air pollutants and , at the same time , pull its cash-strapped power distribution sector out of colossal debt ? A new study suggests that retiring old coal power plants could be an effective strategy to achieve all these aims . <p> If 54 coal plants that are 20 years or older , located across 11 states , could be shut down over the next two years , it would yield savings of up to Rs 53,000 crore ( $7.2 billion ) over five years for these states ' electricity distribution companies ( discoms ) , as per a new analysis by Climate Risk Horizon ( CRH ) , a non-profit that analyses the risks to the Indian economy from climate change .
