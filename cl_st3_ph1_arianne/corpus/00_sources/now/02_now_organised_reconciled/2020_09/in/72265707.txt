Photo : Wikimedia Commons <p> India was ' poorly prepared ' to deal with ' climate reality ' , due to which it was more vulnerable to extreme natural disasters , according to the World Risk Index ( WRI ) 2020 . <p> India ranked 89th among 181 countries on the WRI 2020 . The country was fourth-most-at- risk in south Asia on the index , after Bangladesh , Afghanistan and Pakistan . <p> Sri Lanka , Bhutan and the Maldives fared better than India in their abilities to cope with extreme disasters , the report said . India also lagged behind these three neighbours in terms of lack of adaptive capacities or the preparedness to deal with extreme events . <p> India and other south Asian nations did improve their ranking marginally in the World Risk Index during the course of a year . Vanuatu was the country with the highest disaster risk worldwide . It was followed by Tonga and Dominica . <p> Small island states , especially in the South Pacific and the Caribbean , were disproportionately represented among high-risk countries , due to their high exposure to extreme natural events . These also included countries at risk from the rise in sea level as a result of global warming . <p> The small island states had limited financial resources and had made small contributions to climate change , but were affected the most by its consequences . <p> Hence , merely providing financial resources for adaptation to climate change was not sufficient .
