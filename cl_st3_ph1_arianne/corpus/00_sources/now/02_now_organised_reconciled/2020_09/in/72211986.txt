<h> EGC bans cutting of trees , killing wild animals , birds <p> 01-Sep-2020 <p> <p> By Our Staff ReporterIMPHAL , Sep 1 : To ensure protection and preservation of environment , the Educational Gym Club ( EGC ) , Wangbal Part-II , Thoubal district has banned cutting of trees and trapping/killing of wild birds and animals in all areas under its jurisdiction.In a statement released by the club , it stated that EGC , in association with the Joint Co-ordination Committee on Wildlife &amp; Biodiversity ( JCCWB ) , Manipur and volunteers of the locality will conduct a mass tree plantation drive as well.The increase number of tree cutting , killing wild animals etc in the State has led to deterioration of the environment , it said and added that these initiatives are being undertaken to protect and preserve the environment and to help battle climate change and global warming.As part of this undertaking , the club organised a social service camp in Thongjao Bazar , Wangbal Part-II today .
