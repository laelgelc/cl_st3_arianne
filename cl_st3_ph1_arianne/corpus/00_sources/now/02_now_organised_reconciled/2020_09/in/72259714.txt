These Bills , along with The Essential Commodities ( Amendment ) Bill , 2020 , are part of the liberalisation plan of the agricultural sector in India . <p> According to Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) of the United Nations , 70% of India 's rural households still depend primarily on agriculture for their livelihood , with 82% farmers being small and marginal .
