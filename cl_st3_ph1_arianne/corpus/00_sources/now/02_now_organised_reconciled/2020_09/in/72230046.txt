<h> Threat of climate change is real and dangerous : Piyush Goyal <p> New Delhi India , September 9 ( ANI ) : Union Minister Piyush Goyal on Tuesday said that the threat of climate change is real and dangerous and the country has to move towards a world of net-zero carbon emissions . " We ultimately have to move towards a world where we are net zero in terms of carbon emissions . The threat of climate change is very real , near and dangerous , " Goyal said at the Sustainability Summit on Circular Economy for Self-Reliant India organised by the Confederation of Indian Industry via video conferencing.Goyal , who is also the railway minister , said railways have introduced mechanised cleaning in trains which brought down consumption of water by 90 per cent . " When we introduced mechanised cleaning of trains , we brought down consumption of water by 90 per cent .
