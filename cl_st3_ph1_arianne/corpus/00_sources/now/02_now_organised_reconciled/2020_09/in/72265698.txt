<p> Modi , who will deliver his formal statement on behalf of India to the General Assembly on Saturday , supported the reforms undertaken by UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres . <p> " We support ongoing reforms by the SecretaryGeneral . We reiterate our call for reforms of three of the principal organs of the UN . We commit to instil new life in the discussions on the reform of the Security Council and continue the work to revitalise the General Assembly and strengthen the Economic and Social Council , " he added . <p> PM Modi highlighted that India 's own philosophies align with the goals of the UN . He said work still needed to be done towards reducing inequality , mitigating conflicts and addressing the threat of climate change . <p> He also highlighted that India was one of the largest contributors to the UN Peacekeeping Missions .
