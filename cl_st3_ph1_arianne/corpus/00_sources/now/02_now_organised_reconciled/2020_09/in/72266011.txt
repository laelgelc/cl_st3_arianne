<p> PennyMac Financial Services , Inc . ( NYSE : PFSI ) ( the " Company " ) today announced that it intends to offer $400 million aggregate principal amount of Senior Notes due 2025 ( the " Notes " ) . The Notes will be fully and unconditionally guaranteed on an unsecured senior basis by our existing and future wholly owned domestic subsidiaries , other than certain excluded subsidiaries . The Company intends to use the net proceeds from this offering for general corporate purposes , which may include the repayment of the Company 's existing secured warehouse borrowings . The offering is subject to market conditions and other factors .
