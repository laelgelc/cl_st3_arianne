<h> NGT forms panel to probe case of missing trees on Pune-Nashik hwy <p> NHAI was supposed to plant more than 23,000 saplings along the stretch ; FILE PHOTO <p> Bench is hearing plaint against NHAI for not complying with compensatory afforestation <p> The principal bench of National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) in a recent order has directed to constitute a committee consisting chief conservator of forest , senior officers of the National Highways Authority of India ( NHAI ) and officials of Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEF &amp; CC ) and asked officials concerned to submit a report on action taken within six weeks regarding NHAI 's role in alleged tree felling along a stretch of Pune-Nashik highway . <p> The bench is conducting a hearing against NHAI , which was involved in developing the four-lane Khed-Sinnar stretch along Pune-Nashik National Highway ( NH-50 ) in 2014-15 , but allegedly hacked over 2,373 fully grown trees belonging to 29 different species in the process .
