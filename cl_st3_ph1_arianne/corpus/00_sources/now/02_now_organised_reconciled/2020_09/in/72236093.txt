Flooding in cities is a worrying trend and getting worse , a sign of rising populations and failure to execute urbanisation policies , Bloomberg reported.Last month , flooding in the Three Gorges Dam , the world 's largest hydroelectric dam , on the Yangtze River peaked and officials feared it could overflow . Authorities elsewhere have blown up barriers that were creating more damage than help.Over 40 per cent of China 's population lives in the Yangtze River Economic Belt and accounts for nearly 50 per cent of export value and 45 per cent of Gross Domestic Product ( GDP ) , according to China Water Risk , a research firm based in Hong Kong .
