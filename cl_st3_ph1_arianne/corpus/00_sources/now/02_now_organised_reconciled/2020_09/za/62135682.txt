<h> What SA can learn from the UK about combatting Covid-19 : Prof Alan Whiteside <p> In this thought provoking article , Professor Alan Whiteside looks at how the UK and South Africa have approached Covid-19 . In South Africa , the government has been praised by many for imposing a strict Covid-19 lockdown in late March . However , that decision also had its fair share of critics . Many lambasted the lockdown , concerned about the effects the nationwide shutdown would have on the economy and businesses . Last week , at a Covid-19 update hosted by Discovery Health , chief actuary Emile Stipp said that the hard lockdown had saved 16,000 lives in South Africa . At the same time , the South African second quarter GDP lost around R750-billion . This , concluded BizNews founder Alec Hogg , translates to roughly ' R46-million for each grateful citizen saved . ' While you certainly can not put a price on a human life , there is no denying the substantial knock the SA economy took as a result of Covid-19 containment .
