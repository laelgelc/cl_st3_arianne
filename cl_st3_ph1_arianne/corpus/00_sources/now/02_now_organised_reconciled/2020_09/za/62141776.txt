It has focused attention on the most vulnerable countries and those parts of society most badly affected by the pandemic . <p> Importantly , the UN has enabled countries to focus on the work that needs to be done to not only to rebuild economies , but to do so in a manner that advances the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals . <p> The UN is leading the effort to ensure that the world that emerges from COVID is better , fairer and more peaceful . <p> In the 75 years of its existence , the UN has proven the value of cooperation and solidarity . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emergencies , transnational crime , conflict and war , climate change , migration or natural disasters -- we must work together .
