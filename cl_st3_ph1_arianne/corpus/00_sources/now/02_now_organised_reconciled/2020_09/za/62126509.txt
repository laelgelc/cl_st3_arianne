Many predictions are made with great care and diligence , yet they are often wrong . <p> The reason is that future inflation is heavily influenced by human traits such as expectations , perceptions and confidence levels . These are all subject to changing mental models , new narratives and emotions . Much like the future direction of the equity market , no one can predict exactly what will happen next . <p> But , despite the inflation bears being wrong for a very long time , what is becoming clearer is that the risks of higher inflation over the medium to longer term are building .
