This is supposed to provide the basis for the Integrated Resource Planning ( IRP ) process for the national electricity system , as a growing part of the energy sector as a whole . <p> There is no need to caution against rapid deployment of renewable energy ( RE ) technologies to make a case for investing in ongoing coal supply for well over a decade ; the case is clear , though not yet the most cost-effective strategy .
