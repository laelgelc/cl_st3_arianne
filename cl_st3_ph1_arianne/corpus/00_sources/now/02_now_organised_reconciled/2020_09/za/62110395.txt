<p> The purpose of the verification guidelines is to support the implementation of the mandatory greenhouse gas reporting regime in South Africa . <p> South Africa is currently implementing a voluntary mitigation system , focusing on the country 's greenhouse gas emission reduction commitments . These are contained in South Africa 's Nationally Determined Contribution ( NDC ) , deposited with United Nations in 2015 . <p> South Africa has committed to mitigating the effects of climate change through the reduction of emissions under the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> In terms of the verification guidelines for reporting GHGs under the National Greenhouse Gas Emissions Reporting Regulations ( NGERs ) , the department said certain processes need to be followed to verify the emissions data and submissions made by data providers . <p> " The guidelines provide further detail required for implementation of Section 11 of the Regulations . This section outlines the legal requirements for verification of information submitted by data providers to the competent authority ( the department ) . <p> " The proposed guidelines provide direction to the competent authority , data providers and independent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ details the responsibilities of these role players . It applies to all anthropogenic emissions by sources , and removals by sinks , " the department explained . <p> The guidelines also outline : <p> The structure of the NGERs Verification Programme ; <p> The independent verification process to be followed ; <p> Accreditation requirements of independent verification bodies , and <p> Important considerations for all role players during the verification process . <p> " The verification guidelines are intended for use in conjunction with the NGERs and the Methodological Guidelines for Reporting of Greenhouse Gas Emissions , " the department said . <p> Members of the public are invited to submit written representations or comments to the minister by September 24 , 2020 . <p> The South Coast Herald has been the ' Leader in Local News ' for the past 78 years .
