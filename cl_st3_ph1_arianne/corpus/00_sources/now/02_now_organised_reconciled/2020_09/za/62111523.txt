<h> Platinum has good lockdown with supply deficit set to widen in 2020 , says WPIC <p> PLATINUM has had a good lockdown , according to the World Platinum Investment Council ( WPIC ) which said on Tuesday demand for the metal outstripped supply in the second quarter . <p> As a result , a supply deficit for 2020 previously forecast by the council is expected to widen to some 336,000 ounces from 247,000 oz . <p> The WPIC also said that the Covid-19 pandemic had accelerated the adoption of the hydrogen economy in which platinum is positioned to play a prominent role . <p> " An evolving global sea-change in the environment debate may also be at play in changing investor sentiment towards platinum , significantly bolstering platinum 's green and precious metal credentials , " the council said . <p> " With the economic impact of Covid-19 depleting climate change funding , there has been growing impetus from China , Japan , South Korea the European Union and the US to prioritise the ' hydrogen economy ' in cost-effectively driving forward the green agenda .
