<h> Sustainable Development Agenda 2030 in the midst of COVID-19 <p> 9th September 2020 By : Creamer Media Reporter <p> The COVID-19 pandemic has come at a time when societies are already facing many challenges related to inequality , poverty , unemployment , and drought and/or lack of access to water amongst others . The cost of the crisis will be felt in years to come , from the traumas and emotional pain of loss of lives to its impact on the economy . The impact of the pandemic on economic , social and political structure is immeasurable . The pandemic will also negatively affect global development objectives , specifically the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> The COVID-19 pandemic has already affected sustainable development in many constructs . The United Nations published a report on the impact of the pandemic in the achievement of the SDG which demonstrated that the pandemic has indeed stalled progress in meeting the goals that are to be achieved by 2030 .
