An increase in rainfall variability results in more frequent flooding and droughts , while the deteriorating quality of water resources adds further stress to the water system . Climate-related impacts are expected to aggravate these challenges as already experienced in some areas across the country . <p> In response , the South African government initiated a process , aligned with international efforts , to define its vision for effectively addressing the impacts of climate change . Through mitigation and adaptation measures , government aims to transition to a climate-resilient , lower carbon economy and society .
