The cost of the crisis will be felt in years to come , from the traumas and emotional pain of loss of lives to its impact on the economy . The impact of the pandemic on economic , social and political structure is immeasurable . The pandemic will also negatively affect global development objectives , specifically the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> Lungile Manzini , corporate sustainability expert <p> The Covid-19 pandemic has already affected sustainable development in many constructs . The United Nations published a report on the impact of the pandemic in the achievement of the SDGs which demonstrated that the pandemic has indeed stalled progress in meeting the goals that are to be achieved by 2030 .
