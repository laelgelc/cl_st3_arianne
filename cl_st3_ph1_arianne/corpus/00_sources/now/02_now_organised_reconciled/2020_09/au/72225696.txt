Healthier lives <p> Getting out into nature and gardening can improve your mental health and physical fitness . Our research suggests that getting involved in urban food growing , or just being exposed to it in our daily lives , may also lead to healthier diets . <p> Urban growers may be driven to make healthier food choices for a whole range of reasons . Our study suggested that urban food growing can also help change attitudes towards food , so that people place more value in produce that 's sustainable , healthy and ethically sourced . <p> 4 .
