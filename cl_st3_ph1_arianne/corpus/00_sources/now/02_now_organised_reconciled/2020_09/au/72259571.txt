<p> COVID-19 has wreaked havoc on the global economy , adding to a growing number of headwinds which include high levels of debt , an ageing population , technological disruption , and environmental disruption brought about through climate change . <p> While we expect COVID-19 to be relatively short-term , we believe these other headwinds are set to persist for an extended period . As such , we think the world is transitioning into a Japanese-style era of economic stagnation . <p> Since the 1990s , the Bank of Japan has engaged in aggressive balance sheet expansion and interest rate suppression to stimulate the economy , while the government issued bonds to finance economic stimulus measures . <p> While Japanese GDP growth was poor in the decade leading up to the GFC , aggregate corporate profit growth was strong , which enabled Value to outperform Growth as an investment style . <p> However , since the GFC Value has significantly underperformed Growth and this is primarily due to low levels of growth in both nominal GDP and aggregate corporate profits .
