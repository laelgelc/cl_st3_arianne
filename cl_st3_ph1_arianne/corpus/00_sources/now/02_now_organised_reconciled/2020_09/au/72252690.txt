No longer operating vertically on the Z-axis and propelled by cables , this new development had a capacity for X-Y-axis movement within buildings using revolutionary flat belt technology . As we now know , another technology curated in deep time and cultured by our hanging out with other species was to take a hand . <p> As a result , two scales are now up against one another -- a metre scale frequented by large structures and a micrometre scale frequented by a pathogen .
