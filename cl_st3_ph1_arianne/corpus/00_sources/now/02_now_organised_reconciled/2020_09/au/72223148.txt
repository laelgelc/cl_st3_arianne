<p> Across the Namadgi , green shoots of hope have sprouted after the devastation of last summer 's fires . But there are some areas which are just as desolate as they were after fires swept through . Australian National University ecologist Dr Ben Scheele visited the eastern part of the park this week , which was one of the areas hardest hit by the Orroral Valley fire due to its hilly terrain and the dry conditions . There 's been little change in the area since April , even though other areas of the park are bouncing back . " Anecdotally , you can see the vegetation is recovering much quicker in areas that were burnt less severely , " Dr Scheele said . Australian National University senior lecturer Dr Marta Yebra flew over Namadgi to assess the damage in the aftermath of the fires . She found 45 per cent of the burnt areas were impacted at a high severity , which could explain the lack of regeneration .
