<h> First woman to sail solo around Antarctica releases debut book <h> In partnership with Australian Geographic , world record-holding sailor Lisa Blair will release her first book ' Facing Fear ' on 1 November , 2020 . This must-read book recounts the inspiring and gripping true story of her solo voyage around Antarctica ; a tale Lisa almost did n't live to tell . <p> A riveting read for people of all ages who are interested in the triumph of the human spirit , Lisa recounts the highlights of her extraordinary voyage . And along the way , she shows readers that positive thinking and perseverance can make a difference . <p> 72 days into her 183-day journey and 1,000 nautical miles from land , Lisa 's mast came crashing down due to rigging failure . She battled in freezing conditions for four hours to save her yacht and her life .
