<h> Wild maple trees ' in serious need of conservation ' <p> One in five maple species are threatened in the wild , according to the first full assessment of extinction risks . <p> Known for the vivid colour of their autumn leaves , the trees are popular in parks and gardens . <p> But in their natural habitats , they face a myriad of threats , including unsustainable logging , climate change , deforestation and forest fires . <p> Botanists are calling for urgent action to protect rare maple trees . <p> And they say seeds should be stored as an insurance policy against extinction . <p> The assessment of all 158 species of maple is part of an effort to map the conservation status of all tree species by the end of 2020 .
