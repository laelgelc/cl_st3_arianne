<p> Watching mob violence in the streets of America , I recalled an encounter in Kosovo in 1999 . Driving through the country one day in a UN vehicle , I stopped at the house of a man who , some months before , had been dragged from his house by Serbian paramilitaries and made to kneel on the dusty road and was poised for execution in front of his seven daughters . <p> It was at the height of the civil war in Kosovo , and the Kosovo Liberation Army and Serbian authorities were embittered foes . The shrill politics of extremes and the whip of war had already unravelled most of former Yugoslavia . Kosovo was the last chapter , and both sides were exacting revenge . Some of the same Serbian paramilitary groups that had committed crimes in Bosnia were marauding in the restive Yugoslav province . <p> A jeep-load of regular Serbian soldiers rounded the corner .
