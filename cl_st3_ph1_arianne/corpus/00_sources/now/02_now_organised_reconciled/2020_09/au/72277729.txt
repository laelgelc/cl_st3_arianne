Apart from being brilliant at their jobs , they are all considered to be autistic by the autism community . <p> It is no coincidence that what makes these characters so good at their jobs are characteristics associated with autistic or non-neurotypical people . Leaving aside The Good Doctor 's and Seven 's savant abilities , they are all logical thinkers , curious , evidence-based decision makers , tenacious , persistent at solving problems and focused . They offer different perspectives and do n't succumb to the sort of groupthink or non-evidence based decision making that lands many companies in trouble . <p> Their tendency to be direct and honest in social interactions can sometimes get these characters into hot water . But it also stops them getting bogged down in office politics .
