<p> A $3 billion renewable energy hub in South Australia will begin construction within the next two years after French-based developer Neoen landed a contract for the first 100MW of wind capacity with the ACT government , with a record low price for Australia . <p> Neoen announced on Tuesday morning that it has been awarded a 14-year contract to supply 100MW of wind energy from Stage 1 of its Goyder Renewables Zone as the result of the Australian Capital Territory government 's latest renewables auction , a win that will also see it build a 50MW big battery in Canberra , with locals offered the opportunity to co-invest . <p> The ACT said the contract was awarded at the price of $44.97/MWh , which is a record low for a publicly disclosed price for a wind auction or power purchase agreement in Australia . <p> Given that this is a fixed and flat price for 14 years , the " real price " for when the contract starts in 2023 relates to $35/MWh to $37/MWh , depending on your assumed inflation rate . There is no direct subsidy for the battery . <p> The ACT has already contracted enough wind and solar to meet the equivalent of all its annual electricity needs , but last year sought a minimum 200MW of wind , or 250MW of solar , plus battery storage , to take into account economic and population growth , and the electrification of transport and building energy use . <p> The second contract awarded on Tuesday by the ACT government is for a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and developer GPG will also build a 10MW/20MWh battery .
