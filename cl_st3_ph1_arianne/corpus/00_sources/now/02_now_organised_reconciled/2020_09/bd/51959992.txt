<h> Bay of Bengal matters <p> As the world economy is shifting towards the Bay of Bengal , Bangladesh should play a role in deepening ties among the seaside countries to harness the potential of marine resources instead of allowing confrontations that can destabilise the region , said foreign relations experts yesterday . <p> They said the Covid-19 pandemic has proved again that all countries are somehow dependent on each other in production and supply chain -- something that requires higher level of cooperation and collaboration . Ironically , rise of protectionist attitudes has been noticed . <p> The observations came at a webinar titled " Increasing political , ecological , economic and regional importance of the Bay of Bengal for Bangladesh in the post-Covid-19 world " . <p> The discussion was organised by the Bay of Bengal Institute Project of Independent University of Bangladesh ( IUB ) , International Centre for Climate Change and Development ( ICCCAD ) and Bangladesh Institute of Maritime Research and Development ( BIMRAD ) . <p> It is the first of a four-part webinar series on the Bay of Bengal .
