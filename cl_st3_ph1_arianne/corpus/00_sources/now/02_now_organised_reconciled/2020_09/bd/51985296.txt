<h> OP-ED : The necessary fusion of climate and the law <p> The successful enactment of laws will play a major role in combating climate change <p> A persistently undermined problem in the world at present is climate change . Climate change is essentially a change in the statistical distribution in the weather patterns and is normally judged by way of the fluctuation of greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions . Because of the pandemic that led to mobility restrictions and brought the world economy to a staggering halt , emission of GHG has fallen by 8% which indicates the biggest fall than in any other year on record . <p> However , the pandemic is an exceptional situation and a permanent solution is necessary . Needless to say , that solution can be reached by way of laws , and such laws must be brought into action right away .
