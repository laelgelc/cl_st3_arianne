One option might be a limited-time donation program that would see every dollar donated by an individual to a particular charity ( up to a limit , of course ) matched by taxpayers . It is especially popular among this government 's left-of-centre vote base . More importantly , nearly 40 per cent of Canadians say they 'd consider donating more to a charitable organization if such a program existed . <p> To be sure , there are a lot of expectations on this government 's throne speech and subsequent budget .
