<h> 5 takeaways from the first Trump-Biden debate <p> President Donald Trump and former Vice President Joe Biden wasted little time in attacking each other Tuesday night in their first one-on-one showdown of the general election . The candidates sparred on everything from the coronavirus pandemic to racism to climate change during the course of the 90-minute debate in Cleveland . <p> But any policy differences were largely lost in a near-constant flurry of interruptions , cross-talk and pleas for decorum by the moderator , Chris Wallace of Fox News . <p> Here are key takeaways from the first debate . <h> Trump 's familiar playbook <p> The president did not do extensive debate prep ahead of Tuesday , but he did come prepared with a plan he deployed early and often : interrupting Biden to attack him , his family , and his record in the Senate and the White House . <p> Trump repeatedly went after the former vice president 's son , Hunter Biden , claiming at one point that he made " tens of millions " in profit illegally from the Ukrainian natural gas company Burisma @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has been found . On Tuesday , Biden was not able to stay above the fray throughout the entire evening -- calling Trump a " clown , " a " racist , " and " the worst president in history " -- but his strategy seemed to be to let Wallace do most of the scolding . <p> That said , Biden rarely was able to break through the three-person cacophony on stage .
