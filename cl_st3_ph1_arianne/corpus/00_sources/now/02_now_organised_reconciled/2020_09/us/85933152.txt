<h> 10 Biggest Solar Companies <h> JKS , CSIQ , and FSLR top the list of the 10 biggest solar companies <p> Governments , businesses and individual consumers increasingly are looking to alternative and sustainable forms of energy beyond fossil-based fuels as signs of climate change escalate . While solar and other alternative energies tend to be more expensive or more difficult to produce compared with traditional fossil fuels , they have the major benefit of leaving behind a significantly smaller environmental impact . Companies attempting to capitalize on increased demand for solar energy have encountered widely disparate levels of success . <p> These are the 10 biggest solar companies by 12-month trailing ( TTM ) revenue . This list is usually limited to companies that are publicly traded in the U.S. or Canada , either directly or through ADRs . But the solar industry is largely based and listed outside of those countries , so excluding many companies would not properly represent the industry .
