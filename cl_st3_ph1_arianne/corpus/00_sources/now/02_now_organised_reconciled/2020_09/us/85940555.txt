<h> More than 150 countries made a plan to preserve biodiversity a decade ago . A new report says they mostly failed . <p> Ten years ago , more than 150 countries set a list of goals to achieve by 2020 that aimed to improve the world 's biodiversity . On Tuesday , the United Nations announced that out of the 20 targets , known as the " Aichi Targets , " the countries fully met none of them . <p> The targets were meant to do five things : Promote mainstream biodiversity across government and society , reduce the direct pressures on biodiversity and promote sustainable use , improve the status of biodiversity , enhance the benefits of biodiversity , and enhance the implementation of these practices . <p> Only six of the 20 targets were even partially achieved -- two of which were partial accomplishments that were supposed to have been completed in 2015 . <p> Increase financial resources in some countries to support biodiversity , although the total resources are not sufficient in relation to needs and there is still overarching support for activities that harm biodiversity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are not enough .
