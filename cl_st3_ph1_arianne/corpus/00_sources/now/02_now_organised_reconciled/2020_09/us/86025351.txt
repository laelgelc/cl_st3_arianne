<p> LOS ANGELES , Sept. 28 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Today The Wonderful Company , aglobal company dedicated to harvesting health and happiness around the world through its iconic consumer brands , announced the launch of the Wonderful Innovation Challenge .
