<h> The End of the Universe Will Probably Be Fairly Disappointing <p> Illustration : Michael Allen Siebold/Getty Images <p> Katie Mack , an assistant professor of physics at North Carolina State University , is quickly becoming one of the internet 's most popular science communicators . In her first book , The End of Everything ( Astrophysically Speaking ) , she explores various scenarios for the end of the universe . <p> " I noticed that when I gave public talks and talked about the end of the universe , that was something that people got really excited about , " Mack says in Episode 430 of the Geek 's Guide to the Galaxy podcast . " It was something that I thought I could have a lot of fun with , and I did . I really enjoyed writing this book .
