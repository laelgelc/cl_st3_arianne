( Noah Berger/AP ) <p> By Associated Press <p> September 9 , 2020 at 7:28 PM EDT <p> Wildfires raged unchecked across parts of the western United States on Wednesday , with gusty winds forecast to spread the fires . <p> In California , Diablo winds in the north and Santa Ana winds in the south stoked fires that had grown explosively . In Washington state , more acres burned in one day than firefighters usually see all year . Fires also forced people to flee in Oregon and Idaho . <p> A temperature drop of as much as 60 degrees helped contain wildfires that spread in Colorado and Montana in the hot , windy weather .
