<p> The total cost of the project is $52 million , according to Jason Shea , a project manager for the Army Corps . The first phase cost $31.5 million , while the second phase will cost $20.5 million . <p> That burden is split between the federal , state and local levels . The federal government pays for 65% of the project . The remaining 35% will be shared , with the state paying three-quarters of that amount and local authorities assuming the rest . <p> In total , the project brings 2.7 million cubic yards of new sand onto the beaches in the three towns . The first phase used 1.7 million cubic yards , and the second round is slated to add another million cubic yards . <p> In this phase , beaches will be enlarged in Long Branch from South Broadway to Joline Avenue , extending partially into Seven Presidents Park . In Monmouth Beach , sand will be added from Cottage Road north , reaching to Garden Way in Sea Bright .
