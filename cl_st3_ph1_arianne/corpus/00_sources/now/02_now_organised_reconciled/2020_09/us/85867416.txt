<h> Thousands of Angelenos Left Without Power In Triple Digit Heat <p> LOS ANGELES , CA -- Thousands of residents across Los Angeles County remained without electricity Sunday on what is anticipated to be among the hottest days ever recorded in the region . <p> Southern California Edison and the Los Angeles Department of Water and Power both scrambled to restore power Sunday amid the deadly heatwave . Some DWP customers may not get their power back until Monday . Since Saturday , the utility had restored power to more than 37,000 customers , but about 7,000 customers still had no electricity Sunday . At noon , SCE officials said they might have to cut power to circuits in high fire-risk areas in some cities and unincorporated areas in Los Angeles and Ventura counties , including Malibu . <p> The outages come as state officials ask residents to conserve electricity to avoid the very real threat of rolling blackouts .
