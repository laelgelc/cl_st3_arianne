Today 's weather starts out with a slight chance of showers , and then will be mainly cloudy , with a high of 20 C and widespread smoke from the U.S. wildfires , according to Environment and Climate Change Canada . <p> The fine particulate matter advisory issued Sept. 8 for Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley because of smoke from the U.S. wildfires remains in effect . <p> The agency says there may be a slight improvement in air quality today but ground-level smoke will remain , possibly until later this week . <p> The poor air quality resulted in Canada Post suspending mail delivery service on Monday in several parts of the province , stating the conditions were unsafe for workers .
