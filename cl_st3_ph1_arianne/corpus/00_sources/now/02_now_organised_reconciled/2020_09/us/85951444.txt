<h> Former Parody Account Was Early in Spreading False Rumors on Fires <p> One of the earliest Twitter accounts to circulate false online rumors that the wildfires in Oregon had been started by activists was one that began as a parody , according to research by the Atlantic Council 's Digital Forensic Research Lab . <p> The account , called @ScarsdaleAntifa and purporting to represent an anti-fascist group from Scarsdale , N.Y. , was started in 2017 by a user of the message board 4chan and tried to " troll or confuse " antifa supporters , according to the lab , which investigates misinformation . <p> The account staged and promoted a fake protest in 2017 , using images from other protests . But this month , the account started deleting all of its previous posts . Once its history of trolling was erased , it posted a new claim : that members of its group had set wildfires to raise awareness about climate change . <p> The message was amplified by other inauthentic accounts and shared hundreds of times , the researchers found .
