<h> Neglected Crops Could Be The Global Solution For Food Insecurity <p> Globally , an over-reliance on a few staple crops , namely maize , wheat and rice , has resulted in limited dietary diversity -- a leading cause of persistent malnutrition . These few foods represent more than half of the world 's calorie intake and have typically played a central role in the fight against food security , but to no avail -- hunger , inequality and non-communicable diseases continue to rise . Perhaps it is time to try something new . <p> Despite the fact that there are more than 30,000 identified edible plant species in existence , only 6,000 to 7,000 are used for food and only 170 are grown commercially , while approximately 30 species fulfil 95 per cent of the world 's calorie requirements , as factory-style farming systems continue to contribute to the overwhelming degradation of the world 's natural resources and contribute to 26 per cent of total global greenhouse gas emissions .
