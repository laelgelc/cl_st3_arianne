The team found that the number of lakes increased to over 14,300 from roughly 9,400 , an uptick of more than 50 percent . That makes sense , Dr. Shugar and his colleagues proposed , because climate change is warming the Arctic faster than other parts of the world . <p> All this growth is troubling , Dr. Shugar and his research team members suggest , because glacial lakes , by their very nature , can pose significant danger to downstream communities . <p> Some glacial lakes sit in bowl-shaped depressions bordered by glacial moraine , the often unstable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ moraine collapses , glacial lake water can course downslope in an outburst flood . <p> These events , which have occurred from Nepal to Peru to Iceland , can be devastating .
