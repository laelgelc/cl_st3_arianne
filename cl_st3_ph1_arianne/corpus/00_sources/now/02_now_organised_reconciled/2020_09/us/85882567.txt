<h> Ratings agencies see reinsurance rate rises as COVID-19 losses bite <p> LONDON , Sept 8 ( Reuters ) - Ratings agencies Moody 's and S&amp;P Global said on Tuesday they expected global reinsurance premium rates to rise at the key January renewal season as companies seek to recoup some of their COVID-19 related losses . <p> The pandemic is expected to lead to as much as $100 billion in losses for non-life insurers due to claims from businesses hit by event cancellations and other disruptions . <p> Reinsurers share the burden of large losses with insurers in return for part of the premium . <p> Reinsurance rates were on a downward trend in recent years due to strong competition , until heavy losses from natural catastrophes in 2017 started to turn the trend . <p> Rate rises in January 2021 were likely to be in the " high single digits " of percentage points , Moody 's analyst Antonello Aquino told a media briefing , while S&amp;P Global analyst Charles-Marie Delpuech told a separate briefing the firm expected rate rises in the mid-single digits . <p> Fitch Ratings also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ doubt-digit percentage point range . <p> Global reinsurer Swiss Re also said on Tuesday it was optimistic for renewals and expected price increases across all segments to continue to be driven by low interest rates , large claims and growing risks . <p> The ratings agencies were downbeat on the outlook for reinsurers , given the COVID-19 dent to their profitability . <p> Moody 's cut its ratings outlook for global reinsurers to negative from stable on Tuesday , citing the pandemic and subsequent economic downturn , as well as the longer term impact of climate change on natural catastrophe losses . <p> S&amp;P Global retained a negative outlook for the sector . <p> The ratings agencies usually update their outlooks ahead of an annual reinsurance event in Monte Carlo each September . The event is canceled this year .
