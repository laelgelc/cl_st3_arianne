Now , Musk has said on Twitter that he hopes to one day provide similar air filtration along with home HVAC systems . <p> Tesla , while primarily an automaker , is also already in the business of home energy and power generation , thanks to its acquisition of SolarCity , its current production of solar roofing products , and its business building Tesla batteries for storage of power generated from green sources at home .
