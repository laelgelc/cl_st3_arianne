<p> Many Minnesotans are opting to vote by mail ahead of Tuesday 's primary . As of Friday , more than 624,000 voters had requested absentee ballots for the Aug. 11 vote , up from about 35,000 at the same point in 2016 . Roughly 375,000 of those ballots have already been accepted and processed , according to the Secretary of State 's office . Those numbers are sure to rise in the coming days , as Minnesotans continue to vote early in person or via mail . <p> So what happens after you seal , sign and send in your vote ? That rule has been waived in light of the pandemic . <p> You can submit your completed and sealed ballot by mail or drop off at your county 's election office .
