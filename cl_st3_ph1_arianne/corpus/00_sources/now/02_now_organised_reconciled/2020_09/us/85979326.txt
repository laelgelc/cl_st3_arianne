This is a port-over from the corporate world , where management tends to focus on problems that can be solved in 90 days . This comes from a human desire to link cause and effect . It 's why we have to-do lists . We like to get shit done . <p> Some of the problems we 're dealing with now -- like climate change -- wo n't be solved in 90 days .
