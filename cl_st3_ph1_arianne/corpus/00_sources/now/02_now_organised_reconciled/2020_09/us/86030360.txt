<h> Candidates need to address the nuclear threat <p> At the debates , the presidential candidates must discuss their strategy for reducing nuclear weapons . For as long as nukes exist , we live with the danger of accidental launch , terrorist theft or a miscalculation leading to nuclear war . <p> The United States , Russia and China are headed toward a dangerous arms race . Building up nuclear arsenals adds to global tensions and also drains precious resources away from society . <p> All nations would be better off if nuclear weapons spending were diverted towards fighting hunger , disease , poverty and climate change . <p> The new Strategic Arms Reduction Treaty has not been extended , meaning we may lose a key pact limiting nuclear arsenals with Russia .
