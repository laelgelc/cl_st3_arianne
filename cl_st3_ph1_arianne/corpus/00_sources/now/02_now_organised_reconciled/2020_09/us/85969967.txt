<h> Five Things About The Pantanal <p> The Pantanal , the world 's biggest tropical wetlands , is being devastated by record wildfires . <p> Here are five things to know about this unique ecosystem , a UNESCO World Heritage Site . <h> What are wetlands ? <p> Wetlands are regions that are largely covered in water part or all of the year . <p> Other major wetlands include the West Siberian Lowland , the Congo River Basin and the Mississippi River Basin . <p> These watery ecosystems tend to be covered in aquatic plants specially adapted to their hydric soil . <p> The Pantanal , which is typically 80-percent underwater in wet season , is also known for its wealth of wildlife . <p> The annual rains , which start in October , bring huge numbers of fish into the floodplain , drawing numerous bird species and , in turn , predators going up the food chain . <h> Where is the Pantanal ? <p> Situated below the Amazon rainforest , the Pantanal stretches from western Brazil into Bolivia and Paraguay . <p> About 62 percent of the Pantanal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ area is estimated at 224,253 square kilometers ( 86,585 square miles ) , a little smaller than the United Kingdom . <h> Why is it burning ? <p> The Pantanal is having its worst drought in 47 years . <p> Rainfall plunged by half for the period from January to May , usually the height of rainy season . <p> Researchers are still studying the factors driving the drought . <p> Climate change is a top suspect . <p> Studies show deforestation in the Amazon is having an impact on rainfall in other regions by shrinking the rainforest 's so-called " flying rivers " : clouds of mist that dump water across a large swathe of South America . <p> The fires are also being driven by the conversion of land for agricultural use and the introduction of non-native plant species more susceptible to fire . <h> How bad is it ? <p> An estimated 23,500 square kilometers , more than 10 percent of the Pantanal , have gone up in smoke since January . <p> There have been a record-shattering 14,764 fires in the Brazilian Pantanal this year , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Institute ( INPE ) -- already an annual record , and increase of 214 percent from the same period last year . <h> What is at stake ? <p> The Pantanal is one of the most biodiverse regions on Earth , with nearly 1,300 animal species and more than 3,500 plant species , according to the World Wildlife Fund . <p> Disruptions to the water cycle threaten its delicate ecosystem , whose famous wildlife includes the endangered hyacinth macaw and shrinking population of jaguars . <p> This copy is for your personal , non-commercial use only .
