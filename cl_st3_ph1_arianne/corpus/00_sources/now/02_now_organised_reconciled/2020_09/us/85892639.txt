Reforming education . Protecting victims of domestic violence . Supporting the arts . Housing the homeless . Feeding the hungry . <p> All are worthy of our energy and consideration . Working on these issues makes our society richer , kinder , more efficient and just . Addressing them makes life more meaningful . And in some ways , these issues define who we are as a country and as a civilization . <p> Many view climate change action as just another cause , one of the many things on a list that we probably should care about when we have more time or energy . It will impact their happiness and ability to succeed more than what school they go to or job they obtain because unchecked climate change will significantly disrupt or break the economic and natural systems that our entire society is built on .
