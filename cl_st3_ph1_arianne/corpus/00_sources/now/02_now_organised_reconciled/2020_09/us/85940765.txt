<p> This week , America 's West Coast found itself on fire . Millions of acres have burned across California , Oregon and Washington . The smoke clouds have been so immense that they have blotted out the sun in certain areas ; air quality has been so poor that hundreds of thousands of people have been forced indoors . <p> There are several key reasons for the extent of these wildfires . Federal and state policies have been geared around stifling wildfires for decades , rather than allowing controlled burns to prevent accretion of flammable vegetation .
