Imagine that either Joe Biden or Donald Trump wins the 2020 election in a close race . There is a surge in voter-suppression claims and mail-in ballot controversies . <p> What happens then ? Well , according to a scenario planning exercise at the Transition Integrity Project , a bipartisan coalition of former officials concerned about the disruptions to the 2020 election , the result in every scenario except a Biden landslide would be " street-level violence and political crisis .
