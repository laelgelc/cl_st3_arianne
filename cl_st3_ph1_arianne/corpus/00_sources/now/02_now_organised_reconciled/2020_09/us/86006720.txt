<h> FEATURE-'Be prepared ' : With water scarcer , Egypt pushes farmers to use much less <p> MANSOURIYA , Egypt , Sept 24 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Hassan Abdel Salam knew he was wasting a lot of water every time he flooded his fields to irrigate his mango crop , but was n't aware of any alternative . <p> Then the farmer from northern Egypt learned about drip irrigation - putting tiny amounts of water just where it 's needed - through a new government scheme . <p> Now pipes with small holes snake around his 10-feddan ( 10-acre ) farm , dripping water directly to the base of each plant . <p> Since adopting the method at the start of the year , the 53-year-old said his farm in the village of Mansouriya , near Giza , uses at least 25% less water , fertiliser and energy and his harvests have increased by nearly 20% . <p> " It was a difficult decision for me at first because I was not familiar with the new system , " he told the Thomson Reuters Foundation . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it has a lot of benefits , I started applying it and it turned out to be beneficial , as I was told .
