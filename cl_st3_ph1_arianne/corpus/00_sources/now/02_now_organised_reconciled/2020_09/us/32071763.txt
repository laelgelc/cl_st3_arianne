<p> Global Citizen and HeadCount have announced Just Vote , a nonpartisan campaign that aims to get 50,000 young people registered to vote before the 2020 election . Artists supporting the campaign include Billie Eilish and her brother FINNEAS , Taylor Swift , Quavo , DJ Khaled , Billy Porter , Usher , and Nicky Jam . <p> " We are proud to launch the ' Just Vote ' campaign to encourage more young people to check their status and register to vote , " Hugh Evans ( CEO and co-founder of Global Citizen ) said in a press release . " For years , we have been activating global citizens to be part of the solution to key issues like extreme poverty , climate change , and now pandemic relief . Voting is a vital way to exercise that power . We are excited about ' Just Vote ' ' s potential to drive young voter turnout in November and beyond . "
