<p> August ( not April ) is the cruellest month , certainly for journalists . The days are long , copy is repetitive and especially during a great health scare , the search for novel thoughts or some change in the story lineup can get a little desperate . <p> Laptops wo n't clack-clack on their own . Like cats , they have to be fed . In this valley , or more properly vacuity , of non-news , the lazy plodder reaches for Meghan Markle and the ex-royal consort to fertilize a script ; the shameless get a new angle on the steatopygous Kardashian clan and those who wander the squalid trenches of Ottawa and federal politics -- well , they reach for the timeless : " Could we see a fall election ? " <p> I therefore discarded this type of speculation as the tired reflex of journalists under strain during the slow season . My attitude on this point has , however , taken a turn .
