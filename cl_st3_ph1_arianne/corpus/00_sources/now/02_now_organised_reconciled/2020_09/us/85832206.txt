<h> Webinar for Educators : Exploring Climate Migration in the Classroom <h> Event Details : <p> Scientists project that with every degree of temperature increase the Earth experiences , approximately one billion people will be displaced . <p> Educators are invited to join Pulitzer Center education staff and journalist-grantees for a professional development webinar on migration and its relationship to climate change . By engaging with a pathbreaking new reporting project from ProPublica and The New York Times Magazine , in partnership with the Pulitzer Center , participants will be able to ... <p> explore new models for understanding how migration will change as the world warms , hear the stories of today 's climate refugees from Central America , <p> evaluate action steps to keep the planet habitable for human life , <p> and identify interactive methods for sharing under-reported stories on migration with students . <p> Webinar attendees will receive a 1-hour certificate of attendance upon completion of a short survey .
