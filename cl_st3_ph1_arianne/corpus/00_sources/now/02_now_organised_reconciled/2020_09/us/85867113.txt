<p> When COVID-19 broke out , Henna Hundal and Sai Rajagopal recognized an opportunity to connect international leaders in an attempt to curb the spread of the virus . They developed the Bridging Borders Project ( **26;1564;TOOLONG ) , an online platform to catalog video interviews with world political leaders about their pandemic response procedures . The pair of 2019 Harvard University graduates began publishing their work in May , releasing a new interview each week . So far , Hundal and Rajagopal have uploaded 10 of the 40 episodes they plan to unveil , each running roughly 15 minutes . <p> Q.
