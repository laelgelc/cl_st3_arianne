<h> How to Draw Social Change <p> The New Yorker 's cartoon editor , Emma Allen , and the artist Liz Montague talk about diversity in comics and finding humor in moments of unrest . <p> Liz Montague first contacted The New Yorker by sending in an incredibly thoughtful letter expressing her disappointment with the dearth of cartoonists of color in the magazine 's pages . I was forwarded that letter and -- newly minted as the cartoon editor , and viewing it as my top prerogative to find a way to publish all the great , diverse voices working in comics -- was thrilled to talk with someone who expressed so well the frustration that I also felt about the historically white-male skew of our cartoonists . ( How many all-white-male boardroom cartoons had I read and internalized as a kid , while my mom was working crazy hours to become one of the very few female partners in a top New York City law firm at the time ? ) Who , I wrote back , did Liz think I should be publishing ?
