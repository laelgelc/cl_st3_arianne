A pandemic ; civil protests against longstanding inequities , racism , and violence ; more frequent and stronger hurricanes ( the recent hurricane Laura packed record-tying winds at 150 mph at landfall ) ; and the most monstrous wildfires we have seen in recorded history , are all happening now . <p> It was only three months ago that our own Santa Catalina Mountains were burning . Our country has seen record-breaking heat , record temperature swings , and record winds all in the past two weeks .
