He is not Donald Trump . He is , by contrast , a thinking , feeling person who engages with and cares very deeply about people who are not Joe Biden . A vote for Biden is a vote to stop a blood tide of madness and pandemic . What really matters is that Joe Biden is not Donald Trump . <p> That 's a taut summary of the foundational ideas upon which the Democratic National Convention has been built .
