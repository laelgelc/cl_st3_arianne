<h> The future of farming in Africa is not agriculture but agribusiness <p> Africa is a farm lover 's dream : abundant uncultivated arable land , roughly over half the global total ; tropical climates that permit long growing seasons ; a young labor force ; and an expanding population that provides a readily available market for produce consumption . <p> Yet , African countries are yet to harness these opportunities to ensure sustainable food security and food production . The average age of farmers is about 60 years -- in a continent where 60% of the population is under 24 years of age .
