Gavin Newsom has yet taken to combat the causes of climate change . <p> Newsom signed an executive order Wednesday requiring that all new passenger vehicles sold in California by 2035 be zero-emission -- a category that includes battery-powered electric cars and those that run on hydrogen fuel cells . The transportation sector makes up more than 40% of California 's greenhouse gas emissions , and has been stubbornly growing .
