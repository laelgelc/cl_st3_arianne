<p> The longer society waits to address climate change , the more likely climate will change society , according to BP 's chief economist . <p> The UK energy giant tries to anticipate that outcome in its latest Annual Energy Outlook , which includes a " Delayed and Disorderly " scenario for the energy transition . <p> " The underlying philosophy of Delayed and Disorderly is the belief that the longer you remain on an unsustainable path , the increasing likelihood that society will change , " said BP economist Spencer Dale , " and societal preferences will change in such a way that it will trigger a decisive shift in carbon emissions . " <p> BP also forecasts a business-as-usual scenario and a rapid transition .
