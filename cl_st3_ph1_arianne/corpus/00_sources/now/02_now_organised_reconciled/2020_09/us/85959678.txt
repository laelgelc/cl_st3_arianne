<p> TORONTO ( Reuters ) - Canada launched a fund on Thursday to invest in community-based projects to curb emissions , with bulk of the money coming from a fine that Volkswagen AG paid for breaking the country 's diesel emissions rules . <p> The C$206 million ( $157 million ) fund is part of the government 's new Climate Action and Awareness Fund which seeks to boost climate science research by empowering youth and communities . <p> " The new Climate Action and Awareness Fund will create jobs for Canadians in science and technology , academia , and at the grassroots community level , " Johnathan Wilkinson , minister of environment and climate change , said in statement . <p> In January , a judge in Canada approved a C$196.5 million fine against Volkswagen after the company pleaded guilty to dozens of counts of diesel emissions violations .
