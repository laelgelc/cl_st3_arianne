<h> FACTBOX-What are the micro-organisms causing elephant deaths in Botswana ? <p> By Reuters Staff <p> 3 Min Read <p> ( Adds codes for additional clients , no change to text ) <p> JOHANNESBURG , Sept 21 ( Reuters ) - Botswana wildlife officials said on Monday that toxins produced by microscopic organisms called cyanobacteria had caused the deaths of 330 elephants this year . <p> Since they were first reported around early May , the elephant deaths had baffled and alarmed conservationists , who feared they could escalate . <p> Below are some facts about cyanobacteria . <h> WHAT ARE THEY ? <p> Cyanobacteria are micro-organisms that are common in water and also occur in soil . Some can produce toxins that damage the liver or nervous system of animals and humans . <p> Sometimes called blue-green algae , they have algae-like characteristics such as using sunlight to make food . <h> HOW DO THEY SPREAD ? <p> Cyanobacteria proliferate in warm , nutrient-rich conditions . <p> When they multiply they can create blooms that spread across the surface of a water body and discolour it . <p> Fertilisers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nutrient loading . <h> HOW IS CLIMATE CHANGE INVOLVED ? <p> Many cyanobacteria favour warmer water temperatures , which scientists say are occurring more frequently because of climate change . <p> Southern Africa 's temperatures are rising at twice the global average , making the region especially vulnerable . <h> WHAT CAN BE DONE ? <p> Once cyanobacterial blooms form , there is no simple solution to get rid of them , according Luyanda Ndlela , a researcher at South Africa 's national science council . <p> Scientists like Ndlela are trying to identify substances that can break down cyanotoxins or control the cyanobacteria themselves without disrupting the ecosystem . <p> Experts are also trying to develop models to predict when cyanobacterial blooms might occur . <h> IS THIS ONLY HAPPENING IN AFRICA ? <p> No .
