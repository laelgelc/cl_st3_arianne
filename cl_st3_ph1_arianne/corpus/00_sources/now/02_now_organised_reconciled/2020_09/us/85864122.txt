<p> Seventeen-year-old Arun Mommileti was devoting his time to hand sanitization long before the COVID-19 pandemic . The senior at King 's Academy in Sunnyvale launched a nonprofit called United2Care in 2017 specifically to increase access to personal hygiene products around the world , and has quickly pivoted to tackling today 's public health challenges . <p> Arun 's global campaign began as a middle school project . His science teacher asked students to choose a social problem in the world to write about , but Arun struggled with what to pick -- there were , as he points out , quite a few choices . He began to research and came across a few places that mentioned the importance of hand sanitization , and it piqued his interest . He said his dad had his own stories of growing up in a rural community in India , of how unsanitary the conditions were . <p> " So that inspired me to try to look into it , but since I was in seventh grade , I kind of said , ' Oh , what can I do ?
