<p> Eric Voegelin was born in 1901 . In 1919 , he enrolled in the University of Vienna . He studied Karl Marx , read " Das Kapital " and embraced Marxism . Therefore , he abandoned socialism and its assumptions of race and class conflict and became a member of the Austrian economics clan . <p> In addition to having a first-hand account of the ways of Vladimir Lenin , Voegelin also had a front-row seat to the rise of Adolf Hitler . He understood the ideology of National Socialism inside and out .
