<h> Transition to Electric Vehicles Would Save Alabama Lives <h> New report finds Alabama could save lives , avoid millions in local health impacts through transition to EV <h> Sep 15 , 2020 10:38 am CT <p> ( American Lung Association ) <p> Today , the American Lung Association released " The Road to Clean Air " -- a new report that outlines the benefits of a transition to an electric transportation sector increasingly powered by clean , non-combustion renewable energy over the coming decades . The report finds a transition to electric cars , buses and trucks by mid-century would both improve air quality and address climate change , benefitting the lives and health of Americans . In Alabama , this transition could result in avoiding 527 asthma attacks annually , and more than $431 million in public health benefits . <p> " The transportation sector is a leading contributor to air pollution and climate change , " said William Barrett , director of advocacy , clean air for the Lung Association .
