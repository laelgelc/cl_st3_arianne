<h> Home of ' Asian unicorn ' becomes nature reserve in Vietnam <p> Research by the University of Leeds has helped secure the highest government protection for **25;8551;TOOLONG Vietnamese forests . <p> Over the past five years , conservation organization Viet Nature , and its partners World Land Trust , IUCN National Committee of the Netherlands ( IUCN NL ) , Birdlife International and the University of Leeds have been working to protect the Khe Nuoc Trong forests -- the last substantial area of lowland forest in Vietnam . <p> In August , the Vietnamese government agreed to formally protect Khe Nuoc Trong 's 22,132-hectare tract of Annamite lowland evergreen forests as a Nature Reserve , the country 's highest standard of protection . <p> The move delivers a safer home for 40 globally threatened species , brought to brink of extinction by loggers and poachers .
