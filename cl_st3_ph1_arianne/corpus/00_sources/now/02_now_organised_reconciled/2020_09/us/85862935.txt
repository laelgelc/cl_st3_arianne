Pardee School of Global Studies at Boston University , delivered a keynote address during the Danish Refugee Council 's ( DRC ) virtual Global Event on Climate Change , Environmental Degradation and Forced Displacement on September 1 , 2020 . The event convened experts , practitioners , and other collaborators from different fields as they shared , explored , and connected ideas related to the nexus of climate change , forced displacement and forced displacement . <p> During his remarks , Najam spoke about pressing climate challenges , the failures that have led to the dire global climate crisis , and how the world must come together in the Age of Adaptation . He argued that the discussion around climate change needs to shift away from carbon management to human development .
