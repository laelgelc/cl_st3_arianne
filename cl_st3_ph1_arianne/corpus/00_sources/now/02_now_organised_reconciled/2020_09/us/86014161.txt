Marine heatwaves destroy ecosystems and damage fisheries . <p> Share : <p> FULL STORY <p> A marine heatwave ( ocean heatwave ) is an extended period of time in which the water temperature in a particular ocean region is abnormally high . In recent years , heatwaves of this kind have caused considerable changes to the ecosystems in the open seas and at the coast . Their list of negative effects is long : Marine heatwaves can lead to increased mortality among birds , fish and marine mammals , they can trigger harmful algal blooms , and greatly reduce the supply of nutrients in the ocean .
