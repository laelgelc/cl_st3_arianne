<h> TMCnet News <p> Canada and British Columbia invest in green energy solutions <p> September 01 , 2020 <h> Canada and British Columbia invest in green energy solutions <p> VANCOUVER , BC , Sept. 1 , 2020 /CNW/ - The governments of Canada and British Columbia are investing in infrastructure to get projects under way that meet people 's needs , address the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic , and help communities restart their economies . <p> Strategic investments in green infrastructure are key to creating clean healthy communities , making life more affordable , and building a sustainable future . <p> Today , the Honourable Jonathan Wilkinson , Minister of Environment and Climate Change and Member of Parliament for North Vancouver , on behalf of the Honourable Catherine McKenna , Minister of Infrastructure and Communities , and the Honourable George Heyman , B.C. Minister of Environment and Climate Change Strategy , announced funding for 11 projects to help B.C. communities save energy and reduce pollution . <p> In Vancouver , the Sewage Heat Recovery Expansion Project will increase the capacity of the Neighbourhood Energy Utility ( NEU ) to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and hot water . The NEU recycles waste heat and uses a mix of renewable and conventional natural gas to reduce harmful emissions . <p> Funding is also going towards expanding the network of Level-2 electric vehicle ( EV ) charging stations across the province . More than 80 new stations will be installed in communities across mid-Vancouver Island , as well as northern and central B.C. , making clean transportation options more viable for more people . <p> These , along with the other projects announced today , will create jobs and strengthen local economies now while promoting sustainable growth and residents ' long-term health and well-being . <p> The Government of Canada is investing more than $28.5 million in these projects through the Green Infrastructure Stream ( GIS ) of the Investing in Canada plan , and local and Indigenous communities are contributing more than $13 million . The Government of British Columbia is contributing nearly $18 million through the CleanBC Communities Fund , part of the federal Investing in Canada plan 's Green Infrastructure Stream . <p> Quotes <p> " Expanding electric vehicle charging stations across Vancouver @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
