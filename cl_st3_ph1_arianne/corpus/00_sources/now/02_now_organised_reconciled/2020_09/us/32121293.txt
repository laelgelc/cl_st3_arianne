<p> California Gov . Gavin Newsom issued an executive order Wednesday to phase out sales of new gasoline-fueled vehicles by 2035 in a bid to combat climate change amid a record-breaking wildfire season marked by weather he called " unprecedented . " <p> Newsom also called on legislators to send him a bill next year banning hydraulic fracking by oil companies . <p> California would be the first state in the nation to ban sales of internal combustion engines , though the governor said 15 other countries including China , Germany , France and Norway have already " established benchmark goals . " <p> Newsom called the order a " bold and big step " that would promote innovation and create jobs . <p> " We want more choice . We want cleaner air , " he said . " We want to increase our manufacturing base and we want to increase our expertise . That 's what we think we are significantly advancing here . " <p> The governor clarified that the order would not ban people from owning gas-powered vehicles , but it would end the sales of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of nearly 40 million people . <p> " You can still keep your internal combustion car , " Newsom said . " You can still have a market for used cars . We 're providing an abundance of new opportunities . " <p> California and the roughly dozen states that follow its lead on auto emissions standards make up a significant part of the U.S. auto market , giving the day 's move huge potential impact for the U.S. automobile industry as well as for long-term efforts against pollution and climate change , which is driven by fossil-fuel emissions . It also is likely to meet opposition from President Donald Trump , who wants to roll back tougher Obama-era auto emissions standards and is battling California to force it to comply . <p> California already has rules mandating a certain percentage of new car sales must be electric or zero-emission vehicles . This rule , if implemented , would make California the first U.S. state with a plan to phase them out completely . <p> Newsom 's order directs the California Air Resources Board to develop and approve regulations to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ make a rule requiring all medium and heavy-duty trucks be 100% zero-emission vehicles by 2045 " where feasible . " <p> Newsom also directed state agencies to speed up development of charging stations across the state and called on the Legislature to eliminate new fracking licenses by 2024 . <p> Fracking is a technique that allows energy companies to extract huge volumes of oil and gas from shale rock deep underground . It involves injecting high-pressure mixtures of water , sand or gravel and chemicals into rock . Fracking opponents say the chemicals involved threaten water supplies and public health . <p> California has a goal of relying 100% on clean , renewable energy by 2045 . Gasoline and diesel-powered cars and trucks are the biggest impediment to reaching that goal as they account for more than half of the state 's carbon pollution . <p> The order comes as massive wildfires have burned a record 5,600 square miles ( 14,500 square kilometers ) in California this year . Experts say the size and intensity of the fires are aided by warmer temperatures and years of drought brought on by climate change .
