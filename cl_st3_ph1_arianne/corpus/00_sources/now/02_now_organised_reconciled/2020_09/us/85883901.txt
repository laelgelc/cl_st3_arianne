<p> If last year 's documentary darling " Honeyland " tickled your fancy , get ready for its tasty spiritual successor . They 're guided by a secret culture passed down through generations , as well as by the noses of their cherished and expertly trained dogs . The documentary subtly explores the devastating effects of climate change and deforestation on an age-old tradition through a visually stunning narrative that celebrates life and exalts the human spirit .
