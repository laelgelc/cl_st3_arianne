<h> Monitoring the World 's Water Access and Use <p> by Andrew Rzepa , Sera L. Young and Alexandros Makarigakis <p> For billions of people worldwide , sufficient , safe and sustainably managed water is not something they can take for granted . And the limited resources they have at their disposal are under increasing threat from a range of climate-related , infrastructural and political reasons . This is more evident among the most vulnerable populations who often suffer the most . However , until recently , researchers and policymakers were not able to track inequalities in living in a water secure environment in a globally comparable way . <p> Northwestern University and UNESCO are working with Gallup to expand surveys that include the Household Water Insecurity Experiences ( HWISE ) scale to 31 countries in Africa , Asia , Central and South America .
