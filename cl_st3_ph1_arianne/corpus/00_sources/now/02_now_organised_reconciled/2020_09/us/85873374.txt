<p> A resurgence of pubic lice as the popularity of intimate waxing declines has been predicted by dermatologists presenting at the virtual 2020 Annual Meeting of the British Association of Dermatologists ( BAD ) last week . <p> While change in attitudes towards body hair around the millennium , the so-called ' Sex and the City factor , ' alongside a rise in over-the-counter treatments and improved sexual health awareness put the pubic lice population under considerable strain , the situation is now changing , the meeting heard . <p> Dermatologists from Norfolk and Norwich University Hospital ( NNUH ) outlined three factors for their belief that pubic lice are here to stay : <p> Changing social attitudes towards hair removal , with less pressure to remove pubic hair , provides pubic lice with a habitat and makes transmission of pubic lice easier . <p> Resistance among pubic lice towards common chemical treatments such as permethrin is increasing , making eradication harder .
