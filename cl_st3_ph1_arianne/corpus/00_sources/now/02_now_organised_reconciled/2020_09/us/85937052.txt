<h> New Zealand to require financial firms report climate change risks <p> FILE PHOTO : New Zealand 's Prime Minister-designate Jacinda Ardern speaks as she stands next to New Zealand Green Party leader James Shaw in Wellington , New Zealand , October 24 , 2017 . REUTERS/Nicolaci da Costa <p> ( Reuters ) - New Zealand will be the first country in the world to require the financial sector to report on climate risks , its minister for climate change James Shaw said on Tuesday . <p> Businesses covered by the requirements will have to make annual disclosures covering governance arrangements , risk management and strategies for mitigating any climate change impacts . <p> Those unable to disclose must explain why , Shaw said in a statement . <p> " The changes I am announcing today will bring climate risks and resilience into the heart of financial and business decision making . It will ensure the disclosure of climate risk is clear , comprehensive and mainstream , " Shaw said . <p> New Zealand is in its second week of campaigning ahead of an Oct. 17 general election .
