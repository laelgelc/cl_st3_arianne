<p> BALTIMORE , Sept. 24 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Three 40-foot shipping containers filled with humanitarian aid that included thousands of handmade quilts and baby-care items , which were reported as destroyed in the August 4 explosions at the port of Beirut , Lebanon , have been found intact and are ready to be distributed victims of the blast and refugees . <p> Footage from one of the Lutheran World Relief shipping containers recovered from the port of Beirut . <p> Authorities assumed the containers were lost in the historic blast that killed more than 200 people and laid waste to parts of the city . It took several weeks for longtime Lutheran World Relief partner Anera to gain access to the port , locate the containers and check their contents . <p> While other Anera shipping containers were heavily damaged , the three containers of quilts and kits were apparently stored behind a concrete block building that helped spare them from the blast .
