Though Captain Planet was never as popular as Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles , it performed well enough to run for six seasons from 1990 to 1996 -- with the series run from 1993 to 1996 being retitled The New Adventures of Captain Planet and airing on Turner Program Services -- and have a spin-off video game , action figure line , Marvel comics limited series , and promotional crossover with Burger King .
