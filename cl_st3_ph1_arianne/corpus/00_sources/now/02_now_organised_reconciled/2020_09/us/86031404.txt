<h> Final Analysis : A Messy Debate With Multiple Attacks <p> It 's safe to say that debate was not the calm exchange of policy ideas the moderators might have hoped for . <p> From the get-go , the night devolved into repeated shouting matches -- sometimes between President Trump and the moderator -- and personal attacks , punctuated by repeated pleas from Chris Wallace for the president to stop interrupting . <p> Joe Biden gave a more forceful performance than the low expectations set for him by the president and his allies . Mr. Trump delivered aggressive attacks , as expected , on his opponent 's record and intelligence and at times appeared to rattle the former vice president with his constant interruptions . But Mr. Biden also counterpunched . <p> Here are a few takeaways from the debate : <p> Election outcome . Mr. Trump 's answers tonight to the moderator 's questions about how he 'll handle the election results did little to calm Americans ' fears of a protracted battle over the outcome . Given the opportunity to say he would accept the results of the election and urge his supporters to remain calm , the president instead called on his backers to remain vigilant at the polls and said he would fight the results of a " rigged " election . Mr. Biden urged voters to cast their ballots and said he would accept the election results whether he won or lost . <p> Trump sought to cast Biden as left-wing . " Your party is socialist , and they 're going to dominate you , Joe , " Mr. Trump said . " Right now , I am the Democratic Party , " Mr. Biden replied . The president later accused his opponent of supporting the Green New Deal and a push to defund the police ; Mr. Biden said both were untrue . <p> Who 's fit for the presidency ? While Mr. Trump cast Mr. Biden as a tool of the progressive wing of the party , Mr. Biden repeatedly accused his opponent of being both unprepared and uninterested in the work of being president , launching attacks on Mr. Trump 's handling of the coronavirus pandemic and climate change . Mr. Trump retorted by asking what Mr. Biden had accomplished in more than four decades in Washington . Mr. Biden also went toe to toe with Mr. Trump for 90 minutes , after Mr. Trump and his allies had questioned whether Mr. Biden was up to it . <p> Personal attacks . Both candidates had clearly come armed with several personal lines of attack . Mr. Biden , who grew visibly frustrated with the president 's interruptions and at one point told him to " shut up , " called Mr. Trump " the worst president that America has ever had . " Mr. Trump retorted that in " 47 years you 've done nothing " and insulted the former vice president 's intelligence . <p> A tough night to moderate . There 's no question that was a rough debate . Chris Wallace struggled to control the conversation and often outright tangled with the president , who spoke over his opponent and the moderator for much of the 90-minute debate . That made it hard to hear what anyone was saying , let alone have a coherent exchange of ideas . Mr. Wallace did get Mr. Trump and Mr. Biden to answer questions they did n't particularly want to answer -- on climate change , violence and expanding the court .
