<p> Humans are dismantling and disrupting natural ecosystems around the globe and changing Earth 's climate . Over the past 50 years , actions like farming , logging , hunting , development and global commerce have caused record losses of species on land and at sea . Animals , birds and reptiles are disappearing tens to hundreds of times faster than the natural rate of extinction over the past 10 million years . <p> Now the world is also contending with a global pandemic . In geographically remote regions such as the Brazilian Amazon , COVID-19 is devastating Indigenous populations , with tragic consequences for both Indigenous peoples and the lands they steward . <p> My research focuses on ecosystems and climate change from regional to global scales . In 2019 , I worked with conservation biologist and strategist Eric Dinerstein and 17 colleagues to develop a road map for simultaneously averting a sixth mass extinction and reducing climate change by protecting half of Earth 's terrestrial , freshwater and marine realms by 2030 .
