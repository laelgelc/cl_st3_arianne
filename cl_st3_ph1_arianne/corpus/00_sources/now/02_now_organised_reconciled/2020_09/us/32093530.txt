In its first phase , Smart Carbon will primarily use circular carbon . <p> Reaching carbon-neutral steelmaking via DRI involves moving from using predominantly natural gas , to hydrogen as the key reductant in ironmaking . As this hydrogen becomes ' green ' , the steelmaking process comes close to carbon neutrality . <p> While both the Smart Carbon route and the DRI-based route have the potential to deliver carbon -neutral steel by 2050 , the important difference between the two routes is that Smart Carbon can deliver results sooner , through its use of complementary technologies which enable incremental progress . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provide carbon-neutral steel , but also carbon-neutral cement , and the building blocks to make recycled carbon materials to replace polyethylene-based plastics .
