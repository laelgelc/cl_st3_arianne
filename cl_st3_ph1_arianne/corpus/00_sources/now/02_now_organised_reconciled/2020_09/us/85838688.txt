<p> For many specialty coffee producers , selling their beans through traders have been the legacy practice . However , the pandemic , which caused travel and social restrictions to be enforced worldwide , has disrupted supply chains and turned coffee trading sluggish . Staging a virtual coffee auction helps to mitigate trade and supply challenges by allowing buyers to source directly from the origin , and also enable coffee producers to establish a presence in the Asian market . <p> The Singapore ( Micro-Lot ) Specialty Coffee Auction will present 56 lots of specialty grade coffee beans including the rare and premium Panamanian Geisha from Panamanian producers -- Caf ?
