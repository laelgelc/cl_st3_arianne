If you add up the various pledges that nations made at that conference , they plan on moving so timidly that the planet 's temperature will still rise more than 3 degrees Celsius from preindustrial levels . So far , we 've raised the mercury 1 degree Celsius , and that 's been enough to melt millions of square miles of ice in the Arctic , extend fire seasons for months , and dramatically alter the planet 's rainfall patterns . Settling for 3 degrees is kind of like writing a global suicide note . <p> Happily , we could go much faster if we wanted .
