Theoretically , it 's big enough to house Kiribati 's 100,000 inhabitants . <p> The idea of moving an entire population en masse is just one of several radical measures under consideration by the island populations most threatened by rising seas . <p> The world 's only atoll nations -- in the Pacific , Kiribati , Tuvalu , and the Marshall Islands ; in the Indian Ocean , the Maldives -- have built entire societies on the coral-and-sand rims of sunken volcanoes .
