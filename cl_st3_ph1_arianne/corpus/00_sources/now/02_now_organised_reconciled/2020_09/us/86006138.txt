<p> The MarketWatch News Department was not involved in the creation of this content . <p> PennyMac Financial Services , Inc . ( NYSE : PFSI ) ( the " Company " ) today announced the pricing of its offering of $500 million aggregate principal amount of 5.375% Senior Notes due 2025 ( the " Notes " ) . The size of the offering was increased from the previously announced $400 million aggregate principal amount . The Notes will bear interest at 5.375% per annum and will mature on October 15 , 2025 . Interest on the Notes will be payable semi-annually on April 15 and October 15 of each year , beginning on April 15 , 2021 . The Notes will be fully and unconditionally guaranteed on an unsecured senior basis by the Company 's existing and future wholly owned domestic subsidiaries , other than certain excluded subsidiaries . The Company intends to use the net proceeds from this offering for general corporate purposes , which may include the repayment of the Company 's existing secured warehouse borrowings .
