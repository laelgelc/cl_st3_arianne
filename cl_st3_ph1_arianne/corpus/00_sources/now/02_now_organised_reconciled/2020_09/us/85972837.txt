Speaker Nancy Pelosi ( D-CA ) hosted a virtual meeting on climate change with the parliamentary speakers and legislative presidents of the G7 nations . Following their opening remarks , the Dalai Lama , environmental activist Jane Goodall and former Energy Secretary Ernest Moniz addressed the meeting , focusing on the urgency for collective action and collaboration .
