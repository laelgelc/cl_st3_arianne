The Secretary of VA has made improving service to our veterans and beneficiaries a top priority . At the Veterans Benefits Administration ( VBA ) we are among those leading this customer-centric transformation . <p> The Under Secretary for Benefits is committed to providing veterans the benefits they have earned in a manner that honors their service by identifying customer service as one of his key priorities .
