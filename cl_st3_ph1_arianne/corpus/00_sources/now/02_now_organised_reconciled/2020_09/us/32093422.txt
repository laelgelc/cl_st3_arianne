PalmerPristine lakes and pollution-free skies marked the early months of the coronavirus lockdown -- environmental benefits of swift action to safeguard against the spread of coronavirus ( 1 ) . For its part , the scientific community canceled or reconfigured national and international meetings as virtual meetings . Study sections , site visits , seminars , and job interviews were held online . The scale of changes made by universities and scientific organizations in the timing and implementation of these measures is inspiring . Many of these actions were at enormous personal and collective sacrifice but based on the scientific evidence showing a dire threat to humanity.As locales reopen , the climate benefits reaped as a result of lockdown appear only temporary .
