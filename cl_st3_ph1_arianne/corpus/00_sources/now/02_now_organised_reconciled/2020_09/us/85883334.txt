And when there are fires , there 's smoke . And when there 's smoke , there is insidious air pollution . <p> The pattern , caused by four fires still burning in Los Angeles , San Bernardino and Riverside counties , is producing columns of smoke that collapse , spreading lung-damaging soot for hundreds of miles . <p> After record-breaking heat and explosive fires over the Labor Day weekend , lingering fire clouds lowered temperatures considerably Tuesday , Sept. 8 , by blocking the sun . Homes could be threatened if the winds carry flames down the canyons . <p> The coming hot winds -- normally seen in October through December -- are an anomaly for early September . That is part of the confusing weather pattern that not only hampers firefighting efforts but plays havoc with the air quality of 22 million basin residents . <p> " Anytime we have significant wildfires they produce a lot of smoke .
