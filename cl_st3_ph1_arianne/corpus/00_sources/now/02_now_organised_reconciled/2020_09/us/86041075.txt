Diablo winds are the equivalent in Northern California . <p> Because of those winds , and the fact that October is usually the month when vegetation is driest after a long summer before the first heavy rains begin in November , October can be particularly deadly for fires . <p> Five of the seven most destructive wildfires in California history , measured by structures destroyed , have occurred in October or November , including the 1991 Oakland Hills Fire , the 2017 Wine Country fires , and the 2018 Camp Fire in Paradise . <p> " Fire season is n't winding down .
