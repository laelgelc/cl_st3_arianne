<p> Walmart ( WMT ) - Get Report said it was upping its fight to protect the environment with an aim to end emissions from global operations by 2040 . <p> As part of its plan , the Bentonville , Ark. , big-box retailer expects to use 100% renewable energy by 2035 . Walmart earlier said it aimed to use 50% renewable power in its operations by 2025 . <p> The company also unveiled plans to restore at least 50 million acres of land and one million square miles of ocean by 2030 to " help combat the cascading loss of nature threatening the planet . " <p> " We want to play an important role in transforming the world 's supply chains to be regenerative , " Chief Executive Doug McMillon said in a statement . <p> Walmart said the proposed moves are designed to help avoid the worst effects of climate change . <p> Those moves include harvesting wind , solar and other renewable-energy sources to power its facilities ; electrifying and zeroing out emissions from all its vehicles ; and transitioning to low-impact refrigerants for cooling . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ - Get Report named the first four recipients of venture capital funds from the $2 billion it set up to help companies develop clean energy technologies . <p> Amazon has pledged to have net zero emissions by 2040 , meaning that any carbon emissions it does place into the atmosphere would be offset by removing the same amount through a carbon removal process . <p> Walmart says it hopes to reach zero emissions without the use of carbon offsets .
