Departing from all across the United States and Canada , the butterflies travel up to 2,500 miles to cluster at the same locations in Mexico or along the Pacific coast where their great-grandparents spent the previous winter . <p> Human activities have an outsize impact on monarchs ' ability to migrate yearly to these specific sites . Development , agriculture and logging have reduced monarch habitat .
