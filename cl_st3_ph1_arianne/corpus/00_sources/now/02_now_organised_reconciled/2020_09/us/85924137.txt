<h> Los Angeles mayor slams Trump over response to California fires <p> Los Angeles Mayor Eric Garcetti ( D ) slammed President Trump on Sunday over his response to the wildfires blazing across California and his dismissal of climate change as their cause . <p> Garcetti during an appearance on CNN 's " State of the Union " praised cooperation from federal officials , but said the leadership " at the very top " needs to be stronger , and that Trump needs to focus on helping Americans in need , regardless of party , instead of basing decisions " on an electoral map . " <p> He also hit Trump for blaming " blue states over red states , " noting that the " million Republicans in the city of Los Angeles " do n't change the mayor 's response to the blazing fires . <p> " We need leadership that is equal across this country , " Garcetti said . <p> " We need actual help , not based on our party affiliation or how we voted , " he added . <p> Trump is scheduled to visit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for California in August , but the president has also claimed the fires are due to years of poor forest management in the state . <p> Asked on Sunday about Trump 's claims on forest management , Garcetti said he listens to fire professionals , not the president or politicians , " when it comes to what causes fires . " <p> " Anybody who lives in California is insulted by that , quite frankly , and he keeps perpetuating the lie , " Garcetti added .
