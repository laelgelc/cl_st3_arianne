<h> Smoke from US fires reaches Europe , satellite data shows <p> by Patrick Galey <p> Smoke from the deadly US wildfires has been observed as far as 8000 kilometres away <p> Smoke from the fires devastating swathes of the US West Coast has reached as far as Europe , the European Union 's climate monitoring service said Wednesday in its assessment of the " unprecedented " blazes . <p> Satellite data from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service ( CAMS ) showed that the wildfires currently raging across California , Oregon and Washington State are " tens to hundreds of times more intense " than the recent average . <p> Thanks to strong pressure systems , the smoke from the fires was trapped along the western part of North America for days , making for potentially dangerous air quality in major cities such as Portland , Oregon and Vancouver and San Francisco . <p> But the weather shifted on Monday , carrying the smoke east along the jet stream .
