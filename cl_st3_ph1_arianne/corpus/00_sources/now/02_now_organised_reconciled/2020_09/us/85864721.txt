<h> Nonprofit proposes plan for innovative park along Ohio River <p> Updated 10:47 am CDT , Saturday , September 5 , 2020 <p> JEFFERSONVILLE , Ind . ( AP ) -- A proposed urban park that would line the Ohio River along southern Indiana could become the first climate change-resilient riverside park designed in the Upper South and the Midwest , supporters say . <p> The River Heritage Conservancy said its proposal for Origin Park is designed with the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers ' 75-year flooding projections in mind . <p> " This is a park that will not close in a flood -- this is a park that will welcome you in a flood , " said Scott Martin , executive director of the nonprofit , which is leading the project . <p> The proposed cost for the project across the river from Kentucky is $130 million , with money coming from public and private entities .
