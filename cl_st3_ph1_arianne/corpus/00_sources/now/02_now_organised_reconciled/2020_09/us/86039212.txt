<p> There is no question that democracy is under attack on a number of fronts this election . That is why it is so refreshing that California voters have the opportunity this November to vote for Proposition 18 , which will allow voters who will be 18 by the general election to also vote in the primary . Voting is a privilege and a responsibility in our country , and our nation is founded on this right . <p> There is no reason why we would withhold the ability to participate in the full election cycle from first-time voters who want to cast a ballot in the primary as well as the general election . <p> We already trust people this age with the responsibility to enlist in the military so they can serve and protect American interests at home and abroad . How can we encourage young people to make the grave decision to put their lives on the line for our country before they turn 18 but consider them not mature enough to participate in the full election cycle ? If we trust young people with the decision @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our government , then surely we can trust them with the responsibility to vote in the primary election . <p> We also count on young people to contribute to the economy before they turn 18 . Many young people have already been working and paying taxes for a couple of years by the time they are 18 , shouldering significant responsibilities as part-time workers or fully employed breadwinners who support their family . We count on them to pay taxes that fuel our economy . <p> Young people are deeply affected by major election issues such as climate change , gun laws , student debt , health care and the economy . Many young voters are highly motivated to get involved and want to make their voices heard on these important topics . This generation will bear the brunt of decisions made today . It 's time that we recognize their interest in civic engagement and allow young people to participate in the full election cycle if they turn 18 by the general election . <p> We in California like to see ourselves as trailblazers . Unfortunately , California will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ voting rights . Our state will simply be catching up to 18 other states , red and blue ones , that already have this law on the books . Young people who turn 18 before the general election in California should have the same opportunity to vote in the primary as those who live in Colorado , Connecticut , Delaware , Illinois , Indiana , Kentucky , Maine , Maryland , Mississippi , Nebraska , New Mexico , North Carolina , Ohio , South Carolina , Utah , Vermont , Virginia , West Virginia and Washington D.C. <p> Letting first-time voters who are eligible to vote in the general election to also vote in the primary is reasonable and democratic . Research shows that voting is a habit-forming activity . Giving first-time voters the ability to participate in the full election cycle helps young people build a lifelong habit of civic participation . Expanding the right for first-time voters to participate in the full election cycle will help safeguard the integrity of our democracy and election systems .
