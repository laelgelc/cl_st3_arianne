<p> Four centuries after the original ship crossed the Atlantic in 1620 , a new Mayflower is set to take its maiden voyage . IBM and ProMare , an ocean-research nonprofit , launched a new uncrewed , solar-powered research vessel called the Mayflower Autonomous Ship , the companies announced on Tuesday . <p> IBM developed the Mayflower as a " safe , flexible and cost-effective way of gathering data about the ocean , " the company said . The ship will help scientists from ProMare , IBM Research , and other organizations study climate change , micro-plastic pollution , and marine-mammal conservation , among other issues . <p> The Mayflower has what IBM calls an " AI Captain , " which allows it to take in information and make decisions without any humans aboard . IBM said the Mayflower operates at level-five autonomy -- meaning it can work without any human intervention -- thanks to more than 30 sensors and six AI cameras . <p> " Able to scan the horizon for possible hazards , make informed decisions and change its course based on a fusion of live data , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ modern bank than its 17th century namesake , " Andy Stanford-Clark , the chief technology officer at IBM UK and Ireland , said in the announcement . <p> IBM and ProMare announced a website that lets people keep up-to-date with the ship 's movements . IBM <p> IBM and ProMare also announced an interactive website , the MAS400 portal , that will provide live updates on the vessel 's whereabouts , environmental conditions , and other data . <p> " Protecting the ocean depends on our ability to engage the public in important matters affecting its health , " Fredrik Soreide , scientific director of the Mayflower project and board member of ProMare , said . " This MAS400 portal is designed to do exactly that and tell people where the ship is , what speed it 's travelling at , what conditions it 's operating in and what science we are conducting . " <p> The Mayflower will undergo testing for the next six months before embarking on its first transatlantic voyage in spring 2021.
