<h> New SJSU wildfire research center seeks to improve forecasting tools to help firefighters <p> A newly created wildfire research center at San Jose State University is aiming to develop advanced tools to forecast wildfire behavior and help California firefighters and forest management officials better respond to blazes throughout the state . <p> The Wildfire Interdisciplinary Research Center will also increase the monitoring of fire weather conditions , and research the impacts of fires in a " social science perspective " on the community , said Dr. Craig Clements , professor of meteorology and director of the center . <p> University officials said the newly-formed center is the largest interdisciplinary wildfire research center of its kind in the United States . <p> The center is housed in the College of Science and is a collaborative , interdisciplinary undertaking with faculty from the College of Social Sciences and the Charles W. Davidson College of Engineering .
