Not long after , they evacuated . No Boundaries eventually burned to the ground in the Valley Fire along with a neighboring hemp farm , Inya Hemp . <p> Though those are only two farms out of many scattered throughout rural areas in San Diego County , a variety of factors make hemp farms particularly susceptible to California 's fire season , which becomes more intense with each passing year . <p> Advertisement <p> December 2018 's passage of the Farm Bill @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gave the green light for individual municipalities to begin investing in the industrial crop . <p> Since then , a new hemp industry has emerged .
