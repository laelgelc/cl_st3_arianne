<p> Once a reliably red state , Arizona has become increasingly purple over the last decade . <p> When it comes to the 2020 election , it 's districts like Arizona 's 9th Congressional District that could swing outcomes on the federal level . <p> The 9th Congressional District falls in the heart of the Valley . It includes east Phoenix , Tempe , south Scottsdale and parts of Mesa and Chandler . It 's home to some of Arizona 's largest employers , such as State Farm Insurance and Arizona State University . <p> The district was originally drawn as one of three competitive House seats in Arizona . Back when it was created , Republicans had a 3 percentage point advantage . Today , Democrats have an 8-point edge . <p> What does that shift mean for President Donald Trump and former Vice President Joe Biden ?
