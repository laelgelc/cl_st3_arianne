<h> CFTC report : Climate change poses serious risk to financial system <p> A task force for a federal financial markets regulator is warning in a new report due out Wednesday that climate change poses " serious emerging risks to the U.S. financial system , " and it calls for regulators to " move urgently and decisively " to confront them . <p> The report obtained by POLITICO and compiled by a task force for the Commodity Futures Trading Commission is the starkest warning to date by a U.S. financial regulator over the stresses that markets will face from climate change , which has become a leading concern for banks and regulators in the European Union , Japan and other nations . <p> " Climate change poses a major risk to the stability of the U.S. financial system and to its ability to sustain the American economy , " the CFTC task force said in its report .
