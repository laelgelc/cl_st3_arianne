They 're likely to collapse from the impact of heat and loss of water due to climate change . Scientists from the University of California , Berkeley determined this after studying bird populations in the Mojave Desert last year . <p> The study found that compared with 100 years ago a third of Mojave Desert bird species are less common today . Heat is stressing them . Additional losses are likely .
