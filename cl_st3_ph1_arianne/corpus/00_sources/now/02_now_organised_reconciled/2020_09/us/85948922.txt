I 've been doing yoga , which has allowed me to tell myself that -- while I may have put on a few pounds -- I 've never been more flexible . I 've grown especially used to exercising with an onscreen instructor who ca n't see me , which means I can shout as many obscenities as I like without bothering anyone ( a habit that has made it hard for me to adjust to the occasional outdoor group workouts that friends have invited me to ) . <p> I 'm not alone in all this change .
