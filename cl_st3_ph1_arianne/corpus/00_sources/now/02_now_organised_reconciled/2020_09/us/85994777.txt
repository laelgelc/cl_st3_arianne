<p> Fox News Sunday anchor Chris Wallace , moderator of the season 's first presidential debate on September 29 , has selected the six topics that President Donald Trump and former Vice President Joe Biden will face . <p> The topics are the Trump and Biden records , the Supreme Court , COVID-19 , the economy , race and violence in our cities and the integrity of the election . The topics might not be in the order that they are listed . <p> There was some criticism to the choice of topics for excluding climate change , which also was barely discussed during the 2016 general election debates . <p> " This is unacceptable -- the climate crisis can not be ignored , " Tom Steyer , a Democratic candidate in 2020 , wrote on Twitter . He included a link to a petition to have it included . <p> But the Commission on Presidential Debates leaves it up to the moderators to select topics and questions .
