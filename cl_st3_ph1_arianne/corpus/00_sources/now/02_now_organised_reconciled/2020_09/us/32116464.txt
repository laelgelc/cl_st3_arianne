MPB include diatoms , cyanobacteria , and other microorganisms and occur on intertidal areas , forming a considerable part of the total primary production in estuaries ( up to 50% ) , and plays a key role in sustaining intertidal food webs . <p> These estuaries provide critical stopovers for migratory birds , and MPB has been linked to the provision of essential lipids needed for long-distance migration . Such habitats are increasingly under stress of coastal development and climate change .
