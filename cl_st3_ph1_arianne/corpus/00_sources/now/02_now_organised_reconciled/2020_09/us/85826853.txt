<h> Canadian wildlife losses fuelling global extinction crisis <p> The Living Planet Report Canada 2020 , published today , found species of global conservation concern ( wildlife assessed as at risk of global extinction by the International Union of Conservation of Nature ) saw their Canadian populations fall by an average of 42 per cent since 1970 . <p> Furthermore , populations of Canadian species assessed as nationally at risk of extinction by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada ( COSEWIC -- the scientific body that determines a species ' risk of extinction ) have declined by an average of 59 per cent . <p> The report examined population trends between 1970 and 2016 using a new dataset that has been hailed by the Zoological Society of London as one of the most comprehensive national-level datasets . WWF-Canada experts also found that these species face an average of five threats , including the increasing and accelerating threat of climate change . <p> This tells us that Canada , as a whole , is not doing enough to protect its most imperiled species .
