<p> COPENHAGEN , Denmark ( AP ) -- An enormous chunk of Greenland 's ice cap has broken off in the far northeastern Arctic , a development that scientists say is evidence of rapid climate change . <p> The glacier section that broke off is 110 square kilometers ( 42.3 square miles ) .
