Global sea-level rise consists of the sum of several components in response to a common forcing , and shows a slow and delayed response to today 's atmospheric warming and GHG emissions . Thermal expansion of ocean water , the retreat of mountain glaciers and ice caps , and the mass loss of the Greenland and Antarctic ice sheets are the main drivers of sea-level rise linked to climate change . The risk of ocean-driven marine-ice-sheet instability hence increases uncertainty in future sea level rise , but the numbers available from process-based simulations suggest a minor role for the timescale considered here .
