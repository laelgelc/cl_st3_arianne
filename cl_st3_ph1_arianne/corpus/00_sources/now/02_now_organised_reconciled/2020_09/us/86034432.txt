<h> Extinction Rebellion protester walks runway at Dior in Paris <p> By Reuters Staff <p> 2 Min Read <p> A woman holds a banner which reads " We are all fashion victims " as she walks on the catwalk during the Spring/Summer 2021 ready-to-wear collection show by designer Maria Grazia Chiuri for fashion house Dior during Paris Fashion Week in Paris , France , September 29 , 2020 . REUTERS/Benoit Tessier <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - A woman holding a banner reading " We are all fashion victims " infiltrated the catwalk at Christian Dior 's show in Paris on Tuesday - walking the runway so calmly that it appeared at first to be part of the event . <p> The protester was acting in the name of Extinction Rebellion , an activist movement that aims to highlight the risks posed by climate change . The act was meant to denounce " the impact of the fashion industry on the environment and society " , a spokesman for the movement in France said . <p> Strolling past guests including " Game of Thrones " actress Maisie Williams and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ front row , the woman walked towards photographers after models had exited the runway . <p> She left the scene without anyone challenging her and leaving some people confused over whether it was a stunt or part of the show . <p> Dior 's womenswear collections are designed by Maria Grazia Chiuri , who has often introduced logos into her collection as well as feminist statements on T-shirts and on set . Dior declined to comment further . <p> Extinction Rebellion , which has led protests at London 's Fashion Week since February 2019 , started a campaign this week highlighting the waste generated by the industry .
