Why is the replacement of internal combustion engine through electric vehicles an important societal goal , why is there still a gap towards achieving this goal , and what needs to be done to close it ? <p> The societal motivation for a large-scale roll-out of electric and other new energy vehicles to displace internal combustion engines is clear : to curb climate change . Crucially , electric vehicles are available already today and over the coming decade could keep us on track to lower the carbon footprint of transportation to the extent necessary to meet the Paris Agreement targets . The electrification of the transport sector is well underway . Battery costs have dropped some 90% in the last decade , more than 600 zero emission models are on track for launch by 2025 and governments around the globe -- cities , sub-national states and 17 countries -- are mandating transitions to clean fleets by 2030 or 2050 . By 2040 , over half of new vehicles sold will be electric , up from 2.7% today . <p> And yet , this change is not happening fast enough . On the current trajectory we will fall short of what needs to be achieved to realize the 1.5 ? or even the " well below 2 ? " Paris Agreement goals .
