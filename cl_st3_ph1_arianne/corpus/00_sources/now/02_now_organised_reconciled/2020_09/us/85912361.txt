Gretchen Whitmer . <p> Whitmer said the Department of Natural Resources ( DNR ) , which holds the easement title , is finishing its review during remarks to the Chicago-based Environmental Law &amp; Policy Center , which held virtual gala on Thursday , Sept. 10 . <p> The DNR review has been pending since last summer . <p> " We know a break in that pipeline would be an utter disaster , " Whitmer said . " As the DNR is wrapping up its easement review , I think it 's really very likely there will be a determination on that particular front in the very near future .
