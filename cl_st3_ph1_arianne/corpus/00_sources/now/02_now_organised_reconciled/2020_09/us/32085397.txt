<h> Facebook prefers to counter false information with more information . It does n't work . <h> Share this story <p> Facebook this week implemented yet another new initiative meant to combat rampant , dangerous disinformation on the platform -- this time , relating to the climate crisis . Unfortunately this initiative , like countless others before it , seems likely to generate positive headlines for about a week before disappearing unremarked into obscurity , solving exactly zero of Facebook 's deeper problems along the way . <p> " One of the biggest lessons we have learned from the COVID-19 pandemic is how powerful Facebook can be for connecting people to accurate , expert advice and information during a global crisis , " Facebook wrote in a corporate blog post . To that end , Facebook is launching a new " Climate Change Information Center " module and landing page that will , in theory , connect users to up-to-date , fact-based information grounded in reality . <h> Further Reading <p> Users in the US and a small handful of other countries may already have seen a notification about the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , either on desktop or on mobile . The green box implores you by name to " see how our climate is changing . " <p> Clicking through takes you directly to the climate science information center , featuring a handful of climate facts , links to a selection of vetted organizations , graphs of temperature facts customized to your location , and a curated selection of relevant news stories from major outlets such as USA Today , Vox , The New York Times , BuzzFeed , and CNN . Users can also choose to follow or like any other page , group , or individual on the platform . <p> Facebook said it modeled the climate information center on its COVID-19 information landing page , which it launched this spring after coronavirus misinformation and falsehoods were already widespread across social media platforms . About 600 million users have at one point or another clicked through to view the COVID-19 information module , the company said -- about 22 percent of Facebook 's 2.7 billion monthly active users . <h> We 've been here before <p> Lies , falsehoods , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the company unfortunately has a spotty track record when it comes to handling misinformation of all types . <p> Company CEO Mark Zuckerberg has long been adamant that his preferred solution for countering bad stuff on Facebook is to have more stuff on Facebook . Rather than banning most forms of falsehood as a policy , Facebook at best labels them with a link to a fact check and encourages users to post their own , ideally more accurate information to counter untruths . <h> Further Reading <p> Sure , the theory goes , some people ( including sitting politicians ) may use Facebook to be cranks , but it 's incumbent on everyone else to use Facebook to say things that are true and hope that the truths can counteract the lies . <p> At this point , however , it seems clear that policy is not working out as well as Zuckerberg may have hoped . People who spread falsehoods , it turns out , tend not to be operating in good faith , and now Facebook faces even more criticism from all sides . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ effects . The impact of Russia 's use of social media to manipulate the 2016 US presidential election is by now well known . In 2018 , the company admitted that false content posted by Myanmar 's military was directly contributing to the genocide of the Rohingya ethnic minority in that nation . As recently as this week , a former data scientist for Facebook wrote a damning memo describing several instances of inauthentic political behavior on Facebook fanning flames worldwide during her two years with the company . <p> Nor are inauthentic actors the only problem Facebook faces . Plenty of people and organizations who spread false information are extremely clear about who they are and what they 're doing . Anti-vaccination groups , for example , spread misinformation far and wide . Facebook prohibits anti-vax groups from posting advertisements on the platform , but organic content still generates engagement , even though Facebook applies fact-checking labels . Now , Facebook even finds itself facing a lawsuit from anti-vaxxers who do n't like being fact-checked . <h> Finding the Information desk <p> Facebook 's fact-check labels for major topics @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change lead to their information centers . " We 're working to make this clearer in our guidelines so our fact checkers can use their judgment to determine whether it is an attempt to mask false information under the guise of opinion . " <p> However , Facebook has at least twice overturned the rulings of fact-checking experts who did review climate-related posts and found them to be partly or entirely false . " <p> In short : fact-checking is not enough -- especially if Facebook is just going to walk it back whenever someone complains . <p> A coalition of media and environment watchdog groups -- including the Sierra Club , Greenpeace , and the Union of Concerned Scientists -- issued a joint statement giving Facebook half credit for their actions . <p> " This new policy is a small step forward but does not address the larger @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ groups wrote .
