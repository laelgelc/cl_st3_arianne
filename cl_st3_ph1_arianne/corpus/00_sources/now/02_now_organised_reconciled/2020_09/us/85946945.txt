California has suffered five of its largest fires ever recorded , while in Oregon , fires have consumed an area larger than the state of Rhode Island . Smoke and ash have saturated the air , and preemptive electrical shutdowns have left thousands without power . <p> These fires , worsened by climate change , have given Americans a sense of what 's ahead .
