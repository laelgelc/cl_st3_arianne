<h> Reaching Britain 's climate target must be fair for all -- Climate Assembly <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Britain 's first citizen 's assembly on climate change called on the government on Thursday to ensure moves to meet the country 's climate targets are fair , with public transport made cheaper and taxes for those flying the most and furthest . <p> Britain last year became the first major economy to set a climate target to meet net zero emissions by 2050 , which will require changes in the way Britons eat , travel and use electricity . <p> The Climate Assembly was established by the government in 2019 to gather views on how best to meet the goal and to consider the impact on the public . It was made up of 108 citizens from across the UK with a wide range of backgrounds . <p> Moves to get people to eat less meat and dairy should be voluntary and education-led , while efforts to curb home heating emissions should be done on a regional basis with local input , the assembly said in its final report @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ transport links cheaper and phasing out new petrol , diesel and hybrid vehicles by 2030-2035 , with government incentives for people to buy cleaner cars . <p> It also recommended taxes on air travel should increase the more often and further people fly . <p> The Assembly does not have any independent power , but its findings will be used to inform broader discussions on how the climate goal should be met . <p> Its views will be considered by the government 's independent climate advisers , the Committee on Climate Change , its chief executive Chris Stark said . <p> ( Reporting by Susanna Twidale ; Editing by Jan Harvey ) <p> Our goal is to create a safe and engaging place for users to connect over interests and passions .
