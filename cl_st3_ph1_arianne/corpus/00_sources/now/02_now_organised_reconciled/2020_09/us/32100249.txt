<h> Data project to model range shifts of hundreds of New England plant , animal species <p> By harnessing diverse current and historical data with space and time dimensions , scientists will be able to better predict and help rural communities respond to the impact of climate change on biodiversity . <p> In response to a changing climate , populations of plants and animals move to more hospitable locations . Predicting where species will end up , and how New England farmers and rural communities need to plan and adapt accordingly is the focus of a new interdisciplinary research initiative led by the University of Maine . <p> The National Science Foundation awarded $4 million over four years to the EPSCoR Research Infrastructure project to develop novel approaches and software for modeling , visualizing and forecasting spatial and temporal data .
