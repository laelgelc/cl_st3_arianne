<h> Nvidia Buys Arm For $40 Billion , Will Create A Super-Computer <h> Nvidia announces a massive acquisition one of the world 's biggest semiconductor companies . <p> After announcing its next series of graphics cards , Nvidia has made another bombshell announcement . The company is buying the British semiconductor company Arm for $40 million . Nvidia acquired Arm from SoftBank , which paid $31 billion for it back in 2016 . <p> In a statement , Nvidia said buying Arm will help the company become the " premier computing company for the age of artificial intelligence , accelerating innovation while expanding into large , high-growth markets . " <p> While SoftBank has now sold Arm , the company is making an ownership stake in Nvidia which is expected to be " under 10 percent . " <p> " AI is the most powerful technology force of our time and has launched a new wave of computing , " Nvidia founder and CEO Jensen Huang said . " In the years ahead , trillions of computers running AI will create a new internet-of-things that is thousands of times larger @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ company fabulously positioned for the age of AI . " <p> The team at Arm will stay at their current headquarters in Cambridge , but thanks to the new investment , the company will expand on the offices and build a " world-class AI research facility " that will support the development of new technology for health care , life sciences , robotics , and self-driving cars . The company also plans to build a " state-of-the-art AI supercomputer " that uses Arm CPUs . <p> " Arm and Nvidia share a vision and passion that ubiquitous , energy-efficient computing will help address the world 's most pressing issues from climate change to healthcare , from agriculture to education , " Arm CEO Simon Segars said in a statement . " Delivering on this vision requires new approaches to hardware and software and a long-term commitment to research and development . By bringing together the technical strengths of our two companies we can accelerate our progress and create new solutions that will enable a global ecosystem of innovators .
