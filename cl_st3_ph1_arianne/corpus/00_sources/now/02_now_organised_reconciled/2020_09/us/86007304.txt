That is a long way off , but still potentially transformational for the global energy sector if followed up with real action . <p> There is a strong taste of geopolitics to the announcement . It comes as China is in the middle of fraught negotiations with Europe -- which has made controlling carbon emissions a priority -- over a bilateral investment treaty . And it follows in the wake of Germany 's release of a new Indo-Pacific Strategy earlier in September which was widely interpreted in part as a shot across Beijing 's bow . <p> Beijing 's pledge helps prevent Europe from adopting a more unified front with the U.S. on trade , technology and human rights . It is a populous and water scarce nation with a very long coastline . Chinese leaders also have made no secret of their ambition to make the nation into a clean energy technological superpower . Chinese investment in renewable energy , already massive , has swelled further this year as economic stimulus has spurred funding for the power sector : China spent 133 billion yuan , equivalent to $19.5 billion , on wind power construction in the first eight months of 2020 , up 179% from the same period last year . <p> For investors and politicians , the best way to gauge China 's seriousness might be its policies on the fuel on the other side of the ledger : coal . In 2019 thermal power still accounted for 70% of electricity generation . Here the news is , for now , less good .
