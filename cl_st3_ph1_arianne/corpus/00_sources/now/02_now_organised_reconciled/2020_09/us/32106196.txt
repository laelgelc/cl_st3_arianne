We look forward to hearing and sharing the issues that you are passionate about -- including women 's empowerment and tackling inequality , " he said . <p> " We at UNDP will also be working to amplify your voice on the socio-economic impact of COVID-19 in developing countries and the importance of a green and equitable recovery for all .
