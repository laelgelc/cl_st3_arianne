<p> BRASILIA ( Reuters ) - The official August data on the number of fires in Brazil 's Amazon needs to be corrected and will likely show an increase over last year , meaning that blazes surged to a decade high , a scientist responsible for the figures told Reuters on Wednesday . <p> Brazilian media has reported that fires in Brazil 's section of the Amazon rainforest fell by 5% in August , citing data currently on the website of government space research agency Inpe that has not been corrected . <p> Alberto Setzer , a senior scientist at Inpe who is involved in producing the official fire data , said that the reporting of finalized data had been delayed by an error with a NASA satellite . <p> But once that issue is corrected , it will likely show that fires increased by between 1% and 2% in August 2020 compared to the same month a year ago , Setzer said . That would mean it is the worst spate of fires for the month since August 2010 . <p> " It 's going to be up . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
