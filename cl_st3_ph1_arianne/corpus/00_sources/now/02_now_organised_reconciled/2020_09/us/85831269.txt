The company 's plant-based egg products will be available at more than 17,000 U.S. retail outlets by the end of September , marking a 41% increase in retail distribution outlets selling JUST egg made with plants , specifically , mung beans . <p> With more consumers interested in the plant-based diet trend and eating at home amid the pandemic , the move gives the company an opportunity to keep growing towards profitability . Earlier this month , Reuters reported that the company hopes to " turn an operating profit before the end of next year and then will consider an initial public offering . " <p> The announcement follows similar news from other plant-based industry players .
