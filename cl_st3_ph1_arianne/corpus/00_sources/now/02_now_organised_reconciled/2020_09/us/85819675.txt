<h> Climate and extinction crises require urgent change <p> The U.S. is fighting a battle on many fronts : a public health crisis , an extinction crisis , a climate crisis -- each compounded by the last and by policies that declare some people , species and places disposable . <p> Scientists , disease ecologists and other experts warn that we could see more pandemics unless we act quickly to protect , not exploit , habitat and wildlife . One million species are at risk of extinction and hundreds of the 1,471 United States ' endangered species are vulnerable to climate change . The interconnectedness of all life is on full display . If our elected officials fail to act to prevent the exacerbation of these crises , the economic , political and environmental downfall will be tragic . <p> While the challenges ahead are daunting to say the least , they are not insurmountable . Just as these crises and their causes are inextricably linked , so are the solutions to address them .
