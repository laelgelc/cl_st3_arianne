<h> A long-term battle : The tech industry 's role in combatting climate change <p> As the world continues to make attempts at urgently containing the COVID-19 pandemic , there is an even more dire issue that needs to be addressed : Climate change . <p> New research , published in last month 's edition of the journal Communications Earth and Environment , found that in 2019 , Greenland 's ice sheet lost an annual record of 532 billion tonnes of ice . For perspective , between 2003 and 2016 , Greenland 's ice sheet lost on average about 255 billion tonnes of ice each year . <p> Further evidence of climate change worsening was seen last week when a large chunk of Greenland 's ice cap , estimated to be 110 square kilometres in size , broke off in the far north-east Arctic , the Associated Press reported . <p> But climate change is no longer a conversation only had among environmental activists .
