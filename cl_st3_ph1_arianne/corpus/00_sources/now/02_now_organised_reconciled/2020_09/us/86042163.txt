<p> There is more than one reason that we need to reforest Planet Earth . Less than a fifth of Earth 's original forests have survived the rise of humans since the last glaciation , and over half of them are in just five countries ( see figure below ) . <p> The biggest effect from loss of forests is loss of habitat and the resultant loss of biodiversity , even if you do n't care about climate change . We 're burning billions of acres of pristine Indonesian rain forests to plant palm oil trees ( Scientific American ) just to get a cooking oil with a better shelf life . <p> Forest biodiversity encompasses not just trees , but the multitude of plants , animals and microorganisms that inhabit forested areas - and their associated genetic diversity .
