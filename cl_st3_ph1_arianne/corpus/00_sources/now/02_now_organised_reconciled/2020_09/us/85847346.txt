He graduated from the University of Sydney with a law and economics degree . He was a Rhodes Scholar at Oxford University , majoring in politics , philosophy and economics , and won two awards in boxing . <p> * Abbott has opposed embryonic stem cell research , same-sex marriage and carbon trading to combat climate change . His book on Australian conservative politics published in 2009 was titled " Battlelines .
