<h> DNR wants your input on plans for forest management <h> DNR wants your input on plans for forest management <p> How do you use and enjoy Michigan 's nearly 4 million acres of state forests ? How important are forests to you ? <p> The Michigan Department of Natural Resources wants to know ! The DNR 's Forest Resources Division is conducting a public survey to help guide creation of a new 10-year plan to keep state forests healthy and productive for generations . <p> Take the survey and offer your input . It will be available online until Oct. 23 . <p> " It 's important for us to involve the public when making plans for the forest because the forests are here for people to use and enjoy , " said Jeff Stampfly , acting chief of the Forest Resources Division . <p> Periodic planning helps forestry professionals respond to factors that affect the forest such as climate change , invasive pests and fire .
