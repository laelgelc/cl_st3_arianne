<p> Last Friday , Amazon announced an exciting global partnership announcement with Mercedes Benz to help lead the charge in sustainability . Mercedes-Benz is the latest big-name signatory of the Climate Pledge , a commitment to meet the Paris Agreement 10 years early and was founded by Amazon and Global Optimism in September 2019 . Everyone who signs commits to be " net-zero carbon across its business by 2040 " . Through this announcement , Amazon will be adding more than 1,800 electric vehicle Mercedes Benz vans to its delivery fleet in Europe this year . By signing , it marks the largest electric vehicle commitment for Mercedes-Benz to date . Amazon 's CSR goals are impressive , and they are leading the charge for many companies .
