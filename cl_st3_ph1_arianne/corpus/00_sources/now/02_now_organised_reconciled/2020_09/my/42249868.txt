education link which is a key pillar in the longstanding relationship between both countries , said British High Commissioner to Malaysia Charles Hay . <p> Hay said some 19,000 Malaysians pursued their higher studies in the UK last year , adding that the number will probably be lower this year due to Covid-19 . <p> " With a track record of academic and research excellence , UK universities are
