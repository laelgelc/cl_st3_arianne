<p> This marks the group 's first loan in which the interest is linked to the achievement of sustainability performance targets . <p> " As we are driving our business to support the growth and development of new services , we are pleased to underpin our commitment to our sustainability strategy by taking up our very first sustainability-linked loan with CIMB , " chief financial officer of StarHub , Dennis Chia , said in a statement today . <p> " We look forward to more opportunities to participate in sustainability-linked financing to proactively drive a sustainable business and enhance the performance of the company , " said Chia . <p> To incentivise environmental transparency and performance , CIMB will grant a rebate against the loan interest based on StarHub 's performance in its annual CDP Climate Change Scores from financial years 2021 to 2023 . <p> StarHub will be eligible for interest savings each year if it is able to achieve the pre-agreed annual targets on CDP Climate Change Scores . <p> CDP , formerly known as the Carbon Disclosure Project , is a leading global benchmark on the management @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environmental stewardship . <p> StarHub , a home-grown Singapore provider of communications , information , and entertainment services , said sustainability is an integral part of its strategy and operations . <p> Its commitment and transformation to become a more responsible and sustainable business began in 2008 . <p> It has since implemented many sustainable and green practices across the business , including tackling e-waste , reducing energy and water consumption , and promoting actions to address climate change while delivering world-class communications , entertainment , and digital solutions to consumers as well as corporate and government clients .
