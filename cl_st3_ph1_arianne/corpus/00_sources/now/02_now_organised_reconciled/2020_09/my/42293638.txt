<h> UPDATE 3-California plans ban on sale of new gasoline-powered passenger vehicles in 2035 <p> 24 Sep 2020 / 05:24 H. <p> ( Adds White House statement ) <p> By David Shepardson and Nichola Groom <p> WASHINGTON/LOS ANGELES , Sept 23 ( Reuters ) - California plans to ban the sale of new gasoline powered passenger cars and trucks starting in 2035 as the top U.S. auto market shifts to electric vehicles to reduce climate-warming emissions , Governor Gavin Newsom said on Wednesday . <p> The dramatic move is the most significant yet by a U.S. state aimed at ending the use of fossil fuel-burning internal combustion engines , and clashes with the pro-fossil fuel policies of the Trump White House . <p> Newsom had declared earlier this month he would step up the state 's already aggressive efforts to combat climate change amid a record wildfire season . <p> " We are marking a new course , " Newsom said at a press conference in Sacramento while standing in front of electric cars made by automakers that support the state 's efforts to reduce vehicle emissions .
