<h> Malaysia committed to ensuring at least half of country is covered with forests <p> PUTRAJAYA : Malaysia is committed to ensuring that at least half of the country 's land area is covered with forests and trees , says the Energy and Natural Resources Ministry . <p> Its minister Datuk Dr Shamsul Anuar Nasarah said this was in line with a pledge the country made back in 1992 during the Earth Summit in Rio de Janeiro , Brazil . <p> " The forest is a precious natural asset that has important roles in many aspects . It provides a supply of clean water , it produces oxygen for our survival , it is an important habitat for millions of flora and fauna , and it also gives a balance to the ecosystem from global warming . <p> " Malaysia is committed to maintaining at least 50% of the country 's land area with forest and tree cover . <p> " At present , Malaysia 's forest cover is at 55.31% with an area of 18.27 million hectares , " said Shamsul Anuar , during the launch of Nestle ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The Lenggong MP also said protecting Malaysia 's forests would help to mitigate climate change . <p> " Forests significantly provide 30% of the solution to climate change .
