<h> MBZUAI and Weizmann Institute team up on AI research <h> ABU DHABI , 19 hours , 53 minutes ago <p> UAE 's Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence ( MBZUAI ) and Israel 's Weizmann Institute of Science will collaborate to advance the development and use of artificial intelligence ( AI ) as a tool for progress . <p> Both of them have signed a memorandum of understanding ( MoU ) , which will see the two higher education institutes work together across a range of fields . <p> The MoU , which is the first of its kind to be signed between two higher education institutes from the UAE and Israel , covers a range of opportunities for collaboration , including student and postdoctoral fellows exchange programmes , conferences and seminars , various forms of exchange between researchers , sharing of computing resources , and the establishment of a joint virtual institute for artificial intelligence . <p> This follows the historic Peace Accord agreement , which establishes full normalisation of relations between the UAE and Israel . Based in Abu Dhabi , the University offers MSc and PhD programmes in the fields of computer vision , machine learning , and natural language processing . <p> MBZUAI , expecting its first cohort of students to start in January 2021 , is introducing a new model of academia and research to the field of AI , providing students and faculty access to some of the world 's most advanced AI systems to unleash the technology 's full potential for economic and societal development . <p> The Weizmann Institute @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the world 's top-ranking multidisciplinary research institutions and offers masters ' and doctoral-level degrees across five faculties .
