<h> A green piece of paradise in heart of Cork city <p> By Andrew Desmond <h> St Stephen 's Sustainable Food Lab in Cork city is a self-funded , member-led urban agriculture project that has sprung up on a once derelict site , explains ANDREW DESMOND <p> A FEW months ago , we started using our brown bin for all our organic kitchen waste , but instead of putting it out for street collection every fortnight , we take it around to our local community garden where there is a compost heap . <p> We love this holistic mini-circular economy of growing , nourishing , composting and growing , called St Stephen 's Sustainable Food Lab ( SSFL ) , a not for profit urban agriculture project in Cork city <p> Yes , we are very fortunate to have the community garden nearby , off Tower Street/Evergreen Street/Barrack Street , but only a few years ago this space was a derelict basketball court , and would still be in a sad state but for an active group of neighbours . <p> These people , when given permission from Cork City @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ concrete yard into a verdant , colourful vegetable and herb garden with a wild flower pollinator bed to attract butterflies and bees . Every neighbourhood has some neglected space waiting to be magically transformed like this . <p> SFL took charge of the space in 2017 and we use recycled materials in all our projects , and with no water supply we collect rainwater for watering plants . <p> We have a score of active members and take no funding commercial or public . This now vibrant communal space is designed to encourage and support innovative , creative and sustainable projects with healthy food at its core . <p> We have a ' one harvest for all ' policy where the produce is shared by all the members . <p> And in the polytunnel , bought second hand and constructed by SFL members in 2019 , we are now growing cucumbers , melons , tomatoes and chillies . <p> There is a profound and lasting satisfaction in eating a crop you have sown , tended and harvested yourself .
