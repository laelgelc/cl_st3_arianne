<h> Asset managers progress biodiversity impact measurement plan <p> AXA Investment Managers , BNP Paribas Asset Management , Sycomore Asset Management and Mirova have selected the research provider that will develop a tool to allow them to measure the impact of their investments on biodiversity . <p> The French asset managers chose Iceberg Data Lab and I Care &amp; Consult as a consortium , with the companies having joined forces to expand a metric quantifying corporates ' impact on biodiversity across their activities . <p> In a statement , the four asset managers said the tool 's expansion would help investors integrate impacts on nature and biodiversity into their risk assessments and research , and that the transparency of the selected approach would contribute to the " required convergence towards more standard and comparable metrics " . <p> " This should serve as an important catalyst for private sector action , with ripple effects throughout our economies , " they said . <p> The appointment of Iceberg Data Lab and I Care &amp; Consult concludes a tender process initiated early this year , accompanied by an investor statement on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by representatives of 30 leading institutional investors around the world . <p> Biodiversity is moving up the agenda in parts of the investment industry . Away from the French asset managers , Robeco , for example , has said the loss of biodiversity is a bigger threat than climate change and that more effort should be put into measuring the biodiversity impact of portfolios . <p> It called on other investors to sign up to a ' Finance for Biodiversity Pledge ' that will be launched on Friday in connection with the 75th United Nations ' General Assembly . <p> Earlier this month The Paulson Institute and The Nature Conservancy published a report in which they said there was a gap of between $598bn to $824bn per year over the next 10 years between what is needed to sustainably manage biodiversity and what is currently invested in conserving nature . <p> Another example of the broadening of the environmental focus in the financial industry to beyond climate change is the plan to create a Task Force for Nature-related Financial Disclosures , with AXA and BNP Paribas among 10 financial institutions @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The initiative is funded by the UK government and is being coordinated by Global Canopy , UNDP , UNEP FI and WWF . <p> In their statement today , the four French asset managers said they would continue to collaborate with key actors and initiatives in the biodiversity field , naming the Task Force for Nature-related Financial Disclosures as one . <p> " The investors recognise that much more needs to be done to promote corporate transparency on nature-related dependencies and impacts , " they said . <p> " Also , many challenges remain to capture nature 's complexity into manageable metrics and to integrate the latest science into investment decision making .
