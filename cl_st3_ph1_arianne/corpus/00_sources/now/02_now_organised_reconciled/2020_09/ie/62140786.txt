<h> Ireland may not have to reduce pesticide use by 50% <p> The implementation of the Farm to Fork Strategy in this country is still to be decided on . However , it is unlikely that a blanket approach will be taken to reduce the levels of pesticide and fertiliser use across the entire EU . <p> For example , Ireland uses a relatively low amount of pesticides compared to other EU countries and so may not be required to reduce pesticide use by the 50% outlined in the document . <p> This was one message coming from Dale Crammond of the Department of Agriculture 's Climate Change Division who was speaking at the Teagasc Crops Forum on Thursday ( September 17 ) . <p> To put this into context , from 2011 to 2018 Ireland had the second largest reduction in pesticide sales overall when measured against 14 other states with complete data .
