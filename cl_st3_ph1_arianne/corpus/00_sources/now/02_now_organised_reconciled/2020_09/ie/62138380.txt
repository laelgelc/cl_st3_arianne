<h> FTSE Russell <h> Sovereign climate risk -- steering through the rear-view mirror <p> For investors and policymakers , effectively addressing climate change poses particular challenges -- not the least of which is the long time-lag between actions and outcomes . The inertia in the climate system means that greenhouse gases emitted today will warm the planet for decades to come . <p> Investors also face a more immediate challenge . Collecting a level-playing field of consistent data sufficient to understand the climate risk faced by sovereign issuers -- that is , comparable national-level data -- takes time . This means that , much more than with other risk factors , investors are steering using the rear-view mirror . <p> The decisive rebound in the EPS revision cycle from the pandemic lows in June has provided much of the firepower behind this summer 's risk rally . But signs that this uptrend may be losing steam bear watching .
