<h> UK consultants push for improved sustainable investment practices <p> Several investment consulting firms in the UK have set up the Investment Consultants Sustainability Working Group , with the aim of seeking to improve sustainable investment practices across the investment industry . <p> They believe that while the investment industry has made progress on several areas within sustainability , more urgent and constructive action needs to be taken to address systemic challenges , particularly in relation to climate change , an announcement stated . <p> Luba Nikulina , global head of research at WTW , said : " It is critical that we use our collective voice now to not only influence how the investment industry approaches sustainability ; but importantly to provide greater clarity on what is needed and more support on what actions can be taken to make a tangible difference . " <p> As a critical link between asset managers and asset owners , the group said its members are optimally placed to promote a focus on sustainability which could ultimately help to drive greater change within the industry . <p> The group has outlined six commitments @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of stakeholders , including asset owners , asset managers and regulators ; <p> Seek investment outcomes that are genuinely sustainable and not treat sustainability as a tick box exercise ; <p> Align with , and support , existing industry bodies and initiatives ; <p> Support their respective clients that are too small to meaningfully engage with industry initiatives ; <p> Create a guiding set of principles that indicate good practice with practical advice ; <p> Be a body where regulators and other stakeholders can seek input when they need a view from investment consultants . <p> The consultancies have also identified six individual workstreams that they plan to explore initially : reporting , asset owners , asset managers , stewardship , regulation , and lastly innovation , new products and impact investing . <p> On areas where there is already work underway by industry bodies , the working group said that input from its members could be even more impactful . It will look to support current initiatives and provide additional input in key areas where there may be further work to do . <p> All members of the working @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it said , and while some of the firms ' activities extend to other services , clients or jurisdictions , these will fall outside of the group 's scope . <p> The member firms will take appropriate measures to avoid any conflicts of interest between the group 's work and these other activities and will ensure that the group 's activities are conducted in accordance with all applicable laws . <p> Joanne Job , managing director and head of research at MJ Hudson Allenbridge , said : " As the momentum underpinning sustainable investing continues to gather pace , we believe that we need to act together and align our approaches in order to really make a lasting difference . <p> " As investment consultants , we believe that we have a responsibility to enhance transparency and standardisation across the investment industry , across traditional and alternative investments . This will be the best way to not only effect change , but also to improve standards across the board . "
