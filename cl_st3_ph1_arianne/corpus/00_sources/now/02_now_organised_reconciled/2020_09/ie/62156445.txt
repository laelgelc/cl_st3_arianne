" <p> Gell , a published poet with a PhD in marine ecology , grew up on the Isle of Man and has over 20 years ' experience working in marine science and conservation . For 13 years she was the marine biodiversity officer for the Isle of Man government and now works on climate change and ecosystem policy . <p> She said : " I 'm very excited to be sharing the wonders of the Isle of Man 's marine life .
