<h> EU calling for ideas ' inspired by Apollo 11 ' to restore soil health <p> The European Commission is calling for people to present it with ambitious ideas for tackling climate change and improving soil health -- so ambitious , that it wants them to be " inspired by Apollo 11 " . <p> The commission has opened a call for ideas on new EU missions , with submissions from citizens focusing on " how to adapt to climate change , fight cancer , build climate-neutral and smart cities and ensure healthy oceans , soil and food " . <p> The collected ideas that are submitted will feed into the design of the new ' Missions under Horizon Europe ' , a novelty in the next EU research and innovation framework programme . <p> The commission said that the research and innovation mission it hopes to carry out on the back of submissions is to be " partly inspired by the Apollo 11 mission to put a man on the moon " . <p> " As part of the future Horizon Europe programme , missions will help define clear @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ challenges facing our world . <p> " For this we need citizens to express their views , make proposals and engage in their design and implementation . Together , we will make Europe healthier , greener and more resilient . " <p> The results of the latest call for ideas will be presented on September 22 to 24 . The selected missions will be announced at the end of 2020 and launched in 2021 . <p> According to the commission , the final missions will play a " crucial role in achieving EU priorities , such as the European Green Deal " . <p> At global level , it is hoped that the missions will contribute to meeting the Sustainable Development Goals . <h> The importance of soil health and food <p> One of the five main " mission areas " that the EU is accepting submissions for is improving soil health and food . <p> Advertisement <p> According to the commission , this area is important because : <p> " Land and soils are essential for all life-sustaining processes on our planet .
