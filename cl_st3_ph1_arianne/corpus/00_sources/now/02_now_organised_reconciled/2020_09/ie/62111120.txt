<h> ? 107,000 in funding approved for walking trails around bogs <p> Minister of State with responsibility for heritage Malcolm Noonan has announced today ( Friday , September 4 ) that grant funding of just under ? 107,000 has been approved for peatland community initiatives this year . <p> The funding will go towards eight community groups and organisations for projects focused on the conservation and revitalisation of raised bog Special Areas of Conservation ( SAC ) , Natural Heritage Areas ( NHA ) and other peatland areas . <p> Deputy Noonan said that due to the " level of interest and the quality of applications " received from community groups and environmental organisations under the Peatlands Community Engagement Scheme , the award was more than double the original allocation of funding of ? 50,000 . <h> ' Championing of conservation benefits ' <p> This funding is aimed at supporting the " development , maintenance and upgrading of walking trails and looped walks " around bogs in counties Offaly , Roscommon , Cavan , Galway and Westmeath . <p> This includes : a peat carbon project ; the installation of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ production of promotional videos and website creation ; the installation of recycled plastic seating and wooden bench seats ; a moth trap and arachnid study ; the restoration of old bog equipment ; the erection of wooden access control gates ; and waste removal . <p> Commenting , deputy Noonan said that during the pandemic , people " reconnected with nature and the outdoors " and that it is encouraging to see " such a sense of place and championing of conservation benefits for local peatlands " . <p> " Peatlands are a unique habitat that we have in Ireland and play an important role in supporting biodiversity and climate action for the benefit of all . <p> " The involvement of local community groups is paramount to biodiversity conservation .
