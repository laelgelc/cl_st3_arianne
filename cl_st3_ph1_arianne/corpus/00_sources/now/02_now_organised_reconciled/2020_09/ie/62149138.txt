<p> Shellfish studies require more communication with the fishing industry to ensure the survival of cockles into the future , according to scientists at University College Cork . <p> Cockles are a well-known shellfish across Europe . It is a species valued for its meat , cultural symbolism and ecological value . <p> Records of cockles can be found throughout history , from a wide range of sources including museums , scientific works and fisheries records . <p> However , scientists involved in the collaborative Europe-wide COCKLES project say they are concerned about the lack of focus of the previous work conducted . <p> This is particularly worrisome , as an understanding of cockles ' past survival is essential in order to predict how species will fare in a future of climate change . <p> A study recently published in PLOS ONE , led by scientists at University College Cork , examined a large volume of historical information about cockles .
