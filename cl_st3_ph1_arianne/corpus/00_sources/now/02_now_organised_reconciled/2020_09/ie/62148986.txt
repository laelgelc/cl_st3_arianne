<h> ' Largest ever ' asset manager ESG evaluation launched <p> More than 1,700 asset managers globally have been sent a request for information about environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) issues facing investors and their ESG-centric investing strategies , according to the UK industry bodies behind the move . <p> TISAtech , a new digital platform launched by The Investing and Saving Alliance ( TISA ) , sent the request for information to the asset managers . The Pensions and Lifetime Savings Association ( PLSA ) is collaborating with TISAtech on the initiative as part of its ' Investing for Good ' programme . <p> The deadline for response is 22 October . IPE was told that 25 asset managers registered to respond within the first hour of the request for information being posted via investRFP.com . <p> The latter said a primary objective of the request was to support UK pension schemes ' active management of their exposure to climate change ( risk ) , in line with Taskforce on Climate-related Financial Disclosures recommendations . <p> " The aggregate data will be used to present those @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ capabilities to UK pension trustees and asset owners , highlighting expertise and innovation in the investment process , " it said . <p> A report is also to be published to all key industry policymakers and stakeholders " as a mechanism to drive change and greater engagement across the industry " . <p> UK pension schemes and their trustees are to be granted access to the asset manager responses at no cost . <p> " This results of this work has the potential to create unprecedented transparency in the industry , " <p> Joe Dabrowski , head of DB , LGPS and standards at the PLSA <p> Joe Dabrowski , head of DB , LGPS and standards at the PLSA , said pension schemes were " keenly aware " of the impact of climate change on their investments and on saver outcomes but needed the services and support of asset managers to investor accordingly and adapt to a fast-changing regulatory environment . <p> " This results of this work has the potential to create unprecedented transparency in the industry , " he said .
