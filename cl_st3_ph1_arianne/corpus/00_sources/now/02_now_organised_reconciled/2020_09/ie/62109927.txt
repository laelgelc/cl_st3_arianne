<h> Back on track : Tipperary rail service return is welcomed <p> The reopening of train services on the Ballybrophy line last Monday has been given a broad welcome . The line closed in March under Covid-19 restrictions . <p> Sinn Fein reprentative for the Nenagh area Damian O'Donoghue said that he very much welcomed the reopening of the line . <p> " This is great news for area . The line passes through Nenagh , Cloughjordan , Birdhill and Roscrea and is very important to people in North Tipperary . This is overdue , but very welcome , " said Mr O'Donoghue . <p> He said that he and Deputy Martin Browne had corresponded with Irish Rail and the Department of Transport over the last few months and he had been concerned that the route might not be reopened at all . <p> " Irish Rail did inform us in June that they planned to reopen the line once the Covid-19 restrictions were lifted so I am glad to finally see it happening , " he said . <p> Mr O'Donoghue commended the workers who were working on the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ would like to see the service upgraded and a high speed train used along the route .
