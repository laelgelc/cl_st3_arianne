<h> EV Policy for Cars is Coming Soon -- Hammad Azhar <p> It was revealed by the Federal Minister for Industries and Production , Hammad Azhar via a tweet that the four-wheel electric vehicles ( EV ) policy is currently in works . Soon , a proposal will be presented to the cabinet once the Ministries of Climate Change and Science &amp; Tech offers consultation regarding the matter . <p> Through his twitter , he revealed that the Engineering Development Board has collaborated with the Ministry of Industries to look into the dominant policy for the automotive sector . Furthermore , the tweet said that both entities are looking at options to help lessen the practices where automakers charge high due to vehicles overbooking . <p> Hammad Azhar tweeted , " Previously we announced Electric Vehicle policy for 2-3 wheelers , buses , and trucks . We are now working on EV policy for 4 wheelers and will be presenting policy proposals to cabinet in this regard after consultations with Ministries of Climate Change and Science &amp; Tech . " <p> Also , earlier in response to a user question @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Pakistan Federal Minister for Science and Technology Fawad Chaudhry said , <p> " The standards for vehicles made in Pakistan have been set . The initial standards will be submitted to the cabinet for approval within a month . I believe that it was a very difficult task . There were many obstacles placed in my way , but I will do what I set out to do . "
