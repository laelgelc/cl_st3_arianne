<p> Pakistan , Britain agree to foment ties in all areas of common interest <p> September 09 , 2020 <p> Pakistan and Britain have agreed to continue work together to strengthen bilateral cooperation in all areas of common interest . <p> The understanding was reached during a video teleconference between Foreign Secretary Sohail Mahmood and British Minister of State for South Asia and the Commonwealth Lord Tariq Ahmad . <p> Views were exchanged on matters relating to bilateral cooperation , regional situation , and multilateral issues including climate change and Commonwealth . <p> The two sides discussed ways to further advance the mutual cooperation . <p> Bilaterally , the importance of frequent high-level exchanges , a closer trade and economic partnership , and stronger role of the diaspora in forging a robust bilateral relationship was emphasized . <p> In the regional context , the Foreign Secretary reiterated Pakistan 's steadfast support for the Afghan peace process and underlining the need for the earliest commencement of Intra-Afghan negotiations . <p> He also underscored the importance of an inclusive , broad-based and comprehensive political settlement for durable peace and stability in Afghanistan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the grave situation in Indian illegally occupied Jammu and Kashmir , marked by egregious violations of human rights , illegal measures to change the demographic structure of the occupied territory , and threat to peace and security posed by intensified ceasefire violations and India 's belligerent actions . <p> He underlined the need for the international community to play its part in diffusion of tensions and peaceful resolution of the Jammu and Kashmir dispute .
