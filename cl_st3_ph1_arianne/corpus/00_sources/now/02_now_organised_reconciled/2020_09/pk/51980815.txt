<h> Stimulus hosts regional final round of ClimateLaunchpad <p> Islamabad - Stimulus , the Pakistan partner for Climate Launchpad Global Competition , hosted the Regional Final of the competition on September 17 &amp; 18 , 2020 , with support from Engross Foundation . With participation from 14 countries across Eastern Europe , Middle East &amp; Central Asia , the event attracted 200+ participants who logged in to attend the event virtually , across the region . <p> Climate Launchpad is the world 's largest green business ideas competition with the mission to unlock the world 's clean-tech potential that addresses climate change . Pakistan is participating for the second time in the Climate Launchpad program along with 57 countries . A total of 41 teams participated in the Regional Final round including 3 teams from Pakistan from which two teams , O'Naps and Aabshar have been shortlisted for the semi-final round on September 30 , 2020 .
