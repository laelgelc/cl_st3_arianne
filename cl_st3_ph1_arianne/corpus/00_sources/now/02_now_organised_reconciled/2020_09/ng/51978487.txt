United Nations General Assembly yesterday . <p> According to the Nigerian leader , the disbursement of the sum is part of efforts to reduce the suffering of Nigerians imposed by the COVID-19 pandemic palliatives , " he said . <p> Mr Buhari restated Nigeria 's commitment to working with other member states to promote the wellbeing of citizens . <p> " I should also state that Nigeria is committed to
