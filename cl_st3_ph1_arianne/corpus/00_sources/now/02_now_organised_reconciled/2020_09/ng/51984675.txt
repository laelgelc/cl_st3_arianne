<h> Africa Needs $3.2trn To Combat Climate Change <p> BUSAN , ( Sundiata Post ) -- African needs no less than $3.2 trillion by 2030 to combat the devastating effect of climate change on the continent . <p> President of the African Development Bank , Dr. Akinwumi Adesina , who made this known at the 53rd Annual Meetings of the Bank ( https : //am.AfDB.org/en ) , in Busan , Korea , noted that of the US $820 billion in climate finance flows for 2015 and 2016 , only US $16 billion was for Africa . This represents a mere 2% of the total . <p> Scientific evidence shows that African economies are already reeling from the devastating effects of climate change , further exacerbating their development challenges . Of the 10 countries in the world that are most threatened by climate change , seven are in Africa . <p> Cognizant of this situation , the African Development Bank ( www.AfDB.org ) led the way to what may turn out to be Africa 's most decisive action to ensure that the continent is not short-changed by climate finance . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : //am.AfDB.org/en ) , in Busan , Korea , the institution brought stakeholders together for an " open dialogue " to discuss the establishment of the Africa Financial Alliance for Climate Change ( AFAC ) .
