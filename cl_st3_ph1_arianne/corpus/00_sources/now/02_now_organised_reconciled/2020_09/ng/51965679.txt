<h> Wave of Devastating Forest Fires in U.S is a Result of Climate Change -- Experts <p> The wave of devastating forest fires currently sweeping parts of the western United States is , in part , a result of man-made climate change , an expert at Germany 's Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research ( PIK ) said on Friday . <p> " Man-made climate change had contributed significantly to an increase in drought-stricken areas and extremely dried-out vegetation in the western U.S. , ' ' the institute 's wildfire expert , Kirsten Thonicke , told dpa . <p> According to Thonicke , small spark and extreme winds were enough to set off ferocious blazes , which can cause considerable damage . <p> " Through this chain of events , climate change contributes to the fact that forest fires take on such proportions , with these results , ' ' she added .
