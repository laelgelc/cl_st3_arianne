<h> Hundreds Of Anti-Coal Protesters Break Into German Mine <p> Hundreds of anti-coal protesters entered a mine in western Germany on Saturday to protest the continued extraction and use of fossil fuels . <p> Environmentalists object to the German government 's decision to allow the mining and burning of coal in the country until 2038 , a deadline the activists say is too late to effectively tackle climate change . <p> Activists are also protesting the planned destruction of several villages to make way for the expansion of the Garzweiler strip mine , west of Cologne . <p> Police and utility company RWE said some protesters broke into the mine and coal storage facilities early Saturday . <p> The Garzweiler mine and nearby power plants have been a focus of protests for several years . Environmentalists say they are among the biggest sources of harmful pollution and greenhouse gas emissions in Europe .
