<h> Researchers at IITA , along with partners from the NextGen Cassava Breeding Project , have uncovered new details about the genetic architecture of cassava , which is one of Africa 's most vital crops . They analyzed large breeding populations composed of 5130 clones developed in Nigeria at the IITA Cassava Breeding Program . <p> The 4-year study involved extensive multilocational testing at four IITA field trials in Nigeria .
