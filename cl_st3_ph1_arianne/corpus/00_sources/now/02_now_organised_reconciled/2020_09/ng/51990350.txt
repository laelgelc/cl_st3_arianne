<h> Ex-AFAN chief gives reasons for rising cost of food items <p> Dr Tunde Arosanyin , a farmer , has identified insecurity , farmers/herdsmen clashes , poor mechanization , and unstable government policies as factors responsible for the high cost of food items . <p> Arosanyin , a former Chairman of the All Farmers Association of Nigeria ( AFAN ) in Kogi on Tuesday said that other variables included poor credit facility to farmers and the negative effect of climate change . <p> He also blamed the inadequacy of extension services and the high price of agriculture input among others for the high cost of food items . <p> Arosanyin , who is the National Coordinator , Zero Hunger Commodity Farmers Association , said that even though the country had enormous potentials in the agriculture sub-sector of the economy , it had not been able to harness the benefits . <p> Many farmers , he said had been displaced and killed in the North East , North West , and North Central by insurgents and bandits . <p> " Farmers and Nomadic Fulani clashes in the middle belt down South @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change has impacted on our food production in the last 15 years negatively from flooding to rain failure and drought . <p> " Unstable government policies is another critical factor .
