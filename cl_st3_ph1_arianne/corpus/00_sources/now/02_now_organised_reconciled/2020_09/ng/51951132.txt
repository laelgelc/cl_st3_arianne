<p> Chinese inflation slowed in August as a slowdown in the surging price of pork tempered food costs . The Consumer Price Index ( CPI ) moderated to 2.4% y/y ( July : 2.7% y/y ) , the lowest figure since May . Month-on-Month , CPI was up 0.4% ( July : 0.6% m/m ) . Food inflation eased to 11.2% y/y ( July:13.2% y/y ) on the back of the moderation in pork price inflation , which slowed last month from a higher base a year ago , after prices began to surge in August 2019 as African swine fever decimated China 's pig herds . Pork prices rose 52.6% in August from a year earlier , easing sharply from an 85.7% y/y jump in July . Increased food supply in markets , following the containment the country 's flood crisis , also contributed to the slowdown in inflation . Elsewhere , core inflation , rose 0.5% y/y in August , unchanged from July , suggesting domestic demand remained soft .
