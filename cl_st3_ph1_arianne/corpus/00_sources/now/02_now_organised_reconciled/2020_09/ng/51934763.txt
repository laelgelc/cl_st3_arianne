<h> FG To Lift 100m Nigerians out of poverty through Waste Recycling , Tree Planting <p> The federal government has explained how it plans to lift 100 million Nigerians out of poverty . <p> The government said it would achieve this through the planting of trees , waste recycling , and setting up and equipping vocational centres , which would be done in collaboration with foreign partners . <p> The government ruled out the option of distributing motorcycles , grinding machines and generators , describing tactics as temporal empowerment . <p> The head of Inter-Ministerial Expert Technical Committee on the Coordination of Inter- Governmental Projects under Federal Ministry Special Duties and Intergovernmental Affairs , Bassey Etim-Ikang , stated this yesterday , in Abuja , at the end of a two-day meeting , said the committee would compile its report and forward it to the committee of nine ministers in charge of the project implementation . <p> Etim-Ikang , who is the director , Human Resources , explained , " We are looking at the hanging fruits . What we do is to form a cluster , and in any cluster , we have a link MDAs . <p> " Climate change is not rainfall and sunshine . We look at it from the environment that we can provide an alternative . <p> " We are looking at waste to wealth , to address power and others . We are looking at empowering our people when it comes to vocational centres . <p> " We have looked at cottage industries along economic corridors . We have areas with surplus fruits , soybeans , cassava and palm oil .
