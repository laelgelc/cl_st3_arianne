<p> " In the 2020 Wet Season farming , 11,250 farmers will be empowered in Adamawa by the Central Bank of Nigeria Anchor Borrowers Programme in collaboration with Maize Association of Nigeria and Unity Bank . <p> " In Adamawa state , in 2018 and 2019 , the Anchor Borrowers Programme empowered 4,293 small scale farmers , through the provision of inputs , extension services and linkage with market outlets , " Mu'azu said . <p> He said that the farmers empowerment was a soft loan which would be repaid with farm produce . <p> The chairman observed that the rate of population growth , climate change , insurgency , farmers/herders conflict , flood effect and COVID-19 pandemic was alarming and threatening national food production in 2020 . <p> Adamu Yusuf , the CBN representative , said that the apex bank was committed to ensure effective national agricultural growths . <p> Yusuf called on the beneficiary farmers to utilise the inputs given to them judiciously , to improve their standard of living and national food production . <p> He noted that the inputs were given as soft loam to famers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ due , to enable the programme capture additional farmers in the next farming season . <p> Speaking on behalf of the Adamawa Government , Kabir Bello , commended the Federal Government for the gesture . <p> Mr Bello said the loan was convered by the Nigeria Agricultural Insurance to reduce negative and natural risks on the farmers . <p> According to him , the programme will go a long way in improving food production , adding that the state government will ensure farmers make effective use of the inputs . <p> Responding on behalf of the beneficiaries , Usman Michika , State Chairman , All Farmers Association of Nigeria , AFAN , also lauded the federal government for initiating the Anchor Borrowers Programme . <p> Mr Michika , however , urged the CBN to extend the programme to cover more farmers in the state .
