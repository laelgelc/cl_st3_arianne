<h> Opening Remarks at the 2020 International Conference on Sustainable Development <p> Thank you for the opportunity to speak to this distinguished audience . <p> As you are only too aware , we are in the midst of a crisis like no other . The pandemic has sent shockwaves through the global economy . People are worried about their lives and livelihoods . Many businesses are either closed or waiting for customers to return . Governments are stretching their spending capacity . And the stability of financial systems is threatened . Policymakers all over the world are looking for ways to contain the spread of the virus while limiting the economic fallout . <p> And this is a truly global crisis . The COVID-19 virus does not stop at borders . For this reason , coordinated , international strategies to fight the virus and its economic impact are more effective than going it alone . <p> It is time now for global cooperation and multilateral coordination .
