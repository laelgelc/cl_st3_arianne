<h> Low grapes production attributed to climate change , Dodoma famers <p> ? ? ? ? ? ? ? CLIMATE change that had contributed to the increase of rains in Dodoma Region this year is among the reasons for the drop of the region 's grapes production this season . <p> At the weekend a reporter visited areas with grapes farms in Mpunguzi , Mbabala and Hombolo wards and witnessed insignificant grape production compared to previous seasons and farmers said this year production was not good and the harvests had all been sold away even before the end of the season . <p> " This year the harvest was insignificant due to too much rains , the seedlings were destroyed before flowering , whilst other did not bear the fruit at all , said Maneno Joseph , a grape farmer . <p> This paper also spoke with grapes dealers who said the crop is unavailable , one has to wait for a long period of time to collect the required quantity . <p> Zawadi Manzije , a grapes buyer said : " Customers know that the grapes are grown here @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ buy at 1,000/- or even 2,000/- compared to the past price which was between 300/- and 500/- only . " <p> Archard Kato , owner of Alko Vintages wine factory said the demand for grapes had been great despite low production from farmers , and added that wine factory owners have been greatly affected as well . <p> Speaking on the issue , the Permanent Secretary in the Ministry of Agriculture Gerald Kusasya admitted the drop of grapes harvests this season due to both heavy rains and the world market due to coronavirus . <p> He said in the last season grapes production went up to 15,000 tonnes compared to 2015/16 season when the production was 8,336 tonnes . <p> However , he mentioned his ministry 's strategies to increase production as including enabling the TARI-Makutuporato increase production of grapes seedlings from 100,000 to 300,000 . <p> " The centre has been provided with funds from the ministry including a new tractor to expand production of seedlings for grape farmers . <h> Top Stories <p> Mwanza Regional Commissioner , John Mongella ( 1st-R ) listening to GGML senior communication officer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ activities at the ongoing third Mining and Technology Exhibition being held in the region .
