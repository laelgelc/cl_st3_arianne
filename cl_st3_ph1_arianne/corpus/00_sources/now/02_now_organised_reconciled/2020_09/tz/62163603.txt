CONCERNS over health , environment and employment issues have dominated the 23rd model of the United Nations General Assembly taking place in Arusha attended by over 200 youth from around the world . <p> TIMUN Coordinator , Philemon Mweleka . <p> This version of Tanzania International Model United Nations ( TIMUN ) is a simulation of the United Nations General Assembly where young people from the youth essentially become more creative and productive if they are healthy , " explained the TIMUN Coordinator , Philemon Mweleka . <p> According to Mweleka , environment matters are also of paramount concern because in the wake of global warming and issues of climate change the youth are very much affected with related effects . <p> As for employment , it was pointed out during the mock
