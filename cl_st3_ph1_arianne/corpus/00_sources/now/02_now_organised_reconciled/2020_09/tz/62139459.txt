SMALLHOLDER farmers in Pawaga village of Iringa District in Iringa Region are miles ahead of their counterparts in other villages when it comes to reducing loss and waste in the rice value chain . And it is not about storing the crop in modern warehouses ; - <p> -the farmers do n't sell unprocessed rice and they have acquired their own brand for their products . <p> Currently smallholder rice farmers in 12 irrigation schemes in Iringa District strive to reduce loss and waste before the crop is harvested and when it is transported for storage by using improved agriculture technology in the preparation of farms as well as during planting . Warehouse system is a new development that enables farmers to reduce postharvest losses . <p> With training and awareness raising campaigns , the famers are able to manage water for irrigation , ensuring that they use only that amount of water needed for the crops to grow well .
