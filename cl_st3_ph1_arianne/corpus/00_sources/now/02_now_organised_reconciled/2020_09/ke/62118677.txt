<h> Tea Factories Affiliated with KTDA Ramp Up Firewood Plantations <p> NAIROBI , Kenya , Sept 2 -- The 69 KTDA-managed tea factories have acquired 824 acres for tree plantations in the last two years as they ramp up a programme that will see them become self-sufficient in firewood supply and reduce energy costs . <p> Kenya Tea Development Agency Management Services says the acquisitions have pushed the total land holdings for the wood fuel programme to 21,550 acres spread out across the country . <p> Out of these , 16,135 acres have already been planted with trees in various stages of growth while the remaining 5,400 acres are in the process of preparation for planting . <p> The Agency 's MD Alfred Njagi says wood fuel programme is a project meant to ensure all tea factories have commercially and environmentally sustainable sources of firewood . <p> According to the MD , Energy costs comprise up to 25 percent of the cost of tea production and wood is the cheapest source of energy- up to four times cheaper than oil . <p> " Planting of trees is meant to provide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as manage climate change by ensuring constant re-planting/ regeneration of cleared areas , " Njagi said . <p> KTDA started the wood fuel project in 2001 and has been accelerating the programme especially since 2018 when the government issued restrictions on logging .
