<h> Attachments <p> The children and youth consultation on the climate crisis and Disaster Risk Reduction ( DRR ) was conducted from August 2019 to January 2020 , across 12 countries and 1 regional consultation , with representatives from 21 countries , in Asia and the Pacific . Nearly 10,000 children and youth participated in the consultation . The consultation was jointly organised by a group of partners that included the Asia Pacific Coalition for Safe Schools , UNICEF , and UN Major Group Children and Youth and UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction , World Vision International , Plan International , and Save the Children . <p> During the consultation , children and youth shared their views on climate-related disasters . They recognised the need to strengthen policies and plans to mitigate disaster risks and promote resilience . <p> This report is a compilation of the face-to-face and online consultation data gathered . Children and youth in Asia and the Pacific are no strangers to the negative impact of the climate crisis and call for a more inclusive approach to DRR and Climate Change Adaptation ( CCA ) processes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the key findings include : <p> 77% of children and youth have noticed more climate-related disasters locally in the last two years . <p> 99% of children and youth reported experiencing disaster risks in the past 12 months , with hydro-meteorological disasters risks ( e.g. flooding , drought , typhoon/cyclone , El Ni ? o , and La Ni ? a accounting for over 50% of the reported experience . <p> 23% reported experiencing extreme temperatures , while 30% experienced floods or more severe rainfall . A decrease in economic opportunities is also reported as a visible impact of climate change . <p> Children recognise themselves as the most vulnerable during disasters followed by the elderly , people with disabilities and pregnant women . <p> 45% of children and youth expressed that people with disabilities are not given enough support to prepare for disasters . <p> Formal education is the top channel through which children and youth acquire knowledge about the climate crisis and DRR , followed by news and social media . <p> Mainstreaming and localising children and youth voices in climate discussions <p> The findings of the consultation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ children and youth stakeholder-group action statement and presented to governments and regional bodies at the next Asia-Pacific Ministerial Conference on Disaster Risk Reduction ( APMCDRR ) . <p> COVID-19 has severely impacted the vulnerable communities and children in Asia-Pacific .
