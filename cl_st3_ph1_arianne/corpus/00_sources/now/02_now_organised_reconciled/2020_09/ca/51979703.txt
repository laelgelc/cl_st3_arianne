<h> Carbon-tax hearings begin at top court as lawyers spar over provinces ' rights <p> The Supreme Court of Canada began historic hearings about whether the federal government 's carbon tax is constitutional , or whether it unconstitutionally breaches the provinces ' rights to develop and manage their own natural resources . <p> It 's a mammoth legal wrangle spread over two days , and it 's the first time the nine judges have been physically together in the courtroom since early spring . <p> On Tuesday , judges were seated behind Plexiglas , spaced two metres apart on a redesigned bench . There were long pauses between submissions , as court officials wiped down the podium each time a different lawyer spoke . <p> The appeals , first scheduled for March but put off due to the COVID-19 shutdown , are the most significant and complex legal questions the highest court will hear this year .
