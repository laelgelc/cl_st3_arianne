<h> Agricultural policy ideas should be at the forefront of Canada 's post-pandemic recovery plan <p> As we head closer to September 23 and the federal Throne Speech , some of us will be listening closely for words like " agriculture , " " family farm " and " rural communities ... " <p> In all of the discussions regarding the pandemic and recovery scenarios in public fora , I have rarely heard mention of food security or agriculture . Even when there was near panic regarding food sources and seeds earlier this year , there was more talk about what might happen if the border with the United States remained closed . <p> Little or nothing was ever said about food self-sufficiency .
