As the economy reopens , consumers are starting to return to auto showrooms -- but finding an electric vehicle continues to be a problem , especially outside Quebec , British Columbia and Ontario . <p> A series of roadblocks are contributing to the shortfall , including battery shortages and manufacturers prioritizing shipments to China and Europe rather than North America .
