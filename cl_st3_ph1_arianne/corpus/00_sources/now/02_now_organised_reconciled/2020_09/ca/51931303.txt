<p> MacKenzie Scott , the novelist ex-wife of Amazon CEO and wealthiest man in the world Jeff Bezos , has been proclaimed the wealthiest woman in the world , according to Bloomberg 's Billionaire Index . <p> Scott has a net worth of US$68 billion , the index reported , which pushed her past L'Oreal heiress Francoise Bettencourt Meyers who has a net worth of US$66.8 billion . <p> Scott is now the 12th most wealthiest person in the world . Her ex-husband still ranks at the top of the chart with a net worth of US$207 billion . <p> More than half of Scott 's wealth comes from her divorce settlement in 2019 , in which she received a quarter of Amazon shares , totalling a four percent stake worth more than US$35 billion at the time . The couple who had been married for 25 years , announced their decision to divorce in January 2019 via a joint statement posted on Twitter : " As our family and close friends know , after a long period of loving exploration and trial separation , we have decided to divorce @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hours after their statement was released , Bezos 's relationship with former news anchor Lauren Sanchez was confirmed . According to the Enquirer , the couple began dating shortly after he and Scott separated and still remain together . <p> In a July blog post , Scott wrote that she donated US$1.7 billion of her wealth in the past year to several different causes including racial equality , LGBTQ rights , public health and climate change . In a post on her Medium account , she wrote , " there 's no question in my mind that anyone 's personal wealth is the product of a collective effort , and of social structures which present opportunities to some people , and obstacles to countless others . " <p> However as the value of her Amazon stake grew , Scott earned the money back by the end of the same week .
