<h> Alberta farmers join coalition calling for feds to shore up food supply in wake of COVID-19 <p> Alberta farmers have joined a national coalition of producers calling for the federal government to help producers improve their climate resiliency after COVID-19 impacted national food supply . <p> The Farmers for Climate Solutions ( FCS ) is calling on federal policymakers to incentivize farmers to turn their farms into green energy powerhouses and provide incentives for climate-friendly farming . They are also calling for help turning farmers into mentors and increased supports for new and young farmers . They also want to see farmers who reduce their climate risk rewarded . <p> Brenda Barritt , a rancher who operates Earth Work Farms near Alix , said FCS wants policymakers to help producers turn their land into climate solutions instead of carbon producers . <p> " I really want to see programs in place that really just help us invest in those practices that give us long-term benefits and then even help us out economically , " said Barritt . <p> Barritt said producers do n't want government handouts that dictate what @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said they need incentives that will help farmers implement practices that create a healthy future and improve their bottom line . <p> " So no-till cropping , I understand you get carbon credits for that .
