Fridays for Future Climate Action Group is holding a rally that day from noon to 2 p.m. in front of Province House on Grafton Street . Fridays for Future group plans to review the throne speech and take an opportunity at the Sept. 25 rally to speak on its compatibility with the principles of a green and just recovery . <p> Greta Thunberg recently offered a grim assessment of progress on the climate crisis since she began her Friday school strikes two years ago in Sweden .
