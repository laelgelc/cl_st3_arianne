<h> Op-Ed : Climate Change -- The great disruptor of life as we know it <p> America 's worsening climate change problem is as polarized as its politics . Some parts of the country have been burning this month while others were underwater in extreme weather disasters . Yet this worsening situation is being ignored by many of us . <p> How bad is Climate change becoming ?
