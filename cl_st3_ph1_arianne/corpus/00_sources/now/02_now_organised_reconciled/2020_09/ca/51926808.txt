<p> A recent LED lighting retrofit and design at the Leduc Recreation Centre ( LRC ) will help to save the city over $65,000 and 593 tonnes of eCO2 per year . <p> The project was partially funded with a grant from the Municipal Climate Change Action Centre 's ( MCCAC ) Recreational Energy Conservation program . <p> " The project total was about $900,000 but through the MCCAC we were able to get an approximately $600,000 grant , " Mayor Bob Young said . <p> " It absolutely makes sense for the City of Leduc to reduce our cost by almost $70,000 per year . It 's only going to cost our taxpayers about $300,000 , so the payback will be very soon . " <p> All the lights in the LRC have been replaced with LED lighting while specific areas have been designed to allow for more efficient lighting . <p> " The LRC is a hub of activity in the Leduc region and this initiative has solidified our position as a leader in both recreational facilities and environmental stewardship , " said Young in a news release @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ facility users will benefit from for years to come . " <p> The project was part of the city 's Greenhouse Gas Reduction Plan that was adopted by council in 2019 . Along with the LED lighting retrofit , the LRC , operations building and Eco-station building are all equipped with solar panel systems to help reduce energy costs . <p> " A continuing goal for us is to make sure we 're planting trees in the city .
