<h> Government of Canada invests $1.76 million in restoration at PEI National Park and Green Gables Heritage Place following post-tropical storm Dorian Fran ? ais <p> CHARLOTTETOWN , PEI , Sept. 9 , 2020 /CNW/ - The COVID-19 pandemic reminded Canadians of the importance of connecting with nature . Parks Canada places offer Canadians opportunities to connect with their natural and cultural heritage , from coast to coast to coast . <p> Today , Wayne Easter , Member of Parliament for Malpeque , on behalf of the Minister of Environment and Climate Change and Minister responsible for Parks Canada , the Honourable Jonathan Wilkinson , announced a federal investment of $1.76 million for Parks Canada sites on Prince Edward Island . This funding will support ongoing rehabilitation efforts following the impact of post-tropical storm Dorian at Green Gables Heritage Place and PEI National Park , including Cavendish Campground . <p> The Government of Canada will invest in trail and roadway repairs , as well as clean up in areas along trails at Green Gables Heritage Place and throughout PEI National Park . In addition , some campsites will be relocated to a new loop that will be built for un-serviced tenting sites in an area of the campground less vulnerable to the impacts of climate change . A new , off-grid roofed accommodation offer will be created at the campground as well . The realities of climate change on PEI 's north shore are informing these developments to ensure the campground and infrastructure are resilient long into the future . <p> PEI National Park was heavily impacted by post-tropical storm Dorian in September 2019 . Cavendish Campground is PEI National Park 's largest campground , featuring over 200 sites , a supervised white sand beach , the popular Homestead Trail and many other amenities for visitors . <p> Parks Canada is a leader in conservation . The Agency is taking measures to ensure that sensitive plant species , cultural resources , and environmental features are protected while work to Cavendish Campground is taking place . <p> Parks Canada values the input of the First Nations and has engaged First Nations for feedback on all planned rehabilitation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Edward Island National Park and Green Gables Heritage Place are beloved by Parks Canada visitors and are key parts of PEI 's world class tourism offer . Cavendish Campground hosts 55,000 visitors annually . <p> Post-tropical storm Dorian made landfall on Prince Edward Island on September 8 , 2019 . <p> One of the known impacts of climate change on coastal locations such as PEI is an increase in the rate of coastal erosion . Parks Canada continues to monitor rates of erosion over time . <p> Early recovery efforts in PEI National Park were supported by specialized Parks Canada team members from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Quebec , Alberta and British Columbia . <p> Parks Canada estimates more than 80 percent of the trees in the Cavendish Campground area were destroyed or damaged and that 2-4 meters of coastline eroded during post-tropical storm Dorian . <p> Cavendish Campground remained closed for the 2020 operational season due to damage caused by post-tropical storm Dorian . Many significant repair and clean-up activities to address the damage caused by the storm that were planned for spring 2020 were delayed due to COVID-19 . <p> The rehabilitation of affected areas is ongoing and will continue through calendar years 2020 and 2021 . Cavendish Campground is expected to be open for camping in summer 2021.
