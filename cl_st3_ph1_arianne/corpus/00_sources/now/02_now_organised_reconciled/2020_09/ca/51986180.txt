For the unhoused , there is little shelter from the devastation brought on by this year 's wildfire season -- including some of the worst air quality in the world . <p> Environmental catastrophe is coming for us all , if it has n't already arrived . But unhoused people are unwillingly at the frontlines in places like California , where wildfires have become a yearly crisis .
