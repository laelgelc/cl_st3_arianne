It made sure Australia made neither strong international commitments to emissions reductions nor passed domestic legislation which would affect the profitable status quo . <p> Its first major victory was to destroy and prevent a modest carbon tax in 1994-95 , proposed by Keating Government environment minister John Faulkner . Profits from the tax would have funded research and development of renewable energy . <h> Questionable funding and support <p> The Australian Aluminium Council is also in the roundtable . ABARE agreed with or considered many of the Ombudsman 's recommendations . <p> Back in July 2014 , the Business Council of Australia and Innes Willox ( head of the Australian Industry Group ) both welcomed the outcome of then prime minister Tony Abbott 's policy vandalism : the repeal of the Gillard government 's carbon price .
