<h> The climate impact of stimulus spending <p> As the COVID-19 virus stealthily migrated around the world , the inevitable impact upon economic activity arrived in the form of lockdowns . <p> The stock market tumbled , and citizens of the most polluted cities were awed as the skies cleared and the stars twinkled . The incessant rumble of air transportation faded , and contrails vanished from the sky . The urban dawn chorus evoked joy in many who had not heard it for so long . <p> As governments took stock , grappling with public health and safety issues , they developed and delivered economic stimulus packages .
