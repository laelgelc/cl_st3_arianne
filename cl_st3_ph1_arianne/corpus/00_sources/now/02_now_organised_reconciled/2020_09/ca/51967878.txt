But for us all to live well within the planet 's finite limits , we need to scale and speed up efforts now , not tomorrow , " said Eva Zabey , executive director , Business for Nature . <p> Last year the IPBES international panel of scientists said a million species were at risk of extinction . <p> About two-thirds of the world 's animals - mammals , birds , fish , amphibians and reptiles - have vanished over the last 50 years , according to the World Wildlife Fund .
