<h> Billboards in Vancouver Raise Awareness of Overpopulation <p> VANCOUVER , BC , Sept. 24 , 2020 /CNW/ -- A bold billboard campaign has launched in Vancouver to increase awareness and knowledge about overpopulation . The " One Planet , One Child " ad campaign is an effort to hasten progress toward a small family norm , leading to a sustainable world population . <p> Billboards and transit stop ads in high-traffic areas feature a variety of sustainable population messages . Executive Director Dave Gardner notes , " We want to get Canada talking about overpopulation . A surprising number of journalists , elected officials , and the public are unaware the world is overpopulated or uncomfortable talking about it . Many do n't realize the solution to overpopulation -- and much of the environmental destruction it causes -- is simply to embrace the accelerating trend toward freely chosen smaller families .
