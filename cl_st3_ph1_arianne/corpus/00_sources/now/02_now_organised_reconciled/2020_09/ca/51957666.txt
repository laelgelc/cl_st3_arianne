<p> Communities across Canada will be receiving support for green innovation and climate change resiliency initiatives , the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) announced this week . <p> The County of Simcoe is receiving $585,730 for the Simcoe County Archives ' energy-efficiency measures and rooftop solar project . <p> Now that the FCM has announced funding approval , the ball is in the county 's court to come up with their 20 per cent of the funding to see the project through . <p> The county 's contribution to the project is 20% of the total prior value , or $146,433 . This request for funding from county council will be included in the 2019/2020 Archives budget , therefore county staff can not comment on the funding until after the project comes before council next year . <p> The project will include retrofitting its archives building to make it more green . <p> The county 's archives process and store sensitive documents and artifacts which represent the county 's history .
