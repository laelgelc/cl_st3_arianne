<h> London is a great city -- and much of it could be on your doorstep <p> Gavin Poole is chief executive of Here East and sits on the advisory board for Tech London Advocates . <p> ( via Getty Images ) <p> We live in cities because we want to be in the heart of the action . We are often willing to overlook the daily downfalls -- expensive rent and overpriced pints to name a few -- simply because there is no better place in the world to work , socialise and embrace opportunities than in this city . <p> However , this year the relationship with the cities we live in has changed drastically . London has transformed -- the stark images of an empty Piccadilly Circus and Covent Garden are still clear in our minds . <p> Many are speaking about a societal " reset " globally due to the effects of the Covid-19 pandemic -- re-shaping our routines and business landscape around the urgency of climate change . For example , the Centre for London has this week launched a major new report to set out long-term recommendations for the city for the first time since 1991 . This is a pivotal moment to remap London 's structure to support diverse business communities across all its boroughs -- not just those congregated in the city centre . <p> A polycentric , sustainable London is a very real development we could see emerging , and should encourage , centring around local boroughs and neighbourhoods to a greater extent . <p> Many people have realised their lengthy commutes to global HQs in central London locations were negatively contributing to their quality of life -- why do n't we look at the boroughs around us and what they can offer ? <p> The Mayor of Paris Anne Hidalgo 's " ville du quart d'heure " looks like a great place to start -- essentially , everything you need on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or journey on public transport .
