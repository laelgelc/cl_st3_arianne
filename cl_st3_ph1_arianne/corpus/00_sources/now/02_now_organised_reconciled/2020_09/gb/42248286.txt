They also encountered numerous different groups of Native American people who spoke a variety of languages and were not necessarily inclined to be welcoming . Many tricky situations were encountered by Lewis and Clark and their men -- and they were all men , except that they were greatly aided on their enormous trek by a young woman called Sacajawea , who travelled all the way with them from North Dakota to the Pacific and back . She was probably about 16 when she joined the expedition . <p> She was a native-speaker of Shoshoni , a Numic language which is a member of the Uto-Aztecan language family -- some Uto-Aztecan languages are spoken as far south as El Salvador .
