questions about the environmental sustainability of its products , as the tech giant prepares to reveal new devices to the world . <p> The Environmental Audit Committee ( EAC ) said the iPhone maker continues to swerve questions on its environmental record and how repairable its devices are . <p> Apple has pledged to become carbon neutral across its entire business , manufacturing supply chain , and product life cycle by 2030 , meaning every device it sells will have net-zero climate impact . <p> But committee chairman Philip Dunne said the firm 's " unwillingness " to address politicians leads him to believe its environmental obligations are " not taken seriously enough " . <p> Apple was invited and agreed to appear before MPs on July 16 for an inquiry into electronic waste but
