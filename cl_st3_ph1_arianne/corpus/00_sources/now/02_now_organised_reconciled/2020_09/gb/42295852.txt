<h> London riders hold protest vigil outside CPS <p> London cyclists were gathering today ( Friday July 3 ) outside the offices of the Crown Prosecution Service to protest at what they see as lenient treatment of drivers who kill cyclists . <p> The demonstrators were due to gather at 4pm at the CPS offices at 50 Ludgate Hill EC4 to hold a short vigil and read out a prepared statement demanding that the CPS take a more thorough approach when investigating cyclist deaths . <p> July 3 is the first anniversary of the death of Berkshire cyclist Anthony Maynard , who was hit from behind by a van driver who claimed he did n't see him . Another cyclist was seriously injured in the crash , but the CPS decided not to prosecute .
