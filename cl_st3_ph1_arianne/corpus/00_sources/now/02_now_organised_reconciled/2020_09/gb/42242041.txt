The Paris Agreement aims to keep increases in global temperature during this century to less than 2 ? C above pre-industrial levels . <p> We use a framework of 12 indicators spanning politics , business , technology and energy . <p> By focusing on longer-term indicators of progress and action , the Climate Progress Dashboard provides a relatively stable view of the long-run temperature rise we face . <p> On the face of it , the Covid-19 crisis appears to have sparked a turning point in global greenhouse gas emissions .
