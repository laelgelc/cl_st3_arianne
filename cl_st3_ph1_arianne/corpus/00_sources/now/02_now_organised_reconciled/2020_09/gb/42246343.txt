This lecture offered broad but , in many ways , ambiguous suggestions on this government 's priorities for civil service reform . There have already been a number of critical rebuttals to this lecture . I want to add to this by highlighting another area in which it failed to offer a vision for the civil service : how it responds to current and future ecological crises .
