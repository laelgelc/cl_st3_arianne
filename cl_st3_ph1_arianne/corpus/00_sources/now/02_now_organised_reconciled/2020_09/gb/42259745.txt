<h> We must put decarbonisation at the heart of agricultural policy <p> Farmers are ready to rise to the climate challenge , but they ca n't do it alone . We need an Agriculture Bill that provides support and certainty , writes David Drew MP <p> How we manage our land is fundamental to tackling the climate crisis . Farmers are the custodians of much of our countryside , so putting decarbonisation at the heart of agricultural policy is vital . <p> Yet this Government has failed to deliver any framework for the future of farming and agriculture , let alone one that supports an effective response to the climate emergency . <p> It 's very disappointing that , 11 months after we concluded the committee stage of the Agriculture Bill , the government has failed to allow it to continue to enactment . <p> Though there were many faults with the proposed bill , which my shadow Department for Environment , Food &amp; Rural Affairs colleagues and I pointed out at the Committee stage , we broadly welcomed the use of public money for public goods as a way @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ greater public good than reducing our contribution to climate change . However , farmers are now left in limbo with no agricultural policy in place . <p> The Environment Land Management Schemes ( ELMS ) to be piloted are crucial to enable us to understand how farms and agriculture already support decarbonisation as well as the importance of soil and water management , and biodiversity . <p> So it 's a great pity that , because of the Government 's timetable slippage , the ELMS pilots will now coincide with the start of the newly applied agriculture payments regime at the beginning of 2021 . <p> I have been a key member of the APPG on Agroecology , working with organisations such as the Soil Association , Sustain , the Landworkers Alliance and the Campaign to Protect Rural England to look at how to achieve a better , more sustainable approach to agriculture , as well as becoming more self-reliant in food production . <p> Farmers ' role as custodians of the countryside is a huge responsibility .
