<h> Post-pandemic Citizens ' Assembly on climate emergency moves forward <h> 5th August 2020 <p> Plans for a North of Tyne Citizens ' Assembly on Climate Change have been refreshed following the Coronavirus pandemic . <p> In February the North of Tyne Cabinet approved proposals that would see the region become the first Mayoral Combined Authority in the UK to consult the public on its response to the climate emergency in this way . <p> When the country was moved to lockdown as a result of Coronavirus the plans were put on hold as large gatherings were no longer deemed safe . <p> As the UK is now gradually is moving out of lockdown the North of Tyne Combined Authority is procuring an external organisation to design and facilitate the Citizens ' Assembly when it is safe to do so . <p> Organisations bidding for the contract are expected to outline different options for delivering the Citizens ' Assembly , including a flexible mixture of online and traditional meetings .
