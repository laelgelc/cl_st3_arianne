This is true not just of Australia but around the world . <p> Of course , some may already be starting their university journey now , having already received their grades , while others are still weighing up their options . <p> And the wheels of education never slow , which means another group are following fast on their heels facing their final years of secondary education against a backdrop of such uncertainty . <h> The coronavirus generation <p> Whatever the situation , I think we would do well to look back and reflect on just how much this generation has gone through . <p> This year , of course , coronavirus has wreaked havoc on school -- not least @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ some of their most significant year of learning and locking them out of classrooms when they needed friends , teachers and stability most . <p> But , in truth , crisis has long been " the new normal " for the Class of 2020 . <h> Crisis after crisis <p> This is a generation who were born in the wake of 9/11 and started their educational journey in the shadow of the global financial crisis . <p> Theirs has been the age of terrorism , climate change , racial violence and frightening political vitriol . <p> Now they finish their schooling in the midst of a pandemic that looks set to claim 1 million lives before the year is over . <p> More than our praise , they command our respect .
