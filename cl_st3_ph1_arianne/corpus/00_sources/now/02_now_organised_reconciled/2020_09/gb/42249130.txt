<h> More than 600 arrested during five days of climate protests in London <h> More than 600 arrested during five days of climate protests in London <p> More than 600 people have been arrested during five days of climate change protests in central London , police have said . <p> Environmental campaign group Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) reignited its efforts to highlight the dangers of climate change this month after they were largely placed on hold by the coronavirus pandemic . <p> In a series of daily actions , protesters have marched on Parliament Square , blocked roads , staged sit-ins and glued themselves to the ground . <p> It led the Metropolitan Police to impose conditions on where demonstrations could take place , while protesters were also warned they risked a large fine if they failed to comply with coronavirus rules banning gatherings of more than 30 people . <p> Scotland Yard also said on Saturday that 20 people had been reported for consideration of a ? 10,000 Fixed Penalty Notice ( FPN ) for holding regulation-breaching gatherings representing a range of different causes . <p> Commander Kyle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , said protests had caused " serious disruption to local communities " in the past week . <p> He added : " Throughout this period we have become increasingly concerned the organisers of these events have not always taken all reasonable measures to limit the risk of transmission of coronavirus , thereby posing a risk , not only to those involved , but the wider public and communities of London . <p> " We remain in the middle of a pandemic and we all need to play our part in keeping each other and our communities safe .
