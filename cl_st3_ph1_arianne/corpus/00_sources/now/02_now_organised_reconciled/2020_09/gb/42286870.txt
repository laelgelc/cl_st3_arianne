<h> NHS trust pledges to become carbon neutral by 2040 <h> NHS trust pledges to become carbon neutral by 2040 <p> The NHS Trust responsible for mental health and disability services in north Cumbria has pledged to become carbon neutral by 2040 . <p> The Cumbria , Northumberland , Tyne and Wear NHS Foundation Trust ( CNTW ) is developing a comprehensive plan to reduce its carbon emissions to net zero by 2040 . <p> Anna Foster , deputy director of commissioning and quality assurance and lead for sustainability at the trust , said : " The NHS overall is estimated to be responsible for around five percent of all UK carbon emissions . <p> " Five percent of all journeys on UK roads are estimated to be healthcare-related . <p> " The trust has already taken some steps to reduce waste and lower emissions , but we have big plans to come .
