<p> The New European ( TNE ) was launched days after the June 2016 EU referendum result in an attempt to rebalance the right-wing extremes of much of the UK national press . <p> Originally launched as a four-week pop-up publication , the award-winning weekly newspaper and online output offers pro-European in-depth analysis of the Brexit process , its implications and progress , as well as celebrating European life . <p> Since its launch the publication has gone from strength-to-strength , now reporting on the political response to the coronavirus outbreak , climate change and international politics . It also devotes significant coverage to the arts and culture . <p> TNE can be purchased in all good newsagents every Thursday , or delivered to your door every week through a subscription . Digital subscriptions are also available to purchase through the website and mobile app to read the publication on your phone , tablet or computer . <p> There is also a weekly podcast available every Friday morning , which can be downloaded through this website , Spotify , or Apple and Google podcasts.
