<h> Beheaded Toothill statue The Watchers restored after vandal attack <p> The Watchers , outside Toothill Community Centre , was attacked in an act of vandalism last November . <p> It represents a family looking over the community but the head of the father figure was decapitated . <p> Old Town stonemason Toby Robson offered his services and started working on the statue two weeks ago . <p> He said : " I offered to repair it because it 's a local sculpture and it 's at the heart of the community . <p> " It 's a big challenge because the sculpture is more than 40 years old and nobody knows what materials went into it originally . In terms of sand and the cement mix that was used , nobody remembers . <p> " It was difficult . There were n't many pictures of the head when it was brand new .
