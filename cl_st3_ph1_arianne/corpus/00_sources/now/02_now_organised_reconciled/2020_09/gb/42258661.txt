<h> Bringing back flora and wildlife at a housing site <h> Bringing back flora and wildlife at a housing site in south Cumbria <p> HOUSEBUILDER Oakmere has joined forces with leading art and environment charity , Art Gene , to encourage native wildlife and flora back to areas around Barrow through environmental initiatives that will create thriving living environments for the community . <p> An initial 12-month project has commenced between Oakmere and Barrow-based Art Gene , to produce a series of practical expert-led workshops which will engage the local community in ecological and sustainable food growing . <p> In supporting Art Gene 's wider quest with Barrow-in-Furness Borough Council for climate change action , Oakmere has committed to the environmental projects in Barrow , to share knowledge and ideas that will create environmentally friendly open spaces for everyone to enjoy . <p> Workshops will be based at ' Allotment Soup , the Isle of Walney Community Growing Space ' , Art Gene 's long-term project now in its fifth year , to develop an acre of scrubland on Walney Island into a community resource for food , futures and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , he is working with Art Gene on the future development of the Isle of Walney Community Growing Space . <p> Oakmere , which this month launched exclusive new homes development , Abbey Heights , in Barrow-In-Furness , has an adjacent 18-acre meadow dedicated to improving biological diversity , creating an environmental legacy in the community .
