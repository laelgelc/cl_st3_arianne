<p> Hundreds of elephants were found dead in Botswana in May and June . ( Reuters ) <p> Elephant carcasses were spotted in Botswana 's Okavango delta in May and June . The country is home to up to a third of Africa 's declining elephant population . <p> Cyril Taolo , deputy director of the Department of Wildlife and National Parks , told a news conference the number of elephant carcasses found since deaths were first reported around early May had risen to 330 , from 281 in July . <p> He said : " What we just know at this point is that it 's a toxin caused by cyanobacteria .
