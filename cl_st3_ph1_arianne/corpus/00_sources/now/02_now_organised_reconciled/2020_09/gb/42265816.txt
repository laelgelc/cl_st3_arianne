<h> Mercury Prize 2020 : Everything you need to know <p> As with all awards ceremonies this year , the Hyundai Mercury Prize is going virtual . Normally , there would be an event at London 's Hammersmith Apollo , where the 12 shortlisted acts would all make an appearance with performances from many of them , but this year , of course , everything will take place on TV . <p> Even without the usual proceedings , the Prize has become a major cultural talking point this summer , actually more so than in the last few years .
