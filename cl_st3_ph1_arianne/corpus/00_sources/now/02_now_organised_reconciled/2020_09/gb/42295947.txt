Scots are worried about the impact that a second wave of Covid-19 would have on food supplies , a new YouGov survey has shown . <p> Fears for the food system 's resilience in the face of disruption from climate change and trade interruptions after the Brexit transition period ends are also playing on public consciousness , highlighting a growing desire to future-proof the UK 's food system . <p>
