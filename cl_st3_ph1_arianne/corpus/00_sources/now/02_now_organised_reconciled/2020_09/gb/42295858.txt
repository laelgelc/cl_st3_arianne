<p> BNP Paribas Capital Partners ( ' BNPP CP ' ) announces the launch and first close of BNP Paribas Singularity Fund I ( ' Singularity ' ) , with commitments totalling EUR 80 million . Singularity is a new private equity fund of funds that aims to deliver strong financial performance , together with measurable positive impact , framed around the UN Sustainable Development Goals ( ' SDGs ' ) . <p> Singularity will focus on sustainable growth trends including demographic and technological developments and challenges relating to climate change , which offer opportunities to create long term value . BNPP CP is BNP Paribas Asset Management 's ( ' BNPP AM ' ) specialist in external alternative manager selection . It has launched the fund in line with BNPP AM 's Global Sustainability Strategy , overseen by Jane Ambachtsheer , BNPP AM 's Global Head of Sustainability . <p> Singularity will be managed by Anne-Sophie Kerfourn and Emmanuel Haumesser , and will invest in both traditional and impact private equity funds . It will predominantly target buyout and growth capital funds active on the European lower mid-market through primary commitments and co-investments . It may make secondary commitments depending on market opportunities . Initial limited partners include BNP Paribas and a major insurance company .
