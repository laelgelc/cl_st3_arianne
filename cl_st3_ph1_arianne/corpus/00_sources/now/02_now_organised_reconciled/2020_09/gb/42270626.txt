<h> Arts By The Sea 2020 : What to expect and where <h> Arts By The Sea 2020 : What 's on and where <p> ORGANISERS of the Arts By The Sea festival have released the physical and online programme for the 10th instalment of the annual arts and culture celebration . <p> With a mix of outdoor events that includes largescale fire and light installations and immersive sound experiences , as well as dance workshops and pre-recorded music performances , this year 's festival covers all corners arts and culture scene . <p> Here is a breakdown on what to expect from the week-long event and how to watch , listen and attend : <p> Physical programme <p> The live outdoor programme for 2020 welcomes back Arts by the Sea Associate Artists and long-time collaborators , Ithaca Studio , who present an outdoor adaptation of their large-scale light extravaganza , ' Sea of Light ' . <p> Guests can enjoy thousands of light spheres in a dazzling 360-degree light experience accompanied by a surround-sound music composition . <p> Organiser will also present outdoor arts experts , Walk the Plank @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Arts By The Sea festival . <p> ' The Fire Garden ' will see an everyday space transformed into a dazzling world of illuminating arts , brimming with flaming flower boxes , crackling trees and plumes of fire . <p> Elsewhere in central Bournemouth , festival goers can step into ' The Congregation ' ; an immersive sound experience curated by local musician and composer , Guy Dowsett . <p> Through sensor triggered design , hundreds of insects , birds and other creatures can be heard in a vast and entrancing tapestry of living sound , as guests journey back in time to when humans inhabited the forest . <p> Film fans will be intrigued by the arrival of TOM by award-winning choreographer and film maker , Wilkie Branson . <p> In a socially distanced set-up at Pavilion Dance South West , TOM tells the story of one man 's journey to rediscover who he really is . <p> It will be found on Sandbanks Beach and it offers a place for the public to remember those lost in the Covid-19 pandemic . <p> A display of bed sheets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ones passed and pay tribute to brave NHS staff members and volunteers . <p> Inspired by the themes of ' In Memoriam ' by Luke Jerram , local arts and wellbeing organisation , CoCreate are looking for audience interaction for their open-to-all , participatory event ' Speak To The Sea ' . <p> Set on the beach , guests will be prompted by a series of signboards to ' speak to the sea ' about their own personal experiences and feelings from the last few months . <p> Speak To The Sea will also feature artwork from members of the public who have been personally affected by Covid-19 via CoCreate 's community artwork project , Process . <p> Those wishing to take part should visit **28;9298;TOOLONG before the sign up deadline of 27th August . <p> Further participatory community events come from CoCreate with #PostcardsFromMemory . <p> From extravagant road trips to disastrous holidays ;
