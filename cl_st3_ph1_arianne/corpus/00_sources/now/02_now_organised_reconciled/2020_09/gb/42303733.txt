<h> Hundreds of anti-coal protesters break into German mine <h> Hundreds of anti-coal protesters break into German mine <p> Hundreds of anti-coal activists have entered a mine in western Germany to protest against the continued extraction and use of fossil fuels . <p> Environmentalists object to the German government 's decision to allow the mining and burning of coal in the country until 2038 , a deadline the activists say is too late to effectively tackle climate change . <p> Activists , dressed in coloured overalls and braving wind and rain , are also protesting over the planned destruction of several villages to make way for the expansion of the Garzweiler strip mine , west of Cologne . <p> The Garzweiler site in Grevenbroich , western Germany ( dpa/AP ) <p> An Associated Press reporter witnessed some protesters break through police lines early on Saturday before being detained by officers . <p> Utility company RWE said some protesters also entered coal storage facilities . <p> The Garzweiler mine and nearby power plants have been a focus of protests for several years . <p> Environmentalists say they are among the biggest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> In her weekly video message , German chancellor Angela Merkel called for sustainable development , to help confront the threat of global warming . <p> Mrs Merkel cited Germany 's increase in renewable energy production as an example of the measures her country has taken .
