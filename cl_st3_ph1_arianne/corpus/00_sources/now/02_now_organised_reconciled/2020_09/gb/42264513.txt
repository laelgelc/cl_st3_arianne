<h> Controversial homes scheme is finally given approval <h> Controversial homes plan on outskirts of Eastbourne approved <p> CONTROVERSIAL proposals to build 250 homes on the outskirts of Eastbourne have been approved by Wealden planners following a second hearing . <p> At a virtual meeting held on Thursday , Wealden District Council approved an outline application from Wates Developments for the Friday Street Farm site in Stone Cross . <p> The decision follows an earlier refusal of the same scheme in May , on the grounds it falls outside the council 's agreed development boundary . While some councillors maintained this as grounds for refusal , the wider committee 's feelings appeared to have shifted . <p> Proposing the scheme be approved , Johanna Howell ( Con , Frant and Wadhurst ) said : " Whatever we say we need the housing . We have to provide thousands of houses in Wealden and this is one of our strategic sites . If it does n't go here where does it go ? Nobody wants housing , none of us . We would all love to live in our splendid isolation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and we are duty bound to provide them . " Cllr Howell 's proposal was backed Neil Waller ( Con , Crowborough South West ) , who argued that he could not see a reason for refusal which would stand up at appeal . <p> A very different view was put forward by Gavin Blake-Coggins ( Lib Dem . Hailsham East ) who highlighted the number of public objections the application had attracted . <p> He said : " It is commendable that so many people have written in voicing their concerns over this . I 'm finding the whole thing exceedingly difficult . I have read again everything which I have received over the last 24 hours . As we are more than well aware , Friday Street is overloaded with traffic and we are proposing probably an additional 400 car movements a day . How many times a day is that going to take place with the school runs and so forth ? <p> " Are the roads in and out of this estate going to be sufficiently wide enough ? <p> " There is no evidence in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to move without cause for concern . " <p> " What about the green issues ? The climate change issues ? We are talking about extending and building beyond the agreed boundaries . " <p> He added : " It is beyond comprehension frankly that the validity of our decisions is being brought into question with this as we have already once said no . " <p> Meanwhile Cllr Waller 's comments came in for criticism from Bob Bowdler ( Con , Horam and Punnetts Town ) . He said : " I am extremely disappointed to be in the position I am today looking at this again . <p> " We had an extremely emotional debate back in May when a decision was made . If a planning inspector had found against us then I could have held my head up and be proud I had done what I thought was right . <p> " Cllr Waller speaks of speculating on what a planning inspector might think . Well we are quite often told at these meetings that we ca n't speculate on what may happen , yet here @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> Cllr Bowdler went on to raise concerns about the impact of the development on a nearby caravan park and other amenities .
