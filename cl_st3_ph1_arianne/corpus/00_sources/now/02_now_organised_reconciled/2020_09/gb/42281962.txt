And research has found that it is possible to interpret people 's emotions by analysing their eyes . In 2017 , researchers at Cornell University showed volunteers images of eyes expressing different emotions : sadness , disgust , anger , joy , surprise or fear . <p> The participants were able to consistently rate how well different words describing mental states matched the ' eye expression ' . The researchers concluded that the eyes provide essential interpersonal insight , and that different aspects of the eyes ( such as how open they are or how sloped the brow is ) give information about different mental states . <p> The neuroscience is also interesting here .
