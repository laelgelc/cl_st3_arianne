They 're also meant to " illustrate the importance of seed diversity in today 's time " by bringing attention to regional seed-saving resources , and unpacking the reasons why mass-marketed seed varieties are less likely to stand up to diseases and climate change , she said . <p> Ken Greene , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and the keynote presenter of the two-day celebration , will introduce Monday 's 5:30 p.m. screening of Open Sesame : The Story of Seeds , from the Canadian filmmaker M.
