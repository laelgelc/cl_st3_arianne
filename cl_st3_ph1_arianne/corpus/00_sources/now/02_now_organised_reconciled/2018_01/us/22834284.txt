<h> ' Land reforms must to achieve economies of scale in agriculture ' <p> " Substantive reforms in land policy are required to achieve economies of scale in agriculture , " Vice-President M Venkaiah Naidu said . <p> Addressing the 47th Founder 's Day of the Institute for Social and Economic Change ( ISEC ) in Bengaluru , Naidu said : " Absentee land lords in many States have put large tracts of land out of productive use . Promotion of contract farming , legislation on land leasing and encouragement of land sharing among both farmers and landowners keeping interest of farmers in mind may be some alternative policy options . " <p> On water use , Naidu stressed that there has to be a much better understanding . " The future growth in agriculture will be knowledge and technology driven facilitated by protection and management of ground water and surface water . 70 per cent of irrigation is provided by groundwater , as the current crop pattern is leading to groundwater over exploitation , it is crucial to cultivate low-water high-value crops using groundwater . I am glad that government has introduced two major responses to climate change -- National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture for Development and National Initiatives for Climate Resilient Agriculture ( NICRA ) for research . " <p> These key initiatives are aimed at transforming agriculture into a climate resilient production system through adaptation and mitigation measures .
