<h> More than a third of Americans give Trump 's first year a resounding " F " : Poll <h> The country is deeply unhappy with its president <p> Americans are n't happy with President Donald Trump . While this is n't news per se , his low approval ratings may leave you wondering just why exactly most citizens are disappointed . <p> A new poll offers something approaching an answer . According to Politico/Morning Consult 's latest findings , the country is most dissatisfied with the president 's performance in several domestic and international categories . <p> Of the 1,988 people surveyed in early January , Trump earned an F grade from the majority of respondents . The issues covered include fixing the national debt , immigration and fighting terrorism , among others . When asked for an overall grade on Trump 's first year in office , the lion 's share of respondents ( 35% ) gave Trump a failing grade . <p> Unsurprisingly , the president performed poorest on climate change . A whopping 40 percent said Trump has utterly failed to combat global warming , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Paris Agreement and the enormous tax cuts he gifted to the oil and gas industry as part of the GOP 's tax reform bill . <p> Credit : Politico/Morning Consult <p> Trump fared almost as badly on foreign relations and health care . In both categories , the largest group of participants ( 38 percent ) gave the president an F. <p> Politico 's readers tend to lean liberal , though that 's not the case with this poll . Participants are spread evenly between race , gender , geographical location , political leaning , party affiliation , and age . Most earn under $50K and have some college experience , but not a bachelor 's degree . <p> Interestingly , the poll shows that exactly the same percentage of people think Trump will get better ( 37 percent ) as those who think he 'll get worse ( also 37 percent ) .
