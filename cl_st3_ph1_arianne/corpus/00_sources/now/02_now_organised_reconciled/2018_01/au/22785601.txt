<p> For many Australians summer is synonymous with cricket and tennis . But as Australian summers become more prone to extreme heat conditions , sustainable and climate-adaptable stadium design has become a leading consideration for both sporting codes and governments . <p> The final Ashes Test played at the Sydney Cricket Ground recently showed that the cricketing community must adapt to heatwaves made worse by climate change . <p> And in recent years the Australian Open has produced many stories of both tennis players and spectators suffering in extreme heat .
