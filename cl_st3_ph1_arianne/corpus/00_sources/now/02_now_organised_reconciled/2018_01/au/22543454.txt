<p> Stories of people forced from their homes both locally and globally will be told through photomedia at two exhibitions opening at UNSW Galleries on Saturday as part of the 2018 Sydney Festival . <p> From refugees in Tunisian transit camps to the homeless in downtown Brussels , In Your Dreams captures the individual stories of some of the millions of people forced to leave their homes due to war , natural disaster and poverty . <p> Fourteen artists from Australia , Bangladesh , China , France , Greece , Jordan , Mexico , Nigeria , Papua New Guinea , South Africa and the US will exhibit works that highlight the gulf between rich and poor and the impact of inequality on communities around the world . <p> " In Australia we can be cocooned from the major problems that face of the world , " said Felicity Fenner , curator of the exhibition and director of UNSW Galleries .
