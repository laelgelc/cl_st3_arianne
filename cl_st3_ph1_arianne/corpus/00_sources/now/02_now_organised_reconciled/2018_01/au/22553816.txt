In other words , we compared the number of deaths in September and October last year with data from the same period of time in 2010 to 2016 . This is how we concluded that there were 1,085 extra deaths , in excess of historical ranges . <p> So why are more than 1,000 deaths missing from the official count ? My colleagues and I suspect it may come down to how deaths are recorded by government officials . <p> In Puerto Rico , deaths are recorded using international classifications . This system does n't capture all of the circumstances surrounding a death that happens following a natural disaster . The death may have been accelerated by some conditions -- like difficulty communicating during the emergency . <p> Deaths associated with a particular natural disaster can be classified as direct or indirect deaths .
