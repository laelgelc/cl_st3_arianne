<p> The 1089.7 pound ( 496.3 kilogram ) blue marlin landed on January 1 out of Exmouth , WA , by angler Clay Hilbert with game skipper Eddy Lawler and his crew from Peak Sportfishing Adventures will go down in the record books as the first " grander " blue caught in Australian waters . <p> It has also put game fishing in the spotlight , with many critics using social media to put forward the view that " trophy " fishing is unsustainable and ethically dubious . <p> Social media and mainstream press attention about the capture of this massive blue marlin has also resulted in the local and international game fishing community getting pretty hyped up . A catch of this significance has n't been recorded for more than 50 years . <p> The last time there was this much hoo-haa about a big fish getting caught was back in 1966 when Cairns-based angler George Bransford weighed a 1064lb black marlin , the first grander ever caught in Australia . <p> As social media was non-existent back then , the excitement about Bransford 's big black marlin was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ recent catch . <p> Still , that particular fish is notable in that it kick-started an entire industry based on the now world-renowned -- and very lucrative -- Cairns heavy tackle game fishery . <p> There 's every opportunity for Hilbert 's big blue marlin to do the same for Exmouth as Bransford 's big black marlin did for Cairns . <p> Already well regarded as a diverse and appealing sport and game fishery , this remote WA town now has the potential to lure in well-heeled international game fishermen keen on trying their luck -- and spending their bucks -- for the chance at a genuine thousand-pounder . <p> There are only a few locations around the world offering legitimate shots at grander marlin . <p> Thanks to Hilbert 's big blue , Exmouth now joins an elite coterie of game fishing hotspots including Cairns , Hawaii , Brazil , Maderia and Tahiti . <h> You have to kill it to weigh it <p> While most marlin these days are caught and released , the economic potential of the Exmouth blue as a genuine " grander " was doubtless @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ port to be weighed . <p> This process had to happen in order for the fish to be considered for records and for verification as an " official " grander . <p> Hilbert and Lawler could have elected to release it .
