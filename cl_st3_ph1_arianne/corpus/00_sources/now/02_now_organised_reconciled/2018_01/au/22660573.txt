<h> Pope Francis ' ' state-of-the-world ' speech <p> In his annual ' state-of-the-world ' address for the start of the new year to ambassadors and other members of the Diplomatic Corps , Pope Francis said that stability of the family is integral to a prosperous future . <p> Pope Francis scrutinised the growing preference for ' fleeting relationships ' and urged us to seek the ' stability of a definitive life project . ' <p> " What is needed is a rock on which to build solid foundations . And this rock is precisely that faithful and indissoluble communion of love . " <p> Therefore , it is urgent , the Pope continued , that government policies support the family on which " the future of the development of states depend , " and without which future challenges can not be met . <p> Pope Francis also took a strong stand on anthropogenic climate change , telling the gathering of diplomats that global warming is a result of human action . <p> " We must not downplay the importance of our own responsibility in interaction with nature , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ See . <p> " Climate change and the global rise in temperatures and their devastating effects are also a consequence of human activity , " he said . <p> It 's the first time Pope Francis has made a definitive stance on man-made climate change . <p> In 2015 , the Pope declared : " The Church has no reason to offer a definitive opinion ; she knows that honest debate must be encouraged among experts , while respecting divergent views .
