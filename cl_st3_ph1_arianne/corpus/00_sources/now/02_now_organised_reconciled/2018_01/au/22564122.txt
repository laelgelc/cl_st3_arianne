<h> Funding for carers of creatures great and small <p> The Victorian Government is helping local wildlife rehabilitators continue to care for sick , injured and orphaned wildlife . <p> Energy , Environment and Climate Change Minister Lily D'Ambrosio recently announced the launch of the 2017/18 Wildlife Rehabilitator Grants -- recognising the importance and value in the work that rehabilitators undertake , and the costs associated with caring for Victoria 's diverse wildlife . <p> Now in its ninth year , the Government 's Wildlife Rehabilitator Grants Program provides funding to support wildlife rehabilitators , including shelter operators and foster carers , to purchase a range of equipment and infrastructure to assist with the operation of their facilities and to attend appropriate training . <p> The program aims to help wildlife rehabilitators by increasing their knowledge/skills in the care of wildlife ; improving their facilities to treat , house and rehabilitate wildlife for release ; improving the standard of wildlife care provided for specialist species ( such as raptors ) or threatened species ( such as brush tailed phascogales ) . <p> Grants are open to any wildlife rehabilitators in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ up to $2000 available to successful applicants . <p> Ms D'Ambrosio said the program is about giving a helping hand to many of Victoria 's dedicated volunteers who house , feed and care for wildlife when they need it most . <p> ' ' Our wildlife carers should be commended for their time and commitment to ensuring our native species continue to flourish and I thank them for their dedication , ' ' she said . <p> Applications for the 2017/18 round of the Wildlife Rehabilitator Grants Program are now open .
