<h> Rapid rate of bleaching threatens very existence of coral reefs <p> January 22nd , 2018 <p> The current rate of coral bleaching is happening faster than in any other era in history , a new study published this month has found . <p> According to the study in the journal Science , the frequency of the bleaching is undermining the capacity of coral reefs to recover and is threatening their very existence . Coral reefs are the most diverse marine ecosystems on Earth and provide ecosystem services for millions of people . <p> Coral bleaching occurs where corals expel crucial algae living symbiotically within their tissues as a stress response to increasing ocean temperatures . When bleaching is severe and prolonged , many of the corals die and it can take several decades for new coral to grow back . <p> The study from the ARC Centre of Excellence for Coral Reef Studies ( CoralCoE ) measured the bleaching rate at 100 globally distributed Coral Reefs from 1980 to 2016 . He added that mass bleaching of corals , basically unheard of before the 1980s , have now become the norm as temperatures continue to rise across the world . <p> Coral Bleaching in the Anthropocene Era <p> The study claims that our reefs are now entering a new era called the Anthropocene . In this era , coral bleaching is much more frequent in all El Nino-Southern Oscillation phases , expanding the chances of annual bleaching in the following decades . <p> Co-author of the research , Dr C. Mark Eakin of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration said that we are now seeing the emergence of bleaching during every hot summer . <p> The Great Barrier Reef , for example , has bleached four times since 1998 , including back to back events during 2016 and 2017 , leading to significant environmental damage .
