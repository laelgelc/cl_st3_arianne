<h> India and Southeast Asian nations commit to climate smart agriculture <p> Mr. Radha Mohan Singh was bringing to a close the fourth ministerial meeting between India and the ASEAN union of Southeast Asian countries . The meeting , which focussed on agriculture and forestry , concluded with a commitment on both sides to prioritise projects that take early action on climate change . <p> A joint statement after the summit stated : " we underscore the importance of addressing climate change as it negatively impacts food security and look forward to cooperation ... to promote enhanced resilience of natural systems , and improve the adaptive capacities of people to cope with environmental hazards " . <p> The partnership has already been successful with closer cooperation on vital areas , such as food security , fisheries , and forestry helping to increase prosperity across the region .
