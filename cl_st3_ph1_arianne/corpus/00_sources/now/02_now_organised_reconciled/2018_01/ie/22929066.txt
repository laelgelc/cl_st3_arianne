<h> Davos ' an opportunity ' to slam Mercosur talks <p> Taoiseach Leo Varadkar can use discussions with EU Trade Commissioner Cecilia Malmstrom at the World Economic Forum in Davos , Switzerland , as an opportunity to restate Ireland 's opposition to the Mercosur trade deal , according to Irish Farmers ' Association ( IFA ) president Joe Healy . <p> The EU Trade Commissioner is guilty of " undermining European policy and values " in her " sell-out " approach on beef in Mercosur , Healy said . <p> " There is a clear contradiction at EU level when one arm of the commission is prepared to do this deal , while European farmers are asked to play their part against climate change . " <p> With a ministerial meeting on Mercosur scheduled for Brussels next Tuesday , Healy said the Taoiseach has to launch an all-out offensive against this deal . <p> The growth in Brazilian beef exports is driven by the destruction of the Amazon rainforests and the carbon footprint of Brazilian beef is four times that of EU production . <p> " There are no controls @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in rural areas and the beef sector , " Healy said . <p> Advertisement <p> The president said EU consumers want food that is produced sustainably , is healthy , protects the environment , helps fight climate change , respects the animals , is fair to farmers and produced locally . <p> He said Brazilian beef is not sustainable in terms of its failure to meet EU standards on the key issues of traceability , food safety , animal welfare and the environment . <p> " The ' Weak Flesh ' fraud highlighted again the food safety issues with Brazilian meat , where there are no controls on the use of hormones , drugs and beta-agonists -- all banned in the EU . " <h> Davos <p> The World Economic Forum will take place in the Swiss ski resort of Davos this morning .
