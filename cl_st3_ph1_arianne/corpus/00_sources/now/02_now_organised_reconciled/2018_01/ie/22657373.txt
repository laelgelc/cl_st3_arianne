<h> Scaling tech companies take shape at The Digital Hub <p> The Digital Hub , Ireland 's largest and most established cluster of technology , digital media and internet companies , is currently witnessing a growing demand from within its base of enterprise companies to support their need to scale while remaining in The Digital Hub .
