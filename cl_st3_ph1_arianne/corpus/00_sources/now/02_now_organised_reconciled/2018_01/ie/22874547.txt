<p> The mid-term review of the Forestry Programme to be published in early February will contain a range of proposals which should help to address concerns about the Programme while helping to ensure that planting targets are reached , according to Mairead McGuinness , MEP and first Vice-President of the European Parliament . <p> Speaking at the Irish Natura and Hill Farmers Association ( INHFA ) meeeting in Carrick-on-Shannon last Friday , the MEP said measures aimed at promoting greater species and habitat diversity , with higher grant and premium rates for broad leaves and diverse conifers are among the changes proposed . <p> " Higher levels of broadleaf planting per site is also proposed and continuous forestry cover is to be supported to create more diverse habits for wildlife , " she said . <p> " The national target is to increase the level of forestry cover by almost 44,000 hectares , " she said . " But fears around over-intensity in certain areas must be faced and openly addressed .
