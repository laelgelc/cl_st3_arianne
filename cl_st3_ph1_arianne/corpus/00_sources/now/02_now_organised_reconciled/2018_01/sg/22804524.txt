That party , typically a bank , certifies you are not trying to send the same money to two different parties simultaneously , or otherwise trying to deceive the system . Blockchain allows users to do business directly with each other without involving a central authority like a bank . <p> In this instance , blockchain acts like a digital ledger that simultaneously exists on every computer belonging to the same network , and the ledger is updated with every transaction . Because the ledger exists everywhere all at once , it is incredibly difficult to hack . That 's where climate change comes in . <p> A major obstacle to addressing climate change is that our energy system is highly centralised , while the technologies we will need to cut carbon pollution , by and large , are not . Currently , most people buy electricity from a coal- or natural gas-fired power plant . As the price of solar panels continues to drop , more people will generate their own electricity from rooftop solar . <p> Rooftop solar owners want a way to sell their surplus power .
