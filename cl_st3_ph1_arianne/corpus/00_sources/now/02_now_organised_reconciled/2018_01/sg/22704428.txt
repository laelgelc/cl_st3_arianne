Despite these efforts , athletes at both games complained of poor snow that they said led to unfair conditions . <p> Skiers , for example , may consider a competition unfair when shifting conditions make a course faster or slower depending on when the skier races . At Sochi , snowboarders complained that the half-pipe was dangerous because of bumps and sugary snow that can slow down riders when they should be gaining speed for manoeuvres that involve launching as much as 6m above the half-pipe 's 7m top edge .
