More judicial battles - <p> The Norway case illustrates how the battle against global warming is increasingly being fought in the courts . <p> The London-based Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment has listed more than 260 climate-related cases in 25 jurisdictions , most of them during the past decade . <p> This figure excludes the United States where the number of such cases exceeds 700 . <p> Judicial battles are sometimes successful . <p> The Netherlands was ordered in 2015 to reduce its greenhouse gas emissions by 25 percent by 2020 , a decision that has been appealed . <p> In November , a German court also agreed to consider a lawsuit by a Peruvian peasant who wanted to force the energy giant RWE to compensate him over the effects of climate change in the Andes .
