<h> Electric shuttle trial rides between NTU and JTC 's CleanTech One to start from July <p> From July this year , Nanyang Technological University ( NTU ) students and teachers can travel between NTU 's Halls of Residence at North Hill and Jurong Town Corporation ( JTC ) ' s CleanTech One on Singapore 's first flash-charging electric shuttle as part of a trial programme . <p> Launched on Monday ( January 22 ) by NTU and electric vehicle car-sharing company BlueSG , which is a subsidiary of Blue Solutions , the NTU-Blue Solutions Flash Shuttle can carry a maximum of 22 people and only requires 20 seconds to fully charge . <p> The 6-metre long electric shuttle -- a Bluetram vehicle by parent company Bollor ? Group -- is powered by a set of supercapacitors and a lithium metal polymer battery , unlike other electric vehicles that function solely on batteries . <p> Supercapacitors can be charged quickly and can last for millions of charge and discharge cycles .
