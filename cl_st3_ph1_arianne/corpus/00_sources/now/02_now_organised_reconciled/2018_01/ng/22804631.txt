<h> ' Empowering Indigenous Women 'll Help Fight against Hunger , Poverty ' <p> Empowering indigenous women who have higher malnutrition rates than any other social group and typically earn far less than men is key to successfully fighting hunger and extreme poverty , the United Nations agriculture chief has said . <p> Speaking at the recent Forum on Indigenous Women in Mexico City , the Director-General of the Food and Agriculture Organisation , Jose Graziano da Silva , said " Indigenous women face a triple discrimination -- poverty , gender and ethnicity , both within and outside their communities -- making them highly vulnerable . " <p> They confront far higher rates of poverty , chronic malnutrition and illiteracy while having the least access to health care and political life , he said to the participants from a dozen countries , according to a UN release . About five per cent of the global population and 15 per cent of the world 's poorest , or some 370 million people , self-identify as indigenous . The situation is even worse for women in the more than 5,000 indigenous groups @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " Their social and economic empowerment is not only an excellent way to support them , but a necessary condition to eradicate hunger and malnutrition in their communities , " underscored Mr. da Silva . He noted that the UN 's decision to begin a Family Farming Decade in 2019 offers a platform to focus on rural livelihoods -- where most indigenous peoples work . <p> The FAO-organised three-day forum aims to develop public policy recommendations to empower indigenous women , strengthen their decision-making and recognise their rights . Although indigenous women are key actors in protecting biodiversity , adapting to climate change and varying nutritious diets , they are often forgotten in development plans . <p> " They have fundamental roles in the spiritual , social and family arenas and are seed guardians -- critical carriers of specialised knowledge , " underscored Mr. da Silva . " With political will , we can put an end to discrimination against indigenous women , " he affirmed .
