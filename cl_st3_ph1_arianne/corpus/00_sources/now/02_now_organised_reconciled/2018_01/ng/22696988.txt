<h> U.S " could conceivably " reenter Paris Climate Agreement -- Trump <p> U.S. President Donald Trump announces his decision regarding the United States ' participation in the Paris climate agreement in the Rose Garden at the White House June 1 , 2017 in Washington , DC .
