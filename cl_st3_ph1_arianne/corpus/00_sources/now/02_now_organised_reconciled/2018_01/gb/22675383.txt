<h> High or low : which of IAEA 's future nuclear capacity scenarios is most realistic ? <p> Last year , the International Atomic Energy Agency ( IAEA ) presented two possible scenarios for global nuclear power capacity up to 2050 . Heidi Vella explores the scenarios side by side to see which is most feasible . <p> Presently , there are 447 operational nuclear power reactors in 30 countries , and a further 60 are under construction in 15 countries .
