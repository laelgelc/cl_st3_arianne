<h> UK to end 140 years of coal power by 2025 <p> Britain will end its use of coal in power plants by 2025 after more than 140 years burning the fossil fuel . <p> The Government confirmed that its 2015 pledge to phase out coal-fired power within a decade would move ahead under a new rule that limits the ' carbon-intensity ' of power plants . <p> The limit will allow thermal power plants that use lower-carbon gas to act as back-up generation , but coal plants will be forced to close unless they are fitted with carbon capture technology . <p> Coal-fired power has dwindled in recent years due to the rising tax on carbon emissions to curb greenhouse gases and the boom in renewable energy . <p> In 2017 coal-fired power made up just 2pc of the UK 's electricity mix , down from 9pc in 2016 and 22pc in 2015 .
