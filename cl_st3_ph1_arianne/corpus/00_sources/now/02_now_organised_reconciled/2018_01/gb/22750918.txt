<p> Arup has hired former AECOM and Haliburton consultant Ben Smith as Director of Energy , Cities and Climate Change . Smith will be responsible for expanding Arup 's efforts to help cities take practical steps to address climate change and increase their resilience . <p> Ben Smith has been appointed as Director of Energy , Cities and Climate Change to further expand Arup 's efforts to help cities take practical steps to address climate change and increase their resilience . <p> Following his graduation from the University of the West of England , with a BSc Hons in Environmental Science , Smith began his consulting career with Halliburton in 2001 , working as a consultant with the oil and energy giant for three years before joining civil engineering group AECOM in 2004 . Over the course of Smith 's near-14 year stint at the firm , he fulfilled roles in three divisions and two countries . <p> On his way to becoming Director of Sustainable Development , Buildings and Places for the professional services provider 's UK&amp;I wing , Smith fulfilled a succession of energy-related roles , including Associate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ VIC in the Melbourne office of AECOM 's Australian outlet , as of May 2012 . Returning to the UK in 2014 , Smith took up his final role of Sustainable Development Director at the firm before seeking a new challenge at the end of 2017 . <p> Arup is a consulting firm found at the heart of many of the world 's most prominent projects in the built environment and across industry . With over 80 offices in 35 countries Arup has more than 13,000 planners , designers , engineers and consultants , and Smith will now play a major role supporting the firms work for partners . This included projects such as the C40 Cities Climate Leadership Group Programme and 100 Resilient Cities , pioneered by The Rockefeller Foundation working with Arup Director Paula Kirk . <p> The C40 is focused on helping cities accelerate practical action on climate change and with support from Arup has grown to represent over 650 million people and one quarter of the global economy . He specialises in sustainable development , energy strategy , climate change and urban resilience . <p> Remarking on his new role , Smith said , " Joining Arup provides me with a great opportunity to continue working closely with cities on the practical steps towards addressing climate change and improving urban resilience , helping them cope with the challenges they are facing such as population growth , natural hazards , pollution and resource depletion .
