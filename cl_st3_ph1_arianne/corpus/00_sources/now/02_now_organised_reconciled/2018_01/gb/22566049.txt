Just last month , the Prime Minister and I attended the One Planet Summit in Paris , to mark two years since the Paris Agreement . We now have the biggest installed offshore wind capacity in the world , and the cost of new offshore wind is tumbling thanks to government support . <p> On 21 April , the UK had its first day without coal power generation since 1882 . We committed to phasing out power generation from unabated coal by 2025 . <p> The shift to clean energy presents a multi-billion pound investment opportunity for businesses .
