Nevertheless , after the passing of more than a couple of months of this decision , the ministry is still reluctant to send amended bills to the law ministry . <p> There may be two possible reasons for this delay . On the one hand , the present federal government led by the PML-N seems reluctant to dive into matters of religious significance for Christian citizens .
