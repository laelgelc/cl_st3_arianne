It is , therefore , important to take measures to protect the environment from further damage . In this regard , we must identify the factors that are responsible for environmental pollution . One of the primary causes of air pollution in our country is the smoke that is emitted from vehicles . <p> Punjab Environment Protection Minister Begum Zakia Shah Nawaz Khan believes that the environmental protection department and the National Highways and Motorway Police ( NHCP ) should work together to increase awareness among drivers regarding air pollution and how their vehicles contribute towards it . All drivers and vehicle owners should be informed about the importance of tuning vehicles . It is heartening to note that the two departments will now work jointly to deal with the problem .
