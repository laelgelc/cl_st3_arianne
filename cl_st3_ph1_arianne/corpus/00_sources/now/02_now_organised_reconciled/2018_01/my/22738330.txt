<p> Kota Kinabalu : Democratic Action Party ( DAP ) Sabah Chairman cum Sandakan MP Stephen Wong Tien Fatt likened the proposed construction of a lignite-based power plant in Tawau to going back to the old days and affecting people 's health . <p> Wong said while the world is reducing coal usage for power generation , advocating clean energy , reducing carbon emissions and paying attention to climate change , the Government has not changed , or as before , opted to use lignite for power generation . <p> " Although contractors and the government keep saying that the coal is clean , the greenhouse gases and suspended particles produced by lignite eventually will generate more compare to natural gas , " he said . <p> " Everyone is aware that coal power generation will endanger the health of the citizens and destroy the earth 's ecosystem .
