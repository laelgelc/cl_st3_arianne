Severe coral bleaching events used to happen once every 25 to 30 years . Now , they occur every six years on average , said the report in the journal Science . " Before the 1980s , mass bleaching of corals was unheard of , " said lead author Terry Hughes , director of the Australian Research Council ( ARC ) Centre of Excellence for Coral Reef Studies .
