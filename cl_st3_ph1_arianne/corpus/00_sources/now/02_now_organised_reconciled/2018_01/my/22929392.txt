<p> War on washers may affect S'pore Thursday , January 25th , 2018 at , Business World <p> By BLOOMBERG <p> SINGAPORE * US President Donald Trump 's attack on washing machine imports may end up putting the Singapore dollar through the spin-cycle . <p> Economic growth in the Asian city state has closely followed year-on-year changes in global trade volumes for almost twenty years , as the nation handles the second highest amount of containers in the world . Any escalation of US protectionism , with Trump seen advocating his " America First " policies at Davos , could have an adverse reaction on investor expectations for Singapore 's growth , monetary policy and the local dollar . <p> " Singapore would be more adversely affected than other economies should the US step up protectionist policies , " said Hirofumi Suzuki , an economist at Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corp . " A drop in world trade volumes could damp speculation of monetary policy tightening and weigh on the Singapore dollar . " <p> Trump comes to the World Economic Forum with a rap sheet in the eyes of proponents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , he 's withdrawn the US from the Trans-Pacific Partnership free trade deal and the Paris Agreement on climate change . He 's threatened to renege on the Iran nuclear deal , a free-trade agreement with South Korea , and the North American Free Trade Agreement . <p> The Singapore dollar is particularly exposed to investor expectations of future growth trends as the country 's central bank uses the currency as a monetary policy tool instead of interest rates . The local currency has risen about 1.6% against the US dollar so far this year . <p> The Monetary Authority of Singapore is expected to shift to a 0.5% appreciation in the nominal effective exchange rate in April from the current zero appreciation , according to a research note from United Overseas Bank Ltd on Tuesday .
