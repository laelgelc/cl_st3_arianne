<p> RABAT -- A meeting was held at Morocco 's interior Ministry on royal instruction at the weekend after King Mohammed VI directed the authorities to find ways to help the people cope with a cold snap which has been experienced in the kingdom recently . <p> Chaired by Interior Minister Abdelouafi Laftit , the meeting , which was attended by representatives of various government sectors and the security services concerned , provided an opportunity to the examine the measures already taken to deal with the impact of this cold snap and alleviate its effects on the local population . <p> Laftit called for the mobilization of all sectors of government and other stakeholders to take the necessary measures in this context . The Interior Ministry , throughs central and provincial services and all the services involved , including the Royal Gendarmerie , the auxiliary forces , emergency services and the various components of local authorities are willing to assume their responsibilities in relieving the burden on the population , in line with royal instructions , he said . <p> Laftit underlined that the organization of food aid distribution operations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ready . <p> The ministry has implemented the provisions of its national plan , which this year targeted 205 douars from 169 municipalities in 22 provinces , covering a total population of 514,000 people , said the minister , who added that during the current cold season , the ministry had activated a command and vigilance centre at the level of his department in order to monitor the situation , co-ordinate intervention operations and invite the governors of the regions and provinces concerned to mobilize and take the necessary proactive and preventive measures . <p> Laftit also lauded the efforts of the various sectors involved in a proactive approach to manage this situation in a reasonable and comprehensive way and to combat the impacts of climate change , especially during heavy snowfalls , which isolate these regions ' inhabitants .
