AFP Photo <p> NEW YORK : Scientists on Thursday moved ahead by half a minute the symbolic Doomsday Clock , saying the world was at its closest to annihilation since the height of the Cold War due to world leaders ' poor response to threats of nuclear war . <p> It was the second occasion the timepiece , created by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists as an indicator of the world 's susceptibility to cataclysm , was moved forward since the 2016 election of US president Donald Trump . <p> At two minutes to midnight , the clock is at its closest to catastrophe since 1953 , due to dangers of a nuclear holocaust from North @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ China Sea tensions , and other factors , the Chicago-based group said in a statement . <p> " Hyperbolic rhetoric and provocative actions on both sides have increased the possibility of nuclear war by accident or miscalculation , " the group said of North Korea 's nuclear programme and the Trump administration 's response to it . <p> Unchecked dangers linked to climate change were another factor scientists cited for moving the clock forward . <p> An overarching concern was what scientists described as the demise of diplomacy under the Trump administration . <p> " International diplomacy has been reduced to name-calling , giving it a surrealistic sense of unreality that makes the world security situation ever more threatening , " they said . <p> To rewind the clock , scientists recommended Trump refrain from provocative rhetoric regarding North Korea , the two countries open multiple communication channels and the world community seek a cessation of North Korea 's nuclear weapon and ballistic missile tests . <p> The bulletin was founded by scientists who helped develop the United States ' first atomic weapons .
