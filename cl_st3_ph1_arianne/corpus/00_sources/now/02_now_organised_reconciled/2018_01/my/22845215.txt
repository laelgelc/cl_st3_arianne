<h> Penang state govt announce goodies for the people <p> GEORGE TOWN : The Penang state government has announced a number of goodies for the people . While addressing a group of senior citizens at Komtar , Chief Minister Lim Guan Eng , took the opportunity to reveal that Penang will now increase its annual contributions to senior citizens by RM30 each . <p> Each recipient - registered with the state , will now get RM130 instead of RM100 which was accorded for the last five years . <p> Lim said the handout will be given to all entitled recipients , aged 60 and above . <p> Last year , Penang gave RM100 each to 167,489 senior citizens . <p> The state will spend around RM22 million on senior citizens this year . <p> Besides the elderly , Penang also offers incentive payouts to the disabled , the destitute , students , single mothers and other needy groups . <p> For this year , the state will also issue a smart card for residents living here under the slogan of " I-Love-Penang .
