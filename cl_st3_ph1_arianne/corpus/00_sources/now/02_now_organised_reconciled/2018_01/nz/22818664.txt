<h> 2017 one of hottest years on record <p> 2017 has broken into the record books as one of the hottest years ever recorded . <p> The UN says last year was the second- or third-warmest documented , while NASA says global surface temperatures were the second warmest since 1880 . The only hotter year was 2016 , helped by El Nino weather patterns . <p> Seventeen of the warmest 18 years since records began in the 19th century have now happened since 2000 , confirming that ever more greenhouse gases are driving up temperatures , the UN 's World Meteorological Organisation ( WMO ) said . <p> Among extreme weather events last year , the Caribbean and the United States suffered a battering from hurricanes , the Arctic ended 2017 with the least sea ice for mid-winter and tropical coral reefs suffered from high water temperatures . <p> " Arctic warmth has been especially pronounced and this will have profound and long-lasting repercussions on sea levels , and on weather patterns in other parts of the world , " WMO Secretary-General Petteri Talaas said in a statement . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ indistinguishable " from 2015 . <p> The 2015 Paris agreement , which seeks to shift the world economy from fossil fuels this century , aims to limit temperatures to " well below " a rise of 2degC above pre-industrial times - preferably below 1.5degC . <p> US President Donald Trump , who doubts climate change is caused by manmade emissions , plans to remove the US from the Paris Agreement .
