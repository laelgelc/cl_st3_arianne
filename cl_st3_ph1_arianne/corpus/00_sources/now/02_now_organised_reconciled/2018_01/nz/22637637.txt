The Baltimore Sun reports a United Nations Intergovernmental Oceanographic Commission work group led by Smithsonian Environmental Research Center marine ecologist Denise Breitburg found that since 1950 , the number of " suffocating " coastal ecosystems has grown tenfold , while the problem has grown four-fold in the generally more stable open ocean environments .
