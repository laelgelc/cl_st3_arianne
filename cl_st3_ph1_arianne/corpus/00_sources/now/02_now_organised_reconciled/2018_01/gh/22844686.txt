<p> Mr Salifu Saeed , the Northern Regional Minister has advised the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) to remain committed to work effectively to mainstream sustainable land and water resources into agriculture . <p> This , he said would lead to the increase in productivity of agriculture land , forests and water resources to reduce poverty and the vulnerability of citizenry to build " Ghana beyond aid " . <p> Mr Saeed gave the advice in a speech read on his behalf in Tamale at the fourth Local Steering Committee ( LSC ) stakeholders meeting to deliberate on the Ghana Sustainable Land and Water Management project ( GSLWMP ) . <p> He said the region was threatened by devastating effect of climate change , especially on agriculture and food security , which the project must envision as revolutionary environmental management in the zone . <p> Mr Saeed said indiscriminate tree felling and bushfire constituted to the unsustainable farming practices contributing to land degradation and a threat to desertification reducing the productivity of agriculture land , forest and water resources . <p> The GSLWMP is funded by the Global Environment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Ghana aimed at reducing land degradation and to enhance the maintenance of biodiversity of watersheds in the three Regions of the North . <p> The project implemented in over 163 communities intends to reverse land degradation through improved soil fertility , protecting watersheds to benefit communities in its implementation area . <p> Mr Abu Iddrisu , the Northern Regional Director of the Environmental Protection Agency said with the implementation of the project , a number of watersheds in the Northern , Upper East and Upper West Regions had been protected while improvement in the soil fertility of lands had also been enhanced . <p> He said several agricultural technology and information sharing had been enhanced to improve the knowledge bank of communities and ensure the practice of appropriate farming system to protect the environment . <p> Mr iddrisu indicated that , the project intends to facilitate community members to plant trees and to equip them with logistics to fight bushfire ,
