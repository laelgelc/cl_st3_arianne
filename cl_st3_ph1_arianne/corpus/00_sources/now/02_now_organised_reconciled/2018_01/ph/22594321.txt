<h> Inflation rate steady in December at 3.3% <p> The rate of increase in consumer prices held steady in December , bringing the country 's full-year average well-within the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas ' ( BSP ) target , data from the Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) showed . <p> The headline consumer price index ( CPI ) , or inflation , last month remained at 3.3 percent , the same pace as in the previous month , but faster compared with the 2.6 percent registered in the same month in the previous year . <p> Socioeconomic Planning Secretary Ernesto M. Pernia said the year-on-year rise in inflation was due to faster increases in food prices , like corn , meat , fish , fruits , cereals . <p> However , Pernia said the increases in food prices were tempered by lower non-food items including transport , housing , water , electricity , gas and other fuels . <p> The December inflation brought the country 's full-year average at 3.2 percent , within the government 's target at 2.0 percent to 4.0 percent for the year , but at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2016 . <p> " We see inflation over the near-term to remain stable despite pressures that may be brought about by the newly enacted TRAIN Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion Act program , weather patterns , and uncertainties in international oil markets , " Pernia said in a statement . <p> Pernia added that the moderate full-year inflation rate last year is a good basis for maintaining the government 's inflation target at 2.0 to 4.0 percent for 2018 . <p> NEDA also said supply conditions , particularly for major agricultural commodities appears favorable within the near term . <p> But to relieve the inflationary effects of TRAIN , Pernia said that the government needs to prioritize amending domestic laws that will end quantitative restrictions on rice and replace them with tariffs . <p> " This measure will remove the policy uncertainty in rice trade and thus encourage more investments in production and post-production innovation .
