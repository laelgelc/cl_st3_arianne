<h> Report : The impact of climate change on Alaskan 's physical and mental health <p> Emily Boerger Jan 12 , 2018 <p> The Alaska Department of Health and Social Services has released a 77-page review of the health impacts Alaskans may face in the coming years due to climate change . The report touches on climate change 's effects on mental health , infectious diseases , likelihood of accidents and injuries , exposure to hazardous materials , and effects on health services infrastructure . <p> Alaska is a unique position in that it is the only state Arctic state in the nation and has experienced faster changes in air and water temperatures than the rest of the country . The environmental , historical , and regional impacts of climate change in Alaska have received attention in the past , but this is the first statewide review about human health impacts . <p> According to the report , there are a variety of ways in which climate change negatively impacts health .
