<p> The workshop is set on April 21 and 22 at Handuraw Pizza , White Gold Annex Building , White Gold Club , North Reclamation Area in Cebu City . <p> This year , 12 fellowships are available for emerging writers from Visayas and Mindanao . <p> Non-Cebu-based applicants , if selected , shall shoulder the expenses entailed by food , accommodation , and transportation during the duration of the event . <p> The fellows will be provided with food and workshop materials . The workshop 's screening committee shall also choose 10 observers from the shortlisted applicants to witness and engage in the workshop . <p> The panelists for this year 's workshop are poet Vicente ' Butch ' Bandillo , fictionist-essayist Richel G. Dorotan , screenwriter-fictionist CD Borden , and fictionist Carlos Cortes . Other writers from the south will also serve as guest panelists and lecturers . <p> Manuscripts <p> To be considered , submissions in Sugboanong Binisaya and/or English must consist of any of the following : <p> * Poetry : a suite of five poems ; <p> * Fiction : one short story or three @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one creative non-fiction or three works of flash nonfiction ; and <p> * Drama : one one-act play . <p> All manuscripts should be previously unpublished and have not been included in creative writing thesis . <p> Alumni of any national writers ' workshop are not eligible to apply . <p> The general theme for works to be submitted this year is on the ' ecocritical ' -- works that discuss climate change , global warming , and natural/man-made disasters in the locale ; affinity to rural/urban homelands ; and **25;59;TOOLONG displacement narratives in the aftermaths -- but is not confined only to these themes . <p> Send submissions with subject " **29;86;TOOLONG " ( example : KAGISDela CruzHJuanCreative nonfiction ) to kagisworkshop@gmail.com on or before March 16 , 2018 . <p> Incomplete applications will not be considered . <p> For inquiries from applicants , interested partners and sponsors , the workshop coordinators Karla Quimsing and R.
