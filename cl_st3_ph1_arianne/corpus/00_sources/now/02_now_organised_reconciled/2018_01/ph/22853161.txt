<h> Limlingan : A different disaster movie <h> Limlingan : A different disaster movie <p> THERE are a number of post-apocalyptic movies that were made and shown to the public in recent years . There are the zombies , mass-killing epidemics , viruses and inter-continental ballistic missiles and of course climate change . <p> These movies are based perhaps on real life or what movie makers perceive as the next things that will happen in a few years or so to humanity . It may or may not happen but they are possibilities that world authorities are considering to save us humans on this planet . <p> There are times movie makers base their masterpieces on actual events . Vice-versa , there are events that happen based on movies . Art imitates life and life imitates art , remember ? <p> Recently , I had this medical condition that I had to do some thorough resting that left me stuck watching movies . One came to my attention : the 2017 film " Geostorm . " <p> Watching its beginning seemed to me at first as another global disaster movie @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by the idea that everything in that movie may definitely happen to earth , sooner or later we do not know . This maybe depends on how we destroy ourselves through the destruction of our planet . <p> We are not blaming anyone or anything but , we may find it imperative for us humans to do our share and start saving our planet before its too late . We may share our efforts no matter how big or small but we can always have even the smallest deed of cleaning our residence ' surroundings and planting trees where we can . <p> Going back to the movie , it highlights climate change as a very possible reason of getting mankind out of this planet . In a segment , it showed a portion if a desert in Afghanistan that despite being hot and arid was instantly made to freeze by an abnormal weather condition . <p> I will not make any further story of that movie in order not to make prospective viewers be pre-empted but I can say that its worth watching .
