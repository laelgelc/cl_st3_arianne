<h> Recovering rice production using hybrid seeds pushed <h> Recovering rice production using hybrid seeds pushed <p> THE Office of the Provincial Agriculturist ( OPA ) banked on rice farmers using hybrid seeds to recover reduction in yield brought by effects of climate change as well as calamities and pest infestation this year . <p> Provincial Agriculturist Japhet Masculino said calamities as well as pests and diseases among crops are attributed to climate change . <p> Masculino said these factors are already affecting the province 's rice production output since this early part of the year thus , there is really a need to increase yield . <p> Recent heavy rains which triggered flooding in different parts of Negros Occidental have initially caused about P5.1 million worth of damage and production losses to local crop sector , mostly rice . <p> During the first week of the month , Tropical Storm Agaton left almost P17 million worth of damage to agro-fishery sector . <p> These figures are on top of possible additional losses to rice production brought by reported grain bug infestation in Pontevedra and La Carlota City , which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ highly vulnerable to flooding and pest infestation which can be considered as effects of climate change , " Masculino said , adding that increasing rice production by using hybrid seeds is one priority of the province this year . In hybrid rice production , the yield is 30 percent higher compared to ordinary seeds . <p> Hybrid is still more efficient than the yield using certified seeds , which is 10 percent higher than ordinary seeds , Masculino said . <p> OPA currently has 1,000 15-kilogram bags of available hybrid rice seeds up for distribution to farmers . <p> These seeds stored at Negros First Rice Processing Center at Barangay Tabunan in Bago City can cover 1,000 hectares of farms . <p> In terms of ratio , a 15-kilogram bag of hybrid seeds is good for a one-hectare farm . <p> Masculino had earlier said that a portion of these available seeds will be distributed to some of the 286 farmer-graduates of the School-On-Air Program who signified to plant hybrid after finishing the four-month Hybrid Rice Production and Rice Crop Manager course last week . <p> Latest issues of SunStar @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and tablets .
