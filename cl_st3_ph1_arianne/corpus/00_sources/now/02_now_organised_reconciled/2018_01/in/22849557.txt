<p> On a summer morning in 1988 , as workers at a coal mine in Borken , in western Germany , were going about their business , they were taken aback by a thunderous explosion . It blew the roofs off three of the buildings in sight . The explosion had come from under the ground . The intensity was so great , the ground they were standing on became scorching hot within seconds . <p> Something had set off 4,400 pounds of explosives stored in the underground mines at Borken . When rescue teams arrived , they found 31 bodies . But there were another 57 miners who were never found . The reasons for the negative public perceptions towards nuclear energy are manifold . West and East Germany became the principal theatres of the Cold War . The threat of nuclear annihilation loomed large in this period as NATO troops from the U.S. , France and U.K. were equipped with nuclear weapons on German soil . <p> There were many small scale nuclear reactors constructed in the 1960s in West Germany , although the first commercial operations began in 1969 .
