More than 99 percent of the turtle populations of the northern , warmer area of the Great Barrier Reef are females , and this has baffled the scientists . As per the study , the increasing seawater temperature due to climate change has resulted in this unexpectedly high number of females in one of the world 's largest turtle populations .
