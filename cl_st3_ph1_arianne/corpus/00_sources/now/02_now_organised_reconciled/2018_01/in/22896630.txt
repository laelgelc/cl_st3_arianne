" India offers unique opportunities to the entire world . It offers wealth with wellness , health with wholeness of life , prosperity with peace ... India welcomes one &amp; all , " Shah tweeted . <p> PM @narendramodi 's speech at @wef plenary in Davos is a reason of pride for all Indians . He has perfectly articulated India 's strengths and aspirations in the multi-conceptual world ! <p> Referring to Modi 's speech at WEF 's opening plenary , Shah said the prime minister had superbly invoked how Indian culture has always believed in uniting , not dividing . " Our scriptures , written thousands of years ago @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all united by our shared destinies , " he said . <p> Citing Modi 's remarks on climate change , Shah said it is a potent global challenge . Under Modi 's leadership , India has not only set ambitious targets in this regard but is also taking unprecedented initiatives to achieve them , he added . <p> Modi , Shah said , had raised his voice against terrorism at every global forum . At WEF Davos 2018 , too , he gave a clarion call to the world , to unite to defeat the menace of terrorism . <p> Modi on Tuesday became the first Indian prime minister in 21 years to address the WEF.
