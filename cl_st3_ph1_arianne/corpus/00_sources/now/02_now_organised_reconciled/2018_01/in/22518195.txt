( HT File ) <p> Maharashtra lost 6,346 hectares or 63 sq km of forests to non-forestry purposes including mining , between 2014 and 2017 , reveals data from the union environment ministry . <p> The forest land lost in the last three years is roughly equivalent to the area of Mumbai 's island city , which is 65 sq km ( Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation data ) . <p> At the same time , the country lost 62,369 hectares or 623 sq km forest area , roughly the area of six Sanjay Gandhi National Parks spread across 104 sq km . <p> Maharashtra ranked fourth in India for maximum forest land diverted , with 40 proposals sanctioned over three years .
