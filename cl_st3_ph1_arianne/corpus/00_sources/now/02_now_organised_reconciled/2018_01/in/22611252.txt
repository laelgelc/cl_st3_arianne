<h> State of Environment Report for Telangana released <p> State of Environment Report ( SoER ) for Telangana , for the year 2015 , was released by the Minister of State for Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) Mahesh Sharma , and the Minister for Forest and Environment Jogu Ramanna on Saturday . The report has been prepared by Environment , Protection , Training &amp; Research Institute ( EPTRI ) , an empanelled member in the preparation of SoE reports . SoER -- TS , 2015 , is the first report for the State of Telangana , with the objective of ensuring sustainable development . It provides credible environmental information to policy makers , organisations and the general public for proper planning and implementation of developmental projects , a press statement has informed .
