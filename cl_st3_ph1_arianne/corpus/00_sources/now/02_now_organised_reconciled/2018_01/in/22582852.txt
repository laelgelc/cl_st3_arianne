For marginal farmers ( those who own up to 2.5 acres of land ) KCCs are more flexible , covering warehouse-storage credit , consumption expenses and small-term-loan investments to purchase farm equipment or start mini dairies . <p> Farmers with a KCC could earn nearly Rs 1.49 lakh every year , according to this 2016 NABARD estimate , double the Rs. 69,850 they might earn without one . <p> But a KCC automatically registers farmers with a crop-insurance programme , regardless of consent , and money is deducted from their bank accounts as premia towards crop insurance that they may not want , The Wirereported on 31st March , 2017 .
