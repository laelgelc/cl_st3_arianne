<h> UoH professor nominated as member of NCMR <p> THE HANS INDIA Jan 03,2018 , 02:52 AM IST <p> , <p> Hyderabad : Professor and Head of the Department of Plant Sciences , School of Life Sciences , University of Hyderabad , Ch Venkata Ramana has been nominated as member of the Scientific Advisory Committee of the National Centre for Microbial Resource ( NCMR ) by the Union Department of Biotechnology ( DBT ) . Ramana has been mentioned as one of India 's best researchers in the field of Immunology and Microbiology . <p> This study was undertaken by compiling the Elsevier Science 's Bibliographic indexing database -- SCOPUS for three years ( 2012-2014 ) . He has been selected based on his publication count and h-index . His research publications output is 11.7 with an impact of 10.7 and has the highest h-index of 8 in the field of Immunology and Microbiology . Last year too Ramana was mentioned as one of India 's best researcher . <p> He had published his research findings in high impact journals like Int . J. Syst . Evol . Microbial. , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Res. , J. Proteomic Res. , Genome Announcements. , PLOS1 and Appl . Microbiol . Biotechnology . He received the prestigious TATA Innovation Fellowship from the Department of Biotechnology , Government of India , in 2016 . <p> In recognition to his outstanding contribution to the taxonomy of bacteria , Environment , Forest and Climate change Ministry has honoured the prestigious Dr E K Janaki Ammal National Award for Microbial Taxonomy in the year 2016 . Ramana works on bacterial diversity and metabolomics . He is indexing the bacterial wealth of India and assessing the assets and liabilities of bacteria and their metabolites .
