<p> Which state government department is the most corrupt of them all ? Going by the number of corruption cases busted by the Anti Corruption Bureau , the Home department tops them all . In the year gone by , one Class 1 , one Class 2 and 46 Class 3 officers of the Home department were either caught red-handed taking a bribe or nabbed by the ACB decoy teams . While the Home officials outranked all in number of corruption cases lodged against them , the Law department took the top spot in the bribe amount recovered -- Rs 20.62 lakh from two cases . <p> The bribe money involved in cases against 49 home officials was Rs 5.10 lakh .
