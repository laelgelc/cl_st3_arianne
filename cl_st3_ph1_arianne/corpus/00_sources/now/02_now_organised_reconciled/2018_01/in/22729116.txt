" Of the largest economies of the world , the two largest economies of the G77 are strongly committed to the leadership in climate action - and I refer to China and India , " Guterres said on the occasion of handover ceremony of the Chairmanship of the ' Group of 77 ( G77 ) and China ' in New York @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the group from Ecuador .
