<h> National Conference on Welfare of Laboratory Animals <p> A one-day National Conference on Welfare of Laboratory Animals was organised by CPCSEA , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , with the theme of " Implementation of 3Rs ( Replacement , Reduction and Refinement ) while using animals in academic research and regulatory testing in India " . The conference deliberated upon elaborating the understanding of animal use in academics , as well as regulatory testing and evaluating the use of alternatives , wherever animals can be exempted from testing . The conference also included brainstorming sessions in the form of panel discussions , which had eminent scientists working with animals , versus those working in the field of alternatives . <p> The conference laid emphasis on the issue of ethical use of animals in academics and regulatory testing in India . The discussions were focussed on evaluating the possibilities of exemption of animal experiments in academics and regulatory testing . The conference was also attended by representatives from animal welfare organisations like PFA , and PETA India , to encourage exchange of views between the stakeholders . <p> The Conference was attended by 200 participants from all over the country from various research establishments . The inaugural session of the Conference was chaired by Additional Secretary , MoEF&amp;CC , A.K Jain and Drug Controller General of India ( DCGI ) , Dr. G. N. Singh . <p> The CPCSEA is mandated to alleviate pain &amp; suffering to the laboratory animals before , during or after the performance of experiments on them . CPCSEA is trying to find appropriate alternatives to the use of animals in experiments in India .
