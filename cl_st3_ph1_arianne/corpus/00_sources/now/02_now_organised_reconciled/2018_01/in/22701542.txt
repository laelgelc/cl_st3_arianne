<h> States <h> Leisure <h> The Telegraph <h> Film rings green alarm bell <p> Sudeshna BanerjeeJan 12 , 2018 00:00 IST <p> Soumitra Chatterjee alerts Calcuttans about climate change in a still from the documentary film Kolkata 2070 <p> Calcutta : The city is sinking . Or so it might in 50 years if action is not taken collectively as well as individually by citizens . This is the message that Soumitra Chatterjee is taking to a cross-section of Calcuttans he speaks to in a documentary film titled Kolkata 2070 , directed by Suman Mukhopadhyay , produced by the Centre for Contemporary Communication ( CCC ) and funded by the German consulate general . <p> The storyline and the script of the documentary , the makers say , are based on established scientific evidences and documents like the latest report of the Intergovernmental Panel of Climate Change ( IPCC ) and the World Bank 's report on climate change for Calcutta . The choice of 2070 as the year in which the imaginary scene of inundation is shown to take place is based on predictions in such documents . IPCC @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ global climate strategy is built , states in its report , titled Climate Change 2014 , that " by the 2070s , the top Asian cities in terms of population exposure are expected to be Kolkata , ... " followed by others . A World Bank report on the city 's climate change has predicted that pockets of the city may be under water for 15 days at a stretch if drainage is not improved as heavy rain will become a regular occurrence . <p> The film opens with an imaginary scene of the city submerged in neck-deep water in 2070 and residents , with their heads barely out of the water , lamenting the loss of the city they have grown up in . <p> " The level of ignorance in the city about the effects of climate change is amazing . It makes you want to cry , " says Mallika Jalan of CCC which works to make the city environmentally sustainable . " The apathy in people is because of ignorance . " <p> In the film , there is footage from the Sunderbans , the city @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on Ghoramara island . While neighbouring Lohachura has already sunk under the rising sea , Ghoramara too is sinking , leading to mass displacement of the islanders , " said director Suman Mukhopadhyay . He also shot extensively across Calcutta showing how the canals continue to drain untreated sewage in the river and unsegregated garbage is piling up . The voice of Kabir Suman explains how that is pushing the city towards the edge . <p> Chatterjee talks to a cross-section of people in a dramatised setting , asking them if they are aware of the impending peril . " Any Calcuttan could have voiced those concerns but we wanted him because of his immense credibility , " said Mukhopadhyay . <p> The film has been funded by the German consulate general in Calcutta . " The issues in the film are threatening and of utmost importance - clean drinking water , sanitation , health , energy , eco-friendly production and usage . Our goal is to establish channels of communication between civil society and politics , addressing issues of sustainable development and transformation towards a low carbon economy , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Incidentally , the latest UN Climate Change Conference took place in Bonn last November . <p> " The makers have come forward to raise awareness and I did my bit as an actor , " said Chatterjee , who recently protested and stalled the filling up of a waterbody in his Golf Green neighbourhood . <p> The bi-lingual documentary has been shown in over 30 schools and colleges and will be screened on Sunday at the Goethe Institut . " We will be happy to screen it anywhere on invitation . We want Calcuttans to wake up before it is too late . We do not mean to be alarmist but we want to alert people by holding up a mirror to the future , " says Jalan.
