<h> SA Weather Service lash out at Zille for comments made regarding the water crisis <p> The SA Weather Service has lashed out at Western Cape Premier Helen Zille for comments made during an interview on BBC on Wednesday . <p> Speaking on BBC Newsnight Zille blamed the water crisis currently facing her province on the weather service 's inability to correctly predict when a drought would hit . <p> " This drought could never have been foreseen , " Zille said . <p> " The South African Weather Services have said to me their models do n't work anymore , in an era of climate change . " <p> The SA Weather Services responded to these comments , in a rather scathing manner , via a statement on Friday calling her remarks a " cop-out " . <p> " We view this in a very dim light as the Premier only had one briefing from SAWS and already draws conclusions on our work , " the statement reads . <p> " This is regrettable as SAWS would not comment on policy makers and the lack of action on advice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Premier and respectfully requested her to refrain from casting aspersions on the work of the Service . " <p> According to Zille , the drought currently being experienced in the Western Cape was not supposed to hit until 2025 and , due to climate change , SA Weather Services models could not pick up that it would happen much earlier . <p> In retaliation to these remarks , SA Weather Services called on Zille to refrain from making " unwarranted comments " as she is not skilled in Meteorology nor is she a scientist . <p> " Blaming the weather or climate and the Weather Service is a cop-out for policy inaction and ineptitude in the implementation of multidisciplinary research and reports that have long pointed to water challenges in the country , the Western Cape and in Cape Town , " the statement continues .
