<h> More people went hungry in Africa last year . But new initiatives also bore fruit <p> File : The Nigerian government has allocated R3.1-billion to agriculture and subsidised farmers ' purcases of new equipment . Photo : REUTERS/Philimon Bulawayo <p> The number of people experiencing food insecurity in Africa rose from 220 million people to 224 million people this year . The Food and Agriculture Organisation 's regional overview for the year attributes this to changing weather patterns that led to poor harvests , a loss of livestock , conflict and recurrent droughts . <p> Food security occurs when all people , at all times , have access to enough food to meet their dietary needs . To declare countries " food secure " analysts look at a combination of availability , access and utilisation . <p> Mauritius , South Africa , Ghana , Namibia and Senegal are among the top 10 most food-secure countries on the continent . This is attributed to the use of new crops and agricultural production technologies . <p> But ultimately the ability to achieve food security depends on political will and the effectiveness @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sector . <p> Though the 2017 rise in food insecurity is disheartening , some African countries have made progress over the last decade . And there were some initiatives from this year that deserve a special mention for the precedents they set . <p> And in Kenya , the practice of conservation agriculture has taken root , resulting in bigger yields . <p> Equally important are the growing number of innovations in financing . To improve productivity , and achieve food security , farmers must be able to invest in technology . <p> But , many African farmers live below the poverty line . They can rarely borrow from formal financial institutions as they do n't have collateral . Today , only 6 percent of total bank credit on the continent goes to agriculture . <p> Tanzania addressed this challenge by rolling out a toolkit that allows farmers to pre-pay for discounted agricultural inputs using mobile money . Farmers are also provided with customised plans that guide them on how to maximise crop yields . <p> New policies <p> This year several African countries strengthened and launched new agricultural policies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . For example , policies can lower the cost of agricultural input prices by introducing subsidies . Or they can allocate resources in a way that benefit farmers -- like road construction for market access . <p> The Nigerian government deserves mention for some key initiatives it introduced this year . <p> For starters , it launched the National Policy on Food and Nutrition , which allocated 92 billion naira ( R3.1-billion ) to the agricultural sector . Policies were also enacted that gave farmers access to agricultural inputs and provided them with modern equipment at subsidised prices . One example is a fertiliser initiative that delivered over four million bags of fertiliser to farmers at affordable prices . This meant more farmers could buy fertiliser , giving them bigger yields . <p> In terms of areas of policy areas , 2017 turned out to be a good year for irrigation . <p> Only 7 percent of Africa is irrigated . In Nigeria 's case , the potential irrigable area is 21 million hectares ( about the size of Italy ) -- with only 200,000 hectares currently irrigated . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 33,000 hectares of land . <p> And things are set to improve even further following a World Bank announcement that it 's investing $495-million ( R6.1-billion ) in irrigation . <p> In Rwanda , the government has made a commitment to intensify irrigation initiatives to help citizens cope with climate change . Over 44,000ha of land would be developed with formal irrigation infrastructure -- that 's about 82,000 football fields . <p> In Malawi , the government launched a similar initiative which would put over 100,000 hectares of land across the country under irrigation -- about 187,000 football fields . <p> Because irrigation improves food security , these initiatives will continue to play a vital role in moving towards food security . <p> So , even though the number of food insecure in Africa has risen this year , discovering effective new technologies and policies that work is important for the future . This year , these gains were in targeted government interventions and the adoption of modern agricultural technologies . This allowed farmers to improve productivity , allowing African countries to make progress in the quest to attain food security .
