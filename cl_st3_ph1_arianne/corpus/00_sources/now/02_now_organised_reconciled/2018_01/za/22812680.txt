The Constitution gives municipalities exclusive power over " potable water supply systems and domestic waste-water and sewage disposal systems " , but higher tiers of government must monitor and support development of local government capacity . And , what is more , they must " see to the effective performance " of municipalities ' water functions . <p> The Constitution says everyone has the right to access sufficient water . It says : " The National Government , acting through the Minister , has the power to regulate the use , flow and control of all water in the Republic .
