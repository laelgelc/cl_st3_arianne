<p> The loss of power during recent Fraser Valley ice storms meant Jennifer Quick , who lives on a Mission farm , had no running water , could n't cook with appliances and still had to tend to a daughter sick with stomach flu . <p> As if that was n't enough , she had to endure the sight of her shivering llamas . <p> " I brought them outside at one point and when I brought them back in , they had icicles on their fur , " she said , adding the animals stayed in the warmth of their barn from then on . <p> For three and a half days , Quick and her family were among more than 160,000 BC Hydro customers in the Fraser Valley left in the dark after ice storms whipped through the region . <p> BC Hydro expects to get all customers back online Tuesday , five days after the storm hit . <p> And with another storm possibly on the horizon , the utility is defending its response to the treacherous weather . <p> Update #MissionBC : crews have restored final @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> BC Hydro spokesperson Mora Scott said the utility has a " best in class " storm response system . <p> " In a typical storm situation we normally have 95 per cent of our customers back up within 24 hours .
