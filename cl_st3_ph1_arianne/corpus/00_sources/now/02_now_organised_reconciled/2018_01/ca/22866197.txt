<h> News Brantford-Brant <h> Financial assistance for flooding forthcoming <p> A financial assistance pilot program to help residents of a Brantford neighbourhood better protect their homes from flooding has been approved by city councillors . Councillors voted unanimously in favour of a basement flooding prevention grant program at a recent committee of the whole meeting . They are expected to give the program final approval at a Jan. 30 meeting of council . If approved by council , there will be a public information session on Feb. 26 at Branlyn Community Centre from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m . Detailed information about the program will be posted to the city 's website - www.brantford.ca - during the first week of February . " I fully support this , " Ward 3 Coun. Greg Martin said at Tuesday 's meeting . " With the changes to the environment this is something that will be important to the community . " I 'm glad to see this come forward . Funding from the program will support a list of eligible prevention measures including a basement risk assessment and cover up to 80 per cent of the cost of the work upon completion . By way of example , the program will support the installation of sump pumps and storm and sanitary valves to help prevent basement flooding . The city will also , waive the plumbing permit fee of $105 for work that would normally require a permit . The flooding prevention program is in response to the impact of an Aug. 11 , 2017 storm that had a major impact on residents in the north-eastern section of the city . An estimated 46 millimetres of rain fell on the city in a 24-hour period and 32.4 millimetres fell in just 20 minutes . The rainfall put tremendous pressure on the city 's storm water management system and led to flooding in some homes in the area . Residents on Ashgrove Avenue were particularly hard hit by the storm .
