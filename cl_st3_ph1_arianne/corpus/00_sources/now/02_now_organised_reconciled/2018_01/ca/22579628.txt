<p> A proposal to use some manmade , low- ? carbon fuel in place of fossil fuels at Carmeuse Lime and Stone 's Beachville facility has drawn disapproval from local environmental groups . <p> Carmeuse announced Wednesday it has requested approval from the Ministry of the Environment to conduct one of the first-ever demonstration projects using alternative low-carbon fuel ( ALCF ) derived from non-hazardous waste materials such as paper , plastic , rubber and wood . <p> The project is the result of new regulations that allows energy-intensive industries such as Carmeuse to replace fossil fuels as part of a temporary demonstration project and test the impact the alternative fuel has in an effort to decrease greenhouse gases linked to climate change . <p> " We are proud to be the first industry in Ontario to pursue a demonstration project at our Beachville operation to use ALCF in place of coal or natural gas , " said Christopher Martin , Carmeuse 's regional environmental manager . " The demonstration project is consistent with our company 's mission of promoting sustainability and protecting the environment .
