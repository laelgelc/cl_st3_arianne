<h> Local greenhouse subject of ministry scrutiny after waste water leak <p> A local greenhouse is under scrutiny after waste water spilled into a nearby creek in early December , a ministry spokesperson confirmed Tuesday . <p> Ontario Plants Propagation Limited , a 15-acre greenhouse operation located near Shedden , was visited by Ministry of Environment and Climate Change staff when nutrients , fertilizers and floor drainage managed to make it to the creek . <p> " ... A pump used to manage the facility 's waste water failed , causing an onsite waste water pond to overflow . The waste water flowed over land into a municipal drain that discharges into a nearby creek , " Gary Wheeler , ministry spokesperson , said in an email . <p> Wheeler said the ministry takes spills " very seriously .
