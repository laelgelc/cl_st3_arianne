<p> The federal and Northwest Territories governments announced they will spend about $96 million to fix up community roads , fill in ditches and to build a new community youth centre , among other projects across the territory . <p> The federal government promised the majority of the funds -- up to $72.12 million -- while the territorial government will provide up to $23.87 million for the Small Communities Fund . Local communities will contribute the remainder of $354,700 . <p> The majority of the money will go towards fixing up community access roads and winter roads across the territory ; Highways 1 , 3 , and 7 will also get facelifts . The Ingraham Trail near Yellowknife , and the Dempster Highway from Tsiigehtchic to Inuvik will also be upgraded , according to a government project list . <p> Other projects include : <p> Upgrades to roads , ditches and culverts in the Nahanni Butte region <p> A cold storage garage for Sambaa K'e <p> A new 1,500 square foot youth centre in Colville Lake <p> A new sewage vacuum truck for Wrigley <p> Territorial ministers Caroline Cochrane and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Behchoko , N.W.T. , Thursday for the announcement . <p> The new money will help create jobs and improve Northerners ' quality of life , said Federal Minister of Infrastructure Amarjeet Sohi , in a news release . <p> Schumann , minister for the territory 's Department of Infrastructure , says the projects will " increase the resiliency of our transportation system to challenges related to age , increased traffic loads , and the impacts of climate change .
