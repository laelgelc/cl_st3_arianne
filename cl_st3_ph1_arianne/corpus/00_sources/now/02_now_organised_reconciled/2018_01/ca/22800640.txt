<p> All sides in the debate about density and intensification in the downtown core need to get beyond simplistic arguments about the height of tower developments , a leading urban planner said . <p> Vancouver-based urban planner Brent Toderian , in town to speak at a special meeting of city council Wednesday night and Friday 's climate change symposium , said that to achieve successful urban intensification -- getting more people living in an area -- city planners , community groups and individual citizens need to change how they approach development . <p> " How to do density well is a complex and creative thing and it is a heck of a lot more than just how many floors a building has , " Toderian said on Wednesday afternoon in front of City Hall . " I think the community conversation has to evolve substantially . <p> " You can point at a horribly designed building and say , ' But at least it is n't a few floors taller , ' " he said . " That shows a very simplistic perspective of what is good or bad density @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ his impression of the city is one of a municipality that has in the past not put much thought into the quality of residential buildings that were constructed downtown . <p> " I look at things done in previous generations and it is easy for me to conclude that in the past , Kingston was just grateful to have anything happen and the only debate was whether you should tear down a heritage building . That was the only conversation , " he said . <p> " You have some of the worst architecture on a waterfront that I have ever seen . And I have seen waterfronts all over the world . " <p> Toderian said all cities of Kingston 's size are having the same discussions about growth and intensification . Many , he said , are approaching it in a better way than Kingston . <p> He said density is important because it will help determine how cities are able to respond to challenges , such as climate change , public health and transportation . <p> Toderian said city staff need to get better in their approach @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Developers need to be open-minded to new approaches to building . <p> And the community needs to think about more than height . <p> " We all have to rethink our roles , " he said .
