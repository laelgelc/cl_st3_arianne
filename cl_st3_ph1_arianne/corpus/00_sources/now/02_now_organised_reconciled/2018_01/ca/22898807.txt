The IYOR aims to strengthen global awareness of the value of coral reef ecosystems , promote partnerships , and identify and implement effective management strategies for the conservation , sustainable use and resilience of reef ecosystems . <p> According to the UN Environment Programme ( UNEP or UN Environment ) , the primary threats to coral reefs are rising temperatures , ocean acidification , pollution , plastics and sediments , destructive fishing practices , and overfishing . In a statement , Bainimarama said the nomination aims to conserve the area for future generations .
