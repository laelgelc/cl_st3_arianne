<h> Public has chance to review revised Ridge Landfill expansion plans <p> The owner of the Ridge Landfill has reworked its proposal for a 20-year expansion of the site to meet more stringent provincial diversion requirements , and the public now has an opportunity to review it . <p> Cathy Smith , project manager with Waste Connections of Canada , said shortly after the original terms of reference were submitted in January 2017 for the proposed expansion , the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change released a new Strategy for a Waste Free Ontario . <p> " Although we had considered diversion targets , in the first terms of reference they were n't completely consistent with what the ministry released , " Smith said . <p> She said Waste Connections , working in consultation with the MOECC , agreed to withdraw its original terms of reference , and submit something that is " more reflective of the new waste diversion targets set by the province .
