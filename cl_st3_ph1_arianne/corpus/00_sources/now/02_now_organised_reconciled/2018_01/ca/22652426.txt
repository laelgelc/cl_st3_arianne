<p> Nunavut will get more money to prepare for climate change as part of the federal government 's 2017 budget , starting this year . <p> The money is part of Canada 's Climate Change Preparedness in the North program , which is managed by the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs . <p> The government of Nunavut started planning the projects with funding from the 2016 federal budget and with this additional money , the projects will get underway this year . <p> The chosen projects will get a combined total of $1.7 million over four years .
