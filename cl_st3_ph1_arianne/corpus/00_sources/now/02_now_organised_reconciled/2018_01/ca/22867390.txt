<p> On March 7 , from 11 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. , York will be holding its sixth annual multidisciplinary Undergraduate Research Fair in the Scott Library Collaboratory on the second floor . The event will consist of an award ceremony of monetary prizes and a reception following the Fair . <p> There will be a showcase of undergraduate research , work , and an art exhibition , the Art Walk , will lead spectators to the Fair with an array of student art and design work . <p> This multidisciplinary event is open to all communities , thus , an emphasis is made on presentation in plain language , so that everyone understands the project 's purpose . <p> The Fair is co-sponsored by York libraries and the Office of the Vice President , Research , &amp; Innovation . <p> The 2018 undergraduate research fair welcomes applicants from all York faculties , including Glendon . <p> This is a great opportunity to gain valuable presentation experience or artwork exhibit credit , receive a signed certificate honouring participation in the university 's juried Undergraduate Research Fair , earn a chance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have work published on the cover of York e-journal , Revue YOUR ( York Online Undergraduate Research ) Review . <p> Revue YOUR Review is an annual , multidisciplinary , open-access , bilingual , and refereed e-journal , offering an opportunity for York students to prepare a paper for publication . <p> All submissions are reviewed by an editorial board comprised of York faculty members , writing instructors , librarians , students , and expert readers within the discipline . The journal 's licensing falls in line with that of Creative Commons , which governs the author 's licensing rights . <p> Students are encouraged to start filling the application forms as soon as possible , submitting a graded , research-based project or honours thesis prepared between December and January . <p> Applications must also include a 250-word abstract , and " the work must have been completed for an undergraduate credit course and have received a grade of B+ or higher , " says Barbara Joy of York 's Media Relations .
