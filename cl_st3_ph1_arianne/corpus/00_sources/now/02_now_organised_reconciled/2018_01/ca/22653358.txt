<h> So-called " wicked " policy problems might not always be solvable , but there are ways to better understand and manage them . <p> Since the 1980s , there has been much talk about using better evidence to solve our big policy problems . The idea is simple -- evidence helps us to understand the scope of the problem , the causal relationships at work , and the optimal solutions . <p> This notion has been widely promoted by the OECD and its member countries , including Canada , the US , and many others .
