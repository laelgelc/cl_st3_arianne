<h> In the eye of the refugee hurricane <p> Atmospheric hurricanes have devastated many lives recently , their ferocity enhanced by warm seas and moist air associated with climate change . <p> And that same historic environmental crisis , coupled with massive droughts and huge social and political stresses in the Middle East and North Africa , is creating human migrations on this planet so damaging and turbulent that they could be described as ' refugee hurricanes . ' Violent winds of forced exile tear families apart , flinging them to different continents on opposite ends of the planet . As political storms brew , foundations of people 's lives are uprooted and tossed into oblivion . Even when they make it to safety , a torrent of prejudice and xenophobia rains down on them , dampening their dreams . <p> My own journey started as an environmental scientist in Canada , attempting to reclaim land destroyed by oilsands mining ; but ended as self-described carbon buster , recognizing the greater need to reclaim our atmosphere by not digging up oilsands .
