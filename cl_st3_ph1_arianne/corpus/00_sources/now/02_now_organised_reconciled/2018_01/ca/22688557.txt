<p> The City of Lethbridge will soon boast a close-to-home option to jumpstart the careers of future ironworkers . <p> An introduction to ironworking is being offered by Ironworkers Local 725 based out of Calgary , and is available through the Chinook Regional Foundation for Career Transitions . <p> The group first came to Lethbridge last year and had previously rented space from Lethbridge College . With an eye to permanent placement in the city , however , it was decided the group would look for a place of their own . <p> " With all the wind turbine work and all the green energy stuff coming , and with the Indigenous programs we want to run , we needed a place to set up , " said Rob Calver , business manager for Ironworkers Local 725 . " So we found a double bay and decided to rent it . It was perfect for us . " <p> It was at that point they were informed the property was not zoned for their intended use . <p> On Monday , following a public hearing , a change was made @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ group to operate a training facility in an industrial area . <p> Calver said the change was incidental and stemmed from the location of the property they have secured for the program . <p> The program involves 10-week block training for " Trades and Climate Change . " <p> The curriculum includes theory and classroom activity , but is primarily hands-on learning . <p> " They will have a better idea about what we do in the trades , " said Calver . <p> In this way , the course is not an employment program so much as an introduction to ironworking and an opportunity for skill development . The program takes a ground-up approach to training . <p> The group is also planning on running an Indigenous upgrading program called " Tradewinds to Success . " <p> " It brings young Indigenous people in for six weeks to do tradestreaming and upgrading to an entrance exam , " Calver said . " From there , we go into eight weeks of intensive iron working training , with work placement at the end . " We supply 55 employers in the province with ironworkers . " <p> The program has competitive entry but is free for those accepted . <p> " Our membership has agreed that this is the way to grow , " said Calver . " To rejuvenate our workforce , we need to get young people out and we need to get them that training ahead of time .
