<p> The Davao City Health Office ( CHO ) on Monday declared a measles outbreak as the number of people infected by the highly-contagious virus rapidly increased in just a span of three months . <p> CHO has already recorded a total number of 222 cases of measles , locally known as tigdas , from November 2017 to January this year . 68 of those who have been found positive of the infection were in January alone . <p> According to the pamphlets released by the Department of Health ( DOH ) , measles is described as a virus that prospers well in the country due to climate change , and it infects most when temperature levels are low . <p> The DOH stated that symptoms of measles infection include high fever , coughs , inflammation of the eyes , and a total-body skin rash . <p> Doctor Josephine Villafuerte , city 's chief health officer , said a total of 13,000 children aged five months to 59 months have been given vaccination since the first case of measles was reported two weeks ago . <p> She explained that these @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ center measles immunization . <p> Just last week , there are also some 16 call center agents reported positive of the virus . <p> " We wanted to avoid the spread of the virus , " Villafuerte said in a press release . <p> Villafuerte added that a single case of measles could " lead to an outbreaks " as a " highly infectious " nature of the virus . <p> To avoid the spread of the virus , the chief health officer said they have been increasing information campaign on measles awareness , and that the agency keeps on advising workplaces that if any of their employees shows signs of measles , that employee should not report for work . <p> According to the Regional Epidemiology Surveillance Unit of DOH , Davao City had the most number of measles cases , apart from dengue , in the first three months of 2014.
