But I must state that all the 17 goals are intricately intertwined . It is impossible , therefore , to create a sustainable and inclusive world by treating any of them with lesser attention than the others . <p> The SDGs of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development officially came into force on January 1 , 2016 , following adoption by world leaders at a historic United Nations Summit the previous September . They are designed to build on the successes of the Millennium Development Goals ( MDGs ) . <p> Nations are expected to frame policies and focus their efforts around the goals to end extreme poverty and inequalities and tackle climate change while ensuring no one is left behind .
