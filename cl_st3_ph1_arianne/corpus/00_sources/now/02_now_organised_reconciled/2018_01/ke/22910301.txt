one-day Urbanisation and Housing Workshop on Kenya 's new cities , to identify key areas of intervention by Government to advance the new cities that effectively address the urbanisation agenda . <p> 2030 , " said Vision 2030 Delivery Secretariat , Director General , Dr. Julius Muia . <p> The workshop is aligned with President Uhuru Kenyatta 's BIG FOUR agenda of providing affordable housing for all Kenyans by 2022 . <p> This goal will be achieved by constructing at least 500,000 affordable houses employing new technology , low cost financing and promoting large-scale investment in low-cost
