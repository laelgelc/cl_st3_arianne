<h> Busia ' fish cages ' to increase stock , end fishermen arrests in Uganda <p> The Busia government has introduced fish cage culture in Busia in a bid to save Lake Victoria , whose fish population is dwindling due to overfishing , poor fishing methods , pollution and climate change . <p> The project will also go a long way in putting an end to arbitrary arrests of Kenyan fishermen , who go to fish on the Ugandan side of the lake . It is supported by the Finnish Government through the Programme for Agriculture and Livelihood in Western Communities . <p> Bukoma Beach in Bunyala West ward , Budalang'i constituency , received 18 fish cages , each stocked with 2,000 fingerlings . The county water ambulance was on hand to take dignitaries to Munaka , about 10km from Bukoma beach , where the cages are positioned . <p> Fisheries director Timothy Odende said the county will introduce 60 more fish cages in other beaches in Bunyala and Samia subcounties , which will be managed by the Beach Management Units chairmen . <p> He said the fish cage project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We shall have two days in a week where the fishermen will not interfere with the lake , to give it a breather . Two of the remaining fish cages will accommodate the tilapia fish from the 18 cages as they grow bigger . <p> The first fish cage will be ready for harvesting after six months , with a single fish cage expected to produce at least 1,800 fish , which is equivalent to 720kg . <p> " If the farmers get good harvest , they can raise about Sh216,000 from one cage , and this will translate to about Sh3.9 million , " Odende said , adding that fish farmers from other parts of the county not bordering the lake can also take part . <p> The county government has assured the fish cage farmers that it will outsource a firm that will offer technical management services , with the BMUs providing security .
