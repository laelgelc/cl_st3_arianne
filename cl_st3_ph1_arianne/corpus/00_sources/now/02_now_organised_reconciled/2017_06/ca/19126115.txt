<h> Winter scene melts minutes after it lands <p> BRANDON -- Covering the highway like snow , a short-lived but extreme weather event produced a burst of pea-sized hail along a stretch of Highway 45 on the west side of Oakburn Monday . <p> Motorist Sherry Brandon took a photograph of it . Without it , she said , some people might not have believed it took place , since minutes later , it had melted .
