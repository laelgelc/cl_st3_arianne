<p> In an opinion piece published in The Hamilton Spectator , Donaldson said that the economic prospects of the Bay Area " depend in large part on the vitality and resiliency of its industries and employers , " and that extreme weather and climate change can disrupt it all . <p> " The issue of climate change is a significant and growing concern for the industry because claims for weather-related damage are growing fast , " Donaldson argued in the piece . <p> " For many years , fire was the number one cause of insured damage , but no more . Flood events now constitute the largest proportion of disaster claims in Canada , " she explained . " As climate change unfolds , expect to see extreme weather-related damage continue to trend upward . The impact on individuals , their communities and governments will be dramatic .
