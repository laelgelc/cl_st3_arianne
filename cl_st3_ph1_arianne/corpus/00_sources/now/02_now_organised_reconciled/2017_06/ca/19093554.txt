<h> Province Announces Winners of the Minister 's Award for Environmental Excellence <p> Ontario is recognizing the outstanding efforts of local champions doing their part to protect the environment and helping Ontario achieve its commitment to transition to a zero-waste , low-carbon and growing economy . <p> Reducing greenhouse gas pollution by using innovative tools to conserve energy and increasing energy efficiency in the building sector <p> Helping prepare for the negative impacts of climate change by increasing the resilience of infrastructure and natural landscapes in cities . <p> These efforts support the province 's move toward a circular economy , which reuses and recycles valuable materials destined for landfills , protecting the environment while creating new jobs and investment opportunities . <p> The work of these organizations and individuals also supports the government 's own actions to combat climate change , including putting a cap on pollution and investing all proceeds from the province 's carbon market into programs like energy efficient home retrofits , electric vehicle incentives and new cycling infrastructure to help families , communities and businesses reduce their greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Protecting our environment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ create jobs , grow our economy and help people in their everyday lives . <h> Quick Facts <p> Since
