<h> Spreading Yoga Globally <p> In his maiden speech at the United Nations General Assembly on Sept. 27 , 2014 , the prime minister of India Narendra Modi said , " Yoga embodies unity of mind and body . Thought and action . Restraint and fulfillment . Harmony between man and nature . A holistic approach to health and well-being . It is not about exercise but to discover the sense of oneness with yourself , the world and the nature . By changing our lifestyle and creating consciousness , it can help us deal with climate change . Let us work towards adopting an International Yoga Day . " <p> In less than 90 days later -- on Dec. 11 -- 177 member states of the United Nations became co-sponsors of the resolution moved by India on celebrating the International Yoga Day and it was adopted by 193 Member States , making it one of the most universally accepted UNGA resolutions of all times . <p> Yoga does not adhere to any particular religion , belief system or community . India is happy to share its ancient heritage and knowledge of yoga with the world , in line with the traditional philosophy of " Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam . " This Sanskrit phrase from ancient Indian texts means " the world is one family . " <p> Yoga has the ability to change the course of our future . We can achieve development , prosperity and well-being without necessarily going down the path of reckless consumption . It does n't mean that economies will suffer ; it will mean that our economies will take on a different character . For Indians , respect for nature is an integral part of spiritualism .
