<h> Improved Air Quality Has CK Citizens Breathing Easier <p> Chatham-Kent residents can take a deep breath knowing our air is cleaner than it was a decade ago . <p> Tony Munoz , who specializes in air quality for the Ontario Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change , says like many cities in Ontario , the quality of our air is only going up . <p> " Air quality in Ontario overall is improving and Chatham is following the same trend . We 're seeing improved air quality across the area from 2006 to 2015 , " he says . <p> Munoz says the ministry 's initiatives to reduce emissions have contributed to the improvement in air quality . <p> " We 've been working hard to reduce emissions from common air pollutants that form smog . We saw drops in particulate matter , nitrogen dioxide , sulfur dioxide , and volatile organic compounds .
