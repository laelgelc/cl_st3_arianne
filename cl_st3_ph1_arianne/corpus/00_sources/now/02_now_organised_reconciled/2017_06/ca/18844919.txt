<h> Digitization top choice for tech investment as companies chase a more sustainable future : DNV GL <p> Of 15 emerging technologies surveyed , digitalization was chosen as the area in which oil and gas companies are most likely to invest in 2017 -- across R&amp;D , trials and full-scale implementations -- indicating the industry has recognized it as a path to improved profitability and reduced risk , according to DNV GL research . <p> The outlook for the oil and gas industry in 2017 is an industry benchmark study from the Norwegian consultancy that was first launched in 2011 . DNV GL surveyed 723 senior professionals and executives across the global oil and gas industry , along with 14 in-depth interviews with a range of experts , business leaders and analysts . Two-thirds of respondents are employed by suppliers and service companies across the industry , while 26 per cent work for oil and gas operators . The remaining respondents come from regulators and trade associations . <p> About 40 per cent expect their organization 's spending on digitalization to increase in 2017 and that the low oil and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 45 per cent say their focus has remained the same . Other obstacles were a lack of awareness among senior management about digitalization and the potential benefits , a lack of the required skills and bureaucratic/procedural obstacles . <p> " It is striking how , funding aside , these top barriers to digitalization all relate to people and culture .
