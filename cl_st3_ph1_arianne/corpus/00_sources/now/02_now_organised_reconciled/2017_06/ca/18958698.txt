<h> Ontario Announces Results of June Cap and Trade Program Auction <p> Today , Ontario announced the results of the province 's auction of greenhouse gas emission allowances , held June 6 , 2017 . <p> A total of 25,296,367 current ( 2017 ) allowances were sold at a settlement price of $18.72 and a total of 1,674,000 future ( 2020 ) greenhouse gas emission allowances were sold at a settlement price of $18.30 .
