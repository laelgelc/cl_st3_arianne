<h> Climate-induced Food Scarcity Looming Issue <p> COLUMBUS , Ohio -- While climate change is expected to lead to more violence related to food scarcity , new research suggests that the strength of a country 's government plays a vital role in preventing uprisings . <p> " A capable government is even more important to keeping the peace than good weather , " said Bear Braumoeller , co-author of the study and associate professor of political science at The Ohio State University . <p> While previous studies had examined the impact of climate change-induced weather patterns on violence and the increased danger of violence in weak or failing states , this is the first study to demonstrate that the combination of the two risk factors is even more dangerous than they would be separately . <p>
