Bees are critically important pollinators for many crops and most wild flowering plants ; estimates of the benefits they provide humans through these " ecosystem services " is measured in the tens of billions of dollars . <p> Lab experiments have shown that neonicotinoids -- the most widely-used class of insecticide worldwide -- have negative effects on bee life spans , foraging behaviour , the production of new queen bees , and other measures of health . But evidence from the real world has been scant and conflicting .
