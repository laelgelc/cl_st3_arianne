<p> Former United States President Bill Clinton and former Canadian Prime Minister Jean Chretien are set to hold a summit highlighting the relationship between their two countries in Montreal this fall . <p> The two will meet on Oct. 4 at an event that will be open to the public and has been organized by the Canadian American Business Council . It 's part of a series of events featuring former heads of government from the two countries and which celebrate Canada 's 150th anniversary of confederation . <p> Next Tuesday , former President George W. Bush and former PM Stephen Harper will meet in Dallas , TX . <p> The meetings come at a fraught time of Canada-US relations , as President Donald Trump has repeatedly threatened to renegotiate NAFTA and accused Canada 's softwood lumber and dairy industries of unfair trade practices . This week , Trump withdrew the United States from the Paris Agreement on climate change , a decision which drew condemnation from Prime Minister Justin Trudeau .
