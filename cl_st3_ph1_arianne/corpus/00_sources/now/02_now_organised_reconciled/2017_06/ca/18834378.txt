Their challenge will be to minimize the damage that a rogue U.S. president can inflict in the coming months and years . <p> In Brussels , Mr. Trump harangued other members of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization , many of which sacrificed their own citizens ' lives fighting alongside the U.S. in Afghanistan and elsewhere . He refused to reaffirm the United States ' commitment to defend NATO countries from external attack -- the mutual security guarantee at the heart of the alliance . In Italy , he repudiated positions that G7 nations have long championed , from an open global trading system to action against climate change .
