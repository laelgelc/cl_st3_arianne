<h> Province Gets $45 Million From Ottawa to Help Reduce Emissions <p> June 17 , 2017 4:10 pm <p> Newfoundland and Labrador is getting nearly $45 million from the federal government to help fund projects to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> This week the federal government announced a $1.4 billion Low Carbon Economy Leadership Fund to help provinces and territories reach their emission reduction targets as part of the Pan-Canadian Framework on Clean Growth and Climate Change . <p> The money will go to projects which help reduce greenhouse gas emissions such as rebates and incentives to retrofit homes , supporting lower carbon fuels , and programs for reforestation and better forestry management . <p> In total , Newfoundland and Labrador is expected to receive $44.6 million from the fund .
