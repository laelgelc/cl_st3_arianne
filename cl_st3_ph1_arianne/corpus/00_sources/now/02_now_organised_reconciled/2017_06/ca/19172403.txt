<h> Springbank asks for neighbourly support <p> As the landowners and residents of Springbank who face the potential loss of our homes , businesses , history and heritage if a proposed Springbank dam is built , we share the view that Calgary ( we would add neighbouring upstream communities like Bragg Creek , Redwood Meadows and Tsuut'ina ) needs effective long-term flood mitigation . <p> But the reason no dam has been built yet is because Springbank is deeply flawed public policy . It relies on the expropriation of vast areas of private property . It does not prevent upstream flooding . The cost of land acquisition will render it vastly more expensive than other options . The best of those other options is a dam at McLean Creek . Equally effective for Calgary and doable , it would protect upstream communities , and it is built on Crown land , with no private land acquisition required .
