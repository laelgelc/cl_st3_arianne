<h> Opinion Letters <h> Opinions are stupid <p> Re : " Ignore the experts , " June 2 , Letters to the Editor -- Craig Mostat , The News : <p> If Mr. Craig Mostat was being " overtly sarcastic " about me , then I think the Sherwood Park News has wasted a lot of column inches publishing his letters ! Sarcasm has its place , but I was trying seriously to correct his misapprehensions about an important issue of our time . <p> Let me be completely clear : It is Mr. Mostat 's right to hold contrary opinions about whether climate change is real , and to express them publicly . But equally , it is my right to say Mr. Mostat 's opinions are just plain stupid . In his attempt to discredit me , he could have raised any number of common denialist objections , but for some reason , he focused on my assertion that humans have caused atmospheric CO2 concentrations to increase by close to 50 per cent since 1850 ; he said this is a lie ! I was completely baffled by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fact of the entire debate . The near-perfect correlation with trends in fossil fuel burning and global deforestation has been established over decades . Changes in atmospheric carbon isotope ratios are consistent with an increasing fraction coming from fossil sources . This is a case where the " common sense " explanation is almost universally accepted , because no one can offer a better one . <p> But then , comprehension dawned : Mr. Mostat explained ( with neither facts nor logic ) that it 's sheer arrogance ( on the part of people like me ) to suggest humans could perturb nature , and actually change global climate . Then he described Earth as a " highly complex planet like no other that we know about in our universe , uniquely designed for our existence . " Mr. Mostat sounds suspiciously like a creationist . This would explain his deep contempt for education , science , and expert knowledge which contradict his own beliefs . It might also explain his paranoid delusions that he is like Galileo , with " climate change zealots " clamoring to imprison him for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was persecuted by the Church for discovering " inconvenient truths " which threatened to disrupt its hegemony . A better analogy is how the global scientific community has been treated by the fossil fuel industry , and its political cronies as observations of climate change and the understanding of its impacts , today and in the future , have become increasingly alarming . People are being affected , both economically and psychologically , and the impacts are getting closer to home . Last July , Global News reported that six of Canada 's 10 biggest insurance payouts for climate-related disasters were for events which occurred in Alberta since 2000 ( two wildfires and four intense storms ) . Is this coincidence , Mr. Mostat ? Or could it be divine retribution ? Anyone of reasonable intelligence and education can see the changes , and understand there could be a more rational explanation ; at least some of us are prepared to take it seriously .
