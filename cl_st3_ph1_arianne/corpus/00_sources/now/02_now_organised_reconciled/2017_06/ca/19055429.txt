<h> Related Stories <p> A lengthy conversation about art and climate change occupied my Thursday afternoon in an artist 's home studio in uptown Waterloo . <p> " I am trying to give people an experience in my art of having to wait , wondering if something is going to happen or not . Trying to figure out if something happens , what will it be , " Paul Roorda said . <p> Standing inside the small studio nested on the second floor of his home , he told me how he explores the idea of time and its ties with climate change and how we perceive it though his sculptures . <p> He likened it to the process of finding evidence for climate change and the waiting game involved . <p> This interaction with Roorda was a week in the making . <p> And it began with me finding a box . <p> Bottom half of the first box I spotted in the neighbourhood , on a foot path nearby Menno Street and Dawson Street .
