Here 's what I am talking about : <p> A few years ago , the Natural Resources Defense Council -- an international environmental advocacy group -- published an illuminating study saying that bottled water was not any better than the tap variety . In fact , more than 25 percent of water-in-plastic is labeled , " from a municipal source " -- really just extra-treated tap water . <p> It gets better : While tap water is severely regulated for quality by Health Canada , bottled water is classified as a food , and therefore goes under the comparatively less stringent Food and Drug Act . In reality , the bottling industry is expected to only voluntarily self-police itself against anything beyond " harmful substances . ( True story . ) <p> Now for the math : Bottled water costs approximately 16 cents a litre in bulk at grocery chains . Tap water costs about a tenth of a cent per litre . The water industry is worth over $170 billion . So when a corporation sells you water from a public source , imagine the mark-up on what you thought was better than what you already had in the kitchen . On average , in North America , bottled water now outsells milk and beer combined . Experts predict that water will eventually replace oil as the new black . <p> Good so far ?
