<p> A 6,000-hectare swath of forest and wetland with historical significance in western Quebec will be conserved forever thanks to the Nature Conservancy of Canada and federal and private funding . <p> The non-profit Nature Conservancy of Canada made the announcement of the protection of the Kenauk property , near Montebello , at a news conference on Monday . The day is designated World Environment Day . <p> The land , which once belonged to Patriote leader Louis-Joseph Papineau , who led the 1837-38 rebellions against British rule in Lower Canada , is about halfway between Montreal and Ottawa . <p> The protected land is along a 20-kilometre corridor that 's three kilometres wide and is home to more than 170 species . The area contains rivers and lakes , so the land is n't contiguous . <p> The land contains the largest known population of black maple trees in Quebec . Black maple forests are considered to be at risk under the province 's Threatened or Vulnerable Species Act . <p> It is also the habitat of the Eastern grey wolf , a species that is considered to be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and the American black bear . <p> The Kenauk property -- the name of which comes from mukekenauk , Algonquin for " turtle " -- was granted as a seigniorial domain in 1674 by Louis XIV , King of France , to Monseigneur Laval , the first bishop of Quebec . The Papineau family owned it for a century starting in 1801 . <p> The Nature Conservancy of Canada began buying up the land for conservation in 2013 . <p> The budget for the project was $9 million , including the land purchase price and stewardship costs . <p> The Canadian government contributed $3.3 million through the Natural Areas Conservation Program of Environment and Climate Change Canada . The other two-thirds of the funding came from the Nature Conservancy of Canada and private donors , including American Friends of the Nature Conservancy of Canada , the TD Bank Group through its TD Forests program , the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service , the Echo Foundation and about 50 individual donors . <p> The Nature Conservancy of Canada has set up an endowment fund of more than $1 million to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Joel Bonin , vice-president for the Quebec region for the Nature Conservancy of Canada , said . <p> With global warming , most species are expected to move farther north , making the area to be conserved even more important , he said . <p> " It 's a very sizeable transaction and it 's very important for us , " Bonin said . <p> The Nature Conservancy of Canada is a charitable organization that has 1.1 million hectares of conserved land in Canada .
