<h> Government of Canada to provide $1,606,643 for Canada 150 activities in B.C. <p> Canada 's 150th is a big deal , and the Government of Canada is sparing no expense to help Canadians explore our country 's diverse culture and heritage . <p> The Department of Canadian Heritage announced at a press conference this morning that the Government of Canada will be providing $1,606,643 through the Canada 150 Fund to support 23 new community projects in the province . <p> " Our government is supporting projects that celebrate diversity and promote inclusion , that strengthen our connection with the natural environment , that focus on reconciliation with indigenous peoples , and projects that empower our young people , " said Jonathan Wilkinson , Parliamentary Secretary to the Minister of Environment and Climate Change and Member of Parliament ( North Vancouver ) , on behalf of the Honourable M ? lanie Joly , Minister of Canadian Heritage . <p> Some of the initiatives that will receive funding include Asian-Canadian Special Events Association 's " We Are Canadians , Too !
