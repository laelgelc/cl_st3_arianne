Environmental regulations enacted under President Barack Obama have n't helped any but they 've played a much smaller role . <p> Most of those regulations have n't even taken effect . <p> In March , Trump ordered a review of the Clean Power Plan , which seeks to reduce emissions from coal power plants , and the lifting of a moratorium on the sale of coal mining leases on federal lands . <p> So far , those moves have spurred a couple of relatively minor coal leases @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at the state of the U.S. coal industry : <p> AN INDUSTRY IN DECLINE <p> Experts say coal 's biggest problem is n't climate change regulations but cheap and abundant natural gas . Gas prices dropped as advances in drilling such as hydraulic fracturing , or fracking , greatly increased the amount of gas on the market . For many utilities , that 's made gas more attractive than coal . <p> Meanwhile , companies have gotten more efficient at extracting coal , meaning fewer workers are needed . Mountaintop removal mining , in which hilltops in Appalachia are blasted off with explosives to expose coal seams , is less expensive and more automated than underground mining . So are the massive strip mines developed since the 1970s in Wyoming and Montana . <p> U.S. coal production fell to 739 million tons last year , the lowest in almost four decades . In fact , the coal industry nationwide accounted for a total of 51,000 jobs through May , up only about 400 jobs from the month before . <p> Over the last decade , coal 's share of the U.S. power market dwindled from more than 50 per cent to about 32 per cent last year . Gas and renewables both made gains , and hundreds of coal-burning power plants retired or are scheduled to shut down soon . <p> Utilities " are not going to flip on a dime and say now it 's time to start building a whole bunch of coal plants because there 's a Trump administration , " said Brian Murray , director of environmental economics at Duke University 's Nicholas Institute . <p> None of that appears to have shaken the administration 's backing of coal .
