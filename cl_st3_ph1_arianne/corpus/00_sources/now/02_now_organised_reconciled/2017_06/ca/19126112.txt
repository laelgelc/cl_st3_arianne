<h> Letters and comments , June 23 <h> Solutions for Churchill <p> Canada needs a reliable , all-weather rail and road link to Churchill . Churchill is an important economic and military link to the Canadian North and foreign ports . The rail link was begun in 1883 and worked on in fits and starts until completion in 1929 . <p> For decades , the line was operated by the Canadian National Railway , but in 1997 the federal government allowed CNR to sell the rail line linking southern Manitoba to Churchill and her port to the American rail operator Omnitrax . <p> Considering the strategic location of Churchill as a deep-sea port and military supply link , the sale to a private American company is incomprehensible . Now the rail line is closed due to spring flooding . Churchill sits abandoned . There is no Plan B. This incredible gap in our national infrastructure and security is receiving little attention from the federal government . <p> The federal government is solely responsible for marine , rail and road links to international shipping .
