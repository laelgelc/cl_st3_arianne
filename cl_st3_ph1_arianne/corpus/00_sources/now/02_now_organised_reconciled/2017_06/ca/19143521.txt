<p> Cenovus added to its list of uncertainties this week with the surprise resignation of chief executive Brian Ferguson , on Tuesday , and a further downgrade of its debt -- to BBB status -- on Friday by the rating agency DBRS . <p> None of this is good news for a company that 's been pummeled in the market since its $17.7-billion acquisition of ConocoPhillips ' oilsands and natural gas assets earlier this year . <p> Reaction to the deal from investors was nothing short of visceral . In fact , when major shareholders were informed of the transaction the night before , they suggested Cenovus call it off . <p> Those investors had bought shares in a company that was a pure-play on the oilsands -- and increasingly , one that was technology-focused through its devotion of significant resources to reducing emissions and water use while increasing productivity . <p> They were focused on realizing the upside on the horizon , thanks to work done on the well pads and well pairs that would bring operating costs down to $5 per barrel .
