<h> China begins first of eight large-scale carbon capture projects <p> Beijing - China has definitely taken the lead globally in its effort to mitigate the effects of climate change , just recently beginning construction of the first of eight large-scale carbon capture and storage projects . <p> Just two weeks ago , Digital Journal featured the world 's first commercial carbon capture and storage ( CCS ) plant opened in Zurich , Switzerland with the goal of selling compressed CO2 gas to industry , basically re-purposing the gas . <p> China 's first CCS project was started by state-owned Yanchang Petroleum , reports AFR Weekend . The facility will capture CO2 emissions from a coal-to-gas petrochemical plant near the western Chinese city of Xi'an .
