This was previously allowed before the wording was changed in 2011 . <p> The changes made six years ago eliminated the ability for a municipality to delegate authority to declare a state of local emergency to an individual or committee . The amendment required either a council vote , vote of a regional commission or joint body of two or more local authorities to declare a state of local emergency .
