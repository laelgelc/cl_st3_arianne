<h> News Local <h> Residents challenged to use alternative transportation <p> With melting polar ice caps , rising water levels , extreme weather events , and warmer seasons , the importance of combating climate change has never been more critical than it is now . <p> And while the world waits for nations to agree and implement plans to protect the Earth , local citizens can take action immediately on a micro level by participating in Environment Week , June 4 to 10 . <p> " We 're trying to get people to leave their cars at home for at least one day next week -- so ride their bike , walk , take the bus , carpool , " said Michelle Gairdner , environmental stewardship manager with the city . <p> " The city is actively promoting the use of transit in order to show it as a viable alternative . " <p> Those interested in the commuter challenge need to register online at https : //commuterchallenge.ca . Participants have the opportunity to win one of seven $100 gift cards to Ernie 's Sports or Fourword , while those who are registering as a workplace are eligible for a $500 cash prize . <p> Individuals must sign-in and log their participation to be eligible for the prizes , while workplaces must register and create an account and will receive a unique URL . <p> The city reported an 8% increase in the number of people who participated in 2016 over 2015 . <p> " We 're hoping the prizes will entice people to participate , " said Gairdner , noting there are also physical and wellness benefits of using alternative modes of transportation . <p> In 2013 , Canada emitted 13.53 metric tonnes per capita of carbon dioxide compared to 16.39 metric tonnes in the United States and 3.95 metric tonnes in Mexico , according to a 2013 report by the Carbon Dioxide Information Analysis Center in the U.S.
