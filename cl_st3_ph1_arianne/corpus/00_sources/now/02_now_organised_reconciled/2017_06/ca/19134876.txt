<h> News Local <h> Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change responds to protest <p> Members of Water Wells First held an information picket near where an industrial wind turbine will be erected on Darrell Line north of Chatham , Ont. on Thursday June 22 , 2017 . ( Ellwood Shreve/Chatham Daily News ) <p> Concerns about groundwater and the health of residents are always taken seriously , says the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change . <p> The ministry was responding to a peaceful protest against the North Kent Wind project , which took place Thursday by members of Water Wells First . <p> For the past year , the citizen group has sounded the alarm about the wind development -- co-owned by Samsung Renewable Energy and Pattern Energy . <p> The main concern the group has is that the vibration from piles being driven into the bedrock to anchor the turbines , and from the ongoing operation of the devices , will cause dangerous heavy metals in the shale , such as arsenic and uranium , to contaminate the shallow aquifer that supplies well water for residents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Friday , ministry spokesperson Gary Wheeler said a " cautious , science-based approach " is used when setting standards for renewable energy projects . <p> " In response to concerns raised by area residents , the ministry imposed a number of conditions in the Renewable Energy Approval , " he said . " The company was required to conduct ground-borne vibration monitoring during construction and operation of the wind turbines . The company undertook a test pile driving vibration program before construction of production wind turbines could proceed . " <p> Wheeler added that vibration data collected from the test program helped the ministry make the decision to allow the company to proceed to the construction phase of the project . <p> " The company will continue monitoring for vibrations throughout the duration of the construction phase , as outlined in an approved vibration monitoring plan , " he said . <p> Ministry staff are scheduled to meet with residents next week to provide additional details concerning the project .
