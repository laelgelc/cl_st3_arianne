<h> Why HK financial hub story has a long way to go <p> The 20th anniversary of the handover of Hong Kong is a time for the media to look back -- and especially to make comparisons between life in 1997 and 2017 . <p> There is a pattern to much of the commentary . On the whole , if things have not changed much , it is usually considered a good thing . For example , the Basic Law has successfully protected our way of life . Where things have changed , it seems to be in a negative way . For example , our society has grown more divided . <p> But in some cases , major change has undoubtedly been a good thing . <p> If we look back at the Hong Kong stock market in 1997 , we see a much smaller platform . The Hang Seng Index constituents ' market capitalization totaled US$313 billion at the end of 1997 .
