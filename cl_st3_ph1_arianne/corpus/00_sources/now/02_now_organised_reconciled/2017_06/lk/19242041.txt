<h> The Sunday Times Business Club <p> The Sunday Times Business Club recently discussed issues surrounding climate change and its impact on the country 's environment , infrastructure and economy . The three panelists include veteran architect Dr. Surath Wickremasinghe , President of the Chamber of Construction Industry ; Suranga Kahandawa , Disaster Risk Management Specialist at the World Bank Colombo office and Dr. Eric Wikramanayake , conservation biologist with over 25 years of experience in several countries in Asia , and current Chair of the Environmental Foundation ( Guarantee ) , Ltd.
