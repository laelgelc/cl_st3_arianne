<h> India , China show Trump how to do climate <p> Donald Trump 's decision to pull America out of the Paris Climate Accord should come as no surprise considering he termed climate change a ' hoax ' during his presidential campaign . The Paris Accord was the pinnacle of Barack Obama 's environmental policy and was a significant step in the right direction to counter the challeng . The basic premise of the Accord rests on each country adopting carbon emission levels which would limit human-led global warming to 2 degrees Celsius . The Accord also stipulates that America would provide US$3 billion in aid to developing countries to pursue environmentally friendly policies . <p> America 's decision to retract from promises enshrined in the Paris Accord has significant reverberations for developing countries , and has opened the door for China and India to adopt a more responsible approach towards climate change . Although historically against restricting their carbon emissions , both China and India have shown maturity in the wake of America 's decision to withdraw .
