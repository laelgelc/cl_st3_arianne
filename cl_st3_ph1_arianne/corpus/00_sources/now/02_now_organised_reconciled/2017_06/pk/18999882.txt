Island , located about 39 kilometres east of Pasni , Balochistan , in the Arabian Sea , has been declared the first dedicated Marine Protected Area ( MPA ) in the country . <p> Covering an area of about 400 square kilometers , Astola is considered the largest island along the coast of Pakistan , and the Balochistan government through a notification issued on Thursday declared it the first dedicated marine protected area of the country . <p> The Convention on Biological Diversity ( CBD ) , to which Pakistan is a signatory , requires nations under Article 2 to designate , regulate and manage geographically defined areas ( marine protected areas ) to achieve the specific conservation objectives . <p> By declaring the Astola Island a marine protected area , Pakistan has started complying with the Aichi Target 11 , which requires that by 2020 at least 17 percent of terrestrial and inland water areas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Many organisations , including the WWF-Pakistan , step would help in protecting and conserving species , habitats and biodiversity that were under threat due to human activities . <p> Federal Secretary Climate Change Syed Abu Ahmad Akif appreciated Balochistan Chief Secretary Aurangzeb Haque and Forests and Wildlife Secretary Ali Imran for the efforts in getting the island and area around it declared a marine protected area , and said several other officials and people also played their role in this regard . <p> WWF-Pakistan 's technical adviser ( Marine Fisheries ) Muhammad Moazzam Khan said Astola Island was a biodiversity hotspot as many species of marine and terrestrial animals and plants were known to be found there . <p> The subspecies , Russel 's viper ( Echis carinatus astolae ) , is an endemic species and a highly poisonous The Arabian humpback whale , which is considered to be one of the rarest marine mammals , is also occasionally reported from the surrounding area . <p> The WWF-Pakistan official maintained that Astola Island 's new status would ensure that the biodiversity of the area was conserved , the use of deleterious fishing methods was banned and recreational activities were controlled . <p> It would also ensure that protected , threatened and endangered species such as crustaceans , coral , mammals , sharks , turtles , whales , and mobulids were conserved .
