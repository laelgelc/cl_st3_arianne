<h> Govt to collaborate with UN on Climate Change issues <p> ISLAMABAD : Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid has said that the government will continue to collaborate with the United Nations Development Program on Climate Change issues . <p> He was talking to United Nations Resident Coordinator and United Nations Development Program representative in Pakistan Neil Buhne here on Tuesday . The minister informed that the government will soon declare its Marine Protected Areas . <p> He said in this regard United Nations can guide the Ministry of Climate Change on best practices in the world . Neil Buhne appreciated Zahid Hamid for his efforts on Climate Change Act .
