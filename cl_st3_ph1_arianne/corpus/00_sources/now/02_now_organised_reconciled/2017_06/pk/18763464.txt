From the very beginning , the opposition went all-out to disrupt the president , with some lawmakers whistling , others sloganeering , and still more thumping their desks as shouts of ' Go Nawaz Go ' and ' traitor ' rang out in the Parliament House . It was only after walkout by the entire opposition that calm returned to the House . <p> The opposition parties are annoyed over government 's refusal to allow live coverage for their speeches on state-run TV .
