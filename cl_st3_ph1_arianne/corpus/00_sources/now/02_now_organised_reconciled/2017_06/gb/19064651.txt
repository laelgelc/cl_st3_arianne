<p> Tulane University establishes energy law chair with appointment of European expert <p> 20 June 2017 at 06:01 BST <h> Tulane University establishes energy law chair with appointment of European expert <p> Kim Talus , a European scholar of energy law , has been named the inaugural holder of the James McCulloch Chair in Energy Law at Tulane University Law School . <p> welcomia <p> The new chair was launched with a $2 million-endowment gift from energy industry veteran Jim McCulloch and his wife , Susan . Mr McCulloch is the executive vice president and general counsel for Forum Energy Technologies in Houston .
