<p> According to his Twitter feed , President Trump will today announce his decision on whether the US will remain a party to the Paris Agreement on climate change . He is widely expected to announce America 's withdrawal . This will be a major setback , particularly for the US . <p> For Paris itself , it is a heavy blow . The agreement is less than two years old and has yet to be implemented , yet it will have lost the world 's second largest emitter . That said , Paris was designed to withstand upheavals of this sort . It has a flexible structure based on voluntary pledges to reduce emissions . Governments set these individually based on national circumstances , rather than through quid pro quo negotiations as happens during trade rounds at the WTO .
