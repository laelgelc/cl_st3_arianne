<p> On 26 April 2016 , the same month the EU-Turkey deal trapped 60,000 refugees in Greece , migrant solidarity activists broke the locks on City Plaza , a shuttered hotel in Athens ' anarchist Exarchia neighborhood , and gave 400 stranded people a home . Over the next year , City Plaza grew into the best known of over a dozen squats that house refugees in Greece 's crisis ravaged capital . It has been covered by Time , Al Jazeera and the New York Times.Volunteers pass through from all over Europe . <p> City Plaza boasts a clinic , a delicious cafeteria , language classes , a caf ? .
