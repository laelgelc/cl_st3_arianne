<h> Norman Lamb ( Betfair 4/1 , Ladbrokes 2/1 ) <p> Lamb has built cross-party respect for his work on mental health , both during the coalition and afterwards . While working with the Conservatives , Lamb was also one of the driving forces behind plans for a cap on the cost of social care , later abandoned by David Cameron . <p> A former solicitor , Lamb has been an MP since 2001 , but lost out in the last leadership contest , which saw more than 30,000 votes cast . <p> Having been a senior figure under Farron , Lamb would need to present a clearly distinct agenda from his predecessor . <h> Ed Davey ( Betfair 11/1 , Ladbrokes 8/1 ) <p> Another former minister returning to parliament , Davey also recovered his south west London seat last week after being booted out in 2015 . <p> Davey also has a track record as an outspoken campaigner on climate change issues and served as energy secretary in the coalition . <p> But could he be held back by his association with the decision to hand EDF the contract to build the Hinkley Point nuclear reactors ?
