<h> The amazing journey of the eels <p> Every year thousands of eels travel from the Sargasso Sea to Europe and back . Due to climate change and human activities though , the European Eel is now a critically endangered species . So on the river Brent in West London there are a group of ' citizen scientists ' taking part in an eel conservation programme run by the Zoological Society of London . We went down to meet eel enthusiast Terry Jones and Joe Pecorelli , the biologist leading the project . ( Photo credit : BBC. )
