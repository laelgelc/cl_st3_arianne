" <p> Ken Achenbach founded Camp of Champions in 1989 , and has organised freestyle coaching sessions for skiers and snowboarders every summer at the Canadian resort . In a recent Facebook post , Achenbach said that conditions on Whistler 's Horstman glacier had made it impossible to build a terrain park to an acceptable standard . As a result , the 2017 camp has been cancelled and all bookings are being refunded .
