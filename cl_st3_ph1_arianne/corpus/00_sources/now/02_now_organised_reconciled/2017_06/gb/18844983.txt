<p> Long after we each cease to exist , the physical outcomes of the choices we make today , and tomorrow , will linger . Shadows of our decisions on policy , energy and lifestyle will manifest as the consequence of our injection of greenhouse gases into the Earth 's atmosphere . <p> The forecasted outcomes of unchecked climate change are serious and long-lasting . Climate science regularly emits dire warnings and precipitous graphics illustrating dangerous changes to the oceans and atmosphere in the coming decades . They also fail , profoundly , to inspire widespread preventative action , partly because the future is distant and dim . <p> The fact that long-term warnings bounce off audiences is a zone of friction for science communication .
