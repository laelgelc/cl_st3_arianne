<h> Trump pulls the US out of the Paris Climate Agreement <p> The President of the United States of America , Donald Trump has announced that the US will pull out of the Paris Climate Agreement signed in 2015 under former President Barack Obama . <p> This move is the latest of a long list of controversial decisions that have defined the Trump administration . <p> The Paris agreement on climate is a consensus by various nations to reduce carbon emissions and reduce global warming . This agreement was reached under the auspices of United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) in December 2015 . <p> In a statement delivered at the Rose Gardens at the White House on Thursday , June 1 , 2017 , President Trump described the Paris agreement as one which " disadvantages the United States to the exclusive benefits of other countries .
