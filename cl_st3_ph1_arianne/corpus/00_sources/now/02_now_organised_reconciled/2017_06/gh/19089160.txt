<h> Phoenix flights cancelled because it 's too hot for planes <h> 19h ago Source : BBC <p> As temperatures climb in Phoenix , Arizona , more than 40 flights have been cancelled - because it is too hot for the planes to fly . <p> The weather forecast for the US city suggests temperatures could reach 120F ( 49C ) on Tuesday . <p> That is higher than the operating temperature of some planes . <p> American Airlines announced it was cancelling dozens of flights scheduled to take off from Sky Harbor airport during the hottest part of the day . <p> The local Fox News affiliate in Phoenix said the cancellations mostly affected regional flights on the smaller Bombardier CRJ airliners , which have a maximum operating temperature of about 118F ( 48C ) . <p> The all-time record for temperatures in Phoenix is just slightly higher , at 122F , which hit on 26 June 1990 . <p> The cancelled flights were scheduled to take off between 15:00 and 18:00 local time . <h> Why ca n't planes fly ?
