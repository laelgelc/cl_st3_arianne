<h> German Chancellor Angela Merkel on Friday pledged " more decisive action than ever " to protect the climate after the US pullout from the landmark Paris accord . <p> " We in Germany , in Europe and the world will band together to take more decisive action than ever to confront and successfully surmount major challenges to humanity such as climate change , " she told reporters . <p> In a strongly worded statement , Merkel said US President Donald Trump 's announcement to turn his back on the Paris pact was " highly regrettable , to put it very mildly " . <p> " This decision can not and will not stop those of us who feel obligated to protect our Earth , " she said . <p> Calling the Paris pact a " historic quantum leap , " she said that Germany would live up to its " obligations under this agreement , particularly on financing of climate assistance for the poorest and most vulnerable countries in the world " . <p> She said it would be a " rocky road " toward full implementation of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hailed private initiatives around the world on climate protection , including in the United States , saying they would help ensure " more prosperity and opportunities for the world " . <p> She vowed to make a success of the next round of UN climate talks in the western Germany city of Bonn in November . <p> " I say to all those for whom the future of our planet is important : let us continue on this path together so that we can succeed for Mother Earth , " she said .
