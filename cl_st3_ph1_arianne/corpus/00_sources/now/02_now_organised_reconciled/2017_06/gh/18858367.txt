<h> Namibia 's livestock farmers to benefit from new grant <p> Drought-hit livestock farmers in Namibia are expected to benefited from a coming European financial grant program , which will run over a period of six years worth 20 million euros . <p> EU Press and Information Officer Susan Lewis on Tuesday said the Namibian government and the EU will launch the program called " Support to the livestock sector in the Northern Communal Areas of Namibia " on Friday . <p> Lewis said the program will focus on three intervention areas , with the first zoning in on the improvement of livestock production systems and resilience to climate change . <p> " This is to be achieved through the promotion of livestock oriented agricultural extension , technical and animal health support services , " she said . <p> Another intervention aims to increase access to domestic , regional and international markets , which is expected to be achieved through the promotion of local capacity for animal slaughtering and meat processing , food safety awareness campaign and food hygiene inspection services . <p> Furthermore , she said the full implementation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a focus of the grant . <p> According to FAO Namibia , the communal areas occupy about 48 percent of the total farming area of Namibia and hold approximately 62 percent of the total cattle population , 72 percent of the goats and 17 percent of the sheep , but due to the recurring droughts many communal farmers have lost their livestock .
