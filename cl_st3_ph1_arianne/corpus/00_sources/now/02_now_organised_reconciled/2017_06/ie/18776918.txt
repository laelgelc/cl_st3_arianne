<p> Yet as a climate scientist and a climate policy scholar , I know market forces and current policies are far from adequate to limit the rise in global temperatures , as envisioned in the Paris Agreement . <p> And so the Trump administration 's decision to withdraw from the Paris Agreement could have a range of consequences for the United States and for humanity . But how broad will these impacts be ? <p> Part of the uncertainty stems from how the climate system will respond to humanity 's greenhouse gas emissions . If we are lucky , the climate will be less sensitive than scientists think is most likely ; if we are unlucky , it will be more sensitive .
