Photograph : Nick Bradshaw <p> The cost of holding the Citizens ' Assembly has run over the Department of the Taoiseach 's initial estimated cost of ? 600,000 . Up to May this year , ? 651,144 had been spent running the assembly after five of the group 's nine meetings had taken place . <p> The group was set up to consider the future of the Eighth Amendment in Ireland 's Constitution , which prohibits abortion -- as well as other issues such as how Ireland responds to its ageing population , climate change , and the manner in which referendums are held in Ireland . <p> The assembly of 99 randomly selected citizens and one chairwoman , retired Supreme Court judge Ms Justice Mary Laffoy , were to consider the topics in turn over nine weekends . <p> Members of the Citizens ' Assembly can claim @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the first five weekends of the project , ? 74,460 was claimed in expenses . <p> On average , the expenses claims would work out at more than ? 100 per weekend for each attendee . The cost of overnight rooms for the participants in the north Dublin hotel , as well as providing catering , was ? 266,551 between last November and May this year .
