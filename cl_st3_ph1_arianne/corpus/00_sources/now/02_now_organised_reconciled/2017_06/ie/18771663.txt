<p> ESPO Secretary General , Isabelle Ryckbost , ESPO Chairman , Eamonn O'Reilly , and Sotiris Raptis , EcoPorts coordinator , handed over the PERS certificates to the ports ' representatives during the ESPO Conference dinner , which took place yesterday evening at Casa Llotja de Mar in Barcelona , Spain . <p> " The number of ports under the EcoPorts network certified with the PERS standard has recently risen to 28 members . This marks the anniversary of 20 years of EcoPorts and further strengthens the network whose primary objective is to assist more European ports to protect the environment , improve public health and address the challenges of climate change " said Sotiris Raptis , EcoPorts coordinator and senior advisor for environment at ESPO . <p> PERS is the only port sector specific environmental management standard .
