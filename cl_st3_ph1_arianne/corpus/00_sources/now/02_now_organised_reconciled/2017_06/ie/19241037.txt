Put simply , aid works . <p> It can be easy to look at the horrendous imagery pouring into our homes from the devastating famine in South Sudan , or the emerging crises in Yemen , Ethiopia and Nigeria , and to assume that nothing has changed since Live Aid in the 1980s -- that all of the money spent on aid in the intervening 30 years has had no real impact on poverty and hunger . The Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) were signed in 2015 -- following international negotiations co-chaired by Ireland . They represent a transformative agenda -- one that commits us to eradicating extreme poverty , ending hunger and tackling climate change . By working together , we can make that a reality by 2030 . <p> Irish people know this . And that 's why they continue -- year after year -- to agree that we should increase our spending on overseas aid .
