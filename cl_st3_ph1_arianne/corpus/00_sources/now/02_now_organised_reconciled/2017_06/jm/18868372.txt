<p> THE ENVIRONMENTAL Foundation of Jamaica ( EFJ ) has awarded grants valued at close to J$49 million to 11 local entities among them community-based organisations such as the Rocky Point Development Council Benevolent Society to help them confront the challenges of climate change . <p> The grants were made possible by the Ministry of Economic Growth and Job Creation and the Inter-American Development Bank , under the Special Climate Change Adaptation Fund ( SCCAF ) . <p> The SCCAF is supported by the Adaptation Programme and Financing Mechanisms project of the Pilot Programme for Climate Resilience and administered by the EFJ .
