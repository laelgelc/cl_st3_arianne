<h> CARICOM dismayed by US decision on climate change pact <p> Chairman of the conference of the Caribbean Community ( CARICOM ) heads of government and Guyana 's President , David Granger , said the regional body is deeply dismayed and disappointed by the decision of the United States to withdraw from the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> The Paris Agreement was more than just another international agreement on another complex international issue , he said , adding that it reflects the acknowledgement and acceptance that climate change is a global issue requiring global solutions . <p> " For us , climate change is an existential threat . As members of a community of small-island and low-lying coastal developing states , the reality of climate change is making itself manifest to us , especially given that our region is the most prone to natural disasters globally , " Granger said in a statement released yesterday . <p> He said the need for global action to combat this phenomenon is past urgent . " As one of the prime contributors to the emissions which continue to push global temperature @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ United States in efforts to curb the warming of the planet is an important factor , " said the Guyanese president . <h> NEGATIVE SIGNAL <p> " Abdication of that responsibility sends a negative signal , particularly to those of us most vulnerable to the effects of climate change , despite the fact that we do little or nothing to contribute to its causes , " he added . <p> He said , however , that CARICOM is encouraged by the fact that the overwhelming majority of nations remains firmly committed to the Paris Agreement . " Our own commitment will never waiver . Our existence is at stake , " Granger said .
