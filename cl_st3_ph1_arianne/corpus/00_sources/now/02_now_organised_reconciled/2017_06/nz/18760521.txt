<h> NZ must step up as US steps down - Greens <p> New Zealand must join with other countries and step up as a climate leader in the wake of the US decision to withdraw from the Paris Climate Agreement , the Green Party said today . <p> This morning President Donald Trump announced the United States would withdraw from the Paris Climate Agreement , joining just two other countries - Nicaragua and Syria - who have rejected the global accord . <p> " The US might be abdicating its moral leadership on climate change , but New Zealand should join with world leaders today and promise to put the environment and future generations first , " said Green Party Co-leader James Shaw . <p> " As long as the rest of the world stands united I am confident that global action on climate change will continue over the next four years . <p> " New Zealand should join with China , the European Union and other countries promising to accelerate efforts to reduce climate pollution . <p> " National should not use the US decision as an excuse to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to justify plans to open new coal mines and expand risky oil and gas exploration in New Zealand waters . <p> " There is simply too much at stake in New Zealand for us to sit on our hands and do nothing . Climate change will see our farmers facing longer , more severe droughts and all of us experiencing more frequent intense storms and flooding . <p> " Starting the transition away from pollution-intensive industries is also in our best interest . Investing in modern public transport , clean energy and sustainable farming is good for jobs , our health , and our environment . <p> " We should be taking action to create a cleaner economy and healthier environment irrespective of what happens in the US , " said Mr Shaw .
