<h> Thousands welcome the return of Hokule'a in Hawaii <p> Thousands of people crowded into Ala Moana Beach Park on Saturday to watch the Hokule'a , a traditional Hawaiian canoe , make its return to Oahu after a three-year voyage that included visits to 150 ports in 27 countries . <p> After more than 40,000 nautical miles at sea , the Hokule'a was met just outside of the shores of Hawaii by a fleet of canoes from across the Pacific who led the Hokule'a to shore where the crew were greeted with flower leis ( necklaces ) and traditional Hawaiian chants . <p> Hokule'a returns to the shores of Hawaii after a 3 year voyage .
