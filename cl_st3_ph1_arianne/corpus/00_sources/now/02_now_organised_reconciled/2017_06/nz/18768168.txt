<h> Leaving Paris Agreement will backfire on US - political scientist <p> Donald Trump 's ditching of the Paris climate accord has been called a backfired shot . <p> The United States ' president cited American sovereignty and jobs for workers as the reason for backing out of the climate agreement . <p> Victoria University political scientist Dr Adrian Macey told Rachel Smalley it 's a concession of the country 's political power around climate change . <p> " Really the effects of this are going to be worse on the United States itself than on the world . You are seeing world leaders standing up , and the leadership vacuum . " <p> Dr Macey told Rachel Smalley the plan is bigger and more long-term than the current US president . <p> " If you look at reactions around the world , this is being treated as a temporary apparition because the Paris Agreement itself really only starts to take affect after 2020 , well through the first of the Trump administration . "
