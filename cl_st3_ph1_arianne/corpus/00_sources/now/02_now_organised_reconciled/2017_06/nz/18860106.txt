<h> Dance Review : Fire in the Water , Fire in the Sky <p> Miria George is a young choreographer/poet whose future development promises works of relevance and serious intent . <p> Fire in the Water , Fire in the Sky Tawata Productions Pataka Art and Museum , until June 10 <p> This innovative performance piece is part of the annual Kia Mau Festival of Maori and Pasifika Theatre and Dance . <p> It was created and directed by Miria George in Hawai'i earlier this year , as part of her Fulbright Creative New Zealand Pacific Writer in Residency at University of Hawai'i . <p> Only 30 minutes long , this cautionary tale concisely explores the effects of colonisation and climate change in the Pacific , using movement , voice and text . It is simply and beautifully designed by Cara Louise Waretini and Tony De Goldi . <p> The work is performed with dignity and gravitas by Te Hau Winitana , Mapihi Kelland and Manarangi Mua who also devised it with George . The three women engage with their delightful humour and there is a lovely feeling of connectedness @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ audience moves with the performers through various exhibition spaces . The piece cleverly utilises displayed artefacts to subtle yet strong effect . <p> The women move slowly and with grace , which allows the audience time to reflect on what they are seeing and to form their own opinions of it . We watch them planting , singing ( beautifully ) , digging , cleaning , bathing , attending church -- doing everyday things . <p> Part-way through , the mood changes as they unroll strips of material , on which are written various slogans such as " Do n't breed tinned fish " . These are wrapped around the women 's mouths , effectively gagging them . Sickness arrives with these products . There is a lack of water and grain will not grow . Water companies are arriving to drain the land further . Is direct action the only way to prevent this happening ? <p> The closing image is both telling and moving , as one woman remains still trying to till the arid soil , while the other two row away in search of a better life .
