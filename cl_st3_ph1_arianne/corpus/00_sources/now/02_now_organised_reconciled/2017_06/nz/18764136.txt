<h> PM must confront US with impact of climate decision - Greens <p> The Green Party is calling on the Prime Minister to invite Pacific Island ambassadors to meet with the US Secretary of State next week so they can explain first-hand the consequences of the US decision to withdraw from the Climate Agreement . <p> The call comes ahead of US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson 's visit to New Zealand on Tuesday , and following the decision by US President Donald Trump to withdraw the United States from the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> " Bill English has a responsibility to convey to the Trump Administration the consequences of its decision . He should invite Pacific ambassadors to talk about the threat climate change poses to their countries , " said Green Party Co-leader James Shaw . <p> " Bill English will be one of the first leaders to meet with the US Secretary of State following the decision to withdraw .
