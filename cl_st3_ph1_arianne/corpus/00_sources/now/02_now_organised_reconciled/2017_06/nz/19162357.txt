<h> NZ law student takes government to court over climate policy ' failure ' <p> A New Zealand law student is battling the country 's government in court today over an alleged " failure " to properly address climate change , reports Greenpeace . <p> A win could potentially mean the government would be forced to go back to the drawing board and come up with more ambitious climate targets . <p> Along with similar lawsuits , it will set a bar for the type of action that developed countries must take to prevent dangerous climate change . <p> Sarah Thomson , 26 , claims that as a developed country , New Zealand has an obligation under the Paris Agreement to set a target in line with the scientific consensus . <p> " So far the New Zealand government has shirked its responsibilities , set unambitious and irrational targets , and justified it all by saying we 're too small to make a difference , " she says . <p> " Climate change is a global issue affecting us here and now , and we all have a responsibility to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ events , including cyclones , droughts and floods , which are leaving entire communities devastated . <p> -Partners- <p> " I 'm young and I 'm terrified of a time when I might have to look my kids in the eye and explain to them how we let this happen . " <p> Inspired by global litigation Thomson decided to launch the lawsuit after being inspired by global climate change litigation , including the 900 Dutch citizens who filed a case against the Dutch government , and a US case where 21 kids are taking on the Federal government and fossil fuel companies . <p> The litigation is the first of its kind in New Zealand and will be heard over the course of three days , starting from today , in the Wellington High Court . The outcome of the case is not likely to be known for several months . <p> Greenpeace New Zealand has helped organise a public mobilisation in support of Thomson , which is taking place this morning on the steps of the court . <p> The case has the backing of several world-renowned climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ awareness " , former NASA researcher , James Hansen , who is a witness for the case . <p> Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) author Dr James Renwick will also be giving evidence . <p> One of the targets under review is New Zealand 's Paris Agreement commitment to reducing greenhouse gas emissions by 11 percent below 1990 levels by 2030 . The lawsuit will ask the Minister for Climate Change Issues , currently Paula Bennett , to justify the way in which this target was set . <p> In her preliminary statement of defence , Bennett has denied that the New Zealand government needs to set a target that strengthens the global response to climate change to hold warming to " well below " 2C , with an aim of 1.5C , as stipulated by the Paris Agreement . <p> Lawyer Dennis van Berkel , who successfully argued the Dutch climate litigation case in 2015 setting a global precedent , said he would be watching what happens in New Zealand with keen interest . <p> " The Dutch case proves that all governments have a legal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their part to lower emissions , " he said . <p> " Given the notoriously inadequate climate policies of New Zealand , this case may lead to the same conclusion .
