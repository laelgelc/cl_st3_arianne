<p> As the world marked this year 's World No Tobacco Day on Wednesday , the Minister of Health , Isaac Adewole , announced 9 regulations in the Nigeria Tobacco Control Act that would be implemented by the federal government . <p> The National Tobacco Control Act , which was signed into law in 2015 by former President Goodluck Jonathan , is aimed at reducing the use of tobacco in the country . <p> According to Premium Times reports , the theme for this year 's event , ' Tobacco : A Threat to Development , ' also aims at highlighting the link between tobacco use and development as well as show that tobacco control can break the cycle of poverty , contribute to ending hunger , promote sustainable agriculture and economic growth , and combat climate change . <p> Below are the regulations listed by the minister to be implemented : <p> Prohibition of the sale of tobacco products to and by anyone below age 18 . <p> Ban of sale of cigarettes in single sticks ; cigarettes must be sold in packs of 20 sticks only . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pack of 30 grammes . <p> Ban of sale or offer for sale or distribution of tobacco or tobacco products through mail , internet , or other online devices . <p> Prohibition of interference of tobacco industry in public health and related issues . <p> Prohibition of smoking in anywhere on the premises of a child care facility , educational facility , and healthcare facility . Other prohibited for smoking include playgrounds , amusement parks , plazas , public parks , stadia , public transport , restaurants ' bar , and other public gathering spaces . <p> Prosecution of owner or manager of any of the places listed above who permits , encourages or fails to stop smoking in the above listed places . <p> Prohibition of tobacco advertising , promotion and sponsorship of any kind . <p> Compliance with specified standards for content . <p> The Health Minister , a Professor of Obstetrics and Gynaecology , had earlier said that the implementation of the tobacco control Act had been slow because the draft Regulations need the National Assembly approval . <p> The World Health Organization also revealed that tobacco @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ increasing risk factor in non-communicable ( NCDs ) , including cardiovascular disease , cancers and chronic obstructed pulmonary disease .
