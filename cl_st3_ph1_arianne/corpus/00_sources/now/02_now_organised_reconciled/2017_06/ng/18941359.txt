<h> World Environment Day : Vicfold Recyclers Advocates For A Clean Planet <p> What can one do to help sustain the planet ? That 's a question on the lips of Amusa Temitope Victor , the CEO of Vicfold Recyclers a recycling firm in Ilorin , giving voice to environment advocacy by promoting recycling through reward for waste initiative . <p> To commemorate this year 's world environment day , they organized a road walk which kicked off at 8am , from General Post Office Area , Ilorin , along the way participants engaged in litter picking and environment advocacy to passersby and shop owners along the axis . The group eventually converged at Pake LGEA where a tree planting and beautification ceremony was held . <p> This year 's theme is about connecting people to nature , which means encouraging people to get outdoors and appreciate the beauty of the planet in a bid to show people the importance of protecting it for future generations . <p> In his welcome charge , the Kwara state commissioner for environment Otunba Tunde Joseph commended Vicfold Recyclers for the initiative and their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to use this opportunity answer the call to connect with nature and contribute to the environment . <p> Mr Amusa added that trees help fights climate change by absorbing carbon dioxide and providing us with the oxygen we breathe . He also encouraged people to organize litter pickups , not only in their neighborhoods , but in schools and community centers . <p> A cross section of participants at the event include ; media representatives , staff of the Kwara state ministry of environment , students from tertiary institutions and lovers of nature .
