<p> The Pacific Alliance has expressed its " profound disappointment " at the U.S. decision to pull out of the Paris climate change pact and stressed its commitment to the agreement . <p> Created in 2011 , the Pacific Alliance has four members -- Chile , Colombia , Mexico and Peru -- and currently , 52 observer states , while seeking to increase free commerce and trade . <p> Trump announced the withdrawal on Thursday , saying that participating in the pact would undermine the U.S. economy . <p> Following the announcement , China and Europe and other countries have however , signalled their continued commitment to the accord . <p> Russian President , Vladimir Putin on the other hand , said while the United States should have remained in the 2015 deal , he would not judge Trump , and has warned about the accord 's impact on jobs and poverty .
