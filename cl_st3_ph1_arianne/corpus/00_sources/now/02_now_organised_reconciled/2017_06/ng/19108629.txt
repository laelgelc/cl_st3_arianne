<h> The heavy rains drenching Ivory Coast since May have killed at least 15 people in the economic capital Abidjan and injured over two dozen , the government said on Wednesday . <p> West Africa 's rainy season , which lasts three to four months , regularly causes fatalities and damage across Abidjan 's hillside shack communities , with 16 people killed last year and 39 in 2014 . <p> " We are today at 15 dead and 25 hurt in total , " said Communications Minister Bruno Kone . <p> Most of the victims lived in the shack communities which cling to hillsides that become unstable in heavy rain . <p> A previous toll put the number of dead at 11 with several hundred more affected . <p> The heavy rains also led to the collapse of a bridge into the southern city of San-Pedro , cutting it off for several days .
