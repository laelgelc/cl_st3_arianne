<h> FCT residents decry improper handling of refuse by contractors <p> Some residents of the Federal Capital Territory ( FCT ) have criticised waste management contractors for their shoddy way of handling of waste evacuation and disposal across the territory . <p> The residents , who spoke with News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Abuja on Tuesday in separate interviews , insisted that the evacuation of refuse ought to be carried out in a manner that would enhance the cleanliness of the surroundings . <p> Mr Augustine Madu , an estate agent , particularly bemoaned the manner in which the waste management contractors littered the roads with garbage during waste evacuation processes , while blocking the roads with trucks . <p> " The whole idea of evacuating refuse is to keep the environment clean but what some of these contractors do is to handle the waste evacuation in a messy way . <p> " They forgot the whole essence of their service and think only of the money that would
