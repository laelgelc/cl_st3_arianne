<p> An environmentalist , Mr. Nnimmo Bassey , has identified economic reason as being responsible for the massive harm done to the environment in the country and non-implementation of the necessary legal framework to protect it . <p> Bassey , who is the Director of a non-governmental organisation ( NGO ) , Health of Mother Earth Foundation ( HOMEF ) , made the assertion weekend at a workshop held at the University of Uyo ( UNIUYO ) , Akwa Ibom State . <p> He said governments at all levels , multinational and local companies as well as individuals were guilty in this direction as they have played major roles in creating negative impact on the environment now tagged climate change . <p> Speaking on the theme : " Unpacking Re-Source Democracy " , he said rising consumption and wastages had led to massive extinction of species and severe environmental change , some of which might well be irreversible , adding that environmental degradation disrupts " our linkages to nature , shrivels our humanity and throws us into unhealthy rivalry and struggles to whatever goods remain " . <p> " It @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of crude oil and petroleum resources literally poisoned and damaged our environment , economy , politics and socio-cultural relationships .
