<h> Treat nature like a mother , says environmentalist <p> President of the Lekki Urban Forest Animal Sanctuary ( LUFASI ) Desmond Majekodunmi has charged Nigerians to treat nature as a mother to reverse the effects of climate change . <p> He lamented the " continuous upsetting of the balance of nature " ca n't support life on the planet earth . <p> He warned the natural environment was facing an impending catastrophe owing to the impacts of degradation . <p> Majekodunmi spoke at the weekend during activities marking the 2017 World Environment Day . <p> He said unless people " treat and respect nature the way individuals treat their mothers , " the danger would become irreversible . <p> The environmentalist pointed out benefits of technology was numerous with advancements making life more meaningful . <p> He however said : " Over carbonising the atmosphere ca n't support life . " <p> He emphasised the needs to reduce the carbon foot prints . <p> The theme for the year is Connect with Nature with LUFASI marking the commemoration with a debate on Climate Change for secondary school pupils in Lagos . <p> Pupils of Refiner 's College , Lekki , who opposed the motion : we can reverse climate change before it permanently destroys our natural environment won the debate . <p> They squared up with their counterparts from Greenspring School who supported the motion . <p> Other schools who participated in the event were the Redington School and Albesta College .
