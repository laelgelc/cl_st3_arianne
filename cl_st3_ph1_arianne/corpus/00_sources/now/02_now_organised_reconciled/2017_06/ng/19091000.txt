<h> Association seeks more support for refugees <p> The Commonwealth Scholars Fellows Alumni Association on Wednesday appealed to the government at all levels to give more support to refugees within their localities so as to enable them secure a future . <p> Dr. Biola Adimula , the Secretary General of the association , told the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Abuja that if refugees were accepted and welcomed , they will contribute socially and economically to their host communities . <p> Adimula , who is also a Lecturer , Centre for Peace and Strategic Studies , University of Ilorin also called on the private sectors to spur efforts in combating the refugee crisis globally . <p> She said that governments should ensure that " every refugee child gets education and every refugee family has somewhere safe to live as well as work or learn new skills to support their families . " <p> She explained that the displaced persons had emotions , ambitions , feelings and aspirations like every other human being so they rely on the rest of the world for support . <p> " It @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ perseverance of refugees and the contributions they make to communities around the world . <p> " So the least we can do is to welcome refugees into our communities . <p> " Because if refugees are accepted and welcomed and have a chance to learn and grow , then they will contribute socially and economically to their receiving countries , and to your community . <p> " As international organisations continue their effort in stabilising displaced people and securing a future for them , we charge governmental organisations to intensify effort in fighting this battle , " she said . <p> Adimula , however , noted that there was need to consolidate existing efforts , saying " our efforts should not only be reactive , but also proactive in order to prevent scenarios that lead to displacements . <p> " While reaching out to displaced persons , governmental organisations and the international community should employ more proactive measures to minimise conflicts , thereby minimising displacement rates , " she said . <p> According to the United Nations report more than 65 million people globally are currently displaced by conflicts emanating @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ governance . <p> " It says each day thousands of people are made to feel unwelcome in their own countries .
