<h> Hollywood star turned activist Arnold Schwarzenegger joined politicians and legal experts in Paris Saturday to launch a campaign for a global pact to protect the human right to a clean , healthy environment . <p> French President Emmanuel Macron promised to present the pact which its supporters want to see become an international treaty to the United Nations in September . <p> " With the planetary plan , we need to move on to a new stage after the Paris accord , " said Macron , referring to the landmark agreement signed in December 2015 by 196 nations to take steps to reduce greenhouse gases and combat global warming . <p> The end goal of the new pact is a legal treaty under which states can be brought to justice for flouting the rights of a group or individual . <p> " We already have two international ( human rights ) pacts ...
