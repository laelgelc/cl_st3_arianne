<p> The Low Emissions Target scheme advocated by the Climate Change Authority could simply adopt the mechanisms of the existing Renewable Energy Target . <p> That is , it would oblige electricity retailers and generators to obtain a certain proportion of their energy from qualifying low emissions sources - not just from renewable energy such as wind and solar power . <p> As with the Renewable Energy Target ( RET ) , certificates would be issued for qualifying low emissions electricity and surrendered to the government to acquit liabilities under the Low Emissions Target ( LET ) . <p> Former prime minister John Howard went to the 2007 election proposing a LET that defined low emissions energy as producing less than 200 kilograms of carbon dioxide per megawatt hour of electricity - less than a quarter of the current National Electricity Market average . <p> Federal Election 2007 .
