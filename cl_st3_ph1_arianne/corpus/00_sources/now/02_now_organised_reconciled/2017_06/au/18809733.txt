<h> Nanna ' knit in ' highlights CBA 's support of Adani <p> The Knitting Nanna ' knit in ' took place on Friday , 2 June outside the Commonwealth Bank in Mullumbimby . They are requesting that the CBA cut all ties with Adani . Photo supplied . <p> Protestors were once again brightening up the streets of Mullumbimby on Friday ( June 2 ) as they gathered outside the Commonwealth Bank ( CBA ) . The Knitting Nannas were keeping up the pressure on the bank to cut all ties with the Adani coal mine as part of a country wide protest against the coal mine proposed in the Galilee Basin in central Queensland . <p> ' It recently came to light that it was a CBA transaction that transferred $1.6 million from Adani to the Dept of Resources QLD in payment for a Water Licence . Also that the wages and payroll are through CBA for Adani Australia , ' saidKnitting Nanna , Rosie Lee . <p> ' This is notwithstanding previous public statements by the commonwealth bank that they would not fund Adani . We are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protect Australias best interests in the face of undeniable climate change . All responsible global financial institutions risk assessment addresses climate change and lack of social licence as unacceptable risk factors for new coal mines . ' <p> A spokesperson responded to The Echo that the Commonwealth Bank is ' aware of the recent protests at its Mullumbimby branch . We believe people have the right to express their views as long as they do so in a peaceful manner in compliance with the law and common standards of courtesy . ' <p> ' As a major bank we support nearly every sector in the economy . Strict environmental , social and governance standards are incorporated into our business-lending decisions and we will only fund projects that meet these standards . ' <p> The Knitting Nannas highlighted the fact that the pollution and depletion of Great Artesian Basin and the destruction of the Great Barrier Reef are not all that is at stake . <p> ' An estimated 70,000 jobs in tourism are in jeopardy if this mine , purported to be the biggest coal mine in the world , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and burning of fossil fuels is regarded as highly irresponsible in the current push around the world to halt the worst effects of climate change , ' continued Nanna Lee . <p> ' We urge the CBA to take unequivocal action to can coal and fund renewables .
