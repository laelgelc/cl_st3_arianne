<h> Economists value the Great Barrier Reef <h> Published : 26 June 2017 <h> How could you ever put a price tag on one of the most spectacular marvels of the nature ? <p> Apparently , quite easily . And for the record , it 's $56 Billion . <p> In a new report commissioned by the Great Barrier Reef Foundation , Deloitte calculated the economic , social and ' icon ' value of the heritage site . <p> The tourism industry is the biggest contributor to the grand sum , accounting for $29 billion . <p> But , the reef 's economic value is also largely put down to its status as an ' employer ' -- 64,000 people nationwide , with 39,000 of those jobs being direct . <p> This makes the Great Barrier Reef a bigger employer than company giants like Telstra , Qantas , NAB , and the oil and gas extraction industry . <p> " There has never been a more critical time to understand precisely what the reef contributes , and therefore , what we stand to lose without it , " Dr John @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ AAP . <p> " This report makes it clear that the Great Barrier Reef is a treasure that is too big to fail . " <p> The report says that this sum , $56 Billion , should be reason enough for Australians to care about maintaining the reef . <p> The reef 's value , $56 Billion , is the equivalent to that of 12 Sydney Opera Houses .
