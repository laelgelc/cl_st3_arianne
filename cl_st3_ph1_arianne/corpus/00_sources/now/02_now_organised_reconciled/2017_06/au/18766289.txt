The resolve is quickly going to smash into three incontrovertible truths . <p> First , the Paris treaty will be the most expensive global agreement ever . Cutting emissions without affordable , effective replacements for fossil fuel means more expensive power and less economic growth . <p> Calculations using the best peer-reviewed economic models show the global price tag of all the treaty 's promises would reach $1 trillion to $2 trillion every year from 2030 . <p> Without US involvement , the rest of the world will need to cough up between $800 billion and $1.6 trillion annually .
