<h> This Robotic Exosuit Could Turn You Into A Super Athlete <p> Researchers from Wyss Institute and Harvard SEAS have developed a soft robotic exosuit that significantly boosts a person 's running performance . The device requires a tether and external power supply to work , but once it becomes portable , it could help athletes run faster and further than before , smashing their existing running records without having to undergo additional training . <p> Credit : The Wyss Institute at Harvard University <p> In tests on treadmills , the device was shown to reduce the metabolic cost of running by 5.4 per cent ( metabolic cost being the amount of oxygen consumption and carbon dioxide produced while running ) . That might not sound like much , but to a long distance runner , it 's a substantial increase in performance . In real terms , it could make a 42km marathon feel like 40km , or improve a runner 's pace from 5:44km/s to 5:28km/s .
