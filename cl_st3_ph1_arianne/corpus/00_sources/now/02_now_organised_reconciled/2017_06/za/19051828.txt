celebrating 30 years of assisting rural communities . <p> Since its inception in 1987 by founders Dr Chris Saunders , Bruce Forssman and Duchesne Grice , the Siyazisiza Trust continues to make a substantial contribution to the lives of hundreds of rural dwellers through food security projects and the support of craft enterprises . <p> Khumbulani Craft , Siyazisiza 's daughter organisation , was established in 1999 and focuses on craft development , production and market access . <p> Based on the knowledge that crafters are not marketers , Khumbulani Craft and Siyazisiza Trust merged in 2012 to form one entity focusing on the establishment of black-owned secondary marketing businesses to serve rural crafters . <p> Five such businesses have been established and have secured sizeable
