" <p> " Illegal , unreported , and unregulated -- or IUU -- fishing around the world is jeopardising international food security and economic growth , and threatening marine ecosystems , " said Balton , the US deputy assistant secretary @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ocean conference opened Monday under the shadow of the US exit from the 190-plus nation pact aimed at combating global warming . <p> The United States , the world 's biggest carbon emitter after China , signed the agreement last year under the previous administration , but Trump has argued that the deal would harm the US economy . <p> UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres opened the conference with an appeal to countries to put aside national gain to save the oceans and avert a " global catastrophe .
