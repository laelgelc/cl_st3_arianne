<p> Tripura Environment Minister Bijitha Nath flagged off an awareness race from the Museum area today . <p> The idea was to disseminate the messages highlighting the threat of global warming , human-induced climate change , the thinning of the ozone layer and the impact of chemical pollution and deforestation . <p> The minister said , " This year 's theme from the UN is ' People Connect to Nation ' . The balance of nature is getting disturbed and to save that , every year new themes are being adopted and which we follow dedicatedly and not only in the capital but throughout the all district by involving all school students . " <p> Meantime , Biswendu Bhattacharya , an environment activist said , " After U.S. has withdrawn from the Paris Agreement the role and responsibility of India as a world leader in saving the environment gets more committed . " <p> Students , representatives from non-government organisations ( NGO ) and paramilitary forces participated in the race organised by Association for Research on People and Nature ( ARPAN ) , an NGO . <p> Priyanku Sarma , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ On the occasion of World Environment Day , a cross-country race was organised and in which I had participated and came first . I am very happy . " <p> Meantime , Tripura Pollution Control Board organised ' a sit and draw competition ' on various issues related to protection of the environment . Prizes were distributed among the winners . <p> Several programmes have been organised across the state to create awareness about environmental disasters and ways to mitigate them . <p> The environment activists had also urged the people in Tripura to reduce use of petrol and diesel in transport sector and curb the burning of Green House Gases ( GHGs ) which ultimately contributed to increase in the global temperature .
