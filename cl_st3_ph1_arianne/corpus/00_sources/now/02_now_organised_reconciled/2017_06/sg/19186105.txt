<h> EU to support Blue Growth through renewable energies <p> The European Union ( EU ) Council has exhorted member states to support Blue Growth through renewable energies . <p> The EU adopted Blue Growth Strategy in 2012 to support sustainable growth in the marine and maritime sectors as a whole as seas and oceans are drivers for the European economy and have great potential for innovation and growth . <p> The Council on Monday called upon the member states to manage the oceans and seas sustainably , promote renewable resource efficiency and renewable energies , remove barriers to growth , boost investment and strengthen trade cooperation . <p> The EU Council vowed to strengthen trade and business cooperation to maintain and develop further the EU 's position in global Blue Growth value chains and continue providing jobs , supported in particular by cooperation with science , governments and civil society . <p> The Council has expressed its support to the ' blue economy ' concept as
