<p> Hyderabad 's Rajiv Gandhi International Airport ( RGIA ) , situated at Shamshabad , is all set to expand at an estimated cost of Rs 2,629 crore , after it reportedly got the green nod from the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF ) . <p> The nod came from the Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) , which held several meetings over the issue . <p> " The EAC recommended the project for environmental clearance . RGIA is located within 5,495 acres of land of which about 2,000 acres has been developed towards airport needs which mainly constitutes of 1,700 acres of airside &amp; 300 acres of landside facilities . No land acquisition is involved as all the expansion activities are planned within the RGIA site area of 5,495 acres , " TNIE quoted the EAC as saying . <p> Last month , GMR Hyderabad International Airport Ltd . ( GHIAL ) , which operates the airport , announced that it had converted the entire taxiway edge lights from halogen lamps to energy efficient LEDs ( Light Emitting Diodes ) . <p> The airside @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the airport , had close to 500 halogen lamps , which covered around 26 km of the area . <p> The RGIA , which currently serves more than 15 million passengers per annum ( MPPA ) with close to 400 daily air traffic movements , has a mission to move from conventional energy to renewable energy sources for environmental sustenance . It recently commissioned a captive 5MW Solar Power Plant for its use as part of a plan to become completely carbon neutral . <p> In April this year , the airport received the carbon neutrality status by Airports Council International , GHIAL said . <p> GHAIL announced that it was conferred with the carbon neutrality ( level 3 : neutrality ) certificate at a ceremony organised at Doha , Qatar .
