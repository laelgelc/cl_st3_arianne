<p> TOKYO * It is crucial that Japan and China lead Asia in tandem to achieve greater integration , Emeritus Senior Minister Goh Chok Tong said yesterday , urging both countries to set aside their rivalry and historical mistrust . <p> " It will not work as well if only one country leads the charge . And if both countries provide competitive but divergent leadership , it will be destabilising , " he said at the Nikkei Future of Asia conference . <p> " The lack of trust due to history is expected ... But at some stage , both sides must cut the shackles of the anchor and move forward . " <p> This vision , Mr Goh admitted , was " a little idealistic " . This , said Vietnamese Prime Minister Nguyen Xuan Phuc in a separate session , showed " Asia is leading in international integration " . <p> Going forward , Mr Goh said , Japan and China can take the lead as Asia 's top two economies and vocal proponents of free trade . <p> Japan is now spearheading the Trans-Pacific Partnership ( TPP ) , and Mr Goh said Tokyo should extend an invite to Beijing when the latter is ready to join the pact . <p> Small countries , too , have their part to play , he said , as they are non-threatening , outward-looking and can " propose creative ideas without raising suspicions of an ulterior motive " . <p> " But they need to advance their interests based on principles and not take sides based on a patron- client relationship , " he said .
