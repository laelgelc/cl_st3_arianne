<h> The Sunday Times says <h> Heeding the call of biodiversity <p> The discovery of over 480 new species of animals and plants here over the last five years is something of a miracle . Given the size of the island and its ever-expanding concrete jungle , one would have expected biodiversity to be on the back foot . That it is flowering instead is a tribute to the resilience of nature , as well as the efforts of the National Parks Board , Nature Society , researchers , naturalists and eco bloggers . <p> Any undertaking to protect Singapore 's natural heritage can not be left to conservationists alone , of course . Broad public participation is needed , not just because of the scale of the work to be done but also to ensure efforts to shield the fragile existence of indigenous flora and fauna are not undermined by careless human actions or invasive alien species which can cause extinctions . <p> The loss of biodiversity can trigger a complex chain of consequences , which could harm humans if the links are not understood .
