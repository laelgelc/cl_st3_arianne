<h> World Environment Day : Harsh Vardhan urges to protect nature <p> On the occasion of ' World Environment Day ' , Union Environment Minister Dr. Harsh Vardhan on Monday said that the people across the country should pledge to protect and respect nature , adding small amends in daily life would lead to a better environment . <p> " Environment belongs to all of us and it belongs to our future generation also . Our ancestors gave us clean rivers , pure air , rich forests and fertile soil . They also gave us the ancient wisdom to protect and respect mother nature . We have to pledge to make small amends in our day to day lives to make the environment better , " Dr. Vardhan told the media here . <p> He added that due to lack of understanding or lack of commitment , humans have probably ruined everything . <p> " The time has come to reaffirm , reassert and recommit ourselves to make a social movement . Small interventions from our side will certainly pass on a healthy environment to the future generation .
