<h> Green building conference gathers global voices in HK <p> The much-awaited culmination of the three-year Sustainable Built Environment conference cycleView of the Golden Pavilion temple in Nan Lian Garden , Hong Kong . Marco Bicci/Shutterstock The Sustainable Built Environment ( SBE ) series will culminate its current three-year cycle with a global conference in Hong Kong on 5-7 June . <p> Around 1,800 green building advocates , policymakers , academics , and industry practitioners representing 55 countries are set to descend on the Chinese SAR , which won the hosting rights to the prestigious conference after rigorous selection process . <p> World Sustainable Built Environment Conference 2017 will collate all the insights from 20 regional conferences that the SBE mounted in 2016 . This year 's global conference , will be themed around " Transforming Our Built Environment through Innovation and Integration : Putting Ideas into Action . " <p> Begun in 2000 , the SBE series undergoes a three-year cycle , beginning with planning in the first , regional events in the second , and a global conference in the third . <p> WSBE 2017 will be timed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kong SAR . The conference will be jampacked with events , including an exhibition , keynote addresses , roundtable discussions and more than 100 parallel sessions . <p> A plenary session on climate change and sustainable development will highlight the potential of the building sector and its role in environmental protection and present solutions to further the Paris Climate Agreement objectives and the Sustainable Development Goals . This session will feature leading voices from UN Environment , including former Executive Secretary of the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change Christiana Figueres , now vice-chair of the Global Covenant of Mayors for Climate &amp; Energy . <p> The three-day event is co-organised by the Construction Industry Council ( CIC ) and the Hong Kong Green Building Council ( HKGBC ) .
