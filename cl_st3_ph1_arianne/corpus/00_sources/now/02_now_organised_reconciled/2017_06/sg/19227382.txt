<h> Share this content <p> SINGAPORE : The construction of Singapore 's fourth desalination plant began on Thursday ( Jun 29 ) in Marina East . <p> It is the first dual-mode desalination plant , meaning it can treat both freshwater from the Marina Reservoir and seawater , depending on weather conditions . <p> Currently , freshwater from reservoirs is processed at eight water works across Singapore , while seawater is treated at SingSpring and Tuaspring desalination plants . On rainy days when water levels in the reservoirs are high , the excess rainwater will be discharged into the sea . But with the new plant , the water will be treated for use . <p> Designed by Keppel Infrastructure Holdings , which will also build , own and operate the Marina East desalination plant , the facility will be able to produce around 30 million gallons of fresh drinking water per day , enough to fill about 55 Olympic-sized swimming pools when it is completed by the end of January 2020 . <p> " SLEEK MODERN DESIGN " <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <p> The Keppel Marina East Desalination Plant @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be used by the public for recreation . Water from both sources will be filtered from the get-go , removing larger impurities before it goes through a " dual flow chamber " . <p> This chamber then pumps the water to the plant through a 1.8km underground pipeline above the Marina Coastal Expressway ( MCE ) . Such a system has been years in the making -- a similar dual-mode technology has been in operation since 2007 at a plant in Pasir Ris to test it out before applying it on a larger scale at Marina East . <p> There will be a play area above the dual-flow chamber which will pump water to the desalination plant through a 1.8km underground pipeline .
