<p> LONDON : Increasingly scorching summer heat in India is leading to a big jump in heatwave deaths -- and much worse is likely on the way , researchers said on Wednesday . <p> A modest 0.5 degree Celsius rise in average temperatures in India over the last 50 years has led to a nearly 150 percent hike in heatwaves that kill at least 100 people , said researchers at the University of California in Irvine . <p> But with India now on a path to between 2.2 and 5.5 degrees Celsius of temperature rise by the end of the century , the rate of heatwave deaths in India -- and other Asian nations -- could soar , scientists say . <p> " I was taken aback by how large the increase in the likelihood of these mass mortality events was with a modest increase in temperature , " said Steven J. Davis , an earth systems science professor at the university and one of the authors of the report , published in the journal Science Advances . <p> " Even under the lower global warming scenario , you still @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
