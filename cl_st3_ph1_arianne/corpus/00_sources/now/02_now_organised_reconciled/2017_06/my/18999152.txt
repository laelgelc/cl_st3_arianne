<h> Nation <h> Pushing bicycles as mode of transport -- and for the environs <p> KUALA LUMPUR : What Maria Castillo Fernandez ( pic ) wants to see on the streets of Kuala Lumpur is the presence of more cyclists . <p> The Ambassador and Head of the European Union Delegation to Malaysia believes that the key to combating global warming and climate change lies not only with governments around the world , but with individuals as well . <p> " Individually , everyone counts . Individually , what each of us do counts , " she said in an interview . <p> This is where cycling played a part for Castillo Fernandez . <p> It is a way for her to be more environmentally friendly . <p> " I am a big user of bicycles , even in Kuala Lumpur , and especially on the weekends , " she said , adding that apart from cycling , she would also bring her own bags when shopping to lessen her dependency on plastic bags . <p> For individuals to play their part in being environmentally friendly , she said there @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " It is very important to make the public , especially the younger generation , aware of the impact of climate change and how we can all contribute , " she said . <p> To raise awareness on climate change among Malaysians , she said the delegation will be holding the " Connecting the Dots : Fighting Climate Change " event on Monday in collaboration with Taylor 's University Malaysia . <p> " We thought it would be good to reach out to the students . <p> " We will have a panel discussion , where there will be representatives from the Government , the university and private sectors .
