<h> World <h> Britain urges United States to take climate change seriously <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Britain wants the United States to take a leading role on addressing climate change , Foreign Secretary Boris Johnson said on Thursday but he would not be drawn on what his response would be if President Donald Trump pulls out of the Paris accord . <p> Trump is expected to say whether to keep the United States in a global pact to fight climate change , and a source close to the matter told Reuters said he was preparing to pull out of the Paris accord . <p> " We 're not there yet . We continue to lobby the U.S. at all levels to continue to take climate change extremely seriously , " Johnson told Sky news . <p> He added that other countries such as India and China needed to make further progress on reducing carbon dioxide emissions .
