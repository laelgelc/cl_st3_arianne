<p> Leading researchers have condemned attempts to change the way carbon from trees will be counted in Europe . <p> The scientists fear that millions of tonnes of CO2 from forests will disappear from the books if the changes go ahead . <p> Trees are important carbon sinks as they soak up about 10% of Europe 's emissions every year . <p> But some countries want to cut more trees down in future without counting the resulting loss of carbon . <p> Europe 's forests have been increasing for the last century , and over the last 10 years the equivalent of 1,500 football pitches of trees have been added every day . <p> However accounting for carbon contained in trees is a fiendishly difficult task . Forests can both soak up and emit carbon depending on how old they are , and how they are managed and harvested . <p> As the European Union tries to put in place wide-ranging plans to restrict future carbon emissions , officials want to ensure that accounting for the impact of forests on the atmosphere should be based on sound science . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ forestry at the levels seen between 1990 and 2009 .
