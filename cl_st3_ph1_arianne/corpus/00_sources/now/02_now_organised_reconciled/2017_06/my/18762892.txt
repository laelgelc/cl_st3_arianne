<h> World <h> Slowing climate change ' a moral imperative , ' says Mexico <p> MEXICO CITY ( Reuters ) - Mexican President Enrique Pena Nieto on Thursday said his country was still fully committed to the Paris climate treaty , after U.S. President Donald Trump said the world 's No. 1 economy will pull out of the global pact to tackle climate change . <p> In a statement shortly after the Mexican president 's tweet , Mexico 's foreign and environmental ministries issued a joint statement , saying climate change is an " incontrovertible " fact that requires cooperation from all nations . <p> " Actions to put the brakes on climate change are a moral imperative , " the statement said .
