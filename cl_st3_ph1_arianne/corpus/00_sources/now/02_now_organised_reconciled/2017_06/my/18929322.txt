Both are reluctant to condemn Russia for its actions in Syria and Ukraine . <p> President Trump 's positive views of Russian President Vladimir Putin have been the subject of much media attention , and their underlying motivation will be the subject of congressional investigation in the coming months . But why is Francis so reluctant to speak out ? <p> Although he is usually careful when he speaks on international issues , Francis is not reluctant to condemn the arms trade , the use of armed force in settling disputes , and the inhospitable treatment of refugees and migrants .
