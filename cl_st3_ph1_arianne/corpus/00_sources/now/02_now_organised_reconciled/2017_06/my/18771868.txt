<h> Chemical cocktail found in Barrier Reef turtles <p> SYDNEY : Human medicines and household substances have been discovered in the blood of green turtles in Australia 's Great Barrier Reef , researchers said Friday , highlighting the impact of man-made matter on marine life . <p> Medications for the heart ( milrinone ) and gout ( allopurinol ) , as well as cosmetic and industrial chemicals , were among hundreds of thousands of substances detected in the reptiles ' bloodstream as part of an ongoing conservation project . <p> Scientists said exposure to the substances had caused side effects in the turtles , with indications of inflammation and liver dysfunction . <p> " Humans are putting a lot of chemicals into the environment and we do n't always know what they are and what effect they are having , " said Amy Heffernan of the University of Queensland . <p> " What you put down your sink , spray on your farms , or release from industries ends up in the marine environment and in turtles in the Great Barrier Reef .
