<h> Secure water supply -- MWSS <p> " Much needs to be done for the country to become water secure , " declared Reynaldo V. Velasco , chairman of the Metropolitan Waterworks and Sewerage System , before the Environment Committee of the Financial Executives Institute of the Philippines . <p> Velasco pointed out that the Philippines ' national water security score stand at 40.4 out of 100 , which means that the nation is not quite secure in its water supply . <p> " Water supply must be secured through additional water sources . We can not depend solely on the Angat Dam especially when the ' Big One ' or a 7.2-magnitude earthquake strikes , " Velasco added , citing that MWSS is pursuing the Laiban and Kaliwa water dam projects to ensure adequate , steady and sustainable water supply for consumers in Metro Manila and nearby provinces in the next 25 to 50 years . <p> The MWSS chief spoke at Finex 's " Water Security Forum Series 4 : Averting a Crisis , " in Finex 's bid to raise awareness on how government and private agencies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensure long-term water security . <p> The fourth segment of the water security forum shed light on the overall water situation in Metro Manila and other parts of the country .
