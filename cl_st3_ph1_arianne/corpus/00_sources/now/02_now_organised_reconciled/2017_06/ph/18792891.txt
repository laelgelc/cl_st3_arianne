<h> For peace in Mindanao , ' Pinoy Aquaman ' to swim from Camiguin to Balingoan <p> SURIGAO CITY ( MindaNews / 03 June ) -- After surviving the icy-cold waters in New York , United States last month , triathlete and environmental lawyer Ingemar " Pinoy Aquaman " Macarine will endure a record-setting 12-kilometer open water swim across Macajalar Bay , between Misamis Oriental and Camiguin Island on Sunday , June 4 . <p> Macarine will kick off from the volcanic island to Balingoan town in Misamis Oriental . <p> Ingemar Macarine at the start of his Hudson River swim by the Newburn-Beacon Bridge in New York state . Photo courtesy of Atty . Ingemar Macarine 's Facebook page <p> He is gunning for his 30th successful open-water swim since he started his open-water swim in December 31 , 2013 in Surigao City . <p> Last May 14 , the Pinoy Aquaman successfully swam the 8.4-kilometer distance , nonstop and unassisted , in one hour and 49 minutes from Newburgh-Beacon Bridge to Bannerman Island in New York , the only Filipino so far to do it . <p> Macarine @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said this is part of his preparation for the English Channel swim this year . In August last year , he failed in his bid in the English Channel due to bad weather . <p> Early this year , Macarine planned to do two-way swim from Camiguin to Baligoan and back to volcanic island to come up with a total of 24-kilometer distance but he cancelled due to typhoon " Auring . " <p> Macarine got into open-water swims as part of his lifetime advocacy for clean seas , marine resource protection , environmental tourism and climate change awareness . But for this particular swim , Macarine dedicated this for peace in Mindanao . <p> " I am a Mindanawon and I dedicate this particular swim for peace in Mindanao particurly in war-torn city of Marawi , " Macarine told MindaNes.
