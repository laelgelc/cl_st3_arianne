<h> Climate change threatens heritage sites around PH , says UN official <p> OLD COUNTRY A visit to Vigan City gives visitors a peek into how Filipinos lived during the Spanish period through mansions and historic buildings on Calle Crisologo , the center of the heritage district , and in other areas around the Ilocos Sur provincial capital . -- EV ESPIRITU <p> CLARK FREEPORT -- The historic city of Vigan in Ilocos Sur province , the rice terraces in the Cordillera region and Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park in Palawan province , are suffering the effects of climate change and human activities , and require better protection , according to a top heritage official . <p> Eric Zerrudo , vice chair of the United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organization ( Unesco ) Philippine cultural committee , said the degradation and risks observed in Ilocos Sur , Cordillera and Palawan required local governments , communities and tourists to take more action and protect these destinations listed as Unesco World Heritage Sites . <p> Zerrudo , also the Philippine representative to the World Heritage Committee and director of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Conservation of Cultural Property and the Environment in the Tropics , spoke during a Pampanga tourism forum here . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> In Vigan , he said , the city government , which has been tasked as Unesco site manager , has put up concrete breakers along the coast to protect the 16th-century Spanish colonial town from flooding due to rising sea levels . <p> Thousands of tourists come year-round especially to Crisologo Street , causing shortage of parking areas and rooms while increasing the volume of trash . <p> As more Vigan lands are bought for resorts and inns , the sources of soil used for making local bricks and tiles have diminished , Zerrudo said . <p> Craftsmen have adapted by mixing inorganic waste in those products . " Authenticity requires costs and energy . We have to be meaningful in the present , " Zerrudo said . <p> Idle terraces <p> In the centuries-old rice terraces in Ifugao province , landslides have occurred but some terraces are repaired by farmers using native stone-walling techniques , he said . <p> Several terraces , however , are idle because @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rice farms . <p> Commercial shops , subdivisions and extreme sports destinations have risen in four Ifugao towns that are gateways to the rice terraces . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> In 2016 , the Ifugao provincial government , designated Unesco site manager , stopped the construction of a parking building in Banaue town due to complaints that it would block the view of the rice terraces , Zerrudo said . <p> He said the UN Food and Agriculture Organization had declared the rice terraces as a Globally Important Agricultural Heritage System . <p> At Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park , Zerrudo said the local monkey population had displayed stress over changes in temperature and the presence of as many as 1,500 tourists . Crocodiles there have been moving to wetter ponds . <p> The park , which features a limestone karst landscape with an underground river , " is a very vulnerable site , " Zerrudo said . <p> Need for ethics protocol <p> For tourists visiting these heritage sites , Zerrudo said their " posture should be conservation .
