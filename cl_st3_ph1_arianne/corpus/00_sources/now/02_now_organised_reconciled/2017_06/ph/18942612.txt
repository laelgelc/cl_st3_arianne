<h> Youth 's low interest in environment alarms group <p> DAVAO CITY ( MindaNews/13 June ) -- The Interface Development Interventions ( IDIS ) expressed alarm over the result of a survey conducted by the University of Mindanao 's Institute of Popular Opinion showing a low interest of the youth in Davao City in environment . <p> " Everyone should know the impacts of climate change and everyone should contribute in addressing it , especially students . They should contribute in mitigation and adaptation measures , in their own capacity , " IDIS acting executive director Chinkie Pelino-Golle told MindaNews on Monday . <p> The IPO survey called " Davao Youth Situationaer " surveyed 264 youth from May 24 to 30 . <p> It showed that only 1.1 percent of them are affiliated with environmental groups , the lowest among those engaged in sports and recreation ( 49% ) , church and religious groups ( 29% ) , civic organizations ( 20% ) , and socio-political activities ( 1.2% ) . <p> Golle said she is disheartened to know only a few youth are engaged in environment organization or part @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , she said they are taking this as a challenge to further their advocacy by improving their strategies in reaching more young people and enhancing their awareness on ecological issues . <p> She called on academic institutions to give importance to environmental education and activities to raise their awareness . <p> " It is not enough that students are required to plant trees before they graduate . They have to understand that tree planting is just part of the tree growing , " she said . <p> She said they are hoping to encourage " more youth to care and protect the environment . " <p> But she acknowledged that " education and awareness-building should come from their home and should be enhanced in schools . " <p> " IDIS will also try to partner with more schools in environmental advocacy and education , " she said . ( Antonio L. Colina IV/MindaNews )
