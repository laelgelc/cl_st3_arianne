<h> EO to require real estate companies to plant trees <p> A proposed executive order ( EO ) from the President to require real estate companies to increase their investment in the environment sector by planting more trees within their housing projects is now in the works . <p> Environment Secretary Roy Cimatu said he would propose to President Rodrigo Duterte to sign an EO that will make tree planting mandatory not just in public areas but also inside privately owned housing projects . <p> DENR Undersecretary Demetrio Ignacio explained that Cimatu prefers an EO on the mandatory tree-planting over just an administrative order because it requires inter-agency coordination . <p> " An EO is better since it will be an inter-agency effort . We will require tree planting or set up areas for planting of trees in all subdivisions , " he said . <p> Cimatu on Tuesday called for a collective effort among members of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) to achieve economic and socially equitable growth without further compromising the environment in the face of ASEAN economic integration and climate change . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to push for urban biodiversity conservation and green technology in building resilient cities and in achieving inclusive economic growth . <p> " I want you to know that the Philippines , through the DENR , espouses such approaches to sustainable urbanization , and I encourage everyone present here today to reconcile all efforts to make such strategies effective and fruitful , " Cimatu said . <p> Cimatu cited a research showing that urbanization is the major driver of the world 's changing climate brought about by global warming . <p> He noted that city dwellers worldwide consume 72 percent of energy from fossil fuels and contribute to emission of 80 percent of greenhouse gases ( GHG ) , and that unsustainable practices in urban areas push the Earth beyond its ecological threshold such as change in land use , which leads to land degradation and biodiversity loss . <p> The forum was part of the 50th founding anniversary of the ASEAN , which is chaired by the Philippines this year .
