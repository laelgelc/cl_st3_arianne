<h> Maynilad invests P42 B in projects over last 5 years <p> The amount of investment that west zone concessionaire Maynilad Water Services , Inc . ( Maynilad ) has poured in for its water projects already amounted to P42 billion over the last five years , Maynilad president and chief executive officer Ramoncito S. Fernandez said . <p> During a Water Security Forum organized by the Environment Committee of the Financial Executives Institute of the Philippines ( FINEX ) , Fernandez said the company has been building new facilities such as additional pumping stations and reservoirs to improve the water supply system 's climate change resiliency and to create redundancies in the event of a disaster . <p> " We need to make these investments to enhance water reliability despite the threat of climate change , infrastructure limitations and the rising demand for water due to population growth and urbanization , " Fernandez said . <p> As of now , Maynilad 's La Mesa and Putatan Water Treatment Plants are currently undergoing upgrades and retrofitting to enhance treatment capacities in the face of rising turbidity levels in the raw @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ application of advanced treatment technologies to harness other water sources , such as seawater and used water , given the urgency of meeting the water supply needs of a growing population . <p> Maynilad is now working closely with the Metropolitan Waterworks and Sewerage System ( MWSS ) , which is facilitating the development of additional raw water sources for Metro Manila and adjacent areas . <p> Fernandez said the appointment of MWSS Administrator Reynaldo Velasco is a welcome development for water utilities like Maynilad . <p> " We are heartened by the MWSS Administrator Reynaldo Velasco 's promise to balance the continued business viability of the concessionaires and the welfare of water consumers . As long as government and the private sector work hand-in-hand , we can overcome the challenges of water security , " Fernandez said .
