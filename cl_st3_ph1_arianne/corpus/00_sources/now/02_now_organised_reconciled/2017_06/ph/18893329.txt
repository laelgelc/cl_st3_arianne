<h> New onion seeder from PhilMech is profitable to use <p> The Philippine Center for Postharvest Development and Mechanization(PhilMech) has launched an onion mechanical seeder that can plant one hectare in less than three hours and is highly profitable to use . This is the 10-ROMS or Ten-Row Onion Mechanical Seeder which was unveiled during PhilMech 's 39th anniversary celebration recently . <p> By using this machine , the farmer can expect an additional 2 to 2.5 tons harvest of bulbs from one hectare . Not only that , the cost of production is significantly reduced . <p> TEN-ROW ONION MECHANICAL SEEDER -- This is the 10-Row Onion Mechanical Seeder ( 10-ROMS ) that was launched during the 39th anniversary celebration of PhilMech . It is attached to a hand tractor which can be operated by one man . The machine can plant one hectare in less than three hours . A complete unit that includes the hand tractor costs about P240,000 .
