<h> Baguio council sets public hearings on pending measures <h> Baguio council sets public hearings on pending measures <p> Friday , June 23 , 2017 <p> THE Baguio City Council is set to conduct a series of public hearings on proposed ordinances to gather inputs and determine the sentiments of the people on the proposed measures . <p> The first consultation will be on 9 a.m . Friday , June 30 , at the City Council Session Hall , the City Council committee on laws , human rights and justice under Councilor Faustino Olowan will be on the proposed amendatory ordinance filed by Councilor Edgar Avila amending section 6 of Ordinance 1 or the Baguio City Number Coding Scheme .
