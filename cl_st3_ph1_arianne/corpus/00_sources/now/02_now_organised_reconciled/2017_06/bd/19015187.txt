<h> Bangladesh authorities making ' mega-plan ' to tackle deadly landslides <h> The government is making a mega-plan to avert and tackle disasters in hills after deaths of over 150 people in landslides triggered by heavy rains in five southeastern districts . <p> The disaster management and relief ministry has called a meeting next week and informed other ministries about it on Thursday , Disaster Management and Relief Secretary Md Shah Kamal . <p> He said a high-level committee of 21 members was formed to assess the damage in the disaster and set the next course of action . The committee has been given 30 days to make a report . <p> Top officials and experts say possible natural and manmade reasons behind landslides should be identified in the mega-plan . <p> They say it should also prioritise issues like ensuring protection in hills , removing people from the hillsides , and technological intervention . <p> Death toll in the landslides in Rangamati , Chittagong , Bandarban , Cox 's Bazar and Khagrhachharhi rose to 156 on Thursday . The low advanced from northwest to southwest at that time . <p> But the low this time chose the opposite direction and advanced towards northeast . It weakened into rains in Rangamati and the other hill districts . <p> Rainfall records in Rangamati <p> June 21 , 2004 - 304mm <p> June 26 , 1999 - 307mm <p> July 15 , 1998 - 317mm <p> Aug 4 , 1983 - 335mm <p> July 11 , 2004 - 337mm <p> June 11 , 2017 - 365mm <p> Samarendra Karmakar , a former director at the Meteorology Department , feared that the number of such incidents may rise in the future following effects of climate change . <p> He recommended measures to stop cutting hills and trees to avert disaster . <p> " Heavy rains will take place anytime during the rainy season . " And the unplanned habitation has caused deaths , " he added . <p> The Department of Disaster Management Director General Reaz Ahmed said they had taken measures to tackle landslide disaster as per their capability . <p> " The process to take a big action plan is under way .
