<h> India is a land of opportunities : Narendra Modi tells Dutch CEOs <h> The two countries signed three MoUs in the fields of social security , water cooperation and cultural cooperation . <p> Describing the Netherlands as India 's " natural partner " in its economic development , Prime Minister Narendra Modi today invited Dutch companies to invest in the country which he said is the " land of opportunities " . <p> Focusing on trade , technology and investment , Modi interacted with CEOs of major Dutch companies after holding bilateral talks with his Dutch counterpart Mark Rutte . <p> The two countries signed three MoUs in the fields of social security , water cooperation and cultural cooperation . <p> During his interaction with top Dutch CEOs , Modi said the reforms introduced by his government seek to enhance ease of doing business and bring standards at par with global standards . <p> The prime minister , who is here on the final leg of his three-nation tour , said India has made 7,000 reforms for attracting FDI in various sectors including real estate and defence . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with over 7 per cent growth rate and 1.25 billion population of which 800 million people are below 35 years . <p> Water is a prime sector for cooperation between the two countries , Modi said , referring to a joint water technology initiative , Dutch Indian Water Alliance for Leadership Initiative ( DIWALI ) . <p> Irrigation and water conservation are the areas where the two countries can boost cooperation , he said . <p> Seeking investments , he said pension funds are especially welcome to India . <p> During the bilateral talks , Modi and the Dutch PM reaffirmed their commitment to climate change accord and strengthen cooperation in developing renewable energy .
