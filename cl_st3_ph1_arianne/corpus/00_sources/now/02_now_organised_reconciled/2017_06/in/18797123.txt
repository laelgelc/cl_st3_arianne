<p> Since the first celebration in 1974 on June 5 , United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) has been announcing the theme of the World Environment Day to raise awareness and generate political momentum for conservation of environment and nature . " Connecting People to Nature " is the theme of this year 's World Environment Day . <p> The UNEP has urged people ' to get outdoors and into nature , to appreciate its beauty and to think about how we are part of nature and how intimately we depend on it . It challenges us to find fun and exciting ways to experience and cherish this vital relationship ' . <p> According to the UNEP , billions of rural people around the world spend every working day ' connected to nature ' and appreciate full well their dependence on natural water supplies and how nature provides their livelihoods in the form of fertile soil . However , they are among the first to suffer when ecosystems are threatened , whether by pollution , climate change or over-exploitation . <p> Governments and the organizations in the United Nations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their concern for the preservation and enhancement of the environment . <p> At personal level , each of us needs to commit to protect Mother Nature to which we owe our existence and life of future generations . <p> Of late , there had been growing concern about the depletion of the ozone layer , increase in toxic chemicals , desertification and global warming . <p> Over the years , the observance has developed into a global platform for taking action on urgent environmental issues . Millions of people have taken part over the years , helping drive change in their consumption habits , as well as in proper implementation of national and international environmental policies .
