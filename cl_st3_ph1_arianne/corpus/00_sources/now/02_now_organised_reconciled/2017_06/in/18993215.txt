<h> BRICS eyes second golden decade of cooperation <p> Beijing , Jun 15 ( PTI ) India and other BRICS members today pledged to strengthen partnership and jointly usher in a second " golden decade " of cooperation among the major emerging economies . <p> The pledge came during the second BRICS ( Brazil , Russia , India , China , South Africa ) Sherpa meeting that began yesterday in Qingdao in eastern Chinas Shandong Province . <p> Participants in the meeting reviewed the progress of BRICS cooperation in key areas such as political , security , economy , people-to-people and cultural exchanges , and focused on the outcome of the upcoming 2017 BRICS Summit , which is scheduled between September 3-5 in the coastal city of Xiamen , Fujian Province , state-run Xinhua news agency reported . <p> " The five countries are not together by chance . The consensus building is very easy among us . " <p> Indian deputy sherpa Alok Dimri said the priority of BRICS is the economic and financial market forum , which focuses on the reform of the economic and global governance system . <p> " There are multiple dimensions and initiatives on which BRICS is working together . We have the New Development Bank ( NDB ) , which works on infrastructure , and lots of initiatives in the fields of finance and people-to-people exchanges , " he said . <p> The NDB , established by the BRICS in 2014 , was a milestone for pragmatic financial cooperation among the BRICS . <p> " The bank can be seen as a platform for the BRICS as a whole to reach out to other countries . One of its central aspects is to support the financing of infrastructure in member countries .
