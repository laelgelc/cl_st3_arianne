<h> Lok Janshakti demands recall of beef ban law <p> SHILLONG : The Lok Janshakti Party has asked the Union Government to roll back the law of cattle sale for slaughtering purposes.The Party , in a petition to the Minister in charge , Environment , Forest and Climate Change , said that beef is a primary food for the people of North East and the ban on sale of cattle for slaughtering purposes has affected the local population vigorously.While opposing the ban , the Party demanded an early roll back of the law imposing the ban on sale of cattle for slaughtering purposes . " By the imposition of the Law it seems that the Government of India is dictating the people to eat according to the Government guidelines , which can not be justified in any manner whatsoever , " the Party added .
