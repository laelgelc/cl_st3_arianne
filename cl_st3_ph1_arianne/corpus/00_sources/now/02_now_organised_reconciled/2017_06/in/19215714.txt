<h> HC seeks details of shoot orders against tigress <p> Nagpur : The Nagpur bench of Bombay High Court on Wednesday passed strictures on forest officials , including principal chief conservator of forest ( PCCF , Wildlife ) , for issuing a shoot-at-sight order against a man-eater tiger without identifying the animal . <p> PCCF will be held responsible for any human loss until further orders if a wrong feline is killed , a division bench comprising Justices Bhushan Dharmadhikari and Rohit Deo said . <p> The judges told additional government pleader Ketki Joshi to reply to the court 's queries till Thursday and fixed the hearing at 10.30am . <p> The bench also directed the registry to take identification records of the tiger ( T-27 cub-I ) submitted by deputy conservator of forest ( DCF ) into its custody . " You are taking drastic steps to satisfy public sentiments , " the judges said . <p> Petitioner Dr Jerryl Banait moved court after chief wildlife warden of South Brahmapuri range of forests issued shoot-at-sight orders against the animal that had killed five villagers and injured three in villages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment , Forest and climate change ( MOEFCC ) , National Tiger Conservation Authority ( NTCA ) through its Delhi based inspector general , State Forest Department principal secretary in Mumbai , principal chief conservator of forest ( wildlife ) , and Chandrapur collector were made respondents in the case . <p> The petitioner through counsels Tushar Mandlekar and Rohan Malviya accused respondents of blatantly flouting NTCA 's norms in such cases . <p> Stressing NTCA guidelines , they said if tigers are man-eaters they must be tranquillized and then released in core area . The guidelines further state that the recommendation of local advisory committee , comprising local forest officers and villagers , is must for execution . <p> They also said the forest department did not have the right to kill animals and questioned the department 's methods to identify a tigress among 80 tigers in Tadoba . <p> Earlier , the DCF said that they had installed 25 camera traps to track the tigress ' movement in the forest . TOI had reported that four teams comprising sharp shooters of the police department and two veterinary doctors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abandoned the guns and equipment and ran away when the feline charged at them . <p> The judges asked forest officials to explain procedure on how they zeroed on the man-eater beast . The AGP failed to properly reply to the court 's query .
