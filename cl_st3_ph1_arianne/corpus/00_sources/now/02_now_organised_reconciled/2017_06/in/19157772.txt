Their boss is Chinese and they communicate with him through signing as they do not speak the same language . Both men , in their twenties , have travelled from a village in Sehwan in Sindh , to Gwadar to work as labourers at the port site under construction . Even after paying rent for accommodation , meals and transport , they save more than they ever earned in their own village .
