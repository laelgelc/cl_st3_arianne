<h> Students turn diplomats , deliberate global issues <p> TNN Updated : Jun 3 , 2017 , 01.21 AM IST <p> Lucknow : The auditorium of La Martiniere Girls ' College turned into a micro United Nations conference on Friday with more than 350 student delegates from around 15 schools of the country debating crucial issues . <p> The topics of discussion ranged from climate change to black market trade faced by the global community . This was the third session of the Model UN Conference ( MUN ) 2017 . <p> MUN is an educational simulation of the UN in which students participate as delegates and learn about diplomacy , international relations , social issues and the UN . <p> " It is a student-led initiative in which students from all over the country participate as delegates or executive board members , " said Fizza Suhel , a student of LMGC , who is heading the international press cell of MUN . <p> Many committees like the United Nations Environment Assembly , International Criminal Police Organization and United Nations Security Council among others were simulated to deliberate on issues @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Shubhi Singh and chief guest Dr Kirti Narain , the entire delegate body along with the secretariat embarked to a hotel on Sapru Marg where the three-day convention will continue till Sunday . <p> From Lucknow , Students of Seth MR Jaipuria School , City Montessori School , La Martiniere College , Delhi Public School and Millennium School are participating in the event . <p> Purujit Arora , a class XII student of Jaipuria School , said , " MUN has made me politically aware . Not only has the experience expanded my knowledge of international affairs but it has also helped in expanding my analytical skills . " <p> Eerishika Pankaj , an alumna of LMGC pursuing graduation in Pune , said , " Organising these conferences has given me a first-hand exposure of the world of marketing .
