Global health requires a deeper diplomatic perspective of how national economies are programmed and structured and what it means when implemented at grass-roots levels . It is very clear that the recent decades have seen progress in life expectancy around the world due to the improvement noted in medico-technological advancements , political will and administrative reforms that have taken place to mainstream global health and ensure improved outcomes .
