<h> Gorewada zoo project gets final forest clearance <p> Nagpur : In a big push to the Rs451.35 crore Gorewada International Zoo project on the outskirts of the city , the Forest Advisory Committee ( FAC ) of the ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) on Tuesday granted final ( stage-II ) forest clearance . <p> TOI was first to report on May 25 and June 16 about the city 's signature project getting stage-I clearance and Forest Development Corporation of Maharashtra ( FDCM ) subsequently depositing Rs13.73 crore towards net present value ( NPV ) and compensatory afforestation cost . <p> " Following the final clearance , work order for the construction of Indian Safari and reservoir will be issued soon to both the successful bidders who have been shortlisted through e-tendering process , " forest officials told TOI . <p> The letter issued by the MoEFCC states that stage-II approval to the project has been granted following the conditions fulfilled by the FDCM on June 14 . It will house animals like tigers , leopards , sloth bears and herbivores . <p> Similarly , the reservoir will be built on 28 hectares and its water will be utilized for the wildlife . Both the attractions will be built on the left side of the Nagpur-Katol Road .
