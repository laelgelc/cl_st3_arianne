<h> Gujarat Environment Management Institute to fund 35 studies each year <p> The Gujarat Environment Management Institute will come out with its policy to facilitate research and development activities in the field of environment , depending upon current requirements of the state . <p> TNN Jun 4 , 2017 , 09.34 AM IST <p> Representative image <p> AHMEDABAD : The Gujarat Environment Management Institute will come out with its policy to facilitate research and development activities in the field of environment , depending upon current requirements of the state.The GEMI has decided that they would on an average facilitiate 35-odd studies in the field of air and water pollution every year . <p> Sanjiv Tyagi , director , Gujarat Environment Management Institute , says " It has been decided that under the policy each researcher will get a maximum of Rs2 lakh . " <p> " The institute has not kept any age barrier for grants under the policy . Even professors or their students can undertake any research , and the only criteria is that the institute from which the student or professors hails should be from Gujarat , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is one of the very few centres in the country that provides solutions for pollution problems related to water , air and soil , " said Tyagi . <p> " It has chalked out an extensive water , air and land conservation plan and acts as a referral institute , " he said , adding , " Accordingly , GEMI has mandated that it would also act as an R &amp; D Centre . The funds for such activity will come from both government funding and from the institute 's own corpus . " <p> Officials said that research which attempts to solve any environmental and ecological problem perceived to be important for the state would be given more weightage .
