<h> Model United Nations to test students ' ability <p> TNN Jun 21 , 2017 , 03.57 AM IST <p> Nagpur : The way the United Nations ( UN ) functions , addressing concerns of member countries and taking decisions in the interest of global community , will come alive at Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering on Saturday and Sunday . Students will be witness to the nuances of the UN working at a two-day international conference organized by Nagpur International Model United Nations ( MUN ) . <p> The Model United Nations will provide students an opportunity to get an insight into and understand how top diplomats argue cases of the countries they represent , take position on the issues of international importance like terrorism , climate change , global economy and gender equality . Representatives of various colleges of India and abroad will rub shoulders with each other and replicate how proceedings of the UN assembly are held during the Nagpur conference . <p> Started two years ago , MUN is an organization that gives students exposure to international diplomacy . Around 15 committees have been formed for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ find a solution to the problems the world today faces . The committees at the conference will be on the lines of GA DISEC , SPECPOL , ECOSOC , UNHCR , WHO , HRC , UNSC , Lok Sabha , UNESCO , SOCHUM , UN Women , OMEN , NATO , IAEA , India-Canadian meet and international press . <p> Secretary General of Nagpur MUN Rutwik Joshi told TOI , " Delegates at the conference will come fully prepared to discuss the issues listed on the agenda of the respective committee . The event will thus test writing and oratory skills of the participating students as they will deliberate on a variety of issues from the global perspective . " <p> Vinay Ajmera , hospitality in-charge of Nagpur MUN , said , " With the hope of creating future world leaders , the event will open up many opportunities for the delegates . " <p> The aim is to create awareness about world issues and broaden people 's viewpoint .
