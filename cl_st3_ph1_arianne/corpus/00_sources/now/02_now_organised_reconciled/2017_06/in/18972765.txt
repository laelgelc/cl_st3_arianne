<h> With US out of Paris deal , can India take the lead ? <p> First published : June 14 , 2017 , 7:04 PM IST Updated : 15 hours ago <p> US President Donald Trump created global uproar by pulling out of the Paris Climate Change Accord . While making the announcement , Trump said India and China have " benefitted " under the Paris deal . He even claimed that under the Paris deal , India had the freedom to double its coal production -- which is simply not true . This gives India and China the opportunity to become a leader in the fight against Climate Change . Can India rise to the occasion ?
