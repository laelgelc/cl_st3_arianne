<h> Coastal zone plan for Goa may be delayed <p> Panaji : The implementation of the coastal zone management plan ( CZMP ) for the state is likely to be delayed with the state government asking the National Centre for Sustainable Coastal Management ( NCSCM ) to conduct " ground truthing " to verify the accuracy of coastal maps , along with effecting other six corrections . <p> The Chennai-based institution had submitted the new draft plan to the Goa government on May 9 and NCSCM representatives had made constituency-wise presentations of the draft coastal zone management plan to MLAs and ministers , who strongly objected to the draft plan . <p> A senior government officer told TOI that seven areas of concern were raised before the director of NCSCM by the department of environment , in a letter . " These concerns have to be addressed and this will delay the finalization of the coastal plan for Goa , " the officer said . <p> The government has asked for water areas of the coastal regulation zone ( CRZ ) IV to be clearly demarcated as sea , lagoons @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
