The GCF has been set up to help developing countries adapt to climate change and move to low-carbon technologies . With defunding GCF and other international climate change-related initiatives , Trump had already reneged on his commitments to UNFCCC and the Paris agreement . <p> So , today 's decision was a formal death blow to the agreement .
