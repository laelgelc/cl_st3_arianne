<h> Make the Planet Great Again : Macron Takes A Jibe At Trump <h> File photo of French President Emmanuel Macron . ( Reuters ) <p> Paris : French President Emmanuel Macron said Donald Trump had made an historic error Thursday by abandoning the Paris climate agreement , and urged frustrated US climate scientists and entrepreneurs to come and work in France . <p> And cheekily adapting the nationalist slogan used by Trump on his election campaign trail , Macron urged defenders of the climate to " make our planet great again . " <p> In a TV address broadcast both in French and English , Macron said he respected Trump 's decision , " but I do think it is an actual mistake both for the US and for our planet . " <p> " Climate change is one of the major issues of our time . It is already changing our daily lives but it is global , " Macron said . <p> " Everyone is impacted and if we do nothing our children will know a world of migrations , of wars of shortage , a dangerous @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ourselves , it is not the future we want for our children , it is not the future we want for our world . " <p> Referring to Trump 's idea of redrawing the 2015 accord , he said , in the French version , " we will not in any way renegotiate an agreement that is less ambitious " than the present one . <p> " I reaffirm clearly that the Paris Agreement remains irreversible and will be implemented not just by France but by all the other nations , " Macron vowed . <p> He added : " To all scientists , engineers , entrepreneurs , responsible citizens who were disappointed by the decision of the president the United States , I want to say that they will find in France a second homeland . <p> " I call on them , come and work here with us , to work together on concrete solutions for our climate , our environment . " <p> Macron said that he would move swiftly to define " a common strategy and to launch new initiatives " with France 's partners . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , because wherever we live , whoever we are , we all share the same responsibility : make our planet great again , " said Macron , tweaking the " Make America great again " slogan promoted by Trump .
