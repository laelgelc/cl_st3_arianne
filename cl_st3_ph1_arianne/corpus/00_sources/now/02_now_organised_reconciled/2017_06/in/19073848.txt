Even if emissions are aggressively reduced , the percent of the world 's human population affected is expected to reach 48 percent . <p> " We are running out of choices for the future , " said Camilo Mora , associate professor of geography in the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawai ? i at Manoa and lead author of the study .
