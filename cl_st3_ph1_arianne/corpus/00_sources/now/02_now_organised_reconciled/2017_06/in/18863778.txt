A research team led by Dr Jan E. Janecka , a professor of biological science at Duquesne University in Pittsburgh , PA , conducted the first range-wide genetic assessment of snow leopards by analysing the DNA in scat samples collected on wildlife trails and other areas frequented by the cats . <p> Janecka and team determined that there are three primary " genetic clusters " of snow leopards , each of which qualifies as its own subspecies : a northern subspecies in the Altai region , a central subspecies in the core Himalaya and Tibetan Plateau regions and the eestern subspecies in the Pamir , Tian Shan and trans-Himalaya mountain ranges . <p> The researchers also discovered , based on their DNA analysis , that snow leopards experienced what 's called a " genetic bottleneck " or " population bottleneck " about 8,000 years ago , meaning their population size and genetic diversity were greatly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ warmer and the tree-line was shifting to higher elevations .
