<p> Can conservative intellectuals think straight about climate change ? Back in 2014 , I argued that their fixation with the so-called global warming " pause " demonstrated most of them could not . After 2014 , 2015 , and 2016 all came in as the hottest years ever recorded , each one breaking the previous record ( and the last by a huge margin ) , I checked back in to see whether such people -- like TheNew York Times ' Ross Douthat -- had recanted and admitted their previous error . As of a few weeks ago , he had not . <p> Finally , Douthat has returned to the subject and admitted fault on the since-vanished pause . But he has made no change to his underlying view on climate change , still subscribing to what he calls " lukewarmism , " a belief that climate change is real , humans are causing it , but it wo n't be as bad as the greens say .
