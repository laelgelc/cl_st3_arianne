<h> Kewaunee County raises awareness of Lyme disease <p> KEWAUNEE COUNTY , Wis . ( WBAY ) - Kewaunee County is stepping up efforts to raise awareness about Lyme disease . A sharp increase in cases of the tick-borne illness prompted county health officials to take action . <p> At Bruemmer Park in Kewaunee County , new signs are being installed along the Ahnapee State Trail . <p> " We never used to get a call from people that would say ticks are in the area or ' My dog has a tick on it , ' but we 're getting that pretty regularly now , " Kewaunee County Public Health Director Cindy Kinnard said . <p> Kinnard says after averaging around three cases of Lyme Disease each year , that number shot up to ten last year . <p> " We really want people to know that it 's not just a northern Wisconsin thing any more , " she said . " I think that climate change is helping to bring some of that closer to our area .
