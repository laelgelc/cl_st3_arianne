<h> The crack has grown 11 miles in a week as scientists keep researching how climate change is affecting Antarctica <p> Scientists say that a major crack along one of the largest ice shelves in Antarctica will soon break off , creating an iceberg that 's approximately the size of Delaware , according to Reuters . While it is not an immense cause for concern , it is contributing to a broader trend in Antarctica that scientists are worried stems from man-made greenhouse gas emissions , and could significantly increase sea levels . <p> In just the past week , the crack as spread an additional 11 miles and " fits within a broader trend of shrinking ice shelves in the region , which scientists believe is linked to global warming , " Reuters reported . Only eight miles of ice now sit between the potential iceberg and the open ocean .
