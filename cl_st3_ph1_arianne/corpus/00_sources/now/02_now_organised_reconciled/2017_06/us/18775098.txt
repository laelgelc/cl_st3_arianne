But he also retweeted two items related to the economy : A tweet of his son 's about the Dow Jones industrial average hitting a new high Thursday and one from his friends at " Fox and Friends " that offered that same bit of data , with a twist . <p> " Wall Street hits record highs after Trump pulls out of Climate pact , " the headline reads , and that 's true .
