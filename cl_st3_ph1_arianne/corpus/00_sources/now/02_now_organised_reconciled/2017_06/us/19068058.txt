<h> Mixed conifer and beech forests complement each other in the use of resources , unless rainfall is low <h> June 20 , 2017 <p> Complementarity between Scots pine and broad-leafed species in the use of available resources such as water may increase the growth of mixed forests comprising both species compared with pure forests with only one . However , the lack of rainwater reduces this advantage in species that , like the Scots pine , do not tolerate shade , because increased competition for water does not allow them to compete for light . <p> On the other hand , the effect of growing in a mixed forest is less important for highly competitive and shade-tolerant species like the beech .
