Seventy-four percent of those surveyed said they have no confidence in Trump . <p> Compared to the final years of Obama 's presidency , Trump received higher ratings in just two of the 37 countries surveyed -- Russia and Israel . <p> Loading ... <p> The Trump administration is aiming to improve relations with Russia .
