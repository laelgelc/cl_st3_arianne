( Nasdaq:CRAY ) today announced the Company has been awarded a contract with the National Institute of Water and Atmospheric Research ( NIWA ) valued at more than $18 million to provide NIWA and its partner , the New Zealand eScience Infrastructure ( NeSI ) , with three Cray supercomputers - two Cray ? XC50 ? supercomputers and a Cray CS400 ? cluster supercomputer . <p> Headquartered in Auckland , NIWA is New Zealand 's largest and preeminent provider of climate and atmospheric research , and freshwater and ocean science .
