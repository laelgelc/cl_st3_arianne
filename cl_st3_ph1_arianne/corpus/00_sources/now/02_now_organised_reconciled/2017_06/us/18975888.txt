<h> A decade of leadership <p> Since taking office in July 2007 , Harvard President Drew Faust has spearheaded a sweeping expansion of financial aid , opening Harvard 's doors to many more qualified applicants , and has championed increased diversity and inclusivity on campus . She oversaw the re-establishment of ROTC programs , the creation and naming of the Harvard John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences , and the launch of the record-setting Harvard Campaign . She promoted innovation in the ways the University pursues teaching , learning , and research across fields and disciplines , and she shepherded the " One Harvard " approach to collaboration and excellence among Schools and programs . Under her tenure , the University expanded its global impact while renovating and reinvigorating its physical spaces at home , with improvements ranging from the Harvard Art Museums to the Smith Campus Center to the residential Houses , along with imagining and beginning major campus additions in Allston . <p> Faust announced that she will step down as president on June 30 , 2018 .
