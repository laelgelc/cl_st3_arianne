<h> Trump is leaving Paris climate agreement even though majority of Americans in every state supported it <p> President Trump decided to pull out of the Paris climate agreement on Thursday even though a majority of Americans supported it . <p> According to a climate note posted by the Yale Program on Climate Change Communication earlier this month , more than 50 percent of residents in each U.S. state supported the Paris Agreement on climate change , which was originally signed in April 2016 . <p> " Majorities of Democrats ( 86% ) and Independents ( 61% ) , and half of Republicans ( 51% ) say the U.S. should participate ( including 73% of moderate/liberal Republicans ) , " the Yale Program said . " Only conservative Republicans are split , with marginally more saying the U.S. should participate ( 40% ) than saying we should not participate ( 34% ) . " <p> Even Trump 's base was skewed toward participating in the deal .
