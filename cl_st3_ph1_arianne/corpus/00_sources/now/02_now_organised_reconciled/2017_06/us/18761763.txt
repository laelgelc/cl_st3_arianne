It undermines America 's standing in the world and threatens to damage humanity 's ability to solve the climate crisis in time . But make no mistake : if President Trump wo n't lead , the American people will . <p> Civic leaders , mayors , governors , CEOs , investors and the majority of the business community will take up this challenge .
