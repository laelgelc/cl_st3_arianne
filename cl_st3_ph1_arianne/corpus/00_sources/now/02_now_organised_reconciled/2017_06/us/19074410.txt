<h> Kauai mayor signs proclamation in support of Paris Agreement <p> Kauai County Mayor Bernard Carvalho on Monday signed a proclamation in support of the Paris Agreement , a move that comes a few weeks after President Donald Trump announced he was pulling the United States out of the climate accord . <p> " The impacts of climate change can be seen all over the world and we need leadership to address this major challenge , " Carvalho said . " Although Kauai is a small island , we believe it is our responsibility to take a leadership position on climate change mitigation , and we are strongly committed to staying on course to build a more sustainable and resilient future for our community and beyond . " <p> Carvalho , who signed the proclamation at the Kauai Energy Conference , said the county has already been preparing for the impacts of climate change for several years . He cited examples such as changing out the county 's street lights to LED lights -- an effort that would save taxpayers approximately $400,000 annually -- as well as efficiency improvements at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the county fleet . <p> Last week , Carvalho also joined the Mayors National Climate Action Agenda -- comprised of nearly 200 mayors across the nation -- in an effort to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Earlier this month , Hawaii became the first state to formally enact portions of the Paris Climate Accord pledges into law after Gov . David Ige signed two bills aiming to make Hawaii a greener place to live . <p> " As an island state , we are especially aware of the limits of our natural environment . We see the impacts of our actions , " Ige said , at the ceremony . " In this day and age , it is time for states and governors to lead . " <p> The Paris Agreement aims to bring participating countries together to address global climate change in concert . Under the accord , the United States pledged to cut greenhouse gas emissions 26 to 28 percent below 2005 levels by 2025.
