They discussed the fight against climate change and how China and California could work together to wage it . <p> " It 's highly significant that the governor of California can meet with the President of China and talk about the foremost issue of our time , " Brown told CNN . <p> Though Brown and Xi did n't directly discuss the Paris accord , Brown told an audience at a forum in Beijing that climate change could be more dangerous than the threat of fascism during World War II @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Paris accord " crazy . But Xi 's meeting with Brown could easily be interpreted as a thinly veiled message to the Trump administration : China believes climate change is a problem and does n't think the US is doing enough to solve it . <p> On its face , it might not seem odd that Xi would meet with Brown .
