<h> Geoffrey Norman : The End of the World ? ... ( Or Worse , Maybe ) <p> President Trump has pulled the country out of the Paris arrangement on doing something about climate change . It is his most provocative action so far and that is saying something . But , then , if something is n't done to halt the advance of climate change -- or at least slow its pace -- then the planet is doomed . This , anyway , is the most extreme take . Less apocalyptic scenarios predict flooded coastlines , desertification what is now farmland , and wars between desperate , starving nations . <p> You can still find skeptics who think that these scenarios are overstated , that the nature of climate change is not well understood and , even that it is a fiction . But these are increasingly marginalized views . The consensus is that climate change is real . That it comes with great risks .
