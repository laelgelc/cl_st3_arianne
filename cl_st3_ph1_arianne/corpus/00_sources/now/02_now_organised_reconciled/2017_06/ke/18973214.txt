According to some estimates these volcanoes and hot springs could supply up to 25% of the state 's energy needs . Alaska is well known for its seismic activity and many volcanoes . <p> State officials are ready to begin auctioning off exploration rights , the first being Mount Spurr , a 3.4-km ( 11,000 ft ) tall snowcapped stratovolcano in the Aleutian Volcanic Arc of Alaska . Another volcano considered for exploration is the Augustine stratovolcano , which resides near Anchorage and reaches 1.3 km ( 4,300 ft ) in height .
