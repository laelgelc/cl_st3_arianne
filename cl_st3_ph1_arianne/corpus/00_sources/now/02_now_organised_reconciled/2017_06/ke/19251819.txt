<p> THE AGRICULTURAL Development Corporation ( ADC ) has spelt out strategies to combat effects of climate change in order to avoid losses . <p> The ADC farms ' managing director Richard Aiyabei said that the corporation is planning to irrigate more than 5,000 acres in Trans Nzoia to avert the effects of climate change . <p> " We are also looking at different agro-ecological zones in order to relocate crops that require a lot of water to places where there is enough water and enough rainfall and those that require little rainfall are planted in areas that are receiving less , " he said . <p> Other measures that the corporation will take include diversifying into a variety of crops other than maize and investing in farm machinery to encourage planting without much cultivation in order to preserve enough moisture for the growth of crops . <p> The MD noted that climate change effects were being felt , saying it had plunged the corporation into debts making it difficult to pay its workers . <p> He gave an example of how windstorms and hailstorms experienced in 2014 destroyed maize @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ corporation to pay its workers . <p> The MD added that the government has recently released over Sh200 million to the corporation to pay its 25,000 workers dues that had accumulated since 2000 when the company was facing financial challenges . <p> He said workers salaries had accumulated to over Sh100 Million . <p> " Some of the workers retired without the pay but we have records which we shall use in reaching all of them to ensure every one receives his or her salary to live better lives , " he added . <p> While addressing workers from Namandala farm during the issuance of cheques on Monday , Mr Aiyabei urged them to utilise the funds well .
