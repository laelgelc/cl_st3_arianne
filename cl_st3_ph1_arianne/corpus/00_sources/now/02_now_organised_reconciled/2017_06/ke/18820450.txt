<h> Organic , regional and ' old ' <h> Traditional vegetable varieties new at Alnatura <p> Now , at the beginning of the harvest , Alnatura is once again offering " old " vegetable varieties from regional and seasonal organic farms , this time with an even more comprehensive range of 15 products . In cooperation with the nonprofit organisation ProSpecieRara , which is committed to the preservation of endangered crops and the preservation of biological diversity , all Alnatura natural supermarkets offer a regionally specific range . Tomatoes , courgettes , radishes , aubergines , kohlrabi or potatoes : all 15 traditional vegetables are characterized by their intense taste and sometimes unusual colors and shapes .
