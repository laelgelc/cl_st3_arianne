<h> Follow ThinkGeoEnergy on Social Media <h> News <p> Costa Rica and Japan sign $240 million loan agreement for the 55 MW Berinquen I geothermal power project , the third of Costa Rica state power utility ICE . <p> As reported today from Costa Rica , the country signed a loan agreement with Japan for $241 million in loan funding of the Borinquen I geothermal plant . <p> This was reported by state-owned Costa Rican Electricity Institute ( ICE ) . the developer of the project . When ready the plant will be the third geothermal power plant of Costa Rica , with the existing Miravalles and Las Paillas . <p> The loan was officially signed in a ceremony at the office of the President of Costa Rica . The loan from Japan will be complemented by additional loans from the Inter-American Development Bank and ICE . The plant is expected to add 55 MW in power generation capacity when it is to enter commercial operation in 2023 . <p> The loan was granted by Japan as part of a loan granted in 2013 for the development of geothermal projects @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2014 .
