<h> Natural disasters in Peru result of climate change : WWF <p> LIMA - The natural disasters that have blighted 11 regions of Peru in recent weeks are the result of climate change , which has been intensifying worldwide for decades , WWF 's director in Peru , Patricia Leon-Melgar , highlighted Saturday . <p> In an interview with Radio Programas del Peru , Leon-Melgar pointed to the impact of the El Nino phenomenon , which has brought torrential rains , overflowing rivers , floods and landslides , as being caused by global warming . <p> She said that the planet 's glaciers have lost 40 percent of their coverage and that traditional rain patterns have shifted , changing sowing and harvesting seasons . <p> According to the WWF-Peru representative , the intensification of the climate change process means the scientific community is less able to predict weather phenomena . <p> Leon-Melgar warned that , should this climate degradation process continue , humanity would face more droughts , forest fires and floods . She called on urgent prevention efforts to be made . <p> El Nino has caused at least @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 20 missing and around 800,000 people affected . <p> She added that a lack of urban planning and prevention had worsened the situation in Peru , causing great damage to homes , highways , fields and public infrastructure . <p> Peru 's National Institute of Civil Defense has updated its numbers to show 153,329 homes have been damaged , as well as 1,250 schools , 509 bridges and 7,500km of roads .
