( Contributed Photo ) <p> THREE electric cooperatives in Negros Occidental have noted a total 10.7 megawatts ( MW ) reduction in power consumption during the Earth Hour on Saturday night , March 25 . global movement initiated by the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) that demonstrates a commitment to fighting climate change . <p> The Central Negros Electric Cooperative ( Ceneco ) , which serves
