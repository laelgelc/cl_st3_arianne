<h> Ayala Land readies its properties for Earth Hour <p> Ayala Land , Inc . ( ALI ) is preparing all of its properties nationwide for this year 's Earth Hour by consistently going beyond the switching-off of lights , and integrating energy efficiency , water efficiency , and solid waste management into its day-to-day operations . While Earth Hour is held only once annually , the company has embedded sustainability into the year-round planning , design , and management of its properties nationwide . <p> Through its subsidiary Ayala Property Management Corporation ( APMC ) for instance , ALI has effectively lowered its energy intensity ( kwh per square meter of space ) by 38 percent from 2011 to 2015 . This was achieved through retrofits , redevelopment , and other interventions which include the replacement of old light bulbs with LED bulbs , and investments in new air conditioning technologies . <p> These avoidance and efficiency measures reduce unnecessary load on the national grid , and also lead to cost savings which may then be reinvested into the overall sustainability effort . Last December , 10 APMC-managed properties @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bonifacio High Street , were recognized by the Department of Energy through the Don Emilio Abello Energy Efficiency Awards . The year prior to that , APMC-managed malls and offices saved Php 131.5 million by avoiding 16,409 MWh of electricity consumption . <p> Tower One &amp; Exchange Plaza in Makati gets ready to switch off for Earth Hour . <p> " While eco-efficiency is an everyday endeavor at Ayala Land properties , we fully support activities such as Earth Hour that help us engage with all our stakeholders and encourage them to join us in our commitment to sustainability , " said APMC President Laurent Lamasuta . <p> APMC also recently partnered with ABS-CBN Lingkod Kapamilya Foundation Inc. to recycle and sell APMC 's used oil and used lead acid batteries . Revenues from the partnership will help fund environmental initiatives such as the reforestation of the La Mesa Watershed , and the rehabilitation of the La Mesa Ecopark . <p> ALI is the Philippines ' leading developer of sustainable estates , offering a diverse mix of properties such as residential , retail , office , hotels , and leisure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ As part of its sustainability efforts , it recently announced that it is embarking on an aggressive plan to reduce the greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions in its commercial properties and to aim for carbon neutrality by 2022 . The company will achieve this through a combination of initiatives , including passive cooling design , energy efficiency , renewable energy sourcing , and carbon offset mechanisms such as forest regeneration and protection . <p> Earth Hour 2017 will be on 25 March , Saturday , at 8:30 PM Local Time.The event originated from Sydney , Australia in 2007 as an attempt to conserve energy and electricity and combat global warming . Participants are asked to simultaneously shut-off lights and unnecessary electrical items for one hour .
