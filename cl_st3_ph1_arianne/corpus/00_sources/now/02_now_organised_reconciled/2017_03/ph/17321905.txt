<h> Follow Us <h> DA conducts consultation for FY 2018 budget <p> CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY , March 9 -- With citizen engagement as a vital component in the process of providing a responsive budget allocation , the Department of Agriculture -- Regional Field Office 10 ( DA-RFO 10 ) conducted a consultation of the proposed plan and budget for FY 2018 with its partner Civil Society Organizations ( CSO ) and stakeholders . <p> In his message , DA-RFO 10 Regional Director Carlene C. Collado reiterated the importance of the consultative event in practicing good governance and transparency within the department . <p> " This consultation will serve as an avenue to have a better output for our budget that could answer the present needs of our farmers in the region , " Dir . Collado said . <p> In comparison to the present FY 2017 as per approved through the General Appropriations Act ( GAA ) of P1.649 billion , DA-RFO 10 aims to increase its budget allocation to P4.908 billion for FY 2018 . <p> With bigger appropriation , Dir . Collado is optimistic that it will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to make food available and affordable for all , increase income of farmers and fisherfolks , and increase the resilience to climate change risks . <p> An open forum on issues and concerns also transpired during the course of the discussions which were deliberately responded by the Assistant Regional Director Roxana H. Hojas , Field Operation Division ( FOD ) chief Carlota S. Madriaga and DA programs and projects focal persons . <p> The CSO consultation was done in adherence to the principles of constructive engagement crafted by the Department of Budget and Management ( DBM ) which integrates citizen participation in fund preparation and allocation . <p> The proposed budget is yet to be presented to technical hearings and is subject for reviews before approval . Along this process , the amount is still subject to change .
