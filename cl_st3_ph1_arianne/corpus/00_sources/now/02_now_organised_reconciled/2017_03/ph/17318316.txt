<h> Youth as climate leaders take center stage in Earth Hour 2017 <p> This year 's Earth Hour switch-off event will be held on March 25 from 8:30 to 9:30 p.m. , with the celebration focusing on the youth 's role as future climate leaders , the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) Philippines said . <p> WWF said the main switch-off event in the country would be held at the SM by the Bay at the Mall of Asia compound in Pasay City from 4 p.m. to 10 p.m . The Earth Camp featuring interactive climate adaptation and mitigation booths starts at 4 p.m. while the program proper begins at 7 p.m . <p> WWF urged Filipinos , particularly young people , to participate in the annual switch-off event to raise more awareness on climate action .
