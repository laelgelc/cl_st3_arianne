<h> CLSU students develop ICT app vs. climate change effect to crops <p> SCIENCE CITY OF MUNOZ , Nueva Ecija -- Information Technology ( IT ) students of the Central Luzon State University has developed an ICT-based application that can analyze crop needs , from planting to post-harvest , while mitigating the effects of climate change . <p> Called Crop++ , the application can monitor the current temperature and humidity of a farm area through a temperature sensor . <p> It can also detect movements within the area and capture photos to identify the presence of pests . Its soil moisture sensor is used for water sufficiency for better resource management . <p> The collected information are then analyzed and interpreted by a web-based information system and monitored through a computer . <p> The developers , namely Jerome Rivera , Angeliko Sevilla , Aliyah Ventanilla with their coach , Mike Christian Gragasin , have expressed hope that this technology will help farmers become efficient in their farm management , crop production , and eventually , increase their yield . <p> With this innovative application , the developers won a five-month @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ improve and expand their prototype , and a cash prize worth PHP10,000 in the recent 3rd AgriHackathon , an application development competition held at Philippine Rice Research Institute ( PhilRice ) here . <p> The winning applications from the first two Agrihackathon events have been turned into projects , which are being used in PhilRice 's researches . <p> " This initiative aims to ensure that ideas from the academe are not only kept as research papers in schools but are also applied in the real world for the future of our country 's agriculture sector , " Nehemiah Caballong , PhilRice ICT expert , said .
