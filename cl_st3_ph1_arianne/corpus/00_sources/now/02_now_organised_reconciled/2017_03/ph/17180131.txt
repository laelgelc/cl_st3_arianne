<h> Solidum is new DOST undersecretary <h> Willard Cheng , ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA -- Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology ( Phivolcs ) director Rene Solidum has been appointed undersecretary for Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Affairs at the Department of Science and Technology . <p> DOST Secretary Fortunato Dela Pe ? a said Solidum will continue to lead Phivolcs as officer-in-charge . <p> " Dr. Solidum Jr . brings with him years of experience in disaster risk reduction and management particularly in the field of geological hazards like earthquakes and tsunamis , " the DOST said in a statement . <p> " As Phivolcs director , Dr. Solidum Jr . initiated several projects that include the following : improved volcano monitoring system , tsunami warning system , earthquake monitoring system and the Rapid Earthquake Damage Assessment System , a software that can produce seismic hazard and risk maps before and immediately after an earthquake , " it added .
