<h> Eco-park in Ifugao showcase solid waste management <h> Eco-park in Ifugao showcase solid waste management <p> Thursday , March 02 , 2017 <p> KIANGAN , Ifugao -- The pilot Ecological Park located at Barangay Bolog is now completed and ready to serve its purposes . <p> The project costing P314,500 is a joint endeavor between the barangay constituents , the personnel of the Alternative Learning System ( ALS ) of the Department of Education and their learners and the municipal government of Kiangan through its Ecological Solid Waste Management Program ( ESWMP ) fund . <p> The eco-park is riprapped with bottle bricks , motor and jeepney tires and beautified with plastic vases , vases made of brown and white papers and hanging plants . Seats out of drums and water containers are established under the trees or shaded areas for anyone to feel the freshness and beauty of nature . <p> This collective effort among the different stakeholders is designed to pioneer the eco-park as the community show-window in the implementation of the ESWMP that will motivate not only Barangay Bolog but adjacent barangays to replicate the project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the ESWMP activities in response to the pursuit of sustainable development through proper waste segregation , collection , transport , storage , treatment and disposal of solid waste through the formulation and adoption of best practices in solid waste management . <p> It is also intended to promote best practices in solid waste management activities in the barangay , broaden the knowledge and understanding of the residents , school personnel and barangay officials in proper waste management and encourage resource conservation , recovery to promote greater public participation . <p> This way , they will be capacitated to reduce , re-use , recover and recycle items in order to help solve the garbage problem of the community , protect and preserve the environment to mitigate the impact of climate change . <p> Municipal ESWMP focal person Ernesto Baglan and ALS facilitator Wilma Pumihic shared the eco-park will also present best practices in waste diversion among other best practices possible and a source of information to strengthen solid waste management in the area as the school , municipal and barangay local government units will ensure its maintenance and improvement through the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of all stakeholders .
