<p> Branding himself as " Fault Finder " , being a renowned geologist who studies the different fault systems in the country , Dr. Renato U. Solidum Jr . was sworn in by Science Secretary Fortunato T. de la Pe ? a as undersecretary for Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation effective February 27 . <p> Solidum will continue to head the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology ( Phivolcs ) as officer in charge . Solidum brings with him years of experience in disaster risk reduction and management , particularly in the field of geological hazards , like earthquakes and tsunamis . <p> As Phivolcs director , Solidum initiated several projects that include the following : improved volcano-monitoring system , tsunami warning system , earthquake-monitoring system and the Rapid Earthquake Damage Assessment System , a software that can produce seismic hazard and risk maps before and immediately after an earthquake .
