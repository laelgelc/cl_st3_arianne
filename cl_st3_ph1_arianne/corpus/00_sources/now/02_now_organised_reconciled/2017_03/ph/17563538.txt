<p> According to an annual report published on Wednesday by China 's State Oceanic Administration , average coastal sea levels in 2016 were up 38 millimeters compared to the previous year , and saw record-breaking highs in the months of April , September , November and December . <p> " Against the background of global climate change , China 's coastal air and sea temperatures have soared , coastal air pressure has fallen and sea levels have also soared , " it said . <p> It warned that high sea levels would lead to problems like coastal erosion as well as more frequent and severe typhoons . <p> It added that vulnerable coastal regions needed to step up their flood prevention efforts by improving drainage systems and building dykes and dams . Underground water extraction also needed to be cut in order to ease the risk of subsidence . <p> China 's coastal waters have risen 3.2 millimeters per year since 1980 , higher than the global average increase over the period .
