<h> Agriculture seen as No 2 environmental problem <p> Agriculture-related issues are seen as the second-biggest environmental problem for New Zealand , a Lincoln University survey shows . <p> Results of the 2016 Public Perceptions of New Zealand 's Environment survey were revealed on February 17 in an 82-page report by Profs Ken Hughey , Geoff Kerr , and Ross Cullen . <p> Asked to identify the most important environmental issues facing New Zealand today , 31.1% of survey respondents said ' ' water related ' ' , 9.9% said ' ' agriculture related ' ' , 8.8% said ' ' greenhouse gases , climate change and ozone ' ' , and 8.4% said ' ' waste ' ' . <p> The survey is carried out every three years .
