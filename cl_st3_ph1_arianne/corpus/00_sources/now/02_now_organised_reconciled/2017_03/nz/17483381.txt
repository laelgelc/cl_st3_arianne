A decade ago , when publisher Craig Potton asked New Zealanders to pick their favourite local poem , Hone Tuwhare 's Rain topped the poll . It is a meteorological love story in 85 words . <p> But rain -- the stuff that falls from the skies , as opposed to the poem -- has become something of a four-letter-word around our place this month .
