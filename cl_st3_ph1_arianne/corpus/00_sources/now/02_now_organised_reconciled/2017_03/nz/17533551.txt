Whenever someone is diagnosed with cancer , GDP goes up . Whenever someone drives a car to work rather than walks , GDP goes up . GDP captures " official " economic activity , no matter if it is good and bad , and does n't capture a whole lot of other activity like raising kids , helping your neighbour , or biking to school . <p> New Zealand has been using GDP as the primary measure for progress since 1948 but , in many ways , GDP is deeply flawed . Since the 1980s , New Zealand 's GDP has more than doubled , yet so too have our child poverty rates . One in 10 kids growing up in New Zealand were poor in the late 1980s .
