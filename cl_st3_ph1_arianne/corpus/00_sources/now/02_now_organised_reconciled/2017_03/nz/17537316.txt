<p> The event is attended by members of the oil and gas industry , with the Government due to announce the 2017 Block Offer to the industry - an annual tender of petroleum exploration permits . <p> Delegates wait outside the conference . ( Briar Marbeck/Newshub ) <p> The protesters , however , are demanding an end to all new oil and gas operations . <p> " Oil is wrecking our climate and we need to wean ourselves off oil , " Amanda Larsson , campaigner for Greenpeace told Newshub at the protest . <p> " The oil industry was hoping to come here and quietly make plans to drill more oil on land and sea here in New Zealand . We 've shone a spotlight on that and not let them make those plans quietly , because we 're here to say that if we 're to have a future , oil can have no future . " <p> The protest is being co-ordinated by the People 's Climate Rally , who say it 's significant that the event is being held in Taranaki . <p> " Taranaki has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ personal impacts from oil and gas activities including drilling , flaring and fracking . For many Maori this is seen as a continuation of colonisation , " spokesperson Emily Tuhi-Ao Bailey said in a statement issued by the People 's Climate Rally . <p> Minister of Energy and Resources Judith Collins was due to give the opening address and announce the Block Offer at 9am . <p> She tweeted that the protesters preventing the kaikaranga from entering the venue , but Greenpeace and Shaun Keenan , the leader of Ngati Te Whiti 's delegation , say that 's not true . <p> " Greenpeace has stopped Ngati Te Whiti kaikaranga from entering the Petroleum Conference . #totally disrespectful " , Ms Collins tweeted , referring to the woman who calls visitors onto a marae or venue at the beginning of a powhiri . <p> The Petroleum Exploration and Production Association of New Zealand ( PEPANZ ) said it is " disappointed " in the protest action , but were not surprised . <p> " We want to use the Conference to celebrate Taranaki 's oil and gas industry and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than it being targeted by protestors to cause disruption to the community , " says chief executive Cameron Madgwick . <p> " We expect protestors to respect the rights of our delegates to attend the conference . " <p> In a media statement sent out this morning , Ms Collins said New Zealand is well-placed to take advantage of oil and gas exploration . <p> " The Government will continue to ensure we reap the benefits of petroleum and mineral exploration while adhering to strong environmental and health and safety provisions , " she said . <p> The total area up for tender is 481,735 km2 -- with 5,102 km2 onshore and 476,632 km2 offshore . <p> PEPANZ said the climate change is a serious issue , but " we simply can not switch off fossil fuels overnight . " <p> Protesters did not allow Newshub to enter to building to cover the Block Offer , forcing the crew to sneak in through another entrance .
