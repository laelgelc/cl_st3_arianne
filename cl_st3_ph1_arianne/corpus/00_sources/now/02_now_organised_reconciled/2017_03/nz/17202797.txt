<h> Branson to be interviewed by Key in Auckland <p> Former Prime Minister John Key and Sir Richard Branson at a 2007 party in Auckland . Photo/File <p> Sir Richard Branson is coming to New Zealand and will be interviewed by former Prime Minister John Key at a black-tie event . <p> On March 26 , Branson will speak at a charity dinner at Auckland 's Vector Arena to an expected 1500 guests . <p> The event will be the Virgin Group founder 's second major public speaking event in New Zealand . <p> During 2011 he spoke at a fundraising gala dinner for Outward Bound during the charity 's 50th anniversary . <p> Key will also interview Branson for a second time , after conducting a Radio Live interview in September 2011 while Branson was in the country for the Rugby World Cup and gala dinner . <p> Branson said he was " looking forward to visiting New Zealand again and participating in the event " . <p> The Briton and Key are expected to discuss the challenges and opportunities of the 21st century . <p> The businessman will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Newshub journalist Samantha Hayes will MC the event , with entertainment by the Peter Urlich Swing Quartet . <p> The event is projected to raise at least $300,000 for Virgin Unite , Branson 's global philanthropic effort to create a better world . <p> The non-profit charity was founded in 2004 and promotes entrepreneurship , business solutions to climate change , creating disruptive collaborations , changing business for good and promoting human dignity . <p> Branson founded the Virgin Group in 1970 , and Forbes currently lists the 66-year-old 's wealth at more than NZ$7 billion . <p> The philanthropist also serves on the Global Commission on Drug Policy and supports ocean conservation with the Ocean Elders . <p> Those wanting tickets for this month 's event , which are being sold from $595+GST , can join a waiting list at www.ducoevents.com.
