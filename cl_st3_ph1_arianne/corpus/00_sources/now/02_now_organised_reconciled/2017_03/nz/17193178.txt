<p> DropVkal Family , a local ni-Vanuatu band made up of unemployed male youth living in the same neighborhood , is making its mark in the music and social activism scene . <p> They play mainly acoustic reggae , rock and roots music . <p> Their vision is for the group to promote local vernacular languages and be an alternative to mainstream full-set band reggae popular among youth groups in Port Vila and throughout Vanuatu . <p> DropVkal Family has been performing at bars and restaurants around Port Vila . They play regularly at Coconut Palms Resort on Saturday nights and Anchor Inn on Sundays . <p> " We 're a group of friends from different islands of Vanuatu that have come together because of our love of music , and our interest in contributing to the development of Vanuatu and its special local culture , " says musician Carlos Noronha . <p> " The band is evolving and is inclusive . It is a product of the merging of the bands DropVcull Groove and Family Roots .
