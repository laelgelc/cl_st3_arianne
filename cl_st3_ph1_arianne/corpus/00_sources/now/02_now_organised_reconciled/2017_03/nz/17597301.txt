A lot more rainfall in the Bay but in intense bursts , rather than spread out which means more droughts and more floods . " <p> Mrs Hallett said a good way to start combating the issue was to look after existing native forests and plant more trees , everywhere . <p> " If you have good forests in place , that helps control the way water is moved through the environment and help buffer against those droughts and floods . <p> " If rain falls on hard ground , it washes straight over it but if you have rain on a forest , scrub , any form of vegetation , the fall of the rain is broken , slowed down and the water slowly makes its way down through to the ground .
