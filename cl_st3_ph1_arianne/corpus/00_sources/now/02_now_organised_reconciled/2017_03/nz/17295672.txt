<h> Gareth Morgan 's Opportunities Party on the hunt for candidates <p> The Opportunities Party leader Gareth Morgan says his party will make case-by-case decisions on whether to stand in an electorate . <p> Millionaire economist Gareth Morgan 's political party has put out a call for candidates after officially registering with the Electoral Commission . <p> Morgan says The Opportunities Party ( TOP ) will decide on a case-by-case basis whether to stand in an electorate , and " political careerists " need not apply . <p> He had previously indicated the party would be list-only , but said in January he had been " approached by some pretty outstanding candidates " who wanted to fight for electorate seats . <p> In a statement on the party 's website on Wednesday , Morgan said it was " on the hunt for people with a genuine commitment to build a prosperous nation founded on a non-negotiable principle of fairness " . <p> " We 're not entering Parliament to make up the numbers , to tread water and mark time .
