<h> Solar boreholes , irrigated crops throw lifeline to Kenyan herders <p> At the only borehole with water for miles around , the troughs are under siege in Saretho village as hundreds of camels , cattle , sheep and goats await their turn . <p> On the other side of the solar-powered well , women and children fetch water for household use , loading 20-litre jerry cans onto donkeys or dragging them off home . <p> On a single day , thousands of livestock drink here , while hundreds of people collect water . <p> Two years ago , the scene would have been different . Without the solar-powered pump they have now , the villagers found the local boreholes difficult and costly to operate . <p> But since the Kenya Red Cross installed a solar system in 2014 , they no longer have problems getting water . <p> The village of Saretho , which lies between the eastern town of Garissa and Dadaab , is home to around 6,000 people and the local water supply is more than enough for them -- even as swathes of the country are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ serves thousands of other livestock that flock from neighbouring areas . <p> Abdi Ibrahim , a former chief of Saretho and chair of the borehole committee , said the pump was previously powered by a diesel generator - but it often malfunctioned , especially in the heat . <p> " During droughts ... we did n't pump water often and most people used to drive their cattle for many kilometres , " he said . <p> To operate and repair the generator was costly for villagers who had to pay for fuel and sometimes a mechanic , said Ibrahim . <p> The solar power system means they no longer incur the bulk of those expenses . They still use the generator at night but recoup some of the money by levying fees . <p> " We charge Sh10 for every camel , four shillings per cow and one shilling for every goat and sheep , " said Ibrahim . <p> According to Red Cross project officer Saidi Katana , around 65 solar panels were installed for the project . <p> " This enables the borehole to pump 32 cubic metres @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ensures that the residents of Saretho never lack water , " said Katana . <p> Fruit &amp; veg <p> Elsewhere in the county , a group of former pastoralists have taken to growing fruit and vegetables .
