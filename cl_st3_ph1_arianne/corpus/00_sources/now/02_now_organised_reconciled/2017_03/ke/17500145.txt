<p> The Enapuiyapui swamp , it is believed , hides a large snake whose lithe body emerges after several days ' swim in Lake Victoria . <p> The 13-acre swamp is the source of two major streams : Nyangorese and Amara , which eventually join and form the Mara River . <p> It is also the source of Molo River that flows up to Lake Baringo and Njoro River , which flows into Lake Nakuru . <p> That swamp has now fallen ill . <p> The snake tale belongs to the surrounding Maasai and the Ogiek community , who fear it will rear its ugly head soon and wreak havoc as the swamp dries up . <p> In the past decade , Enapuiyapui has been exposed to uncontrolled human activities , which now threaten its very existence . <p> It 's growing smaller and could dry up if nothing is done to protect it . <p>
