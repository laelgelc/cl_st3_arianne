<p> When it powers down next week , the Hazelwood plant in Victoria 's Latrobe Valley will be the ninth coal-fired power generator to close its doors since 2012 . <p> No one should be surprised . The miracle is more that it 's been able to pump out cheap and dirty baseload power for a decade longer than anyone expected . <p> Built in the 1960s , Hazelwood is no longer the marvel of modern technology it once seemed . In fact , we 've known since 2005 that Hazelwood is the bottom of the class globally , rated as the least carbon efficient power station in the OECD . <p> Sensibly , the Federal Government has refused to peddle false hope and quickly slapped down last-minute , economically irrational pleading to keep it clunking along . <p> Why ? <p> Because , like an old car , Hazelwood is beyond its use-by date . It 's dangerous . <p> Five of Hazelwood 's eight boilers are desperately in need of major repairs . The deadline for an extensive and expensive revamp expires on April 1 .
