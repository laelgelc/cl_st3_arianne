This development is reminiscent of the so-called " legal fiction " involved in corporate personhood , a deliberate hallucination that 's meant to uphold the rights of groups and to smooth business processes . <p> Like corporate personhood , the notion that lifeless natural objects are persons could lead to some strange and undesirable outcomes .
