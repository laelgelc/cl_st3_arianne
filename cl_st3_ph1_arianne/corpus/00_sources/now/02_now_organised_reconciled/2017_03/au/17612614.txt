<h> Drought decimates crops threatening millions of lives in East Africa <h> Published : 26 March 2017Image credit : whoishussain.org <h> Relief agencies across the world are appealing for help as famine threatens the lives of more than 11 million people across East Africa . <p> This is the equivalent of half of Australia 's population -- all of whom are on the brink of famine and could soon run out of water . <p> Add to this at least another 10 million people who are running short of food . Somalia , South Sudan , Ethiopia and Kenya are all effected . With little media coverage , many people are unaware of the scope of this potential catastrophe . Rural communities can be difficult to access , which has been hampering rescue efforts . <p> Climate change has been blamed for the devastating drought which has plagued the area for years , destroying crops and killing millions of livestock . Conflict and economic breakdown are also at fault . <p> It has been 6 years since famine has been declared anywhere in the world .
