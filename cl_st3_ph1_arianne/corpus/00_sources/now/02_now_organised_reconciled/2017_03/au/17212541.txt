<h> Hit pause for the World Day of Prayer <p> Today we come together for the World Day of Prayer to question " Am I Being Unfair to You ? " <p> World Day of Prayer was established in the 1920 's by Christian women in US and Canada to gather women , men and youth for prayer . Its purpose is to highlight issues that affect women and also society as a whole . Each year there is a new theme and host country , with an estimate of 16 million Christians participating in this event over the last six years . This year World Day of Prayer is held in the Philippines where women are subject to various forms of oppression . This includes sex-trafficking , domestic violence and women being viewed as subordinates . Some of these issues affect women worldwide especially domestic violence . <p> This year 's theme is derived from Matthew 20:1-16 where Jesus tells a parable about the kingdom of heaven .
