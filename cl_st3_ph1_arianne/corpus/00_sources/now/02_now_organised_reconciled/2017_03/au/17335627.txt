<h> Victorian climate law shows limits of Paris targets <p> With the passage of the Climate Change Act ( CCA ) that mandates a target of zero net emissions by 2050 , Victoria is formally in the leadership among state and federal governments . <p> If the response of Victoria 's climate denying Coalition opposition is anything to go by , the Victorian government must be on the right track . The LNP voted against the climate bill , adding to its opposition to the state 's renewable energy target , which it declared only weeks earlier . In light of the wrecking tactics that state and federal Coalition are using against climate action , a clear state target to initiate action is obviously welcome . <p> The CCA is a framework for action , not an exhaustive list of actions . It specifies a 2050 carbon emissions ' target to be achieved with interim targets set at five-year intervals .
