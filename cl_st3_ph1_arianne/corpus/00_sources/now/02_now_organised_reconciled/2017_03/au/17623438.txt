<p> University of New South Wales researchers have made a discovery that could lead to a drug that actually reverses aging - and could even help NASA get its astronauts to Mars . <p> The team have identified a critical step in the molecular process that allows cells to repair damaged DNA . Their experiments in mice suggest a treatment for DNA damage from ageing and radiation that is so promising , it attracted the attention of NASA , which believes the treatment can help its Mars mission . <p> While our cells have the capability to repair DNA damage - which happens every time we go out into the sun , for example -- their ability to do this declines as we age . The scientists identified that the metabolite NAD+ , which is naturally present in every cell of our body , has a key role as a regulator in protein-to-protein interactions that control DNA repair . <p> Treating mice with a NAD+ precursor , or " booster , " called NMN improved their cells ' ability to repair DNA damage caused by radiation exposure or old age @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ months . <p> " The cells of the old mice were indistinguishable from the young mice , after just one week of treatment " said lead researcher Professor David Sinclair of UNSW School of Medical Sciences and Harvard Medical School Boston .
