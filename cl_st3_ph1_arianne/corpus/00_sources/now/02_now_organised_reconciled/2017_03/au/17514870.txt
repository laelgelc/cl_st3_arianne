<h> Billionaire banker David Rockefeller dies in New York home aged 101 <p> Billionaire banker and philanthropist David Rockefeller has died aged 101 . <p> Mr Rockefeller died at his home in Pocantico Hills , New York , a family spokesman confirmed . <p> He died in his sleep due to congestive heart failure at his home in Pocantico Hills , just north of New York City , spokesman Fraser Seitel said . <p> The last living grandson of Standard Oil co-founder John D. Rockefeller , he led Chase Manhattan , now part of JPMorgan Chase , in the 1960s and 1970s . Forbes magazine ranked Mr Rockefeller 581st on its annual list of billionaires released Monday with a fortune of US$3.3 billion . <p> His accomplishments in finance included opening the first Moscow offices of an American bank and the first in mainland China after President Richard Nixon 's 1972 visit there . He also oversaw the growth of Chase Manhattan 's operations in the Middle East , Latin America and Japan , Seitel said . <p> The banker was also well known in political circles in the United @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from close to 100 countries and was known as an outspoken champion of American capitalism . <p> He famously helped persuade then-President Jimmy Carter to allow the shah of Iran into the United States for cancer treatment in 1979 . The move was widely seen as spurring the Iranian revolution led by the Islamic fundamentalist Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini . <p> Among his most important civic roles , Mr Rockefeller served as chairman of the Council on Foreign Relations and executive committee chair of the Museum of Modern Art , which his mother founded . <p> The Rockefeller Foundation mourned " the loss of great man and philanthropist , " president Rajiv Shah said in a statement . <p> " David was one of the world 's foremost advocates for the power of partnership and collaboration , " he said . <p> " Long before it became popular wisdom , he believed effective partnerships across sectors and geographies was the only way to affect lasting change .
