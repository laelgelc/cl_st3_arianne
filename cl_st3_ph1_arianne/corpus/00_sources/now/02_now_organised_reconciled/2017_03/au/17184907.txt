<h> Trump poised to lift federal coal ban <p> US President Donald Trump will target a handful of Obama-era green regulations , including a federal coal mining ban and an initiative forcing states to cut carbon emissions , in an executive order as soon as next week , a White House official tells Reuters . <p> Trump and his fellow Republicans who control Congress are seeking to unravel former Democratic President Barack Obama 's initiatives to combat global climate change , which they say are costly for US business and have hampered drilling and mining without providing any clear benefits . <p> " Rescinding the federal coal leasing moratorium is part of that executive order , which has lots of different components , including the Clean Power Plan , " the White House official , who asked not to be named , told Reuters on Wednesday .
