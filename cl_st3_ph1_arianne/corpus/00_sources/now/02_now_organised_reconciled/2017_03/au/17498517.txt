<h> Columbia University Starts Dumping Coal Investments <p> Columbia University has joined the growing ranks of higher learning institutions to begin divesting from coal . <p> The University 's Trustees announced last week it would be dumping investments in companies that derive more than 35% of their revenue from the production of thermal coal . <p> Additionally , the University will begin participating in the Carbon Disclosure Project 's Climate Change Program . <p> " Divestment of this type is an action the University takes only rarely and in service of our highest values , " said University President Lee C. Bollinger . " Clearly , we must do all we can as an institution to set a responsible course in this urgent area . " <p> The University was also urged by the Trustees to do more towards lightening its own carbon footprint . Something the University does n't have a lot of ( or any it seems ) is solar energy -- but that will start to change soon .
