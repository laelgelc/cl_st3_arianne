<h> Farmers to be told to innovate for exports <p> Published : 3:33 pm , Sunday , 5 March 2017 <p> One of the key industries to contribute to last week 's strong economic growth figures is holding its annual get-together in Canberra to find better ways of capturing even more opportunities in a fast changing world . <p> The Australian Bureau of Agricultural and Resource Economics and Sciences ( ABARES ) two-day Outlook 2017 conference starts on Tuesday , bringing together experts from the agricultural , forestry and fisheries sectors , as well as economists and parliamentarians . <p> Its key emphasis this year is on innovation at a time of expanding global markets and diversifying consumer preferences . <p> It also comes at a time of great uncertainty in the global economy . <p> Head of Rabobank financial markets research for the Asia Pacific Michael Every will tell the conference of the volatility of global politics following the election of US President Donald Trump , although he says markets appear ' strangely serene , for now ' . <p> ' Trump appears intent on rolling back much of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Every told AAP . <p> ' At the very least he is strongly opposed to how free trade has been conducted over the past few decades , with China and Mexico in his crosshairs .
