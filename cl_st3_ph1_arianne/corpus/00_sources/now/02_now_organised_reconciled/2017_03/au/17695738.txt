<h> Environmental litigation ' going to happen ' <p> Earlier this week , an expert panel discussed the prospect of climate change litigation in Australia and internationally . It found that climate change is a serious liability for companies , and that a major environmental litigation case is not far away . <p> The Climate Change Liability panel discussion , held at Herbert Smith Freehills ' Sydney office on Tuesday , featured four experts in environmental law and litigation . HSF partner and head of environment , planning and communities Peter Briggs , class actions partner Jason Betts and London-based energy partner Silke Goldberg were joined by eminent silk Noel Hutley SC .
