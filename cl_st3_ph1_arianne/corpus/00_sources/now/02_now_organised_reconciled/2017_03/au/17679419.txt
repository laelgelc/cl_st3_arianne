<h> Lack of clean water threatens millions : UN <p> LONDON -- Nearly 27 million people in Nigeria , Somalia , South Sudan and Yemen do not have access to safe water , which can be as deadly to severely malnourished children as a lack of food , UNICEF says . <p> Last month the UN said more than 20 million people -- equivalent to almost the entire population of Australia -- risk dying from starvation within six months in the four countries due to conflict and climate change . <p> ' Unsafe water can cause malnutrition or make it worse , no matter how much food a malnourished child eats , he or she will not get better if the water they are drinking is not safe , ' Manuel Fontaine , UNICEF director of emergency programmes , said on Wednesday . <p> ' The combination of malnutrition , dirty water and poor sanitation sets off a vicious cycle from which many children never recover .
