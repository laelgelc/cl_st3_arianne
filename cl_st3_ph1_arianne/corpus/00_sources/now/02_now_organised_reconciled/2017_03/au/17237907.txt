<p> I have been the senior minister at St Barnabas Broadway , in Sydney 's inner city , for six years . Before that I was on staff for another eight . <p> In all that time , not one unmarried woman has shared with me that she has fallen pregnant , or sought an abortion . <p> This terrifies me . <p> I think -- I fear -- this means that there is something in our church culture that promotes , and actually increases , the number of women seeking abortions . <p> What I think is happening is that young couples are having sex out of marriage , young women are falling pregnant , and abortions are being procured . <p> There is an established Christian tradition of viewing the pregnancy , from point of fertilisation , as a vulnerable , emerging human being . <p> The purpose of this article is n't to argue this claim , or the case against abortion . <p> However , it is important to understand that many Christians believe that , except in the case of danger to the mother , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ abortion is both harmful to the mother and unjustly ends an emerging human life . <p> Anecdotally at least ( we 've never taken a survey ) , this is the view of most of members of my church . <p> You might think , then , that St Barnabas is a rare case of religious non-hypocrisy .
