<h> News <h> UNDP help for Clarendon communities <p> Mike Henry ( left ) , minister of transport and mining and Member of Parliament for Clarendon Central , accepts a copy of the United Nations Development Programme 's ( UNDP ) 2016 Human Development Report from the organisation 's resident representative , Bruno Pouezat , at the launch of the report on Tuesday at the Wembley Centre in Hayes , Clarendon . Henry has hailed the UNDP for earmarking a number of Clarendon communities for assistance . They will be receiving development support to lessen the impact of climate change , increase access to water supply , improve agricultural output , and upgrade educational institutions .
