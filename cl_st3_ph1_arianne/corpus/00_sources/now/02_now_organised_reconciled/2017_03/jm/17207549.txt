<h> News <h> CARICOM , Korea commit to areas of closer cooperation <p> KINGSTON , Jamaica ( CMC ) -- The Caribbean Community ( CARICOM ) and the Republic of Korea have agreed to several areas for closer cooperation . <p> The decision was made at the first high-level meeting held here on Monday . <p> In a joint statement issued at the end of the meeting both sides committed to further strengthen their ties of friendship and spirit of cooperation . <p> They also agreed to reinvigorate a Consultation and Cooperation Mechanism , which was established in 2006 to guide future CARICOM-Korea political and economic relations . <p> In relation to some key issues before the meeting , the delegates underscored the serious impact of climate change and global warming especially on Small Island and low-lying Developing States ( SIDS ) like those in the CARICOM Region . <p> They committed to work together to enhance capacity building and knowledge sharing , while exploring avenues for cooperation on projects aimed at combating climate change and its impacts . <p> They also acknowledged the CARICOM Region 's ongoing high dependence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ promote cooperation
