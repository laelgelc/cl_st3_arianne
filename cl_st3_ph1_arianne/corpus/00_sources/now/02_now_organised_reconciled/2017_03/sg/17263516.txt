<p> The Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) has appointed Dr. Renato U. Solidum Jr . as its Undersecretary for Disaster Risk Reduction and Climate Change Adaptation . <p> He was previously the Director of Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology ( PHIVOLCS ) . In that position , Solidum initiated projects that led to improved volcano monitoring system , tsunami warning system , earthquake monitoring system , and rapid earthquake damage assessment system that is able to produce seismic hazard and risk maps before and immediately after an earthquake . <p> He will continue to head PHIVOLCS as Officer-In-Charge . <p> " Dr. Solidum Jr . brings with him years of experience in disaster risk reduction and management particularly in the field of geological hazards like earthquakes and tsunamis , " said DOST in a media statement .
