<h> AP FACT CHECK : Some Trump boasts stumble , but jobs do grow <p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- Some of Donald Trump 's boasts from the first weeks of his presidency were dashed by developments in recent days . For example , builders of the Keystone XL pipeline were let off the hook from a buy-American requirement that Trump had promised . <p> On another front , though , there 's now some substance behind his cherry-picking claims that jobs are growing under his watch . A robust jobs report gave him a fresh load of cherries . <p> Over the past week , Trump took credit when it was not always due and assigned blame that was misplaced .
