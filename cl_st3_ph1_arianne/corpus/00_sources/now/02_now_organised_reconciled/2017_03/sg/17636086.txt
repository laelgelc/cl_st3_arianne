They are largely responsible for carrying out the day-to-day activities required by pan-national programmes and agreements , such as finding homes for migrants . Moreover , it is in cities that social cohesion is most evident and where high concentrations of diverse peoples means that intercultural cooperation has to succeed . As such , the future of Europe is heavily dependent on the success of its cities . <p> Europe has many achievements to be proud of : enduring peace , greater wealth and solidarity .
