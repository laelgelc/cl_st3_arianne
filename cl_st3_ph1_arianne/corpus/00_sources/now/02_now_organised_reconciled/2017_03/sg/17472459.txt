<h> Berlin takes firm line over Trump 's aim to tax German cars made in Mexico <p> BERLIN * Germany could file a suit against the United States at the World Trade Organisation ( WTO ) over President Donald Trump 's proposed border tax , Berlin said yesterday , ahead of a meeting between the leaders of the two countries later in the day . <p> Mr Trump has warned that the US will impose a border tax of 35 per cent on cars that German automaker BMW plans to build at a new plant in Mexico and export to the American market . <p> Asked how Germany would react to the proposed tax , Economy Minister Brigitte Zypries told Deutschlandfunk public radio it was very difficult because of the complexities of such a tax system .
