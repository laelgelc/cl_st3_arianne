<p> " Everybody talks about climate change , but what are we doing to make a change ? We , at The Blue Yonder , believe that it is important to actually do something , rather than preaching about it , " says Gopinath Parayil , founder of the tourism organization . <p> An organization that attempts to promote responsible tourism , The Blue Yonder has come up with an alternative to plastic straws , bamboo straws . <p> Bamboo , easy to grow and a sustainable grass , was The Blue Yonder 's answer to doing their extra bit for the environment . Since over a year , they have been giving straws made of bamboo shoots to their guests , who are delighted by the idea of using environment-friendly products . Many travellers also treat the straws as a designer souvenir and keep them . <p> And how do they manage to get hold of so many bamboo shoots ? Through their tree-planting initiative called Travellers ' Forest . It was in 2009 that they began the initiative . They asked each traveller to plant at least one @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planting exercise expanded to other places , including to lands owned by private individuals . Through this , the initiative encouraged the concept called one-metre forests . <p> Started in 2004 , The Blue Yonder attempts to provide meaningful travel experiences for their travellers . Speaking to TNM , Gopinath recounts how the organization began to give bamboo straws to their guests . <p> " Earlier we never used to provide our guests with straws when they were given tender coconuts . However , there would be a few in every group who would always ask for a straw . The idea of making straws out of bamboo came when we visited a friend 's place in Thrissur last year . Although initially we tried making straws out of papaya , they turned out to be bitter . So , we switched to bamboo , " Gopinath says . <p> Commercializing the idea <p> After TBY spread the word about bamboo straws , they have been flooded with inquiries , Gopinath sounds overwhelmed . <p> " We have been getting orders from hoteliers , restaurants and showrooms asking to supply @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not rocket science , it is easy and sustainable , " he adds . <p> Although Gopinath is glad that the practice will now be replicated in other parts of the country and urges people to realize its potential , he insists that his organization is not keen on commercializing the idea .
