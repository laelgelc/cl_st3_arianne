<p> The administration has unveiled President Donald Trump 's first budget , including a proposed 31% cut in funding to the Environmental Protection Agency . The cuts would remove funding for the Clean Power Plan and scrap all climate change research programs and partnerships . White House budget director Mick Mulvaney confirmed on Thursday that the new administration had no interest in funding to combat climate change , saying : ' We 're not spending money on that any more . We consider that to be a waste of your money '
