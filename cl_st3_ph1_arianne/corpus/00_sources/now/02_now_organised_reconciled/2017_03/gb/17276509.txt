<p> Researchers investigating the risks posed by climate change on the sustainability of fish and shellfish in the Irish Sea are being given more than ? 6m in EU funding to carry out two projects . <p> It is hoped the projects will help protect and develop the marine life and fisheries industry in Wales and Ireland . <p> Around ? 4.7m will support the Bluefish marine science partnership , led by Bangor University in partnership with Irish and Welsh organisations . <p> It will investigate how climate change is affecting the health of fish stocks , the migratory movement of commercial fish , and risks from new non-native species . It aims to develop solutions to help fisheries businesses adapt to environmental changes and capitalise on new commercial opportunities . <p> Dr Shelagh Malham , senior research fellow at Bangor University 's School of Ocean Sciences , said the research " will ensure these vital industries receive the information and support they need to be more resilient to the changes the industry is facing " .
