<h> Nuclear and the politics of energy security <h> Most of the EU 's nuclear fuel needs are met , directly or indirectly , by Russia <p> Nuclear appears to be back in vogue . Here at home , after a protracted debate , work is about to start on a new fleet of nuclear plants that could in the future provide as much as 35 per cent of the UK 's electricity . <p> Huge construction programmes are already going on in the developing world . China is currently , in some form , planning no fewer than around 200 nuclear power plants , while India is busy going about ensuring 25 per cent of its energy is supplied via nuclear fission by 2050 . <p> Alongside environmental concerns , a key consideration in relation to nuclear is political in nature and to do with energy security .
