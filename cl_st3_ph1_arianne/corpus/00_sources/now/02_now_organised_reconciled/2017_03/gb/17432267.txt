<p> The progressive loss of the Arctic 's sea ice due to climate change is increasing the intensity of Beijing 's winter air pollution , finds a study published in the journal Science Advances . Both the loss of ice in the Arctic and increased snowfall across Eurasia have affected China 's winter monsoon season . Such changes are due to climate change , and their combined effects are said to be leading to increasingly stagnant air in China , trapping pollution in the country 's major cities . <p> The study focused on haze in the area around Beijing , the East China Plains .
