<p> Theresa May could come under pressure to curb the foreign aid spending <p> Dr John Constable , head of the Renewable Energy Foundation , told the Daily Telegraph : " Overseas development projects have long been plagued with expensive failures , but the current crop of climate change and renewable energy initiatives are showing strong signs of being the most negligently conducted and scandalously wasteful to date . " <p> The Department for International Development insisted the renewable energy projects are large infrastructure programmes that will take time to yield results , while much of the money is paid out in the form of loans . <p> A Government spokesperson said : " The Climate Investment Fund is helping provide the world 's poorest people with stronger defences to extreme weather which can cause life-threatening crises such as floods , droughts and famine . <p> " The UK 's investment is already delivering results by producing reliable sources of food , improving infrastructure and giving people access to clean energy , and all programmes are on course to meet the published performance targets for 2023. "
