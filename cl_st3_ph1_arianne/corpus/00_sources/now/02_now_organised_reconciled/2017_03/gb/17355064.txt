The Batagaika crater is the largest of them all , exceeding 50 hectares in area . It is also 86m deep . <p> Slumps and megaslumps are scattered across Siberia , Alaska and Canada . Because trees shade the soil from the summer heat , this had the effect of increasing the amount of heat entering the soil . Trees also transpire , which has the effect of cooling the soil , so when their number was reduced , this had an impact on the soil 's temperature . The permafrost started thawing from the top down . <p> There was a gully that extended down a slope , it started deepening soon after the trees were cut down and the permafrost started to melt , leading to the creation of the megaslump . <p> The crater is likely to keep expanding in coming years.Courtesy of Julian Murton <p> Murton told IBTimes UK : " The formation of the megaslump was triggered by human disturbance but it 's symptomatic of climate warming -- where the climate warms you get more permafrost thawing .
