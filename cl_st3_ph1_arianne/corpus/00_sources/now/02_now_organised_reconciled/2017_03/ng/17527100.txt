<h> World Bank To Invest $57bn In Nigeria , Others <p> The World Bank Group president , Mr Jim Kim , said the bank would be investing 57 billion dollars to accelerate growth and development in Sub-Saharan African countries over the next three years . <p> In a statement released to journalists by the Senior Communications Officer , World Bank Nigeria , Mrs Olufunke Olufon , yesterday in Abuja , Kim said the money would be raised by three subsidiaries of the group . <p> According to Kim , the funding will run from July 1 , 2017 , to June 30 , 2020 . <p> " The bulk of the financing , 45 billion dollars will come from the International Development Association ( IDA ) , the World Bank Group 's fund for the poorest countries . <p> " The financing for sub-Saharan Africa will also include an estimated eight billion dollars in private sector investments from the International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) , a private sector arm of the bank group . <p> " Four billion dollars in financing , will come from the International Bank for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " he said . <p> Kim said the move presented an opportunity to change the development trajectory of the countries in the region , including Nigeria . <p> " With this commitment , we will work with our clients to substantially expand programmes in education , basic health services , clean water and sanitation , agriculture , business climate , infrastructure and institutional reform . <p> " The IDA financing for operations in Africa will be critical to addressing roadblocks that prevent the region from reaching its potential . <p> " To support countries ' development priorities , scaled-up investments will focus on tackling conflict , fragility , and violence ;
