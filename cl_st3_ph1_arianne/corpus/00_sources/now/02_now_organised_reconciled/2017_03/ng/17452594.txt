<h> Tech Here 's how climate change is already affecting your health , based on the state you live in <p> Climate change is already beginning to wreak havoc upon the planet . In the short term , we 're facing more winter storms , miserably hot summers , and a longer allergy season . In the long term , entire coastlines will likely disappear , threatening communities and wildlife . <p> But just how is all of this affecting you -- your state , your coastline -- right now ? <p> A new report from the Medical Society Consortium on Climate and Health reveals that different geographic regions in the US are facing a range of effects , many of which are already taking shape today . Some of them are as geographically specific as to affect only one state . <h> Heat , heat , heat <p> Climate change lengthens summer months and makes them hotter and more humid . During these episodes , it 's more likely that people will suffer heat-related illness like heat stroke or dehydration .
