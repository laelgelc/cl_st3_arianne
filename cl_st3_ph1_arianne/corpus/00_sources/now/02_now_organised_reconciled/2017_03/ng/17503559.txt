" <p> Commenting on this development , World Bank Group President Jim Yong Kim , said : " This represents an unprecedented opportunity to change the development trajectory of the countries in the region . With this commitment , we will work with our clients to substantially expand programmes in education , basic health services , clean water and sanitation , agriculture , business climate , infrastructure and institutional reform . " <p> The IDA financing for operations in Africa will be critical to addressing roadblocks that prevent the region from reaching its potential .
