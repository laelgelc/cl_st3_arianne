<h> Foundation decries slow pace of work on Nigeria 's side of GGW Project <p> Ede Dafinone , the Chairman , Nigerian Conservation Foundation ( NCF ) , National Executive Council ( NEC ) , has advised the Federal Government to involve stakeholders in the building of the Great Green Wall Project . Dafinone gave this advice in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Lagos on Thursday.The Great Green Wall is an African-led project with an epic ambition to grow an 8,000km natural wonder of the world across the entire width of Africa . Its goal is to provide food , jobs and a future for the millions of people , who live in a region on the frontline of climate change . According to Dafinone , work on the Nigerian side of the Great Green Wall project is very slow which is not encouraging . <p> " The Great Green Wall project progress is not good enough .
