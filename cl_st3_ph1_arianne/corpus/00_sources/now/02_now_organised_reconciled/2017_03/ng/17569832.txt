<p> Mr Micheal Osakuade , Acting Director , Department of Forestry , Federal Ministry of Environment , says Nigeria should make concerted efforts to promote sustainable utilisation of its forests for national development . <p> Osakuade said this while speaking with News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Abuja on Thursday . <p> He said that forests served as a depository of energy and carbon sequestration as well as an avenue for scientific and educational research . <p> " We need to mobilise all stakeholders for massive action against drivers of deforestation , forest degradation and desertification ; we should also renew our focus on massive planting of trees in our degraded environment . <p> ' ' Nigerians should be committed to ensuring that actions , aimed at climate change mitigation and adaptation , are given due consideration . <p> " They should do this through tree planting and sustainable management of our forest reserves , which we consider as vital . <p> ' ' Forests are the lungs of our land ; they clean the air we breathe and provide fruits and wood for economic gains , ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of fuel wood in Nigeria far exceeded sustainable production and supply from natural and artificial forests , depicting large-scale deficit . <p> " Therefore , it is necessary to address this trend in order to guarantee sustainable utilisation of our forests . <p> " National forests alone can not sustain the growing need for fuel wood consumption in the country , so there is the urgent need for a shift to alternative resources . <p> " It is estimated that Nigeria 's fuel wood consumption is about 87 per cent and the daily consumption by the rural communities is estimated at about 27.5 million kilogrammes per day . <p> " We need to increase the use of cooking gas , liquefied natural gas , while increasing plantation of woodlots in communities or individual farms and avoiding indiscriminate bush burning . <p> " We must endeavour to stamp out illegal tree felling and we should intensify tree planting in urban and rural areas ; this should be emphasised and promoted , ' ' he said .
