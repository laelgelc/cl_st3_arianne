<p> ISLAMABAD : Despite China agreeing to provide $200 million to the Pakistan Meteorological Department to upgrade its weather monitoring system earlier this year , so far no progress has been made in this regard . <p> " Even the PMD has not been officially informed about this new development and the procedure required for procuring the equipment , so far , " an official working closely with the project told The Express Tribune while requesting anonymity . <p> The official said : " Pakistan is under strong influence of climate change ; therefore , considering the gravity of the situation there is a need to upgrade its weather monitoring system on a war-footing . " <p> " This project has already been delayed over six months due to the shortage of funds last year and later this year it has been made part of the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor ( CPEC ) , but still there has been no progress in this regard . " <p> " Though under CPEC the majority of the road construction and power generation projects received funds well on time , this project seems to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " It is because it is not on the priority list of the government . " <p> When contacted PMD chief Dr Ghulam Rasul said so far he had not been officially informed about the latest development , adding earlier this year Federal Minister for Planning , Development and Reforms , Ahsan Iqbal had informed him verbally at an event . <p> Meanwhile , according to the officials of the Ministry of Planning , Development and Reforms : " The project is in its inception phase and funds will be released in later stages .
