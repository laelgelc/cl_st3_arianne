<p> LIMA : Scientists have reportedly grown potatoes in conditions that are similar to the ones present on Mars . <p> Scientists in Peru linked to the US space agency NASA have carried out an experiment trying to grow potatoes in conditions that mimic the Red Planet . <p> The experiment has shown promising results .
