<h> 40m Pakistanis without toilets , 27m without clean water : report <p> LAHORE : More than 27 million Pakistanis do not have access to clean water whereas 40 million are defecating openly due to lack of proper toilets . <p> According to a report issued by World Resources Institute , Pakistan is on 36th number in the list of 180 countries who is facing severe pressures over providing basic necessities of life including clean water . <p> Ministry of Climate Change spokesman Saleem Sheikh said that the report has stated that Pakistan is facing scarcity of water which can be turned into famine in near future . He said the only way to avert drought is timely preservation and right use of water . <p> The report further mentioned that 60 years ago , 50lac litre per person water was available in Pakistan but now the quantity has decreased by five times . <p> More than 39,000 children below age five fall prey to Cholera every year due to dirty water and inadequate cleanliness situation in Pakistan .
