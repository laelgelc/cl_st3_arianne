<p> ISLAMABAD : PPP Senator Farhatullah Babar on Tuesday said the next NFC Award needs to be linked to the implementation of sustainable development goals . <p> He said this at a seminar organised by the Population Council at a hotel in the capital . He proposed that political parties , gearing up for general elections next year , should be urged to make population issues part of their manifestos . <p> " A strong case for national population emergency be made by highlighting the acute water scarcity , worsening climate change , massive internal migration of population and environmental degradation faced by the country , " he said . <p> " The provinces should also set up provincial financial commissions and link the award to regions and districts to the achievements of SDG targets by the local government , " he said . <p> He said since 2002 Pakistan has been making commitments , announced population policy and perspective plans but there has been no tangible progress .
