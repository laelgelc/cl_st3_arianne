<p> Nijimbere put the cost of fighting malaria at $31 million , as she appealed for donations to help fight the disease . <p> She attributed the rise in infections to climate change , increased marshland for rice-growing and the population 's misuse of mosquito nets . <p> Burundi has been plunged into chaos since President Pierre Nkurunziza 's controversial decision in April 2015 to run for a third term . <p> Hundreds of people have been killed and hundreds of thousands of others have fled the country . <p> The crisis also led to a 54 percent cut to the government 's health budget in 2016 from the previous year . <p> " This malaria crisis is even more dramatic because it is striking an impoverished , hungry population that has no resources and for whom even the slightest shock can have life-or-death consequences , " a diplomat told AFP on condition of anonymity .
