<h> Climate change toll <p> Sir : Pakistan is on the 135th number on the list of countries emitting carbon emissions and is moving up on the list of countries most affected by climate change . The country is confronting many climate change challenges in the form of glacier melting , flooding , desertification , sea intrusion , etc . Various international reports highlight that Pakistan is among the world 's top ten countries vulnerable to climate change . Frequency of both drought and flood increased in the recent past causing huge losses in the form of life and materials . <p> According to some media reports , a $36 million Climate Change Adaptation Project responding to Glacial Outburst in Northern Pakistan will benefit 185 million population of the country . The project was approved by Green Climate Fund ( GCF ) during its 14th Board meeting held in Republic of Korea last year . The project was submitted by Ministry of Climate Change , Government of Pakistan , together with UNDP for board 's approval . The project will benefit approximately 700,000 people on average directly in 5 districts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 30 million indirect beneficiaries of whom half are women and girls . The project will thus benefit about 15 percent of the total population of the country estimated at 185 million . It merits a mention that the project will impact the lives of thousands of people who are living in constant danger of periodic glacial outbursts in the Northern Pakistan . <p> It is pertinent to mention another remarkable achievement that is launching of Green Pakistan Programme . The basic outline of Green Pakistan Programme ( GPP ) for revival of forest and wildlife resources in Pakistan is that this programme will be implemented with the support of all provinces and federating units . An allocation of Rs2 billion being made in the current budget for the GPP . In view of the above , it is a commendable step . Therefore , the government must take safety measures to control environmental pollution . Also , the government must control emission of greenhouse gases . The Chief Ministers of all provinces must also fully involve in mitigating pollution in their respective provinces . Also , a comprehensive strategy is needed to plant more trees and conserve forests .
