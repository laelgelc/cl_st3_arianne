Just one in five ( 19% ) said that users should be allowed to do as they wish in VR , regardless of its legality in real life , and over half ( 58% ) think virtual reality should have age restrictions . 41% said that they would be concerned about committing a crime in VR . <p> Alexander Ross , a partner in the Technology team at Wiggin , said " These results are entirely at odds with the huge popularity of violent gaming .
