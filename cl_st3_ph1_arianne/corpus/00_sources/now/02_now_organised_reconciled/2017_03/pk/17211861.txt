<p> KARACHI : Amid a dispute between industrialists and the Sindh Environment Protection Agency ( Sepa ) over the construction of effluent treatment plants , the losers are the citizens and the environment . <p> Industrialists in Karachi have threatened to shut down their industries in the city and eventually move them abroad if Sepa does not stop the action against them . <p> In the last month-and-a-half , the environmental watchdog shut down 30 industries and issued notices to 300 others for lack of treatment plants and releasing untreated effluent and wastewater directly into the Lyari River . <p> According to Jawed Bilwani , spokesperson of the All Karachi Industries Association , industrialists are ready to cooperate with the government but believe the treatment meted out to them is very harsh . <p> Responding to a question about installing effluent treatment plants at each factory , Bilwani told The Express Tribune that the Sindh government had already announced construction of four Combined Effluent Treatment Plants ( CETPs ) in SITE , Korangi , North Karachi and the Super Highway .
