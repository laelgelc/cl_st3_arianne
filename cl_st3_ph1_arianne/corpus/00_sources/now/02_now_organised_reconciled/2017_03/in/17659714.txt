" Despite exaggerated claims , regulatory costs are usually a very small portion of many companies ' cost of doing business . " <p> The oil majors ' annual filings come after the industry and its political allies have spent years criticizing the Obama administration for policies aimed at reducing fossil-fuel consumption , curtailing drilling on federal lands and subsidizing renewable energy . <p> Trump promised during the campaign that a rollback of the Democratic administration 's policies would help free the nation from reliance on imported oil . <p> " Under my @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " said Trump , describing regulation as a " self-inflicted wound .
