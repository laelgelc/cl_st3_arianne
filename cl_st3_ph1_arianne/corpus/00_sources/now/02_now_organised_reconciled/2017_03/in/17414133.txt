<p> Down the winding , narrow alleys of one of the world 's largest informal settlements is a revelation . <p> The Dharavi Transit Camp Municipal School in the teeming metropolis of Mumbai is educating the most vulnerable and excluded children . Those with special needs -- cerebral palsy , autism , as well as hearing , visual and speech impairments -- receive therapy and rehabilitation and join other pupils in regular classes . <p> This example of how the government of India is delivering services to the most vulnerable is a hopeful sign for the country . By making sure that even the most vulnerable have a chance to participate in society , the government is helping the country to benefit from the productive potential of as many of its citizens as possible . <p> Dharavi 's jumble of cramped tenement blocks is proof of this potential . As one of the world 's largest informal settlements , it is also home to countless informal businesses that have an estimated annual turnover of $1 billion . <p> India 's economic dynamism -- even visible in Dharavi -- is part @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ spot in the global economy .
