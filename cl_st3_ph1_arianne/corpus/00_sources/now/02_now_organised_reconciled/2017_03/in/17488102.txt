<h> Climate change causing increase in rainfall in city <h> Share Via Email <p> HYDERABAD : The frequency of high intensity rainfall causing floods in the city , like it happened last year is increasing and will continue to increase , reports a study conducted by researchers from National Institute of Technology-Warangal . <p> In a paper published recently , the researchers also reported that not just frequency of the sudden downpours , but over the years the intensity will also increase sharply . <p> Seriousness of the issue can be gauged from the fact that as per the NIT study , there is a chance that once in two years there will be rainfall measuring 42.85 millimeter per hour(mm/hr) , which can go up to 43.61 mm/hr . <p> The storm water drains in Hyderabad have been designed to handle just 12 mm/hr rainfall , which means that there is a chance that at least once in two years there will be situation of floods due to extreme rainfall in Hyderabad . <p> Larger study on urban flooding being conducted The study was published by NIT researchers as part @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Research Academy under Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology . <p> In other two studies published by the researchers , they reported that there has been 60 per cent increase in El Nino effect on very extreme rainfall during non-monsoon months in Hyderabad city and that the rainfall in June-August months in Hyderabad is following steep increasing trend . Since 1970 , the extreme rainfall occurring over Hyderabad is also increasing . <p> " The frequency of high intensity rainfall has been increasing in Hyderabad due to climate change . It has also been observed that apart from frequency the intensity of rainfall is also increasing . There has been a change in rainfall pattern as well . <p> We have observed that rains are occurring more towards evening unlike earlier when they used to occur uniformly across the day , " Dr NV Umamahesh of the Department of Civil Engineering at NIT-Warangal and one of the researchers said . <p> Lack of enough support from Telangana government The Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre has helped Indian Institute of Science .
