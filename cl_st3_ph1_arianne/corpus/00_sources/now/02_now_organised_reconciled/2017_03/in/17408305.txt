<h> Natural variations cause major chunk of Arctic sea ice loss <p> Washington , Mar 14 ( PTI ) A substantial chunk of summer sea ice loss in the Arctic in recent decades is due to natural variability in the atmosphere , although climate change driven by human activities is still playing a key role , a new study has found . <p> Arctic sea ice in recent decades has declined faster than predicted by most models of climate change , researchers said . <p> Many scientists suspect that the trend now underway is a combination of global warming and natural climate variability . <p> " Anthropogenic forcing is still dominant - it is still the key player , " said Qinghua Ding , a climate scientist at the University of California Santa Barbara . <p> " But we found that natural variability has helped to accelerate this melting , especially over the past 20 years , " said Ding . <p> Previous research found changes in the tropical Pacific Ocean have created a " hot spot " over Greenland and
