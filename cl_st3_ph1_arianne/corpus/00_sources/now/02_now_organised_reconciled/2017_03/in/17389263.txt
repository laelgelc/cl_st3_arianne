<h> Syrian rebels agree to leave Homs ' al-Waer district <p> BEIRUT Syrian rebels have agreed to leave the Homs district of al-Waer with their families over the next few weeks , opposition media said on Monday , with state media quoting the governor of Homs province as saying the same . <p> Opposition-affiliated Orient News reported that negotiators from al-Waer had signed an agreement for rebel factions and their families to go to the area around Jarablus in northern Syria , held by Turkey-backed insurgent groups . <p> The Homs Media Centre , run by opposition activists in Homs , also told Reuters that an agreement had been reached , but said the ultimate destination of the fighters had not been decided . <p> The deal follows other agreements that were never fully implemented between the government and rebel groups in al-Waer , their last bastion in Homs , which has been pounded by air strikes in recent weeks . <p> Fighters and their families would leave in batches for northern Syria , Homs governor Talal Barazi was quoted as saying on state television and by news agencies , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Homs Media Centre said between 10,000-15,000 people would leave in groups over the coming weeks , with an initial batch including about 1,500 people . <p> In September , Barazi said al-Waer was home to about 40,000 civilians and 800 fighters .
