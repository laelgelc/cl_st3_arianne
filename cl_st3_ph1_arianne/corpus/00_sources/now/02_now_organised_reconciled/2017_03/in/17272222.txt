<h> International honour for Indian photographers <p> THE HANS INDIA Mar 07,2017 , 02:54 AM IST <p> Swapnil Deshpande 's work <p> Swapnil Deshpande 's work <p> The judges of the 2017 Sony World Photography Awards , honour the work of four Indian photographers by short listing their work as top 10 in the world in the awards ' Professional , Open and Student Focus competitions . Judges were looking for images representing the finest contemporary photography today . <p> Jayanta Roy from Calcutta , India was shortlisted in the Landscape category of the Professional competition and Mahesh Shantaram from Bangalore , India was shortlisted in the Portraiture category of the Professional competition , both judged upon strong bodies of work between 5-10 images . Swapnil Deshpande from Alibag , Maharashtra , India was shortlisted for a single image in the Open competition 's Travel category and Shravya Kag from Vijayawada , India was shortlisted for a single image answering the brief ' Memories ' in the Student Focus competition . <p> Over 227,000 images from 183 countries were submitted to the 2017 Sony World Photography Awards , and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Student Focus competitions are all announced today . Jayanta Roy , Professional Landscape category Roy 's shortlisted series , ' Himalayan Odyssey ' is an impressive black-and-white series , revealing the beauty and magnificence of Himalayas and expressing his ongoing love for the planets largest and most diverse mountain range . <p> Based in Calcutta , India , Jayanta Roy is especially interested in black-and-white landscape and nature photography . Working around the Indian Himalayas , he is deeply preoccupied with the climate change , which is a major threat for the venerable and fragile landscape . His photography aims to give food for thought and raise awareness of environmental issues . <p> Talking about being shortlisted , Roy comments : " It 's really a great moment of my life ; I feel extremely honoured .
