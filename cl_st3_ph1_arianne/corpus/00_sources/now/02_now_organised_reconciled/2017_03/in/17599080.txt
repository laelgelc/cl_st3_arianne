<h> Scientists warn about heat burst along Kerala coast <p> The scorching wind that led to the wilting of plant leaves at some areas of coastal Kerala in June 2015 , was ' heat burst ' in all possibility , say scientists , who feel this is the indication of the changing tropical monsoon climate <p> TNN Mar 25 , 2017 , 10.46 AM IST <p> Representative image <p> KANNUR : It was in June 2015 that a strange event of scorching wind that led to the wilting of plant leaves was witnessed at some areas of coastal Kerala including Kasaragod , Kannur , Kozhikode , Thrissur , Ernakulam , Alappuzha and Kollam . <p> Though it was earlier thought sea-salt spray or acid rain induced the dehydration of leaf cells , the scientific study of the incident points to the possibility of ' heat burst ' that resulted in an oxidative stress on the leaves , which resulted in the wilting , according to a group of researchers who published a research paper on this subject in the latest edition of the ' International Journal of Earth and Atmospheric @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lightly , it is a major indicator of climate change and we have to take the issue very seriously , and the authorities should ponder as to what steps they have taken in the mitigation and adaptation to face such issues , " said M K Satheesh Kumar , professor at Department of Atomic and Molecular Physics , Manipal University , who conducted the research along with M G Manoj , Jiji Joseph , S Sandeep , K A Sreejith , K M Sunil and K Mohankumar . <p> " What causes the concern is that heat burst is a rare phenomenon , not reported earlier in tropical regions , though it has been reported in countries such as the United States , Australia , Canada , which are outside the tropics , " he said , adding that these are indication of climatic instability . <p> In the backdrop of such rare occurrences , we should think more about climatic conservation , because , the condition is such that anything can happen climatically , added the researcher . <p> " This year also the monsoon is likely to weaken @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2015 , which brought highly sporadic and spatially asymmetric rains over the Kerala coast is not yet over , " he said . <p> Though monsoon onset occurred in June in 2015 , it was not active and uniform over this region in the subsequent leg . Moreover , the rains were characterized by both monsoon and pre-monsoon showers , with frequent lightning/thunder and gusty winds , pointed out the study . In a climatological sense , lightning and thunder are not common during the south-west monsoon season , it added . <p> The wind speed also increased about five times during this event , while the humidity was reduced . A sudden high temperature rise and lowering of moisture content in air wipe out cell water content leading to oxidative stress and wilting , said researcher M G Manoj . <p> The heat burst , accompanied by chemical reactions involving oxidation could enhance the temperature which , in turn , could damage the chlorophyll content , and produce a burning effect to the leaves , he said . <p> " The unprecedented heat burst event could be ringing the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for multidisciplinary research initiatives to explore its impacts on the biota , " added the researcher . <p> Stay updated on the go with Times of India News App .
