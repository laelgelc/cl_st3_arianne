<p> Biodiversity-the contracted form of Biological Diversity has now become a buzzword on social media and other academic websites . The originator of the term Biodiversity ( E.O . Wilson who was the first to publish the term Biodiversity ) would have never speculated that his contracted term will be so popular in the 21st century . Basically it was in last decade of 20th century when there was sense of urgency about the mass scale degradation of environment and its negative impact on flora and fauna . Biodiversity is a major hallmark of the Mother Earth that makes it unique among other celestial bodies . For a layman , Biodiversity simply means variety or variability of living forms , but for an environment conservationist or an ecologist , biological diversity has a deeper meaning . For example , researchers in recent past have shown how decline in diversity affects the disease occurrence by a phenomenon called , Dilution Effect ( Felicia Keesing , 2010 ; Nature ) . Fifty years back we had a rich diversity primarily due to less anthropogenic pressure . Urbanization , industrialization , modernization as well as agricultural activities have put tremendous pressure on the limited freshwater resources , causing eutrophication and pollution of freshwater bodies . Freshwater systems of Kashmir have not remained immune to the anthropogenic pressures and many of these especially those located close to the human habitations , have deteriorated during the last fifty years .
