<p> Decision on wheat duty in final stages of agri ministry 's consideration <p> Saturday , 25 March , 2017 , 08 : 00 AM IST <p> Our Bureau , New Delhi <p> The decision to impose import duty on wheat ( or not ) is in the final stages of consideration by the ministry of agriculture and farmers welfare . This was stated by Shobhana K Pattanayak , secretary , department of agriculture , co-operation and farmers ' welfare , inaugurating a conference on integrated water resource management for increasing farmers ' income , which was organised by the Associated Chambers of Commerce and Industry of India ( ASSOCHAM ) and took place in New Delhi . <p> " However , the ministry has not proposed any increase in the duty . That is under process . Although it is being considered , no decision has been taken , " he added . Pattanayak also stated that the Food Corporation of India ( FCI ) was going to procure wheat on a massive scale .
