" Although Pal has harvested a good paddy crop this January , memory of the sudden tidal surge three years ago remains fresh . <p> An unpredicted high tide broke the embankments of Muriganga River on the night of July 12 , 2014 , and swept through 14 villages spread over 30 sq km . The damage was extensive in the eastern part of the island . It disrupted life for about 25,000 people . More than 4,000 houses were destroyed and some 500 hectares of cropland turned saline .
