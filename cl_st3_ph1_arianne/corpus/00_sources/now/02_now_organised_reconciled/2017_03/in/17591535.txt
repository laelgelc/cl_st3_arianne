<h> Heat takes sap out of Palakkad palmyra trees <p> TNN Updated : Mar 24 , 2017 , 10.33 PM IST <p> Palakkad : The palmyra trees , that define the landscape of Palakkad , are drying up fast causing anxiety and concern on the climatic change and global warming . <p> They are highly drought resistant and can withstand excessive heat . Yet large number of these trees in different parts of the district got dried up causing concern among the people and the scientists alike . <p> The trees growing about 30-metre high is known for surviving extreme hot weather condition . They also survive more than 100 years and are mostly disease-free . <p> Scientists promote it as a key species to combat global warming and climate change . <p> The literature of Palakkad , known as the land of Palmyra , has stories revolving around palm trees by many of its writers . They see it as a cultural heritage and literary giant of the district . <p> Once the tree had a major role in the rural livelihood of the people with many food and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Panikkar of Jana Jagratha , a voluntary and environment organisation . <p> It is a mythical tree that would offer anything to those who stand underneath , it is believed . <p> " The tall palms with their fan-shaped leaves are getting dried up , particularly in the eastern parts of the district in Chittur taluk bordering Tamil Nadu , including Ozhalapathy , Govindapuram , Gopalapuram , Muthalamada , Vadakarapathy , Eruthiampathy and Kollengode , " said Muthalamthode Mani , general secretary , Desheeya Karshaka Samajam . <p> Apart from the shift to other crops due to drought , difficulty in finding tree climbers and the disappearance of the once-thriving palmyra-based industries have spelt the death knell for the Asian palmyra palm ( Borassus flabellifer ) in Palakkad , he said . <p> " Growing up to 30 meters , the hardy species have been known to survive extreme heat conditions and live up to 100 years " , he said . <p> Stay updated on the go with Times of India News App .
