( Reuters ) <p> That human activity is a cause of global warming and changing temperatures is a fact that can not be debated , but until now , scientists had little clue as to what was the extent of damage due to human intervention . A paper published in Nature , based on model simulations of different climate conditions , shows that humans may be responsible for 50-70% change in climate conditions , leading to melting of ice at the Arctic sea .
