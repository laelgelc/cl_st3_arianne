The association also demanded the government to remove seigniorage on rubber trees and provide financial support for soil and conservation facilities as the state is facing one of the worst drought conditions . <p> The association chairman Thomas Jacob said the crop production has come down by 30 per cent in tea , 14 per cent in rubber , 60 per cent in cardamom and 40 per cent in coffee due to drought conditions .
