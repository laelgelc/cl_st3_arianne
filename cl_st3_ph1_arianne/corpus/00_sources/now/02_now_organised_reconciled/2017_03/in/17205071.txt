<h> Kodagu chosen for pilot study of National Forest Monitoring System <p> Forest Survey of India ( FSI ) has chosen Kodagu in Karnataka to implement the theoretical model of the National Forest Monitoring System ( NFMS ) . <p> E Vikram , deputy director , FSI , Deharadun on Thursday said the draft modules prepared by FSI under the NFMS include checking the tree cover , growing stock , carbon footprint , soil erosion and capacity building . " We have chosen one hectare of land in Kodagu for the year long study . The report will be submitted to the Ministry of Environmental and Forests and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) .
