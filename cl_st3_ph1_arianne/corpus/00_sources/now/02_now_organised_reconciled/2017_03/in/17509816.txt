A fourth event after only one year is a major blow to the Reef , " Wachenfeld said . <p> What is bleaching ? <p> Bleaching occurs when abnormal environmental conditions , such as warmer sea temperatures , cause corals to expel tiny photosynthetic algae , which results in draining them of their colour . <p> Algae are essential for corals , as it uses the organic products of photosynthesis to help it grow . The algae is also responsible for their lively colors , as most coral polyp bodies are clear and colourless . <p> The loss of algae makes them more susceptible to disease and would eventually lead to their death . <p> However , if the water temperature drops and the algae are able to recolonise them , coral can recover . <p> What is coral reef ? <p> Coral reefs are large underwater structures composed of the skeletons of coral , which are marine invertebrate animals .
