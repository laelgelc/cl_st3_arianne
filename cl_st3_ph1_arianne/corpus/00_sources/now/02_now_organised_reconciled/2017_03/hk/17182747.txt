<h> Green infrastructure takes flight in Asia <p> It is anticipated that developing Asia will need to pool about US$26 trillion for infrastructure investment between 2016 and 2030 , or $1.7 trillion per year , as climate change alters infrastructure needs in the region . This is more than twice the initial forecast of $750 billion in 2009 . <p> This is according to a new report entitled Meeting Asia 's Infrastructure Needs published by the Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) , which reveals views of all 45 ADB member countries in developing Asia .
