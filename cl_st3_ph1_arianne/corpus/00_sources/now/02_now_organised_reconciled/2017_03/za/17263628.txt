<h> New online platform to help foster innovative water solutions <p> On the eve of the COP21 conference , 184 countries covering around 95 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions had delivered their national climate action plans to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) . <p> Through an online portal , an organisation called Waterforeveryone has provided a platform for communities and organisations to develop innovative approaches to improve access to fresh and safe water , as well as share knowledge and raise global awareness . <p> The platform allows individuals and organisations , both public and private , to connect with one another and share their knowledge and expertise , support existing concrete initiatives and create new ones , promote innovative solutions , and contribute to the online resource centre by posting articles , news , research findings etc . <p> Waterforeveryone is a non-profit organisation registered in Switzerland . It was developed by a multidisciplinary group of professionals who aim to increase the amount and access of clean water available to millions of people across the planet . <p> " With more than @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ safe , drinkable water , there is more we can do to help alleviate this statistic , " said Mathieu Lamolle , founder of Waterforeveryone . " We strongly believe that every step counts to find out effective and sustainable local solutions for this global challenge . " <p> Lamolle said the purpose of this community platform is to educate people while encouraging collaborations for a more efficient end goal . <h> Crowdfunding tool <p> To assist with getting projects off the ground and actually seeing them through , the portal supports a crowdfunding mechanism . <p> This tool enables online members to run fundraising campaigns for their initiatives . These campaigns will focus on how to develop and implement sustainable solutions in order to tackle water related issues . The platform will also serve to promote collaboration on projects across countries around the world . <p> The organisation invites everyone to join the global community to spur innovation , knowledge and successful projects . <p> " We strongly believe that every step counts and so does every drop : your support will contribute to help thousands , if not millions of people , " the organisation said .
