<h> News feed <h> Today 's the day to hug a tree <p> THE International Day of Forests was established by the United Nations in 2012 . <p> Each year , various events celebrate and raise awareness of the importance of all types of forests -- and trees outside forests -- for the benefit of current and future generations . <p> Each year more than 13-million hectares of forests are lost , an area roughly the size of England . As the forests diminish , so do the plant and animal species that they embrace -- 80 percent of all terrestrial biodiversity . <p> Most importantly , forests play a critical role in climate change including global warming . Crucially , healthy forests are one of the world 's primary ' carbon sinks . ' <p> Today , forests cover more than 30 percent of the world 's land and contain more than 60 000 tree species , many as of yet unidentified .
