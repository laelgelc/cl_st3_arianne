<h> " Start your own seed stokvels " <p> News - Date : 27 March 2017 <p> Written by : Elmon Tshikhudo / Viewed : 501 <p> Rural maize farmers in Vhembe were told to start seed stokvels that would help them purchase and curb the spiralling cost of seeds . <p> This was the advice of seed expert Mr Charles Matlou of the Pioneer Seed Company , who addressed farmers during a maize information day held at the local Maraxwe Juventus sports grounds outside Thohoyandou last Tuesday . <p> During the function , experts touched on several issues pertaining to maize farming . If the event is anything to go by , farmers in the area could possibly soon be smiling all the way to the bank and competing with other provinces in terms of maize production . <p> The event is the initiative of the Department of Agriculture and seed manufacturing company Pioneer Seeds . Hundreds of farmers from villages such as Mutshenzheni , Mbahela , Maraxwe , Mianzwi , Tshiombo and Matangari attended the day . <p> Matlou also urged the farmers to start producing maize on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ terms of maize production . " People do not default in paying clubs for groceries , funerals and other things . Why ca n't you start a stokvel that would help you in purchasing seeds ? You can start saving now , and when the time comes you wo n't have any problem , you will just go and purchase your seeds and you share accordingly . " <p> He also mentioned that it was of great concern that the farmers had land , but this land was not producing anything , with others even quitting farming because they had nothing to show . <p> Experts from different companies made presentations during the event . One of the coordinators , Mr Calvin Netshithuthuni , an agricultural extension officer , said the event came about as a way of empowering local farmers and making them aware of the new developments in the maize-production sector . " There are new developments in maize production that come daily . Climate change has affected production on a bigger scale , so that farmers are finding it hard to cope , " he added . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Converse Nekhwevha spoke on behalf of Thohoyandou Farm City . They both told farmers that they were available and their doors were open and they would assist them free of charge on the different challenges they might have as farmers . <p> Chief Tovhowani Mathoho thanked the organisers of the event , saying what they had learnt would go a long way in improving production of maize in their areas . " We are excited about this initiative and not long from now we will transform from subsistence to commercial farmers and make money from our produce , " he said . <p> Seed expert Charles Matlou shows some of the maize cultivars that give a big yield . <p> The Matangari Service Centre Pioneer Maize seed trial team that is doing the experiment and working with the communities .
