<h> Mutharika urges Malawians to conserve water <p> President Peter Mutharika has urged Malawians to start conserving water in the country to mitigate effects of climate change . <p> Mutharika made the remarks today in Mangochi during commemoration of this year 's World Water Day . <p> Mutharika commission water project in Mangochi <p> In his remarks , Mutharika said Malawians must start conserving water by among others harvesting the water during the rainy season . <p> According to Mutharika , water that can be harvested during the rainy season can be used for various purposes in dry season . <p> " Our population is growing and there is an increasing demand for water , climate change is affecting rainfall patterns and our water resources are dwindling day by day . We must start harvesting water in the rainy season for use in dry season , " Mutharika said . <p> The Malawi leader stressed that government will use all possible ways to make sure water is available , preserved and safe so that it is utilized for agricultural production , domestic and industrial use in the country . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ remains our first priority , with this reason we will continue implementing projects that will provide safe water across the country , " Mutharika said . <p> Mutharika also hailed some Malawians who have come together to clean Lake Malawi in Mangochi . <p> " I also heard that some people from Cape Maclear have formed a group to be taking care of the lake like to be waste free and also to make sure the lake has several fish species . We have to take care of the lake . This is our lake . When I say this is our lake , I mean the whole lake , " said Mutharika . <p> " This lake is one of the greatest treasures of this country . Lake Malawi has some of the most of beautiful shores in Africa . Lake Malawi has various varieties of fish in the world . It is the source of water which we use in homes and offices , " Mutharika said . <p> Earlier Mutharika commissioned the Mangochi Water Treatment Plant , a facility which will serve over 60 thousand people around @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ importance of water that is why it has embarked on the water project .
