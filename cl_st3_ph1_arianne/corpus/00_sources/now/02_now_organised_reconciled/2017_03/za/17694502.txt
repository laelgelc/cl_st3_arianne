<p> Agriculture and food security in the Western Cape are under threat due to increased heat , water stress on crops and livestock and droughts resulting from climate change . <p> Delivering his Budget Speech in the Western Cape legislature on Wednesday , Agriculture MEC Alan Winde said the Western Cape government in partnership with the private sector are arming themselves against the adverse effects of climate change through its long-term climate change response strategy known as SmartAgri . <p> SmartAgri sets out detailed scenarios and response proposals to combat the impact of extreme weather events on the province 's agriculture sector . <p> " Part of the SmartAgri approach to mitigate the danger posed by climate change is conservation agriculture . This method of farming includes minimum tillage , year-round soil cover and crop rotation to maintain the soil 's natural structure and increase the level of nutrients able to be absorbed . We 've taken this message to farmers across the province in the past two years .
