<p> NUI Galway has officially committed to divest from fossil fuel shares by the end of 2017 . <p> Students from the Climate Change , Agriculture and Food Security ( CCAFS ) Society of NUI Galway were informed that their campaign had been a success recently . <p> The CCAFS society had submitted a petition of over 1,000 signatures , and a report highlighting the case for divestment of the ? 3.4 million worth of shares that NUI Galway has invested in companies such as Gazprom and Statoil in late November . This report was welcomed by University President Dr James Browne , who highlighted divestment of fossil fuel shares is in line with the values held by NUI Galway with regards to sustainability . <p> Colm Duffy , Auditor of the CCAFS Society , Director of the Fossil Free Campaign and Students ' Union Postgraduate Officer was pleased with the results so far . <p> " We are glad that this campaign has come to a conclusion , and we look forward to assisting NUI Galway in the formation of its Ethical Investment Policy " . <p> Students ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with the CCAFS society , added : " NUI Galway 's decision to completely divest from fossil fuels shows leadership in the right direction .
