<h> Trump administration approves Keystone XL pipeline <p> The Trump administration has issued a presidential permit to TransCanada , to build the Keystone XL pipeline . <p> The move ends years of delay for the controversial $8 billion project . And it 's a sharp reversal from the Obama administration , which considered the pipeline contrary to national interests . <p> The 1,700-mile pipeline would carry oil from tar sands in Canada to refineries along the Texas Gulf Coast . Some 800,000 barrels of oil would pass through the pipe every day , more than one-fifth of the oil that Canada exports to the U.S. <p> Advocates say the project will improve U.S. energy security and create jobs . TransCanada has promised as many as 6,500 construction jobs a year for two years . Opponents argue the jobs will be minimal and short-term , and that the pipeline wo n't help the U.S. energy demand since the oil is destined for export . <p> A previous Trump presidential directive required new or expanded pipelines to be built with American steel " to the maximum extent possible .
