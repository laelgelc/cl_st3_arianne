<h> Climate change topic of talk in Basking Ridge <p> BERNARDS TWP. -- David Robinson , the New Jersey state climatologist , will speak about climate change at the Environmental Education Center ( EEC ) , 190 Lord Stirling Road , in Basking Ridge , from 7 to 8 p.m . Thursday , March 9 . <p> Robinson , who is also a Rutgers University professor , will present " Exploring New Jersey 's Climate Variability and Change " at the EEC . <p> With New Jersey experiencing what is considered the wettest and warmest weather in 120 years of observation , combined with an abundance of extreme weather events , Robinson 's lecture will address the state 's current climate , what may lie ahead , and the impact on the citizens of New Jersey . <p> He will discuss his interpretation of the climate trends based on analysis of data from across the state , including the EEC weather station .
