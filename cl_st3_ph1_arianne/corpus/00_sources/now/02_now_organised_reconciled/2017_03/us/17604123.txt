The consumptive use portion is what crops actually use , but the diversionary water rights are used to transport the consumptive use . The water then dissipates into the ground and is used further down stream by other irrigators . <p> " If you fallow your 40 acres and you have a 300 foot water right , you do n't have a 300 foot water rights .
