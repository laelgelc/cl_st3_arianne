<p> Just hours after President Donald Trump and Speaker Paul Ryan failed to replace Obamacare , there was an immediate reminder that fight against the current administration is on many fronts . More than 100 protesters gathered in Columbus Circle to express their outrage over the White House 's approval yesterday for the completion of the Keystone XL Pipeline . <p> The construction of the $8 billion TransCanada KXL Pipeline , which would transport oil directly from the drilling fields of Canada and North Dakota to refineries and export facilities along the Gulf Coast , was rejected by President Obama in 2015 .
