<h> International Day of Forests : Plug In to nature 's powerhouse <p> Did you know that the International Day of Forests is celebrated every year on March 21 ? Instituted by the United Nations General Assembly , the day recognizes the importance of forests and raises awareness of the role they play in environmental sustainability . <p> To mark the day : <p> Visit a forest near you and share photos on social media , # IntlForestDay <p> The theme of this year 's International Day of Forests is forests and energy . Wood provides more energy than solar , wind , and hydroelectric sources combined . It makes up about 40 percent of the world 's energy supply . <p> Managed properly , wood is a renewable resource . Healthy , thriving forests , such as those certified to standards of the independent , non-profit Sustainable Forestry Initiative ( SFI ) , provide the wood we need today without sacrificing the needs of the future . <p> SFI program participants have invested $1.6 million in forest research since 1995 . <p> SFI also awards Conservation and Community Partnerships @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ SFI has awarded 53 Conservation grants and 49 Community Partnership grants since 2010 .
