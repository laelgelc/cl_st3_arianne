<h> Seventh-grader paints dark future for himself because of climate change <p> by KATU News <p> Jeremy Clark speaks to KATU News Thursday about his experience testifying before a joint committee at the Oregon Capitol last week . He encouraged lawmakers to pass two bills intended to curtail climate change . ( KATU Photo ) <p> SALEM , Ore . -- <p> A 12-year-old boy from Portland took on climate change at the Capitol . <p> Jeremy Clark testified in front of a joint committee last week . He urged the state to take action to reduce climate pollution and invest in clean energy . <p> " Droughts , wildfires , floods , hurricanes , sea level rise resulting in millions of climate refugees , an economy collapse and a world not suitable for life , and that 's not a pessimistic view . That sounds more like an apocalypse than a future , but it is my future , " he told the committee . <p> The seventh-grader is specifically fighting for two Senate bills to move to the floor .
