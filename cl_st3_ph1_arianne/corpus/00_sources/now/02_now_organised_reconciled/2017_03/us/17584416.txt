<h> Free Screening Of The Documentary Forgotten Farms <p> There will be a free film screening of the documentary Forgotten Farms Thursday , March 30 , from 4-6 p.m. at Newport City Cinema , 137 Waterfront Plaza , Newport , Vt . <p> Orleans County Conservation District will be hosting the free community viewing of Forgotten Farms , which examines class divides in our farm and food communities , followed by a discussion panel with filmmakers , local dairy farmers and Vermont Housing and Conservation Board Agricultural Director , Nancy Everhart . <p> Most people buy their food in supermarkets and do n't have a chance to " know your farmer , " as the bumper sticker recommends . In more affluent communities , farm-to-table restaurants , farmer 's markets and CSAs are booming and the new farmers are celebrated . But there is another farmer who is left out of the local food celebration . <p> The Vermont Dairy Promotion council economic assessment lists Orleans County as having 15.3% of the state 's dairy farms with 131 dairy farms , 22 of which are Certified Organic , with a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ foundational part of our local economy and cultural in Orleans County but often these farmers are overlooked . This event showcases these farmers by highlighting the importance of the dairy industry which is very relevant to our Northeast Kingdom communities . <p> Only 100 years ago , New England produced most of its food on 16 million acres of farmland . And now , climate change demands that more of our food is grown closer to where we live . As we strive to revive local production , we have much to learn from dairy farmers who have been managing most of the farmland and sustaining the farm economy for generations .
