<h> Morro Bay National Estuary Program releases State of the Bay report <p> The State of the Bay report is out and we are learning a lot about the condition of the Morro Bay National Estuary . <p> Every three years , the Morro Bay National Estuary Program studies the health of the bay . They look at the water quality , how climate change is affecting the estuary , and whether important natural areas are being protected . <p> " So people ask us , ' Is the water clean enough to swim in ? Are there enough birds and fishes in the bay ? ' Those are the kinds of questions we answer , " said Lexia Bell of the estuary program . <p> The report says the bay is safe for swimming in most areas and it is safe for commercial shellfish harvesting .
