So much of what frightens us today has its roots in climate change . Even when we use the shorthand " ISIS " or " Syrian refugees , " we are in fact discussing a two-part climate disaster in the Middle East . Anyone who is serious about preventing Muslims from becoming anti-Western terrorists must therefore fight global warming . <p> Closer to home , further climate change will make the U.S.-Mexican border meaningless . Future food price spikes are likely to destabilize Mexico 's government and immigration control ( all rhetoric aside ) is impossible without a partner .
