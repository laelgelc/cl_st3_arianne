<h> What is this ? <p> Parts of Australia 's Great Barrier Reef will never recover from the impact of unseasonably warm waters , scientists said on Thursday , as more of the World Heritage Site comes under renewed threat from a recent spike in sea temperatures . <p> Warm seas around the reef killed some two-thirds of a 700 kilometre ( 496.4 miles ) stretch of coral last year after warm water caused the coral to expel living algae , triggering it to calcify and turn white , a process known as bleaching .
