Martin Luther King III was the opening speaker at the Greatness in Leadership Management Development Day at Enmax Centre on Tuesday , where he talked about the serious issues facing all men of colour , creed and social standing , and the importance of coming together in equality for all . King said the environment is the single biggest issue facing people today and that climate change is a problem that knows no boundaries . " Of all the great injustices on our planet , the environmental one is the one which requires the most immediate action , " he said . " Time is running out .
