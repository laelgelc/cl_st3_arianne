<h> Joint initiative pumping $6.6 M into community transit <p> More than two dozen Manitoba communities will get new handi-vans for mobility-impaired passengers , as part of a $6.6-million program announced on Friday in Winnipeg , by the three levels of government . <p> No money was earmarked for Winnipeg in this round of infrastructure and transportation funding . The city received $79.4 million for transit initiatives last summer that included $35.2 million in federal-provincial funding to purchase buses and $37 million to expand its bus maintenance space . <p> The money will finance 43 vehicle purchases and projects , it was announced Friday at the Manitoba Legislative Building . In addition to handi-vans , transit buses will be purchased and transit bus storage upgraded . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau and several members of his cabinet travelled the country Friday to make announcements related to transit , touting the initiatives as a means of reducing commutes and decreasing air pollution . <p> Natural Resources Minister Jim Carr represented Ottawa in Winnipeg .
