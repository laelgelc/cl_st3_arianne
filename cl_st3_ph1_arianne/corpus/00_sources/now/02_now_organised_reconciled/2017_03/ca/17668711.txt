<h> Green vision in short supply at all levels of government <p> Trucks bring clay and earth to landscape the pile of garbage at the Brady Road Landfill . Winnipeg still does not have organic waste collection . <p> March 2017 will go into the record books as the month when the only environmental action that took place was turning the lights out for Earth Hour . The rest of the month felt like Throwback Thursday , as governments at all levels seemed in competition to see who could turn back the clock the most . <p> Starting closest to home , Winnipeg city council set aside its own resolutions on organic waste collection and opted to remain one of the few large cities in North America where composting is a mystery too hard to solve .
