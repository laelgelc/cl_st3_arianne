The association celebrated its ninth annual Earth Hour from 8:30 p.m. local time until midnight.Photo by Clifford Skarstedt <p> More British Columbians turned off the lights for Earth Hour this year compared with 2015 , saving the equivalent of 1.1 million LED bulbs . <p> Electricity use across B.C. dipped 24 megawatt hours Saturday night -- a more enthusiastic response compared with the paltry 15 megawatt hours of electrical savings recorded in 2015 . <p> " The idea is to get people thinking about power conservation , " said B.C.
