<h> Province asks Manitobans to weigh in on carbon pricing options <p> THE province is asking the public for input on Ottawa 's carbon pricing plan . <p> Manitoba announced Friday it is launching an online survey so Manitobans can have their say " on the carbon pricing plan being imposed by the federal government , " Sustainable Development Minister Cathy Cox said in a statement Friday . <p> Last fall , Ottawa was criticized for the unilateral way it rolled out its plan . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau announced in October that Ottawa planned to set a national floor price on carbon that all provinces would have to levy on emissions . <p> He gave provinces two years to adopt the carbon price plan or a cap-and-trade system that would meet Canada 's requirements under the Paris climate change accord to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> About 85 per cent of Canadians live in jurisdictions that have some kind of price system on greenhouse gases .
