<h> Electricity rate relief at the centre of Queen 's Park debate <p> The Legislative Assembly of Ontario resumed sitting on February 21st . Activity has been at a fever pitch in the first few weeks back . Electricity prices continue to dominate the discussion . <p> Premier Wynne has finally acknowledged the damage that has been done by the Liberal government 's energy policy and is now frantically searching for a way to lower energy bills . I 'm glad that Premier Wynne has announced rate relief ; I support rate relief because Ontarians are suffering . However , many in the public and media seem skeptical of the Premier 's plan , which will shift billions of dollars in energy system costs onto the bills of future electricity users . These electricity customers ( you , your kids , and your grandkids ) will pick up the tab for billions in new interest charges -$14 billion in interest payments to be exact -- as the Premier attempts to reduce prices today , or at least until the next provincial election in 15 months .
