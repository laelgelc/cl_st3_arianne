<p> The Grizzly Bear Foundation wants to see the grizzly hunt ended in British Columbia . The recommendation is one of 19 made by the charity following a tour of the province with stops in six towns and cities . <p> The findings of the group 's three-person board of inquiry are included in a new 88-page report . <p> " Today , this species has been gradually killed off . Ninety-seven per cent of the territory in the contiguous United States to our south is now devoid of grizzly bears , " said Michael Audain , the housing developer and art philanthropist who chairs the Grizzly Bear Foundation . <p> " So many British Columbians seem to care about the bear . They seem proud of grizzly bears and they seem to have a great interest in bears .
