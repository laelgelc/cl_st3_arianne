<h> Meatless burger company aims to win over carnivores <p> Patrick Brown founded Impossible Foods with the goal of supplanting the meat industry . He believes the United States ' 230 million omnivores can be made to trade their hamburgers and steaks for a plant-based equivalent , scienced into being . <p> That vision may yet be a long way off -- even Brown admits as much .
