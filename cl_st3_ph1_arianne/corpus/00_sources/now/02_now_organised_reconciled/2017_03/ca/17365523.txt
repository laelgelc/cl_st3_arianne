<h> The Global Picture - Rate of ocean warming has doubled since 1960 <p> The buildup of heat-trapping greenhouse gasses has caused ocean warming to nearly double since 1992 , compared with the previous thirty years , but perhaps more concerning , the increase in warming has reached the deeper water column of the oceans . <p> In a new study published in the journal Science Advances on Friday , researchers from NOAA , the National Center for Atmospheric Research ( NCAR ) , the University of St. Thomas and the Chinese Academy of Sciences , found that the oceans may be storing as much as 13 percent more heat than previously estimated . <p> The study marks a first step in piecing together current and historical observations and measurements from ships , self-propelled floats , satellites and even seals to get a clearer picture on how the oceans are being impacted by global warming , says Carbon Brief . <p> Oceans absorb more than 90 percent of the energy accumulated in the climate system from human emissions of greenhouse gases <p> Hector Mata , AFP/File <p> The new analysis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , total ocean warming was 13 percent greater than the Fifth Assessment Report of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) in 2013 . " In other words , the planet is warming quite a lot more than we thought , " said NCAR scientist Kevin Trenberth , a study co-author . <p> More heat finding its way into the oceans <p> Researchers found that ocean warming was relatively slow before 1980 . Then there was a speed up of upper ocean warming between the surface and 700 meters ( 2,297 feet ) between 1960-1991 and 1992-2015 when the rate of warming quadrupled . <p> However , since the 1990s , more heat has been finding its way into the deep ocean between 700 and 2000 meters ( 2,297 to 6,562 feet ) .
