<h> Time to end subsidies of fossil fuel industry <p> I 've been reading the federal budget . Efforts to reduce reliance on diesel in Indigenous and northern communities is commendable . <p> But why is the government still subsidizing the fossil fuel industry to the tune of hundreds of millions of dollars when we need to be fighting climate change ? <p> Let 's put our money into subsidizing the clean energy industry instead -- wind , solar , geothermal -- and train people to work for production of clean energy . We can create an economy that will allow people to have clean air to breathe , that will create long-term jobs for them to work at , that will reduce extreme weather events , and that will finally eradicate the boom-and-bust energy cycles that have created job problems for so many Canadians past and present .
