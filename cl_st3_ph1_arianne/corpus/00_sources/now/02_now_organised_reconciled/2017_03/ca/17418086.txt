<h> City aims to measure value of natural assets <p> City of Saskatoon officials are in the preliminary stages of compiling an inventory of the city 's natural assets and assigning value to them , a city council committee heard Monday . ( GREG PENDER/The StarPhoenix ) Greg Pender / The StarPhoenix <p> Most think of municipal infrastructure as roads and bridges , but Brenda Wallace wants to change that . <p> Wallace , the City of Saskatoon 's director of environmental and corporate initiatives , intends to compile an inventory of the city 's natural assets and apply value to them in the same way the city looks at built infrastructure . <p> Wallace said the city 's green infrastructure strategy has the potential to turn the conversation about climate change and its financial implications " on its head . " <p> " That 's really significant , " Wallace said in an interview Tuesday . <p> The work began earlier this year and Wallace said she expects an update in the spring . <p> Wallace pointed to the tiny B.C. community of Gibsons , with fewer than 5,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pioneering this new way to look at infrastructure . <p> Gibsons ' so-called eco-asset strategy stems from a realization that maintaining the municipality 's traditional infrastructure would cost more than the tiny community could afford .
