<h> Poll <p> Our demand for energy is increasing and society aspires to reduce fossil fuel use by substituting other sources of energy . In Alberta these other sources are , in order of prevalence , wind , hydro and solar . Following this path the fossil fuel of natural gas , which when burned produces half as many greenhouse gases as coal burning , will become our main energy source . How much non-fossil energy ( alternative ) should we produce to meet our future electrical power needs ( transportation , home and industrial power , etc . ) ? <p> Each energy source has its unique infrastructure and distribution needs . I learned from Cosmos Voutsinos that the process of defining the energy source and construction of its specific infrastructure takes decades . Many additional years of use are needed to make the system cost effective . <p> We need leadership to guide us in this costly venture . This is a new and long-term type of venture for society . If this leadership resides in government then how will changes in the leading political party influence our best energy plan ? How much bitumen , how many wind farms , solar panels , pipelines or nuclear plants do we need now and in 50 years time ? How do we reach agreement on our future energy plan ? Only good quality national and international data , well analyzed , will give us confidence in the right path . Feelings or opinions will not do . <p> Will different leading parties agree to follow the same energy plan for many years ?
