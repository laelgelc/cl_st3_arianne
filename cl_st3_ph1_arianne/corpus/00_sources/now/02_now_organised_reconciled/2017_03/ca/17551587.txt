The group meets every Monday at lunch to plan events and initiatives aimed at encouraging positive , sustainable change . <p> " We are driven to make any changes , even as small as possible , within our community , " Carmel Flores , a Grade 12 student and member of the group , explained . <p> " When I started this group up , the focus was helping out in the community , " added Megan B ? dard , a French teacher at TCI who facilitates the Titans for Tomorrow student committee .
