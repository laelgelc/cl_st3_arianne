Here 's How <p> Bring the regulatory agency -- first founded way back in 1959 when the realities of climate change were n't readily known -- into alignment with our carbon-constrained present . <p> That recommendation , coming from the Pembina Institute , comes in a report released Friday to coincide with the end of a federal review of the National Energy Board that brought an expert panel into halls and meeting rooms of 10 cities across the nation . <p> The National Energy Board has been called a " captured regulator " that has " lost touch with what it means to protect the public interest , " by Marc Eliesen , former head of BC Hydro and former deputy minister of energy in Ontario and Manitoba . <p> Eliesen was one of many groups and individuals to publicly pull out of the NEB review of the Kinder Morgan Trans Mountain pipeline after the process was called " fraudulent " and an act of " public deception .
