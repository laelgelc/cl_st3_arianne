<h> Rising pH a threat to wild and hatchery trout in B.C. lakes <p> The Freshwater Fisheries Society of B.C. puts about five million trout into about 800 lakes around the province each year.Photo by <p> About one quarter of the lakes stocked with trout by the Freshwater Fisheries Society of B.C. suffer from increased fish mortality due to highly alkaline lake water . <p> At least 60 lakes in the highest pH category " can not support effective stocking programs , " said Patricia Schulte , a biologist at the University of B.C. <p> Those lakes are consistently above 9 pH , while another 100 lakes show moderate to high alkalinity , above 8.5 pH , according to the FFSBC . The pH scale is logarithmic , meaning a value of 9 is ten times as alkaline as 8 . <p> High pH is is part of a complex interrelationship between low precipitation , water temperature and oxygen levels , driven by climate change . <p> " We know experimentally that of the fish that go into the very high pH lakes a lot of them do n't survive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ five million trout into about 800 B.C. lakes each year , but the number of high-pH lakes and the pH levels detected have both been rising for decades , endangering both wild and hatchery stocks . <p> " We usually stop stocking a lake if it looks like the pH is stable at 9.4 to 9.5 pH , " said society spokeswoman Stacy Webb . Most of the high pH lakes are in the Okanagan , Thompson and Cariboo regions . <p> " Slightly elevated pH might be fine , but when water temperatures are high that compounds the stress on the fish , " she said . <p> Two research projects are underway to get a clearer picture of the relationship between trout mortality and pH and retool hatchery programs and wild fish conservation for an increasingly hostile future environment . <p> An FFSBC study will cross-reference mortality data with pH levels to decide whether to alter the pH conditions in hatcheries to produce fish that are more resistant to alkaline waters and to detect genetic strains of trout that are more tolerant to high pH . <p> Schulte @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in wild rainbow trout and steelhead associated with resilience in warm and high pH waters .
