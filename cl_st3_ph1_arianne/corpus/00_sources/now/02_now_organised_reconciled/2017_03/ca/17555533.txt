We must move away from our reliance on resource revenue , we must return the budget to balance , " said finance minister Kevin Doherty to reporters in the pre-budget conference call . <p> " Our plan will return the budget to balance by controlling spending , modernizing and expanding the tax base , and ensuring our economy remains strong @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is the hike in the Provincial Sales Tax . You will now pay the PST for children 's clothing , restaurant meals and snack foods , for insurance premiums , construction services , and permanently mounted equipment used in the resource sector . <p> Also going up is the Education Property Tax , which is increasing to provide 40 per cent of funding to K-12 education . <p> Among the exemptions being eliminated is the one for bulk purchases of gasoline . The exemption for bulk purchases of diesel fuel is being reduced to 80 per cent of purchases . <p> The exemption for used cars will stay , but the value of a trade-in will no longer be deductible in determining the PST on the purchase of a new vehicle . <p> Unsurprisingly , tobacco tax rates are increasing as of midnight , March 23 , from $.25 to $.27 a cigarette . Doherty explained that a decade ago the STC subsidy was $25 per passenger ; now it is $94. " $85 million would have been needed from government to continue operating STC for the next five years .
