In some parts , one in four trees was an American chestnut . The trees were a refuge for hundreds of species and a thrumming industry : its wood was so hardy that chestnut fences still stake Ontario fields . <p> Then , in less than 50 years , the trees were gone . An exotic blight , accidentally carried over on an Asian chestnut variety , began infecting American chestnuts as the 20th century dawned .
