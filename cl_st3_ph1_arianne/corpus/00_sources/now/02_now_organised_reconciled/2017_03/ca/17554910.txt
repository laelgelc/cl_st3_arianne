<h> Alberta government puts up $12M for management of water resources <p> The groups in charge of overseeing the province 's water resources are receiving $12-million over the next three years . <p> The 11 local watershed councils , and the Alberta Water Council are the groups who work with the public , First Nations , governments , industry and conservation groups to support community flood and drought-resiliency planning . <p> Environment Minister Shannon Phillips made the announcement in Calgary on Wednesday . <p> Each year for the next three years , the watershed councils will receive $3.2 million per year to assess watershed health and plan for water management . The Alberta Water Council will receive $750,000 per year for the next three years to preserve water resources . <p> " These folks are the folks on the ground . They can accomplish consensus-based work in the community that potentially government ca n't , " said Phillips . <p> The minister said it 's important that these councils are able to keep doing their work because there are challenges . <p> " We do have growing communities .
