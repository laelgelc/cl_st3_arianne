<p> The United States military has warned President Donald Trump about its view that climate change presents a risk to U.S. national security and also international security , and that the United States should advance a comprehensive policy to address that risk . <p> Concerns raised by the military include the possibility of failed states and international conflict as a result of the stress created by climate change . <p> The evening will begin with a documentary , " The Age of Consequences , " which is about the U.S. military position on this issue . <p> After the documentary , speakers include Rick Cober Bauman , executive director of Mennonite Central Committee Ontario ; and Simon Dalby , CIGI chair in the Political Economy of Climate Change at the Balsillie School of International Affairs in Waterloo . They will discuss building peace at a time of ecological crisis . <p> The event is to be held in the Great Hall at Conrad Grebel University College at University of Waterloo .
