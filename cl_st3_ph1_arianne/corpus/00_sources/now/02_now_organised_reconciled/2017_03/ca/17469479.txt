<p> Certain labs investigating viruses , climate change , and other scientific pursuits have received funding for infrastructure upgrades after an announcement early March . <p> The federal government announced on March 2 that it is awarding $2.1 million from the John R. Evans Leaders Fund to 14 professors in the Faculties of Science and Engineering . Their projects range from the study of human motor functions to robot-assisted surgery . <p> Duane Froese , a scientist who studies climate change in the arctic , was one recipient of the John R. He 'll be using it to upgrade equipment for the lab and for his trips to the field . <p> " ( The upgrades will ) allow us to do more things in-house , " Froese said .
