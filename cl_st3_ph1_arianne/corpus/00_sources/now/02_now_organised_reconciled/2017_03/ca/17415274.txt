It 's the latest in a series of steps that began in 2012 and will end this spring to update the plan . <p> The current Official Plan was approved in 2006 , and replaced the 1978 Official Plan for the Sudbury Planning Area and 12 other land use planning documents . <p> To date , planning staff have had 12 meetings with various advisory panels , 14 public @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ public input session and one day-long stakeholder workshop . Staff has also held more than 50 meetings with residents , landowners , developers , CANs , community stakeholder groups and public agencies . <p> And more than 225 written submissions have also been received , and two more public meetings were held on March 7 and 9 . <p> The plan governs every land use decision in Greater Sudbury , and must conform to provincial planning rules . The amended plan focuses on several areas . It encourages intensification , the process by which more people live closer together , making city infrastructure and services more viable . <p> " The amendment establishes a built boundary , sets a new target for the amount of growth to be accommodated through intensification in the built boundary , and calls for ongoing monitoring of this area , " the staff report says . " New policies are introduced to guide future intensification in a manner that is compatible with and reinforces the existing and planned character of an area , while protecting the stability of established residential neighbourhoods .
