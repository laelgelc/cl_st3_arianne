<h> France bans plastic cups , plates and cutlery <h> France has passed a new law which requires all plastic cups , cutlery and plates to be made of biologically-sourced , compost material , according to a report by The Independent . <p> The law is part of an ambitious plan known as Energy Transition for Green Growth , and is set to take effect in 2020 . The government hopes it could make an effective contribution to tackling climate change . <p> However , critics of the law argue that it violates the European Union 's rules on the free movement of goods . <p> Pack2Go Europe , which represents European packaging industry , vows to fight the law and prevent it from taking effect in other countries . <p> " We are urging the European Commission to do the right thing and to take legal action against France for infringing European law , " The Independent quotes Pack2Go Europe secretary general Eamonn Bates as saying . " If they do n't , we will .
