<h> Malaysian lawyer bags top British entrepreneurial award <p> Mary Ann Ooi Suan Kim explains how her passion for the environment led to her involvement in green entrepreneurial activities . <p> PETALING JAYA : Malaysian Mary Ann Ooi Suan Kim has won this year 's British Council Entrepreneurial Award for her work at Builders Biomass , an environmentally friendly business venture . <p> The award , presented to the City Law School alumna on March 16 , recognises " extraordinary individuals who are active in initiating or contributing to innovative new business opportunities with strong growth prospects " , an announcement on the school 's website said . <p> In an interview with the law school , Ooi said she had been nominated for the award by the University of Sheffield as she had read law at Sheffield . She is also an advisory board member at Sheffield 's School of Law . <p> When it came to the Bar exam , Ooi said choosing City Law School was a no-brainer for her . <p> " It has always been my dream to qualify as a Barrister of the Honourable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ original provider of bar training , none other than the prestigious Inns of Court School of Law , now known as the City Law School , " she was quoted as saying . <p> Ooi , who is currently a practising lawyer and head of her Kuala Lumpur-based firm , O S Kim &amp; Associates , said she was first exposed to sustainability projects during her time as a student in the UK . She founded Builders Biomass after returning from the UK in 2001 . <p> When asked about the inspiration behind the business venture , Ooi credited her passion for the environment and for finding solutions to reduce the effects of climate change . <p> Other factors included the preservation of biodiversity , the protection of ecosystems , and growing concerns about global warming , she added in the interview . <p> Biomass is organic matter used as a fuel , especially in a power station for the generation of electricity . <p> In the interview , Ooi said she balanced her entrepreneurial activities and her legal career by having automated ISO management systems in place . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ certified organisation , which adopts automation in its manufacturing facility . <p> " I am able to strike a balance in my activities by having these ISO management systems in place , together with well trained , competent teams for the corporate organisation and in my legal firm , " she was reported as saying . <h> Comments <p> Readers are required to have a valid Facebook account to comment on this story .
