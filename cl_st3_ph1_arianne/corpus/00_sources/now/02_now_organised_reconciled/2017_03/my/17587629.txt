<h> Community <h> Heading towards becoming a low carbon city <p> Ampang Jaya Municipal Council ( MPAJ ) is taking pro-active steps to reduce 30% of carbon emissions by 2022 , in line with tackling the threat of climate change . <p> MPAJ has been working together with the British High Commission through The Carbon Trust , United Kingdom , since 2016 in developing mid to long-term plans . <p> These include the Carbon Management Plan 2017 to 2022 and Ampang Jaya Low Carbon City Action Plan 2017 to 2030 . <p> After conducting some research , The Carbon Trust associate manager Olivia McGregor said Ampang Jaya produced 2,400 kilotons of carbon emissions in 2015 . <p> Using themselves as a launchpad , MPAJ will be monitoring energy usage and implementing more efficient energy management systems at Menara MPAJ . <p> " The cost of implementing the projects has been estimated at RM15.4mil by 2022 . <p> " However , it will result in an estimated annual financial savings of RM2.2mil with an overall payback period of approximately seven years , " she said . <p> In adhering to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ methods include installing solar panels and LED lights , implementing cooling system upgrades as well as practicing behaviour change . <p> MPAJ will also use LED lights for its street lamps , and encourage the use of public transportation , said MPAJ president Abdul Hamid Abdul Hussain . <p> " We will allocate about RM3mil each year to implement the programmes . <p> " Although , there is no enforcement in terms of laws or by-laws to regulate what type of lights a building should use . <p> " Instead , we will create awareness through programmes on energy saving by letting the public know that it is an investment , where they can reduce carbon emissions and also benefit through savings , " he said .
