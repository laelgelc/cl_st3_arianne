<p> Focusing on green energy and technology , yesterday , Minister of Energy , Green Technology and Water Malaysia ( KeTTHA ) Datuk Seri Dr Maximus Ongkili , officiated Malaysia 's journey to be a part of an international dialogue , Astana EXPO 2017 , that will be held in Kazakhstan from June 10 until September 10 , 2017 . <p> The three-month exhibition aptly themed ' Future Energy ' , will focus on future energy as well as innovative and practical energy solutions , which is deemed to help Malaysia employ energy resources more efficiently to lessen environmental damage and address energy security . <p> The Malaysian Pavilion theme is ' Powering Green Growth ' , reflecting the expo 's main theme , and Ongkili says they will be " driving the agenda of carbon reduction and climate change with futuristic and green energy technologies as the core drivers , including solar and biomass technologies , energy efficiency practices , electric mobility , low carbon cities , green buildings , smart grids as well as ICT technologies . <p> Ongkili also believes the upcoming event will serve as a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially in trade , and that we will be able to highlight the country 's sustainable developments implemented to reach our goals by 2020 . <p> It was reported that 50 of Malaysia 's best industry players will showcase the country 's innovations , best practices and capabilities in green energy and technology at the EXPO 2017 . <p> The minister previously relayed that other ministries such as tourism , agriculture , domestic trade cooperatives and consumerism , international trade and industry and higher education will also be involved in the expo . <p> As of October last year , the event has received over 100 participating countries and is optimistic on receiving five million visitors throughout the three months .
