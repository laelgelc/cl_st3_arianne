<h> Snow postpones Trump-Merkel talks until Friday <p> Journalists disembark from the German Air Force 's Airbus A340 ' Theodor Heuss ' on the military part of Tegel Airport after the take off was cancelled due to expected bad weather in the Washington area , in Berlin , Germany , 13 March 2017 . Merkel was about to meet US President Donald J. Trump in the White House in Washington DC , USA , 14 March 2017 . The White House in the meantime said the meeting was postponed and will take place on Friday , 17 March 2017 . EPA Photo <p> WASHINGTON : President Donald Trump 's meeting with German Chancellor Angela Merkel has been postponed from Tuesday until Friday due to a major snow storm closing in on the US East Coast , the White House said . <p> " The president spoke to the Chancellor of Germany . They agreed due to the weather that the meeting should be postponed , rescheduled to March 17th , " Trump 's spokesman Sean Spicer told reporters on Monday . <p> The Oval Office tete-a-tete , expected to be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , as the two leaders are far apart on a host of issues , from immigration to climate change . <p> In the run-up to the meeting , the White House has emphasized the strength of ties between the two nations , and said that Trump is hoping to learn from the chancellor 's depth of experience . <p> The US president will also be " very interested in hearing her insights on what it 's like to deal with the Russians " and Merkel 's views on resolving the Ukraine conflict , said a senior administration official . <p> The reserved German leader has underlined that she is travelling to Washington not only as Germany 's leader , but also as an envoy of the European Union . <p> " I will of course point out that for us , our country and our membership in the European Union are two sides of the same coin , " Merkel said in Brussels ahead of the visit .
