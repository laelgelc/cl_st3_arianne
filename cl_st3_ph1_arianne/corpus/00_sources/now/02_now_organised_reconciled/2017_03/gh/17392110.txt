<h> Women call for groundbreaking change <p> Keta , March 13 , GNA - Women beneficiaries of the Global Alliance for Green and Gender Action ( GAGGA ) , a non-governmental organization , have called on government to take pragmatic steps towards " actions that truly drive the greatest change for women " . <p> In a press release copied to the Ghana News Agency ( GNA ) , they called for efforts at addressing the underlying causes of climate change . <p> " We kick against false solutions ; we call for sustainable solutions that protect the rights of women and children , " the release said . <p> It said the region , which was endowed with resources : water , mangroves , reeds , turtles among others with huge eco-tourism potentials were yet to be resourced and be developed to improve livelihoods and to alleviate poverty , which had become a cancer in the nation . <p> The release signed by leaders of the women grouping said climate change was having devastating impact on women and threatened their very survival . <p> The women also appealed to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as eco-tourism , salt winning , craft and kente weaving , fish processing , among others . <p> They also called for sustainable solutions that protected and empowered women because " women empowerment will lead to economic growth .
