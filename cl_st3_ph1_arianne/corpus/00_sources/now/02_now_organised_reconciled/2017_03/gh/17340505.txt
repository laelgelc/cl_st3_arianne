<h> Burundian women eager to contribute to well-being of families <p> As the world celebrated on Wednesday the International Women 's Day , Burundian women said that they are eager to contribute to the well-being of their households . <p> " A woman plays a big role in the development of her household and therefore the development of the country . Both my husband and I have to work hard and no one between us should take a rest , " Clotilde Ndayishimiye , a seller of clothes at Buyenzi City Market said . <p> She added that more and more women are now aware that they play an important role in the promotion of the well-being of families . <p> Another woman selling fruits indicated that she helped her husband build a house . <p> " My husband bought a plot and I contributed to raising walls of the house , " Bernadette Hategekimana said . <p> She added that it has been nowadays difficult for men alone to pay school fees for children . <p> In a declaration at the international women 's day , the Burundian government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ when the east African nation is facing hunger caused by climate change . <p> " Poor agricultural harvests observed these days do not allow women to enjoy their life in their households , " Burundian Gender Minister Martin Nivyabandi said . <p> He also indicated that the high fecundity rate reported in Burundi weakens Burundian women . <p> In Burundi , the women 's day has been celebrated in the country 's eastern province of Ruyigi under the theme " Protecting the environment : key to food security and women empowerment .
