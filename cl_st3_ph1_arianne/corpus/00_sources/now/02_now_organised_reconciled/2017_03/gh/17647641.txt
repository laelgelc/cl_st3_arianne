<h> Operators of Ameri must go for renegotiation of deal-IECP <p> Tuesday 28th March , 2017 6:17 am <p> The Institute of Energy and Climate Change Policy ( IECP ) has charged operators of the Ameri Power Plant to accept the recommendations of the Philip Adisson Committee and renegotiate the deal with government . <p> According to the institute , a relook at the terms of the deal will benefit both operators of the Ameri Power Plant , as well as the government of Ghana . <p> The Philip Adisson Committee charged to review the Ameri deal advised government to renegotiate the contract or abrogate it on grounds of fraud <p> Speaking to Citi Business News on the issue , the Director of Research and Policy at IECP Dr. Philip Adom advised the power company to accept the report and renegotiate the terms . <p> " If they are interested in doing business with Ghana then I will say that they will have to listen to the advice of going back to the table to renegotiate this whole contract so that at least there will be mutual benefit but if @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he said . <p> He was of the view that operators of the plant can not be held liable for the deal since they negotiated in their best interest . <p> " If they are not the root cause , it is the responsibility of the other party to make sure that they government the broker the deal seek the interest of the nation , but if you fail to do that and just rush into the contract and later it comes out that actually the price was inflated then Ameri can only renegotiate the deal , " he argued . <p> He reiterated that even though the company can insist on the current arrangement it will however create tension between it and Ghana , hence a need to go back for a renegotiation . <p> Background <p> The committee charged to review , restructure and recommend areas of amendment of the Ameri power deal , recommended that the deal be re-negotiated or abrogated on grounds of fraud , after it emerged that Ghana was made to pay 150 million dollars extra in commission to Africa &amp; Middle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the construction of the power plant . <p> Ameri in its agreement with Government dated February 10th , 2015 , charged Ghana significantly higher than what it was charged by the Turkish registered company -- PPR , which financed and executed the project . <p> The Turkish firm pegged the total cost of the project which is to span over a 5 year period at a maximum of 360 million dollars . <p> Bloated Commission <p> However in the Build Operate Own Transfer ( BOOT ) agreement signed between Government and Ameri the deal was pegged at a minimum of 510 million dollars . <p> Leaving Ameri with a commission of 150 million dollars . <p> According to the 17 member committee inaugurated by the Energy Minister Mr. Boakye Agyarko on February 01,2017 to review the Ameri power deal , on grounds of that anomaly , as well other financial , technical and legal issues , the power company must be made to re-negotiate the deal or be rejected by Government on grounds of fraud . <p> The deal was signed as an emergency power agreement on the 10th of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Minister of Power and Ameri Energy , to help reduce the country 's power deficit at the time . <p> According to the Committee chaired by private legal practitioner Mr Philip Addison , though the plant is currently operational a number of omissions and concessions made in the agreement are not only unfair but also not in the interest of Ghana . <p> Plant has no operating license <p> According to the committee 's report the Ameri Energy plant currently does not have an operating license . <p> The development , the committee states , is contrary to section 11 and 25 of the Energy Commission Act , 1997 ( ACT 541 ) which requires the operators of the plant to secure the licenses and permits . <p> The committee urges that Ameri Energy must get the required license and permits as soon as possible . <p> It adds that a Grid Interconnection Agreement must also be signed between the plant operator and the Grid operator ( GRIDCo ) . <p> Meanwhile the Energy Commission ( EC ) is reported to have officially The Committee in its report stated that the move was not acceptable and directed that moving forward , contractors must be made to pay taxes such as corporate and income tax . <p> No due diligence was carried out on Ameri <p> According to the Committee 's report no due diligence was carried out on Africa and Middle East Resources Investment Group LLC ( Ameri Group ) nor Ameri Energy Power Equipment
