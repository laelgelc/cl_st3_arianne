<p> The Canola Council of Canada ( CCC ) sees potential in the federal government 's proposed rules for the Clean Fuel Standard ( CFS ) released late last week . The organization says it 's pleased to see the CFS provides options for Canadian farmers ' growing practices to be accepted at a national level without any additional on-farm requirements . <p> In the coming weeks , the CCC will be seeking additional detail from the Government of Canada on the methodology for calculating carbon intensity values which will be a key factor that drives demand for canola under the CFS . <p> " We 're pleased to see the proposed CFS allow canola to be used for biofuel without complex and costly on-farm regulatory burden , in line with US biofuel regulations , " said CCC President Jim Everson . " The CFS can create a strong domestic market for canola , reducing reliance on volatile global markets and at the same time contribute to improved air quality through greenhouse gas reduction .
