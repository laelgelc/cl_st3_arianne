<p> LNG development will not only prevent Canada from meeting its emissions reduction targets , it may also be an economic non-starter due to decreased demand for fossil fuels and the increasing affordability of clean energy alternatives . <p> LNG is a gas that has been cooled to liquid form for easy and safe storage and transport . While LNG plants emit 50 to 60 per cent less carbon dioxide than coal plants , their carbon footprint grows substantially when emissions from the entire gas supply chain are factored in , including hydraulic fracturing ( fracking ) , venting and leaks , which release methane , a powerful GHG with a global warming potential 86 times that of CO2 over a 20-year time frame . <p> Methane venting is responsible for about 20 per cent of emissions from gas production in B.C. , according to the province 's Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory .
