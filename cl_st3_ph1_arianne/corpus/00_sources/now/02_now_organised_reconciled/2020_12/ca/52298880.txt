<p> The Western Canadian Wheat Growers Association ( WCWGA ) says it 's shocked that the federal government is hiking the carbon tax during the middle of a pandemic and with no regard to the impact on the bottom line of grain farmers . <p> The Liberals plan to increase the carbon price by $15 per year , starting in 2023 , rising to $170 per tonne in 2030 . <p> " The federal government has put a carbon tax on everything that is shipped to our farms , but those costs can not be passed on to the end consumer . We sell our grain at world commodity prices , regardless of our input costs , " said President Gunter Jochum . <p> In a news release , WCWGA says the introduction of the carbon tax hurt farmers income by tens of thousands of dollars for each farm . The group notes that as the regulations for the new Clean Fuel Standards are being unveiled , the cost of fuel will continue to rise .
