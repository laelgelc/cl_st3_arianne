We like to look at that list , and read these stories , to gauge Canada 's position as an international leader in research innovation . The 2020 list is impressive . <p> This year , Canada is winning big -- including University of Alberta researcher Michael Houghton sharing the 2020 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine .
