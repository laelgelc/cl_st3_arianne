<p> As seas rise and coastal storms intensify , policy-makers and low-lying cities around the globe are increasingly wrestling with the reality of needing to relocate entire communities to higher , safer ground . Scientists estimate that up to 340 million people in coastal areas could be displaced by 2050 , and 630 million by 2100 , in locations from the United States to Nigeria to the Philippines . <p> Such widespread , organized retreat from the shore will take massive amounts of planning and funding , but geographer Jola Ajibade warns it also needs to be done in an equitable way .
