<h> Vast wildfires in Siberia linked to warming Arctic <p> WASHINGTON -- This year 's vast wildfires in far northeastern Russia were linked to broader changes in a warming Arctic , according to a report Tuesday by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration . <p> Wildfires are a natural part of many boreal ecosystems . But the extent of flames during the 2020 fire season was unprecedented in the 2001-2020 satellite record , and is consistent with the predicted effects of climate change , said Alison York , a University of Alaska Fairbanks fire scientist and a contributor to the annual Arctic Report Card . <p> The recent wildfires were exacerbated by elevated air temperatures and decreased snow cover on the ground in the Arctic region , the report found . <p> The past year -- from October 2019 to September 2020 -- was the second warmest on record in the Arctic , the report said .
