A pre-feasibility study looked at the Innovation Hub in downtown Kitchener as a good candidate . Energy for heating and cooling buildings in the area would be , in part , from briny water drawn from an underground aquifer . Besides reducing greenhouse gas emissions by 53 per cent , sharing this clean energy source would be cost-effective by reducing building and maintenance costs .
