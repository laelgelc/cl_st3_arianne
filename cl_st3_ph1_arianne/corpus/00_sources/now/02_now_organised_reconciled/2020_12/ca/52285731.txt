<h> UN chief wants worldwide ' state of climate emergency ' <p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres has called for a worldwide " state of climate emergency " to tackle global warming . <p> Five years after the Paris Agreement on climate change , the world is still not going in the right direction , he told the Climate Ambition Summit on Saturday co-convened by the UN and the governments of the UK and France , reports Xinhua news agency . <p> The Paris Agreement promised to limit temperature rise to as close to 1.5 degrees Celsius as possible . But the commitments made in Paris were far from enough to get there . And even those commitments are not being met , he noted . <p> " Carbon dioxide levels are at record highs . Today , we are 1.2 degrees hotter than before the industrial revolution . If we do n't change course , we may be headed for a catastrophic temperature rise of more than 3 degrees this century .
