<p> New measures for the Manitoba Climate and Green Plan are expected to deliver approximately 220,000 tonnes of greenhouse-gas ( GHG ) emissions reductions per year . <p> The province has announced new blend requirements for ethanol in gasoline and for biodiesel in diesel . <p> " We are taking the steps necessary to meet our GHG reduction targets , and have extensively worked with and consulted stakeholders , " said Conservation and Climate Minister Sarah Guillemard . " We are leading the nation with our creation of a cumulative GHG reduction goal as part of our carbon savings account -- a made-in-Manitoba solution to address climate change in our province . " <p> Effective January 1 , 2021 the amount of ethanol required in gasoline will increase to 9.25 per cent from 8.5 per cent . <p> It will further grow to 10 per cent on January 1 , 2022 . <p> The biodiesel requirement will increase to 3.5 per cent from two per cent on January 1 , 2021 , and will rise again to five per cent on January 1 , 2022 . <p> These amendments are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's one-megatonne target for the first carbon savings account for the period ending in 2022 . <p> Over time , they will result in yearly reductions of approximately 220,000 tonnes of GHG emissions . <p> When the cities of Winkler , Altona , Morden , Carman , and the rest of Southern Manitoba want local news , weather , and sports online and on-demand , they turn to Pembina Valley Online .
