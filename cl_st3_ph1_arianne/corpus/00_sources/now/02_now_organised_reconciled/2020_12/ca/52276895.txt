Well , it can certainly start tomorrow by extinguishing that flame on Parliament Hill . I do not know how many countless homes that flame could have heated through our cold Canadian winters over the years . I do know that this flame is using precious oxygen to stay ignited . It is creating greenhouse gases . It is needlessly adding to climate change by warming the air around it . It is actually wasting a finite energy resource and taxpayers ' money . <p> In conclusion , if our Canadian government is really serious about going green , this flame should extinguished immediately . <p> ROBERT J.
