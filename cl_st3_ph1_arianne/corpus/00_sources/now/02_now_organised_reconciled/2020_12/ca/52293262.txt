<h> Nearly two-thirds of Toronto Hydro customers feel emergency preparedness is increasingly important now <p> Toronto Hydro is powering preparedness to help keep customers safe this winter season <p> TORONTO , Dec. 16 , 2020 /CNW/ - With the winter season upon us , Toronto Hydro is reminding customers about the power of preparedness when planning ahead for emergencies such as power outages . <p> In a recent poll , nearly two-thirds ( 63% ) of Toronto Hydro customers surveyed agreed that emergency preparedness is more important to them now compared to previous years . With extreme weather events expected to increase in the coming years , we 're doing our part to prepare . However , we also want our customers to take action , especially as many of them may be spending more time at home this winter . <p> We factor climate impacts from extreme weather into our construction standards , system design , and maintenance processes to help us be better prepared to withstand and respond to extreme weather-related grid disruptions <p> We continue to monitor weather patterns around-the-clock and are prepared to mobilize our crews @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We 're working with the City of Toronto to support its First Resilience Strategy -- which focuses on people , neighbourhoods and infrastructure -- to build a city that is resilient to climate change <p> While Toronto Hydro continues to take proactive steps to help harden the grid , extreme weather events such as wind , freezing rain and wet snow still have the potential to damage our equipment and lead to outages . That 's why we want our customers to be prepared to be without power for up to 72 hours in case of a major emergency . <p> What customers can do to prepare : <p> Download our Emergency Preparedness Guide -- now available in eight languages -- for a list of key resources , an emergency preparedness checklist , and tips on what to do before , during and after an outage <p> Stock your emergency kit with essential items , including water , non-perishable food , a flashlight , a battery-powered or crank radio , extra batteries , a first aid kit and cash <p> Keep your emergency kit in an easy-to-access location known to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ -- aging parents , young children and pets may require special preparation <p> Check your kit twice a year to replace any items you 've used or that may be out-of-date <p> " This year more than ever , we 've seen the value of emergency preparedness and being able to adjust to a new normal . For our part , Toronto Hydro is continuing to factor climate change into our standards and processes to help us better respond to increasingly frequent extreme weather . We 're also encouraging our customers to plan ahead this winter so they can keep themselves safe and comfortable in the event of a power outage or other emergency . " <p> Russell Baker , Spokesperson , Toronto Hydro <p> ABOUT TORONTO HYDROToronto Hydro owns and operates the electricity distribution system for Canada 's largest city . Recognized as a Sustainable Electricity Company ? since 2014 , it has approximately 782,000 customers located in the City of Toronto and distributes approximately 18% of the electricity consumed in Ontario .
