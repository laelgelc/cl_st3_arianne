<p> The Living Lab-Eastern Prairies project has been launched throughout the last year on farms within the Upper Oak Lake , Swan Lake , North Shannon Creek and Main Drain watersheds . <p> On Wednesday , Jim Carr , Member of Parliament for Winnipeg South Centre , on behalf of the Honourable Marie-Claude Bibeau , Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food , announced that the Government of Canada is providing up to $5.9 million for on-farm research activities . Under the project farmers and scientists will work in collaboration to develop and implement new technologies and best practices for environmental management . <p> " Manitoba is a world-class leader in agriculture and we are proud to be home to the Living Labs-Eastern Prairies initiative , " said Carr . " Collaboration between our local farmers , watersheds , and government organizations will help keep Manitoba on the cutting edge of ag innovation and research for years to come .
