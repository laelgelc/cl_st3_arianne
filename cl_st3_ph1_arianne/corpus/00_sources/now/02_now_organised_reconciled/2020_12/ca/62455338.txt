Its first reading was on Nov. 5 . <p> The omnibus budget bill includes amendments to dozens of pieces of legislation , including changes to the Conservation Authorities Act in schedule six . <p> Seven members of the Greenbelt Council resigned from their positions in protest to schedule six of the bill , including a former director of community planning for the Region of Waterloo , Kevin Eby and former Toronto mayor and former Conservative federal cabinet minister David Crombie . <p> " This will have significant long-term consequences . And the problem is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to put it back together , " said Eby to the Record previously . <p> " So , the likelihood is that the conservation authorities are effectively neutered at this point , and we 're going to lose some phenomenal expertise that 's so critical to Ontario as we start to look at how do we respond to climate change , how do we respond to significant growth ? " <p> Bill 229 gives authority over issuing development permits to the Minister of Natural Resources and Forestry . <p> Environmental opponents who called for the removal of schedule six from the bill , including Environmental Defence , Ontario Nature and the Canadian Environmental Law Association say the bill allows for political influence to determine environmental , conservation and development decisions over conservation authorities ' watershed-based decision-making . <p> The bill also forces conservation authorities to issue permissions for development if a minister 's zoning order has been passed and the area in question is not within the greenbelt . <p> Minister 's zoning orders bypass public input to change zoning of an area for development . <h> RELATED STORIES <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by Conservation Ontario and others , including allowing up to 30 per cent of board members to be citizens instead of being made entirely of elected municipal officials , returning the main duties of conservation authority board members to furthering the objectives of the conservation authority rather than their individual municipalities , and allowing the yet to be proclaimed ability for conservation authorities to issue stop work orders to persist . <p> " This undermining of Conservation Authorities takes the province back over 70 years , to a more dangerous time when we did n't know better .
