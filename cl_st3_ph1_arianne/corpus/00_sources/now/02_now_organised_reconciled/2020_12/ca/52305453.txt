<p> TORONTO -- Nuclear power is essential to meeting Canada 's climate-change goals , and developing portable mini nuclear reactors is a key part of that strategy , the federal energy minister said on Friday . <p> In announcing an " action plan " for developing small modular reactors , Seamus O'Regan said the new technology has the potential to help Canada cut emissions and stimulate economic growth . <p> " I believe in the development of this technology , " O'Regan said from St. John 's , N.L. " You 've got to lay the groundwork for that now . " <p> Among steps in the plan is developing prototypes and demonstration models . <p> O'Regan did not offer any new funding as part of the announcement of the reactor plan to which dozens of private , public , educational and Indigenous partners contributed . <p> Small modular reactors -- SMRs -- would be smaller in size and energy output than traditional nuclear power units , and more flexible in their deployment .
