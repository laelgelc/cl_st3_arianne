<h> The Drilldown : COVID hurting oilsands operations in Alberta <p> Oilsands operations in Alberta are struggling with the spread of the COVID-19 virus , with seven sites in the Fort McMurray region currently on the provincial outbreak list . <p> There are hundreds of cases currently linked to the sites in the northern health zone , where this week , around 13 per cent of people tested for the virus were found to be positive . Also on the outbreak list are the Sturgeon Refinery and Heartland Petrochemical Complex . <p> In an effort to combat the virus , internal occupational health teams within energy companies are focusing on contact tracing . <p> At Heartland Petrochemical , for instance , contact tracers are examining all on-site locations where workers that have been diagnosed with the virus and reaching out to people with whom they have been in contact . Once secondary contacts are identified , the contact tracers notify them , send them home and arrange a COVID-19 .
