<p> Dec. 12 marked the fifth anniversary of the Paris Agreement , a day that brought world leaders together to celebrate and recognize the resolve of so many to work toward a safer , more resilient world with net-zero emissions -- a world we can be proud to leave to our grandchildren . <p> The last five years have certainly tested the determination of the international community on the climate-action front , even before the pandemic hit , leaving devastated livelihoods and economies in its wake . <p> One may wonder , then , whether it 's realistic to call for stronger climate action during a pandemic . We say the case is more relevant now than ever . Looking at forest fires and flooding in Canada , the droughts and devastating swarms of locusts in Africa , infrastructure collapsing in Siberia as permafrost melts , and typhoons in Asia , a more pressing question is : Can we afford not to ? <p> The science is irrefutable ; for future prosperity , we must move beyond the harmful carbon economy and invest in greening the global economy .
