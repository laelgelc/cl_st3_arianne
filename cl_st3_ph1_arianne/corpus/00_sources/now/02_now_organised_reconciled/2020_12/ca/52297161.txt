<h> WWF-Canada announces new Chair of the Board , Lloyd Bryant <p> Toronto , Dec. 16 , 2020 ( GLOBE NEWSWIRE ) -- WWF-Canada is pleased to announce that Lloyd Bryant has been named the new chair of its Board of Directors . Mr. Bryant is the retired president and CEO of HP Canada , where he was a champion of sustainability in his sector . He has served on WWF-Canada 's board since 2014 . <p> Julie Gelfand also joins WWF-Canada as Board Director , bringing over 35 years of experience in the field of environment , sustainable development and corporate social responsibility for government , NGOs and the private sector .
