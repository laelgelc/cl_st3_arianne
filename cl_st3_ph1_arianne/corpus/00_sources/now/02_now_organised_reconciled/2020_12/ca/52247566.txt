It has n't quite managed this , but in mid-November , the government introduced the Canadian Net Zero Emissions Accountability Act . <p> Bill C-12 is not a plan but it specifies there must be one within nine months . <p> Now a government press release states : " Experience at home and around the world shows that understanding the risks and opportunities posed by climate change and the emerging low-carbon economy is now an essential part of good decision-making .
