( Courtesy Cameco ) <p> Cigar Lake uranium mine in northern Saskatchewan confirmed its first positive case of COVID-19 over the weekend . An employee tested positive for the virus on Saturday , and has been in isolation at the mine since November 22 . <p> " This is the first positive COVID case we have experienced at Cigar Lake . Contact tracing for the individual was completed through the Northern Population Health Unit , " Cameco spokesperson Jeff Hryhoriw told the Prince Albert Daily Herald on Monday . <p> " Cigar Lake continues to operate safely , and we are monitoring the situation very closely . We recognize developments around the pandemic are fluid , and will move quickly to adjust our plans should circumstances warrant .
