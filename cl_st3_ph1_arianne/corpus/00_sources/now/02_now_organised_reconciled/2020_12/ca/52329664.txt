<h> Why humans are n't hardwired to respond well to crises <p> Common wisdom has it that the lesson of COVID-19 is that we are capable of marshalling a mass , evidence-driven response in an emergency . But the bigger lesson is that we are n't actually very good at this . <p> It 's not a criticism -- it 's just the way our brains are wired .
