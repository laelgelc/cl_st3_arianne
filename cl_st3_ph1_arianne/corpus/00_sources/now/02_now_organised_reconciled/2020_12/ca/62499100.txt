If you guessed a tree , you 'd be right . <p> I 'm horrified when I see trees recklessly and needlessly chopped down . Just because it 's easier and cheaper to do so before building does n't mean we should . In fact , in Iceland , in the early days of the pandemic , the Forestry Service was encouraging people to hug trees to help deal with social isolation . <p> Being in nature , especially in a forest , is both mentally and physically good for us . Knowing this , British Columbia Parks Foundation recently started " guided nature therapy " sessions for health care workers to reduce their stress levels . I encourage everyone to do the same whether it 's on your own or through the multitude of organized and guided nature hikes , naturalist groups , forest bathing , and yoga hikes . <p> Germany creatively protects forests with more than 60 friedwalds , or forest cemeteries , where the cremated ashes of your dearly departed can be worked into the soil at the base of the trees .
