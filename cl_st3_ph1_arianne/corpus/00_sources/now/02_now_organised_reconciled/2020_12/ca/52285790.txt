<p> The Clean Fuel Standard ( CFS ) will be revealed by the federal government and published in the Canada Gazette by the end of the year , Moira Kelly , spokesperson for Environment and Climate Change Minister Jonathan Wilkinson , confirmed yesterday . <p> Canada 's CFS will consist of regulations requiring fuel providers and refineries to reduce their emissions by 12 per cent below 2016 levels by 2030 . The CFS will also introduce a nationwide carbon-credit trading system , with harsh per-tonne penalties . In implementing the standard , Canada will become the first country in the world to extend its regulations to gaseous fuels , such as propane and natural gas , and solid fuels , such as coal , by 2023 . <p> Industry representatives are worried that the timeline to implement the CFS is too short , and that they wo n't have time to make the necessary changes . <p> " If it 's not done right , we 're going to lose existing infrastructure , " said Bob Laracque , CEO of the Canadian Fuels Association .
