<p> In Friday 's announcement that the federal carbon tax would soar to $170 a tonne by 2030 , adding 28 cents to the average price of a litre of gasoline , some Canadians see further evidence that Prime Minister Justin Trudeau is joining a global revolution -- a wholesale , tax-induced adoption of less mobile , more vegetarian and generally far less consumptive lifestyles in the world 's wealthiest countries , with the COVID-19 pandemic serving as a catalyst . <p> Nothing Trudeau has ever said or done makes me think he believes especially strongly in that vision or has the backbone to stake his job on it .
