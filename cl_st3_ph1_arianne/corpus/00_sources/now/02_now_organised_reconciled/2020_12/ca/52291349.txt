<h> The Drilldown : Government to spend $3 billion to plant two billion trees <p> The federal government is planning to spend $3.16 billion to plant two billion trees over the next 10 years , Natural Resources Minister Seamus O'Regan announced Monday . The program is to start in the spring . <p> " There is no path to net-zero carbon emissions that does n't involve our forests , " O'Regan said . " Trees increase our community 's long-term resilience to climate change . They reduce the likelihood of flooding and the risk of wildland fires . " <p> The tree planting will support Canada 's goal of emitting net-zero greenhouse gases by 2050 , O'Regan added . The program will create 4300 jobs and cover an area about twice the size of Prince Edward Island . <p> The announcement comes just over a year after Prime Minister Justin Trudeau 's 2019 campaign promise to plant two billion trees over a decade . <p> O'Regan said the delay was due both to the pandemic and the complexity of the planning process .
