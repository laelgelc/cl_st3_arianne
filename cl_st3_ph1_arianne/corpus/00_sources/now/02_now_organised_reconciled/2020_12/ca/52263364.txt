<p> The Ministry of Environment is asking hunters to submit the heads of deer , moose and elk harvested this hunting season for chronic wasting disease ( CWD ) testing . <p> CWD is a fatal , infectious central nervous system disease affecting mammals in the deer family that has no known cure . It may or may not be able to spread among humans . <p> " It is possible and plausible although very low that CWD could transmit to humans . So far we have not seen that but of course we are taking precautionary measures to make sure that does n't happen , " Terrestrial Biologist Rick Espie told the Prince Albert Daily Herald . <p> Although no human case of CWD has ever been identified , the province recommends that hunters avoid eating the meat until they receive their test results . <p> " A good safe rule of thumb is to not consume an animal until you 've had it tested and not to consume it until you get those test results back .
