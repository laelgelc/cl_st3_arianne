<h> Buhari approves establishment of 10 additional national parks in Nigeria <p> President Muhammadu Buhari has approved the establishment of 10 additional national parks in Nigeria . <p> The Minister of Environment , Mohammad Abubakar , who disclosed this at a press briefing in Abuja , said the development brought the number of national parks in the country to 17 . <p> Some of the national parks in Nigeria are the Kainji Lake National Park in Niger State , the Gashaka Gumti National Park located between Adamawa and Taraba States , the Old Oyo National Park in Oyo State and the Yankari Games Reserve in Bauchi . <p> The minister said an executive bill would be sent to the National Assembly on the development in the coming days . <p> He said : " The president has graciously approved the establishment of 10 additional national parks bringing the total number of the national parks in the country to 17 . <p> " To give credence and legal backing to this worthy cause , an executive bill will soon be presented to the National Assembly for further necessary action . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ become imperative in view of the prevailing effects of climate change across the globe . <p> " This action is geared towards the United Nations policy of placing 25 percent of its member countries ' landmass under permanent vegetation cover for carbon sequestration to mitigate the effects of climate change and ameliorate other ecological challenges .
