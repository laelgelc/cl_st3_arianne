<p> A record 235 million people are expected to be in need of humanitarian aid in 2021 because of the COVID-19 pandemic , 40 percent more than this year , UN forecasts published on Tuesday have indicated . <p> " The rich world can now see the light at the end of the tunnel . The same is not true in the poorest countries , " UN humanitarian aid chief , Mark Lowcock , said in a statement . <p> " The COVID-19 crisis has plunged millions of people into poverty and sent humanitarian needs skyrocketing , " he added . <p> In addition to natural disasters , conflicts , and climate change , the pandemic has created additional hardship , as extreme poverty and the risk of famines are rising , said UN 's emergency aid office OCHA . <p> OCHA is asking donor countries for 35 million dollars to provide food , shelter and medical care for 160 million people in 56 countries in 2021 . Other organisations like the Red Cross are set to help the rest of the 235 million people . <p> For 2020 , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than half of this amount had been offered to the UN office as of late November . <p> The projected sum for 2021 is lower , because UN appeals are becoming more targeted and more efficient , said OCHA spokesperson Jens Laerke . <p> " The situation going into 2021 is that there are more people in need , but that does n't mean that the response they require is the same , ' ' he said .
