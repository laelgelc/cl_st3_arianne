<h> Trump : Leaving World Politics Worse Than He Met It , By Owei Lakemfa <p> Not satisfied with re-drawing the map of the Middle East , Trump this week decided to redraw the map of Africa by inserting the Saharawi Arab Democratic Republic , also known as Western Sahara , into Morocco . In this , the out-going American president , in the manner of colonialists and imperialists , seeks to reduce the member countries of the African Union ( AU ) from 55 to 54 . <p> President Donald Trump is a joke carried too far . He is a problem child the United States imposed on the universe . There is an African saying that if you give birth to a problem child , it is your responsibility to keep such child in check . Americans formally performed that onerous responsibility this Monday when its Electoral College voted 306-232 for Joe Biden as the incoming American president . In other words , Trump now has less than five weeks to vacate the White House . <p> Aberrations like Trump litter the landscape of history . Cultured Germany that produced famous philosophers like Georg Hegel , incredible human minds like Karl Marx and matchless cultural icons like Bertolt Brecht , also produced Adolf Hitler . The same France , which produced Rene Descartes , Voltaire , Albert Camus , Auguste Comte and Simone de Beauvoir , and gave humanity the inspiring slogan of ' Liberty , Equality and Fraternity ' , was the same that produced mass butchers in countries like Vietnam and Algeria , exterminating people for making similar demands . I am not sure how many people saw the possibility of a racist , tax-dogging , playboy with questionable deals and lack of respect for women , emerging an American president in a highly globalised world . <p> As the 2016 primaries progressed , the focus of some in the Republican Party was not in winning the election , but in stopping Trump . He was clearly on the rampage , accusing Mexicans of being criminals and rapists , and Muslims of being Jihadists who : " have no sense of reason or respect for human life . " He campaigned for the legalisation of torture on the international scene and a repeal of the Affordable Care Act , popularly known as Obamacare . He saw women as play objects and spoke of the Democratic Party candidate , Hillary Clinton , in sexual terms : " If Hillary Clinton ca n't satisfy her husband what makes her think she can satisfy America ? " For him , the media are devils who report falsehood . <p> Trump believes he is ordained to win any contest he engages in and in 2016 declared that if he lost to Hillary Clinton , the results would be unacceptable , as the elections must have been rigged . He still manifests this delusion by refusing to concede defeat to Biden , even after the Electoral College votes . Four years of Trumpism has turned many aspects of world politics upside down and in some instances , reduced America to a laughing stock . <p> Trump also withdrew the U.S. from membership of the World Health Organisation , thereby weakening the joint human response to fighting the pandemic . For instance , he has recognised illegal Israeli settlements in the seized Syrian Golan Heights . <p> Imagine a non-scientist who relies on beliefs , declaring in a magisterial manner that climate change is a hoax . " He discouraged the wearing of masks and encouraged civil resistance against the lockdown programmes of states in the country designed to slow down the rate of infection . This doubtlessly contributed to the American infection rate , which , as at December 15 , stood at over 16,700,000 , with 304,000 COVID-19-related deaths ; the highest casualty figures in the world . Unfortunately , he influenced the copycat actions of Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro , which has seen that country record 737,597 infections and 181,402 deaths . <p> Trump also withdrew the U.S. from membership of the World Health Organisation , thereby weakening the joint human response to fighting the pandemic . He has also recognised illegal Israeli settlements in the West Bank , which even the racist Zionists in Israeli acknowledge as not being Israeli territory . Trump has also been involved in getting some Arab countries to reject the fundamental human rights of Palestinians to a homeland and self-determination by ditching Palestinian rights in favour of being in bed with the racists in Tel Aviv . <p> Not satisfied with re-drawing the map of the Middle East , Trump this week decided to redraw the map of Africa by inserting the Saharawi Arab Democratic Republic , also known as Western Sahara , into Morocco . It is instructive that Western Sahara was admitted into the Organisation of African Unity ( OAU ) in 1982 and is a founding member of the AU . <p> Africans say no matter how bad a person is , he must have positive aspects ; so is it with Trump . To provoke Iran into a war , Trump , in January , ordered the assassination of Iranian General Qasem Soleimani , while on a diplomatic mission in Iraq . He had tried a similar tactic on Syria in 2016 by ordering a missile attack on a Syrian air base . Also , he tried provoking American neighbour , Mexico , by declaring it criminal and swearing to build a wall between both countries , with the bill going to Mexico . So those countries wisely ignored the Trump provocations . <p> The Trump government has almost pushed the opposition in Venezuela into oblivion , first by encouraging it to carry out a coup against elected President Nicolas Maduro .
