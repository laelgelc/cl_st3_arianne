" <p> The top UN official stressed that " poverty , inequality and hunger are rising " while jobs are disappearing and debts are mounting . <p> " Children are struggling ; violence in the home is increasing and insecurity is everywhere . " <p> On a positive note , the UN chief said that " a New Year lies ahead and with it , we see rays of hope " . <p> " People extending a helping hand to neighbours and strangers ; frontline workers giving their all ; scientists developing vaccines in record time and countries making new commitments to prevent climate catastrophe , " the secretary-general elaborated . <p> " If we work together in unity and solidarity , these rays of hope can reach around the world . <p> " That 's the lesson of this most difficult year . <p> " Both climate change and the COVID-19 pandemic are crises that can only be addressed by everyone together , as part of a transition to an inclusive and sustainable future . " <p> Guterres said the central ambition of the UN for 2021 is to build a global coalition for carbon neutrality -- net zero emissions -- by 2050.
