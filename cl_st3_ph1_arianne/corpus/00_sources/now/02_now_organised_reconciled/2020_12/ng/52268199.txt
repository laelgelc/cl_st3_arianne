<h> Nestle To Tackle Carbon Emission With $3.5bn <p> World largest food company , Nestle plans to spend $3.58bn to reduce carbon emission in the next five years . <p> Nestle said this on Thursday in a statement seen by THE WHISTLER . <p> The company in 2018 emitted 92 million tonnes of greenhouse gases , but targets to reduce climate impact to zero emission by 2050 . <p> Nestle said , " The company expects to invest a total of CHF 3.2 billion over the next five years to accelerate our work , including CHF to spark regenerative agriculture across the company 's supply chain . " <p> The company which has huge presence in Nigeria 's consumer goods sector said it would finance its investments in renewable energy through operational and structural efficiencies to keep the initiative earnings neutral . <p> Based on the plan , Nestle would reduce half its emission rate in the next ten years , while it also targets 100 per cent of renewable electricity in five years time .
