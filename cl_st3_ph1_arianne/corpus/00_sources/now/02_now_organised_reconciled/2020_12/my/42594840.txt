<h> Social activist : Imminent end of Civid-19 pandemic heralds a new renaissance <p> KUCHING : The development of the Covid-19 vaccine in a relatively short time is ' the silver lining to the dark cloud ' of one worst ever crises and disasters that mankind had gone through in 2020 , said social worker Datuk Seri Ang Lai Soon . <p> Looking forward to the New Year , he said the development of the vaccine would be an amazing achievement if it could put an end to the Covid-19 scourge . <p> " Indeed , it is a good cause for celebration as it gives mankind confidence in the otherwise not so cheerful and bleak future . <p> " This incredible achievement could also lead to other problems being solved , such as a cure for cancer and other incurable diseases , and indeed , the colossal environmental problem -- the climate change . <p> " It appears that when we do put our minds to it , the human race can overcome insurmountable problems , and triumph over the worst that nature might have for the human race @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ end of the Covid-19 pandemic would herald ' the dawn of a second renaissance on a global scale ' . <p> " The human race is entering a completely new phase of relationship with planet Earth , beginning a move from exploitation to guardianship of all its bounty in all its aspects . <p> " The steps taken to counter the pandemic undeniably improved the environment . We must recognise that the problem of global warming that is leading to increasingly unstable climatic conditions needs to be addressed . <p> " Let us ensure that 2021 begins a ' long march ' to living in equilibrium with nature , before it is too late . <p> " Let us learn to treasure life in all its beauty , the importance of fostering life in all its forms , maintaining the complexity of the global integrated ecosystem , and most importantly , of recognising that life in all its forms is sacrosanct . <p> " Happy New Year to all wherever you may be . God bless us all , " he added .
