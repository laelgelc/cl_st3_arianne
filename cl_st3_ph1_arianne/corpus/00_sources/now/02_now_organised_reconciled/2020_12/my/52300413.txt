<p> DESPITE the constant negative publicity on palm oil , Denis J Murphy , Professor of Biotechnology at University of South Wales in the UK , is optimistic that oil palm can become one of the world 's leading crops . <p> And he sees better days ahead for the crop as scientists use technology to improve the palm tree 's physiology , yield and nutritional value . Using gene editing tools , namely CRISPR and biotechnology , Murphy is confident that new breeds of oil palms will overcome the many issues the crop faces now . <p> With limits on land expansion , the way forward is through biotechnology to create breeds of palms with higher oil yields . Biotech is also the answer to a healthier oil by lowering its composition of saturated fats ( palmitic and stearic acids ) and raising its unsaturated fats ( oleic acid ) content .
