<h> CCC , UNDP spearhead devt of climate-smart buildings standards <p> QUEZON CITY , Dec. 15 ( PIA ) -- Representatives from the building industry gathered for a two-day online conference on the development of country standards on climate-smart buildings ( CSB ) as part of the 13th Annual Global Warming and Climate Change Consciousness Week . <p> The event , organized by the Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) together with the UN Development Programme ( UNDP ) , was streamed on Facebook live . <p> As the country continues to face the adverse impacts of climate change such as extreme weather events , the CCC said that design standards for buildings must incorporate elements that make CSBs the first line of defense against natural climate-related disasters . <p> The building sector also accounts for nearly 40 percent of the global energy-related carbon emissions , making it a critical player in the country 's low carbon transition efforts .
