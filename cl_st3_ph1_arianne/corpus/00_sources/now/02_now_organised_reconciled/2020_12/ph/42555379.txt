<h> Palawan wildlife rescue center now under NRDC supervision <p> TO generate higher revenues and pursue financial sustainability , supervision over the Palawan Wildlife Rescue and Conservation Center ( PWRCC ) has been transferred to the Natural Resources Development Corp . ( NRDC ) , the corporate arm of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) . <p> In DENR Administrative Order ( DAO ) No. 2020-10 recently issued by Secretary Roy Cimatu , the transfer of supervision from the Biodiversity Management Bureau ( BMB ) to NRDC was in line with the need to streamline the supervision over PWRCC , formerly known as the Crocodile Farm and Nature Park . <p> The PWRCC is a breeding place for the endangered Philippine crocodile , as well as a research and rescue center for other species endemic to Palawan , such as bearcats and Philippine cockatoos . <p> Included in the transfer are all documents , records , equipment , facilities , supplies and materials , concerned personnel except those holding permanent items in DENR-MIMAROPA Region , budget and all related matters . <p> " Within three months @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and the DENR-MIMAROPA Region shall formulate and implement smooth transition of the transfer and develop collaborative arrangements to strengthen the operations of the Center , to be contained in a Memorandum of Agreement ( MOA ) , " as stated in the order . <p> The order also stated that within the transition period , the NRDC shall develop a business plan for PWRCC , as well as identify new , innovative , competitive and related business ventures , with the objective of financial sustainability . <p> Under the DAO , NRDC is authorized to update and fix prices , fees and charges related to PWRCC 's commercial and business operations , in accordance with existing laws , rules and regulations . <p> However , the BMB will continue to enforce its regulatory authorities over the crocodile and other wildlife species in PWRCC . <p> Meanwhile , BMB Director Ricardo Calderon , also Assistant Secretary for Climate Change , said the bureau will continue to provide technical assistance and guidance in the management and conservation of the wildlife species in the center . <p> " The BMB has crocodiles , as well as as for the care and rehabilitation of rescued , donated and confiscated wildlife species in Palawan , " Calderon said . <p> He added that the NRDC 's business plan will be developed " in consultation with the BMB to ensure that the conservation aspect of PWRCC 's operations are adequately addressed .
