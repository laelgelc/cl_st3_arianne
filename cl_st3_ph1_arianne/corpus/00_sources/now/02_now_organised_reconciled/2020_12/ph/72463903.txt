<h> NAPC moves for establishment of prov'l consultative body <p> ILIGAN CITY , Dec. 1 ( PIA ) -- National Anti-Poverty Commission ( NAPC ) Secretary and Lead Convenor Noel Felongco said on Tuesday that the agency pushes for the establishment of a Provincial Consultative Body ( PCB ) in all provinces in the country . <p> " PCB is a mechanism that aims to promote valuable participation of the basic sectors in the creation , implementation and monitoring of anti-poverty strategies in the province , " said Felongco during the Regional Inter-agency Convergence Initiative towards Inclusive Development in the Province of Lanao Del Norte . <p> The NAPC executive also said that PCB seeks to institutionalize close coordination and cooperation among government agencies , local government units , and the communities . <p> " The government collaborative and convergent efforts coupled with vigorous participation of the people especially those at the community and grassroots level are significant weapons to attain an inclusive development in the province and in the whole country , " he added . <p> Felongco emphasized that social participation is a vital factor in achieving just @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and development is everyone 's shared responsibility . <p> " Sustaining peace and development is not just the government 's task , all of us here right have roles to play and we are accountable for a peaceful future of the next generation , " he said . <p> According to NAPC , Lanao del Norte is among the Top 40 poorest provinces in the country in 2019 and is also identified as the most vulnerable to climate change . <p> With this , the anti-poverty czar seeks for more fruitful partnership and collaboration with various stakeholders in the province to attain a more peaceful and prosperous Lanao del Norte .
