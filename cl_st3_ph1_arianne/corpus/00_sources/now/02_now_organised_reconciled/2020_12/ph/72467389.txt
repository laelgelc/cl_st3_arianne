<h> Rice sector to get biggest chunk of DA 's budget <p> MANILA -- Rice sector will remain the priority of the Department of Agriculture 's ( DA ) modernization and industrialization programs next year . <p> This , after the Senate voted 22-0 to approve the proposed PHP4.5 trillion national budget for 2021 on successive second and third readings on Thursday . <p> Under the proposed general appropriations law or House Bill No. 7727 , the DA will get PHP85.6 billion , including PHP60.9 billion allocated to the Office of the Secretary which manages seven commodity banner programs , bureaus , central and regional field offices , as well as locally-funded and foreign-assisted projects . <p> " Majority of the agriculture players are rice producers , " DA field operations director Roy Abaya said on Thursday when asked why DA maintains its focus on the rice sector . <p> According to the 2021National Expenditure Program for Rice presentation , DA envisions to " maximize income opportunities in the global market while promoting inclusive growth " . <p> Agriculture Secretary William Dar thanked the legislators for their hard work in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " On behalf of the DA family , I sincerely thank our Senators , especially our main sponsor , Senator ( Cynthia ) Villar , for this favorable action on our proposed 2021 budget that will sustain the implementation of our umbrella Plant , Plant , Plant program and continued modernization and industrialization of Philippine agriculture , " Dar said in a news release on Thursday . <p> Dar said he is hoping for continued guidance and budget support from the lawmakers " to boost the productivity of our farm and fishery resources , increase the incomes of farmers and fisherfolk , achieve global competitiveness , and ensure greater sustainability and resilience from natural disasters , climate change impacts , and global pandemics . " ( PNA )
