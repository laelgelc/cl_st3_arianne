But " as a co-benefit , it will help to reduce carbon dioxide emissions . " <p> He said he was confident that China , which accounts for about 40% of growth in world energy demand , would move ahead with its policies , noting it has met other strict targets in the past for reducing population growth , sustained economic growth and bringing electricity to half a billion people in rural areas . <p> Birol said China is perhaps more likely to meet its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development .
