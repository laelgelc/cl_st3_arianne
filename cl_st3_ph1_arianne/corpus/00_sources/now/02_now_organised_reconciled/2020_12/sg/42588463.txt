<h> Eco-Business Special Reports <h> This section features in-depth special reports by our editorial team on various issues related to sustainable development in the region and beyond . <p> With the global population set to rise from 7 billion today to almost 10 billion in 35 years , feeding the sheer number of people will be one of the world 's greatest challenges . To mark World Food Day on 16 October , Eco-Business looks at the global food system and why we need to be smarter about how we grow , consume and manage food . <p> New research has traced the unrest in Syria and unfolding European migrant crisis to a drought induced by worsening climate change . Could a similar fate be in store for Asia , a region most vulnerable to its impacts ?
