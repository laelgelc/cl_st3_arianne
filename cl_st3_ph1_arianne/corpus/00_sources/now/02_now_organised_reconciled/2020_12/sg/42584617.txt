<h> People may not receive cash from REDD scheme <h> Share <p> Local people involved in efforts to protect forests may not receive money in cash since the government 's ongoing forest carbon scheme pilot projects did not use a cash handout system . <p> Forestry Ministry director for environmental services Tony Suhartono said the government had not settled the financial mechanism of the reducing emissions from deforestation and forest degradation ( REDD ) scheme . <p> " We may prevent directly providing cash to local people to promote sustainable REDD projects , " he said Tuesday . <p> Tony said previous projects in West Kutai , East Kalimantan , when the government gave concessions to local people showed the risk of handing out cash . <p> " The local people cut trees in their concession areas and sell it to get money . It renders the project unsustainable , " he said . <p> Millions of Indonesian rely on forests for their livelihoods . <p> The government has said the high poverty of people living near forests was the main driver of deforestation , leading to illegal logging . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1 million hectares per year . <p> The government said REDD could be effective in Indonesia if the main instruments of deforestation , especially illegal logging and forest conversion , were properly addressed . <p> The REDD scheme was expected to be an alternative to cutting emissions to deal with climate change . <p> Deforestation contributes some 20 percent of global emissions . <p> Under the scheme , forest nations would receive financial incentives from rich nations to stop converting their forests to prevent the release of carbon into the atmosphere . <p> The government is developing REDD pilot projects in the forests of Central Kalimantan , East Kalimantan , East Java and West Nusa Tenggara provinces . <p> The governments of Australia , Germany and Korea have developed REDD pilot projects in Indonesia . <p> The projects would assess , among others , the drivers of deforestation , institutional and legal frameworks and the establishment of REDD incentive mechanisms . <p> The Environment Ministry said money from REDD schemes should be used to empower local people to encourage them to protect forests . <p> " The money could be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ local area , " Masnellyarti Hilman , the deputy minister for environmental damage control at the Environment Ministry , said . <p> Indonesia has signed a new agreement with the Norwegian government to reduce deforestation in Indonesia 's forests and peatland . <p> In turn , Norway pledged to provide US$1 billion that would be disbursed based on emission reductions in the forest sector .
