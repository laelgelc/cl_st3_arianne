It will buy allowances from Tianjin Jinqiang Building Energy Saving Industry Development Co. for an undisclosed price . <p> Stephen Thomas , a Citigroup spokesman based in Shanghai , could n't immediately provide any comments . <p> China , the world 's biggest emitter , is a participant in last year 's Copenhagen Accord and pledged to cut its CO2 output per unit of gross domestic product by as much as 45 percent through 2020 . The new program established by the Tianjin Climate Exchange and Arreon New Energy Co. initially covers heating suppliers serving more than 2 million square meters of residential buildings . <p> Tianjin 's municipal government will set an emissions- intensity cap , the statement said .
