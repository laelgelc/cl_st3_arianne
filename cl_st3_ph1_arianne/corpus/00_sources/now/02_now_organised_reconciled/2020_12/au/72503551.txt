The councillors dumped their concreting decision after a presentation by students who had participated in a growing movement empowering young people throughout Australia . Today 's guest in The Zone is the founder and managing director of that movement -- Kids Teaching Kids , an organisation that for the past 15 years has been building peer-learning programs , in which children become the teachers . " Wood 's Mildura home was literally on the banks of the Murray River ; his awareness of nature became ingrained by this proximity , and by parents , both teachers , who encouraged his environmental passion . He studied forestry at the University of Melbourne and had worked in the water industry . It has driven him far . At the last municipal elections , he won a seat on Melbourne City Council , where he chairs the environment portfolio and is deputy chairman of the economic development portfolio . His innovation with Kids Teaching Kids led him to be named Young Australian of the Year ( Environment ) in 2001 . In 2006 , he won the United Nations ' Individual Award for Outstanding Service to the Environment . He won the Prime Minister 's Environmentalist of the Year the following year . In 2000 , he set up an environmental communication and education consultancy , Firestarter . He distilled his experience into a book , Billabong Boy , published in 2010 . " He cites a workshop at Mildura West Primary School , where students researched the life cycle of the carp , an environmental pest in many rivers . They made models of carp , and showed how the eggs are laid . They made a model of a weir and demonstrated that changing the water level at the wrong time of the year exacerbates the carp invasion . Students in Canberra learnt about alternative energy by making a series of solar-powered model cars , with which they then produced a musical , adapting The Wizard of Oz . Wood says the educational benefits can cascade ; projects will often involve using mathematics and statistics to measure flora and fauna , others use filmmaking , dance and decorative arts .
