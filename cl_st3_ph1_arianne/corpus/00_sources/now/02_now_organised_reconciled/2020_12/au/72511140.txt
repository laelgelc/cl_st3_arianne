The researchers found that almost 25 kilograms of carbon dioxide equivalent emissions were created for each kilogram of beef produced . <p> Broiler chickens also had a high emissions footprint ( 13.2 kilograms of CO2-eq per kilo of meat ) , followed by pork ( 5.5 kilograms of CO2-eq per kilo of meat ) .
