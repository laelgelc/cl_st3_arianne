Planktic forams grow their own shells using calcium carbonate , the material used to make the same shells you can find on the beach . Since planktic forams are so tiny , you need a microscope to see their shells . When forams grow their shells , they incorporate the chemistry of the water into them , which allows researchers to reconstruct the specific water conditions under which they lived .
