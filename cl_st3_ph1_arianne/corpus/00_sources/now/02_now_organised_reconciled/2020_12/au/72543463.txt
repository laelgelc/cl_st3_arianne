Picture : David McNew/Getty Images/AFPSource:AFP <p> THE federal government has reportedly been sitting on almost a year 's worth of pollution data . <p> Australia 's greenhouse gas emissions have risen sharply since the government last released its quarterly data in December , according to independent estimates . <p> Fairfax Media reports that documents obtained under freedom of information laws by the Australian Conservation Foundation ( ACF ) reveal the government has n't published data on greenhouse pollution for the two quarters leading up to the end of last year . <p> ACF chief executive Kelly O'Shanassy said the Turnbull government was going to " extreme lengths " to remove global warming from the political agenda . <p> " Hiding pollution is n't @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change and energy policy , but it 's exactly what you would do if you knew you were going to fail to meet your international commitments to reduce pollution , " she told Fairfax Media . <p> " We are concerned Australia is not going to meet its 2030 target to reduce emissions by 26-28 per cent . " <p> She added : " Australians deserve transparency . I think its crystal clear the Australian government is not taking its commitment to reduce pollution seriously . "
