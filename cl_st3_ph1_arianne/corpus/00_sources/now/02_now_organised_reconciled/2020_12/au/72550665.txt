<h> Thousands of Australian students strike for climate action <p> Thousands of students across Australia are skipping school to demand more action on climate change . They join millions around the world taking part in a Global Climate Strike , which comes ahead of the United Nations Climate Action Summit in New York on 23 September . Australia 's School Strike 4 Climate demonstrators have a list of three demands for the federal government to meet - no new coal , oil or gas projects , reaching 100 per cent renewable energy generation and exports by 2030 , and assisting fossil fuel workers impacted by climate crisis with the transition into new employment . The rallies are inspired by Swedish teenager Greta Thunberg who is waging an international climate campaign . Image : News Corp Australia
