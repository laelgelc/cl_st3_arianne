<p> With 18 months of training and experience under my belt , I still felt like a fresh-faced firefighter at the start of the 2019 summer . Far from my wetsuit and longboard , sandy feet and salty hair , a bright orange volunteer uniform felt foreign for this time of year , when I would normally be soaking in vitamin D and waiting for the thrill of the next wave . It was hard to escape talk of the fires in my home town of Anglesea in NSW , it was everywhere ; the TV , radio , and social media news feeds . It was in conversations with strangers in the supermarket and friends and family over dinner . As someone who cares deeply about the environment , from years spent on a surfboard , travelling in and out of coastal towns in search of the perfect wave and building strong friendships in many coastal communities , I knew I could n't sit idle and watch as these assets of Australia burned .
