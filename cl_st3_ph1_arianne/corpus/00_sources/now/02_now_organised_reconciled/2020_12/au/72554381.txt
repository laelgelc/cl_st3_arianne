<p> No surprise this year of rolling crises has spawned a raft of expressions we 'll forever associate with 2020 . Here 's my list of the good , the bad and the ugly , forged in fire , flood and pandemic . SmoKo This almost tops the list as the favourite of 2020 . The daggy dad PM 's extraordinary lapses in judgment during the bushfire crisis came back to bite him . Social media was lightning fast , changing his Scomo nickname to SmoKo . And it went on from there . When he lapsed into his trademark smirk , he became Smirko .
