In some places , water and soil quality and supply will be compromised due to the intrusion of saltwater . Flooding and wave power will damage infrastructure , including drinking water and sanitation services . And disease vector ecology will also change , such as higher mosquito densities in coastal habitats , potentially causing greater transmission of infectious diseases like dengue or malaria . <p> However , people and communities may adapt by moving away .
