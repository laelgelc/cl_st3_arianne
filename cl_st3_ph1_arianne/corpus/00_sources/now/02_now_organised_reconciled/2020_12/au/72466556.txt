<p> THE WESTERN AUSTRALIAN government have implemented a new streamline process for evaluating sustainable development sites that have the potential to boost the state 's economy and increase tourism . <p> The strategy , called a significant development assessment pathway , is a short-term amendment to the Planning and Development Act 2005 , Planning and Development ( Local Planning Scheme ) Regulations 2015 and State planning policies , to assist with COVID-19 recovery . It grants temporary authority to the Western Australian Planning Commission ( WAPC ) to approve development projects equal to $20 million in metropolitan areas and $5 million for regional areas . <p> From a tourism and economic perspective , the amendment is beneficial , but there may be unforeseen issues for areas of environmental significance under consideration for development . <p> In the Great Southern region of Western Australia is the coastal city of Albany .
