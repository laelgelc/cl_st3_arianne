<p> The Trump administration had a slam dunk way to improve air quality and save lives . Instead , the Environmental Protection Agency flat-out rejected the opportunity to tighten regulations on soot from fossil fuel power plants , tailpipes , incinerators , and industrial other sources on Monday . <p> The EPA is required to review its National Ambient Air Quality Standards regulations every five years . The rule currently caps companies ' emissions of fine particulate pollution , also known as PM2.5 or soot , at 12 micrograms per cubic metre of air each year . In a draft report last year , the agency 's own staff scientists said that lowering that standard to 9 micrograms per cubic metre could save roughly 12,200 lives a year that are lost to the health impacts of pollution .
