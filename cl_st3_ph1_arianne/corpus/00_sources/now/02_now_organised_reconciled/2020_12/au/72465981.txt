<h> Perspectives - The trouble with the Browning Effect <p> By Jack Burton and Lucas Fowler 3 December 2020 <p> Jack Burton ( L ) Lucas Fowler , Ogilvy . <p> Jack Burton and Lucas Fowler , Ogilvy creative team <p> What was the Best of 2020 ? <p> On first thought , the mute button . <p> On second thought , the best part of 2020 for us was probably just seeing the faces of our friends and work mates again - IRL without lagging , glitching or shady backlighting . We got to see how they 've changed , got hairier , gained weight , lost weight , became wiser , got fresh tatts , or even just stayed exactly the same . It 's like Kate Tempest said , there is indeed so much peace to be found in people 's faces . Here we are getting all sappy ! We do feel extremely fortunate though , especially being based in NSW this year . Our hearts go out to our mates in Victoria and other parts of the globe still in lockdown , you guys sure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was the Worst of 2020Definitely the Browning Effect . Oh , you have n't heard ? That 's fair , Jack just made it up . Not to discount any of the daily atrocities that continue to out-2020 the long list of other atrocities that have made 2020 so unique , but the Browning Effect has made its impact felt on many of us , and caused all the vibrant colour to turn brown . The symptoms ? Fatigue , the inability to switch off and Microsoft Teams taking up more screen time on your phone than Reddit . How do we deal with it ? The taller one of us does yoga in the mornings and tries not to touch any work before 9am , the better-looking one of us ensures he tends to his garden daily - if his snow-peas are happy , so is he . <p> What Changed for us ? As the much-celebrated prophet , Taylor Swift , wrote in her 2012 folk hit Everything Has Changed - " everything has changed " . Little did we know she was speaking about 2020 . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ pick two , it 'd be our pants and our process . We used to have such smart chinos , now we 're just sitting here in sweatpants stained with the flicks of 2-minute noodles . <p> Our creative process also changed . There was nothing we loved more than the two of us sitting in a room talking smack that could probably get us fired , hoping a good idea would come along between the gibberish . Now , in our small inner-city homes , we must do all this over video calls with our girlfriends and housemates in eardrop . This means less random animal noises to get the synapses firing , and more strained silent thinking . <p> What we want to see for 2021For the world , a vaccine . <p> For the world of advertising , we ca n't wait to see brands start celebrating the end of the COVID . We expect a Superbowl-level of epic-ness to try and win brand-love during a massive shared moment in consumers lives . Some will get it right , others will get it wrong , either way @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our masks off . <p> And for the big finale , we want to see brands continue to become the global leaders of change that 2020 brought about . <p> Brands that do , rather than just say . In particular , doing something about climate change . Now is the time for companies to make sustainability part of their fabric , like Burger King ( US ) reducing their cow 's methane emissions and Patagonia ( US ) with their unashamedly vocal stance on all-things climate-action . We need to see other brands step up and actually take the issue head-on , because unfortunately , no big brands in Australia spring to mind . <p> Prove us wrong , please ! <p> Have something to say on this ?
