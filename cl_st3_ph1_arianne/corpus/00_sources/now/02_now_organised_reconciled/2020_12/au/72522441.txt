His fantasy fiction , often marketed to young adults , has sold more than six million copies worldwide and been translated into more than 40 languages . <p> In the 30 years since his first novel , and nearly 30 since he committed to full-time writing , he has imagined into being several beloved series , countless short stories , and seven standalone novels .
