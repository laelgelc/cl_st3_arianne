To understand the strategy , it is important to realise that the reason there has been so little new activity in the UK 's nuclear sector since the 1990s is due to difficulty in financing . Nuclear plants are cheap to fuel and operate and last for a long time . In theory , this offsets the enormous upfront capital cost , and results in competitively priced electricity overall . <p> But ever since the electricity sector was privatised , governments have been averse to spending public money on power plants .
