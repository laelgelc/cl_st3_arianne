The index shows that over about 20 years up to 2017 , Australia 's threatened plant populations declined by 72% . This is faster than mammals ( which declined by about a third ) , and birds ( which declined by about half ) . Populations of trees , shrubs , herbs and orchids all suffered roughly similar average declines ( 65-75% ) over the two decades . <p> Of the 112 species in the index , 68% are critically endangered or endangered and at risk of extinction if left unmanaged .
