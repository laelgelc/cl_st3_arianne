<p> Authorities are confident that better-than-expected conditions will enable them to steer the huge bushfire raging on Fraser Island around a major resort and avoid significant damage to unique rainforests , six weeks after the blaze began . <p> But the news is cold comfort for business operators and residents forced off the island , as ecologists warn of the potential for long-lasting damage to the World Heritage-listed island , also known by its Indigenous name , K'gari . <p> The bushfire , sparked in mid-October by what authorities believe was an illegal campfire in the island 's north , continues to move south and threaten key tourism and ecological regions .
