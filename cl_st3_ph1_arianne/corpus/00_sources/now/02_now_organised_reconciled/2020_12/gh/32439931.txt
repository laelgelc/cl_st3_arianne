<p> The Friends of the Earth-Ghana has appealed to government to recognise the links between the current energy crisis , the problems of desertification and climate change and the toll they take on national and local economies . <p> A statement issued by the NGO called on the government to take steps to address the problems . <p> " We implore the government to be concerned and to take practical actions at the local level such as environmental conservation , restoration and rehabilitation , and training for local communities in alternative sustainable livelihood activities in order to mitigate the effects of desertification and climate change that have engulfed the country " . <p> The statement was signed and issued by Mr. Theo Anderson , Director of Friends of the Earth-Ghana to commemorate this year 's United Nations Day to combat desertification and drought which fell on Sunday June 17 . <p> The day has been set aside by the UN under the auspices of the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification ( UNCCD ) to create awareness on the causes and impacts of desertification on people and the environment and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crisis in the country is one of the ripple effects of the lack of political will of the government to show real commitment to dealing with the threats of desertification and climate change , " the statement said . <p> " We are also dismayed at the lack of action that continues to prevail around the use and implementation of action programmes to combat desertification and climate change which have been agreed and put in place for several years " . <p> It said despite the fact that adequate knowledge about the impact of desertification had long been identified and made available by relevant stakeholders to government , the respond was not encouraging . <p> It is estimated that 35 per cent of the total land area of Ghana was seriously threatened by desertification and a further 20,000 hectares of savannah land were subjected to desertification each year . <p> The savannah zones of the Northern , Upper East and Upper West are the worst affected while the South-eastern coastal belt is also under threat . <p> The statement said unfavourable climate conditions of long drought and short rainfall seasons @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as indiscriminate bush burning , poor farming practices and lack of awareness were the
