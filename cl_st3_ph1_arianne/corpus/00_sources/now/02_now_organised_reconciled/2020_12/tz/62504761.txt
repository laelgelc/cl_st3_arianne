Meeting in a retreat early this week , industry stakeholders expressed determination to expand the coverage to at least 50 percent of adults in the next decade . <p> If this were to happen , they reasoned , the industry 's contribution to the GDP would be around three percent -- in line with the government 's plan for the sub-sector to reach a minimum coverage of 50 percent of the population by 2030 . <p> In strategizing for this , stakeholders should focus on harnessing and using big data ; streamlining their operations ; reducing operating costs ; improving customer experience and expanding market share . <p> Innovation is crucial to achieving the goal of bolstering insurance penetration . <p> Some of the challenges that the players may need to innovate around include the impact of climate change , including rapid desertification , ever-increasing water scarcity and growing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ challenges are low resilience to natural disasters ;
