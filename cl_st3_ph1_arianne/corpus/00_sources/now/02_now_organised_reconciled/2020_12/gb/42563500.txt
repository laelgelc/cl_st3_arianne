<h> Scotland 's renewable energy meets 90 per cent of power needs <p> SCOTLAND generated enough electricity from renewable sources last year to meet the equivalent of 90.1% of its total electricity consumption , according to latest figures . <p> In 2019 , the country generated 30.5 TWh of electricity from renewable sources , 13.4% up on 2018 , Scottish Government data showed . <p> This was enough to charge almost 6.7 billion mobile phones for a year and boil around 700 billion kettles . <p> The target is for 100% of the country 's electricity to be generated from renewable sources by 2020 . <p> The Annual Energy Statement 2020 said that Scotland 's heat demand is still primarily made up from fossil fuels but progress on renewables has been made , with the amount of renewable heat generated in 2019 up 4.8% on the previous year . <p> Energy Minister Paul Wheelhouse said : " This year 's energy statement shows that we are continuing to make strong progress towards building a greener , fairer and more equal society and economy , and to meeting our world-leading climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to make good progress in areas such as renewable electricity with , in 2019 , Scotland 's renewable electricity generation having grown to such an extent that it was able to meet the equivalent of 90.1% of Scotland 's gross electricity consumption , making 2019 another record-breaking year for the sector . <p> " However , to decarbonise our energy intensive industries , heat in buildings and transport through electrification and other means like hydrogen , we know we need to continue to grow our renewable electricity supplies further . <p> " Our recent Climate Change Plan update , published this week , contains more than 100 new policies and proposals to support Scotland 's green recovery and a just transition to net-zero .
