<h> ' It is our time to shine ' - MP says nuclear future has the support of Whitehall <h> ' It is our time to shine ' - Copeland MP says nuclear future has the support of Whitehall <p> RECOGNISING the urgency to go further to tackle climate change , the Prime Minister has laid out an ' ambitious ' further commitment to achieving Net-Zero . <p> The new plan aims for at least 68 per cent reduction in greenhouse gas emissions by the end of the decade , compared to 1990 levels , the first target set by the UK following its departure from the EU , demonstrating the UK 's leadership in tackling climate change . <p> The announcement comes ahead of the UK co-hosting the Climate Ambition Summit on Saturday , which coincides with the fifth anniversary of the historic Paris Agreement .
