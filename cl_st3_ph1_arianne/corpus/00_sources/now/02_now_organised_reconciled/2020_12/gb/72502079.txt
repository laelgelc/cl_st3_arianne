<h> Divers discover rare flame shell reef off Arran coast <h> Share via <p> A rare flame shell reef has been discovered by recreational sea search divers off the coast of Arran . <p> The exotic looking flame shell is a small two shelled saltwater clam which uses stones and other seabed debris to create nest . <p> A few of the creatures can live in each nest . When hundreds of these nests come together they form a reef , like the one discovered within the South Arran Marine Protected Area . <p> It was feared most of the habitat had been destroyed decades ago after the introduction of seabed trawlers and bottom towed scallop dredgers in the Clyde . <p> ADVERT <p> Historically there were seven mapped flame shell reefs in the Clyde but until now it was thought only one had survived . <p> It is expected this reef is a remnant bed which has gone previously undiscovered .
