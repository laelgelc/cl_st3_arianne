<h> Consultation for Dorset Council Local Plan to start next year <p> A CONSULTATION on a plan which will guide decisions on planning applications in Dorset for the next 15 years will begin in January . <p> The Dorset Council Local Plan sets out planning policies and allocations to meet the needs of the whole of the Dorset Council area until at least the year 2038 . <p> The council is holding a public consultation on their draft plan , which outlines the strategy for ensuring the growth that the area needs happens in the right places and is of the right character and quality , while protecting Dorset 's natural environment and acting on climate change . <p> It also ensures that the right level of community services is provided , including schools , as well as retail , leisure and other community facilities . <p> The first consultation will begin in January 2021 and will last for eight weeks . There will be a further opportunity to comment in late 2021 , before the plan is submitted for independent examination and adopted in 2023 . <p> Councillor David @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : " We are planning for Dorset 's future , for the future of our residents . <p> " It is so important that we understand everyone 's view on the draft Local Plan as much as we can . " <p> The council is required to consult with a range of stakeholders , including public bodies such as the Environment Agency , and voluntary bodies , such as local charities . <p> Town and parish councils will be consulted as they can provide local knowledge and represent the concerns of local residents , and the council will work with local landowners and the development industry , alongside infrastructure and service providers . <p> Cllr Walsh added : " We are working closely with all our stakeholders , our own councillors and town and parish councils to make sure we reach all parts of Dorset . " <p> The draft plan will be available on the Dorset Council website .
