<h> Key questions on Heathrow expansion ruling <h> Key questions on Heathrow expansion ruling <p> Heathrow expansion has been a controversial project for more than a decade . <p> Here , the PA news agency answers the key questions around the scheme and the legal challenge : <p> -- Who originally brought the legal challenge to court ? <p> A group of councils in London affected by the potential addition of a third runway , as well as environmental charities and London Mayor Sadiq Khan . <p> -- What was their argument ? <p> They claimed the Government 's decision to support Heathrow expansion was incompatible with the UK 's climate change commitments <p> -- Why has the case come before the Supreme Court ? <p> Back in February , the campaigners won a Court of Appeal ruling on environmental grounds , with three leading judges concluding former transport secretary Chris Grayling failed to take account of the Government 's commitments to tackling climate change when setting out support for the project in an Airports National Policy Statement ( ANPS ) . <p> They ruled the ANPS was unlawful because @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ commitment to the Paris Agreement -- which commits signatories to tackling climate change by taking measures to limit global warming to well below 2C . <p> ( PA Graphics ) <p> Heathrow appealed against the judgment , taking it to the Supreme Court -- the highest court in the UK . <p> -- What has the Supreme Court ruled ? <p> The Supreme Court has ruled in Heathrow 's favour , finding the Government 's decision was lawful . <p> It concluded Mr Grayling 's decision was lawful , he did take the Paris Agreement into account and he was " not legally required to give it more weight than he decided was appropriate " in line with the advice of the Committee on Climate Change . <p> The ANPS is " valid " , the court said . <p> -- Will the expansion definitely now go ahead ?
