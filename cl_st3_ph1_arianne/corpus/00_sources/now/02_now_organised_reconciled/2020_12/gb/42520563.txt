<p> When Typhoon Vamco ( known locally as Ulysses ) hit the Philippines on 11 November , the country was already reeling from the aftermath of Super Typhoon Goni ( Rolly ) -- the most powerful storm since 2013 -- killing at least 25 people and affecting more than two million . <h> What were the effects of Typhoon Vamco ? <p> Vamco has killed 67 people in the Philippines , leaving 13 missing and dozens injured . More than 350,000 people were evacuated as the main island of Luzon was hammered by winds of up to 150kmph and heavy rainfall , leading to widespread flooding in the Metro Manila region , as well as mudslides in areas in the north . At the same time , the country is still battling Covid-19 with more than 1,000 new cases recorded daily -- showing the immense difficulty of having to tackle two crises simultaneously . <p> President of the Philippines , Rodrigo Duterte , has now declared a " state of calamity " on Luzon , adding that climate change is to blame for the recent disasters .
