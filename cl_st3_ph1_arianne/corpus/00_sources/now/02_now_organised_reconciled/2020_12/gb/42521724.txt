<h> University of Glasgow targets net-zero emissions by 2030 <p> A strategy document has been approved after more than 1,300 staff and students were surveyed . <p> Subscribe to our daily newsletter ! <p> University of Glasgow <p> The University of Glasgow has announced plans to commit to a target of net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2030 . <p> Bosses have approved a strategy document -- Glasgow Green : The University of Glasgow 's Response to the Climate Emergency -- after surveying more than 1,300 staff and students . <p> Seminars and discussions were also held over the plan for the university to go " further and faster " in its response to address climate change . <p> University chief operating officer and secretary David Duncan , who is co-chairman of the Sustainability Working Group that co-ordinated the agreement of the strategy , said : " Universities have a unique role to play in the fight against climate change . <p> " We are educators , researchers and contributors to our local communities . <p> " It is vital that we rise to the challenge of shaping a liveable world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We believe that our new strategy is a very significant plan to ramp up our existing efforts to deal with the climate emergency , both locally and internationally . " <p> The document sets out five key actions for the university to continue reducing its carbon footprint , including investing in restoration programmes across Scotland and in low and middle-income countries around the world . <p> Emissions across the university campuses have already fallen by more than 13% from 2015-16 levels to 60,358 carbon dioxide equivalents ( tCO2e ) in 2018-19 . <p> But the report suggests energy efficiency improvements , water source heat pump and solar panel installation and a reduction in business travel would further reduce the footprint to 32,000 tCO2e by 2035 . <p> Professor Daniel Haydon , of the Institute of Biodiversity Animal Health and Comparative Medicine , and co-chairman of the working group , said : " There 's a clear demand from students and staff that we go further and faster , and we 've responded with serious new commitments to cut our own carbon emissions and offset the remainder . <p> " As @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , we now have a clearer roadmap on where we need to be in 2030 to help contribute to that international effort . <p> " We 're pleased to be setting out on this journey with students , staff and the local community . "
