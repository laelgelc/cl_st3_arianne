<h> Letters : Green target promises ring hollow <h> Letters : Green target promises ring hollow <p> JOHN Rankin ( Letters , December 2 ) alerts us to the downside of electric vehicles and our useless politicians who are led by the nose by the green lobby who want us to return to the Stone Age . OK , I exaggerate : back to the horse and cart . Is China , which is responsible for 30 per cent of global emissions going to go all-electric ? No , it is too busy growing its economy by building yet more coal-fired plants and importing seven million barrels of oil every day . <p> Can we believe China when it says it will be net zero by 2060 ? I doubt it , since it refuses to sign a legally-binding Climate Change Act . I do not blame the Chinese , since less than a dozen countries have done so . The other 180-plus only made promises and one knows that promises can be broken . <p> Clark Cross , Linlithgow . <p> BRIDGING THE GAP <p> REGARDING your article on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bridges : ? 2m crossing to maintain vital link for island " , The Herald , December 1 ) , surely a temporary bridge costing a probable ? 2 million , with a lifetime less than a third of a long-term bridge costing a probable ? 5m , is a false economy .
