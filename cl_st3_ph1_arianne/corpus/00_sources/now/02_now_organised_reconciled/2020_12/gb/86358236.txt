We 'll stop the wave of feral pests and really protect the special wildlife living there . " <p> The fence will allow Parks Victoria to focus on eradicating any pests or predators inside the park . <p> It will be built deep into the ground and almost 2 metres tall to prevent animals finding a way around , underneath or over it .
