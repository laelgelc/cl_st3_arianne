<h> My fateful appearance at the Bank of England 's Christmas drinks <p> Tidings of comfort as the vaccination programme advances , but shortage of joy . That 's my summary of a season in which there 's no Spectator Christmas bash for the first time in my 29 years on the magazine ; in which my panto-dame ball gown hangs forlornly as a decoration in the foyer of the theatre where social distancing has made it impossible for us to mount a show ; and in which I ca n't even offer my customary restaurant tips , because there have been so few opportunities to eat out anywhere and , apart from a brief French escape in July , no chance at all to travel abroad . <p> Nevertheless I count my blessings , the greatest being is that I 'm still here writing for you . And I can at least offer a blast of Christmas past that combines elements of all the above : gastronomy , conviviality and slapstick comedy , plus the City name-dropping that is this column 's more conventional forte .
