<h> New version of global temperature dataset shows greater climate warming <p> Updates to one of the world 's major temperature datasets reveals the planet has warmed more since the 19th century than the record previously indicated . <p> The HadCRUT temperature dataset stretching back to 1850 is managed by the Met Office and University of East Anglia , and is one of a handful used to calculate global temperature rises due to climate change . <p> A new version of the record shows the world was 1.07C warmer on average for 2010-18 than in the second half of the 19th century , 0.16C more than in the previous set of measurements . <p> The change comes after improvements to the dataset , not because climate change has suddenly got worse , scientists said . <p> The main contributor to warming over the past 170 years is humans putting more greenhouse gases into the atmosphere , they added . <p> Improvements in the new dataset include a significant increase in weather stations used over land
