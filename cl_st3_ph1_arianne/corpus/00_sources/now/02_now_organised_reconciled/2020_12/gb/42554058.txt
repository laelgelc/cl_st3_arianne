Many experts agree that a turning point in the French anti-vaxxer phenomenon was the campaign against Hepatitis B in the ' 90s . A vaccine had been available for years , but in the country immunisations were lagging and the disease was on the rise . In 1994 , the government decided to launch a mass vaccination of all newborns and kids entering middle school . France 's vaccination rate quickly became one of the highest in the world ( about one third of the population ) , but the debate about side effects also grew in intensity .
