They outline post-2020 action to tackle climate change , with many of the current plans running to 2030 . <p> -- What is the Paris Agreement ? <p> A global deal , agreed under the United Nations ( UN ) in the French capital in December 2015 , which will see action by all countries to curb rising temperatures . <p> It is the world 's first comprehensive treaty on climate change . <p> The UK is submitting its climate plans under the Paris Agreement , negotiated in France in 2015 ( Emily Beament/PA ) <p> -- What does the treaty involve ? <p> Yes , according to a report for governments by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) in 2018 . <p> It found a 2C rise would lead to more heatwaves , extreme rainstorms , water shortages and drought , greater economic losses and lower crop yields , higher sea levels and greater damage to coral reefs than a 1.5C rise . <p> As more greenhouse gases lead to more warming , stabilising the planet 's temperature at any level will require emissions to fall to zero overall . <p> To keep temperatures from rising to more than 1.5C in the long term , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ levels by 2030 and to net zero by 2050 , with steep cuts in other greenhouse gases . <p> That requires a rapid , far-reaching and unprecedented change across society to cut greenhouse gas emissions from every sector , from power , transport and industry , to homes , farming and food systems . <p> -- What is net zero ? <p> Net zero , also sometimes referred to as carbon neutrality , means that overall the output of emissions must be zero . <p> To achieve it , emissions have to be cut as much as possible , and any remaining pollution , from hard-to-tackle sectors such as aviation , need to be " offset " by action that absorbs carbon , such as planting trees or using new technology to capture it from the air . <p> The central objective of the @UN for 2021 is to build a Global Coalition for Carbon Neutrality . <p> -- If the Paris Agreement was negotiated in 2015 , why are we unveiling our NDC now ?
