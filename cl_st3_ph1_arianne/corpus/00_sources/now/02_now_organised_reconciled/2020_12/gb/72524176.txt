<h> Have your say on future of transport network across Colchester <h> Have your say on future of transport network in Colchester <p> RESIDENTS are being asked to help shape the future of the transport network in Colchester . <p> Essex County Council is working with Colchester Council on the Colchester Future Transport Strategy , which aims to increase sustainable transport use across the borough , reduce car usage and improve air quality . <p> Before the strategy is progressed , the public are being urged to complete a survey to help shape it . <p> Kevin Bentley , Essex County Council infrastructure boss , said : " As we move forward and think about the future , we need to take a ' safer , greener , healthier ' ethos into our future transport planning and look at how we plan and invest in making it easier and preferable to walk , cycle or use public transport . <p> " There were 20,000 fewer car journeys made during the summer lockdown than at the same time in 2019 . <p> " With that reduction came an improvement in air @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and cycling as means of getting around , with obvious health benefits . <p> " If we can build on that , we could see a huge difference in the town . " <p> The finished strategy will include small-scale improvements to the network as well as larger projects . <p> The Colchester Future Transport Strategy sets out the current transport issues and opportunities , as well as future plans and , once agreed , will help to identify potential schemes , ranging from small-scale improvements and interventions through to larger-scale strategic projects . <p> It will look to limit traffic levels in the town centre , reduce delays , improve journey times and reliability and ensure the transport network is maintained well , transforming it into a place which prioritises active and safe , sustainable travel to bring about health , environmental and economic benefits . <p> Mark Cory , leader of Colchester , said : " How we travel around our town and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " We have set out our vision , including growing our economy , providing homes for those in need , celebrating our culture and heritage , and creating safe and active communities . <p> " All of these are vital for creating a Better Colchester , as is our commitment to tackling climate change and protecting our environment into the future .
