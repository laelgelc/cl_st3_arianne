<h> How values , interests and power must shape South Africa 's foreign policy <p> The COVID-19 crisis is one of many indicators that we live in dangerous and uncertain times . Others include the international community 's struggle to respond to technological and climate change , demographic shifts , growing poverty and inequality as well as increased global insecurity . <p> The global governance arrangements for managing these changes are no longer fit for purpose . Take the decision making procedures in the UN Security Council and the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) . They do n't account for the growing importance of developing countries . Nor do they meaningfully accommodate non-state actors such as transnational corporations and civil society groups . <p> In the book , a group of senior and junior South African authors offer suggestions on how the country can formulate and implement a foreign policy that responds to a changing global world . <p> We maintain that foreign policy making should be guided by the national interest .
