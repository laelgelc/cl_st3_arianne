( CTRG ) Sasol is pleased to announce that a Sale Securities Purchase Agreement has been signed with Azura Power Limited for the divestment of the Company 's full shareholding in CTRG , the gas- to-power plant located in Ressano Garcia , Mozambique . The transaction is subject to a number of conditions precedent , which include regulatory approval and the waiver of pre-emption rights held by Electricidade de Mocambique ( EDM ) , the Mozambican state-owned electricity company .
