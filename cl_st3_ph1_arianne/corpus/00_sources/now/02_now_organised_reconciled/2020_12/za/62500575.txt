<h> Global economic rebound expected for 2021 <p> After enduring its deepest recession in 74 years in 2020 , the global economy is expected to rebound in 2021 , according to the annual Top-10 Economic Predictions released today by IHS Markit . <p> IHS Markit 's top prediction for 2021 : a successful , widespread Covid-19 vaccination program is expected to enable a transition to post-pandemic economic growth of 4,5% . <p> " The rapid deployment of effective vaccines and reopening of economies should gradually unleash a new wave of spending on travel and services , driving robust growth in the later part of 2021 , " says Sara Johnson , executive director : global economics at IHS Markit . <p> " After a 4% decline in 2020 , the global economy is expected to expand 4.5 percent next year , with global output expected to reach a new peak . " <p> The report 's second prediction shows a divergent timeline for the economic recovery , with a return to economic growth varying widely across regions .
