<h> Puppets bring together a town divided by apartheid <h> " Reboot Eden " celebrates ten years of puppetry in Barrydale <p> Barrydale will celebrate ten years of puppetry with ' Reboot Eden ' , four short films with nature themes , and our human connection with it . Photos : Ashraf Hendricks <p> For ten years , giant puppets in the shape of animals , insects and futuristic robots have performed and danced down the streets of Barrydale . The annual puppet parade , started in 2010 by the Handspring Puppet Trust , aims to bring together the residents of this small Klein Karoo town which was divided by apartheid . But this year , Covid-19 has chased the parade off the streets and onto screens instead . <p> Starting on Reconciliation Day , the Reboot Eden event will celebrate ten years of puppetry with four short films about nature and the human connection to it .
