The affected areas are in a crisis situation and there is evidence to support this . Data regarding rainfall and storage trends of dams indicate that the areas are being subjected to typical El Nino climatic conditions . The declaration of a drought is a process and last year had the lowest recorded rainfall in 30 years at Hazelmere . Umgeni Water can not agree with the statement that it is responsible for the present restrictions . As a water services provider it has a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the northern supply areas and a 30-year capital expenditure programme for future expected organic growth . What is currently being experiencing is a natural phenomenon that is difficult to plan for . Umgeni Water has a long history of excellence in water quality and supply . It reports to the Department of Water and Sanitation ( DWS ) on its performance , and has service level agreements with the customers it supplies potable water in bulk . The writer can be assured that the water supply shortage is due to the drought and nothing else . Restrictions are necessary to ensure that the dwindling resources can be stretched as far as possible . The application of restrictions was in response to current water shortages , and has no connection or relevance to " spin doctoring " . There is evidence that the restrictions have assisted in meeting the demands of the communities in the North . The emergency pipeline between the uTongathi River and Hazelmere dam is one of many options being considered . Restrictions were the first mitigation . The uTongathi pipeline is the second and if the situation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ writer must also take cognisance of is that the situation is constantly changing . Storage at Hazelmere dam is recovering due to the recent good rainfall and Umgeni Water does not need to supply the entire North Coast from the emergency scheme . Water resource planning for organic growth must not be confused with drought planning . The raising of Hazelmere wall will boost storage capacity that will allow for greater assurance of supply . But in times of protracted drought , water shortages can still be experienced . Umgeni Water performs operation and maintenance of the Hazelmere dam on behalf of DWS . Currently there are no structural integrity challenges . Dam maintenance is up to date . DWS has recognised that the wall will have to be anchored if it is to be raised . This is not a maintenance issue , but rather a requirement to ensure structural integrity . Umgeni Water has already implemented many capex projects on the North Coast to mitigate demand growth . These include the Hazelmere upgrade project , as well as the R1.4 billion Lower Thukela bulk water supply scheme which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ supply 55Ml/d of bulk potable water . These projects will ensure that Umgeni Water 's infrastructure has capacity to supply the demand required . Climate change is currently impacting on the ability of WSPs worldwide . The question is whether the impact of climate change is avoidable given the fact that it is a natural phenomenon . The recent drought is unique in that it could not be predicted from the historical rainfall trends . Risk assessments done on the water resources within the Hazelmere catchments are weighted heavily by the probability of occurrence and , therefore , the risk was rated low . Should the frequency of droughts increase , the risk will also increase and this will inform future resource planning .
