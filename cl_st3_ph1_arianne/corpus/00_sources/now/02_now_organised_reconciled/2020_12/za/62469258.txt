<h> Environmental activist removes waste along M1 road <p> Environmental activist Maloto Chinkombero has removed waste along the M1 road from 6 Miles in Lilongwe as a way of fighting against poor waste disposal . <p> Chinkombero started the exercise on Tuesday and on Friday he was at Biwi Triangle with the hope of reaching Lilongwe Bridge . <p> One of the cleaned stretches <p> According to the activist , he observed that one of the contributing factors to environmental degradation and disasters is poor waste disposal and waste management . <p> " Malawians have a serious problem when handling wastes . For instance , this M1 road is supposed to be kept clean but there is are piles of wastes along the road and nobody seemed to care for this . So I decided to take action of cleaning it up to set an example , " he told Malawi24 . <p> When he started , Chinkombero only had a pair of gloves , shovel , two waste bins and a tricycle that he used to transport waste collected to area 38 where there is an official dumping site @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with their team and a vehicle and they also joined on Friday when he was cleaning at Biwi Triangle . <p> Chinkombero <p> His efforts also got the attention of Minister of Forestry and Natural Resources Nancy Tembo who also joined the exercise at Biwi Triangle . <p> Chinkombero has since urged Malawians to change their mindset and start cleaning their surroundings <p> He said : " Having clean environment will boost tourism sector as our country will look beautiful , will also help to mitigate climate change and will reduce the risk of waterborne diseases outbreak such as cholera .
