<p> Docupoetry is a genre of socially engaged poetry that includes nonliterary sources such as legal texts and news reports . It has been described loosely as where journalism meets poetry . Accountability Now director Paul Hoffman has penned a ' docupoem ' , focusing on the sheer scale of corruption in this country and how it has become one of the greatest challenges of our time . Hoffman proposes that we hold government to its promise of that better life by uprooting the corrupt , voting out unaccountable politicians , sending the crooks to jail and recovering stolen funds .
