<h> GG MEETS INDIA 'S TOP ENVOY <p> New High Commissioner of India to Jamaica R Masakui ( centre ) presented his credentials to Governor General Sir Patrick Allen on Friday , December 4 , at King 's House , St Andrew . <p> Discussions that followed the presentation of credentials focused on the commitment to enhance the multifaceted relationship between the two countries that , among others , include closer and more frequent engagements at all levels of government , increased business relationship , people-to-people contacts , cooperation in the fields of climate change , disaster management , UN reforms , extremism and terrorism as well as the strengthening of India-Caricom relations . <p> Before coming to Jamaica , Masakui served as India 's ambassador to Zimbabwe for the last four years .
