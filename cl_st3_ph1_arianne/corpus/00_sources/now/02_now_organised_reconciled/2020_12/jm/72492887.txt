<h> The United Nations wants to hear from Jamaica <p> To mark its 75th anniversary in 2020 , the United Nations is igniting a people 's debate called " UN75 " which promises to be the largest and furthest-reaching global conversation ever on building the future we want . <p> UN75 celebrations are filled with interactive engagement opportunities which target young people . Key to these is a 1-minute survey as well as consultative dialogues which encourage persons from all over the world , including Jamaica , to share their ideas on how enhanced international cooperation can help realize a better world by 2045 , the UN 's 100th birthday . Participate in the 1-minute UN75 survey by visiting www.un75.online <p> The UN 's 75th anniversary is also tied with the 72nd anniversary of the signing of the Declaration of Human Rights . This December 10 Human Rights Day is themed , ' Recover Better , Stand Up for Human Rights ' and brings into focus the COVID-19 pandemic and the need to Recover Better with the Universal Declaration on Human Rights as a key roadmap for the recovery efforts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ human rights , health , as well as how their individual countries and the world should respond to various issues including climate change and the COVID-19 response . Gary Conille , United Nations Resident Coordinator for Jamaica said , " the decisions that the larger countries make often have a stronger effect on smaller ones . With Jamaica being a small island state , this survey gives Jamaicans the opportunity to have their voices heard in decisions making as well as give input into how Jamaican 's will want their world to look . " <p> Gary Conille , United Nations Resident Coordinator for Jamaica <p> While UN75 seeks to drive conversation in all segments of society and seeks the input of every individual , there is a special emphasis being placed on getting responses from young people . Conille says this is because " the issues that we face today , particularly as they relate to climate change and health especially in the context of COVID-19 , will affect the world that exists years from now . As such a special emphasis is placed on hearing from young people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ they will inherit the world we are creating . " <p> UN75 offers a more inclusive approach to consultation and decision-making . It borrows from the tenets of the Sustainable Development Goals which require everyone , everywhere to take action .
