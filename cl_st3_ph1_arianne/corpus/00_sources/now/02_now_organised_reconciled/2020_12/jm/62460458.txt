<h> Latest News <p> KINGSTON , Jamaica - Minister of Housing , Urban Renewal , Environment and Climate Change , Pearnel Charles Jr , says the proposed amendments to the Rent Restriction Act will provide opportunity for more people to get into the real estate industry . <p> " The amendments could very well see a positive impact where persons will now see this as an industry that they can get more involved in , with more developments , more landlords and more persons having access to better places to rent , " he said . <p> Charles Jr was speaking at the Realtors Association of Jamaica 's Annual General Meeting today at Jamaica College in St Andrew . <p> The Rent Restriction Act , enacted in 1944 , is being amended to strengthen the outdated provisions and to introduce new measures to better manage the relationship between landlords and tenants .
