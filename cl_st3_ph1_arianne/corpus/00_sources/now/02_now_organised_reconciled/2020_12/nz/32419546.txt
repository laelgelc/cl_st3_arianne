<p> The Hawke 's Bay coastal hazards project is re-engaging with the community to build solutions for the coast between Clifton and Tangoio . <p> The Joint Committee met to discuss a series of community workshops , aimed at testing the project workstream outputs ahead of formal consultation in 2021 , as well as a proposed funding review . <p> " This is complex work that we 're asking the community to look at and consider . We 're talking about engineering design , and the related impacts - costs , communities , housing , recreation , longevity of solutions .
