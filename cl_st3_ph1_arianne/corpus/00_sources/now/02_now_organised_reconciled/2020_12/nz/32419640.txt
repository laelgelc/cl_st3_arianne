<h> New Green MP Ricardo Menendez March says the work ahead is an extension of the work he 's done on the ground <h> New Green MP Ricardo Menendez March says the work ahead is an extension of the work he 's done on the ground <p> 8 December 2020 The former Auckland Action Against Poverty co-ordinator delivered his maiden speech in Parliament this afternoon . <p> He says he 's honoured to have been trusted with the social development portfolio - to follow on the work of previous staunch predeccessors . <p> March says he 's thankful to those in the union movement who have been unapologetic about supporting the unemployed as well as the workers . <p> He says all of these groups and people understand that addressing climate change requires tackling the extractive practices that drive inequality - and workers rights are intricately linked to climate justice .
