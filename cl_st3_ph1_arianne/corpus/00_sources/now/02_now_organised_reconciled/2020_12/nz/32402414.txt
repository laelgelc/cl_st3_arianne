<p> News from Porirua City Council It was an awful end to spring for some of our community , when torrential rain caused flash flooding , damaging a number of properties . <p> The flooding , on Sunday 29 November , left 15 Plimmerton homes uninhabitable and two Whitby properties were evacuated due to a slip the following day . <p> Many more homes were flood-damaged and the clean-up effort saw the Council , Wellington Water , Downer , regional response agencies and an incredibly supportive community team up to deal with the aftermath of the storm . <p> Mayor Anita Baker says the very heavy and localised rain that fell on the Sunday morning in a short time coincided with high tide , leaving the water which usually runs out to sea with nowhere to go , overwhelming the system . <p> " We are aware our infrastructure is ageing , and climate change is causing more severe weather events . This is why we 're proposing to more than double our 3-Waters infrastructure investment in our next Long-term Plan to $800 million .
