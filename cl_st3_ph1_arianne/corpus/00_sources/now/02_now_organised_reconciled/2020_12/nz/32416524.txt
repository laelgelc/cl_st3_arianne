<p> News from Lime Lime is making a return to Upper and Lower Hutt today with 100 scooters , and will increase the fleet to 250 over the coming weeks . <p> The micromobility company is bringing scooters back to the Hutt Valley streets after it paused services in the area over the 2019 winter period . <p> Following Lime 's relaunch of JUMP scooters in Wellington in August , this extension into the Hutt Valley will see Lime 's operations servicing the wider Wellington region . <p> " We always intended to return to the Hutt Valley and we are thrilled to be back once again , to provide residents with a safe , convenient and sustainable alternative to private motor vehicles , " says Lauren Mentjox , Lime 's ANZ Government Relations and Public Affairs Manager . <p> " We look forward to continuing to work with Upper Hutt and Hutt City councils to help more people move around and get to the places they want to go . <p> " As always , we encourage riders to wear a helmet , be responsible and be mindful of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ created slow speed areas in the town centres as well as preferred parking areas , including in Jackson Street , Petone . The scooters will cost $1 to unlock and 40 cents per minute . <p> Lime has been busy expanding its New Zealand services recently with the launch of scooters into Tauranga in October and e-bikes hitting the streets of Christchurch in November . These new destinations provide more opportunities for Kiwis across the country to enjoy the benefits of micromobility . <p> Lime 's local expansion demonstrates the company 's drive toward a cleaner transport mode and this commitment was further emphasised with the recent launch of Lime 's Ride Green initiative , working to create cleaner cities in a fight against climate change . <p> Lime 's mission is simple ; create cleaner , more liveable cities by revolutionising urban transportation toward a shared , electric and carbon-free future .
