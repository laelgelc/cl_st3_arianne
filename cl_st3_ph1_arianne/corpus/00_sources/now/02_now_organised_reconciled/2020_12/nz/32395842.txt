<p> " Soil biodiversity and sustainable soil management is a prerequisite for the achievement of many of the Sustainable Development Goals " , said FAO Deputy Director-General Maria Helena Semedo . " Therefore , data and information on soil biodiversity , from the national to the global level , are necessary in order to efficiently plan management strategies on a subject that is still poorly known " , she added . <h> Biodiversity below <p> According to the report , despite the fact biodiversity loss is at the forefront of global concerns , biodiversity below ground is not being given the prominence it deserves and needs to be fully considered when planning how best to boost sustainable development . <p> " We hope that the knowledge contained in this report will facilitate the assessment of the state of soil biodiversity as an integral part of national- and regional-level biodiversity reporting and any soil surveys " , Ms. Semedo advanced . <p> Being one of the main ' global reservoirs ' of biodiversity , soils host more than 25 percent of the world 's biological diversity . It is this diverse community of living organisms that keeps the soil healthy and fertile , which constitutes soil biodiversity , and determines the main biogeochemical processes that make life possible on Earth . <p> This year , by addressing the increasing challenges of soil management , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Keep soil alive , protect soil biodiversity " aimstoraise awareness of the importance of sustaining healthy ecosystems and human well-being . They play a key role in carbon sequestration and reducing greenhouse gas emissions .
