<p> The United Kingdom is entering its deepest recession in 300 years . Millions of jobs are at risk . And national debt has exceeded 100% of GDP . Now is hardly the time to explore the conceptual underpinnings of economic theory , right ? <p> In fact , as governments everywhere are borrowing , spending , and regulating on an unprecedented scale , a deeper understanding of economic decision-making is essential both to accelerate the recovery and to avoid longer-term risks .
