<h> Kiwifruit in the fast lane <p> The aim of the new Kiwifruit Breeding Centre ( KBC ) JV is to bring the best kiwifruit cultivars to the market faster . <p> Zespri and Plant and Food Research are discussing forming a 50/50 joint venture to fast track the development of breeding new cultivars of kiwifruit . <p> A proposal is before the boards of both organisations to form a stand-alone Kiwifruit Breeding Centre ( KBC ) . The aim is to build on the present successful plant breeding partnership between the two entities , but bring the best kiwifruit cultivars to the market faster . <p> The KBC would have its own board comprising representatives of both Zespri and Plant and Food , plus some independent directors . It would have its own CEO and up to about 50 staff -- drawn from the two organisations .
