<h> Kiwi founder of COGO explains how the app can help you live sustainably <p> With the Government this week calling for action , declaring a Climate Change emergency , one New Zealand company is already ahead of the game . <p> The company is COGO and they have an app that can help us all with the daunting task of living more sustainably . <p> The app allows you to improve your carbon footprint by connecting with your banking app and tracking your spending habits . <p> The app 's Kiwi founder and CEO Ben Gleisner launched it in the UK , but has since returned to New Zealand as a result of the Covid-19 pandemic . He joined Francesca Rudkin to discuss how it works and hows it can make your life more eco-friendly.
