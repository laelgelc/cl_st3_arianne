<h> BOPRC adopts flood protection bylaws <p> The Bay of Plenty Regional Council has voted to adopt the Flood Protection and Drainage Bylaws 2020 . <p> In a statement released overnight , Council says the latest moves brings to fruition an 18-month-long process of consultation , review , submissions and hearings . <p> The new Bylaws come into force on February 1 and until then , the Floodway and Drainage Bylaws 2008 remain in effect . <p> The Bylaws only apply to assets managed by Bay of Plenty Regional Council as part of the region 's flood protection and land drainage schemes . <p> The Local Government Act 2002 requires all bylaws to be reviewed every ten years to ensure they are fit-for-purpose , informed by the latest science and data , and in this case , the learnings from flood events in the intervening years . <p> The review also took into account climate change projections , land use changes and increased pressure from land development . <p> " The overarching purpose of the Bylaws is to safeguard flood protection and land drainage assets from damage or misuse so @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ properties and livelihoods from flooding , " says von Dadelszen . <p> " I 'd like to thank the people who engaged in the consultation process and those who made submissions . <p> " Submissions were received from across the region with the highest proportion being from the Eastern Bay of Plenty . Of the 43 submitters , 15 chose to present either in person , or via zoom , to the hearing held on August 26 . " <p> The Flood Protection and Drainage Bylaws 2020 , together with a Responses to Submitters document will be available at **31;62;TOOLONG or can be viewed in hard copy in reception areas of Bay of Plenty Regional Council offices across the rohe from Monday , December 21 . <p> Background : <p> What are flood protection assets ? <p> The Bay of Plenty Regional Council looks after $370 million worth of flood protection assets . These include floodgates , pump stations and 352 kilometres of stopbanks , which are part of a flood protection system designed to help manage river flows and collectively work to minimise flood risks . <p> Council manages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : <p> Kaituna Catchment Control Scheme <p> * Waioeka-Otara Rivers Scheme <p> * Whakatane-Tauranga Rivers Scheme <p> * Rangitaiki-Tarawera Rivers Scheme <p> * Rangitaiki Drainage Scheme <p> The Bay of Plenty Regional Council Flood Protection and Drainage Bylaws applies to drains , pumping stations , defences against water , river edge protection works and floodways owned by Council . <p> Under the bylaws it is an offence to endanger these assets and landowners can be held liable for any damage . <p> Bylaw Applicable areas <p> This is a defined area within which you may need a bylaw authority to carry out certain activities that could potentially affect a council flood protection asset . The area differs for each of the river or drainage schemes and specific information is available on the project web page . <p> It is not Council 's intention to prohibit activity within the Bylaw Applicable Areas . These areas are identified in the Bylaws to indicate that certain activities may need ' prior written authority ' before they are undertaken . The Bylaw Authority process enables the landowner/leasee to communicate with Council about the intended @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to a council flood protection asset . <p> What is a bylaw authority ? <p> Anyone who wants to carry out certain activities in the vicinity of a flood protection asset must first apply for a bylaw authority . These activities include constructing or demolishing a structure ; undertaking earthworks ; planting or removing trees , hedges or shrubs ; installing a culvert or crossing in a scheme drain ; intensive development of farmland ( e.g. for horticulture ) . <p> Council may issue bylaw authorities , often with specific conditions , to allow landowners to carry out these activities while minimising any impact on the flood protection assets . <p> How can I find out if the Flood Protection and Drainage Bylaws applies to me ? <p> Email **29;95;TOOLONG with ' Does the FAD Bylaw apply to my property ' in the subject line and provide your full name , contact details ( email and phone ) and property address . <p> We will let you know if you are within a Flood Protection and Drainage applicable area and provide advice on applying for a bylaw authority if required .
