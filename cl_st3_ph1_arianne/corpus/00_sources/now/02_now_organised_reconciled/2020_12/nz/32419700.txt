<p> Press Release -- Wellington City Council Wellingtonians are invited to join in the fun at Waitohi 's first birthday party this Saturday . <p> " It has been an outstanding success in bringing both the local and wider Wellington community together , " says Councillor Jill Day . " So , we hope everyone comes down to say Waitohi ra whanau/happy birthday . " <p> The first birthday party will have a range of events where people of all ages and interests can play , learn and have fun . Whether it 's getting creative in the Library 's ' Hive Maker Space ' , playing games in the park , contemplating the artworks within the space , or watching the entertainment in the sun -- there is something for everyone . <p> " Bring your whanau and friends down for a fun day of play and activities , including competitions , spot prizes and giveaways , " adds Cr Day . <p> There are also four new Fast EV Chargers recently installed in the Waitohi hub parking area . <p> These are in addition to the 28 other @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a two-year pilot between Wellington City Council , Energy Efficiency and Conservation Authority ( EECA ) and ChargeNet NZ , says Councillor Tamatha Paul , Climate Change portfolio lead . <p> " With Waitohi 's birthday and the new EV Chargers operating , it 's a double celebration for the neighbourhood . <p> " EV Chargers are an integral part of our Te Atakura plan to reduce emissions to zero by 2050 .
