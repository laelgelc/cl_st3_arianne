I 'm tempted to think my piece had an influence on the result . <p> The Herald ran a similar poll in March last year for the worldwide day of action , Skolstrejk f ? r klimatet events . <p> I was privileged to address our district 's student strikers at Heipipi Endeavour Park on the Friday . He said it was " a lot of hot air " ; I commented that a meaningful declaration makes authorities more accountable . <p> Say it is not so to the many millions already suffering more frequent and severe floods , droughts , heatwaves , wildfires and coastal tidal events ; tell all the climate refugees that it 's not a climate emergency . <p> Continuing the theme of my last column , " We think and see things differently " : <p> Our brains have evolved to react to short-term crises @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and to be less concerned about the long term and more distant happenings . <p> Nearly everyone follows the " self , family and friends must come first " motto no matter what . But to prevail , pressure to conform must be overcome . <p> In my column , I used my experience of a stroke limiting my field of vision as a lesson on looking at things a different way ; even changing a viewing point to better see a fuller picture . <p> Imagine an advanced , intelligent alien time-traveller monitoring human activity here since the ice ages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ modern technologies to continually increase their attack on nature . <p> Would that alien not think us a most ignorant form of life ? Declare it for what it is , acknowledge the emergency , act , and prepare for consequences . <p> I 've given up trying to talk sense to the people who have the ability to make it happen Bob .
