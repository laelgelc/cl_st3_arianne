<h> Decline averted , let 's keep recovery going <p> They have an uncanny ability to camouflage themselves from some predators , but this skill is also their greatest danger . <p> Once one of the country 's rarest birds , only 2500 New Zealand dotterels remain . The plucky northern New Zealand dotterel has seen a turnaround in fortune as numbers increase steadily in the region . Last summer 108 chicks fledged , the most since 2008 when monitoring began . This has helped to improve the bird 's conservation status from ' declining ' to ' recovering ' . <p> While the dotterel is now no longer considered a threatened species , that could all change in the blink of an eye if their breeding grounds are not protected . <p> " It 's a huge relief to see the improved conservation status for tuturiwhatu pukunui , or NZ Dotterel " says the Chair of the Environment and Climate Change Committee , Councillor Richard Hills . <p> " Tuturiwhatu pukunui used to be a widespread species but were reduced to near-extinction by introduced predators , coastal development and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is still one of the biggest threats to these birds , and the disappointing acts of a few individuals can turn back the years of hard work and dedication from our staff , Mana Whenua , and countless volunteers .
