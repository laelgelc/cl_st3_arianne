<h> Congestion charge proposed for Auckland City roads <p> Toll roads have n't been in place in urban Auckland since 1984 , but they could soon be back according to a new proposal pieced together by the government and Auckland Council . <p> According to Stuff , the proposal has been four years in the making between the two groups , and is known as The Congestion Question . Set to be revealed in full today , it details the potential benefits of a toll for Auckland motorways , while also naming some alternative ideas . <p> Reduction of traffic , a push to encourage people onto public transport , less greenhouse gases due to fewer cars , and increased revenue are listed as potential gains if a toll road system becomes a reality . <p> The question becomes just how much it will cost the average motorist . While more is expected to be known when the proposal breaks , Stuff notes that a working group proposed a $2.80 charge per trip back in 2014 during an earlier toll road study . <p> " It 's an important piece @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to take a bit of time to consider carefully , " Transport Minister Michael Wood told Stuff . " We 'll take it seriously -- it 'll probably inform further thinking and further work . " <p> Response to the notion has been mixed so far . Stuff notes that AA New Zealand considers itself a " cautious supporter " of a toll road scheme , while other groups have noted that even if a proposal is approved it 's unlikely to be actioned in the short term . <p> Council environment and climate change committee chair Richard Hill , meanwhile , noted that the impacts of a congestion charge on Auckland 's most vulnerable communities need to be considered . His comments are consistent with concerns raised back in 2014 during a previous study into tolls . <p> " Auckland 's most vulnerable households would pay 11 per cent of the additional charges , " said the report . Phil Goff noted at the time that " around 3.4-3.9 per cent of low-income households would experience a high financial impact . There needs to be a discount policy in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on low incomes . " <p> New Zealand has some toll roads already , of course . The Northern Gateway is perhaps the best known , having been a toll road since the completion of its construction in 2009 . The Auckland Harbour Bridge started out as a toll bridge in 1959 , and remained as such until 1984 . <p> Toll roads are much more prolific overseas , from the US to across Asia , to much closer to home in Australia where congestion charges are extensive in Melbourne , Sydney , Brisbane , and more . <p> NZ Autocar is New Zealand 's leading automotive magazine .
