<p> News from Porirua City Council The Porirua City Council has confirmed its commitment to addressing the effects of climate change , officially adopting a strategy . <p> A climate change emergency was declared by the Council in June 2019 , with a strategy , Rautaki o Te Ao Hurihuri , developed from that point . <p> A meeting of full Council adopted the strategy tonight . <p> While the Council acknowledges climate change is a long-term issue in nature , it has supported a three-year timeframe as the start of the longer term planning and implementation . <p> The three focus areas for the strategy are a zero-carbon Council , a resilient city , and a low-carbon future . Sea-level rises , higher rainfall events , dry summers and increased flood flows on our rivers and streams are among the effects of climate change in our city . <p> The strategy includes a commitment to work with mana whenua Ngati Toa as it is implemented . <p> " Climate change is one of the biggest challenges of our time , " she said .
