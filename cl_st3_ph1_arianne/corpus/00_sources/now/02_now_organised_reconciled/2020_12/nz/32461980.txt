<h> Scientists unite across world for ocean research <p> Scientists are uniting across the world for a decade of ocean research , carrying on in the voyage of scientific exploration that began more than a century ago . <p> In 1872 the HMS Challenger left Portsmouth in the UK on a four-year circumnavigation of the globe to explore the deep sea . <p> It was a scientific voyage of discovery that has since been recognised as the origin of deep sea science and modern oceanography .
