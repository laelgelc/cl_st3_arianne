<h> John Donohoe <p> Cllr Ronan Moore , Social Democrats , has welcomed Meath County Council 's adoption of his motion in the Draft County Development Plan to encourage the use of measures specifically designed to enhance wildlife in residential schemes such as channels being left in boundary walls to allow for passage of hedgehogs between gardens as well as bat and swift boxes . <p> Cllr Moore said : " The Meath County Development Plan is perhaps the single-most important influencer of how our county develops over the next six or seven years and this is reflected in the range of topics that it covers from economic and employment ; infrastructure ; climate change ; and settlement and housing to name just a few . " This motion gives rightful significance in future planning to our wild-life and protected native species , and encourages biodiversity in residential and urban settings .
