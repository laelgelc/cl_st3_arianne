<h> Publica joins international organisations for ESG best practice <p> Switzerland 's largest pension fund , Publica , has joined four organisations active in the field of sustainable investments internationally . <p> The organisations include the Principles for Responsible Investment , the Institutional Investor Group on Climate Change , Climate Action 100+ and the Montr ? al Carbon Pledge . <p> With the new memberships , Publica plans to step up its efforts on the use of environmental , social and corporate governance ( ESG ) through the access to international best practices . <p> Stefan Beiner , head of asset management at Publica , said : " The goal to take long-term investment trends into account takes an important step through the new memberships " . <p> Publica is a funding member of the Swiss Association for Responsible Investments ( SVVK-ASIR ) , alongside SBB , the pension fund for the federal railways , and the scheme for the canton of Zurich , BVK , among others . <p> According to its report on climate-related opportunities and risks for 2019 , Publica excluded investments in the coal sector , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , through a strategic asset allocation worth 3.5% of its total assets . <p> The deputy chief investment officer , Patrick Uelfeti told IPE that the fund plans in the future to overweight its portfolio in companies with " promising and high-quality patents " in the renewable energy sector against other companies , based on its climate efficient equity index . <p> Publica 's approach to sustainability in relation to investment activities is based on a statutory mandate and normative basis , it added . Its financial analysis explicitly integrates ESG in the investment process and it uses an array of tactics to reiterate the importance of ESG criteria and put pressure on invested companies . <p> It has also decided to exclude from its portfolio companies that develop , manufacture , sell or store products that violate international conventions ratified by Switzerland or that violate Swiss law . <p> In addition , the scheme exercises voting rights for listed companies in Switzerland and engages in dialogue with domestic and foreign companies to identify " problematic investments " . <p> The disposal of holdings is considered as a last resort @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the intention to carry out positive change . <p> Publica also exercises a veto right on investment in infrastructure to avoid financing projects that lead to risks , including climate risks . <p> In the next few years , Publica will build up a database with further climate-specific indicators as a decision-making basis for investments , it said . <p> It also wants to measure the carbon footprint in buildings to apply sustainability criteria for direct real estate investments in Switzerland .
