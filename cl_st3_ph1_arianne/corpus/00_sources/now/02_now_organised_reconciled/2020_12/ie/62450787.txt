<h> Federated Hermes <h> Impact investing : a key role to play in reshaping the future <p> Today , the world is confronting the global coronavirus pandemic , which has put a severe strain on health systems and economies worldwide . <p> But amid this crisis , we have a chance to build a better future . For responsible investment strategies , Covid-19 has resulted in a paradigm shift : it has put a focus on the critical need to build resilience in healthcare , food and water security , and across supply chains . It has also put climate change and worker rights under the spotlight . <p> Impact investing is forward-looking : it aims to identify businesses and organisations pursuing purpose-led activities whose products and services tackle the pressing needs of the global economy and environment .
