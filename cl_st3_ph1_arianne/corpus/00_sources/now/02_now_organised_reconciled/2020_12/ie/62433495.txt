<h> Is sustainability mispriced ? <p> Living in the developed world over the past 50 years , life has been stable , even idyllic , for most people . That is certainly compared with their grandparents and previous generations who lived through two world wars and the Spanish flu . But , as COVID-19 has shown so cruelly , there are existential dangers that can lie hidden . These can rip the established world order asunder if not tackled beforehand . <p> The world is waking up from its complacency . Perhaps one positive outcome of COVID-19 has been a better appreciation of the dangers of critical long-term trends that need to be tackled before the next crisis they create overwhelmes . These pose questions not only for politicians , but also investors for whom the Chinese word for crisis , weiji , is often translated as danger plus opportunity . <p> Chris Varco and his colleagues Annachiara Marcandalli and Lydia Guett at Cambridge Associates published a note earlier this year highlighting five key sustainability trends they believe will help shape investment portfolios over the long term .
