<h> FEATURE : Reef system declared turtle sanctuary <p> Ciaran Cannon <p> 26 December , 2020 , 11:45 pm <p> Tagged and released ... Adi Tota heads out to sea . Picture ; SUPPLIED <p> The sand cay on Duff Reef north of Vanuabalavu Island in Lau was declared a turtle breeding sanctuary last month . Working with Conservation International under the Lau Seascape Initiative , the traditional fishing ground ( qoliqoli ) owners , the yavusa Qalitu of Mavana Village in Vanuabalavu , Lau declared the traditional taboo . <p> The traditional taboo also includes the entire reef system surrounding the sand bank comprising 1180 hectares of prime marine structure . <p> Its sand bank area is five hectares and is a natural breeding ground for turtles . Marine biologists have identified the Duff Reef sand cay as a major breeding ground for the endangered green and hawksbill species in the Pacific Ocean region . <p> CI Fiji marine program manager Semisi Meo expressed his appreciation to the strong leadership of Mavana Village in embracing the value of science to inform sound natural resource management . <p> " The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have highlighted the value of the sand bank to the nesters population and in restoring the future of the turtle 's population . <p> " This has led to the community to share their love as a community of people that cares for the animals and to safeguard its reproductive grounds on the sand bank island , " Mr Meo said . <p> The sand bank at Duff reef is the breeding ground for at least the two main species of turtle in Fiji and recent studies have shown that up to more than 50 nests on the sand bank during turtle nesting season and confirmation that the surrounding Duff reef is not a turtle feeding ground hence turtles swim there simply to lay their eggs . <p> To mark the Duff reef declaration , two green sea turtles ( Chelonia mydas ) were tagged on Thursday , November 19 before a celebration by the villagers of Mavana on Friday , November 20 . <p> The tagged turtles were named Adi Vatulami and Adi Tota respectively .
