<h> Extreme Weather Cost Billions Worldwide in 2020 , Says Study <p> Extreme weather in 2020 caused huge financial losses and loss of human life , according to a report by Christian Aid . Chalked up to " Climate Change " , experts believe things will only get worse in the future . <p> But such events are not just about the financial costs that they incur . Human lives in the places affected were also severely impacted . <p> For example , an unusually rainy monsoon season in Asia resulted in some of the most damaging storms in living memory . Over a period of a few months in 2020 , heavy flooding in India resulted in more than 2,000 deaths , with millions of others displaced from their homes . <p> China was also badly affected by these storms , with damages running up to $32 billion between June and October of 2020 .
