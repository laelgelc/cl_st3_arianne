<h> Free water bottle fillers installed at library and pool <p> Free water bottle fillers have been installed by Westmeath County Council at Mullingar library and the town 's swimming pool in a bid to reduce the amount of single use plastic waste generated locally . <p> Cathaoirleach Ken Glynn says that the fillers have been installed to encourage users to bring their own bottle and refill as required . <p> " I am delighted to see these units , offering a free water refill , placed in key public locations . This is a pragmatic solution to tackling the plastic pollution problem , as users have the opportunity to refill their own reusable bottle . It also gives people the opportunity to take a practical step in tackling climate change as so often we hear about the need to ' take action " ' to tackle climate change and it can seem unreachable but this is a simple step that anyone from nought to ninety can take " . <p> Mary Stuart , County Librarian , welcomed the installation of the water bottle filler units in Mullingar Library and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to have these facilities available for our customers . Unfortunately , Covid restrictions have meant that they have not been utilised to their maximum capacity , but we are looking forward to seeing them utilised and enjoyed by our customers when normal activity returns , " she said . <p> The installation of the water bottle fillers is a waste prevention initiative by the Council 's Environment Department . Ruth Maxwell of the Environment Department stated : " Every day in excess of 220,000 single use plastic bottles are used in Ireland . Under this initiative twelve water bottle fillers are being installed with the remaining installation scheduled for Belvedere House , Park and Gardens in the new year . Each one of these units will play a part in reducing the demand for plastic bottles and some of the units have been installed in schools . Installing the units in schools has a dual effect -- it creates an amenity in the school and is also an educational tool to practically demonstrate how our actions can make a difference -- one sip at a time ! Swimmers are delighted to have this facility on their entry/exit and the unit is proving to be a great success with all ages " . <p> The water bottle fillers are a unit that allows the user to insert their reusable bottle into the unit and it fills the bottle automatically , without any need for touch or contact . There is a digital counter on the unit and it records the number of refills per unit . The units have been supplied and installed by Crystal Clear Ltd.
