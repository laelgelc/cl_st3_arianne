<p> A new GAA Green Club programme will see 45 clubs and two regional venues explore a range of sustainability projects designed to enrich their physical and social environments . <p> The GAA Green Club Programme , supported by the County &amp; City Management Association and the local authority Climate Action Regional Offices , is delivered in partnership with the LGFA and Camogie Association , with additional support from expert agencies across the five thematic focus areas of the programme , namely : Energy , Waste , Biodiversity , Water , Travel &amp; Transport . <p> The all-Ireland nature of the initiative was reflected in the contribution to the online launch event by Eamon Ryan , Minister for the Environment , Climate and Communications in the Republic and representatives of Northern Ireland Executive 's Department of Agriculture , Environment , and Rural Affairs . <p> The Green Club Phase One participants were selected from 220 clubs that responded earlier this year to a questionnaire designed to gauge interest in developing a Green Club initiative . The responses highlighted a wealth of sustainability work already being undertaken by GAA units and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ future-proofing their communities and facilities . <p> Phase One will run for twelve months from December 2020 with the intention of amassing as much practical learnings from the participating clubs and venues as possible . This will inform the creation of a GAA Green Clubs toolkit that will be made available to all GAA units in Ireland ( 1,600 clubs , plus county and provincial venues ) , replete with useful case studies , established partnership models , and funding avenues . <p> GAA President , John Horan , said : " The Green Clubs Project further demonstrates that at its core the GAA is a community-based organisation grounded in place . The project seeks to support our hard-working volunteers in future-proofing their club facilities while contributing to the sustainability of their community from an environmental and cultural perspective . It also demonstrates the GAA 's commitment as an official SDG Champion of the Irish government .
