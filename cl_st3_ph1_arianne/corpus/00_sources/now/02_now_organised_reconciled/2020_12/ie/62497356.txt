<h> New Iron-based Catalyst Converts Carbon Dioxide into Jet Fuel <p> Researchers at the University of Oxford have successfully developed a way of turning CO2 into a viable form of jet fuel . Effectively reversing the combustion process , this new method could open the door for " carbon-neutral " jet fuels in the future . <h> The need for carbon-neutral fuels is growing <p> Over the last few decades , the aviation industry has been pulling out all the stops to find ways to reduce its so-called " carbon footprint " . Initiatives have included carbon offsetting through planting trees or investing in renewable energy sources in order to compensate for the large amounts of CO2 emitted by aircraft on a daily basis . <p> The aviation industry is looking for ways to decarbonize as much as possible like Airbus ' " Hydrogen Pod " concept aircraft .
