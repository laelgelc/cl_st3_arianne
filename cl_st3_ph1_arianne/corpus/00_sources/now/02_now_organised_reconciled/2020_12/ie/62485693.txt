<h> Viewpoint : How trustees can create practical carbon journey plans <p> Dealing with climate change is a responsibility we all share -- but trustees may be wondering how they get from their starting point to the required end-goal . This ' carbon journey ' needs a plan . <p> For pension trustees that are familiar with the concept of a traditional funding journey plan , the creation of what we describe as a carbon journey plan can provide the climate-related equivalent . <p> For many pension funds , the traditional journey plan objective focuses on full funding ( likely with a risk buffer ) on a technical provisions , self-sufficiency or buy-out basis . <p> Similarly , well-defined climate goals have emerged within the international community over the last decade , including the Paris Agreement aim of limiting global temperature increases . <p> Common metrics for assessing progress against those goals include carbon emissions , carbon intensity and allocations to climate solutions . <p> In truth , carbon emissions of asset owners themselves are often trivial in the global context . In the case of climate risks , and specifically carbon emissions , this will depend on current global trends and portfolio trends . <p> What 's desirable from a carbon journey plan perspective -- the funding journey plan equivalents of maturity and covenant -- will arise from a combination of factors such as peer benchmarking , government pledges and policy , and ongoing scientific evidence . <p> It could also reflect a scheme 's ambition or competitive advantages . As with other similar objectives , using a blend of short , medium and long-term targets will help with good governance and accountability . <p> Once a target is set , pension funds will have a range of levers with which they can make adjustments in their carbon journey . <p> At one end , divestment is relatively straightforward ; selling or excluding assets that are most exposed to climate risk .
