<h> What does field vegetable production look like in Ireland ? <p> 2021 is the UN International Year of Fruit and Vegetables which aims to raise awareness of the nutritional and health benefits of eating fruit and vegetables . <p> In Ireland , the field vegetable sector is worth almost 16% of horticulture output , valued at ? 77.9 million . <p> Lorcan Bourke , fresh produce and potato manager for Bord Bia , explains more about the sector , which is composed of many different crop types with many common challenges . <h> Production <p> The main business of fresh vegetable production is focused around large-scale grading and packing of vegetables for supermarkets and foodservice channels . While there is no significant vegetable processing sector in Ireland , there is a distinct separate market for fresh prepared vegetables sold into foodservice . <p> The total production area for field vegetables in 2019 was circa 4,369ha . Agreeing long-term crop programmes with retailers has become an essential part of planning for today 's grower and will be important in the future to ensure a supply of local grown seasonal vegetables . <p> Ireland has a strong cohort of well-established professional growers with the skills and capacity to increase the domestic market share of sustainably produced fresh produce . <h> Weather and labour challenge <p> Many growers report it is more difficult to grow vegetables nowadays , with extremes in weather taking place more frequently as a result of climate change . <p> The weather in any given year has a massive bearing on the land conditions , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the profitability of field crops . <p> Securing suitable land for vegetable production has become a difficult task in itself as finding fertile soils near water courses for irrigation can be challenging . Despite investment in increased mechanisation and labour-saving devises , on a typical vegetable farm there will always be a huge dependence on manual labour for harvesting and packing . <p> The ' outdoor ' nature of working on field vegetable farms can often make working there less attractive and it can be difficult to recruit staff . <p> Advertisement <h> Retail market <p> The key market for this sector in Ireland is the retail market .
