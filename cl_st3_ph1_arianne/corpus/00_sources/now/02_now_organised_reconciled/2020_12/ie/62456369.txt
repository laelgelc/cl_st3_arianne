<p> There are neither plans to increase visitor numbers to Skellig Michael nor increase the number of boat operators to the tourist site on the Iveragh peninsula , according to a new draft management plan for the UNESCO World Heritage Site . <p> The new plan - which , when finalised - will oversee the management of the island from 2020 to 2030 . Its key objective is the conservation and preservation of the island , while balancing the demand for visitors to the tourist attraction . It is vital , given its status , that the island is maintained to the highest international standards . <p> There are 11 objectives outlined in the plan , which is currently out for public consultation , and chief among them is the need to ensure no damage is caused to the island , particularly by visitors .
