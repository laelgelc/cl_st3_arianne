<h> Low-Lying Island Grows Despite Sea Level Rise , Puzzling Scientists <p> A new study has revealed a surprising phenomenon : Scientists have seen that a sprawling chain of volcanic islands and atolls located in the central Pacific Ocean grow in size when examining the evolution of the island . <p> This is especially surprising since scientists were expecting to see otherwise . Low-lying atolls have long been considered especially vulnerable to climate change since rising sea levels could easily submerge them . However , it is now seen that some islands are doing the opposite and gaining mass since 1943 . <p> The inhabited Jeh island , which is made up of a remote chain of coral atolls and volcanic islands , is in the low-lying Pacific nation of the Marshall Islands . Atoll islands are heaps of reef-derived sediments deposited in the last 5000 years or less , and according to the study , the low-lying islands have been called the " most endangered nation on Earth due to the potential effects of sea-level rise .
