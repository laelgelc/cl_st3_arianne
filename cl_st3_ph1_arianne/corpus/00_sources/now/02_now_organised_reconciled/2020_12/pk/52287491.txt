<h> 8th round of Pak-Netherlands political consultations held <p> ISLAMABAD - The 8th round of Pakistan-The Netherlands Bilateral Political Consultations was held in the virtual format on Tuesday . <p> Special Secretary ( Europe ) Dr Aman Rashid and Director-General for Political Affairs , Ministry of Foreign Affairs , The Netherlands , Thijs Van Der Plas , led their respective delegations . <p> The two sides discussed the whole spectrum of bilateral relations , including political , economic , trade and investment , climate change , cooperation on multilateral issues as well as developments in their respective regions . <p> While expressing satisfaction at the steady growth in bilateral ties , it was reiterated that high-level interactions were necessary for further strengthening relations . The two sides expressed satisfaction at the level of cooperation at international fora , including at the UN and agreed to continue supporting each other , said a foreign ministry statement said . <p> The Dutch side was briefed on the Covid-19 situation in Pakistan and the steps being taken by the government to save lives and livelihoods .
