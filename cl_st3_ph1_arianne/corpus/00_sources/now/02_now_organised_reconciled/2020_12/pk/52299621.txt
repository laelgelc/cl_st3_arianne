<p> The Balochistan 's Environment Protection Agency has recommended closing the Charna Island for divers , fishermen and boats because of coral bleaching . <p> The agency prepared its report on coral bleaching and requested the government to stop tourists , divers , fishermen and boatsmen from coming to the area . Some people anchor their boats at the island and cleaned them . This should be banned too , the report recommended . <p> The agency has asked permission to set up its monitoring station at the island . The island should be declared Marine Protected Area ' , it said . <p> A Marine Protected Area is a clearly defined geographical space dedicated and managed through legal means to achieve long-term conservation of nature , the International Union for Conservation of Nature describes . Pakistan 's first Marine Protected Area was Astola Island . <p> EPA Director Muhammad Khan said that a widescale survey needs to be conducted to study the effects and impact of coral bleaching in the area . <p> Coral bleaching is when colorful and vibrant coral turns white because of negative environmental conditions , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ WWF , coral are bright and colourful because of microscopic algae called zooxanthellae . The zooxanthellae live within the coral in a mutually beneficial relationship , each helping the other survive . But because of climate change , the coral stresses out and expels the algae . Residing as deep as 100 feet in the sea , the ocean depends on coral to keep many species alive .
