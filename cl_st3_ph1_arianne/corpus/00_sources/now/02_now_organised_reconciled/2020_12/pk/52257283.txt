" <p> Cowne wrote that the minister 's aim to develop wildlife sanctuaries for the reintroduction of White Rhinos and cheetahs in Pakistan will be beneficial in recreational aspects for people and the substantial international eco-tourism market . <p> " Free The Wild would be really pleased to assist you , and the government , with the plan , " the letter added . <p> Pakistan was given Kaavan as a diplomatic gift from Sri Lanka .
