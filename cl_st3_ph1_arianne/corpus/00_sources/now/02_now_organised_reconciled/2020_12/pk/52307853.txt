<p> KARACHI : Sindh Government Spokesperson and Chief Minister 's Advisor on Law , Environment , Climate Change and Coastal Development Barrister Murtaza Wahab on Saturday inaugurated a neighborhood park here in Clifton and announced that Burns Road and Haqqani Chowk will also be converted into urban recreational places for the citizens . <p> During the inauguration of the park , Pakistan Peoples Party ( PPP ) leaders Khalil Haut , Najmi Alam and Karamullah Waqasi , Commissioner South Irshad Sodhar , Municipal Commissioner South Akhtar Sheikh and some others were present . <p> Murtaza informed the media that colorful benches and sheds have been set up in the park and a space for various sports has also been allotted to the park in collaboration with a local Non-Governmental Organisation ( NGO ) . <p> He said : " The NGO had come up with a plan and put it into practice for the welfare of the public . This place had a devastating look , which has now been turned into a hub for walking and playing basketball .
