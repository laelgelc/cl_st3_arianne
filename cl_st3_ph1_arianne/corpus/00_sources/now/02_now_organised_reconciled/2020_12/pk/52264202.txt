<h> PM Khan Inaugurated 2 National Parks in GB <p> PM Khan has inaugurated Himalaya and Nanga Parbat National Parks , covering a huge area of 3,600 square kilometers in GB , for protecting the endangered wildlife and promote the eco-tourism in the area . <p> As per the shared details , this was stated by PM 's Special Assistant on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam while addressing a press conference in Islamabad on Wednesday . <p> He said these parks will comprise a five percent area of Gilgit-Baltistan , adding that they will form a nature corridor between Azad Kashmir and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa . He said the newly notified Himalaya National Park and the Nanga Parbat NP comprise unique ecological areas with very rich high altitude biodiversity as well as precious flora and fauna which include snow leopards , Himalayan brown bears , Laddakh Urial , ibex , markhor , and blue sheep . <p> The two national parks have been announced under the PMs ' Protected Areas Initiative ' which aims to ensure the protection and preservation of Pakistan 's natural assets via designation and management of national @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have also been made for the natural breeding of Laddakh Urial so as to boost the population of endangered species . He said that proper community-based management regimes will be put in place in all of them . <p> " In this regard , the PM also approved the formation of Gilgit Baltistan 's first ' National Parks Service ' which will provide 5,000 green jobs to the youth in the province .
