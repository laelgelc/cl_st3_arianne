<p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : Prime Minister Imran Khan will participate in the Launch Ceremony of the Billion Tree Honey Initiative , a national apiculture program . <p> This initiative is part of the prime minister 's vision of a Clean &amp; Green Pakistan . <p> It aims at promoting tree plantation to support the production of Honey in the Country under the Ten Billion Tree Tsunami Programme ( TBTTP ) . <p> Accordingly , the plantation of plants including Kao , Phulai , Ber , Kikar , and other bee flora is being encouraged under the TBTTP . <p> Meanwhile , an exclusive analysis was conducted of Honey Production in the Country to devise a mechanism for encouraging the promotion of bee flora improving the quality of honey production , providing livelihood to the beekeepers , and sustain the activity through the provision of financial resources . <p> It is estimated that about 10,000 beekeepers are using 300,000 colonies for producing 7,500 metric tons of honey annually . <p> The potential can be enhanced to produce 70,000 metric tons of honey from the same harvest by using modern beekeeping @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the product , and intensive marketing . <p> It is anticipated that the marketing of 70,000 metric tons of honey will generate an income of about Rs 35-43 billion in the national economy and provide about 87,000 green jobs . <p> The " Billion Tree Honey " Initiative is being launched by the Ministry of Climate Change as a coherent effort of different Ministries/ Agencies to promote Apiculture in the Country . <p> The existing forestry resource available in the Protected Areas and the new areas being planted/regenerated under TBTTP till 2022-23 is estimated to expand up to about 5.5 million hectares . <p> The requisite forest resource shall be used by the beekeepers to produce honey specific to particular flora and shall be branded accordingly . <p> The National Vocational and Technical Training Commission ( NAVTTC ) shall provide training to the selected beekeepers along with technical support , follow-up of on-ground activities and product extraction . <p> The certified beekeepers shall be provided financial support through the Kamyab Jawan Programme . <p> The Ministry of Science and Technology shall be responsible for certifying the honey produced under the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shall be purchased by the National Commission of Pakistan ( SECP ) under Section 42 of the Companies Act , 2017 aligned with the Ministry of Science and Technology . <p> The NAP shall provide an end to end solution for promoting and marketing quality honey nationally and internationally . <p> A pilot testing of the programme shall be initiated at selected sites during the upcoming Spring 2020.
