<h> Bundal Island development , a policy disaster in waiting . <p> Bundal , a small island off the coast of Sindh , is at the center of a political storm . The Prime Minister Imran Khan is determined to develop Bundal into a world-class island city under the jurisdiction of the recently established Pakistan Island Development Authority . But this move has sparked yet another dispute with the Sindh administration over ownership and control of the province . <p> This kind of all-too-familiar controversy has obscured any reasonable analysis of the pros and cons of the Urban Development and Bundle Island Project ( UDBSP ) . Presumably , the island will provide additional land to house the city 's growing population , and thus address the transport , service access , and sanitation problems currently facing residents due to runaway urbanization . It should be said that building cities on the sands that sustain and regulate a complex ecosystem complete with fishing villages , marine life , and forests is irreconcilable with the PM 's recognition of -- and commitment to preserving -- the environment . <p> But the policy implications for this about-turn run deeper than a failed campaign promise . At stake here is the culture and livelihood of fishing communities that have occupied the coastal waters for hundreds of years .
