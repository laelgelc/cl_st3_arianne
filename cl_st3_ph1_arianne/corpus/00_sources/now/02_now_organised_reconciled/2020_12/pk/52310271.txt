<h> Languishing development <p> No question that significant strides have been made to enhance the green nature of the country <p> December 20 , 2020 <p> The latest Human Development Index ( HDI ) report has shown that Pakistan is languishing at the 154th position out of the 189 countries in the world who were evaluated . Pakistan lags far behind other South Asian nations such as India , Sri Lanka , Nepal and Bangladesh in the latest index . Rich countries including Norway , Ireland , Hong Kong ( China ) , Iceland , and Germany led the rankings . The US came in at 17 . The poorest countries on the earth , including Niger , the Central African Republic , Chad , South Sudan and Burundi had the lowest scores in the HDI measurements . <p> The index compares countries on indicators of health , education , and standards of living . However , this year , it included two more elements : a nation 's carbon dioxide emissions and material footprint that put enormous strain on the planet .
