<h> The great reset <p> 2020 has been much maligned as the year we want to forget . The regrettable events of the year notwithstanding , the year deserves to be recognised for being the harbinger of change and the trigger for the great reset that will mark this decade . <p> The final weeks of this momentous year has been marked by a welcome boost to market sentiment , riding on the tail of a slew of recent positive vaccine developments ; easing uncertainty over the US Presidential election outcomes and rising expectations of a US fiscal package . <p> Our view is that 2021 will be a year of economic recovery , despite near-term headwinds from surging new Covid-19 cases in some parts of the world and waning global economic momentum . Global central banks remain committed to ultra-loose monetary policy and continued fiscal support for the nascent recovery .
