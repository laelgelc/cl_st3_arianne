Should the United States establish a truth and reconciliation commission to heal national divisions ? <p> These questions do not have obvious answers , especially when such difficult times still lie ahead . The rule of law calls for laws to be promulgated publicly , enforced equally , and judged independently . If Trump were to issue a self-pardon , the distrust that has been eroding US institutions would surge to new heights . <p> The second thing that should not happen is a further outpouring of scorn toward others .
