<h> This new orchid species is the ' ugliest ' in the world <p> Orchids are often thought of as beautiful , delicate and vibrant -- but a newly identified plant might just change your mind . <p> The " Gastrodia agnicellus " -- labeled the " ugliest orchid in the world " -- is one of this year 's newly described plants and fungi , researchers from the Royal Botanic Gardens , Kew ( RBG Kew ) have announced . <p> The orchid , located in Madagascar , has no leaves , grows from a woolly tuberous stem , and spends most of its life underground , emerging only to flower or produce fruit . <p> Beauty is in the eye of the beholder , Johan Hermans , a researcher on orchids behind the find , told CNN on Thursday . <p> Still , he said : " It 's not very attractive , I must say -- it 's fleshy looking , red inside and brown on the outside .
