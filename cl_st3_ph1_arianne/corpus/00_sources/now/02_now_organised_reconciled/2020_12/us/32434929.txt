One remarkable thing I learned while on the committee was that there is a direct connection between forest management in New England and clear-cutting in the Amazon , or even Siberia . <p> This is because the demand for hardwood remains relatively constant regardless of its origin . We believe that by harvesting responsibly in the Northeast , we take off some pressure from unregulated timber markets in other parts of the world , where the destruction of forested land has searing and long-term impacts .
