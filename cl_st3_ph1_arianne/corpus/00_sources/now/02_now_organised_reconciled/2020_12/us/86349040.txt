Perhaps the best measurement of a season 's overall activity is not the number of named storms but rather its " accumulated cyclone energy , " or ACE , which sums up the intensity and duration of storms . So a weak , short-lived tropical storm counts for almost nothing , whereas a major , long-lived hurricane will quickly rack up dozens of points . <p> The ACE value for the 2020 Atlantic season to date is 179.8 -- and another weak tropical or subtropical storm could still form . This is notably higher than the climatological norm for ACE values ( about 104 ) , but it would not quite make the top 10 busiest Atlantic seasons on record , which is paced by the 1933 and 2005 seasons .
