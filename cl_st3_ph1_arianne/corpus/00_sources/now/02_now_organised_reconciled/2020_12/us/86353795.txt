CBS News <p> Routine wildfire is a natural and healthy aspect of forests . Cyclical fire acts to eliminate the less healthy parts of the ecosystem , recycles nutrients back into the soil and clears out overgrown vegetation . This not only helps maintain a healthy ecosystem , but also helps naturally reduce fuel so that fires do n't become too large . <p> Most trees are well adapted to these routine fires . In the mountainous slopes of California , for example , ponderosa pines , sugar pines and giant sequoias have thick bark that keeps the living tissue underneath insulated from extreme heat . Some tree species also drop their branches which grow closest to the ground . That helps impede fires from climbing up into the canopy and more easily spreading . <p> For some trees fires are necessary for reproduction .
