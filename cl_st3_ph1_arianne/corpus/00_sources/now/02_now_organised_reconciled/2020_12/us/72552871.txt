<p> While action by governments , cities and businesses is key to curbing warming , individuals also have a major role to play , Giki said in a statement . <p> On a global basis , individuals account for almost three-quarters of greenhouse gas emissions through their homes , transport , food , goods and services consumption , it added . <p> People 's carbon footprints vary widely across the world . <p> On average , each person accounts for 5 tonnes of carbon dioxide emitted every year - but that increases dramatically with wealth , according to the " Cut a Tonne in ' 21 " backers . <p> The richest 1% has an annual footprint of about 70 tonnes , while the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ report , co-authored by Richard Carmichael of the Centre for Environmental Policy at Imperial College London . <p> That per capita tally needs to drop to 2.5 tonnes by 2030 to be in line with the 1.5C warming goal , it said . <p> How do I cut a tonne of carbon ?
