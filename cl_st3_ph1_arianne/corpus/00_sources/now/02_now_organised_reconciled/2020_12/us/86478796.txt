<h> Beyond Coronavirus And Economic Relief : What Voters Want <p> " Mandate , schmandate , I 'm sitting here and you 're sitting there , " is the phrase president-to-be John F. Kennedy is supposed to have used when a young reporter pressed him about the meaning of his election . The story , told to me by a former AEI colleague , the great psephologist and Census director Richard Scammon ( 1915 -- 2001 ) , is probably apocryphal . But it speaks to a fundamental truth that a president , even in deeply polarized times , has the responsibility to set the agenda . His successes and failures depend to some extent on public sentiment . <p> So what do Americans want president-elect Biden to do ? What do recent polls tell us about Joe Biden 's mandate ? Actually , quite a lot . An extensive Morning Consult/Politico post-election poll of registered voters asked people to prioritize 25 items for the new administration .
