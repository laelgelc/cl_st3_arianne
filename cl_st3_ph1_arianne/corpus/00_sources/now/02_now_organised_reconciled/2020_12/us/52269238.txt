<h> How activists stopped a coal plant in Ghana <p> In 2013 , the government of Ghana announced plans for the country 's first coal-fired power plant . Environmental activist Chibeze Ezekiel went to work building a diverse grassroots coalition to stop the plant , and he won . He is now the recipient of the Goldman Environmental Prize for his work .
