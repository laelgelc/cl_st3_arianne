The technology helps support realistic AR experiences and provides a huge boost to photography in low-light . <p> LiDAR is not a new technology . It relies on tiny lasers that bounce light pulses against objects around them to figure out spatial information based on the journey it takes to send and return that light . Most people first became aware of it when it was used to help Apollo 15 map the moon 's surface . ( NASA is now developing the technology for Mars missions . ) <p> LiDAR has seen multiple uses since -- it 's being used to inform collision detection systems in autonomous and semi-autonomous vehicles , and it 's expected to be deployed in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2030 .
