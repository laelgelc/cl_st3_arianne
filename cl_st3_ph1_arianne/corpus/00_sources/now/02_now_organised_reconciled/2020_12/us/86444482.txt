<h> How climate change could undo 50 years of public health gains <p> It 's hard to remember , in the midst of a calamitous global pandemic , that people around the globe are healthier now than they were just a few decades ago . We 're smoking less , eating fewer trans fats , dying from cancer less frequently , getting vaccinated more , and living , on average , longer . The planet , however , is getting sicker . A landmark report published Wednesday in the medical journal The Lancet contains a stark warning : " A changing climate threatens to undermine the past 50 years of gains in public health . " <p> An international team of experts drew on decades of work on climate and health in order to demonstrate that rising temperatures and other consequences of burning fossil fuels are inextricably linked to every facet of human health . They looked at 43 indicators , things like heat , infectious disease , and drought , to track the health effects of the changing climate . <p> Lancet has produced an updated version of this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on Health and Climate Change , " every year since 2015 .
