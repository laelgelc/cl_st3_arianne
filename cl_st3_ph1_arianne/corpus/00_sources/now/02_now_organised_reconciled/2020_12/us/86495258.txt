Covid-19 has accelerated that preexisting trend . <p> To avoid confusion , let 's be clear about the word liberal here . It has nothing to do with its American meaning of lefty , woke or big government ; nor with its European caricature as " neoliberal " and market fundamentalist . The harm principle , as defined in 1859 by John Stuart Mill in his treatise " On Liberty , " says that " the only purpose for which power can be rightfully exercised over any member of a civilized community , against his will , is to prevent harm to others . " <p> So there 's nothing inherently illiberal about , say , a lockdown to slow the spread of Covid-19 . Or about a regulation that requires us to wear masks in public . Nor is it automatically illiberal for the state , in a situation of economic disruption and distress , to step in temporarily as the primary economic actor , paying firms and people until they can earn their livelihoods again . <p> The trouble starts when these measures are adapted , extended or abused .
