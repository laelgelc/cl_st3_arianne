Instead , there were student activists , public service retirees and educators abound . <p> As Ernie Niemi mentions in his recent op-ed , there are deep-pocketed interests leading to the lowest bar of environmental protections in place for Wall Street investment purposes . The science is out there for forestry practices beneficial for fire , climate change and forest communities .
