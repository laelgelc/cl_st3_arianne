<h> Patrick Semansky/AP <p> The incoming Biden-Harris administration will have a long list of disaster-related issues to contend with -- and we do n't just mean the urgent necessity to combat the still-raging COVID-19 pandemic . <p> When Hurricane Iota made landfall in northeastern Nicaragua as a Category 4 storm last month , it became the 30th named storm this year alone -- setting a record high for a year also marked by some of the worst wildfires ever seen , as well as a plethora of climate change-related storms , severe droughts , and a variety of catastrophic events across the planet . By September , the U.S. alone had already counted 16 separate billion-dollar climate-related storms . <p> It 's worth recalling , too , that until the pandemic took hold , the prevailing sense may have been that disasters were indeed more frequent and more severe -- but invariably local . It was the Gulf or the U.S. West Coast that took the hit . That was then .
