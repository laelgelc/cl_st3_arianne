<h> French blue-chips back climate risk disclosure code <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - France 's blue-chip CAC-40 companies threw their support behind global recommendations on climate risk disclosures , a major Paris business organisation said on Saturday . <p> All 40 companies on the benchmark index endorsed the code developed by the international Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures ( TCFD ) , Paris Europlace said . <p> The pledge came as President Emmanuel Macron and British Prime Minister Boris Johnson co-hosted an online U.N . event marking the fifth anniversary of the adoption of the Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> " We have very little time before us , " Macron told the meeting . " We need immediate action . " <p> The TCFD , established by the global Financial Stability Board , in 2017 published voluntary disclosure principles designed to inform investors of climate-related financial risk .
