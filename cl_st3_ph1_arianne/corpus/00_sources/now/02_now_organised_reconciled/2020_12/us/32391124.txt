<p> In 2009 , the U.S. government extended a $535 million dollar loan guarantee to a manufacturer of solar panels named Solyndra . The aim of this subsidy was to expedite the development of renewable energy technology , as the global economy 's reliance on carbon energy was threatening the long-term survival of human civilization . Shortly after Uncle Sam bankrolled the solar firm , the price of a material called polysilicon plummeted by 89 percent .
