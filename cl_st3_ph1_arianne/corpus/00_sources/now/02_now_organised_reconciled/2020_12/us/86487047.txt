<p> It 's the time of the year when talking heads and think tank scholars peer into their crystal balls and try to foresee BIG THINGS that might happen in the new year . Here are three in national security : <p> The Taliban drive the current Afghan government out of power , then civil war breaks out . <p> Pres. Trump intends to reduce US troop levels to 2,500 , a minuscule amount compared with the surge high of 100,000 . The number of contractors is higher , 22,000 ( according to the last figures ) , but the bottom line is that the United States is winding down its efforts to defeat the Taliban . The Democrats seem to agree . The Democratic Party platform called for ending " endless wars " ( does anyone actually support " forever wars " ? ) as has President-elect Biden . The American people are in the same place , having long ago soured on efforts to defeat the Taliban and transform Afghanistan .
