Today 's weather is expected to be mainly cloudy , with a 30 per cent chance of showers this morning and early this afternoon , and a high of 8 C , according to Environment and Climate Change Canada . Overnight , it continues to be mainly cloudy , and then the region should see some sunshine on Thursday . The agency is forecasting a mix of sun and cloud , with a high of 7 C , and an overnight low of 3 C. On Friday , expect mainly cloudy weather with a high of 6 C and possible snow overnight , as the temperature drops below freezing to a low of -- 1 C. Looking ahead , the weekend weather looks mixed , with partial sun on Saturday and 5 C , then more rain overnight , with a low of 2 C.
