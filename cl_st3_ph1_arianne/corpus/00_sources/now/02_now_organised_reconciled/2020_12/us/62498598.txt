Ballot drop boxes blow up <p> The Register 's third most-read story online this year broke the news about unofficial ballot drop boxes that started popping up in Orange , Los Angeles and Fresno counties in early October . <p> Any mystery about the origins of the boxes was short-lived . The California Republican Party soon took credit for placing dozens of boxes at businesses and campaign headquarters throughout the state . The GOP insisted the boxes were legal under California 's permissive ballot collection laws , though it agreed that initially labeling some boxes as " official " was a mistake .
