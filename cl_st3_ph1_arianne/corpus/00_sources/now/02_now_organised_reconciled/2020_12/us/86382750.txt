<h> The 2020 election is over , but our problems are not . Where do we go from here ? <h> Opinion : Where do we go after such a divisive presidential elections ? For one , we must make a commitment to the truth . <p> The eyes of the world have been on Arizona this election . Voters in this swing state elected a Democrat for president for the first time since 1996 and are sending a second Democratic senator to Washington . <p> During the counting , armed protesters at a Phoenix ballot tabulation center stoked unfounded suspicions about the election 's legitimacy even as state officials assured voters that ballots marked with Sharpie pens would be counted . <p> Record turnouts affirm that democracy works , with some Arizona counties seeing more than 80% participation rates . Yet razor-thin margins , and pockets of denial about the results , underscore how deeply divided we remain . Our president-elect has called for healing , but we know that uniting our state and country wo n't be easy . <p> So , where do we go from here @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We can begin by seeking out and listening to people who voted differently than we did . We can break out of the curated news feeds and social media bubbles that reinforce our views . <p> We can resist treating politics like a religion -- and quit separating out the righteous from the heretics . We can greet one another as fellow citizens who want the best for America and Arizona . Most importantly , we can affirm the truths we hold in common . <p> As co-directors of the Recovering Truth Project at Arizona State University , we lead a team of faculty and journalists studying how a cavalier disregard for truth has consumed our politics and culture , inflaming divisions that threaten our elections and our democracy . <p> Moving forward will require us to recover a commitment to truth : to disavow conspiracy theories and disinformation , to acknowledge scientific and historical evidence , and to respect experience and expertise . <p> We have much work to do . <p> Ignoring and sidelining public health guidance has contributed to more than 265,000 COVID-19 cases and more than @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ friends , and co-workers .
