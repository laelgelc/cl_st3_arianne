As a former U.S. governor , my goal frankly was to put WFP out of business . Not that I was hostile at all to its worthy mission , but I wanted to create an efficient and sustainable food security system , rather than an endless cycle of dependency on outside help . <p> I never imagined what I would find when I started traveling to the over 80 nations where we provide food .
