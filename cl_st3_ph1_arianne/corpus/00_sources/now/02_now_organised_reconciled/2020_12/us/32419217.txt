<h> Guest View : Control births , save the Earth <p> David Berg and Judith K. Berg <p> Guest View <p> " Driven by humanity 's ever-expanding footprint , habitat loss is the single biggest cause of species extinction . " So opens the feature , " Critical Habitat , " by Noah Greenwald in the Center for Biological Diversity 's fall newsletter , " Endangered Earth . " Its message is echoed in the feature " All Creatures , Great and Small " in the fall issue of Defenders of Wildlife magazine and repeated ad-infinitum throughout the annals of contemporary conservation literature . <p> Biological diversity is the single most essential component of species survival and sustainability of life on Earth -- including humans . Yet , human intervention is the single most critical cause of biodiversity loss . <p> Remove the keystone species from an ecosystem and the system collapses . Yellowstone is still recovering following the return of wolves in 1995 . Hopefully , we will yet see the reintroduction of sea otters along the Oregon coast within our lifetimes and , with them , the return @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Which gets us back to the subject of this essay : habitat loss due to the expanding human footprint . We recently attended a series of virtual lectures on a variety of critically endangered species throughout the world -- elephants , lions , leopards , rhinos , bears , wolves , okapi , pangolins and others . The message was always the same regarding the principal contributors to their endangerment are : human encroachment on wildlife habitat and migration corridors , human consumption of , and the consequent pollution of , natural resources and trafficking in wildlife and wildlife parts , the latter for wealth and greed or unfounded medicinal purposes , and which incidentally carry the risk of transmitting zoonotic pathogens , such as COVID-19 . <p> So , herein lies the problem . It is the wont of the human condition to have and want more . Sustaining economic growth , sadly , is the paradigm that drives human existence . Economic growth is achieved by increasing demand for products within a population , or creating new demand by increasing the population . <p> Everything we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is grown -- or raised on products that are grown -- in nutrient-based soils . Every other product is manufactured or fabricated from one natural resource or another . All that consumption , in turn , creates waste that pollutes the soils , the air , the waters -- the very resources on which all life , including ours , depends . <p> It is not hard to see that with increased consumption -- and , consequently , increased pollution , there will be insufficient resources to sustain the growth demanded by a consumptive audience . <p> Add to that the anthropogenic contribution to climate change and we are fast running out of Earth to sustain our growing population . <p> So , what do we do ? E.O . Wilson suggests setting aside half the earth for biodiversity . Is that enough -- and can it happen soon enough ? Whatever we do , we have to shrink the human footprint , and that may mean finding a new paradigm to satisfy our wont . We are creating our own trophic cascade and we must restore life 's balance @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the principal factor in harm 's way for biodiversity , rarely does anyone suggest what to do about it . <p> Well , we will . <p> Earth 's human population must be reduced if life on Earth as we know it is to continue . No , we do n't propose death squads . However , we do propose curtailing the global birth rate -- especially in areas where it is out of control , such as south Asia and Africa . In the U.S. , at least , we can impose tax disincentives -- such as limiting the Child Tax Credit to two children . Equivalent measures need to be taken globally . <p> In " The Land Ethic , " Aldo Leopold encourages enlarging the boundaries of ethics to " include soils , waters , plants and animals , or , collectively : the land . " No longer can we allow economic growth to define the paradigm of success for human life on Earth .
