The New Jersey Board of Public Utilities recently approved a program from PSE&amp;G that will help residential and business customers cut their power use , avoid 8 million metric tons of carbon emissions , and realize a net savings of $1 billion on their energy bills . <p> One of the most potent tools we have to battle climate change is energy efficiency , which helps to lower carbon emissions by taking steps to reduce the amount of energy we consume . Energy efficiency may not capture the public 's imagination like solar or wind , but when it comes to fighting climate change , it 's every bit as effective as renewables -- while also lowering customers ' bills . <p> New Jersey has taken a historic step forward in achieving its climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efficiency . The New Jersey Board of Public Utilities recently approved a program from PSE&amp;G -- the state 's largest electric and gas utility -- that will help residential and business customers cut their power use , avoid 8 million metric tons of carbon emissions , and realize a net savings of $1 billion on their energy bills . <p> PSE&amp;G already was an energy sector leader in energy efficiency , spending approximately $500 million since 2009 on programs that have won 14 awards . Now we 're preparing to double that amount of investment -- and the result is a win for our customers , the environment and New Jersey 's economy . <p> Under Gov . Phil Murphy , New Jersey has been a national leader in clean energy and climate policy . With continuing innovation and investment , the state now has an opportunity to be a leader in energy efficiency , as well . <p> New Jersey 's new energy efficiency programs will help customers reduce their electricity usage by at least 2% and gas usage by 0.75% by the fifth year , bringing us closer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ state 's Energy Master Plan . <p> Energy efficiency is n't a single tool , but rather covers an array of changes that every customer can make today that bear immediate results -- whether they 're a residential , commercial or large industrial customer , whether they 're wealthy , middle-class or low-income , whether they live in a house or a multi-family building . The savings will vary based on a customer 's level of participation . <p> PSE&amp;G 's programs will offer customers rebates and other financial incentives to make energy-saving purchases such as energy-efficient lighting , HVAC equipment and smart thermostats . Customers also will be eligible for free or affordable energy audits and energy efficiency kits . Our program was designed with special emphasis on meeting the needs of low-income , multi-family and small business customers . <p> In addition , our program comes just in time to help New Jersey recover from the economic impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic , by creating an estimated 4,300 direct and indirect jobs . Nationwide , energy efficiency investments since 1990 have helped the U.S. avoid building the equivalent of 313 coal-burning power plants . <p> Unfortunately , there 's a misconception that using less energy means sacrifice and discomfort . The truth is , energy efficiency 's climate benefits match those from renewables -- reducing the amount of carbon released when generating electricity -- without affecting your lifestyle . While subsidies for solar and wind add millions of dollars per year to customers ' costs , energy efficiency provides the same environmental benefits while helping customers spend less . <p> And there is enormous potential to do more . With future investments in energy efficiency and technology -- such as smart electric meters that would create a two-way communication connection between customers and their energy providers -- utilities will be able to help customers achieve even greater energy efficiency savings while improving reliability .
