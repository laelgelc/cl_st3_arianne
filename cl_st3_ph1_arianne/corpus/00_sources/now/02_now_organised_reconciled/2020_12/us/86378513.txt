The XPrize says entrants will need to create consistent cuts of alternative meats that look , taste , smell , feel and cook like a regular fish fillet or chicken breast . They 'll also need to show their approach is more sustainable and that they can scale it to meet global demand . <p> Part of the inspiration for the competition came out of XPrize 's recently released Future of Food Impact Roadmap .
