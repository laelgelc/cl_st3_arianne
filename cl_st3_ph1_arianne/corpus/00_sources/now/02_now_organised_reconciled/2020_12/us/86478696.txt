They 're Americans who know that we 're in this together . They 're Americans who wish for our country to lead the world to solutions . They 're patriots who want the new administration to succeed . <p> Climate change is upon us ; we 're seeing the whites of its eyes now . We 've got to take a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shot . This is a moment to bring us together . <h> Sweeping legislation is needed <p> With a Supreme Court that may be skeptical of regulatory action on climate , we need legislation -- sweeping legislation at the scale of the problem and legislation that 's bipartisan and therefore durable . Having waited too long to act , the policy that we devise must be stable . It must n't be done and undone , enacted and repealed .
