<h> Daily Spotlight : Debt Lofty Level of GDP <p> Market Outlook <p> Bullish - Short term <p> Summary <p> Everyone is justifiably talking about the latest wave of the coronavirus . As such , little attention is being paid to U.S. debt levels that have quietly and quickly grown over the past 10 years . Total U.S. debt is now more than 120% of GDP -- the highest level since World War II . In the 1980s , the debt/GDP ratio was solidly in the 40% range , and moved up toward the 60% level by 2000 . The debt level soared around 2010 , as the government spent aggressively to brake the recession and rekindle growth ( which is what the government is supposed to do ) . However , despite more than 10 years of economic growth prior to the pandemic , the debt level has only grown as a percentage of GDP . Now , in light of the fiscal spending in 2020 , debt levels have surged further . This is not a problem that has to be fixed today .
