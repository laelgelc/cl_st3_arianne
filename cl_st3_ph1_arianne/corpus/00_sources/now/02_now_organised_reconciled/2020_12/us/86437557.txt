( sTo Len ) <p> By Mark Jenkins <p> Dec. 18 , 2020 at 12:00 p.m . UTC <p> There will be more plastic than fish in the world 's oceans by 2050 , the World Economic Forum estimated in 2016 . So it 's apt that sTo Len has updated gyotaku -- the traditional Japanese practice of inking a freshly caught fish and imprinting it on paper -- into gomitaku -- almost the same thing , but employing a piece of trash rather than a fish . It 's also fitting that Len did so while serving as artist-in-residence at AlexRenew , the deodorized name for what used to be called the Alexandria Sanitation Authority . " <p> The water itself , pungent with " FOG " ( fats , oils , grease ) and " chocolate milk " ( mostly biosolids ) , is shown in close-up in a series of photographs .
