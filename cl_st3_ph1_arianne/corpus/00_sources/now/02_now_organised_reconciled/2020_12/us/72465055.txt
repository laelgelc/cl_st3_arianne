<h> Letter : A carbon-free economy <p> Alaska needs to address both climate change and new revenue sources . Our state leaders need to work with our congressional delegation to promote legislation that would allow Alaska to pursue carbon-free industries , such as gaming . We should promote selling the Egan Center to the Native Village of Eklutna and make the property a reservation . Eklutna could then open a casino , which would be a huge boost to tourism , downtown Anchorage and Alyeska ski resort . <p> -- Frank Rast <p> Anchorage <p> Have something on your mind ?
