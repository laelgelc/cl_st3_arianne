<p> The Federal Reserve said Tuesday it has formally joined the Network for Greening the Financial System , an international group of central banks and financial regulators developing rules to address climate risks . <p> Why it matters : The Fed is the first federal agency to join the group , which also includes the European Central Bank , the Bank of England and the Bank of Japan . The New York Department of Financial Services until now was the only formal member from the U.S. <p> What 's next : The move signals an escalation in the Fed 's work to address potential climate risks lurking in the financial system . The Fed began participating in network discussions and activities more than a year ago but in recent weeks officials at the central bank have taken a more assertive approach on addressing climate change . President-elect Joe Biden 's appointees to the Treasury Department and financial regulatory agencies are expected to make the issue a high priority . <p> " As we develop our understanding of how best to assess the impact of climate change on the financial system @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with our NGFS colleagues from around the world , " Fed Chair Jerome Powell said in a statement . <p> Last week , a group of House Republicans warned the Fed about participating in the NGFS and said the work should not disadvantage U.S. banks or their customers .
