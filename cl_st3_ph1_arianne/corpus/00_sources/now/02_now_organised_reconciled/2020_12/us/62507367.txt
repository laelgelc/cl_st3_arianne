<p> This has been the worst administration when it comes to the environment . In December alone , Trump has ignored the Supreme Court when it comes to the Clean Water Act , blocked attempts to strengthen air pollution regulations despite scientific links to the coronavirus pandemic , continued to allow a dangerous pesticide that especially impacts children , pushed drilling in the Arctic , and rolled back the Migratory Bird Treaty Act . <p> We need to keep fighting . Once President Biden is in office , he must use his executive authority to freeze Trump 's rules and rollbacks . He can sign executive orders to repeal Trump 's anti-environmental executive orders and use them to start the process of eliminating Trump 's rollbacks .
