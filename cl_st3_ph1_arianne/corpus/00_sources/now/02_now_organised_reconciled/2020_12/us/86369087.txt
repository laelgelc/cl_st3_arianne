<p> Portland officials on Thursday unveiled a new proposal that seeks to tax major greenhouse gas producers and air polluters in the city to help combat climate change . <p> Large manufacturers , hospitals and colleges and universities would pay most of the tax championed by the Bureau of Planning and Sustainability , documents show . City officials estimate it would raise more than $10 million a year . <p> The proposal , which the City Council wo n't take up until next year , comes as Portland seeks to achieve ambitious climate goals over the next several years . <p> The tax would be the first of its kind anywhere in the nation . It 's sure to face strong opposition from powerful business groups and industries that employee thousands of people . <p> " We are in a climate crisis , " said Andrea Durbin , director of the city 's planning bureau . " We need to act like it .
