<h> The CPG manufacturer plans to do 2,021 positive deeds in 2021 <p> Earlier this month , Hormel Foods , the maker of Spam and Skippy peanut butter , announced plans to donate $20,000 to Make-A-Wish Minnesota and another $20,000 to Feeding America . Kraft Heinz 's coffee brand Maxwell House recently pledged $200,000 to building homes for veterans . Kellogg 's has helped provide 13 million meals to people in need throughout 2020 . Many more examples of corporate philanthropy on the part of CPG conglomerates exist . <p> Procter &amp; Gamble , the maker of multiple prominent household brands , is no stranger to using its might to make the world better -- whether that 's through fighting racism , confronting bias against LGBTQ people or promoting young women of color pursuing a career in filmmaking .
