<h> Denmark to phase out oil and gas in landmark decision <p> The EU 's biggest oil producer has decided to call it quits . The Danish parliament voted this month to stop issuing new leases and end oil production by 2050 , making Denmark the first major-oil producing country in the world to decide to phase out production .
