Tom Vilsack as USDA secretary , a job he held under Barack Obama <p> President-elect Joe Biden tapped Tom Vilsack on Thursday for a second stint as U.S. Agriculture secretary , saying the former Iowa governor has the experience and innovative mindset to address issues such as hunger and the economic stress farmers and other rural Americans face . <p> " With an estimated one in six Americans and a quarter of U.S. children facing a hunger crisis , farmers reeling , and rural communities struggling to weather the pain and economic fallout of the pandemic , Vilsack will bring the experience and bold thinking needed to deliver immediate relief to farmers , ranchers , producers and families all across the country , " Biden said in a news release announcing the nomination . <p> Vilsack , who turns 70 on Sunday , served eight years as Agriculture secretary under President Barack Obama and has been a longtime supporter of Biden .
