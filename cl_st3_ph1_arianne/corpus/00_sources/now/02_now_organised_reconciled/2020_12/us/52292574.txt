<h> Fate of the monarch butterfly still hangs in the balance <p> The fate of the monarch butterfly still hangs in the balance after the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service made a decision Tuesday on adding it to the list of threatened species under the Endangered Species Act . <p> The inclusion of the butterfly on the endangered species list is " warranted but precluded , " according to a press release from the agency . <p> Under the decision , the monarch butterfly becomes a candidate for listing as an endangered species , but it will not yet be listed as the agency is prioritizing other candidates , U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service Director Aurelia Skipwith said in a statement . The butterfly 's status on the list will be reviewed each year . <p> " We conducted an intensive , thorough review using a rigorous , transparent science-based process and found that the monarch meets listing criteria under the Endangered Species Act , " Skipwith said .
