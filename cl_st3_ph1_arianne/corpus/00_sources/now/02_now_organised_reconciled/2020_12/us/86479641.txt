<p> Children get sick less often than adults due to Covid-19 , but children are at risk . This is the key insight from a paper in the Journal of American Medical Association ( JAMA ) , a peer-reviewed medical journal , as the year draws to an end . The paper summarizes the current understanding of how children are affected by Covid-19 . <p> So far those under the age of 18 represent about 8.5% of reported cases , with relatively few deaths compared to other age groups , according to the World Health Organization ( WHO ) . The disease in children is usually mild . <p> While risk of death in children is far lower than in adults , the article sums up that some children have died . <p> Children can get Covid-19 from their close contacts such as friends , teachers and from sports activities . Their risk of getting sick enough to be hospitalized increases with age , although new-borns and young infants are also at increased risk . This appears to be the body 's strong response to being infected with the virus . Some of these infected children have had no symptom of Covid-19 at all .
