<p> Pope Francis is a divisive figure in the Catholic community . While some progressives embrace his relatively forward-thinking views on homosexuality , immigration , and climate change , conservative critics claim his openness is corrupting the faith . This year , the Vatican has taken an important step toward unity by presenting a Nativity that almost everyone can agree is deeply strange . <p> Unveiled last week , this year 's Nativity scene features 19 ceramic figures created in the 1960s and 70s.
