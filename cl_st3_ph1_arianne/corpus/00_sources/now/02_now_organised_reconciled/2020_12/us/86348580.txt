<h> Phoenix again breaks record for latest 90-plus degree heat in a year <p> When will it end ? We 're officially eight weeks into fall , and Phoenix is still having a hard time forgetting its summer . <p> Tuesday again set the record for the latest day in the year to reach 90 degrees . <p> At around 1:40 p.m. , the mercury at Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport hit 90 , easily eclipsing the previous high for this date of 87 degrees set in 2008 . The normal high for this time is 75 degrees . Seconds after the Weather Service tweeted the new record , it sent a new tweet saying it had hit 91 . <p> " Still a chance it could climb another degree or two , " the National Weather Service in Phoenix said in a tweet . <p> Wednesday may break the record as well , said weather service meteorologist Marvin Percha , as high pressure persists over the desert southwest and exacerbates already warm , dry conditions . <p> Fortunately , this year will likely leave at least one heat record @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ record for most 90-plus days ever in a single year , which is currently set at 196 days . The second-highest number is 192 days , set in 1934 . Tuesday 's high marked 191 days in 2020 . <p> Relief is in the forecast for later this week , Percha said . After Wednesday , temperatures will hopefully begin to cool off and hit the 70s by the weekend -- normal temperatures for this time in the fall . There is still no rain in sight , though . <p> The last day most of the Valley had measurable rainfall was Nov. 9 . According to measurements from the weather service , 2020 has seen a little more than 4 inches of rain , just two-thirds of the normal 6 inches Phoenix typically sees by this time of the year . <p> Human-driven climate change has been pushing up average temperatures throughout the year and bringing warmer days in the fall on average .
