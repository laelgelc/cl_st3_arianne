<p> HYDERABAD , India , Dec. 22 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- CtrlS , Asia 's Largest Rated-4 Hyperscale Datacenter provider , was awarded the prestigious Golden Peacock Award for Eco-Innovation . The company became the World 's first datacenter to be awarded in the datacenter category . In the past , CtrlS has bagged several awards in Datacenter energy efficiency and Innovation from Confederation of Indian Industries ( CII ) . <p> The jury selected CtrlS Datacenters , after having evaluated 234 companies on parameters such as innovation , environment , health and safety , energy conservation , and impact on climate Change . The winners in other than data center category include Tata Motors ( ERC ) , ITC , JSW Cement , and ITC Life Sciences &amp; Technology Centre . The past winners include Tata Steel , Mahindra &amp; Mahindra , and Reliance Industries . <p> Instituted in 1991 by the Institute of Directors ( IOD ) , India , the Golden Peacock Awards is globally regarded as a Corporate Excellence benchmark . It follows a transparent five step evaluation process of evaluation by expert evaluators , site @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ winners , and finally award the winners . <p> CtrlS focused on rightsizing of equipment and operations combined with 80 innovations in energy efficiency resulting in industry lowest PUE of 1.36 . Besides energy efficiency , the company also focused on toxicity and carbon footprint reduction by exercising control on usage of hazardous substances , adoption of eco-friendly chemicals , and optimum consumption of water thus contributing to United Nations SDG goals . <p> Celebrating this achievement , Mr. Sridhar Pinnapureddy , Founder &amp; CEO of CtrlS said , " We are delighted and proud to receive the prestigious Golden Peacock Eco-Innovation Award . This recognition is a testament to our continued commitment to excellence in energy efficiency , sustainability and addressing the critical environmental challenges .
