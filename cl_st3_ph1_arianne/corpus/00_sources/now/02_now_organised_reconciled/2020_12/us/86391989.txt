Otherwise , you 're paying for a lie -- or at least getting a smaller box of tea than you ordered . <p> Finding out whether you 've been lied to is genuinely difficult . Carbon regularly changes form and area code , cycling among the atmosphere , oceans , ecosystems , and even bedrock . Plants take carbon atoms from CO2 in the air to grow . Herbivores consume plants and exhale the carbon as CO2 again . Weathering rocks pull CO2 out of the air . Volcanoes release it into the atmosphere .
