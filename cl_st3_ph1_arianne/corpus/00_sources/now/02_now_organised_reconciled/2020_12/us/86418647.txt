<p> Switching to plant-based alternatives can have a huge impact on reducing carbon emissions . <p> The retail value for dairy and meat alternatives is growing in the EU and the UK as consumers want more environmentally friendly products and companies are investing in the space . <p> This article is part of Changemakers ' Playbook , a series that looks at innovation across different industries . <p> Animal husbandry to produce meat and dairy is a highly resource-intensive activity that profoundly affects land use and quality . The global meat and dairy industry contribute to 60% of agriculture 's greenhouse gas emissions , and in 2018 we consumed 385 million tons of meat worldwide , accounting for at least 1.7 megatons of CO2 emissions , 380 million hectares of land , and consumption of 87 billion square meters of water . That is a huge environmental impact -- from just meat and dairy . <p> One way to reduce that impact is by switching to plant-based alternatives such as almond milk , tofu , and veggie burgers . " And it is a radical transformation of the industry . " It 's essentially doing away with the need for animal raising , " he says . " This is a huge game changer once the technology is perfected . " <p> This will be a long-term process , however .
