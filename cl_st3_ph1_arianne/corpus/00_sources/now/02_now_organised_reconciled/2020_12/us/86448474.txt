<p> Amid Covid-19 , 2020 has seen gender justice funders step up to support women and girls , through flexible grant-making , emergency funding and a collaborative approach . While there is more attention of funding efforts to support women and girls , they still make up a very small percentage of philanthropic giving . When it comes to women and girls of color , they receive an even lower percentage of philanthropic giving .
