<h> New EU ' green-washing ' rules will force automakers to be transparent <p> Up until now , the pressure on companies to do good has largely come from a sense of ethical or moral obligation . But a new green initiative from the EU is raising the stakes by tying environmental , human rights and governance standards to access to capital and risk management . <p> The initiative , called the Sustainable Finance Framework , is forcing companies to be transparent about how well they are complying to those standards . It applies to big institutional investors ( funds and asset managers , for example ) and the companies ( automakers , for example ) that they invest in . <p> By forcing transparency , the rules also seek to cut down on " green washing , " the practice of claiming that a product is environmentally friendly , often on scant evidence , to burnish your corporate image .
