<h> working from home <p> VMware said it will pursue a hybrid work approach for its 33,000 employees as a way to advance its environmental and diversity goals . <p> The company , as part of its Future of Work initiative , said its employees will have the choice to work in the office , remotely and with a combination of the two . VMware said that the Future of Work effort is part of its 2030 Agenda . <p> While other companies have announced plans to enable more remote work , VMware has tied climate change and diversity and inclusion to a hybrid work model . Companies have evolved on remote work by initially focusing on lower commercial real estate costs and a more diversified labor pool and then moving to other benefits . <p> For VMware , enabling a remote work force also serves as a strong case study since its software enables business continuity as well as the employee model it is pursuing .
