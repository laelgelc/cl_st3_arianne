<h> SPONSORED : <h> Poll : Two-thirds of Americans want US to join international climate pact <p> Two-thirds of Americans want the United States to join an international agreement to fight climate change , a new poll found . <p> The survey , from the New York Times and CBS , could provide a tailwind to the United Nations talks that launched Monday in Paris , where world leaders plan on finalizing such a global pact . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> A majority of Republicans in the poll do not want the U.S. to join an international agreement . <p> But overall , 63 percent of Americans said they support domestic policies to reduce greenhouse gas emissions , including most Republicans . <p> The poll and others like it have found support among people in the U.S. for policies to cut the amount of carbon dioxide and similar gasses being released into the atmosphere , and Monday 's findings show that that support extends even to international policy . <p> It stands in contrast to congressional Republicans , who are nearly united in their attempts to stop President Obama 's climate policies .
