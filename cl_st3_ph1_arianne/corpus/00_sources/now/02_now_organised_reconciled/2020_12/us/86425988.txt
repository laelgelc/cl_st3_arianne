<h> Britain 's top court gives go-ahead to Heathrow expansion <p> By Reuters Staff <p> 1 Min Read <p> LONDON , Dec 16 ( Reuters ) - Britain 's top court gave the go-ahead to the expansion of Heathrow Airport on Wednesday , overturning a previous court decision which had blocked the 14 billion pound ( $18.90 billion ) plan on environmental grounds . <p> The Supreme Court ruled in Heathrow 's favour , allowing the UK 's biggest airport to proceed with a plan to build a third runway . Heathrow is keen to proceed despite the plunge in air traffic during the pandemic . <p> In February , a court had declared the expansion unlawful on environmental grounds , with the judge saying that a failure to take into account the UK government 's commitments on climate change was " legally fatal " to the plans .
