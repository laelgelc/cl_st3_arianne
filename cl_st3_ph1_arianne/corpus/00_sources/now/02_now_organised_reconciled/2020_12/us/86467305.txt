<p> Cambridge , Massachusetts , will require area gas pumps to post stickers warning of the harms of climate change , becoming the first US city to implement such a mandate , The Guardian reported . <p> The Guardian reported Friday Cambridge will require all gas pumps in the city to display bright , yellow stickers that say " burning gasoline , diesel , and ethanol has major consequences on human health and the environment including contributing to climate change . " <p> The stickers are intended to " remind drivers to think about climate change and hopefully consider non-polluting options , " a city spokesperson told the outlet . <p> In 2014 , California 's Berkeley and San Francisco proposed a similar idea to put stickers on gas pumps , but the idea was n't ultimately put into action . <p> Last year , the US greenhouse gas emissions rate dropped by 2.1% , according to the Rhodium Group , a private economic data firm . Even so , the emissions rate did not see net reductions between 2016 and 2019 , according to Rhodium.
