<h> Using the courts to address climate change <p> Royal Dutch Shell is being sued to make it commit to reining in its carbon emissions 45% by the year 2030 . In another case , six Portuguese teenagers filed the first-ever climate-related case at the European Court of Human Rights , charging that 33 European countries have not done enough to prevent the impacts of climate change from violating their citizens ' human rights . Marco Werman speaks with Joana Setzer , an expert on climate governance who has authored reports on climate litigation around the globe .
