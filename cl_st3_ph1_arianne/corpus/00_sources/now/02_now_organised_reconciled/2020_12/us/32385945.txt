<p> Addressing COVID-19 , which has infected over 1 million Texans and killed over 21,000 , is the most pressing issue facing the state as the 2021 legislative session approaches , a survey by the Hobby School of Public Affairs at the University of Houston has found . <p> But the survey found a divide along racial and partisan lines , with nearly twice as many likely African American voters ( 56.8% ) listing COVID-19 as their top concern compared to 28.9% of likely white voters . While it was n't a majority , 45.8% of likely Latino voters said COVID-19 was their highest concern . <p> Those numbers resemble partisan lines -- 58.9% of Democrats said the pandemic is their highest priority . That number dropped to 26.7% among Republicans and 27.6% for Independents . <p> In a news release , Mark Jones , a research associate at the Hobby School , said the survey shows that Texan voters view the most pressing issues as the topics that most affect their day-to-day lives . <p> " The national conversation has focused a great deal of attention on racial equity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Jones said in the release .
