<p> In 2019 , deaths and disability caused due to air pollution cost India a GDP loss of 1.36% valued at $36.8 billion . This is the key takeaway from a recent study published in the peer-review scientific journal The Lancet . <p> The study estimated the economic impact of air pollution as it led to loss of output due to premature deaths and morbidity . Researchers looked at both indoor and outdoor air pollution across states and measured its impact . <p> " The basis for linking air pollution to health is grounded in the large pool of epidemiological studies that now straddle all regions of the world , " said Kalpana Balakrishnan of Sri Ramachandra Institute of Higher Education and Research , and one of the study co-authors . " The large burden results from the ubiquitous exposures in India , the high prevalence of conditions impacted by air pollution and the population density .
