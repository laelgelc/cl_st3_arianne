<h> Biden victory , vaccine and an anniversary : good karma for the Mediterranean ? <p> Twenty-five years ago , the Barcelona Process established the Euro-Mediterranean Partnership between 15 European Union member states and 12 Southern and Eastern Mediterranean countries to bring peace , stability and prosperity to the region . However , 25 years later , progress in stability , peace and shared economic progress has not been achieved . <p> Since its initiation , several events have affected ( and continue to affect ) this process and its objectives . The 9/11 terror attacks in the U.S. ( 2001 ) , the Iraq War ( 2003 ) and Al-Qaeda terror attacks in Istanbul ( 2003 ) , Madrid ( 2004 ) , and Amman ( 2005 ) led to change in security concerns in the Mediterranean . The 2008 global economic crisis and the subsequent austerity measures led to 2011-2012 anti-austerity protests in Greece , Spain , Portugal and Italy along with rising economic inequalities between and within the states of the Mediterranean .
