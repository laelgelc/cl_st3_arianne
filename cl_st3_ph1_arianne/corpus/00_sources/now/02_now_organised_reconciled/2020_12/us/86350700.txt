<h> Australia endures hottest spring ever , with average temperatures more than 2C above average <p> Australia has sweltered through its hottest spring and November on record , with both the season and the month more than 2C warmer than the long-term average . <p> Temperatures in spring were 2.03C hotter than average across night and day . In November , they were 2.47C above the long-term mean , which is measured across the years 1961-90 . <p> Maximum heat in November went even further beyond what Australians are used to , on average reaching 2.9C above the historic mark . The previous record average was 2.4C above the long-term mark , set in November 2014 . <p> Large areas in the east and south-east experienced maximums more than 10C above average on a number of days late in the month , with many weather stations in New South Wales breaking their record for the hottest day and night on 28 or 29 November . <p> The rise in heatwaves is consistent with a recent state of the climate report by the bureau and CSIRO , which said Australia had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to increasing atmospheric concentrations of carbon dioxide . <p> Dr Andrew Watkins , the bureau 's head of climate operations , said the record-breaking temperatures were particularly remarkable as they had been recorded during a La Ni ? a , a climate event in the Pacific Ocean that typically brings cooler and wetter weather . <p> " As a climatologist it is really not what you would expect during a La Ni ? a , " he said . <p> Watkins said the heat spike in November could partly be explained by the La Ni ? a temporarily weakening and what is known as southern annual mode returning to neutral .
