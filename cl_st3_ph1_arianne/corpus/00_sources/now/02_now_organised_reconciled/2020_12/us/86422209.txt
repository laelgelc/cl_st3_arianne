<h> Heathrow Airport wins court backing for third runway <p> LONDON ( AP ) -- Plans for a third runway at London 's Heathrow Airport have been given a new boost after the airport 's owner won a Supreme Court challenge Wednesday . <p> The Appeal Court had blocked the project earlier , ruling that the government failed to take account of its climate commitments when it approved the expansion plans . <p> Heathrow Airport Ltd. , which owns and operates the airport , challenged that ruling . The Supreme Court overturned it Wednesday and ruled that the runway plans were lawful . <p> The latest ruling clears a hurdle for Heathrow , but there are still more in the way .
