<h> ATLAS research project discovers new species in lowest depths of the ocean <p> In 2019 , the Canadian Coast Guard Icebreaker Amundsen travelled through some of the iciest waters of the north Atlantic Ocean just off Greenland . <p> On board were scientists from one of the largest oceanic research projects in the world . <p> It is called the ATLAS project and it has just handed down the findings of its five-year study of the Atlantic Ocean . <p> After sending special robotic landers to the lowest depths of the ocean , it brought back a trove of scientific riches . <p> Among them was the discovery of 12 new deep-sea species , including a coral growth called Epizoanthus martinsae . <p> The species lives on black corals up to 400 metres below the ocean surface . <p> A moss animal , or bryozoan , named Microporella funbio was also discovered .
