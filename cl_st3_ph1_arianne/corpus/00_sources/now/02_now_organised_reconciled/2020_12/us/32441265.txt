<p> Tourism , energy production and water supplies are all tightly connected to snow availability . In a context of climate change , keeping an eye on snowfall is ever more important . <p> At the end of December 6 , 2020 , many slopes in Italy 's Dolomites and in southern Austria 's Alps sat under 3 metres of snow . A snowstorm had swept Southern Europe , hitting some of its high-altitude areas with near-record levels of 900 mm of snow in 24 hours . It blocked traffic , and boosted the risk of avalanches , while teasing skiers who could n't access some of Europe 's best slopes as resorts stayed shut down under COVID-19 . <p> Skiing or not , whether we get snow or not still matters plenty ; especially for natural ecosystems , for our water supplies , and for the various industries relying on it .
