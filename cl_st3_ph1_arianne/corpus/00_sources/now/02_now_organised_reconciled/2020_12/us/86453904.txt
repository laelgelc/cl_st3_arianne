<p> While the coronavirus sickened more than 78 million people this year , another rolling disaster was unleashing new terrors in the background . Its effects seeped into thousands of lungs and turned skies red . Dams burst and forests burned . Homes toppled and flood after flood of ocean water inundated coastal towns . <p> The climate crisis surged to new heights in 2020 . In the US , no region was spared . <p> A polar vortex brought record-breaking temperature lows to the Northeast , and heat waves brought new highs to the West . That heat dried out the landscape , which later fed an unprecedented wave of wildfires in late summer . <p> On the other side of the US , a stunning hurricane season battered the Gulf Coast and the Southeast with more powerful cyclones than any previous year on record .
