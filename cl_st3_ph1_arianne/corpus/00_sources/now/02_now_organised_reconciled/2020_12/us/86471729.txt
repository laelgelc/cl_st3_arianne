<h> 3 Stocks You Can Keep Forever <p> The comedian Steven Wright reportedly once quipped : " I intend to live forever . So far , so good . " That 's kind of how it is for me and a handful of my stock holdings . I intend to hold them forever , or at least for a very long time , and ... so far , so good . Here are my caveats : I will probably sell some or all of them when I 'm in retirement and need extra income , and I may sell them far sooner , if any of them suddenly seem less promising and less likely to keep growing over the long run . <p> That said , a bunch of my holdings are ones I envision hanging onto for at least 10 or 20 more years . Here 's a look at three of them : Apple ( NASDAQ : AAPL ) , Amazon.com ( NASDAQ : AMZN ) , and American Express ( NYSE : AXP ) .
