Widespread coronavirus vaccinations are not only the best way to keep people from dying , they will also help revive business and the economy . <p> Advertisement <p> There is a way to get greater compliance : Businesses , which have spent the past several years championing their social responsibility , can require vaccination of employees and , in many cases , customers . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ expert , told me that until 75% of people are vaccinated , we should all continue to wear masks and maintain social distancing , even if we 've been vaccinated . Mandating vaccines would be especially important to help protect workers in minority and lower socioeconomic communities that have been hardest hit in the pandemic .
