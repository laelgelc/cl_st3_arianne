<p> NEW YORK , Dec 10 ( Reuters ) - Time Magazine reveals its Person of the Year for 2020 on Thursday , choosing the winner from nominees that include healthcare workers battling the COVID-19 pandemic , the racial justice movement and two U.S. presidential election winners . <p> The news magazine will reveal at 10 p.m . Eastern its selection for the title that signifies " who affected the news or our lives the most , for better or worse , " Time said on its website . <p> Teenage climate activist Greta Thunberg last year became the youngest individual winner of the accolade . The 16-year-old Swede inspired young people to take action in 2019 against climate change , grabbing headlines when she regularly skipped school to demonstrate outside Swedish parliament in a push for her government to curb carbon emissions . <p> The Person of the Year is usually an individual , but multiple people who greatly affected the nation and the world have been named . Time started the honor in 1927 . <p> This year 's four finalists include Joe Biden , who is due to be inaugurated as the 46th U.S. president on Jan. 20 , after winning a " historic and unprecedented election , " Time said . " He broke former President Barack Obama 's record for the most votes ever cast for a presidential candidate . " <p> U.S. President Donald Trump is also in the running . " Since losing the election , he 's baselessly claimed that there was fraud and falsely said that he 's the winner , " Time said . " His Administration has been highly criticized for its response to the COVID-19 pandemic . " <p> Battling the deadly pandemic are frontline healthcare workers and infectious disease expert Dr. Anthony Fauci . Together they are the third finalist under consideration . <p> While the virus that has claimed 1.57 million lives globally has put the world on hold , healthcare workers soldiered on .
