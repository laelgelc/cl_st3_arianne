Some of the most celebrated names in the industry will be releasing new work , from Jhumpa Lahiri and Kazuo Ishiguro to Haruki Murakami and Viet Thanh Nguyen . The new year also features a slate of new and rising voices like Morgan Jerkins , Ashley C. Ford and Zakiya Dalila Harris .
