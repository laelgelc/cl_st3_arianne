<p> Each year , wildfires seem to ravage the West Coast with more intensity . According to ClimateCheck , a platform that provides property climate change risk to users , wildfires will only get worse in coming years , with 9.3 million acres burning nationally each year through 2050 . <p> Still , according to ClimateCheck 's analysis , people keep migrating in large numbers to areas most at risk for wildfires -- in fact , six of the areas most at risk are among the fastest-growing counties in the country . <p> Placer County , which is northwest of Sacramento , California , has the highest wildfire risk in the country , tied with Chelan County , Washington . The typical property in the county ranks at a wildfire risk of 98 out of 100 with a projected 64,000 acres to burn there per year by 2050 .
