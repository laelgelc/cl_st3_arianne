UTC <p> The Nov. 29 editorial " The climate change president " missed one vital opportunity for President-elect Joe Biden to launch bipartisan climate action : tapping America 's forests to deliver natural climate solutions . Throughout the 116th Congress , even during the tense election season , unexpected bipartisan coalitions have come together to build on the 15 percent of U.S. carbon dioxide emissions already captured in our forests annually . This includes legislation to more rapidly replant national forests after wildfires and other disasters , expanding incentives for private forest owners to sequester more carbon dioxide and planting trees to naturally cool our cities -- saving lives , energy and greenhouse gas emissions in one fell swoop . <p> Forest-climate solutions are popular because they bring many ecological co-benefits , such as protection for our drinking water , and generate as many as 40 green jobs per $1 million invested @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ consensus out of wood .
