The figures point the way to the central scene , a simple stable framing a tableau of the holy family . <p> In Italy , the nativity scene or presepe is an integral part of Christmas decorations . Venice 's floating nativity located in the waters near Burano has been a particularly spectacular example . It consists of 60 plywood figures dispersed across a stretch of water that laps at their feet for several hours a day . The scene was created by local greengrocer Francesco Orazio and has been praised for its " magical effect " , although it has also prompted a few quips about climate change . <p> Cinque Terre , on the Ligurian Riviera , is famed instead for the largest nativity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ depicting the holy family , shepherds , angels and animals adorn the hillside of Manarola .
