According to a survey by The Washington Post , only 27 Republican members of Congress are willing to say that Joe Biden won . Despite the complete lack of evidence of significant fraud , two-thirds of self-identified Republicans said in a Reuters/Ipsos poll that the election was rigged . <p> But you really should n't be surprised by this willingness to indulge malicious , democracy-endangering lies . After all , when was the last time Republicans accepted a politically inconvenient fact ?
