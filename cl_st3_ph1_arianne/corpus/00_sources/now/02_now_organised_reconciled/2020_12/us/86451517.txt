<p> Congress 's $900 billion stimulus bill includes some unexpected climate provisions , including new restrictions that will change the way Americans cool their homes and refrigerate their groceries . The new measures will phase out hydrofluorocarbons ( HFCs ) used in air conditioning and refrigeration , " super " greenhouse gases that are thousands of times more potent than carbon dioxide in their climate impact . <p> The goal of the provision is to cut the production and import of HFCs by 85 percent by 2035 . Under the new provision , that goal will be federal policy and the EPA will have the power to implement new restrictions on manufacturers and importers in order to meet it . <p> Those measures will bring the US in line with an important climate agreement that it had previously refused to ratify , the Kigali amendment to the Montreal Protocol .
