China , the U.S. and South Korea led on green stimulus after the financial crisis , but today during the Covid-19 pandemic , the EU , France and Germany have announced the most green spending . <p> After the financial crisis , governments put about $520 billion into green measures like railways , energy efficiency , grid modernization , renewable energy and water and waste management , according to WRI 's analysis , representing 16 percent of the total global fiscal stimulus .
