<h> Alamy <p> In the twelfth century BCE , civilizations across the Mediterranean came to an end . <p> The cause of this phenomenon -- known to some as the world 's first Dark Age -- is debated . Climate change , the ensuing collapse of social structures , and local uprisings all contributed the cultural apocalypse . Yet even as civilizations collapsed , a new group emerged : a shadowy group known as the Sea Peoples . They are almost unknown outside of academic circles , but this migrant group , which travelled the Mediterranean in search of homes and new lives , left a deep impression in the region . We do n't know where they came from , and we do n't know where they went , but over the course of the twelfth century they left enough of a mark to become ancient scapegoats for global catastrophe . <p> Almost everything we know about the Sea Peoples comes to us from Egyptian inscriptions . It was in Egypt that the Sea Peoples met their match . They were twice defeated -- in 1207 and 1177 BCE -- by Merneptah and Rameses III . But , according to the Egyptians , in their wake there was destruction : the great civilizations of the day -- the Hittites , the Mycenaeans , the Canaanites , and the Cypriots all crumbled .
