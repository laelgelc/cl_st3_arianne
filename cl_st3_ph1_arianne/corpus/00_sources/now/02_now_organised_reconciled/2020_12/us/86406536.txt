" <p> The UK Export Finance agency has offered guarantees worth billions of dollars to help British oil and gas companies expand in countries such as Brazil , Iraq , Argentina and Russia , Burrows said . <p> Johnson had faced particular criticism from campaigners for UKEF 's role in backing French major Total 's planned $20 billion liquefied natural gas project in Mozambique . <p> The government said the new policy would come into effect " as soon as possible " and would mean no further state support for oil , natural gas or coal projects overseas , including via development aid , export finance and trade promotion . <p> There would be " very limited exceptions " for gas-fired power plants within " strict parameters " in line with the Paris deal , the statement said .
