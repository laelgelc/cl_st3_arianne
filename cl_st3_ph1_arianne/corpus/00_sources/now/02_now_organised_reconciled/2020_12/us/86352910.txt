<h> Agency declines special protection request for tufted puffin <p> By BECKY BOHRERDecember 3 , 2020 GMT <p> JUNEAU , Alaska ( AP ) -- The tufted puffin -- a black seabird known for its white " mask " and orange bill -- does not warrant Endangered Species Act protections , the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service announced Wednesday . <p> The agency was petitioned in 2014 by the Natural Resources Defense Council to provide protections . The environmental group cited , in part , steep declines in breeding populations over the last three decades in California , Oregon and Washington . <p> It asked the agency to list as threatened or endangered the contiguous U.S. population as a " distinct population segment , " or to list the entire species if the population in the contiguous U.S. did not meet agency standards for a distinct population . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The Fish and Wildlife Service on Wednesday said climate change and oil spills pose the biggest threat to the species .
