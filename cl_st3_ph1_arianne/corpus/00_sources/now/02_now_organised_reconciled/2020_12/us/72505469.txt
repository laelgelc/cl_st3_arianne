<p> On Friday , the U.S. Supreme Court ruled unanimously against an effort by Texas to overturn the result of the 2020 presidential election by throwing out the results of four states and more than 10.3 million votes . The rejection of the suit brings a close to President Donald Trump 's legal avenues for reversing his election loss . And although the majority of Alaska voters cast ballots for Trump , they have cause to celebrate this ruling , as it upholds principles foundational to American democracy and conservatism . <p> What 's baffling is why Gov .
