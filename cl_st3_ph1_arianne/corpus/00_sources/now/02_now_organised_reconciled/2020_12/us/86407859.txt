<h> Mural gets to the heart of warming sea waters <p> Felipe Ortiz 's " The Heart of the Ocean " covers a wall adjacent to the Bremen Park Condominiums in East Boston.Erin Clark/Globe Staff <p> A coral heart bleeds on a wall in East Boston . Surrounding it are vivid colors and images of fish , seals , and other sea life . It 's all part of a new 150-square-foot mural painted by Colombian-American artist Felipe Ortiz for Sea Walls : Artists for Oceans , an international public art initiative addressing climate change and ocean deterioration conceived by the nonprofit PangeaSeed Foundation . <p> Sea Walls Boston , presented by donor Linda Cabot and HarborArts , stretches its messaging across seven brand-new murals in East Boston , each completed by a local artist . Ortiz 's work , titled " The Heart of the Ocean , " is the local project 's final installment for 2020 .
