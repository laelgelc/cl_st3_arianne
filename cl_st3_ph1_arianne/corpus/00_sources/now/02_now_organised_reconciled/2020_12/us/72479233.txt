<h> Actions <h> City of Hampton uses innovative tool to fight flooding <p> HAMPTON , Va . - It 's no secret . It does n't take much rain to flood Hampton and the rest of the region . <p> While that 's a challenge , another obstacle is finding ways to pay for projects to mitigate flooding as local governments face tight budgets these days . <p> Hampton is now moving forward with three projects to help fight flooding with a total cost of $12 million , but the money is coming from Environmental Impact Bonds . Private investment will put up the money , with the city paying back over time . <p> " We know now that Hampton Roads is leader in a less desirable category - one of the country 's most vulnerable areas to sea level rise and climate change , " said Hampton Mayor Donnie Tuck on Thursday . <p> The Chesapeake Bay Foundation partnered with a company called Quanitified Ventures to find a city to take on the project . Hampton becomes the third city in America to use Environmental Impact Bonds . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ projects that can benefit communities and also require reporting of the outcomes of the projects to ensure they 're doing what they were meant to do . <p> " In total , we estimate the these three projects will help us store an additional 8.6 million gallons of water per storm event , " said Tuck . <p> The three projects will increase storm water capacity at Lake Hampton , raise North Armistead Avenue and reduce flooding around Newmarket Creek .
