And call volume at the companies that collect payments rose to the highest level since April , a sign of growing distress among homeowners , the trade group said Monday . <p> With long-term unemployment rates rising and coronavirus cases surging , " it is not surprising to see more homeowners seeking relief , " Mike Fratantoni , chief economist at the MBA , said in a statement . <p> The percentage of homeowners who have started seeking forbearance is relatively low , but the rising proportion comes even as the economy has shown signs of recovery , underscoring how uneven the turnaround is .
