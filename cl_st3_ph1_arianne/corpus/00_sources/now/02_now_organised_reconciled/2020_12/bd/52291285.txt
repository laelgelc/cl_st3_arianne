<h> 5 brick kilns fined Tk8 lakh in Sylhet <p> Five brick kilns in Sylhet 's Beanibazar and Golapganj upazilas have been fined Tk8 lakh . <p> A joint drive by Rab and the Department of Environment ( DoE ) slapped the fine on Thursday . <p> Rab-9 deputy commander Major Md Shawkatul Monaem and DoE Sylhet office Director Mohammad Imran Hossain led the drive , Rab said in a press release on Friday . <p> A research report by the DoE and the World Bank published in March last year on the sources of air pollution in Bangladesh identified three main sources -- brick kilns , fumes of vehicles and dust from construction sites . <p> " Brick kilns are responsible for 58 percent air pollution in the capital . Plans have been taken to shut traditional kilns currently in operation . We 're working to produce eco-friendly bricks , " Environment , Forests and Climate Change Minister Md Shahab Uddin had told UNB . <p> He said the government plans to stop burning bricks at kilns by 2025 and use block bricks to construct buildings under government projects @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 10 percent block bricks , the minister said , adding that it will be increased to 100 percent over time and the use of block bricks will gradually be made mandatory in private projects , too .
