<h> Rabab Fatima elected Vice President of UN bodies ' Executive Board <p> Bangladesh 's Permanent Representative ( PR ) to the United Nations Ambassador Rabab Fatima has been elected as Vice President of the United Nations Development Programme ( UNDP ) , United Nations Population Fund ( UNFPA ) , and United Nations Office for Project Services ( UNOPS ) Executive Board . <p> This will enable Bangladesh to work closely with the other board members and the leaderships of these three vital UN organs and provide them strategic guidance to their work , said the Bangladesh Mission on Tuesday . <p> This election comes as a demonstration of the trust and confidence that Bangladesh enjoys due to her strong legacy of working together with the UN entities and their Executive Boards for implementation of the global development agenda under the leadership of Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina , it said . <p> Ambassador Fatima is also serving this year as the President of the UNICEF
