<h> The other side of climate friendly foods <h> Published : Friday 04 December 2020 <p> Eating is intrinsic to every living organism . We eat what is around us , be it plants or animals , as long as we eat moderately . But now , making good food choices is progressively becoming difficult . <p> Consider this . <p> Managing the food system is now part of the new formula for fighting climate change . The food systems -- that includes cultivation , storage , processing and managing waste -- produce somewhere between 21-37 per cent of greenhouse emissions . <p> There is an effort to reduce this to be able to meet the Paris Agreement commitments . Most of these emissions come from animals reared for their meat . The meat industry is obviously upset about losing business . <p> Unilever alone is offering nearly 1,300 vegan products in Europe . The company acquired the fake meat company The Vegetarian Butcher in 2018 . For example , Impossible Burger , which is said to have a carbon foot print which is 89 per cent smaller than that of a burger made from a cow , is made of soybean and potato protein mixed with heme from genetically engineered yeast for flavour . Additionally , it has coconut and sunflower oil along with methylcellulose and food starch . <p> Not only would this be unhealthy because it is ultra-processed but it has genetically engineered ingredients that could be of concern to those who want to eat good food . For example , access is going to be difficult as despite being made from cheap raw material , mock meat it is more expensive than the real thing . While factory-farmed beef burgers cost $2.80 per pound , mock meat burgers cost $6.25 per pound . Experts point out that this would lead to a shift in power from farmers to multinationals . <p> While mock meat might be the lesser evil , it is not actually food . A better solution would be to either shift to real plants or to consume less meat . <p> Another alternative is to consume animals in lower trophic levels of the food web . These would include chicken , fish and even insects .
