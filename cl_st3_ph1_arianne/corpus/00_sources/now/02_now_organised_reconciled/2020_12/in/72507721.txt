<h> Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the global climate summit on December 12 . <p> PM Narendra ModiFile Photo <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi will address the global climate summit today on the occasion of the fifth anniversary of the landmark Paris Climate Agreement , said Prakash Javadekar , Union Minister of Environment on Friday . <p> The United Nations , United Kingdom and France will be holding Climate Ambition Summit 2020 , in partnership with Chile and Italy today , five years since the adoption of the Paris Agreement . <p> The Summit will be highlighting the fast-growing coalition of countries , cities , regions , businesses and investors committing to net-zero emissions and boosting ambition in climate finance , and resilience and adaptation said an official statement . <p> It further said , " The Summit will feature leading examples of countries producing concrete near-term plans and policies to deliver on their longer-term climate ambition , highlighting the work ahead for other countries to deliver new Nationally Determined Contributions through 2021 in advance of COP26 . It will provide leaders with a global platform to showcase commitments to tackle climate change , " the statement read . <p> The Summit will also be giving a platform to the business , sub-national governments and civil society , including youth , indigenous and faith-based climate champions . <p> Besides , there will be speeches from co-convenors UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres , UK Prime Minister Boris Johnson , President of France Emmanuel Macron , Prime Minister of Italy Giuseppe Conte , President of Chile Sebastian Pinera , COP26 President-Designate Alok Sharma , and dozens of other world leaders who will present ambitious climate commitments . <p> The Paris accord , adopted in 2015 and signed a year later , is designed to boost greenhouse-gas-emissions mitigation worldwide . Its main goals include keeping the global average temperature to well below 2 degrees Celsius ( 3.6 degrees Fahrenheit ) , and efforts to limit warming to 1.5 degrees in a bid to reduce the impact and risks of climate change .
