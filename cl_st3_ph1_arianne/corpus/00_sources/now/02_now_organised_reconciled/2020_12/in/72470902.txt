The absolute rule of life is to survive , sustain and evolve by passing on your genetic legacy through offspring . <p> Since the beginning of life on Earth , countless organisms have constantly worked towards defining their place and significance in this fight for survival . Many evolved and many lost . <p> The ones who adapted to their changing habitats flourished while others became extinct . Thus , this process of evolution is dependent on the fundamental of ' survival of the fittest ' . <p> The earliest humans ( Homo habilis ) evolved around 2.8 million years ago . A compromise so outlandish that it bypasses our primary need -- ' The compromise of reproduction ' ? <p> Women are carriers of genetic makeup . They gestate , brood life together with men , thus keeping the cycle of life running . <p> A number of well-educated women , especially the ones in climate conscious circles , are joining the group and movement called ' Birthstrike ' due to climate change . <p> A month-old study titled Fertility decisions of the Anthropocene , conducted by Ryan Ricciardia of George Washington University indicated that 51.1 per cent of the respondents did not wish to have any children . <p> Many individuals have started questioning the future sustenance of their children . Firstly , numerous upcoming evidences are suggesting the influence of climate change on children 's health before conception , during pregnancy , childhood and adolescence . <p> This indicates the greater public health challenges that will be faced in the future . Rising temperatures and associated complications are expected to generate higher stress levels on maternal and child health . <p> Research suggests heat-induced disorders in mothers can lead to complications like pre-mature labour , intra-uterine growth retardation , neonatal and infant mortality . <p> Environmental factors like increased humidity are associated with eclampsia . <p> Second , there are concerns regarding increased resource consumption .
