<h> Global warming is real and 2020 is among 3 warmest year ever <h> Published : Wednesday 02 December 2020 <p> The year 2020 is set to be the among the three warmest on record , according to the latest State of the Global Climate provisional report by World Meteorological Organization on December 1 , 2020 . Also , the decade 2011-2020 would be the warmest ever . <p> The global mean surface temperature for January-October was 1.2 degree Celsius higher than the pre-industrial baseline ( 1850-1900 ) . For that period , 2020 is the second-warmest year on record . <p> The record heat in 2020 has been despite near-La Ni ? a conditions prevailing in the equatorial Pacific Ocean since August and moderate La Ni ? a conditions prevailing since October . <p> The La Ni ? a phase of the El Ni ? o Southern Oscillation ( ENSO ) phenomenon generally has a cooling effect on many parts of the world . <p> Scientific evidence indicates increasing temperatures are a direct result of human-led global warming -- an impact of the emission of green house gases ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ there has been a slight dip this year due to measures taken by many countries to fight the ongoing novel coronavirus disease ( COVID-19 ) pandemic , according to the report . <p> It . however , added that the reduction " will be practically indistinguishable from the natural inter-annual variability , driven largely by the terrestrial biosphere .
