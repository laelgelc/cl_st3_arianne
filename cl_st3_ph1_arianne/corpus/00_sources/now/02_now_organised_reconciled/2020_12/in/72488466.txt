<h> UKPM to be chief guest at R-Day parade <p> KT NEWS SERVICE . Dated : 12/3/2020 10:55:54 AM <p> NEW DELHI , Dec 2 : British Prime Minister Boris Johnson ( 56 ) , who belongs to the Conservative Party , is most likely to be the chief guest at the Republic Day parade here on January 26 . Prime Minister Narendra Modi extended a formal invitation to him during their November 27 telephonic . In turn Johnson wanted Modi to attend the G-7 summit in the United Kingdom next year.The last British Prime Minister to grace the Republic Day parade in 1993 was John Major.In his November 27 tweet , Modi remained secretive about his invite to Johnson , while claiming excellent friendly discussions on an ambitious roadmap for India-UK times in the next decade . He tweeted : " We agreed to work towards a quantum leap in our cooperation in all areas -- trade and investments , defence and security , climate change and fighting Covid-19 . " A positive fallout of their telephonic conversation was an offer by PM Johnson for a free trade agreement with India .
