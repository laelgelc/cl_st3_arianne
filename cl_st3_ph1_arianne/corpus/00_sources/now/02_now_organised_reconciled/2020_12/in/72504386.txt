<h> IUST hosts ' Biodiversity Month ' celebrations <p> Awantipora , Dec 11 : To commemorate the completion of United Nations Decade on Biodiversity ( 2011-2020 ) , Mantaqi Centre for Science and Society ( MCSS ) , Islamic University of Science &amp; Technology ( IUST ) hosted Biodiversity Month celebrations here on Thursday . The programme is part of the project objectives sponsored by the Department of Science and Technology ( DST ) under HICAB-Climate Change Programme , Government of India . <p> Vice Chancellor , IUST Prof. Mushtaq A. Siddiqi while formally inaugurating the event congratulated the MCSS , IUST for their efforts in organizing the event . Prof. Siddiqi opined that the need of the hour is to incorporate the vibrant students and faculty to play a vital role in preserving biodiversity through research in order to build institutional strength . He further said that the universities need to gear up to transform from typical academic institutions to institutions of relevance in order to preserve the biodiversity and natural resources around us .
