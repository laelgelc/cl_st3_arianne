<h> NGT wants bird diverters installed on power lines <p> The National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) has directed the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) and five other states to install bird diverters on all existing power lines within four months and that new power lines be laid underground to prevent death of the critically-endangered species Great IndianBustard . <p> It has also directed the MoEF&amp;CC to take appropriate steps to ensure that the environment impact assessment ( EIA ) includes impact on biological diversity for wind/solar energy projects . <p> A bench headed by NGT chairperson Justice Adarsh Kumar Goel passed the order after considering a report filed by a six-member expert committee appointed by the Tribunal . <p> The NGT said laying of transmission lines underground needs to be ensured for all new projects henceforth , as recommended by the six-member expert committee , by making it a mandatory condition for the grant of consents under the Water/Air Acts by the concerned state pollution control boards which may be overseen by the central pollution control board and the MoEF&amp;CC . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Wildlife and Environment Litigation , alleging that steps taken by the Ministry are not adequate and mortality of the Great Indian Bustard bird continues to be high due to the high-tension transmission lines . <p> The respondents in the matter were MoEF&amp;CC , National Board of Wildlife , National Biodiversity <p> Noting that the wind projects did not require any EIA studies , the Tribunal said that if consent is not required , even then undergrounding of the transmission lines may be ensured .
