At the end of my first semester in Indian School of Mines , now Indian Institute of Technology , Dhanbad , I had faced a dilemma whether to choose staying in mining engineering or to apply for a change of branch to petroleum engineering . With a filled-in application in my pocket I went around for two days asking everyone who could offer an advice , only to get even more confused . On the last day of application , I boarded the bus that took us to Jharia coalfields excursion . I had resolved that if we returned before deadline of 2 pm , I would submit the application , and if not , then I would accept it as a sign that I should stay put . An act of omission , a bias that I now understand better having studied behavioural economics . The reason for this torturing indecisiveness was that several of my seniors and even my batchmates had learnt that the world had reached a level of oil and gas production that the entire oil and gas reserves would last only for a decade or so . The year of exhaustion was being predicted to be close to 2007-08 . This year of exhaustion was computed adding remaining life of oil and gas reserves based on total known reserves divided by annual production rate . I had only known two graduating seniors from petroleum engineering when I was in my first year , of which one had gotten a campus placement in GAIL , towards the end of the eighth semester , and the other eventually got a job only after a few months after graduating . Scarcity of resources and high prices of oil in the first decade of new millennium propelled high investments in exploration . So much so that towards the end of first decade of the millennium , India allotted coal reserves for coal-to-liquids and coal-to-gas projects , and the non-traditional oil and gas from shale became economically viable and started production to a large extent making USA self-reliant and even an exporter of petroleum and petroleum-based chemical products . The recent months , however , have yielded to high degree of slowdown in demand partly due to the COVID pandemic and partly due to energy transition in transportation that has been emerging as a disruptor . In April , 2020 , the crude prices went below zero as the producers ran out of storage capacities and were willing to pay for the of-take . Analysts have begun to converge that peak demand for oil and gas may have already passed . <p> How do we reconcile with these ? The year of exhaustion is a manifestation of the fairly old social @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . The fallacy of this economic myth is explained on account of two phenomena . First , that the scarcity of supply and high prices force consumers to look for substitutes and replacement of consumption , including the efficiency improvement in demand side management . And , second , that the proven reserves are not the total available resources , proven reserves are estimated based on price of products and cost of extraction , price of product being an independent market driven variable . Also , as a practice , the resource companies do no build large reserve inventories as it requires expenses in exploration , hence , they plan for proving reserves for a period of next 5-10 years only . Computing remaining life based on such reserves therefore is tautological . This interplay between price and lower exploration investment in a regular course of operation makes the numerator in the year of exhaustion computation erroneous . Higher prices incentivise greater degree of exploration , and innovative technologies for better extraction and thus , reserves swell when prices go higher . With climate change concerns haunting the policy makers across the globe , any possibility of containing global warming requires them to focus their attention on transportation . However , more than the policy interventions , the market forces have brought in the disruption . Crashing costs of energy generation from renewable sources and battery storage have initiated an energy transition that will change the landscape of cities across the globe . Hydrogen cell based technologies that can be used for long hauls will make a further dent in market for petroleum products . Resource exhaustion concerns emanate from lack of applied economic rationale . Peak demand on the other hand recognizes the undercurrents of technological disruption pushed by environmental concerns , and these have found support of market forces and policy-makers that have the capacity to sanction the regulatory policies supplementing the market forces . A study of policies in the US and Australia , and perhaps even in India and China , goes to show that market forces have taken a lead towards the transformation while governments have faced inertia . The inertial however , is making way for acceptance of change and soon the policies are likely to be aligned with renewables , green-hydrogen and more . <p> Coal industry is likely to face a similar trajectory . Merely the fact that coal-based generation assets have been built will not guarantee the continued demand for coal as is being consistently argued in India and elsewhere .
