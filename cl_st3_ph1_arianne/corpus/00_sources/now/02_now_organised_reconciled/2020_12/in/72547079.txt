The wide range of topics that made the top 30 by sheer numbers was telling . But at the heart of it all were universal concerns around the implications of the COVID-19 pandemic ; the rise of China and Chinese aggression in its neighbourhood , particularly after the skirmish between Indian and Chinese soldiers in Ladakh this summer ; the growing disillusionment with globalisation ; climate change and the need for a " green recovery " and India 's national security were recurring themes in the long reads that ORF published this year . <p> Picking out the 15 that now constitute this series took into account the need for diversity not just in subject matter but in viewpoint , with half of the publications being written or co-written by women . Our most read security and defence research publications , traditionally considered a primarily male-dominated subject , have been written by our women faculty . <p> As we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reads will be seen by posterity as part of a critical chronicling of history at a pivotal moment when the entire world has been gripped by a deadly pandemic . Years from now , when scholars study this particular epoch in history , we hope to be part of the rich library of works that will provide a deep insight into the pandemic that has come to define the zeitgeist at the dawn of the third decade of the 21st century .
