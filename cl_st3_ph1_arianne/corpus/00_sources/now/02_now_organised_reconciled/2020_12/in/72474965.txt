<h> Public hearing held over Dabolim airport expansion <p> Vasco : The public hearing over the proposed expansion of the Dabolim airport terminal building was held on Friday , where divergent views were expressed by six participants . <p> Social activist Savio Correia and Fr Eremito Rebello were among the six participants who spoke up at the hearing , which was held at the community centre of the Airport Authority of India colony near NSD , Alto-Dabolim . <p> Correia said that he raised some issues , pointing out that the environmental public hearing was " irregular " and was not in compliance of the EIA notification and other environment-related norms . <p> " I demanded that the hearing be adjourned , and should be held on some other day as the Sancoale village panchayat had been kept in dark about the hearing . " <p> The project proponent has failed to make arrangement for parking facility as the AAI expects the passenger movement to increase to 11.22 millions in two years , Correia argued . <p> Fr Rebello , on the other hand , appreciated the expansion plans of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The Dabolim airport should be operated only by the AAI and not by private players , Fr Rebello said , appealing to the people not to oppose the airport expansion plans .
