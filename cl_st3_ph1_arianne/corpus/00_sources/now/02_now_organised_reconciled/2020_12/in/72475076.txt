<h> Goa 's Water Insecurity <p> A study conducted by the NABARD Consultancy Services has revealed that inconsistency in light and moderate rainfall over the last 100 years threatens water security in the state . The study predicts that Goa 's water security is likely to deteriorate further under the ongoing climate change scenarios . The study was conducted at the behest of the Goa State Biodiversity Board to prepare the draft Goa state action plan on climate change 2020 . The researchers who investigated climate projections in Goa in the 21st century based on high resolution regional climate models have found very heavy and extreme daily rainfall events of 150mm and 200mm rains , which are rare and exceptional in today 's climate . The researchers have said that such high intensity rain will become commonplace in climate change scenarios of Goa in the future .
