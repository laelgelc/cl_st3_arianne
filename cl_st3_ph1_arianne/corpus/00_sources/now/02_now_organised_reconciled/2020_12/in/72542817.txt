<h> Youth greatest source of hope , says Guterres in NY message <p> Amidst turmoil , the worlds young people are its greatest source of hope , UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres said in his New Year 's message for 2020 . <p> " We enter 2020 with uncertainty and insecurity all around . Persistent inequality and rising hatred . A warring world and a warming planet ... " Guterres said on Sunday . <p> He added that climate change was " not only a long-term problem but a clear and present danger , and we can not afford to be the generation that fiddled while the planet burned " , reports Efe news <p> But there was also hope , he said . <p> " This year , my New Year 's message is to the greatest source of that hope : the world 's young people , " he added . <p> The UN chief highlighted the initiatives of the new generations in climate action , gender equality , social justice and human rights , issues in which he said they were " on the frontlines and in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ your passion and determination . You are rightly demanding a role in shaping the future , " Guterres said in his speech . <p> " I am with you . The UN stands with you - and belongs to you . <p> " This year , the world needs young people to keep speaking out . Keep thinking big , keep pushing boundaries , and keep up the pressure , " he added . <p> Guterres also said that 2020 would be the 75th anniversary of the UN and announced the launch of a " decade of action for the Sustainable Development Goals , our blueprint for a fair globilization " . <p> The Lisbon-born UN chief assumed office on January 1 , 2017 , taking over from South Korea 's Ban Ki-Moon.
