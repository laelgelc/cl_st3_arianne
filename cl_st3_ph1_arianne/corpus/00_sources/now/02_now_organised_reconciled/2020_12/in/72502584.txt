<h> UNDP announces launch of new COVID-19 Data Futures Platform <h> The Data Futures Platform comes at a time when countries around the world are dealing with the consequences of multiple waves of the pandemic , a protracted economic recession , and rapidly shrinking foreign and domestic financial resources . <p> The UN Development Programme ( UNDP ) launched today a new COVID-19 Data Futures Platform -- open access and interactive platform that aggregates multiple sources of information across UNDP and UN agencies , academia , and the private sector . The platform offers a simulation option that allows any user to visualize costs , the impact of socio-economic policies and interventions across countries and regions ; and the welfare implications of targeting vulnerable households . <p> The Data Futures Platform comes at a time when countries around the world are dealing with the consequences of multiple waves of the pandemic , a protracted economic recession , and rapidly shrinking foreign and domestic financial resources .
