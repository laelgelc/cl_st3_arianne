<p> Students across a warming globe pleaded for their lives , future and planet on Friday , 15 March , demanding tough action on climate change . <p> From the South Pacific to the edge of the Arctic Circle , angry students in more than 100 countries walked out of classes to protest what they see as the failures by their governments . <p> More than 150,000 students and adults who were mobilised by word of mouth and social media protested in Europe , according to police estimates . But the initial turnout in the United States did not look quite as high . <p> Here are glimpses of students from across the world , who skipped classes on Friday to take to the streets and protest against their governments ' failure to take sufficient action against global warming .
