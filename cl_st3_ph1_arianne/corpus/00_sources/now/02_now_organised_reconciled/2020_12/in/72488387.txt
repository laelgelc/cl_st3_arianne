<h> Prof Arvind Jasrotia is new Registrar , University of Jammu <p> Jammu : Prof Arvind Jasrotia , Director , College Development Council in addition to his own duties has been given additional charge of Registrar , University of Jammu till further orders relieving Prof Neetu Andotra , Dept of Commerce of the additional charge on her request . Recently he has been appointed as Member of State Level Expert Appraisal Committee , J&amp;K by the Ministry of Urban Development , GOI . Dr. Jasrotia has varied publications in peer reviewed journals to his credit on varied topics such as Environmental Law , Human Rights , Constitutional Law , etc . Dr. Jasrotia is also a member of World Commission on Environmental Law , the NAAC Peer Team as well as a Member Academic of Judicial Services Examination . He has guided number of Ph.D and LL.M scholars . He has travelled extensively in India and abroad and has attended Study Session on Human Rights organized by International Institute of Human Rights at Strasbourg ( France ) and got scholarship to attend International University Human Rights Teaching and Research Programme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) . Prof. Jasrotia completed as Project Director , a Major ICSSR Research Programme titled : " Impact of Kashmir Conflicts on Refugees , Forced Migrants and Displaced Population . He was also Co-Principal Investigator in DST sponsored ( Inter- University Consortium ) Research Project titled " The Himalayan Cryosphere : Science and Society . In collaboration with NLSIU , Bengaluru , he is also working on another UGC-STRIDE -3 Project titled ' Urbanization in Jammu &amp; Kashmir in the Face of Climate Change ' . He is also a Master Trainer in UNDP-GEF ( Global ABS Project ) : Strengthening Human Resources , Legal Frameworks and Institutional Capacities to Implement the Nagoya Protocol ( NLSIU &amp;UNDP-GEF Project ) . His area of specialization includes Constitutional Law , Environmental Law and Human Rights Law and his research interests include Climate Justice , Environmental Ethics and Sustainable Development .
