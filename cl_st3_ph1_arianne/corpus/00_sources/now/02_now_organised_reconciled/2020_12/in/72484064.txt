<h> Anti-coal group protests against felling of trees for power supply line <p> Panaji : People 's movement Goyant Kollso Naka on Friday submitted a letter to the Principal Secretary ( Forests ) , the Principal Chief Conservator of Forests , the Deputy Conservator of Forests ( North ) , and the Regional Office of the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , Bangalore as regards stopping the felling of 18,000 trees for the construction of the 400 kV/DC transmission lines from Xeldem to Mapusa , and further reviewing the Stage-II Forest Clearance granted to the state government on November 19 , 2020 . <p> The letter written by the co-convener of GKN Abhijit Prabhudesai and others , demands immediate related actions , failing which , it says , " The people will be forced to defend the nation and carry out their constitutional duties by preventing the unnecessary felling of trees that is also threatening our very existence by imposing fossil fuels and global warming upon us . " <p> " The state forest department and the Deputy Conservator of Forest , North Goa division must not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ both in forest and non-forest areas , or work orders under Section 2 of the Forest ( Conservation ) Act , 1980 pursuant to the Stage-II Forest Clearance granted on November 19 , 2020 , " the letter states . <p> " The Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change should reassess the subject proposal and all the three linear projects through the protected areas of the Western Ghats in light of the issues , which have been raised in this letter and all previous correspondence received by them , " it adds . <p> The letter , pointing out that majority of the citizens oppose the subject project , states that the subject transmission line is proposed to transmit power from coal power plants , rather than from solar installations as committed by our country . <p> " This commitment to our future generations demand that we stop the subject project and reject this proposal outright in view of the threat to all humans , " he noted .
