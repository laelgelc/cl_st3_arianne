Discussions on issues that need global cooperation , such as climate change or the management of the covid pandemic , are being boycotted by countries that matter . A humanitarian concern like refugee migration is being portrayed as religious expansionism . Thomas Friedman 's book , The World Is Flat : A Brief History of the Twenty-first Century , written in 2005 , looks irrelevant now . <p> The story is no different in individual countries . Hate crimes against immigrants and minorities have gone up . Racial tensions have never been this high in recent times .
