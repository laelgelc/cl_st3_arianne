<p> While the new master plan will incorporate policies to enable the city to cope with urban risks and climate change challenges , it will also look into the gaps of the second master plan that is set to end in 2026 <p> CHENNAI : The acute water crisis that troubles the city every time the monsoon fails will be the top priority in the third master plan that is to be prepared for the Chennai Metropolitan Area ( CMA ) . With the terrible summer of 2019 , when the rock bottom levels of the reservoirs and groundwater grabbed global attention , the Chennai Metropolitan Development Authority ( CMDA ) has decided to keep the focus on finding a permanent solution for the issue that keeps city residents on their toes every summer . The terms of reference for consultants to prepare the vision document for the third master plan will also stress on coastal management issues . Sources in the housing and urban development department said that the new master plan would be confined to the present CMA covering a radius of 1,189 sq km in Chennai , Chengalpet , Kancheepuram and Tiruvallur districts . " As of now , the third master plan does not include the proposed extended areas falling in the three neighbouring districts that may be part of the CMA in the future , " an official said .
