<p> Millions Forced to Migrate because of Climate Change , Numbers to Treble by 2050 <p> Climate Change caused 14 million people in India to migrate in 2020 <p> Over 62 million people in South Asia will be forced to migrate from their homes due to climate change-fueled disasters by 2050 -- a three-fold increase from current displacement figures -- unless countries act now to mitigate effects of climate change , stated a report released by Climate Action Network South Asia ( CANSA ) and ActionAid International . <p> " South Asia is already experiencing some of the highest fatalities due to extreme weather conditions . Future projections see South Asia as an ' epicentre ' of deadly heatwaves , chronic droughts , rising sea levels , and intensified cyclones , " Harjeet Singh , global climate lead at ActionAid told The Citizen .
