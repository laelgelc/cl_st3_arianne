In today 's day and age , safeguarding consumer 's rights have become an important aspect . In India , we observe National Consumer Rights Day on December 24 , every year . <p> Significance of this day : <p> This day provides an opportunity for individuals to highlight the importance of the consumer movement and the need to make every consumer more aware of their rights and responsibilities . <p> On December 24 , 1986 , Consumer Protection Act 1986 received the approval of the President of India and hence , came into force .
