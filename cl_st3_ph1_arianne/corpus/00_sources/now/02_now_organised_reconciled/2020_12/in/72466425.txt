<p> A single year in the vast timeline of Himalayan history is all but insignificant . The 366 days that have made up 2020 matter very little when compared to the prolonged changes occurring over thousands of centuries . In the rapid-fire world we have created for ourselves , boasting instantaneous communication and our ability to gratify virtually any desire with a keystroke or the swipe of an index finger , there is a tendency to imagine that everything around us is accelerating at the same pace . Yet , mountains teach us patience . They are not measured in megabytes or gigahertz .
