<h> LETTERS TO THE EDITOR ( 09/12/2020 ) <p> INDIAN agitating farmers have gained support from all across the world . Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau was the first world leader to speak up in support of the farmers . Now , the United Nations and 36 MPs from the United Kingdom have rallied behind the farmers , who have been protesting against the four farm acts . The world leaders are defending the right of Indian people to demonstrate peacefully . On the other hand , the central government is trying to salvage its sullied image saying that the world leaders ' statements amount to interference in the internal affairs of the country , knowing well that the Indian diaspora is spread across the continents and any adverse policies could affect them in equal measure . National TV channels , which are caged parrots of the ruling dispensation , did not cover the rallies held in various cities of the US , Canada and Australia ; they dish out censored and lopsided news . It is the social media that dispensed the news which went viral . We must @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ against anti-people initiatives by the Centre , which are meekly implemented by the state . It is hoped that European countries , United Nations and international tourists will stand up against the three contentious projects in Goa . People across the state have been up in arms against the three infrastructure projects , making it clearer that they do n't want to see Goa as a coal hub . The Centre and the state are pursuing misplaced priorities and incurring the wrath of the people . <p> EVERETTE ASSIS TELLES , MARGAO <p> Strategise To Prevent Water Crisis <p> THE rainfall inconsistency over the last 100 years has threatened Goa 's water security , making the state vulnerable to flooding . It has been reported that the ' draft Goa state action plan on climate change 2020 ' has been kept open for public scrutiny , inputs and suggestion till December 10 . The government had planned to introduce water efficiency across all sectors by 2020 . This reminds me of how our government against the views of eminent experts and economists on boosting our finances , went ahead to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ money in people 's pocket to increase the demand side first ! Increasing tariff should be the last resort : there should be priority on increasing water conservation and capacity to store rainwater , preventing the water running unused and unsaved into the sea . There should be focus on ' generating ' potable water from the sea by distillation , and also from the atmosphere . We must act now to prevent a South Africa-type crisis where cities ran out of water even to drink or wash hands ! The measures can not be adopted overnight , but time is running out fast . Besides the determined struggles of social reformers , the entertainment media and theatre too has contributed immensely to these efforts . But superstition continues to be deep-rooted @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ undoing and seems to be a legacy handed down from generation to generation . So much so that even in the times of the pandemic , the country is fighting COVID-19 with superstition and showmanship . It is not at all surprising that the scourge of the virus has triggered the surge of several superstitions on how to fight and overcome the disease . How can anyone forget the video which went viral and had a prominent politician and Union minister raising slogans of ' go corona , corona go ' as if to send the virus back from India ! At a time when the nation 's media should have been promoting science and not superstition , it is equally shocking to have newspapers and TV channels endorsing views on the consumption of certain ' substances ' popularised by eminent personalities which serve to act as an elixir effective in warding off the harmful effects of the virus . Besides , the idea of special rituals and indulgence in a lot of mumbo-jumbo to drive away evil spirits seems to have caught on with the public . The fastidiousness @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lends credence to the belief that the hopelessness that has been brought about by the pandemic situation will continue to have people falling prey to the deviousness of the unscrupulous . Social media has once again been the main culprit in fanning the flame of misbeliefs and superstitions even as the government is engaged in combating the virus through the limited means at its disposal . Known to be synonymous with the times , it has been propagating a lot of myths about COVID through unconfirmed posts which often end up getting ' forwarded ' countless times . People need to be told in no uncertain terms that they need to refrain from following ' superstitious ideas ' being circulated on social media . They need to realise that the only ' magical cure ' for now is social distancing and wearing of face-masks which needs to be followed very religiously.
