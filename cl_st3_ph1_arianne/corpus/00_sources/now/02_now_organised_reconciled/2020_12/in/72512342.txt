<h> ' Frogs on decline , pushing up risk of vector-borne diseases ' <p> There has been a decline in frog population , which has exacerbated the outbreaks of vector-borne diseases like malaria , filaria , dengue and chikungunya in the state , says the ' draft state action plan for climate change ' of the Goa State Biodiversity Board . <p> The report has maintained that the outbreaks of malaria and dengue are connected to the decline in amphibians because they eat mosquitoes that transmit these diseases . <p> " There has been an increase in vector-borne diseases likes malaria , filaria , dengue and many more due to the reduction in the breeding of frogs , which has led to an increase in mosquitoes , " it has said . <p> Researchers have found a significant increase in dengue cases due to the decline in frog population ; cases rose to 335 in 2018 and last year the state registered the all-time high of 637 dengue cases in 11 months . <p> The state recorded 293 dengue cases in 2015 , which was the worst case scenario . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ began to decline in 2016 as around 742 cases were reported . The cases came down to 653 in 2017 and further declined to 377 in 2018 . <p> A total of 246 malaria cases were reported in the state from January to November last year . <p> There were exponential rise in chikungunya cases as well , which saw 400 per cent increase in five years from 49 in 2014 to 245 cases in 2019 . <p> The report has also mentioned that Kyasanur forest <p> disease has spread its tentacles in Goa due to climate change . The KFD is a tick-borne disease , and was confined to Karnataka for more than 50 years . <p> However , KFD cases are now commonly reported every year in Goa from December to May . <p> In 2015 , cases of fever accompanied by vomiting and diarrhoea were reported in Pali village of Sattari Taluka . On investigation , it was confirmed to be a KFD outbreak . <p> Since then KFD cases have been reported every year . <p> The report has maintained that human migration poses major challenges @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cases were reported among migrants at construction sites , which may also result in ' imported ' cases and potential re-introduction of disease into low-transmission or free zones . <p> In the report , the researchers have shown as to how the increase in breeding of frogs can benefit humans as well as local ecosystems , warning that the declines and losses are undermining the ecosystems on which many lives are dependent . <p> The researchers have reckoned that the increasing use of pesticides and fungicides may be responsible for frog deaths . Poaching of frogs for meat has also been attributed to the decline . <p> The action plan has proposed a ten-year-long strategy , which should be implemented by the forest department for improving wildlife habitats . The plan envisages raising of grasslands , planting of suitable fruit-bearing trees , creation of water bodies and adequate protection .
