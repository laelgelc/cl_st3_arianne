<h> Christy Clark ' Determined ' to see LNG launch in Kitimat <p> Officials from industry , local government and First Nations welcomed Christy Clark to Kitimat today , where the Premier had a clear message for the Nation 2 Nation Forum . <p> " We need LNG for the future of British Columbia , we need it for the future of First Nations communities and we need it now . " said Clark . <p> The province is on the verge of LNG development becoming a reality as the LNG Canada project considers a final investment decision in Kitimat , while the Federal Government considers granting final approval to the Pacific Northwest LNG facility near Prince Rupert . <p> Just days ago a group of 90 scientists from around the world called on the Federal Government to reject the 12-billion dollar Prince Rupert project because of possible climate damage from greenhouse gas emissions . <p> " What they say is that while LNG will reduce the impact of climate change , there 's no evidence that it will displace coal " said Clark .
