They recycle <p> Barn swallows build their nests with mud pellets and like to reuse nests year after year . Dive-bombers <p> " Barn swallows can act territorial around their nests , and they will swoop and dive-bomb you or start making alarm calls if you 're deemed a threat , " said Tol . They 'll target your cats and dogs too ! <p> " From my experience it does seem like they get used to the farmers that are in and out of the buildings , and do n't mind some human activity once they get used to you , " she added . <h> 8 . Four years old <p> Barn swallows live on average four years , beginning breeding at one year old . The oldest recorded barn swallow in North America was eight years old , recaptured in Maryland in the US , and its age was likely discernable because it was banded , said Tol. <h> 9 .
