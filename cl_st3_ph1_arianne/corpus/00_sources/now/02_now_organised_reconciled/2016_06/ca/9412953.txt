<h> Third Instalment of the Provision Project : Leeds Hydro Store Comments <p> Leeds , England -- ( SBWIRE ) -- 06/17/2016 -- The Clarence Fitzroy Bryant College are set to head to Barbados to implement the 3rd instalment of The Provision Project -- A OAS-funded hydroponics project which has already proved to be a success for around eight years . This instalment will include project leader Dr. Leighton Naraine and technical expert Stuart LaPlace training communities regarding the hydroponics model which has been created and a number of university professors will also be present . <p> The hydroponics model created is going to be replicated in five Caribbean countries ; Guyana , Barbados , Nevis , Trinidad and Haiti to enable all to eat well regarding the terrible effects of climate change and other things . The first implementation took place in Guyana last year and in April this year Nevis saw their first water-based agriculture system which allows for more crops to be grown efficiently . <p> Leeds Hydro Store a UK based hydroponics supplier was keen to comment saying , " The Provision Project is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ huge amount of individuals and we fully support that .
