<p> The Trudeau government has begun a review of the processes and methods it uses to decide which natural resource development projects will be approved or rejected . <p> The Liberals said six ministries are all in one way or another essential to delivering a process for approving projects that are based on science , fact and social responsibility . <p> The ministries that will be part of the review include : <p> Fisheries and Oceans . <p> Environment and Climate Change . <p> Transport . <p> Science and Innovation . <p> Indigenous and Northern Affairs . <p> Natural Resources . <p> " Developed wisely , major resource projects can power economies , help solve environmental challenges , support communities and create jobs , " said Dominic LeBlanc , fisheries minister . " Without proper oversight however , they can lead to environmental degradation , stranded assets , broken trust and lost opportunities .
