<p> Ken Smith is one frustrated oil sands labour leader . The giant wildfire that forced Fort McMurray 's evacuation came as the Unifor Local 707A president was in the middle of contract negotiations , fighting to save bitumen mining jobs despite the tough times for oil companies . <p> But he found himself fleeing the flames along with thousands of others . Negotiations to protect the 3,450 Suncor workers he represents were put on hold . <p> " I really ca n't talk right now , " he said in an interview from his truck May 3 . " We are literally in bumper to bumper traffic . It 's a disaster . I 've never seen anything like it , nor want to . " <p> The burly 55-year-old truck mechanic , who used to drive an 850-tonne hauler the size of an " apartment building , " had been ordered to evacuate , along with everyone else . <p> Weeks earlier , The Tyee had interviewed Smith in the once-booming oil city about the impact of the lowest global oil prices in a generation on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had been bleeding thousands of jobs .
