<p> FRANKFURT , GERMANY-- ( Marketwired - Jun 21 , 2016 ) - Today , SGI ( NASDAQ : SGI ) , a global leader in high-performance solutions for compute , data analytics and data management , announced a significant investment in extreme scale software research at The Edinburgh Parallel Computing Centre ( EPCC ) at the University of Edinburgh . The investments at this top tier research centre highlight the commitment of SGI to the European software research community . These resources , including SGI application software and supercomputing hardware expertise , will assist scientists as they explore issues related to healthcare , materials science , climate change and renewable resources , among many other topics . <p> EPCC scientists , in collaboration with SGI , will further research a diverse set of scientific applications and systems software tools that enable production supercomputing at extreme scale .
