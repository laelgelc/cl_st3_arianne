<p> Six months ago , Justin Trudeau went to Davos , Switzerland to pitch the World Economic Forum on a vision of shared prosperity , proper leadership and embracing diversity . <p> A day later , David Cameron appeared on stage and tried to explain why he was preparing to plunge Britain into a referendum over its membership in the European Union . <p> " Now , some people ask me , ' Well , why are you holding a referendum ? ' " he acknowledged , after explaining that his aim was for Britain to remain within a " reformed " EU . <p> " I think it 's absolutely essential to have full and proper democratic support for what Britain 's place should be in Europe and that 's why we 're holding the referendum . " <p> Oopsie . <p> If Cameron is invited back to Davos next year , it will be as a former prime minister .
