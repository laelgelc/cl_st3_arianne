<p> It is an encouraging sign of the times that more and more electric cars are making their way onto our streets and highways , turning heads not with the roar of powerful engines , but with the conspicuous silence of battery-powered efficiency . <p> However , a bill recently introduced in the National Assembly that aims to put many more of these environmentally friendly vehicles on our roads , while well-intentioned , is problematic . <p> Clearly , the fight against climate change is urgent , and few would object to the goal of Bill 104 , officially " an act to increase the number of zero-emission motor vehicles in Quebec in order to reduce greenhouse gas and other pollutant emissions . " <p> The bill seeks to make Quebec the first province to set a minimum quota for the number of electric or hybrid vehicles sold , and this is where the hazard lights go on . If passed , car makers would be required to sell or lease a minimum quantity of electric vehicles every year -- specifically , 15.5 per cent of their total @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The rules would apply only to manufactures that sell or lease at least 4,500 new vehicles annually , and they would be allowed to trade credits among themselves to make up for quota deficits , similar to cap-and-trade programs for other polluting industries . <p> Fines would be collected by the province and go toward its Green Fund , which it established in 2006 to support sustainable development and fight global warming . <p> There are valid reasons why even the most environmentally minded motorist might choose a conventional vehicle over an electric one . <p> In the end , the consumer should be free to make his or her own choice , and it 's unfair to penalize car makers for whatever that might be . <p> Instead , the government should stick to its incentives to increase sales . Already it offers rebates of up to $8,000 on the purchase of an electric vehicle , and has committed to spending $420 million on public charging stations and related measures . Monday , the premier announced that additional charging stations will be added along Highway 20 . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to help the government come up with more ways to create consumer demand .
