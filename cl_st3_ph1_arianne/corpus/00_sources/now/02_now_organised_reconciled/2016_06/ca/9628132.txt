<h> Official visit of the President of Mexico Enrique Pena Nieto in Quebec City <p> President of Mexico Enrique Pena Nieto speaks to the Chambre de Commerce et d'Industrie de Qu ? bec . <p> On Monday June 27th Mr. Enrique Pena Nieto President of Mexico and Mr. Philippe Couillard , Premier of Quebec , exchanged points of view on the different partnerships between Mexico and Quebec during a lunch hour organized by the Chambre de Commerce et d'Industrie de Quebec . The event was sold out and people from all over the province gathered to hear the leaders speak about the bilateral relationship of Quebec and Mexico . <p> The relationship between Quebec and Mexico has been growing strong since the early 1980 's when Quebec established its Official Office in the Federal District of Mexico . The main areas of collaboration between the two states are education , culture , research and innovation and economy . In 1982 , the creation of the Quebec-Mexico Work Group which will celebrate 35 years in 2017 helped consolidate the relationship in a more concrete matter . Over 600 Quebec companies have commercial trade with Mexico and import and export of goods represent close to 6 billion dollars every year . For commercial trade , the most developed sectors are aeronautic , with Bombardier for example , agrifood , energy related technologies in particular the development of hydroelectricity in Mexico and many more . According to President Pena Nieto and Premier Philippe Couillard , Mexico and Quebec make the environment and global climate change a priority throughout their trade agreements and bilateral relationship . <p> During this visit , the different ministers from Quebec and their homologues from Mexico discussed and agreed upon developing five new trade agreements on climate change , on the promotion of renewable energy , on higher education and training , on the protection of culture and on the development of digital culture .
