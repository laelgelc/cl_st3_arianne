<p> US vice-president Joe Biden said tonight he was " disappointed " by Britain 's decision to leave Europe . <p> Biden made the remarks in a speech he gave in the grounds of Dublin Castle during his six-day visit to Ireland . <p> Noting the importance of the US economic relationship with the European Union , Biden said : " As long standing friends of the United Kingdom , the United States respects their decision . <p> " It is not how we would have preferred it to be . " <p> Biden said that the US relationships with Britain and allies across Europe " are indispensable for America 's economic as well as national security " . <p> He added : " We will continue to work with our partners to navigate a new road ahead while continuing to promote stability , security , and prosperity around the world . " <p> Speaking about recent global unrest , particularly in the US , Biden listed mass migration , war , terrorism , infectious disease , and climate change , as forces that have led to " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the terrain for reactionary politicians and demagogues peddling xenophobia , nationalism , and isolationism , " he said . " Some politicians find it convenient to scapegoat immigrants rather than welcome them , to play to our fears rather than as Abraham Lincoln said ' appeal to our better angels ' . " <p> Biden said this attitude is present in not only the US , but also in Europe and other parts of the world . " It is un-American what we have seen . Its not who we have become and it is not who we are . " <p> Biden also noted the similarities of Ireland and the United States . " We have been both self critical and we are both self correcting .... our nations are places where hope reigns , " he said .
