<p> The relationship between energy and the environment will be one of the defining issues of the 21st Century . Climate change has led to growing calls for action to be taken to halt it . The most recent major effort was last year 's global Climate Change Conference in Paris . There , world leaders agreed to reduce emissions to stop global temperatures rising by 2 degrees centigrade , an ambitious target given current trends . Ireland pledged to do its bit by slashing greenhouse gases by at least 40pc by 2030 , a tall order too . <p> Renewable energy is not only central to reducing the risk of runaway climate change , it is also a matter of energy security . Ireland is one of the most dependent countries in Europe on imported energy .
