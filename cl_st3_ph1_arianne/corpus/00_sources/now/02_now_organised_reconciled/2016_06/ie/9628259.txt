If European voters swing heavily to the left there will not be an individual , satisfying moment of political theatre when left-wing forces take control . Instead , bit by bit as each country elected a left- wing government , the voting balance in the Council of Minsters would shift leftwards . <p> This lacks the political theatre of national politics but is democratic : European voters ultimately decide the make-up of EU institutions . <h> Significant powers <p> It is not clear the EU is any less accountable than national governments .
