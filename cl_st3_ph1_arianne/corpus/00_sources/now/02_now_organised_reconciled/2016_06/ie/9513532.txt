<h> Inconvenient then and now <h> Gore 's climate movie was timely when released ten years ago this month <p> How 's this for a deeply unpromising script idea : making a movie about a failed politician trailing around the world presenting wonkish slide shows on his laptop to mostly small audiences about , of all things , climate change ? <p> It hardly helped that the ex-politician in question , former US vice-president Al Gore was reviled across the political spectrum . Democrat supporters blamed him for gifting the White House to George W Bush with his incompetent run and premature concession in Florida in November 2000 , while Republicans hated him mostly for not being a Republican . <p> It might have been only a slight overstatement to call Gore a pariah in the mid-2000s .
