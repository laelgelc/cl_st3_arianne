The Republic has lost its status as the country that contributes the most to humanity in the latest Good Country Index . File photograph : Cathal McNaughton/Reuters <p> The Republic has lost its status as the country that contributes the most to humanity . <p> It has been overtaken by our Euro 2016 rivals Sweden as the " goodest " country in the world , according to the latest Good Country Index . <p> The author of the biannual index , Simon Anholt , said the differences between the countries in the top 10 are minute and , as a small country , the State is subject to volatile changes . <p> The Good Country Index was founded by Mr Anholt , a British government adviser , and seeks to measure how countries contribute to the global good . <p> Some 35 different UN @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ culture , peace and security , climate change and health and equality . <p> There was surprise and puzzlement in 2014 when the Republic topped the first Good Country Index , outranking 130 other countries . <p> The State claimed the number one spot in the equality and prosperity category and was ranked fourth when it came to world order , seventh in culture , and ninth in the health and wellbeing category in that ranking . <p> The results were somewhat surprising , as the data used was from 2010 , during the depths of the recession . <p> Mr Anholt joked that the " Irish people were beastly to me " following the 2014 index and many emailed him pointing out the country 's failures . <p> He said many Irish people confused being a " good " country with being the best , which would be solely down to internal factors . <p> He said Irish people suffered from " a severe case of Groucho Marx syndrome " , by not wanting to be part of any club that would rank them as first in the world . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ figures from 2011 , the Republic ranked 19th in terms of its contribution to science and technology , 22nd in the culture category , 51st in international peace and security , 17th in the world order , 23rd in prosperity and equality and 17th in health and wellbeing . <p> Climate <p> However , there was some good news in the latest index , with the State moving from 45th to 13th in terms of its positive contribution to the planet and the climate . <p> Mr Anholt said the improving economy may be the contributing factor in the State 's drop from 1st to 23rd in terms of its contribution to prosperity and equality . <p> He said the improving economy meant that the State 's contribution to overseas aid was smaller as a total of GDP , which led to the fall .
