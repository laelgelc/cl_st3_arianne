<p> Britain 's EU referendum next Thursday is a momentous decision that could have huge implications for the country -- and the continent -- for years to come . <p> The choice we must make looks simple enough , but it masks a difficult and complex decision that entails weighing an imperfect status quo against an unknowable alternative future . <p> Obviously the wilful half-truths , wild extrapolations and baseless assurances both sides have been peddling have not helped .
