<p> In the summer and early autumn of 2014 Gordon Brown made a Scottish patriot 's case for rejecting Scottish independence and remaining within the United Kingdom , and did so compellingly . Now in this new book he sets himself to make the patriotic British case , which is also the patriotic Scottish one , for remaining within the European Union , and does so persuasively and cogently once again . <p> He has an advantage over most commentators , an advantage which enables him to speak with unusual authority ; he has a deep knowledge of the workings of the EU . From 1997 to 2010 , first as Chancellor of the Exchequer and then as Prime Minister , he was at the heart of Europe , involved in all debates and discussions concerning its functioning and development . He knows from experience that Britain has a powerful voice in Europe and can influence debate and win arguments there . <p> This is a fair book . Brown is not blind to the attractions of leaving . As Chancellor he kept us out of the euro , though Tony Blair was in favour of us joining . Nevertheless he successfully argued that Britain should participate in the discussions of the Eurozone countries , because what they decided was of our concern too . As Prime Minister he took the lead in concerting the European response to the global financial crisis . <p> His argument now is straightforward . In a globalised , inter-dependent world it is in our interest to remain part of the largest transnational trading block in the world . Britain may be an island -- more correctly archipelago -- but isolation is dangerous and anything but splendid today . The EU is not going to become a federal state . It will remain an association of nation-states , in which we collaborate with some countries in certain areas , with others in other ones . Much is said about the democratic deficit in the EU , but he points out that the most important decisions are made in the Council of Ministers , by the elected heads of national governments . The Commission -- the EU 's civil service -- is not the driving force it was 20 years ago . What we have is a United Europe of States , not a United States of Europe .
