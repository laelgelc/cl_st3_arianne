<p> Planet Aid applauds Notre Dame Global Adaptation Index for a recent article and its larger work focusing on climate change 's disproportionate impact on the world 's poor . <p> Elkridge , Maryland ( PRWEB ) June 09 , 2016 <p> Planet Aid applauds and agrees with the conclusions of a recent article by Joyce Coffee , managing director of the Notre Dame Global Adaptation Index ( ND-GAIN ) , that describes the disproportionate impacts of climate change across the economic spectrum . The article , " The World 's Poor Face an Unequal Climate Risk -- Here 's What Needs to Happen , " indicates that in 2015 alone the African continent faced a slew of extreme weather events that impacted more than 20 million people . <p> ND-GAIN calculated that least-developed countries have a 10-fold greater chance of being affected by a climate disaster than that of wealthy countries and have a relatively minimal capacity to adapt . <p> Events related to extreme weather resulted in damages in excess of $2.5 billion in Africa last year .
