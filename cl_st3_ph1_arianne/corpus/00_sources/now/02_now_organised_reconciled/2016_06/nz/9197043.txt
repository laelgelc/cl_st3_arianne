<h> NZ helps boost housing - in Kiribati <p> A New Zealand-funded housing development in Kiribati aims to ease overcrowding on the main island of Tarawa . <p> The Kiribati capital and most populated area , South Tarawa , consists of several islets , connected by a series of causeways .
