The differences , though , are enormous with the United States maize ( corn ) grower producing crops that are five times per hectare more productive than in Africa . <p> Worth thinking about is that water security for many countries is becoming more and more of an issue for governments -- 54 per cent of India faces continually high to extremely high water stress . <p> In many ways big corporations control much of the world 's foods . Europe is presently rethinking parts of its resistance to GM foods . <p> We can be sure that once this becomes mainstream the large companies will make their presence felt . <p> About 70 per cent of the world 's agricultural land is devoted to raising animals . The major threats to agricultural production worldwide would appear to be population Increase , climate change and water stress . <p> One person out of every nine people in the world today has to live on less than $1 per day and what is the major cause of hunger -- poverty . <p> How many different weed species around the world have developed resistance to common herbicides ?
