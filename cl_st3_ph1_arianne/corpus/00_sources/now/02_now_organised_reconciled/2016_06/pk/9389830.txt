<p> LAHORE : Two young Pakistanis Usman and Zainab have been selected for Queen 's young Leaders Award . The award is going to be presented by Queen Elizabeth during a marvellous ceremony at Buckingham Palace next week , coinciding with the Queen 's 90th birthday . <p> 28-year-old Muhammad Usman from Punjab is selected in acknowledgement of his keenness to bring education to all . He designed an educational programme called " Back to Life Edutainment for Street Children " which he upheld through social media and managed to inspire over 500 young people to voluntarily implement the project in four cities . <p> While 26-year-old Zainab Bibi is the first Pakistani female to win the award for her work and research in turning utilised tissue papers into bio-diesel . Her outstanding work and efforts for the environment is a source of pride for the nation that is regarded as to suppress women in the society . <p> They are selected from a competitive process where thousands of young people from all over the Commonwealth applied to be a Queen 's Young Leader . 60 young people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ make a lasting impression in their communities , are selected for this award .
