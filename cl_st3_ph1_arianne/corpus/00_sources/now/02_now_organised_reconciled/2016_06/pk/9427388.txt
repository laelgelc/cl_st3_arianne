<p> ISLAMABAD : The National Assembly on Saturday approved as many as 76 demands for grant totaling Rs 1.226 trillion , for which no cut motion was moved by any parliamentarian . <p> On Monday , the demands of grant with cut motion would be moved when the proceedings resume at 14:00 hours . Federal Minister for Finance Senator Ishaq Dar presented 76 demands for grant of Rs 1,226,686,098,000 before the House for the fiscal year 2016-17 . <p> The National Assembly approved grants worth Rs 2,921,119,000 for the Defence Production Division , Rs 41,148,525,000 for the Planning and Development Division and Rs 51,307,296,000 for the States and Frontier Regions Division . The House also approved grants worth Rs 11,600,778,000 for the Kashmir Affairs and Gilgit Baltistan Division and Rs 865,189,625,000 for the Defence Division . <p> The National Assembly also approved demand of grants worth Rs 13,113,665,000 for the Communication Division , Rs 8,448,123,000 for the Information Division and Rs 1,572,901,000 for the Climate Change Division . The House approved demands of grant of Rs 113,000,000,000 for the Railways Division and Rs 10,552,308,000 for the Housing Division @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ demands so the speaker just put the motions before the House to give the final approval . The remaining 72 demands of grant for which the parliamentarians have suggested cut motions would be put before the House on Monday . Other demands pertained to the commerce , culture , economic affairs , education , environment , finance , food and agriculture , foreign affairs , health , human rights , information technology , inter-provincial coordination and other divisions .
