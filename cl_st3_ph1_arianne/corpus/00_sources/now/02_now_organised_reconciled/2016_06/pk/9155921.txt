<h> Climate Change Authority being set up to face environmental challenges , Senate told <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid on Thursday informed the Senate that Climate said that the government was pursuing multipronged strategy including short , medium and long-term measures to meet the challenge of climate change . <p> " We have prepared a national forest policy in coordination with the provinces in order to enhance the forest cover in different parts of the country , " the minister said . <p> Zahid Hamid said the government has also launched Green Pakistan
