<p> CAPE TOWN - The Western Cape government has set aside at least R28 million to assist emerging farmers amid a severe drought . <p> This is one of the resolutions that came out of a two-day dialogue hosted by the provincial Agriculture Department this week . <p> The discussions involved Agri Wes-Cape , the African Farmers ' Association and academics . Talks were aimed at finding solutions to mitigate the impact of the drought . <p> Economic Opportunities MEC Alan Winde says it 's important to keep the new farmers afloat through the difficult period . <p> " They do n't have the long-term experience nor the asset buildup to financially carry them through so , for example , we have R28 million to support emerging farmers . " <p> He says they expect the dry season to continue . <p> " People might think it 's raining but we 're in the middle of the drought and dam levels are half of what they were last year .
