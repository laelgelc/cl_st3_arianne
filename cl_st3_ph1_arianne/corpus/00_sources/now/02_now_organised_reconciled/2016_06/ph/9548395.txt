<h> Taklub takes the spotlight at 39th Urian Awards <p> With only the most important prize of the evening remaining to be announced , Taklub came from behind and walked off with the Best Picture trophy at the 39th Gawad Urian Awards on Wednesday night at the Kia Theater in Cubao , Quezon City . <p> Winners ' circle . Gawad Urian top winners in a photo op after the awarding ceremony <p> The Brillante Mendoza ensemble drama about grieving survivors of typhoon Yolanda , topbilled by Superstar Nora Aunor , had to wait right up to the end of the show to take its first and only one trip to the stage , upstaging Jerrold Tarog 's historical epic Heneral Luna , which had grabbed the most awards including best director before the shocking final result of the night . <p> Best Actor John Lloyd Cruz <p> Taklub , which was screened at the Un Certain Regard section of 2015 Cannes Film Festival and awarded the Ecumenical Jury Prize-Special Mention for its sensitive portrayal of individuals and communities working for life in the midst of suffering and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' official entry to the 2016 Oscars Best Foreign Film category .
