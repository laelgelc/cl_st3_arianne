Mercado <p> TUGUEGARAO CITY , Cagayan , June 15 ( PIA ) -- The Home Development Mutual Fund ( HDMF ) or Pag-IBIG Fund Office here has informed all employers in the region to remit membership contributions and loan payments using the electronic system of the Fund 's partner-banks . <p> Lilia Angoluan , Pag-Ibig Area Head , said all employers are now required to pay premiums and loans through the Fund 's accredited partner-banks via the Electronic Payment and Collection Facility ( EPCF ) platform . <p> She said the EPCF is an electronic payment system that gives employees a facility to remit their contributions or premiums and short term loan amortizations through the banks with electronic payment channel . <p> " The EPCF platform provides employers with an accurate recording , faster transactions , payments can be made anytime and anywhere , and its saves time and transportation costs , " Angoluan said . <p> Angoluan advised employers that starting July 1 , 2016 , the Fund will no @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ loans and short-term loans , except for housing loans where employees or members can still transact directly at any Pag-Ibig Fund Office . <p> She said this is because of the implementation of the EPCF platform in partnership with the Fund 's various accredited banks in compliance with Republic Act No. 8792 ( E-Commerce Act of 2000 ) and Pag-IBIG Fund Circular 355 . <p> She said the circular mandates all government and private employers with at least 10 employees to remit membership savings and loan amortizations through the e-collection facility .
