<h> National government budget surplus at P55 B in April 2016 <p> June 30 , 2016 <p> MANILA , June 30 -- The national government posted a surplus of P55.0 billion in April 2016 on the back of strong revenue collections , bringing the year-to-date deficit down to P57.5 billion . Net of interest payments , the national government 's primary balance for January-April 2016 stood at a surplus of P59.9 billion . April 2016 surplus stood at P69.8 billion . <p> National government revenues for the month amounted to P246.6 billion , 18% or P37.5 billion higher than recorded in April 2015 , while expenditures totaled P191.6 billion , 22% or P35.1 billion wider than year-ago figures . <p> Finance Secretary Cesar V. Purisima said , " I am pleased to report a strong finish on both the revenue and expenditure sides of the Republic 's balance sheet . Consistently solid fiscal performance has put the nation on its firmest fiscal footing in history .
