<p> TWO of the country 's most famous diving spots were placed under official government protection due to the threat of being destroyed by tourist activities . <p> Environment Secretary Ramon Paje said he had declared Sabang Bay in Puerto Galera in Oriental Mindoro and Coron Bay in Palawan as water quality management areas ( WQMAs ) to boost efforts to protect their rich marine biodiversity from the impact of tourism . <p> Sabang Bay and Coron Bay are world-class dive sites with great visibility and masses of underwater life .
