<p> LEGAZPI CITY , Philippines -- Albay will host the joint humanitarian program " Pacific Partnership , " involving some 1,000 troops including from the US Pacific Fleet and Australian Navy and the world 's largest hospital ship , the USNS Mercy , from June 27 to July 11 . <p> First held after the 2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and its deadly tsunami , Pacific Partnership has become an annual event between the Pacific Fleet and regional governments and military forces , along with humanitarian and nongovernmental organizations . The exercise aims to build inter-operability during disasters and strengthen security ties . <p> The activity in Albay is held under the Philippine-US Mutual Defense Treaty . <p> The foreign troops will be joined by the Philippine Naval Forces in South Luzon , and will be received by Albay Governor Joey Salceda . <p> Salceda said Pacific Partnership provides an opportunity to enhance goodwill and cooperation among the region 's military forces and governments , needed during disaster relief operations , and at the same time deliver humanitarian and engineering assistance to local stakeholders . <p> It also opens @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wealth . A UNESCO World Biosphere Reserve , Albay is also a UN global model for Climate Change Adaptation and Disaster Risk Reduction , and a pioneer in novel strategies for disaster resiliency . <p> The USNS Mercy is slated to treat some 4,000 local patients onboard and in designated areas around the province . <p> Pacific Partnership engineers are also scheduled to build or improve school rooms , water deposits and handwashing stations , and undertake beach cleanups . The troops and local resource persons will also conduct seminars on DRR , sanitation , environment protection and sports events . <p> USNS Mercy 's last visit to the country was to Roxas City after super typhoon Yolanda struck in 2013.
