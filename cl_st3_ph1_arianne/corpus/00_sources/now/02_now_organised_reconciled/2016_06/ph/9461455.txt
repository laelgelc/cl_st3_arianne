<p> MANILA -- Many parts of Asia have been gripped by searing temperatures , and the worst drought in decades . Millions of people face shortages of food or water , leading to the loss of lives , livelihoods , crops and livestock . As water shortages depress productivity , reduce energy output at hydro plants , and cut food exports , economies are suffering . <p> To find relief , we must look to the skies -- but not in the way one might expect .
