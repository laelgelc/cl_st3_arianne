<p> Tacloban City - According to the Department of Health ( DOH ) Region 8 office , which covers Eastern Visayas , the effect of El Ni ? o or the long dry spell , was a factor that contributed to the scarcity of potable water , and was seen as a reason for numerous cases of diarrhea in some towns of the region . <p> As of Sunday afternoon , DOH 8 , through Regional Director Minerva P. Molon , had recorded 3,495 cases and 44 deaths , up from 33 deaths Friday . <p> In Samar , there were 32 deaths , 8 in Northern Samar , 3 in Eastern Samar and 1 case in Leyte . <p> " We started this record at the onset of the dry spell , the intensification of which could be partly attributed to climate change . The supply of water from traditional sources diminished , and so people resorted to drawing water from surface sources , such as rivers and streams , which were more prone to being contaminated , " Molon explained . <p> DOH has called a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which was attended by stakeholders like the Department of the Interior and Local Government , Department of Social Welfare and Development , the Bureau of Fire Protection , Department of Education , Philippine Health Insurance ( PhilHealth ) , the United Nations Children 's Fund , the Red Cross , Plan Philippines and Save the Children . <p> The participants in the meeting discussed the reduced water supply during the El Ni ? o event , the limitations of the local water system distribution , lack of manpower to monitor water quality , households not practicing safe water handling practices , and the need for laboratory tests to monitor possible contamination of drinking water . <p> Director Molon announced that Oxfam adopted the municipality of Calbiga to help them in their water problem . Of three towns ( Catbalogan , Calbiga and Sta . Rita ) in Samar having an outbreak , the cases in Catbalogan and Calbiga are now controlled . <p> Meanwhile , the Municipality of Basey is now under their observation as it recorded a high number of cases . <p> Of the 44 who died , many @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was a 75-year old . <p> " Yung mga deaths , mostly dead on arrival kasi malalayo ang kanilang mga lugar sa mga RHU . May namamatay din sa kani-kanilang bahay nalang ( Many of the fatalities had been dead when brought in , partly because of their remote location far from the rural health unit . Some had died in their homes ) , " she added . <p> She is calling the public not to wait for many days before taking action when their children are experiencing diarrhea : " They must bring them to the hospital right away for proper medication . " <p> Meanwhile , DoH has also been distributing toilet bowls in the region for their Zero Open Defecation Program . Of the 833,888 households in Eastern Visayas , 621,245 households have their own toilets , corresponding to 74 percent of the total households . The health agency continues this effort to achieve the 90% target .
