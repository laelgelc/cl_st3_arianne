FILE PHOTO OF FLOODS IN MAGUINDANAO <p> MANILA - The Philippine College of Physicians supports the call of other groups to craft and implement policies that will ease the burden of climate change impact on the health of the young , the poor , and other vulnerable sectors . <p> In a news release , the group on Monday warned that the Philippines is one of the countries most vulnerable to the impact of climate change with stronger typhoons and incessant rainfall that cause relentless flooding , and unpredictable extreme weather pattern that leads to dry spell and droughts . <p> " We see the devastation of homes and properties forces victims to evacuation centers , makeshift structures , tents , that are unfavorable for healthy living conditions . Poor sanitation and the variability of the weather after disasters give rise to respiratory and gastrointestinal illnesses and vector-borne @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said Dr. Nenita Collantes , PCP president . <p> Collantes said climate change burdens and threatens the systems on which human life depends -- air , water , food , and security -- virtually all the fundamental determinants of the population 's health . <p> " Through PCP 's Crisis Preparedness Management committee , we continue to commit to respond to areas hit by typhoons through our network and resources . We support the call of the health sector to the new government to strengthen the country 's health systems to better uphold public health in disaster risk reduction management plans and mechanisms , " she added . <p> The Philippine College of Physicians Foundation adopted the National Action Plan of the Climate Change Commission for 2011-2028 , particularly the programs , Knowledge and Capacity development , Biodiversity Protection and Preservation , and Human Health Security . <p> " The PCP Foundation initiated the filing of House Bill 4981 in the last Congress , proposing Nov. 8 as National Day of Rising ( Climate Change Day ) . This is also an attempt to set a Guinness world Record of 200,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a beaming passion by public health advocates to respond to the call of " State of Climate Emergency " in the country , " explained Dr. Anthony Leachon , president of the PCP Foundation . <p> According to Leachon , the PCP Foundation launched its climate caravan in cooperation with local government units and other relevant agencies through school-to-school climate education . <p> The health organization developed its own climate resource material , climate change " black book " and lecture template . <p> The PCP , PCP Foundation , and Health Care without Harm ( Asia ) urge the newly-elected leaders to heed the health community 's proposals to intensify its policies to address climate change effects on people 's health , strengthen health systems to protect public health , and lead the transition to healthy energy sources adopt green and sustainable practices in health facilities .
