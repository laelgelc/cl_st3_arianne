<p> If you despair of Australia 's lacklustre climate policies , you might take heart from the Greens ' stated goal of limiting global warming to 1.5 ? . But are the party 's own policies up to the job ? <p> Shortly after announcing this target late last year , the Greens launched an ambitious renewables policy , promising to achieve 90% renewable electricity by 2030 and save money in the process . <p> But as wonderful as it sounds , even this plan is insufficient to meet a 1.5 ? target . <p> The arithmetic is simple . According to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , to preserve a two-thirds chance of avoiding 1.5 ? warming , future carbon dioxide emissions must not exceed 200 billion tonnes . As annual global emissions are now around 40 billion tonnes , we will blow the budget within five years . <p> Now let 's suppose that the entire world achieves the Greens ' emissions reduction targets of 60-80% by 2030 ( relative to 2000 levels ) , and 100% by 2040 .
