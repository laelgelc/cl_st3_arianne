<h> Species you will only find in WA ! <p> Though you might not find all of them in your backyard , these organisms are native and unique to WA . Some are cute , others are funny , some are just plain weird . Read on and get to know your neighbours better this WA Day ! <h> 1 . Dibbler <p> Since 1997 about 809 dibblers have been successfully introduced at Escape Island near Jurien Bay , Peniup Creek Reserve near Jerramungup , Waychinicup National Park near Albany , and Whiteman Park in Perth . Credit : Perth Zoo <p> The dibbler is only the size of a chocolate bar , making it one of our cutest little marsupials ( though it is hard to play favourites ) . Land clearing , predation and climate change are responsible for low dibbler numbers , but with breeding programs coordinated by the likes of Perth Zoo their future is not as tenuous as it once was . Western Swamp Tortoise <p> This adorable little tortoise is one of WA 's most critically endangered reptiles , but we 're not going to let it go without a fight ! It was actually thought to be extinct until its rediscovery in 1950 , and at the turn of the decade there were only forty individuals left in the wild , all in WA nature reserves . But , with multiple Australasian universities focusing on this little guy 's wellbeing , I 'd say they 're in safe hands ! Credit : Dillettantiquity <p> Numbats have been struggling since pests such as cats and foxes were introduced .
