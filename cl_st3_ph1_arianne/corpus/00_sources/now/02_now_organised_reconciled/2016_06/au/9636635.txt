We are investigating these possibilities now . <h> Choosing between green and cool roofs <p> Green and cool roofs each offer advantages and disadvantages . Installed on a large scale , green roofs could add a substantial amount of green space to cities and provide habitat for plants and insects across urban areas . Additionally , plants can improve urban air quality by removing solid and gaseous pollutants from the air . <p> On the other hand , cool roofs do not require watering and cost less than green roofs to install and maintain . Green roofs also increase local humidity : moisture is released when plants are watered , and plants release more water to the atmosphere from their leaves . <p> Many other issues will also influence decisions about where to install green and cool roofs . One important consideration is environmental justice .
