<h> Film4Climate Global Video Competition : Are you a young filmmaker ? Here 's your chance to make it big <p> As a part of UN 's Sustainable Development Goals , the World Bank Group 's Connect4Climate program , the United Nations and Vulcan Productions , in collaboration with the Italian energy company Enel , have organised the Film4Climate Global Video Competition . <p> The competition - which will be held between 20 June and 17 September - is aimed at promoting sustainability in the creative industries by engaging young people in the fight against climate change . <p> This competition aims to provide a global platform for filmmakers between the age group of 14 to 35 from across the world . <p> Applicants are invited to create a Public Service Advertisement ( PSA ) of less than 1 minute or a short film of 1-5 minutes about climate change .
