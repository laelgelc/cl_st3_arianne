<p> According to a report by the International Energy Agency ( IEA ) , around 1.26 million electric cars were produced in 2015 . In 2005 , the number of electric cars in existence numbered only a few hundred . The key drivers of this growth are China and the United States , which have been the biggest adopters of the electric car trend . <p> China has the highest growth of electric car use in the world . In Q4 2015 , Electric Vehicles ( EVs ) alone took 1.4 per cent of China 's entire auto market . New Energy Vehicles ( NEVs ) , which includes hydrogen fuel cell vehicles , hybrid vehicles , and other " alternative fuel " vehicles along with electric cars , reached 188,700 sales that same year . Still a small number compared to regular vehicles , but an impressive 223 per cent increase since 2014 . <p> The United States already has 400,000 EVs on the road .
