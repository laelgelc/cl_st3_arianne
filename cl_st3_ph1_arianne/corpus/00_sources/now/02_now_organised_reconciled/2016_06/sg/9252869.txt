<h> Fighting climate change at any age <h> A new board game to teach youths about climate change took the spotlight at the recent National Climate Change Youth Conference 2016 , challenging participants to think about the trade-offs in balancing economic development and sustainability . <p> Participants take on the role of city planners in the board game ' Broken Cities ' , and , in the process , learn how to achieve the careful balance between urban development and environmental sustainability .
