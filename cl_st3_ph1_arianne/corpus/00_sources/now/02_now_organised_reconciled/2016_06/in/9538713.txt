<h> India gets more H-1B visas despite fee hike : Richard Verma <p> Hyderabad : India continues to get the " lion 's share " of H-1B visas from the US government despite the fee hike , US ambassador to India Richard Verma said on Friday . <p> " India continues to receive the lion 's share of H-1B and L1 and even after the fee increase , they continue to get 70% of those H-1B visas , " Verma said on the sidelines of The Future is Now : From COP21 to Reality conference . <p> " We understand the concern about the fee hike . I think there is an ongoing conversation . We also know this is an important part of travel and commercial enterprise in the US . And again , there is an increase in the number of visas issued ; in fact , there is a slight increase , " he added . <p> The US , under the 9/11 Health and Compensation Act , has imposed a special fee of $4,000 on certain categories of H-1B visas and $4,500 on L1 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ paying between $8,000 and $10,000 per H-1B visa as per the hike . <p> According to Nasscom , this is expected to have an impact of about $400 million annually on India 's technology sector . <p> Earlier in his speech , Verma said the ongoing deforestation and poor land management is responsible for nearly a quarter of the world 's greenhouse gas emissions as each day , greenhouse gases emitted by human activities trap the same amount of heat energy as would be released by 400,000 atomic bombs . <p> " Climate change is not just an environmental challenge ; it is a national security issue .
