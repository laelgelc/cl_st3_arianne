<h> Allow GM in food crops , but with strong safeguards : ICAR chief <p> TRILOCHAN MOHAPATRA Director-General , Indian Council of Agricultural Research <p> Trilochan Mohapatra has taken over as the Director-General of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research ( ICAR ) , the central agency that takes care of research in a variety of crops . In Hyderabad last week to attend a Regional Committee meeting of the Council , Mohapatra spoke about the importance of allowing GM research in food crops with necessary safeguards . With weathermen forecasting normal rainfall , he said States should make submergence-tolerant paddy varieties available . Excerpts : <p> How has the kharif season been progressing ? And , what are your expectations ? <p> As weathermen predict a good monsoon , we expect a good kharif this year . Good rainfall also means that some areas might get more rainfall . The States must be ready with submergence-tolerant paddy varieties . A large number of varieties can survive up to 15 days of submergence .
