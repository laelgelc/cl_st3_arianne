Indian scientists are concerned over the pressure building up for years in the Kashmir Reasi fault-line near Jammu , which could endanger the lives of over a million people . <p> Geological investigations have been on since the 2005 earthquake when over 75,000 people were killed in India and Pakistan . <p> Mild tremors are recorded every day the world over .
