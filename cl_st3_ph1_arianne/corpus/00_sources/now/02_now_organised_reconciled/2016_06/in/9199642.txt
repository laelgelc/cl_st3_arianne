<h> ISRO , NASA and other space agencies of other nations to fight climate change together <p> ISRO , NASA , CNES , JAXA and several other space-faring nations have agreed to share satellite data to study and monitor the effects of climate change induced by green house gasses . In picture : Indian Space Research Organization ( ISRO ) scientists and engineers monitor the movements of India 's Mars orbiter at their Spacecraft Control Center in the southern Indian city of Bangalore November 27 , 2013Reuters <p> In a meeting held on Friday in New Delhi , conducted by the Indian Space Research Organisation ( ISRO ) and French space agency Centre National d ' ? tudes Spatiales ( CNES ) , over 60 nations with earth-observing satellites have agreed , for the first time , to share collected data to be used to study and monitor human-induced green house gases and their effects on the envirnment . <p> " The world 's space agencies decided to establish ' an independent , international system ' to centralise data from their Earth-observing satellites through the ' New Delhi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 2016 , " according to a statement released by ISRO on Saturday . <p> According to the statement , the COP21 climate conference held in Paris last December was a wake-up call that brought about this initiative . " Without satellites , the reality of global warming would not have been recognised and the subsequent historic agreement at the United Nations headquarters in New York on April 22 , 2016 , would not have been signed , " the statement said . <p> It added that of the 50 climate change indicators being measured , 26 could only be measured from space . The 26 indicators include sea levels , sea ice and the concentration of greenhouse gases across different layers of the atmosphere . <p> " It is overwhelming to see the unilateral support of all space agencies to use space inputs for monitoring climate change " said ISRO Chairman AS Kiran Kumar in the statement . <p> The network of satellites will be used to generate large volumes of " big space data , " which will be studied over time . The statement noted that the key @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nations are following through in their efforts to curb emissions . The effects can apparently only be accurately monitored through satellites . <p> The 60 space organisations that have signed the New Delhi Declaration include the National Aeronautics and Space Administration ( NASA ) from the U.S. and the Japan Aerospace eXploration Agency ( JAXA ) .
