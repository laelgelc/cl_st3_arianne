<h> Probe sought into samiti 's role in Kerala tusk trade <p> Involvement of forest protection group leaders in anti-wildlife activities has been alleged . <h> The VSS is a Forest Department initiative launched to ensure public participation in forest management . <p> The arrest of Vana Samrakshana Samiti president at Gurunathanmannu in the Ranni Forest Division for illegal possession and transaction of elephant tusks has thrown light on the involvement of forest protection group leaders in anti-wildlife activities in Kerala , Heritage Animal Task Force ( HATF ) secretary V.K. Venkitachalam has said . <p> In a letter to the Director of the Project Elephant in New Delhi , he called for a detailed probe into the killings of wild elephants and the transaction of their tusks with the help of VSS office-bearers in the State . <p> Two days ago , Gurunathanmannu VSS president K.C. Sabu and his accomplice Rajendran of Urumbini were arrested by the flying squad of the Forest Department from the former 's house on charge of possessing two elephant tusks . Department sources said the tusks were two years old .
