<p> New Delhi : With India 's membership of the exclusive Nuclear Suppliers Group ( NSG ) set to come up before the bloc 's plenary meeting in Seoul later this month , India may have one more chance to persuade China to give up its opposition to India 's candidature . <p> This is expected to be at the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organisation ( SCO ) meeting in Tashkent where both Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chinese president Xi Jinping are expected to be present . The meeting of the SCO , which is a Eurasian political , economic , and military organisation , founded in 2001 in Shanghai by the leaders of China , Kazakhstan , Kyrgyzstan , Russia , Tajikistan and Uzbekistan is to take place on 23-24 June in Tashkent , Uzbekistan . The NSG works on the basis of consensus and therefore all members have to agree on admission of new members . <p> India which got a one-time waiver from the NSG to buy atomic power plants and related technologies from the global market , is looking for NSG membership so as to access an entire range of sensitive knowhow from NSG members . Formed in 1974 in the aftermath of India 's first nuclear test , NSG counts nuclear weapon states the US , Russia , China , Britain and France as members .
