<h> Rich stock of ant species in State <h> Survey of ants at the PTR documents the existence of 91 species <p> A survey of ants in the Periyar Tiger Reserve ( PTR ) has brought to light the diversity of the species in the region . The highlight of the survey includes the rediscovery of three genus groups , Dracula Ant , Leptanilla , and Myrmopteras , in the State . <p> The four-day survey from May 21 , part of a year-long survey conducted with the support of the Travancore Natural History Society 's ( TNHS ) ant research group , documented existence of 91 species . <p> PTR Deputy Director Krishan Kumar said an earlier survey had put the number of ant species in the reserve at 31 . " This is the first phase of the survey on ants to be held systematically in the reserve , " he said , adding that the areas assessed were Vallakadavu , Kozhikanam , Kochupampa , and Gavi . <p> Ecological value <p> " Ants have great ecological value . The diversity of ants in a habitat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ indicators and responds to ecological changes . Climate change could be analysed by monitoring ant diversity along ecological and attitudinal gradients . They are also an important part of the food chain , pollination , and nutrient cycles , " Mr. Kumar said . <p> He said the data on ant diversity were fragmented and needed to be microanalysed for assessing environmental and climate changes in the region . <p> Mr. Kumar said the standard field methodology was being used for the survey . Canopy ants were collected using canopy beat method , tree bark and trunks observed for arboreal ants , leaf-litter was assessed by tray sifting , and soil-core was analysed using Winkler and Berlese funnel method . Active sampling was done for forest floor ants . <p> The total ant species discovered in the State so far was nearly 250 and it was expected that some species endemic to the Western Ghats would be discovered once the survey was over . <p> The survey found ant species Harpegnathos , Paratopula , Strumygenys , and Gnamptogenys in the sanctuary .
