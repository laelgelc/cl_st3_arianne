" <p> Salma Dam , which was inaugurated by PM Modi and Afghanistan President Ashraf Ghani earlier this month , has been built by Wapcos , an Indian Mini Ratna I. <p> Situated on the upper reaches of Hari Rud River in Herat province , Salma Dam project involved construction of a 107.5 meter high earth and rock -- fill dam and a 42 MW power house with three units of 14 MW each . It also has a provision for releasing water for irrigation of 75,000 hectares of land . <p> At over $275 million , this is the most expensive of India 's infrastructure projects in the region .
