<h> Supreme Court to hear plea against culling of Nilgai , monkeys and wild boar <p> New Delhi , Jun 15 ( PTI ) : The Supreme Court has agreed to hear a plea challenging the Union government 's decision to allow the culling of Nilgai , rhesus monkeys and wild boar populations in some states . <p> The plea for urgent hearing was mentioned before the vacation bench of Justices Adarsh Kumar Goel and L. Nageswara Rao , which agreed to list the matter during the week . <p> Senior advocate Anand Grover appearing for the petitioner animal activist Gauri Maulekhi said the Centre does not have power to issue such notification . <p> He said that people were being hired for mass killing of these three animals , which have been declared as vermins . <p> The first notification of Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , dated December 1 , 2015 , declared Nilgai and wild boar as vermin in some districts of Bihar for one year . <p> The second notification of the ministry dated February 3 , 2016 , declared wild @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one-year period . <p> The third notification , issued on May 24 , 2016 , declared rhesus macaque ( monkey ) to be vermin in some districts of Himachal Pradesh . <p> " The impugned notifications have been passed in absolute disregard of the human-wildlife conflict plaguing the country and without any scientific survey backing them , " the plea said . <p> It said that once an animal is declared vermin , it is deprived of the protection provided to wild animals by the Wildlife Protection Act . <p> " The State is no longer responsible for safeguarding the life and well-being of such animals . The indiscriminate killing of these animals will have a detrimental effect on the food chain and in turn lead to an ecological imbalance , " the plea said . <p> It said the provisions of the Act " authorises the government to permit mindless slaughter of protected wildlife without any inquiry or investigation into the need for declaring protected species of wildlife as vermin for purpose of slaughter .
