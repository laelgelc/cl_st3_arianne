<p> Judith 's father Denzil at their home in Kolkata on Friday . ( Express Photo by Subham Dutta ) <p> It was a sleepless night for the D'Souza family in Kolkata 's Entally colony . At 1.30 am on Thursday , Denzil D'Souza received a call from the Indian Embassy in Kabul informing him that his daughter has " in all probability been abducted " . In the morning , he was rushed to the hospital for dialysis . <p> With 40-year-old Judith abducted from Kabul , her family -- her ageing parents and older sister Agnes -- are in shock . The stairs leading to their small , first-floor apartment have been invaded by mediapersons . A cross is nailed to the dark mahogany front door , inscribed with the words : " Peace to all who enter .
