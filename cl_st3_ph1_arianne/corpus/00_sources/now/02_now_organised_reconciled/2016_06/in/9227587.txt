That is the gap this Forum is expected to close . You have a critical mission to accomplish , " he underlined . <p> Convened by the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) , the two-day Forum aims " to ensure a life of dignity for all , with greater shared prosperity on a clean and healthy planet . " But science , technology and innovation must not be limited to SDG 17 . They cut across all the SDGs as an important element of implementation . " <p> He cautioned that science , technology and innovation can not be confined to the use of new technologies or software , but rather , innovation is a mindset and an attitude : " It means questioning assumptions , rethinking established systems and procedures , and introducing new strategies .
