But we 'll find out , " Obama said . <p> As per the latest Washington Post/ ABC News poll two in three Americans view that Trump is unqualified to lead the country . <p> " I think it 's important for Democrats , progressives , moderates , people who care about our traditions , who care about pluralism , who care @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change is real , who think that we have to reform our immigration system in an intelligent way , who believe in women 's equality and equality for the LGBT community -- I think it 's important for those of us not to be complacent , not to be smug , " he said . <p> " The one thing I 've tried to do during the course of my presidency is to take seriously the objections and the criticisms and the concerns of people who did n't vote for me , " he said . <p> Obama said the core of his message has been that the country is better when together . <p> " I do not believe in stoking divisions and scapegoating .
