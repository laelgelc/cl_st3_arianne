IN SCHOOL <h> Green film with message of hope <h> French docufilm Demain ( Tomorrow ) has had audiences on its feet because of positive story on preventing climate change <p> When the French actress Melanie Laurent read a now famous 2012 paper by leading scientists warning that the Earth 's climate was reaching a tipping point from which it may never recover , she sat down and wept . <p> " I was pregnant , and I was so shocked I spent the whole day crying , " she said , wondering what kind of world she was bringing her child into . <p> But instead of succumbing to fatalism , Laurent -- best known for her role in Quentin Tarantino 's Inglourious Basterds -- went looking for the positive . <p> With study after study confirming the dire warnings by experts about the state of the biosphere , Laurent and her friend Cyril Dion felt people needed hope . <p> Demain ( Tomorrow ) , their feelgood film about how the world can change for the better -- and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the way -- has petrol-driven machinery . <p> It highlights how major cities like San Francisco are recycling and composting 80 per cent of domestic waste -- and making money from it -- while Copenhagen aims to run on renewable energy alone within less than a decade . <p> The US city of Detroit hopes to feed half its population from its booming urban vegetable plots in a country where food travels on average of
