NATIONAL <h> Jacqueline stars in remake of Spice Girls song for campaign <h> Jacqueline stars in remake of Spice Girls ' song <p> Actor Jacqueline Fernandez has starred in the video remake of former British girl band Spice Girls ' song ' Wannabe ' . The remake is part of a Global Goals campaign to tell world leaders what girls and women want in 2016 . <p> In September this year , world leaders will mark the first anniversary of the adoption of the Sustainable Development Goals . These goals aim to end poverty , fix climate change and address inequalities . <p> Project Everyone has convened the first Global Goals campaign for girls and women . <p> The campaign on Monday previewed the " #WhatIReallyReallyWant " video at the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity in partnership with Getty Images and SAWA , the global cinema advertising association . Quality education , an end to violence , an end to child marriage and equal pay for equal work are some of the demands the campaign makes .
