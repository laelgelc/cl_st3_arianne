<h> MoEF to HC : DLF did not breach building norms <p> KOCHI : The Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF ) has informed the Kerala High Court that DLF Universal limited had adhered to the conditions laid down by the State Environment Impact Assessment Authority ( SEIAA ) and did not violate any of the provisions while constructing building on the banks of the Chilavannoor backwaters . <p> Earlier , a Single Judge of the Kerala High Court had directed to demolish the building , for alleged violation of the Coastal Regulation Zone ( CRZ ) notifications . Later , a Division Bench of the court stayed the order . <p> In an affidavit filed on May 19 , the MoEF stated that the construction was carried out technically as per provisions of the CRZ notification-1991 and the EIA notification-2006 . <p> The Central Government filed the affidavit in response to an appeal filed by DLF , seeking to set aside the Single Judge 's order .
