<h> June 16 , 2016 <p> The homologous temperature of a crystalline material is defined as the ratio between temperature and the melting ( solidus ) temperature ( Tm ) in Kelvin . Because Tm of a crystalline material is controlled by the bonding force between atoms , T/Tm has been widely used to compare the creep strength of crystalline materials . As the most abundant mineral in the upper mantle , olivine is the solid solution of forsterite ( Mg2SiO4 ) and fayalite ( Fe2SiO4 ) . Recent deformation experiments have revealed the influence of water , temperature , pressure , stress and partial melting on fabric development of olivine . However , how to extrapolate laboratory results to mantle deformation is still under debate . <p> In a recent review in Science China Earth Sciences , Qin Wang from Nanjing University established the phase diagram of dry olivine up to 6.4 GPa using previous melting experiments and generalized means .
