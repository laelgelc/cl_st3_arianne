The 39 unreported sources were found guilty of emitting toxic sulfur dioxide . Sulfur dioxide ( SO2 ) is one of the six air pollutants regulated by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency . Agencies concerned with monitoring these air pollutants need to locate the sources first in order to measure and moderate them .
