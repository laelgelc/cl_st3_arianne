<p> What you heard the other day , when Pacific Gas &amp; Electric and a group of environmental organizations and labor unions announced a plan to permanently shutter the Diablo Canyon nuclear plant , was the sound of the door shutting on nuclear power in the United States . <p> Or was it ? <p> The landmark deal , which must be approved by the California Public Utilities Commission , sets the terms for shutting down Diablo Canyon 's two reactors in 2024 and 2025 , the original expiration dates of their federal licenses . Since Diablo is the last operating nuclear plant in California , that will close the book on the state 's checkered relationship with nuclear power . <p> The agreement calls for PG&amp;E to withdraw its pending application to extend the licenses for another 20 years , and to replace the plant 's 2,240-megawatt capacity with a combination of efficiency improvements and renewable sources .
