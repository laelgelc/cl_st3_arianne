<h> Study of stalagmites in caves in China reveals 640,000 years of Asian monsoon history <p> ( Phys.org ) -- A team of researchers with members from China , the U.S. , Austria and Singapore has used their analysis of stalagmites in a cave deep in central China to map over 640,000 years of monsoons in Asia . In their paper published in the journal Nature , the team describes their analysis of the cave formations , what they found and how they were able to use what they learned to better understand other world events over the same time period . Nele Meckler with University of Bergen in Norway provides a more in-depth description of the work done by the team in a News &amp; Viewsarticle in the same journal issue . <p> The annual monsoon season in Asia is a major event , bringing rains that are used to grow crops for an enormous number of people . Because of its importance , scientists would like to know more about it , such as what might happen as the planet heats up . Here , using our new speleothem data , we extend the Chinese record to cover the full uranium/thorium dating range , that is , the past 640,000 years .
