It has lived through all the mass extinctions our planet has seen , including the one that killed the dinosaurs , and the biggest one , which wiped out more than 95% of marine species . It has done all that without changing much in the last 450 million years . The crab 's remarkable evolution , or lack thereof , puts it in the small number of species called " living fossils , " because their current forms resemble their oldest fossils . <p> But the hardy crab has n't fared so well during the ascent of mankind . In the last two decades , its population has been steadily falling . In some parts of the world , climate change is making its habitat in the sea unlivable .
