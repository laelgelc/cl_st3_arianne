In bone , the proportions of protein and mineral are roughly equal -- the mineral gives bone stiffness and hardness , while the protein gives it toughness or resistance to fracture . While bones can break , it is relatively rare , and they have the benefit of being self-healing -- another feature that engineers are trying to bring to biomimetic materials . <p> In eggshell , the ratios are different : about 95% mineral to 5% protein , but even this small amount of protein makes eggshell remarkably tough considering how thin it is . <p> When making the artificial bone and eggshell , the mineral components are ' templated ' directly onto collagen , which is the most abundant protein in the animal world . But it may be some time before we 're living in bone and eggshell houses . <p> For one , the collagen that Oyen needs to make these materials comes from natural ( meaning animal ) sources .
