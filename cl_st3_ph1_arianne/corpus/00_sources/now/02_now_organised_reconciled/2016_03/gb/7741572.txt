Amin , director-general of IRENA , in a statement . " REmap shows this is not only the most economic pathway , but also the most socially and environmentally conscious . It would create more jobs , save millions of lives from reduced air pollution and set us on a pathway to limit global temperature rise to two degrees as agreed in Paris .
