<h> Green Energy Investors ' Spooked ' By Government <p> The Government is risking higher energy bills for families in the long term as investors have been " spooked " by " sudden and unexpected " policy changes , MPs have warned . <p> The Energy and Climate Change Committee said the self-styled policy " reset " , which included cuts to solar , onshore wind and biomass subsidies , had resulted in renewable and greener energy projects being placed on hold . <p> It argued the country could not afford such delays , given warnings about an energy crunch and the need to spend ? 100bn replacing ageing infrastructure . <p> The report was released against the backdrop of a delay to the expected final investment decision on the Hinkley Point C nuclear facility by French firm EDF - a topic expected to be raised at an Anglo/French summit today . <p> The Department of Energy and Climate Change ( DECC ) said its action , including cutting subsidies , was aimed at getting value for taxpayers and bill-payers and cutting carbon emissions at the same time @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tackle a legacy of under-investment in our energy system - creating the right environment for businesses to invest in clean , affordable energy and building an energy infrastructure fit for the 21st century , " a spokesman added . <p> The blueprint included , DECC said , 10 gigawatts of new offshore wind in the next decade and a drive for shale gas to help guarantee domestic supplies despite opposition from local communities and environmental campaigners . <p> Committee chair , Angus MacNeil , urged the creation of a long term plan to provide clarity . <p> He said : " Nervousness among investors will make it harder and more expensive to build the new energy infrastructure that we need . <p> " Any increase in the cost of project capital will ultimately get passed on to consumers through higher energy bills .
