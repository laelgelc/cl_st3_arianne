<p> The Great Australian Bight is a pristine marine environment . It 's a haven for humpback and sperm whales , blue whales and beak whales . It 's Australia 's most significant seal lion nursery and said to be the world 's most important southern right whale nursery . It sustains huge fishing and tourism industries -- and BP is planning to drill it for oil . <p> Yes , that BP - BP of the " Deepwater Horizon " oil disaster in the Gulf of Mexico back in 2010 , in which a well exploded and sank , killing 11 people and creating the biggest oil spill disaster in US history .
