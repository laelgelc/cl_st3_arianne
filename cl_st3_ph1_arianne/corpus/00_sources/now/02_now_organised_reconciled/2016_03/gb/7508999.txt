<h> Climate change : 500,000 people face death in 2050 from malnutrition <p> By 2050 , more than 500,000 people a year worldwide will be dying from the effects of climate change on food supplies . Food shortages and changes to our diet could instigate half a million adult deaths across 155 countries . <p> Research published in The Lancet shows that humans will be forced to eat around 3% less food every day in 2050 ; a change that will cause life-threatening consequences . <p> " Much research has looked at food security , but little has focused on the wider health effects of agricultural production , " said Marco Springmann , lead author of the study , from the University of Oxford . <h> Modelling food stocks in 2050 <p> The researchers created a model to simulate global food production in the year 2050 . This model used current projections for global emissions to simulate how climate change will effect global food stocks . <p> They then factored in projections for rising population and economic change .
