<p> JOHN Swinney has launched a consultation on how best to reform and abolish Air Passenger Duty . <p> It is the first step in the Government 's plan to first cut the tax in half before scrapping it altogether , claiming the move would benefit business and tourism . The Greens said the policy would put " more money in the pockets of a polluting industry " . <p> Speaking at Edinburgh Airport , Swinney said : " Scotland is already an attractive destination for business and inbound tourism , and it is important that we continue to open Scotland up to key and emerging markets in order to further capitalise on the opportunities that exist . " <p> Powers to replace APD are being devolved to Holyrood as part of the Scotland Bill , and the Scottish Government have said they plan to start reducing the tax by 50 per cent from April 2018 and implementing that in full before the end of the next parliament .
