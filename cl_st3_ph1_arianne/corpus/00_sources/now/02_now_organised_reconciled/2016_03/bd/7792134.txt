<h> BNP executive committee expanded with ' one man , one post ' provision <h> The Sixth National Council of the BNP has expanded the party 's executive committee barring leaders from holding more than one position . <p> The councillors entrusted BNP chief Khaleda Zia with the ' responsibility ' to form the new Standing Committee and the National Executive Committee . <p> The council 's working session held at the Institution of Engineers , Bangladesh endorsed the decision on Saturday . Khaleda presided over the five-hour session that began around 5pm . <p> The BNP constitution was amended to expand its executive committee , increase number of posts at different levels , and introduce some new positions . <p> The number of vice-chairman posts has been increased to 35 from 17 while division-based positions of organising secretaries and two assistant secretaries under each divisional secretary have been created . <p> The amended constitution has also kept provision for different affairs-based sub-committees . <p> The affairs are finance and planning , public health and family welfare , international relations , defence , science and information technology , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ research , industry and commerce , local government and rural development , women and children development , youth development , environment and climate change , labour and expatriate welfare , communications and public transport , energy and mineral resources , human rights , flood control , liberation war , relief and disaster management , micro-credit , governance and public administration , human resources development , national reconciliation and ethnic minority . <p> Seven new assistant secretary posts for international affairs , two assistant secretary positions for training and another two for family welfare and health have been created . Number of posts of religious affair assistant secretaries has been enhanced to four from three .
