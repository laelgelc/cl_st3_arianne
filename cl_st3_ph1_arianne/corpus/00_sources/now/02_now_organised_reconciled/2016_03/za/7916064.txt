<h> 400,000 New Yorkers March For People 's Climate March <p> Last morning , around 400,000 people have joined a march in Manhattan where the People 's Climate March has just started . It is the largest climate march in the world ( till date ) and is a result of extensive organizing done in the last few months by 1,574 " partner organizations " which included a wide variety of organizations from faith groups to labor unions and social justice communities as well . Members from all these organizations also joined the march . There were 550 buses , and 25,000 people were brought in to Manhattan on these buses . The march was headed by " Frontlines of Crisis , Forefront of Change " and their main demand is to force the governments of all the nations to take some strict actions against climatic damages and global warming . <p> A True Intermingling Of Race and Generation Seen in the March <p> The march was headed by minorities , indigenous communities and marginalized communities . There were a high percentage of youths and children in the march , and it was heartening to see them as concerned by environmental changes as the older population . Faith based groups were not lagging behind as well , and all of them together made it a moral as well as ecological and economic cause . <p> Marchers Optimistic About Recent UN Climate Summit <p> By late afternoon , the number of marchers and protestors had shot up so much that people were requested to disperse so that the march could proceed through the streets . All the marchers sounded the rally cry as they marched , with bands joining in as well . The marchers are hopeful that something concrete may come up at the UN climate summit , where India , China , Australia and Germany will be participating .
