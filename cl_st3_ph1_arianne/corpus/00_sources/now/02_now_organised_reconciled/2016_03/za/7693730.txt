<h> Up to 13 Million Americans Are at Risk of Being Washed Away <p> Tyrrell County sits just inland from North Carolina 's Outer Banks barrier islands . With 3,600 people living and farming along 400 square miles , it 's an ecologically rich enclave . It also ranks number one among 319 U.S. coastal counties facing long-term risk from rising seas . By 2100 , according to a new study , 94 percent of Tyrrell 's future population may be at risk from encroaching seawater . <p> The swelling ocean may threaten the homes of up to 13.1 million coast-dwelling Americans by the end of this century , according to the study published today in the journal Nature Climate Change . Led by Mathew Hauer , a doctoral candidate at the University of Georgia , the research is novel because it combines population projections with sea-level rise projections . <p> Tyrrell County leads the country only by percentage of its population at risk . The largest absolute numbers of people at risk are in Miami-Dade and Broward counties , in Florida .
