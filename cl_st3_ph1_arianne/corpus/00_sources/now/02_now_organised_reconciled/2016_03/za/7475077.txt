<h> ' Africa rising ' not over despite doom and gloom narrative <p> Windhoek -- The " Africa rising " story is not over despite the narrative gloom and doom on Africa , even when the continent is projected to experience a better economic growth rate making it the best place to invest , the president of the African Development Bank ( AfDB ) , Akinwumi Adesina , told the Africa 2016 Forum in Egypt on 21 February , highly enthusiastic and impassionate speech , in which Adesina concluded by asking the attending presidents of Egypt , Nigeria , Gabon , Kenya , Senegal , Sudan , Ethiopia , Egypt , Equatorial Guinea and Togo to " Give Africa a High 5 . " <p> The " High 5 for Africa " is the AfDB 's theme for its ambitious plans to accelerate development of Africa through " Light up and Power Africa ; Feed Africa ; Industrialize Africa ; Integrate Africa ; and Improve the quality of life for Africans . " <p> Adesina implored African Heads of States to Africa to tap into and securitize remittances @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ help Africa fund its own development programmes . <p> " Africa must not fall again into the debt trap . To avoid it , there is need to urgently focus on macroeconomic stabilization and fiscal consolidation , and rapidly diversify African economies , broaden the export market destinations , and expand the export mix . And most importantly , Africa must shift its focus to domestic resource mobilization for capital formation for sustained growth , " he said . <p> Adesina also spoke of the 2015 agreement to open up trade between the East African Community , Common Market for East and Southern Africa , and the Southern Africa Development Community , calling it " the boldest step towards the Continental Free Trade Agreement . " " There is no doubt : an open Africa will be an unstoppable Africa . Breaking down the barriers is the key to Africa 's long time growth and prosperity . Your Excellencies , bring down these walls , " he said . <p> " Yes , African economies face economic headwinds from the significant decline in the price of commodities , be it oil @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ demand from China , whose economy accounted for a bulk of the trade with Africa , has compounded the current account situation and domestic fiscal imbalances for many countries . But we must not believe the narrative of doom and gloom on Africa . <p> Just look at the facts : while the global economy is projected by the IMF International Monetary Fund and the OECD Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development to grow at 3% this year , Africa is projected to grow at 4.4% in 2016 and strengthen further to 5% in 2017 . That is good news ! African economies are not unraveling -- they are resilient ! Africa is still the best place to invest , " he said . <p> In attendance were the presidents of Egypt , Abdel Fattah el-Sisi , Muhammadu Buhari of Nigeria , Ali Bongo of Gabon , Uhuru Kenyatta of Kenya , Macky Sall of Senegal , Omar al-Bashir , of the Sudan , Teodoro Obiang of Equatorial Guinea , Faure Gnassingb ? of Togo and the Prime Minister of Ethiopia Hailemariam Delasegn . <p> Nonetheless , Africa still faces challenges @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , taking advantage of the record low interest rates have rushed to the international capital market to raise financing by issuing bonds . Between 2006 and 2014 , African nations issued Eurobonds of about US $26 billion to ride on the advantage of low interest rates in the international money market . <p> " While the rating of countries provides a point of strength for the economies -- as long as real interest rates remained low -- the challenge now is rising cost of financing the foreign currency denominated debt , " he said . <p> Thus Africa must tap into and securitize remittances for development . " Africans investing in Africa sends a powerful signal : remittances to Africa have risen from $11 billion in 2000 to over $62 billion in 2014 , far exceeding Official Development Assistance inflows . Sovereign Wealth Funds assets under management in Africa have risen from $114 billion in 2009 to $162 billion in 2014 . Pension funds currently stands at $334 billion . And Africa today generates about $500 billion in domestic taxes . Africa must mobilize all these domestic resources to accelerate its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and pace of growth . It can develop itself with pride , " he said . <p> Adesina singled out Egypt 's bold $8.2 billion Suez Canal expansion project that was completed in just one year , and whose funds were mobilised in just eight days entirely by Egyptian citizens . There is also the plan to build a new modern capital city with the price tag of $45 billion , with an audacious completion timeline of seven years . Further the AfDB has extended $140 million co-financing support for the government of Egypt for the Sharm el Sheikh Airport Expansion Project , bringing the AfDB 's active commitments in Egypt to nearly US $2 billion . The bank also approved and fully disbursed $500 million in budget support to Egypt in December 2015 . <p> " Such is the level of pride I see in Egypt 's development . President el-Sisi , your vision for Egypt is a bold one : you are thinking big , acting big -- delivering with speed . The message and lesson are clear : African countries should accelerate their pace of growth and development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ deliver big . We must never have low aspirations for Africa . And the African Development Bank -- your Bank -- will be there to support you , " said Adesina . <p> Delving into AfDB 's High 5 for Africa Adesina explained that the ' Light Up and Power Africa ' is aimed at addressing the needs of over 640 million Africans with no access to electricity , and 700 million without access to clean cooking energy . The AfDB has developed the New Deal on Energy for Africa to accelerate universal access to electricity in Africa by 2025 by adding 160 Giga Watts of new generation capacity via the grid , deliver 130 million new grid connections and 75 million off-grid connections . There would be investment of $12 billion in the energy sector over the next five years , and leverage $40-50 billion into the energy sector . The bank will also triple its climate finance to Africa to $5 billion per year by 2020 to support climate change adaptation and mitigation efforts . <p> The ' Feed Africa ' is to reduce Africa 's spending of over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ support for massive agricultural transformation across Africa -- while building resilience to climate change -- to fully unlock the potential of agriculture , to lower food prices , save scarce foreign exchange , increase foreign exchange earnings , strengthen macroeconomic and fiscal stability , revive rural areas -- and in particular , create jobs for hundreds of millions of Africans . <p> The ' Industralise Africa ' would see bank support private sector and financial market development for the rapid industrialization of Africa , and help Africa move to the top of global value chains . <p> Africa currently accounts for just 1.9% of global manufacturing . <p> ' Integrate Africa ' address African trade that currently represents only 2% of global trade while intra-African trade is just about 12% compared to 60% in Europe , and 35% in Asia . " This is not acceptable . Regional integration is critical for boosting economic growth in Africa , " he said . The bank will continue to invest heavily in high quality regional infrastructure -- especially rail , transnational highways , power interconnections , information and communications , air and maritime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for Africans ' the bank will accelerate investments in urban infrastructure , public health and nutrition , water and sanitation , education , vocational training and skills development . <p> We will grow the next generation of knowledge-work force for the continent . AfDB would soon be launching the Jobs for Africa 's Youth Initiative -- which will work across all African countries -- with the goal of reaching 50 million youths over a 10-year period and stimulate the creation of 25 million jobs . It is expected to add an additional US $30 billion to African economies . <p> " More importantly , it will help us to stem the tide of migration within Africa and into Europe . We will keep Africa 's youth in Africa by expanding economic opportunities . This will help Africa to turn its demographic asset into an economic dividend , " he said .
