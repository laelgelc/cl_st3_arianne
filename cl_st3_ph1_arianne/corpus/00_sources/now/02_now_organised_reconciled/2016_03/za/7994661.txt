<h> EEP S&amp;EA opens bid windows for clean power projects <p> On Tuesday , the Energy and Environment Partnership Programme of Southern and East Africa ( EEP S&amp;EA ) announced that they are seeking high-quality applications for projects in the two regions . <h> Increasing access to sustainable power resources <p> The project objectives are to alleviate and reduce the effects of poverty by actively promoting an inclusive and job-creating green economy , which can be facilitated through improving energy security while mitigating global climate change . <p> The Ministry for Foreign Affairs of Finland ( lead donor ) , jointly funds the programme together with the UK Department for International Development ( DFID ) and the Austrian Development Agency ( ADA ) while the EEP Coordination Office ( ECO ) is hosted by KPMG . <h> Two funding windows launched <p> EEP Southern and East Africa have opened two funding windows for Early Stage ( CfP12 ) and Market Ready ( CfP13 ) projects , which are currently open for submission of first proposals . <p> The partnership has stressed in a statement that specific attention needs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gender , poverty reduction , innovation and difficult markets . <p> For the projects selected through CfP12 and CfP13 , the project implementation period starting in August 2016 is limited to 15 months . <h> CfP12 - Early Stage projects <p> The projects supported in CfP12 can be ' last-mile ' feasibility studies , pilot or demonstration projects . The scope of this Call for Proposals ( CfPs ) is limited to projects that deal with the development phase of renewable energy and energy saving/energy efficiency projects . <h> CfP 13 -- Market ready projects <p> The projects supported in CfFP13 are scale-up to commercial operation , replication of commercially proven concepts to new markets ( nationally or internationally ) and rejuvenation of existing renewable energy and energy efficiency generation projects , EEP S&amp;EA explained in a statement . <p> It is under this term that project finance is to be offered to those projects which display strong potential for boosting other commercial sources of finance for project implementation , replication and/or scaling up .
