<h> Community <h> Mall ties up with WWF to form eco school <p> Kids from The children 's house performing ' We Are The World ' by the late Michael Jackson . <p> IT was a momentous occasion for The School in Jaya One on Earth Hour as it marked the launch of the Eco-Schools Training Centre as part of the Eco-Schools Programme , in collaboration with World Wide Fund for Nature-Malaysia ( WWF-Malaysia ) . <p> A total of 10,000 visitors including students and representatives from several schools and non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) turned up for the two-day Earth Hour event in Petaling Jaya to raise awareness on environmental and climate change . <p> The Eco-Schools Training Centre is the first initiative in Malaysia designed to guide participating schools and communities in implementing a whole-school approach towards environmental and sustainability education . <p> The training lessons are conducted by a certified WWF trainer , education manager and specially invited NGOs on a monthly basis incorporating holistic and participatory approach aimed at improving the environments of local communities . <p> This is to empower students , teachers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and networking as well as funding opportunities to embark on a sustainable journey in their schools . <p> The training centre provides monthly training for schools within the Klang Valley based on the Seven-Step methodology and the 10 Eco themes . <p> Part of the training includes a showcase on Jaya One 's waste management , rainwater harvesting and oil separation initiatives . <p> In addition , it also enables the public to learn more about the habitats on a rainforest tree and how certain items are being recycled and reused . <p> " We are delighted to work together with WWF-Malaysia as we constantly strive to be a family-friendly mall that not only provides a fun and stimulating place for children to learn and grow , but also various avenues to empower the communities with meaningful initiatives , " said Tetap Tiara Sdn Bhd executive director Charles Wong , the developer for Jaya One and The School . <p> " We hope this implementation will be able to educate the public on the importance of recycling , " he added .
