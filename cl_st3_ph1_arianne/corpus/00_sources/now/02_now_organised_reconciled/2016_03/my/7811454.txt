<h> Letters <h> Jail those who destroy our environment indiscriminately <p> WHILE the Government pays attention to the wellbeing of the people and continues to transform Malaysia into an advanced , high-income economy , I as a consumer advocate would like to see some attention being paid to our environment . Nature must be protected and safeguarded at all times . <p> I hope Prime Minister Datuk Seri Najib Tun Razak would allocate sufficient funds for the protection of our environment . Those who destroy nature must be taken to task . Show no mercy on them as too much is at stake if we ignore the needs of nature . <p> Trees are chopped down and hills are being cut indiscriminately for development , causing erosion that can lead to landslides . This can not be allowed to continue . <p> I would also like to see the Environment Court taking stern action on those who destroy our environment . The culprits must be jailed as it is n't enough just to fine them . <p> It is interesting to note Najib 's recent comment that Malaysia @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emissions need not be correlated . He is spot on here so let us work at this too . Any action on climate change is proof that the economy and environment can prosper together . <p> It is time , too , to encourage the use of clean vehicles and to commit to energy efficient construction , energy efficiency ( EE ) and renewable energy ( RE ) . <p> According to data from the Energy Commission 's Demand Side Management Unit , significant savings can be made by adopting EE models of consumer products such as lamps ( compact fluorescent instead of incandescent ) , refrigerators ( five-star rated instead of three-star or lower ) , air-conditioners , fans and television sets . <p> Malaysian consumers must also play their part to help the country achieve its goal of reducing carbon emission by 40% , as declared by the Prime Minister in December 2009.
