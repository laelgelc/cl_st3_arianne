<p> When a helicopter pilot spotted the first crater in summer 2014 , everyone was baffled . <p> The 100-foot-wide hole appeared on the Yamal Peninsula seemingly out of nowhere , during a tense season of Russian military action in Ukraine and international sanctions . <p> And then more appeared . It revealed that the air had an extraordinarily high concentration of methane . <p> It 's not just explosions and melting permafrost that we should worry about , either .
