NEWS <h> Airasia Takes Part In Earth Hour Initiative <p> KUALA LUMPUR , March 19 ( Bernama ) -- Low-cost carrier AirAsia is taking part in the Earth Hour initiative by supporting a local green event organised by Free Tree Society to plant seedlings and build a rainwater harvester . <p> The Earth Hour initiative is a worldwide movement organised by the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) to engage a massive mainstream community on a broad range of environmental issues . <p> AirAsia in a statement today said it has mobilised over 50 volunteers led by Chief Executive Officer Aireen Omar for the event , which is part of the airline 's #GREEN24 movement to further educate the public about the effects of climate change , as well as to advocate climate action for a sustainable future . <p> In addition to the event , it said over 17,000 AirAsia employees around the world would also be observing Earth Hour Day and encourage their respective communities to shine a light on climate action . <p> Earth Hour Day is a worldwide event @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ turn off their non-essential lights for one hour , from 8.30 pm to 9.30 pm local time towards the end of March , as a symbol of their commitment to the planet . <p> AirAsia said its participation in the event continues its previous initiative in January , when it initiated over 30 activities throughout its global offices across ASEAN and beyond to promote energy conservation as well as public education about the effects of climate change . <p> It said it has also been actively sourcing for ways to inject the sustainability factor into its operations , including engineering , flight operations and fuel saving initiatives to reduce its carbon footprint.
