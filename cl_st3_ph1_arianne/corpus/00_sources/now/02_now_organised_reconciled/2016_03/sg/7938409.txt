<h> Vietnam sharply lowers growth expectations due to disaster impacts <p> Vietnam 's gross domestic product ( GDP ) for this year will be much lower than last year 's and also sharply below the prior estimate , as various natural disasters have pulled down agricultural , forestry and fishery production of the country , said the minister for planning and investment . <p> The minister Bui Quang Vinh said if such problems remain then the growth rate will fall to 5.45% in 2016 compared to the more than 6% growth registered in 2015 .
