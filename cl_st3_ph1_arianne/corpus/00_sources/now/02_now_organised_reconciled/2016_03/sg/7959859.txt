<h> DiCaprio visits Indonesian jungle to support environmentalists <p> This handout picture taken on March 26 , 2016 and released on March 29 , 2016 by the Foundation of Forest , Nature , and Environment of Aceh , shows Oscar-winning actor Leonardo DiCaprio ( R ) posing with elephants as he visits Leuser National Park in Aceh province . <p> The actor , an ardent supporter of environmental causes , was pictured accompanied by local environmentalists and flanked by two critically endangered Sumatran elephants . <p> The elephants are among a dizzying array of rare animals who live in Leuser 's dense rainforests . <p> DiCaprio said on his Instagram account that his foundation , which supports numerous environmental projects , was backing local groups to establish a " mega-fauna sanctuary " in the area . <p> He described the area as " the last place on Earth where Sumatran orangutans , tigers , rhinos and elephants coexist in the wild " . <p> Local green activist Farwiza Fahan , who met DiCaprio on his visit , said the sanctuary was aimed at giving more protection to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stages . <p> Like much of Indonesia 's rainforests , the area is under threat from the aggressive expansion of palm oil and pulp and paper plantations , while endangered animals are targeted by poachers and locals who view them as pests . <p> But the area faces an additional threat after authorities in the province of Aceh - which includes much of the Leuser ecosystem - pushed through a plan to open up new swathes of virgin forest for commercial exploitation and lay roads . <p> The central government in Jakarta , which must approve such locally made plans , has asked Aceh to revise it , but activists claim that local authorities are pushing ahead with it regardless . <p> DiCaprio , who attended the COP21 climate change talks in Paris last year , has been raising the alarm on global warming since 1998 when he founded the Leonardo DiCaprio Foundation . <p> He won the best actor Oscar for his role in the " The Revenant " , a film about a 19th century fur trapper filmed under extreme winter conditions in Canada and Argentina .
