<h> Voices <h> Awareness of plastic waste is insufficient to bring change <h> Elaine Sam Hui Xian , President , 23rd Management Committee , NUS SAVE <p> The use of plastic is increasing , but we must go beyond awareness to reduce plastic waste . <p> While environmental issues and climate change have been discussed for several years , and people are generally aware of the need for a sustainable lifestyle , the impact of awareness is limited here . <p> Singaporeans are distant from the environmental degradation that happens when plastic waste accumulates . In the National University of Singapore , where most people are educated on these issues , my team and I have observed that their choices are largely contextual . <p> Since 2010 , NUS canteens have led the way in plastic reduction by doing away with free plastic bags . To ask people to do without disposable takeaway containers , however , is not as easy . <p> Our observations and our peers tell us that although people believe saving the environment is important , they feel they have no choice but to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ or going for project meetings . <p> The NUS Students Against Violation of the Earth ( NUS SAVE ) work closely with our supportive NUS offices to provide our university population with alternatives that can aid in these situations . <p> Our Project Box rewards people with meal discounts when they order takeaway with a reusable lunchbox . The scheme has been well received by the NUS community and has managed to save approximately 30,000 disposable takeaway boxes to date . <p> We have also piloted a lunchbox rental system to offer even more choices when students need to eat takeaway . If it succeeds , we hope more food establishments and outlets can do the same , so that Singaporeans have more takeaway options . <p> We hope our initiatives will provide more platforms to help individuals choose a sustainable lifestyle . Collectively , these small steps can make for a greater change and can nurture a responsible society that values the environment in which we live .
