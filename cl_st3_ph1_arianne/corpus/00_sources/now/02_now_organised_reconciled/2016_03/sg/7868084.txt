<h> Abrupt climate shift perilously close : Study <p> NEW YORK * The nations of the world agreed years ago to try to limit global warming to a level they hoped would prove somewhat tolerable . But leading climate scientists have warned that permitting a warming of that magnitude would actually be highly dangerous . <p> The likely consequences would include killer storms stronger than any in modern times , the disintegration of large parts of the polar ice sheets , and a rise of the sea sufficient to begin drowning the world 's coastal cities before the end of this century , the scientists declared . <p> " We 're in danger of handing young people a situation that 's out of their control , " said Dr James Hansen , the retired Nasa climate scientist who led the new research . " They conflict with the mainstream understanding of climate change to the point where the standard of proof is quite high . " <p> However , virtually all climate scientists agree that society is not moving fast enough to reduce emissions of greenhouse gases .
