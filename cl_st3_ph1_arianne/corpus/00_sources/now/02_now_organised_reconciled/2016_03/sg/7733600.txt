<h> Discovery partners with WWF- India for Earth Hour 2016 <p> MUMBAI : Coming together with one of the biggest environment campaigns across the globe , Discovery Channel has pledged its support to Earth Hour 2016 . <p> The channel is partnering with WWF-India to help raise awareness on the importance of climate change and renewable resources to inspire people to move to solar . The promotional partnership will be leveraged through Discovery 's network in India spanning on-air , digital and social media platforms . <p> Earth Hour is the world 's biggest celebration where people come together for the planet . On the last Saturday of every March , millions of people across the globe unite in a show of support to raise awareness about climate change . This year , Earth Hour will be observed on 19 March from 8:30 pm-9:30 pm . <p> Earth Hour 2016 is calling on citizens across the country to switch off non-essential lights for the hour and go solar .
