<h> Rising sea levels could hit 13 million Americans <h> New research into impact of global warming takes population increases into account <p> PARIS * Rising sea levels driven by climate change could upend the lives of more than 13 million Americans by the end of the century . <p> If global warming lifts oceans 1.8m by 2100 , as some scientists forecast in worst-case scenarios , 13.1 million people living in United States coastal areas will become vulnerable to flooding and storm surges , said a new study published on Monday in the journal Nature Climate Change . <p> In Florida alone , some six million residents could be affected . <p> Even if sea levels increase by only half that amount , at least four million Americans will be in harm 's way . <p> Previous estimates of the number of people in the US who would be disrupted or displaced by rising ocean levels have not taken into account population increases , the researchers said . <p> " This research merges population forecasts with sea level rise , " said Mr Mathew Hauer , lead @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ University of Georgia . <p> " The impact projections are up to three times larger than current estimates , which significantly underestimate the effect of sea level rise in the United States , " he said . <p> More than 25 per cent of people living in major urban centres such as Miami and New Orleans are likely to face severe flooding by century 's end , the study warned . <p> " The longer we wait to imple- ement adaptation measures , the more expensive they become , " Mr Hauer said . <p> All told , there are 31 US counties where more than 100,000 residents would be hit hard by a 2m jump in sea levels , according to the new calculations . In three of them , 80 per cent of the population would be adversely affected . <p> On a global scale , a rise of 1m to 2m in sea levels would have a similar or even more devastating impact on hundreds of millions of people , previous research has shown . <p> One study estimated that - even under optimistic scenarios that assume swift @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ occupied by some 200 million people would be submerged , though over a longer time scale . <p> Climate change has unleashed three main drivers of rising seas . <p> One is thermal expansion - water takes up more space as it warms .
