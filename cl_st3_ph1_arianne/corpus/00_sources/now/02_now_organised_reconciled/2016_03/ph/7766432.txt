<h> Brgy Health worker cited as Tagum City 's 2016 Most Outstanding Woman <p> TAGUM CITY , Davao del Norte , March 18 ( PIA ) - - A barangay health worker of Magugpo North , Tagum City was hailed as this year 's search for the Most Outstanding Woman in the city . <p> Wevina Martinez bested six other nominees in the search to receive a painting , a plaque and a cash worth P10,000.00 during the Women 's Month celebration at the New City Hall . <p> Martinez , 62 , who has four children and five grandchildren with sixth on its way , has been a barangay health worker for 34 years . <p> " I started in 1982 as a volunteer and it was not easy then .
