Joining the site inspection was a camera crew taking footage of the solar installation for an upcoming documentary on climate change . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The 2.7 MW MOA Solar Carpark is nearly twice the size of the 1.5 MW SM North Edsa Solar Carpark , making it one of the world 's largest on-site solar projects .
