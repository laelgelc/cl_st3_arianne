<h> Earth Hour 2016 shines a light on renewable energy sources <p> On March 19 , the Philippines joins the world once again in switching off the lights for one hour to highlight the urgent need for decisive climate change solutions . <p> This year 's Earth Hour is special , not only because it is the 10th year that hundreds of nations will stand in solidarity with the cause of climate actions , but also because while darkness envelops parts of the country during Earth Hour ( from 8:30 to 9:30 p.m . PHT ) this Saturday , the Quezon City Memorial Circle will be alive with lights and music courtesy of kinetic energy . <p> The world celebrates the 10th anniversary of Earth Hour on March 19 . The stationary bicycles are made by the popular bamboo bike maker , Bambike , whose wheels are fashioned to fit the generator that transforms the kinetic energy ( from continuous pedaling ) to electricity . <p> " This is the first time we will use kinetic energy for Earth Hour to power the event itself , " shares Earth Hour Philippines national director Gia Ibay . <p> She adds , " We thought it is but fitting to go back to the roots of Earth Hour wherein we 're just showing that things are possible . Stationary bikes will partially power this year 's event to show that , when united , Pinoys can light up this country with their collective energy . " <p> The move aims to showcase climate solutions that individuals and nations can take , such as transitioning to renewable energy sources . <p> According to Ibay , they will have people who will start the pace but everyone 's invited to pedal the bicycles .
