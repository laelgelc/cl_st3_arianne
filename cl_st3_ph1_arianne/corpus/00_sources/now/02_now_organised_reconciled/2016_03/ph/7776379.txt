<h> PH marks Earth Hour <p> An hour-long blackout will again occur in various cities in the Philippines tonight and across over 170 countries for Earth Hour , the global movement against climate change . <p> The switching off of lights at exactly 8:30 p.m. until 9:30 p.m. marks the 10th year of the Earth Hour initiated by the World Wide Fund for Nature to deliver a simple , yet powerful message on the need for decisive climate change solutions . <p> Earth Hour , which started as a single city event in Sydney , Australia , has been considered as the largest global environmental campaign , participated in by billions of people and even the world 's most iconic landmarks , said the WWF . <p> " It shall be a showcase of climate solutions , " said Earth Hour Philippines Director Atty . Gia Ibay in a statement . <p> The Earth Hour will be held from 8:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. in local time zones in 7,000 cities and 172 countries . <p> Earth Hour Philippines ' main switch-off event this year will be held @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> This year 's Earth Hour will be highlighted by bicycles , electric vehicles , renewable energy technologies , and other climate change solutions , according to Ibay . She said that over 500 bikers are expected to gather at the venue before the switch-off . ( Vanne Elaine P. Terrazola )
