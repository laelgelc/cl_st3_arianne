JOAN BONDOC <p> PRESIDENT Aquino on Wednesday inaugurated the 63.3-megawatt Calatagan Solar Farm , the biggest solar farm in Luzon that has claims to be able to power the noontime energy needs of western Batangas . <p> Solar Philippines CEO Leandro Leviste said the Calatagan Solar Farm is one of the first of dozens of solar projects completed this year .
