<h> Leonardo DiCaprio says China can be ' climate change hero ' <p> BEIJING -- Leonardo DiCaprio has praised China 's work to combat climate change and says he believes the world 's largest emitter of greenhouse gases could be " the hero of the environmental movement . " <p> The environmentalist actor is in Beijing to promote his movie " The Revenant .
