<h> Al Gore knocked over by articulate Pope on climate change <p> MANILA - United States Vice President Al Gore is so impressed by how Pope Francis speaks out about climate change that he might just switch religions . <p> At least , this was a joke the politician made during his speech at the 31st Climate Reality Leadership Corps international training Monday at Sofitel Philippine Plaza Manila , which was attended by some 700 delegates from 58 countries . <p> Gore said he visited Tacloban City two days ago and saw the place where the pontiff met survivors of Super typhoon Yolanda in January last year . <p> " I am not a Catholic but I could become a Catholic because of Pope Francis , " Gore quipped , his words swallowed by the laughter that erupted during the conference held in the Catholic country . <p> " I really admire his delivery of the message of Jesus Christ , " Gore said , pointing out that this included the issuance of the encyclical on climate change , Laudato Si .
