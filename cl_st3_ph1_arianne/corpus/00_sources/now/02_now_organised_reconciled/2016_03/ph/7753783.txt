<h> Cebu Holdings joins Earth Hour <h> Cebu Holdings joins Earth Hour <p> Thursday , March 17 , 2016 <p> CEBU Holdings Inc . ( CHI ) heeds the global need for action against climate change , as its two premier estates take part in this year 's Earth Hour . <p> Cebu Business Park and Cebu I.T . Park will join the global hour-long switch-off in a symbolic effort to raise awareness on the alarming effects of climate change . Together , these estates have 61 buildings for retail , office and residential uses , with 20 more currently under construction . These integrated masterplanned mixed use estates are the preferred business , commercial and IT/BPO addresses outside Metro Manila . <p> More locators and merchants from both estates are expected to participate during this year 's annual switch-off . <p> " Our target is to get a majority confirmation by our locators and get a collective action from everyone to fight and stop the worsening effects of global warming , " said Kim Serate , VisMin associate director of Ayala Property Management Group . <p> Last @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ kilowatts of conserved energy from both estates -- an equivalent to 838.02 kg of CO2 avoidance .
