Apo-like fires , imperil NGP gains : Recto <p> MANILA -- Only one forest guard watches over every 3,376 hectares of forestland , rendering the country 's diminishing woodlands prone to poachers and leaving no frontline personnel to put out fires like the one razing the slopes of Mount Apo , the country 's highest peak . <p> Senate President Pro Tempore Ralph Recto reminded the nation of this dismal ratio on Monday , citing data from official reports sent to the Senate , and noting that to neglect forest protection is to waste the P24 billion invested thus far in the National Greening Program , centerpiece of the Aquino administration 's environment agenda . <p> In fact , Environment Secretary Ramon Paje , who as a young DENR employee had started out in the forestry bureau , had made this - calling attention to the lack of guards - a continuing advocacy .
