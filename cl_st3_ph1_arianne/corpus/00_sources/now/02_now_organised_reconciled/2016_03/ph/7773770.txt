Former Benguet State University ( BSU ) employee Dr. Feleciano Calora Jr . takes oath before the Board of Regents as the 6th University President . ( BSU photo ) <p> LA TRINIDAD , Benguet -- Newly appointed Benguet State University ( BSU ) president Dr. Feliciano Calora Jr . assumed post recently and hopes to continue what the university has gained the past years . <p> " This term is special for me because BSU will be celebrating its centennial year and I want to be a part , " Calora told Sun.Star Baguio . <p> Calora said he will make BSU a student-friendly university under a transparent administration . <p> " I have spent half of my life in BSU , it is high time that I give back to the university , " Calora added . <p> Calora 's proposed vision is for BSU to be a premier university in Asia delivering world-class education responsive to changing paradigms of sustainable development and climate change . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be able to provide quality graduates which will be known for greatness and its administration should be transparent at all times , he said . <p> " We should be able have a transparent and inclusive administration , we have to lay down the dice , and what are we doing ?
