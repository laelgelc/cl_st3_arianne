<h> Benguet State U has new president <p> LA TRINIDAD , Benguet -- The Benguet State University Board of Regents has named Dr. Feliciano Calora , from the Forestry and Environment Division of the Philippine Council for Agriculture , Aquatic and Natural Resources Research and Development , as the state university 's sixth president . <p> He takes over the school from Dr. Jones Feleciano , who has been BSU officer-in-charge since December last year . <p> Calora said he envisions BSU becoming " a premier state university in Asia , " delivering world-class education responsive to changing paradigms of sustainable development and climate change . <p> Some of the goals he aims for during his term are sustaining , developing and providing strategies to enhance food security , quality education , gender equality , sustainable communities , industry innovation , responsive consumption , climate resilience , institutional development and partnerships .
