This week , the group conducted a training program for some 700 climate advocates from 58 countries at Sofitel Philippine Plaza Manila . <p> Despite the grim picture that he painted , Gore assured the participants that solutions were available . <p> Clean energy sources like solar and wind could now be harnessed more easily than ever , and these did not even have to be expensive . <p> Communities in Bangladesh and Tanzania for example , are already using solar energy .
