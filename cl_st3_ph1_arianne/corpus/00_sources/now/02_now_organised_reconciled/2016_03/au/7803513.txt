Photo : Alice Archer <p> Woodside Petroleum sacked 14 staff last year for breaches of its code of conduct , including five for fraud , its sustainable development report has revealed . <p> Nine of the terminations involved harassment or other " inappropriate behaviour , " the oil and gas producer said . <p> Woodside operates a " zero-tolerance " approach to fraud , bribery and corruption , and other unethical behaviour , with employees and business partners able to use a confidential , external helpline to report suspected abuses . Six reports to the helpline were made last year . <p> The report also discloses that Woodside missed its target for increasing the proportion of its Indigenous workforce to reflect the 3.3 per cent demographic in Western Australia , with the count equalling 2.7 per cent last year .
