<h> Recent eastern Mediterranean drought worst of past 900 years and more details <p> NASA scientists have revealed through a new study that the recent drought in eastern Mediterranean Levant region which began in 1998 is the worst of past 900 years . <p> The drought is affecting Cyprus , Israel , Jordan , Lebanon , Palestine , Syria , and Turkey and according to scientists it could be that the Levant region has already started bearing the brunt of the human-induced climate change . <p> To recreate the drought history of the Mediterranean , researchers at NASA resorted to tried and tested technique of studying tree rings -- thin rings indicate stressful years due to drought and thick rings indicate the year when the water was plentiful . <p> Ben Cook , lead author of the study published in Journal of Geophysical Research-Atmospheres and climate scientist at NASA 's Goddard Institute for Space Studies , and colleagues used the tree-ring record called the Old World Drought Atlas to better understand how frequently and how severe Mediterranean droughts have been in the past . Rings of trees both @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from northern Africa , Greece , Lebanon , Jordan , Syria and Turkey . Combined with existing tree-ring records from Spain , southern France , and Italy , these data were used to reconstruct patterns of drought geographically and through time over the past millennium . The results were accepted for publication in the Journal of Geophysical Research-Atmospheres , a publication of the American Geophysical Union . <p> Between the years 1100 and 2012 , the team found droughts in the tree-ring record that corresponded to those described in historical documents written at the time . According to Cook , the range of how extreme wet or dry periods were is quite broad , but the recent drought in the Levant region , from 1998 to 2012 , stands out as about 50 percent drier than the driest period in the past 500 years , and 10 to 20 percent drier than the worst drought of the past 900 years . <p> The team involved with the study did n't stop there . They also discovered patterns in the geographic distribution of droughts that provides a " fingerprint " for identifying @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ range of natural variation in Mediterranean drought occurrence , which will allow scientists to differentiate droughts made worse by human-induced global warming . The research is part of NASA 's ongoing work to improve the computer models that simulate climate now and in the future . <p> " The magnitude and significance of human climate change requires us to really understand the full range of natural climate variability , " said Cook . " If we look at recent events and we start to see anomalies that are outside this range of natural variability , then we can say with some confidence that it looks like this particular event or this series of events had some kind of human caused climate change contribution .
