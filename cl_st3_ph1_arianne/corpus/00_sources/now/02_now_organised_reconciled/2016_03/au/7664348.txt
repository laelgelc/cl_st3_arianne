<h> Climate change link to extreme weather easier to gauge -- U.S. report <p> Scientists can now attribute some extreme weather events to human-induced climate change , a new National Academies of Science , Engineering , and Medicine report has claimed . <p> Until a decade ago , scientists have n't been too confident about attributing extreme weather events like heat waves and cold events to climate change ; however , the new report says that science now has the answer and that scientists can confidently attribute some heat waves and cold events , and to a lesser degree droughts and extreme rainfall , to human-caused climate change . <p> One of the primary reasons why scientists have n't been able to point fingers at climate change for the extreme weather events is the complexity involved with such events . Consider the case of a hurricane . There are so many relevant factors apart from climate change that it becomes almost impossible to blame human-induced climate change for these events .
