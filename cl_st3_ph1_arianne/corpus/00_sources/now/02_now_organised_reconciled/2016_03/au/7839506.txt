<h> The ABCC : an election trigger few understand <p> The PM is using the building industry regulator as an electoral trigger , but few even know what it is . <p> The PM wants to rein in building unions . Photo : AAP <p> Malcolm Turnbull will likely take Australians to the polls using his plans to reinstate the Australian Building and Construction Commission ( ABCC ) as the electoral trigger needed to call a double dissolution . <p> While former prime ministers have fought elections on issues like the GST , border protection and climate change , Mr Turnbull is using a little-known , and currently defunct , watchdog for the construction industry as his trigger . <p> The ABCC was introduced by then PM John Howard in 2005 as a way of reining in the wilder elements of the Construction , Forestry Mining and Energy Union ( CFMEU ) , who were said to be the cause of industrial disruption and corruption that damaged the national economy . <p> It 's been a political football ever since .
