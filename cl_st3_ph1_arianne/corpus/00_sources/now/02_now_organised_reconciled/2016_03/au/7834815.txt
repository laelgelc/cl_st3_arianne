CSIRO handed over the document to the Senate committee investigating plans to slash 350 staff overall , and it has been made public on the Senate 's website . <p> Doubts over the rationale and planning of the cuts flared on Tuesday in another CSIRO section facing deep job losses , with many Land &amp; Water staff walking out of a meeting with chief executive Larry Marshall . <p> Advertisement <p> " People got fed up of having their questions marginalised , trivialised , and with being lied to , " one senior researcher told Fairfax media .
