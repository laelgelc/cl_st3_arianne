To develop a deeper understanding of the associated impacts on Arctic biological and physical systems as well as the people living in the North and elsewhere , seven major recommendations for a pan-Arctic observing system have been developed at the Third Biennial Arctic Observing Summit , held recently in Fairbanks , Alaska . <p> " It 's vital we implement these recommendations , " says Maribeth Murray , executive director of the Arctic Institute of North America at the University of Calgary , who co-organized the summit . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to move forward on an internationally co-ordinated pan-Arctic observing system that tracks change across time and space . <p> ' Change in the Arctic has national and global repercussions ' <p> " Improved observational data are absolutely necessary to track trajectories of change , to improve regional and global climate models , and for the identification of emerging trends , " says Murray . " This is especially important for Arctic peoples , and also because change in the Arctic has national and global repercussions . <p> " We require a very diverse range of information to manage , plan , adapt , and ensure sustainable development at every scale from local to planetary . " <p> Summit works to create Arctic observing system <p> The Arctic Observing Summit is an international forum to co-ordinate the design , development and implementation of a comprehensive and sustained pan-Arctic observing system . The 2016 Summit was held at University of Alaska Fairbanks , March 11-18 , bringing together over 450 delegates from 30 countries representing scientists , indigenous peoples from Northern Canada , Alaska , Greenland , Arctic Scandinavia and Arctic Russia , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organizations , and Arctic Council observers . <p> The conference statement outlining the final seven recommendations was released March 23 . The statement recognizes that the observing system must be co-designed to incorporate the best of Indigenous Knowledge and scientific approaches with local expertise . <p> Inuit communities want to be part of the process <p> " After 200 of years of active Arctic research , Inuit knowledge holders are still concerned about the type of research occurring in Inuit Nunaat , " says Okalik Eegeesiak , chair of the Inuit Circumpolar Council which supports the conference statement . " There is a lack of communication with communities , lack of engagement with Indigenous Knowledge holders as research partners and little use of Indigenous Knowledge in evidence-based decision-making . Communities want to be part of the process , contribute their knowledge to evidence-based decision-making and will do what is necessary to protect the Arctic , our food and our well-being . " <p> The observing system will include a comprehensive network of instrumentation ranging from satellite remote sensing to human and community observers . <p> The Third Arctic Observing Summit was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ program at the Arctic Institute of North America at the University of Calgary -- in partnership with the International Arctic Science Committee , the Sustaining Arctic Observing Networks initiative of the Arctic Council , the Inuit Circumpolar Council and the University of Alaska Fairbanks . <p> The pressures of our rapidly growing global population are driving unprecedented changes in our social , political , cultural and natural systems .
