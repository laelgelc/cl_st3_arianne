<h> Changing climate spurs Nye to action <p> In recent times , most humans have become increasingly aware of climate change and its effects on those whose lives are likely to be most directly affected . The position of American Bill Nye , best-known for his having hosted the Disney/PBS TV show Bill Nye the Science Guy , is pretty straightforward : " Climate change is coming and it is coming right at you . " In other words , wherever people live on Earth , change is inevitable . <p> People can influence the directions that change takes ; what it takes is awareness and a willingness to take action . <p> Nye identifies a leadership role for himself as he moves beyond his position as critic ; as a notable public figure , he 's optimistic about the influence of his opinion on the general public . <p> No longer content with serving as just a science educator , he challenges the present generation to " change the world . " <p> Nye holds a plethora of advanced university degrees , dividing his time between his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and several universities . Nye is also the bestselling author of Undeniable : Evolution and the Science of Creation . <p> Both articulate and witty , Nye manages to retain a sense of humour while explaining climate change and making observations about how to deal with the problem , drawing examples from many sources -- from baseball to gardening and beyond . <p> Nye starts with the big picture : " Earth is our home ... It 's our residence . We are not renters passing through ... We live here . Right now , we are doing a pretty bad job as caretakers . " <p> Nye 's provocative book can be rather scary . He puts forth plenty of data and many arguments which vividly describe the developing reality , as well as the actions needed to turn around the negative impacts of climate change . <p> Nye 's opponents are well-represented ; as he explains , part of the problem with combating climate change is that " many of our richest people made their fortunes in the fossil fuel industries .
