Flora Bank is a sandy area located next to Lelu Island , the proposed site for the $11.4-billion terminal . <p> " A worse location is unlikely to be found for PNW LNG with regards to potential risks to fish and fisheries , " according to the letter sent by biologist Jonathan Moore and other scientists .
