<h> City of Barrie installs new drinking water protection zone signs <p> The City of Barrie has recently installed the new provincially standard ' drinking water protection zone ' road signs . <p> CTV Barrie Published Tuesday , March 22 , 2016 2:38PM EDT <p> The City of Barrie has recently installed the new provincially standard ' drinking water protection zone ' road signs . <p> The sign was designed by the province in partnership with the Source Protection Regions , with the goal of creating public awareness that certain activities in these areas have the ability to impact our water supplies . <p> The signs will be placed within the most vulnerable source water areas ( near municipal wells and areas around surface water intakes ) in the next few weeks . The first roads signs were installed last week on Lakeshore Drive , just north of Tiffin Street . <p> " It 's important that we are all aware of where our drinking water sources are most vulnerable , so that we can help protect them , " says Katie Thompson , Risk Management Official . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ responders , so that public water sources can be protected in the event of a spill . " <p> The Source Protection Plan for the South Georgian Bay Lake Simcoe Source Protection Region , which includes the City 's drinking water sources , received approval by the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change and came into effect on July 1 , 2015 . <p> The Plan , which was developed under Ontario 's Clean Water Act , introduces a variety of policies to keep contaminants out of municipal drinking water sources , including the installation of ' drinking water protection zone ' signs . About 780 of these signs will be installed in the province with 23 of those in the City of Barrie . <p> Barrie is among the first municipalities in the province to install the signs . <h> MyNews <p> If you are on the scene of breaking news and capture it with your mobile device , share your pictures or video with MyNews.
