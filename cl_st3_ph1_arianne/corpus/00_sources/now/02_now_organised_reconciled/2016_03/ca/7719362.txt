<h> National parks improvements planned , with goal of attracting more immigrants , urbanites <p> OTTAWA -- Parks Canada is warning that it faces a growing challenge in remaining relevant to Canadians -- especially in the largest cities -- so it plans to improve camping , accommodations , trails and amenities to meet the changing expectations of visitors . <p> The strategy for the coming few years is laid out in its 2016-17 report on plans and priorities , which details some of the challenges facing Parks Canada and how it hopes to engage Canadians at risk of becoming disconnected with nature . <p> " Parks Canada is faced with the challenge of remaining relevant to Canadians , particularly in some of Canada 's largest cities . Changing demographics , which contribute to shifting leisure and tourism patterns , have had an impact on visitation to Parks Canada heritage places , " the agency says in the report . <p> One in five Canadians are foreign-born and most new immigrants settle in urban areas , one of multiple factors affecting the numbers and kinds of people visiting parks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ youth are currently under-represented in the visitor base , the report notes . <p> As well , other outdoor tourism organizations " are adapting more quickly than the Agency to the changing needs and expectations of visitors , " including such things as offering more diverse accommodations , amenities such as Wi-Fi and new activities . <p> " In order to maintain its relevance and appeal to Canadians , the Agency is working to attract new audiences and influence them to visit its places by undertaking outreach , engagement and promotional activities in urban centres , " the report says . <p> Environment and Climate Change Minister Catherine McKenna recently told the Ottawa Citizen she 's concerned about past budget cuts to Parks Canada , and wants to find ways to attract more Canadians to parks while ensuring the agency is " true to the mandate " of protecting their ecological integrity . <p> McKenna is in Banff this week to make a funding announcement for mountain parks . <p> Overall attendance at national parks has been on the rise over the past few years , likely influenced by the declining @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ foreigners . <p> Close to 20 million people visited national parks , historic sites and marine conservation areas by Sept. 30 , 2015 , the end of the primary visitor season -- an increase of six per cent over the same period in 2014 . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau 's mandate letter to McKenna calls on her to attract more Canadians , including immigrants , to parks through a few different initiatives , including an expanded learn-to-camp program and ensuring " that more low- and middle-income families have an opportunity to experience Canada 's outdoors . " <p> The Liberal government is making admission for all visitors to national parks free in 2017 to commemorate Canada 's 150th birthday . Beginning in 2018 , admission will be free for children under 18 , while any adult who has become a Canadian citizen in the previous 12 months will get one year 's free admission . <p> As well , the report says Parks Canada 's plans to : <p> Offer new and unique camping experiences , develop and expand programs for children and families , offer " equipped camping " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more Canadians out to the parks ; <p> Develop new technology-based interpretive programs for teens and young adults that encourage exploration and provide various challenges ; <p> Launch a pilot project that offers day trips to select national parks in 2016 with a view to expanding the program in 2017 ; <p> Some of the specific investments planned for 2016-17 include improving the camping experience in Terra Nova , Cape Breton Highlands , Fundy , Forillon , Banff and Waterton Lakes national parks . <p> The agency will also further expand the oTENTik products -- a cross between a tent and a cabin -- at various parks , and along some heritage canals like the Rideau Canal . Parks Canada has promised to add 240 oTENTik accommodations over the next three years . <p> For Rouge National Urban Park in the Greater Toronto Area , the agency plans to open reception/orientation areas , as well as offer interpretive programs and guided hikes . <p> The Canadian Parks and Wilderness Society ( CPAWS ) is hoping to see new funding in the March 22 budget to restore some of the scientific capacity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , such as conservation measures and environmental monitoring . <p> National parks have important economic benefits for surrounding communities and the country , and it 's important to remove barriers that prevent people from enjoying them , said Alison Woodley , national parks program director with CPAWS . <p> However , the ecological integrity is the " foundation " of parks and something that ca n't be compromised , she said . <p> " The core values of national parks are about protecting and experiencing nature , " Woodley said . <p> " We 're hopeful . We 'll be looking ( at the budget ) to see if there has been that reinvestment in science-based management , which was a commitment ( of the government ) . "
