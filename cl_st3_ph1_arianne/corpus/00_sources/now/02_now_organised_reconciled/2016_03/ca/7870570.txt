<h> Cochrane Power denied renewable energy opportunity <p> The Northeastern Ontario Municipal Association is furious that the closed-down Cochrane Power Station was left out of a selection process that granted 16 renewable energy projects across the province valuable contracts with the Independent Electricity System Operator ( IESO ) . <p> NEOMA and the Town of Cochrane have been pushing to have the biomass power plant brought back into operation ever since it lost its own energy selling contract and had to be shutdown in May . Association president Mike Doody blames the fact that Cochrane Power was n't even considered was because " Toronto-based bureaucrats seem able only to look as far as their desks . " <p> " They see nothing but their desire for shiny new power projects , " said Doody in a statement . " These , they declare are ' the winners . ' The losers , however , are the people of Ontario -- taxpayers and ratepayers -- both . Here , in Northeastern Ontario , we have a 12-megawatt biomass power plant , ready to go ; a forest and agriculture @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that says it is dedicated to addressing climate change ... Yet , we are being ignored . " <p> The procurement process Cochrane Power was supposedly excluded from is known as " Large Renewable Procurement Process " where the IESO was looking to acquire about 500 megawatts of energy from renewable sources . <p> That process concluded earlier this month , and 16 applicants were selected with a collective output of 454 megawatts . Many of the winning projects were nominated by municipalities , but all the contracts were awarded to either solar , wind or hydroelectric projects . There were no contracts were given to biomass plants . <p> Although the IESO 's press release does not mention any intention to award any wattage specifically for biomass , Doody said NEOMA was told there were 50 megawatts available for bio-energy producers , which Cochrane Power would have been ideal for helping fill . But he said the IESO ignored enquiries from NEOMA about getting the local power plant into the selection process . <p> " We see that IESO was unable to procure any of its allocated 50 megawatts of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Cochrane , and all of the members of the NEOMA that if we had any enquiries about the process , we should ( email them ) , " said Doody . " This is not what we would call quality community engagement . " <p> While the Large Renewable Procurement Process was going on , Doody says that NEOMA was in negotiations with the IESO 's clean energy procurement department as part of an effort to get Cochrane Power a new contract as a " non-utility-generator , " or more plainly , a small power producer , similar to the 16 producers who got contracts . <p> Despite their efforts to get Cochrane Power a new contract independently , NEOMA says no one at IESO " connected the dots " and decided to include it in the ongoing procurement process . <p> The process , the municipal association concludes , was rigged . Rigged so that it would attract " only new , capital-intensive facility . " <p> " For whatever reason , the IESO chose to specifically ignore existing facilities that had already covered all their capital costs , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , and a proven operator , " concludes the organization . <p> The Daily Press was not able to obtain a response from the IESO on these accusations .
