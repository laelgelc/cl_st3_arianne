<p> A provincial home audit and energy retrofit program announced last month leaves rural Ontario " out in the cold yet again , " says Perth-Wellington MPP Randy Pettapiece . <p> The province announced in early February that it would invest $100 million from its Ontario Green Investment Fund to help homeowners reduce energy bills and cut greenhouse gas emissions . <p> " In partnership with Enbridge Gas Distribution and Union Gas , the program will help about 37,000 homeowners conduct audits to identify energy-saving opportunities and then complete retrofits , such as replacing furnaces , water heaters and upgrading insulation , " the government announcement stated . <p> Of concern to opposition MPPs like Pettapiece is the fact the program does not apply to anyone who is not a customer of Union Gas or Enbridge . The 37,000 homes represents only a fraction of the approximately 5 million private households in Ontario . <p> " Unless you have natural gas , the program as it was described to us will mean absolutely nothing , " said Pettapiece , adding that he 's received many calls from constituents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " It 's unacceptable that the government will not assist individuals who are looking to improve their home 's energy efficiency unless they are lucky enough to have access to natural gas . <p> " Yet again , it looks like the Liberals are leaving rural Ontario out in the cold . " <p> The province gave no details about the start date of the program or the rebate amounts , saying those would be announced in the coming months . <p> However , in its media release , the province said that the owner of a Toronto home built before 1990 could expect to see annual bill savings of about $400 on average by investing in new high-efficiency heating systems , attic insulation and air sealing . <p> The Minister of the Environment and Climate Change , Glen Murray , is the MPP for Toronto Centre . <p> Pettapiece submitted an " Order Paper " question in the legislature this week , asking Murray to " please explain why many residents in Perth-Wellington who do not have access to natural gas were deliberately left unable to participate in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> The Order initiates a process that will compel the government to respond in writing . <p> Rural residents without access to natural gas are n't the only ones left out of the program . Kitchener residents have natural gas supplied by Kitchener Utilities , a city-owned utility , and are also ineligible . <p> " Everyone who wants to participate in an audit and retrofit program should be able to . After all , we 're the ones ultimately funding it , " Pettapiece said . <p> The government touts its Green Investment Fund as a down-payment on its cap-and-trade program " to strengthen the economy , create jobs and reduce greenhouse gas emissions . " <p> By the government 's own estimates , cap-and-trade will raise gasoline prices by 4.3 cents a litre and bump the average natural gas bill by $5 a month .
