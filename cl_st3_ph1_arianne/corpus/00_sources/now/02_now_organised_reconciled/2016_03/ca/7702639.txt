<p> The city recently received a $2.3-million grant from the Ontario Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change that will enable it to study nine subwatersheds throughout the Sudbury region . <p> A subwatershed is " an area of land divided by natural features , " Paul Javor , a drainage engineer at the city , said Monday . " So , watersheds can be further divided into subwatersheds . Junction Creek , for instance , has all the water that would drain into it . " <p> There are four major watersheds in Sudbury , including the Vermillion River ( which includes Ramsey Lake ) , Wahnapitae , the French River and the Spanish River . <p> " You ca n't study too big ; you do n't get the detail , " Javor said . <p> The plan for the subwatershed studies -- the first of their kind in Sudbury -- go back to 2006 , when 17 areas were identified as priorities .
