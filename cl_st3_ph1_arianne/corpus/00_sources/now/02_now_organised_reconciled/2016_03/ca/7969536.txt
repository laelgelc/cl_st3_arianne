Milner plant , saying the Grande Cache facility is losing money at current spot market prices . <p> Alberta power is selling for under $20 per megawatt-hour -- the lowest it has been since the market was deregulated 15 years ago . <p> " It 's no fun in Alberta anymore , " Bobenic said in an interview .
