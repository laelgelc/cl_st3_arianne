<h> The importance of State dinner for American-Canadian relations <p> It is no surprise that there is an important relationship between the United States and Canada as these two countries share the longest border in the world and numerous similarities . In many ways , American and Canadian society are interwoven together . However , recently , the political relationship has been strained between the two countries . This tension is stemming from a number of co-financed projects , such as the Keystone XL project . <p> That once strained relationship changed ; in mid-October of 2015 the relationship between Canada and the United States saw new hope as Justin Trudeau was elected as Prime Minister of Canada . With a new Prime Minister , there arose an apparent opportunity to mend the relationship between the two countries . An opportunity that President Barack Obama did not pass up . As early as December of 2015 , Obama had invited Trudeau to be the guest of honour at a State Dinner . The State dinner is an event of both culture and politics , and is considered a high honor by many . As the two heads of state are expected to discuss a number of problems facing both countries and the world , every Canadian as well as every American ( especially those in University ) , should be paying close attention to the events of this three-day trip , as they are establishing the foundations of future relations between Canada .
