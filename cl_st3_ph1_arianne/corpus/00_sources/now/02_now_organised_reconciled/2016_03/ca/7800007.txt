Last month , for example , Ontario introduced climate change legislation and draft rules for a cap-and-trade program . These are smart steps that provide the foundation for an effective approach to address climate change and foster continued , clean economic growth throughout the province . <p> In California , we 've been effectively operating the world 's most comprehensive cap-and-trade program for a number of years . They can reduce emissions themselves , or " trade " with others who may be able to reduce emissions more easily . In California , we 're investing billions to incentivize clean technologies and build sustainable infrastructure , with a specific focus on investing in communities that most need emissions reductions and economic opportunities . For example , in Los Angeles County , we 've allocated nearly $150 million to improve transit and support affordable housing , provided incentives for about 16,000 electric cars and over 100 clean hybrid or electric trucks , and supported hundreds of solar and weatherization projects for low income households .
