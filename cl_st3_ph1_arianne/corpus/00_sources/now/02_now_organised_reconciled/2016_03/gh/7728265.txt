<h> Mahama pledges support for ECOWAS ERERA project <p> President John Dramani Mahama of Ghana has assured the ECOWAS Regional Electricity Regulatory Authority ( ERERA ) <p> of Ghana 's support for the launch and effective take-off of the regional power market later in the year . <p> The President made the commitment while receiving a five-member ERERA delegation , led by the institution 's acting Chairperson , Mrs Ifey Ikeonu , and Regulatory Council Member , Mr Alagi Gaye , at the Flagstaff House in Accra , Ghana . <p> President Mahama stated that Ghana was working towards meeting its dual target of energy self-sufficiency , as well as being a net exporter of electricity in the region . He , therefore , commended the initiatives of ERERA towards the development of the regional electricity market . <p> He also spoke of the sundry activities at improving energy access to all areas of
