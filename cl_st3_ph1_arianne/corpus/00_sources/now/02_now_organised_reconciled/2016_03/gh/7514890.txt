<h> World 's Total Hydropower Capacity Hit 33 Gigawatt <p> Thirty-three gigawatt ( GW ) of new hydropower capacity was commissioned around the world in 2015 , including 2.5 GW of pumped storage , according to a new briefing by the International Hydropower Association ( IHA ) , 2016 Key Trends in Hydropower . <p> At the end of last year , the world 's total installed hydropower capacity reached 1,211 GW , including 145 GW of pumped storage . <p> Continuing the trend of recent years , the majority of new hydropower capacity was commissioned in China , with total hydropower capacity topping 320 GW , according to the China Electricity Council -- a 19.4 GW of new installed capacity . <p> The IHA briefing also identifies China 's increasing global influence as a sector trend . <p> For example , the Chinese-owned Three Gorges Corporation became the second-largest private power generator in Brazil , after buying concessions to operate two hydropower stations totalling 5 GW . <p> There was also increased hydropower capacity in Africa , with several major projects having recently been completed or nearing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ notable sector trends identified by IHA include increased recognition of the value of electricity storage through hydropower , innovative financing options pioneered by the multilateral lenders , and the increasing influence of climate aspects on hydropower project design and rehabilitation . <p> Richard Taylor , chief executive of the IHA , said : " The latest data shows that the hydropower sector is continuing its strong growth trend across the world . <p> " The new Sustainable Development Goals and the Paris Agreement on climate action have emphasised hydropower 's vital role in meeting the world 's energy , water and climate challenges . <p> " Through its ability to support clean energy systems and provide multiple water services , hydropower can be the key to realising the ambitious global targets outlined at COP21 UN Climate Change Conference . " <p> The IHA is a not-for-profit , international organisation working to advance sustainable hydropower . IHA has members active in more than 100 countries , and also partners with other international organisations , research institutions , governments and civil society . <p> The association 's mission is " to advance sustainable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ renewable energy systems , freshwater management and climate change solutions " . <p> Key Trends in Hydropower is an annual briefing on important hydropower data and activities within the sector . <p> It is built on data from the IHA 's global hydropower database , which contains information on over 11,000 of the world 's hydropower stations . <p> The 2016 edition is expected to be widely cited as the most up-to-date source of statistics on hydropower development worldwide . <p> The IHA gathers statistics and monitors deployment and use throughout the year . <p> The briefing includes graphical information on hydropower capacity commissioned in 2015 by region ; global total installed hydropower capacity at the end of 2015 , broken down by leading countries ; worldwide distribution of pumped storage capacity at the end of 2015 ; and hydropower 's contribution by region in terms of installed capacity , generation and technical potential
