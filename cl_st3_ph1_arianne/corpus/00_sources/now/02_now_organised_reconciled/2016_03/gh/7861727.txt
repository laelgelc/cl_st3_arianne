<p> The Canadian government , in its unveiling of a new economic approach for 2016 , said the decline in crude oil prices has already taken a toll on the economy and will continue to exert pressure moving through the year . <p> " The magnitude of the shock facing the Canadian economy is substantial , " the outlook said . <p> The government said it expects to run a deficit of around $22.5 billion through fiscal year 2017 , a figure that will gradually decline to a deficit of $11 billion by the end of fiscal year 2021 . <p> Canada 's economy relies heavily on oil and natural gas . The provincial government of Alberta , where most of the oil and gas sector is centered , said the pressure from lower crude oil prices meant it was facing a budget crisis not seen in roughly 25 years . <p> Alberta said it expects to linger in recession .
