<h> Losing Marshalls candidates take key roles <p> Five candidates who lost election bids in the November national election were named in key government positions by the Marshall Islands nitijela or parliament on Thursday . <p> Marshall Islands parliament Photo : Supplied <p> They will fill numerous vacancies that had been awaiting action by President Hilda Heine , who took office at the end of January . <p> Former foreign minister Tony deBrum , nine-term senator Ruben Zackhras and former United Nations Ambassador Amatlain Kabua , who each failed to secure seats in the November poll , have been made Ambassadors . <p> Mr deBrum is now the Marshalls ' first roving ambassador for climate change ; Mr Zackhras becomes new Ambassador to Fiji , and Ms Kabua returnes to her previous Ambassadorial post at the UN in New York . <p> The former Nitijela speaker , Donald Capelle becomes chair of the Public Service Commission , and one-term senator Jiba Kabua is to be a Public Service Commissioner . <p> In addition , the United States Ambassadorial post has gone to Gerald Zackios , a former MP @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Note in the mid 2000's.
