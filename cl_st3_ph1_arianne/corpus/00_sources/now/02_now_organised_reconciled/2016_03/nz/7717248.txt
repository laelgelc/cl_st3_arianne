<h> NZ opposition caucus taskforce to Tuvalu and Kiribati <p> A taskforce from the caucus of New Zealand 's opposition Labour party will next week visit Tuvalu and Kiribati . <p> Labour 's Pacific Climate Change spokesperson Su'a William Sio said the delegation will meet with the Tuvalu and Kiribati governments and communities in a fact finding mission with a focus on climate change . <p> Su'a said the taskforce will also talk to a range of people on the ground including students and young adults about how they 're coping with climate change , and their hopes and dreams for the future of their homelands . <p> Labour MP , Su'a William Sio . Photo : parliament <p> He said the taskforce will also explore policy ideas that the Pacific nations have in relation to climate change , including about migration with dignity . <p> The MP said the visit and future climate change missions are an important step towards repairing a credible New Zealand voice in the Pacific on climate change .
