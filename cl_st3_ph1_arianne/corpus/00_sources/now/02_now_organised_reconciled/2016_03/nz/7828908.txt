( NASDAQ : ENPH ) , a global energy technology company , today announced that it has made a USD$4.4 million investment to expand its global research and development ( R&amp;D ) facility in Christchurch , New Zealand . <p> Located in Wigram , Christchurch , the new R&amp;D facility was officially opened today by the Hon. Steven Joyce MP , Minister of Science and Innovation . Gareth Hughes MP , Green Party of New Zealand and Dr Megan Woods for Wigram , were in attendance at the event . <p> This new facility will allow Enphase to conduct global compliance testing and verification capabilities for its microinverters and expand @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , a modular energy storage system that integrates with the Enphase Home Energy Solution .
