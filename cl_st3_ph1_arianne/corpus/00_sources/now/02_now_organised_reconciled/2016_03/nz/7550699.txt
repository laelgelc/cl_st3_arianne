<h> Climate change could region cost US$72b - PNG <p> Climate Change would cost the Pacific nearly 72 billion US dollars if issues are not addressed appropriately , according to Papua New Guinea 's Climate Change and Development Authority acting director . <p> Joe Pokana says PNG is highly vulnerable to the impacts of climate change , as is most of the Pacific . <p> He says low-lying islands include almost 2000 coastal villages with populations totaling well over 500,000 which make them vulnerable to sea level rise and other weather-related manifestations of climate change . <p> Mr Pokana says the region is at risk of having social and economic development overturned but PNG can be a global leader in pushing a strategic framework of economic development , mitigation and adaptation action . <p> Photo : RNZ / Alexander Robertson <p> The Post Courier reports PNG is aiming to achieve GDP per capita of $3,000 by 2030 , as part of this strategy . <p> Mr Pokana says the government also wants to reduce emissions of green-house gases , by at least 50 percent by the same time @ @ @ @ @ @
