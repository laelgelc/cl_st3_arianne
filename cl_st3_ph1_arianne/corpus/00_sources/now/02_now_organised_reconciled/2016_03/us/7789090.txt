<h> Good Friday walk will follow Stations of the Cross <p> For the second year , Billings First Church will sponsor a Stations of the Cross Sacred Walk in downtown Billings this coming week , on Good Friday . <p> The walk will begin at noon in front of First Church , at 310 N. 27th St. , and take about one hour . The public is invited to take part in the event that will take a route wending through the downtown area . <p> The Rev. Mike Mulberry , senior minister at First Church , will be accompanied by the Revs . Steve Gordon of Mayflower Congregational Church and Stacey Siebrasse of First English Lutheran Church in Billings , and members of their congregations . They will be joined by Sister Mary Dostal and Sister Pat Funderhide , directors and co-founders of Angela 's Piazza . <p> Mulberry and the others will follow a manuscript based on Pope Francis ' encyclical , " Laudati Si , " on the environment and human ecology . Participants will engage in responsive readings , Scripture , prepared reflections and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Stations of the Cross . <p> Those stations depict the journey that Jesus took on Good Friday , Mulberry says . " It goes up through Easter , but the primary journey is the one of the incredible suffering and pain he goes through at the hands of the Roman government . So it is a reminder of what courage it takes to walk this path . " <p> It 's a reminder to Christians , Mulberry said , that to be a person of courage requires suffering and pain . <p> " We walk that path as a way to make it part of our bloodstream , to say sometimes life is hard , but we can do it if we do it together , " he said . <p> In tying the walk to the words from the pope 's encyclical , it forges a solidarity with the Earth " as it begins to hemorrhage as a result of our lack of care and love , " Mulberry said . And often , the problems of the Earth affect the poor . <p> " Pope Francis was eloquent in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the care for the poor , and how really to lift up one is to lift up the other , " he said . " The poor bear the brunt of things like climate change . " <p> Volunteers are still needed for the readings that will take place along the walk 's way .
