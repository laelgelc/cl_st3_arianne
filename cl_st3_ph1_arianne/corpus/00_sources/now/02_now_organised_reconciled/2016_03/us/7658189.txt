<h> Department of Education releases science standard recommendations <p> CHEYENNE -- The public finally is getting its first look at the new science standards that the Wyoming Department of Education is recommending for adoption in state schools . <p> The department released its draft recommendations on Friday . Before the standards ' adoption , the State Board first must consider them and open them for another period of public comment . <p> The State Board meets March 17-18 in Hulett to consider the draft standards .
