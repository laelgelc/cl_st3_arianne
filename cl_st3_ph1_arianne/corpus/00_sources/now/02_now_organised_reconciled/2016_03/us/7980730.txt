The White House says more ... more &gt; <p> President Obama commuted the sentences of 61 people Wednesday , boosting a push for leniency in his final year that has seen him grant more commutations than the previous six presidents combined . <p> Mr. Obama granted clemency mostly to low-level drug offenders , part of his pledge to use his pardon and commutation power for people jailed on drug possession charges . <p> The president was meeting Wednesday with some offenders who have been granted commutations of their sentences by him and previous presidents . <p> In a letter to those he granted clemency , Mr. Obama said , " The power to grant pardons and commutations ... embodies the basic belief in our democracy that people deserve a second chance after having made a mistake in their lives that led to a conviction under our laws @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Obama " cautioned those receiving clemency that what they do with this unexpected opportunity reflects not only on each individual person , but also on all those still behind bars who are seeking that same shot at a new life . " <p> Mr. Obama now has commuted prison sentences for 248 men and women in more than seven years . Combined , Presidents George W. Bush , Bill Clinton , George H.W.
