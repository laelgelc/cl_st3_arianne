<h> NGO Trains Kuje Residents On Climate Change <p> As part of move to create awareness on the effect of climate change , a Non- governmental organization , Foundation for Economic Empowerment &amp; Educational Development ( FEED ) has engaged residents of Chukuku Community in Kuje area council in a two days Train the Trainer Awareness Creation and Sensitization Workshop on Climate Change , Impacts and Adaptation . <p> The workshop brought together over 100 community people comprising of men and women , as well as the youths within the community . <p> The workshop aimed at creating awareness and sensitization of local community dwellers on climate change , the impacts and coping and adaptation methods which can be adopted by the community dwellers . <p> Mr. Kevins Dzeremo , the executive director of FEED , in his address , highlighted the negative impacts of climate change on rural communities , adding that the community dwellers have low knowledge on coping and adaption strategies . <p> According to Dzeremo , the NGO was founded on the ideals of community service , self enhancement , with the overall goal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unavoidable impact of climate change . <p> A representative of the community , Yunusa Mahmud thanked the NGO for their efforts and noted that the community has been experiencing a litany of environmental problems ranging from land degradation to a reduction in rainfall . <p> He also noted that a good part of their land is been sold off to developers without their consent .
