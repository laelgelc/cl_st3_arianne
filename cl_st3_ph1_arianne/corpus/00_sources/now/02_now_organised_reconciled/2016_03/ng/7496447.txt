<h> Alternative Sources of Power Average Nigerians Could Use <p> Power is the driving force behind every industrialized nation . In fact , countries with more power generation capabilities can deploy more resources than countries lacking such capabilities . Humans have generated sufficient energy from the earth to destroy it 10000 times over . Some of the procedures used for power generation have led to climate change , which is currently one of the major problems the world is combating at the moment . <p> The Nigerian economy has always been blighted by persistent power problems and mismanagement . This is one aspect of our economy we have never gotten right since independence . Nigerians virtually live like a semi-state within a sovereign state , where each household provides basic utilities such as power and water . This situation has made some metropolitan cities in the country difficult to live in due to the persistent noise produced by each household 's power generating sets . <p> The Harmful effects of power generating sets <p> The fumes ( Carbon Monoxide ) generated by power generating sets have untold consequences @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in their sleep as a result of accidental exposure to Carbon Monoxide ( the silent killer ) . Some other health consequences of consistent , indirect exposure to generator fumes may not manifest at the moment , but in many years to come . <p> What are the Alternative sources of power for Nigerians ? <p> I must say , due to the peculiar nature of the Nigerian state , the alternatives sources power for Nigerians are quite few and far between . Nigeria is still a developing nation , and as such we are mostly dependent on imports and foreign technical inputs for local energy sustenance . This means that most the alternative sources of power are out of the reach of the common man . The initial cost of acquisition and installation of most of the equipment used to harness other forms of energy are more expensive than the current power generating options we have , but their maintenance is a lot easier to manage . <p> Solar Energy <p> Solar energy is simply energy gotten from the sun . The Sun 's energy is free for all and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ limitless . This alternative source of power generation is n't new to most Nigerians . In fact , it seems to be an over-flogged issue in the country . Most solar energy projects in the country have failed to live up to expectations . The government agencies responsible for the effective implementation of solar projects in the country have been unable to draw up a functional solar plan . <p> Solar panels are made up of an array of solar cells that have the ability to absorb the sun 's energy , which is often stored in batteries . The average cost of an 80W solar panel goes for N38,000 . As you can see , the average cost per watt of a solar panel is more expensive that of fuel generating sets . However , the source of energy is free and limitless . You require about 5 to 10 solar panels of 80Watts to power a two bedroom flat with moderate use of electrical equipment . <p> Bio Fuel <p> According to kdbiofuel.com , " a biofuel is a fuel that is produced through contemporary biological processes such as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ by geological processes such as those involved in the formation of fossil fuels , such as coal and petroleum , from prehistoric biological matter . " This definition pretty much sums up everything about biofuel . You may be wondering if Biofuels are readily available in Nigeria , I tell you , they are . There is this greenish-looking Bio gel use for clean cookstoves . These Biofuel gels can be gotten from your favorite supermarkets in town , and are more economical to use than coal and kerosene . <p> Clean cookstoves burn efficiently and are very portable and affordable . They are a lot safer to use than your standard Coal , gas , and kerosene powered stoves . Unlike solar panels , the initial investment cost for Biofuel -- powered stoves are low . A single bio-fuel-powered stove can go for prices between N5000 to N6000 , just about anybody can afford them . <p> Bio Gas <p> The biogas generation process is almost similar to the ones use for biofuels . Bio gas can be used for cooking and other activities that require the use of Gas . The process of generating biogas simply involves constructing a special equipment known as " Biodigester . " The Biodigester is built using empty drums or metal sheets and then stuffed with animal dung for about three days . Methane is generated through this process and can be utilized for domestic cooking if sufficiently produced . <p> In conclusion , the alternative sources of power are n't restricted to the ones mentioned above , just that most of these alternative energy sources are out of the reach average Nigerians . Nigerians can only hope that the government of the day puts in place policies to encourage significant research and investment from the private sector in alternative energy .
