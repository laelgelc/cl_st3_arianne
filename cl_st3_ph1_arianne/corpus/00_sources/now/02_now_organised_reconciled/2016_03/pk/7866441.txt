<h> Heatwave , flood this year 's biggest climate change threats : PMD <p> Pakistan has become the third top-most affected country in the world hit by climate change in the past few years . This year 's biggest challenge will be the heatwave and flood . <p> This was stated by Pakistan Meteorological Department ( PMD ) Director General Dr Ghulam Rasul on Wednesday while addressing a seminar titled " World Metrological Day " organised by the PMD in collaboration with International Center for Integrated Mountain Development ( ICIMOD ) at PMD Headquarters . <p> The PMD official said they would ensure early warning system to facilitate public during a heat wave situation so that the death rate could be minimised as compared to deaths reported last year . <p> He said the PMD was working with the Karachi Electric ( KE ) for ensuring flow of information with it about heat wave so that the KE could stop load shedding during the heat wave this year .
