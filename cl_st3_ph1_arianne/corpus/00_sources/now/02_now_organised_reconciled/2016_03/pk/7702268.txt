<h> Cyber warfare <p> Cyber warfare is an internet-based conflict . It 's the new threat to national security and international peace . We should counter it at the global level . This non-traditional issue is more dangerous like other non-traditional issues which are climate change , terrorism etc . There is a dire need of cooperation among states to counter this cyber terrorism . Our enemy can easily steal the information of our highly sensitive institutions . This generates more political conflict among the states . For example India attacked many times on our website . This created more problems in normalizing relations . Robbers also use this for robbery . It 's my request to the concerned authorities this issue should be taken seriously . We should make new institutions to maintain a process of fool proof security and checks on our high sensitive websites , and to work as a watchdog for the safety of nation . -- Islamabad
