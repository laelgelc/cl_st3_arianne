<h> LEAD Pakistan urges practical steps to conserve planet <p> ISLAMABAD ( Staff Report/PR ) -- Chief Executive Officer Leadership for Environment and Development ( LEAD ) Pakistan Ali Tauqeer has said Earth Hour is not about switching off lights for an hour but is a commitment to play a role in conservation of planet by conserving energy and combating climate change . <p> He said this while addressing at a discussion session about celebrating EH organised by LEAD . <p> He said that their dream was to switch on lights one day without using fossil fuels . " Urged all to support conservation of planet by celebrating EH -- a voluntary grassroots movement celebrated globally to inspire people to protect the planet " , Tauqeer said . <p> " Simple steps like not wasting water in our daily use , switching over from plastic bags to paper bags and curtailing greenhouse gas emissions by resorting to energy efficient travel and lifestyles can help conserve life on earth , " he explained . <p> He said Pakistan ranks number five among top 10 countries most vulnerable to climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Floods , droughts , energy crisis , food shortages , endemic diseases , mass migration , economic deterioration and ecosystem degradation were among major threats of climate change . <p> EH celebrated on Saturday from 8:30pm to 9:30pm to advocate for greater environmental action by switching off unnecessary lights .
