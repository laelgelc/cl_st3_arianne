<h> 10-year flood protection plan awaits CCI nod <p> ISLAMABAD - The federal government yesterday recommended the fourth 10-year National Flood Protection Plan ( NFPP ) worth Rs177.66 billion to the Council of Common Interests . <p> The Plan will span over 2015-16 to 2024-25 and aims at integrated flood management by focusing on non-structural measures such as reservoir operations , flood forecasting and early warning , flood risk zoning , watershed management , flood proofing and insurance , disaster management and other measures . <p> Besides National Flood Protection Plan ( NFPP ) , the National Drought Management Plan was also recommended to the CCI , a source privy to the meeting told The Nation . The source said that during the meeting , the Sindh government presented a suggestion about making a National Drought Management Plan .
