<p> According to a media report , the prime minister has asked the Ministry of National Food Security and Research ( MNFSR ) to start consulting provincial governments and other important stakeholders so that the required funds can be allocated in the upcoming budget to develop an integrated framework for the agriculture sector . The PM 's intervention was much required because the sector has failed achieve the required GDP growth . This also resulted in the downfall of exports . <p> The MNFSR is required to diversify the agriculture sector by promoting new crops and modern techniques , revitalising agriculture research institutions and re-establishing a national agriculture research system by synergising federal and provincial research institutions . Reorientation of agriculture research for development of better crop varieties in terms of yield and disease/pest resistance are also needed . There is also a need for a new system of research funding with focus on research output . Measures for mitigating the impact of climate change on the sector are to be undertaken by the MNFSR .
