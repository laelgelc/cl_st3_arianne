<p> KARACHI : Lights of key buildings in Karachi and other cities of Pakistan were turned off for an hour ( 8:30 to 9:30 pm ) on Saturday to celebrate Earth Hour that is held worldwide annually as a symbol to show commitment to the planet earth . <p> Geo News also joined millions around the world to celebrate the Earth Hour in collaboration with World Wildllife Fund ( WWF ) . Lights of key buildings including Frere Hall , Governor House and Mausoleum of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnaj were switched off from 8:30 to 9:30 pm . <p> Each year , the WWF asks for turning off non-essential lights on March 19 . The event was launched in Australia in 2007 as a way to draw attention to environmental issues including the climate change .
