<h> Dar pins high hopes with Pak-ADB future cooperation <p> Islamabad -- Finance Minister Ishaq Dar , Tuesday , expressed high hopes that Pak-ADB cooperation would scale greater heights in future . Talking to the Executive Director , Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) Sami Saeed here , he observed that Pakistan highly valued ADB 's continued support for different development projects . Sami Saeed extended to the Finance Minister invitation to attend the Annual Meeting of the ADB scheduled to be held at Frankfurt , Germany from 2 to 5 May 2016 . He also shared with the Finance Minister detailed schedule of the meeting which will focus on key topics for sustainable development in Asia , including clean energy and climate change ; partnering with cities ; developing urban areas ; and improving production and supply chains . Minister Dar exchanged views with the ADB senior official regarding current ADB portfolio in Pakistan and also discussed proposals and projects which are in the pipeline . ADB funding has specially aimed to improve Pakistan 's infrastructure and services , and to support reforms .
