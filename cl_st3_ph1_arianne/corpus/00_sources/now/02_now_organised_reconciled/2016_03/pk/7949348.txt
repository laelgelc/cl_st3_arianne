<h> Ashtar Ausaf appointed Attorney General <p> Islamabad -- Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif has accepted the resignation of Attorney General Salman Butt and appointed Ashtar Ausaf as new AG . Sources said AG Salman Butt had sent his resignation to PM Nawaz , who accepted it and appointed Ashtar Ausaf as new attorney . Ashtar Ausaf Ali was serving as Minister of State , as Special Assistant to the Prime Minister on Law since 25th February 2015 . He previously served as Advocate General of Punjab twice , from 1998 to 1999 and 2012 to 2013 . He also served as Prosecutor General of the province from 2011 to 2012 , and as Advisor on Human Rights to Prime Minister Nawaz Sharif in 1997 . Meanwhile , Zahid Hamid has been made Minister for Law and Justice . He will also hold additional charge of Ministry of Climate Change . Zahid Hamid tenured as the Minister of Science and Technology from 22 June 2013 until voluntarily resigning on 21 November 2014.
