<p> Senior track-II interlocutors have urged Pakistani and Indian governments to take forward the bilateral discussions in recent years for a framework to resolve the issue of Jammu and Kashmir . <p> The appeal was made by senior parliamentarians , former diplomats and civil society activists from India and Pakistan who met in Bangkok for the 18th round of the Chaophraya Dialogue last week to discuss the state of bilateral relations , the issue of Jammu and Kashmir , terrorism and extremism , bilateral connectivity , the conflict in Afghanistan , and the shared challenge of climate change .
