<h> Environment Day at NUML to create awareness about climate change <p> Islamabad -- Faculty of Management Sciences of National University of Modern Languages ( NUML ) organized an Environment Day at the Varsity to create awareness among the students about the importance of green environment and climate change . Rector NUML Major General ( R ) Zia UD Din Najam was the Chief Guest of the ceremony while Dean Faculty of Management Sciences Brig ( R ) Maqsoodul Hassan , Directors , Head of Department MS Dr. Naveed Akhtar , HoDs and a large number of faculty and students participated in the environment day . Rector NUML Maj Gen Zia UD Din Najam while addressing the inauguration ceremony said that pollution has become a world phenomena but situation in developing countries like Pakistan is very worst . " We have to educate our young generation about the benefits of environment to secure the future as global warming has become a global threat " , he said . Zia UD Din Najam said that Pakistan is in the list of countries who are likely to face water shortage in coming years , it is the time to aware our youth about the importance of water . He said that we together as nation can defeat any threat but we have to educate our society first and students can play their key role in this regard . Rector NUML inaugurate the ceremony by planting a sapling in NUML lawn and later visited different stalls where students displayed different items regarding green atmosphere .
