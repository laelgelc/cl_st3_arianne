<h> Pakistan among 10 most vulnerable countries <p> Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid has said that Pakistan is among top 10 countries which are the most vulnerable to climate change.Talking to newsmen after inaugurating a water filtration plant at Pasrur city , he said that Pakistan is the sixth most populous country in the world . It is geographically located in a region that is likely to be affected by climate change . He said the government was making efforts to fight climate change in the country . He added that the effective steps could ensure the provision of pollution-free environment in the country . He added that climate change has become global reality and the whole world acknowledges it . It is considered one of the most dangerous phenomena of 21st century . This phenomenon poses serious threat to various countries .
