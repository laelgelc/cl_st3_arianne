<h> Extreme weather events <p> Sir : With reference to a news item titled " Urbanisation blamed for climate change in Pakistan " ( Daily Times , February 29 , 2016 ) , Mr Saleem has made an incorrect link between extreme weather and global warming . This is one of the few areas of agreement between the United Nations Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) and the Nongovernmental International Panel on Climate Change ( NIPCC ) . <p> In 2012 , the IPCC asserted that the correlation between global warming and wildfires , rainfall , storms , hurricanes , and other extreme weather events has not been proved . In their latest assessment report released on September 27 , 2013 , IPCC scientists concluded that they had only " low confidence " that " damaging increases will occur in either drought or tropical cyclone activity " as a result of global warming .
