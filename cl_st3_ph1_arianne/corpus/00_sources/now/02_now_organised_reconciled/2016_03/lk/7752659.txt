<h> The President says the developed nations that were instrumental in environmental degradation are now leading environmental protection . <p> President Maithripala Sirisena says the developed nations that were instrumental in environmental degradation with harmful technology have now turned to nations that are committed to protect the environment itself . He added that the environmental degradation has posed a threat to existence of the human kind itself . The president made these comments at the Ratnapura district environmental summit which was held this morning . The event was attended by more than 3000 people.The president also pointed out that environmental pollution has resulted bad health and poor living conditions for the human beings . <p> The president re called that almost all the state leaders attended world climate change summit in France in January . As a consequence of the summit a protocol over the environmental protection has already been drafted . All the state leaders are scheduled to meet in New York to ratify the protocol .
