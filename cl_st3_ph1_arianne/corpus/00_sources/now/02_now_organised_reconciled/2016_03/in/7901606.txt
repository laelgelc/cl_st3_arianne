<h> The unfolding of leaves of sedge and grass species on the Tibetan Plateau can indicate the arrival of the Indian monsoon , says a new study . <p> Scientists at the Institute of Tibetan Plateau Research have discovered that plants found on the Tibetan plateau unfold their leaves as they " sense " the onset of the Indian summer monsoon . The plateau is the highest and largest meadow in the world , and the scientists studied the behaviour of a variant of Kobresia -- a species of sedge -- that grows at a height of 4,400 -- 5,200 metres above sea level for seven years . <p> They found that the leaf unfolding rates of sedge and grass species that dominate the meadows are in sync with the onset of the monsoon . This is regardless of air temperature .
