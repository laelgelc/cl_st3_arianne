<h> Rising cases of dolphin and turtle deaths spark concern <p> The recent incidents of carcasses of dolphins getting washed up on shore in different places in the state , as also the turtles dying after getting injured by the propellers of the boats , are a matter of concern because it points to the dangers that marine life faces today . <p> TNN Mar 31 , 2016 , 11.37 AM IST <p> KANNUR : The recent incidents of carcasses of dolphins getting washed up on shore in different places in the state , as also the turtles dying after getting injured by the propellers of the boats , are a matter of concern because it points to the dangers that marine life faces today . <p> In the last few of weeks , at least six carcasses of dolphins were washed ashore in different places between Kozhikode and Kasaragod . Many turtles were also washed ashore in the similar manner . Similar incidents were reported from other parts of the country too , including Mumbai and Chennai . This often results in them getting trapped under the boat , thus getting injured by the propellers , " he said . Moreover , both turtles and dolphins come to the surface of the sea to breath and this also increases the risk of getting cut by the propellers after they get disoriented . Both turtles and dolphins are highly sensitive to sound like bats , say experts . <p> The rise in sea surface temperature could also be one reason for the deaths .
