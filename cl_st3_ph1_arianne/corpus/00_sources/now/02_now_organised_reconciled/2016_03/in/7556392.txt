<h> Sustainable development not possible without rule of the law : Rajnath <p> Saying that sustainable development would not be possible without the rule of law , Union Home Minister Rajnath Singh has called for ensuring access to justice for all coupled with building effective , accountable and inclusive institutions at all levels . <p> Addressing the International Conference on Rule of Law for supporting 2030 Development Agenda here yesterday , Mr Singh said peace and harmony were essential for sustainable development . <p> " It is impossible to visualise the world without poverty , hunger , deprivation , gender discrimination , etc. unless and until we create an atmosphere which allows people to use available resources for their development instead of unproductive activities , " he added . <p> " We need to ensure access to justice for all and build effective , accountable and inclusive institutions at all levels .
