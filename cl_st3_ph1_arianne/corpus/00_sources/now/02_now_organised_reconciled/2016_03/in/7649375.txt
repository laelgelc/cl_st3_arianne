<p> Warren Buffett 's compensation for running Berkshire Hathaway Inc edged up 1 per cent last year to $470,244 , reflecting an increase in the cost of keeping the billionaire safe . <p> Buffett 's salary remained at $100,000 , the same amount he has received for more than a quarter century , Berkshire said in a Friday regulatory filing . <p> The cost of providing home and personal security services for Buffett rose to $370,244 from $364,011 a year earlier . Buffett also reimbursed $50,000 to Berkshire for personal costs . <p> Buffett often eschews trappings commonly associated with billionaires , and lives in a 10-room home on less than three-quarters of an acre in Omaha . <p> But he still controls nearly 19 per cent of Berkshire 's stock , and 33 per cent of its voting power , according to the regulatory filing . <p> Buffett is worth $66.2 billion , Forbes magazine said on Friday . <p> Vice Chairman Charlie Munger , who is also a billionaire , drew a $100,000 salary last year , Friday 's filing showed . <p> Chief Financial Officer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ compensation of $1.36 million in 2015 . <p> Executives who run some of Berkshire 's roughly 90 units make more than Buffett . <p> Among them is Gregory Abel , chairman and chief executive of Berkshire Hathaway Energy , who made $40.77 million last year . <p> Berkshire 's filing also said the Nebraska Peace Foundation would propose at the company 's April 30 annual shareholder meeting that Berkshire issue a report describing risks it faces from climate change . <p> The nonprofit group , which owns one Class A share of Berkshire , said such reporting could help Berkshire 's insurance units " identify and anticipate gaps and opportunities , " and confirm their status as industry leaders . <p> Berkshire opposes the proposal . <p> In his annual shareholder letter on Feb. 27 , Buffett said it was " highly likely " that climate change posed a " major problem for the planet , " but that Berkshire 's insurance units would not face big losses . <p> Climate change should not be on any " list of worries " for Berkshire shareholders , he said .
