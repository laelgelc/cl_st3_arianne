<h> Naidu Keen on Getting Relics Back From London <p> HYDERABAD : Chief Minister N Chandrababu Naidu focused his attention on attracting investments to the new capital Amaravati with special focus on tourism during his second day tour of London on Saturday . <p> Naidu , who wants to develop the new capital city as a tourist attraction , given the region 's ancient connection with Buddhism , explored the possibility of bringing back some of the artifacts related to ancient Amaravati , which are on display in the British Museum in London . <p> The Chief Minister visited the British Museum and went round the Amaravati gallery which has stupas , sculptures , stone carvings , pillars and other relics excavated from the Buddhist sites in the ancient Amaravati near the new capital city . According to sources , Naidu showed curiosity in knowing about the period and the details about the original sites from where these relics were unearthed . <p> He reportedly spent quite some time appreciating the limestone carving of ' The Great Departure of Prince Siddhartha ' .
