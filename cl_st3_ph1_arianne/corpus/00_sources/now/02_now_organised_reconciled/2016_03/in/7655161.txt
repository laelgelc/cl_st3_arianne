<h> EMRI device to log spots where heat can be deadly <p> For the first time , the 108 EMRI emergency services will deploy a special device - a heat logger - with the paramedical staff who attend to many heat-related emergencies in the city . <p> TNN Mar 12 , 2016 , 02.03 PM IST <p> Ahmedabad : For the first time , the 108 EMRI emergency services will deploy a special device - a heat logger - with the paramedical staff who attend to many heat-related emergencies in the city . This device will record , in real time , the actual temperatures to which the patient is exposed , right from the place of emergency to the hospital . The project is being undertaken with the help of Indian Institute for Public Health ( IIPH ) University , Gandhinagar , an institute which has played a pivotal role in scripting a heat action plan for Ahmedabad , the first in the country . <p> Jashvant Prajapati , COO of GVK-EMRI in Gujarat , told TOI the heat data from the personal logger device will actually @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ city are associated with heat-related emergencies in the city . " We are currently working on the plan , " said Prajapati . On Friday , at the Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation ( AMC ) head office at Danapith , municipal commissioner D Thara and mayor Gautam Shah revealed the Heat Action Plan-2016 for Ahmedabad city . Thara said that cities , towns and villages across the country are facing the brunt of climate change . " It is , therefore , important that cities start adapting to this change , " she said . <p> The launch of the plan also saw Dr Jayant Sarkar , director , IMD Ahmedabad , announce IMD 's collaboration with AMC and IIPH to provide heat forecasts for the city for the next five days . A major awareness drive like digital display boards , handbills and community programmes through AMC 's health department will be organised to inform people about the precautions they need to take to stay safe from heat . <p> " Climatic situations are a harsh reality of the world today and it is important that we start recognizing these realities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Action Plan is one way we can adapt to the changing climate , " she said.Mihir Bhatt of Climate Development Knowledge Network ( CKDN ) told journalists that the Heat Action Plan forms an essential component of smart cities .
