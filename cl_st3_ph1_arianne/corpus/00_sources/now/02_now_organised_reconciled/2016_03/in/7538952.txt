Photograph : Press Information Bureau <p> The government of India today released a new colour-coded categorisation of industries based on their pollution potential . Industries are now to be classied as Red , Orange or Green , depending upon their pollution loadand will see different rules . A fourth category , White , is for ' practically non-polluting industries ' . <p> The industry categorisation is based on a pollution index ( PI ) which takes into account the emissions ( air pollutants ) , effluents ( water pollutants ) and hazardous waste generated and the resources a particular industry consumes . The increasing value of PI denotes the increasing degree of pollution load from the industrial sector . <p> Industries which score 60 and above on a 1-100 scale are rated Red . Those scoring between 41-60 are Orange , while a score between 21 and 39 is rated Green .
