<h> Dawn of a new light <p> KIPHIRE , MARCH 27 ( MExN ) : On Easter Sunday , Fakim Village under Pungro Subdivision of Kiphire District became the first complete LED Light village in Nagaland . <p> The village is situated near the Indo-Myanmar border in the remote eastern part of Nagaland State , tucked away in the rugged mountains . While villages in this area are blessed with rich biodiversity , their remoteness has deprived them of basic infrastructure like roads and electricity . <p> The Nagaland State Department of Environment , Forest and Climate Change took up an initiative with the help of the District Administration and the State Power Department in organising a programme during Easter Sunday service in the three fringe area villages of Fakim Wildlife Sanctuary . <p> They distributed eco friendly materials and disseminated information on the benefits of using eco-friendly technologies like LED bulbs and Solar lamps . <p> In Fakim village , Chumremo Odyuo , Deputy Commissioner Kiphire participated in the Easter Sunday church service and spoke about the advantages of adopting energy efficient and eco-friendly technology like Light @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ SDO ( C ) Kiphire Hq , Lankonsen impressed upon the villagers that Fakim village being the first complete LED powered village should spread the message of " good light " to others . <p> The villagers submitted their traditional 100 watt tungsten bulbs to Deputy Commissioner and received 15 watt LED bulbs , making the village the first complete LED light village in the state . <p> The Village Council also made a resolution to ban use of tungsten light . Considering that the use of LED bulbs with solar lamp is going to save around 90% of electricity consumption for domestic lighting , the Village Council submitted a representation to Daniel Yimchunger , Junior Engineer , Electricity Department urging an equivalent concession on their community electricity bill . <p> Meanwhile , Lansothung Lotha , Range Forest Officer and John Yimchunger , Forest guard participated in the special Easter church services in Thanamir and Vongtsuvong villages respectively . He appealed to other government departments to replicate similar programmes . <p> Likewise solar lights are a boon in rural areas . They extend working hours and are health friendly unlike kerosene lamp and reduce fire chances particularly in wooden houses of rural area . They save energy , reduce maintenance and help in controlling local pollution and climate change . In the initial stage , switching to LED and solar technology may cost more but within one year , the investment can be recovered since the power consumption will be reduced and the technology is more durable .
