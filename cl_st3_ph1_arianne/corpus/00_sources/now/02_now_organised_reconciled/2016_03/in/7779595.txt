<h> 64,187 electric units saved in NMC efficiency campaign <p> Proshun Chakraborty &amp; Manka Behl <p> Mar 19 , 2016 , 04.04 AM IST <p> Nagpur : Since it was launched in January 2014 , the Nagpur Municipal Corporation 's ( NMC ) energy efficiency campaign has continued to receive huge response and desired results . Mayor Pravin Datke on Friday claimed that the civic body has saved 64,189 units of power in the last 24 full moon days . Subsequently it has also saved 5 lakh litres of water , 32,000kg of coal and prevented carbon emission of 64,000kg . <p> " The then mayor Anil Sole had , in association with a city based NGO , Green Vigil Foundation ( GV ) , launched the NMC 's energy efficiency campaign on January 15 , 2014 , " said Datke . <p> Sole had also initiated the Earth Hour programme in which many countries in the world kept their lights off for one hour every year in order to preserve electricity . He had also appealed the citizens to cooperate in NMC 's efforts to save electricity , the environment and protect mineral resources . <p> The joint campaign for keeping lights off every full moon or ' Poornima Diwas ' seems to have worked , as a total of 32,751 individuals made commitments to switch off the lights of their residences between 8pm and 9pm . Now , after considering every individual acted as per his/her commitment and as per the study of the electric department , the campaign has actually helped the NMC to save 64,189.16 units in the last two years . <p> Co-founder of the GVF Kaustav Chatterjee explained how the campaign has helped the civic body to protect the environment .
