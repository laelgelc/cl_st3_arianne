<h> Rumjhum Chatterjee becomes first woman chairperson of CII NR <p> LUCKNOW : Rumjhum Chatterjee has become the first woman chairperson of the Confederation of Indian Industry ( CII ) Northern Regional Council for the year 2016-17 . Sumant Sinha , an advocate of solutions to climate change through business and public policy , has been elected Deputy-Chairman of CII NR . The new incumbents were elected at the first meeting of the new Regional Council members held in New Delhi on March 18 . <p> Rumjhum Chatterjee is one of the co-founders of Feedback Infra Group and serves as the Group Managing Director- Human Capital Development ( HCD ) and is also the chairperson of the Feedback Foundation Trust . CII Northern Region comprises eight states of Punjab , Haryana , Himachal Pradesh , Jammu and Kashmir , Uttarakhand , Rajasthan , Delhi , Uttar Pradesh and the Union Territory of Chandigarh . <p> Rumjhum is a leading practitioner of the management of human capital in the infrastructure sector . She has represented the industry 's views on the subject before Parliamentarians in India and also participated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with Prime Minister of Japan Shinzo Abe , during his visit to India in January 2014 . She is also a member of the Governing Board of HelpAge India and a Trustee of HDFC Schools . <p> Speaking on the occasion Rumjhum said , " The driving force behind the much-propagated entrepreneurship development and startups are the youth of the nation . India needs to have an improved strategic framework to effectively skill lakhs of people , in skills to help them achieve better careers and job prospects . With this our industries will be more competitive and give real teeth to the ambitions of Make in India , the Digital Literacy Mission and the dreams of a 100 Smart Cities . Mapping of the skill requirements must be done at the sectorial , district and trade level . Skill Development Hubs can also be created under the PPP mode near MSME clusters . Industry must set up skill labs in schools . " <p> The new CII NR Deputy Chairman Sumant Sinha is the Founder Chairman and CEO of ReNew Power . He has authored more than 100 articles on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issues related to climate change in leading publications .
