OTHER STATES <h> Haryana to change name of nilgai to roze <p> The Haryana government on Thursday said it will soon bring a proposal to change the name of nilgai to ' roze ' and will also give permission to kill the animal to protect crops . <p> During question hour in Assembly , Kehar Singh MLA asked Haryana Forest Minister Rao Narbir Singh whether there was any proposal under consideration of the government to tackle the problem in the State . <p> In his reply , the Minister said that Haryana government had issued 42 permits for hunting of nilgai . " But only two nilgai were actually hunted , " he informed the Assembly . <p> The Minister said people in the State are reluctant to kill the animal because as per Hindu religion , this animal is called nilgai . <p> " We are now bringing a proposal to change the name of nilgai to ' roze ' and we shall give permission to kill the antelope wherever the need arises , " the Minister said . <p> He @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ areas to kill nilgais . <p> Ministry of Environment , Forest &amp; Climate Change , government of India vide notification date December 1 , 2015 has declared Nilgai and wildboar as vermins in Bihar for a period of one year from the date of issue of this notification , the Minister informed the House . <p> He said the government of Haryana has authorised Divisional Forest Officers of respective districts to issue hunting permit for nilgai.
