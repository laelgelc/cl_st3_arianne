<h> Earth Hour is an annual international event created by WWF that urges households and business establishments across the world to turn off their non-essential lights . <p> New Delhi , Mar 19 : Delhi today saved around 229 MW of POWER as electrical appliances were switched off across the city between 8.30 AM and 9.30 PM as part of the global Earth Hour initiative aimed at energy conservation . Power major BSES said its twin arms BSES Rajdhani and BSES Yamuna , that caters to large parts of the national capital , saved around 130 MW and 77 MW of electricity each . <p> " This is more than last year when Delhi had saved around 200 MW , " it said in a statement . BSES had earlier appealed to its over 37 lakh customers to unequivocally support the event and voluntarily switch off non-essential electrical appliances for an hour .
