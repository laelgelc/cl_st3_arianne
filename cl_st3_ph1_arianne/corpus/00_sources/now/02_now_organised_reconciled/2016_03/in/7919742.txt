<h> Bidiruvana , A Ray of Hope for Tribals <p> MYSURU : To mark the birth centenary celebration of Devaraj Urs , a bamboo grove , ' Sri D Devaraj Urs Bidiruvana ' will be developed on 150 acres of forest land near Nallurupala in HD Hote which falls under Sollepura Reserved Forest . <p> More than 50 varieties of bamboo , some of them classified as extinct , will be collected from various countries and will be planted at Bidiruvana . The place chosen has ideal weather and soil conditions for bamboo growth . <p> Of the 150 acres , 20 will be used for growing the bamboo of different varieties . With the help of officials of other government departments , tobacco farmers will be encouraged to switch over to bamboo forestry . <p> Old buildings in the area will be renovated and converted into demo centres to conduct training for Medars , a tribe engaged in bamboo works . They will be provided with training to enhance their income by selling bamboo . <p> The project aims to educate primitive tribals in the region by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ honey and other forest produce . <p> Through these initiatives , the government is paying tribute to visionary D Devaraj Urs . <p> Under the theme ' Interpretation Park ' , there are plans to set up an education centre , research centre on climate change and farm forestry , **25;360;TOOLONG and wildlife research . Deputy Conservator of Forests Balachandra said ' Biduruvana ' which is first of its kind in the state , will be developed on the lines of Bamboo Museum in Thrissur , Kerala . <p> " Bamboo saplings will be planted under tree park scheme . Along with 150 acres of Bidiruvana , the overall development of Kalahalla village will be taken up and the village will become a model one , " he added . <p> Nagarahole Chief Conservator of Forests H C Kantharaju said though there are more than 150 varieties of bamboo , at least 50 selected varieties can be grown in Bidiruvana .
