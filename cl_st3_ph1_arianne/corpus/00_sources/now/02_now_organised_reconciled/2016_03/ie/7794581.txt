<h> The world turns off the lights for Earth Hour to raise awareness of climate change <p> Saturday , March 19 , 2016 <p> Some of the world 's tallest skyscrapers and best known landmarks vanished from city skylines during Earth Hour , the globe 's biggest annual environmental event . <p> This year more than 10.4 million people in Britain joined in with the global blackout , which now includes 178 countries worldwide . The Palace of Westminster , Buckingham Palace , The Shard , The Ritz hotel in London , Old Trafford and Edinburgh Castle were among the famous buildings where lights were dimmed . <p> And across the world , landmarks including the Eiffel Tower , the Empire State Building and Tokyo Tower also faded into in the night sky , as more than 350 landmarks spanning from New York to Beijing fell dark between 8.30pm and 9.30pm local time on Saturday .
