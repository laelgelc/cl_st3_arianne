<h> Donegal people urged to switch off lights tonight for climate change awareness <p> 09:33 Saturday 19th of March 2016 <p> As part of climate change awareness , " Earth Hour " will take place between 8.30pm and 9.30pm tonight . <p> During this hour , Donegal people are encouraged to switch off lights as an awareness exercise around climate change and the amount of energy we are using up from the earth 's resources . <p> Catholic Churches around the county are also supporting this initiative and helping to increase awareness . <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Donegal Now editorial team .
