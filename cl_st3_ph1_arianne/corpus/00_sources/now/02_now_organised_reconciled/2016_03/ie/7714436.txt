<h> Climate change expert urges Louth County Council to take preventative measures on flood protection <p> Leading Irish climate change expert Prof John Sweeney has warned that increased winter flooding will affect the whole of the country in years to come and has urged Louth to take action sooner rather than later . <p> The NUI Maynooth professor hosted a talk in The Spirit Store last week and afterwards spoke to Talk of the Town about what the future held for both Ireland and Dundalk . <p> He said : " Ireland is warming at present along similar lines to the global average and everywhere is warmer by 0.5oC than 30 years ago . <p> " However significant changes in rainfall and water availability is likely to accompany such changes in the years ahead .
