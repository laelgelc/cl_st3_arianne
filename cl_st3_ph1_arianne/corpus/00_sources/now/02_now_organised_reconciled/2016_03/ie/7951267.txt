<h> The Australian prime minister promised change and then sat back : until defeat loomed <p> Since becoming Australia 's prime minister last September , by overthrowing Tony Abbott in an internal Liberal party coup , Malcolm Turnbull has been dogged by failure to meet expectations . <p> Abbott was the most right-wing prime minister the country had seen and had led his Liberal-National coalition government to the brink of losing power after one term . <p> His replacement , Turnbull , was a small " l " liberal who believed in same-sex marriage , Australia becoming a republic and addressing climate change . Australia expected a new broom to sweep things clean and the government immediately re-established a strong poll lead . But then he did precisely nothing about any of the policies he held dear , and the honeymoon turned sour . <p> With most polls putting Labor in with a chance of winning an election due by January 14th , 2017 at the latest , Turnbull needed a circuit breaker .
