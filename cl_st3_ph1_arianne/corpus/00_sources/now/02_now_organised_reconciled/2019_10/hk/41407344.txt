As the rivers and the streams in this Himalayan region run dry during this season , most of the people living here -- where many villages are at elevations as high as 4,000 meters -- are compelled to seek other means of obtaining water for their daily needs . This water crisis is mainly believed to be due to climate change . <p> In March 2010 , The Hindu newspaper reported that since 1993 farmers in the region have noticed a dramatic decline in plant biodiversity , increasingly warmer temperatures , and shrinking natural waterways .
