<p> Sharachchandra Bhandary , KATHMANDU:Preparations for the 15th ministerial meeting of the Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation ( BIMSTEC ) , established with the goal of jointly attaining economic growth and social prosperity by realizing the spirit of equality and collaboration , have been completed . <p> According to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs , the preparations required for the ministerial meeting of the BIMSTEC scheduled to take place in capital from Thursday have been completed . The sub-regional organization of the southern and south-eastern countries of Asia was established 20 years ago for the rapid economic development of the member countries . The secretaries and high-ranking officials of foreign affairs of some member-states of the BIMSTEC arrived here this afternoon to attend the 18th meeting of Senior Officials Meeting ( SOM ) before the ministerial meeting , shared Chief of Regional Organization Division at Ministry of Foreign Affairs Mani Prasad Bhattarai . Representatives from India , Bhutan , Bangladesh , Myanmar , Sri Lanka and Thailand have arrived Kathmandu for the meeting , Bhattarai shared . Prime Minister Sher Bahadur Deuba is scheduled to inaugurate the ministerial meeting tomorrow . As per the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countries are arriving Kathmandu tomorrow . In the meeting , discussion would be held on 14 different areas identified by BIMSTEC including trade , investment , transport and communication , energy , tourism , public health , anti-terrorism and climate change . Similarly , it has been expected that the date for the fourth Summit of heads of the state and government of BIMSTEC countries would also been finalized . Likewise , the meeting has the agenda to approve the report of 17th SOM held in the beginning of this year in Kathmandu and report of 18th meeting taking place on Thursday . Discussion on the progress of the outcome of the BIMSTEC Leaders ' Retreat held in Goa , India on 16 October , 2016 and the Agenda of Action , agreed by the Leaders of BIMSTEC during informal consultations at the Retreat will be held . According to the program agenda received by RSS , the meeting will discuss the status of the progress and achievement in the 14 areas of cooperation , appointment of the next Secretary General , date and venue of the 16th BIMSTEC ministerial @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 15th BIMSTEC Ministerial meeting . The Nepali delegation in the meeting is being led by Deputy Prime Minister and Minister for Foreign Affairs Krishna Bahadur Mahara . Likewise , Foreign Secretary Shanker Das Bairagi is representing Nepal in the SOM .
