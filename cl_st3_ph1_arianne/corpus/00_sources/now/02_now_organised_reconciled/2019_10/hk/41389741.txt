<h> Nepal-New Zealand meet <p> KATHMANDU : The Nepal-New Zealand First Bilateral Foreign Ministry Consultation meeting was held in Kathmandu today . <p> Joint Secretary , South East Asia and the Pacific ( SEAP ) Division Tapas Adhikari , and Additional Secretary , South and South East Asia Division , Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade of New Zealand Andrew Needs led their respective delegations to the meeting , according to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs . <p> During the meeting , the two sides took stock of various aspects of bilateral relations and exchanged views on further promoting cooperation especially through enhancing economic engagements between the two countries . The meeting also discussed the Climate Change issue and participation of New Zealand in the forthcoming Sagarmatha Sambaad to be held in Kathmandu next year . <p> The New Zealand delegation consisted of Ambassador of New Zealand to Nepal Joanna Kempers , Second Secretary at the New Zealand Embassy , New Delhi Daniel Thompson .
