Image from facebook.com <p> As protests against political inaction in the face of the climate crisis continue around the world this month , many Buddhists have joined Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) , an environmental pressure group known for bold acts of civil disobedience . In the UK , an " International Rebellion " has been declared with protesters planning to create what organizers call a " peaceful shutdown of Westminster , London " by blocking traffic at key points in the city . Events began Monday , 7 October and are expected to continue until 20 October .
