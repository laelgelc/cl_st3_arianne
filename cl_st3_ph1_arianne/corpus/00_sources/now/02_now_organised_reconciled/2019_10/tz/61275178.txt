" The situation in the Sahel has global stakes , and it requires a global response , " said Issoufou . Niger 's farmers lose 100,000 hectares of arable land every year , said Issoufou , as unpredictable rainfall , extreme temperatures and droughts are drying up watering holes that livestock herders relied upon .
