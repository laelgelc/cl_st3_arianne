<h> The World Bank and IMF mark 75th Anniversary <p> As ordinary citizens around the world are taking to the streets to disrupt the status quo and demand climate action , the World Bank and International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) are quietly marking their 75th anniversary at their annual meetings in Washington DC . <p> In a year of unprecedented public protests demanding transformative system change to tackle the climate emergency , these international financial institutions and their policies are looking decidedly outdated . <p> Between 2014 and 2018 , the World Bank Group has advised on fossil fuel projects in 45 countries , and provided over $12 billion in direct finance . This came after a majority of governments signed up to the Paris Climate Agreement and undermines its goals to limit global warming to 1.5 degrees . <p> The IMF also has a role to play in the climate crisis . Its incessant calls for reductions in public spending have left many developing countries , where climate disasters are most likely to hit , seriously under-prepared for such shocks .
