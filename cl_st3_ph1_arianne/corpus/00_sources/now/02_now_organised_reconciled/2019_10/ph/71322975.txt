Life had been difficult for him , but seeing the urgency of the situation made him volunteer as a sea patroller in 1997 . <p> Thinking about his children 's future made Bigyan want to be a sea patroller for as long as he lives : " It would be a shame if our children could only look at fish and corals in books and photographs .
