<h> ' School of Humanity ' for underprivileged children starts its journey in Satkhira ' <p> This altruistic initiative was undertaken by Idris Ali , a former student of the University of Dhaka , who is currently teaching History at Satkhira Government College <p> To provide underprivileged children with education , an educational institution named ' School of Humanity ' was inaugurated at Patakhali village , under Padmapukur union in Satkhira on Friday . <p> This altruistic initiative was undertaken by Idris Ali , a former student of the University of Dhaka , who is currently teaching History at Satkhira Government College . <p> Children who do not have the ability to attend school due to their parents ' financial crisis will get the chance to study in this ' School of Humanity ' . Besides , they will try to enroll students who are currently engaged in child labor due to poverty , said founder of the school Idris Ali . <p> He also said : ' ' We want to teach students in a different way , which will make them attentive at this school . They will learn their lessons through educational videos , songs and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ food . ' ' <p> The school will also provide knowledge about climate change and how to protect our Sundarbans from destruction in the coming days , he added . <p> During the inauguration ceremony , GM Saiful Islam , lecturer at Patakhali Fazil(Degree)Madrasa , Yasminur Rahman Linken , headmaster of Gabura GLM High School , and Mawlana Mahamudunnabi , acting headmaster of Char Chandipur government primary school were also present .
