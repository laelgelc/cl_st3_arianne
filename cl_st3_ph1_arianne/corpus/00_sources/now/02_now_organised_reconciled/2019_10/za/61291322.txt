<h> Global movement against human-caused climate change steps up its South African presence <p> Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) picketed outside the building of the Department of Mineral Resources in Pretoria on Friday . <p> The 80 or so protesters called on government to make switching to renewable energy an emergency . They also called on government to be truthful about the state of human-caused climate change in the country and to reduce net carbon dioxide emissions to zero by the year 2025 . <p> The protest was supported by several organisations , including Right2Know and Mining Affected Communities United in Action . <p> Extinction Rebellion launched in the United Kingdom in 2018 and has since become a worldwide movement dedicated to fighting human-caused global warming . The movement has been inspired by Swedish teenage environmental activist Greta Thunberg . <p> A protest leader read a letter addressed to the Minister of Mineral Resources Gwede Mantashe . The letter stated : " This business-as-usual scenario has put the planet on to a trajectory that places us in a perilous situation . We need our government to tell the truth and declare an emergency . Today we are calling on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and make the bold steps that are required to a just transition to renewable energy . " <p> Mitchell Black , an XR member , said : " South Africa has some of the lowest climate change literacy in the world , where only 14% of our population even knows that climate change exists ... The government is prioritising businesses and business developments instead of trying to build resilient food and water systems for its people . " <p> Deputy Director General of the Department of Mineral Resources Ntokozo Ngcwabe addressed the protesters : " I am here to receive this letter on behalf of the minister . We are going to submit this letter to him . He is the responsible minister and will be responding to you . " <p> XR has given Mantashe until January to respond . Protesters promised to disrupt some activities should they not get a response from him .
