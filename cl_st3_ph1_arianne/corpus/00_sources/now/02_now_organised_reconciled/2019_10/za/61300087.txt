<h> Index maps the world 's social progress <p> Oct 15 , 2019 <p> South Africa currently ranks 73 out of 149 countries in the Global 2019 Social Progress Index released last month by the Social Progress Imperative . <p> The Social Progress Index Framework asks universally important questions , which influence the overall scoring and ranking : Do people have enough food to eat and are they receiving basic medical care ? Do people feel safe ? Is the society using its resources so they will be available for future generations ? Are people 's rights upheld and do they feel included in society ? <p> Based on these questions , the Index captures outcomes related to all 17 Sustainable Development Goals and is a comprehensive snapshot of a country 's overall progress towards the achievement of the goals . <p> Commenting on the results , CEO of Social Progress Imperative Michael Green says : " The results of the 2019 Social Progress Index shows that social progress is not advancing quickly or widely enough . Our current projection is that the world will not successfully fulfil the UN SDGs until 2073 .
