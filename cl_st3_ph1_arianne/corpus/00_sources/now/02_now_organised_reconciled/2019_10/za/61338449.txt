<h> Political Analysis South Africa <h> Senegal launches its ' greener and cleaner " Bus Rapid Transit project <h> Senegal launches its ' greener and cleaner " Bus Rapid Transit project <h> The Bus Rapid Transit ( BRT ) Project , whose work was launched on Monday by President Macky Sall , is part of Senegal 's commitments under the Paris Climate Agreement to make Dakar " greener and cleaner . " <p> This " green " project was identified at the twenty-first United Nations Conference on Climate Change ( COP21 ) in Paris , as part of Senegal 's contributions to reducing greenhouse gas emissions and improving air quality . Reports on Tuesday , 29 October 2019 , confirmed that the project was launched by President Macky Sall on Monday , 28 October 2019 . <p> " While promoting , among other things , the modal shift of the personal car , even the public transport system , to BRT , Dakar will be greener , cleaner , more viable and more attractive , " President Sall said . <p> In the long term , this bus system with " a high level of service , high frequency @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to " establish sustainable mobility " in the capital by transporting 30 000 passengers daily . <p> The project , which costs 300 billion CFA francs , is built on an 18km line from Guediawaye ( suburbs ) to Petersen ( city centre ) . <p> According to Nathan Belete , the World Bank 's Director of Operations for the Republic of Cabo Verde , The Gambia , Guinea-Bissau , Mauritania and Senegal , who attended the launch ceremony , Senegal will also be able to count on the contribution of the Green Climate Fund .
