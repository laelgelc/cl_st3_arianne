<p> Port Elizabeth -The Eastern Cape , Limpopo and parts of Gauteng are facing dire water shortages due to high consumption and low rainfall . However , if every citizen plays their part to save water , the country will be able to avoid Day Zero , Water and Sanitation Minister , Lindiwe Sisulu , said on Monday . <p> " Water users must do whatever is necessary to ensure that water is conserved . There will not be a need for water shedding if water consumers adhered to calls to save water , " she advised . <p> Sisulu said the current has forced a number of municipalities to implement water restrictions . <p> The Minister noted that climate change is a reality and that South Africa is one of the countries feeling the effects of the phenomenon . <p> While acknowledging that the situation is very serious , Sisulu stressed that there 's no need to panic at this stage , provided that " all water users take collective action to save water " . <p> " I 've been informed by my advisors that the earliest we can expect rain is December ... so we are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ alone . Global water stress surveys place us as high on the list , as we are experiencing what we call water stress .
