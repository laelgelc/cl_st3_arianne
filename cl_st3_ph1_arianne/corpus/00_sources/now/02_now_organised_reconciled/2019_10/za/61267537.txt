<h> Hot under the collar : How heat prevents school kids from learning <p> A new article in the South African Medical Journal recommends that SA schools should be safeguarded against the health and educational impacts of heat . Image : ESA ALEXANDER <p> Changes in climate affect children and adolescents in unique ways - and this is felt most acutely in " non-brick " school infrastructure in SA . <p> University of the Witwatersrand scientist Matthew Chersich , in a guest editorial in the South African Medical Journal , says schools should be safeguarded against the health and educational impacts of heat . <p> In the editorial - titled " Climate change and adolescents in South Africa : The role of youth activism and the health sector in safeguarding adolescents ' health and education " - Chersich said today 's youth would inherit a world made hazardous by greenhouse gases . <p> He said there were around 10-million adolescents ( aged between 10 and 19 years ) in SA , who make up about 20% of the country 's population . <p> " They already face a gamut of challenges , ranging from HIV infection , sexual and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , inequality and gangsterism .
