<p> Greenpeace ship , the Arctic Sunrise , has arrived in Cape Town today and will remain in the harbour until Sunday , October 20 . This forms part of their expedition to provide a platform for deeper engagement and dialogue in understanding national climate and ocean issues . <p> Greenpeace aims to create awareness around pressing environmental issues and educate people worldwide . <p> Climate and Energy campaigner for Greenpeace Africa , Bukelwa Nzimande , told oceans faced a crisis from climate change , plastic , mining and overfishing , and these threats were growing bigger and more urgent every day , reports IOL . <p> " The Arctic Sunrise docking in Cape Town is symbolic of the need for a larger commitment to protect the oceans and fight the climate crisis , and will further illustrate the rich and plentiful marine life in southern Africa , " he added . <p> " The Cape hosts unique and diverse ecosystems , which are a bountiful resource for fisheries and associated fishing communities , and all of them need to be protected .
