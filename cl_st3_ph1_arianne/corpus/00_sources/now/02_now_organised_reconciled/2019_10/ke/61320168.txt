<p> MAROONED Business activities and transport in parts of Turkana were brought to a standstill following the heavy rains Image : HESBORN ETYANG <p> At least 20 people have died and more than 1 , 000 affected by the ongoing floods , devolution CS Eugene Wamalwa has said . <p> He said the government will airlift food and non-food items to people in the affected areas . <p> " We have requested the military and police to help in airlifting food to villages that have been cut-off by floods especially in Bute in Wajir , " Wamalwa said . <p> He said so far eight counties have been affected by the heavy rains . They are Marsabit , Wajir , Mandera , Garissa , Turkana , Tana River , Busia , Trans Nzoia and Kisumu . <p> The CS spoke yesterday while meeting with a task force in Nairobi that will be establishing a digital platform for provision of relief assistance . <p> " If the rains continue , we expect more residents to be displaced and we are therefore calling on Kenyans living in flood-prone areas to move to higher grounds to avoid any further loss of lives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government has set aside Sh5 billion for relief food distribution and water tracking from June to December . <p> " The law allows county governments to set aside two per cent of their budgets .
