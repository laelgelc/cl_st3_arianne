<h> JKUAT to host scientific conference <p> Researchers and Scientists meet at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology main campus . File , Standard Researchers , scientists and exhibitors from various parts of the world are set to converge for the International Scientific , Technological and Industrialization Conference and Exhibition at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology ( JKUAT ) , mid next month . <p> The two-day international conference which enters its 14th edition this year will be held on November 14 and 15 , 2019 , under the theme : Leveraging on Research , Innovation and Entrepreneurship for Sustainable Development . The conference has attracted researchers , scientists and exhibitors from across Africa and beyond . Slated to be held at JKUAT Main Campus , Juja , the participants will deliberate on all spheres of life under various sub-themes including , basic and applied sciences for advancement of research and industrialization , advances in medical and veterinary sciences for quality life , engineering technologies , built environment and infrastructure for industrialization , information and communication technology and big data for development . <p> Other sub-themes include : policy , governance , entrepreneurship , cultural and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for sustainable food and nutritional security , as well as water , energy , environment and climate change management for sustainable development .
