<p> Chennai : Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping had six hours of direct discussion on an one-to-one level during the informal summit at Mamallapuram . The Kashmir issue did not figure in the talks between the two leaders , Foreign Secretary Vijay Gokhale said . <p> In a press briefing after the summit , Gokhale affirmed quite categorically that Jammu and Kashmir ( J&amp;K ) was not raised and not discussed between the two leaders . He noted that the government of India has already made it clear that it is an internal affair of the country . <p> Replying to a query on radicalisation and terrorism , he said that it was an overarching discussions on strategic issues . There was no specific discussion of any kind on Pakistan . <p> In a press briefing on Friday late night Gokhale had said that Prime Minister Modi and Xi acknowledged that radicalisation is a matter of concern and a common challenge faced by both countries , which are diverse , in an increasingly complex world . He said the leaders decided to work together to overcome the challenge of terrorism . <p> There was a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issue of reducing trade deficit , it has been decided to set up a new mechanism to discuss trade , investment and services at an elevated level of Vice-Premier of China and Finance Minister of India . The discussion on activating this mechanism will be discussed at the diplomatic level . <p> Xi raised the issue of the two countries engaging more on the defensive security side . In this regard , the Defence minister has been invited to China , Gokhale said . <p> There has been a long discussion on enhancing people -to-people ties and on the occasion of 70 years of India-China relationships next year , it has been decided to celebrate it by organising 70 events- 35 on each side . <p> Modi also specifically proposed on giving greater importance to tourism . Xi gave a positive response to this suggestion . The other areas focussed included on having an independent and autonomous foreign policies and climate change . The international issues discussed during the summit included Afghanistan , the foreign secretary said .
