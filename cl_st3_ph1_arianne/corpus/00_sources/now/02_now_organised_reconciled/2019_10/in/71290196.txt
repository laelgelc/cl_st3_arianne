<h> Monsoon bids adieu to Gujarat , winter to set in by Nov 15 <p> Winter is likely to commence in Gujarat from November 15 . The southwest monsoon withdrew from Gujarat on Monday . Till winter officially commences in state , ' mixed season ' is likely to continue where days would be hotter and nights a bit cold , according to private weather forecasters . <p> Mahesh Palawat , Vice President of Meteorological and Climate Change in Skymet weather said , " The south west monsoon got official withdrawn from Gujarat on October 14 . There has also been an increase in overall temperature which will rise in the coming days as this is called the second summer . With the start of November , the temperature will gradually decrease . Winter is likely to commence in Gujarat after November 15 . " <p> The onset of monsoon in Gujarat this year was in the last week of June .
