<p> While climate change and global warming have become major talking points across the world , a recent study conducted by scientists of Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology ( IITM ) has thrown up an alarming finding . According to researchers of IITM , the Indian Ocean , the third-largest of the world 's oceanic divisions , is experiencing marine heatwaves similar to the ones observed on land , making the ocean warmer and affecting marine life . <p> Experts from IITM have found that the increase in the water temperature is also affecting the monsoon cycle by weakening it . <p> The findings have been published in a report of Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , a United Nations organisation that works on climate change . The study shows that warm events in the Indian subcontinent are on the rise . <p> " The marine heatwaves are similar to the ones on the ground , rapidly changing the temperature of the ocean . Largescale warming , with devastating effects on sea life , is taking place , " said Roxy Koll , climate scientist at IITM and lead author of the IPCC report . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ even coral reefs and other biodiversity hotspots are getting affected due to the soaring temperatures in major waterbodies . <p> Apart from marine life , the warming is directly affecting the weather conditions on land as well . <p> " A 2017 study highlighted a threefold increase in extreme weather events such as cyclones and fluctuations in rainfall along the west coast and central India from the 1950s to now , " Koll said . <p> The scientist further added that the warming of an ocean increases the moisture-holding capacity of the waterbody .
