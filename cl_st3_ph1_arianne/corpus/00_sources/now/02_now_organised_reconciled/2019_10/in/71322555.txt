To some , high-tech developments in agriculture are a cause of dismay . The innovations cost a lot of money , and the companies will recover it from farmers , says Miguel Altieri , Professor of Agroecology at the University of California , Berkeley . They would make farmers more dependent on the corporations , not less . There were traditional systems of agriculture that have stood the test of time , and are resilient and sustainable . " When you look at the total output ( of eggs , milk , vegetables ) , and not just the yield from one crop , you will see that small farms are more productive than large farms , " said Altieri , keeping the sustainability debate open .
