<p> With heavy deforestation , jewel beetle species is now confined to Nallamala forest in Telangana . <p> Nagarkurnool : Jewel beetles , once commonly found and treasured by children , who used to play with them by putting them in empty matchboxes , ventilated boxes and feeding them with leaves during the Dasara vacations , have now become rare in the State . <p> The jewel beetles , called called ' Binganna , ' ' Jinganna ' and ' Bangaru Purugu ' in different parts of Telangana , that used to be common during the monsoon , a couple of decades ago ; have now become rare . <p> Experts say that this is a result of large-scale deforestation in plain areas , ground , air and water pollution , use of chemical fertilizers and pesticides and as a result climate change . These conditions have resulted in these beetles being confined to only tropical deciduous forests such as the Nallamala forests in Telangana . <p> These insects are members of the 15,000-odd Sternocera species in the family Buprestidae under the order of Coleoptera and lay their eggs in soil close to the host tree , where they @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the crevices of the bark and tender roots . They only leave the tree after becoming adults , when their glossy external body develops rich metallic colours , which change as the angle of view changes . <p> Among many species of jewel beetles that have made Nallamala their habitat , three commonly found ones are Sternocera Sternicornis , Sternicornis Aequisignata and Sternocera chrysis chrysidioides . Though these three look similar in shape and sizes , they vary slightly in their colours . <p> According to scientists familiar with the Nallamala , these beetles make fruit-bearing trees such as Canthium Dicoccum ( Balusu tree ) , Albizia Adorustissimia ( Chanduga tree ) , Ber and other minor fruit-bearing trees native to the forest , Amla and other types of shrubs as their host for feeding and living . During their adult stage , in plain areas they are also attracted to various types of millet crops and flowering plants like marigold , but are least invasive towards them . <p> While cultivation of millets has drastically come down in rural areas in the past couple of decades and successive droughts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in rural areas has rendered the natural habitat of these beautiful creatures untenable for their survival , they have now confined themselves only to forests like Nallamala , where biodiversity has still been preserved despite various attempts to destroy it have failed . <p> In certain countries like Laos , China and Thailand , some species of these beetles are eaten as a delicacy , due to which their population has been on the decline in these countries . <p> ' Elytra , ' the protective wing cases for the hind wings of these beetles have for ages been used as jewellery , apart from other body parts of jewel beetles . <p> Research by Scientists at the Georgia Institute of Technology 's School of Polymer , Textile and Fiber Engineering in Atlanta , US in 2009 has shed much light on how jewel beetles change colour because of light-reflecting properties of the cells that make up their external skeletons .
