<p> Chile , grappling with violent protests that rocked the capital Santiago for days , will not let the riots derail plans to host two major global meetings in coming months , Foreign Minister Teodoro Ribera said on Thursday . He said that there was " no chance " of calling off the Nov. 16-17 Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation ( APEC ) summit or the United Nations climate change conference COP25 in December . <p> Riots that started over a hike in public transport costs prompted days of arson attacks and looting that led to the arrest of more than 6,000 people and at least 16 dead in one of the region 's traditionally most stable and wealthy nations . Vandals set fire to metro trains and stops , sowing $300 million in damage and hobbling public transportation . Chile 's military has since taken over security in Santiago , a city of 6 million now under a " state of emergency , " imposing curfews for five consecutive nights . <p> " I 'm certain that under no circumstances will this come to impact the conferences , " Ribera told reporters .
