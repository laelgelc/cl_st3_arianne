One should talk about emissions not not coal production . This whole premise needs to be re examined , " said Raj Kumar Singh . <p> This comes in the backdrop of India 's growing requirement of energy as the National Democratic Alliance government has set a goal of making India a $5 trillion economy by 2024 .
