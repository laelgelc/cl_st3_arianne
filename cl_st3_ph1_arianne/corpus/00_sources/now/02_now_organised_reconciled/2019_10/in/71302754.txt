<h> 5th JK Agricultural Science Congress concludes <p> 5th J&amp;K Agricultural Science Congress concluded today at the Baba Jito Auditorium of Main Campus Chatha of Sher-e-Kashmir University of Agricultural Sciences and Technology of Jammu . The Congress on the theme " Climate Change Management for Sustainable Agriculture , Livestock Farming and Ecological Development " was organized in collaboration with J&amp;K State Council for Science &amp; Technology Government of Jammu and Kashmir . More than 300 delegates across the country participated in the three day deliberations . Professor Nazir Ahmad Hon'ble VC SKUAST-K was the chief guest in the valedictory function of the Congress that was divided into seven different sub-themes focusing on climate change . Dr Nazir spoke about emerging challenges in plant and animal sciences , sustainable use of land and water resources , farm diversification , upcoming abiotic and biotic problems and strategies to overcome them . Dr. JP Sharma , Director Research , SKUAST-Jammu and organizing secretary of the congress presented the significant recommendations formulated during the congress that include climate resilient agriculture , promotion of conservation and organic agriculture for rainfed areas , conservation tillage , promoting the use of aqua ferti-seed drills for maintaining @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ village ponds for ground water recharge and low cost farm reservoirs through participatory approach and government support . Dr. KS Risam , Vice Chancellor SKUAST-Jammu informed that there is a huge yield gap in rainfed and irrigated farming and potential still exists that can be filled by implementing above recommendations . Dr. Vikas Sharma Chairman Press and Media briefed the reporters that recommendations coming out of the show will provide a way for improving the overall agricultural scenario of J&amp;K state and effective technologies will be developed for the conservation and management of natural resources . Earlier Dr MMS Jama Dean Faculty of Veterinary Sciences extended warm welcome to the dignitaries and delegates for showing keen interest in attending this grand educational show on agriculture , livestock farming and ecological development .
