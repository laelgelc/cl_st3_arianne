Not so , reminds the latest Global Hunger Index that has classified India at Rank No.102 among 117 nations . It is indeed depressing to know that India has slipped seven notches from No.95th in 2010 , and that our immediate neighbours -- Bangladesh , Nepal and Pakistan -- have performed better . <p> The Global Hunger Index ( GHI ) , brought out annually by German NGO Welthungerhilfe and an Irish aid agency , Concern Worldwide , reports on hunger and under-nutrition worldwide based on four key indicators -- undernourishment , child stunting , child wasting and child mortality . While India improved its score in under-5 mortality rate , its child wasting rate ( children who have low weight compared to their height , and is a reflection of undernutrition ) at 20.8 per cent was the worst and a huge cause of concern . However , climate change , violent conflicts and wars economic slowdowns continue to drive hunger in many parts of the world . <p> IS INDIA DOING ENOUGH ? There have been protests at home from government authorities who point out that India 's size is too big for comparison ; that its population is equal to 193 countries .
