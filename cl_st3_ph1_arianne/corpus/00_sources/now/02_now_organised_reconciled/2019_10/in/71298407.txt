<h> Two Bengaluru highways get green cover <p> A N19-Km stretch on NH4between Bengaluru to Nelamangala ( milestone 10 tp 29 ) is now home to 1,200 avenue plants . Similarly , the NH7 which runs from Bengaluru to Hyderabad ( from milestone 524 to 527 ) has also become a 7-km green stretch with 2,000 plants , . <p> Recognising the imminent threat of global warming and rapid climate change , the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways had promulgated green highways ( plantation , beautification and maintenance ) policy of 2015 . The National Highways Authority of India ( NHAI ) put this into action in September 2018 and had completed the planting of trees along the NH4 and NH7 by November . " Planting trees along the highways is very important . It will help in reducing air pollution and dust . It will also provide shade on glaring hot roads during summer and it will reduce the effects of wind and incoming UV radiations , " said Somashekhar , NHAI Project director . <p> On the Nelamangala Highway , some of the plants were already in place but were few in numbers .
