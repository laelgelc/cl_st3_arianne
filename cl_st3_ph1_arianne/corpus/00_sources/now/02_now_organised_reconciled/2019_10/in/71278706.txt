Hakim visited Copenhagen to attend the international mayor 's conference . KMDA had bagged an award on solid waste management three years ago in Seoul . KMDA bagged the award for introducing electric buses and electric ovens replacing the age-old earthen ones . <p> Mayors of 93 cities attended the conference at Copenhagen that started on Wednesday . Hakim and his wife took part in the inaugural session . He presented a paper on the steps taken by the Kolkata Municipal Corporation ( KMC ) and the state government to check air pollution . <p> He said plantation of more than a lakh saplings in Kolkata , along with creation of vertical gardens , have been taken up as a part of the anti-pollution measure .
