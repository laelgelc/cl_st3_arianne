<h> FAC clearances to projects <p> Kashmir Times . Dated : 10/23/2019 12:55:13 AM <h> The hush-hush manner in which clearances are being given for development projects will harm fragile ecology of J&amp;K <p> With the reported clearance of a record number of 125 development projects by the Forest Advisory Committee ( FAC ) of the Union Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) between August and October , the NDA-government appears to be in a tearing hurry to take over forests areas for this purpose . Though the nature of projects cleared for this purpose have not been disclosed by the J&amp;K government , yet it is feared that such projects will cause an irreparable loss to the fragile environment and ecology of the state . Till the Article 370 was in place , it has been mandatory for the FAC under J&amp;K Forests Conservation Act -1980 to get such clearances from the state , which is under President 's Rule since December last year . It appears that all the norms for clearances of such projects have been thrown to winds for extending benefits in terms of laying electrical cables for evacuation of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to few favourite corporate houses of the country . As per the set procedure in J&amp;K , the local FAC has to recommend transfer of forest land for any projects has to be recommended for a nod from the J&amp;K Cabinet . But in the absence of any elected government in J&amp;K , such clearances amount to gross violation of rules and regulations . In a state wherein , most of the forests and vast tracts of non-forest land are owned by the government , it is mandatory to seek its clearance for any project with the concurrence of the local residents , who include forest dweller as well . It is being given to understand that clearances of such projects will lead to the bringing in more development projects besides provide for better transport communications for the people . But such steps will have huge impact on the flora and fauna of the state , which is host to unique wildlife and plants besides life sustaining organisms that flourish in J&amp;K.Apart from the concerns of the environmentalists , it is important to note that a total of 125 clearances @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ total of 97 projects were cleared by the FAC in its 11 meetings in 2018 . It is also surprising to note that none of the local residents or communities have been involved in the decision making process . No public notices were given to the common residents to be affected by such projects in the state , which becomes necessary under erstwhile J&amp;K Forest ( Conservation ) Act-1997 . It is widely recognized that human-animal conflict in J&amp;K has been on the rise as the habitat for the wildlife has been shrinking and people living in forests and close to green areas have become victims of the wild animals with frequent attacks . Encroachments on forest lands and expansion of human settlements in their vicinity have been blamed for such accidents . In view of these incidents , it become incumbent on the rulers that all development projects are conceived keeping in the fragile ecology of J&amp;K besides protection of forest resources . It is also well known that J&amp;K is host to three major rivers which supply water for all purposes to not only the state but also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ millions of people on the water resources of J&amp;K , which is also host to the largest glaciers in the world providing wherewithal to populations of more than two countries in the Asian sub-continent . Instead of pushing through clearances for development projects in J&amp;K , it is expected that better sense will prevail keeping in view the environmental and ecological concerns in minds in the backdrop of Aarey Colony in Mumbai case , where only 2500 trees were to be felled for Metro shed .
