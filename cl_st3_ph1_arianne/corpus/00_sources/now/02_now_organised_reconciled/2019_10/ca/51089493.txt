Unlike plant-based products in which existing food ingredients are used , Atomo 's coffee is created . It 's molecular and includes quinic acid , dimethyl disulfide , niacin , 2-ethylphenol and a handful of other elements . <p> The process , which remains proprietary , has given the world its first synthetic coffee .
