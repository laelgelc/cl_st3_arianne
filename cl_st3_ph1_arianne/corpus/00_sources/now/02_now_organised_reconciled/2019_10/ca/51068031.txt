<h> Province stepping-up to assist cattle producers : minister <p> Steps are being taken to help cattle producers feeling the effects of a long , dry summer , Manitoba 's agriculture minister says . <p> Twelve rural municipalities -- mostly in the Interlake and Parklands regions -- declared a state of agricultural emergency after drought prevented farmers from producing enough hay to feed their cattle . Costs to transport hay for livestock or drill additional wells for water added up , and some producers had to look at selling their livestock . <p> In the wake , the province is taking immediate and long-term steps to help , Ralph Eichler said during a conference call Monday . <p> Struggling cattle producers from the drought-stricken areas can apply to the Manitoba Agricultural Services Corporation to defer loan payments for six months with deferrals up to a year .
