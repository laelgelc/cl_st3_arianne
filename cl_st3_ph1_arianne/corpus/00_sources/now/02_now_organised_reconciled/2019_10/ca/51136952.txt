In the 1950s , the United States pushed a program called " Atoms for Peace , " acting as if it were possible to disentangle the peaceful uses of nuclear technology from the manufacture of weapons . <p> The problem here is that the atoms do n't know the difference between war and peace . When you train nuclear physicists , there 's not one textbook on atoms for peace and a different textbook on atoms for war . Giving people the knowledge and technology to build and operate nuclear power plants gives them almost all the knowledge they need to make at least crude nuclear weapons . <p> Once you know the physics , the only real limitation is getting your hands on enough fissile material to make a weapon .
