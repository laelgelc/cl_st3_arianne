<p> A new map will help nature lovers identify key conservation areas in Eastern Canada . <p> The Nature Conservancy of Canada ( NCC ) has completed a first for the region : a research and mapping project of all the rivers and streams in New Brunswick , Nova Scotia , P.E.I. , eastern Quebec as well as the northeastern U.S. rivers and streams that feed into Canada . <p> The Freshwater Conservation Blueprint provides assessments of the health of rivers and streams , with the goal of improved conservation throughout the region and across borders . <p> The NCC led the three-year project with input and support from more than 80 organizations , including Environment and Climate Change Canada . <p> " The Freshwater Conservation Blueprint can assist municipal and provincial planners , watershed organizations , and anyone else working to protect habitat for fish and other aquatic wildlife , " said Josh Noseworthy , Atlantic director of science for the NCC . <p> " We feel this information is valuable and hope it will be widely used as we consider how climate change and other threats might impact our streams and rivers in the future .
