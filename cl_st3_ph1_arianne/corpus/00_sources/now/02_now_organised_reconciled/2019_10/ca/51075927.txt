<p> They go about their business cleaning the air and filtering toxins without a word to anyone about their effort . They are victims of " plant blindness " , a malady suffered by humans who , for the most part , do not see trees . <p> If bird song arrives at our ears from a shroud of leafy foliage in a tree , we hear the bird , not the sound of the wind rustling through the branches . <p> On a smoggy day , we see the haze , not the clean air machines that are filtering toxins out of the air as fast and efficiently as any man-made affair . Trees . <p> This is life as a tree . The cross that they bear is to go unnoticed until they drop their leaves in autumn only to hear complaints about the work they create for homeowners .
