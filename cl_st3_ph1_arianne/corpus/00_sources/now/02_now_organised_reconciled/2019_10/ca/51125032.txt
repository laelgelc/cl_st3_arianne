<h> David Cavey : Let 's take a close look at what " Forward " means in this election campaign <p> Every time I hear the imperative " Forward ! " from the Liberals in this election , my mind immediately conjures an image from my studies of World War 1 . <p> It is of an out-of-touch British officer cynically blowing a whistle ( as they did then ) and ordering well-meaning citizen soldiers to go up and over the top of the trench into murderous machine gun fire in another futile attack that lacked ingenuity or imagination . Each of those citizen soldiers did so to preserve the society that higher-ranking officers benefited from . <p> With that in mind , let 's look at what " Forward ! " means today and where Justin Trudeau 's elitist government would take us if re-elected . <p> Let 's start with your wallets . <p> Currently , our government is $730 billion in debt . That equates to more than $50,000 per household . You and I owe that . <p> What 's more , this Liberal government has added an astronomical $71 billion to that debt over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Justin Trudeau spend $71 billion on since he has been elected ? Has your life become $71 billion better since 2015 , or $71 billion more affordable ? <p> Remember , it was Justin Trudeau who said during the 2015 election , " I am looking straight at Canadians and being honest ... We said we are committed to balanced budgets . And we are . We will balance that budget in 2019 . " <p> Well , it 's 2019 and Justin Trudeau delivered another crippling $20-billion deficit . He lied to get our vote then and he will do it again . <p> I do n't believe that voters here in Van Centre want four more years of a government that lies , not only about spending , but about things that go on behind the scenes . <p> Unfortunately , your current representative , Hedy Fry , has blindly gone along with everything Trudeau has done . <p> Hedy Fry stayed silent as her Liberal colleague , Jody Wilson-Raybould -- like her , a woman , Vancouverite , and member of a minority -- was tossed under @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ amid the SNC-Lavalin scandal . <p> Hedy Fry did nothing when Trudeau shamefully ( and unsuccessfully ) tried to bully and crush Vice-Admiral Mark Norman because Norman had the audacity to defend the neutrality of our military procurement process from blatant Liberal political interference . <p> In both cases , the SNC affair and the Norman case , Trudeau illegally interfered specifically in order to help his elite Liberal friends . Not for ordinary Canadians . <p> Hedy also stood silent in the midst of Justin Trudeau 's unforgivable , multiple uses of black and brown face . <p> And she 's gone along with Justin 's tax increases that make the cost of living higher for you and other hard-working people of this riding -- young families , business owners , seniors who have paid their fair share throughout their lives . <p> Is that the ignorant and elitist " Forward ! " the Liberals speak of ? <p> It 's time for change . Loblaw is a hugely profitable conglomerate that competes directly against our struggling family-owned independent , grocery stores -- which received exactly zero dollars and zero help from this government . <p> It 's time to end all that -- the arrogance , the virtue-signaling , and the patronizing approach to governing in which the Liberals think they are better at spending your money than you are . <p> I and the Conservatives believe voters are best equipped to decide what to do with their taxes in order to succeed . <p> It 's time for a fresh start and a new representative in Vancouver Centre .
