It always gets really awkward around the table , " says Difruscio , whose family is in Niagara Falls , Ont . <p> Difruscio says he and his older brother generally try to get all sides to find common ground . Failing that , they change the subject . <p> " No one 's really changing their minds , " he admits of what results from these exchanges .
