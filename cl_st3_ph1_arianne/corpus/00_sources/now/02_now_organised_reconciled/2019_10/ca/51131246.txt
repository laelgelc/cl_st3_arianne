<h> Quirks and Quarks ' Bob McDonald taking LitFest to the stars with new book <p> Bringing his new book An Earthling 's Guide to Outer Space to LitFest Thursday , one of Canada 's most approachable science journalists is hoping to give something back . <p> " When I was a kid , seven or eight , " says Bob McDonald , now 68 , " my mother gave me a Golden Library of Knowledge called Planets . I knew nothing about them . But flipping through those pages over and over I was inspired and fell into it . And the space race was happening and I thought , ' One day I 'm going to be out there having holidays on the moon . ' <p> " It did n't quite turn out that way , " he laughs , " but if I can maybe inspire some kids to get there . " <p> Bob McDonald 's An Earthling 's Guide to Outer Space stands on the shoulders of the Golden Library of Knowledge 's Planets.jpg <p> McDonald has hosted CBC Radio 's Quirks &amp; Quarks since 1992 , third in an impressive lineup including @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , full of curiosity , he 's watched numerous launches and landings from NASA command centres , been weightless multiple times near the edge of the atmosphere .
