<p> Climate-change activist Greta Thunberg warned world leaders that " change is coming whether you like it or not " during a Vancouver climate strike that featured First Nations activists from across Canada . <p> Thunberg , 16 , spoke from the steps of the Vancouver Art Gallery during the ninth climate strike organized by the Sustainabiliteens . The Metro Vancouver youth group has been staging Fridays for Future events inspired by Thunberg , who began going on strike from school on Fridays to protest government inaction to deal with the climate crisis . ( Read the full text of her speech . ) <p> The Swedish teen travelled to North America by sailboat in August and has been criss-crossing North America in an electric car to speak with leaders and support local climate activism .
