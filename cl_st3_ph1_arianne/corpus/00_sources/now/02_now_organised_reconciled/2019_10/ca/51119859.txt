<h> 100K in climate march <p> Roughly 100,000 people marched for climate action in the streets of downtown Vancouver Friday , far greater than the organizers ' expectations of 15,000 . <p> The march from City Hall into downtown , reported by CTV News , was one of several held around the world to protest government inaction on climate change , inspired by 16-year-old Swedish environmentalist Greta Thunberg . <p> Similar smaller rallies were held in Kelowna , Penticton , and Vernon , while an estimated 400,000 people filled the streets of Montreal Friday . <p> Students skipped school while adults took time off work to fill the streets across the country , and across the world . <p> " It 's really powerful because this is a youth-organized strike and this time is special because we 're inviting adults and other people to join us in our movement , " Vancouver 's co-organizer Samantha Lin told CTV News .
