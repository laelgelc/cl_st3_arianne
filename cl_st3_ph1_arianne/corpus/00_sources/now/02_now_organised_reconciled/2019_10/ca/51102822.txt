<h> Michael Stipe Releases First Solo Single Since The Breakup Of R.E.M . <p> By Brent Furdyk. 5 Oct 2019 3:56 PM <p> Michael Stipe has some new music to share , with Saturday marking the release of his very first single since the 2011 breakup of R.E.M. , along with an accompanying video directed by acclaimed filmmaker Sam Taylor-Johnson ( " A Million Little Pieces " ) . <p> Titled " Your Capricious Soul " , the new track marks Stipe 's debut as a solo artist , with the release timed to coincide with the worldwide International Rebellion climate change protests scheduled to take place on Sunday , Oct. 7 . <p> According to a press release on his website , Stipe will be " redirecting all of his earnings from sales and synchronization licensing to Extinction Rebellion to help aid their work of non-violent protest of government inaction to the climate emergency . " <p> " I took a long break from music , and I wanted to jump back in , " said Stipe , 59 , in a statement . " I love ' Your Capricious Soul ' -- it 's my first solo @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ exciting shift in consciousness . Extinction Rebellion gave me the incentive to push the release and not wait . Our relationship to the environment has been a lifelong concern , and I now feel hopeful -- optimistic , even . I believe we can bring the kind of change needed to improve our beautiful planet earth , our standing and our place on it . " <p> In an interview earlier this year with Italian publication Repubblica , Stipe says that he 's got even more music in the pipeline . <p> " Eighteen songs are already ready , " he revealed . " For five years I 'd had a clean break from music . Now I 'm writing , composing and recording all by myself and for the first time . "
