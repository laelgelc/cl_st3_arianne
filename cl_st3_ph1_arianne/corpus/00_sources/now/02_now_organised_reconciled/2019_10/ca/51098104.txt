Specifically , the trio requested that the government return compensation agents into affected departments , so employees can have face-to-face interactions " with someone who cares about their pay . " <p> Taman , who is running in Ottawa Centre , said compensation agents are needed because the system could take five-to-10 years to be fixed . <p> " This would not be tolerated in the private sector , " she said . <p> Last year , iPolitics reported that issues with the pay system affected over half of the country 's 300,000 federal public servants , and was estimated to take around five years to fix , costing the government up to $3.5 billion . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said that though the problems began during the former Conservative government , the current Liberal government has not done enough to resolve these issues and compensate people for damages . <p> " The Liberals have not treated this crisis for what it is -- a crises , " Gay said . <p> MacEwan , who 's running in Ottawa West-Nepean , said she 's been speaking to affected employees who do n't know when the problems are going to be solved .
