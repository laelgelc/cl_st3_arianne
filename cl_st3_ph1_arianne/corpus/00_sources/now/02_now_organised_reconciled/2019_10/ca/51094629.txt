The study adds to a dire body of research on birds , one of nature 's most important sentinels . <p> Two groups of birds in Canada , those that live in the Arctic and those that live in the boreal forest , could be almost entirely wiped out if the global climate warms by 3 degrees Celsius , the report 's authors found .
