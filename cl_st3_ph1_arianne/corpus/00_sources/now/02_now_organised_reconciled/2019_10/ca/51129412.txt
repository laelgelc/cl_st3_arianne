Why is CRISPR superior to other approaches , what benefits does CRISPR offer over other these other methods ? JD : Scientists have been curious about the possibility of making modifications to the genome for quite some time . Early tools for genetic manipulation include TALENs , ZFNs , and more recently , CRISPR-Cas9 and related RNA-guided proteins . Both TALENs and ZFNs are based on proteins that have an affinity for a particular gene sequence . Therefore , while they can target a specific gene , each requires a technically non-trivial protein engineering process in order to ensure specificity for the gene of interest . <p> In contrast , CRISPR-Cas9 does n't need this step as it uses RNA as a guide to find a desired DNA sequence . The same Cas9 protein can be programmed to target a new gene simply by providing it with a new RNA guide , which can be ordered online or made quickly in the lab . <p> ?
