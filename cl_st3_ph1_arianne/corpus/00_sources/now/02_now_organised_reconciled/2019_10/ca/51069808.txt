When a person gets all their news and political arguments from Facebook and all their Facebook friends share their political views , they 're in an epistemic bubble . They hear arguments and evidence only from their side of the political spectrum . They 're never exposed to the other side 's views . <p> An echo chamber leads its members to distrust everybody on the outside of that chamber .
