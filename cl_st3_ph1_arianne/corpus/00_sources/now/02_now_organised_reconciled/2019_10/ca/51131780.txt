<p> OTTAWA -- The Conservatives made minor gains while the Liberals stumbled in the province of British Columbia on Monday , part of a scattered election result in which parties failed to make major inroads in the region . <p> Both the NDP and the Green Party also fell short of their most ambitious projections in B.C. , leaving room for Conservative Leader Andrew Scheer to scoop up a handful of new seats in the Lower Mainland and B.C. interior .
