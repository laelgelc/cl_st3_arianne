" <p> The coalition was one of two groups that released survey results Tuesday . The other was the Climate Action Network , which represents 110 environmental and First Nations groups . <p> In both surveys , the Conservative party declined to answer questions and instead provided a brief blanket statement summarizing its climate plan .
