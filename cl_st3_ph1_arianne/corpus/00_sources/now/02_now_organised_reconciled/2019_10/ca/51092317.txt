<h> Market News <h> Head of British Columbia Green Party , which supports government , to step down as leader <p> ( Reuters ) - The head of British Columbia 's Green Party , which is crucial to keeping the left-leaning New Democratic Party in power in the Canadian province , said on Monday he would step down as leader once a successor is chosen and will not run in the next election due in 2021 . <p> Andrew Weaver 's party was instrumental in helping the NDP form a government in 2017 , ending 16 years of Liberal Party rule in the West Coast province .
