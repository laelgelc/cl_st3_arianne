<h> THE SKINNY : Diversification key in addressing housing crisis <p> Anyone who spent any time sitting in a planning advisory council meeting a decade or so ago would recognize that word and recall how often it was used . Back then , Tom Lafferty , who was serving as a city councillor and a member of the planning advisory board , was a vocal supporter of ensuring new developments -- subdivisions or otherwise -- were diverse in the style of homes being offered . Lafferty was regularly discussing the benefits of having diverse neighbourhoods and how developers need to recognize the need for varied levels of housing in the city . <p> Flash forward to today and we have a development embracing that idea as Settler 's Ridge developers are hoping to increase the number of townhouses in the subdivision . Essentially , this would mean a decent mix of single-detached homes and the townhouses . The change would mean not only diverse styles of homes in the subdivision but , one can assume , with varied prices for those homes and units , a more of a mixed bag of residents .
