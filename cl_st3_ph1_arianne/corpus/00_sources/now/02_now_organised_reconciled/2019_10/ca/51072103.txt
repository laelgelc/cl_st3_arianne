<p> One year ago today , Vancouver saw its first climate strike protest . About 20 students gathered at city hall without much fanfare . Today , tens of thousands participated -- young , old and everyone in between -- calling for all levels of government to take action on climate change . <h> related <p> It was organized by Sustainabiliteens Vancouver , a group of teens from across Metro Vancouver who are part of the international #fridaysforfuture and #climatestrike movement .
