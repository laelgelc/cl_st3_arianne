With that in mind , candidates are stepping up their game , working long hours to ensure they reach as many people as possible and convince them to vote . <p> For the Toronto-Danforth Liberal Party candidate , that means starting every weekday around 7:30 a.m. at a transit stop in the neighbourhood . <p> " ( It ) gives me a chance to meet people because as much as we talk about knocking on doors , not everyone is home when we get there , " Julie Dabrusin said . <p> On a relatively quiet post-Thanksgiving Tuesday morning , Dabrusin stood outside Chester Station wearing jeans and a bright red coat , shaking hands and handing out pamphlets .
