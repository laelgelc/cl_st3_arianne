<h> Scientists Warn Nitrogen Pollution Posing a Major Risk <p> LONDON -- ENVIRONMENT -- More than 150 top international scientists are calling on the world to take urgent action on nitrogen pollution , to tackle the widespread harm it is causing to humans , wildlife and the planet . <p> The scientists highlight that " the present environmental crisis is much more than a carbon problem " and are asking all countries " to wake up to the challenge " of halving nitrogen waste from all sources globally by 2030 . <p> Nitrogen , through its many forms -- which include ammonia , nitrogen dioxide , nitrous oxide ( ' laughing gas ' , a greenhouse gas 300 times more powerful than carbon dioxide ) and nitrate -- is polluting our air , soil , and water , posing a threat to human health , biodiversity , economies , and livelihoods . <p> A future focus on sustainable nitrogen management would help prevent millions of premature deaths , help ensure food security , and simultaneously help protect wildlife and the ozone layer . <p> Currently , 80 per cent of nitrogen used by humans -- including through crop , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , energy , industrial and wastewater processes -- is wasted and enters the environment as pollution . Nitrogen losses in the UK are estimated at 1.4 million tonnes a year , at a market cost of US $1.4 billion , globally this amounts to 200 million tonnes a year at cost of US $200 billion . <p> UK scientists are leading global research efforts into the sustainable use of nitrogen in agriculture , transport , and industry .
