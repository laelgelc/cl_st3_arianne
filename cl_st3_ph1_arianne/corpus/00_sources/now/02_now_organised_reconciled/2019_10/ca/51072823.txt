This program , coupled with several earlier phosphate-control programs , have been " fairly " effective . <p> Fairly because they largely addressed sewage treatment plants . Twenty years ago , treated sewage was responsible for six per cent of Lake Simcoe 's phosphate load . Now this has dropped to three per cent and that 's for all the sewage treatment plants around our shared lake . <p> Barrie 's treatment plant is one of Canada 's best-performing sewage facilities . The provincial target is a maximum phosphate concentration of 0.1 milligrams per litre ; our sewage plant averaged 0.03 mg/litre during the year 2017 . <p> But , where does the 97 per cent come from ? Twenty per cent comes from agriculture -- water draining from crops and grazing land .
