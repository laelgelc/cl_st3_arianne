The province formally recognized it as a crisis earlier than most -- Gordon Campbell , when he was premier , declared " war " on climate change 12 years earlier in a floridly dramatic throne speech . The " war " ebbed and flowed over that time , but B.C. has made a determined and fairly sustained effort on numerous fronts to reduce greenhouse-gas emissions . <p> There were legislated reduction targets . The government as an entity was mandated to become carbon neutral , and did so . Local governments and virtually all public entities were encouraged/coerced to do the same .
