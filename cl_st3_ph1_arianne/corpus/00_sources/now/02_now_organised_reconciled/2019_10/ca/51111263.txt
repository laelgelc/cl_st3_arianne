<h> NDP Will Advance Gender Equality and End Violence Against Women <p> THUNDER BAY -- POLITICS 2.0 -- The New Democrats have announced that an NDP government will make an investment of $590 million over four years to advance gender equality and to end violence against women in our communities . For far too long , women and girls who are survivors of violence have been left behind by Liberal and Conservative governments . <p> NDP candidate Anna Betty Achneepineskum ( Thunder Bay-Superior North ) says that this announcement will bring the necessary help and support for those who need it in the region . <p> " Women and girls who are survivors of violence in Thunder Bay are finding it hard to get the support they need and it should n't be that way , " said Achneepineskum . " The Liberals failed to back up their words with strong action to end violence against women and the Conservatives cut funding for women 's groups .
