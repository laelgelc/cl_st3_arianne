A federal carbon-pricing program , which took effect early this year , taxes provinces that do n't have equivalent pricing or cap-and-trade regimes , but compensates individuals with rebates . If re-elected , the Liberals will keep raising the per-tonne price -- from $20 now to $50 by 2022 -- and are aiming for net-zero Canadian emissions by 2050 .
