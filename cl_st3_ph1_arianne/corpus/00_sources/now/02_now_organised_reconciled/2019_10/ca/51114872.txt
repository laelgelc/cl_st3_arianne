This is Tipping Point , a new VICE series that covers environmental justice stories about and , where possible , written by people in the communities experiencing the stark reality of our changing planet . <p> Canada 's North is warming three times faster than the global average . Nowhere is this more acutely felt than in places such as Old Crow , the northernmost community in Yukon and home to the Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation . They were called the Porcupine caribou because they would always cross the Porcupine River to go to their wintering grounds . Climate change has changed their migration patterns . The sun heats up the water and when it reaches a certain temperature , salmon will not swim upstream because their internal clock tells them the temperature is too high .
