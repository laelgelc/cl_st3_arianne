<h> Nature has solutions for a capitalist world awash in waste <p> The climate change and biodiversity crises have the same causes : unlimited development , industrial agriculture and resource extraction . These destroy habitats and emit greenhouse gases . <p> If this keeps up , populations of all species -- including humans -- will crash . <p> Trying to impose " limits " on development , industry , and resource extraction is a recipe for failure . It only triggers push-back from powerful corporate actors and perpetuates capitalism . <p> Ecosocialism -- a Green New Deal -- will also fail if it narrowly addresses climate change . A collective effort to tackle waste reduction and create a " circular economy " is urgently needed -- a flourishing , diverse , job-rich economy based on repair , recycling and reuse . <p> Industry is not inherently bad , but must learn from nature and learn to work with nature . <p> Nature represents a model economy to emulate .
