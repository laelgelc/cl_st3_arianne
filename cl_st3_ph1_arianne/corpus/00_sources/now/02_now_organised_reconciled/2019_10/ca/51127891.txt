The question is how much PR does Canada need ? If it is a matter of regional under-representation , that is , that parties and their supporters lack representatives in regions despite voter support , then one need have only a partly proportional system , and the most straightforward would be that advocated by the 1979-80 Task Force on Canadian Unity AKA Pepin-Robarts Commission . It proposed that in addition to a Commons elected by FPTP there would be sixty members elected through PR either nationally or by province to mitigate but not fully correct the regional distortions of plurality voting . This type of system would be adopted by a number of countries beginning ion the 1990s .
