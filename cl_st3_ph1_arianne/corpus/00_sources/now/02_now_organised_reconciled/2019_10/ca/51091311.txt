<h> Weaver will not seek re-election in 2021 <p> Andrew Weaver will not seek re-election in the next provincial election and will step aside as leader of the BC Green Party . <p> Weaver said in a press conference Monday , October 7 , that he will stay on as MLA for Oak Bay-Gordon Head until the next election in 2021 , but will not seek re-election . <p> The Green Party will begin a process to find a new leader , a process Weaver said he expects to be concluded next summer . He will stay on as leader until then . <p> Weaver was recently diagnosed with labyrinthitis -- an inner ear condition that can cause dizziness , nausea , and loss of hearing - but did not cite health concerns as his reason to step aside and not seek re-election . <p> Rather , he said it was time for someone younger to take over the leadership , since younger generations have more at stake when it come to the impacts of climate change , and cited the recent youth-led climate strike movement as an opportunity . <p> " This incredible movement is being led by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the decisions that we make , " Weaver said . " And their moment is growing . This is also one of the main , if not the main , reasons why I believe it is time for the BC Greens to elect a new leader . It 's time to let another generation take the lead . " <p> The next provincial election is to be held on October 16 , 2021 . <p> Weaver was a renowned climate scientist before entering politics . He was a professor at the School of Earth and Ocean Sciences at the University of Victoria , and has been a lead author with the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate change ( IPCC ) . <p> He left academia to run for the Green Party in 2013 . He was the first Green MLA elected in B.C. and that year became the Green Party 's leader . Weaver said the reason for entering politics was that B.C. had gone from a climate leader to a climate laggard . <p> The Green Party won three seats in the last provincial election , and ended up having @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ NDP , which resulted in the NDP forming a minority government , despite having won fewer seats than the Liberals . <p> The Greens pressed the government to hold a referendum on electoral reform . But last year , in a referendum British Columbians rejected a change from the current first-past-the-post system to proportional representation . <p> Weaver also failed to convince the NDP government to cancel the Site C dam project , and he also failed to convince the government to reject an LNG industry . <p> However , Weaver pointed to the CleanBC plan as one of his accomplishments , since the Greens had lobbied hard for more robust climate change policies . <p> " The foundation of this minority government is climate change , " Weaver said .
