<h> Electric vehicle owners make stop in P.A. <p> The Peavey Mart parking lot in Prince Albert hosted a different kind of car show Saturday afternoon . <p> SaskEV , a province wide electric vehicle group , brought out some electric cars to show the public . There were also vehicle owners on hand to answer any questions people had . <p> Jason Cruickshank , the lead organizer for SaskEV said it is important for the general public to be made aware about electric vehicles . <p> " As we 're looking to tackle climate change and reduce emissions , electric vehicles have a big role to play , just even with the electrification of transportation and so , I think people have a lot of questions , " he said . <p> The stop in P.A. is part of National Drive Electric Week . It has seen SaskEV make stops in Regina , Davidson and North Battleford before dropping in on the Gateway to the North . <p> Cruickshank said according to Electric Mobility Canada , in the second quarter of this year , Saskatchewan was the third fastest growing market in Canada for electric vehicles .
