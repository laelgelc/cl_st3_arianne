<h> Grand Council Treaty #3 Leaders Declare State of Climate Emergency <p> Couchiching First Nation Chief Brian Perrault and Ogichidaa Francis Kavanaugh , Grand Chief of Grand Council Treaty #3 at Grand Entry this morning at the Treaty #3 Spring Assembly being held in Couchiching First Nation . <p> Wauzhushk Onigum -- On Thursday , October 3 , 2019 , Grand Council Treaty #3 Chiefs gathered at the Fall Assembly in Wauzhushk Onigum and passed a resolution declaring a state of climate emergency in Treaty #3 Territory . <p> Declaring the climate emergency is in response to the ongoing global climate crisis and the need to act to protect Mother Earth for future generations . <p> " The wellbeing of our Nation and way of life is of the utmost importance to our leadership and declaring a climate emergency is just one way we can continue to care for each other and mother earth , " said Ogichidaa Francis Kavanaugh , Grand Chief of Treaty #3 .
