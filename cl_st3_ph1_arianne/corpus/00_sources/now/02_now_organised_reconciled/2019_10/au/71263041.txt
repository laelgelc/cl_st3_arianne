<h> Cambodian Rice Federation appeals to European Union to save livelihoods of half a million families <p> Comments Off on Cambodian Rice Federation appeals to European Union to save livelihoods of half a million families <p> In the first six months of 2019 , Cambodia exported more than 93,000 tons of rice to the European Union ( EU ) . That 's a lot of rice , but it 's almost half the amount that was exported during the same period in 2018 . As of the first of this year , the EU imposed duties on Cambodian rice in order to protect domestic producers . This has been acutely felt by most of the 500,000 families in Cambodia who eke out a living farming jasmine and fragrant long grain rice , in spite of the fact that these varieties are geographically specific and do not compete directly with products grown in the EU . <p> As if this were n't painful enough , the EU is now considering the withdrawal of its " Everything But Arms " ( EBA ) program . The goal is to encourage a system that is fair and one that results in clear benefits for growers . <p> In particular , Cambodia is rapidly expanding cultivation under Sustainable Rice Platform ( SRP ) standards . Varieties are grown under stringent conditions that comply with product standard and fair-trade certifications .
