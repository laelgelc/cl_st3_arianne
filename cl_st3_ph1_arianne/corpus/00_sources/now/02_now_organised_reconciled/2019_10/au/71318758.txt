<h> Climate at ' heart of our business ' : IAG <p> IAG 's Elizabeth Bryan says climate concerns " go to the heart of our business as an insurer . " <p> IAG chairman Elizabeth Bryan says concerns over the impact of climate change on Australians " go to the heart of our business as an insurer " . <p> IAG has overrun its provisioning for natural disasters 11 times in the past 14 years has set aside $641 million this financial year to cover such payouts . <p> " We see first-hand the devastating impact that a changing climate has on our customers , and on our communities , " Ms Bryan told IAG 's annual general meeting in Sydney on Friday . <p> Ms Bryan said the insurance giant has reduced the carbon footprint of its investment portfolio by 60 per cent in two years , no longer invests in thermal coal companies , and committed to ceasing any residual underwriting of fossil fuel extraction or use by 2023 . <p> Shareholders will vote at the meeting on special resolutions including that IAG set targets to further reduce investment in coal , oil and gas consistent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1.5C above pre-industrial levels .
