<p> Coal is not the answer to the maintaining an affordable and reliable electricity supply , and a national energy policy must be paired with a price on carbon emissions and incentives for flexible generation , the CEO of Origin Energy Frank Calabria has told a meeting of business leaders in Sydney . <p> Speaking at an event hosted by CEDA , Calabria said coal will not be the solution to Australia 's long term challenges in the energy sector , and that the answer to the growing share of wind and solar instead rests with incentivising truly flexible supplies of electricity , including gas generation , pumped hydro and battery storage . <p> Calabria said the current design of the National Electricity Market was not supporting investment focused on the long term needs of consumers , including reliability and price while reducing emissions .
