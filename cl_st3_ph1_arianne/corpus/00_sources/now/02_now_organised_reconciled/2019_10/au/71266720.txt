Within minutes it inundated Samoa 's coastline , before rolling on to American Samoa and Tonga . <p> While a tsunami warning was issued by the Pacific Tsunami Warning Center and relayed by Samoan officials , it was not rebroadcast everywhere . Regardless , the tsunami arrived too quickly for many to escape . In Samoa , 189 people died when the tsunami reached up to 14 metres above normal sea level and many more were injured across the region .
