<p> BHP sure got a lot of headlines about its declaration this week that the world is plunging towards a climate catastrophe , and that it is determined to Do Something about it , starting with its supply chain . <p> Climate and clean energy activists gave it a round of applause . The conservatives and BHP 's industry peers said it was going too far ; the climate deniers within the Coalition government accused it of drinking climate cool-aid to satisfy left-wing investors ; and the minister for emissions reductions tried to pretend that he took the issue as seriously as BHP says it should . <p> But how good is it ? BHP CEO Andrew Mackenzie has committed to spending $US400 million on low emission investments , and said that the company will put pressure on its supply chain to cut its emissions .
