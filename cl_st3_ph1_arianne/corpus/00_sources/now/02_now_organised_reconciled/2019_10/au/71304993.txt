<h> Diagnosing Asia 's Australian problem <p> For decades , there has been a familiar refrain : Australia has an Asia problem . We do n't engage enough with Asia , or understand its languages or diverse cultures . From Ross Garnaut in the 1980s to the Australia in the Asian Century White Paper , the narrative has been that if only Australia could focus on Asia , all would be well . Indications are that this line will continue under Malcolm Turnbull 's prime ministership : he has urged Australians to seize the opportunities of economic change , and characterised trade with China as one of the most important foundations of Australia 's prosperity . <p> I do n't doubt for a moment that Australia does have an Asia problem . Australia is at pitiful levels when it comes to the learning of foreign languages . Many Australians still see Asia merely as a place to fly over on the way to Europe . <p> However , I do n't think this is the only issue .
