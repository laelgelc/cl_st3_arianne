<h> Complex Dam receives $68k for upgrades to water storage <p> The McGowan Government announced yesterday it will provide grants for dryland agricultural areas to establish or improve non-potable community water supplies . <p> The Complex Dam in the Shire of Broomehill-Tambellup will receive more than $68,000 for upgrades to stormwater storage . <p> The Shire is one of four to receive funding from a $285,000 Statewide committent as part of the Community Water Supply Program . Water Minister Dave Kelly said the partnership would help communities adapt to the impacts of climate change . <p> The Shire of Pingelly , the Shire of West Arthur and the City of Greater Geraldton are the three others to receive funding . <p> In West Arthur , $53,911 will be used to reinstate the heritage-listed railway dam , Kylie Dam , to provide non-potable water . <p> Mr Kelly said it was important to invest now to ensure emergency water needs were met in these rural communities . <p> " Farmers will benefit from these projects as they will have access to emergency livestock water supplies , " he said .
