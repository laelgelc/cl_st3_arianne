Minerals are solid , naturally occurring substances that are not living , such as quartz or haematite . This new mineral was discovered after an examination of the Wedderburn Meteorite , a metallic-looking rock found in Central Victoria back in 1951 . <p> Edscottite is made of iron and carbon , and was likely formed within the core of another planet . It 's a " true " mineral , meaning one which is naturally occurring and formed by geological processes either on Earth or in outer-space . <p> But while the Wedderburn Meteorite held the first-known discovery of Edscottite , other new mineral discoveries have been made on Earth , of substances formed as a result of human activities such as mining and mineral processing .
