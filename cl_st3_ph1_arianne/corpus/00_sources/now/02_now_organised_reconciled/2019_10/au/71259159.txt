The renewable energy part is fairly simple : it refers to energy that is naturally replenished and does not run out . The 100 per cent means that there is enough of it to meet Canberra 's energy needs . It 's the net where things get complicated . <p> If it was a gross target that Canberra had met , it would indeed be running entirely on energy from wind and solar farms .
