<p> University of Wollongong academics have called on Vice Chancellor Paul Wellings to declare a climate emergency . The call kicked off Global Climate Change Week , an event organised by academics , including Philosophy lecturer Dr Keith Horton . " Some universities have taken big steps , " Dr Horton said . " Charles Sturt University is already carbon neutral and a number of other universities have at least made commitments to head in that direction as well , but UOW is lagging behind unfortunately . " Dr Horton said he would like to see the university declare a timeline for becoming carbon neutral as well as looking to divest from fossil fuel investments and introduce climate education throughout the university . Other events over Global Climate Change Week featured talks from academics , including UOW climate scientist Kerrylee Rogers .
