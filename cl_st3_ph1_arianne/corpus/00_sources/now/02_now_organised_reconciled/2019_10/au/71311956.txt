Win win . <p> Of course , cutting back on air travel when possible is the best route -- when possible , train or bus travel has a much lower carbon footprint . For example , a trip to Europe from Australia is virtually impossible without air travel , but when you do touch down , utilise the continent 's extensive network of trains in lieu of air travel . Likewise , plan to visit geographically-near areas in one trip , instead of jetting from , say , London to Rome and back up to Copenhagen all in one holiday .
