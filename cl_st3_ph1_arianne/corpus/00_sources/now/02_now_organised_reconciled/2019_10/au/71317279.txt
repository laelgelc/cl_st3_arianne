After four years of consultation , the council reached agreement with residents in two areas that affected land would be filled in over time , and there would be no forced retreat from homes . <p> The council is continuing to plan , with community involvement . It is developing suburb-specific adaptation plans designed so residents understand the science and embrace the solutions -- including the chance to identify adaptation options themselves . <p> But across Australia , much work remains .
