<p> Two leading energy academics from the Australian National University have predicted that Australia 's overall greenhouse gas emissions will fall over the next few years , driven by surging investment in renewable energy projects , in an analysis that has been widely panned by senior figures in the clean energy sector . <p> Professor Andrew Blakers and Dr Matthew Stocks have made the prediction in a new analysis published by the university , that is likely to be leapt upon by the Morrison Government as proof that its policies are working . But the analysis simply reinforces how much more could and must be done given the technologies at hand . <p> " This is a message of hope for reducing our emissions at low cost , " professor Blakers said . <p> " Solar and wind energy offers the cheapest way to make deep cuts in emissions because of their low and continually falling cost .
