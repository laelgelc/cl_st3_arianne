<p> The Australian Energy Market Commission ( AEMC ) has released a discussion paper as part of its Coordination of Generation and Transmission Investment Review ( COGATI for short ) . The paper outlines the Commission 's proposed approach to transmission access reform . <p> Many people will be aware that the Commission had published a directions paper back in June . There has been extensive engagement with stakeholders : generators , network companies ; investors ; consumer groups ; governments ; and non-government organisations ( NGOs ) . This engagement has been through submissions , workshops and general ongoing dialogue between the AEMC and our many stakeholders . <p> A consequence of this extensive engagement has been some important changes to the proposed model of reform , relative to the June directions paper . <p> Engagement is not just about going through the motions -- it 's best done around the principles of transparency , inclusivity and responsiveness .
