<p> Zimbabwe wildlife authorities say at least 55 elephants have died of hunger and thirst in Hwange national park , the country 's largest natural reserve . <p> Tinashe Farawo , the Zimbabwe national parks and wildlife authority spokesperson , confirmed the deaths to state broadcaster , on Monday . <p> He said the situation at the game reserve was desperate and the authority was looking for funds to sink more boreholes in the game reserve . <p> " We can confirm that a total of 55 elephants have died at the national park over the past two months due to starvation , " he said . <p> " The situation at most of our parks is dire due to the climate change-induced drought . " <p> The game reserve depends on groundwater pumped from several boreholes . <p> Samson Chibaya , another wildlife management official , said the available boreholes were not enough to meet the water demand . <p> " While we have made efforts to try and convert some of the water pumping stations from diesel to solar powered pumps , we have seen that some of the water pans are drying ( up ) , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more resources for installation of diesel pumps to complement the solar pumps .
