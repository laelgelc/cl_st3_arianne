<h> Sindh 's ban on use of plastic bags comes into effect <p> KARACHI : The ban on the use of plastic bags has come into effect in Sindh from today , after the provincial government issued a notification banning the use of plastic bags . <p> According to Murtaza Wahab , the Sindh Chief Minister 's adviser on Law , Environment , Climate Change &amp; Costal Development , the production , sale and usage of plastic bags is a crime under the environmental laws of the province . <p> The Sindh government has also formed teams from the provincial environment department will be dispatched to carry out raids , and ensure that the law is being followed . The teams will be paired up with the police . <p> Those who will be selling products in plastic bags will be arrested and a fine of Rs 10,000 will be imposed on them . <p> A day earlier , a notification by the provincial government invoked the Sindh Prohibition of Non-Degradable Plastic Products and the Sindh Environment Protection Act 2014 . <p> The government further directed the use instead of oxo-biodegradable plastic , which is known to degrade in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of single-use plastic packaging . <p> On August 14 , Islamabad became the first city in Pakistan to declare itself free from polythene bags .
