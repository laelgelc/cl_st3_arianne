<p> by Ian Apperley New Wellington city councillors will be shortly assigned portfolios . But I understand that the model may be changed . It certainly needs a refresh because right now it 's barely manageable . <p> I hear from multiple sources that certain councillors are having temper tantrums over this process . In something akin to throwing toys around LGWM , and that infamous letter , I understand that there is some brute force negotiation going on . <p> That 's a shame . Because if that is true , then we have councillors putting their interests ahead of the city -- a sign that this Council is off to a rocky start . <p> Not everyone is going to get the portfolios they want , so they need to suck that up , realise they are working for us , the residents , and get on with it like a bunch of grownups . <p> If I were King of the World , I would start by collapsing the portfolios into something that is far more manageable than the overlapping and often pointless portfolios of the last Council . <p> The basic model I 've dreamed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the original twenty-five to a more manageable fourteen , which matches the total number of councillors .
