<h> General strike for climate : everything you need to know <p> The School Strike 4 Climate has been widened to a general nationwide strike . Ten of thousands of New Zealanders are expected to skip work and classes today . Here is your guide to what 's going on , and where . <p> What and when ? <p> The School Strike 4 Climate movement has invited people of all ages to a nationwide strike today . More than 40 rallies and marches are planned around the country and upwards of 90 businesses , including The Spinoff , have committed to downing tools and joining the movement . <p> In Auckland , protestors will gather at noon at Aotea Square . <p> Hamilton protestors are meeting at Civic Square at 1pm . <p> In Tauranga , it 's a 12pm start at the south end of The Strand . <p> Wellington protestors are meeting at 11am at Civic Square ahead of a march on parliament . <p> In Christchurch , protestors will gather at 1pm in Cathedral Square . <p> Dunedin 's strike kicks off at 12pm outside the Dental School ahead of a march to the Octagon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said opening the strike to workers and employers strengthened the movement and diversified their base . <p> " We know that everyone has a reason to care about climate justice so it 's incredibly powerful to see so many people connecting with their ' why ' , across different ages and backgrounds .
