Climate Change Minister James Shaw has today announced the appointment of Dr Rod Carr as Chair-designate for the Climate Change Commission . <p> Pending the outcome of the Select Committee and Parliamentary process , the Climate Change Commission will be established by the passing of the Zero Carbon Bill . <p> " The Commission will have a critical role to help New Zealand transition to a low-emissions economy . <p> " As the Chairperson role will have a big impact on the success of the Commission , I have sought to appoint someone who will ensure that the Commission will be independent , analytical and credible , " James Shaw says . <p> " The Commission will be crucial to keep us on track for our 2050 target and hold successive governments accountable to meet emissions budgets . <p> adaptation . <p> " Dr Carr and also served as Deputy Governor and Chair of the Reserve Bank , so has strong governance experience , which will be much needed as Chair of the Commission . <p> " I am still considering my preferred candidates for the remaining six Commissioners and hope to announce appointments to these roles in the following months , " James Shaw says . <p> In acknowledging the Minister 's announcement and support for his appointment as Chair-designate , Dr. Carr said : <p> " Each of us has the opportunity and the responsibility to use our skills and experience , our
