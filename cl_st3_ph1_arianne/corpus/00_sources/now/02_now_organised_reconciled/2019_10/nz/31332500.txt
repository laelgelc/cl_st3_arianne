<p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw has named Rod Carr , ex-Vice Chancellor of the University of Canterbury and former Deputy Governor and Chairman of the Reserve Bank , as Chairman-designate of the Climate Change Commission . <p> The Climate Change Commission will be established if and when Parliament passes the Zero Carbon Bill . The Bill sets out a desire to reduce emissions of all greenhouse gases , aside from biogenic methane , to net zero by 2050 . Shaw expects the Bill to pass by Christmas and says the advice the Commission provides to government on future emissions budgets has the potential to shape and reshape industries and communities for decades to come . <p> " The Commission will have a critical role to help New Zealand transition to a low-emissions economy .
