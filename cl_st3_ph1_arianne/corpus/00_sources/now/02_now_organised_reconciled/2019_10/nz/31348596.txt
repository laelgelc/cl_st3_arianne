<h> Brussels ' next nightmare <p> This week 's election result in Poland is a nightmare outcome for the EU , writes Oliver Hartwich . <p> This week , all eyes will be on Brexit -- again . With the Halloween deadline just a couple of weeks away , the coming days will be crucial for the future of Britain , the European Union and their relationship with each other . <p> Yet while Brexit dominates the news , another country could become an even bigger headache for Brussels over the coming years . <p> Poland went to the polls on Sunday and returned the government led by the Law and Justice party PiS of former Prime Minister Jaroslaw Kaczynski . It is a nightmare outcome for the European Union , which for the past four years had regular clashes with Warsaw . <p> In some ways , what PiS stands for is the opposite of the Western European political mainstream . Its conservativism is of a kind no longer present in other European countries . <p> To add some background , Poland has a four-year parliamentary cycle . Its conservativism is of a kind no longer present in other European countries . PiS is not only emphasising the role of the nation , the family and traditional social structures . It is also actively opposing and even demonising anyone not living accordingly . <p> Thus the 2019 election campaign was shaped by PiS attacks on the LGBT community . Quite on the contrary , just before the election members of the Polish government announced further interference with the justice system in a second PiS term . <p> And that would not even be the only development to worry about .
