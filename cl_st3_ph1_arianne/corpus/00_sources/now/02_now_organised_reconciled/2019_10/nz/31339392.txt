<h> Directors urged to improve understanding of climate change <p> Company directors are not as aware of climate change challenges as they should be and risk leaving their firms insufficient time to respond , says Karen Silk , deputy chair of the Sustainable Business Council . <p> While some firms are taking strong positions , many others are reacting to regulation and tending to do the minimum . <p> Directors have " a wee way to go " in lifting their understanding , she said . Boards need to recognise that the time horizons for making decisions on strategy have shifted , and understand the potential breadth of impacts and opportunities faced by the businesses they are responsible for . <p> " They set the tone from the top which drives the culture , " Silk told delegates at the Climate Change and Business Conference in Auckland yesterday . " Given the degree of challenge that we are facing , we actually need to see leaders step up and lead and not manage to that minimum .
