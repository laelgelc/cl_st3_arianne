How can responsible Asian leaders win ? <p> The rise of populism across the West in recent years has been the subject of countless discussions , and for good reason : populists ' misguided policies often have severely adverse political and economic consequences . Now , those risks are coming to Asia . <p> There is no straightforward definition of populism . It may be ideological , economic , social , or cultural . It may reflect left-wing or right-wing views . And it is often interpreted in a country-specific context . <p> But populism 's various iterations tend to share common features . This approach is most effective at times when the public is deeply frustrated with established leaders or political parties , owing to deepening economic and social disparities , rising insecurity , or overt corruption . <p> Once in power , however , populists end up making matters much worse . For starters , they frequently undermine the fundamental institutions of representative democracy , including the systems of checks and balances that restrain institutional excesses and prevent abuses of power . They claim that these institutions impede their ability to serve " the people . The economic crises that many Latin American countries experienced in the 1970s and 1980s resulted from this dynamic . <p> Although today 's populists are not pursuing wildly expansionary macroeconomic policies , they still rely on fiscal stimulus and government intervention in markets . For example , while US President Donald Trump preaches fiscal discipline , the combination of higher spending and lower revenues ( owing especially to the massive tax cut for corporations enacted in 2017 ) is blowing up the federal budget deficit . Likewise , Trump advocates protectionist trade policies . <p> And yet , despite the damage populists have done in the West , Asian voters are increasingly falling victim to their blandishments . Concepts like religious traditionalism , law and order , and national sovereignty provide a useful pretext for discriminatory policies , like Trump 's ( legally dubious ) immigration crackdown . <p> It is this type of cultural populism that has taken hold in Asia .
