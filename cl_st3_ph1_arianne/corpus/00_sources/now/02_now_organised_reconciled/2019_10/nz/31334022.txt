<h> Rod Carr appointed as chair of new Climate Change Commission <p> 9:00pm Tuesday 08 Oct , 2019 <p> Former deputy Reserve Bank governor and University of Canterbury vice-chancellor Rod Carr has been appointed as the chair of the new Climate Change Commission . <p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw announced Dr Carr 's appointment in Auckland this morning , saying his leadership skills , strategic vision and professional experience will be a huge asset as the commission is established . <p> Mr Shaw said the chairperson needs to be someone independent , analytical and credible . <p> " As the Chairperson role will have a big impact on the success of the Commission , I have sought to appoint someone who will ensure that the Commission will be independent , analytical and credible , " Mr Shaw said in a statement . <p> " Dr Carr 's strong leadership skills , strategic vision and professional experience will be a huge asset in the Commission 's establishment years , " he said . <p> He said the government will be required to take the commission 's advice very seriously . <p> " The whole point of the Climate Change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economically grounded advise and to de-politicise the process in Parliament and so obviously anything that the commission says we would have to take very seriously . " <p> Mr Shaw said he was still considering his preferred candidates for the remaining six commissioners . <p> Dr Carr will come out of retirement to fill the role - a decision he said was influenced by his children . <p> " It certainly is the case that all of us are going to have to play a part using our own skills , and own experiences and own spheres of influences . This is not a trivial change to transform an economy from a fossil fuels based economy into a net zero emissions economy . " <p> The independent Climate Change Commission is a result of the Zero Carbon Bill , which is expected to be enacted by the end of the year .
