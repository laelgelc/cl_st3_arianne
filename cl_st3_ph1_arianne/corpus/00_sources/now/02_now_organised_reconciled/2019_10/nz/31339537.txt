<p> The tour , which was at the request of the New Zealand Government , is set to take place between November 17th to 25th , with Their Royal Highnesses arriving into Auckland where they will receive an official welcome at Government House by Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern . <p> During their time in the country 's largest city , the couple will visit programmes and organisations working in areas they are committed to supporting , the statement says , with Prince Charles focusing on young people and youth opportunity as well as environmental issues -- with particular emphasis on tackling plastic weight . <p> Prince Charles , 70 , and Camilla , 70 , will then travel to the Waitangi Treaty Grounds to receive an official welcome and to honour the relationship between The Crown and Maori . <p> Charles last visited the historic grounds on Waitangi Day in 1994 , with this visit marking 25 years since a member of the royal family has visited the Waitangi . <p> Prince Charles during his most recent visit to Waitangi in 1994 , marking 25 years since a member of the royal family has visited the grounds .
