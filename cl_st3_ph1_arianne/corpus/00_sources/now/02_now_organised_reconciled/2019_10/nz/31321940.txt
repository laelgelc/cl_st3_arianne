<h> EV uptake on the rise as new Tesla model leads the charge <p> The uptake of electric vehicles is on the rise as pure EVs broke into the top three passenger/SUV models registered for the first time last month , Motor Industry Association figures show . <p> September saw 14,525 new vehicles registered , a rise of 4.4 percent from the year before . There were 500 sales of pure electric vehicles during the month and a further 105 sales of plug-in hybrid vehicles , the MIA said . <p> The Tesla Model 3 led the charge , with 379 sold in the month . The car sells for around $75,900 to $102,900 depending on the specs . In comparison , a new Nissan Leaf , the country 's most common EV , sells for $59,990 . <p> So far this year , 1,406 new pure EVs have been registered compared to 768 in the prior year . At the end of August , there were 16,031 electric and hybrid light vehicle registrations according to data from the Ministry of Transport . That is up from 9,762 in August 2018 and 4,596 in August 2017 . <p> While @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ economic slowdown may curb that growth . The market had been buoyed by high migration levels , low interest rates and the strong New Zealand dollar .
