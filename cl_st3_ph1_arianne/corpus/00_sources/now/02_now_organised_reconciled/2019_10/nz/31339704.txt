More dairy needed to achieve sustainable global diet -- Fonterra <p> Global dairy output will have to increase -- not fall -- if the dietary targets of an international study on sustainable food production are to be achieved , Fonterra says . <p> While dairy consumption in Europe and North America exceeds the levels proposed in a sustainable reference diet modelled in an EAT-Lancet study earlier this year , the global average is still only about half that targeted , Jeremy Hill told delegates at the Climate Change and Business Conference in Auckland this week . <p> The diet proposed by EAT , an Oslo-based think tank dedicated to transforming the global food system , assumes just one serving of dairy a day .
