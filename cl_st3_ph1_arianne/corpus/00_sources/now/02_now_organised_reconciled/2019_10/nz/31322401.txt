<h> Fined $86,250 for pollution <p> Climate change is to blame for Cedenco Foods New Zealand Limited 's pollution of an important waterway on the Poverty Bay flats in August last year , a lawyer for the company says . <p> The company was this week fined $86,250 by Environment Court judge Brian Dwyer , $67,500 of it for breaching a resource consent , at the company 's Dunstan Road waste site , last August . The other $18,750 was for breaching an abatement notice , issued by Gisborne District Council last April , after the company polluted the same waterway last February . <p> Leachate from a stockpile of corn waste escaped down a ditch dug by contractors trying to clear surface water , into the Dunstan Road drain , causing a high level of contamination by health and ecologically threatening pollutants , Judge Dwyer said . <p> He agreed with the council 's prosecutor Adam Hopkinson that the offending was " reckless " . <p> Mr Hopkinson said any penalty needed to have a significant financial sting .
