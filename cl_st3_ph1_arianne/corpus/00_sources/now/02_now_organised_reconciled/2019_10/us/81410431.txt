<p> MOSCOW , Oct 2 ( Reuters ) - The Kremlin said on Wednesday that Moscow was watching closely after Turkey said it would act alone on its plans to form a " safe zone " in the northeast of Syria , which is a close ally of Russia . <p> Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan said on Tuesday that Turkey had no choice but to act alone as too little progress had been made with the United States on forming a " safe zone " , his most direct indication of a cross-border offensive . <p> Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told reporters in Moscow that Turkey had the right to defend itself , but that Syria 's territorial integrity must be preserved .
