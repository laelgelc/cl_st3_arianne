<p> On Saturday , delegates considered dozens of resolutions , including one that would see AFN form a climate change task force . <p> The resolution was drafted at the earlier Elders and Youth Conference and was presented to AFN by two high school students . <p> Debate on whether to adopt the resolution became a proxy fight for how to combat climate change and whether there could be a risk that outside interests would try to prevent development of Alaska Native lands . <p> 15-year-old Nanieezh Peter of Arctic Village emotionally presented the resolution to hundreds of delegates . She urged AFN to declare a state of emergency on climate change , saying its threats have to be tackled now . <p> " We should n't have to tell those in charge that we want to survive , " she said . <p> One-by-one delegates stood to support the resolution or offer caution .
