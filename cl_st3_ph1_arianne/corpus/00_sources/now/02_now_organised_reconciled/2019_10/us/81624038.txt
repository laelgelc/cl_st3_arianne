( Credit : ROBYN BECK/AFP/Getty Images ) <p> Brendan Cummings does n't need to go far to find a dying signature of Southern California 's desert landscape : He stands on his porch and eyes whole forests of Joshua trees that have n't produced seedlings in decades . <p> Interspersed among the trees are exotic grasses that feed off nitrogen-laden smog wafting in from the Los Angeles area .
