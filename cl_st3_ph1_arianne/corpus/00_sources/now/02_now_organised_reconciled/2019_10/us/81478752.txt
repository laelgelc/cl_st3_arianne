<h> Deliver Us The Moon arrives on Steam this Thursday <p> Wired Productions and KeokeN Interactive have announced that their narrative driven adventure , Deliver Us The Moon , will arrive on Steam for Windows PC this Thursday , 10th October . This sci-fi thriller takes players on a journey to the Moon in order to save a dying Earth . <p> Deliver Us The Moon takes players to a world where humanity has depleted all Earth 's precious resources . In a last ditch effort to solve the energy crisis the world 's super powers have created the World Space Agency and colonised the moon to mine for valuable resources . However , the Lunar base suddenly goes quiet so it 's up to players to become Earth 's last astronaut and , accompanied only by a small robot called ASE , travel into the vaccuum of space to discover what befell the lunar colony . <p> Featuring : <p> Focuses on topical issues such as climate change and the depletion of the world 's resources . <p> Explore the open lunar landscape by foot , rover or monorail and enjoy the weightless freedom . <p> Use your @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ colony . <p> Enjoy the company of the ASE drone and utilise advanced tech ' including new age space-suits , cutting lasers , rockets and robot arms . <p> Survive the hazardous environment and deal with an ever decreasing oxygen supply .
