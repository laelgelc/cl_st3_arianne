<p> HANOI , Oct 17 ( Reuters ) - Vietnam has urged farmers to restore pig herds battered by an outbreak of African swine fever as the spread of the disease has shown signs of slowing , officials said on Thursday , to prepare for a surge in demand expected for Lunar New Year in January . <p> Since the disease was first detected in February , it has spread to all 63 provinces in Vietnam , forcing the cull of more than 5 million pigs , or 18 percent of the total hog herd , and driving up prices nearly 70% . <p> " Farmers ' efforts to enhance hygienic conditions on farms have helped put a brake on the spread , " Nguyen Xuan Duong , the head of the department of livestock production , told Reuters , adding that improving weather conditions had helped . <p> It is important to restore pig production to meet pork demand , which is forecast to be particularly high closer to Lunar New Year in late January , Agriculture Minister Nguyen Xuan Cuong said this week . <p> The outbreak has " initially been contained and shown signs of slowing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , but did not elaborate . <p> While not harmful to humans , African swine fever is deadly to pigs , with no available vaccine . <p> The outbreak has driven up prices for live pigs in Vietnam by about 70% since early this year to around 60,000 dong ( $2.59 ) per kg , traders say . <p> In May , Cuong had urged farmers to refrain from developing pig production and instead shift focus to expanding production of cattle and poultry , as well as aquaculture . <p> Vietnam 's annual poultry output rose 13.5% in the first nine months of this year , while beef production was up 4.2% and aquaculture production up 6.1% , government data shows .
