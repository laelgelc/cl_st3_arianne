<p> MOSCOW , Oct 25 ( Reuters ) - Alleged Russian foreign agent Maria Butina , arrested and jailed in the United States in April , is expected to arrive in Moscow by Saturday morning , Russian Foreign Ministry said on its official Telegram messenger channel . <p> " On October 25 Maria will be transferred from the federal prison in Tallahassee , Florida , to the migration centre in Miami , from where she will fly to Moscow on the same day . Arrival - in the morning of October 26 , " the ministry said . <p> Butina was sentenced to 18 months in prison after the Siberia native expressed remorse for conspiring with a Russian official to infiltrate a gun rights group and influence U.S. conservative activists and Republicans .
