<p> BARCELONA , Oct 17 ( Reuters ) - Catalonia 's pro-independence regional chief Quim Torra said on Thursday he aims for the region to be independent from Spain by the end of 2021 if Catalan parties agree to it . <p> Addressing the regional parliament , he also said he wanted a proposal for a " Catalan republic " to be ready by the spring of next year . Torra previously said a new vote would have to be held to decide on self-determination for Catalonia . <p> The regional parliament 's term expires in December 2021 .
