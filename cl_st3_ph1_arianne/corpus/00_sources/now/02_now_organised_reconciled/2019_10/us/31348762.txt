<h> Quartz Wins Digiday Branded Content Award <p> Quartz used 360-degree video technology to immerse the viewer in the Arctic landscape . <p> Quartz Creative has won Digiday 's 2019 Award for " Best Branded Content Program B2B " for its creation of " The New Global Economy : A 360-Degree Docuseries " in partnership with OppenheimerFunds . <p> The award-winning series is composed of two long-form branded interactive features that combine immersive 360-degree video and far-flung storytelling . In 2018 , Quartz and OppenheimerFunds set out to create a project with this first-to-market technology that would satisfy their goal of amplifying awareness of OppenheimerFunds global product offerings as well as drive engagements on Qz.com . <p> To create the series , Quartz and OppenheimerFunds shot on-site at the Yiwu Market ( the world 's largest wholesale market ) in China to bring viewers to the ground-level of China 's rising e-commerce giants and highlight investment opportunities like Alibaba and Tencent . They also traveled to the glaciers of the Arctic to explore how climate change is giving rise to Arctic tourism , highlighting investment opportunities with cruise operators like Carnival .
