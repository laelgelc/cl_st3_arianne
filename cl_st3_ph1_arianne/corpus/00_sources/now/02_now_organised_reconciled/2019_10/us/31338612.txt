<h> Department of Ecology says more information needed before a decision on proposed Kalama methanol plant <p> OLYMPIA -- The Washington Department of Ecology wants more information about the environmental impact of a proposed methanol plant to be built in Cowlitz County before it decides whether to approve the project . <p> The plant would use natural gas to make methanol , which NWIW has said would then be sold to customers in Asia to manufacture plastics . <p> The company has said the plant would be much cleaner than comparable coal-fired facilities in Asia . New technology would cut pollution even further , company officials say , and the plant would n't discharge any liquids into the Columbia River . <p> In a letter Wednesday to Cowlitz County , the Department of Ecology requested more information about how the project would reduce its carbon emissions in Washington before the agency decides whether to approve a shoreline conditional use permit . <p> Ecology also wants more analysis of the project 's possible greenhouse gas emissions both in Washington and around the world . <p> " Our review of the county 's permit decision found significant information missing from the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the project 's potential effects on Washington 's environment , " according to an Ecology news release . <p> The department has set a Nov. 7 deadline for a response from Cowlitz County and the applicants .
