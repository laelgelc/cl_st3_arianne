<p> MADRID , Oct 14 ( Reuters ) - Spain 's Supreme Court on Monday sentenced nine Catalan separatist leaders to between nine and 13 years in prison for sedition over their role in the region 's failed bid for independence in 2017 . <p> The three other defendants in the landmark ruling over a banned referendum and short-lived independence declaration were found guilty only of disobedience and not sentenced to prison . All defendants were acquitted of the most severe charge , rebellion . <p> Much is at stake , both for Spain and for its wealthiest region in this trial over an independence drive that attracted worldwide attention , triggered Spain 's biggest political crisis in decades and unnerved financial markets . <p> The main questions are how separatists will react to the verdicts , whether the promise of peaceful protests holds and whether and how the reputation of both Spain and the separatist movement could be affected . <p> Both the government and separatists circulated their own video on social media ahead of the verdict .
