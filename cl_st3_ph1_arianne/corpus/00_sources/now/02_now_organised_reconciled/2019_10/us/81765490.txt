<p> SEOUL , Oct 24 ( Reuters ) - Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe and South Korean Prime Minister Lee Nak-yon shared the view that the neighbours ' relations should not be left in their current difficult state , South Korea 's foreign ministry said on Thursday . <p> Lee met Abe on a visit to Japan for the enthronement ceremony of Emperor Naruhito . <p> The two men also agreed on the importance of cooperating on the North Korean issue with common ally the United States , the South 's vice minister of foreign affairs , Cho Sei-young , told a televised briefing . <p> South Korean President Moon Jae-in , in a message passed on by Lee , also urged attempts to resolve the issues complicating ties , the Yonhap news agency said .
