<h> More than 70% of the island 's land area has washed away over the past 50 years , the Chesapeake Bay Foundation says <h> Published Oct 21 , 2019 at 6:02 AM <h> Receive the latest local updates in your inbox <p> An environmental group says sea level rise and erosion are bringing an end to the use of an education center on an island in the Chesapeake Bay in Virginia . <p> The Chesapeake Bay Foundation recently announced that this fall is the last season for its Fox Island Education Program , the Capital Gazette reports . <p> Educators with the foundation say sea level rise and erosion are destroying the protective salt marshes surrounding the island located in Virginia waters between the Tangier and Pocomoke sounds . They say that 's making the educational center that served teachers and students unsafe . <p> More than 70% of the island 's land area has washed away over the past 50 years , the foundation said in a blog post . It calls the center 's closure an example of the " real and immediate threat " climate change poses .
