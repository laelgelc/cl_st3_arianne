Lesser drought conditions also have expanded in parts of Arkansas , Louisiana and Mississippi . <p> Overall , nearly 20 percent of the lower 48 U.S. states is experiencing drought conditions . <p> The drought accelerated rapidly in September , as record heat combined with little rainfall to worsen the parched conditions , said Brian Fuchs , a climatologist at the National Drought Mitigation Center @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as being a slow onset , slow-developing type phenomenon compared to other disasters that rapidly happen , so this flash drought term came about , " Fuchs said .
