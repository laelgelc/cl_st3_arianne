<p> MISSISSAUGA , Toronto , Oct 12 ( Reuters ) - Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau donned a bulletproof vest for an election campaign rally on Saturday , a Reuters eyewitness said , and the Canadian Broadcasting Corp . cited sources saying a security threat had been received . <p> Pictures taken by Reuters photographer Stephane Mahe at the event in the Ontario city of Mississauga outside Toronto showed Trudeau wearing bulky protection under his shirt . <p> Police wearing backpacks surrounded Trudeau on stage for the first time since the start of a six-week election campaign leading up to what polls suggest will be a closely contested vote on Oct. 21 . <p> The scenes were almost unprecedented in recent Canadian political history , which has been overwhelmingly peaceful . <p> CBC cited unnamed sources inside Trudeau 's Liberal Party as saying he donned the bulletproof vest after the campaign received a threat . <p> Liberal spokeswoman Zita Astravas declined to comment on Trudeau 's security arrangements . The Royal Canadian Mounted Police declined to comment . <p> Trudeau , who arrived 90 minutes late for the event , addressed the rally without incident .
