<p> BEIJING , Oct 18 ( Reuters ) - China produced 31.81 million tonnes of pork in the first nine months of 2019 , down 17.2% from a year earlier , official data showed on Friday , after African swine fever devastated its hog herd . <p> Output fell as the size of the herd declined 28.5% from a year ago to 306.75 million head , the National Bureau of Statistics said . That was down 11.76% from the 347.61 million head it reported in the first half of the year . The number of slaughtered hogs fell 17.3% to 409.78 million head . <p> African swine fever , an incurable virus that kills pigs but does not harm humans , has swept through China 's hog herd , the world 's largest , since August 2018 .
