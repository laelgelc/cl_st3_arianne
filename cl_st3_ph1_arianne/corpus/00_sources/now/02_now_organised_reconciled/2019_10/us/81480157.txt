<h> Readers respond : We could act on climate change <p> Why is the climate crisis so much less important than the oil embargo in the ' 70s ? To save fuel , the U.S. lowered speed limits to 55 mph . Why have n't we done that today ? Yesterday ? Ten years ago ? In the " hippie " ' 60s , we washed clothes in cold water and hung them to dry . Is n't that possible in the 21st century ? I read that light-colored roofs reduce the need for air conditioning , which itself adds to climate heat . I do n't see those roofs being installed anywhere . <p> Even a small town like McMinnville has acres of paved surfaces : roads , parking lots , driveways . Is the heat generated by streets and hardscape offset anywhere in city planning ? Are there plans to gradually eliminate gas-powered lawn mowers , weed whackers and blowers ? What about subsidies for solar installations where feasible ? <p> Greta Thunberg is right to be angry . There is so much that can be done individually and , especially , collectively .
