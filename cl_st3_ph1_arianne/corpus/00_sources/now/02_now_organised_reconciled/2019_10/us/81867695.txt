<p> PARIS , Oct 29 ( Reuters ) - France 's Foreign Minister Jean-Yves Le Drian called on Lebanese authorities to guarantee the country 's unity and ensure its stability amid and economic and political crisis . <p> " Prime Minister Hariri has resigned just now , which makes the crisis even more serious , " Le Drian told the French Parliament . <p> " It ( France ) calls for Lebanese officials to do everything to ensure stability of institutions and the unity of Lebanon . It is paramount " .
