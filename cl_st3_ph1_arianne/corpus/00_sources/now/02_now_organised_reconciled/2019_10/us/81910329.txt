At the same time , it has been leading the sector in shifting from oil into lower-carbon natural gas and electricity production . <p> The company partially offset the impact of lower prices with strong earnings on LNG and oil products trading as well as higher retail margins at Shell 's 45,000 retail locations . New retail sites in China , India and Mexico added to the results -- as did an increase in margins resulting from improved offerings of nonfuel retail products such as coffee as well as services like oil changes . <p> Shell also posted a respectable return on average capital employed of 8.1% and with gearing , or net debt as a percentage of total capital , of 27.9% is closing in on its 25% target .
