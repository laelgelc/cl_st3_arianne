<h> The complete guide to the battery revolution <p> German ministers are n't known for making exaggerated statements . So when , this past January , Anja Karliczek , the German government 's minister for education and research , said battery technology was an " existential " matter , she was n't stoking unfounded fears . Instead , she was boiling down the reality of what the German car industry needs to stay relevant in the future . <p> In the home of Volkswagen , BMW , and Mercedes-Benz , the car industry makes Germany 's economic engine turn . It accounts for one-seventh of the country 's jobs , one-fifth of its exports , and one-third of its spending on research and development . But these giants of the internal-combustion engine era are facing a new challenge .
