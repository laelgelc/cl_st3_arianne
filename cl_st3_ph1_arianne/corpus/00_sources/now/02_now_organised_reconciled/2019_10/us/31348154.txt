Combining ecological , ethnographic , historical , and contemporary knowledge , author and mycologist Lawrence Millman discusses how mushrooms are much more closely related to humans than to plants , how they engage in sex , how insects farm them , and how certain species happily dine on leftover radiation , cockroach antennae , and dung . He explores the lives of individuals like African American scientist George Washington Carver , who specialized in crop diseases caused by fungi ; Beatrix Potter , creator of Peter Rabbit , who was prevented from becoming @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gordon Wasson , a J. P. Morgan vice-president who almost single-handedly introduced the world to magic mushrooms . Millman considers why fungi are among the most significant organisms on our planet and how they are currently being affected by destructive human behavior , including climate change . <p> Fungipedia presents a delightful A -- Z treasury of mushroom lore . With more than 180 entries -- on topics as varied as Alice in Wonderland , chestnut blight , medicinal mushrooms , poisonings , Santa Claus , and waxy caps -- this collection will transport both general readers and specialists into the remarkable universe of fungi . With charming drawings by artist and illustrator Amy Jean Porter , Fungipedia offers a treasure trove of scientific and cultural information .
