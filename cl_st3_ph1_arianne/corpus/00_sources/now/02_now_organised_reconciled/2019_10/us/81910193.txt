<p> Viacom 's Channel 5 has bagged another couple of BBC presenters to make an event show about the climate change emergency . <p> Chris Packham -- who has worked at the BBC for decades on shows including Springwatch , Autumnwatch , and Winterwatch -- will host 100,000 Trees with Chris Packham ( working title ) , which will raise money in partnership with charity The Woodland Trust to plant 100,000 trees across Britain . <p> Packham will be joined by John Humphrys , who last month left BBC Radio 4 's Today show after 32 years , as they shine a light on the UK being one of the least wooded countries in Europe and delve into the science of trees . <p> 100,000 Trees with Chris Packham will be produced by ITN Productions , with Ian Rumsey executive producing .
