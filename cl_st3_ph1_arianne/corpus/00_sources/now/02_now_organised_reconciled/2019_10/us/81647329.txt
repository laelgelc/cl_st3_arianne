<h> Is it ' political ' for students to take steps on climate ? <p> Your Oct. 14 article explains how education about the science of climate change , and its effects , is spreading through California . <p> We also need to teach students what they can do to slow down climate change . We need more than schools implementing recycling and installing solar panels . We need strong national legislation to shift our economy toward a net zero carbon footprint . <p> Young people have a powerful voice with which to demand this legislation . They can say to our Washington representatives , " You are messing up the world that we 're going to inherit . Do something ! " <p> I agree that schools should avoid espousing political positions . But is it " political " for students to take the next logical step , after learning the science , to advocate meaningful actions to reverse the current trend toward more droughts , hurricanes , fire storms and , now , power outages ?
