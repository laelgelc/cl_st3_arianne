<h> Australia 's Great Barrier Reef coral cover has halved <p> Australia 's Great Barrier Reef may be losing coral cover faster than previously thought , according to a new study on Friday which revealed between 40 to 50 percent of live , hard corals have disappeared from four major inshore islands since the 1990s . <p> So alarmed by the findings , researchers at James Cook University said they checked and re-checked the data several times after the marine survey was complete . <p> " We were shocked when we calculated the changes , " lead author of the study Daniela Ceccarelli explained . <p> " Normal cycles of disturbance and recovery are natural , and the reef historically has had good recovery potential . " <p> " But human impacts are increasing the frequency of disturbances such as coral bleaching , leaving little time between events to allow a full recovery .
