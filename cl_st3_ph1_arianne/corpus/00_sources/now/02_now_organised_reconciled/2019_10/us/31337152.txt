One of the first patents for a battery-powered bicycle was registered in 1895 by an inventor named Ogden Bolton Jr . His idea was simple but quite interesting : a DC electric motor installed on a bike 's rear wheel hub . That motor could take up to 100 amps from a 10-volt battery , which was placed under the horizontal tube of the frame . There was no gearing system or pedals . <p> Bolton did n't actually end up making or selling any of these bikes .
