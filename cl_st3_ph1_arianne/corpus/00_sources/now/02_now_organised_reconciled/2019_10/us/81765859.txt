<p> DUBAI , Oct 23 ( Reuters ) - Iran welcomes any steps that bring security to Syria , Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesman Abbas Mousavi said on Wednesday , adding that an agreement between Russia and Turkey was a positive step towards securing stability in the country . <p> " Iran welcomes any steps that will bring security and calm to Syria and to secure the country 's integrity , " Mousavi said . <p> " The agreement between Russia and Turkey is a positive step . We hope this agreement will remove Turkey 's concerns and will bring peace and security to Syria .
