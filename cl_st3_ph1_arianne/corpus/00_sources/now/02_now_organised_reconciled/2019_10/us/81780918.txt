<h> Bernie Sanders , returning to Iowa after heart attack , claims no lost momentum <p> U.S. Sen. Bernie Sanders returned to Iowa on Thursday to discuss corporate corruption at a series of events across the state . <p> It was Sanders ' first visit since suffering a heart attack earlier this month and canceling several events . He told the Des Moines Register in an interview that despite the cancellations and the decision to scale back the pace of his schedule , his campaign has not lost momentum at this crucial point in the race . <p> " I will tell you , what was very emotional for me is that , when I had the heart attack , what we saw all over this country was people say , ' We 're going to stand with Bernie , ' " he said . " And they got even more involved . " <p> At Sanders ' first event of the night , a town hall in Marshalltown , the audience was widely unconcerned by the Vermont senator 's health . <p> " I 've seen people go through heart attacks and come through it ... in better @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from Marshalltown . I plead guilty : I am old , " Sanders said , to cheers and laughter from the audience . " But there are some advantages to being old .
