More info : 850-572-5849. @ <h> Malawi <p> At Liwonde National Park , a new tool is preventing poaching . Created after an elephant census discovered that nearly a third of the continent 's elephants were killed between 2007 and 2014 , EarthRanger can predict locations where poachers are likely to strike next . Poacher cams use algorithms to distinguish human movement and send pictures with GPS coordinates to EarthRanger and park staff . In the two years that EarthRanger has been building and using its database , poaching in Liwonde has drastically dropped . In an interview with NPR , an EarthRanger designer predicts that within two years technological improvements will help the park stop poachers before they can even fire a shot .
