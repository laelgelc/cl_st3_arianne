<h> Tillerson testifies in suit over Exxon 's climate accounting <p> Published Wednesday , Oct. 30 , 2019 6:32 a.m . <p> Updated Wednesday , Oct. 30 , 2019 6:32 a.m . <p> NEW YORK ( AP ) -- Ex-Secretary of State and former ExxonMobil CEO Rex Tillerson is testifying in a New York lawsuit over claims that the energy company misled investors about how much climate change could cost it . <p> Tillerson took the stand Wednesday in the securities fraud lawsuit , brought by New York Attorney General Letitia James ' office . <p> James 's office says ExxonMobil Corp . deceived investors about its future profitability in light of global warming and the increased regulations it is expected to spur . <p> The Irving , Texas-based company says it did nothing wrong and indeed is an industry leader in gauging potential climate costs . <p> The case has revealed that Tillerson used the email alias " Wayne Tracker " while at Exxon . The attorney general 's office may seek to question him about whether it was used to discuss climate change .
