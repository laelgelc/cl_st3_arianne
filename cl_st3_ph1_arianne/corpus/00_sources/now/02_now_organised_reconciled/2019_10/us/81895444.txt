In celebration , we 're going over some of the most jaw-dropping images taken from space . <p> On Tuesday , October 29 KhalifaSat turned one year old . The pioneering satellite was the first ever 100% Emirati developed satellite in the world and has since captured thousands of images to provide information on the world 's most pressing issues and development demands . <p> The satellite , which orbits around 580-620 kilometres above the earth , has already in its lifetime sent over 355 terabytes of data to the Mohammed Bin Rashid Science Centre back on earth .
