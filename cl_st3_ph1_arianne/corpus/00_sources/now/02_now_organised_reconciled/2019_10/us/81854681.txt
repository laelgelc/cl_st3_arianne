<p> WASHINGTON , Oct 28 ( Reuters ) - U.S. authorities have disposed of the remains of Islamic State leader Abu Bakr al-Baghdadi and have no plans to release photos or videos of his death at this time , the chairman of the U.S. Joint Chiefs of Staff , Army General Mike Milley , said on Monday . <p> Al-Baghdadi detonated a suicide vest to kill himself as U.S. forces closed in on him , President Donald Trump said on Sunday . " The disposal of his remains has been done and is complete and was handled appropriately , " Milley told reporters at a Pentagon briefing .
