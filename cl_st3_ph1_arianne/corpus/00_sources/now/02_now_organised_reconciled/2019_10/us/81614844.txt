<h> St. Gregory parish forum discusses role of young Catholics and environment <p> Posted Tuesday , October 15 , 2019 1:36 pm <p> The St. Gregory Social Action Committee is holding a discussion , " Catholic Youth and the Environment , " on Wednesday , October 23 from 7 to 8:15 p.m. in the Church Hall . <p> Three panelists will bring their perspectives to the role that young Catholics are playing in promoting greater awareness about our environment and actions they 're taking , particularly around issues related to climate change . <p> Speakers include Allen Ottaro , the executive director of Catholic Youth Network for Environmental Sustainability in Africa ( CYNESA ) ; Bill Patenaude , an engineer with the RI Department of Environmental Management and a frequent contributor to the Rhode Island Catholic who holds a master 's degree in theology ; and Cole DeSantis , who is studying for his master 's degree in theology . <p> In his encyclical on the environment , " Laudato Si , ' or Care for Our Common Home , " Pope Francis stressed that religions can play an important role in protecting the environment and defending human rights @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This forum will be of special interest to young people who are involved or interested in becoming involved in environmental issues at the local , state or global level . They may be particularly inspired by the work of Mr. Ottaro and CYNESA , which draws young Catholics from Kenya , Ethiopia , Zimbabwe , Tanzania , Zambia , Rwanda and South Africa who promote responsible stewardship of the environment . <p> The forum is free and open to everyone . St. Gregory the Great Parish is located at 360 Cowesett Rd. in Warwick .
