<p> RIYADH , Oct 30 ( Reuters ) - Global growth is slowing and has modestly impacted the United States , U.S. Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin said on Wednesday , adding the U.S. economy remained strong with good capital inflows . <p> " There is no question that the global economy is slowing down and that has had some modest drag on the U.S. economy , " he said at an investment conference in the Saudi capital Riyadh . <p> Mnuchin also said Europe needed to do more on the fiscal and regulatory side to maintain growth .
