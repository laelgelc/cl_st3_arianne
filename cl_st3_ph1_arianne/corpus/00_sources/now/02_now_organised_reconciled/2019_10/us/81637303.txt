Bangash ) <p> ISLAMABAD ( AP ) -- Britain 's Prince William and wife Kate on Wednesday traveled to Pakistan 's scenic northern mountains and glaciers , drawing attention to the challenges of climate change in the South Asian nation , where glaciers are melting at a fast pace . <p> The Duke and Duchess of Cambridge flew to the city of Chitral , where they were accorded a warm welcome . <p> Elders from the non-Muslim Kalash community presented them with traditional coats , hats and scarves . <p> The couple spent time with men , women and children from the Kalash community before enjoying performances of traditional dances and music by local residents . <p> The visit comes a day after Prince William made a speech at a cultural event in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ see some of the impacts of climate change -- including a melting glacier -- as well as meet with local communities faced with the impact of a damaged environment . <p> Pakistan 's northern areas have witnessed flash floods in recent years , causing damage to property and casualties . <p> Britain 's Press Association reported that William and Kate sat on a wall in a village square in the Kalash Valley , surrounded by local children , as they watched a traditional dance . <p> The couple also visited the village of Bumburet in the Chitral region , which was destroyed by flooding in 2015 and met with survivors of the disaster . The royal pair watched an emergency response drill in which members of the community carried would-be casualties over a river . <p> The royal couple arrived in Pakistan on Monday and their visit ends on Friday .
