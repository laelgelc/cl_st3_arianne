<p> LONDON , Oct 14 ( Reuters ) - Britain plans to strengthen its powers to block or intervene in the foreign purchase of any company that could affect national security , it said on Monday . <p> Currently the British state can intervene in the foreign takeover of any company that plays a role in national security , the provision of media plurality or the stability of the financial system . <p> But setting out its new legislative agenda in a Queen 's Speech on Monday , the government said it planned to be able to intervene in deals of any size in any sector that could pose a risk to national security . <p> Under the plan , a notification system allowing businesses to flag to government a transaction with potential security concerns will be introduced . <p> The government will then be able to add conditions to the transaction , block it or add sanctions for non-compliance . Companies will be able to appeal . <p> The move is designed to tackle national security risks wherever they arise and reflects the fact a small company with expertise in cyber could be as important as a major @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ open economies in the world but it does ask for a thorough examination of the impact of certain deals and it is currently reviewing acquisitions of satellite group Inmarsat and defence company Cobham . <p> While Prime Minister Boris Johnson does not have a majority to pass bills in parliament , the new legislation does indicate how his party will seek to govern if it holds an election later this year .
