<h> Council Member Cabrera to challenge AOC for congressional seat <p> Council Member Fernando Cabrera announced Thursday that he will challenge first-term Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez for her seat . <p> Cabrera has served as the New York City Council member representing the 14th District in the Bronx since January 2010 . <p> Cabrera tells News 12 he is a proud centrist . He says he has been more involved in the local community than Ocasio-Cortez . <p> Cabrera says Ocasio-Cortez has not done enough to create jobs and cited her support of Amazon 's decision not to open up a new headquarters in New York City . <p> The councilman says his campaign officially kicked off on Thursday . Cabrera says he is working on gathering grassroot support across the 14th District . This includes parts of the Bronx and Queens . <p> Several Republicans have already announced their intentions to run against Ocasio-Cortez . Cabrera is the first Democrat to announce a challenge to the freshman lawmaker . <p> In a statement , Cabrera said in part , " My entire life has been dedicated to the service of our community and our merciful God . She gained national attention for her support of the Green New Deal addressing climate change and economic inequality .
