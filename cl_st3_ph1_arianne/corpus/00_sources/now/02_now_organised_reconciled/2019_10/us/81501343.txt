<h> New Incentives Reduce Driving For Downtown Santa Cruz Employees <h> Downtown employees who bike , walk , carpool or bus to work will be eligible for incentives and rewards . <h> Oct 8 , 2019 6:00 am ET Updated Oct 8 , 2019 6:02 am ET <p> ( City of Santa Cruz ) <p> City of Santa Cruz:Downtown Santa Cruz employees are the focus of the City of Santa Cruz 's expanded Go Santa Cruz program , starting Oct. 1 . The City 's Go Santa Cruz promotes the benefits of alternative transportation commute options . Downtown employees who bike , walk , carpool , or bus to work will be eligible for incentives and rewards that encourage alternatives to driving , including : <p> * Free transit passes <p> * Free $20 bike locker cards <p> * Discounted JUMP memberships <p> * Bike safety trainings <p> * Carpool incentives <p> * Carpool ride matching and commute information <p> The GO Santa Cruz program is part of the City 's ongoing effort to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and play an active role in addressing climate change . The City is a national leader in reducing single-occupant car trips @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the U.S. <p> " With the free transit pass , I 'll take public transit instead of paying for parking , " said David L. , Cruzio employee . " The Cedar Street parking garage has a two year waiting list for monthly parking permits , so this is a great way to save money commuting ! " <p> To get started with Go Santa Cruz , downtown employees must sign up with the GO Santa Cruz online platform , Cruz511 , which offers transit options , trip logging , carpool matching , and bike and walk routing . The incentive prizes can be chosen in the " My Rewards " section .
