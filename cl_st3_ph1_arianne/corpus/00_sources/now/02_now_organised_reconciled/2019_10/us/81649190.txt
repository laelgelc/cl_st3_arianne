<h> More than 1,500 FirstEnergy customers still without power in Northeast Ohio <p> CLEVELAND , Ohio -- More than 1,500 FirstEnergy customers were still without power Thursday morning after overnight rain and strong winds in Northeast Ohio . <p> The bulk of the outages -- more than 1,450 -- were in Cuyahoga County . They included 762 in Cleveland , 313 in East Cleveland and 164 in Lakewood , according to data updated at 7:30 a.m . Thursday on FirstEnergy 's website . <p> The company also reported more than 300 outages in Ashtabula County and more than 200 in Lake County . <p> FirstEnergy has not provided an estimated time when their power will be restored . <p> The latest information about FirstEnergy outages can be found on the company 's website . <p> Cleveland Public Power also reported on its Facebook page late Wednesday that high winds caused power outages for its customers . The public utility has not provided an update on those outages . <p> High winds throughout the city are resulting in scattered outages . We currently have crews responding .
