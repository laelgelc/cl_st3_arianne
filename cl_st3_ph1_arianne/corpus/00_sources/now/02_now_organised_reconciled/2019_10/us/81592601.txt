<h> Bassnectar Provides Free Meals For Flint Children <p> Bassnectar routinely gives back to the community . Be Interactive , his personal non-profit routinely works with local charities , supporting good causes through music . Freakstyle : Day of the Dead , Bassnectar 's upcoming event in Grand Rapids , Michigan , is no exception . <h> The Flint Brain Food Drive <p> Together , Be Interactive and Conscious Alliance plan to donate over 30,000 meals to the Food Bank of Eastern Michigan in Flint . Bassnectar plans to deliver the meals following the three-day event . <p> In addition , Be Interactive is asking bassheads to bring non-perishable foods and cash donations . Those who donate over 20 items or meet the suggested donation amount receive an exclusive metal lunch box . Tyler Nally and Nathaniel Deas designed the artwork . <p> Be Interactive gave a statement on their website , regarding the drive . <p> " Years after the Flint Water Crisis , despite heroic activism and resilience in the city , lasting challenges remain .
