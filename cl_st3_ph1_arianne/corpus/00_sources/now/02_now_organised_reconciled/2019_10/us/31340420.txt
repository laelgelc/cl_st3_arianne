<p> Nuns and bikers -- they both wear a lot of black , and that 's usually where the similarities end . <p> But later this month , the two will come together to help kids in need . <p> The 15th annual Nun Run , Oct. 26 in Houston 's East End , is a 40-mile motorcycle ride in which the nuns will hop on the back of a motorcycle to benefit the Christus Foundation for Healthcare school clinics . <p> Anyone with a motorcycle can ride with a $35 donation . But bikers can pay extra to " ride double " and escort a nun from the starting line at Villa de Matel , 6510 Lawndale , to the finish line at Topwater Grill in San Leon . <p> The event , sponsored by the U.S. Family Health Plan , raises money for families who live in economically underprivileged situations and need help paying for basic medical care , said Yvonne Mills , a longtime participant and committee member . <p> Students from 16 schools in the Greater Houston area receive access to healthcare , immunizations and mental health counseling during the school day with the money @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ president and executive director for the Christus Foundation for Healthcare , said the school clinics provide more than 12,000 healthcare visis to students in this area . The foundation was created more than 50 years ago to deliver care to the underserved from primary care to mobile and school clinics , the release states . <p> Registration is $35 per individual rider .
