<p> WASHINGTON , Oct 14 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Vice President Mike Pence on Monday said President Donald Trump had spoken to Turkish President Tayyip Erdogan and asked for an immediate ceasefire in Syria . <p> " The United States of America did not give a green light for Turkey to invade Syria , " Pence said to reporters in front of the White House . <p> U.S. Treasury Secretary Steven Mnuchin , who joined Pence , said the Treasury Department was taking economic action against Turkish ministries and senior Turkish government officials in response to the country 's military operations in Syria .
