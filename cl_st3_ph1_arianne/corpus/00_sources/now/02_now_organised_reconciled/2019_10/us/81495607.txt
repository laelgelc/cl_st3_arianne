<h> Exhibit depicts Minnesota 's future with climate change <p> Artist David Luke will be showing his work on Minnesota landscapes at Gallery St. Germain through Nov. 2 . ( Photo : David Luke ) <p> Artist David Luke is exploring the future of Minnesota landscapes as the Midwest grapples with the effects of climate change and invasive species . <p> His exhibit " Invasive : Envisioning the Ecological Transformation of the Boundary Waters Exhibition " is open now through Nov. 2 at Gallery St. Germain . <p> Luke morphs photographic imagery of the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness and Minnesota 's southern and central prairies . These image collages depicting change in the boreal forest are his interpretation of the future based on research by Dr. Lee Frelich , director of the University of Minnesota 's Center for Forest Ecology . <p> Together , Luke and Frelich will present their findings and artist interpretations during the artist talk and reception for the show from 5:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m . Oct. 11 in Gallery St. Germain . The talk and reception are free and open to the public . <p> The exhibit , said Laura Ruprecht , visual @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , is a different way to reach people and give the community another way to open discussion about complicated topics . <p> " It 's a good way to educate the public , " Ruprecht said .
