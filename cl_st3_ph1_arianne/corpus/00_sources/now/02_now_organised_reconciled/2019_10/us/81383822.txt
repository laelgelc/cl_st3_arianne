<p> WASHINGTON , Oct 1 ( Reuters ) - The whistleblower who reported concerns over U.S. President Donald Trump 's July 25 call with Ukraine 's president should be heard out and their identity protected , " Republican U.S. Senator Chuck Grassley said in a statement on Tuesday . <p> " This person appears to have followed the whistleblower protection laws and ought to be heard out and protected . We should always work to respect whistleblowers ' requests for confidentiality , " Grassley said . " No one should be making judgments or pronouncements without hearing from the whistleblower first and carefully following up on the facts .
