<p> On a chilly fall morning at Tonaquint Nature Center in St. George , 20 or so birdwatchers gathered at the small pond . Looking out across the pond , it looks like there are just a lot of ducks -- some mallards , some domesticated ducks that Paul Jaussi says people drop off . <p> But if you know what you 're looking for , you can find dozens of bird species in the park hidden in trees and bushes . The group , which is organized by the Red Cliffs Audubon Society , spotted 25 different species of birds in just two hours . <p> Jaussi , the president of the Audubon Society in St. George , walks around listening to the calls , pointing out small birds -- some just a few inches tall -- flitting around . He 's spent 20 years birding , most of that time spent in Washington County , Oregon . Since moving to St. George two years ago , he said he 's been shocked to see how many bird species he 's been able to see .
