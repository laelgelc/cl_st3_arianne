<p> Trinity Episcopal Church in Redlands will hold a Celebration of Creation at its 8 and 10:15 a.m. services Sunday , Oct. 6 . <p> The Celebration of Creation marks the end of a five-week Creationtide Season observed by the church through conversations about climate change and possible responses to it by people of faith , according to a press release from the church . <p> Dr. George Christison , a member of the church , has led a series of Adult Forum programs focusing on climate change . The final program in that series will be at 9:15 a.m . Sunday , Oct. 6 , in the church 's lounge . <p> Guest celebrant at the 10:15 a.m. service will be the Rev. Canon Lou Hemmers , the church 's rector emeritus . The Rev. Paul Price , rector at Trinity Episcopal Church , will preach , and the Rev. Liz McQuitty , associate priest , will assist . <p> The community is invited to attend both the Adult Forum and the Celebration of Creation service . <p> Trinity Episcopal Church is at 419 S.
