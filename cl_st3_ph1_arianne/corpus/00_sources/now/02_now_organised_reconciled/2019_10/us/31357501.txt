For many years , due to its geographic isolation , the industry in the Americas was spared from coffee rust , Hemileia vastatrix . But in 1970 , the first known case was found in Brazil . And since then , it has reportedly spread to 70% of the farms in Latin America . Consequently , some countries have experienced 50-80% losses in yields . In Central America , that translates to $3.2 billion in damage and 1.7 million jobs lost . <p> In 2012 , coffee rust destroyed more than 85% of some Guatemalan producers ' coffee crops .
