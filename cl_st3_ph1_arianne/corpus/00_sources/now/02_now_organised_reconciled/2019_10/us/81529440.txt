<p> Benedict Cumberbatch joined Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) protesters camped out in London on Wednesday night . <p> The Doctor Strange actor , who is currently filming the movie Louis Wain in the British capital , was pictured speaking to activists from the group in Trafalgar Square , as they camped out for the third night of a planned two-week protest against government inaction on climate change . <p> Photographs posted by members of XR on Twitter showed the star , wearing a beige flat cap , smiling and joking with the activists . <p> According to London 's Evening Standard newspaper , Benedict spent a " good couple of hours " at the camp . <p> XR protests have taken place in cities across the world are calling for urgent action to save the environment . <p> Other stars who have attended the British protests include the model Daisy Lowe , as well as actors Mark Rylance and Juliet Stevenson . <p> Members of bands The Wombats , Disclosure , and Mystery Jets are among the acts who have performed impromptu gigs at the London camp this week .
