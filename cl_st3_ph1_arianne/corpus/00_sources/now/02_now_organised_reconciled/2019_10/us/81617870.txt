<h> British police order a halt to climate change protests in London <p> 1 Min Read <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - British police have ordered a halt to Extinction Rebellion protests in London after a week of civil disobedience by climate change activists who have targeted government building and major financial institutions . <p> " Any assembly linked to the Extinction Rebellion ' Autumn Uprising ' ... must now cease their protest(s) within London , " the police said in a statement late on Monday . <h> Related Coverage <p> Extinction Rebellion uses civil disobedience to highlight the risks posed by climate change and the accelerating loss of plant and animal species . <p> " The climate and ecological emergency is n't going away and we remain resolute in facing it , " the group said in response to the police move . " We urge the government and the authorities to join us in doing the same . We can not do it alone . "
