<p> Police has identified more than a dozen victims , the majority from Quebec but moved to Ontario and across Canada for sex trade purposes <p> By Moira Warburton <p> TORONTO , Oct 16 ( Reuters ) - More than 300 charges have been laid and 31 arrests made in a multi-province human trafficking criminal investigation , Canadian police said on Wednesday . <p> York Regional Police in southern Ontario received tips from two female victims of trafficking in October 2018 , who were " coming to the end of their rope , " said Thai Truong , an inspector with the YPP , in a press conference . <p> Initially the women were unwilling to share information and police had only " bits and pieces of what was happening , " he said , but within a few days one of the women became willing to cooperate . <p> Truong said the two had " attempted to flee the primary suspect of this investigation , " whom police identified as a pimp and considered to be the " kingpin " in the organization . <p> From there from Quebec " but had been moved to Ontario and across Canada for sex trade purposes , " the press release said . <p> The year-long investigation involved five different police forces from two
