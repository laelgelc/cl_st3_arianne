<h> Climate change expert to speak at SUNY <p> A national expert on climate change is coming to Fredonia as part of the SUNY Fredonia Convocation Series . Jim D'Aloisio will speak on Tuesday at 7 p.m. in Room 105 of the Science Center at SUNY Fredonia . <p> D'Aloisio is a structural and building envelope engineer who is the chair of the Structural Engineering Institutes 's Climate Action Team . He considers himself a " conditional optimist " and his talk will reflect that . <p> Part one will be about where we are now covering the current state of our changing climate . Part two will discuss various options for action that may be most effective . Part three will provide an overview of encouraging developments that suggest progress is being made .
