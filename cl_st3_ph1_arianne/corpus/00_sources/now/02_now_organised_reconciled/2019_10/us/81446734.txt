<p> BAGHDAD , Oct 4 ( Reuters ) - The death toll from three days of anti-government protests in Iraq climbed to 44 , police and medical sources told Reuters on Friday . <p> The largest number of casualties occurred in the southern city of Nassiriya , where 18 people were killed , followed by the capital Baghdad where the death toll stood at 16 , they said . <p> The protests , in which hundreds of people have also been injured , began over unemployment and poor services but have escalated into calls for a change of government and pose one of the country 's biggest security challenges in years .
