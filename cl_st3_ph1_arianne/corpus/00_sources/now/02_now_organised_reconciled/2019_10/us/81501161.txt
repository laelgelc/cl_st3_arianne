<p> ABU DHABI , Oct 8 ( Reuters ) - Sudan has received half of $3 billion in aid promised by Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates in April and expects the remainder to be paid by the end of next year , Sudan 's finance minister said late on Monday . <p> The Gulf countries agreed the aid package soon after former president Omar al-Bashir was ousted , throwing a lifeline to Sudan 's new military leaders at the time . nL8N2435L8 <p> Finance Minister Ibrahim Elbadawi said Saudi Arabia and the UAE had deposited $500 million in the Sudanese central bank , while $1 billion worth of petroleum products , wheat and agricultural production inputs had been received . <p> Long lines for bread and fuel are a recurring feature of Sudan 's economic crisis . <p> " I met with the ambassadors of the kingdom and of the UAE , and we agreed on a programmed schedule that will God willing take us to the end of 2020 , to finish off the rest of the grant , " Elbadawi said . <p> He was speaking on the sidelines of an event in Abu Dhabi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to Riyadh earlier in the week . <p> There was no immediate response from the government media offices of Saudi Arabia and the UAE to a Reuters request for comment . <p> Sudan 's new prime minister was accompanied on his visit to the Gulf by General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan , head of the transitional sovereign council , and other senior officials . <p> Last month , Elbadawi announced a nine-month economic rescue plan aimed at curbing rampant inflation while ensuring supplies of basic goods , which would keep bread and petrol subsidies in place until at least June 2020 .
