<h> Cars and Downed Power Lines <p> WBGZ Radio 10/27/2019 <p> By RFD Radio Network <p> Would you know what to do if power lines fell on your car ? Ameren Illinois is reminding everyone that you should not get out of the car unless it is smoking or on fire . The message is part of a campaign during the recently finished National Teen Driver Safety Week . <p> The utility 's Ron Pate says a Central Illinois teen faced this situation during a strong storm , and got out of the car , not knowing if the lines were hot or not . He says the girl could have been killed if the lines were still live . <p> Click here for Pate 's comments <p> Pate says if your car comes into contact with power lines , you should always assume the lines are hot .
