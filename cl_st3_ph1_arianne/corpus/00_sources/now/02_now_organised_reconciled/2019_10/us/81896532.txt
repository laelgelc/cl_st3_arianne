<p> DUBAI , Oct 30 ( Reuters ) - Iran on Wednesday accused the United States , Saudi Arabia and Israel of stoking unrest in Lebanon and Iraq , and called for calm in both countries . <p> " Our advice has always been to call for peace and ( stopping ) interference by foreign forces in these countries , " President Hassan Rouhani 's chief of staff Mahmoud Vaezi was quoted as saying by state media . <p> The United States , Saudi Arabia and Israel were riding a wave of popular demands and providing those forces with financial support , he added .
