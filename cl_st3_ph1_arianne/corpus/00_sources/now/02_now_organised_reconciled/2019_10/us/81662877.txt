<p> LONDON , Oct 17 ( Reuters ) - British Prime Minister Boris Johnson plans to make a statement to parliament on Saturday and then lawmakers will debate whether to approve his new Brexit deal or leave the EU without a deal , leader of the House of Commons Jacob Rees-Mogg said on Thursday . <p> Lawmakers are due to vote later on Thursday on whether to go ahead with the Saturday sitting , which would be the first since 1982 . If approved , parliament will begin sitting at 0830 GMT , Rees-Mogg said . <p> " The prime minister will make a statement updating the House on the outcome of the negotiations at the European Union Council . The debate that follows will be a motion to either approve a deal or to approve a no-deal exit , " he said , adding that the government planned for the debate to last 90 minutes . <p> " What we want is a yes or no answer from this House - does it like the deal or not ?
