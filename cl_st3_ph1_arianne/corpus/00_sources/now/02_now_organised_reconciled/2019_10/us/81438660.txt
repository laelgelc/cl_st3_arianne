<p> OHRID , North Macedonia , Oct 4 ( Reuters ) - United States Secretary of State Mike Pompeo said on Friday that he had warned leaders in North Macedonia about the risks of Chinese investment in sensitive technologies . <p> He said China may use bribery to win some infrastructure deals in the Balkans . <p> Speaking after meeting Prime Minister Zoran Zaev , Pompeo also said he was confident the U.S. senate would ratify North Macedonia 's accession to NATO in autumn . <p> North Macedonia expects to become the 30th member of NATO by the end of the year .
