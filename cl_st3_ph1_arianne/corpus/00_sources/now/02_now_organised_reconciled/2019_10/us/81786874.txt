<p> MADRID , Oct 24 ( Reuters ) - The exhumation of Spain 's former dictator Francisco Franco was a step towards reconciliation , acting Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez said on Thursday , after the general 's remains were removed from the state mausoleum where he was buried in 1975 . <p> " Modern Spain is the product of forgiveness , but it ca n't be the product of forgetfulness , " he said in a televised address . <p> Around 500,000 people were killed in the 1936-1939 Civil War between Franco 's nationalist rebels and left-wing Republicans . Many more were killed in the ensuing four decades of dictatorship resulting from Franco 's victory . <p> The antagonism between the two sides remained during the transition to democracy in the 1970s , resulting in deep divisions between right and left . <p> " A public tribute to a dictator was more than an anachronism it was an affront to our democracy , " Sanchez said . " Ending it was an obligation for the generations that did not grow up with the trauma of the Civil War and dictatorship .
