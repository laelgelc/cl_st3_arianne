It was about meat . <p> Eating red and processed meat , the headlines declared , was no longer unhealthy . It seemed -- at a glance -- that a bad thing was now a good thing . The stories were based on a recently published analysis of existing evidence in which one group of researchers recommended that " adults continue their current levels of meat consumption .
