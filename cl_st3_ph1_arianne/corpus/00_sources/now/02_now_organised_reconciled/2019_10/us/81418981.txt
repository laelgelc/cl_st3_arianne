<h> Pedestrian struck , killed on South Side <h> Thursday <p> A South Side man was struck and killed late Wednesday while walking on Lockbourne Road . <p> Columbus police and fire medics were called to the 3300 block of Lockbourne Road just north of Williams Road at 11:04 p.m. on a report that a pedestrian had been struck , according to a crash report . <p> Emergency responders found 31-year-old Matthew L. Stapleton critically injured . Stapleton was pronounced dead at the scene less than 10 minutes later , according to the report . <p> A witness said Stapleton had been walking southbound in the northbound lanes of Lockbourne Road , facing oncoming traffic , when he was struck . The witness said the vehicle that hit Stapleton fled the scene , police said . <p> The driver of that vehicle , 24-year-old Colin Stumbo , of the South Side , called police to turn himself in shortly after the crash , the report stated . <p> As of 9 p.m .
