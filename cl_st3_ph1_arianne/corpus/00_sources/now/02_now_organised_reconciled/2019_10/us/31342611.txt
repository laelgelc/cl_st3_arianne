<p> In a federal report being called " the loudest and clearest alarm bell to date " regarding climate change , the Tampa Bay area is labeled as one of three areas in Florida particularly vulnerable to rising sea levels , with the others being Miami and Apalachicola . <p> The report , the Third National Climate Assessment , also warns of increases in harmful algae blooms off Florida 's coast , worsening seasonal allergies for people already made miserable by springtime pollen and heavier rainstorms and flooding in low-lying areas , such as the storm that clobbered the Panhandle last week . <p> Unveiled Tuesday , the report , a product of five years of work by a team of 60 scientists , spells out that climate change is not something awaiting in the future -- it 's affecting life now . <p> Based on weather records going back to the 1800s , the period from 2001-2012 was the warmest on record globally .
