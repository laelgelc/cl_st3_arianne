<p> MOSCOW , Oct 22 ( Reuters ) - The Kremlin said on Tuesday that preparations to organise a four-way summit aimed at finding a resolution to the conflict in eastern Ukraine had effectively ground to a halt . <p> A breakthrough in talks last month appeared to open the way to a possible summit between Russia , Ukraine , France and Germany , known as the Normandy Four . But Moscow said on Monday that Kiev had since been sending conflicting signals . <p> " Preparations ca n't be conducted at the moment because the demands of one of the parties are constantly changing , " Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov told reporters on Tuesday . He did not say which side he was talking about . <p> " When one of the sides is claiming to have some new approaches , the ' Normandy Format ' experiences certain difficulties for such a summit to be prepared , " he said .
