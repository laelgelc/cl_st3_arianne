<p> Jay Price reports that the Army has opened a new center to study heat related injuries among troops . <p> Fort Benning , Ga . is now officially the heart of the Pentagon 's efforts to fight heat-related injuries , with the creation of a new , one-of-a-kind " heat center . " <p> Such injuries already were getting increased attention from the military . And with climate change driving ever-hotter weather , it 's getting more important to learn how to better fight them . <p> Benning is the best place for the center simply because it gets so many heat related injuries , said Dr. Meghan Galer , an emergency room doctor at Martin Army Community Hospital on base , who helped come up with the idea . <p> " No other facility sees the volume and severity of heat related illnesses that we do here to be in a position to do clinically meaningful research , " she said .
