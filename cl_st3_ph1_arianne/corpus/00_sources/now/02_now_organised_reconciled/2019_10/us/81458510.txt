<p> It 's fall and that means leaves are littering lawns around the country . <p> Time to take out the rake and bag up them up , right ? Wrong . <p> Environmental experts say raking leaves and removing them from your property is bad not only for your lawn but for the planet as a whole . <p> Although people often rake fallen leaves and send them to a landfill to prevent their lawns from being smothered and to make yards look better , in most cases , you 're fine not moving them . <p> " Just leave them where they are and grind them up , " said John Sorochan , a professor of turfgrass science at University of Tennessee . <p> However , if you have a lot of trees dumping leaves or the piles begin to mound up , Dan Sandor , a postdoctoral researcher of turfgrass science at University of Minnesota , advises mowing over the leaves with a mulching blade about once a week . <p> Here are a few reasons why you should n't rake Because leaving leaves on your lawn serves as a fertilizer , if no other fertilizers are added , it will reduce runoff , Sorochan said . <p> Blowing leaves into the street is also bad , said Minnesota 's Sandor .
