<h> Global warming eclipses nuclear war as top concern : Nobel laureate <p> Climate change is already producing longer and more frequent droughts in areas like northwestern Kenya <p> The threat of climate change has overtaken the prospect of nuclear war as the most pressing concern facing humanity , a former Colombian president and Nobel peace laureate warned Monday . <p> " After World War II , the world was facing a threat from a nuclear war , " Juan Manuel Santos , a staunch environmentalist , told the World Green Economy Summit in Dubai . <p> " The threat from a nuclear war remains ... but we have something worse , which is a certainty . That is climate change , " he said . <p> " If we do n't act , we will perish . We must act for survival .
