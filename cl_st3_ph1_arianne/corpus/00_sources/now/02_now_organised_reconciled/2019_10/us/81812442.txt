<h> Scituate to host meeting on protecting harbor from climate change <h> Friday <p> SCITUATE -- Town officials will host a meeting Tuesday for residents to share their thoughts about how Scituate can best protect its harbor from the effects of climate change . <p> Suggestions from the meeting will be included in the Scituate Harbor Resiliency Master Plan , which the town is developing together with the Metropolitan Area Planning Council to identify ways to mitigate the effects of coastal flooding , storms and sea level rise over the next 25 years .
