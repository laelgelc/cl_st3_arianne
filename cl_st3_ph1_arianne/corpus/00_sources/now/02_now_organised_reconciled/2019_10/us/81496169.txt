Aleph 's growth technique replicates cows ' muscle tissue regeneration process , just in artificial conditions -- the only big change for spaceborne creation is the faster maturation , since you can print from all sides at once instead of coping with the limitations of gravity . <p> It 's far from ready for mass production . However , Aleph 's Yoav Reisler toldSpace.com that the company planned to build on the experiment and make synthetic beef steaks available on terra firma using large-scale " bio-farms .
