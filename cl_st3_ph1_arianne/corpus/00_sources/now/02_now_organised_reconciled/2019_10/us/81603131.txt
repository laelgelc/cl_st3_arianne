<h> Former IA state rep . Roger Thomas endorses Tom Steyer <p> DES MOINES ( KWWL ) -- Former state representative Roger Thomas has announced that he will endorse democratic presidential nominee Tom Steyer . <p> Thomas says he plans to endorse Steyer because of his " win-win strategy for a green economy . " Rogers himself championed win energy and other economic developments while in the Iowa legislature . <p> In a statement Thomas said quote , " Tom really gets it . He 's a cattle rancher . He knows that farmers are part of the solution to climate change and pollution , and we do n't get anywhere by demonizing folks who are just trying to do a hard day 's work . " <p> Steyer will take the stage tomorrow night for the 4th Democratic Debate . He has also qualified for the November debate .
