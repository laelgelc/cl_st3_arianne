<h> Share : <h> Mayor calls for Boston to be carbon-free by 2050 <p> BOSTON ( WHDH ) - Boston Mayor Martin J. Walsh is calling for the city to be carbon-free by 2050 . <p> He announced the goal while discussing his new climate action plan at the Chamber of Commerce on Tuesday . <p> " We 're setting the example that we want to be , " Walsh said . " We 're letting business leaders know as we build buildings , we want to be carbon zero . We want to make sure our buildings are built with the standards that we want for the future . " <p> Walsh is heading to Denmark for a summit to discuss efforts to slow climate change . <p> ( Copyright ( c ) 2019 Sunbeam Television . All Rights Reserved .
