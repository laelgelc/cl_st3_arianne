<p> The Ice Drilling Design and Operations ( IDDO ) group at the University of Wisconsin-Madison designed and built the South Pole Ice Core ( SPICE ) drilling system , called the Intermediate Depth Drill . Based on a Danish drill called the Hans-Taunsen drill , the Intermediate Depth Drill was purpose-built for coring 1,500 meters of ice . The ice cores collected at this depth ( from the South Pole ) contain atmospheric gases from the past 40,000 years , the time of transition from the last ice age to the present warm climate . Hartman was advised by Kurbatov and Earth and Climate Sciences assistant professor Alicia Cruz-Uribe . <p> She found several particles from a volcanic interval that in the last three decades was attributed to a volcanic eruption from the Kuwae volcanic center in Vanuatu . <p> Hartman determined the geochemical signatures of the particles , that provide a unique volcanic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ composition of Kuwae volcanic products . <p> She discovered the composition was similar to volcanic products from the South American volcano Reclus , not Kuwae . <p> " The discovery challenges the established volcanic source for one of the largest ice core sulfate signals from the last millennium , " Kurbatov says .
