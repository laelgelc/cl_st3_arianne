The result partly reflected the hits Mr. Trudeau took from recent scandals -- allegations that he and his aides interfered in a criminal prosecution , and images of the Canadian leader wearing blackface -- that damaged his reputation as a champion of clean governance and diversity . <p> The task of managing a minority government should be made slightly easier by the distribution of votes .
