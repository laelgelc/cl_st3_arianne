<p> SHANGHAI , Oct 21 ( Reuters ) - China will step up efforts to end the illegal dumping of hazardous chemical waste over the next six years , raising its monitoring and treatment capacity and blacklisting violators , the environment ministry said on Monday . <p> China 's chemical sector came under heavy scrutiny this year after a factory explosion in the province of Jiangsu killed 78 people .
