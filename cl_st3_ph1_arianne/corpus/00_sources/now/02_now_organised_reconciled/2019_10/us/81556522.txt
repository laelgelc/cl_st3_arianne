<p> GENEVA ( AP ) -- An international conservation group is warning that more than half of the European tree species that exist nowhere else in the world are threatened with extinction . <p> The International Union for the Conservation of Nature said in a new report Friday that 58% of Europe 's 265 endemic trees face an elevated risk of disappearing from the continent . <p> More than 150 experts contributed to the report , which the conservancy called the first comprehensive assessment of the extinction threat for all types of trees native to Europe . <p> The European Red List of Trees classified 37% of Europe 's 454 native tree species as " threatened . " Of those , 15% are " critically endangered , " a step away from extinction , the report said .. <p> The findings come amid heightened concern about environmental issues and extinction risks in Europe and beyond .
