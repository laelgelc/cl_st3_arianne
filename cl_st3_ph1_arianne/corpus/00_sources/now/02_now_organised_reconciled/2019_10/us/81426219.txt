<h> Pennsylvania to move forward on power plant emission caps <p> HARRISBURG , Pa . ( AP ) -- Gov . Tom Wolf 's administration says it 'll start working to bring Pennsylvania into a regional consortium that sets a price and caps on greenhouse gas emissions from power plants . <p> Administration officials said Wolf will make the formal announcement Thursday that he 's ordering a start to the process of joining the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative , a consortium of northeastern and mid-Atlantic states . <p> Wolf 's aides have approached top lawmakers in recent months about passing legislation to authorize the move , without success . However , Wolf 's administration also has maintained that it can write regulations for the cap-and-trade program under its existing authority to regulate air pollution . <p> The Democrat 's move is part of his effort to fight climate change in the nation 's No. 3 electric power state and could mean the worst-polluting power plants must pay hundreds of millions of dollars to the state annually . <p> ( Copyright 2019 The Associated Press .
