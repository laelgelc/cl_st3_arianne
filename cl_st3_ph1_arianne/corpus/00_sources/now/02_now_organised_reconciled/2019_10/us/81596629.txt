<h> Thailand unveils puppies with royal pedigree <p> BANGKOK , Oct 14 ( Reuters ) - Thailand has unveiled eight puppies related to the pet dog of late King Bhumibol Adulyadej , as the country observed the third anniversary of the monarch 's death . <p> The black , brown and white puppies were born from two royal dogs artificially inseminated with semen from two male offspring of Khun Thong Daeng , the late king 's pet . <p> The project was supported by King Maha Vajiralongkorn , who was crowned this year after a mourning period for his father . <p> " King Maha Vajiralongkorn kindly wished that Khun Thong Daeng 's lineage be extended so that the people may remember late King Bhumibol Adulyadej and Khun Thong Daeng , " Kasetsart University said in a statement . <p> University veterinarians used semen from the male offspring of Khun Thong Daeng to impregnate two royal dogs .
