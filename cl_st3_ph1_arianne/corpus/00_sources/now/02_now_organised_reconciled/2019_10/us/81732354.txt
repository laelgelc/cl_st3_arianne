As a result , the company may have been exposed to far greater risk from climate change regulations than investors were led to believe , the complaint said . An expert witness for the state estimated potential damages to shareholders of $476 million to $1.6 billion . <p> The state also claims Exxon inaccurately concluded that it faced little risk associated with a " two degrees scenario , " in which the consumption of fossil fuels was severely curtailed to limit global temperature increases to below two degrees Celsius compared to pre-industrial levels .
