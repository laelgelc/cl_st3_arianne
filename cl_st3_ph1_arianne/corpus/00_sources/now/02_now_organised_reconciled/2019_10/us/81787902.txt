<h> Post <h> The Link Between Green Roofs and Energy Efficiency <p> The concept behind green roofs is n't exactly new technology . But thanks to ordinances in some parts of the world , the concept is coming into focus as a practical way to reduce energy costs , cut down on some types of pollution , retain stormwater so cities do n't get overwhelmed during weather events and sequester carbon in an age of climate change . <p> Here 's a closer look at each one of these and the reasons green roofs are the key to energy efficiency -- and so much more . <h> Green Roofs Reduce Energy Consumption <p> The basic structure of a green roof is as follows , starting with the topmost layer : <p> A study by the National Research Council of Canada found even a modestly sized green roof could reduce energy demands in the summertime by 75% or more . Why ? Because the layers of vegetation absorb and use solar energy instead of absorbing it the way asphalt roofs do . This technique is as natural as home or building insulation gets . <p> Because structures are better insulated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the hotter months , people turn to their heating and cooling appliances less often . <p> Green roofs are especially critical in cities , where the density of people , buildings and heat-absorbent materials creates what 's known as the " urban island effect . " This term refers to the pent-up , sweltering heat that creates city environments that are up to 22 degrees Fahrenheit hotter than the surrounding countryside . The EPA notes that the demand for cooling appliance electricity climbs by up to 2% for every one degree Fahrenheit the outside temperature rises . <p> Green roofs even have a role to play in keeping cities dry and functioning after heavy rainfall and storms . Green roofs can retain a considerable amount of water and allow it to evaporate harmlessly . They reduce the flow entering city stormwater systems by up to 65% and ensures cities have the necessary tools to stay on their feet with the lights on , even during intense weather events . <h> Green Roofs Help Cut Manufacturing Waste <p> It 's not unreasonable to expect a green roof to last two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conventional materials . A green roof , therefore , becomes a cost-saving measure over the lifetime of the structure and a way to avoid the manufacture of specific roofing materials in the first place . <p> Common asphalt shingle and built-up roofing systems are petroleum products . Environmental scientists warn us regularly that we need to leave as much oil in the ground as we possibly can . <p> Plus , it makes sense as a homeowner or business owner to purchase a roofing system that requires rare repairs and replacements , compared to other materials . Apart from the personal energy savings , making this change also keeps our shared manufacturing footprint , and the accompanying energy and carbon footprint , smaller than it would be otherwise . <h> Green Roofs Improve Air Quality and Reduce Pollution <p> In addition to replacing environmentally suspect roofing materials with verdant expanses for flora and fauna , green roofs are a key ally in the reduction of pollution .
