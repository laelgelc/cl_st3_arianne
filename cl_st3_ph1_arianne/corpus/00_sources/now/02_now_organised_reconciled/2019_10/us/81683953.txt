Money talks , as the saying goes . As a result of reforms , carbon prices in the European Union Emissions Trading System ( ETS ) have increased fivefold in the last two years . The headline " revenues from polluters for a fair transition " proposes using newly auctioned aviation allowances to fund measures to address housing and transport poverty and boost clean business in regions negatively affected by the transition .
