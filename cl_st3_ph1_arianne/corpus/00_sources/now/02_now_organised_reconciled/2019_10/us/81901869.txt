<h> Smog-plagued Warsaw to limit access by car , coal heating <p> Print <p> Wednesday , October 30 2019 AP <p> WARSAW , Poland ( AP ) -- Authorities in smog-plagued Warsaw revealed plans Wednesday to further limit car access to the city center , cut down on coal heating in private homes and expand green areas in a drive to improve the city 's air quality . <p> Especially in the fall and winter , when the air is damp and people use coal heaters , Warsaw can be veiled in a cloud of smog . The problem is even more intense in southern Poland , in Krakow and in the industrial and coal mining region of Silesia , which are also taking steps against it . <p> Warsaw Mayor Rafal Trzaskowski said downtown parking fees and fines will be significantly increased and more Park and Ride lots will be built to persuade drivers to use city subways , buses and trams .
