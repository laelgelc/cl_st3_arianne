<h> Weather Talk : Weather and climate are not the same thing <p> Written By : John Wheeler Oct 22nd 2019 - 6am . <p> The difference between weather and climate is often misunderstood . Climate is the long-term , generalized expectations of the averages and extremes the weather has to offer any certain location . Climate change , therefore , is not represented by the weather on any given day . <p> For example , the climate of our region went through a trend toward cooler and wetter weather in the 1960s and early 1970s . During this time , however , there were the usual variations of weather . There were dry spells and wet spells . There were colder periods and warmer periods . <p> Overall , however , temperature averaged a tad lower and rainfall a tad higher than what we had experienced in the 1940s and 1950s . <p> Recently , our climate has been growing warmer and wetter . From this , however , we should not assume the present extremely wet weather will continue forever . Likewise , our cool temperatures lately do not mask the overall warming trend . @ @ @ @
