<p> BEIRUT , Oct 27 ( Reuters ) - A Lebanese state prosecutor on Sunday banned traders and money exchangers from taking significant amounts of physical dollar currency out of the country at air and land borders , state news agency NNA said . <p> The order , which it said was issued by Public Prosecutor Ghassan Oueidat , imposed the ban until the central bank determines a new mechanism for regulating such transfers , NNA reported . <p> People had previously been able to take large sums of dollar cash out of Lebanon with a permit from customs authorities . <p> " The customs directorate will subject the transfer of funds to the mechanism that Lebanon 's central bank will determine , " NNA said . <p> Eleven days of nationwide protests have caused banks to be closed for eight working days . The country 's banking association said banks would remain closed until stability is restored , amid growing fears that a rush to withdraw savings when they reopen could deplete dwindling foreign currency deposits . <p> Separately , Central Bank Governor Riad Salameh said in a statement on Sunday that currency exchangers must be licensed by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ licensed companies or apply for a licence . <p> " Then there are no restrictions on the amounts shipped and there is no need to request prior authorisation , " the central bank statement said . <p> " The Lebanese exchange market will remain a free market in accordance with the current central bank laws and circulars . The BDL will not issue any new circular on this subject , " it said . <p> As protests grip the country , Lebanon 's banking association has held meetings in recent days in search of a way to reopen banks without triggering a run on deposits . <p> The historic wave of protests in Lebanon is targeted against political leaders blamed for corruption and steering the country towards economic collapse . There is no sign of moves towards a compromise between the government and protesters , whose demands include its resignation . <p> Lebanon , where the Lebanese pound currency is pegged to the dollar , is one of the world 's most heavily indebted states . <p> Capital inflows needed to finance the state deficit and pay for imports have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ decades , including the emergence of a parallel market for dollars .
