<p> BEIRUT , Oct 30 ( Reuters ) - The Lebanese army command on Wednesday urged protesters to open blocked roads so that life could return to normal after 13 days of demonstrations that have paralysed the country . <p> The statement followed Saad al-Hariri 's decision to resign as prime minister on Tuesday , which toppled his coalition government , satisfying one of the demands sought by protesters . <p> In a statement , the army command affirmed the right to peaceful protest as protected by law but said it applied " in public squares only " .
