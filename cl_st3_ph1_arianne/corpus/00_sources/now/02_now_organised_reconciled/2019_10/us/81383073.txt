<p> MOSCOW , Oct 1 ( Reuters ) - Russian border guards have detained 87 North Korean poachers and 11 small vessels in waters in the Sea of Japan which Moscow considers its own exclusive economic zone , the Interfax news agency cited the Federal Security Service as saying on Tuesday . <p> It is the third such incident in recent weeks amid what the Kremlin has said is a clamp down on illegal poaching by North Korea 's fishermen . Last week Russia detained three North Korean vessels and more than 200 crew .
