All-electric homes are making a comeback , in part because state leaders and environmentalists see them as a solution to global warming . ( Photo by Paul Bersebach , Orange County Register/SCNG ) <p> No doubt , many all-electric homes will be more affordable as gas becomes an antiquated and rare fuel . But there are many variables computed into electric and gas price forecasts , and models reflect a minimal impact on either side . <p> What is more quantifiable is the carbon , safety and health benefits that we know we can gain when we reduce carbon emissions in buildings . <p> The choice to build zero-emission buildings versus buildings that contribute to climate change should be a clear one , and this journey must start now because buildings constructed now will be around for decades .
