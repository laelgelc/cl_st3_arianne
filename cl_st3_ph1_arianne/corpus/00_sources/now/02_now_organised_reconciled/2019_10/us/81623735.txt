Fortunately , we have the knowledge and tools we need to clean up the industry . <p> NEW YORK -- " Men 's natures are alike , " Confucius observed ; " it is their habits that carry them far apart . " But there is one habit that unites people worldwide : coffee . Some 80% of the world 's coffee is grown on 25 million smallholder farms .
