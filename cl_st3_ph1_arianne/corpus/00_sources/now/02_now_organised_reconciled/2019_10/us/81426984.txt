<p> The Cuomo administration recently learned of new technology to remove the harmful algae blooms . Florida , which has its own state algae task force , is one of the only other states to use it . <p> A pilot program will test this new filtration technology over the next two weeks in Lake Agawam in Southampton , long plagued by algae , and similar bodies of water across the state . <p> The 40-acre body of water is regularly attacked by the blue-green algae blooms , or cyanobacteria , which are fueled by heat , phosphorus , and nitrogen . <p> " For those who live around here , the new technology ca n't come soon enough . Last year , Lake Agawam had the densest algal bloom ever recorded in a Long Island body of water , " said Elizabeth Yastrzemski , a Southampton Village resident . <p> Climate change is a likely reason for the increase in these algae blooms . Much of the nitrogen in Lake Agawam comes from runoff from the Southampton village business district .
