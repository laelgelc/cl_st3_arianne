<p> BEIJING , Oct 10 ( Reuters ) - Beijing said on Thursday that comments by U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo accusing China of human rights violations in its treatment of Muslims constituted a smear against China . <p> Foreign Ministry spokesman Geng Shuang made the comments at a daily briefing Thursday . He did not mention Pompeo in particular . <p> U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo said in a television interview on Wednesday that China 's treatment of Muslims , including the Uighurs , in western China was an " enormous human rights violation " and Washington will continue to raise the issue .
