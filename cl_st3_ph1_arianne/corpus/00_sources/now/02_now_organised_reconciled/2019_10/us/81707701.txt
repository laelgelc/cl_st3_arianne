<p> BEIJING , Oct 21 ( Reuters ) - The United States and South Korea must produce new solutions for the current standoff in the Korean Peninsula , a senior North Korean military official said on Monday , warning that hostile policies towards Pyongyang will lead to serious consequences . <p> Kim Hyong Ryong , North Korea 's Vice Minister of the People 's Armed Forces , said at the Xiangshan Forum in Beijing that North Korea has made endless efforts to build lasting piece and criticised the U.S. ' " anachronistic " policy towards Pyongyang .
