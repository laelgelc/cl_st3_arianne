<h> Do n't overburden ECB with climate goals : Weidmann <p> FRANKFURT ( Reuters ) - The European Central Bank should not be given an explicit role in fighting climate change as this could overburden policy , threaten its independence and risk its neutrality , Bundesbank President Jens Weidmann said on Tuesday . <p> With a growing number of policymakers discussing financial risk from extreme weather events , incoming ECB President Christine Lagarde recently argued that it should be a priority for central banks and supervisors to look at how they can contribute to mitigating climate change . <p> Weidmann argued that while climate change risk is not sufficiently priced into assets and most banks still fail to take such risks into consideration , it would be wrong to use bank regulation or monetary policy to achieve political goals . <p> " A monetary policy that explicitly pursues environmental goals risks being overburdened , " he told a conference . " And in the long term , its independence could be called into question .
