<h> Fariha Naqvi-Mohamed : Democracy only works if we participate <p> One of the earliest memories I have about voting dates back to my early teens . Election-time had rolled around , and I was sitting in our living room surrounded by my parents and their friends sipping chai and talking politics ; a common sight in a South Asian household . When I asked whether they would be voting , a few chuckled and said they did not need to , that there was no point . The ones who always won would win once again , and life would carry on . The message I took away was that these grownups felt their votes did n't matter . Several years later , I realized I had witnessed a perfect example of the voter apathy that often plagues many immigrant communities . <p> Over the next few elections , I remember staying up watching national real-time coverage of the results . Some ridings were being decided by a few hundred or a few thousand votes . We have come a long way since those days . Though we still have a long way to go before visible minority women feel fully welcome at the political table , we 're moving in the right direction toward governments that reflect the diversity of those who elect them . <p> We live in a massive country . The election is huge and our federal leadership hopefuls travel from coast to coast campaigning on big-ticket issues . We can sometimes get lost in the enormous scale of it all and lose sight that it actually comes down to whom we voters choose to elect , in the end . And every vote counts . <p> Every election is important and determines what sorts of policies will shape the next few years of our lives . This time around , climate has been a big issue . But will those who turned out to march turn up at the polls in similar numbers ? That remains to be seen . <p> Some feel disenfranchised when it comes to voting , others feel apathy . We can not afford to feel either . Between the economy , environment , minority rights and immigration , too much is at stake to not each do our share . It 's up to us to fulfil our civic duty and vote in the leaders of tomorrow who , in our estimation , best represent our vision for what we want our country to look like . <p> A democracy only works if we participate -- if all of us participate . We live in the best country in the world , and we all need to do our part to make it better .
