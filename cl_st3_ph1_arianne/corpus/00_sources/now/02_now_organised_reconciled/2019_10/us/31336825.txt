I know this because when I asked her a question at the Iowa State Fair on Friday afternoon , she leaned in close -- very close . <p> " Actually reaching people requires a more expanded conversation than just a conversation about externalities , " Williamson told me in a voice that was somehow both breathy and stern , her face inches from my own . I had just asked the 2020 candidate about her apparent aversion to detailed policy plans , and in response , she 'd explained that inspirational and unifying messages , more than concrete proposals , are what appeal to voters .
