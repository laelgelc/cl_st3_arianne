<h> Online Dating Linked To Increased STD Cases In Hawaii <p> Sexually transmitted diseases ( STD ) in Hawaii are at an all-time high in decades , which according to officials can be linked to the increase in online dating and hookups . <p> There has been a significant rise in the cases of chlamydia , gonorrhea , and syphilis in Hawaii due to the prevalence of online dating , the Honolulu Star-Advertiser reported . The reported cases were highest in 30 years according to the Department of Health 's Harm Reduction Services Branch . <p> Chlamydia cases went up from 5,972 cases in 2008 to 7,732 in 2018 , whereas gonorrhea cases more than doubled from 611 to 1,496 cases in the last 10 years . Syphilis cases increased from 38 a decade ago to 180 cases in 2018 . <p> According to officials , the increasing connections with other people due to online dating could be behind the undesirable and unexpected rise in the case of STDs . <p> " As people rely on digital means of making connections , it can lead to circumstances where they might be more exposed to infection without them knowing it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " said Gerald Hasty , program coordinator for the state harm reduction branch . <p> The decreasing use of protection like condoms and prophylactics also has contributed to the increase in the cases of chlamydia , gonorrhea and syphilis infections . <p> " That sets the stage for the infections to be spread , " Hasty said . " Lack of regular screenings or routine screenings contributes to increasing rates . " <p> According to Hasty , the lack of distinct and troubling early symptoms of these STDs leave people with no idea that they have contracted the infection . <p> The number of STD cases , which correspond with the national rates , have risen for the fifth straight year , officials said . <p> Experts have warned that a little known sexually transmitted infection , that causes infertility , can be the next superbug .
