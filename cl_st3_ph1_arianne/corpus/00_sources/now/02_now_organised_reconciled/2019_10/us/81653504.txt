<p> WASHINGTON , Oct 17 ( Reuters ) - The U.S. ambassador to the European Union plans to tell the House of Representatives ' impeachment inquiry into President Donald Trump 's dealings with Ukraine that asking a foreign country to act to influence a U.S. election " would be wrong .
