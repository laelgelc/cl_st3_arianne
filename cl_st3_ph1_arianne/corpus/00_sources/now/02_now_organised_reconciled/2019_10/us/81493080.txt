<h> Reader : Save birds -- keep your cat indoors <p> Letter to the editor <p> Published 5:00 am EDT , Tuesday , October 8 , 2019 <p> To the editor : <p> Given recent articles in local and Detroit papers about the decline in bird populations ( 30 billion fewer now than 1970 ) , I urge cat owners to keep your cats in your houses rather than turning them out every night to kill birds , chipmunks and whatever they can . The Audobon Society estimates that cats kill 1.4-3.7 billion birds a year -- yes , billion . That 's , to be conservative , 6.3 million per night . <p> Cats , and this is n't like they have a choice , are little killing machines . I do not understand why " living free " is a defense . Besides tearing through the bird population , it is better for the cat to be kept indoors . The average life expectancy of an indoor cat is 17 years . Outdoor cats ? Two to five years . So if you ca n't care about the victims of your babies , care about your babies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ four years rather than 15 ? <p> This drop in bird populations is on top of climate change and a problem so easy to control that I urge cat owners to keep your cats indoors . The cat wo n't know the difference but the birds and other victims will .
