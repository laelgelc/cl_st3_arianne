<h> Foundation : Sea level rise leads to island center 's closure <p> GREAT FOX ISLAND , Va . ( AP ) -- An environmental group says sea level rise and erosion are bringing an end to the use of an education center on an island in the Chesapeake Bay . <p> The Capital Gazette reports the Chesapeake Bay Foundation recently announced that this fall is the last season for its Fox Island Education Program . <p> Educators with the foundation say sea level rise and erosion are destroying the protective salt marshes surrounding the island located in Virginia waters between the Tangier and Pocomoke sounds . They say that 's making the educational center that served teachers and students unsafe . <p> The foundation says in a blog post that more than 70 percent of the island 's land area has washed away over the past 50 years . It calls the center 's closure an example of the " real and immediate threat " climate change poses .
