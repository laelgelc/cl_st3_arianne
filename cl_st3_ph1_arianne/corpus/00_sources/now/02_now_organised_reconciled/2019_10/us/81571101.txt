<h> Dutch police detain 130 Extinction Rebellion protesters <p> 1 Min Read <p> AMSTERDAM ( Reuters ) - Dutch police on Saturday said they had detained 130 protesters with environmental group Extinction Rebellion who blocked locations in downtown Amsterdam to draw attention to climate change . <p> The group , which wants governments to take immediate action to avert the worst devastation outlined by scientists studying climate , is engaged in two weeks of civil disobedience in cities worldwide . <p> In Amsterdam , demonstrators brought a boat decorated with climate change slogans to the famous " Blauwbrug " bridge in front of City Hall . <p> Police said in a statement they had decided to make arrests because the demonstration had not been approved and activists were blocking traffic . <p> The total number of arrests in the Netherlands has surpassed 200 this week . <p> The Extinction Rebellion protests are aimed at forcing governments to cut carbon emissions and avert a climate crisis it says will bring starvation and social collapse .
