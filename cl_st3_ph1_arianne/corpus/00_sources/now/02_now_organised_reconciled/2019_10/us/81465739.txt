<p> STOCKHOLM , Oct 5 ( Reuters ) - Working-level nuclear talks between North Korea and the United States have broken off , North Korea 's chief negotiator said on Saturday . <p> " The negotiations have not fulfilled our expectation and finally broke off , " the North 's chief nuclear negotiator , Kim Myong Gil , told reporters through an interpreter outside the country 's embassy in Stockholm . <p> The North Korean delegation arrived in Stockholm on Thursday for the working-level denuclearization talks . <p> North Korea said on Wednesday it had successfully test-fired a new submarine-launched ballistic missile . The launch was the most provocative by North Korea since it resumed dialogue with the United States in 2018 . U.N . Security Council resolutions ban Pyongyang from using ballistic missile technology .
