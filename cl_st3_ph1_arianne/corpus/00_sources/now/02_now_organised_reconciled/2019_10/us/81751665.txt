<p> HONG KONG Oct 22 ( Reuters ) - Hong Kong Financial Secretary Paul Chan announced on Tuesday fresh relief measures of HK$2 billion ( $255 million ) to support the economy as anti-government protests roil the Chinese-ruled city which is bracing for its first recession in a decade . <p> The move follows a HK$19.1 billion ( $2.4 billion ) package in August to support the underprivileged and business enterprises as the government grapples with five months of protests that have battered the retail and tourism sectors .
