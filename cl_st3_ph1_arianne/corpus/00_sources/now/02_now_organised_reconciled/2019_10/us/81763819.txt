<h> Nokia Mobile India to host design meetup with fans on November 9 <p> As a phone manufacturer , it is important to connect to your fans , and one of the ways to do that is to listen to their suggestions and feedback . Over the past couple of years , we have seen brands like OnePlus organize meetups ( called Open Ears Forums ) with its fans to talk about ideas for improving its current and future devices . Nokia Mobile India is borrowing a leaf from OnePlus with its design meetup . <p> The official Nokia Mobile India Twitter account has announced that it will be hosting a meetup on November 9 in New Delhi to talk about designing the future of Nokia smartphones .
