<h> South Africa announces water restrictions as dam levels drop <p> JOHANNESBURG -- South Africa 's government is announcing water restrictions as reservoir levels drop and rainfall is not expected until December . <p> Lindiwe Sisulu , the country 's water and sanitation minister , says Monday that many parts of South Africa are facing water scarcities as summer arrives in the Southern Hemisphere and climate change brings hotter , drier days . <p> South Africa 's government says the country is one of the world 's 30 driest nations . The city of Cape Town early last year faced the prospect of " Day Zero , " or the date when most taps would have to be cut off because of a long drought . <p> Sisulu says more than 60% of the country 's water supplies go to agriculture , leaving only 27% for its population of over 58 million people .
