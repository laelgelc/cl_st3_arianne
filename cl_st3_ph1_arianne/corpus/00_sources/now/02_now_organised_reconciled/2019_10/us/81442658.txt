<p> DUBLIN , Oct 4 ( Reuters ) - The police in Northern Ireland will not staff border security after Brexit , the Police Service of Northern Ireland 's chief constable said on Thursday . <p> " We will of course police the border as normal . But if the underpinning assumption is that you will see armed officers effectively staffing checkpoints in various parts of the border area , no , " Simon Byrne said in comments reported by the Irish Times <p> " You 're not going to see lines of Land Rovers waving vehicles over with PSNI people checking documents , goods , the back of white vans and so on . " <p> The chief constable told a public meeting of the Northern Ireland Policing Board that he will ask the British government for 300 additional officers to bring the force 's strength to 7,500 , at a cost of 40 million pounds ( $49.31 million ) . <p> The extra officers are in addition to 200 officers who are expected to be in place by March .
