<p> CHICAGO , Oct 16 ( Reuters ) - Chicago Public Schools , the third-largest system in the United States , canceled Thursday classes for 361,000 students , ahead of a strike deadline set by the teachers who are locked in protracted labor negotiations . <p> The district said Chicago public school buildings will be staffed by district officials and open during the day for students who need a place to go . Breakfast and lunch will be served .
