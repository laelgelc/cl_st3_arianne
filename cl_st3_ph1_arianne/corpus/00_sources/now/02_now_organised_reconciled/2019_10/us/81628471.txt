<p> TOKYO -- Japan on Tuesday was questioning its defenses against flooding after a larger-than-expected toll from a weekend typhoon , which caused more than 180 rivers to overflow or break their banks . <p> Although Tokyo and other large cities were mostly spared major damage , floods hit dozens of rural communities across a band hundreds of miles long . Typhoon Hagibis , which hit late Saturday and early Sunday , killed 74 people and left 12 missing , public broadcaster NHK said . The government 's official death toll , which has trailed media estimates by a day or so , stood at 54 , including seven crew members of a container ship that sank in Tokyo Bay confirmed dead -- six from China and one from Myanmar . <p> More than 10,000 houses were inundated , according to official figures .
