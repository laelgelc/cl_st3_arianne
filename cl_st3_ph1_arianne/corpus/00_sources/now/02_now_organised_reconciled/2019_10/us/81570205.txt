<h> Actions <h> California power shutdowns raise air pollution worries <p> Power shutdowns intended to prevent more devastating California wildfires are raising concerns about another environmental threat : air pollution . <p> Utilities temporarily halted service to more than 2 million people this week , fearing high winds would knock down power lines and ignite infernos . <p> That led many hospitals , businesses and others to fire up standby generators , some fueled with diesel and gasoline that spew toxic emissions . <p> Some officials say that could further harm California 's already poor air quality . <p> They say it 's too early to know how significant the effects could be . <p> But the need to consider environmental impacts of blackouts is a new wrinkle for policymakers and planners dealing with a constant threat of catastrophic fires and more extreme weather due to climate change . <p> Copyright 2019 Scripps Media , Inc .
