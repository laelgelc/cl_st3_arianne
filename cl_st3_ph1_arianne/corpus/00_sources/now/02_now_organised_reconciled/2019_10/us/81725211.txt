<p> BERLIN , Oct 21 ( Reuters ) - German Defence Minister Annegret Kramp-Karrenbauer on Monday suggested the creation of a security zone in northern Syria to protect displaced civilians and ensure that the fight continues against Islamic State militia . <p> " My suggestion is that we set up an internationally controlled security zone involving Turkey and Russia , " Kramp-Karrenbauer , also party leader of Chancellor Angela Merkel 's conservatives , told Deutsche Welle broadcaster in an interview . <p> The step should also stabilize the region so that civilians could rebuild and refugees could return on a voluntary basis , Kramp-Karrenbauer said . <p> Kramp-Karrenbauer added she had liaised closely with Syria and that she had informed Germany 's most important allies about the proposal . <p> It is the first time that the German government has proposed a military mission in the Middle East .
