<h> Letters : Resolve is needed to fight climate change <h> Share this : <p> The debate on anthropogenic climate change is over . The debate now lies in our reaction to this new reality . We know that sea levels will rise , species will increasingly go extinct , ecosystems will be significantly disrupted , weather extremes will increase , and the oceans will acidify . The question is to what extent these events will occur and to what extent we are capable of reducing the severity of these changes . <p> Business as usual has to change at a systems level and this will require a significant shift in priorities and investment into restructuring how our society operates . If you look at the incredible rate of societal change currently , you know we are capable of this magnitude of change . The question is whether we have the resolve and foresight to accomplish it . <p> In the Fourth National Climate Assessment Report where 22 different climate economic impacts related to health , infrastructure , electricity , water resources , agriculture , and ecosystems were examined , two scenarios were presented .
