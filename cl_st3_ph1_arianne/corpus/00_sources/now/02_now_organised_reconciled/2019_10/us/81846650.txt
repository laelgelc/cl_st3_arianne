<h> Environmental forum scheduled for Tuesday in Attleboro <p> ATTLEBORO -- State Rep. Jim Hawkins is sponsoring a hearing on the environment Tuesday night at Murray Unitarian Universalist Church , 505 North Main St. <p> The hearing is the latest in his series of forums on top issues and will touch on climate change and energy policy . It will start at 6:30 p.m . <p> Hawkins , D-Attleboro , said he was asked to organize the forum by people interested in environmental issues . <p> Groups that will be participating include Environmental League of Massachusetts , Citizens Against the Rehoboth Compressor Station , Coalition of Social Justice , Attleboro Land Trust , and Unitarian Universalist Action Group . <p> Speakers will include state Senators Becca Rausch , D-Needham , and Paul Feeney , D-Foxboro . <p> Hawkins said he is expecting a large turnout because so many groups are involved . <p> He has previously held forums on health care , opioid addiction and other issues . <h> Watch this discussion.Stop watching this discussion . <h> ( 0 ) comments <h> Welcome to the discussion . <p> Keep it Clean .
