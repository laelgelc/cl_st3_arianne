<p> The teenager faced pressure to withdraw a complaint to police in March accusing the school principal of attempted rape <p> ( Adds details , comments ) <p> DHAKA , Oct 24 ( Reuters ) - The principal of a religious school in Bangladesh was among 16 people sentenced to death on Thursday for the murder of a teenage girl who refused to withdraw a complaint of sexual harassment against him , the public prosecutor said . <p> The perpetrators poured kerosene over Nusrat Jahan , 18 , and set her on fire on the roof of her madrasa in April in the southeastern district of Feni . Police said in their charge-sheet the murder was carried out on the orders of the principal . <p> " The judgment proves that no one is above the law , " public prosecutor Hafez Ahmed told reporters after the court verdict . <p> He said the defence lawyers had tried unsuccessfully to establish that Jahan had committed suicide . <p> Defence lawyer Giasuddin Nannu said all the convicts will challenge the verdict in the High Court . <p> Jahan 's death sparked public outrage and mass demonstrations calling for her killers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a complaint to police in March accusing the school principal of attempted rape , her family said . <p> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina had met her family and vowed to bring the killers to justice . <p> Two female classmates and two local leaders of her party were among those convicted . <p> After the murder , the government instructed some 27,000 educational institutions to form committees to prevent sexual assaults . <p> " I ca n't forget her for a moment .
