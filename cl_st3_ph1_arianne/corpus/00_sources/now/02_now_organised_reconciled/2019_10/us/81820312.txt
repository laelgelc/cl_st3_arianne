<h> Frontline releases Camp Fire documentary on Tuesday <p> Long-form news program Frontline features a story on the Camp Fire in its next broadcast on Tuesday , " Fire in Paradise . " <p> Narration -- punctuated by interviews with police , firefighters , a 911 operator and other first responders -- takes viewers through the fire 's progression from its beginning to its escalation into California 's most destructive wildfire . <p> Paradise residents tell personal stories about what happened on Nov. 8 , 2018 -- the day the fire burned most of their town and nearby communities . The fire killed 85 people . <p> The program includes dash-camera footage of people frantically fleeing the fire and news footage of the fire at each stage of its development . <p> Documented are emergency plans , contrived in the 1990s and updated 10 years ago , that were insufficient to handle a town-wide evacuation . <p> Other topics covered include the role of climate change in the speed and temperature of the Camp Fire and scientific explanations for fireballs and other extreme phenomena caused by the fire . <p> Reflections on what was learned and remembrances of some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ program . <p> While the subject is touched on throughout the show , the last 10 minutes take an intensive look at the degree to which Pacific Gas &amp; Electric Co. should be held responsible for the fire and if negligence was the cause . <p> Previewed at the beginning of the program and shown in full toward the end is an interview with Butte County district attorney Mike Ramsey .
