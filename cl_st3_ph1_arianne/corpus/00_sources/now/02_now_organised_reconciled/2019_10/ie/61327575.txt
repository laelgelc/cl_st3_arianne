<h> New species of beetle named after climate activist Greta Thunberg <p> A tiny new species of beetle that was found more than 50 years ago has been named after environmental campaigner Greta Thunberg . <p> Scientists at the Natural History Museum in London have officially called the insect Nelloptodes gretae to honour the 16-year-old Swedish activist 's " outstanding contribution " in raising global awareness of climate change . <p> The arthropod , which has no eyes or wings , is less than 1mm long and belongs to the Ptiliidae family -- which is made up of some of the world 's smallest beetles . <p> Dr Michael Darby , a scientific associate at the Natural History Museum , said : " I chose this name as I am immensely impressed with the work of this young campaigner and wanted to acknowledge her outstanding contribution in raising awareness of environmental issues . " ( The Trustees of the Natural History Museum/PA ) <p> The beetle was first found in 1965 by British naturalist Dr William C Block in Nairobi , Kenya . <p> His collection was donated to the Natural History Museum in 1978 and the beetle was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's Spirit Collection . <p> Dr Max Barclay , senior curator in charge of Coleoptera at the Natural History Museum , said : " The name of this beetle is particularly poignant since it is likely that undiscovered species are being lost all the time , before scientists have even named them , because of biodiversity loss -- so it is appropriate to name one of the newest discoveries after someone who has worked so hard to champion the natural world and protect vulnerable species . " <p> The beetle has been featured in The Entomologist journal 's monthly magazine . <p> Ms Thunberg was nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize in 2019 , which was later awarded to Ethiopia 's Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed .
