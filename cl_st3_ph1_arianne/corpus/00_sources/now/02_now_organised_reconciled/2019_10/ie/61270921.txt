One might well ask whether the country is ready for a new left-wing political entity . <p> Paul Murphy 's group is called Rise , which stands for radical , internationalist , socialist , environmentalist . Rise will , according to Murphy , try to build a new socialist movement in Irish society . <p> The split that gave birth to Rise is not the typical one known in Irish politics , where splits traditionally lead to rancour and bitterness and occasionally violence .
