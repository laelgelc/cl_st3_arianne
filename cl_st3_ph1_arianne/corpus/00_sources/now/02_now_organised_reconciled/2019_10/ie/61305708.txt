In a food secure community , all people at all times have economic and physical access to food that is safe and nutritious . <p> Water security is the capacity of a population to ensure sustainable access to adequate quantities of water for human well-being and socio-economic development . In a water secure community , quality water is available for people , industry , and the environment . <p> Discussions of a green economy or sustainable development often focus on issues of transport , energy , waste management , or ecological conservation . According to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) 2014 report , agriculture produces a quarter of global greenhouse gas emissions , which is more than all of our trucks , cars , and planes combined . Agriculture is also the largest consumer of freshwater resources in the UAE .
