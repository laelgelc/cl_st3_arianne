<h> Rodney Farry <h> Windfarms are ' putting fear into communities ' <h> Photo shows , Cllr Tom Farrell . <p> The prospect of windfarms being built across central Westmeath is " putting fear into communities " , according to Cllr Tom Farrell . <p> Speaking to the Westmeath Examiner , Cllr Farrell , who is a farmer ( according to the Fine Gael website ) , and is based in Walderstown , said that representatives from at least two windfarm developers have been approaching farmers in Kilbeggan , Ballymore , Drumraney and Moyvoughley about leasing land . <p> While he said that it was up to individual farmers to decide what they do with their land , he urged them to seek independent advice before agreeing to anything . <p> " I am afraid that if someone signs a contract , it leaves their farms open to a lot of unforeseen circumstances if anything happens , " said Cllr Farrell . <p> " I would appeal to farmers that if they are approached , they would seek independent assistance . <p> " I have been told that some windfarm companies are offering to pay fees and I think @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hope the farming bodies get on board . I have been in touch with the IFA president , Joe Healy , on this matter and I hope that they give their members good advice .
