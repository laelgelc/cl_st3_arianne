<h> John Bird : Beyond soap and sandwiches , there must be a better solution <p> I got a very sincere letter from people who were worried that if you were homeless then you had nowhere to wash . They suggested showers and baths be reinstalled , the public baths of old , in our cities . At least then the homeless could wash . <p> Other people I get letters from suggest a cleverer , more deeply spread use of the soup line . And other things , like getting all of the food stuff from supermarkets , out of date stuff , and getting it to people in need . <p> I never ever get a letter from anyone saying , " How can we end this need ? How can we stop the bestial way in which countless stopgaps do n't do more than cover the day ? " <p> I think that is because we are taught how to think not deeply , but sketchily , as if life was some large supermarket , made up of many aisles . We can not seem to get over this hurdle , which would allow us to imagine what is necessary to dismantle the problem , rather than to make those with the problem more comfortable . <p> Do n't think you are alone in all of this . " . <p> Stopgaps lead only to people actually having no future <p> Of course I am not so silly or rude as to do that .
