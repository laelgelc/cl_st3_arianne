The strategy will ensure that renewable energy accounts for 70% of all electricity use in Ireland by 2030 -- more than double current levels . <p> Key to the new strategy is upgrading the power system so that it can handle world-leading levels of renewable energy , supplied through a combination of offshore and onshore wind , along with solar energy . <p> EirGrid Group estimates that the cost of delivering this strategy is in excess of ? 2 billion over the next five years . <p> Launching the strategy at the EirGrid Group Annual Conference at the National Conference Centre today , CEO Mark Foley said : " The strategy EirGrid is setting out today is a direct response to the significant but necessary challenges we have been set in the Government 's Climate Action Plan .
