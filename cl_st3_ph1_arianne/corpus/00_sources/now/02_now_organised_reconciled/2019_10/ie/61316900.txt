<h> Monaco 's new ' eco ' district takes shape <p> The caisson belt surrounds the area of reclaimed land , stretching for 500m <p> Work is underway to install approximately 750,000 tonnes of quarry sand , as part of a plan to extend the shoreline and build a new ' eco ' district , known as L'Anse du Portier , in Monaco . <p> The sand will be used to fill the space between the caisson belt and the shoreline using two bulk carriers to carry out this operation . <p> The caisson belt is a protective barrier-type structure 500 metres in length , comprising of 18 concrete " boxes " . These individual blocks are assembled 200m from the coast of Monaco and will protect the new district from the swelling nature of the sea waves . <p> 750,000 tonnes of sand is being installed within the caisson belt <p> The wave power is intended to be absorbed by damping chambers on the wall membrane .
