Quality of life has three main dimensions that we should take into account . <p> First , an economic one , where household incomes rely on working with productive and competitive companies . <p> The second is social , with households able to access a broad set of services and where local society is cohesive and supportive . <p> The last is a local environment , that is a pleasant place to live . <p> Our SMART Villages for Rural Communities Intergroup initiative tries to take account of both technological and social innovation .
