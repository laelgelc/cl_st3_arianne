<h> Operation WildNation aims to protect under threat wild bird species <p> ONE of Ireland 's biggest garden centres is on a mission to drive primary school pupils absolutely wild . <p> CountryLife Garden Centres want to educate the nation about wild bird conservation through its exciting and engaging new Operation WildNation initiative . Ultimately they 're helping to protect some of our under threat wild bird species -- among these thrushes and finches - during the tough autumn and winter months . <p> The group , which has 14 CountryLife Garden Centre outlets across the country along with an online gardening website www.countrylife.ie , has teamed up with renowned environmentalist , author and broadcaster , ? anna Ni Lamhna .
