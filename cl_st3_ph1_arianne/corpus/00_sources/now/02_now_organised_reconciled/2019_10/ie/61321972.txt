She called for " reconsideration " of the environmental principles during the passage of the bill . <p> " We remain convinced that the aspect of the bill on environmental principles is not fit for purpose , since the duty to apply the environmental principles is not explicitly set out in the bill and defers to a policy statement , " she said . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , which makes it difficult to judge how they will be interpreted and applied .
