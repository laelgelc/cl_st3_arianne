<h> Europe 's peatlands are drying out en masse , and that 's a big problem <p> Once a vital part of local economies in Ireland , peatlands are now drying out across Europe , reducing their ability to absorb CO2 . <p> Findings published to Nature Geoscience by researchers in the UK have shown that peatlands which were once considered one of Europe 's greatest carbon sinks could be about to do the complete opposite as a result of the climate crisis . <p> According to the BBC , peatlands hold almost five times more CO2 than forests . However , this recent study found that 31 peatlands across Ireland , England , Scandinavia and continental Europe are experiencing substantial change . <p> It said that 40pc were found to be drier than they have been in the past 1,000 years , while 24pc were drier than they have been in the past 2,000 years . <p> It 's estimated that Britain and Ireland alone hold 20pc of the world 's blanket bog .
