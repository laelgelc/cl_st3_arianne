<h> New wave of CCS activity : 10 large-scale projects announced <p> As of October 2019 , there are now 51 carbon capture and storage ( CCS ) facilities globally -- 19 in operation , four under construction and 28 in various stages of development . <p> These facilities have an estimated combined capture capacity of 96 million tonnes of CO2 per annum . <p> That 's according to think tank the Global CCS Institute who has added 10 large-scale CCS facilities to its database . <p> Eight of the new facilities are in the US and cover applications such as ammonia production , ethanol production , power , direct air capture , and integrated commercial storage hubs . <p> CO2 storage hubs , which can store large amounts of CO2 , remove barriers to CCS investment and help to bring down costs . <p> " These carbon capture and geologic storage projects across multiple industries and different regions of the country underscore how the revamped federal 45Q tax credit , coupled in some cases with Department of Energy ( DOE ) funding for engineering , is stimulating early project development and investment , " said Brad Crabtree , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ director of the Carbon Capture Coalition . <p> " This is just the beginning -- we will see even more projects emerge as soon as the US Treasury comes out with its long-delayed guidance on the 45Q credit .
