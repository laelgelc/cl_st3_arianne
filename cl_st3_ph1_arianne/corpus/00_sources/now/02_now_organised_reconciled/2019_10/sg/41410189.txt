<p> The results of India 's elections , out on Thursday , will have a huge impact on the direction of the country over the next five years . Exit polls seem to suggest that the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party will remain in power , though others have warned that these surveys have hardly been reliable in the past . <p> If the BJP does return , critics of the government have argued that its willingness to attack Indian institutions and even nominate a terror-accused as a Parliamentary candidate could irrevocably change India .
