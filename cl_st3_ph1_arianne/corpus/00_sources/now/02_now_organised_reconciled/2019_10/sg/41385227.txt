<p> Water is arguably the most important natural resource on the planet and , considering the growing fears about its availability as the world 's population grows and climate change makes it scarcer , it is unsurprising that investors are starting to pay attention . <p> Water may not be a tradeable commodity like oil or gold , but it is vital and in limited supply . Over 70% of the Earth is covered in water but less than 1% of it can be consumed as drinking water , and fears about droughts and water shortages are increasing . The world desperately needs to improve the way it manages its water supplies .
