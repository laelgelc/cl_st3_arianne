<h> Singapore nun who counselled death row inmates on BBC list of inspiring women <p> A Roman Catholic nun has become the first Singaporean to make the BBC 's annual list of 100 influential and inspiring women around the world . <p> Sister Gerard Fernandez , 81 , worked with the prisons here as a death row counsellor for more than 40 years until 2017 .
