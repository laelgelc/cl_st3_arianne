<h> " IMF warning for Belize " <p> BELMOPAN , Belize -- The International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) says while Belize 's economic recovery is continuing , the pace is slowing . <p> An IMF delegation headed by the Deputy Division Chief in the IMF Western Hemisphere Department , Daniel Leigh , has ended a two week visit to the Caribbean Community ( CARICOM ) country holding talks with Prime Minister Dean Barrow , the Governor of the Central Bank of Belize , Joy Grant as well as Joseph Waight , Financial Secretary ; and representatives of the opposition , private sector , and public sector unions . <p> In a statement , the Washington-based financial institution said real gross domestic product ( GDP ) grew by 3.2 per cent last year and by an estimated four per cent in the first quarter of this year . <p> It said that unemployment rate reached a historic low of 7.6 per cent in April this year , with inflation near zero .
