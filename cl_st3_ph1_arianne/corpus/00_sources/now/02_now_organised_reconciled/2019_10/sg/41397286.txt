<h> Flipkart creates a spirit of goodwill for the underserved through Daan Utsav <p> As a leading ecommerce marketplace in India , Flipkart is leveraging its platform to enable its wider community of buyers and sellers to give back to the society for various social causes with the Daan Utsav programme . <p> Flipkart , India 's leading ecommerce marketplace , is not all about business . The Walmart-owned company believes strongly in giving back to the society . In partnership with GiveIndia , one of the leading donation platforms in the country , Flipkart has launched programmes like Daan Utsav and Giving Tuesday India to offer help to those in need . <p> Giving Tuesday is a global programme started in 2012 in New York , US , while Daan Utsav was launched in 2009 in India . Both aim to serve the underprivileged . <p> The Daan Utsav week is being held from October 5 to 10 this year and will highlight the various causes citizens can contribute to from the Flipkart platform .
