<h> Virtual reality simulations could bring home , in a visual , visceral way , the realities of the consequences of global warming , writes Emma Bailey . <h> Share this story <p> As with many emerging technologies , virtual reality advancements seem to be relevant at present only to serious gamers and hardcore tech enthusiasts . However , we can expect that as investments in the technology grow , and the necessary equipment becomes more stable and inexpensive , new applications will be developed that serve the needs of a more mainstream audience . <p> VR has the potential to focus public attention on topics that are serious but are relatively inaccessible to laypeople , such as the importance of climate change and environmental degradation . <p> Despite the seeming accord among climate science experts about the need to combat global warming , many others lack an empathic , emotional connection to this subject . <p> It 's not easy for most people to think decades down the line and envision what our world will look like if pollution is left unchecked . The Des Moines Register used the Oculus Rift to cover the history and transformation of a family farm .
