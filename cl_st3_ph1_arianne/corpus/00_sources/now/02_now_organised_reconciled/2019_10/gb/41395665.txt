<h> Symphony Orchestra hits the right notes with Scottish school kids <h> BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra hits the right notes with kids <p> AROUND 1500 young musicians from one of Scotland 's most diverse communities had the chance to learn from some of the country 's top instrumentalists this week . <p> Scotland 's oldest professional orchestra , the BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra ( BBC SSO ) , took up residence at Holyrood Secondary School in Glasgow 's Govanhill to share the joy of making music . <p> Alongside children 's music programme , Big Noise Govanhill , the orchestra gave primary and secondary pupils from nearby schools the chance to take part in song-writing workshops , side-by-side coaching and concerts . <p> BBC SSO director Dominic Parker said the experience had been " inspiring " and a " learning curve " for the whole orchestra . <p> He added : " The pupils have been really engaged and creative . Working with them to explore their hopes for the future and anxiety about climate change through song-writing has been a real highlight .
