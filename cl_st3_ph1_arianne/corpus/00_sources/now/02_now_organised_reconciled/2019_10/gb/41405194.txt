<h> New electric charging points installed in Calderdale <p> A new rapid charging point for electric vehicles has been installed in North Bridge car park , Halifax , and is ready to use . A second charger , in the town 's High Street car park , is due to follow soon . <p> This is part of a collaboration between West Yorkshire Combined Authority , Bradford , Calderdale , Kirklees , Leeds and Wakefield councils , and Leeds-based energy and services company , ENGIE . In total , 88 rapid charging points will be installed across West Yorkshire , supporting a joint commitment to reducing harmful emissions . Each charging point will have one bay exclusively for taxis and private hire vehicles and a second bay for use by the public . <p> As part of the network , in addition to the charging points in North Bridge and High Street car parks , a further six will be installed across Calderdale throughout the year to help tackle the climate emergency . <p> The rapid charging points can typically take under an hour to provide a full charge , depending on your vehicle .
