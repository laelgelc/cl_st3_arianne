This will surely infuriate her critics to no end , particularly President Trump , who has made it abundantly clear that he believes he is a more deserving candidate for the award , but for the generation of young people she 's inspired to take a political stand against climate change , it will only affirm what they already know : that Thunberg is a serious force for change . <p> In just over a year , Thunberg has mobilised millions of people across the planet , encouraging students and now also adults to strike from school and work in protest of climate change . Eventually , her weekly protests picked up momentum and what started as a one-person effort to make a change transformed into a global movement . Now , she is one of the most influential people in the world . So often we tell ourselves that we are individually powerless to have a meaningful impact on the things that matter to us , but Thunberg 's story proves otherwise .
