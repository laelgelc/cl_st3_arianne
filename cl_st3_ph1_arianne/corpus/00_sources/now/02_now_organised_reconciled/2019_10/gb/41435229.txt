Yet , one of the very great things about a garden is the way that it puts you in touch with the reality of those cycles that humans mark out so uniformly and clinically as " time " . Yet , simultaneously , to garden is also to invest in the future , to think about how plants and trees will look and the space they will need in six months , or five years or 20 years .
