We have decarbonised faster than any major economy , reducing our emissions by 38% since 1990 . Yet we know we need to go further and faster , which is why Parliament supported the world-leading net zero target . Now the Government must outline a strategy , concrete policies and a road map on how we are going to get there . <p> Climate change and the decline of our nature is the most serious threat we face . It 's an opportunity to grow our economy more sustainably . Every week I have meetings with people from a wide range of organisations fully invested in seeing us succeed in meeting our net zero target . <p> In every meeting , there is agreement on what the challenge is and the conversation moves onto the how and when they can play their part . If we are to harness this enthusiasm , we will first and foremost need to provide more information . <p> Not everyone will read the 277-page Committee on Climate Change ( CCC ) net zero report -- or even the 630-page Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change report on the impacts of 1.5 degrees of global warming -- or the daily announcements coming from different government departments . It has been great to see businesses come forward with their own net zero targets , such as the water industry which has committed to carbon neutrality by 2030 . <p> The climate change movement fails when it fails to bring people with it .
