<h> BE Design appoints new Associate Director <p> 07 October 2019Consultancy.uk <p> Chartered environmentalist Charlotte Booth has been appointed Associate Director at professional services firm BE Design . Booth brings more than a decade of experience to the firm , and joins from Hodkinson Consultancy , where she most recently served as Head of Environmental Modelling . <p> BE Design is a professional services firm specialising in architecture and built environment planning . Founded in 2013 , the multidisciplinary company based in Newark , Nottinghamshire works to orchestrate the success of clients and their projects across the UK and Europe . The firm provides high-level review and due diligence services alongside its traditional planning and project delivery offerings . <p> As BE Design looks to strengthen its capabilities , the firm has installed Charlotte Booth as a new Associate Director . A chartered environmentalist with more than a decade of consultancy experience , Booth will lead on sustainability and well-being at the firm 's practice , an area where she has significant experience . <p> Booth joined Climate Consulting in 2008 after obtaining a Degree in Natural Sciences from the University of Durham . She became a Senior Consultant @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hodkinson Consultancy in 2011 as a Senior Energy Specialist . At this point , Booth opted to return to studying , obtaining an MSc in Building Energy and Environmental Performance Modelling from the Welsh School of Architecture , Cardiff University , alongside her work . She remained at Hodkinson Consultancy until 2019 , becoming Director Of Business Development &amp; Head of Environmental Modelling in that time . <p> Looking forward to her new role , Booth said , " There 's an incredible team here , who are committed to creating exceptional places to live and work in , which have minimal environmental impact and which are fit for future generations . Climate change is a very real threat , one that is n't going to go away , so we have to be incorporating sustainable practices into everything we do in terms of building design . Businesses and councils are declaring states of emergency . But now , we have to deliver practical solutions to deliver the net zero carbon aspirations of our clients . " <p> Anna Tsartsari , BE Design Director , added , " Sustainability is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , an ethos , a commitment to the future of our planet . Designing sustainably for the future has been at the core of our business approach to design from day one . Charlotte will hugely benefit our clients , bringing an incredible amount of sector knowledge to their projects . She has already proven to be a fantastic addition to the team and we 're delighted to have her on board . "
