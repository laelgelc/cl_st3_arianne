One was clutching his damp rucksack , silent in his drunkenness . His companion looked as if he was about to throw up . <p> " You will have to take them home , " I said , with all the weary experience of the mother of two , now grown and sensible , sons . It is a tourist brochure come to life . <p> At night it is a dark , mysterious place , prone to infestations of nkhungu , the Malawi midges which converge in their millions , sometimes suffocating fishermen . And the temperature drops , making it an inhospitable place for a drunk , unconscious teenager to spend the night . <p> " I know , " chorused my husband and Chimwaza , who proceeded to bundle the limp , deadweight into the back of our car , along with his two , bewildered companions . The lake , which once teemed with so many fish that villagers could literally scoop them out of the water , is now under threat . <p> Fishing controlled <p> Climate change has already brought erratic rainfall patterns and extremes of temperature , threatening water levels .
