<h> Gwent schoolchildren plant tree in unison with children in Uganda <h> Cwmcarn Primary School pupils plant tree as part of The Size of Wales Mbale programme <p> PUPILS from Cwmcarn Primary School helped first minister Mark Drakeford and teenage environmental activist Leah Namugerwa plant trees on two continents to help tackle climate change . <p> Leah Namugerwa , 15 , planted the 10 millionth tree in the The Size of Wales Mbale scheme - which has been planing trees in the heavily deforested region of Mount Elgon region in eastern Uganda - on Friday . And , at the same time , the first minister , helped by youngsters from Cwmcarn Primary , planted a ' twin tree ' in Cardiff 's Bute Park . <p> ( Mark Drakeford with a member of Cwmcarn Primary School 's eco team ) <p> ( The tree planting happening at the same time in Uganda ) <p> As well as fighting climate change , fast growing trees will protect local people in the Mount Elgon region from the effects of soil erosion , which can cause deadly landslides .
