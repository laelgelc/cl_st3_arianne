<p> As Scotland seeks to reduce climate emissions even faster than before , we are in serious danger of taking the wrong road , writes Dr Richard Dixon . <p> Last week 's vote on the Climate Bill saw a welcome increase in Scotland 's short-term target to a 75 per cent reduction by 2030 . This was a huge success for the 40,000 people in Scotland who marched and rallied on the streets the Friday before . <p> Now that we have agreed the new targets , the Climate Change Plan will be thoroughly revised by the spring of next year . In parallel , the Scottish Government will revise its Energy Strategy . <p> We have a target of 100 per cent of the electricity we use in Scotland to come from renewables by 2020 . <p> We wo n't make it , mostly because of the UK Government 's total lack of enthusiasm for renewable energy , but we 'll get close , with the figure at 74 per cent last year .
