<h> Creating a healthier future for Calderdale <p> Calderdale Council is furthering its commitment to tackling the obesity crisis by considering adoption of the Local Government Healthy Weight Declaration . <p> The declaration is a council commitment to promote healthy weight and looks to ensure that the healthy choice is the easier choice . <p> Initially developed by the Food Active programme , the declaration has already been adopted by a number of councils in the North West of England . <p> Calderdale Council 's Cabinet will now discuss the recommendation to join them and adopt the 14 standard commitments and up to seven local commitments of the declaration , all aimed at tackling obesity and its related health issues . <p> Being overweight is linked with a wide range of diseases , including type 2 diabetes , hypertension , some cancers , heart disease , stroke and liver disease . It 's estimated that the NHS spends around ? 6 billion on overweight and obesity related ill health each year . <p> Latest figures indicate that 69.9% of adults in Calderdale are either overweight or obese . The great work on nutrition in Calderdale is also supported by Active Calderdale -- the Council 's physical activity movement to help Calderdale become the most active borough in the north of England by 2024 . <p> The item will be discussed by Calderdale Council 's Cabinet on Monday 4 November 2019 at Halifax Town Hall starting at 6pm.
