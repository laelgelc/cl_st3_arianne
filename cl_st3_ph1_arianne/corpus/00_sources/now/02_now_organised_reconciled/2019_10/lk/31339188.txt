At the other end of the scale , only 61% of Western Europeans and 70% of North Americans say they consider cooperation to be extremely or very important . <p> Asked whether their country has a responsibility to help other countries in the world , South Asians again registered the highest levels of concurrence , with 94% answering positively compared to a global average of 72% .
