<h> Dengue-related deaths almost double this year as rainy season deepens <p> There have been 88 dengue-related deaths this year , almost double the reported confirmed deaths for the disease in 2018 - and there are still three months left to go in the year . <p> For the period January 1 , 2018 to September 30 , 2019 , there were 135 suspected and confirmed dengue-related deaths , according to Health Minister Dr Christopher Tufton . <p> He said that 47 deaths were reported in 2018 , while 88 occurred in 2019 . Of that number , he said 10 occurred in September which is historically a month that is a harbinger of hurricanes and inclement weather . <p> During Tuesday 's sitting of the House of Representatives , Tufton noted that despite the efforts of the Government , the reduction in the number of cases has not been sustained sufficiently below the epidemic threshold .
