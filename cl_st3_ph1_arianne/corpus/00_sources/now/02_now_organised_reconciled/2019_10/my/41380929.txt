<h> Importers of plastic waste must bear costs , says Yeo <p> GEORGE TOWN : Malaysia will insist that importers who bring in plastic waste bear the costs of sending the items back to their places of origin . <p> Minister of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Yeo Bee Yin ( pix ) said this is part of the negotiations initiated by the ministry with the foreign embassies and high commissions as well as importers and local agents . They have in negotiations over the last three months . <p> Yeo also said contractors must also be held liable for the holding charges while the cargoes remain unloaded at the Malaysians ports . <p> " They were the ones who brought it here ( illegally ) and it does not make sense that we must pay for the costs of sending it back .
