<h> Banks ' green bonds can spur RE sector , RAM Sustainability says <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The move by Malaysian banks to issue green bonds and sukuk will spur the growth of the renewable energy sector , RAM Sustainability says . <p> RAM Sustainability CEO Promod Das said the sound credit profiles of Malaysian banks and their ability to utilise issuance proceeds to fund green projects make them ideal candidates as issuers of green bonds and sukuk . <p> " Issuing green bonds and sukuk is a tangible means for banks to signal to their stakeholders and the capital markets their response to climate change and their commitment to responsible finance , " he said . <p> To support the thrust towards achieving Malaysia 's targeted renewable energy mix of 20% by 2025 , the Green Investment Tax Allowance and Green Income Tax Exemption incentives will be extended to 2023 . <p> This was announced in the Budget 2020 proposals last Friday . In addition , a 70% income tax exemption of up to 10 years will be given to companies undertaking solar leasing activities . <p> " The issuance proceeds from bank green bonds can be utilised @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their own , may be too small to tap the capital markets directly . This will further catalyse the development of Malaysia 's renewable energy sector , " he added . <p> Dass also said bank green bonds and sukuk could also be in a sweet spot for institutional investors that adhere to responsible investing principles and are beginning to seek out green assets for their portfolios .
