According to a widely accepted precedent , if a Supreme Court vacancy falls closer to a presidential election , the Senate confirmation hearing is postponed with bipartisan support until after a new presidential term begins . <p> But this time , Trump and his democracy-defying Republican Party Senators ditched the convention and decided to begin the Senate confirmation hearing , giving credence to the Democratic Party 's allegation that the Republicans were trying to pack the nine-judge Supreme Court bench with conservative judges .
