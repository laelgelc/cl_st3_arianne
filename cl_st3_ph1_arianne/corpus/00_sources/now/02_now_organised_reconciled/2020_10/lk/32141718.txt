However , the ongoing COVID 19 pandemic has challenged the truthfulness of many of these companies regarding the steps taken to protect the three pillars of sustainability . <p> After the onset of COVID 19 , many organisations had to revise and regenerate their sustainability initiatives . In particular , they had to strengthen their managerial decision making to ensure sustainability in every organizational initiative .
