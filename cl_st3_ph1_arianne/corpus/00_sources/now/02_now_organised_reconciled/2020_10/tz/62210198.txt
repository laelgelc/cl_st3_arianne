With the launch of a new comprehensive strategy with Africa , the next EU-AU Summit in October , and the planned endorsement of the post-Cotonou Agreement in November , the EU intends to open a new chapter in EU-Africa relations . In addition , Germany 's Council Presidency during the second half of the year has a clear regional focus on Africa .
