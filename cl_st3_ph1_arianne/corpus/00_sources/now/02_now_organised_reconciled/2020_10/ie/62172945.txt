<h> Climate justice demonstration to march in Galway city <p> A climate justice demonstration will take place in Galway City later today , with protestors to march through the city centre to the Spanish Arch . <p> The protest , which is being organised by a coalition of local groups , will assemble at the Town Hall Theatre at 1pm this afternoon . <p> It will then proceed to Eyre Square via Woodquay , before continuing down to the Spanish Arch . <p> " Climate change remains the greatest issue we as a society may ever face . However , the effects of climate change will widen already existing inequalities in society , " said event organiser Cian Mortimer . <p> " Climate justice demands that a human rights based approach is taken in climate action , and that the most vulnerable communities are placed at the heart of the solution . " <p> The groups behind the demonstration are demanding the Ireland achieve net-zero carbon emissions by 2030 , and " justice for climate refugees " . <p> Climate change is expected to be a major driver of international @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ resources , like water , becoming scarcer in many areas . <p> To ensure a " just transition " to a net-zero emissions economy , the demonstrators that there should be no general carbon tax , with taxes instead levied on corporations ; public transport should be free and green ; and small-scale , environmentally friendly farming should be promoted . <p> Part of the goal of this event is to highlight the role which junior transport minister , and former chair of the climate action committee , Galway TD Hildegarde Naughton can play in this transition Cian Mortimer said .
