<h> Time to reward farmers ' efforts <p> THROUGHOUT the Covid-19 pandemic so far , our farmers have continued -- in spite of obstacles such as rural isolation -- to produce food for the nation and this has become better appreciated by consumers , especially during the lockdown months when more people were at home and cooking for themselves . You can read about how things are for farmers and those in agri-business generally now in the midst of the pandemic , and how they hope to cope with other threats to their livelihoods , such as a no-deal Brexit , climate change and CAP reform , in our autumn West Cork Farming magazine in this week 's edition of The Southern Star . <p> Having kept the show on the road , so to speak , the efforts of our local farmers deserve to be recognised and , while some awards schemes have fallen by the wayside this year due to the pandemic , The Southern Star and the Celtic Ross Hotel felt it was important to keep their annual West Cork Farming Awards going , albeit in a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with the presentation ceremony to be live-streamed online in early November . <p> Our faithful sponsors have endorsed this decision by , unhesitatingly , giving the awards their backing once again , which we are most appreciative of .
