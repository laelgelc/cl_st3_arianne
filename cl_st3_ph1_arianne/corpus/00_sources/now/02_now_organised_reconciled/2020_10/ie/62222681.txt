High Speed 2 , the controversial ? 106billion railway project , has begun felling trees in Hampstead Road , outside the Cartmel block of homes on the Regent 's Park Estate , as part of its Euston station works . Residents suffered sleepless nights after being woken up by noise in the area between midnight and 4am last week . <p> " That 's the way HS2 works , " said Dr Maxey , who has a science PhD .
