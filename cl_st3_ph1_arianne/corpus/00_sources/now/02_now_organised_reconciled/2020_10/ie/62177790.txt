<h> Rapid diagnostic test for Botrytis launched <p> Mologic has launched a new rapid test for Botrytis . BotrytisAlert uses lateral flow technology to enable earlier disease intervention and prevent wide-spread crop failures . It is the company 's first crop pathogen diagnostic test . <p> According to Mologic , BotrytisAlert is a low-cost test which can be used to measure the pathogen Botrytis both in air and plant material . This enables growers to apply controlled measures ahead of symptom development and so prevents disease establishment and post-harvest rot . <p> Intervening early in the disease cycle allows for improved efficacy and more sustainable control measures , which in turn results in a reduced use of pesticides . <p> Botrytis can infect over 200 species of plants and can cause huge financial losses .
