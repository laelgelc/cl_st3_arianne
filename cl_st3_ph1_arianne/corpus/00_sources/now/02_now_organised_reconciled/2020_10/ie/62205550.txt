<h> Budget to benefit buyers of hybrid cars , says Toyota <p> Galway Advertiser , Thu , Oct 22 , 2020 <p> Ireland 's new motor tax system announced in the recent Budget will see new car buyers who opt for more sustainable vehicles , such as hybrid electrics and EVs , benefit with better value through lower VRT . Petrol and diesel cars with higher emissions will be more expensive . <p> One example of savings for lower emissions vehicles as an outcome of the Budget is a ? 360 VRT reduction on Ireland 's best-selling car , the Toyota Corolla self-charging hybrid . This saving is in addition to the two per cent VAT benefit that customers who purchase a new car before February 28 , 2021 , will receive . In the case of a Corolla hybrid , it adds up to ? 930 in tax savings overall . <p> As such , there is never been a better time to buy a hybrid and save tax , says Steve Tormey , CEO of Toyota Ireland , which predominantly sells hybrid cars . <p> " I welcome @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ proposed in today 's budget , " Tormey says .
