<h> PRI consults on new three-year strategy , real-world outcomes key <p> The Principles for Responsible Investment ( PRI ) has launched a consultation on a new three-year strategy , which it has billed as being about " building a bridge between financial risk and real-world outcomes " . <p> The draft strategy was discussed during the organisation 's signatory general meeting last week and is under consultation until 25 November . The final strategy is due to be launched on 1 April next year . <p> According to the consultation document , " supporting ESG incorporation " would remain core to the strategy , but the PRI would also " support the evolution of the industry " . <p> " Increasingly we see investors adding a third dimension to their thinking , building from a pure risk and return focus to also considering their role in driving real-world outcomes , " wrote Martin Skancke , chair of the PRI , and Fiona Reynolds , CEO . <p> " This trend is only being accelerated by COVID-19 , so over the coming years we will begin to build a bridge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the PRI 's website , Reynolds said the draft strategy was developed by the PRI taking the input and insight it gained from signatories and mapping this back to its 10-year " Blueprint for responsible investment " from three years ago . <p> Plans covered by the proposed three-year strategy include for the PRI to take its stewardship work into " two distinct , complementary areas " : co-ordinating large-scale collaborative engagements on systemic environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) issues , and supporting signatories ' own work on targeted , local issues . <p> Initiatives would include developing investors ' understanding of , and approach to , stewardship , " to prioritise outcomes over process , and universal goals over relative performance " , and collaborating with others to develop a " single global sustainability reporting system for investors and corporations " .
