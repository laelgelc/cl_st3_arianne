<h> IKEA to buy back unwanted furniture from customers to sell second hand in IKEA stores <h> We can see this offer proving quite popular . <p> Furniture giant IKEA has announced a new initiative in which it will ' Buy Back ' unwanted furniture from customers that it will then sell second hand in stores across the UK and Ireland . <p> The ' Buy Back ' initiative is due to launch just under a month before Christmas on 27 November , the day on which ' Black Friday ' falls this year . <p> As part of the initiative , customers selling back an item will receive an IKEA refund card to spend in store , which will have no expiry date in order to encourage customers to purchase items only when they 're really needed . <p> Depending on the condition of the furniture traded in , the voucher could be worth as much as 50% of the original price if deemed as good ' as new ' . Anything that can not be resold will be recycled or donated to local community projects . <p> Customers who wish to sell back their old IKEA furniture simply need to visit IKEA.co.uk and submit items for consideration by filling out a simple online offer request . <p> The tool will automatically generate a preliminary offer and customers will then be invited to bring the fully assembled product and preliminary offer to the Returns &amp; Exchanges desk in their nearest store , where they will then receive an IKEA refund card to spend in store . <p> IKEA says the initiative is just one way in which it is working towards its goal of becoming a fully circular and climate positive business by 2030 . <p> Commenting on the Buy Back initiative , Hege S ? bj ? rnsen , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : " The IKEA vision has always been to create a better everyday life for many people , which right now means making sustainable living easy and affordable for everyone .
