<h> UAESA signs deals to advance space cooperation <h> ABU DHABI , 2 days ago <p> The UAE Space Agency ( UAESA ) is amongst the first signatories of Nasa 's Artemis Accords at the 71st International Astronautical Congress ( IAC ) , running between October 12 and 14 , 2020 . <p> In May 2020 , Nasa , the American Space Agency , formally announced the Artemis Accords , based on a shared vision for principles , grounded in the United Nations ' Outer Space Treaty of 1967 , to create a safe and transparent environment that facilitates exploration , science and commercial activities for the benefit of all humanity . <p> In a virtual event hosted by Nasa , Sarah Al Amiri , Minister of State for Advanced Technology and Chair of the UAE Space Agency and Jim Bridenstine , Nasa Administrator along with the heads of the Space Agencies of Australia , Canada , Italy , Japan , Luxembourg and the United Kingdom . <p> The UAE is committed to the advancement of space exploration while ensuring the sustainability of space . With this mission the UAE will release the data to the global science community without an embargo period . <p> This aligns with the accords that emphasises the importance of data sharing across missions along with the interoperability of systems to drive space exploration . <p> Speaking on this partnership , Al Amiri said : " We welcome this initiative Nasa has taken to strengthen the broad principles of peaceful human exploration and co-existence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the UAE is pleased to become a signatory of the Artemis Accords , and our endorsement of this agreement is in keeping with our principle of the peaceful use and exploration of outer space to enhance development and sustainability goals here on Earth .
