<p> Apeel has announced US$30m in new funding from the International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) , Temasek , and Astanor Ventures . <p> The group has said that it will use its new funding and technology to help smallholder farmers in emerging markets , who suffer from some of the highest levels of food insecurity and waste , extend crop shelf-life and gain access to new markets , including the US and Germany . <p> " It 's a misconception that people go hungry because we do n't grow enough food , " said James Rogers , founder and CEO of Apeel . " The issue is the intermittency of supply and an inability to convert perishable assets into economic value . <p> " The harsh reality is that it is nearly impossible today for most smallholder farmers to get their produce to a marketplace that will pay for it before it spoils , " he continued .
