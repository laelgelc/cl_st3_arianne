<p> It took 15 years , nine attempts and the changing of many minds , but Ireland finally has a climate law that fully recognises the grim reality of global warming and requires fast and forceful action to address it . <p> The law is still a bill so it has to be passed by the Oireachtas but , with all three coalition party leaders jointly presenting it , that process should , barring unforeseen circumstances , be as close to a formality as any transformative legislation can hope for . <p> Unfortunately , everything else about the law was foreseen . <p> Unabated greenhouse gas emissions , global temperature rise , climate chaos , extreme weather and the knock-on effects on everything from the reliability of the growing seasons for providing the food we eat , to the replenishment of the rivers that fill our taps , to the ability of our homes to withstand furious floods and ferocious gales . <p> All of that was laid out in 2005 when the first attempt at enacting
