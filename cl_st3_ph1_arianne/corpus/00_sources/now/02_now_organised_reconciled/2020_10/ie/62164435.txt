<h> ETFs for ESG : The devil in the detail of ' low carbon ' ETFs <p> Sustainable market indices are nothing new . The Dow Jones Sustainability index was launched in 1999 and the FTSE4Good index in 2001 . <p> Fixed income investors can also get in on the act : the first green bond was launched in 2007 by the European Investment Bank , and indices followed in 2014 , driven by major World Bank issuance . <p> Index providers and fund managers have responded to investor demand by issuing ever more specific product offerings : there are now ETFs focusing on solar , women 's empowerment , clean energy and sustainable water . And , of course , low carbon . <h> Greenwashing <p> But embedding a sustainable investment strategy in an index is challenging .
