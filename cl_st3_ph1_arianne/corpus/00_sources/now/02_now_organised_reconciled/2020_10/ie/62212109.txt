For the past 10 years or so , more than half of the world 's population has lived in cities . Moreover , there 's no end in sight for this migration of people to urban areas . On the contrary , the latest UN forecast predicts that 70pc of the world 's population will be living in cities by 2050 . At that point , the world 's total urban population will be almost equal to the Earth 's entire population today . Within a mere century , the number of people living in big cities will have grown from 1bn to almost 6bn . <p> This trend will also lead to the rise of more and more megacities -- cities that have more than 10m inhabitants .
