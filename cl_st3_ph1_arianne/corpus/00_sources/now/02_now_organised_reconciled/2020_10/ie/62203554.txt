<h> Most frequently reported pressures for habitats and species ' stem from agriculture ' <p> The European Commission has published its latest assessment of the state of nature in the EU , showing that the most frequently reported pressures for habitats and species " stem from agriculture " . <p> The report provides a review of the situation of Europe 's most vulnerable species and habitats protected under EU nature laws . <p> Decline of protected habitats and species still continues , caused mostly by intensive agriculture , urbanisation , unsustainable forestry activities and changes to freshwater habitats , according to the report . <p> Pollution of air , water and soil also impacts habitats , as well as climate change , over-exploitation of animals through illegal harvesting and untenable hunting and fishing . <p> If not addressed , this decline will " inevitably result in the continued erosion of our biodiversity and the vital services it provides , putting human health and prosperity at risk " . <h> Most frequently reported pressures stem from agriculture <p> According to the report , the most frequently reported pressures for both habitats @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ relative scale of agricultural land use and changes in farming practices intensification and abandonment of extensive agriculture " . <p> The report states : <p> " Extensive agricultural management creates and maintains semi-natural habitats with diverse fauna and flora .
