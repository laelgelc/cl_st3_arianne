<p> The 2nd edition of this citywide art event will welcome international artists such as Anish Kapoor , Marina Abramovic , Bill Viola , Andres Serrano , and Yoko Ono . <p> The fate of the Bangkok Art Biennale ( BAB ) 2020 has been confirmed and , barring any unforeseen circumstances , will go ahead as planned -- starting on October 29th , 2020 and running until January 31st , 2021 . <p> The 2020 biennale will welcome to Thailand artists from 34 nations , who will exhibit alongside 31 Thai artists , with well-known local art scene figures such as Yuree Kensaku , Uttaporn Nimmalaikaew , and Nipan Oranniwesna scheduled to take part . The works will be showcased across multiple venues , such as the Bangkok Art and Culture Centre ( BACC ) , Museum Siam , BAB Box , The Parq , The Prelude , and Lhong 1919 .
