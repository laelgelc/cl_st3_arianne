Cynics immediately began to question his motives . <p> Was he trying to corner the vast market in renewables , was he trying to upstage climate-denying and coal-loving President Trump , was he trying to divert attention from internal human rights issues and Hong Kong , or from accusations against China over the Covid crisis ?
