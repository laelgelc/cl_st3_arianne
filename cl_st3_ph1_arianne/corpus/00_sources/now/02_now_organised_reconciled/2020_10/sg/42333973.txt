<h> Share <p> Somruedee Bunthonglek has not repaired the bullet holes in the pickup truck her husband was driving when he survived an assassination attempt barely a year after her father was shot dead on the same communal farm in southern Thailand . <p> The grey truck , with about half a dozen bullet holes on the driver 's side , is a reminder of the risks to the community that occupied about 237 acres of land in Klong Sai Pattana as an act of defiance after a palm oil plantation 's licence expired . <p> Four members of the Southern Peasants ' Federation of Thailand ( SPFT ) in Surat Thani province were killed between 2010 and 2015 , and many more sued and jailed since , in a more than decade-long fight for land and community titles . <p> " We worry about being attacked again , or being sued , but this is our land , and we are staying on to fight for it , " said Somruedee , 33 , adding that her husband left the community after he was attacked in 2016 , fearing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ land . So what choice do we have - this is all we have , " she told the Thomson Reuters Foundation . <p> No one was convicted for the attacks , which the villagers believe were carried out by gunmen hired by the local real estate mafia . The cases were closed for lack of sufficient evidence , police officials have said . <p> About a third of Thailand 's 69 million people depend on land for a living , but nearly 80 per cent of private land is formally owned by just about a fifth of the population , according to land rights groups who have long called for reforms . <p> Beginning in 1901 , various land laws were passed , including a 2010 regulation for community land titles under the Abhisit Vejjajiva government , and a 2014 legislation for communal plots . A community forest bill was unveiled last year . <p> Yet issuance of community titles has been slow , and the farmers do not get ownership rights . <p> " Many governments have said they would allocate land to the landless poor , but @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ land or where licences had expired , " said Pratheep Rakhanthong , a community leader of SPFT , which was formed in 2008 . <p> " Sustainability of livelihoods and lives is only possible with a title , and when the right to own and manage the land belongs to the community , " he added . <p> The Agricultural Land Reform Office ( ALRO ) , a government agency , initially allowed the villagers to take over the land in Klong Sai Pattana as a pilot for community farming , then asked them to leave , Pratheep said . <p> The ALRO did not respond to requests for a comment . <h> Root cause <p> Land rights groups in Thailand estimate that there are more than 8 million landless people in the country . <p> Under the 2010 regulation , 50 communities were promised collective rights . But few such titles have been awarded , according to the department of land . <p> While the 2007 Thai Constitution gives people the right to participate in managing natural resources and the environment , evictions from forests and farmland have risen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protests nationwide . <p> The government adopted a Western land code in 1901 that was based on private property ownership , and subsequent land laws have been in the same vein , said Jessica Vechbanyongratana , an assistant professor of economics at Chulalongkorn University . <p> " It leaves little space for alternative ownership arrangements , including community land rights , " she said . <p> " Community activists have managed to bring about small changes that have resulted in greater land rights for some groups of people , but there has not been a complete overhaul of the system , " she said . <p> It is unlikely to be a priority now , she added , as authorities focus on reviving the economy after the coronavirus . <p> SPFT advocates a progressive land tax , a national land bank to support landless peasants , and the protection of agricultural lands for farmers . <p> It joined other land rights groups in protests in 2018 , when authorities committed to speeding up community land titles . <p> Now the groups back ongoing anti-government protests led by students across the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ realise farmers are struggling , and that we need to amend the constitution and the land laws , " said Pienrat Boonrit , president of SPFT in Phoem Sap community in Surat Thani , which has occupied 267 acres of abandoned land . <p> " The system does not address the root cause of the problem , which is the unequal land distribution .
