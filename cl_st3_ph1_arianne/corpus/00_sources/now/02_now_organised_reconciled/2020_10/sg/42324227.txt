In the past three years , there has been little progress on the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor ( CPEC ) , touted by both Beijing and Islamabad as the cornerstone of friendship between the two countries . <p> During the landmark visit of Chinese President Xi Jinping to Pakistan in 2015 , China pledged USD 46 billion for a range of energy and infrastructure projects .
