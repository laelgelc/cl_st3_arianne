<p> BUENOS AIRES ( Reuters ) - Climate change and human activity are harming Antarctica and threatening wildlife from humpback whales to microscopic algae , more than 280 scientists and conservation experts say in urging protections for the icy region . <p> The coalition - all women - called for creating a new marine protection area around Antarctica , as governments on Monday began a two-week meeting of the Commission for the Conservation of Antarctic Marine Living Resources . <p> Two Antarctica areas are already protected : The South Orkney Islands and the Ross Sea . The new protection area , proposed in 2018 by commission members Chile and Argentina , would cover the western Antarctic Peninsula , the northernmost part of the southernmost continent . <p> The peninsula 's complex ecosystem includes penguins , seals and tiny crustaceans called krill , which are the main staple for hundreds of marine animals , including fish , birds and baleen whales . <p> Unlike with the rest of Antarctica , one-third of which remains unvisited , there are no large areas of the peninsula untouched by humans .
