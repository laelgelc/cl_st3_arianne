disaster-prone region that often comes under threats from floods to typhoons . <p> It started when its co-founder Heng Li Seng , who runs a social enterprise in Singapore on reducing waste and coastal cleanups since 2017 , began to receive growing number of enquiries about sustainability-related jobs . <p> When Singapore 's trade-reliant economy plunged into a recession in July , forcing some
