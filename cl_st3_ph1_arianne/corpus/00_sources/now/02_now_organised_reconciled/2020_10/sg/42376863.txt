<h> Share <p> India will surpass its commitments on greenhouse gas emission checks and energy transition to renewables made under the 2015 Paris climate pact 10 years ahead of time , making it a global leader in climate action . <p> " We had pledged to reduce emissions intensity by 33-35 per cent by the year 2030 and have already achieved 21 per cent , " India 's Environment Minister Harsh Vardhan said at the annual climate summit being held in Katowice , Poland . If the reductions were to include the food and agriculture sector , the reduction is around 26 per cent , the minister said . <p> India 's climate action plan under the Paris Agreement promised that it would increase the share of renewables to 40 per cent of its power generation capacity , reduce its economy 's emission intensity by 33-35 per cent by 2030 from levels seen in 2005 , sequester an additional 2.5-3 billion tonnes of carbon dioxide through additional forest cover . <h> " <p> Right now India 's goal is to see that whatever we have promised , we achieve it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , India <p> The goal of adding to the forest cover is the one lagging behind schedule . India 's forest cover has increased by one percentage point in the past one year , Harsh Vardhan said at the December 2-14 summit . <p> The minister said being on the road to achieve two of the three main goals does not necessarily mean India will submit a more ambitious pledge by 2020 . " Right now India 's goal is to see that whatever we have promised , we achieve it well before time . " <h> Second biennial update <p> India is gearing up to submit its second biennial update to the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , stating that the country is on track to achieve its Paris pledges . The report will be released in New Delhi after the Katowice climate summit gets over in December 14 , an environment ministry official told indiaclimatedialogue.net in Katowice . The report is ready , but needs formal clearance from the union cabinet .
