<p> The Archdiocese of Rio de Janeiro has launched a partnership with a Canadian company to reduce plastic disposal into the oceans . <p> Church officials said the venture with Plastic Bank offers a more sustainable way to rid the oceans of dangerous plastic waste and falls in line with Pope Francis ' plea to protect the earth in his encyclical , " Laudato Si ' , on Care for Our Common Home . " <p> " This year in which we celebrate the fifth anniversary of the publication of ' Laudato Si ' , we are happy to announce this partnership , which is yet another concrete gesture from our archdiocese that engages in the care of the common home , " said Cardinal Orani Joao Tempesta of Rio de Janeiro . <p> Called the Program of Faith , the project launched Oct. 4 , the feast of St. Francis of Assisi .
