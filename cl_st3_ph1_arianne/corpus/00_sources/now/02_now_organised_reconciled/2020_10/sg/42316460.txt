<h> Newsletters <h> Newsletters <p> A national research centre of the Nanyang Technological University ( NTU ) , the NTU Centre for Contemporary Art Singapore ( NTU CCA Singapore ) , which is supported by the Economic Development Board ( EDB ) , has played a vital role in building the region 's vibrant arts scene since its inauguration in 2013 . Known for its enriching exhibitions , residencies as well as research-led programmes , the centre has given many artists a platform to push artistic boundaries , challenge the creative status quo and explore issues in the Southeast Asian contemporary art scene . <p> But come March next year , NTU CCA Singapore 's exhibition space and residency studios in Gillman Barracks will close , when funding from EDB ceases . However , its research centre will remain open , channelling its efforts on two ongoing projects : the digitalisation of the centre 's archives and the production of Climates . Habitats .
