<p> Annika , along with her younger brother Michael and cousin Addison , joined gramma Lynn Sharkey as part of a project to help grow trees in the area . <p> A total of 20 grandparents and grandchildren met at the end of Main Street East near Chippewa Creek to take part in an initiative coordinated by the North Bay-Mattawa Conservation Authority , Trees for Nipissing , Living Fit Inside Out , along with funding from TD Friends of the Environment Foundation . <p> The partnership is collectively hoping to plant about 900 seedlings ( 16 species ) in three nursery beds on the North Bay-Mattawa Conservation Authority property . <p> Troy Storms , manager of lands and stewardship with the conservation authority believes this event is important for many reasons . <p> " Collaboration , getting the community out , getting outside , I think that is important , " said Storms . <p> " But the environmental aspect is important so we are working hard to combat climate change and we are improving the air and water quality along rivers , lakes , and streams .
