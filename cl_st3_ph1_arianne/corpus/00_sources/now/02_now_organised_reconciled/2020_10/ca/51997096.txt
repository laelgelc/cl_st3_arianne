<p> TORONTO -- Ontario will not appeal a court ruling against its anti-carbon tax stickers , the government said Thursday , putting an end to an 18-month political and legal battle between Ottawa and the province . <p> Last month , a Superior Court judge struck down a law that forced Ontario gas stations to display the stickers , calling it unconstitutional . Energy Minister Greg Rickford said the province will abide by that decision . <p> " We stand by our position that Ontarians deserve to know the true cost of the federal carbon tax , " Rickford said in a statement . " Right now , however , our sole focus is protecting the health and well-being of the people of Ontario as we continue to battle COVID-19 . " <p> Justice Edward Morgan ruled in early September that Premier Doug Ford and his Progressive Conservative government overstepped in mandating the stickers , saying the Federal Carbon Tax Transparency Act could not be justified under the charter . <p> " A government or political party can , in the words of Ontario 's Minister of Energy , ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ party as a matter of free speech in an election campaign or otherwise , " Morgan wrote .
