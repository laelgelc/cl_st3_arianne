It 's encompasses all the values and myths we hold holy -- seemingly pollution-free stewardship of the land , green vistas of vibrant crops , and contented animals munching grass . <p> If only the realities and economics of small farming were so engaging . <p> The vast scope and power of corporate agriculture presents ferocious competition ; the aspiring small farmer will not find much here to help make the venture profitable . <p> " A Small Farm Future " joins a barnful of books and articles in recent years on small farming , a romance with the land that has eluded profitability . <p> However , several factors may hasten Smaje 's farm revolution , at least in the United States : <p> -- Climate change , which will render some of our current farmlands too hot , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water table in California 's central and Salinas valleys , where most of America 's salads originate in industrial-scale farms . <p> -- Washing away of the topsoil in the Great Plains , the result of corn and soybean monocultures and failure to plant cover crops , such as clover , in the winter to hold the soil in place when it rains . <p> -- In California , a failure of the winter rains , and conversely , deluges in the central states , surely will elevate the urgency of an alternative agriculture discussion . <p> If those factors are not enough to ignite a shift to more sustainable small farms , consider this statistic : Federal payments to farmers are expected to reach a record $46 billion this year , the New York Times reported earlier this month .
