The grassroots , non-partisan project -- the HRM 4 Kids Children and the City Questionnaire -- invited comments from candidates in four areas : <p> critical issues affecting children ; <p> proposed approaches to working with communities , city staff and council to address the issues ; <p> The questionnaire was delivered to 83 of the 86 individuals listed on the Official Candidates -- 2020 Municipal and CSAP Elections page . The incumbent District 16 councillor is the only candidate who will be acclaimed in this election . <p> Of 11 incumbents reoffering for council , five ( or 45 per cent ) , responded -- a lower participation rate than for the other 69 candidates .
