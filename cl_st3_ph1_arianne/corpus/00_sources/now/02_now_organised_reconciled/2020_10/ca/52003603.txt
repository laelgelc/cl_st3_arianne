<h> Climate Monitors Say 2020 Ozone Hole One of ' Largest and Deepest ' in Recent Years <p> Scientists with the Copernicus Climate Change Service ( C3S ) said on October 6 that the 2020 ozone hole had reached its maximum size , and was " one of the larger and deeper " holes of recent years . <p> The hole had grown to 23 million square kilometers in size , over twice the size of the US . The hole is a consequence of the use of ozone-depleting chemicals , particularly during the 20th century . An international treaty , the Montreal Protocol , was adopted in 1987 to regulate the production and consumption of nearly 100 man-made ozone-depleting chemicals . <p> Since its adoption , the ozone hole has decreased in size . " Since the ban on halocarbons , the ozone layer has slowly been recovering ; the data clearly show a trend in decreasing area of the ozone hole , " said C3S . <p> Speaking to Mashable , Paul Newman , the chief scientist in the Earth Sciences Division at NASA 's Goddard Space Flight @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ good thing .
