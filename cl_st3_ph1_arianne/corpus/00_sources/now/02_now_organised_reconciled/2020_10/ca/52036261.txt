<h> Global-scale teenage angst <h> Documentary gets close-up look of focused young woman taking world leaders to task over climate-change inaction <p> I Am Greta provides a thorough record of how Greta Thunberg became the face of the global climate-change movement , while also capturing surprising and endearing moments from her life away from the spotlight . <p> It 's easy to forget that Greta Thunberg is just a kid . <p> The teenage climate activist with a penchant for holding world leaders to account has become a household name , but media coverage of her global movement has so far failed to capture her true personality . Nathan Grossman 's 2020 documentary , I Am Greta , offers an illuminating reintroduction . <p> Grossman , a little-known Swedish filmmaker , reportedly had no intention of making a feature-length film about Thunberg when he started documenting her school strike for climate in August 2018 .
