These items , which include plastic straws and cutlery , disposable beverage cups , and plastic bags , have a negative impact on the environment . <p> The strategy outlines recommendations in seven areas for the corporation . The recommendations are accompanied by tips to help staff take action in these areas . <p> To raise awareness among county staff and to show their corporate commitment to sustainability , they have agreed to eliminating the unnecessary use of single-use items from operations and services , replacing single-use items with reusable/recyclable alternatives when feasible , and when unavoidable , doing their best to minimize the use of items and ensure proper disposal . <p> Acknowledging that this is not yet a mandate from the federal government , they agreed during the regular council meeting on Oct. 7 that now was a good time to start getting used to the idea , as it will become law at some point soon . <p> Changes that will happen include purchasing reusable cutlery and dishware to use in kitchens and lunchrooms or bringing your own to work .
