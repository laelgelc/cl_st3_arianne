<p> NEWS RELEASE NATURE CONSERVANCY OF CANADA **25;1116;TOOLONG The Nature Conservancy of Canada ( NCC ) announced today the protection of 32 hectares ( 80 acres ) in the globally important Minesing Wetlands , just 15 kilometres west of Barrie . <p> The newly created Baldwick Bluff Nature Reserve is a collection of forests , fields , wetlands and headwater streams surrounding the core of the Minesing Wetlands . <p> This area acts as a protective buffer to the main wetlands , ensuring that wildlife have the room they need to thrive . It helps control floods and provides clean water for nearby communities . <p> The area also provides important habitat for species at risk , including eastern meadowlark ( threatened ) , bobolink ( threatened ) , and Hine 's emerald dragonfly ( endangered ) , which is found only in the Minesing Wetlands in Canada . <p> The Minesing Wetlands play an important role in filtering the water that is carried by the Nottawasaga River into Georgian Bay , and that flows out of thousands of taps in nearby homes . With a rapidly changing climate , protecting large , intact wetlands is vitally important to the health and resiliency of local communities . <p> Comprising approximately 11,000 hectares ( 27,000 acres ) of wetlands , fields and forests , the Minesing Wetlands are often referred to as the " Everglades of the North " due to their designation as a Ramsar Wetland of International Importance -- a distinction they share with their Floridian counterpart . <p> Over 70 per cent of southern Ontario 's wetlands have been converted to alternative uses since European settlement . The province 's remaining wetlands are threatened by non-native invasive plant species , pollution and habitat fragmentation . <p> For more than 45 years , NCC and its partners , like the Nottawasaga Valley Conservation Authority ( NVCA ) , have been working to protect the Minesing Wetlands and deliver restoration and stewardship programs , including reforestation , stream bank stabilization , erosion control and nutrient management projects , and invasive species control .
