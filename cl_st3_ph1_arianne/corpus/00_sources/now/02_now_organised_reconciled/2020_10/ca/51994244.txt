Eco-Seed Co-op , a co-operative of roughly a dozen seed farmers in the province , saw its sales skyrocket , signalling a major shift in where British Columbians get their seeds -- and who grows them . <p> Most Canadian vegetable seed is imported , with roughly $128 million worth entering the country last year from as far afield as Tanzania . It 's a long supply chain , one COVID-19 hit hard as companies raced to meet soaring demand and forcing at least one U.S. seed company to stop shipping to Canada . <p> That left farmers and North America 's surge of pandemic gardeners scrambling for seeds -- a rush B.C. seed growers strived to meet . <p> B.C. is Canada 's largest consumer of organic vegetable seed , with the province 's farmers and gardeners spending about $ 8 million a year on seed .
