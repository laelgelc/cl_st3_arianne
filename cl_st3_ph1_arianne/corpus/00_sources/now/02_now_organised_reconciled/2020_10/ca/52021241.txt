The United Nations forecasts that Canada 's greenhouse gas emissions will be at least 15 per cent higher than its 2030 target . <p> And even if Canada and other countries meet their targets , the United Nations forecasts that world temperature will still rise by 3 to 3.5 C by the end of this century , more than twice the Paris target of 1.5 degrees . Clearly , stronger measures to address climate change are imperative . <p> While the government 's commitment to stronger climate action is therefore laudable , its actions raise serious doubts about its sincerity .
