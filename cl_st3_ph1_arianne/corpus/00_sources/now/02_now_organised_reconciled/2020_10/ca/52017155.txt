<h> City of Powell River receives climate action award <h> Community recognized for its work in corporate strategy <p> GIVEN RECOGNITION : City of Powell River was given an award for its overall corporate climate action strategy and climate action in a small coastal town . At the recent awards presentation were from left councillor CaroleAnn Leishman , chair of the city 's climate change mitigation and adaptation committee , Dale Littlejohn , executive director of the Community Energy Association , who presented the award and sustainability planner Ana Lukyanova .
