GHGSat unveiled Pulse on Wednesday , an interactive map available for free online with a resolution of approximately two kilometres by two kilometres.THE CANADIAN PRESS/HO-GHGSat ** <p> MONTREAL -- A Montreal company has unveiled what it boasts is the most accurate map of global methane concentrations ever made . <p> On Wednesday , GHGSat unveiled Pulse , an interactive map available for free online that has a resolution of approximately two kilometres by two kilometres . <p> GHGSat produced the map from data generated by the company 's satellites along with information from partners such as the European Space Agency . A paid version of the map with a resolution of 25 metres by 25 metres is also available . <p> Methane accounts for a quarter of all man-made greenhouse gas emissions . The map shows the evolution of methane concentrations over the last six months . <p> " The concentrations shown are in the atmosphere -- not just at ground level , " Germain said during his presentation . " The concentrations in one region therefore may be due to emissions in another region .
