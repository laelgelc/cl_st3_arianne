<h> Canada , Britain pledge to protect 30% of their land and seas <p> On Monday , Canada and Britain joined a coalition of countries that have promised to protect 30 percent of their land and seas by 2030 to stem " catastrophic " biodiversity loss . The pledges come about as the UN Summit on Biodiversity is set to begin . <p> Our planet 's human inhabitants are intimately linked , and depend , on biodiversity - and this partnership between humans and nature is essential to the " circle of life " as we know it . On September 30 , in New York , the United Nations Summit on Biodiversity will bring together heads of state and other government representatives under the theme of " Urgent action on biodiversity for sustainable development . " <p> Urgent action is required if the nations of the world look at all the unmet promises that governments made 10 years ago to protect and restore nature .
