<h> Throne speech laid down markers for a clean and caring economy <p> A key goal laid out in the federal government 's recent speech from the throne was to " build back better to create a stronger , more resilient Canada . " In our view , " building back better " must include placing growing clean industries ( such as electric vehicle manufacturing and zero-carbon power generation ) at the centre of Canada 's industrial policy . At the same time , our social contract must be rejuvenated to take care of our young and old with affordable and accessible childcare and long-term care . The sketch provided by the throne speech suggests the government is on the right track -- but it did not explain how we will be able to afford the significant investments needed to make the vision into a reality .
