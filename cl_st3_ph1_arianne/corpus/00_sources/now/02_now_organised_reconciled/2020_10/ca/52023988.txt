The trans-oceanic voyage from the old world to the new was once a dangerous and uncomfortable undertaking , before becoming routine and even opulent in the last century and then , with the advent of air travel , rare and frightening once more . <p> Thunberg made the trip in 2019 to avoid the charge of hypocrisy -- why talk about saving the planet if you 're going to travel by jet to do it ? -- but also to show that it was both possible and yet , for most travellers , unthinkable .
