<h> Short-term pain could mean long-term gain for Erin O'Toole <p> This has been an eventful week for climate change in Canadian politics . <p> First , the new Green party leader , Annamie Paul , announced that her party would not vote in favour of the throne speech , because " the Liberal government is also failing to demonstrate national and international leadership on climate change . " <p> Then the Alberta government announced the province will focus on natural gas production , plastics recycling , and " blue hydrogen that is generated from natural gas and has a lower carbon footprint . " <p> And a day after that , the federal government announced a plan to expand its reduction of single-use plastics . <p> The federal Conservative party under its new leader , Erin O'Toole , could finally have a window of opportunity in which to develop a climate change plan .
