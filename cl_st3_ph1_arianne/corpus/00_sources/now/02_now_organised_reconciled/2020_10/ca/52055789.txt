Such climate-driven events increasingly affect inflation , economic growth and financial stability . <p> European Central Bank President Christine Lagarde this month indicated that policymakers were up for the task , saying the ECB may need to address climate risks via its stimulus programmes and other policies . <p> Here is why central banks are getting involved and what they can do : <h> 1/WHY CENTRAL BANKS ? <p> The Network for Greening the Financial System ( NGFS ) , a group comprising 74 central banks here and regulators including all the largest ones with the exception of the U.S. Federal Reserve , argues that climate change , as a source of financial @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Banks and financial companies that lend against or insure assets such as buildings in Italy 's lagoon city Venice or oil refineries could face major future losses .
