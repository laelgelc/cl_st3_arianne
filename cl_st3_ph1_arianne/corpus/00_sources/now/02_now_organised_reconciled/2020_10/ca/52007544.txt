<h> MD of Taber to receive $13M for flood mitigation projects <p> EDMONTON , AB . -- The Municipal District of Taber is in line for nearly $13 million to improve the Horsefly Regional Emergency Spillway to protect the region against overland flooding . <p> Officials say upgrades to the Horsefly spillway , from the main canal of the St. Mary River Irrigation District ( SMRID ) to the Oldman River , are the best way to increase the flood reduction capacity of the regional system . <p> The project will " protect water and wastewater facilities and transportation and other public infrastructure from repeated damage due to overland flooding , " said Minister of Environment and Parks Jason Nixon . <p> The flood mitigation project in Taber is one of 10 flood mitigation projects across the province announced today by Nixon , totalling $45 million . <p> " Some of these projects will address longstanding issues that have disrupted and damaged communities in recent years while others will protect against flooding risks that thankfully have not materialized yet , " said Nixon at the announcement in Edmonton . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ address risks posed by the North Saskatchewan River and the Cooperative Stormwater Management Initiative project in Rocky View County east of Calgary . <p> The government is also providing $4.5 million to the Municipal Climate Change Action Centre ( MCCAC ) for a new Climate Adaptation Program to help municipalities and Indigenous communities in Alberta better understand , manage and adjust to a changing climate . The MCCAC is a partnership between the Alberta Urban Municipalities Association , Rural Municipalities of Alberta 's and the province .
