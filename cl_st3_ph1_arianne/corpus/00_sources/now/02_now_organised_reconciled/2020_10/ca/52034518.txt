<h> Little change on Municipality of Pictou County council <p> Robert Parker , who served as warden , and Wayne Murray , who served as deputy warden for the Municipality of Pictou County , are both returning to council . Murray was one of six council members who won by acclamation. - File <p> PICTOU , N.S. -- <p> There 's only going to be one new face out of 12 on the Municipality of Pictou County council following the Oct. 17 election . <p> Mary Wooldridge-Elliott is the only new councillor . She was acclaimed for District 4 . The seat was previously held by Ron Baillie , who decided not to run this election . <p> Councillors this term will be : <p> District 1 : Don Butler ( acclaimed ) <p> District 2 : Deborah Wadden ( acclaimed ) <p> District 3 : Darla MacKeil <p> District 4 : Mary Wooldridge-Elliot ( acclaimed ) <p> District 5 : Wayne Murray ( acclaimed ) <p> District 6 : Robert Parker <p> District 7 : David Parker ( acclaimed ) <p> District 8 : Larry Turner <p> District 9 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Randy Palmer <p> District 11 : Andy Thompson <p> District 12 : Chester Dewar <p> In the Municipality of Pictou County , the warden is chosen by the council members . Robert Parker was warden for the last four years and said if he has the support of council , he will offer for the job again . <p> He said the fact that voters returned the members of council to their seats is a good sign . <p> " I think it puts some degree of confidence in council and the work we 're doing . " <p> The No. 1 priority for the council will be continuing the work they 've started to bring high-speed internet to the county . The county is putting in the infrastructure , which it then plans to lease to internet service providers for the benefit of residents . <p> " That 's the big one to carry through and deliver on , " Parker said . <p> He said council will be carefully watching the financial end of the project as they move forward . The county was unsuccessful in getting provincial @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ federal grants . He believes the project will help bring more financial growth to the county because so many businesses require high-speed internet . <p> In addition to that , he said council will be tackling some projects related to climate change and recreation . <p> He 'd also like to see the county continue to work with the local Regional Enterprise Network to help generate economic growth in the region . <p> During the last four years , the county saw a great debate between environmental and economic priorities centred around the issue of Northern Pulp and its proposal to put an effluent pipe into the Northumberland Strait . He said that maintaining a balance between the environment and jobs is never easy , but something that residents expect council to help make happen .
