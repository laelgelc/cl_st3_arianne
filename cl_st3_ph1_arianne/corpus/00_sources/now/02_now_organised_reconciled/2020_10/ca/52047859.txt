Tocilizumab blocks one of these cytokines , so was considered a potential way to prevent the storm , and some early studies suggested it might be effective . But a more thorough study found it has no effect . <p> A mathematical algorithm that can screen large groups of people for COVID-19 could be around 20 times cheaper than individual testing . In this method , tiny quantities taken from individual swabs are mixed to create combined samples and then tested . If this initial test finds that the mixed sample contains positive cases , the algorithm is able to pinpoint individual positive swab results within the combined sample .
