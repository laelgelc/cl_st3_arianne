<h> OP-ED : Hear her voice <p> The theme for this year 's International Day of the Girl is " My voice , our equal future , " under which the Unicef has called on the nations of the world to " seize the opportunity to re-imagine a better world inspired by adolescent girls -- energized and recognized , counted and invested in . " <p> For Bangladesh , October 11 , the International Day of the Girl , brings the occasion to focus on the state of the country 's girl children , how far we have come , and how far we are yet to go to fulfill the rights , aspirations , and potential of our girl children . <p> According to the definition of the World Health Organization , those who are 10-19 years old are adolescents . Bangladesh has 34 million adolescents , which constitutes 21% of its population . <p> Our country has made significant progresses in improving the lives of its adolescent population , particularly in terms of reducing maternal and child deaths and education , but there still remain a number of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 5 , and 8 , which are directly related to the well-being and development of adolescent population . <p> Incidences of child marriage , teenage pregnancy , child mortality due to teenage pregnancy , abuse and violence of teenage wives by husbands and in-laws , school dropouts , and malnutrition are high among adolescent girls in Bangladesh . <p> A 2017 report of Gender and Adolescence : Global Evidence ( GAGE ) , an international research effort , observed in Bangladesh : " Of the women aged between 20 and 24 years , as many as 53% are married before the age of 18 . Despite significant recent progress in reducing child marriage , especially for the youngest girls , 16% of girls are married by the age of 15 and 59% are married by the age of 18 . Nearly 90% of adolescent girls experience sexual harassment . Of married girls , over 40% experienced physical violence and nearly 25% experienced sexual violence at the hands of their husbands . " <p> The Bangladesh government , along with its national and international development partners , and to some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ advance the multidimensional development of its adolescent population . The National Strategy for Adolescent Health 2017-20 is a most significant instrument in this regard , giving direction to the right path under the lead of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare . <p> The National Action Plan on Violence Against Women is another key initiative , led by the Ministry of Women and Children Affairs , implementing major interventions to protect the rights of the girl child . <p> The UN convention on the Rights of the Child , ratified by Bangladesh , provides the primary policy direction to any nation of the world that has signed it . While Article 19 of this Convention makes it binding for the state parties to implement laws to protect children against all forms of physical and psychological abuse and violence , its other articles provide directions to fulfill their other rights , making sure that nations adopt a holistic approach towards achieving the well-being and development of children . <p> These articles of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child include , but are not limited to , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for children in vulnerable situations to be especially protected ( Articles 20 , 22 , and 23 ) , the right to health ( Article 24 ) , the right to be protected from dangerous work ( Article 32 ) , the right to be free from sexual and other forms of exploitation ( Articles 34 , 35 , and 36 ) , and the right to be protected from torture and ill treatment ( Article 37 ) . <p> We all know that there is no alternative to education which also includes life skills to empower girl children . While quality education creates space for freedom , helping them avoid child marriage and ensuing exploitation , other opportunities and facilities should also be offered to help them realize their full potential . Robust strategies and plans for protection of girl children is a need of the time for Bangladesh to also tackle the heightening risks of natural disasters caused by climate change as well as man-made disasters such as the situation of Rohingya refugees who have taken shelter in Bangladesh after facing extreme levels of violence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ champion initiatives that would create safe spaces for girl children 's education and life skills , and inspire them to participate as active agents in self-development and community-building as well . <p> Girl children carry enormous potential . It is the family , community , and state that often fail them . Therefore , it is the state , community , and family that have to come forward to create the space for them to unfold their potential for the fulfillment of their own aspiration and the well-being of their family and community .
