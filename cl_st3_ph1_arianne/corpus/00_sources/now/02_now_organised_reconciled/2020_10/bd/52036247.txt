<p> To further augment scope of foreign investment in the country , 11 more services of the Bangladesh Economic Zones Authority ( BEZA ) One Stop Service ( OSS ) Centre have come under online for ensuring quicker services for the investors of the Special Economic Zones ( SEZs ) . <p> Prime Minister 's Principal Secretary Dr Ahmad Kaikaus on Thursday inaugurated the services as the chief guest at a function at Sonargaon Hotel in the city while Executive Chairman of the Bangladesh Investment Development Authority ( BIDA ) Md Sirazul Islam , Land Secretary Md Maksudur Rahman , Environment , Forest and Climate Change Secretary Ziaul Hasan and Bangladesh Garment Manufacturers and Exporters Association ( BGMEA ) President Dr Rubana Hoque attended the function as special guests . <p> BEZA Executive Chairman Paban Chowdhury presided over the function . <p> In his speech , Ahmad Kaikaus said the present government is determined to build Bangladesh as a developed and prosperous nation by 2041 . <p> " For this , the government is working relentlessly to raise the per capita income to 12 thousand US dollars . We are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Only by increasing the quality of services , we can bring about a positive change in the position of the Ease of Doing Business index and achieve the target of attracting investment , " he added . <p> Paban Chowdhury said BEZA OSS is ensuring one-stop service to investors not only on paper but also in real sense and will continue to do so in the future . <p> He further said that even during the Covid-19 period , all kinds of services were being provided from BEZA OSS Center which has led to new investment proposals . <p> He hoped that the investment will be further enhanced through the added new services . <p> BEZA is now providing a total of 125 services under OSS centre . Out of the services , investors are now getting 32 services through online .
