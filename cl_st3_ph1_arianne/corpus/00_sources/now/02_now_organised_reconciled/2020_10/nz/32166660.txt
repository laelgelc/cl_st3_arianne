<h> What your targeted rates have preserved over the past year <p> The last long weekend of the year is fast approaching . And as the warmer days draw longer and Aucklanders dust off their summer gear to head outside , the city has plenty on offer . Hikers will get to enjoy more open kauri-safe tracks and be able to experience greater biodiversity which has been protected by the targeted rate . <p> " Auckland has a world-class natural environment , with beaches , harbours , forests and coastlines that are much loved by locals and visitors , " says Mayor Phil Goff . <p> " In a year where COVID-19 has caused economic disruption and uncertainty , we remain committed to cleaning up and protecting our ecosystems so they can be enjoyed by future generations . <p> " We are investing $763 million over 10 years to restore our natural environment and clean up our waterways . This record investment is delivering results . <p> " We 've reopened 60 kilometres of tracks in our regional and local parks after making them safe from kauri dieback disease , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Waitakere Ranges before Christmas . <p> " We 've undertaken animal pest control on more than 88,000 hectares of our mainland ecosystems to protect our precious native species , and our biosecurity team is working hard to target pests and keep our island sanctuaries safe . <p> " We 're improving water quality across the region too . Over the past year we 've built 1.8 kilometres of pipes to reduce overflows into the inner city harbour . Meanwhile 15 kilometres of storm and wastewater networks have been inspected across Auckland and 108 stormwater outlets have been screened by our Safe Networks teams to help make beaches more swimmable . <p> " This critical investment is made possible by the water quality and natural environment targeted rates , and I want to thank Aucklanders for supporting our region 's future .
