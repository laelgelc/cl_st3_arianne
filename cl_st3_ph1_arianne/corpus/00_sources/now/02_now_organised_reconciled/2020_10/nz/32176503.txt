<h> Carbon emissions focus of summit <p> Six days of talks , workshops and activities start tonight in Wanaka as part of the WAO 2020 Reset Summit co-organised by Monique Kelly . PHOTO : KERRIE WATERWORTH <p> A Wanaka grassroots organisation is hoping its 2020 Reset Summit will give businesses the " how " in how they can reduce their carbon emissions in the future . <p> We Are One ( WAO ) co-founder Monique Kelly said one of the main objectives of the six-day summit of talks , workshops and activities was to get businesses to understand about emissions , and their water and land footprints . <p> " We know builders are wanting to change . We surveyed members of our Building Better working group last year and 100% said they knew they had a problem with waste , mental health and improving the building code to make more energy efficient homes . " <p> Ms Kelly said the construction industry workshop on waste management was designed to make it easy for builders to attend . <p> It was being held on a construction site , with experts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to identify reuse and recycling opportunities and breakfast would be included . <p> Other summit talks and workshops included how to estimate your household or business emissions , a tour of high country stations to look at riparian planting , water monitoring and soil health and a presentation on digital tourism . <p> The summit opens tonight with a " conversation " on " Where To From Here ? " <p> Dr Carly Green and Dr Jim Salinger , both lead authors for the United National Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , are the guest speakers .
