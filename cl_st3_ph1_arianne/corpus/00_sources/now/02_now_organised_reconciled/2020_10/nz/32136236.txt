<p> News from WCC New parking racks for e-scooters will start being installed around central Wellington from next week to provide spaces where scooters can be left safely out of the way of pedestrians . <p> The Wellington City Council is installing the racks mainly to accommodate the public share e-scooters at the 10 busiest places where people start or end their rides . People who have their own e-scooters or push scooters can also use them . <p> The racks are known as Ruru racks as they resemble an owl 's face . <p> Deputy Mayor Sarah Free says the e-scooter parking will help to keep footpaths clear for pedestrians and provide much needed space for people to park scooters responsibly . <p> " We know that e-scooters are well used for making short trips around the city , but the convenience has to be balanced by people being considerate as to where they leave scooters at the end of their ride . <p> " The new racks will give scooter riders more options for where they can park safely , and it 's good to find a solution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on how well they are used and install more or relocate racks if there 's demand in some areas and not others .
