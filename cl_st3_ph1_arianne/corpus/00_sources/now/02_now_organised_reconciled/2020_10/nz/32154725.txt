The number of acutely hungry people in the world could increase by more than 100 million this year , according to WFP estimates . Every year , WFP increases the amount of food it procures locally from smallholder farmers , providing training in post-harvest storage and in how to access markets . The aim is to build dynamic food systems which contribute to community-based agricultural growth and the strengthening of national economies . <p> The need for concerted action to improve agricultural production while enhancing global supply chains and ending food waste is reflected in this year 's World Food Day theme : " Grow , Nourish , Sustain .
