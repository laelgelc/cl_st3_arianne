<p> Farmers are passionate about being good stewards of their land and want to do the right thing . However , the scale and pace of new government regulations is impacting the financial viability of farming , affecting farmers ' confidence in their industry and having adverse effects on mental health . <p> In the next government term , we need to see improvements in the essential freshwater regulations to make the rules workable for farmers so they can get on with achieving the desired water health outcomes . <p> Meanwhile , the government must get fossil fuel emitters to reduce their emissions rather than just planting their pollution on our farms . Limits must be set on the amount of offsetting allowed in the ETS before it 's too late and further swathes of productive sheep and beef farmland are converted to forestry for carbon farming .
