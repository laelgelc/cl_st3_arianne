<h> Do n't Be Scared Of WorkSafe <p> The latest PR exercise from WorkSafe NZ encourages businesses not to be scared of WorkSafe and to fully engage with assistance offered from them . <p> They cite an example from a Masterton based printing firm where an experienced staff member cut their finger when clearing out a machine waste gate during a busy shift . The employer found out the next day that the hospital had to amputate the end of the employee 's finger and as such immediately reported it to WorkSafe . <p> WorkSafe consequently investigated this and decided that a Duty Holder Review ( DHR ) was to be undertaken . <p> A DHR is where WorkSafe will ask a business to do a self review of policy &amp; procedures after an incident has occured . It is usually after a serious harm incident but falls short of the prosecution threshold . The aim of course is to stop an incident of a similar nature from happening again . The intention is not as a punitive measure but preventative for the future .
