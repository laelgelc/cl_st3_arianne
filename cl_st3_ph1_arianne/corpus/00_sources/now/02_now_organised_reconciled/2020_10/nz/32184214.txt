<h> UN report says up to 850,000 animal viruses could be caught by humans , unless we protect nature <p> Human damage to biodiversity is leading us into a pandemic era . The virus that causes COVID-19 , for example , is linked to similar viruses in bats , which may have been passed to humans via pangolins or another species . <p> Environmental destruction such as land clearing , deforestation , climate change , intense agriculture and the wildlife trade is putting humans into closer contact with wildlife .
