<h> Jason Walls : Inside the Labour-Green deal and the referendum results <p> The Greens have voted to accept a deal with the Labour Party which will see the party 's two highest-ranking MPs become Ministers outside of Cabinet . <p> After close to four hours of negotiations between key Green Party delegates , 84 per cent of voters backed the deal offered by Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern . <p> " The Green Party is thrilled to enter into this governing arrangement with Labour , " co-leader Marama Davidson said . <p> The deal -- which is officially called a " Cooperation Agreement " -- reflects the mandate Labour received when it won its 64-seat majority in the election , Ardern said . <p> But , after the Greens announced it would back the deal , Ardern pointed out that it was the Labour Party which won a " clear mandate " to form a majority Government . <p> " I will use the mandate that we have been given . " <p> Ardern unveiled the proposed deal in its entirety half an hour after the delegate call began -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ made public for " transparency " reasons . <p> The Prime Minister also signalled that she will be looking to work with the opposition on a number of areas of cross-party cooperation , such as extending the parliamentary term and the abolishing of the " coat-tailing " rules . <p> The highlight of the deal is the portfolios co-leaders James Shaw and Marama Davidson would hold outside Cabinet . <p> Davidson will become the Minister for the Prevention of Family and Sexual Violence and Associate Minister of Housing ( Homelessness ) . <p> Shaw will be the Minister of Climate Change and Associate Minister for the Environment ( Biodiversity ) . <p> Climate Change is a portfolio he held in the previous Government and is an area he knows " inside out " , Ardern said . <p> The Greens will also have the chairmanship of a Select Committee -- which one would be decided at a later date . <p> Ardern said that she did n't consider offering the Greens a coalition agreement -- similar to the one Labour offered NZ First after the 2017 election . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our system of MMP , " she said . <p> In total , 136 Green Party delegates voted on the deal . <p> Some 114 voted for , 17 voted against and five did not vote -- that means the majority was 84 per cent . <p> At its essence , the deal means the Greens have committed to supporting Labour to provide stable Government for this term of Parliament . <p> The deal said : " The Green Party agrees to support the Labour Government by not opposing votes on matters of confidence and supply for the full term of this Parliament . " <p> That means they are not allowed to vote against the Government when passing the budget . <p> They are , however , allowed to abstain from a vote such as that . <p> " This has the effect of strengthening the Government 's stability and will ensure there is always a strong majority in Parliament on the most important votes , " Ardern said . <p> As well as the ministerial posts , Labour has promised to work with the Greens in a number @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ achieving the purpose and goals of the Zero Carbon Act , protecting our environment and biodiversity and improving child wellbeing . <p> " This deal will provide New Zealanders with the certainty of a strong , stable Labour Government with support from the Green Party over the next three years , " Ardern said . <p> Ardern said Shaw and Davidson have " very clearly defined " areas that they will be working on if the deal was approved . <p> " We have had three years working together , " she said of the Greens over the past three years . <p> " We both have a win-win from this arrangement . " <p> The official agreement will be signed in Ardern 's Beehive office on Sunday . <p> Looking ahead , Ardern will name her new Cabinet on Monday and ministers will be sworn in on Friday afternoon .
