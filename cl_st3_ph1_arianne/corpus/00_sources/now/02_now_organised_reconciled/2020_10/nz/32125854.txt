<h> DCC votes in favour of Zero Carbon Alliance concept <p> A zero carbon alliance looks set to become a reality , after Dunedin city councillors voted in favour of the proposal on Tuesday . <p> This was despite some reservations about the " undoubtedly ambitious " target . <p> The agreement approves in principle the concept of establishing a Zero Carbon 2030 Alliance involving the University of Otago , the Otago Regional Council , Otago Polytechnic , local runanga and the Southern District Health Board as founding members . <p> It will look to take a partnership approach to reducing the city 's emissions . <p> Principal policy adviser Jinty MacTavish emphasised the zero carbon 2030 target set by council last year was " undoubtedly ambitious " ; however , conversations with key partners , including the tertiary institutes , indicated they were willing to work towards the goal . <p> Cr Jim O'Malley said he would prefer the zero carbon goal was set for 2035 . <p> He was supportive of the alliance , but wary it would not produce results after the initial " big hurrah " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ forward but I will become louder and louder in my criticism if nothing comes of it . " <p> Further organisations and businesses needed to be added to the alliance to ensure it had impact in reducing emissions , he said . <p> Cr Lee Vandervis said he could not support the alliance as it would lead to an " ever-wider talk fest " . <p> Council efforts to reduce emissions so far had been " all talk " , he said . <p> " None of this massive spending we are undertaking on behalf of ratepayers produced any real emissions reductions . " <p> Cr Chris Staynes said climate change was not something council could ignore . <p> " The world is burning ... 2030 zero carbon is slower than what this planet needs . " <p> All councillors except Cr Vandervis voted in favour of the alliance . <p> Crs Marie Laufiso and Carmen Houlahan were absent . <p> Terms of reference will be developed with potential members of the alliance before a final draft is given to council . <p> Last DCC meeting transcript .
