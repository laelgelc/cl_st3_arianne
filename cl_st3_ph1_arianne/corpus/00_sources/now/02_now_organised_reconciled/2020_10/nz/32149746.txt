<h> Workshop To Lift Google Profile Of Whanganui Businesses <p> Economic development agency Whanganui &amp; Partners is running a free workshop to help business owners develop their Google My Business profiles . <p> Google My Business ( GMB ) is a free service provided by Google to help businesses manage how they appear in search results . GMB allows businesses to appear on Google Maps and local search results , allows customers to leave reviews and gives businesses access to useful insights about their audience . <p> " If you 've ever searched for coffee and gotten a map with nearby cafes marked , or looked for a restaurant and found their opening hours on the search page , that 's Google My Business . " <p> During the two-hour workshop , business owners will claim their profile and learn how to optimise their content with simple , easily-actioned tips . <p> Chaplow says there are a lot of benefits to businesses developing their GMB profile and no downsides . He hopes to see all 100 available spaces in the workshop filled . <p> " Google My Business is free @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a very powerful marketing tool . After the workshop , business owners will not only know how to claim their profile but how to take full advantage of all the features available . " <p> Chaplow says that in addition to being useful for individual businesses , the workshop will result in a stronger digital presence for Whanganui overall . <p> " Whether it 's where to go , what to do or what to buy , most people start with Google . By developing a comprehensive digital ecosystem in Whanganui , we 're improving the experience of people planning a visit here as well as making it easier for them when they 're here . " <p> The workshop will be facilitated by Miles Partnership , who specialise in helping destinations enhance their Google presence . The workshop will be held in the Pioneer Room of the War Memorial Centre on Wednesday 21 October from 10am to noon .
