<h> Introducing Fred the Forester <p> Eastland Wood Council is sponsoring 500 children 's story books into the kura and schools of Tairawhiti , and the HB Williams Library . <p> Fred the Forester Discovers -- Where do the Pine Trees Go ? will be delivered to kura and primary schools across the region . <p> The book is aimed at students aged 5 to 10 , and explores through a child 's eyes , the journey of trees and then logs from home , first to a sawmill and then to local housing . <p> The emphasis in the story is on families managing their own forests , local uses of timber and introduces climate change and environmental themes that will be explored further in later editions . <p> There are activities in the back of the book that teachers can photocopy , or children can complete them in the book . <p> The activities include a forestry Word Find , a maze and maps of New Zealand showing the location of forests and sawmills , as well as pictures to colour in .
