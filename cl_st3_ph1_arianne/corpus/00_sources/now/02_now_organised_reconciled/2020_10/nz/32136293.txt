It may seem like New Zealand does not have a big part to play in any solutions to the global problem . Yet despite our low total emissions , our emissions per person are some of the highest in the OECD . So something needs to change . But what -- and how ? <p> Episode 5 <p> We turned Aotearoa into a farm .
