<h> Sustainable Tairawhiti , and nation <p> Oh what a night ! Election 2020 has been intriguing , argumentative and , due to covid , a rather drawn out affair . The finale on Saturday night however was a real showing of winners and losers , and perhaps the overall winner was participatory democracy with such a great turnout of voters this election . <p> So , first , congratulations to all who voted.Second , congratulations to all the winners . And third , commiserations to those who lost . <p> It was evident that Prime Minister Ardern 's management of covid was a catalyst for the landslide victory . It will be interesting to see what her leadership style will mean for the forming of a new government . With no NZ First " handbrake " , I hope there is transformative green thinking and a strong emphasis on action in health , housing , climate change , transport , employment and equality . <p> This region has had significant investment pre- and post-covid , but just how sustainable this investment is will depend on governance wisdom . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ East Coast is the largest general electorate in the North Island . It extends from the outskirts of Te Puke , through Whakatane and encompasses the entire East Cape including Gisborne .
