<p> As part of The Spinoff 's commitment to Covering Climate Now , a week-long worldwide initiative aimed at strengthening the media 's focus on climate change , the food section is going vegan . This week , we 'll publish only recipes that do n't feature animal products , and our stories will have a vegan bent too . <p> If you 're thinking , " What does a vegan diet have to do with climate change ? " , where you been bro ? ! As I wrote in this piece last year , science tells us that avoiding meat and dairy products is the single biggest way to reduce your environmental impact . <p> So that 's why we 're doing it . But first , a few caveats . Many vegans do n't like when you say things like " going vegan for a week " , because it 's a lifelong commitment , not something you can dip in and out of .
