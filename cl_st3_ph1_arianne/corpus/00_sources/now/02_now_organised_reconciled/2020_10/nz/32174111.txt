" Deputy Leader Gerry Brownlee came out in support , saying his weight was solely his responsibility . <p> They are half-right . <p> Or @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ social media post that garnered over 20,000 likes , Collins " is not half wrong . " <p> " We do have to accept personal responsibility for the choices we make . However , I am a product of my surroundings . " <p> Growing up in Otahuhu surrounded by takeaways and liquor stores and living on fattier cuts of meat like lamb flaps that are staples for many Pasifika do not help . <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern agreed that it was more complex , saying , " We are all products of our environment , " as did National MP Mark Mitchell who broke ranks with Collins . <p> Practical dialogue needed <p> Even Winston Peters said that it was a combination of factors , calling for a " seriously practical dialogue about it rather than just condemning people . " <p> For this kind of dialogue to happen , reductive , oversimplified arguments need to be called out for what they are .
