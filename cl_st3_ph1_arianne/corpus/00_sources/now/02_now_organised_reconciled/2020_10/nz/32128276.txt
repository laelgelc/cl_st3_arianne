<h> Income policy underplayed <p> Last week Jacinda Ardern claimed her Government had done more than all previous ones in " laying foundations " for reducing both child poverty and climate change . The second is true , thanks to Green initiatives and her admirable collaboration with them . <p> The first is not , because you do n't have to lay foundations to reduce poverty , you do it ! You can instantly increase benefits , and guarantee them without all the cruel barriers and conditions ( unjust and impossible to administer fairly ) ; this is exactly what the Green Party 's Guaranteed Minimum Income can do .
