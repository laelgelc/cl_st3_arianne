Labour and the Greens tend to fare disproportionately well with special votes . <p> Even Judith Collins ' own Papakura went to Labour in the Party Vote , although the margin was narrow . <p> The Rangitata Seat <p> Perhaps no electorate better exemplifies the depths of this failure than the Rangitata seat .
