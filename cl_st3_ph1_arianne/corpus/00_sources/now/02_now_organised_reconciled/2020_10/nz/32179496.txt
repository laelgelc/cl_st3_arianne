<h> Grants help community groups in Puketapapa <p> More than $35,000 has been allocated to community groups by the Puketapapa Local Board through its grants programme in October . <p> The grants cover a range of activities across support for seniors and youth , promoting environmental protection and awareness and encouraging creativity . <p> Puketapapa Local Board Chair Julie Fairey is glad the local board was able to provide this local community support in a tough financial environment , saying <p> " The value of the positive impacts from these grants is indisputable . Our community will gain opportunities to encourage creativity , protect our environment and improve their well-being , through the great work of these local groups . " <p> Budget cuts and limited funding has meant that not every organisation that applies for funding receives it .
