As we navigate through Covid-19 and fast approach the end of the Brexit transition period , there remains significant uncertainty within the current business landscape . However , there is clarity around the future we are looking to create , and it is an exciting time to be helping clients grow their businesses in increasingly sustainable , diverse and innovative ways . " <p> In her new role she will lead over 200 partners and 4,500 staff , with responsibility for some of the firm 's largest global clients .
