<p> JBA Risk Management ( " JBA " ) , the global leader in flood risk science , today announces the launch of its Climate Change Analytics ; a sophisticated data suite developed to help banks , lenders , investors , and the wider global financial sector to understand the potential long-term impacts of climate change and to meet regulatory requirements . <p> The launch of Climate Change Analytics represents the largest range of data available for financial services .
