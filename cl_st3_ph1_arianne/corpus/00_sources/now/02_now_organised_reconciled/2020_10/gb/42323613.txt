<h> Southampton deluge is ' clear indication ' of climate change <p> A SOUTHAMPTON councillor has claimed that flooding across the city is " not down to maintenance " issues . <p> Councillor Steve Leggett , Cabinet Member for Green City and Place , has said that the flooding in Millbrook Road West is a result of climate change , and is not due to maintenance in the area . <p> It comes after Storm Alex battered the south coast this weekend bringing torrential rain and strong winds . <p> Millbrook Road West flooded on Friday with two lanes of the outbound carriageway reported as closed . <p> Motorists were urged to avoid the area and find alternative routes . <p> The road has been flooding several times a year when heavy rain hits the city , and has been repeated met with anger towards to local council from residents of Millbrook . <p> Cllr Leggett said : " Southampton City Council is working with Southern Water , ABP and other stakeholders to improve the infrastructure . <p> " The fact that we have had more rain in three days @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ September is not down to maintenance , but is a clear indication that climate change is already with us . <p> " The Green City Charter and the measures we have put in place already in the Green Transport Recovery Plan and other initiatives are intended as a contribution to combating climate change , which will lead to even more catastrophic flooding around the country in the future . <p> " It is a pity that the Conservatives are so keen to avoid any measures being implemented to combat climate change , but rather prefer to complain about its inevitable consequences .
