<h> Further calls for public inquiry into Battle of Orgreave <h> Further calls for public inquiry into Battle of Orgreave <p> A PETITION has been submitted to Parliament calling for a public inquiry into the actions of police during one of the most violent clashes in British industrial history . <p> The Battle of Orgreave , on June 18 , 1984 , saw 123 men injured and 95 men arrested after riots broke out between pickets and police during the miners ' strike , at Orgreave coking plant in South Yorkshire . <p> The event has come to symbolise the bitter struggle between the striking pit men and Margaret Thatcher 's Conservative Government over the closure of coal mines across the country . <p> The petition was delivered by County Durham MP Grahame Morris as part of the Orgreave Trust and Justice Campaign . <p> Mr Morris said : " The miners ' strike is not a long-forgotten piece of history , as the Government would like us to believe . <p> " The actions of state collusion and police brutality caused lasting damage to our family , friends and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ blacklisted , criminalised , wrongly convicted , attacked , and denied basic civil liberties by the state , in a focused effort to smash Trade Unionism in the UK . <p> " The historic injustice of the miners ' strike did not end in 1984 , but lives with those at Orgreave to this day .
