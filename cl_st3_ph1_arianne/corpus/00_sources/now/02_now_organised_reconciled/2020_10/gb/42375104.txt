It immediately , lazily and arrogantly , frames any assertion of a moral or political principle as an act of narcissism . <p> And here 's the thing . ' Virtue signalling ' is no bad thing . People affirming values publicly is how values propagate through a society . How else do you think it 's supposed to happen ? It 's just not the case that all we need is to go about quietly doing good and let our deeds be an example . Why is that a bad thing either ? Anthropologically speaking , our moral views are bound up with our group identities . Peer pressure is one of the reasons nobody in Croydon these days has much to say in favour of forced marriage , cannibalism or human sacrifice .
