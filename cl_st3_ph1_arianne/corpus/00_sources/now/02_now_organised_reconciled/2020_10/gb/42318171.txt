<h> Will environmental protections go up in smoke after Brexit ? <p> Analysis of the impact of a no-deal , or weak deal , has focused on the impact on trade . But , says DR MARK AVERY , it will also bring consequences for the environment . <p> Four years after the Brexit vote , and almost nine months through the one-year transition period , we are still unclear as to the impacts of Brexit on future environmental protection in the UK . That 's because our environmental future will be greatly determined by the type of trade deal , or lack of it , that the UK agrees with the European Union . Many would say that it is a topsy-turvy world where environmental protection picks up the scraps from a trade negotiation rather than the other way around , but so it is . <p> The terms of any trade deal with the EU will determine , through any commitments to level playing fields , how close to current EU-wide ( and currently , UK-wide too ) environmental standards the UK will remain .
