<h> Letters : ' If the Leamside Railway is opened and electrified and it would be a clean mode of transport for many ' <h> Letters : ' If the Leamside Railway is opened and electrified and it would be a clean mode of transport for many ' <p> IT was good to see the excellent photograph in last Tuesday 's Northern Echo of the Amazon facility at Bowburn on the new Integra 61 development providing many jobs ( Echo , Sept 22 ) . <p> Mention was made of the excellent road transport links to the site from the A1(M) etc . <p> With climate change now upon us , however , cleaner transport will have to be considered in the near future . <p> The bottom left side of the photograph shows a sandy coloured track . This is the formation of the Leamside railway line which is being considered for re-opening which could provide direct links to Tyneside , Wearside , Teesside and the south . <p> If electrified this would make an environmentally clean mode of transport .
