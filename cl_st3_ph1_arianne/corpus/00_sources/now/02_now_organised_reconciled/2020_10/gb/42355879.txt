<h> Plans revealed for new multi-million pounds waste treatment facility <h> Plans revealed for new waste treatment facility at Parley <p> A CLEAN energy company is hoping to expand with a new waste treatment facility . <p> Eco Sustainable Solutions is celebrating 25 years in business by announcing the new multi-million pounds investment which will allow it to process more local waste and generate more green energy . <p> Eco has announced plans to build an Energy Recovery Facility ( ERF ) at its Eco Park site in Parley , east Dorset that would help it to process an extra 60,000 tonnes of waste -- up to a fifth of that would be recycled and the rest would be used to generate more low-carbon energy . <p> The planned facility would play a key role in helping local authorities tackle the region 's waste in the years to come , as well as having the potential to provide low carbon electricity and heat for local homes and businesses . <p> The plant would join Eco 's existing clean energy portfolio -- a combined heat and power plant , 77MW of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ anaerobic digestion facility at Piddlehinton near Dorchester , and add to the 250,000 tonnes of local waste the company already recycles . <p> Tackling climate change is a growing priority both nationally and locally , with the UK government setting a target to achieve net zero carbon emissions by 2050 and BCP and Dorset Councils both declaring a climate emergency . <p> BCP Council has set out 153 measures they will take to reduce the impact of climate change , which include tackling waste by reducing items such as single use plastics , increasing recycling and avoiding landfill . <p> At 60,000 tonnes , Eco 's proposed ERF will provide less than half of the capacity that the councils ' joint waste plan says it could build at the Parley site .
