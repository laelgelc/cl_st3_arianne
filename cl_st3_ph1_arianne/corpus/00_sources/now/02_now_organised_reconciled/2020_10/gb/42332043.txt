<h> What We Do <h> Project <h> Citizens ' Assembly on Climate Change <p> The NTCA will be the first devolved English region to involve the public in a Citizens ' Assembly on climate change . <h> Plans have been refreshed for a Citizens ' Assembly on climate change in 2020 <p> The North of Tyne Combined Authority has refreshed plans for a Citizens ' Assembly on climate change , following the coronavirus pandemic . <p> After cabinet approval in February 2020 , the NTCA plans for the Citizens ' Assembly were put on hold due to the nationwide lockdown . However , the NTCA have now refreshed our plans for a Citizens ' Assembly on climate change and have gone through the process of procuring an external organisation to design and facilitate the Citizens ' Assembly when it is safe to do so . <p> North of Tyne will become the first devolved English region to involve the public in this way . <p> A Citizens ' Assembly is a particular method of engaging a randomly-selected representative sample of the public to consider an issue in depth and produce @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of public consultation . <p> The North of Tyne Citizens ' Assembly will examine a specific set of issues relating to climate change and produce recommendations for the North of Tyne Combined Authority to consider . <p> It is hoped that the North of Tyne Citizens ' Assembly can meet for the first time this Autumn and complete its work by the end of the year . <p> " Our councils are upgrading vehicle fleets , planting trees and installing solar panels . But it 's vital that we get our citizens involved . They can tell us what changes are possible in daily life to make rapid progress . " <p> Jamie Driscoll <p> NTCA Mayor <p> The process involves : <p> Commissioning an external organisation to design and facilitate the Citizens ' Assembly and to recruit participants . <p> Establishing an Oversight Panel to determine the topic and questions to be put to the Citizens ' Assembly to consider . <p> We will be doing both over the coming months and posting further details here when we have them .
