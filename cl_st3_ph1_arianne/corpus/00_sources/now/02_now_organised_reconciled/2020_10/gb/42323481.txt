<h> Recovery fund clears Council hurdle with majority of ministers <p> Finance ministers backed a bill to create the EU coronavirus recovery fund despite concerns that checks on uses of the money could slow payouts . <p> A qualified majority of ministers agreed on the compromise proposal Tuesday by videoconference . Their support clears the way for EU ambassadors to sign off on the text Friday and open up Council talks with the European Parliament on a final form of the legislation . <p> " Looking at all the interventions , I would say the text is a good basis to start negotiations with the Parliament , " German minister Olaf Scholz said while chairing the live-streamed meeting . <p> Rising infection rates spurred ministers to strike a deal , overcoming north-south divisions over some elements , out of concern that further containment measures may hamstring Europe 's economic recovery . <p> Ministers smoothed over several areas of dispute , including the fund 's spending targets on projects intended to tackle climate change , as well as links to how countries uphold the rule of law . <p> Getting access @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fund proved more important to most ministers than dragging out negotiations , especially after the European Central Bank warned about the vulnerability of the economy . <p> " The recovery is starting to lose momentum , " ECB Vice President Luis de Guindos said in the meeting .
