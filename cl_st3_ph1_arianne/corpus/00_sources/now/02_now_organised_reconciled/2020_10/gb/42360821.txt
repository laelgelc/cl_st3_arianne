<h> Xi 's world : Covid has accelerated China 's rise <p> Back in February , the Chinese state appeared to be in trouble . A terrifying virus had infected thousands of people and the country 's social media exploded in anger against the authorities faster than Chinese censors could scrub away the critical comments . Like governments elsewhere , the Chinese Communist Party ( CCP ) turned to the emergency analogy of choice , the second world war . Channelling Mao Zedong 's guerrilla campaign against the Japanese in the 1930s , state media declared that China was fighting a ' people 's war ' against the virus . <p> As in that earlier war , China 's conflict with the virus has shifted from a defiant retreat to a declaration of victory . Nor is this just bluster .
