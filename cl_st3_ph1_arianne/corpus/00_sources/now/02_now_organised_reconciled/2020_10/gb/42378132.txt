<p> You 've probably never met a horrid ground-weaver spider -- and if you do n't hurry up you probably never will . The small bristly creature was discovered , new to science , in 1995 in a former quarry near Plymouth . Ten years later developers planned to build 57 new houses on the site and that would have been the end for another unglamorous bit of British biodiversity . Does it matter ?
