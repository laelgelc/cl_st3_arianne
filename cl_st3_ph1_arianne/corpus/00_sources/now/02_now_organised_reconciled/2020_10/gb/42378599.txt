The latter is curiously reminiscent of Japanese attempts at the end of World War 2 to extract oil from pine roots , for which thousands of children were dispatched into the countryside for hours of backbreaking and largely pointless toil . It 's depressing in that the faint hope that Suga , with his humble background and reputation for being in touch with the concerns of ordinary people , would offer an alternative to the grand headline-making but ultimately inconsequential policy pronouncements of his aristocratic predecessor , seems on this evidence unlikely to be borne out . <p> Suga 's first blast has the familiar feel of hot rather than fresh air .
