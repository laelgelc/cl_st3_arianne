<h> David Coles <h> October 21st , 2020 <h> Take Action seminar series <h> David Coles <h> October 21st , 2020 <h> Take Action seminar series <p> The Volunteer Centre is delighted to launch a new series of events for the 2020/21 academic year called " Take Action " . The seminars in this programme will explore some of the most pressing issues facing society and how the LSE community can make a difference . We are starting with the issue of homelessness but we will also explore climate change , racial inequality , educational inequality and others . <p> We 're launching this programme , with the support of Student Futures , for two reasons . Firstly , through our research and listening to LSE students , we understand that whilst there is a huge passion to get involved in many causes sometimes students are n't sure how to start , what skills they have to offer or they are just unaware of the opportunities available .
