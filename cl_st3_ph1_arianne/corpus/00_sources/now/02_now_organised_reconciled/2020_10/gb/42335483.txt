<h> Southampton petrol pumps branded with ' health warnings ' as part of Extinction Rebellion protest <p> ALMOST 50 petrol pumps in Southampton have been branded with " public health warnings " as part of a national protest lead by Extinction Rebellion . <p> The labels were designed by Doctors for Extinction Rebellion to resemble existing cigarette packet health warnings ; the government 's " Stay Alert " coronavirus banners ; and the wartime " Your country needs you " posters , with David Attenborough 's face replacing that of Lord Kitchener . <p> It comes as calls for health warning labels on petrol pumps from the medical profession . <p> Public health experts Professor Sir Andy Haines , Professor Kristie Ebi and Mike Gill , wrote recently in a British Medical Journal article : " There is an opportunity for national and local governments to implement labelling of fossil fuels in the run-up to COP26 in Glasgow , and in particular for the UK Government , as the host of the COP , to show leadership , as part of a package of measures to accelerate progress on getting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ label links to further information about the health effects of air pollution , which reportedly causes 112 excess deaths per day in the UK . <p> The group stated that the action did not " set out to lay the blame at the door of individual drivers , but to inform and create political pressure " . <p> Dr Chris Newman , GP and founder of doctors for Extinction Rebellion said : " The tragedy of all is this is that , as with coronavirus , the alarm bells have been rung for years . <p> " Climate change is coming , it is preventable , and our leaders are negligent in their inaction . Doctors and nurses from all over the UK are calling for health warning labels on petrol pumps . <p> " We believe that an ever-present warning of the harm we are doing ourselves will drive innovation and change .
