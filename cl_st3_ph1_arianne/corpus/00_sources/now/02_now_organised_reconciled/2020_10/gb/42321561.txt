A political strategy strongly associated with the Third Way politics of Tony Blair and Bill Clinton , it represents a superficially canny way of wrong-footing your opponents , synthesising their most popular policies , and leading parties back into government from the mythical " centre " -- usually defined in terms of the right-wing political preoccupations and talking points of the corporate media . The preferred political clich ? for this is " modernisation " . <p> Sometimes , the triangulation is mainly posturing and positioning , articulated through press releases and puff pieces .
