But there was one asset they could easily have overlooked , and yet which would have outperformed almost any of them : silver . <p> For much of the past decade , silver has been ignored as a largely irrelevant alternative to gold . In the past few months , however , it has started to shine . The precious metal has soared in price , outperforming most alternatives . Nothing ever happened . <p> That was reflected in the price . In January 2015 silver was trading at $16.50 an ounce . In January this year it was trading at $17 .
