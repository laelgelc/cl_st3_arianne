With American voters preparing to head to the polls on November 3 , Trump seemingly has one more gift to give to Chinese President Xi Jinping : an election meltdown . <p> For China , at least , US President Donald Trump is the gift that keeps on giving . His calamitous response to the COVID-19 pandemic has made China , whose government mishandled the initial outbreak in January this year , look like an exemplar of effective governance . Moreover , Trump 's " America First " foreign policy has alienated traditional US allies , making it difficult to build a broad coalition to counter China . <p> To be sure , Trump has delivered painful blows to Chinese President Xi Jinping .
