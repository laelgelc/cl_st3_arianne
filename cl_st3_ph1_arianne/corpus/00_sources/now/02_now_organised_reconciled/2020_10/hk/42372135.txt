I had been meditating regularly , perhaps more so than at any other time in my life . I had been invited to teach meditation in my community , and that was precisely what I was doing on the evening of the election . <p> As I exited the yoga studio where I taught , I saw that I had received a flurry of text messages from my now-wife , concerned over early election result favoring Trump . <p> " Do n't worry , " I reassured her . " It 's still early and the Westernmost states will pretty much all go to Clinton . He had made demeaning and derogatory statements about countless women in public and was accused by many of sexual assault and harassment . <p> His rhetoric and plans were filled with hatred toward so many people and yet everyone had to come to terms with the fact that he would be the leader of the United States for four years . <p> I remember going to sleep that night grateful . I saw and heard the messages of worry , outrage , concern , and despair among liberal and progressive friends . I felt all of it .
