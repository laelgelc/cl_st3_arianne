<h> Nigeria needs livestock ministry -- Prof Yahaya <p> Mohammed Kuta Yahaya is a professor of agricultural extension at the University of Ibadan . We have come through different phases , from pre-colonial , colonial and we are now in post-colonial . We have , at different times , multitude-based interventions and so on ; until the World Bank came in around 1973 and they started enclave projects in Gusau , Gombe and Bida . It later became nationwide in the late 80s . Unfortunately , it was driven by funding and structure that was imposed based on Training and Visit type of agricultural extension . <p> Apparently , the Indian who pioneered this type of extension was using it based @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ system . He has been deliberate in agriculture as the fulcrum of the diversification agenda . His administration has been promoting all the variables that make agriculture thrive . He inherited the agricultural policy of the Goodluck Jonathan 's administration , which had to initially do with agricultural transformation agenda . I must say ' kudos ' to President Buhari for his doggedness which has now made agriculture very viable in Nigeria . For the first time in our country , fertiliser is available at a controlled price , unlike before .
