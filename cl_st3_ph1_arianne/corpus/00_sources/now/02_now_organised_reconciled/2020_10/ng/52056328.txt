<h> Nigerian YFAI wins Green NGO of the Year in Africa <p> Adeyemo Yusuf Adeniyi of YFAI being announced the winner of Green NGO of the Year <p> By Iyiola Pedro <p> A Non-Governmental Organisation ( NGO ) , Youths for Apiculture Initiative ( YFAI ) , has emerged the Green NGO of the Year in Africa . <p> The recognition was given at the just-concluded 9th edition of the Africa Green Waste &amp; Energy Expo and Conference held at the Business HUB/Harare Exhibition Park , Robbie Mupawose Centre , in Harare , Zimbabwe . <p> The three-day event , which started on Oct. 28 had the theme , " A Zero Carbon , Climate Resilient Future ; Setting the Stage for Transformation " . <p> Speaking to NAN on Saturday , President of YFAI , Mr Yusuf Adeyemo , described the recognition as a call to service . <p> Adeyemo said : " The award is indeed an honour and something we see as a call to do more in the African space . <p> " We are very delighted that we were recognised in far away Zimbabwe . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ encourage ourselves to take advantage of marketing opportunities . <p> " But it 's not about us alone , but the collective responsibility of everyone . It ( the award ) will no doubt motivate our members and assure our partners across the world that our efforts are being noticed . " <p> Adeyemo noted that direct and indirect engagement in beekeeping is capable of creating wealth . <p> " If we continue promoting beekeeping , more youths will be gainfully employed and improve their standard of living , promote food security and reduce use of chemicals . <p> FYAI 's Public Relations Officer , Mr Oluwaseun Johnson , described the award as timely . <p> He said that the world is currently in a time where everyone is conscious of the contribution of Agriculture to greenhouse gases . <p> " The good news about beekeeping is the fact that it contributes zero to greenhouse emission , hence it has very good carbon footprint .
