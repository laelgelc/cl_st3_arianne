It will surely be either Nigeria 's Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala and South Korea 's Yoo Myung-hee . <p> Members of WTO have since picked the two celebrated women as the final candidates . Hence , Okonjo-Iweala and Myung-hee have both advanced to the final round , in the race to lead the Geneva-based trade body . <p> It is already a fait accompli that WTO will have the first female DG in its 25-year history , in either of the two finalists . <p> Okonjo-Iweala , who served two stints as Nigeria 's finance minister and one term as foreign affairs minister , has invaluable experience working at international governance bodies . She was a former managing director of the World Bank , and chairman at the Global Alliance for Vaccines and Immunization . <p> Myung-hee is South Korea 's trade minister .
