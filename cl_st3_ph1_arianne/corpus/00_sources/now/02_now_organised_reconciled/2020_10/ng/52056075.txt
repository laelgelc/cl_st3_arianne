<h> UN Raises Alarm Over Dumping Of Used Cars In Developing Nations , Driving Up Pollution <p> Used vehicles exported from richer countries are contributing to increased air pollution in developing nations and hindering efforts to mitigate the effects of climate change , according to a report published on Monday by the UN Environment Programme ( UNEP ) . <p> The study , first-ever of its kind , found that some 14 million used cars , vans and minibuses were exported worldwide from Europe , the United States and Japan between 2015 and 2018 . <p> Around 80 per cent , went to low and middle income countries , with more than half going to Africa . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The UNEP Executive Director , Inger Andersen , said " Cleaning up the global vehicle fleet is a priority to meet global and local air quality and climate targets . <p> " Over the years , developed countries have increasingly exported their used vehicles to developing countries ; because this largely happens unregulated , this has become the export of polluting vehicles .
