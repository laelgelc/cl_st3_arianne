<h> Climate Change : Microbiologist urges recycling of plastics , stoppage in production <p> Mr Jaafar Abdullahi , a lecturer in the Department of Microbiology , Kaduna State University ( KASU ) , has called for the recycling of plastics to save the environment from avoidable disasters like floods . <p> He also called for a total halt in further production of plastics in view of their danger to the natural habitat . <p> Abdullahi spoke on Thursday in Kaduna at a one-day training for science journalists organised by Climate Change Reporters . <p> The News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) , reports that the training was carried out in collaboration with Motherhood Foundation and Gusau Institute . <p> In a paper he titled : " Effects of climate change in the distribution of diseases " , Abdullahi said that climate change had brought about new diseases and caused dangerous organisms to spread to new places . <p> According to Abdullahi , Nigeria can come up with the idea of recycling existing plastics which could serve and last for hundreds of years . <p> " The recycling outfits can @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for the government . <p> " Let the world stop production of new plastics while we recycle the ones we already have in place , " he said . <p> He added that apart from saving lives , recycling of plastics would create jobs and keep youths busy so as to reduce social vices committed by them . <p> According to Abdullahi , plastics and products such as water bottles , water sachets and polythene bags which end up in drainage are partly responsible for floods and diseases across the country . <p> " Floods provide more breeding grounds for mosquitoes and other vectors which means , indirectly , greenhouse distributes diseases , " he said . <p> He called on Nigerians to admit that climate change is everybody 's problem and that no agency , government or scientist could fix it alone . <p> " We are all in this together ;
