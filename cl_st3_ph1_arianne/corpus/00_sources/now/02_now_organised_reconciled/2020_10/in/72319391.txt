<h> Importance of eggs to boost immunity on Worlds Egg day <p> Hyderabad : The importance of this protein-rich food during the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> The experts got together to discuss the vision forward for the industry and the importance of eggs in diets . Eggs are counted amongst the most economical , accessible healthiest foods across the globe . <p> At a webinar hosted by Suresh Chitturi , Chairman of the International Egg Coordination Committee ( IEC ) along with Tim Lambert , former chairman of IEC , Dr Raghav Sunil , MS Orthopaedics , and Sanjoy Mukerji , CEO , Indian Poultry Review , they discussed case studies and shared business insights about the industry during the new normal . <p> Suresh Chitturi noted that the egg supply chain remained strong in 2020 and even during the pandemic , egg farmers worked with gusto with their partners to deliver fresh , local , high-quality eggs in India . <p> " That 's why this World Egg Day , we 're expressing our appreciation for their contribution , and the importance of eggs .
