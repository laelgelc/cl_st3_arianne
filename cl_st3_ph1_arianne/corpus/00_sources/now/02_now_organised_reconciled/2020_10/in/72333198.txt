<p> In a short span of ten years , the international discourse around China 's environmental record has shifted dramatically . From the environmental " bad boy " that " wrecked " the Copenhagen climate talks in 2009 , the country 's image in the environmental field has undergone a makeover . Its upholding of the Paris Agreement after the United States withdrawal is celebrated as an act of global leadership . And its domestic environmental efforts , including the successful control , in a matter of years , of devastating urban air pollution , have been hailed as examples of effective state intervention into environmental crises . <p> In China Goes Green : Coercive Environmentalism for a Troubled Planet ( Polity , 2020 ) , New York University Shanghai Assistant Professor Yifei Li and American University Professor Judith Shapiro deconstruct the popular narrative .
