<h> Spectre Of Deluge <p> Goa must implement climate-friendly strategies to prevent a catastrophe <p> YET another serious warning on the rapid deterioration of Goa 's ecological balance has come , and if the state government and people of Goa do not wake up , the face of the state would change for the worse , making it unrecognisable . The latest warning has come from the Goa State Biodiversity Board ( GSBB ) which says almost 15 percent of the land in Goa is severely vulnerable to flooding caused by extreme rainfall or rising sea level . The GSBB , which has drawn the state action plan for climate change , says that 50 percent of Goa could be hit by flooding if anti-flood measures were not taken . The state cabinet has accorded its approval to the remedial measures suggested by the GSBB , and people of Goa would hope the government has not done it merely as a formality . It has to be taken seriously to avoid the impact of climate change .
