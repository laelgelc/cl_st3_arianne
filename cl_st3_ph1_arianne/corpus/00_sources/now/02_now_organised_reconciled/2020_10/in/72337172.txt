Kaleshwaram lift irrigation scheme <p> The NGT October 20 directed the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) to constitute a seven-member expert committee to assess the extent of damage caused due to the Kaleshwaram Lift Irrigation Scheme ( KLIS ) from 2008 to 2017 and identify the restoration measures necessary . <p> Relief and rehabilitation measures adopted and required to be further adopted would also be looked into by the joint committee . <p> The NGT refused to accept the stand of the project proponent , stating that KLIS was primarily meant for water supply and water management and that irrigation was subsidiary , and hence , environmental clearance was required prior to execution of the project . <p> The court noted that the project was infrastructure was being constructed prior to applying for Environmental Clearance ( EC ) . It also waived the cost imposed vide NGT order August 9 , 2019 . <p> The NGT March 18 , 2020 had directed all states and union territories to place on record the present status of compliance of directions passed by the NGT vide orders August 9 , 2019 and March 18 , 2020 related to constitution of BMC and preparation of PBR by the BMC so constituted . <p> The report informed the court that around 4,850 out of 6,459 BMCs had been constituted and the rest would be constituted by the end of the year . <p> However , preparation of an exhaustive PBR documenting all the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ biodiversity hotspot and peculiar topographical and geographical conditions of Meghalaya having places where humans are yet to set their foot would take time , the report said . <p> The short timeline to complete the preparation of all PBRs for other parts of the country was not practical and feasible for Meghalaya , the report added . <p> The novel coronavirus disease ( COVID-19 ) pandemic also impeded the pace of constitution of BMCs . Physical visit to each of the 6,459 villages , most of them inaccessible by paved road , was difficult and would take more time . <p> Similarly , preparation of the PBRs also requires physical visits of the BMC members to their respective areas and consultation with local people .
