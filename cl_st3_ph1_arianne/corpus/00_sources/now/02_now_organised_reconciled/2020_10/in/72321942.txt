SNS &amp; PTI CHANDIGARH/NEW DELHI , 14 OCTOBER The Punjab government today decided to convene a special session of the Punjab Assembly on 19 October to " bring in legislation to counter the dangerous anti-farmer farm laws of the Central government " . A decision in this regard was taken at a meeting of the Punjab Cabinet , chaired by chief minister Captain Amarinder Singh , through video conferencing . CM Capt Amarinder had earlier announced that his government will fight the " anti-federal and vicious farm laws " tooth and nail through legislative , legal and other routes . He had said some days ago that he would call a special session of the Assembly to bring in necessary amendments to the state laws to negate the " dangerous impact of the central legislations , which are designed to ruin the farmers as well as the state 's agriculture and economy " . The previous session of the 15th @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was passed by a majority to reject the contentious farm legislations including the Farmers ' Produce Trade and Commerce ( Promotion and Facilitation ) , Farmers ( Empowerment and Protection ) Agreement on Price Assurance and Farm Services , and Essential Commodities ( Amendment ) legislations besides the proposed Electricity ( Amendment ) Bill 2020 . Meanwhile , farmers ' organisations from Punjab today boycotted a meeting called by the Union agriculture ministry in New Delhi to resolve their concerns over new farm laws and accused the government of playing double standards with no minister present to hear them out . The representatives from 29 farmers ' organisations , including Joginder Singh-led Bharati Kissan Union , were miffed at the absence of Union agriculture minister Narendra Singh Tomar or even his minister of state for agriculture in the meeting . The meeting was convened at the Krishi Bhavan amid police security to avoid any protest . After the meeting , agitated farmers ' representatives were seen shouting slogans and tearing copies of new farm laws outside Krishi Bhavan . More than 30 representatives , who had come in a bus @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ face masks despite the Covid pandemic . " No proper discussion took place . Neither Union agriculture minister nor junior ministers were present to hear our concerns . We asked why the minister is not meeting us , why the government is playing double standards by calling us here and ministers holding virtual meetings in Punjab . There was no proper response , " Darshan Pal , member of the coordination committee of 29 farmers ' organisations , said after the meeting . Since there was no response from Union agriculture secretary Sanjay Agrawal , who was chairing the meeting , farmers ' organisations decided to boycott the meeting , he said .
