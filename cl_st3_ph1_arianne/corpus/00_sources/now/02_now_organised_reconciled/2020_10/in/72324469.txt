<h> World to reach climate goals by using tenth of pandemic stimulus spend : Study <p> The world could get on track to avert catastrophic climate change by investing a tenth of a planned USD 12 trillion in pandemic recovery packages in reducing dependence on fossil fuels , according to a study published on Thursday . With the stimulus representing about 15 percent of global gross domestic product , or three times the commitment after the 2008 financial crisis , scientists say the money could prove pivotal in meeting the temperature goals of the 2015 Paris Agreement . <p> " It makes absolute sense not just to keep your economy alive with palliative care , but to restructure your economy so it 's future-ready , " Joeri Rogelj , a climate scientist at Imperial College London , and a co-author of the paper told Reuters .
