<h> Behind the science <p> The three Mumbai-based recipients of the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award <p> The three Mumbai-based recipients of the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Award this year discuss their path-breaking research . <p> Even before he became a recipient of the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for 2020 , Kinshuk Dasgupta 's work had put him in the spotlight . A few years ago , the scientific officer at Bhabha Atomic Research Centre and associate professor of engineering sciences at the Homi Bhabha National Institute , had developed the ' Bhabha Kavach ' , a lightweight bulletproof jacket made with hard boron carbide ceramics , carbon nanotubes and composite polymer . Currently , a regular-sized , bullet proof jacket that offers Indian defence forces Level III plus protection -- meaning , it can shield against INSAS rounds as well as hard steel-core bullets -- weighs above 10 kg ; a comparable version of Dasgupta 's jacket only weighs 6.8 kg .
