<h> The Bhilwara principles <p> Today are we doing the right things in the wrong way or the wrong things in the right way ? The lack of accountability is felt most acutely by ordinary citizens , particularly the most vulnerable and marginalised . It takes the form of violation of rights , denial of access , discrimination , deliberate exclusion and democratic marginalisation . <p> We have a muscular optimistic nationalistic government that is aiming for a $5 trillion economy in the midst of dire economic slowdown state of affairs , loss of jobs and rapidly changing world scenario . Add to this climate change , increasing population , high prices , administration and power structures finding multiple ways to circumvent democratic rule as we knew it ! Power is being centralised and federation of States losing their powers with the danger of one party rule . What better than taking the definition of accountability from the very same people suffering from lack of it ! A group of Dalit students from Bhilwara , Rajasthan articulated five ways in which routine engagement with the State accountable to the people , actually results in their disempowerment ! These social accountability principles were acknowledged by the Comptroller and Auditor General ( CAG ) of India , and incorporated as " minimum principles " of social audit . <p> 1 . In spite of social media , mobiles and vast data freely and easily available , there is widening gap between information disclosed and that which is relevant and required in public domain !
