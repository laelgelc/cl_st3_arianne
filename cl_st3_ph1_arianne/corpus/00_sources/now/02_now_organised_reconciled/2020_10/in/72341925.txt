<h> Published : Thursday 22 October 2020 <p> The idea of private investments in forest projects has returned : The Madhya Pradesh government has proposed to hand over acres of degraded forest to corporates for afforestation . <p> The state government based the proposal to hand over 37,420 square kilometres of degraded forest lands to private companies on a concept note ( a summary of the project ) prepared September 23 , 2020 . It was titled : Concept Paper on Private Investment in Rehabilitation of Degraded Forest in Madhya Pradesh . <p> It said the restoration of the degraded forest land would require an investment of Rs 56,130 crore and the government would not be able to afford it on its own . <p> The proposal , however , was mooted by the state Principal Chief Conservator of Forest and forest services chief , Rajesh Srivastava , in a letter to all regional forest department officials on October 20 , 2020 . Srivastava sought comments on the proposal within 15 days . <p> But experts have flagged several legal issues in the proposal . <p> " What is the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ?
