<h> Participants engaged with NASA 's free and open data to address real-world problems on Earth and space <p> Welingkar Institute of Management Development and Research ( WeSchool ) has collaborated with NASA International Space Apps Challenge as an academic partner for conducting the national-level hackathon for the states Maharashtra and Karnataka virtually in the first week of October . The e-inaugural witnessed over 800 participants and respective institutes from Maharashtra , Karnataka , and Telangana . <p> In the ninth edition , the international hackathon invited participant such as coders , scientists , designers , storytellers , makers , builders , technologists , and others in cities across the globe . The participating teams engaged with NASA 's free and open data to address real-world problems on Earth and in space . <p> Space Apps Covid-19 Challenge 2020 , had over 20,000 participants from India in the pre-qualifier round and 100 groups ( each group of 4-6 participants ) contested in the national-level hackathon from across different states in India . <p> Student achievements <p> Two groups from WeSchool were shortlisted in the national top ten .
