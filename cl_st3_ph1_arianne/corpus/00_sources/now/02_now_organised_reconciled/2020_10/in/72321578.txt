<h> Telangana 's heavy showers have sure shot imprint of climate change <h> Published : Wednesday 14 October 2020 <p> An earth mover rescues a family stranded due to floods in Malkajgiri : Telangana has received extremely heavy rainfall since October 13 . Photo : Rachakonda Police @RachakondaCop / Twitter <p> The unusually excessive rainfall that Telangana has received since October 13 , 2020 may have been due to global warming . <p> The main cause of the rainfall is a deep depression which de-intensified into a depression and currently lies over north interior Karnataka and some nearby areas of Maharashtra and Telangana , according to the India Meteorological Department ( IMD ) . <p> IMD predicts that the intensity of the system will further decrease to a low pressure area by the evening of October 14 . A deep depression is a cyclonic circulation that carries a lot of rainfall and has moderate wind speeds just less than a cyclone . <p> The current system had formed as a low pressure area in the Andaman Sea . Many observers have claimed that they have never witnessed such rainfall in their lifetimes . <p> Fifteen people have lost their lives in rain-related incidents in the city in the last two days , according to media reports . The city , as a whole , received 37.3 mm absolute rainfall October 13 , which is 18 times the normal for this time of the year . <p> Many places inside the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation ( GHMC ) limits such as Singapur township received 320 mm of rainfall October 13 .
