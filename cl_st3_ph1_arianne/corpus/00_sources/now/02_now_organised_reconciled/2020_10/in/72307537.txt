<h> The Quad and the Indo-Pacific <p> Despite all the hype , the Quad ministerial meeting in Tokyo turned out to be predictable and fairly routine . The meeting , hosted by the Japanese Foreign Minister Toshimitsu Motegi , US Secretary of State Mike Pompeo , Australian Foreign Minister Marise Payne and the External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar , was significant since it was the first " stand-alone " one , as compared to the first which was held on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly session last year . <p> The meet has come at a time when the Indo-Pacific region , along with the rest of the world , has been reeling from the impact of the Covid-19 pandemic . Simultaneously , China 's aggressiveness has been manifested in the straits of Taiwan , South China Sea , eastern Ladakh and , of course , Hong Kong .
