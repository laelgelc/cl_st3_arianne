<h> Daily Court Digest : Major environment orders ( October 9 , 2020 ) <h> Last Updated : Friday 09 October 2020 <p> Mining in Tikola <p> No mining operation was noticed at Tikola village in Sonipat district , the Haryana State Pollution Control Board ( HSPCB ) said in its report to the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) . Mining activities were closed due to the rainy season . <p> The HSPCB report was in compliance of the NGT order to look into the construction of a temporary bridge by construction designing firm DSP Associates , Sonipat , for the purpose of mining activities , in violation of conditions of EC . <p> DSP Associates was issued a Letter of Intent by the Mining and Geology Department , Haryana .
