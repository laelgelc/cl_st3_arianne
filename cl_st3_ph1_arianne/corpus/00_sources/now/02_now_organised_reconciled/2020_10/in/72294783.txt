<h> Are India 's Social Protection Schemes ' Future Fit ' ? <p> As with the rest of the world , 2020 will remain etched in India 's memory . We have the world 's second highest Covid-19 cases and what that really means to a poor country like ours , how devastating and far-reaching its impact will be are yet unknown . <p> As the virus has raged , the damage from climate change has been alarming . Unseasonal heavy rains and strong winds have brought the worst locust attack in decades , a warming Indian Ocean has led to two deadly cyclones , heat waves have gripped the nation and flooding has killed hundreds . The calamities are many . <p> What 's worse , India 's first ever climate change assessment report predicts more disasters to come . By century-end , India 's average temperature is projected to rise by 4.4 ? C , heat waves are expected to increase 3 to 4 times in frequency , tropical cyclones are set to grow in intensity and sea levels to rise by 30 cm . <p> The cumulative @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ India 's extreme poor deeper into misery . <p> The pandemic response has demonstrated the significance of social protection schemes such as Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme ( MGNREGS ) for channeling relief . At the time of a crisis , such schemes provide a ' safety net ' by guaranteeing 100 days of employment to rural households . The government almost doubled the MGNREGS budget from INR 61,000 crores to 101,000 crores in 2020-21 and the scheme has already generated 1.25 billion days ' wages in the first quarter of this year . <p> But high demand is slow to be met due to weak delivery systems and low capacity . Four key measures would ensure social protection schemes like MGNREGS are fit for dealing with future crises , particularly those relating to climate change . <p> The Infrastructure for Climate Resilience Growth ( ICRG ) programme in the states of Bihar , Chhattisgarh and Odisha aims to strengthen capacity and technical support for designing natural resource management assets to help communities build climate resilient water harvesting , land development and soil conservation infrastructure . While the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ unfold , a technical evaluation is already showing positive results including stronger community engagement , better understanding of climate risks and assets that are climate responsive , more durable and easier to use . Farmers have been able to double their crops and continue irrigation even under drought , helping to increase income , savings and to repay debts . <p> ICRG programme is a product of the collaboration between the UK and the Indian government and is supported by its research partner , the International Institute for Environment and Development ( IIED ) . <p> MGNREGS provides an additional 50 days wage employment when a climate crisis ( such as drought or flood ) is declared , which can help families cope and recover during the most critical of periods . But recent IIED research found there are four to six months delays in governments officially declaring drought -- which delays the additional 50 days being distributed . <p> If forecasts were used to predict weather and climate hazards , the 50 days ' wages could be provided in advance . With monetary reserves , households are more able @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ able to rebuild assets , repay loans and buy insurance . Evidence shows every dollar invested in actions before disaster strikes saves US$ 4 -- 7 in relief costs . <p> How can this be done ? A forecast-based delivery model with a decision support tool can help the government to sanction anticipatory wage employment . It can use weather forecasts to anticipate climate hazards and define thresholds that would trigger the distribution of additional wage employment days . <p> The state can help a community at risk to better prepare and cope immediate crisis with assured wage earnings . It can help them plan appropriate soil and moisture conservation and harvesting structures using future climate change forecasts that can disaster-proof their agriculture production . <p> IIED is working with national and state level meteorological and planning entities to pilot such a model in Madhya Pradesh , under the ICRG programme . In due course it will be looking to scale up the model in other states . <p> ' Convergence ' is a focus area for MGNREGS , whereby the government encourages MGNREGS to integrate with other development schemes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ better development outcomes and more effective climate risk management . For example , convergence of farm pond activity from MGNREGS combined with irrigation infrastructure , better seeds and market access from the government 's agriculture and other departments can help farmers increase their income and resilience . However , ground-level execution of such cross sectoral planning and delivery is missing ; more government coordination is needed . <p> The MGNREGS safety net is only accessible to people living in their native village -- if displaced or forced to migrate they are not covered by social protection programs and often have to work in sub-human conditions to survive , stripped of rights , benefits and entitlements . <p> Social protection schemes should provide all citizens -- migrants or not -- with the basic rights to secure work , housing and sanitation . Schemes like MGNREGS need to be redesigned , so that they do not operate just under rural silos and cover facilities that help families cope and survive under climate induced duress .
