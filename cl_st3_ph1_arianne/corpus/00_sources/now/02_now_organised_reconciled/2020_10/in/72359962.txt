<h> PCMC will connect all its 184 gardens by walkways , cycle over next 10 years <p> Civic body has invited suggestions from public till Nov 15 ; experts to deliberate on plan later <p> The Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation ( PCMC ) will be the first civic body in the country to start the ' Connecting Green ' project , under which all of its 184 gardens and few green zones will be connected through pedestrian and cycle routes . The ambitious project is touted take a decade to complete . <p> PCMC chief Shravan Hardikar announced the work on the project during a press meet on Thursday . Taking a citizen-centric stance , the local body will invite suggestions and feedback till November 15 and then will prepare a draft plan with the help of experts , planners , architects , and non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) . <p> Hardikar deemed the project important for many reasons . Primarily , he believes that the living conditions will be far from ideal over the next 20 years given the present pace of development .
