<p> BENGALURU : IT firm Infosys Ltd on Wednesday said it has turned carbon neutral thirty years ahead of th 2050 timeline set by the Paris Agreement . <p> " Infosys is now carbon neutral in compliance with PAS 2060 standards , " the Bengaluru-headquartered company said . <p> Over the past years , Infosys said it has reduced its per capita electricity consumption by over 55% with the ambition to transition to renewable energy . Infosys was also India 's first signatory to the RE100 , a global initiative by companies committed to using 100% renewable energy . <p> Infosys said it has successfully developed a portfolio of community-based carbon offset projects focusing on socio-economic development of rural communities and contributions to the UN Sustainable Development Goals . The projects not only address climate change , but also benefit over 100,000 rural families . <p> " Longevity and success for a company comes from living in harmony with the context in which it operates .
