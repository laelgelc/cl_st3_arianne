<h> ( BW ) ( PA-TRINSEO ) <h> Trinseo <h> Trinseo Forms Commercial Partnership with Tyre Recycling Solutions <h> Partnership will accelerate the development of sustainable synthetic rubber tire formulations , through increased use of recycled tires <p> Berwyn , Pa. , United States : <p> Trinseo ( NYSE : TSE ) , a global materials solutions provider and manufacturer of plastics , latex binders and synthetic rubber , has reached definitive agreements with Tyre Recycling Solutions ( TRS ) on a commercial collaboration and an equity investment in TRS . The transaction is expected to close later this quarter , subject to ordinary closing conditions . <p> The new collaboration agreement will see the two companies collaborate on research and development , pooling their combined technology expertise to help global tire manufacturers develop more sustainable tire formulations . Trinseo and TRS share the long-term goal of developing new value-creating systems for the manufacture of tires that reduce environmental footprint and create a sustainable outlet for end of life tires . <p> " This collaboration is extremely important for developing real circular solutions for the tire and technical rubber goods @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the tire industry , we are committed to helping our customers achieve their sustainability goals and believe that the most successful way to do this is through collaboration across the value chain . TRS has developed proprietary technologies to process end-of-life tires and deliver output products with higher circular economical value .
