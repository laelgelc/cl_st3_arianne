Dated : 10/2/2020 11:15:30 AM <h> CAG points out non-observance of guidelines , deviation from objectives <p> JAMMU , Oct 1 : The Comptroller and Auditor General of India has questioned poor performance of Jammu &amp; Kashmir authorities under the National Afforestation Programme.A performance audit of the NAP for the period between 2011-12 to 2016-17 has brought to fore that programme implementation had suffered in J&amp;K due to non-observance of guidelines and deviation from the objectives during execution of various activities and there was shortfall in achievement of targets under plantation . " The overall forest cover to geographical area in the State decreased from 16.09 per cent in 2009 to 15.78 per cent in 2015 . Against target of 66 per cent of geographical area in hills of the State to be covered under forest and tree cover as per National Forest Policy 1988 , only 24.02 per cent was under forest and tree cover , leaving a shortfall of 41.98 per cent of the envisaged goal , " reads the report which was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the 10th Five Year Plan by merging four Centrally Sponsored Afforestation Schemes of the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change .
