<h> Solar power plants using the rivers in Manipur <p> 20-Oct-2020 <p> <p> Prof I Tomba SinghWith the rapid depletion of fossil fuel reserves , it is feared that the world will soon run out of its energy resources . Energy has an established positive correlation with human population and economic growth . Primary energy requirements are expected to increase manifolds in the years to come . Providing adequate , affordable and clean energy is a prerequisite for eradicating poverty and improving productivity . The inevitable increase in the use of fossil fuels alongside a country 's economic growth presents associated side effects of threat to the Nation 's energy security as well as environmental degradation through climate change . A feasible alternative to the indiscriminate burning of fossil fuels lies in the accelerated use of renewable energy . Major types of renewable energy sources include solar , wind , hydro and biomass , all of which have huge potential to meet future energy challenges . This form of renewable energy occupies less space compared to the space occupied by hydropower projects . The amount of solar energy incident on the earth 's surface has been estimated approximately 1.5x10x18 KWH/year which is about 10,000 times the current annual energy consumption of the entire world . In fact , a standalone solar photovoltaic system does not provide a continuous supply of energy due to reasonable and periodic variations .
