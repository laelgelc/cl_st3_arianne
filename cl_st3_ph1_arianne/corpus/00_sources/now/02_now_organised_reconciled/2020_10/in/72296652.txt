<h> UNPREDICTABLE BUT FATHOMABLE AND A FUTURE UNCERTAIN <p> Alexandre Moniz Barbosa <p> The monsoon of 2020 will probably be remembered for a long time as the wettest in living memory . As Goa struggled with the COVID-19 pandemic that has resolutely refused to abate and a new normal in living and working conditions due to this virus , it also had to contend with the wettest monsoon in a hundred years that could be a harbinger of a new normal in weather conditions -- changing patterns . The State ended the four month period of June to September with 41 per cent more rain than the normal , as the rain guages recorded the seasonal rainfall at 4201.7 mm or 165.4 inches . The last time the State had come anywhere close to this figure was in 1961 , exactly 59 years ago when the rainfall had been counted up to 160 inches . <p> This has been indeed a high amount of rain , as the average for Goa is 2975.6 mm or 117.14 inches , and though the season has officially ended , the withrawal of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more rain in the current month . It has been above normal six times , below normal at other times , but the oscilation in the amount of rainfall has been high .
