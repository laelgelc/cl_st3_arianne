<h> NATO allies to stand together regardless of US election result : Stoltenberg <p> ANI Updated : Oct 21 , 2020 16:17 IST <p> Washington US , October 21 ( ANI/Sputnik ) : NATO allies will stand together regardless of who wins the upcoming US presidential election , Secretary-General Jens Stoltenberg said Wednesday . <p> " Regardless of differences we see on burden-sharing or trade , climate change , and all the issues , is that NATO allies always are able to stand united and stand together on the main tasks of NATO , that is to protect and defend each other . And that is exactly what we have done over the last years and I am absolutely certain that regardless of the outcome of the US election that will also be the case , " Stoltenberg told a press conference . ( ANI/Sputnik )
