<h> Final debate offers Trump a late chance to reshape presidential race <p> President Donald Trump gets a final chance to make his case for re-election before a vast audience of Americans when he squares off against Democratic rival Joe Biden on Thursday in their last debate before the Nov. 3 vote . <p> The televised meeting comes as Trump badly needs to alter the trajectory of the race . He trails Biden significantly in national polls less than two weeks before Election Day , though the contest is much tighter in some key battleground states . <p> Opinion polls show there are relatively few undecided voters . A record 42 million Americans have already cast ballots ahead of the debate in Nashville , Tennessee , meaning Trump 's window to influence the outcome of the race may be closing . <p> The contentious first debate between Biden and Trump was watched by at least 73 million viewers . Trump passed up a second debate after it was switched to a virtual format in the wake of his COVID-19 diagnosis . <p> The illness kept Trump , a Republican , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and he has been furiously been trying to make up ground since , sometimes holding two rallies a day . <p> His travel schedule has been heavily focused on states , including Arizona , Florida , North Carolina and Pennsylvania , where polls show the race is the closest and where winning could allow him to squeeze out a tight victory in the U.S. Electoral College . <p> The latest Reuters/Ipsos poll showed Biden up by nine points nationally , down from 12 points during the first week of October . He retained a sizeable edge in the competitive states of Michigan and Wisconsin . <p> To make up ground in the debate , Trump likely will have to modify his behavior from the first face-off , in which he repeatedly interrupted Biden , attacked him personally and showed little respect for the moderator . Swing voters , particularly women , were turned off by his actions , post-debate polls and focus groups showed . <p> Michael Steel , a Republican who served as a top aide to then House Speaker John Boehner , said Trump needs to take a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " There is probably nothing President Trump can do in terms of attacking Biden that he did n't already try in the first debate , " Steel said . " He now needs to rope-a-dope , laying back and acting presidential while needling Biden to flub -- and flub badly . " <p> This time around , each candidate 's microphone will be switched off while his opponent makes his two-minute introductory statement on a topic , so as to give him uninterrupted speaking time . Both microphones will then be active for the discussion period that follows . <p> Debate topics will include the pandemic , race relations , climate change and national security . The Trump campaign argued that the entire debate should be focused on foreign policy . <p> " I think the mute is very unfair , " Trump told reporters on Wednesday . " And I think it 's very bad that they 're not talking about foreign affairs . " <p> Trump is expected to return to a line of attack that surfaced in the first debate , accusing Biden and his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ business dealings in Ukraine while his father was vice president . No evidence has been verified that support the corruption allegations by Trump and his aides , and Biden in the first debate called them false and discredited . <p> Beyond holding rallies , the president has been spending much of the week leading up to the debate looking to pick fights . He criticized his top expert on the coronavirus , Dr. Anthony Fauci , as well as NBC journalist Kristen Welker , who will moderate the debate . Welker will be the first Black woman to moderate a presidential debate in almost 30 years . <p> Biden spent the week off the campaign trail preparing for the debate . He is expected to continue to bash Trump 's handling of the pandemic with cases on the rise in several states , while arguing that the outbreak has had a disproportionate economic impact on low-wage workers . The pressure will be on the former vice president to deliver a disciplined performance to appeal to the small sliver of voters who have yet to make up their minds .
