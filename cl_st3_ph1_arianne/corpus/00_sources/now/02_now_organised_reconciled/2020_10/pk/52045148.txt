<p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The experts have stressed the need for taking immediate actions to protect the food system to produce enough food in quantity and quality . <p> While addressing the COMSTECH virtual seminar on World Food Day on the theme of " grow , nourish and sustain together " organized in collaboration with UPSIGN and Pakistan Academy of Sciences , the panelists urged that the COVID pandemic has already posed a serious threat to our food system ; we need to develop resilient and holistic food system to fight hunger . <p> The panelists warned that the food system is facing tremendous pressure from climate change causing the depleting natural resources , soil degradation , water shortage , and increasing threat from invasive species of pests and diseases . <p> It was noted that the new varieties have a low update of trace elements and essential minerals that needs to be addressed . <p> A panel of distinguished scholars and policymakers who participated in this seminar included Dr. Tina Barsby OBE , Director and Chief Executive Officer , National Institute of Agricultural Botany ( NIAB ) , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Genetic Engineering ( NIBGE ) , Professor Iqra Ahmed Khan , former VC of the University of Agriculture , Faisalabad . <p> Shandana Gulzar Khan , Chairperson of the Commonwealth Women Parliamentarians ( CWP ) network and chair of Agri products committee in National Assembly of Pakistan informed that the government of Pakistan is keen to develop a long-term strategy to protect its natural resources , especially soil and water for the coming generations in her closing remarks . <p> Professor Lal stressed the need of teaching our young generation to protect our soil 's health , which is living and improve organic contents , through the restoration of soil health , we can improve human health . Resilient systems are those that can bounce back , we need to encourage urban agriculture . Good food is a good medicine , improving nutritional value . He quoted verses from the Holy Quran meaning " Eat lawful and what is good on earth " . <p> Dr. Tina Barsby said , " If the world is to grow enough food for the projected global population in 2050 , agricultural productivity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ may need to grow more than double . Maize , Rice , Wheat and Soybean are only increasing by about 0.9% to 1.6% a year . We need to increase it to 2.4% per year to double yield by 2050 " . <p> Dr. Shahid Mansoor emphasized the need of adopting the speed breeding technologies , which is a combination of red and blue lights to reduce generation time ( 5-6 generations in a year instead of one or two ) , reduce breeding time 2-3 years , adopting genomics/genomic selection and use of genetic markers to select desirable traits ( such as yield , disease resistance , nutritional value , quality ) both for crops and livestock . <p> Professor Iqrar Khan alluded the need of soil stewardship schemes especially the inclusion of legumes in the cropping system . <p> Dr. Khalid Mahmood thanked the speakers on behalf of UPSIGN , COMSTECH , and Pakistan Academy of Sciences .
