<h> HEC 's PERIDOT Research Funding now also open for faculty members <p> The Higher Education Commission ( HEC ) of Pakistan has now opened their PERIDOT Research funding program for full-time faculty members of both the public and private sectors . <p> These faculty members must , however , possess PhD degrees . <p> The PERIDOT program , first launched in 2014 , is a joint venture between Pakistan and France , under the Hubert Curien partnership . It aims to support he development of new scientific research and technological cooperation between the two countries . <p> The 6th phase of the project this year , has prioritised the following categories : <p> Health <p> Human and Social Sciences <p> Environmental Sciences ( Water , Climate Change , Pollution , etc ) <p> Selected research projects will be funded by the program five times a year for the next three years . <p> The deadline for proposal submission has been set for the 7th of December 2020 .
