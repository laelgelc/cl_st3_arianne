<p> GENEVA : Climate change is largely to blame for a near doubling of natural disasters in the past 20 years , the United Nations said on Monday . <p> The UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction said 7,348 major disaster events had occurred between 2000 and 2019 , claiming 1.23 lives , affecting 4.2 billion people and costing the global economy some $2.97 trillion . <p> The figure far outstrips the 4,212 major natural disasters recorded between 1980 and 1999 , the UN office said in a new report entitled " The Human Cost of Disasters 2000-2019 " . <p> The sharp increase was largely attributable to a rise in climate-related disasters , including extreme weather events like floods , drought and storms , the report said . <p> Extreme heat is proving especially deadly . <p> " We are wilfully destructive , " UNDRR chief Mami Mizutori told reporters in a virtual briefing .
