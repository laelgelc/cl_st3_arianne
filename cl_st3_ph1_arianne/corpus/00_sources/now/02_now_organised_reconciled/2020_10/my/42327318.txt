<p> We 've all dreamt about accomplishing something great but this 9-year-old will totally amaze you as he 's actually got a long list of achievements -- especially this recent one ! <p> UNICEF recently announced the top 20 finalists for their very first Climate Comic Contest . Children and youth were invited to design and submit comic characters with special powers to fight climate change , and Emerson was one of them ! Way to go Emerson ! <p> Emerson 's submission , Earth Boy , is the ONLY Malaysian submission selected as a finalist among almost 3,000 submissions from over 99 countries in a comic contest organised by UNICEF . Malaysia represent ! <p> Earth Boy , along with 19 other characters , will be featured on the Climate Comic Contest website where members of the public can vote for their favourite characters from now until 17 November . The character with the most votes will be announced the winner and go on to be featured in a full-length professional comic book !
