<p> BANK Pembangunan Malaysia Bhd ( BPMB ) has become an official signatory to the United Nations Environment Programme Finance Initiative ( UNEP FI ) Principles for Responsible Banking ( PRB ) . <p> PRB is a single framework for a sustainable banking industry developed through an innovative partnership between banks worldwide and UNEP FI . <p> BPMB president and group CEP Arshad Mohamed Ismail said the bank is proud to be one of two Malaysian banks and the first among the country 's development financial institutions to commit to the UNEP FI 's framework . <p> " The bank is committed to be Malaysia 's leading developmental partner while supporting the nation 's future progress through digitalisation , sustainability and inclusivity . <p> " In this regard , BPMB will assess projects to ensure they positively impact the people , environment and national development , " he said in a statement yesterday . <p> The PRB set out the banking industry 's roles and responsibilities in shaping a sustainable future and in aligning the banking sector with the objectives of the UN Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the focus of the PRB , banks can identify areas where they can make the most impact for a better economy and world . <p> It also provides banks with an avenue to leverage new business opportunities with the emergence of sustainable economic growth . <p> The UNEP FI membership allows BPMB to contribute to its collaborative programmes through which the bank can develop leadership initiatives , guidelines and resources to align the banking sector with the UN Paris Agreement on Climate Change and SDG . <p> The bank is owned by Minister of Finance Inc and has been accorded the highest rating " AAA " by both RAM Rating Services Bhd and Malaysia Rating Corp Bhd . It is regulated and supervised by Bank Negara Malaysia under the Development Financial Institution Act 2002 . <p> With a group total assets value of RM25 billion ( as at Dec 31 , 2019 ) , the BPMB Group is well-positioned to meet the financing and business development needs of Malaysian businesses .
