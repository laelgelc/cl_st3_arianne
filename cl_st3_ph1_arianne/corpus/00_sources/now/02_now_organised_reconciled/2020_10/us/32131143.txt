Of these , the nominate subspecies , Struthio camelus camelus , commonly known as the North African red-necked ostrich , is the largest . Adult males grow to 9 feet and 340 pounds , making them the largest birds on Earth . <p> They 're also among the rarest . The North African red-necked ostrich is classified as " critically endangered " by IUCN , and it 's listed on CITES Appendix I. It once inhabited 18 countries in northern Africa , from Senegal on the Atlantic clear across to Sudan on the Red Sea . Today , remnant populations are identified in only four countries ( Cameroon , Chad , Central African Republic and Senegal ) . These populations are fragmented and there is doubt whether any of them is viable . <p> The ostriches were exterminated for several greedy reasons @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ women 's fashions . That survey detected several ostrich footprints , a few fecal samples , and one feather . This project is being supported by Friends of Animals , a US-based NGO which has worked with both the Senegalese and Israelis for more than 30 years . <p> The project intends to ship ostrich eggs from Israel 's Hai-Bar Wildlife Reserve to Senegal 's Ferlo North Wildlife Reserve .
