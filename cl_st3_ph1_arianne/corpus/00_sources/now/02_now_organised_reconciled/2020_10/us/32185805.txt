<h> LES to follow national trend increasing fixed portion of bills <p> In future years , turning off lights and buying more efficient appliances will mean less savings for customers as Lincoln Electric Service shifts the way it charges by increasing fixed service costs . <p> LES , like many other electric utilities , is planning to gradually increase the fixed portion customers pay each month and decrease the rate it charges for electricity . <p> The trend has consumer watchdogs and conservation advocates sounding the alarm . Critics warn it hurts low energy users , many of whom are cash strapped , and that it could make efforts to reduce carbon emissions more expensive . <p> Activists held a rally Tuesday in Omaha and delivered petitions to the Omaha Public Power District opposing plans there to increase residential fixed service fees 341 percent from the current $10.25 per month to $35 by 2019 . <p> The Omaha-based utility recently scaled back its plans , and its board is expected to approve plans Thursday to raise the fixed portion of residential customers ' bills to $30 over the next three @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ same period . To soften the blow for low-income , low-use customers , OPPD plans to offer a credit of $2.50 to $7.50 a month . <p> OPPD has said the restructuring is necessary in a changing industry facing new carbon sequestration regulations and declines in the average usage per customer . <p> With unanimous approval by the LES board and the Lincoln City Council , LES is increasing its fixed rate for residential customers in 2016 from $11.15 to $13.40 . <p> LES plans to continue hiking the fixed rate , LES director of finance and rates Emily Koenig said in an interview . <p> She said increasing the fixed portion of bills makes rates more equal because under the current structure , higher energy users are subsidizing the fixed cost of infrastructure to provide electricity to those who use less . <p> " We want to make sure we 're recovering the costs that are incurred to serve our customers from the appropriate customers and there is n't subsidization between customers , " Koenig said . <p> At what speed and to what extent LES will shift its @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ move will be revenue neutral for LES . <p> " We do not have a set timeframe or methodology for doing it yet , " she said . Spokeswoman Kelley Porter said officials in Lincoln are aware of arguments being made by those who oppose OPPD 's plans . <p> The shift in rate structure favors those who use more electricity as the cost of using electricity declines . <p> That 's a move in the wrong direction for conservation and fighting global climate change , said Holly Bender , deputy director of the Sierra Club 's Beyond Coal campaign , which has been active in opposing the OPPD rate restructuring . <p> " LES and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efficiency and demand reduction programs .
