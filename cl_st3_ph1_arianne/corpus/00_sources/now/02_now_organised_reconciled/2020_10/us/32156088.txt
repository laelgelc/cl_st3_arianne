<p> Every morning , wildland firefighters gather around radios to listen to the weather forecast . This summer , I was part of the team that fought a fire near Big Sur . When I heard the staticky voice announce that temperatures would exceed 105 degrees , the forecast sounded like a death sentence . <p> Across California , unprecedented heat has made wildfires more difficult to predict and control . During the heat wave in Big Sur , the fire , which had been 40% contained at 30,000 acres , tripled in size in a matter of days . It has now burned nearly 125,000 acres . <p> We have entered the age of megafires . Since 1970 , yearly fire seasons in California have grown by 78 days . The amount of land burned annually across the Western U.S. has doubled since 1980 .
