<h> Poet Jane Hirshfield on bearing witness to the beauty of an imperiled world <p> In the midst of a global pandemic , what do we need from poetry ? Should the art form provide a vehicle of solace and escape , or a spur toward engagement and deeper grappling with the multiple crises of the moment ? <p> The distinguished American poet Jane Hirshfield declines to choose . Born and educated on the East Coast , she later migrated west and trained extensively at the San Francisco Zen Center . In language of uncanny lyrical precision , her work redraws boundaries between the self and the natural world . And her poetic vision could not be more timely : The most recent of her nine poetry collections , " Ledger , " was published just as the pandemic closures forced a turn inward while also prompting a reckoning with the planet 's fundamental interconnectedness .
