Today , the passage to adulthood is far longer and less uniform than it was during the 1950s or 1960s . No longer can most young people achieve the markers of adulthood during their 20s : a secure job , a steady relationship and a stable place of residence . <p> Like many other aspects of contemporary life , a secure adulthood is becoming a class privilege . <p> What we need to do sounds straightforward but is in fact enormously challenging .
