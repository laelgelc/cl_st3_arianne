In our new series " Imagining the Next Future , " Polygon explores the new era of science fiction -- in movies , books , TV , games , and beyond -- to see how storytellers and innovators are imagining the next 10 , 20 , 50 , or 100 years during a moment of extreme uncertainty . Follow along as we deep dive into the great unknown . <p> To make the understatement of the year : The future of humanity is uncertain .
