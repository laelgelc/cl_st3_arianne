While they were originally developed to replace ozone-depleting substances that are being phased out under the Montreal Protocol , many HFCs are potent greenhouse gases with a global warming potential up to 12,400 times that of CO2 over a 100-year period . <p> The Kigali Amendment to the Montreal Protocol , which entered into force in 2019 , aims to phase down the consumption of HFCs by 2050 .
