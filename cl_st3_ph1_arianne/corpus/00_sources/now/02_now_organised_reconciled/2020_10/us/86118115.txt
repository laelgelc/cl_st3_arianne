<h> Return of the non-soon : Arizona 's second dry monsoon season breaks records <p> Arizonans might have noticed a conspicuous lack of thunderstorms and rainfall this summer . That 's because Arizona has just experienced its driest monsoon season on record with an average of just 1.51 inches of rainfall across the state . <p> It 's not a coincidence that it was also one of the hottest summers on record . <p> Typically , monsoon season in Arizona lasts between the months of June and September , bringing " us our cool relief , " in the hot summer months , said Nancy Selover , state climatologist at the Arizona State Climate Office . The longterm average rainfall in the state is 5.37 inches . <p> " Those thunderstorms tend to cool the temperatures down when they happen ... and when that does n't happen we end up staying hotter , " she said . <p> Selover calls monsoon seasons that do n't produce much rain " dry monsoons , " and 2020 was the second consecutive " dry " year in a row . <p> Multiple years @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Arizona 's wildlands .
