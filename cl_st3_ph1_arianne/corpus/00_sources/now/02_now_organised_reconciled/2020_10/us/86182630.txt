<h> Long-Lost Tectonic Plate Discovered Hundreds of Miles Below Canada <p> A team of scientists say they have uncovered evidence of a mysterious tectonic plate beneath northern Canada that some experts argue never existed . <p> In a study published in the Geological Society of America Bulletin , the researchers from the University of Houston identified the remains of the ancient plate -- which had mostly disappeared by around 40 million years ago -- hundreds of miles beneath Canada 's Yukon territory . <p> Whether or not the plate -- dubbed " Resurrection " -- ever existed has long been a hot topic of debate in the field of geology . <p> Tectonic plates are vast slabs of the planet 's crust , which are in constant , albeit very gradual , motion .
