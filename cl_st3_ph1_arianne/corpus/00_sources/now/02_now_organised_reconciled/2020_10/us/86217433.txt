<h> Global China : Global governance and norms <p> China 's efforts to secure a larger role for itself in multiple international institutions have generated questions about the scale of its ambitions and the tools it will use to advance them . From human rights to energy to trade , China 's growing weight in the international system is bending institutions , rules , and norms in its preferred directions . At the same time , in other areas , such as internationalization of the renminbi and international law , China 's aspirations continue to exceed its impacts . <p> The papers in this final installment of the Brookings Foreign Policy project " Global China : Assessing China 's Growing Role in the World " examine China 's approach to global governance , and specifically to China 's efforts to influence institutions , norms , and rules at the heart of the modern international system .
