<h> Harris Says Climate Plan Would Add Jobs , Not Eliminate Them <p> Vice President Mike Pence , echoing President Trump , accused the Democratic nominees in Wednesday 's debate of supporting the Green New Deal concept pitched by liberals in Congress and of planning to ban the drilling technique known as fracking . Sen. Kamala Harris denied those claims , but her campaign 's climate plan does support elements of the Green New Deal and of banning fracking in some cases . <p> " It 's essentially the same plan , " Mr. Pence said . " You put your radical environmental agenda ahead of American workers and ahead of American jobs . " <p> The Biden-Harris plan would end fracking for drilling only on federally-owned land , which is just a small part of oil and gas drilling in the U.S. Ms. Harris said climate change is an existential threat to humanity , and the campaign has said such a ban is an important step to help reduce the greenhouse-gas emissions that cause climate change . <p> Their plan also rejects many of the mandates of the Green @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the platform 's concept of merging climate policy with economic policy in an attempt to boost employment . Mr. Biden wants to spend $2 trillion to help shift the country 's economy to cleaner energy that lowers greenhouse-gas emissions , saying that infrastructure spending will also create jobs , especially for union laborers . <p> It 's a fine line they have to walk , an effort to convince voters that aggressive regulation and government intervention in the economy are more likely to create jobs than to reduce them . Ms. Harris cited Moody 's Analytics estimates from last month that the Biden economic plan would create 7 million more jobs than the Trump campaign 's plan . <p> " Part of those jobs that will be created by Joe Biden are going to be about clean energy and renewable energy , " she said . " They do n't believe in science . And Joe 's plan is about saying we 're going to deal with it , " referring to climate change , " but we 're also going to create jobs . "
