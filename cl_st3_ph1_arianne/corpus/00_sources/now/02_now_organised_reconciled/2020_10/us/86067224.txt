<h> Sunday Reading : The Global Climate Crisis <p> Climate change threatens the natural world , our world , the only one we have , and yet you would hardly know it from the way it was treated at the first Presidential debate -- particularly by the President . But attention must be paid . What is more important ? For nearly sixty years , The New Yorker has published groundbreaking coverage of environmental issues and the growing crisis of climate change . From Rachel Carson 's " Silent Spring , " published in 1962 , to Elizabeth Kolbert 's " Climate of Man " series , from 2005 , the magazine has brought awareness to the urgency of global warming and the rise in pollution . This week , we 're releasing " The Fragile Earth , " a new anthology filled with some of the best pieces we 've published in the magazine on this crucial subject . To accompany its publication , we 're bringing you a selection of stories from our archive on the environment and the global climate crisis .
