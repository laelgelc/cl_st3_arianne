<h> PG&amp;E to cut power to over 1 million people in California <p> SAN FRANCISCO -- Pacific Gas &amp; Electric will cut power to over 1 million people on Sunday to prevent the chance of sparking wildfires as extreme fire weather returns to the region , the utility announced Friday . <p> The nation 's largest utility said it will black out customers in 38 counties -- including most of the San Francisco Bay Area -- as weather forecasts called for a return of bone-dry , gusty weather that carries the threat of downing or fouling power lines or other equipment that in recent years have been blamed for igniting massive and deadly blazes in central and Northern California . <p> The safety shutoffs were expected to begin as early as Sunday morning and last into Tuesday , affecting 466,000 homes and businesses , or more than 1 million residents assuming between two and three people per home or business customer . <p> Cuts are predicted to encompass parts of the Sacramento Valley , the northern and central Sierra Nevada , upper elevations of the San Francisco Bay Area , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of southern Kern County . <p> The shutoffs included 19,000 customers in parts of Butte County , where a 2018 blaze ignited by PG&amp;E equipment destroyed much of the town of Paradise and killed 85 people . <p> Forecasts call for the " the driest humidity levels and the strongest winds of the wildfire season thus far , " a PG&amp;E statement said . <p> The National Weather Service issued red flag warnings for many areas , predicting winds of 35 mph ( 56 kph ) or higher in San Francisco and lower elevations and up to 70 mph ( 113 kph ) in some mountains .
