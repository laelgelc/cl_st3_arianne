<p> Though the biggest energy headline coming out of last week 's U.S. presidential debate was whether a President Biden would end fracking , President Trump 's critical portrayal of renewable energy warrants more attention than it has gotten -- and a closer look at the reality of growth in the sector . <p> During the debate , Trump argued that renewable energy is too expensive , would n't power up America 's " beautiful factories " , and is bad for birds . In addition to being seriously outdated , this view flies in the face of capital flows in the energy sector . Renewables-rich electric utilities are on the ascendency at the same time that oil and gas company valuations are in serious decline .
