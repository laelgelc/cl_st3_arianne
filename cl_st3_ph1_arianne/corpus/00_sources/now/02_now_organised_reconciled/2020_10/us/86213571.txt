At the same time , it can be pushed too far . The promises of political leaders have real-world effects that we 're already seeing . On the path to getting the binding and comprehensive emissions policies that the world needs , there will be plenty of partial , vague and unenforceable pledges . Each of them , though , sets a new baseline that will help create the conditions for further , more ambitious policies . <p> Take the broadly accepted target that the world must stabilize atmospheric carbon dioxide at or below 450 parts per million .
