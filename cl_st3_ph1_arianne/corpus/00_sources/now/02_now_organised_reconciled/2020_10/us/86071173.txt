<p> WASHINGTON , D.C. -- As the nation remains in a pandemic-induced recession , U.S. registered voters say the economy is the most important issue of 16 that may potentially affect their choice for president . Nearly nine in 10 registered voters consider the presidential candidates ' positions on the economy " extremely " ( 44% ) or " very " ( 45% ) important to their vote . <p> At least three-quarters of voters consider six other issues to be important to their vote choice -- terrorism and national security ( 83% ) , education ( 82% ) , healthcare ( 80% ) , crime ( 79% ) , the response to the coronavirus ( 77% ) , and race relations ( 76% ) . <p> Bar graphs .
