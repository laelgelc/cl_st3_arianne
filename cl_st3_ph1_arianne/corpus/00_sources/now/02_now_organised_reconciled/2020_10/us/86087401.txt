<h> Unemployment is world 's biggest risk , business leaders say <p> FILE PHOTO : People attend a makeshift application center to apply for a commercial vehicle driving position in Stafford , Virginia <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - Unemployment is seen as the biggest worry over the next 10 years for business executives around the world , closely followed by concern about the spread of infectious diseases , according to a survey by the World Economic Forum . <p> Unemployment rates have rocketed due to lockdowns and other restrictions to combat the coronavirus pandemic , with fears of worse to come in countries which have furloughed workers . <p> " The employment disruptions caused by the pandemic , rising automation and the transition to greener economies are fundamentally changing labour markets , " said Saadia Zahidi , Managing Director at the World Economic Forum ( WEF ) . <p> " As we emerge from the crisis , leaders have a remarkable opportunity to create new jobs , support living wages , and reimagine social safety nets to adequately meet the challenges in the labour markets of tomorrow . " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ surveyed 12,012 business leaders from 127 countries , makes up part of the WEF 's global competitiveness report , to be published next month . <p> The study surveyed respondents ' views of 30 risks in total . Worries about the spread of infectious diseases have also come to the fore , rising 28 places from last year 's survey . <p> Fiscal crises , cyber attacks and profound social instability were the third , fourth and fifth biggest risks , the survey showed . But climate change risks are also rising up the agenda , WEF said , while worries about militant attacks fell . <p> The survey has been released ahead of WEF 's first Jobs Reset summit later this month .
