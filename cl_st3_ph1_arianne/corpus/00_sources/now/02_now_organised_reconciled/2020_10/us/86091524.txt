( AP Photo/David Zalubowski ) <p> If approved , Denver 's proposed climate tax would charge shoppers an extra 2.5 cents on every $10 purchase of goods and services . Food , water , fuel , medical supplies and several other necessities would be exempt from the tax . The tax would raise an estimated $36 million in its first year , which would have to be spent creating jobs in the areas of renewable and clean energy technology and management of natural resources ; and on solar power , battery storage and other renewable energy technologies . It could also be spent on neighborhood-based environmental and climate justice programs , among other things .
