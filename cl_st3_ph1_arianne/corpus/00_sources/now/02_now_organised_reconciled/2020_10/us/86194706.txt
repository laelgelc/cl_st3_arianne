<p> If there is one thing we have learned from the last few months , it 's how important it is to be able to get outside into nature . With health experts warning us against indoor gatherings , meeting a few friends for a socially-distanced picnic or hike has been one of our few social outlets . And when smoke blanketed the skies recently , we all realized how much we had been relying on nature to be there for us . <p> Now , we have an opportunity to cast a vote to protect our ability to enjoy the outdoors . Measure T , on the ballot for most of Santa Clara County , will enable the continued protection of open space preserves and trails , preservation of wildlife habitat , and land conservation around creeks and streams -- which will prevent water pollution and improve water quality . Measure T also enables responsible land management to reduce the risk of wildfires and floods . <p> Measure T does not raise taxes . The measure would provide the Santa Clara Valley Open Space Authority with the critically needed funding to continue maintaining nature preserves such as Sierra Vista , Rancho Canada del Oro and the Coyote Valley Open Space Preserve . <p> As environmental advocates , we have long called for the protection of all of Coyote Valley . In 2018 , San Jose voters supported our call by approving an infrastructure bond that included $50 million for land acquisition in Coyote Valley . That vote led directly to the 2019 partnership between the City of San Jose , the Open Space Authority and the Peninsula Open Space Trust that resulted in 937 acres of Coyote Valley being permanently acquired for conservation . <p> Time and again , San Jose voters have proved that they care about nature and open space and will vote to protect it . And it makes sense . After all , study after study has shown that nature is good for our health .
