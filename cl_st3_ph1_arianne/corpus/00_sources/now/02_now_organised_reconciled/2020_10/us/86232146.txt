<h> Capitalism is in crisis . To save it , we need to rethink economic growth . <p> Even before the covid-19 pandemic and the resulting collapse of much the world 's economy , a crisis in capitalism was plainly evident . Unfettered free markets had pushed inequality of income and wealth to extremely high levels in the United States . Slow productivity growth in many rich countries had stunted financial opportunities for a generation . Businesses , if no longer quite oblivious to global warming , seemed impotent to make changes that might slow it . <p> And then came the pandemic , with millions losing their jobs , and then the raging wildfires , fueled by climate change , that blazed up and down the US West Coast . All the simmering signs of a dysfunctional economic system suddenly became fully evident , full-blown disasters . <p> No wonder many in the US and Europe have begun questioning the underpinnings of capitalism -- particularly its devotion to free markets and its faith in the power of economic growth to create prosperity and solve our problems . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ degrowth " was coined in the early 1970s .
