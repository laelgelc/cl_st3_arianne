<h> County needs healthy environment and healthy economy <p> By Anna Prizzia <p> Special to The Sun <p> From protecting our drinking water while working with the county and the water management district to founding and directing the University of Florida Office of Sustainability , the UF/IFAS Field and Fork Program and the non-profit Working Food , I have been working on the front lines of our institutions and our community for two decades . <p> My work to protect our natural resources and provide healthy food access , education and economic development has shown me that disparities are our greatest weakness , and I am committed to helping us become a place where all our citizens can thrive and lead healthy , happy lives . <p> When I learned Robert Hutchinson was not seeking another term on the Alachua County Commission in 2019 , I decided it was time to take the next step in community leadership and run for office so I could work on policy to protect our environment , build our local economy and ensure equity . <p> I will ensure policies are free from racial @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , pay equity and contracts with people of color and women . I will continue efforts for a living wage and to be sure everyone has access to safe , affordable housing and healthy food . <p> Providing support for small businesses and start-ups is critical for a healthy economy . As someone who has built a local business in our community , I know firsthand the challenges entrepreneurs face . I want to be sure that economic development programs are focused on these challenges and on providing equitable opportunities for people to access capital -- allowing income growth , building multi-generational wealth and maximizing economic opportunity . <p> Working Food has been doing youth education in our community for several years , and I have witnessed the grave inequalities in reading , science and math skills for our Black children . I also know the challenges many children face that cause trauma and mental health issues as they grow up .
