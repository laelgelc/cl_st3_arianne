<h> Most Ohioans Support Gov . DeWine 's Handling Of COVID-19 : Poll <p> OHIO -- A new poll finds that a majority of Ohioans view COVID-19 as the biggest issue facing the state ... and most Ohioans support Gov . Mike DeWine 's handling of the crisis . <p> The Spectrum Networks conducted its first news poll in Ohio . The poll focused on how Ohioans feel about a variety of issues facing the state and their community , including COVID-19 , voting , the movement to impeach DeWine and the president contracting the virus . <p> The poll found that 55 percent of Ohioans believe COVID-19 is the biggest problem facing the state . The next most concerning issue was opioid or drug addiction ( which 35 percent of Ohioans cited as the biggest problem in Ohio ) . Unemployment was voted the third largest problem in the state . <p> The vast majority of Ohioans ( 70 percent ) believe a return to normal life post-pandemic will take at least 6 months . <p> While support for DeWine has appeared to wane since the start of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efforts to contain COVID-19 and keep the economy sputtering along . The Spectrum poll found 65 percent of Ohioans support the governor 's work during the pandemic . <p> Ohioans also agree with the state law mandating mask usage in public ( 61 percent approval ) , while only 30 percent of Ohioans feel lockdowns locally have been too restrictive . Nearly half of Ohioans feel the mandate closing bars early has helped limit the spread of the virus ( only 36 percent of Ohioans disagree with the law ) . <p> While many Ohioans agree with DeWine 's efforts to stymie the virus , others would like to see the governor 's powers greatly restricted . <p> However , 31 percent of Republicans favor impeaching the governor . And less than half of Ohioans approve of the governor 's efforts on gun control ( 47 percent approval ) and in combating climate change ( 40 percent approval ) . <p> The battle against COVID-19 has also had an impact on how Ohioans view national politics . <p> A slight majority ( 51 percent ) of Ohioans said they believe @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ distancing have soured them on the president . The president contracting the virus earlier did n't do him any favors , with 48 percent of Ohioans saying it did not change their opinion of Trump and 32 percent saying it made them view him less favorably . <p> The latest Baldwin Wallace Great Lakes poll shows Ohio is currently a toss-up state , with no clear frontrunner between Trump and his challenger Joe Biden . The general election is Nov. 3.
