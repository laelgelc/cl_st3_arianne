<h> 30% of California land must be conserved under Gov . Newsom 's new order <p> by Andrew Sheeler , The Sacramento Bee <p> Credit : CC0 Public Domain <p> Citing a need to tackle the growing problem of climate change , California Gov . Gavin Newsom on Wednesday gave the order for state agencies to conserve 30% of state land and coastal water by no later than 2030 . <p> The move is a bid to store carbon in the state 's natural and working lands and remove it from the atmosphere , according to a statement from the governor 's office . <p> The order directs state agencies to pursue strategies and partnerships that focus on healthy soil management , wetlands restoration , active forest management and boosting green infrastructure , according to the governor 's office . <p> " It 's an audacious goal , and it 's an achievable goal , " Newsom said shortly before signing the executive order on Wednesday . <p> California becomes the first state in the nation to take such a step , the Democratic governor said , and it does @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The governor said that the new order builds off of the momentum generated by the executive order regarding fossil fuel-free cars by 2035 , as well as other measures being taken by his administration to combat climate change . <p> The governor 's move was hailed by the Center for Western Priorities , whose executive director , Jennifer Rokala , said in a statement , " Accelerating climate change and plummeting biodiversity are plainly visible across America right now .
