Science says we should ban those products . <p> But we do n't . <p> Let 's think about lockdowns from a purely medical perspective . Would the Food and Drug Administration approve a drug , would any doctor recommend a course of treatment when there has been no assessment of its efficacy ? Of course not . Yet that is precisely what the lockdowns were -- a course of treatment that had not been examined or judged to be efficacious prior to its imposition . <p> Let 's wander into mathematics and policy analysis , which are handmaidens of science . <p> We are well on our way to sacrificing a year in the lives of all of our citizens .
