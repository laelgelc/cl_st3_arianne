Data and research help us understand these challenges and set priorities , share knowledge of what works @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Business Plan ? <h> Project Level Technical Assistance <p> Future climate change and variability are increasingly difficult to predict and quantify , making planning and project design challenging and costly . AFRI-RES provides catalytic grants to World Bank projects to demonstrate the benefits of allocating dedicated funding toward enhancing climate resilience of investments during the project design phase . Project teams are using the funds in a variety of ways , including : ( i ) climate risk assessments ; ( ii ) capacity building trainings ; ( iii ) climate studies ; and ( iv ) hiring technical experts . Each project team shares their final reports or products , and the insights from those will be distilled for lessons learned .
