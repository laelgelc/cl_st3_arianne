<h> China says environment still grim despite five years of progress <p> SHANGHAI ( Reuters ) - China 's environment conditions are " grim " , falling short of public expectations even after five years of efforts to improve air quality , boost clean energy and curb greenhouse gas emissions , a senior official said on Wednesday . <p> There was still a long way to go , said Zhao Yingmin , the vice-minister of ecology and environment , even though China had met a series of targets on smog , water quality and carbon emissions over the five years from 2016 . <p> " While seeing the improvements ... it should be clearly recognised that the quality of the ecological environment remains far from people 's expectations for a better life , " he told reporters in Beijing . <p> China remains dependent on heavy industry and coal , and the " grim environmental trends " have not fundamentally changed , he added . <p> Last month , President Xi Jinping set a 2060 deadline to attain " carbon neutrality " , as part of China 's commitments to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ emissions to peak by 2030 . <p> The announcement was seen as a challenge to the United States , set to withdraw from the Paris deal on Nov. 4 .
