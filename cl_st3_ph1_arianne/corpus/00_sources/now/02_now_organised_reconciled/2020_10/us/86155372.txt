<h> We need improved climate-resilient infrastructure <p> For most Americans , climate change has long been a distant consideration -- the theories of scientists and partisan politicians . Yet just as the bombing of Pearl Harbor shattered the fiction of America 's immunity to war , so too has the summer of 2020 brought home the reality that climate change is endangering lives , property , and infrastructure not just abroad , but right here at home . <p> Ultimately , our ability to supply essential services to our citizens is becoming so endangered , that the changing climate itself is presenting dire threats to our national security . In the face of rising threats , building resilience into our infrastructure and better encouraging private capital flows in support of government efforts has become a global priority . <p> The daily headlines remind us that the frequency , scale , and severity of hurricanes , floods , wildfires , droughts , rising sea levels , and extreme weather events are accelerating at an unprecedented pace .
