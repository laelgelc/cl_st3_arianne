Candidates Brittni Kiick , a photographer and community organizer , and Pete Patterson , an account manager at a medical distribution company , are vying for the seat for District 3 , located in southeast Livermore . Incumbent Councilman Robert Carling is running unopposed for District 4 , located in the southwest of the city . <p> Mayor John Marchand is being termed out of his position , leaving his seat open .
