<p> The wildfires ripping across Napa and Northern California are heartbreaking to watch -- it is hard to conceive of a destination that brings visitors so much joy and delight suffering through so much devastation . As of this moment , the Glass Incident fires , which ignited Sept. 27 along the border between Napa and Sonoma , have consumed over 40,000 acres -- many of which included damage to beloved wineries such as Newton Vineyards , Burgess Cellars , Sterling Vineyards , Davis Estates , Castella di Amarosa Winery , as well as luxury resorts Calistoga Ranch and Meadowood . Unfortunately , the list continues to grow , and much of the region is still at risk . Tom Gamble , owner of Gamble Family Vineyards , weighed in via phone from the top of Mount Veeder where he and his team were back to picking grapes after an emergency evacuation the day before .
