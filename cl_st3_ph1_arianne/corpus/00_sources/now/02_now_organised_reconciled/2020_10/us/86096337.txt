They speak the truth and understand that their words are as important as their actions . They see beyond the immediate transaction to a long-term vision . They are open to new ideas from the people around them . They are accountable and admit their mistakes without blaming others . Most critically , they unite us around a goal and appeal to our best selves to achieve that goal together , rather than divide us based on fear and anger . <p> Yes , being a mensch is a big responsibility . The outpouring of love and respect for McCain , crossing party divisions , made clear that he embodied the character and behavior we want from our leaders : Decency , honesty , speaking truth to power and serving others before oneself . A president with respect for knowledge and science would create a plan for dealing with the pandemic , and not dismiss 200,000 deaths with " it is what it is , " nor would he or she reject the devastating impact of climate change on our environment . A president with tolerance would not encourage divisiveness around racial and ethnic lines , and refuse to condemn white nationalists .
