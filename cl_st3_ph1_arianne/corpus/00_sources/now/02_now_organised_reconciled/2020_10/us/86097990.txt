<p> NORTH BETHESDA , Md. , Oct. 8 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- Federal Realty Investment Trust ( NYSE : FRT ) today announced the pricing of its public offering of $400 million aggregate principal amount of 1.250% notes due 2026 ( the " green bonds " ) at an effective yield of 1.379% , maturing February 15 , 2026 . The offering is expected to close on October 13 , 2020 , subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions . <p> The company intends to allocate an amount equal to the net proceeds from this offering to the financing and refinancing of recently completed and future eligible green projects ( " eligible green projects " ) .
