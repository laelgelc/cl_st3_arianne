<h> Climate change spurs doubling of disasters since 2000 : UN <p> Major floods have more than doubled since the turn of the century over the previous 20 years <p> Climate change is largely to blame for a near doubling of natural disasters in the past 20 years , the United Nations said on Monday . <p> The UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction said 7,348 major disaster events had occurred between 2000 and 2019 , claiming 1.23 lives , affecting 4.2 billion people and costing the global economy some $2.97 trillion . <p> The figure far outstrips the 4,212 major natural disasters recorded between
