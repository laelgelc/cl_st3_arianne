<p> Novelist Rumaan Alam refuses to be confined to one race , gender , or sexuality when he picks up the pen . In his newest novel , Leave the World Behind , he slides easily into an array of personas , which proves that when it comes to the end of the world , we all pretty much all freak out the same . <p> Alam 's third novel features an omniscient narrator trundling us along as a white middle-class family heads to Long Island for a week at a stunning Airbnb .
