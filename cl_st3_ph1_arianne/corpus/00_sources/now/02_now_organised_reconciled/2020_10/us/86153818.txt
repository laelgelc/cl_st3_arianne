How this transition to a bioeconomy will affect Nordic water resources , is something BIOWATER , a Nordic Centre of Excellence , is currently looking into . <p> In a recent special issue of the human environment journal Ambio , BIOWATER 's researchers have explored how the answer may be found in plausible scenarios , long-term datasets , and modeling . " <p> Long-term monitoring data crucial <p> The main messages from BIOWATER , in addition to the risk of bioeconomy negatively affecting freshwater resources , is that unsustainable land use in combination with climate change will have severe impacts on the ecosystems .
