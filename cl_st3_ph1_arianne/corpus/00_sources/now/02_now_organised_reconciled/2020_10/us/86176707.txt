The U.S. oil and gas industry lost 107,000 jobs -- or about 7 percent of the 1.6 million employed nationally -- between March and August , according to global consulting firm Deloitte . Texas , the nation 's top oil producing state , has borne the brunt of the industry 's layoffs . <p> Chevron this month plans to lay off 700 workers in Houston , and more layoffs are expected after the California oil giant recently acquired Houston-based Noble Energy . Chevron earlier this year announced plans to lay off 6,000 workers globally by the end of the year . <p> Shell last month said it plans to cut up to 9,000 jobs over the next two years .
