<h> Opinion : Election recusal left up to Barrett <p> By Ann McFeatters <p> Tribune News Service <p> Oct 18 , 2020 at 6:05 PM <p> Judge Amy Coney Barrett speaks after President Donald Trump announced Barrett as his nominee to the Supreme Court , in the Rose Garden at the White House , Saturday , Sept. 26 , 2020 , in Washington . ( AP Photo/Alex Brandon ) ( Alex Brandon/AP ) <p> Ann McFeatters is a TNS op-ed columnist . ( HANDOUT/TNS/TNS ) <p> If the 2020 presidential election goes to the Supreme Court , as happened just 20 years ago , whether Amy Coney Barrett helps Donald Trump get a second term is totally up to her . <p> Refusing to vote on a case before the Supreme Court because of a conflict of interest ( in this case that Trump nominated her just as he is running for reelection after promising he would expect his nominee to support him ) is not governed by any ethics code . The justice himself or herself decides whether to recuse or rule . <p> Advertisement <p> Twenty years ago @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ elected over Al Gore , who won the popular vote by half a million votes but lost the presidency when the Supreme Court stopped the Florida recount in a 5-4 decision . Thus , one Supreme Court justice decided Bush would be president based on 500 disputed Florida votes . <p> Three years out of law school , Barrett was sent to Florida by a Washington , D.C. , law firm to help Bush 's legal team find thousands of Republican absentee ballots to help Bush win . <p> Advertisement <p> Ironically , Chief Justice John Roberts and Justice Brett Kavanaugh also worked for Republicans to help Bush win his case against Gore . Thus , three members of the current court will have agreed that Bush v. Gore was decided correctly . In addition to Kavanaugh , Trump named Neil Gorsuch to the court . <p> Barrett , who likely will be confirmed to fill Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg 's seat on the nation 's highest court before the election on a strict party-line vote , was chosen by Trump from a list provided by the Federalist Society . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ selection of judges who are conservative and promise to vote the way they think the writers of the Constitution intended 233 years ago . Such judges are called textualists or originalists . <p> Barrett considers herself such a judge . That is code for anti-progressivism . <p> Barrett 's mentor , former Justice Antonin Scalia , once wrote that married couples had no constitutional right of privacy to buy and use contraception without government restrictions . He was overruled in Griswold v. Connecticut . Barrett refuses to say what she thinks . <p> She also said she refuses to discuss her belief widely expressed in the past that Roe v. Wade , legalizing abortion , is a " barbaric legacy " wrongly decided in 1973 . <p> She will not say if she opposes in vitro fertilization . She will not say whether legalizing same-sex marriage was a correct decision . <p> She also has said she refuses to discuss a previously expressed opinion that John Roberts was wrong when he held the Affordable Care Act to be constitutional . This Supreme Court will hear arguments to abolish the ACA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Barrett has ruled in the past that a felon who fleeced Medicare out of hundreds of thousands of dollars has the right to buy guns because the prohibition on felons buying guns meant only felons who committed physical violence . Now the courts are deciding whether felons have the right to vote ; conservatives say no . In other words , people convicted of felonies who have served their prison terms have a constitutional right to bear arms but not a constitutional right to vote . <p> She has not questioned the 2013 gutting of the Voting Rights Act of 1965 on grounds it no longer needs to be enforced as written to prevent voter suppression because the days when it was needed are gone . Tell that to people trying to vote who must stand in line for 11 hours , may produce a gun license but not a student ID to vote , or who must have an absentee ballot witnessed , even in a pandemic . <p> As is her prerogative , Barrett refused again and again and again to give an opinion on issues that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as climate change , health care , civil rights , voter rights , immigration , the funding of elections , workers ' rights and women 's rights . She refused to opine on polygamy , a president pardoning himself , a president delaying the election , even a president refusing to peacefully leave office . <p> But most worrisome for Americans should be is that big business has spent millions of dollars to advocate for Barrett . Why ? Because this court , so far by a 5-4 margin , has repeatedly advocated for big business interests and an unregulated free market . This means tax cuts . Regulatory decisions that help big business . Elimination of workers ' rights . This means Goliath will always win against David . And big business wants the insurance of 6-3 Supreme Court decisions to ensure that . <p> Barrett has a minimalist resume for a justice ; she served only three years as an appellate judge . But it is fair to say she gives us every reason not to expect any surprises from her . She is Trump 's third justice .
