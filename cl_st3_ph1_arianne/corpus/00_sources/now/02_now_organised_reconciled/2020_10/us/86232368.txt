<p> In the 20 years since the new millennium began , the United States has steadily lost ground in technology exports . The one big exception is aerospace , where America continues to dominate the world . <p> Last year , the U.S. sold $55 billion in weapons overseas , mostly aerospace in nature , eclipsing rivals such as Russia and China . Meanwhile Boeing BA , for all its problems , remained one of only two major producers of commercial transports , and U.S. turbofan suppliers strengthened their dominance of the propulsion market . <p> At the moment , the biggest threat to aerospace is the coronavirus pandemic , which this week led aerospace behemoth Raytheon Technologies RTX to disclose the possibility of 15,000 layoffs going forward . GE Aviation , Raytheon 's biggest competitor in commercial turbofans , is reducing its workforce of 52,000 by one-quarter . <p> But one way or another , the pandemic will run its course and global demand will then revert to trend -- meaning steady growth .
