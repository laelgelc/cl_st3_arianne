<p> Government sets aside SG$49 million ( $36.05 million ) to support R&amp;D efforts in low-carbon energy technologies such as hydrogen , and kicks off an initiative to pilot a lithium-ion battery energy storage system on a " floating " lab . <p> The Singapore government is setting aside SG$49 million ( $36.05 million ) to drive research and development ( R&amp;D ) efforts in low-carbon energy technologies such as hydrogen and carbon capture , utilisation , and storage ( CCUS ) . It also announced an initiative to pilot a lithium-ion battery energy storage on a " floating " lab , utilising seawater to cool the battery cells . <p> Spanning five years , the SG$49 million R&amp;D investment is aimed at pushing the " technical and economic viability " of technologies that could help reduce the country 's carbon emissions .
