The numbers come from a report co-written by President Trump 's former chief economic adviser , so it 's clearly designed for campaign weaponization . The $6,500-figure is an estimate for 2030 , a decade in the future -- not even during a theoretical two-term Biden presidency . Ditto for the 5 million jobs figure . <p> Such numbers lack context .
