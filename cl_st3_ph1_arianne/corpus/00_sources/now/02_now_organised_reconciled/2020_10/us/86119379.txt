<h> ' I can carve that path ' : Nashville teen one of the first girls to earn Eagle Scout status <p> Weaving confidently through the woods around Shelby Bottoms Nature Center , 17-year-old Anna-Christina Laporte chatted about declining bee populations and how pollinators are needed to sustain the global food supply . <p> The teen , who goes by Christina , set her sights on becoming an Eagle Scout as soon as Boy Scouts began allowing girls into the program in February 2019 . She joined Troop 934 in Mt . Juliet and started working toward her goal , going on to earn a total of 109 badges . <p> Laporte built pollinator " hotels " at Shelby Bottoms as part of her Eagle Scout project .
