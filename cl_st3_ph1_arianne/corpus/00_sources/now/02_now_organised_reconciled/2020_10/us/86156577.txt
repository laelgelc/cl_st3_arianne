While NBS can make important contributions to climate-related challenges -- while providing diverse other benefits -- here I 'll discuss why the potential benefits of NBS do not let energy systems off the hook when it comes to climate change . <p> A critical distinction between NBS and traditional nature conservation is that , although NBS activities protect or restore ecosystems , that protection or restoration is not the main purpose of the investment . Rather , the main purpose is the delivery of some other societal benefit , such as reducing people 's risk from floods . <p> In the climate context , the " societal challenge " addressed by NBS can either be reducing greenhouse gases ( mitigation ) or reducing risk for people from climate-related stresses ( adaptation ) .
