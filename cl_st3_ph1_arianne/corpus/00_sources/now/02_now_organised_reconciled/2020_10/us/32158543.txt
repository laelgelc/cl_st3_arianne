That could result in a loss of as much as 800,000 barrels a day to 1 million barrels a day in American oil production by 2025 under Biden compared to Trump . <p> By contrast , U.S. production 's current gradual recovery from its April-May pandemic low point would likely continue under a Trump second term . <p> After that , it 's anyone 's guess where production goes , but most experts see oil prices gradually recovering next year into the low $50s .
