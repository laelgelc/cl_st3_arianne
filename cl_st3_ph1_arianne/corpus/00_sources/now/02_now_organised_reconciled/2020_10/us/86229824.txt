But disclosures by companies on the financial impact remain low , according to a new report from the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures . <h> Popular Searches <p> The group has urged businesses to measure and disclose how different climate outcomes might weigh on their performance . That includes nearly 60% of the world 's 100 largest public companies . <p> Michael R.
