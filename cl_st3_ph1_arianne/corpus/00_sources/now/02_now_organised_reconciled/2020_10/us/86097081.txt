<h> How Pence , Harris compared in the vice presidential debate <h> Spoiler alert : Pence scored higher in interruptions , time talking over Harris and ignoring the moderator <p> Javier Zarracina , and George Petras , USA TODAY <p> While the vice-presidential debate between Republican Vice President Mike Pence and Democratic Sen. Kamala Harris on Wednesday was more civil than that of their presidential counterparts , it may be best remembered for the housefly that stole the show . <p> As candidates attacked one another on the coronavirus , taxes , climate change , the Supreme Court and the health of President Donald Trump , it was a fly that landed on Pence 's hair and stayed for two minutes that caused social media to go wild . <p> Two minutes was a theme moderator Susan Page of USA TODAY returned to frequently Wednesday night as Pence frequently flew through that agreed-upon limit . <p> Though the candidates were mostly polite , there were interruptions and talk-overs . A USA TODAY debate analysis found Pence talked over Harris and exceeded his allotted speaking time more often than the senator .
