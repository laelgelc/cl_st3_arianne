<h> Malcolm Ross 's Most Recent Stories <p> Wind and solar remain the renewables of choice , but they are only part of the solution says Malcolm Ross . The world also needs what some , like Duke Energy , call a " ZELFR " ; a Zero-Emitting Load-Following Resource . That 's quite a mouthful , but it just means a clean zero-carbon energy source that can be produced on demand . <p> Unfortunately , ZELFRs have been few and far between . Low-carbon renewables , like wind and solar , are not available on-demand since they only produce when the wind blows and the sun shines . As a result , coal , gas , nuclear and/or massive storage capacity units ( e.g. batteries ) are still needed to ensure reliable power . But the ability to be completely green , using ZELFRs , could be on the horizon . <p> Recently , a start-up called Eavor ( pronounced " ever " ) developed what may be the world 's first ZELFR capable of operating on a scale similar to oil and gas . <p> So what is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , it has been lurking right beneath our feet the whole time -- it is the inexhaustible heat of the earth itself . This is where Eavor begins to change things . <p> The geothermal industry has remained a niche solution because of its need for hard-to-find hot but permeable aquifers . This requirement can add tremendous cost , risk , and delays to traditional geothermal projects .
