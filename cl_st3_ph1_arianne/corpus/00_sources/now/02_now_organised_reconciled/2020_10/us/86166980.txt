<h> French dairy giant accused of polluting country 's famous rivers for years <p> During a year-long investigation into dairy giant Lactalis , Disclose found that 38 of the company 's production sites in France had breached environmental regulations . In many cases , according to the report , this involved the release into rivers of milk-related derivatives -- which can be deadly to aquatic life in large volumes -- or byproducts from wastewater treatment plants . The report contains evidence of several instances that it says resulted in the death of fish . <p> Among the rivers allegedly affected are some of the country 's most famous , such as the Loire , in the area around Saint-Florent-le-Vieil .
