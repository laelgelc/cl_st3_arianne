<p> The debate over shareholder versus stakeholder capitalism has been going on for decades , and it has been especially fraught in recent years . <p> Proponents of shareholder capitalism say corporations have one purpose -- to make as much money as possible for their shareholders -- and that attempting to do anything else would backfire . People who back stakeholder capitalism say companies should consider all their stakeholders -- not just the owners , but also employees , customers , and suppliers -- and that doing otherwise means companies may well damage the environment while entrenching inequality . <p> Both sides can point to some eye-popping statistics in America , the country which is perhaps ground-zero for shareholder primacy . The US poverty rate declined to 10.5% last year , the lowest since records were first published in 1959 . At the same time , inequality has risen , with the top 20% of highest earners taking home more than half of US income .
