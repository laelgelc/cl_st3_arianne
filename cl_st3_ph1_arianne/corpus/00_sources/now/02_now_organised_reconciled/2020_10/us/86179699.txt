For his own new album , Urcola hired D'Rivera as a featured sideman to put him in a fresh setting , a quartet of two horns and two rhythms . Our jazz critic Kevin Whitehead has a review . <p> In a typical Latin jazz band , piano is the spine where harmony and rhythm connect .
