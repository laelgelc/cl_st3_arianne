It 's a business problem Gregory Barker is trying to solve . <p> Mr. Barker is the executive chairman of En+ Group PLC , the world 's largest aluminum maker outside China . It uses hydroelectricity to power smelters in Siberia that make aluminum with one-fifth the emissions of rivals in China burning coal . <p> But there 's no market for low-carbon aluminum , and the provenance of metal is hard to promote . Even when you can certify aluminum is low-carbon , there is no premium for it . <p> This highlights the challenge of decarbonizing metals manufacturing and other heavy industries , which contribute an estimated 26% of global greenhouse-gas emissions , according to the International Energy Agency .
