That would allow both stocks to trade at premiums to the asset-management group , according to Deutsche analysts led by Brian Bedell . <p> Asset managers " have an opportunity to substantially enhance organic growth " by developing products focused on offering a positive impact in terms of ESG , or environmental , social and governance factors , Deutsche Bank wrote . Many asset managers have been embedding ESG into their investment processes , it said , but strategies specifically focused on ESG are doing far better at attracting assets . <p> That trend is expected to continue " given rising awareness of ESG issues globally and the greater importance younger generations place on investing for ESG impact @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in sustainable investing jumped as concern over climate change mounted and the Covid-19 pandemic highlighted the importance of social issues , it said . <p> According to one survey from Federated Hermes , nearly two-thirds of respondents now consider social factors as part of their investment process . <p> In the past , one hurdle for ESG investing was a perception that such funds underperform relative to others , or that investing with a social purpose would violate fiduciary responsibility . That has been the position of the Labor Department , which is weighing an unpopular proposal to curb ESG options in 401(k) retirement plans . <p> Those concerns are unfounded , Deutsche Bank suggested , because investors do n't appear to be sacrificing performance by buying ESG funds .
