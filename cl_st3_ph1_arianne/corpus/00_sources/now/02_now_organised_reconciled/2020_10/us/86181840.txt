<p> At the time of writing , 6 of the 20 largest wildfires in the state 's history have occurred in 2020 , including the largest on record , known as the August Complex , which has been burning since the middle of August . <p> Wildfires release large amounts of particulate matter ( PM ) and toxic gases , such as carbon monoxide and nitrogen oxides . <p> Smoke particles that are 2.5 microns or smaller in size , known as PM2.5 , are carried long distances by the wind . Their small size means that they are breathed deep into the lungs , where they have a wide range of effects on human health . <p> In addition to the severe harms to health and well-being , there are also financial costs . <p> Daniel Cullen , who recently gained his Ph.D . in health economics from the University of California Santa Barbara , has estimated the annual healthcare costs of wildfires in the state between 2012 and 2018 in terms of hospitalizations for respiratory and circulatory illnesses . <p> As part of research for his doctoral thesis @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ much as fourfold over this period , from around $88 million in 2012 to $348 million in 2018 . <p> This year is likely to be the worst on record , says Cullen , but given how extraordinary the 2020 wildfire season has been thus far , it is difficult to predict exactly how bad it will be .
