<p> A group of Australian scientists exploring the Great Barrier Reef have discovered an enormous new coral reef that stretches taller than some of the world 's largest buildings . <p> The detached reef -- the first one discovered in over 120 years -- is more than 1,600 feet high , meaning it towers over the Empire State Building in New York City , Sydney 's Sydney Tower and the Petronas Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur , according to the Schmidt Ocean Institute . <p> The massive discovery was made on Oct. 20 by scientists mapping the underwater seafloor of the northern Great Barrier Reef aboard the Institute 's research vessel Falkor , which is currently on a year-long exploration of the ocean surrounding Australia . <p> Five days later , they conducted an exploratory , live-streamed dive with SuBastian , the Institute 's underwater robot . <p> Dr. Robin Beaman of James Cook University led the expedition , and said in a press release that the group was " surprised and elated " by the discovery . <p> " To not only 3D map the reef in detail , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " he said . " This has only been made possible by the commitment of Schmidt Ocean Institute to grant ship time to Australia 's scientists . " <p> The release described the reef as " blade-like , " and said it was a little less than a mile wide , with it sitting just 131 feet below sea level at its most shallow depth . <p> It joins seven other tall , detached reefs in the area , which has been mapped since the late 1800s . Among those is the reef at Raine Island , which the release called " the world 's most important green sea turtle nesting area . " <p> " This unexpected discovery affirms that we continue to find unknown structures and new species in our Ocean , " Wendy Schmidt , co-founder of the Schmidt Ocean Institute , said in a statement . " The state of our knowledge about what 's in the Ocean has long been so limited . Thanks to new technologies that work as our eyes , ears and hands in the deep ocean , we have the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ opening to us , revealing the ecosystems and diverse life forms that share the planet with us . " <p> In addition to the reef , the Institute has discovered a variety of other things this year , including the longest recorded sea creature , a 148-foot siphonophore . <p> The Great Barrier Reef , which is comprised of more than 3,000 individual reef systems , is the world 's largest coral reef system , but is facing increasing threats to its existence thanks to climate change . <p> Among those threats are rising water temperatures , poorer water quality from sediment run-off and pollution , more severe cyclones and crown-of-thorns starfish outbreaks , according to the Great Barrier Reef Foundation .
