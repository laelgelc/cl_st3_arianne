<p> The familiar phrase , " necessity is the mother of all invention , " is traced back to Plato 's Republic . The need for social distance has sent waves of disruption through our lives and the economy . For some industries , the trouble presented an opportunity . Video streaming and grocery and meal delivery services have experienced an incredible increase in demand . Some , such as Netflix , have shown the ability to scale deftly . Others , like Instacart , have found seizing the opportunity a bit of a challenge . <p> Other industries are struggling to survive .
