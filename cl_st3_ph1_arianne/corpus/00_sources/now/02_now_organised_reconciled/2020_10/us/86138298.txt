<h> How much is your Panera order costing the climate ? <p> Panera Bread partnered with the World Research Institute to determine the carbon footprint of producing their ingredients . <p> The national chain will now include the carbon footprint of each dish on its menu . <p> The company hopes to raise awareness of the effect of food production on climate change and encourage others to do the same . <p> A decade ago , Panera Bread became the first national chain to voluntarily post the calorie information for its menu items both online and in stores , inspiring others to follow . Now , they 're putting another count on their menu : the carbon footprint . <p> " We are hoping that this too will become an industry standard that other industry peers follow in our footsteps , " said Niren Chaudhary , CEO of Panera . <p> Starting today , Panera will include the carbon footprint of each item on its menu based on the ingredients in its recipe .
