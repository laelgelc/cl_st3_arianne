<h> Contango to acquire Mid-Con in sign of accelerating energy consolidation <p> Contango Oil and Gas Co. plans to acquire Mid-Con Energy Partners in an all-stock deal valued at $154.8 million including debt . <p> Photo : Sue Ogrocki , STF / Associated Press <p> Contango Oil and Gas Co. plans to acquire Mid-Con Energy Partners in an all-stock deal valued at $154.8 million including debt . <p> The Houston independent oil and gas company on Monday said its " strategic merger " with its Tulsa , Okla. -based rival will expand its oil and gas operations in Oklahoma and Wyoming , adding proven reserves of 23.9 million barrels to Contango 's asset portfolio . The sale , which is pending approval from shareholders and regulators , is expected to close this year or early next year . " <p> The Contango-Mid-Con deal is the latest in a wave of consolidation sweeping the oil and gas industry after crude prices crashed during the coronavirus pandemic . Last week , ConocoPhillips announced plans to acquire Concho Resources for $9.71 billion , followed just days later by Pioneer Natural Resources ' $4.5 billion pursuit of Parsley Energy . The deals come on the heels of Chevron 's nearly $12 billion acquisition of Houston-based Noble Energy this month , and Devon Energy 's plans to purchase WPX Energy for nearly $2.6 billion . <p> Industry leaders have long acknowledged the need for consolidation as crude prices have crashed and as the outlook for oil demand has soured with the rise of actions to slow climate change . Contango ended the third quarter with $66 million of debt , $8 million of cash on hand and access to a $75 million line of credit .
