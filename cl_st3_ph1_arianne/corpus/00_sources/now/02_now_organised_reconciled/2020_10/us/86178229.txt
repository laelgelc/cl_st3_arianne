<p> A new study from the University of Cambridge has found that millennials are more disillusioned with democracy than generations were before them . <p> Fifty-five percent of millennials -- compared to 45% of Generation X'ers and a small minority of Baby Boomers surveyed at the same age -- are unhappy with democracy .
