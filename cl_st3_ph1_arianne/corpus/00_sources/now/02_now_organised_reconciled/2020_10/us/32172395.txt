<h> Climate-protected citadels , virtual worlds only for the privileged : Is this the future of inequality ? <p> Imagine visiting the Europe of 2048 . The cities are strangely silent . Desperate communities in Paris , London and Berlin struggle in the shadow of hollowed-out buildings . The once-popular coastal resorts have also been abandoned , as rises in sea level have taken their toll . <p> Heading out to rural areas , you find where the fortunate few have gone : shining citadels , high-tech gated communities designed to protect residents from the ravages of climate change and ongoing pandemics . <p> That does n't mean life in 2048 is always grim . The world is gearing up for the 39th Summer Olympics , to be held in Kuala Lumpur , Malaysia . Even if it is n't , what can imagined scenarios like this teach us about the challenges we face in the present ? <p> Identifying inequality blindspots <p> Researchers from 13 countries are currently thinking about such questions as part of the IMAJINE project , funded by the European Commission 's Horizon 2020 program . IMAJINE 's aim is to explore inequality and injustice across the European Union 's member states . <p> Inequality is n't just the gaps between the haves and have-nots in the present . As the world changes and societies evolve , different forms of unfairness can arise . <p> COVID-19 has fuelled uncertainty and , as both the pandemic and our response to it have reshaped societies , made these issues more urgent . We need to anticipate new and emerging forms of inequality and injustice -- and understand how we can counter them . <p> To do this , IMAJINE uses scenario planning , a tool for envisioning different future worlds . <p> Scenarios are n't predictions ; they are valuable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the four visions we 've created is n't to forecast what will certainly happen , but to find our blindspots when it comes to planning for the future of inequality . <p> A future of digital citizenship or five-person marriages <p> In our first scenario , outlined above , we picture a world in which cities are abandoned and today 's rural backwaters become the most desirable places to live . <p> The fight against climate change is a global priority and next-generation clean manufacturing technology is expanding rapidly .
