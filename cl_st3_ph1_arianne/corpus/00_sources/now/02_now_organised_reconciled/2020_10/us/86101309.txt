<p> Google has released a first look at its next massive campus -- and it looks nothing like those before it . <p> The company released renderings and sketches of guidelines for its mixed-use , 80-acre campus in downtown San Jose , which will house 25,000 employees . More than half of the " Downtown West " 80-acre project -- which is being built in coordination with the city of San Jose -- will be allocated for residential and public space and include features like childcare centers , outdoor moving screenings and ecological viewing stations . <p> " Thousands of conversations helped us hone in to what we really want in a site , which was much less the corporate campus and the financial district and much more a resilient neighborhood , " said Alexa Arena , Google 's district lead for San Jose in a video .
