<p> Chinese President Xi Jinping last week committed his country to carbon neutrality by 2060 . That makes it the second biggest in the world , narrowly trailing India 's 2.245GW Bhadla solar park . Until now , China 's biggest solar station was the Tengger Desert Solar Park , with a capacity of 1.54GW .
