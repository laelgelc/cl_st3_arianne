It 's an infrastructure improvement for Miami cyclists , but it 's also part of a larger , interstate network of trails that will eventually make it possible to ride from Florida to Maine with little interaction with cars . And even that enormous project is itself just a small part of an even bigger dream : a network of protected bike lanes connecting cities across the country , making it possible to bike from city to city -- and ocean to ocean -- safely . <p> advertisement <p> advertisement <p> Called the Underline , the park in Miami will link into the East Coast Greenway project and is an example of the kind of trail that could form car-free connections across the entire country .
