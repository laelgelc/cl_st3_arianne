<p> A recent financial good-news story has been the growth of environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) investing among corporates and wealthy individuals . Now , however , there are signs of that growth slowing , led by the West . <p> In a new survey , 65% of U.S. investors adopted ESG considerations when managing their money , compared with 94% of those in Europe , 89% in Canada and 72% in Asia . <p> While in the past three years the amount of money invested in ESG has boomed , in the U.S. it has remained static , says RBC Global Asset Management , which conducted the survey . <p> In total , $11.9 trillion was invested sustainably in the U.S. versus $14 trillion in Europe according to a Global Sustainable Investment Alliance report in 2018 . <p> With the largest money markets in the world , the U.S. is key to ESG making a difference to the world .
