" <p> Our federal government 's disastrous response to the COVID-19 pandemic is a perfect example of this mentality 's hubris , closed-mindedness and ignorance about the rest of the world . Many Asia-Pacific countries have succeeded against COVID-19 -- South Korea , Vietnam , Singapore , New Zealand , Australia . Even China learned a great deal from its disastrous SARS response , and , despite early stumbles this time , Beijing has managed to crush COVID-19 so completely that the disease has virtually disappeared from the country where it began . <p> To respond successfully to today 's huge challenges -- a pandemic , an economic recession , climate change and racial reconciliation -- our government leaders need to abandon the self-destructive belief in American exceptionalism . They need to adopt Bill Gates ' approach to problem-solving . He starts by asking two fundamental questions : " Who has dealt with this problem well ? And what can we learn from them ? " Gates suggests we apply the same philosophy to the pandemic.
