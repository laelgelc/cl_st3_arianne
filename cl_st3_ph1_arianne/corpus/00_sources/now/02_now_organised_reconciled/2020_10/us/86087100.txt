<h> Prince William announces ' Earthshot ' prize to spur climate innovation <p> Britain 's Prince William unveiled an ambitious initiative on Wednesday to tackle the biggest environmental challenges threatening the planet . <p> Known as the Earthshot Prize , the project is designed to incentivize and support efforts to curb climate change and environmental degradation over the next 10 years . It is modeled after President John F. Kennedy 's " moonshot " program , which in 1961 jumpstarted intense innovation and technological advancement with the goal of landing American astronauts on the moon by the end of the decade . <p> " The plan is to really galvanize and bring together the best minds , the best possible solutions , to fixing and tackling some of the world 's greatest environmental challenges , " William , the Duke of Cambridge , said in a short film announcing the initiative . " We 've got to harness our ingenuity and our ability to invent . " <p> The prizes focus on five main areas : protecting and restoring nature , cleaning the air , reviving oceans , building a waste-free world and fixing the climate . <p> Nominations for the Earthshot Prize will open on Nov. 1 , and an awards ceremony will be held in a different city each year , beginning with London next fall . Nominees can include individuals , communities , businesses and organizations . <p> Winners will be selected in a five-stage process that culminates in judgments from as-yet-unnamed members of a prize council . <p> The Earthshot Prize is funded by the Global Alliance , a network of philanthropic organizations that include members such as Bloomberg Philanthropies , The Jack Ma Foundation , The Paul G.
