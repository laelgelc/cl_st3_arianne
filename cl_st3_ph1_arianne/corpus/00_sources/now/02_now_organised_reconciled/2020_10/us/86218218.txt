<h> Japan PM Suga Sets 2050 Deadline For Carbon Neutrality <p> Japan 's Prime Minister Yoshihide Suga on Monday set a 2050 deadline for the world 's third-largest economy to become carbon neutral , significantly firming up the country 's climate change commitments . <p> Delivering his first policy address since taking office , Suga placed the environment front and centre as Japan plays catch-up with climate pledges made by other major economies . <p> " We will bring the total amount of greenhouse gas ( emitted by Japan ) to net zero by 2050 , meaning carbon neutral , " Suga told parliament . <p> " I declare we will aim to realise a decarbonised society , " he added , to applause from Diet members . <p> Tokyo had previously only aimed to achieve carbon neutrality some time in the latter half of the century , a goal that critics called vague . <p> The new target was praised by activists and welcomed by the UN .
