<p> Leaning into the renewed sense of connection and spirit of community inspired by the global pandemic , Kors looked closer to home to cast the talent for his latest Watch Hunger Stop campaign , the brand 's long-running philanthropic initiative in partnership with the United Nations World Food Programme ( WFP ) . Renowned for starring a long list of celebrity ambassadors including Kate Hudson , Halle Berry and Lupita Nyong'o , this year 's campaign instead focuses on Michael Kors employees from across all levels of the organization , individuals who volunteered to further support the cause by stepping in front of the camera . <p> The resulting imagery , shot by Menelik Puryear against brilliantly colored backgrounds and accented with still photos of fruit and vegetables , is an exuberant , heartfelt expression of collaboration and commitment by Team Kors . The staffers are pictured wearing the special-edition Watch Hunger Stop 2020 designs : a white , 100% organic cotton LOVE T-shirt , $40 ; and a blue denim tote , $58 , made with 25% Repreve ? recycled polyester .
