But focus on the oceans -- they ultimately control our climate destiny . <p> The oceans contain 268 times the amount of mass of the atmosphere and can store 1,000 times more heat . The oceans absorb the heat equivalent of 5 Hiroshima style atomic bombs per second , more than 90% of climate change 's excess global heating . <p> As a result of this excess heat , the oceans are becoming significantly more " stratified , " according to a new study published Monday by some of the world 's top climate scientists .
