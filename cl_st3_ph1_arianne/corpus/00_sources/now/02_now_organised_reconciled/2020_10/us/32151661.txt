<p> Buildings and infrastructure also need to adapt to the changing climate . Updating structural design standards is crucial to improving European climate resilience and ensuring the safety of constructions , that are expected to suffer from changes in atmospheric variables and more frequent and intense extreme weather events . <p> In 2017 , the Joint Research Centre ( JRC ) - the European Commission 's science and knowledge service - established the scientific network on adaptation of structural design to climate change .
