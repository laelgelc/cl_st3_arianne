<h> South Sudan families displaced by fighting forced out again by floods <p> LANYARIS , South Sudan ( Reuters ) - By the time the waters started rising , ethnic violence had already forced South Sudanese mother Vorgol Poulo , her husband , and their seven children to flee their home twice this year . <p> They were desperate to stay home when the rains began in July but ten days of heavy rainfall destroyed most of their possessions , forcing them out for a third time . <p> " The cows are sick . We have lost so many , we do n't know the number , " said Pulo , who sold charcoal before flooding put most of the market under water . <p> She spoke to Reuters on a patch of grass poking above standing water , near the remote town of Pibor as thin cows limped by . <p> The family can not afford plastic sheeting for shelter or firewood , she said .
