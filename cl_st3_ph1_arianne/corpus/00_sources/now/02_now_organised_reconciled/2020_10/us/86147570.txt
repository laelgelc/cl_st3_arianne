" I 'm not going to waste my time , " he told Fox News . <p> Biden 's town hall was held in accordance with state and local government health and safety regulations . The Democratic nominee tackled questions from voters on topics such as Trump 's handling of the coronavirus , tax reform , climate change , police reform and more . <p> Celebrities have taken to Twitter to react to Biden 's thoughts .
