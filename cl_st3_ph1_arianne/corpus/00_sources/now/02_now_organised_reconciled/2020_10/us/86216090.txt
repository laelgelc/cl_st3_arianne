<h> Coal magnate Robert Murray dead at 80 <p> Robert Murray , the coal titan who fought against federal mine safety regulations , died Sunday at his Ohio home . <p> Murray , 80 , died surrounded by family early Sunday morning , just days after he announced his retirement as board chairman of American Consolidated Natural Resource Holdings Inc. , the largest privately-owned U.S. coal company , a spokesman told WTOV . <p> A cause of death was not revealed but Murray was diagnosed with pulmonary fibrosis in 2016 and had recently applied for black lung benefits , claiming he was dependent on oxygen and " near death , " NPR reported last month . <p> " Saddened to learn of the passing of Bob Murray , " West Virginia Sen. Shelley Moore Capito tweeted . " He was a staunch ally for coal miners , a good friend , and a one of a kind person . " <p> Murray , who claimed he lied about his age so he could begin working in a coal mine at just 16 , founded Murray Energy in St. Clairsville , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2019 , when he was replaced as CEO a day after the company filed for bankruptcy . <p> In 2014 , Murray , a fervent climate change denier , filed an unsuccessful lawsuit against regulations that would cut the amount of coal dust in coal mines , which is directly correlated to black lung disease . <p> He also repeatedly filed defamation lawsuits against media outlets who reported on him , including " Last Week Tonight " host John Oliver in 2017 . <p> " Throughout my life in and about coal mines and in our industry , I have given all that I had to our employees and their families to sustain their family livelihoods and jobs for the hard working people in our mining communities , " Murray said in a statement less than a week ago when he announced his retirement .
