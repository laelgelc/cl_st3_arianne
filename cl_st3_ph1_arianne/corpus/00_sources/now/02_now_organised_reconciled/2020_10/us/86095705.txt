Which sectors are on track for climate alignment and where work is still in progress is illustrated in a Climate Alignment Dashboard ( CAD ) . <p> Furthermore , targets for all nine sectors are presented to ultimately align our portfolio with the Paris climate goals . We aim to do this by supporting and engaging with existing clients to shift their investments more towards low-carbon technologies and by shifting our own capital allocation choices more towards low-carbon technologies and away from high-carbon . This includes reducing our financing of sectors that require a decline in production over time to meet the Paris goals , such as coal and upstream oil &amp; gas , while financing more renewables . <p> As announced earlier , this year 's Terra progress report for the first time includes the oil &amp; gas sector , including a target . ING aims to reduce financing to upstream oil &amp; gas by 19% by 2040 from 2019 levels .
