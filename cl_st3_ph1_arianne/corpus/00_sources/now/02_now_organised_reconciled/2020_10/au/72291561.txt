I have been thinking a lot about what role The Age plays in Melbourne and Victoria in 2020 . One of the reasons I wanted to lead The Age was because of my increasing concern in recent years about the media becoming so polarised , reflecting perhaps the polarising of our community . I worry that if people only read or watch what they agree with , the chance for us to have conversations and debates about our big and complicated challenges diminishes .
