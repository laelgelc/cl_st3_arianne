To put it in perspective , when the Liberals launched their " debt truck " scare campaign in 2009 , they did so with the figure " $315 billion " emblazoned on the side - one-third of the level of projected peak debt under the Coalition today . So what does Australia get from that spending ? The economy came into this crisis from a position of weakness .
