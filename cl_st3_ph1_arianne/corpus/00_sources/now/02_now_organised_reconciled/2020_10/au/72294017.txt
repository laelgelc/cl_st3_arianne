<p> A new report has highlighted the state 's coastal waters are at risk due to the lack of an integrated management system . The report , from The Australia Institute Tasmania and the Tasmanian Independent Science Council , said multi-level ecosystem assessment process needed to be adopted and an independent review of marine legislation undertaken . It said more marine areas needed to be included in the state 's marine protected area system . IN OTHER NEWS : The two bodies said adequate resourcing needed to be provided to the state 's Parks and Wildlife Service to manage these protected areas with Tasmania 's Aboriginal population in collaboration with marine scientists and stakeholders . Tasmanian Independent Science Council member Eloise Carr said there were many marine species only found in Tasmanian waters that were under threat from climate change , aquaculture operations , agricultural run-off , and urban development . She said just 1.1 per cent of Tasmania 's marine waters were fully protected , no-take zones . This excludes Macquarie Island .
