As the answers often form the bedrock of public decision-making , it becomes crucial we can trust the people who make the decisions behind the scenes . <h> " Total faith " in Australian numbers <p> When the Statistical Society of London was founded in 1834 , discussion of interpretation or " opinion " was banned at its meetings . Murphy 's " total faith " in " Australian numbers " is reflected in a 2020 survey conducted by the Australian Bureau of Statistics , which found 85% of the general community trust ABS statistics . The figure is even higher among " informed users " : a near-unanimous 99% . <p> Our communal faith in the numbers produced by the ABS reflects a web of trust and relationships built over decades . <h> Why trust matters <p> For many policy issues , such as unemployment , ABS statistics are taken as our absolute measure of reality .
