Perhaps we should . <p> Coming from opposing ideologies across the left-right political divide , we expect the left to push dissimilar socio-economic agendas to the right when it comes to managing public affairs , just as we are not surprised if they aspire to achieve different goals when it comes down to the future of the nation . Why should we anticipate anything else ? Is this polarisation not an integral aspect of the political status quo ? An inviolable component ?
