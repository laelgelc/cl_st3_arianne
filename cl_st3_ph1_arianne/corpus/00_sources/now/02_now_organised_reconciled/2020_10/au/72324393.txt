<p> The Leadership ( M , 97 minutes ) 3 stars Taking a group of professional women on a three-week cruise combined with a leadership workshop was an inspired idea . No doubt about it . The trip to the exquisite , endangered wilderness of Antarctica would be a reminder of what science was fighting for . The journey would offer a fundamental reset for the participants who had been selected from the fields of science , engineering , technology , mathematics ( STEM ) and medicine . It was designed to help them become the sort of the leaders they " hoped to be " , honing the skills necessary for contributing to meaningful and necessary policy change around the world . Course leader Fabian Dattner had lofty hopes that were even underpinned by a great quote from poet T. S. Eliot . The prominent businesswoman , leadership consultant , and self-described dreamer has a background in corporate consultancy .
