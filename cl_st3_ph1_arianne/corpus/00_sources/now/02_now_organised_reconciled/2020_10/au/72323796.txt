<p> Companies are n't only under pressure to reduce their contribution to climate change by reducing emissions of greenhouse gases , but to also demonstrate that their business models are robust enough to cope with the changes that may flow from climate change . <p> However , businesses face a much broader set of sustainability related challenges . <h> What are the barriers to becoming more sustainable ? <p> My research suggests that the main barrier is n't around values , but rather value . <p> The leaders I work with want to contribute positively to their communities and live in a more sustainable world . However , they equally value their careers , their ability to create jobs for employees and their commitment to their shareholders . <p> To move forward , they need to understand the value that being more sustainable can generate for their firm . <p> And they also need to be confident in their ability to successfully generate that value .
