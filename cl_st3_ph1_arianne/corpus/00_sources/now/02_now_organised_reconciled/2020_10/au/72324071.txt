Author of Hold On ! Saving the Spotted Handfish Dr Gina M Newton said the handfish species used to be found globally but are now only found in Tasmania , with the exception of NSW . " Tasmania is a biodiversity hotspot region when it comes to the marine world but also the conditions are good there compared to other places , " she said . " It 's like the last stronghold if you like . " Dr Newton said there are 14 species and only one of those is common .
