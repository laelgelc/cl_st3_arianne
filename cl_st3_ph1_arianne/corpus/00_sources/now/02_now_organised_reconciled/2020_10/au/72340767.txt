<h> Adelaide Film Festival 2020 <p> From Sundance award winners to Aussie classics , there 's 54 feature films from more than 40 countries . <p> Adelaide Film Festival ( AFF ) returns this month with 54 feature films , 22 world premieres and 27 Australian premieres from more than 40 countries . <p> The inaugural program from AFF CEO and creative director Mat Kesting will open with futuristic thriller 2067 ( starring Kodi Smit-McPhee , Ryan Kwanten and Deborah Mailman ) , which was filmed in Adelaide and is set in a world devastated by climate change . <p> Other local highlights include When Pomegranates Howl , directed by Tehran-born , Adelaide-based Granaz Moussavi ; queer coming-of-age story My First Summer , starring Adelaide 's Maiah Stewardson ( Girl Asleep ) ; and a restored version of landmark Australian film Black and White , a dramatisation of the Stuart Case ; plus Stephen Maxwell Johnson 's powerful follow-up to Yolngu Boy , High Ground , an unflinching look at the brutality of white settlement starring Simon Baker , Jack Thompson and Jacob Junior Nayinggul . <p> There 's also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an absurd , poignant reflection on memory and identity that Variety described as " Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind in reverse " ; plus How To Build A Girl , based on Caitin Moran 's bestselling novel and starring Beanie Feldstein ( Booksmart , Lady Bird ) ; Toronto International Film Festival selection Blackbird , starring Susan Sarandon , Sam Neill , Kate Winslet and Mia Wasikowska ; and closing night film , the heart-warming Minari , which won both the Grand Jury Prize and the Audience Award at Sundance . <p> " Festivals are about bringing people together and we are so lucky in South Australia to be able to do this during this challenging time , " said CEO and artistic director Mat Kesting . " We invite audiences to embrace all that the festival offers , including lots of fabulous nights out . "
