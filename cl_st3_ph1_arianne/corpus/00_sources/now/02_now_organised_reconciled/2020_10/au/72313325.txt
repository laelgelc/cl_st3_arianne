<h> Thirty days to go - Boris Herrmann gets set for the Vendee Globe <h> In just 30 days , the 33 skippers of the IMOCA fleet will be on the start line for the 9th edition of the Vend ? e Globe . Among them will be Boris Herrmann , the first German sailor to compete in this incredible race . <p> Boris will be competing with his 60ft foiling race machine , Seaexplorer -- Yacht Club de Monaco ( previously Malizia ) . Since the race 's inception in 1989 , the Vend ? e Globe has seen some of the greatest sailors in the world battle it out , against the elements and each other , to be the fastest person to sail non-stop alone around the world . Such is the challenge , that less than 100 people have ever completed this brave feat . <p> The race happens every four years , with a current record of just over 74 days . It is also about climate protection and education . Whilst Boris is competing in the Vend ? e Globe , he will also be measuring important ocean CO2 data to aid scientists with evidence to further understand and mitigate climate change . Via an onboard laboratory , Seaexplorer will take water sample readings automatically from the most remote ocean areas and send this rare and valuable data back to our scientific partners at Max Planck , Geomar and Ifremer . Here it will be analysed and made available via SOCAT to be used by scientists around the world . <p> The team have also just relaunched their kids ' education programme to engage children around the world on climate change and the ocean .
