<p> The University of Tasmania will no longer invest in companies or funds that have exposure to fossil fuels under a bold new policy announced on Monday . UTAS is hosting a week of events for Global Climate Change Week . UTAS vice-chancellor Rufus Black announced the climate change policy as part of a broader plan that will support the creation of a zero-carbon economy . It includes a commitment from UTAS to divest from fossil-fuel-exposed investment funds by the end of 2021 .
