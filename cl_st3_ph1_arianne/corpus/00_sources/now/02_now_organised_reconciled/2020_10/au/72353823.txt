<p> Professor Holmes has been studying viruses for 30 years but he shot to prominence in early January when he became the first person to publish the genetic sequence of the newly identified SARS-COV-2 , the virus that causes COVID-19 . <p> In fact , Professor Yong-Zhen Zhang at Fudan University in China , working in collaboration with Professor Holmes , was the first to sequence the virus .
