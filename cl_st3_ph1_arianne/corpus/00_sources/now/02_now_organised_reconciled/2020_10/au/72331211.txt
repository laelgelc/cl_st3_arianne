<h> New awareness campaign to inspire waterwise action at start of National Water Week <p> ? State Government , through the Water Corporation , launches new climate change and waterwise awareness campaign at start of National Water Week <p> Think Climate Change . Be Waterwise campaign to inspire action among individuals , communities and organisations to use water more wisely <p> Faced with the impacts of climate change and reduced rainfall , Western Australians are being urged to rethink their water use and reminded to practice waterwise habits as part of a new awareness campaign launched today by Water Minister Dave Kelly . <p> Launched on the first day of National Water Week ( October 19-25 ) , the Water Corporation 's new campaign aims to educate the community on how climate change has reduced rainfall and impacted WA 's water supply , how we as a State are responding , and what the community can do to keep up their waterwise practices . <p> WA 's climate has changed significantly since the 1970s , with the State 's south-west now one of the areas on the planet most affected @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , we have seen a reduction of over 80 per cent in the amount of rainfall runoff into our dams compared to the 1970s . <p> While the Water Corporation continues to secure Perth 's water supplies by adding more climate-independent sources to the mix , individuals , businesses , and governments all have a part to play in conserving our precious water resource . <p> This year 's National Water Week theme is Reimagining our Water Future , which invites Australians to reimagine the way they use and reuse water to ensure there is enough for future generations . <p> The educational campaign launched today , encourages Western Australians to make small changes to their daily water use practices and incorporates the tagline Think Climate Change . Be Waterwise . The first part of the campaign will run until the end of December 2020 across print , broadcast and digital media . <p> " Responding to the significant impacts of climate change on our water supply has never been more important . <p> " Perth has just experienced its fourth driest and warmest winter on record and , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ south-west of the State , we all need to continue being waterwise in the home and workplace to help conserve this precious resource . <p> " While the State Government is responding to the impacts of climate change on the ground , it is the Commonwealth Government that ultimately needs to be part of the global solution by <p> engaging with the international community on the issue , and taking real action to reduce the causes of climate change . <p> " Addressing climate change requires collaboration and action from everyone -- by thinking about the impacts of climate change on our water supply , and being waterwise , we can make a real difference .
