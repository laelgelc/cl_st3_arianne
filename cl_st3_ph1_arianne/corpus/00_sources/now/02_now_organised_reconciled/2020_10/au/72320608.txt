<p> PennyMac Financial Services , Inc . ( NYSE : PFSI ) ( the " Company " ) today announced the pricing of its offering of $150 million aggregate principal amount of its 5.375% Senior Notes due 2025 ( the " New Notes " ) . The New Notes will be issued at an offering price of 103% of the principal amount thereof , plus accrued interest from September 29 , 2020 . The New Notes will be issued under the indenture governing the Company 's $500 million aggregate principal amount of 5.375% Senior Notes due 2025 issued on September 29 , 2020 ( the " Existing Notes " ) . The New Notes , if issued , will be treated as a single series with the Existing Notes and will have the same terms as the Existing Notes , other than with respect to the date of issuance and the issue price . <p> The Company intends to use the net proceeds from this offering for general corporate purposes , which may include the repayment of the Company 's existing secured warehouse borrowings .
