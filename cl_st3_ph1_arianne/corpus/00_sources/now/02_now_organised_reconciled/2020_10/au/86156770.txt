In this new series , we 've invited them to share their unique photos from the field . <p> Though it may not be as famous a stereotype as shrimps on the barbie , deadly snakes or Vegemite , Australia is renowned in certain scientific circles for being the " land of the lizards " . <p> Australia has a higher diversity of lizards than anywhere else in the world . The number of different species within a single part of remote , central Australia well exceeds similar desert environments , such as the Kalahari in Africa , or the US . <p> Over the last 50 years , scientists have tried to understand the cause of this extraordinary and unique diversity . <p> Some suggest unpredictable resources in the arid outback , such as sporadic rain , favour low-energy animals like lizards over birds and mammals .
