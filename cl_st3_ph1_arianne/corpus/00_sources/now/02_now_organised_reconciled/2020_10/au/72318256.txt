<p> The recent release of the International Energy Agency ( IEA ) World Energy Outlook ( WEO ) has just been released -- all 450 ish pages of it . The IEA -- once set up to represent the oil industry , but now trying hard to shake off that history -- has finally turned the big ship around and has taken a small step towards joining the rest of us . <p> After several years of taking criticism for underplaying the growth of renewables like wind and solar , the IEA now declares that solar will become the ' new king ' of electricity , and has updated bullish forecasts for the growth of renewables in the coming decades , even in higher-emissions , worst case scenarios . <p> On the surface , this is a welcome change . But it 's still insufficient -- and could potentially clear space for the creation of new fossil fuel infrastructure , and increased extraction of oil and gas .
