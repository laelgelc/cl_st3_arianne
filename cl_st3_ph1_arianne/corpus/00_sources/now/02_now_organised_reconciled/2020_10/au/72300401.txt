To oversimplify them for the sake of brevity , there 's an oscilation between the two that makes a particular region of the planet either drier than average , or wetter than average . While the cycle itself is being affected by climate change long-term ( and not in a good way ) , that 's beyond the scope of this article . We 're interested in road safety right now .
