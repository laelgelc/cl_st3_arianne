<p> Minister of Housing , Urban Renewal , Environment and Climate Change , Hon. Pearnel Charles Jr. , has welcomed the Government 's partnership forged with the United Nations Children 's Fund ( UNICEF ) , through the Ministry , to address the impact of climate change on Jamaica 's children . <p> " Children and young people have contributed the least to the current climate change and environmental degradation , but they are the most affected and will continue facing the consequences . This undermines the rights of every child , particularly the most vulnerable and disadvantaged , " he told JIS News . <p> He spoke following a recent meeting with UNICEF Jamaica Representative , Mariko Kagoshima , and Deputy Representative , Vicente Teran , at Jamaica House . <p> Among the negative climate change factors impacting children are : limited access to potable water and healthcare , and developmental risks . <p> " Our children must remain at the centre of this climate change agenda and this collaboration with the Ministry will address the challenges and impact of climate change on our children , " Minster Charles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ partnership is expected to advance the ongoing policy and programme review exercise , to help ensure the integration of issues impacting young citizens . <p> UNICEF 's Representatives gave their undertaking to support the Ministry 's efforts to , among other things , rollout a youth consultation on climate change to elicit ideas from targeted participants on how to safeguard the environment . <p> Mr. Charles also took the opportunity to emphasize the need for greater partnerships as Jamaica looks to strengthen its resilience to climate change .
