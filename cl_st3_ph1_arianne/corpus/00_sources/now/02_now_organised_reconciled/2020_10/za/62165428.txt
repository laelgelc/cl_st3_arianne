<h> Australian state defers Adani coal mine royalties ahead of election <p> MELBOURNE - The Australian state of Queensland has agreed to defer royalty payments for Adani Enterprise 's Carmichael thermal coal mine , the state 's treasurer said on Thursday . <p> The royalty deferment comes ahead of a Queensland state election set for October 31 and with the current Labor state government set to enter a so-called caretaker period on October 5 , when no major decisions are typically announced prior to the vote . <p> State Treasurer Cameron Dick did not say when Adani would start to pay royalties or how much revenue the state would collect , citing commercial-in-confidence agreements . <p> " I can assure you that Adani will pay every dollar in royalties that they have to pay to the people of Queensland ... with interest , " Dick said in a news conference . " That 's absolutely locked in now . " <p> Carmichael 's development became a key issue during Australia 's 2019 parliamentary election and part of the reason for the Labor party 's shock defeat , after it failed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from voters in coal heartlands near the mine in central Queensland . <p> The royalty deferral is the second by the Queensland government in a policy to support new mines , after a similar arrangement was brokered for New Century Zinc last year . <p> Carmichael has provoked controversy in Australia because it would open up a new thermal coal basin at a time of growing concerns over global warming , in a region that is in need of jobs . <p> Analysts have questioned the mine 's viability , given a steep fall in coal prices in the past two years , and increasing pressure on banks and insurers not to lend to thermal coal companies because of climate change . <p> Adani has begun construction at Carmichael , which will start by producing 10 million tonnes per year of coal , and an associated rail project and expects first production in 2021.
