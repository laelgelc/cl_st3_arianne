<p> The Sasol share price has been on a phenomenal run , soaring more than 500% off its 52-week low with day traders suspected of fuelling its performance . In this announcement , the South African chemicals company has reached an agreement with Dutch-domiciled LyondellBasell Industries to sell a 50% stake of its three Base Chemicals Units ( Ethane cracker , LDPE and LLDPE ) for U$2bn to slash its debt . Sasol said the sale will reduce debt from U$10bn to U$8bn to improve its financial outlook .
