<p> The decision was reached on Tuesday , October 20 when the City of Cape Town 's Mayoral Committee ( Mayco ) unanimously supported the City 's decision to lift water restrictions in Cape Town and to move to the lowest tariff , being the no restriction , water-wise tariff . <p> The decision to lift water restrictions and lower water tariffs is based on the following three key considerations : <p> -- The National Department of Water and Sanitation 's ( DWS ) lifting of its restrictions applicable to the Western Cape Water Supply System ( WCWSS ) of shared dams , of which Cape Town is one of the users . Overall , the WCWSS dam levels reached 100% <p> -- City projections indicating dams are unlikely to drop below 50% by next winter .
