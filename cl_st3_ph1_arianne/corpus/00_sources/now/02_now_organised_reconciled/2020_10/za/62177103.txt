<h> There is a need for all countries to conserve the environment amid the ravages of the COVID-19 pandemic , which has added to the destruction of biodiversity worldwide , Cyril Ramaphosa has said . <p> President Cyril Ramaphosa said this when he addressed a virtual summit on biodiversity , which took place on the sidelines of the 75th UN General Assembly in New York City on Wednesday , 30 September 2020 . <p> " The coronavirus pandemic has badly affected the ability of national economies to respond to challenges like environmental degradation and climate change , " Ramaphosa said . " But even as we prioritise economic revival and reconstruction , we must maintain our collective commitment to environmental conservation . " <p> According to the South African leader , environmental conservation is more important , considering that there were strong links between environmental destruction and the emergence of new deadly diseases in humans . <p> " Biodiversity loss , deforestation , the loss of farmland , animal habitat loss and the consumption of wild species are creating conditions for infectious diseases that we will soon be unable to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Nations Environment Programme in the development of tools to track the future emergence of any Zoonotic diseases , " Ramaphosa said . <p> As the third most mega-biodiverse country in the world , with unique species and ecosystems found nowhere else on earth , Ramaphosa said that South Africa is committed to conserving the environment , in-keeping with its role as a signatory to the Convention on Biological Diversity .
