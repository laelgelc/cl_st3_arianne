<p> The Two Oceans Aquarium is proud to announce that it has joined forces with the international philanthropic organisation the Save Our Seas Foundation to increase awareness about sharks in order to protect and conserve these much-persecuted animals . <p> The Aquarium 's largest exhibit , the Predator Exhibit , which houses five ragged-tooth sharks , as well as other fish species , has been renamed the Save Our Seas Foundation Shark Exhibit . The two organizations will collaborate to increase communication efforts about marine conservation and research specifically related to sharks . <p> This partnership also includes the development of Shark Alley , shark information space behind the Save Our Seas Foundation Shark Exhibit , which will highlight charismatic shark species , shark biology , sharks under threat , shark-human relationships , and will provide other learning opportunities about sharks . <p> " The Save Our Seas Foundation is proud to sponsor the Two Oceans Aquarium , which is recognised around the world for its conservation and education efforts , " said Dr James Lea , CEO of the Save Our Seas Foundation .
