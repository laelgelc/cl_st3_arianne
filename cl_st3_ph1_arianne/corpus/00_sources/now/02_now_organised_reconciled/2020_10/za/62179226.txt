<h> John Steenhuisen asks DA delegates to support policy to do away with race <p> John Steenhuisen has urged party delegates to support the resolution that SA must stop classifying people based on race . Image : Esa Alexander <p> DA interim leader John Steenhuisen has written a letter to party delegates asking them to support the party 's resolution to embrace non-racialism and do away with race . <p> His letter comes as the party gears up for its elective conference later this month . <p> " In support of the recent decision taken by our policy conference , I 'd firstly like to ask you to support my resolution confirming that the DA wants a non-racial future for SA , " he said . <p> " Adopting this resolution will position us as the only political party in the country that unequivocally rejects the dangerous ideology of multiracialism , as well as the use of race as a way to categorise people in legislation , and commits the DA to actively working in government to end the practice of classifying and treating people on the basis of race @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for party leadership is hotting up . <p> He is due to go up against KwaZulu-Natal MPL Mbali Ntuli for the position of party leader . <p> In his letter , Steenhuisen said embracing non-racialism also holds the key to designing empowerment policies that work for millions of South Africans still trapped in poverty and locked out of opportunity . <p> " That is why I have submitted another resolution confirming the DA 's embrace of means-tested empowerment , which will put an end to the politically connected enriching themselves and ensure that empowerment policies benefit the 30 million citizens surviving on less than R992 per month , " he said . <p> Steenhuisen said the DA was more focused than ever on removing power from the " incapable " state and giving it to the people of SA . <p> " It is a battle we are waging with more vigour than ever before , because we know that our country will only succeed if the DA succeeds , " he said . <p> Both our economy and our environment are being held hostage by the current national government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " It is with this task in mind that I have submitted a number of resolutions that will be considered by the federal congress at the end of October . " <p> He said meaningful empowerment could only happen where there is sustainable economic growth . <p> " Both our economy and our environment are being held hostage by the current national government 's obsession with protecting state-owned enterprises like Eskom and SAA . <p> " The result of this obsession with state control makes it impossible for private citizens and companies to solve the electricity crisis , while simultaneously preventing a transition away from fossil fuels towards renewable energy . " <p> He said the DA should resolve to free citizens from Eskom so that it can tackle climate change and the electricity crisis head-on . <p> " We must also do everything we can to stop the government from taking R19bn meant for job creation and Covid-19 relief and giving it to SAA , so that a few wealthy people can fly around the world . "
