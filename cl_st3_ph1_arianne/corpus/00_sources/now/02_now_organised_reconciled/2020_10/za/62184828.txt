<h> Pandemic highlights Africa 's need for infrastructure spend <p> The African Development Bank estimates Africa 's infrastructure financing needs at up to $170-billion a year by 2025 , with an estimated financing gap of as much as $108-billion a year . <p> This is among the conclusions from an online webinar hosted by the African Development Bank . The panel comprised Lamine Lo , director : financing and PPPs in the Ministry of Economic Planning and Cooperation of Senegal ; Solomon Quaynor , Bank vice-president : private sector , infrastructure and industrialisation ; Jerome Haegeli , group chief economist and MD of Swiss Re , and Dr Srinivas Sampath , chief of the PPP Thematic Group at the Asian Development Bank . <p> He also says the pandemic posed a challenge also for institutions like the African Development Bank .
