<h> Kaliati wants Mbowe Park closed over child sexual abuse cases <p> Minister of Gender , Community Development and Social Welfare , Patricia Kaliati , has asked relevant security authorities to expedite the closure of Mbowe Filling Station car park , a popular public drinking joint in Lilongwe City , where cases of drug and sexual abuse on young girls are reportedly on the rise . <p> Speaking during a press briefing at the Capital Hill on Wednesday ahead of this year 's International Rural Women 's Day which falls on October 15 , Kaliati said it is appalling that some men are using the premises as a breeding ground for initiating girls into the drug trade . <p> The minister also expressed dismay with the indecent behavior of sexual abuse in broad day light mostly by car owners who patronize the joint . <p> She said : " I ask police officers and other security agencies to intervene in protecting our women facing different kind of abuses at Mbowe . <p> " We have learnt that at this place there are drug dealers christening themselves as ' Baptists ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Owing to this , we ask the law to take its course and bring to book all perpetrators involved .
