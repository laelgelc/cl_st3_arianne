<h> Newsletters <h> Newsletters <p> The province of Palawan is known for its majestic mountains and vast bodies of water , ultimately making it a favourite destination for tourists to explore . In contrast to its tropical scenery , the main city consists of souvenir shops , gardens , and other establishments tourists might want to visit . However , just beyond the bustling city are smaller communities residing on elevated terrains . <p> During a global crisis , small communities living far from urbanised cities are one of the most vulnerable . Unfortunately , one of its communities , Binta't Karis , has little to no water resources . <p> Considering that the community is situated around Mount Mantalingahan Protected Landscape , the highest point of Palawan , its water infrastructure is working against gravity .
