<h> DILG-NCR holds CDRA training for key officials of Pateros , LGOOs <p> CALOOCAN CITY , Oct. 9 ( PIA ) -- The regional office of the Department of the Interior &amp; Local Government in the National Capital Region through its Local Government Capability Development Division ( LGCDD ) held a Climate and Disaster Risk Assessment ( CDRA ) training for key officials of the Municipality of Pateros and select Local Government Operations Officers ( LGOOs ) . <p> According to DILG-NCR , participants from Pateros conducted a face to face training following prescribed health protocols , while LGOOs attended virtually via the Zoom platform . <p> The activity is a follow up of the 2019 results of the Comprehensive Development Plan ( CDP ) assessment of NCR LGUs conducted by the Metro Manila Comprehensive Development Plan Technical Assessment Committee and Secretariat ( MM CDP TACS ) . <p> Based on the review of the team composed of the Metro Manila Development Authority ( MMDA ) and DILG-NCR , Pateros requires capacity development intervention in the creation of their CDP . <p> DILG-NCR Regional Director Ma . Lourdes Agustin said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pateros will be able to create a data-driven CDP . <p> " The CDRA will help the municipality in determining investments necessary for local development , particularly in addressing the vulnerabilities and sensitivities of the LGUs for its Rehabilitation and Recovery Plan which is also a component of the CDP , " Agustin said . <p> " As for the DILG-NCR LGOOs , the training would help them in providing technical assistance to LGUs as well as in the formulation of policies that can help in the CDP formulation process , " she added . <p> Serving as resource persons for the five-day activity are key personnel from the Department of Human Settlements and Development ( DHSUD ) led by Julia Angela Mae Collado , Ibani Padao , Ruth Roxas , and Evangeline Palileo . Among the topics discussed are understanding fundamental concepts , terminology , and principles on hazards and climate change , the conduct of inventory of hazards , records of disaster and hazard susceptibility , climate projection , and exposure and risk analysis . <p> Lectures followed by breakout activities using actual LGU data was done to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for planning . As a final output , participants will do a virtual presentation of their draft CDRA as a follow-through activity .
