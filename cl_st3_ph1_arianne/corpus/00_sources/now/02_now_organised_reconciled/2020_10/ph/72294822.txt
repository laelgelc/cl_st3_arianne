coal use by 2030 and go carbon neutral three decades later . <p> The announcement gives China -- the world 's biggest polluter and second-largest economy -- an opportunity to show environmental leadership at the same time as the United States retreats from the issue under climate change sceptic Donald Trump . <p> While light on details , the plan is a game-changer if China is good to Xi 's word , and the pledge was welcomed by the European Union , which is already toughening up its own emissions targets . <p> " It comes at a very good time , " says Wendel Trio , of Climate Action
