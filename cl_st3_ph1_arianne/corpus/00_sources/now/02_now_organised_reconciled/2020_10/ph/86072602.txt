<h> Long-term COVID-19 containment will be shaped by strength , duration of immunity <p> New research suggests that the impact of natural and vaccine-induced immunity will be key factors in shaping the future trajectory of the global coronavirus pandemic , known as COVID-19 . " In the short term , and during the pandemic phase , NPIs are the key determinant of case burdens . However , the role of immunity will become increasingly important as we look into the future .
