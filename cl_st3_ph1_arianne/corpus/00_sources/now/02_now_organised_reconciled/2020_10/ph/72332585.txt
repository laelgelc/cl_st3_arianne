<h> ABS-CBN News <p> Federico " Piki " Lopez , Chairman and CEO of First Philippine Holdings Corp . is the MAP Management Man of the Year 2020 . Photo from FPHC <p> MANILA - The Management Association of the Philippines has chosen Federico " Piki " Lopez , Chairman and CEO of First Philippine Holdings Corp , as the MAP Management Man of the Year 2020 . <p> MAP said it chose Lopez " for passionately pushing for the country 's transition to a low-carbon economy through his various advocacies to proactively address the irreparable damage of climate change . " <p> It also recognized Lopez for championing the power industry 's deregulation , and steering the Lopez Group to the forefront of energy security and sustainability , and clean technologies for renewable energy and natural gas . <p> Lopez 's father Oscar , the family patriarch and a staunch environmentalist himself , also received the same award in 2000 . <p> MAP said the prestigious award is given to individuals in the business community or government for attaining unquestioned distinction in the practice of management and for contributing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for their integrity , leadership , and management qualities ; contribution to nation-building and values formation ; effective stewardship within the confines of the highest standard of business and management practice ; among others , MAP said . <p> The distinction of " MAP Management Man of the Year " has only been conferred 44 times in the five-decade history of the award . <p> Lopez was also cited for developing a deep bench of Filipino technical and managerial talent who are globally competitive , as well as his leadership role in the substantial contributions of the Lopez Group to national development in terms of production , value added , income generation and job creation . <p> The Lopez Group executive was also recognized for his charity work and for setting an example for Filipino managers through a track record of integrity , entrepreneurial excellence , professional competence and great leadership in his management career .
