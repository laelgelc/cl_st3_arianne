<p> Together with the local government unit of Ajuy and Iloilo State College of Fisheries - Tiwi Campus , BFAR Iloilo Provincial Fisheries Office implemented the rehabilitation of the mangrove area of Brgy . Silagon , Ajuy , Iloilo through a mangrove planting activity . <p> Office of the Presidential Assistant for the Visayas Assistant Secretary Jonji Gonzales said some 22,500 pieces of " bungalon " were planted at the 3-hectare mangrove area during the planting activity which was also participated in by members of the Silagon Fisherfolk Association who will benefit from the project . <p> Gonzales said " bungalon " is one of the native species of mangrove trees . <p> BFAR in partnership with the Commission on Higher Education ( CHED ) is implementing the mangrove resource rehabilitation and livelihood provision , one of the components of the Philippine National Aquasilviculture Program ( PNAP ) to help address climate change , food security and poverty among coastal fisherfolks . <p> It provided the funding while CHED provided the logistic support for the program . <p> Gonzales said another project launched by BFAR in northern Iloilo , particularly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sigla sa Ilog at Lawa ( BASIL ) . <p> A total of of 40,000 tilapia fingerlings were dispersed by the Iloilo Provincial Fisheries Office-BFAR 6 , local government of Batad LGU and the Mapinadayunon Rural Association of Barangay Alapasco through the project . <p> Gonzales said this will enhance the production of tilapia , one of the staple foods and main livelihood of the residents in Alapasco . <p> " Another component of the project in Alapasco is establishing Brush Park and planting of fruit-bearing trees in the area , " said Gonzales in his report during the Laging Handa Network Briefing on Monday , October 26 .
