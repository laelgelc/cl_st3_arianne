<h> PH leaning towards declaration of climate emergency -- DENR chief <h> ( FILE PHOTO ) <p> Metro Manila ( CNN Philippines , October 12 ) -- Programs and initiatives of the Philippine government are geared towards the declaration of a climate emergency , according to Environment Secretary Roy Cimatu . <p> In a statement over the weekend , Cimatu , who chairs the Cabinet Cluster on Climate Change Adaptation , Mitigation and Disaster Risk Reduction , said that declaring a climate emergency would help deepen the country 's commitment to protect the economy and environment from the adverse affects of climate change . <p> " The Philippines has already suffered billions of losses , damages and disruptions due to the impacts of hydrometeorological hazards , so there 's an urgent need to address more projected adverse impacts to ensure climate justice for the current and future generations of Filipinos , " Cimatu said . " All government programs and initiatives are leaning towards the declaration of climate emergency .
