<p> RM will turn 25 on Sept. 12 and fans in different countries have various projects to mark the occasion . <p> In the Philippines , Pinoy BTS ARMYs , led by Kim Namjoon PH , went to Sierra Madre , the longest mountain range in the country , in Siniloan , Laguna on Sept. 8 to plant the indigenous trees . This is the second year that RM and BTS fans held the " Plant Today , Save Tomorrow " project . <p> " To celebrate RM 's birthday , we planted 600 indigenous trees in Sierra Madre , the longest mountain range in PH , hoping to leave a legacy for RM &amp; ARMY and be part of the solution to climate change , " according to the Pinoy RM fans . <p> The tree planting was made possible in collaboration with Fostering Education &amp; Environment for Development ( FEED ) , a non-government organization that supports tree planting and sustainable development . <p> Before the activity was held , the fans were taught about the status of forests in the country , climate change and about FEED . <p> RM , whose real name is Kim @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ District in Seoul and grew up in Ilsan in Gyeonggi Province , South Korea .
