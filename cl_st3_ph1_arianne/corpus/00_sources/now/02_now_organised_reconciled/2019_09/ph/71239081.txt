<h> DOE hosts Visayas PubCon and IEC on EE&amp;C Act <p> CEBU CITY , Sept. 20 -- As the lead agency in institutionalizing energy efficiency and conservation , the Department of Energy ( DOE ) is currently developing the draft Implementing Rules and Regulation ( IRR ) of Republic Act 11285 , or the Act Institutionalizing the Energy Efficiency and Conservation ( EE&amp;C ) , through enhancing the efficient use of energy and granting incentives to EE&amp;C projects . <p> DOE Secretary Alfonso G. Cusi said , " The EE&amp;C Act is one of the priority programs of the Department for 2019-2022 . Thus , we will ensure the sustainable implementation of the rules and laws for the security of our energy supply through competition , access to bilateral markets , anti-monopoly measures , least-cost power , and the protection of the environment . " <p> The EE&amp;C Act aims to standardize energy efficiency and conservation measures in the country by regulating the use of energy efficient technologies in buildings . Enhanced EE&amp;C will go a long way in securing the sufficiency and stability of the country 's energy supply . Attaining these goals would also help cushion the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ market , and spur sustainable economic and social development . <p> During the public consultation , the DOE Energy Utilization Management Bureau ( EUMB ) Director Patrick T. Aquino emphasized that the DOE envisions the EE&amp;C Act as a national way of life , geared towards building the Philippines . <p> The EE&amp;C is seen to help bring down energy demand and increased energy security that would improve productivity and yield energy savings for consumers . <p> In addition , the DOE will implement be implementing EE&amp;C measures that could be utilized by the residential sector , through an intensified Energy Efficiency Labeling system . <p> Employees were also briefed on the Government Energy Management Plan ( GEMP ) that would reduce monthly energy consumption of Government Offices by 10% . The compliance to the GEMP is one of the mitigation options for the Philippines ' Nationally Determined Contribution ( NDC ) to reduce greenhouse gas emissions under the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> Moreover , the DOE is set to conduct more information campaigns and series of public consultations to solicit ideas , suggestions and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ EE&amp;C Act .
