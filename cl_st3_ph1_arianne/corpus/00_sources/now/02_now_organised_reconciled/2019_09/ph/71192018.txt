" <p> Globalization 4.0 , Briones said , served as a " rude awakening " to everyone given the proliferation of technological and digital innovations that are fraught with volatility , uncertainty , complexity and ambiguity . <h> ' Hard hit ' <p> " Agriculture will be hard-hit .
