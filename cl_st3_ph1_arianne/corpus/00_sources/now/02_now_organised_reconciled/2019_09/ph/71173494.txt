<p> " When we were young , the Earth was beautiful . We play in fields and plains , bathe in the rivers and were nourished by the seas and the mountains . Now that we are old , so is the Earth . It withered , paled and is now dying because of our doing -- wrongdoing . We have forgotten how to take care of our trees , animals , waters and land . We have become greedy , and disrespected our common home . And so today we appeal to everyone : let us renew our pledge to respect one another and the web of life , " Tagle said before the Walk for Creation 2019 opening mass at the Liwasang Aurora of the Quezon City Circle in Quezon City Sunday . <p> In 2015 , Pope Francis declared September 1 as the World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation . He also urged dioceses around the world to celebrate the Season of Creation . <p> Thousands of people from different schools , parishes , religious communities , civil society organizations , non-government organizations and private sectors joined the third Walk for Creation and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will address the challenges of the current ecological crisis . <p> Bishop Virgilio David of Caloocan and Bishop Honesto Ongtioco of Cubao were also present during the celebration . <p> According to Fr . Edwin Gariguez of Caritas Philippines , now is the time for people to act together to stop the effects of climate change . <p> " Sabi ni Pope Francis , ang ating care for our common home is very much part of our faith . This is not only a political or economic issue , this is very much part of living out our Christian faith , " he said . <p> Bishop David also remembered how the late Gina Lopez of ABS-CBN Foundation worked against mining and protected the watersheds .
