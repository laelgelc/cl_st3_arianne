<p> CALAMBA CITY , September 23 ( PIA ) -- To encourage local government units and provincial offices to go green in the construction , maintenance and operation of their buildings , DENR 4A hosted a Green Buildings Orientation in their Regional Office at Brgy . Mayapa , Calamba City last September 16 . <p> In partnership with the Laguna Climate Change Adaptation Office and the Philippine Green Buildings Council , the Department of Environment and Natural Resources Region 4A ( DENR 4A ) organized the activity also in celebration of the International Day of Preservation of the Ozone Layer . <p> " This activity is very important to us especially that we have just transferred in this building . It would also be good to share how we strive to be green in our activities and with our structures , " DENR 4A Regional Public Affairs Office Chief Ann Hazel Javier . <p> In general , the activity aims to orient the participants on the impacts of infrastructures and buildings to climate change and otherwise the impacts of climate change to buildings and infrastructures and especially the people who use these infrastructures . <p> Dr. Dolores Borja of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that the construction of buildings are done in accordance to sustainable development and ensure that resources are sustainably managed . <p> " Green Buildings consider four components including materials , energy , water and health to make it more sustainable , " she pointed out . <p> Buildings should have low maintenance and operation cost ; must be energy , water and material efficient ; have better and quality environment ; and should reduce impact to local resources , she further explained . <p> " A Green Building is designed in such a way to reduce overall impact on environment and human health , " she added . <p> Quoting DENR Secretary Roy Cimatu , she said there is a need for all the local government units to work together with DENR in addressing all the environmental challenges that confronts our society . <h> Philippine Information Agency <p> The Philippine Information Agency is the official public information arm of the Government of the Republic of the Philippines .
