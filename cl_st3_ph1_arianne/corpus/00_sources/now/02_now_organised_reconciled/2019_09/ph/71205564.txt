<h> Honasan calls for focus on climate change <p> MANILA , Philippines -- United Nationalist Alliance vice presidential bet Senator Gregorio " Gringo " Honasan II called on the public to pay attention to the worsening state of the country 's environment and the effects of climate change . <p> " I would agree with the Nobel prize winner , former US Vice President Al Gore , that we must develop people power to call for more public awareness on climate change and push for renewable sources of energy like solar , wind , and possibly , wave technology to be a source of power , " Honasan said . <p> Honasan , who hails from the Bicol Region which was awarded for having the Best Disaster Response Practice , said the " present generation should not only focus on the protection of the environment , but also in balancing public interest in the climate change issue . " <p> He added : " If we can protect our environment and at the same time balance public interest , this can very well be translated to public policy . " <p> In terms of local response , Honasan lauded @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disaster response protocol . <p> The senator is a known environmental advocate who continues to push for laws such as the Clean Air Act , Solid Waste Management Act and the bill on Land Use Policy . <p> Honasan also recommends that the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council ( NDRRMC ) focus more on improving its coordinating mechanisms to make sure that disaster protocols are properly followed and are understood by local chief executives down to the level of barangays
