<h> Back to basics : A climate resiliency approach <p> Amidst the threat of climate change , there is no other way to withstand but to go back to basics . <p> This is how the livestock industry advocates perceived to sustain the sector . <p> Reverend Father Rolie Ritchie Gomez , Director of Missionaries of Sacred Heart ( MSC ) - Center for the Poor said that going back to traditional knowledge is the best way to be resilient in the current times . <p> " In times of calamity , who will survive , it is the native , " he stressed . <p> Gomez coined the current situation as climate emergency that needs an immediate response of every Filipino to take the stand to save the planet earth , and at the same time be resilient . <p> " As you can see , when a disease will sweep an area , commercial animals will be easily wiped out , but our native survives against all odds , " Gomez said . <p> The advocacy of the Roman Catholic priest is in line with the advocacy campaign of the Department of Agriculture through the Livestock Section @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Back to Basics promoting native domesticated animals such as chicken , pig , goat , and ducks called Itik Pinas . <p> Dr. Rene Santiago , Center Director of the National Swine and Poultry Research and Development Center of the Bureau of Animal Industry ( BAI ) in Quezon province said that raising native farm animals has a great advantage over hybrid . <p> " Native animals has its natural ability to be climate-resilient . If its raining native chicken knows how to seek shelter but the hybrid line wo n't , thus , they have a high risk of diseases .
