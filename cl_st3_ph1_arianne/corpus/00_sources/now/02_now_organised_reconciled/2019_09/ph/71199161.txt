<p> Tagbilaran Bishop Albert Uy and Talibon Bishop Patrick Daniel Parcon said the move will not only cut power cost in parishes , parochial schools , and pastoral centers . " More importantly , it will help preserve the environment , " the bishops said . The prelates , in a statement , said they were currently in talks with private companies for the wide-scale installation of solar panels in the dioceses . <p> " Part of the cross that we carry as disciples of Jesus is the responsibility to care for the world that is entrusted to us . The solar program will minimize our dependence on power whose rates continue to go up while the services provided leave much to be desired , and hence our experience of brownouts , " Uy and Parcon said in a Catholic Bishops ' Conference of the Philippines ( CBCP ) News post . <p> The CBCP , earlier , called for the immediate transition to safe , clean , and affordable energy to help combat climate change . The Church leaders called for the use of renewable sources of energy as they rejected environmentally destructive sources such as coal and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ faithful to do their share in environmental protection . " Take good care of our common home because we are only servants , not owners . We are just stewards acting on behalf of a God whose love and compassion does not end , " the bishops said . <p> Caritas Philippines said that of the 85 archdiocese and dioceses in the country , around 40 are in the process of installing solar panels as alternative power sources . The CBCP said , the Diocese of Maasin in Southern Leyte shifted to the use of solar energy in August 2018 in its 42 parishes , making it the first ecclesiastical territory in the country with all its churches using solar power .
