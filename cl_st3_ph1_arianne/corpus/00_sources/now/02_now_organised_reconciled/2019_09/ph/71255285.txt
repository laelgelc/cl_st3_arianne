<p> L.A.-based Melina Duterte , who goes by her musical moniker Jay Som in indie music circles , finds her loudest voice on her third and latest album as Jay Som . In her previous album , Ms. Duterte already earned serious praise as an important emerging artist in both the international and local press . Today , she assumes a definitive profile in her indie music and at the same time , celebrates her Filipino roots starting with the album title , " Anak Ko , " the Tagalog phrase for My Child . <p> Calling her new body work her child can only mean she has produced or " given birth " to the full measure of Melina as a musician and as a Filipino .
