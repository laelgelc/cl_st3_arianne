<h> Is the Govt 's ban on new oil and gas exploration brave or naive ? <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern 's move to ban new oil and gas exploration permits is at once justifiable and yet arguably cavalier with a major industry . <p> Just transition or heart over head ? In grabbing back the political agenda after a messy March , the Government has made a string of brave calls , all intended to prove that it will walk the talk on a " transformative " agenda . <p> Some were well-signalled . For example , shifting transport funding from highway construction in favour of urban public transport , rail and road safety should have come as no surprise .
