<p> Media release from Dr Jenny Condie , Conor Hill and Norbert Hausberg In a full field of mayoral candidates for Wellington City , RNZ have made the controversial decision to broadcast a debate featuring only sitting councillors . <p> Mayoral contenders Dr Jenny Condie , Conor Hill , and Norbert Hausberg have banded together to offer a counter-debate . At 12pm today Tuesday 17 September , they will livestream and record a debate between themselves , moderated by Alex Braae of The Spinoff . <p> " RNZ 's decision has the unfortunate effect of excluding the two youngest candidates , myself and Conor , and excluding the two candidates who are running primarily on climate change , myself and Norbert , " said Dr Condie . <p> In national elections the criteria for being included in a debate are reaching 3% in the polls or currently having an MP in Parliament . <p> " I have n't heard of any polls conducted on the Wellington mayoral race , so there is no objective data for RNZ to justify excluding us , " Condie said . <p> When asked by Conor Hill why they had been excluded , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the prominence , reach and influence of each candidate 's campaign ... we also drew on ... news reports , informal polls , and conversations with community leaders . Those helped us figure out the top candidates . " Mr Hill has not received a response to a request to view the data referred to . <p> " Calvert is a first term councillor who was elected by a narrow margin , and more people voted for Foster as councillor than mayor last election , " said Hill . " It seems like RNZ just did a whip round the coffee room to decide , and now they are trying to justify that laziness . " <p> " It 's a nonsense , " Norbert Hausberg stated . " As the politically-neutral national broadcaster I think it 's irresponsible for them to promote only the incumbents , who already have all the advantages of being known figures . " <p> The debate is closed to the public .
