<h> Ban of investor rights to sue govts in giant RCEP trade deal <p> " Community groups welcome the reports of a statement by the Malaysian Trade Minister Datuk Darell Leiking that Malaysia and other governments have succeeded in excluding foreign investor rights to sue governments ( Investor-State Dispute Settlement or ISDS ) from negotiations for the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership ( RCEP ) , " Dr Patricia Ranald , AFTINET Convener said today . <p> The RCEP includes India , China , Japan , South Korea the ten ASEAN countries , Australia and New Zealand . <p> Dr Ranald said that Malaysia , India , Indonesia and New Zealand have policies against ISDS but this is the first public report that all RCEP countries have agreed to ban it from the RCEP deal . The RCEP talks have dragged on for seven years and ISDS opponents have made the ban a condition of finishing the negotiations this year . The report says that the ban may be reconsidered two years after the deal comes into force . <p> " All trade deals have government-to-government dispute processes .
