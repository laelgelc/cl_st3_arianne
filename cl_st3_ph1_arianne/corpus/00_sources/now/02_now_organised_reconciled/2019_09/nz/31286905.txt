<h> Global coffee entrepreneur Michael Allpress on the perfect brew <p> Michael Allpress has some advice for drinkers of the brew who want to help save the planet . <p> In 1986 , Michael Allpress fired up an expensive , shiny , handmade Italian espresso machine and set about , one cup at a time , converting Aucklanders to freshly ground and brewed coffee . It was a gamble for the 26-year-old , freshly returned from his OE in North America . He 'd saved just enough while working in hospitality in the US and Canada to buy the coffee machine and cart on which his future plans depended . <p> Saturday shoppers at Victoria Park Market voted with their taste buds : after three years , and with a trio of financial backers , Allpress upgraded to a La Marzocco , the Ferrari of coffee makers , found a permanent base and established what is now Allpress Espresso .
