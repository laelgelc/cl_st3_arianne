<p> Media release from Gwynn Compton Kapiti Coast mayoral candidate Gwynn Compton says Statistics New Zealand 's latest residential building consent numbers highlight that the current Council has failed to take meaningful action on housing , took the strong residential consent numbers it inherited for granted , and how fresh leadership is now needed to treat the issue with the urgency it deserves . <p> Statistics New Zealand 's data on residential building consents to July 2019 shows they are continuing to fall in Kapiti , now being 27 percent lower compared to the same 12 month period two years ago .
