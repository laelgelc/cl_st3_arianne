<h> A dehydrated national icon <p> Droughts and dying trees are hardening forest floors and starving Northland 's kiwi . Climate change threatens to double the region 's time spent in drought . <p> It was not what the farmer expected to find in the paddock . Desperate for water and unable to probe the dry , hardened forest floor for moisture-rich insects , young kiwi were venturing into exposed paddocks to find food . <p> It 's a problem Whangarei Native Bird Recovery Centre 's Robert Webb has seen in recent dry summers and it 's unlikely to stop .
