<h> Ecostore to be carbon neutral by year 's end <p> Ecostore is once again leading the way with today 's announcement the Australasian business will be carbon-neutral by the end of 2019 . <p> The goal is a keystone of ecostore 's 2018 Sustainability Report which will see both the New Zealand and Australia sites become carboNZero certified . <p> The only business in Australasia that makes and packages their products in a manufacturing plant that has been carboNZero certified since 2010 , ecostore has already offset 769 tonnes of carbon through the plant 's participation in the certification programme . <p> Ecostore has partnered with New Zealand 's leading provider of environmental certification , Enviro-Mark Solutions . The organisation will measure ecostore 's carbon emissions and help the team reduce and offset these emissions to achieve carboNZero certification beyond the manufacturing plant to cover the entire organisation , including the finished goods warehouse facility , flagship store , head office and satellite office in Australia . <p> Enviro-Mark Solutions chief executive Dr Ann Smith says , " Taking action on climate change , by reducing carbon emissions , is one of the biggest challenges faced by business @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ innovation . With ecostore 's ambitious plans to offset their impacts in the short term , and commitment to continual emissions reductions and environmental improvement for the long term , they are setting an excellent example for other organisations . " <p> Ecostore managing director Pablo Kraus says , " Businesses need to step up and recognise that they can and must be a force for good . Corporates have an incredible opportunity to pave the way for future generations . We must lead by example , empower others , act , make changes - and this report communicates how ecostore is delivering on our promise to lead the way . <p> " Climate change and plastic pollution are two of the biggest challenges we face in the 21st century . At ecostore we tackle this holistically through our operations , how we choose our partners , how we source our materials , and what we do about the lifecycle of our products . The company has a target to achieve 100 percent renewable or recycled content in their packaging by 2025 . <p> The company also has introduced new electric forklifts . Much like EV cars , these forklifts have no emissions , are quieter , require less maintenance and generally have a longer life than internal combustion engines . By recharging the battery during off-peak times these forklifts have less impact on the grid . <p> Ecostore general manager of supply chain , Tony Morpeth , says they are investigating further ways to minimise electricity , LPG and fuel use , waste and water use during manufacturing , along with carbon emissions from the freighting of their finished goods . <p> Morpeth says they are constantly seeking ways to reduce water use and wastage .
