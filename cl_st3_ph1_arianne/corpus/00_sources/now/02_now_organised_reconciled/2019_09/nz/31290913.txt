The award recognises people who have shown unique leadership and courage in standing up for human rights -- people who have acted on their conscience and used their talents to inspire others . <p> Amnesty International Aotearoa New Zealand Advocacy and Policy Manager Annaliese Johnston says the New Zealand groups have been pivotal to the increase in awareness on climate change and its impacts . <p> " The impacts of climate change are already hindering rights to health , food , water , housing , work and even life itself .
