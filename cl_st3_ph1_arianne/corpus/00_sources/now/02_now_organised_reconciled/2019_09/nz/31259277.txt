<p> As global citizens , we all want to change the world for the better -- but in anxious times , it can be difficult to know how to join the conversation about solutions to the world 's major issues . That 's where the Social Good Summit comes in . <p> Now in its tenth year , the Social Good Summit brings together leading thinkers -- activists , politicians , scientists , diplomats , artists , and more -- for a day of inspired conversation on how we can all help our global community achieve the UN 's Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) by the target year of 2030 . These goals seek to improve the quality of life for all people , lifting up everyone and leaving no one behind . <p> This year 's Summit will focus on a truly global issue , one that affects every single one of the SDGs : climate change .
