<h> Chief Sustainability Officer departs <p> Auckland Council 's Chief Sustainability Officer John Mauro has resigned to take up a role as a City Manager/Chief Executive in the USA . <p> John was appointed to the council role in November 2014 and finished in August . He has worked to advance progress on a range of issues on behalf of Auckland Council , including urban forestry , climate change , green energy and green finance .
