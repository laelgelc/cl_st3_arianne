<h> Export hydrogen an opportunity from 100% renewable generation - Woods <p> Hydrogen will play an important role in de-carbonising the New Zealand economy , but the bigger gain could be from the development of a new export fuel industry , Energy and Resources Minister Megan Woods says . <p> Hydrogen is being investigated as a potential heavy trucking fuel , a low-carbon process heat option and a way of reducing emissions from natural gas use . It has also been touted as a means of storing surplus wind and solar energy . <p> Woods told journalists today the value of a domestic hydrogen economy is " potentially huge " and will be " incredibly important " to the country 's efforts goal of having a net-zero carbon economy by 2050 . <p> " But what we want to do the work on , and talk further to industry around , is what it could be worth as an export industry , " she said after launching the government 's Vision for Hydrogen in New Zealand green paper . <p> " Everybody knows there will need to be some over-build in order to get to the 100 percent renewable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is a way for us to make that over-build , that over-capacity , economic and to actually create a whole new export opportunity .
