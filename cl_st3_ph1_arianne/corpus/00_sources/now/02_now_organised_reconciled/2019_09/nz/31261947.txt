" Today we are launching a Green Paper - A Vision for Hydrogen in New Zealand - that lays out the role hydrogen can play in New Zealand 's economy , and what we can do to accelerate its use , " she explained . <p> " For a country blessed with abundant renewable energy , the ability to convert our clean electricity into green hydrogen which can fetch a premium on global markets is a major economic opportunity . <p> " There 's already clear international interest in hydrogen sourced from New Zealand .
