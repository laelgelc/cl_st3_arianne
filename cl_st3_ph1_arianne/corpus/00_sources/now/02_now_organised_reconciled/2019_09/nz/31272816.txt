<h> Hydrogen in New Zealand - what 's going on ? <p> There 's been more talk recently about using hydrogen to power vehicles in New Zealand but notably little about the infrastructure required to support it . So just what 's going on ? <p> Energy and Resources Minister , Megan Woods , recently said that hydrogen power is one of the key ways we can fight climate change , and that our " green hydrogen " can fetch a premium on global markets . <p> The green paper supporting the announcement said there are several known hydrogen projects being explored by the private sector in New Zealand . These include a refining study at Marsden Point , the Ports of Auckland ( PoA ) hydrogen demonstration project , and a joint venture between Hiringa Energy and Ballance Agri-Nutrients in New Plymouth alongside the H2 Taranaki Roadmap . There 's also a geothermal-powered hydrogen project in Taupo and the rather vague " decarbonisation of tourist transport " in the South Island . <p> Let 's touch on the Ports of Auckland work first .
