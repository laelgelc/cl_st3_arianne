<h> Delivering power to the people <p> The energy costs for households and businesses in Tairawhiti are too high and a significant barrier to economic and social development . Energy costs are not limited to electricity but include heating ( wood/gas ) and transport ( oil/petrol/diesel ) costs . The simple fact is that in our region we pay far too much for energy and given our region 's below-average incomes , the cost of energy is disproportionately greater . <p> Our council does not have an effective energy strategy . There is no provision for it in the 10-year plan . Our council needs to urgently take a leadership role in identifying , initiating and influencing real energy-saving practices in Tairawhiti . This must occur on a macro and micro level . <p> Sustainable and renewable energy will positively impact on climate change and is an area in which our region and council should be a leader . <p> In 2013 Kapiti Coast District Council commissioned a report called Energise Otaki : Building a Sustainable Otaki Community -- Energy Use Patterns and Scoping of Renewable Energy Options for Otaki .
