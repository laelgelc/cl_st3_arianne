<h> As Amazon smolders , Indonesia fires choke the other side of the world <p> Trees and peatland are pictured during a fire in Palangka Raya , Central Kalimantan province , Indonesia , September 17 , 2019 . REUTERS <h> Brazil has captured global attention over deliberately set fires that are burning the Amazon rainforest , often called the earth 's lungs . Now Indonesia is compounding the concern with blazes to clear forest on the other side of the world . <p> Hundreds of wildfires burned across Indonesian Borneo and Sumatra on Tuesday , producing thick clouds of smoke that disrupted air travel , forced schools to close and sickened many thousands of people . Poorly equipped firefighters were unable to bring them under control . <p> Officials said that about 80% of the fires were set intentionally to make room for palm plantations , a lucrative cash crop that has led to deforestation on much of Sumatra . <p> The slash-and-burn conflagrations , which tore through sensitive rainforests where dozens of endangered species live , immediately drew comparisons to the wildfires in the Amazon basin that have destroyed more than 2 million acres . <p> " That @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ method and conducted by many people , " said Agus Wibowo , a spokesman for Indonesia 's disaster management agency . <p> The fires in Indonesia and the Amazon contribute to climate change by releasing carbon dioxide , a major greenhouse gas , into the atmosphere and by destroying trees and vegetation that remove such emissions from the air . <p> Aerial footage showed huge clouds of white smoke billowing up across vast tracts of Kalimantan , the Indonesian part of Borneo . Both Borneo and the island of Sumatra are home to endangered species of orangutan . <p> The disaster management agency identified 2,900 hot spots throughout Indonesia , including a large number of wildfires burning on Sulawesi and Java islands and in Papua province . <p> The fires occur annually at this time of year , the dry season , and have long been a contentious issue between Indonesia and its neighbors as the smoke drifts over Singapore and parts of Malaysia , including the capital , Kuala Lumpur . <p> The fires now are the worst Indonesia has seen in several years , in part because this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Joko Widodo , visited an area of Sumatra on Tuesday that has been among the hardest hit and said the government would seed clouds in the hope of bringing rain .
