Scientists found that diets in which meat , fish or dairy products were consumed only once a day would leave less of a footprint on climate change and water supplies than a vegetarian diet including milk and eggs , in 95 per cent of countries they analysed . <p> That is partly because raising dairy cows for milk , butter and cheese requires large amounts of energy and land , as well as fertilisers and pesticides to grow fodder , emitting greenhouse gases that are heating up the planet , the study said . <p> Diets that contain insects , small fish and molluscs , meanwhile , have as similarly small an environmental impact as plant-based vegan diets but are generally more nutritious , said researchers at the Johns Hopkins Center for a Livable Future . <p> They calculated
