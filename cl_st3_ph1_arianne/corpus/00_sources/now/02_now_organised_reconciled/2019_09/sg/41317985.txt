<h> " UWI to send post-Dorian relief for Bahamas " <p> Aid is on the way for the Bahamas in the wake of Hurricane Dorian , as the University of the West Indies ( UWI ) is readying its tactical support to aid the islands in the wake of the Category 5 hurricane . <p> Vice chancellor Professor Sir Hilary Beckles spoke on behalf of UWI , and expressed his deep sadness and concern for the people of the Bahamas and reiterated the university 's commitment to regional colleagues and partners . <p> Sir Hilary Beckles said that the UWI Disaster Risk Reduction Centre ( DRRC ) , established in 2005 , will work closely with the Caribbean Disaster Emergency Management Agency ( CDEMA ) to advise on and support the needs of the country as necessary . <p> " The extreme vulnerability of our region is now finally globally recognised as an existential threat caused by climate change and global warming . Once again we have witnessed this truth in the extensive destruction and tragic deaths caused by the growing intensity of hurricanes .
