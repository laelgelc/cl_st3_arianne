An accident of geography The trauma experienced by Begum and Hossain is becoming increasingly common in low-lying Bangladesh , one of the most vulnerable countries to the impact of climate change , including rising sea levels , cyclones , floods and river erosion . Millions of rural and coastal villagers are at grave risk of further climate change-infused disasters in the near future , according to a BBC report published Sept. 2 .
