French luxury fashion label Dior is the latest to tap on the trend . <p> Its Toile de Jouy is a beautiful box set containing six straws in two designs . One has a fully gold-plated body , and the other features a gold spiral across a transparent body . The straws are made of glass that boast the artisanal treatment of being hand-blown and hand-painted .
