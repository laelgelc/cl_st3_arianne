Some global brands even release new collections weekly . <p> This system leaves around 350,000 tonnes of used clothing going into landfill every year in the UK alone . The cost to the national economy of this wastage is estimated at over US$1 billion . <p> Worldwide , the fashion industry accounts for around 10 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions , contributing more each year than the aviation and maritime sectors combined , according to the UNFCCC , the body in charge of international climate negotiations . <p> The production of cellulose- and protein-based fibres competes for arable land , often encroaching on forests in Indonesia , Canada and on the Amazon . <p> Up two-fold in five years , 150 million trees were felled in 2018 for the production of fibres such as viscose , one of the most commonly used for fast fashion products , according to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ throughout the textile manufacturing process , beginning with the harvesting of fibres such as cotton .
