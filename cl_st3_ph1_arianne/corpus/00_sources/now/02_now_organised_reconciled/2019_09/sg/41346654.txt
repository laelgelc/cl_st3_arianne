<h> Concerted effort needed to tackle climate change <p> Climate change and environmental woes now constitute a long-term existential threat for many nations , and eventually for the whole world . <p> Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong suggested we need to spend $100 billion over the next 100 years to mitigate it . For Singapore , a threat of this scale and nature requires well-coordinated efforts involving every one of us . <p> First , we need to identify all man-made factors , such as policies , mindsets , priorities and behaviours that exacerbate the woes , and conversely , those that could reverse or improve the situation . <p> For example , what is the optimal economic or population growth we should achieve so as not to speed up the deterioration of our environment ? Also , what are the opportunity costs of over-conservation ? <p> We need to cultivate a culture of sharing . What are the hindrances to the development of such a culture ? Once our sharing culture is at a high level , we could cut down on buying many types of equipment and services . <p> We must make the best use of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ percentage of electric cars on the roads and number of households using solar and other non-fossil fuel energy . We should examine why we use big buses during off-peak hours . These buses are almost empty for most of the day . Are smaller electric buses a better option ? <p> Re-examine our urban planning blueprint to see how we could cut short the journey from home to workplace . For example , how many people staying in the east of the island travel to the west to work , and vice versa ? <p> We could also speed up research on developing underground habitats and floating cities . <p> It requires a holistic , whole-of-government effort to study and implement ideas to make the best use of our resources with minimal adverse impact on the environment in the long term .
