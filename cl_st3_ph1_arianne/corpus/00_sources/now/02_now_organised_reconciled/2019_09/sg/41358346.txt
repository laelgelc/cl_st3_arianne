<p> The Indus River , the vertebra of our country , runs 3,200 kilometres in total and , if cared for , is capable of providing sustenance to all , from Kashmir to the Arabian Sea . But without the release of freshwater into the Indus , the coastal region of Pakistan is running dry . The fifth-largest delta in the world is shrinking . <p> A delta is formed at the mouth of a river , when the river sheds its sediment load , before meeting a slower moving water body such as an ocean , sea , lake and sometimes another river . <p> The currents of a fast moving river eventually become weak , making it difficult for the river to carry its sediment load any further . The sediment is then dropped at a delta , making it a highly fertile area , before the river concludes its journey by joining another water body . <p> The Indus Delta , this meeting between the Abasin ( Pashto for ' Father of Rivers ' ) and the Arabian Sea , was once a union of prosperity . Residents there used to be traders , agriculturalists and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ despondency and death . <p> Spread out in the shape of a fan , the Indus Delta covers an area of 41,440km ? and approximately 210km where it meets the sea . It has shrunk manyfold over the decades . It is a complex ecosystem , consisting of swamps , streams and the seventh-largest -- and now threatened -- mangrove forest in the world . <p> It is also home to various species of fish and the famous pink Indus dolphin , along with being an important stop on the route for migratory birds . A dying Indus Delta restricts migration and may lead to the extinction of many species of rare birds . <p> A source of ecological services and economic benefits , the Indus Delta was added as a wetland site to the 1971 Ramsar Wetland Convention , an international treaty for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands .
