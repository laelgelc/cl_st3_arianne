<h> Voices Of Youth <h> Green ideas for school commutes <p> If the Government can play a part by subsidising school bus fees , perhaps more parents would be persuaded to put their children on the school bus , and that would help reduce our carbon footprint.PHOTO : ST FILE <p> I appreciate the Government 's efforts to mitigate climate change by reducing our carbon emissions and have some suggestions . <p> Many parents in Singapore ferry their children to and from school . Rising school bus fees only exacerbate the problem because parents who may have otherwise let their children take the school bus now find it does not make sense financially . <p> If the Government can play a part by subsidising school bus fees , perhaps more parents would be persuaded to put their children on the school bus , and that would help reduce our carbon footprint . <p> For parents who still prefer to drive their children to school , schools could step in and facilitate carpooling among them . This would take many cars off the road and ease the early morning traffic jam around Singapore . <p> In the name of reducing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ endure a few more chattering children during the early morning journey . <p> Ashlyn Chua , 14 <p> Secondary 2 student <p> CALLING YOUNG READERS : If you are a student or aged 21 years or below , and want to air your opinion on any report or letter in The Straits Times , e-mail your letter to stforum@sph.com.sg , with the subject header " Voices of Youth " .
