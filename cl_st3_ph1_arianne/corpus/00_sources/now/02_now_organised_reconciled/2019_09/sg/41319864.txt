<h> Singapore 's oasis-in-the desert pavilion set to steal the show at Expo 2020 Dubai <p> As many as 192 countries will take part in the six-month Expo 2020 Dubai opening on October 20 next year but Singapore will indubitably steal the show at the once-in-five-years event with its sprawling pavilion representing the nation 's unique City-in-a-Garden image . <p> Themed ' Nature . Nurture . Future ' , the 1,550-square-metre pavilion will be a net-zero energy building that will showcase a ' city in a garden ' , a multi-layered three-dimension green space with three nine-metre-tall , tree-like garden cones reflecting Singapore 's liveability , sustainability , and resilience . <p> Designed by Woha Architects and produced by Radius Experiential International , both based in Singapore , the eye-catching structure is being constructed by the country 's Urban Redevelopment Authority , and will be ready by August 2020 . Singapore had earlier participated in the World Expo in 2000 , 2005 and 2010 . <p> According to Woha Architects ' co-founding director , Wong Mun Summ , the nature-focussed project is aimed at creating an oasis in the Arabian desert .
