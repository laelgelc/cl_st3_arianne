We ask all delegates to attend , " he appealed . <p> TPSF , he explained , will have to meet members ' expectations , address problems and challenges faced by the private sector in order to remain relevant to national vision of making Tanzania a middle-income industrial country by 2025 . <p> TPSF is the umbrella organisation of the private sector , which champions the positions and interests of the private sector within and outside the fora of the Tanzania National Business Council ( TNBC ) . <p> On @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ TNBC , President John Magufuli called on the private sector to have impact on rural lives . <p> Simbeye said the AGM will have to discuss the presidential call and offer guidelines on implementation of that agenda . The TNBC is composed of 40 members ; 20 from the private sector and 20 public sectors . <p> Simbeye said delegates will also review Tanzania 's policy towards industrialization , corporate governance within the TPSF , extension of the foundation 's services , protection of environment and climate change . <p> The TPSF Director of Membership , Zachy Mbenna , called on delegates to provide concrete proposals on unity within the business community . <p> " We ask all members to come forward with solid proposals to tackle emerging challenges in order to have positive business results in trade and industry , " he said . <p> Minister of State in Prime Minister 's Office tasked for investment , Angellah Kairuki is expected to be the Chief Guest during the important meeting .
