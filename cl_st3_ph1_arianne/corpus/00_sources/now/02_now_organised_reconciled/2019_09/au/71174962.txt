<h> Feuding Sensory Lab Customers Stage Peace Summit at The European <p> Their battle for the title of number one customer has involved framed photos , custom T-shirts , wall-sized projections and even a tattoo . But can they work it out ? <p> Photography : Nick Buckley <p> Published on 02 September 2019 <p> by Nick Buckley <p> Share <p> Two weeks ago a feud began between two customers of Collins Street coffee shop Sensory Lab . Both auction house worker Fraser Harvey and urban planner Harriet Noall believe they are the store 's number one customer and have gone to extraordinary lengths to make their cases . What began with Harvey taping an A4 " Customer of the Week " print-out with his face on it to the cafe 's wall quickly escalated after Noall installed her own tribute to her patronage : a framed photograph of herself . <p> " On a daily basis stuff happens , and I ignore lots of it until it brews into something slightly more , " says Sensory Lab owner Salvatore Malatesta . " When I first saw this A4 sheet of paper I thought , ' That 's cute @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . And then an hour later saw a framed photo . I thought that was the end . " <p> A bigger frame was installed by Harvey , followed by a wall-sized projection from Noall . Harvey kicked things up a notch with T-shirts and an unauthorised , Fraser Harvey-specific loyalty-card program before Noall sent the situation nuclear by getting a tattoo of a Sensory Lab cup on her ribs ( read the Guardian 's thorough investigation for a full account of the antics ) . <p> On Thursday last week Noall and Harvey staged a peace summit at The European , and Broadsheet was there . " If we can resolve this then long-running issues like ... climate change should n't really be that hard to agree on , " said Noall . <p> But at the meeting -- a lunch Malatesta footed the bill for -- a resolution was not forthcoming . <p> " In light of the recent G7 summit and the lack of progress there , we do n't hold a high standard for ourselves , " said Noall . Neither party was willing to budge @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ blows were traded . <p> " Harriet 's heading over to England . I think with all that 's happening to Brexit over there she could inspire the UK to be even more petty than they fuckin ' already are , " said Harvey . <p> Harvey has suggested that free coffee for life at Malatesta 's Sensory Lab and St Ali outlets could bring an end to the feud . <p> " I 'm happy to buy both of them lunch , " says Malatesta . " But coffee 's one thing we do n't give away . " <p> As they were leaving the European Broadsheet asked the pair to pose for a photo outside the restaurant . They shook hands and then headed inside to drink red wine on Malatesta . Even without definitively settling the issue there seemed at least to be a truce between Sensory Lab 's number one customers . Which is also as much as can be said for the attendees of the recent G7 summit .
