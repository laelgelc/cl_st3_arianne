Consider the number of moving surfaces in such a unit . Each piston begins its cycle by sucking in fuel on a downward ( intake ) stroke , compressing it on the upward ( compression ) stroke , being driven down by combustion on the next stroke and then expelling the waste gases on its next upward travel ( the exhaust stroke ) .
