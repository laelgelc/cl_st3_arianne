About 500 people are expected to congregate at Castlemaine station , to catch the train to Melbourne 's School Strike for Climate where they will put their demands to the state 's leaders . Nine Mount Alexander Shire Council Mayors - eight former , one current - will be among them , signalling their support for the young people and their demands .
