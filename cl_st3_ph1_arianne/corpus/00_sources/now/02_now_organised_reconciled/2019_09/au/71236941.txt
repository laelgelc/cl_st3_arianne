Internationally some pharmacies have a ' buddy system ' for emergency and disaster response with surrounding pharmacies . This ' buddy system ' allows for sharing of resources ( such as medications and staff ) during a crisis . In a pandemic this ' buddy system ' could be used to condense all pandemic services ( such as antiviral dispensing ) into one pharmacy , reducing the risk of otherwise well people being exposed to those that are infected . <p> " Partner with those around you .
