<h> This new Maldives hotel combines luxury with sustainability <p> Sustainability has become a key concern for holidaymakers , with many travellers wanting to make more eco-friendly travel choices when we vacation . <p> Prince Harry recently launched his new eco-tourism project , Travalyst , which will bring industry players ( like Booking.com and TripAdvisor ) together to protect wildlife habitats and combat climate change . " I believe we can -- and we must -- find new ways to minimise the dangers and maximise the opportunities of tourism , " he said at the launch of the initiative . <p> It 's a sentiment shared by InterContinental Hotels and Resorts .
