<p> When the climate campaign group Extinction Rebellion occupied five prominent sites in central London in April 2019 , just six months after it launched , it became one of the biggest civil disobedience events in recent British history . Over 11 days of demonstrations , thousands of activists marched , blocked bridges , glued themselves to trains and parked a pink boat in the middle of Oxford Circus . A total of 1,130 protesters were arrested , 10,000 police officers were deployed and an estimated half-a-million people were affected by the disruption . <p> Since then , the movement has gained a global following : its Berlin branch chained themselves outside Angela Merkel 's Chancellery ; protesters in Paris blocked the Pont de Sully bridge over the Seine ; and campaigners in New York stopped traffic outside the New York Times , urging the newspaper to take action ( it eventually scrapped plans to sponsor one of the world 's biggest oil industry conferences ) . <p> Up next ?
