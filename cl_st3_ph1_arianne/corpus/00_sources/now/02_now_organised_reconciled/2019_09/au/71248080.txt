<p> WA 's Health Department will spend almost $750,000 on a year-long inquiry into how it can help tackle climate change and cope with its impacts . <p> While Health Minister Roger Cook has defended the inquiry , which he said was a " priority recommendation " of a long-running review into the sustainability of the health system , opposition spokesman Zak Kirkup argued the money should be spent on fixing hospital ramping or violence in emergency departments . <p> According to its terms of reference the inquiry will review how the health system is responding to the health impacts of climate change and " recommend a framework for evaluating future implications " . <p> It would also focus on " the likely benefits ( health and wellbeing , social and economic ) arisingfrom climate change mitigation strategies " . <p> Mr Kirkup questioned whether the money could be better spent given " nearly every key indicator is showing that our state 's health system is getting worse " . <p> " We 've got record high levels of ambulance ramping , historic blow outs in emergency department wait lists , the highest levels of assaults on nurses and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it needs to urgently spend nearly $750,000 ?
