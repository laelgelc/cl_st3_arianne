<p> DYSTOPIAN futures are all the rage but Brisbane novelist Rohan Wilson 's latest book is a bit close to the bone . Still , it 's set in 2075 and some people wo n't be around to worry about what happens then . <p> Those who will be should be a tad worried and his novel Daughter of Bad Times explores that world of the future .
