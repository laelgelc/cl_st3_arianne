<p> Burning is a common farming practice and has been used for decades in many parts of Brazil . After clearing vegetation , lighting a fire can be a relatively fast and easy way to clear remaining bush and debris , and prepare the soil for agriculture . <p> In fact , cattle farming and ( to a lesser extent ) soybean production are the main reasons for land clearing in the Amazon . However , selective logging , road construction , and other infrastructure developments also motivations for illegal , small-scale land clearing . <p> But , recently the situation has become more volatile with longer and more severe droughts , and that means wet areas of forest are becoming drier . <p> As a result , fires that start in pastures and once stopped in the forest , are now spreading more easily and consuming rainforest areas . <p> Brazilians have joined global protests against President Jair Bolsonaro 's stance on the environment .
