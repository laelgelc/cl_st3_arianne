<h> Catastrophic gray whale deaths as Arctic ice melts <p> A catastrophic marine ecosystem crisis of unimaginable consequences is taking shape . One that is impacting whales , walruses , polar bears , seals , sea birds and the fundamental zooplankton , on which the cycle of life for the entire food web depends . <p> The Arctic is melting . Sea ice is rapidly disappearing as a result of climate change and warming seawater temperatures . According to a CNN report , the Arctic is heating up twice as fast as the global average causing massive melting of sea ice . <p> Since 1990 , the thickness of sea ice has decreased by a third , according to the Fram Strait Arctic Observatory .
