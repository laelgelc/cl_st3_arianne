Meanwhile , we 've seen increasing rates of suicide among rural communities . <p> Elsewhere in the world , research similarly shows being affected by extreme weather events is a major risk factor for mental illness . The relationships between climate events and mental health are complex and not always apparent . <p> Extreme heat has been observed to be harmful to multiple aspects of mental health and wellbeing . Data from South Australia demonstrates hot days are associated with increased hospital admissions for mental and behavioural disorders . <p> A less-obvious impact arises from the strong connection between nutritional status and mental health . Climate-related impacts on agriculture lead to reduced availability of nutritious foods , and poor nutritional intake can affect mental health . <h> So , what can be done ? <p> The AMA 's recent statement has echoed calls from other medical associations for leadership on a national strategy for health and climate change .
