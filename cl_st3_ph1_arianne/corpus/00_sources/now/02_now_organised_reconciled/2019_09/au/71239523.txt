<p> It 's the kind of astonishing story that could serve as the dramatic spine of an alternate history novel : a Chinese billionaire steps in to save the victims of US manufacturing 's decline . And yet American Factory , now on Netflix , is a stunning work of documentary -- and all the more nightmarish for its depiction of the real-world consequences of capitalist degeneration . <p> On the concrete plains of Dayton , Ohio , the working class and the middle class became the new precariat in 2008 , when General Motors shuttered its assembly plant and 10,000 jobs were lost . <p> In 2010 , that factory was touted as an icon of manufacturing 's rebirth via foreign investment , with a Chinese industrial giant -- Fuyao Glass America -- at the helm . <p> Two thousand American workers were hired back by Fuyao and many more Chinese workers and supervisors were imported on low wages and lower expectations of workplace safety .
