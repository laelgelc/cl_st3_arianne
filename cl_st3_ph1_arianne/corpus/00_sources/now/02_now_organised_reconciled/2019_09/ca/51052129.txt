They carry bows and arrows , but feel increasingly vulnerable as they brace for run-ins with illegal loggers . <p> Tension over territory reached a new height after a surge in annual fires , usually set to clear land , devastated large areas of the Brazil 's Amazon region in past weeks . Some indigenous people who live in the Amazon say deforestation is encroaching on their lands and way of life . <p> The problem is old -- and escalating .
