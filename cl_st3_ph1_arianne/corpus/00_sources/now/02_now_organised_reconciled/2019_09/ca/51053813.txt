These early experiences inspired him to pursue a lifelong research career in marine biology and ecological conservation . <p> Pentz is a PhD candidate in Environmental Studies at UTSC . He was awarded the AbbVie IBD Scholarship by Crohn 's and Colitis Canada in August for academic excellence and community service as a graduate student . <p> The urgency of overcoming the climate crisis <p> The ocean is a familiar subject to Pentz .
