With polls suggesting the two could be in a fight for third place , both leaders will need to stake out their own turf as they jockey for left-of-centre voters concerned about the environment . <p> While the NDP released its full campaign platform in June , the Green Party put out its environmental vision , Mission Possible , last May . Though the Greens have yet to release a full platform , the party does have an extensive policy document available on its website , which it says was used to build the platform .
