<p> Communities across southern Manitoba were drenched with rain over three days going into the weekend , and Environment and Climate Change Canada has released numbers to show exactly which ones were hit the heaviest . <p> Zhoda tops the list , having received 144 mm of rain between Thursday night and Saturday night .
