<h> Fuels , Plastics and Feedstocks From CO2 ? They 're Working On It <p> Researchers at Stanford and SLAC are working on ways to convert waste carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) into chemical feedstocks and fuels , turning a potent greenhouse gas into valuable products . The process is called electrochemical conversion . When powered by renewable energy sources , it could reduce levels of carbon dioxide in the air and store energy from these intermittent sources in a form that can be used any time .
