Submitted photo <p> NEWS RELEASE SIMCOE MUSKOKA DISTRICT HEALTH UNIT **25;4074;TOOLONG Simcoe Muskoka Medical Officer of Health Dr. Charles Gardner is one of three speakers at a special forum on climate change and health , taking place in Barrie . <p> Hosted by a group of Barrie physicians , the talk on the health impacts of the climate crisis takes place on Sept. 18 , from 7 to 9 p.m. in the Rotunda of Barrie 's City Hall . Elected representatives from each level of government -- municipal , county , provincial and federal -- have been invited as well . The event is called Barrie Doctors On Climate Solutions ( Barrie DOCS ) . <p> The physicians hope to increase awareness and educate the community about health impacts by the climate crisis .
