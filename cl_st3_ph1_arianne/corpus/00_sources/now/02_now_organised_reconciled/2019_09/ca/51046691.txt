<h> TD Tree Days to help drive target of one million trees by 2030 <p> Chippewa Creek EcoPath got another boost today when 30 volunteers planted 500 new trees and shrubs as part of the 10th Annual TD Tree Days . This effort will help provide more tree cover for the city , build climate change resiliency and help reduce run-off to Chippewa Creek . <p> TD Tree Days has been bringing people together since 2010 and is helping to create a more vibrant planet by growing and enhancing green spaces , contributing to TD 's Ready Commitment target of helping plant one million trees by 2030 . Over 390,000 native trees and shrubs have been planted in communities across North America through TD Tree Days . <p> This fall , 10,000 volunteers will come together to enhance over 100 green spaces with more than 38,000 trees and shrubs . <p> Together with TD employees , their families and friends , and community volunteers , TD Tree Days helps create green spaces that are critical to building more resilient and livable communities . <p> In 2018 , TD launched a new corporate citizenship platform called The Ready Commitment -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ inclusive and sustainable tomorrow by supporting initiatives and programs within four areas : Financial Security , Vibrant Planet , Connected Communities , and Better Health . <p> North Bay-Mattawa Conservation Authority hosted TD Tree Days . <p> NBMCA has been planting trees and restoring shorelines within the watershed for the past five years as part of the Restore Your Shore Program . <p> Dialogue and debate are integral to a free society and we welcome and encourage you to share your views on the issues of the day .
