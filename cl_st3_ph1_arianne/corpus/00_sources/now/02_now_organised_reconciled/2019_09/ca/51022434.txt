<h> Town focuses on high priority areas for Climate Action Plan <p> Organics Recycling Underway in Canmore Canmore 's residential food waste program officially launched on September 3 with residents disposing of food scraps and food soiled paper at the five different locations throughout the community . On the first day , 1,000 kg of food waste was deposited . The Town said they only found one piece of garbage in the food waste containers . Council approved the purchase of 2,000 additional home food waste bins . The original purchase of 2,000 bins were picked up in the first two weeks by residents eager to reduce the amount of waste they send to the landfill . The 2,000 additional home food waste bins will cost $10,000 .
