<p> As millions of Canadians eagerly anticipate the arrival of warm weather , many people living in Canada 's North will be lamenting the end of winter . <p> For the Inuit , milder temperatures mean the sea ice is melting , making travel more difficult . This ice melt is happening earlier and earlier in the spring because of climate change . In the tiny community of Rigolet , in Nunatsiavut -- located on the northern coast of Labrador -- research shows this is affecting the mental health of the population . <p> For Derrick Pottle , 60 , the land is the gateway to his livelihood .
