His mission also offered insights into the effects of climate change on the hard-to-reach communities living in the Amazon . <p> " When I saw those images of the Amazon on fire , like everyone else , I felt shocked , powerless and angry . It did n't make sense to me , " Coster-Waldau said . " Which is why I wanted to come to Peru and find out why the Amazon is on fire . " <p> What Coster-Waldau discovered was that the deforestation in the Peruvian Amazon , to which the fires are attributed , can be traced back to economic and social inequalities facing the communities living in this region @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in safeguarding the Amazon rainforest , which covers more than 60 percent of Peru and is critical for our planet because it holds about 20 percent of the river water on Earth . <p> But in the Peruvian Amazon , many of these communities also face high levels of poverty and inequality and lack basic infrastructure and resources .
