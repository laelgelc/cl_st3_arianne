<h> INSPIRE <p> We are delighted to announce our 9th Annual INSPIRE event featuring an evening of networking , celebration and education , that will take place on October 1 , 2019 at Vancouver 's iconic Science World . Come and join us to launch the 2020 Small Business BC Awards alongside 400 of BC 's leading entrepreneurs and CEOs , small and medium-sized business owners , government representatives and industry influencers . <p> This year 's INSPIRE will address the positive impact businesses and entrepreneurs can have on climate change . Encompassing networking , inspiration and a lively panel discussion , we 'll tackle how starting a sustainable business , or adapting new green technologies and operations , has allowed for increased business success and profitability . Looking for inspiration before the big night ? Check out Clean BC 's Resources for Small Business .
