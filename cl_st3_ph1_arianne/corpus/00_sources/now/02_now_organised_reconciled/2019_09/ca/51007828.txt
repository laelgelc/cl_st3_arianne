<p> A new national park reserve will mean the protection of a series of barrier islands off of Prince Edward Island 's north shore . <p> On Wednesday , federal Environment Minister Catherine McKenna announced the first phase - a feasibility assessment - of making the Hog Island Sandhills into protected land . <p> McKenna said Canada is in the process of doubling the amount of nature protected to help recover species at risk , fight climate change and provide Canadians with the opportunity to discover the country 's nature . <p> " The Hog Island Sandhills are very special to the Mi'kmaq people . We propose to protect and preserve this special place , including its nature and its cultural sites , " said Lennox Island First Nation Chief , Darlene Bernard . <p> Hog Island Sandhills is a stretch of barrier Islands that stretches 50 kilometres along the northwest coast of the Island . The islands ( George Island , Fish Island and Bill Hook Island ) are P.E.I.
