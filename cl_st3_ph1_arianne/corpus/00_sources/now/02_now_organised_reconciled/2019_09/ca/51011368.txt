<p> Each year the EIU gives 140 cities scores out of 100 on a range of factors such as living standards , crime , transport infrastructure , access to education and healthcare , as well as political and economic stability . <p> Vienna -- known for its convenient public transport , refreshing Alpine tap water and varied cultural life -- scored 99.1 points out of 100 , as it did last year . <p> " Western Europe and North America continue to be the most liveable regions in the world , " the EIU said in a press release . <p> Europe claimed eight of the top 20 spots , with cities in Japan , Australia , New Zealand and Canada making up the rest . <p> However , the City of Light , Paris , dropped six places to 25th due to the anti-government " yellow vest " protests -- images of which went around the world . <p> For the first time , the index noted the effects of climate change on liveability , with New Delhi and Cairo plunging in the rankings to 118th and 125th place respectively due to " poor air quality , undesirable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We expect problems relating to climate change to put increasing pressure on liveability scores in the coming years and for the number of cities affected to grow , " Agathe Demarais of the EIU said . <p> Improvements in livability that had taken place in emerging markets ' cities in recent years -- thanks to greater stability as well as better education and healthcare -- " are under serious threat from an increasingly adverse climate , " she said . <p> London and New York rank 48th and 58th respectively as they continue to struggle under the perceptions of the risk of crime and terrorism and overstretched infrastructure . <p> The least livable cities were Karachi , Tripoli , Dhaka , Lagos and , at the very bottom , Damascus . <p> Vienna also won top place in another survey , the annual quality of living index by human resources consulting firm Mercer , for the 10th time in a row in 2019.
