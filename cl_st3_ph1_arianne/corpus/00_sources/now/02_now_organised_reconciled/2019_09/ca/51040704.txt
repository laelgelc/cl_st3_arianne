On Saturday , a potential star Liberal candidate was touring the riding , reaching out to voters . <p> Several residents told Steven Guilbeault they have no plans to vote for him but he 's hoping his credentials could help him convince them otherwise . <p> " I 've been an environmental activist . I worked at Greenpeace , I co-founded Equiterre , " he said . <p> Guilbeault 's background may not be enough to sway left-leaning voters who are leery of the Liberals ' environmental record .
