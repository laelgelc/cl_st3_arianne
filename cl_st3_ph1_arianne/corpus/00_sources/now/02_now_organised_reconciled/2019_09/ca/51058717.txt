<p> The Town of Hanna regular council meeting on Aug 13 detailed various reports from senior administration . <p> Senior administration first presented an update to the Climate Change Task Force which is in the process of disbanding . The Task Force was commissioned in 2016 to identify the impacts of the NDP 's proposed provincial climate change policy . <p> Formed by the Special Areas Board , Town of Hanna , Alberta , and Cactus Corridor Economic Development Corporation , worked to help move the town from coal to alternative energy sources like natural gas . <p> In the update regarding the Task Force provided to council , administration detailed the work currently being done . The renewable energy group of the Task Force is negotiating a transfer of funds or partial funds from the ERA Best program . The $10 million designated to a large solar project will go to a potential community generation project . The profits of this community generation project would go to an economic development fund for the region . <p> The Task Force is also working with CWL Energy Management on Phase II of the Municipal Community Generation Challenge Project .
