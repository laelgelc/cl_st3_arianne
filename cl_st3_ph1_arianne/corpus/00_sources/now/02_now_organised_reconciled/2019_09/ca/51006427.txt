<p> The 70-year-old royal and heir to the British throne teamed up with sustainable fashion pioneers Vin + Omi to create an avant-garde collection of clothing made entirely from nettles ( a common weed found around the world ) , all supplied from his private Highgrove estate in Gloucestershire . <p> " It was actually his idea to use nettles from his estate and turn them into clothes , " co-founder of the brand Omi tells PEOPLE from his London studio . " And what do you do with a suggestion like that ? It was almost like a challenge for us ! " <p> The conversation started last May over tea at an event hosted by the British Fashion Council to promote sustainability , equality , diversity and craftsmanship . It was at a time when the designers were experimenting with using cow parsley to make fabric . The duo , who go by their first names only , said they were excited when Charles offered to donate the weeds . <p> Sustainable designers Vin + Omi Vin + Omi <p> " He casually said that he had loads of nettles if we wanted them .
