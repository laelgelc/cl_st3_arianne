<h> Letter of the Day : Mom concerned about the future <p> I am a mother of two boys in West Kelowna and always vote in every municipal , provincial and federal election . My greatest concern is the state of our climate and environment . <p> I fear that people are not educated on the facts and are n't well informed . For every fact listed on social media or elsewhere , there 's another article claiming the opposite , and proving the opposing views as incorrect . <p> People feel there is no use making any efforts towards fixing the problem as they are just one household . It 's the big corporations that cause 70% of the pollution , so " what 's the point ? " <p> In this election , regardless your views on the validity on climate change claims , please consider that we all need to make a difference and start using clean energy .
