Instead , we already have a much better solution : an economy-wide carbon tax and rebate . And while putting a price on pollution may not be as exciting or attention-grabbing as some of the lifestyle changes Greta has made , it has already proven to be effective at reducing emissions . <p> That evidence has been provided by a wide array of economists , including all living former chairs of the U.S. Federal Reserve and the most recent Nobel Prize winner . But it 's not just the policy wonks that like carbon taxes . They have also been embraced by business groups , oil companies like Suncor and Cenovus , and even spiritual leaders like the Pope .
