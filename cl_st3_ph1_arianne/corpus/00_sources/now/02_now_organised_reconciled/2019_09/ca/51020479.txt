<p> NAPANEE -- For Mike Bossio , the MP for Hastings-Lennox and Addington and Liberal candidate for the upcoming federal election , it 's about continuing his government 's progress to help middle class families . That 's the message he presented as he officially opened his Napanee campaign office on Wednesday . <p> Surrounded by a crowd of more than 50 supporters at his Bridge Street office , Bossio first thanked them for their support , and the support of his wife Irene . <p> Bossio highlighted in particular how policies such as cutting taxes on the middle class and raising them on the wealthiest one per cent are helping families in Hastings-Lennox and Addington get ahead . He added the Canada Child Benefit is helping low and middle-income families keep up with the cost of raising children , and increases to the Guaranteed Income Supplement for low-income single seniors are helping some of our communities ' most vulnerable . <p> The Conservative party has voted against all three of these measures . <p> The Liberal government has put forward an historic infrastructure plan to make Canadian communities sustainable long into the future .
