<p> California 's Sonoma County has always been a progressive wine growing region . Often portrayed in the shadow of Napa Valley , it seems as if that rivalry has stoked the coastal County to push harder than any other wine region in the world to tell its story , and the results are coming home to roost . <p> Last week Sonoma County Winegrowers announced that five years after they launched a program that would see the entire county 's vineyards hold a certificate of sustainability they have already reached that goal . <p> Karissa Kruse , president of Sonoma County Winegrowers , reports that " 99 per cent of the members are certified sustainable . " In essence everyone is certified , given that some three per cent of growers are already certified organic or biodynamic and chose not to participate in the program . <p> It 's an impressive achievement that places Sonoma County among the most sustainable vineyard areas in the world of wine . <p> Many wine regions , including the Okanagan , are pursuing sustainability goals but in most cases the program is voluntary .
