<p> Today , the Minister of Environment and Climate Change , Catherine McKenna , announced up to $80,000 in funding through the Climate Action Fund to Nature Canada . This funding will help organize a national conference for Canada 's leading conservation organizations . Participants will discuss the threat of climate change to our natural ecosystems , mitigation and adaptation to climate change , and best practices to make our ecosystems more resilient . Our climate crisis is inextricably linked with nature . By protecting and preserving Canada 's natural spaces , we can help mitigate the effects of climate change and ensure we protect our wildlife , today and for future generations . <p> The Climate Action Fund provides up to $3 million to support projects delivered by students , youth , Indigenous Peoples and organizations , not-for-profit organizations , small and medium-sized businesses , and research and educational institutions . <p> Funded projects will raise awareness of climate change and clean growth and encourage others to take action in support of Canada 's climate goals . <p> Quotes <p> " I am inspired by the initiatives being brought forward by Canadians from coast to coast to coast @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take positive action to tackle climate change .
