Trudeau chatted with coffee shop customers . Minutes later , he walked over to Lake 's office and gave a quick speech . <p> " Terry , thank you for your leadership , your devotion to community , your significant engagement over many , many years in public service . Thank you for stepping up and choosing forward along with the rest of us , " Trudeau said .
