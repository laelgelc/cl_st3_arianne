<h> Rainy days ahead brighten the outlook for gloomy farmers <h> It 's not been a good year for South Africa 's agricultural sector . Production has been down thanks to the dry spell , but the weatherman sees plenty of rain on the horizon . <p> Statistics South Africa released second-quarter figures in September which showed seasonally adjusted gross value added in the agricultural sector fell by 4.2% quarter on quarter on an annualised basis , after a 16.8% decline in the first quarter . Could this be the new normal ? <p> So , it 's unsurprising that trade data continue to paint a similar picture of a decline in performance from levels we saw in 2018 . South Africa 's agricultural trade surplus narrowed by 30% in the second quarter of this year compared to the corresponding period in 2018 , recorded at $789-million , according to data from Trade Map . <p> The driver was not an increase in imports , but rather a decline in export volumes of wool , edible fruits , wine and grains .
