<h> News feed <h> Russia adopts Paris climate agreement : decree <p> Russia 's prime minister on Monday gave formal support to the Paris climate agreement and ordered Russian laws to be adapted to its obligations , according to a decree posted on the government 's website . <p> The document signed by Prime Minister Dmitry Medvedev says that Russia is formally adopting the 2015 Paris agreement and will now " allocate financial resources ... to developing countries for prevention and adaptation to climate change . " <p> While not formally named a " ratification , " the government said in a statement that the decree signifies Russia 's adoption of the agreement and " Russia 's consent to the obligations under the Paris Agreement " . <p> A government source told AFP that the document is the " last step in the procedure of Russia 's adoption of the Paris agreement " . <p> Presenting his decree at a government meeting , Medvedev said that it is important for Russia to participate in the process of reducing emissions . <p> " The threat of climate change is ( the ) destruction of the ecological balance , increased risks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , threat to safety of people living on permafrost and increase of natural disasters . " <p> The news comes just hours ahead of a new major UN climate summit , aimed to reinvigorate the faltering Paris accord as mankind is releasing more greenhouse gases than ever into the atmosphere . <p> One of the world 's key energy producers , Russia has remained one of the largest economies and polluters that signed but failed to ratify the accord . <p> Greenpeace Russia in a statement welcomed the decision . <p> " Russia 's actions have great significance : our country has a huge potential to cut down on greenhouse gas emissions , " the environmental organisation said in a statement .
