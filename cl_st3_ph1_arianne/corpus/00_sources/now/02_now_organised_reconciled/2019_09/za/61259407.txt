<h> Some countries use poverty to control other nations -- Mutharika <p> Malawi President , Peter Mutharika , has said some United Nations member states of use poverty to control people of other countries . <p> The Malawi leader said this in his address at the United Nations General Assembly ( UNGA ) in the United States on Thursday . <p> Mutharika speaking at the UN <p> Mutharika told leaders that the world has enough resources to end poverty , send every child to school , slow down climate change and save the planet . <p> He , however , noted that that only one percent of the world controls ninety-nine percent of all resources and it is that group that makes real decisions . <p> According to Mutharika , the global inequality in decision-making undermines the United Nations ' ability to make the world a better place . <p> " Poverty eradication remains an elusive objective in Sustainable Development Goals . We have built empires and cities of the West with our blood , sweat and minerals . Africa has given more resources to the developed world , much more than we ever receive in aid or any rhetoric of philanthropy , " Mutharika said . <p> The president warned the world leaders that the rising population of youth trapped in poverty is a danger to the world because poverty is a breeding ground for radicalization in Africa . <p> " This is one reason we must collectively act on poverty , and act swiftly . We can not ignore and marginalize an entire Continent of 1.3 billion people . <p> " It is a mockery that we meet to galvanize multilateral efforts while we marginalise and repress other Member States , " Mutharika said . <p> He added that Africa will never relent on this position saying a few nations should not be allowed to monopolise power in the Security Council and make the United Nations look like an undemocratic organization .
