Greenpeace reported in 2018 that South Africa 's coal belt in Eastern Mpumalanga had the most polluting cluster of coal-powered stations in the world , producing record levels of nitrogen dioxide and found that 2,200 premature deaths resulted from the NO2 pollution from Mpumalanga . Added to this is the fact that burning coal is regarded as the biggest single source of carbon dioxide emissions that leads to climate change . And the world is turning away from polluting coal faster than predicted .
