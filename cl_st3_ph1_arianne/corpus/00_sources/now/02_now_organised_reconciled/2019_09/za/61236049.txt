<h> Football Manager 2020 Packaging Is Recycled , Recyclable , Even Vegan <h> " Imagine what happens if every other game ... switches to this packaging . " <p> Football Manager 2020 will be released physically using entirely eco-friendly packaging , with all of it recyclable in some way - a first for the industry according to developer Sports Interactive . <p> Announced by studio director Miles Jacobson in a YouTube video about climate change , the packaging consists of recyclable shrinkwrap , recycled cardboard packaging and a recycled paper manual . It 's all printed with vegetable-based ink - " so if you do want to eat the packaging , " explains Jacobson , " it 's vegan . " <p> Football Manager 2020 is part of the Google Stadia launch window line-up - check out the full list in the video below : <p> The discs themselves ca n't be dropped in normal recycling bins , but Sports Interactive will provide addresses for where they can be sent for recycling .
