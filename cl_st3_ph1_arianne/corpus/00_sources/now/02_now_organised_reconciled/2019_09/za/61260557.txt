<h> Commonwealth makes gender equality progress <p> A new Commonwealth paper finds " impressive improvements " in women 's access to maternal health , enrolment in primary school and participation in the workforce . <p> The technical paper was released at the 12th Women 's Affairs Ministers Meeting in Nairobi , Kenya . <p> Findings suggest that in the Commonwealth , a girl is as likely to attend primary school as a boy is , and in some countries more so . In the parliaments of 13 Commonwealth countries , 30% or more of members are women . <p> Against this progress , the paper highlights that persistent gender stereotypes and prejudices in policymaking , budgeting and service delivery are disrupting progress . <p> For instance , only one in five Commonwealth parliamentarians is a woman . Of every 10 girls , only seven attend secondary school .
