<p> BERLIN , Sept 13 ( Reuters ) - Seeking to tackle climate change , Germany 's conservatives want to increase taxes on domestic flights and reduce the cost of long-distance train tickets , a party document seen by Reuters on Friday showed . <p> The German government - made up of Chancellor Angela Merkel 's conservatives , their Bavarian sister party CSU and the Social Democrats ( SPD ) - is expected to present a far-reaching package of climate protection measures on Sept. 20 . <p> The tax for domestic flights is now 7.40 euros ( $8.21 ) . <p> " We want to double this ticket tax for all domestic flights and triple it for short-haul domestic flights under 400 kilometers , " said a party document that the board of Merkel 's Christian Democrats ( CDU ) is due to review on Monday . <p> The document , called " Climate-friendly Germany - using innovations as we head into the future " , showed that the party also wants to make long-distance rail travel cheaper by reducing the value-added tax on train tickets . <p> A spokeswoman for Germany 's flagship airline , Lufthansa , said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other transport and such national measures could result in traffic shifting to regions where levies are smaller or non-existent . <p> " Such a high burden for domestic flights disproportionately affects German airlines and deprives them of money for modern airplanes and climate-friendly technologies , " the spokeswoman said of the CDU proposal . <p> She added that domestic flights only accounted for 0.3% of Germany 's overall carbon dioxide emissions and it was questionable whether such measures would have any tangible ecological impact . <p> The document also said a premium worth several thousand euros should be introduced to encourage people to scrap old , inefficient heating systems so that climate targets for buildings can be achieved .
