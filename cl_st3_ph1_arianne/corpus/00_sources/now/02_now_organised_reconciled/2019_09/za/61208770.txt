<h> Breakfast with Bongani Bingwa <p> The three-year drought that culminated in the " Day Zero " slogan that characterised Cape Town internationally as the first world city to come within a hair 's breadth of running out of water , is not something of the past . <p> As the effects of climate change seem to accelerate around the globe , what have we learned from a catastrophe narrowly avoided and what lessons does the experience hold for other cities ? <p> This is the focus of Day Zero -- One City 's Response to a Record-breaking Drought , by science writer Leonie Joubert and Gina Ziervogel , associate professor at the University of Cape Town 's ( UCT 'S ) African Centre for Cities . <p> Ahead of the book 's launch on Tuesday evening , Pippa Hudson invited the authors to discuss their findings with a live studio audience . <p> Joubert highlights the fact that responsibility for water can not be assigned to one particular person or body . <p> It turns out that you 've got national government responsible for some aspects of the system , some is provincial , some is the City @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ well - not just as consumers of water but as active citizens who are holding government accountable . <p> She says although the City was criticised for adopting what some viewed as the alarmist term ' Day Zero ' , it served to galvanise Capetonians virtually overnight by making them aware of how severe the crisis was . <p> They knew what to do , and there were very clear communication processes , teaming up with business , using the media etc , to tell people how to cut water use . <p> And what about the blame game that entered the conversation at the time ? <p> While acknowledging that it is difficult to predict long-term climate change scenarios , Joubert believes measures can be put in place nonetheless even if these possible situations are not certain to happen within only a few years . <p> The authors say Cape Town 's drought served as a wake-up call to understand how a shortage of water is not simply an environmental issue , but a complex one that affects all aspects of society . <p> As cities around the world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change ... we need to think about how we put in place measures to deal with that because it can have significant impacts .
