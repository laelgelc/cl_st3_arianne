<h> The top risks that could derail business in SA <p> Sep 4 , 2019 <p> Kathy Gibson is at the World Economic Forum on Africa in Cape Town -- Unemployment and under-employment top the list of risks that could derail South Africa as it seeks to embrace the Fourth Industrial Revolution . <p> Other stumbling blocks to realising our potential could include a water crisis , a failure of national governance , profound social instability and a failure of critical infrastructure . <p> Overall for the sub-Sahara African region , executives rate unemployment and underemployment as top risks for doing business , followed by challenges related to governance , energy prices , infrastructure and debt . <p> These are among the findings of the Sub-Saharan Africa Risks Landscape report , published ahead of the World Economic Forum on Africa , starting today in Cape Town . <p> It comes at a time of modest economic growth across the region .
