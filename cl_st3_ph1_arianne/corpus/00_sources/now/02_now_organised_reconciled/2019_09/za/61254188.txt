<h> Mutharika complains over declining assistance to poor countries <p> Malawian President Peter Mutharika has expressed concern over decreasing development assistance to Least Developed Countries ( LDCs ) . <p> Mutharika expressed the concern when addressing a United Nations ( UN ) conference on Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) in the United States on Monday . The Malawi leader was speaking on behalf of LDCs . <p> Mutharika speaking at the conference <p> He noted that in 2018 , bilateral official development assistance to LDCs fell by 3 percent . <p> " As I speak , less official development assistance is going to LDCs and African countries , where it is most needed , " he said . <p> In his speech , Mutharika also talked about the plight of developing countries . <p> He said the countries are behind in development and are at risk of being left further behind even though they are touted as countries with the greatest potential to develop . <p> The Malawi leader backed up his statement with statistics . As a result , we are forced to be importers and consumers while unemployment of the youth rises , " Mutharika said . <p> He also noted that poor countries suffer most devastations of climate change despite contributing the least to pollution and climate change . <p> " Our societies are characterized by widespread inequality . We are societies with the most vulnerable people on Earth . This means we have more vulnerable people who suffer the worst of tragedies of climate change , " he said . <p> The Malawi leader noted that poor countries ' efforts to mobilize resources domestically are constrained by serious challenges .
