<p> The UK Government has made known its plans to invest in two new solar plants in Kenya as part of the Department for International Development 's Transforming Energy Access Programme . <p> The investment move is in a bid to provide more people with access to affordable and clean energy . <p> Kenya 's current energy mix mainly consists of biomass and wood fuel ( 69 percent ) , petroleum ( 22 percent ) and electricity ( 9 percent ) . Biomass ( charcoal and wood fuel ) is the most consumed energy in Kenya . It is mainly used for cooking , heating and lighting . This is despite the fact that the consumption of biomass poses grave health risks to households due to exposure to smoke pollution . <p> Also uncontrolled biomass consumption leads to mass deforestation that accelerates climate change which then interferes with food production and puts the current and future livelihoods in jeopardy . <p> Electricity access in Kenya has remained low despite the government 's tremendous effort in scaling up electricity connectivity from the current 15 percent to at least 65 percent by 2022 .
