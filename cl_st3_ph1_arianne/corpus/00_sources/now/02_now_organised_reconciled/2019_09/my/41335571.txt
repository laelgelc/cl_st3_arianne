<h> Malaysia to send diplomatic note to Indonesia over worsening haze <p> KUCHING ( Sept 7 ) : Malaysia will send a diplomatic note to Indonesia so that immediate action can be taken to put out forest fires that have been causing haze in the country . <p> Deputy Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Isnaraissah Munirah Majilis said the diplomatic note will be drafted and sent out as soon as possible . <p> Speaking to the media here yesterday , she said as of 8am today , 61 monitoring stations showed moderate Air Pollutant Index ( API ) levels . <p> " Six more stations , all in Sarawak , showed unhealthy air quality levels and they are Sri Aman which recorded an API of 166 , Miri ( 133 ) , Kuching ( 125 ) , Sibu ( 123 ) , Samarahan ( 120 ) and Sarikei 109 , " she said . <p> Isnaraissah said based on reports from the Asean Specialised Meteorological Centre , a total of 1,393 hotspots had been detected in Indonesia , with 306 in Sumatera and 1,087 in
