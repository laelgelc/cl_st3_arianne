<h> Malaysian fire dept prepared to help put out forest fires in Indonesia : Zuraida <p> PUTRAJAYA ( Sept 13 ) : The Malaysian Fire and Rescue Department is prepared to help Indonesia put out forest fires in the republic to address the haze problem , said Housing and Local Government Minister Zuraida Kamaruddin . <p> However , the matter has yet to be discussed by the Ministry , she told a media conference after the pre-launch of the 7th Asia-Pacific Urban Forum ( APUF7 ) here yesterday . <p> She said this in response to a statement by Malaysian Ambassador to Indonesia Zainal Abidin Bakar that Malaysia is not pointing fingers at Indonesia over the current haze problems , but the country is offering assistance to tackle the forest fires in several provinces in the republic . <p> According to Zainal Abidin , an official letter from the Malaysian Government , made through Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin had been sent to the Indonesian Government recently . <p> Based on media reports , more than 42,000ha of forest area and plantations have been destroyed by fire since last January @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provinces in
