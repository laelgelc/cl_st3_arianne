<p> KUALA LUMPUR ( Sept 21 ) : An environmental law expert has proposed for the government to study the United States ' extraterritorial legislation known as Foreign Corrupt Practices Act ( FCPA ) 1977 , in drafting the proposed Transboundary Haze Act . <p> Dr Hanim Kamaruddin of Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia said the FCPA was the first to introduce corporate liability , responsibility for third parties and extraterritoriality for corruption offences , meaning that any US-based companies and persons can be held criminally and civilly responsible for corruption offences committed abroad . <p> " Hence , the proposed Transboundary Haze Act should also have provisions with a similar concept in order to tackle transboundary haze pollution crime . <p> " This is to enable the Attorney-General 's Chambers to take legal action against any Malaysian companies if they or their subsidiaries abroad were found to be involved in causing transboundary haze pollution , " she told Bernama . <p> She said the extraterritorial legislation would be easier as the subsidiaries have a direct connection with their holding companies in Malaysia . <p> On Sept 19 , Minister of Energy ,
