<h> Largest Alpine Glacier Could Disappear by 2100 <p> The largest Alpine glacier Aletsch could completely disappear by the end of 2100 . If there is nothing done to control climate change by taking effective measure , there is a strong likelihood that the biggest Alpine glacier will disappear by the end of this century . <p> A cutting-edge simulation was done by a team of researchers in Switzerland to display how the Aletsch Glacier will alter as the planet is warming . This was revealed in a statement by the ETH technical university in Zurich . <p> Already the glacier that covers 86 square kilometers and is estimated to hold almost 11 billion tonnes of ice has seen its tongue recede by around one kilometer . <p> The scientists have predicted that this trend will continue even if the 2015 Paris Agreement target is met by the world . So even global warming is capped at " well below " 2 degrees Celsius the Alpine Glacier might disappear . <p> Even if we consider the best-case scenario , by 2100 the glacier will lose 50% of its volume .
