Assistant Commissioner , one member each from MoCC , Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency and Metropolitan Corporation Islamabad who are presently working on mass awareness of shopkeepers and general public to shun the use of plastic bags , he added . <p> One thing should be kept in mind that no plastic is biodegradable where we have found some plastic bags producers and international
