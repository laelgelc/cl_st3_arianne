<h> PM Khan arrives in US on a week-long trip <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan reached the United States on Saturday to attend the 74th session of the United Nations General Assembly ( UNGA ) . <p> According to a post on the Government of Pakistan Twitter account , the PM arrived in New York for a week-long visit.During his week-long visit to US , the prime minister will hold several bilateral meetings with his counterparts from various countries and participate in high-level UN summits on climate change , sustainable development , universal health coverage and financing for development . <p> PM Imran 's US visit will be Kashmir-centric , revolving around India 's decision to revoke the special status of occupied Kashmir and the ongoing human rights abuses in the disputed territory . <p> PM Imran is slated to address the UN General Assembly session on September 27 . Prior to the UN General Assembly session , the premier will meet the heads of various states and apprise them about the Indian government 's violations of human rights in the valley . <p> The prime minister will also attend and speak at high-level side events co-hosted by Pakistan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and poverty alleviation , co-hosted by Malaysia and Pakistan.A trilateral summit meeting of Pakistan , Malaysia and Turkey will also be held on the sidelines . The prime minister will also interact with a cross-section of international media outlets including meetings with editorial boards . <p> His speaking engagements at leading think-tanks and meetings with heads of major international human rights organisations are also envisaged . <p> The prime minister 's visit will further reinforce Pakistan 's abiding commitment to the UN Charter , international law and cooperative multilateralism , with the UN at its core . <p> On Thursday , PM Imran arrived in Saudi Arabia for a two-day visit where he met King Salman bin Abdul Aziz and Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman . The two sides underlined the deep and multi-faceted quality of the relationship . They reiterated the resolve to further intensify bilateral trade , energy investment and people-to-people contacts .
