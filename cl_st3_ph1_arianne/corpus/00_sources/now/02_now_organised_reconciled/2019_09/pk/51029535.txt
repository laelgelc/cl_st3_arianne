<h> Russian Firm Shows Interest to Invest in Pakistan <p> A Russian tech company has expressed its willingness to invest in Pakistan . <p> As per the reports of local media , the Russian tech firm is interested to make the investment in the electric vehicle industry of Pakistan with the most recent technology called Nano Tubes . <p> As per an issued statement by the Press Information Department -- PID , a delegation of the Russian private firm , headed by Mr Igor Kim , called on to the Federal Minister on Climate Change -- Malik Amin Aslam . <p> During the conversation , Mr Kim said that the Nano Tube technology was being used in all kinds of materials with scores of purposes from reinforcement to increase conductivity . <p> They added that their product was microscopic and was extensively being used in the electric batteries , in the making of LED screens , construction materials , plastics , solar panels , conductors , tires and clothes . <p> Malik Amin Aslam said that they want to make Pakistan centre for the right-hand-drive electric vehicles all across the world .
