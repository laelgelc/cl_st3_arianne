information . <p> Speaking at an appreciation dinner with editors and other top operators of the media industry at the State House Conference Centre in Abuja , Secretary to the Government of the Federation , Mr Boss Mustapha , said government would not muzzle the press as it performs the important work of providing the public with reliable and quality information . <p> He said no real democracy could exist without freedom of the press , and called on media organisations to partner with relevant stakeholders in combating the menace of fake news and disinformation in the society .
