<h> Taraba 's health sector under Gov Ishaku <p> Governor Darius Ishaku marked 100 days in office recently . ANDREW OJIH writes on his scorecard in the health sector . <p> For the people of Taraba state , Governor Darius Ishaku has raised the bar of expectations of better things to come in the health sector . <p> The executive secretary , Taraba State Primary Healthcare Development Agency , Alhaji Aminu Jauro , said the governor 's achievements in the health sector had surpassed many across the country . Gov Ishaku since 2015 had said his administration would effect major reforms that would transform the health sector to meet a world-class standard . <p> Jauro noted that since Darius resumed office , his administration has employed a good numbers of community health workers to meet to health needs of those living in hard to reach places . <p> He added that the governor gave the agency go ahead to sign an MOU with the Federal Ministry of Health , Ministry of Finance and the World Bank to come out with a concept that would elevate the health sector in the state . <p> He also gave the agency an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ come up with what is called the hard to reach project to deliver free health delivery to the most difficult areas in the state . <p> The state government in conjunction with the World Bank , he noted , succeeded in making over 70 primary health care centres functional in six local government areas of the state . <p> Each of the wards have one functional health care centre to carter for the health need of the people . <p> Taraba which has 168 political wards today have health centres from two local governments from the three zones in the state . <p> The agency has made concerted effort to provide another health care centres to two local government areas of state .
