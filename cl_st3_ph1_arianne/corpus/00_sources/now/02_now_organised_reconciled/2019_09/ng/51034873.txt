<h> Climate change , biggest threat to human rights , says UN <p> The global climate emergency violates people 's rights in a massive way , UN human rights chief Michelle Bachelet said on Monday , urging governments to tackle climate-related conflict and poverty , and to hold businesses responsible for their actions . <p> In her opening speech at a session of the UN Human Rights Council , High Commissioner Bachelet pointed out that climate change is already increasing hunger , reversing economic development and driving violence and migration . <p> " The world has never seen a threat to human rights of this scope , " Bachelet said . <p> " All people , everywhere , should be able to live in a healthy environment and hold accountable those who stand in the way of achieving it , " she added . <p> Bachelet cited the example of Africa 's Sahel region , where arable land has been decreasing as the desert expands . <p> This development had not only fuelled clashes between farmers and herders , but also increased ethnic tensions and extremist violence . <p> " This is a key area where the international community @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a growing crisis , " Bachelet said . <p> The UN rights chief also warned that destruction of environments including the Amazon affects the rights and livelihoods of indigenous peoples . <p> In addition , Bachelet urged governments to step up protection for environmental activists , who have been attacked in nearly every world region , especially in Latin America . <p> " I am disheartened by this violence , and also by the verbal attacks on young activists such as Greta Thunberg and others , who galvanize support for prevention of the harm their generation may bear , " she said .
