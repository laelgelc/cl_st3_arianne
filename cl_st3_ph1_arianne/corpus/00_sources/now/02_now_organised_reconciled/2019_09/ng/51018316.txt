It is also expected to intra-African trade through better harmonisation and coordination of trade liberalisation and facilitation and instruments across the regional economic Communities ( RECs ) and across Africa in general . The AfCFTA is also expected to enhance competitiveness at the industry and enterprise level through exploitation of opportunities for scale production , continental market access and better reallocation of resources .
