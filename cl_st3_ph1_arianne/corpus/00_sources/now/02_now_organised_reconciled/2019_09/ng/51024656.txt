<h> ICT will solve 80% NHIS " transparency " challenges -- ES <p> Robust deployment of Information and Communication Technology ( ICT ) will solve 80 per cent of the " unacceptable transparency " challenges of the National Health Insurance Scheme ( NHIS ) . The Executive Secretary of the scheme , Mohammed Sambo , said this when he visited the Chairman , Senate Committee on Health , Ibrahim Oloriegbe , at the National Assembly complex in Abuja , according to a Tuesday statement by Ayo Osinlu , the NHIS spokesperson . <p> Over the years , the NHIS has failed to develop an effective ICT system , resulting in challenges and bottlenecks such as ghost enrollees . In 2017 , about 23,000 ghost enrollees were removed from the NHIS system after a routine check by an ICT team deployed by the former executive secretary , Usman Yusuf . <p> Effective use of ICT can aide service delivery of health insurance .
