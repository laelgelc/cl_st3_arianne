<h> Nigeria 's Muhammad-Bande declares for UNGA presidency <p> Nigeria 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations , Prof. Tijani Muhammad-Bande , has formally declared his intention to vie for the presidency of the 74th UN General Assembly . <p> Muhammad-Bande made the declaration at a cocktail party attended by world diplomats and delegates in New York on Friday evening . <p> This came barely eight days after the current UNGA President , Maria Espinosa , announced him as the first candidate for the position . <p> The News Agency of Nigeria reports that election of the president of the 74th UNGA will hold at the UN headquarters on June 4 . <p> In a statement on its website , UNGA said the presidency of the 74th session was zoned to Africa in " full respect of the established principle of geographical rotation " among other reasons . <p> The current president , who is the Foreign Minister of Ecuador , was elected on June 5 , 2019 and assumed duty on September 18 , 2018 . <p> Thus she will step aside on September 17 , 2019 as the UNGA presidency runs on a one-year tenure . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Muhammad-Bande is scheduled for May 13 in line with UNGA 's resolution 71/323 , titled : " Revitalisation of the Work of the General Assembly . " <p> At the session , the Nigerian permanent representative will have the opportunity to respond to questions from other stakeholders . <p> Addressing guests at Friday 's event , the candidate pledged to make the organisation stronger and work better for its member states and their citizens . <p> He said as president of the 74th Session , he would focus on the effective implementation of existing mandates , and make a contribution in all defined follow-up areas . <p> The candidate promised to promote international peace and security , prevent conflict , strengthen global action to tackle climate change , ensure inclusion , human rights , and the empowerment of youth and women . <p> He also pledged to ensure that the decisions reached and resolutions passed at the general assembly were implemented for the benefits of citizens . <p> Muhammad-Bande , who hails from Zagga in present day Kebbi State , has had an outstanding career as a scholar and diplomat @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ University , USA ( 1981 ) and a PhD in Political Science from the University of Toronto , Canada ( 1987 ) . <p> Between January 2000 and February 2004 , he was the Director-General of the Centre African de Formation et de Recherche Administrative pour le Development in Tangier , Morocco . <p> CAFRAD is Africa 's premier institution with responsibility for training and research in public administration and management . <p> Besides other positions both locally and internationally , he was the Director-General of the National Institute for Policy and Strategic Studies between 2010 and 2016 . <p> He served as the Vice-President of the General Assembly during the 71st session and remains active in several fora , including as Chair of the United Nations Special Committee on Peacekeeping Operations ( C34 ) ; Member , Advisory Board of the United Nations Counter-Terrorism Centre and Chair of the ECOWAS Group ( 2018-2019 ) . <p> Muhammad-Bande has also been an assessor for the National Merit Award ( Nigeria ) and for professorial positions in universities . <p> He has won merit awards and honours from institutions and Governments @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ notably , he is a recipient of Nigeria 's Officer of the Order of the Federal Republic of Nigeria , one of the country 's highest national awards .
