<h> Minister backs Africa women conference <p> Minister of State for Federal Capital Territory ( FCT ) Dr Ramatu Tijjani has pledged to support the Africa Women Conference ( AWC ) and other groups working to enhance the economic well-being of women and the less privileged . <p> Tijjani gave the assurance in Abuja when the co-convener of AWC and founder , Helpline Foundation for the Needy , Abuja , Dr Jumai Ahmadu led a delegation of other women on a courtesy visit to her office . <p> The minister assured the AWC of her support through various productive dialogues for the sustainable growth of the African woman . <p> She called on women in positions of authority to support the cause of the AWC , adding that the initiative is designed to benefit every African woman including those of colour outside the continent . <p> Dr Tijjani , who is also an inductee of the Africa Women Conference Hall of Fame , commended the role played by Helpline Foundation in promoting entrepreneurial skills of rural women in Nigeria . <p> She stated the foundation has consistently embarked on programmes for women and children with the singular aim of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> She promised to identify with the vision and mission of the foundation and any other group that will further enhance the economic growth of women and the less privileged in the society . <p> " I am particularly honoured to be associated with you and your foundation and I hope that together , we will fight this good fight for women to the finish , " she affirmed . <p> She stated that an award model will be initiated by the FCT administration to recognise hard working members of staff and also encourage others on the path of diligence and commitment to duty . <p> Earlier , the leader of delegation , Dr. Jumai Ahmadu pledged the commitment of the Helpline Foundation to carrying out programmes that have direct impact on the wellbeing of women in the FCT . <p> The AWC was inaugurated in March 2018 with
