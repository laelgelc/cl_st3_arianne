<h> Why we shutdown Enugu cattle market -- MACBAN <p> As part of efforts to purge itself of violent elements , the Miyetti Allah Cattle Breeders Association of Nigeria ( MACBAN ) , South East zone says it will extend the shutdown of cattle markets and settlement in other states in the zone , Agronature Nigeria reports . <p> Southeast Zonal Vice President , MACBAN Alh.Gidado Siddiki , who said this in Akwa , Anqmbra state said the successful shutdown of Enugu shutdown cattle market exposed people that do not have business with herding or other legitimate activities . According to him , they have been asked to leave their fold . <p> " We shutdown livestock and perishable good markets to enable us sensitise our members on the need for peaceful coexistence , that they should be watchful to know when bad people infiltrate their midst and to chase them .
