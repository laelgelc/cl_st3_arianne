<h> Tribunal dismisses petition against Kaduna APC lawmaker <p> The National and State Assembly Election Petitions Tribunal in Kaduna , has dismissed the petition filed against the election of Alhaji Bala Salisu-Dandada . <p> Salisu-Dandada of the All Progressives Congress ( APC ) won the March 9 election to represent Igabi East constituency in the state House of Assembly . <p> He was , however , dragged before the tribunal by Alhaji Bashir Idris Aliyu of the Peoples Democratic Party ( PDP ) , over alleged certificate forgery . <p> In the petition , Aliyu alleged that Salisu-Dandada had forged his primary and secondary schools certificates and used same to contest for the election . <p> The petitioner averred that the Independent National Electoral Commission ( INEC ) and 1st respondent in the petition erred in declaring the 2nd respondent as winner of the poll . <p> Aliyu had prayed the tribunal to declare him winner , saying he scored the highest valid votes . <p> However , the tribunal chairman , Justice Adamu Suleiman , said there was no substantial evidence provided by the petitioner to support the allegations . <p> Suleiman said the evidence before the tribunal @ @ @ @ @ interchangeably used in the two certificates , he obtained from the said schools , " Suleiman said . <p> According to him , there was no one who came forward to either contest or lay claims of ownership of the 2nd respondent 's certificates . <p> " There was also no one who came forward to lay claims of the certificates or credentials used by the respondent . <p> " The petition is , therefore , dismantled as the petitioners failed woefully to provide any evidence to defend the petition , " Suleiman ruled . <p> The Tuesday 's ruling brings to seven , the number of petitions , the
