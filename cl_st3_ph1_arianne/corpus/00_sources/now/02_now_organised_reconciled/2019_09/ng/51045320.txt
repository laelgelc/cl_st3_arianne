<h> GMOs important for food security -- FG <p> The Minister of Science and Technology , Dr. Ogbonnaya Onu , Tuesday stressed the importance of genetic engineering and genetically modified organisms ( GMOs ) in achieving food security in the country . <p> Onu said this at a five-day workshop on Basic Laboratory Training on Living Modified Organisms ( LMOs ) Detection and Identification , hosted by the National Biotechnology Development Agency ( NABDA ) in Abuja . <p> According to him , genetic engineering and GMOs have important roles to play in the nation 's search to ensure food security . <p> " It is important that we can feed our citizens and start the process of producing locally , many things that we import with GMOs , " he said . <p> Onu commended NABDA for the work it had done in GMOs , adding that the ministry would continue to support the agency on biotechnology activities . <p> He said the ministry would also support the agency in its mandate to protect the environment in ensuring that the desire of the country to industrialise is achieved . <p> " I believe through genetic engineering and biotechnology @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ achieved .
