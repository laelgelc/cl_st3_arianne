<h> Scientist advocates ' eating human flesh ' to fight climate change <p> Magnus Soderlund , Swedish scientist , has proposed cannibalism as a possible way to combat the negative impacts of climate change . <p> According to The EPOCH TIMES , Soderlund made the proposal while speaking at a summit for food of the future called Gastro Summit , in Stockholm , capital of Sweden , on September 4 . <p> The researcher , from the Stockholm School of Economics , said the world must " awaken the idea " of eating human flesh in the future , as a way of combating the effects of climate change . <p> In his speech titled " Can You Imagine Eating Human Flesh ? " , Soderlund said people across the world need to disregard taboos on human corpses and eating of human flesh . <p> He described the taboos as " conservative " . The scientist said people will overcome the resistance for such acts gradually , starting from attempts to just taste human flesh . <p> " Are we humans too selfish to live sustainably ? , " his seminar 's talking points ask . <p> " Is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Does Generation Z have the answers to our food challenges ?
