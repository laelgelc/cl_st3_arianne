<h> Largest Solar Plant In Africa Opens In Bayero University Kano <p> Solar power projects being commissioned under the Energizing Education Programme of the Buhari administration is a testament to the fulfilment of its Next Level agenda , according to Vice President Yemi Osinbajo , SAN . <p> Prof. Osinbajo stated this on Tuesday in Kano State , where he commissioned the 7.1MW solar hybrid power plant in Bayero University Kano , which is the largest off grid solar hybrid power plant in Africa . <p> The Vice President further said the solar hybrid power plant was ' just one touch point of progress and impact within this Administration 's Next Level agenda . The Federal Government is committed to powering Nigeria ; one university , one market , one community at a time . " <p> This is the second solar plant to be commissioned by the Vice President within a period of about one month , following the first one in August -- a 2.8 MW decentralized solar hybrid power plant at the Alex Ekwueme Federal University Ndufu Alike Ikwo ( FUNAI ) . <p> BUK is the second of the 37 federal universities and 7 teaching @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to provide sustainable and clean power supply around the country .
