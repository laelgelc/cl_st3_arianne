<h> Makinde , Ajimobi 's aides at war over Oyo forest <p> Oyo State Governor Seyi Makinde 's aide and his immediate predecessor Abiola Ajimobi 's ex-aide on Monday engaged in a social media war over the alleged conversion of 26 , 000 hectares of forest to personal use by some officials of the Ajimobi government . <p> A former Speaker of the House of Assembly and current Commissioner for Environment and Natural Resources , Mr Kehinde Ayoola , took to his verified Facebook account and wrote how ex-public officers converted the forest to personal use . <p> Ayoola wrote , " On August 16 , my second day in office , I invited directors in the ministry to brief me . You know , just to get a hang on things . It was while I took on the forestry department that I got a shocker about how the last government pillaged our forest resources . <p> " They just awarded prime forest land to themselves . <p> " For the records , Oyo State has nine forest reserves . I was told they took 6,000 hectares for themselves at Gambari Forest Reserve . <p> " At Opara @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ makes these acquisition reprehensible , and environmentalists and conservationists will agree , is that forests are very vital in the fight against global warming and its attendant problem of climate change . <p> " Of course , with the concurrence of Governor Seyi Makinde , I have revoked the illegal allocations . <p> " We shall collaborate with the Ministry of Justice to take further necessary action . <p> " I hope someone will sue me for exposing this . They will then meet maggots inside their plate of salt . " <p> However , Ajimobi 's former Special Adviser , Media , Mr Bolaji Tunji , on his Facebook page said the ex-governor focused on agric revolution in the state . <p> He wrote , " Though Senator Abiola Ajimobi is no longer the governor of Oyo State , it is difficult to forget some of the lofty plans he had for the state which earned him the sobriquet of the father of Modern Oyo State . <p> " One of such was his plan to revolutionise agriculture in the state .
