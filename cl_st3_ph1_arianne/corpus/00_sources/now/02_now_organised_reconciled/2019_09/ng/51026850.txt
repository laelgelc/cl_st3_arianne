<h> Alaibe disowns claims on defection from PDP to AD <p> Chief Timi Alaibe has disowned the claims by some politicians in Bayelsa state that he has defected from the Peoples Democratic Party ( PDP ) to the Alliance for Democracy ( AD ) over the alleged poor conduct of the last governorship primaries of the party . <p> According to Alaibe , he has not joined any political party as being bandied in the media asserting that he is a loyal member of the PDP despite being bombarded by requests and offers of opportunity by other political parties . <p> Chief Alaibe , in a statement issued by his campaign organization and signed by the administrative secretary , Professor Seiyefa Brisibe , said no decision has been taken on his next move . " No decision has not been taken and any speculation to the contrary remains what it is -- mere speculation " <p> According to the statement entitled ' HOPE IS ON THE WAY ' , the " Timi Alaibe Campaign Organisation observed that since the declaration of the results of the PDP primary election for the governorship of Bayelsa state , our teeming supporters have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ next step to be taken by our principal , Chief Ndutimi Alaibe , in search of justice in what was generally seen as a deliberate adulteration of due process in the conduct of the primary election . <p> " Be assured that this evil act against the wish of the people of Bayelsa state , carried out by a handful of selfish people who decided to play god by arrogating to themselves the unrighteous act of imposing their personal decisions on us , will not go unchallenged . In the end , Bayelsa people -- who are in the majority -- will have their way . <p> " As it is traditional in matters of this kind , we needed to carry out due consultation across board with our supporters ; particularly the stakeholders -- including our vibrant youth and women -- before taking any step or embarking on any decision in search of redress or alternatives . We are almost at the end of the necessary consultations . <p> " However , let it be stated that Chief Alaibe has not joined any political party as bandied in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ PDP despite being bombarded by requests and offers of opportunity by other political parties .
