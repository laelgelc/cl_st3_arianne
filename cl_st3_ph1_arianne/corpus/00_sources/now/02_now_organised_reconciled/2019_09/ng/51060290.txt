<h> Banks begin charges on deposits , withdrawals today <p> The Central Bank of Nigeria ( CBN ) has announced that it would begin to charge bank customers making cash deposits and withdrawals from September 19 , 2019 . <p> In a circular made available to the public , the apex bank disclosed that the new policy of the transaction fees was designed to reduce cash in use . <p> The charges , according to the Central Bank of Nigeria ( CBN ) , would attract three per cent processing fees for withdrawals and two per cent processing fees for lodgments of amounts above N500,000 for individual accounts . <p> CBN also made known that for corporate accounts , the Deposits Money Banks ( DMBs ) would charge five per cent processing fees for withdrawals and three per cent processing fee for lodgments of amounts above N3,000,000 . <p> For now , these charges are only applicable to bank customers in Lagos , Ogun , Kano , Abia , Anambra , Rivers and the Federal Capital Territory ( FCT ) .
