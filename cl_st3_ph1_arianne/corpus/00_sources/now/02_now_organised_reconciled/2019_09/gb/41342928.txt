<h> UK enjoys ' painted lady summer ' with influx of migratory butterflies <h> UK enjoys ' painted lady summer ' with influx of migratory butterflies <p> This summer was a " painted lady year " , with almost half a million of the migratory butterflies recorded as part of an annual count , experts have said . <p> Results from members of the public taking part in the survey run by Butterfly Conservation have allowed the wildlife charity to confirm that 2019 was a year when unusually high numbers of painted ladies arrived in the UK . <p> A " painted lady year " is a natural phenomenon that occurs about once a decade , the experts said , with the last big influx of the migratory butterfly taking place in 2009 . <p> Results from the Big Butterfly Count , which took place over three weeks in the summer , show that the number of painted lady butterflies was almost 30 times greater than in 2018 . <p> Marbled white numbers were significantly up on last year ( Bob Eade/Butterfly
