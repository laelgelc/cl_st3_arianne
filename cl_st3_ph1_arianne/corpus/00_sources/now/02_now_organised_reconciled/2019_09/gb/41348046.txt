<h> PETA joins Extinction Rebellion to disrupt London Fashion Week <h> PETA and Extinction Rebellion are set to stage protests over the five days of the London Fashion Week . <p> Topless PETA activists protest use of fur and leather at London Fashion WeekReuters <p> Climate change awareness group Extinction Rebellion has planned a five-day protest against the London Fashion Week . The protest is to highlight the impact of the fashion industry on the environment . People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals ( PETA ) has also joined Extinction Rebellion at the 180 Strand , London venue to get their own message across about the impact of the leather industry . <p> London Fashion Week ( September 13-18 ) is a clothing trade show where fashion designers and fashion houses from around the world showcase their talent . However , Extinction Rebellion wants to point out how irresponsible the fashion industry is towards the environment . <p> Extinction Rebellion activist Sara Arnold is the founder of a fashion rental business . She told the Daily Mail that instead of choosing sustainability for the environment , most fashion houses choose sustainability for their business . Extinction Rebellion had asked the British Fashion Council to cancel this year 's event to support the movement . However , the event was not affected by the activist 's request . <p> Before the event began , Extinction Rebellion started staging their protest at the event venue . A " Die-in " protest had activists in white T-shirts throw fake blood on the payment and lie down in it as if they were dead . Another protester read a poem talking about the impact of the fashion industry on the environment . Five other activists with fake blood on their face and clothes created a human chain blocking the venue entrance .
