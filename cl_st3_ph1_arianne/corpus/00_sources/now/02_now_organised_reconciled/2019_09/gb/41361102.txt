<h> Wootton Bassett to rally in global strike <h> Wootton Bassett to rally in global climate change strike <p> PROTESTERS will be rallying in Royal Wootton Bassett as part of a global strike today . <p> People around the world are joining what is expected to be the largest environmental protest in history . <p> And children are being encouraged to join the strike beside environmental groups and people who are passionate about saving the planet . <p> Dave Knight , a member of the Royal Wootton Bassett Environmental Group is a long-term campaigner and will be joining the protest demanding action against climate change . <p> He said : " We are trying to make others understand that it 's a massive threat so the point of the protest is to raise awareness . It 's for the next generation , they will have to deal with it . It 's not a blame thing , we have all been a part of this . " <p> It 's the UK 's first general strike since 1926 and protesters are calling on the government to do more to fight against climate change .
