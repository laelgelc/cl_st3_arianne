<p> In 2005 , Randall Munroe -- then a physics student at university in Virginia -- started scanning the doodles he 'd been scribbling in lectures , drawing square boxes around them and uploading them to a website to share with his friends . <p> Today , xkcd is one of the most popular webcomics on the internet , with millions of readers a month . Munroe 's simple line-art is instantly recognisable , and his growing library of more than 2,000 science-tinged comics pops up all over the internet . Just as The Simpsons has tackled every conceivable plot-line in its three decade run , there seems to be a relevant xkcd for everything .
