<h> Letter to the editor : We should congratulate the young climate strikers <h> Letter to the editor : We should congratulate the young climate strikers <p> WE should be congratulating our children and using the opportunity of their strike against climate change as an opportunity to teach them what protests can achieve . Protests unite people . Protests raise awareness . Protests demonstrate the incredible power of ordinary people . They give a voice to the voiceless and hold government forces accountable for their actions . <p> So the next time you scoff at another protest and tell our children that they should be in school , not marching for their future , take the opportunity to teach them about some of the brave people who came before them and shaped the course of history with their voices and their lives . <p> Teach them that the rights they have today , including the right to march , were fought for by those who joined together in all the movements , marches , protests and rallies that they have to learn about in Key Stage 3 . And there is no better way to learn than to experience @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , something that they are passionate about , and something that is worth protesting about . <p> Remember the women 's suffrage movement ; Gandhi 's salt march ; the abolitionist movement ; the anti-war movement ; the storming of the Bastille ; the Peasants ' Revolt ; the civil rights movement ; the Tolpuddle Martyrs ; the miners ' strike and the poll tax riots and so on . <p> In a democracy , the power lies with the people . When multiple voices come together in opposition to something in the form of peaceful protest , powerful change can happen . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
