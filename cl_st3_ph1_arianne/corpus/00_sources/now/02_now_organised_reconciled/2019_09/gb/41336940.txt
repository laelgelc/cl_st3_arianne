<h> As part of its emphasis on sustainability and environmental conservation ATDD goes plastic-free <p> This move is also in line with UAE Vision 2021 and Ajman Vision 2021 , which underline the importance of a happy society that contributes to the creation of a green economy <p> HE Saleh Mohamed Al Geziry , ATDD Director General <p> Press Release <p> Ajman : Ajman Tourism Development Department ( ATDD ) has reinforced its commitment to the environment by banning the use of plastic utensils in its departments and divisions in response to the Ministry of Climate Change and the Environment 's campaign to encourage responsible consumption for a sustainable future , which was launched on the occasion of Earth Day . This move is also in line with UAE Vision 2021 and Ajman Vision 2021 , which underline the importance of a happy society that contributes to the creation of a green economy . <p> As part of this initiative , ATDD will replace the plastic items that were used in ATDD 's offices with other biodegradable materials in the interests of protecting and conserving the environment .
