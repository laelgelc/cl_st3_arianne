<h> Climate emergency : we have no option but to respond , says Welby <p> THE Archbishop of Canterbury has thanked young people for taking part in climate strikes around the globe on Friday . Responding to the climate emergency was an essential act for Christians , he said . <p> In a Twitter post sent on the eve of the strike , Archbishop Welby shared a photograph of cards sent to him by children concerned about the lack of action on climate change . <p> He said : " It 's inspiring to see young people so passionate about protecting God 's creation and calling our attention to the #ClimateEmergency . Thank you for showing us where our priorities should be . Praying for your voices to be heard here and around the world . <p> " Please be safe in all you do -- and encourage your school to organise participation activities , as many schools , churches and cathedrals are already doing . . . <p> " As a Christian , responding to the #ClimateEmergency is not optional -- it 's essential .
