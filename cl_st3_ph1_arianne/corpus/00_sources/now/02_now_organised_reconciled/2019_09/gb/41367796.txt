<h> Dorset moves a step closer to National Park status <h> Dorset moves a step closer to National Park status <p> A LEADING review areas of outstanding national beauty has recommended further assessment of Dorset for National Park designation . <p> The findings in the Glover Landscapes Review , an independent review launched by government , have been welcomed by the Dorset &amp; East Devon National Park Team ( DEDNPT ) , which is working towards gaining National Park status . <p> A statement from DEDNPT said : " We consider Dorset &amp; East Devon is the outstanding candidate to be England 's next National Park . <p> " This is shown by the wide-ranging evidence provided to Natural England since 2013 and then to the Glover Review by the National Park Team and the proposal 's many partners and supporters . <p> " The team looks forward to Natural England 's further assessment and consideration of the case for National Park designation . <p> " We encourage Natural England to begin this work as soon as possible . " <p> The Glover Review concluded Dorset , the Cotswolds and the Chilterns were all strong candidates to be considered @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Campaign to Protect Rural England ( CPRE ) also produced a report backing National Park status . <p> The proposed park would cover the whole of the current Dorset and East Devon AONBs , as well as Purbeck and Egdon Heath . <p> The Glover Review recognised Dorset " contains some of the greatest concentrations of biodiversity in Britain and opportunities for enjoyment . <p> " It includes the Jurassic World Heritage site . " <p> Meanwhile , the DEDNPT team added : " We welcome the review 's emphasis on the vital contributions which National Parks can make to thriving and sustainable communities and rural economies , and to health and wellbeing for residents and visitors . <p> " And we support the review 's call for designated landscapes to play a vital role in nature recovery networks and in the response to climate change . <p> " We support their call for national funding for this vital work to be sustained and increased . <p> " In the 70th anniversary year of the National Parks and Access to the Countryside Act , we look forward to continuing to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ England and DEFRA , to bring to fruition this long-overdue National Park .
