<h> New labels are appearing at petrol stations : here 's what they mean <h> What the new labels at petrol stations mean <p> The Department for Transport has been rolling out a new set of labels for petrol and diesel pumps at fuel stations across the UK . All fuel stations should now have these in place , with the rules making it mandatory by September 1 . <p> What do they mean ? <p> Petrol is now labelled E5 and diesel is B7 . <p> The letter and number tell you the type of renewable fuel and the maximum percentage of the relevant renewable fuel it contains . <p> The labels will be on fuel dispensers and nozzles at all petrol stations and on new vehicles . <p> The DfT say on new vehicles you can match the label on the pump with a label near the fuel cap . <p> What if my car has a sticker saying it can use E10 petrol ? <p> You can use E5 petrol as well . E10 is not yet available to use in the UK . It could be available in the UK in future to help reduce overall carbon dioxide emissions of petrol vehicles and meet climate change targets . <p> My car sticker says not to use biodiesel - can I still use B7 ? <p> Yes , B7 diesel can be used by all diesel vehicles . The fuel has not changed . <p> The DfT say some cars have a sticker saying ' no biodiesel ' near the fuel filler cap . <p> This is to stop people using very high biodiesel blends , or even 100% biodiesel . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
