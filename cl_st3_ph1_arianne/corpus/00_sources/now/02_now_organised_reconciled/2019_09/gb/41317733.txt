That 's 48 years of campaigning and counting . <h> Their first campaign became iconic <p> Around 2,000 ' non-returnable ' bottles were dumped outside Schweppes London headquarters in 1971 in Friends of the Earth 's first UK campaign <p> Friends of the Earth UK gained national attention with their first campaign in 1971 - a bottle dump outside the London headquarters of soft drinks giant Schweppes . <p> The protest was aimed at the company 's ' non-returnable ' bottle policy and to raise awareness of the extra waste that was destined for landfill . <p> Schweppes did n't back down , but the government took notice and offered the organisation a place on their Waste Management Advisory Council . <p> To this day the image remains one of the most iconic photographs in environmental campaigning . <h> What has the organisation done since ?
