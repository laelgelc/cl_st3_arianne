<p> An architecture student has designed a concept waterborne city that would float in the River Severn estuary on the outskirts of Bristol in order to help the existing city deal with changing sea levels . <p> Envisaged by Alfie Hope , a final-year student at UCL 's Bartlett School of Architecture , the project , which is called " Sea Rise City " , has been designed to replace land lost to sea level rises with a floating bamboo landscape . Hope worked on the premise that " nearly three billion people currently live in low elevation coastal areas around the world who are vulnerable to the effects of sea level rise " . " The project anticipates that the Bristol area will likely experience a sea level rise of 7m by the year 2275 , " writes Hope . " The project , deals with the issue using a method to effectively ' grow ' new land from bamboo as the sea level gradually rises . This method was inspired by the ancient methods of Chinampa farming used by the Aztecs on lake Texcoco to establish floating agricultural landscape made from organic materials .
