<h> ' Gen Xers care more about sustainability than millennials ' <p> People from Generation X -- defined as those between 38 and 50 years of age -- appear more motivated to invest sustainably than millennials . <p> New research found 61 per cent of " Generation Xers " always consider sustainability factors when selecting an investment product , compared with 59 per cent of millennials , who are 18 to 37 years old . <p> Half ( 50 per cent ) of Baby Boomers ( 51 to 70 years old ) said they always considered sustainability factors , while it was 40 per cent for the Silent Generation ( over 71 years old ) . The average for all investors was 57 per cent . <p> The findings were part of Schroders Global Investor Study ( GIS ) 2019 , which measured the views of more than 25,000 investors in 32 locations around the world .
