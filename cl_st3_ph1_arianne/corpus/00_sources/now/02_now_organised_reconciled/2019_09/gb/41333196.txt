Jenner inserted pus taken from a cowpox pustule and inserted it into an incision on the boy 's arm . He was testing his theory , drawn from the folklore of the countryside , that milkmaids who suffered the mild disease of cowpox never contracted smallpox , one of the greatest killers of the period , particularly among children .
