<h> A Yale University study has revealed that the current slowdown of the Atlantic Ocean might be reversed because of the global warming effect on the Indian Ocean . <p> One of the questions of importance to climate change researchers is a slowdown of the main circulation system of the Atlantic Ocean . Last year , it was reported that the ocean 's circulation was the weakest in 1,600 years , which could lead to greater flooding and extreme weather . <p> However , help , it seems , could come from unexpected quarters -- from the Indian Ocean . <p> It seems that global warming has initiated an ocean-to-ocean exchange between the two oceans , which may potentially stabilise the Atlantic Ocean meridional overturning circulation ( AMOC ) . <p> AMOC , one of the largest circulation systems in the world , works by delivering cold water to South Atlantic and warm water to North Atlantic . Its stability has existed for millennia but the current global warming trends , collated over the last 15 years , have started affecting it . <p> Alexey Fedorov , who led the Yale University research on the subject , stated in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " There is no consensus yet , but I think the issue of AMOC stability should not be ignored . The mere possibility that the AMOC could collapse should be a strong reason for concern in an era when human activity is forcing significant changes to the Earth 's systems . " <p> He further stated that the lack of AMOC stability may have many repercussions -- it may cause harsh winters in Europe and storms in Africa . <p> Federov has worked out the model along with Shineng Hu from the Scripps Institution of Oceanography , University of California at San Diego using observational data and computer modelling to plot out the changes . <p> According to the researchers , India Ocean is the most affected by global warming , and warms the fastest . However , what happens when it warms up is interesting . Because of warming , it generates additional precipitation , which draws air from areas such as the Atlantic . <p> The increased precipitation over the Indian Ocean decreases the precipitation over the Atlantic Ocean . This decreased precipitation in the Atlantic leads to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rainwater . When this salty water moves north because of AMOC , it gets cooler and sinks . <p> However , the researchers warn that this inter-ocean balance is fragile .
