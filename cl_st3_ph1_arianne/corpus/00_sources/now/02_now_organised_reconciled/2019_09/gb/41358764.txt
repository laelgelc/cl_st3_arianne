That 's why this month , Oxfam has been asking people to take a pledge to say ' no to new ' clothes . Set up to help the planet and its people , our new campaign , called Second Hand September , aims to break the fast fashion cycle and help people reduce their impact on the climate . <p> Our research suggests that more than two tonnes of clothing are bought every 60 seconds in the UK .
