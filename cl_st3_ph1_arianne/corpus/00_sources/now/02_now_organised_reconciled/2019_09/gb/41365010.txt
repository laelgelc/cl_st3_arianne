<h> Calderdale communities pull together during weekend flooding <p> Calderdale 's kindness and community spirit shone through once again , after persistent heavy rain caused flooding issues on Sunday 28 July . <p> Local residents , flood wardens , Calderdale Council teams and organisations including the Environment Agency , West Yorkshire Fire and Rescue Service , West Yorkshire Police and Yorkshire Water were amongst those braving the bad weather on Sunday and working throughout the night to help flood-affected communities . <p> The Environment Agency issued flood warnings across the Upper Calder Valley on Sunday and sounded the flood sirens . Surface water flooding led to a number of road closures , including roads into Todmorden . There were some cases of cellar flooding in homes . The Council set up an emergency hub to coordinate the joint response . People were urged not to travel due to hazardous driving conditions , and to report blocked gullies on the Council 's website . <p> Extra resources , measures and plans were in place before the flooding hit , with Council teams busy checking and clearing drains , culverts and trash screens in flood-prone areas last week and over the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after the flooding , additional teams , gully wagons and road sweepers were deployed to deal with flood-related issues .
