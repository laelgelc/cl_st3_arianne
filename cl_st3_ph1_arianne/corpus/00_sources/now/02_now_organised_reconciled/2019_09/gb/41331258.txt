<h> Brexit Britain : could the Canada-US relationship be a model for the UK and the EU ? <p> The vote to Leave was a huge blow , says Simon Hix . But with so few British politicians willing to engage with the EU 's political project , the threat of Brexit had been apparent for some time . He sets out a plan that could see Britain eventually enjoy a relationship with the EU that resembles Canada 's with the United States . A ' quick divorce ' under Article 50 would be followed by exit negotiations during which Britain would remain a member of the EEA , with some restrictions on free movement . However , the EEA is not a long-term proposition , and we would eventually have to move to an ' association agreement ' . None of this will be easy . <p> So we have voted to leave the European Union . I was not in favour of this , but accept the outcome as legitimate . Currently , being in the EEA means accepting full free movement of people . As UK membership of the EEA is likely to be temporary , I would favour a special agreement on the free movement of people . <p> For example , this could be an " emergency brake " , such as a cap on the number of work permits/social security numbers the UK grants each year to EU workers and that the EU member states grant to UK workers . <h> Moving on <p> Membership of the EEA would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it would inevitably mean surrendering even more sovereignty than being a member of the EU , as we would have to accept EU single market rules , without any say over them .
