<p> The EBRD 's Climate Resilience Bond will be issued in conformity with the four core principles of the Green Bond Principles , while the projects earmarked for the Use of Proceeds are selected and managed in alignment with the Climate Resilience Principles , published on 17 September 2019 by the Climate Bonds Initiative ( CBI ) . <p> The principles provide clarity on the broad range and scope of potential resilience investments , incorporating climate resilience in the Climate Bonds Standard . <p> " This is a major step forward in the development of capital market instruments that can crowd in private finance at scale for climate resilience , " said Craig Davies , Head of Climate Resilience Investments at the EBRD . <p> Anna Creed , Head of Standards at the Climate Bonds Initiative , added : " We congratulate the EBRD on the launch of their dedicated Climate Resilience Bond and taking the lead in the initial implementation of the Climate Resilience Principles within their green bond programme .
