A country 's well-being is measured by the aggregation of the well-being of all residents and the health of the ecosystem. -- Clair Brown , Buddhist Economics , 2017 . <p> A practicing Buddhist , a professor of economics at the University of California , Berkeley ( UCB ) , and director of the Center for Work , Technology , and Society at UCB , Clair Brown has a formidable academic pedigree .
