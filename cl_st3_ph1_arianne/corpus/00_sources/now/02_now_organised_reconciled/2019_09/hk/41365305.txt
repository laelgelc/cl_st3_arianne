As they constitute quite literally the future of our society , we should not underestimate or neglect the young . They are the key to a viable future for any institution , be it a country , a religious community , or a family . It is critical , therefore , that they are provided sound , productive , and empathetic guidance . <p> Yet counsel alone is not enough . They need to be given the space to explore their vocations , to develop their skills , and to express themselves within Buddhist communities .
