<h> Kildare local named on Teachers Inspire shortlist <h> Shortlist <p> Mary Daly , a local of Celbridge , has been named on the shortlist for the inaugural Teachers Inspire Ireland initiative . <p> Ms Daly has lived in Celbridge since 1985 and taught in St Dominic 's College , Ballyfermot . She was nominated by Niamh Smith , from Ballyfermot , who nominated Ms Daly for her impact on individual students and the wider community . While Ms Daly still taught at St Dominic 's , Ms Smith returned to the school to work in its after-school programme and , encouraged by Ms Daly , went on to study Community and Youth Work at Maynooth University . Ms Smith is now the Project Leader of a youth service in Ringsend . <p> The initiative , which is focused on highlighting the enormous contribution made by teachers in Irish society , was launched by Dublin City University ( DCU ) earlier this year , with the support of businessman and philanthropist Dermot Desmond . <p> Over May and June , DCU called for members of the public to share their personal stories of how a teacher transformed their lives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from each province -- have been shortlisted from over 400 entries received from every corner of Ireland . None of the teachers knew they were nominated in advance . <p> Announcing the shortlist at DCU earlier this week , Professor Brian MacCraith , President of DCU , said : " The entries submitted to Teachers Inspire Ireland demonstrate just how significant a role teachers play in Irish life . <p> " We received stories about teachers dealing in an inspirational way with major societal challenges . We received stories of teachers who have played -- and continue to play -- a key part in helping individuals and whole communities adapt to change . <p> " The nominations highlighted teachers dealing with issues such as homelessness , mental health , immigration , unplanned pregnancies , gender identity support , community regeneration , and climate change .
