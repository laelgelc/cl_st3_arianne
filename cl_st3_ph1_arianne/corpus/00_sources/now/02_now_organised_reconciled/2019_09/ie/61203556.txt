<p> My perfect day in Tipperary starts with an early morning run in the hills of Coum near Portroe . Whatever the weather , the rolling forest trails run in the company of long time friends are always good for the soul , head and body . <p> My end to that perfect day is watching a dramatic sunset over Lough Derg or meeting school friends for a pint or a coffee to discuss the issues of the day . <p> Who has made the greatest contribution to Tipperary in your lifetime - and why ? <p> It 's difficult to credit just one person . For me many people make great contributions to Tipperary every day . I admire volunteers - the lifeblood of any community - whose contributions keep local clubs going year-in-year-out . <p> Another , and sometimes forgotten contribution , is that of indigenous export driven companies that have made Tipperary thrive over the years . I hold in high esteem , the self-employed shopkeepers fighting to keep the economic lifeblood of traditional towns flowing whilst people turn to shopping online or in bigger cities . These men and women - many working endless hours and sometimes without due credit and praise , work tirelessly at both national and local level for the county and their localities to sustain continued improvement in the county . <p> The greatest contribution to Tipperary in my lifetime comes from the people of the county itself who put themselves out there to make a difference and assert change . <p> What 's your first Tipperary memory ? <p> The smell of freshly cut lawns whilst playing hurling and soccer on the green at Melrose estate in Nenagh - the estate I grew up in . Those children , still my friends , have a special place in my early memories because that early socialisation shaped me for later years . <p> However , learning to swim with Sean Dealy - a lifeguard in Nenagh swimming pool - is my earliest memory . On a good day you can overlook the spectacular Lough Derg to County Clare . On a bad day , the elements - wind and driving sideways rain - can blow the cobwebs from your soul during a run or ramble . <p> The latter is sometimes more exhilarating than the best stone splitting sunny day . There 's nothing like running against a stiff wind up hill to harden your resolve ! Coum is the place where I find it easy to relax - and that 's in all weathers : wind , rain , sun and snow . <p> What do you think gives Tipperary its unique identity ? <p> Tipperary 's people , their talents and attitudes to life gives the county its unique identity . In many walks of life Tipperary people have excelled by working hard and inspiring others . <p> Being from Tipperary it 's hard to choose a favourite writer .
