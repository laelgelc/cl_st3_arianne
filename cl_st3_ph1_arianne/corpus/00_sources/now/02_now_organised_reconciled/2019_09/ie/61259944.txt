<h> Green wave surging across Mayo <p> Mags Sheehan , secretary for the group , says they are welcoming a lot of new members , especially since the European elections campaign started earlier this year . <p> She commented : " There is a new-found awareness around climate change and the biodiversity emergency , and people know that we need to take action urgently . " <p> The rise in Green support has come at a time when the Arctic ice is melting at an alarming rate , much faster than experts predicted , forests burn all around the globe , there have been record breaking temperatures across Europe , and horrific destruction in the Bahamas . At the same time , schoolchildren all around the world are ' striking for their future ' , led by young people like Greta Thunberg . <p> " The Green Party fully supports young people around the world who have taken drastic action . They know that they will be the ones most affected by impending climate chaos , " said Saoirse McHugh , who ran a very successful campaign as candidate for the European Parliament . <p> " My generation is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ demand a change in our politics .
