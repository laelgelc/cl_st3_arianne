<h> ' You have stolen my dreams ' , Greta tells UN summit <h> The eyes of future generations are on world leaders to take urgent action on climate change , teenage activist Greta Thunberg has told the UN . <p> In an impassioned speech at the UN climate action summit , the 16-year-old , who has inspired a global climate strike movement , told delegates they would not be forgiven if they failed to tackle warming . <p> Ms Thunberg , who travelled to the US by yacht to avoid flying , said she should not be up on stage , but in school in Sweden . <p> " You come to us young people for hope - how dare you ? You have stolen my dreams , my childhood with your empty words , " the teenager said . <p> " People are suffering , people are dying , entire ecosystems are collapsing , we are in the beginning of a mass extinction and all you can talk about is money and the fairytale of endless economic growth .
