<h> Kilkenny school children invited to share their local actions for global change through exciting new Goal Changemakers Award <h> Enter now ! <p> Reporter : <p> Sian Moloughney <p> 26 Sept 2019 <p> Email : <p> **33;2379;TOOLONG <p> 0 <p> International aid agency , GOAL , is inviting primary school pupils from Kilkenny to share their local actions for global change through its exciting new Changemakers Award launched today ( Thursday September 26th ) <p> The Award aims to show young people that a sustainable and fair future is achievable through the right actions . It will introduce children to the UN 's 17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , and ask them to share the actions they are taking to contribute to creating a better world . <p> Speaking at the launch of Changemakers , GOAL 's Global Citizenship Manager , Nina Sachau , said : " As a humanitarian aid agency GOAL is acutely aware of the challenges the global community is facing into the future - climate change , poverty , migration , and global injustice - and hearing about this can be a bit overwhelming , especially for young people . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and fair future is achievable and equip them with the tools to help contribute to the future . With the new Changemakers Award we want to introduce the Sustainable Development Goals to the classrooms and celebrate , broadcast and share the great ideas primary school pupils are already putting into action to create a better world . We are lucky we have a generation of incredibly well informed , open minded and active young people and educators , doing so many things every day to bring about change . " <p> The Award is open to 3rd , 4th , 5th and to 6th primary school classes who are being invited to get to know the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , and to share the local actions they take that contribute to their achievement . <p> As part of the competition teachers will be provided with curriculum linked lesson plans that introduce children in an engaging and fun way to the SDGs .
