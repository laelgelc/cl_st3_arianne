<h> WATCH : Young climate change protesters take to streets of Limerick <p> OVER A THOUSAND protesters took to the streets of Limerick city this Friday afternoon in support of the global climate strike happening across the world today . <p> Leading the protest was teen climate change campaigner , 14-year-old Saoirse Exton , who set up Fridays for Future Limerick earlier this year . <p> Saoirse , who has been striking every Friday for months now in aid of the climate change movement told the Limerick Leader : " We 're striking here today because not enough action is being taken by our government locally , nationally or internationally so this is why we are fighting here today . " <p> Hundreds of pupils from numerous schools across the city and county took part in the march , which set off from Arthurs Quay Park at 1.30pm , many of whom carried signs calling for action . <p> " Teenagers all around the world have asked for adult support , so that 's why we 're here today .
