<h> Festival 's popular forum puts the focus on climate change and food <p> At Baltimore Sailing Club for the food forum , as part of the Taste of West Cork festival were , from left : Fiona Field , festival manager , Baroness Bryony Worthington and Dr Aifric O'Sullivan , UCD . <p> CLIMATE Change was the theme of the annual Food Forum , held as part of the Taste of West Cork Food Festival , in Baltimore last weekend . <p> Entitled ' Facing the Challenges of a Rapidly Changing World -- Our Survival ' , the event was held at Baltimore Sailing Club and drew a large crowd , for one of the festival 's most popular events . <p> Owen Brennan , executive chairman of Devenish , the agri-tech firm which supplies animal feeds worldwide , highlighted the crucial part that quality soil plays in the production of quality food . <p> Soil quality has diminished significantly worldwide , said Mr Brennan , and efforts must be redoubled to reverse that trend , yielding better returns , better products , better health outcomes and better overall water quality .
