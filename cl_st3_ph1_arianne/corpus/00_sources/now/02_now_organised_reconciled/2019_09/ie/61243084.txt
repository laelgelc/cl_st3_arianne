<p> The unique survey of over 3,500 respondents from across the globe found that over 90 percent of prospective students think universities could do more to be environmentally sustainable . The respondents were asked a series of questions relating to how sustainable they perceive universities to be and how they expect universities to address climate change . <p> 94 percent of respondents think universities could do more to be environmentally sustainable . <p> Almost 90 percent of respondents think a university should take further action to reduce its environmental impact . <p> 78 percent of respondents said that universities are better than other sectors such as finance and construction when it comes to being environmentally friendly . <p> 79 percent of respondents would be more likely to choose a degree if the content taught them about reducing their environmental impact . <p> As part of the survey , QS also asked respondents what they most want to learn from studying at university . Over half of applicants said that using resources efficiently to limit the impact on the environment is a skill they most want to learn while studying . <p> Protesters at the People 's Climate March highlight @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ DC on April 29 , 2017 , President Trump 's 100th day in office . Reducing single use plastics and reducing the amount of waste that goes to landfill were the second and third most important activity respectively . <p> The new research published on September 18 coincides with the Global Climate Strike that starts today ( September 20 ) , where thousands of young people from around the world are set to walk out of schools , colleges and universities .
