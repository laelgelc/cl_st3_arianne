Our emissions increase each year and accumulate in the atmosphere , while their impacts accelerate . They acidify and heat our oceans and Earth 's surface , melt polar ice , rise sea level , and are severely altering and destabilizing our climate system . If left unmitigated , they are likely to decimate practically all life-forms that share Earth 's natural resources . Scientists warn that at a +3C rise , vast regions of Earth 's landmass will have become inhospitable and uninhabitable .
