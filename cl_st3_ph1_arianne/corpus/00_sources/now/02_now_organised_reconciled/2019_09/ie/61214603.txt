<h> Carbon capture champions <p> Renewable energy will not be enough . A massive improvement in energy saving will help but will not tick all the boxes . New parliamentarians will discover that EU climate change policy is strongly infl uenced by prejudice . <p> We may claim support for technological neutrality but it 's not true .
