<h> Wasp count has been high in Mayo <p> MAYO has accounted for the second highest number of wasp callouts from Rentokil this summer . <p> The pest control provider experienced its highest number of wasp callouts in the past six summers during the period June - August 2019 . The company has experienced a continual rise in wasp callouts of 136% during the summer period since 2017 . <p> The company is warning the public to treat the stinging insects with caution as the early autumn period is when they are most aggressive . <p> This summer ( June -- August ) , Cork accounted for the highest level of wasp callouts ( 29% of all callouts ) . Mayo came in second at 28% , with Sligo ( 10% ) and Kildare ( 7% ) the next counties most affected . <p> Rentokil attributes the rise in callouts to climate change and cautions that as the country experiences increasingly warmer conditions every year , the wasp population will continue to thrive .
