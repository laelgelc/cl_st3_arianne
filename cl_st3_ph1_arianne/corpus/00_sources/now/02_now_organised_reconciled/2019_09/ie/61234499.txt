<h> Answers to climate crisis on the agenda for Irish tech during Climate Finance Week Ireland <p> A week of specially themed events in November will help Irish tech businesses tackle how they respond to climate change and the challenges and opportunities involved in financing Ireland 's move to a low-carbon economy . <p> Climate Finance Week Ireland 2019 , sponsored by AIB , is a unique programme of more than 20 events across five days . It will include climate-themed talks on sustainable finance and investments , interactive workshops and events attended by senior domestic and international corporate leaders , financiers and key policymakers . <p> The week of events ( November 4th to the 8th ) is being convened by Sustainable Nation Ireland with the Department of Finance and is part of the Government 's Ireland for Finance Strategy to promote Ireland as a hub for sustainable finance and responsible investment . <p> A highlight of Climate Finance Week Ireland 2019 will be the Annual AIB Sustainability Conference which will be held in Dublin .
