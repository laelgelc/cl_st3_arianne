<h> Developing climate change action plans for the Dublin local authorities <p> 13 December 2016 <p> The four local authorities in Dublin have come together with Codema to address national and EU climate-change targets , with a focus on seven areas : citizen engagement , planning , energy , transport , water , waste , and ecosystems and biodiversity . Suzanne Fitzpatrick reports <p> This is the first time that neighbouring local authorities in Ireland have come together to help address national and EU climate change targets.The Dublin region is central to Ireland 's development , and with an increasing population comes a greater responsibility to tackle the effects of climate change that are largely caused by human activity . <p> The strategy is the first step towards developing collaborative , transboundary Dublin climate change action plans that will help to prevent the harmful activities that are releasing greenhouse gases into the atmosphere , and limit the impact of the effects that are inevitable . <p> These action plans will be developed and finalised throughout 2017 and 2018 , with one action plan developed for each local authority region . For Dublin , this target effectively translates into challenging reductions in fossil-fuel use for transport ( petrol/diesel ) and for heating buildings ( gas/oil ) . <p> This act has two main aims : the creation of a national mitigation plan to reduce or prevent greenhouse emissions , and the development of a national adaptation framework to reduce the negative effects of climate change . <p> The National Adaptation Framework and National Mitigation Plan put a statutory obligation on local @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plan for their own area .
