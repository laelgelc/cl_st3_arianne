<h> WATCH : Eco Eye 's Duncan Stewart has shocking facts on climate change <p> Environmentalist and Eco Eye presenter Duncan Stewart was speaking in Laois last weekend at a workshop on climate change . <p> He described a stark future unless fossil fuel use is halved by 2030 . <p> " It 's going to shock us , what 's coming down the track , " he said . <p> He said hundreds of millions of people will be forced to emigrate from climate stressed regions , and huge regions of the world will be inhospitable and uninhabitable for people and most species . <p> Laois is the focus for a series of public workshops led by Duncan Stewart and other experts over the coming weeks to see how it can lead the way in becoming a low carbon county . <p> Farmers , householders and students are all urged to take part .
