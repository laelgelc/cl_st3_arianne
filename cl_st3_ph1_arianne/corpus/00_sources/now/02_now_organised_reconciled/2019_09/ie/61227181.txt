<h> Galway City adopts climate change adaptation strategy <h> Galway City Council has officially adopted a Climate Change Adaptation Strategy for the period from 2019 to 2024 . <p> The strategy to prepare for the effects of climate change in the city and to try and mitigate negative impacts was unanimously adopted by the council at the monthly meeting for September . <p> A total of 24 amendments were attached to the draft strategy before it was voted on by the council . <p> The adoption of the Climate Adaptation Strategy 2019-2024 is a requirement under the National Adaptation Framework , which requires all Local Authorities to have one in place by the end of September 2019 . <p> The draft was brought before the city council after a period of public consultation , and will now be finalised by the elected members before publication .
