<h> Laois students demand climate action at Mountrath school <p> Students at Mountrath Community School were among pupils from across Laois who made their voices heard in demanding action to tackle climate change on the climate action strike day . <p> Mountrath fifth-year student Daniel Moore Daniel organised and coordinated a protest in the school on Friday , September 20 . He said that the response to this global problem of climate change is phenomenal . <p> " We want to make a difference . We want the government to actually listen to us , and respond . We are all inspired by the incredible example of environmental activist Greta Thunberg , in Sweden , " he said . <p> Fifth year student Niamh Robertson said that the support for this protest is so encouraging . <p> " We are living in an environmental crisis , and no one is acting , " she said . <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Derry Now editorial team on 028 7129 6600 for Derry City stories Or 028 7774 3970 for County Derry stories .
