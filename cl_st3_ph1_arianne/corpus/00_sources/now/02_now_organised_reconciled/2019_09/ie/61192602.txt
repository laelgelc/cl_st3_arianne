<p> The number of pension schemes in the Netherlands fell to 208 at the end of last year , according to De Nederlandsche Bank , from over 1,000 at the end of the 1990s . Owing to strong regulatory pressure , the number has declined much faster compared with other countries with similarly high numbers of schemes , such as Ireland , Switzerland or the UK . <p> Pension fund closures or mergers matter a great deal to their participants and sponsors . But it is generally accepted that fewer , larger and better organised pension funds will result in better pensions in the long term .
