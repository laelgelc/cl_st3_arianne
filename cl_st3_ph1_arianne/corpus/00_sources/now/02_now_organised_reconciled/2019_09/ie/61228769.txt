<h> Subset to open new exhibition on climate change in Dublin <h> " We hope this macro expression of the micro will give pause for thought . " <p> A new exhibition by Subset about climate change and humanity 's effect on the environment is opening up in Dublin soon . <p> ' Micro vs . Macro ' is described as a presentation on microplastics , a collection of works intended to bring awareness to the damning effects of climate change - and how so many of us have been wilfully blind to our own involvement . <p> Opening in Dublin 's RHA Gallery later this month , the exhibition will focus on creation and consumption , dwarfing the viewer using magnified imagery , pointing to the agency we have lost through the sheer size of the issue . <p> " The main artwork destabilizes the preconception that humans have control over what we create , " says the collective . <p> " The extent of the consequences of microplastics is widely unknown . With each technological advancement in pollution detection , our situation seems more and more hopeless . <p> " The repercussions are overlooked and now the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We hope this macro expression of the micro will give pause for thought . " <p> The exhibition will open as part of Subset 's wider project , ' Climate ' - a selection works aiming to raise much needed awareness around the current climate catastrophe . <p> Following on from the success of the collective 's much loved David Attenborough mural , ' Climate ' will include art pieces in Denver , Colorado at Crush Walls Festivals , as well as in Temple Bar . <p> The Temple Bar piece will feature on a prominent building , its scale intended to grab the attention of the viewer . <p> It is due to be unveiled at the end of October . <p> Subset 's ' Micro vs . Macro ' exhibition opens in Dublin 's RHA Gallery on September 20 and runs until January 26 , 2020.
