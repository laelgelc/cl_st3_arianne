<p> What are EuroRAP 's main goals and objectives and what programmes are you running to achieve them ? <p> The European Road Assessment Programme ( EuroRAP ) is an international not-for-profit organisation that aims to reduce death and serious injury on Europe 's roads . We address this through a programme of systematic risk assessment and by identifying shortcomings that can be addressed by practical road improvement measures . <p> Road Assessment Programmes ( RAP ) were introduced following the success of the European New Car Assessment Programme ( Euro NCAP ) , which has already saved tens of thousands of lives and prevented countless serious injuries .
