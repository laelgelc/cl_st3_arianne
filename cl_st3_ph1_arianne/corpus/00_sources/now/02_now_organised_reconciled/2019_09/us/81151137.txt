<p> STRASBOURG , Sept 18 ( Reuters ) - A deal to ensure a smooth British exit from the European Union is still possible , but the risk of a no-deal is also " very real " , European Commission President Jean-Claude Juncker said on Wednesday . <p> " There is very little time left . The prime minister has told us he wants a deal , but he will leave with or without a deal , " Juncker told the plenary in Strasbourg . " The risk of a no deal is very real , " said Juncker , who met British prime minister Boris Johnson on Monday in Luxembourg .
