<h> HC3 to host discussion on climate change and the Colorado River this Wednesday <p> FRISCO -- The High Country Conservation Center and the Summit Climate Action Collaborative are hosting a speaker at the Frisco Adventure Park Day Lodge on Wednesday to discuss the past , present and future of the Colorado River . <p> The event , from 6 -- 7:30 p.m. on Sept. 18 , will feature a talk by Colorado State University senior water and climate research scientist Brad Udall , who will discuss the future of the Colorado River in regards to climate change . Food and drinks will be provided , and the event will also include a cash bar . <p> According to a release , the Colorado River has been in an extended drought since 2000 , spurring a massive drop in two of the nation 's largest reservoirs -- lakes Mead and Powell -- that have lost more than 40% of their water storage .
