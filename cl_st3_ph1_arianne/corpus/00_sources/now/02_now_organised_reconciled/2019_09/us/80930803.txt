<h> Shared Link <p> Some of the first financial relief the Bahamas receives in the aftermath of Hurricane Dorian may be a rapid insurance payout from a novel program just 12 years old . <p> CCRIF , once short for the Caribbean Catastrophe Risk Insurance Facility , was established in 2007 to provide funds for countries hit by cyclones , earthquakes or extraordinary rainfall . The segregated portfolio company is an example of parametric insurance , designed to make immediate injections of money in the wake of a catastrophe .
