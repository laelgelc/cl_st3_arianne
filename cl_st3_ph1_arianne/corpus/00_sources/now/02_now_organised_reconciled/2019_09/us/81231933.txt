<p> Saturday was one of the most important days of U.S. Sen. Cory Booker 's presidential campaign , he and his staff said . <p> The New Jersey Democrat spoke at the Polk County Steak Fry along with 16 other Democrats running for president and made two other campaign stops , filling rooms in Marshalltown and Grinnell . But before all that , his campaign dropped an announcement : It would need to raise $1.7 million over the last 10 days of September or potentially fold . <p> Addisu Demissie , Booker 's campaign manager , told reporters on a conference call Saturday morning that without the cash surge , " we do not see a legitimate long-term path forward to ultimately winning the nomination . " <p> Booker has so far failed to gain much traction in Iowa . In the latest Des Moines Register/CNN/Mediacom Iowa Poll released Saturday night , 3% of respondents listed the him as their first choice in the first-in-the-nation caucuses . <p> But 60% of people polled said they have favorably views of him , and only about 20% of likely Democratic caucusgoers said they are locked into their choices .
