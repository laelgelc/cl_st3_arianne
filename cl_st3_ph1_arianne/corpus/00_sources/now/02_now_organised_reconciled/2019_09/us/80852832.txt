Levin : Gazette 's article of praise for David Koch off the mark <p> Published : 9/1/2019 9:00:08 PM <p> The Gazette 's paean to David Koch ( " Academy mourns Koch 's passing , " Aug. 24 ) couches the profound damage he has done -- to the process of governance in this country , to social and political relations in this country , to the economy of this country , to the physical world in which we live ( read : environment ) -- in terms of upstanding , admirable sounding political and civic involvement . <p> Your description hardly does justice to the scale or the nature of his destructiveness . The New York Times ran an op-ed ( " David Koch was the ultimate climate change denier , " by Christopher Leonard ) which , if your word-limit had allowed , I would have pasted into this letter . I encourage you to read it . <p> Perhaps it will fill out your knowledge of him and spur you to publish a more accurate portrayal .
