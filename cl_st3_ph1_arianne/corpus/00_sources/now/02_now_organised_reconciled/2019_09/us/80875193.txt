<p> WROCLAW , Poland , Sept 3 ( Reuters ) - A rare Palawan Hornbill chick has left its nest and is exploring its outdoor enclosure at Wroclaw Zoo in western Poland , delighting staff and members of the public . <p> The bird , identifiable by its distinctive beak and black and white plumage , hatched in May 2019 and is the fourth chick to be born in the zoo . <p> Palawan Hornbills are notoriously difficult to breed , the chairman of the zoo 's board , Radoslaw Ratajczak , said , adding that they need to have the right food and the right conditions . <p> Wroclaw Zoo 's six Palawan Hornbills now represent almost 90% of the world 's population living in captivity , according to information on its website . <p> The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species classifies the birds , native to the island of Palawan in the Philippines , as ' vulnerable ' .
