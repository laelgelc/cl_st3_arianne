<h> Wahluke High School students partake in global-wide environmental protest <p> MATTAWA - Wahluke High School students partook in global climate strike event on Friday in Mattawa . Multiple students staged a walkout and displayed signs protesting business and government inaction on climate change . The picketing event was part of a global-wide display of activism with over 2,500 events scheduled in over 150 countries between September 23 and September 27 . It is likely to be one of the largest environmental protests in history . <p> " I 'm here for Strike For Climate Change because it will impact us in many different ways .
