<p> According to the annual Global Investor Study conducted by Schroders , global asset manager with $565.5 billion in assets under management , American investors are positive on sustainable investing and believe it makes a difference , but are n't necessarily incorporating it into the investment decisions . <p> The study , which surveyed over 1,500 U.S. investors and over 25,000 investors globally , found that six in ten Americans agree that all investment funds should consider sustainability factors ( 61 percent ) or believe they can contribute to a more sustainable world by choosing sustainable investment products ( 62 percent ) . However , their actions do n't necessarily reflect these beliefs : <p> Only 15 percent say they actually invest in sustainable themed investments , and 31 percent say they are interested and want to invest . <p> Just over half ( 51 percent ) say they always consider sustainability factors when selecting investment products , less than the global average of 57 percent .
