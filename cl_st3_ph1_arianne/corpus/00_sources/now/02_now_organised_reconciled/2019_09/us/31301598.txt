<p> What inspired you to write this book ? What do you want people to understand about consumerism and climate change ? <p> Before I became an environmental journalist , I felt that so many of the articles I read about climate change dealt with it on a scale that did n't make sense to me -- it was either eliminating plastic bottles or transitioning to 100 percent renewable electricity in the next decade . It seemed like there had to be something in between those two extremes that might help all of us better understand this issue , which often feels so abstract and distant , but with which we all interact constantly , whether we 're aware of it or not . <p> So I wanted to write a book that did that -- that helped us understand climate change in the terms and scale of our own lives -- and also made the issue feel immediate and relatable .
