<p> Michigan Democrats have a lot of questions for candidates participating in the second primary debate in Detroit next week . <p> Twenty candidates will take the debate stage from the historic Fox Theatre , split into two groups of 10 on July 30 and July 31 . Candidates visited the state more than 30 times during the first seven months of 2019 , but the debate offers the first chance for a wide audience to hear directly from the presidential hopefuls . <p> MLive.com met with a dozen likely Michigan Democratic voters across the state , most of whom still were undecided on where their support will lie next year .
