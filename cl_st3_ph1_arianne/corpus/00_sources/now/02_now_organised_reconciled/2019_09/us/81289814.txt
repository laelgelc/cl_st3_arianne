<h> TOP NEWS <h> Thursday , September 26 , 2019 <p> Los Angeles-based Circular Systems , a startup developing material science technology to transform waste into fiber , yarn , and textile fabrics for the fashion industry , has scored funding as part of a Levi Strauss &amp; Co . ( LS&amp;Co. ) accelerator effort , Levi 's announced today . Amount of funding was not announced , however , Levi 's has been offering up as much as $50,000 to startups to help tackle some of the sustainability challenges in the apparel industry . The award is part of a larger , $380,000 in funding Levi Strauss &amp; Co. said it provided to the second class of its LS&amp;Co . Collaboratory , a set of cmpanies that are working to create a more sustainable apparel industry . LA 's Circular Sytsems is led by co-founder Isaac Nichelson . Levi Strauss &amp; Co. said this class of its program is focused on climate change , which it says is most important issues of our time and a critical challenge for the apparel industry .
