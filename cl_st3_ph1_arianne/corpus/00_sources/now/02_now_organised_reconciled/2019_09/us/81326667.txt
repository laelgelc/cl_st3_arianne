<h> Lioi : For the beauty of the earth <p> This coming Friday , Oct. 4 , is the Feast of Saint Francis of Assisi . Four years ago , Pope Francis declared Sept. 1 the World Day of Prayer for the Care of Creation . He invited us to pray this month for our delicate biosphere in peril from the effects of climate change ; he asked us to pray until the feast of the patron saint of creation , Francis of Assisi , on Oct. 4 . This Prayer for Our Earth is taken from Pope Francis ' encyclical Laudato Si . <p> All-powerful God , you are present in the whole universe <p> and in the smallest of your creatures . <p> You embrace with your tenderness all that exists . <p> Pour out upon us the power of your love , <p> that we may protect life and beauty . <p> Fill us with peace , that we may live <p> as brothers and sisters , harming no one . <p> O God of the poor , help us to rescue the abandoned <p> and forgotten of this earth , so precious in your eyes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we may protect the world and not prey on it , <p> that we may sow beauty , not pollution and destruction . <p> Touch the hearts of those who look only for gain <p> at the expense of the poor and the earth . <p> Teach us to discover the worth of each thing , <p> to be filled with awe and contemplation , <p> to recognize that we are profoundly united <p> with every creature as we journey towards your infinite light . <p> We thank you for being with us each day . <p> Encourage us , we pray , in our struggle <p> for justice , love and peace .
