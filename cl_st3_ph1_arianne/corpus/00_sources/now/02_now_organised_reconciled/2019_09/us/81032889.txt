<p> GOSHEN -- Mayors from northern Indiana and officials from across the state will gather Thursday for the Indiana Climate Leadership Summit at Goshen College 's Church-Chapel . <p> The focus will be on resiliency , with an emphasis on flooding , stormwater infrastructure and climate modeling , given last year 's historic floods in northern Indiana , according to organizers with the advocacy group Earth Charter Indiana . There will be presentations by experts from Purdue University 's Climate Change Research Center , the University of Notre Dame University and others . <p> The event will run from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. , and the morning activities are free to the public .
