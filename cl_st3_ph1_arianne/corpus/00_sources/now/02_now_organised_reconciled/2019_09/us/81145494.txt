<h> Climate Change Coalition Screens ' Paris to Pittsburgh ' <p> The Climate Change Coalition of Door County ( **27;391;TOOLONG ) , with support from five county churches , will screen Paris to Pittsburgh -- an acclaimed documentary from Bloomberg Philanthropies and the National Geographic -- on Oct. 2 , 7 pm , at Bj ? rklunden , 7590 Boynton Lane in Baileys Harbor . <p> The film explores the social and economic impacts of disasters fueled by climate change and features local leaders and everyday Americans who tell stories of climate-related recovery , resiliency , innovation and tireless efforts to reduce carbon emissions . Pittsburgh , now committed to energy efficiency , is one of many examples of bold economic and climate leadership in the film . <p> The Climate Change Coalition is presenting Paris to Pittsburgh with support from Ephraim Moravian Church , Hope United Church of Christ , Shepherd of the Bay Lutheran Church , St. Luke 's Episcopal Church , the Unitarian Universalist Fellowship of Door County and Wisconsin Interfaith Power and Light .
