<h> International organization set to meet in Juneau next week <p> JUNEAU , Alaska ( AP ) - Representatives of over 30 nations will attend the International Forum of Sovereign Wealth Funds next week in Juneau . <p> The Juneau Empire reports the gathering is the first time the voluntary global organization 's annual conference has met in the United States . <p> The Alaska Permanent Fund is hosting the event , which helps sovereign fund managers make investment decisions . <p> Alaska Permanent Fund Corp . CEO Angela Rodell says the meeting will be an opportunity to highlight U.S. priorities . <p> A Juneau environmental group plans to be active in dissuading these fund managers from investing in fossil fuel companies . The group , 350 Juneau , plans to hold two public events aiming to underscore the seriousness of climate change . <p> The conference takes place from Tuesday through Friday . It will mostly be closed to the public .
