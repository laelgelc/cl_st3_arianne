<h> NextEra Energy : U.S. Can Hit 50% Renewable Energy by 2030 <h> The world 's largest producer of electricity from the wind and sun is surprisingly optimistic about the future of renewable energy . <p> If you 're anxious about America 's purported slow response to climate change , then you might want to take a close look at NextEra Energy 's ( NYSE:NEE ) June investor presentation . Aside from updated numbers , not much stands out in the 235-slide presentation , which includes the usual colorful graphs and charts communicating the latest monthly results for the business 's most important metrics . <p> But sharp-eyed investors may have noticed a subtle change to one pie chart in particular : the one that predicts how the United States will generate electricity in 2030 . NextEra Energy 's latest published projection estimates the mix from renewable energy sources could approach 50% . <p> That 's up from the 25% share for renewables projected in the company 's May investor presentation , and significantly more optimistic than virtually every other public estimate .
