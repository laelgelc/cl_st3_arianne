<p> MOSCOW , Sept 20 ( Reuters ) - A new outbreak of African swine fever has been found at a privately-held farm in a village in Russia 's Primorsk region near the border with China , Russia 's agriculture watchdog said on Friday . <p> The virus - which is highly contagious among pigs , but is not dangerous to humans - has been detected in several areas in the region in recent months . <p> China has reported more than 140 cases of the incurable disease since it was first found in the country in August last year .
