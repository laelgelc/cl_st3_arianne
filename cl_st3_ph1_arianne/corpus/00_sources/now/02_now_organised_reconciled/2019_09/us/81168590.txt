<p> RENNES , France , Sept 19 ( Reuters ) - A Belgian F-16 fighter jet crashed near the town of Lorient in northwestern France on Thursday , the Belgian Air Force said . <p> Both pilots ejected from the aircraft , the air force said on Twitter . <p> " One of our F-16s crashed near Lorient in France , the pilots left the plane with their ejection seats . " <p> The fighter jet ripped off part of the roof of a house before crashing , an official at the regional police headquarters told Reuters . <p> Emergency services rescued one of the pilots . The second was initially suspended on a high-voltage line by his parachute . <p> Local newspaper Le Telegramme said a team from Enedis , which manages the electricity grid in France , had brought him down safely . <p> The F-16 jet took off from the Florennes air base near Namur in Belgium and was going to the Lann-Bihou ? aeronautical base in Morbihan . The aircraft was not armed .
