<p> NEW YORK , Sept 24 ( Reuters ) - Saudi Arabia Minister of State for Foreign Affairs Adel al-Jubeir said on Tuesday that he expects the investigation into who was responsible for the recent attack on the kingdom 's oil facilities to be completed " fairly soon . " <p> Jubeir , who was speaking at a conference on the sidelines of the U.N . General Assembly , said Saudi Arabia believed Iran was behind the attacks , and would consider " all options " for its response once the investigation is done .
