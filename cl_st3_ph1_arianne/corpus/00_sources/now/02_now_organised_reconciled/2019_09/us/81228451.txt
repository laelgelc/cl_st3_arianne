<p> One person is dead after a motor vehicle crashed into Six Mile Creek in Ithaca on Saturday night . <p> The accident took place around 6:39 p.m. near the intersection of Fulton and West Clinton streets , according to the Ithaca Police Department . <p> Units from the Ithaca Police Department , Ithaca Fire Department and Bangs Ambulance responded to the area and discovered the vehicle on its roof in the creek , and a bystander trying to hold the driver 's head out of the water , police said . <p> Police and fire personnel forced a car door open and removed the victim from the car . He was transported to Cayuga Medical Center , where he was pronounced dead after efforts to resuscitate him failed , police said . <p> Police have not yet released the victim 's identity . A passenger in the vehicle was uninjured , police said . <p> The crash happened shortly after a body was found in the same area of the creek . Around 5 p.m. , kayakers found a body floating behind the railroad bridge off of Fulton Street .
