<p> KABUL , Sept 2 ( Reuters ) - Afghan President Ashraf Ghani on Monday told the U.S envoy for Afghanistan to share details of a draft deal between the U.S. and Taliban with all Afghan leaders , the president 's spokesman said . <p> Zalmay Khalilzad , the Afghan-born U.S. diplomat who has completed nine rounds of talks with Taliban representatives , met Ghani and is scheduled to hold meetings with all Afghan leaders in Kabul this week to build a consensus before the deal is signed . <p> The U.S.-backed government will need to " study and assess " details of the draft deal , the spokesman , Sediq Sediqqi , told reporters . <p> Khalilzad has met with Ghani twice in the last two days , he said .
