Glaciers in the tropical Andes have been retreating faster than most other glaciers since scientists began keeping detailed records in the 1970s , because tropical latitudes get more direct sunlight and radiation . <p> When a glacier melts away , at first only bedrock is left behind -- sometimes rough gravel and sometimes smooth rock , worn down by centuries or millenniums of grinding ice . <p> But within a few years or decades , bacteria and lichen began to colonize the area .
