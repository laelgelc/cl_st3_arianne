<p> LONDON , Sept 18 ( Reuters ) - Britain 's opposition Labour Party leader Jeremy Corbyn promised on Wednesday that if he won power he would hold an in-out referendum on Brexit but repeatedly declined to say which way he would campaign . <p> " We are the only party that is offering the people a choice , " Corbyn told reporters , adding that there would be a credible choice between leaving on the terms of a Brexit deal he would have negotiated or whether to remain . <p> " The people of this country make the final decision , " he said . <p> " I 'm offering the people a choice , " Corbyn said when asked which way he would campaign .
