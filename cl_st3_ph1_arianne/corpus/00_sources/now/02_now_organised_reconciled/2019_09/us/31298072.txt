<h> Hundreds killed in Brazil 's Amazon over land , resources : HRW <p> Human Rights Watch finds that of 300 cases of killings in the Amazon in the last decade , only 14 were tried in court . <p> 17 Sep 2019 18:38 GMT <p> Protesters hold a Brazilian flag with the letters SOS written on it during a demonstration to demand more protection for the Amazon rainforest , in Sao Paulo , Brazil File : Nacho Doce/Reuters <p> A Human Rights Watch ( HRW ) report has found that more than 300 people have been killed over the past decade in conflicts over the use of land and resources in the Amazon , many by organised criminal networks profiting from illegal deforestation . <p> Of those cases , only 14 were tried in courts , the nonprofit said in the report based on 170 interviews . <p> " This really shows the level of impunity , " Cesar Munoz , a senior investigator at HRW told Reuters news agency on Tuesday on the sidelines of an event in Sao Paulo to discuss the report .
