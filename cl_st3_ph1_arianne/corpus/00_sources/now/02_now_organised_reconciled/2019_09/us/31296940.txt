<p> ANN ARBOR , MI -- Washtenaw County will play host to one of many rallies worldwide Friday as part of a " Global Climate Strike , " urging action on climate change by staging a youth walkout . <p> Starting at 11 a.m . Sept. 20 , Washtenaw 's strikers will gather at Ingalls Mall and Rackham Auditorium , 915 E. Washington St. on the University of Michigan campus . <p> Some local businesses and organizations have decided to close throughout the day in solidarity , while many more are closing between 11 a.m. and 1 p.m. , according to the event 's webpage . The page also displays a list of others that support the strike but " ca n't close . " <p> Some students or staff from 12 Ann Arbor-area grade schools are expected to join the demonstration , according to the website . More than 500 people have indicated they will attend on the event 's Facebook page .
