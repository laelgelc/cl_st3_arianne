<h> Jenny Fleming -Ives : Appreciates Gazette 's climate coverage <p> Published : 9/29/2019 9:00:11 AM <p> Twenty years ago when I was part of an interfaith environmental group in the Pioneer Valley working to raise citizen awareness of climate change , we were grateful for whatever small amount of media coverage we could get . <p> Imagine my deep gratitude to read in the Daily Hampshire Gazette on Monday , Sept. 15 , of your editorial decision to join Covering Climate Now , the global collaboration to strengthen coverage of the very real and pressing issue of climate change . <p> Then I read Tzivia Gover 's guest column on " Dreams " published in the same edition . So , here in our small city in western Massachusetts , you have made one of our dreams come true . Thank you .
