<p> ISTANBUL , Sept 25 ( Reuters ) - A bomb explosion ripped into a bus carrying Turkish police in the southern city of Adana on Wednesday morning and five people were slightly wounded including one police officer , the local governor 's office said . <p> CNN Turk broadcast video of a badly damaged bus surrounded by debris and other damaged vehicles under a pedestrian overpass in the Yuregir district of Adana , near Turkey 's Mediterranean coast . <p> The wounded were in good condition and an investigation has been launched into the blast , the Adana Governor 's office said in a statement on its website . <p> Security sources said ambulances took the wounded to hospitals in the area . Armed police sealed off the area and were examining the scene . It was not clear who might be behind the attack . <p> Kurdish , Islamist and leftist militants have all carried out bomb attacks in Turkey in the past .
