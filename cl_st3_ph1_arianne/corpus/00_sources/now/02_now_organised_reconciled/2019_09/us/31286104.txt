<h> Climate change protests hit Frankfurt as auto show opens <p> FRANKFURT , Germany -- Thousands of people are demonstrating in Frankfurt to demand more action against climate change as the German city 's auto show opens to the public , with some cycling into the city along highways that were temporarily closed for the occasion . <p> Demands raised by the organizers of Saturday 's protests include an end to the combustion engine , climate-neutral transport by 2035 , a speed limit on the autobahn and a strong German climate policy package . <p> Environmental groups say the trend toward bigger and more powerful cars , particularly SUVs , is eating into the fuel efficiency gains of recent decades . <p> Left : Several thousand cyclists ride on the A648 motorway in the direction of the Frankfurt Exhibition Centre to demonstrate against the International Motor Show ( IAA ) and for a rapid turnaround towards climate-friendly mobility . The cyclists had come to Frankfurt in a rally . Photo by Lennart Stock/picture alliance via Getty Images
