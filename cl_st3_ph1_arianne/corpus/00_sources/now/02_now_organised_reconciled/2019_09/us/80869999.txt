<p> GUATEMALA CITY , Sept 2 ( Reuters ) - A Guatemalan judge said that former presidential candidate Sandra Torres , who was detained at her home on Monday on charges of violating campaign finance rules , will be placed in custody to await trial . <p> Torres was arrested just hours before the departure of a U.N . anti-corruption commission whose investigations over 12 years shook the political class . <p> A former first lady , the 63-year-old Torres finished second to President-elect Alejandro Giammattei last month in her third attempt to win the office .
