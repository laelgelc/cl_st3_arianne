<p> ANN ARBOR -- A University of Michigan doctoral student who spoke at Climate Strike demonstrations at the University of Michigan on Friday called on the university and county prosecutors to drop charges against protesters who staged a sit-in in a UM building earlier this year . <p> Jonathan Morris was among the group of activists who demonstrated at the UM Administration Building after March 's Climate Strike rally . The sit-in continued three hours after the building 's closure before police arrested those remaining . Several protesters were arrested and eight people were charged with trespassing . Morris was not among them . <p> " Start treating climate change like the ... existential crisis it is , " Morris said . " You can arrest us , but we will continue to resist . " <p> Morris called for the charges against the protesters to be dropped , and for the university to do everything in its power to get them dropped . All eight charges are still pending , with a motion hearing scheduled for Oct. 10 , according to court records . <p> " This matter is in the hands of the county judicial system , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Michigan . " It 's really out of the university 's hands at this point . " <p> Morris said he does n't believe the university is powerless in this situation , and he suspects it is still aggressively pursuing the case , instead of stepping away . <p> The youth-led Climate Strike protests , which took place in March and again on Friday in Ann Arbor , coinciding with demonstrations around the world , advocated for UM to divest from fossil fuels and take steps toward carbon neutrality . <p> " If the university was doing those things , we would n't be there sitting in , " Morris said . " The optics speak for themselves . "
