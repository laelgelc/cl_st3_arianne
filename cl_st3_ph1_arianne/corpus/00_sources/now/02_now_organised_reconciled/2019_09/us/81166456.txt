<p> Winthrop 's College Democrats sponsored the event which started at 6 p.m. at the outdoor amphitheater . Doors opened at 5 p.m . <p> The event was free and open to the public on a first-come , first-served . Tickets were not required but an RSVP was advised . <p> Hundreds of people gathered on the campus as Sanders laid out his vision for the country . <p> Sanders talked about climate change , universal health care , raising the minimum wage , and wiping out student debt . He also talked about gun control . <p> " The Republican party and the president of the United States are frightened by the NRA . I wish I could tell you it was more complicated than that but it 's not . As president of the United States , I will not be intimidated by the NRA , " Sanders said . <p> The latest NBC News poll shows Sanders among the top three in the battle for the Democratic nomination , with former Vice President Joe Biden polling at 31% , Senator Elizabeth Warren at 25% , and Sanders at 14%.
