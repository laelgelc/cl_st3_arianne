<p> Students from Plattsburgh High School flooded the streets Friday , marching in stride with students from across the globe . They joining in a student-led climate change strike - skipping lessons in the hopes of teaching one . High school students , college students , and working adult joined together at SUNY Plattsburgh , showing their support for more action on climate change issues . People took turns sharing their concerns about climate change and their hopes for the future .
