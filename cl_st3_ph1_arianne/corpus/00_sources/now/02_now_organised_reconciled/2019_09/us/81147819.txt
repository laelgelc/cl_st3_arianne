<h> Virginia wants 2.5 GW of offshore wind to back green goals <p> September 18 ( Renewables Now ) - The US state of Virginia has voiced its offshore wind ambitions with the signing of an executive order that sets a goal for the development of up to 2.5 GW of offshore wind capacity by 2026 . <p> Executive Order 43 was signed on Tuesday by Governor Ralph Northam , setting a target for the state to supply 30% of its total power from renewables by 2030 . It will also aim to be producing 100% carbon-free electricity by 2050 . <p> Commonwealth agencies and executive branch institutions , meanwhile , will have to meet at least 30% of their electricity consumption with renewable energy sources by 2022 . <p> In order to achieve these goals , the state will need to ensure that it has at least 3 GW of solar and onshore wind projects under development by 2022 and that up to 2.5 GW of offshore wind projects are fully developed on an accelerated timeline by 2026 .
