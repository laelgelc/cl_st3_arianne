<h> Worried about climate change ? Here are some simple steps that can make a difference <p> GREEN BAY - Ace Manufacturing Industries gets a tenth of its electricity through solar energy . That works out to be " quite a bit " of electricity at an 88,000-square-foot industrial facility with a lot of machinery , said AMI 's president , Kelley Krouth . <p> But Krouth said the first step in AMI 's efforts to be energy-conscious was to reduce the energy it was already using . <p> First , in 2014 , the Green Bay manufacturer turned off all its equipment and measured its base electricity consumption -- the amount it uses when the machines are n't running -- at 100 kilowatts per hour . By converting to larger transformers and using relays to shut off washers and air filters when not in use , they were able to cut that down to 75 kilowatts per hour . <p> Buy Photo <p> Solar Panels at Ace Manufacturing Industries in Green Bay .
