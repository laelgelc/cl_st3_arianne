<h> Test Drive an Electric Vehicle at the Ventura National Drive Electric Week Event <p> VENTURA , Calif -- The County of Ventura is partnering with Ventura County Regional Energy Alliance to host the Ventura Electric Vehicle ( EV ) Ride and Drive event on September 17th . The public is invited to test drive a variety of electric vehicles , learn from industry experts and enjoy food and giveaways . This free event is part of National Drive Electric Week 's EV outreach activities staged in more than 300 communities across the country to highlight the clean-air benefits , cost-savings , and fun provided by driving an electric vehicle . <p> Chair of the Board of Supervisors , Steve Bennett , stated , " By increasing access to EVs , we are supporting the state 's goal of putting five million zero-emission cars on its roads by 2030 . In reducing the number of gasoline- and diesel-fueled vehicles on the road , we are reducing air pollution , protecting the health of our citizens , and helping reduce the impacts of climate change .
