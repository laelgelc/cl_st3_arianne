<h> Climate strike coming to SUNY Geneseo on Wednesday <p> Students at SUNY Geneseo will join the Global Climate Strike with a climate rally on Sept. 25 . <p> The event , scheduled from 2:30 to 3:30 p.m. in the space between the Integrated Science Center and the College Green , is a way for the students to demonstrate their support for climate action . <p> The event will serve as the formal action taken by the SUNY Geneseo student body to raise awareness for and demand action to combat the climate crisis , and has been recognized by the Global Climate Strike website as an official event , according to Harper Coleman , a senior at SUNY Geneseo and the primary organizer and host for the rally . <p> Several speakers , including both students and faculty members , are expected at the Geneseo event . Several campus organizations are also getting involved in the event , including Geneseo GEO , Geneseo Peace Action Coalition , and Geneseo Composting . <p> SUNY Geneseo will be photographing the event to help document how their campus body is rising up in the face of the climate change crisis , and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ community , Coleman said . <p> The Geneseo rally is among events taking place worldwide between Sept. 20 and 27 , a " Week of Action . " People in 150 countries have pledged to organize events during that week . <p> The strikes - similar events have taken place or are scheduled in Rochester - coincide with the United Nation 's 2019 Climate Action Summit , which began Saturday and runs through today in New York City . <p> The events are stressing the importance of community and the need for individuals to get involved on taking action on what is now called global climate disruption . <p> The biggest local event will likely be the Rochester Climate Strike , scheduled for 3:30 p.m . Sept. 27 outside Rochester 's City Hall , 30 Church St. , Rochester . That event is being organized by several Rochester-area organizations , including Metro Justice , the Rochester People 's Climate Coalitio , Hope Climate Movement and other youth climate groups , and SEIU 1199 .
