<p> MADRID , Sept 6 ( Reuters ) - Firefighters backed by over a dozen aircraft battled forest blazes raging in Spain 's northwestern region of Galicia since Thursday , cutting motorways and forcing the evacuation of several villages , regional authorities said on Friday . <p> Fanned by strong winds amid hot and dry weather , the wildfires have destroyed over 500 hectares of forest so far . <p> Regional authorities said two fires burning since Thursday in the municipalities of Barbantes and Monforte de Lemos have been stabilised but another two , in Fisterra and Porto do Son , remained active . <p> In October 2017 , Galicia suffered from a wave of deadly wildfires that were fanned by gusty , shifting winds and killed at least four people , at the same time as in neighboring Portugal 45 people died in similar blazes . <p> Last month , massive wildfires on the Spanish island of Gran Canaria left a path of destruction of 10,000 hectares , mostly in protected natural parks .
