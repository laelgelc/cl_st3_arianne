<h> Post <p> FERC Commissioner Rich Glick in his dissent to the CAISO FERC Order provides a prudent recommendation to help guide the energy transition : " the way to manage the transition to the electricity grid of the future is to identify clear , resource-neutral definitions of the services that the system operator will need to operate the grid safely , economically , and reliability and then put in place transparent , market-based solutions for procuring those services . " <p> Commissioner Glick is describing one of the key components of an Always on Capacity Exchange proposal , a market-based solution for procuring grid services , currently under consideration by NAESB for it 's 2020 Annual planning process in the Wholesale Electric Quadrant . A Priority Services Hierarchy ( PSH ) defines the type of services that may be bought/sold within a Capacity Exchange . These service offerings , by qualified supply resources , are placed into the Capacity Exchange as offers to " sell a service " indicating a delivery location , i.e. Central Mass , price and quantity for each type of service offering . Buyers , any qualified party with sufficient financial resources , may @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a corresponding bid that aligns with the offer , resulting in a " clearing of the bid/offer " at an agreed to location , price and duration ( commitment period ) . ISO 's issue capacity supply obligations to these established capacity commitments based on 1 , State Energy Targets for renewable energy and 2 the PSH , in sufficient quantities to meet reliability requirements . So , what does a PSH contain ? <p> The current strawman PSH identifies 12 unique service types , with more expected to be identified through a consensus-based standards development initiative .
