<p> On a calm and cloudy morning , rowers launched out into the glassy water at the Northwest Maritime Center beach in their wooden crafts for the yearly Wooden Boat Festival rowing race on Sept. 7 . <p> The rowing race of 23 boats included sculling singles , doubles , triples , quads and an oct , as well as a sweep eight , longboats , kayaks , wherries , dinghies , a gig and a paddle board . Several of the boats were historic wooden Pocock shells restored by Rat Island Rowing and Sculling Club . <p> Out in the Admiralty Inlet , the waters roughened , said racer Steve Chapin . <p> " It looks nice and calm , but around the corner there were big rollers , tide rips and a pretty fresh breeze , " he said . <p> Bows burying in the waves , racers fought to get back to the Maritime Center first , the air horn sounding out as each crossed the finish line .
