<h> Virginia governor sets renewable energy goal : 100% by 2050 <p> RICHMOND , Va . ( AP ) -- Gov . Ralph Northam has issued an executive order setting a goal for Virginia to produce 100% of its electricity from carbon-free sources by 2050 . <p> The order announced Tuesday says the goal will help address climate change , a challenge that " poses potentially devastating risk to Virginia . " <p> The order sets an intermediate goal of reaching 30% renewable energy by 2030 . It also says the Commonwealth 's agencies and executive branch institutions will aim to procure at least 30 percent of their electricity from renewable resources by 2022 . <p> Virginia 's GOP-controlled legislature has thwarted Northam 's attempts to limit carbon emissions from power plants . Control of both the House and Senate are up for grabs in November 's elections .
