<h> Jackie Wilson Kicks Off Her Campaign <p> Tuesday , September 24th 2019 , 8:20 PM EDT by Alex Bielfeld <p> ELMIRA , NY ( WENY ) -- Election season is right around the corner and one local resident kicked off her campaign for an Elmira City Council position this evening . Jackie Wilson , who has 40 years of experience as a teacher , counselor , and social worker kicked off her campaign today at the Ill Eagle Taproom in downtown Elmira . At today 's event , many local residents attended and raised questions to Jackie and they ranged from increasing the amount of bicycle lanes to issues on climate change . <p> We spoke with Jackie on why she has decided to run , " District five is where I live , I represent constituents on the south-side of the city . I 've lived here my whole life . I am very excited about the things that are happening in Elmira . I 'm a big cheerleader for Elmira , I think it 's a great place to live , and I just want to be a part of all these good things that are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have a lot of concerns , " said Jackie Wilson . <p> We also spoke with Jackie on why she chose the Ill Eagle Taproom to kick off her campaign . <p> Jackie said , " I just thought that this showcases what Elmira is doing , to really revitalize downtown , and this is a historic building . They have done so much to preserve the historical integrity . So I just though this would be the perfect place . " <p> She is eager for this years elections and is ready to get things going in her district .
