<h> There are 3 billion fewer birds in North America than there were in 1970 <p> North America has lost nearly three billion birds since 1970 , a new study says , which also found significant population declines among hundreds of bird species , including those once considered plentiful . USA TODAY <p> If you 've noticed fewer birds in your backyard than you used to , you 're not mistaken . <p> North America has lost nearly 3 billion birds since 1970 , a study said Thursday , which also found significant population declines among hundreds of bird species , including those once considered plentiful . <p> Overall , bird populations in the United States and Canada have declined by 29% in the past 50 years , according to the study , which authors say is a sign of a widespread ecological crisis . <p> " Multiple , independent lines of evidence show a massive reduction in the abundance of birds , " said study lead author Ken Rosenberg , a senior scientist at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and American Bird Conservancy , in a statement . Overall , the drop was from about 10 billion birds in 1970 to about 7 billion now . <p> The cause is primarily habitat loss , as birds are losing the places they need to live , find food , rest and raise their young . <p> Other threats to birds include deaths because of free-roaming cats , collisions with glass , toxic pesticides and insect decline . Climate change compounds all of these problems and also accelerates the loss of the habitats that birds need , experts say . <p> North America has lost almost 3 billion birds since 1970 .
