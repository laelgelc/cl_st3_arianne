They urged the independent body to order Member States to take action to protect children from the devastating impacts of climate change . <p> SOUNDBITE ( English ) Greta Thunberg , Climate Activist : <p> " What I ... is to raise awareness and to raise attention on this crisis and to actually make it clear that it was 30 years ago that you signed the Convention of the Rights of the Child and world leaders have failed to keep what they 've promised . " <p> The complaint was filed through the Third Optional Protocol to the Convention on the Rights of the Child , a voluntary mechanism which allows children or adults on their behalf to appeal directly to the United Nations for help if a country that has ratified the Protocol fails to provide a remedy for a rights violation . <p> SOUNDBITE ( English ) Greta Thunberg , Climate Activist : <p> " This @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that they are united behind the science and that they are going to take real action .
