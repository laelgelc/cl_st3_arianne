Created to increase the tree canopy , improve the environment , and enhance the quality of life in New Jersey 's largest city , the Renaissance Trees Program has planted 3,439 trees . <p> More than 21,128 square feet of concrete have been removed , improving stormwater management and promoting a healthier community forest . In the spring 2019 season , they planted 111 trees and removed 160 square feet of concrete . <p> Each year , hundreds of volunteers join forces with Newark residents and the New Jersey Tree Foundation to dig holes , plant trees , lay mulch , and have fun at tree planting events .
