<h> Video : Astronaut 's climate message <p> Astronauts on the International Space Station have a unique and incredible view of Earth . However , they also see its fragility and the impact we as humans have on the world around us . <p> In this message addressed to leaders at the UN Climate Summit in New York , ESA astronaut Luca Parmitano communicates the need to act now and work together to stop climate change and protect the future of our planet . The race is on , but it is a race we can win . The future is in our hands . <p> Luca was launched to the International Space Station for his second mission known as Beyond on 20 July 2019 . On 2 October he will become the third European and first ever Italian Space Station Commander . <p> This document is subject to copyright .
