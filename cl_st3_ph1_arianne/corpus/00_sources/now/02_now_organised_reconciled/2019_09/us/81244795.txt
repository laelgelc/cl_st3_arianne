Secretary-General Antonio Guterres will open the General Assembly proceedings with a " state of the world " speech . He 'll be followed immediately by the traditional first speaker -- Brazil , represented by its new president , Jair Bolsonaro -- and the United States , represented by President Donald Trump . <p> The United Nations , designed to promote a multilateral world , has struggled in the face of increasing unilateralism by the United States and other nations that favor going it alone over the brand of collaboration that the global body advocates . <p> The event unfolds against the backdrop of flaring tensions between Iran and Saudi Arabia , backed by its longtime supporter , the United States .
