<h> WeatherMinds : Choosing trees that adapt to climate change <p> GOLDEN VALLEY , Minn. -- When considering what kind of trees to plant in your yard , or choosing what trees will stay after an outdoor make-over , climate should be one of the factors you consider . <p> According to the Minnesota DNR Forestry Service , climate change will be the most significant threat to Minnesota forests over the next century , so planting a tree that will weather the Minnesota weather is crucial ! <p> Trees that are less resistant to changing climate : <p> Aspen <p> Paper Birch <p> Tamarack <p> Black Spruce <p> These are four varieties you may want to avoid , as they will be less resistant to higher rainfall and warmer temperatures . <p> Trees more resistant to changing climate : <p> American Basswood <p> Black Cherry <p> Northern Red Oak <p> Burr Oak <p> Sugar Maple <p> Red Maple <p> Eastern White Pine <p> These trees will be the best bet for a healthy tree that will live for generations to come .
