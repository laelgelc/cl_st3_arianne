<h> Another Climate Strike Set For Friday Morning In Buckhead <h> Police are expecting a large gathering for the Southeast Climate Strike and Rebellion outside the Buckhead MARTA station . <h> Sep 26 , 2019 9:57 am ET Updated Sep 26 , 2019 9:58 am ET <p> A rally was held in New York City to protest climate change . ( Sydney Pereria/Patch ) <p> ATLANTA -- Police are expecting a large presence at a Friday morning climate strike at the Buckhead MARTA station . Last Friday , youths from around the world and across the United States staged a strike to demand an end to fossil fuels and reductions in corporate pollution and environmental destruction . While there were several events in Atlanta that day , another rally , this one called the Southeast Climate Strike and Rebellion , is set for Sept. 29 beginning at 9:30 am , and police said the gathering may impact local traffic . <p> The international protests came before the United Nations Climate Action Summit , which happened earlier this week . Leaders from government , business , and civil society addressed the global climate emergency . @
