Houston is flooding , " he said . " We have 11 years to act . It 's not too late . " <p> The local strikers are demanding a Town-Gown Climate Accord ; an agreement between the University of Iowa and the City of Iowa City to prevent a climate catastrophe . Specifically , they want a commitment by the institutions to achieve 100% renewable energy by 2030 and the immediate end of coal burning at the university 's power plant . <p> United Nations scientists say that unless " unprecedented changes in all aspects of society " are made by 2030 , the planet will experience disastrous impacts due to warming temperatures . <h> Interested in this topic ?
