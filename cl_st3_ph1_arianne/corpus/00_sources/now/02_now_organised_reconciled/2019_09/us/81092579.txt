<h> The case for backup generators <p> The lack of permanent backup generators on water agencies ' storage tank sites contributed to the disastrous destruction caused by the Thomas Fire . Each water agency in the state should be mandated by law to have permanent emergency backup generators at all of their water storage sites . <p> Presently some water agencies have portable generators that are moved from site to site on an " as-needed basis . " And some water agencies only have one portable generator even though they have multiple storage sites . In today 's world of climate change and wild fires being more prevalent , this is no longer the acceptable practice .
