<h> Climate Hot Zones Are Already Here <p> A 2-degree Centigrade change in temperature has already happened in some places . <p> by Eric Holdeman / September 17 , 2019 <p> Two degrees centigrade is supposed to be the point of no return for major climate impacts to all forms of plants , animals and humans inhabiting this place we call Earth . In this Washington Post article , Dangerous new hot zones are spreading around the world , they call out the fact that the 2 degree Rubicon has been passed in some parts of the world already . <p> Based on my previous reading , having the frozen tundra warm is a worst-case situation since there is an abundance of carbon trapped in the frozen ground . When it thaws , there will be a massive release of carbon that can hyper-feed the rate of climate change throughout the world . I guess there will be those who point to that event as being naturally occurring and not connected to humans .
