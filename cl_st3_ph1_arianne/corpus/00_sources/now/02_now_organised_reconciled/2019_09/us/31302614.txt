<h> Youth leaders at UN demand bold climate change action <p> UNITED NATIONS -- Fresh off the climate strike that took hundreds of thousands of young people out of classrooms and into the streets globally , youth leaders gathered at the United Nations Saturday to demand radical moves to fight climate change . <p> " We showed that we are united and that we , young people , are unstoppable , " Swedish 16-year-old activist Greta Thunberg , who started the climate strike movement with her lone protest in front of her country 's parliament about a year and a half ago . <p> More than 700 mostly young activists attended the first of its kind Youth Climate Summit , according to Luis Alfonso de Alba , the U.N . special climate summit envoy . <p> Friday 's strike across six continents and Saturday 's youth conference presage a full-on climate conference next week at the U.N . General Assembly , which has placed the issue of climate change at front and center as world leaders gather for the annual meeting . <p> Activists at Saturday 's gathering demanded money for a fund to help poorer nations adapt to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ insisted that the world should wean itself quickly from coal , oil and gas that cause climate change . <p> Fiji climate activist Komal Karishma Kumar said global warming is not just taking a toll on the planet but on her generation , especially people from vulnerable places like her Pacific island nation . <p> " Young people from different parts of the world are living in constant fear and climate anxiety , fearing the future , the uncertainty of a healthy life or a life for their children at all , " Kumar said . <p> She added : " I do not want our future generations to submerge with our sinking islands .
