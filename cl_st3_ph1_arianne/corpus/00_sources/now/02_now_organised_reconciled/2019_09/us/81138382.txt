<h> Artist turns Austrian football stadium into a forest <p> Architecture &amp; Design <h> An estimated 300 trees have been replanted in a 30,000-seat stadium to draw attention to climate change and deforestation <p> Spotted : Swiss artist Klaus Littmann has temporarily turned an Austrian football stadium into a European forest . The exhibition is the largest public art installation to be held in the country , according to the organisers . <p> The installation is called For Forest : The Unending Attraction of Nature , designed by Klaus Littmann . The work , based on the ideal image of a European mixed forest , is an interaction with a drawing by Austrian artist Max Peintner . The work covers 7,000 square meters using 299 specially selected trees . The forest floor was designed by Enzo Enea and his team incorporating various plant species , such as shrubs and woody plants , to authentically create the image of a forest . <p> The aim of the exhibit is to remind people about the dangers of deforestation and climate change . The trees have been used to create a temporary forest in the W ? rthersee Stadium in Klagenfurt , Austria . <p> Access to the temporary forest is free . The exhibit is set to run until 27 October . The trees will be replanted in a nearby plot . This permanent display will include an education centre where people can learn about deforestation . <h> Takeaway : <p> The exhibit coincides with growing international concern over deforestation in the Amazon and elsewhere . National Geographic has reported that 70 per cent of the Earth 's animals and plants live in forests . Many can not survive the deforestation process . While there is concern over deforestation and climate change , average people struggle to relate to these problems . Exhibits like For Forest have the potential to present the issue in a way that is accessible and educational .
