<p> JUBA , Sept 9 ( Reuters ) - Former South Sudanese rebel leader Riek Machar is due to make a rare visit to the capital Juba on Monday and meet President Salva Kiir , officials said on Monday , raising hopes for progress in a stalled peace process . <p> The two men signed a pact a year ago to end a civil war that has killed hundreds of thousands of people , displaced a third of the population and wrecked the economy . <p> But the roll-out of the accord , which called for a unity government , has been delayed because the government says it does not have enough money to fund disarmament and the integration of all the armed factions . <p> " The meeting aims at discussing the outstanding issues related to the implementation of the R-ARCSS ( peace deal ) with President Kiir and other head of the parties to the agreement , " Machar 's director for information , Puok Both Baluang , said . <p> " It will be a two-day visit " , he added . <p> South Sudan split away from Sudan in 2011 after decades of war but @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after Kiir sacked Machar as vice president . <p> Troops loyal to both men clashed in the capital that December and ethnically charged fighting soon spread across the state , shutting down oil fields , forcing millions to flee and killing hundreds of thousands of people . <p> Both sides signed a peace deal in September 2018 , under pressure from international and regional powers . Machar who lives in Khartoum , has since returned to Juba just once , in October that year , to celebrate the peace deal . <p> A key provision of the peace deal is integration of former rebels in the army , which has still not occurred .
