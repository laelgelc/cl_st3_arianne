<h> Letter : Trump can get lesson in climate change from Clemson University Experimental Forest <p> President Trump was in the news regarding his decision to roll back federal air-quality emission standards for automobiles , and to strip California 's authority to set stricter standards than the rest of the country . On the same day I toured the Clemson University Experimental Forest in Pickens County . <p> The forest is a healthy , ecologically diverse natural area used for recreation , research , hunting , and logging , but it had been destroyed by intensive cotton farming nine decades ago . It was hard not to make a comparison between the wisdom of the federal government back then , when the feds took action to deal with land destroyed by cotton farming , and what is being proposed today to deal with air pollution .
