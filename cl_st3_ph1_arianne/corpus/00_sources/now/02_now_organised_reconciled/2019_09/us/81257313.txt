<h> International climate change protests deserved better play <p> The Sept. 21 front page featuring the reopening of Archie 's Hough Bakery ( " Sweet tradition gets a new life " ) is an example of how The Plain Dealer 's recent change to highlighting local news can be misguided . The Archie 's article totally overshadowed a smaller front-page article about the international and local rallies protesting inaction on climate change by world leaders ( " Cleveland joins the globe in a climate rally " ) . <p> While I assume many Clevelanders welcome Archie 's reopening , which will certainly not help with the national obesity epidemic , it pales in importance to concerns about how climate change imperils the future of our planet . We depend on our media to enlighten the public and not just play to our pleasure-seeking . The international climate change actions deserved more prominence .
