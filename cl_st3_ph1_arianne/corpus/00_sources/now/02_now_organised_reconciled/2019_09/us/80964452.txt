<h> UPDATE : Police investigating 2 Sunday morning shootings in South Bend <p> Posted : Mon 7:13 AM , Sep 09 , 2019 <p> SOUTH BEND , Ind . ( WNDU ) - South Bend Police are investigating two separate shootings that occurred Sunday morning . The two shootings are not believed to be related . <p> Police say the first shooting happened around 1:24 a.m. when officers responded to a ShotSpotter Activation in the 1700 block of N. Huey . A 25-year-old male arrived at the hospital about 10 minutes later with apparent gunshot wounds to the leg . He was treated and released . <p> Meanwhile , officers responded to another ShotSpotter Activation around 6:05 a.m. in the area of Kenwood and Fremont . Police say a 30-year-old male had a non-life-threatening injury to his shoulder . He is expected to be okay . <p> Police say several occupied homes in the area were hit by gunfire .
