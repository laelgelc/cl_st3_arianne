Pope Francis will visit venues across Leyte and Manila during his visit to the Philippines from January 15 - 19 . The visit is expected to attract crowds in the millions as Filipino Catholics flock to catch a glimpse of the leader of the Catholic Church in the Philippines for the first time since 1995 . The Pope will begin the tour in Manila , then travelling to Tacloban to visit areas devastated by Typhoon Haiyan before returning to Manila to hold a mass at Rizal Park . The Philippines is the only Catholic majority nation in Asia with around 90 percent of the population professing the faith .
