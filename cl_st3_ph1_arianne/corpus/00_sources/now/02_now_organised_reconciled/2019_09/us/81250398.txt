<h> PlayStation 5 aims to be vastly more power efficient than PS4 <p> Sony has revealed that the next-generation PlayStation 5 will be vastly more power efficient than this gen 's PS4 . <p> Revealed through a post on the PlayStation Blog , the upcoming next-gen console is promising to consume less power during suspended games . <p> Revealed by Sony Interactive Entertainment boss Jim Ryan , the new PS5 is aiming to be an eco-friendly machine . With the company joining forces with the United Nations to Combat Climate Change , PlayStation is aiming to be a more environmental console . <p> " I am also very pleased to announce the next generation PlayStation console will include the possibility to suspend gameplay with much lower power consumption than PS4 ( which we estimate can be achieved at around 0.5 W ) , " Ryan announced . <p> " If just one million users enable this feature , it would save equivalent to the average electricity use of 1,000 US homes .
