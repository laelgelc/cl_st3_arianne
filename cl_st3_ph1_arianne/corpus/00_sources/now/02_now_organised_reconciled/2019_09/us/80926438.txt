<h> Climate Hope in Unexpected Places <p> Finding hope in unexpected places in the battle to address the climate crisis will be the subject when the Climate Change Coalition of Door County hosts a program featuring videos from Project Drawdown 's Katharine Wilkinson on Sept. 16 , 7 pm , at the Kress Pavilion in Egg Harbor . Free-will contributions to support the coalition 's work will be appreciated . <p> The videos highlight two strategies that scientists have identified to protect our planet from irreversible warming . In the first , Wilkinson explores the crucial links between gender equity , educating girls and the climate . In the second , she delves into the many ways in which food waste contributes to global warming and what we can do . Discussion will follow each video . Roy Thilly will be the facilitator.
