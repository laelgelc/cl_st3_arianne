Dianne Feinstein have offices , and marched 1.1 miles down Market Street , past the offices of Bank of America and PG&amp;E , before ending at Embarcadero Plaza near the Ferry Building . <p> Chanting and banging drums , the crowd , largely made up of young people , held signs saying " Grown-ups do something , " " There is no Planet B " and " Governor Newsom , stand up to big oil . The 10 hottest years over that period all have occurred since 1998 , according to NASA and NOAA . <p> Record heat waves have gripped Europe this summer . Hurricane Dorian battered the Bahamas and the East Coast , its category 5 peak strength driven in part by warmer-than-normal water temperatures . Large fires have charred millions of acres across Alaska and Siberia in recent months .
