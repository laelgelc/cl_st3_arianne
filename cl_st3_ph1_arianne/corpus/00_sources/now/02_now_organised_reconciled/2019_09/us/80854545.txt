<p> AMSTERDAM , Sept 2 ( Reuters ) - More than 60 flights were cancelled or delayed at Amsterdam 's Schiphol Airport on Monday due to a strike by ground staff at KLM airline and more action is planned for later this week . <p> The two-hour work stoppage from 0600 GMT to 0800 GMT was intended to influence ongoing negotiations between trade unions and KLM , the Dutch subsidiary of Air France-KLM , on pay and working conditions . <p> But trade union association FNV said the airline had not responded and that a second strike was planned at the same time on Wednesday . <p> " We heard nothing from KLM , " FNV 's airline industry head Jan van den Brink said in a statement . " Therefore we will press ahead with more strike action . " <p> Talks between the parties over a new collective bargaining agreement began in May , with unions demanding a salary increase of 8% over two years , as well as more full time employees and better working hours . <p> On Thursday KLM offered a 6-7% pay rise over three years , which unions rejected . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ well as Delta and other " SkyTeam " allied airlines .
