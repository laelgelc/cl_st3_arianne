It also plans to have 100% of its energy use come from solar panels and other renewable energy by 2030 . That 's up from 40% today . <p> " We 've been in the middle of the herd on this issue and we want to move to the forefront , " said Amazon CEO and founder Jeff Bezos , who announced the initiatives at an event in Washington . <p> Amazon said it emitted 44.4 million metric tons of carbon dioxide last year , a number that comes close to pollution rates of some small nations . <p> " Its greenhouse gas emissions are about 85% of the emissions of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ professor at the Research Institute for Environment , Energy and Economics at Appalachian State University . <p> Amazon 's employees have pressured the company to do more to combat climate change .
