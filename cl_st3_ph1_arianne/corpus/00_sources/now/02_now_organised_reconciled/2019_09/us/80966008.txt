<h> Maryland county taking steps to protect bees <p> Associated Press <p> Sep 08 , 2019 12:00 PM <p> BALTIMORE <p> One Maryland county is taking new steps to help protect bees . <p> TV station WBAL reports Howard County is joining the Bee City USA Coalition . As part of that , the county is committing to reducing pesticide use and has created 55 acres of pollinator-friendly meadow habitat on county parkland . <p> Advertisement <p> County Executive Calvin Ball says Howard County is one of the first counties in the nation to join the coalition . <p> Bees , which pollinate billions of dollars worth of crops , are critical to the human food supply , but their populations have been declining . Scientists blame a variety of factors including pesticides , parasites , disease and climate change . <p> Advertisement <p> <p> Information from : WBAL-TV , http : //www.wbaltv.com <p> Copyright 2019 The Associated Press .
