Intriguingly , this interval is also marked by perturbations to the global carbon cycle , as evidenced by extreme fluctuations in climate and carbon isotopes . The Neoproterozoic isotope record has defied parsimonious explanation because sustained 12C-enrichment ( low d13C ) in seawater seems to imply that substantially more oxygen was consumed by organic carbon oxidation than could possibly have been available . We propose a solution to this problem , in which carbon and oxygen cycles can maintain dynamic equilibrium during negative d13C excursions when surplus oxidant is generated through bacterial reduction of sulfate that originates from evaporite weathering . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ feedback loop whereby net oxidation of marine organic carbon can sustain greenhouse forcing of chemical weathering , nutrient input and ocean margin euxinia . Our proposed framework is particularly applicable to the late Ediacaran ' Shuram ' isotope excursion that directly preceded the emergence of energetic metazoan metabolisms during the Ediacaran -- Cambrian transition .
