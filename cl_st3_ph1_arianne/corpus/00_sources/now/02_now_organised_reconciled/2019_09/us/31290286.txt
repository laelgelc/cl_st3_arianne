It 's nearly a sea of its own , extending from Cape Cod in Massachusetts to Cape Sable at the southern tip of Nova Scotia , including the Bay of Fundy , and the coast of Maine , its connections with the open Atlantic to the south nearly blocked by two shallow plateaus , Georges Bank and Browns Bank . <p> Two narrow channels provide the only deep-water connections into the gulf .
