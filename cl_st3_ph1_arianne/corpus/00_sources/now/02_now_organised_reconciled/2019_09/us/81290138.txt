Using a 200 d in situ carbon dioxide enrichment study on Heron Island , Australia , we simulated future ocean acidification conditions , and found reduced pH led to a drastic decline in net calcification of living corals to no net growth , and accelerated disintegration of dead corals . Net calcification declined more severely than in previous studies due to exposure to the natural community of bioeroding organisms in this in situ study and to a longer experimental duration .
