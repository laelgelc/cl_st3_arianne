<p> WASHINGTON , Sept 25 ( Reuters ) - The top U.S. spy official threatened to resign over concerns the White House might press him to withhold information from Congress in scheduled testimony on Thursday about a whistleblower complaint about President Donald Trump , the Washington Post reported on Wednesday . <p> Citing unnamed current and former U.S. officials , the Post said acting Director of National Intelligence Joseph Maguire told the White House he was not willing to stonewall Congress . <p> It said the move was partly aimed at forcing the White House to make an explicit legal decision on whether it was going to assert executive privilege over the whistleblower complaint , which Maguire has so far withheld from Congress .
