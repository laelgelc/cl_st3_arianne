<h> Exelon , NY nuke plant owner , invests $20M in sustainability <p> ALBANY , N.Y . ( AP ) -- The company that runs three upstate nuclear plants is launching a $20 million initiative to boost clean energy start-ups . <p> Chicago-based Exelon Corp . and its associated Exelon Foundation plan to detail its investment on Wednesday at a clean energy forum during Climate Week NYC , an annual summit on climate change . <p> Entrepreneurs and early-stage companies can apply for a piece of the $20 million . Projects must involve efforts to reduce carbon emissions , boost local climate resiliency , or increase sustainability in a city or state . The company plans to focus the funding on projects that would serve its major customer markets . <p> Exelon operates the James Fitzpatrick plant and Nine Mile Point plant in Oswego County as well as the Ginna plant in Wayne County . It also operates plants in Illinois , Pennsylvania and Maryland . <p> . <p> This story has been updated to correct that Exelon operates a nuclear plant in Maryland instead of Delaware . <p> ( Copyright 2019 The Associated Press .
