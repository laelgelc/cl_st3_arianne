" <p> " If there is something we are not lacking in this world , it 's money , " she added . " Of course , many people do lack money , but governments and these people in power , they do not lack money . And also we need to have the polluters ... actually pay for the damage they have caused . So , to that argument , I would not even respond to that argument , because it has been said so many times , the money is there . What we lack now is political will and social will to do it .
