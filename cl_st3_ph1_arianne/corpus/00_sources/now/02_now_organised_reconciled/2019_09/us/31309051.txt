<h> Thousands gather on UM campus for youth-led Global Climate Strike <p> ANN ARBOR , MI -- Hundreds of signs were held aloft in a crowd of thousands as the young , old and somewhere-in-between gathered at Ingalls Mall on the University of Michigan campus Friday , Sept. 20 to take part in a " Global Climate Strike , " joining communities across the world . <p> Students were encouraged to lead the way in the demonstrations , and Ann Arbor Public Schools allowed excused absences with parental permission to attend the rally for action on climate change . Ann Arbor city workers were also encouraged to attend . <p> Several speakers and entertainers took the stage in front of Rackham Auditorium , among them poets , a band and activists . <p> Elijah Hatcher-Kay , a 15-year-old climate activist and freshman at Pioneer High School , was among the first speakers . Hatcher-Kay spoke passionately about demonstrating , arguing that calls for action are otherwise ignored . <p> " ' Business as usual ' takes one timid step towards justice and steps back into inequality , " Hatcher-Kay said . They were among many students to leave classes for the rally , and were supervised by teachers from their school . <p> " All of our seventh and eighth grade classes came , " Rosenberg said . <p> Some of the boys cited Greta Thunberg , the 16-year-old climate activist who has drawn international attention for her protests , as an inspiration for attending . The boys said they were worried about their futures if climate change continues unchecked . <p> " It 's really important that everyone 's aware of this issue , " Rosenberg said .
