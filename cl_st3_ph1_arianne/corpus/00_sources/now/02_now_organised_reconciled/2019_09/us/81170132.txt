<h> Amazon Pledges To Meet Paris Climate Goals 10 Years Early <p> Amazon chief Jeff Bezos pledged Thursday the US corporate giant will become carbon neutral by 2040 and would encourage other firms to do the same , in a bid to help meet the goals of the Paris climate accord 10 years early . <p> " We want to use our scope and our scale to lead the way , " Bezos -- the world 's richest man -- told a news conference in the US capital . <p> Amazon announced its " Climate Pledge " initiative and said it would be its first signatory as part of an effort to reduce emissions in line with a 2050 goal for carbon neutrality set by the Paris agreement . <p> As part of the ramped-up pledge , Bezos said Amazon had agreed to purchase 100,000 electric delivery vans from vehicle maker Rivian , to help cut its carbon footprint . <p> The first will hit the road in 2021 , with the fleet to be fully operational in 2030 .
