Many in attendance were dressed in formal , black funeral attire . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> CNN reports the Pizol glacier in the Glarus Alps has lost roughly 80 to 90 percent of its volume since 2006 , according to Matthias Huss , a glacier specialist at ETH Zurich university . <p> Huss told the news outlet there are only 26,000 square meters of ice left in the glacier and it will be the first glacier in the country to be taken off the Swiss glacier surveillance network . <p> " Pizol glacier has disappeared . " If Pizol goes , this is a warning sign . This is what is going to happen if we do n't change something about our behavior .
