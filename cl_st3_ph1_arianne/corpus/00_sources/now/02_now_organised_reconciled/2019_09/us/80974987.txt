<h> Letter : Climate change should be a top issue <p> Published : 9/9/2019 12:01:17 AM <p> In the same day 's mail , I had a donation-solicitation letter from the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee ( DCCC ) and a new copy of Rolling Stone magazine . Where the DCCC communication is weak on the topic of climate change , the Rolling Stone article by Jeff Goodell , " Can We Survive Extreme Heat ? " ( found through Google , too ) , is strong . <p> My DCCC form gave a list of issues to check off as a " Yes , I agree " with zero mention of climate change . I wrote it in at " other . " On the envelope itself , I used a Sharpie and wrote " climate change " and highlighted it with pink and green . <p> The article by Goodell raises the issue of climate change 's fuzzily discussed repercussions -- heat deaths of humans for instance , happening already on the worst days in Arizona . Predicted for New York City , Miami and Los Angeles with 5.4 degrees of warming increase : thousands of deaths . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change among their issues . Perhaps others who get the DCCC envelope and its defective list of concerns could do what I did -- include climate change in " other " and also write it on the envelope . <p> I love my grandchildren and their peers too much to just let it pass : A preventable tragedy in their futures must activate my citizen powers now .
