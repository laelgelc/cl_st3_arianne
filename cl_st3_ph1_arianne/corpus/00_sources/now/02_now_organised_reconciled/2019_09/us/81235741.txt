<p> Greece 's Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis will make a presentation today , Monday , at the UN Special Climate Conference concerning the risks posed by climate change to world cultural heritage , at the level of heads of state and government within 2020 . <p> Mitsotakis who has been in New York since yesterday , is expected to point out that climate change affects us all , adding that any action for limiting its effect needs work distribution , coordination and funds . <p> The Greek President is expected to stress that Greece has taken clear initiatives to protect cultural heritage from the effects of climate change , including erosion of monuments . He will also refer to initiatives taken -- with the support of UNESCO and the World Meteorological Organization -- to tackle the risks posed by climate change to the world cultural heritage . <p> Additionally , he will also talk about Greece 's actions to boost renewable energy , waste management while stressing the need for a green economy model .
