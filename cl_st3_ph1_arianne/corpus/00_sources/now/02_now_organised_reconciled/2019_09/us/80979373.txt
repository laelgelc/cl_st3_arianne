<p> OSLO , Sept 8 ( Reuters ) - Equinor will clean up the onshore oil spill discovered this week at its Bahamas storage terminal in the aftermath of hurricane Dorian , the Norwegian energy company said on Sunday . <p> In preparation for the hurricane , Equinor shut down operations at the South Riding Point terminal on Aug. 31 and none of its staff were at the site during the storm . <p> " Based on current visual assessments , there are no indications of continued oil leakage from the tanks or of oil spills from the terminal to sea or beaches , " Equinor 's statement said . <p> " Further examination is ongoing to assess the full impact of the spill . " <p> The company 's website says the terminal on Grand Bahama is a key businesses intended to strengthen the company 's market and trading position in the Caribbean region . <p> " The situation is complex and challenging , with damage to infrastructure hampering progress in relief and response efforts , " Equinor said , adding that it has yet to determine the size of the spill .
