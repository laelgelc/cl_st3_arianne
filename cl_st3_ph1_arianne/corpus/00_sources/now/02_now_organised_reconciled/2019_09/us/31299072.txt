<h> Galloway : A few tips for Canada 's federal leaders in this election campaign <p> A little more humility from Trudeau , a little more clarity from Scheer , better targeting from Singh and more policy bandwidth from May ... clear goal-setting and strategy discipline will be appreciated by voters when they make their final choices Oct. 21 . <p> At this point , only two leaders appear to have a shot at becoming prime minister , but voting day is a long way off . They all need to hone their goals and strategies for achieving those goals . Gavin Young / Calgary Herald <p> It is important for political leaders to have overarching goals that remain in place even as the campaign ground shifts beneath their feet . <p> I have never been a political strategist . But , as a journalist , I have been on multiple media buses to witness campaigns implode , and I have seen politicians gain traction even when it seemed at the outset there was none to be found . <p> So here is the goal-setting advice I would humbly offer the leaders of the four main national parties as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Justin Trudeau : Show some humility . <p> The persona you sold to voters in 2015 was that of a decent guy who wanted to do politics differently , who had compassion for people and who was willing to make big and difficult promises -- assisted dying legislation , climate change mitigation , marijuana legalization , help for Indigenous people -- because you believed they were the right thing to do . <p> Green Party leader Elizabeth May pretends to shake hands at the empty mic where Liberal leader Justin Trudeau would have been if he had not declined to participate in Thursday 's debate . Trudeau needs to show a bit more humility in this campaign.POOL / REUTERS <p> That image was tarnished during your four years in government . Some of the decline is the natural result of being in power . Some of it is your fault . Take the blame , admit where you went wrong , state publicly that you have learned from your mistakes . <p> You are right to believe you still have star power . But a little bit more of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not hurt . <p> There are Canadians whose guts roil at the mention of your name . They were never going to vote for you anyway . Your job is to convince the other voters -- left , right and centre -- who gave you the benefit of the doubt four years ago that they were not wrong . <p> Those efforts will be squandered if you make a significant error . So do n't go off script . But write some self-deprecation into your messaging . <h> Related <p> To Andrew Scheer : Sell yourself and your policies with force and conviction . <p> You are not yet a well-defined figure in the minds of many Canadians . You need to get known while walking the most difficult line in this election . <p> Soft-centrist voters who would consider casting a Conservative ballot are not looking for a prime minister in the dictatorial mould of Stephen Harper . Yet , you can not afford to be mushy . When you refuse to give direct answers on issues like abortion and climate change that the Liberals will raise to paint @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for reporters and columnists to exploit . <p> You must be direct and honest when asked what you think about difficult issues . But you must also make it clear that , as a leader , you would act in the interests of all Canadians and not just of those who share your personal belief systems . <p> Your chances of winning will be amplified when the Liberals and Trudeau trip over their own feet . Pounce when that happens , as you did this week with the new developments in the SNC-Lavalin affair . But do n't just be the scowling opposition leader poking up at the foibles of Justin Trudeau . Demonstrate leadership qualities of your own . <p> To Jagmeet Singh : No , you are not fighting this election to beat Justin Trudeau . You are fighting to keep party status in the House of Commons and the New Democrats together as a political entity . <p> Stop vying for the centre . Leave that battle for the next election when you have figured out how to put more money in the coffers , and when @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ writ is dropped . <p> Become , once again , the left-wing populist party that will get your core constituency to the polls . Invoke the names of Tommy Douglas and Jack Layton . Save the seats that are naturally yours and stop worrying about the rest . <p> To Elizabeth May : Concentrate your efforts in the eight or so ridings that you can possibly win . Anything more than two seats in Parliament would be a huge victory and would put you in a great spot for 2023 . <p> That means waging an aggressive ground campaign in those constituencies . Lots of signs . Lots of door-to-door visits by you . <p> And yes , you have the high ground on the climate change issue . But voters need to know more than that about you .
