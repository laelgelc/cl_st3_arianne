<h> Big money urges governments to tackle climate change <p> A group of more than 500 major institutional investors is urging governments to do more to tackle climate change , ahead of a U.N . summit on the issue next week . <p> The banks , insurance companies and pension funds warned Thursday that current national commitments wo n't meet the goal of the 2015 Paris climate accord . <p> Signatories of the Paris pact committed to keeping global warming well below 2 degrees Celsius ( 3.6 Fahrenheit ) , ideally aiming for an increase of no more than 1.5C ( 2.7F ) by the century 's end compared with pre-industrial times . <p> The investors , who say they manage $35 trillion in assets , said failure to meet the Paris goals would lead to an " unacceptably high temperature increase that would cause substantial negative economic impacts .
