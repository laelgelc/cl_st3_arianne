<h> The Best Signs From The Climate Change Rallies Around The World <p> Activists are getting their messages across with some funny , angry and wittily worded placards . <p> By Lee Moran and Amy Russo <p> Millions of protesters are taking to the streets of thousands of cities and towns around the world on Friday to demand urgent action on climate change . <p> And many ? from Australia to America , Germany to Kenya to India ? are coming armed with wittily worded signs . Some placards take aim at political figures , including U.S. President Donald Trump . Many lament the current climate crisis .
