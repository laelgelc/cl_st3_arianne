<h> InFocus <h> Climate Change Threatens Somaliland With Disaster <p> The self-declared republic of Somaliland is one of the world 's most vulnerable places to climate change . Poor and drought-hit , and without legal status as a country , it is struggling to adapt for the future . Researchers say that average daily maximum temperatures have risen by about a degree over the last 30 or so years , to about 34 degrees Celsius ( 93 Fahrenheit ) .
