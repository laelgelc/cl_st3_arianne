<h> Germany reports landmark case of West Nile virus <p> Authorities are reporting the first known human case of West Nile virus transmitted by mosquitoes in Germany , a development apparently hastened by climate change . <p> The national disease control and tropical medicine centers said Friday the person developed encephalitis but recovered after hospital treatment . <p> The virus originated in Africa but has been spread northward by migratory birds and mosquitoes . There have been several outbreaks in southern and central Europe in recent years . It is found in much of the U.S. <p> Jonas Schmidt-Chanasit of Germany 's Bernhard Nocht Institute tropical medicine center said that " the unusually warm summers of the last two years , triggered by climate change , apparently have contributed to WNV establishing itself north of the Alps .
