<h> China is Committed to Multilateralism <p> In China 's view , the failure of existing international institutions to adjust to the growing clout of emerging and developing economies undermines the legitimacy of the entire multilateral system . But , far from trying to upend that system , China is working to strengthen it . <p> BEIJING -- In recent years , China 's lead role in establishing new multilateral institutions -- including the Shanghai Cooperation Organization , the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank ( AIIB ) , and the New Development Bank -- has raised fears that the government aims to topple the existing world order . This interpretation misses a crucial point : China has benefited immensely from and continues to participate actively in -- and even ardently defend -- that very order . <p> China had no say in the formulation of today 's prevailing multilateral rules and structures , but it has generally adhered to them .
