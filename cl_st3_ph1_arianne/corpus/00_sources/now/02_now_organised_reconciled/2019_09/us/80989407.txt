<h> Experts say adapting to climate change can pay off manifold <p> BERLIN ( AP ) -- A group of leaders from business , politics and science says a massive investment in adapting to climate change over the next decade would reap significant returns , as countries avoid catastrophic losses and boost their economies . <p> The Global Commission on Adaptation , made up of dozens of prominent figures including Bill Gates and former U.N . chief Ban Ki-moon , released a report Tuesday urging governments and businesses to tackle the consequences of climate change , in addition to trying to curb it . <p> The report proposes investing $1.9 trillion between 2020 and 2030 in areas such as early warning systems , infrastructure that can withstand rising sea levels and extreme weather , and boosting agriculture to cope with droughts . <p> The experts cited examples from the Netherlands , Bangladesh and London that they said had helped save lives or protect valuable land .
