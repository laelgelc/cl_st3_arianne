<p> IOWA CITY , Ia. -- Soon after Iowa 's university students returned to campus this fall , U.S. Sen. Bernie Sanders was there . <p> Sanders , of Vermont , is the first Democratic presidential candidate to swing through Iowa State University , the University of Iowa and the University of Northern Iowa this semester . Younger caucusgoers fueled his 2016 surge against Hillary Clinton -- one estimate showed 84% of the under-30 crowd backed him . <p> At the start of each event this week , a speaker implored the crowds -- 500-plus in Ames , 1,350 in Iowa City and nearly 600 more in Cedar Falls , according to the campaign -- to raise their phones in the air and sign up for volunteer shifts at phone banks and neighborhood canvassing events . <p> Sanders then would deadpan .
