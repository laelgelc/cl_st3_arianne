<h> Cape Kids Speak Out Friday in Truro <h> Wednesday <p> Sep 18 , 2019 at 5:48 PM <p> TRURO -- The climate movement 's most compelling power is currently coming from children and teens , according to a press release from Bob Higgins-Steele , of Truro . Therefore , on Friday , Sept. 20 , Truro will be hosting a live communication between the Outer Cape 's youth and adults called " Listening to Our Youth : Outer Cape Kids &amp; Teens Speak Up on Climate Change . " <p> Children , teens and adults are invited to express their feelings about climate change from 3:30 to 4:30 p.m. outdoors at the Truro Community Center . <p> Children and teens from Provincetown , Truro and Wellfleet may create posters if they wish . <p> This is a local response to Global Climate Strike initiated by 16-year-old Greta Thunberg , a Swedish activist who has inspired the world with her speeches .
