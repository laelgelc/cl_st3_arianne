<p> MADRID , Sept 23 ( Reuters ) - Spain 's High Court on Monday ordered that a fugitive Peruvian Supreme Court judge should be extradited home to face charges of influence peddling among other crimes , rejecting his allegation that he was a victim of political persecution . <p> Cesar Hinostroza has been at the centre of a cronyism scandal since phone conversations recorded by police in a drug trafficking probe were leaked to the media , allegedly revealing what prosecutors have described as a criminal network of judges who traded favours with politicians and businessmen . <p> He has denied committing any crimes . <p> He was captured in Madrid last October after fleeing Peru through normal border exit points in an embarrassment to Peruvian President Martin Vizcarra 's tough anti-corruption drive .
