<h> Be prepared for PG&amp;E power shut offs during high wildfire risk <h> PG&amp;E may shut off electrical power to avoid wildfires <p> -- Due to the threat of wildfire from power lines , California 's three largest energy companies , at the direction of the California Public Utilities Commission , are coordinating to prepare all Californians for the threat of wildfires and power outages during times of extreme weather . <p> A Public Safety Power Shutoff occurred in Santa Barbara County this past weekend . <p> To help protect customers and communities during extreme weather events , electric power may be shut-off for public safety to prevent a wildfire . This is called a Public Safety Power Shutoff . <h> What residents can expect <p> Ongoing Updates -- PG&amp;E will provide ongoing updates through social media , local news outlets and their website .
