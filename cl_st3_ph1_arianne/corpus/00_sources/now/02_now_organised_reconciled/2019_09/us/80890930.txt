<p> ZURICH , Sept 4 ( Reuters ) - A Swiss parliamentary panel voted 9-6 against recommending the re-election of Attorney General Michael Lauber after undocumented meetings he held with world soccer body FIFA President Gianni Infantino , Swiss public TV SRF reported on Wednesday . <p> The Judicial Committee made the recommendation to parliament members who vote on Sept. 25 on giving Lauber another term in office as the nation 's top prosecutor . Lauber held at least three confidential meetings with Infantino amid his office 's investigation of several cases of suspected corruption surrounding Zurich-based FIFA , though the prosecutor has defended his office 's handling of the case . <p> " The Federal Prosecutor 's Office has taken note of the decision of the court commission , " his office said in a statement following the vote . " Michael Lauber maintains his candidacy for the 2020-2023 term .
