<h> Somerville candidates to address climate change <h> Monday <p> After four million people worldwide participated in a strike to stop climate change , the environment and climate is dominating the public conversation . <p> Youth are advocating for big changes , such as an end to the use of fossil fuels and single use plastic , but there are also many changes that are possible at the municipal level . <p> On Oct. 5 , the Climate Coalition of Somerville is hosting a Candidate Climate Forum to hear how Somerville 's candidates for election and reelection on Nov. 5 plan to prepare for climate change . <p> Julia Taliesin of the Journal will moderate a conversation about the challenges the city faces and the opportunities for the city to respond . All city council and mayoral candidates have been invited . <p> Doors open at 10:45 a.m. for an 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. conversation at the VNA at 259 Lowell St. Spanish , Portuguese , and Haitian Creole translation will be provided . Registration is encouraged but not required .
