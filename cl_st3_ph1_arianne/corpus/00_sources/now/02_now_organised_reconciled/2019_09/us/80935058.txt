<p> However , the city , through Bill 25 , still needs to do its part to close a loophole that has long plagued the state 's program to ensure hot water systems in new homes rely on renewable energy . <p> Bill 25 allows for " a gas-powered water heater that is fueled by a source that is not less than 90 percent renewable , " but Hawaii Gas has roughly 3% renewable fuels after a decade to address the issue . This legislation is intended to address climate change , not preserve industry profits and the fossil-fuel status quo . <p> If Hawaii Gas feels threatened by the 90% renewable-gas clause , it should consider significantly ramping up capital investment and production of renewable energy . Otherwise , its energy portfolio will continue to be mostly fossil fuels and hot air .
