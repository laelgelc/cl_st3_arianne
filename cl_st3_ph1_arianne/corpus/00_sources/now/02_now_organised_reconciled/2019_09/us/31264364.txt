<p> The future is not looking bright for oil , according to a new report that claims the commodity would have to be priced at $10-$20 a barrel to remain competitive as a transport fuel . <p> The new research , from BNP Paribas , says that the economics of renewable energy make it impossible for oil to compete at current prices . The author of the report , global head of sustainability Mark Lewis , says that " renewable electricity has a short-run marginal cost of zero , is cleaner environmentally , much easier to transport and could readily replace up to 40% of global oil demand " . <p> The oil industry faces a disruption on the same scale as that which has hit the European utilities sector over the last decade , he adds . <p> The report , Wells , Wires , And Wheels ...
