<p> BELFAST , Sept 12 ( Reuters ) - A Northern Irish rights campaigner will appeal the dismissal by Belfast 's High Court on Thursday of his case arguing that a British exit from the European Union without a withdrawal agreement would contravene Northern Ireland 's 1998 peace accord . <p> " We are seeking to appeal , we will be back before our ( Northern Ireland 's ) court of appeal today and we do hope to be before the ( United Kingdom ) Supreme Court next week , " Ciaran O'Hare , lawyer for Raymond McCord , told reporters after the judgment . <p> " These are groundbreaking legal cases and the plan is for all of these cases to meet in the Supreme Court , " O'Hare said , referring to two others challenges to Prime Minister 's Boris Johnson 's Brexit strategy that will be heard in the UK 's highest court next week .
