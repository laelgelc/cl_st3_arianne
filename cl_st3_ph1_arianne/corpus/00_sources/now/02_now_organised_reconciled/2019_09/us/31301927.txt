<h> Students lead climate change protests across the world <p> BERLIN ( AP ) -- In Canberra and Kabul , Cape Town and Berlin , and across the globe , hundreds of thousands of people took the streets Friday to demand that leaders tackle climate change in the run-up to a U.N . summit . <p> Many were children who skipped school to take part in the second " Global Climate Strike , " following a similar event in March that drew large crowds . <p> Events kicked off in Australia , where protesters marched in 110 towns and cities , including Sydney and the national capital , Canberra . Demonstrators called for their country , the world 's largest exporter of coal and liquid natural gas , to take more drastic action to reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> " Even though we ourselves are n't sick , the planet which we live on is , and we are protesting and fighting for it , " said Siobhan Sutton , a 15-year-old student at Perth Modern School . <p> Organizers estimate more than 300,000 protesters took to Australian streets in what would be the country 's biggest demonstration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are partly inspired by the activism of Swedish teenager Greta Thunberg , who has staged weekly demonstrations under the heading " Fridays for Future " over the past year , calling on world leaders to step up their efforts against climate change .
