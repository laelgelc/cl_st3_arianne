He and Yitzhak Shamir agreed to cooperate in order to navigate Israel to a safe shore , " Netanyahu said in the speech , attended by Gantz , who appeared tied with the incumbent after Tuesday 's Israeli ballot . <p> " In this election , too , there is no clear win .
