<p> WASHINGTON , Sept 6 ( Reuters ) - U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo said on Friday that he thinks the Trump administration will unveil its much-delayed Israeli-Palestinian peace plan in the coming weeks . <p> " I think in the coming weeks we 'll announce our vision , " Pompeo said in response to a question after a speech at Kansas State University in Manhattan , Kansas . Last week , the outgoing White House Middle East envoy Jason Greenblatt said the United States will not release the long-delayed political portion of its peace plan before Israel 's Sept. 17 elections .
