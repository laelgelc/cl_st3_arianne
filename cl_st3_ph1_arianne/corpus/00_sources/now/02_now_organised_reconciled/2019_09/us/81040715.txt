<h> Merkel : Germany , auto industry face ' Herculean ' climate task <p> Environmentalist staged a protest Thursday at the start of the Frankfurt Motor Show , hours after German Chancellor Angela Merkel acknowledged that her government and the country 's powerful automakers face a " Herculean task " meeting the goals set for combating climate change . <p> Speaking ahead of the show 's public opening , Merkel confronted industry leaders with a painful statistic : unlike other areas of the economy , emissions of greenhouse gases from Germany 's transport sector have remained unchanged since 1990 despite increasingly efficient vehicles . <p> " Each time we got more efficient cars ( ... ) the amount of traffic increased , " Merkel said . <p> Environmental groups say the trend for bigger and more powerful cars , particularly SUVs , is eating up the fuel efficiency of recent decades . Greenpeace activists unfurled large banners at the show branding the models on display " climate killers .
