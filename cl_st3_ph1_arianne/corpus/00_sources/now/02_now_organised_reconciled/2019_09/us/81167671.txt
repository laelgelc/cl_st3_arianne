<p> VIENNA , Sept 19 ( Reuters ) - Austrian lawmakers have rejected the proposal for a free trade pact between the European Union and the Mercosur bloc of four South American countries , in the latest blow to the deal . <p> Nearly all parties in the Austrian parliament 's European Union subcommittee voted against the draft for a EU-Mercosur free trade agreement late on Wednesday . <p> That means the government must vote against the deal at the EU level , where according to rules all 28 member states and their parliaments must agree to trade deals . <p> Brazil and Argentina , along with Uruguay and Paraguay , are the four countries in the Mercosur trade bloc , which in summer agreed one of the world 's biggest trade deals with the European Union after 20 years of negotiations . <p> Brazil is under pressure from European countries over its environmental policies and an epidemic of forest fires in the Amazon basin . <p> France blocked the deal in August after Brazilian President Jair Bolsonaro rejected criticism over the wildfires raging through the Amazon .
