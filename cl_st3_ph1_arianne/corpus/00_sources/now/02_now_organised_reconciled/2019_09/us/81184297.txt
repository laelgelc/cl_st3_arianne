<p> Greece 's President Prokopis Pavlopoulos signed a declaration with another 31 heads of state and government , calling for an initiative to adopt more ambitious climate goals , ahead of the United Nations Climate Summit in New York on September 21-23 . <p> The initiative comes after the conclusions of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change Special Report and the results of COP 24 . <p> All 32 leaders call on all parties attending the U.N . Summit to view 2019 as a year of ambitious climate goals and support the adoption of specific plans and initiatives aimed at significantly reducing greenhouse gas emissions beyond the current targets set for 2030 . <p> They also call for immediate action , stressing that climate change is the main challenge of our time , and point out the need to give our children and future generations a world , as they say , ' worth living in.
