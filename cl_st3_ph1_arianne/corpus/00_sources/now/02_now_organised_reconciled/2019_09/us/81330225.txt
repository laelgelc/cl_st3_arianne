<h> Letter : Why Graham must ' do the right thing , ' lead on climate change <p> As a concerned mother and grandmother , I am deeply worried about the impacts of climate change on South Carolina and on the rest of our beloved Earth . Our recent heat and drought are emblematic of our future as climate change intensifies . The oceans and the life they support are being hurt by sea rise and acidification , which will cause tremendous hardship throughout the world , including ( along ) our own South Carolina coast . <p> I am asking our government officials , especially our own Sen. Lindsey Graham , to become strong advocates for doing the right thing . First , or Graham and other leaders must convince the current administration , especially President Trump , that climate change is not a hoax . It is an emergency of the highest order . We must have the political will to act now before it is too late . <p> Young people are knowledgeable about climate change , and they are very worried about their future on a planet being ravaged by accumulating carbon dioxide and methane @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ back onto our Earth . They object to canceling Obama-era regulations , including vehicle emission standards and clean air/water standards . These regulations are not hurting our economy , but canceling them will hurt our young people long after Graham and I are gone . <p> Over the years I have come to know Graham to be an intelligent and compassionate leader and a positive role model for our kids .
