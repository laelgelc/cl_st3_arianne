<h> Letter : US should n't roll back standards for methane <h> Friday <p> Sep 6 , 2019 at 4:10 AM <p> The Trump administration and U.S. EPA are proposing rolling back oversight of methane emissions from more than 850,000 existing oil and gas wells , including over 1,400 here in Ohio . Methane is a major factor in climate change , whose effects are felt every day by farmers , consumers , coastal businesses and public health . <p> Midwest farmers have not yet recovered from record flooding and abnormally high temperatures , threatening their livelihoods and our food supplies . Methane also is associated with unhealthy levels of ozone pollution . Ozone is linked to cardiovascular disease , asthma attacks and premature death . We ca n't wait to address these issues . <p> Shell , ExxonMobil and BP have already urged the EPA to not roll back methane emission protections , but to instead tighten those standards .
