Climate Summit that will be attended by leaders from about 60 countries . <p> Those attending the New York summit will include the leaders of small island states most at risk from rising sea levels and companies expected to make fresh pledges to cut emissions of greenhouse gases . <p> " One thing we 've learned is that shutting down a city is a very effective way to communicate the gravity of
