<p> PARIS , Sept 8 ( Reuters ) - France 's foreign minister said on Sunday that , as things stand , the European Union would not grant Britain an extension beyond Oct. 31 to negotiate its exit from the bloc . <p> " It 's very worrying . The British must tell us what they want , " Jean-Yves Le Drian told Europe 1 radio . <p> Asked if an extension beyond Oct. 31 was possible , Le Drian said not under current conditions . " We are not going to do ( extend ) this every three months , " he said .
