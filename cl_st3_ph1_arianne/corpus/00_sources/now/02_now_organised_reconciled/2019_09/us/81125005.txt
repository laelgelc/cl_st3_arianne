<h> Letters : Website disproves a theory about ' theory ' <h> Share this : <p> Reading Paul Narlock 's letter of September 12 was an amusing way to begin the day . In it he suggests that addressing climate change at the local level is not only unimportant but dangerous , that tens of thousands of climatologists and scientists throughout the world are paid to support the " theory " of human-caused climate change , and that volcanoes are more to blame than humans . <p> At the end of his letter he provides a website to support his claims , so I went there and read the following : " A number of gases are involved in the human caused enhancement of the greenhouse effect ... This increase in carbon dioxide in the atmosphere is primarily due to the activities of humans . " Oops .
