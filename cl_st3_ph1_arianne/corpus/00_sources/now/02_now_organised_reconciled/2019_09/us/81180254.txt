<p> Arcata High Student and event organizer Zoe Reiss speaks at Humboldt 's version of the Global Climate Strike on Friday Photos : Andrew Goff <p> Hundreds of people packed onto the Arcata Plaza this afternoon , joining cities around the world for the second Global Youth Climate Strike -- a protest against climate change inspired by Swedish environmental activist Greta Thunberg . <p> Arcata High and Humboldt State University students , after walking out of their classrooms , joined together to march to the plaza . Their arrival was met with an eruption of cheers from community members already gathered in the town square in support of today 's protest . <p> " Money wo n't matter when the earth is dead , " was one of several chants the crowd shouted , expressing their anger with the state of the planet and a lack action from world leaders . <p> The Outpost attended today 's event and snapped a few photos of the sign-wielding youth in revolt .
