<h> University of California to dump fossil fuel investments <p> Updated : Sep 17 , 2019 - 8:13 PM <p> 0 <p> University of California to dump fossil fuel investments <p> LOS ANGELES ( AP ) - The University of California is dumping fossil fuel investments from its nearly $84 billion pension and endowment funds because they are a financial risk , its top financial officers announced Tuesday . <p> " Our job is to make money for the University of California , and we 're betting we can do that without fossil fuels investments , " said an opinion article in the Los Angeles Times written by Jagdeep Singh Bachher , UC 's chief investment officer and treasurer , and Richard Sherman , chair of the Board of Regents Investments Committee . <p> UC 's $13.4 billion endowment fund will be " fossil free " by the end of the month and its $70 billion pension fund " will soon be that way , " the article said . <p> " We want to ensure that the more than 320,000 people currently receiving a UC pension actually get paid , that we can continue to fund research and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and medical centers earn the best possible return on their investments , " the article said . <p> The article appeared the same day that UC announced its president and chancellors had signed a letter declaring a " climate emergency , " joining more than 7,000 colleges and universities around the world . <p> The UC leaders agreed to increase climate research and environmental education and to achieve climate neutrality by 2025 . <p> " We have a moral responsibility to take swift action on climate change , " UC President Janet Napolitano said . " This declaration reaffirms UC 's commitment to addressing one of the greatest existential threats of our time . " <p> A global campaign for universities and other organizations to disinvest has been waged by climate activists for nearly a decade .
