<p> LONDON , Sept 9 ( Reuters ) - Britain 's opposition Labour Party will not vote for an election until the possibility of a no-deal Brexit at the end of October has been taken off the table , Labour leader Jeremy Corbyn said on Monday . <p> " Until ... no-deal has been taken off the table ... we will not vote to support the dissolution of this house and a general election , " Corbyn told parliament in a debate about holding an early election . Britain is due to leave the European Union on Oct. 31 . <p> " We 're eager for an election . But as keen as we are , we are not prepared to risk inflicting the disaster of no-deal on our communities .
