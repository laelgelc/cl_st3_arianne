<h> Senate Bill could provide $403 million to WIPP , fund other energy projects in New Mexico <p> Almost half a billion dollars could be coming to the Waste Isolation Pilot as federal legislation advanced to the floor of the U.S. Senate , led by U.S. Sen. Tom Udall ( D-NM ) and intended to fund operations at U.S. Department of Energy sites and projects across the country . <p> Udall serves as senior member of the Senate Appropriations Committee , which unanimously supported moving the bill to the Senate floor for a full vote . <p> The legislation provided federal funds for New Mexico 's national labs , environmental clean-up projects and tribal energy programs throughout the U.S. , along with about $403 million for recovery operations and ongoing work at WIPP -- an underground repository for transuranic ( TRU ) waste near Carlsbad . <p> " This funding will help bolster New Mexico 's thriving science and technology economy -- while providing the resources needed to ensure environmental clean-up and public safety , " Udall said . <p> " This bill invests in New Mexico 's state of the art national labs and the men and women @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tech transfer initiatives that energize and diversify our economy . " <p> He also pointed to investments the bill would make in water projects throughout New Mexico , the day after New Mexico . Gov . Michelle Lujan Grisham announced a consortium and memorandum of understanding between New Mexico State University and the New Mexico Environment Department to study how produced waste water from oil and gas operations could be treated and reused . <p> " The bill also invests in New Mexico 's most precious resource -- water -- to ensure a more sustainable future for our state , " Udall .
