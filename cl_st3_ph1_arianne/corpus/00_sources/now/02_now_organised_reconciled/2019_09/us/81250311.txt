<h> Shutdown DC is working <p> " Shutdown DC " seems to be working . My wife and I checked the traffic flow maps for DC and the amount of red ( stopped traffic ) is amazing . The idea behind the protest is to demonstrate to the " political elite " that the people want action on climate change . <p> The protest is timed to coincide with the United Nations Climate Action Summit in New York , at which climate activists and leaders , including16-year-old Greta Thunberg , are slated to speak . It follows a strike across six continents Friday and a youth conference at the United Nations on Saturday . <p> The traffic shutdown is meant to send a particular message to D.C. 's powerful political elite , according to Liz Butler , an organizer for Shut Down D.C. and vice president of organizing and strategic allegiances for Friends of the Earth Action .
