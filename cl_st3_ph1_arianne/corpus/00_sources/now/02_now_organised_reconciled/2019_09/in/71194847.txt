<h> Last Updated : Monday 08 July 2019 <p> Activists urged Odisha Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik to arrange for suitable rehabilitation of climate refugees in the state 's Puri and Kendrapada districts and also formulate a proper rehabilitation policy for them . <p> Thousands of villagers on Odisha 's coast are gradually being pushed back by an inundating sea because of climate change and related challenges , members of Water Initiatives , Odisha ( WIO ) found . <p> WIO Convenor Ranjan Panda wrote to Patnaik on July 8 , 2019 , urging his government to initiate necessary steps to address the issues of the thousands of climate refugees of the state . <p> WIO , a forum of civil society organisations and experts working for water conservation , studied Udayakani and Tandahara villages in Puri district , and Satabhaya and Bagapatia villages in Kendrapada district . <p> The people of Udayakani village under Sisuo Panchayat , Astaranga block of Puri have been suffering because of the rapidly invading sea , the letter claimed . <p> The advancing sea has forced villagers to relocate thrice : <p> During the 1999 Super Cyclone the entire village went into the sea @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ During the 2014 Cyclone Phailin they had to shift inland again after the village got sandcast . <p> The recent Cyclone Fani worsened their condition further by making their remaining land and water sources more saline . <p> In neighbouring Tandahara village , even though the sea has not yet engulfed the land the situation is pretty much the same , wrote Panda , popularly known as Water Man of Odisha . <p> He apprised Patnaik of the current state of affairs of Bagapatia rehabilitation colony where the government has relocated climate refugees of Satabhaya -- the seven villages which have already been engulfed by the sea during the last few decades . <p> While the amount of support given for house building is too little considering the low-lying swampy area they have been allotted , they are provided with no livelihood opportunity . <p> Recommendations <p> Panda requested Patnaik to take the following measures : <p> 1 .
