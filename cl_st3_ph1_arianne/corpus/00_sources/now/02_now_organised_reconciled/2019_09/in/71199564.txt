The exposition , which will be held on September 17 and September 27 at the Hall Bungener in Geneva , is part of a movement to elevate the voices of the Pashtuns.The Pakistani military establishment through various operations in the region has continued to wreak havoc and injustices on the entire community of the Pashtuns . Hundreds of civilians have been killed or forcibly disappeared .
