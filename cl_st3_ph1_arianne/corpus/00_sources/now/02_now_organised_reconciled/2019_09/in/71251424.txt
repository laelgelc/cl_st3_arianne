Just a month old in the city , Ismail highlighted the plight and miseries that minorities are facing for decades in Pakistan.Gulalai joined the Muhajirs , Pashtuns , Balochis , Sindhis and many other minorities protesting outside the United Nations headquarters while Pakistani Prime Minister Imran Khan 's addressed the UN General Assembly .
