<p> As the Legislature mulls a bill that proposes to ban single-use plastic bags in the CNMI , the Commonwealth is taking yet another step toward cleaning its shores -- this time , with the rest of the Micronesia island community -- by joining a Micronesia-wide cleanup that 's happening this Friday , Sept. 13 . <p> Last Friday , Gov . Ralph DLG Torres proclaimed Sept. 13 , 2019 , as Micronesia Cleanup Day , which calls for an islandwide cleanup this Friday , with activities to be done simultaneously on all Micronesian islands . <p> " I call on all people who call the Marianas home to join our government and private partners to clean up our beaches , villages , and our community . Together , we can create a cleaner Marianas and a sustainable Micronesia , " Torres said in the proclamation . <p> Recognizing that the increase of trash , on land and in the water , poses a great threat to the way of life on the islands , Torres , with the leaders of the Republic of Palau , the Republic of the Marshall Islands , the Federated States of Micronesia , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that led to the Micronesia Cleanup Day declaration . <p> File photo shows Larry Maurin participating in a beach-side cleanup at the back of the TSL Plaza in Gaparan .
