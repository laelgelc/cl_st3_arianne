( Photo : ANI ) <p> Greater Noida : Union Minister for Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Prakash Javadekar on Monday said that India has already achieved half of its renewable energy production target . <p> The government plans to achieve 175GW of renewable energy capacity by 2022 as part of its climate commitments . Reportedly , India currently has an installed renewable energy capacity of around 80 gigawatts . <p> Javadekar was addressing the 14th Conference of Parties ( COP14 ) to United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification ( UNCCD ) in Greater Noida . <p> " The world did not believe it when we announced our renewable energy target of 175 GW , which will be 40 per cent of our total energy capacity . However , we have already achieved half of our target , " he said . <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Prime Minister of Saint Vincent and the Grenadines , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " We are a party to the Paris Agreement . The commitment of Prime Minister Narendra Modi , towards sustainable development , is full and complete . He also played a leading road at the Paris summit , " Javadekar said . <p> He said that the Modi government has also levied a tax on coal production , promoted e-vehicles by offering various tax concessions and set up a new ministry to tackle the water scarcity in the country . <p> " We will come out with an ambitious and appropriate Delhi Declaration tomorrow , which will ensure speedy restoration for degraded land for sustainable growth , " Javadekar said . <p> He added that delegation from around 200 countries has participated in the event . <p> UNCCD was adopted in Paris on June 17 , 1994 , and was ratified by 196 countries and the European Union . India had ratified the UNCCD Convention in 1996.
