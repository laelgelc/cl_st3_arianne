Other than microbes , and may be some ferns and moss , these weedy herbs can therefore be expected to respond to the changing climate and adapt themselves the fastest . Knowing and recognising them , therefore , is a skill for now and the future , that everyone must have as we gear up to combat the effects of climate change . Recognising healthy and nutritious herbs in the volunteer weeds that are growing around us may allow us to adapt better to the changes .
