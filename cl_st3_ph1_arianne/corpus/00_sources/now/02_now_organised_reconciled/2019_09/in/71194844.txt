" <p> But the problem of land degradation and desertification is acute in Asia and Africa . Asia holds almost 60 per cent of the world 's population . <p> Of this , nearly 70 per cent live in rural areas and depend directly on land and land-based ecosystem services . As a result , Asia is most severely affected by land degradation , desertification and drought in terms of the number of people . <p> In Africa too , the situation is alarming .
