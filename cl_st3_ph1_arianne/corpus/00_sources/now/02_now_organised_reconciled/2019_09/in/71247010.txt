<h> Convention must not be rewritten , assert developing countries at COP <h> Last Updated : Tuesday 08 December 2015 <p> Speaking on behalf of the G-77 and China , South African Ambassador Diseko on Monday , reiterated the group 's stance that the new climate agreement should be under the principles , rules and provisions of the Convention . She insisted that there was no scope to rewrite , renegotiate or reinterpret the Convention . <p> The ambassador pressed for principles of equity and Common but Differentiated Responsibility and Respective Capability ( CBDRRC ) to be reflected in all elements -- mitigation , adaptation , capacity building , transparency and means of support and implementation . She also urged for scaling up of the pre-2020 actions and said that still there are five years before the new agreement comes into effect in 2020 . Since the G-77 and China group comprises of 85 per cent of the world population , Diseko made it clear that no agreement could be arrived at unless the concerns of the group are taken into account . <p> Miguel Arias Ca ? ete , commissioner for climate action and energy at the European @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ EU ) , expressed optimism that the new climate agreement is within reach in Paris . " We need a credible , robust and globally legally binding agreement , " he remarked . He also assured that the money is flowing both domestically and internationally in both mitigation and adaptation and the EU was a leader in providing climate finance . <p> On mitigation , the Commissioner called for a long term operational goal , a regular review of the climate efforts by all Parties as the initial contributions in the form of INDCs , though a welcome step , are still not ambitious . Accordingly , an upward review mechanism was essential . On adaptation , he pressed for ambition in all countries .
