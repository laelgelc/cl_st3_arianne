It is alarming to hear how much trash was collected in a few hours . We urge all residents to do their part to minimize the risk of spreading the dengue virus , which is transmitted through mosquitoes . They can do so by eliminating mosquito breeding sites around their homes , " said Gov . Lou Leon Guerrero . <p> Micronesia Cleanup Day was a direct result of Leon Guerrero 's participation in the Micronesia Island Forum held in Chuuk earlier this year .
