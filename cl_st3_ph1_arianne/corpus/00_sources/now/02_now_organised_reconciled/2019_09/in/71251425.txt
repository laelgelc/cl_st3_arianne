<h> US State Department expresses grief over killing of America 's first Sikh Police Officer <p> ANI Updated : Sep 28 , 2019 23:18 IST <p> Washington US , Sept 28 ( ANI ) : Bureau of South and Central Asian Affairs , US Department of State on Saturday expressed deep grief over the killing of America 's first turbaned Sikh police officer , Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal . " We mourn alongside colleagues , friends &amp; family of Lt Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal , 1st Indian-American in Texas to serve as a police officer while maintaining articles of faith . Dhaliwal put the Sikh imperative of selfless service on display for all &amp; touched a nation with his example , " it said in a statement.Earlier , Indian External Affairs Minister , S Jaishankar mourned Dhaliwal 's killing.Extending condolence to the family of the officer , Jaishankar tweeted , " Deeply grieved to learn of the shooting of Deputy Sandeep Singh Dhaliwal , a Sikh Indian-American officer in Houston . We have just visited that city . My condolences to his family . " Dhaliwal died on Friday afternoon in the line of duty during a traffic stop .
