<p> Unexpected rains with gusty winds lashed Ahmedabad on Wednesday evening bringing relief from heat coupled with high moisture . Most citizens , particularly those commuting by two-wheelers , were caught unawares and had to take refuge under flyovers and trees . Such was the downpour that visibility was severely affected between 4 and 5 pm . <p> Till 6 pm , the city had received 14.66 mm rainfall , with the East zone receiving maximum rainfall of 28.34 mm , followed by west zone ( 23.5 mm ) and South west zone ( 21 mm ) . <p> So far , Ahmedabad has reached 757.90 mm ( 29.84 inch ) season 's rainfall . Maximum season 's rainfall has been registered in the south west zone at 977.50 mm .
