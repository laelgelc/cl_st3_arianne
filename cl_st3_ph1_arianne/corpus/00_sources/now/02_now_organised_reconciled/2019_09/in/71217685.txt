<h> Search in Archive <p> Not that it might be a burning concern at this point but do you know which Indian states could lead the spending sweepstakes in tackling climate change and diminishing forest cover ? <p> IndiaSpend'sDanielle Collacofiled a Right To Information ( RTI ) with the Ministry of Environment &amp; Forests to find that Chattisgarh , Madhya Pradesh &amp; Maharashtra lead the table with the maximum allocated spend from the Central Government to take on global warming , at least for the kick-off ( preparatory ) efforts . <p> The spends form part of the National Mission For A Green India , a 10-year-long project that will span the 12th and 13th Five Year Plan periods . The main focus of the Mission is to add about 5 million hectares of forest cover and improve the quality of forest cover on another 5 million hectares . <p> Incidentally , forests and trees account for about 23.8% of the country 's geographical area . The Forest Survey of India released a report earlier this year saying India 's forest cover declined by about 367 square kilometres since 2009 . <p> Funding The Green India Mission <p> A @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this Mission for 10 years , starting 2012-13 .
