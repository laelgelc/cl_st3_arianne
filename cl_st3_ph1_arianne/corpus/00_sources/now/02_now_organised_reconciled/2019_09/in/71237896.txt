" We all knew restoring forests could play a part in tackling climate change , but we had no scientific understanding of what impact this could make , " said study co-author Thomas Crowther . <p> According to the team of researchers , the study shows clearly that forest restoration is the best climate change solution available today because trees capture and remove carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) from the atmosphere , widespread reforestation has been considered one of the most effective weapons against climate change . <p> Findings of the study were published in the Journal of Science .
