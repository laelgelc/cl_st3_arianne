The ' zero-emission ' journey came a year after Thunberg shot to fame after protesting outside the Swedish parliament for stronger action against climate change . From September 20 , Thunberg and other young activists around the world are organizing a coordinated global climate strike in 150 countries , a landmark moment in the history of environmental protest . <p> After decades of insufficient action by governments and individuals alike , it is perhaps not surprising that the world 's most prominent and blunt climate change activist today is a teenager -- representing a generation that will most severely face the brunt of the climate crisis . <p> The signs and chatter are everywhere , and some kids are starting to feel the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the reasons behind the unfolding crisis .
