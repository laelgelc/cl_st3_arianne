However , the intensity was high . This was particularly due to the cumulonimbus clouds resulting in such rains , " said Anupam Kashyapi , senior scientist at IMD . <p> Cumulonimbus clouds are formed as a towering mass with a flat base in a substantial low altitude and also have aflat top , similar to thunderstorms . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ due to local convection activity . " The damage is more in low-lying areas and the rivers flowing through the city only aggravate the situation as the riverbed swells , " he said . <p> Speaking to Mirror , Kashyapi said such an event can have varied rainfall in neighbourhoods .
