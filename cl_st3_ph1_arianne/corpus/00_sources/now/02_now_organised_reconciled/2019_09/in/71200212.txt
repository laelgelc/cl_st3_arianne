<h> Image Credit : The Hindu <p> The National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) , Principal Bench has directed the CPCB ( Central Pollution Control Board ) to implement a proper mechanism for plastic waste management , on a plea filed by petitioner Amit Jain . <p> The NGT was considering a remedial action against cheap waste paper and the import and export of waste for firing of brick kilns . As per Amit Jain , almost 900,000 tons of hazardous waste is being imported . <p> In the earlier hearing , CPCB suggested potential remedial measures to the Principal Bench , such as , restriction on import of plastic waste and proper management of hazardous waste , and had also issued certain guidelines . The NGT had then directed the Board to restrict the import of plastic waste , and submit an ' action taken ' report on the implementation of its guidelines on plastic waste management . <p> The Report submitted by the CPCB acknowledged the lack of proper mechanism for plastic waste management , which was either being dumped in open or , burnt in brick kilns , resulting in pollution . The Principal Bench told the Board to oversee the compliance of its directions and , to take up the matter of illegal import with the concerned authorities , being a statutory regulator . <p> In conclusion , the NGT 's order required the CPCB to take further steps in the matter and file the report before the next date of hearing by e-mail at judicial-ngt@gov.in . <p> The matter will be next heard on 16th October , 2019 for further consideration .
