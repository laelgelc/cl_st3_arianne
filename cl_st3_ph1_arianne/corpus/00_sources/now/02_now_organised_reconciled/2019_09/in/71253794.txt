The result is that what the two said seems to have been drowned in the generated passion , with of course the play out on Kashmir becoming secondary to who scored more points than the other . <p> Looks , language , dress et al has been discussed bare by the Twitterati in their highly academic 140 character outpourings , but at the end of the day the substance of what the PMs said has been lost . And this despite the fact that both PM 's made arguments , one through what he did not say , and the other who laid it bare insofar as words are concerned .
