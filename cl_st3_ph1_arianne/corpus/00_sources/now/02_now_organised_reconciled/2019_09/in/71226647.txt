<h> Rain likely to play spoilsport on first 3 days of Navratri <p> Pvt weather websites predict scattered showers and thunderstorms in city on Sept 29-30 and Oct 1 <p> While Amdavadis are gearing up for Navratri which is less than 10 days away , rain is likely to play spoilsport on the first three days of garba as per private weather forecasters . Scattered showers and thunderstorms have been predicted for September 29-30 and October 1 , following which the weather is likely to remain dry . Event organisers across the city are well-prepared to tackle the vagaries of the weather ; artificial lawns are in place along with covered centre stages . <p> Mahesh Palawat , vice president of Meteorological and Climate Change at Skymet Weather , said , " There are chances of light rain in the city on the first two days of Navratri , following which the weather will be dry . Scattered light rainfall can be expected in the city on September 29 and 30 . The sky will remain cloudy to partly cloudy the remaining days . In Gujarat , monsoon is expected to stay till September end , but the rains will subside . " <p> Another spell of rain is expected in the city on September 26 and 27 . AccuWeather predicts scattered showers and thunderstorms for Ahmedabad and surrounding areas from September 29 to October 1 . <p> Jason Nicholls , lead international forecaster and senior meteorologist IV , at AccuWeather , USA said , " Scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected in Ahmedabad and surrounding areas from September 29 to October 1 with rainfall totalling 25-75 mm . The weather will be largely dry in western Gujarat . Rain will be a concern from September 29 to October 1 , and then it should be largely dry . " <p> Nicholls added , " There will be scattered rains in southern and eastern Gujarat from the 29th to around the 1st of October , and then weather will be largely dry from the 2nd to the 7th .
