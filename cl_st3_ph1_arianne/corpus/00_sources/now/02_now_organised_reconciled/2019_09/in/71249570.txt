<h> SEBI Watch : Lessons from abroad for launching social stock exchange <p> Thursday , Sep 26 <p> By Chiranjivi Chakraborty <p> In India , making profit while pursuing a social purpose is still an infant concept . The government in its bid to help companies that work for social causes such as climate change has asked the Securities and Exchange Board of India to take up the mantle of forming India 's first social stock exchange . <p> A social stock exchange is intended to get the equity market closer to the masses and at the same time formalise and streamline the funding of social businesses and enterprises . <p> SEBI will regulate and control the social stock exchange and had last week formed a working committee for creating the country 's first such exchange . <p> Globally , social stock exchanges have been part of the impact investing movement that started almost three to four decades ago . Countries such as the UK , Brazil , South Africa and Canada have their own form of social stock exchanges . <p> However , each country has developed its own format of the social stock exchange .
