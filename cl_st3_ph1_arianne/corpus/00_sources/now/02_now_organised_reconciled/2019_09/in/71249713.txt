<h> Shifting Frames <p> Attend an exhibition of artworks by visual artists attempting to interpret the meaning of art <p> The meaning of art and its philosophical basis , have been continuously defined and analysed both within and outside the art world . It was to foster a dialogue among the new generation of visual artists that Danish Shaikh , Rohit Tharwani and Ajinkya Bhalerao , co-founders of city-based Palat Studio , decided to launch Art Baithak earlier this year . The events held under their banner aim to generate artistic responses to preconceived notions of art . " It is a group of artists brought together to encourage conversations that help in understanding what is art in present times , " says Shaikh , whose artistic name is Doodlewaala . <p> Debangshu Moulik ( top left inset ) and Mehek Malhotra ( inset ) are some of the participating artists . A portrait by Arpit Vyas ( Right ) <p> At an upcoming exhibition organised by Art Baithak on Sunday , 13 visual artists will present an array of artworks that interpret the meaning of art . Ranging from realistic to abstract and mixed media work , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ themes such as politics and philosophy , explains Shaikh . His own work , inspired by his observations from routine life , will also be on display in the exhibition . Alongside his art will be the work of caricature artist Arathy Aluckal , who believes that every face tells a story . She translates the distinct and quirky elements she observes , into funky and quick caricatures . Aluckal will host a live caricature booth at the event . <p> Experimental art is among the highlights of the show . Mehek Malhotra , who creates " conversational art " , will showcase digital prints of her work , which is based on text and derived from her interactions with people . " Most of my work is textbased and done in really bright colours , with quotes like ' My favourite extreme sport is freelancing ' . I take relatable conversations and design pieces around it , " says Malhotra , founder of Giggling Monkey Studio . While Shrishti Bhandari and Ankit Vichare , who experiment with mediums and themes , and whose art pieces will be exhibited too @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Shruti Katiyar are inspired by mythological stories and cultural idiosyncrasies . <p> Debangshu Moulik , a student of fine arts at Abhinav Kala Mahavidyalaya , will display his series of acrylic paintings and sketches . They are based on his life in the city , and his interactions with people . " I will also be displaying my sketchbook , where I ideate before translating it on canvas . This shows how my artwork develops , " he says . The works of Arpit Vyas , engineerturned-artist who produces portraits with a surrealist touch , and a series of eerie stillimages by photographer Swapnil Kale , will also be seen at Art Baithak . <p> Besides art , the event will host musical performances too . Meet Rao , known as Khushnuma Panchi , will perform an acoustic set , which will be followed by Dream Space , an audio-visual musical presentation by Mohit " Mohito " Tanpure . Tanpure 's presentation is based on a movie script he 's been working on . Crafting a collage of post-punk futuristic trance music , the presentation is interspersed with snippets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environmental activist Greta Thunberg and actor Leonardo DiCaprio . " The story revolves around the present speculations about space travel . The sound tracks have been designed around the story , of which one track is dedicated to the concern of climate change . I want to spread awareness about this issue through electronic music , as I feel everyone likes electronic music , but it generally lacks a message , " says Tanpure , an interdisciplinary artist who studied advertising at Bharati Vidyapeeth in Nashik . <p> Art Baithak will also host a workshop for illustrators and designers , titled Client Brief 101 , which will be conducted by Nikhil More , co-founder of Junglegym , a creative studio in the city . The workshop will delve into working within client parameters while also focussing on innovative thinking processes . Only 30 spots are open , and all the materials required for the workshop is covered in the fee , which is Rs 300 .
