In an effort to stop the massive massacre of Amur falcons ( a migratory raptor ) near Nagaland 's Doyang reservoir , WCT along with other NGOs worked at various levels , engaging with the government and local communities to get a regulation passed to protect the falcons . <p> A teaching manual that sensitised locals about the importance of the falcons was also designed and distributed .
