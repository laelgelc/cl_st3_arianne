<h> Greta and the Power of One <p> Thunberg has inspired over 4 million people to protest against climate change <p> Teen activist exemplifies Margaret Mead 's famous quote , that the power of a few people can change the world <p> A girl came in a boat across the ocean and conquered a whole nation , without firing asingle bullet . This is the story of Greta Thunberg storming into New York and speaking at the United Nations Climate Action Summit . She sailed across the Atlantic Ocean instead of taking a plane , to make apoint . <p> It was just one year ago , that a loner 15-year-old was sitting on a dharna outside the Swedish parliament . She was upset that not enough was being done about what she calls the " climate crisis " . By May this year she was on Time magazine cover , featured as the next generation leader . This month she inspired more than four million people worldwide to march in the city streets , demanding stronger action from their governments to curb global warming . Her speech at the UN has gone mega viral with a hashtag of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " This is all wrong . I should n't be up here . I should be back in school on the other side of the ocean . Yet , you all come to us young people for hope . How dare you ! You have stolen my dreams and my childhood with your empty words and yet , I 'm one of the lucky ones . People are suffering . People are dying . Entire ecosystems are collapsing . We are in the beginning of a mass extinction and all you can talk about is money and fairy tales of eternal economic growth . How dare you ! " <p> The pessimists and cynics may think this is alarmist talk , and she is craving for celebrity status . On the former there can be debate , whether we are past the tipping point in climate crisis . But on the latter , her critics are wrong . She has so far come across as persistent , focused and devoid of any overt need for ego gratification . At 16 , she is not even an adult ! But this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is the incredible power of one person , without any access to formal political power or money power , who through sheer passion and focus can move global opinion . There is tangible change . <p> At the UN summit , 77 countries have committed to cutting greenhouse gas emissions to zero by 2050 . More than 100 business leaders , including some top CEOs , have said they will align themselves with the Paris accord of 2016 . It does n't matter that some of the biggest emitters like the US , China , Brazil were absent from the summit , but the tide is turning in favour of action , rather than mere lip service . <p> India 's ban on single-use plastic , commitment to go fully with electric vehicles , increasing renewable energy , including solar , to 40 per cent , are all examples of concrete goals and actions . It is going to be disruptive and many conventional industries will face high cost or extinction . But as Greta says , we are otherwise facing mass extinction . Four years ago , this lawyer , and his neighbour , 84-year-old Harbansh Mathur , started voluntary cleanup of Versova beach , one weekend at a time . In four years , thousands of volunteers have joined him and made beach clean-up a movement . School kids , film stars , even politicians have joined him and help bring amazing change to Mumbai 's beach . It is still an uphill almost impossible task , but Afroz 's sheer determination and focus is inspirational . The fact that so many have joined him spontaneously speaks a lot about his infectious enthusiasm . <p> Greta and Afroz are fortunately two of dozens if not hundreds of individuals all over the world , doing what they believe in , and achieving real change for the better . Their achievements against impossible odds are inspirational too , but in a different sense . <p> So also are stories of people with humble beginnings achieving the peak glory , whether in an IIT or IAS entrance examination , or business or electoral politics . India 's milk revolution , too , owed a great deal to the vision and sheer determination , nay doggedness of one person , Verghese Kurien . He landed with a mechanical engineering degree in a state where he did n't speak the local language , and in a field about which he had zero knowledge . And look where he took Amul .
