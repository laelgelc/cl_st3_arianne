<h> Humans have impacted climate since 1937 <p> Washington : A new study has found that humans have triggered the last 16 record-breaking hot years experienced on Earth ( up to 2014 ) , with their impact on the global climate going as far back as 1937 . <p> The study suggested that without human-induced climate change , recent hot summers and years would not have occurred . <p> The researchers also found that this effect has been masked until recently in many areas of the world by the wide use of industrial aerosols , which have a cooling effect on temperatures . <p> Researcher Andrew King said that everywhere they look , the climate change signal for extreme heat events is becoming stronger and the recent record-breaking hot years globally were so much outside natural variability that they were almost impossible without global warming . <p> The researchers examined weather events that exceeded the range of natural variability and used climate modelling to compare those events to a world without human-induced greenhouse gases . <p> The study is published in the journal Geophysical Research Letters .
