Farmers here traditionally grow almond and olive crops , which take up a lot of water . But climate change has made rain scarce in this arid and semi-arid region . In 2012 , social entrepreneur Sarah Mag Toumi provided them a new farming opportunity with acacia . <p> The tree can grow in desert areas and has roots up to 100 m , providing soil with nitrogen that restores fertility . Its plantation is an internationally recognised method to halt desertification . <p> In 2012 , the farmers planted 1,500 trees and another 5,000 the next year .
