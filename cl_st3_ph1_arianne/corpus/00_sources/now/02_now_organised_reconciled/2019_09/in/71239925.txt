The unit which stored and sold the items was fined during a raid in West Delhi 's Raghubir Nagar on Friday . The Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change has earlier prohibited the use of single-use plastic with a thickness less than 50 microns under the Plastic Waste Management Rules , 2016 . <p> The unit which has now been seized was found keeping and selling plastic carry bags and other plastic products with a thickness of fewer than 50 microns .
