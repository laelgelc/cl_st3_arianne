The Coriolis force , named after a French engineer and mathematician , determines general wind directions and is responsible for the rotation of hurricanes and other low-pressure systems . Studies have proved earlier that the difference in Coriolis force between the northern and southern regions of Bay of Bengal makes the wind blow in a northwest direction . On certain occasions , the wind flow bypasses Orissa and Andhra coasts and is pushed towards Bangladesh by anticyclonic formations or the westerlies from the land . So , West Bengal remains safe from the cyclones . <p> But Aila formed in the central Bay of Bengal and moved north because of two forces the anticylone in Myanmar from the east and westerlies from the west , said Mahapatra .
