In a historic address , delivered in Hindi , at the 74th Session of United Nations General Assembly in New York yesterday , his focus was on development in the country , climate change and terrorism . He said , India is an example to the world and the world could seek motivation from the developing country . <p> Lamenting the lack of unanimity amongst the member states on the issue of terrorism , Prime Minister said , it hurts those very principles , that are the basis for the creation of the United Nations .
