<h> Video games industry levels up in fight against climate change <p> Some of the biggest names in the video games industry , with a combined audience of 970 million players , on Monday formally committed to harness the power of their platforms to take action in response to the climate crisis . <p> Combined , these commitments from 21 companies will result in a 30 million tonne reduction of CO2 emissions by 2030 , will see millions of trees planted , new " green nudges " in game design and improvements to energy management , packaging , and device recycling . <p> These voluntary commitments were announced today at UN Headquarters on the side-lines of the UN Secretary-General 's Climate Action Summit . Under the banner of the Playing for the Planet Alliance , CEOs from 14 platforms and games makers , including Sony Interactive Entertainment , Microsoft , Google Stadia , Rovio , Supercell , Sybo , Ubisoft and WildWorks , were present to showcase their commitments.The Alliance intends to support companies in sharing learning and monitoring progress on the environmental agenda . <p> " The video games industry has the ability to engage , inspire and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
