<h> Donald Trump ' planning UK summer visit ' <p> US president Donald Trump is planning on visiting the UK in the summer , it is reported . <p> Mr Trump has not visited the UK since he was elected to the White House in November 2016 , with a potential visit to London to open the US Embassy in January not taking place . <p> The Daily Mail report a visit from Mr Trump has been " pencilled in " for July , while the Daily Telegraph quoted an unnamed US official saying the president was looking at coming in the " late summer " . <p> Mr Trump cancelled a visit to the UK to open the new American embassy , criticising its move from Grosvenor Square in the prestigious Mayfair district of central London to an " off location " at Nine Elms , south of the Thames . <p> The president blamed the cost of the new embassy and its location south of the River Thames , saying it was a " bad deal " . <p> Theresa May was one of the first foreign premiers to visit the US president ( AP ) <p> His cancellation prompted media speculation that reasons for the snub included that Mr Trump had been offended by perceived slights against him by UK public figures . <p> Foreign Secretary Boris Johnson accused London Mayor Sadiq Khan of endangering the so-called " special relationship " after he said president had " got the message " from Londoners and would have been met by " mass peaceful protests " if he went ahead with the visit . <p> The president has endured a turbulent relationship with Mrs May since taking office , with the Prime Minister publicly criticising statements he has made on Muslims , terrorism and climate change . <p> However Mr Trump said he was grateful for the support from the UK and France when a coalition of the three countries conducted targeted air strikes in Syria last weekend . <h> Ipsoregulated <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
