<h> A grandfather cooking with his son , Hindu worshippers breaking their fast , and DOGS being reared for meat : Prestigious food photography prize pays tribute to the beauty , rituals and politics around how we eat <p> The Pink Lady Photographer of the Year awards were held on Tuesday night in central London <p> These stunning images showing how beautiful food can be have all won prizes in different categories <p> The overall winner was Praying for Food by Noor Ahmed Gelal , of Bangladesh <p> Winners were as young as under 10 and the competition received thousands of entries <p> A bird 's eye view of Hindu worshippers eating after fasting all day , a chef skillfully making his artisan pies and two elderly men making dinner together : these are the subjects of some of the most stunning food photographs of the year . <p> Captured in all four corners of the world , these mesmerising pictures reveal how and what we eat across the globe , and how beautiful it can be . <p> The images will make you pause to think as well as salivate , with some raising awareness of crucial issues affecting the food industry such as the the treatment of animals reared for meat , and how climate change is threatening the livelihood of fishermen . <p> The beautiful pictures have all won honours at this year 's Pink Lady Food Photographer of the Year awards , which was held on Tuesday night in central London . <p> The annual competition had thousands of entries from photographers of all ages based in 60 countries , which was whittled down to under 30 winning images . <p> Noor Ahmed Gelal , of Bangladesh , won the overall prize of Pink Lady Food Photographer of the Year and in the Food for Celebration category for Praying for Food , this simply stunning image of a Hindu congregation breaking their day-long fast in one of the local temples at Swamibag , Dhaka , Bangladesh . They believe their fasting will redeem their sins <p> British photographer John Carey won the The Philip Harben Award for Food in Action prize for his picture , Calum in his pie room , which shows Calum Franklin , an executive head chef in his wonderful Pie Room at Holborn Dining Rooms in the Rosewood Hotel , London <p> Romanian youngster Stefan Dedu won in the 11-14 age category for his photo , Fresh Chicken Eggs , which have been laid by his family 's hens <p> British photographer Guillaume Flandre won the Food for the Family prize . He said of the photo : ' This is the last time my father and grandfather cooked together in his apartment before my grandfather passed away . I captured this scene on Christmas Eve , a moment when , traditionally , the whole family would gather to share a meal ' <p> American photographer Linda Taylor won the Marks &amp; Spencer Food Portraiture award for this mouthwatering image of small pears from her own tree which were used to create a delicious brioche pastry dessert by a local baker <p> British snapper Aniko Lueff won the Food Bloggers prize for this delicious-looking picture of honeycomb ( left ) . Tom Parker , of the UK , won the Intercontinental London Park Lane Food at the Table for his image , Fine Dining Columbia , of the executive chef of the famous 1621 restaurant at the Santa Clara Hotel in Cartagena , Colombia ( right ) <p> British youngster William Lindsay-Perez won in the 15-17 age category for his picture Finishing Touches , which shows a sprinkle of cocoa powder over a raspberry meringue with a white chocolate rocky road . He said : ' In this image , I wanted to combine rich colour and movement to create an image that looks as good as the dessert tasted ' <p> Probal Rashid took this stunning image of a fisherman cooking on a fishing boat in Bhola , Bangladesh . The Bhola district is located in the southern part of Bangladesh , a country ranked as one of the world 's most vulnerable to climate change . On the Bhola Island , fisher communities live in close proximity to the river where land is cheaper but this location makes them vulnerable to river bank erosion <p> Andy Grimshaw won the One Vision Imaging Cream of the Crop for his stunningly detailed macro image of green beans , which look almost abstract <p> British photographer Jade Nina Sarkhel won the Food for Sale prize for her image Rex Bakery - a bakery that has bullet holes in the wall . The bakery churns out 18,000 paos a day , operating 24/7 and selling bread through the keyhole counter overnight . Bread is given for free to those who ca n't afford it . Jade said : ' It 's places like this that knit communities together in India ' <p> We 'd like some of those ! German photographer Oliver Hauser won the Production Paradise Food Off the Press prize for his image , Schnitzelberg , of the classic dish Wiener Schnitzel <p> Polish photographer Grzegorz Tomasz Karmas won the Politics of Food prize for this upsetting image of caged dogs reared for meat in Vietnam being sprayed with a hose . He said : ' The dogs are kept alive by being showered during transport to the slaughter houses . It is around 45 degrees out there ' <p> British snapper Paul Steven won the On the Phone ( in aid of Action Against Hunger ) prize for snapping this beautiful picture of Highland cows on Exmoor in Somerset <p> Becci Hutchings won Student Photographer of the Year for her image , Honeycomb and Wax . She explained : ' Frames are placed into the bee hives to encourage bees to build honeycomb and fill with honey . Once filled , the frames are removed and the wax capping is scraped off using an uncapping fork . The honeycomb is then either broken off or the frames are placed into an extractor to withdraw the honey ' <p> Indian photographer Debdatta Chakraborty won the Bring In The Harvest prize for her beautiful image , The Fishermen . She explained : ' Silabati is a perennial river , only getting inundated when water is released from the nearby dam , Medinipur , West Bengal .
