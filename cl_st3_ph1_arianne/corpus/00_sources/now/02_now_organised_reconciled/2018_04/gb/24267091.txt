natural world , to the complexities of experimental physics , are all covered in captivating detail . <p> Here are seven that you simply must watch : <h> Planet Earth <p> Not only is Planet Earth the BBC Natural History Unit 's crowning glory , it 's also arguably the pinnacle of nature documentaries in general . <p> The show delivers incredible footage of creatures great and small from across the globe , and all in glorious high definition . <p> With peerless narration from Sir David Attenborough , it 's a stunning feat of documentary making , filled with amazing visuals and countless stunning insights . <p> Advertisement <p> Advertisement <h> The Blue Planet <p> Another gem from the BBC here , this time focusing on the incredible array of marine life in our oceans . <p> As with Planet Earth , the winning combination of eye-catching footage , genuinely fascinating details and the soothing tones of Sir David Attenborough make for compelling viewing . <p> It 's a fascinating look at a part of our world we so rarely get to see up close . <h> Cosmos <p> A spin-off from the popular Carl Sagan TV series of old , Neil deGrasse Tyson leads us on a fascinating voyage through the known universe . <p> Bold concepts and complex scientific theories are simplified and put into terms we can all understand . <p> Light speed , life on other planets and the composition of stars are just a few of the topics Cosmos seeks to enlighten us on . <h> Chasing Ice <p> This eye-opening documentary comes courtesy of documentary photographer James Balog . <p> Balog attempts to show the extent of climate change by mapping the conditions of glaciers as part of his ' Extreme Ice Survey ' . <p> His shocking findings should provide a timely wake-up call -- Balog himself was once sceptical of climate change , but tellingly this mission was enough to change his mind . <h> Virunga <p> Virunga follows the work of a group of park rangers in Congo 's Virunga National Park as they battle to protect its wildlife , including the world 's last mountain gorillas , while a civil war rages through the country . <p> The film showcases not only the park 's natural beauty , but also the unenviable task facing these rangers and the incredibly work they do in life-threatening circumstances . <h> Particle Fever <p> This is a documentary that focuses on top scientists at Switzerland 's Large Hadron Collider and the pioneering experimental work which they undertake . <p> It serves as both an accessible window into a complex element of particle physics , and also an insight into the dedicated and passionate people who put so many years of work into these bold and innovative experiments . <h> The Ivory Game <p> The Ivory Game is a powerful and tragic documentary , which attempts at bay makes for gripping viewing . <p> It 's a brilliant piece of investigative journalism , and one which brings home the severity of the situation in emphatic fashion .
