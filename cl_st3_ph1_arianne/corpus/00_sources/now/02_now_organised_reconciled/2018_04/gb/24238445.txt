<h> Apple reveals new robot to take apart and reuse iPhones as part of Earth Day <p> Apple has created a new robot -- not for building products , but for ripping iPhones apart . <p> The robot , named Daisy , can take nine different iPhones models apart and extract the important parts of them , in ways traditional recyclers can not . They can then be used all over again , helping to cut wastage out of the process of making phones . <p> The new announcement is part of Apple 's broad plans for Earth Day , the event held on 22 April each year to mark green efforts .
