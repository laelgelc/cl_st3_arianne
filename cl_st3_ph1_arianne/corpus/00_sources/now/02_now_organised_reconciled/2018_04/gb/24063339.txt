<h> Can Lake Chad Be Saved ? <p> Once the fourth largest lake in Africa , Lake Chad has been shrinking rapidly over the last few decades . In the 1960s , the lake covered up to 10,000 square miles -- today , its extent is a mere fraction of that , at an estimated 520 square miles . <p> The majority of that shrinkage came prior to the 80s , through the effects of an exploding population , climate change and uncontrolled irrigation . At the time , an Italian company suggested an elaborate restoration scheme which was shunned back then . Now , it may finally come to fruition . <h> A critical situation <p> Over the last 40 years , it 's believed that Lake Chad may have lost up to 95% of its surface area .
