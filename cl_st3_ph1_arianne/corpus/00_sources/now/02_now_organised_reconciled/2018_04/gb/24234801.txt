If we continue with business-as-usual emissions , then I do n't think the reef can survive . " <p> The study added the that the death of corals after bleaching represents a fundamental challenge to the long-term future of the Great Barrier Reef . <p> " The coral die-off has caused radical changes in the mix of coral species on hundreds of individual reefs where mature and diverse reef communities are being transformed into more degraded systems , with just a few tough species remaining , " said Andrew Baird , another of the report 's authors .
