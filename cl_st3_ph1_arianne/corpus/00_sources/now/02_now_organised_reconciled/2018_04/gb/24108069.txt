<h> New web maps tell full story of climate change Featured <p> New interactive maps combine precipitation and temperature to show climate change in more detail , and can be used to compare climates worldwide <p> Climate change maps usually fall into one of two categories : maps that show changes in precipitation , and maps that show changes in temperature . Unfortunately , focusing on one of these factors only tells half of the story . For example , a map of global temperature increase would show a warming Arctic , but it would not show the decrease in rainfall predicted to impact West Africa . Now , researchers at the University of Cincinnati have created a map that shows both . <p> ' Our goal was to create a web-based app that is relatively easy to use by everyone , ' says Tomasz Stepinski , geography and geospatial professor at the university . ' When people think about climate change , they think about temperature -- global warming . But climate has many components , including precipitation ' .
