<h> Clash of the podcasts as former leaders refuse to go quietly <p> Sir Nick Clegg has launched an eight-part series that features an interview with Nigel Farage <p> After taking a kicking from voters , politicians used to take the hint and adopt a period of silence . Now they get a podcast . <p> Sir Nick Clegg has joined the former leaders speaking from beyond the political grave , and has renewed hostilities with Ed Miliband , although this time it 's over their position in the podcast charts . They are jostling with Nigel Farage for the ears of the nation . <p> Mr Miliband was the first former party leader to launch a podcast , with his friend and radio DJ Geoff Lloyd . Reasons To Be Cheerful began last September . <p> Featuring interviews with " smart thinkers from around the world " , it has tackled climate change , surveillance , wealth , homelessness and trade unions . Each episode gets about 70,000 to 80,000 downloads.
