<p> Dr Sam Gardner , WWF Scotland 's acting director said : " Renewables have provided an incredible amount of power during the first three months of this year . An increase of 44 per cent on the record-breaking equivalent period in 2017 is clear evidence the investment made in this technology has paid off for the economy and the environment , putting Scotland at the forefront of the fight against climate change . <p> " However , if Scotland 's full renewables potential is to be unleashed to power our economy , heat our homes and charge our cars , then the UK Government needs to stop excluding the cheapest forms of power , like onshore wind and solar , from the market .
