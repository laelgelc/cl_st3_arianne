However she added Asia was behind as it dealt with the relatively new expectations of offshore investors in their markets . <p> " In Asia it 's quite different because a number of companies have block shareholders - either family or state . We 're at very early stages of engaging with a number of companies on these issues . " <p> " Having said that , there is a lot of innovation around environmental issues , " Bennett said .
