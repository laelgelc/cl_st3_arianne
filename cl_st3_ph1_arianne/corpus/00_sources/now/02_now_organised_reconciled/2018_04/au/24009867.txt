<p> Southern right whales journey thousands of kilometres from their sub-Antarctic feeding grounds to the Head of Bight , South Australia , to give birth . <p> The right whales stay for about three months to fatten their calves before returning to Antarctica . <p> For about four months they do not eat and rely solely on their fat stores . <p> Here 's drone footage , taken by Christiansen at the Murdoch University Cetacean Research Unit : <p> " We quantified the cost of early calf growth for the mothers over a three-month breeding season by comparing the relationships between calf growth rate and the rate of loss in maternal body volumes , " says Dr Christiansen . <p> Southern right whales give birth to offspring about 5 metres long , or one-third the size of the mother when born . They double in size by the time they are weaned three months later . <p> " Calves grow really fast during the first months of their life and so there is a considerable energetic cost to the mother during lactation , since she is not feeding during this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " says Dr Christiansen . <p> A mother with its calf : <p> Lactating females lose an average of 25% of their body volume in the first three months while the calf grows by an average of 3.2 cm in length each day . <p> " The study shows the considerable energetic cost that females face during lactation and highlights the importance of having sufficient maternal energy reserves to reproduce . " <p> It costs a mother around 126 litres of volume per day to feed their calf and support their own metabolic needs . At the same time the calf grows on average about 80 litres in volume daily . <p> Longer and more round females invest more energy in their calves compared to shorter and leaner females . <p> However , a big female in poor condition can invest the same energy as a small female in superb condition so human disturbance of a small female may have a bigger negative impact on reproduction . <p> A drone close up : <p> Dr Christiansen says the findings provide important baseline information about the body condition of southern right @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " By knowing the cost of reproduction for southern right whale females , we can now use drones to monitor the body condition of this population between years to see if females have sufficient energy reserves to successfully wean their calf , " he says . <p> Other factors such as shipping , oil and gas development and climate change can all negatively affect the body condition of whales . <p> The drone working at the Head of the Bight : <p> The DJI Inspire 1 Pro multirotor unmanned aerial vehicle ( UAV ) used to measure the body condition of southern right whales .
