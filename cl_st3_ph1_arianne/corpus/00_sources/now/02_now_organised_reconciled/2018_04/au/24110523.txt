" <p> Currently , individual sectors are each working toward a 26 to 28 per cent reduction to achieve the same figure nationally . <p> ACT Greens leader and Climate Change Minister Shane Rattenbury said that is a concern . <p> " We do n't think that Australia can meet its Paris targets under this proposal because it does n't extract enough emissions reductions from the energy sector , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that it 's a cheaper place to do it than say the agriculture sector or the transport sector , so that 's certainly an issue .
