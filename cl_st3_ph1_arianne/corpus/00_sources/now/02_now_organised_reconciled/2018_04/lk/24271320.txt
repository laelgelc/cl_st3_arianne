Speaking further , President Sirisena said Sri Lanka extended its support to the ... <h> President Sirisena affirms SL 's commitment to PCA , SDA <p> London Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting 2018 <p> View(s) : <p> Sri Lanka remains firmly committed to the full implementation of the Paris Climate Agreement ( PCA ) and the 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda ( SDA ) , said President Maithripala Sirisena said , when he addressed the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting ( CHOGM ) session in London on Thursday ( 19 ) . <p> Speaking further , President Sirisena said Sri Lanka extended its support to the Clean Ocean Alliance , proposed by the United Kingdom ( UK ) , and this was part of the country 's commitment towards this end . He pointed out that Sri Lanka joined the international community in similar endeavours , including Climate Change . <p> He also welcomed adoption of the ' Commonwealth Blue Charter ' in joint efforts to ensure the Oceans are a shared responsibility and the common heritage of mankind . <p> He added , Sri Lanka was pleased to have the opportunity to serve as a ' Blue Charter Champion , ' and that the charter , along with the PCA and the SDA , are " tools to promote a sustainable future . " <p> The inaugural ceremony of the 25th CHOGM 2018 was held under the patronage of Queen Elizabeth II at Buckingham Palace on Thursday ( 19 ) . <p> On Wednesday ( 18 ) , President Sirisena delivered the keynote address to the Commonwealth Business Forum . <p> Among the other engagements during his visit to the UK , President Sirisena met with UK Secretary of State for International Trade , Liam Fox , who stated that , his Government is ready for direct investments in Sri Lanka , while increasing its investments in the country . He further said that the necessary process in this regard will be implemented in the near future . <p> The President also met with Lord Naseby , a member of the UK 's House of Lords , and thanked him for supporting Sri Lanka in the UK Parliament . <p> On Thursday , the President attended a special banquet hosted by Queen Elizabeth II in honour of leaders attending CHOGM . <p> While in the UK , President Sirisena met with members of the Sri Lankan community in London . In his address to the community , the President claimed that certain media and social media platforms are attempting to highlight falsehoods and suppress the truth , to gain narrow political advantage and commercial benefits . <p> On Friday , the President was the Chief Guest at the opening of the Commonwealth Centre for Digital Health . <p> President Sirisena arrived in London on April 16 and , as part of the New Year rituals , the President participated in the Oil Anointing Ceremony held at the London Buddhist Vihara , soon after his arrival .
