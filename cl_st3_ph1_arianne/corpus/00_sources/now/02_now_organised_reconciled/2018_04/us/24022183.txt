<p> The global shift from fossil fuels to renewables is being undermined by the very organisation that ought to be leading the charge , according to a scathing new critique of the International Energy Agency ( IEA ) . <p> Governments across the world rely on IEA projections to set energy policies , but the agency 's figures -- which are influenced by the oil industry -- are pushing them off track to reach the targets of the Paris climate agreement , says the report . <p> In its annual World Energy Outlook , the IEA provides a series of possible scenarios , each of which is dependent on the decisions taken by governments .
