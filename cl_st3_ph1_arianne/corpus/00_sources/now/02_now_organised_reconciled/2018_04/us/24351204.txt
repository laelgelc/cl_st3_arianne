<h> Macron believes Trump will pull out of Iran deal <h> By Euronews <p> Emmanuel Macron says he expects Donald Trump to pull out of the international deal designed to prevent Iran acquiring nuclear weapons . <p> A Question and Answer session with students at George Washington University wrapped up the French president 's three day visit and he voiced his doubts over Iran at a final press conference before returning to France . <p> " I do n't know what the American decision will be , " he said . " But the rational analysis of all President Trump 's statements does n't lead me to believe that he will do everything to stay in the JCPOA " . <p> After what had been called a " bromance " between the two Presidents during the first two days of the visit , Macron 's address to the joint houses of Congress was highly critical of Trump 's position on climate change and international trade .
