She can be reached at shannonw481@gmail.com . <p> Dappled light filters through a grove of coast redwoods , and only the occasional chatter of a Pacific wren breaks the silence . <p> Words fail to describe the enormity of these ancient trees , which can live 2,000 years . <p> Michael and I recently visited several California parks that preserve about 4 percent of the redwoods ' pre-European-American range . <p> Once thought to cover about 2 million acres , coast redwoods now reside on fewer than 90,000 acres . We are fortunate to have even this fraction of trees , thanks to a small group of determined people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> One of the largest and oldest is aptly called " Giant Tree , " located in Humboldt Redwoods State Park , which was named after the great 19th-century scientist , Alexander von Humboldt . <p> The Giant rockets more than 363 feet skyward with a circumference of more than 53 feet . The average width of its crown is 62 feet . <p> Redwoods are the tallest trees on the planet . Both the giant sequoia and bristlecone pine are older and the sequoia is larger in diameter , but for grace , it 's tough to beat the redwood . <p> To reach the Giant , summer visitors can take a short walk across a footbridge , but in early spring , the bridges are not yet installed . The day we were there , younger visitors were walking across a downed redwood , but Michael and I thought it might be prudent to take a 3-mile round-trip hike to reach this mighty tree . Falling into the creek was not a good option . <p> We had the trail to ourselves through Rockefeller Woods , the largest @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ spectacular place . <p> I was devastated to think that loggers would have clear-cut every one of these massive trees without the foresight of a handful of conservationists . We need these trees , just like we need every other member in our family of life . <p> Now , the remaining trees may be threatened by climate change . The increase in average temperature and decrease in thick summer fog and rain endanger the coast redwoods and all the other plants and animals that depend on them . <p> Ironically , giant trees are some of the greatest defenses against climate change because they sequester large amounts of carbon dioxide . <p> Back in Lynchburg , I was reminded of our own pending loss of ash trees , not from climate change , but because of a tiny Asian invader called the emerald ash borer , accidentally shipped to the U.S. by humans . <p> Those who would like to try to save their ash trees may want to attend a workshop on April 26 from 1 to 4 p.m. at Miller Park on " Managing emerald ash borer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Space is limited to 25 participants and pre-registration is required at $5 per person or $10 per household for materials and refreshments .
