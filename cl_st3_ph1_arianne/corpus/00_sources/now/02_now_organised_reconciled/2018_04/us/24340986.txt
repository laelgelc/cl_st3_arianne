" By polluting the oceans , not mitigating CO2 emissions , and destroying our biodiversity , we are killing our planet . Let us face it , there is no planet B. " <p> The comments - which prompted a standing ovation on the Democratic side of the aisle - stand in stark contrast to Trump 's environmental agenda since he took office . The US President last year set in motion plans to pull out of the global Paris climate treaty , while his adminstration is seeking to roll back Obama-era pollution controls and mount a major political offensive to ramp up America 's fossil fuel industry . <p> But Macron issued a thinly veiled critique of Trump 's decision to prioritise the jobs of coal miners over the health of the Earth 's atmosphere . <p> " Some people think that securing current industries and their jobs is more urgent than transforming our economies to meet the global challenge of climate change , " he said . " I hear those concerns . But we must find a smooth transition to a low-carbon economy . Because what is the meaning of our life , really , if we work and live destroying the planet while sacrificing the future for our children ? What is the meaning of our life if our conscious decision is to reduce the opportunities for our children or grandchildren ? " <p> While Trump has repeatedly said he would be open to rejoining the Paris Agreement if America was offered a " better deal " , he has given no indication of the type of climate action he would propose in the US , or indeed any sign that he accepts ambitious action to tackle climate change is needed . <p> But Macron expressed hope the US would re-enter the international fold on the issue . " I 'm sure , one day the United States will come back and join the Paris Agreement , " he said . " And I 'm sure we can work together to fulfill with you the ambitions of the global compact on the environment . " <p> Led by French environment minister Nicolas Hulot , the communique was also signed by climate and environment ministers from Sweden , Portugal , Finland , Germany , the Netherlands , and Luxembourg . <p> The call comes days before negotiators meet for interim UN climate talks in Bonn , as they seek to make further progress towards finalising the ' rulebook ' for the Paris Agreement and take stock of collective action to date . <p> But any changes to the EU 's climate targets will depend on winning the support of the remaining 20 EU states , including coal dependant nations such as Poland , which have resisted more ambitious targets in the past .
