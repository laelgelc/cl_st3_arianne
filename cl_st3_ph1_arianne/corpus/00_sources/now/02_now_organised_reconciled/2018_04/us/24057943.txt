<p> Spring has been sluggish this year , fighting to get out from under the shroud of winter . Here in New York , winter was very much present on the official first day of spring -- a nor'easter that swept in to dump just over a foot of snow before swanning out dramatically . Even knowing climate change is real was no consolation : as I crunched through the snow , face wrapped in a scarf and head down against the wind , it felt spiteful . <p> But now , mere days later , the sunshine feels a little less weedy . The other day I left my hat at home and went so far as to expose my ears when I wore my hair up . Lent is over ; return to your damaging vices , if you must , but for me spring means possibility and renewal . If you , too , have had a rather rough first quarter , you will , like me , be greeting the onward march of the seasons with open arms .
