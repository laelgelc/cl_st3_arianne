California 's resistance has extra weight because it involves 13 other states -- mostly in the Northeast and Northwest -- which adopted the California rules . Together , these states account for nearly 40 percent of U.S. car and truck sales . <p> Thus there could be two sets of rules : One for California and its allied states and another for the rest of the country .
