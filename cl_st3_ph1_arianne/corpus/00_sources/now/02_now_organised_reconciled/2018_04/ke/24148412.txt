<h> New Zealand to ban new offshore oil drilling over climate change <p> New Zealand has said it will ban new permits for offshore oil exploration as it attempts to move towards a low-carbon future . <p> The announcement has been lauded by climate groups but industry groups have vociferously opposed such a move . <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern said it would help " protect future generations from climate change " . <p> The move will not affect 22 existing permits , which could even extend to mining permits in the decades ahead . <p> Ardern , who was voted in as New Zealand 's leader last year in a tight race , campaigned heavily on climate change prevention and says the decision is a responsible step forward . <p> " Transitions have to start somewhere and unless we make decisions today that will essentially take effect in 30 or more years ' time , we run the risk of acting too late and causing abrupt shocks to communities and our country , " Ardern said on Thursday . <p> " We have been a world leader on critical @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we could be world leading in becoming carbon neutral . " <p> But industry groups and some politicians have slammed the move , saying it will hurt local jobs , local businesses , and the nation 's economy . <p> New Zealand 's $186.4bn ( ? 130.9bn ) economy relies most heavily on agriculture , together with manufacturing and tourism , but it is estimated that the oil and gas industry contributes about $1.84bn ; ( ? 1.29bn ) every year . <p> New Zealand Oil and Gas , a leading energy firm , said it had not been warned of the move and that it was a sudden change of policy for the centre-left Labour government . <p> Ardern 's government is backed by the country 's Green Party , a tie-up some analysts say is less than business friendly .
