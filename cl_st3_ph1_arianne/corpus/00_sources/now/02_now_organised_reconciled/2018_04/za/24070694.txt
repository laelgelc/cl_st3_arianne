It follows US President Donald Trump 's campaign promise of more intense vetting of foreigners in the quest for better security . <p> The latest proposal comes six months after the Trump administration first announced that applicants for immigrant visas would be asked for social media accounts . <p> This means that 14 million people who apply for a US visa each year will be subjected to the new rule .
