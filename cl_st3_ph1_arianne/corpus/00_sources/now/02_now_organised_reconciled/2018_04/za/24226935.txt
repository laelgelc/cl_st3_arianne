<h> This is what 's on the agenda for Sustainability Week 2018 <p> This year 's Sustainability Week , set for 5-7 June at the CSIR International Convention Centre in Tshwane , will cover sustainable development in Africa at various scales , from Africa 's capital cities to the people who sustainable development affects . <h> Africa 's capital cities heed the call <p> From Cairo to Kampala , from Windhoek to Ougadougou , the mayors of Africa 's capital cities are coming to call . The African Capital Cities Sustainability Forum ( ACCSF ) , to be held on day one of Sustainability Week , will see a growing network of mayors gather to share how they are responding to the numerous challenges that are threatening growth and development in African cities . These threats include rapid urbanisation , energy and water access and stresses , sanitation , the global economic slowdown , rising unemployment and social inequities , trade facilitation , connectivity , land and biodiversity degradation , amongst others , not to mention the significant and growing impacts of climate change . <p> Since 2009 , Germany @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Potentials in Sustainable Development , honouring 25 young researchers every year ... <p> 13 Apr 2018 <h> Sustainable Cities Africa <p> Drawing on the insights derived from the ACCSF , the second day will be about how to deal with these threats . Sustainable Cities Africa will present technical solutions to the challenges faced by cities tasked with leading pro-poor , climate-resilient social and economic transformation . <p> The first session , Financing the city , is about the relationship between responsible leadership and project funding . Making cities safe and sustainable means ensuring access to safe and affordable housing , and upgrading slum settlements , as well as investing in public transport , creating green public spaces , and improving urban planning and management in a way that is both participatory and inclusive .
