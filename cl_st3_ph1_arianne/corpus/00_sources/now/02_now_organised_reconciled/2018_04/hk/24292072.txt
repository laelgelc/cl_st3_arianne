<h> EIB to launch new sustainability bond <h> The European Investment Bank has announced plans for a new debt product : a Sustainability Awareness Bond <p> By The Asset <p> The European Investment Bank ( EIB ) has announced plans for a new debt product : a Sustainability Awareness Bond . The bond is expected to support social , green and sustainable projects . <p> " With our new Sustainability Awareness Bond we hope also to launch a new dialogue on sustainable investment , " says EIB President Werner Hoyer in a statement . <p> The new Sustainability Awareness Bond , currently being prepared by the Finance and Projects Directorates of the EIB , will initially focus on activities in the water sector , and then encompass activities in other areas with positive social impacts , such as health and education . <p> " We want to discuss how we can contribute to raising the trillions that we know are needed to tackle poverty , raise living standards and combat disease around the world . This is also a debate about impact , transparency , and reaching out to new investors , " says Hoyer . <p> " Green Bonds have created a new way to connect investors with green assets and put the issue of green investment at the heart of the climate change agenda globally . Capital market engagement has definitely helped boost the prominence of environmental matters in the policy arena , " Hoyer adds . <p> The bank launched the world 's first green bond in 2007 , and remains one of the most active green bond issuers . <p> EIB says activities in the water sector were selected at first as the EIB has considerable experience in the sector . <p> ? The first issuance of the new Sustainability Awareness Bond is expected to take place this autumn , according ot the statement . While the EIB 's Green Bonds ( or Climate Awareness Bonds , CABs ) focus on climate action-related investment , the new product will have a wider scope . It will however maintain the clarity of criteria , definitions and reporting requirements that have contributed to the success of the CABs . <p> The United Nations estimates that around US$6 trillion will be needed annually over 15 years to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals .
