<h> City councilor : No closure for Baguio <h> City councilor : No closure for Baguio <p> BAGUIO City Councilor Elmer Datuin assured that the city will not suffer the same fate as Boracay . <p> " We do not see that happening , " assured the councilor . <p> Datuin brushed off speculation of a closure to be imposed on the city like the beach haven saying President Rodrigo Duterte will not do that to the city . <p> The lawmaker said the strong immigration of the city has contributed to the growth in population but stressed , the city has been augmenting the growing needs of the mountain resort . <p> Writer Mary Ann Ll .
