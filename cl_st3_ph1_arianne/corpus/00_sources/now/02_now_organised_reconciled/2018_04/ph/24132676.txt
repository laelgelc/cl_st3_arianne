<h> Pampanga braces for regional Gawad Kalasag award anew <h> Pampanga braces for regional Gawad Kalasag award anew <p> Thursday , April 12 , 2018 <p> PAMPANGA . The Provincial Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council together with Vice-Governor Dennis Pineda join the validation team for the Regional Gawad Kalasag Awards at Capitol on Friday . ( Pampanga PIO ) <p> Gawad Kalasag is an annual search for the outstanding organizations , institutions , groups , and individuals who have exemplary measures and preparations in disaster prevention and response as well as climate change adaptation programs . <p> The Regional Gawad Kalasag validation team visited the Provincial Capitol and reviewed the best practices of the provinces provided by the PDRRMO headed by Angelina Blanco . <p> The team was composed of Michael Dumlao from Office of Civil Defense III , Glenn Cosio from the Department of Interior and Local Government , Muffy Gopez from National Economic Development Authority , Estrella Salta from the Department of Health , and Carlo Lorenzo Datu from Philippine Information Agency . <p> Provincial Administrator Andres S. Pangilinan Jr . welcomed the validation team as well @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that it is the nature of Kapampangan 's resiliency that has made the province survive various calamities . <p> He added that the provincial government is very proud of the yearly nomination of Pampanga in the Gawad Kalasag award . <p> " We are proud that we have been nominated for our efforts and now we are vying for our fifth award , as well as the national award if God allows it , " he said . <p> Vice-Governor Dennis " Delta " Pineda said it is a fitting reward for the efforts laid by the provincial government in preventing and responding to various disasters and situations . <p> " This is just a bonus if we win the award . We are always ready for any disaster or calamity and we will never get tired of serving people , " he added . <p> The Province of Pampanga already bagged the regional Gawad Kalasag award since 2013 up to 2017 .
