<h> Union Environment Ministry releases the draft of National Clean Air Programme <h> Rupali Pruthi <p> Apr 18 , 2018 14:29 IST <p> Union Environment Ministry releases the draft of National Clean Air Programme <p> The Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change on April 17 , 2018 released the draft of the National Clean Air Programme ( NCAP ) . The ministry has put up the draft on its website and invited comments from various stakeholders by May 17 , 2018 . <p> The NCAP is a national action plan that proposes multiple strategies to reduce air pollution.Highlights of the National Clean Air Programme <p> * NCAP aims to enhance and develop an effective and a proficient network to monitor the ambient air quality across the country for a comprehensive and reliable database . <p> * The plan calls for an efficient data distribution and public outreach mechanism for timely measures to prevent and mitigate air pollution . <p> * It also aims to have a feasible management plan for prevention , control and abatement of air pollution . <p> Video : Check out the latest current @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ measures to bring down air pollution <p> The Ministry has detailed a host of measures to bring down air pollution under the NCAP and few of these are : <p> * Air quality monitoring network <p> * Identification of alternative technology for real-time monitoring <p> * Setting up of 10 city super network <p> * Indoor air pollution monitoring and management <p> * Air pollution health impact studies <p> * Air quality forecasting system <p> * Issuance of notification on dust management <p> * Three-tier mechanism for review , assessment and inspection for implementation of the plan <p> * National emission inventory <p> However , this new plan lacks the mention of targets of achieving absolute pollution reduction of 35 per cent in three years and 50 per cent in five years . Earlier , the environment ministry announced that it hopes to bring down air pollution in around 100 non-attainment cities by 50 per cent in the next five years under the NCAP . <p> Non-attainment cities are those which have air quality worse than the National Ambient Air Quality Standards .
