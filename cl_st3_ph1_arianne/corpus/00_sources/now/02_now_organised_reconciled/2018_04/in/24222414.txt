<h> On an adventure of a lifetime <p> THE HANS INDIA Apr 19,2018 , 04:22 AM IST <p> RJ Prathyusha Parakala becomes the first woman from TS &amp; AP to go on an expedition to Antarctica <p> RJ Prathyusha Parakala , is on cloud 9 these days after completing her maiden voyage to Antarctica as part of ClimateForce : Antarctica ( CFA ) expedition 2018 along with around 80 participants from across the globe to raise awareness about climate change and work towards creating a more sustainable future . <p> The expedition commenced on February 26 and concluded on March 12 . The 29-year-old was the only girl selected from the two Telugu states and only RJ ever to be part of such expedition in the world . For the journey , she took a flight to Argentina and reached Ushuaia , the tip of the country from there the team commenced their expedition to Antarctica . <p> " I have a Masters degree in computer software and was placed in three software companies , however , I felt that it is not my calling and I joined ' Teach @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an under-resource course .
