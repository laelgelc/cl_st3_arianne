compensatory afforestation fund , funds for wood-based industries , environment cess and those collected in illegal mining cases . <p> A bench headed by Justice Madan B Lokur was informed about the Secretary in the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change to make a compilation of all the funds and the amount lying in each of those funds as on March 31 , 2018 . <p> The Secretary in
