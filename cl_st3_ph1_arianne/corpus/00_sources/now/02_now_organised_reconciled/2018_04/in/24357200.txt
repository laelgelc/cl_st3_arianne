<h> Climate change biggest challenge for farmers : Desarada <p> Chaitanya Deshpande <p> TNN Apr 26 , 2018 , 22:00 IST <p> BOX in P/Common/Nashik Nashik : Noted agriculture economist and former member of State Planning Commission H M Desarada claimed that farmers should understand the impact of climate change on agriculture activities and must adopt alterations in farming techniques to cope up with the issue . <p> Desarada was addressing a valedictory function of Agriculture Extension Service Providers ( AESP ) course held at Yashwantrao Chavan Maharashtra Open University ( YCMOU ) on Wednesday . <p> A total of 30 youths from rural and tribal areas successfully completed this month-long training . The youths will travel across the district , meet farmers and provide them need and demand-based knowledge in agronomic techniques and skills in a systematic manner . <p> Krishi Vidnyan Kendra ( KVK ) of YCMOU conducted this crash course and trained the youngsters in interactive activity . " While adopting modern techniques , the young AESPs should be able to convince farmers that only high production should not be their aim . They should concentrate on maintaining the quality of both food and soil , " Desarada said . <p> Nitin Thokey , who conducted the course , expressed hopes that the trained youths will make a difference . " The AESPs will inform about latest technological developments to the village farmers . They will convince the farmers to adopt technology and share useful and practical information related to agriculture .
