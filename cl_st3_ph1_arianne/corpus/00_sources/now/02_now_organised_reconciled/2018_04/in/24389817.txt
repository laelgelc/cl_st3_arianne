<p> India and China at the informal summit between PM Narendra Modi and Chinese President Xi Jinping in major decisions have decided to launch joint economic project in war-torn Afghanistan in what would be first ever Sino-Indian joint initiative in a third country besides agreeing for Strategic Guidance to their respective militaries for stability along the over 4,000-km-long Line of Actual Control that witnessed Doklam Standoff last year . <p> These were biggest takeaways from the Modi-Xi Summit was informal summit that was aimed at generating confidence between the Asian giants . The two sides also decided give to momentum to economic partnership and joint global projects in health and disaster management even as Delhi expressed its sensitivities over Belt and Road Initiative , Pak-based terror infrastructure and NSG membership . In an indirect reference to this including BRI Foreign Secretary Vijay Gokhale who was present in the delegation level talks on Friday said , " The two leaders highly assessed the opportunity for direct , free and candid exchange of views offered by the Informal Summit and agreed on the utility of holding more such dialogues in the future . <p> The forward-looking dialogue raised the level of strategic communication about the perspective , priorities and vision that guide their respective policy choices domestically , regionally and globally . It also helped them in forging a common understanding of the future direction of India-China relations built upon mutual respect for each other 's developmental aspirations and prudent management of differences with mutual sensitivity . " <p> The two leaders believed that the simultaneous emergence of India and China as two large economies and major powers with strategic and decisional autonomy , has implications of regional and global significance . They shared the view that peaceful , stable and balanced relations between India and China will be a positive factor for stability amidst current global uncertainties . Modi and Xi also agreed that proper management of the bilateral relationship will be conducive for the development and prosperity of the region , and will create the conditions for the Asian Century . To this end , they decided to strengthen the Closer Development Partnership in a mutually beneficial and sustainable manner , in pursuit of national modernization and greater prosperity for their peoples . <p> India and China in the past had twice held bilateral dialogue on Afghan situation which has been stalled for the past few years even as both sides are part of several multilateral groups on Afghanistan . The two leaders have decided on a Sino-Indian joint economic project in Afghanistan and two nations would work on the finer details later , pointed out a person familiar with the matter . While India and USA collaborate for development project in Afghanistan , Delhi and Moscow have held dialogue on Afghanistan . India and Russia have joint project in Bangladesh ( nuclear power project ) . India and Japan are working on connectivity projects in Africa and Asia eyeing to give alternative to China 's OBOR . <p> Afghan investment is not a new idea and was suggested in April 2010 but reflects on the problems between the two on Pakistan in the last decade , noted China expert Srikanth Kondapalli told ET . It is no secret that Beijing has to take its close ally Pakistan on board before India and China could work jointly on any project in Afghanistan . China depends heavily on Pakistan to deliver on its security challenges in bordering Afghanistan . However , in recent days Beijing has not found many answers to challenges confronting its challenges in the landlocked country . <p> " The two leaders expressed their support for the work of the Special Representatives on the India China Boundary Question and urged them to intensify their efforts to seek a fair , reasonable and mutually acceptable settlement . The two leaders underscored the importance of maintaining peace and tranquility in all areas of the India-China border region in the larger interest of the overall development of bilateral relations . To this end , they issued strategic guidance to their respective militaries to strengthen communication in order to build trust and mutual understanding and enhance predictability and effectiveness in the management of border affairs . The two leaders further directed their militaries to earnestly implement various confidence building measures agreed upon between the two sides , including the principle of mutual and equal security , and strengthen existing institutional arrangements and information sharing mechanisms to prevent incidents in border regions , " Gokhale told reporters in the backdrop of Doklam standoff that impacted ties last year . <p> The two leaders agreed to push forward bilateral trade and investment in a balanced and sustainable manner by taking advantage of complementarities between their two economies . They also discussed ways to promote greater cultural and people-to-people exchanges and agreed to explore establishing new mechanisms in this direction . <p> Prime Minister Modi and President Xi underlined that as two major countries India and China have wider and overlapping regional and global interests . They agreed on their need to strengthen strategic communication through greater consultation on all matters of common interest . They believe that such strategic communication will have a positive influence on enhancing mutual understanding and will contribute to regional and global stability . <p> The two leaders agreed that India and China have separately made major contributions to global peace and prosperity through their respective growth and economic development , and would continue to act as engines for global growth in the future . They reiterated the importance of building an open , multipolar , pluralist and participatory global economic order which will enable all countries to pursue their development and contribute to the elimination of poverty and inequality in all regions of the world . They spoke of their respective efforts to contribute to the regional and global economic development . <p> The two leaders also shared views on their respective foreign policy visions of achieving global prosperity and security . They agreed to jointly contribute in a positive and constructive way in facilitating sustainable solutions for global challenges including climate change , sustainable development , food security etc . They underscored the importance of reform of multilateral financial and political institutions to make them representative and responsive to the needs of developing countries . <p> The two leaders agreed that as two major countries and emerging economies , India and China , given their vast developmental experiences and national capacities , should join hands to take lead in offering innovative and sustainable solutions to challenges faced by humankind in the 21st century . These include combating diseases , coordinating action for disaster risk reduction and mitigation , addressing climate change and ushering digital empowerment . They agreed to pool together their expertise and resources in these areas and create a global network dedicated to these challenges for the larger benefit of humanity . <p> Modi and Xi recognized the common threat posed by terrorism , and reiterated their strong condemnation of and resolute opposition to terrorism in all its forms and manifestations . They committed themselves to cooperate on counter-terrorism.
