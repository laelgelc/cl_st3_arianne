<h> FLOOD GATES of anger &amp; frustration BREAK OPEN <h> 28 Apr 201807:01am IST <h> 28 Apr 201807:01am IST <h> Report byTeam Herald <h> Goans break out of party and other politics , for a common cause , in a throwback of Dec 18 , 2006 ; 4000 strong and counting , sons and daughters of the soil reaffirm support for people-centric Regional Plan ; Issue of honest people planning which languished on the backburner burst forth at the iconic Lohia Maidan on feisty Friday <p> MARGAO : Around 4000 people attending the public meeting held at Lohia Maidan called by the Goencho Awaaz may go down into the history books for being a successful public movement of Goa . The movement now looks to be the trigger for people getting the powers vested in them under the 73rd and 74th Amendment and the demand has been made to make people a part of the RP 2031 . <p> The enthusiasm and the response from the crowd could be felt till the end of the meeting and is going to be a sign of alarm for all the Goan representatives now . The Governments and the representatives have been lambasted by all the speakers for having systematically ignored the voices of the people and left the Lohia Maidan with a pledge of coming back with a much larger crowd if the Government fails . <p> Songs were played , khell tiatrs staged , kantarams were sung at the meeting which saw a lot of youth participation making the entire environment electrifying . <p> At the offset the Convenor of Goencho Awaaz Capt . Viriato Fernandes said : " We are not fond of getting on to the roads . It is the failure of the public representatives who have not done their bit to reach our voices to the assembly . Without baseline data of the villages and areas of Goa the plan was prepared . What is this plan ? Who prepared it ? The 2021 plan still exists " . <p> He further added that the NGOs have done a good job by collecting data and that will put things straight in the future . <p> Ramkrishna Jalmi of the Kul Mundkar Sanghatna alleged that the Government in 2014 changed the Kul Mundkar law by not even honouring the legislature . " 2012 you had 23 , you are reduced to 13 now , you will go down to 3 if you do n't change your ideas regarding the RP , Kul Mundkar law , Coal and river nationalisation " warned Jalmi . <p> Arturo D'souza from Santa Cruz said " We will meet again at Azad Maidan with a bigger agitation ... We do n't want your PDA , ODP , we want one people oriented Regional Plan <p> because this plan has to be done by people not Chief Town Planner . " <p> " Regional plan has to be scrapped land has to be returned back to the original owners " , demanded Abhijit Prabhudessai and Swapnesh Sherlekar of Rainbow Warriors . <p> Central Minister Nitin Gadkari also came in for criticism at the meeting . He was called sycophant of PM Modi and was mocked for his claim of having given Rs 16,000 crores to Goa . " This money has been sent for highway development for Coal transport and not for the Goans " said speakers at Public meet . <p> Olencio Simoes of Goenchea Ramponnkarancho Ekvott , said : " Government is least bothered about people 's dependence on the land and the rivers in Goa while promoting river nationalisation . All 40 MLAs have to resign on these issues and Alina Saldanha has to be first . " <p> The meeting resolved that the people are committed to the welfare of the people of Goa , its future generations , the oppressed and the voiceless and to the sustainable and equitable development of Goa . <p> The people also resolved that the Regional Plan RP 2021 is defunct , since March 19 , 2012 , when the Governor of Goa declared on the floor of the Assembly , in resonance with the demands made by the people of Goa , " My Government will denotify the Regional Plan RP 2021 and a new plan will be prepared in consultation with all stakeholders " . <p> The crowd also resonated that the PDAs and ODPs are against the interests of Goa and Goans and the authorities must be scrapped and the Plans must be denotified so that further speculation with land within urban areas is stopped immediately . <p> The meeting also demanded that the 73rd and 74rd Amendments be implemented fully and incorporated into the TCP Act . <p> They also demanded that all the baseline data regarding Goa , Goans , the impact of past policies and economic activities and future challenges such as climate change on the environment and socio-economics of Goa must be collected by the Government Departments and made available to the Gram Sabhas and Ward Sabhas , who shall verify the data and then prepare the Regional Plan RP 2031 , spatial plans and policies , for the villages and municipal areas of Goa . <p> The meeting also resolved against the coal transportation through Goa not to be allowed under any circumstances , to protect humanity against climate change and to protect the health of the residents of Goa from the disastrous impacts of all pollution due to coal transportation and proposed burning of coal if neighbouring parts of Karnataka , land use changes and impact on fishing communities , rivers and fisheries of Goa . <p> The crowd also was unanimous on the removal of six rivers of Goa included in the Schedule to the National Waterways Act , 2016 immediately , as they conveyed that the inclusion of these rivers is unlawful and anti-people , since these rivers were created by the Gaonkaris of Goa , belong to the local communities , support the lives and livelihoods of tens of thousands of people and the rich biodiversity , ecology and environment .
