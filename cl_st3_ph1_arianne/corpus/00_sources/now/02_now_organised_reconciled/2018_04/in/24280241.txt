Photo : Indranil Bhoumik/Mint <p> Dehradun:Forest cover has increased by 6,778 sq km in the country during the last two years , with Andhra Pradesh , Karnataka and Kerala making a significant contribution , according to the Forest Survey of India ( FSI ) . <p> " The growth in forest cover can be attributed to plantation and conservation activities , both within and outside the recorded forest areas , " FSI director general Saibal Dasgupta told PTI in Dehradun . <p> Improvement in interpretation due to better radiometric resolution of the recent satellite data from Resourcesat-2 also helped in making the observation during 2015-17 , he said in an interview . <p> Dasgupta said Andhra Pradesh , Karnataka and Kerala have contributed to an increase of 2,141 sq km , 1,101 sq km and 1,043 sq km forest area respectively . <p> He said the total forest and tree cover in India , according to the current assessment is 8,02,088 sq km , which is 24.39% of the country 's geographic area . <p> " The present assessment shows that 15 states/UTs have above 33% of the geographical area under forest cover , " said Dasgupta , who also holds the post of additional DG of forest in the Union Ministry of Environment and Forests and Climate Change . <p> Besides , the forest cover in the hill districts of the country was recorded at 2,83,462 sq km , which is 40.22% of the total geographic area of these districts . <p> " The latest assessment shows an increase of 754 sq km in all hill districts of the country , " he said . Likewise , the total forest cover in the tribal districts is 4,21,170 sq km , which is 37.43% of the geographical area of these districts , showing an increase of 86.89 sq km in all the tribal districts of the country , the FSI DG said . <p> Referring to the northeastern region , deputy director FSI , E Vikram , informed that the total forest cover in the NE states was 1,171,306 sq km , which is 65.34% of its geographical area . <p> " The current assessment shows a decrease of forest cover to the extent of 630 sq km in the north eastern region , " Vikram said . <p> He said all altitude zones above 1,000 metres have registered a small decrease in forest cover , while those below 1,000 metres show an increase . Vikram also said that according to a latest official report there has been a net increase of 2,647 sq km in the extent of water bodies over the decade ( 2005-15 ) in general . <p> " All the states/UTs show an increase , except Uttar Pradesh , Haryana , Arunachal Pradesh and Goa , " he said , adding that Maharashtra , Gujarat , Madhya Pradesh , Telangana , Andhra Pradesh , Tamil Nadu and Karnataka show an increase of over 100 sq km over the decade under review . <p> Similarly , mangrove cover has increased by 181 sq km and the bamboo bearing area grew by 1.73 million hectare , he said . Vikram said that going by the present assessment , the total carbon stock in forests was estimated at 7,082 million tonnes , showing an increase of 38 million tonnes over the last assessment in 2015.
