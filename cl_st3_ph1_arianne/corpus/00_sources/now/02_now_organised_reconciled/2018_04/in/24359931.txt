<h> Regulate people movement in forests , directs env ministry <p> The state forest department does n't have data on forest fires for the last two months despite the department going hi-tech <p> Nagpur : The ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) has issued advisory to all the states regulating movement of people inside the forests . Surprisingly , despite existing laws in place against trespassing in forests , MoEFCC directions on April 20 come only after an application filed before the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) , principal bench , Delhi , against death of 10 trekkers charred in forest fire in Kurangani reserve in Theni district of Tamil Nadu on March 11 . <p> The environment ministry has said that the loss of lives is unfortunate and states should prevent such incidents and take appropriate steps and curative measures . The ministry is formulating a National Action Plan on forest fire soon , it informed . <p> The MoEFCC has also asked forest departments to put a system in place to regulate movement of people in forest and activities like trekking , especially during the fire season of February to May . In Maharashtra , several wildlife parks have been opened for trekking without any safety measures . <p> The MoEFCC advisory seems to be a mere formality as there is no accountability for massive forest fires . In Maharashtra alone , last year 31,875 hectares of forest was gutted through 5,844 fire incidents , causing a loss of Rs42 lakh . <p> " The state has failed to learn lessons from the past as more than 20,000 hectares of forest has been destroyed in the last two months ( March-April ) in Gondia , Bhandara , Nagpur , Wardha , Yavatmal , Chandrapur , Gadchiroli , Amravati and other districts , " says Sawan Bahekar of Sustaining Environment &amp; Wildlife Assemblage ( SEWA ) , Gondia . <p> The state forest department does n't even have data on forest fires for the last two months . This is despite the fact that Maharashtra forest department has gone hi-tech . APCCF ( protection ) RS Yadav said , " We have data on forest fires till February only . " <p> " There is no action against staff . Most of these fires were ignited at the behest of tendu patta traders . In Gondia territorial forest at least 6,000 hectares , Melghat 3,000 hectares , Tipeshwar 2,000 hectares of forest was destroyed , " Bahekar said . <p> " In Maharashtra , lack of action against people venturing deep into the forest has led to increasing man-animal conflict and ultimately flagship species like tigers and leopards have to pay the price . In Pandharkawda , where shoot orders for tigress were issued , at least 10 people have died in conflict .
