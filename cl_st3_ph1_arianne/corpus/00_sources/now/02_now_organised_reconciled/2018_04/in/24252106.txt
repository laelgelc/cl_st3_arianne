( Image Credit : Wikimedia ) <p> United Nations aims to create a global culture of sustainable development and to achieve the desired goal , UN has set an agenda for achieving SDG through the drafting of measurable targets . <p> There are total 17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) which UN wants to achieve by the end of 2030 . <p> The sustainable development agenda 2030 desire to create a platform where the government , business , and civil society will come together and contribute to the cause of sustainable development . This also includes the Paris agreement on climate agreed by the participated countries in 2015 . <p> Some of the noticeable goals include the provision of quality education , incorporating better practice in infrastructure , innovation , and industry , making positive climate change impacts , focusing on clean and sustainable energy sources , preservation and enhancement of the natural environment , and achieving targets on general well-being . <p> The conference , to be held on June 8-9 in Delhi at Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute , intends to create a space for debate and conduct deliberation in the areas where the higher learning institute will try to contribute to the change and adaptation of the sustainable development goals . It also aims to bring out the latest academic as well as practical aspects that are being introduced in India and Canada towards the creation and contribution of the respective country in sustainable global society . <p> The conference will strengthen and promote the Canadian and Indian scholarship , boost bi-national networking , training and will bring more opportunities . Papers are invited from faculty , post-doctoral researcher , and doctoral students from Shastri member institution that discuss the result of their research and the impact it has in developing a partnership , learning methodologies , and socio-cultural narratives that empower interdisciplinary research .
