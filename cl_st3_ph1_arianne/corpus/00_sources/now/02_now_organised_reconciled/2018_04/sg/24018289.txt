The peninsula is a fragile ecosystem that holds one of the last great reserves of hydrocarbons , reserves that are being made accessible by anthropogenic climate change . <p> Development of the region , which is being driven by the competing interests of world powers , threatens the traditional way of life of the Nenets and could exacerbate the impacts of climate change . <h> Strategic vision <p> One of those powers is China , whose state enterprises have invested in Yamal LNG , a natural gas extraction , liquefaction and export project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tonnes per year , according to Novatek , the project 's majority owner . <p> It was officially opened in December 2017 , sending its first cargo of liquefied natural gas ( LNG ) along the Northern Sea Route to Boston in the United States , after being sold on the spot market .
