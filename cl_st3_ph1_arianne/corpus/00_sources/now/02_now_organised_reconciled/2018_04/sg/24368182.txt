<h> Time for Asean to prioritise climate action <p> As 2018 chair of Asean , Singapore is ideally placed to prioritise climate change on Asean 's agenda . After all , among the world 's 10 countries most affected by climate change , four - Myanmar , the Philippines , Thailand and Vietnam - are Asean members .
