As a result , her hands were red , raw and severely numb . <p> She added : " Some of the other safety concerns included being careful not to fall overboard when the waters were very choppy . " <p> Her short film will be screened on June 4 at an exhibition at the Singapore Botanic Gardens , showcasing her trip to Antarctica . <p> Ms Cheam added : " It is an extreme , deathly cold environment but beautiful in its desolateness .
