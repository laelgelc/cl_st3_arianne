<p> Even though the title race is over , Guardiola 's champions played on the front foot throughout , boasting a fast , attacking line-up . <p> Even though the threat of relegation still hangs over the gloomy London Stadium , Moyes ' line-up was an exercise in cynicism . <p> He recognised he could n't beat City so he scarcely bothered to try . Perhaps he intended to bore them to death . <p> West Ham deserved a degree of sympathy for the manner in which the first two goals were conceded , but there 's no sympathy for the beleaguered manager . <p> Moyes typically sets his sides up not to lose , a rudimentary approach that served him well at Everton , but has since proved disastrous at Manchester United , Sunderland and now West Ham . <p> There 's no shame in parking a bus or two against goalscoring behemoths who reached their Century for the season , but Moyes essentially parked a fleet of old bangers and threw away the keys . <p> There was no alternative , no Plan B beyond keeping nine Hammers in their own half in the hope of pinching a point - an unrealistic ambition against City at the best of times . <p> West Ham 's narrow 3-5-2 was mostly a 5-4-1 and once Leroy Sane broke the deadlock in the 13th minute , when his strike flew in off Patrice Evra 's head , the contest seemed effectively over . <p> A comedy of errors led to Pablo Zabaleta tapping in an own goal after 27 minutes and suddenly the hosts were two-down and arguably the victims of considerable misfortune . <p> But if the goals were unlucky , Moyes ' meek line-up and underwhelming negativity certainly were not . His approach was premeditated and self-defeating . <p> West Ham defended in vast numbers without ever imposing themselves . <h> RUGBY SCORE <p> The only surprise was that City did n't establish the kind of scoreline witnessed at the Singapore Rugby Sevens earlier in the day . <p> Instead , West Ham reduced the deficit , entirely against the run of the play , when Aaron Cresswell whipped in a fine free-kick shortly before the interval . <p> Then the Hammers persisted with their statuesque defending , with too many bodies behind the ball serving as bowling pins . <p> After a delightful one-two , Gabriel Jesus collected the return ball from Raheem Sterling and slipped his shot beneath Adrian in the 53rd minute . <p> Just 10 minutes later , Fernandinho swept in City 's fourth , but at no time did Moyes attempt to address the obvious philosophical imbalance . <p> Managers like Moyes , Alan Pardew and Sam Allardyce often claim that they 'd deliver the goods if they had the resources of Guardiola , Juergen Klopp or Jose Mourinho . They 'd win more often if only a level playing field existed . <p> Well since November , Moyes ' West Ham have conceded three or more against City , Everton , Liverpool , Arsenal and Swansea ( all four ) and Newcastle , Bournemouth , Brighton and Burnley ( all three ) . <p> His deeply ingrained conservatism inhibits the Hammers , even against lesser opponents . Like Allardyce , he persists with Jurassic football that is anathema to Guardiola , Klopp and Mauricio Pochettino and yet the old boys ' club continue to throw up jobs for the Brits . <p> Based on last night 's evidence , Moyes ' brief tenure can not end soon enough .
