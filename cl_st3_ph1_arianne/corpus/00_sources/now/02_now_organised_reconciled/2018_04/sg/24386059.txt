<h> Climate , cuisine , defence on agenda of Macron 's Australia visit <p> French officials said President Emmanuel Macron will discuss security in the South Pacific with Australian Prime Minister Malcolm Turnbull on his first visit to the country as president <p> Climate change , cuisine and defence will be on the menu next week when French President Emmanuel Macron visits Australia . <p> French officials said Macron would also discuss security in the South Pacific with Australian prime minister Malcolm Turnbull on his first visit to the country as president . <p> The May 1-3 trip comes hot on the heels of Macron 's pomp-filled visit to Washington and Turnbull 's journey to Normandy for World War 1 Anzac Day commemorations . <p> Macron 's advisers said he wanted to discuss a " common response " to security and climate tensions in the South Pacific , which includes the French territory of New Caledonia . <p> The visit will also have a strong trade and cultural flavour . <p> France 's 40-year-old leader will discuss food Wednesday at a lunch with Australian and French chefs . <p> As often on his foreign visits , he will also meet local artists , specifically Aboriginal artists whose work deals with the climate change -- a subject on which Macron , custodian of the 2015 Paris Agreement , has taken a leadership role . <p> He will also try to drum up business for French defence companies , a-year-and-a-half after Australia signed an Aus$50 billion ( US$37 billion , 31 billion euros ) deal to purchase 12 next-generation French submarines . <p> Australian Foreign Minister Julie Bishop said earlier this month that , with Britain poised to leave the European Union , Australia was anxious to deepen ties with other European countries . <p> Australia was already working " very closely " with France on the submarine project , she was quoted by Australian media as saying .
