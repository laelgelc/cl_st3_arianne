<p> SINGAPORE - Singapore has benefited from being part of the Commonwealth and will pay it forward by sharing its developmental experiences with fellow countries in the grouping , said Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong . <p> Mr Lee made the pledge ahead of the biennial Commonwealth Heads of State Meeting ( Chogm ) , which he will attend this week in London . <p> Writing in the Chogm 2018 Report , he said Singapore faced grave social and economic problems when it joined the Commonwealth in October 1965 , a couple of months after gaining independence on Aug 9 . <p> The regional situation was also unstable then , with the Vietnam War brewing and South-east Asian countries at odds with one another . <p> " The odds were against our survival , but we pulled through . We were deeply grateful to the friends who came to our help in times of need , many of whom were in the Commonwealth , " Mr Lee wrote . <p> Singapore is glad it can now help other countries in the Commonwealth through technical assistance programmes , he added . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ member nations gathering for the Chogm Summit , hosted by London this year . <p> The meetings run from Thursday ( April 19 ) to Friday . <p> But starting on Monday , a series of forums and other events will be held to kick off the Summit . <p> Foreign Minister Vivian Balakrishnan will attend the Commonwealth Ministerial Meeting on Small States on Tuesday and the Commonwealth Foreign Ministers ' Meeting on Wednesday . <p> The Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement on Monday that the foreign ministers will discuss issues of concern to members states and review developments in the Commonwealth . <p> In his article , Mr Lee noted the similarities between Chogm and Asean , which Singapore chairs this year . <p> Singapore is working with fellow Asean members and partners on initiatives to strengthen resilience against such threats as terrorism , cybercrime and climate change , and to help Asean economies innovate and use technology , he said . <p> These aspirations dovetail with this year 's Chogm themes of security , prosperity , fairness and sustainability , he added . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ share core values such as tolerance , respect and understanding .
