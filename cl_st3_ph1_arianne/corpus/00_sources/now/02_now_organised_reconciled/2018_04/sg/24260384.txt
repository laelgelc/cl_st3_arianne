<p> Schools and young people are being pushed to reduce their carbon footprint this year as part of the Year of Climate Action in Singapore . <p> Minister for the Environment and Water Resources , Mr Masagos Zulkifli , called on students to recycle waste and reduce consumption on Youth for the Environment Day on Friday ( April 20 ) , held at ITE College Central . <p> " Climate change affects Singapore very badly , " he said . " In about 80 years from now , scientists have predicted that in Singapore , the sea levels will rise by at least one metre . " <p> Among things students could do were switching off electrical appliances when not in use and buying only the items they needed . <p> During the event , Mr Masagos and a panel of five secondary school students discussed how young Singaporeans could take action to fight climate change . All five were leaders of the environmental clubs in their respective schools . <p> The students voiced concerns that while teenagers knew about the impact of climate change , many were reluctant to do much about it as it would affect their daily lives . <p> For instance , recycling waste at home would mean they had to separate food waste from other types of waste like paper - which some considered a hassle . <p> They also discussed their schools ' environmental initiatives . For example , Dunman High School 's Gan Rui Yi mentioned that students there collected discarded orange peel to turn into detergent , while Lim Yang Zhi from River Valley High School spoke on a schoolwide bingo event to get students involved in environmentally-friendly efforts like using their own containers for takeout food and setting the air-conditioner temperature to 25 degree Celsius , held this week to commemorate Earth Day . <p> Yang Zhi , also the president of his school 's Eco-Sustainability Leadership Academy , said that an area he felt could be improved in his school was electricity usage . <p> " In our tutorial rooms , we lower the temperature of the air-conditioner because it 's too hot . But after we do that , we wear our jackets , " he said . <p> Separately , the National Environment Agency also launched the Climate Action Challenge , in which schools are encouraged to send creative photos or video submissions demonstrating how climate action can be part of their students ' daily lives in terms of practising the 3Rs - reduce , reuse and recycle - and using electricity efficiently . <p> Schools also received a guide to conducting climate action-related activities . <h> The Straits Times <p> We have been experiencing some problems with subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
