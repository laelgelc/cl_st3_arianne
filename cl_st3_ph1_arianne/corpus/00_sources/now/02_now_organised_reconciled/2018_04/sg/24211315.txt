<h> He will also hold bilateral talks with leaders of other states in the grouping while in London <p> Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong will be in London this week for the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting ( Chogm ) , a biennial summit that most of the 53 leaders of the Commonwealth nations are expected to attend . <p> This year 's meeting is likely to be the last for Queen Elizabeth II , the 91-year-old head of the Commonwealth , who is winding back on long-haul travel . She will host a dinner for leaders at Buckingham Palace tomorrow . Britain last hosted Chogm in 1997 . <p> PM Lee arrives today for the two-day meeting that takes place tomorrow and Friday , and will hold bilateral talks on the sidelines with other Commonwealth leaders . <p> At Chogm , the top government leaders are expected to discuss global and regional issues of concern and review developments in the Commonwealth , said the Prime Minister 's Office ( PMO ) in a statement yesterday . <p> The theme for this year 's meeting is " Towards a Common @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ issues ranging from human rights and democracy to trade and investment , as well as climate change , terrorism and cybercrime . <p> Although the Commonwealth has focused on development and democracy , British Prime Minister Theresa May is expected to make a pitch during the meeting for greater trade with the historic network of countries , as Britain prepares to exit the European Union under Brexit . <p> The Commonwealth , a voluntary organisation made up of Britain and countries with past links to the British Empire , is home to 2.4 billion people .
