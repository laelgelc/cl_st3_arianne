<h> Collective work needed to mitigate climate change : PM Modi <p> On the occasion of World Earth Day , Prime Minister Narendra Modi called for collective work to mitigate the menace of climate change . <p> The Prime Minister took to Twitter and said , " On #EarthDay , let us reaffirm our commitment towards creating a better planet for our future generations . Let us work together to mitigate the menace of climate change . This would be a great tribute to our beloved Mother Earth .
