<h> Michael Bloomberg to write $4.5 million check for Paris climate pact <p> Special envoy to the United Nations for climate change Michael Bloomberg speaks during the One-on-One discussion panel with International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) Managing Director Christine Lagarde on side of the IMF/World Bank spring meeting in Washington , U.S. , April 19 , 2018 . REUTERS/Yuri Gripas <p> WASHINGTON ( Reuters ) - Former New York mayor and billionaire Michael Bloomberg said on Sunday he will write a $4.5 million check to cover this year 's U.S. financial commitment to the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> President Donald Trump last year pulled the United States out of the pact , making the country the only one opposed to it . <p> Bloomberg , in a CBS interview , said he hopes by next year Trump will have changed his mind . <p> Bloomberg will continue to provide money for the pact if the United States does not rejoin the agreement , according to a news release from Bloomberg Philanthropies , the charity he founded . <p> " Our foundation will uphold our promise to cover any cuts to UN climate funding by the federal government , " Bloomberg said in the statement . <p> Trump staunchly opposes the agreement and his administration has rolled back a number of environmental regulations .
