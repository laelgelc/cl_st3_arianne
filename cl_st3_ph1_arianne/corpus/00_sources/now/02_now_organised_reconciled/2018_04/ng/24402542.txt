<h> Inside Gashaka- Gumti National Park <p> By Magaji Isa Hunkuyi , Jalingo Publish Date : Apr 29 2018 4:09AM <p> ( 0 Likes ) <p> Gashaka-Gumti National Park is one of the largest parks in Nigeria.It is located in Gashaka local government area of Taraba state , and stretches to parts of Toungo local government area of Adamawa state . <p> The park is rated highest in term of beautiful scenery , waterfalls , ever green mountains and above all the park houses over 500 different forms of wild life and close to a million birds.Gasahaka - Gumti National park is among the seven National parks in Nigeria.It is the largest , most scenic and biologically diverse conservation enclave . <p> Similarly , thousands of birds migrate to the park yearly from across the globe , making the park to be a refuge of different species of birds in the entire West African sub region <p> However , despite the attractions which make it a unique park in the country , Nigerians are not patronizing the park , instead they travel to other parks abroad which are less attractive and spend millions of Naira while doing so . <p> The park is also located at the foot of the famous Mambilla plateau , which is also one of the best tourist 's attraction in the country , and it has both tropical and temperate climates . <p> The distance from Jalingo , the Taraba state capital to the tourists camp in Serti is about 340 kilometers , and the road leading to the camp is good with vehicles plying it on a 24 hour basis . <p> All around the Gashaka -Gumti National park is natural scenery . The park covers 6731 square kilometers , and shares a common boundary with the republic of Cameroon .. <p> GashakaGumti National park started as a forest reserve in the 70s and was transformed into a national park in 1999 by federal Decree , because of its richness in biodiversity and eco system . It derived its name from two of the area 's oldest and most historic settlements , the Gashaka Village in Taraba state and Gumti village in Adamawa state . <p> A visit by Daily Trust to the Park recently revealed more of the natural content of the park that attracted tourists and researchers from across the globe , more especially from Europe and America . <p> My guide , a ranger , told me that there are few conservation enclaves in the world that can boast of such unique spectacular and diverse scenery , as the vast and relatively unspoilt wilderness . <p> Going deep inside the park from the main gate of the park at Bode village along Gembu- Serti high way , l had no reason to argue with the Ranger on the richness of the park . <p> Beautiful scenery and the mountains as well as waterfalls and rivers with fast flowing water , were there from every direction you look at . <p> It was not surprising that foreign tourists coming into the park , were on the high side compared to local tourists . <p> The conservator of the park Mr.Saidu Yohana told Daily Trust that local tourists have not really understood the richness of tourist 's attraction of the park . He explained that Nigerians under scored the richness of the park , and that was why they go to other parks , or even travel abroad for holidays . <p> Mr Yonana said the park is the largest in the whole of Nigeria and even the West African sub -region . According to him the park encompasses , Savannah , forest , wetland and montane habitats in a continuous ecological system that makes it rare if not unique in the entire West African Region . <p> The largest and multi diverse conservation enclave in the country , Gashaka- Gumti national park has the highest mountain peak in Nigeria , the Chappal Waddi is 2400 feet above sea level . The footage one sees of parks across East Africa do not match or rival what is obtainable in the park , and that was why until recently the park was a Mecca to hundreds of tourists and researchers coming from different parts of the world . <p> I was amazed with the beautiful scenery , the coolness and quietness of the environment , it is indeed a good place to spend days in relaxation , but yet not even people in Taraba where the facility is located , appreciate the unique beautiful attraction inside the park . <p> The two Rangers who were my guides on the trip said that for one to fully appreciate the richness and the eco diversity of the park , requires a trek of a whole day or two into the park , as most parts could be accessed by Range Rover used because of the many Rivers and streams at this period of rainfall . <p> I was told that there is a well maintained over 1000 kilometers network of jeep tracks and trails which links Gashaka and Gumti sector all together , and to other strategic locations around the park . <p> Daily Trust also discovered that at the moment a network of seasonal motorable dirt road and tracks was being developed for park viewing , anti poaching patrols and for biodiversity research among others . <p> Because of the terrain at this period my movement was only limited to some parts of the park , and even at that point I was delighted with what I saw . <p> Hippos in one of the rivers <p> The park it was gathered was also suitable for adventure and recreation because of its beautiful scenery , fine subtropical climate conditions , flora and fauna . It also offers an amusing opportunity to a visitor to watch wild animals , birds as well as for sport fishing . <p> Out of the seven National parks in the country , it was further gathered only Gashaka- Gumti offers potential of sport fishing and one of the recognized places is the Mayokam Hippo-pool . <p> " Because of many rivers and streams inside the park , vehicles have limits to where they take visitors , the rest is by trekking and it takes hours to move into all the zones " a Ranger said . <p> He said if a visitor wants to see the animals he would have to trek kilometers into the park , and one is expected to carry his food and water along . <p> Its altitude according to my guide is 450 meters above sea level in the wild savannah plains of the Northern sector , to the peaks and pinnacles of Gangirwal in the Southern park sector , which is at 2,400 meters above sea level , and this point represents Nigeria 's highest mountain <p> The conservator of the park , Mr Saidu Yohana told our correspondent that the fauna of the national park is very diverse and contains about 103 species of mammals . Species include yellow-backed duiker , African golden cat , African buffalo , the largest population of chimpanzees in Nigeria , and there are over1700 chimpanzees in the park , African elephant , klipspringer , West African wild dog , hartebeest , the world 's largest antelope , the giant eland , Roan antelope , Kob antelope , oribi , Black-and-white colobus monkey , baboon , warthog , and the rare mountain reedbuck . Its largest unspoilt rivers contain hippos , crocodiles , otters and a wide variety of fishes . <p> He explained that the avian fauna is very rich with about a million birds . The park is officially labelled as one of Africa 's " Important Bird Areas " - and with more than 500 species found , visiting bird watching enthusiasts are constantly adding new species to the list . <p> He said the mandate of the parks is to protect and manage the major catchment of the upper Benue River system , which originates from the park . <p> " We have the mandate to protect the high biodiversity of species of fauna and flora of the area , some of which are endangered or at the verge of extinction , " the conservator said . <p> There were a number of waterfalls in the park with falling water all the year round and that has added more attraction to visitors . Some of the waterfalls inside the park include Kiri waterfalls , Yoma waterfall located on Karlai hills , Mayo Ngaska waterfalls and Ngiti waterfalls . The water falls it was gathered , apart from being site attractions has the potential to generate electricity , but yet the entire park relies on generating plants for its power supply . <p> He said charges are moderate as visitors were charged N7500 per day N7000 is for a room , while N1500 was for transport and camera tours into the park . <p> According to him the charge was the lowest in any park in the country , adding that there was the need for holiday seekers to patronize the park , because it has all the potentials needed by tourists . <p> Gashaka- Gumti National Park <p> " Local tourists need not to travel abroad as here in Gashaka - Gumti park there is what it takes to give pleasure to persons that want to see wild life of any type , as well as the beautiful scenery " he said . <p> He said that 32 chalets were built to accommodate tourists and most of chalets were built at tourists camp located inside SERTI town , while few other chalets were provided inside the main park . <p> Our correspondent however discovered that Gashaka local government area where the park is located is yet to be connected to the national grid , hence the park relies on generating plants for power supply . <p> The cost of diesel has forced the management of the park to provide electricity for a few hours in the day , and such supply is normally between 6.30pm to 11.30pm , but however water supply in the tourist 's camp went for 24 hours and without interruption . <p> The conservator of the park said the park needs urgent attention from the federal government , as some of the facilities needed to be upgraded . According to him electricity supply was highly needed to add comfort to visitors . <p> He said government should provide adequate funds to provide facilities in order to attract local and foreign tourists <p> He also expressed the need for additional man power for effecting patrols against poaching , logging of tress , illegal mining of Gold and illegal grazing , which have been a source of concern to the park authorities . <p> " The park contains the most important area of montane forest in Nigeria , and the Caps of the montane forest are identified to be critical to watershed conservation and climate change regulation " he said . <p> " This area of the montane forest also harbors at least 24 threatened plant species , believed to possess certain medicinal properties for the cure of prostate cancer , and which need to be protected " he added . <p> He also said there was the need for more logistics and security , pointing out that the park is located in the North- East region that faces insurgency problems , and there was tendency for criminal elements to use the area as their hide out . Mr. Saidu Yohana lamented that the revenue of the park has dropped drastically because of insecurity in the North- East region , and as a result the European nations and United States of America has banned its citizens from visiting the park . <p> " More than 90 percent of tourists that come to the park either for holiday or for research are from Europe , and now they were not coming because of insecurity " he lamented . <p> He said the issue of security was a serious challenge which needs to be addressed urgently . He appealed to the police and the Army to increase patrols around the park . <p> " Our rangers that patrol the park need patrol vehicles , weapons and therefore the state government should come to the aid of the park by donating patrol vehicles to ensure proper security surveillance in the park " he said <p> Mr Yohana explained that part of the mandate of the National park was to protect the wild life as well as the eco- system , but there were cases of illegal mining , illegal logging of trees and illegal grazing which should be addressed to protect the eco system in the park <p> " We have Chinese using locals to cut a particular tree which they take to china through the ports , and has been a major threat to the vegetation as well as the eco system in the park " he further stated <p> Our findings revealed that students from Nigerian universities , colleges of Education , colleges of agriculture as well as other research institutions , visit the park for practical purposes and research work . <p> The conservator while commenting on students coming for practicals , said another mandate of National park was to provide an avenue for science student as well as those in other fields to conduct research work and practicals in various fields . <p> According to him , the park has all the species of insects , wild life , trees and fishes , adding that two types of fish species and 7 species of Monkeys were found in the park by a team of researchers , that were never found elsewhere in the world . <p> He said why students from high institutions were coming for practical and research purposes was because the park 's mountains harbour populations of rare and endemic species . <p> He said students coming for practical purposes into the park were charged N500 accommodation fee per night . He therefore called on federal government to build more structures to accommodate the increasing number of students coming to the park . <p> The conservator of the park however commended Conservator General Ibrahim Musa Goni for doing very well to revive all the national parks in the country . He said it was under the new conservator General that the first female was appointed as a conservator of a national park . <p> Mr Saidu Yohana explained further that Gashaka- Gumti National park has the potential to generate huge revenue if facilities at the park are uplifted , adding that the park has a diverse ecosystem hardly found in any part of Africa . <p> A resident of the area , Malam Garba Yakubu told our correspondent at Goje village that the park was neglected by the federal government over the years . <p> He said some of the structures in the park were constructed when the park was a forest Reserve , and since then not much was done to uplift the existing facilities . <p> He said a similar park in neigbouring Cameroon was better in terms of modern facilities and security , adding that both local and foreign tourists were visiting those parks on daily basis . <p> Commenting , the Lamdo Gashaka , Alhaji Zubairu Hammangabdo said the park has a rich ecosystem hardly found in any part of Africa , and if uplifted with more facilities the park would attract tourists from within and outside the country . <p> The Lamdo , a first class chief in Taraba state appealed to the Army headquarters to increase military surveillance in his domain to protect the people , adding that with insecurity in parts of the North- East region , criminal elements may likely try to use the forests and mountains in the area as their hiding place . <p> If you are happy to be contacted by a Daily Trust journalist please leave a telephone number that we can contact you on .
