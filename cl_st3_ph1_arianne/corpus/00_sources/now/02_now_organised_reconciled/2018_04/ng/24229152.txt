<p> President Muhammadu Buhari has identified Trade and Investment Facilitation to generate resources for sustainable development ; Ease of Doing Business and Regional Integration that expands markets with safeguards against injurious trade practices from third parties ; More Inclusive Growth with the empowerment of women and the youth , as factors that are capable of making business among Commonwealth countries easier .
