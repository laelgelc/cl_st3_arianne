<h> Government 's oil move atones for our environmental sins <p> OPINION : The Government 's move to stop oil exploration brings to mind the entreaty of the early Christian intellectual Augustine of Hippo who prayed , " Lord , make me chaste but not yet . " <p> The announcement means the Government will indeed be making us chaste on oil but we will have plenty of time to make the transition . <p> We have all been environmental sinners for a long time . We have loved wallowing in our oil , driving gas guzzlers and fume belchers , avoiding public transport and building ever bigger roads and bridges instead of investing in a decent rail system . <p> This week Jacinda Ardern 's cobbled -- together coalition put a stop to all that . Well , it made a gesture anyway , by announcing it would be issuing no new oil and gas off-shore exploration permits . The day of reckoning has come , and as such , it is pretty mild . <p> Certainly no immediate job losses and company collapses such as those during the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be good for you . <p> The oil change was a bit like the last National Government announcing it was raising the age of superannuation to 67 in a year so far away that it was academic for most people . <p> Radical change to the oil industry , it is not . About 30 existing exploration permits will continue until at least 2030 and viable oil and gas finds made under those permits could mean production for years after that . <p> We could still have a massive oil industry off the coast of Canterbury and Southland and more onshore wells in Taranaki . But pain will no doubt be felt especially since exploration is a major part of the industry . <p> Nevertheless a line in the sand has been drawn , even if it feels a little hypocritical . <p> The big infrastructure projects the Government wants to pursue and the houses it wants to build and the tourists it wants to attract will still see a lot of fuel burnt . <p> We are still closer to the horse and cart that we are to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be able to depend on other countries like Russia and Brazil not being so squeamish about continuing to explore for those hydro -- carbons on which the economy still depends heavily . <p> Heading for the scrap heap . <p> It 's a bit like exporting our recycling to places like India and China . We could be seen to be simply shifting the problem instead of making improvements . <p> NZ Oil and Gas , for instance , says it is investing in production and exploration in other jurisdictions . <p> But that is to quibble . I applaud the move if only because it demonstrates this Government is prepared to make uncomfortable changes we all know need to happen . <p> Commentators will say this sort of move combined with new fuel taxes will cost Labour and its coalition partners the next election , especially if fuel prices go up as they inevitably will . <p> However the electorate can be surprisingly forgiving on points of principle .
