<h> New study says future EVs will be even cleaner <p> Words : Nile Bijoux <p> 24 Apr 2018 <p> All-electric cars are expected to be between 30 and 70 percent cleaner by 2040 , a new study has found . <p> The research was done by a team from the Paul Scherrer Institut in Switzerland , Leiden University ( Netherlands ) and PBL Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency , that performed Life Cycle Assessment ( LCA ) . <p> It included factors such as changing driving patterns due to autonomous/connected vehicles and changes to the electricity sector . The team found that future cars should have at 20 percent lower environmental impact from technological improvements alone , with overall improvement to be between 30 and 70 percent . <p> Brian Cox , one of the researchers , said : " The future environmental impacts of battery electric vehicles ( EVs ) are very important given their expected dominance in future transport systems . Previous studies have shown these impacts to be highly uncertain , though a detailed treatment of this uncertainty is still lacking . <p> We help to fill this gap by considering two additional factors that have not yet been addressed in the field . " <p> " First , we include changes to driving patterns due to the introduction of autonomous and connected vehicles . Second , include the impacts of changes to the electricity sector on the environmental burdens of producing and recharging future EVs . <p> Future EVs are expected to have 45 -- 78 per cent lower climate change impacts than current EVs . Electricity used for charging is the largest source of variability in results , though vehicle size , lifetime , driving patterns , and battery size also strongly contribute to variability . <p> We also show that it is imperative to consider changes to the electricity sector when calculating upstream impacts of EVs , as without this , results could be overestimated by up to 75 per cent , " Cox finished . <p> Green Car Congress said the model considers vehicles ranging from mini to luxury cars , with vehicle mass an uncertain parameter .
