National 's still in a state of shock , along with what at the moment is their 43 percent solid support base , that they 're not sitting on the Treasury benches today . Their supporters still feel they 've been robbed and refuse to give Labour the benefit of the doubt , refusing to accept that less than 51 percent simply is n't good enough . <p> Labour 's gone from its first 100 days in office where they at times appeared like student politicians and where even Ardern only gave them seven out of ten , to at least giving the appearance of being more self assured , but should they be ? <p> They 've had a rough few months with the drunken Youth Labour camp revelations where Ardern was kept in the dark , to the embarrassing blunders by their Broadcasting Minister Claire Curran along with allowing themselves to be the blind eye of the Five Eyes countries when it came to condemning the misdeeds of Russia , not to forget the shivers they sent up the spine of business by the cancellation , without consultation , of all future offshore oil and gas exploration even though it 'll cost billions and do nothing for climate change other than sending production offshore . <p> So they 're the fails . The success for the Labour luvvies would be the paid parental leave extension , the families package instead of tax cuts , lower winter power bills for the elderly , the inquiry into historic child abuse and their multiple handouts to students.The for and against tend to balance each other out but the real tests are to come , starting with the Budget next month which Labour 's been at pains to talk down , which is an old political ploy . Dampen expectation but deliver enthusiasm . <p> Three weeks after that they 've got the Northcote by-election , a seat that 's been comfortably held by National since 2005 but before that , it was Labour 's .
