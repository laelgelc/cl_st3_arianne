<p> Greenpeace executive director Russel Norman has pleaded guilty over his involvement in a seismic blasting ship protest last April . <p> Source : 1 NEWS <p> The former Green Party co-leader , along with climate activist Sara Howell , pleaded guilty today in Auckland District Court for swimming in front of the largest oil exploration ship in the world , the Amazon Warrior , Newshub reports . <p> The Greenpeace director and climate activist Sarah Powell argued their conduct was reasonable and honourable , given the threat of climate change . Source : 1 NEWS <p> The pair were charged under the Anadarko Amendment which prevents protest against oil ships at sea , which carried a fine of $200,000 . <p> On April 27 , Greenpeace were advised the Ministry for Business , Innovation and Employment would not pursue charges against the organisation , but those against Mr Norman and Ms Howell still remained . <p> Prior to the charges being heard today in court , Mr Norman said he " never denied the facts " of the case . <p> " We see the best course of action now is to plead guilty while making a plea in mitigation in court on the grounds that our actions were reasonable and honourable given the threat of climate change , " Mr Norman said . <p> The pair will be sentenced in July . <p> He was among a small group of protesters who entered the water near the Amazon Warrior yesterday .
