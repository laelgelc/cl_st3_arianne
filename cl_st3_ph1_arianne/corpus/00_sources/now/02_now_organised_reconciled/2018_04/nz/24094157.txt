<h> Resetting the Pacific relationship <p> Development of New Zealand 's relationship with the Pacific Islands is just as important to this country 's future as it is to that in the islands . <p> OPINION : More money is necessary to transform our relationship with the Pacific beyond aid , but re-setting the relationship requires more than money . <p> We give a smaller proportion of our national economy in aid than most OECD countries provide -- about 0.22 per cent of our GDP , well below Australia , most of Europe and the OECD average of 0.4 per cent . <p> But we have something that no-one else has : a Pacific diaspora who identify as both New Zealanders and Pacific people . <p> They are Pacific business groups based in New Zealand , health organisations , Pacific women 's groups , New Zealand NGOs , and iwi organisations . <p> They are devolving responsibility for development to the Pacific and waiting for governments to catch up . <p> A friend in Samoa told me , " New Zealand and Australian experts turn up and say , ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Chinese come along and say , ' What do you want ?
