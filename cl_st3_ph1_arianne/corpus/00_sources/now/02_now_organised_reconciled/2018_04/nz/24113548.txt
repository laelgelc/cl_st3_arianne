<h> Victoria University to host panel of international arbitration experts <p> Victoria University of Wellington will host a round table discussion with international arbitration experts offering a range of perspectives . <p> Organised in partnership with the International Law Association and the Arbitrators ' and Mediators ' Institute of New Zealand , the event will provide Wellington professionals with an opportunity to learn about the latest developments in international arbitration and to ask questions in a frank discussion with experts . <p> Professor Petra Butler from the University 's Faculty of Law will chair the discussion , and will be joined by arbitration specialists from London , Hong Kong , Vienna , the United States and New Zealand . <p> Professor Butler says the event will offer a multifaceted view of some of the biggest issues in international arbitration , including investor-state arbitration , third party funding and climate change . <p> " This round table event is an exciting opportunity for New Zealand arbitration practitioners to engage with experts from around the world and gain inside knowledge on a range of contemporary issues pertaining to international arbitration .
