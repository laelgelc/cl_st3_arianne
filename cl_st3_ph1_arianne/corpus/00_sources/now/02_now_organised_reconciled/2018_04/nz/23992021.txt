<h> One billion trees , one billion opportunities <p> An important focus is about planting the right tree , in the right place , for the right purpose . <p> ADVICE : Landowners across New Zealand , including Maori , hold the key to realising the social , economic and environmental benefits from planting trees . <p> The Government has set the goal of planting one billion trees over the next 10 years -- an ambitious , but achievable goal -- and good progress is already being made . <p> Trees have a tonne of benefits . They can complement a landowner 's existing operations by providing another source of income ; increasing the productivity of marginal land ; and transferring wealth across generations . <p> Planting trees reduces erosion by helping to keep the soil on the land , improving pasture productivity and water quality . StatsNZ estimates that about 192 million tonnes of eroded soil enters New Zealand 's rivers each year . <p> Planting trees is also New Zealand 's best method for storing carbon to offset emissions .
