<p> Environment Minister David Parker wants officials to work on a national policy statement for versatile land and high-class soils in response to a report showing changing land use such as urban sprawl and intensified farming are adding to natural pressures from climate change and earthquakes . <p> The Ministry for the Environment and Statistics New Zealand today released the ' Our Land 2018 ' report on the state of the soil and the state of indigenous biodiversity and ecosystems .
