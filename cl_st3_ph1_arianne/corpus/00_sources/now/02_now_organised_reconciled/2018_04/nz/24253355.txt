Ardern 's statement said New Zealand " accepts " why they responded to the grave violation of international law , and the abhorrent use of chemical weapons against civilians . <p> That is not sitting on the fence . It is not a statement of neutrality . It is a statement of acceptance -- perhaps implying reluctant support from a country which has consistently held that such actions should be authorised by the Security Council . <p> The notion that New Zealand is so small that it does n't matter is used inconsistently by politicians . For a country that lectures others about the rule of law and international rules-based order , New Zealand is careful about endorsing actions if the legality of them is not clear . <p> But reading between the lines of New Zealand 's statement , it is essentially saying that even if the legality of the strike against Syria is not clear , New Zealand accepts it . <p> Yes , it fell short of the unconditional " support " of other Five Eyes partners but there is no reason New Zealand should simply follow them because it 's them . <p> The Five Eyes alliance is not a security alliance . Every Five Eyes partner except New Zealand is in a security pact with the US . <p> Australia is through Anzus , as is Canada through its membership of Nato , along with Britain . <p> New Zealand need not be pressured to " support " actions simply on the basis that our friends do . <p> We can know who our best friends are and whose side we are on without having to slavishly follow them .
