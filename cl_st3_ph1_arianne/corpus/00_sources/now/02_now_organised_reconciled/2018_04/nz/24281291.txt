<h> Watch : Dance filmed in Antarctica premieres in London for World Earth Day <p> share <p> Source : <p> The beauty and fragility of Antarctica is being highlighted on the world stage with an ice dancing film premiering in London for World Earth Day . <p> The dance movie was created by NZ choreographer Corey Baker and the star of the Royal NZ Ballet . Source : 1 NEWS <p> The film , ' Antarctica : The First Dance ' , was created by Kiwi choreographer Corey Baker , and Royal New Zealand Ballet star Madeleine Graham . <p> The creative duo both spent 12 days on the ice for the film . <p> " Never in my wildest dreams did I expect to be there dancing . It was incredible , " Ms Graham said . <p> The Australian-born ballerina started dancing at age seven . <p> ' Antarctica : The First Dance ' features Ms Graham suspended within a glacier and sliding down mountainsides as temperatures reached as low as -16 degrees . <p> " As dancers , we 're used to looking out into the complete darkness , so to be dancing and looking out into something so spectacular and inspiring gave me that extra kind of feeling and reason to do what I was doing , " she said . <p> For choreographer Corey Baker , the film has been a project two years in the making . <p> Mr Baker said the film is " hopefully beautiful but also powerful to be able to share a message around climate change that sticks with people in a different way " .
