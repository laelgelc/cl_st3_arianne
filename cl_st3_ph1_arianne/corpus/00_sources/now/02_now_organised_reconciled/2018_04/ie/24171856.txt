<h> Bid to accelerate State use of electric vehicles <p> The Defence Forces , An Post , the HSE , and education bodies are all being encouraged to switch over to using electric cars to reduce carbon emissions and government spending . <p> Environment Minister Denis Naughten recently met the Defence Forces chief of staff , Vice Admiral Mark Mellett , and they discussed how army personnel could switch to using carbon-free vehicles in future . <p> An Post CEO David McRedmond and the minister also met last week and discussed using electric vans in the postal fleet , as well as the possibility of charge points being installed at post offices . <p> The moves come as the first dedicated electric vehicle public information campaign is launched this week . <p> Departments predict that , by 2020 , there will be an estimated 20,000 electric vehicles on Irish roads .
