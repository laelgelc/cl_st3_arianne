<h> Shell could be taken to court over climate change <p> Friends of the Earth is threatening to take Shell to court in the Netherlands over its part in causing climate change . <p> The NGO has accused the oil &amp; gas giant of " acting like big tobacco " in ignoring the science and damage its activities are doing to the planet . <p> Friends of the Earth is willing to challenge the energy company for breaching Dutch laws on hazardous negligence if it does n't accelerate its plans to combat climate change . This could mean forcing the company to increase its level of renewable energy investments , which the group claims sits at only 5 percent . <p> Shell , which is headquartered in The Hague , has made initial progress towards moving away from fossil fuels , including investing in hydrogen fuel .
