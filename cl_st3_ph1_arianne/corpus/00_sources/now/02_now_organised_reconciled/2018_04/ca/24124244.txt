" It has seen wine importation bans , a civil war between provincial wings of the NDP and the arrest of two B.C. MPs for protesting . And , it would appear , the sophisticated chess match is just getting started . <p> Over the weekend , the inevitable occurred ; Kinder Morgan , the proponent of an expanded Trans Mountain Pipeline form near Edmonton , Alberta , to Burnaby , British Columbia , said it had enough . I suppose there is enough blame ( or credit depending on your viewpoint ) to go around . The federal Liberals are in a pretty tight spot . The National Energy Board is the regulator of the proposal ; but its members and protocols are determined by the cabinet . Having conditionally approved Trans Mountain , the Trudeau government is both regulator and cheerleader , a difficult tightrope to balance under the best circumstances , which are certainly absent in this mess . <p> And Trudeau , as Canada 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . One year from now , Albertans go to the polls . The premier has staked her political future , and defended her unpopular and ever escalating carbon tax , on pipeline construction . Christy " I want to make sure B.C. gets its fair share " Clark was replaced by John " it-ain't-gonna-happen " Horgan . <p> It is now inconceivable that a major pipeline can be completed in twelve months . Governments inherently do not understand that private enterprises want to be out of the headlines , left alone in an economic climate that allows it to do what it does .
