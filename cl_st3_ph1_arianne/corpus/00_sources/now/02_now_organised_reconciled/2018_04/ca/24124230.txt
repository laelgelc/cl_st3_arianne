Prominent institutional investors , like the University of Toronto and the Ontario Teachers Pension Plan , have been slower to fully embrace the divestment movement in the same way as many of their American counterparts . <p> At least one Toronto city councillor believes City Council should reverse that trend , and should begin looking at all options available to ensure sure neither municipal capital nor municipal pension funds can profit from fossil fuel-based investments going forward . <p> " I pay into one of the biggest pensions in Canada OMERS , and I want to make sure that that money does n't have any @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Layton ( Ward 19 , Trinity-Spadina ) .
