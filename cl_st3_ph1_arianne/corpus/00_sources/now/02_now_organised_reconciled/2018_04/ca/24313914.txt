To improve air quality and tackle climate change , we need to electrify much of the economy . Switching vehicles from diesel to battery power is crucial in reducing greenhouse-gas emissions . Of course , the grid that charges the batteries produces emissions . But we can address that by phasing-out natural gas and expanding renewable energy sources such as wind and solar power .
