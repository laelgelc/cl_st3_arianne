<h> News Local <h> Get to know your septic system <p> The Greater Sudbury Watershed Alliance has announced its Septic Social , to take place May 3 from 7-9 p.m. at Lockerby Legion , 2200 Long Lake Rd . <p> " GSWA 's Septic Social is an opportunity to learn about the pros and cons of the many different types of private sewage treatment systems , the dos and don'ts of maintaining an effective sewage system , the inspection and regulatory responsibilities that all homeowners carry and the potential effects on our environment if it is n't working properly , " Richard Denton , chair of the Greater Sudbury Watershed Alliance , said in a release . <p> There will be beverages and snacks , great door prizes , and an opportunity to meet contractors and ask those in the know your burning questions about sewage treatment systems . <p> A little about the evening 's speakers : <p> Brittany Pellerin is a chemical engineering technologist currently employed by exp . Services in Sudbury , working as a lab technician in the materials testing division . She spends a great deal of her time working with septic clients and contractors looking to have percolation tests completed . <p> Ed Wierzbicki graduated from Ryerson University and worked as a public health inspector at the Sudbury and District Health Unit for 33 years . His responsibilities involved public health programs , and in particular private sewage disposal . After retiring in 2007 , he established his own business , Sewage Systems by Design , designing private sewage systems as an official building code designer under the Ontario Building Code . <p> Charles Ramcharan is an associate professor in the school of the environment at Laurentian University and an aquatic biologist working with the co-operative freshwater ecology unit at the Vale Living with Lakes Centre . His research includes invasive species , the recovery of lakes from acidification , the effects of small scale hydro on our rivers , and the effects of urbanization and climate change . <p> The Greater Sudbury Watershed Alliance " invites you to come out to our Septic Social , " organizers said in a release . " You will find it highly informative and very pertinent to ensuring clean and healthy lakes and rivers , and robust property values . "
