" ( SAUL LOEB / AFP/GETTY IMAGES ) <p> By Christal HayesUSA Today <p> Sat. , April 28 , 2018 <p> France 's first lady Brigitte Macron said Melania Trump is charming but is very constrained and ca n't even seem to go outside due to her role in Washington . <p> Macron opened up about the three-day Washington visit with her husband , French President Emmanuel Macron , and her time spent with her U.S. counterpart in an interview with Le Monde . <p> Macron said Melania is " actually really fun . We have the same sense of humour . We laugh a lot together . " But , she added , Melania , who has kept a relatively low-profile becoming first lady , " can not do anything . " <p> " She ca n't even open a window at the White House , " she said . " She ca n't go outside . She 's much more constrained than I am . I go out every day in Paris . " <p> Macron said Melania has a " strong personality " but is someone who " works hard to hide it , " and her stern and sometimes sad appearances in public is " over-interpreted . " <p> The French first lady said there is pressure all the time and she also has to " be careful what I say . I have the impression that every word is a word too many , I 'm constantly holding myself back . " <p> She said there 's never a time when you can just relax as first lady or " when you can be completely calm . " But , she added , she tries to live normally and does n't feel like she 's changed much . <p> " I 'm still the wife of Emmanuel Macron , not of the president . I do n't feel like a first lady , " she said . <p> During the three-day trip , she and Melania visited the National Gallery of Art and toured the paintings of French artist Paul Cezanne . <p> Meanwhile , Emmanuel Macron held closed-door negotiations with President Trump over everything from the U.S. involvement in Syria to the fate of the Iran nuclear accord , which he believes Trump will pull out from . <p> He also spoke at a joint session of Congress and appealed to politicians to reject the president 's America-first policies and construct a " new breed of multilateralism " equipped to confront urgent global threats -- from climate change to terrorism . <p> The White House also hosted a state dinner Tuesday where Macron applauded the bonds between the U.S. and France . <p> " May our friendship grow even deeper , may our kinship grow even stronger and may our sacred liberty never die , " he said during the event . <h> More from the Toronto Star &amp; Partners <h> LOADING <p> Copyright owned or licensed by Toronto Star Newspapers Limited .
