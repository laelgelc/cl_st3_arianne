<h> Economical reaffirms support for brokers through sponsorship of MSA <h> Economical reaffirms support for brokers through sponsorship of MSA Insurance Business <p> Reconfirming its commitment to the broker channel , Economical Insurance has renewed its title sponsorship of MSA Quarterly Outlook Reports . <p> The MSA Quarterly Outlook Reports provide summarized financial results for insurers , as well as research pieces on insurance industry trends . MSA Research , the producer of the quarterly reports , is one of Canada 's leading providers of insurance analytics . <p> " At a time when our industry is impacted by climate change , increased consolidation , technological innovation , and changing customer expectations , we believe now more than ever in sharing MSA 's industry data and insights with our broker partners to ensure they have all the information they need to succeed , " Economical chief distribution officer Tom Reikman said in a release . <p> " We are delighted that Economical has reaffirmed its support for this initiative coast-to-coast providing members of IBAO , IBAA , IBAM , IBANB , RCCAQ , IBABC , IBAS , IBANS , IBAN @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be ' in ' on the latest analysis , trends and developments affecting the markets in which they operate , " added MSA Research CEO Joel Baker . <p> The sponsorship renewal was met with praise by the broker community . <p> " We very much appreciate Economical 's strong support for the broker channel , " commented Insurance Brokers Association of Canada ( IBAC ) CEO Peter Braid . " Their renewed sponsorship for MSA 's research products ensures that brokers will continue to have access to valuable industry data and analysis that assists them in providing expert advice , choice , and advocacy for their clients . " <p> MSA 's first report following the renewed sponsorship will be distributed later this month .
