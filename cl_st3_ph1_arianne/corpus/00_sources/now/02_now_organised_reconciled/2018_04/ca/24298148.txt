<h> Bloomberg gives $4.5M to UN climate body , softening US cuts <p> BERLIN -- Former New York City Mayor Michael Bloomberg is donating $4.5 million to the United Nations body that oversees climate change negotiations . <p> Bloomberg , who has long championed the fight against global warming , will make up the shortfall in the agency 's budget caused by U.S. funding cuts . <p> Congress cut funding for the Bonn , Germany-based UNFCCC from $7.5 million in previous years to $3 million this year . <p> Bloomberg 's charitable foundation linked the donation to the billionaire philanthropist 's effort , with California Gov . Jerry Brown , to show that the United States remains committed to the Paris climate accord despite President Donald Trump 's plan to withdraw from the agreement . <p> In a statement late Sunday , Bloomberg Philanthropies said further donations would be made if the U.S. fails to pay its share of the budget in 2019 . <h> This Week 's Flyers <h> Comments <p> We encourage all readers to share their views on our articles and blog posts .
