<p> Southern Manitobans can expect to keep enjoying the warm weather we 've been having . <p> " We do see rather warmer conditions in the forecast , " said Natalie Hassell of Environment and Climate Change Canada . <p> " For the week of April 27th to the 4th of May , Southern Manitoba , or most of Southern Manitoba , should see above normal temperatures , " she added . <p> This is good news for those who enjoy the warmer weather , especially considering Manitoba had a late spring compared to last year . <p> " Temperatures were well below normal at the end of March and the first two weeks of April , " said Hassell . " Last year , the beginning of April was much warmer than it was this year . " <p> Hassell also said weather systems show some precipitation on the way . <p> " We do have some systems coming through , early next week we might get a little bit of precipitation in the Red River Valley . The models are suggesting some type of precipitation towards the end of the month of April . " <p> The precipitation will likely show in the form of rain , considering daytime temperatures . <p> " Right now the temperatures for that time period are above zero , " said Hassell . " So it might be a question of snow turning to rain as we go from night time to daytime . " <p> Finally , Hassell suggests we could see above average temperatures for the start of summer as well . <p> " For the months of May , June , July , part of Manitoba does have a slightly above normal temperature trend , " she said .
