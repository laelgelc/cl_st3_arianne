Cancelled flights , messed up social plans , multiple car collisions on slick roads- and the need to hunker down inside . Throw in confronting the effects of climate change and the mood is not optimistic . <p> On the upside , extreme weather has always been pure poetry . Read the first line of " Storm Fear " by Robert Frost , written in 1915 long before we realized we had most probably screwed up the planet : " When the wind works against us in the dark ... " <p> That is much more visceral than whining , " The weather is so gross today ! " which is what I heard from friends and family -- and admittedly myself- all this past weekend in Toronto , where , like most of southern Ontario we were subjected to a series of what seemed like extreme weather events that created existential despair in even the most hardy of us . <p> There was rain , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
