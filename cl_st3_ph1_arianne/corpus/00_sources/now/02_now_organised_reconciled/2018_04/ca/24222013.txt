<p> A major flood of British Columbia 's Fraser River or a coastal flood between now and 2100 could trigger economic losses of between $20 billion and $30 billion , a recent flood strategy report found . <p> Released in 2016 , Phase 1 of the Lower Mainland Flood Management Strategy showed that there is a " significant risk " of a large-magnitude flood in the province 's Lower Mainland . That risk is projected to worsen over the next 85 years , both in terms of flood frequency and size , due to sea level rise and other projected impacts of climate change . <p> A sandbag dike holds back the water from the Fraser River in Chilliwack , B.C. , on June 28 , 2012 . A major flood of B.C. 's Fraser River could impact as many as 2.2 million people , devastating residents , destroying First Nations ' lands and putting a damper on the province 's economy . The $20-30 billion estimates were mostly direct losses , Litke said , including impacts to buildings , infrastructure and the agriculture sector . " A fairly conservative estimate , " he said , adding that the study " did n't look very deeply at indirect economic losses related to damage and disruption of critical infrastructure .
