<h> Paris Climate Goals Depend on Promoting Biodiversity <p> A sweeping new report released on March 23rd emphasizes just how intertwined the challenges of climate change and loss of biodiversity truly are . <p> The Paris Climate Agreement and several other United Nations ( UN ) pacts " all depend on the health and vitality of our natural environment in all its diversity and complexity , " said Dr. Anne Larigauderie , executive secretary of the UN-backed organization behind the report . " Acting to protect and promote biodiversity is at least as important to achieving these commitments and to human well-being as is the fight against global climate change . " <p> The report comes from the efforts of more than 550 scientists in over 100 nations , corralled by an organization often dubbed " the IPCC for biodiversity .
