Some of the most pristine areas left in the world are " being choked " by plastic debris , she warned , citing a recent study which found plastic in almost every oyster tested in B.C. <p> Another study , by the Vancouver Aquarium last month , cited 25,000 microplastics per square cubic metre in the Strait of Georgia . <p> Plankton -- a species at the bottom of the marine food chain -- ingest microplastics . " <p> And changes have to make economic sense , she added . The challenge is that plastics are " very cheap , ubiquitous and useful .
