<p> The Ontario Ministry of Agriculture , Food and Rural Affairs says it continues to encourage public sector organizations to use more locally grown and produced food by delivering on the commitment to support the province 's agri-food sector . <p> The province has proclaimed the final requirement of the 2013 Local Food Act to encourage public sector organizations , such as hospitals and schools , to use more local food . These organizations represent a big market opportunity for local food and economic growth in communities across the province . <p> Consultations will be held throughout the fall with agri-food stakeholders , public sector organizations and the public to establish goals and targets , the ministry said in a news release . <p> " When we invest in our local food system , we grow our economy , strengthen rural communities and create good jobs for the future , " Agriculture Minister Jeff Leal said in a prepared statement . <p> " By proclaiming the final requirement of the Local Food Act , we demonstrate this government 's legacy in growing and making more Ontario local food available for all Ontarians . " <p> The measure is the final step in the government 's five-year plan to promote local agriculture . <p> Since 2013 , the ministry has : <p> Established the first full week in June as Local Food Week ; <p> Prepared an annual report highlighting progress made in local food activities ; <p> Created a tax credit for farmers who donate to local food banks and other community food organizations ; <p> Established local food literacy goals ; <p> And established local food access goals . <p> ... <p> The Ontario Federation of Agriculture and National Leasing have teamed up to bring federation members alternative business financing . <p> As a benefit of membership , federation members are eligible to receive up to $200 off their first lease payment on new or used farm equipment when they establish a lease line of credit , the federation says in a news release . <p> For more information about the program , contact Natalee Pollard , leasing specialist , at **35;2743;TOOLONG , or call 1-877-242-2203 , ext. 5898 . <p> ... <p> The Ecological Farmers of Ontario is sponsoring a workshop , entitled Exploring the Soil Carbon Sponge with Walter Jehne , on May 19 , from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. , in the Orchard Park Office Centre , Ignatius Jesuit Centre , 5420 Highway 6 North in Guelph . <p> The full-day workshop will be led by Jehne , a climate scientist , soil microbiologist and innovation strategist , who will discuss agriculture and climate change . <p> Founder of Healthy Soils Australia , Jehne has delivered lectures and directed seminars around the world . <p> Admission costs $75 for EFAO members , $95 for non-members , or $40 for the morning workshop only . <p> Lunch costs an extra $20 . <p> The workshop is organized in collaboration with Holistic Management Canada , the EFAO and the Soil Health Coalition . <p> There is also an open session from 7 p.m. to 8:30 p.m .
