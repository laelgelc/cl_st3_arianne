<h> A giant leap to save mankind and Mother Earth <p> Today is a momentous time on the final day of the forum at Manila Hotel for leaders of civil society , government and international community seeking ways to help the Philippines ' transition to a green economy . <p> The consultation forum for the " Philippine Tripartite Partnership for a Roadmap to fulfill the Paris Climate Accord " has gathered many of the best minds in our scientific , technological , academic , legal and legislative communities who " are ready to determine collective and coordinated adaptation and mitigation measures and to act fast towards a climate-smart economy . " <p> The historic event is graced by no less than Dr. Hoesung Lee , chair of the United Nation 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , whose leadership has newly focused on " innovative economic policies that not only reduce greenhouse gas emissions but also increase economic opportunity and raise millions of people out of poverty . " <p> The IPCC , awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 2007 together with former US Vice President Al @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ studies on climate change provided the rationale for the Paris Climate Agreement adopted in December 2015 . <p> My good friend , former Sen. Mother Earth is getting overheated with global warming as climate change wreaks havoc across the planet , causing cataclysmic storms , droughts , and other calamities that jeopardize food security and deepen poverty especially in the most vulnerable countries .
