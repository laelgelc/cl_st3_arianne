<h> Visayas News Roundup ( May 26 , 2017 ) <p> The Mines and Geosciences Bureau ( MGB-6 ) of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) must identify possible sinkholes in Kalibo , the provincial capital of Aklan . <p> Town Councilor Cynthia Dela Cruz said a topographic survey must be done soonest to identify these sinkholes . <p> Dela Cruz , who chairs the committee on environment , said a topographic survey is important for proper land use planning . It will be basis as to what buildings can be given construction permits . <p> According to dela Cruz , she prompted to sponsor a resolution calling MGB-6 's attention following a series of earthquakes and flooding incidents across the country . <p> Dela Cruz expressed concern that Kalibo may be prone to sinkholes since it is a coastal town . <p> She added that the proposed topographic survey may avail of funding from the People 's Survival Fund ( PSF ) , which is stipulated in Republic Act No. 10174 or the Climate Change Act of 2009 .
