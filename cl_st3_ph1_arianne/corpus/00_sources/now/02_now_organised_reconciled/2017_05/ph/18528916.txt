<p> Some of the seedlings were planted in owners ' private compounds while others were given to the City Environment and Parks Management Office Forestry and Regreening Division nurseries for use this planting season . <p> For every matured tree cut with permit , 30 seedlings are either planted or collected and stored at the botanical garden for future planting . To date , there are 5,880 seedlings prepared for planting in various venues around the city . <p> The move is also in support of the National Greening Program which aims to reforest 100 million hectares for the mitigation of climate change and environmental degradation .
