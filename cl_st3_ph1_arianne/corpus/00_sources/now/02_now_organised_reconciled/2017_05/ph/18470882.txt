<h> BRT project to affect more than 2,000 trees <h> BRT project to affect more than 2,000 trees <p> Forum . Engr . Nigel Paul Villarete , Cebu City administrator , assures stakeholders that the City will not neglect the affected trees once the BRT project is implemented . He made the assurance during the consultation forum at the USC Law Building . ( SunStar Photo/Amper Campa ? a ) <p> PROPONENTS of the Cebu Bus Rapid Transit ( BRT ) project released a final list of the more than 2,000 trees that will be affected once the project will be fully implemented . <p> Nida Cabrera , consultant of the Cebu City Environment and Natural Resources Office , said that aside from the 2,166 trees , some 16 shrubs along the BRT routes from Barangays Talamban to Bulacao will also be affected . <p> Of the 2,182 trees , close to 200 gmelina , mahogany and ipil-ipil trees will have to be cut due to their size and age . <p> At least 30 fruit-bearing trees like jackfruit , chicos , guavas , tamarind and mansanitas are also open @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The remaining trees , on the other hand , will be transferred to the sidewalks and center islands in the South Road Properties . Some of the trees will be included in the greenery project covered by the rehabilitation of the Inayawan Sanitary Landfill . <p> As for flower-bearing trees like fire , Thailand shower , calachuchi and banaba , Cabrera said that these will be transferred to the sidewalks of the newly widened roads once the project is finished . Some 10 mango trees will also be transferred along the General Maxilom Ave . <p> Aside from this , the City Government will also be replacing the old trees with 350,000 new ones . <p> The proponents assured the community stakeholders who attended a consultation on the matter yesterday that the trees will be handled with care and that the City will not be committing any environmental violations . <p> " One of the City 's commitments is to increase public awareness and action on the preservation and protection of the trees .
