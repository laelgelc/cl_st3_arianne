<h> Disaster response a priority in 1st PH-US Balikatan under Duterte <h> Patrick Quintos , ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA - Philippines and the United States launched the 33rd iteration of the Balikatan Exercises , the first under the administration of President Rodrigo Duterte . <p> Lt. General Oscar Lactao , Balikatan co-director , said this year 's focus of the 10-day military exercises include " non-traditional security challenges " such as humanitarian assistance , disaster response , and counter terrorism training . <p> " This prioritization is in line with the pressing issues of climate change , " said Lactao in the opening ceremony at Camp Aguinaldo . " There is also threat from foreign and local terrorist groups which is inimical to peace and security . " <p> Lactao said President Duterte wanted to focus on disaster response instead of territorial defense which was the highlight of last year 's activities amid escalating sea disputes with China .
