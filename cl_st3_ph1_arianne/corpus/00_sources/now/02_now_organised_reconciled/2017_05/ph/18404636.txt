<h> PPA shifting to renewable energy to make port operations cleaner <p> The Philippine Ports Authority ( PPA ) is greening all ports that it controls , using renewable energy for its operations throughout the archipelago to reduce its carbon footprints and be at par with international maritime stakeholders . <p> The " Green Port " project is part of the 25-year port development roadmap which local port stakeholders adopted in last month 's summit , to be submitted to President Rodrigo Duterte . <p> " Our international shipping counterparts have already agreed to reduce carbon emissions by a certain percentage in the next decade and it 's only fitting to do the same in our ports , " according to PPA Assistant General Manager for Operations Hector E. Miole . <p> PPA will use sustainable energy in all its aspects of operations , not only in the Head Office but including all Port Management Offices and Terminal Management Offices nationwide , he
