<h> ADB expert says LGUs not tapping climate change funding <p> Davao City -- A climate change expert from the Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) said there was a need for local and national government agencies to be proactive in seeking funding for climate-adaptive projects . <p> Ancha Srinivasan , ADB principal climate change specialist , said local government units ( LGUs ) in the country were not proactive enough in asking for funding for projects . <p> According to the ADB expert , there are at least $6 billion in funds available for climate adaptation measures that are not being fully utilized due to the lack of requests from LGUs and even the national government . <p> Srinivasan noted that this was noted " in areas where there are floods , not only because of rain but because of the poor management of risks . " <p> The ADB official said communities were not aware they could tap resources even within the government 's purse , such as the People 's Survival Fund . <p> The Local Government Code and other national laws such as the Disaster Risk @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ allow for the building of infrastructure for climate change adaptation measures .
