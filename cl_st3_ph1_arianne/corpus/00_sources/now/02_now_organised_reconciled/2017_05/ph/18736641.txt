<h> Cebu mango production down <p> Cebu City -- Warmer weather and the inadequate supply of nitrates have resulted in the decrease in the production of the world-renown Cebu mangoes , according to the Department of Agriculture ( DA ) . <p> DA Regional Agricultural and Fisheries Council chairman Lou Cabalde said Central Visayas harvested up to one million metric tons of mangoes during from 2003 to 2007 . Mango production , however , has fallen to a mere 700,000 to 900,000 metric tons . <p> Cabaldo said mango products in the mid-90s can have an early induction every December and can be harvested by April . But because of climate change , the schedule and production have changed . <p> As a result , the price of mango has risen to as much as P120 per kilo . <p> Aside from climate change , Cabalde attributed the dwindling mango production to the strict monitoring of inducers , including potassium and calcium nitrates .
