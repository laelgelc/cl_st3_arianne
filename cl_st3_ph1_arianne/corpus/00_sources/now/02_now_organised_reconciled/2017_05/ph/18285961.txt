<h> BOI okays perks for container hotel <p> Eco Container Hotel , made out of stacked up steel containers with investments of P29.1 million , has qualified for tax inventives granted by the Board of Investments ( BOI ) . <p> Owned by Ecohotels , Inc. , the tourist facility located in Tagaytay is made up of steel containers stacked up to build a three-storey high building with additional structural support to ensure safety and comfort . <p> Steel containers though are designed to be stacked-up up to seven levels high without the need for additional structural support . <p> Based on its application , Ecohotels , Inc. stated their containers are made of Corten Steel which is considered very thick , strong and durable . <p> The 33-room Eco Container Hotel boasts uniqueness as it is made of repurposed shipping containers . <p> The installation of proper wall and ceiling insulation plus a concrete roof deck at the top floor , combined with passive cooling and excellent cross ventilation , with the option of air-conditioning , will make their container rooms very comfortable and cozy just like any other @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ equipped with a number of eco-friendly features such as solar panels , water recycling , energy saving lights and upcycled materials once in operation . <p> The Eco Container Hotel seeks to provide unique but comfortable accommodation as Tagaytay City is one of the country 's rapidly growing tourist attractions . <p> The 33-room Eco Container Hotel started operations in April , 2017 with 25 personnel . <p> " Hopefully , this will inspire other hotels to adopt eco-friendly innovations in the future in response to climate change , " said BOI Managing Head Ceferino S.
