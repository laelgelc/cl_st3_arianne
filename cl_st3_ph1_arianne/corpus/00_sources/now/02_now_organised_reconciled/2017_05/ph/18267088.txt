<h> Davao region 's economic growth 3rd highest in PH <p> Davao City -- Davao region 's gross regional domestic product ( GRDP ) grew by 9.4% in 2016 , the highest among Mindanao 's six regions and the third highest among 18 regions in the country . <p> Davao Region 's 2016 performance outpaced 2015 's 8.2% . It also outgrew the national GDP at 6.9% . <p> In a press conference on the 2016 Economic Performance of Davao , National Economic Development Authority ( NEDA ) -Davao director Maria Lourdes Lim said the service sector contributed the biggest at 51.1% to the region 's GRDP growth , followed by industry with 36.7% , and agriculture , hunting , forestry and fishing ( AHFF ) with 12.2% share . <p> The services sector registered a sustained 7.5% growth for 2015 until 2016 . <p> This sector was valued last year at P170.2 billion , surpassing the lower limit target of 7.4% on the back of strong financial and trade subsectors . <p> She also attributed the improved performance of the services sector to the recovery of the exports during @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as the region 's top export of commodity and gold in semi-manufactured form among the top 10 commodities . <p> Also , she said Information Communications Technology-Business Process Outsourcing ( ICT-BPO ) continued to expand .
