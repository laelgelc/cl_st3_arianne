<h> PH roadmap for Paris Agreement on climate change pushed <p> With the country 's ratification of a landmark global deal on climate change , various civil society groups , national government agencies and international community have gathered together to help define a Philippine roadmap that will enable the country to fulfill its obligations under the Paris Agreement . <p> " The aspirations of Paris Agreement will not happen on its own . There has to be a roadmap on what the agencies and nations , big or small , must do , " Senator Loren Legarda said during the opening of the three-day " Tripartite Conference on the Philippines Roadmap for the Paris Accord " held at the historic Manila Hotel last Monday , May 29 . <p> The Paris Agreement on climate change , which was adopted in December 2015 and entered into force in November 2016 , has been ratified by the Philippines just last March . <p> The Agreement is hailed as raising global ambition on climate change , seeking to cap global average temperature increase to 1.5 ? C , a position that the Philippines @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ encourage urgent action and mobilization of support on the part of developed countries . <p> Legarda said the budget of the government agencies should support the country 's nationally determined contributions or NDCs . <p> According to Heherson Alvarez , chairman of the Advisory Board of the Climate Institute , the country 's capacity to handle the carbon reduction " can be done systematically even before 2030 . " <p> " We have to stop the increase in temperature to 2 degrees Centigrade but we will struggle not to go beyond 1.5 degrees Centigrade . We are now 1.3 degrees Centigrade . If we did hit 1.5 degrees Centigrade , the glacier in the Himalayas and Greenland will begin to melt , " he pointed out . <p> Although there were some setbacks in the country 's efforts to mitigate the effects of climate change , such as in 2015 when the Department of Energy approved the construction of 20 coal-fired power plants in addition to the 17 existing coal-fired power plants , Alvarez said the consultation can help re-determine the country 's goals .
