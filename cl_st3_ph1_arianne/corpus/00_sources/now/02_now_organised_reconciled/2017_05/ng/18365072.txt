<p> Prof. Catherine Falade , a Malariologist at the University College Hospital ( UCH ) , says the prevalence of malaria is higher in under-five children during rainy season as a result of climate change . <p> Falade , who is also a consultant Pharmacologist said this in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) , in Ibadan on Wednesday . <p> She has urged parents to take necessary measures in tackling malaria during the rainy season . <p> According to her , children under 5 and 10 years should wear warm clothes to school and mothers should always endeavour to make their children sleep under mosquito treated nets .
