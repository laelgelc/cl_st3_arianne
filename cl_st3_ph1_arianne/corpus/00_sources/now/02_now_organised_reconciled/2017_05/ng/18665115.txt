<p> The G7 refers to a group of 7 advanced nations with more than 64% of the net global wealth ( $263 trillion ) according to the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) . The group consists of Canada , France , Germany , Italy , Japan , the United Kingdom and the United States . <p> The current and 43rd G7 summit commenced on Friday and is taking place in Taormina , Italy where members of the G7 have called on internet service providers to intensify efforts at cracking down on terrorist groups promoting themselves on the internet , according to the BBC . <p> World leaders present are however said to have failed to agree on steps to combat climate change .
