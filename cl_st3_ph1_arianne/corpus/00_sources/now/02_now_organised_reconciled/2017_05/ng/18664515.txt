<h> Nigeria to issue first green bond in next few weeks -- officials <p> Nigeria plans to launch its delayed " green " bond -- the first for an emerging market country -- within a few weeks , the country 's former environment minister said . <p> Amina Mohammed , now deputy secretary-general of the United Nations , told the Thomson Reuters Foundation proceeds from the sovereign bond -- which is expected to raise about 20 billion naira ( $63.6 million ) from its first tranche -- would be used to fund renewable energy , transport and agriculture projects . <p> Nigeria 's green bond could lay the groundwork for other African countries to follow suit , she added . <p> Green bonds have been around for a decade but sovereign borrowers had been absent from the market , which was traditionally dominated by international development banks . <p> The green bond market has enjoyed strong growth in the last two years , with issuance jumping 105 percent last year alone to a record $72 billion ,
