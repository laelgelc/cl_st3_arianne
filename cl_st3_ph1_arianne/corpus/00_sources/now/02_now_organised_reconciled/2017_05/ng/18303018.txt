<h> See the First Plastic Bottle House Constructed in Nigeria Photo <p> The first plastic bottle house in the whole of West Africa has been unveiled in Nigeria by a group called the Developmental Association for Renewable Energy . <p> A group , Developmental Association for Renewable Energy in Nigeria ( DARE ) , said it has constructed the first plastic bottle house in West Africa <p> The Chief Executive Officer of the group , Mr Yahaya Ahmed made the disclosure when he conducted newsmen round the house , located at Yelwa village on Kaduna-Zaria highway . <p> Ahmed said the house was made from plastic bottles as part of the group 's efforts to encourage recycling of waste materials in the country , create jobs and ensure safer environment . <p> According to him , a plastic bottle house is 20 times stronger than brick walls . <p> " A plastic bottle house can last for more than 200 years if constructed properly and carefully and is fireproof , bulletproof , earthquake resistant and can adapt to all kind of climate changes .
