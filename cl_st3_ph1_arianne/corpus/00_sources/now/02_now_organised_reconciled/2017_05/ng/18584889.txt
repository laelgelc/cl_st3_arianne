Several other coastline settlements in Lekki have been touched and threatened by the rising sea levels in recent past . For instance , the Barb Wire community is just next to the popular Alpha Beach before Lafiagi village which had been torched in the past before the intervention of the Lagos State Government , which erected granite sea groins from the Victoria Island to Alpha Beach . This intervention was said to have provided succour for the distressed communities . <p> But this ' temporary ' intervention did not cover Barb Wire community on the same Lekki Coast , as the community is now crying out for help and intervention by the government , especially the Federal Government .
