<h> Fed Govt eyes N20b green bond <p> The Federal Government plans to launch its delayed " green " bond worth N20 billion within the next few weeks , former environment minister , Amina Mohammed , has said . <p> Mohammed , now deputy secretary-general of the United Nations , told the Thomson Reuters Foundation proceeds from the sovereign bond -- which is expected to raise about 20 billion naira ( $63.6 million ) from its first tranche -- would be used to fund renewable energy , transport and agriculture projects . <p> Nigeria 's green bond could lay the groundwork for other African countries to follow , she added . Green bonds have been around for a decade but sovereign borrowers had been absent from the market , which was traditionally dominated by international development banks . <p> The green bond market has enjoyed strong growth in the last two years , with issuance jumping 105 percent last year alone to a record $72 billion , according to data compiled by Thomson Reuters and the Climate Bond Initiative . <p> Poland and France have both launched sovereign green bonds @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ initiated the Nigerian bond while she was environment minister , said its issue had been postponed from an initial date at the end of March because the country 's budget had to be passed first . <p> " We 've just had the budget approved , so I believe Nigeria will look , probably within the next few weeks , to doing ( the bond issue ) , " Mohammed said on last Thursday on the sidelines of a U.N . conference in Mexico on disaster risk reduction . <p> Announcing the plan for the bond in February , the environment ministry said it was aimed at widening Nigeria 's funding options and diversifying the OPEC member 's oil-dependent economy , which is the largest in Africa . <p> Peter Tarfa , director of the climate change department in the environment ministry , told a separate conference in Barcelona that the bond would be launched in the coming month , and would generate resources for climate change projects , including forest-planting and a mass rapid transit system .
