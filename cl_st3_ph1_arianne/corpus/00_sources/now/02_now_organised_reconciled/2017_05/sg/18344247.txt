The phases affect the strength of the trade winds that blow east-to-west across the tropical Pacific Ocean . These winds are themselves driven by warm air rising along the equator and the rotation of the Earth . <p> During a negative IPO phase , the trade winds strengthen , driving heat into the deep Pacific Ocean , which brings cooler water to the surface .
