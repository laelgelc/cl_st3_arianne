<h> Cattle ban outrage : After Kerala , ' beef fest ' organised in IIT Madras <p> A group of students at the Indian Institute of Technology ( IIT ) Madras conducted a beef festival , showing their agitation against the Center recent order against selling cattle thus imposing an indirect beef ban . <p> More than 50 students supported the cause by participating in this fest late on 28 may 's night . <p> Earlier on May 27 , Students ' Federation of India ( SFI ) staged similar protest in Kerala by eating beef outside University College , Trivandrum . <p> Similar fest was also organised at different locations across Kerala . <p> Minister for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Harsh Vardhan on 26 may ordered that the ministry has notified the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals ( Regulation of Livestock Markets ) Rules , 2017 to ensure that the sale of cattle is not meant for slaughter purposes . <p> " Aim of the rules is very specific . It is only to regulate the animal market and the sale of cattle in these markets , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the rule provides for a strict animal monitoring committee and an animal market committee at the local level , " Vardhan told ANI . <p> He said the seller and buyer both have to ensure that the cattle is not being bought or sold in the market for slaughter purposes . <p> " An undertaking to this effect has to be given to the member secretary of the animal market committee from the seller as well as the buyer , " Vardhan added . <p> As per the notification , cattle is defined as " bulls , bullocks , cows , buffalos , steers , heifers and calves and camels " . <p> The rules also state that the purchaser shall not sacrifice the animal for any religious purpose or sell it to a person outside the state without permission and must keep in with the state 's cattle protection laws .
