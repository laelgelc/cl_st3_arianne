The following are some key climate measures that illustrate the risks of global warming . <h> 1.1 DEGREE CELSIUS <p> Earth 's average surface temperature hit a record level last year for the third straight year since records began in 1880 . The global average temperature was 1.1 deg C over pre-industrial levels , and 0.06 deg C above the previous record set in 2015 , according to the World Meteorological Organisation . <p> The 21st century has already seen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ summer sea ice shrank to 4.14 million sq km last year - the second lowest after 2012 when it reached 3.39 million sq km . The Arctic Ocean could be ice-free in summer as early as 2030 . <p> At the other end of the world in Antarctica , sea ice also hit its lowest extent ever recorded by satellites at the end of summer . High-altitude glaciers , meanwhile , declined in surface area in 2015 for the 36th year in a row . <h> 400 PARTS PER MILLION <p> The atmospheric concentrations of the three most potent greenhouse gases - carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) , methane and nitrous oxide - all hit new highs last year . <p> For the first time on record , in 2015 , the amount of CO2 in the atmosphere averaged 400 parts per million ( ppm ) . <p> Most climate scientists agree that greenhouse gas concentrations in the atmosphere must be capped at 450 ppm of CO2 equivalent for a fighting chance at limiting average global warming to 2 deg C over pre-industrial levels .
