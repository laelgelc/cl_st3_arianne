<p> Union Minister Dr. Harsh Vardhan has been given the additional charge of Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , in addition to his existing portfolios . <p> The portfolio went vacant after Anil Madhav Dave , who was holding the charge of very important ministry , passed away this morning . <p> Dr. Harsh Vardhan holds the charge of the Ministry of Science and Technology ( India ) and the Ministry of Earth Sciences . <p> Dave 's last rites , who passed away earlier today following a cardiac arrest , will be performed in Madhya Pradesh 's Indore . <p> Minister of State ( MoS ) ( Home ) Jitendra Singh told the media about the cause of his death and last rites ceremony . <p> " It was a cardiac arrest . He will be taken to Bhopal this evening . Thereafter , he will be taken to his ancestral place Indore where his last rites will be performed . Talks regarding the same are on with his family and his Madhya Pradesh members , " Singh said .
