<h> Water plant gets efficiency boost <p> Japan-based company Metawater will provide its power-saving inverter technology to a water treatment plant in Cambodia 's capital through an arrangement that will help the Japanese government earn carbon reduction credits , according to a Nikkei Asian Review report . <p> The inverter equipment , which controls water-pumping rates to match demand fluctuations , will be installed at the Phum Prek treatment plant of Phnom Penh Water Supply Authority . <p> The plant 's daily processing capacity is 150,000 tonnes . " In Cambodia , electricity accounts for about 30 percent of costs to operate water purification facilities - a higher ratio than in Japan , " the report said . <p> Although water demand in Cambodia varies widely between the monsoon and dry seasons , the facility has not adjusted pumping rates to match the changes , so inefficiency has been an issue . <p> However , Phnom Penh Water Supply Authority general director Sim Sitha said he had not yet received any information about the provision .
