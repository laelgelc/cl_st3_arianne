<p> SMK Sentul Utama and SK Batu Muda came out tops in the MyCommunity Project competition , which won them the Frog classroom makeover as a result of their creative projects . <p> YTL 's Frog classroom programme was established to transform classrooms throughout Malaysia into modern learning spaces , designed to promote technology enabled and student focused learning . <p> The MyCommunity Project is where schools carry out projects based on the United Nations ' 17 sustainable development goals in efforts to transform the world , which is a global initiative supported by the Department of Environment . <p> Among the themes highlighted by the schools were quality education , climate change , sustainable cities and communities , and zero poverty . <p> SMK Sentul Utama 's project , under the Secondary School category , involved baking and selling cupcakes . Their winning entry , Jolly Cupcakes , was aimed to boost confidence among students to speak and communicate in English . <p> Teacher Rajamani A.
