<h> Nation <h> ' Holy See ties will promote moderation ' <p> Raising the flag : The Malaysian Embassy in Vatican City . The Malaysian flag can be seen on level three towards the right . <p> PUTRAJAYA : The start of diplomatic relations between Malaysia and The Holy See shows the Government 's commitment to improving existing mutual ties between both countries . <p> It will also help promote better religious understanding and is in line with Malaysia 's Global Movement of Moderates initiative , said Foreign Minister Datuk Seri Anifah Aman . <p> " Malaysia strongly believes that moderation is a fundamental element for fostering greater integration and in ensuring peaceful coexistence through the rejection of extremism that undermines the universal values of religion , " he said . <p> He was speaking at the inauguration of the Chancery of the Malaysian Embassy to The Holy See yesterday , which was held exactly 11 months after the first resident ambassador , Tan Sri Bernard Dompok , presented his credentials to Pope Francis . <p> The inauguration was attended by about 100 guests , including the Assessor for General @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Holy See , Dean of the Diplomatic Corps to the Holy See and members of the diplomatic corps . <p> Anifah said that through the establishment of the embassy , Malaysia was able to participate in and contribute to worldwide dialogues on various regional and international issues , particularly on promoting religious understanding , education , poverty eradication , migration , and climate change . <p> He said one of the biggest challenges in today 's world was fighting extremist groups which were growing regionally and internationally . <p> Extremism was a cancer to the world because it created chaos and political instability , he said . <p> " We have witnessed in the media the chaos caused by the deeds of extremists . There is nothing to gain from such deeds . In fact , the suffering of innocent people is sad and inflammatory , " he said . <p> Anifah said Malaysia was a strong advocate in the promotion of moderation as a way of life and as a means to resolve misunderstandings in society through dialogue and education . <p> " Malaysia 's efforts to promote moderation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , dynamic and progressive nation rising to the challenges of a multicultural society , " he said .
