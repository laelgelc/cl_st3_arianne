<p> ADDIS ABABA -- The United Nations Economic Commission for Africa ( UN-ECA ) has stressed the importance of investing in effective management of environmental assets in order to reduce poverty , hunger and disease . <p> The Director of the ECA Special Initiatives Division , Fatima Denton , told a meeting of the UN-ECA Committee on Sustainable Development here Wednesday that " we want to be an ecosystem that will exploit innovations and technology for shared prosperity , an ecosystem that will use climate actions as the new trigger for ushering in low-carbon development " . <p> Furthermore , she added , the Green Economy is synonymous with a new growth model which will create jobs and new skills for the youth and support intrinsic rights to explore other forms of development . <p> Natural resources management remains a critical lever for economic growth and for reducing poverty and hunger , she said , while stressing that current natural resources practices are unsustainable and " we are gradually pushing the boundaries beyond their capacity for renewal " . <p> According to Denton , the reliance on natural resources and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ low adaptive capacity made the continent highly vulnerable to climate change . African economies as a whole are still primarily agrarian , with little value against a weak infrastructure where records of infrastructure penetration remain extremely low , she said . <p> Touching on inequality , she said it was still rearing its ugly head in Africa , which is home to seven out of 10 most unequal countries in the world . Denton said the African growth story had not come to terms with inclusive development . <p> The Committee on Sustainable Development was established by the UN-ECA as one of its functional and sectoral subsidiary organs .
