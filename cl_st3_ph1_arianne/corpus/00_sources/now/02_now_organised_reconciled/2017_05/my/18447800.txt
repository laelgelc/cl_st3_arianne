<h> World <h> China 's Belt and Road communique pushes open trade centred on WTO <p> BEIJING ( Reuters ) - Leaders at China 's Belt and Road summit on Monday agreed to promote a rules-based , open and multilateral trading system with the World Trade Organisation at its core , according to the summit communique . <p> The 30 heads of states who signed the document , including China , said that they recognised the challenges the world economy faced and welcomed the initiative to improve connectivity between Asia and Europe . <p> It was important to expanding trade and investment based on a level playing field , they said . <p> The countries also encouraged all signatories of the Paris climate change agreement to fully implement the accord .
