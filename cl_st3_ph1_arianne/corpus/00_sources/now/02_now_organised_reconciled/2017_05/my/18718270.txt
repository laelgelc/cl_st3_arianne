But for Safira , an elfin 11-year-old , this meal is a luxury . Provided free by her school , it 's also the first and only meal she will have for the rest of the day . <p> The daughter of a factory employee and a day laborer who travels hours each day to find work , there is little money to cook meals at home . Dinner is usually a banana or two . When the school does not provide lunch , Safira goes hungry . <p> " On days without school meals I do n't bring anything from home .
