<h> World <h> Trump says he will decide on Paris climate deal next week <p> TAORMINA , Italy ( Reuters ) - U.S. President Donald Trump said in a Twitter post on Saturday that he would make a decision on whether to support a landmark international agreement on climate change next week . <p> " I will make my final decision on the Paris Accord next week ! " he tweeted on the final day of a Group of Seven ( G7 ) summit in Italy at which he refused to bow to pressure from allies to back the landmark 2015 agreement .
