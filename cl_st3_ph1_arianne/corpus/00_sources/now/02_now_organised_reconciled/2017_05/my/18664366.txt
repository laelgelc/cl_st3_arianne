He passed away in Falls Church , Virginia , family said . <p> Brzezinski was an active professor and author well into his 80s . <p> He did not support President Donald Trump 's election , and criticised his foreign policy as vague . <p> In 2011 , he penned Strategic Vision : America and the Crisis of Global Power , arguing that US strength abroad was key to global @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ability to foster " social consensus and democratic stability " at home , he said , referring to the need for less income inequality and a meaningful response to climate change . <p> He developed his ties to Carter on the Trilateral Commission , the group David Rockefeller created in 1973 as a forum for political and business leaders from North America , Western Europe and Japan .
