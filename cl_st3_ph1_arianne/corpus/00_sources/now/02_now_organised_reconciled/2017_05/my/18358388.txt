" <p> He said the academy has launched the International Outreach Initiative and is cooperating closely with the World Academy of Sciences for the Advancement of Science in Developing Countries . <p> The Chinese academy has trained about 1,800 people from Belt and Road countries , focusing on common issues such as climate change , water security , green energy , and disaster prevention and relief , Bai said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ China also has similar issues , " he said . <p> " Our research aims to help .
