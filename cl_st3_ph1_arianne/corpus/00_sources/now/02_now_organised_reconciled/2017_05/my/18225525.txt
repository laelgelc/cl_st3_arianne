<p> As Trump appointees take their new positions in the federal bureaucracy , a legal battle is escalating over alleged intimidation of the civil servants who make the government function . <p> The nonprofit United to Protect Democracy is suing the Departments of Energy and of Health and Human Services for records that would document or disprove such allegations . One of the group 's lawyers , Ben Berwick , wrote in an online post that the goal is " protecting the civil service from purges , intimidation or politicization . " <p> The DOE suit seeks documents concerning an attempt by the Trump transition team to get the names of civil servants and contractors who worked on climate change programs . The HHS case seeks similar documents that would have targeted employees who worked or expressed views on the Affordable Care Act or abortion rights . <p> United to Protect Democracy , which includes some lawyers from the Obama White House , earlier filed Freedom of Information Act requests for the documents .
