<h> Letters <h> Slick victory over fake news <p> A NEW topic has made the headlines recently . This concerns the spread of fake news . News which can not be substantiated by facts . There is always a hidden agenda behind such made up news . It became a big issue during the US Presidential elections . <p> Listening to some expert discourse on the issue , it is apparently not a new phenomenon . The spreading of untrue news has always been a strategy to shape public opinion . Take the claim of Iraq having weapons of mass destruction . That piece of news was fuelled by the legitimate western media . But now , after all the evidence came out , that news has been proven to be grossly inaccurate . The hidden agenda was to create a case to remove Saddam from power . Many would not be surprised there may have been other such invented news in the past . Most were unfortunately orchestrated by the main line media . <p> With the advent of the Internet , the online social media has taken over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ reach is even more devastating . The word viral is no longer synonymous with only the spread of H1N1 . It is more synonymous with the spread of news on the Internet . Though sometimes done for fun , fake news has metamorphed into a phenomenon which can be damaging for society . Already we hear of such news being cooked up to tarnish the image of individuals . The regulatory people have come out with a new approach to suppress such practices . They have created a website called dotcom to provide the platform for the community to crosscheck and validate such news . Time will tell whether it will be an effective instrument to rein in the spread of fake news . <p> However , fake news is not new to the palm oil industry . This May , the palm oil industry in Malaysia celebrates 100 years of the first planting of oil palm in Tennamaram Estate way back in 1917 . The annual earnings from palm oil are fast approaching the RM100bil mark . It would not be wrong to say that Malaysia has made what palm oil it is today . From nowhere , palm oil now leads the world trade in edible oils , accounting for over 30% . But the success has not been without its share of threats and challenges . Fake news has been one of the biggest threat . <p> In the early years of its entry into global competition , the fake news concerned the nutrition value of palm oil . The spread of such news was rampant in the West . They claimed palm oil was bad for health . Palm oil was described as the edible oil which contributed the most to the rise in heart related diseases . All such unsubstantiated claims have been proven wrong through research and sound science . Now the claim is that palm oil is bad for the environment . And that palm oil will not help the global fight against climate change . But the facts say otherwise .
