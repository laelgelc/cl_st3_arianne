<h> Time to activate all peace actors to forestall poll chaos <h> share <p> To the majority of Kenyans , the General Election is a moment of trepidation . Rather than being a time to celebrate our hard-earned democracy , tension usually becomes extreme and people can not wait to get over the event . Of course , there are those who reap big by making hay while the sun shines ! <p> But that is a story for another day . Since the advent of multiparty politics about 25 years ago , every election period in Kenya has been accompanied by some level of violence . The culture of competitive politics is still not ingrained in the national psyche . <p> We vote along tribal lines , the winner being the party or candidate with the " tyranny of numbers " . After what happened in the 2007/8 post-election violence ( PEV ) , we all know that the risk of extreme violence ( VE ) is not an empty threat . <p> Ever since PEV , an ardent call for peace has become a major , if not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ players outside the core electoral players have taken up the responsibility of promoting inter-party harmony in the electoral process , during campaign time , elections and the post-election period respectively . <p> With the current Constitution , the electoral field has gradually become complicated and overcrowded . According to the Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission , there is an unprecedented number of electoral candidates this time , including presidential , gubernatorial , senatorial , MPs , women representatives , and ward representatives . <p> This year has also seen the phenomenal rise of independent candidates . Luckily , Kenya 's development partners have continued to support the necessary peace initiatives . <p> For instance , programmes like NIWETU and Uraia , both funded by the United States Agency for International Development , seek to mobilise various stakeholders to spread peace messages across the country , especially in certain political hotspots . The target include individuals who are potential perpetrators of violence either through sympathy , advocacy , material or moral support . <p> There are also those who are unaware of the threat posed by extreme violence and might , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ peace initiatives must work in partnership with trusted influence persons to disseminate messages that counter VE . <p> Appropriate communication channels range from social media to traditional community information-sharing networks . Indeed , ensuring peace during Kenya 's elections is a daily activity until the elected president is sworn in . Peace monitors must constantly keep their feelers on high alert , picking any vibes that threaten to blow out of proportion . <p> In any VE eventuality , we need rapid response teams to analyse the situation immediately it arises to help return normalcy . The media here , and communication in general , is key . The media should not appear to take sides in the political divide . <p> Oftentimes , messaging and media coverage around violence has been characterised by sensationalised reports on violence and opportunist political rhetoric , all of which exacerbate divisions . <p> Journalists have not just a professional , but also a moral obligation to ensure that their messaging is absolutely neutral , and aimed at creating rapprochement in conflict situations . Insinuations that can incite should also be avoided . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ side of the bargain . True , State security organs must be eternally vigilant during this period . The police and intelligence services need to work closely together , constantly exchanging notes about what is happening on the ground . <p> Meanwhile , it is reassuring to note that the European Union delegation to Kenya has noted , and appreciates the government 's promise to ensure free , fair and peaceful elections . IEBC must ensure it conducts a free and fair poll regardless of any attempts to manipulation or intimidation . <p> The Kriegler Report that investigated the causes of the 2007-2008 PEV observed that the then Electoral Commission of Kenya under the chairmanship of the late Samuel Kivuitu , played a considerable role in exacerbating the situation , by stating that it could not tell exactly who won the presidential poll .
