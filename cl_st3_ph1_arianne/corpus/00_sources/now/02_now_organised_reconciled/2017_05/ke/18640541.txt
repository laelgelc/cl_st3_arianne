<h> Kenya food prices rated among highest globally <h> share <p> Food commodity prices in Kenya are among the highest in the world , says a report by a policy research institute . Tegemeo Institute of Agricultural Policy and Development yesterday said prices of basic food commodities are more than three times those in the global market . <p> " A tonne of maize in the local market costs Sh50,000 compared to Sh15,000 in the global market . Equally , prices of sugar stand at Sh120,000 per tonne in Kenya and Sh40,000 in the global market , " said Timothy Njagi , Tegemeo institute research fellow and development economist yesterday in Nairobi . <p> Tegemeo Institute , an affiliate of Egerton University conducts research and analysis on policy in agriculture , rural development , natural resources and environment . Njagi added that low output and high cost of production are to blame for the escalating prices . <p> Currently , the cost of producing a 90kg bag of maize is Sh2,150 for small-scale farmers and Sh1,800 for large-scale farmers .
