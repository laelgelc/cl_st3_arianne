<h> Integrated control required for fall armyworm menace <h> share <p> Massive awareness campaigns , scientific innovation , quick and coordinated action as well as multi-institutional collaboration will be required to tackle the tenacious fall armyworm menace , a consultative meeting on the pest recent held in Nairobi was told . <p> The fall armyworm attacks over 80 different plant species including maize -- which is a staple food of over 200 million Africans . It origininated in the Americas . Kenya government officials warn that the pest has been observed in various parts of Kenya . This includes Kisii , Busia , TransNzoia , Bungoma , Uasin Gishu , Nandi , Machakos and Embu counties , among others . <p> " The truly frightening risk of the fall armyworm to food security in Africa must be recognised and tackled with holistic integrated pest management approach , " says B M Prasanna , a director with the Global Maize Programme at the International Maize and Wheat Improvement Centre ( CIMMYT ) . <p> The major threat is to smallholder farmers , already reeling from the effects of climate change and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not eliminate the pest from Africa -- now that it is here . <p> But we can provide support for farmers and give options to manage their crops against the fall armyworm , " said Dr Prasanna . The female fall armyworm can lay up to 1,000 eggs at a time and can produce multiple generations quickly in tropical environments . The moth has been known to fly up to 1,600km in 30 hours . <p> It is estimated that the loss of Africa 's maize could hit $3 billion ( Sh306 billion ) in the 2017/18 . Among the key ideas for building are developing host plant resistance , use of low-cost chemicals , protective clothing and spraying equipment . <p> Others include development of drought , pest resistant hybrid crops , identification of predatory insects , pheromone traps to lure moths to target and destroy eggs and larvae as well as use of bio-pesticides.
