According to UK-based Public Affairs Networking , public affairs involves an organisation 's relationship with stakeholders . <p> The latter include individuals or groups with an interest in the organisation 's affairs , such as politicians , civil servants , customers , local communities , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ business groups , charities , unions and the media . <p> Public affairs practitioners engage stakeholders to explain organisational policies and views on public policy issues , assisting policymakers and legislators in amending or laying down better policy and legislation . <p> They provide statistical and factual information and lobby on issues which could impact upon the organisation 's ability to operate successfully . Now , while public affairs has some considerable public relations components like government relations , media communications , issue management , corporate and social responsibility , information dissemination and strategic communications advice , it goes way further in attempting to influence public policy . <p> It is not merely consensus or image building . The end game is much more global than the factors surrounding the immediate business or operational environment of an organisation . <p> The government has made a good attempt at public affairs through the establishment of the Presidential Strategic Communications Unit ( PSCU ) . Although the unit has been designed along the modus operandi of America 's Bureau of Public Affairs , our version is a one-way street . PSCU lacks a clear policy for its engagement with the public . <p> Many government press releases read or sound like government directives that the mass is unable to comprehend or identify with . Communication experts need to understand the nature , import and need for public affairs . Practitioners of public affairs spend half of working time on the ground , returning to the office for more research and to draft feedback for the people . <p> Consequently , the information they finally disseminate talks directly to everyone , thus resonating with their fears , hopes and aspirations .
