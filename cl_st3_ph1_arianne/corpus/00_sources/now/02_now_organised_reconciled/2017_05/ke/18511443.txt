<h> Good governance tenets critical for new PRSK team <h> share <p> The PR fraternity in Kenya went to the polls in April this year in the biennial elections of the Public Relations Society of Kenya ( PRSK ) . It was a pretty professional affair as the Society used the services of an external player , the Institute for Education in Democracy , to manage the whole electoral process . The last PRSK elections were held in September 2014 . <p> The numbers are also increasing , and PRSK 's secretariat was all excited about the " historical " 300 pax attendance of the 2017 annual general meeting . PRSK committees , through which the governing council plans and implements its main agenda , were also revamped for more effectiveness . <p> The committees include partnerships , fundraising and consultancy ; governance , integrity and compliance ; education , training and research ; membership and events and ; publicity and editorial . What has been lacking , though , is critical mentorship so the new kids on the block come in with the right attitude about the profession . <p> According to the chairman 's report for the year 2017 , the executive committee spent the last two years trying to streamline PRSK 's governance by developing a strategic plan , creating systems to enhance financial accountability , establishing an independent secretariat , and re-examining the Society 's legal framework . <p> A lot of work has been put on the draft strategic plan , which basically aims at transforming the society to the Kenya Institute of Public Relations ( KIPR ) and the planned enactment of the PR Practitioners Bill . <p> Overall , work in progress includes six priority areas as follows : Legal status , skills development , re-positioning , resource mobilisation , establishment of independent secretariat and developing a communication strategy . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planned restructuring is mandatory accreditation . This will help to weed out imposters who have at times given PR a bad reputation . <p> To make it easy , one should get automatic accreditation on enrollment of membership . Although the Society is cash strapped , the fee should take into consideration the fact that many new members are entry level practitioners struggling to get a job , or just recently employed . <p> Once KIPR gets its act together , there is a lot of money to be made through organising seminars , workshops and conferences , and undertaking programmes in both capacity building and continuous professional development . <p> For the task that PRSK has set itself going forward , it better make lots of money ! PRSK has a long history , having been formed in 1971 . <p> Many organisations of similar calibre started during the same period are now vibrant national organisations . However , the Society has had a chequered past , having at times appeared , and oftentimes operating like a cartel . <p> There were no attempts at active recruitment as the incumbents appeared to benefit from operating a closed shop .
