<h> Rainfall 's new low <p> SOUTHERN England has just had its driest ten-month period since records began in 1910 . <p> For the UK overall , it was the joint ninth driest April , recording 49 per cent of average rainfall . It has left soil dry and river flows well below average , the Centre for Ecology and Hydrology reports . But it said water restrictions are unlikely . <p> It came as the Association of British Insurers warned high winds resulting from climate change will push up insurance losses .
