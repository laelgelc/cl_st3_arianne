<h> Green Party candidates in Pembrokeshire pull out of General Election <p> PEMBROKESHIRE Green Party is standing down its General Election candidates in both the Preseli Pembrokeshire and Carmarthen West &amp; South Pembrokeshire constituencies . <p> The party said its two candidates , Valerie Bradley and Frances Bryant would no longer be running for the seats . <p> A party spokesperson said " We would normally advocate ' vote for what you believe in ' . <p> " However , with this election , given the nature of the voting system and time running out for action on climate change , the biggest threat to Green values and objectives would be a further five years of reactionary Tory government . <p> " In both constituencies Labour has the best chance of defeating Tory candidates and we are asking Green Party members and supporters to ' bite the bullet ' and vote tactically . " <p> Frances Bryant , who will be stepping aside in Preseli Pembrokeshire , said ; " This has not been an easy decision but the coming election is pivotal to the future of our communities . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nothing short of disastrous for our remaining public services and our environment . <p> " Progressive parties need to unite to defend our communities at this time and we think that a combined effort to vote tactically aimed at preventing a Tory victory is the way forward " . <p> Valerie Bradley , who is standing down from Carmarthen West &amp; South Pembrokeshire , said ; " As someone committed to Green principles , to suggest voting for another party comes hard . On this occasion however it is clear that voting tactically offers the best chance for damage limitation .
