<p> Billions of people around the world are now exposed to devastating natural disasters , including earthquakes , tsunamis , and volcanic eruptions , according to a new study . <p> In the last few decades , the risk has dramatically increased ; the number of people living in seismic areas , for example , has increased by 93 percent in just 40 years . <p> The findings , compiled in the Atlas of the Human Planet 2017 , reveal the global exposure to natural disasters has doubled since 1975 , largely as a result of population growth and development . <p> Earthquakes were found to be the largest hazard , with the number of people potentially affected jumping from 1.4 billion in 1975 to 2.7 billion in 2015 .
