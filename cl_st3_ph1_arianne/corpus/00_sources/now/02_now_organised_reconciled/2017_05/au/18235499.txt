<h> The cold air of change at Victorian alpine resorts <p> ALPINE WORKSHOPS : The Alpine Resorts Climate Adaptation Planning project is now under way . The project hosted workshops in Mansfield and Mount Buller last week . Paul Howorth ( pictured ) led the workshops with local stakeholders . <p> THE Department of Environment , Land , Water and Planning ( DELWP ) has recently undertaken an assessment for the Victorian alpine resorts sector on planning adaptation to climate change . <p> The Alpine Resorts Climate Adaptation Planning project hopes to identify current scientific research on the anticipated impacts of climate change for alpine environments and the vulnerabilities of our alpine resorts . <p> But operators on Buller claim they are already in the " adaptation phase " and that the ill effects of climate change on the mountain are happening right now . <p> Problems already being raised are warmer winter months , less precipitation and snow fall , and less total time at snow making temperatures . <p> One way to combat these problems is through diversification of tourism , especially in the fields of technological adaptation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ these adaptive actions , some recurrent problems on the mountain will abate , according to one operator on Mount Buller.
