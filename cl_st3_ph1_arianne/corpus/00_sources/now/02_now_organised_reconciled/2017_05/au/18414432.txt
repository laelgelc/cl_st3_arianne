<h> This Dolphin Getting Breathalysed Is All Of Us <p> By now , you probably know that humans are really screwing up the ocean . Climate change aside , we often dump oil into it , ruining the lives of whatever animals live nearby . Just imagine if someone drilled for oil right in the centre of your house and then accidentally got the toxic black sludge all over your bedroom , bathroom , car , and all of your food . <p> Image : Pasamontes et al <p> Thankfully , scientists are trying to help the animals their human counterparts are sickening . But how are we even supposed to tell if oil has sickened dolphins ? They 're most interested in seeing how the catastrophic 2010 Deepwater Horizon oil spill has impacted bottlenose dolphins in the Gulf of Mexico . <p> Breathalysing dolphins requires netting them , transferring them to a research boat , and then placing the breathalysing device carefully over the dolphin 's blowhole . As described in a study recently published in the journal Environmental Science and Technology , the team captured and analysed breath samples from a small group of dolphins from Barataria Bay , an area of the Gulf of Mexico near Louisiana affected by the oil spill . The researchers did n't want to jump to conclusions , since they had a small sample and were just measuring levels of different chemicals . But the Barataria Bay dolphins definitely showed signs of respiratory problems that could have come from swimming around in oil . That should n't be surprising -- past research studying live and dead dolphins exposed to oil has demonstrated lung and reproductive problems . <p> But most importantly , the scientists felt that they did what they set out to do . They showed that breathalysing dolphins is a reasonable way to tell how healthy they are .
