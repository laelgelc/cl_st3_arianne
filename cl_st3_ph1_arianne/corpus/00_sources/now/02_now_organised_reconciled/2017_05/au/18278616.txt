<p> The 2017 March for Science was a powerful political statement by scientists . The marchers opposed political interference , budget cuts and lack of support for science at a government level . <p> More commonly , though , scientists stay in their labs and avoid the public political spotlight . <p> CSIRO scientist John Church -- who initially acted as an individual ( not a representative of his research institution ) to " stand up for science " in 2015 -- is cited as a recent example of the career ramifications that can flow from public activity . <p> Actually , he 's not alone .
