<h> G7 communique notes split on climate <p> In an unusual admission , Group of Seven ( G7 ) leaders have said in their final communique they have failed to bridge differences over climate change , with the US unable to join other countries in committing to the Paris Agreement . <p> " The United States of America is in the process of reviewing its policies on climate change and on the Paris Agreement and thus is not in a position to join the consensus on these topics , " the communique read on Saturday . <p> " Understanding this process , the heads of state and of government of Canada , France , Germany , Italy , Japan and the United Kingdom and the presidents of the European Council and of the European Commission reaffirm their strong commitment to swiftly implement the Paris Agreement , " it said .
