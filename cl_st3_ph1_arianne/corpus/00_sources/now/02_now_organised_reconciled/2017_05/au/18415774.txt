<h> Wilson wastes no time with new board role <h> Chair Seema Abdullah said she was excited about the challenge of heading up the organisation . <p> CAMPASPE Shire councillor Leigh Wilson is one of four new board members on the Goulburn Valley Waste Resource Recovery Group ( GVWRRG ) . <p> Earlier this week Energy , Environment and Climate Change Minister Lily D'Ambrosio announced Seema Abdullah , Leigh Wilson , David Lowe and Eric Lording would join four continuing members on the board . <p> The GVWRRG is responsible for undertaking waste and resource recovery infrastructure planning across the Campaspe , Shepparton , Strathbogie , Moira , Murrindindi and Mitchell local government areas . <p> Chair Seema Abdullah said she was excited about the challenge of heading up the organisation . <p> " It certainly is a very dynamic industry and the group has worked particularly hard since its establishment in 2014 to plan for the region 's future , ' ' she said . <p> " With more than 1000 jobs engaged in waste and recycling in the region , I am looking forward to understanding further the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ support councils and reprocessors , " she said . <p> The four new board members join Geoff Dobson , Andrea Richards , Anthony Lewis and Geoff Cobbledick and will oversee the delivery of a plan which will guide waste management and resource recovery infrastructure in the Goulburn Valley for the next 10 years . <p> Membership of the GVWRRG board has been based on required skill sets which include local government , financial management , contract management , risk management , environmental policy , waste management and materials efficiency .
