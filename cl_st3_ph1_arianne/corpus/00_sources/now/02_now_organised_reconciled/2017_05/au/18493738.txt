<h> Iconic penguin species could go extinct <p> An iconic species of penguin could be extinct on the New Zealand mainland in less than 40 years unless urgent action is taken , researchers say . <p> A peer reviewed study from the University of Otago has found the Yellow-eyed penguin population on the mainland is dropping dramatically . <p> ' Between 1996 and 2013 we lost more than 75% of our population , and that trend seems to continue , ' Thomas Mattern , a researcher at the university said . <p> ' If it does so we might lose the penguins completely within the last 20-40 years . ' <p> The study found human activity from fishing , polluted rivers , and dog walking all contributed to the declining population . <p> ' adult penguins die in fishing nets ... bottom trawling and ouster dredging destroys their habitat , their sea-floor habitat , where the penguins find their food , which limits their foraging ranges .
