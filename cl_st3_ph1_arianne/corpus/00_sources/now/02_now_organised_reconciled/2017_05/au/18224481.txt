<h> Polar Bear at Sea World Australia Gives Birth to Twin Cubs3:31 <p> A 16-year-old polar bear gave birth to a pair of cubs on April 26 at Sea World Australia and has been caring for them in her den since . The cubs are the second litter to Liya , who is part of the Polar Bear Shores exhibit at the Gold Coast marine park in Queensland . Zookeepers said the cubs weighed approximately 600 grams and were 15 centimetres long . They were born blind and toothless , with short , soft fur .
