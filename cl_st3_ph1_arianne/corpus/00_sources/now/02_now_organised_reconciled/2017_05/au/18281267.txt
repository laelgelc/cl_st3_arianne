<h> Activists charged after scaling CBD bridge <p> Two activists who scaled a busy motorway and suspended a huge banner to protest coal financing by the Commonwealth Bank have been charged . <p> The Darling Harbour protest stopped Friday morning commuters in their tracks as many took photos of the male abseilers and the sign that read " CommBank : Dump Coal ! Invest in our Future " . <p> Police say the men , aged 31 and 36 , ignored directions to come down after they climbed both pillars of a suspension footbridge which faces the CBA headquarters about 5am . <p> Greenpeace activists hoped the 70 square metre sign would gain the attention of the bank 's chief executive , Ian Narev , as part of the protest aimed at the CBA 's continued support of fossil fuel projects including the Adani coal mine . <p> Rescue officers eventually helped bring the pair down after a few hours . <p> They were arrested and subsequently charged with several offences including remaining on prescribed premises without a lawful excuse . <p> The men were granted conditional bail to appear @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Greenpeace climate and energy campaigner Nikola Casule said he was proud of the abseilers . <p> " It 's such an important message , " he said from the footbridge . <p> " It 's a really simple choice for Comm Bank : do they want to stand with millions of Australians that want action on climate change , that want Comm Bank to dump coal and fund our future by investing more in renewables , or do they want to stand with fossil fuel companies that are threatening the Great Barrier Reef and threatening our environment .
