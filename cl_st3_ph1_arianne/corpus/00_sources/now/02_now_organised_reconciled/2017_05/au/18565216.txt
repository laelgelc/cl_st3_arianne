In contrast to terms like resilience ( complicit with neoliberal incitements of bootstrapping ) or sustainability ( which suggests we get to keep something intact ) , weathering invites us to consider what we will lose along the way . <p> Weathered bodies , weathered houses , weathered cars , weathered clothes , weathered relationships , weathered dreams -- these all bear scars of what has worn them down , and of what they have been asked to carry , to survive , and to hack . <p> Weathering leaves the scars of both what has been lost and what has been survived.Development Planning Unit at University College London/flickr <p> Bringing this sense of lived climate change to our everyday perception is neither an easy nor comfortable thing . It also underscores the politics and activism we hope this tactic can engender . <p> Such attentive acclimatisation reveals that , even though we 're all in the same planetary boat when it comes to global warming , we 're not all in it in the same way .
