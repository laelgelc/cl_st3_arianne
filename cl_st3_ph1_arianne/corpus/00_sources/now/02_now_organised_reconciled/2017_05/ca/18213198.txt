<h> Westport Fuel Systems Announces the Closing of the Sale of the IMPCO APU Assets <p> VANCOUVER , May 1 , 2017 /PRNewswire/ - Westport Fuel Systems Inc . ( " Westport Fuel Systems " ) ( TSX:WPRT / Nasdaq:WPRT ) today announced the closing of its previously announced sale of the assets of its IMPCO Auxiliary Power Unit ( " APU " ) business . Net proceeds to the company at closing were approximately $60 million US dollars , after adjusting for estimated net working capital , transaction costs , hold back amounts and other deal related expenses .
