<p> German Chancellor Angela Merkel is urging European powers to pull together and take care of their own interests because they ca n't rely on the United States and Britain as they once did . She drew this conclusion after watching U.S. President Donald Trump at work among the NATO heads of government and the Group of Seven heads of government last week . <p> Canada 's trade and its military security are tightly linked to both the U.S. and Europe . If Chancellor Merkel is right -- if Mom and Dad are quarrelling or drawing apart -- Canada may have to make some new friends and explore new ways of ensuring its trade and its safety . <p> Among his NATO allies , Mr. Trump declined to endorse the doctrine that an attack on one alliance member is an attack on all . He used the NATO gathering as a backdrop for a speech complaining that other alliance members do n't spend as much on defence as the U.S does .
