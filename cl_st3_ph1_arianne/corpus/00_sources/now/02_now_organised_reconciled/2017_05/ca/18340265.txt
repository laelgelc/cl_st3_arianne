<p> The Alberta government has hired Gary Doer to defend Alberta 's softwood lumber industry in Washington , D.C. <p> Doer was Canada 's ambassador to the United States from 2009 to 2016 , so he knows his way around American politics . He was Manitoba premier from 1999 to 2009 , so he knows his way around Canadian politics . <p> Not only that , he was an NDP premier who was appointed as ambassador by a Conservative prime minister . <p> " He is great at building and developing relationships , but most importantly , he 's the former Canadian ambassador to the U.S. , " Alberta Economic Development Minister Deron Bilous told reporters Monday . " He knows this file , and he has contacts that we will absolutely leverage .
