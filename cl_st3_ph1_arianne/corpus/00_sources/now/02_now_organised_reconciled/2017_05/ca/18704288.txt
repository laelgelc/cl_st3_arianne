Ramadan is significant for the fast but also because the Qur'an was first revealed this month . One of the principle Islamic religious observances , the fast will range from 10 to 21 hours depending on where one lives in the world . <p> Believers ( except the elderly , sick , menstruating , children , etc. ) are instructed to refocus on the spiritual and physical discipline during this 30-day training @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after Ramadan while others lapse right back . Religious traditions highlight the physical , spiritual and communitarian benefits of fasting . <p> Unfortunately , given what transpires in far too many Muslim settings , outsiders could be forgiven for assuming that it was more about feasting . Not unlike other religious occasions , including Christmas , there has been a shift to the outward manifestations and celebratory aspects . <p> Yet Ramadan holds out hope for Muslims and non-Muslims alike . Muslims can make a number of changes and a conscious effort to make this month spiritually uplifting for themselves while improving the lot of others including less fortunate Canadians . <p> The self-reflection called for is vital for all people today , as we confront materialism , extremism , populism , environmental devastation and climate change , etc . <p> Conventional wisdom would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Indeed , medical studies confirm health benefits . However , overconsumption is a serious problem . In fact , according to Dr. Al Madani , head of Emirates Diabetes Society , many Muslims " tend to overeat upon breaking their fast , and usually the meal involves heavy , fatty foods that are high in calories " . <p> I am not aware of any studies here , but anecdotally far too many gain weight during the month . The increased intake of richer and deep fried foods , along with sweets during iftar ( fast-breaking ) and decreased physical activity are two culprits . The prophet Muhammad 's teaching of eating to fill one third , leaving one third each for air and liquid is highly relevant . <p> Fasting also seeks to teach empathy . It allows one to experience -- albeit for a short period -- how a segment of humanity manages . Arguably , this objective is not achieved if people lounge around , sleep more , work less , change their routines and consume more during the month . <p> In fact , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Canada , in many Muslim countries worker productivity " declines in the holy month by 35 to 50 per cent as a result of shorter working hours and the change in behaviour , " says Samer Sunnuqrot , a Jordanian economist . Some decline is understandable , but if empathy is the goal then we need to try harder because the less fortunate do n't have the same luxury . <p> Thankfully , charitable giving and acts increase during this blessed month . Muslim organizations typically institute campaigns to help the needy . In fact , food banks across North America report surges during the month . Reducing wastage and extravagance and redirecting this to local causes helps fulfil religious obligations while helping to tackle the growing poverty around us . <p> What can non-Muslims do ? Thankfully , unlike some parts of the world , we do n't have the haram ( prohibition ) police , forcing Muslims to fast and making sure that non-Muslims do n't eat in public .
