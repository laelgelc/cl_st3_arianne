" as he was dubbed by The Toronto Star after he won the Tory leadership in 1976 by riding up the middle and defeating favoured candidates from Quebec -- Senator Claude Wagner and Brian Mulroney . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only 39 when elected prime minister . Scheer is 38 . <p> Clark hailed from small town Alberta and had been a political organizer , candidate , or MP for most of his adult life . Scheer was born in Ottawa but carved out a political career for himself in Regina . He was only 25 when he defeated long-time NDP MP Lorne Nystrom , and only 32 when he was elected speaker of the House of Commons . <p> But a lot has changed since Clark stumbled into the PC leadership . Dubbing Scheer Andrew Who ? speaks more to the reluctance or sheer short-sightedness of central Canadians to recognize that the Conservative 's power base has shifted west . Stephen Harper was n't just a temporary aberration . Lots of Conservatives were off the mark when they assumed the next leader would have to come from Quebec or Ontario because " the West " had had its turn . <p> Article Continued Below <p> Even favoured candidate Maxime Bernier curried support in Alberta by declaring that Alberta was the foundation of the Conservative party . <p> Scheer , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ margin , won British Columbia , Saskatchewan , Ontario , and Atlantic Canada and significant support in Quebec . Bernier won Alberta , Manitoba and Quebec . <p> As the Reform Party used to point out it was never a problem as far as Liberals or Progressive Conservatives in central Canada were concerned that so many prime ministers hailed from Quebec ( St. Laurent , Trudeau , Mulroney , Chretien , Martin ) . <p> But it was seen as a problem by many Western conservatives who saw it as a permanent power grab by Quebec at their expense . And hence the Reform slogan -- " the West wants in " that carried the party to victory in the West , eventually led to a takeover of the PCs , and the election of Stephen Harper as prime minister . <p> But of course that 's not the only thing that has changed . A recent poll found that the budget is so unpopular if an election were held now Brad Wall and the Saskatchewan Party would lose to the NDP . That 's quite a come down since last year when they were re-elected with 62 per cent of the popular vote . <p> Scheer is a social conservative , who supports anti-abortion and anti-gay marriage views , although he did not campaign on those issues .
