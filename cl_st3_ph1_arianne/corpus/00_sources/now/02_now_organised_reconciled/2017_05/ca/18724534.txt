<p> Global warming is expected to have a big impact on the economy , and a new study says cities will be hit the hardest . <p> The study published Monday in the Nature Climate Change journal found that the total economic costs of climate change for cities could be 2.6 times higher due to an effect researchers call the " urban heat island . " <p> Researchers looked at 1,692 cities and found that by 2100 , they could be as much as 8 degrees C warmer . Nearly 5 degrees would be due to global warming , but the rest is from the heat island effect , which happens when parks and lakes are replaced by heat-trapping concrete and asphalt . <p> By 2050 , cities could see temperatures rise by two degrees from heat islands . That could mean more air pollution , worse water quality , and poorer health . <p> " Any hard-won victories over climate change on a global scale could be wiped out by the effects of uncontrolled urban heat islands , " said Richard S.J.
