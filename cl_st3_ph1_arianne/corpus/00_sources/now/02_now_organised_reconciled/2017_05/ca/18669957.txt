<h> Trudeau , Trump Meet One-On-One To Talk Trade , Security At G7 Summit <p> TAORMINA , Italy -- Prime Minister Justin Trudeau met one-on-one with Donald Trump Saturday on the sidelines of the G7 summit in Sicily , where the U.S. president has been clashing with other leaders over climate change and free trade . <p> Trudeau also had trade on his mind as he grabbed a few minutes with the president , as last week Trump began the countdown to renegotiate the North American Free Trade Agreement . <p> Donald Trump , right , waves next to Justin Trudeau following a family photo with participants of the G7 summit on Saturday . ( Photo : Jonathan Ernst/AFP/Getty Images ) <p> " The prime minister and the president reaffirmed their commitment to the deepest economic partnership in the world , " said a statement Trudeau 's office released following the informal meeting in Taormina , Italy . <p> " Trade between our countries supports job creation on both sides of the border and the growth of the middle class , and in 2016 was valued at nearly $882 billion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ also discussed a range of issues of mutual interest , including international security , " said the statement . <p> " They stressed the importance of shared leadership on issues of global concern as a means to promote peace and prosperity , " it said . <p> The Liberal government says Trudeau is making the case for a progressive trade agenda around the G7 table , and will be pointing to the trade agreement between Canada and the European Union as an example . <p> Earlier , Trudeau spokeswoman Kate Purchase said the prime minister was expected to take that same message to Trump when he brings up NAFTA during the brief bilateral chat . <p> Trudeau has also sat down with French President Emmanuel Macron , British Prime Minister Theresa May , and Shinzo Abe , the prime minister of Japan , while at the G7 . <p> He will have an official visit with Italian Prime Minister Paolo Gentiloni when he heads to Rome .
