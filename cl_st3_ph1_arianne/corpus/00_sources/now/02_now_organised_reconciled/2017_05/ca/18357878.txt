<p> Alberta 's electricity market watchdog is investigating a provincial agency for its handling of controversial power contracts that are losing billions of dollars . <p> The Market Surveillance Administrator ( MSA ) says it 's investigating complaints lodged against Alberta 's Balancing Pool , the arm 's length agency that has been dealing with money-losing power purchase arrangements after they were dropped by industry . <p> The head of the group conducting the investigation would not comment on the details of the probe or disclose who made the complaints . <p> The administrator said the complaints refer to potential breaches of the Electric Utilities Act , including a provision that demands the Balancing Pool " carry out its duties in a manner that is responsible and efficient . " <p> The Balancing Pool said it has done nothing wrong . <p> " We have nothing to hide and we are fully co-operating with the MSA 's investigation , " said Bruce Roberts , CEO of the Balancing Pool .
