<h> News Local <h> MP Rudd defends cap-and-trade systems <p> A day after the Fraser Institute came out with a study faulting the premise of cap-and-trade systems to reduce carbon , **27;1281;TOOLONG South MP Kim Rudd was asked by the Opposition to defend her government 's approach to this . <p> The Fraser Institute said it found " practical realities of carbon taxes and cap-and-trade systems across Canada undermine potential benefits " and Conservative MP Karen Vecchio asked when the Prime Minister was going to " tell his Ontario Liberal friends " not to apply HST to the provincial cap-and-trade plan , further taxing the middle class . <p> " Madam Speaker , after 10 years of Conservative inaction on climate change , it is time to put a price on what we do not want : pollution . This really does make sense , " MP Rudd replied in the House last week . " Polluting should not be free , and polluters should not get a free ride . When we put a price on pollution , we can invest the money in things we do want : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cleaner energy . <p> " The evidence clearly shows that pricing carbon pollution is the most effective way to reduce emissions and grow the economy . It will also stimulate innovation , increase energy efficiency , and promote investment in clean technology . <p> " Already 97% of Canadians live in a province that either has a price on pollution or has committed to doing so . Under our approach , we have given provinces and territories the flexibility to design their own policies to meet the federal benchmark , including their own policies to price carbon pollution . The revenues from these policies will remain in the province or territory where they originate , as we have always said . " <p> Rudd went on to say that revenue generated by the provinces through their carbon pollution reduction plans are putting " money back into the pockets of people who need it the most by giving the money back to families through rebates , by cutting personal income tax and small business taxes , and by investing to support entrepreneurs and clean technologies that create good-paying , middle-class jobs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ MPP cited the large solar farm constructed by Alderville First Nation on Rice Lake near Roseneath as both a revenue and job creator . <p> " Pricing carbon pollution is only one part of the climate plan , which includes accelerating the phase-out of coal , developing a clean fuel standard , and taking action on short-lived climate pollutants , " she went on to say . " Canada 's climate plan is one that economists , business leaders , and Canadians across the country have been calling for . It will allow us to meet our international obligations under the Paris agreement on climate change and will lay the foundation for the transition to a clean-growth economy . It will also give us a competitive advantage in a market that Mark Carney , governor of the Bank of England , says is worth trillions of dollars . "
