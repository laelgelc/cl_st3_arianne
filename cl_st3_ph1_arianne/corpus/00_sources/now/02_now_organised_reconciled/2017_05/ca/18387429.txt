<p> The executive committee of the Montreal Metropolitan Community has tasked its development commission to produce a report on the floods that struck the area in May , a study that will include recommendations on future developments in the context of climate change . <p> In a statement , Montreal mayor Denis Coderre , who heads the MMC , noted that while " the recent spring floods have had tragic consequences for thousands of homes ... we must learn lessons from the events of the last few weeks and collectively better prepare ourselves for the unpredictable consequences of climate change .
