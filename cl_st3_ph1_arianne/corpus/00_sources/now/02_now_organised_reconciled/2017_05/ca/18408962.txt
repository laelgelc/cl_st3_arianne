<h> National Post View : Let Canadians learn from Ontario to be wary of politicians who claim green schemes wo n't be costly <p> It was just weeks ago that we remarked upon Ontario Premier Kathleen Wynne 's strange definition of fairness . Facing dismal poll results and serious public anger over her party 's epic mismanagement of the province 's energy sector , the premier tried to salvage her deteriorating situation . Sky-high monthly bills would be lowered imminently , she announced . <p> First , the government promised to drop the provincial share of the HST from hydro bills ( the lost revenue would naturally have to be made up elsewhere ) . Second , the province essentially pledged to refinance many of the exorbitantly priced renewable energy generation deals that were already in place -- stretching out existing contracts over a longer period .
