<h> Federal government has no plan for phase-out of fossil fuel subsidies , auditor general finds <p> The federal government does not have a plan in place to meet its commitment to phase out inefficient fossil-fuel subsidies by 2025 , according to an auditor general 's report tabled Tuesday in the House of Commons . <p> The opposition is also accusing the government of " stonewalling " the auditor general 's office by withholding relevant information about the subsidies . <p> The new report says the finance and environment departments lack a clear understanding of which subsidies are covered by the 2025 commitment , as well as a timeline for phasing them out . <p> But the audit was hampered by the finance department 's refusal to disclose some budget analysis documents to auditors , claiming cabinet confidence . <p> " Our right to freely access information is fundamental to our work , and a cornerstone that protects our independence , " said auditor general Michael Ferguson in a message accompanying the report . <p> In response , Finance Minister Bill Morneau said Tuesday that the government has issued a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " unprecedented " access to budget information .
