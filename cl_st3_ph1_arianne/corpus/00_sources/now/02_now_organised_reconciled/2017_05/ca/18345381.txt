<h> Farmers offered financial carrot to help save threatened wood turtle <h> New program aims to preserve reptile in Nova Scotia <p> There are an estimated 2,000 to 8,000 wood turtles in Nova Scotia . ( Richard Lafleur ) <p> Farmers are the focus of a new Nova Scotia Federation of Agriculture program to protect the wood turtle , which is about to wake from its winter hibernation and head for dry land for something to eat . <p> The initiative aims to limit the number of the reptiles that are badly injured or killed by farm machinery , especially in hayfields , by providing a financial incentive to farmers to change where and how they mow . <p> Project co-ordinator Simon Greenland-Smith told CBC 's Information Morning that the species is in trouble . <p> " They are listed provincially and federally as a threatened species , which means they 're really at risk of becoming extinct . They 're declining throughout the province . So they 'll be looking for worms , slugs and berries in the spring to eat .
