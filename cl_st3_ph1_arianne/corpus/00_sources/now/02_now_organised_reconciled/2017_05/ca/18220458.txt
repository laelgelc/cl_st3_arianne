<p> Kivi Park is inviting the community to take part in a monumental tree-planting event on Saturday , May 13 , thanks to a generous donation of 10,000 seedlings from Vale 's Sudbury operations and A&amp;M Reforestation . <p> " Vale is pleased to support Kivi Park 's mission to create a truly remarkable destination park for residents and tourists alike , " Stuart Harshaw , vice president of Vale 's Ontario operations , said in a release . " The thousands of seedlings we are donating have been grown with care as part of our ongoing re-greening efforts in Sudbury and we invite residents of all ages to come out and be part of this historic event . " <p> Park benefactor Lily Fielding said she is excited to see a new generation of residents participate in environmental stewardship . <p> " Not too long ago , our children played on the black rock and moonscape of Sudbury , " Fielding said . " They were part of a generation that reclaimed and restored our natural environment .
