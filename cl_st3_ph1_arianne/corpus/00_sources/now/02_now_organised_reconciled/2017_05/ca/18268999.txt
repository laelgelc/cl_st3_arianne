<h> Get the real dirt ; own a piece of Puslinch Lake <p> Puslinch Lake soil - screened , rock-free , and rich in organic nutrients - is on sale on May 13 . <p> Bring your truck , trailer or container and a shovel ( for smaller quantities ) to McCormick 's Point ( from Townline Road turn east on Concession 1 ) . Sales will support the lake 's restoration fund . <p> The Puslinch Lake Conservation Association ( PLCA ) , a non-profit organization , was founded in 1997 by a small group of concerned residents who wanted to restore the lake to its former natural , healthy condition . <p> In 2000 the first trial using a dredger was tested and was used with minimal success until 2009 . <p> In the years 2001 to 2006 and again from 2010 to 2012 , permits were not issued for the dredging operation . <p> In 2013 a new dredging concept was introduced . The basic barge platform is 40 ' by 60 ' . It was outfitted with a hopper/container measuring 18 ' by 36 ' by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ tons of material . <p> In 2014 and 2015 dredging continued from July until the end of the season . <p> In 2016 major repairs on the equipment stalled dredging until the end of August . A trench was excavated between Big Island and Harvey 's Island insuring passage to the eastern location for dredging in 2017 . <p> In 2017 the PLCA is awaiting new permits from the Grand River Conservation Area and the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change to continue dredging . <p> The storage pit , located at McCormick 's Point , now has lake sediment from the last five years , which amounts to hundreds of tons of nutrient-rich lake soil . Bring your own shovel , containers and transport is advised . A few dump trailers will be available for those who do n't have the needed equipment , and require delivery up to 10km away . A delivery fee will be charged . Call 519-651-1488 in advance for delivery . One cubic yard of material will cost $20 . A regular sized recycle box will be $5 .
