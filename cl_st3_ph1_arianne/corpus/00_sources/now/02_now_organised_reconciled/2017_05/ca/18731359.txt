<h> Little chance carbon tax will be revenue-neutral <p> Public Safety Minister Ralph Goodale said when it comes to the planned federal carbon tax , ' every single penny remains in Saskatchewan and under Saskatchewan 's control . ' <p> Did you really believe them when they said it would be revenue-neutral ? <p> Earlier this month , the Trudeau government unveiled a " technical paper " on its proposed federal carbon tax which suggests it will mimic Alberta 's carbon tax plan , quashing the optimism of those who believed the federal government when they said their carbon tax would be revenue-neutral . <p> The federal government had long insisted its plan would be " revenue-neutral for the federal government , " with any revenue generated staying in the province where the tax is imposed . Last October , Saskatchewan-based federal Public Safety Minister Ralph Goodale said publicly of the planned federal carbon tax that " every single penny remains in Saskatchewan and under Saskatchewan 's control . " The Saskatchewan government was highly skeptical -- as it turns out , for good reason . <p> When Alberta 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Notley and other ministers tried to spin the tax as revenue-neutral . In all previous uses of this term , it meant that every dollar raised by the government would be returned to taxpayers . <p> The Alberta government 's new definition was that every dollar taken from Albertans would be spent by the Alberta government . By that definition , every tax in Canadian history would be considered revenue-neutral . <p> Alberta 's carbon tax takes billions from taxpayers and the government plans to spend that money , at least partially , on corporate welfare to big emitters such as oil companies and green-energy corporations . A smaller portion of the revenue is dished out in rebates to some Albertans . Additionally , the small business tax was cut by one percentage point . <p> Alberta 's carbon tax has been criticized for making the necessities of life cost more , taxing rural and suburban residents disproportionately , indirectly raising other taxes and contributing to corporate welfare -- all without making a dent in global climate change . It was a desperate attempt to sell the carbon tax to a population that does n't support it . <p> Carbon tax proponents have slammed the Alberta carbon tax for not being revenue-neutral , being confusing and lacking transparency . <p> Even the so-called gold standard of carbon taxes in Canada , British Columbia 's revenue-neutral model , was revealed by the Fraser Institute not to be revenue-neutral . The government had been playing accounting tricks , costing taxpayers hundreds of millions . The idea of a revenue-neutral carbon tax , even if it fits the accepted definition , involves such incredible faith in government that it 's difficult for persistently misled taxpayers to swallow . <p> But the federal government 's suggestion that its carbon tax would be revenue-neutral is even more disingenuous . Even the original suggestion of revenue neutrality ( that money was recycled back to provincial governments ) was misleading -- it came with no guarantee that provincial governments would give that revenue back to taxpayers .
