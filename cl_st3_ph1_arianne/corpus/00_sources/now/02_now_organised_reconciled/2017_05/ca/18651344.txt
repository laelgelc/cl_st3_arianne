<h> More than 705,000 owners say the trucks are rigged with defeat devices . <p> VW 's Dieselgate scandal continues to trickle down to the rest of the automotive industry . While FCA , Mercedes-Benz , and Peugeot have already been scrutinized , GM is now the fifth automaker accused of using defeat-like devices in its vehicles . <p> According to a report from Bloomberg , more than 705,000 GM Duramax diesel trucks will now be investigated as part of a lawsuit put forth by owners and lessees . The suit was filed on Thursday in U.S. federal court , and claims that GM installed at least three defeat devices in two models in its heavy-duty truck line from 2011 to 2016 , the Chevy Silverado HD and the GMC Sierra HD . <p> The 190-page complaint reportedly references back to the VW lawsuit 83 times , and suggests that the environmental damage caused by GM could be greater than that of VW . The supposed defeat devices allowed trucks to pass U.S. emissions inspections , though they reportedly produced five times the legal limit on the road . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ourselves , " GM spokesman Dan Flores said in a statement .
