He held the lead over the first 12 ballots . <p> However , once an overly complicated and elongated vote-counting process was completed , Saskatchewan MP Andrew Scheer triumphed on the baker 's dozen ballot . Bernier was gracious in defeat , but the crushing disappointment of the final result was plain to see . <p> That disappointment might have been matched by the mood in the Liberal party .
