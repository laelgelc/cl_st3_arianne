<h> Arctic Council sets climate marker : Global shifts at top of docket for circumpolar nations <p> Secretary of State Rex Tillerson offered reasoned and reflective words to his circumpolar counterparts on the subject of global climate change this week in his opening remarks at the Arctic Council gathering in Fairbanks . <p> It is the Arctic nations -- and the U.S. is one of them because of its possession of the state of Alaska -- that are witnessing the effects of climate change within their borders , so Secretary Tillerson 's comments were going to be closely watched for indication of the new U.S. administration 's position . <p> There have been many news reports about how the administration of President Donald Trump has a markedly different view than that of the Obama administration on human action as the cause of , or accelerator of , climate change . Opponents of President Trump breathlessly decry any decision they see as a change of course on the matter . <p> Secretary Tillerson said the Trump administration does n't have a set position .
