<h> Hayden Christensen 's Back in Trailer for Numb , At the Edge of the End <p> Hayden Christensen never really went away after Star Wars III : Revenge of the Sith brought to a close the Anakin Skywalker phase of his life , but his career never took off the way many people expected . Perhaps Christensen 's new project will be the one that finally begins putting him back on the map . At the very least , it looks like an intriguing vehicle for the actor as he attempts to get out from under the long shadow of Star Wars <p> In Numb , at the Edge of the End , Christensen plays a veteran with PTSD who becomes convinced that the apocalypse is upon us and begins making preparations . The disaster-movie tone of the trailer seems to leave no doubt that something really bad is happening , though the exact nature of that disaster is kept obscure ( there are references to storms so perhaps climate change is the culprit ) .
