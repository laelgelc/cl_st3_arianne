<h> Economic costs of climate change could be much worse than thought <h> Economic costs of climate change could be much worse than thought <p> The mercury is rising , and it 's going to cost the world some serious money . <p> A new report , published in the journal Nature Climate Change , shows that urban centres will have to shell out more for the cost of climate change due to the " urban heat island " effect . <p> The report , which analysed 1,692 cities worldwide , found that the total economic costs of climate change for urban centres could be 2.6 times higher when the urban heat island effect is taken into account .
