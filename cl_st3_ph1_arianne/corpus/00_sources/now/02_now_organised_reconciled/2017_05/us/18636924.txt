Kennedy used to entertain her while they were sailing aboard the Honey Fitz . <p> " He would point out the white shark and the purple shark who always followed the boat , although I could never quite see them , " Kennedy said . " He would say they especially liked to eat socks and would have his friends throw their socks overboard , which I loved .
