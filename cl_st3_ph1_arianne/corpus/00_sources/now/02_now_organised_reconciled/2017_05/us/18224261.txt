<h> Time to end climate of violence <p> There 's been another horrifying gas attack in Syria . The evil Assad did it again . That 's how I heard it reported on the radio , anyway . But did he do the awful deed the first time ? I ca n't say . I do n't remember being presented with any indictable proof , like Gen. Colin Powell 's presentation of Saddam Hussein 's weapons of mass destruction . The whole story has the flavor of the Russians hacking our presidential election . Maybe I should dig out some old papers or listen to some old news broadcasts . <p> Speaking of Vladimir Putin , I heard that the Russians are saying the responsibility lies with the rebels ; the attack was with conventional weapons and the rebels were manufacturing chemical weapons . That could very well be true .
