The pieces are united by a common theme : the unexpected . <p> " I put out a call for artists to propose something that scared them , artistically , " said Darren Evans , founder and producer of the festival , and producing artistic director of Theatre on Fire . " <p> The play , performed by Anthem Theatre Company , combines themes from 1960s protest plays with the reactions of real people across the country -- taken from a survey -- in the month after the 2016 presidential election . The result : a performance that " weaves together poetry , movement , music , clown , satire and tragedy to form a living document about the world we live in now , " said Boice .
