<h> Drought-quenching bacteria the future of farming ? <p> NAU doctoral candidate Rachel Rubin teamed up with artist Victor Leshyk to illustrate rhizobacteria as a " prescription " for drought . <p> Though we can not see them with the naked eye , bacteria are virtually everywhere . A single teaspoon of soil contains about one billion bacterial cells . From the barren valleys of Antarctica to the gassy corners of the human gut , bacteria are thriving and multiplying . But contrary to popular association , not all bacteria are harmful . <p> In fact , some bacteria can even help us meet global food demand while handing adverse effects of climate change . <p> In a recent study , NAU researchers investigated the microscopic world buried in earth 's soil . Led by doctoral student Rachel Rubin at the Center for Ecosystem Science and Society , they published findings that show how certain bacteria , called rhizobacteria , could help mitigate crop losses due to drought . <p> Rubin explained that the bacteria can be collected from arid regions where they grow naturally , and introduced to areas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impact on food security , water conservation , and sustainability . <p> The team found 20-40 percent increase in growth when a plant was introduced to rhizobacteria , and the effect was consistently stronger under drought . <p> " This is encouraging because it means that the places most vulnerable to climate change will benefit the most " Rubin shared . <p> In the next 30 years , global food demand is expected to double to keep up with a rapidly growing population . And it 's no secret that crops need water to survive . <p> While the global climate changes , the demand for water increases . Drought stress already reduces crop yield worldwide , and an increase in soil saltiness threatens more than half of the world 's farmable lands . <p> In the past , drought has been combated with improved irrigation methods . However , irrigation already accounts for over two-thirds of global water consumption and will not be enough to solve this problem alone . <p> To come to these conclusions , Rubin performed a meta-analysis .
