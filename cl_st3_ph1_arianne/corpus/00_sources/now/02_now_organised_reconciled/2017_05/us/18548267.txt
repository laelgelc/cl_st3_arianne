<h> New proposed science standards released after weeks of public comment <p> TWIN FALLS , Idaho ( KMVT/KSVT ) Students in Idaho public schools will be learning about climate issues differently in coming years . <p> A committee focused on revisions to Idaho 's science public education standards submitted recommended changes to the curriculum after the State Legislature requested a review of five paragraphs discussing climate change in the Science Content Standards in March . <p> The committee took more than one thousand public comments on the curriculum over a three-week span in April . That included six public hearings across the state , including one in Twin Falls . <p> " I appreciate all the Idahoans who took time to participate in the process , " said Superintendent of Public Instruction Sherri Ybarra . " I also want to thank the science standards committee for its hard work this spring and the prior two years as it identified the standards that would support our state 's goal to grow our STEM focus in education to meet the demands of our economy .
