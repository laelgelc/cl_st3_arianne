The World Science Festival will launch Science in the Square , a four-day event that brings science -- and climate science in particular -- to the crossroads of the world , May 31 , 2017 , beginning with a press preview at 11am on the Broadway Pedestrian Plaza . The preview will include an excerpt from Holoscenes , an epic performance-installation , created by the artist Lars Jan and his company Early Morning Opera , that features a cast of performers in a twelve-ton , 8 ' x 6 ' x 10 ' glass aquarium and viscerally connects everyday actions to climate change .
