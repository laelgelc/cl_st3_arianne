Kennedy Presidential Library and Museum in Dorchester on Sunday , for his achievements in the White House . In addition to the ACA , Obama was honored for restoring diplomatic ties with Cuba and his effort to fight climate change . <p> " Courage , President Kennedy knew , requires something more than just the absence of fear , " Obama said . " Any fool can be fearless . Courage , true courage , derives from that sense of who we are .
