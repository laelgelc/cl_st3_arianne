<p> Researchers at Concordia College this summer will conduct what they say is the first-ever academic study of the impact of Minnesota 's more than 500 private lake associations . <p> The study will be done in association with Minnesota Lakes and Rivers Advocates , a Twin Cities-based nonprofit group that works with policymakers and lake property owners to promote healthy waters . <p> The study intends to quantify the demographic makeup of Minnesota 's lake associations , assess their current projects , document the money they 've raised , and understand their roles in areas such as fish stocking , aquatic invasive species prevention and lake level control . <p> " Lakes are complex ecosystems that are increasingly threatened by development , invasive species and climate change , among other factors , " said Michelle Marko , an associate biology professor at Concordia and co-director of the school 's environmental studies program . <p> " Successful management of these resources involves understanding the role of all stakeholders in the use and management of these resources , " Marko said .
