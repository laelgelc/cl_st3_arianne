<h> Letter of the Day Culture shift crucial to disaster mitigation <p> The devastation caused by the recent torrential rains has yet again opened up debate about the poor planning and the inadequate infrastructure by successive administrations , as well as the unwise decisions and negligence demonstrated by many citizens . <p> Prime Minister Andrew Holness told reporters last Thursday that funds were set aside to deal with disasters , but not enough were available to deal with the magnitude of the problem that faces us . He also said that there is a master drainage plan that is yet to be implemented . <p> It should not be lost on us that the irregular cleaning of the drains directly contributes to flooding . Thus , significant efforts should be made in providing the resources to maintain the drainage system . <p> We are cognisant of the substantial damage to the infrastructure , the economic loss , and the inconvenience that disasters bring .
