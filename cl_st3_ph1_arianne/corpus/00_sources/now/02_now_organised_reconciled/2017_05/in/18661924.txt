It was there that a global , fossil fuel economy first took shape , through its coal-powered factories , railways , and steamships , which drove the emergence of modern consumer capitalism . <p> What might we now find if we look again at the literature of the 19th century ? Although Victorian writers lacked our understanding of a warming planet , we can learn from their deep awareness of the rapid and far-reaching ways that their society was changing .
