<h> Miffed Kerala seeks Prime Minister 's intervention <p> Chief minister Pinarayi Vijayan on Saturday sought the Prime Minister 's intervention to lift the restrictions the ministry of environment , forests and climate change has imposed on the trading and slaughtering of cattle . <p> TNN Updated : May 28 , 2017 , 07.56 AM IST <p> Kadakampally Surendran ( C ) along with CPM district secretary P Rajeeve participate in a ' Beef Fest ' <p> THIRUVANANTHAPURAM : Chief minister Pinarayi Vijayan on Saturday sought the Prime Minister 's intervention to lift the restrictions the ministry of environment , forests and climate change has imposed on the trading and slaughtering of cattle . <p> The central ministry has notified the stringent Prevention of Cruelty to Animals ( Regulation of Livestock Markets ) Rules , 2017 , under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act , and banned the sale and purchase of cattle from animal markets for slaughter . <p> In a letter , Vijayan cautioned Prime Minister Narendra Modi that the environment ministry 's decision would impact the livelihoods of millions of Indians . Withdrawing the notification was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ he said . <p> " It may be noted that the notification mandates that cattle can be traded only by those with valid documentation that the cattle will be used only for agricultural purposes . Of the millions of farmers in our country , only a miniscule have documentation to prove that they are engaged in agriculture . Therefore , it would be extremely difficult for the vast majority of our farmers to legally procure draught animals " , Vijayan said in the letter . <p> Criticising the suggestion to form district animal market monitoring committees and animal market committees to oversee the imposition of new rules , the chief minister said such a move would jeopardise the free trade of cattle between farmers in our country . <p> " Legitimate fears are also being raised if they would take up the role of Gau Rakshak Samitis which have attacked cattle traders and transporters " , the letter read . <p> Vijayan also reminded Modi that meat formed the primary source of protein for millions of poor and ordinary Indians , particularly the dalits . <p> " Such restrictions being @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ certain communities of our country as a direct attack on them . People of all faiths consume meat in our country . The prohibition will not only deprive them of adequate nutrition , but also prevent the availability of raw material for the leather industry . More than 2.5 million people work in India 's leather industry and most of them are dalits , especially those engaged in skinning the carcasses " , he said . <p> The chief minister also criticised the move to implement the decision without consulting the state governments . " The absence of efforts to take the states into confidence on such a drastic move with far-reaching consequences is detrimental to our democracy . I am afraid it amounts to an intrusion into the rights of the states in our federal structure , " he said . <p> Opposition leader Ramesh Chennithala also wrote a letter to Modi raising the same demand . He said the move was the first step to impose beef ban in the country and to encroach into the freedom of having one 's choice of food . <p> Senior Congress @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ irrational order by the central government should be trashed . " It is clear that the government is imposing the RSS 's agenda , " he said at a public function here . <p> Meanwhile in Kottayam , minister for animal husbandry K Raju termed the Centre 's decision " impractical " , and added that the state would register its strong protest . He opined that the restriction would push the dairy sector in the state into a deep crisis . <p> " If the restrictions are implemented , the cattle markets functioning in the state will have to be closed .
