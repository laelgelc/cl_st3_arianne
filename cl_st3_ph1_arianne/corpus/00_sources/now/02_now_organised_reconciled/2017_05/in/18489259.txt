Absar/Village Square <p> A young scientist has just discovered a new species of cave-dwelling crabs in the north-eastern Indian state of Meghalaya . It is the first ever species of a cave dwelling crab to be ever discovered in India . <p> Nestled in the Himalayas , Meghalaya is known for the magical beauty of its rich forests and water bodies . It is one of India 's four biodiversity hotspots -- in the whole world there are 34 biodiversity hotspots . <p> Less known is the fact that Meghalaya also boasts some of the world 's longest natural caves and it is in once such cave that these tiny creatures were discovered by 29-year-old Parveen Farzana Absar . This is only the third species discovered so far under this genus , the first one was found in Myanmar and the second one was found in the Indian state of Mizoram but both the previous species were either forest or freshwater species unlike this one that dwells in caves .
