<h> The move followed the sudden demise of Anil Madhav Dave , union minister for environment , forest and climate change . <p> NEW DELHI -- Science and Technology Minister Harsh Vardhan was on Thursday given additional charge of Environment Ministry , due to sudden demise of Anil Madhav Dave . <p> President Pranab Mukherjee , as advised by Prime Minister Narendra Modi , has directed that Harsh Vardhan , Cabinet Minister shall be assigned the charge of the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , in addition to his exiting portfolios , a press release issued by Rashtrapati Bhavan said .
