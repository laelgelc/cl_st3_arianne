<h> Telangana government wants no buffer for KBR Park <p> Soon , Hyderabad 's largest green cover , the Kasu Brahmananda Reddy National Park , might be stripped off its Eco-Sensitive Zone ( ESZ ) , to make way for the Telangana government 's multi-crore Strategic Road Development Plan ( SRDP ) . <p> A proposal to this effect has been tabled before the union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) by the state government , which has urged the Centre to " limit the ESZ around KBR buffer zone as zero metres " . <p> This letter , dated March 9 , 2017 , ( a copy of which is in TOI 's possession ) , has been sent from the office of the Telangana special chief secretary ( environment , forest , science &amp; technology ) and addressed to the additional secretary of MoEF&amp;CC . <p> In may be recalled that in mid-2016 , the state government had written a similar note to the union ministry suggesting that the ESZ of the park , which , as per the SRDP design , has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at 3-7 metres ( from the previously proposed 25 to 35 metres ) to accommodate the project . But even before that proposal could be processed , the Telangana government has sought a further reduction in the ESZ . <p> Result : if green signalled , this will give the authorities here a free hand to carry out any construction or developmental activity - including felling of trees -- within this zone that was , incidentally , made mandatory by the union ministry to protect the sanctity of national parks and wildlife sanctuaries in India . <p> " The protective wall around KBR NP provides total protection to flora and fauna inside the national park from biotic pressures . Hence , there is a strong case for notifying zero metres ESZ around KBR NP in Hyderabad just as was done for Guindy National Park in Chennai , " reads the letter , a copy of which has also been marked to Telangana 's principal chief conservator of forests &amp; chief wildlife warden and department of municipal administration &amp; urban development . <p> Justifying the move , the letter further states @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in mind the interest of the public and accommodate the SRDP " to ease traffic congestion around the national park " that is located in the heart of the city . <p> While MoEF officials in New Delhi , when contacted by TOI , confirmed the receipt of the fresh proposal , they said the matter was still under consideration .
