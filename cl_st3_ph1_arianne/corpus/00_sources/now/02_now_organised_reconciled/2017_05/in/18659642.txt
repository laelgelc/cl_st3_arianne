<h> Prawn farm owners halt demolition drive <p> Forest officials on Friday failed to undertake the demolition of illegal prawn farms at Bhitarkanika National Park in Kendrapada as many prawn farm owners and locals vehemently opposed the move . <p> TNN May 27 , 2017 , 02.33 AM IST <p> KENDRAPADA : Forest officials on Friday failed to undertake the demolition of illegal prawn farms at Bhitarkanika National Park in Kendrapada as many prawn farm owners and locals vehemently opposed the move . <p> " Around 200 prawn farm owners and locals gathered as soon as we arrived with JCB machines to demolish illegal prawn gherries ( farms ) at Nalitiapatia , Iswarapur , Paramanandapur and Dangamala villages within the park . They blocked the village road near the prawn farms for which we halted the demolition work , " said Akshaya Kumar Nayak , forest range officer of the park . <p> " We have already razed 200 acres of illegal shrimp farms within four days .
