<p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi is on a four nation visit . After the first stop over at Germany , Prime Minister will meet the top leadership of Spain , Russia and France and will discuss issues of mutual interest . Deepening bilateral trade and investment ties is a common thread across PM 's visit to these countries . <p> On May 31 , Prime Minister Narendra Modi will arrive in Spain where he will hold consultations with Spanish Prime Minister Mariano Rajoy and discuss bilateral and other issues of mutual interest . <p> As part of the programme , Prime Minister would be calling on King Felipe VI of Spain . He would also have a Round-Table interaction with leading Spanish business leaders and encourage them to partner with ' Make in India ' Initiative . This will be the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to Spain in almost three decades . <p> Prime Minister Modi will be in Russia on 1st and 2nd June . In his pre-departure statement on Facebook . <p> PM Modi said he greatly looks forward to his visit to St. Petersburg in this special year for the bilateral relationship as both countries celebrate the 70th Anniversary of diplomatic relations . <p> In the last leg of his visit , Prime Minister Modi will visit France . He will hold official talks with French President Emmanuel Macron on June 3 and discuss issues of mutual interest with a view to further strengthen India-France strategic ties . <p> PM Modi in his Facebook post said that he will exchange views with the French President on important global issues including UN Security Council reforms , India 's permanent membership of the UN Security Council , counter-terrorism cooperation , collaboration on climate change and International Solar Alliance . This will be the first meeting of the two leaders . Macron was elected French President in the recently held Presidential polls .
