<h> NGT sends Centre notice on CRZ rule <p> TNN May 31 , 2017 , 03.36 AM IST <p> Panaji : National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) has issued a notice to the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MoEFCC ) on the issue of an amendment to Coastal Regulation Zone ( CRZ ) 2011 to allow shack operators to retain their structures during June-August monsoon season . <p> MoEFCC had carried out the amendment on a pre-condition that the shacks remain non-operational during that period . <p> Social activist Kashinath Shetye , Aleixo Arnolfo Pereira , Ketan Govekar and three others have challenged the amendment , alleging that non-demolition of these structures would have an adverse impact on the beach morphology . <p> The shack operators erect their shacks traditionally in September/October at the start of the tourism season and dismantle them in May . Arguments were heard in the matter and the tribunal has fixed the matter again on July 7 , 2017 .
