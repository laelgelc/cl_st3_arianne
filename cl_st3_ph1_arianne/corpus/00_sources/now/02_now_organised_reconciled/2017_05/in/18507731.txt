<h> Cabinet condoles death of Environment Minister Anil Madhav Dave <p> The Union Cabinet today expressed its sorrow over the demise of Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Anil Madhav Dave . In a resolution passed at a special meeting , chaired by Prime Minister Narendra Modi , the Cabinet said in passing away of Dave , the country has lost a veteran and distinguished leader who held many eminent positions as a Member of Parliament and as Minister of State ( Independent Charge ) Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change . <p> While extending its heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family on behalf of the Government and the entire nation , the Cabinet noted , " Dave was a river conservationist , environmentalist and social worker . ' ' He was a prolific writer , authoring several books in Hindi and English . He was the founder of Jan Abhiyan Parishad and also edited the monthly magazine " Charaiveti " and JAP , Jan Abhiyan Parishad Journal " . He was elected to the Rajya Sabha in 2009 and re-elected to the Upper House in 2016 and became the Environment Minister , ' ' the resolution said . " Anil Madhav Dave was a true social worker widely known for his empathy for the poor and under-privileged . As an environmentalist , he worked tirelessly for conservation of Narmada river . He was a true nationalist and handled his public responsibilities with politeness and grace . In his passing away , the nation has lost an eminent leader from Madhya Pradesh , ' ' the Cabinet resolution said . UNI RBE/AR AE 1750
