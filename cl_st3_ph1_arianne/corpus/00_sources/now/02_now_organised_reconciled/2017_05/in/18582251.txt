<p> Two women from the state went to the White Continent to witness the ills of climate change , and now they are devoted to save the planet , more than ever <p> Bhavna Singh has n't had her favourite Lotte Choco Pie in two years . No , the Mysuru-based aerospace engineer is n't on a diet . She is just trying to reduce the consumption of plastic -- each pie comes in a red foil . In Bengaluru , when Praveen Chature orders food over phone , she asks the eatery staff to not pack her food . Instead , the urban planner goes to the outlets with her steel dabbas to take the order away . That 's again to fight the plastic use . <p> This concern for the planet got the two selected for International Antarctica Expedition ( IAE ) 2017 this March , organised by polar **25;872;TOOLONG Robert Swan . An ice shelf refers to a floating ice sheet attached to a land mass . " The Larsen B split was the first major sign of climate change back then , and the chunk has been drifting ever since . My heart sank at the mere sight of it ( it 's 1.5 times the size of Goa ) , " shares Singh , who 's originally from Delhi . <p> In return , Swan expects the participants to spread the lessons they learn , on this 10-day-cruise that embarks from Ushuaia , the southernmost city in the world . To refresh your Geography lessons , the atmosphere of Antarctica moderates heat and energy transfers between the ocean , sea ice , land and living systems of Earth , so any change here ( it has 90 per cent of Earth 's ice and 70 per cent of freshwater ) has global repercussions . <p> The sight of humpback-whales , penguins , and seals jumping and frolicking also lives on . " Antarctica is a planet in itself , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . We were welcomed by whales , penguins and seals , not humans . It warmed my heart to see them so happy , so free . We , human beings , must leave them alone , " says Chature , who is in her early 30s . <p> No plastic , please <p> To up her green game , Chature is now blogging her Antarctica experience in a bid to sensitise others of the reality . She 's also started composting her kitchen waste . Plus , she carries leaf plates to restaurants . How do their staff respond ? " Oh , they are too busy for any question and answer , " says Chature . She does n't buy clothes until necessary ( Textile industry is the second biggest polluter , after the oil ) . Old clothes and extra food are given away to others . " I do n't want anything to go to our landfills . <p> They are already overburdened . That 's my aim . " Judicious use of electricity , water and detergents that pollute them , are old-time habits . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " In 2014 , I was staying in Phase 2 of Electronic City in Bengaluru , close to my office . But walking till my office was a hell . Every day I had to pass by a landfill , which used to stink so much . I would ignore it , cover my nose with a hanky , and walk past thinking it will disappear one day . It did not . Instead , the landfill kept growing taller . That 's when I decided to look into my dustbin , and figure out how I can prevent my things from going to a landfill . " <p> So she started composting her kitchen junk ( usually accounts 60 per cent of the household waste ) , segregating it , and giving plastic packets away for recycling . The junk food lover gave up on instant noodles , chips , and , yes , choco pies , to trim her plastic footprint . She stopped shopping online , because the orders bring with themselves " a lot of plastic and bubble wrap " . She started buying vegetables @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ superstores that load you with plastic . " Earlier I used to empty by dustbin every day , now I do it once a week . " The 27-year-old has also switched to using bamboo toothbrushes . " Every year , the world trashes 1.2 billion ( plastic ) toothbrushes , " she tells us . <p> And when Singh travels , which she does often , she carries a steel bottle and a mug for her fill of water , coffee or tea , and an aluminium straw . Plus , she mostly uses public transport . <p> After the Antarctica trip , she has been mulling to try reusable menstrual cups , and even make organic shampoos , soaps , and detergents at her Mysuru room , where she currently stays . <p> But Chature and Singh do n't go about town lecturing people on the need for a sustainable lifestyle . They are leading by examples , and have piqued the interest of their families , friends and colleagues .
