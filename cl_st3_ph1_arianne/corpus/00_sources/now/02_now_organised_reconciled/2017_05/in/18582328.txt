<h> ' India must learn from Bhutan 's sustainable tourism model ' <p> PANAJI : India has much to learn about sustainable tourism from Bhutan , said Amita Prasad , additional secretary , ministry of environment , forest and climate . <p> " The small country focuses on high value , low impact tourism and is not concerned with the number of tourists it receives .
