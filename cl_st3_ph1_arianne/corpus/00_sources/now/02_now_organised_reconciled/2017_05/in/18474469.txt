<h> IMPLEMENTATION OF SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT RULES <p> Failure of authorities to comply with the Solid Waste Management Rules-2016 has prompted the state government to constituted State Level Advisory Board for its implementation . <p> The sanction for constitution of the Board was accorded vide order number 594-GAD of 2017 , on May 15 . <p> The Board having Administrative Secretary Housing and Urban Development Department as its Chairman , has 25 members . It is authorized to review the matters related to implementation of Solid Waste Management Rules , 2016 . <p> It has been entrusted to review State policy and strategy on Solid Waste Management and give advice to the State Government for taking measures necessary for expeditious and appropriate implementation of these Rules . <p> As per the order , the Board will act as a Monitoring Committee for proper implementation of directions of National Green Tribunal and Solid Waste Management Rules , 2016 . <p> " The Committee shall be serviced by the Housing and Urban Development Department and meet at least once in every six months , " reads the order . <p> The order @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ waste picker or informer recycler or solid based management shall be nominated by the Housing and Urban Development department as the members of the Board . <p> As reported earlier by Greater Kashmir in a series of stories , the Srinagar Municipal Corporation ( SMC ) and other organizations have failed to implement Solid Waste Management Rules ---2000 ( SWMR ) , needed to dispose bio-degradable and non-biodegradable waste in segregation . <p> Under SWMR , segregation of waste at source has been declared important process as it solves half the city 's problem of waste management . <p> Under these rules , scientific disposal of solid waste through segregation , collection and treatment and disposal in an environmentally sound manner minimizes the adverse impact on the environment .
