<h> Nalsarovar affected too <p> TNN May 9 , 2017 , 04.02 AM IST <p> AHMEDABAD : The state government while sending a revised proposal for clearing final ESZ boundaries around Nalsarovar stated that they had earlier included catchment areas which were not significant . The government shrunk the ESZ range from earlier 2.35 km near limit and 13 km far limit to 1 km near limit and 4.1 km far limit . <p> " These non-significant areas have now been excluded from the ESZ area in the revised proposal , " the government told the ESZ expert committee of ministry of environment , forest and climate change . <p> However , officials said that earlier the 13-km radius of the ESZ had been made to carefully cover catchment for the only Ramsar site in Gujarat , Nalsarovar , and extend it up to Surendranagar .
