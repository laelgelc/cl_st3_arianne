<h> Sensitisation programme on Management of Medical Waste held <p> Mysuru : A sensitisation ( Training ) programme on the Project ' Environmentally Sound Manage-ment of Medical Waste in India , ' for administrators of the district , was held at Abdul Nazeer Sab State Institute of Rural Development ( SIRD ) on Lalitha Mahal Road in city yesterday . <p> The programme , which is a training component of the project , was jointly organised by Global Environment Facility ( GEF ) , Government of India , United Nations Industrial Development Organisation ( UNIDO ) and M.S. Ramaiah Medical College , Bengaluru . <p> Speaking after inaugurating the programme , DC D. Randeep thanked the Union Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change for selecting Mysuru as the model district for Karnataka . <p> Seeking the support of all those involved in making the district a model for the country in Medical Waste Management , the DC assured all necessary help from the District Administration in making the project a big success . <p> Dr. S.P.
