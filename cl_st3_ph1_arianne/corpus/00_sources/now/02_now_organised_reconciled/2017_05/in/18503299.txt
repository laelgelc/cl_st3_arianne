<h> Harsh Vardhan gets additional charge of Environment Ministry <p> Science and Technology Minister Harsh Vardhan was today given additional charge of Environment Ministry , due to sudden demise of Anil Madhav Dave . <p> President Pranab Mukherjee , as advised by Prime Minister Narendra Modi , has directed that Harsh Vardhan , Cabinet Minister shall be assigned the charge of the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , in addition to his exiting portfolios , a press release issued by Rashtrapati Bhavan said .
