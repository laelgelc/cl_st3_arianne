<h> Farm forestry enters a good spell <p> More trees are being planted for farm forestry on the back of healthy log prices . <p> With log markets " humming along " and indications that the Government was going to have to ensure a higher carbon price if New Zealand was to meet its Paris Climate Change Accord , interest in new tree planting was growing , said farm forester , Denis Hocking . <p> To help new planters the Middle Districts Farm Forestry Association has organised a field day later this month , covering the basic skills of planting , pruning , thinning , chainsaw and general safety . Marketing and harvesting of plantations will also be covered . <p> Hocking said forestry experts would also provide guidance about alternative species . <p> " Examples of young to reasonably mature alternative timber trees including cypresses , macrocarpa and relatives , blackwoods and eucalypts , will be seen and discussed , including a number of the high durability eucalypt , " said Hocking .
