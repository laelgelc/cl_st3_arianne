<h> Wellington 's short-tailed bat feared extinct <p> The Wellington region may have lost its short-tailed bat to extinction . <p> As the Government prepares to reveal its new strategy for saving our threatened species , the Wellington region may have lost its own short-tailed bat to extinction . <p> Forest and Bird advocacy manager Kevin Hackwell said specialised listening equipment monitoring the bats in the Tararua Ranges had detected no conclusive recordings of them for months . <p> But Department Of Conservation ( Doc ) acting operations manager for Wairarapa , Briggs Pilkington , was not quite ready to call the population extinct , saying two possible recordings captured recently in a neighbouring catchment suggested they may have moved . <p> The bats are usually found in the Waiohine Valley .
