Not considered part of French Polynesia , Wallis Islands and the Hoorn Islands ( Futuna and Alofi ) sit among Fiji , Tonga , Samoa , Tokelau and Tuvalu , and have a population of about 12,000 - most residents live in New Caledonia . The capital is Mata'utu on Uvea in the Wallis Islands , and as well as French , the mostly Polynesian population speaks Wallisian and Futunan . <p> What do you do for fun ?
