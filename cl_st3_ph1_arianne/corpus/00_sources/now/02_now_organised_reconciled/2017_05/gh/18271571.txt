<h> Ghana resumes moratorium on Rose Wood harvesting and export <p> Ghanaian authorities announced here on Thursday the resumption of moratorium on the harvesting and exportation of Rose Wood . <p> This is to enable the country take steps to institute strict regulations of the harvesting , as part of its Climate Change control mechanisms ; as well as ensure orderly processing and export of the wood from the country . <p> " Future extraction of all Rosewood would be based on the CITES ( Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species ) approved yearly volume quota for the country , " Deputy Chief Executive Officer ( Deputy CEO ) of the Forestry Commission John Allotey announced this at a press briefing . <p> Harvesting of Rosewood commenced five years ago in the West African country with some timber companies given the license to trade in the wood , but the activities surrounding the Rosewood trade got out of hand necessitating a ban by the government . <p> " Rosewood is still being harvested indiscriminately , particularly in the three Northern Regions . This situation poses serious threat to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ minister to revisit the placement of a ban on the harvesting , transportation and exportation of Rosewood in the country , " the Deputy CEO explained . <p> With the stocks already at the ports and under various stages of preparation across the country , the official announced that the companies would be allowed to export them in order to decongest the ports , after they are made to pay penalties for flouting orders of the authorities .
