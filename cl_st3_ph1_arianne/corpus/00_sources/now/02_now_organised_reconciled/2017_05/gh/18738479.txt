<h> UNEP to help Somalia tackle climate change <p> UN Environment Executive Director Erik Solheim has pledged to support initiatives by Somalia in environmental conservation and use of renewable energy to mitigate the effects of climate change . <p> Solheim who visited Somalia on Tuesday to assess the extent and impact of the prevailing drought on the environment also discussed support for the new Somali government on other environmental issues , including the charcoal trade . <p> " Environment is a very central issue to any government and in particular it is central to Somalia because drought , as witnessed here over the last few months , is linked to climate change , " Solheim said in a statement issued by the UN Mission in Somalia ( UNSOM ) . <p> Solheim who held meetings with senior government officials including Prime Minister Hassan Khaire also noted that a reverse in widespread deforestation will be beneficial to the economy and the Somali people <p> " Equally critical is the need to tap into Somalia 's vast solar and wind energy resources , for the production of renewable energy , " Solheim @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to make environment conservation central to its agenda . <p> He also pledged the support of United Nations Environment Programme to protect the country 's fish resources , through the support of initiatives aimed at ending illegal fishing by international vessels in Somalia 's waters . <p> During the meeting , the Special Representative of the UN Secretary-General ( SRSG ) for Somalia Michael Keating stressed the importance of natural resource management , which he said is central to conflict resolution , peace , stability and economic growth . <p> " Competition for natural resources , if unregulated , can result in more conflict and more disputes , which in turn increase political instability , " he noted .
