<h> Awareness campaign to reduce water loss <p> DESPITE the recent rains , eThekwini is still affected by the drought and residents are urged to continue to conserve water . <p> To further spread the message of being water-wise , the eThekwini Water and Sanitation Unit will be erecting billboards , street pole posters and T-shirts with message of water conservation . <p> This comes as eThekwini Municipality has been , and still is , beset with grave drought adversity . <p> As a result , the eThekwini Water and Sanitation Unit has developed various intervention strategies to mitigate the drought effects including awareness campaigns regarding the importance of conserving water . <p> The campaigns have also raised awareness on the side of residents about the role water plays in social and economic development with the view of changing attitudes and behaviours of citizens . Community meetings have been held with ward councillors and print and electronic campaigns have been undertaken to ensure audibility of the water scarcity message . <p> There have been scheduled water interruptions from 7pm to 6am in certain areas .
