<h> President Zuma welcomes WEF delegates <p> President Jacob Zuma has extended a warm welcome to delegates to the World Economic Forum on Africa to take place in Durban from Wednesday , May 3 to Friday , May 5 . <p> The delegates include Heads of State and government from the African continent , global captains of industry and commerce and civil society leadership . They will meet at the Inkosi Albert Luthuli International Convention Centre , in Durban , under the theme " Achieving inclusive growth : responsive and responsible leadership " . <p> WEF on Africa will deliberate on important issues relating to the achievement of inclusive economic growth globally and with specific focus on Africa . <p> " We welcome all the delegates to this important gathering of the World Economic Forum on Africa in Durban , " said Zuma in a statement issued by the presidency .
