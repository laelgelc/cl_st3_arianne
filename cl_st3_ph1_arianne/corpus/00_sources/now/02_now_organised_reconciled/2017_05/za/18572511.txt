<h> New water and sanitation master plan to help SA achieve 2030 SDGs <p> Water security and sustainability took centre stage at the recent launch of the National Water and Sanitation Master Plan . <p> The plan , which was launched at the by the department of water and sanitation ( DWS ) in collaboration with the Netherlands , took place at the CSIR International Convention Centre last week . <p> Speaking at the launch , deputy-general Dan Mashitisho said there were inherent challenges in the supply of water and sanitation across the country . He said sustainability was something that needed to be addressed , and asked : " to which extent can we , or can we not be able to deliver ? " <p> He said these questions needed to be answered through proper planning processes which looked at the supply , preservation and management of water . <p> For many South Africans living in rural areas , proper water and sanitation infrastructure remains a luxury that they still do not have access to . " <h> Master Plan <p> The department 's deputy minister Pamela Tshwete said the Master Plan was about re-engineering the manner in which the water and sanitation business in South Africa is coordinated and guided . <p> " Working with all water sector partners we want to see a high level actionable plan , role-players , milestones and resources required towards achieving the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals , " she said .
