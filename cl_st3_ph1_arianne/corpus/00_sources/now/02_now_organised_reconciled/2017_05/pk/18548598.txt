<h> Various factors affecting crops yield <p> Injudicious use of inputs , poor techniques , postharvest practices and effects of climate change are affecting crops . This was said by University of Agriculture Faisalabad Vice Chancellor Prof Dr Iqrar Ahmad Khan at media briefing on the objectives and successes of US Pakistan Center for Advanced Studies ( USPCAS ) in Agriculture and Food Security on Saturday . He said sixty per cent of water and inputs at agricultural fields were being wasted due to imbalance usage and unavailability of modern technologies . He added climate changes were playing havoc with the crop production and food security like issues . He said under USAID funded USPCAS , five chairs , including climate change , precision agriculture , biotechnology , outreach and policy , had been established to address problems of the sector by making it compatible to international market . He said the USPCAS had initiated commissioned research keeping the need of farmers and trends in agriculture in view . -- APP
