<h> Inaction over climate change <p> Sir : Like other countries , Pakistan is also confronted with the effects of climatic change which is why temperature in some parts of the country often raises up to 50 Celsius . It has been noticed that federal and provincial governments are not making extraordinary efforts to cope with it . Merely documentation has nothing to do with the reality . The measures on ground by concerned authorities could be changing in this regard . <p> Unfortunately , third world countries like Pakistan do not take it as the biggest problem as other countries do . For us terrorists are formidable but the climatic change is not as important . Like terrorism , climatic change also kills people every year in the form of floods , drought , tornadoes , earthquakes etc . <p> It not only kills people but is also a threat to the economy of the country . Heat wave in Karachi did a lot of damage , hundreds died , but there was complete inaction from the government . <p> In the federal Capital , metro has been built @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ system but not at the cost of destruction of its greenery which surely affecting its environment too . <p> These a few examples in fact shows the priorities as well as lack of awareness on the highest level or it might be possible that the highest authorities are neglecting these factors because of temporary political gains . If the issue of climate change is not tackled properly , the consequences would be disastrous . <p> The Pakistani government must priorities its response towards climatic change in order to alleviate threats to environment . Maximum cultivation of trees should be ensured to prevent future calamities and cope with the present climatic changes .
