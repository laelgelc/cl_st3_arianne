<h> Good-looking scientists perceived as ' less able ' : study <p> MIAMI : People are more interested in learning about the work of attractive scientists , but they see these good-looking researchers as less able than their average-appearing counterparts , a study suggested . <p> The report in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences ( PNAS ) highlights the biases that come with judging people on looks , particularly in the field of science , in an era of popular TED talks and increasing online engagement . <p> " It seems that people use facial appearance as a source of information when selecting and evaluating science news , " said lead author Will Skylark from the Department of Psychology at the University of Cambridge . <p> " It 's not yet clear how much this shapes the spread and acceptance of scientific ideas among the public , but the rapid growth in visual media means it may be an increasingly important issue .
