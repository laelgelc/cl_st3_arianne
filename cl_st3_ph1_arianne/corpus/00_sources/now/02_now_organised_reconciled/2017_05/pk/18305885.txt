Fair weather politicians are all set to dust the eyes of the voters by promising them the moon . The current charged political scenario makes it clear that all political parties are flexing their muscles to win the elections 2018 with little concern for the problems of the common man . At the moment Pakistan is facing a plethora of problems . It is dealing with acute energy crisis , poverty , unemployment , adverse climate change , illiteracy , terrorism and internal and external militancy . Generally , political parties get votes by winning the hearts of the people through debates where they discuss their future plans and policies . They convince people through the strength of their argument . In Pakistan , leaders have evolved a bizarre course of action to run the election campaign . At rallies , instead of talking about the new and innovative ideas @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , befool and hoodwink the audience . They try to subdue their rivals by mocking , taunting , coining uncanny similes , far-fetched metaphors and by cracking hilarious jokes against them . <p> The members of political parties are busy in mud-slinging . Hurling allegations and counter-allegations have become the norm of our politics . Instead of addressing the core issues , our parliamentarians endeavour to prove their own leaders as the greatest person while at the same time they move heaven and earth to dub their opponents as people who 'd lie and deceive the people . They are least concerned about the difficulties faced by the common man . People in our country are forced to fight for their rights on their own . It is high time the people brought change through the power of the ballot paper .
