<h> Iqra University launches tree plantation campaign <p> Iqra University Islamabad Campus in collaboration with Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) , kicked off tree plantation drive here on Monday during a ceremony at the Sylvan Park . Students of the university , faculty members , and senior officials from CDA participated in the tree plantation drive and planted more than 200 saplings on the occasion . The basic purpose of the campaign was to create awareness among the general public about the protection and conservation of forests and the fragile ecosystem . The activity was organised by the university 's Eco-Club . The ceremony was graced by CDA Director for Parks Ghezwan Shamshad . Dr Muhammad Islam , Dean of the Islamabad Campus , welcomed the guests and thanked the CDA for all-out support . Dr Islam said the sole purpose of plantation drive was to keep the surroundings clean , green and pollution-free , in addition to resisting global warming and adverse effects of climate change . The activity started with a brief orientation about the plantation , followed by a lecture by Ghezwan Shamshad .
