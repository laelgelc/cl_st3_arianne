Mr. Trump 's lack of a decision after three days of contentious private debate and intense lobbying by other leaders came even as the six other G-7 nations reaffirmed their commitment to cutting planet-warming pollution in a joint statement issued on Saturday afternoon . The lobbying essentially ended in a stalemate , with Mr. Trump remaining opaque about his intentions regarding the 2015 pact as he prepared to return home after a nine-day overseas trip . It underscored the continuing division between the United States and its allies about the global environmental pact .
