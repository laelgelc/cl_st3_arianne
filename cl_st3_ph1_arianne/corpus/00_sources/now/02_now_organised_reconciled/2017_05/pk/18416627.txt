' <p> Yet Africa 's huge youth population could provide a " demographic dividend " benefiting the region , so long as young people could be encouraged not to leave , he said . <p> About 12 million people in Ethiopia , Kenya and Somalia are at risk of hunger due to recurring droughts , the UN Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) says .
