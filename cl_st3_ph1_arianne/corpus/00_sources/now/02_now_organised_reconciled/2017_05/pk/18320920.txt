" While progress in Asia has been impressive , the battle against poverty is not over yet as reminded by the participants , he added . " Reducing poverty will remain our major focus , " he added . <p> He said ADB will continue to prioritise the needs of the poorest and the most fragile countries , including small island countries in the Pacific . Growing inequalities are a major concern for many countries , he added .
