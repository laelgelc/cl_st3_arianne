PHOTO : EXPRESS/FILE <p> ISLAMABAD : After remaining dysfunctional for seven years , the country 's central lab for sampling and analysing air pollution is likely to be operationalised soon . <p> " On persuasion from the Pak-EPA and a letter written by Ministry of Climate Change Secretary Syed Abu Ahmed Akif , the ministry of finance has approved a supplementary grant of Rs93.3 million besides sanctioning 11 posts for laboratory 's appraising and staff , " Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) Director General Farzana Altaf Shah told The Express Tribune . <p> She said that a work order in this regard had been issued after completing all paperwork to re-operationalise the laboratory monitoring system . <p> " A contract of Rs12.7 million had been awarded to Maxtech Corporation for rehabilitation of fixed and mobile air quality monitoring stations besides analysers for all industrial emissions and vehicular emission analyser , " the Pak-EPA official said adding that Maxtech was expected to have started work @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's central lab for sampling and analysis of air pollution in Islamabad had been dysfunctional for the past seven years . A special grant of Rs18 million allocated for the lab 's revival had lapsed in June 2016 . <p> The EPA 's Central Laboratory for Environmental Analysis ( CLEAN ) , was completed in 2007 at a cost of Rs713.37 million with help from the Japan International Cooperation Agency ( JICA ) . However , it has not been operational since 2010 . <p> Based on requests from the Ministry of Climate Change in January 2016 , the finance ministry released Rs18 million to revitalise the lab . However , lax attitude of officials at the EPA and climate ministry meant that the allocated sum lapsed without being used . <p> The facility , which aims to form the basis of a nationwide analysis of environmental pollution and enhance the technical capacity of the environmental watchdog , consists of a main lab along with an air and water lab , a stake emission data centre and a microbiological lab with equipment capable of testing industrial and municipal waste as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ facility and sub-facilities , however , have been gathering dust at the EPA 's head office and provincial stations primarily because the Ministry of Environment was devolved to the provinces in 2010 . <p> But after six years of inactivity , there was some hope when on May 30 , 2016 , Akif and EPA officials had announced that the lab would be revived within a week . <p> The scheme , however , could not materialise , allegedly because of failure on part of the equipment suppliers in fulfilling procedural requirement against cash payment . <p> However , sources in Pak-EPA suggest that the EPA also lacks trained staff to run the lab . <p> The lab currently has only three staff -- a lab director , an inspector and an assistant .
