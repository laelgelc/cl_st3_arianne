<h> Humans have 100 years to find a new planet : Hawking <p> UST WASHINGTON - It 's no secret that physicist Stephen Hawking thinks humans are running out of time on planet Earth . <p> In a new BBC documentary , Hawking will test his theory that humankind must colonize another planet or perish in the next 100 years . The documentary Stephen Hawking : Expedition New Earth , will air this summer as part of BBC 's Tomorrow 's World season and will showcase that Hawking 's aspiration " is n't as fantastical as it sounds , " according to BBC . <p> For years , Hawking has warned that humankind faces a slew of threats ranging from climate change to destruction from nuclear war and genetically engineered viruses . While things look bleak , there is some hope , according to Hawking . Humans must set their sights on another planet or perish on Earth .
