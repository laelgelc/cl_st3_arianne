<h> IHC directs Climate Ministry to regularize employees <p> Islamabad High Court ( IHC ) Friday directed Ministry of Climate Change to issue notification for regularization of its contractual employees by May 9 . Justice Mohsin Akhtar Kiani heard the contempt of court plea filed by the some contractual employees of the ministry . During the proceeding , the bench inquired that why the notification for employees regularization was not issued so far despite of court orders ? The counsel for the petitioner , Saif ur Rehman said the secretary Ministry of Climate Change had ensured to issue this notification but it was not done so far . The court directed the ministry to issue the notification for employees regularization and adjourned the case by May 9 . The contractual and daily wages employees of the ministry had filed a contempt of court plea in the IHC for not complying court orders for their regularization . -- APP
