<h> Forestry Act comes into force , with a focus on felling <p> The commencement of the Forestry Act 2014 came into effect yesterday ( Wednesday , May 24 ) . It was announced by Andrew Doyle , Minister of State at the Department of Agriculture , Food and the Marine , with responsibility for Forestry . <p> New forestry regulations , giving the regulatory basis for various forestry activities such as the licensing of felling , afforestation , aerial fertilisation and forest road construction , have also come into effect from Wednesday . <p> In the announcement , Doyle said : " The Forestry Act 2014 is an important piece of legislation , which will make a significant contribution to the development of forestry by providing a more flexible system for regulating forestry activities , in general , and the felling of trees in particular . <p> The previous Forestry Act , 1946 , while it has endured and served us well for 70 years , was in need of an overhaul . <p> " I am confident that the new Forestry Act , which provides for the making @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will facilitate the development of our national forest resource and industry -- both at a local level which is primarily rural-based , and at a national level in terms of its contribution to the economy , " he stated . <p> One of the key changes brought about by the act -- and its associated regulations -- is the streamlining of the felling licensing system .
