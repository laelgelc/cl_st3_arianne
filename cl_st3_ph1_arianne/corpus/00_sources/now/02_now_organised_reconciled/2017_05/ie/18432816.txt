<h> European Innovation Partnership projects shortlisted <p> A total of 22 projects have been shortlisted for the next phase of the European Innovation Partnership ( EIP ) initiative under the RDP . <p> A total of 22 projects have been shortlisted for the next phase of the European Innovation Partnership ( EIP ) initiative under the RDP . <p> The 22 projects shortlisted for round two of the EIP will receive funding to develop a detailed project plan . A number of these projects will then be selected to go forward for full implementation . <p> The EIP forms part of Ireland 's Rural Development Programme 2014-2020 , and is centred on groups of actors coming together to develop and implement their innovative ideas to address challenges they have identified . <p> A very useful approach to addressing challenges in a collaborative and innovative way <p> " The proposals received in response to this call are of a very high quality and a clear indication that the EIP initiative is recognised in the rural economy as a very useful approach to addressing challenges in a collaborative and innovative way , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the shortlist on Sunday . <p> Competitive fund <p> Support for this element of the EIP is structured around a competitive fund , with an open call for proposals addressing key challenges in the agri-food sector . <p> The first stream is focused on themes such as farm viability , economic performance , sustainable forest management , and innovative technologies . The second stream is focused on challenges related to environmental , biodiversity and climate change issues . <p> " Successful proposals include projects to address such diverse themes as water quality , the restoration of upland peats , developing the potential of biomass , livestock management and production , forestry , organic production methods , food chain management , and a number of biodiversity challenges , " the minister said . <p> Second call <p> A further open call under the EIP will be issued later this year .
