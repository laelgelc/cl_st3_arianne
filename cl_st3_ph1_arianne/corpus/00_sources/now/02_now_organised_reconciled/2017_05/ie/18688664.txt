Yet again , the improving weather has coincided with a spike in the number of migrants attempting to cross from north Africa to Europe in rubber dinghies and rickety boats . In just two days last week , more than 4,000 people were plucked from makeshift vessels , bringing the total rescued and brought to Italy so far this year to well over 50,000 -- a 46 per cent increase on the same period in 2016 . They were the lucky ones . Last Wednesday , more than 30 people , mostly toddlers , drowned when about 200 people without life jackets fell from a small boat into the sea off the Libyan coast .
