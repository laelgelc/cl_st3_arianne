<h> President Higgins was expected to discuss ' issues of global importance ' with the Pope in the Vatican today . <p> PRESIDENT MICHAEL D Higgins has given Pope Francis a Climate Bell today as part of his visit to the Vatican . <p> The artwork is meant to signify the community effort that President Higgins believes is needed to limit climate change . <p> The hand bell is the fifth in the Climate Bell series by artist Vivienne Roche , and was inspired by the address by President Higgins gave at the Summit of Conscience for Climate 2015 . <p> In that address , Higgins said that " each citizen will be just as decisive in reaching the ambitious agreement mankind needs " as heads of state . <p> This particular bell , the fifth bell in the series , is called a ' Mother Earth Bell ' .
