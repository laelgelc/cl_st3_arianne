<h> Offaly school receives prestigious ' Diplomatic Global Passport ' <p> Killina Presentation Secondary School was recently presented with the Diplomatic Global passport at the annual Worldwise Global School conference in Croke Park . <p> The Global Passport is awarded every two years to schools to recognise and validate the integration of development education in post primary schools . There are three levels of award <p> *Citizen for emerging engagement in school*Diplomatic for establilshed engagement in school *Special for exceptional engagement in development education in school . <p> Killina received the first passport , Citizen , two years ago and this year further work was recognised and they were awarded the Diplomatic passport . <p> Some of the good work recognised in awarding the passport included Killina 's work on a global partnership with Mahilo School in Kaoma , Zambia , which some students will be visiting this June . <p> The two schools engage in joint learning with colleagues and peers through a series of projects , this year focusing on the theme of agriculture . <p> The school has established a Development Education committee which is student-led with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is responsible for raising awareness of development education issues throughout the school . <p> Killina has also managed to integrate DE into different curricular areas -- for example , links between Leaving Cert Chemistry &amp; Geography have been established regarding climate change and population densities . <p> On the day of conference some members of the Dev Ed committee attended along with teachers , Ms. Connaghan and Mr. Austin .
