<h> The link between gorse fires , farming and a disregard for nature <h> Michael Viney : A blazing furze bush is a furnace of wildlife <p> A furze bush burns in a sizzling eruption , as its pyrolitic oil takes fire . Conifer forest burns with a roar , creating its own winds from superheated air . Both are now the sounds of the incendiary crisis in the uplands . <p> It comes from a coincidence of climate change with a change in the use -- or disuse -- of land , and the spread of a shrub that was once a mainstay of the rural economy . Now it is a useless weed that can cost farmers payments on land they may not even use . <p> " Gorse fires " is the headline term , but heather , hill grasses and sedges fuelled most of the fires that have raged across the island over the past decade .
