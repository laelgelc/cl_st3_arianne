" For years we have been relying on arrivals as the impact but we need to examine the impact in terms of the economy , jobs , society , communities , " Dr. Rifai said during the discussion that looked at climate change impact , over-crowding at tourism sites and strains on infrastructure . " We need to move into a truly green economy . Growth and sustainability must go hand in hand .
