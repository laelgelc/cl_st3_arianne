<h> The Story Behind Our Solid Waste ( Mis ) management <p> In 1969 , at an oil well in Santa Barbara , California , a drill sunk under the sea-surface made a wound too deep . An uncontainable eruption of pressurised crude oil followed , and the underwater seepage continued to spread , poisoning about 10,000 sea creatures to death . Sufficiently chastised , governments finally paid heed to the doomsday warnings of conservationists and scientists . In 1970 , the first Earth Day was organised . The enthusiasm quickly spawned a financially lucrative industry of private recyclers . <p> Beginning as an idealistic campaign , recycling has by now surpassed its initial ambitions . Some nations within the European Union now recycle about 65% of their total waste , burning about 20% of the rest for energy , and minimising the remainder that would be disposed of in landfills . By April 14 this year , Sri Lanka , too , experienced its instructive disaster .
