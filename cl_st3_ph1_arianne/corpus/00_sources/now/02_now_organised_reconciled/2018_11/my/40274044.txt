<p> THE government has some RM2 billion worth of projects up for bidding in January 2019 to produce 500MW of electricity via solar power . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin ( picture ) said in the third cycle of the Large-Scale Solar ( LSS ) programme , three projects collectively worth RM2 billion will be carried out through an open tender . <p> " Details of the project tenders will be announced by then , " Yeo said at the Dewan Rakyat yesterday . <p> She was responding to a question by Kota Melaka MP Khoo Poay Tiong who asked if there would be more LSS projects to achieve the target . <p> The projects were in addition to the two ongoing LSS projects to produce 958MW of electricity between the end of this year until 2020 . <p> " Out of which , three projects with a capacity of 32.5MW have achieved commercial operating dates , which were otherwise expected to start generating energy from the end of 2018 to 2020 , " she added . <p> According to Yeo , the projects came under the purview of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the 20% renewable energy ( RE ) target for the nation by 2020 . <p> " The government aims to increase electricity generation from RE sources to achieve the 20% target by 2025 . <p> " It can be done through the implementation of several initiatives such as feed-in-tariff ( FiT ) , net energy metering and through the LSS generation project , " she said . <p> That , however , excludes the electrical power of large hydroelectric generators which are more than 100MW . <p> " Based on analysis , the nation must produce an additional 3,991MW or 4GW of RE to reach this target , " she said . <p> In planning the country 's electricity supply , Yeo said the government always needs balancing in terms of the energy trilemma -- reliability , affordability and sustainability .
