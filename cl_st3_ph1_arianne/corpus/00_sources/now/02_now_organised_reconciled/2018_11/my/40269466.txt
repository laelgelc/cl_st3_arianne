<h> Download MyCuaca for fast info on disasters <p> TAWAU : Malaysians are advised to download the MyCuaca application for free through Google Play and the App Store for updates on disasters including earthquakes and tsunamis in the country . <p> Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( Mestecc ) , Deputy Secretary-General ( Science , Technology and Innovation ) Associate Professor Ramzah Dambul said the application developed by the Malaysian Meteorological Department was a fast platform for society to receive information on disasters other than current weather conditions . <p> " The application has long been developed but the public 's awareness to use it is low , and , so far only 95,000 users have downloaded MyCuaca compared to some 30 million people in the country , " she said . <p> She said this when launching a public awareness programme on the risk of earthquake and tsunami and the 2018 tsunami practical training here today . <p> Meanwhile , Ramzah said that according to Malaysian geologists , disasters such as earthquake and tsunami would take place again after 20 to 30 years . -- Bernama
