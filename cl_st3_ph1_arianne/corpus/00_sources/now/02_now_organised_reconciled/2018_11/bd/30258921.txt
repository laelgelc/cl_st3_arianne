<h> 70 Bangladeshis receive Australian scholarships in 2019 <h> Seventy Bangladeshi public and private sector representatives have received the Australian government scholarships to study masters courses at Australian universities in 2019 . <p> The scholars will study a range of disciplines including , energy , climate change , cyber-security , education , engineering , public policy and health , the Australian High Commission in Dhaka said on Thursday . <p> High Commissioner Julia Niblett , celebrating their achievements at a farewell event , said as future leaders , there are many opportunities to contribute to the development of Bangladesh . <p> " Your Australian Masters qualification will assist you in positively supporting the future growth of Bangladesh , " she added . <p> State Minister for Power , Energy and Mineral Resources Nasrul Hamid thanked the Australian government , and the high commission for this " valuable initiative " . <p> " They have done a wonderful job in not only creating the training opportunity , but dealing with our agencies to bring out 70 brilliant graduates . I hope that we will work out a mechanism to extend this training programme further , " he said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after completing their studies in recent times . <p> Australia has supported over 3,000 Bangladeshis to study in its intitutions since 1972 . There is gender balance among the scholars with 50:50 ratio of men and women . <p> Ten percent of the 2019 scholars are from indigenous communities and six percent of scholars have a disability .
