<h> Danish , Iranian envoys present credentials to President <p> Envoy of Iran presents his credential to President Abdul Hamid at Bangabhaban on Wednesday , Nov 7 , 2018 . <p> Dhaka , Nov 7 ( UNB ) -- The newly appointed envoys of Iran and Denmark presented their credentials to President Abdul Hamid at Bangabhaban on Wednesday . <p> The ambassadors are Winnie Estrup Petersen of Denmark and Mohammad Reza Nafar of Iran . <p> Welcoming the Danish envoy to Bangabhaban , the President said the relation between Bangladesh and Denmark in the fields of trade and investment is very excellent , hoping that it will further be expanded during his assignment here . <p> Terming Bangladesh a country of huge potentials , Abdul Hamid stressed the importance of availing all possibilities in the interest of both the countries to boost businesses and trade ties . <p> He said Bangladesh is interested to increase the strategic partnership in different sectors , including climate change , environment and energy . <p> Praising the socio-economic development in various sectors of Bangladesh , Winnie Estrup Petersen said Denmark is also interested to contribute to the journey of
