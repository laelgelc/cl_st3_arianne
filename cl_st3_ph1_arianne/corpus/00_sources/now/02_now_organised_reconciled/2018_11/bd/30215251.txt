<h> ' Problems of poor groups must be tackled from multiple perspectives ' <h> Gowher Rizvi tells conference <p> Problems of the country 's poor groups should be looked at from multiple perspectives while addressing their disabilities and disadvantages , Prime Minister 's International Affairs Adviser Dr Gowher Rizvi said yesterday . <p> " The poor are not only monetarily poor ... They are poor in education ; they are politically disempowered ; they are socially non-populous , " he said at an international conference on human rights at a hotel in Dhaka . <p> " When we talk of this group , we need to look at what their disadvantages are , " he added . <p> The National Human Rights Commission ( NHRC ) with support from the human rights programme of United Nations Development Programme ( UNDP ) arranged the daylong conference , titled " Sustainable Development Goals and Human Rights : Role of NHRIs " . <p> Human rights defenders from different countries including Bangladesh , Nepal , Philippines and Malaysia attended the event divided into several sessions , said organisers . <p> Speaking as chief guest at the closing session , Dr Rizvi said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ did better than its neighbouring countries .
