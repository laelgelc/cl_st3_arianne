REUTERS <h> Fijian Prime Minister Frank Bainimarama will stay in power for a second term as he narrowly won a general election on Sunday after voting resumed following delays due to bad weather . <p> Bainimarama 's Fiji First party led with about 50 percent of the votes , closely followed by nearly 40 percent for opposition Social Democratic Liberal Party ( SODELPA ) , according to the Fiji Elections Office ( FEO ) . <p> Bainimarama , who has been the Prime Minister of Fiji since leading a bloodless coup in 2006 , has won 27 of 51 seats , according to the electoral commission . <p> " I 'm proud to become your prime minister once again , " Bainimarama told FBC News from Auckland , New Zealand , where he had been attending his brother 's funeral @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ three other losing parties urged the electoral commission and the FEO to refrain from officially announcing results , saying the tally process was not transparent . <p> Fiji went to polls on Wednesday , only the second time the country has held democratic elections since 2006 . <p> " The supervisor of elections has been in a great hurry to get the results out , " Mahendra Chaudhry of the Fiji Labour Party said on Facebook Live video , along with SODELPA , National Federation Party and Unity Fiji . <p> " He ( the supervisor ) has , in the process , compromised the procedures and the requirement of the law , so that should be set right if this election is to have any credibility , " Chaudhry said . <p> Elections Supervisor Mohammed Saneem said in response that the authorities had been open . <p> " The people of Fiji deserve better in terms of information . And the Fiji Elections Office is giving all the information accurately in a timely manner , " Saneem said , also on Facebook Live . <p> Endorsing Saneem 's claims @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said in a short interim statement on Friday that the Fijian election campaign was conducted according to international standards . <p> " We understand that there are some members of the public who have concerns about the integrity of the pre-poll ballots , and therefore we recognised the need to look closely at this process , " it said . <p> " The MOG assesses that the legal framework underpinning the electoral system complies with the fundamental international principles of universal suffrage and non-discrimination . " <p> Earlier in the week , heavy rain disrupted the election in some venues . Voting in those places was rescheduled to Saturday , Nov 17 , to ensure all eligible votes were counted .
