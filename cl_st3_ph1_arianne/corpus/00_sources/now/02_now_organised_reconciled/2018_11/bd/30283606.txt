<h> Militant suspect arrested <h> Books on jihad , toy rifle seized in Jhenidah <p> A team of Rapid Action Battalion ( Rab ) in a drive arrested a suspected militant from a house at Kaluhati village under Sadar upazila on Wednesday and recovered a toy rifle and books on jihad . <p> The arrestee is Aktar Hossain , alias Safari , 25 son of Sharafat Hossain of the village . <p> Jhenidah Rab-6 Commanding Officer Hasan Emon Al-Tajin said , acting on a tip-off , they raided the house of Sharafat in the village at around 4:00am . A bomb disposal team from Khulna joined the drive at around 8:00am . <p> During the drive they arrested Aktar and recovered some books on jihad and a toy rifle from the house . <p> Aktar passed the latest Dakhil exam from the local Dakhil Madrasa and got married six months ago . <p> As the news spread , hundreds of curious people gathered at the spot . <p> Earlier , in 2016 , police and Rab unearthed five militants ' dens in different places in Jhenidah , where huge quantities of explosives were recovered .
