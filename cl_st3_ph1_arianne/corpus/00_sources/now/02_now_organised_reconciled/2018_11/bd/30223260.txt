<h> Dolphin fair kicks off in Khulna to raise awareness on its conservation <p> UNB News <p> Publish Date - November 04 , 2018 , 04:06 PM <p> UNB NEWS - UNB NEWS <p> 293 Views <p> Update Date - November 04 , 2018 , 04:14 PM <p> Khulna , Nov 4 ( UNB ) -A 10-day ' Shushuk Mela ' ( Dolphin Fair ) kicked off at Hadis park in the city on Sunday , aiming to protect the endangered mammals through raising mass awareness on its conservation . <p> The Sundarbans is the home to Asia 's last two remaining freshwater dolphin species - globally endangered Ganges River Dolphin and Irrawaddy Dolphin . <p> Both these species are threatened with extinction due to over exploitation , unsustainable resource harvesting and livelihoods , poaching , industrial development , maritime traffic , and unplanned tourism . <p> United Nations Development Programme ( UNDP ) and Bangladesh Forest Department with financial support from the Global Environment Facility ( GEF ) have initiated a project called " Expanding the Protected Area System to Incorporate Important Aquatic Ecosystems ( EPASIIAE ) " in 2016 to engage community in conserving the aquatic animals @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water resources . <p> The project is also working to expand the operational coverage of dolphins and create alternative nature-based livelihood option for the fishermen . <p> October 24 was the International Fresh Water Dolphin Day and to mark the day , UNDP in partnership with the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) and CNRS organized a nationwide campaign . <p> After a three-day event in Dhaka , a ' Shushuk Mela ' is being organized in Khulna and around the Sundarbans . <p> The boat-based , interactive exhibition will travel to remote fishing villages for 10 days to gain support for aquatic wildlife , sustainable fisheries and climate change adaptation , and engaging local people in respectful consultations on natural resource management . <p> Puppet show , bioscope , art and crafts exhibition etc were also arranged for the children . <p> Mayor of Khulna City Talukdar Abdul Khalek inaugurated the fair that will continue until November 15 . . <p> Addressing the programme , He said , " The Sundarbans is not only an asset for Khulna but also for the country , we all @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ nature . And to protect the Sundarbans we need to conserve the all aquatic animals to keep the biodiversity alive . " <p> Arif Mohammad Faisal , Programme Specialist , UNDP said , " UNDP is working to bring the government , civil society , private sector , researchers and climate experts and fisherfolk community together in a single platform to protect aquatic biodiversity of Sundarbans which is essential to achieve the SDG14 : life below water . " <p> He also urged the government to declare dolphin as the ' Aquatic National Animal ' that will help for the conservation of dolphins . " If we want to save dolphins we must stop pollution around the Sundarbans " Arif said .
