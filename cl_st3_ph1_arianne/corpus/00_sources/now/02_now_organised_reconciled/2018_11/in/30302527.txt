Previously , scientists had found that increasing summer warmth in the Arctic was encouraging vegetation to grow , turning areas green . <p> " Despite the scale of Arctic browning , until now we knew very little about its impacts on ecosystem carbon balance ; the balance between carbon uptake by vegetation and its release from vegetation and soils , " said Rachael Treharne , a student at the university .
