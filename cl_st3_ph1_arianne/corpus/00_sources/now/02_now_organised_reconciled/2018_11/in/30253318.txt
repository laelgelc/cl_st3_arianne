<h> Capsule course for wildlife veterinarians starts at IVRI in Bareilly <p> BAREILLY : In a first , ministry of environment and forests and climate change ( MoEF &amp; CC ) has sponsored a 15-day capsule course on " Wildlife health management and conservation : issues and way forward " for wildlife veterinarians working in national parks and sanctuaries of 10 states . <p> AK Sharma , principal scientist and incharge of Centre for Wildlife at IVRI , said , " We are unable to diagnose the cause of death of animals in few cases because we do not receive sample of carcass in a proper manner . To rule out such problems , the experts will give training to wildlife veterinarians on how to preserve and send samples of carcass for post-mortem . Live demonstration will be given on how to restrain and tranquilize animals in case of man-animal conflict . Experts will conduct sessions on how to handle reptiles if it enters the house of humans . " <p> Sharma added , " We will also teach veterinarians on what precautions they should take after tranquilising an animal . Apart from theoretical classes at IVRI , the wildlife veterinarians will be sent to IVRI Mukhteshwar and Nainital zoo for live demonstrations for two-day . Apart from scientists at IVRI , experts from Wildlife Institute of India , Bombay History Natural Society , Wildlife SOS , School of Wildlife Forensic and Health will give lectures and conduct laboratory demonstrations . <p> RK Singh , director , IVRI , said , " More training of wildlife veterinarians will be organised in phases till all the states are covered .
