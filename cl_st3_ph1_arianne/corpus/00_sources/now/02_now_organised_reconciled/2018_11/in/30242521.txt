<h> Experts : Fast warming of Indian Ocean , a worry <p> TNN Nov 10 , 2018 , 06:07 IST <p> KOCHI : Marine experts have expressed their concern over fast warming of Indian Ocean due to global warming , thereby posing threat to species extinction . <p> Speaking at the opening session of a 21-day winter school on climate change in marine fisheries , organized by Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute ( CMFRI ) here on Thursday , experts pointed out that climate change is affecting fisheries through changes in stock productivity and its distribution . <p> Inaugurating the winter school , Kerala University of Fisheries and Ocean Studies ( Kufos ) VC A Ramachandran said climate change is causing floods and drought across the globe . <p> Ramachandran said increased water temperature and higher carbon dioxide concentration make ocean more acidic .
