<p> Across France , people are protesting the hike in taxes on fuel , wearing yellow vests and giving the movement the name Gilets Jaunes <p> 26 Nov 2018 <p> People across France are protesting the rise in taxes on fuel prices wearing the yellow vests which are mandatory for French drivers , giving the movement the name Gilets Jaunes . <p> On November 24 , thousands of protesters hit the streets of Paris to protest the rising fuel prices in the country . The demonstrations were independently organised in different locations , and have been identified as the ' Yellow Vests ' ( Gilets Jaunes ) , because of the yellow high-visibility vests worn by the protestors . The demonstrations involved blocking of important intersections , roundabouts , gas stations , and roads by protesters wearing the high-visibility vests which are mandatory for all drivers in France . <p> This is the second round of protests on the same issue . Earlier , on November 17 , more than 280,000 people participated in over 2,000 different demonstrations across France to protest against the government 's plan to increase taxes on fuel .
