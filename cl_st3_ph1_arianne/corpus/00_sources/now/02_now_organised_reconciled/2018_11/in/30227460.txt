<h> 19 new AIIMS to have Ayurveda centres : Minister of State for AYUSH <p> New Delhi : Ayurveda Departments will be opened in the new 19 AIIMS . This was announced by Minister of State for AYUSH Shripad Yesso Naik on Monday . <p> Inaugurating the third Ayurveda Day in New Delhi , Naik said that his ministry will organise 50 workshops in collaboration with MSME to enhance entrepreneurship in Ayurveda and AYUSH sector . <p> He also declared that work has started to have Ayurveda Departments in hundred ESIC hospitals under Ministry of Labour . <p> He further informed that approval has been received from Union Ministry of Home Affairs to open Ayurveda Departments in seven hospitals of Border Security Force and other paramilitary forces . <p> Union Minister for Science and Technology , Earth Sciences and Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Dr. Harshvardhann was also present during the inauguration of the third Ayurveda Day which was celebrated throughout the country on the occasion of Dhanwantari Jayanti . <p> In a bid to mark the day and with the intention to encourage entrepreneurs and Ayurveda stakeholders towards businesses opportunities in the sector , the Ministry @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Development in Ayurveda " in association with NITI Aayog.
