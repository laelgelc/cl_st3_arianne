<h> Course correction for Ashram underpass <p> NEW DELHI : The much-awaited underpass at Ashram Chowk , connecting Nizamuddin railway overbridge in north and CSIR Apartments in south , is a step closer to reality . The expenditure finance committee , chaired by deputy chief minister Manish Sisodia and with PWD minister Satyendar Jain and environment minister Imran Hussain as members , on Thursday gave its approval to the Rs 77.92-crore project , which was caught in a funding feud between the Delhi government and the Centre , leading to a delay in construction . <p> The project includes the construction of a 750-metre tunnel under the Ring Road towards Mathura Road and a pedestrian bridge near the CSIR Apartments . It was first conceived in 2014 and approved by LG and the Unified Traffic and Transportation Infrastructure ( Planning and Engineering ) Centre in January 2016 . The project will now require the approval of the Delhi cabinet before the Public Works Department calls tenders for construction . <p> " The planning and execution of the project will take about 18 months .
