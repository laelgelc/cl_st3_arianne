<p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi is in Buenos Aires for the 10th version of G20 Summit that is being held amid deepening uncertainty in global financial , economic and geopolitical order . An escalating trade war between the US and China is threatening to uproot global trading system and upend the stability of a WTO-led multilateral order . Volatile oil prices are adding to the pressure and stunting global growth . A populist backlash against post-Cold War globalism and free trade is sweeping across continents . <p> At its heart lies a yet unaddressed malady of trade gains not getting redistributed , creating tribes of winners and losers .
