<p> A group of people gathered outside the office of the Environment Ministry on Tuesday , demanding clean air and quick implementation of the National Clean Air Programme that will help in combatting the alarming rise in the pollution level that has raised health concerns . <p> The protesters asked the government to stop stubble burning in areas surrounding Delhi . <p> They also delivered a letter to Satyendra Kumar , Deputy Secretary , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change demanding faster implementation of the National Clean Air Program ( NCAP ) with time-bound targeted pollution reduction plans . <p> The key components of the NCAP include city-specific air pollution abatement action plans for 100 polluting cities of the country , increasing the number of monitoring stations , data dissemination , public participation on planning and implementation . <p> " For my children to have the lungs of a smoker by their teens , though no fault of their own , is absolutely unacceptable .
