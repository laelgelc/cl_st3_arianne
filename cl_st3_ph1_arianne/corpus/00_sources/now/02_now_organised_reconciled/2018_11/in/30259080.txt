The President made these observations while addressing the eighth convocation of National Institute of Technology ( NIT ) -Patna and first convocation of Dr Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University ( RAU ) at Pusa in Samastipur . <p> " Our daughters are performing well in academics , but it is also palpably evident that their number is not big in technological institutes . Their interest also seems to be limited in this field , " Kovind said at NIT-P . <p> Governor Lalji Tandon , CM Nitish Kumar and NIT-P director Pradip Kumar Jain were among others who spoke at the function . The country 's First Lady -- President 's wife -- also graced the occasion . <p> The President noted with concern that of the eight recipients of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ only one was a girl student . Similarly , of 733 students in the batch , only 96 or around 73% of them were girls , he said . <p> " I have special interest in education , because education provides foundation to any good society . I interact with students in central universities and other institutes as I am Visitor for them . The reality in NIT-Patna with regard to the presence of girl students is also visible at all other technological institutes in the country . Our daughters must come forward in the field of technology . They should also aspire to become missile women , " Kovind said , adding NIT-Patna has " national character " as its students come from 18 states from across the country . <p> Insisting that there should be substantial investment in research and development in India , the President said the countries which have given priority to R&amp;D have become developed ones . <p> The President also appealed to the students and faculties of NIT-Patna to coordinate with RAU to give boost to the technological aspects of sugar industry . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have become powerful as they constitute that country 's human capital . <p> The CM said the state government had taken active interest in the establishment , growth and expansion of NIT-Patna , which currently has 3,917 students .
