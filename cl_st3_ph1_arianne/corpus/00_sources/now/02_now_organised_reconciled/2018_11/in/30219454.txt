Alarmingly , experts have now found that Pune -- and the rest of Maharashtra -- may face a similar crisis over the next 50 years , if immediate steps are not taken to reverse the impacts of climate change here . <p> A World Bank report earlier this year showed temperatures in the state scaling up by 2-2.5 degrees Celsius in recent times .
