<h> Impact of Delhi 's foul air equals smoking 15-20 cigarettes a day <p> New Delhi : Doctors in the national capital on Saturday said the impact of air pollution on public health can be compared to that of smoking 15-20 cigarettes a day . <p> " I have seen a change in the colour of lungs over the past 30 years that I have been operating . Earlier , I used to see black deposits only in smokers and others would have pink lungs . <p> " But , nowadays , I only see black lungs . Even teenagers have black spots on their lungs . This is frightening . With this unique installation , we hope to show people the reality of what 's happening to their lungs " , said Dr Arvind Kumar , founder trustee , Lung Care Foundation , Chairman - Centre for Chest Surgery , Sir Ganga Ram Hospital . <p> " The impact of the foul air on public health can be compared to smoking 15-20 cigarettes a day , " he said . " Air pollution has reached alarming levels in Delhi and is causing severe damage to the health of the citizens . <p> We have to act immediately to control this menace ; otherwise , the health consequences will be disastrous .
