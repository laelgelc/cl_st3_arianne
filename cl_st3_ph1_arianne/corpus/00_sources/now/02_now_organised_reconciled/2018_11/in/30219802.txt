<h> A case to make Bengaluru the Sci-Fi hotspot <p> The internet is full of articles telling you why entrepreneurs or even big company executives should read science fiction . Many large companies even hire futurists -- who work closely with science fiction writers -- to imagine many different futures for the world . <p> Companies such as Volkswagen , Hershey 's and Capital One , according to this report , have futurists working for them . Their job is not exactly to predict the future but to help people think through possible futures . <p> We 've interviewed folks from companies like Lowes , where the leadership has made attempts to learn from science fiction and put some of those concepts to test : The retailer put a 3D printer in space 2015 and experimented with virtual reality . <p> Google 's assistant , Amazon 's Echo , Apple 's Siri and Microsoft 's Cortana have long been envisioned in science fiction . Virtual reality and augmented reality has been part of many science fiction books much before Mark Zuckerburg or Pokemon Go put a spotlight on them . Uber 's flying cars ...
