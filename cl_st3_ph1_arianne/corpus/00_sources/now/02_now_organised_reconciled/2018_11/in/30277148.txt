<h> Meet the Students Tackling Waste Problem with ' Plastic Arrest ' <p> The busy Palayam market in Thiruvananthapuram witnessed some unique activities on Monday , 19 November , as school and college students were seen interacting with shoppers and asking them to replace plastic carry bags with cloth bags and to plant saplings at the corporation office ground . <p> This group of students was on an organic mission : to trash-hunt and segregate waste properly ; to create awareness among people to not use plastic ; to inform them about the City corporation 's material recovery facilities ; and to ask them to plant tree saplings for the future . <p> They also carried out an extensive marketing audit on the products that come in plastic bottles and packets as well as the companies that produce them .
