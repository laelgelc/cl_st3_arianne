<h> Will we miss fireworks and fun this Deepavali ? <p> The Supreme Court has limited the bursting of crackers to just two hours this festival season . How will it affect our upcoming Deepavali celebrations ? <p> TNN Nov 5 , 2018 , 00:00 IST <p> Malayalis might not celebrate Deepavali as elaborately as North Indians , but , when it comes to the craze for crackers during such festivities , we are not very different from them . When the Supreme Court recently directed to limit the year 's Deepavali fireworks to two hours , it got many talking , though the court allowed the Southern States to pick their own time of choice for the celebrations . How does Kerala see the cracker curb and how far will it change our Deepavali ? We find out : <p> ' A Timely , Welcome Ban ! ' <p> K Sajeevan , the chairman of Kerala State Pollution Control Board(PCB) , feels Supreme Court 's two-hour restriction is good as compared to a blanket ban on crackers . She says , " A pencil-shaped sparkler , which was only supposed to emit colours when lit , flared up in my hands and I saved myself by a whisker . " But that got her thinking how unsafe crackers are . " The Puttingal Temple tragedy is a sore reminder .
