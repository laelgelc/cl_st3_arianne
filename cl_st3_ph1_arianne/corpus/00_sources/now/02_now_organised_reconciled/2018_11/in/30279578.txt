<h> EU unveils strategy on boosting ties with India <p> NEW DELHI : A new European Union strategy for India calls for greater military to military relations " including between leaders of the Indian armed forces and the EU military structures . " <p> Tomasz Kozlowzski , EU 's ambassador to India , speaking to journalists , said the EU strategy came after year-long discussions with different stakeholders in Europe and in India . The broad direction , he said , came from the two India-EU summits of 2016 and 2017 . The strategy , developed by the EU Commission , will have to be cleared by the EU Council and debated by the European Parliament . EU officials do n't expect to see any changes to the strategy , though priorities and focus areas would be spelt out . <p> India-EU relations have been defined by the collective failure to complete a free trade agreement . It has also been characterised by India not really taking EU seriously , while developing deeper relations with member countries . That may be changing , Kozlowski said , with India and EU charting a new path . <p> In the past @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government had come to regard Europe as a more useful partner in its modernization and economic agenda , including climate change and environment , smart cities , water etc . <p> Kozlowski said EU was working with four Indian cities -- Delhi , Bengaluru , Goa and Raipur -- to improve air quality . The EU plans to join the International Solar Alliance by year end , he said . The EU has already financed the ISA secretariat as well as other programs . <p> The strategy says , " EU and India share common security interests in a growing number of areas " -- these range from counter-terrorism to maritime security , including cyber security , crisis management etc . <p> EU officials said India and EU had just completed a set of intensive discussions on counter-terrorism last week , which included discussions on combating financing of terrorism , with special emphasis on Pakistan , where India 's approach may not always match EU 's , but " both sides are learning to understand each other better " . <p> The strategy also focuses on securing sea lanes of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ both EU and India ... Both are strong promoters of the respect for international law , in particular UN Convention on Law of the Sea ( UNCLOS ) . " <p> Politically , the two sides have been cooperating a lot more , officials familiar with developments said . India coordinated closely during the crisis in Maldives , while the two sides have intensified discussions on Sri Lanka , Bangladesh and Nepal . These were traditionally " no go " areas for India . <p> The tough talk remains on the pending trade and investment agreement where the two sides are yet to find meeting ground . Kozlowski observed that talks on BTIA " used to be treated as the single issue on the economic agenda .
