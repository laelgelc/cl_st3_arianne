<h> Rain of sorrow falls on farms <p> Rajkot : Nothing seems to be going right for farmers in Saurashtra . They have been battling on a multitude of fronts to keep agriculture running , but failing at every step . Getting minimum support prices for crop yields have consistently been difficult or impossible . Production costs have shot up for many reasons . Pests have been increasing due to change in weather , and the barriers in getting crop insurance seem insurmountable . For the past several months , Saurashtra 's farmers have been forced to sell their produce at rock bottom prices . And now crops have begun to fail due to lack of water . Crop failure due to insufficient rainfall has resulted in at least 14 debt-ridden farmers committing suicide since September . <p> The deepening distress of farmers and suicides come barely six months before the Lok Sabha elections . In 2017 , the state BJP had already faced the farmers ' wrath with the party losing 17 out of 18 seats in five districts long considered to be saffron bastions .
