<p> The state Environment and Climate Change Department has issued detailed instructions to ensure strict compliance of the Supreme Court 's order regarding not allowing bursting of crackers beyond the stipulated timeframe of two hours on Diwali . <p> " In view of growing air and noise pollution which rises to serious proportions especially during winter season with the unbridled bursting of fire crackers on Diwali or Gurpurab night , there is an urgent need to initiate strong measures to curb this menace , " said the state Environment and Climate Change KS Pannu . <p> Pannu said that all the concerned authorities have been ordered to maintain strict vigil on Diwali night to stop bursting of crackers outside the allowed limit of 8 pm to 10 pm only . In case of any violation , the SHO of the concerned Police Station would be held personally liable , and strictly action would be initiated , he added . <p> He pointed that the crackers not only release obnoxious molecules in the air but also release Particulate Matter ( PM ) , both PM 2.5 and PM 10 increasing the level of air pollution in the state . On Christmas eve and New Year eve , when such fireworks start around midnight , that is 12 am , the bursting of crackers have been allowed from 11:55 pm till 12:30 am only . <p> Exhorting one and all to make sincere efforts in safeguarding our planet for future generations , Pannu said that it was high time people realize the importance of impending air threat which could hamper the overall quality of our living . <p> He hoped to see a major behavioural shift this time around and the government is duty bound to ensure a clean pollution free environment to its citizens , and implored them to celebrate green Diwali this year .
