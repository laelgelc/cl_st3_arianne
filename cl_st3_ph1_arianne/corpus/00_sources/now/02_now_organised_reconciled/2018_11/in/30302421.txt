<h> The cloud seeding process needs to work with existing clouds , which do not usually form over Delhi in the winter . <p> As Delhi continues to choke from air pollution , the Union environment ministry plans to create artificial rain through cloud seeding in the hope that it will wash away pollutants . This is the process of introducing chemical agents such as silver iodide , dry ice and common salt in certain types of clouds to induce rain . " The Centre has decided that if the air quality crosses the 500 air quality index mark then they will ask authorities to induce artificial rain or cloud seeding over the capital , " said Mahesh Sharma , Union Minister of State for Environment , Forest and Climate Change on November 20 . <p> However , even as scientists are yet to pick a date for this experiment , experts point out that winter conditions do not usually permit the formation of clouds suitable for seeding . Others are not sure if this method is a feasible plan to improve Delhi 's worsening air quality .
