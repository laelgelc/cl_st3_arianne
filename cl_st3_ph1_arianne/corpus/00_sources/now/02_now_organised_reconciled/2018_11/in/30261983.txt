Polluted air is damaging India 's future -- its children . Girl children bear the worst brunt of polluted air . <p> A new report by the WHO reveals that in 2016 , over a lakh ( 100,000 ) Indian children below the age of five were killed by exposure to air pollution , indoors and outdoors . One key initiative mentioned several times by Indian officials is the Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana , which seeks to move poor Indians to cleaner cooking fuels .
