<p> CHENNAI : Nearly three weeks before the cyclone Gaja wreaked havoc in Nagapattinam and other districts and uprooted thousands of overhead electric lines ( OHEL ) and electric poles , the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change had given clearance to the Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation ( Tangedco ) to convert the overhead distribution lines into underground cable in Nagapattinam district . The Tamil Nadu Coastal Zone Management Authority ( TNCZMA ) recommended the Rs 186crore project in November 2017 . <p> The ministry had given its nod for the proposal to take up underground cabling system for a stretch of 211.38 km , including 28.2km in Costal Regulation Zone ( CRZ ) - II and 15.24km in CRZ-III . It would cover Nagapattinam town , Thonithurai , Nagore , Velipalayam and Velakani . <p> The grant of clearance to the project was communicated to the chief engineer of Tangedco on October 25 . The project would be carried out under coastal disaster risk reduction project with assistance from the World Bank . Experts have also been emphasising on converting to underground cabling systems in cyclone prone areas to minimise loss of property and lives . <p> The Union ministry , in its official communication , stated that the Institute of Remote Sensing of Anna University , Chennai , should prepare a map superimposing the cabling route along the CRZ . The underground cabling work should be executed by excavating trenches of 1.2 metre depth and 0.5 to 0.9metre width . <p> It insisted that Tangedco adheres to norms in accordance with the provisions of CRZ notifications , 2011 .
