<h> Seed collector Jayaraman 's work now preserved <p> CHENNAI : In 2008 , the Svalbard Global Seed Vault -- a " Noah 's Ark for the world 's seeds -- was built under a mountain on the Norwegian island of Spitsbergen to protect rare and precious seeds from climate change , new plant diseases and increasing food production . A great initiative to preserve agricultural knowledge , but in Tamil Nadu a lone farmer had begun a similar journey of conservation a couple of decades ago to revive traditional paddy varieties . Nel Jayaraman travelled across the state and could revive more than 170 traditional paddy seeds . A couple of weeks ago , 51 rice lines from Jayaraman 's collection were handed over to the Indian Council of Agricultural Research to be preserved in a vault at the National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources ( NBPGR ) . A great recognition for a humble farmer like Jayaraman , but coming too late . He has been undergoing treatment for cancer for the past three months . <p> But , Jayaraman is happy that the vault containing the first set of 51 paddy seeds will be used for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ time a farmer from south India is being recognised for his collection at a national institute . <p> M J Prabhu , a senior agricultural journalist who has been travelling with Jayaraman for more than 15 years , said it was challenging to move across Tamil Nadu in search of seeds . " Jayaraman always wanted to create repositories for traditional paddy seeds . He travelled to remote villages and collected traditional paddy seeds from farmers . But I believe that we have failed in giving him due recognition , " said Prabhu . <p> A school dropout-turned farmer , Jayaraman revived the paddy seeds as part of the Save our Rice ( SoR ) Campaign , a national movement aimed at conserving traditional varieties of paddy , a decade ago . A native of Adirangam village in Tiruvarur district , it was through various experiments and observations that he realized native varieties of paddy were best suited for the coastal districts which are prone to climatic variations . " It 's unfortunate that the chief minister of Tamil Nadu has not visited the hero of agriculture , who is having a tough time in hospital , " Prabhu said , adding that whatever little the 53-year-old Jayaraman got as donations for his treatment , he has sent to the Kerala chief minister 's flood relief fund after the devastation caused in August . <p> The seed vault in the name of Jayaraman is a great initiative , said N K Krishnakumar , former deputy director general of the ICAR . " The NBPGR will use the paddy seeds for various genetic purposes . This will ensure future food supply .
