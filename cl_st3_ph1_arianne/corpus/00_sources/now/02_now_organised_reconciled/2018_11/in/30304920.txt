Trump has shocked the European Allies , by threatening a relook at Trans-Atlantic cooperation , unless European governments contributed more to NATO . He has imposed higher tariffs on selected European exports . <p> In brief , Trump has replaced American advocacy of globalisation , by an " America First " doctrine .
