<h> China is by far the biggest consumer of coal which is mainly used to produce electricity <p> Paris : Coal remains the most widely used means of electricity production in the world . It also happens to be the biggest emitter of climate-changing carbon dioxide of any fuel.Despite efforts to tackle global warming , worldwide demand for coal was up one percent last year , mainly due to demand in Asia . <p> China is by far the biggest consumer of coal which is mainly used to produce electricity . <p> However even in China there is now political pressure to improve air quality in urban areas , with a new trend towards using natural gas and renewables . <p> In 2017 , after two years of declines , International Energy Agency figures showed global coal demand rising to 5.357 million tonnes of coal equivalent ( TCE ) . <p> While many advanced economies , such as Canada , Germany and the United Kingdom are considering how to phase out coal use in power generation , the same is not true everywhere . <p> India seems set to replace China as the world 's biggest coal consumer while @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Philippines and Vietnam have also registered big increases . <p> " Many developing economies view coal as important to their economic development because of its ready availability and relatively low cost , " the IEA said in it World Energy Outlook 2018 report . <p> The prediction is for demand to breach 5,400 million tonnes before 2040 with an expected drop in use in China , the European Union and the United States set against the rises in India and Southeast Asia . <p> Coal is key to the climate change issue as it was responsible for 40 percent of carbon dioxide emissions last year , ahead of oil ( 34 per cent ) and natural gas ( 19 per cent ) , according to figures from the Global Carbon Project group . <p> The IEA warns that " urgent action " is needed to boost levels of carbon capture and storage . <p> At present the costs of such technology can be prohibitive . <p> There are currently two large-scale carbon capture , utilisation and storage ( CCUS ) projects in operation , according to the IEA ;
