The AQI was 281 at 7 pm . It rose to 291 at 8 pm and further deteriorated to 294 at 9 pm and 296 at 10 pm , according to the CPCB . <p> The Supreme Court had allowed bursting of firecrackers from 8 pm to 10 pm only on Diwali and other festivals . Areas like Anand Vihar , ITO and Jahangirpuri recorded very high pollution levels . <p> The police admitted that there were violations , adding that they would take serious legal action against those violating the apex court order . <p> The online indicators of the pollution monitoring stations in the city indicated " poor " and " very poor " air quality as the volume of ultra-fine particulates PM2.5 and PM10 , which enter the respiratory system and manage to reach the bloodstream , sharply rose from around 8 pm . <p> According @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ PM2.5 and PM10 were 164 and 294 micrograms per cubic metre respectively . <p> The SAFAR forecast " bad " air quality Thursday even though partially toxic crackers were burst as compared to 2017 .
