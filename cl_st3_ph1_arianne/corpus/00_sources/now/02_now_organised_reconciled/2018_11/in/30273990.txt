Our main points of contention were : <p> Full system cost : Our work puts the operating cost of such removal to be in the range of $400-600 per tonne of CO2 . That 's way higher than some of the other mitigation technologies that are thought to be unviable . While some recent experiments have shown the costs to be much lower ( put David Keith 's article ) , the scalability of these successes remain a big question mark . Most of these experiments ignore the capital costs of scaling this technology , which can be prohibitive . <p> Operational challenges : I 'll go technical for a moment here but the amount of solvent required for taking about CO2 at scale , about 1 gigatonne , is nothing short of impossible . The captured CO2 will have to be sequestered . By some estimates , the size of pipeline infrastructure needed for US to sequester this scale of CO2 will be equal to the current one to ship oil . That kind of capital investment is no joke and it wo n't materialise at the last minute .
