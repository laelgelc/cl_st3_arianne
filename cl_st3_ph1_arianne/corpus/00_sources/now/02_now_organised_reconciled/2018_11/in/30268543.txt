<h> Govt to promote Tharu art and craft items <p> BETTIAH : Old tales , as also vintage pictures , speak and show the ' Sola hat ' that the British regularly wore in the hot plains of India . On Sunday , witnessing a handicrafts exhibition that displayed the traditional art and crafts of the Tharu and Oraon population inhabiting the sprawling Valmiki Tiger Reserve ( VTR ) and its vicinity in West Champaran , CM Nitish Kumar was drawn to the traditional ' topi ' hand-crafted and worn by the Tharus . Picking it up , Nitish quipped , " This is the Don hat ! " A brand was thus launched off the cuff , instantly . <p> Don is a cluster of 22 predominantly Tharu and Oraon villages interspersed in a valley criss-crossed by streams in the foothills on the Nepal border in West Champaran . Falling in the midst of VTR , the region , to the human eye , resembles a bowl that is ' Don ' in Bhojpuri . " The government shall strive to brand their products , " the CM added . <p> Traditional song and dance forms of these communities too shall find a platform in the government 's tourism road map .
