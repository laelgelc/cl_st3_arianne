The clearances from multiple government departments for flying the Indian Space Research Organisation ( ISRO ) aircraft to conduct the never-attempted experiment to seed clouds are there only till November 25 . <p> " There is no indication of rain clouds till November 25 , " confirmed BP Yadav , deputy director general of IMD . To make things worse for the prospects of artificial rain , the aircraft to carry out the artificial rains experiment is also needed to carry out pilots in the run-up to the Chandrayaan 2 mission .
