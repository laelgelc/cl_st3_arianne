<p> Indian Institute of Science ( IISc ) professors Navakanta Bhat and S.K. Satheesh are among the six winners of the software major 's Infosys Prize 2018 in the Engineering &amp; Computer Science and Physical Sciences categories said its Science Foundation on Tuesday . <p> " Bhat is awarded the prize for work on the design of novel bio-sensors and Satheesh for a pioneering work in climate change , " said the city-based Foundation in a release . <p> Bhat is also Chairman of the Centre for Nano Science and Engineering in the renowned institute . His work is based on research in biochemistry and gaseous sensors that push the performance limits of metal-oxide sensors . <p> " His ( Satheesh ) studies on black carbon aerosols , the dark , light absorbing , microscopic particles in the air , which influence the energy balance of the atmosphere over the Indian subcontinent have enabled a better understanding of their role on climate change , precipitation and human health in the subcontinent , " said the statement . <p> The annual prize celebrates the success of the recipients in science and research by recognising their achievements in the six categories @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Mathematical Sciences , Physical Sciences and Social Sciences . <p> The prize carries a gold medal , a citation and a tax-free purse for Rs 73 lakh . <p> " Our laureates have achieved milestones in their domains and 2018 's winners will raise the bar for science and research over the next decade , " said the Foundation . <p> By recognizing outstanding researchers and celebrating their achievements , the Foundation aims to inspire young minds to explore science as a career option and advance innovation in the country . <p> " Motivating researchers at the right stage in their careers is critical for their success as they represent the future of India in their respective fields , " said the statement . <p> Set up in 2009 as a not-for-profit organisation by Infosys co-founders N.R. Narayana Murthy , Nanda Nilekani , S. Gopalakrishnan , S.D. Shibulal and Dinesh , the Foundation promotes interest in science and research in the country .
