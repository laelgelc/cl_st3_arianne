Ecosystems that host a carbon-dioxide-rich type of soil called peat , known as peatlands , are the most efficient natural carbon sink on the planet . When undisturbed , they store more carbon dioxide than all other vegetation types on Earth combined . <p> However , when they are drained and deforested , they can release nearly six per cent of global carbon dioxide emissions each year . Climate researchers are worried that many of the peatlands soaking up carbon now will soon be doing the opposite .
