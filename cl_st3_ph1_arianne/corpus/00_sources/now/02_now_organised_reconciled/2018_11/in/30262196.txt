<p> Countries across the world are facing threat of climate change and there is an urgent need for innovation in agriculture , said Union Minister of Commerce &amp; Industry and Civil Aviation , Suresh Prabhu . <p> He was speaking at an international conference and awards function for innovations by agri startups in New Delhi today . <p> The Minister said that in the next few years there will be more demand for food as the world population is growing and therefore there is a need for agri startups to produce more food crops in limited fertile soil and use less water . <p> Prabhu also called for innovations to minimise wastage of food grains , fruits and vegetables . <p> The Commerce Minister said that the agricultural sector will have immense possibilities for startups , which are now confined mainly to think tanks and manufacturing . <p> He said that high yielding and disease resistant crop varieties , soil nutrient maps , improved design of dairy , poultry and fisheries technologies should be developed with new innovations . <p> Urging the agri startups to become more innovative Suresh Prabhu said that the Government is working on a comprehensive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Ministry is actively engaged with the startup community . <p> The Minister gave away innovation awards to agri startups in different categories and also released the PWC Knowledge report of FICCI.
