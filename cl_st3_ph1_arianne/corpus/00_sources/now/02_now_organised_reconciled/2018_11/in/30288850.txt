<h> Naveen Patnaik salvo may delay Polavaram works further <p> VIJAYAWADA : The latest salvo fired by the Odisha government against the Polavaram project might further delay the work of the dam across Godavari . Besides writing a letter to the Centre , Odisha chief minister Naveen Patnaik has approached the supreme court seeking a halt in the dam work . The apex court has adjourned the next hearing to November 27 . <p> Andhra Pradesh has been going ahead with the Polavaram project , considered the lifeline of the Godavari and Krishna deltas , based on temporary relief provided by the union ministry of environment , forest and climate change . The relief is extended every year and the last time the state got the nod from the Centre was on July <p> 3 .
