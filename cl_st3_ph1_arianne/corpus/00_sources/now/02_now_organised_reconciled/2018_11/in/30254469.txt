<h> Time for our scientists to solve pollution , water woes <p> Murthy highlighted water and power as two areas on which Indian scientists could focus their efforts to significantly benefit the country . <p> BENGALURU : Infosys founder NR Narayana Murthy is shocked that little is being done to clean up the toxic air in Delhi , named the city with the worst pollution in the world . He said this and healthcare were national issues that needed long-term solutions rather than short-term fixes . <p> " Delhi was declared the most polluted place in the world -- I was shocked . It is time we all came together as Indians and say we will solve this problem , " Murthy said in an interview on the sidelines of the Infosys Science Foundation 's annual awards . <p> The National Capital Region has faced weeks of high air pollution , with particulate levels reaching the double the upper limit of what is permitted -- the equivalent of smoking a packet of cigarettes a day . <p> Murthy highlighted water and power as two areas on which Indian scientists could focus their efforts to significantly benefit the country . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ our scientists work on how to make seawater potable and usable for washing ... I would say that has to be done . It is very , very important . Scientists should look at how to improve the efficiency of conversion of solar power to electricity , while making it cheaper , " Murthy added . <p> He said science needed to be seen as a viable career option and that the country should encourage children to go into scientific and engineering research . For that , leaders would need to talk about the role of scientists and honour them . <p> " Look at our Padma awards . How many scientists do you see ? We give them to musicians and those guys . I have nothing against music but science is also important and research is important . In any society , people respect any career -- there is recognition and respect , " he said . <p> Infosys established the Science Foundation toward this end .
