For example , global coverage for the Hib vaccine is 72 per cent , but only about 44 per cent of the world 's children receive the complete course of the pneumococcal vaccine . <p> Universal vaccination for the pneumococcal vaccine alone would lead to an estimated annual reduction of 11.4 million days of antibiotics for children under five . <p> Simply put , there is an urgent need to increase uptake of vaccines that are already available -- including for typhoid -- and to develop new ones .
