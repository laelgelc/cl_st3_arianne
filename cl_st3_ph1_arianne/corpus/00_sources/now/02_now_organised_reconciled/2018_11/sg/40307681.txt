REUTERS/Nacho Doce//File Photo <p> By Marcelo Teixeira <p> SAO PAULO ( Reuters ) - The destruction of Brazil 's Amazon rainforest reached its highest level in a decade this year , government data released on Friday showed , driven by illegal logging and the encroachment of agriculture on the jungle . <p> Satellite images for the 12 months through the end of July 2018 showed that 7,900 square kilometers ( 3,050 square miles ) of forest were cleared in the Amazon , equivalent to more than half the territory of Jamaica . He called on the government to increase its policing of the jungle . <p> The states of Par ? and Mato Grosso were the largest contributors to the deforestation increase . Mato Grosso is Brazil 's top grains producer , leading Brazil 's booming soybean output . <p> Brazil 's Climate Observatory , a network of non-governmental organizations , said in a separate statement that the increase was not a surprise . Besides illegal logging , it said Brazil 's growing commodities sector was contributing to forest destruction as farmers sought to expand . <p> Marcio Astrini , from the local office of Greenpeace , said the Brazilian government had not done enough to fight deforestation and recent policy moves such as ones reducing areas under federal protection fueled environmental destruction . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ further during the government of President-elect Jair Bolsonaro , due to take office in January .
