According to the latest Global Burden of Disease ( GBD ) study , a project unprecedented in its scope and funded by the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation , the continual improvements in human health that we have taken for granted at least since the end of the Second World War have , in global terms , halted . <p> It is rare to see an inflection point for a major phenomenon in such stark perspective , but the GBD is clear : the figures for 2017 show plateauing death rates , with some disturbing and surprising trends underlying the data . We ca n't be surprised , for example , that half of all global deaths arise from four key risk factors , some interrelated -- high blood pressure , smoking , high blood glucose and high body mass index ( BMI ) .
