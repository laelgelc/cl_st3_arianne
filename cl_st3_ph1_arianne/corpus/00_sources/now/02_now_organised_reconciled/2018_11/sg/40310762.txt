<p> As Lam Chi-ngai , 76 , sets foot on the remote Sunshine Island , mosquitoes swarm parts of his exposed skin , hungry for a rare taste of human blood . <p> But the ex-resident soldiers on , not bothering to swat at the pests . " I 've been on this island for too long , " he said . " Its plants , its animals and I are one . " <p> Sunshine Island , a mostly unknown land mass in the waters east of Hong Kong 's Lantau Island , is Lam 's spiritual home , with its clean air and untouched environment having healed him in times of illness .
