<p> The world is running out of time to take action and limit the worst impacts of climate change . The IPCC has issued stark warnings that we have to cut carbon pollution by half by 2030 and wean our global economy completely off carbon by 2050 . <p> Meanwhile China 's Belt and Road Initiative ( BRI ) promises to plough USD 1 trillion into energy and infrastructure across over 100 countries . It will play a determining role in shaping the direction of future development , particularly among emerging economies -- and whether we meet our climate goals . <p> Launched by President Xi Jinping in 2013 , BRI encompasses land routes ( the " Belt " ) and maritime routes ( the " Road " ) with the goal of improving connectivity in the region primarily through infrastructure investments .
