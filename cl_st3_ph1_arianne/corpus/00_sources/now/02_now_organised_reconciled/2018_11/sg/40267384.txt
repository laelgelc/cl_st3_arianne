<h> How Changi Airport is inspiring Singapore 's hospitals <p> Imagine that a visit to the doctor could be as smooth as checking in at Singapore 's Changi Airport . <p> Design and technology have helped the airport turn an often-frustrating experience into a painless , efficient and award-winning one . At a new innovation lab in Singapore , we are learning from the approaches used by Changi Airport to re-design patients ' experience at clinics and hospitals . <p> We are only scratching the surface of what is possible -- and we at DXC Technology have launched a lab to innovate , create , and push the envelope . The Digital Innovation Lab at our Asia headquarters in Singapore will be an incubator for new ideas and prototypes in healthcare and beyond . <p> AI for antibiotics <p> The Digital Innovation Lab enables us to co-create with our customers and partners in the region , aided by AI and machine learning , 3D printing , virtual and augmented reality , robotics , and many other emerging technologies . <p> What does this co-creation look like in practice ?
