<h> Black Friday misery for French shops as Macron and protesters lock horns <p> People wearing yellow vests , a symbol of a French drivers ' protest against higher fuel prices , block the motorway in Antibes , France , November 17 , 2018 . REUTERS/Eric Gaillard/Files <p> By Richard Lough and Dominique Vidalon <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - French retailers warned after days of unrest over fuel taxes on Friday that prolonged protests could spoil the Christmas shopping season and threaten jobs , while President Emmanuel Macron showed no sign of backing down . <p> For a week , protesters clad in fluorescent yellow jackets have blocked highways across France with burning barricades and convoys of slow-moving trucks , obstructing access to fuel depots , shopping centres and some factories . <p> The unrest is a dilemma for Macron who casts himself as a champion in the fight against climate change and wants the French to switch to greener energies . He has been derided as out of touch with common folk and is fighting a slump in popularity . <p> Now he faces upsetting businesses with a warning from retailers that the protests are damaging trade @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ revenue fell 35 percent last Saturday , the first day of the so-called " yellow vest " protests , when nearly 300,000 people took part . <p> " We have a real problem as some stores have been closed for a week now because of the blockades , " said Jacques Creyssel , head of the French Retail Federation ( FCD ) . " One week without revenue is dramatic for a company . " <p> The FCD represents retailers including Carrefour , Casino and Fnac Darty , for whom Black Friday , a now annual event imported from the United States on the day when retailers traditionally begin turning a profit , marks the start of the Christmas shopping season . <p> " It 's a crucial time for retailers . If retailers can not work and customers can not shop , it 's bound to have a major impact on employment , " said Creyssel . <p> The protests will enter a second week on Saturday with police braced for confrontations with violent fringe elements though the overall number of protesters has fallen sharply . <p> Tourism is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be closed on Saturday because of the planned protests , its operator said . <p> And the disruption has spread to carmaker PSA Group , which was forced to temporarily halt two production lines on Wednesday at its Sochaux plant , where it assembles Peugeot and Citroen vehicles , because of problems getting supplies delivered . <p> " GENTLE TOUCH " NEEDED <p> The government has sought to calm the uprising by earmarking 500 million euros to double a 2,000 euro bonus granted to motorists on low incomes who change old-model cars for cleaner ones . <p> But it was enough . The " yellow vests " demand the government reverse fuel tax hikes which , combined with higher global oil prices , have led to a roughly 20 percent rise in the pump price of diesel in the last year . Diesel now retails at about 1.50 euros ( $1.70 ) per litre in the Greater Paris region . <p> Macron 's decision to dig in his heels is showing signs of upsetting the young , centrist En Marche party .
