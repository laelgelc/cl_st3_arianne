<h> MOU to tap expertise in waste management , resource recovery <p> Amsterdam has been battling floods since it was founded in the 13th century , and the Netherlands has become a leader in waste management and resource recovery . <p> Singapore will now be able to better tap such expertise under a new agreement inked with the Netherlands yesterday . <p> Both countries will also share knowledge on water solutions and support industry efforts to work together on new water production technologies . <p> A memorandum of understanding on environmental and water cooperation was signed by Environment and Water Resources Minister Masagos Zulkifli and Dutch Minister for Foreign Trade and Development Cooperation Sigrid Kaag . <p> It was witnessed by President Halimah Yacob , who is on a state visit , and King Willem-Alexander of the Netherlands . <p> " In the environmental sector , the Netherlands is a forerunner in growing the circular economy , " said Mr Masagos .
