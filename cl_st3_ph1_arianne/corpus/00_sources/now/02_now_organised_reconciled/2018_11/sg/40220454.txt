<p> Yeo Bee Yin said a study estimated that Malaysia had potential energy savings of up RM46.92 billion from 2016 to 2030. -- Picture by Zuraneeza Zulkifli <p> KUALA LUMPUR , Nov 1 ? A joint study by the government 's Economic Planning Unit and the United Nations Development Programme estimated that Malaysia had potential energy savings of up RM46.92 billion from 2016 to 2030 , said Yeo Bee Yin . <p> During her winding up session on the Midterm Review of the 11th Malaysia Plan ( RMK11 ) , the energy , science , technology , environment and climate change minister said this showed huge potential for the energy efficiency industry . <p> " The study shows that the main obstacle from widespread use of energy efficient practices is the lack of regulatory framework . Therefore the government is in the process to come up with the framework for the Energy Efficiency and Conservation Act ( EECA ) . <p> " The EECA draft will be tabled next year . At the same time the government has also reviewed and improved the National Energy Efficiency Action Plan ( NEEAP ) 2016-2025 .
