<h> Suing the World Bank Group to save its mission <h> Indian farmers and fishermen are suing the World Bank Group over the environmental and health impacts of a coal-fired power plant project . <p> Coal fired power plant along the Chambal River in Rajasthan . Farmers and fishworkers from rural India sued the World Bank Group in Washington DC for funding coal-fired power plants that they said destroyed their livelihoods and negatively impacted their health . Image : Carol Mitchell , CC BY-NC-ND 2.0 <h> Share this story <p> In 2015 , farmers and fishworkers from rural India sued the World Bank Group in Washington , DC , over impacts from a disastrous coal-fired power plant project that they said destroyed their livelihoods and damaged their health . Last week , their lawsuit made it to the US Supreme Court . While the World Bank Group is fighting the lawsuit tooth and nail , losing just might save its mission . <p> The World Bank Group includes five institutions , such as the World Bank itself and the International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) , which makes profitable loans to private corporations . Accountability should help these institutions . <p> The World Bank Group 's mission is twofold : " To end extreme poverty and promote shared prosperity in a sustainable way . Some of them were identified by the IFC before the plant was built . <p> The plant has been cited multiple times by local pollution control authorities , and violates many industry standards . <p> And it is not even providing the promised benefits . <p> Due to a glut of electricity and higher-than-expected operating costs , the plant is functioning only at one-fifth of its capacity , and still losing money .
