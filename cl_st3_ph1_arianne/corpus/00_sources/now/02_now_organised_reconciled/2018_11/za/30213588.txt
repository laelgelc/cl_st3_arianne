The reality is that coal extraction and burning has devastating impacts on the people living in the area . This satellite data now confirms that there is nowhere to hide : Eskom 's coal addiction in Mpumalanga means that millions of people living in Johannesburg and Pretoria are also impacted by the pollution from coal " continued Steele . <p> " Air pollution is a global health crisis , with up to 95% of the world 's population breathing unsafe air .
