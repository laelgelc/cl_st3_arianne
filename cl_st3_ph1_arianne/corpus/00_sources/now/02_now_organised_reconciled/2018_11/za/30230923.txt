Sharply falling battery costs is a key driver of the boom . BNEF sees the capital cost of a utility-scale lithium-ion storage system falling another 52% by 2030 . <p> But cost is n't the only factor . Governments from China to California are spurring demand , as is the rise of electric vehicles and solar power .
