<h> Revised Carbon Tax Bill introduced to Parliament <p> Last week the Minister of Finance introduced the revised Carbon Tax Bill ( Bill ) to Parliament . The Bill takes into account feedback received during the Parliamentary hearings on the December 2017 draft Bill that took place earlier this year . <p> The Bill aims to give effect to South Africa 's objectives and commitments to reduce greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions under the National Climate Change Response Policy and the Paris Agreement , respectively . <h> Carbon Tax -- take home points <p> after more than eight years in the making , the carbon tax is expected to take effect on 1 June 2019 ; <p> the carbon tax will be implemented in a phased approach with the first phase running until 31 December 2022 and the second phase from 2023 to 2030 .
