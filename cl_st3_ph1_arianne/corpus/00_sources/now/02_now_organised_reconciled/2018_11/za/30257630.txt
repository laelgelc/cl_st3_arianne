<h> City to sign R1.3bn wastewater loan deal with German bank <p> Cape Town -- The City of Cape Town will next week sign a loan agreement with Germany 's KfW Development Bank for urban wastewater management which will be payable at a favourable rate over 15 years . <p> The loan is in line with the City 's focus on the upgrading of urban wastewater management facilities and the diversification of Cape Town 's future water mix , and is an integral part of German-South African co-operation to mitigate the effects of climate change and contribute to water resilience . <p> The signing will coincide with a state visit next week by German Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier to South Africa . <p> Steinmeier will hold talks with President Cyril Ramaphosa in Cape Town after an economic round-table discussion with Minister of Trade and Industry Rob Davies and German business representatives . <p> It was reported that the City wanted to borrow R4 billion over the next three years to fund its capital projects . <p> One of the loans the City reportedly wanted approved was an 80 million ( R1.3 billion ) loan from the German government-owned KfW @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the City said it had chosen to obtain funding from KfW Development Bank after it was approached by this institution and a funding offer was submitted to the City at a highly subsidised interest rate . <p> " The City then advertised a Request for Funding Proposal to compare this offer to the market . <p> " Responses were received from both local and international banks and KfW proved to have the best rate compared to the proposals received , being 2% lower than the local market rates . <p> ' ' The rand amount of the unsecured loan would be fixed upfront and repaid over the term of the loan , which is 15 years , " read the statement .
