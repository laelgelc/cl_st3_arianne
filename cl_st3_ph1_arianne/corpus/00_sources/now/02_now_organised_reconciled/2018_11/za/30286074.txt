<h> Beware : You will be fined for illegal dumping , says City <p> Durban - DUMP your rubbish illegally or litter and you could face a spot fine of up to R5000 , the eThekwini Municipality has warned . <p> Illegal dumping continues to plague Durban , with rotting waste becoming a breeding ground for rats and posing a health risk . <p> eThekwini Municipality spokesperson Msawakhe Mayisela said those found guilty of illegal dumping could face a R5000 fine . <p> He said the city provided a weekly refuse removal service to 945000 households , in formal and informal areas , and there was no excuse for illegal dumping . <p> Mayisela added that there were a number of landfills where rubbish could be diverted and that they have implemented nine new by-laws aimed at dealing with the various offences . <p> " Among these by-laws is the Nuisances and Behaviour in Public Places By-law , under which littering is also prohibited . Enforcement is already under way and police have started taking a tough stance against offenders . Metro Police have written summonses and issued fines to those who contravene the by-laws .
