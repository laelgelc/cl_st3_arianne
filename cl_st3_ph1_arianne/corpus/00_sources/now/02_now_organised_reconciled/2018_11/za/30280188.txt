Now , as the United Nations ' global collective of climate scientists issues its most strident warning yet -- that we have 12 years in which to bring planetary carbon emissions in check or face run-away climate breakdown -- it seems the world might finally be waking up to the fact that nature has been sending out warning flares for decades . The planet is , in fact , burning .
