<p> South Africa , like the rest of world , can not and does not deny the negative effects of climate change . The Climate Change Bill , published on June 18 this year , is meant to be an effective and progressive response to it . <p> The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change was established in 1988 , which led to the tabling of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change in 1992 . The convention 's objective was to achieve the stabilisation of greenhouse gases at a level that would prevent dangerous anthropogenic interference with the climate system . <p> This treaty did not contain provisions for targets or specific ways for dealing with climate change , but instead gave principles and commitments to guide future engagements . <p> The Kyoto Protocol , which sets internationally binding emission reduction targets , was adopted in December 1997 and acceded to by South Africa in July 2002 . The protocol recognises that developed countries are principally responsible for greenhouse gas emissions as a consequence of decades of industrial activity . Accordingly , it places a heavier burden on developed nations . <p> The Paris Agreement , which addresses @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , to set emission targets and to commit themselves to not exceeding those emissions , was adopted in December 2015 and ratified by South Africa in November 2016 . <p> This agreement gives developing countries the flexibility to develop and be in control of setting their own targets . <p> South Africa 's Climate Change Bill recognises that any policy must be integrated with economic development , which will be done in an environmentally and socially conscious manner . <p> The Bill is divided into four broad categories : <p> Policy alignment for climate change mitigation by government institutions ; <p> Responses by provinces and municipalities ; <p> Strategic national adaptation strategy ; and <p> The plan for governance of greenhouse emissions . <p> The Bill obliges the minister to set out a greenhouse gas emissions trajectory for South Africa and to provide a national emissions reduction target . This is binding on organs of state and private persons as representatives of legal entities .
