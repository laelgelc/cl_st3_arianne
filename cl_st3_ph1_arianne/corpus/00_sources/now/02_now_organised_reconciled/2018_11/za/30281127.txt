<h> Investigation reveals shocking facts on declining animal population because of humans <p> 22 November 2018 - 07:17 By Odwa Mjo <p> A newborn gorilla rests in her mother 's arms in his cage at the ZooParc of Beauval in Saint-Aignan , Central France , on October 29 2018 . Image : GUILLAUME SOUVANT / AFP <p> Climate change , deforestation and illegal trade have added to the significant decline in wildlife . <p> According to the World Wildlife Fund , 60% of the animal population in the last 40 years have been wiped out by human activity . <p> A Greenpeace International investigation found that palm oil used by American food and beverage company Mondelez has resulted in massive deforestation , destroying the habitat of the endangered orangutan in Indonesia . <p> An orangutan is part of the ape species , centred mainly in Malaysia and Indonesia . According to the WWF , the orangutan clan has declined significantly in the last century . From 230,000 overall , there are only an estimated 112,200 Bornean and Sumatran orangutan left . <p> Sumatran orangutan are severely endangered , with only 7,500 left .
