<h> In a sign of the times , ' toxic ' is named word of the year <p> Used in the English language since the 17th century , the medieval term was borrowed from the Latin word toxicum meaning " poison " . The Latin word was borrowed from the Greek toxikon pharmakon , a lethal poison used by ancient Greeks . <p> The word of the year is a word or expression that reflects the mood and ethos of the times . The editors say toxic became a dominant descriptor for the biggest topics in 2018 . There was a 45% rise in the number of times the word was searched on oxforddictionaries.com in the past year . <p> All these words are a reflection of some of the biggest topics in 2018 including climate change and patriarchy . <p> According to Oxford , " toxic masculinity " was used in relation to the #MeTooMovement and related conversations around gender-based issues .
