<h> CoCT signs R1.3bn wastewater management loan deal with German bank <p> Cape Town -- The City of Cape Town on Tuesday signed an urban wastewater management loan to the value of about R1.3 billion with the German government-owned KfW Development Bank . <p> The loan , which was offered at a highly subsidised fixed interest rate of 8.107% , will be used to finance the expansion and refurbishment of some of the city 's 25 wastewater treatment plants over the next few years , the City said in a statement . <p> This unsecured loan is payable over 15 years to spread the related costs over the lifetime of the wastewater projects , to limit any undue burden on the ratepayer . All costs associated with taking up the loan have been considered in terms of affordability in advance . <p> " Cape Town is a growing city and it is important that we are able to sufficiently invest in infrastructure , the diversification of resources and the reliable provision of water services over the coming years as we build our resilience . <p> " We thank KfW for approaching the city with this offer and for their @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " The taking up of debt is standard practice for any major city administration and all cost implications have been considered in terms of affordability in advance . <p> " This loan is in line with our commitment of becoming a more sustainable city and to adapt better to climate change challenges . It also forms part of the foundation of building a thriving , water-sensitive city , ' said Executive Mayor Dan Plato . <p> " The city 's debt level is at an acceptable rate and the city will continue to follow a conservative approach to loan funding while balancing what investment is required for the metro going forward .
