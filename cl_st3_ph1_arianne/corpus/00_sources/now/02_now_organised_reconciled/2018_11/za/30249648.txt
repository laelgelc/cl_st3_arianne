<h> Kenya targets 100 per cent transition to green energy by 2020 as it scales up investment in renewable energy to address the climate change challenge , President Uhuru Kenyatta has said . <p> The investments to reach this figure have been made simultaneously with Kenya , almost tripling the size of its population served by the electricity grid to more than 60 per cent of the population . <p> " Our target is to attain 100 per cent green energy sufficiency by 2020 . We will do this while we achieve 100 per cent access to power for our population , and sharply lower costs to industry to aid our manufacturing push , " he added . <p> The Kenyan leader spoke during a roundtable discussion on " Do n't drop climate efforts " session of the Paris Peace Forum in France . <p> According to a statement issued in Nairobi on Monday , Kenya 's investment in green energy is proof that doing the right thing is more than a cost , but rather a chance to offer immense opportunities for business growth and enhancement of citizens ' quality of life . Kenyatta , the statement continued @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , where sustainability has been embraced , to support such investments in green energy in Africa . <p> Kenyatta observed that although African countries were nowhere close to the top producers of greenhouse emissions , they are nevertheless champions of sustainability because they know the painful price of inaction in the face of the climate change reality . <p> " Africans are living this as a reality , and scientific evidence shows that Africa may continue to bear the greatest brunt of climate change , " he concluded .
