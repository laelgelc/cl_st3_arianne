<h> Future of water mobilises international specialists <p> Water specialists from around the world are expected to gather in Rio de Janeiro this month to discuss the future of water on the planet at Rio Water Week 2018 . <p> This key event on the water calendar will see around 1 500 specialist from country 's such as the United States of America , France , Spain , South Africa , Chile and Brazil , gather from the 26th to the 28th of November to participate in lectures focused on ensuring access to water and sanitation for all by 2030 . <p> The conference will focus on topics such as governance and planning , efficient management , education and training , financing , and climate change .
