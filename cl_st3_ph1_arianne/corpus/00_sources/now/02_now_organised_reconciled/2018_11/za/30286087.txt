<h> UCT contributes to report on G20 countries ' climate action <p> Tutuka Power Station is a coal-fired power station located near Standerton in Mpumalanga province , South Africa . <p> The University of Cape Town 's Energy Research Centre ( ERC ) is concerned that South Africa 's electricity has been declared the dirtiest of the G20 countries ' , as revealed by the fourth annual Brown to Green Report released last week . <p> The report found that South Africa has the highest intensity of emissions in the power sector , with emissions per capita roughly double the G20 average . And this has been increasing in recent years because of South Africa 's dependency on coal and expansive new coal-generation projects , the report said . While renewable energy has been growing rapidly , the share of this type of electricity is still low . <p> Subsidising unsustainable energy <p> Co-author of the South African report , Bryce McCall from the ERC , expressed concern about the intensity of domestic industry emissions , which remain some of the highest among reporting G20 countries . <p> " South Africa has above G20-average expenditure on fossil fuel subsidies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as fifth behind Saudi Arabia , Italy , Australia and Brazil .
