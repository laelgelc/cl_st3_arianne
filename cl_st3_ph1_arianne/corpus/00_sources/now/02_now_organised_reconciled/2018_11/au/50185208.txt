<p> What arrogance on the part of the Morrison government to immediately dismiss the latest findings of the world 's climate scientists , and the recent Intergovernmental Panel on Climate change report , as being irrelevant to Australia . Have these politicians lifted their heads from the sand lately ? Are they unaware of the staggering economic costs associated with the escalating number and frequency of natural disasters across the world , and from which Australia is far from immune ? A significant part of the country is in serious drought , with no end in sight . Bushfires are now an ever-present threat and if fires are raging in one part of the country , another part faces floods .
