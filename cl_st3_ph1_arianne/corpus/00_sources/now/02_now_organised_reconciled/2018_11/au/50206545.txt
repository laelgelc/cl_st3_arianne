<p> Victoria is pressing ahead with full steam to try and bring more Tesla batteries into the state as it capitalizes on a movement to store cleaner energy and combat the effects of climate change . <p> This news comes just a short time after US tech giant Tesla confirmed the arrival of its first big battery in Victoria . The company unveiled it in Ballarat , well ahead of schedule . The next battery is set to debut at the Gannawarra Solar Farm , and it will provide 25MW of storage capacity . <p> The developer for this project , Wirsol Energy , hails from Germany . Each of its current projects is ticking the right boxes , and the company has met all its needs for commissioning the new big battery at the Gannawarra location . Its other site , the Wemen Solar Farm , already connects to the grid and is producing 110MW of energy . <p> The two big batteries will massively help Victoria deal with potential overloads from people using energy at peak times , which tend to be when the average Australian returns home from work . Without them , many costly inefficiencies could occur . <p> One issue involving energy waste is when a load of solar power feeds into the grid and ships far away before returning to its original location . Big batteries will be able to alleviate this problem . <p> Given that these big batteries are the first of their kind to arrive in Victoria , Wirsol said that it had to sort out some specific new regulations , but after meeting all the qualifications , it now has the green light to get the process up and running . <p> Wirsol 's Managing Director , Mark Hogan , said that the company is " delighted to reach two crucial milestones at Gannawarra Solar Farm " and that this is a great moment for renewable energy processes and battery storage in general .
