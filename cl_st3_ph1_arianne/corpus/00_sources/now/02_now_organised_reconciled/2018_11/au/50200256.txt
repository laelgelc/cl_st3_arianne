<h> A bad risk for sharks : Wary locals knew better than to take a dip in Cid Harbour <p> Locals have always known that Cid Harbour -- a heavily forested bay , rather than a township -- was a bad risk for swimming . <p> It 's listed as such in the 100 Magic Miles , the Whitsundays sailing almanac . That 's according to Tom Pugh who works in operations with Whitsunday Escape , a bareboat charter company . <p> However , Mr Pugh said it was " after recent events " that the company had put the following notice on its website : " There is no swimming at Cid Harbour at any time . " <p> Those " recent events " are three shark attacks in close succession that killed a Melbourne medical researcher , took the leg of a 12-year-old Victorian girl and left a Tasmanian woman lucky to be alive . The attacks on the woman and the girl occurred within hours of each other in September .
