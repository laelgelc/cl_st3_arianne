<h> Modern Guru : Should we change the words of our anthem ? <h> By Danny Katz <p> 10 November 2018 -- 12:00am <p> Q : Regarding recent news about the national anthem , I think the music is " anthemy " enough , but we need new lyrics . Your thoughts ? P.C. , BANNOCKBURN , VIC <p> A : By recent news , I guess you 're referring to Harper Nielsen , the nine-year-old Brisbane schoolgirl who refused to sing Advance Australia Fair at school assembly because " it completely disregards the Indigenous Australians who were here before us " -- which is pretty impressive thinking for a kid . At nine , I was still learning my farmyard animal sounds , and really struggling to do a goat : it just seemed too close to a sheep for any clear differentiation . Kids laughed at me . <p> Illustration : JohnShakespeare . <p> I 'm no fan of the anthem , either . <p> The music is trite and every time I hear the refrain " advance Australia fair " , it sounds like " Go forward White Australia with your appalling history of racism , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more succinctly . <p> Advertisement <p> Inspired by Harper Nielsen , I think we should look to the children for a solution , and sing the alternative anthem-lyrics regularly sung in school assemblies across the country . <p> Lyrics like these : " Australians all are ostriches " ( a scathing indictment of our apathy towards climate change and Indigenous issues ) . " Four minus one is three " ( an impassioned reminder of the importance of education in our nation 's prosperity ) . " With golden soil and wealth for toys " ( because the children are the future and deserve lots of presents ) . " Our home is dirt by sea " ( just a geographical fact : they ca n't all be beautiful beaches ) . " Our land abounds in nature strips , of beauty rich and rare " ( dazzling imagery , perfectly describing our vast suburban wastelands ) . " In history 's page , at every stage , advanced Australians care ! " ( a bit forced and hokey , but better than what we were singing before ) .
