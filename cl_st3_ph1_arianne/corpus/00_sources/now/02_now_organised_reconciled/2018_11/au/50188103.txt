Over the past 35 years , China has pulled the largest number of people out of poverty in history and , with 39% , has made the largest contribution to global growth since 2008 . It is now the world 's leader in manufacturing , exports , energy use , and urbanisation . With a growing middle class and national resources , it is only natural that China 's international influence should also have increased . <p> Today , China shapes almost every global issue of note , from climate change to trade , and from regional security in Southeast Asia and the Indian Ocean to investment trends in Africa and Central Europe . <p> Concerns persist despite Beijing adopting a less confrontational approach over the past few months towards Southeast Asia , Japan , and India . <p> Why , then , does China 's rise generate so much anxiety ? This is problematic because China is no longer an isolated and inward-looking country , but a state whose decisions have direct implications for the rest of the world .
