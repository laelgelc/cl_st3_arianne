<h> Actions to Ensure the Protection of Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site <h> Backgrounder <p> In July 2015 , the World Heritage Committee issued a decision requesting that Canada invite a World Heritage Centre ( WHC ) /International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) Reactive Monitoring Mission to Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site to evaluate its state of conservation . The Committee also requested that Canada undertake a Strategic Environmental Assessment ( SEA ) to assess the potential cumulative impacts of all developments on the Outstanding Universal Value of the property , including hydroelectric dams , oil sands development , and mining . <p> Canada welcomed the Reactive Monitoring Mission and worked closely with the WHC , the IUCN , and Indigenous partners to plan the visit . The mission occurred between September 26 and October 4 , 2016 in which representatives of the WHC and the IUCN met with federal , provincial and territorial governments , Indigenous communities , industry , and environmental non-government organizations . <p> The World Heritage Committee adopted a decision at the July 2017 Committee meeting requesting that Canada submit to the World Heritage Centre , by February @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with its implementation of the 2016 Reactive Monitoring Mission recommendations , and to submit by December 1 , 2018 , an updated report on the state of conservation of the property and an action plan to address the challenges to Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site . Canada has received an extension for the final action plan which will be submitted to the World Heritage Centre by February 1 , 2019 . The material will be considered by the Committee at its 43rd session in 2019 . <p> Working with partners and stakeholders , including Indigenous peoples , the Government of Canada is taking action to respond to the World Heritage Committee 's recommendations . We are confident that through this collaboration we can create a path forward and secure the future of Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site , so that it remains a treasured place with Outstanding Universal Value for generations to come . <p> * In August 2017 , the SEA Draft Scoping Report was shared with other federal departments , provincial and territorial governments , Indigenous partners , industry groups , and conservation organizations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> * In the spring of 2018 , The SEA for Wood Buffalo National Park World Heritage Site was completed in fulfilment of the World Heritage Committee request to undertake such an assessment .
