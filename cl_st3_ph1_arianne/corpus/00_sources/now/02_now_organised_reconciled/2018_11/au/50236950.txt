Trump 's 1980s are over . Bilateral responses to multilateral problems conducted via megaphone diplomacy outside the forum will -- at best -- entrench APEC-style deadlocks . At worst , they will legitimise fake solutions that risk permanently damaging the global system . <p> Trade is a clear example . The United States trade deficit can not be " solved " bilaterally with China . A country that invests more than it is saves ( such as the US ) imports foreign capital from other countries ( such as China ) to finance investment . This inflow of capital appreciates the US dollar , which reduces exports and increases imports , resulting in a trade deficit . <p> The US trade deficit simply reflects its capital inflows from countries such as China .
