<p> WITH his call for the abolition of state governments ( Letters , 7/11 ) Eric Roach presents an excellent case for a civics program in schools . The federal government exists at the pleasure of the sovereign states , not the other way around . In the past few decades , state governments have proven the least intrusive when it comes to the lives of private citizens . Compare and contrast with federal government and local councils , who seem to have little to do but dream up new ways of meddling in things that are none of their business . Bloated and inefficient local councils should be the first in the cross-hairs .
