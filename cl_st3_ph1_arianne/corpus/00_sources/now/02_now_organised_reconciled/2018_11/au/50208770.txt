The ever generous Prince Charles and his wife , the Duchess of Cornwall , Camilla , have kindly opened their British palace to members of the public ( read , commoners ) in honour of the Prince 's 70th birthday . <p> Teaming up with Google , the heir apparent has shared a glimpse inside Clarence House , his official London residence , through a series of virtual online tours . Featuring everything from the couple 's library to their dining room , the tour looks inside a number of royally decked out rooms , allowing us that are n't Meghan Markle or Kate Middleton to take a peek inside one of England 's most famous homes . <p> Prince Charles has lived in the home since 2003 , when he took over from the late Queen Mother who occupied the house for a huge 50 years @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ honour of his grandmother . Grand and expertly cultivated , the gardens are a favourite for the Prince , who famously loves all things nature -- and is a big advocate of climate change . So big , in fact , that he requested Aston Martin to engineer his car to run off white wine rather than traditional gasoline . Talk about committed to the cause ! <p> Above : The Morning Room <p> The Prince also made a host of structural interior changes to the house , including the removal of the entrance way . " The original front door and entrance hall have been replaced by The Library ( between The Morning Room and The Dining Room ) and there was a conservatory where The Entrance Hall now stands , " senior curator of decorative arts for Royal Collection Trust , Kathryn Jones says . <p> Inside , the home has been curated to reflect both Charles and Camilla 's personalities .
