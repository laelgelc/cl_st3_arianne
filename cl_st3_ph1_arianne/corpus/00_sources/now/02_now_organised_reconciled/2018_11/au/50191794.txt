<h> Robyn Dowling appointed head of architecture at University of Sydney <p> Robyn Dowling , appointed as head of school and dean of the University of Sydney 's School of Architecture , Design and Planning . Image : University of Sydney <p> Australia 's oldest university , the University of Sydney , has appointed urban geographer and planner Robyn Dowling as head of school and dean of its School of Architecture , Design and Planning -- the first woman to fill the role . <p> Dowling has been at the university since 2016 , when she joined the school as an associate dean .
