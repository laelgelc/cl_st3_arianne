In my nearly 14 years of financial journalism , never have I encountered a more vociferous backlash than the occasions on which I 've had the audacity to point out that mortgage brokers -- the supposed best friend of Aussie borrowers -- face a conflict of interest in the advice they provide borrowers , owing to the way they are paid : not by borrowers , but lenders . <p> And I 've written about climate change . <p> For many decades now , Aussie home borrowers have been dining out on the apparent " free lunch " that is mortgage broking services . <p> Brokers face a shake-up.Credit:Dominic Lorrimer <p> Overwhelmed by the choice of loans on offer and bamboozled by the paperwork required to make what is often the biggest financial choice of their lives , Australians have lent heavily on the mortgage broking industry to cut through the complexity , hold our hands and select for us the best loan . <p> Advertisement <p> Except , in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that .
