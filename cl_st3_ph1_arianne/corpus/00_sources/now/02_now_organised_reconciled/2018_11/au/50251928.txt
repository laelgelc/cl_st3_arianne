<h> How a student locked-out of their own campus became a national convenor <p> On university campuses across Australia , big personalities vie for political control , enabled by factions of their supporters . It 's easy for things to go wrong : for lobbying to descend into manipulation , for assertiveness to become intimidation . It happens at the University of Sydney . And it 's only magnified when you reach the national level . <p> At USyd , students are removed from the national political movements that determine the direction of the Australia 's student union . Our university is so distracted by its local stupol scene that it 's quite easy for Sydney students to forget a wider world exists past Parramatta Road or King Street . <p> For students at smaller campuses , or with student unions hamstrung by university management , national representation is much more important . It 's a chance to have your voice heard , and influence politics on a wider scale . At this year 's EdCon , they came together with the aim of forming a more powerful voting bloc , electing Ethan Taylor as one of their national convenors .
