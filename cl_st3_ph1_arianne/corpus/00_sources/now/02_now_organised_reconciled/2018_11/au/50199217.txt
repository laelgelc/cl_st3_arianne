<h> Festival of Open Minds Podcast -- Jo Dodds , from the front line of climate and fire <p> Vimy Ridge resident and Bega Valley Shire Councilor , Jo Dodds strongly believes climate change is at play in her community . She points to the bushfires of March 18 that ripped through her community at Tathra . <p> Sixty-five homes were lost on that day , Jo 's home was very lucky not to be one of them . <p> Mr McKibben invited Jo to be a speaker on his ' Fossil Free Acceleration Tour ' , which took place in Sydney , Melbourne , Canberra , Adelaide , and Newcastle in April and May . <p> Jo called on leaders to act now to reduce the risks of further climate change , advocating as she does for regional communities . <p> When Jo spoke at The Festival of Open Minds on September 1 , the vast Yankees Gap Fire was burning in the hills west of Bega .
