Governors , leaders across the divide and common mwanachi might have ideas on how to utilize water development but they do n't have a platform , " said Kimemia during the proceedings at the Kenyatta International Conference Centre . <p> The conference which officially begun on Monday and ended yesterday sought to promote sustainable use of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ resources for sustainable economic development . <p> Kimemia , a former high ranking state officer in government called upon the national government to either form a commission or an intergovernmental body to look into and fast-track the post conference agreements . <p> Earlier on , Kimemia had expressed concerns at the biasness centred on the oceans , seas during the meet . He said emphasis should be to all water sources including lakes , dams and even smallest of rivers that join to form the blue economy . <p> The governor proudly showcased his County indicating the potential of the blue economy for Nyandarua and for its neighbouring environments .
