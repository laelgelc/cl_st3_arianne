" <p> He added that " we partner with rural agro products stores , we transform to look fresh , we give them the tools they need to empower their local farmers , empower them to access quality inputs through farmers pride and so on . " <p> How do they disseminate information to farmers ? <p> Munguti says they leverage on the massive mobile penetration in Kenya to send climate information to farmers through a Short Message Service ( SMS ) . <p> " Our platform is unique , we call it last mile franchise stores and mobile technology , " he asserted . <p> Their stores , he said have shop assistances who advises farmers on the right seeds to choose and other farm products . <p> The mobile platform is called " Daktari Pap " that avails weekly climate information via an SMS to farmers . <p> Such information , he said is " interpreted and cascaded to a language that locals ' farmers can consume .
