Exclusive Economic Zone ( EEZ ) , which stretches 200 nautical miles . <p> Fishing vessels <p> Speaking at the ongoing Blue Economy Conference , the CS said within the Indian Ocean fisheries 4.35 million metric tonnes annually , 25 per cent is fished off Kenya 's coast . <p> Kiunjuri added that the Government was also promoting the private sector to develop domestic fishing fleet aimed at
