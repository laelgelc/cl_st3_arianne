This strategy is under the Africa Union 's Agenda 2063 policy which seeks to convert Africa into a world class powerhouse with a $1.5 trillion contribution from the blue economy . <p> Kenya is among the ten countries that form the Western Indian Ocean ( blue ) economy besides Tanzania , Somalia , Madagascar , Mauritius , Mozambique , Seychelles , South Africa , Comoros and France . <p> It is thus imperative that it assesses the benefits and challenges we are likely to experience as we the first blue economy conference closes . <p> A clean and safe coastline is an undeniable step towards a flourishing blue economy .
