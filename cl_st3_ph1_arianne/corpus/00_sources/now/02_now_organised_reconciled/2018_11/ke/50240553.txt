<h> Kenya launches joint global push to save water masses <p> Final preparations ahead of the Sustainable Blue Economy Conference at the KICC in Nairobi . PHOTO FRANCIS NDERITU NMG <p> Kenya has launched a global call to decisive action by governments to safeguard water masses for economic gain . <p> Speaking at the inaugural Sustainable Blue Economy Blue Economy will be formed to oversee conservation and sustainable exploitation of marine resources . <p> " Kenya banned use of one-off plastics and we have embarked on reducing release of toxic wastes to our lakes and oceans , " he said . <p> Like-minded countries <p> He said conservation efforts will only bear fruits if Kenya co-operated with like-minded countries . <p> Mr Kenyatta said the
