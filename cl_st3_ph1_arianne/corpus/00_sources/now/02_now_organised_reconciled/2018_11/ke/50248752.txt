In partnership with the SIDS , it will develop and implement national Maritime Economy Plans to ensure the programme leaves a lasting legacy . <p> At the Commonwealth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ agreed a landmark Commonwealth Blue Charter which sets out how member states will lead international efforts to sustainably develop and protect our ocean . The CME Programme is an integral part of the UK 's effort to protect the health of the world 's oceans and promote the growth of blue economies . <p> The programme supports CME SIDS by building the capacity of the islands to manage their marine resources and develop their national maritime economies . It is also helping to address climate change , including the UN Sustainable Development Goal 14 ( to conserve and sustainably use the oceans , seas and marine resources for sustainable development ) and the Paris Climate Change Accord . <p> The CME Programme ensures that activities and initiatives across nations and regions are harmonised to avoid overlaps , and recognise synergies .
