<h> Holness off on three-nation working trip <p> Prime Minister Andrew Holness left the island on Sunday for an extended working visit to three countries , the Office of the Prime Minister ( OPM ) has announced . <p> The prime minister 's first stop is the United States capital of Washington , DC , then on to Buenos Aires , Argentina , before travelling to Port of Spain , Trinidad and Tobago . <p> While in Washington , Holness will participate in the International Monetary Fund 's ( IMF ) Conference on Building Resilience to Natural Disasters from November 26 to 28 . He will give a keynote address on Monday on the topic , ' Building Resilience to Disasters and Climate Change in the Caribbean . ' <p> The Washington stop will also see the prime minister engaging in a series of bilateral discussions , including meeting with Managing Director of the IMF , Christine Lagarde , as well as key officials of other international multilateral agencies .
