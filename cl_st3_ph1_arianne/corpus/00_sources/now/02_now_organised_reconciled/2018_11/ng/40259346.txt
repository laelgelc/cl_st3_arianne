<h> 9mobile offers weather forecast service to farmers <p> 9mobile , in partnership with weather forecasting portal , Ignitia , has improved the lives of farmers and event planners , a statement on the impact of the technology said . <p> The statement added that the Iska Weather Service could predict the weather with 84 per cent forecasts accuracy by empowering farmers to militate against adverse weather conditions that could impact negatively on their crops . <p> Speaking about the innovative service , the Head of Digital Media , 9mobile , Bolanle Afuye , said that it had started receiving commendations from event planners and traders , and other people whose means of livelihood or activities could be adversely impacted by poor weather . <p> Afuye stated , " Climate change is a serious environmental condition that affects all of us whether as humans , plants or animals . Weather being a major issue impacts on farming and food security . Thus , farmers are so concerned about the weather , especially considering that too much rain can cause the death of plants or infect plants with disease . " <p> Copyright PUNCH .
