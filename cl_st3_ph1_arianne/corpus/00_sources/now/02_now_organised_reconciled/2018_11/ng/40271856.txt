<p> The Director-General , Nigerian Meteorological Agency , Prof. Sani Mashi , has urged Nigerian universities to run degree programme in meteorology to support ongoing campaign on combating negative impacts of climate change . Mashi made the call on Thursday in Abuja while signing a Memorandum of Understanding with Umaru Musa Yar'adua University Katsina , on the development of meteorological infrastructure in the university . The News Agency of Nigeria reports that the Vice-Chancellor of the university , Prof. Idris Funtua , signed the MoU on behalf of the university . Mashi said that NiMet as an agency had a training centre , but had no mandate to run degree programmes . He said the Nigerian universities could play a big role in encouraging students to study meteorology as a discipline . He said the collaboration would be mutually beneficial , adding that the university already had a structure that would enable NiMet to achieve its objectives . According to him , NiMet will deploy resources to help the universities in upgrading infrastructure in the programme . He said : " At least on the long run , the university will be in position to introduce a degree programme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we can not run degree programme because we do n't have mandate to award degrees . " Your university currently does not have a programme in meteorology but we believe that it is a programme that will help in the fight against climate change . " NiMet will give the universities the necessary support to run the programme in terms of training and resources . " We are going to help you in getting your meteorological infrastructure get to WMO standards . " We are also going to support your academic department by making contributions towards introduction and development of meteorology as a course . " He added that NiMet decided to collaborate with educational institutions to stimulate development process across all the sectors of the economy . He said NiMet needed to partner strongly with the universities if it was to achieve its mandate . He said : " We believe we can help the universities upgrade their infrastructure so that we can enhance their capacity to delivery their services to their students . He gave assurance that UMYU had the requisite infrastructure with a well-laid programme for a conventional university . He said : " It really gladdens our hearts from the briefing of the Director-General has actually touched all the three core areas of our mandate . " I can assure you that you will find Umaru Musa Yar'Adua University as a ready partner in this project and whatever requirement that is needed we are ready to oblige . "
