<h> Think outside the box , ex-Ghana President charges graduands <p> Immediate past President of Ghana , John Dramani Mahama , weekend , urged Nigeria and Ghana to leverage on their human and material resources to propel the subregion to greater economic prosperity and dignity . <p> Mahama made the appeal at the 16th Convocation of the Igbinedion University , Okada , Ovia North East Local Government Area of Edo . <p> He said , " Ghana and Nigeria being the two biggest economies in the West African sub-region , place certain responsibilities on us to become the engine room that propel our sub region to greater prosperity and dignity for our people . " <p> In bid to proffer solutions out of the economic recession and climate change , the ex president of Ghana said the various institutions of learning must be able to produce graduates that are creative and also able to think out of the box , in order to make the society better for all . <p> " Institutions of higher learning have a significant role to play in the transformation of our countries , first , by producing the required human resources , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of our people . <p> " Our societies need innovative and creative graduands with questioning minds , independent of thoughts and creative thinking , to be able to think outside the box and proffer solutions that can help in the transformation of our countries . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
