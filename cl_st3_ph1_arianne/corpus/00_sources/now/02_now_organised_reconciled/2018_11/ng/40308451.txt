<h> CLO cautions against withdrawal of police from Akwa Ibom Assembly <p> A human rights group , the Civil Liberties Organisation ( CLO ) , has cautioned against the withdrawal of police from the Akwa Ibom House of Assembly premises , saying such moves could leave room for an attack by hoodlums . <p> The police on Friday vacated the assembly after barricading the road leading to the complex since Wednesday morning . <p> Some angry youth , apparently members of the All Progressives Congress ( APC ) , who came in several buses , were seen hanging out on the road leading to the assembly complex . <p> The youth later in the afternoon went on a peaceful protest along a major road , Nsikak Edouk Avenue , in Uyo . <p> The Senate on Thursday had passed a resolution , asking the police to vacate the assembly which was rocked by a violent crisis on Monday following the sacking of five lawmakers who defected from the ruling Peoples Democratic Party ( PDP ) to the opposition APC . <p> The sacked
