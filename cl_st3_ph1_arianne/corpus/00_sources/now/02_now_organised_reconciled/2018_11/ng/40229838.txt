<h> Increase Budgets For Environment Management , Says UN <p> The UN Environment on Friday called on African governments to increase budgetary allocations for the management of environment . <p> David Ombisi , Head of African Ministers of Environment secretariat ( AMCEN ) , at the UN Environment , said an allocation of one per cent annually is not enough to help tackle environmental problems . <p> " More than 23 per cent of premature deaths in the continent are attributable to environmental causes , " Ombisi told journalists in Nairobi . <p> Ombisi noted that Africa 's figure is the highest for any region in the world . <p> He said that less allocation of money to the environmental sector is to blame for the spread and outbreak of a number of diseases in the continent . <p> Ombisi noted that AMCEN saw the need to incorporate ministries of health , finance and planning to help discuss the funding aspect of environmental programmes . <p> " The Inter-Ministerial Conference on Health and Environment has since 2010 been discussing how to enhance investment to address challenges posed by environment , " Ombisi revealed . <p> The UN official observed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ relating to
