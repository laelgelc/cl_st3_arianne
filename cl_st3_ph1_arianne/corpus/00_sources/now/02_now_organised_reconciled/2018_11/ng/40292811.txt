<p> Maiduguri -- The Centre for Democracy and Development ( CDD ) on Tuesday organized a town hall meeting to sensitise communities ravaged by Boko Haram insurgency to forgiveness , reconciliation and reintegration of repentant insurgents into the society . <p> CDD 's Coordinator , Malam Musa Zarami , said the exercise was part of a comprehensive awareness creation campaign to encourage dialogue in communities to facilitate reconciliation and peace restoration at the grass roots . <p> Zarami explained that the exercise also focused at educating the affected communities and victims of insurgency , to forgive the insurgents and accept them into their folds to achieve sustainable peace in the northeast . <p> " It is part of our objectives and engagement in the northeast to further enhance collaboration between relevant stakeholders in peace building , forgiveness and reconciliation . <p> " We are sensitising community and religious leaders to the de-radicalisation of Boko Haram insurgents . <p> " The aim is to provide platform to stimulate sensitization of stakeholders and members of the public on de-radicalisation programme and how best the repentant insurgents could be reintegrated in the society . " <p> Zarami disclosed that about 100 participants @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ attended the exercise , adding that the organisation had earlier conducted similar exercise at Damaturu . <p> According to him , the exercise is conducted in collaboration with the Presidential Committee on Northeast Initiative ( PCNI ) and Operation Safe Corridor while Mc Arthur Foundation supports the exercise . <p> Maj.-Gen . Bamidele Shafa , the Coordinator , Operation Safe Corridor , noted that such dialogue between community members was imperative to end insurgency and enhance peace restoration in the region . <p> " It is desirable for community members to forgive and accept repentant insurgents into the society . <p> " The repentant insurgents were forcefully conscripted and they had under gone intensive de-radicalization and rehabilitation process to enable them to rejoin the society , " he said . <p> Shafa disclosed that the repentant insurgents were trained on various trades to enable them to engage in productive activities and become responsible members of the society . <p> He added that over 200 of them were de-radicalised and rehabilitated , noting that they were ready to re-join the society . <p> The coordinator reiterated the commitments of Nigerian Army @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that the authorities would not condone release of repentant insurgents , who could become a threat to the society . <p> However , some of the participants expressed concern over the government plans to reintegrate the insurgents in to the society . <p> Alhaji Kachalla Kyari , a district head in Bama , said they were wary over the reintegration programme in view of the insurgents ' extreme ideologies and atrocities perpetrated by the group . <p> Kyari recounted that the insurgents had poisoned food items and water sources in Bama , and wondered why the government trained them on processing of sachet water . <p> " After their reintegration , it is good not to allow them to engage in sachet water processing to guard against poisoning . " <p> Another community leader , Alhaji Zanna Yale , noted that it would be difficult for the affected persons to forgive and accept the insurgents after the atrocities they committed against the families . <p> Yale further called on the government to postpone the reintegration programme until peace was achieved and called for prayers for the return of peace in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ NAN ) reports that the Operation Safe Corridor is initiated by the Federal Government to encourage Boko Haram insurgents to surrender . <p> It is also designed to rehabilitate and de-radicalise the repentant insurgents for possible reintegration into the society .
