<h> Ex-Ghanaian President wants graduating students to proffer solution to African problems <p> Sunday , November 25 , 2018 9:47 pm <p> Former Ghanaian President , John Dramani Mahama <p> Jethro Ibileke/Benin <p> John Mahama , the immediate past President of Ghana , has urged Nigeria and Ghana to leverage on their human and material resources to propel the sub-region to greater economy prosperity and dignity . <p> He made the appeal at the weekend during the 16th Convocation of the Igbinedion University , Okada , Edo State . <p> He said " Ghana and Nigeria being the two biggest economies in the West African sub-region , place certain responsibilities on us to become the engine room that propel our sub region to greater prosperity and dignity for our people . " <p> While proffering solutions to the economic recession and climate change on the continent , the ex President of Ghana said the various institutions of learning must be able to produce graduates that are creative and also able to think out of the box , in order to make the society better for all . <p> " Institutions of higher learning have a significant role to play @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ producing the required human resources , with the requisite skills to transform our societies for the benefit of our people . <p> " Our societies need innovative and creative graduands with questioning minds , independent of thoughts and creative thinking , to be able to think outside the box and proffer solutions that can help in the transformation of our countries . <p> " We need our Universities to also research on the major issues confronting today 's world , such as climate change , the like of populism in our politics , migration , financial recession , depressed commodity price etc , " he said . <p> On his part , the Deputy Chancellor of Iginedion University , Chief Lucky Igbinedion , wants the federal government to resolve all impasses with the Academic Staff Union of Universities ( ASUU ) , to ensure smooth running of the academic sessions in the Nigerian 's institutions of learning . <p> He added that any countries that toys with the education of its citizens has no future .
