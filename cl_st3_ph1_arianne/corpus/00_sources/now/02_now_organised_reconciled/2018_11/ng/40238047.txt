Scientific Assessment of Ozone Depletion : 2018 ' , is the latest in a series of reports , released every four years , which monitor the recovery of ozone in the stratosphere . <p> Ozone is a layer that protects life on Earth from harmful layers of ultraviolet rays from the sun . <p> According to the News Agency of Nigeria , NAN , the study shows that the concentration of ozone-depleting substances continues to decrease , leading to an improvement in the layer since the previous assessment carried out in 2014 . <p> The findings are being hailed as a demonstration of what global agreements can achieve , and an inspiration for more ambitious climate action to halt a catastrophic rise in world temperatures . <p> Ozone in parts of the stratosphere has recovered at a rate of one
