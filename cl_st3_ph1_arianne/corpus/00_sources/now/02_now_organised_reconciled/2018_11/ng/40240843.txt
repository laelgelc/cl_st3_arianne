<h> Kogi donates relief materials to flood victims in Idah <p> The Kogi Government on Monday , donated foodstuff and other relief items to victims of 2018 flood in Idah and Igalamela/Odolu Local Government Areas of the state . Sanusi Yahaya , the Commissioner for Environment , handed over the items to leaders of the affected communities in Idaho on Tuesday . Dorcas Enehe , Director , Climate Change , State Environment Ministry , represented the commissioner at the occasion . The commissioner recalled that the state government had in October donated relief items to flood victims in Lokoja , Bassa , Ajaokuta and Ofu Local Government Areas . Yahaya said : " Today , we are doing same for Idah and Igalamela-Odolu LGAs . " He noted that the National Emergency Management Agency inaugurated the distribution of relief items to victims in Lokoja , assuring that the exercise which would be extended to all flood affected LGAs , last week . The commissioner , who appealed to the leaders and representatives of the communities to ensure the items get to the affected persons , urged the people to shun violence .
