<h> Former EU ambassador advises govt on attainment of SDGs <p> A former Ambassador and Head of the European Union delegation to the African Union , Ambassador Tim Clarke , has urged Nigerian government and the academic community to be more committed to the attainment of the Sustainable Development Goals . <p> Clarke gave the advice on Thursday while delivering the 16th Convocation Lecture of the Igbinedion University , Okada , with the theme , ' Creating an African Academic Network to Promote Regional and Political Integration in Africa . " <p> He said it would be a difficult task to achieve the SDGs in Nigeria by the set date of 2030 , because the country ranked 150th out of 156 nations in achieving the SDGs . <p> According to him , there are 17 goals with 2030 as their target date with a 15-year implementation period . He said challenges for African countries were higher than those of most other continents . <p> Clarke stated , " The reality , with a few exceptions , was that no one is doing anything serious to meet these goals .
