<h> This is what Prince Charles discussed with some traditional leaders yeterday <p> The Prince of Wales , Prince Charles on Tuesday met with some traditional leaders . <p> Prince Charles and his wife , Princess Camilla met with the traditional rulers in Abuja on Monday during a visit to Nigeria , the final leg of an eight-day tour to three West African countries to discuss , among other issues , peace and security , and educating the girl-child <p> Speaking with newsmen , following closed-door meeting , the Emir of Kano , Muhammed Sanusi II said the Prince gave a brief summary of discussions held with President Muhammadu Buhari on a range of issues , from climate change to demographic explosion . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Sanusi said that the proper education of the girl-child would address the issue of demographics in the country . <p> " We talked about town planning . Now take the north for example We have had a huge demographic explosion in the last 40 to 50 years but we have not yet changed the way we build our houses . So every time there is pressure for housing , the government takes over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to feed Kano and we are not going to have grazing routes . We have got to start thinking how we could build vertically so that more families live on one plot , we free up the land , improve the yield on agriculture and begin to confront some of these challenges of demographics . Educating the girl child basically kills so many birds with one stone ; we had a conversation around and the whole idea is how to work with the British Government in dealing with these issues and see how we can learn from each other 's experience , " the Emir said . <p> The Oba of Benin , Omo N'Oba Ewuare II also told newsmen that the issues of human trafficking and youth empowerment were discussed adding that " I made my commitments " . <p> The seven traditional leaders received by Prince Charles are the Obi of Onitsha , Nnaemeka Achebe ; Etsu Nupe , Alhaji Yahaya Abubakar and the Emir of Kano , Muhammad Sanusi II .
