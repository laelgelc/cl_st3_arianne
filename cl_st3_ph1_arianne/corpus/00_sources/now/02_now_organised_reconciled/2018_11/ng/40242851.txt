<h> Give Us Back Our Artifacts- Oba of Benin Demands From Prince Charles <p> Oba Ewuare II of Benin has requested that the Duke of Cornwell , Prince Charles , return the artifacts of Benin Kingdom which were carted away by the British government in 1897 <p> According to the Oba , this would enable him to build a museum where the artifacts can be housed and could be a source of Tourism revenue <p> Oba Ewuare said this in a statement read in a closed-door meeting with the Prince <p> The Oba said : " Suffice to say that Nigerians in general and Benin people in particular will be most delighted to have your royal highness throw his royal weight behind our efforts to have some of our ancient artifacts that were taken in 1897 from the Royal Court of Benin returned to Benin to establish Oba Palace Museum for the promotion of tourism in Benin City , Edo State .
