<h> Interviews With Trump , Jobs , Prince Williams Memorable -- Max Foster <p> Max Foster is part of Prince Charles ' entourage on his maiden royal tour of West Africa , covering Ghana and Nigeria . The entourage touched down at Abuja on November 6 , visiting Lagos in the course of a three-day visit . Foster shares his experience as a royal correspondent with our reporter . <p> When did you become a royal correspondent and what sustains your interest on this beat ? <p> I was blown away at the international reaction to the wedding of Prince William and Kate Middleton and wanted to find out more . I have travelled widely with the family since and learned that each country has their own interest in them . They are a truly global brand and by working with them I have gained access to people and places I would n't easily reach . This is my first visit to Nigeria for example .
