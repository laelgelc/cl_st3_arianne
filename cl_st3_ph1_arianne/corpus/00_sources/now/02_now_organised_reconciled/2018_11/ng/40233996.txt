<h> Ogun distributes relief materials to 1,800 flood victims <p> The Ogun State Government has said it distributed relief materials to 1,800 flood victims in the Abeokuta North and South Local Government Areas of the state . <p> The Governor , Senator Ibikunle Amosun , during the distribution exercise to the July 13 , 2018 flood victims at Arcade Ground , Oke-Mosan , Abeokuta , was quoted in statement as saying that the flooding was a global phenomenon , which occurred as a result of climate change and had continued to wreak more havoc in different parts of the world . <p> Represented by his Deputy , Mrs Yetunde Onanuga , the governor disclosed that structures mostly affected were situated around flood-prone areas with poor sanitation . <p> He said the relevant agencies of government should be more effective at ensuring good environmental sanitation , so as to prevent reoccurrence . <p> The Director General , National Emergency Management Agency , Mr Mustafa Maihaja , who was represented by the Chief Planning Officer , Mr SegunAfolayan , noted that 1,800 victims registered with the agency . <p> He also urged the Federal Government to sign the NEMA bill into @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The State Commissioner for Environment , Mr Bolaji Oyeleye , stated that relief materials distributed included food , building materials and mattresses , and implored beneficiaries to effectively utilise the items . <p> Two of the beneficiaries , Mrs Anike Ogunsola and Mr Abayomi Idowu , while speaking on behalf of others , appreciated the federal and state governments for the relief materials given to them , and called for more measures to promote sanitation . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
