<h> Lake Chad : UNESCO , AfDB collaborate with Nigeria <p> The United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organisation and the African Development Bank have begun a training programme for environmental experts from Nigeria , Central Africa Republic , Cameroon , Niger and Chad to enable them to empower communities in the Lake Chad Basin region to reduce pressure and halt its degradation . <p> The Forestry Research Institute of Nigeria in Ibadan is supporting the capacity building training tagged Green Economy in Biosphere Reserve . <p> National Programme Officer for Sciences , UNESCO Office , Abuja , Dr Osuji Otu , told our correspondent that the project was aimed at supporting the efforts of the Nigerian government in preserving the Lake Chad resources . <p> According to him , the communities around the lake depend on the water for survival . <p> He said , " Currently , UNESCO is working in the Lake Chad region to address the problem of environmental degradation in the area . There are five countries involved in the project . Environmental experts from these countries are brought together under this project to be trained in establishment of Green Economy in Biosphere @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ we are fortunate that the project was executed in Nigeria in 2010 . <p> " The local communities are supposed to enjoy what the environment brings but we found out that they exploit it and it becomes a problem to control it . The green economy project teaches the local communities to preserve the environment and at the same time benefit from it economically . " <p> Director-General of FRIN , Adesola Adepoju , lamented that Lake Chad water body had shrunk from 25,000 square kilometres in 1963 to 1,350 square kilometres in 2017 , blaming the loss on climate change and poor enforcement of environmental legislation . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
