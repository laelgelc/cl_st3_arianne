<h> Obey the rule of law , British envoy tells Shi'ites , security agencies <p> The outgoing British High Commissioner to Nigeria , Paul Arkwright , has called on the Islamic Movement in Nigeria ( Shi'ites ) and security agencies to obey the rule of law in their activities . <p> He stated that the group should exercise their rights to protest peacefully , adding that security agencies should also respect the rule of law . <p> Speaking to journalists in Abuja on Thursday , Arkwright expressed hope that the move by the government to investigate the clashes between the group and security forces would would help handle such situations in the future . <p> He said , " Just as the IMN must obey the rule of law by using their rights to demonstrate peacefully , and the key word here is peacefully , so also it is important for the security forces in Nigeria to respect the rule of law . " <p> Arkwright said that the clashes between herdsmen and farmers were not based on religious . <p> He attributed the crisis to the issues of climate change and desertification , noting that Tuesday 's meeting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and traditional rulers had further confirmed the reasons for the crisis . <p> He said , " This is a conflict that is driven by complex reasons of climate change , pressure on resources , land and desertification of Northern Nigeria . " <p> The envoy said population explosion management and sustainable economic development remained , among other things , the focal point of the British government 's engagement with Nigeria . <p> The envoy described his stay in Nigeria as memorable , adding that the UK government would continue to support the fight against corruption by the Federal Government . <p> Arkwright 's successor , Catriona Laing , is expected to resume in the country on November 26 . <p> Copyright PUNCH .
