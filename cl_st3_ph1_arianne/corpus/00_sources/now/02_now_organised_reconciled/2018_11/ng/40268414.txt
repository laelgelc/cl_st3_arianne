" <p> Sanusi , also , said climate change , particularly the shrinking of Lake Chad , has had a devastating effect on local livelihoods . <p> " We can not divorce ... horizontal inequalities from some of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and bringing peace to the northeast has to be accompanied by regenerating that economy and recharging the Chad basin , " he said . <p> The emir said such demographic change would create huge social upheaval that would potentially make the effects of the Boko Haram conflict " child 's play " in the north . <p> Nigeria needed to build a strong economy and slow population growth to avoid poverty worsening , he added . <p> Recall that in November 2014 , Sanusi called on residents of the city to rise up and defend themselves against the jihadists . <p> But barely two weeks after his statement , the city 's central mosque near his palace was targeted by gunmen and bombers during Friday prayers .
