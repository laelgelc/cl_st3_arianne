<h> Lack of land discouraging youths from farming , says agric entrepreneur <p> Abuja -- A young agricultural entrepreneur , Mr Abdulmumin Kori on Thursday in Abuja identified lack of land as one of the major factors discouraging the youths from farming . <p> Kori , the Chief Executive Officer of Binkori Farms in Masaka , Nasarawa State , said this at the 5th meeting of the consultative committee on federal and state agricultural policies alignment . <p> The meeting was hosted by Synergos , a global non-profit organisation , that drives the initiative with support from the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation . <p> Kori , who had a joint presentation with Amina Jumare , another youth agripreneur , said that he had a lot of friends willing to go into agriculture but lacked the means to acquire farmland . <p> He said that efforts by the government to encourage youths into farming should include provision of leasable land at affordable rates . <p> On her part , Jumare , who is the Chief Executive Officer of Cabra Farms in Kuje , FCT , said there was need for sensitation of smallholder farmers on bio-security and other modern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ case of a farm in Kuje where thousands of birds died just because the owner lacked basic knowledge of biosecurity to properly manage them . <p> Kori and Jumare , who shared their experiences as young farmers , itemised their initial challenges to include lack of market , equipment , farm inputs and regular water sources . <p> However , they said they were able to overcome the challenges with technical support from the Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development and Synergos .
