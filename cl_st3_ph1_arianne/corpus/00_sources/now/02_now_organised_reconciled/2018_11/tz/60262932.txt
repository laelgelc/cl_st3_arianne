<h> WWF hands watering trough in stakeholders ' initiative <p> THE World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) -Tanzania has handed over a cattle trough costing more than 60m/- to Mwakaganga communities in Mbarali District , Mbeya region to end conflicts in water use and alleviate prevailing poverty . <p> Speaking at the official handing over this week , the WWF Tanzania freshwater coordinator Engineer Christian Chonya , said the project was being implemented in collaboration with the Mbarali District Council and the local communities . <p> He said an estimated 1,650 people will benefit from the project and nearby villages for domestic water supply , noting that it can sustain more than one thousand animals kept by people in the communities . <p> The project is part of collaborative initiatives ( CIs ) resulting from multi-stakeholders process or platforms ( MSP ) piloted by the WWF via its Ruaha Water Programme ( RWP ) from 2011 to 2017 in the two sub-catchments of Ndembera and Mbarali . <p> Engineer Chonya said the project was initially identified by stakeholders attending one MSP workshop held in Makambako town in 2015 where the WWF engaged diverse stakeholders to participate in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ experiences . <p> The coordinator noted that during the workshop , stakeholders pinpointed challenges of cattle watering in Mbarali River and damaging water sources , river banks and polluting water in the rivers , along with impairing of irrigation infrastructure , farms . <p> " Accomplishing this project will enhance improvement of people 's livelihoods and overcoming the prevailing poverty among community members , and the district will benefit in ending conflicts in water uses in the area , " he said . <p> . <p> Furthermore , the project will strengthen environmental conversation , wildlife corridors , Mbarali River conservation , and water sources/wetland conservation . It will be a climate change mitigation project in the area as the pump is powered with a solar power generating system . <p> In addition , Engineer Chonya said the project will foster other stakeholders to come and learn the benefits of integrated working together from stakeholders ' engagement in solving the water sources conservation problem . <p> In the project , WWF used its expertise , knowledge and experience in engaging multiple stakeholders at different levels , and consequently many stakeholders @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ problems . <p> " Effective community participation and involvement in conservation interventions is the key area for sustainable conservation and poverty reduction among communities in the catchments and sub-catchments , " he emphasized . <p> WWF believes that conservation was about people and conservation without people is destined to fail , so the program strives to ensure that people and the community are at the center of the conservation effort .
