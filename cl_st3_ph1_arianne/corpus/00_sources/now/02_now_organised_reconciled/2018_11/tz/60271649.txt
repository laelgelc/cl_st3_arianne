<h> Tanzanians called on to adopt sustainable charcoal practices <p> Swiss ambassador has called upon Tanzanians to adopt sustainable charcoal use practices to reduce deforestation at the same time conserving the environment . <p> Flocence Tinguely Mattli made the call yesterday in Dar es Salaam during the launching of the Kilimanjaro Project dubbed Tuje Pamoja Challenge literally translates ' Let us come together ' in Dar es Salaam yesterday , as an environmental and social impact initiative aimed at planting million trees both in the Kilimanjaro Region and across the country in the coming years . <p> She said through sustainable charcoal production the community can use in a very sustainable manner to ensure they give a chance to the tree growing and to be ready for the next year so that they can be cut . <p> " It is very important to keep trees as the free resource ; otherwise there will be no trees in the country , with deforestation and climate change we will have no trees for no charcoal and the population including thr most the poor will suffer , " she said . <p> She called on for the village authorities to divide @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planting trees and farming in which their use can change after using the part for sustainable charcoal almost in 20 years , saying the division of land for appropriate use will allow the village to collect money from legal sustainable charcoal while the government will get levies . <p> The project leader Sarah Scott said the Kilimanjaro project believes that there is hope for future generations if everyone plays part to protect the environment . The project endeavours to work with key environmental stakeholders and hopes to connect these stakeholders through the Tuje Pamoja campaign . <p> " We want to make it more economically viable to keep a tree in the ground than to cut it down . Our hope is that in three to five years , the Kilimanjaro project will be a social enterprise , empowering smallholder farmers , communities , and schools to create additional income through tree planting and environmental protection , " she said . <p> For his part , Minister in the Vice President 's Office ( Union and Environmental ) January Makamba assured the government 's support to the efforts made by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the implementation of sustainable charcoal production .
