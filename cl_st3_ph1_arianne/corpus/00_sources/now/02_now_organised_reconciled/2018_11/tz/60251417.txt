Minister for Agriculture and Irrigation , Omari Mgumba said that the funds are part of the 13trillion/- intended for the second phase of the Agriculture Sector Development Programme ( ASDP ) . <p> The government is committed to adopt smart agriculture irrigation systems , develop infrastructures and increase irrigated land from 460,000 hectares at the start of the plan to one million hectares by 2020 . <p> " We are now conducting a nation-wide feasibility study on various basins and canals to establish their status and how they can be improved to promote irrigation agriculture , " he said .
