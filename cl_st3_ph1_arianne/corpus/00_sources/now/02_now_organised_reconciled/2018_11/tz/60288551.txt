Deep in the scenic and biologically rich mountains of the East Usambara in Muheza District , Tanga Region , primary schoolchildren are busy in their tree nurseries . <p> They are watering a variety of tree seedlings they would later plant in their school compound and at their homes . <p> They are members of an environmental club established through the Echo School programme that is revolutionising the way young people think about protecting the environment . The programme is part of a three-year climate change adaptation project known as ' Integrated Approaches for Climate Change Adaptation being implemented in eight villages of the East Usambara .
