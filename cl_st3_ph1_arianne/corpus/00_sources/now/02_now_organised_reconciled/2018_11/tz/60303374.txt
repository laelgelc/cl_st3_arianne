<h> Norway issues Sh25 billion for Tanzania 's tax reforms <h> Thursday November 29 2018 <h> In Summary <p> The Visiting Norwegian minister who is in the country for three days has promised to cement bilateral relations with Tanzania with initial support of Sh25 billion for tax reforms programmes . <p> Advertisement <p> By udger Kasumuni @TheCitizenTZ **28;746;TOOLONG <p> Dar es Salaam . Norway has pledged to give the government Sh25 billion for improving capacity of the Tanzania Revenue Authority ( TRA ) to collect more revenues from domestic sources . <p> Speaking on Tuesday after the official launch of the ceremony for Tanzanians who were trained in Norway , the visiting Norwegian minister of International Development , Mr Nikolai Astrup , said such programme was agreed during their meeting with the deputy minister of Finance and Economic Planning , Dr Ashatu Kijaji . <p> " I also held a meeting with Tanzania Tax Revenue Authority who requested Norway to support the ongoing programme of tax reforms , " said the Norwegian minister who is in the country on a three-day visit . <p> " This is my first trip to Tanzania as minister of International Development . Earlier @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ President Magufuli , " he said . <p> According to Norwegian minister , during their talks with President John Magufuli they agreed to strengthening bilateral co-operation in the areas of building the capacity of private sector , ongoing climate change support , supporting training programmes for Tanzanians , support the country 's energy projects , petroleum and natural gas development and other development projects . <p> Earlier , the deputy finance minister said the government had increased tax collection volumes from the monthly collections of Sh800 billion in 2015 to Sh1.4 trillion now , but more efforts were called for . <p> Norway has been supporting the country through Global Fund , especially the climate change projects . According to the latest report of Global Fund the annual contribution of Norway to such global facility for mitigating climate change amounts to $66 million and the country has already received $56 million support from such facility . <p> At the same occasion , those present included Dr Kijaji , the Speaker of the National Assembly Job Ndugai , deputy minister for Health , Community Development , Elderly and Children , Dr @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Investment , Ms Stella Manyanya . <p> The Norwegian ambassador to Tanzania , Ms Elisabeth Jacobsen , said at the occasion that the visit of Norwegian minister was set to cement relations with Tanzania through the established Norway-Tanzania Alumni known as Al-Ta-Tano . <p> There were more than 300 Tanzanians trained in Norway who attended the Al-Ta-No ceremony held at the residence of Norwegian ambassador on Tuesday night . <p> The Speaker , Mr Ndugai , was one of such Alumni who studied in Norway in 1994 before joining active political affairs . <p> The Al-Ta-No network was established in late 2017 . The aim is to strengthen and maintain the ties between Norway and Tanzania . Its members are Tanzanians who have studied or worked in Norway and the Norwegian community in Tanzania .
