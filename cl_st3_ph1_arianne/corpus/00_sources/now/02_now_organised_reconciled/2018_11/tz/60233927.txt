<h> Irrigation technology means more food for the country <p> Japan is assisting Tanzania to roll out modern farming initiatives ; so that the latter can achieve the national target of ensuring one million hectares of arable land is placed under irrigation schemes by 2020 . <p> The Japanese envoy to Tanzania , ambassador Masaharu Yoshida , in January last year said during the inauguration of a proposed 150 hectare irrigation project being established under the Arusha Technical College ( ATC ) , with funding from the Japan International Cooperation Agency ( JICA ) . <p> Ambassador Yoshida said they are already assisting to bolster Tanzania 's paddy farms because Japan is an expert in this field and through this cooperation , the country 's rice production has doubled within a ten-year time frame . <p> Therefore , the only way to combat the effects of climate change and deal with natural disasters such as floods was to invest heavily in irrigation schemes . <p> It targets to expand farming areas under irrigation and also to overhaul the existing schemes that still operate through outdated traditional irrigation structures . The Oljoro Training Farm includes a man-made lake , rainwater harvesting facilities , sprinklers , and drip irrigation including water channels crisscrossing the vast 150-hectare real estate . <p> Future plans for the project which runs under the ' Irrigation Human Resources Development , ' include establishing student hostels and lecture rooms at the farm located about 20 kilometres from the Arusha City Centre .
