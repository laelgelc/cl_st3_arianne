<h> Private sector urged to venture into irrigation farming <p> DEPUTY Minister for Agriculture and Irrigation , Omary Mgumba has called on the private sector to invest in irrigation farming as it is a sure way of food security in the country . <p> Mgumba made the call on Monday when he visited the National Irrigation Commission ( NIRC ) Headquarters in Dar es Salaam . <p> He said that Tanzania has an irrigation potential area of 29.4 million hectares whereby 2.3 million hectares is of high potential , 4.8 million hectares is of medium potential and 22.3 million hectares is of low potential . <p> Mgumba said that the government through the National Irrigation Commission ( NIRC ) has planned to initiate irrigation projects under the Public-Private partnership arrangement in order to attract more private investors into the sector . <p> The deputy Minister directed the Commission to cooperate with districts councils in the country to identify potential areas for irrigation and put in place plans to engage private sector including banks such as Tanzania Agriculture Development Bank , NMB and Postal Bank in order to invest in the irrigation sector . <p> The minister urged the Irrigation Commission to revolutionise the agriculture sector as over 75 per cent of Tanzanians are engaged in the sector . <p> Earlier , acting director for irrigation infrastructure development ,
