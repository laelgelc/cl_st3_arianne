<h> Research-based project launched in Dar <p> A research based project aimed at building capacity to experts and decision makers on using an integrated approach in planning development corridors that consider the protection of the integrity of the ecosystem at an earliest stage has been launched . <p> The development corridors partnership is a south-south research and capacity building collaboration among Chinese and African institutions and is designed to carry out interdisciplinary and international research programmes to assess current development corridor impacts , processes and practices . <p> The project which is funded by the UK government , was launched in Dar es Salaam yesterday and brought together think tanks from universities in UK , Kenya , and China with Tanzania being represented by the Sokoine University of Agriculture ( SUA ) . <p> By the end of the day the project is expected to provide guidance on how corridors can be better designed to deliver sustainable , inclusive and resilient economic growth . <p> " We will conduct outreach and engagement activities in East Africa and China with corridor decision makers and stakeholders , " said Neil Burgess from the United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) who @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said despite the fact that development corridors have the potential to diversify and improve livelihoods by making trade , communications and services more efficient , the benefits can be maximized if corridors are planned and implemented through and integrated approach using sound policies , plans and practices . <p> According to them , negative impacts of poorly planned and implemented corridors may threaten natural capital , ecosystem , integrity , livelihoods and social cohesion . <p> In his remarks Prof YingLong Xu from the Chinese academy for Agriculture Sciences said there is need to develop principles to guide how to carry out development activities especially corridors by adopting high technology for positive results . <p> " Climate change poses risks to development , therefore there is need to build capacity to our people on how to integrate development ideas into concepts that take biodiversity into consideration , " he said . <p> World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) country director Dr Aman Ngusaro said the project will help to ensure that planning of corridors should be planned with biodiversity considerations .
