<h> IFC funding for African renewable energy projects <p> THE International Finance Corporation has partnered with Gaia Energy to develop renewable energy projects in Africa . <p> As part of their efforts to increase the pipeline of bankable projects in developing countries , World Bank Group member the International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) and Gaia Energy , a Morocco-headquartered renewable energy developer , have teamed up to create a joint platform for the growth of wind energy across Africa . <p> The funding has been provided to develop a joint platform of 22 pipelines across nine countries in North , West and East Africa . The pipeline , originally developed by Gaia , will then be enlarged to new countries . <p> Two IFC funds will be implementing and supporting the project : InfraVentures , a USD 150 million global infrastructure development fund , and the EUR 114 million Finland-IFC Blended Finance for Climate Program , which was developed to spur private sector financing for climate change solutions . <p> Commenting on the partnership , IFC head of InfraVentures for Sub-Saharan Africa David Donaldson said : " Wind energy and renewable energy in general are priority areas identified @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ solutions .
