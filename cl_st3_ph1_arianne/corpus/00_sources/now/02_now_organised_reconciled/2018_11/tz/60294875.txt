<h> 10 million trees are expected to be planted in Musoma <p> MORE than 10 million trees are expected to be planted in Musoma municipality and Rural Council next month so as to reduce the climate change related impacts and environmental pollution . <p> Musoma District Commissioner Dr Vicent Naano Anney . <p> Musoma District Commissioner Dr Vicent Naano Anney said yesterday that the government has planted more than ten million seedlings , which have now been growing well and so far the remaining work is planting them in various areas of the two councils in Mara Region . <p> " The administrative authorities in Musoma District have designed seedlings production project which had seen more than ten million tree seedlings distributed in our two councils just for free and possibly next month or in January we may start its implementation . <p> " The aim is to reduce the drought resulting from climate change facing most of the countries in the sub-Saharan Africa , " Dr Anney said . <p> He added that the wanton felling of trees is a major problem in Mara Region , and large-scale tree planting will help to fight drought following severe damage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ include Rural Musoma Rural legislator Professor Sospeter Muhongo and his Musoma Urban counterpart , Vedastus Mathayo , who are playing a central role on volunteer basis , " said the DC . <p> " Prof Muhongo has contributed one tank of 3,000 litres water storage , 5,555 seedlings and 1,390 seeds have been purchased by other development stakeholders . These seedlings will be planted in nine wards with almost 28 villages as planned . Also Mathayo offered one million/- to support this programme , " he said . <p> However , Dr Anney asserted that , at the beginning of this year , 12,500 species of trees were planted in Musoma rural areas to ensure that the areas become green belt as it was in the past by planting tree seedlings .
