Picture : SUPPLIED <p> HER passion to deliver the best quality stoves for rural dwellers has resulted in the invention of the smokeless energy efficient wood stoves . <p> For widow Anaisi Mataitoga , her passion to help the marginalised grew even stronger after various visits to the peri-urban and rural communities and seeing first-hand the depleting state of living still experienced by some families in this 21st century . <p> The Nawaikama villager from Gau is the founder for Gender Climate Change Alliance Fiji Ltd ( GCCAF Ltd ) which became a registered business on October , 21 , 2012 and she also designed the smokeless stove to reduce indoor air pollution ( IAP ) -- the biggest killer for children and women in third world countries for inhaling smoke from cooking fires . <p> " The company was initiated by a number of women from different walks of life and professions who had a common vision to bring a difference in the day-to-day lives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " Ms Mataitoga said . <p> The word gender is included in the business name to refer to both male and female inclusiveness . <p> " We the pioneers recognised the importance of gender equality and the need to be sensitive and inclusive with our policies and more practical approach to be able to achieve our vision and mission effectively . <p> " Most importantly we know that our calling will be amidst the giant male counterparts and we rate ourselves ( GCCAF Ltd ) as equal partners .
