<h> Merkel focuses on foreign security policy in European Parliament address <p> Claudia Dalby reports at the European Parliament in Strasbourg , where German Chancellor Angela Merkel addressed MEPs under the theme of the Future of Europe . <p> Chancellor Angela Merkel has called for the creation of a " real , true , European army " and a European security council , in an address to the European Parliament on 13 November . <p> Her speech focused on " solidarity " and " tolerance " , words which were repeated often as being " at the soul of Europe . " She spoke on the importance of community and national independence for European countries , but that " solidarity means we have to overcome nationalism and egoism " which " no longer have a tradition in Europe " . <p> Merkel 's statements echo those made by French President Emmanuel Macron earlier in the week , who said " nationalism is a betrayal of patriotism " in front of over 60 heads of state in an armistice commemoration speech on 11 November . <p> Merkel noted that such a European army would be separate from NATO , but @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ so that we can tackle issues on the ground " as the " time that we can rely on others has passed " . <p> The " deep wound " of Brexit was only briefly mentioned , and the focus of her speech was on foreign security policy , digital taxation and immigration policy . <p> " We need a border protection force " , she said on the topic of the migration crisis , adding that Europe is " infringing " on the principle of the Schengen area if a common European asylum policy is not established . <p> Merkel remarked on the " economic and social problem " of climate change , which had been voted on that morning by the Parliament .
