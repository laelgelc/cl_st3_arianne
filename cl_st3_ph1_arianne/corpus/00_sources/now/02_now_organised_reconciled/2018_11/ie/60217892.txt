<h> Greenhouse gas emissions in Ireland rise 3.6% in a year <p> Ireland 's total greenhouse gas emissions increased by 3.6% to 61.5m tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent from 2015 to 2016 . <p> According to figures released by the Central Statistics Office , the industry sector accounted for the largest share of greenhouse gas emissions for the third year in a row , at 34.4% of the total . <p> This follows a UN warning on Wednesday from the United Nations that Ireland is failing to take effective measures to tackle climate change . <p> This failure is a breach of human rights , said a UN special rapporteur . <p> In a legal submission in support of a High Court challenge to the State 's plan to tackle climate change , the special rapporteur , David Boyd , said : <p> " Ireland must reduce emissions as rapidly as possible , applying the maximum available resources . " <p> The CSO report , meanwhile , shows that the share of emissions from agriculture , forestry , and fishing in 2016 was 32.5% . Emissions from households made up 21.9% of the total , and the services sector produced 11.1% of total greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Emissions from agriculture , forestry , and fishing fell from 19.3m tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent in 2007 to 18.2m tonnes in 2011 .
