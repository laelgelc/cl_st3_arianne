Photos : LWF Nepal <h> LWF work with Nepal 's Haliya community targets women 's rights and education <p> ( LWI ) - Thirty-year-old Basanti Mahar of Bashulinga village in Nepal says talking to other women about alcohol-fueled domestic abuse in their community has changed her life and transformed the whole village . <p> " Our village feels like heaven now , " said Mahar . <p> The women in the village located in the far western district of Doti , witnessed the change in their lives as they gained awareness about their rights , after silently enduring humiliation , physical abuse and mental torture . <p> No one was there to listen to their personal stories . <p> Following the establishment of a transformative education program ( TEP ) supported by The Lutheran World Federation ( LWF ) Nepal , Evangelical Lutheran Church in America and local partner Rastriya Mukta Haliya Samaj , that scenario has changed . <p> Started in May 2018 , TEP is a nine-month project , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . They then demanded that the local authorities , police or district administration shut down the village alcohol outlet . It was closed following our request , " said Mahar , referring to the benefits of the LWF skills ' training program . <p> The women went further and decided to levy fines for acts of violence and alcohol abuse .
