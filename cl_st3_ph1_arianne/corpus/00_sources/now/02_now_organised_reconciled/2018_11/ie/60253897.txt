<h> A process from another time - It 's time to bin junk mail <p> A postman has lost his job and received a suspended sentence because he dumped mail to shorten his working day . <p> The post was primarily marketing material -- junk mail -- but the bundle also contained details of medical appointments . He should not have behaved as he did and paid a penalty . <p> The case raises one of those issues we all recognise and occasionally complain about . Junk mail is an affliction and as the Christmas tsunami of marketing material gathers momentum an almost daily torrent of mail , most of it left unread before it is dumped , falls through our letterboxes . <p> It is not hard to imagine that the national junk mail mountain is measured in hundreds of tons or that its disposal , if it is not recycled , makes the argument for incinerators plausible . <p> Foisting physical junk mail on the public seems a practice from a time when we were less aware of the negative environmental impact of our actions , especially unessential behaviour .
