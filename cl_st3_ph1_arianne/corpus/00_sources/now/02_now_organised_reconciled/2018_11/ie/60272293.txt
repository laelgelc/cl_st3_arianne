<h> Youth delegate Helena Funk visits UN Church Center and GreenFaith in New York <p> ( LWI ) -- Last year , Helena Funk , a youth delegate from the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Northern Germany , attended the Bonn Conference of the Parties ( COP23 ) to the annual United Nations ( UN ) climate gatherings as part of the Lutheran World Federation ( LWF ) delegation and gained her first experience with such high-level meetings . This year she will be part of the LWF delegation at COP24 in Katowice , Poland , from 2 to 14 December . <h> Tips for advocacy work <p> At the UN Church Center , which is directly opposite the UN headquarters , she came across Rebekka Pohlmann from the Evangelical Lutheran Church in Bavaria , who was pursuing special ministerial training with the LWF in New York .
