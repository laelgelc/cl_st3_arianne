<p> Eating more bacon instead of beef could help the UK meeting its greenhouse gas emissions targets <p> The UK must cut levels of the gas to 160million tons by 2050 . Last year they were at 456million , falling from 800million in 1990 . <p> Lord Deben , chairman of watchdog Committee on Climate Change , has written to Environment Secretary Michael Gove saying the Government should encourage healthy diets with less lamb and beef and dairy to cut emissions . <p> The CCC will release a report this week warning that agriculture emits the equivalent of 49million tons of CO2 every year . A supporting study also commissioned by the watchdog adds : " Reducing ruminant meat production would be the fastest way to meeting net zero emissions with the majority of methane emissions coming from livestock . " <p> The dietary advice will not please former Labour leader Ed Miliband , who was snap-ped awkwardly eating a bacon sarnie in 2014.
