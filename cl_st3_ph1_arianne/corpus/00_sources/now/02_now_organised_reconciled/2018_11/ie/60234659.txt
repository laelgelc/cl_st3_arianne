Read our policy . <h> Alternative science <p> I am a journalist , not a scientist , but both professions face unprecedented obstacles in the current cynical era of alternative facts . This became apparent at a recent National Association of Science Writers conference in Washington DC . <p> The event began with members voting on a motion that science writers are responsible for building public trust in science . Initial polling before the debate suggested it would pass easily , with 65% approving and 35% objecting . Ultimately , however , things flipped and the measure failed with 60% voting against . That is how science evolves , improves , earns people 's trust and betters our lives . <p> That science is currently under attack and is being increasingly politicised became abundantly clear at the event .
