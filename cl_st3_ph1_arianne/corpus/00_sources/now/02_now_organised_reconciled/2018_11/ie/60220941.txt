<p> Heritage and climate change were the themes of the Meath Association of An Taisce 50th anniversary and Ellison Awards . <p> The event had a tremendous turnout in the wonderful Francis Johnston 's Townley Hall last Tuesday night . Timothy Smyth as MC started the evening introducing the Chair Jean Carr and Mayor of Drogheda Frank Godfrey . <p> In her address the Jean spoke about the role of An Taisce , the National Trust for Ireland in Meath in the last 50 years , beginning with the legendary figures of Canon Cyril Ellison , Elizabeth Hickey , Jim Reynolds , Terry Trench &amp; Eithne Olden . She stressed the fact that as a purely voluntary organisation the association works hard to safeguard the built &amp; natural environment of Meath &amp; that work must continue . <p> The Mayor Frank Godfrey spoke fondly of his involvement with An Taisce spanning decades and his lifelong interest in the Boyne navigation . He recalled the many events he attended and his own personal days as a member of the local association committee .
