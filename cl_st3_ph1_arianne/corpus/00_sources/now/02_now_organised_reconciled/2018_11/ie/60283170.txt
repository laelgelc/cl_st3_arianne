<p> The IFA and ICMSA have hit out at the possibility of carbon taxes being introduced to curb greenhouse gas emissions . <p> The farm organisations ' comments follow warnings from Richard Bruton , the Minister for Communications , Climate Action and the Environment , that Ireland is " far off course " in achieving its CO2 reduction targets . <p> Ireland is facing massive fines because of its failure to meet strict EU targets , and Minister Bruton said that imposing carbon taxes will be among a suite of measures aimed at controlling emissions . <p> However , ICMSA president Pat McCormack said the reality of farming 's importance to the rural economy must be given equal consideration to the " scientific and environmental reality " of climate change . <p> " Farming and food production is not just a part of the rural economy ; to a huge degree it is the total of our rural economy , and undermining it through fuel tax hikes will simply destroy the only meaningful economy in whole swathes of the state outside the cities and larger towns , " said Mr McCormack . <p> IFA environment chairman Thomas Cooney said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , which he said would directly impact on " farming 's competitiveness without reducing climate emissions " . <p> " Farmers in Ireland have a proud record as carbon-efficient food producers .
