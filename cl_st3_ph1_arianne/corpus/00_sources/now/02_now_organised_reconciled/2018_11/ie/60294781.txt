<h> Dubai World Central in deal to boost aquaculture <h> DUBAI , 3 days ago <p> UAE 's Ministry of Climate Change and Environment ( MOCCAE ) has inked an agreement with Dubai World Central ( DWC ) , also known as Dubai South to scale up the aquaculture industry in the UAE through establishing a fish farm . <p> Under the agreement , MOCCAE will facilitate the registration and licensing procedures for the DWC fish farm and provide consultancy advice and know-how on the aquaculture industry to the technical cadres of DWC . <p> Following a feasibility study of proposed species with regard to their local market value , the Ministry will select the species best suited for cultivation based on their key attributes . Furthermore , MOCCAE will test the Recirculating Aquaculture System used on the farm to identify the key challenges that the aquaculture industry faces in the UAE and share the findings with DWC . <p> DWC will outline the project 's concept and core objectives for the Ministry 's review . Fish is an important component of our local cuisine in the UAE , and we need to make sure our fisheries and fish stocks can continue to support the growing demands of our population .
