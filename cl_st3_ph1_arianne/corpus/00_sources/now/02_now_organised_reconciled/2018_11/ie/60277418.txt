<h> Carbon tax bill rise report way off the mark -- Leo Varadkar <p> Taoiseach Leo Varadkar has described a new economic report suggesting households could face a hike in carbon tax bills of up 3,000 euro ( ? 2,674 ) a year as " way off the mark " . <p> Analysis from the Economic Social Research Institute ( ESRI ) has outlined that dramatic tax increases would be needed unless the Government implemented new policies to tackle climate change . <p> Mr Varadkar said : " In terms of carbon tax the ESRI is way off the mark . Certainly this government of Fine Gael and independents does not intend to raise carbon tax to the kind of level suggested in that report . " <p> He added that the Government had not yet worked out what those tax increases would be . <p> You will not be able to reduce income taxes by 600 million euro a year without increasing taxes elsewhere ...
