" <p> The relative ease of use of single-use plastics is the prime reason this unsustainable form of consumption has entrenched itself into everyday life . Even as some of these plastic articles are recyclable , awareness about what can and can not be recycled is still a grey area . For instance , coffee cups are perceived to be recyclable but in reality , most of these cups have an inner lining of plastic that makes them unsuitable for recycling . Moreover , containing more than 5% moisture renders such articles un-recyclable . This essentially points to the realisation that we need to rethink more than one aspect of our lifestyle when it comes to being environment-friendly .
