<h> ' Woman At War ' wins 2018 Lux Film Prize <p> Benedikt Erlingsson 's eco-terrorist comedy-drama Woman At War has won the LUX prize , awarded by the European Parliament in Strasbourg today ( Wednesday November 14 ) . <p> Launched in 2007 , the prize intends to shine ' a spotlight on films that go to the heart of European public debate ' , according to the European Parliament . <p> Woman At War was chosen ahead of Wolfgang Fischer 's Styx , which came second , with Mila Turajlic 's The Other Side Of Everything in third . <p> Director and co-writer Erlingsson was present in Strasbourg to collect the award , and commented , " I feel like a politician , and I think politicians are also storytellers .
