<h> T.Dining 's Best Of The Year Awards 2018-2019 <p> The Best Of The Year awards is a celebration of those at the top of their game in the hospitality industry . Each year , the T.Dining team compiles a shortlist of nominees for each category , which are then sent out to our panel of expert judges -- among them , respected food writers , editors , and influencers . Each panelist is required to vote for their favourites in each category , assigning relevant scores to their choices in order of preference . Should a panelist believe that the shortlist is missing a nominee , they are free to suggest it and offer a justification for their choice , but most importantly , the panelists are not able to discuss their choices with each other and the votes are submitted individually to the editorial team . The scores are then tallied and the winner and runner-up decided .
