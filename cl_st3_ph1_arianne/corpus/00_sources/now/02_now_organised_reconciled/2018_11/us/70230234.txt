<h> Despite Flood of Facts , Climate Change Deniers Persist <h> Charles Rogerson <p> West Virginia has always been schizoid in its attitude toward the natural beauty of its landscape . We celebrate our Wild and Wonderful mountains and waterways even while we destroy and pollute them . Our country roads may indeed take us home , but look in those roadside ravines and you will likely see discarded washing machines , tires , couches , etc . <p> We also have an attachment to coal that borders on cultish . Hence , we live in the land of climate-change denial . It certainly infects our politics , as the War on Coal slogan was a decisive factor in the Republican takeover of the state government . Powerful fossil-fuel interests have always prevailed here because they say you better let us exploit the landscape or you will have no jobs . <p> Denial is a curious phenomenon , one of the enduring traits of our species . It consists of turning one 's brain into an airtight fortress , sealing out facts and evidence , paralyzing the thinking process . How about a cascade of catastrophic weather events , mostly torrential rains , which bring deadly and destructive flooding ? Seeing one 's car float down a raging torrent that was once a street makes a rather strong impression . And it would make a greater impression if you were occupying the car at the time . How about when your house slides over the hill , or the hill behind and above the house comes roaring down over and through it ? Or the road to your house slides over the hill , or the hillside slips and blocks the road with a giant gooey pile of mud and uprooted trees ? <p> I have to believe the people in charge of our roads and bridges are aware of how biblical deluges are destroying our transportation infrastructure . And the raging torrents are gnawing away the road edges until the roads themselves collapse . <p> Havoc caused by these torrential rains is a daily feature of the news . Small streams run through so many of our towns and villages . When the deluges come , these streams turn into Class A rapids , with cars and buildings careening along instead of rafts and kayaks . One town in Maryland got hit with hundred-year flood events two years in a row . And it 's getting worse . Scientists have been warning us for years that warming air holds more water . As arctic ice melts , more water enters this hotter atmosphere . Climatologists , meteorologists , NASA scientists have long been pointing out the direct link between the burning of fossil fuels , greenhouse-effect heating , and extreme weather . They patiently provide data , graphs , photographic evidence of climate change . But this all gets scorned and ignored by the deniers . <p> I assume someone is monitoring all the flood control dams around here . The large concrete and steel drains on the lakes where I fish for trout are clogged and covered with debris . What if the drains totally clog during one of these storms and the water goes up and over the dam ? Is n't this a recipe for dam failure ? Burning our old friend Mr. Coal produces fly ash which they dump all over the state in giant sludge lakes behind dodgy dams that have been known to burst . The practical effect is a gutted EPA , withdraw from the Paris Accords , and a burn baby burn energy policy . The shame is that the technology for a clean renewable energy grid already exists and could save the planet . <p> All the signs point toward even more extreme weather , getting even worse , far into the future . With this in mind , we have to upgrade our infrastructure , adapting it to catastrophic rain events . Our engineers need to design , and we need to finance , roads , culverts , bridges , dams , and drains that can handle these rains .
