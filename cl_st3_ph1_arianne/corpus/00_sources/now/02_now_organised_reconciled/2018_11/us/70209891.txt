Keeping all these moving objects from knocking into each other or into us will take further electronic wizardry . <p> All of this will come under the rubric of smart cities . The only impediment to this stunning new world of efficiency and convenience is a cyberattack that takes down the electric grid for days , weeks or longer . We 'd all be reduced to the state of primitive man without the skills of the Stone Age . <p> In its way , cyberattack is a greater threat than anything posed by the arsenals of China and Russia .
