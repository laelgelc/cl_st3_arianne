Draining and cutting hundreds of thousands of hectares of turf on an industrial scale generated desperately needed jobs and reduced dependence on oil imports for almost a century . <p> So there was some nostalgia last month when Bord na M ? na , the peat-harvesting company , announced it was closing 17 of its " active bogs " and would close the remaining 45 within seven years .
