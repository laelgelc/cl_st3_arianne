<p> You 'd be hard-pressed to find a better name for a mysterious isle than Deception Island . Located in Antarctica 's South Shetland island group , it was given this memorable moniker by an American sealer named Nathaniel Palmer , who was surprised that this outwardly-looking normal island was in fact a horseshoe-shaped ring of rock surrounding a flooded bowl . <p> No-one knew it at the time , but that type of island is frequently the remnants of a rather explosive type of volcanic eruption : enormous volumes of magma rush out from hellish depths , and the rocky roof of the island -- no longer supported by buoyant magma and ripped to pieces by the blast -- falls in on itself , forming a cauldron-like shape , or caldera . <p> Volcanologists know that Deception Island was once the site of a cataclysmic eruption ; after all , it left a 7-kilometre ( 4.3-mile ) wide hole in the middle of the island , which takes a considerable amount of energy to carve out . Notably , however , no-one was sure exactlywhenit happened .
