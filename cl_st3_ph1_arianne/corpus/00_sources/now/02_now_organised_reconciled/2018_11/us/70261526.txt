" We have seen sub-national and local governments stepping up and taking forceful commitments , for example , at the recent Global Climate Action Summit in San Francisco . While it is increasingly recognized that urban planning is critical , in many places planning capacity is lacking . " <p> For Otto , what is needed is a " planning revolution " that yields strategically structured , compact cities with mixed-use neighbourhoods and buildings , and with an emphasis on integrated urban systems . Some countries , such as Britain , Denmark , France , Germany , and Norway , are planning to ban the sale of new diesel and petrol cars in the next few decades .
