Canada 's push to be a world leader in the fight against climate change may be hampered by its distinction for producing the most greenhouse gas emissions per person among the world 's 20 largest economies.THE CANADIAN PRESS/Nathan Denette <h> Expert to speak on extinction of species and humanity 's peril in Kelowna <p> UBC Okanagan is hosting a global-change expert who will discuss the extinction of species and humanity 's peril next week . <p> Conservation ecologist Corey Bradshaw , professor at Flinders University , comes from an eclectic background . Growing up as the son of a trapper in Canada , he had the opportunity to form a unique view of the environment . From his childhood experiences , he learned that without intact environmental functions , precious resources quickly degrade or disappear .
