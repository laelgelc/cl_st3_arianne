The Western Pacific nation has joined Hawaii in banning sunscreens containing chemicals that scientists say harm coral reefs.CreditCreditItsuo Inouye/Associated Press <p> SYDNEY , Australia -- The Western Pacific nation of Palau has become the first country to ban many kinds of sunscreen , in a move to protect its coral reefs from chemicals that scientists say cause significant damage . <p> Under the ban , which will take effect in 2020 , " reef toxic " sunscreen -- defined as containing one of 10 prohibited chemicals , a list that could grow later -- can be confiscated from tourists when they enter the country , and retailers who sell it can be fined up to $1,000 . <p> Palau passed the ban into law last week .
