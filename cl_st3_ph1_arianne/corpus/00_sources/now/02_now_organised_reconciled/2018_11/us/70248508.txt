<h> Clarke 's view : Of birds and windmills <p> As I See It Jack Clarke <p> Nov 17 , 2018 <p> President Trump recently criticized " windmills " as a source of energy , claiming : " They kill so many birds . You look underneath some of those windmills , it 's like a killing field . " All this while he attempts to prop up a fading coal industry that is responsible for killing 24 times as many birds as wind energy . <p> But do they really " kill so many birds ? " <p> It is one of the most commonly repeated criticisms of wind power -- that they are giant Cuisinarts for birds . <p> Last winter , Trump 's Interior Department Secretary Ryan Zinke told an oil and gas industry audience that wind farms kill 750,000 birds a year . Yet his own Fish and Wildlife Service put the estimate at less than half that number . Meanwhile , Zinke is doing away with century-old protections under the Migratory Bird Treaty Act . <p> Estimated bird deaths from wind turbines are small when compared to other human-caused sources of avian mortality . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a result of encounters with a variety of hazards ranging from domestic cats to building glass , turbines are a much smaller risk . <p> Climate change produces warmer temperatures that alter the length of seasons , interrupting traditional migration patterns . It also causes accelerated sea level rise and stronger ocean storms which wreak havoc on coastal bird habitats , drowning out the nesting and foraging areas for species such as the federally-protected roseate tern and piping plover . <p> The effects of climate change on birds will become even more severe unless we reduce our over-dependence on fossil fuels which are clogging the planet 's atmosphere and heating up the planet . <p> We can do this by increasing conservation and efficiency , and producing clean energy . Wind energy is now among the most cost-effective , competitive , and reliable clean technologies available . <p> Today , the American wind energy industry is primarily comprised of land-based turbines . In comparison , the US has just five operating structures , planted in the sea bed off Block Island , R.I. <p> Bay State voters support offshore wind , as a WBUR/MassINC September poll showed that an overwhelming 80 percent of those tallied think we should rely more on wind for our electricity . <p> Consistent with this opinion , Beacon Hill lawmakers recently passed legislation that will hopefully result in at least 3,200 megawatts of offshore wind energy as we work to reduce our reliance on dirty fossil fuels and reduce greenhouse gas emissions . <p> In response , the Vineyard Wind company aims to build America 's first industrial-scale wind farm 35 miles south of Cape Cod . Its 80 to 100 turbines would remove 2 million tons per year of heat-trapping carbon dioxide , in addition to thousands of tons of poisonous nitrogen and sulfur oxides .
