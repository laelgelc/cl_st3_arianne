<h> Climate change protesters block off 5 London bridges <p> LONDON ( AP ) - Hundreds of protesters have turned out in central London and blocked off the capital 's main bridges to demand the government take climate change seriously . <p> A group called " Extinction Rebellion " encouraged sit-ins on the bridges Saturday as part of a coordinated week of action across the country . <p> Metropolitan Police said emergency vehicles were hampered from getting across London because of the " blockade " of five bridges . The force said it had asked all protesters to congregate at Westminster Bridge where officers can facilitate lawful protest . <p> About two dozen people were arrested on Monday after protesters blocked traffic and glued themselves to gates outside the Department for Business , Energy and Industrial Strategy . <p> Copyright 2018 The Associated Press .
