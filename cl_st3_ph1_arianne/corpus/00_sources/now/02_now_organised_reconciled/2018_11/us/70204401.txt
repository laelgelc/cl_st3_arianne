The British Geological Survey detected the earthquakes , which were classed as a " red " event on the Oil and Gas Authority 's traffic light monitoring system . <p> The process of hydraulic fracturing , or fracking , involves injecting water , sand , and chemicals into bedrock formation at high pressure to fracture the rock and release the shale natural gas or oil below . The process is used when natural gas or oil is dispersed throughout a larger area , rather than concentrated in one location . <p> Under current UK legislation , fracking firms must temporarily halt operations if an earthquake of a magnitude greater than 0.5 on the Richter scale is recorded .
