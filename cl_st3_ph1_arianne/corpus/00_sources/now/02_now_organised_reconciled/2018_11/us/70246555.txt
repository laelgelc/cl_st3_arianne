The more land you pave , the more rain turns to surface runoff instead of soaking into the ground . If an area is connected by storm sewers , a lot of runoff can quickly come together in the same spot and pile up inconveniently . <p> A hurricane is no ordinary rainstorm , but the same problem applies .
