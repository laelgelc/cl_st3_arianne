<h> Threat of climate change requires ' revolution in how we live ' , says Bruton <h> New Minister for Climate Action wants to make Ireland a leader in its response <p> Richard Bruton : ' I want to make Ireland a leader in responding to climate change , not a follower ' . File photograph : Tom Honan <p> If Ireland is to respond adequately to the threat of climate change it will require " a revolution in how we live " , according to the new Minister for Climate Action and Environment Richard Bruton . <p> In his first statement on climate change , Mr Bruton said he was determined to make Ireland a leader in climate action , and announced he is to lead in the development of an " all of Government " plan which set out what actions every department must take in response to climate change . <p> The decisions taken now in tackling climate change would define the next century , he underlined at the climate innovation conference at Dublin Castle . " Ireland is far off course to achieve the C02 reduction targets which we have committed to . I want @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , not a follower . That will require a significant step change across government . " <p> Being a leader meant " acting now , stretching ourselves and seizing the enterprise opportunities in a low carbon economy , including the new circular and bioeconomies . Being a follower means the final costs of adjustment are much higher and opportunities much lower or completely lost " . <p> He added : " It will require a revolution in how we live . Every person , every community , every business , every home and every school will have to make changes in the way we live and work and learn . Nothing less will do if we are to make the changes that are needed to create a sustainable future for everyone . " <p> Given the scale of the challenge , there was a need for a growing political concensus on this , Mr Bruton said . " I intend to work with all parties and none , and with the Oireachtas Committee on Climate Action . We will identify actions with clear timelines and the steps needed to get there , and assign clear lines of responsibility for delivery , " he told the conference .
