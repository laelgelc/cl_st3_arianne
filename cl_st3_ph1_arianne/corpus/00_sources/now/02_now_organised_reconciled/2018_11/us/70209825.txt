<h> Peck : Time to take advantage of nature 's fall turnaround <p> To outdoors-minded folks , " turnover " and " rut " have nothing to do with undesirable working conditions . <p> But both of these changes in the natural world mean its time to get down to business with rod and bow . <p> The Madison lakes went through " turnover " this past week . Turnover is a mixing of water in highly stratified lakes that occurs each spring and fall . <p> Chilly nights cool the lake 's surface temperature . When water cools down it becomes more dense -- essentially heavier . Gravity pulls the " heavier " water down into the water column , displacing water in the thermocline and hypolimnion layers . <p> All three layers mix together , causing these fertile waters to become an uglier green than normal .
