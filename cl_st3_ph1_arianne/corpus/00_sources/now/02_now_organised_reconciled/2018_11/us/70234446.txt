<p> The near-term future of Earth is one of a warming planet , as urban expansion and greenhouse gas emissions stoke the effects of climate change . Current climate projections show that in U.S. cities temperatures are expected to rise by 2 to 7 C ( 3.6 to 12.6 F ) by the year 2099 . <p> To try and adapt to this warming and maintain livability on the planet , researchers are looking into new ways of designing and building cities with climate mitigating technologies and finding that as their predictive models increase in sophistication they are unveiling a complex interdependency of effects . <p> For example , new modelling is revealing the dynamics of climate change and urban sprawl on a more detailed level leading to new understanding of what might be in store as cities grow and regions warm .
