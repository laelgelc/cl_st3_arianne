<h> Roots of Wisdom : Give a touch of Mexico to your poinsettia <p> By Paul Rogers <h> Sunday <p> Nov 25 , 2018 at 3:01 AM <p> The season of the poinsettia has arrived ! In the plant world , there are more poinsettias grown than any other kind of potted plant . The wide recognition and use of the poinsettia during the Christmas season would lead you to believe that its needs and culture are well-understood by its users . Sadly , this is not the case . <p> Most homeowners are aware that poinsettias are a plant of southeastern Mexico . They grow as a weed in the dry , sunny , almost desert-like region . Some plant lovers may even know that poinsettias are a member of the Euphorbia family , which contains over 1,000 species found growing naturally around the world . Numerous cacti and succulents growing as trees , perennials , annuals and weeds are found within this plant group . <p> When we look for information concerning the cultural needs of a plant , the place to look first is the native environment of the plant . Certainly , the need of sun is easily understood , along with heat and dryness . <p> Full , bright light is a no-brainer . Plants require the energy of the sun to grow , and plants located in a desert-like environment would exhibit a tolerance , if not preference , for high light . The twin requirements of dryness and heat are easily guessed . Before we progress any further , a nod should be given to the more than 100 years of cultural attention and plant breeding . There has been focus paid to qualities that increase salability : longer-lasting " flowers " and an increase in the range of colors , plant shape and form , and the strength and vigor of plant leaves and stems .
