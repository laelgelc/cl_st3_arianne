<p> Exxon Mobil Corporation today announced estimated third quarter 2018 earnings of $6.2 billion , or $1.46 per share assuming dilution , compared with $4 billion a year earlier . Cash flow from operations and asset sales was $12.6 billion , including proceeds associated with asset sales of $1.5 billion . During the quarter , the company distributed $3.5 billion in dividends to shareholders . Capital and exploration expenditures were $6.6 billion , up 10 percent from the prior year . <p> Oil-equivalent production was 3.8 million barrels per day , down 2 percent from the third quarter of 2017 . Excluding entitlement effects and divestments , liquids production increased 6 percent , as growth in North America more than offset decline and higher downtime .
