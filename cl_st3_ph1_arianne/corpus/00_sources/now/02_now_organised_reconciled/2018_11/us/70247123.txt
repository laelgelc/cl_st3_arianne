<p> As of Saturday morning , the Camp Fire in Butte County , California , had burned 146,000 acres , destroyed more than 13,000 buildings , and killed 71 people . Hundreds remain missing . More than 5,000 fire personnel and 600 engines continue to fight the fire , which is just 50% contained . Smoke has been pouring south , blanketing the San Francisco Bay Area with what has become the world 's worst air quality . <p> And yet things are looking up today for PG&amp;E .
