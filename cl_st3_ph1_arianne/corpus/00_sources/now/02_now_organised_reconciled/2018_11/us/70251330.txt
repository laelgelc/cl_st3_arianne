<h> Book of the week <p> Nov 18 , 2018 <p> One of the library 's newest young adult titles is Dry by Neal Shusterman and Jarrod Shusterman . <p> The story is set in southern California , which is in the grip of an extreme drought . Alyssa and her family are making the best of it until the faucets go dry . As water becomes more and more scarce and people become more and more desperate , Alyssa and her brother will have to make some critical choices in order to survive . <p> Compelling and thought-provoking , this story takes an intense look at what could happen if nothing is done to halt the effects of climate change . This book will please dystopian fans of all ages . <p> This book is available at the Beckley branch of the RCPL . <p> -- Elizabeth Hoyle is a reference assistant at the Raleigh County Public Library <h> LATEST PHOTOS <p> First Amendment : Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion , or prohibiting the free exercise thereof ; or abridging the freedom of speech , or of the press ; or the right @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Government for a redress of grievances .
