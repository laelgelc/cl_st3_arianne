U.S. crude-oil production hit a record milestone in August , when it exceeded 11 million barrels per day for the first time , according to the federal Energy Information Administration . Oil output from one geologic region alone , the Permian Basin in West Texas , has exceeded the output of eight of the 13 members of the Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries .
