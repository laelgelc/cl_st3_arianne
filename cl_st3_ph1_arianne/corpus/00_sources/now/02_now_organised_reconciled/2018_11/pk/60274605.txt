<h> China glacier melt speeds up in ' wake-up call ' for world : Greenpeace <p> Glacier melt at major sites in western China is accelerating fast , Greenpeace said on Tuesday , prompting thousands to evacuate in recent months and underscoring the vulnerability to climate change in a " wake-up call " for the world . <p> Satellite analysis showed that the rate of retreat at glaciers in China 's remote western regions had more than doubled , Greenpeace said , with the annual rate at the Tianshan Glacier No. 1 in Xinjiang rising from an average of 5,000 square metres between 1962 and 1986 to 10,600 square metres between 1986 and this year . <p> " This is a wake-up call for China and the world , " said Greenpeace 's climate and energy campaigner Liu Junyan . " Glaciers in China supply water to 1.8 billion people , and they 're melting , fast . In just the last few months , thousands of people have been evacuated from their homes due to threats of flooding . "
