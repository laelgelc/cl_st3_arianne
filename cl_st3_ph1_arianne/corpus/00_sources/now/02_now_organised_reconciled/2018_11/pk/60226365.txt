<h> ' A World of Tomorrow Reimagined ' enthrals Karachi audience <p> KARACHI : The second and final day of ' A World of Tomorrow Reimagined ' enthralled Karachi audience with a medley of engaging discussions and critical points to ponder . <p> On Sunday , November 4 , 2018 , the Beach Luxury Hotel saw a steady flow of visitors from 11:00 a.m. onwards , building up to a crescendo by the afternoon and evening . <p> More than 25 sessions ran through the day , featuring both veteran and contemporary powerhouses of their respective industries . High profile political figures included Federal Minister for Information and Broadcasting , Mr Fawad Chaudhry , who spoke about the importance of educating people to differentiate between facts and fake news and Provincial Minister of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa ( KPK ) for Finance Mr Taimur Khan Jhagra who proposed educational reforms to address differences and disparities in learning . <p> Even as the ambit of the discussions was extremely wide-ranging , powerful sessions showed significant shifts in the audience 's perception and opinions based on the event 's pre- and post-session polling . Sensitive topics were dealt with care , yet discussions remained candid and led to awareness-raising that included advice from experts , shared personal experiences , and evidence-based information . <p> Other areas covered were issues related to the importance of preserving intangible cultural heritage , the growing interest and engagement with eSports , ethics and legalities of Artificial Intelligence ( AI ) , students ' own hopes , fears and dreams about the future , changes in the nature of work and what it means to be a digital nomad and , the need for us to accept the reality that is climate change and what we can do about it . <p> There were also open mic sessions on preparing children for an unknown future , and ' separating the self from the selfie ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The day ended with packed halls for a session entitled ' Television : The New Cinema ' , stand-up comedy with Shafaat Ali , and a cultural dance performance . <p> Visitors were spoilt for choice between fascinating and pertinent topics , as well as amazing speakers . If meaningful engagement around issues that affect us all , no matter what our individual differences may be , has the power to alter the shape of the future for the better , A World of Tomorrow Reimagined has certainly been a step in the right direction . <p> Popular media personalities also participated including Adnan Malik , Sanam Saeed , Sarmad Khoosat , Khalid Malik , Khalid Ahmed , Tina Sani and Alyy Khan , to name but a few . There were also experts from the fields of climate and water research , education , clinical psychology , music , and journalism , among other areas .
