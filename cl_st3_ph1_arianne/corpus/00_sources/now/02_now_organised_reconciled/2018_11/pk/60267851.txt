<h> 200,000 mangroves planted <h> Karachi <h> Share <p> As part of a " Rung Do Pakistan " campaign , the WWF-Pakistan and Pakistan Telecommunication Company Limited ( PTCL ) completed their mangrove plantation drive in which 200,000 saplings of three species of mangroves -- Ceriops tagal , Rhizophora mucronata and Avicennia marina -- were planted in Sonmiani , Lasbela district , Balochistan . <p> Sonmiani 's local community contributed to this drive by establishing a mangroves ' nursery at the site . The initiative aims to not only help restore the lost mangrove forest cover but also build resilience of local communities to tackle climate change and other natural disasters , particularly soil erosion and coastal flooding in the area . <p> It also aims to mitigate the adverse impacts of climate change , regulate water cycles and increase the green cover across the country .
