<p> ISLAMABAD : The Executive Committee of the National Economic Council ( ECNEC ) on Wednesday approved mega projects related to social sector development including energy generation , water reservoirs and communication . <p> The meeting held here with Finance Minister Asad Umar in the chair approved development projects in various areas of the country , says a press release . <p> ECNEC discussed and accorded approval of the project for evacuation of power from hydro power projects of Suki Kinari , district Mansehra in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , Kohala district , Muzaffarabad and Mahal , district Bagh in Azad Jammy and Kashmir at a cost of Rs.79,929.73 million . <p> The main objective of the project is construction of 500 KV transmission network to provide interconnection facilities for evacuation of electricity from the above mentioned projects being constructed under CPEC . <p> The ECNEC discussed and approved Sindh Solar Energy Project ( SSEP ) at a cost of Rs. 12,848.11 million . The project aims to support the scale up of solar power in Sindh province and increase access to electricity .
