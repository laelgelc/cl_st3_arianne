<h> SOC Films launches second part from Climate Change Animated Series <p> After launching Pakistan 's first Climate Change Animated Series last month in honor of International Climate Change Awareness Day , SOC Films has launched the second animated short film from the series . <p> The second part highlights the science behind global warming , the rising sea levels and the pollution that is caused by products human beings consume . <p> The animated series has been conceptualized , produced and directed by SOC Films , led by Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy , and is a public awareness campaign that aims to educate people about the importance of climate change and global warming . Each short film from the series is in Urdu with English subtitles and is two to three minutes long . <p> " Understanding the science behind global warming is the first step towards change , " Sharmeen Obaid-Chinoy wrote on her official Instagram handle . <p> " In this second part to our 5-part series , we explore why our sea levels are rising and the pollution caused by the products we consume . SOC Films would like to join hands with those seeking to create @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ releasing these animated films in Urdu to inform everyone about the small steps we can take as individuals to make a positive collective change , " she added .
