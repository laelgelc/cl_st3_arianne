<h> ' Focus on rural health centres crucial for achieving MDGs ' <p> Punjab Minister for Health Dr. Yasmin Rashid while emphasizing the need of concrete and long lasting health policy has said that focus on rural health centers was crucial for achieving Millennium Development Goals ( MDGs ) . <p> Dr. Yasmin Rashid noted that Pakistan was facing lack of health facilities , particularly rural areas were badly neglected in the past . She said that conducting such workshop was a milestone in engineering new health policy . <p> " Pakistan is facing double burden of diseases , the burden is higher in the poor , and many of these condition can be controlled at relatively low cost intervention and best practices through primary and secondary care level , " she added . <p> Moreover , the Provincial Minister that said health system faces challenges of vertical service delivery structure in low performance accountability within the government , creating efficiency and quality issues . <p> " Largely unregulated for quality care and pricing there was also duplication of services for the private sector , also the public sector is inadequately staffed and job satisfaction and work environment needs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ suggested that pyramid of medical facilities should be reversed and time has come to focus on rural areas with a fresh political will . " Prime Minister Imran Khan always worried about health facilities and he wanted to start the process of change with the improvement of health facilities " said that Minister . <p> She also mentioned the complications due to climate change and regretted that man himself was responsible for disturbing the Mother Nature .
