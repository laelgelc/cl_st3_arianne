<p> According to World Wildlife Fund ( WWF ) statistics , Pakistan 's deforestation rate was the highest in Asia whereas under Bonn Challenge , Pakistan made a commitment to increase 2 per cent of its forest resources . <p> The data reveals , " It is estimated that a single tree planted absorbs atmospheric carbon dioxide at an average of 50 pounds a year . " <p> Based on WWF calculations from watershed projects , a patch of trees can recharge up to 0.96 million litres of water per year per hectare . <p> A single mature tree can release enough oxygen back into the atmosphere to support 1.4 per cent of the population of Pakistan , it added . <p> Talking to APP , an official of Ministry of Climate Change , on anonymity , informed that the initiatives were going on across the country without any foreign financial assistance rather the country was utilizing her own resources to mitigate the climate change impact in the region . <p> To a question , he said the saplings planted under the massive Clean and Green initiative would sustain during the winter season as it was a natural process @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the areas . <p> He however , rebutted the concerns made by the people as it the frost falling at nights in the winter would damage the sapling at its very initial stage , adding in cold season mostly plants wither and they in spring and favourable conditions revitalize . <p> The plantation season in the country prevails mostly in Monsoon and Spring season whereas the plantation drives would propitiously add sustainable results as it has taken the entire nation on board , he added . <p> He added that the Billion tree plantations and clean and green initiatives were acclaimed by international agencies as the country was serving the global cause of climate change mitigation and risk management within its limited available resources .
