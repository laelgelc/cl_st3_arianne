On current trends , temperatures are on track to rise roughly 4C by the century 's end , a scenario that would tear at the fabric of civilisation , scientists say . <p> To hold the rise in Earth 's temperature to 1.5C above the preindustrial benchmark , such efforts would have to increase fivefold .
