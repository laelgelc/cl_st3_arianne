<h> Access to information : NHA launches mobile application <p> State Minister for Communications Murad Saeed Sunday said in order to ensure right of access to information , the National Highways Authority has launched at mobile app enabling the road users to obtain information regarding details of NHA projects , their status , CPEC routes , road safety guidelines , toll plazas and information relating to motorways police . <p> Addressing the launching ceremony of the mobile app held at the NHA headquarters , Murad Saeed said after coming into power , the targets set for the Ministry of Communications and its attached departments have almost been achieved . He said transparency demands placing all information before the people and NHA 's mobile app will pave the way to bringing about transparency in road building schemes . <p> The application allows users to get information about ongoing projects with project name , source of funds , current progress , contact details and the project location . He said endeavors were being made to increases revenue of NHA through utilization of its right of way .
