<h> Rs124.2bn released for development projects under PSDP 2018-19 <p> ISLAMABAD : The government has released over Rs 124.2 billion under its Public Sector Development Programme ( PSDP ) 2018-19 for various ongoing and new schemes against the total allocations of Rs 675 billion . <p> The released funds include Rs 67.74 billion for federal ministries and Rs 18.6 billion for special areas , according to a latest data released by Ministry of Planning , Development and Reform . <p> Out of these allocations , the government released Rs 32.38 billion for National Highway Authority out of total allocation of Rs 185.2 billion , whereas Rs 3.137 billion have been released for NTDC and PEPCO for which an amount of Rs 33.36 billion was allocated under PSDP 2018-19 . <p> Similarly Rs 2.78 billion have been released for Communication Division ( other than National Highway Authority ) for which the government has earmarked Rs 13.97 billion under PSDP 2018-19 .
