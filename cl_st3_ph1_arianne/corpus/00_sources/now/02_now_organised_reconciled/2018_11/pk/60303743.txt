<h> British PM heads for G20 in Argentina trip first <p> LONDON : Theresa May left Thursday for the G20 summit in Argentina in what will be the first-ever visit by a British prime minister to Buenos Aires . <p> The trip should give May the chance to explain the Brexit divorce deal to other global leaders and set out Britain 's plans to engage with the wider world . <p> " She will deliver the message that as we leave the EU , the UK will be an active and open global trading partner , " her spokesman told reporters . <p> She will also set out Britain 's commitment to tackling climate change . <p> However , she faces risks in leaving the domestic battlefield in the middle of a fight to stop the Brexit deal collapsing into chaos -- which could trigger her downfall . <p> The chance to appear on the global stage would normally be a welcome distraction but the G20 comes at an awkward time for May . <p> She has limited days left to convince a hostile parliament to back the agreement she has struck with Brussels -- with anti-deal Brexiteers in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on December 11 . <p> British premiers on foreign visits are customarily dogged by questions about domestic issues back home . <p> She will be the first serving British premier to visit the Argentine capital . The only previous visit a British prime minister ever made to Argentina was in 2001 when Tony Blair crossed the border from Brazil for two hours . <p> Relations between Britain and Argentina , who went to war in 1982 over the Falkland Islands , have improved since the election of centre-right President Mauricio Macri in December 2015 . <p> Ties soured under his nationalist leftist predecessor Cristina Kirchner and her emotional demands for control of Britain 's Falklands overseas territory . <p> Facundo Rodriguez , an Argentinian international lawyer specialised on the Falklands sovereignty dispute , told AFP : " Theresa May 's is a relevant visit that will strengthen the new but not novel way the local administration has adopted to build a relationship with the UK . " <p> The visit follows Tuesday 's announcement of a second monthly air link between the Falklands and Argentina .
