<h> Wheat sector stakeholders conference held <p> LAHORE : The Punjab government in collaboration with Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) of United Nations and World Bank ( WB ) jointly organized " Punjab Wheat Sector Stakeholders Conference " under the World Bank 's financed programme of Strengthening Markets for Agriculture and Rural Transformation ( SMART ) here at P&amp;D Complex on Tuesday . <p> The stakeholders discuss the ways and means to move forward for serving the interests of all . <p> In his opening remarks at the conference , Punjab P&amp;D Chairman Habib-ur-Rehman Gilani said wheat sector had great importance for Punjab economy for which agriculture could be an engine for future growth by shifting towards high value-added commodities , boosting export revenue and creating jobs , raising productivity &amp; future-proofing the sector in terms of water availability and climate change . <p> Gilani added that modernising wheat sector was a key part of the SMART Punjab Programme launched earlier this year . " SMART is a coherent package of policy change , institutional strengthening and reorientation of public investments in agriculture over the next five years that is designed to increase farm productivity , incomes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ development . SMART Punjab is supported by a US $300 million Programme-for-Results loan from the World Bank , " he added . <p> Provincial Food Secretary Shoukat Ali said the Punjab Food department was committed to assist food security in the province . " Achieving the goals , we have to formulate sustainable policies , " he added . <p> The Punjab Food department must look at options to modernise the wheat sector including a revision of existing policies that were designed more than half a century ago , he said and asserted that discussion with stakeholders was an important input towards getting this right , coupled with support from FAO and the World Bank , they were preparing a note in the next month on how can wheat reserves gradually reduce to sustainable levels whilst fulfilling the core objectives . <p> Dmitry Prikhodko from FAO Investment Centre , and a leading expert on wheat markets and policy , said that international experience had shown that moving to a more market-oriented wheat marketing system benefited all through raising productivity , incomes and quality , thus reinforcing the sectors ' growth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Modernising the sector could be ensured through commitment and stakeholders working together , he said and welcomed the Punjab Food department 's careful reflection and consensus building approach as a key step in successfully moving forward . <p> He advised that there was a need to reinforce the PFD 's ability to monitor evolving markets , ensuring that there was sufficient liquidity for farmers , and to review the existing policy set to ensure that it could cope with any future situation and provide a basis for a modern wheat sector to thrive and develop , as the maize sector in Punjab already had . <p> Hans Jansen from the World Bank set out how the SMART programme was a comprehensive package designed to encourage positive change in agriculture sector in Punjab , and welcomed the Punjab government 's willingness to address long standing but now largely outdated policies , such as the wheat procurement policy , that had hindered a much needed structural change in Punjab 's agriculture sector .
