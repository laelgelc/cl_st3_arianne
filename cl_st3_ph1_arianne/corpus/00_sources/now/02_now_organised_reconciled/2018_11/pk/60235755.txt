<p> KP 's Energy and Power Department has signed a deal with the South Korean company to build a 496MW Hydel Power project . The South Korean company Korea Hydro and Nuclear Power ( KHNP ) will collaborate with Pakistan to build the 496 megawatts Spat Gah Hydropower project in the Kohistan district . The project will be set up through a Public-Private Partnership ( PPP ) mode . <p> Chief Minister KP Mahmood Khan spoke at the signing ceremony of this project and said that foreign investment in this sector in KPK is a welcoming step .
