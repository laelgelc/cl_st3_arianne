<p> The Senate was informed today that government has increased import duties to cut imports . <p> Adviser on Commerce Abdul Razzaq Dawood told the house during question hour that import substitution policy has also been launched in order to increase domestic production and exports . <p> He said duties on raw material will be reduced in order to support and strengthen the manufacutring sector . <p> He said oil import substitution is a long term policy . He said refining capacity of the country has reduced over the years , due to which value added petroleum products are also being imported . He said once the refining capacity improves , the situation could be changed . <p> Minister of State for Climate Change , Zartaj Gul told the House that government has embarked on a plan to replicate successful model of Billion Tree Tsunami project all over the country . <p> She said government has a target of planting ten billion saplings in its campaign . The Minister said provinces have been asked to prepare PC-1s to set their targets and records of planting the trees . <p> Zartaj Gul said Environmental Protection Agency is assessing the rate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ other major cities and taking measures to overcome it . <p> She said Juniper trees in Balochistan are a great asset for the province and these will be planted more under Green Pakistan program . <p> Speaking on a Point of Order , Senator Raza Rabbani said accountability should be across the board and not one sided . He suggested setting up a Federal Accountability Commission , in which all stakeholders including politicians , bureaucrats and judiciary be members and it carry out accountability for all . <p> Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs Ali Muhammad Khan said government keeps the Senate in high esteem and will always ensure sanctity of the House . <p> He said Prime Minister Imran Khan is very clear that he will not tolerate any corrupt person in his cabinet . <p> The House also passed the Elections ( Amendment ) Bill , 2018 with consensus , aimed at amending the Election Act , 2017 . <p> The bill was moved by Senator Azam Swati on behalf of Minister of State for Parliamentary Affairs Ali Muhammad Khan .
