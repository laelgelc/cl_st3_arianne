<h> Government increased import duties to cut imports : Senate informed <p> The Senate was informed on Friday that government has increased import duties to cut imports . <p> Adviser on Commerce Abdul Razzaq Dawood told the house during question hour that import substitution policy has also been launched in order to increase domestic production and exports . <p> He said duties on raw material will be reduced in order to support and strengthen the manufacturing sector . <p> He said oil import substitution is a long term policy . He said refining capacity of the country has reduced over the years , due to which value added petroleum products are also being imported . He said once the refining capacity improves , the situation could be changed . <p> Minister of State for Climate Change , Zartaj Gul told the House that government has embarked on a plan to replicate successful model of Billion Tree Tsunami project all over the country . <p> She said government has a target of planting ten billion saplings in its campaign .
