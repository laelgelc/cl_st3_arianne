<p> SAARC Chamber of Commerce and Industry ( SAARC CCI ) concluded its 76th Executive Committee Meeting ( ECM ) on November 10 , 2018 in Chennai , India under the chair of Ruwan Edirisinghe , President SAARC CCI . The meeting succeeded in chalking out the way forward , under President 's vision to be the world 's best Chamber by 2030 . Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce &amp; Industry ( FICCI ) hosted said meeting at Raddison Blu Hotel . <p> The SAARC CCI is an apex business organization of SAARC dedicated to promote regional economic integration through creating strong business linkages among the entrepreneurs of South Asia . <p> The house finalized the 77th Executive Committee &amp; General Assembly meeting in Kathmandu , Nepal , along SAARC Investment Forum and SAARC Trade Fair in Feb 2019 .
