<h> Smog mitigation demands public attention <p> ISLAMABAD : The atmospheric mixture of smoke and fog the two environmental opposites respectively have brought unprecedented results causing serious health complications leading to human deaths and ecological degradation however Smog mitigation in its real form demands public attention . <p> Talking to APP , Rabia Naeem an Environment Sciences student said that the smog is an all over the year process that becomes visible due to temperature inversions . <p> In summer season , she added that under normal circumstances temperature decreases as height increases . <p> In case of a thermal or temperature inversion , the opposite is true : from a certain height temperature increases again and becomes higher than the temperature in lower layers . <p> The air pollution , which is normally diluted vertically , is now contained by this temperature inversion , she added . " The inversion layer acts as a kind of blanket that blocks air pollution . <p> Thermal inversion often occurs during winter , when we are under the influence of a high pressure system , in which downward air currents are present , " she added . <p> She informed that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ effects of smog where youth of 20-year age died of lungs cancer which had grown huge concerns of environmentalists and health experts on the lurking threat in the region . <p> " There is no either or situation in the country , no matter how small contributors we are to the global pollution but the truth is that Pakistan is under direct impact of climate change . <p> It is not only the responsibility of the state and concerned authorities rather the entire society has to play its role in mitigating climate change particularly Smog , " she remarked . <p> The ongoing Prime Minister 's Clean and Green initiative and Billion tree tsunami implemented in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa were necessary and right decisions taken at suitable time , she said . <p> She said , " It is heartening to see people burning garbage , fallen leaves and plastic to warm hands in the cold weather despite the fact that it directly synthesizes or facilitates Smog breeding into the atmosphere " . <p> There should be strict punishments and condemnations over this act to make people realize the sensitivity of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , adding a massive awareness campaign on the part of media should be initiated create awareness among the hazards of Smog , she said .
