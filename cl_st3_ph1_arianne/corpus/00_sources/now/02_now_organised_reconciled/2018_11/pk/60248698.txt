<h> Grey Gorals reach near extinction in Margalla Hills <h> Islamabad <h> Share <p> Islamabad : Margalla Hills National Park ( MHNP ) forming the foothills of Himalaya is nurturing a world of biodiversity amid extinct species of grey gorals with existing barking deer strolling in the woods of different hiking trails embracing the sunshine and early morning visitors of the park . <p> Talking to this agency , Islamabad Wildlife Management Board ( IWBM ) Deputy Director , Mumtaz Alam Khan said IWBM is exclusively preserving the national park which sprawls over 17,000 hectares and over 12,000 acres ranging from Taxila to Bara Kahu . <p> He said MHNP is home to diverse species of flora and fauna which is now facing the extinction of grey goral species which were ruthlessly hunted .
