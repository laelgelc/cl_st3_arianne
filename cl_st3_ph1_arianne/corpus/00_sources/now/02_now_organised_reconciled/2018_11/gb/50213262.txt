<h> Opinion : What 's scarier than climate change ? We can think of someone ... <p> In the wake of the publication of the United Nations Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) report on the looming climate change ' catastrophe ' , US President Donald Trump has suggested that leading scientists on the matter have a ' political agenda ' . <p> Backtracking from his earlier views , in which he stated that he believed global warming was a ' hoax ' , Trump has now acknowledged the existence of climate change , while simultaneously proposing that such effects on the planet could ' change back ' . For some , these views seem to indicate a change of heart for Trump , who just over a year ago withdrew from the Paris Agreement , which aimed to " strengthen the global response to the threat of climate change by keeping a global temperature rise this century well below two degrees Celsius " . <p> The evidence for the existence of climate change caused by human activity is overwhelming ; according to NASA , the planet 's temperature has risen by " about 0.9 degrees Celsius @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the warming taking place in the last 35 years . 2016 was the hottest year on record , and eight of 12 months were the warmest ever recorded for their respective month . Alongside this is the warming of the oceans , rising sea-levels , shrinking ice-sheets , glacial retreats , decreased snow cover and declining Arctic sea ice . <p> While it is true that Trump 's views have progressed from his initial denial of the existence of climate change , his stance on action has not . During a spell of particularly cold weather last December he tweeted that " perhaps we could use a little bit of that good old Global Warming . " Such refusal to make decisive action , according to the UN , could have disastrous consequences , especially considering that leading scientists have declared there are only 12 years to reduce the effects of global warming to 1.5 degrees , and that any higher would lead to ' disastrous ' results for the planet . <p> Arguably , Trump 's acceptance of the existence of climate change is a progressive step in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planet is immediate and conclusive action , and Trump has made no indication that this is his intention . In that regard , very little about his statement is truly progressive , as it makes no difference what his view is on climate change if he does not attempt to make any headway in solving these problems .
