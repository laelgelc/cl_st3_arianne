<h> MENA will remain main global supplier of oil and gas , says DNV GL <p> Created : Friday , 16 November 2018 06:45 <p> The energy transition will redefine oil and gas exploration and production activity in the lead-up to 2050 . ( Image source : Anita starzycka/Pixabay ) The world will need less energy from the 2030s onwards , but it will still require a significant amount of oil and gas in the lead-up to mid-century , according to DNV GL 's 2018 Energy Transition Outlook , an independent forecast of the world energy mix in the lead-up to 2050 <p> Energy consumption in the MENA region will continue to grow moderately and increase 36 per cent by 2050 . Growth in energy use is driven by manufacturing , buildings , and transport up to 2040 , the year when transport energy demand peaks and starts to decline . <p> In the MENA region , oil is forecast to peak in 2035 at only slightly more than its current level . Due to low-cost domestic gas reserves , the uptake of renewables starts later than in most other regions . In 2030 , onshore wind starts to grow rapidly , followed by solar PV and offshore wind . By 2050 , solar PV will be the main source of power , generating 39 per cent of total supply . Onshore wind will then be second , with a share of 28 per cent .
