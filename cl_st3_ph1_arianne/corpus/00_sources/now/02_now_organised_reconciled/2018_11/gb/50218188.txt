<h> POLICE have arrested 85 people after eco-protesters blocked five main London bridges in a " rebellion day " over climate change . <p> Activists brought traffic on Southwark , Blackfriars , Waterloo , Westminster and Lambeth bridges to a standstill during Saturday 's demos . The Extinction Rebellion protesters are demanding the government takes more measures to tackle climate change . Organisers estimate 6,000 people joined the protests , which began at 10am . <p> Police criticised Saturday 's demonstration , with four London bridges remaining blocked on Saturday afternoon , although all have since been fully reopened . <p> Metropolitan Police Superintendent Waheed Khan said : " The demonstration is having a direct impact on others across London who wish to go about their daily business -- and the emergency services from using the bridges to travel around London . <p> " Given that the organisers failed to engage with police prior to the event , we were unable to work with them around their plan and to make considerations for other Londoners . " <p> Extinction Rebellion is also calling for a " citizens ' assembly " to give people a say in environmental policies .
