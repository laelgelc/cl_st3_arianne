<h> LETTERS : Why not embrace the ' green farming ' idea ? <h> LETTERS : Why not embrace the ' green farming ' idea ? <p> IN response to Ben Boothman 's article on arable farming ( " Knowing the drill on autumn sowing " ) , I have great sympathy for UK farmers and am very aware of the precarious nature of the profession as many of my family are in the business . <p> But it is not just " fashion " to say that we should be concerned about the state of our soil , even if there has been one good year . <p> How many times each year do I drive through clouds of dust blowing across our roads ? <p> We lose from 1cm to 3cm per year of topsoil and climate change will only make things worse . Why not embrace the vision of " green farming " instead of deriding it ? <p> The use of new technologies such as precision farming , robotic harvesting and no-till planting , when used appropriately , can increase yield and profits , produce healthier crops , and substantially reduce use of fertiliser , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , but which have seen only slow uptake in this one . <p> UK farmers should be welcoming support for environmentally sound practices . Seventy per cent of the land in the UK is agricultural with about 25 per cent arable .
