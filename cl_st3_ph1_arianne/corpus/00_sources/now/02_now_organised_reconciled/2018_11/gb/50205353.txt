<h> Diocese of Salisbury becomes the first Eco Diocese in the UK <p> THE Diocese of Salisbury is the first diocese in the UK to be granted Eco Diocese Status . <p> It was awarded as part of the Eco Church Scheme , which is a nationally developed web-based survey and award scheme run by A Rocha UK . <p> The award looks at a Diocese or Parish 's green ' credentials ' in five areas : Worship and Teaching ; Buildings ; Land ; community Engagement and Lifestyle . <p> Andy Atkins , CEO of A Rocha UK the Christian based environmental charity , said : " Salisbury is the first diocese to be awarded Eco Diocese status and it 's our very great pleasure to do so . <p> " When we launched Eco Church in 2016 we had high hopes and expectations . To have got this far so quickly is humbling , exciting and such a huge encouragement that so many churches in a diocese are working together to care for God 's earth should be an encouragement to us all . " <p> Its success in achieving this national green award was announced at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ' Church in Salisbury . <p> The Bishop of Salisbury , Rt Revd Nicholas Holtam , said : " I am delighted and very encouraged that the Diocese of Salisbury is the first to win this award . As the lead bishop on the environment for the Church of England I am delighted my own diocese is first and I also hope that others will be close behind us . <p> " Our target to become an Eco Diocese was to have 50 churches registered and now have 70 with Bronze , Silver and Gold awards . And we have more churches still working towards getting their own award . <p> " It shows that as a Diocese we recognise that the care for God 's creation is at the heart of ministry and mission . The recent IPCC report on climate change and the WWF report on the destruction of biodiversity means the care of God 's earth is urgent . I hope that becoming an Eco Diocese will encourage us to do more and better .
