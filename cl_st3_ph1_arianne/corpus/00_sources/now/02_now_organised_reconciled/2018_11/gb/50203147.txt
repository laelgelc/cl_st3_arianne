<h> Dignitaries and wellwishers attend historic presidential inauguration <h> Dignitaries and wellwishers attend historic presidential inauguration <p> Hundreds of people from across Ireland descended on St Patrick 's Hall in Dublin Castle for the president 's historic inauguration . <p> A number of government ministers joined Chief Justice Frank Clarke , Garda Commissioner Drew Harris , EU Commissioner Phil Hogan , public service representatives and dignitaries from more than 25 countries to watch Michael D Higgins be sworn in as president of Ireland for a second term . <p> Michael D Higgins arriving at his inauguration ceremony in Dublin Castle as he begins another seven year term . **26;1798;TOOLONG <p> The ceremony reflected many of Michael D Higgin 's passions , including poetry , music , the Irish language and a diverse range of voices from different communities . <p> It began with homilies from Christian and Jewish religious representatives and Ahmed Hasain , from the Islamic Cultural Centre of Ireland , sang verses from the Koran . <p> A humanist minister made a speech praising the president for his inclusivity , saying : " We are grateful to live in an country that values freedom of speech and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ republic of equality is one that we share . , " to a round of applause from the audience . <p> Leo Varadkar arrives at Dublin Castle ( Niall Carson/PA ) <p> The Taoiseach also thanked those who could not be in attendance , referring the combined bands of the Defence Forces School of Music . <p> Mr and Mrs Higgins opted to showcase Irish fashion during the inauguration . <p> The president wore a suit by Dublin-based tailor Declan
