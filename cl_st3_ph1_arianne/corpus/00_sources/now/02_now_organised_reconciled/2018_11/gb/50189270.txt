<p> Legendary Northern Irish champion jockey AP McCoy is one of the contributors to an art project of 100 painted globes that go on show in Belfast today . <p> What On Earth , which saw more than 100 artists and celebrities paint or design a resin planet Earth , will be in the Titanic Quarter until Sunday in aid of the charity , GOAL . Themes include migration , climate change , gender equality , sport , music and animals . <p> McCoy worked with English artist Martin Gale on a globe called Bloodlines about the history of horses , horse racing and horses in art . <p> Belfast-based artist Colin Watson made a globe called Flight that represented war , conflict and the refugee crisis . <p> He said : " It is a reminder that humanity has not moved any closer to eliminating the causes forcing people to flee conflict and persecution . " <p> GOAL is an international humanitarian and development agency which provides food , healthcare and other basic items for poor and vulnerable people across the world .
