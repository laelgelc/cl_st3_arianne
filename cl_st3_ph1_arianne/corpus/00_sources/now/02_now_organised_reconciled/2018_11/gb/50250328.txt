<p> Is it remotely feasible to remove 12 billion tonnes of carbon dioxide from the air ? Every year . For decades to come . <p> That 's the challenge posed by the latest conclusions of the UN 's climate science panel . <p> It says that only by pulling this heat-trapping gas out of the atmosphere can we avoid dangerous climate change . <p> But according to one leading researcher , there 's a bit of a hitch : " We have n't a clue how to do it " . <p> The problem is that scientists reckon that even if the world manages to cut emissions of the gas , it will no longer be enough to avoid the worst impacts . <p> We 've got to go a step further , they say , and find ways of doing something never attempted before : get rid of the gas that 's already out there .
