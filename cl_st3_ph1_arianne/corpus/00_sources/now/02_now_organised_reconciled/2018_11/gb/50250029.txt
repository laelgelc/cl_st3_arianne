Malnutrition and starvation <p> The increased risk and severity of devastating heavy rainfall , flooding , droughts and wildfires is starting to affect peoples ' access to food , with the world 's poorest being hardest hit . Already 30 countries have seen downward trends in agricultural yields , reversing a decades-long trend of improvement .
