Has art grown too obscure and could it ever be instructive to scientific advancement ? <p> To answer these questions Dr Tristram Hunt ( Director , Victoria and Albert Museum ) and Sir Paul Nurse ( Director , Francis Crick Institute ) went head-to-head in a special debate which was recorded for BBC Radio Three 's Free Thinking programme .
