<h> Gas leaks : methane and fossil fuels <p> Not just the preserve of flatulent cows , methane is causing problems on a much wider scale discovers Marco Magrini <p> Odourless , colourless and ruthless . In its structural simplicity ( one carbon atom surrounded by four hydrogen atoms ) , methane can be a real pain in the neck . The gas is naturally generated by the fermentation of organic matter , such as pig manure , but also comes from sources as disparate as rice fields and cattle belch . As a greenhouse gas , it is at least 80 times more potent than carbon dioxide , while the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change estimates that it has been responsible for 20 per cent of global warming since pre-industrial times . <p> In industrial times the worst emissions have taken place . Along with humanity 's excessive craving for pig and cow meat , the very fossil fuels that are responsible for carbon dioxide emissions have spewed out massive amounts of methane too . It escapes from oil operations too , where it is often burned-off on site -- so-called ' flaring ' . ' Roughly half of the methane the oil and gas industry is emitting could be cut at no cost , ' recently claimed Fatih Birol , head of the International Energy Agency . <p> With an added cost , it could be restrained almost completely . In oil wells in which hydrogen sulphide is present -- an odourless , colourless gas that is much more deadly , killing people as they inhale -- the oil industry has developed zero leak technology .
