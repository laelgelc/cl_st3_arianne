<p> Heatwaves in the UK are lasting twice as long as they did 50 years ago and tropical nights are on the rise , a Met Office report has revealed . <p> The duration of warm spells , when temperatures are well above average for the time of year , have more than doubled , the new data showed . <p> The Met Office 's report , which focuses on weather extremes , also suggests the coldest days in Britain are not as cold as the climate continues to change . <p> The duration of warm spells have more than doubled between the periods 1961 -1990 and 2008 to 2017 , the report which tracks trends since the 1960s said . <p> Sunbathers relax on Hampstead Heath in London ( AFP/Getty Images ) <p> Scorching summer days are getting hotter , with the hottest day of each year in the most recent decade on average 0.8C warmer than each year 's hottest days in the period 1961-1990 . <p> And the lowest temperature of the year 1.7C milder in the last decade than it was in the three decades up to 1990 .
