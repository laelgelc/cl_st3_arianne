<p> As a teacher in London who sees every day the struggles faced by children from many different backgrounds , I know how powerful it can be for them to share experiences , break down barriers and learn from each other . Seeing a world beyond their own horizons is the best future for all our children everywhere . <p> That 's why I am so very proud of what the Evening Standard 's Learn to Live campaign has achieved in such a very short time , and what it promises to achieve through its legacy . Twinning London pupils with children affected by war has been an act of bridge-building that will have a profound and lasting effect on those who have taken part . <p> When these young people speak to each other they discover how different yet interlinked their lives are . <p> Four London schools have been twinned during Learn to Live , but more than 300 others across the country have signed up as the ripple effect of this initiative spreads . That 's many thousands of UK children reaching out to talk about their lives . <p> I also support this newspaper 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ back its call on governments to do more to help young people with the emotional impact of living through conflict . If we fail to help children deal with the horrors they have seen , we rob them of their future . <p> Technological connections have shrunk the world . The Standard understands this shift and the need to help bring young people across the globe together . After all , it is they who will inherit a planet that right now appears so divided and full of formidable challenges : from climate change and global inequality to poverty and terrorism . <p> This generation will need all of their collective wisdom , wherever they are , to tackle these pressing problems . Through programmes like Learn to Live , young people are understanding that a problem abroad is a problem at home . <p> Taking part in this project has been inspirational . I and my colleagues have been moved to tears listening to the stories of our students expressing their journeys . It 's been a privilege to acknowledge the reality of their past and gives us the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ better future . <p> Andria Zafirakou works at Alperton Community School .
