<p> KunstHuis Gallery opened in 2014 by curator and artistic director Cecile Creemers in the village of Crayke . <p> Originally from the Netherlands , Cecile , a former graphic designer , taught art classes , curated exhibitions and creative holidays before relocating with her family to the UK . <p> Cecile has led Kunsthuis from strength to strength , with the gallery gaining recognition as a venue for showcasing and selling contemporary art in the region . The gallery has also been nominated and shortlisted twice as finalists in the ' Welcome to Yorkshire ' White Rose Awards for Arts &amp; Culture in 2016 and 2017 . <p> Featuring a range of work by prolific British &amp; International professionals , demonstrating an assortment of mixed media . Kunsthuis brings a varied selection of work to Yorkshire , dedicated to innovative developments in contemporary art through a diverse and vibrant programme of exhibitions . <p> Shades of Clay 2018 complements Kunsthuis contemporary exhibition ( our ) eARTh 2018 which is a body of artists reflecting and responding to the Environment and Climate Change . <p> The exhibition runs until Sunday , December 23 .
