Separately , an initiative that transforms air pollution into ink is turning heads in Asia . Developed by MIT Media Lab , AIR-INK is made out of captured air pollution particulate matter -- namely the unburned carbon soot you see coming out of the exhaust pipe of cars , chimneys , generators , and more . It takes just 45 minutes worth of vehicular emissions to produce 1 fluid ounce of ink , which is enough to fill one of the pens being offered through the inventors ' Kickstarter campaign .
