<p> Airing on BBC1 on Sunday nights at 8.30pm , this five-part programme follows on from the hugely popular Planet Earth II and Blue Planet II to explore the lives of different tribes from the animal kingdom . <p> Each episode will take a different species in a different part of the world for its subject . <h> Watch more <p> The first follows a troop of chimpanzees in the jungles of Senegal and Guinea , West Africa , and the plight of its alpha male , also named David . <p> The primate faces challenges to his leadership after three years at the helm and will have to fight to retain control .
