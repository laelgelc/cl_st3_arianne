<h> How do sunscreen products harm corals ? <p> Scientists have been raising concerns about the impacts of sunscreen products on marine life for many years . <p> They are particularly worried over the role of two ingredients called oxybenzone and octinoxate . These are used as sun protection factors as they absorb ultraviolet light . <p> However , they are believed to make coral more susceptible to bleaching . Research published in 2015 showed that the oxybenzone could stunt the growth of baby corals and was toxic to several different coral species in laboratory tests . <p> " Oxybenxzone is probably the baddest actor out of the 10 chemicals that have been banned , " said Dr Craig Downs , an expert on the impacts of sunscreens on marine life . <p> " It causes corals to bleach at lower temperatures , and it reduces their resilience to climate change .
