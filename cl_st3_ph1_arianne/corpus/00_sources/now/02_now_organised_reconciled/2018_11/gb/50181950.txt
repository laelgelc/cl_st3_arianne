<h> Government must help farmers to meet climate challenge , NFU says <p> The UK government must do more to help farmers invest in their businesses to meet the increasing challenges brought by extreme weather , according to the NFU . <p> The union 's call for more government assistance to enable farmers to invest in their businesses was made as a new Met Office study showed the UK 's climate is changing , with an increasing trend for warmer and wetter weather . <p> A report by the forecaster , published on Friday ( 2 November ) , revealed further details about the UK 's climate since the 1960s . <p> It summarises temperature and rainfall data across four periods -- 1961 to 1990 , 1981 to 2010 , 2008 to 2017 and 2017 . <p> The UK 's hottest days are an average of 0.8C warmer than between 1961 and 1990 . Other practical measures include installing grass margins to trap sediment before it reaches watercourses . <p> Soil health is another critical factor as a well-structured soil acts like a sponge and can absorb more of the heavy rain , thus reducing run-off . <p> Having less run-off will also cut the risk of flooding during periods of intense rainfall .
