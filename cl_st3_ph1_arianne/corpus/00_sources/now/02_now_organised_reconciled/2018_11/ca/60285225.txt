A study of polar bears in the Chukchi Sea between Alaska and Russia finds that the population is thriving for now despite a loss of sea ice due to climate change . Lead author Eric Regehr of the University of Washington says the Chukchi may be buffered from some effects of ice loss . Regehr says polar bears can build fat reserves and the Chukchi 's abundant seal population may allow bears to compensate for a loss of hunting time on ice .
