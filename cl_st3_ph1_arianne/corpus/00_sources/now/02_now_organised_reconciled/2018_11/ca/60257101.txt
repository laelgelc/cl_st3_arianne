<h> Township receives loan , funding of $5.7 million for wastewater plant <p> ARTHUR - Wellington North council will receive $5.7 million from the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ' Green Municipal Fund ( GMF ) to upgrade the Arthur wastewater treatment plant . <p> The announcement was made at the plant on Nov. 13 by Marco Mendicino , parliamentary secretary to federal infrastructure and communities minister Francois-Philippe Champagne , and Berry Vrbanovic , member of the GMF and Mayor of Kitchener . <p> Representatives from FCM , members of Wellington North council and Mayor Andy Lennox were also in attendance . <p> The investment consists of a $5 million loan and a $750,000 grant from the GMF . <p> The interest rate on the loan has yet to be determined as the township and FCM need to finalize the agreement . However , both Vrbanovic and township CAO Mike Givens said it is likely to be set at the prime rate . <p> Together the investment will be used to upgrade the 30-year-old facility through a two-phase process . <p> The first phase will include an upgrade to the sewage pumping station , while the second phase will include @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to a secondary treatment unit . <p> According to Lennox
