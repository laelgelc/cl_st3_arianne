<p> A group of Independent senators is calling on the Trudeau government to do a full environmental assessment of a " dangerous " plan in Nova Scotia to take effluent from a pulp mill , pipe it 10 kilometres out into the Northumberland Strait , and dump it . <p> In the Red Chamber on Monday , Sen. Mike Duffy called it " a looming environmental crisis in the Gulf of St. Lawrence . " <p> After years of receiving industrial-scale effluent from the kraft pulp mill in Pictou , Boat Harbour Basin is scheduled to be closed by the Nova Scotia government next year . ( Kraft is a process of converting wood into wood pulp , which consists of almost pure cellulose fibers , the main component of paper . ) That means the company , Northern Pulp , must come up with a new treatment plan for its effluent . <p> Though no formal plan has been filed by the company , the proposal is to build a 10-kilometre , $19-million pipe and dump the treated sludge from the mill directly into the Strait . <p> Northern Pulp has committed to a new $70-million oxygen de-lignification @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to discharge into the Strait .
