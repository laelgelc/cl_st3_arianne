<p> After decades of commitment to the Catholic ministry and serving her community for the greater good , Sister Pauline Lally of the Sisters of Providence is now being recognized for it . <p> On Friday morning , Lally will be presented with the YMCA Peace Medal for her local work . She admitted on Monday that she 's embarrassed . <p> " I am , " Lally emphasized while speaking with the Whig-Standard at the Sisters of Providence of St. Vincent de Paul Motherhouse on Monday . " Nothing like this happens alone . There are all kinds of people with you and behind you in an award like this . ... <p> " I 'm only a symbol of a whole bunch of people . " <p> Lally has been involved in numerous social movements and causes , including a 20-year-long silent vigil every Friday night after cuts to social housing in 1995 , protesting the closure of the prison farms , promoting a living wage , and most recently for Peace Quest -- a concert and conference held over the Remembrance Day weekend in an effort to promote peace in the world .
