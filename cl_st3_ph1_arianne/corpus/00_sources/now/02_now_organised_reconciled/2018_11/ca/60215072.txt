<h> More Canadians now support a carbon tax than oppose it : poll <p> TORONTO -- A new poll released Thursday shows most Canadians support the federal Liberals ' carbon tax . <p> Since Prime Minister Trudeau unveiled his plan last week to rebate all cash from the carbon tax back to Canadians , the poll from the Angus Reid Institute shows 54 per cent of people now support the scheme . <p> That 's a nine-point increase from the last poll the firm conducted in July . But the nearly 50-50 split shows the carbon tax is still " profoundly divisive . " <p> " The attitude has gone from ' Hell , no , ' to ' OK , maybe , ' " said Shachi Kurl , head of the Institute . <p> The online survey was conducted from Oct. 24 to 29 . The company says it used a representative , randomized sample of 1,500 Canadian adults who are members of Angus Reid Forum . The firm says the poll was self-commissioned and self-funded . <p> Ontario Premier Doug Ford made axing the tax a key promise and rallying cry during the province 's spring election @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ shows support for a carbon tax has actually gone up . <p> In July , only 43 per cent of respondents supported a carbon tax .
