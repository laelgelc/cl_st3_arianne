<h> Categories <h> " Siberian Unicorn " Lived on Earth Among Ancient Humans <p> A supermassive rhino who gave birth to the unicorn myth lived on our planet , among ancient humans , until about 40,000 years ago . Called the " Siberian Unicorn , " this early rhinoceros possessed a long horn and roamed the grasslands of Eurasia . The recent study on this extinct animal helped scientists learn more about rhino extinction , hoping to save the remaining modern-day rhinos which are also on the brink of extinction . <p> " Any change in their environment is a danger for them . And , of course , what we 've also learned from the fossil record is that once a species is gone , that 's it , it 's gone for good , " said Prof Adrian Lister of the Natural History Museum , London , for BBC News . <p> Weighing about 4 tons , the " Siberian Unicorn , " scientifically dubbed " Elasmotherium sibericum , " lived on Earth among ancient humans and vanished about 40,000 years ago .
