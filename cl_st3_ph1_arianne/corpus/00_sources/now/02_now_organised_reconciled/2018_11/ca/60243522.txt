<h> Woman sharing story of street drug dangers after overdose , boyfriend 's death <p> A Regina woman is speaking out about the dangers of street drugs after her own overdose and the death of her boyfriend . <p> Kyla Dunbar is still adjusting to her life without her boyfriend , John Lubenow . He died in October after what Dunbar says was a heroin overdose . <p> " It 's hard on me , it 's hard on his family and it 's hard on his friends , " she said . <p> Lubenow died while Dunbar was in the hospital after overdosing on what she thought was heroin for the fourth time . While she was in the hospital , her boyfriend was home alone . <p> " He was at home and I guess he took too much of the heroin , and he overdosed , " she said . <p> He was taken to hospital , where he died on Oct. 9 . <p> Dunbar said she and Lubenow believed they were only taking heroin .
