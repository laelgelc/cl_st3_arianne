<p> Callander , Cobalt , West Nipissing and Temiskaming Shores are among eight Northern communities where sewage has polluted local waterways . <p> The information was released earlier this month by Ontario environmental commissioner Dianne Saxe in a report titled Back to Basics . <p> The report looked at the state of the province 's waterways between April 2017 and March of this year . <p> During that time , the report found that raw sewage overflowed into southern Ontario waterways 1,327 times . More than half of those overflows -- 766 -- were from nearly 60 outdated municipal sewer systems that combine sewage and stormwater . <p> There are 44 communities identified where mixtures of untreated sewage and stormwater from combined sewers overflow or bypass directly into nearby streams , rivers and lakes . <p> According to Saxe 's report , raw sewage has been overflowing into Ontario 's lakes and rivers at an alarming rate and the province is doing little to stop it . <p> The report identifies these overflows from combined sewers as a " major water pollution hazard . <p> Their fatal flaw occurs in wet weather , when heavy rainfall or rapid snow @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and overwhelms the capacity of the sewage treatment plant . <p> Jay Barbeau , West Nipissing 's chief administrative officer , called it " unfortunate " to be included in the report . <p> " West Nipissing has several smaller systems as we service a large geographical area .
