<h> Quebec group sues federal government over climate change <p> Seven-year-old Samuel Bedard from Quebec City runs through a water fountain as he beats the heat in Montreal , on July 2 , 2018 . <p> Graham Hughes/The Canadian Press <p> A Quebec group is taking the groundbreaking step of bringing climate action into the courts by launching a legal battle against Ottawa for violating the rights of young people by failing to tackle climate change . <p> The environmental group is initiating a lawsuit on behalf of nearly 3.5 million Quebeckers at or below the age of 35 -- the cohort that , it argues , faces the most dire consequences of global warming . <p> " It 's our generation -- the children of today and future generations -- who will feel the full force of climate change , " said Catherine Gauthier , executive director of the group and the representative plaintiff in the case . <p> Story continues below advertisement <p> Environnement Jeunesse filed a motion for authorization of a class-action suit against Ottawa in Quebec Superior Court on Monday . It marks an effort to widen the efforts of activists beyond the political and policy arenas and into the courts , he said . " It 's the first time we see a broad , rights-based litigation against the federal government , trying to force it to do more . " <p> Other countries are seeing similar challenges . In the United States , 21 child and teenage plaintiffs have asked the courts to force their government to act against global warming , claiming their fundamental constitutional rights are being violated . <p> Last month , a court in The Hague upheld a landmark ruling ordering the Dutch government to cut the country 's greenhouse gas emissions after a foundation took the government to court on behalf of nearly 900 citizens in Holland . <p> The Quebec group wants to use both the federal and Quebec charters to argue that climate change violates young people 's rights .
