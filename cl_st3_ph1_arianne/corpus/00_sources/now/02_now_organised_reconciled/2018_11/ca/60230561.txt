<h> LETTER : It is time to act on climate change <p> The Western Star <p> Published : Nov 05 at 11:54 p.m . <p> Letter to the Editor <p> I have been following the science of Global Warming for several years , and now the findings of recent publications , and especially the report of the IPCC -- the UN expert panel on global warming -- point to inevitable warming to a certain disaster point in 12 years , unless ALL the world 's governments put ALL their efforts and will into solving this crisis . <p> Why am I crying about this ? <p> I am not in politics . I am not a climate scientist ( Not that the governments of this world are paying much attention to those scientists who have been warning the world for many years , that we must change our attitudes or face disaster . ) <p> I am an older man , and the increasing heat will not make much difference to me ; but it will to my children , and particularly to my grandchildren -- and yours also , who are going to be inheriting a frightening world @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The Canadian Government has recently announced a carbon tax amid a lot of criticism that it will increase citizen 's costs too much . The data certainly seems to support the idea that this carbon tax will lower emission rates . I am sure it will just a little bit -- which is all to the good -- but it is ignoring the obvious route to save our planet . <p> So , I ask . <p> 1 . How can this slight reduction in production of Carbon Dioxide by this carbon reduction legislation be used by our governments as justification , when at exactly the same time our governments -- both federal and provincial -- are encouraging further exploration and drilling and exporting of natural gas , and oil , and tar sands bitumen ; and legislating and encouraging further pipelines to export more fossil fuels to other countries . <p> 2 . Are Canada and Newfoundland and Labrador , along with Alberta , British Columbia and Saskatchewan , not responsible for the added CO2 and methane produced in China and elsewhere when Canadian oil is burned in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ international agreements . It is only the emissions produced in and by the country that burns the fuel that is accountable . <p> That may be the UN agreement , but in my view it is immoral , even if not illegal . <p> 3 . What is the government of Newfoundland and Labrador going to do to keep all the oil and gas reserves in the ground as recommended by our panels of international scientists ? <p> Is the government of Nova Scotia going to cancel the offshore drilling proposed in that province ? <p> Will Alberta and British Columbia co-operate in international agreements to lower greenhouse gases ? But to do this globally would require accepting responsibility on the transfer and export of their oil and gas . <p> And getting honest answers to these three questions is unlikely . Always we hear the same statements -- all of which are true , but none of which answer the problem . <p> The answers always state it will cost too much or the science is not yet in -- yes it is . <p> The underlying political theme @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ votes are imperilled or I 'm looking after my constituents . <p> Yes . <p> Dear Mr. or Ms. MHA or MP , you are looking after the very short-term interest of a few people today ; but you are ignoring the lives and fates of the next generations . <p> Your motto should be short term pain for long term gain , but you have reversed it . <p> It is past time for Newfoundland and Labrador and Canada , and indeed the whole world to start honestly thinking about the world we are leaving our Grandchildren . It is beginning to frighten me.
