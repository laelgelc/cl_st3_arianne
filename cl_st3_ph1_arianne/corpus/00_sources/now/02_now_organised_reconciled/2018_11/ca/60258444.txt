Small farms , organic certification , and local farms are generally accepted as guidelines for environmentally sustainable produce . But what do these considerations really mean , and how do they affect cost ? <h> Small Farms <p> Advocates of small farms argue that they allow for environmentally friendly farming practices that larger farms tend to discourage . <p> Large farms are often monocultures , meaning that fields only produce a single crop .
