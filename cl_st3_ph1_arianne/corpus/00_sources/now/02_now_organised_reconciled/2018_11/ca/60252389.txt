<h> Things not going swimmingly for Canada 's fish stocks <p> Oceana Canada has released its 2018 fishery audit , and it 's fair to say many of this country 's fish stocks are floundering . <p> This is the non-profit 's second annual report on the health of Canada 's fisheries , which also assessed efforts to maintain and rebuild them . It found that , in 2018 , only about a third ( 34 per cent ) of stocks can be considered healthy -- slightly less than in 2017 . The number of critical stocks remains the same at 13.4 per cent , while almost 16 per cent are in the " cautious " zone . <p> " They 're hovering on the brink of dropping down into that critical zone , " ? said Robert Rangeley , director of science at Oceana Canada . <p> Of Canada 's 26 critically endangered stocks -- nearly all of which are in Atlantic Canada -- only three have published rebuilding plans . <p> While there 's been some improvement , a lack of sufficient data means the status of 37 per cent of stocks remains uncertain .
