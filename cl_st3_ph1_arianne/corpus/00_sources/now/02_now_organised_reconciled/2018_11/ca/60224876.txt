<h> Fungi eats plastics and benefits environment <p> Fungi that eats plastic seems like a plot-twist right out of a sci-fi book or movie ; however , this particular species of fungus which was found at a landfill site in Pakistan could be the real life solution to the ever growing plastic waste problem . <p> The Aspergillus tubingensis , is the species of fungus that researchers have found to feed off of plastic , breaking it down in weeks rather than years . <p> This is the not the first time finding organisms that feed off of plastic waste , as there were discoveries of bacteria that could break down plastic as well as the wax worm which can naturally degrade plastic due to its similar structure to that of its natural food , beeswax . <p> One of the reasons plastic had ballooned into the human substance of choice was because it was inert and therefore sterile .
