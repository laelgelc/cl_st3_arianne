<p> HLGAAGILDA SKIDEGATE , HAIDA GWAII , BC , Nov. 19 , 2018 /CNW/ - The Gina ' Waadluxan KilGuhlGa Land-Sea-People Management Plan for Gwaii Haanas National Park Reserve , National Marine Conservation Area Reserve , and Haida Heritage Site was tabled in Parliament today . The Gwaii Haanas Land-Sea-People Management Plan , signed by the Haida Nation , Parks Canada and Fisheries and Oceans Canada , is a first of its kind -- one that sets direction for how the land and sea of a protected area are managed , from mountain top to seafloor . <p> Since 1993 , Gwaii Haanas has been cooperatively managed by the Haida Nation and the Government of Canada through the Archipelago Management Board . <p> The Gwaii Haanas Land-Sea-People Management Plan outlines the following key goals for the next 10 years : <p> Culture -- maintaining a strong and enduring Haida relationship with Gwaii Haanas , a place that has significant cultural , spiritual and economic importance . <p> Conservation -- conserving and protecting ecosystems and the diversity of terrestrial and marine species that live in Gwaii Haanas , including their ability to adapt to climate change . <p> Sustainable Use @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for other activities that are ecologically sustainable and provide social , cultural and/or economic benefits . <p> Working Together -- advancing a respectful and collaborative relationship between the Haida Nation and the Government of Canada . <p> The Land-Sea-People Management Plan integrates historic and contemporary Haida knowledge and also considers opportunities for those who depend on Gwaii Haanas for their livelihood . This will further increase appreciation and learning opportunities in Gwaii Haanas for people today and generations to come . <p> Furthermore , the Land-Sea-People Management Plan strengthens marine protection by increasing areas of strict protection from the current level of 3 percent to 40 percent . Since 1993 , the Haida Nation and the Government of Canada have worked together to protect and share this amazing place . <p> Gwaii Haanas is home to 42 species-at-risk , over 700 known archaeological sites and 20 different species of marine mammals . On average , Gwaii Haanas hosts 2500-3000 visitors annually . <p> Gwaii Haanas translates to " Islands of Beauty " in Haida . Located in the southern @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Columbia 's coast and 640 km north of Vancouver , Gwaii Haanas National Park Reserve , National Marine Conservation Area Reserve , and Haida Heritage Site encompasses more than 5,000 km2 from mountaintop to seafloor . Gwaii Haanas is renowned for its spectacular wilderness and its vibrant cultural resources as well as for the intimate connections it has between land , sea , and people . <p> Gwaii Haanas National Park Reserve , National Marine Conservation Area Reserve , and Haida Heritage Site are located 130 kilometres off the northwest coast of British Columbia and cover the southern third of Haida Gwaii . Gwaii Haanas means " Islands of Beauty " and is part of Haida territory . <p> For the last 25 years , Gwaii Haanas has been co-operatively managed by the Haida Nation and the Government of Canada through the Archipelago Management Board ( AMB ) . The AMB is responsible for all aspects of planning , management , operation and use of Gwaii Haanas . Almost 6,800 species have been documented in the area , including 42 species-at-risk and over 20 marine mammals . The archaeological record of human occupation dates back at least 14,000 years . Today , Gwaii Haanas is an important area for Haida traditional use , commercial and recreational fishing , education and tourism . In restricted access areas , visitor access is not allowed . Conditional access areas protect sensitive sites while allowing visitor access under certain conditions . The backcountry zone protects the cultural and ecological resources in Gwaii Haanas while allowing visitor access . This zone comprises over 99% of the Gwaii Haanas terrestrial area . The infrastructure zone is used for sites where existing and/or new infrastructure is , or could be , located .
