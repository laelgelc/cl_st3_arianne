<p> PARADISE , Calif. -- No one is left in Paradise . Abandoned , charred vehicles clutter the main thoroughfare , evidence of the panicked evacuation a day earlier as a wildfire tore through the Northern California community . <p> Nine people have been found dead . Entire neighbourhoods are levelled . The business district is destroyed . In one day , this Sierra Nevada foothill town of 27,000 founded in the 1800s was largely incinerated by flames that moved so fast there was nothing firefighters could do . <p> The blaze that started Thursday outside the hilly town of Paradise has grown to 156 square miles ( 404 square kilometres ) and destroyed more than 6,700 buildings , almost all of them homes , making it California 's most destructive wildfire since record-keeping began . But crews have made gains and the fire is partially contained , officials said Saturday . <p> The dead were found inside their cars and outside vehicles or homes after a desperate evacuation that Butte County Sheriff Kory Honea called " the worst-case scenario .
