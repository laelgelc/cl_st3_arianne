<p> A Portage la Prairie PCI teacher got the trip of a lifetime to the Arctic on an expedition that 's benefited her in her teaching capacity here in our city . Jessica Painter teaches grade ten geography , grade eleven leadership and she 's leading a course called Envirothon , as well as grade nine science . <p> Jessica Painter ( photo by Mike Blume ) Painter was the only teacher from Manitoba who went on the trip along with five other teachers from across Canada . She notes 125 students also came from 20 different countries . She was able to do it through a scholarship from the Teachers On Ice ( TOI ) program . <p> " They took us through Greenland , and we were supposed to go through the Canadian Arctic and end there and fly back to Ottawa , " explains Painter . " But because there was so much sea ice , we did n't get to follow that itinerary .
