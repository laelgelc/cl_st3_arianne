Hon. Justin Trudeau , Prime Minister of Canada <p> Quick Facts <p> Canada is a founding member of APEC . This forum provides Canada with an opportunity to further strengthen trade and economic ties with some of the Asia-Pacific region 's most dynamic economies . <p> In 2017 , APEC partners accounted for nearly 84 per cent of Canada 's total merchandise trade . <p> In 2017 , foreign direct investment from APEC economies in Canada was $481 billion . <p> Four of Canada 's top five trading partners are APEC members ( United States , China , Mexico , and Japan ) . <p> The APEC Business Advisory Council provides advice on business sector issues . It is composed of up to three business representatives from each of the 21 member economies appointed by APEC leaders . <p> This is Canada 's first leader to leader engagement with the Pacific Islands Forum .
