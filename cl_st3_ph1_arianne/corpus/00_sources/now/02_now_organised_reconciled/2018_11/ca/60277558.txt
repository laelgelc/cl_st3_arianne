<h> Hat trick for St. Clair River cleanup <p> There has been progress in long-standing efforts to reduce the number of beneficial use impairments of the St. Clair River . The federal and provincial governments have approved taking three items off a list of impairments to use of the Great Lakes waterway.Paul Morden / /The Observer <p> Good news about the improved health of the St. Clair River came in threes , recently . <p> Federal and provincial environment ministries have approved changing the designation of three of the river 's beneficial use impairments ( BUIs ) to no longer impaired . <p> Recent decisions by Environment and Climate Change Canada , and the Ontario Ministry of Environment , Conservation and Parks , removed beach closings , bird or animal deformities or reproductive problems , and restrictions on dredging from a long-standing list of beneficial use impairments on the Canadian side of the river . <p> " It 's really good news , " said
