<h> Environmental groups urge feds to protect caribou in Ontario <p> TORONTO -- Three environmental groups are calling on the federal government to protect boreal caribou in northern Ontario , saying a decade of mismanagement by the province has put the animals increasingly at risk . <p> The David Suzuki Foundation , Ontario Nature and the Wildlands League petitioned Environment Minister Catherine McKenna this week , requesting that she recommend federal cabinet issue a " safety net order " under the Species at Risk Act for two boreal caribou populations about 120 kilometres northwest of Thunder Bay . <p> The move would prevent damage to or destruction of 65 per cent of caribou habitats -- the amount of undisturbed land that the federal government said in a 2012 report would give the animals a 60 per cent shot at becoming self-sustaining . <p> " Caribou is an indicator of a healthy boreal forest , " said Anna Baggio , director of conservation planning for Wildlands League .
