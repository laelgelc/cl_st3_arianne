<h> Study aims to shed light on the elusive wolverine in B.C. 's south coast region <p> VANCOUVER -- A new project aims to find out more about the life and lifestyle of the ghost of the deep forests of British Columbia , the wolverine . <p> The Habitat Conservation Trust Foundation along with the Forest Enhancement Society of BC will undertake a project to study the range , movements , and habitat of wolverines in the south coast region , including Squamish . <p> Brian Springinotic of the Habitat Conservation Trust Foundation says it is important to study these creatures because human activity and climate change may be having a significant impact on them . It is also important to understand what and how far ranging those impacts are , he says . <p> " Wolverines are this iconic , elusive species in British Columbia . Wolverines capture people 's attention . There 's a lot of myth and stories about wolverines , " Springinotic said in an interview . " But we do n't know enough about the science around wolverines on the south coast . The study will include setting up of camera traps and bait stations to find out the habits and movements of the furry , ferocious predators . <p> In spite of the image that they portray , Springinotic says the animals are shy , elusive creatures that do n't seek human contact . <p> " I spend a lot of time in the woods and I 've never seen a wolverine in the wild , " he said . <p> Wolverines are found in various regions around the province , according to the provincial environment ministry 's website . The only areas of B.C. where they do not regularly occur are the Lower Mainland , the dry sections of the Fraser and Okanagan valleys in the southern Interior and the Queen Charlotte Islands . <p> Springinotic says they live in the deep forest and at high elevations . <p> He says his organization feels a moral obligation to do what it can to maintain wolverine populations so future generations of British Columbians have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " They are a real articulation of wilderness and B.C. , " he said . <p> The project to study wolverines is one of about 150 that are being funded by the foundation , which describes itself as a " non-profit charitable foundation " supported by hunters , anglers , trappers and outfitters .
