<p> Mental health resources in Canada have long been stretched too thin . The dearth of professionals serving rural areas , and children and youth , is particularly acute . <p> Canadians aged 16 to 24 have the poorest access to mental health and addictions services in the country , according to a 2017 study by the Centre for Applied Research in Mental Health and Addiction . The second leading cause of death for this age group is suicide . ( The first is accidents . ) Unfortunately , several emerging trends suggests things may get a good deal worse before they get better . <p> Let 's start with pot . <p> Now that it 's legal , more Canadians are expected to partake .
