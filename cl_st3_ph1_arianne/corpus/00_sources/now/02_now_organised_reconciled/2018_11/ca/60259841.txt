Hanad Adan <p> Landscape of Loss is a painting and photography exhibition by second-year visual arts student Asifa Khan . Located at the Gales Gallery in Accolade West , this exhibition powerfully depicts the effects climate change and natural disasters have had on the tropical islands of Trinidad and Tobago . <p> Khan drew inspiration for the exhibition directly from hiking in Trinidad , her native country . While exploring the island , she noticed that , despite all the natural beauty and tranquility , there was an accumulating of garbage . <p> Khan aims to show the ugly side of these two islands , which are well-known tourist attractions for affluent travellers . Moreover , she wants to bring awareness to the importance of preserving a clean and sustainable ecosystem . Her exhibit powerfully achieves both ends . <p> Khan uses the Batik technique for her paintings . First , she takes a somewhat thick , plain brown cotton cloth . The wax seeps into the material and seals it , so when colour passes over the wax line , it does n't stain where the wax is touched , " she says . <p> Khan notes that Batik is a convenient artistic method due to its mobility -- she simply rolls up her cloth , and packs it into a little bag . Also , since dye gets directly absorbed into the material , she does n't have to wait long for it to dry . <p> The exhibitions ' photography is raw and visceral .
