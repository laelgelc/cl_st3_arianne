Does income inequality have harmful effects on the nature of our social interactions and ties ? <p> Over the past several years , my collaborators , students , and I have been working on uncovering the negative effects of income inequality on our social relations , testing the broad hypothesis that inequality breeds social discord , damaging relationships among strangers , acquaintances , neighbors , colleagues , friends , and spouses . We 've conducted several studies in which we create a situation of " income inequality " between two individuals in a laboratory setting and assess how the individuals perceive , judge , and behave toward one another . For example , in one study we asked two individuals to role-play a scenario in which they are running an art gallery . The individuals were randomly assigned to play the role of either the Assistant or Owner of the art gallery and to receive a specific payment for their efforts .
