<p> The port of Churchill and the rail was sold in late August to the Arctic Gateway Group Limited , which includes northern Manitoba First Nations , Fairfax Financial Holdings and AGT , which is based in Regina . <p> Murad Al Katib is the President and CEO of AGT Food and Ingredients . <p> He believes Churchill has a bright future . <p> He says climate change has extended the Churchill shipping season . <p> He says vessels coming from Russia have delivered fertilizer and left with Canadian grain for an Italian pasta maker . <p> He says that has happened in the past and it can happen again . <p> The federal government is providing 74 million dollars to help with the rail-line sale and repairs . <p> Another 43 million will subsidize operations of the rail line and port for the next ten years . <p> Most of the grain shipped through Churchill is from northeast Saskatchewan .
