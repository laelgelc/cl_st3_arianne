<h> Ottawa Public Health issues first frostbite advisory of the season <p> A frostbite advisory is being issued by Ottawa Public Health for the overnight hours Wednesday into Thursday . <p> The public health agency wants to ensure people take appropriate precautions before going outside and to notify service providers of people experiencing homelessness to encourage clients to seek indoor shelter from the cold . <p> OPH issues a frostbite advisory when a wind chill of -25 C or colder is forecast by Environment and Climate Change Canada for the Ottawa area . <p> With a wind chill of -25 C or colder , exposed skin can freeze in less than ten minutes . <p> A frostbite warning is issued when a wind chill of -35 C or colder is forecast . <p> OPH says with extreme cold temperatures , there is an increased risk of hypothermia for people who stay outside for long periods of time without adequate protection . Overexposure can result in severe injury and even death . It recommends that you wear several layers of clothing to keep warm and make sure that the outer layer protects you from wind and moisture . <p> Frostbite @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is white and waxy and feels hard to the touch .
