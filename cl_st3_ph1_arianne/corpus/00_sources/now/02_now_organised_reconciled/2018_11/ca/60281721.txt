<h> Letters to the Editor : Nov. 23 <h> Mail held hostage <p> How dare the Canadian Union of Postal Workers have its members hold our mail , our property , hostage ? I trusted Canada Post with my property , which I paid for , and they can keep it indefinitely ? There are scores of trailers full of undelivered mail being held everywhere . This negatively affects too many people , especially those dependent on money , such as businesses . <p> How poorly off are the postal workers compared to the rest of the workforce that we have to come to this ?
