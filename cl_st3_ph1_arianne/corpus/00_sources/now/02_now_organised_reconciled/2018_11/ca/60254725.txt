<h> Art class looks to inspire women <p> Some of the city 's leading female artists will be at the head of the class on Saturday for an interactive afternoon of creativity and inspiration . <p> As part of the Inspiring Women Among Us initiative hosted by UNBC , the Two Rivers Gallery will put four pivotal artists in the spotlight to talk about their artistic process , a little bit about their personal paths , and show how they do it . <p> article continues below <h> Trending Stories <p> " The gallery was approached and asked if we would be interested in holding one of the events , and we enthusiastically replied ' of course . ' I wanted to gather a group of leading female artists , but not leaders just because of their art , but because of all the other elements of their community works and their lives , " said Twyla Exner , the director of public programs at the Two Rivers Gallery . <p> She selected Cat Sivertsen , Mo Hamilton , Kathleen Angeleski and Audrey McKinnon for this motivational panel . So I 'm excited to explore and share that next Saturday and Sunday . " <p> Hamilton confessed that she was confused by the invitation . She said she was so immersed in her artistic process , a daily endeavour , that she hardly considered herself inspiring . <p> " I am very intimate with my own shortcomings , insecurities and daily struggles in believing in my artistic voice and yet regardless of this , I tenaciously carry on . I suppose this could be considered inspiring , " she said . <p> " I do find inspiration in creative women 's stories and who , despite the odds , keep going in a genuine and committed way .
