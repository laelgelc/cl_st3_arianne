<h> Provincial government puts B.C. on path to 100% zero-emission vehicle sales by 2040 <p> IN a move aimed at removing a major source of air pollution and climate change , the provincial government has put British Columbia on a path to require the sale of all new light-duty cars and trucks to be zero-emission vehicles ( ZEVs ) by the year 2040 . <p> Premier John Horgan on Tuesday announced the government , in partnership with the B.C. Green caucus , will introduce legislation next spring to phase in targets for the sale of zero-emission vehicles ( ZEVs ) . This legislation will set targets of 10% ZEV sales by 2025 , 30% by 2030 , and 100% by 2040 , while government will take additional steps to make ZEVs more affordable . <p> " There 's nothing more important than taking care of the place we call home , " said Horgan . " As a province , we need to work together to put B.C. on a path that powers our future with clean , renewable energy and reduces air pollution .
