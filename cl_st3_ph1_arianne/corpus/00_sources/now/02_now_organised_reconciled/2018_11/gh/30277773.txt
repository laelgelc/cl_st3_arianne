<h> Droughts and floods management workshop opens in Ouagadougou <p> A cross section of journalists at the opening ceremony of the workshop on integrated drought and flood management <h> A three-day workshop on droughts and floods management in the West-Africa sub-region for journalists has begun in Ouagadougou in Burkina Faso . <p> The workshop , themed : " Integrated drought and flood management : Contribution of the media in advocacy and awareness-raising in West Africa " , is organised by the Global Water Partnership West Africa ( GWP-WA ) . <p> It is aimed at equipping the journalists with relevant information and a proven approach towards the management of droughts and floods in West-Africa . <p> The workshop is also aimed at providing a framework for exchanges between the media , local communities , institutions and partners on good practices in drought and flood prevention and management in the sub-region . <p> In his opening remarks , the Executive Director of the Volta Basin Authority , Mr Robert Dessouassi , said it was imperative for the media to be armed with the needed information to enable them to help raise awareness on current climate effects and challenges which have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sub-region . <p> He noted that although issues of droughts and floods could not be prevented as they were natural occurrences , being equipped with information on the success stories of other regions could help mitigate if not eliminate the effects . <p> Mr Dessouassi , therefore , urged the participants to form a network of media actors , aimed at information sharing so as to help raise awareness in their respective countries . <p> The Executive Director of the GWP-WA , Mr Armand Houanye , noted that the effects of climate change was evident as already vulnerable populations have been rendered homeless as a result of floods in the sub-region . <p> He noted , however , that the occurrences of floods could be to the advantage of countries if it is properly managed . <p> He explained that if governments put in proper mechanisms to manage the occurrences of floods , it could help achieve the Sustainable Development Goals 2 , 6 , 7 , 13 , 15 and 17 . <p> He , therefore , urged the participants to take advantage of the opportunity that the workshop @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ create awareness and assist their respective governments in building
