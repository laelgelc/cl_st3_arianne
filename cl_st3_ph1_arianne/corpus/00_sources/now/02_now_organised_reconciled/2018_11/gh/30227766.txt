<p> The Heir Apparent to the British Throne and head of the Commonwealth , Prince of Wales , Prince Charles , has described the era of " Slave Trade " as a Shameful period in Ghana 's relations with Europe and in particular with the United Kingdom . <p> In his Keynote address at the Commonwealth inspired Public Lecture on the theme ; " Towards A Common Future " , at the Accra International Conference 's Centre ( AICC ) , the Prince of Wales , described the slave trade as the most painful chapter in the history of Ghana 's relationship with the United Kingdom and the rest of the European Union . <p> The United Kingdom and the rest of the World he said , ought to ensure that " Slave Trade " does not re-occur in any form or shape in the history of mankind . <p> " While Britain can be proud that it later led the way in the abolition of this shameful trade , we have a shared responsibility to ensure that the abject horror of slavery is never forgotten , that we uphold the existence of modern slavery and human trafficking and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ make it incomprehensible for most of us that human beings could ever treat each other with such utter inhumanity " Prince Charles said . <p> Climate Change <p> On the issues of climate change , the Prince said the impact of global warming and climate change are already been felt by far by many Commonwealth citizens . He further observed that even though there are several examples of real evidence of the impact of climate change such as hurricanes and cyclones , the Commonwealth of Nations can still join hands and share best practices to arrest the situation . <p> " There is it seems to me , tremendous potential for the Commonwealth to share best practice and coordinate its response to these kind of disasters " Prince Charles said . <p> The Blue Economy <p> One of the fundamental challenges that the planet is facing according to Prince Charles is the issues of plastics that are finding their way into the sea .
