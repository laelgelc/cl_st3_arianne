<h> Engaging governments and political parties to govern ' green ' <p> We see our shared task as that of spreading awareness of the threat of climate change -- and of generating preventive action against global warming . Awareness of climate change -- and its cause by human activity -- is certainly growing worldwide . But it is not growing fast enough to get people to demand immediate and decisive action from those who lead them . <p> We ourselves believe governments and political parties must deal with the threat of climate change and global warming with a greater -- and more sustained -- sense of urgency than they have done so far , because the stakes are higher than in any political or economic crisis of the moment . <p> We need to match with our efforts the dimensions and the gravity of the danger humankind faces . <p> Governments can make a difference <p> We know governments can make a difference -- if only they focus on specific , practical aspects of the problem -- as the three European states have focused on the degradation of the oceans they share ; and the Communist Party of China @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's great cities . <p> Getting Asian governments to do so , we have made one of ICAPP 's prime missions .
