<h> Lawmaker seeks creation of national soil and water preservation program <p> A lawmaker sought on Thursday the passage of a bill seeking to empower local government units ( LGUs ) and marginalized farmers in protecting and managing the country 's water resources . <p> Cong . Sharon S Garin . ( FACEBOOK/ MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> Deputy Speaker and AAMBIS-OWA party-list Rep. Sharon Garin made the call as the country observes the Global Warming and Climate Change Consciousness Week . <p> Citing the World Resources Institute , she said the Philippines is projected to reach high levels of 40 to 80 percent of water stress by 2040 . <p> She expressed concerned that such projection is " bound to have adverse effects on the country 's food security and agricultural productivity . " <p> By definition , baseline water stress measures the total annual water withdrawals ( municipal , industrial , and agricultural ) and indicates more competition among users , Garin explained . <p> " It is time we double our efforts in battling climate change .
