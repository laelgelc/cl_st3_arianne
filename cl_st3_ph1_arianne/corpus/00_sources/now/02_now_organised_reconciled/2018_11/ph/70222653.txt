The organization counts more than seven hundred Filipino climate reality leaders trained personally by Gore . <p> The Climate Reality Leadership Awards is the group 's latest flagship advocacy program , apart from the Let 's Talk Cleaner Energy campaign and other high-level climate policy lobbying and community empowerment initiatives in the grassroots and youth sectors . <p> The program recognizes outstanding Filipino individuals , organizations , networks and initiatives that have been catalyzing global solutions to the climate crisis by making urgent action a necessity across every level of society . <p> This year , there are 36 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Award will be given to United Nations Special Rapporteur on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples Victoria Tauli-Corpuz for advancing and advocating the stewardship role of IPs especially with regard to natural resources found in ancestral domains . <p> Another special category , The Luntiang Aligato Award for Journalism , recognizes media personalities who shed light on critical environmental issues .
