Initial data from the Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) showed 6 of 10 Filipino families suffer deprivation of basic education services . <p> THE dream for a better life , a comfortable life , has always included the education of Filipino children . Based on the AmBisyon 2040 , the Philippines 's long-term vision , 73 percent of families want their children to be college-educated by 2040 . <p> However , this aspiration may be difficult to attain given the latest data from the Philippine Statistics Authority ( PSA ) , which showed Filipino families were most deprived in education . <p> PSA data showed that , of the 13 indicators , educational attainment had the highest incidence of deprivation among families at 59.3 percent in 2016 and 49.4 percent in 2017 . <p> This meant 6 out of 10 families in 2016 and 5 out of 10 families in 2017 were deprived of basic education . " <h> AmBisyon , targets <p> In order to meet the AmBisyon by 2040 , the national government has laid out education targets , which not only focused on tertiary education but also primary and secondary schooling . <p> Based on the AmBisyon , Filipinos want to take up courses related to various fields , such as education science and teacher training , 16.5 percent ; business administration , 12.7 percent ; information technology-related , 11.6 percent ; and tourism and hospitality . <p> The list also includes industries , such as engineering and technology , 10 percent ; law and jurisprudence , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; trade , craft and industrial , 6.7 percent ; and maritime , 4.8 percent . <p> In order to be on track to meeting these , the government , through the Philippine Development Plan 2017 to 2022 , has identified a number of basic education targets and strategies . <p> The government aims to increase functional literacy for kindergarten to 95 percent , from 74.65 percent , the baseline using 2015 data ; elementary , 95 percent from the 91.05-percent baseline ; and junior high school , 75.44 percent from the 68.15 percent baseline . <p> Functional literacy , according to the United Nations Education , Scientific and Cultural Organization , was more than just being able to read and write . Functional literacy means literacy and numeracy skills combined . These are part of lifelong learning aims -- included among the Sustainable Development Goals . <p> " While functional literacy and numeracy will mean different things in different countries and to different organizations , our own view is that they both involve a continuum of skills development that builds over time , " according to a Unesco blog @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ International Development Group and Silvia Montoya , director of the Unesco Institute for Statistics . <p> This also means mastery of basic skills . To this end , the government aims to increase the proportion of elementary students to 74.39 percent from 63.93 percent , and junior high school , 20 percent from 14.37 percent of performing at moving toward or approximately mastered skills . The government also aims to bring down the proportion of students at low mastery to 10 percent from 14.88 percent for junior high school . <h> Completion rate <p> Apart from functional literacy , the government also aims to increase the completion rate for basic education . For elementary education , the government aims to increase this to 90 percent from 83.43 percent , and for junior high school , 78.48 percent . <p> " Lifelong learning will be pursued to attain both personal and national goals . The CCTs started with 284,000 beneficiary households in 2008 . By 2015 beneficiaries reached 4.1 million households . <p> In terms of population , the number of beneficiaries rose from 662,000 children aged zero to 18 years old in 2008 to 10.2 million in 2015 . Today , the program covers about 79 percent of poor households whose income is less than the amount needed to basic necessities . <p> The CCT extends a health grant of P500 monthly year-round and an education grant of P300 per child for 10 months each year to each participating household . <p> To receive these cash grants , pregnant women must avail themselves of pre- and postnatal care , and be attended during childbirth by a trained professional ; and parents or guardians must attend the family-development sessions , which include topics on responsible parenting , health and nutrition . <p> Other conditions : children aged 0 to 5 must receive regular preventive health check-ups and vaccines @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ twice a year ; and children aged between three and 18 must enroll in school , and maintain an attendance of at least 85 percent of class days every month . <h> Multidimensional poverty <p> The data was based on the initial methodology developed for the Multidimensional Poverty Index .
