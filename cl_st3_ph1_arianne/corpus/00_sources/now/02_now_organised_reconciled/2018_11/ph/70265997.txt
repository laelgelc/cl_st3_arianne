<h> Legarda files bill on phase out of single-use plastics <p> Sen. Loren B. Legarda , chairwoman of the Senate climate change committee , has sought the phase out of single-use plastics in the country . <p> Senator Loren Legarda ( Facebook / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> This came after Legarda filed Senate Bill 1948 or the " Single-Use Plastics Regulation and Management Act of 2018 " which seeks to regulate the manufacturing , importation , and use of single-use plastic products ; and provide penalties , levies , and incentives for industries , businesses , and consumers . <p> The measure , according to Legarda , aims to phase out single-use plastics in the country by prohibiting importation and use in food establishments , stores , markets , and retailers . <p> Consumers shall be encouraged to use reusable materials , while all single-use plastics already in circulation shall be collected , recycled , and properly disposed in accordance with the provisions of the Republic Act No. 9003 or the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act . <p> A minimum levy of five pesos shall be charged to consumers for each single-use plastic used in transactions , 20 percent @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the 80 percent shall be remitted to the proposed Special Plastic Fund . <p> The bill also urges government agencies to undertake research and development on alternatives to single-use plastics ; assess impact on affected industry workers and employees ; and develop capacity building programs for alternative livelihood opportunities . <p> Moreover , businesses and individuals engaged in the manufacture of single-use plastics alternatives shall be given incentives provided under existing laws and from local government units , while violators shall be imposed penalties ranging from P5,000 to P500,000 and permanent suspension of business permit . <p> " This bill provides for an ambitious yet comprehensive approach to solving our problem on single-use plastics . I hope for the support from the government and all sectors on this urgent policy promoting a circular economy , " Legarda explained . <p> The filing of the bill coincides with the observance of National Environmental Awareness Month . <p> Legarda , author of Republic Act No. 9512 or the Environmental Awareness and Education Act that designated November as Environmental Awareness Month , said that single-use plastics end up as litter , clogging drainages @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ health , and ecosystem . <p> " Single-use plastics , such as cigarette butts , drinking bottles and caps , food wrappers , grocery bags , lids , straws , stirrers , and take-away containers , are immediately discarded and end up in landfills . They are harmful for the environment and human health because they pollute our water ; cause blockages in sewerage and drainage systems , which lead to flooding ; and release toxic emissions when burned , " she explained .
