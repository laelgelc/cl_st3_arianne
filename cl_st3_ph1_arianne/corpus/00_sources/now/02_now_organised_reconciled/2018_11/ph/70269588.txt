<h> Negros bishops cite Pope 's plea for environmental protection <p> BACOLOD CITY -- The four Roman Catholic bishops in Negros cited the appeal of Pope Francis to " every person living on this planet " to protect the environment in opposing coal-fired power plants in the island . <p> In a collegial pastoral statement released on Thursday , they called on Negrenses to stand firm together -- with each other and with civic leaders -- to oppose any coal-fired power plants and phase out those still in operation . <p> The joint statement , based on the Pope 's encyclical letter " Laudato Si : On Care for Our Common Home , " was issued by Bishops Gerardo Alminaza of the Diocese of San Carlos , Patricio Buzon of the Diocese of Bacolod , Louie Galbines of the Diocese of Kabankalan , and Julito Cortes of the Diocese of Dumaguete . <p> " Our desire to ensure energy for all must not lead to the undesired effect of a spiral of extreme climate changes due to a catastrophic rise in global temperatures , harsher environments , and increased levels of poverty , " the bishops said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ officially published on June 18 , 2015 , the highest Catholic Church leader called for ecological conversion , for humanity to reduce consumption of coal and other fossil fuels that are major contributors to climate change , and to embrace an energy future that is clean , renewable , and equitable for the health and well-being of the planet and for future generations . <p> The bishops appealed to Negrenses to heed the call for ecological conversion and transformative renewable power that is shared by all . <p> They pointed out that " coal-driven power will never truly be cheap , especially when we factor in the numerous environmental , social , and health costs associated with its usage . " <p> In fact , solar power is increasingly becoming the lowest-cost energy option , they said . <p> With the continuing emergence of storage solutions , renewables will provide greater resiliency and energy independence , immune from the unpredictability of global commodity prices , they added . <p> The bishops ' statement noted that there are already nine solar power plants , eight biomass plants , and 10 hydro @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of 579.43 megawatts . <p> They added that the four dioceses have been increasingly demonstrating that sustainable energy practices work for them and their respective communities . <p> Decentralized rooftop solar energy systems in the dioceses of Bacolod and San Carlos , for instance , showed how small-scale distributed renewable energy generation is climate-friendly , sustainable , and affordable . <p> The local churches of Dumaguete and Kabankalan are equally committed to seriously implement the Laudato S ? Challenge of the Holy Father and are finding concrete ways to safeguard the environment , they added . <p> " And yet , the dark specter of fossil fuels remains with a proposed coal-fired plant in San Carlos City , hanging over our future , exacerbating climate change , threatening our resources , our environment , our health , and our sustainable development , " they further said in the statement . <p> Alminaza expressed his opposition to the proposed 300-megawatt coal-fired plant by the SMC Global Power Holdings Corp . in San Carlos City in a position letter issued by the diocese last month .
