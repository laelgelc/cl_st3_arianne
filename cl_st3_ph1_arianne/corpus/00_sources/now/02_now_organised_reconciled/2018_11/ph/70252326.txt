<h> Advocacy for Integrated Reporting <p> WE in the accountancy profession should listen to the needs of the time . The financial statements and reports that we prepare for various stakeholders have several limitations . It is oftentimes criticized that these FS and reports are difficult to understand , not forward looking as these dwell on historical information , are excessive in mass content with all the disclosures and financial numbers , and still do not present more relevant information required by the users of the FS . <p> The world is also addressing various concerns and dilemmas . Foremost of these are environmental problems , including global warming , air and water pollution , climate change , ozone-layer depletion and a host of others . <p> How are accountants addressing these immense problems ? <p> A recent initiative supported by the leaders in the profession and its stakeholders is the integrated reporting &lt;IR&gt; and thinking and the aligned sustainability reporting . As defined by the International Integrated Reporting Council , the &lt;IR&gt; is a concise communication about how an organization 's strategy , governance , performance and prospects , in the context of its external environment , lead @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and long term . The &lt;IR&gt; is not meant to replace the traditional FS and reports prepared by accountants , but is intended to supplement the reports to be made available to the users of the FS . Though there are already other reports , including the Annual Report and Corporate Governance Report , being prepared by enterprises , the &lt;IR&gt; will result not necessarily in more reporting but better reporting . The limitations of the traditional reporting can sufficiently be addressed with the inclusion of the &lt;IR&gt; in the reporting menu made available to the users of the FS and other reports . <p> The many issues on environmental impact reporting are also being addressed by a number of organizations in the various reports that they prepare . Business enterprises and accountants have long been exposed and involved in environmental accounting and corporate social reporting , which contain information on how enterprises and environment are impacting each other and the programs being adopted to alleviate the environmental problems . Another positive development in this area is the emergence of sustainability reporting . Several global organizations are developing knowledge bases and reporting framework and standards on sustainability reporting , including the Global Reporting Initiative and the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board . <p> I encourage our accountants in the Philippines to participate in these initiatives and developments . As participants and members of the business ecosystem , we are duty bound to first be aware of these important developments , and , second , to actively be proficient over time for us to engage as part of what we do at work and in our community . <p> I anticipate that &lt;IR&gt; and thinking and sustainability reporting will more and more be ingrained in the Philippine setting . <p> To be continued <p> Joel L.
