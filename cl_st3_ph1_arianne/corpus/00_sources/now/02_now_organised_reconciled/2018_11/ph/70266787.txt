<h> Recent Posts <h> How times have changed <p> Monitoring the recent Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation ( APEC ) in Papua New Guinea brought back memories of the formative years of the economic grouping when I served on the team of then President Fidel V. Ramos and Joseph Estrada as Managing Head of the Board of Investments and Undersecretary of Trade and Industry . <p> APEC is an inter-governmental forum for 21 Pacific Rim member economies that promotes free trade throughout the Asia-Pacific region . At the initiative of Australian Prime Minister Bob Hawke who believed in the necessity of region-wide cooperation on economic matters , a first meeting was held in Canberra in November 1989 followed by APEC 's establishment with 12 economies ten months later . In its 1994 meeting in Bogor , Indonesia , the APEC leaders adopted the Bogor Goals that aim for free and open trade and investment in the Asia-Pacific by 2010 for industrialised economies and by 2020 for developing economies . <p> There were quite a number of preparatory meetings to ensure the smooth functioning of the Leaders ' meetings . Since the heads of delegation had to have similar logistical arrangements , we had to provide suites for all . We prevailed on Shangrila Mactan to convert some of the rooms to suites and got the support of Cebu 's known furniture makers to transform the suites into the showcase of Philippine artistry . They were quite successful that I recall a head of delegation bought all the furniture in his suite . <p> If one thought that meetings in Bali and Santiago were rest and recreation , he would be terribly mistaken . It was a lot of work as the Philippines could only afford to send a lean working group while our counterparts from the developed economies had full staff support . The United States team even had a comissary set up so we always invited them to informal get togethers for the food and drinks they would bring . <p> The United States , Australia and Singapore were in the forefront of pushing for liberalization .
