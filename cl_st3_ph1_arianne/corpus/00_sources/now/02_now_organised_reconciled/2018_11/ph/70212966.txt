<h> Scientists Work To Decode Genome Of Complex Creatures <p> By Fernando Whiten on November 5 , 2018 <p> The Earth BioGenome Project is on a mission to study and sequence genome of all living beings on earth so that the data can be used to conserve threatened species over a period of time . The project will include all known animals , plants , fungus and even protozoans and is being described as a " moonshot for biology " . The Wellcome Sanger Institute , which had played a major part in previous human genome project , will be a part of this endeavor too and has committed its intention to sequence genomes of 66,000 UK-based species . <p> Director of Science at Wellcome Trust Dr. Jim Smith described this project as a responsibility of the human race to raise awareness about biodiversity among all . The aim of this project is to create a new inventory about existing life on earth by reading the genetic code of every existing living organism .
