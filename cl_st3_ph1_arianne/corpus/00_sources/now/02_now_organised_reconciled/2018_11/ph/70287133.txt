<h> Brazil withdraws offer to host UN climate change conference <p> Brazil has withdrawn its offer to host a large U.N . conference on climate change next year , the foreign ministry said Wednesday , in a move that environmental groups said put into question Brazil 's commitment to reducing carbon emissions . <p> Brazil pulled its offer to host the 2019 climate change conference because of " the current fiscal and budget constraints , which are expected to remain in the near future , " according to a foreign ministry statement sent to the Associated Press on Wednesday . <p> Environmental groups interpreted the decision as a nod to President-elect Jair Bolsonaro , who promised during his campaign to pull Brazil out of the Paris Accord on climate change . <p> Since being elected , Bolsonaro has publicly wavered on those promises .
