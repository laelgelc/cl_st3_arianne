<h> DepEd showcases school safety innovations to Sri Lankan delegation <p> The Department of Education ( DepEd ) showcased innovations on school safety to Sri Lankan delegation in line with its efforts to help promote school-based disaster risk reduction and management ( DRRM ) . <p> Saint Francis Integrated National High School DRRM Coordinator explained the evacuation procedure during multiple hazards posted around the school premises . ( photo courtesy of DepEd website / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> Organized by the DepEd 's Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Service ( DRRMS ) , the study visits imparted the value of mainstreaming the Comprehensive School Safety ( CSS ) Framework which aims to strengthen the pillars of safe learning facilities , school disaster management , and risk reduction and resilience education for schools . <p> The DepEd Order No. 37 , series of 2015 , or the Comprehensive DRRM in Basic Education Framework , institutionalized the core elements of CSS into all the resilience interventions of the public education sector . <p> DepEd spearheaded study visits in select schools for Sri Lanka 's Ministry of Education delegation last month . Rosa Road Safety Kiddie Park . <p> DepEd-DRRMS director Ronilda Co underscored the importance of all DRRM officers , coordinators and personnel in ensuring the sound implementation of various disaster resiliency efforts in schools and in communities . <p> Co also acknowledged @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efforts in schools or at their own homes .
