<h> Oceana holds photo exhibit on Philippine Rise <h> ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA - Advocacy group Oceana launched Monday a photo exhibit at the National Museum for Natural History to raise awareness on the need to protect the Philippine Rise . <p> Gloria Ramos , vice-president of Oceana Philippines , said the Philippine Rise is probably the only pristine area in the country that can serve as a refuge for marine life amid coral bleaching caused by climate change . <p> She also warned against poaching and the use technology to monitor the area . <p> Ramos said they have also trained judges and lawyers on laws pertaining to Philippine Rise , as well as existing technology that may help identify boats fishing illegally in the area . <p> The exhibit , " The Journey Towards the Protection of the Philippine Rise , " is a celebration of the declaration of Philippine Rise as a marine protected area .
