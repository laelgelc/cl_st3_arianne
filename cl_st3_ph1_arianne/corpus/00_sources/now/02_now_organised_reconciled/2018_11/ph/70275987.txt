<h> Children 's book launched to promote healthy eating <p> IN time for the celebration of National Children 's Month this November , Greenpeace Philippines launched a book , titled Paano Kumain ng Kulay ? which seeks to promote nutrition as a way for the Filipino youth to growth healthy and fulfill their potentials . <p> Published by the environmental independent global campaigning organization , together with Adarna House and Me &amp; My Veg Mouth , it features poems by author Mabi David , complemented by illustrations of Yas Doctor , to encourage six- to eight-year-old kids to eat colorful everyday fruits and vegetables that contain vitamins and minerals . <p> " We try to use colors as the theme of the book to educate youngsters that they can actually taste the colors of fruits and vegetables , " the writer told reporters in a sideline interview during the kickoff event held recently at the Museo Pambata along Roxas Boulevard in Malate , Manila . <p> " This is inspired by the idea that the colors in plant foods actually determine their nutrient content . It 's a bilingual book in Filipino and English . The United Nations Children 's Fund said more than 29,000 Filipino children die annually due to undernutrition . <p> " People always say it 's so hard to eat healthy , " David said . " One of the easiest ways to make sure we get our nutrients daily is to ' eat the rainbow . ' Plant nutrients that make malunggay green , or carrots orange , or tomatoes red , are compounds that have nutritional benefits . So a plate with different colors -- not just brown -- tends to be balanced and nutrient-dense . " <p> The author , who is also a certified plant-based cook , shared that she used to give nutritious meals to her nephew when he was very young .
