( Photo by Erwin P. Nicavera ) <p> BACOLOD CITY -- The Sangguniang Panlalawigan ( SP ) or provincial board will hold consultations with various government agencies and resource groups in the course of deliberations on the proposed coal-free ordinance for Negros Occidental . <p> Third District Board Member Manuel Frederick Ko , chairman of the committee on energy , said the provincial government can not make a concrete stand on the matter as of now . <p> Ko , who led the joint committee meeting on the proposed legislation at the Vice Governor 's Office on Wednesday , said consultations are needed for the body to have a holistic approach on the issue . <p> " Maybe we could start in understanding first the power outlook of the province before proceeding to the economic , social and environmental impacts of the proposed ordinance , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sides first before acting on it . " <p> The proposed ordinance , submitted by the Office of the Governor , seeks to declare Negros Occidental a coal-free , clean energy and environment-friendly province . <p> It was passed by the SP for first reading on October 24 and was referred to the committees on energy and environment . <p> Based on the draft ordinance , the Provincial Government of Negros Occidental follows the " noble and brave " examples of its government counterparts like Negros Oriental , Bohol , Guimaras , Cebu City , Ozamis Oriental and South Cotabato . <p> It vows to " protect its people and its vast natural resources from environmentally unsound power generation technologies such as coal , by engaging and investing in responsible renewable energy initiatives such as , but not limited to , solar power , geothermal , hydro and wind energy sources . " <p> It also states there should be no coal-fired power plant and any environmentally unsound power generation technologies , including fossil fuels in Negros Occidental . <p> The province shall use all its power to encourage initiatives @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and technologies , it added . <p> The meeting on Wednesday was also attended by First District Board Member Rommel Debulgado , chairman of committee on environment , and other board members , <p> Also , among the attendees was Rodne Galicha , head of the Climate Reality Project , which recently awarded Negros Occidental as one of the champions of environmental causes and movers of climate change mitigation . <p> It was reported last month that San Miguel Global Power Holdings Corp . has expressed interest to put up a 300-megawatt coal-fired power plants in San Carlos City , Negros Occidental .
