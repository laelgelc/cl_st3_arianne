<h> Mayor Sara calls on women to unite on common causes <p> DAVAO CITY -- Women should work together to advance causes that affect them all . <p> This was the message of Mayor Inday Sara Z. Duterte to the members of the Philippine Federation of Local Councils of Women ( PFLCW ) on Wednesday , as she highlighted the collective power of women in enabling others through social enterprise and other initiatives . <p> Mayor Sara , who spoke at the three-day 16th General Assembly of the PFLCW here , said women leaders should use their platforms to lead and serve fellow women and men with compassion , devotion and integrity . <p> She underscored the importance of moving together and pooling resources to address common concerns among women . <p> " With our collective efforts , let us use voice talents to lead and to serve our fellow women and men with compassion devotion and integrity , " she said . <p> As a local leader , the mayor vowed to champion the various causes of women and continue advocating their rights and welfare -- including the children -- through the implementation of the Women Development Code @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ committed to continue pushing the establishment of the Barangay Women 's Desk , provide livelihood assistance through the Magnegosyo Ka Day , health care support , and shelter for abused women and children . <p> Under the city 's " Magnegosyo Ka Day " initiative , she noted that the local government has already assisted four cooperatives with a loan package of PHP1 million each . The program , she added , has also benefited some 1,161 disadvantaged women through a capital seed fund totaling PHP8.6 million . <p> These pro-women initiatives , she said , aim to empower Dabawenyas to develop economic independence and enhance their potentials . <p> She also underscored the government 's continued promotion of the rights of all residents across genders . <p> " This is the guiding principle behind the Anti-Discrimination Ordinance of Davao City , " she said . <p> However , the mayor acknowledged that much more need to be done as she encouraged the assembly participants to use the gathering as an opportunity to find solutions to common concerns . <p> The PFLCW is a network of women organizations in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ total membership of 500 Local Councils of Women composed of 50,000 women . <p> This year 's Assembly of PFLCW , which is hosted by Compostela Valley province , focuses on Women in Climate Change , the Role of LGUs in Peace Building , ICT , Digitalization of Government Processes , micro and small medium enterprises , and the rights and leadership of women in the locality .
