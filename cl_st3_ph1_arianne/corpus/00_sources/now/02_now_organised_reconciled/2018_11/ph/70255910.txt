<h> PHL yet to tap UN-backed climate financing facility <p> THE Philippines has so far received zero financing from the Green Climate Fund ( GCF ) , a funding facility set up by the 194 countries who are parties to the 2010 United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> According to Climate Change Commission Vice Chairman Emmanuel M. de Guzman , however , the Philippine delegation will demand more funding to replenish the GCF when they sit down at the negotiating table in Katowice , Poland in early December . <p> There were pledges worth $10.3 billion for the GCF in 2014 . However , recent reports point to doubts over the sustainability of the GCF . <p> De Guzman said the Philippines would " hopefully come up with a project proposal next year to access some of the funds , " with the recent approval of LandBank as a conduit for the GCF . <p> " We need to mobilize or call for replenishment of the GCF , which is the financing mechanism under the Paris Agreement .
