<h> Biosecurity champs announced <p> Biosecurity New Zealand announced the winners of the 2018 New Zealand Biosecurity Awards last night . <p> Environment Southland 's Fiordland Marine Pathway Management Plan took out the top prize , the New Zealand Biosecurity Supreme Award . Their project also received the New Zealand Biosecurity Eagle Technology Local and Central Government Award . <p> The Fiordland Marine Pathway Management Plan the first of its kind in New Zealand , aims to protect one of New Zealand 's most unique and nationally significant areas from marine pests being carried in on local and visiting vessels . <p> " The New Zealand Biosecurity Awards celebrate iwi , environmental organisations , science providers , community groups , local , regional and central government agencies who are working hard to help ensure Aotearoa is safe from pests and diseases , " says Roger Smith , Head of Biosecurity New Zealand . <p> The Awards ' winners included a wide variety of interesting biosecurity-related projects from the protection of New Zealand 's kiwifruit industry , to biosecurity excellence in Tauranga moana to improving the outcomes for kokako , and even innovative products like a portable footwear cleaning system @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ due to climate change , trade and passenger dynamics and volume , and the spread of global pests and disease , a lot can change - even over a 12-month period .
