<p> New Zealand 's High Performance Computing Facility has officially opened at research institute NIWA 's Greta Point campus in Wellington . <p> Research , science and innovation minister Dr Megan Woods cut the ribbon on the complex , comprising three new interconnected supercomputers -- two based in Wellington and one in Auckland -- that will be used by Kiwi researchers to investigate scientific issues of national significance . <p> " This marks a step change for science in New Zealand and a further advancement towards an innovative , future-focused society , " said Woods . " The supercomputers are a significant upgrade with 10 times the computing capability of its predecessor . <p> " This will have a whole range of benefits for scientific research , including better understanding issues around climate change , genomics , the management of New Zealand 's freshwater assets and resilience to natural hazards .
