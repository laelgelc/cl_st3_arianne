<h> NZ Pacific voice on justice at Rome synod <p> Young people are looking for a Church that " sounds and acts like Jesus " , a young representative from New Zealand and Samoa told the synod of bishops in Rome . <p> Joseph Moeono-Kolio , 31 , who was representing Caritas International Youth Forum , said many young people are hoping for a Church that will take the lead in implementing social justice and be able to confront injustices within its own walls . <p> " A Church that can confront injustices within its own walls is a Church that can stand up to any injustice far beyond any walls and that is where many young people wish to be -- at the front lines of a missionary Church in action , with our pastors and elders guiding us , " he said . <p> In his four-minute intervention , Mr Moeono-Kolio called attention to the problem of climate change that is threatening the Pacific . <p> " We live at the mercy of a quiet persecution , on the very front lines of climate change , where every day sees the erosion of our islands and , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Pacific people , " he said . <p> " The memories of our ancestors slip away when their graves succumb to the sea and the very future of our young people comes under threat each time we are forced to migrate . " <p> He told the bishops that during intense cyclones , people gather at the village church where they find refuge . <p> " This is how the Church must operate , a haven of safety from life 's storms , and protector of the most vulnerable , " he said . <p> " Both globally and at the local level , the Church must initiate an awakening of conscience , bringing the faithful to a deeper understanding of what it means to follow Jesus -- positioning ourselves as he did to call out injustice , to uphold the dignity of all and to make a prophetic impact on our society .
