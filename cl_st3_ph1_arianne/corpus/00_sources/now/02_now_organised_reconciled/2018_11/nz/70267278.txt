<h> Scientists ' voices absent from ' anti-science ' GMO hearings <p> In a trend described as " extreme " and " anti-science " the Northland Regional Council is the latest council to consider proposals to ban genetically modified organisms at a local level . <p> Stringent national rules are already in place to assess the safety of any genetically modified organism ( GMO ) before allowing its release . To date no genetically modified crops or animals have been approved for release from containment . <p> Additional local body rules are akin to adding braces when the belt is already tight . <p> Local rules prohibiting GMOs are already in place in Hawke 's Bay and Auckland . <p> Initially no rules regarding GMOs were in the Northland 's Proposed Regional Plan , however , the council received so many submissions it decided after receiving legal advice it needed to run two days of hearings . <p> Of the over 100 submissions received by Northland Regional Council to their Proposed Regional Plan most wanted the council to add additional controls .
