Our Prime Minister knows this . She got elected saying that climate change is her generation 's nuclear-free moment then rushed legislation through Parliament to harass petrol companies for charging motorists too much . A recovering dollar and falling global oil prices ( an Ardern-Trump common interest through 2020 , more later ) have cut prices at the pump , but a bigger energy problem is coming that she can not duck so easily . <p> Low Lakes Lake levels across the South Island are low and a dry summer is forecast .
