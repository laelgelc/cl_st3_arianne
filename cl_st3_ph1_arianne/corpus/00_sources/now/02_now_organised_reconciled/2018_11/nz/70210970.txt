<h> Henderson car park part of global sustainability development competition <p> International and local designers joined mana whenua and ward councillors for a tour of two car parks in Henderson this week , as part of a global design competition . <p> The Falls and Alderman car parks were nominated last year for Reinventing Cities , an international contest to inspire world-leading sustainable design run by C40 , a group of cities working together on combating climate change . <p> Five successful development teams have been selected and were given a chance to visit the sites to learn more about their environmental and cultural significance . <p> Project Sponsor for Henderson from Panuku Development Auckland Angelika Cutler says the visit was a hugely valuable exercise which will help shape the bidders ' design thinking . <p> " Being clear about the local and cultural context of these sites is key to ensuring quality outcomes that connect to in the Henderson community .
