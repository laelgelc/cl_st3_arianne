<p> As global emissions of heat-trapping gases continue and the world warms , westerly airflow across New Zealand will ramp up . <p> This will bring more water to an already wet West Coast . With the Southern Alps acting as a barrier , most of this water will fall as rain , and rivers will rise . <p> While climate change is liable to affect flooding anywhere in New Zealand , current research suggests the West Coast is the first in line for larger and more frequent river floods . <p> Southland and Otago are also particularly exposed . This threat is compounded for coastal communities by sea level rise , and compounded further to the north by increasing intensity of extra-tropical cyclones . <p> What we can expect , more or less , is that the largest floods will become larger and floods of all sizes will become more frequent .
