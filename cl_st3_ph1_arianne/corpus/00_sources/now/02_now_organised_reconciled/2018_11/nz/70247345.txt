<p> Kids across the world expressed concerns about global warming by joining forces to create what organisers say is the world 's biggest postcard on a glacier in the Swiss Alps . <p> Bearing messages of hope and commitment , more than 125,000 colourful and hand-written postcards from kids around the world have emblazoned a glacier in Switzerland to create one giant one , half the size of a football field . <p> It 's a cry of help - from New Orleans to Hong Kong , from sub-Saharan Africa to India - ahead of an upcoming UN-backed climate conference in Poland next month . <p> The Swiss development and cooperation agency and partners unfurled overnight a " compound postcard , " on top of the threatened Aletsch glacier , the longest and deepest in the Alps and which is on track to melt to nonexistence by the end of this century if global warming trends continue . <p> Organisers say the individual postcards delivered to the 3,400-metre height near Switzerland 's famed Jungfraujoch , aimed to set a Guinness World Record for the " postcard with the most contributions .
