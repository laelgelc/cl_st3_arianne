<h> Govt opens $238M of grants to encourage tree planting <p> The government is offering $238 million in grants to encourage new tree planting , especially of natives , as it tries to get one billion new trees in the ground by 2028 . <p> Cabinet ministers Shane Jones and Damien O'Connor today announced the opening of two grants , funded through the Provincial Growth Fund and allocated by the Ministry for Primary Industries ' forest unit . <p> The first scheme will offer direct grants to landowners to help cover the cost of planting and establishing trees and indigenous regeneration , with $118 million available over three years . Indigenous trees will get preferential treatment , with the scheme supporting the planting of 60 million trees in that time , of which two-thirds are hoped to be native . <p> A second scheme will offer $120 million to co-fund projects that reduce barriers to planting . The idea is that the applicants for commercial projects will fund at least half , but applications with strong social and environmental benefits may be able to negotiate better terms .
