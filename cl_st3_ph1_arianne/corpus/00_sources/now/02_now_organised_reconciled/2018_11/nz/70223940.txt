<h> NZ 's love of diesels kills hundreds a year <p> Pollution from cars kills more than 256 people a year but the Government has yet to move on shifting dirty diesel vehicles off the roads -- in fact , its policies are making the problem worse . <p> Documents released under the Official Information Act show the Government is concerned about the level of toxic air pollution and the effects on people 's health . This is especially true in Auckland where cars account for more than half the human emissions of PM10 , a particle particularly harmful to health . <p> The documents refer to a 2012 study which estimated the 256 premature deaths a year due to air pollution from motor vehicle emissions . <p> The Ministry of Transport pointed the finger firmly at diiesels .
