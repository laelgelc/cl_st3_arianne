<h> Climate change 101 : The most important things to understand about this urgent problem <p> Tackling climate change is seen by many as the most urgent goal of this generation . <p> For many , tackling climate change is the ultimate responsibility of our time -- working out how to stop the planet warming up so much and so quickly that it causes irrevocable damage that humanity is ill-prepared to cope with . <p> But for the average person , it can be hard to decipher what it all means , how it will affect us and , crucially , whether we can do anything about it . <p> A key step is understanding the basics of climate change itself , a term used to explain the consequences of our warming planet . <p> Carbon dioxide emissions are the major pollutant of the planet 's atmosphere . <p> WHY IS THE PLANET WARMING ? <p> Earth 's warming is largely related to the quantity of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere . <p> These absorb heat from the sun as it is reflected off the earth 's surface and send it back again . <p> Professor James Renwick , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ process is akin to having a blanket on a bed . <p> " A thicker blanket , a warmer body ; a thicker blanket over the earth , a warmer earth .
