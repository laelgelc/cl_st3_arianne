<h> Australian govt to deliver budget early <p> The promise of a healthy budget comes as Scott Morrison seeks to repair his government 's standing with voters . Photo : Getty Images <p> Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison says his government will deliver its annual budget early as it prepares for an election due by May next year , but suffered an immediate setback when one of his MPs said she was quitting his party . <p> Morrison said his conservative Liberal-National coalition government would deliver Australia 's first budget surplus since 2007/2008 . The new budget year begins on July 1 . <p> Morrison said the budget would be delivered on April 2 , about a month earlier than usual . His government must call an election by May and opinion polls suggest the coalition is on course for a heavy defeat by the Labor opposition . <p> " It will be a surplus budget . It will be a budget which is the product of the years of hard work of our government , " Morrison told reporters in Canberra on Tuesday . <p> The promise of a healthy budget comes as Morrison seeks to repair his @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a party-room revolt in August this year that ousted former prime minister Malcolm Turnbull . <p> The toppling of Turnbull pushed the government into minority when it lost Turnbull 's seat , normally a Liberal stronghold , in a by-election this month after the former premier quit politics . That minority status came into effect on Monday . <p> Already relying on the support of independent lawmakers to progress his legislative agenda , Morrison suffered another major blow on Tuesday when backbench MP Julia Banks said she was quitting the Liberal party immediately to sit as an independent . <p> The Liberals are the senior partner in the governing coalition . <p> Banks said she was quitting the party of over disunity , its treatment of women and its policies on energy and climate change . <p> However , she also said she would support the government on matters of confidence and supply until the election .
