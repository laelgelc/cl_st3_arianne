The Pacific-European Union Marine Partnership Programme has been designed to address some of the most serious challenges faced by the Pacific Islands region . Among these are the increasing depletion of coastal fisheries resources ; the threats to marine biodiversity , including negative impacts of climate change and disasters ; the uneven contribution of oceanic fisheries to national economic development ; the need for improved education and training in the sector ; and the need to mainstream a rights-based approach and to promote greater recognition of gender issues within the sector . <p> This 5-year programme is funded by the European Union ( EUR 35 million ) with additional targeted support from the government of Sweden ( EUR 10 million ) . Therefore , country consultations -- during the 15 November inception workshop -- are pivotal to ensure national ownership and timely responses to national priorities . <p> With PEUMP , the Swedish Government , EU and Pacific countries established a longstanding partnership on sustainable natural resources management and ocean governance . <p> PEUMP is an innovative programme that through a holistic , multi-sectoral and community-centred approach contributes to social , economic and environmental development in the Region . <p> The PEUMP Programme official launch ceremony will conclude the day with a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Child Restraint Technician Bruce Wilson , a Director of Cheeks in Seats , a national charity who organized the event said " It was great to be able to support a community that had requested our presence .
