<h> Amps in your pants - Stick It To The Man <p> Words : Arnold Difflock <p> 23 Nov 2018 <p> Like it or not , the electric car debate is gathering momentum . The Drive Electric lobby group is doing a very good job of telling the world why electric cars are so good for us ( but forgetting to mention the downsides ) , and now climate change Minister James Shaw ( who does n't even drive ) has signalled that ' decent ' incentives will soon be in place . <p> You know what that means . It means that if electric cars are unaffordable for the zealots who want to drive them , the rest of us are going to have to pay . <p> Make no mistake , electric cars are sensible in several ways , and the incredible , silent torque of the more expensive ones adds a new dimension to driving pleasure . <p> But electric cars are a long way from taking over the world and we need to know why . This is causing a huge escalation in the price of batteries which is more than cancelling out the economies of scale of increased electric car production . <p> If there is hardly enough raw material for electric cars at one per cent of the market , how is there ever going to be enough for 30 per cent , never mind 100 ? One of the precious metals needed for electric car batteries is cobalt , which is the safe element in the cathode , necessary to prevent overheating and helping to prevent battery degradation . The bulk of the world 's cobalt comes from the Congo , where it 's mined by children working in dangerous and oppressive conditions which have attracted the attention of Amnesty International . <p> This fact is conveniently ignored by the environment-loving Green faction .
