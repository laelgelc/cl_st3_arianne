<h> Low-Carbon Transport by 2050 is Possible with Trade-Offs <p> Reducing the carbon footprint from transport in New Zealand by up to 90% by 2050 is achievable with a combination of new vehicle technologies , behavioural change and substantial investment in renewable energy generation , states a new report by sustainability advisors thinkstep . Renewable electricity generation would need to nearly double , or 5% of all agricultural land in New Zealand would need to be converted to biofuel production , to support low carbon transport from local energy sources . <p> " Currently transport is responsible for roughly one fifth of all GHG emissions , " says Dr Jeff Vickers , Technical Director of thinkstep and lead author of the report . " When considering the carbon footprint of products and services that New Zealanders consume -- rather than including those that are destined for offshore markets -- transport 's contribution jumps to over 40% .
