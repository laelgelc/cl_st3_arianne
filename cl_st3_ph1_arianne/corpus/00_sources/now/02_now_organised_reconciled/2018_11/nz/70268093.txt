In fact they shot him with arrows , tied a rope round his neck and then threw the body on the beach as a warning to others . <p> It 's not like John Chau was not forewarned . These guys on the Sentinel Islands have been repelling the 21st Century since the day they were first discovered . There was the epic TV shots back in the day when an islander spent some time one sunny afternoon shooting arrows at a circling helicopter . <p> John Chau 's family has asked that the islanders are not prosecuted for the so called crime , which shows that the arrogance of the missionary extends to his family .
