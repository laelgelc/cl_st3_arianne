<h> Virtual power station hailed as ' a game-changer ' <p> A virtual community power station launched today will collect solar electricity from 3000 roof-tops and allow households to trade power . <p> Photo : RNZ <p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw said the new virtual power station would be the largest in the world and would act like a bank for electricity , which the national grid would be able to access . <p> " This comes at zero up front cost . It 's like a leasing scheme , so you 're not actually paying to install the system on your roof . You 're participating in a virtual power plant and you get the benefit of that in the form of reduced household electricity bills . <p> " If Transpower needs to use a bit of your battery as backup then you get paid for that as well . " <p> He said when electricity use was peaking , Transpower would simply draw on some of the battery sitting in someone 's garage . <p> The virtual power plant would store up to 10 megawatts of available peaking electricity for the New Zealand system . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ It 's not just about the solar power on my or your roof but how those work together , " said Mr Shaw . <p> Solar energy company Solarcity will charge a leasing fee to install the solar panels on rooftops . Chief executive Andrew Booth said households taking part would be able to trade electricity and halve their power bills . <p> " They can exchange power with each other and also share it with families and friends . It really helps to drive down the amount they pay for power and helps the national grid at times of crisis . <p> " Each system that 's deployed has a battery system and that 's a real game changer . It allows New Zealanders to take control of when and how they use power , buy power when it 's cheap or buy it from their own roof and also sell it but also share it with community members .
