<h> In the face of US sanctions and corrupt ideologues , Iranians get by on defiance <p> In the Islamic Republic of Iran , Peter Calder finds almost everything is forbidden , yet accessible . <p> In Iran , entering a roundabout is an undertaking of knuckle-whitening implausibility . The rule , if you can call it that , seems to be that the traffic already on the roundabout gives way to traffic entering it . More likely , no one and everyone gives way all at the same time . Somehow it seems to work . <p> As a passenger in a range of stop-start vehicles during a month I spent in the country in September , I found the driving an apt metaphor for the way the place runs .
