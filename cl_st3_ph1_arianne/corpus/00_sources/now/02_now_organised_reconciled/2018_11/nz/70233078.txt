<p> You may think climate change is something we will face in the future , but going by last summer , it may already be here . <p> There has never been a season like it , Jeff Sinnott from Vinesense says of the 2018 Central Otago summer . Days of extreme heat from December through to February eventuated in the earliest harvest ever -- in some places 33 days early when compared with the long-term average . <p> Pity the poor grapes when the vineyard is hitting temperatures into the high 30 's . <p> How can you protect that fruit from the inevitable sunburn that accompanies such highs ? <p> That was the question Sinnott found himself asking , when one block was inadvertently leaf plucked in early January . <p> The contractors went in to trim and as they have done every year before , they also leaf plucked .
