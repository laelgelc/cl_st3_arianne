I even started an electricity retailer , energyclubnz , to help people reduce both their usage and the carbon intensity . These were actually under-sized for the space that they were heating . <p> When set at 21 degrees Celsius , the house consumed 80kWh per day of electricity ( between $16-$25 per day ) which was ridiculous . <p> Reducing by 1C reduced the daily usage by more than a third to 50kWh and then reducing to 19C reduced consumption to 35kWh . <p> Without heating , we now consume an average of 9-11kWh per day , which results in a really low energyclubnz bill . <p> One degree can make a big difference , but more importantly , try to only heat the space needed by the most efficient method . <p> Another area to consider is your hot water or heat pumps , which could be causing very high bills if they have n't been serviced recently .
