<h> Dunedin : new hospital puts many balls in air <p> In the second of two pieces about the renewal of Dunedin , David Williams examines a single construction project that will force change across a city . <p> Dunedin 's hospital , north of the Octagon towards the university , has been substandard for years -- parts of it for decades . <p> A hodgepodge of old buildings crammed onto a limited site throws up predictable problems of poor design and bad layout . But those issues are compounded by leaks , asbestos scares and a spiral of health board cost-cutting , including maintenance spending , brought on by years of rising deficits . <p> Hospital staff -- there are about 3000 -- have been frustrated and demoralised by the neglect . Trainee doctors have been warned by unions to stay away . The hospital lost accreditation for intensive care training and radiology services , the latter of which remains operational but means it ca n't access ACC revenue . <p> All this increases risks to patients .
