<h> Project Ireland 2040 aims to Brexit-proof Ireland with a particular focus on the Border Regions <p> Reporter : <p> Leitrim Observer Reporter <p> 16 Feb 2018 <p> 0 <p> The Cabinet met in IT Sligo this morning . <p> The ? 116 billion Project Ireland 2040 plan , launched this morning at the Institute of Technology in Sligo , aims to Brexit-proof Ireland by investing in the future with a particular focus on the Border Regions . <p> An Taoiseach , Leo Varadkar launched the plan following a special Cabinet meeting at the IT . <p> According to the Government , Project Ireland 2040 takes a radically different approach to future planning by focusing not just on bricks and mortar , but on social , economic and cultural development . It links planning and investment for the first time in Irish history , balances rural and urban investment , and will avoid the mistakes of the past . <p> Three quarters of new growth will be outside Dublin , with 50% of the projected population growth planned for our towns , villages and rural areas and 50% for our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ up and not out . And it 's underpinned by a 10 year ? 116 billion National Development Plan . This is a dramatic increase in public investment for Ireland , and makes Ireland a European leader for investment , leaving behind the lost decade since 2008 . <p> It includes four new funds designed to stimulate renewal and investment in rural and urban areas , the environment and innovation : * ? 2 billion Urban Regeneration and Development Fund ; * ? 1 billion Rural Development Fund ; * ? 500 million Climate Action Fund ; * ? 500 Disruptive Technologies Fund . <p> There is also a significant focus on the environment with ? 22 billion allocated to tackling and dealing with climate change across transport , energy and commercial State agencies . <p> An Taoiseach said : " This marks a significant milestone in our country 's development , the point at which we put a lost decade behind us and move forward into a new decade of expansion .
