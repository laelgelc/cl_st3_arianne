<h> New climate-smart technologies will boost agriculture and jobs in West Africa <p> A new programme is being launched to equip farmers in Africa with climate-smart agricultural tools and technologies . <p> The project is being rolled out by CORAF , a West African research institute , with the support of the World Bank . <p> It seeks to place innovative technologies in the hands of farmers to better protect them from the impacts of climate change . <p> It is hoped that by scaling up the use of new technologies , farmers across the continent can improve productivity , increase climate resiliency , and encourage the younger generation to seek a career in agriculture . <p> ICT tools and geo-mapping are two such examples that can be used to help crop yields . These provide farmers with added knowledge to improve soil management and growth . <p> It is thought the scheme , which will be officially launched later this year , will extend across West and Central Africa , from Sierra Leone to Cameroon . <p> " This program has assigned itself very ambitious targets because West and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 40 percent must be women . The technologies disseminated have to be linked to critical areas such as climate-smart agriculture , nutrition , mechanization , and processing . And it will be judged on the number of permanent and seasonal jobs it creates , " said Dr. Abdou Tenkouano , Executive Director of CORAF . <p> The project is designed to build on the success of the West Africa Agricultural Productivity Program , which was launched in 2008 with World Bank funding . It has impacted nine million people : increasing annual incomes , boosting crop yields and reducing the prevalence of hunger in West Africa . <p> However , more needs to be done , particularly to insulate the region from extreme weather events , such as drought and flooding , which are made more common due to climate change . <p> " Despite the progress made , agricultural productivity in the West and Central Africa sub-region still lags behind the rest of the World , " said Dr. Ni ? yidouba Lamien , who is the programme 's regional coordinator . <p> " Focus has to go beyond @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ system to address the demand of an increasing population , address youth unemployment , climate change , migration , gender , and nutrition " .
