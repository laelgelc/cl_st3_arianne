<h> Prince of Wales urges immediate action to protect coral reefs <p> The United Kingdom 's Prince of Wales has spoken of the need for a " rallying cry for action " to stop the decline of the world 's coral reefs . <p> Speaking at the launch of the International Year of the Reef in London yesterday , the Prince said that coral reefs were " at a critical tipping point " and it was up to humans to ensure their survival or " fatally compromise our ability to safeguard the world 's coral reefs and the species that will support future generations of humans and countless other species " . <p> The International Year of the Reef is a campaign designed by The Ocean Agency , working with both UN Environment and the International Coral Reef Initiative .
