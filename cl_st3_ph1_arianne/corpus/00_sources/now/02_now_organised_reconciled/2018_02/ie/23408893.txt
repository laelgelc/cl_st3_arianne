<h> Over 100 global cities now powered by at least 70% renewables <p> Global cities are powering ahead with the renewable energy transition it was revealed today . <p> New data has shown that 102 cities around the world are now sourcing at least 70 percent of their electricity from renewables . Encouragingly , this is more than twice the figure from just a few years ago . <p> The cities have large populations , such as Seattle , Vancouver , Oslo , Auckland and Nairobi . <p> The analysis , provided by the non-profit group CDP , also shows that 43 cities have now reached the astounding feat of running on 100 percent renewable energy . <p> This includes Reykjav ? k in Iceland and the city of Basel in Switzerland , both of which benefit from high levels of hydropower or geothermal . <p> Burlington in the US state of Vermont also gets all its electricity from wind , solar , biomass and hydropower . <p> Kyra Appleby , Director of Cities at CDP said that the news was important given that cities are responsible for over two-thirds @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ potential for them to lead on building a sustainable economy " , she said , <p> " Reassuringly , our data shows much commitment and ambition . Cities not only want to shift to renewable energy but , most importantly - they can . We urge all cities to disclose to us , work together to meet the goals of the Paris Agreement and prioritize the development of ambitious renewable energy procurement strategies . The time to act is now . " <p> Burlington 's Mayor , Miro Weinberger , said " We have seen first-hand that renewable energy boosts our local economy and creates a healthier place to work , live , and raise a family . We encourage other cities around the globe to follow our innovative path as we all work toward a more sustainable energy future " . <p> The data was released ahead of an international conference on the role of cities in tackling climate change . It shows that 275 cities are reporting using hydropower , 189 are harnessing wind and 184 are benefiting from solar photovoltaics . An additional 164 use biomass and 65 geothermal.
