<h> New vertical forest to be built in Eindhoven <p> The Dutch city of Eindhoven will play host to new apartments designed to provide a model for sustainable residential buildings . <p> The Trudo Vertical Forest Tower will be a 75-meter high skyscraper , which will include space for 125 trees , and 5,200 plants . <p> Eindhoven is the latest city to support the construction of the new design , after similar towers were built in Milan , Nanjing , Paris , among others . <p> Vertical forests are the brainchild of Italian architect Stefano Boeri , who hopes the buildings will help boost air quality , increase biodiversity and reduce urban expansion . <p> The latest tower is also the first to be adopted as a social housing project with 125 units made available for low-income families . <p> Mr Boeri commented : " The high-rise building of Eindhoven confirms that it is possible to combine the great challenges of climate change with those of housing shortages . The Liuzhou Forest City project is currently under construction in the southern region of Guangxi . <p> 175 hectares of land along the Liujiang river is being transformed into a model for how to build sustainable urban areas in the 21st century . The new city will be home to 30,000 people , 40,000 trees and one million plants . Renewable technologies will also be widespread with solar panels on roofs and geothermal energy providing interior air conditioning .
