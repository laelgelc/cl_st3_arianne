<p> Roscrea Branch of the Mothers ' Union hosted an information evening on household recycling recently . The speaker was Mr. Dave Corboy of the Recycling Ambassador Programme which is funded by the Dept.of Communications , Climate Change and Environment . <p> Mr. Corboy stated that 40% of material in household recycling bins ends up being treated as general rubbish because of contamination or mixed materials being put in the bins . All items must be washed out , dried and placed separately in the bins . If any item is dirty or wet , it can contaminate the surrounding items which must then be put in the general rubbish . <p> Glass should not be placed in the recycling bins but instead brought to the designated glass recycling bins found in every town . When the material is sorted into the various categories , it is baled and shipped mostly to the UK or Holland for re-processing .
