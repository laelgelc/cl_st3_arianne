<h> Government opposes Bill to stop the State issuing licenses for fossil fuel exploration <p> February 7th , 2018 <p> The Government today opposed a Bill to stop the State from issuing any new licenses for the exploration and extraction of fossil fuels in Ireland . <p> The Climate Emergency Measures Bill seeks to stop the government from issuing any new licenses for the exploration and extraction of fossil fuels in the State , both onshore and offshore . <p> Speaking before the Dail this evening , the Minister of State with responsibility for Natural Resources , Sean Kyne , TD said that the Government " must oppose the Bill today " . <p> With the Government 's opposition , the Bill will now go to a full vote , which is expected to take place in the Dail by lunchtime tomorrow . <p> There are currently over 40 licenses granted for a range of fossil fuel , some of which have contracts up to the late 2020s . <p> People Before Profit ( PBP ) Deputy Brid Smith , who introduced the Bill last November , said that the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ called for the cabinet to " get their priorities in place " . <p> She added : " If we take the Paris Climate Agreement seriously the Oireachtas will support this Bill . If we take the National Transition Objective seriously , we will support this Bill . If we take the National Mitigation Plan seriously , we will support this Bill . " <p> People Before Profit TD is putting forward a climate emergency bill- the bill is supported by many climate campaigners including Friends Of The Earth , Not Here Not Anywhere , Trocaire and experts in the field .
