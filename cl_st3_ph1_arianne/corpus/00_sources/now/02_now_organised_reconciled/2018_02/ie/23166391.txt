<p> Millions of Americans every year are affected by hazardous public drinking water . <h> By Madison Dapcevich <p> 12 Feb 2018 , 20:00 <p> A study spanning more than three decades suggests millions of Americans are using public water from systems violating health standards . <p> That 's as much as 8 percent of public water systems in the US . <p> The study , published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences , is the first to assess nationwide trends in drinking water quality violations across several decades by looking at violations in 17,900 community water systems -- or 87 percent of the population -- between 1982 and 2015 . <p> In any given year , between 9 and 45 million people are affected , roughly 4 to 28 percent of the US population . <p> In 2015 alone , nearly 21 million people were using water systems in violation . <p> More than 600,000 observations over a 34-year period indicate that those violations are more likely to happen in low-income rural areas in public-owned systems . <p> Bad news for those of you in Oklahoma @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The good news : Not all are health-based violations . <p> Under the 1974 Safe Drinking Water Act , the Environmental Protection Agency regulates drinking water quality . If a water system does n't meet standards then a violation is issued , but occurrence and reporting differ from state to state . <p> The EPA uses the number of " total coliforms " ( bacteria not harmful to humans ) as an indication of the health of water systems . It 's the most common type of violation and most of the time it does n't pose a health risk . However , it can indicate whether E. coli , parasites , and other harmful viruses might be present . <p> However , the majority of violations come right from your kitchen .
