<p> On 8 January that year , a severe cold snap kicked in which saw the whole island covered in snow for 10 days . The Big Snow was appropriately named -- the unprecedented blizzard saw record levels of snow and saw many areas cut off from the rest of civilisation . <p> Obviously there have been more recent cold snaps ( in 2010 and 2011 for example ) . In 1982 , as now , however the east of the country was the worst affected , with 25 centimetres of the wintry white stuff falling at Dublin Airport . <p> The snowstorm resulted in traffic chaos across the country , and saw fuel and food shortages together with mass school ( for as long as two weeks in some places ) and workplace closures .
