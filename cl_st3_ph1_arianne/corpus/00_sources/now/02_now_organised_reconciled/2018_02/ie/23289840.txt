<p> The Survival Game is set in a world ravaged by climate change . Main character Mhairi has only two things , a gun with no bullets and her identity papers , and she helps a young boy who no papers find safety . <p> McNeil said : " The Survival Game is a searing , timely story , as arresting as it is beautiful . Tautly powerful and brilliant , this is a book of our times -- every reader will imagine it is their story . It 's a game changer . " <p> Singer is also the author of Feather Boy ( HarperCollins Children 's Books ) , which won the Blue Peter Best Book of the Year prize in 2002 . Her 2015 book Island ( Caboodle Books ) was longlisted for the CILIP Carnegie Medal .
