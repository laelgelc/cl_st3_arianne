It had been at least a decade since I had been this way and I had n't seen these before . I checked into my hotel , the Lake House near Narin , and cautiously asked for the Wi-Fi password . It turned out the broadband was pretty decent . <p> On this remote , storm-blasted , far-western edge of Europe , the 21st century was making its presence felt .
