<h> Governments all across the world are starting to shift away from coal-powered energy out of environmental and economic concerns . And it 's not just climate change and the Paris agreement that are the catalyst . Renewable energy is now cheaper than coal in most parts of the world . <p> In late 2017 , the conservative UK government -- the country that kicked off global coal use worldwide -- announced it would close the country 's eight remaining coal power stations by 2025 and phase out coal , with bipartisan political and public support .
