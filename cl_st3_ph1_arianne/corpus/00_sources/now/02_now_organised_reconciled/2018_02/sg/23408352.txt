They should follow the example of Fiji , which last year became the first Asian sovereign issuer of a FJD100 million ( $48.13 million ) green bond . <p> Sovereign issuers in developing Asia see green bonds as an opportunity to set a benchmark for other domestic issuers , and increase liquidity through a growing local currency green bond market . <p> Issuing green bonds also demonstrates the sovereign 's commitment to meeting its Nationally Determined Contributions to reduce greenhouse gas emissions under the Paris Agreement in the medium term . This , in turn , encourages green business and shows citizens that their government is taking environmental issues and climate change seriously . <p> Finally , green bonds allow tapping into new segments of long-term capital like environmentally conscious groups of people , and thus help diversify the investor base .
