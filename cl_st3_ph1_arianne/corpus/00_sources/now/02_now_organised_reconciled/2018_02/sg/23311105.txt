REUTERS/Wolfgang Rattay/File Photo <p> BRUSSELS ( Reuters ) - The European Union should raise taxes on fossil fuels to help meet goals on climate change and plug a budget gap after Britain leaves the bloc , former senior EU officials said in a letter to EU leaders . <p> European leaders will discuss options for the bloc 's next long term budget from 2021 to 2027 at a summit on Friday and the taxes were not among those laid out by the EU executive ahead of the talks . <p> Instead , the European Commission proposed that a share of the revenue from national auctions of carbon permits under the bloc 's cap-and-trade Emission Trading System be used . <p> " We call on the EU to create a contribution of the oil , gas and coal sector to the EU budget , " 19 economists wrote in the letter seen by Reuters on Wednesday and dated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ former WTO director Pascal Lamy , former German finance minister Hans Eichel and Belgian economist Paul de Grauwe were among the signatories . <p> A " modest " price level of five euros per tonne of carbon dioxide on coal , oil and gas burnt in Europe would generate revenues of around 17 billion euros per year , they wrote . <p> The Brussels-based climate campaign group Transport &amp; Environment said the letter added clout to policy debates over using taxes to tackle air pollution from transport , Europe 's largest source of emissions . <p> As part of the Paris Agreement to limit global warming to no more than 2 degrees , the bloc has pledged to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by at least 40 percent below 1990 levels by 2030.
