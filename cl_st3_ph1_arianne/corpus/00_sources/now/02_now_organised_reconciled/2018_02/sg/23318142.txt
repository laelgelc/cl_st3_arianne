<p> Singaporean producer Low Ser En has won a Bafta award for Best British Short Animation for her stop-motion short Poles Apart . <p> The 12-minute film , about a polar bear and a grizzly bear who are brought together by their changing habitats , is the graduation project of a multinational team of 10 students from Britain 's National Film and Television School ( NFTS ) . <p> The annual Bafta awards are widely seen as the British equivalent of the Oscars in Hollywood . <p> Poles Apart beat two other films : Have Heart , about a looping gif that has an existential crisis , and Mamoon , which tells the story of a mother forced to leave her home with her child using projected animation on polystyrene film sets . <p> Ms Low , 27 , who returned to Singapore on Tuesday night after her triumph in London on Sunday , told The Straits Times that the team was " very surprised " by the win . <p> " It was incredible , " she said . " We were very surprised and very happy about it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ came after a year of festival runs . " <p> Her statuette , which she received alongside British director Paloma Baeza , now sits in her Housing Board flat in Kallang . <p> Ms Low had both a creative and a business role as producer , working with the director on the script , as well as coming up with the budget and schedule for the production and looking through the edits . <p> " We got the inspiration from an article we read online about a phenomenon where the polar bears are moving south and grizzly bears are moving north because of climate change , " she said . <p> " It 's a climate refugee story . " <p> The team took 15 months to complete the film as stop-motion is a painstaking process involving capturing each scene with animated figures . The bears are voiced by two-time Oscar nominee Helena Bonham Carter and actor Joseph May . <p> The film is not yet available here , but there are plans to put it on video platform Vimeo . <p> Ms Low received a scholarship from local film @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Singapore to study at the NFTS . <p> She graduated in February last year with a Master of Arts degree and now works with mm2 Entertainment .
