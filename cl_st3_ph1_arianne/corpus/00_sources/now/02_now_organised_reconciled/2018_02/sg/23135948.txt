<h> Bolivia 's fabled carnival marches on despite deaths in floods <p> Bolivia Oruro Carnival got under way Saturday with stunning costumes and parades , though the mood was dampened by the loss of 17 people killed in flooding and 8,200 families left homeless by heavy rains since November . <p> President Evo Morales said climate change had " shown up at Carnival , leaving disasters , with the loss of human lives and of homes . " <p> About 25,000 dancers take part in the pre-Lenten blowout that is Bolivia 's biggest tourist attraction and brings as many as half a million people to the sleepy town . <p> The pageant along the city 's cobblestones is a mixture of Spanish colonial traditions with those of the ancient indigenous Inca and Aymara peoples in a ritual seeking better farming and good health . <p> In 2001 , the UNESCO cultural organization declared the carnival an intangible cultural heritage of humanity .
