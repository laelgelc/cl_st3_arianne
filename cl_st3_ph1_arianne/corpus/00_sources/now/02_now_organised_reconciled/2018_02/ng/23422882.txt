<h> African Union urged to take ownership of Lake Chad project <p> The Lake Chad Basin Commission ( LCBC ) on Tuesday called on the member-states of the African Union ( AU ) to take ownership of the project to save Lake Chad from imminent extinction . <p> Its Executive Secretary , Alhaji Sanusi Abdullahi , made the call at the opening of the 63rd Ordinary Session of the Council of Ministers of the LCBC in Abuja . <p> He underscored the need for Africa to collectively tackle challenges facing the survival of Lake Chad by demonstrating the needed political will . <p> He said it was an indisputable fact that Climate Change was the major cause of the shrinking of the lake and the degradation of the lands and environment , which had resulted in the loss of agricultural productivity and ecosystem . <p> " Thus , it is now time for the African Union to face the challenges in the Lake Chad basin as an African strategic problem . <p> " The AU should factor in the restoration of the lake as part of the pan-African initiative for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ West Africa and the Sahel . <p> " Africa must collectively resolve to tackle these issues head-on with all we have through the strong insurgents , adding that a large number of the insurgents were surrendering because of the intensified efforts of non-military actors . <p> The executive secretary , nonetheless , urged the people to
