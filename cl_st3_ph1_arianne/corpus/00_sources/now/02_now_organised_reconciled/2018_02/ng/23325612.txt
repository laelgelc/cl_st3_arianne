<p> The National Universities Commission ( NUC ) has granted approval to the Maritime University , Okerenkoko , Delta , to begin undergraduate degree programmes from the 2017/2018 academic session . <p> The Senior Special Assistant to the Vice President on Media and Publicity , Mr Laolu Akande , gave the indication in a release issued on Wednesday . <p> He said the approval underscored FG 's commitment to develop Niger Delta in line with the Buhari administration 's new vision to develop the region . <p> According to the release the NUC approved that academic activities begin in three faculties namely : Transport , Engineering and Environmental Management . <p> Akande said that Faculty of Transport has four departments , namely : Nautical Science , Transport Logistics Management , Marine Economics and Finance , and Port Management . <p> He said Faculty of Engineering has five departments -- Marine Engineering , Civil Engineering , Electrical Engineering , Mechanical Engineering and Petroleum and Gas Engineering . <p> Also Faculty of Environmental Management has four departments : Environmental Management and Pollution , Meteorology and Climate Change , Fisheries and
