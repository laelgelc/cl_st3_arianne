<h> Union Government approves new project to control pollution of River Sal at Navelin , Goa <p> Feb 5 , 2018 14:56 IST <h> Rupali Pruthi <p> Union Government approves new project to control pollution of River Sal at Navelin , Goa <p> The Union Government on February 4 , 2018 sanctioned a new project to control pollution in River Sal at Navelim town , Goa . <p> The project was approved by the Union Ministry of Environment , Forest &amp; Climate Change under the National River Conservation Plan ( NRCP ) . <p> Highlights of the project* The project will be completed at cost of Rs 61.74 crore and this cost will be borne by the Centre and State on 60:40 basis . <p> * Under the project , around 32 km of sewers will be laid and sewage treatment plant of 3 million litres per day ( MLD ) will be constructed . <p> * The project is scheduled for completion by January 2021 . <p> * It will help in reduction of pollution load in the river Sal and improvement in its water quality , besides improving @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ It will take care of entire catchment area of the river through measures like rejuvenation of lakes and wetlands , construction of a small-check dam to ensure continuous water flow and afforestation . <p> In the year 2015 , the Central Pollution Control Board ( CPCB ) had identified the Sal river stretch at Navelim as one of the 302 polluted river stretches across the country based on Biochemical Oxygen Demand ( BOD ) levels .
