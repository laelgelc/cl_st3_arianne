<h> Science film festival from Feb 20 <p> Guwahati : Gauhati University ( GU ) will host the eighth National Science Film Festival of India for the first time from Tuesday to Saturday . <p> The festival will be organised by Vigyan Prasar , department of science and technology , government of India , in collaboration with the department of communication and journalism of the university . For the first time , Vigyan Prasar will organise a national film festival in any university . <p> The five-day programme will be held to celebrate golden jubilee celebrations . Prior to this , such programmes were organised in Calcutta , Chennai and other cities of the country . <p> " The aim of the festival is to promote scientific temperament so that one can think and act scientifically . The festival will have three categories - professional , unprofessional and students . All the films are based on important issues such as health , water reservoir system , pollution , climate change and scientific achievements , " said head of Science Film 's division , Vigyan Prasar , Nimesh Kapoor , in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ : " The films will be from countries including Germany , France , North Korea , Switzerland among others . " <p> The programme will be attended by Mike Pandey who is chairman of jury members , Manoj Patairiya , director of Council of Scientific and Industrial Research ( CSIR ) -National Institute of Science Communication and Information Resources ( NISCAIR ) , New Delhi , minister of science and technology , government of Assam , Keshav Mahanta and Sambhu Nath Singh the director , School of New Media Studies , Ignou , New Delhi . <p> The programme will witness participation of around 200 filmmakers , film critics , scientists and around 500 students from across the country . A total of 82 films , out of 200 related to the topic of science communication will be screened in the festival . <p> The award winning film Forest - Securing The Future by three-time Green Oscar awardee Mike Pandey will be screened in the inaugural session of the festival .
