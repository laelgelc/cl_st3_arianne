The central committee of the Communist Party of China has proposed removing the constitutional limit of two consecutive terms for the offices of the president and the vice-president . China is increasingly aiming to be seen as the top-most global leader . Xi 's speech in Davos last year defending globalization and China 's growing role in climate change conferences should be seen in this light . While Mao believed in helping revolutionary movements around the world , Deng 's approach was informed by his mantra of " hide your strength , bide your time " . Xi is now reversing Deng 's legacy on this front too . <p> Deng was the great economic reformer and his policies can be credited with pulling millions of Chinese out of poverty .
