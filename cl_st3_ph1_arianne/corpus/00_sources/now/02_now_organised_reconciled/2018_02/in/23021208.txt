<h> Budget non-populous , special scheme without substance : Green body <h> Share Via Email <p> NEW DELHI : The Union Budget 2018-19 has left the environment sector in the lurch as it is " neither populous nor helping ' Ease of Doing ' business " , with the government 's special scheme to combat air pollution being " without any substance " , says SANDRAP , a network working on issues related with water sector . <p> The South Asia Network on Dams , Rivers and People ( SANDRP ) said the budget had a " lacklustre " approach towards the crucial sector . <p> The budgetary allocation for the environment ministry for 2018-19 stood at Rs 2,675.42 crore , identical to last budget 's allocation . The ministry had got an increase of nearly 19 per cent during the 2017-18 budget . <p> Presenting his fifth straight budget in the Lok Sabha , Union Finance Minister Arun Jaitley had said air pollution in the Delhi-NCR region was a " cause of concern " . <p> He said a special scheme would be implemented to support the efforts of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the NCT of Delhi to address air pollution and subsidise the machinery required for institutional management of crop residue . <p> " The budget has nothing to offer for environment - even its promise of a scheme for incentivizing use of machines in Delhi and adjoining states ... is without substance , sustainable development , democratic governance , equity , participatory or transparent decision making , " Himanshu Thakkar of SANDRP said . <p> The recently released Economic Survey had called the national capital one of the " unhealthiest " cities in the world in terms of air pollution . <p> The survey had suggested heavy penalties for burning agricultural waste and more incentives for farmers to prevent the " alarmingly " poor air quality in Delhi-NCR and adjoining areas . <p> Thakkar said one had thought that the budget would have something concrete to offer with regard to environment and climate change , given the impending elections and Prime Minister Narendra Modi 's recent visit to Davos , wherein he said climate change was one of the biggest challenges of future . <p> " We are mostly disappointed . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ statements , but lacked substance to inspire confidence . The track record of the present government has been so poor that such empty words are not going to convince anyone , " he added .
