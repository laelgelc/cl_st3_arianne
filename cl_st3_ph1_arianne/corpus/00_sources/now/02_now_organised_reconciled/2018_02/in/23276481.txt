<h> NTPC pays interim dividend of Rs 2,251 crore for current financial year <p> New Delhi : NTPC Ltd , India 's state-owned power generator , today announced it has paid an interim dividend of Rs 2,251.01 crore , equivalent to 27.30 per cent of the paid-up equity share capital of the company , for the current financial year . <p> " The RTGS advice for the transfer of Rs 1,401.81 crore to Government of India , being the share of Government of India in the interim dividend , was presented by Gurdeep Singh , Chairman and Managing Director of NTPC to R K Singh , Minister of State ( Independent Charge ) for Power and New &amp; Renewable Energy , " the company said in a statement . <p> " To save the environment and to fight climate change , my government has planned a major campaign . By 2022 , we want to generate 175 GW of renewable energy . In the last three years , we have already achieved 60 GW or around one-third of this target , " he said .
