<h> Drawback in Tamil Nadu Coastal Zone Management Plan draft ? <h> Share Via Email <p> CHENNAI : Tamil Nadu becomes one of the first few States in the country to prepare the draft Coastal Zone Management Plan ( CZMP ) , a key document that streamlines the development along the coast and protect ecologically sensitive areas . The State Department of Environment ( DoE ) on Monday uploaded 115 toposheets of all 13 coastal districts in Tamil Nadu in its official website , seeking comments and objections from public within next 45 days . However , some critical informations needed for the active public participation are missing from these CZM maps . <p> Firstly , there is no hazard line , which forms the basis of CZMP taking into account sea-level rise , tides , waves and shoreline changes . In case this CZMP is approved without hazard line , it would potentially open up thousands of acres of low-lying wetlands for real estate . <p> As per the annexure-1 of the CRZ Notification , 2011 , which lists out the guidelines and preparation of the Coastal Zone Management @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the area and the existing authorised developments on the seaward side . But , none of these were incorporated , which defeats the very purpose of calling for public consultation , alleged the environmentalists . <p> Nityanand Jayaraman of Coastal Resource Centre and Save Ennore Campaign , talking to Express , asked how fisher folk could give their opinions when there was no long-term housing plan put in place in the draft . <p> Also , the draft CZMP should be given wide publicity .
