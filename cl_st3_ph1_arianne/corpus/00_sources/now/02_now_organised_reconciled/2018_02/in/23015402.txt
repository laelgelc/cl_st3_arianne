<h> CMFRI open house points to impact of climate change <p> The visitors also displayed keen interest in understanding the functioning of the fish ageing laboratory of the CMFRI . <p> Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute <p> Kochi : The open house , an exhibition cum education programme , held by the Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute ( CMFRI ) on its 71st foundation day on Saturday here highlighted the impact of climate change in fisheries . Aimed at creating awareness among the public , the CMFRI displayed the research output of the study on climate change conducted under the scheme of the National Innovations on Climate Resilient Agriculture ( NICRA ) . <p> Explaining the details of the study to the visitors who thronged CMFRI to understand the latest developments in the marine research and enjoy the wonders of marine biodiversity , the scientists said that there had been changes in scores of areas such as sea surface temperature ( SST ) , chlorophylla , wind and rainfall , ocean current , spawning season , maturity , distribution and catch of various marine fishes owing to the climate change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ commercially important fish resources affects the catch and thereby livelihoods and national economy to sustainable level . " Climate change coupled with the intense fishing pressure has an adverse effect on fisheries resources " , said Dr P.U . Zacharia , project coordinator of NICRA . Adaptive measures such as reducing fishing efforts , forestration of mangroves , farming of seaweeds and promotion of cage fish farming are the need of the hour , he said . <p> According to the NICRA study report , habitat mapping and ecological modelling could be done to prevent over-exploitation and manage climate stressors . During the open house , students and the public also got an opportunity to enjoy a rare privilege of getting a ringside view of the secrets of marine life . As part of the programme , the National Marine Biodiversity Museum , various state-of-the-art laboratories , marine research aquarium , Agricultural Technology Information Centre ( ATIC ) and hatcheries were opened to the public . The visitors also displayed keen interest in understanding the functioning of the fish ageing laboratory of the CMFRI.
