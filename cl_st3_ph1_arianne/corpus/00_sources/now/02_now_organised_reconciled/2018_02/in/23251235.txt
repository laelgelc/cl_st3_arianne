This would help to save Rs 2.63 crore per month , said the spokesperson . <p> The Cabinet gave its approval to strengthen the Himachal Pradesh Forest Ecosystem Climate Proofing Project being implemented in Chamba and Kangra districts with the assistance from the KFW Development Bank of Germany by providing more requisite staff . <p> This project is covering 600 panchayats in both districts for minimising and mitigating the negative impacts of climate change by increasing biodiversity and sustained income in rural areas through sustainable management of forest resources .
