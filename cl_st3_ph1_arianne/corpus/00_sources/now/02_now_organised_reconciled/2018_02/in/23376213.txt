<h> Government forms Niti forum for inclusive growth of northeast <p> PTI <p> Updated : Feb 25 , 2018 , 06.51 PM IST <p> 0Comments <p> The forum will also be tasked to review the development status in the northeast , said Singh , the DoNER minister . <p> NEW DELHI : A special Niti forum has been created to accelerate central government 's efforts to boost inclusive growth in the northeastern region of the country , Union minister Jitendra Singh said today . <p> Singh said the Niti forum for northeast will have its secretariat office in the Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region ( DoNER ) . <p> " The exclusive Niti Aayog forum will work with the objective to identify various constraints in the way of accelerated , inclusive and sustainable economic growth of the eight states of the northeastern region of India , " the minister told . <p> It will recommend suitable interventions for addressing the identified constraints , he said . <p> The forum will also be tasked to review the development status in the northeast , said Singh , the DoNER minister @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chairman of the Niti Aayog and the DoNER minister . <p> Other members of the forum will include secretaries of Ministry of Road Transport &amp; Highways , Railways , Power , Water Resources , River Development &amp; Ganga Rejuvenation , New &amp; Renewable Energy , Health and Family welfare , Human Resource Development and Environment , Forest and Climate Change . <p> The chief secretaries of all the eight states of the northeastern region will also be members of the forum . <p> In addition , secretary , North East Council ( NEC ) will be member secretary , while joint secretary ( North East ) in MHA and several other experts from other fields will be members .
