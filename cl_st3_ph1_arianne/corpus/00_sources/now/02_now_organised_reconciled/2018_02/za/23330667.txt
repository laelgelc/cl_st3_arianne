<h> Dam levels increase but certain provinces remain disaster areas <p> While the national average dam levels reported by the Department of Water and Sanitation are showing a slight increase around the country , certain provinces still remain disaster areas . <p> The latest dam levels report issued by the Department of Water and Sanitation show a slight increase of 2.1% from 60% last week to 62.1% this week after measuring the 215 dams across the country . <p> The department , however , noted that even though there was a meagre increase , Eastern , Western and Northern Cape Provinces remain national disaster areas , as announced by the Department of Cooperative Governance and Traditional Affairs recently . <h> Severe drought <p> This is due to the severe drought effect that is currently being experienced in the three provinces .
