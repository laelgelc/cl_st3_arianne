<h> Trump and climate change subject of pupils ' debate at Parliament <p> My annual inter-school debating competition is returning for a sixth year , and will be held on Friday , February 23 in the House of Commons . <p> All local secondary schools have been invited to take part and compete against each other for the title of Watford Debating Champions . The winners will also take home a trophy , vouchers and an invitation to tea with the House of Commons Speaker . <p> This year , teams of students from year 10 to 13 will take part from eight schools . There will be four knock-out rounds and they are set to debate a range of topics , including climate change , online censorship , Donald Trump and university fees . <p> The debates will be judged by an illustrious panel which is made up of journalists ( including the editor of the Watford Observer ! ) , county councillors and members of the House of Lords . <p> We are lucky to have such fantastic schools in Watford and I am always so impressed at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ put into it .
