The Government 's consultation on a new Climate Bill notes that reducing emissions can provide wide economic and social benefits like new jobs , improved air quality , and better health . While it proposes increasing the target to a 90 per cent reduction , the date it suggest would actually represent slower emission cuts than we 've achieved so far . <p> But aiming earlier would drive essential innovation in transport , energy and agriculture -- the biggest sources of emissions .
