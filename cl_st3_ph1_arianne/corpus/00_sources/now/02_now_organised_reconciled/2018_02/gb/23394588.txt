<h> David A Kerfoot MBE , chairman of York , North Yorkshire and East Riding Enterprise Partnership <p> As I read about the potential closure of the Royal Mail sorting office in Hawes , I was once again drawn to reflect on the crucial role businesses play in breathing life into communities across York , North Yorkshire and the East Riding . <p> Whilst I scope plans for my time as the new chair of the Local Enterprise Partnership , I feel this is something we need to take account of . This is why economic development has been devolved to local partnerships like ours : so that we can truly reflect the local concerns . <p> Clearly , a sorting office closing does n't have the same devastating economic impact as a steel works shutting up shop . However , it will nevertheless have a profound impact on the community of Hawes , and on the lives of the people living there including rural business . It is impossible for them to understand that what makes the North special is the mix of urban and rural . <p> Of course , our fabulous coast and countryside provide a fantastic playground for the residents of Leeds , Bradford and Manchester , but they provide much more than that . This living and working landscape provides a raft of benefits from clear water to fresh air , food production to flood alleviation , and it even helps mitigate against climate change as our vast uplands store millions of tonnes of greenhouse gases . <p> We can see that change is happening now as austerity continues to bite , whilst efficiency dissipates its gradual impact on communities bit by bit . <p> We know there is more to come as Brexit drives the withdrawal of Common Agricultural Policy payments to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ use . This is what I believe the Local Enterprise Partnership is here for , to deepen that understanding , to be a voice in the corridors of power , and to draw on the expertise and resources of all our partners in coordinating a response . <p> We are uniquely positioned between national government , local government , institutions and local businesses .
