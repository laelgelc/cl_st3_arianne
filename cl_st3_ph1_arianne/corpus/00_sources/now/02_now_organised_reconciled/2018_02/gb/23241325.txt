<p> This fluorescing deep-water coral emits a warm , orange glow into the cool , dim waters where it grows tens of metres below the ocean 's surface . It 's a natural spectacle -- but , as researchers have recently discovered , that steady light also ensures the organism 's survival . <p> All corals rely on sunlight to enable their resident algae to photosynthesise and make food that keeps the coral alive . But for the deep-water variety , that 's a challenge because sunlight is scarce down below .
