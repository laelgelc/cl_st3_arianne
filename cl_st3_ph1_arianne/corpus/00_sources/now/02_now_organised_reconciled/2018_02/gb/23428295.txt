It 's particularly worrisome considering that most of these people are n't even aware of the risk they face . This research will help fill this critical information gap . " <p> Another key concern , Wing says , is that people continue to build in harm 's way , often without realising they are doing so . <p> The study -- which integrates new maps from the Environmental Protection Agency that more precisely estimate where @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ -- predicts that under potential population growth and development projections , more than 60 million Americans may be vulnerable to a 100-year flood by 2050 . <p> Wing added : " Because climate change may cause so-called ' 100-year ' floods to occur more frequently , even more people may be exposed to flooding in the future . All of this highlights the critical need for comprehensive floodplain and flood risk management planning . " <p> Kris Johnson , a scientist with The Nature Conservancy and co-author of the study , said : " By knowing those places most at risk from floods , we could avoid development in these areas . <p> " Protecting floodplains or developing them in ways that can withstand flooding -- such as flood-tolerant parks -- can prevent unnecessary risk to people and help avoid expensive damage to property and infrastructure .
