<h> Opinion Letters <h> Eliminate drive-thrus to combat global warming <p> I get so tired of the hypocrisy of our governments haranguing us about global warming . If we really want to reduce emissions , eliminate drive-thrus . According to the National Resources Canada website : " For every litre of gasoline used , a vehicle produces about 2.3 kilograms of ( carbon dioxide ) , the principal ( greenhouse gas ) linked to climate change .... If Canadian motorists avoided idling for just three minutes every day of the year , ( carbon dioxide ) emissions could be reduced by 1.4 million tonnes annually . This would be equal to saving 630 million litres of fuel and equivalent to taking 320,000 cars off of the road for the entire year .
