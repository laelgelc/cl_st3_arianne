<h> Kazakhstan proposes developing roadmap for Kazakh-EU cooperation <p> By George D. Gleboff and Aigerim Seisembayeva inInternationalon 27 February 2018 <p> BRUSSELS -- Kazakhstan has suggested developing a roadmap to implement the Enhanced Partnership and Cooperation Agreement ( EPCA ) between the nation and the European Union ( EU ) and its member states . Kazakh Foreign Minister Kairat Abdrakhmanov presented the idea in Brussels Feb. 26 during the 16th meeting of the Kazakhstan-EU Cooperation Council , the highest level dialogue platform between the two . <p> The proposed roadmap would seek to ensure the EPCA is effectively executed for both sides . The agreement , signed in 2015 , has so far been ratified by 21 of 28 EU member states and was approved by the European Parliament in December . It has been in force provisionally since May 2016 , after it had been ratified by Kazakhstan and approved by the European Commission , but requires the ratification by all member states and consent of the European Parliament in order to enter into force in full .
