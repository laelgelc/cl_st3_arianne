<h> Study points to agricultural fertilizer as leading cause of algae blooms in Lake Erie <p> A report issued by the International Joint Commission leaves little doubt that commercial fertilizer and manure applications are the largest sources of excess phosphorus into the western basin of Lake Erie . <p> The IJC commissioned the assessment to " better understand the influence of past , current and possible future nonpoint agricultural runoff of phosphorus into western Lake Erie , and their potential to cause atrophic conditions and nuisance and harmful algal blooms . " <p> The areas studied include the binational western basin and the St. Clair-Detroit River system . <p> " Even a small ' leakage ' of excess phosphorus may be sufficient to contribute to algal blooms , " the report states . <p> The report also indicates the widespread use of subsurface artificial tile drains may also increase rates of phosphorus entering the lake . <p> Authors say additional monitoring is needed to evaluate climate change impacts on nutrient inputs which will help them " adapt management actions that will meet targets for nutrient reductions into the western lake @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Lake Erie near the City of Toledo off the shore of Curtice , Ohio , on Aug. 3 , 2014 .
