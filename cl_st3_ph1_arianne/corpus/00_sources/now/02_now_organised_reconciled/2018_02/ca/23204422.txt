<h> Feds on track with funding to help developing countries grapple with climate change <p> Despite persistent federal deficits , the government says it is on track to meet a post-election promise to spend $2.65 billion by 2020 to help developing countries grapple with climate change . <p> As of February , Canada had provided more than $900 million in climate financing as part of its November 2015 commitment , one of the first major Liberal measures on the heels of the federal election , a spokesperson for International Development said Wednesday . <p> A further allotment of $169.7 million toward the program was included in new spending estimates the government tabled in Parliament this week , as it laid out new requests for spending authorities for the final three months of the fiscal year . <p> The funding will go towards development of low carbon , climate resilient economies in some of the regions that are most affected by weather and oceanic changes stemming from the warming of the atmosphere . <p> The allotments included $200 million for the second phase of the Canadian Fund for the Private Sector @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to the Green Climate Fund and $150 million in support of
