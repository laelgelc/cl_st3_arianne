In 2016 we did our first event at the University of Manitoba in Winnipeg and we already knew right from the start that we had outgrown our space , " said Organic Specialist for the Province and Key Organizer , Laura Telford . " This year we are happy to say we have already outgrown our space , so we are probably going to annex a few more rooms in 2020 .
