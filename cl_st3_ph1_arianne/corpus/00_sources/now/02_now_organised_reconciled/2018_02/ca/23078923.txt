<p> PARIS -- President Donald Trump and Emmanuel Macron will get another chance to test their tough handshakes and argue over climate change when the French president visits Washington in April . <p> French Foreign Minister Jean-Yves Le Drian said Wednesday on BFM television that Macron will visit Washington in late April , on Trump 's invitation . <p> Le Drian called it an " official visit , " short of the state visit that the White House had earlier promised . Macron 's office would n't comment or confirm a date for the trip . <p> Macron has struck up a friendly rivalry with Trump , starting from their first white-knuckle handshake last year . Macron has vocally opposed Trump on the Paris climate agreement and the Iran nuclear accord , but also made Trump the guest of honour at France 's elaborate Bastille Day celebration .
