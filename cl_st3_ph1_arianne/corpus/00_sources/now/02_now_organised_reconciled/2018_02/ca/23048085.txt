<h> Province confirms mercury at Dryden mill site <p> The Ontario government has confirmed high levels of mercury in the soil of the Dryden mill site , upstream from the communities of Grassy Narrows and Wabaseemoong First Nations . ( Jody Porter/CBC ) <p> Ontario 's environment ministry has confirmed elevated levels of mercury in the soil at a mill site in Dryden , Ont. , upstream from Indigenous communities where residents have suffered mercury poisoning for decades . <p> In a report dated December 2017 , the province said soil testing was done at 24 spots at the site .
