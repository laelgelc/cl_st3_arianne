<h> Region nets climate change award <p> The Durham of Region is being recognized for its local climate adaptation measures . <p> The region has been chosen as one of nine recipients of the 2018 Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) Sustainable Communities Awards , as the winner in the Climate Change category for the Durham Community Climate Adaptation Plan ( DCCAP ) . <p> According to a regional media release , the plan " addresses the local climate adaptation measures for the region , and is about protecting Durham from the changing global climate , and preparing our communities to cope with and respond to the increasing frequency and intensity of extreme weather events . " <p> The plan was the result of a collaborative effort by more than 60 experts from across the region . <p> " Durham Region is honoured to receive this award , and continues to work towards helping the region and our eight municipalities to prepare to adapt to the changing climate , " says region CAO Garry Cubitt . " We know that the weather is only becoming warmer , wetter and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ communities to meet that reality . " <p> The award will be presented to Cubitt on Feb. 8 at FCM 's annual Sustainable Communities Conference in Ottawa , which showcases the latest in municipal green innovation and best practices . <p> This award adds to the accolades the DCCAP has received from the sustainability community , including the Insurance Bureau of Canada , Intact Centre on Climate Adaptation , and the Ontario and federal Ministers of the Environment and Climate Change .
