<h> Arctic ringed seals are threatened species , U.S. appeal court rules <p> A ringed seal looks out of a snow cave on the ice off of Barrow , Alaska . Ringed seals , the main prey of polar bears , will receive threatened species protection , a U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled Monday . ( Brendan P. Kelly/Associated Press ) <p> A seal that depends on Arctic sea ice for reproduction will receive threatened species protection , the 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals ruled Monday . <p> A three-judge panel overturned a lower court decision and ruled that the National Marine Fisheries Service acted properly in listing ringed seals as a threatened species because of projected loss of sea ice due to climate warming . <p> The decision reverses a ruling by U.S. District Court Judge Ralph Beistline of Anchorage , who in March 2016 said the agency 's decision was speculative . <p> The decision is closely related to a judgment on another ice-dependent species , bearded seals . The smallest of Alaska 's ice seals , ringed seals are the only ones that thrive in completely ice-covered Arctic waters . They use stout claws to dig and maintain breathing holes . <p> When snow covers those holes , females excavate snow caves , where they give birth to pups that can not survive in ice-cold water .
