To help make it happen , we must increase taxes on those who are amassing a greater and greater share of our province 's wealth . <p> British Columbians hold some widely shared values values and priorities and want our political leaders to take bold action to address economic insecurity , inequality and climate change . <p> Yet , we 've grown accustomed to being told -- and too often believing -- that making this type of change is not realistic or affordable . <p> In reality , what is unaffordable is to stick with the status quo .
