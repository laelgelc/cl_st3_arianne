<h> Red and Green : The Ecosocialist Perspective <p> The contemporary international political economy is marked by a great contradiction . On a planet characterized by finite resources , the economy is predicated upon an absurd and irrational logic of infinite expansion and accumulation . With its fossil fuel based operations continually spewing carbon into the earth 's atmosphere , the capitalist system 's productivist obsession with profit has brought humanity to the brink of an abyss . Climate change is accelerating much faster than predicted -- the accumulation of CO2 , the rise in temperature , the melting of the polar ice , the drought , and the floods : everything is happening too quickly . In fact , the scientific assessments are now perceived as being too optimistic . The question is : after a certain level of increase in temperature -- say six degrees -- would the planet still be inhabitable for our species ? <p> How should we respond to this enormously frightening scenario ?
