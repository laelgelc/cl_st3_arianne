The partners for these @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Department , the Biodiversity Secretariat of the Environment Ministry and the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) <p> The aptly-named ' Life ' Project is bent on restoring this fern-covered 10-hectare patch in Halgahawala , Opatha nestling within the Forest Reserve , to its former status of an important rainforest . <p> It is at a simple function held on February 7 at the MJF Centre for Dignified &amp; Sustainable Empowerment at Katubedda that the project is unveiled . While forest restoration is the action to re-establish native species which inhabited that forest before , reforestation is to replant trees may be of one species in a denuded area and afforestation is to plant trees in barren areas to make a forest . <p> And what the Life Project is making a concerted effort to do is slowly and surely bring back the different layers of a rainforest which is defined as a tropical forest with an annual rainfall of at least 2,500 millimetres . <p> The four layers of a rainforest are : <p> Emergent Layer -- Giant trees with huge mushroom-shaped crowns getting the most sunlight but challenged by high temperatures , low humidity and strong winds . <p> Canopy Layer -- Trees with broad and irregular crowns forming a close-knit canopy . These tree branches have a dense covering of other plants @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Understorey -- Young trees and leafy herbaceous plants living in low sunlight or darkness , as only 2-15% of the sunlight penetrates the canopy . <p> Forest Floor -- Hardly anything grows here other than plants which have adapted to very low , less than 2% , sunlight . On the ground is a thin layer of fallen leaves , seeds , fruits and branches that decay and decompose . <p> Underscoring that three million hectares of forest are lost annually across the world and the average annual deforestation rate in Sri Lanka has been 7,147 ha/year ( 1992-2010 ) , Prof. Weerakoon says that forest degradation causes loss and fragmentation of habitat . <p> The Life Project to restore the degraded rainforest patch at Halgahawala allocated to it by the Forest Department will be a pilot programme . <p> Prof. Weerakoon explains that the ecological baseline of the area has already been established and the forest Restoration Plan for the site -- currently colonized by the false stag horn fern ( Dicranopteris linearis or Kekeilla ) -- developed .
