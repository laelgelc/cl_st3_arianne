<h> SORR 2018 run open for registration <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The annual Save Our Rainforest Race ( SORR ) is back and is now open for registration . <p> Organised by Pertubuhan Pelindung Khazanah Malaysia ( Peka Malaysia ) , the race which is into its sixth year , will be held at Hutan Simpan Gading in Hulu Selangor on Mar 24 . <p> Peka president Puan Sri Shariffa Sabrina Syed Akil said that they are expecting some 2,000 participants of all ages to be part of the annual event , as they are aiming to plant some 1,000 trees . <p> " Apart from having the race to start and end at the Pelangi Hill Resort Camp , there will be several checkpoints throughout the race which tests and exposse participants on the importance of rainforests . <p> " Similar to previous races held before this , the main objective of the race is to educate participants on the conservation and preservation of forests , " she said during her opening remarks for SORR 2018 . <p> The environmental activist added that it was her hope that the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ especially the younger generation , to appreciate and better understand the importance of preserving the rainforests . <p> " Peka Malaysia is extremely concerned about indiscriminate logging nationwide which affects the water catchment areas and loss of wildlife habitats . <p> " If we do not start doing something , there might not be any rainforest left for our future generations , " she said . <p> She explained that among the benefits of forest conservation were reducing the effects of climate change , protecting the ecosystem and preserving water catchment areas for the benefit of all .
