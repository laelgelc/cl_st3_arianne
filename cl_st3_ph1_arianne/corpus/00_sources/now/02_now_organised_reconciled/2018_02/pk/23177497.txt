<p> The American Soybean Association announced the selection of Ryan Findlay as its new Chief Executive Officer . Findlay replaces Stephen Censky , who left ASA in October after confirmation by the U.S. Senate as Deputy Secretary of Agriculture . <p> " ASA is extremely pleased to have found someone of Ryan 's caliber and experience to lead us into our next chapter as a first-class U.S. commodity organization , " said ASA President John Heisdorffer , a soybean producer from Keota , Iowa . " Ryan 's background growing up on a farm and working for the Michigan Farm Bureau and for Syngenta give him the right combination of life and work experience that will serve ASA well in the coming years . " <p> Findlay is a native of Caro , Mich. , where his family still farms row crops . He earned a degree in political science from Western Michigan University and an MBA from Northwood University in Midland , Mich . <p> The last four years Ryan worked for the global agricultural company Syngenta , focusing on freedom-to-operate issues impacting farmers .
