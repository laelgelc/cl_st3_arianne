<p> The Sindh government has decided to develop a forest area of over 20,000 acres of land between Karachi and Hyderabad in order to improve the overall environmental conditions . The move to transform a vast tract of barren land belonging to the forest department was badly needed as both these urban centres have expanded exponentially with drastic reduction in green cover .
