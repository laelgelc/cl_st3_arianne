<h> Voice of the People , Feb. 17 , 2018 <p> Recently , two tragic and horrifying events involved children who were continually tortured , beaten and starved by their parents . The abuse of 12 children in California is being well publicized . The other case happened in Pennsylvania and resulted in the death of a 4 year old boy . <p> As a trained volunteer working with children in foster homes , I know similar things happen in South Jersey as well . <p> The California kids were home schooled and had no chance that a caring teacher or nurse would spot the signs of abuse , and the 4 year old , probably not yet enrolled . <p> Some neighbors were interviewed and said they noticed some odd behaviors at these homes , but did n't want to get involved , were not sure it was necessary to tell anyone , or maybe they did not know how to report abuse . <p> We are all responsible and need to keep our eyes open .
