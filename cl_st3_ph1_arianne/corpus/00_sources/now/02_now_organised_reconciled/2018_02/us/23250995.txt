Photograph : Ralph Lee Hopkins/Getty Images/National Geographic Creative <p> The orangutan is one of our planet 's most distinctive and intelligent creatures . It has been observed using primitive tools , such as the branch of a tree , to hunt food , and is capable of complex social behaviour . Orangutans also played a special role in humanity 's own intellectual history when , in the 19th century , Charles Darwin and Alfred Russel Wallace , co-developers of the theory of natural selection , used observations of them to hone their ideas about evolution . <p> But humanity has not repaid orangutans with kindness . The numbers of these distinctive , red-maned primates are now plummeting thanks to our destruction of their habitats and illegal hunting of the species .
