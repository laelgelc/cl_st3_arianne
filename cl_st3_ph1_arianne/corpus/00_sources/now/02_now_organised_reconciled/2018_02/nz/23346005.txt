<h> The surprising threat from below that is destroying the world 's coral reefs <p> PETER HANNAM <p> Last updated 00:27 , February 24 2018 <p> FAIRFAX AUSTRALIA <p> Not loving it enough : coral reefs face multiple threats from climate change , including as it turns out , from below . <p> The world 's coral reefs , already enduring multiple threats from bleaching to nutrient run-off from farming , also face another challenge - this time from below . <p> New research , published in the journal Science on Friday , has found the sediments on which many reefs are built are 10 times more sensitive to the acidifying oceans than the living corals themselves . Some reef bases are already dissolving . <p> The study used underwater chambers at four sites in the Pacific and Atlantic oceans , including Heron Island in the Great Barrier Reef , and applied modelling to extrapolate results for 22 reefs in three ocean basins . <p> FAIRFAX AUSTRALIA <p> Coral reefs provide important habitats for many species , such this reef at Tufi in Papua New Guinea . <p> As oceans @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the calcium carbonate that forms their base .
