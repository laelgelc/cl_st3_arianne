<h> New economic report tracks greenhouse gas impact <p> The System of Environmental-Economic Accounts is the first of its kind in New Zealand . ( Photo / Getty ) <p> The country 's greenhouse gas emissions are now tracked alongside the economy . <p> The first ever report of the System of Environmental-Economic Accounts reveals the impact economic decisions have on our natural environment , and in turn , the importance of natural resources to the economy . <p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw says there are good news stories out of this . <p> He says the stats show the amount of pollution being produced per unit of production , known as emissions intensity , is falling in a number of industries . <p> " What that shows is that it is possible to have economic development and at the same time reduce greenhouse gas emissions . "
