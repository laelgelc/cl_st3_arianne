<h> European Union and New Zealand strengthen their partnership <p> The European Union ( EU ) and New Zealand today reaffirmed their commitment to deepen their partnership in the coming years , and explored opportunities for further collaboration . <p> In 2016 the EU and New Zealand signed a Partnership Agreement on Relations and Cooperation ( PARC ) to strengthen ties and deepen cooperation on issues of mutual interest . Today marked the first meeting of the Joint Committee established under the PARC , which provided an opportunity for a strategic review of the bilateral relationship and its future direction . <p> Despite geographic distance , the friendship and partnership between New Zealand and the European Union is very close .
