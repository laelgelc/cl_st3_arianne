<p> The new $1 billion per annum Provincial Growth Fund has been officially launched in Gisborne today by Regional Economic Development Minister Shane Jones . <p> " As of today , the Provincial Growth Fund ( PGF ) is open for business and has the potential to make a real difference to the people of provincial New Zealand , " Mr Jones says . <p> " We are being bold and we are being ambitious because this Government is committed to ending the years of neglect . Nearly half of us live outside our main cities . If this country is to do well , then our provinces must thrive . <p> " Our first regional packages support the regions most neglected by the last government : in Northland , Tairawhiti-East Coast , Hawke 's Bay and Manawatu-Whanganui and the West Coast of the South Island . <p> " We are providing an immediate boost to these areas by investing $61.7 million into forestry initiatives , tourism ventures , rail and roading projects , and supporting these regions develop their proposals further to help them get off the ground . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ support will create more than 700 direct jobs , and 80 indirect jobs -- an impressive start to what will be an exciting three years for our provinces . <p> " If all the projects we 're funding realise their full potential , this will equate to more than $344 million of public and private investment for our regional economies . " <p> The PGF aims to enhance economic development opportunities , create sustainable jobs , contribute to community well-being , lift the productivity potential of regions , and help meet New Zealand 's climate change targets . <p> " The $3 billion will be fully committed over three years , making investment in the provinces more attractive for private sector investment , which has strongly favoured our main urban centres in recent years . <p> " We will form genuine partnerships up and down the country and provide the capital needed to stop projects languishing in long-term plans and regional action strategies . " <p> All regions are eligible for funding . <p> " An Independent Advisory Panel has been appointed to assist the decision-making of ministers and officials @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Ministry of Business , Innovation and Employment to work directly with regions , ensuring this is n't a Wellington-driven Fund . <p> " Our provinces are full of ideas and proposals .
