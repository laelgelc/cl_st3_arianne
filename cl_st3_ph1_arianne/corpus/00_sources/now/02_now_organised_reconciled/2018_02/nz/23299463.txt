Generation Zero kicked off a cycle tour which will go from Auckland to Wellington starting with the " Bike the Bridge " event on Sunday 18th February . The cycle tour is the first of a number of initiatives that Generation Zero has planned before the start of the government 's official consultation on the Zero Carbon Bill in May . <p> The five person cycle crew will take 12 days to cycle down the north island , stopping for conversations about climate change along the way . They will be welcomed by a large crowd of supporters on Parliament lawn when they arrive down in Wellington on March 1st .
