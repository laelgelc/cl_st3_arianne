Photo New Zealand Geographic <p> Howick 's Rev Dr Richard Waugh has spoken out against a new Air New Zealand safety video filmed in Antarctica . <p> He joins a chorus of upset family members whose loved ones died in the 1979 Mt Erebus disaster .
