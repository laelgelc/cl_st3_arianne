" Louis is one of few Filipino OFWs and business owners who 've made this country home . He drove us to Reykjavik where all the people live . Outside , the sky was summery and it would be daylight till midnight . I felt like a child for whom bedtime had been canceled . Louis has never completed the Ring Road , the two-lane highway that circles the country , but swears it 's going to be chada kaayo . <p> There are no motorways in Iceland , an oddly comforting thought . There will be no towns for long stretches , and the only sign of life is Despacito on the radio . <p> On day three , our road took us inland from the sea . A glacier grew on our car windows : Vatnajokull . It 's a massive dollop of icing on a cake of land . We crossed a bridge to Jokulsarlon , or glacier lagoon , a popular tourist spot . We are met by Hokan , a Nordic man and glorious wearer of Gore Tex . " I bet you have n't seen anything like this where you 're from , " pointing to the bright blue icebergs , " very beautiful and haunting . We ate the iceberg as an ode to Vatnajokull , the great halo-halo , succumbing alas , to global warming . <p> A storm came , chasing us along the sea and we drove on , penetrating the rain .
