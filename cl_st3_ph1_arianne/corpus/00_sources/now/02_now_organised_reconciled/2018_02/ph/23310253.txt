<h> Angeles City participates in climate change workshop <h> Angeles City participates in climate change workshop <p> Wednesday , February 21 , 2018 <p> PAMPANGA . League of Cities of the Philippines President Mayor Edgardo Pamintuan , with key members of the planning team in climate action plan , attended the four-day capacity building activity organized by UN-Habitat Philippines on February 19 to 22 at the Century Hotel , Barangay Balibago . ( Contributed photo ) <p> ANGELES CITY -- Mayor Edgardo Pamintuan and key members of the planning team from local government agencies participated in the four-day Local Climate Change Action Planning ( LCCAP ) organized by UN-Habitat Philippines on February 19 to 22 at the Century Hotel , Barangay Balibago . <p> The four-day capacity building activity serves as guide to local government units through the steps of LCCAP formulation process , from assessment of their risks and vulnerabilities , setting targets , defining local priority actions for both adaptation and mitigation , to creating a climate action plan . <p> Angeles City is one of the five partner cities , which include Cagayan De Oro , Legazpi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ serves as a good opportunity for city-to-city sharing and learning in the course of improving climate resilience at the local level . <p> " Our country has experienced a lot of calamities which destroyed millions of infrastructures and families . We have to work fast , or be swept away to oblivion , " said Pamintuan . <p> " With the help of this workshop , we will be able to save lives and prevent billions of pesos worth of economic damages caused by weather disasters . We , at the local government of Angeles City , will be committed in working double time because the entire country will benefit from our work , " he added . <p> During the workshop , participants were tasked to capture urban plans and design-related opportunities , implications , issues , and challenges from the results of their respective risk and vulnerability assessments . <p> The last day involved a city tour in Angeles City where two sites were featured : Agyu Tamu Parks ( the old railway converted into pocket parks ) to showcase urban greening efforts specifically in the downtown area @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hall , Pamintuan Mansion , Santo Rosario Church , and the nearby educational institutions ) . <p> According to the UN-Habitat Project Manager Yen Flores , the site visits are an opportunity to discuss in concrete terms the technical concepts from the workshop . They serve as on-the-ground manifestations of the principles , challenges , and potential that will emerge from the insights , agreements , and action points drawn from the workshop . <p> " The main purpose of this workshop is to support the five cities in terms on improving their existing local climate action plans so that we can contribute later on , on the programming and development of urban plans , " Flores said . <p> This event is in partnership with Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board , Climate Change Commission , and League of Cities of the Philippines .
