<p> Environmental groups have dropped out of consultations over native logging on public lands , saying the Berejiklian government has already decided to roll over 20-year old agreements regardless of a review . <p> The NSW National Parks Association and the Nature Conservation Council had been participating in workshops over the future of three regional forestry agreements ( RFA ) originally signed between 1999 and 2001 to end the so-called " forests wars " in previous decades . <p> Forestry in state forests has been loss-making , and environmental groups have long called for it to end . " There will be a new RFA irrespective whether they 've been successful in meeting their objectives , and whether they are the best use of our forests , particularly in the era of climate change . " <p> Kate Smolski , chief executive at the NCC , said koala populations in the state had " plummeted " during the RFAs , underscoring the need " for independent scientific @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ harm forestry has done to our threatened wildlife " over that time . <p> Minister for Forestry Paul Toole said the government had committed to renewing the RFAs in late 2016 but still " wants to hear from all who have an interest in forests " . <p> " RFAs represent the most effective framework for the sustainable management of forests , " he said .
