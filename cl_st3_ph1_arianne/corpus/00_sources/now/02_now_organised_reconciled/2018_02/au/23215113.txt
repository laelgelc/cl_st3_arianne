We need them not only for their relaxing beauty but also for the oxygen they generate , the shade they provide for our overheated cities and the urban wildlife they sustain . <p> The Indian Banyan Tree at the famous Moana Surfrider Resort is so expansive it 's more like a forest than a single tree .
