<h> This online course is helping to make a new generation of designers more sustainable <h> Gucci and Bottega Veneta 's parent company , Kering , is launching a free online course to teach aspiring and existing fashion professionals how to be more sustainable . <p> Sustainability has been a hot topic in fashion for years , but until recently there were few resources available for brands and designers who wanted to be more environmentally savvy . Four years ago , Kering and the London College of Fashion , the prestigious constituent college of the University of the Arts London , partnered to create a series of courses that offered just that . Now they are taking their partnership beyond the classroom with the launch of their first open-access digital course . <p> Launching on April 9 , the 18-hour online course , titled " Fashion &amp; Sustainability : Understanding Luxury Fashion in a Changing World " , will provide free and flexible access to both Kering and LCF 's experts over a six-week period . " We could n't be everywhere at once , " Marie-Claire Daveu , chief @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , tells Vogue of the demand her office saw for appearances , speeches and trainings on the subject . As a result , they decided to make the materials of their courses with LCF widely available through a Massive Open Online Course ( MOOC ) . <p> The new platform was presented by Fran ? ois-Henri Pinault , chairman and CEO of Kering , at an event during London Fashion Week , highlighting the opportunity that sustainability and innovation provides not just for the world , but businesses and consumers too . <p> " We believe that to change the business model of the fashion industry to include sustainability you need also to know how to do it , " says Daveu . " With this MOOC we give guidelines and how to manage them , everything from material dimensions and sourcing for luxury fashion , to how to measure your environmental footprint . Everytime we speak about an issue - for example climate change , biodiversity , resource scarcity - we give concrete tools and solutions to determine what , as a young designer , you have to do @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> The course , for which registration is now open , is divided into six modules , presented through videos , podcasts , activities and discussions that participants can undertake at their leisure . Plans for a translations , namely in Chinese , are underway , as well as as an abridged version that all new Kering hires can undertake . " This course is not a revolution , and I 'm not saying that after it everything will be perfect , " says Daveu , " but I am convinced that it will concretely change the way designers and the fashion industry can do their job .
