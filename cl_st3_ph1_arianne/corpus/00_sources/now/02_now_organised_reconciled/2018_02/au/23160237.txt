<p> A visit to Taronga Zoo in Sydney has been a ritual for many schoolchildren in NSW , but they are about to get a revamped experience , with the zoo due to open a $32 million Taronga Institute of Science and Learning with support from high-profile donors . <p> The institute 's new " immersive classrooms " will include spaces that mimic different climates and geography , such as the desert and the rainforest , and children will have the opportunity to meet animals such as bilbies and monkeys in the classrooms . <p> The institute will also have five labs , one of which is a " frozen zoo " , which will preserve frozen coral cells from the Great Barrier Reef until they are needed to re-seed the reef . <p> Taronga Zoo chief executive Cameron Kerr said he hoped the experience would make a lasting impression on children who visited .
