<h> NSW Climate Change Fund underspent more than $250m in past three years <p> The Berejiklian government 's Climate Change Fund has collected in excess of a quarter of a billion dollars more than it has spent in the past three years , delaying investment the Greens say should have gone on bolstering communities against the effects of global warming . <p> Details of the underspend of $252.9 million from 2014-17 are contained in the fund 's latest annual report that was released without fanfare late last year . <p> Climate change impacts are likely to get worse , with storms expected to intensify , and sea levels to keep rising . Photo : Peter Rae <p> Revenue for the fund last year alone reached $289.6 million , collected from electricity providers - Ausgrid , Endeavour and Essential Energy . <p> Outlays for the year totaled $160.6 million , with about $94.3 million going towards the Solar Bonus Scheme Reimbursement .
