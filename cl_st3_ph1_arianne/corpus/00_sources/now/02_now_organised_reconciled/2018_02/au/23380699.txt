<p> Surfers are becoming citizen scientists by attaching fins to their boards that collect data from the ocean . <p> The Smartfin is fitted with a GPS , a circuit board , a Bluetooth chip , a rechargeable battery , and sensors that measure multiple ocean parameters including temperature , location , motion , and wave characteristics . <p> The fin collects data from the sea while surfers are out riding their boards . <p> The data is then uploaded into a smartphone app and becomes accessible in near real-time to the international scientific community . <p> Surfer and Southern Cross University researcher Renaud Joannes-Boyau said constant ocean monitoring would help a wide range of scientists , but particularly climate change researchers . <p> " Using the data collected with Smartfin , we 'll be able to better understand trends in ocean warming and acidification and mobilise communities to take action to combat these problems caused by climate change , " Dr Joannes-Boyau said . <p> " The coastal range is very hard to monitor because there 's a lot of currents , movement , and it 's really hard @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ thing is surfers go out continuously in one area . <p> " We thought it was a great idea to use people who love the ocean , and use the ocean every day , to be part of helping scientists monitor the ocean .
