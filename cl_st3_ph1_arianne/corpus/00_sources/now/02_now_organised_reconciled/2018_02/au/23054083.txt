<h> ' We might be entering the age of the unfailing sunburn ' : ozone layer getting worse in populated areas <h> By Liam Mannix <p> 6 February 2018 -- 4:42pm <p> The ozone layer is not healing as scientists had hoped . In fact , depressing research released on Tuesday finds our UV-blocking ozone layer is still thinning in more populated parts of the world . <p> A global effort to cut certain airborne pollutants over the last three decades has been credited with allowing our damaged ozone layer to recover . But the shock new findings -- which scientists can not explain -- suggest we may have been celebrating too early . <p> " This study is scary , " said Professor Bill Laurance , a climate change scientist at James Cook University . <p> " Until we understand what 's really happening you 'd be silly to sun yourself , except in polar regions . The era of suntanning could be over ; we might be entering the age of the unfailing sunburn .
