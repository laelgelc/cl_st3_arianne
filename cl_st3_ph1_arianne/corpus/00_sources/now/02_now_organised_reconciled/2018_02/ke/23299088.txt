<h> MPs approve policy on climate change <h> share <p> Members of the National Assembly yesterday supported the passage of the national policy on climate change that will see the government spending Sh200 million annually over five years to help put in place measures to reduce the impact of the condition . <p> Led by Leader of Majority Aden Duale , the MPs expressed concern that global warming caused by climate change will have an adverse effect on all the sectors of the economy including agriculture , industry , energy , water , trade and tourism . In particular , Duale urged MPs to approve the policy to help transform Kenya by implementing the Vision 2013 . <p> He regretted that the cost of managing climate change impacts is increasing day by day and thus need to be addressed urgently .
