<p> MPs want the government to impose a total ban on logging countrywide and revoke all existing logging licences in a bid to protect a diminishing forest cover . <p> Parliament 's Environment committee on Wednesday raised the alarm over increased logging in the country , warning that , if not stopped immediately , the country will not have any forest cover in the next few years . <p> Chairman of the committee , Kareke Mbiuki , also called on the formation of a multiagency team to undertake a holistic audit of the country 's forest cover . <p> " As a nation , we must take bold steps to save our forests and preserve our water towers . The unchecked logging must cease immediately , " Mr Mbiuki said at a press conference at Parliament Buildings . <p> " The devastating impact of climate change is increasingly being experienced through extreme weather conditions in form of recurrent droughts and increased flooding . These are closely linked to the disappearing forest cover in the country , " Mr Mbiuki said .
