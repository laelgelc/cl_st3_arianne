No one should be living in harm 's way <p> We need to reduce the numbers of people affected by natural disasters by ensuring that land use regulations and building codes are strictly implemented by the authorities . This helps to ensure that the people live , work and study in safe areas and in buildings constructed to the standards required by the risk profile of the location . People who may be vulnerable often have an acute understanding of disaster risk and how to manage it .
