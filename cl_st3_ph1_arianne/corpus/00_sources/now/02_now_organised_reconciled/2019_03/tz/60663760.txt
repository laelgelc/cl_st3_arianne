<p> Dar es Salaam . Switzerland 's ambassador to Tanzania has underscored the important role played by civil society organisations ( CSOs ) in complementing the role of the government in development . <p> The ambassador , Ms Florence Mattli , yesterday urged close collaboration between the two to enhance public service delivery . She said her country believes that a vibrant civil society was crucial to achieving Tanzania 's vision for a middle income economy . <p> The envoy was speaking during the signing of an agreement with three local civil society organizations ( CSOs ) aimed at promoting accountability . <p> CSOs that were involved in the signing of a Sh18 billion ( $7.64 million ) grant are Foundation for Civil Society ( FCS ) , Policy Forum ( PF ) and Twaweza . <p> " The fund will be provided through the Switzerland Development Corporation ( SDC ) and is intended to enhance responsiveness and accountability . They will be issued through implementation of the third phase of Social Accountability Programme ( SAP3 ) , which are among important pillar programmes of the Swiss Cooperation Strategy to Tanzania 2015-2020 , " she said . <p> She @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ noting that implementation of policies required formal and informal accountability enforcing mechanisms for better provision of services to citizens . <p> " No government can do this on its own . To increase accountability and efficiency of policies and measures alliance of the government and CSOs is required . Vibrant CSOs should act as interlocutors , as eye openers to the reality on the ground for challenges to be efficiently encountered , " she said . <p> She further observed that : " The Tanzania government can team up with CSOs in advocating important issues like mitigating climate change , preventing teen pregnancies and combating corruption . " <p> Ms Matli said that apart from serving as voices of citizens and dialogue initiators , CSOs can collaborate with the government to come up with better policies for citizens and the country . <p> FCS executive director Francis Kiwanga said his organisation would utilise the funds to enhance good governance , monitoring and control of resource utilisation in collaboration with the government and other stakeholders . <p> " I 'm also happy that the process has been transparent as stipulated by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ accountability to funds received for implementations of projects under CSOs , " he said . <p> PF board chairman Japhet Makongo said his organisation is charged to ensure voices of citizens were heard especially in provision of feedback to services given the government . <p> " In provision of services some people may not deliver either accidentally or intentionally . It is our role to show and remind them that things did n't go well somewhere , " he said , promising to spend the money for that purpose . <p> For his part , Twaweza executive director Aidan Eyakuze said apart from transparency and citizen 's participation , another important aspect of good governance is creativity especially for delivering projects created for the benefit of citizens .
