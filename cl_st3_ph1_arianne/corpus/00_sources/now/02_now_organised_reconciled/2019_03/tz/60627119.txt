Children , no matter how thirsty , would often refuse to drink . The rehabilitation of the mangrove forest has also restored breeding grounds for fish and crustaceans in the shaded shallows . <p> " Before , we would catch about 20 crabs , but now we get 50 or 60 , " said Moussa . " Instead of cutting wood , people are doing more farming and we plan to start beekeeping soon , because the mangroves have grown thick enough to have hives and bring bees .
