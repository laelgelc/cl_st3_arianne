<h> TCB distributes pesticides to farmers in Singida <p> THE Tanzania Cotton Board ( TCB ) in Singida Region has distributed 160,000 bottles of pesticides to farmers in the area to combat millburgs and other pests attacking the fibre crop used to make clothes . <p> TCB cotton inspector , Abubakari Sempule said when he presented a report to the regional cotton development officer , who visited Mayaha village to inspect farmers activities . <p> Despite admitting the havoc being caused by the pests , Sempule assured the farmers that the board was working to address the matter . <p> During the event farmers expressed the challenges facing them including climate change , drought and pests which contributed to low yields . <p> According to the farmers , despite following all the advices they get from agriculture experts , pests had been a big challenge to thwart their efforts . <p> The farmers requested the board to send pesticides on time to the villages because most of the time it is brought when the pests have wreaked havoc . <p> One of the farmers Christancia Charles used the opportunity to urge women in the village to make use of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ added that cotton as a crop
