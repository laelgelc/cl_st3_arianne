To increase accountability and efficiency of policies and measures alliance of the government and CSOs is required . Vibrant CSOs should act as interlocutors , as eye openers to the reality on the ground for challenges to be efficiently encountered , " she said . <p> She further observed that : " The Tanzania government can team up with CSOs in advocating important issues like mitigating climate change , preventing teen pregnancies and combating corruption . " <p> Ms Matli said that apart from serving as voices of citizens and dialogue initiators , CSOs can advise the government on better policies for citizens and the country . <p> FCS executive director Francis Kiwanga said his organization would utilise the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ resource utilisation in collaboration with the government and other stakeholders . <p> " I 'm also happy that the process has been transparent as stipulated by the new regulations to CSOs requiring enhancement of transparency and accountability to funds received for implementations of projects under CSOs , " he said . <p> PF board chairman Japhet Makongo said his organisation is charged to ensure voices of citizens were heard especially in provision of feedback to services given the government . <p> " In provision of services some people may not deliver either accidentally or intentionally .
