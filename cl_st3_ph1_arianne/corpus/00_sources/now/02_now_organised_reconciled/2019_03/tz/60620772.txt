<h> Latest Video <h> Hunger looms as dry spell persists <h> Monday March 11 2019 <p> Geita . Hunger is looming large in the drought-stricken Bwelwa Village in Mbogwe District after a three-season dry spell that a recent report has attributed to climate change . <p> According to a report from Mbogwe Ward , a population of 12,431 people largely depends on rice and maize farming for commercial and subsistence , respectively . <p> But for almost three consecutive years , farmers could not harvest paddy due to lack of rainfall . However , in an effort to rescue themselves from the situation , some villagers have decided to pursue alternative activities such as beekeeping , and yam farming . <p> Mbogwe acting district executive director Samuel Makala expressed concern over the shortage of food in the area as a result of bad rainfall patterns . <p> " We advise farmers to engage in potato and cassava cultivation because these are drought-resistant crops , " he said .
