<h> Govt directs district councils to employ more game scouts <p> DEPUTY Minister for Natural Resources and Tourism , Constantine Kanyasu , has directed district councils in the country to employ more game scouts to prevent wild animals from invading human settlements and farms . <p> Kanyasu made the call over the weekend during his tour in Mara Region following reports of deaths of people and destruction of farm crops by wild animals that raided human settlements and farms in different parts of the country . <p> He said successful anti-poaching operations pioneered by President John Magufuli resulted in an increase in the populations of elephants , hippos and buffalos that strayed into human settlements and farms , wreaking havoc . <p> He said : " Most of the raids by the wild animals occur in villages located near the game reserves and the national parks .
