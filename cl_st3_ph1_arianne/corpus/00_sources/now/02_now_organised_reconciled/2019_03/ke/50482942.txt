<p> The health service recorded 9,886 new cases of FGM from 2016 to 2018 . In almost all cases the FGM happened before the person 's arrival in Britain . <p> FGM has been a criminal offence in the UK since 1985 , but there have only been three prosecutions under the legislation . <p> In 2003 it also became a criminal offence to take a child abroad for FGM . <p> The maximum penalty for FGM is 14 years in prison . <p> An FGM helpline run by Britain 's leading children 's charity has responded to over 2,100 contacts about FGM since 2013 . <p> Police hold operations at airports targeting flights to and from countries where FGM is prevalent . These often coincide with school summer holidays when it is thought girls are most likely to be taken abroad to be cut . <p> Doctors , nurses , teachers and social workers are required to report to police any cases of FGM in girls they come across . <p> Since 2015 , courts have issued more than 290 FGM prevention orders to safeguard girls at risk .
