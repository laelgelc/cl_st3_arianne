<p> Experts say world has the science and funds to improve matters A United Nations report assessing the state of the environment in the past five years has warned of damage to the planet and people 's health unless urgent action is taken . The sixth Global Environment Outlook , or GEO-6 , which was released yesterday at the ongoing fourth UN Environment Assembly in Nairobi , was produced by 250 scientists and experts from more than 70 countries . The report says if human beings do not drastically scale up environmental protections , cities and regions in Asia , the Middle East and Africa could see millions of premature deaths by 2050 . The GEO-6 also warns that pollutants in our freshwater systems will see anti-microbial resistance become the number one cause of death by 2050 , affecting male and female fertility as well as child neuro-development . But it is not all gloom as the report highlights the fact that the world has the science , technology and finances it needs to move towards a more sustainable development pathway . " The report shows that policies and technologies already exist to fashion new development pathways that will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all people , " said Joyeeta Gupta , one of the co-chairs of the GEO-6 process . Paul Ekins , also a co-chair of the report , said what is currently lacking is the political will to implement policies and technologies at a sufficient speed and scale . " This assembly in Nairobi needs to be the occasion when policymakers face up to the challenges and grasp the opportunities of a much brighter future for humanity , " he said . What is missing , the GEO-6 authors further note , is sufficient support from the public , business and political leaders who are still clinging to outdated production and development models . " The science is clear . The health and prosperity of humanity is directly tied with the state of our environment , " said Dr Joyce Msuya , acting executive director of UN Environment . She continued : " This report is an outlook for humanity . We are at a crossroads . Do we continue on our current path , which will lead to a bleak future for humankind , or do we pivot to a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ political leaders must make , now . " The report projects that a future healthy planet with healthy people is based on a new way of thinking where the " grow now , clean up after " model is changed to a near zero-waste economy by 2050 . According to the report , green investment of two per cent of countries ' GDP would deliver long-term growth as high as presently projected but with fewer impacts from climate change , water scarcity and loss of ecosystems . At present , according to the GEO-6 , the world is not on track to meet the 17 sustainable development goals by 2030 or 2050 . The GEO-6 urges governments to take urgent action now as any delays in climate action increases the costs of achieving the goals of the Paris Agreement , or reversing progress made .
