<h> SEKU University courses and fee structure <p> South Eastern Kenya Univerity ( SEKU ) is a higher learning public institution located in the Eastern Province of Kenya . The university is fully fledged having been awarded a charter on 1st March 2013 by the retired president of Kenya , His Excellency Hon. Mwai Kibaki . Its main campus is located along the Kitui-Machakos main road and 12 kilometers from Kwa Vonza market in Kitui County . Before its accreditation as a certified institution for higher learning , SEKU was a constituent college of the Nairobi University . <p> Image : facebook.com , @SEKU-main campusSource : UGC <p> The university has managed to establish itself as one of the best institutions for the advancement of tertiary learning not only in Kitui County but nationwide as well . With a favorable learning environment , South Eastern Kenya University offers a variety of programmes ranging from the diploma , undergraduate and postgraduate levels . <p> Since its certification , the institution boasts of other campuses like Kitui Town Campus , Wote Town Campus , Machakos Town Campus , Nairobi Town Campus , and Mtito Andei Campus .
