Over the years the number of hives he keeps has been steadily dwindling . <p> " In my youth I had only known bees as a source of income on my farm since they provided me with a produce to sell . All bee keepers in this village will tell you that the venture is no longer a sweet deal , " Tangus said at his Baruti farm . <p> Some 50 Kilometres away at Kuresoi North Sub-County , 61 year old Gabriel Maritim , has almost nothing to sell after harvesting just 12 liters of honey , down from his usual average of 3000 litres . <p> " I have managed beehives since 1981 but the venture is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ told to take up bee farming as a way of broadening our income in the face of harsher weather , including heat , droughts and floods that can decimate crops .
