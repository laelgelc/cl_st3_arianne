<h> ANC expresses solidarity with cyclone victims <p> The ANC has expressed solidarity with the victims of the devastating tropical cyclone which hit Mozambique , Zimbabwe , and Malawi , and called for the Paris agreement on the emission of greenhouse gasses to be legally enforced . <p> The party 's secretary-general Ace Magashule says the terrible magnitude of the disaster that the sheer brutal force of Cyclone Idai caused , and the devastation that it left in its wake , caused the United Nations to declare that it is the worst natural disaster to have ever hit the Southern Hemisphere . <p> Magashule said thousands of people had lost their lives , and the destruction of homes and overall infrastructure and crops was devastating and would continue to negatively impact on the lives of millions of people for a long time . <p> Preliminary estimations indicate that more than 1000 people died in Mozambique alone , and that hundreds more died in neighbouring Zimbabwe and Malawi . <p> Magashule pointed out that the intensification and magnitude of natural disasters were directly related to the scientifically proven climate change , and specifically global warming . <p> SMS us : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the inverted commas ) or ' border ' for the Border Drive or ' garden ' for the Garden Route ( Cost is R1.50/sms .
