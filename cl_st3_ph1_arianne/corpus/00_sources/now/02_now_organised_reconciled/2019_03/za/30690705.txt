Yet hairdressing , both as a sector and as an individual activity , can have a massive carbon footprint . <p> Hairdressing uses high levels of hot water , energy and chemicals . Similarly , in our homes , heating hot water is typically the most energy intensive activity . For the cost of a ten-minute shower that uses an electric immersion heater , you could leave a typical television on for 20 hours . <p> So while it helps to turn lights and appliances off , the real gains in terms of reducing energy usage are in slashing our use of hot water .
