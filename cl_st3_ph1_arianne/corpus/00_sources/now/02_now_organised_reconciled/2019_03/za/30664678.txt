Depending on your tax bracket , you 'd receive a healthy lump sum back from the government . <p> In some instances , particularly in the US , your employer may also match your contribution to your RA , as a part of your benefits package . In addition to reducing your taxable income , you 're pretty much receiving free money from your boss .
