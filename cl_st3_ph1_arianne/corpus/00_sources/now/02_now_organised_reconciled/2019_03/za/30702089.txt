<h> Solutions for Mozambique disaster not only from Rich North <p> LONDON -- The authors of this article in the Conversation make the point that the world 's wealthiest countries are largely to blame for disasters such as the one unfolding in Mozambique where thousands of people are in desperate need of help following cyclone Idai . They argue that industrialisation first in the West and now in the East has caused climate change and current political economy forces the poorer countries in the south to remain the suppliers of raw materials leading to massive inequalities between the northern and southern hemisphere . This inequality has hampered human development in southern countries . This could all be true ! The authors ' solution of pro-active aid is laudable and climate justice is perhaps a way of ensuring that communities are not so vulnerable to floods . However , in the case of Mozambique it illustrates another African phenomenon , that the Strong Men that govern these countries and the acceptance by their electorate of corrupt leaders are also to blame for poverty and their inability to mitigate climate change .
