Great white hope <p> The first map of the DNA of great white sharks has revealed mutations that protect the animals against cancer and other illnesses . Scientists hope further research will help treatment for cancer and age-related illnesses in humans . The research was carried out by a team of scientists at the Save Our Seas Foundation Shark Research Center at Nova Southeastern University in Florida .
