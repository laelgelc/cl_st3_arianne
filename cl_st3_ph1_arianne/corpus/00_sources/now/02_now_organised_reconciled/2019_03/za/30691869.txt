<p> Cape Town 's CBD and surrounding suburbs are purposefully not being load shed . <p> This is to ensure public safety , the city said . <p> Certain industrial areas may also not experience load shedding if they reduce their load . <p> The City of Cape Town central business district ( CBD ) and surrounding suburbs are purposefully not experiencing load shedding , the city confirmed on Friday morning . <p> Cape Town entered its seventh consecutive day of stage 4 load shedding on Wednesday and the areas , which include the upmarket suburbs of Gardens and Vredehoek , was noticeably not load shed . <p> Officially though , these suburbs are on the load shedding schedule on city documenation . <p> Phindile Maxiti , mayoral committee member for energy and climate change , said the areas are excluded from load shedding due to the " sheer volume of the population " in the areas . <p> " Public safety is the prime consideration as well as the impact on transport due to extreme traffic congestion caused by commuters moving into and outside of the CBD , " Maxiti told Business Insider South Africa .
