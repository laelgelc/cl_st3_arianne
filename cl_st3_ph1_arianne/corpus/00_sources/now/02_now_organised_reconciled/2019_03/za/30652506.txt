<h> South African municipalities now have access to the Green Book risk tool <p> Last week the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research ( CSIR ) launched a new online climate risk profiling and adaptation tool named Green Book . <p> According to SA News , Green Book is designed to help municipalities across the country to access climate risks and growth pressures , and also implement adaptation actions towards the development of climate resilient human settlements . <p> " The ultimate goal of the Green Book is to contribute to resilient , sustainable and liveable human settlements through climate change adaptation . It is also intended to be an applied knowledge resource for municipalities to address climate change impacts and vulnerabilities in human settlements , " said CSIR senior researcher and project lead , Willemien van Niekerk . <p> The development of Green Book was funded by the International Development Research Centre in Canada and the CSIR , and partnered by the National Disaster Management Centre . <p> CSIR added that , the multidisciplinary nature of Green Book is combined with the high-resolution scientific evidence , making it one of the most novel , innovative and information dense @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the African continent . <p> " The Green Book forecast to the year 2050 by projecting human settlement growth , combined with quantitative , scientific evidence of the likely impacts that climate change will have on South African towns and cities .
