What has the City done so far to become more energy secure ? <p> Energy2040 Goal : The City has demonstrated its commitment to accelerated climate action by signing the C40 Deadline 2020 : Climate Action Planning in Africa and the C40 South Africa Buildings Programmes . This goal models a more resilient , resource efficient and equitable future for Cape Town ; it commits the City to diversifying Cape Town 's energy supply and of becoming significantly more energy efficient and reducing carbon emissions . <p> Lower Carbon City Development : To achieve lower carbon city development and growth , significant efficiencies need to be realised across all sectors . With the transport sector in Cape Town driving energy consumption growth in the future , it is critical to reduce the number of private transport trips and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ through transit-oriented development ( densification around transport routes and nodes ) , travel demand management , higher vehicle occupancy and improved accessible , affordable , safe and user friendly public transport . <p> Carbon Neutrality by 2050 : The City is currently in the process of building its evidence base towards planning for carbon neutrality by 2050 by updating the City 's greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions inventory and updating its energy futures modelling . <p> Feasibility of small City-owned renewable energy generation plants : The City is exploring the feasibility of building a fleet of smaller City-owned renewable generation plants and developing a ' wheeling ' framework which will allow independent generators to sell electricity directly to electricity consumers embedded in the City 's electricity grid . <p> Cleaner supply : The City and US Trade and Development Agency have signed a R12,7 million donation agreement for a study to be conducted into the usage options of natural gas . <p> Waste-to-energy : Projects are being developed as part of the City 's Integrated Waste Management Strategy .
