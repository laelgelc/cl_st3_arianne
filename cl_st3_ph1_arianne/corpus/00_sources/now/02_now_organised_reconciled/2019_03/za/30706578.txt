Students in cities worldwide skipped classes Friday in protest over their governments ' failure to act against global warming . ( Henning Bagger/Ritzau Scanpix via AP ) <p> OPINION - AMID all the recent news of evil deeds , it seems a great pity to find fault with what appears to be good work being done by schoolchildren . <p> Yet it should be of some concern to adults that there are children taking time off school to " show that they care about the environment " . <p> Apparently they have been inspired by a Scandinavian schoolgirl who has learning problems . <p> She will probably be helped to make up for all the lost lessons , but will all the others ? <p> Schoolchildren should not miss school ( truancy it used to be called ) except under exceptional circumstances . Why could they not gather at a weekend ? <p> What the children should do is encourage the cleaning up of the environment , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Then they should go home after school and collect vegetable and garden refuse matter , cut it into small pieces and make compost for their garden , even if they only have pot plants . <p> The water in the home can be recycled by putting " grey " water ( used , soapy ) in the cistern instead of yanking on handles or chains to flush potable ( drinkable ) water down the toilet . <p> The Earth would benefit much more from this type of sensible behaviour than from all the marching around with placards.
