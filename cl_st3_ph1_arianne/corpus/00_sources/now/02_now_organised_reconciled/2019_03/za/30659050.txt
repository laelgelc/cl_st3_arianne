<h> Air pollution deadlier than smoking , researchers find <p> Air pollution is killing more people every year than smoking , according to research published on Tuesday that called for urgent action to stop burning fossil fuels . <p> Researchers in Germany and Cyprus estimated that air pollution caused 8,8 million extra deaths in 2015 , almost double the previously estimated 4,5 million . The World Health Organisation ( WHO ) estimates smoking kills about seven million people a year globally . <p> The researchers found that in Europe -- the key focus of the European Society of Cardiology research -- air pollution caused an estimated 790 000 deaths , between 40% and 80% of them from cardiovascular diseases such as heart attacks and stroke . <p> " Since most of the particulate matter and other air pollutants in Europe come from the burning of fossil fuels , we need to switch to other sources for generating energy urgently , " said the study 's co-author , Professor Jos Lelieveld of the Max-Plank Institute for Chemistry in Mainz and the Cyprus Institute Nicosia , Cyprus . <p> " When we use clean , renewable energy , we are not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change , we could also reduce air pollution-related death rates in Europe by up to 55% . " <p> ' Smoking is avoidable , not pollution ' <p> On average , a toxic cocktail of pollutants from vehicles , industry and agriculture shortens the lives of those who die prematurely by 2,2 years , the researchers calculated . <p> Worldwide , air pollution caused 120 extra deaths in every 100 000 people in a year , with deaths in parts of Europe at an even higher rate of up to 200 in 100 000 . <p> " This means that air pollution causes more extra deaths a year than tobacco smoking , which WHO estimates was responsible for an extra 7,2 million deaths in 2015 , " said senior author Thomas Munzel , a professor at the University Medical Centre Mainz in Germany . <p> " Smoking is avoidable , but air pollution is not .
