<p> Climate , political , legal and other risks had also become more prominent . Trade barriers " ... could reduce demand for certain of our commodities or restrict our suppliers , " the group said in the report . <p> Commenting on market volatility , Glencore said that significant falls in metals such as copper or zinc , or in the coal price , would have " ... a severe drag on our financial performance " . <p> The group said it had established an ethics committee which would examine climate risk posed by its business activities . <p> Ivan Glasenberg , CEO of Glencore , in February said that after nearly three years of engagement with stakeholders , including investors , the company had decided to cap coal production at 150 million tonnes a year . Investors were increasingly wary of , or restricted from , investing in companies that contributed to climate change . <p> The coal production cap decision has been criticised , however , as not going far enough ; succeeding only in building a supply deficit in an industry where Glencore was a dominant force .
