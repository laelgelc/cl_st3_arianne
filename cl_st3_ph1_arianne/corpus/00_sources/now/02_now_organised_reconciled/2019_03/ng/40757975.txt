<h> Planet savior or horror show ? Green New Deal inflames debate <p> WASHINGTON ( AP ) -- To Democratic supporters , the Green New Deal is a touchstone , a call to arms to combat climate change with the full measure of the nation 's resources and technological might . <p> To Republican opponents , it 's a dystopian nightmare , a roadmap to national bankruptcy in pursuit of zealous environmentalism . <p> Announced in February , the Green New Deal is a non-binding resolution that calls for a " 10-year national mobilization " to shift the economy away from fossil fuels such as oil and coal and replace them with renewable sources such as wind and solar power . <p> All six senators running for the Democratic presidential nomination support the plan , which is set to come up for a Senate vote next week . Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell hopes to test how far left Senate Democrats are willing to go .
