<h> Coal power generation : FG advised against implementation of policy <p> Friday , March 1 , 2019 2:16 pm <p> FILE PHOTO : Coal <p> An ecologist , Mr David Michael , has urged the Federal Government not to implement coal power generation policy because it is against Nationally Determined Contributions ( NDCs ) efforts on emissions reduction . <p> Michael , the Executive Director , Global Initiative for Food Security and Ecosystem Preservation , an NGO , gave the advice in Abuja on Friday . <p> Nationally Determined Contributions ( NDCs ) are at the heart of the Paris Agreement on Climate Change and the achievement of these long term goals . <p> NDCs embody efforts by each country to reduce national emissions and adapt to the impacts of climate change . <p> The executive director said that the Ministry of Power , Works and Housing must jettison the idea of coal power generation " this is going against the Nationally Determined Contributions . ' ' <p> " However , the solution to our energy poverty especially in rural areas remains through solar and other forms of renewable energy , ' ' he said . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Muhammadu Buhari on his victory at the polls . We also sincerely appreciate the commitment shown so far by Mr President to the ratification of the Paris Agreement . <p> " As an organisation that is concerned about the adverse effects of climate change on our populace , we entreat Mr President to intensify the implementation strategies and policies of government to achieve the targets of 2030 , ' ' Michael said . <p> He urged the government to implement policies that would build the resilience of farmers to respond to climate change challenges by ensuring climate insurance and climate-smart agriculture . <p> According to him , it should work towards total removal of fuel subsidy and implementation of a carbon fee and dividend policy in the country .
