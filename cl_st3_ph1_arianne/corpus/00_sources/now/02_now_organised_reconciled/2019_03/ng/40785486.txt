<h> Cities around the world turn off lights to mark Earth Hour <p> HONG KONG ( AP ) -- Cities around the world are marking Earth Hour by turning off the lights in a call for global action on climate change . <p> In Hong Kong , major buildings along Victoria Harbour turned off their non-essential lights at 8:30 p.m . Saturday , and the city 's popular tourist attraction known as the Symphony of Lights was canceled . <p> Over 3,000 corporations in Hong Kong signed up for Earth Hour 2019 , according to the WWF Hong Kong website . Iconic skyscrapers including the Bank of China Tower and the HSBC Building in Central , the city 's major business district , switched off their lights in response to the global movement . <p> Beginning in Sydney in 2007 , Earth Hour has spread to more than 180 countries , with tens of millions of people joining in.
