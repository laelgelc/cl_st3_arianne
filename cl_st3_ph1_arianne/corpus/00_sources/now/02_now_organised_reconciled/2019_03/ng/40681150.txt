<h> Forest fire rages on Mount Kenya , Africa 's second highest peak <p> 03/01/2019 <p> Tell your friends <p> Wildlife rangers , soldiers and huge teams of volunteers fought on Friday to stem a fierce forest fire raging on Mount Kenya national park for nearly a week.The forest blaze has destroyed more than 80,000 hectares <p> Kenya Wildlife Service ( KWS ) , the government force in charge of national parks , said the fire began last Saturday , and estimated that more than 80,000 hectares ( 200,000 acres ) around the wider Mount Kenya region had been destroyed . <p> " We have made good progress in containing the Mt Kenya fire , " KWS said in a statement , but noted reports of fresh outbreaks . <p> Helicopters flew shuttle runs to support the volunteers doing the dangerous work in the forest to stop the blaze . Others made giant water drops to stem the fire . <p> Above the plumes of smoke , the peak of the mountain -- at 5,199 metres , or 17,057 feet high , Africa 's second highest after Kilimanjaro in Tanzania -- was covered in ice and snow patches . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kenya Forest Service ( KFS ) and the army , the Kenya Defence Force ( KDF ) . <p> " Hundreds of community members , volunteers , KWS and KFS rangers and the KDF teams currently are on the mountain clearing fire breaks and beating out fires , " the Mount Kenya Trust , a body set up to conserve the forests said on Friday . <p> The park , with the peak at its centre , straddles the equator , lying some 193 kilometres ( 120 miles ) northeast of the capital Nairobi . <p> Mount Kenya was inscribed as a World Heritage Site in 1997 by the United Nations , which lists climate change as being " probably one of the most serious long-term threats to the site .
