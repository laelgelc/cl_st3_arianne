<h> Temperature 'll be abnormally high March-May , says NiMet <p> Nigeria will experience abnormally high temperatures this year , especially in the months of March , April and May , the Nigerian Meteorological Agency said on Saturday . <p> NiMet also stated that it would install 100 weather stations across the country this year to help address the extreme weather situation and increase the dense network of weather stations in Nigeria . <p> The agency 's Director-General , Prof. Sani Mashi , disclosed this in Abuja at an event to commemorate the 2019 World Meteorological Day . <p> The global community celebrates World Meteorological Day on March 23 every year , and this year 's celebration had its theme as ' The Sun , the Earth and the Weather .
