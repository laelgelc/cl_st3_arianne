<h> US , NGOs call for concerted action on marine wildlife conservation <p> THE United States Consulate General in Lagos has urged Nigerians to treat the conservation of the oceans and aquatic wildlife as an individual responsibility , in order to preserve marine habitats for future generations . <p> The Public Affairs Officer of the US Consulate General Lagos , Mr Russell Brooks , made this known , delivering his remark at an event attended by leading environmental conservation activists , in Lagos . <p> The event commemorating the 2019 World Wildlife Day , was organised in collaboration with the International Climate Change Development Initiative ( ICCDI ) and Wildlife of Africa Conservation Initiative . <p> Brooks , speaking on the theme of Life below Water , observed that as much as 40 per cent of the world 's oceans is negatively affected by human activity , including overexploitation of marine species , loss of coastal habitats and pollution . He highlighted the crucial importance of the oceans and marine species to human development and emphasised the necessity for increased conservation education . <p> " Marine wildlife has sustained human civilisation and development for over a thousand years , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and construction . We can not take their survival for granted . We can reduce some of the negative effects of our activity on life under the water , by working hard to spread the message to reduce marine pollution , " Brooks said . <p> Delivering welcoming remarks earlier , the Communications Director at ICCDI , Mr Abiodun Adekoya , noted that effective climate change mitigation in the country would require concerted action by governments and individuals , with an emphasis on conservation education , recycling programs and the creation of a legal framework for the nation 's policy on the environment . <p> World Wildlife Day is observed annually on March 3 , following a proclamation by the United Nations General Assembly on 20 December 2013.
