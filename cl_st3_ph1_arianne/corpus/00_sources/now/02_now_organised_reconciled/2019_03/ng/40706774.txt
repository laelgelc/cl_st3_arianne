<p> The Federal Government has sent its condolences to the family of the late former Ambassador Abiodun Oluremi Bashua who was among the 149 passengers and 8 crew members that boarded the ill-fated Ethiopian Airlines Flight ET302 . <p> The plane crashed shortly after take-off from the Ethiopian capital , Addis Ababa on Sunday , on its way to Nairobi , Kenya , killing all on board . <p> In a statement , the spokesperson for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs , George Edokpa , described the death as ' untimely ' , adding that the minister , Geoffrey Onyeama , and the ministry were in great shock . <p> " The Honourable Minister of Foreign Affairs , Geoffrey Onyeama and members of Staff of the Ministry received the sad news of his death with great shock and prayed that the Almighty God grant his family and the nation , the fortitude to bear the irreparable loss . " <p> Ambassador Bashua was a seasoned UN Expert who had extensive experience in several United Nations peacekeeping operations in Africa . <p> He joined the African Union-United Nations Hybrid Operation in Darfur ( UNAMID ) in 2009 and was appointed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Operation in Darfur in 2014 by the Secretary-General of the United Nations . <p> Ambassador Bashua also served as Secretary to the Conference of Parties of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change .
