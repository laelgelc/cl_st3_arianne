<h> IITA , NCF Widens School Conservation Clubs Training <p> The International Institute of Tropical Agriculture , IITA , with the collaboration of the Nigerian Conservation Foundation , NCF , are exerting more energy in the effort to train trainers , who are coordinators of various Schools Conservation Clubs ( SCCs ) in the South west . <p> Through its Forest Centre , FC , the IITA , organised a workshop in Ibadan where it gathered 23 SCC Coordinators from primary and secondary schools in Ekiti , Osun , and Oyo states for further learning process . <p> In partnership with the NCF , the FC has set up 43 SCCs in the South west with funding from the US Consulate General Lagos and AG Leventis Foundation . <p> The event aimed to instill in coordinators environmental conservation activities , which they will pass on to students , who are future leaders and nature ambassadors . <p> In her opening remarks , at the workshop , Hilde Koper-Limbourg , IITA Deputy Director General , Corporate Services , highlighted the activities of IITA , including those relating to environmental protection . <p> " Here in IITA , we are keen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ assist in stabilising different ecosystems . I am very happy to hear that 23 new schools have joined the FC 's conservation clubs . It is a great honor to have you here at IITA and I am pleased to see that you all are interested in environmental education , " she said . <p> Speaking on the Child Protection Policy , George Piacentini , IITA School Head Teacher , highlighted the need to take drastic measures to halt the abuse of children which may be physical , emotional , sexual , or neglect . He said , " IITA recognizes its responsibility to ensure the protection and safety of every child within the campus . " <p> Speaking on the need for protecting biodiversity , FC Manager Adewale Awoyemi explained , " Biodiversity conservation is basically the protection , enhancement , and scientific management of the varieties of life on earth . <p> " In recent years , the rate of loss of biodiversity is worrisome .
