<p> 28-year-old Oluwaseun Ayodeji Osowobi , renowned for helping thousands of sexual and domestic abuse victims , has been named Commonwealth YoungPerson of the Year . <p> Osowobi is among four exceptional young people from India , Nigeria , the Solomon Islands and Saint Lucia that won prizes at the 2019 Commonwealth Youth Awards in recognition of their efforts to end hunger , sexual violence , sanitation issues and climate change . <p> The Nigerian won based on her focus on the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) 5 on Gender Equality while Padmanaban Gopalan of India won the Asian prize with her focus on SDG 2 , " Zero Hunger " . <p> Johanan Dujon of Saint Lucia , however , picked the prize for the Caribbean and Canada with a focus on SDG 9 : Industry , Innovation and Infrastructure with Bobby Siarani of Solomon Islands won for the Pacific through a project that focused on SDG 11 : Sustainable Cities and Communities . <p> During a brief ceremony held on Thursday at the Commonwealth 's headquarters in London , Osowobi and other winners shared a ? 12,000 grant for their innovative projects .
