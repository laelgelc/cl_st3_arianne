<p> A weather expert agency , Nigeria Meteorological Agency ( NiMet ) has advised Nigerians to expect and prepare themselves as there will be hotter weather withing March and May . <p> This was disclosed by the Director General of NiMet , Prof. Sani Mashi , during the celebration of the World Meteorological day with the theme ' The Sun , the east and the weather ' . <p> He advised Nigerians to avoid crowded areas . <p> Prof. Mashi , also advised farmers to disregard the pockets of rainfall witnessed in some parts of the country , adding that they should adhere to the agency 's prediction to avoid crop loss . <p> The agency also allayed fears that Nigeria may experience extreme weather events . <p> It said the location of the country makes it immune to extreme weather events like the tropical storms witnessed recently in Mozambique , Zimbabwe and Malawi . <p> Mashi said : " It is very clear that , our environment is in serious challenge of extreme weather events as a result of climate change , which is seen as the single most prominent risk . <p> " As such , 2018 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on record , and was the warmest year without an El Nino . <p> " While 2019 as an ElNino year , has already begun with pockets of extreme events in form of severe storms currently ravaging Mozambique and parts of Australia . <p> " This came after the passage of Polar Vortices as well as soaring temperature of about 50 degrees Celsius in Australia recently .
