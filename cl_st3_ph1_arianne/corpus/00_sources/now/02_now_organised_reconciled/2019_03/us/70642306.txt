It 's Earth Hour <p> The Eiffel Tower in Paris loses its glow during the 2016 Earth Hour . <p> ( CNN ) -- Do n't be surprised if you find your neighbors turning off their lights for a full hour Saturday night . They 're taking part in a global movement called Earth Hour . <h> How did it start ? <p> Earth Hour is an annual tradition in which people switch off their lights to spread awareness about sustainability and climate change . Started by the World Wildlife Fund , it takes place this year on March 30 , from 8:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. local time . ( And it 's not to be confused with Earth Day , which is April 22 . ) <h> When did it start ? <p> Since then , millions of people around the world have taken part .
