<h> Christianne Wilhelmson : After a decade of green , will Vancouver 's first citywide plan have a splash of blue ? <h> It 's time to bring Vancouver 's city planning up to speed and put a splash of blue , alongside all of the green , in our first citywide plan . <p> Many Vancouverites may be unaware that city council is on the verge of doing something new and exciting . It 's scrapping its decades-old city planning structure -- the one that creates custom community plans by analyzing one neighbourhood at a time -- in favour of a city-wide perspective . It 's an indication that our city is maturing . <p> It 's deepening its understanding of the intricacies and co-dependencies of land use and the need to view the city as a whole when planning for the future . As the city takes this big step , it 's also a chance , hopefully , to finally integrate the interface between land and sea into our city view . As a metropolis , we 're exceptionally concerned about zoning and bylaws as these dictate design and density .
