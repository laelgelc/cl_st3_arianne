<h> Share <h> The IEA 's Search For A New Narrative Of Doom <p> The International Energy Agency ( IEA ) released its Oil 2019 report March 11 , providing forecasts to 2024 . The IEA paints an extraordinary picture of transition for the oil industry , but it is not the ' energy transition ' driven by climate change ; the impacts on the oil market of policies designed to mitigate climate change appear relatively weak over the next five years . The seismic changes illustrated by the IEA are those wrought by the US shale industry . <p> The IEA 's former narrative on US shale was that once it had burst upon the scene it would eventually burn itself out in the mid-2020s .
