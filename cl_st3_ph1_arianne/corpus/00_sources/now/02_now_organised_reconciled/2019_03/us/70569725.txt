<p> Science is all over the news these days . We hear about climate change and automated cars and the mysteries of the brain . <p> A lot of what happens with science takes place behind closed doors , hidden on the leafy campuses of universities or in quiet corners of medical office buildings . <p> But the good news for people who live in North Texas , and their families , is that The Dallas Morning News and its partners want to help you peer inside research labs while meeting some of the most important scientists in the country . <p> This year , " Science in the City " will unfold over a series of Saturdays from March 16 to April 13 .
