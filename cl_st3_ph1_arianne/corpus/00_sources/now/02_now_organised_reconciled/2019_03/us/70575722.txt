<p> On Feb. 21 , the Press Herald reported ( Page B1 ) on people recording sea-level rise in Casco Bay , due in part to climate change . What to do ? <p> Consider supporting HR 763 , introduced to the House in January with the goal of charging fossil fuel companies for the garbage -- gases -- they send into our atmosphere . Fees collected would be sent as dividends directly to households to help citizens afford to transition to clean energy . <p> Too untried a notion ? British Columbia has been pricing carbon since 2013 , and all other Canadian provinces have since followed suit . Results : Canada 's Ecofiscal Commission reports , " Economists are virtually unanimous in the view that carbon pricing reduces greenhouse-gas emissions at the lowest possible cost to the economy . " <p> Interestingly , two leading sponsors ( one a Republican , the other a Democrat ) of HR 763 are from low-lying Florida , where rising seas flood more property than in Maine .
