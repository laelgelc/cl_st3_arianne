<p> On the International Day of Forests , Mohit Gera , Director of the Institute of Forest Genetics and Tree Breeding ( IFGTB ) , conducted a session for school students at Gass Forest Museum here on Thursday . <p> He addressed students of Class IX on ' Climate Change and Forest Ecosystems . ' <p> Stressing the importance of trees to humans , Mr. Gera said that trees should be paid for rendering their service to us . <p> At the end of the session , a short film titled ' The Value of Forests ' was screened for the students . <p> After the session , Mr. Gera spoke to The Hindu about further steps needed to educate young people about the importance of conserving forests . <p> " Specific films and programmes are required for schoolchildren explaining the importance of trees and forests . Children must also be taken to field trips for first-hand experience , " he said . <h> Policy decisions <p> Mr. Gera said the government must take some policy decisions such as insurance mechanisms to encourage farmers to grow more trees .
