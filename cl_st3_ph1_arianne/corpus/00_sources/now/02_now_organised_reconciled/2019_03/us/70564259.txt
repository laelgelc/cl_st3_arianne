It 's one further step in the direction of destruction of the Democratic Party . Let 's look at some of the facts . <p> First of all the Green Deal is based on a false premise . That premise is the concept of climate change is valid and that it is accepted science . Not true . They are quoting impossible outcomes based on a very limited number of factors . <p> Second , the Green Deal promises to electrify everything in the United States and produce zero emissions .
