The Democratic Party itself is a coalition , and that coalition now includes people who used to be Republican moderates or independents , especially in the suburban areas where Democratic victories in 2018 gave them control of the House . If Democrats ignore that side of the party , they will not only lose the presidency in 2020 -- they 'll lose the House too . Democrats now hold 31 districts won by Trump in 2016 , while Republicans hold only three won by Clinton .
