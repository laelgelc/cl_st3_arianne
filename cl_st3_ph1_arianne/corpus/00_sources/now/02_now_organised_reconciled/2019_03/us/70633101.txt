<h> ' Doomsday Preppers ' on Netflix : Do These People Fear The End Of The World , Or Are They Hoping For It ? <p> " I 'm not obsessed with prepping . I 'm obsessed with living . " This is the mission statement of one of the titular subjects of Doomsday Preppers , the National Geographic reality show now available for streaming on Netflix . As we meet a wide and impressive variety of people determined to live through the end of the world , it becomes very clear : their vision of living is very different than most of ours . <p> It 's not uncommon these days , even for seemingly-normal people , to worry about disasters . Climate change , global unrest , terrorism -- there are genuine threats to comfortable society out there . The subjects on Doomsday Preppers all have their own pet notion of what form our destructor will take , though . A sudden rush of destructive F5 tornadoes .
