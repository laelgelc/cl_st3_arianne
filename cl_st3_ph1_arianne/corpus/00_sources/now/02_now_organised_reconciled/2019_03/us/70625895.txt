Working with toothpaste " was a very unusual request , " says Bill Crawford , the general manager of MB Bark . It may be the first time that a factory 's toothpaste waste has been composted . The company worked with Tom 's and the experts from the Maine Compost School , a program run through the University of Maine , the Maine Department of Environmental Protection , and the Maine Department of Agriculture , Conservation and Forestry , to generate and test soil samples . <p> Photo : Tom 's of Maine " As you can imagine , everything is natural from Tom 's , " he says . " But the ingredients were suitable for composting , and some were even beneficial . The toothpaste , it turned out , is considered a " good processing residual , " meaning that it works well in composting .
