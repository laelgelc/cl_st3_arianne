<h> SENIOR MOMENTS How Do We Get Out of This Traffic Jam ? <p> By Stephanie Vendig on March 28 , 2019 <p> Stephanie Vendig <p> Driving or commuting in Los Angeles has become more of an endurance task than a pleasure and it is getting worse . The car has defined our culture because our original city planning was all about the car moving quickly through town . Now we have to change direction . <p> The pervasive " car culture " of Los Angeles has had an impact on how the city developed its public transportation . While other major cities designed their public transit systems along with their cities , intending them to be used by everyone , public transit in Los Angeles has always found it hard to compete with the car . <p> In 2008 , the organization " Move LA " built a constituency advocating for a more comprehensive public transportation system for Los Angeles County . They convinced LA Metro to put Measure R on the ballot and it won with 67.9% of the votes . In 2016 , Measure M , an extension of Measure R , was approved by 71.1% @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for transportation improvements over the next 40 years . <p> But when the recession and housing crisis hit , combined with fare increases and services cuts , public transit ridership decreased 6% and by 17% in 2015 , perhaps due to the displacement created by surging housing prices of so many low income riders . <p> To add to our woes , we continue to face climate change as a result of , in part , emissions of our heavy-duty trucks and the abundance of cars on the road . <p> But there is hope . <p> The recent 10th annual Transportation Conversation of Move LA featured Metro CEO Phil Washington , California State Senator Kevin de Leon , and Chairperson Mary Nichols of the California Air Resources Board . They , along with other panelists representing public , private and nonprofit sectors strategized solutions to the challenges of getting from here to there in SoCal . <p> Among the ideas explored were free transit for students , seniors and the disabled , and increasing affordable housing closer to transit hubs . Piloting a Congestion Pricing program was also discussed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the times and areas that have the most congestion . <p> In November 2020 , " Vision 2020 " will be on the ballot in the South Coast Air Quality Management District , including Los Angeles , Orange , San Bernardino and Riverside counties , excluding the high desert . It would propose a sales tax to help reduce emissions , expand and electrify Metrolink , and enlarge the Move LA Coalition to become Move SoCal . <p> Solutions are on the horizon . We need to keep an eye out for them and vote accordingly .
