<p> The largest solar farm east of the Rocky Mountains could soon be built in Virginia and , depending on whom you ask , it would be either a dangerous eyesore that will destroy the area 's rural character or a win-win , boosting the local economy and the environment . The solar panels would be spread across 10 square miles -- 1.8 million panels soaking up the sun 's rays . <p> The project is planned for Spotsylvania County , about 60 miles south of Washington , D.C. Amid the county 's Civil War battlefields , farms and timberland , a fight is raging over the future of energy in Virginia , and in the Eastern U.S. <p> The heart of the solar resistance is in a gated community called Fawn Lake , built around a golf course and man-made lake . <p> " I mean we live at a resort , essentially , " says Dave Walsh , one of the many Fawn Lake residents organizing against the planned solar farm . One corner of the massive project would butt up against the back of the gated community .
