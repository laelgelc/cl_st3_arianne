<h> Letter of the Day : Bill C-69 <p> The claim that Bill C-69 would put a stop to all large energy projects in Canada caught my attention for a couple of reasons . <p> First , it demonstrates the belief that only fossil fuel and nuclear energy projects are worthy of private investment , invariably subsidized , and sometimes heavily , with taxpayer dollars . <p> But more importantly it brings to mind the " moral " argument for storing nuclear waste in Saskatchewan . If we produce it , we should be willing to store it , so the argument went . <p> Of course , the argument immediately fails because it is based on the fact that nuclear waste is extremely hazardous and dangerous , which puts into question the reasons for mining uranium in the first place . <p> Similarly , if environmental regulations and impact assessments pose such a threat to large scale energy projects that they would likely go extinct , does n't this tell us that we should perhaps reconsider their viability in the first place ? <p> Since there are no Asian markets at present for oilsands products , it would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy , a sector which will employ far more individuals than fossil fuel or nuclear energy sources as well as help to engage the environmental crisis caused by climate change . <p> W.R.
