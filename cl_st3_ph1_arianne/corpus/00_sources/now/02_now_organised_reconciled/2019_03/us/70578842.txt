<p> It snowed in the mountains Friday more than it did in Washington , setting up a terrific weekend of late winter skiing at many resorts . <p> A dusting to several inches fell Friday . In eastern West Virginia , Canaan Valley picked up over a foot in the past week . The conditions are so prime that Pennsylvania 's Seven Springs has opened up its glades ( tree skiing , to the uninitiated ) that rely solely on natural snow pack . So needless to say , the conditions are awesome . <p> The natural snow has allowed Canaan Valley to boost its trail count from 24 to 40 . Other resorts have taken advantage of the week of cold to reinforce their bases with a fresh layer of man-made powder . Just about every lift and run in the Mid-Atlantic will be in business this weekend . <p> It 's hard to believe , but Pennsylvania 's Liberty is calling uncle on the snow making . " Snow-making in progress ? No need , " said its Friday morning report .
