We 're no longer the singular dominant power that we were just after the Cold War , but we 're still the pivotal player . We still have lots of assets : the world 's best military ; an economy that is bigger and more innovative than anyone else 's , even though it is plagued by inequalities ; and a capacity for alliances and coalition building unmatched by our rivals .
