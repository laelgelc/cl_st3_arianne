Her parents are keeping her at home to protect her from the toxic air outside . <p> The oldest of three children , Jargalmaa is a resident of Ulaanbaatar , Mongolia , the world 's coldest capital city and one of its most polluted . As Ulaanbaatar continues to grow and industrialize , the problem has only worsened .
