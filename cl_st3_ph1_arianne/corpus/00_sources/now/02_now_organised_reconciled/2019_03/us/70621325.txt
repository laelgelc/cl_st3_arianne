<h> " No Retreat " is theme of upcoming Kansas Indian Series <h> Sunday <p> Mar 24 , 2019 at 1:01 AM <p> The Kinsley Library discussion series , " Kansas Indians : Walking Paths of Yesterday and Today " , will hold its last session from 2-5 p.m. on Sunday , March 31 . Presentations will focus on contemporary Native American issues and contributions . The event will be held at the Kinsley High School cafeteria , 716 S. Colony , Kinsley ( entrance on 8th St. ) . <p> Dr. Bonnie Lynn-Sherow will begin the afternoon speaking on " No Retreat . " She will be using direct quotations from current leaders to survey recent tribal efforts and projects underway in Indian country . Dr. Lynn-Sherow received her PhD . in American History from Northwestern University . Currently , she is Associate Professor of American History and the Executive Director of the Chapman Center for Rural Studies at Kansas State University . " The presentation will examine the environment and how we live with it from the viewpoint of ancient Native American wisdom and nature-centered beliefs . Dr. Wildcat is the author of " Red Alert : Saving the Planet with Indigenous Knowledge " ( Fulcrum Publishing , 2009 ) . In it , he suggests that " Current global climate change issues will require the exercise of indigenous ingenuity -- indigenuity -- and wisdom if human kind is to reduce the ecological damage that is well underway . " Dr. Wildcat received a PhD in Interdisciplinary Studies at the University of Missouri at Kansas City . He has taught for 27 years at Haskell Indian Nations University where he is the Director of the Haskell Environmental Research Studies Center . Anyone interested in attending , if they have not already done so , should register on the library website ( www.kinsleylibrary.info ) or by calling the library , 620-659-3341 .
