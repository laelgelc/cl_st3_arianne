<p> The treacherous journey to the summit of Mount Everest is riddled with obstacles -- falling ice , ragged terrain , biting temperatures and incredible heights that cause altitude sickness . While nearly 5,000 people have successfully climbed the mountain , another 300 are thought to have died along the way . <p> Some of these bodies ended up covered in ice and remained hidden that way for many years . But now , climate change is accelerating the ice melt around them , exposing multiple limbs and bodies , the BBC reported last month . <p> Last year , a group of researchers found that the ice on Everest was warmer than average , and a study conducted four years ago found that ponds on the mountain were expanding with melting ice water , according to the BBC .
