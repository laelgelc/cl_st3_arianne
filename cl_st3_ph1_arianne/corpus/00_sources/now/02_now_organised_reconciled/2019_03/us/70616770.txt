In fact , the U.S. could cut its carbon-dioxide emissions 100 percent , and it would n't make a difference in averting temperature increases or sea level rise . <p> Using a model developed primarily with funding from the Environmental Protection Agency , climatologists estimate that eliminating all of America 's CO2 emissions mitigates global temperatures a mere 0.11 degree Celsius by the year 2100 . With the overwhelming majority of emissions coming from the developing world who see energy poverty as more pressing threat , U.S. action is meaningless . <p> In other words , the expensive energy , lost jobs , expansion of government power and opened flood gates of corporate welfare and cronyism would all be for a change in the earth 's temperature that 's barely measurable .
