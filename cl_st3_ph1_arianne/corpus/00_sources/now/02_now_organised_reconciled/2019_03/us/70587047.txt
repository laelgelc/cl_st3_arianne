<p> California Gov . " I think we hear what we want to hear . So no , I ca n't ' admit ' to what I said to the president privately , because that 's not what I said . " <p> In his CPAC speech , which stretched on for more than two hours , Trump claimed that Newsom called him up and lavished praise on him . <p> " He called me up the other day , recently -- let 's say four weeks ago or so . He said , ' I just want to tell you @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the smartest people I 've ever met . ' That 's what he said . Now , that 's what he said . Will he admit it ? No , I doubt it . But that 's what he said , " Trump told the crowd .
