<h> Income of J&amp;K farmers lowest in country : GoI report <p> Farmers in Jammu and Kashmir have lowest income in comparison to their counterparts in rest of the country , reveals a government of India report . <p> The compound annual growth rates ( CAGR ) of real incomes between 2002-03 ( NSSO survey ) and 2015-16 ( NABARD survey ) of agri-households in Jammu and Kashmir is minus 2.3 which is not only lowest in the country but it is the only state with minus CAGR , according the report . <p> Overall CAGR is 3.7 percent at the all India level , with a high of 8.4 percent for Odisha . <p> Experts attribute the failure of state governments over the period of time to draft agriculture policy as the one of the reason for dwindling income of farmers . <p> Interestingly , the largest employer and the primary driver of Jammu and Kashmir 's economy -- agriculture -- continues to be in decline . <p> Over the past decade , its contribution to the state 's gross domestic product ( GSDP ) has fallen by 8 percent . <p> According to the Draft Srinagar Master @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 28 percent to 16 percent , while industries ( secondary sector ) has stayed stagnant at 43 percent and the share of services ( tertiary sector ) has gone up to 57 percent in the past ten years . <p> Agriculture , which has been categorised as the weakest contributor in the draft master plan , provides employment to about 60 percent of the population , followed by the industries and services sectors .
