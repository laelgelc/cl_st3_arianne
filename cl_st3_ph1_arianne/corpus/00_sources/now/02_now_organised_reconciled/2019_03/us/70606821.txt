Around the country , the loose alliance of politicians , activists , and organizations concerned about climate change is mobilizing . They are deploying a new set of strategies aimed at changing the minds -- or at least the behaviors -- of a large swath of Americans , including utility managers , school principals , political donors , and rank-and-file voters . <p> They make a ragtag group : United by little more than common concern , they do n't agree on an ideal federal policy or even how to talk about the problem .
