<h> Trump Proposes a Record $4.75 Trillion Budget <p> WASHINGTON -- President Trump sent Congress on Monday a record $4.75 trillion budget plan that calls for increased military spending and sharp cuts to domestic programs like education and environmental protection for the 2020 fiscal year . <p> Mr. Trump 's budget , the largest in federal history , includes a nearly 5 percent increase in military spending -- which is more than the Pentagon had asked for -- and an additional $8.6 billion for construction of a wall along the border with Mexico . It also contains what White House officials called a total of $1.9 trillion in cost savings from mandatory safety-net programs , like Medicaid and Medicare , the federal health care programs for the elderly and the poor . <p> The budget is unlikely to have much effect on actual spending levels , which are controlled by Congress .
