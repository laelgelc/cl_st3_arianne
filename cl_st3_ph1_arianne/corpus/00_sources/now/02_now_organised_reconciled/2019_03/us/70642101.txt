In fact , for most of the paper 's history you never saw it at all -- not because fart was n't in regular use ( it has been since at least half a millennium before The Times existed ) , but rather because of its crudeness . It was n't until 1972 that it made its first appearance , in an extended quotation in an article about the writer Samuel Beckett . For the first story to feature farts , it had the perfect title : " As Close to Silence as a Man Can Get . So many , in fact , that they have their own section . <p> " We had too many good stories from writers about farts , " the NYT Kids editor , Amber Williams , said . " So we needed a farts page .
