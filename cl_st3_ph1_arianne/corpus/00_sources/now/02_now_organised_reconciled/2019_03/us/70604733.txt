<p> Record-breaking , historic , even Biblical -- that 's how many are describing the scale of flooding in the Midwest , where thousands of people in Iowa and Nebraska have been forced to flee their homes . In a region that 's no stranger to rising floodwaters , a perfect storm of melting snow , " bomb cyclone " rain and climate change conspired to push these floods over the top . <p> As of Monday , 100 river gauges in the Midwest and Mississippi River valleys remained at major or moderate flood stage , with many in record-setting territory .
