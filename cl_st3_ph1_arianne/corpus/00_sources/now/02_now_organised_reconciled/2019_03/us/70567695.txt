The desperate rush by " law and order " conservatives to defend a child rapist has shown there is no principle that the right of Australian politics wo n't abandon in order to protect one of their inner circle . The " tough on crime " brigade are usually quick to criticise judges for lenient sentencing , but with George Pell we are told that the trial was ordeal enough for the frail old man . The political strategy of the tribal right in Australia is built on such hypocrisy . <p> For decades the free marketeers in the business community , the social conservatives in the Catholic church , the rent seekers in the Farmers Federation , the small government Institute of Public Affairs and the Murdoch media outlets have been engaged in a coalition of convenience .
