<p> Cyclone Idai triggered flooding across a wide swath of coastal Mozambique , washing away bridges , roads and homes , and leaving people stranded on the banks of a river in the village of John Segredo on Sunday.CreditCreditMike Hutchings/Reuters <p> March 25 , 2019 <p> People in parts of Mozambique , Zimbabwe and Malawi are now picking through the wreckage left behind by Cyclone Idai , a category four storm that brought weeks of rain , floods and high winds to a swath of southeastern Africa . <p> Mozambique was hit especially hard . About 450 people died in Mozambique , 260 in Zimbabwe and 55 in Malawi , according to preliminary estimates from officials . Rescue workers say that thousands more in coastal areas of Mozambique may have been swept out to sea as the floodwaters rose . Their bodies may never be found . <p> At least three million people were affected by the devastation , said Antonio Guterres , the Secretary General of the United Nations .
