<h> Anderson : A jolt of reality in the ' reality show ' <p> By R. Bruce Anderson Ledger columnist <h> Sunday <p> Mar 31 , 2019 at 12:01 AM <p> The agonizing cries from the frustrated Democrats coming as a result of the release of Robert Mueller 's report means that impeachment -- never really an option -- is probably now out of reach for even the most rabid and wild-eyed Trump-detractors . <p> Sorry , folks , there was an election , and no amount of being horrified by the outcome is going to alter that decision . Learn to live with it . It 's far past time to move on . To govern . <p> We have essentially spent the past two years and more waiting around to see if something , anything , would overturn the election of 2016 . Marking time . The GOP did so for a couple of years , waiting for the Mueller shoe to drop -- a flock of " Chicken Littles " fearful that their president would implode . <p> And now the Democrats , certain that Trump would be unceremoniously pitched out , have done the same @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . It ends with a whimper . Trump is still president , and all the issues we faced two years ago are still in front of us . <p> And now everyone wants to jump directly into a melee about the 2020 presidential race . <p> Stop it ! There 's work to do ! <p> What have we basically ignored for the past two years ? I decided to come up with my own , personal short list of stuff we 've done nothing about , but desperately need to : <p> * Guns . The U.S. is not New Zealand , and it seems that no matter what the trade-offs are , gun culture is a reality . So is crime . We fortunately have the best-trained , best-led law enforcement folks in the universe , but even they are fighting a ( sometimes ) losing battle . We 've played the margins but never addressed the issue . Kids are getting killed . <p> * Carbon output . We have the same sort of fixation on muscle cars and fat , bloated lane-tanks that other nations have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us on this one . So what are the alternatives ? <p> * Climate change . See #2 . <p> * Scandal . We 've spent 500 hours on scandal and " gotcha " for every minute spent on policy for the past two years . Thank heaven the economy is good and the North Koreans have not invaded . Can we condemn this to Hollywood , where it belongs ? <p> * A love of cheap , often illegal , foreign labor . There are tons of jobs Americans wo n't do , and we seem to be happy having others , mostly from poorer countries , do them . Unfortunately , this also leads to ... <p> * Stupid , confusing , contradictory immigration laws . Despite the best efforts of the president to try to solve for this ( however silly or misguided his policies may be ) , the " people 's representatives " refuse to participate . Get with the program . Any program . <p> * The deficit . We ca n't seem to control our appetite for spending money we do not have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Note , though , that the taxpayer is also complicit in this . We agree that the government should cut spending , just not on OUR projects and priorities . Let 's wise up . <p> * Polluting packaging . It should not require a chainsaw to free a bottle of shampoo from its Amazon wrapper . See also " climate change . " <p> * A media strewn with the yammering of ignorant , no-solution dingbats swarming around all of the things cited above . I include non-critical purveyors of silly notions , anti-science know-nothings , party polarity political fanatics ; the " let 's rule by prosecution " folks and their investigations ( it was bad under Barack Obama ; just as bad now ) . Online trolls of all sorts , but especially people who think having a Twitter account bestows on them that law degree -- or medical degree ( see #7 ) -- that they neither earned nor deserve . They come from left and right , and we need to get an arm on this crew . <p> Of course , we have no @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> We hit the " pause " button . One of the great trade-offs of a republican form of government is that as long as the people are distracted , nothing really has to be done . But the problems fester on . We have to engage . We have to color within the lines . We need to know , and work , the process . <p> None of the things above are things we " have " to live with ( well , probably nothing can be done about #10 ) . We 've wasted two precious years in phony , escalating posturing ; is n't it time to get to work ? Can we now get a fresh start ? <p> R.
