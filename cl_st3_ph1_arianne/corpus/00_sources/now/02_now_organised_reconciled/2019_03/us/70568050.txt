<h> How The Division 2 video game recreated and destroyed DC <p> In one of the biggest video games of the year , players have to fight their way through the apocalyptic ruins of Washington , D.C. Recreating the District , and destroying it , for The Division 2 was no small task . Here 's how developer Massive Entertainment did it . See photos and video . <p> There 's no shortage of political fighting in the District , but a different D.C.-based battle will soon be coming to the video game console in your living room . <p> Imagine the steps of the Supreme Court overgrown with grass and vines . Imagine the dome of the Capitol Building fractured and billowing smoke . Or Metro stations broken down without a commuter in sight . While that might seem difficult to fathom , it 's the reality of a new apocalyptic video game that uses D.C. as its backdrop . <p> Gamers will fight their way through the near-future wasteland of Washington and its iconic landmarks in Tom Clancy 's The Division 2 , coming to PC , Xbox One and PS4 on March 15 . <p> A @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ players battling to bring order out of chaos following a deadly viral outbreak that left millions dead -- while also helping rebuild civilization and save the District . <p> Gamers will duke it out with enemies on the National Mall ; at the White House ; the Lincoln Memorial ; the Washington Monument . They will see combat Downtown , in Georgetown , in Chinatown and even at The Kennedy Center . <p> Production of The Division 2 required teams from Sweden-based developer Massive Entertainment and publisher Ubisoft to tour the capital and map it in order to provide players with the best representation of Washington possible -- even in ruination . <p> WTOP was invited to join those behind the game on one such tour through D.C. <p> The lawn of the White House is wild and overgrown in Tom Clancy 's The Division 2 . " <h> Recreating D.C. <p> Gerighty and the team at Massive Entertainment say they spared no effort in tackling the challenge of rebuilding Washington as accurately as possible . <p> " The size of the map we 've chosen is around 20 percent bigger than the midtown Manhattan that we did ( for The Division 1 ) . And we wanted to capture all of these icons , " he said . <p> " Transforming the city , and transforming the cities in New York and Washington -- that 's my favorite part of the game . It 's taking locations that have a purpose , that have an architecture , that have a function -- seeing what they would be used for in moments of crisis , " Gerighty explained . <p> " It 's the same data that a GPS company will use in your car 's GPS . But the data that you can get from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can get locations of trees , you can get locations of benches , you can get locations of super precise things , " Gerighty said . <p> And unlike the NYC of The Division 1 , the D.C. of The Division 2 is a 1:1 replica of the city , so precision is critical . <p> " We could find out the placement and age and type of every tree that 's been planted in Washington , " Massive lead environmental artist Chad Chatterton explained , speaking near the Washington Monument during the tour . <p> " So it was really about making choices as to ... we obviously ca n't represent everything as it is exactly , so we try and focus on those trees , like those beautiful pink trees -- not just the cherry blossoms , but the ones that you see outside of the museums , for example . Try and get a grasp of what feels and looks like Washington , D.C. , in the player 's hands .
