<h> Supervisor gives state of the town address <p> Story By : GLENN ROHRBACKER 3/14/2019 <p> In his annual State of the Town address , supervisor Ed Romaine touted the successes of 2018 and promised that a " united board " would continue to make the town a better place moving forward . He delivered remarks in a speech at town hall on Monday , March 11 . <p> In his opening remarks , councilman Dan Panico said the supervisor 's speech would be a story of the town , and of " hard work and real results . " Officials from Suffolk County were in attendance , including several legislators , the county clerk , union leaders , the town board , and town hall staff . <p> One of the top achievements Romaine cited in his speech was a balanced budget without using reserve funds . He also was proud of the AAA bond rating given to the town by S&amp;P last year , which financial advisors have estimated will save " millions " of taxpayer dollars . Estimates say the consolidation effort will save $61 million . <p> The supervisor took on development , saying the Brookhaven IDA closed on 15 projects in 2018 , and expects to close on 19 in 2019 .
