<h> Global food crops at risk from changing rainfall patterns <p> Climate change is likely to alter rainfall patterns in some of the world 's most important food crop growing areas over the next 20 years , a study has found . <p> By 2040 , up to 14% of land dedicated to wheat , maize , rice and soy will be permanently drier than in 1986-2005 while 31% will be wetter , according to the research . <p> A number of regions are already experiencing a climate re-set , with rainfall patterns different to what they were a generation ago , say scientists . <p> The rapid speed of the change means that many farmers are going to have to act quickly to adapt , they warn . <p> The four crops studied together account for about 40% of global calorie intake and are vital to feeding the world 's growing
