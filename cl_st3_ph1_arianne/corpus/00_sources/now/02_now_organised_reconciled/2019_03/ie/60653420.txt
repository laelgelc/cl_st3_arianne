<p> Two newly formed environmental groups will host a talk on small scale farming , climate change and biodiversity decline at a sustainable farming event at Cloughjordan Ecovillage this weekend . <p> Both Talamh Beo and Extinction Rebellion will hold a group conversation on the Saturday evening of the weekend-long Feeding Ourselves 2019event in Co Tipperary . <p> Talamh Beo , formed this month in Galway , is a grassroots farmers movement aimed at exploring how ecosystems and people can live alongside each other . <p> Speaking to The Irish Examiner last month , Talamh Beo 's Fergal Anderson said that as we are facing a " multitude of crises " from climate change to species extinction and need a " radical transition " away from intensive to smaller-scale farming . <p> Extinction Rebellion , a civil disobedience environmental movement founded in the UK last October , formed in response to the threat of biodiversity loss and climate breakdown . <p> The Irish branch of the grassroots group has started to take shape around the country .
