As a mum of a young family I know many people in the community who are a bit worried about climate change but do n't know all the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of how serious the climate situation is and how it 's going to impact on our lives in the not-too-distant future . ' <p> She said the Government is failing abysmally to do anything approaching meaningful action on the issue . ' Ireland 's inaction on this is shocking , and those in power should be ashamed , ' Susan said .
