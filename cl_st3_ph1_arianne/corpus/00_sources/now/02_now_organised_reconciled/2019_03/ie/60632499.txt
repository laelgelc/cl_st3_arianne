<h> Louth students to host school walkouts for climate change <p> Teens from schools across Louth will be taking part in school walk outs to protest against climate change tomorrow . <p> Pupils from Ardee Community School ( who organised the protest ) , the Grammar school , the Bush , St.Vincents and Colaiste Lu will gather at Market Square , Dundalk from 12pm . <p> The strike will see local teens stand in solidarity with youths from across the globe to call for Governments to act now on global warming . <p> The climate action group from Ardee Community School said : " As you may already be aware , on Friday 15th of March there will be school strikes taking place all over the globe in order to provoke action from the governments in the face of the threat of climate change . So far , these strikes have taken place in over 100 countries , concentrated in well over 270 major cities . <p> " The Irish strikes ( **27;3831;TOOLONG ) are due to take place in Dublin and Cork on March 15th , however , none have yet been planned in Louth .
