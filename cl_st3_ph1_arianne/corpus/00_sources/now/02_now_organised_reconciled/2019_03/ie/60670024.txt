<p> An Irish-led research project aims to harness the power of supercomputers to help vineyards across the globe improve grape quality and yields . <p> If successful , the project could lead to better-tasting and potentially cheaper wine . <p> ' Agriculture is a high-volume business often plagued by low operational efficiency ' -- DR STEVEN DAVY <p> The Climate Smart Predictive Models project led by the Telecommunications Software and Systems Group ( TSSG ) at Waterford Institute of Technology is just one of nine real-life case studies being monitored across the crop , fishing and livestock industries in 14 countries . <p> Sensors are being placed in vineyards in Spain , and the data they produce is being fed to a supercomputer that tracks the impact of climate change and sets off early warning alerts for frost and other potentially devastating weather events for vines . <h> Smart agriculture <p> Dr Steven Davy , TSSG .
