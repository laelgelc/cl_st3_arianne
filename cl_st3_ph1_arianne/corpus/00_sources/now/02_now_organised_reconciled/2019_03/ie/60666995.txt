<h> Fine Gael seeks to water down 2050 ambition in climate action report <p> March 24th , 2019 <p> Fine Gael amendments to the special climate action committee 's much-awaited report look to water down recommendations for more ambitious 2050 emissions targets . <p> Overall , the latest draft of the report seen by The Green News is widely ambitious as the committee recognises that the " window of opportunity " to reduce emissions and avoid severe climate impacts is " rapidly closing " . <p> The proposed legislation will " provide a new legal framework for tackling climate change " , the draft report states , and ensure a " continuous climate policy " that transcends short-term electoral cycles . <p> The proposed legislation would also set ambitious climate and renewables targets and require five-year carbon budgets across all departments . <p> Fine Gael also wants to remove a line in the committee 's draft report that calls for the new legislation to include a requirement that all new policies and measures are made " subject of a climate change impact assessment " . <p> The ruling party wants to alter this line to state that the " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ consider relevant policies " outside of the proposed legislation . <p> At present , the draft also calls for a " coherent all-of-Government approach " to deliver on the proposed climate action . Another Fine Gael amendment seeks to add a provision that this approach should be adopted " using a least cost pathway " . <p> Fine Gael was contacted by The Green News for comment on its proposed amendments but did not reply at the time of publication . <p> The amendments are set to be voted on in private session this Tuesday , with the committee to consider the report in public session on Wednesday afternoon .
