Photo : GCCM <h> LWF General Secretary speaks at international conference in the Vatican on ' Religions and the SDGs ' <p> ( LWI ) - The Lutheran World Federation ( LWF ) General Secretary , Rev. Dr Martin Junge addressed an international conference in the Vatican on Thursday , urging faith-based organizations to play a more incisive role in the implementation of the United Nations ' Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) . <p> In his presentation as part of an interfaith panel , Rev. Junge spoke about the UN Agenda for the year 2030 with its slogan ' Leave no one behind ' . Comparing it to the biblical parable of the shepherd who sets out to find the one lost sheep , he said this message " resonates well with the Christian community " as it reflects " our deeply-held faith convictions and practices . Churches need to be straight-forward in stressing that " a development paradigm that operates on the basis of exclusion ca n't be called development " , he said , adding : " We 'd rather call it for what is : exploitation " . <p> The Lutheran leader noted that while faith drives the Christian vision of inclusivity , the secular SDG Agenda must be shaped by the Human Rights framework which is under threat in many parts of the world today . If the obligations enshrined in that framework are not upheld , he said , the SDG Agenda could lead to the weakening of accountability to these obligations , thus continuing to leave " people and entire communities behind " . <p> In particular Rev. Junge pointed to gender inequality , which " if left unaddressed " , he said , " will always leave women behind : underpaid , less educated , stigmatized and altogether on an unequal footing when it comes to development " . <p> While he acknowledged that " gender discourse is often difficult " for faith based organizations , he @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ meeting , talking and listening attentively to women to illustrate God 's vision of leaving no one behind . <p> Rev.
