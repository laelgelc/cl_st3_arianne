<h> IFA TO HOLD FOUR EURO ELECTION MEETINGS <p> IFA will hold four open farmer meetings for the candidates running in the European elections in May . <p> IFA President Joe Healy said the meetings would be an opportunity for farmers to ask the candidates about their position on key farming issues . <p> Joe Healy said the European Parliament has an increasingly important role in making decisions at EU level &amp; the meetings will look at a range of topics , including the next CAP , retail regulation and climate change . <p> The IFA President encouraged farmers across the country to attend a meeting as it will allow them to hear what candidates are willing to do for farm families . <p> Ahead of the meetings , IFA will be launching its Election Manifesto on Wed , April 10th.
