<h> Teenage climate activist nominated for Nobel Peace Prize <p> A Swedish teenage activist who has become a prominent voice in campaigns against climate change has been nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize . <p> Three Norwegian politicians said they believe the massive movement Greta Thunberg has set in motion " is a very important peace contribution " . <p> The 16-year-old has encouraged students to skip school to join protests demanding faster action on climate change , a movement that has spread beyond Sweden to other European nations , including Britain.Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg holds a protest banner ( Daniel Reinhardt/dpa via AP ) <p> Norwegian politician Freddy Andre Oevstegaardtold said that " climate threats are perhaps one of the most important contributions to war and conflict " . <p> Any national politician can nominate somebody for the Nobel Peace Prize . <p> The Norwegian Nobel Committee does not publicly comment on nominations , which for 2019 had to be submitted by February 1 .
