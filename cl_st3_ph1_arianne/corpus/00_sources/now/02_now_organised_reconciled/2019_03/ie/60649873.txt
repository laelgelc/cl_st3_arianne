<h> Kildare children strike for action on climate change <p> A YOUNG girl from Kildare town lead a campaign for climate action in Newbridge on Friday . <p> 12-year-old Ena-Mai Carroll Patel , a pupil of Educate Together in Newbridge , spearheaded the campaign for action . She was joined by 40 others , adults and children , who gathered outside of Murphy 's in Newbridge before making their way to Fine Gael TD Martin Heydon 's office . <p> " Ena-Mai is an amazing young lady . Her mother , Tracie Carroll Patel , contacted me about the campaign , " said Deirdre Lane of Fridays for Future in Newbridge , a group of students , children , teachers and parents striking for climate action . <p> The Kildare children campaigning for action on climate change outside Deputy Martin Heydon 's office in Newbridge last Friday <p> " She thought it would just the two of them striking and did n't expect such a crowd . They are sick of the lack of action and the government 's inaction on climate change . <p> " The children said they wanted to see Martin Heydon and they decided to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ at the march in Dublin , " said Ms Lane . <p> The group also paid a visit to the jobs centre where they called for more green jobs in the future . <p> They called for fossil fuels to be left in the ground , for the government to declare a climate emergency , to make transitioning to a CO2-neutral Ireland socially fair , that all recommendations of the Citizens Assembly on climate change be implimented , stronger enforcements and regulations on corporations and that a Green New Deal be put in place . <p> Students took part in school strikes and demonstrations around the country on Friday in protest as part of a global movement ignited by 16-year-old Swedish studentGreta Thunberg , she started the Friday protests outside the Swedish parliament eight months ago .
