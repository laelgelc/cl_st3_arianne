<h> Sembang@WWF : A storytelling platform for students <p> A good story will captivate an audience , glue a reader 's gaze to the page and spark a real-world impact . <p> By sharing stories and insights , we open up a safe dimension for discussions to flow free and ideas to grow . <p> For issues relating to climate change , storytelling can act as an anchor that implements action against the destruction of our neglected rainforests , wild oceans and surrounding communities . <p> Understanding the importance of sharing , the national conservation trust , WWF-Malaysia , has set up a storytelling platform named Sembang@WWF . <p> A core part of the ESD Youth Programme , the Sembang sessions give students a chance to speak about various environmental issues and discuss what they mean to them . <p> With the aim to engage and transport the student minds to personal stories on poverty , haze , wildlife poaching , shark finning and many more topics , Sembang@WWF spreads awareness and allows young people to share their concerns . <p> Last year , the platform spoke about the plight of the Malayan Tiger .
