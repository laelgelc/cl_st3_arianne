<h> Firm imports 40 tonnes of honeybees through RAK airport <h> RAS AL KHAIMAH , 4 days ago <p> Al Najeh Honey , a leading honey producer in the Gulf region , recently brought 40 tonnes of live honeybees through the Ras Al Khaimah International Airport in the UAE . <p> On March 14 the honeybees landed in Ras Al Khaimah on an Egypt Air Cargo flight from Cairo , Egypt , for the first time in 2019 .
