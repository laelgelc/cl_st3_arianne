<h> Spring into action for national Spring Clean Campaign <h> Sign up to our weekly newsletter <p> Wicklow County Council is joining forces with An Taisce for the annual National Spring Clean campaign . Working together with communities throughout the county we can tackle the problem of litter . <p> Throughout April the National Spring Clean will encourage communities and local groups to take on the problem of litter in local areas in order to see the benefit of a litter free environment . National Spring Clean is operated by An Taisce in partnership with Wicklow County Council and supported by the Department of Communications , Climate Action and Environment . <p> Over the past 21 years , the National Spring Clean has been focused on the message of collective responsibility we share in tackling and preventing our national litter problem .
