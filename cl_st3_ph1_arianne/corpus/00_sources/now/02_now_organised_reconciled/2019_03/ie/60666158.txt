<h> KILDARE OPINION : Beyond Brexit -- survival requires sharing <h> Henry Bauress gives his views <p> I know everyone is probably very tired of Brexit chat now but there are many side elements to it which raise questions . <p> For me the big wonder has been the question as to whether there is a common cause between wealthy millionaire investors and the relatively poor , unemployed and deprived . <p> The first lot are getting wealthier and the second are getting poorer . In many respects the technological improvements that have made life better for all of us have not been shared out . <p> My fear is that the wealthy UK Brexiteers will remain in the European Union by setting up companies in EU countries and/or getting passports for EU countries . <p> Some have already done so . But the middle to lower financial orders in society -- most of us -- will suffer from the barriers put up . <p> For the sake of argument I will keep this a black and white issue even though it is not .
