<h> National review of land use called for in climate action report <p> The Oireachtas Joint Committee on Climate Action has called for a national review of land use to optimise planning and help tackle carbon emissions . <p> The committee made the recommendation in its climate action report , which was published yesterday ( Thursday , March 28 ) . <p> The document was adopted by majority vote by the committee with all but Sinn Fein and Solidarity People before Profit committee members backing the publication . <h> Review <p> The review called for would include adaptation co-benefits such as rewetting or forest regrowth to mitigate flooding risks in river catchments , according to the committee , drawing on a similar report published by the UK Climate Change Committee for inspiration . <p> Irish hedgerows are highlighted for their contribution to carbon sequestration , storage and climate adaptation , as well as wildlife , water quality and flood management . <p> The committee calls for further work to maximise these biodiversity and climate through a county-based hedgerow survey strategy . <p> In addition , under CAP and national funding , a range of payment schemes should be made @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Such schemes would incorporate : <p> Active maintenance of ecosystems including re-wetting of agricultural wetlands ; <p> Ensuring the eligibility of farmers for CAP payments on existing **31;2126;TOOLONG lands . <p> On the topic of forestry , the committee acknowledged that there are problems with Sitka spruce plantations , including impacts on biodiversity and water quality . <p> Advertisement <h> Forestry <p> The report cited the current study being conducted on the matter in Co .
