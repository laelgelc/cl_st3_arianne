<h> Secondary school students to strike to demand action against climate change <h> Students from secondary schools across the country are planning on striking next week over the Governments lack of action against climate change . <p> The Schools Strike for Climate Action will see students striking and marching in Dublin and Cork and there will also be strike events outside local authority offices as well as school gates in counties from Tralee to Donegal , Meath to Galway on March 15 . <p> This event was inspired by 16-year-old schoolgirl Greta Thunberg who first striked the Swedish parliament last August . <p> The issues they want the Government to deal with are : <p> Ensuring all fossil fuels are left in the ground and preventing any new fossil fuel infrastructure from being built <p> Ensuring Ireland uses 100% renewable electricity by 2030 <p> Declaring a climate emergency <p> Prioritising the protection of life on Earth <p> Transitioning to a CO2-neutral Ireland <p> Implementing all the recommendations of the Citizens ' Assembly on climate change <p> Stronger regulations on corporations that cause climate issues <h> Advertisement <p> Maisy Morgan-Sutton , 14 , a second-year student from Cork Educate Together @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " doing nothing " to deal with the issue .
