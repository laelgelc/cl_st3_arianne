<h> Positive vibes for tillage machinery , but not all benefit <p> The market for cultivation machinery showed a " clearly positive development " across Europe in 2018 . Sales of fertiliser spreaders and seed drills also increased ( compared with 2017 ) . <p> However , for sprayers and precision drills the European market was weaker in 2018 ( than in the previous year ) . <p> That 's according to a report from CEMA -- the European association that represents the agricultural machinery industry .
