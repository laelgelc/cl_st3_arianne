<h> Regulator to be quizzed over Killala plant <p> FINE Gael Senator Michelle Mulherin has succeeded in her efforts to have the energy regulator appear before the Oireachtas Committee on Communications , Climate Change and the Environment to answer questions on the Mayo Power plant in Killala . <p> " I have received confirmation from Hildegarde Naughton , chair of the committee , that the committee has acceded to my request to expedite this matter by inviting the regulator to the meeting on Tuesday next , April 2 , rather than waiting until that date to discuss whether or not the regulator should be invited , " she explained . <p> Senator Mulherin has warned that the viability of the ? 255 million renewable energy project in Killala is at risk following the decision of the energy regulator ( CRU ) to grant an 18% Certificate of High Efficiency to the plant rather than the 100% it had granted in 2012 . <p> " The kernel of the questions to be answered by the regulator is why has the certification changed so drastically for this renewable project now when it is materially the same as the project which was @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has been no change in regulation and where the technology proposed is still current by international standards ? <p> " Mayo Power is partially built and would be completed and operating today but for a financial difficulty in 2016 . <p> " Mayo Power is a very important project for north Mayo and is also of national importance as it will help Ireland achieve its targets on renewable energy . The regulator 's decision calls into question the viability of the plant . <p> " I am delighted the committee acceded to my request and we can now get down to the business of getting answers from the regulator next Tuesday . "
