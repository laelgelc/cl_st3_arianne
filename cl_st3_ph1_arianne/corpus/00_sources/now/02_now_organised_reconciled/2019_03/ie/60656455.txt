<h> INOVYN joins North West Hydrogen Alliance <p> INOVYN has joined the North West Hydrogen Alliance ( NWHA ) , an association aiming to put the North West of England at the forefront of the UK 's hydrogen journey . <p> The chemical company has been producing hydrogen at its Runcorn Site for more than one hundred years as a co-product of the chlor-alkali process . <p> Working with fellow NWHA member Cadent , and other project partners -- Atkins , BOC , Costain , Peel Environment , Shell and the University of Chester -- INOVYN recently received government funding to advance a possible 100MW power-to-gas energy storage facility at the Runcorn Site . <p> The project , dubbed Project Centurion , will explore how energy storage can produce low carbon hydrogen for heat , decarbonisation of industry and transport fuels as well as contributing towards energy security . <p> Richard Stevenson , INOVYN 's Storage Projects Manager , said , " Hydrogen energy could be transformational for the North West and INOVYN is delighted to be working alongside other Alliance members to drive this forward .
