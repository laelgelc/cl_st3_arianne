Trees improve air quality , provide shade that makes streets cooler in summer , increase biodiversity , provide food and shelter for wildlife , reduce local wind speeds and reduce the effects of flash flooding . Without trees , life on earth would be intolerable . As well the environmental benefits , there are social and economic reasons to plant trees . <p> Since its inception , over half a million trees have been planted throughout the country during Tree Week .
