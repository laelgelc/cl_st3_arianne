<h> IFA to hold four meetings to hear from European election candidates <h> CANDIDATES TO BE QUIZZED ON FARMING MATTERS <p> IFA will hold four open farmer meetings for the candidates running in the European elections in May . <p> IFA President Joe Healy said the meetings would be an opportunity for farmers to ask the candidates about their position on key farming issues . <p> Joe Healy said the European Parliament has an increasingly important role in making decisions at EU level &amp; the meetings will look at a range of topics , including the next CAP , retail regulation and climate change . <p> The IFA President encouraged farmers across the country to attend a meeting as it will allow them to hear what candidates are willing to do for farm families . <p> Ahead of the meetings , IFA will be launching its Election Manifesto on Wed , April 10th . <p> This website and its associated newspaper are full participating members of the Press Council of Ireland and supports the Office of the Press Ombudsman .
