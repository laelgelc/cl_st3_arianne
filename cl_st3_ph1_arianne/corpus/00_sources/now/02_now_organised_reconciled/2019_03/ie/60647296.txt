<h> Confronting the housing fiasco - Going Dutch may help ease crisis <p> Many European cities share common problems , but because of cultural differences , they respond differently . Some ban or limit cars and make some streets pedestrian-only . <p> Others shy away from such innovation , imagining it too risky . Some , in an effort to stop killing their citizens by air pollution , ban diesel vehicles . Others order fleets of new diesel buses . <p> Housing , especially housing within the reach of working families , is an issue for every city , especially capitals . Housing costs in London and Dublin are reshaping those cities and the communities who live in them , and in their hinterlands , but not always positively . <p> Dormitory towns and long , anti-family , stressful commutes have become the norm for very many people . <p> This , no matter how it is managed , undermines communities and especially the volunteerism that underpins so much that is good in Irish life .
