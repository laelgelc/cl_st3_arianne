<p> Country 's only private weather forecasting agency , Skymet on Thursday said that monsoon rains in India are expected to be below normal in 2019 and probability of El Nino is 50-60 percent . <p> In an interview to CNBC-TV18 , GP Sharma , president of metrology &amp; climate change , said , " El Nino probability is on the rise now . According to our earlier projection , it was reaching the peak in the month of December and early January and thereafter , the projections were likely to decline and it did decline also . Then from the last week of February and almost the entire March so far , it is on the rise . " <p> Speaking about delayed and deficient monsoon , Sharma said , " Normally El Nino is linked with the poor performance of the monsoon . Around 80 percent of the cases are like that and 60 percent go to droughts also . The only difference this time is this is an evolving El Nino . Normally , the evolving El Ninos are more punishing in terms of the monsoon than devolving ones . They could be a little soft @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ could lead to below normal monsoon . Since the El Nino conditions are there , they will remain till the onset of the monsoon . So the start is going to be a little slow .
