<h> ' Controversial hunter ' trains state forest staff <p> RANCHI : The state department of forest , environment and climate change is training its front line foresters in mitigating human-animal conflict and the art of sedating wild animals through tranquillizer darts . <p> Forty forest guards , 25 range officers and 15 divisional forest officers from the state were given a day-long training at Birsa Zoological Park in Ormanjhi in air-rifle shooting , sedatives and their use and safety measures to be undertaken to prevent human or animal skirmishes when the latter veers into human settlements . The training will continue till Wednesday . The department roped in Nawab Shafath Ali Khan for the training . Based in Hyderabad , Khan is a licenced hunter who brings down wild beasts at the request of various state governments . In November last year , Khan courted controversy over the elimination of Avni , a man-eating tigress operating in Maharashtra . <p> " Man-animal conflicts have become severe in Jharkhand . Foresters are feeling the heat of it and are spending sleepless nights , " Khan told TOI .
