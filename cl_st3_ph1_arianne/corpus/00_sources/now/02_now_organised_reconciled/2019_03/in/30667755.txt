Wash , pack and keep them in your car , and whenever you spot a patch of barren land while travelling , throw these seeds and watch the future grow . " If every person here did this , we could make Gurugram green again , beautiful again , clean again , " said the young environmentalist from Pathways School , addressing a group of students , parents and fellow green activists . <p> On Friday , over 600 students bunked school to gather at Wazirabad bundh -- location of a group of life-size art installations created from waste -- to take part in the Global School Strike for Climate Change .
