A new report by IQAir AirVisual has revealed that 15 of the 20 most polluted cities in the world are in India . This indicates that the air pollution in our cities has not come down over the years , despite many mitigative actions from local governments . In 2015 , the World Health Organization ( WHO ) had reported that 13 of the 20 most polluted cities were from India . <p> The fine particulate matters ( PM2.5 ) considered in this study is notorious for its ability to penetrate deep into lungs and bloodstream causing serious long-term health complications leading to premature death . The pollutants also cause respiratory disorders and cardiovascular problems such as heart attacks and strokes .
