<h> Ghana tackles floods , waste and pollution with Accra resilience plan <p> DAKAR , March 28 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Ghana plans to replace its polluting " trotro " buses and formalise waste collection in slums as part of a new plan to help its crowded capital withstand climate change and disasters . <p> The West African country on Thursday became the second in the region after Senegal to launch a plan to help its capital city , Accra , grow safely as part of the 100 Resilient Cities global network . <p> " We hope that this will go a long way to help other cities in Africa , " said Accra 's Chief Resilience Officer James Mensah , who created the plan with the city council in response to frequent floods , fires , building collapse and disease outbreaks . <p> Greater Accra , home to about 4 million people , is built on an eroding coast . More than half the population live in informal housing that coexists with skyscrapers in the beachfront city . <p> One of the city 's 27 initiatives aims to reduce flooding by improving waste management .
