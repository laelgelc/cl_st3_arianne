<p> NEW DELHI : Noting disastrous impact of plastic waste on human health and environment , more than 170 countries have jointly pledged to " significantly reduce single-use plastic products by 2030 " - a resolution for which was piloted by India at the UN Environment Assembly at Nairobi in Kenya . <p> The five-day assembly which concluded on Friday adopted scores of other resolutions including measures to address food waste , making efforts to deal with marine plastic litter and sustainable management of nitrogen . <p> The resolution on ' sustainable nitrogen management ' was also piloted by India with senior officials from the environment ministry representing it . The global nitrogen use efficiency is currently quite low , resulting in pollution by reactive nitrogen which threatens human health and contributes to climate change and stratospheric ozone depletion . <p> " We will address the damage to our ecosystems caused by the unsustainable use and disposal of plastic products , including by significantly reducing single-use plastic products by 2030 , and we will work with the private sector to find affordable and environmentally friendly products , " said countries ' representatives , including ministers , in a final @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environmental forum . <p> India had , on the occasion of the World Environment Day on June 5 last year , pledged to eliminate single-use plastic from the country by 2022 . <p> The single-use plastics , often referred to as disposable plastics ( use-and-throw items ) , are commonly used for packaging and include items intended to be used only once before they are thrown away or recycled . These include carry bags , food packaging , bottles , straws , containers , cups , cutlery and even campaign materials . <p> The assembly took note that only a small proportion of the plastics produced globally are recycled with most of it damaging the environment and aquatic bio-diversity . In India alone , over 10,000 tonnes of plastic waste remains uncollected every day , causing soil and water pollution , choking of drainage and river systems , littering marine ecosystem and ingestion by stray animals . <p> " We have decided to do things differently .
