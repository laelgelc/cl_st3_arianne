<h> After NGT rap , MCG draws up Bandhwari plan <p> GURUGRAM : To ensure that the Bandhwari landfill does not get bigger in the absence of a waste-to-energy plant ( WTP ) , the Municipal Corporation of Gurugram ( MCG ) plans to increase decentralised composting and bring down the percentage of waste by 50% . <p> The MCG has prepared a detailed action plan , which will be submitted to the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) on Friday . <p> On March 2 , the NGT had pulled up the MCG and its concessionaire for waste management in the city , Ecogreen Energy , over improper waste management in Bandhwari . On March 8 , it had asked them to submit plans to rectify the situation . <p> Gurugram generates 900 tonnes of municipal waste on a daily basis , which goes to Bandhwari . The proposed WTP , being developed as part of the integrated waste management plan at Bandhwari along Gurugram-Faridabad road , will have a capacity of processing up to 2,500 tonnes of waste per day .
