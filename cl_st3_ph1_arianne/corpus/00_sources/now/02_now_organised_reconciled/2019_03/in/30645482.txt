<h> Workshops , seminars mark Women 's Day in city <p> TNN Mar 9 , 2019 , 07:52 IST <p> Ludhiana : Various workshops and seminars marked the celebration of Women 's Day in the city on Friday . A workshop on the eve of International Women 's Day , titled ' Empowering rural women for addressing agricultural air pollution ' was organized by Icar-Atari Ludhiana , and International Maize and Wheat Improvement Centre ( Cimmyt ) , in collaboration with international agencies -- Borlaug Institute for South Asia ( Bisa ) , and International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics ( Icrisat ) -- under the aegis of CGIAR Programme on CCAFS ( Climate Change , Agriculture , and Food Security ) . World Food Laureate Dr GS Khush was the chief guest of the workshop . Dr Khush , in his address , thanked farm women who came from faraway places to exhibit their ventures and participate in the workshop . Further , he emphasized that balanced nutrition was essential for good health . <p> Dr Arbinda K Padee , director , Country Relations and Business Affairs , Icrisat New Delhi , expressed his happiness over the convergence among national and international institutes . He also discussed the two products -- ' healthy snack ' and ' smart breakfast ' , based on sorghum and millets developed by Icrisat Hyderabad , which are beneficial for health . He further said the institute was working towards preventing malnutrition and degradation of the environment . <p> Earlier , Icar-Atari director Dr Rajbir Singh briefed the house about various activities carried out by the Krishi Vigyan Kendras ( KVKs ) of Zone-I for the empowerment of farm women . He said a knowledge of healthy diet is very important for women , as they not only plan their own diet , but also for their families . He spoke about the institute 's efforts in reducing agricultural air pollution , as stubble burning in the northern parts of India was severely affecting the soil and human health .
