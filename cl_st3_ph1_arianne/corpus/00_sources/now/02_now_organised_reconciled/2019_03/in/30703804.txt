<h> State funding for Kochi Biennale is well worth it : Glenn D Lowry <p> Glenn D Lowry has been the director of the Museum of Modern Art ( MoMA ) in New York City for a quarter century . Ever since the Kochi-Muziris Biennale began in 2012 , Glenn , one of the most influential figures in the international art world , has never missed a single edition . Once again , he is in Kochi , even though it is as sweltering as an oven , to catch the latest edition before it winds to a close . Paying glowing tributes to the movement , he calls it one of the most important biennales of the world .
