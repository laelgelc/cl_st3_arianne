<h> Last Updated : Monday 25 March 2019 <p> Ilyas Khan , 50 , of Banban village in the Tijara block of Rajasthan 's Alwar is a worried man . He was one among thousands of villagers , who mined the nearby hills to eke out a living and prospered . But the sudden prosperity has come at a cost : there is a severe depletion of the groundwater table in the region . <p> " A decade ago , we used to get groundwater at a depth of 10-15 metres . These days , we have to bore up to 60 metres to get water for drinking and irrigation , " says Khan . <p> A study of Alwar 's groundwater by the Central Ground Water Board ( CGWB ) in 2010 also reveals that in a major part of the district , the depth of water varies from 10 to 40 metres below ground level . All blocks of district are in the " Over Exploited " zone . <p> Banban residents used to earn Rs 500 per day while working at mining sites for four years between 2008 to 2012 .
