( Reuters ) - As an uncontrollable wildfire turned the California town of Paradise to ash , air pollution researcher Keith Bein knew he had to act fast : Little is known about toxic chemicals released when a whole town burns and the wind would soon blow away evidence . <p> He drove the roughly 100 miles to Paradise , located in the Sierra Nevada foothills , from his laboratory at the University of California , Davis , only to be refused entrance under rules that allow first responders and journalists - but not public health researchers - to cross police lines . <p> It was the second time Bein says he was unable to gather post-wildfire research in a field so new public safety agencies have not yet developed procedures for allowing scientists into restricted areas . <p> Fires like the one that razed Paradise last November burn thousands of pounds of wiring , plastic pipes and building materials , leaving dangerous chemicals in the air , soil and water .
