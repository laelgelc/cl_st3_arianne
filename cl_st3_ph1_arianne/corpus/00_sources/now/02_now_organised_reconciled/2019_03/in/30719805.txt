<h> India , Bolivia call for finalisation of CCIT <p> Santa Cruz : In the wake of growing threats and acts of terrorism across the world , India and Bolivia have called for early finalization of Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism ( CCIT ) . <p> Briefing media on President Ram Nath Kovind 's visit to Bolivia , Vijay Thakur Singh , Secretary ( East ) Ministry of External Affairs ( MEA ) said : " Bolivian President Evo Morales strongly condemned Pulwama attack and denounced cross border terrorism . There was an assertion that terrorism in all its forms constitutes a threat to humanity . Bolivia-India called for early finalization of Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism . " <p> Thakur informed that the two leaders discussed bilateral as well as multilateral issues of interest . <p> She said that President Kovind and his Bolivian counterpart Morales focused on three issues in the bilateral arena- terrorism , United Nations Security Council reforms and Climate change . <p> Thakur further said that there was an acknowledgment that current membership of UNSC is not reflective of realities of the 21st century . <p> " Bolivian President Evo Morales expressed full understanding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . He extended support for India 's membership as a non-permanent member , " she stated . <p> Thakur further said that Morales has signed the International Solar Alliance Framework Agreement and Bolivia is now a member of ISA . <p> " On climate change- Both sides are emphatic that there needs to greater focus on the issue as well as looking at renewable sources of energy . In this context , President Morales signed the International Solar Alliance framework agreement , " Thakur said . She added that the two sides discussed bilateral issues including trade and economy . <p> Talking about trade , Thakur stated that the two nations are looking at diversification of export components of the trade and are exploring ways in which they can work together . <p> " Growing trade between the two nations stands at 875 million USD in the last calendar year . From point of view of Bolivia , India is the third largest market for India in terms of export and seventh largest trading partner , " she said . <p> Lithium is a resource that the South American nation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the help of India as it is trying to move towards industrialization of its mineral resource . <p> Thakur said , " As far as Lithium is concerned , there was a discussion on all the three aspects of Lithium including , help in the exploration of the mineral , assured supplies of Lithium Carbide to India and also the possibility of joint ventures of Lithium batteries production plant in India .
