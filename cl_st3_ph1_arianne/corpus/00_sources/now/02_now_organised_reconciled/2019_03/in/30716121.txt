<h> Meet Madhuri Sastry -- FII 's Featured Writer Of March 2019 <p> We have been featuring the best writers from our writers ' community for their committed contribution to FII , making it what it is today . FII would not exist if not for the passionate and loyal feminist writers ' community that we have steadily been building over the last three years . <p> Madhuri Sastry : I 'm a human rights lawyer turned writer ! I studied a lot , and have worked with refugees and in gender . Right now , I am writing a lot of essays and opinion pieces on feminism , pop culture , food , women 's human rights , identity and sexuality . <p> FII : How did you become a part of the FII writer family ? <p> MS : When the #MeTooIndia movement had a resurgence last October , I got involved in a lot of conversations on Twitter -- several of them with FII . One of these tweet-chats was about how men could be good allies during this time ( and forever , honestly ) . I came up with the idea of a listicle @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to help dismantle it . I wrote it and sent it to FII , who published it -- and the rest is history . 10 Things Men Can Do To Dismantle Patriarchy In The Time Of #MeToobecame one of the most read pieces last year ! <p> FII : How and when did you become a feminist ? Which issues within feminism are close to your heart ? <p> MS : I have been a feminist all my life . My parents were feminists and set fantastic examples for me . My mother , in particular , was so influential in teaching me to assert myself , and that my voice had value . The issues that are particularly important to me are women 's fundamental human rights , sexual freedom , war and militarism , toxic masculinity , body positivity , and the intersection between gender , race , and class . I also write about ageism , the portrayal of female/non-binary characters in pop culture , and the media 's role in perpetuating unhelpful narratives of gender . <p> FII : What is your favourite piece on this @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on this site that you have read ?
