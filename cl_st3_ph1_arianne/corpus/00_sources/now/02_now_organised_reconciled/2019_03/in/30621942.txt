Species included fish , crustaceans such as shrimp , and molluscs such as sea scallops . <p> They combined global data on fisheries with ocean temperature maps to estimate temperature-driven changes in the sustainable catch ( known as the maximum sustainable yield ) from 1930 to 2010 . <p> Their analysis covered about one third of the reported global catch , and losing species outweighed the winners as the oceans warmed . <p> The greatest losses were in the North Sea , Iberian Coastal , Kuroshio Current and Celtic-Biscay Shelf regions .
