<h> Info <p> The Importance of Trade - Stephen S. Poloz , the Governor of the Bank of Canada , speaks before the Baffin Regional Chamber of Commerce ( BRCC ) and the Nunavut Mining Symposium ( 15:10 ( Eastern Time ) approx . ) <p> The Bank of Canada today announced that it has joined the Central Banks ' and Supervisors ' Network for Greening the Financial System ( NGFS ) . The NGFS was established in December 2017 in Paris at the One Planet Summit to strengthen the global response to the Paris Agreement on climate change . Made up of more than 30 members , the NGFS defines and promotes best practices in climate risk management for the financial sector and conducts analytical work on green finance . <p> Governor Stephen S. Poloz said the Bank is committed to doing its part to better understand the implications of climate change for the economy and the financial system , and is proud to have been accepted as a member of the NGFS : <p> " The importance of climate-related issues for financial stability and monetary policy have become increasingly clear . This is particularly true for Canada @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and where the natural environment is a defining feature of our national identity . Joining this network is part of the Bank 's broader efforts to understand climate-related risks for the Canadian economy and financial system . " <p> Some highlights of the Bank 's broader climate-related workplan are <p> building climate-related risks into our financial system review process -- and highlighting that work on our Financial System Hub ; <p> continuing to green the Bank 's operations -- including pursuing Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design ( LEED ) certification , energy and waste reduction , and carbon disclosure ; and <p> developing a multi-year research plan focused on climate-related risks to the macroeconomy and financial system . <h> Notes for editors <p> The Bank 's 2016 Head Office renovation resulted in a 50 per cent reduction in annual energy use -- the equivalent of removing more than 1,300 homes from the electricity grid . For more information on the Bank 's head office , and its pursuit of LEED certification , visit the Bank 's website.
