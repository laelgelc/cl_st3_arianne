<p> Saskatoon had a monthly mean average of -24.2 C , more than nine degrees colder than normal . That made this past February the fourth coldest ever recorded in the city , in more than a century of record-keeping . <p> The province 's other major city , Regina , did n't fare much better , with a monthly mean average of -23.5 C , twelve degrees colder than normal . Its February was the fifth coldest recorded . <p> It was La Ronge 's coldest winter , in fact , in the 53 years since records were kept , with a monthly mean of -23.6 degrees . <p> That was eight degrees colder than normal . <p> Other Sask. communities that suffered through the unusually frosty conditions included : <p> Estevan with a monthly mean of -22.4 ( second coldest winter on record ) <p> Meadow Lake with a monthly mean of -25.6 ( second coldest winter on record ) <p> Prince Albert with a monthly mean of -24.6 ( fourth coldest winter on record ) <p> Swift Current with a monthly mean of -22.1 ( second coldest winter on record ) <p> The darkest blue colour @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lower than normal . That allowed cold Arctic air to spill southward into the Prairies . <p> This undulation in the jet stream dominated for much of the month , allowing day after day of extremely cold weather . <p> Saskatchewan was n't alone in the misery .
