<p> With a cruising speed of 45 knots ( 84 km/h ) the Sipu Muin can cover a lot of distance quickly . <p> It 's an all-terrain vehicle , hovering about one inch above whatever it 's gliding on -- earth , water or ice . <p> That means it can get into shallow waters and up into small rivers and lakes to break up fast ice -- the ice attached to the shore . <p> The Sipu Muin can be equipped with a polar crane to help with buoy maintenance .
