Glencore promised to refocus on metals such as cobalt , nickel , vanadium and zinc , key battery components for a lower-carbon world . <p> It looked like a coup for Climate Action 100+ , a heavyweight group of global institutional investors on a five-year mission to convince the planet 's largest emitters of greenhouse gases to take action on climate change . But is it really a win ? <p> As several industry watchers pointed out , the world 's largest coal producer will benefit most if future supply shortages drive coal prices up .
