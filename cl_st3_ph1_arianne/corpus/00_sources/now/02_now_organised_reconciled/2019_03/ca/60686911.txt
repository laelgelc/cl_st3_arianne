<h> ? <h> High Concentrations of particulate matter = Poor air quality <p> PRINCE GEORGE - An Dust Advisory for Prince George has been issued due to high concentrations of particulate matter . The Ministry of Environment and Climate Change Strategy in collaboration with Northern Health has issued the advisory just before nine this morning . Residents with chronic medical conditions should postpone strenuous exercise near busy roads until the advisory is lifted .
