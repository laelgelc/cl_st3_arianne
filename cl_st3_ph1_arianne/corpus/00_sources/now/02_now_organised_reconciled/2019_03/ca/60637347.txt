<h> Bruce forests part of carbon effort <p> Bruce County has entered a forestry partnership agreement with Bluenose Canada , a leading North American carbon offset developer , which will help evaluate options for the county and develop offsets on their behalf . <p> Warden Mitch Twolan said Bruce County is the first public sector forest owner in Ontario to respond to the global climate crisis in this way . <p> " And we are proud of our meaningful response to the international rally cry from the 2015 Paris Accord . " <p> Trees take in carbon dioxide from the air and the process of capturing it , helps to slow climate change . <p> The county has a long history of sustainable forest management . <p> In the early 1900s the county began purchasing privately owned land devastated by overharvesting or land clearing . These marginal lands were replanted mainly with evergreens and managed to promote a natural forest condition consisting of both hardwoods and conifers . <p> " Since the inception of the program and through proven sustainable management practices the county is the proud steward of 12,800 acres of vibrant forests , " Twolan said . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Bluesource , to develop the forest carbon offset opportunity , will demonstrate to other private and public land owners the value of meeting a high standard of sustainable forest management . <p> In a media release Jamie McKinnon , vice president of environmental solutions for Bluesource Canada , said , " We are thrilled by Bruce County 's leadership and we look forward to working with the county to realize the carbon offset opportunity . " <p> " We are also confident that the carbon market can provide many more land owners with a monetary incentive that meaningfully rewards land owner commitment to sustainable forest management . "
