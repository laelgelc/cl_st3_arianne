<h> Province launches climate change action plan <p> The provincial government would like to see more electric vehicles on the road . <p> It 's one of the goals of its plan to help battle climate change . <p> Dubbed , " The Way Forward on Climate Change , " the plan includes actions that will reduce greenhouse gas emissions from across the economy , stimulate clean innovation and growth , and build resilience to the effects of climate change . <p> Municipal Affairs and Environment Minister Graham Letto presented the plan at an ACAP Humber Arm South 's Coastal Matters information session Friday , explaining how Newfoundland and Labrador will address the shared challenge of climate change over the next five years . <p> It includes 33 actions to reduce provincial greenhouse gas emissions and 17 actions to build resilience to the impact of climate change . <p> The plan is part of the government 's " The Way Forward " initiative , which is aimed at strengthening the province 's economic foundation and delivering better outcomes for residents . <p> The Newfoundland and Labrador Environmental Industry Association ( NEIA ) commended the government for releasing the plan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the province is focusing on ... allows the private sector to respond in-kind , " NEIA executive director Kieran Hanley said . <p> He said NEIA members were extremely engaged in the development of the climate change action plan , pointing out that the organization launched an industry engagement campaign in 2016 , which offered recommendations to the government . <p> " We know that years of hard work by our public sector has gone into this plan , " Hanley said . <p> " NEIA looks forward to studying the result and is eagerly anticipating more information on programming available to help businesses thrive in the lower-carbon economy .
