<h> Opinion : The power of one <p> By Sue Tiffin <p> Published March 26 , 2019 <p> When you already feel despair about the current trajectory of the plight of the earth due to human 's impact , when you 've read the research on altered agricultural patterns and rapid acceleration of species loss , when you 've seen the images of the impact of severe weather in the form of droughts and wildfires around the world , when you are faced with yet another discouraging headline highlighting today 's new record amount of plastic found in a whale 's stomach , the idea that you might make a difference feels hopeless . <p> What does it matter if you remembered your reusable grocery bags when every bit of fresh produce you buy is enclosed in plastic packaging ? If you diligently bring your reusable container for coffee , only to have the barista use a disposable cup to pour the coffee from the machine into your mug ? If you go plant-based when you 're the only person in the room who chooses that meal ?
