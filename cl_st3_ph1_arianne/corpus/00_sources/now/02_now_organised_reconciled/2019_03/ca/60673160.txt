<h> Big U-turn : Key melting Greenland glacier is growing again <p> WASHINGTON -- A major Greenland glacier that was one of the fastest shrinking ice and snow masses on Earth is growing again , a new NASA study finds . <p> The Jakobshavn ( YA-cob-shawv-en ) glacier around 2012 was retreating about 1.8 miles ( 3 kilometres ) and thinning nearly 130 feet ( almost 40 metres ) annually . But it started growing again at about the same rate in the past two years , according to a study in Monday 's Nature Geoscience . Study authors and outside scientists think this is temporary . <p> " That was kind of a surprise . We kind of got used to a runaway system , " said Geological Survey of Denmark and Greenland ice and climate scientist Jason Box . " The good news is that it 's a reminder that it 's not necessarily going that fast . But it is going .
