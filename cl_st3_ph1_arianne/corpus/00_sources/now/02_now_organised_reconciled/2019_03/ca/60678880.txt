<h> You are here <h> Federal budget tinkers around the edges of food and agriculture <p> The 2019 federal budget was handed down on March 19 , and the media frenzy has come and gone . Time to reflect on what might hit the mark in terms of agricultural issues . <p> After careful review , I have noted an attempt to include food and some agricultural issues into the mix . That makes me happy , maybe even hopeful . At least I can find mentions of food when I search , and also a few mentions of farming and agriculture . <p> But , I use the word " attempt " because , while the budget purports to " introduce " a national food strategy , the 2019 federal budget is barely a first step , with a mix of food and agriculture initiatives that are largely unconnected . <p> For a solid national food strategy , you need a solid agricultural strategy <p> Since their election in 2015 , the federal Liberals have been promising a national food policy , and while there are notes of support for food issues and some farm issues peppered throughout @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ detail , and even less depth . This budget also includes measures to encourage the reduction of food waste , as well as a " buy Canadian " food program . <p> And there is $950 million in money for innovation in five industry-led " super clusters " or groupings , one of which is food production .
