<h> Grimes Announces ' MissAnthropocene ' LP <p> Grimes has been talking about her new album(s) for a while now , and her LP finally has a title . The Canadian pop experimentalist has revealed that the album will be called MissAnthropocene , and she has shared more details about the forthcoming collection . <p> In an Instagram post , Grimes revealed that MissAnthropocene is a play on the words misanthrope ( someone who dislikes humanity ) and Anthropocene ( the current geological time period during which humans have begun having an impact on the environment ) . <p> She wrote , " It 's a concept album about the anthropomorphic Goddess of climate Change : A psychedelic , space-dwelling demon/ beauty-Queen who relishes the end of the world . She 's composed of Ivory and Oil . " <p> The recent AI-themed single " We Appreciate Power " will be the opening track on the album . According to Grimes , " Each song will be a different embodiment of human extinction as depicted through a Pop star Demonology . " She described the album as " ethereal nu metal ( ish ) .
