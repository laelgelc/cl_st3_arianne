<h> ' A very good day for Quebec ' : Finance minister sports new sneakers ahead of budget <p> Thursday 's provincial budget will leave Quebecers with a spring in their step , according to Finance Minister Eric Girard , who donned a new pair of sneakers for the occasion . <p> " I 'll have some very good news . We 're working on some structural movement for the economy , improving its services , " said Girard at a pre-budget photo op Wednesday . " Tomorrow is going to be a very good day for Quebec . " <p> The CAQ will table its first budget , and to mark the occasion , Girard made a twist on the finance minister 's tradition of buying a new pair of shoes . <p> Picking a pair of running shoes in CAQ blue from a local shoe company , Girard said he chose sneakers because Quebec has to pick up the pace to close the gap with Ontario when it comes to productivity and wealth . <p> " Sports is part of the discipline that helps me work hard , and the small businesses are very important to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ small business from Quebec City , so that was an obvious choice , " said Girard of the running shoes . <p> Liberal finance critic Carlos Leitao had words of warning ahead of the budget . <p> " One needs to be careful and remain very prudent because the economic situation is very good now , but who knows down the road , " he said .
