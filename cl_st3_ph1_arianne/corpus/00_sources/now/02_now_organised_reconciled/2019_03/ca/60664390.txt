" <h> ' It takes some resiliency ' <p> While the effects of Idai are certain to last for months , Carr said Gorongosa National Park hopes to develop a long-term plan that would help protect areas surrounding the park from the worsening weather caused by climate change . <p> Beira , in particular , sits partially below sea level and is susceptible to flooding . The natural path for rain that falls on Gorongosa is through the city to the Indian Ocean . <p> Carr believes the development of a wetland corridor could help funnel that water away from areas that people live and into the ocean . <p> " It really is rethinking our relationship to the natural world .
