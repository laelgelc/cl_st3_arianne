( Submitted by Kyla Bruff . ) <p> It 's hard to look at the future of the North Atlantic without acknowledging the dramatic impact climate change will have on the ecosystem , says one of the organizers of a weekend conference on the issue . <p> " From ocean acidification , to the loss of plankton , to the migration of fish to colder waters , to the loss of ice coverage in Labrador ... there is no aspect of the North Atlantic ecosystem that is not currently being affected by climate change , " says Sean McGrath . <p> " And if trends continue , the effects will be far far more devastating for us in the future .
