<h> Tropical anomaly Storm Iba swirls off Brazilian coast <p> A rare South Atlantic tropical storm -- the first to form in that ocean since 2010 -- continues to swirl off the coast of Brazil , bringing strong winds and rough seas . <p> As of Monday morning , the storm boasted maximum gusts of 87 km/h , stronger than the previous day , as it churned the seas off the coasts of the Brazilian states of Bahia and Espirito Santo .
