<h> Time to focus on climate change <p> I agree with Geoffrey Stevens -- end the histrionics about SNC-Lavalin . However , I disagree that Canada lacks genuine trauma . We have a very real crisis facing Canadians and the world : climate change . <p> If the media , politicians , business , education , community services leaders and citizens focus their energies and outrage on addressing the climate crisis , we could make a significant impact . <p> Scientists have shown we have 12 years left to limit -- not solve , but limit -- the climate crisis . There are many steps to take immediately : eat less meat , walk , cycle , use transit more and demand action from elected officials . <p> Instead , the provincial government has ended our cap-and-trade program , removed electric vehicle charging stations and is wasting significant financial resources and time fighting the federal government 's carbon tax . This is nonsense . <p> This ongoing outrage about SNC-Lavalin is the very definition of ' fiddling while Rome burns . ' Canada does have trauma and a crisis .
