<h> Municipal , First Nation members encouraged to participate in regional energy and emissions project <p> MINDEMOYA -- The Smart Green Communities program affiliated with reThink Green has launched a new collaborative project to build energy and climate change leadership in communities on Manitoulin and the North Shore of Lake Huron . The project , as outlined by Barbara Erskine , Manitoulin Energy Plan Coordinator , aims to consider actions for adaptive asset management ; build regional networks for collaboration ; enhance local staff and council knowledge of climate change-and how to address impacts using a sustainability planning approach ; develop strategies to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from community buildings and activities ; prepare communities to meet changing energy demands and scenarios and explore economic development opportunities related to clean energy and other innovative technologies . " The ultimate goal is to have a regional plan with a Manitoulin group of First Nations and municipalities and the second being a North Shore group , " Ms. Erskine told the meeting . She pointed out support and guidance will be provided by reThink Green .
