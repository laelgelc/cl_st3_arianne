<p> Hundreds of high school students rally at Province House on Friday , March 15 , to urge government to act on climate change . - Francis Campbell <p> This month , climate change made the local news . Twice . <p> On March 12 , the government introduced legislation to protect the province 's coastlines from the effects of rising sea levels caused by climate change , and on March 15 , Nova Scotian high school students participated with others around the world in the global climate strike . <p> Both events are tacit acknowledgements of the fact that over the past 40 years of climate meetings , despite agreements and protocols requiring countries to mitigate the effects of climate change , global emissions have continued to rise . Between 1990 and 2017 , they increased by almost -two-thirds . <p> To be fair , there has been some mitigation due to improvements in energy efficiency . <p> The traditional , post-Second World War manufacturing economies built around heavy industries , with very strong coupling of energy consumption and economic growth , are in decline .
