We would miss these trees in many ways . <p> We would miss how trees moderate the extremes of climate . In summer , their shade cools parks and walking paths and can help cool our homes and yards if strategically planted . In winter , branches and trunks act as windbreaks . <p> People who live near rivers or in flood zones would miss the way trees lessen erosion and hold soil in place . <p> We would miss how trees help muffle the sounds of the city . Would you rather listen to the rustle of breeze blowing through leaves , or traffic noises from several blocks away ? <p> It 's likely no one would miss the forest tent caterpillars that drop from trees onto human heads and clothing .
