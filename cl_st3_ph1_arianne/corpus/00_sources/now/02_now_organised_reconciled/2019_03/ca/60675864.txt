<h> U of T students win prestigious prize from Architect magazine <p> A group of architecture and landscape architecture graduate students who reimagined new ways that Florida 's Broward County could deal with rising water levels and climate change-related flooding have been honoured by Architect magazine for their innovative designs . <p> The Coding Flux studio of the John H. Daniels Faculty of Architecture , Landscape , and Design was recognized on March 21 as one of six winners of the 2018 Studio Prize , from more than 100 submissions by architectural schools across North America . The Studio Prize honours thoughtful , innovative and ethical university studio courses . <p> The studio 's prize was one of two awards sponsored by Sloan , which makes commercial restroom products . Sloan Awards recognize investigations of sustainable water conservation and management ideas . <p> " It really puts in perspective that the work that is being done here is at the forefront of innovation , " said Assistant Professor Elise Shelley , who with Assistant Professor Fadi Masoud were the studio instructors involved . <p> While other schools examined a range of issues spanning locations from Switzerland to San Francisco @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ led to an investigation of the flood-prone region by the Coding Flux studio 's 19 graduate students . <p> A recent report underscored the growing threat Florida 's east coast cities face from climate change , with the Atlantic Ocean on one side and the swampy Everglades on the other . The study showed sea water levels in the region are rising six times faster than average . <p> Working closely with county planners , the students developed a geographic information system ( GIS ) map of the region to understand which areas are most susceptible to rising sea levels , studied the limitations of existing zoning codes , and then designed proposed solutions . <p> Those solutions ranged from flux zoning and hybrid street and canal systems -- called " via networks " -- to freshwater credits that encourage rainwater harvesting and sustainable development ideas for areas abutting the Everglades . <p> " One of the benefits of working with a forward-thinking county like Broward that does n't have many resources is that the studio helps them think about innovative ideas that they otherwise would n't have the time @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ They are very much interested in learning from these students and finding ways to implement at least parts of these ideas and proposed solutions .
