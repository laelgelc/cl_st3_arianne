<p> CALGARY , March 8 , 2019 /CNW/ - TransAlta Corporation ( " TransAlta " or the " Company " ) ( TSX : TA ) ( NYSE : TAC ) today announced that the Alberta Electric System Operator ( " AESO " ) granted the extension of the mothballing for the below Sundance units : <p> Sundance Unit 3 will remain mothballed until November 1 , 2021 , extended from the previous date of April 1 , 2020 ; and <p> Sundance Unit 5 will remain mothballed until November 1 , 2021 , extended from the previous date of April 1 , 2020 . <p> The extensions were requested by TransAlta based on TransAlta 's assessment of market prices and market conditions .
