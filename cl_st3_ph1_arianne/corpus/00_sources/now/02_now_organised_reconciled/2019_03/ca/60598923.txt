<h> Washington Gov . Inslee joins Democratic presidential field <p> SEATTLE -- Washington Gov . Jay Inslee will seek the 2020 Democratic presidential nomination , mixing calls for combating climate change and highlights of his liberal record with an aggressive critique of President Donald Trump . <p> The 68-year-old governor is launching his bid Friday in Seattle , following recent visits to the first primary state of New Hampshire and the early caucus state of Nevada . <p> " We went to the moon and created technologies that have changed the world -- our country 's next mission must be to rise up to the most urgent challenge of our time : defeating climate change , " Inslee says in a video announcement ahead of a public announcement later Friday in Seattle . <p> Inslee is the first governor to join a Democratic primary that has been dominated by senators .
