How many appear on the sun 's surface depends on what cycle the sun is in . Every 11 years our star goes through a maximum , followed by a minimum ( the entire magnetic cycle of the sun , when the poles flip , is 22 years ) . <p> This graph shows the decrease in solar activity over the past 33 years . " The next cycle looks like it 's going to be very much like this one .
