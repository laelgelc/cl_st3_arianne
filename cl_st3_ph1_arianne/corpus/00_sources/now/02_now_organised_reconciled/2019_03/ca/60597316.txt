<p> MONTREAL , March 1 , 2019 /CNW Telbec/ - The Inlandsis Fund is proud to announce the appointment of David Heurtel as a member of its Board of Directors . According to Louis Roquet , Chair of the Inlandsis Board of Directors , " By joining the Fund 's Board , David Heurtel will be able to share his vast experience in the transition of the energy sector . I am confident that he will provide a point of view and insight that will contribute to the Inlandsis Fund 's positioning and growth .
