It is an incredibly important asset that we urgently rely on . <p> This golden piece of the Greenbelt is found out on a side road in Aurora . ( Warren Martil ) <p> At over 2 million acres , the Greenbelt is a uniquely Ontarian solution to some of the world 's biggest challenges -- food insecurity , water protection , rural economic prosperity , growth management , biodiversity loss , and , of course , climate change . Our shared commitment to protecting farmland , forests and wetlands in Canada 's most densely populated region is a source of pride and inspiration . <p> The past year has been an important one for the Greenbelt , after communities and residents @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and actions , that a permanently protected Greenbelt is integral to the health and prosperity of the region . Indeed , at $9.1 billion a year in economic activity and $3.2 billion in ecosystem services , the Greenbelt is both an economic and environmental asset in Ontario . The Greenbelt encompasses 28 municipalities and supports 161,000 jobs as a result of its unique protected status . <p> At the Greenbelt Foundation , we have spent the past 14 years investing in the Greenbelt 's success . From working with the agricultural community to improve soil health and increase access to local food , to partnering with the Waterfront Regeneration Trust to develop the Greenbelt Cycle Route , the Greenbelt Foundation has elevated the Greenbelt from a land use plan to a dynamic , working landscape . <p> Article Continued Below <p> As much as the Greenbelt gives us , we see a significant opportunity to use the natural advantages of the Greenbelt to do even more to make our region resilient to the effects of climate change . By working closely with municipalities , conservation authorities , and the farm community , the Greenbelt Foundation is investing in improving the resilience of the Greenbelt 's natural systems to increase other protections they provide . <p> And there 's another reason to protect this valuable asset . <p> As hard as it is to believe , despite its proximity to major urban centres , the Greenbelt is the most bio diverse region in Canada . Perhaps a little easier to believe -- it 's also the region where biodiversity is most at risk due to sprawl and population growth .
