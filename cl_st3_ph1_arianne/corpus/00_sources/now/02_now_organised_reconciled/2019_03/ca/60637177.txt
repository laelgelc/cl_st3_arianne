<p> About 50 New Westminster Secondary students marched out of class Friday as part of a global student strike demanding action on climate change . <p> The worldwide demonstration was inspired by 16-year-old Swedish student and climate activist Greta Thunberg who , in August 2018 , was thrust into the spotlight when she organized the first student strike against climate change in front of the Swedish Parliament Buildings . <p> Thunberg was invited to speak at the UN Climate Talks in Poland in December and at the World Economic Forum in Davos in January . Last week she was nominated for this year 's Nobel Peace Prize . <p> " She inspired an international movement and I wanted our school to take part in that , " said Karter Deedman , organizer of the New Westminster Secondary School student strike . <p> Deedman is in Grade 10 at NWSS and was one of about 50 students who left school Friday morning to march in protest against climate change . <p> " Climate change is a problem that everyone is facing , and we really need to make sure everyone in our city and in our country and all over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ together to solve it , " she said . <p> Students marched around the high school chanting " Change the system ! Fight the system ! Fix the system ! " while passing drivers honked in support .
