<p> Labor candidate for O'Connor Shelley Payne has reaffirmed her position on the jetty issue , stating she would continue to advocate to save the 85-year-old structure . The Esperance Shire councillor said she would always stand up for heritage . " While there is still a group fighting to save the jetty I will be giving them my support , " Mrs Payne said . " I 'll also be fighting for more of our fair share of funding for road and rail infrastructure to help improve road safety for all of us living in the regions . " When the Esperance Express asked Mrs Payne if she supported the $4 million of Building Better Regions Funding the shire had received for the jetty , Mrs Payne said Labor would " not rescind on any current commitments " . The candidate said she would take leave from council during the campaign to avoid any potential conflict of interest . " If elected I would resign .
