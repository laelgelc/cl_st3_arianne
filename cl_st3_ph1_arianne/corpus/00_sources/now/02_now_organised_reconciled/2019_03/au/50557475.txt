One Nation this week named Stuart Bonds , who fears scores of jobs are under threat from Labor 's anti-coal stance , at its local representative . Mr Fitzgibbon has held the seat since 1996 . But , Mr Bonds said he was " stepping up " to be a sensible and unwavering voice for countless Australians who feel their issues and circumstances are being ignored by major political parties .
