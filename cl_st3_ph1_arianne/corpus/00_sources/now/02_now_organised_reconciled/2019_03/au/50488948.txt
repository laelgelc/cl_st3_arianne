<p> Five of the six candidates expected to contest the seat of Upper Hunter in this month 's state election attended a forum in Singleton on Monday night ( March 4 ) where the discussion centred on mining and climate change . Views on these subjects ranged from true believers to absolute deniers with a passing mention to Christian based environment protection and the need to maintain economic growth in the region . Each of the candidates was given 10 minutes to make their pitch for a vote from the 60 attendees . The one absentee on the night was the sitting member and endorsed National Party candidate Michael Johnsen who told the organisers , Hunter Environment Lobby , he could not rearrange his diary to make the event . Given the seat is classed as marginal his non-appearance had attendees questioning that decision . Final nominations for the seat have closed and the ballot paper draw will be held in Singleton on Thursday morning ( March 7 ) . The candidates who did attend the event were : Greens - Tony Lonergan , Shooters , Fishers and Farmers - Lee Watts , Christian Democrats - Richard Stretton , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Dagg . On the question of climate change all the candidates , with the exception of Mr Ellis , accepted the science that climate was changing and it was having an impact on the environment . Mr Ellis argued in fact the earth was cooling not heating and weather patterns always swing from heating to cooling - a naturally occurring variation . This particular argument drew strong criticism from Mr Lonergan who said our community was sleep walking towards a catastrophe and the science was settled a long time ago that climate change exists and temperatures were rising . He wants to see 100 per cent renewable power generation by 2030 and mining royalties used to fund a transition out of coal . Ms Watts said the Upper Hunter had been ignored by both major parties . " We have towns in the Upper Hunter running out of drinking water because the money that should be spent on infrastructure is being spent on Sydney stadiums , " she said .
