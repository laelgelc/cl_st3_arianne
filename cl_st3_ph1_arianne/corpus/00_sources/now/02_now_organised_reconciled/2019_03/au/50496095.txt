Symposium : Cities might be the engine of climate change but they might also offer its salvation . Local and international experts including Daan Roosegaarde , Joost Bakker and Ross Harding outline how to achieve a sustainable city . <p> NGV International Great Hall , March 19 <p> Craig Reucassel hosts the Victorian Design Challenge . <p> Victorian Design Challenge : Join the ABC War on Waste 's Craig Reucassel as he hosts a live design pitch to determine which designer presents the best solution to reduce , recover or eliminate waste . <p> Advertisement <p> NGV International Great Hall , March 19 <p> Supply chain : A lamp made from a recalled car airbag and furniture made from building site waste challenge the typical design material . Fifteen designers including Jonathan Ben-Tovim and Dale Hardiman question the lifecycle of things we consume . Where do the materials of design come from ? What are the ethical dimensions of long and short supply chains ? @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Waterfront : Open House Melbourne hosts several Yarra river tours -- including River Swim , River ( Re ) Wild and Forgotten Ecologies of Birrrarung -- that offer insights into the indigenous history of Birrarung and the future design aspirations for it , including swimmable waters . <p> Welcome to Wasteland : Using everyday waste -- lettuce leaves , blood and coffee beans , to name three source materials in the show -- designers demonstrate how waste can be an opportunity in a time of crisis . <p> Compound Interest , 15-25 Keele St , Collingwood <p> Film program : Experience awe , despair and inspiration in a documentary film program that includes the epic Homo Sapiens , and Watermark by acclaimed photographer Edward Burtynsky.
