<h> FastForward Sydney unveils 2019 keynotes and speakers <p> Music industry conference FastForward returns to Sydney in April for a second year and has just announced its keynotes and speakers . <p> Tania Yuki , CEO of US social listening platform Shareablee , will present a keynote on " standing from the crowd " and Heidi Lenffer , lead vocalist with Cloud Control on " transforming touring ; in response to climate change . " <p> Lenffer said , " I 'm proud to be part of the movement of artists shifting gears on climate action . <p> " I 've been working on a serious upgrade for touring practice that meets the challenges of this current era head-on , and I 'm excited to present it at future-oriented conference FastForward . " <p> Overall the event will host 40 speakers including 4 keynotes , 7 panels and 11 FastFifteen sessions on a wide range of topics .
