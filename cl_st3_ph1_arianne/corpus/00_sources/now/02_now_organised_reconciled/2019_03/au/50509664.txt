Over the course of his 18-year career , the designer 's presentations for both his eponymous label and Givenchy , where he served as creative director from 1996 to 2001 , were the stuff of legend : 10 minutes of pure theatre that enthralled audiences with their audacity and conceptual brilliance . <p> The recipient of four British Designer of the Year awards , as well as a CBE , and now heralded as a genius , it 's easy to forget that McQueen 's creations were divisive from the offset . For " Dante " in 1996 , a skeleton was seated front row , while the 1997 show " It 's a Jungle Out There " saw a car catching fire , albeit accidentally . <p> McQueen 's shock tactics were n't always a hit with his sponsors . The designer 's spring/summer 1998 show , partly financed by American Express , was originally titled " The Golden Shower " before the company demanded the name be changed . McQueen conceded , calling it " Untitled " , but that did n't stop him sending models down a runway made of water-filled tanks while rain poured from above and spotlights bathed them in a suggestive yellow glow .
