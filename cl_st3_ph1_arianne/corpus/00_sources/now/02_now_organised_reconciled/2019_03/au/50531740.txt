<p> Australians eat around 400,000 tonnes of seafood every year -- but how much is sustainable ? <p> ABC News : Andrew O'Connor <p> Australians love seafood . We eat about 400,000 tonnes of it every year . <p> And we have one of the better-managed fisheries in the world . <p> But about 70 per cent of the seafood we eat comes from overseas where issues like overfishing and even slave labour can be present in the supply chain . <p> And Australian-grown seafood is n't completely without its issues : we still have tarnished species like orange roughy on our menus , and some fisheries use controversial methods like longlining and bottom trawling , which can damage habitat and kill things like turtles and other protected species . <p> So if we want to enjoy seafood sustainably , are there species we should favour , others we should avoid , and how can we find out where our food comes from ?
