New rules announced on Thursday will force new and expanding projects in the mining and oil industry to meet more stringent emissions targets and greater scrutiny over the processes involved . <p> Meanwhile , larger projects with emissions in excess of 100,000 tons of carbon dioxide will also need to offset emissions . EPA chairman Tom Hatton said the new requirements will ensure the region helps rather than hinders Australia 's quest to meet targets set out under the Paris agreement . <p> Dr Hatton added : " Our emissions in this state have gone up significantly .
