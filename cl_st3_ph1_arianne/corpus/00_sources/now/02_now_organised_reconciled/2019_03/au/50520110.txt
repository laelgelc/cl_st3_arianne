<h> New Energy Retail Protections For Victims Of Family Violence <p> Comments Off on New Energy Retail Protections For Victims Of Family Violence <p> Minister for Prevention of Family Violence <p> Energy retailers will be required to recognise family violence as a form of financial difficulty and consider this before pursuing debt , with an updated retail code planned to protect the rights of affected customers . <p> Minister for Prevention of Family Violence Gabrielle Williams welcomed the proposed new Energy Retail Code , which was a recommendation of the Andrews Labor Government 's Royal Commission into Family Violence . <p> The Essential Services Commission has released the draft code after the Royal Commission found that essential services could be used to coerce other people -- by putting a service into a victim 's name without their knowledge or consent , or obtaining contact details of a joint account holder from a provider . <p> The proposed Code would require retailers to have a family violence policy and new standards of conduct to boost protections , with better training for staff and improved account security and debt management practices . <p> Feedback from energy retailers and family violence specialists @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ create meaningful and long-term change with these new measures . <p> Family violence can have a significant financial toll on victim-survivors , especially where the perpetrator has used access to money and debts to exert control . These changes will offer greater protection and awareness . <p> The updated code is set to take effect from 1 January 2020 and will reflect similar changes to the water service code made in 2018 , to protect customers experiencing family violence . <p> The Andrews Labor Government commissioned a world-first Royal Commission into Family Violence and then accepted all 227 of its recommendations . <p> Stakeholders can comment on the draft energy code at **34;2475;TOOLONG , until 16 April . <p> As noted by Minister for Prevention of Family Violence Gabrielle Williams <p> " Family violence is a scourge on society and our number one law and order issue . This move is one of many underway to ensure women and children are protected .
