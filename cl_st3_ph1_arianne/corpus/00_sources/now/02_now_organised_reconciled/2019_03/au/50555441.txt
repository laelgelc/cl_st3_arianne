<p> Wiebe Wakker is mad about sustainability and the environment , and set out to prove to the world electric cars can do everything ordinary cars do . The 32-year-old from the Netherlands has spent the last three years driving through 33 countries in his car , funded by the generosity of others . In total he gathered around $30,000 for the journey , including costs to ship the car between continents . Mr Wakker has been in Wollongong for several days before his journey ends in Sydney on April 7 . " I 've tried to show the world that the possibilities and advantages of electric mobility , " he told the Mercury .
