Corals receive most of their nutrition from the algae , so without them they starve and often die , turning white -- coral bleaching . <p> Climate change also means that oceans absorb more carbon dioxide from the atmosphere , which makes seawater more acidic . This can be between different species or between distantly related populations of the same species . <p> " We chose to focus on Acropora corals because they are among the most important corals responsible for building the immense substructure that supports the Great Barrier Reef .
