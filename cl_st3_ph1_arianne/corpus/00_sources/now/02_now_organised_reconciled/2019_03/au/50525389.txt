<p> The Bathurst Showground will be hosting an event this weekend aimed at raising public awareness of waste management and renewable strategies . Running in conjunction with the Bathurst Farmers Markets , the Bathurst Sustainable Living Expo will introduce members of the community to the variety of local groups promoting environmental awareness . " Environmental awareness is important for all residents as it is in their power to make a positive contribution to Bathurst 's clean and green image , " Bathurst mayor Graeme Hanger said . " Nearly all of Bathurst 's community environmental groups will be at the expo conveying a diverse range of messages related to waste management , climate change action , bush regeneration and wildlife rescue .
