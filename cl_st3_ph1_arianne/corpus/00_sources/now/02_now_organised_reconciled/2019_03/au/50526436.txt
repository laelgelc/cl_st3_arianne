<h> One Of Our Local Council Wants To Declare A " Worldwide Climate Emergency " <p> Mar 21 , 2019 <h> SHARE <h> A local SA council is demanding action on climate change " while we still have a chance " as it falls foul of new Local Government Association guidelines designed to help councils stick to their " knitting " , reports FIVEaa Breakfast 's David Penberthy . <p> Penbo said during Thursday 's Breaking @ 8 segment : <p> " There 's a bit of a stink going on involving the Gawler council that has a councillor or a small group of councillors who are of the view that the situation at the moment in terms of climate change with our planet is so dire that the township of Gawler should declare a worldwide climate emergency . <p> " There 's pages and pages of supporting evidence and it 's all written in full caps with lots of exclamation marks : <p> ' Now is the time to act while we still have a chance , but time has almost run out . Now is the time to declare that we face a climate emergency . Now @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ action plan , for the LGA to take this necessary and bold move and declare that there is a climate emergency and to commit to developing a climate emergency action plan that guides all future policy and decisions making ' ... <p> " It just goes on and on and on . <p> " The LGA has said thanks for that guys , it 's not really what we 're after . <p> " I believe in climate change and I think that it 's important that we have the debate about the policies that we put in place ... However the idea that the Gawler council should down tools and forget about all basic service issues ... so the LGA said we are going to get back to our knitting and I think most ratepayers will welcome it with open arms . "
