<h> Transport <p> Transport makes up about 19 per cent of Australia 's total emissions and they 've been rising steadily for decades . <p> Rohan Lund , the CEO of motoring body NRMA , says our love for big gas guzzlers is partly responsible . Many of our top-selling cars last year were SUVs or utes . <p> " We 're a big country , we 've a big driving culture and particularly in outer suburban areas in the regions we like big cars , " he said . <p> One way to get less carbon-intensive cars on our roads would be to introduce a fuel efficiency standard . <p> Australia is one of the only countries in the developed world that does n't have one . The result is that our cars are , on average , less fuel efficient than those in Europe and the US . <p> That standard would essentially force manufacturers to sell a larger number of more efficient cars , but it 's become a politically controversial idea in Australia .
