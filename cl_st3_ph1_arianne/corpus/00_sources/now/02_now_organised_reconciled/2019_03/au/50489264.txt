<p> March has come around very quickly , and introduces the season of autumn . We are fortunate here in Orange to be able to delight in the autumn colours that grace our landscape , and the beautiful golden hues are already in evidence . There 's a phrase running round in my head from a little song which says , ' the seasons are like a circle that goes round and round and round ' . Seasons , of course are not just limited to Mother Earth and the climate - there are also Liturgical Seasons celebrated in various religious traditions . In the Christian churches we have just begun the season of Lent , with the commemoration of Ash Wednesday this week , and preceded by Shrove Tuesday . For many people , Shrove Tuesday is more about Mardi Gras and Pancake Day than the day of preparation for Ash Wednesday . In the ancient Jewish tradition , steps were taken to use up all yeast products before the fasting began , and unleavened bread was the custom . The season of Lent is a time of prayer and penance in preparation for the most important Feast @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Jesus at Easter . Penance is an interesting activity . Some people see it as punishment , going without something we are used to enjoying , even self flagellation in atonement for sin . Many of us will remember as children putting the lollies in a jar instead of eating them , and then having a beano when Lent is over . Another custom was saving all the loose coins for the 40 days , followed by a splurge when the time was up . Another totally different way of looking at our Lenten observation could be a decision to do something more constructive that would benefit others . The loose coins could find their way into a charity bin or collection for some needy cause . We might pledge ourselves to commit a small act of kindness each day to someone we meet , or to someone who appears to be in need . Of course , only a select few will get the seats , and the rest will have to lick their wounds . Issues like climate change , drought relief , water devastation , refugees and asylum seekers , and corruption especially at high levels will all continue to be aired , touted and debated . We need to be sure of the facts before we start sprouting our opinions among family and friends . These are just a few random thoughts that might help our readers to look on the penitential season of Lent as a two-way street -- doing something to enhance our own spiritual life , and in so doing to benefit others who may have special needs or simply find peace and gratitude in their hearts for sharing the kindness of another , even if it is as small as a welcoming smile .
