This study found dramatic erosion in support for the major parties . In 1987 , 72 percent of those questioned said they had always voted for the same party . In 2016 , that number was down to 40 per cent . <p> That leakage was underscored in the NSW election results where the Liberals ' and Nationals ' combined primary vote hovered in the low 40s and Labor in the low to mid-30s .
