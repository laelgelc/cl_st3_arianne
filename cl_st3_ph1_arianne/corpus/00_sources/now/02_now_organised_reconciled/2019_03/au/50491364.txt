<p> Premier Mark McGowan was last night under mounting pressure to quash a " job-killing " plan by WA 's environment watchdog to make big polluters pay for their carbon emissions . <p> The Environmental Protection Authority yesterday recommended that new projects in WA emitting more than 100,000 tonnes of greenhouse gases a year be forced to offset those emissions by planting trees or buying carbon credits . <p> The State Government has the final say on the approvals process and is free to ignore the recommendation , but Federal Resources Minister Matt Canavan said it needed to immediately rule out the requirement . <p> " This is a massive proposed act of economic self-harm to the WA economy and it would do nothing to help the environment , " Mr Canavan said . " It 's silly for WA to try to change the temperature of the globe from Perth .
