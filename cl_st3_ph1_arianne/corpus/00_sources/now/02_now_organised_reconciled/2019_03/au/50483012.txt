As per his usual routine , Bloom arrived in Australia from his hometown of Newbridge in County Kildare five days before his media engagements and gigs began . But mostly , it was spent engaging with Aussies , who he holds a deep affinity for . " I do call Australians Paddies with suntans , " Bloom says .
