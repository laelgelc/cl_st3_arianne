Their absences will be noted as " unauthorised " but it is unclear what punishment , if any , will result . <p> Organisers have called for a class boycott after being inspired by 16-year-old Swedish activist Greta Thunberg , who staged a school strike outside parliament in Stockholm last August over climate change .
