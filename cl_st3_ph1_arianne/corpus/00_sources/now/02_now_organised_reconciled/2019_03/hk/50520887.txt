<p> The Development Bureau has said that parts of Chief Executive Carrie Lam 's Lantau Tomorrow Vision development project will cost approximately HK$624 billion . <p> The estimate includes the reclamation of a 1,000-hectare artificial island at Kau Yi Chau , off the eastern side of Lantau Island , as well as reclamation at Sunny Bay , Lung Kwu Tan , as well as developments and infrastructure around Tuen Mun 's coast . <p> The Kau Yi Chau reclamation , and related projects will cost the most , at HK$256 billion . The estimates were made on the basis of engineering costs as of September 2018 . <p> Michael Wong . Photo : RTHK Screenshot . <p> The estimate does not include a reclamation plan for 700-hectares at Hei Ling Chau , another outlying island off Lantau Island . Secretary for Development Michael Wong said there was no timetable for Hei Ling Chau . <p> " If we put an estimate out , it would be very misleading , " he said . <p> Wong said that , according to an estimation by the Hong Kong Institute of Surveyors , the government could earn between HK$974 billion and HK$1143 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ revenue from housing property sales , Wong added . <p> Wong said it was not the government 's usual practice to provide a cost estimate before engineering studies are conducted . <p> " Will the project use up all the money in the treasury ? After our research , our clear conclusion is that it will not , " he said . " But it is meaningless to keep saying it will not . On the basis of information we have and our professional experience , we made some estimates . " <p> " Through land sales , we will reclaim the costs , " Wong added . <p> Lantau Tomorrow Vision .
