<h> Student climate strikers - mature thinking given youthful voice <p> EDITORIAL : In August 2003 about 1500 rural Southlanders gathered to protest the much-derided " fart tax " that the Helen Clarkgovernment planned to introduce on livestock methane emissions in the name of living up to the Kyoto Protocol climate change measures . <p> Details such as this populist nickname being targeted at the wrong end of the cow ( the problematic emissions were more from belching ) were scooted over by this being an acronym : Fight Against Ridiculous Taxes . <p> The protesters were able to chalk their efforts up as successful , at least in political terms . The howls of opposition put a break on the Government 's plans and the country set about what many a sage adult agreed , at least in passing , should be more sensible policy making about what , if anything , needed to be done about climate change . <p> After which this unexcitable majority proceeded to treat climate change with all the sense of purpose and far sightedness that has brought us to the position in which we now in .
