<h> Kiwifruit Growers appoint Labour Co-ordinator <p> New Zealand Kiwifruit Growers Inc. has appointed Gavin Stagg as a Labour Co-ordinator to implement initiatives to ensure the industry has the workers it requires for the 2019 season . <p> Gavin has been involved in the kiwifruit industry since 2012 , working in operations management roles at Mount Pack and Cool Ltd and more recently Birchwood Packhouse Ltd in Tauranga , with a major focus on health and safety .
