<h> Group hosting international business event known for charity <p> Auckland group hosting important international business event is well-known for its charitable work . <p> <p> From The 20th Convention of Teochew international Federation News Center <p> The Auckland-based group that won the right to host an important international business event , that launches later this week , is well known in the Kiwi-Asian community for its charity work . <p> The Chao Shan General Association of New Zealand is set to start hosting the 20th Convention of Teochew International Federation , which will be held in two parts -- from March 17-19 , and from September 27-29 . It will be an opportunity for the country to promote tourism , commerce , logistics and educational products to nearly 2,000 overseas industry investors . <p> The association -- one of the biggest associations in New Zealand , with 3,000 registered members -- won the hosting rights at a closely contested bidding competition during the last convention . <p> The international biennial business event has been running since 1981 and was originally set up as a networking opportunity for expat Teochew residents from the Chaoshan area of Guangdong Province , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Zealand is listed on the official website of the National Ethnicity Community Department 's official website . Some emigrated from Hong Kong around 1997 . More have been coming to call New Zealand home since immigration restrictions on mainland Chinese were relaxed in 2000 . <p> According to incomplete statistics , there are more than 60 million Teochew people from all over the world .
