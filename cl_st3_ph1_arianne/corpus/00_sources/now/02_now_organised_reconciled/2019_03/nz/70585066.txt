<h> Median KiwiSaver balances rose 62% over three years <p> March 12 ( BusinessDesk ) - The median value of KiwiSaver nest eggs grew to $13,000 at June 30 last year , up 62 percent or $5,000 from three years earlier , Stats NZ says . <p> The growth reflects employee , employer and government contributions as well as any investment gains . <p> " KiwiSaver is still a relatively young superannuation scheme and median values should continue to rise as the scheme matures , " the government statistician says . <p> Unsurprisingly , those aged 15-24 had the smallest KiwiSaver accounts and are the only age group where men 's accounts are about the same size as women 's at about $3,000 . In all other age groups , men 's accounts are larger than women 's . <p> Those aged 55-64 have the largest holdings , although men 's accounts were larger at about $30,000 compared with those of women at about $23,000 . <p> " Lower income and breaks in work can affect the build-up of retirement savings . They had a median value of $73,000 at June 30 , up 37 percent , from $53,000 , since June 2015 . <p> Males aged 15-24 are now just as likely as females to be in a KiwiSaver scheme .
