<h> More stories about QBE <h> New initiative launched to steer Australian economy through " critical decade " Insurance Business <p> Three insurance bosses have joined a new initiative designed to set out a roadmap for realigning the finance sector to support greater social , environmental , and economic outcomes for Australia . <p> IAG 's Jacki Johnson , QBE 's Mark Joiner , and Swiss Re 's Mark Senkevics are among the financial sector leaders who have signed up for the Australian Sustainable Finance Initiative -- a collaboration that will help shape an Australian economy that prioritises human wellbeing , social equity , and environmental protection , while underpinning financial system stability , in what it described as a " critical decade " ahead . <p> " This unique and high-level collaboration will set out a roadmap for achieving this goal and shows the willingness of the financial services sector to step up and play an active role in building a more sustainable and resilient economy for all Australians , " said Simon O'Connor , initiative co-chair and CEO of the Responsible Investment Association Australasia ( RIAA ) . <p> The initiative , modelled on international best practice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Expert Group on Sustainable Finance and the UK 's Green Finance Taskforce , will be guided by a steering committee that is set to deliver an Australian Sustainable Finance Roadmap in 2020 . <p> The roadmap will contain recommendations that will assist the financial services sector to : <p> Mobilise capital to deliver on national and global sustainable development and climate goals ; <p> Enhance disclosures and transparency on environmental , social , and governance risks and opportunities ; and <p> Deliver a financial system that meets community expectations around sustainability . <p> " The roadmap we create will include pathways , policy signals , and frameworks that will better enable the financial services sector to contribute to delivering on international commitments , such as the Paris Agreement on Climate Change and the UN Sustainable Development Goals , while underpinning economic stability and prosperity for Australia , " said Johnson , IAG group executive and co-chair of the initiative .
