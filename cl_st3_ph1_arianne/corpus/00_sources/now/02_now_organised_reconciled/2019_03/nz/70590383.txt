" <p> Now , eight months later , Thunberg 's solitary action has snowballed into a worldwide movement in 92 countries , at last count . On Friday , March 15 , New Zealand teenagers will be holding their own school strike against climate change , as part of the global action being held on that day . <p> Yet the strikers do n't want mere expressions of support .
