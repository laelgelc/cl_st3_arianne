<h> Call for closure of Golden Bay freedom camping area near bird roosting site <p> A banded dotterel , pictured here with a chick , is one of the bird species that has been spotted near the Taupata Gravel Reserve freedom camping site . Forest &amp; Bird wants freedom camping banned at the site . <p> Forest &amp; Bird is calling on Tasman District Council to close a freedom camping site in Golden Bay , fearing for the future of some vulnerable species of birds . <p> Committee member Cynthia McConville said the Taupata Gravel Reserve site , near Puponga , was a high-tide roosting area for hundreds of birds including godwits , South Island pied oystercatchers , white-fronted terns and banded dotterels , " a bird that is facing the risk of extinction in the medium term " . <p> Most of the freedom campers were " really nice people " but often did not realise the birds were there or understand the importance of leaving them undisturbed . The campers were at times just metres from the roosting sites and had even used them as recreational areas , McConville said . <p> " All species recorded at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ vulnerable .
