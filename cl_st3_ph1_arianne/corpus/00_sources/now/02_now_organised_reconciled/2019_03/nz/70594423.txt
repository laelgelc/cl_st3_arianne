<h> Protesters march for the future of the planet <p> Hundreds of students demand action over climate change , in The Square , Palmerston North . <p> Rachael Stone 's voice through the megaphone led students young and old , some accompanied by teachers and parents , through the middle of Palmerston North . She told them to stand up and fight back against climate change . <p> The 16-year-old St Peter 's College student organised the city 's march as part of a national Schools For Climate Action movement that has origins in Sweden . She led the chants from the front of the march , encouraging people to get louder and keep chanting . <p> A crowd of hundreds marched around The Square on Friday . There was plenty of tooting and support from people as the crowd moved along the road . <p> Stone said it was " awesome " being part of a movement full of such passionate people .
