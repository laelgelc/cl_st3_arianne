<h> AM Best maintains Momentum Life 's positive credit rating <p> Momentum Life , a licensed and trusted direct-to-consumer life insurer in New Zealand , will maintain it 's Financial Strength and Long-Term Credit Ratings from AM Best . These ratings of B++ ( Good ) and " bbb " , respectively , reflect the insurer 's balance sheet strength as it continues to grow in 2019 . <p> AM Best is an independent credit rating agency that reports on the financial stability of insurers worldwide . Best 's Credit Ratings are a vital tool to help consumers evaluate an insurer 's ability to honour their financial obligations to policyholders . <p> Momentum Life received its life insurance licence on 12 March 2018 , becoming the underwriter of their products sold after that date . The current ratings from AM Best are based partly on the company 's strong performance since receiving its licence . <p> AM Best 's decision to affirm Momentum Life 's ratings was welcomed by Chief Financial &amp; Operating Officer Eugeniu Jalba .
