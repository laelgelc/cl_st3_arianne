<h> Government suspends negotiations with Westland <p> March 19 ( BusinessDesk ) - The government has suspended negotiations with Westland Cooperative Dairy Company to conclude financing arrangements for a $10 million loan after news the dairy company had reached a conditional agreement to sell the cooperative to Inner Mongolia Yili Industrial Group . <p> " The proposed sale means that a loan from the Provincial Growth Fund will not go ahead at this stage . If the sale is ultimately confirmed by shareholders then the loan will no longer be required by the company , " the head of the Provincial Development Unit Robert Pigou said in a statement . <p> The government has allocated $3 billion over a three-year term to invest in regional economic development through the PGF . <p> Last November , the government announced the PGF would provide Westland with an interest-bearing , repayable $9.9 million loan for a milk processing plant . Today , however , the cooperative said it had signed a conditional agreement to sell to Yili for $3.41 per share in a deal worth $588 million , including debt and liabilities .
