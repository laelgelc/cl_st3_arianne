<h> Climate change : Rising sea level could hit 43,000 Aucklanders ' homes <p> ? The homes of up to 43,000 Aucklanders , and up to 30 per cent of the land in the city centre , are at risk from sea level rise , according to a new analysis . <p> New work by Auckland Council researchers shows high-quality rural land will be an early casualty of forecast rising sea levels , but under longer-term extreme scenarios , built-up areas will also be exposed . <p> The gradual effect of the sea rising by a metre by 2100 , will be aggravated by storm surges , dramatically expanding the affected areas . <p> The report takes a regionwide look at the impact of sea level rise , with a more detailed localised look due out soon . <p> The researchers have looked at the impact of 25cm of sea level rise forecast by 2045 , 50cm by 2060 , 1 metre by 2100 , 2 metres by 2160 and
