<h> Mt Albert Community Comes Together for Open Letter to MP <p> Mt Albert resident Marianne van der Haas is calling for Mt Albert MP Jacinda Ardern to speed up the process of passing a Zero Carbon Bill in Parliament in an open letter signed by over 150 local residents . <p> Beginning in December 2018 , Marianne has sought signatories to her open letter by travelling to farmers ' markets in Mt Albert , Point Chevalier and Grey Lynn . Mt-Albert-based Generation Zero volunteer Maulik Thakkar has supported Marianne 's efforts by spreading awareness about the Zero Carbon Bill and its potential impacts for Mt Albert 's residents . <p> " Residents in my area are concerned about climate change and how we 're all going to be able to cope . I 'm surprised that our local MP has n't yet been more vocal about how we can make a difference in her home electorate , " said Marianne , who works as an occupational therapist and is a passionate advocate for the environment . <p> " I 'd like to see all of government , whether in Wellington or in Auckland , take a more proactive @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ follow - particularly given the alarming news about the melting of Antarctica that we 've seen in the last week . " <p> " I absolutely agree that climate action is this generation 's nuclear-free moment , and I look forward to concrete goals in the upcoming Zero Carbon Bill , with a clear roadmap for local and regional governments to get New Zealand 's emission levels to net zero by 2050 . " <p> The open letter is handwritten by Marianne based on input from Generation Zero 's policy team . Together with the support of a broad range of local residents of all ages , Marianne has put together all signatures in an appendix to the letter .
