<h> Kiwi students encouraged to draw , paint and Speak 4 The Planet <p> Thousands of students filled Christchurch 's Cathedral Square on March 15 as part of the global student climate action . <p> They might be small but their message is loud : The environment needs our help , stat . <p> For the first time in New Zealand , children have been invited to compete in a speech and art competition and have their say on initiatives to try tackle climate change , a hot button issue for young people . <p> The challenge , Speak 4 The Planet , was first posed to Australian students six years ago and launched in Christchurch this year . For intermediate and high school-aged students , the competition is based on messages from the United Nation 's annual World Environment Day , on June 5 . <p> This year 's theme is yet to be confirmed but will likely have the same focus as last year : tackling plastic pollution . It calls for speeches and artworks showcasing innovative solutions to environmental challenges and ideas about sustainable consumption and production . <p> Sustainable Otautahi Christchurch ( SOC ) project @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ find students who could articulate their ideas well , and would support them to share speeches , posters and artworks to a wide audience , including public officials . <p> The categories are broken down into prepared speech , impromptu speech and visual art work . Presentations will be grouped by school year . The final registration day is April 14 , the end of term one , and can be made through SOC . <p> " When it comes to the environment and climate change we 're not that good at listening to the facts or to the adults , so it 's time to support the young people to have their say , " Whitaker said . <p> GEORGE HEARD/STUFF <p> Lucy Gray , left , and her classmates protested for climate change in Christchurch as part of a global movement started by Greta Thunberg . <p> It comes after hundreds of Christchurch pupils staged a school strike and protested in the street as part of a global campaign for action on climate change on March 15 . Topics covered at previous Australian-run versions of the event included illegal trade in wildlife , energy saving measures , reducing food waste , and poverty . <p> Speak 4 The Planet is being organised by SOC and the New Zealand Association for Environmental Education ( NZAEE ) , and is supported by the Sustainable Initiatives Fund Trust , a charitable trust that funds projects creating long-term environmental benefits . <p> NZAEE spokeswoman Jocelyn Paprill said more than 10 schools had registered their interest , plus some students who are homeschooled . <p> " It 's all about building the confidence and abilities of young people .
