<h> Students strike for a better world <p> Pictured : Hundreds of local students went on strike last week to protest about the lack of action being taken to address climate change . Photo : Ollie Blyth <p> Last Friday , students across the globe walked out of school to protest the little action being done by high ranking world leaders to combat global warming . About 200 Mount Aspiring College students flooded the Wanaka lakefront on Friday afternoon to participate in the international strike . This was part of the School Strike 4 Climate movement , inspired by 16-year-old Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg who has been nominated for the Nobel Peace Prize for sparking the international movement . It has been estimated that at least 1.5 million students participated in over 110 countries . <p> Ben Pell , a year seven student at the school , is part of MAC 's Team Green group who helped to organise the protest . <p> " We protested because climate change is a big problem and politicians are n't doing anything about it , " he said .
