Further refreshments available until 7:30 pm following the conclusion of the presentation and question time at 7 pm . <p> Topic : <p> New Zealand shares the global challenge of reducing greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions in order to limit harms from climate change . Successive New Zealand Governments have participated in international processes . Out of those international processes have emerged a number of different targets for future reductions in emissions . To date , however , actions to achieve those targets have been limited in scope and have had little impact .
