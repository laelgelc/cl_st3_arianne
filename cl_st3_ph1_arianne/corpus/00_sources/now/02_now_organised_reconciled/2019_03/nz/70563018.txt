<p> Conservation Minister Eugenie Sage said $76 million of government funding had been confirmed for the Department of Conservation ( DOC ) over the next four years to help tackle the loss of native species . <p> The funding will cover the cost 150 more DOC staff . <p> The extra money will also be used to protect high priority ecosystems , increase management of marine reserves and freshwater systems , and protect an extra six islands from pests . Two research projects to protect marine areas will also be carried out . <p> Ms Sage said the increased funding was necessary to ensure the survival of endangered species . <p> The numbers of 22 species , including Okarito brown kiwi , have increased due to funding for their recovery and protection . <p> " Where there has been an investment in conservation with our threatened species , we have been able to turn around their decline , " Ms Sage said . <p> " We need to invest in conservation to ensure that we stop the decline and that we allow numbers to rebuild .
