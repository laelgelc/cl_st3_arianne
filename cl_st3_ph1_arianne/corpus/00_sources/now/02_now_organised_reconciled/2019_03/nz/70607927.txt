Meanwhile , the number of new mortgage registrations for investors has continued its negative trend , now down 2.5% annually , currently 16.9% of the market . <p> More people have been taking advantage of the low interest rates , with refinancers up 16% , now 23.6% of mortgage registrations . <p> Over half of New Zealand 's housing stock is currently valued at more than $600,000 , while
