<h> Air NZ , Contact , Genesis and Z Energy to create forestry portfolio <p> Air New Zealand , Contact Energy , Genesis Energy and Z Energy have joined forces to create a partnership that will establish a forest portfolio to sequester carbon and help meet their annual requirements under the emissions trading scheme . <p> Dryland Carbon LLP , or Drylandcarbon , chief executive Anthony Beverley said that now the partnership has been created , the next task is to focus on the land and the portfolio establishment . <p> The main aim is to produce a stable supply of forestry-generated NZU carbon credits for the four companies .
