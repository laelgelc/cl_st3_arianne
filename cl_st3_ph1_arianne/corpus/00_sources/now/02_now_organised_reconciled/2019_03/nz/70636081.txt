In today 's edition : Remembrance service in Christchurch to be broadcast nationwide , skepticism about social media extremism crackdown , and warnings made before bridge collapsed . <p> All eyes will turn to Christchurch today once more . A National Remembrance Service will be held to mark two weeks since the mosque attack , which took 50 lives . The service will begin in Hagley Park in Christchurch at 10.00am , and will be jointly led by the government , the city of Christchurch , Ngai Tahu and the Muslim community . Environment Canterbury is waiving bus fares for the day , so those wanting to go can get there for free . <p> Across the country , many places will also be broadcasting a live stream .
