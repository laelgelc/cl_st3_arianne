Why Stuff is reining in its comment section <p> With questions for media swirling about what their platforms get used for , New Zealand 's biggest news site has closed a huge swathe of their notorious comments section . It 's rare , but it happens . <p> But from now on , a lot less of the former will be happening on Stuff .
