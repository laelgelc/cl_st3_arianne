<h> Post-Christchurch , corporate NZ is calling big tech to account <p> A coalition of major New Zealand advertisers is building a coalition to demand change from the tech giants . <p> New Zealand 's biggest advertisers are working both individually and collectively to try and force change from the tech giants in the wake of the Christchurch terror attacks . At midday on Monday the Association of New Zealand Advertisers ( ANZA ) and the Commercial Communications Council ( CCC ) issued a joint statement calling for urgent change to the technology that allowed the Christchurch gunman to livestream his actions for 17 minutes on Friday . <p> Since then this sentiment has coalesced towards forcing the tech giants to reckon with the consequences of what their platforms allow . Conversations with multiple leaders in communications and marketing across brands and ad agencies suggest that once positions are formalised the next step will be to try and gather international support from a group of major advertisers . <p> The CCC 's Paul Head , who represents advertising agencies , says " Saturday was about sitting in tears . Some smaller businesses are going further , with Freedom Farms announcing it was quitting the platform entirely . <p> On Monday the sense of anger snowballed into what Mouat describes as a " moral groundswell " .
