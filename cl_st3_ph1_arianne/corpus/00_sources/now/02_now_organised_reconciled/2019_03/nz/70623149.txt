<h> Inaugural New Zealand Spirits Awards Comes to Wellington <p> Wellington is hosting the first-ever NZ Spirits Awards on 10 May at the Embassy Theatre to celebrate the best of the best of New Zealand-made and imported spirits . <p> " It 's amazing to think the growth in New Zealand-produced spirits now means we can celebrate the extraordinary products being made and sold here , and exported all around the world , " said Distilled Spirits Aotearoa Inc . DSA Chair , Sue James . <p> " It really is a sign of growing maturity that NZ-made spirits will be judged alongside the imported spirits which consumers are well and truly familiar with , " said Sue . <p> The DSA , along with Spirits New Zealand , are jointly organising the Awards . Spirits NZ Chief Executive , Robert Brewer , says a focus of the Awards is to help create the recognition of the quality and growing strength of New Zealand product as well as provide the opportunity to promote the overall category . <p> " What 's amazing is we have accepted 254 entries for the Awards , which is just an incredible number in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and we 're absolutely thrilled , " Robert said . <p> " It goes to show that now is the right time for a judging and awards showcase , given the thriving local spirits industry , and it will also be a great indicator of how we stand up against overseas producers . " <p> Judging takes place on 26-27 at the Grand Mercure Wellington , with 17 local and overseas judges participating in the judging process . There are seven categories : Gin , Rum , Vodka , Whiskey , Liqueurs , Brandies and Botanicals &amp; Other Spirits , with each category hosting a number of subcategories . <p> Medal winners will be announced following judging , and once results are confirmed .
