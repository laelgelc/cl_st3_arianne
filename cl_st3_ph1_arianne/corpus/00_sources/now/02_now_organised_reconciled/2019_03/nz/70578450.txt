<h> Small Pacific country roasts Australia over its coal mining policies <p> One of the smallest countries in the Pacific has given Australia a roasting over its coal mining policies . <p> The low lying island of Tuvalu faces the full impact of climate change , and given the Regional Pacific Forum meeting is to be held there , fireworks are likely . <p> It wants firm action from Australia on its coal mining policies . <p> " I would implore and impress on the leaders of Australia to reconsider their coal mining policy and the $64 million dollars they are getting from selling their coal , " says Tuvalu 's prime minister , Enele Sopoaga . <p> It 's a big call for financially strapped Tuvalu to tell Australia to keep its aid . <p> " There is no point in giving ODA ( Official Development Assistance ) , we appreciate it of course but giving it at the same time as continuing polluting the atmosphere and increasing the cost of adaptation I think just does n't work well , " Mr Sopoaga says . <p> But Tuvalu has a trump card .
