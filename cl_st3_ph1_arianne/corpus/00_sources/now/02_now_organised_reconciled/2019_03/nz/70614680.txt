<h> NZ 's Smallest Bird Brought Back to Wellington City <p> A titipounamu about to be transported to ZEALANDIA . This male bird weighed just 6 grams , lighter than a $1 coin . Photo Credit ZEALANDIA <p> Wellington , NZ - New Zealand 's smallest bird has flown into Wellington City . ZEALANDIA has translocated a total of 59 titipounamu ( rifleman ) into the ecosanctuary from Wainuiomata Mainland Island . <p> The translocation occurred over a number of days , with the official welcome on 17 March by Taranaki Whanui ki te Upoko o Te Ika . Capturing , processing , and transporting the birds involved over 60 people from many organisations . <p> The translocation provides another safe-guarded population for the small endemic bird , as well as a chance for more people to see and learn about them . <p> " The titipounamu has been absent from the Wellington city area for many decades , so this is an important step in the conservation of the species , " said ZEALANDIA Centre for People and Nature Director Dr Danielle Shanahan . <p> " This reintroduction also contributes to the 500-year restoration vision for Te Mara @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We hope that the birds here will quickly flourish , and later disperse successfully beyond the fence as predator control across the region gets better and better .
