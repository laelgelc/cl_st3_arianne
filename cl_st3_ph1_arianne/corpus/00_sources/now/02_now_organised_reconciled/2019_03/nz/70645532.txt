<p> News from WCC The Wellington City Council and the Auckland Council have developed New Zealand 's first gamified carbon calculator to help people to make choices to reduce their impact on climate change . <p> FutureFit lets people work out the carbon impact of their lifestyle and lets them choose positive changes in the way they live to help reduce it . The change the tool helps will support New Zealand 's commitment to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and the Wellington City Council 's goal to become a low-carbon capital . <p> The tool has been tailored for people in Wellington , Auckland and the " rest of New Zealand " and its calculations reflect the differences of living and transportation in those cities . It measures and quantifies individuals ' climate impact based across four categories -- Transport , Food , Energy and Living . <p> FutureFit then provides people with everyday actions and challenges , tailored to their personal carbon emissions profile , and will remind them each week via text or email for as long as they want to participate . People can share their results and have fun competing with others . <p> Wellington @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ says the tool is a great use of technology to tackle New Zealand 's climate footprint . <p> " The Council developed FutureFit as a key part of Wellington 's low carbon capital plan to support the community in reducing greenhouse gas emissions . Giving people information about the climate impacts of their lifestyles helps them make choices about what they do and consume , " says Cr Lee . <p> People who use the tool would first estimate their carbon footprint , register to develop their carbon reduction actions and reminders , and even invite their friends , co-workers and whanau to a friendly competition to see who can reduce their footprint the most . <p> " Everyone can make a difference . The little reductions individuals make can add up to big reductions in the carbon we produce overall . " <p> FutureFit is free for individuals .
