<h> AfDB is doubling its commitments on the continent with $25 billion fund <p> 03/18/2019 <p> Tell your friends <p> The African Development Bank ( AfDB ) said it will double its climate finance commitments for the period 2020-2025 with US$25 billion.Akinwumi Adesina , AfDB president speaking at the One Planet Summit in Nairobi . <p> Akinwumi Adesina , the Bank 's President , announced the commitment last week at the One Planet Summit in Nairobi . <p> " The required level of financing is only feasible with the direct involvement of the entire financial sector , " said Adesina . <p> " Consequently , the Bank launched the African Financial Alliance for Climate Change ( AFAC ) to link all stock exchanges , pension and sovereign wealth funds , central Banks and other financial institutions of Africa to mobilise and incentivise the shift of their portfolios towards low carbon and climate resilient investments . " <p> Adesina announced the development bank is on course to achieve its target of allocating 40% of its funding to climate finance by 2020 , a year ahead . <p> Speaking at a plenary in the presence of Heads of State , including @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Macron , Adesina said bank 's commitment is the highest among all multilateral development banks , has progressed steadily from 9% in 2016 to 28% in 2017 and 32% in 2018 . <p> AfDB President , Akinwumi Adesina speaking at a plenary in the presence of Heads of State , including President Uhuru Kenyatta of Kenya , and French President Emmanuel Macron. ( flickr/AfDB ) <p> Africa Climate Week kicks off in Ghana <p> The climate financing is coming at a time when top government officials , policymakers across the continent will gather in Accra , Ghana for the Africa Climate Week . <p> The Climate Week kicks off today , Monday 18 and will run through till Friday . The African event is a build-up to the next UN climate change conference -- COP25 which will take place in Chile this December .
