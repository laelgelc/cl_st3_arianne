<h> Mahama rallies support for 3 Cyclone Idai countries <p> It 's been almost a week since Cyclone Idai slammed into the Southern part of Africa.Cyclone Idai countries <p> Former President John Mahama has sent messages of condolence to the peoples of Malawi , Zimbabwe , and Mozambique following the devastation caused by the tropical cyclone , Idai . <p> According to him , " Watching the humanitarian crisis unfolding , following the landfall of this category 2 cyclone , it shows that our continent is totally unprepared for the extremities associated with climate change manifesting around our planet . " <p> Mozambique , Zimbabwe , and Malawi have all declared emergencies and appealed for humanitarian assistance . <p> Mahama in a statement urged " African countries to quickly take the lead to mobilise a humanitarian response in support of our brothers and sisters . <p> " With about 1,000 people now confirmed dead , buildings flattened and survivors struggling to find hard surfaces to cling to , I call on our continental organization , the African Union ( AU ) , to lead an emergency response to help save the lives of affected persons in our sister countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Ghana showed leadership and led such emergency efforts such as during the Ebola outbreak in some of our West African neighboring states . " <p> " It will be a great sense of pride if our President , Nana Akufo-Addo , rallies his colleagues to mobilise an African-led support package to alleviate the harrowing stories we are beginning to see on our screens , " he noted . <p> Former president John Mahama <p> Effects of Cyclone Idai <p> In Zimbabwe , at least 98 people have died and 217 people are missing in the east and south , the government said . <p> In Malawi , the UN says more than 80,000 people have been displaced by the cyclone . <p> Large parts of Mozambique have been engulfed after the cyclone smashed into its low-lying coastal areas , while heavy rains caused landslides and floods in Malawi and Zimbabwe .
