<p> Hajia Alima Mahama , the Minister of Local Government , has asked the international community to make adequate contributions into the Green Climate Fund or the Africa Development Fund ( ADF ) for disbursement towards climate change activities at the local level . <p> She said there was the need to decentralise climate change activities , with adequate funding at the local level , through simple climate and environmental improvement measures like tree planting , dredging and sanitation . <p> Hajia Mahama made the call in an interview with journalists after participating in a panel discussion on the sidelines of the Africa Climate Week ( ACW ) 2019 conference , which closed on Friday in Accra . <p> The panel discussion examined how to facilitate implementation of countries ' Nationally Determined Contributions ( NDCs ) under the Paris Agreement on Climate Change and SDG-13 , among other goals . <p> The Minister , who is also the Member of Parliament for Nalerigu/Gambaga , said climate change activities should be targeted mainly at the local level since any response would have its impact at the local level . <p> She said every year , the District , Municipal and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ activities and the National Development Planning Commission took out issues from the planned activities and develop guidelines for all the local authorities . <p> " Now they have a three-year Medium-Term Development Plan , that is 2018 to 2021 , and in that , they are required to have climate change issues , and they have done that .
