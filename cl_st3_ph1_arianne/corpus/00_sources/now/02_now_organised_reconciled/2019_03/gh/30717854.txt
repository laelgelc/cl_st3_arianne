<h> The Ghanaian government wants to introduce electric buses to fight climate change <p> The government of Ghana has announced that it will introduce electric buses and buses powered by compressed natural gas . The Ghanaian government wants to introduce electric buses to fight climate change <p> This forms part of its efforts to reduce air pollution on the roads in the country . <p> The Deputy Minister of Transport , Daniel Titus Glover who was speaking at a roundtable discussion made this revelation . <p> The discussion focused on " Investing in climate actions for job creation and economic development . " <p> According to the deputy minister , electric mobility was a promising technological solution to addressing climate change pollutants , and , therefore , called on investors and interested parties to partner the government in rolling out the initiative . <p> " As phase-one , we are collaborating with the African Development Bank ( AfDB ) to access funds from the Green Climate Fund to introduce electric buses into our public transport system , " he said . <p> He then added that " Though there are potential barriers , we have an opportunity to implement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be aggressively pursued . " <p> Fighting climate change has been a major issue in Ghana and Africa as a whole . Veen banks have joined in the fight against the change . <p> Recently , the President of the Bankers Association of Ghana , Alhassan Andani said the banking sector will not finance projects which endanger the climate . Indicating that it has resolved to halt funding of projects which do not follow the guidelines for sustainable investment . <p> The environmentally-friendly buses are expected to phase-out the operations of urban buses popularly called ' trotro ' that use dirty fossil fuels such as diesel and petrol .
