<p> Industry and Commerce Minister Rishad Bathiudeen handing over the letter of appointment to new CAA Chairman Dr. Lalith Senaweera ( centre ) yesterday as Secretary to the Ministry KDN Ranjith Ashoka ( left ) looks on <p> Sri Lanka 's national consumer protection is to be led by an internationally accredited quality professional and a leading academic in management science . <p> " With this appointment , we leverage your local and international experience for our consumer protection , " said Industry Commerce Minister Rishad Bathiudeen , while handing over the letter of appointment to Dr. Lalith Senaweera ( PhD in Management-USA , MBA-Colombo , BSc Hons- Sri Lanka ) , the ninth Chairman of the Consumer Affairs Authority ( CAA ) of Sri Lanka at the Ministry yesterday . <p> As the former Director General -- CEO of Sri Lanka Standards Institution , Dr. Senaweera led it for 36 years , infusing various aspects of international standardisation to the Sri Lankan domain . He introduced the ISO series to Lankan business sector for the first time 1994 , starting with ISO 9000 . He was the Chairman of DEVCO -- ISO during 2013-2016 , the International Organization @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ result he brought honour to Sri Lanka being the only Sri Lankan to hold this distinguished international position in global standardisation . The global action plan for Standardisation ( 2016-2020 ) now being implemented across the world was developed during his Chairmanship of DEVCO . DEVCO-ISO integrates implementation of ISO across the world . <p> Considering his significant contribution to propagate and promote quality and quality management locally and internationally , he was recognised by global authorities and was awarded the fellowship of American Society for Quality ( ASQ ) and Chartered Quality Professional of Chartered Quality Institute of UK . He is the sole Sri Lankan to hold both these recognitions in Sri Lanka today . He is an approved Assessor Team Leader for Clean Development Mechanism ( CDM ) UN Framework on Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) and conducted a number of institutional assessments in leading countries including UK , Japan , Korea , China to accredit applying country-organisations . He has published many academic articles and research papers and also the author of three volumes on Quality and Quality Management .
