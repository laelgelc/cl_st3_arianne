<h> Britain 's greenhouse gas emissions fall by 3pc <h> Top Story <p> LONDON : The UK 's greenhouse gas emissions fell 3 per cent in 2018 as pollution from the energy sector continued to fall , provisional government figures show . <p> Emissions of the gases which drive climate change have fallen for six years in a row , and are 44 per cent below the 1990 baseline for the UK . Emissions of carbon dioxide , the main greenhouse gas , are at the lowest level since before the start of the 20th century , when Queen Victoria was still on the throne , government officials said . <p> The fall was mainly due to a 7 per cent reduction in pollution from energy supplies , as the power sector continued to switch away from coal to low-carbon technology such as renewables . There was also a 3 per cent drop in emissions from transport , which have barely fallen since 1990 and now make up the biggest share of the UK 's climate pollution .
