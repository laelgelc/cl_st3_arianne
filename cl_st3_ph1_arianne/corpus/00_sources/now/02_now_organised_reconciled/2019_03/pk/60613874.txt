<p> -- Minister says Pakistan is assessing environmental damage and plans to approach UN and other forums soon <p> The forestry department filed a First Information Report ( FIR ) against unidentified Indian Air Force ( IAF ) pilots on Friday for bombing trees in Balakot area of the country 's north on February 26 . <p> The FIR also details the damage caused to 19 trees after Indian aircraft " hastily dropped their payload " . Terming India 's actions as " eco-terrorism " , Pakistan has plans of approaching the United Nations ( UN ) against the damaged caused by India to the Pakistani environment . <p> Climate Change Minister Malik Amin Aslam said that Pakistan was assessing the impact caused to the environment by the bombing and the results would be the basis for Pakistan 's complaint at UN and other international forums .
