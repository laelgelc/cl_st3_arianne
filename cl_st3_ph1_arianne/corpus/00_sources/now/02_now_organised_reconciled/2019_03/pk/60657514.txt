<h> In battle with ' land mafia ' , Pakistan targets win for forests and climate <p> ISLAMABAD : Pakistani Prime Minister Imran Khan is aiming to fight climate change and pollution by planting trees reclaimed land at Balloki Headworks on the River Ravi in Nankana Sahib district , about an hour 's drive from the city of Lahore . <p> On Feb. 9 , he planted the first of 652,500 saplings , mainly local species , to be put in by April on 1,500 acres ( 607 hectares ) which had previously been turned into farm fields . <p> Khan has promised to plant 10 billion trees across the country over the next five years . <p> He hopes to scale up the success of the " Billion Tree Tsunami " in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , where his party , which runs the province , has been protecting existing forests and
