<h> Netherlands sympathises with victims of New Zealand terror attacks : envoy <h> Karachi <p> Netherlands sympathises with victims of New Zealand terror attacks : envoy <p> The Netherlands Ambassador to Pakistan Ardi Stoiosbraken conveyed her disgust at the recent terrorist attack in New Zealand and sympathised with the affected people . <p> She said this during a meeting with the chairman and members of the Karachi Council on Foreign Relations ( KCFR ) , said a press release issued on Tuesday . Stoiosbraken said there was religious freedom in her country , which safeguarded human rights and supported them all over the world . <p> She arrived at the KCFR House where she was received by Chairman KCFR Ikram Sehgal and CEO Commodore ( retd ) Sadeed A Malik . She addressed the council 's Board of Directors , including Lt-Gen ( retd ) Moinuddin Haider , Vice Admiral ( retd ) Khalid Mir , Saifuddin Zoomkawala , Ambassador Mustufa Kamal Kazi and Treasurer Kalim Farooqui . <p> Stoiosbraken discussed international diplomacy , investment and cooperation between various countries of the world , especially with the Netherlands . On this occasion , CEO Sadeed A Malik gave a presentation .
