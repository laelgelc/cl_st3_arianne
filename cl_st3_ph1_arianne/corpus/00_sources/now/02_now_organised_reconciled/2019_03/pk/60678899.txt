<h> People , plant must be integral part of CSR strategies : Sanjrani <p> ISLAMABAD : Chairman Senate Muhammad Sadiq Sanjrani Wednesday said that plants not only make the environment aesthetically beautiful but also contribute positively to improving the human health . <p> The Upper House of Parliament was actively pursuing the agenda of Clean and Green Pakistan as its Standing Committee on Climate Change and a dedicated Climate Change Casus had suggested workable plan for conservation of environment while taking stakeholders on board . <p> " There is a need to plant maximum trees in those areas which are having less density of forest . " <p> The Chairman Senate said that active participation of the public and private sector in plantation drive would pave the way for increasing the forest cover in the country . He said that better coordination among the federating units was essential for taking concrete measures for promoting the cause of environment . <p> Sanjrani said that Upper House of Parliament had always taken a proactive approach to legislate on environmental issues . <p> He called upon the private sector to come forward for planting maximum trees in far flung areas of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ campaign drive was launched at the eve of the meeting of Asian Parliamentary Assembly for the Green Gwadar Program . He said that Gwadar was the future of the country and special focus should be given to make it greener and beautiful . <p> Sanjrani called upon the corporate sector entities to focus on devising strategy revolving around people and planet and make such initiatives part of their corporate social responsibility . <p> He said that developing world was bearing the brunt of climate change in the shape of different natural disasters and there was a need to make coordinated efforts to mitigate negative effects of climate change .
