<p> According to a press release issued here from Singapore Environment Council , Syed Muhammad Abubakar , a Lahore based environmental journalist , is among the fifteen journalists and media outlets from around Asia who were recognised for their outstanding environmental reporting at the Asian Environmental Journalism Award ( AEJA ) . <p> The award honours individuals and organisations that report news stories on sustainability issues facing our planet and the Asia region . <p> Guest of Honour , Mr Masagos Zulkifli , Singapore 's Minister for the Environment and Water Resources presented the winners with their awards . <p> This year 's AEJA entries cover everything from sustainable farming in Pakistan to hail storm disasters in Myanmar . The breadth of environmental topics covered was amazing . <p> The work exemplifies the professionalism of Asia 's environmental journalists and how they take highly complex issues and make them clearly understood by the Asian community , " , reads the press release . <p> " Environmental journalism brings global trends and issues to the public 's attention and makes them relevant to the average person , " said Ms Isabella Huang-Loh , Chairman of Singapore Environment Council . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and recognises them as unsung heroes for the green cause . <p> Abubakar is passionate about climate change , global warming , deforestation , food security , and sustainability and biodiversity issues .
