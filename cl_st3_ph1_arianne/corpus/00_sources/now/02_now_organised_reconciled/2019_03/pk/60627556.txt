<h> LNG growth and the environment <p> Where the domestic energy mix is increasingly tilting towards Liquefied Natural Gas(LNG) , the demand for the fuel globally is also growing by leaps and bounds . According to Shell Energy Outlook 2019 , the global energy demand is set to increase by 18 percent by 2035 , and gas is expected to meet around 40 percent of this additional demand . <p> LNG continues to be the fastest growing gas supply source globally with more than half of LNG import growth in Asia . Strong demand in Asia is driving rapid growth in LNG where China , India , South Korea and Pakistan led LNG demand growth in 2018 as per the latest LNG Outlook . Going forward , Shell sees near term supply growth being absorbed by Europe and Asia with increased need for investment in supply and infrastructure to meet the rising demand . <p> Interestingly , gas demand globally is not solely being driven by the power sector today .
