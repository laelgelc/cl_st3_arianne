built and how will determine how effectively the world combats climate change , experts told a conference on sustainable infrastructure in Barcelona . <p> Without more effort to ensure infrastructure uses greener materials and sources of energy , " we will totally explode our carbon budget ... which we can not allow " , said Christiana Figueres , a former U.N . climate chief . <p> Buildings , for
