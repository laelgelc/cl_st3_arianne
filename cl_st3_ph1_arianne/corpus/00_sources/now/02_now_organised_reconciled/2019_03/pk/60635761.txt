<h> Eni pledges higher returns with buyback and fatter dividend <p> MILAN : Italian oil and gas group Eni promised investors higher returns on Friday through a share buyback and bigger dividend , pledging strong cash flow growth over the next four years . <p> In its 2019-2022 plan the company announced a four-year buyback programme with an initial allocation of 400 million euros this year and a 3.6 percent rise in its dividend to 0.86 euros in 2019 . <p> It said after this year the buyback would be 400 million euros per year with Brent prices between $60-65 per barrel but would rise to 800 million euros if Brent was above $65 a barrel . <p> Brent crude was trading around $67 a barrel on Friday . <p> " We will generate some 22 billion euros free cash over the plan in our upstream business , almost double our dividend need , " Chief Executive Claudio Descalzi said on a conference call . <p> Over the last year the world 's top oil and gas companies have come under pressure to return more cash to shareholders as profits and oil prices rise after a three-year crunch . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 3.5 percent per year to 2025 , Eni said , adding it expected 2.5 billion barrels of oil equivalent of new resources . <p> Eni , which in 2018 produced 1.85 million barrels per day , was struggling to replace reserves a decade ago but giant gas finds in Mozambique and Egypt have since given it the strongest discovery record in the industry . <p> The company , which produces more than half its oil and gas in Africa , has made a move to diversify away from the continent by clinching a series of deals in the Gulf region . <p> Eni will invest a total of around 33 billion euros in its four-year plan to 2022 and will plough 1.4 billion euros in new energy solutions . It plans to have more than 10 gigawatts of renewable capacity by 2030 . <p> It also said it would be net zero carbon neutral by 2030 .
