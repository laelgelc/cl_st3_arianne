<h> Pakistan asks UN to declare India an ' eco-terrorist ' country <p> A view of damage caused to trees in the hilly terrain after and Indian fighter plane dropped its payload in Balakot , Pakistan. -AFP <p> Pakistan on Friday submitted a dossier to the UN 's environment programme seeking to declare India an ' eco-terrorist ' for damaging trees in an attack in Balakot on February 26 . <p> The dossier was submitted by Malik Amin Aslam , Prime Minister Imran Khan 's adviser for climate change . It said that Indian jets dropped their payload in a forest before fleeing back after an intrusion into Pakistan 's territory . <p> Advisor to PM Malik Amin Aslam said the purpose of this document is to let the world know that Pakistan is serious in protecting its environment . <p> The dossier urged the international community to ask New Delhi to pay compensation money for causing damages to natural assets , especially trees and forests .
