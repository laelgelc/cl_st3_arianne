<h> Why UK and US are on roads to ruin <p> Why are Britain and America , the two greatest democracies of the 20th century , careening towards separate roads to ruin when China and Russia are seemingly in the ascent ? And what if anything can be done to reverse these situations ? <p> In the UK , the 2016 Brexit referendum has now rendered Prime Minister Theresa May and the Conservative Party impotent and the country , barring a reverse Dunkirk-like miracle , without any seemingly viable options for leaving the European Union . Meanwhile , the Labour Party under Jeremy Corbyn has become toxic over charges of anti-Semitism . <p> In America , Donald Trump 's hostile takeover has led the Republican Party to reject fiscal conservatism at home and active engagement abroad . Emboldened by winning back the House of Representatives and refreshed with a new crop of young politicians , traditional Democrats are being forced dramatically left with plans for Green New Deals and medical care and education for all that are unaffordable no matter how much the super rich are taxed . <p> Without a solution to Brexit , whether through delay or @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rate power .
