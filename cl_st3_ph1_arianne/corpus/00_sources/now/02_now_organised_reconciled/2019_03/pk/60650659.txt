Gen. ( R ) Muzammil Hussain at the Aiwan-e-Sadr , Islamabad on Tuesday . <p> According to a statement released from his office , President Dr. Arif Alvi said that Pakistan had the largest contiguous irrigation system in the world and added that the great Indus river system had historically been the lifeline of the Indus valley civilization . <p> He said , " As per capita surface water availability has declined from 5,260 cubic meters in 1951 to around 1,000 cubic meters in 2016 . He stated that this is likely to further drop to about 860 cubic meters by 2025 marking our transition from a ' water stressed ' to a ' water scarce ' country .
