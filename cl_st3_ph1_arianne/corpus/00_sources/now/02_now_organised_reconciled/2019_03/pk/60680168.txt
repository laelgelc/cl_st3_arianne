<h> Chill waters , arid land : climate change arrives on Easter Island <p> Ludovic Burns Tuki first felt the chill of climate change while diving in the waters around his home on Easter Island , locally known as Rapa Nui , a remote land mass in the South Pacific famed for its distinctive ancient stone statues . <p> The Polynesian diver has swum in these waters all his life , but found himself recently needing to wear a wet suit as water temperatures have dropped around the island , which sits isolated some 3,700 kilometers ( 2,299 miles ) west of continental Chile . <p> " We are facing a problem that is becoming a reality .
