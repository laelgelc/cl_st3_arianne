<h> CPNE voices concern at impacts of climate change <p> Dr Jabbar Khattak , the secretary-general of the Council of Pakistan Newspaper Editors ( CPNE ) , on Wednesday , expressed concern over the serious threats of climate change to Pakistan , especially when it was slated amongst the ten most vulnerable countries worldwide . <p> He along with other office-bearers of CPNE stressed the government to pay attention to this grave issue during an educational seminar the council organised on climate change in collaboration with UNDP , here in Lahore . <p> Provincial Minister , Muhammad Rizwan , also spoke on the occasion and claimed that the government was aware of the repercussions of climate change as well as its responsibilities in this regard . He further maintained that the Punjab government was acting under the guidance and vision of Prime Minister Imran Khan to utilise all possible means to combat climate change and create mass awareness . While appreciating the steps taken by the CPNE in this regard , Rizwan said that media 's role had become increasingly significant these days in creating public awareness .
