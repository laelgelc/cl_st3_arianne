<h> Trees provide guard against natural calamities , says Murad <h> Islamabad <p> Islamabad : Federal Minster for Communications &amp; Postal Services Murad Saeed has said that models roads shall be made available in the country through a forestation , beautification and cleanliness . This attempt will help provide environment friendly traveling to the people . Trees provide guard against natural calamities and disasters . As the chief guest , he was speaking to inauguration ceremony of Clean and Green Pakistan Week , here on Monday . <p> The week is being observed in 15 cities throughout the country simultaneously . The National Highway Authority is initiating this campaign with cooperation of PHA , ICT , FWO , Government of Punjab , CBR , SLMP , UNDP , Civil Society , Traders community and local administration . State Minister for Climate Change Zartaj Gul also participated . Besides Secretary Communications , Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner Rawalpindi and Islamabad , Chairman PHA , DIG Motorway Police , Brig Shamim Akram of FWO and representatives of UNDP and CBR were also present in the ceremony . Mr. Murad Saeed invited the youth in particular to come forward to make this campaign @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trees along motorways and national highways throughout the country . Recalling Memorandum of Understanding ( MOU ) earlier signed between NHA and Ministry of Climate Change he reiterated his commitment to achieve the targets within available resources without seeking funds from Ministry of Finance . Lahore-Abdul Hakeem Motorway having Intelligent Transport System ( ITS ) will be opened to traffic on March 30 , 2019 , he added . <p> Zartaj Gul in her address said , our country has received excess rains this year which is a symbol of blessing of the Allah Almighty . She said , Prime Minister of Pakistan Imran Khan is striving hard to put country on the track to development . She said , trees cast pleasant effects upon human life and we are to further this drive hand in hand to make it a success . <p> On this occasion , a MoU was also signed between National Highway Authority and Parks and Horticulture Authority Rawalpindi . Director Environment NHA and Director PHA signed the MOU . <p> A detailed briefing was also given to the chief guest regarding countrywide afforestation campaign . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ guests .
