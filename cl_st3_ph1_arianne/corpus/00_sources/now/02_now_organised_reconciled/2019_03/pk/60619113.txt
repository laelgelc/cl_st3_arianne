<h> Billion Tree Tsunami increased KP 's forest cover : PM <p> Prime Minister Imran Khan Sunday said the success of Billion Tree Tsunami had been unprecedented which led to increase the forest cover of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province by 4 percent . <p> He said the very success of Billion Tree Tsunami gave the government confidence to take the initiative to 10 Billion Tree Tsunami . <p> He said the KP 's forest cover had increased contributing to the fight against climate change . <p> The prime minister also shared four images , including his selfie with some grown trees in the backdrop . The other images showed the dense green forest and the schoolchildren holding saplings for plantation . <p> He said in a tweet : " From nurseries to full forest areas , the success of Billion Tree Tsunami has been unprecedented with public &amp; pvt involvement . Has given us confidence to take the initiative to 10 bn trees . KP 's forest cover has increased by 4% , contributing to the fight against climate change .
