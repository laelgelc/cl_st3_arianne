<h> Safe drinking water , sanitation key priority areas : President Alvi <p> by Staff Report , ( Last Updated March 21 , 2019 ) <p> ISLAMABAD : President Dr Arif Alvi has said that safe drinking water and sanitation are the key priority areas of the ' Clean Green Pakistan ' Movement . <p> The President was the Chief Guest at the inaugural ceremony of Procter &amp; Gamble ( P&amp;G ) Pakistan 's Paak Saaf Pakistan initiative announcement in support of Prime Minister 's ' Clean Green Pakistan ' Movement . <p> The President further said on the occasion , " Ensuring the health of our people requires that we invest inadequate infrastructure to increase access to safe and affordable drinking water as well as sanitation facilities . It is also very important to create awareness about conservation of water , sanitation and good health and hygiene practices at the grass-root levels . P&amp;G deserves to be commended for making continued efforts to help improve the health and hygiene of Pakistani communities . Ms. Zartaj Gul , Minister of State for Climate Change was the Guest of Honor .
