<p> An agriculture official said on Tuesday that air pollution is seriously damaging seasonal crops . <p> Addressing a conference titled ' Parliamentary Role in Meeting the Clean Air Challenge ' , organised by Clean Air Asia in Islamabad , the additional secretary of the ministry climate change , Babar Hayat Tarar , informed the parliamentarians present that air pollution is already causing a decline in wheat productivity in three strands . <p> During the conference , Adviser to the Prime Minister for Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam also criticised the non-calibrated air quality data provided by private sources and faulty satellite imagery depicting Pakistan as the second most polluted country in the world with alarming ratios of serious air pollutants . <p> Ali Touqeer Sheikh of Lead Pakistan spoke about how vehicular emissions are the worst offenders when it comes to causing air pollution . " 70% of air pollution is because of transportation emissions , " he explained . He said we need compatible data from different sectors . <p> " We need a National Action Plan on air quality and health , " said Shiekh . " <p> " Pakistan is at the frontline of climate change and we need to amplify the message that if we do n't act , we will have a world which will not be livable , " said Senator Sherry Rehman . <p> " Air quality in Pakistan is a public health emergency . We really need to understand that this is not a first-world abstraction . Air pollution is a bigger killer than terrorism . We need to pay attention to it and scale up our ability to measure it , adapt to it and reduce emissions , " she added . <p> She also remarked that air pollution impacts women , children , pregnant women and other vulnerable segments of society . <p> Aslam discussed the National Clean Air Policy and committed that he would do better regarding the issue of air pollution . He also announced that the World Bank would be helping finance and implement the clean air project .
