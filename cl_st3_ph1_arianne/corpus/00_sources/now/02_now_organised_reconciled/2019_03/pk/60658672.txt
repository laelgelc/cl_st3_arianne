<h> Punjab govt obtains development funding from NDRMF <p> LAHORE : The Punjab government has obtained development funding worth Rs 1353.171 million from the National Disaster Risk Management Fund ( NDRMF ) through efforts of the Planning and Development Board . <p> According to a handout issued here on Thursday , funding by the NDRMF is aimed at projects that improve the resilience to climate change and natural disasters which in turn result in sustainable socio-economic benefits . <p> Punjab Planning &amp; Development Board Chairman Habib ur Rehman Gillani said this grant would support people and result in prosperity and sustainable development . <p> The projects from Punjab that got funded include rehabilitation of Hajipur Gujran flood protection bund , **26;4397;TOOLONG of Jalala flood protection bund , protection of village locality Shahpur , Chanjura , Fatehpur Gujran and Sukho Chak against erosive action of Bein Nulla and protection of infrastructure , localities and agricultural land of villages Jagojil and Baryar from erosion of River Ravi downstream .
