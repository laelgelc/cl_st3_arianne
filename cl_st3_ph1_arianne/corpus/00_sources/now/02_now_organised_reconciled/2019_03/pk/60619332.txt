<h> Success of billion tree tsunami led to 10 bn tree initiative : PM <h> Top Story <p> ISLAMABAD : Prime Minister Imran Khan said on Sunday that the success of the billion tree tsunami gave the government confidence to take the 10 billion trees initiative . <p> The PM in a tweet said : " From nurseries to full forest areas , the success of Billion Tree Tsunami has been unprecedented with public &amp; pvt involvement . Has given us confidence to take the initiative to 10 bn trees . KP 's forest cover has increased by 4% , contributing to the fight against climate change . " Under PM Imran , Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf ( PTI ) , which came to power this year , pledged to plant billions of trees to tackle the effects of climate change .
