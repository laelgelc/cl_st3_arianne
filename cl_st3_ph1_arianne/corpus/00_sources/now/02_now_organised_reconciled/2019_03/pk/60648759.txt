<p> ISLAMABAD : Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul Wazir Tuesday said government was ready to help the affected families of recent terrorist attack in New Zealand . <p> Talking to private news channel , she said government would honour them with special bravery awards as they sacrificed their lives while saving lives of people offering prayer those who taken bullets on their chests while performing their duties . <p> Responding to a question she said Pakistan was a peace loving country which gave the message across the globe that our country being an Islamic state never wanted wars , but to being honoured as a martyred while saving lives of others . <p> She further said , " It was time to challenge these type of attacks being a sister of my martyred brother I strongly condemned such incidents " . <p> " I feel honour and pride while sitting with these brave families and I pray may Almighty Allah rest them in eternal peace . "
