<h> Agreement on Clean Green Pakistan initiative <h> Islamabad <p> Islamabad : The privately-owned Reckitt Benckiser company has committed an investment of Rs1 billion for the government 's ' Clean Green Pakistan ' movement under the Company 's social vision of Hoga Saaf Pakistan . <p> This announcement was made by Fahad Ashraf , Chief Executive , RB Pakistan in a press briefing held at a local school . Malik Amin Aslam , adviser to the Prime Minister on Climate Change and Siddiq Ahmed , Country Director WaterAid Pakistan , were also present as part of the collaboration between RB , Ministry of Climate Change and WaterAid Pakistan . <p> The officials visited the School Program , one of the initiatives undertaken by RB under the Clean Green Pakistan ( CGP ) movement . This program aims to educate over 2 million school going children about cleanliness , health , and hygiene . <p> Talking to the media , Fahad Ashraf said , ' We at RB believe that as corporate citizens we have a responsibility to drive a positive change in the country .
