<h> Pak to pursue eco-terrorism charges against Indian pilots for razing trees <p> The Forestry Department , on Friday , registered a complaint against pilots of the Indian Air Force ( IAF ) for destroying trees in the Balakot area . <p> According to the information available from media sources , the First Information Report ( FIR ) further detailed the damage suffered by 19 trees after Indian aircraft " hastily dropped their payload " . <p> Islamabad has also announced plans to lodge a complaint against India at the UN , accusing New Delhi of committing eco-terrorism . <p> Climate Change Minister Malik Amin Aslam had said that Indian warplanes had bombed a " forest reserve " and the government was undertaking an environmental impact assessment , which would be used to register a complaint at the UN among other forums . <p> He exclaimed , " What happened over there is environmental terrorism . " <p> While making note of the dozens of pine trees that had been razed , the minister maintained " There has been serious environmental damage .
