<h> Ahmadiyya leader warns of nuclear war in keynote address to hundreds of delegates <p> The global leader of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community warned of the " critical need for peace " at the community 's 16th annual Peace Symposium this Saturday ( March 9 ) . <p> His Holiness Hazrat Mirza Masroor Ahmad , the Caliph of Ahmadiyya Muslims around the world , highlighted the proliferation of nuclear weapons and the dangers posed by climate change and extremism as key threats to peace globally during his keynote address . <p> Over 800 hundred delegates from 30 countries attended the event , which was held at the Baitul Futuh Mosque in Morden , South West London , to listen to the Caliph speak . <p> The Surrey Comet was invited to the Symposium as a guest of the Ahmadiyya Muslim Community UK ( AMA UK ) , and asked Hazrat Mirza Masroor Ahmad what he considered the most serious threats to peace in the UK and around the world . <p> The Caliph hinted at the content of his keynote address , where he focused on the renewed threat of nuclear war . <p> Hazrat Mirza Masroor Ahmad said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are trigger happy and appear not to appreciate the truly grave consequences of nuclear warfare . <p> " Not only do such weapons have the power to annihilate the countries targeted , but also have the potential to destroy the peace and stability of the entire world . <p> " Thus it is imperative that nations and their leaders not only focus on their own national interests but consider what is best for the world at large .
