<h> Muir of Ord flea market set for Mother 's Day launch as zero-waste drive stepped up <p> MUM 's the word for a Ross-shire woman determined to play her part making the world a less wasteful place . <p> Lynsey Burns is founding what she believes to be the first zero-waste flea market in the Highlands -- and has chosen Mother 's Day to launch it . <p> She said : " It is our goal to demonstrate that you do n't have to buy new and when you do shop it is great to shop local . You can have a beautiful wardrobe of clothes and furnish your home without shopping at fast fashion outlets and big brand stores . <p> " The Highlands has an amazing array of charity shops and warehouses , beautiful producers and a huge selection of local produce . We can reduce our carbon footprint by shifting our buying behaviour and make a small impact to the fight against climate change -- while having a great consumer experience . " <p> She 's dubbing it the Moo(n) Market , inspired by the work of the MOO Food community group active in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Muir of Ord village hall from noon to 2pm on Sunday , March 31 . <p> Lynsey added : " Inspired by the work of MOO Food , this is my effort to make a difference . With Mother 's Day in mind there will be some lovely stalls selling flowers , chocolates and eco-friendly , self-care products . All plastic-free and kind to the planet . <p> " We encourage shoppers to bring their own containers and bags . " The markets will be held on the last Sunday of each month . <p> The award-winning Bad Girl Bakery will be there to provide tea , coffee and cakes . <p> Her ultimate goal is to have a permanent venue where shoppers can browse , eat and socialise.
