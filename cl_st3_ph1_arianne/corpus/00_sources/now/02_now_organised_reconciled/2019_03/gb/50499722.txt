<h> Arctic researcher whose work led to discovery of wrecked whaling ship to speak in Sunderland <p> THE Arctic has been described as the " canary in the mine " by a researcher who will be speaking in the region this month . <p> Matthew Ayre , who works at the Arctic Institution of North America , based at the University of Calgary in Canada , will be sharing some of his work on the British whaling trade and its connection with the current Arctic climate . <p> The 31-year-old , from North Tyneside , who did his PhD at the University of Sunderland , is returning to the city for the talk on Thursday , March 21 . <p> He said : " I 'm really looking forward to returning to Sunderland to discuss climate change which is the largest challenge facing humanity , and the Arctic is the canary in the mine . <p> " For nearly 40 years we have witnessed a near continual and increasing downward trend in the extent and thickness of Arctic sea ice . <p> Scottish whaling ship Nova Zembla , which was found thanks to Matthew Ayre 's research <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
