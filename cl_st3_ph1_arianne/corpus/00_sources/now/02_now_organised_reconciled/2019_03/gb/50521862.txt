<h> New ' compelling evidence ' of illegal killing of protected birds <h> New ' compelling evidence ' of illegal killing of protected birds <p> A NEW study has uncovered " compelling evidence " of the illegal killing of protected birds on grouse moors , it is claimed . <p> The claim is based on 10 years ' worth of data centred around hen harriers in the north of England . Many of the raptors had a range which took them into southern Scotland and beyond . <p> Aberdeen University , working with colleagues at South Africa 's University of Cape Town , analysed records produced by satellite tags on almost 60 birds . <p> They found the chances of hen harriers dying or disappearing over areas predominantly covered by grouse moor was ten times higher than areas without the sporting estates . <p> More than 70% of tagged harriers were either confirmed or considered very likely to have been illegally killed . <p> Speaking to The National , Professor Steve Redpath , chair in conservation science at Aberdeen University , said : " We found the tags were suddenly failing on grouse moors at a much higher rate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an alternative explanation . <p> " What do we do about it ?
