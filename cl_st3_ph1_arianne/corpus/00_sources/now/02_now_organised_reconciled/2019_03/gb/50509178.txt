What was left was smashed into thousands upon thousands of disconnected pieces , and the inhabitants of this world had to piece together their understanding of science and technology from what was lost , trying to line up the remnants of the earlier age as best they could . <p> Scrabbling , ignorant , and in the darkness , they would sometimes get things right . More often , however , they would get things seriously wrong . Most of all , they had lost any sense of science as a system , depriving them not only of the existing knowledge , but how to generate new ideas and make new discoveries . <p> It 's an image that haunts me , repeatedly , when surveying the state of the left today .
