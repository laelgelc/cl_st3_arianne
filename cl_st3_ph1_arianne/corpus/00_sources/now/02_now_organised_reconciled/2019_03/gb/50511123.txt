<p> According to the House of Lords Select Committee on Communications conventional TV viewing fell by 5% last year , while between 2015 and 2018 the number of UK homes with access to a subscription VOD service has doubled . <p> The Lords Committee has opened calls to organisations and individuals to submit evidence on the state of the UK public service broadcasting ( PSB ) seeking to address if broadcasters have done enough to compete in the growing VOD content landscape . <p> Written evidence is to be submitted by Friday 26 April aiming to answer three main questions : What is the value of public service broadcasting , is the concept becoming outdated and does public service broadcasting do enough to reflect and serve the demographics of the UK ? <p> The investigation will examine the consequences of the rise of VOD services and the declining audiences of linear TV viewing for the production of original UK content for UK audiences . <p> The House of Lords chairman of the committee Lord Gilbert of Panteg said : " PSBs must fulfil a range of obligations , including on the volume and type of adverts they show , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the audiences they serve and the watershed . <p> " On-demand services do not have these obligations and it has been suggested that these big budget productions are pricing public service broadcasters out of the market by inflating production costs .
