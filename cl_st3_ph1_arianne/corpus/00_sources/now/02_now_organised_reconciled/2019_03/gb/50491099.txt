<p> THE recent , unseasonal warm weather brought all sorts of wildlife out much earlier than usual - including bees ; one can only hope any drop in temperature now back to more usual levels for the time of the year will not have an impact on their numbers . <p> Help is at hand however for the under threat bee , as well as other essential pollinators , such as butterflies and hoverflies . <p> The Clapham based charity , The Yorkshire Dales Millennium Trust ( YDMT ) is calling on community groups to ' Bee Together ' and join a new project to create wildflower meadows to create ' insect stepping stones ' all the way from Leeds to Lancaster . <p> And , all that is needed is some willing volunteers , and an accessible piece of public open space , which can be about the size of a tennis court .
