<h> Letter of the Day : Appreciate scale of problem <h> Letter of the Day : Appreciate scale of problem <p> SIR -- I would like to publicly thank the organisers of the youth climate strike in Worcester on March 15 . <p> This was part of a global movement inspired by the Nobel Peace Prize nominee Greta Thunberg who , aged 15 years old , said at a United Nations climate conference : " You are not mature enough to tell it like is . Even that burden you leave to us children . " <p> On March 5 , I asked Worcester City Council 's environment committee to support the climate strike . Only three councillors out of 10 voted to support it , including the chairman of the committee . <p> The overwhelming majority of climate scientists around the world believe that humanity is responsible for climate change . The UN 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change issued a report last year that concluded that we have 12 years to massively reduce carbon emissions .
