<h> Timperley park dedicated as a protected site in memory of World War One dead <p> A Timperley park has been dedicated as a protected site in memory of those who lost their lives in World War One . <p> A large area of Newton Park has been dedicated as a Centenary Field by Fields in Trust , supported by the Royal British Legion . <p> The Centenary Fields programme protects parks and green spaces forever to honour the memory of the millions of men and women who fought in the Great War . <p> The nomination for the dedication was put forward by a local resident and supported by the Friends of Newton Park . In response , Trafford Council applied to Fields in Trust for the selected area of the park to be dedicated . <p> Newton Park in Timperley <p> The Friends of Newton Park plan to hold an event on May 24 to celebrate the commemoration . <p> Cllr Stephen Adshead , Trafford Council 's Executive Member for Environment , Air Quality and Climate Change , said : " The Centenary Fields programme is very important .
