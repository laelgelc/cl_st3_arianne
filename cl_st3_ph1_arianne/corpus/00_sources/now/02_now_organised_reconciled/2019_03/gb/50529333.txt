<h> Vega Rocket Launches Earth-Watching PRISMA Satellite for Italy <h> It lit up the sky over French Guiana . <p> A European Vega rocket carried a new Earth-observation satellite into orbit for the Italian Space Agency late Thursday ( March 21 ) , lighting up the nighttime sky over its French Guiana launch site . <p> The PRISMA Earth observation satellite lifted off at 9:50 p.m . EDT ( 0150 March 22 GMT ) from the South America 's Guiana Space Center in Kourou , French Guiana . The satellite will operate in a sun-synchronous orbit , meaning that it circles the Earth in such a way that the sun is always in the same position as the satellite takes pictures of the planet below . <p> PRISMA ( an Italian acronym for Hyperspectral Precursor of the Application Mission ) is designed to provide information about environmental monitoring , resources management , pollution and crop health . The satellite includes a medium resolution camera that can view across all visual wavelengths , as well as a hyperspectral imager that can capture a wider range of wavelengths between 400 and 2500 nanometers . <p> " The mission can provide a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the study of key environmental processes , such as interaction between atmosphere , biosphere and hydrosphere , observation of global climate change and effects of human activities ecosystems , " said the Italian Space Agency in a 2017 statement announcing the mission . <p> PRISMA was designed and manufactured by an Italian consortium led by OHB ITALIA SpA and LEONARDO SpA . This is the third Vega mission of 2019 and the 14th since the launcher began operations at Guiana in 2012 , Arianespace said in a statement .
