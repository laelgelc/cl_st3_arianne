<h> Rebecca Pow voted chairman of government group encouraging use of electric vehicles <h> Rebecca Pow voted chairman of electric vehicles government group <p> TAUNTON Deane 's MP has been elected as chairman of a government group which aims to encourage usage of electric vehicles . <p> Rebecca Pow has been announced as the new chairman of the All-Party Parliamentary Group on electric vehicles . <p> She was elected after being nominated by the former chairman , Dame Cheryl Gillan MP . <p> Dame Cheryl will continue on as vice chairman after growing the membership to more than 40 parliamentarians over the past year . <p> Ms Gillan said : " It has been extremely rewarding to chair the APPG through its first year . <p> " In the past 14 months , membership has expanded greatly , our events have been a successful and well-attended , and the group has become an important forum for discussion within parliament and the industry . <p> " Now is an exciting time for not only the group but for the electric vehicles sector as a whole .
