<h> Cities across world go dark to mark Earth Hour <h> Cities across world go dark to mark Earth Hour <p> Cities around the world have been marking Earth Hour by turning off the lights for 60 minutes in a call for global action on climate change . <p> The Earth Hour movement aims to create greater awareness and more sparing use of resources , especially fossil fuels that produce carbon gases and lead to global warming . <p> Beginning in Sydney in 2007 , Earth Hour has now spread to more than 180 countries , with tens of millions of people joining in . <p> In Paris , the lights on the Eiffel Tower -- which normally dominate the city 's skyline after dark -- were turned off for the event , from 8.30pm local time . <p> In Berlin , the Brandenburg Gate was plunged into darkness -- illuminated only by the lights of environmental campaigners depicting a map of the world . <p> In the Polish capital Warsaw , the landmark Palace of Culture and Science turned off its night illumination , along with some churches and Old Town walls . <p> Acropolis hill in Greece @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ off to mark Earth Hour ( Yorgos Karahalis/AP ) <p> Lights were also switched off in several landmarks in the Greek capital . The Acropolis , Athens City Hall and Lycabettus Hill , which towers above the city , went dark and the Parliament building also joined in . <p> As night fell in Hong Kong , major buildings along Victoria Harbour turned off their non-essential lights and the city 's popular tourist attraction known as the Symphony of Lights was cancelled . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
