our everyday lives can be a useful corrective to the obsessive attention that is paid to the more newsworthy , but frequently more ephemeral national drama . <p> But sometimes the corrective we should apply is instead to widen the angle of our lens so that we can observe the larger scene beyond our shores . <p> I was reminded of this at the end of the last , again , rather tumultuous week in
