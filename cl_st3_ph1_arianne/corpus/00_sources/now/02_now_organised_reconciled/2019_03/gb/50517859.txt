<h> Post navigation <h> Online Tool Highlights " Hotspots " Of Unsustainable Practices <p> UN Environment has launched a new tool that allows countries to see " hotspots " of unsustainable practices in consumption and production . <p> The tool , known as the Sustainable Consumption and Production Hotspots Analysis Tool , is an online application that analyses the environmental and socio-economic performance of 171 countries over the past 25 years to provide scientific evidence of areas where improvement can be made . <p> The tool builds on a powerful national footprint calculator that combines environmental and socio-economic data with trade information . It allows the tracing of environmental pressures and impacts along the supply chain of the goods and services consumed within a given country , an essential dimension of our globalized economy . <p> " For a long time , we have known that we need to change the way we produce and consume goods and services to make better use of the planet 's natural resources . Now we have a tool that pinpoints where we need to be taking action if we want to make truly impactful change , " said Ligia Noronha , Director @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Director of UN Environment 's Economy Division -- " For a long time , we have known that we need to change the way we produce and consume goods and services to make better use of the planet 's natural resources . " <p> " It is appropriate to launch this new tool against the backdrop of the Fourth UN Environment Assembly , where we are focusing on innovative solutions for environmental challenges and sustainable consumption and production . <p> " In that spirit , we hope that this innovative hotspots analysis tool helps put us on a more equitable and sustainable path . " <p> The tool integrates a wide range of data on raw material use , greenhouse gas emissions and climate change , air pollution and health , land use and biodiversity loss .
