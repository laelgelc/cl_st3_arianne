<h> ' Wallasea 's wild coast is one of the the best around ! ' <h> ' Wallasea 's wild coast is one of the the best around ! ' <p> THE Wallasea Island Wild Coast project has been awarded a Green Heart Hero award at a ceremony in Parliament . <p> Jeff Knott , RSPB 's Regional Director for Eastern England , travelled to the Houses of Parliament to attend the awards ceremony where he received the Outdoor Innovation award on behalf of the project . <p> The awards -- which range from accolades for green schools , businesses and MPs in recognition of sustainable sports projects and overseas schemes -- were hosted by television and radio presenter Clive Anderson , and featured Sky News journalist Kay Burley , alongside a number of others . <p> Mr Knott said : " It 's great that decades of partnership work at Wallasea has received this recognition . It shows that we can work together to create inspiring new landscapes that deliver for people and wildlife . <p> " Climate change and loss of our wildlife are existential threats facing us all , but all the exciting projects and people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can deliver solutions to these twin crises . <p> " To secure the future of our shared home , we must continue to do so at even greater scale and the commitment and passion of everyone at the awards ceremony have me convinced that we will . " <p> The annual Green Heart Hero Awards are coordinated by The Climate Coalition , a group of more than 130 organisations -- including the Women 's Institute , CAFOD , National Trust , WWF and the RSPB -- who together represent over 15 million people nationwide . <p> Clive Anderson said : " The awards are the finale to a month long celebration of the things we love that could be lost forever to climate change . <p> " The campaign has been turning hearts green for Valentine 's Day for the last five years and this year 's campaign has reached more hearts than ever before .
