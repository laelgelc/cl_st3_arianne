<h> Trump 's 2020 NASA Budget Will Take US from Moon to Mars , Agency Chief Says <p> NASA Administrator Jim Bridenstine praised the agency 's 2020 budget proposal from the Trump administration today ( March 11 ) , describing how the budget will help the agency reach the moon , and then continue on to Mars . <p> This path is a common refrain for Bridenstine , who as NASA administrator is tasked with executing the president 's Space Policy Directive 1 , which called for a renewed focus on human exploration , including a journey to the moon and eventually Mars . <p> " For the first time in over 10 years , we have money in this budget for a return to the moon with humans , " Bridenstine said in a presentation at NASA 's Kennedy Space Center in Florida . " I 'm talking human-rated landers , compatible with Gateway , that can go back and forth to the surface of the moon .
