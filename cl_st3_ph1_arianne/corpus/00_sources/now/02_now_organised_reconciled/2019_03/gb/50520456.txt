Among the most important : How can the oil behemoth keep up with the times ? <p> " There 's a lot of doom and gloom about the sector , and what 's happening in the world in general , and all sorts of things , " Looney said . " A lot of your days get consumed in stuff like that , and to spend an hour with Connor , where he is the best and the brightest , it 's energizing . " <p> Tann , who has a masters in experimental and theoretical physics from the University of Cambridge , is the picture of what Big Oil wants to become : smart , progressive and tech savvy .
