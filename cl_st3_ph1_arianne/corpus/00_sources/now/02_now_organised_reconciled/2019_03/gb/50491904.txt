<p> An invasive bug with a taste for beans has jumped into the top 10 list of the worst garden pests as it benefited from the warm summer . <p> The Royal Horticultural Society ( RHS ) said its latest annual list of which pests and diseases are causing the most trouble in gardens revealed the impacts of the hot dry weather last summer . <p> The southern green shield bug , which is thought to have been introduced from mainland Europe and was established here in the 2000s , came sixth in 2018 's ranking based on inquiries to the RHS by gardeners . <p> The bug is a sap feeder with some preference for vegetables , particularly beans , and benefited from the warm weather in early summer , the experts said . <p> Young stages of the bug are distinctively black with white or pink markings and an adult looks similar to the harmless native green shield bug but does not have a brown marking at its rear . <p> Pear rust is also of growing concern to gardeners , with the fungus that causes orange hedges as box tree caterpillars spread to Wales and Northern Ireland . <p> The caterpillar , which strips box of its leaves , topped the list for the worst pests for the third time , the experts said
