<p> The head of the Environment Agency is warning that England could run out of water in just 25 years . <p> Sir James Bevan is set to announce that because of the country 's rising population , we are now in the " jaws of death " because of increased demand . <p> He says the country 's rising population is on the verge of exceeding the falling supply of water . Climate change is also a factor , he said . <p> He said : " Around 25 years from now , where those demand and supply lines cross is known by some as the ' jaws of death ' -- the point at which we will not have enough water to supply our needs , unless we take action to change things . " <p> He said he wants water waste to be as taboo as " blowing smoke into a baby 's face " or " throwing plastic bags into the sea .
