<h> Our comments on Future of Urban Mobility <p> We commented on the Government 's Future of Urban Mobility strategy , announced as part of the Industrial Strategy . <h> James Diggle , CBI Head of Energy and Climate Change , said : <p> " The UK has an opportunity to be a global leader in the shift to zero-emission transport systems . <p> " Businesses are already committed to this revolution , using the UK 's expertise in research , standards and innovation to develop mobility solutions that will improve the movement of people and goods across our towns and cities . <p> " A review of regulation is needed to ensure that it supports innovation and the development of new technologies . " <p> Labour 's plans to renationalise parts of the UK 's infrastructure , utilities and public services will cause profound harm to the country 's economy and hurt the very people they are intended to help , the CBI 's Director-General says .
