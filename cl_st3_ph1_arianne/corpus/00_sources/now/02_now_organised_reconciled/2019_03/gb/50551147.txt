Just make sure these actions have long-term considerations in place and are n't just a knee-jerk reaction that ultimately drives consumerism . <p> Keep moving in uncertain times <p> It 's very rare to read a piece about Brexit and not see the word " uncertainty " dotted throughout . Organisations could and have used the Brexit negotiations to stand still and reassess situations largely related to finance and business growth . <p> Banks and automakers have acted quickly , moving production and headquarters out of the UK as a response to projections of a post-Brexit Britain .
