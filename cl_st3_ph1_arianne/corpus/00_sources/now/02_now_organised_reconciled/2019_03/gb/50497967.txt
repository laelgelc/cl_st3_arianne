<h> Explore the stealth-wealth resort of Verbier <p> VERBIER is a peculiar beast . Although this Swiss ski resort is popular with some of the world 's wealthiest people , there are no Prada or Chanel shops here ( unlike in , say , Courchevel ) and no Ferraris . Even though regulars include Richard Branson , various international royals and James Blunt , you could easily spend weeks here and be none the wiser . The vibe is loaded but discreet . <p> Alpine retreat : the Experimental Chalet <p> That 's why the resort 's latest opening is such a good fit .
