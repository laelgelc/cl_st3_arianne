<h> Big Bang Fair makes explosive impression on hundreds of students <p> BUDDING scientists from across Essex took part in the sixth annual Big Bang Fair . <p> In partnership with Engineering UK , the event at Essex University showcased the career paths available in Science , Technology , Engineering and Maths ( STEM ) subjects . <p> More than 900 Year 9 students had the opportunity to engage with businesses , take part in interactive workshops and challenge themselves to think differently about STEM subjects . <p> This year 's theme was Preserving the Planet and students attended workshops including Bees , Bees , Bees - the impact of climate change , Science in the Wild , Space Innovation , designing the perfect housing development and a Science Escape Room . <p> Education Outreach Officer Jenna Gifford said : " This year 's Big Bang Fair at the University of Essex was one of our largest , and we 're delighted that so many schools and students took part in the day . <p> " These sort of events can have such an impact on a student 's perspective in subjects that have such wide ranging possibilities attached @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was a chance to see science , technology , engineering and maths in action . " <p> The fair was also an opportunity for university academics to showcase new research . <p> They included from the School of Computer Science and Electronic Engineering , School of Health and Social Care , Department of Psychology , School of Sport , Rehabilitation and Exercise Sciences , School of Biological Sciences , and the Edge Hotel School . <p> Among the many external organisations taking part in the event were EDF Energy , Public Health England , GSK , Royal Navy , Institution of Civil Engineers , Zeelandia , Colchester Zoo , Royal Astronomical Society , National Space Academy , BT , Essex Wildlife Trust and Anglian Water . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
