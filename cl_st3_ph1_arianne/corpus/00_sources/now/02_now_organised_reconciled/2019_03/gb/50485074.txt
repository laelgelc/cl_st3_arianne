<h> ' It is no secret that farming is the backbone of the Welsh food and drinks sector ' <h> FUW president stresses that farming matters <p> LAST week we celebrated St David 's Day and all that is great and wonderful about Wales , including our unique culture . <p> Up and down the country , people took to the streets in traditional costumes , participating in parades and many will have enjoyed the finest Welsh food and drink . <p> Part of our culture is , of course agriculture , which has to be appreciated for its uniqueness and the almost 60,000 people who are employed either full or part time on farms in Wales . <p> Indeed , Wales is a country of some 2.1 million hectares of land , of which 88 per cent ( 1.8 million hectares ) is managed by farmers and is home to 16,500 farms . <p> It is no secret that farming is the backbone of the Welsh food and drinks sector , which employs 222,400 people , 17 per cent of the Welsh workforce . <p> What we can take from this is that farming matters . <p> It @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ mentioned as such in the Welsh Government 's Economic Action Plan . <p> However , looking at the direction of travel outlined , with some arguing that this plan places a greater emphasis on tackling inequality , it worryingly signals a shift towards a ' something for something ' relationship with business . <p> And whilst that may be a suitable approach for some sectors , arguing that agriculture needs to do ' something ' in order to be supported , is baffling . <p> It already does . <p> Let 's be absolutely clear - Welsh family farms do so much more than just produce food . <p> By providing employment and economic growth in rural areas , the farming sector mitigates rural depopulation , protects rural culture and heritage and provides a means by which the Welsh language can be maintained . <p> In addition , Welsh food production sustains tens of thousands of other businesses from upstream businesses such as feed merchants , agricultural contractors and engineers , to downstream businesses such as hauliers , processors and retailers . <p> It will not come as surprise therefore @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 60 is spent within seven miles of the farm . <p> Managing over 80 per cent of Wales ' landmass , farmers play an invaluable part in managing and preserving a landscape which provides clean drinking water for millions , is diverse in habitat and species and includes more than 1,000 Sites of Special Scientific Interest ( SSSIs ) . <p> Other benefits include major contributions to negating the causes and symptoms of climate change , whether through the storage of carbon in farm woodland , hedgerows and peat bogs , or the generation of green electricity through on-farm wind and water turbines . <p> The fact remains that all of these public goods are provided by farmers on the back of food production . <p> Failing to recognise that and the wider economic benefits it brings for Wales and beyond and the solid role it plays as a foundational economy will do little in creating prosperity for all in Wales . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
