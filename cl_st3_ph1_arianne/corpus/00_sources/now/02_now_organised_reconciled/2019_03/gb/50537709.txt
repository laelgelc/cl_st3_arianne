<p> The Scottish Government has been urged to ban fracking after a legal opinion indicating it is within the power of MSPs . <p> Ministers announced an " effective ban " on fracking in 2017 . However , a legal challenge was mounted by Petrochemical firm Ineos . <p> A Court of Session ruling last June found that , as a matter of law , there is no prohibition against fracking in Scotland . <p> A legal opinion published by Aidan O'Neill QC now suggests that the Scottish Parliament has the legislative competence to pass a fracking ban . <p> It indicates doing so would be less likely to result in successful legal challenges from firms with an interest in the industry . <p> Friends of the Earth Scotland , who commissioned Mr O'Neill , say that the Scottish Government must now act to definitively ban fracking . <p> " Communities on the frontline of this dirty industry have been waiting for over four years for the Scottish Government to bring its long drawn-out process on unconventional oil and gas to an end , " said Friends of the Earth Scotland head of campaigns Mary Church . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rhetoric , " she said . <p> A Scottish Government spokesman said further assessments would be required before finalisation of policy on the issue . <p> " The Scottish Government does not consider new legislation is necessary to control unconventional oil and gas development in Scotland , and the adoption of a strong policy would provide appropriate and proportionate means to regulate such development , " he said . <p> " The practical effect of the current moratorium , and the policy-making process currently under way , is that no fracking or other unconventional oil and gas activity can take place in Scotland at this time . " <p> Scottish Labour 's spokeswoman for climate change and environment Claudia Beamish MSP said : " This legal opinion shows Holyrood not only has the powers , but crucially that a ban in primary legislation is the surer way to defeat future legal challenges . "
