<p> A row of " beach hut " houses will be built as part of a waterfront regeneration -- despite a call for the colourful homes to be halted because " Granton is not Saint-Tropez " . <p> The city council 's development management sub-committee granted planning permission to Edinburgh Marine Holdings to build 144 apartments and 24 houses at two plots of the wider Granton Waterfront project -- despite concerns over too much car parking being provided . <p> The agreed housing to be built is made up on 61 one-bedroom flats , 63 two-bedroom flats , 20 three-bedroom flats and 18 three-bedroom " beach hut " houses . <p> Two blocks will be six stories high and contain 66 flats and will " mirror each other " . A 2,800 sqm area of open space will be constructed in the middle of the site to separate the blocks . Another block of flats , which will front a new road , Ross Kesrel Drive , will be six stories high and contain 12 apartments . <p> A row of 18 two-storey terraced houses will be built at the north of the site , which will be different @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ front onto Hesperus Crossway .
