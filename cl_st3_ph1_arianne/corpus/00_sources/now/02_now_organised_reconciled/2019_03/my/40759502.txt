<h> Govt to put waste-to-energy management system out to tender <p> PETALING JAYA : The government will hold an open tender exercise for companies interested in developing a waste-to-energy ( WTE ) management systems for safer waste disposal in the country . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister , Yeo Bee Yin , said the government is sorting out the tender details which will be ready in three months ' time . <p> " After that , we will launch a request for proposal , " she told Bernama today , after opening the Pharmacy Renaissance Summit by the Malaysian Community Pharmacy Guild ( MCPG ) , supported by among others the BP Healthcare Group . <p> Yeo said the time had come for the country to modernise its waste management system and shift away from landfills to a better waste management solution <p> In her speech , Yeo said the chemical pollution incident in Pasir Gudang should serve as a lesson on how every kind of waste , particularly scheduled waste , must be managed with caution . <p> " Malaysia needs a paradigm shift in terms of how we monitor our @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Malaysians need to be more responsible , and as a government , how can we better enforce , " she said . <p> She praised MCPG 's Green Pharmacy pilot project which was begun in 2017 and aimed at empowering pharmacists in educating the public on sharps waste ( hypodermic needles etc ) disposal . <p> In her speech , MCPG organising chairman and president , Lovy Beh , said the initiative , started with 30 community pharmacists in Penang , was supported by the Penang state government . <p> She said many people do not know how and where to dispose of sharps waste , as well as unwanted or expired medicine , and many a time it would be thrown out with household waste or flushed down the toilet . <p> " Such an act is dangerous because disease can be easily transmitted , " she added . -- Bernama
