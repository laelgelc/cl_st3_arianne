<h> Environment minister declares Sungai Kim Kim safe <p> Sungai Kim Kim in Pasir Gudang , which was polluted following the dumping of chemical waste 13 days ago , is now confirmed clean and safe , according to Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> However , it would be monitored for 15 days to ensure the situation was really safe , especially to the public . <p> " Even now , I no longer wear a mask , but the monitoring is crucial because we have to take into account the unknown . <p> " Overall , it is clean but we will monitor to convince the people that the river is safe , " she told reporters at the first location of the dumping of the chemical wastes today . <p> Yeo said the cleaning works were complete and the contractors were now in the process of transferring the chemical waste collected to a disposal area . <p> The process of cleaning the affected 1.5 km stretch of the river , which started on March 13 and ended fully on March 17 , involved many quarters , including @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Rescue Department . <p> Yeo also said that the scientific committee report , which encompasses data analysis on the pollution as well as methods and solutions used to clean the river , would be announced tomorrow . <p> When asked on whether the cleaning works would affect the natural flow of the river , she said : " Anything related to the science of removal , we will give a full report tomorrow . <p> " Tomorrow we will give details on whether this ( river ) flows and other things , how we see things , how we calculate . Tomorrow we will answer . " <p> As much as 900 metric tonnes of polluted soil together with sludge and 1,500 metric tonnes of polluted water had been collected by the appointed contractors . <p> The process of transporting the waste in stages is expected to be completed by March 21 . <p> - Bernama <h> Share this story <p> To post or ' like ' comments on Malaysiakini stories , you will need to sign in with an active paid subscription .
