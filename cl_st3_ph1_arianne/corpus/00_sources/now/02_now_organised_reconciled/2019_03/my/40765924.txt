<h> Pillars of Sabah launch in conjunction with Earth Hour <p> The Pillars of Sabah project near Suria Sabah shopping mall in Kota Kinabalu . <p> KOTA KINABALU : The Pillars of Sabah community art project is currently undergoing a renewal . <p> It is proudly sponsored by WWF-Malaysia and Nippon Paint ( Sabah ) , with the support of Sabah Art Gallery under the Sabah Cultural Board , Sabah Tourism Board , Ministry of Tourism , Culture and Environment Sabah as well as Kota Kinabalu City Hall . <p> Occupying an abandoned pre-war site in the heart of Kota Kinabalu , Pillars of Sabah was established with the aim to bring together the local community through art , and in the process highlight issues relevant to Sabah . <p> Involving only artists from the Sabahan community , the project would also serve as a vital platform for these artists who may not otherwise have any opportunity for exhibition . <p> Since its inaugural launch in September 2018 , the project has been well-received , creating a new , popular place of interest in the city for locals and tourists alike . <p> Not only has it succeeded in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ but it has also inspired the community towards a sense of active appreciation for art and its message . <p> The message this time round is that of environmental awareness . <p> Thirty new artists have come together to highlight threatened animal species found in Sabah . <p> To be launched in conjunction with Earth Hour on March 30 , featured animals will include the Sunda pangolin ( Manis javanica ) , Sumatran rhinoceros ( Dicerorhinus sumatrensis ) , Bornean sun bear ( Helarctos malayanus euryspilus ) , as well as Borneo pygmy elephant ( Elephas maximus borneensis ) . <p> This project comes at a time when global loss of biodiversity can no longer be ignored . <p> Exacerbated by anthropogenic climate change and other disturbances on the natural environment , the planet is currently experiencing a sixth mass extinction event in the planet 's history . <p> The first edition of the project highlighted members of the Sabahan community who are considered to be a personal inspiration to the participating artists .
