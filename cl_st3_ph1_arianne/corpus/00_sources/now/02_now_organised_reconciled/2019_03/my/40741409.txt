<h> Kim Kim River free of pollutions : Yeo <p> Hazmat team members conduct an inspection before the cleaning of toxic waste at Sungai Kim Kim , Pasir Gudang on March 14 , 2019. -- BBX <p> JOHOR BARU : The Kim Kim River , which was polluted following the dumping of chemical wastes 13 days ago , is confirmed clean and safe , said Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> However , it would be monitored for 15 days to ensure the situation was really safe especially to the public . <p> ' ' Even now , I no longer wear a mask , but the monitoring is crucial because we have to take into account the unknown . <p> ' ' Overall , it is clean but we monitor to convince the people that the river is safe , ' ' she told reporters at the first location of the dumping of the chemical wastes , here today . <p> Yeo said the cleaning works were complete and the contractors were now in the process of transferring the chemical waste collected to the disposal area . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ river , which started on March 13 , ended fully on March 17 , involving numerous quarters including three appointed contractors and authorities such as the Fire and Rescue Department . <p> She also said that the report of the Scientific Committee would be announced tomorrow encompassing data analysis on the pollution other than the methods and solutions used in overcoming the pollution in the river . <p> When asked on whether the cleaning works would affect the natural flow of the river , she said : " Anything that ( is ) related to the science of removal , we will give a full report tomorrow . Tomorrow we will give details on whether this ( river ) flow(s) and other things , how we see things , how we calculate . Tomorrow we will answer . " <p> Meanwhile , 900 metric tonnes of polluted soil together with sludge and 1,500 metric tonnes of polluted water had been collected by the appointed contractors . <p> The process of transporting all the wastes in stages was expected to be completed by March 21 ( Thursday ) . -- Bernama
