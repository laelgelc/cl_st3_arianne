<h> 20 sen pollution charge in FTs effective March 15 <p> A pollution charge of 20 sen will be implemented in the Federal Territories , effective March 15 , said Federal Territories Minister Khalid Samad . <p> He said its implementation would be in line with the government 's objective to address pollution of plastic waste through a Roadmap Towards Zero Single-Use Plastics 2018-2030 , helmed by the Ministry of Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( MESTECC ) . <p> " MESTECC also allows all state governments , as well as Kuala Lumpur , Putrajaya and Labuan to impose pollution charge of 20 sen for each plastic bag used by consumers during shopping . <p> " The charge is only imposed for a biodegradable plastic bag , while enforcement action continues against traders and hawkers who still use conventional plastic bags , " he said in a statement today . <p> Khalid said the approach , " polluter pays principle " , is intended to enhance awareness among Malaysians on expenses incurred by the government to clean plastic pollution . <p> It is also to make them aware of the green initiatives and ensure a sustainable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ use of biodegradable products was introduced in the Federal Territories , there had been a good response from traders and hawkers . <p> He said it is the ministry 's hope that it will be well-received by the people towards making a success of the Federal Territories ' Green Technology Development initiative . <p> " You are encouraged to bring your own bag for shopping as it is easier , and for those buying takeaway food , they are encouraged to bring their own containers , " he said , adding that the ban on the use of plastic drinking straws would be enforced in January 2020 . <p> - Bernama <h> Share this story <p> To post or ' like ' comments on Malaysiakini stories , you will need to sign in with an active paid subscription .
