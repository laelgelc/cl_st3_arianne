<h> Fuziah : Unfortunate that many in gov't convinced by Lynas ' narrative <p> Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department Fuziah Salleh today lamented that many in the government have fallen for the " low-level radiation narrative " of Lynas for the waste it produces at its Gebeng rare earth processing plant . <p> " Lynas is very good with their messaging strategy , that it is easy to fall for their low-level radiation narrative . <p> " Lynas has always been silent on the very long half-life -- the 14 billion-year half-life of thorium in their radioactive waste . <p> " Unfortunately , many people in the government fell for it and were convinced by their narrative , " she said in a statement . <p> As Fuziah noted , the long half-life of the radioactive waste could have long-term effects on people 's health . <p> " Now , only after five years of operations and dumping of radioactive waste onsite , as well as scheduled waste amounting to a total of 1.5 million metric tonnes , sadly , the groundwater beneath Lynas is already contaminated with heavy metals , " she said in a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of heavy metals in the groundwater , but claimed that this was from external sources . <p> Yesterday , Utusan Malaysia quoted Entrepreneur Development Minister Mohd Redzuan Yusof as saying that the Lynas factory would not be shut down , to send a signal that foreign direct investment was welcomed in the country . <p> Fuziah , in her statement , said Malaysia welcomed FDI , but not from corporations that cause pollution . <p> The Lynas Advanced Material Plant ( Lamp ) is located within Fuziah 's constituency on Kuantan . She had previously lobbied for Lynas to remove all its waste from the country . <p> The plant produces two kinds of waste , namely radioactive water leech purification ( WLP ) and neutralisation underflow ( NUF ) waste -- the latter of which , while not radioactive , carries the risk of heavy metal contamination . <p> Up to December last year , the accumulated WLP residue at Lynas ' facility was 451,564 tonnes , while NUF amounted to 1.113 million tonnes . <p> Lynas intends to storethe NUF pending research to process and commercialise the waste @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry said Lynas must remove its radioactive WLP waste from Malaysia . <p> As for the non-radioactive NUF , Lynas was instructed to submit an action plan on its disposal . <p> However , the company may still be required to remove the NUF if the government does not approve of its storage facility . <p> Lynas has maintained that it complies with strict government regulations , and that the products it intends to produce from the waste will be safe . <h> Share this story <p> To post or ' like ' comments on Malaysiakini stories , you will need to sign in with an active paid subscription .
