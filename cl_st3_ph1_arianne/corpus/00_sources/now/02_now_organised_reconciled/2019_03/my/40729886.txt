<h> Sungai Kim Kim clean-up team has two days to complete task <p> JOHOR BAHRU : The team cleaning up the chemical pollution in Sungai Kim Kim has just two days left to ensure that work is completed on schedule , said Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> This is because rain is forecast on Tuesday ( March 19 ) , which could impede efforts to clean up the affected river and worsen the pollution . <p> Yeo said that work to clean up the river was coming along smoothly , so far . <p> " We hope that two days , today and tomorrow , will be the maximum we need to clean it up . If there 's no rain , the weather is good , we can do it . <p> " Work to clean up Sungai Kim Kim went well yesterday . It 's a good development . Swift operation . We cleaned up one kilometre of the river , " she told reporters after visiting the Medic Base at the Pasir Gudang Municipal Council Stadium here yesterday . <p> Meanwhile , at a separate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ director Datuk Yahaya Madis feared that heavy rain could make the situation more hazardous as the pollution would spread to a wider area . -- Bernama
