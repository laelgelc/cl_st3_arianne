<h> One of three suspects in chemical dumping to be charged : Yeo <p> One of three men suspected to have dumped chemical waste in Sungai Kim Kim in Pasir Gudang is to be charged in court tomorrow , according to Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> Yeo said the completed investigation papers have been sent to the office of the deputy public prosecutor for evaluation and consideration . <p> " We hope to charge the suspect in court tomorrow . <p> " Upon conviction , the person can be sentenced to a maximum of five years ' jail and fined RM500,000 under Section 34B of the Environmental Quality Act 1974 , " she told reporters at Menara Aqabah in Pasir Gudang today . <p> The provision pertains to prohibitions against placing and depositing scheduled wastes . <p> Yeo said she has been in contact with attorney-general Tommy Thomas on the possibility of bringing multiple charges against the suspect due to the serious nature of the offence and inadequacy of the penalty . <p> Almost 500 people , many of them students , had landed in the hospital after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " As the Environment Department has completed the investigation , we will go ahead and prosecute , but it is not limited to this ( environmental ) law . <p> " The person can be charged under the Penal Code and , possibly , other laws too , " she said . <p> - Bernama <h> Share this story <p> To post or ' like ' comments on Malaysiakini stories , you will need to sign in with an active paid subscription .
