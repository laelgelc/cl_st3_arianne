<h> Trending Now <h> Bank Pembangunan launches RM1b fund for sustainable development <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( March 13 ) : Bank Pembangunan Malaysia Bhd has launched a RM1 billion fund to support the nation 's sustainable development target by incentivising more companies to adopt sustainable practices in their business strategies and operations . <p> Dubbed Sustainable Development Financing Fund ( SDFF ) , the development bank will provide financing at a financing rate subsidy of 2% to companies that demonstrate continuous contribution to the 17 sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) under the United Nations Development Programme ( UNDP ) . This is part of efforts for Malaysia to achieve all the SDGs by 2030 . <p> The SDFF is one of three funds totalling RM4.5 billion which the Ministry of Finance has mandated Bank Pembangunan to manage
