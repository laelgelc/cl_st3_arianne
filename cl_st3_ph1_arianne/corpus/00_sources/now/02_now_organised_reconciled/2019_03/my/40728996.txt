<h> Two days left for team to clean up chemical waste in Sungai Kim Kim <p> The team cleaning up the chemical pollution in Sungai Kim Kim has just two days left to ensure that work is completed on schedule , said Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin . <p> This is because rain is forecast on March 19 , which could impede efforts to clean up the affected river and worsen the pollution . <p> Yeo said that work to clean up the river was coming along smoothly , so far . <p> " We hope that two days - today and tomorrow - will be the maximum we need to clean it up . If there 's no rain , the weather is good , we can do it . <p> " Work to clean up Sungai Kim Kim went well yesterday . It 's a good development . Swift operation . We cleaned up one kilometre of the river , " she told reporters after visiting the Medic Base at the Pasir Gudang Municipal Council Stadium in Johor Bahru today . <p> Meanwhile , at a separate press @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ feared that heavy rain could make the situation more hazardous as the pollution would spread to a wider area . <p> " I was told that clean-up work would be expedited over the next two days following forecast of heavy rain by the Meteorological Department , " he said . <p> So far , the air quality index is below 3ppm ( particle per million ) , which is the alert level . <p> " Should it exceed this level , we will discuss with the operations commander , who is the state police chief , and take it to the state disaster management committee for further action , " he said . <p> Currently , it is understood that about 100 people , including safety officers and members , Department of Environment ( DOE ) staff , contractors as well as other agencies were involved in the Sungai Kim Kim clean-up operation .
