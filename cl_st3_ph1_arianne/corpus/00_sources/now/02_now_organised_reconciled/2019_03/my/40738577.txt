<h> Air quality in Pasir Gudang improving <p> JOHOR BAHRU ( March 18 ) : The air quality in Pasir Gudang is improving while monitoring continues to ensure the condition is really stable since the district was hit by the effects of chemical waste pollution of Sungai Kim Kim on March 7 . <p> The Department of Environment ( DoE ) said 42 schools had been monitored by 30 monitoring teams of the Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change . <p> " Almost all the schools monitored showed the volatile organic compound ( VOC ) level at zero except Sekolah Taman Rinting 3 which showed VOC readings of 5 ppm ( parts per million ) and 3 ppm at 3pm yesterday on the ground floor and first floor of the school respectively , " said the DoE in a statement issued yesterday evening . <p> The readings were recorded at noon , 3pm and 6pm on Sunday . <p> Meanwhile , there were no readings of hydrogen sulfide recorded at all the schools . <h> Property News <h> EdgeProp.my <p> EdgeProp.my is Malaysia 's most useful property website for home buyers and investors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on property , and hundreds of thousands of properties for sale and rent with detailed information such as past transacted prices , maps and photos .
