<h> Parliament to debate on Pasir Gudang chemical dumping today <p> KUALA LUMPUR ( March 14 ) : The Dewan Rakyat saw an emergency motion being tabled by Hassan Abdul Karim ( PH-Pasir Gudang ) for the house to debate on the chemical dumping matter in Pasir Gudang , Johor . <p> Hassan said judging from the severity of the matter , which resulted in over 500 residents there being admitted to the hospital , it is urgent for the house to deliberate on this issue and for the government to declare an emergency status in that area . <p> Speaker Datuk Mohamad Ariff Md Yusof said there are three criterias to fulfill for an MP to table an emergency motion , where it has to be a specific issue ; it involves the general public ; and there is an urgency for it to be tabled . <p> " This matter fulfills all three criterias and I hereby allow it to be debated today , " he said . <p> Deputy Minister in the Prime Minister 's Department ( Law ) Mohamed Hanipa Maidin ( PH-Sepang ) then read the said motion and sought the house approval to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ motion , and allocated 60 minutes to debate the motion later today . <p> The motion , which was not included in the order paper today , was being brought forward after Hassan queried the rationale of the original schedule to debate this matter on Monday . <p> " I have submitted an application for an emergency motion to be tabled here today , as the matter is so serious , with over 500 people having been admitted into the hospital , I do n't understand why do we have to wait until Monday to debate this matter , " he said earlier during Ministerial Question Time . <p> Minister of energy , science , technology , environment and climate change Yeo Bee Yin , who was the answering Minister at that time , said she will advance this matter to the authority in charge .
