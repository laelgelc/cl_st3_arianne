<p> During his visit , Ambassador Miller reviewed USAID 's work with local communities to build resilient livelihoods and forest management systems that sustain biodiversity , the region 's water supply , and improve incomes , said the US Embassy in Dhaka on Friday . <p> Ambassador Miller , accompanied by USAID Mission Director Derrick Brown , met residents and local government and community leaders . <p> In his engagements , Ambassador Miller said the U.S. government works closely with the Government of Bangladesh and other development partners to improve economic opportunities for local communities and promote conservation of the unique natural resources in the Chittagong Hill Tracts . <p> USAID has helped 24,000 local community members improve their livelihoods through vegetable , poultry , pig , and goat farming . <p> As part of improving water security , USAID programs have increased access to safe drinking water while reducing community dependence on timber and other forest products . <p> USAID has also partnered with the Bangladesh Forest Department and local communities to plant 625 hectares of trees in reserve forests . Another 920 hectares will be reforested this year . <p> These training sessions enhance the capacity of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ preparedness for mass casualty and disaster management . <p> The site visits were undertaken in collaboration with the Government of Bangladesh and the United Nations Development Programme . <p> The US government , through USAID , has provided more than $7 billion in development assistance to Bangladesh since 1971 . <p> In 2018 , USAID provided nearly $219 million in development assistance to improve the lives of people in Bangladesh through programs that expand food security and economic opportunity , improve health and education , promote democratic institutions and practices , protect the environment , and increase resilience to climate change .
