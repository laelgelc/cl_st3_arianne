<h> Meet Greta Thunberg , the climate change warrior <p> Swedish environmental activist Greta Thunberg , 16 , is seen on stage as she takes part in a protest claiming for urgent measures to combat climate change , in Hamburg , Germany , on March 1 , 2019 . Photo : Reuters <p> Star Online Report <p> Swedish teen activist Greta Thunberg has been nominated three Norwegian lawmakers for the Nobel Peace Prize for her prominent voice in campaigns against climate change . <p> Greta , a 16-year-old school student , began her lone protest outside Sweden 's parliament on August 20 , 2018 . <p> She raised her voice against the inaction of politicians regarding climate change by holding a hand-written plackard saying " SCHOOL STRIKE FOR CLIMATE " . <p> Later , she continued her protest every Friday . Some of her school mates also joined her every Friday in the protest which went on to receive worldwide attention . <p> Inspired by her , teenage students all over the world in as many as 270 cities held strikes to make the world leaders realise the impact of climate change is greater than they perceive . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Parties ( COP24 ) of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) held between December 2- December 15 last year in Katowice , Poland . <p> Thunberg has almost 250,000 followers on Twitter where her movement carries the hashtags #FridaysForFuture and #SchoolStrike4Climate . <p> She also talked on TEDx on climate change . The video , now carried on TED 's main website , has garnered more than 1.2 million views . <p> Thunberg joined protests last month in Belgium , where she won a European Union pledge to spend billions of euros to combat climate change .
