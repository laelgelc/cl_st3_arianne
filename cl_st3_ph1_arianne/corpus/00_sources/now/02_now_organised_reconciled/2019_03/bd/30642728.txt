<h> US envoy visits CHT , reviews USAID 's work <p> The ambassador visited Rangamati Sadar Hospital to observe a USAID training program <p> US Ambassador to Bangladesh Earl R Miller has visited the Chittagong Hill Tracts and key United States Agency for International Development ( USAID ) development programs this week . <p> During his visit , Ambassador Miller reviewed USAID 's work with local communities building resilient livelihoods and forest management systems that sustain biodiversity , the region 's water supply , and improve incomes , said the US Embassy in Dhaka on Friday . <p> Ambassador Miller , accompanied by USAID Mission Director Derrick Brown , met residents and local government and community leaders . <p> In his engagements , Ambassador Miller said the US government works closely with the Government of Bangladesh and other development partners to improve economic opportunities for local communities and promote conservation of the unique natural resources in the Chittagong Hill Tracts . <p> USAID has helped 24,000 local community members improve their livelihoods through : vegetable , poultry , pig , and goat farming . <p> As part of improving water security , USAID programs have increased access to safe @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ forest resources . <p> USAID has also partnered with the Bangladesh Forest Department and local communities to plant 625 hectares of trees in reserve forests . Another 920 hectares will be reforested this year . <p> These training sessions aim to enhance the capacity of healthcare personnel -- throughout the Chittagong Hill Tracts -- for emergency preparedness for mass casualty and disaster management . <p> The site visits were undertaken in collaboration with the Government of Bangladesh and the United Nations Development Programme . <p> The US government , through USAID , has provided more than $7 billion in development assistance to Bangladesh since 1971 . <p> In 2018 , USAID provided nearly $219 million in development assistance to improve the lives of people in Bangladesh through programs that : expand food security and economic opportunity , improve health and education , promote democratic institutions and practices , protect the environment , and increase resilience to climate change .
