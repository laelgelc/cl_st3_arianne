<h> Human-made factors pose serious threat to fisheries <p> Abu Sazzad : The fisheries sector is playing significant role to mitigate the protein demand for the countrymen but several factors including climate change , natural disasters , unbalanced urbanisation and industrialisation , overfishing and environmental pollution are posing significant threat to the income and fish security of the growing population , said fisheries experts . They blamed over exploitation and excess use of pesticides for agriculture as major causes of depleting the variation of indigenous fish resources . According to the Fisheries Department , in 2000-01 the total number of fish caught from inland water bodies was 14.75 lac tons and in 2016-17 the figure rose to 34.96 lac tons . Bangladesh has recorded surplus fish production with an annual output of 41.34 lac metric tons against a demand of 40.50 lac metric tons , said the sources . The fisheries sector contributes 3.61 percent to GDP and 24.41 percent to agricultural GDP . Fish supplements to about 60 percent of daily animal protein intake . The government is trying to sustain this growth performance , which eventually ensures to achieve the projected production target of 4.55 million metric tons by 2020-21 . Bangladesh has ranked the third fish producing country in the world in 2018 after China and India which is contributing significantly in food security through proving safe and quality animal protein . Bangladesh earns a considerable amount of foreign currencies by exporting fish , shrimps and other fisheries products . Bangladesh exports frozen shrimp and other fish and fisheries products to more than 50 countries , including Belgium , UK , Netherlands , Germany , USA , China , France , Russian Federation , Japan and Saudi Arabia . Dr Mokhlesur Rahman , executive director of Centre for Natural Resources Studies ( CNRS ) told the Daily Industry that Bangladesh fisheries have ample scope of development to strengthen the national economy . Concerned government departments , development partners , researchers and non-government organizations can play important role in the wide-ranging advancement of the fisheries sector . The massive infrastructure of roads and embankments , urbanization and housing projects has blocked many water bodies and such condition has adversely affected breeding and spawning of many indigenous fish species , he observed . The inland aquaculture production is constrained mainly by three problems related to -seed , feed , and extension service , Rahman explained . To overcome these constraints , it is needed to maintain purity of brood stock of indigenous carp and other indigenous fish species conserving the natural breeding , spawning , nursery and grow-out areas to complete the whole lifecycle and natural reproduction process .
