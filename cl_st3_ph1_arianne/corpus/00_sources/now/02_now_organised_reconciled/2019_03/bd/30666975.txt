<h> Global Strike for Climate <h> School students rally against climate change <p> Despite it being the weekly holiday , children of Barishal city gathered at their respective schools and held rallies on Friday , demanding a safe future and protection against climate change . <p> They also brought out a procession from Ashwini Kumar Hall to Shaheed Minar and formed a human chain . <p> Future of Bangladesh is more vulnerable due to climate change and its effects . <p> " We demand climate justice against this risk , " said Arif Jobayer , a student . <p> " Ensure our safety ; we want implementation of the Paris treaty , " he added . <p> Sohanur Rahman , chief coordinator of YouthNet for Climate Justice , said children of different countries walked out from their classrooms -- as part of a school strike -- demanding assurance of actions against climate change from policymakers . <p> Although schools were closed on Friday , children of Barishal brought out the processions in solidarity . <p> Deputy Commissioner of Barishal SM Ajior Rahman said such kinds of awareness-raising programmes should be arranged more often , to start a public discussion @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will continue more programmes on this issue , " said Sakila Islam , one of the organisers . <p> YouthNet for Climate Justice arranged the programme with assistance from Barishal City Corporation .
