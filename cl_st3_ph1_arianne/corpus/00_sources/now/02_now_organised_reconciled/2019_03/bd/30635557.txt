UNB file photo <p> Dhaka , Mar 6 ( UNB ) - The Parliamentary Standing Committee on the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change on Wednesday recommended issuing health alert if the air pollution level exceeds AQI value 300 as per a guideline of the World Health Organization ( WHO ) to create public awareness . <p> The parliamentary watchdog came up with the recommendation at its 2nd meeting held at Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban with committee chairman Saber Hossain Chowdhury in the chair . <p> The JS body also suggested taking necessary steps to hold a coordination meeting on emergency basis involving the ministries concerned and agencies to reduce environment pollution . <p> The meeting also suggested submission of a report by the Forest Department on the forest area of the country using Geographic Information System ( GIS ) . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Md Rezaul Karim were also present at the meeting . <p> Bangladesh , one of the most densely-populated countries in the world , has been struggling with air pollution for a long time and Dhaka continuously ranks among the world 's most polluted cities .
