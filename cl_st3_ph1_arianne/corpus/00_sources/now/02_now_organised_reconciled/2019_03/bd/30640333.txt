<h> Women still struggle globally to find a job : ILO <h> New UN report calls for ' quantum leap ' <p> UNB News <p> Publish Date - March 07 , 2019 , 10:52 PM <p> 408 Views <p> Update Date - March 08 , 2019 , 01:47 AM <p> Dhaka , Mar 7 ( UNB ) - Women 's job opportunities have barely improved globally since the early 1990s , UN labour experts said on Thursday , warning that female workers are still penalised for having children and looking after them . <p> Released on the eve of International Women 's Day , celebrated on March 8 , the International Labour Organization ( ILO ) report found that 1.3 billion women were in work in 2018 , compared with two billion men -- a less than two percent improvement in the last 27 years , according to UN News . <p> A future of work in which women will no longer lag behind men is within reach , but it will take a quantum leap , not just hesitant incremental steps , to get there , according to a new ILO report . <p> " We need to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leap for gender equality : For a better future of work for all , provides a way forward , " said Director , ILO Conditions of Work and Equality Department , Manuela Tomei . <p> The report is the culmination of five years of work under the ILO 's Women at Work Centenary Initiative . <p> It finds that in the last 27 years the difference in the employment rates for men and women has shrunk by less than two percentage points . <p> In 2018 , women are still 26 percentage points less likely to be in employment than men . Mothers experience a ' motherhood wage penalty ' that compounds across their working life , while fathers enjoy a wage premium . <p> " A number of factors are blocking equality in employment , and the one playing the largest role is caregiving , " said Tomei . <p> " In the last 20 years , the amount of time women spent on unpaid care and domestic work has hardly fallen , and men 's has increased by just eight minutes a day .
