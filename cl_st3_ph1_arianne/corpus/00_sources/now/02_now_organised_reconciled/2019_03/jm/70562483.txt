<h> More than 560,000 cases of dengue in the Americas in 2018 - PAHO <p> The Pan American Health Organisation ( PAHO ) has disclosed that 560,586 cases of dengue were reported in the Region of the Americas last year . The number includes 3,535 severe cases and 336 deaths . <p> The statistics also show that , for the first six weeks of 2019 , almost 100,000 cases of dengue were reported , including 632 cases of severe dengue and 28 deaths . <p> The information is contained in PAHO 's latest epidemiological update on dengue , published last week . <p> It has called for countries in the Caribbean and Latin America to prepare for a timely response to possible outbreaks of the mosquito borne dengue fever . <p> " Dengue is a viral infection transmitted by mosquitoes that is widespread in the Americas , " said Dr Marcos Espinal , Director of the Department of Communicable Diseases and Environmental Determinants of Health in a statement on Thursday . <p> " Its complexity has been increasing over the years due to several factors such as unplanned urban growth , water and sanitation problems , climate change , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ all four types of dengue , which increases the risk of serious cases and outbreaks , " he added . <p> Dengue is endemic in the region and , since its reintroduction in the 1980s , has caused cyclical outbreaks and epidemics every three to five years . <p> The first dengue epidemic with over one million cases occurred in the region in 2010 . Three years later , in 2013 , the first epidemic with more than two million cases occurred . At the beginning of 2019 , there was an increase in cases compared to the same period of 2018 . <p> According to Espinal , the timely diagnosis of the disease has become more complex with the arrival of two new arboviruses : The Chikungunya virus in 2013 and the Zika virus in 2015 , which present similar symptomatology . <p> Espinal noted that despite the introduction of these new arboviruses , dengue is the one that presents the highest number of diseases . <p> " Dengue is also a more lethal arbovirus than chikungunya and Zika , but its treatment is relatively simple , inexpensive and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is recognising the warning signs early and providing the care required to prevent it progressing to more serious forms , " said Jos ?
