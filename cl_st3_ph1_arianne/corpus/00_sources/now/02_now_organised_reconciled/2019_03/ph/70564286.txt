<h> 27 hazard-prone towns to get GIS <p> TWENTY-seven local disaster risk reduction and management offices ( LDRRMO ) in Cebu Province will receive the Geographic Information System ( GIS ) Software and computers from the Capitol . <p> The GIS is a system designed to capture , store , manipulate , analyze , manage and present spatial or geographic cata . It can be used for spatial analysis on hazard , risks and vulnerable areas in town and cities and as reference for planning and creating action plans for the local government unit ( LGU ) . <p> Beneficiaries <p> The software is seen to assist LGUs in the formulation of their Climate Change Adaptation Management and contingency plans . <p> Personnel of LDRRMOs will undergo training on light detection and ranging ( LiDAR ) and the process and application of drone mapping . <p> Making a dream come true <p> Last year , Gov .
