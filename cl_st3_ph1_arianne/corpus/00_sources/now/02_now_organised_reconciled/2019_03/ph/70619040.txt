<h> Here 's what can actually be done to help solve the water crisis <p> While endless debates about who 's to blame for the current shortage -- and a more than a few conspiracy theories -- abound , a former DENR secretary weighs in on what we can do about it . Sometimes the fire trucks come past midnight . Dwellers of residential buildings wait for their turn to fetch water from their building 's swimming pool that is already half-empty by the fifth day of the water interruption . The prices of water containers have gone up -- as high as thrice the average price . Two days into the chaos , which started on the second week of March , people are angry and frustrated .
