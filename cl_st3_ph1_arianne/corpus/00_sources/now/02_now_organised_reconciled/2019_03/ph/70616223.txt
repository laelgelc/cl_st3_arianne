<h> Plant more bamboo for livelihood , fight climate change <p> SAN JOSE DE BUENAVISTA , Antique -- Farmers are encouraged to plant more bamboo for their livelihood and to help address the pressing problem on climate change . <p> Edgardo C. Manda , president of the Philippine Bamboo Furniture , Inc , on Thursday said the market is ripe for bamboo commercial and industrial use . <p> Speaking at the First Provincial Disaster Risk and Reduction and Management/Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation Summit here , Manda said there is a big demand for bamboo products both domestically and internationally . <p> However , he cited that farmers have to be trained on steps starting from bamboo propagation , nursery , establishment of livelihood and linkage to the market in order to sustain their interest . " There is a need to train farmers on crop management , " he said . <p> Another challenge is for local government units , Department of Agriculture and Department of Environment and Natural Resources to give special attention to bamboo like coconut , coffee and rubber . <p> " The problem is that the Department of Agriculture does not accept bamboo as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " Manda said . <p> He added the Department of Trade and Industry , while it provides the needed training and equipment for the commercial and industrial use of bamboo , allegedly forgot to extend training on the production of raw materials . <p> Manda added that by planting more bamboos , farmers can help in climate change adaptation and mitigation because the bamboo does carbon sequestration . <p> " For one hectare of bamboo , it absorbs 62 tons of carbon dioxide , " he said . <p> He also said that bamboo , which has very strong and extensive roots , is effective in terms of erosion control and landslide .
