<h> Ensure availability and sustainable management of water for all <p> PASAY CITY , Mar. 22 -- In observance of World Water Day , March 22 , Senator Loren Legarda supported the global call to ensure availability and the sustainable management of water to provide for current consumption and conserve for future generations . <p> Legarda said that this year 's celebration carries the theme " Leaving No One Behind , " which recognizes the human right entitling everyone , without discrimination , to sufficient , safe , acceptable , accessible , and affordable water for personal and domestic use . <p> " Having safe water means we secure public health . With people dying due to unsafe water and poor sanitation , as well as being displaced or forced to relocate in areas with safe water , we must ensure we afford every human being their basic right to access safe water , " Legarda said . <p> Senator Legarda also expressed , in light of the water supply shortage in Metro Manila , that authorities must take necessary measures to adapt to climate change impacts , such as increasing temperatures , sea level rise , and changes @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of El Ni ? o and jeopardize the long-term water supply in the country . <p> She also reiterated the call for households , offices , businesses , and industries to use water more wisely and recycle used water , as we are already experiencing weak El Ni ? o conditions wherein rainfall is below normal or scarce . <p> Legarda said that PAGASA confirmed that 2019 is set to be the warmest year on record due to El Ni ? o , which could affect provinces with dry condition ( two consecutive months of below normal rainfall conditions or 21-60% reduction ) , dry spell ( three consecutive months of below normal or two consecutive months of way below normal rainfall conditions or more than 60% reduction ) , and drought ( three consecutive months of way below normal or five consecutive months of below normal rainfall conditions ) , from March until June this year . <p> " We must treat this issue as a climate change concern and not just a water supply issue .
