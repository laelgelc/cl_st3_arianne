<h> Moratorium on reclamations pressed <p> Progressive leaders and members of fisherfolk , urban poor , environment , and church groups called for a moratorium on reclamation projects that could threaten coastal and marine landscapes around the country . <p> They issued the call during a two-day conference titled " 2nd People 's Summit on the Impacts of Reclamation " held on March 26-27 in Manila . <p> Fisherfolk , environmentalist groups , Manila Yacht club members , and some members of Philippine Dragonboat team hold a protest rally against the reclamation projects in Manila Bay in Wednesday , March 27 , 2019 . ( JANSEN ROMERO / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> The advocacy groups said at least 11.24 million people may significantly be impacted by reclamation projects covering 39,414 hectares that threaten coastal and marine landscapes around the country . <p> Majority of the reclamations or at least 80 percent of these projects are concentrated in Manila Bay . Other reclamation projects are located in Cebu , Iloilo , Laguna , Cagayan de Oro , Iligan City , Davao City , Davao del Norte , Batangas , Bacolod City , Palawan , and Aklan . <p> These are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ migratory birds converge , where fish breed and feed , and where millions of people subsist for their sustenance and livelihood , the groups added . <p> These are the green corridors that are our first and last line of defense from growing risks of storm surges , floods , and other disasters , they also said . <p> International ocean conservation group Oceana also urged the government to uphold and enforce the law in several reclamation projects submitted to and being processed by the Philippine Reclamation Authority ( PRA ) . <p> " We are alarmed by the apparent disregard of our progressive laws for protection of our vastly threatened natural life support systems , and allowing these reclamation projects to pave our mangroves , sea grass beds , and other marine habitats , " Oceana said . <p> " Dumping and filling of critical marine support systems in Manila Bay are not only violating our laws ; these deprive our artisanal fisherfolk of their living and sustenance as well as fisheries and biodiversity which are heavily impacted , " it added . <p> Oceana questioned the PRA and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ processing the application of these projects despite possible violations of the Local Government Code , Fisheries Code , Environmental Impact Statement System Act , and Climate Change Act , among other laws . <p> " Local government units expand their land territory further into the sea , substantially changing its territorial boundaries . We dare ask the proponents of these projects to prove that they complied with these requirements and they can stand before the people that they did not violate our laws , " she added . <p> Under the Local Government Code or Republic Act 7160 , a local government unit 's boundaries may only be substantially altered by law enacted by Congress , she pointed out . <p> Section 10 of the same Code provides that no substantial alteration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ approved by a majority of the votes cast in a plebiscite called for the purpose in the political unit or units directly affected . <p> These are in addition to the other requirements of the Code and pertinent environmental laws , such as consultation of local communities , she also cited . <p> According to Ramos , some local government units use these illegal dumping and filling projects to increase their internal revenue allotment ( IRA ) without going through the rigid process set by the Local Government Code . <p> " Thus , disbursements by the Department of Budget and Management of the IRA to a number of LGUs who have not complied with the legal requirements for reclamation projects are questionable , " she added . <p> The reclamation summit was organized by the People 's Network for the Integrity of Coastal Habitats and Ecosystems ( People 's NICHE ) , a national alliance advocating for ecological restoration as an alternative to land reclamation .
