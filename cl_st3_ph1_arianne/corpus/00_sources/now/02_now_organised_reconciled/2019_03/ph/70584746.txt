It is the first purpose-built environmental campaigning ship specifically made for Greenpeace , and is one of the most energy-efficient ships in the high seas today . <p> Intended to support Filipinos most affected by the brunt of climate change , the tour inspired solidarity in local communities across the nation . Filipinos bravely faced fossil fuel corporations to demand that they take responsibility for the climate crisis . This year , the most iconic ship in the Greenpeace fleet returns to our islands to campaign against one of the greatest threats presently affecting every form of life on Earth : plastic pollution .
