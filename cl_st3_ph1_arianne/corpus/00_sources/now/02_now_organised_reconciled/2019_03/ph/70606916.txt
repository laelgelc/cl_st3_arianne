<p> THE word " sustainable " has become a catchall term describing a better way of producing goods as sustainable processes are kinder to the environment . Because of climate change awareness , consumers nowadays are more conscious with what they 're buying . In effect , producers have become more conscious as well with what they 're offering . <p> The fashion industry is one of the major polluting industries in the world . But homegrown fast fashion shop ATE ( Alter to Enhance ) by Tatah is trying to contribute in minimizing pollution coming from the fashion industry , most especially from fabric scraps , and created a zero waste initiative with its " Retaso Shoes " and " Ugly Bags . " <p> " Most people who do n't have any knowledge about these materials wo n't be able to reuse them properly . Every fabric has its own unique characteristic and it is made of different materials . Some fabrics are best for making gowns , rugs or swimwear , while others are not . Each pair of the sleek , cute and comfortable shoe , named Carmen -- designed by branch manager , stylist and designer Alexandra Eturma -- is unique . This is because of the various retaso prints and patterns per pair of shoes . One may also customize an order , based on the chosen excess fabric or for those who want to pair them with their gowns . <p> Aside from its retaso shoes , another innovation the team conceived using its excess fabrics are the " Ugly Bags . It is called an " Ugly Bag " because of its imperfections . Think unparalleled sides ; uneven sizes .
