<h> Palace urges Filipinos to cut use of plastics <p> Malaca ? ang yesterday called on Filipinos to cut down the use of plastics as the world celebrates Earth Hour , with this year 's theme focusing on the issue of single-use plastics . <p> " A United Nations report shows the Philippines as one of the top five contributors of plastic waste in the world 's oceans .
