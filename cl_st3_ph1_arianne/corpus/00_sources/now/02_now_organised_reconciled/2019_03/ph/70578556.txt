Magno , MMSU StratCom ) <p> BATAC CITY -- An official of the Philippine Society for Microbiology , Inc. in Northern Luzon ( PSM-NL ) attributed microbial infections , such as the measles outbreak among children , to the adverse effects of climate change . <p> " The measles outbreak , which we are battling right now , the emerging microbial pathogens , and antimicrobial resistance are the consequences of this changing world climate and an increasing global mobility that should be immediately addressed to , " PSM president Michelle Dalmacio said during the two-day convention at the Mariano Marcos State University on the ongoing battle against microbial infections in the country . <p> Other regions also have rising measles cases and are at risk of possible outbreaks if the epidemic is not contained . <p> Records show that more than 4,300 measles cases have been officially reported by DOH , with 70 deaths from January 1 to February @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ measles cases since Health concept recognizes that the health of people is connected to the health of animals and the environment . <p> Some agencies involved in disease control and prevention use this by working with
