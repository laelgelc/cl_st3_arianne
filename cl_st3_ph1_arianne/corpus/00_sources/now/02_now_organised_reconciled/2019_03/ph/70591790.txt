<h> DENR Eastern Visayas Steps Up Forest Protection Drive <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) in Eastern Visayas is raising awareness on forest protection in aiming to end illegal logging activities within the year . <p> DENR Regional Executive Director Crizaldy Barcelo said the department is stepping up forest protection efforts in time for the global celebration of International Day of the Forests ( IDF ) on March 21 . <p> " The DENR 's forest protection activities all relate to the objectives of the celebration of IDF , which is to raise awareness on how sustainably managed forests contribute to the healthy environment , " Barcelo told reporters on Thursday . <p> He said forests secure the soil and water resources , mitigate climate change , provide habitat for animals , and livelihood for human . <p> In January , Barcelo ordered field officials to conduct massive information , education and communication drives , strictly monitor timber poaching activities in their assigned areas , and closely coordinate with apprehending authorities for the conduct of anti-timber poaching operations . <p> To intensify the effort , the agency is also implementing a biodiversity protection system @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ system uses cyber tracker mobile application to report real-time environmental abuses from the site . <p> This system is used in identifying threats within forest conservation areas such as cutting , perennial farming , forest fires , and pests and diseases , as well as in recommending immediate action to identified threats during patrol works . <p> " In instances of apprehension and confiscation of forest products , it is regrettable that trees were already cut , but the department is sending a strong message to the culprits that their action will not be left unpunished , " Barcelo said . <p> Sanctions will be imposed to violators of forestry laws as a way of emphasizing to the public that forests are important . This also serves as a warning against activities that are abusive of forest resources . <p> The United Nations established every 21 of March as IDF to raise awareness of the importance of all types of forests . <p> This year , the celebration promotes education to " Learn to Love Forests " .
