As interest rates increase , bond prices decrease . Moreover , the required return for investors tends to increase as the credit risk assessed to the issuer increases . Also affecting bond pricing are the anticipation of the project 's success and backup plans for future business opportunities . All of these are taken into consideration in calculating bond return . <p> Slightly deviating from a conventional bond , other additional terms and characteristics of a green bond -- whether it is a floating rate , cancellable or callable -- also affect its price . Further studies from Harvard Business School show that most US municipal green bonds are issued at a premium , where after-tax yields are six basis points lower than a conventional municipal bond .
