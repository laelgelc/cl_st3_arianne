Cruz . No other paper has assembled such great opinion writers on the same page before or since . While the last three had seriously treated their subjects and awed their readers with their depth and substance , Joe also charmed his followers with his wit and humor . <p> I wish somebody had collected Joe 's daily compositions . I 'm sure we 'll find his biting commentaries on the political scene still relevant today . <p> It 's notable that despite the heat and frenzy of the current political campaign , no one has captured the imagination of the Filipino voters in the manner that Rep.
