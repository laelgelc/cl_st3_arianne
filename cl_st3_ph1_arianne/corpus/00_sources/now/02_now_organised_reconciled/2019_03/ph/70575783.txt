<h> Risk resiliency program benefits 9 LGUs in Iloilo , Antique <p> Maurice John Caban , Department of Social Welfare and Development-Western Visayas Regional Focal Person for Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation , on Friday ( March 8 , 2019 ) says local government units that are landslide prone or are experiencing sea level rise are prioritized in the implementation of the cash-for-work program . ( Photo by Perla Lena ) <p> ILOILO CITY -- Some 6,000 individuals from the vulnerable sectors in nine local government units ( LGUs ) in the provinces of Iloilo and Antique are recipients of the cash-for-work program of the Department of Social Welfare and Development ( DSWD ) 6 ( Western Visayas ) . <p> The recipients of the Risk Resiliency Program Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation ( RRPCCAM ) , which has a funding of PHP16.425 million , were chosen through cross-matching done at the local government level . <p> The nine LGUs are Tubungan and Igbaras in Iloilo and Anini-y , Hamtic , Tobias Fornier , Patnongon , San Remigio , Sibalom , and Valderrama of Antique . <p> " We overlay the hazard map and prioritized those that are in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . They are landslide-prone areas or are experiencing sea level rise , " DSWD-6 Focal Person for Climate Change Adaptation and Mitigation , Maurice John Caban , said in an interview Friday . <p> Households that have not yet received interventions from the DSWD , such as the cash-for-work , families living in hazard areas and those considered as part of the vulnerable sector , are prioritized in the selection . <p> They will be assigned to help rehabilitate barangay roads or projects that are attached to their Local Climate Change Action Plan ( LCCAP ) , he said . <p> They will be paid 70 percent of the regional prevailing wage of PHP273.75 for 10 days . <p> In a phone interview Thursday , Luna Moscoso , chief of the Disaster Response Management of DSWD-6 , said they are now gathering the proposals of recipient LGUs so they can already start next week . <p> " This is one of the programs in the prevention and mitigation of disasters , " she explained . <p> Moscoso recalled that last year , they also implemented the program in 29 municipalities @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Batiano Rivers . The recipient towns were from Antique , Capiz and Iloilo benefitting some 31,607 individuals . <p> Luna said that they are not yet sure if there would be additional LGUs because they are dependent on their central office . <p> " But actually it is one of the directions of the government , which is geared towards climate change reduction , " she added .
