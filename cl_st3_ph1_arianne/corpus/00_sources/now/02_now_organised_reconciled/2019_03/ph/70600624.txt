<h> Environmentalists warn about water shortage <p> ANGELES CITY -- A group of environmentalists here has warned that the Metro Clark area and some parts of Pampanga may also experience water shortage because of massive development which destroys rainforests and watershed areas . <p> The newly-formed Pampanga Aguman Ding Talaingat Danum Incorporated ( Talaingat Danum ) issued the statement following the water shortage being experienced by thousands of residents in Metro Manila . <p> Renato " Abong " Tayag Jr. , chairman of the Talaingat Danum Board , said the water supply chain should not be cut otherwise the people will suffer from water scarcity . <p> " The missing link now is the watershed . Our water supply chain is like a circle . It starts with rain and then water shed which holds and processes water to replenish our ground water table where we draw our traditional source of water from deep wells , " Tayag said <p> He also suggested the massive tree-planting in the mountain ranges of Clark in order to restore water shed areas . <p> " We should also learn to conserve water during these times . Waste water can be used in other purposes like washing your car or watering the plants , " Tayag said . <p> Metro Manila will not experience water shortage if watersheds in Antipolo and Tanay are protected , according to Tayag . <p> People should cooperate to address environment issues including water shortage and climate change , he said . <p> " We should unite and stop blaming each other .
