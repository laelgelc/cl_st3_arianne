<h> BNPP AM to divest from coal mining and coal-fired power firms <p> BNP Paribas Asset Management ( BNPP AM ) will divest from companies that mine thermal coal and use coal to generate electricity , acting on its goal to align its portfolios with the Paris climate change accord . <p> The French company 's coal exclusion policy , which will take effect at the beginning of next year , will apply to all of its actively managed open-ended funds and segregated mandates , BNPP AM says in a statement on March 14 . <p> Under the policy , BNPP AM will exclude companies that derive more than 10% of their revenues from mining thermal coal and/or account for at least 1% of the total global production . <p> According to an investment consultant based in Hong Kong , it 's too early to predict the impact on the asset management firm 's allocations to Asia . <p> " But I do n't expect there will be a dramatic change for its Asian investments .
