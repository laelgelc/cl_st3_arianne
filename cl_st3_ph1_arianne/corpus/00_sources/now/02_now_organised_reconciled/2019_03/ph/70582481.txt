<h> Dry Spell Affects Over 13,000 Rice Farmers In Iloilo <p> A total of 13,630 rice farmers in Iloilo province have suffered crop damages caused by the dry spell , the Provincial Agriculture Office ( PAO ) said . <p> Elias Sandig , Assistant Provincial Agriculturist , said the affected farmers are those who planted rice from November last year until February this year . <p> " From December to February 23 , 13,630 farmers coming from the 27 towns in the province are affected by the dry spell , " said Sandig in an interview on Monday . <p> Based on PAO 's partial damage assessment report for cereals , he said that 1,575 hectares of rice farms were totally damaged while 9,518 hectares were partially damaged . <p> The reported damage reached a total volume of 24,692 metric tons which equates to PHP401 million in value . <p> Sandig added that southern Iloilo experiences rainy season for only four to six months which is why it is considered as a " highly vulnerable area " . <p> " Most of the damages were in southern Iloilo , the first district , because they experience the shortest rainy @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The towns affected in Southern Iloilo are Oton with 504 affected farmers ; Guimbal with 36 ; Igbaras with 388 ; Tigbauan with 318 ; and Tubungan with 683 . <p> Land areas in these towns are both irrigated and rainfed . <p> The damaged crops are mostly in their reproductive and ripening stage , Sandig noted , adding that other towns are yet to submit their reports . <p> " The affected farmers are basically insured under the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation , " he said , encouraging all farmers to register with PCIC . <p> Furthermore , with the challenge posted by the climate change , Sandig said the agriculture office mainly assists farmers in farm planning . <p> " We can not anymore determine the rainy and dry season . Farm planning is the key to lessen the damages of climate change which continuously becomes a challenge for the agriculture sector , " he said .
