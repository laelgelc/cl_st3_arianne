<h> Temperatures spike to new highs as fires and floods ravage country <p> The three-month summer in 2018-2019 , which officially finished at the end of last month , roasted much of the country in a series of crippling heatwaves that triggered blackouts , fuelled conditions ripe for huge bush fires and worsened an already severe drought in the eastern half of the nation . <p> Many businesses were affected as temperature extremes made work during the day hazardous to health . <p> " This summer was so hot we witnessed fruit cooking on trees . In Sydney , authorities warned steel train tracks could buckle under the extreme heat , " said Ms Amanda McKenzie , CEO of the independent , publicly funded council . <p> Climate change was cranking up the intensity of extreme weather , and as mankind continues to pump more pollution into the air from burning fossil fuels , those extremes are set to intensify , said the study . <p> Across the country , heatwaves are becoming more intense and longer lasting .
