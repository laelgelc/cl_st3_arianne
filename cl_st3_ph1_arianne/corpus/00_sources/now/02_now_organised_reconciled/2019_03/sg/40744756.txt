<p> A NZ$ 400,000 from the New Zealand Government 's Provincial Growth Fund was granted to Massey University to help establish a Rural Innovation Lab in the Manawatu-Whanganui region . <p> According to a recent press release , the Lab will engage farmers and growers across Manawatu-Whanganui to drive new thinking in the primary sector , particularly digital farm opportunities . <p> In addition , it will serve as a pilot initiative for the development of similar programmes across the country . <p> Rural Innovation Lab <p> Under-Secretary for Regional Economic Development Fletcher Tabuteau explained that this initiative will help develop and potentially support the commercialisation of new ideas and technologies , which will improve land use in the primary sector . <p> The Rural Innovation Lab is a model example of local people , businesses and the community , developing a project that aligns with their economic aspirations . <p> For Manawatu-Whanganui in particular , land use optimisation is a central plank in the region 's economic action plan . This project will help to unlock new economic opportunities across the region . <p> The Government is proud to support this work .
