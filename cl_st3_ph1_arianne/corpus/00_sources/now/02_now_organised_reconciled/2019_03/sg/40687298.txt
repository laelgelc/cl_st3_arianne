<h> Larry Summers : Modern Monetary Theory is ' grotesque ' <p> Former Treasury Secretary Larry Summers slammed a hotly debated economic theory for suggesting that government debt should not be interpreted as a constraining factor to fiscal policy . <p> Modern Monetary Theory , or MMT , argues that a country borrowing in its own currency can finance fiscal stimulus by printing money . By extension , MMT would allow the government to control inflation through tax policy . Instead of asking the Fed to stabilize prices through monetary policy , the government could raise taxes when prices get too high and cut taxes when prices get too low . <p> Summers , who served as Treasury Secretary under Bill Clinton and later briefly headed the Obama administration 's National Economic Council , says the theory is " voodoo economics . " <p> " It takes ideas that have a little bit of validity and extends them to a grotesque point where they defy the laws of arithmetic , " Summers said in an interview with Yahoo Finance .
