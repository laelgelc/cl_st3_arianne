<p> Seals and whales in the Arctic are shifting their feeding patterns as climate change alters their habitats , and the way they do so may determine whether they survive , a new study has found . <p> Researchers harnessed datasets spanning two decades to examine how two species of Arctic wildlife -- beluga whales , also known as white whales , and ringed seals -- are adapting to their changing habitat . <p> The research focused on the area around Svalbard -- northwest of Norway -- which is experiencing rapid impacts from climate change and particularly a " large collapse in sea-ice conditions in 2006 that has continued to the present day , " said lead researcher Charmain Hamilton . <p> " Both white whales and ringed seals were tagged in Svalbard before this collapse occurred to study their basic ecology .
