<h> Plastic waste site as big as six football fields found in Penang as nation battles widespread problem <p> BUKIT MERTAJAM ( Penang ) * From the north to the south of Peninsular Malaysia , there seems to be no end to the discovery of illegal dump sites . <p> Although Penang was the first state to implement the " no single-use plastic " initiative , it has a lot more environmental battles to take on , with illegal disposal of waste becoming a hot-button issue of late . <p> Soon after the report of chemical waste being dumped in an oil palm estate in Bukit Teh , an illegal plastic dump site the size of six football fields has been discovered in nearby Machang Bubok . <p> During a visit to the site on Tuesday , The Star saw mounds of shredded plastic almost two storeys high . <p> Excavators and forklifts were at work unloading more of such waste from trucks . <p> It is believed that the marshland was previously an illegal sand quarry that had been shut down many years ago . <p> Besides plastic waste , items such as discarded furniture and construction debris @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ In one corner of the dump site , thick black smoke could be seen billowing into the air from the open burning of waste . <p> The illegal plastic dump site is near the oil palm estate where several barrels of waste chemicals were earlier discovered by the local authorities . <p> DANGEROUS FOR ALL <p> When proper protective measures are not in place , neighbouring communities will not be the only ones affected .
