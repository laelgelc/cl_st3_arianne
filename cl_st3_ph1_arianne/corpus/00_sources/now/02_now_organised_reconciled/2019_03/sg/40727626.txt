<p> The Achilles ' heel of nascent technologies such as 5G , artificial intelligence and big data , which have the potential to boost productivity and resolve social issues from eldercare to climate change , is the lack of a common global standard on data protection , a business forum heard yesterday . <p> Yet another weakness is the poor digital literacy among some quarters of the world , which means a segment of the global population could miss out on the benefits of the digital economy . <p> Business leaders from the Group of 20 ( G-20 ) bloc of the world 's largest developed and developing economies noted that the surge in protectionist sentiment worldwide should serve as a cautionary tale of the need for inclusive growth .
