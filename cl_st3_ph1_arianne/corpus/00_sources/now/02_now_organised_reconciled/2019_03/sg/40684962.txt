<h> Water conservation a sustained , collaborative effort : Halimah <p> While the Government will continue to plan and invest in infrastructure to ensure a secure , diversified and sustainable supply of water , Singaporeans must play their part and use water wisely , said President Halimah Yacob . <p> " Each drop of water does not come easy , " she said at yesterday 's launch of the annual Singapore World Water Day at Marina Barrage . " Water conservation goes beyond a mere campaign , it has to be a sustained and collaborative effort . " <p> She noted that one of the Sustainable Development Goals under the United Nations ' 2030 Agenda is having access to clean water and sanitation . Singapore is doing well on this front with its tap water well within the World Health Organisation 's drinking water guidelines . <p> It has gone a step further to transform its drains , canals and reservoirs into streams , rivers and lakes , she said . <p> " Through hard work , perseverance and ingenuity , we have overcome our physical limitation of not having sufficient land to collect and store enough rain water @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the four national taps in place - reservoirs , imported water , Newater and desalinated water - Singapore has been able to supply clean and adequate water for a growing nation . <p> " In spite of this , water security remains a challenge , " said President Halimah . " While we have diversified sources of water , imported water is still a major water source . " <p> With increasing demand and economic growth , Singapore 's total water consumption is projected to double by 2060 , she added . <p> Another factor : the effects of climate change which may hit Singapore badly , bringing about a water crisis if the country does not plan ahead . <p> President Halimah noted that the PUB has been investing in water infrastructure and several key projects have either been completed or are in progress . The Stamford Detention Tank , the Stamford Diversion Canal and the Tuas Desalination Plant were completed over the past year . <p> Other mega projects such as the Jurong Island Desalination Plant , Keppel Marina East Desalination Plant , Deep Tunnel Sewerage System Phase @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are costly but necessary investments as they will take us well through the next 50 years , " President Halimah said . <p> She urged individuals to play their part and use water wisely . <p> " Our eventual goal is for each of us to take ownership of and be proactive in transforming Singapore 's water journey , not just in the month of March , but also as a lifelong commitment , " she said . <p> Yesterday 's event was attended by about 3,000 representatives from schools , businesses and grassroots organisations , as well as Environment and Water Resources Minister Masagos Zulkifli and Senior Minister of State for the Environment and Water Resources Amy Khor . <p> PUB also launched a new year-long campaign to promote conservation and show Singaporeans how clean water is produced . <p> About 60 roadshows and events will be held islandwide this month , and water rationing exercises will take place in about 150 schools . <p> World Water Day on March 22 will also see 23 landmarks and buildings lit in blue .
