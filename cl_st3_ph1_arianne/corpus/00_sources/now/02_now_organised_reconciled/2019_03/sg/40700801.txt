<h> Brazil 's Bolsonaro to visit Trump this month <p> Jair Bolsonaro , who entered office in January , has vowed to forge close ties with Washington after over a decade of leftist rule in Brazil , and has been dubbed " South America 's Trump " <p> US President Donald Trump will host his Brazilian counterpart Jair Bolsonaro on March 19 , the White House said Friday , the first meeting between the two rightwing leaders who have lavished praise on each other . <p> The visit was announced by Brazil 's foreign ministry last month but a firm date had not been finalized . <p> Bolsonaro , who entered office in January , has vowed to forge close ties with Washington after over a decade of leftist rule in Brazil , and has been dubbed " South America 's Trump . " <p> " President Trump and President Bolsonaro will discuss how to build a more prosperous , secure , and democratic Western Hemisphere , " the White House said . <p> " The leaders of the Hemisphere 's two largest economies will also discuss opportunities for defense cooperation , pro-growth trade policies , combatting transnational crime @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , they will talk about the major role that the United States and Brazil are playing in the effort to provide humanitarian assistance to Venezuela . " <p> Bolsonaro and Trump both favor regime change in Venezuela , and are harsh critics of the Cuban and Nicaraguan governments . <p> Like Trump , Bolsonaro is a climate change sceptic , whose pro-business agenda and appointment of a like-minded right-winger as environment minister have done nothing to ease concerns about deforestation in the Amazon .
