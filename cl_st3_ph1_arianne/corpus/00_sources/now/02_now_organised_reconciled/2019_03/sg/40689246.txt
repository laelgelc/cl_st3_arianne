Singapore has in practice been supplying 16mgd of treated water at Johor 's request . The agreement expires in 2061 . <p> " Surely , your conscience will tell you that this is a raw deal for the people of Malaysia and especially Johoreans , many of whom may be your own family members . <p> " Do you not feel guilty that you are enjoying drinking water that you have bought so cheaply from your kin ?
