<p> KOTA ISKANDAR , March 20 -- The government has been urged to establish a standard operating procedure ( SOP ) for hazardous chemical disasters in light of the Sungai Kim Kim incident in Pasir Gudang , an academic said today . <p> Universiti Teknologi Malaysia Science Faculty Dean Prof Abdull Rahim Mohd Yusoff , who heads a scientific committee formed to look into the incident , said there was currently no SOP to deal with such situations . <p> " We have an SOP when it comes to natural disasters such as floods , but when comes to incidents involving chemical pollution , we do not have one . <p> " After the incident in Sungai Kim Kim , there is an urgent need for such guidelines that the authorities can follow , " said Abdull Rahim on the sidelines of a media briefing today . <p> The Sungai Kim Kim incident led to about 4,000 people being hospitalised after they inhaled the fumes emitted by the illegally dumped chemical waste . <p> Abdull Rahim said his committee had also suggested that a comprehensive air quality monitoring system be put in place in and around Pasir Gudang . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Gudang is an industrial area and a system to monitor toxic gases must be implemented , " he said . <p> Yesterday , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said the polluted section of Sungai Kim Kim had been cleaned up and the area was now safe . <p> She said the authorities will , however , continue to monitor the situation at the site for the next 15 days . <p> " The cleaning work by the contractors that started on March 13 and ended on March 17 saw a total of 900 tonnes of contaminated substance and 1,500 metric tons of contaminated water cleared , " Yeo had told a press conference . <p> The toxic pollution in Sungai Kim Kim began on March 7 after a group of students were hospitalised from inhaling fumes emitted by the waste . <p> All 111 schools in Pasir Gudang have been closed until further notice .
