<p> SYDNEY/LONDON ( Reuters ) - Tens of thousands of school students around the world walked out of classes on Friday in a global strike to protest against government inaction on climate change . <p> " Climate change is worse than Voldemort , " read a handmade sign carried by one student in Wellington , referring to the evil wizard in the hugely popular Harry Potter books and films . <p> " The oceans are rising , so are we , " read another in Sydney . <p> In Europe , students packed streets and squares in Paris , Madrid , Rome , Brussels and other cities for " Fridays for Future " protests . <p> Demonstrations also took place across the United States . In Washington , some 1,500 students rallied in front of the Capitol chanting " climate action now ! " and waving homemade placards with slogans such as " Our planet , our future . " <p> The worldwide student strike movement started in August 2018 , when 16-year-old Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg began protesting outside her parliament on school days .
