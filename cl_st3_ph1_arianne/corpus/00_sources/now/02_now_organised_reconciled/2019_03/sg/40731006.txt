REUTERS/Philippe Wojazer <p> By Leigh Thomas and Simon Carraud <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - Rioters set fire to a bank and ransacked stores on Paris 's Champs Elysees avenue on Saturday , in a new flare-up of violence as France 's yellow vest protests against President Emmanuel Macron and his pro-business reforms entered a fourth month . <p> Police fired tear gas and water cannon as the protests turned violent after weeks of relative calm , during which marches had attracted declining numbers of participants . <p> A Banque Tarneaud branch spewed flames before firefighters arrived and rescued a woman and her infant from the building , with 11 suffering minor injuries , the fire department said . <p> Rioters also set fire to an upmarket handbag store and two newsstands on the Champs Elysees , while @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cobblestones at riot police through clouds of tear gas in front of the Arc de Triomphe monument , which was ransacked at the peak of the protests in December . <p> The canvas awning was later set on fire of the swanky brasserie , known in France as the place where conservative Nicolas Sarkozy celebrated his presidential election victory in 2007 . <p> Macron cut short a weekend ski trip in the Pyrenees to return to the capital on Saturday night for a crisis meeting with ministers . <p> " We are attached to constitutional rights , but we 've got people who through all means quite simply want to make a wreck of the republic , to break things and destroy , running the risk of getting people killed , " Macron said . <p> " I want us to very precisely analyse things and as quickly as possible take strong , complementary decisions so this does n't happen again , " he told ministers . <p> Police said 42 protesters , 17 of their own officers and one firefighter were injured . <p> The interior ministry estimated 10,000 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with 3,000 on the previous Saturday . Nationwide , protesters were estimated at 32,300 , compared with 28,600 last week . <p> ULTRA VIOLENT <p> Interior Minister Christophe Castaner said that although the protest was relatively small , there had been more than 1,500 " ultra violent " people out looking for trouble . <p> " They decided , perhaps as a swansong , to come attack - and I use their words - Paris , " Castaner said , adding that more than 1,400 police officers were mobilised . <p> A separate , peaceful march against climate change through central Paris drew as many 36,000 people , police estimated . Some 145,000 people marched nationwide . <p> Yellow vest protesters had promised to draw bigger numbers to mark the fourth month since the movement erupted in mid November over since-scrapped fuel tax hikes and the cost of living . <p> Named after the high-visibility vests French drivers have to keep in their cars and worn by protesters , the revolt swelled into a broader movement against Macron , his reforms and elitism . <p> However , the weekly demonstrations @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have been generally getting smaller since December , when Paris saw some of the worst vandalism and looting in decades . <p> After the spike in violence , Macron offered a package of concessions worth more than 10 billion euros ( $11 billion ) aimed at boosting the incomes of the poorest workers and pensioners . <p> His government ordered police to crack down on the protests in January , leading to complaints of police brutality . <p> The 41-year-old former investment banker also launched a series of national debates aimed at determining what policies people want the government to focus on . Saturday 's protests coincided with the end of the debates .
