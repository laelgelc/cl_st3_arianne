<h> A river is rejuvenated : The revival of Jaipur 's Dravyavati River <p> While the rivers that flow through our cities are our lifelines , they are often ignored , abused and reduced to sewage channels and rubbish dumps . This then causes severe damage to marine life and vegetation that depend on the river and harms the people who live around them . One such river is Jaipur 's Dravyavati River , which originates from the western slopes of Nahargarh -- Amber Hills and flows through the west side of Jaipur city . Much of Jaipur city 's residents reside in the 10 k periphery of the river , which was once a source of water for the city . <p> Over the decades , due to rapid urbanisation , dumping of sewage , solid waste and industrial waste , and rapid encroachment , the river was reduced into a 47.5 km long cesspool . Major flooding that occurred in 1981 , which washed away many dams and caused a huge loss of lives and property , further aggravated the situation . The river had become a ' nallah ' where municipal wastewater , stormwater , sewage , which was discharged from 82 drains , major and minor , and industrial waste got collected and mixed . Further , the numerous encroachments along the river had also reduced its cross-section , which obstructed the flow of water . <p> The untreated waste from industries , especially from the textile mills and printing units near Sanganer , flowed into the nallah , polluting and contaminating the groundwater . Since the water was being used for cultivation purposes , it was affecting the health of residents .
