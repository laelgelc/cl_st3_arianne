<h> New report suggests that Singaporeans agree climate change is a major concern but do not feel they can make a difference <p> A new report on the Singapore public 's opinion on climate change shows that the vast majority of Singaporeans understand that manmade climate change is an urgent and serious matter , and they feel a strong responsibility to act . <p> The public research report , commissioned by NGO Climate Conversations and carried out by Singapore-based consumer research firm Milieu Insight , surveyed over 3000 Singaporeans between October 2018 and January 2019 . <p> According to the report , 92% of respondents agree that climate change is mainly due to human activities . But there is still some confusion on that . When presented with more options for the question on the causes climate change , people become less certain of their answer with responses citing natural causes rising from 6% to 13% .
