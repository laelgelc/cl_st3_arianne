Happiness is serious business for them over there . It is the journey and mindful pursuit of happiness that leads to fulfillment in one 's life , and contributes to a peace of mind . <p> Here are some serious lessons on happiness that I 've learnt during my time here : <p> 1 . HAPPINESS IS ABOUT HAVING A SENSE OF BELONGING <p> Dancers performing a cultural dance during a festival.Photo : Tourism Council of Bhutan <p> Humans have an inherent desire to have a sense of belonging , whether it is in social groups , among friends , or even in a country , sharing the same connection makes people feel secure and happy . A strong cultural identity helps to promote belonging and gives a group of people a place they can call home . <p> Every Bhutanese is proud of how far they have come . They adore their Kings , they guard their culture and traditions jealously , and they 're proud about the fact that they have never been colonised by external powers . <p> Their cultural identity is visible in the traditional costumes that they wear every day to work , school , temples and festivals . Even the designs of buildings must adhere to strict cultural guidelines , incorporating traditional Bhutanese elements on the facade . These are just part of a set of protocols or code of conduct @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , practices and traditions are maintained and not lost in an increasingly modernising Bhutan . <p> This sense of belonging allows the people to feel safe in their home country , and with that comes motivation , health and happiness . A simple life means not going out for sushi dinners or window shopping .
