<p> WELLINGTON ( REUTERS ) - New Zealanders have expressed shock after a government minister was punched in the face while walking to work , a rare attack in a country where politicians mingle freely with the public with no security cover . <p> Mr James Shaw , the climate change minister and leader of the Green Party , was walking alone to Parliament on Thursday ( March 14 ) when he was approached by a man who started talking to him before grabbing him and punching him several times in the face . <p> Two people came to help the minister but the man quickly jumped into a vehicle and drove away . Police said a 47-year-old man was later arrested and charged over the attack , and is expected to appear in court on Friday . <p> Mr Shaw , who suffered a black eye , went straight to work after the incident but later visited the hospital as a precautionary measure .
