<p> SINGAPORE - With global warming heralding new threats , resource scarcity will be the new normal . <p> So the Government is throwing its weight behind efforts to protect and provide for the country 's survival - in the areas of water , making the most of waste , food and climate change research - Environment and Water Resources Minister Masagos Zulkifli said on Thursday ( March 7 ) . <p> " Climate change is bringing new and wicked problems , " he said in Parliament . <p> " It is no coincidence that we are building more desalination and Newater plants - we need to produce water almost at will , regardless of whether the rains come . " <p> And just like the country 's water success story , the same can be done in other areas , through long-term planning decades before a problem surfaces , he said , pointing out that Newater was more than two decades in the making . <p> " Faced with a challenge , we start small , learn from others , harness technology , invest in R&amp;D .
