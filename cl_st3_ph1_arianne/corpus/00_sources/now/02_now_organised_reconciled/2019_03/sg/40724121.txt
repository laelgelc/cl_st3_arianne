<h> California declared totally drought free for first time in 7 years <p> LOS ANGELES ( Reuters ) - California was declared totally drought free for the first time in more than seven years on Thursday , following unusually abundant winter rains and snowfall statewide , according to the government 's weekly report on U.S. drought conditions . <p> The U.S. Drought Monitor 's latest survey reflected an astonishing turnaround - at least for now - from a severe , prolonged dry spell that reduced irrigation supplies to farmers , forced strict household conservation measures and stoked a spate of deadly , devastating wildfires . <p> A relatively small swath of California 's southern-most region , including most of San Diego County , remains labeled " abnormally dry " on the drought map index , as does a tiny patch at the state 's extreme northern end along the Oregon border . <p> But this week marks the first time since mid-December of 2011 that 100 percent of the state has been classified as being free of drought , defined as a moisture deficit severe enough to cause social , environmental or economic ills .
