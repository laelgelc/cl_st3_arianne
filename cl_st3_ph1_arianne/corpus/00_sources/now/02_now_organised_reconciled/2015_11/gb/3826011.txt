When the Vikings colonised Greenland in the 10th century , they were determined to preserve their tribal customs , which included raising cattle . Because pasture was limited and the growing season only three months , virtually all of the land was used to grow silage to see the cattle through the winter . After nine months indoors , cattle were so weak they had to be carried out to pasture . When the climate turned harsher this agricultural system became unsustainable and the Vikings of Greenland perished .
