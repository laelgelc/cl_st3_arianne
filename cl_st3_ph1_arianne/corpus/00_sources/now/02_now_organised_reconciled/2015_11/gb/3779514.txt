<h> Explainer : The legal form of the Paris climate agreement <p> The aim of the UN summit in Paris is to seal a universal , international agreement on avoiding dangerous climate change , that has legal force . <p> In broad terms , this means the Paris agreement is almost certain to include a legally binding treaty at its core , despite headlines to the contrary . Yet the treaty 's precise legal form remains unclear . <p> What will the treaty bind countries to do ? Will it even be called a treaty ? Carbon Brief has read the lengthy legal texts and spoken to the experts on the legal form of the Paris climate agreement -- and whether the legal form matters . <p> The Durban Platform appears to present three different options as a basis for agreement in Paris .
