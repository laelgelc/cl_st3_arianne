We often talk of ' breaking the mould ' of British politics , of offering a ' real alternative ' to the neoliberal consensus . Describing this to Professor John Barry , Green Party Northern Ireland councillor and Queen 's University lecturer , I then naively asked : " So how does all this fit into the sectarian backdrop of Northern Ireland politics ? How do the Greens here fit in with Stormont politics ?
