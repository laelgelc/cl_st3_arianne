<p> At least 150,000 adult saiga antelopes were buried during a fortnight in May , but scientists say the actual figure will be significantly higher as many more carcasses were found but not counted as part of the burials . Calves were not counted , but it is thought that hundreds of thousands died too . <p> Known for their distinctive cylindrical snout , bulging eyes and curled horns as well as their ability to survive dramatic changes in temperature , the animals are one of the most endangered species on the planet . Before the most recent die-off , the estimated population was between 250,000 and 320,000 . The die-off has only occurred in the plains of Kazakhstan , where 90% of the global population resides . <p> The mass mortality defies understanding of how biological systems normally behave , scientists have said . They believe the deaths occurred too quickly to be attributed to a transmissible disease . <p> There are no wounds or evident trauma that would point to poaching and no obvious signs of malnutrition .
