<h> Will COP21 accelerate sustainability for all ? <p> 27 November 2015 <p> From 30 November to 11 December , global leaders and influencers will be gathering in Paris for the 2015 United Nations Conference on climate change ( COP21 ) . Research from IDS , the STEPS Centre and SPRU , at the University of Sussex , highlights how we must restructure our economies and societies to address the increasing global demand for resources in a fair and just way to meet the urgent challenges of rapid climate change . <p> Reducing the use of carbon emissions should not be pursued at the expense of rights and social justice . Instead , climate policies and finance should be used to build long-term innovation capabilities and support locally-defined pathways to low carbon development . <p> Through REDD schemes , countries that have vast tracts of forests are rewarded for keeping their forests instead of cutting them down as they are reducing emissions from deforestation and forest degradation . <p> REDD programmes have been controversial with issues such as land access and tensureship for forests dependent communities , biodiversity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ carbon innovation -- in energy , transport and food -- is crucial to creating pathways to tackle climate change .
