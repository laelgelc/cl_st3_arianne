<h> Pacific Beat <h> The Pacific connects with the Caribbean on the issue of food security <p> Updated November 05 , 2015 10:20:32 <p> With the drought reaching ever further throughout the Pacific , the issue of food security is at the forefront of many minds because small island developing states are particularly vulnerable to natural disasters and climate change . <p> This week a Pacific delegation has travelled all the way to Barbados to take part in the first Pacific Caribbean AgriFood Forum , where the aim is to link farmers from the Caribbean and the Pacific , allowing them to share their experiences and learn from each other . <p> Ni-Vanuatu Samson Vilvil Fare looks after the Intra-ACP Agricultural Policy Programme at the Technical Centre of Agricultural and Rural Development ( CTA ) in the Netherlands . <p> He says it 's been valuable to hear about the experiences of countries with similar vulnerabilities to those in the Pacific .
