<h> New $1.15 million grants program to help Victorian councils respond to climate change <p> A new $1.15 million grants program has been set up in Victoria to support local councils in their efforts to respond to the challenges of climate change . <p> The Victorian Climate Change Grants 2015 will extend support to Victoria 's councils as they help their local communities and businesses prepare for climate change . A critical contributor to addressing climate change , Victoria 's local government sector has already made significant progress in driving local action . <p> Councils can use these grants to drive local climate change action including reducing greenhouse gas emissions and making effective decisions on increasing resilience of services and assets . <p> The Victorian Climate Change Grants 2015 program consists of a competitive round of grants open to all councils with amounts ranging from $20,000 to $80,000 , and a non-competitive round of grants of up to $10,000 specifically for resource-constrained councils . <p> To be eligible for the Victorian Climate Change Grants 2015 all projects must : <p> align with at least one of the two @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ vulnerable to respond to climate change <p> address priorities stated in a council strategic document relating to councils ' action on climate change , or be clearly identified as adding value to what councils are already doing as part of climate change actions , or be a part of future climate change planning ;
