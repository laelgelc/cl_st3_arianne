By taking a moment to reflect on just three facts . <p> Fact one:The climate -- average weather over a 30-year period -- has been in a state of change ever since the planet acquired an atmosphere . Change is what the climate does . <p> As Victor Frankenstein said while climbing the Montanvert glacier in 1818 : " We are but clouds that veil the midnight moon , nought may endure but Mutability " . <p> Advertisement <p> Frankie got it in one .
