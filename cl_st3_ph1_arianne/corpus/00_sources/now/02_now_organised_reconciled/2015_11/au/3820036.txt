Reuters/Petar Kujundzic/Files <p> The landmark climate lawsuit filed by 21 young Americans has gotten the attention of the fossil fuel industry , as almost all oil and gas companies in the world seek to oppose the suit brought against President Obama and the U.S. federal government . <p> The complaint , filed on Aug 12 , 2015 by youths aged eight to 19 years old , orders the government to reduce its carbon dioxide emissions . The lawsuit claims that the federal government has violated the youngest generation 's constitutional rights to life , liberty , property , as well as failed to protect essential public trust resources by supporting the use of fossil fuels . <p> Industry associations including the American Fuel and Petrochemical Manufacturers , the American Petroleum Institute and National Association of Manufacturers requested for permission to oppose the climate lawsuit . <p> " The fossil fuel industry does n't @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stricter climate change program , " claimed Gerald Torres , a law professor of Cornell University , Yahoo News reports . The organisation also claims that the federal government has not done enough to stop the danger of carbon emissions despite being aware of it since 1965 . Instead , it continued to allow and support activities that have significantly increased the atmospheric concentration of CO2 . <p> Currently , this level of concentration is around an alarming 400 parts per million ( ppm ) .
