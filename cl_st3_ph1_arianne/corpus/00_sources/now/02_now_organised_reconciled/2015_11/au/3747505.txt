Mr Hunt 's message to the renewable energy forum marks a significant shift in the Coalition 's thinking . <p> Although officially supportive of a mandatory renewable energy target , Tony Abbott 's government gave renewables no more backing than it considered absolutely necessary and , at one point , considered abandoning the renewable energy target altogether amid claims it was pushing up electricity prices and making manufacturing uncompetitive . With an abundance of sunlight , and proven expertise in renewables technology , Australia can benefit substantially if the international uptake of wind and solar energy continues . Mr Hunt 's new-found enthusiasm for renewables also bodes well for further reductions in Australia 's future greenhouse gas emissions , which are likely to come under close international scrutiny at next month 's climate change conference in Paris . <p> Government enthusiasm for renewables , at least to the point of significantly underwriting their uptake , must be tempered with the knowledge that wind turbines and photo voltaic panels are not , of themselves , guaranteed to meet the challenges of Australia 's future energy requirements . While unit costs are coming down , the capital outlay for systems capable of powering major cities remains prohibitively expensive .
