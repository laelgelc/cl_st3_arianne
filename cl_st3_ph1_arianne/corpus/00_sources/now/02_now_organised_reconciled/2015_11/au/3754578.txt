<p> Carbon-cutting pledges from 146 nations are " far from enough " to stave off dangerous global warming , the United Nations has warned , three weeks ahead of a crucial climate summit in Paris . <p> Countries have submitted pledges , known as Intended Nationally Determined Contributions ( INDCs ) , to a UN roster that will form the backbone of an agreement to be inked at the November 30 climate talks . <p> Achim Steiner , executive director of the UN Environment Program ( UNEP ) , said in a statement that countries had made " an historic level of commitment " with their pledges . <p> " However , they are not sufficient to limit global temperature rise to the recommended level of 2 degrees Celsius this century , " he said . <p> To stay within the 2C limit , which scientists say is necessary to avoid the worst impacts of climate change , global emissions levels should not exceed 42 billion tonnes in 2030 . <p> Even if all countries ' plans for emission cuts are implemented fully , emissions could rise to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 12 billion tonnes , an annual UNEP report said .
