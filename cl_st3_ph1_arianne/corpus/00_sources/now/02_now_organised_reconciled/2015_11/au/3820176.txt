These range from training agreements , to research centres funded by donations from multi-national mining companies . <p> This is a significant dimension of the increasing privatisation of Australian universities , which may be stopping climate change action . <p> While there are " fossil free " campaigns at at 15 major Australian universities , no university has committed to completely divesting their interests in fossil fuels , and just two have committed to a partial divestment . <p> In 2014 , the Australian National University ( ANU ) announced a partial divestment amid much controversy .
