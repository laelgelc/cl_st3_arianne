<p> The Marshall Islands is calling on Australia to bring forward its carbon emissions reduction target by five years to be in line with the United States . <p> Foreign Minister Tony de Brum also wants Australia to pledge " significant " funds to help developing nations like the Marshall Islands adapt to climate change and repair damage . <p> Mr de Brum believes Australia should be a team player and sit down at December 's United Nations climate change conference in Paris on the side of the Pacific Islands . <p> Australia is at the back of the developed nations pack with its pledge to slash emissions by 26 to 28 per cent by 2030 on 2005 levels and Mr de Brum has been critical of the goal . <p> The US has committed to the same targets by 2025 and Mr de Brum urged Australia to match that aim . <p> " If they move the date from 2030 to 2025 with the same figures , that 's a very good beginning , " he told AAP on Tuesday . <p> " It 's important that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not running around saying that coal is the lifesaver of the world .
