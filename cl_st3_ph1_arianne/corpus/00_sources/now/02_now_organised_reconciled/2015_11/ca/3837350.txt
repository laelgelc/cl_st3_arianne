<h> Op-Ed : Sustainability -- What is it and how does it apply to us ? <p> Sustainable and sustainability are words we hear and read about almost every day . And with so many products and activities being labeled as " green " or " sustainable , " the term has become nothing more than a lot of hot air . <p> So what does sustainability mean ? According to Dictionary.com , the word sustainable means , " pertaining to a system that maintains its own viability by using techniques that allow for continual reuse . " <p> A great example of a sustainable biological system would be healthy wetlands or a forest . In broader terms , the accepted definition of sustainability today comes to us from the Brundtland Commission of the United Nations on March 20 , 1987 . <p> The commission said sustainable development is defined as " development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs .
