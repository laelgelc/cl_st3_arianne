The masses were so attached to their antiquated , awkward " individual " rights you could n't say much out loud . Better to work indirectly through " family planning , " especially among the poor . Not today . <h> Read &amp; Debate <p> By intervening violently in the most intimate aspect of private life , the policy has torn society apart . It had already been relaxed to allow rural and ethnic minority couples two children , then urban couples if each was a single child , and now anyone . But it 's too little , too late . People still need state permission to breed , and they do n't want to . <p> Social engineering rips societies apart because people are not machines .
