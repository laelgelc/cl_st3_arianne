<p> FORT SASKATCHEWAN , Alta . - The CEO of Royal Dutch Shell Plc says a decision to back away from its Carmon Creek oilsands project last month does not mean it 's backing away from the oilsands in general . <p> Ben van Beurden told reporters last week that his company ranks investment opportunities in its global portfolio project-by-project , not region-by-region - so the whole industry can not be painted with the same brush as the halted 80,000-barrel-a-day Carmon Creek project in northwestern Alberta . <p> More important than its upfront cost was the project 's " resilience " under a variety of different scenarios , said van Beurden . <p> " The most sensible thing was to shelve it and to focus our cash elsewhere , " he said . <p> For Carmon Creek , Shell looked at a host of variables - from having the means to export the crude to sourcing the diluent needed to help the bitumen flow through pipelines . <p> " There were just too many uncertainties causing a range of outcomes from the absolutely fantastic to the absolutely disastrous @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that we can not live with , certainly not in today 's environment . " <p> Van Beurden noted Shell 's existing oilsands operations around Fort McMurray , Alta. , make money even in today 's weak market , with operating costs of $25 a barrel during the third quarter . <p> " We also , by the way , think that in the longer run , oil prices are going to stabilize at slightly higher levels than where they are today . " <p> The U.S. benchmark , West Texas Intermediate , settled below US$44 a barrel on Monday . <p> Van Beurden made his remarks during a visit to Shell 's Scotford refining and upgrading complex northeast of Edmonton last Friday , where its Quest carbon capture and storage project had its grand opening . <p> The Quest project aims to capture more than a million tonnes of carbon dioxide a year from the Scotford oilsands upgrader and store it two kilometres underground rather than release it into the atmosphere . <p> The event came on the same day as U.S. President Barack Obama 's rejection of the contentious @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ direct route for 830,000 barrels a day of oilsands crude to make its way to the lucrative U.S. Gulf Coast market . <p> Much of the opposition that contributed to Keystone XL 's downfall was focused on the pipeline 's role in enabling more oilsands development - and more carbon emissions as a result . <p> Van Beurden said there 's been a " general frustration " about a lack of progress on climate change and that 's spilled over into the pipeline debate .
