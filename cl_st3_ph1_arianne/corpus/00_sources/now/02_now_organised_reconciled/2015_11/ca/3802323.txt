<h> Sandi 's story : Sustainability and climate change : two sides of the same issue <h> Sandi Stride <p> This article continues the series of personal stories ahead of the Paris Climate Change negotiations that begin on Nov. 30 . Environment Hamilton asked some local residents why they are motivated to take action on climate change . <p> Today 's story is from Sandi Stride , a Hamilton homeowner and businessperson . Sandi is working to help businesses create sustainable prosperity in Hamilton and Burlington . <p> Q. When did you first hear about the dangers of climate change ? <p> A. I 've felt a connection to nature since I was a teen ; I studied geography , ecology and environmental science and learned then of some of the climate change issues . <p> Q. How did you feel when you first heard about the dangers of climate change ? <p> A. Three words come to mind -- Horror , Indignation , and Urgency . I felt horror at the implications for earth , for our society and for us . I felt indignation that we @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ do something about it . <p> Q. What made you take action ? <p> A. First , having studied environmental science and then going to business school , I wanted to try and bridge the gap between the environment and the economy . I saw a need to get business on board . Second , being a parent and grandparent is a huge motivator . I see it as very much a moral and ethical issue ; I think the more people realize the implications , the more they will take action . <p> Q. What did you do / what are you doing ? <p> A. Most of my professional life has been directed at sustainability . In addition to providing sustainability consulting advice to businesses , I 've worked with several groups and advisory committees to develop ways to address climate change . I worked with a group that got Hamilton city council to sign up to a Climate Charter and then establish a Community Climate Action Plan , which was recently approved by council . You ca n't talk about sustainability without talking about climate change . We try to help companies discover opportunities to grow and prosper by reducing their environmental impact and providing greater social value . <p> Our members are setting greenhouse gas emission reduction targets and reducing or eliminating negative environmental impacts . For example , one company has achieved zero waste to landfill . Another has prevented over 10 million pounds of synthetic chemicals from being released into the environment . <p> One of our next priorities is to develop a climate change tool kit for businesses . Staying on top of developments at the Paris Climate talks is also important , to understand what needs to be done next . <p> In my personal life I 'm working to reduce my household emissions . I grow some of my own food , compost waste , and try to be as efficient as possible in using energy .
