<p> Sectors representing about 95 per cent of Canada 's GDP would face little competitive pressure if a B.C.-style carbon price was applied in all provinces , according to a report Wednesday from some of the country 's top economists . <p> Ontario 's economy would feel it even less . Sectors that would take the biggest hit include steel , petrochemicals , fertilizer and refining , which together represent a quarter of the province 's emissions but surprisingly just 1 per cent of GDP . <p> " Overall , the business community should not perceive carbon pricing as a significant economic threat , " concludes the EcoFiscal Commission , an independent coalition of Canadian economists created to inform policymakers . <p> Carbon pricing can take many forms , including a tax applied to emissions from fossil fuel use , like B.C.
