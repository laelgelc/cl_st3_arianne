<h> School of Public Policy says Obama rejection of Keystone simply an act of symbolism <p> You can add the School of Public Policy to the growing list of Canadians disappointed by President Obama 's decision to reject the Keystone XL Pipeline . <p> The school says the US President 's decision is simply symbolism to pick on the oil sands so that he looks good at the UN Climate Change Conference in Paris later this month . <p> Gaetan Caron , with the School Of Public Policy says Canada has a clean and safe environmental record . <p> " You can not find a country better regulated in Canada in terms of the economic and environmental and safety regulations of it 's energy sector , so hopefully this is just a low point in the history of market economies and energy security for the North American continent " says Caron . <p> In 2013 Alberta 's emissions totaled around 267 megatonnes according to environment Canada , which accounted for 36.8% of the country 's total emissions .
