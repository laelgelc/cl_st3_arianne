<p> Keystone XL was to be just one of many pipelines , among other modes of transportation , shipping Alberta oil across North America . But environmental activists in the United States set their sights on Keystone XL and convinced President Barack Obama to reject the proposal in the name of climate change . David Roberts explains why . <p> The full interview is available in the audio player above .
