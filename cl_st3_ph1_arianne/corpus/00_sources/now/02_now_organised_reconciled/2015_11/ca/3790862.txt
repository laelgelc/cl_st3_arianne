<p> The Sask . Party government is literally " selling the farm " by selling off Crown farmland at below market value for a one-time gain to help balance the budget , says Public Pastures -- Public Interest , an environmental group formed to prevent the sale of federally owned community pastures . <p> " These properties under the Wildlife Habitat Protection Act ( WHPA ) have a long-term value that ca n't be given a bargain basement price and liquidated , " said PPPI co-chair Trevor Herriot in a press release Monday . <p> Earlier this month , Agriculture Minister Lyle Stewart announced that 600,000 acres of Crown land would be offered for sale to current leaseholders . " All agricultural Crown land , including cultivated , grazing and hay land that is deemed to have no public value will be sold under this program , " the ministry said in press release . <p> A 15 per cent discount will be offered to lessees until March 31 , 2016 , then reduced to 10 per cent for the rest of 2016 . " In the future , we may want to use our Crown farmland to help young farm families get started or to foster more sustainable land use practices . That will not be possible if we just sell Crown assets at discount prices to a select group of farmers and then put most of the revenue into the treasury to balance the budget . " <p> Herriot noted some of the Crown-owned acres on the auction block are WHPA lands identified in the 1980s and ' 90s as having significant biodiversity value . " All Crown land has ecological value , " Herriot said .
