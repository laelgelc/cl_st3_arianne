<p> OTTAWA -- The Canadian delegation headed to Paris for climate change talks will include Assembly of First Nations National Chief Perry Bellegarde -- a move the aboriginal leader sees as significant . <p> " When did that happen historically ? That 's never happened , " Bellegarde said in an interview with The Canadian Press . " We 're going to build upon that openness . " <p> Bellegarde , who was invited to the conference by Environment Minister Catherine McKenna , said the new Liberal government is working to build a more respectful relationship with First Nations . <p> " It 's a whole new shift , " he said . " We want to build upon the positive energy that exists so that indigenous peoples are involved in every shape and form when any policy and-or legislation is being developed that impacts on our people and impacts on our rights . " <p> It is critical for indigenous people to be part of the conversation on climate change , Bellegarde added . <p> " We pretty much have to be there ... we are really @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " he said . <p> " Our people still are of the land and of the water ... we still hunt , we still fish , we still trap , we still gather medicines ... the indigenous peoples , we say we have rights ... but we also have responsibilities as protectors and stewards of the land and water . " <p> In a statement Thursday , McKenna said climate change is a collective responsibility and that the federal government is committed to reaching an ambitious global agreement . <p> " At home , we will ensure national leadership , join with the provinces and territories to take action on climate change , put a price on carbon and reduce carbon pollution , " McKenna said . <p> Prime Minister Justin Trudeau , who will also attend the Paris conference , has promised to work out a national climate change strategy with the premiers 90 days after the summit . <p> Bellegarde said he has not received an invitation to the followup meeting with the premiers but he plans on asking for one . <p> " I think that would send @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ states , when they see Canada fully involving indigenous peoples , jointly developing strategies and plans and programs going forward , jointly working on the appropriate target , " he said . <p> On Monday , Trudeau said he was pleased to see a united commitment to fight climate change following a meeting with provincial and territorial leaders . <p> " It is clear that the way forward for Canada will be in a solution that resembles Canada , that is shared values and a shared desire for outcomes and different approaches to achieve those outcomes right across this great country , " Trudeau said . <p> Bellegarde said his organization will continue to push Canada for full inclusion . <p> " We 've got to push nation states for recognition of rights and title , " he said . <p> The national chief also said First Nations understand the need to strike the right balance between development and environmental protection -- a concern raised this week by Saskatchewan Premier Brad Wall . <p> " There 's ... the understanding that , in the long term , there is only one @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that balance , " Bellegarde said .
