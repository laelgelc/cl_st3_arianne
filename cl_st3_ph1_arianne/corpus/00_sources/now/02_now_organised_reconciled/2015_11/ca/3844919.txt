<h> VOICE OF THE PEOPLE NOV. 30 , 2015 <p> Yet another conference on climate change ; or , how to maintain the status quo while encountering an already visible problem . <p> Unlike Capt . Smith on the Titanic , we have teams of researchers giving fair warning of the hazards ahead .
