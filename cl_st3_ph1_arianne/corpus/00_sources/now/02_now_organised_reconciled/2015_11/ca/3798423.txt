<h> SPOTLIGHT ADVERTISEMENT <h> Wildlife Photographer of the Year 2015 <p> Wildlife Photographer of the Year exhibition is returning to the Royal Ontario Museum for its third consecutive year , with some of the very best wildlife photos taken this year on display . This year 's big winner is Canadian photographer Don Gutoski 's Tale of two foxes , a powerful image featuring a red fox preying upon an arctic fox . A symbol of the effects of climate change , and the very real consequence of the changes in habitat due to global warming . <p> This year the exhibition moves out from the ROM 's basement to the third floor and features photos from 11 different categories . Categories include black &amp; white , urban wildlife , photo journalism , and several categories for young photographers under 18 . In addition to the still photos , two time lapse images are also included . <p> Each photograph is framed with a special lightbox , letting each image pop-out .
