While the province is abundantly supplied with water , it 's a finite vital resource . If we let that apparent abundance of water lull us into taking it for granted , we could find ourselves coming up short of usable water . <p> The rainy season might not seem the time to be worrying about water supplies , but on southern Vancouver Island , we know how quickly the wet season can be replaced by extended drought . Most of us are aware that one or two dry winters could result in low reservoir levels and water rationing . <p> As part of implementing new regulations , the province faces a huge task in assessing the quantity , quality and distribution of B.C.
