Canada and the Green Climate Fund <p> I know it might not seem like it when many of us scramble every month to make ends meet , but relative to the rest of the world , Canada is filthy rich . <p> Canada 's GDP in 2013 was USD $1.58 trillion , or $43,100 per person , putting us in the top 10 wealthiest countries in the world . To compare , the GDP of Kenya in 2013 was USD $79 billion , which works out to $1,800 per person . Harper 's decarbonization by 2100 that he signed onto with other G7 nations is not a BHAG . <p> A goal of decarbonization by 2100 is more like a WWUG ( pronounced : " woog " ) : Wimpy Waffley Uninspiring Goal . <p> According to reports from the G7 meeting where the WWUG was agreed to , the original thought was a stated goal somewhere around 2050 , but Canada , along with Japan , whined about it until they got their way . Hence , a WWUG of 2100 , instead of a BHAG like 2050 was signed to paper .
