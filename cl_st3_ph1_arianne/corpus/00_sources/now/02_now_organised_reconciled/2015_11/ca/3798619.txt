In order to achieve food security , issues like agriculture , poverty , economy , environment and climate change must all be brought to the table . <p> " It 's an issue that is complicated and inter-related , " said Bruce Kelly , environmental program co-ordinator for Farm and Food Care Ontario and a 20-year pork producer . <p> One key issue that has to be addressed in talking about food security , Kelly explained , is climate change .
