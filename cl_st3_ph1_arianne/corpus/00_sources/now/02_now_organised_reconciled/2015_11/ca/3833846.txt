<h> Climate change policy strengthens hand : chief <p> EDMONTON - Alberta 's new climate change policy strengthens the hand of First Nations as they argue for a greater role in planning energy development , says an aboriginal leader . <p> " Anything that 's done to our land , our resources , meaningful consultation needs to happen before anything starts , " said Steve Courtoreille , Grand Chief of the Treaty 8 First Nations , whose land is where most oilsands development has occurred . <p> The role of aboriginal people is a prominent feature of the panel report that formed the basis of the government 's new policy announced last Sunday . <p> " We have integrated throughout our recommendations , and addressed specifically , the importance of full inclusion of Aboriginal Peoples in action on climate change , " it says . <p> The report recommends " concrete partnerships " with First Nations on renewable energy and climate change adaptation . It also says aboriginal communities should be sheltered from the effects of carbon reduction and a centre for traditional knowledge should be created . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ land and water ca n't be separated from the issue of climate change . <p> First Nations have long argued that they should be involved in designing projects rather than being simply presented with completed plans and asked for approval . <p> They have also demanded a stronger role in monitoring the effects of development , not only by conducting the work themselves but also in directing what is monitored . <p> Aboriginals want to ensure the right questions are being asked to address their concerns about the health effects of development , Courtoreille said . <p> " We 're not saying no development . Do it in a responsible way . " <p> Environment Minister Shannon Phillips said the government will assist First Nations in developing the technical capacity to contribute to better environmental planning and monitoring . <p> " We need to take seriously our work around building capacity , so that indigenous people can meaningfully participate in the growing of a greener economy such that the new economy does not replicate the exclusions of the old , " she said . <p> The government could also consider how @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ auctioning off renewable power credits , she added . <p> " The government could consider weighting how we do those auctions towards projects that can demonstrate meaningful participation by indigenous people . " <p> Phillips agreed that aboriginals should have input into how proposals are designed and monitored . <p> " We do intend to work collaboratively to ensure we are getting it right on traditional land use . " <p> She promised more information by the end of the year or early in 2016 . <p> Courtoreille said he thinks the greenhouse gas emissions cap placed on oilsands development is a good idea . He called the policy a good first step and is waiting to hear how the government plans to follow up . <p> " I 'd like to see where we can go from here . We 're waiting to respond to what are the next steps . "
