<h> The Spacer , a winning combination of classical and electronica Special <p> Digital Journal speaks to Tapio Kangas and Norman Hall , two talented musicians who formed an artistic bond 30 years ago and who are now hard at work on a brand new project that should be out next year . <p> Finnish-born Tapio Kangas met American Norman Hall when he was studying for a music and marketing degree in the US in the mid-1980s . Although Kangas came from a classical background and Hall was a rock fan , the two instantly hit it off and began writing music together . <p> Calling themselves The Spacer , the ambitious pair created an exciting soundscape , drawing inspiration from a variety of genres , until 1993 when Kangas returned to Finland to do his military service .
