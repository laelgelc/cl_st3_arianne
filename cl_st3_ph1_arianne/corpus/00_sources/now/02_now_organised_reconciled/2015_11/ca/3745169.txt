<h> Trudeau , team of 30 cabinet members sworn in to kick off new Liberal era <p> OTTAWA - Justin Trudeau kicked off a new Liberal era Wednesday with a 30-member cabinet that features predominantly fresh faces , an equal number of men and women and probably the most diverse line-up of ministers in Canadian history . <p> The newly minted prime minister emerged Wednesday from the formal swearing-in ceremony boasting that he 's put together a cabinet " that looks like Canada .
