<p> A Prince Edward County organization is seeking a judicial review of the Renewable Energy Approvl for turbines in South Marysburgh and Athol . <p> " It asks for the REA to be set aside as unauthorized , invalid and infringing on the rights of rural Ontario communities and on the provisions of national and international agreements and treaties , " said Garth Manning , director , with CCSAGE Naturally Green ( County Coalition for Safe and Appropriate Green Energy ) . <p> The application was filed Nov. 30 to the Divisional Court in Ottawa . <p> CCSAGE , a not-for -profit corporation , seeks judicial review of the process by which on July 16th the Ontario Ministry of Environment and Climate Change issued a Renewable Energy Approval for the White Pines wind energy project of 27 , perhaps 29 , turbines in Prince Edward County . As part of this REA approval process , on Sept. 11th , 2015 , the Minister of Natural Resources and Forests issued a permit for the project to kill , harm and harass endangered or threatened species at risk . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ claiming that the Ontario government 's approval process has violated several constitutional rights of rural citizens and communities as well as international treaties and agreements . " <p> CCSAGE NG was formed to work with citizens and other groups on " Green Energy " initiatives of governments and industry that are safe and appropriate for the citizens , the wildlife and the natural and heritage environments of Prince Edward County . <p> It supports other groups appealing two wind energy projects in the County -- The Alliance to Protect Prince Edward County ( APPEC ) and The Prince Edward County Field Naturalists ( PECFN ) . <p> However , notes Manning , Ontario 's Green Energy Act permits Environmental Review Tribunal ( ERT ) appeals only on grounds of serious harm to humans or serious and irreversible harm to animal and/or plant life and to the natural environment . <p> " That Act does not permit ERT appeals on any other grounds such as biased approval processes , denial of natural justice , violation of constitutional rights , harm to local economies , harm to tourism , harm to heritage assets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ treaties and agreements . CCSAGE NG has therefore filed its application for a Judicial Review of this project 's approval process , in an effort to restore equity , accountability and justice . " <p> " At an ERT , it is difficult for citizens to get a fair hearing of their grievances against government-approved wind projects because the ERT process is heavily biased in favour of the wind energy developer and the government ministries that approve its projects , " said Anne Dumbrille , CCSAGE chair . " ERTs are government-appointed tribunals that follow government rules and use taxpayer-funded lawyers to permit destruction of environmentally sensitive areas and to deny natural justice to local citizens who have constitutionally assured rights and freedoms . Our only recourse is to Canada 's courts , where rules of equitable justice prevail , " she said . <p> In preparing the Application , CCSAGE NG had the benefit of considerable research contributed by five students from the Osgoode Hall Law School at York University .
