<p> Screening of " This Changes Everything " will be followed by panel discussion and audience question and answer . <p> Suggested donation is $10 . All welcome . <p> It event is hosted by The Council of Canadians ( National ) , The Council of Canadians ( Sudbury Chapter ) , Laurentian University School of the Environment and Coalition for a Liveable Sudbury , in association with Sudbury Indie Cinema . <p> " This Changes Everything " presents seven powerful portraits of communities on the front lines of both fossil fuel extraction and the climate crisis it is driving . Interwoven with these stories of struggle is Naomi Klein 's narration , connecting the carbon in the air with the economic system that put it there . <p> The film builds to its powerful key idea : that in addressing the crisis of climate change we can transform our failed economic system into something radically better . <p> The film is based on the critically acclaimed non-fiction best-seller by Klein , an award-winning journalist and author . <p> " Traveling around the world with the book in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bold , visionary responses . .. The real leadership is coming from the streets , from communities : we 're honoured to tell these stories and bring these voices to a wider audience , " she said . <p> Elaine Porter , a member of the Sudbury Chapter of the Council of Canadians , said the group is happing to bring " This Changes Everything " to the city . <p> " We see the impact of climate change in our own community , in the form of increased storms and flooding , the growth and spreading of invasive species , in the arrival of lyme disease , and in shorter snow seasons , " Porter says . <p> " We see the impact around the world . And , we see a growing need and desire for a better world where we care for each other and for our Earth . We are thrilled to be bringing this film to Sudbury , on the territory of the Atikameksheng Anishnawbek First Nation , and we encourage everyone to attend . "
