<h> External Links <p> ( Note : CBC does not endorse and is not responsible for the content of external links . ) <p> The Manitoba government plans to introduce legislation to protect the province 's wetlands . <p> In June 2014 , the province released a " surface water management strategy , " and now it 's introducing the legislation to go with it . <p> The bill will introduce a host of measures , including creating water quality targets for nutrients , increasing fines for illegal drainage to a maximum of $500,000 , making landowners compensate the province for any wetlands lost on their property and establishing a " lake friendly stewards alliance . " <p> Provincial officials said protecting the wetlands helps prevent greenhouse gases from entering the atmosphere , and the bill is part of Manitoba 's forthcoming climate change strategy deemed " TomorrowNow .
