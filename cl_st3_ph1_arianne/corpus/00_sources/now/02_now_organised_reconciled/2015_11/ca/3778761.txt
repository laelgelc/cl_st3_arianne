<h> Alberta 's ' accelerated ' phasing out of coal worries industry <p> A faster phase-out of coal-fired plants is at the top of Premier Rachel Notley 's climate-change strategy for Alberta . One industry group fears the phase-out might mean the loss of thousands of jobs .
