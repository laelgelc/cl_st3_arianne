<p> After a moment of silence , one lawmaker spontaneously began to sing the French National Anthem , a song whose lyrics oppose the tyranny and foreign invasion the country has faced in its past . Watching the video of that moment , you see cocked heads and curious eyes shift towards the lone-chanter during the first few seconds of song . Soon , others join in and by the end several representatives can be seen wiping their eyes . <p> The scene parallels a similar moment in the Canadian House of Commons in October of last year following the attack on Parliament Hill . Stephen Harper , Justin Trudeau and Thomas Mulcair all exchanged what appeared to be genuine , and in some cases tearful hugs on the floor of the House of Commons . <p> Just days before the shooting in Ottawa , they 'd been posturing , setting political and rhetorical traps for one another in question period . But on the day after the shootings , Stephen Harper himself had this to say : " Today more than ever before , I am quite @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in good health . " <p> Most days , of course , our party leaders appear anything but pleased to see one another . The responses offered to questions in the House of Commons suggest that even the simplest of queries are unwelcome . <p> Moments of unity across party lines are rare in politics . The most recent coalition-governments we 've had in Canada have happened during wartime . A threat from something outside of our borders seems to be the best catalyst for helping us remember how much exactly we have in common within them . <p> Attacks like those in Paris last week , and in Ottawa a year ago , are the collective equivalent of a near-death experience . They put into perspective those things that matter most , and give us pause from trying to solve problems that do n't really exist . <p> Like the near death experience of an individual , there are those are able to learn from those moments , and change our ways , and others who soon forget the lessons offered , and quickly return to the way of life @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ after the attack on parliament before the Canadian House of Commons rekindled it 's usual , chaotic and toxic atmosphere . <p> In a few weeks , Members of Parliament will arrive in Ottawa for their first sitting since the October election .
