<p> Friends and fights . These are the things that often define our political leaders , especially in the early going . <p> Of friends , Justin Trudeau has many these days . He gained thirty new ones on Wednesday when he appointed the most female , most fresh-faced , most diverse and most **33;210;TOOLONG cabinet in Canadian political history . Why ? Because it 's 2015 as Trudeau told the media , instantly winning the caption contest accompanying the official portrait of his new cabinet . <p> But also because he 's in the business of cementing new friendships -- with women and first-time voters , with British Columbia and French-speaking Quebec and with the multicultural and multi-ethnic communities of the 905 . It 's called coalition building and one gets the feeling that Trudeau 's team knows exactly what it 's up to as it forges the base of a new Liberal Party , one engineered to recapture the political centre and deliver future election victories . <p> Still , it would be unfair to suggest that electoral ambitions are all that motivates Canada 's new @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , which is now decidedly out of fashion . Cynicism was the fuel of Stephen Harper 's time in office . His government fed off it and , frequently , fed it back to Canadians . They relied on cynicism to lower people 's expectations , their interest and their engagement . In this , as in so many other ways , Trudeau is -- and will work hard to be seen as -- the opposite of his predecessor . <p> In fact , the secret to Trudeau 's ' sunny ways ' , the reason that his approach seems to resonate so visibly with so many Canadians , is that it 's honest . It 's a sincere and direct reflection of the man who now occupies Langevin . He wears a smile far more easily than a scowl . And , at least for these first few months , so will his government . <p> There is no brighter and better example of this than Ottawa 's own cabinet representative , Catherine McKenna . She is the living , breathing personification of Trudeau 's chosen slogan of hope and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Liberal nomination in Ottawa Centre . She was n't supposed to win the election against incumbent Paul Dewar . She was n't supposed to help catalyze the highest riding voter turnout in all of Canada . And she certainly was n't supposed to end up in cabinet , handed the enormous responsibility of guiding Canada 's environmental and climate change policies . And yet , there she stands having conquered each and every one of these obstacles . <p> How did she do it ? Unlimited quantities of energy , enthusiasm and effort . An infectious , irresistible good nature . Smarts and hustle . And an old-fashioned , refreshing desire to do good things for the sheer satisfaction of getting good things done . In Trudeau 's ongoing campaign to win new friends she will be an asset of incomparable value . To boot , she 's a human rights lawyer . An expert on international affairs . Founder of a successful NGO . And a mother of three . There is no hyperbole that goes too far , no description too over-the-top . And fights are not so far away even for one as optimistic as the new prime minister . Already , there are whispered grumbles from the " been there , done that " crowd that lingers over Ottawa like a rain cloud of gloom . <p> Too young . Too inexperienced . Too ovarian . <p> That 's their quick review of the cabinet so far . In the awesome flush of Trudeau 's current triumph , they take care to register their concerns quietly and narrowly . But they 're sure that they 're right and that others will soon come around to their way of thinking . Knowingly , they tut-tut that Trudeau has taken the symbolism of change a few steps too far . That he 's letting expectations get out of hand . That he ca n't possibly unleash departments like Finance and Defence and Justice to a bunch of newbies . They predict trouble ahead . In a half-hearted way they rather hope for it . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . Overstuffed with fossilizing remnants of earlier regimes ( alongside the occasional remarkable Canadian , it should be acknowledged ) , the upper chamber is always happy to tell people in charge how it used to be done so much better . These days , that institutional arrogance comes with even more-than-usual edge . <p> The new Conservative caucus Chair in the Senate , Linda Frum , commands the institution 's majority . She has yet to tip her hand as to how she 'll proceed -- obstructionist or cooperative . But she at least has the benefit of good sense . Let 's hope she keeps the hawks in check . <p> Oddly , the greater challenge may come from the ranks of remaining former Liberal senators . Banished to exile in early 2014 by Trudeau , many of them now bleat publicly about feeling disrespected and under-loved . Lately , a few have even taken to sending public signals that if the new prime minister hopes to see his legislative agenda proceed , then maybe he should start to court their favour . <p> The cheek of these people @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ contempt in which they are held by Canadians . To imagine that they might respond to the strength of Trudeau 's democratic mandate , while still in the disgraced shadow of Duffy , Wallin , Brazeau and Harb , by threatening to hold hostage the proper functioning of Parliament ? It is astonishing . <p> For Trudeau , already positioned as an agent of change and an instrument of reform , it would be hard to imagine a more welcome fight . Pitted against a gaggle of appointed , self-entitled senators his victory would be utterly assured , thoroughly savage , and cheered from sea to sea to sea . <p> In these early days Trudeau already enjoys the benefit of good friends .
