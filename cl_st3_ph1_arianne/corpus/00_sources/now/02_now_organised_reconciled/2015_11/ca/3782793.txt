" You will implement our government 's plan to help Canada 's agriculture sector be more innovative , safer , and stronger . " <p> Climate change and the environment figured prominently in the directive to MacAulay . <p> The agriculture sector will need to focus on water and soil conservation and development issues to help it adjust to climate change , the letter said .
