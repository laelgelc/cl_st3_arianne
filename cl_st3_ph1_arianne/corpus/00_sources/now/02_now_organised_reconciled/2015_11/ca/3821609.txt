<h> Company and UWO Research Park plead guilty <p> Combined fines of nearly $97,000 have been imposed on Woodland Biofuels Inc. and the UWO Research Park because of a waste water discharge in 2013 . <p> Guilty pleas to allowing a waste water discharge into Perch Creek that may impair water quality were entered Tuesday in Sarnia provincial offences court . <p> Following an investigation of the August 2013 incident by the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change , charges were laid under the Ontario Water Resources Act . <p> An agreed statement of facts was presented to the court . <p> Woodland had a lease agreement with the University of Western Ontario Research and Development Park on Modeland Road to establish a pilot plant that would convert woodchips into ethanol . <p> The objective was development of a low-cost , environmentally-friendly alternate energy source . <p> On Aug. 26 , 2013 a test run of the plant started . But the waste water was inappropriate for the sanitary system . <p> During the time of the discharge rain caused the storm water pond to discharge into Perch Creek . <p> There were 266,000 litres of waste water put into the storm water system , but 55,000 litres were recovered from the storm water pond and ditches . <p> It 's unknown how much waste water went into Perch Creek , a fish habitat . <p> The waste water contained petroleum hydrocarbons and volatile organic compounds .
