Thus nothing Canada can do -- up to and including closing down the entire economy -- would have any impact . <p> The Harper government committed to an Intended Nationally Determined Contribution ( INDC ) of a 30 per cent reduction in emissions by 2030 , but nobody has the slightest clue how that will be achieved , apart from airy-fairy Ecofiscal Green Shiftery , just as nobody had a clue how Jean Chretien 's commitments under Kyoto would be achieved .
