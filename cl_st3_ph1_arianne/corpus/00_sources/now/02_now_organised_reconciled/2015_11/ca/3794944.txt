<p> With five summits planned for his first five weeks in office , here 's an easy prime ministerial decision for Justin Trudeau : Stay in the office more , roam the world less . <p> He 's entitled to a couple of inaugural honeymoon trips , by all means . But it 's time for Trudeau to catch up on his homework closer to home -- and get ready to give the premiers more face time . <p> Fresh from his overseas debut at the weekend G20 meeting in Turkey , the PM could streamline his summitry by pulling out of the most pointless trip on his itinerary : CHOGM . <p> That would be the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting , perhaps the most obscure and irrelevant meeting of middle and middling powers on the global map .
