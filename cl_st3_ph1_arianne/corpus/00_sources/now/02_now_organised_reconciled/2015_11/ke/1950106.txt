<h> Poor countries deserve climate justice from G20 <h> share <p> Poor people everywhere are the most vulnerable during disasters . Mainly due to their lack of wherewithal , they are usually the hardest hit by both natural and man-made disasters . <p> This is the reason why , during calamities , the media focus is mainly on impact on poor communities , with humanitarian organisations making appeals for aid to alleviate suffering . <p> Unfortunately , the poor often lack strong and united voice with which to demand more resources for mitigation and adaptation to calamities . <p> For instance , the onset of El Nino rains in Kenya is taking toll on poor communities , with several deaths already experienced occasioned by mudslides and flash floods .
