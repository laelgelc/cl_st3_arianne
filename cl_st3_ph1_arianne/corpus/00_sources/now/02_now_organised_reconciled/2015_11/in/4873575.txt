It is a morale booster , " Noor Jehan said as she welcomed scores of BJP leaders and media persons who flocked her home in the nondescript village Beri Dariyawan , which is some 25 kms from here . <p> Noor Jehan provides solar lamps to villagers at a moderate cost of about Rs 3.3 a day or Rs 100 per month , helping about 50 families in the village where government infrastructure is virtually non-existent and electricity has still not reached . <p> Giving examples of how some common people are contributing in their own way towards energy conservation and energy efficiency , Modi during his radio address cited the case of Noor Jehan of Kanpur @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ set up a factory of solar ' lalteins ' ( lamps ) . <p> Hailing Noor Jehan , he said she is working as per the meaning of her name -- ' giving light to the world ' .
