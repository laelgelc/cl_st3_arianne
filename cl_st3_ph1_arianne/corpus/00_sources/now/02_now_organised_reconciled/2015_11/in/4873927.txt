<h> Developed World Should Walk the Climate Talk : Javadekar <p> Protestors clash with riot police during a rally against global warming on November 29 , 2015 in Paris , a day ahead of the start of UN conference on climate change COP21 . ( AFP ) <p> NEW DELHI/PARIS : On a day when protestors linked hands in Paris , giving an emotional jolt to world leaders flying into the French capital to try and save the Earth from climate catastrophe , Prime Minister Narendra Modi said it was the responsibility of all to ensure that the Earth 's temperature did not rise as global warming was already creating disasters like the recent heavy rainfall in Chennai . <p> In Paris , Environment Minister Prakash Javadekar said providing carbon space to developing countries was in favour of earth , developing countries and the developed world itself . The developed world was using two-thirds of the carbon space available , " It should walk the talk , " he said . <p> Meanwhile , a group of protesters wearing black hoods and scarves over their faces chanted " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take away our right to protest " , referring to the measures restricting demonstrations introduced after the November 13 attacks . Around 100 people were arrested . <p> Hours before leaving to attend the global meet on climate change in Paris , Modi , speaking in his monthly radio programme on AIR , ' Mann Ki Baat ' , recalled his proposal for setting up a SAARC disaster response mechanism and said the recently-held ' table talk exercise and best practices seminar ' in Delhi was a good beginning in this regard . <p> He also urged people to go for solar appliances to avoid global warming .
