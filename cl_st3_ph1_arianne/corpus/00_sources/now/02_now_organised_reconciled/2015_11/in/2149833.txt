<h> The declaration drafted after deliberations by over 1,000 delegates from 40 countries and 20 States in 25 sessions recommended effective funding mechanism by creating national disaster mitigation fund . <p> Four-day World Congress on Disaster Management concluded on Sunday with the unanimous adoption of ' Visakhapatnam Declaration ' calling for a comprehensive action plan for a disaster-resilient society . <p> The declaration drafted after deliberations by over 1,000 delegates from 40 countries and 20 States in 25 sessions recommended effective funding mechanism by creating national disaster mitigation fund for resource implementation of local plans and building a platform of knowledge management / good practices / innovations at local level . <p> It mooted setting up of an independent experts ' group with statutory powers like a State Disaster Management Authority for formulating policies on disaster management . <p> Describing the declaration as ' most comprehensive documentation ' listing out priorities and strategies for disaster management and preventive measures , retired IAS officer and Bihar Disaster Management Authority Vice-Chairman A.K.
