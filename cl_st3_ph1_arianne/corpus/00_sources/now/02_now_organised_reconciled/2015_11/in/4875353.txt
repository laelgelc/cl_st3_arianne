( AFP Photo ) <p> A solid majority of Americans say the United States should join an international treaty to limit the impact of global warming , but on this and other climate-related questions , opinion divides sharply along partisan lines , according to the latest New York Times/CBS News poll . <p> Two-thirds of Americans support the United States joining a binding international agreement to curb growth of greenhouse gas emissions , but a slim majority of Republicans remain opposed , the poll found .
