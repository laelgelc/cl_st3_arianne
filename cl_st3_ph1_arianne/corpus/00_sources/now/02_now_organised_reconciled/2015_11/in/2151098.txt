about the country 's view points and guiding principles . <p> TNN Nov 23 , 2015 , 10.47 PM IST <p> NEW DELHI : Seeking political consensus over India 's stand ahead of the Paris climate summit , the Union environment minister Prakash Javadekar on Monday met the CPM leader Sitaram Yechury and briefed him about the country 's view points and guiding principles . <p> The minister had earlier met leaders of the Congress and Samajwadi Party and discussed with them the country 's stand as far as climate negotiation in Paris is concerned . <p> The climate summit will take place from November 30 to December 11 in Paris where the nations are expected to come out with a global climate deal , ensuring post-2020 joint actions by 196 countries to cut carbon emissions and adapt to the challenges of climate change . <p> India 's Prime Minister Narendra Modi and over 100 head @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Obama and Chinese president Xi Jinping , will attend the inaugurals of the summit on November 30 . <p> India will launch an alliance of solar rich countries on the occasion . <p> Yechury is learnt to have placed the CPM 's point of views on the issues of climate finance and transfer of technology before Javadekar during the meeting , asking him to go for the global climate deal that ensures that the developed countries should bear the cost of mitigation and adaptation in developing and poor countries . <p> The CPM leader also told Javadekar that the deal would come in such a manner that the rich nations should transfer green technology to their poordeveloping counterparts , including India , while taking care of Intellectual Property Rights ( IPRs ) . <p> At this stage , all the political parties appear to be on the same page with the government over all the key negotiating points
