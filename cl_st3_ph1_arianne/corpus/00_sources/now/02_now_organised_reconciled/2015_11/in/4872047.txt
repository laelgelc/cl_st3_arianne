A new IEA report on India 's energy outlook shows India would account for 25 per cent of the rise in global energy use to 2040 , exhibiting the largest growth in coal and oil consumption , as well as becoming a major player in renewable energy . <p> " With the growth in oil demand , energy security and geopolitics will be part of daily business in India " Birol said . " While the government , we see , is taking steps in this regard , India needs to mobilize capital to build infrastructure to the tune of $110 billion every year . I can see India needs three things : investment , investment , investment . " <p> ? Referring to the Middle East and its problems , Birol said the fact that terror groups like ISIS were funding their operations by selling oil was a matter of global concern .
