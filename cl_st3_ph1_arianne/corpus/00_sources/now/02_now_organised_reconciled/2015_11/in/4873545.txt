<h> ' Yamuna in Okhla was best fishing ground ' <p> According to Delhi Gazetteers of 1883-84 and 1912 , the best known fishing ground in Yamuna was Okhla " where the weir ensures deep water and collection of food " for the fish . <p> TNN Nov 29 , 2015 , 11.59 PM IST <p> NEW DELHI : According to Delhi Gazetteers of 1883-84 and 1912 , the best known fishing ground in Yamuna was Okhla " where the weir ensures deep water and collection of food " for the fish . Today , Okhla has no freshwater , just untreated sewage . The extracts , on display at an exhibition on Past , Present and Future of Yamuna , are proof of the rich biodiversity along the Yamuna that had been wiped out completely . <p> The gazetteers say even the highly threatened mahseer was found in plenty along with rohu , bachwa , mulley , mirga , kalabans and many others . The banks were infested with " muggers-the gurriyal ( critically endangered gharial ) predominating " .
