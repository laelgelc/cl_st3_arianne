<p> Influenza virus is a member of the orthomyxovirus family that causes acute infectious disease . There are three known types of influenza viruses : A , B and C. These influenza viruses are further divided into two subtypes based on two proteins hemmaglutinin ( HA ) and neuraminidase ( NA ) on the surface of the virus . There are a total of 17 HA subtypes and 10 known NA subtypes . Many combinations of HA and NA subtypes are possible . All known subtypes of influenza are known to infect birds . <p> Avian Influenza A virus <p> This virus can be classified as Low pathogenic avian influenza A ( LPAI ) and High Pathogenic avian influenza A ( HPAI ) . LPAI causes mild or no illness and HPAI causes ' severe illness with 80-90% mortality in poultry . Viruses that can infect only birds are genetically and antigenetically different from viruses that can infect both birds and human beings . <p> There are three important subtypes of avian influenza virus that can infect both birds and humans : <p> 1 . Influenza A H9 is rarely known to infect human beings . <p> Antigenic " Shift " and antigenic " Drift " <p> Influenza viruses have capacity to change by two methods antigenic drift and antigenic shift . These are minor changes in the virus that occurs over a period of time . Antigenic drift is a process that produces a new virus strain that can not be detected by the body 's immune system . This antigenic drift leads to reinfection . Antigenic Shift is an abrupt change in influenza A virus resulting in new hemmaglutinin and neuraminidase proteins . Shift results in a new influenza A subtype or a virus with a hemagglutinin or a hemagglutinin and neuraminidase combination that has emerged from an animal population that is so different from the same subtype in humans that most people do not have immunity to the new ( e.g. novel ) virus . Such a " shift " occurred in the spring of 2009 , when a new H1N1 virus with a new combination of genes emerged to infect people and quickly spread , causing a pandemic . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protection against the new virus . The cells die , in part due to the direct effects of the virus on the cell , and also possibly due to the effects of interferon . Cell death at later times may also result from the actions of cytotoxic T-cells . As a result , the efficiency of ciliary clearance is reduced , leading to impaired function of the mucus elevator ; thus , there is reduced clearance of infectious agents from the respiratory tract .
