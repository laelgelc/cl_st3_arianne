<h> Prime Minister Narendra Modi told G20 leaders on Monday that all elements of the Bali package should be implemented fully . ( AP Photo ) <p> Prime Minister Narendra Modi told G20 leaders on Monday that all elements of the Bali package should be implemented fully , setting the stage for December 's negotiations in Nairobi as part of the Doha Development Round . <p> Modi said regional trade agreements should not lead to fragmentation of the global trading system and should support a more liberalised multilateral trade regime . <p> " We look forward to a successful outcome at the Nairobi meeting in December , " Modi said . <p> " Slowdown in global trade is a major concern . Prospects for increase in trade momentum remain bleak in the prevailing global economic environment . Efforts to accelerate global economic growth will help trade . " <p> Modi pitched for a transparent , equitable , non-discriminatory and rule-based global trading system and laid emphasis on the role of " small and medium enterprises in the global value chain " to help expand global employment . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " That is why India intends to meet its vast and growing energy needs in as sustainable manner as possible . " <p> He mentioned that India has targeted an additional 175 GW of renewable energy by 2022 along with a cut in subsidies on fossil fuels . <p> Calling for international support that will enable India to achieve its goals of meeting energy needs , combating climate change and creating huge economic opportunities , Modi sought the G20 countries ' help in increasing research and development in clean and renewable energy , besides reducing the costs to make it affordable and accessible for all . <p> " Increase financial support and technology transfer to increase access and transition to clean energy and focus research efforts on clean coal technology and coal gasification , " he said . <p> The measures suggested by the PM outlined the need to develop proliferation-resistant nuclear energy technology , increase in energy efficiency and moderate energy use , apart from promoting an integrated global gas market and giving greater voice to major emerging economies .
