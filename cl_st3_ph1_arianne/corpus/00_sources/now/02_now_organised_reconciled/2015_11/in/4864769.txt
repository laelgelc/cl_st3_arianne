<h> Climate , extremism top agenda at Commonwealth summit talks <p> Tackling climate change and countering violent extremism will top the agenda at the Commonwealth summit in Malta this week , attended by Queen Elizabeth II and French President Francois Hollande . <p> PTI Nov 25 , 2015 , 10.08 PM IST <p> LONDON : Tackling climate change and countering violent extremism will top the agenda at the Commonwealth summit in Malta this week , attended by Queen Elizabeth II and French President Francois Hollande . <p> Coming after the Paris terror attacks this month and just before the COP21 world climate talks hosted in the French capital , the meeting will bring together G20 powers and tiny microstates spanning the continents . <p> Because the 53-country group contains such diverse countries , the talks are being closely watched in world capitals for signs of agreement on climate change that could herald a deal at the UN COP21 summit . <p> " It can really help the outcome in Paris , " Liz Gallagher , programme leader at the E3G environmental think-tank , told AFP -- even though @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not involved . <p> The Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting ( CHOGM ) begins on Friday and wraps up on Sunday , a day before the COP21 talks start in Paris . <p> Born out of the British empire , the Commonwealth of Nations brings together around a quarter of the world 's countries and a third of its population . <p> Gallagher said she would be looking out for the organisation 's main developed economies -- Australia , Britain and Canada -- " outlining the contours of a support package for the most vulnerable countries " . <p> " This is one of the big sticking points , " she said . <p> " New voices in the negotiations " , such as Malaysia and Singapore , as well the views of new leaders in Australia and Canada , will also be of particular interest . <p> The climate talks will be attended by United Nations Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon and Hollande , who is
