<h> Indo-European Water Forum Meet to be Held in Delhi Next Week <p> NEW DELHI : First meeting of the Indo-European Water Forum will be held here next week , aiming to provide a platform for policy-makers to discuss issues related to water resources management in the country , and also to take home lessons from experiences and implementation of the European Water Policy . <p> The meeting , being co-organised by the National Water Mission , Ministry of Water Resources , River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation and Environment Directorate-General of European Commission , will be held on November 23 and 24 . <p> The main themes to be covered during the meeting , which will be attended by Union Water Resources Minister Uma Bharti , include issues such as water governance and legislation , river basin management , water policy in India and the EU . <p> Among other issues , the participants will also hold deliberations on coping with water scarcity and ecological aspects of water management and research , innovation and business solutions for water challenges to chalk out the way forward for sustainable water @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ platform for policy-makers and stakeholders to discuss key issues surrounding water resources management in India , as well as transferring lessons from experience and implementation of European Water Policy , " the Ministry said in a statement . <p> It is said that India 's water challenges are complex and include quantity , allocation , quality and management issues . <p> Increasing competition for water among industry , agriculture , energy , domestic use and environment has highlighted importance of managing water on a river basin and multi-sectoral basis in a sustainable manner . <p> Agricultural sector is currently India 's largest user of water , however , as urban and other demands multiply , less water is likely to be available in order to meet nation 's irrigation and individual needs .
