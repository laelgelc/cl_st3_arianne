" he had said . <p> The facility has some very unique features including a very modern aluminum body building plant . <p> Modi 's visit to the JLR plant came on third and final day of his UK visit . <p> Modi will also take part in the G20 summit , which opens tomorrow in Turkey . <p> Chaired by Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan , the summit will focus on the current state of the global economy , sustainable growth , development and climate change , investment , trade and energy . <p> Participating leaders are also expected to discuss political issues such as global terrorism and the refugee crisis in addition to the economic agenda .
