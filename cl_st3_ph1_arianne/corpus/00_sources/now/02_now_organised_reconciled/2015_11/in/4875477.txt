<h> Alternative farming : Farmers of western districts show the wayVillagers in dry areas try new ways of farming , income hike by 25% <p> As world leaders deliberate ways to mitigate adverse effects of climate change at the United Nations Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) in Paris , villagers in the parched areas of western Rajasthan have already shown the way by adopting alternate farming practices . <p> TNN Dec 1 , 2015 , 12.51 PM IST <p> JAIPUR : As world leaders deliberate ways to mitigate adverse effects of climate change at the United Nations Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) in Paris , villagers in the parched areas of western Rajasthan have already shown the way by adopting alternate farming practices . <p> Not only have their yield per hectare of traditional crops like millets , guar , moong , moth ben and cow pea increased , they have moved onto climate ready cultivars like wheat and mustard . Besides , fruits like green berries , pomegranate , cordia myxa ( Lasura ) for nutrition has raised their income levels by 25% . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Barmer are scientifically supported by the Union government funded Centre Arid Zone Research Institute ( CAZRI ) in Jodhpur with International Crops Research Institute for Semi-Arid Tropics ( ICRISAT ) based in Hyderabad . <p> Dry lands leading to poverty is one of the core issues at the ongoing climate summit in Paris . Global leaders will discuss alternate means of agriculture , crops , agro-forestry and pasture cultivation for providing livelihood to the millions . <p> The average rainfall in the dry lands in Rajasthan is less than 230 mm , which is suitable for growing conventional crops ( millets , guar , moong , moth ben and cow pea ) enough to sustain for 8-10 months . For fetching drinking water , the villagers have to scale kilometres in the area which is fast turning into a desert , adding to the poverty of families . <p> This region has witnessed a sharp rise in the frequency and length of dry spells in the region adversely impacting crop and fodder yields due to climate variability or climate change in the recent decades . The social impact was mass migration @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ which face drought every second year . <p> Dr J C Tewari , principal scientist , forestry , at CAZRI is heading the project ' Enhancing Productivity and Resilience of the Drylands through Science-based Intervention ' . The programme was introduced on January 30 , 2013 . " In the first phase , villagers were trained on innovative techniques of sustainable farming with use of improved climate cultivators crop varieties - including pearl millets , legumes , mustard and wheat . Here embankments were constructed for preserving rainwater followed by planting of commercial value trees like acacia and Senegal ( Arabic Gum ) , grasses and shrubs in the catchment area . It prevented soil erosion and enhanced the nutrient value in soil cover , " said Tewari . <p> According to a CAZRI study , this project has increased green cover by 15 to 20% in many villages including Derasar , Dhok in Barmer , Mansagar and Govindpura in Jodhpur and Damodra in Jaisalmer . This green cover has provided fodder for livestock adding economic value to villagers . The community based pasture field in few selective villages have increased @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ second phase , focus was on institutional methods like formation of self-help groups and common grazing grounds in association with local NGOs , which has moved out several families out of poverty . Mani Bai , 65 , a farmer in Derasar village , had never imagined to grow pomegranate in her field . This season she has managed to sell over 30 kg pomegranate and earned Rs 10,000 out of selling Arabic Gum from six Senegal trees grown two years ago . " Our area has high degree of migration to Gujarat until we started using new methods of farming . Now , we do n't have to go anywhere , " said elated Bai . <p> During the summit from November 30 to December 11 , over 195 countries , including several nation heads like Indian PM Narendra Modi , US President Barack Obama , Chinese President Xi-Jinping along with 50,000 activists and volunteers are meeting in Paris . <p> RELATED <h> From around the web <h> More from The Times of India <p> Recommended By Colombia <h> From Around the Web <h> More From The Times of India @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 3000 <h> OR PROCEED WITHOUT REGISTRATION <p> Share on Twitter <h> SIGN IN WITH <p> FacebookGoogleEmail <p> Refrain from posting comments that are obscene , defamatory or inflammatory , and do not indulge in personal attacks , name calling or inciting hatred against any community .
