<h> Green groups re-think massive Paris climate march after attacks <p> * March had been due Nov. 29 , on eve of climate summit <p> * Organisers to meet Monday to review plans <p> By Bate Felix <p> PARIS , Nov 15 A massive demonstration planned by environmental activists for the eve of this month 's U.N . climate summit in Paris is in doubt as organisers weigh the security risks - and the propriety - of gathering in huge numbers in a city where attacks killed 129 people . <p> Environmental groups will meet on Monday to decide a course of action , with mainstream activists saying they will abide by any ban on public gatherings if the state of emergency decreed by French President Francois Hollande is still in place . <p> Organisers from about 130 non-governmental organisations had hoped to draw 200,000 people to the march on Nov. 29 , anticipating a carnival-like atmosphere that would pressure world leaders to take action to combat global warming . <p> " We are horrified by the attacks , " said Alix Mazounie of Climate Action Network in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ put pressure on world leaders attending the Nov. 30-Dec. 11 summit , convened to work out a plan to combat climate change . <p> The coalition organising the Global Climate March will meet on Monday at 1400 GMT to discuss how best to proceed . Among the risks is that security fears will keep the turnout - and the political impact - low . <p> " It is for the French presidency to decide on the way forward , and we will make changes to our plans as appropriate , " said the aid charity Oxfam . <p> Under French law , a state of emergency can last 12 days and Prime Minister Manuel Valls has said it may be extended if needed . France says the summit itself will go ahead , and leaders including U.S. President Barack Obama have reaffirmed attendance . <p> If security and respect for the victims force cancellation of the Paris demonstration , one option is to boost other planned parallel demonstrations in cities such as Sydney , Tokyo , London or New Delhi on Nov. 29 . <p> Organisers said they also hope to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the Paris violence with their appeals for action to protect people from the impacts of climate change , such as floods , droughts or rising sea levels . <p> Activists could be encouraged to take to social media instead of to the streets . <p> The march had been due to start at the Place de la Republique , in the neighbourhood where some of Friday 's attacks took place .
