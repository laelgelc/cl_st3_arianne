<h> Haridwar sand mining to start again soon ? <p> Uttarakhand Forest Development Corporation awaits instruction from the Union ministry of environment and forests and climate change on sand mining in the Ganga and its tributaries in Haridwar . <p> TNN Nov 10 , 2015 , 10.32 PM IST <p> DEHRADUN : Uttarakhand Forest Development Corporation ( UFDC ) awaits instruction from the Union ministry of environment and forests and climate change ( MOEFCC ) on sand mining in the Ganga and its tributaries in Haridwar . UFDC says sand mining here yields revenue of about Rs 88 crore each year . <p> STS Lepcha , additional principal chief conservator of forests and managing director of UFDC told TOI , " Mining will be carried out on 1,380 hectares near the Ganga and its tributaries like Ganga Bhopur , Ganga Vishnupur , Kotawali , Rawasan and Rawasan-II , which flow through Shyampur and Chidiyapur ranges of Haridwar Forest Division . We have got permission from the National Wildlife Board . A final nod is expected by the end of November from the forest department . After the flash flood disaster of 2013 , however , environmental issues were taken up in the Supreme Court , which stalled permission for further sand mining in the river . Under SC orders , there were further environment studies to be conducted by a panel of consultants before sand was mined from the river . <p> The studies had to be conducted twice , before and after the monsoon .
