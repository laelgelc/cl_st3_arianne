<h> The Crunch Issues at Climate Summit 2015 <p> A major contentious issue is that developing nations insist industrialised countries should do more to cut emissions , having polluted for much longer . But rich countries insist on liability for all . <p> Paris : On November 30 , some 120 heads of government and state will kick off a high-stakes negotiation to curb global warming and help poor countries cope with its impacts . <p> The summit runs till December 11 . <p> The objective : the first-ever universal climate deal to include all of the 195 nations under the umbrella of the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> Dozens of issues have stymied the negotiations , now into their third decade .
