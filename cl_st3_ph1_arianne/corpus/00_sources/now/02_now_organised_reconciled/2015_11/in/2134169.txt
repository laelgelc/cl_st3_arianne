<h> Students visit terrace garden at Mogappair <p> The verdant terrace of Jaswant Singh 's house in Mogappair became a classroom for a group of students of Chennai School , Korattur . <p> The terrace has a number of plants and trees . It includes nearly 65 varieties of medicinal and culinary herbs and over 60 species of fruit-bearing trees . Also , the terrace has kurinji plant , predominantly found in Western Ghats , which blossoms once in 12 years . <p> Some of the other plants include strawberry , blackberry , star trees , water plants and cacti . The terrace also has a butterfly garden . <p> " It was exciting to see the plants and trees which I have studied in text books . Such outdoor visits help us learn a lot , " said S. Arun , a student . <p> The educational-visit is organised as part of the students ' project on ' understanding climate change and weather , ' under junior-scientist category . Each team will have five students , under two categories -- class VI to VIII and class IX and X.
