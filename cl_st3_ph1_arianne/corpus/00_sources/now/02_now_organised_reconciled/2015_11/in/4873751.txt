<p> Sambalpur : As the global leaders prepare to strike a new deal to fight climate change during the Conference of Parties 21 ( CoP21 ) meeting at Paris that starts tomorrow , villagers from drought affected districts of Nuapada and Bargarh organized a " March to the Crop Field " asking the world leaders to make a definitive climate deal that reduces global warming and greenhouse gases and support small and marginal farmers in adapting to negative impacts of climate change . <p> This March was organised at two places -- Kharamal village in Bargarh district and Kushmal in Nuappada district -- where about three hundred villagers -- including women and children participated to send out their message to the world governments to save small farmers from vagaries of climate change by signing a strong climate deal in Paris . <p> The villagers have demanded that : <p> 1 . We want the governments to support decentralized water harvesting and management systems . <p> 4 .
