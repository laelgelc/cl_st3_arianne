<h> Need for alternative development model , says Bhutan PM <p> PANAJI : Stating that economic model of the world is based on Gross Domestic Product ( GDP ) , Bhutan Prime Minister Tshering Tobgay emphasised the need for alternative model for development and the need to learn from civilisation for creating a moral society . <p> Addressing the inaugural function of the 2nd India Ideas conclave 2015 at Panaji on Sunday , the Bhutan Prime Minster said in comparison to other civilisations , India is the greatest civilization in the world which has a unique identity . The conclave was organised by India Foundation which is an independent research center focused on the issues , challenges and opportunities of the Indian polity .
