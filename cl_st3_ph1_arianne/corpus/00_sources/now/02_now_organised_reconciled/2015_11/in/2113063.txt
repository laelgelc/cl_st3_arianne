<h> Arctic Ocean to Have Less Ice , More Water by 2050s : Study <p> Washington : The entire Arctic coastline and most of the Arctic Ocean will experience an additional 60 days of open water each year by the 2050s , according to a new study . <p> " We hear all the time about how sea ice extent in the Arctic is going down , " said Katy Barnhart , who led the study while at the University of Colorado Boulder 's Institute for Arctic and Alpine Research ( INSTAAR ) . <p> " That 's an important measurement if you are trying to understand broad impacts of climate change in the Arctic , but it does n't tell us about how the changes in the sea ice in the Arctic are going to affect specific places , " said Ms Barnhart . <p> Ms Barnhart and her colleagues , including INSTAAR Fellow Irina Overeem , set out to investigate the very local impacts of open water expansion patterns in the Arctic . <p> Researchers used climate simulations from the National Centre for Atmospheric Research-based Community @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water , or sea-ice-free , days change from 1850 to 2100 in our planet 's northernmost ocean . <p> They also wanted to understand when open water conditions in specific locations would be completely different from preindustrial conditions . <p> Because most economic activity in the Arctic is along the coastline , the team focused on four coastal locations that demonstrated the range of sea ice change : Drew Point , along Alaska 's North Slope ; the Laptev Sea , along Siberia 's northern coast ; Perry Channel in the Canadian Arctic Archipelago ( part of the Northwest Passage route ) ; and Arctic Ocean regions east of Svalbard , Norway . <p> For example , at Drew Point , open water is already shifting from preindustrial conditions . Once present about 50 days a year on average ( 1900-2000 ) , open water is now present about 100 days a year . <p> By the 2070s , the modelling study concludes , there could be close to 200 days a year with no sea ice at Drew Point , which is likely to worsen coastal erosion . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ stories with respect to the patterns of Arctic Ocean , atmosphere , and sea ice motion - things like coastal erosion or connections to potential sea routes , " said Ms Barnhart , now a postdoctoral fellow at the Annenberg Public Policy Centre of the University of Pennsylvania . <p> " Since we do n't expect the impacts of Arctic sea ice loss to be exactly the same in Alaska as in Greenland , we looked at open water days to provide a more nuanced picture of sea ice change at specific locations , " said Ms Barnhart . <p> For the study , Ms Barnhart and colleagues relied on climate projections from 1850 to 2100 and analysed multiple runs or " realisations " from a single climate model . <p> According to their analysis , the entire Arctic coastline and most of the Arctic Ocean will experience an additional 60 days of open water each year by the 2050s , and many sites will have more than 100 additional days .
