Despite the recent terrorist attacks that rocked Paris and the subsequent high alert that remains in place , President Francois Hollande has affirmed that " the international conference will not only go ahead but will bring hope and solidarity " . The UN has been working on a global climate agreement since 2009 . Can Paris deliver this long-awaited universal outcome ? <p> One hundred and twenty world leaders are expected to attend the opening of the conference . The UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) expects over 10,000 delegates from 195 nations and over 14,000 representatives from civil society . <p> Parisians have returned to the streets and cafes . They have not allowed fear to paralyze the city . The underlying tension is , nevertheless , palpable .
