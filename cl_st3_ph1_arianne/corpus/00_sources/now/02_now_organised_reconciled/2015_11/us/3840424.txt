<p> " You know in another time , Joe McCarthy could look at a problem and spot an invisible communist lurking behind it , " said O'Malley at the Jefferson-Jackson Dinner in Manchester , N.H. , referring to Joseph McCarthy , a Republican senator from Wisconsin , who stirred anti-communist fear in the 1950s . <p> " Today Donald Trump blames everything on New American immigrants and Muslim Americans , " the former Maryland governor continued . " Trump says that we should be monitoring everyone of the Muslim faith , keeping some kind of registry , maybe even issuing special ID cards , " he said to a round of boos from the audience . " Let me ask you this : who 's next ? Catholics ? Trade unionists ? Artists ? " <p> O'Malley said this is a familiar road that does not lead to a good place . <p> " And if you want to make a list of Americans who affirmatively and vocally reject your politics of fear and division ... you can start with me . And when , Donald Trump , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ put the interests working people ahead of the special interests of billionaires ... you can start with me , " O'Malley continued . <p> O'Malley said that while Trump is compiling a list , the GOP front-runner can start with him on determining who will stand up to the National Rifle Association and fight for " common sense " gun safety legislation . <p> " And when you send your new security police out to track down all of us who believe that the Earth is actually round , and that climate change is real ... you can start with me , " O'Malley said .
