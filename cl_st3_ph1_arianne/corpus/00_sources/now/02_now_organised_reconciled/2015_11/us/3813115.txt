" London Fog : A Biography " is a thoroughly researched and generally enjoyable account of the social , natural and cultural history of the peasoupers , from their first appearance in the early 1800s to the final fog of 1962 . <p> The picture Corton paints is not pretty . Thick yellow fogs composed of debris ejected from unfiltered home fires and industrial emissions cloaked London in darkness for days on end , led to thousands of deaths and made the city a haven for thieves .
