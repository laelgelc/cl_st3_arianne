<h> The Biggest Economic and Social Opportunity of All Time <p> When presenting the groundbreaking report " Our Common Future " to the U.N . General Assembly in 1987 , former Prime Minister of Norway and current Deputy Chair of The Elders Gro Brundtland spoke out on the need for a new way of doing business . " We must break away from our sectoral ways of viewing economy and ecology . We must learn to accept the fact that environmental considerations and economic growth are parts of a unified management of our planet . The one is dependent on the other . " <p> As we prepare to head to COP21 , those words ring more true than ever . Representatives from more than 190 national governments will meet to negotiate a new international agreement on climate change .
