The report , Climate Action in Megacities , tracks the progress of the C40 network , a group of now 80 world cities working together to address climate change . Collectively , the cities represent more than 600 million people and a quarter of the global economy . <p> Former New York City Mayor Michael Bloomberg , a UN Special Envoy for Cities and Climate Change , said the group has been critical in catalyzing local action . <p> " It really has changed the dialogue , " Bloomberg said on a call with reporters Monday .
