<h> Chet Bowers : How misuse of political language imperils our future <p> Conservatism and liberalism are the two dominant political terms we use today , with libertarianism being a subset of today 's conservative agenda . What is striking about how these terms are used is that neither of their supporting vocabularies take account of the changes taking place in the environment , such as how climate change is warming both the Arctic and Antarctica , causing droughts and extreme weather , the loss of habitats and species , and the acidification of the world 's oceans . These changes are partly responsible for the 60-million-plus people who are homeless , with ten of thousands now sweeping over Western Europe in search of safe havens . Unfortunately , neither political term provides the supporting vocabulary and thus conceptual/moral framework for articulating the importance of conservatism in today 's world . <p> The irony is that today 's self-identified conservatives are not concerned with conserving species , habitats and the social justice achievements of the past now being undermined by the digital revolution .
