It is the largest coalition of city leadership that puts a spotlight on the effects of climate change , according to the compact 's website . Garcia told KPCC that Long Beach has a lot of emission-cutting plans in development that work seamlessly with the aims of the compact . <p> " We 're going to be committed to really aggressive reduction in greenhouse gas emissions over the course of the next few years and decades ... from greening the port , putting zero-emissions terminals , planting 6,000 trees - we 're committed to really lead when it comes to emission reduction , " he said .
