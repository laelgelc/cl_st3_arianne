<h> Report shows the disproportionate effects of climate change on the global poor <p> It 's not news that climate change disproportionately affects the poor -- but a new reportfrom the World Bank gives us a better idea of just how stacked the deck really is . <p> The report is one of the first to connect climate and poverty at the level of the household . According to the Bank , a warming world will send an additional 100 million people into extreme poverty ( living on $1.90 per day , according to the new standard ) by 2030 -- and nearly half of those people will live in India . <p> It 's easy to imagine how climate change can exacerbate poverty -- warmer temperatures can stimulate disease and less predictable weather patterns can harm crop yields , which in turn affect food security and income .
