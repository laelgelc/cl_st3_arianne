<h> Ban arrives in Turkey ahead of G20 Summit , meets with Turkish President <p> 14 November 2015 ? Meeting with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan ahead of the G20 Summit in Antalya , United Nations Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon said the annual event was " happening in somber times that required unity of action . " <p> A readout issued by the UN spokesperson noted that the two leaders condemned in the strongest terms the attacks in Paris and stressed the need for the international community to address terrorism " in a determined and unified way . " <p> " The Secretary-General expressed appreciation for the Turkish presidency of the G20 , " the readout underlined , adding that Mr. Ban reiterated that he will present in a few months his comprehensive Plan of Action Against Violent Extremism . <p> Mr. Ban and President Erdogan exchanged views on the way forward to resolve the situation in Syria .
