<p> U.K. Prime Minister David Cameron will push for a climate-change deal with a strong legal framework that supports the world 's poorest countries and the role of businesses when talks with global leaders get under way in Paris on Monday . <p> " A deal in Paris must include clear support to the poorest nations " as well as setting a long-term goal for decarbonizing and a transparent way to report progress , Cameron will say , according to an e-mailed statement from his office . " I want the deal in Paris to outline the role that businesses should play . We also need to give businesses long-term certainty for investment . " <p> More than 140 world leaders are gathering in Paris in an effort to reach the first truly global deal to curb emissions of greenhouse gases . The two weeks of talks have already garnered pledges from 177 of the 195 countries involved .
