DW talks to one of the authors , Matthias Garschagen , about who is most at risk . <p> DW : What are the global hotspots for natural disasters listed in the 2015 World Risk Report ? <p> Matthias Garschagen : In the report we assess risk with respect to disasters from natural hazards . The hotspots are sub-Saharan Africa on the one hand , and then parts of Asia on the other . Southeast Asia and southern Asia are also main hotspots . <p> In the World Risk Index there 's a difference between risk to natural disasters and vulnerability . Can you explain what that entails ? <p> You can have two countries facing comparable natural hazards , for example New Zealand and Haiti both had earthquakes of a similar strength , but the impacts from those earthquakes were very different .
