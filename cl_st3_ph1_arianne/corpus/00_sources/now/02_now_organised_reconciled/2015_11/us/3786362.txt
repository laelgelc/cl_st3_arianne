Nevertheless , the UN is happy with the foundation that the number and quality of Intended Nationally Determined Contributions has created for the negotiations . <h> Climate talks : The current state of play <p> Both Vuorelma and Aho have been involved in preparatory meetings , the most recent of which had taken place in Bonn , Germany in late October .
