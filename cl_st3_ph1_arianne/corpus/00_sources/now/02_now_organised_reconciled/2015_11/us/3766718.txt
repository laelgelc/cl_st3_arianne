Our assignment was to gather the materials for a bow drill from what we could find on the river bend . <p> A bow drill is a tool for making fire -- basically , a stick that is spun against a flat piece of wood until the friction creates enough heat to ignite some tinder . The tool consists of a bow into whose string you twist the spindle , aka the drill . The wooden drill spins against another piece of wood , a flat one called the baseboard that sits on the ground . To keep the spindle straight you use a handhold called the socket , usually made from bone or stone .
