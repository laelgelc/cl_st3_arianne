<h> Ask Congress <p> By AMY BETH HANSON - Associated Press - Sunday , November 22 , 2015 <p> HELENA , Mont . ( AP ) - A retired clergyman and an attorney want Montana voters to pass a law requiring power companies to buy and sell more renewable energy to reduce the amount of carbon dioxide - a gas tied to climate change - in the atmosphere . <p> John Soderberg , former minister of the Bozeman United Methodist Church , and lawyer Russ Doty have submitted the ballot measure that would require investor-owned utilities to gradually increase the amount of renewable energy they purchase for retail sale . <p> The goals would be 19 percent by 2018 , 40 percent by 2025 , 70 percent by 2040 and 80 percent by 2050 . <p> The federal government has proposed that Montana reduce its carbon dioxide emissions by 47 percent by 2030 .
