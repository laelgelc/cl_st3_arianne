<h> Call for making climate change part of composite dialogue <p> ISLAMABAD : Taking part in the discussion on sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) and climate change PPP Senator Farhatullah Babar has said that the targets set out in the SDGs aimed at social , environmental and economic development of the country . He said this could be achieved only with the active cooperation of the provinces and local governments as all these subjects had been devolved as a result of the 18th amendment . " We need to take the discourse to the provinces and even to the local level and not forget that Islamabad alone can not do it through regulatory mechanisms . " <p> This requires that SDG monitoring centres be set up first in the provinces instead of Islamabad . He said it was strange that instead of setting up these centres in the provinces the government had announced setting up monitoring centre in Islamabad . Ours is a security driven state in which decisions are taken largely on the basis of security considerations instead of welfare of the people he said and added that if the state policies were not reoriented there is no likelihood of success in achieving the SDG targets . <p> On the issue of climate change the PPP senator said that it was a shared challenge for both Pakistan and India and offered immense potential to them to cooperate . He proposed that climate change and water stress issues should be included in the framework of composite dialogue between India and Pakistan . He also lamented that some elements in the state appeared to be still protecting the reincarnations of banned militant outfits . He said that unless we stopped using some militants as tools of our security and foreign policy the country will remain bogged down in security mould and there is little likelihood of the country becoming a welfare driven state .
