<p> ISLAMABAD : All provinces have supported the revised draft of National Biodiversity , Strategy and Action Plan ( NBSAP ) aimed at stemming the loss of biodiversity , restoring the ecosystem and promoting sustainable use of natural resources for the wellbeing of the present and the future generations . <p> The revised draft of the plan that highlights over 20 targets was launched at a workshop , organised by the Ministry of Climate Change ( MOCC ) and the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) , at a local hotel , here on Thursday . <p> Sustainable use of biodiversity is not only a national need but also a global obligation under the Convention on Biological Diversity ( CBD ) . <p> A biodiversity action plan ( BAP ) was prepared back in 2000 but its implementation remained slow due to a weak institutional framework .
