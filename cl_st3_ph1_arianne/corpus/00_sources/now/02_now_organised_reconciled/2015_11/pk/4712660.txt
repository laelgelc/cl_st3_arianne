<h> Paris Climate Conference -- a moment of hope and solidarity <p> ISLAMABAD ( Staff Report ) -- Paris Climate Conference ( COP21 ) -- the biggest diplomatic event ever hosted in France -- will not only still go ahead but will be a moment of hope and solidarity , according to the top French envoy in Pakistan . <p> Ambassador of France to Pakistan Mrs Martine Dorance was addressing the reception -- On the Road to Paris Climate Conference -- which was overshadowed by the tragedy in Paris last Friday . 129 people died , while more than 350 are injured . <p> " Our thoughts , our sympathy are with them , with their families . Among the victims were several dozen of our foreign friends , representing 19 nationalities , " said Dorance . <p> She said she had been deeply touched by the messages of sympathy and support received in Pakistan . " Pakistan has suffered a lot from terrorism , like so many other countries , and our thoughts are with all the victims of terrorism in France , in Pakistan , in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ many places . " <p> Dorance also expressed the hope that the conference will provide " a moment of solidarity , too , because , there will undoubtedly be more than 100 heads of state and government who will come to Paris " . <p> " In just 11 days , in Paris , world leaders will have a historic opportunity to negotiate a lasting , binding agreement , one which is differentiated so that we can all live and so that our children and grandchildren can continue to have the planet they will inherit , " the French ambassador said . <p> " As the president of the conference , our role has been to facilitate an ambitious compromise between 195 countries , " she added . <p> Acknowledging the expertise of Pakistan 's Minister for Climate Change , Mr Zahid Hamid , who recently took oath , the French envoy said that Pakistan is one of the countries most affected by climate change .
