<h> ' Pakistan not responsible for global warming , paying price for follies of developed nations ' <p> ISLAMABAD - Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid on Monday said that countries like Pakistan which are the least pollution producers have to bear the brunt of climate change . <p> " We are taking adaptation measures to lessen the impact of climate change . But it is a forced adaptation . We are not responsible for global warming or climate change , rather we are victim of it , " he told reporters before his departure to attend the Paris Summit on Climate Change . <p> The conference , which kicked off on Monday , would set international contours for emissions control from 2020 to 2030 . <p> The minister said that total emission of green house gases by Pakistan was 0.8 percent of global emission . It ranked 135th in the international list of polluters , however , it was among the top ten most vulnerable countries , he said . <p> " We are paying price for the follies of developed nations .
