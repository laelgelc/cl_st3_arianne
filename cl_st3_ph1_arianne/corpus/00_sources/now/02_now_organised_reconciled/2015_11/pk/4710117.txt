<h> ' Protection of environmental resources needs political will ' <p> ISLAMABAD : Ministry of Climate Change Secretary Arif Ahmed Khan emphasised on Friday that ownership of environmental resources by political leaders is vital to tackle environmental degradation . <p> " Public leaders should exhibit their political will for and interest in ownership towards protection of environment and play their part in responding to the climate change risks being faced by the country , " Khan told the Senate Standing Committee on Climate Change . <p> This could not be achieved through sensitisation of political leaders at all levels about aggravating environmental challenges and country 's deepening climate change vulnerability to natural disasters becoming more frequent and intense in the country , he suggested . The meeting was called to brief members on the steps proposed by the government to protect the natural resources , particularly forests in the country . The Pakistan Environmental Protection ( Amendment Bill 2015 ) , introduced by Senator Mushahid Hussain Syed , also came under consideration during the meeting on climate change held on Friday . But only around 50 percent of the trees have survived for various reasons including natural reasons and human interference , he added . <p> Illegal tree cutting by timber mafia , forest fires , clearance of forests for agriculture and other land uses , lack of alternative fuel , land erosion and poor implementation of forest protection laws are key drivers of deforestation in the country , he asserted . " Strengthening capacity of provincial forest officials , taping resource-rich private sector and engaging communities in protection of existing forest resources and brining new area under forest can help reinvigorate country 's ailing forest sector , " Arif Ahmed Khan suggested .
