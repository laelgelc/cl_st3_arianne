<h> Sartaj Aziz leads Pakistani delegation at ASEM Meeting <p> As per the press release , the 12th Foreign Ministers ' Meeting of Asia-Europe Meeting ( ASEM ) was held in Luxembourg on 5-6 November 2015 . The Adviser to the Prime Minister on Foreign Affairs , Mr. Sartaj Aziz led Pakistan delegation . <p> The Asia-Europe Meeting is an informal dialogue forum between Asia and Europe , which addresses political , economic and social issues confronting the two regions , aiming to strengthening their relationship in a spirit of mutual respect and equal partnership . <p> According to the press release , the Foreign Ministers discussed the important issues of climate change , 2030 agenda for sustainable development , disaster risk reduction and management , connectivity , and the future of ASEM . The participants appreciated the ongoing efforts to tackle climate change and disaster management , and emphasized the need to focus attention on enhancing connectivity between Asia and Europe .
