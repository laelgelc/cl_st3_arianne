<p> The conference , organized by the Thai Nguyen University of Agriculture and Forestry ( TUAF ) , was held from November 13 to 15 . <p> Dr. Gil C. Saguiguit Jr. , Director of Searca , led discussions on developing jobs that support a sustainable environment . <p> LDEM was an event sponsored by Searca , a treaty organization based in Los Ba ? os , Laguna , Philippines that addresses the rural and agricultural needs of the 11 members of the Association of Southeast Nations ( Asean ) . <p> The conference sought to explore the knowledge , systems , and practices related to livelihood development and environmental management strategies under extreme weather conditions . <p> The participants also tackled issue on how the development approach in the Asian region could be enhanced in the context of challenges posed by climate change . <p> Saguiguit reported that the participants exchanged ideas on international and local experiences as well as their engagement with policymakers . <p> " Our center is a close ally of Thai Nguyen University . And rightfully so , because this university focuses on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Vietnam , a priority country of our organization , " Saguiguit explained . <p> He noted that " over the last four and a half decades , we have seen and felt the effects of climate change on agriculture and forestry , upon which the majority of our region 's population depend for food and livelihood . <p> " The challenge , therefore , is to push for climate change adaptation strategies and to build these sectors ' resiliency to this natural phenomenon . The goal is to ensure that the livelihood needs of the growing population in the uplands are met in the midst of pervasive poverty and worsening climatic conditions . Added to this , is the imperative task of safeguarding our natural resource base . " <p> Participants of the conference included academics , researchers , development practitioners , and representatives of national and local government units ( LGUs ) , non-governmental organizations ( NGOs ) , civil society organizations ( CSOs ) and other stakeholders from countries in Asia and outside the region .
