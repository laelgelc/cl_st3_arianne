BFAR National Director Asis G. Perez said one way of conserving the fishery resources is through the strict implementation of Republic Act 8550 or the Philippine Fisheries Code of 1998 as amended by R.A. 10654 which now imposed increased fines and administrative penalties for violations of the said law .
