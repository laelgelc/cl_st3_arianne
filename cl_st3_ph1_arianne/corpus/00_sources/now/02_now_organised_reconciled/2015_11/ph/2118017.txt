<h> Global climate run-up forum to COP21 culminates in PH <p> Twenty climate change vulnerable countries are set to gather in the Philippines on November 9 to 11 , 2015 for the global conference of the Climate Vulnerable Forum ( VCF ) to develop new contributions aimed at crafting critical climate policy decisions . <p> A statement emailed to the Manila Bulletin showed non-VCF countries will be coming also to Manila to observe . <p> The Philippines chairs the forthcoming VCF , which was formed in 2009 as a global partnership of countries which are disproportionately impacted by global warming 's consequences . It was first held in Maldives . <p> Manila 's VCF is the third global meeting of the partnership . It is aimed at developing new contributions to shaping critical climate policy decisions , promoting the safeguard of most vulnerable groups . <p> Organizers of the forum , a run-up to the United Nations Climate Change Conference at Paris ( COP21 ) that begins on November 30 , will present to President Aquino the VCF 's highlights . <p> The Philippine Climate Change Commission ( @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ) will co-host the conference . As pre-COP21 event , the VCF in Manila will set the agenda and key message of vulnerable countries to the COP21 . <p> Undersecretary/CCC Commissioner Emmanuel de Guzman described the forum as a vehicle for unity for developing countries with a common denominator as the climate change 's impact . <p> " Our nations all have the most to lose if international community fails in this challenge . We also have the most to gain from a successful conclusion of a Paris agreement on climate change this year , " he said . <p> Prior to the third global VCF , other meetings were held in 2014 to 2015 as part of regional consultations in Africa , Asia , Latin America/Caribbean , the Middle East , and the Pacific that covered 50 countries . <p> " The Manila activity also marks the second occasion in the history of VCF that other countries will be considered for confirmation to join the international cooperation group , potentially expanding its membership to , " the statement from CCC said . <p> Aside from the current 20 VCF member-countries @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as international and civil society organizations ( CSOs ) and international experts are also being involved in the Manila conference . <p> It can be recalled that in October this year during the UN climate conference in Bonn , Germany , the VCF , along with CARE International , started an online campaign ( #1o5C www.105C.org ) aimed at mustering support to strengthen the 2 degrees goal to 1.5 degrees Celsius , which is a central priority for over 100 states and many civil society groups . <p> Saleemul Huq , director of the International Centre for Climate Change and Development ( ICCCAD ) , cited the role of the VCF . <p> " The momentum of this forum of the vulnerable countries reflects just how expansive the frontline of climate change has become .
