<p> MANILA , Philippines -- The Philippines is one of the most disaster-prone countries in the world . Just this year , it was struck by nine major typhoons and tropical storms . Women and girls , children and youth , older persons , persons with disabilities , indigenous people , and the urban poor are particularly vulnerable . <p> This is why , according to former Commission on Higher Education Commissioner Nenalyn Defensor , the sister of Senator Miriam Defensor Santiago , the lawmaker has created laws to promote their protection , and plans to do more should she become president . <p> Defensor represented her sister on Tuesday at Tindog Tayo 2016 , a presidential forum for a resilient Philippines at the GT-Toyota Asian Center Auditorium in Quezon City . <p> Track record <p> According to Defensor , Santiago has accomplished the following to make the country resilient . <p> Filed Senate Bill 2446 or the Children 's Emergency Relief and Protection Act . Among its features are heightened measures against child trafficking and other acts of violence against children in the aftermath of calamities ; @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ families to access services and protect against exploitation ; the involvement of children from pre-disaster risk planning to post-disaster needs assessment ; a budget allocation for the program 's implementation ; the formation of a comprehensive emergency program for children and their immediate recovery ; the improved process of family tracing and reunification for unaccompanied and separated children ; child-centered training for barangay , community , and school officials and rescuers ; the timely delivery of resources for pregnant and lactating mothers ; and less disruption of educational activities with the reduction of use of schools as evacuation centers . Defensor noted that her sister committed to get the bill passed before the Christmas break . <p> Co-authored Republic Act 10121 Philippine Disaster and Risk Reduction Management Act of 2010 , which paved the way for the creation of the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council ( NDRRMC ) , as well as the paradigm shift from disaster management to disaster risk reduction and management . <p> Filed Senate Bill No. 2561 or the National Flood Insurance Act . <p> Plans as President <p> The following , meanwhile , are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the highest post in the land . <p> Create an independent disaster risk reduction and management agency to enhance the capacity of government and communities to prevent , mitigate , prepare for , respond to , and recover from the impact of disasters .
