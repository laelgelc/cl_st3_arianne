<h> Land conversion killing coffee farms in Bataan <p> Orani , Bataan -- The yearly production of coffee has been decreased greatly due to climate change and worse , the alarming and unabated conversion of agricultural lands into resorts and other commercial establishments . <p> Cindy Mendoza , investment specialist of the Department of Trade and Industry ( DTI ) , revealed that wide areas of agricultural lands in Barangay Tala , here , that used to be the planting area of local coffee farmers have gradually diminished to the benefit of only a few businessmen . <p> From 60 tons in 2010 , the yearly production of coffee being harvested in the planting sites in Barangay Tala has declined to 10 tons . <p> She said climate change is also contributing to the decrease in coffee production in the area once known as the virgin forest and watershed of Bataan , since coffee plants favor cool weather . <p> Coffee growers have opted to burn their farms and resort to charcoal making as it commands a higher price than their coffee beans . <p> Now , there @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this town , producing robusta , liberica , and excels . But they , too , are disgruntled with the low prices of coffee and the added burden of new establishments surrounding their farms . <p> Two big resorts occupying large areas of once productive lands have been established in Tala , and making money as these are drawing tourists and have become also a unique venue for convention and meeting for government personnel . <p> Poultry farms have also been operating here , emitting foul odor that has affected residents and prompted Mayor Benjie Serrano to order the cessation of their operations . <p> To address temporarily the coffee growers ' woes , DTI Provincial Director Nelin Cabahug said they are provided assistance and other equipment to boost their income . <p> She said , however , that broad intervention is needed from the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) and Department of Agriculture ( DA ) in order to save the dying coffee industry in Bataan.
