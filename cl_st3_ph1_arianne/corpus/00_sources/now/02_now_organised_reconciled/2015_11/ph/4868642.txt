<h> Sea level rise <p> Global climate change is resulting in rising seas , coastal flooding and increasingly powerful storm surges around the world . In 2014 , the World Meteorological Organization reported that sea level rise accelerated .12 inches ( 3 millimeters ) per year on average worldwide . This is around double the average annual rise of .07in ( 1.6 mm ) in the 20th century . Global sea levels have risen about 8 inches since 1870 , and the rate of increase is expected to accelerate in the coming years . <p> The last predictions were made in 2013 by the United Nations Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) . <p> Based on a consensus of international researchers , the IPCC said global sea levels would likely rise from one to three feet by the end of the century . <p> During NASA 's update of " sea level rise " in August this year , NASA suggested the higher end of that range is more likely based on the latest satellite data . Much of the extra water is coming from melting ice and glaciers , and NASA scientists are particularly concerned about the Greenland ice sheet , which shed an average of 303 gigatonnes of ice a year over the past decade . Also , the Antarctic ice sheet has lost an average of 118 gigatonnes a year . Both are melting faster than ever , and oceans are warming and expanding much more rapidly than they have in years past . <p> " More than 150 million people , most of them in Asia , live within one meter of present sea level . Low-lying US states such as Florida are at risk of disappearing , as are some of the world 's major cities such as Singapore and Tokyo . <p> It may entirely eliminate some Pacific island nations , " said NASA 's Earth Science Division .
