<h> Zamboanga City set to draft new development plan <h> Zamboanga City set to draft new development plan <p> THE local government is set to conduct a survey to identify flood basin areas to ensure the safety of Zamboanga City residents . <p> City Planning and Development coordinator Rodrigo Sicat said the result of the survey will form part of the process in drafting a new City Development Plan . <p> Sicat said that all areas that will be identified or classified as flood basins will be declared as no-build zones . <p> The no-build zone areas will include riverbanks , shorelines , and steep slopes . <p> Sicat said the survey result will also be used as among the bases to revise the zoning ordinance , since there are no provisions for climate change adaptation and disaster risk reduction in the existing law . <p> He said the revised zoning ordinance will prevent the construction of houses in disaster-prone and areas declared as no-build zones .
