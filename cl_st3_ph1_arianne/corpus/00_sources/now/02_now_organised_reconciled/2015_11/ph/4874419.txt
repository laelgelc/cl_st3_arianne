<h> Even some children joined the climate change march in Dumaguete City on Sunday , November 29 , 2015 . <p> Dumaguete City ( CNN Philippines ) -- Some civil society groups and local government representatives took to the streets of Dumaguete City to show their support for the negotiations in 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change , which begins on Monday ( November 30 ) in Paris . <p> Zeph Repollo , campaigns coordinator in the Philippines of 350. org , which spearheaded the march , said that the global warming had nearly reached a dangerous level of 3.5 degrees Celsius and has fueled more deadly supertyphoons and unprecedented sea level rise that would affect countries from the global south , including the Philippines . <p> So 350. org is calling on the people of Dumaguete , along with the rest of Negros Oriental , to select leaders who would show strong political will and good leadership in the coming 2016 elections and who would support the objective of the movement in pushing for the financing of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and keeping 80 percent of known fossil fuels underground . <p> Repollo also added that the COP21 is demanding those countries responsible for climate change to cut their emissions and to limit warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> Local church leaders who supported the march , including Dumaguete Archbishop Rev. Julito Cortes , said that the walk is an " urgent call for unified action from all sectors in the community . "
