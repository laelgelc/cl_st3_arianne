<h> Biggest solar power plant in Cebu province to rise in Toledo City <p> MANILA - Filipino renewable energy firm SunAsia Energy announced Friday the construction of a 60Mw solar power plant in the City of Toledo that will entail investments of US$120 million . <p> As conceived and designed , the plant will occupy approximately 70 hectares of land and locate about 200,000 hi-grade solar panels , all manufactured according to European standards . <p> Unlike other solar projects , SunAsia said in a press statement it aligns itself with nature , seeking to preserve the existing landscape of the project area . <p> " Baltao Farms is presently home to a number of cattle , carabao , goats , and chicken . As planned and designed , the present land use will be preserved for livestock production and only a fraction of the area in the farms will contain panels and infrastructure , " said Tetchi Capellan , President of SunAsia and former Undersecretary of the Department of Agriculture . <p> Capellan explained that " the solar modules will be arranged in 5000 arrays spread on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ under and in between the solar arrays , thereby preserving the feedstock production for consumption by the animals , " she said . <p> The plant layout demonstrates that the modules will be raised off the ground at least 70mm high to allow small animals to graze the area . <p> SunAsia Energy tapped its global engineering partners from Germany in order to accommodate the existing stocking density to harmonize agribusiness with solar PV plant operations . SunAsia Energy targets completion by February 15 , 2016 . <p> The Toledo Solar project will be the biggest renewable energy facility in the province of Cebu . <p> Based on the system impact study report prepared by Power Systems Research and Consultancy Group ( PSRCG ) , the 60Mw solar plant is expected to generate some 861,199Mwh annually . <p> Using the formula of the United Nations , with the 2011 baseline period when fossil fuel share in the Philippine generation mix was at 30 percent , the reduction in greenhouse gas emissions that the Toledo Solar Power Plant can contribute will be about 38,936 tons yearly . <p> Therefore , for 20 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be reduced . <p> " This reduction in carbon emissions coming from solar energy power plants will help mitigate climate change .
