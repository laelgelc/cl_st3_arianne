<h> How Vulnerable is Bangladesh To Climate Change ? <p> Along with the Himalayan silt the Ganges and Brahmaputra Rivers have built the largest delta in the world , Bangladesh , one of the world 's most populous countries . Due to vast coastline , low-lying land and the abundance of rivers , Bangladesh is extremely vulnerable to climate change . Being no stranger to frequent floods , tropical cyclones , river bank erosion and droughts , Bangladesh has been facing the consequences of global warming for quite some time . <p> There are more people here at direct risk of sea level rise and climatic change than anyone else in the world . Villagers pay a high price due to this ; their crops , drinking water supply , houses and lives are all at risk . Most houses are made from straw and mud but the constant rises in sea level engulf their homes and villagers are left homeless . They say erosion is also a big problem as their houses practically erode away . Finding year round sustainable water becomes a challenge . Farmers gradually try to grow salt tolerant crops but not many persevere , inevitably farmers become fishermen to this as a blessing in disguise and take advantage of the excess river water to sell shrimps and fish . Roughly estimated 7.2 million Bangladeshis are to be affected by flooding annually due to sea-level rise between 2070 and 2100 , in addition high salinity will further reduce fresh water supplies . <p> However the government has taken steps towards these , they have installed warning systems ; built shelters to protect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to protect food supplies . Environment and Forests Minister Anwar Hussain Manju says that Bangladesh would seek money from the climate fund not as loans but as grants . <p> Due to global warming Bangladesh is on the verge of drastic environmental change .
