<h> Solons block profiling of airport cops <p> House leaders cautioned yesterday the Philippine National Police ( PNP ) against pursuing a lifestyle check on members of the Aviation Security Group ( AvSeGroup ) assigned at the Ninoy Aquino International Airport ( NAIA ) , saying it is " discriminatory and violative of equal protection clause of the Constitution . " <p> " At this point , it is better for the investigators and prosecutors to investigate against the police and if they find any irregularity or probable cause , then , that is the time they should file a case , " Tugna said in an interview . <p> " I do not subscribe a lifestyle check . I suggest a more direct approach through investigation of the actual incident involved , " he pointed out . <p> PNP chief Director General Ricardo Marquez earlier said they are looking at conducting proposed lifestyle check to clear public perception that some police officers were involved in the tanim-bala ( bullet-planting ) scam . <p> " I think that would be profiling a specific class of persons which in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a matter of policy , this is discriminatory and violative of equal protection clause of the Constitution , " Batocabe , chairman of the House Committee on Climate Change , said . <p> " What NAIA management or the PNP management should do is to strenghten its intelligence gathering actiivities and investigate cops who are involved in illegal activities and file the appropriate administrative and criminal actions if warranted , " he said . <p> Marquez said the lifestyle check " is part of the investigative perspective that the PNP is looking at . " <p> He said they have asked the Office of the Transport Security ( OTS ) to provide them the necessary documents following reports that some airport security personnel were involved in the extortion scheme . <p> The PNP-AvSeGroup earlier said they are not involved in the inspection of the luggages of the travellers , saying that their role only comes in when a passenger is found to be carrying bullets , illegal drugs or other contraband .
