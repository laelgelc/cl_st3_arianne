<h> Climate change and health <p> About a year ago our office physician checked some of us for the condition of our lungs . He told me that mine were about 98 percent clean and exceptionally healthy . <p> I attribute this to being a non-smoker my entire life , living in a part of Metro Manila where there are still a lot of trees , and driving around and working in air-conditioned environments as much as possible to avoid the city 's horrid air pollution . <p> Millions of other people , however , are forced to inhale that foul air . Metro Manila smog is an equal-opportunity oppressor .
