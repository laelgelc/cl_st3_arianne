<h> Canada has a new , attractive prime minister <p> Justin Trudeau , the 43-year-old son of the popular former Canadian Prime Minister , Pierre Trudeau , who died in 2000 , has just won the election to be his country 's next Prime Minister , the first time in Canadian history that a son has followed his father for that job . Although his career path has hardly been political -- at one time he taught snowboarding , at another time he worked as a nightclub bouncer -- Justin Trudeau grew up in a political household and he and his two brothers were media celebrities when his father was in office , although his parents tried to give them as normal a life as possible , sending them to public school on the regular school bus , where they mixed with children from poor backgrounds as well as affluent ones . When his father quit public life , the family moved from the capital , Ottawa , to Montreal where Justin studied French and English literature at McGill University . In 2007 , Trudeau ran for a seat in Parliament on the Liberal Party ticket . <p> Political opponents have tried to paint Trudeau as a naive , socially sheltered young man , who is ill-prepared for public office . But friends who know him well insist that he is a thoughtful , intelligent man who can serve his country well . Trudeau is the first Canadian prime minister to be a boxer ( he boxes once a week ) , the first to have acted in a television drama , and the first to have visible tattoos ( a globe and a raven ) . <p> Trudeau says he would run a budget deficit until 2019 , in order to pay for infrastructure spending of $60 billion over 10 years . He would raise taxes on the rich , and lower taxes on the middle class . He would take climate change seriously and scale back Canada 's deployment of troops in the Middle East .
