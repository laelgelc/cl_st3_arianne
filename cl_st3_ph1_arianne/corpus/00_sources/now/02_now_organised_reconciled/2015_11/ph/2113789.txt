The University helped to develop a smartphone app that the network uses to collect and transmit data . <p> PLCN is an alliance of around 120 volunteer members of Prey Lang 's 339 communities -- most of which are indigenous Kuy -- working together to protect 500,000 hectares of forest in northern Cambodia .
