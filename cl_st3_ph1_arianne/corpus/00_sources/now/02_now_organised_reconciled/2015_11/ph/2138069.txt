<h> APEC : Towards open and equal cooperation in the interests of development <p> MANILA , Philippines - The latest Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation ( APEC ) summit will take place on November 18-19 in Manila . This year 's summit takes place under the motto chosen by the Philippines presidency -- Building inclusive economies , building a better world . <p> For a long time trade has been the driving force of economic growth in the Asia-Pacific region and other parts of the world . However , as the effect of tariff liberalisation started to wear off , it became clear that we need additional agreements covering services , investment , non-tariff barriers , competition policy , and subsidies . Of course , we can not reach such agreements without complicated negotiations and mutual concessions . <p> Deeper regional economic integration offers us a possible way out of the current situation . Today , Russia and its close neighbours -- Armenia , Belarus , Kazakhstan , and Kyrgyzstan -- have taken successful steps to develop the Eurasian Economic Union ( EAEU ) .
