<p> The Office of the Governor , through the Provincial Information Office ( PIO ) , has partnered with the National Youth Commission ( NYC ) to call for the participation of Catandunganons in the campaign to lessen the impact of climate change through a signature drive that started Nov. 11 . <p> Dubbed as #NowPH or Not On Out Watch PH , the youth-led campaign aims to harness the collective power of the public to urgently act on climate change issues to prevent global warming from reaching two degrees Celsius . <p> This seeks to empower young people to lead a climate-resilient and smart lifestyle by promoting 15 Ways to Low Emission Development Strategies ( LEDS ) in school and communities . These are ( 1 ) grow a tree ; ( 2 ) switch off and unplug ; ( 3 ) good bye plastic ; ( 4 ) segregate ; ( 5 ) reduce , reuse and recycle ; ( 6 ) no to burning of wastes ; ( 7 ) promote renewable energy ; ( 8 ) bring your own tumbler ; ( 9 ) @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bike or carpool ; ( 11 ) recycle electronics and batteries ; ( 12 ) energy and awareness ; ( 13 ) use pail , dipper and cups ; ( 14 ) think before you print and ( 15 ) support environment-friendly products . <p> The filled up forms will be coursed through Nydia Delfin , Bicol region area officer , for consolidation with the Office of the President NYC Main Office . The #NowPH campaign is targeting 1 million pledges for the 21st Conference of Parties in Paris , France this December . ( JMV/PIO )
