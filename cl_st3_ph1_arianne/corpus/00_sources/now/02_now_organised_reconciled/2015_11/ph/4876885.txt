<p> MANILA , Philippines -- Senator Grace Poe called on President Benigno Aquino III , who is at the 21st Conference of Parties in Paris , to back up his professed commitment to undertake measures to mitigate the impact of climate change by cutting on government 's utilities spending , which average around P13 billion a year . <p> " The government has consistently appealed to the public to save energy and conserve water , it is the government that has yet to heed its own call , " Poe pointed out . <p> Poe also asked government agencies to set an example by modifying their own practices to reduce energy and water consumption . <p> Citing annual reports from the Commission on Audit , Poe noted that since 2011 , the government 's utilities spending has increased by an average of P1 billion each year for national agencies alone , with 80 percent of this paying for power bills . <p> For 2016 , the proposed allocation for utility expenses is P14.13 billion , P1 billion more than the P12.9-billion allocated in 2015 for electricity and water @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ utilities alone . <p> " We often hear the government asking the public to be judicious in their energy and water consumption , but it seems the government is not doing its part . Our government agencies must also prescribe and adhere to measures that aim to reduce the country 's carbon footprint , " Poe , who is running for president next year , said . <p> At times , she noted , government expenditures on utilities actually exceeded the projected budget like in 2012 , when P2 billion more was spent than the P9.2 billion allocation . <p> " How can we ask the public to find ways to contribute to a better planet when the government itself does not employ measures to cut its huge electricity and water consumption ?
