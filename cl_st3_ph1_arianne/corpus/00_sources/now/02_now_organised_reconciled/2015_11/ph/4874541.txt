<h> PNoy off to Paris for climate talks <p> PRESIDENT Benigno S. Aquino III left for Paris yesterday morning to attend the historic 21st Conference of Parties ( COP21 ) and lead a meeting of developing countries like the Philippines which are the most vulnerable to the effects of climate change . <p> In talks with Palace reporters before his departure at the Ninoy Aquino International Airport , the President said the upcoming climate talks in Paris present a good chance for world leaders to take collective action after failing to reach a consensus at the 2009 climate change conference in Copenhagen , Denmark . <p> Aquino left at past 10 a.m. at the NAIA Terminal 2 and is expected to arrive around midnight in Paris . <p> Aside from leading the meeting of developing countries titled " Climate Vulnerable Forum , " Aquino said he will also share before world leaders the Philippines ' experience in responding to and recovering from natural disasters that affected the country in recent years . <p> Super typhoon Yolanda ( Haiyan ) devastated the country in 2013 .
