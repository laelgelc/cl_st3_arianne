<h> No cancellation of Schengen visas - French Embassy <h> No cancellation of Schengen visas - French Embassy <p> Sunday , November 15 , 2015 <p> THE French Embassy in Manila on Sunday belied reports that it has temporarily cancelled its participation in the Schengen agreement after the terror attacks in Paris that killed 128 people and injured more than 200 . <p> Camille Conde of the French Embassy in Manila said that there was no announcement made by the French government regarding the cancellation of Schengen visas . <p> " Not true ! No announcement like that from the French government . Only security check at the borders , but Schengen visa holders can still enter , " Conde said in a Facebook post . <p> She added that even the European Union has not made such announcements . <p> " We 'll be releasing visas this week as scheduled , " Conde said . <p> The news went viral on social media site Facebook that France would suspend its Schengen agreement for a month starting November 13 . <p> The report said that only those with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ precaution against attacks or public disorder prior to the United Nations Conference on Climate Change that would be held in Paris from November 30 to December 11 . <p> Some 40,000 people were expected to participate prior to the attacks last Friday . <p> The Schengen zone currently has 26 members , including most EU members and four non-EU members . <p> The idea behind the agreement is that the citizens of Schengen countries can access any country without the need to go through border controls . <p> It also allows tourists to travel across the Schengen zone with one common visa .
