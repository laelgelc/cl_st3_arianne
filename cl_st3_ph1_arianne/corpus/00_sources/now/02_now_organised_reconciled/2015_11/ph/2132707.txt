<h> Shipping fears rise as Rhine River falls to lowest level in 40 years <p> The Hague -- The Rhine River has been hit by its longest period of low water in 40 years , Dutch officials said , raising inland shipping costs and fears of collisions on one of Europe 's busiest rivers . <p> " The Netherlands is currently hit by the lowest water levels in the Rhine since 1976 , " said the Rijkswaterstaat , the department tasked with dealing with transport infrastructure . <p> On Monday " the 1976 low water level record of 120 days was broken and it 's expected that the situation will continue , " it explained in a statement . <p> RHINE RIVER GOING DRY ( EPA ) -- A man walks along the banks of the Rhine River at low water in Cologne , Germany , November 9 , 2015 .
