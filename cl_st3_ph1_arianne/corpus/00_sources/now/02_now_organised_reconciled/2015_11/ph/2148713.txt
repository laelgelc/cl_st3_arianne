<h> November 21st , 2015 11:24 AM <p> Senatorial aspirant Francis Tolentino . INQUIRER <p> Senatorial aspirant and disaster preparedness advocate Atty . Francis Tolentino said the Philippine government should be at the forefront of the upcoming United Nations ( UN ) climate summit in France at the end of the month . <p> " Given the typhoons and disasters that strike us yearly , the climate summit is a timely chance for us to push our agenda which is the creation of a regional insurance pool , " Tolentino said . <p> Tolentino , who completed his doctoral studies in environmental law at Tulane Law School in New Orleans , Louisiana , expects that the proposal to put up a regional insurance pool to manage disaster risks will be pursued during the talks in Paris which will also be attended by President Benigno Aquino III . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> " We all know that in every disaster , a country incurs heavy losses that mainly affect its economy .
