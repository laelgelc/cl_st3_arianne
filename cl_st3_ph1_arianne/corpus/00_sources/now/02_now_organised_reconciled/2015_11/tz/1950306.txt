<p> The UNDP funded Solar Power Project has fed new hope to the remote village of Manchali , in Chamwino District , Dodoma Region . <p> Valued at USD 150 000 , the project has benefitted over 600 families in Manchali village . <p> " The project aims at supporting vulnerable local communities to mitigate the effects of climate change through solar power solutions , new land use approaches and adopting modern water use technologies , " said the United Nations Resident Coordinator and UNDP Resident Representative , Alvaro Rodriguez . <p> Speaking during the handover ceremony mid this week in the region , the envoy acknowledged that experience in Tanzania and elsewhere around the world shows that small communities are often hardest hit yet least equipped to cope with the impacts of climate change . <p> " Thanks to UNDP support , the project has strengthened adaptive capacities of the local communities in addressing the impact of climate change , " he noted . <p> The project has resulted in clean energy , many children attending school on time , reforestation and an overall improvement in community @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is real and the UN through its agencies are keen to address its local development . <p> Present at the handover event was UNDP Country Director , Awa Dabo , who seconded that mitigating climate change and promoting economic empowerment are essential for communities to prosper . <p> " The Impact of Climate Change in small communities is real and they are the most affected and the most vulnerable , " she underlined . <p> Noting that the government and
