<h> Right thyme for arts , learning <p> Central Otago district councillor Clair Higginson and Mayor Tony Lepper usher in a week of arts and learning as part of Alexandra 's Thyme Festival . Climate scientist Greg Bodeker said it would focus on what could be done about climate change on a community level . <p> The festival included daily concerts from local schools on the St Enoch 's Church lawn from today until Friday . <p> At the same time there would be ' ' lunchbox conversations ' ' in the community house on topics ranging from tiny homes to natural burials . <p> Mr Lepper said the event had reinvented itself over time but was now about raising awareness .
