<h> Residents urged to take part in global climate change marches <p> Nelson and Takaka will take part in global action against climate change this weekend . <p> Residents of Nelson and Tasman are being urged to take to the streets as part of this weekend 's worldwide action against climate change . <p> Nelson is holding the Climate Change Parade and Picnic on Saturday . The event is part of the global climate march organised by online activist group Avaaz and 350. org to send a message to world leaders at the United Nations Climate Change Summit in Paris . <p> Nelson organiser Emily King said they wanted countries to set bold targets for carbon emission levels . <p> " I think it 's about showing them that people actually care and people want them to take this really seriously . " <p> King said the main link to Nelson is the predicted sea level rise reported by NIWA . <p> " The report states up to a 1 metre sea level rise in at least the next 100 years , which will have a significant effect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ parading from Tahunanui beach along Rocks Road and the river as these areas will be affected . <p> " We want this to be a day to celebrate what we love about Nelson , but with the underlying message that this could be lost if action is n't taken . " <p> The Climate Change Parade and Picnic starts at Tahunanui beach at 10am on Saturday to march 5km to Nelson centre and back to Anzac Park . <p> The gathering at the park starts at 12pm . A photo will be taken to send to Avaaz , who will " hopefully " share it with the Summit . <p> The theme for Nelson 's march is ' green hearts ' , with people encouraged to have flags , banners and clothing to support the theme . <p> Jim Barnes and Willow Milligan are organising the event in Takaka , where it is expected more than 100 people will gather on Sunday . <p> " The unique aspect of this climate march , it 's not a protest march , it 's a march of unity , of solidarity really , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " Barnes said . <p> " Because it is a grassroots movement to stand in solidarity with the people and the world , we need to act and make changes to our lifestyles . " <p> Throughout New Zealand and around the world , people will be marching on the eve of the 2015 United Nations Climate Change Conference ( COP21 ) in Paris , to let world leaders know they they expect them to reach a binding agreement to keep C02 emissions below a two per cent rise . <p> Barnes said the marches being held around the world are even more important in the wake of the tragedy in Paris as the French government has cancelled the people 's climate march in Paris for security reasons . <p> Those who participate in the march are encouraged to wear colourful outfits , cultural costumes and bring festive and musical instruments . <p> The People 's Climate March , starts at 1pm on Sunday . Meet at the Village Green in Takaka.
