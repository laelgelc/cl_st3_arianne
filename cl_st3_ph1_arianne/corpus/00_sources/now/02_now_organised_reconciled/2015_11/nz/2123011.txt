<h> Council challenged on regional policy statement <p> Industry , Maori and farmers took aim at the Otago Regional Council 's proposed policy statement for the next 10 years as the public had its say on the issue . <p> The ORC yesterday held the first day of public hearings in Dunedin on its regional policy statement . <p> The hearings followed the public notification of a proposed statement , intended to cover the next 10 years , earlier this year . <p> The hearings are in Dunedin this week , Alexandra and Queenstown next week , and Oamaru the week after .
