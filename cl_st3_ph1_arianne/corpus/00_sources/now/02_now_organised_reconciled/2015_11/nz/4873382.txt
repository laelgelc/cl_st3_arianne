There is no transparency about their subsidies and there appears to be no ambition to remove them . <p> A @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ subsidising the fossil fuel industry by at least $46 million dollars a year . <p> In response to an OIA request last month , the government is now refusing to reveal how much it provides in tax breaks for petroleum mining on the basis that it no longer collects that information . <p> " The NZ government needs to come clean about how much it is subsidising the fossil fuel industry before it can credibly issue a call for other countries to be transparent , said Mr Hardstaff.
