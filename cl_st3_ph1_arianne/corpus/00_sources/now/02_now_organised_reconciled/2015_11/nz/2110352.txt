<h> Take time to appreciate the slice of heaven at our doorstep <p> Phenomenal is the only word for it . Each of us will have our favourites , but for a wee-boukit city we do incredibly well . <p> You have to start with the venerable Botanic Garden . <p> Having worked for more than a decade at Knox College , I thought I knew everything there was to know about it , but every time you visit there 's a new revelation . <p> At this time of year the tangled , voluptuous beauty of the rhododendron dell draws one in , draws out the oohs and ahs at the colours , the burgeoning ranks of blossoms , the familiar absurdity of such delicate beauty emerging from knotty , stubborn wood . <p> The top half of the Gardens is hard to beat , with its mix of chaotic native bush and elegant , enticing spaces to sit and dream . <p> All these intimate nooks and crannies . <p> The aviary , seemingly always building up its exotic display , the skyscraping trees , the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ flatlands for roses and ducks and children , herbs and perennials , and more breathtakingly beautiful trees . <p> Unbelievable the wealth we 're offered at the Botanics . <p> Makes even ratepaying seem worthwhile . <p> Up the Valley to Bethunes Gully , stretching up towards Mt Cargill . <p> A real gully , living up to its name . <p> I counted 14 cars there the other day , mainly dog walkers , parents and kids , but some old stagers like myself . <p> Memories of teenage crayfish hunts ( can that be right ?
