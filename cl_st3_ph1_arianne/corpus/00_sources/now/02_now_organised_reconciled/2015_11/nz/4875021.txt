<h> Xi Jinping heads for Paris as China labour under a cloud of smog <p> On the same day that Chinese President Xi Jinping was due to address climate change talks in Paris , Beijing issued its highest air pollution warning so far this year . <p> Air quality index readings surged beyond 500 , more than 20 times levels considered safe by the World Health Organisation , on Monday morning , prompting local authorities to issue an " orange alert " requiring factories to shut down or cut production and construction sites to down tools . <p> The problem is far from restricted to the nation 's capital . Thick smog has blanketed a 530,000 square kilometre area in northern China - the equivalent of 32 Beijings and more than twice the size of Victoria . <p> And it comes just weeks after AQI readings hit a record 1200 in Shenyang , after central heating was turned on for winter in the north-eastern city . <p> China 's toxic air pollution is the most visible catalyst of the country 's evolution from a relative climate change laggard @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ; for decades , cheap coal-fired power had propelled its staggering economic growth , but at devastating environmental cost . <p> Xi is the first Chinese president to personally attend a United Nations annual international climate change conference , and has made historic joint pledges with US President Barack Obama and French President Francois Hollande in the lead-up . <p> A new report released by the Ministry of Science and Technology , involving 550 environmental experts , warns of the devastating consequences from global warming for China , from rising seas in its east to melting Himalayan snowcaps on its western frontier and shifting snow and rainfall patterns in the north . <p> Despite the political will behind reaching a global accord , Chinese negotiators will not agree to shouldering a disproportionate burden . <p> While China 's longstanding position that it is a developing nation with low per capita income remains , the report recognises that as the world 's leading polluter by far , China faces immense international pressure to further commit to curb greenhouse gas emissions . <p> " There is an unavoidable trend for all countries to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ countries to shoulder larger emissions-reduction responsibilities , " the report said .
