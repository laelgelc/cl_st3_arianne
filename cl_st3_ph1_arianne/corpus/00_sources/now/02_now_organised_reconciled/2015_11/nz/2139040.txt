<h> South city flooding predicted in 2010 <p> The South Dunedin floods were predicted in a 2010 report on climate change , its author says . <p> University of Otago geography emeritus professor Blair Fitzharris , who has worked with the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , was commissioned by the Dunedin City Council in 2010 to write the report . He spoke about it at the St Peter 's Caversham parish last night as part of The Caversham Lectures series . <p> Prof Fitzharris identified ' ' low lying densely populated urban areas , especially South Dunedin ' ' as one of five ' ' sectors ' ' at risk from the projected effects of climate change . <p> The June flooding in South Dunedin was in keeping with that prediction , he said . <p> ' ' Recent events , particularly flooding in South Dunedin , is an indicator of what we said was going to be one of the holdable points . ' ' <p> That kind of flooding would only happen more often with sea level rise , he said . <p> ' ' @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is controlled by the ... height of the ocean , particularly close to the shoreline at St Kilda and St Clair . <p> ' ' So , as sea level rises in the future , the groundwater level is just going to keep rising as well , unless some action is taken . <p> ' ' And then at the times of the storm , you 're going to get a double whammy , with the flooding rain and the storm surge . <p> ' ' So , that 's going to make these flooding events larger as the sea level rises , and also more frequent .
