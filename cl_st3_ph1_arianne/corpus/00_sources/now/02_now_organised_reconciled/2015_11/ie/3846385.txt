<p> Clean air represents what is known as a " co-benefit " of addressing global warming . Co-benefits , as the name implies , are ancillary benefits that accrue from climate change mitigation measures , such as switching from polluting fuels like coal and peat to clean renewables or improving the thermal efficiency of houses . <p> For a long time health issues were not even a part of climate change discussions . A growing awareness and body of research led to the Lima Decision at last year 's 20th COP that requires countries to consider health issues in their formal climate change policy planning . <p> Co-benefits can be as simple as those that arise from successful policies promoting active transport such as cycling and walking .
