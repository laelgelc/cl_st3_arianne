<p> Health was the central theme of LEAF 's conference ' Setting the Agenda : Healthy Food and Farming Fit for the Future ' , which took place in London earlier this week . <p> The conference explored how technology , science , innovation and strong public engagement will be critical to addressing some of the biggest issues facing food and farming systems such as climate change , population growth and increasing public health issues . <p> Speakers from across the food , farming and research sectors set out their vision of how to balance the competing pressures and demands on the global food system . <p> Dame Professor Anne Glover DBE , vice principal for external affairs and dean for Europe at University of Aberdeen , set out her solutions for ensuring policies and legislation for food and farming are firmly based on evidence . " The world 's population should expect access to sufficient healthy food produced without detriment to our environment .
