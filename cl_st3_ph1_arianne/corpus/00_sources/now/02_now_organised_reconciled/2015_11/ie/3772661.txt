<p> Whatever suspense remained over the COP21 climate conference that will take place in Paris from November 30th to December 11th evaporated this week , when representatives of 70 of the 195 participating countries met in Paris for a " pre-COP " . <p> " There will be an agreement on December 11th , " Christiana Figueres , the executive secretary of the UN Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , said emphatically . Asked if it will be a fair agreement , Figueres replied , " Why would anyone accept it if it was n't fair ? " <p> Figueres spoke at a press conference with France 's foreign minister Laurent Fabius , who will preside over COP21 , and the Peruvian environment minister Manuel Pulgar Vidal , who ran last year 's COP20 in Lima . <p> Reports published during the " pre-COP " had driven home the gravity of the situation , Fabius said .
