<p> UN special envoy on climate change Mary Robinson has said she is optimistic an agreement can be reached at the global summit on climate change in Paris . <p> Ms Robinson , former president of Ireland , said on Monday she was very struck at the marked focus on the impact of climate change on people and vulnerable societies in addresses delivered on the opening day of COP21 . <p> Speaking to The Irish Times , she said she hoped such a people-centred focus would carry through to the negotiations . <p> " I have been very struck by the emphasis on it being people-centred . President Francois Hollande used climate justice , President Michelle Bachelet of Chile used climate justice , a concern for the most vulnerable . <p> " The problem is that unless those heads of state say it expressly to the delegations from their countries , it may not carry down . <p> " We may be back to the same not so people-centred approach of the past .
