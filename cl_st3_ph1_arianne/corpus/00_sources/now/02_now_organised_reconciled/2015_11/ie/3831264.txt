<p> Members of campaign group Plane Stupid parked a vehicle across the lanes of the main road into Heathrow and locked themselves to it . <p> They displayed a banner with the message : " No ifs , no buts : no third runway . " <p> The protest has left long tailbacks to terminals 1,2 and 3 . <p> Police were called to the scene at 7.42am and no arrests have been made , a Scotland Yard spokesman said . <p> Plane Stupid spokesman Cameron Kaye said : " Airport expansion would wreck the legally-binding Climate Change Act , risking wiping out 55% of species this century and displacing 75 million more people from their homes by 2035 . <p> " If aviation growth is n't reduced , by 2037 aviation alone could emit all of the carbon it 's safe for the UK to emit . The government needs to choose : build new runways or stop climate chaos : it 's that simple . "
