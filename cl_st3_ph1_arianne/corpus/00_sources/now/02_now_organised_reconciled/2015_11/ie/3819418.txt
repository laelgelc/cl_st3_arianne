<h> Carrefour Recognises Spanish Fish Supplier With Award <p> Retail giant Carrefour has presented one of its suppliers , Lugo fishery Puerto Celeiro , with the ' Grand Climate Challenge Award for Suppliers ' . <p> As reported by EPR Retail News , the fishery , which has been a supplier to Carrefour Spain for 14 years , was awarded for selective hake fishing extraction method . <p> Carrefour Group created this award to highlight and commend the efforts that their suppliers have made in the face of climate change . <p> The award also represents the fishery 's commitment to the sustainability of the marine ecosystem , having already helped reduce fish discards by 99 per cent . <p> Puerto Celeiro has also contributed on a social and economic front by breathing new life into the fishing market and by creating more jobs for sailors . <p> Furthermore , Puerto Celerio has demonstrated a passionate commitment to the environment , and according to EPR Retail News , the Lugo fishery has made significant annual savings of more than 540,000 litres of fuel per fishing boat used .
