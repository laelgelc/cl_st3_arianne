<p> The Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) says that strategic planning is needed to address our climate challenges , with new research showing we face enormous changes in our weather patterns . <p> A new report , ' Ensemble of Regional Climate Model Projections for Ireland ' , used international data on projected global climate change and modelled it to an Irish level . <p> It reveals how key features of our climate , including the average temperature , rainfall and the types of storms , are projected to change over the coming decades . <p> Key findings indicate that by the middle of this century annual average temperatures are projected to increase by 1C to 1.7C , while hot days will be warmer by between 0.7C and 2.6C . <p> Rainfall is expected to drop during spring and summer , putting pressure on agriculture , while heavy rainfall events will increase during winter and autumn , increasing the risk of flooding .
