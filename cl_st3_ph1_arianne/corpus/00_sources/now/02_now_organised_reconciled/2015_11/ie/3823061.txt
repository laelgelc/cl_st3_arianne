So how can we fix it ? <p> The main way in which we can address the problem is through reducing global greenhouse gas emission targets . By doing this we can limit the temperature increase compared to pre-industrial levels to 2C by the end of the century . That is the safe threshold set by scientists , beyond which they say climate change will become dangerous and irreversible . Without action soon , global warming could reach up to 5C above pre-industrial levels . We also need to plan for the move to alternative sources of low-carbon energy , and invest money in such new technologies . Developing countries argue they will require considerable financial assistance to mitigate against and adapt to climate change . And crucially , we need every nation -- big and small -- to sign up to legally binding pledges , so that everyone is playing their part equally . <p> And that 's where Paris comes in ? <p> Right .
