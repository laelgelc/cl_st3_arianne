Fast forward to the Victorian era -- a generation of expert plant breeders and nurserymen was introducing an ever-increasing range of delicious fruit and vegetable species/varieties into cultivation . <p> Some of the problems that 21st-century kitchen gardeners face , including increased urbanisation , dwindling land banks , soil degradation and the threat of climate change , can seem very different to those tackled by the world 's first farmers . <p> In many ways though , they 're not .
