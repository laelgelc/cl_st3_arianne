<h> Mayo events to mark need for action on climate change <h> Share This <p> Belmullet and Westport to host public gatherings to create awareness ahead of French conference <p> Michael Duffy <p> Schools , community groups , churches , businesses and members of the public are set to come together in Mayo this coming weekend to ask An Taoiseach Enda Kenny and his government to represent Ireland in Paris by coming to an agreement to take definite action on climate change . The 2015 United Nations Climate Change Conference ( COP 21 ) will be held in Le Bourget in France , from November 30 to December 11 , and Laura Dixon of An Taisce is asking the Mayo public to support events in both Belmullet and Westport this weekend . " We are calling on world leaders to create a low carbon , just and sustainable future for all and these events in Belmullet and Westport are organised as part of the Global Climate March ( see **26;1163;TOOLONG ) , " said Ms Dixon .
