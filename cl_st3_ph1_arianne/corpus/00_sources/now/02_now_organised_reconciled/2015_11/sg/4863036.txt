<h> Urban SDG indicator on public space improved ; next up , national urban policies <h> The U.N . expert committee tasked with offering final recommendations on how to measure the new Sustainable Development Goals met for a second time in late October . <p> With less than four months to go until the United Nations Statistical Commission meets to approve an expected set of benchmarks for the new Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , statisticians met in late October in Bangkok for the second meeting of the Inter-agency and Expert Group on SDGs ( IAEG-SDGs ) . The group is working to hone a vast array of proposed measurements for the 17 goals and 169 targets that were approved in September by the U. N. General Assembly . <p> For urban advocates closely watching the status of Goal 11 , the urban SDG , concerns over a watered-down indicator on public space have been alleviated to an extent . N. system is still not very clear how the targets on cities are going to be measured in a global framework and country frameworks -- they still talk about national systems , " Moreno said . " There is a need to revise these systems in order to include some level of city responsibility . " <p> For example , if a country were to report its Goal 11 updates from one set of 10 cities in a given year and another set of 10 cities the following year , there would be no accurate comparison over time . For now , this question remains up in the air .
