<p> KUALA LUMPUR : Malaysia has pledged to cut carbon emissions intensity by 45 per cent by 2030 . <p> Malaysia 's Minister of National Resources and Environment Wan Junaidi Jaafar , speaking to Channel NewsAsia ahead of the Paris conference , was confident the country can achieve a 40 per cent reduction in greenhouse gas emissions previously pledged by the county by year 2020 . <p> The minister also said Kuala Lumpur will submit its game plan with specific targets to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change before the Paris conference next week . <p> More than 160 countries have already submitted their plans on the actions they will take for a low-carbon , climate-resilient future , ahead of the UN Climate Conference in Paris , where an ambitious plan to tackle climate change will be negotiated . <p> Minister Wan Junaidi said he will be presenting Malaysia 's game plan on climate change in early December . A key point in the plan is the pledge to conserve the Malaysian rainforest , which is one of the oldest in the world . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ across Borneo from the Indonesian border to Sarawak and from across Brunei to Sabah , " said Mr Wan Junaidi . <p> Measures will be put in place for this to happen . Under a new bio-diversity policy , existing laws will be tightened to curb illegal logging . Tougher penalties too , have been proposed . <p> Carbon dioxide emissions is another area to be tackled . Car ownership in Malaysia is the third highest in the world at a whopping 93 per cent with 54 per cent of households having more than one car . <p> To cut greenhouse gas emissions , Malaysia is phasing out Ron 95 , which is a lower grade petrol but widely used by motorists here because it is much cheaper than the higher-grade Ron 97 . Ron 97 also carries a Euro 4 standard , which means reduced emissions . <p> " Ron 97 is already Euro 4 , but Ron 95 is still Euro 2 , and so we are determined that by 2018 , that we will introduce Euro 4 for both , " said Mr Wan Junaidi . <p> But @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the energy mix in its power supply . Next year , Malaysia is chasing economic growth of between 4 and 5 per cent . It is confident however that growth will not hinder its ability to meet emission reduction targets . <p> Kuala Lumpur is also set to launch its mass rapid transit system in 2016 and it hopes that this , and the expansion of the city 's light rail system , will encourage Malaysians to use public transport and leave their vehicles at home .
