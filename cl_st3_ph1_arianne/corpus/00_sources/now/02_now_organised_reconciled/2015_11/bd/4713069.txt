<h> FM to lead Bangladesh in Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting <h> Foreign Minister Abul Hassan Mahmood Ali has left Dhaka for Malta to lead Bangladesh in the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting ( CHOGM 2015 ) . <p> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina was expected to attend the Nov 27-29 meeting , but she cancelled the planned visit at the last moment . <p> The foreign ministry said Ali , who flew out of the capital on Monday , will also be attending the Pre-CHOGM Commonwealth Foreign Affairs Ministers Meeting and other events starting from Nov 25 . <p> The foreign ministers from 52 member states will discuss global political and economic developments and issues of common interest such as implementation of the SDGs , migration , and climate change in the ministers ' meeting . <p> They will also review the reports to be submitted to the Heads of Government during the CHOGM . <p> The Commonwealth secretary general , different Commonwealth organisations as well as Commonwealth-associated and civil society organisations submit those reports . <p> Ali is also expected to have bilateral meetings with some of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 30.
