<h> Al Jazeera documentary focuses on the effects of climate change in Kenya <p> 6 Nov 2015 12:16Media News <p> Over the last four years , Kisilu Musya , a Kenyan smallholder farmer , has used his camera to capture the life of his family , his village and the impacts of climate change . <p> Over the years he has filmed floods , droughts , and storms but also the more human impacts of climate change - kids are sent home from school when school fees ca n't be paid ; men are moving to towns in search for jobs ; and family relations are strained . <p> Through seamless intercutting of Musya 's video diaries and director Julia Dahl 's footage , the film creates a raw and honest portrait of family life in rural Kenya at the frontline of climate change . <p> Musya and his wife Christina live together with their nine children . They have been self-reliant and surviving off the land for generations , but now they are facing harder times due to more and more extreme weather conditions . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to give up , who continues to drive long-term change in a community fighting for day-to-day survival .
