<h> The Truth About Drought <p> 18 November 2015 , 14:54 <p> ALL OVER THE news , talk is that food security in South Africa is bleak because of the terrible drought that is upon us . Water shortage has hit us hard , particularly in the rural and farming areas . Average crop yields are low , and animal stock is dying in their hundreds as rivers and dams dry up . We are asked to use water sparingly , and save for the next day . Scary , is n't it ? This El Nino weather pattern is creating havoc . <p> Different projections have been bandied about , attempting to quantify the scale of the problem . If something is not done , and done right , we are in for a tough 2016 . The price of staple foods will increase as maize and wheat , poultry and meat will have to be imported . The threat is real . The Earth , our only home , seems to have turned its back on us . <p> Or has it ? <p> Since the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ planet has gone through countless periods of rebirth , destruction and renewal . The Earth has at some point been uninhabitable , and at some other points provided the right conditions for life to thrive . Thus it had gone through some extreme conditions , decimating life , only to renew itself , with even more varied species , and become hospitable for the newer , or adapted species . Think of the extinct 99% of animal species that had lived before . The Earth provides rebirth , sustenance , and also is a very capable destroyer . <p> The problem that we are experiencing with drought and harsh conditions should not be troubling . What should be troubling is that we humans believe that humanity is a special species , and everything Earth provides and produces should strictly be to our specifications . Somehow , we humans have a misguided notion that we exist on Earth to freely inherit its generosity . Far from the truth . We have no power over what Earth is capable of . <p> Drought is natural and should happen . Irrespective of climate change @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ drought should happen . Drought had happened countless times previously , before the advent of even rudimentary technology . And when it happens , it will only be Earth going through some of its amazing natural phenomena , without giving a thought as to who lives on Earth , or who does n't . Man does not determine what nature should do . <p> We should be glad that we occupy this green Mecca . We should go on looking for alternative means of clean water to sustain ourselves , and stop harping and relying on Earth . For us to be on Earth is a privilege , and not a right . Remember , it is the same Earth that kills millions of us without discriminating , through natural phenomena such as floods , heat waves , earthquakes , volcanoes , tornadoes , tsunamis and many others . Earth , this cruel crucible harbouring life , was not designed with human beings in mind . <p> Human beings are far from special . We should stop thinking of ourselves in those terms . For now , while we face drought @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , but it 's only one of Earth 's phases . Just like other more severe droughts in centuries past , this one , too , will pass . No amount of willpower , kindness , and proximity to God through religion can stop it , delay it or reverse it . It is nature . And it is once again a painful reminder that we are a powerless speck of dust in the full power of Earth . <p> It is futile and pointless to gather and pray that the drought ends . It is an even unthinkable impossibility to believe that we can pray and influence weather patterns , thereby creating rain in abundance . Who do we think we are ? <p> We do n't possess power to control anything . We are powerless . The Earth does not take its cue from us .
