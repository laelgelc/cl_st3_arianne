<h> UPDATE 1-World Bank 's IFC issues first green bond in South Africa <p> CAPE TOWN Nov 25 ( Reuters ) - The International Finance Corporation ( IFC ) , a unit of the World Bank , issued its first South African rand-denominated green bond on Wednesday , raising around $71 million for climate-friendly investments in emerging markets . <p> The nine-year 1 billion rand paper was issued at a yield of 8.72 percent and marked the first time a green bond has been issued in South Africa by a multilateral institution . <p> " Green bonds are a powerful force for mobilizing international and domestic savings for climate finance , " said IFC Vice President and Treasurer Jingdong Hua in a statement . <p> FirstRand Bank Limited , one of South Africa 's largest banking groups , is the anchor investor and Rand Merchant Bank the sole manager for the transaction . <p> The IFC , founded in 1956 , has previously issued similar bonds in Chinese and Indian currencies as part of efforts to build the asset class in developing markets . Only through the private sector , only through capital markets can the world raise this sort of money and green bonds are a critical element in crowding in that investment , " he told Reuters . <p> He said that between July 2014 and June 2015 , 38 projects received the IFC 's green-bond financing , with investments expected to reduce greenhouse emissions by the equivalent of 2.5 million metric tonnes of carbon dioxide a year . This is similar to taking 500,000 cars off the road , Hua added . ( Reporting by Wendell Roelf ; Editing by Joe Brock and Hugh Lawson )
