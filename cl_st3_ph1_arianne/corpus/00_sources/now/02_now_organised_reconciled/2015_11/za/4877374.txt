<h> Think before selling fishing quotas <p> Walvis Bay -- Namibians , who are selling their fishing quotas in exchange for quick money should think twice as it is not economically , financially and socially sustainable , says Robert Shiimoshili , the human resource manager at Cavema Fishing . <p> He said the only way that new comers in the fishing industry can acquire necessary knowledge and skills is to partner in joint ventures with fellow citizens and foreign investors in order to contribute to development and employment creation in the country . <p> " You can get money by selling your quotas immediately , but it 's not worth it in the long run , " he advises . <p> The name Cavema was created as a joint venture between two Namibian quota holders , namely Camoposatu Investment and
