<h> Animals and plants <h> South African scientists depart on whale studying expedition <p> 2 November 2015 Scientists have set off on a research voyage to collect data on humpback whales between Dassen Island and Groenriviermond off the west coast of South Africa . Two ships , the Department of Environmental Affairs ' RV Algoa and the Department of Agriculture , Forestry and Fisheries ' FRS Ellen Khuzwayo , left on the expedition on 28 October . The team on the RV Algoa will focus on environmental sampling for analysis of the chemical make-up of the region ? checking the available nutrients , and available food for whales . Researchers will record every whale seen along a pre-designed research path . Those scientists on the FRS Ellen Khuzwayo will be dedicated to biological sampling of humpback whales encountered in the region ? collecting DNA samples , taking photographs of whale tails/flukes and attaching satellite tracking instruments in order to understand their movement and behaviour , both on the west coast and on their return to Antarctic feeding grounds . " The department said it was important to understand the dynamics of the whales for population identification , abundance estimation and conservation management . Upon completion of breeding activities , they begin their 2 ? 500km swimming journey to Bouvet Island , south-west of Cape Town , in late spring/early summer .
