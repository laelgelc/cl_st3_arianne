<h> Sundarbans tiger population stable , finds report <p> Fighting ingress of salt water and erosion of forest land due to climate change , tiger population in the shrinking islands of Sundarbans has remained stable over the last few years with the latest report estimating the presence of 86 big cats . <p> Photo : The Hindu <p> According to the latest camera-trap report conducted by the West Bengal forest department and WWF during November to March this year in Sundarbans , there are a minimum of 83 tigers and could be a maximum of 128 . <p> " The mean of this range has been calculated at 86 using a statistical model . There has been a marginal increase in the tiger count so we can say that the population is now very well stable , " Principal Chief Conservator of Forests and Chief Wildlife Warden Pradeep Vyas told PTI . <p> Since tigers are at the apex of the forest ecosystem , the stable population of the big cats prove that the delta 's ecosystem is also healthy . <p> " Most importantly the population range is close to last year 's range which proves the stability . There can be minor increase and decrease in the population , " Vyas said . <p> In 2014 a similar exercise had put the number of tigers at 76 . <p> " You can say that the population is stable . It is more or less the same since the last survey , " said WWF 's Ratul Saha . <p> It was for the first time that cameras were set not only in the Sundarbans tiger reserve area but also outside it in the rest of South 24-Parganas forest division . <p> The report found that areas outside the core zone have a good population of tigers that are breeding well . <p> Five cubs were also spotted by the cameras but were not taken into account as only adults are counted in population estimates as a rule . <p> Threatening tiger habitat and its prey base , mangrove forests in the Sundarbans delta are hit hard by constant land erosion and salinity due to rising sea levels . <p> According to a study by the School of Oceanography Studies in Jadavpur University , the rate of coastal erosion was measured to be about 5.50 sq km per year . <p> Another report prepared by a group of ecologists led by Dr M Zafar-ul Islam had estimated that if the sea level rises by one meter , the Sundarbans will lose more than half of its area . <p> " In the long-term sea level rise will be an issue for the tiger population as substantial area is being lost to the sea , " Saha said . <p> Divided by a complex network of streams , rivers , tidal creeks and channels , the UNESCO World Heritage site is also home to 40 lakh people often leading to cases of man-animal conflict . <p> Jantakareporter.com is an independent news website covering the latest trends from India and around the world .
