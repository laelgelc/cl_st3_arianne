<h> Authorities warn on looming rice shortage <p> There is a looming rice shortage in the country following a poor crop at the Mwea Irrigation Scheme . <p> Mwea East deputy County Commissioner Geoffrey Talagon says the rice shortage has been occasioned by a massive reduction in water levels at River Nyamindi , the scheme 's main water source . <p> This even as the country grapples with a devastating food shortage in various regions . <p> Get breaking news on your Mobile as-it-happens . SMS ' NEWS ' to 20153 <p> The Mwea Irrigation Scheme is one of Kenya 's biggest rice producing schemes , accounting for about 80 percent of the total rice production in the country . The scheme is served by two main rivers , River Nyamindi and River Thiba . <p> Reports of falling water levels in River Nyamindi have however raised the red flag over the sustainability of rice growing at the Scheme with reports of an acute looming shortage of rice as a result of crop failure due to lack of water for irrigation . <p> Talagon has termed the situation critical noting that the water level in the river has dropped from 11.5 cubic meters to 1.7 cubic meters . <p> The total crop failure has been noticed in Karaba , Wamumu , Mutithi and Ndindiruku which is part of the 4,000 hectares of the total Mwea Irrigation Scheme . <p> Kirinyaga County administration now wants the construction of Thiba dam fast tracked to cushion the rice farmers from the devastating effects of climate change and the continued environmental degradation being witnessed on Mt . Kenya , the main catchment area for River Nyamindi.
