<h> Policy Responses to Climate-related Security Risks : The African Union <h> Attachments <p> Africa is responsible for a mere 4 per cent of global carbon dioxide emissions . Yet , 57 per cent of the countries facing the highest double burden of climate exposure and political fragility risks are located in sub-Saharan Africa . As the risks are most pronounced in fragile countries and transcend boundaries , responses from intergovernmental organizations ( IGOs ) such as the African Union ( AU ) are crucial in developing the relevant solutions . <p> To offer a better understanding of how the AU discusses and responds to security challenges arising from climate change , this Background Paper provides a comprehensive overview of the AU 's climate security policy landscape for interested policymakers in Africa and beyond . The main findings reflect that the AU is increasingly recognizing different security risks , and that its discourse is rapidly developing towards more integrated responses to climate-related security risks .
