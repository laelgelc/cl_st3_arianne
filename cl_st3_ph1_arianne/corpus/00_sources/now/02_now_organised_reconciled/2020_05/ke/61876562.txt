PHOTO JOSE JORDAN STR AFP <h> In Summary <p> *Opportunities have no owners . They are free for all who will seize them . Every opportunity will respond to those who are properly equipped to grab them . <p> How do we get opportunity from adversity ? To answer this , we must first understand what makes up an opportunity . <p> For one , opportunity always sends signals . Former US President George W Bush sounded the alarm about the imminent threat of a pandemic and President Barack Obama , building on this , set up an office in the White House to ensure a readiness and quick response when it did happen . <p> Bill Gates at a Ted talk that went viral , also reiterated the same . So let no one say that they were not warned . <p> Another opportunity that has been giving advance warnings for years has been the effects of climate change . Gladys , a former colleague , saw the writing on the wall concerning climate issues and the environment and launched a consulting firm to help organisations address these issues . Today she is helping clientele from all over the world to prepare . <p> A crisis is the result of changes that were not managed and warnings that were not adhered to . <p> Opportunities are time sensitive . Real winners are those who see it coming and prepare for it . Air travel was dominated by a firm called the Douglas company and they made a name for themselves building the DC series of aircraft . <p> After the second world war , they stepped up production of the DC 7 , which was the star of that era . It was a propeller powered plane and there were many orders . <p> Boeing on the other hand focused on building the jet engined Boeing 707 on speculation and without any confirmed orders . The rest as they say is history . The Douglas company became a part of Boeing . <p> Opportunities have no owners . They are free for all who will seize them . Every opportunity will respond to those who are properly equipped to grab them . <p> We are facing a world where virtually everyone has been affected . Many businesses have already closed and many industries have been brought to their knees . This is a time like no other in history . <p> However , through it all , what is the possibility of some silver linings ? What will characterise those who come out stronger ? We need to look at this era like we would wartime because the biggest winners in a post-war world are always the builders . <p> Every war brings destruction and as such rebuilding becomes the biggest activity of a post-war era . <p> Similarly , in the aftermath of this war with the coronavirus , those who engage in rebuilding will be at the top . But then , what are we rebuilding ? First , a lot of hope has been dashed and as such merchants of hope will thrive during and after the pandemic . People need to know that they can start again . They need to know that in spite of what looks like the end of the road , there is a way forward . That they can and will rise again . <p> Judging by the volume of webinars being held every single day globally , we are seeing a spike in the quest for information . As such , people who have accurate and relevant information will thrive greatly in the post pandemic era . <p> There is a growing appetite for information on how to rebuild , how to think creatively , how to develop agility , and how to navigate uncharted territories . <p> When this is all over , there are those who will thrive because they seized an opportunity .
