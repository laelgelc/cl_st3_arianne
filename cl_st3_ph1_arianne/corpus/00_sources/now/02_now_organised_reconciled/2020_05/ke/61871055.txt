<h> Distribution of potato seeds in six counties begins <p> The Ministry of Agriculture has commenced distribution of 25 tons of potato seeds in six targeted counties in efforts aimed at scaling up production of certified potato seeds . <p> Agriculture Cabinet Secretary Peter Munya , says this is expected to improve farmer access to certified potato seed to improve production and consequently mitigate the effect of climate change on the crop . <p> Munya says 60 county extension staff have been trained on Climate Smart Potato Production Practices to support the project . <p> Under the project , KALRO is providing 25 tons of potato basic seed for planting to the counties to produce certified potato seed that will result in about 2,500 tons after two field multiplications which will be added to the current annual production of 6,500 tons of certified seeds .
