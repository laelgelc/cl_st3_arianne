<h> Weatherman warns of more rains from next week as more die of floods <p> Kenya , May 13- The Kenya Metrological Department has warned of heavy rainfall across the country from next week , even as the governmen announced that 237 people have been killed by floods since last month . <p> The Department Director Stella Aura urged the public to heed the government 's precautionary measures , particularly for people living in lowland areas or along the river banks . <p> Areas worst affected are in Rift Valley , Central and along the Coastal strip on the banks of River Tana that has been overflowing , rendering hundreds homeless . <p> " My appeal is for Kenyans to listen to what the government is advising them to do , " she said . <p> Environment Cabinet Secretary Keriako Tobiko attributed the current flooding crisis to climate change- which has led to a drastic disparity in weather patterns , with water levels in places like Lake Victoria breaking a 56-year record . <p> " We take nature and environment quite lightly . Nature is unforgiving , " the Cs asserted .
