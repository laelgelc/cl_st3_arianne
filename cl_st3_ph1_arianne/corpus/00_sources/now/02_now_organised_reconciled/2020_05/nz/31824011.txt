<h> $19.7 million worth of insurance claims paid out for Southland floods <p> The flooding shown from at farm near Gore taken by Alistair Cocks in February . Almost $20 million has been received in insurance claims from the floods that hit Southland in February . <p> Close to $20 million in insurance claims have been paid out for the damage from the Southland floods in February . <p> Throughout Southland , Fiordland and Clutha , $19.7 million had been received in insurance claims from heavy flooding that hit the region . <p> Of that , $7.3 million were in house and content claims , $900,000 in vehicle damaged claims and $11.4 million was claimed in the damage to commercial property and interruption to business . <p> A State of Emergency was declared at the time and 2400 residents from Gore , Mataura and Wyndham were evacuated from their homes . <p> In Fiordland , hundreds of tourists and residents needed to be airlifted out of Milford Sound after they became stuck when landslips closed Milford Rd . <p> It only reopened to the public in March . <p> Insurance Council of New Zealand chief executive Tim Grafton ? said while the statistics did n't divide the claims between regions the majority of claims would have been in Southland excluding Fiordland . <p> Cyclones such as Gita and Fehi that hit New Zealand in 2018 had a larger sum of claims but were made across a wider area , he said . <p> The Timaru hail storm last year in November had the largest sum of insurance claims for a natural disaster in the past five years at $83 million . <p> Nationwide , flooding in December that particularly hit the Southern Lakes area cost $17 million in insurance claims . <p> Climate change would see an increase in extreme weather events and it showed the importance of having insurance , Grafton said . <p> " It is clear that we need to adapt to the changes we are seeing and take steps to reduce risks where possible to minimise the social impact and cost . <p> " Flood prone areas of New Zealand are well known . We need to be mindful of this as we develop new and existing areas of New Zealand to reduce risks for Kiwis . " <p> While , $19.7 million had been the cost received in insurance claims , the economic impact for the region would be wider , he said . <p> A roading operations report presented to the Southland District Council in March estimated the cost of flood damage could reach $3 million in damaged to infrastructure . <p> In February , Gore District Council regulatory and community services general manager Dr Ian Davidson-Watts ? estimated the flood could cost $2 million excluding landfill restoration . <p> The Southland Climate Change Impact Assessment , authored by NIWA , released in August 2018 showed that floods are expected to be larger across the region and the number of heavy rainfall days are expected to increase . <p> Annual rainfall is predicted to increase by five per cent by mid-century , and as much as 20 per cent by the end of the century , with a larger increase in the northern part of the region , where the rivers flow from .
