<p> The Government 's Budget has been met with mixed reaction from environmental groups and criticised for its lack of targeted climate change spend . <p> Source : Breakfast <p> Budget 2020 set aside $1.1 billion for environmental jobs , expected to create 11,000 . Of that , $433 million is for new jobs in regional environmental projects , $315 million for bio-security , including weed and pest control , $200 million for the Department of Conservation 's nature fund and $154 million for new jobs enhancing biodiversity on public and private land . <p> " It will enable people who have been displaced in the tourism industry , for example , to use their skills they 've got in working with people and organising logistics in these nature-based jobs , " Associate Environment Minister Eugenie Sage said . <p> " It 's not just killing possums and pulling weeds , but also killing wallabies . " <p> " I am confident we can get thousands of New Zealanders working in nature , safeguarding their livelihood and providing major benefits for nature . " <p> " The Finance Minister talked about Covid-19 being a one in a hundred year threat , but climate change is the threat that will decide if we have another hundred years on this planet . <p> " When he starts allocating that remaining pot of $20b , Mr Robertson and the Government will need to focus on the future and the main threat to our existence - the climate and ecological crises . " <p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw said the funding " will not only help restore our natural landscapes , native bush , waterways and coast , it will give thousands of people access to well-paid work so they can continue to provide for their families " . <p> When asked about the lack of targeted climate change spending in Budget 2020 , Mr Shaw said it would benefit through improving forests . <p> " The $1.1 billion going into restoring out wetlands , restoring our biodiversity will leave healthier forests and will lead to soaking up more carbon dioxide emissions . " <p> Mr Shaw also said improvement to infrastructure could see it built in a way " that reduces our emissions over time and make us more resilient to climate change " . <p> He then said there were more decisions to be made , hinting funding for tackling climate change could be on the way . <p> " Cabinet has n't made a decision about that but that is the direction of travel . <p> " We 've kept some money in the kitty ... but we simply have n't made a set of decisions yet about how we will do that . You 've got to remember this Budget was put together in eight weeks ' time . "
