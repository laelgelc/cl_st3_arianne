<h> ' Green tape ' policies should be put on hold - Simon Bridges <p> National leader Simon Bridges wants the government to put off " green tape " policies in the farming and primary sector . <p> Photo : Pool / Getty Images <p> The comments were in response to a question asked during a presentation to the Otago Chamber of Commerce , on whether the government needed to change some of its policies it wanted to introduce in the primary sector . <p> Bridges said it was one thing to have certain policies in good times and another during a time of deep recession or depression . <p> " Some of the policies around climate change , water , a variety of other areas of green tape I think are going to be unhelpful at this time . <p> " I 'm not suggesting these issues are n't important , they are , but the facts have changed and we need to change what we do . " <p> The focus in the next couple of years needed to be on jobs and growth , he said .
