<h> Harry &amp; Meghan : Timeline of what went right - and what went wrong <p> Prince Harry and Duchess Meghan of Sussex have celebrated their second wedding anniversary this week . <p> It has to be said that few would have predicted on that euphoric day of their nuptials at Windsor Castle that they would be living two years later in pandemic quarantine in Los Angeles , the hometown of the former Meghan Markle . <p> Far away in spirit and space from his royal family , they are locked in bitter legal wrangling with British tabloids while living in the capital of the paparazzi , seeking more " privacy . " They have consciously uncoupled from their royal roles and are looking to begin an independent and lucrative life as a king and queen of Hollywood . <p> Prince Harry , Duke of Sussex and Meghan , Duchess of Sussex at the Commonwealth Day service in March 2020 . <p> They were supposed to be the faces of the " modern monarchy , " the glam couple who would combine his good-guy affect and royal heritage with her beauty , biracial background and celebrity American savvy to transform a fusty family firm into an institution more relatable to all citizens of the United Kingdom . <p> " Their rise was phenomenal : Harry 's choice of an American divorcee who identified as biracial and was an actress and an activist and articulate , showed the monarchy had modernised , " says royal commentator Richard Fitzwilliams . <p> " It also had enormous potential for appeal to the Commonwealth , where most people are under 30 and would identify with a person of colour . " <p> That 's not how it worked out . Depending on point of view , that 's either a tragedy or no big deal , the loss of a priceless opportunity or acknowledgment of unrealistic expectations . <p> Two years after Harry , 35 , and Meghan , 38 , enchanted billions around the world , they , their fans and foes have to ask themselves : Could a 1,000-year-old monarchy really adapt to the woke ways of an American actress ? " <p> The wedding run-up : January-May 2018 : <p> Thanks to frequent interviews , it becomes clear that all is not well between Meghan and her Markle family , none of whom are invited to the wedding . <p> Will her half-siblings keep attacking her in the tabloids ? October 15 , 2018 <p> They announced they were expecting just as they arrived Down Under for their first major overseas tour to Australia , New Zealand , Fiji and Tonga . The baby will be the eighth great-grandchild for the queen and husband Prince Philip , and seventh in line to the throne . <p> The duchess as diva ?
