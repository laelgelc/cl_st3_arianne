Yet right now the average age of this 52nd Parliament is 49 years old , " Swarbrick said before dropping an " OK boomer " when she was heckled by Muller before continuing . <p> A " boomer " is shorthand for a baby boomer - someone born between 1946 and 1964 . <p> While Swarbrick 's " OK boomer " insult went global , the jibe was misdirected - Muller is only 52 years old , making him a Generation X baby . <p> Muller has a political history . <p> He was a staff member of former prime minister Jim Bolger and always touted to be a successful politician , with Bolger backing Muller to take over from Simon Bridges . <p> Muller chose to go into the private sector after working for Bolger . <p> He went to parliament in 2014 after working at Fonterra . <p> Muller took a significant paycut to make a run in Bay of Plenty , a safe National seat . <p> In 2017 , Muller rose up the party ranks and was tasked with holding the climate change portfolio . <p> On Wednesday , Muller confirmed he would contest National 's leadership . <p> Muller sent a letter to all National MPs , advising them of his decision to contest the leadership . An emergency caucus meeting will be held at midday tomorrow . <p> " It is essential that National wins this election , " Muller wrote . <p> " I share the view of the majority of my colleagues that this is not possible under the current leadership . " <p> Muller said the country faced the most enormous challenge since the end of World War II . <p> " Labour has failed to deliver on every measure it has set for itself in Government .
