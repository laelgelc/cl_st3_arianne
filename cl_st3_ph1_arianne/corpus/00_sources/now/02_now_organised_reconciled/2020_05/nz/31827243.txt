<p> The coronavirus alert has reinforced her personal goal to have more members use video technology for meetings , to be more productive , cut back on travel time and reduce emissions . <p> For Hahn , that would eliminate a 180km round trip from her home in Waitomo to Hamilton . <p> She takes over as president from incumbent Andrew McGiven , who completed his three-year term at the end of April . <p> Tom Lee/Stuff <p> The top catchment of the Mangaokewa tributary , at the back of the Hahn 's farm , is QEII covenanted . It has 5.71ha filtering 40ha of dairy land , ground and surface water . The farm has a total of 50.63ha of QEII protected area . <p> More women are now in key leadership roles for Federated Farmers , including national board members Karen Williams and Katie Milne , who is also national president , and Sally Dryland who is co-president for Tararua . <p> But Hahn said she did n't consider her appointment as a milestone for the organisation . <p> " We had more elected women around the board table when I was Waikato dairy chairperson than we do now . <p> " We have some Dairy Industry Award winners who are interested , but they have n't put themselves up for election yet . <p> " Ideally you want 50 per cent diversity on a board but your sex is not criteria on its own , I hope I am here because of my suitability and availability , not because I 'm female . " <p> Hahn said Federated Farmers had three main industry groups but only " a smattering are female " members . <p> " There are plenty of majority-women farm groups and I challenge them to put their best farmers up for election into Feds positions . " <p> Hahn is no stranger to the issues confronting farmers , she spent the past three years working as vice-president and was vice-chairperson of the Waikato Federated Farmers Dairy Industry Group . <p> More recently she was selected as one of 15 dairy farmers to work as climate change ambassadors for Federated Farmers , which is a signatory to He Waka Eke Noa , a document that outlines the primary sector 's commitment to reduce on-farm emissions . <p> " When I joined I had a different environmental viewpoint to the national body . <p> " That has changed as farmers like me have become involved and made a difference in the democratic system that drives our policy and advocacy . " <p> Hahn grew up in Te Kuiti and worked on her parents ' sheep and beef farm . She studied horticulture and office systems earlier in life and later completed a qualification in environmental management " with an ag flavour " . <p> She met her husband Sofus , followed him to Europe where she worked on a pig farm and a dairy farm before returning to New Zealand . <p> She had stints working as a shepherd in Tirau and Tokoroa before the couple returned to Te Kuiti to live with their own family . <p> They converted her parents ' farm to dairy and Hahn 's interest in governance and advocacy began .
