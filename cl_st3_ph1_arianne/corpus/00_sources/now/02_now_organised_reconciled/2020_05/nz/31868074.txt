<h> US pulls funding for WHO over its handling of Covid-19 <p> US President Donald Trump says he is terminating the US relationship with the World Health Organisation ( WHO ) over its handling of the coronavirus , saying it has essentially become a puppet organisation of China . <p> US President Donald Trump Photo : AFP <p> Appearing in the White House Rose Garden , Trump went ahead with repeated threats to eliminate American funding for the group , which amounts to several hundred million dollars a year . <p> Trump said the WHO had failed to make reforms to the organisation that the president had demanded in a 18 May letter to WHO Director-General Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus . That letter had given the WHO 30 days to commit to reforms . <p> At his Rose Garden appearance , Trump tied the WHO closely with China , where the coronavirus originated . <p> He said Chinese officials " ignored their reporting obligations " about the virus to the WHO and pressured the WHO to " mislead the world " when the virus was first discovered by Chinese authorities . <p> " China has total control over the World Health Organisation despite only paying $40 million per year compared to what the United States has been paying which is approximately $450 million a year . We have detailed the reforms that it must make and engaged with them directly but they have refused to act , " said Trump . <p> " Because they have failed to make the requested and greatly needed reforms , we will be today terminating our relationship with the World Health Organisation and redirecting those funds to other worldwide and deserving urgent global public health needs . " <p> Cutting the US contribution could have global implications as the WHO is involved in responding to the pandemic , which has hit dozens of countries . <p> Trump has long questioned the value of the United Nations and scorned the importance of multilateralism as he focuses on an " America First " agenda . Since taking office , Trump has quit the UN Human Rights Council , the UN cultural agency UNESCO , a global accord to tackle climate change and the Iran nuclear deal . <p> The World Health Organisation is a UN specialised agency - an independent international body that works with the United Nations . The WHO did not immediately respond to a request for comment on Trump 's decision . <p> " We have consistently called for all states to support WHO , " said a spokesman for UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres when asked about Trump 's decision . <h> Related Stories <p> The World Health Organisation inquiry into how the COVID-19 virus started may be limited with China not willing to take part in the process .
