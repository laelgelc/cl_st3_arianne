<h> Auckland Council secures overseas market to restart some recycling <p> Recycling of paper and cardboard will resume in Auckland this month , but some plastics will be sent to landfill . <p> Bales of recycling Photo : ( Photo by Bas Emmen on Unsplash ) <p> Auckland Council says it has secured an overseas market for up to 4000 tonnes of mixed paper and cardboard . <p> However , a market has not been secured for plastics numbered 3 to 7 , which will now be separated out after collection and sent to landfill . <p> According to the council , these plastics are more difficult to recycle and represent less than 4 percent of reusable waste material in Auckland . <p> It could take at least three months before adequate markets resume for plastics , while recycling markets for paper and cardboard will be evaluated on a month to month basis . <p> Environment and climate change committee chair Richard Hills has urged residents to keep putting out recycling as normal while a solution was found . <p> " We will make sure we recycle everything we can and take out what we ca n't at our end , " he said . <p> Hill said Auckland has the most extensive recycling collection system in the country and even with the market disruptions , 96 percent of materials including aluminum , steel , glass , paper , and most plastics were being recycled . <p> He said the requirement to send some plastics to landfill highlights the need for New Zealand to deal with our recycling onshore instead of relying on other countries .
