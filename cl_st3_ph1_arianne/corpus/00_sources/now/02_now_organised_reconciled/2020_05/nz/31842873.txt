<h> Connecting southern Auckland 's future communities <h> Have your say on southern transport transformation <p> Aucklanders are invited to have their say on a series of future projects that will improve access and travel choices , connect communities and support growth in southern Auckland . <p> Waka Kotahi NZ Transport Agency and Auckland Transport are seeking community feedback on a range of proposals for public transport , rail upgrades , road improvements , and new walking and cycling networks . <p> In partnership with Auckland Council , Waka Kotahi NZ Transport Agency and Auckland Transport are continuing to progress future transport projects as infrastructure will play a critical role in the economic recovery of New Zealand . <p> Deputy Mayor Cashmore says the $2.4 billion injection from the government 's NZ Upgrade Programme announced in January has helped bring forward some projects to unlock growth in southern Auckland . <p> " Projects like the Mill Road corridor from Manukau to Drury , rail upgrades and new train stations , and improvements to State Highway 1 between Papakura and Drury will provide much-needed infrastructure to assist economic recovery , " he says . <p> " Drury is a significant growth area and the government 's infrastructure package recognises this . It will progress the catch-up legacy work required and build in the futureproofing necessary to enable a connected development in all mobility forms . <p> " We recognise that the way people move around their communities and across our city needs to change . Extending public transport networks and offering travel choices is at the heart of these projects and will help us take action on climate change . <p> " Transport was identified as needing the most significant change in our recent Te Taruke-a-Tawhiri : Auckland 's Climate Action Framework public consultation . These proposals have the potential to see an increase in public transport usage by 35 per cent from southern communities . That is significant . <p> " Through this public engagement process , it 's vital we hear from the community and property owners in these growth areas , " Mr Cashmore says . <h> A major step forward in transport planning <p> The Supporting Growth Programme , a collaboration between Waka Kotahi NZ Transport Agency , Auckland Transport , KiwiRail and mana whenua , is leading the planning process and is now identifying and protecting the land that will be needed for these future projects . <p> The Transport Agency 's Director of Regional Relationships , Steve Mutton says the area is an important gateway from the south to the airport and to the city centre and the Upper North Island . <p> " Today 's proposals are a major step forward in transport planning for southern Auckland . The south is growing fast and over the next 30 years , an additional 120,000 people are expected to live in future communities in Takaanini , Opaheke , Drury , Paerata and Pukekohe . We want to provide residents with more accessible and sustainable travel choices . <p> " The proposals include input from local boards , mana whenua , key stakeholders and communities , and are a great example of working together to resolve transport challenges for the future , " Mr Mutton says . <h> Encouraging a significant shift to public transport and active modes <p> Auckland Transport Chief Executive Shane Ellison says he 's delighted the proposals prioritise new public transport networks as well as road improvements to make roads safer and to ease congestion . He says community feedback will help shape these exciting projects . <p> " We 're working together with the community to deliver safe , innovative , and sustainable transport for southern Auckland . <p> " The community has already told us they 're looking for more public transport , walking and cycling connections so that 's a big part of what we 're planning . <p> " Your views are very important to the project . We encourage you to look at the projects online and have your say . We value your contribution and will carefully consider the feedback you provide , " says Mr Ellison . <h> Find out more and have your say <p> Create the future with us -- we 're planning the future transport solutions for the south of Auckland and we need your help . <p> Community feedback is open from 18 May to 12 June 2020 for these projects :
