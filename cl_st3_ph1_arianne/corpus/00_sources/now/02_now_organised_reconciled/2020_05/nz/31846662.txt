The rules of level two remain in place , however : there will be limited capacities of up to 100 people at a time , and individual groups must be no larger than 10 people . The " three S 's " must also be applied : all groups must be seated , socially distanced from other groups and served by a single server . <p> The current limits on gatherings are due to be reconsidered by the government when cabinet next sits on Monday . Figures collated by the Our World in Data website show it had 6.08 deaths per million inhabitants per day on a rolling seven-day average between May 13 and May 20 . In comparison , UK , Belgium and the US had numbers of 5.57 , 4.28 and 4.11 respectively last week .
