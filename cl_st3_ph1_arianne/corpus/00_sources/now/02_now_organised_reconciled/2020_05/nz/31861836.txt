Muller thinks it 's perfectly OK to keep it on display because , well , you know , the Democrats are making a mess of it as well as Trump . <p> What sort of political judgement is that ? <p> But wait , there 's more . He wrote a book as a youngster where he dreamt of being the US President and reigning for decades . Any megalomaniacal tendencies on display here ? Is that weird or what ? He 's a Kiwi for goodness sake . Does he now secretly entertain fantasies of donning his MAGA hat and joining white supremacists complaining about the lockdown in the US whilst being heavily armed with an AR-15 assault rifle , pistols , knives and ammo ? <p> Just imagine he 's our Prime Minister and a serious foreign policy dispute arises between NZ and the US , just like the nuclear-armed and powered warships standoff of yesteryear . Which side of the fence would he be on ? <p> This is seriously kooky stuff and brings into question his fitness for office . <p> I think Bruce is making too much out of Todd Muller owning a Donald Trump " Make America Great Again " hat . As a National Party leader it is understandable he should be speaking favourably about American politics . Since WW2 , his party has always supported America -- right or wrong . Let 's at least give the man credit for his opposite stance to Trump 's climate change denial . On climate action , working with Climate Change Minister James Shaw , he did lead his party 's collaboration with our Government over the Zero Carbon Bill until he was moved from the climate change portfolio to agriculture . While representing agriculture , although rejecting " peak cow " , he at least blasted the farmer climate change sceptics for their unrealistic take on climate change . I say give the man a chance to prove his salt , and wait until his actions prove him at fault before slam-dunking him . <p> I was interested in finding out more about him because I had heard positive things about him . As soon as I saw THAT hat in the photo and heard he did intend to keep it , even in a box , I switched right off him . To me , that MAGA hat represents the following : Children incarcerated , racism , bigotry , stupidity , disrespect and hatred towards women . I do n't care where he keeps it , the symbolism of valuing such an item says a great deal about him . In years to come , memorabilia such as MAGA hats may be seen to be as repugnant as Nazi symbolism . I am sure there will be New Zealanders who share the views of Mr Muller but I am not one of them !
