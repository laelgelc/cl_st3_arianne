<h> Street View 's 15 favourite Street Views <p> Google Maps turned 15 in February , and the Street View team published a list of their 15 favourite Street Views to celebrate . <p> We 've detailed them below as they offer a wonderfully colourful list of places to explore and learn about -- for children and adults . <p> 1 . Scale Yosemite 's El Capitan with a famous climber <p> Go mountaineering in Yosemite without having to take a 3,000-foot climb up El Capitan . Some people did incur risk to bring you up the wall , but trust us , they 're professionals . <p> 2 . Float through the International Space Station in Space View <p> All but one of our Street View image collections have something in common : gravity . But with the right stuff and a collaboration with NASA , we were able to overcome truly unique obstacles to bring everyone a taste of what it 's like to be an astronaut . <p> 3 . Horse around with us at Mongolia 's Lake Kh ? vsg ? l Ice Festival <p> How do you spend a bone-chilling winter in Mongolia ? By going outside and having a festival on an ancient frozen lake , of course ! We took Street View to the party and hopped on a horse-drawn sleigh for the sweetest of rides across a glacial playground . <p> 4 . Climb the monarch of mountains : Mont Blanc in the Alps <p> If you 're ready for dramatic views of snow-tipped mountain tops , take a journey 16,000 feet up Mont Blanc . We partnered with world-renowned alpine photographers , skiers , mountaineers , climbers and runners to collect the Street View of Western Europe 's highest peak so that you can run on a summit , ice climb up a serac and ski knee-deep in some of the best powder on Earth . <p> 5 . Take in Tokyo 's cherry blossom season <p> Japan 's sakura , or cherry blossom season , is world famous . Take a casual stroll with Street View during Tokyo 's spring time and be sure to look left to catch a glimpse of the famous Zojo-ji Temple , located in the Shibakoen neighborhood near Tokyo Tower . <p> 6 . Bathe in La Sagrada Familia 's lights without the crowds <p> Let your jaw drop as you enter the main chamber of La Sagrada Familia . This might be your only chance to witness the church 's famous tree-like columns , shimmering lights and vertigo-inspiring views without having to join a herd of gawking visitors . <p> 7 . Scuttle with crabby company on Christmas Island <p> Head over to Christmas Island , a remote tropical territory of Australia , to shellebrate the island 's annual red crab migration . Along with a sweeping sunset view , you can see millions of crabs march from the forest and right into the sea . <p> 8 . Stroll through the majestic Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque in Abu Dhabi <p> See architectural styles from Muslim civilisations in one of the world 's largest mosques , the Sheikj Zayed Grand Mosque . Able to accommodate 40,000 worshippers , the mosque has an open-door policy , inviting visitors from around the world to see its intricate domes , reflective pools and mesmerising prayer hall . <p> 9 . Drift with the Ilulissat Icefjord in Greenland <p> Feel the chill as you peer at Greenland 's Ilulissat Icefjord , where a glacial ice cap meets the ocean . Named a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2004 , it 's imagery like this that reminds us both of our planet 's beauty and of how climate change is impacting the natural world . <p> 10 . Meet the world 's only flightless parrot : the endangered kakapo <p> Virtually step into the home of the kakapo and explore prehistoric New Zealand just as it was millions of years before the first boat ever landed on its shores . Join Street View on an exclusive tour of windswept sanctuaries and pristine reserves that are off-limits to visitors . <p> 11 . Cruise through the Amazon on the Rio Negro River <p> With Street View , you can ferry down the Rio Negro River and see images taken across more than 30 miles of remote and diverse shore , forest and village . <p> 12 . Visit the floating homes of the Uros in Lake Titicaca <p> Cultures , countries and climates may differ , but we all have a place to call home . See how the Uros people of Lake Titicaca in Peru make their homes on floating reed islands that entire villages rest on top of . <p> 13 . Take in the Eiffel Tower <p> Whether you suffer from crippling vertigo or just have n't had the chance to see the iconic Eiffel Tower , Street View has you covered .
