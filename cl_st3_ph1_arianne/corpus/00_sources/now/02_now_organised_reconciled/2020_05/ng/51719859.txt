<p> Members of Staff of the West Africa Science Service Centre in Climate Change and Adapted land use , WASCAL , Federal University of Technology , Akure FUTA on Thursday , April 21 , 2020 , visited the Ibule -- Soro community to give out some palliatives to some families to cushion the effect of the lockdown occasioned by the Corona-virus pandemic ravaging the globe . The Director , WASCAL , FUTA , Professor Debo Adeyewa who coordinated the event said the gesture was one of goodwill to one of the communities bordering the university .
