<h> Dharma Rain <p> Buddhistdoor Global is pleased to announce the launch of our latest Buddhist media project : a quarterly e-magazine titled Dharma Rain . Some of you might already be familiar with the name Dharma Rain , which was first launched as a quarterly supplement to the main magazine of the Malaysia-based Kwan Inn Teng Foundation . Changing needs and circumstances have prompted the Buddhistdoor Global editorial team to rebuild Dharma Rain into a digital journal in its own right . <p> The relaunched Dharma Rain is a thematic e-magazine that draws on selected topics covered by the main Buddhistdoor Global website that are of consistent interest to our audience . We are kicking off our first edition with a topic close to all our hearts : the global climate change crisis . <p> You can read the magazine here or try using our IFrame Reader below . You can browse each issue by clicking on the Reader , and download your own copy of the issue . <p> We hope you are all doing well during these difficult and troubling times and are able to enjoy the selection of articles we have prepared for you . Feel free to download and share the magazine with your family and community .
