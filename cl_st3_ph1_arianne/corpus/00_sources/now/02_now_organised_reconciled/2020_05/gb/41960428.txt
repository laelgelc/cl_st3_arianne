<h> Lockdown may be more of a threat to health than Covid-19 <h> Letters <p> I WOULD suggest that the concept of lockdown being imposed by most nations is becoming much more of a threat to the health of the individual than that posed by Covid-19 . <p> The idea that one size fits all is a medieval concept that demands all people , whether sick or not , be subjected to limitation of movement . It has been said that quarantine is when you restrict the movement of sick people , tyranny is when you restrict the movement of healthy people , and stupidity is when you ca n't tell the difference ! <p> It is not the virus per se that is the problem , but a dysfunctional and weakened immune system which overreacts to the virus and thereby causes harm . The solution is to bolster the immune system so that it is healthy enough to cope with the virus . <p> Of course those with a positive diagnosis should be isolated , and treated as an infection risk , but the protocol for dealing with epidemics -- test , trace , treat and isolate -- has not changed much in the last hundred years or so . I watched Elia Kazan 's film Panic on the Streets , which was filmed in 1950 , and although the story deals with the potential spread of pneumonic plague , it is dealt with by the public health official using much the same protocol promoted by the WHO against the current pandemic . <p> It is also extremely detrimental to the mental and physical wellbeing of people to be isolated in their homes . Research shows that when you isolate people they become more vulnerable to obesity , smoking , drinking , heart disease etc . Social isolation increases inflammatory compounds in the body and decreases anti-viral compounds , so that the person becomes more susceptible to infection due to the stress they are under . Therefore , under the rubric of social distancing and lockdown , we are harming the population , not protecting it . <p> A strong immune system is the key to dealing with the virus . We were told that the lockdown was enforced to protect the elderly and most vulnerable in our community , but as is now apparent from the available data , the virus has already spread throughout the care home sector , and I would suggest that the lockdown came far too late for it to be effective with no system of testing and tracing in place . <p> Also of particular note is the report which highlights the link between air pollution and Covid-19 . Research shows that almost 80% of deaths across four countries in Europe were in the most polluted regions . One does not need to be a medical genius to understand that poor air quality weakens the immune system and makes the individual much more susceptible to a respiratory illness like Covid-19 . <p> On the other hand , our overreaction to Covid-19 has shown how quickly and easily we can pull the plug on our polluting activities when faced with the existential threat of the virus . Let us hope that we can continue to do the same when faced with the very real threat of climate change and the environmental challenges which lie ahead of us . <p> Solomon Steinbett Glasgow <p> DAVID Pratt 's very moving tribute to Denis Goldberg ( Farewell to the giant who fought injustice all his life and who held Scotland dear , May 1 ) reminds us of unsung heroes who should not be forgotten .
