<h> Eco-friendly shop owner 's high hopes for the future <h> Eco-friendly shop owner 's high hopes for the future <p> A NEW Brampton-based shop has opened online with the aim of making it easier to live an environmentally-friendly lifestyle . <p> Ria-plenish started life as a stall at the Brampton Market but has now moved online with the market postponed by the lockdown . <p> The online shop 's owner Maria Stundon has high hopes for the eco-friendly business and wants to move it into a physical shop in the future . <p> Maria , 32 , said : " I did n't think I was doing enough as an individual . I wanted to be able to be a platform for information and help others make that change . <p> " With being in a small town , there is n't much option for that kind of thing and it 's great that people are making the small changes , that 's what we need . <p> " If we can do it on a bigger scale as a community , that would definitely be very beneficial . " <p> Maria glided into the sustainable lifestyle at first by initially going vegetarian . She then began to educate herself on sustainability and the environment . <p> " I started to look into the benefits on the environment by not eating meat and with that came the single-use plastic and obviously the climate change movement was kicking off back then , " she said . <p> " I just started to look into more of what our own impact was having on the environment so I decided to make my own little changes , like using reuseable water bottles and bringing bags to the store . " <p> At the moment , she is stocking items that will help people make small changes in their lifestyles to move towards more sustainable living . <p> In the online store there are reusable bowl covers , bamboo toothbrushes , reusable food wraps , and cotton make-up pads to name a few . <p> However , Maria wants to be able to offer more than household essentials , and wants to open a zero-waste refill store . <p> " When you look at the statistics , the amount of waste in the UK is scary , " she said . <p> " When you are only thinking about yourself and thinking ' oh , I am only chucking away that one bottle of washing up liquid every two weeks ' but when you total it up for the whole of the UK and then the whole World , it 's just a massive impact . <p> " To be able to have alternatives for no packaging or reusable packaging is definitely beneficial and the way to go .
