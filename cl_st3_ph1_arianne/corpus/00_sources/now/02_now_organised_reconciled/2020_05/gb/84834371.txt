<h> The Porto Protocol Foundation is to host weekly talks on climate-change-related issues starting with an online discussion tomorrow on sustainable wine packaging . <p> Described as " onlife " conversations about the world of wine in a changing climate , starting tomorrow is a series of one-to-one talks and roundtables with members of the wine community on sustainable packaging , viticulture and other topics on wine in a changing climate . <p> Kicking off with a round table tomorrow entitled ' The elephant in the room : sustainable packaging in wine ' , the online event will feature Santiago Navarro from Gar ? on Wines , Tiago Moreira da Silva from Baglass and Nicolas Quille from the Crimson Wine Group . <p> The Porto Protocol was launched at a climate change summit in Portugal last year . It is a voluntary pledge which commits its participants to adopt and promote concrete actions , however small , to help reduce the impact of a changing climate .
