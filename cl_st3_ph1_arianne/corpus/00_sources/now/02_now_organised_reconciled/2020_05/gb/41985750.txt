<p> When you have the name James Bond on your CV , everyone assumes you must have a super-cool car history every bit as glamorous as 007 . Well , in Pierce Brosnan 's case , there is some truth to that theory ... at least , there is once he got going . <p> Possibly inspired by his most famous role -- no , not Remington Steele -- Brosnan has driven his fair share of fast cars , automotive " blunt instruments " and Q Branch-approved high-tech machines . He has also had at least one car completely destroyed , although neither Spectre nor Smersh accepted any responsibility . <p> So , as the most charming 007 ( sorry , Roger ) celebrates turning 67 , we thought we would cast a GoldenEye over his car collection , from his first motor to his most recent . As for the future , we do n't know what Pierce will get next ... after all , tomorrow never drives . <h> Ford Anglia ( 1965 ) <p> When it was launched in 1959 , Ford described the Anglia as " the world 's most exciting light car " . And it was n't finished there . " It 's the dream car you 'll learn to love for the thrill of sheer pleasure , " another ad promised . Sounds preposterous , but a generation of car buyers believed it and the Ford Anglia would sell more than a million cars during its production run up to 1968 . Then again , with its Detroit-style tail fins , electric windscreen wipers ( as standard ) and a perfunctory four-speed gearbox , maybe that should n't come as a surprise . It has also appeared in a few TV shows ( including Z-Cars , Heartbeat and was Roland Rat 's daily drive in Rat On The Road -- that 's one for all the " rat fans " out there . Ask your dad ) and movies involving teenage wizards . " The driver 's door was broken , " Brosnan recalls of his very first car , bought from his step-father for ? 50 in 1971 . " You had to sort of hold it up to get in . It 's the same car Harry Potter drove off into the sky with . " Flying cars ... whatever will they think of next ? <h> Aston Martin V12 Vanquish ( 2002 ) <p> In his four outings as 007 , Brosnan drove an Aston Martin DB5 and a few BMWs . Then , in his final film , Bond was given an Aston Martin Vanquish and Pierce fell in love . Introduced by Aston in 2001 as the successor to the Vantage , the first-generation V12 Vanquish was designed by Ian Callum and took its inspiration from the DB4 Zagato of the early 1960s and was considered the 21st century 's first iconic GT . And of course the out-going 007 had to have one . Three months after Brosnan finished shooting Tomorrow Never Dies and having finally hung up his Walther PPK , Aston delivered his bespoke silver Vanquish . " There was simply no other car like it on the road , " Brosnan recalled wistfully . " Sadly , it burnt in a house fire in 2015 . I looked into the garage and the car cover was engulfed in flames . In that nanosecond , you think , ' Do I try to save it ? ' But it 's just a car . You take the blow and move on , give thanks you 're alive . " All that remains are the two plates that read , " Hand-built in England for Pierce Brosnan . " <h> BMW H7 hydrogen ( 2007 ) <p> Brosnan and his second wife , Keely Shaye Smith , are both passionate about the environment and were early adopters of BMW 's H7 . Only 100 were built and the modified 6-litre V12 engine could run on conventional gasoline and liquid hydrogen . When it was launched in 2006 it was capable of 0-60mph in 9.5 seconds , had a top speed of 145mph and a combined range of 435 miles . However , the cars were never sold ; instead , they were loaned to " people of influence " who could spread the eco-word . " I like being a little green -- it helps , " Brosnan said of the H7 in an interview . " Climate change is upon us . The hydrogen cars are pretty amazing . But the fuel is still in its infancy ; they have n't refined it yet . But I think if you are an actor , if you have a voice , if you have some passion or want to do good things , then you should speak up . " BMW stopped making the H7 after a couple of years , so they are seriously valuable . In other words : do be careful with it , 007 ... <h> Range Rover Vogue HSE ( 2014 ) <p> " It is , " remarked Jeremy Clarkson when the new Range Rover was launched in 2013 , " the best car in the world . " When the flagship of the Land Rover range was designed by Gerry McGovern , he did n't just build a stunning 4x4 , but he also created the luxury SUV market . Where the Bentley Bentayga , Rolls-Royce Cullinan and Porsche Cayenne would follow , the Range Rover Vogue had already been there , done it and bought the hand-made monogrammed silk shirt . Why did Brosnan have one ? Because although the world was not enough , having the best car in the world was probably fine . <h> BMW i8 ( 2014 ) <p> Keeping up his eco credentials , Brosnan swapped his hydrogen BMW for a much more efficient , practical and downright sexy plug-in hybrid i8 . This game-changing futuristic sports car was one of the first to make the possibility of driving on pure electric power desirable . OK , so it was n't a pure EV , but go back five years and you try to find a fast-charging power point with which to juice up . It 's hard enough now . So , for Pierce , it made sense to have a petrol engine alongside the electric powertrain and it definitely looked the part , especially at his home in Malibu . He has n't really talked about the i8 , but then again , we did n't expect him to talk . No , Mr Brosnan , we expect you to drive ... <h> Ford F-150 ( 2017 ) <p> Practical , powerful and does n't mind getting its hands dirty -- that could be the description of James Bond , but it also applies to Ford 's legendary F-150 pick-up . With a lineage that can be traced back to 1948 when Ford released the F1 , the F-150 name has been used since 1975 and it is currently in its 13th generation . Not only has it been the best-selling pick-up in the US since 1977 , but it has also been the best-selling vehicle of any kind in the States for more than 40 years . Brosnan uses his F-150 to carry his paddleboards to the beach where he enjoys the surf . He is quite an accomplished boarder , as you 'd expect , so what 's his secret ? Well , as he once said , " I have been known to keep my tip up . " <h> BMW 750i ( 2018 ) <p> Although forever linked with Aston Martin in the movies ( and a Bentley in Ian Fleming 's original novels ) , for a very brief moment in the 1990s -- specifically on Brosnan 's watch -- Bond went all BMW . In GoldenEye he drove a Z3 ( " Ah , Mr Bond ... we have been expecting you for a viewing of 27 Blofeld Gardens " ) , a Z8 in The World Is Not Enough and , most famously , a 750iL in Tomorrow Never Dies ( as well as a BMW R1200 motorbike ) . The 750iL was one of the least likely Bond cars ( unless 007 had swapped the spy game for the chauffeuring business ) , but the clever twist was that it could be driven via remote control and the subsequent car chase in a multi-storey car park in Germany was a hoot . Years later , Brosnan again found himself behind the wheel of an updated 750i , but this time he was in the front seat and he drove it far more sensibly . When it comes to luxury cars , niemand macht es besser.
