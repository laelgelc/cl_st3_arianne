<p> The world we knew before COVID-19 is gone . The one that comes after is still at least partly up to us . <p> The crisis has put entire countries under lockdown , devastated countless businesses , killed hundreds of thousands of people and upended hundreds of millions , if not billions , of lives . <p> The question now : What comes next ? <p> As the world begins to look at what it will be like to live life with the coronavirus -- or the threat of future outbreaks -- it 's already clear that the pandemic has the potential to disrupt industries , accelerate cultural and economic trends , or be used by policymakers or advocates gunning for transformative agendas . <p> POLITICO journalists asked dozens of experts and policymakers what they believe the epidemic will -- or , in their view , should -- change .
