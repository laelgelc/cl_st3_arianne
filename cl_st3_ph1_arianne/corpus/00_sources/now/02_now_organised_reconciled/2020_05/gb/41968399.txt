<p> Marie-Clare Martin , owner of Marie-Clare Cake Designs , said : " Every year we do something extravagant with our cakes for The Scottish Wedding Show , and with the UN Climate Change Conference coming to Glasgow later this year , we wanted to use our platform to draw attention to this global crisis . <p> " Each of the cakes tackles an important issue associated with climate change . Our fire cake acknowledges the terrible Australian bushfires and the sea cake shows how plastic is polluting the oceans and killing our marine wildlife . " <p> Martin follows sustainable business practices , including using locally sourced organic ingredients and incorporating a " no-waste , must-recycle " scheme in the shop . <p> " As a business we 're trying to do our bit to be more environmentally friendly and encourage our customers to be more aware of sustainability , " Martin said .
