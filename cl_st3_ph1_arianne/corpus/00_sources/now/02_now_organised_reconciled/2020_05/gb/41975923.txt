Any property that is rented out to three or more people who are n't related to each other and who share any facilities like a bathroom , toilet or kitchen lives in an HMO . This will include for the first time , hundreds of shared student houses across Bristol . <p> There already are mandatory licences required throughout the country for larger HMOs -- especially in buildings with more than three storeys , or large numbers of people .
