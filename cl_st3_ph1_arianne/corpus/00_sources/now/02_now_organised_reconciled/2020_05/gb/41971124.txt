Grow back stronger <p> Last month set a new UK record for the sunniest April , with an average 224.5 hours of sunshine , beating the previous record of 211.9 hours in April 2015 . <p> We are already seeing the impact of global warming here in the UK as extreme weather becomes the new normal . April was 1.7C above average , accompanied by devastating floods , and quite rightly the public is worried about climate change . <p> I believe we would all back initiatives that protect our futures . Lowering the rates of this greenhouse gas is key to tackling global warming . <p> Angela Terry <p> Make no mistake , this is a one-off opportunity to reset our economies , enabling them to grow back stronger but also stop our contributions to global warming . <p> We have seen two main developments during this pandemic which hold the key to tackling global warming : Huge reductions in carbon-dioxide emissions and people changing deeply ingrained habits -- quickly -- bringing benefits to our health , nature and finances . <p> As we stay at home , the lower rates of road and air travel have meant emissions of carbon dioxide have reduced massively around the world . Lowering the rates of this greenhouse gas is key to tackling global warming . <p> By the end of March , global road transport activity was almost half of 2019 's average and demand for jet fuel is down 65 per cent year-on-year to April , according to the IEA International Energy Agency . <p> Not only are we creating less pollution , we are also discovering healthier modes of transport . We are walking and cycling more . Our bike shops are running out of stock as we get on two wheels -- to exercise during lockdown but also to prepare for the post-lockdown world . Its president Edmund King has suggested the funds would be better invested in improving broadband . <p> Individuals and businesses have learned how to operate remotely and from people 's homes , again reducing congestion and air pollution . The benefits to our health and environment will be enormous if businesses and workers opt to work from home even after the pandemic passes . <p> People are remembering , noticing and becoming more aware that what matters most is their health , their families , friendships and the environment . The constant pursuit of the latest model of car , phone , or jacket seems less important . <p> Despite the global reductions in carbon emissions we can not be complacent . Our recovery must have a green focus . The carbon emission drop that followed the 2009 recession was followed by a sharp rise of almost six per cent the following year .
