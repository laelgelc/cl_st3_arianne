<h> Martin Garrix 's parents are his number one fans <p> Each week , we ask one act a set of quick-fire questions that all start with ' the first ' , from their first show and paycheque to the first time they got drunk . Up next is superstar DJ Martin Garrix , who released his latest single , ' Higher Ground ' , yesterday <p> Martin Garrix should be gearing up for the Euros next month , prepping for the role of a lifetime as the tournament 's official artist . Instead , the Dutch DJ has , like the rest of us , been holed up at home , in Amsterdam , playing virtual sets from locations as varied as his rooftop ( and even a river ) and celebrating his 24th birthday yesterday with the release of his new single , " Higher Ground " , featuring John Martin . <p> While Garrix , who has performed everywhere from the 2018 Winter Olympics to Coachella , might not be able to play the track in front of live audiences anytime soon , it 's still certain to make the rounds online . This is , after all , the three-time winner of Best DJ In The World , with 25.5 million monthly listeners on Spotify alone and collaborations with the likes of Dua Lipa and Khalid under his belt . Garrix 's debut single , " Animals " , which , in 2013 , turned him into a teenage superstar virtually overnight , is now on 1.3 billion streams on YouTube and has become one of the most iconic EDM tracks of all time . <p> In honour of his latest release , we caught up with Garrix to go back to his very beginnings , from his very first show to the best bit of advice his mentor Ti ? sto ever gave him . <p> The first time you realised you wanted to be a musician ... <p> I never really had a moment when I realised I wanted to be a professional musician , I just enjoyed making music so much and always had a lot of fun doing it ! Seeing how my music can lift spirits up is crazy . I did n't think of this when I started making music , I was just focusing on getting all the ideas I had in my head into productions , but seeing how these ideas can affect people is amazing . <p> The first time you played a set in front of a live audience ... <p> I started playing sets in front of a live audience when I was nine or ten years old . Some of my first gigs were me DJing at weddings . <p> The first time you met a fan ... <p> My first fans were actually my parents . Even though my first songs were pretty bad , they always loved them because they saw how proud I was of the songs . They have always supported me and still do . <p> The first record you ever bought ... <p> Parade Of The Athletes by Ti ? sto . The trance music was so powerful -- it gave me so much energy , happiness and euphoria . It was a true eye opener for me and that 's when I fell in love with electronic music . <p> The first time you made money out of being a musician ... <p> When I started I would literally DJ at someone 's wedding for ? 15 or ? 20 . Then I would save all that money and use it to buy myself an upgrade from the mixer that I had or new equipment -- I was constantly trying to grow as DJ Marty . <p> The first time you heard a song you wished you 'd produced ... <p> " Levels " by Avicii . I have so many beautiful memories to that song . I still get goosebumps when I hear it , it 's timeless . <p> The first time you realised you were actually any good ... <p> I had no clue , actually , it just happened all of a sudden . I was making all my music in my bedroom at my parents ' house and when I made " Animals " I had no idea what was about to happen . Then the songs started to be played worldwide and it was all very unreal for me . But when I looked at the numbers , statistics and amount of people at the shows , I started to realise that what was happening was absolutely crazy . <p> The first time you were given any really excellent advice ... <p> When I felt very down by negative comments . I would read 100 comments , 99 of them would be good , one would be bad and I would focus on the only negative one . I would let it ruin my day . I could n't understand why someone would say something like that because I was just trying to spread good vibes and no one was forced to listen to my music if they did n't like it . Then the best advice I got was from Ti ? sto : he told me , " Why are you focusing on that one person who is not going to like you anyway ? Focus on the 99 people you made happy -- that 's so much more beautiful and powerful . Do n't even waste one second of your time thinking of the guy that does n't like your music . You make music for yourself and for the people who want to listen to it . " I was 16 when he said this and it really meant a lot to me -- it still does . <p> The first thing you 'd ask Kanye ... <p> I would love to have a 12-hour long conversation about life , his vision on so many different things with him . That would be crazy . I think he 's a genius . <p> The first thing you 'd do if you became prime minister ... <p> I would really focus on climate change and the climate crisis . I would present the facts to people every day and try to initiate a huge change . <p> The first time you fell properly in love ... <p> Definitely with music .
