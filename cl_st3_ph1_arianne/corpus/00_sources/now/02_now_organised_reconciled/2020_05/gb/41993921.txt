<p> The coronavirus lockdown has highlighted the " critical role " plants play in health and wellbeing , the Prince of Wales has said in a message for the virtual Chelsea Flower Show . <p> Many people have reconnected with nature at home , gained a heightened appreciation of their local green spaces and discovered a new love of gardening and food production , Charles said . <p> The heir to the throne , who is a long-time advocate of climate action , also said that healthy plants and trees are " vitally important " in mitigating climate change by storing carbon . <p> But just as the world has realised the importance of plants , " we have managed to engineer a global plant health crisis " , he warned . <p> And he urged visitors to the online Chelsea Flower Show to take simple actions themselves to prevent the spread of plant pests and diseases . <p> He said : " In recent weeks the coronavirus lockdown has highlighted the critical role plants play in our own health and wellbeing and quite a few of us will have had the opportunity to reconnect with nature at home . <p> " I know many people have discovered a heightened appreciation for their local green space and a new-found love for gardening and food produce , " which he said is a " welcome outcome from this desperate situation " . <p> " I also know I 'm not alone in feeling plants and trees play an utterly valuable and indispensable role in our lives . <p> " And , in the midst of this tragic health crisis , it 's easy to forget the worsening climate crisis , the catastrophic effects of which have been dramatically felt in recent months , with floods and fires wreaking havoc on communities around the globe . " <p> In the online video message , he said many governments had unveiled plans for tree planting and restoring wetlands to store carbon emissions . <p> " From every angle plants play an absolutely vital role in our existence , but the ultimate irony is , just as we are realising this , we have also managed to engineer a global plant health crisis , " he said . <p> He pointed to the loss of millions of elm trees , the spread of ash dieback , increasing awareness of threats to oaks and xylella threatening herbaceous plants . <p> And he said : " Our current situation shows just how quickly diseases and pathogens of all types are spread around the planet due to the hyper-connected nature of today 's world , and with such tragic effect . " <p> Speaking during the international year of plant health , he said greater caution and vigilance are needed to ensure pests and diseases do not spread . <p> And gardeners can take their own actions to protect plant life , he added . <p> These include cleaning their tools , buying plants from reputable British growers and which have ideally been grown here , resisting the urge to buy plants while travelling abroad , and reporting anything unusual in their own garden . <p> -- Charles 's video message forms part of the virtual Chelsea Flower Show , taking place online this week in place of the physical event which was cancelled due to the pandemic . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
