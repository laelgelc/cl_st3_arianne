<h> A post Brexit mechanism to support farmers and landowners to demonstrate the added value an AONB Designation can bring . <p> England 's 34 AONBs cover nearly 7,000 square miles of countryside . From the open moorlands of the North Pennines , Nidderdale and the Forest of Bowland , through the undulating grasslands of the Chilterns and Cotswolds to the south west peninsula of Cornwall with its diverse and distinct character of high bleak moorland and wild , rugged cliffs to sheltered coves and quiet country lanes bounded by the characteristic Cornish hedges . The twelve sections that make up the Cornwall AONB consist of 10 stretches of coastline including the hinterland of the Lizard and Roseland peninsulas as well as the inland areas of the Camel Estuary and Bodmin Moor . <p> Farming landscape in Cornwall AONB - at Gweek , Helston <p> Each AONB is the product of farming practices and its value to the whole nation is largely dependent on sympathetic future land management practices . It is a legal requirement that public bodies have regard to the purpose of AONB designation ( S85 - Countryside and Rights of Way Act 2000 ) , and any future land management scheme must therefore do likewise . <p> The Cornwall AONB was one of 12 AONBs throughout England to be selected to take part in the Defra Test Trials for the new Environmental Land Management scheme ( ELMs ) . Cornwall AONB chose the Lizard peninsula for its test trial area as it covers a manageable geographical area to test and trial how objectives can be set for the provision of public goods* in a rural farming landscape whilst also demonstrating how farmers , land owners and land managers can work collaboratively to deliver the objectives of the ELMs . <p> The Cornwall AONB Test Trial will examine how the ELMs , based on a fuller understanding of natural capital and ecosystem services of the Lizard , can make a step change for biodiversity and heritage , whilst also delivering multiple benefits for local communities . As a result , the Lizard peninsula will be able to deliver more effectively on biodiversity , clean air , clean water , natural beauty , engagement and mitigation of climate change . <p> The Test Trial will primarily deliver on Chapter 1 of the Government 's 25-year Environment Plan to improve the environment by utilising and managing land on the Lizard more effectively whilst ensuring farm businesses are sustainable . The Test Trial runs from February 2020 to March 2021 and Cornwall AONB has brought in a specialist team of consultants to help deliver the Test Trial for the Lizard consisting of ; <h> Farming and wildlife group south west <p> The Farming and Wildlife Advisory Group South West ( FWAG ) who are best placed to facilitate the activities of the ELMs ( a registered charity representing the region 's farmers and landowners in the delivery of wildlife conservation ) and who have already carried out considerable work in building relationships with farmers and land owners/managers on the Lizard . <h> Gain Consulting <p> Gain Consulting - a Landscape and Environmental Consultancy based in Cornwall with specialist knowledge of natural capital who will establish a comprehensive evidence base necessary to assemble spatial frameworks using Geographical Information Systems as well as producing and publishing a Natural Capital Prospectus and Landscape Scale Management Plan which in turn will be linked to the new Cornwall AONB Management Plan . Further information about the work Gain will be delivering on behalf of Cornwall AONB . <h> University of Exeter Consulting Ltd ( UOE Consulting Ltd ) <p> An individual Farm Action Plan and Farm Audit report on the transition of farmers from the existing agri-environment schemes to the new ELMS will be produced by University of Exeter Consulting Limited ( Environment Sustainability Institute -- ESI ) . Since 2016 the Cornwall AONB has worked closely with the ESI on the ' Farms for AONBees ' project which has specifically targeted farmers on the Lizard to help increase their crop pollination . The key aim of ' Farms for AONBees ' is to enhance pollinators and pollination across outstanding landscapes , finding management solutions that benefit both bees and businesses . <h> What 's next <p> In 2021 , Defra will be delivering a National Pilot with a range of delivery partners who will work together as one team to deliver the Pilot , these are ; <p> The Environment Agency <p> Forestry Commission <p> Joint Nature Conservation Committee <p> Natural England <p> Rural Payments Agency . <p> The National Pilot will test a high level three tier scheme designed in preparation for the delivery of the new Environment Land Management scheme . The aim of the Pilot is to learn and innovate prior to full roll-out of the new scheme and to build confidence in delivery amongst stakeholders .
