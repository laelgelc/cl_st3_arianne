<h> Aurivo report ? 446.8m turnover in 2019 <p> AURIVO have announced full-year financial results for the year ended December 31 , 2019 . <p> In another year of volatility in global dairy markets , Aurivo 's turnover for the year was ? 446.8m - an increase of 1% ( 2018 : ? 443.8m ) . Aurivo reported a 5% improvement in group operating profit ( before exceptional items ) to ? 3.2m ( 2018 : ? 3.0m ) . <p> The group 's five-year investment programme is well underway , with ? 37m of the ? 48m budgeted investment programme completed and installed , ensuring that Aurivo is well-positioned to take advantage of future market opportunities and continued growth in milk supply . <p> Aurivo 's livestock marts recorded a decline of 2% in cattle throughput , though Balla Mart had an increase of some 3% in the number of cattle going through the ring there . <p> Operational highlights include : <p> The completion of three significant capital investment projects in the dairy Ingredients plant , animal feed mill and liquid milk processing site with a cumulative investment over the past three years of ? 37m . <p> Some 459m litres of milk was processed -- a 4.5% growth on 2018 . <p> Two new fixed milk price schemes were introduced in 2019 , bringing the number of fixed milk price schemes to six . <p> A once off exceptional cost of ? 6.4m included an impairment charge of ? 5.96m on goodwill in the For Goodness Shakes brand , restructuring costs in the business of ? 1.3m and settlement gains on defined benefit pension of ? 868,000 . <p> Given the high level of capex in recent years , nett debt increased to ? 18.8m ( 2018 : ? 14.6m ) . <p> Average price paid for milk in 2019 was 34.07cpl . <p> Commenting , Donal Tierney , who was appointed chief executive of Aurivo last January , said : " As we near the halfway point of our 2022 strategy , Aurivo progressed from the planning and building stage to the implementation and realisation of significant capital investment in the co-operative . During 2019 , we completed three significant capital investment projects -- our new ? 26m , five-tonne dryer at our dairy ingredients site , a ? 6m investment in front-end processing at our liquid milk plant , and a ? 1.2m investment in storage and out-loading facilities at our animal feed mill . The strategic objectives of the Engage 2022 strategy continue to resonate throughout the business and underpin our growth . <p> " Through cost efficiencies , we will ensure lower operational costs and eliminate all waste . We intend to invest further in the latest technology to provide enhanced data and insights , which will guide our decision-making and help us concentrate on areas of growth and development . " <p> He continued : " The political volatility that prevailed in 2019 , coupled with the Covid-19 pandemic in 2020 , has brought unprecedented challenges to our business and the economy . For over 120 years we have made every effort to improve the viability of local farming communities and create employment . It is this community ethos along with Aurivo 's proven track record that will see us through this challenging time and enable us to continue to serve our members and communities of the future . " <p> Progress in 2019 is outlined as follows across Aurivo 's businesses : <p> Consumer Foods <p> Turnover in consumer foods decreased marginally in 2019 to ? 95.1m ( 2018 : ? 98.8m ) , due to a combination of lower market prices for cream and a slight reduction in private label volume . <p> Aurivo continued to develop its milk brands , Connacht Gold and Donegal Creameries , and launched 11 new product lines across key retailers in the liquid milk and spreadable butter categories . <p> The group invested ? 6m in its liquid milk plant in Killygordon to increase front-end processing capacity and significantly improve energy efficiency , and ? 0.5m in a new distribution hub for milk and butter in Achonry . <p> The For Goodness Shakes brand retains its position as the leading ready to drink sports nutrition brand in the UK market . However , the loss of a key customer and competition from alternative brands has resulted in profit erosion of the brand , which has resulted in an impairment charge of ? 5.96m in 2019 . For Goodness Shakes remains a very profitable and valuable brand . <p> Dairy ingredients <p> 2019 saw an increase in market returns for protein products but a gradual reduction in pricing for butter . The nett effect of this coupled with the growth in milk volume of 4.5% led to an increase in dairy ingredients turnover growing to ? 169.2m ( 2018 : ? 153.4m ) . The Forto brand of enriched milk powder ( EMP ) has grown by 38% since its launch in 2017 . <p> July 2019 marked the completion of a new five-tonne spray dryer in Ballaghaderreen , increasing capacity by 55% , which was the result of a ? 26m investment in dairy ingredients . <p> Ireland is one of the world 's most carbon efficient dairy producing countries and Aurivo believes the opportunities for Irish dairy in global markets are far reaching . <p> The theme of climate change and sustainability is embedded in dairy ingredients . Aurivo is the only dairy processing co-op in Ireland using woodchip and bulk liquid natural gas ( LNG ) as an energy source to process milk . <p> Agri business <p> Aurivo 's Agribusiness reported turnover of ? 115m , although a 5% reduction on 2018 ( ? 120.7m ) . This was a very satisfactory performance and ahead of budget . <p> Feed and fertiliser tonnage were both on budget as volumes returned to more ' normal ' levels in 2019 , following the exceptional weather-related trading volumes experienced in 2018 . <p> The Homeland and Nutrias brands continued to grow , with a new pet food range , along with organic animal feed , brought to the market . <p> The Homeland.ie website has successfully established an online presence and , coupled with the launch of the click-and-collect service , brings enhanced convenience to customers . <p> Marts <p> Aurivo livestock marts recorded a decline of 2% in cattle throughput for the year , which was in keeping with trends for the region . Balla Mart , however , did show an increase of some 3% in cattle throughput . <p> Average cattle prices were back on average by ? 31 per head . <p> Some 60,000 sheep were sold in the year , marginally back on last year and with prices on a par with 2018 . <p> The reduction in the suckler herd along with an emphasis on a younger age profile for slaughter-fit cattle is driving a reduction in availability for all livestock marts . This resulted in a 5% decrease in turnover to ? 67.5m ( 2018 : ? 71.0m ) . <p> Health and safety at facilities remains a key focus as the group strive for zero notifiable accidents or incidents .
