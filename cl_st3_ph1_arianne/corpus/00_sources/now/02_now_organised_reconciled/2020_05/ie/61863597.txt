<h> Trinity Study Finds 75% of Workers Fear Catching Virus on Commute <h> The study , carried out by College 's School of Engineering , found almost 80 per cent of participants would prefer to work from home . <p> S ? r ? n FogartyAssistant News Editor <p> Some 75 per cent of workers in Ireland are concerned about contracting coronavirus if they use public transport to commute to work , according to Trinity researchers . <p> A survey conducted in Trinity 's School of Engineering has revealed a number of significant shifts in attitudes towards work among Ireland 's labour force . <p> In 2019 , approximately 25 per cent of people said they would be strongly in favour of working from home one or two days each week . In 2020 , almost 80 per cent of participants indicated they would prefer to work from home once the coronavirus restrictions are lifted . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> In both years , the reasons cited for wishing to work from home were to save time and reduce costs . The 2019 results showed that on average , 0.15 tonnes of carbon could be saved every year if employees worked from home one day a week . <p> The survey also asked participants how their method of commuting would change once the coronavirus restrictions are lifted . Some 25 per cent of people said they would commute via public transport less often . Over three in 10 -- 32 per cent -- said they would walk to work more often . <p> Some three quarters of respondents indicated that they were concerned about contracting the coronavirus while traveling by public transport and the vast majority felt driving alone , walking and cycling would result in a much lower risk of contracting coronavirus compared to using public transport . <p> The survey also examined changing attitudes to working from home . <p> In a press statement , Prof Brian Caulfield , who led the survey , said : " The current results from the study show that between the two survey periods working from home has become much more acceptable and that in a post COVID-19 world this might become the norm . " <p> " The findings related to public transport echo those that have been reported internationally and demonstrate the concerns people have about using public transport " , Caulfield said . <p> " One of the positive findings is the potential shift towards working from home , which is shown to provide both personal benefits related to travel time saved but also emissions reductions contributing to our climate change targets .
