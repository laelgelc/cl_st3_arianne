Terence Kearley , Third Viscount Devonport thinks that building a giant Elizabeth Landmark in Northumberland will fire up tourism in the region . And despite not knowing the cost or seeking planning permission , the ardent royalist has already commissioned designs from three artists -- Simon Hitchens , Colin Rose and Peter Evans -- to produce a work that ' will celebrate our Queen as a figurehead and a leader for many , many hundreds of years to come .
