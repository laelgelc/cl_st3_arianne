<h> Consortium established to build ' power-to-methanol ' plant <p> A consortium of seven players has been established to build a ' power-to-methanol ' demonstration plant in the port of Antwerp . <p> Another important step forward in the transition to alternative energy sources and a sustainable , low-carbon circular port , the project comprises Engie , Fluxys , Indaver , Inovyn , Oiltanking , Port of Antwerp and the PMV -- investment company of the Flemish Government . <p> Construction of a demonstration plant at the Inovyn site in Antwerp is scheduled to start by 2022 , with the aim of producing 8,000 tonnes of sustainable methanol annually , avoiding at least an equivalent volume of CO2 emissions . <p> Methanol is an essential multi-purpose raw material for the chemical industry , with many applications in wider industry as well . <p> This key raw material is indispensable to the daily operation of the port of Antwerp as the largest European integrated energy and chemical cluster in the region . <p> Currently , methanol is produced using fossil raw materials . The ' power-to-methanol ' project aims to replace this with sustainably produced methanol , a first for Belgium . <p> Source : Indaver <p> This methanol will be produced from captured CO2 and sustainably generated hydrogen . <p> The carbon dioxide is captured by means of carbon capture and utilisation ( CCU ) , through which CO2 emissions are partially recovered and then combined with hydrogen generated on the basis of green energy produced from renewable electricity . <p> Port alderman Annick De Ridder said , " Our future prosperity will be sustainable , or there will be no prosperity . Innovation is key to tackling climate change . " <p> " In Flanders we have a tradition of innovative entrepreneurship and as Port of Antwerp we play a pioneering role in serving as a testbed for technological and sustainable innovation . " <p> " We combine this innovation with our strengths as a multi-industry port platform to promote promising applications such as CCU and hydrogen . " <p> " In the meantime it gives a strong signal that Port of Antwerp is keeping to its transition agenda and working hard towards it , even during the Covid-19 crisis .
