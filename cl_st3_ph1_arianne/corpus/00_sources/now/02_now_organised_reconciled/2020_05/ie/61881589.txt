<p> The potential of transforming the West Offaly and Lough Ree power plants into " an energy hub " is one of the key recommendations to emerge from the ' just transition ' commissioner 's first progress report . <p> Kieran Mulvey 's report -- which has been published by the Department of Communications , Climate Action and the Environment -- sets out a number of recommendations to guide the midlands response to the region 's accelerated exit from the peat industry -- 15 years earlier than promised under Bord Na M ? na 's initial decarbonisation plan in 2015 . <p> As it stands , the ESB peat-firing power stations ( supplied with peat from Bord na M ? na ) -- in Shannonbridge , Co . Offaly and Lanesborough , Co . Longford -- will officially shutdown at the end of 2020 . Subsequent plans to demolish the plants at the end of the year were also previously confirmed . The closure of the peat industry for electricity generation is expected to impact on 4,000 jobs -- both directly and indirectly -- throughout the region . <p> However , Mulvey 's report -- which makes a number of " practical " recommendations across a number of areas -- now suggests that perhaps the infrastructure of the plants could be diversified towards an alternative use . <p> Some of the key recommendations of the report include : <p> Governance arrangements : The report notes the importance of effective governance in the midlands to deliver a ' just transition ' -- which will be done in the context of the detailed implementation plan ; <p> Use of state agency lands in the midlands for green energy projects : The department will write to state agencies and enterprises to identify land and facilities which could be of use for such projects ; <p> Study on energy hub in the midlands : The department and ESB will commission a study to examine the potential for using the existing infrastructure in the West Offaly and Lough Ree power plants as an " energy hub " in the midlands ; <p> Electric vehicle ( EV ) charging : The Minister for Communications , Climate Action and the Environment , Richard Bruton , has asked for an evaluation study on the potential to further expand the EV charging infrastructure in the midlands region ; <p> Digital hubs for remote working : Under the National Broadband Plan , 91 broadband connection points will be provided in the midlands over the coming months . It is planned to grow the existing midlands network of co-working facilities , through development of additional facilities/hubs through the region . <p> To coincide with the publication of the report , Minister Bruton has launched the first call for proposals under the newly-opened ? 11 million just transition fund . <p> Some of the key priorities for the fund include the retraining of workers -- many of whom have worked at Bord na M ? na and ESB for generations . The proposals are aimed at generating sustainable employment in the so-called area of " green enterprise " in the region . <p> Stage one of accessing the fund is to register project details with the Midlands Regional Transition Team and START ( Secretariat Technical Assistance to Regions in Transition ) . <h> ' Urgent action ' <p> The minister has asked that " urgent action " be taken on foot of the report 's publication . It is understood that a detailed implementation plan will now also be completed -- facilitated by a working group chaired by the Department of the Taoiseach . <p> However , certain " high-priority measures " are due to be implemented before the completion of the implementation plan . <p> Meanwhile , the minister has also provided an update on progress made in other key areas of the ' just transition ' plan for the eight midlands counties . <p> The following updates have been supplied by the department : <p> Midlands retrofitting scheme : The scheme design has been completed and final survey work and procurement of the build programme will commence when public health advice permits ; <p> Retrofit training : A retrofit training programme was officially launched at Mount Lucas , Co . Offaly on March 11 , it provides access to retraining to former Bord na M ? na employees ; <p> EU Green Deal : Ireland is engaging with the European Union to secure an investment in the midlands under the ' just transition ' mechanism as part of the new Green Deal ; <p> Bog rehabilitation : The ? 5 million scheme approved by Government in Budget 2020 for bog rehabilitation has been rolled out by the National Parks and Wildlife Service ( NPWS ) . This scheme will rehabilitate 1,800ha on nine raised bog protected sites across seven counties in the midlands . Restoration works will commence as soon as public health advice allows in 2020 . An extensive programme to rehabilitate Bord na M ? na bogs used for harvesting peat for electricity generation to a high standard is also in the works . An enhanced peatland rehabilitation traineeship , focused on those employees who may be redeployed by Bord Na M ? na in peatland **26;516;TOOLONG projects over the next three to four years is also being developed . It is intended that 14-16 Bord Na M ? na employees will begin a traineeship programme each month during 2020 and 2021 ; <p> ' Centre for Climate Change and Just Transition ' : Bord na M ? na has been working with a number of partners to progress the establishment of a ' Peatlands Centre of Excellence ' which would focus on the role peatlands can play in climate mitigation and adaptation . In addition , there will be a strong focus on the ' just transition ' in supporting communities and SMEs small and medium-sized enterprises in developing low-carbon , sustainable and circular economy enterprises . These developments will be complemented by a visitors centre that promotes climate action . Funding has been applied for under the EU LIFE Programme . A number of Government departments and agencies have also committed resources to the project . <p> As outlined earlier , the publication of the report 's recommendations comes at a very challenging time for peat industry workers in the region . Last April , Bord na M ? na confirmed the temporary layoff of up to 230 employees from the semi-state company 's Energy Business Unit , due to the impact of Covid-19 outbreak . <p> On top of this , Lough Ree power station temporarily ceased electricity generation on April 14 to ensure compliance with its environmental licence obligations . ESB said it continues to monitor the river conditions closely and said that the plant will return to service once it is appropriate . <p> Advertisement <p> Meanwhile , the situation has been further compounded and complicated by a recent High Court ruling that has impeded the company from progressing with its last peat harvest this summer -- which is having a significant knock-on effect on the horticulture and turf-cutting sectors also . <p> Further developments on the company 's bid to secure planning permission to extract peat off its bogs -- covering a landmass of over 30ha -- are expected in the coming weeks . <h> Impact of early exit <p> Responding to the publication of the report Minister Bruton said he is " acutely aware " of the impact the early exit from peat is having on workers , their families and on the midlands more broadly . <p> " Like many other businesses , Bord na M ? na has also been severely impacted by the Covid-19 pandemic which is compounding this impact . <p> Securing sustainable employment opportunities for the region and a just transition for those most severely affected must be at the heart of our response . <p> " The transition fund is opening , so we can get money to those who can make a big difference , " the minister said . <p> Kieran Mulvey -- the former Labour Relations Commission CEO was appointed to the role of ' just transition ' commissioner last November . He has engaged with those affected by the looming closure of the industry during the intervening months . <p> These stakeholders include : Bord na M ? na and ESB ; relevant trade unions and worker representatives ; local community organisations ; relevant structures already established , in particular the Regional Transition Team and the Regional Enterprise Plan Steering Committee ; the Regional Skills Forum ; local authorities , public representatives and relevant state departments and agencies ; the National Economic and Social Council , which is currently undertaking actions on ' just transition ' as part of the Climate Action Plan and also as part of Future Jobs Ireland 2019 ; plus the EU platform for coal regions in transition . <p> Mulvey described the engagement to date as " very positive " , adding that " it is vital " now that the process can " move rapidly " to an implementation plan . <p> I have adopted a ' whole of midlands ' strategic approach with , what I believe , are practical actions across counties to maximise their infrastructural , natural and human resources and building on their existing strengths . <p> " The first part of such a plan is to receive and evaluate employment projects in the midlands that have green enterprise and other employment/tourism potential . <p> " This period has to be used also to finalise the critical pathways for the retention of employment in BNM/ESB , give certainty around the future structure of the workforce and to establish further employment opportunities in the region . <p> " I look forward to continuing to work locally with all the parties involved to achieve and deliver solid and recognisable community gains and to promote the midlands and its potential , " Mulvey concluded . <p> According to the department , the accelerated exit from peat in the midlands region will mean that up to 1.25 million tonnes of carbon emissions will be saved each year and that overall emissions will reduce by up to nine million tonnes by 2027.
