It will be translated by Sophie Collins and published on the Daunt Originals list . <p> According to the synopsis : " The Opposite of a Person is a bold and original novel about climate change and care , which has been compared to Jenny Offill and Rachel Cusk . Ida , a young climatologist , embarks on an internship at an institute tasked with demolishing a dam in the Italian Alps . The job forces her to leave her country and her girlfriend behind , causing her to reflect on climate change , love , and why we find it so hard to protect the things we value the most . Exploring how the individual relates to the complexity of the modern world , the novel mixes fiction with poetry and the essay form .
