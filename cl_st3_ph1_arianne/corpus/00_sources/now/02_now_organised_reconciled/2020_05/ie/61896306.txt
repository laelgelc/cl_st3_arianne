<h> SSE Airtricity , Dublin Zoo launch Eco Explorers Club for kids <p> SSE Airtricity , proud sustainability partner to Dublin Zoo , has launched The Eco Explorers Club -- a digital resource platform to inspire , entertain and educate primary school children on the wonders of the natural world . <p> The Eco Explorers Club is a 10-week course created with the Dublin Zoo Discovery and Learning Team , that will explore five educational topics related to sustainability and the natural world including biodiversity , climate change and pollution . This will help children to continue on their journey to becoming environmental champions of the future , while keeping them connected to Ireland 's favourite family destination , Dublin Zoo . <p> The Eco Explorers Club will launch a series of animated educational videos alongside videos and lessons created by The Eco Explorers Cub ambassadors , including much loved artist and environmentalist Don Conroy . In addition , Reuben the Entertainer will be joined by SSE Airtricity 's Head of Sustainability Mia Fahey-McCarthy for storytelling sessions . <p> Children 's yoga instructor Caragh Egan will deliver yoga exploration classes , enabling children to explore yoga poses related to nature and animals , as well as some relaxation and imagination time . Virtual Tours of Dublin Zoo will also be available , so kids can go behind the scenes at Dublin Zoo , home to more than 400 rare , exotic , and endangered animals from around the world . <p> The range of resources , created together with Dublin Zoo 's Discovery and Learning team , is available on The Eco Explorers Club hub at **34;4261;TOOLONG . <p> Head of discovery &amp; learning at Dublin Zoo , Aileen Tennant said : " We 're really excited to launch The Eco Explorers Club together with our sustainability partner SSE Airtricity . The platform allows us to reach young nature lovers while Dublin Zoo and schools are closed , encouraging a love and understanding of our natural world , as well as promoting the small steps we can all take to make big differences for our environment and the species we share our planet with . " <p> The Eco Explorers Club is available to everyone for free at **34;4297;TOOLONG with new resources available online throughout the course of the 10-week campaign .
