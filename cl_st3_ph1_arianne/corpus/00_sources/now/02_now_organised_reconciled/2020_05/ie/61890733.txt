However , if you would like to , you can change your cookie settings at any time by amending your browser settings . <h> Eco Explorers Club - a digital resource platform to inspire pupils <p> The course will explore five educational topics related to sustainability and the natural world . <p> The course will explore five educational topics related to sustainability and the natural world . <p> SSE Airtricity , proud sustainability partner to Dublin Zoo , has launched The Eco Explorers Club - a new digital resource platform to inspire , entertain and educate primary school children on the wonders of the natural world . The platform was created in response to the closure of schools as well as Dublin Zoo , to prevent the spread of Covid-19 . The Eco Explorers Club is a 10-week course created with the Dublin Zoo Discovery and Learning Team , that will explore five educational topics related to sustainability and the natural world including biodiversity , climate change and pollution . This will help children to continue on their journey to becoming environmental champions of the future , while keeping them connected to Ireland 's favourite family destination , Dublin Zoo . Throughout the 10-week period , The Eco Explorers Club will launch a series of animated educational videos alongside videos and lessons created by The Eco Explorers Cub ambassadors , including much loved artist and environmentalist Don Conroy . In addition , well-known children 's entertainer Reuben the Entertainer will be joined by SSE Airtricity 's Head of Sustainability Mia Fahey-McCarthy for storytelling sessions . Children 's yoga instructor Caragh Egan will deliver yoga exploration classes , enabling children to explore yoga poses related to nature and animals , as well as some relaxation and imagination time . Virtual Tours of Dublin Zoo will also be available , so kids can go behind the scenes at Dublin Zoo , home to more than 400 rare , exotic , and endangered animals from around the world . The range of resources , created together with Dublin Zoo 's Discovery and Learning team , is available on The Eco Explorers Club hub at **34;7251;TOOLONG . Head of Discovery and Learning at Dublin Zoo , Aileen Tennant said : " We 're really excited to launch The Eco Explorers Club together with our sustainability partner SSE Airtricity . The platform allows us to reach young nature lovers while Dublin Zoo and schools are closed , encouraging a love and understanding of our natural world , as well as promoting the small steps we can all take to make big differences for our environment and the species we share our planet with . " Speaking at the launch , SSE Airtricity 's Sponsorship Marketing Manager Leanne Sheill said : " Keeping children engaged and inspired is more important now than ever before . We 're delighted to be working with our partner Dublin Zoo once again , this time bringing The Eco Explorers Club to life . The platform is a digital resource for families looking for educational and engaging material to build children 's interest and understanding of the natural world and the science behind it . " The Eco Explorers Club is available to everyone for free at **34;7287;TOOLONG with new resources available online throughout the course of the 10 week campaign .
