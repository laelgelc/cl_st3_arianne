<h> Government talks ' going well ' - Leo <p> Talks to form the next government are going well , Taoiseach Leo Varadkar has said . <p> He said he hoped to be able to negotiate a programme for the new administration 's priorities before the end of this month . <p> Discussions between Fine Gael , Fianna Fail and the Green Party have been taking place this week to appoint a new team of ministers . <p> Negotiators have been discussing policies on the economy , housing , healthcare and climate change . <p> " Talks are going well , and I 'm still confident that it will be possible to negotiate a programme for government , ideally before the end of the month , " Mr Varadkar said . <p> Consequences <p> Fine Gael and Fianna Fail have been trying to draw other parties and Independents into the talks process , but Aontu , the Social Democrats and the Labour Party have all ruled themselves out . <p> Mr Varadkar warned that governance would be a " hard labour " as Ireland recovers from the downturn caused by coronavirus restrictions . <p> " It 's not going to be an easy time to be in government , " he said . " The economic consequences of this virus are going to be very severe in terms of jobs lost , in terms of businesses which will not open again , in terms of the impact on public finances . This country needs a government that is led by people that want to be in government even when it 's not easy . " <p> Efforts to form a government are intensifying , three months on from February 's inconclusive election . <p> Fianna Fail won 38 seats ( a tally reduced to 37 after one of its TDs was re-elected speaker ) , Sinn Fein 37 and Fine Gael 35 , with the Greens on 12 and Labour on six . <p> A majority of 80 seats is needed to form a government . <p> Yesterday , the Labour Party formally ruled itself out . <p> Leader Alan Kelly wrote in a letter to the Taoiseach and Fianna Fail leader Micheal Martin that his party 's position on economic policy was at odds and criticised a " lack of clear direction " on a coronavirus unemployment payment .
