<h> North Herts District Council is encouraging residents to help newly planted trees on road verges and in parks , by giving them a drink of water . <p> The council is supporting a national campaign to save young trees during the dry summer months , as contractors who ordinarily water them are not available due to the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> NHDC is encouraging residents to get involved and water newly planted trees -- which can be identified as being supported by wooden stakes with tree guards around them . <p> Information posters will be placed on noticeboards in towns and parks to raise awareness for the campaign . <p> Cllr Steve Jarvis , NHDC 's executive member for environment said : " Trees need proper aftercare to be able to survive to maturity . Without receiving enough water to sustain them , they will die . <h> You may also want to watch : <p> " This is a great way that residents of all ages can get involved in helping nature during these difficult times . Nurturing young trees by recycling water in this way will help the trees to grow , benefiting local wildlife and helping to fight climate change for many years to come . " <p> Top tips include : <p> * Water regularly during dry periods with as much as you can -- every little helps <p> * Where possible , water should be sustianably sourced -- bath water , or water that has been used for washing up is suitable . <p> * The best time to water is in the early morning or evening <p> * If the tree has a watering tube , then half of the water should be poured down the tube and the other half on the ground surface near the base of the tree . <p> If you value what this story gives you , please consider supporting the Comet .
