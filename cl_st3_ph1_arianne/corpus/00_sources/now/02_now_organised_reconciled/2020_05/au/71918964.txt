" <p> Some of the 2020 cotton yield at the Emerald cotton gin . ( ABC Capricornia : Erin Semmler ) <p> At a Cotton Australia community engagement day in Sydney , central Queensland-based grower Fleur Anderson said growers did not want to ignore concerns . <p> " Caring for the environment , being water efficient , and tackling climate change -- topics we hear the community question us on -- are all things I care about too , " the Cotton Australia board member said . <p> " We need to bridge the city-country divide . <p> " What 's really key is that we have a calm , rational conversation about concerns , what we have in common , where we have differences and what the industry needs to work on to better engage with the community . " <p> Mr Jones said at a grassroots level growers were doing their best to produce cotton sustainably . <p> " In the end they 're trying to grow the best crops they can ; doing everything they can and right for the environment that they can do , " he said . <p> " It 's really a big part of the whole area , it 's not only the growers it 's also part of the whole community as well . "
