Well , the experts are telling me in no uncertain terms , and not for the first time I might add , ' continuing with the present workload is seriously detrimental to your health ' . I have listened to the experts and I am taking this opportunity to indicate to my radio family that I will be retiring from radio at the end of this month , " he said . <p> Ben Fordham , who currently hosts the drive program and has been touted by some in recent months as Jones ' replacement over rival Ray Hadley , will take over Jones ' role . <p> " I feel a sense of both responsibility and opportunity .
