<h> The Economist launches climate change education series for students <p> The Economist has launched an educational series -- Climate Briefs -- for students with six bi-weekly articles . <p> The announcement : <p> The Economist launches weekly Climate Briefs <p> The weekly Climate Briefs , aimed at students , begins with analysis of the history and politics of global warming <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Today The Economist launches a new six-week series , Climate Briefs , aimed at educating students about climate change . The series will consist of six , two-page articles released weekly online and in print . The first briefing , " A challenge without precedent " focuses on the politics of climate action and can be read online today and in The Economist 's April 25th issue . <p> Upcoming Climate Briefs topics will include : <p> Modelling the greenhouse effect <p> The carbon cycle , present and future <p> The impacts and their timescales <p> Engineering an energy transition <p> The imperative of adaptation <p> The Economist has run occasional series of primers on topical subjects since the early 1980s . These series , originally called " schools briefs " were designed to help students prepare for school -- and anyone else looking for rigorous yet accessible explanation . <p> The Climate Briefs will be part of a dedicated climate change hub that includes five of The Economist 's most recent climate stories . <p> The Climate Briefs will be continued with three explanatory videos from Economist Films , set to launch weekly as the written series ends , which will break down the essentials of climate change .
