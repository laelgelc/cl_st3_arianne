<h> Media coverage of climate infected by coronavirus <p> The number of articles focused on climate change have hit 12-month lows in Australian media as coronavirus dominates the news cycle , according to analysis by real-time media montoring group Streem . <p> Stories mentioning climate change in metropolitan print and online titles fell below 1% in April for the first time in at least a year . <p> Climate coverage peaked during Australia 's bushfire crisis in January , being mentioned in 4.6% of stories . <p> But since then reporting has plummeted . The number of stories mentioning climate change in the first 100 words has fallen to 101 in April from 873 in January . <p> There were four times more COVID-19 stories in April than climate change articles in the past year , as this chart shows : <p> Coronavirus coverage has reached unprecedented levels of media saturation , being mentioned in up to 80% of stories on some days in March . <p> Streem media analyst Conal Hanna says the coronavirus coverage was always going to impact the amount of media attention available for other topics . <p> However some issues have still managed to cut through in recent weeks . <p> " The George Pell verdict , the release of Malcolm Turnbull 's memoir and the ongoing discussion about football season resumptions are all topics that have generated considerable prominence in the media despite the pandemic , " says Hanna . <p> " With coronavirus likely to be around for a considerable time to come , the challenge for climate change academics and activists will be how to wrest back some of the media 's attention .
