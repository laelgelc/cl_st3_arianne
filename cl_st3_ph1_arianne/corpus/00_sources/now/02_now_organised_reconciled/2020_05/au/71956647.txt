<h> Cause of climate change exaggerated <p> THOSE who question whether the impact of mankind 's burning of fossil fuels is responsible for the minuscule warming of the climate over the past century or so , are labelled " climate deniers " . <p> The real " deniers " are actually those who will not recognise that the earth 's climate has never stopped changing and that those changes are almost exclusively due to natural factors . <p> Greenland gets its name from having been a fertile , green country and grapes were grown as far as 55 degrees North 1000 years ago . <p> Forests grew and people lived in areas that were later overtaken by ice and snow thousands of years ago . <p> Human remains and numerous artefacts as well as evidence of thriving forests are reappearing where some glaciers are receding . <p> Ice ages , interspersed with warm periods have always been a feature of the earth 's climate . <p> The human influence now being claimed by alarmists as being due to our use of fossil fuels did not exist over all those centuries of a continuously changing climate . Interestingly , the substantial worldwide reduction in carbon dioxide emissions during this virus crisis has had no measurable impact on the continuing trend of CO2 in the atmosphere . <p> In other words , it seems that our presumed impact on the climate has been hugely exaggerated .
