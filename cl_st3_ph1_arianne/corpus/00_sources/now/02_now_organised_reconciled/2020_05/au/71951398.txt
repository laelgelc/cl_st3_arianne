<h> Category <h> Mamamia Editorial Guidelines <p> Mamamia is a publisher with a purpose - to make the world a better place for women and girls . <p> As a result , it is important to us that we are fair in our reporting , get things right , and do not cause harm to those affected by our reporting . <p> Our commitment to editorial independence , integrity and high journalistic standards are outlined in these editorial guidelines , which apply to our editorial staff and freelancers . <p> If you feel we have not met these guidelines , we would like to know . Feedback and complaints can be directed to email protected , who will look at your complaints and queries against our editorial guidelines . You can read our complaints policy here . <p> Before contacting The Editor , you should read through these guidelines to establish whether your complaint falls outside Mamamia 's editorial guidelines . The Editor reserves the right not to consider complaints : <p> Lodged by someone who is not personally or directly affected by the matter upon which the complaint is based <p> That are matters of personal opinion <p> Below are Mamamia 's editorial guidelines . <p> Mamamia adheres to the statement of general journalistic principles , as outlined by the Australian Press Council , below : <p> Accuracy and clarity <p> Ensure that factual material in news reports and elsewhere is accurate and not misleading , and is distinguishable from other material such as opinion . <p> Provide a correction or other adequate remedial action if published material is significantly inaccurate or misleading ( see our complaints procedure for more information ) . <p> Fairness and balance <p> Ensure that factual material is presented with reasonable fairness and balance , and that writers ' expressions of opinion are not based on significantly inaccurate factual material or omission of key facts . <p> Ensure that where material refers adversely to a person , a fair opportunity is given for subsequent publication of a reply if that is reasonably necessary to address a possible breach of General Principle 3 . <p> Privacy and avoidance of harm <p> Avoid intruding on a person 's reasonable expectations of privacy , unless doing so is sufficiently in the public interest . <p> Avoid causing or contributing materially to substantial offence , distress or prejudice , or a substantial risk to health or safety , unless doing so is sufficiently in the public interest . <p> Integrity and transparency <p> Avoid publishing material which has been gathered by deceptive or unfair means , unless doing so is sufficiently in the public interest . <p> Ensure that conflicts of interests are avoided or adequately disclosed , and that they do not influence published material . <p> Mamamia also adheres to the Australian Press Council 's advisory guidelines in covering issues such as reporting suicide , mental health issues , eating disorders and self-harm . <p> In the range of topics we cover , Mamamia understands that anonymity may be the only way to bring to light stories about certain issues - for legal and personal reasons . <p> However , we are also aware that anonymity may not allow a reader to assess the validity of the source 's claims . <p> Therefore , Mamamia only use anonymous quotations when the reason for doing so is to protect the identity of a vulnerable person . The decision about whether to use anonymous quotations is made by The Editor . <p> Similarly , when assessing anonymous contributions , we consider whether the author 's safety , privacy or livelihood may be compromised by being named . <p> In these cases , we include the caveat : ' The author of this story is known to Mamamia , but has chosen to remain anonymous . ' <p> Where quotes or research from other sources are used , writers must credit the original source and hyperlink to the source . <p> Bylines appear only on articles where the substantial content of the article is that author 's work . <p> Where agency copy is used , that agency will be credited in the first line of the article . <p> Where we have republished a story , with permission , from another organisation , the following copy will appear at the end of the article : This post Link was originally published on XX Link , and has been republished with full permission . <p> Commissioning <p> Mamamia 's terms and conditions for contributions can be found here , and our guidelines for how to submit a post can be found here . <p> Conflicts of interest <p> Writers and freelancers should be transparent with any conflicts of interest that may impact their ability to tell a story fairly . For example , a personal relationship with a source , a political affiliation , or a personal partnership with a brand . Declarations of interest should always be made clear in a story . <p> Copy approval <p> Copy approval is only offered in rare circumstances , where a source 's story may be particularly sensitive . The Editor will approve cases of copy approval . <p> Dealing with children <p> If a writer interviews a child under 16 , or if a child under 16 submits a piece of work to be published , consent should be obtained from the child 's parent or guardian . <p> ' Do n't diss the ladies ' <p> Mamamia is pro-women and strives to not publish any content that unfairly or unjustly criticises women 's choices . We 're also a body-positive publisher , and do not report on women 's physical appearances ( e.g. weight loss or gain ) , unless it is part of a broader conversation around body image and self worth , and we do not report on social media stoushes targeting women for behaviour or actions that are not substantially in the public interest . <p> Errors <p> Mamamia will correct errors as soon as possible . Where we have corrected an error , a line will appear at the bottom of the article to acknowledge the correction . <p> Identifying yourself when working with the public <p> Any writer working on a story for Mamamia must identify themselves as such . In exceptional circumstances , writers must ask The Editor for approval to not reveal the details of what they are working on to those involved . <p> Legal <p> Writers should be familiar with media law , and speak to a senior editor about specific concerns with stories . Writers should be familiar with the legal status of cases they are reporting on . <p> Non-negotiable stances <p> Mamamia will often publish opinions from both sides of a debate . However , there are some topics on which our stance is non-negotiable , and we will not give air time to the other side because we believe this breeds misinformation . For this reason Mamamia does not give attention to the topics of anti-vaccination , anti-abortion , climate change denial or hate speech against same-sex marriage , the LGBTQI community , or cultural minorities . <p> Right of reply <p> A subject of significant criticism or allegations or wrongdoing should always be offered a right of reply . Sufficient time should be offered to a person to provide a reply before a story is published . This timeframe is up to the discretion of The Editor . A person should be provided specific information about the allegations or claims made in a story , so they are given a fair opportunity to challenge them . If Mamamia does not receive a reply , or the subject chooses not to respond , we should make this transparent in the story . <p> Paid endorsements <p> Mamamia staff should not knowingly write content about brands and/or products in exchange for freebies . If staff are sent a product and would like to write about it , they should provide full disclosure about how they obtained it . Mamamia will often be paid for content by brands however , this content goes through the commercial team and is clearly marked as " branded " content . <p> Paparazzi photos <p> Mamamia values the personal privacy of celebrities , as well as their children , and for this reason does not participate in the paparazzi economy . Mamamia does publish red carpet photos or photos where the celebrity is in a public place or clearly consenting to having their photograph taken such as smiling or by posing for the photo . <p> Privacy <p> We should avoid invading anyone 's privacy unless doing so is sufficiently a matter of public interest . It should not be a surprise for someone without a public profile to see their photo or a substantial personal social media post on our site - writers should reach out to individuals to gain permission where possible . <p> We are careful in the specific information we provide , so as not to intrude on the safety of people we may report on . <p> Verification <p> We should always seek to verify the original sources of stories , and ensure that even popular stories circulating in the mainstream media are based on fact . <p> Weight loss/diet stories <p> Mamamia does not publish diet advice , particularly surrounding weight loss and/or how to lose weight . This is because Mamamia believes in diversity and that women of all shapes and sizes should be accepted as they are . Mamamia publishes content about fitness , food , etc , from the perspective of health .
