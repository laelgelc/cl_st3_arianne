<h> World Economic Forum hosts online event for better ocean management <p> The World Economic Forum and a coalition of more than 50 ocean leaders are hosting a global online conference for ocean action . <p> With the theme connecting communities for ocean resilience , innovation and action , the 2020 Virtual Ocean Dialogues will run from June 1-5 . <p> The World Economic Forum and Friends of Ocean Action are curating the event to convene leaders and communities online from across the global ocean space . <p> The conference has been created to help maintain positive momentum in global efforts to better protect and manage the resources of the ocean , according to Peter Thomson , United Nations secretary-general 's special envoy for the ocean , and Friends of Ocean Action co-chair . <p> " Now more than ever , humanity needs a healthy , thriving ocean to protect our communities and build resilience against future crises . To achieve this , those of us who can are invited to commit to ambitious action on behalf of the ocean 's wellbeing , " he said . <p> " It is in humanity 's best interests that we reverse the cycle of decline in which the ocean has been caught . This can only be achieved through implementation of the UN 's Sustainable Development Goal 14 , for the conservation and sustainable use of the ocean 's resources . The Virtual Ocean Dialogues will drive us in that direction , through interactive dialogue between the finest of world ocean expertise and the virtual inclusivity of anyone around the world interested in the challenges facing the ocean today . " <p> Numerous speakers will will cover 12 priority topics , including the sustainable ocean economy , stopping pollution tide , global priorities for ocean protection , and strengthening ocean and community resilience to climate change . <p> A new digital platform will also be launched at the event .
