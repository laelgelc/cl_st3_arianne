But the sands are slipping away and Jamaicans fear the beach , someday , will need a new nickname . <p> Some sections , however , are barely wide enough for a decent-sized beach towel and the Jamaican National Environment and Planning Agency says sand is receding at a rate of more than a metre per year . <p> " The beach could be totally lost within 30 years , " said Anthony McKenzie , a senior director at the agency . <p> Shrinking coastline has long raised fears for the areas environmental and economic future . <p> Now , the erosion is expected to worsen as a result of climate change , and a hint of panic is creeping through this laid back village , one of the top destinations in a country where a quarter of all jobs depend on tourism . <p> The beach-lined coast of Negril , Jamaica , in 1956 .
