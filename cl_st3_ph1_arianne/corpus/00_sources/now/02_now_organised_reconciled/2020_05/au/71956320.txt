<p> The 1975 released their fourth studio album , " Notes on a Conditional Form , " on Friday . <p> After multiple delays , the album finally arrived a full two years after it was initially announced . " Notes " is intended to play as a companion to 2018 's Grammy-nominated " A Brief Inquiry into Online Relationships . " Together , the two albums form the British band 's " Music for Cars " era . <p> Insider 's music team ( reporter Callie Ahlgrim and celebrity and music editor Courteney Larocca ) listened to the new album on our own , jotting down our initial thoughts track by track . <p> Overall , we thought the exhaustive , 22-song tracklist could have been tighter , but there were n't any songs that gave us the impulse to actively press " skip . " <p> There were , however , numerous highlights and bright spots woven throughout the album . <p> The best tracks on the album are " The Birthday Party , " " Tonight ( I Wish I Was Your Boy ) , " and " If You 're Too Shy ( Let Me Know ) , " while we were disappointed by some of the interludes . <p> Here is what we thought of each song on " Notes on a Conditional Form " upon first listen .
