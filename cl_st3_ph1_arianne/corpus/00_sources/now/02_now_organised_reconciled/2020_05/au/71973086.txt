<h> Sacred sites : Where 's our outrage over this destruction ? <p> May 29 , 2020 -- 12.00am <p> To submit a letter to The Age , email letters@theage.com.au . Please include your home address and telephone number . <p> In 2001 when the Taliban destroyed the Buddhas of Bamyan , the world was outraged . When the ancient city of Palmyra was damaged by Islamic State during the Syrian civil war , there was outcry . " Our world heritage is being destroyed , " many proclaimed , and often the sentiments " by barbarians " were added . <p> Illustration : Jim Pavlidis <p> This week Rio Tinto blasted ancient Aboriginal sites with a 46,000-year heritage in Western Australia . Why was this allowed ? Why are we outraged at the destruction of ancient works of art in foreign lands while , at the same time , allowing destruction of artwork in our own land ? All these sites have something in common . They form part of a priceless heritage . If we are outraged at one , we should be outraged at all three . I understand Rio Tinto has permission to destroy further Aboriginal heritage sites in Western Australia .
