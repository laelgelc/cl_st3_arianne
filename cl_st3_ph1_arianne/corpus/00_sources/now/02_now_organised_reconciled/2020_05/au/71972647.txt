years as an Australian actor , achieving the rare honour of the ' Triple Crown ' -- with an Oscar , a Tony and an Emmy in his kitty . <p> He also has three Australian Film Institute honours , three British Academy Awards , two Golden Globes and four Screen Actors ' Guild Awards . <p> He was inducted into the ranks of Australia 's elite with a Helpmann Award last year and was also appointed the foundation President of the newly-established Australian Academy Of Cinema and Television Arts . <p> Community leader Laurie Baymarrwangga of Northern Territory has taken out the Senior Australian of the Year 2012 title for her commitment to the society and her philanthropic work over the years . <p> The great , great grandmother has shown extraordinary courage in preserving the cultural and biological integrity of Crocodile Islands . <p> Engineering student Marita Cheng has been named the Young Australian of the Year for her work towards helping women pursue a career in engineering . <p> Born to Chinese parents , Marita was born and raised in Queensland and now studies at the University of Melbourne . <p> She founded Robogals Global in 2008 that runs robotics workshops for girls across 80 schools in Australia and has 17 chapters across Australia , New Zealand and the United Kingdom . <p> Foster mum Lynne Sawyers of New South Wales has taken out Australia 's Local Hero 2012
