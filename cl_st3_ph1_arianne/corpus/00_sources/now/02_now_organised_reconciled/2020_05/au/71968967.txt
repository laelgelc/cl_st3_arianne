<p> With approximately 110,000 square kilometres ( or 27.2 million acres ) burnt , the impact remains widespread for so many people . <p> Those who lost loved ones , friends or neighbours ; the 3,000 families who lost their homes ; the many who have suffered major disruptions to livelihoods ; the 5.1 million people impacted by bushfire smoke ; and the many people experiencing trauma and its associated impact on mental health . <p> This terrible experience was then closely followed by the COVID-19 pandemic . <p> For many of those directly impacted by the bushfires it is likely to be severely disrupting to their recovery . Needs like clearing up , restarting businesses , regaining income , community support and comfort have all been put on hold . <p> It becomes difficult to fill in application forms when so many important documents have been destroyed . <p> I actually lost my Australian citizenship as a result of a 1983 bushfire as I could n't fill in the official documentation within the set time saying that I wished to remain an Australian instead of British . My birth and marriage certificates had been burnt , along with the house . <p> The complexities of dealing with two major disasters at the same time is evident . <p> The township of Mogo was destroyed as bushfires raged up the NSW south coast . Picture : Getty Images <p> For example , the Commission 's planned community forums on bushfire have been suspended due to the pandemic . <p> However , COVID-19 may well have done the Royal Commission and the Australian public an unexpected favour . Australia was largely unprepared for either the major bushfires or the pandemic . Yet the government 's response to both , was very different . <p> The response to the bushfire crisis has been described as insufficient and slow . Internationally , it is now common to employ the defence forces in direct fire suppression , a tardy ' assistance ' role was provided by the defence force in Australia . <p> Prevention responses were rapidly put in place . Australian borders were closed , and people were asked to isolate themselves in their homes . <p> The government also recognised that parts of the economy needed to be sacrificed to protect lives . Financial responses were put in place to prevent economic collapse in some sectors . Potential job losses were addressed through JobKeeper payments , unemployment benefits were doubled and grants were made to key industries . <p> The Royal Commission will have an opportunity to take on board the learnings from the response to the pandemic . Picture : Getty Images <p> While not perfect , action was decisive and swift . <p> It appears that the response to COVID-19 has been very effective . We will learn more in the future about the effectiveness of the measures to protect the economy . <p> This overlapping of disasters has given the Royal Commission an opportunity to take on board the learnings from the pandemic response . <p> Clearing through my old papers recently , I found many scientific reports and books written in the past two decades , flagging the impact of climate change if prevention measures are n't put in place -- floods , droughts and bushfires -- all seemingly ignored . <p> The Cambridge dictionary defines rehabilitation as ' to return something to a good condition ' . Rehabilitation can have both short and longer-term ' good condition ' targets . Those most at risk need timely and sufficient short-term assistance to enable them to recover successfully from trauma . <p> There are many actions the Commission may recommend , including strong action on climate change by Australia . Picture : Getty Images <p> However , returning to a good condition will also need adequate preventative action to facilitate the smallest possible adverse impact of future disasters . <p> There are many actions the Commission will hopefully recommend . These include strong action on climate change by Australia -- immediately moving away from all fossil fuels , gas included . <p> Yes , this will dent the economy , but it will also be important in returning social , environmental and economic states ' to a good condition ' . <p> According to Stuart Ellis , chief executive of the Australasian Fire and Emergency Service Authorities Council , there may be some value in a new inquiry , but not at the expense of action . <p> While there remains considerable uncertainty about the course of the pandemic , let 's deal with what is certain -- greenhouse gas reductions are urgently needed now . <p> The Australian state and federal governments have now proven they can move swiftly and decisively at a time of crisis -- heeding expert advice .
