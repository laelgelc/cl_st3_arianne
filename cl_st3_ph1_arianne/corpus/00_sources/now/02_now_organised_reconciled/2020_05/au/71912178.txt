<h> More <h> Both sides claim underdog status ahead of June byelection <p> Anthony Albanese and Scott Morrison have both claimed underdog status going into the Eden-Monaro byelection , which is set to be held in mid to late June . <p> The contest , which will double as the first test of the government 's handling of the coronavirus crisis , has been caused by the resignation due to ill-health of Labor MP Mike Kelly . <p> " It has broken my heart to have to do this " : Mike Kelly has resigned due to ill health . Alex Ellinghausen <p> Mr Kelly said severe bouts of dehydration suffered when he served in Iraq had caused chronic kidney damage . He was no longer able to service a large and rugged electorate that spans most of southern NSW . <p> " It has broken my heart to have to do this . It is gut wrenching . And I would n't be doing it if it just was n't absolutely necessary to do it now , ' ' he said . <p> " The Coalition start as favourites in this byelection . I think there 's no question that that 's the case if you look at the analysis of the vote , and how much of a personal vote Mike Kelly has , ' ' Mr Albanese said . <p> " But it is an opportunity for the people of Eden-Monaro to send Scott Morrison , in particular , and the government a message . " <p> Of all electorates in Australia , Eden-Monaro was the hardest hit by the drought and the bushfires over summer . It includes the southern NSW town of Cobargo , where Mr Morrison infamously tried to shake the hand of a hostile bushfire victim as well as an angry firefighter . <p> Deputy Premier and NSW Nationals leader John Barilaro , who holds the state seat of Monaro , is seriously considering running , as is NSW Transport Minster Andrew Constance , a Liberal , whose state seat of Bega also overlaps Eden-Monaro . <p> It is understood Mr Constance will not run if Mr Barilaro runs , and will likely contest the federal seat of Gilmore at the next general election . <p> If Mr Constance and Mr Barilaro both ran , they would have to resign , and that would subject NSW Premier Gladys Berejiklian , who has only a two-seat majority and a restive backbench , to two byelections . <p> Despite the coronavirus pandemic swamping federal politics , Mr Kelly said the impact of drought and fire meant parties would have to campaign hard on climate change .
