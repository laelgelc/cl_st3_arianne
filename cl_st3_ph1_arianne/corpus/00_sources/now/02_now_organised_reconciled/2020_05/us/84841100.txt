<h> Letter : For true leadership I choose Joe Biden <h> Thursday <p> For true leadership , for a united sense of purpose , for genuine patriotism and the kind of experience that can calmly steer our country through crisis , I choose to support Joe Biden for President . <p> His sense of compassion and his character will re-route and restore our standing in the world community . We can expect him to be hard at work on day one to confront the current health crisis , using every resource , based on science , not politics . He will ensure all Americans can access affordable , dependable health insurance . <p> Joe Biden is a lifelong protector of the environment and the all-important need to address climate change . He will support and strive to achieve a fair and thriving economy . His background in procuring legislation to make quality education available to all is high on his agenda . His support of civil rights and his record on women 's rights are legendary and he 's championed the cause of ending domestic violence and abuse . <p> He has proposed reasonable and achievable gun legislation , so that not one more school and not one more family loses a child to gun violence . <p> Joe Biden will be a strong commander in chief . He will strengthen our alliances with friends around the world . There will be no more kowtowing to ruthless dictators . He 'll support our military and our veterans will always be respected and cherished . <p> Joe Biden will choose a female running mate and will select a high-caliber cabinet , whose make-up will reflect the diversity of our country . He does not seek praise or adulation , but instead is able to accept and value the opinions of others . He knows how to get things done . <p> I ask you to support Joe Biden 's candidacy in the November presidential election . <p> We need , now more than ever , a President we can trust . <p> Mary Carey Foley <p> Portsmouth <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
