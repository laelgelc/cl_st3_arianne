We have three House races that we 're watching : the special election runoff in CA-25 , the special election in WI-07 and the Democratic primary in NE-02 . Here 's what you need to know about those races : <p> CA-25 : The special election runoff between Republican Mike Garcia and Democrat Christy Smith is inarguably the main event of the night . Republicans are on the precipice of doing the unthinkable : flipping a House seat in California . Monday 's Score touched on the race , as did my colleague Ally Mutnick 's great story from over the weekend . Polls close at 11 p.m . Eastern , but it is possible ( if not probable ) that we wo n't actually know who wins tonight . The vast majority of votes will be cast via the mail , and recall in California votes can count as long as they 're postmarked by Election Day and received by Friday . It 'd have to be fairly definitive for someone to actually be declared a winner tonight . <p> WI-07 : This race pits Republican Tom Tiffany against Democrat Tricia Zunker to fill a seat that 's been open since now-former GOP Rep. Sean Duffy resigned due to family health concerns . Barring something truly bizarre , operatives in both parties are expecting Tiffany to become the next representative from The Badger State . I previewed this race in Score on Friday ; polls close at 9 p.m . Eastern . Unlike the state 's spring primary , absentee ballots need to be returned to election officials by that time . So assuming everything goes smoothly , we should have a winner here when we gather again tomorrow . <p> NE-02 : The one primary we 're watching most closely tonight is in the Omaha-based swing seat . Two Democrats are facing off for the right to challenge GOP Rep. Don Bacon : 2018 nominee Kara Eastman and attorney Ann Ashford , who is the wife of former Rep. Brad Ashford . The primary continues the Eastman-Ashford feud : Eastman beat the former congressman in the 2018 Democratic primary before narrowly losing to Bacon in the general election . Polls close at 9 p.m . Eastern . <p> Whoever emerges from the primary will have a real tough climb to face Bacon . Eastman has pretty easily outraised Ashford , but both are basically going to be running on empty after the primary . Neither candidate had even six figures on hand as of late April , while Bacon was sitting on about $765,000 . <p> The two other Nebraska House seats are safe Republican territory . Republican Sen. Ben Sasse is also facing a primary challenge from Matt Innis , who 's criticized him for not being " on board " with Trump . But the challenge has quieted down quite significantly since all the chatter about it last year . Sasse released an ad ahead of Tuesday 's primary that even highlights his every-so-often fights with President Donald Trump . " He 's ticked off a lot of folks these past six years , from the radical left to , every now and then , even the president from his own party , " a narrator says in the ad over the image of a Trump tweet mocking Sasse . " Here 's the deal : Ben 's got guts , " the narrator adds . <h> Presidential Big Board <p> CONVENTION SPOTTING -- The DNC 's Rules and Bylaws Committee is meeting today to discuss a handful of changes to primaries due to the coronavirus , and hinted at the potential of some sort of virtual convention . A DNC official told Score the RBC will consider a resolution on the convention that will formally change the date for the convention until August . The resolution will also give the convention team the authority to make future changes to the " date and other aspects " ( read : format or size ) that wo n't have to go through the RBC and full DNC . A clause that stood out : " The Rules and Bylaws Committee 's Resolution further recommends certain changes to the Call so as to safeguard the ability of all validly-elected Convention delegates to participate in the Convention in person or by means that allow for appropriate social distancing " . <p> There 's also changes to the rules and credentials committee that will allow reports from those committees to be considered passed once they 're passed by the committee , instead of voted on by the full membership . The Platform Committee report will be considered by all delegates still . After being passed out of the RBC , the resolution needs to be considered by the entirety of the DNC . Staffers are working on a vote-by-mail system for DNC members for this , which requires a majority vote . <p> The committee will also take up waiver requests from five state parties that requested them and had moved their primary out of the window originally set by the DNC -- Kentucky , New York , New Jersey , Delaware and Louisiana . ( Connecticut , which also moved its primary , is missing from this list . ) The RBC is expected to approve the waivers , and the official told Score that the delegate allocation wo n't change , meaning there will be no monkeying around with bonus delegates . <p> THE CASH DASH -- President Donald Trump and the RNC only narrowly outraised Joe Biden and the DNC in April , POLITICO 's Marc Caputo and Alex Isenstadt reported . Republicans raised $61.7 million and Democrats raised $60.5 million . One place Trump still likely has a strong advantage ? Cash on hand . Biden did not release what he has in the bank , but Trump and the RNC had a combined $255 million in the bank . <p> -- The Biden campaign is hosting a " private three-day virtual forum " for those who have raised over $50,000 for Biden , CNBC 's Brian Schwartz reported . Biden and Jill Biden will host a Q&amp;A , and other prominent Democrats like DNC Chair Tom Perez and several top Biden staffers will give presentations . <p> VEEPSTAKES -- Sen. Kamala Harris ( D-Calif. ) is seen as the best pick for Biden 's running mate among a cadre of major donors , aides and surrogates . " In interviews , more than two dozen Democrats , including advisers , allies and donors aligned with Biden , returned to Harris as an early frontrunner , " POLITICO 's Chris Cadelago and Natasha Korecki reported . My colleagues also reported that Sen. Elizabeth Warren ( D-Mass. ) is seen as " rising above the pack " as well . <p> Those close to Biden said they did n't expect Harris ' attack on Biden in the debate to haunt her . " Joe Biden is a big boy , " Rep. Jim Clyburn ( D-S.C. ) said . " I 've never seen Joe have any animus toward Kamala for what may have been said during the campaign . Subscribe today . <h> Down the Ballot <p> THE PROCESS -- Democrats are set to unveil their opening offer for phase 4 of coronavirus relief ( or CARES 2.0 , whatever you want to call it ) real soon . One bit of the package that 's of interest to you , my dear readers : the election administration portion . A source familiar with the bill told Score that the elections section in the massive package will be similar to the one offered by House Democratic leadership in late March . Still included : mandidating no-excuse absentee voting across the country , expanding early voting and requiring voters be sent ballots when a state of emergency or disaster has been declared . <p> Democrats are also looking to allocate far more in grant money to states than was in the first CARES package . ( $400 million was ultimately allocated in the first round . ) Two Democratic sources told me on Monday afternoon that they were still landing on the exact number , but expect it to be with a " b . " One source said it will be somewhere in the " $2-to-$4 billion range . " A second source described the spending as being " much bigger than $400 million ... capital B , capital I , capital G. " Another thing Democrats hope to get rid of ? The requirement from the first CARES package that states match 20 percent of the funds they receive to unlock federal money , something that secretaries of state from both parties have grumbled to Score about in the past . <p> One thing to keep in mind : This is from where Democrats are going to be negotiating , not necessarily what ends up in the final package ( which , of course , would need to go through the GOP-controlled Senate and signed by a Republican president ) . But , as POLITICO 's Burgess Everett and Heather Cayglereport , there 's little interest ( and time ) from Senate Republicans on pushing through another relief package this month . Republicans on the House Administration Committee also put out a prebuttal of sorts on Monday , walking through their objections to several expected aspects of the Democrats ' pandemic election plan . <p> -- The latest Democratic lawsuit over voter access has landed in Texas . The Texas Tribune 's Alexa Ura reports that the lawsuit seeks to overturn restrictions on absentee voting . <p> FIRST IN SCORE -- SAFE SEAT SCRAMBLE -- Do n't look now , but next month 's primary in the blue NM-03 is attracting some serious outside cash . CHC BOLD PAC , the political arm of the Congressional Hispanic Caucus , dropped just under $425,000 on a television and digital buy supporting Teresa Leger Fernandez , who is facing Valerie Plame in the primary . The ad is a positive bio spot promoting her ties to the district and her health care positions . Women Vote ! , the super PAC tied to EMILY 's List , also dropped about $131,000 in mailers in the last week backing Fernandez . <p> -- The race to replace retiring Democratic Rep. Denny Heckin WA-10 is a crowded one , The Tacoma News Tribune 's James Drew wrote . But three candidates have stood out from the pack with cash and endorsements : former state Rep. Kristine Reeves , former Tacoma Mayor Marilyn Strickland , and state Rep. Beth Doglio . Relatedly : BOLD PAC also recently endorsed Reeves . <p> THE GOVERNATORS -- The Alaska state Supreme Court ruled last week that the recall effort against GOP Gov . Mike Dunleavy can proceed , the Anchorage Daily News ' James Brooks reported . The group seeking to recall Dunleavy still needs to collect signatures of over 71,000 voters . <p> PRIMARY PROBLEMS -- The Chamber of Commerce is going up with an ad criticizing GOP Rep.
