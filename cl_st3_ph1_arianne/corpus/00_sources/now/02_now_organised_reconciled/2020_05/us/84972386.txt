<h> Oregon Primary Election highlights and results <h> Tuesday <p> The Lane County Board of Elections released its preliminary Primary Election results at 8 p.m . Tuesday . <p> Find Lane County local results here . <p> Find statewide race results here <p> Key race highlights <p> Mayor , city of Eugene <p> When Lane County released its first round of voter returns in the Eugene mayoral primary around 8 p.m . Tuesday , Mayor Lucy Vinis held the lead . <p> Vinis had 24,489 votes , or 68% , in that first tally . <p> Vinis declared victory Tuesday night during a speech to supporters on Facebook live , thanking her endorsers , her campaign staff and the voters of Eugene . <p> " I have been saying all along I am eager to build on the foundation and critical issues I began working on in my first term , " Vinis told The Register-Guard Tuesday night . " I am just happy to continue that work . " <p> Any candidate in the primary who gets more than 50% of the vote wins the race . If no one gets more than 50% of the total vote , the race will be decided in November between the two candidates who got the most votes . <p> Vinis had six opponents in the mayoral primary . <p> As of 8 p.m. , Thomas Hiura had the second most votes in the race at 3,560 , or near 10% . Robert Patterson got 1,222 , or about 3% . Benjamin Ricker got 959 votes , almost 3% . Stacey Westover got 2,169 votes , or 6% . Matthew Yook got 1,033 votes , or about 3% . Zondie Zinke got 1,588 votes , or about 4% . <p> There were 869 write-in ballots counted at 8 p.m . <p> Mayor , city of Springfield <p> When Lane County released its first round of voter returns in the Springfield mayoral primary around 8 p.m . Tuesday , Mayor Christine Lundberg held a lead over challenger Mike Eyster . <p> Whoever wins Tuesday 's primary will serve as mayor of Springfield . <p> Lundberg had 6,403 votes , or about 58% while Eyster had 4,605 votes , or about 42% . There were 41 write-in ballots . <p> Lundberg has been an elected official in Springfield for the last 20 years , including two terms as mayor . <p> Eyster told The Register-Guard he believes recent economic growth in Springfield was good , but would have been more pronounced with a different mayor at the helm . Eyster said he plans to organize a business advisory task force to better spread success beyond downtown . <p> Lane Community College 's $121.5 million bond measure appeared poised to pass following the first round of unofficial election results Tuesday night . <p> As of 8 p.m. , 57% of Lane County voters were in favor of the bond , with 48,795 people voting for it . <p> " At this point , we 're just feeling very grateful and very humbled by this vote of support by Lane County voters , " said LCC board member Rosie Pryor on Tuesday night . " We 're hopeful it will hold throughout the evening and into tomorrow so we can roll up our sleeves and get to work . " <p> If officially approved , the bond will fund improvements in LCC 's safety and security , career education and learning space updates . Some of the proposed updates include changes to make the campus more accessible , expanding the nursing program 's simulation lab and a Workforce Development Center to connect displaced workers with training and job resources . <p> District attorney , Lane County <p> The first round of unofficial results for Lane County 's district attorney race Tuesday night showed incumbent Patricia Perlow in the lead against newcomer James Cleavenger . <p> As of 8 p.m . Tuesday , Perlow had 68% of the vote with Cleavenger at 30% . Perlow received 54,825 votes . Cleavenger had 24,572 in his favor , and there were 252 write-ins . <p> Perlow has been Lane County 's DA since being appointed to the position in 2015 . She was first elected to the position in 2016 , and first started working as a trial lawyer in 1990 . <p> The district attorney is the county 's lead prosecutor , who leads a team of deputy district attorneys to prosecute people suspected of crimes , direct investigations and recommend sentences . The position is up for election every four years . <p> City councilor , city of Eugene , Ward 1 <p> Based on the first round of unofficial results , Emily Semple , the Ward 1 incumbent , will be challenged by Eliza Kashinsky for her seat on the Eugene City Council during the November runoff . <p> Semple received 39% of the vote as of the 8 p.m. unofficial vote count for Tuesday 's primary , narrowly edging Kashinsky 's 35% . <p> " I think when you 're running in local races , it 's always really hard to know how things are going , especially in a time like this where you 're not getting a chance to get out and talk to people as much , " Kashinsky said of her expectations going into the evening . " But I do feel like Eugene very rarely has cases where city council ( races ) go to the general ( election ) . I think it has happened about four times in the past 25 years . <p> " And I really felt like this time we would be able to do that . " <p> If a candidate receives more than 50% of the vote , his or her name will be the lone one to appear on the November ballot . If no candidate receives a majority , the two top vote-getters advance to a runoff election in the fall . <p> Tim Morris ( 15.62% ) , Candice King ( 6.78% ) , Sean Dwyer ( 1.75% ) and Daniel Liev Williams ( 1.16% ) will not advance to the runoff . <p> City councilor , City of Eugene , Ward 2 <p> The first round of unofficial results project Matt Keating as the replacement for longtime Ward 2 representative Betty Taylor on the Eugene City Council . <p> Keating received 61% of the vote for a decisive edge over Kate Davidson ( 38% ) in Tuesday 's primary . <p> " It was a great night , " Keating said Tuesday night . " We were confident going into Election Day , based on the work we did over the last six months . " <p> Keating said he will be doing a lot of listening , studying and relationship building as he prepares to take over for Taylor , who had been elected to six consecutive terms and serving since 1996 on the council . <p> City councilor , city of Eugene , Ward 7 <p> Claire Syrett is leading with 59% of the vote for councilor of Eugene 's Ward 7 district , according to the unofficial results released at 8 p.m . Tuesday by Lane County Elections . <p> Challenger Douglas Barr trails with 25% of the vote and Cliff Gray with 14.03% . <p> Eugene 's Ward 7 incorporates a sprawling expanse rising from just north of downtown in the Whiteaker district , into and along River Road and ending finally in Santa Clara . <p> As Ward 7 's incumbent councilor , Syrett identifies three reasons why she 's running again : addressing homelessness ; tackling land use around House Bill 2001 , designed to create more affordable housing choices ; and ensuring that Eugene utilizes municipal economic development efforts , particularly with riverfront development . <p> City councilor , city of Eugene , Ward 8 <p> Randy Groves is leading with 57.53% of the vote for councilor of Eugene 's Ward 8 district , according to the unofficial results released at 8 p.m. by Lane County Elections . His challenger , Ryan Moore , is trailing with 42.12% . <p> Eugene 's Ward 8 includes industry and warehouses along and north of 11th Avenue , 11th Avenue 's retail and restaurant corridor and a diversity of established neighborhoods and housing to the south and west . <p> Groves , Eugene-Springfield Fire 's chief for 37 years , is campaigning upon invigorating Eugene 's Green and science economy with innovation and jobs , addressing affordable housing and homelessness and making sure that we live in a safe place with an up-to-date infrastructure . <p> " I 'm cautiously optimistic , but there 's no celebration just yet , " Groves said Tuesday night . " I 'm at peace with whatever the outcome is . All I want to do is help the city and that 's up to the voters to decide . " <p> City councilor , city of Springfield , Ward 3 <p> Springfield 's Ward 3 city council race was tight when the first round of unofficial results came through 8 p.m . Tuesday , pointing to a potential run-off in November . <p> Kori Rodley held the top spot with 3,691 votes ( 39% ) with Johanis Tadeo close behind with 3,157 votes ( 33% ) . Kris McAlister was third with 2,544 votes ( 26% ) . There were 60 write-in ballots . <p> The three candidates are all newcomers this year . Sitting councilmember Sheri Moore did not run for reelection . <p> Ward 3 is what is often referred to as " middle Springfield , " just east of downtown . <p> City councilor , city of Springfield , Ward 6 <p> Incumbent Joe Pishioneri was in the lead for Springfield 's City Council Ward 6 race when unofficial election results came in Tuesday night . <p> Sixty-eight percent of voters ( 6,807 people ) had selected Pishioneri for the position as of 8 p.m. whereas newcomer Gregg Ybarra had 31% of votes ( 3,121 people . ) This race had 54 write-in ballots . <p> Ward 6 is known as the Thurston area in east Springfield . <p> Lane County Commissioner South Eugene , Position 3 <p> Based on the first round of unofficial results at 8 p.m . Tuesday , it appears there will be a runoff election in the fall for the Lane County Commissioner , South Eugene District 3 seat , which Pete Sorenson is vacating after more than two decades . <p> The top two vote-getters on track to advance from the four-person primary race are Laurie Trieger ( 44% ) and Joel Iboa ( 35% ) . <p> Sandra Bishop received 13% of the early vote with Matt Moore receiving 8% of the vote . <p> " I 'm feeling great . I have a decisive edge at this point , " Trieger said Tuesday night . " Of course , I would have been happier to have received at least 50% of the vote . " <p> If a candidate receives more than 50% of the vote , his or her name will be the lone one to appear on the November ballot . If no candidate receives a majority , the two top vote-getters advance to a runoff election in the fall . <p> Trieger is confident her lead would hold up in November . Iboa believes his campaign will continue to gain momentum . <p> " Looks like we have a real race on our hands , " Iboa said Tuesday night . " I had a great feeling . We put in the work . Myself and my campaign manager and our campaign put in the work to fight . " <p> Lane County Commissioner North Eugene , Position 4 <p> The first round of unofficial results for the Lane County Commissioner , north Eugene District 4 race indicated that incumbent Pat Farr will retain his seat after receiving 60% of the vote to challenger Andrew Ross ' 39% . <p> " Looks pretty decisive , " said a confident Farr , who was celebrating with his wife , Debi , on Tuesday night . " That kind of a margin usually holds up . " <p> Farr , 65 , is seeking reelection for a third term . His previous governmental experience includes serving in the Oregon House of Representatives , on the Eugene City Council and the Bethel School Board . <p> U.S. Representative 4th District , Democrat <p> In first wave of voter returns for the U.S. House of Representatives 4th District Democratic primary released around 8 p.m . Tuesday , incumbent Rep. Peter DeFazio was seriously outpacing his opponent Doyle Canning . <p> DeFazio , who has represented the 4th District for more than 30 years , had 43,443 votes , or about 87% at 8 p.m . Canning had 6,397 votes , or about 13% , at 8 p.m .. There were 246 write-in votes . <p> DeFazio , chair of the House Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure , told The Register-Guard one of his most immediate priorities is a surface transportation bill he believes will fight climate change and generate infrastructure jobs during the pandemic recovery . <p> Canning ran on a promise of big and immediate change when it comes to the environment , including shutting down the controversial Jordan Cove liquefied natural gas pipeline project . Canning never has held elected office before . <p> US Representative 4th District , Republican <p> In a first wave of voter returns for the U.S. House of Representatives 4th District Republican primary released around 8 p.m . Tuesday , Alek Skarlatos was far out in front of his opponent Nelson Ijih . <p> Skarlatos had 19,133 votes , or 85% , as of 8 p.m . Ijih had 2,981 votes , or 13% , as of 8 p.m . There were 273 write-in ballots . <p> " We 're feeling very happy . It 's about as expected , but at the same time that did n't happen by accident . I 'm incredibly proud of my team and all the voters who showed up . I think we can carry this momentum into November , " Skarlatos said Tuesday around 8:30 p.m . <p> The winner of the Republican primary for the 4th District race will go on to face a Democrat in November when the general election will determine who goes to Congress . The seat has been held for more than 30 years by Rep. Peter Defazio , the incumbent and winner in the Democratic primary that took place Tuesday . <p> Skarlatos , a veteran of Afghanistan best known for being one of three men who stopped a would-be terrorist on a European train in 2015 , told The Register-Guard he largely is motivated to make timber harvesting in Oregon more productive and widespread . <p> Ijih , an instructor at Portland Community College , ran his campaign on ideas such as cutting down the number of requirements to obtain a college degree , reducing to zero taxes on overtime pay and to suspend payroll taxes during the pandemic . <p> The GOP 2nd District race was one of the most closely watched of Tuesday 's primary as the incumbent , Rep. Greg Walden , the only Republican in the state 's congressional delegation , is not running for a 12th term . <p> Bentz beat former 2018 Republican Oregon gubernatorial candidate Knute Buehler and former state legislator Jason Atkinson . Bentz will be favored in the November election . <p> Four Democrats sought their party 's nomination and that contest was too close to call . <p> The results Tuesday night were no surprise , as Biden is his party 's presumptive nominee . Biden outpaced Vermont Sen. Bernie Sanders and Massachusetts Sen. Elizabeth Warren , who both suspended their campaigns earlier in the year . <p> In presidential elections Oregon is a solidly blue state . the last time a GOP nominee won the state was 1984 when Ronald Reagan beat Walter Mondale . <h> Never miss a story <p> Choose the plan that 's right for you .
