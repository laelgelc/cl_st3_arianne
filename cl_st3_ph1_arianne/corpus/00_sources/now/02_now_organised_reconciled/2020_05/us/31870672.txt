The rice that my customers are typically accustomed to eating is not good for them , " says Chef JJ . " Our cashier , Kira who lives in the community , has done an incredible job of educating people about why what they put into their bodies , the quality of the food that they eat , is so important . " Chef JJ laughs , as he talks about his rice bowls and the mission behind his work . <p> Field Trip not only supports the community in its hiring decisions , but it also supports a community of farmers . " I support small rice farmers all over the country , " says Chef JJ . <p> Field Trip is also feeding front line workers during COVID-19 . " People in our community are being hit harder and they need to eat the right way , " he says . <p> High-quality Ingredients <p> " If I can get locally grown , freshly milled rice , I will take it , " says Chef JJ , who has a personal preference for locally grown black rice . " Black rice is really high in vitamin B which is really important for diabetics , which of course is a critical issue within the African American community and especially with COVID-19 .
