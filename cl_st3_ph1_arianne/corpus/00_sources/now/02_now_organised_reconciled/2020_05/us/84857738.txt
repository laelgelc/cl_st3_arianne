<h> Sen. Brown reviewed the state of our nation -- the wealthiest , most productive , most prosperous and most free nation in history -- and found it horrendous . <p> The commencement advice of Sen. Sherrod Brown ( D. , Ohio ) stood out in a sea of inspiring messages from others for its highly partisan themes ( " To the Class of 2020 : The Generation of Progressive Change , " Review , May 2 ) . Sen. Brown reviewed the state of our nation -- the wealthiest , most productive , most prosperous and most free nation in history -- and found it horrendous . We let our public-health system atrophy , tolerated gaping holes in the public-safety net and stood by while a tiny sliver at the top grabbed most of the nation 's income and left declining opportunities and stagnant wages for everyone else . <p> The glimmer of hope for our country , according to Sen. Brown , is Generation Z. The rising generation has a daunting task list : tackling the " existential issue of our times , climate change , " ensuring economic justice and electing new , diverse , competent leaders . Sen. Brown is a white , male , baby boomer of European descent who has held positions in state and federal government for 45 years , including 27 years and counting in the U.S. Congress . He has some responsibility for our sad state of affairs even before the pandemic . America is the envy of the world and the desired destination of tens of millions of people yearning for a better life and to be free , but Sen. Brown and a progressive-change generation can remake us into something so much better . One free tip for stopping climate change in its tracks : Put us into permanent pandemic lockdown and fossil-fuel use will stay depressed , thereby eliminating our existential threat for those who survive and enjoy staying in place . <p> Sean Naples <p> Asheville , N.C. <p> I do n't think I have ever read a more disheartening message to graduates as that of Sen. Sherrod Brown . I wish him the opportunity to experience an economy in shambles like Venezuela 's , for instance , or a health system like Cuba 's and maybe the lack of opportunity in Guatemala or Nicaragua . Why is it that people flee to the Western world system ? Maybe Mr. Brown would like a little more Russian influence in our elections .
