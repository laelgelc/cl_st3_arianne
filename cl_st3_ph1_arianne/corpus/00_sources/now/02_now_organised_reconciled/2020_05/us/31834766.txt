In a new Pew Research Center survey , 67 percent of adults said that it was either very or somewhat likely that the coronavirus outbreak will " significantly disrupt Americans ' ability to vote " in November . That includes 50 percent of Republicans or GOP leaners , and 80 percent of Democrats and Democratic leaners . <p> Americans also support allowing voters to cast their ballot by mail , if they want to . Seventy percent of those surveyed said that they either strongly or somewhat favor allowing those who wish to cast a ballot by mail to do so , with 44 percent strongly supporting the idea . A majority of Americans , 59 percent , are confident that the election will be " conducted fairly and accurately , " but that 's not true among Democrats . 54 percent said they are either not at all or not too confident it will be a fair and accurate election ( 4,917 American adults ; April 7-12 ; +/- 2.1 percentage point MOE ) . <p> JUST SUPER -- A group of former aides to Sanders announced that they were launching a super PAC focused on turning out progressives to back Biden . Other aides involved are Chuck Rocha , Tim Tagaris and Shelli Jackson .
