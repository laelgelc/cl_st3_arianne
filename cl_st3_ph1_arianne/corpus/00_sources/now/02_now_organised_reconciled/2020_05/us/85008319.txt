<h> Letters : Flattening the curve goal has been accomplished -- it 's time to reopen <h> Upon reopening more people will get sick and some will die , but we are now prepared to handle it . <p> IndyStar <p> I am a 55-year-old small business owner and an attorney . People in my family have recovered from COVID-19 , most others have stayed healthy . I was never too nervous about getting sick , but I am now becoming very nervous for the health of our country . I believe that if we do n't open the economy nationwide and very soon it may be impossible to recover for decades . I ca n't even imagine the number of bankruptcies that will be filed ; the number of unemployed ; the number of closed businesses ; the number of foreclosures on the horizon ; the undiagnosed illnesses that will worsen ; and the depression all that may cause if we do n't start up the economy very soon . To better understand the continued shutdown and slow reopening I recently delved into some research . I 've come away confused as to why we have n't reopened already . <p> Illness is inevitable with any disease that we have n't developed a vaccine for . This is especially true for a new disease we know almost nothing about -- hence the initial goal of the lockdown was to flatten the curve to buy time for our health system to ramp up its facilities to handle those who would have an extreme reaction to the virus . For weeks now Indiana has had only 15% to 20% of ICU beds taken by coronavirus patients and has had more than 80% of ventilators available . These stats are similar countrywide . We have accomplished the goal . Illness and death from COVID-19 is sadly inevitable . Upon reopening more people will get sick and some will die , but we are now prepared to handle it .
