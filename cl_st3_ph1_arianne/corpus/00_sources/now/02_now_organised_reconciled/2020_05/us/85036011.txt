<h> Sustainability Program Helps Businesses In Tucson Arizona <h> May 28 , 2020 6:27 pm MT <p> Tucson and Pima County have implemented multiple sustainable business programs in hopes to emerge as a sustainable city . ( Shutterstock ) <p> TUCSON -- The Green Business Leaders Program is open to businesses of all sizes in southern Arizona to help strengthen the region 's resiliency to climate change . <p> " This program will help you evaluate your business ' basic sustainability footprint , connect to local green resources , receive recognition for your leadership , and build affinity and loyalty among customers and employees who support environmentally responsible practices . Beyond the checklist , practices identified can also be used to update your business plans , operation plans , employee orientations , and marketing materials . " -- Southern Arizona Business Alliance <h> Southern Arizona Green Business Leaders certification . <p> The newly created Southern Arizona Green Business Alliance ( SAZGBA ) developed a sustainability rating certification system for businesses with one to over 500 employees in southern Arizona.According to SAZGBA they developed the program to empower businesses to adapt more sustainable business operations . <p> They created the program in partnership with Local First Arizona and the Tucson 2030 District.To qualify , businesses must be located in Pima , Santa Cruz , Yuma or Cochise county and meet the 65-point program checklist sustainable business practice criteria in several categories like " waste and procurement " and " food and events . " <p> SAZGBA will reach out to businesses that submit applications if they feel the program and the organization area suitable fit.If awarded certifications are valid for two years . They provide Green Leaders Certified Businesses with logos and promotional assets , along with an entry in the Green Leaders Directory . <h> Evolving SCALE UP and Tucson 2030 District programs . <p> The new sustainability program developed from a pilot program called SCALE UP ( Sustainable Communities Accessing Lending and Expertise Upon Performance ) . This project , which was also in partnership with Local First Arizona , aimed to help businesses leverage sustainability financial incentives , specifically regarding water , energy , transportation and waste . According to tucson.com the program was a success , helping 11 businesses cut their energy bills and make a stronger environmental impact . <p> SAZGBA also aligns with the ethos of the 2030 Districts Network . This program " unites cities " across the nation with a commitment to reduce 50 percent of their energy , water and transportation related emissions by the year 2030 . According to Tucson 's page on their website , the city 's goals with the program are to : <p> Act as a catalyst , connector and advisor to speed up the adoption of best practices , active collaboration and knowledge transfer within the district <p> Make Tucson an attractive place to work , live and enjoy life <p> Make Tucson a resilient community <p> Be sincere stewards of our natural resources <p> Work together to identify systemic barriers to conservation to create solid economic development
