<h> ' We 're going to deliver ' : Esper doubles down on pledge to produce coronavirus vaccine by year 's end <p> ' COMPLETELY CONFIDENT ' : Defense Secretary Mark Esper says the Pentagon 's effort to deliver enough COVID-19 vaccine doses to conduct a mass inoculation of Americans by the end of the year is not simply a goal -- it 's a promise . <p> " I 've spoken to our medical experts about this . We are completely confident that we can get this done , " Esper told NBC Friday . " DOD has the expertise and the capability , of course , to get the manufacturing done and the logistics , and I 'm confident that we will deliver . " <p> Pressed by NBC 's Savannah Guthrie about how he could be so certain when others , like Dr. Anthony Fauci , have cautioned it could take longer , up to 18 months , Esper said in a Today show interview that failure is not an option . <p> " You know , when Eisenhower launched the U.S. military against Nazi Germany , he did n't say , ' We might win World War II . We 'll try . ' When John F. Kennedy aspired to put a man on the moon , he did n't say , ' We 'll give it a good shot . ' He said , ' We will do it . ' " Esper said . " The Defense Department , once again , is committed to get this done . We 're going to live up to the expectations , and we 're going to deliver on this virus . " <p> NO PROMISE TO THE GUARD : Esper , a former Army captain who also served in the National Guard , stopped short of promising to correct what many see a slap in the face to the more than 40,000 Guard troops who have been serving on the front lines of the fight against the coronavirus pandemic . <p> Under the current plan , the Guard 's mission will end June 24 , one day short of the 90 days of service that would qualify them for extended benefits under federal law . Esper told the Today show that the Federal Emergency Management Agency would make the call as to whether the deployment orders would be extended . <p> " If they have a valid what we call mission assignment that 's verified by FEMA , then my view is we should extend those tours of duty , " Esper said . " I 've expressed that to the governors . I 've shared that with the White House . I think it 's the right thing to do . " <p> But Esper indicated that the decision was out of his hands . " I 'm not worried about the number of days . What I 'm worried about is making sure that we win the fight against the coronavirus and we fully support the young men and women who are serving on the streets of America in the National Guard .
