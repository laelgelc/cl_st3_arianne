" <p> A diehard defender of President Trump during the House impeachment hearings , Ratcliffe testified during his confirmation hearing earlier this month that politics would not color intelligence under his watch . <p> " If confirmed as DNI , you have my commitment to deliver timely , accurate , and objective intelligence and to speak truth to power , be that with Congress or within the administration . Let me be very clear , " he said . " Regardless of what anyone wants our intelligence to reflect , the intelligence I will provide , if confirmed , will not be impacted or altered as a result of outside influence . Above all , my fidelity and loyalty will always be with the Constitution and the rule of law , and my actions at DNI will reflect that commitment . " <p> Among the votes for Ratcliffe was North Carolina Sen.
