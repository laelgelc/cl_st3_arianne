<p> SAN FRANCISCO , May 26 , 2020 /PRNewswire/ -- The global heat recovery steam generator market size is expected to reach USD 1.6 billion by 2027 , expanding at a CAGR of 3.2% , according to a new report by Grand View Research , Inc . Shifting preference from simple cycle power plants to combined cycle power plants , with increase in adoption of energy-efficiency measures is anticipated to drive the market for heat recovery steam generator ( HRSG ) during the forecast period . <p> In past couple of years , various countries across the globe faced power shortage issues and to counter such issues , regulatory bodies have been stressing the need for advanced infrastructure for power generation . As a result , increasing number of power plants to compensate for the energy deficit is likely to be commissioned and this is expected to propel the demand for heat recovery steam generators ( HRSGs ) . <p> Key suggestions from the report : <p> The below 100 MW segment dominated the market for HRSG and accounted for a revenue share of 46.8% in 2019 <p> Industrial is anticipated to be the second largest application segment over the forecast period <p> The power and utility segment is anticipated to witness the fastest CAGR of 3.7% from 2020 to 2027 <p> In Asia Pacific , the heat recovery steam generator market is projected to grow at a substantial rate throughout the forecast period and reach USD 586.4 million by 2027 . China is expected to account for the maximum revenue share in the region <p> The increasing demand for HRSGs to product clean and green energy in the various industries has enhanced the growth of the market . Furthermore , ongoing research and technological advancements in the field of HRSG is driving the market for heat recovery steam generator in North America .
