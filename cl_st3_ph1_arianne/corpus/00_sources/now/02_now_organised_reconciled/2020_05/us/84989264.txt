<h> Follow the science <p> The coronavirus epidemic reminds us how important science is in making decisions that affect our lives . No matter how much President Donald Trump wishes -- he claimed the virus will " go away " on its own -- science tells us it will continue to burn through the population until we reach herd immunity , whether by a vaccine or direct infection . Following the science is a much safer course than ignoring it , as most Americans know . <p> I hope Americans will remember to follow the science on the less urgent , but far more important , issue of climate change . Instead of impacting us for a year or two , climate change will be with us for generations . It is already warming our winters , increasing flooding and bringing new pests to the area . <p> Like the coronavirus , early action is simpler and more effective than procrastination . It would have been easy to wean ourselves off fossil fuels if we had started 20 years ago , and we can still make the change gradually with a market-driven , economy-boosting method like the Energy Innovation and Carbon Dividend Act now in Congress .
