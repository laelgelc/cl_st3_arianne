<h> We ca n't ignore the global hunger pandemic <p> The hunger crisis resulting from the coronavirus will claim even more lives overseas in poor nations unless we take action . <p> War and climate change have already caused many countries to suffer food shortages . The coronavirus will only deepen their humanitarian crisis . The U.N . World Food Program Director David Beasley warns that , " due to the Coronavirus , an additional 130 million people could be pushed to the brink of starvation by the end of 2020 . " <p> Congress , in the next relief package , must increase global food aid to help the World Food Program , Catholic Relief Services and other agencies fight hunger . Funding should be increased for the Food for Peace program , America 's main tool in fighting global hunger . <p> Life-saving infant nutrition for Yemen , Syria , South Sudan and other countries must be expanded . Congress should increase funds for the McGovern-Dole program , which is providing take-home rations for hungry school children in impoverished nations hit by the virus .
