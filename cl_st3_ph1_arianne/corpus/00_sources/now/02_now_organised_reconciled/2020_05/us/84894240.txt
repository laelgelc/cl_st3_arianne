<h> WBUR Honored With Eight 2020 Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards <p> WBUR is one of the winningest stations in the country / Edward Murrow Awards 2020 <p> The Radio Television Digital News Association ( RTDNA ) announced the winners of the 2020 Regional Edward R. Murrow Awards honoring outstanding achievement in broadcast and digital journalism , technical expertise and adherence to RTDNA 's Code of Ethics . WBUR is the proud recipient of eight regional awards , making it the winningest large market radio station in the country . <p> WBUR 's Executive News Director , Tom Melville , congratulated the staff , recognizing the incredible work across the station with this message : <p> Again this year WBUR is one of the winningest stations in the country . We won eight regional Murrows in the large market division , putting us atop the pack of our peers in public radio across the country including KJZZ , WLRN and KUOW , with seven wins each . The eight wins come from across WBUR : <p> Shannon Dooling won two for her immigration reporting -- best investigative reporting for her work on the relationship between ICE and the Boston Police , and best continuing coverage for breaking the national story on denial of medical deferrals . Dan Mauzy was Shannon 's radio editor on both pieces . Lisa Creamer and Berto Scalese edited the medical deferral story . The digital effort on the investigative piece was a collaborative effort among Shannon , Lisa and Ally Jarmanning . <p> Ben Brock Johnson and Amory Sivertson won for the Morning Edition special series spawned by their Endless Thread podcast series , Infectious . Of course , all the iLab podcast team -- Iris Adler , Kat Brewer , Paul Vaitkus and Josh Swartz -- helped bring the work to life . <p> Robin Lubbock , along with Frannie Carr and Cloe Axelson , won for best video , based on a stirring Cognoscenti column by Frederick Hewett on climate change and its impact on rivers . <p> Martha Bebinger won best use of sound for her riveting Green Operating Room feature , which was graced by amazing photography from Jesse Costa . Dan Mauzy edited . Mike Garth did the mix . <p> Meghan Kelly won best social media for a rich and vibrant InstaStory on the MBTA . Meghan is supported in this social media work by Lisa Creamer and the entire digital team . Rich photography came from Robin Lubbock and Jesse Costa . <p> Thank you to the engineers , the BRT 's , the newsdesk staff , the journalists who work on our programs , and all the many teammates throughout WBUR who support our work . To all , congratulations . Well done . <p> As a regional award winner , WBUR will advance to the national Edward R. Murrow Awards competition . National winners will be announced later this year .
