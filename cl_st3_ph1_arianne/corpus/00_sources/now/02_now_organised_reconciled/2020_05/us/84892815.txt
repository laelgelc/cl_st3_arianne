<h> High Lake Levels Expected Through Summer <p> Lakes Michigan , Huron , Erie and St. Clair all hit record high water levels in April and they are expected to rise through the end of summer . Drew Gronewald is an associate professor at the University of Michigan 's School of Environment and Sustainability . He told Michigan News Network the past ten years have been the wettest decade in U.S. history , coupled with less evaporation and climate change . <p> " The Earth 's temperature is going up , the temperature of the ocean is going up , and those all affect the way air masses , moisture , and temperature migrate and move their way across Central and North America and the Great Lakes . " <p> Beyond the lakes , Gronewald says soil moisture in Michigan is also at a record high . That can make it harder for farmers to get successful crops from the fields to your table .
