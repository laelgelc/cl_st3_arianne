That 1.2-mile section has now been completed except for some site reclamation activity , TC Energy spokeswoman Sara Rabern said . <p> The Calgary-based company has started site work for labor camps near Baker , Montana , and Philip , South Dakota , but it has not set a date to occupy them . <p> BILLINGS , Mont . -- Major construction projects moving forward along the U.S. borders with Canada and Mexico are raising fears that the coronavirus could race through temporary camps and workers could spread it to nearby rural communities that would not be able to handle an outbreak . <p> Montana officials have not yet received plans requested from the company to make sure it can prevent the camps from spreading the coronavirus , said Erin Loranger , a spokesperson for Montana Gov . Steve Bullock .
