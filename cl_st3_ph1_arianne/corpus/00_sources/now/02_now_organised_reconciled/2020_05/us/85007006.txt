<p> Looking for a way to help the Earth -- something easy you can do even while social-distancing at home ? <p> You may have seen green bins at curbside in your Reading neighborhood on Thursday mornings . They 're part of a new way of handling food waste that 's simple for homeowners and good for the environment : industrial composting . <p> It 's easy : you put your compost bin curbside once a week and it gets picked up , just like your trash and recycling . You can put much more in the bin than in a backyard compost pile : soiled pizza boxes , bones , cheese , meat , pasta , and more . <p> Why is composting important ? When kitchen waste becomes rich compost and is dug into the earth , it returns nutrients to the soil , cutting down on the need for factory-made fertilizers . And it helps the ecosystems in the soil hold onto more carbon -- one step in the fight against climate change . <p> Industrial composting of kitchen waste also diverts tonnage from the Town 's trash stream . <p> Already a backyard composter ? You could share a bin with a neighbor and each put in the bin what you ca n't put in your own compost pile . Live in an apartment building ? Bins are available for renters , too . <p> Several companies offer curbside kitchen-waste pickup . Reading customer Louise Ward is enthusiastic about Black Earth Compost ( blackearthcompost.com ) : " It has been so much easier than what we used to do and we 're able to compost SO much more ( meats &amp; bones , dairy , grease ... ) . We had some compost delivered by Black Earth last fall and our grass seed was in heaven . "
