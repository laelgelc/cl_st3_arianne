If you see comments in violation of our community guidelines , please report them . <h> Oregon primary : Senate Seat 12 primary race is on the Democratic side <p> Return to this story Tuesday night after 8 p.m. to find out who 's winning . <p> The real primary race for Oregon State Senate District 12 is on the Democratic side , since incumbent Brian Boquist is running unopposed on the Republican side . <p> Ballots for the primary are due by 8 p.m . Tuesday , and it is now too late to mail them in on time . So they will need to be dropped off in person . <p> In the Democratic primary , Bernadette Hansen , Lisa Pool , Ross Swartzendruber and Arianna M. Blunt are vying for the chance to challenge Boquist in November . <p> The passion topics for Hansen , a community activist and caregiver who graduated from the University of Oregon , include issues involving income inequality , housing and homelessness . She 's an advocate for prioritizing mental health support to tackle the homeless issue , as well as food , shelter and social services . <p> Lisa Pool ( Photo : Courtesy of Lisa Pool ) <p> In terms of the housing crisis , Hansen wants Oregon to combine resources in the private , public , non-profit and philanthropic sectors , as well as raise the minimum wage , and make sure education aligns with the job market needs . Hansen has received $4,352.35 in contributions this year , and she has a $953.55 cash balance . <p> Pool , a licensed acupuncturist and small business owner , is a supporter of providing affordable housing and healthcare to Oregonians . <p> Pool says affordable healthcare is one way to deal with the opioid crisis because it gives people access to the care they need . Pool supports the Oregon Green New Deal in dealing with climate change and protecting the environment . Pool has received $2,555.00 in contributions this year , and she has a $961.36 cash balance . <p> Ross Swartzendruber ( Photo : Courtesy of Ross Swartzendruber ) <p> Swartzendruber , a sheep farmer who attended Chemeketa Community College , Oregon State University and Boston University , is concerned about voter rights , and he wants to enact anti-corruption legislation to address campaign finance and ethics reform . <p> Swartzendruber supports forming municipal renewable energy coops and electrification of transportation infrastructure in order to bring green jobs to rural Oregon . He also supports forming a " Digital Bill of Rights " to protect the privacy of Oregonians . He has raised $4,149.00 in campaign contributions , and he has a $551.04 cash balance . <p> Arianna Blunt ( Photo : Courtesy of Arianna Blunt ) <p> Blunt , owner of Ravenwood Magikal Arte and a graduate of Pacific University , believes racism is a bigger problem than it should be in 2020 . She also is a supporter of Medicare for all . <p> Blunt wants to create a tiny village as a transitionary phase to help homeless people until they can get themselves back to work and afford their own home . She has raised $601.74 in campaign contributions , and she no cash balance . <p> On the Republican side , Boquist has raised $15,970.00 in campaign contributions this year , plus a beginning balance of $56,557.81 from last year , and he has a $64,519.75 cash balance .
