The documentary Spaceship Earth follows the creation of the center and its use in 1991 , as well as its free-thinking inhabitants from Northern California . ( I 'll stop short of calling them hippies . ) The strange tale of these visionaries becomes a prescient warning about climate change , and it 's all documented with archival footage and interviews with those involved .
