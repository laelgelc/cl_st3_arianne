THE NRCC was up on TV , and CLF ran a digital campaign , and a mail push to get people to return their ballots . CLF only spent $700,000 in this seat . Last cycle , they routinely spent millions of dollars on special elections with mixed results . <p> THE NRCC 'S chairman , Rep.
