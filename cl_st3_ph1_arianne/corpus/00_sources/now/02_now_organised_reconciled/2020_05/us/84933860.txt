<p> Greece on Friday took over the rotating six-month chairmanship of the Council of Europe ( CoE ) from Georgia , in a ceremony that was conducted online due to public health precautions . <p> " The Greek chairmanship of the Council of Europe begins by prioritising the protection of human life , public health and human rights in the Covid-19 pandemic , " Greek President Katerina Sakellaropoulou stated . <p> She also said that the challenges of lockdowns and other safety precautions aimed at stemming the spread of the coronavirus will affect how the CoE operates , making Greece 's the first " e-chairmanship " in the history of the 47-nation body . <p> Other areas that will be at the focus of Greece 's efforts as chairman of the CoE , Sakellaropoulou added , are the efficiency of the justice systems , the rights of young people and climate change . <p> " Given the current context , Greece has chosen to focus its chairmanship on the protection of public health and responses to the health crisis , while at the same time ensuring respect for human rights , democracy and the rule of law , " the CoE said in a post on Twitter , congratulating Greece over its new role . <p> Alternate Minister of Foreign Affairs Miltiadis Varvitsiotis will be the new chair of the CoE 's Committee of Ministers .
