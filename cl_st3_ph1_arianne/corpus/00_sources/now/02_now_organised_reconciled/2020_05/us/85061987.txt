<h> Triodos UK : the ethical bank helping you use your money as a force for good <p> In her column for the Evening Standard , each month Wonderoom 's Mairead Cahill shines a spotlight on the UK 's most innovative sustainable brands . <p> This month , Cahill features Triodos Bank UK , for its pioneering approach to creating a bank where the money is being used to support positive social , environmental and cultural good across the country . <p> Here , she speaks with CEO Bevis Watts , to hear how they 're helping people use their bank accounts as a force for good , some surprising facts about how your money is used by most banks today , and inspiring projects they 're supporting from scaling up electric vehicle recharging points to farms working to support disadvantaged kids . <p> What was the inspiration behind Triodos bank ? <p> Triodos was set up to demonstrate that a bank can finance great things for people and the planet and also be profitable and successful . We want to show people the democratic power of their money and that it can be a real force for good . We give people a connection between the money in their bank accounts and what it 's being used for , as well as the security of knowing that their money is safe and secure with a longstanding and profitable bank . That is something that really sets us apart in banking , a track record of consistent profitability and growth , and a very different sustainable model . <p> To what extent are people aware how their money is being used by their bank ? <p> For the most part people still do n't know how their money is being used by their bank and what it 's supporting , as there 's still a real lack of transparency in the banking sector . For example , in the UK we 've estimated that as much as ? 16 billion could be supporting fossil fuels , just through people 's stocks and shares and ISAs alone . Switching to an ethical bank is one of the most powerful steps you can make for people and planet , yet it is often considered an afterthought . There 's a huge divide between what we say we care about and what the majority of banks we choose are funding with our savings , current accounts and pensions - tobacco , high sugar products , climate change , fossil fuels , intensive agriculture , and biodiversity loss to name a few . At Triodos , we 're giving people the power to help close that gap . <p> What makes Triodos different from most other banks in the UK ? <p> Firstly , it is how we use the money , which is only to finance things that demonstrate positive social , environmental and cultural good . We 're fully transparent about every loan and investment we make , which is very rare within the UK banking system . Another key way in which we 're different is our banking model . We do n't borrow money from other banks , and we do n't pay performance related bonuses to incentivise the wrong behaviours , so we ensure our customers and the outcomes of what the money is trying to achieve comes first . Lastly , we walk the talk ourselves and ensure that as a business we operate at the highest level of environmental and social responsibility . <p> If a consumer switches to bank with Triodos , how are they part of supporting positive change ? <p> For every ? 1 you save with Triodos , 84p is being actively lent out to organisations and projects creating positive impact across the UK . To break this down a little more , over 25 per cent of our money is supporting social housing projects , another 25 per cent is supporting renewable energy projects , 17 per cent on healthcare , and 15 per cent on a range of cultural projects . Last year in total we lent ? 980m to 800 organisations across the UK all furthering positive impact on the environment , society and culture . To bring this to life for our customers , on our current account mobile app , you can see all the projects we 've financed near you . That principle of transparency giving people the power to really look at what we 're financing is very important . <p> I think we should never underestimate how much big businesses observe data and behaviours . Big banks know exactly where people are leaving and going to . So you might think you 're a very small part of a picture of change , but increasingly every individual moving banks and voting for change , means you 're helping to shift the values of that organisation and where it thinks it needs to be . Ultimately the impact is way beyond just carbon emissions and social equality , the impact is that you are supporting bringing about real systemic change within the banking sector . <p> What are some examples of projects and organisations you lend to in the UK ? <p> We recently financed ' Pod Point ' to support the development of electrical vehicle charging infrastructure and charging points across the UK , which is exciting and delivering something at scale with positive environmental benefits . On the social side , we support and finance ' Jamie 's Farms ' who work with kids with challenging behavioural issues that are excluded from schools . That 's a very inspiring project that 's changing lives and society and giving people a better start and role models in life . On the cultural side we 've recently lent to Blackheath Conservatoire , which borders onto Lewisham , and offers scholarships and community outreach work as well as serving their paying students . <p> What is the biggest challenge you face in attracting more customers ? <p> People are generally slow to switch banks , though that is changing as people become more aware of the power of their money and their choice of banking provider as a force for good . Furthermore , our current account switching program makes it incredibly easy to switch . We achieved 14 per cent growth last year and are now top of the tables in terms of net customer gains . That 's a really positive indication , as is recently winning the British Bank Awards for customer service . <p> On a personal level , I take my inspiration and energy from the natural environment and I like to stay connected and grounded to nature . Secondly , our customers inspire me -- the way that they 're waking up to the potential for their savings and current accounts to make positive change . It is a huge privilege as the CEO of Triodos to feel we 're at the centre of a community of people who are making a difference . <p> What is your dream for the business ? <p> My dream is that we become an increasingly large and relevant part of the UK banking landscape , while remaining true to our mission and what we set out to do from the beginning . As one of my shareholders said to me early on in the job - " stay small as you grow " , which is a beautiful way of describing the importance of keeping hold of the original DNA of Triodos as we grow . <p> Triodos Bank is available for personal current and savings accounts at triodos.co.uk . <p> Follow @wonderoom.co on Instagram and join Mairead and Triodos CEO , Bevis Watts for an Instagram Live this Tuesday , June 2 at 18:30-18:50 , to hear more practical tips and insights on how you can easily be part of positive change , and some behind the scenes stories of running a pioneering business .
