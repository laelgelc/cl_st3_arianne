<h> Bernie Sanders : ' Vast majority ' of my supporters understand Trump is ' dangerous ' and will vote for Biden <p> Bernie Sanders expects most of his supporters will come around to voting for Joe Biden because , he said , President Trump is a threat to the country . <p> " Look , I think at the end of the day , the vast majority of the people who voted for me , who supported me , will understand and do understand that Donald Trump is the most dangerous president in the modern history of this country . He 's a pathological liar . He 's a racist and a sexist , a homophobe , et cetera , " the Vermont senator told ABC 's This Week on Sunday . " At the end of the day , I think they 'll be voting for Joe Biden . " <p> Sanders 's former campaign manager Jeff Weaver warned in a memo last week that the former vice president could lose to Trump in November because " a significant portion " of his supporters are " currently unsupportive and unenthusiastic " about his candidacy . <p> A poll last month showed 1 in 7 Sanders supporters did not plan to back Biden in the general election against Trump , and the former presidential candidate 's ex-press secretary has even publicly voiced opposition to the presumptive Democratic nominee . <p> Sanders acknowledged that Biden still needs to work to court his supporters by moving further Left on the minimum wage , climate change , immigration , and criminal justice . <p> " I think Joe and his staff understand that.I think they are going to reach out to our supporters and come up with an agenda that speaks to the needs of working families , of young families , of minority communities , " he said .
