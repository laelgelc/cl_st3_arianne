Despite this , the debate continues as to whether sustainability initiatives aimed at achieving the Paris Agreement Target TGT of 1.5 degrees or less of global warming may be abandoned by the fashion industry as companies scramble to avoid economic ruin . With J.Crew and British retailers Oasis and Warehouse , along with department store Debenhams filing for bankruptcy , are sustainability initiatives still a priority ? <p> Today , the Global Fashion Agenda ( GFA ) , an industry-leading non-profit advocating for public-private cooperation on sustainability in fashion , released a Covid-19 update to its annual CEO Agenda . In this update , they outline what they have deemed as six opportunities for fashion businesses ( brands , retailers , and manufacturers ) to capitalize on the Covid-19- imposed hiatus . The recommendations are underpinned by several consumer and industry surveys conducted by McKinsey and Company over the past two months , offering insights from over 6000 consumers in Europe and over 113 sourcing specialists from fashion companies around the globe . I spoke to GFA CEO Eva Kruse and GFA Chief Sustainability Officer , Morten Lehmann , to gain direct insights into not only the six opportunities but potential solutions . <p> The opportunity areas span supply chain , brand messaging , and technology implementation . Mapping social and environmental impacts along the value chain ( to reveal vulnerabilities and inefficiencies , and help navigate current and future crises ) along with building trust and brand loyalty ( to address consumer attention on fashion brands ' social practices and safety measures ) topped the list . Pivotally , and echoing previous coverage , raising the bar on supplier relationships and shift to equal partnerships is highlighted . GFA advocates for joint investment between brands , retailers , and their supply chain partners to safeguard and respect human rights , with long term strategies in place to ensure this . McKinsey 's Apparel Sourcing in the Age of COVID-19 survey reveals that 73% of sourcing executives anticipate an acceleration of companies entering closer partnerships with suppliers and reducing transactional supplier relationships Furthermore , this crisis presents the opportunity to support sustainability agendas and to pre-empt future disruptions . <p> The fourth opportunity is addressing stock levels and markdowns by scaling new business models ( rental and resale come to mind ) but also a shift in the seasonality of product drops in line with actual seasons . During our interview , Kruse pointed out the commitment already made by some brands and department stores , including Saks 5th Avenue , to selling stock in season . Will this mark an end to winter coats hitting stores in summer ? Kruse hopes so . <p> With travel-restrictions and social distancing in place for the foreseeable future , it 's perhaps no surprise that the acceleration of the digitalization of business processes is a significant opportunity . Virtual showrooms , 3D digital design for virtual sampling , and in-season retail with smaller batch sizes ( with shorter lead times , facilitated by digitalization ) will be needed to support decreasing inventory levels . 76% of the international sourcing community foresee an acceleration of digitalization to drive these changes based on the McKinsey &amp; Company Apparel Sourcing in the Age of COVID-19 survey . Lastly shaping the e-commerce infrastructure of the future is an opportunity as COVID-19 has accelerated the industry 's channel shift towards e-commerce , with an anticipated 35-40% of sales from April to September 2020 coming through online channels , based on the McKinsey Global Fashion Index ( MGFI ) . <p> PPE manufacturing during the Coronavirus ( COVID-19 ) pandemic on April 25 , 2020 in Paris , France . <p> Getty Images <p> While this update offers key guidance to CEOs , policymakers , and regulators globally , as with so many directives , it 's rarely the diagnosis and research that proves the most challenging . It 's the execution and delivery of solutions to instigate change and digital transformation that prove extremely difficult . Some cite mindset , others capital to invest in new technologies -- the question has to be , what will it cost to not heed this advice and accelerate transformation , and also , what is the blueprint for implementation ? <p> To this end , GFA is implementing a series of webinars to matchmake innovators to fashion businesses to tackle the challenges they face and implement strategies and solutions for the six opportunities . These will commence in June and the first suite of tools and solutions will tackle opportunity four , specifically , eliminating overstock . The agenda update recognises that collaboration between all stakeholders , policymakers and regulators will be needed to leverage these six opportunities . Lehmann goes as far as saying that brands should group together and approach suppliers to implement the transformations required to fortify the supply chain and their partnerships . <p> The backdrop to this discussion is the fact that many brands , retailers , and manufacturers are currently in survival mode , and as Kruse describes " may not have the bandwidth " to focus on sustainability . But she warns that sustainability is the only route to resilient , evolved business models that will sustain future blows ( as climate change continues ) , beyond the immediate crisis . Morten Lehmann added that this crisis is polarising the decision-making of fashion companies . Those in survival mode may endure the next six months , but beyond then , it will be companies prioritizing sustainability by reducing overstock , simplifying and mapping their supply chains , and investing in digital transformation who will see financial success , he explained . <p> Lehmann 's assertion is buoyed by the huge price brands are paying right now for ignoring past advice to map and streamline supply chains . These brands have propped up failing supply systems with long lead times , complex and opaque sourcing and pricing and order volumes not based on consumer demand , but on profitable quantities . Covid-19 has exposed the economic cost of doing business unsustainably .
