This is the third time she has been a finalist . <p> The prize is meant to honor people who have dedicated their lives to saving animals . Boersma 's nomination recognizes her decades of work in Argentina protecting penguins from their greatest threats , including climate change . <p> As global warming heats up the earth , penguin populations are declining . They ca n't live in weather that 's too hot , she said , recalling a time when temperatures surpassed 100 degrees and penguins dropped dead on their way to the sea . <p> " Climate change is a major problem for penguins , " Boersma said . " How do you maintain these large colonies of penguins in the midst of all of these other problems ? " <p> The second she first met penguins face-to-face , she fell in love with them . " I thought it was pretty amazing . " <p> For the next several decades , Boersma spent her time working to protect these creatures , most notably helping to redirect ship lanes farther from the shore . Large ships often left behind an oily discharge , coating the penguins who swam behind them and forcing them onto land . When a penguin ca n't swim , it also ca n't fish , and many starved . <p> Boersma has also helped establish marine protected areas in both Argentina and the Gal ? pagos . To protect penguins , she said , it is crucial to protect the waters where they fish . <p> " There 's no fish on land , " Boersma said .
