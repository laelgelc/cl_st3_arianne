( Photo by Mario Tama/Getty Images ) ***BESTPIX*** <h> Bill giving monthly payouts promising as virus takes toll <p> Like many others , I 've grappled with this thought for the past few months . Seeing people lose family and friends , familiar businesses declare bankruptcy , and life milestones pass by , it 's easy to feel helpless as our old lives disappear . <p> As we mourn the unequivocal tragedy brought by this pandemic , we should also take care to look toward the future . COVID-19 has revealed institutional shortcomings that we must address as we recover . One such issue is climate change . <p> While total economic shutdown is unsustainable , we 've seen the benefits of carbon-emission reductions as traffic and industrial activity has slowed . Additionally , with all the talk about monthly payouts as the virus takes its economic toll , another bill with similar promises could help tackle climate change : the Energy Innovation and Carbon Dividend Act , HR 763 , which will tax carbon emissions and return the revenue to the American people .
