REGISTER HERE TO PARTICIPATE . <h> TopLine <p> JUST SPECIAL -- We 're a day away from the hotly contested special election runoff in CA-25 , where Republicans may be on the verge of doing something unthinkable just months ago : flipping a California House seat , which last happened in 1998 . As of late , the jitters in competitive House special elections have come from Republicans , even if their candidate ultimately comes away with a victory . But now , Democrats are the ones downplaying expectations before votes are tallied in the matchup between Garcia and Smith . <p> " We do n't underestimate how much of a Republican-leaning district this could be in May , but that will be a different electorate in November , " Rep. Pete Aguilar ( D-Calif. ) told Campaign Pro 's Ally Mutnick , noting that the winner will serve only a limited time in Congress . " We do n't get in this to lose a race , but I do think that in November , Christy will be successful . " <p> The district is one that turned increasingly blue recently , Ally writes , which makes the closeness of the race even more remarkable . Hillary Clinton carried it by 7 points in 2016 , and now-former Democratic Rep. Katie Hill cruised to a nine-point victory in 2018 . " It is not a unique district . It is similar to many of the districts that we won in the fall , " one Democratic consultant told Ally . " This was an anti-Trump response district , and if we 're ebbing in those districts we need to find out why . We ca n't just brush it off . " President Donald Trump has not shied away from the race , either , endorsing Garcia . <p> The ballots that have already been returned in the predominantly mail-in election have favored Garcia . As of Friday , 118,000 ballots have been returned , with 44 percent from registered Republicans and 36 percent from Democrats , Paul Mitchell , the vice president of Political Data Inc. , told Ally . Mitchell also pointed out that voters 65 and older are returning ballots at three times the rate of those under 35 years old , and that Latino voters are turning out at " half the rate of white voters . " <p> Over the weekend , a fight flared up over Los Angeles County election officials ' decision to add an in-person voting center to Lancaster , one of the more diverse cities in the district . Republicans cried foul , claiming it was a decision election officials made at the urging of Democrats as Smith lagged in returned ballots . It even drew a series of tweets from Trump , who called for the votes to " not count . " <p> Democrats did urge the voting center to be added to Lancaster -- but so did the city 's Republican mayor , a Garcia supporter . " This is something the county should fix immediately , " Lancaster Mayor R. Rex Parris , who has endorsed Garcia , told LAist 's Libby Denkmann earlier in the week . " There should not be even the appearance of affecting the outcome by limiting the ability to vote . " ( Parris echoed that sentiment following the decision to add the center in an interview with the Los Angeles Daily News . ) There are a dozen voter centers total in the district , some of which were only open for some early voting and some that will be open on Tuesday . <p> Covid-19 has triggered an abundance of disparate , rapidly changing policies at the federal and state levels . The guidelines " pulls no punches in calling the casting of ballots over the internet a ' high-risk ' endeavor that would allow attackers to alter votes and results ' at scale ' and compromise the integrity of elections . The guidelines advise states to avoid it altogether or restrict it to voters who have no other means of casting a ballot .
