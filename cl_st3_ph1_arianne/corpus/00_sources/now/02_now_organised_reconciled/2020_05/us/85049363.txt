By signing in , you are indicating that you accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Statement . <p> With construction workers returning to work after lockdowns halted all but the most essential building for months in many parts of the world , we should all think about how the industry can and should exist more sustainably . <p> Human ingenuity has brought us from the simplest Bronze Age constructions to towering feats like the Burj Khalifa in Dubai , but we now need to think urgently about how the buildings where we work and live impact the world around them . <p> It is no secret that the construction industry of today has a huge environmental impact . It is only in recent years that its environmental footprint has become a priority . For centuries , innovation within the construction industry simply followed a cycle of better tools , leading to access to stronger or cheaper materials , and fewer workers being needed . <p> The construction industry is affected by many trends , many of which , such as urbanisation , may themselves slow down or otherwise change as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic . I believe that , right now , climate change is the greatest driver of all and will remain so for years . <p> Yet a new wave of innovation means that the construction industry may finally be moving with a more sustainable tide . <p> After all , construction can also be part of the answer to the climate change question . Now that the environmental toll of its most commonly used materials , such as steel and cement , is increasingly under scrutiny and the workforce is getting older and fewer in number , the industry must look for new solutions that can make it more sustainable , productive and resilient to climate change . <p> Prefabrication is one of the construction methods which has the greatest potential to promote more sustainable construction . When I say prefabrication , I am using it as a catch-all term for assembling all or part of a building in a factory or other manufacturing site , and transporting it to the construction site where the structure is to be located . This should be considered not only as a construction technique but also as an approach to transforming the entire building lifecycle . Prefabrication can address flawed construction practices that negatively affect the productivity , economics and environmental impact of the industry -- including the frequency of design changes , the industry 's lack of productivity , and poor final product quality . <p> By using more prefabrication , the construction industry can unlock many gains by reducing usage of natural resources and waste . Buildings use less materials , are built faster and have less defects . This method produces up to 90% less waste and , due to higher final quality , the resulting buildings are up to 55% more energy efficient ( according to a 2018 paper by researchers at Victoria University of Wellington ) . <p> Furthermore , prefabrication makes more use of wood than traditional construction methods , which rely more on steel and cement . Wood is renewable and represents a carbon storing material whose growing adoption alongside prefabrication techniques , particularly in high rise buildings , can help reduce buildings ' overall carbon footprint . <p> In my view , these features are true sources of sustainable long-term competitive advantage compared to traditional techniques . <p> Within this ongoing effort to reduce the environmental footprint of the value chain there are multiple interesting opportunities . These include prefabricated house developers , as the single houses prefabricated market is set to grow across the world . As this part of the market depends on specific markets and segments , ranging from highly customized luxury houses to large scale development programmes encompassing more standardized living solutions , the key success factors are slightly different in each geography and segment . In each of them , players can create profitable models , matching local taste with leading technologies and flawless operations management . <p> Pre-fabricated kitchen , bathrooms or ducted air conditioning are growing in importance to improve the productivity of projects alongside dedicated construction materials , specifically wood-based projects . At the same time , cutting-edge software is just now extensively digitalizing the construction industry -- connecting pure design to procurement and project management . Woodworking machines , from forestry to factory , will grow in number and complexity to support the value chain transformation and represent a reliable area to look for future growth . <p> As we emerge from lockdown into the new normal , I expect the construction industry to enter a new , flourishing era through the adoption of resource-efficient construction techniques , new software solutions and carefully selected materials .
