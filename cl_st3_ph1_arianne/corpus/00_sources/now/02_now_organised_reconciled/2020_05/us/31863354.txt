By signing in , you are indicating that you accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Statement . <p> On April 22 , the 50th anniversary of Earth Day , the CEO of IQAir , a tech company that specializes in tracking air quality in cities around the world , made a striking comment in an interview with CBS News . On that same date in 2019 , she said , Los Angeles had some of the dirtiest air in the country . But now , just one year later , LA 's air was among the cleanest of any major city . <p> The difference has had much to do with the enforced limits on economic activity arising from the coronavirus pandemic which , among other things , has reduced traffic . Heavily polluted air in other urban and industrial parts of the world also experienced major clearing , including Delhi and Bangkok . For the first time in nearly 30 years , people in the Indian state of Punjab could see the Himalayas 100 miles away . And wildlife has become more active in places ranging from Yosemite Park to leafy suburban neighborhoods . <p> The return of wildlife and the improvements in sight distance are only the most visible benefits of cleaner air and reduced traffic . Air pollution has also been responsible for damaging human health in communities throughout the world . According to the World Health Organization , " In the year 2016 , ambient air pollution was responsible for 4.2 million deaths . " Among those deaths , ambient air pollution was found to have led to lung cancer , chronic obstructive pulmonary disease ( COPD ) , heart disease , stroke and respiratory infection . Beyond the health impacts , air pollution is also contributing to global warming -- or climate change -- a long-lasting and increasing problem . <p> There is little doubt that the enforced shutdown of normal commerce has played a major role in the air quality improvements we 're seeing . The question is whether the temporary environmental gains from this unintended experiment in disease containment can be extended . <p> Eventually , commerce will once again return , but business is likely to have a different look than it did before . That altered business environment will become the " new normal . " However , I believe that certain aspects of that new normal may , as a result of what we 've learned , will prove to be better than before . <p> As workplace activity resumes , those of us who have the privilege of being corporate leaders need to take a close look at what we 've learned . As the chief marketing officer for a company that offers a remote connectivity platform , I believe establishing technology-enabled best practices is a good place to start . By doing so , we can make a huge difference in the lives of our people as well as our environment . We can bring people closer together and , at the same time , enhance the productivity of our workforce . <p> Here are several steps to take : <p> * Establish weekly work-from-home days for every employee of the company . While this would look different for each organization , alternating in-office and out-of-office days or weeks for employees in adjacent workstations would increase personal distance , reduce parking needs , and with hoteling shared , or unassigned workspaces , reduce office space requirements . <p> * Enable all employees to work from home without losing productivity or functionality . Provide employees who are able to work from home with company-owned and configured laptops , mobile devices and subsidized connecting services . Formulate device use and supervisory policies for personnel . <p> * Assure the integrity of your company 's data and IP while your workforce works from home . Make sure your IT departments roll out the latest in remote access and endpoint protection technologies to allow your workforce to work effectively from either company-issued hardware or their own computers without creating situations where malware gets infiltrated into your network . The right remote access technologies can enable employees with a faster and more efficient work experience and also prevent them from having direct access to files and data that can be misused by ill-intentioned employees . <p> * Restrict employee travel and establish a maximum of on-the-road days per month . Depending on the job function , you can explore remote meeting and collaboration options as a regular way of working . For field service workers , the latest in augmented reality instructions and support technologies can significantly reduce travel and let individuals do what previously needed multiple experts on site . Consider incentivizing the use of technology to further reduce travel requests . <p> * Initiate regular cleaning and enhanced air filtration processes in your offices . For rented/leased office space , confer with your landlord about capital improvements and negotiate enhanced cleaning procedures with your housekeeping service . Make sure cleaning is done in between the workday and that self-sanitation supplies are available around the workplace . <p> * Define supply days at your workplace to consolidate the number of trips to the office your suppliers need to make . Reducing delivery trips can benefit the supplier as well as the recipients . Discuss how to achieve this with your key vendors and consider consolidating your sources of office supplies . <p> By taking even a few of these steps , you can help get your company back on track faster and also help to reduce its carbon footprint . Now , imagine that same reduction in carbon footprint applied to every company and to every organization that does n't need its workforce on site in order to function smoothly -- and being able to achieve it without sacrificing economic productivity .
