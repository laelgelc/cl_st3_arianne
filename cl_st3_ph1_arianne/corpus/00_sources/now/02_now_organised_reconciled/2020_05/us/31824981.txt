<h> Owens Corning Announces Pricing of $300 Million Senior Note Offering <p> May 07 , 2020 05:19 PM Eastern Daylight Time <p> TOLEDO , Ohio-- ( BUSINESS WIRE ) --Owens Corning ( NYSE : OC ) today announced that it has priced $300 million in aggregate principal amount of a new series of its unsecured 3.875% senior notes due 2030 in an underwritten public offering under its effective shelf registration statement . The senior notes were offered at a price to the public of 99.891% of par . <p> Owens Corning intends to use the net proceeds of the offering for general corporate purposes . <p> The closing of the senior notes offering is expected to occur on May 12 , 2020 , subject to satisfaction of customary closing conditions . <p> A registration statement relating to the senior notes being offered has been filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission and has become effective . The offering may be made only by means of a prospectus supplement and the accompanying prospectus .
