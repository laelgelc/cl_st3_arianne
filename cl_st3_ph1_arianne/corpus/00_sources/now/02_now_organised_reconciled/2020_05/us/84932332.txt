<h> Tropical Storm Arthur forms , 1st named storm of season <p> MIAMI ( AP ) -- Tropical Storm Arthur formed off the coast of Florida on Saturday , making it the sixth straight year for a named storm to develop before the official June 1 start of the Atlantic hurricane season . <p> The storm 's center was located at 11 p.m. , about 190 miles ( 305 kilometers ) northeast of Cape Canaveral , Florida , the U.S. National Hurricane Center in Miami said . Arthur has top sustained winds of 40 mph ( 65 kph ) and is moving to the north-northeast at 13 mph ( 20 kph ) , officials said . <p> A tropical storm watch remained in effect for parts of the North Carolina coast . Forecasts say Arthur will stay well offshore of Florida and Georgia on Sunday and then approach the North Carolina coast on Monday , where it will drop between 1 and 2 inches of rain Sunday night and Monday . <p> Dangerous coastal surf conditions and rip currents are expected to spread northward from Florida to the mid-Atlantic states during the next few days . <p> While there may be a component of warming waters and climate change in other pre-June storms , Arthur is more of a subtropical storm system than a traditional named storm and its water is cooler than what 's usually needed for storm formation , said Colorado State University hurricane researcher Phil Klotzbach . <p> A lot of these out-of-season storms are weak fleeting ones that meteorologists can see now because of satellites and better technology and would have been missed in earlier times , Klotzbach said . Like most earlier-than-usual storms , Arthur is likely to remain offshore , but could come relatively close to North Carolina 's coast Monday , Klotzbach said . <p> Hurricane season officially starts June 1 . <p> Local forecasters in the Bahamas said showers have lingered over the islands of Grand Bahama and Abaco , which are still struggling to recover after being hit by a Category 5 hurricane last year . <p> However , no flooding has been reported as the depression swirls just northwest of the archipelago and is expected to head into open ocean as it strengthens . <p> Officials said they were prepared to evacuate patients currently housed in tents in Grand Bahama after Hurricane Dorian damaged the island 's hospital , but forecasters said the bulk of thunderstorms are located north and east of the depression and are not expected to affect the region . <p> The tropical storm watch was issued for parts of North Carolina 's coast , from north of Surf City to Duck , including Pamlico and Albemarle Sounds .
