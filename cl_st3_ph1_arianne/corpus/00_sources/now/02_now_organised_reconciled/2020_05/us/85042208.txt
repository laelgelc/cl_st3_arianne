<h> More News <p> Fans of The Vampire Diaries could n't be more psyched about Ian Somerhalder and Paul Wesley 's latest project ! If you are 21 years or older in the United States or legal drinking age in your home country please Go to : Brothersbondbourbon.com to sign up . We ca n't wait to share with you !
