( Photo : Micaela Go ) <p> RICHMOND , Ind . -- When Cyrus Buckman came over to the United States in 2014 from Ghana , it was his first time traveling outside of his home country . Buckman said he did n't know what to expect other earning a degree from Earlham College . <p> He participated in the college 's inaugural global health off-campus excursion to Peru , where he contributed to public health campaigns for families living in underserved communities and also earned a funded internship from Earlham at the University of Louisville Medical Center during his four years , but perhaps his most rewarding experience came right here in Richmond . <p> First Presbyterian Church on North 10th Street is where Buckman said he spent the majority of his four years as a student in Richmond . It 's also where he found his best friend . <p> " One of my friends who attended the church invited me , so I went and loved the community I met over there , " Buckman said . " That 's where I met Lynn and he 's been a great system of support . " <p> It 's not a friendship you see every day . Buckman is a 2018 college graduate from Ghana while Lynn Kennedy is a 74-year-old churchgoer . In fact , the word friend does n't even do the pair 's relationship justice . <p> Nowadays , Buckman and Kennedy greet each as " dad " and " son . " <p> " I 'm 74 years of age and Cyrus is 22 so it should 've been more ' grandpa ' than ' dad , ' " actually , " Kennedy said with a laugh . " We realized that we appreciate one another . We just have that appreciation for another as people and our spirits seem to enjoy the company of certain people who make you feel lighter , better and always upbeat . Many people in today 's world seem like they 're down , do n't have much good to say , think or smile very much and Cyrus is the opposite of that . " <p> Buckman and Kennedy said they do n't know exactly when they began greeting each other as such , but the " dad-and-son " relationship blossomed at the church and continues today even from eight hours away . <h> Stay informed <p> Support local journalism and read more stories like this one by becoming a Pal Item subscriber . <p> After graduating last May with a degree in biochemistry , Buckman was hired as a project coordinator with the Global Environment and Technology Foundation , the same Virginia-based nonprofit he interned with during the summer following his sophomore year at Earlham . The organization advances sustainable development in African nations through partnerships and targeted action in the areas of clean energy and climate change , water and sanitation , sustainability and health systems . <p> After going from Ghana to Richmond and Virginia , Buckman 's next endeavor will take place in The Golden State . <p> Buckman has been accepted into Stanford University 's prestigious Knight-Hennessy Scholarship program where he will be an M.D. candidate at Stanford School of Medicine beginning in the fall . He is the first Knight-Hennessy Scholar from Ghana and the first Earlham graduate chosen for the program . <p> " Disbelief and extreme gratitude is how I 'd describe my reaction to the news , " Buckman said . <p> Knight-Hennessy Scholars receive a free tuition scholarship for up to three years of study , including stipends to offset the cost of living and travel . The scholars in this 100-person cohort hold passports in 26 different countries and will pursue degrees in 39 programs at Stanford . <p> As someone who aspires to work in the global health sector to improve access to healthcare for people in underserved communities , Buckman was looking for exactly this kind of chance to collaborate with bright minds in different fields . <p> " I 'm very well aware that such work is societal , " Buckman said . " If I wanted to go to Ghana to improve healthcare access for people over there , I need to have some background information about insurance and what economic policies can be put in place to ensure that all people are insured . So having this community of people who are all coming in with different perspectives who I can regularly bounce ideas off is why it 's a great fit me for given my interest in pursuing global health . " <p> Buckman has accomplished plenty in almost six years since leaving Accra , Ghana , but the Earlham graduate hopes others will ultimately benefit from the work he 's put in . Whether Buckman stays in California after earning his M.D. or returns to his home country , he 'll always have a friend in Wayne County . <p> Better yet , Buckman will always remember Kennedy and Richmond for treating him as one of its own . <p> " It 's definitely a blessing , " Buckman said . " Being in a place where people welcomed me with open arms was something that warmed my heart and allowed me to see that , yes , we may come from different backgrounds , but at the end of the day , it 's the love and support we show that matters .
