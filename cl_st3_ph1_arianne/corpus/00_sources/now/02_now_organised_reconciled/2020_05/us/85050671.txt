<h> Coronavirus : Pandemic shines light on complexities we face as a society <p> Re : " Could the coronavirus reset society ? " May 20 , Northwest : This piece is a master class in articulating the complexities we face . In highlighting government , the arts , technology , inequity , business , health and race , the author demonstrated how it 's all interconnected . <p> As a teacher , I faced these complexities when motivating students to give and be their best . I search for answers to big problems by listening , trying to understand other perspectives , and figuring out how I can be involved in solutions . <p> My own politics seem personal compared to my drive to help our country depolarize , unite around shared values , and act on the mass injustices of our collective history and current , multifaceted situation . As a millennial concerned about climate change , I learned there is no silver bullet that can fix the past , present or future .
