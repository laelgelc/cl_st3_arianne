<p> As the crow flies , Wildcat Falls near Watersmeet and the Upper Wisconsin River Legacy Forest near Land O'Lakes are only 15 miles apart , on opposite sides of the Michigan-Wisconsin border . <p> But in some ways , these protected places could n't be more different . <p> From one , water flows north to Lake Superior . From the other , it flows south , eventually to the Gulf of Mexico . <p> Huge old-growth trees dominate the area near Wildcat Falls , while a young forest supporting threatened species is common near the Upper Wisconsin River . <p> But they do have one thing in common . <p> The same man , Joe Hovel , made sure they were protected . <p> As he nears it , walking through the woods , the sound of Wildcat Falls appears to Hovel long before its sight . <p> He 's walked through these woods countless times , so finding the falls is no longer a surprise . Even so , it 's no less sweet , he says , paraphrasing a friend 's description . <p> Joe Hovel , with his wife , Mary , in the background . <p> Credit Dan Dumas/Kim Swisher Communications <p> " Wildcat Falls is a place where one can find spiritual harmony through deep discernment , " says Hovel . <p> The 25-foot waterfall cascades over several levels . <p> The rare old-growth forest that surrounds it features hemlock , white pine , and cedar . <p> Exposed rock faces jump off the landscape nearby . <p> " The property itself is an ecological marvel , " Hovel says . " It is n't just the vernal pools , it is n't just the rock outcrops , it is n't just the waterfall , it is n't just the old growth , it 's the fact you 've got all of these things together . " <p> Wildcat Falls , as seen from above . <p> Credit Dan Dumas/Kim Swisher Communications <p> That excitement led Hovel , in his capacity with the non-profit organizations Partners in Forestry and Northwood Alliance , push to protect Wildcat Falls and keep it open to the public . <p> He spent the better part of a decade fighting and negotiating with the Ottawa National Forest and private landowners to make it happen . <p> But now , success has finally arrived , and the land will be preserved as a community forest . <p> " What 's ten years in the life of an old-growth hemlock or those rock outcrops ?
