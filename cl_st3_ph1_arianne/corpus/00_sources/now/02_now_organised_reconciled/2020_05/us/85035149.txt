Its CEO , Elon Musk , said he named the spacecraft after the 1963 song " Puff , the Magic Dragon " as a response to critics who consider his space flight projects to be impossible . However , those of us who connect this song to smoking pot -- an activity of Musk 's -- believe that was the real reason for the capsule 's name . <p> Takeshi Hashimoto , Foster City <p> Invest in public lands <p> Regarding " Let 's invest in natural resources " ( Editorial , May 24 ) : As the nation emerges from the socioeconomic and human tragedy of COVID-19 , let 's put people to work repairing and restoring America 's incomparable public lands . <p> While federal funding is certainly needed , a 21st century Conservation Corps already exists . Comprising organizations like the Student Conservation Association , the California Conservation Corps , American Conservation Experience and more than 130 corps around the country , we can collectively employ thousands upon thousands of young people in service to public lands and communities . They would reduce the risk of wildfire to entire communities by reducing fuels and assisting with prescribed burns . They would work alongside carpenters , electricians , plumbers , painters and paving contractors to repair campgrounds , trails , water systems , buildings , bridges and boat ramps . In California , on our 18 national forests alone , well over 1,600 developed recreation sites are in need of repair or heavy maintenance . America can come together when Americans are working . We can honor the country by investing in public lands to create private jobs .
