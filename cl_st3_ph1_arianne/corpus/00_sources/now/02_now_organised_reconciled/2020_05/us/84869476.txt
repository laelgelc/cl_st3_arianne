<h> Are There Any Positive Effects of Global Warming ? <h> More by Marilyn <p> We constantly hear about the negative effects of global warming . But wo n't there be positive effects ? <p> -- Terry Kirch , Ormond Beach , Florida <p> Yes , but not for most people alive now . Here 's why : The planet has been warming for thousands of years , which has largely made possible the rise of civilization . The problem is that any change -- either warming or cooling -- that takes place in decades ( rather than over centuries or millennia , which is more likely with natural change ) will cause widespread damage to the world we know and the ways of life we have built . That 's why the term " global warming " is being replaced by the term " climate change .
