As we shape the Utility of the future , this community will help you keep up with the latest developments . <h> Post <h> 3 Ways Energy Efficiency Has Improved Over the Last Couple of Years <p> Over the last couple of years or so , improvements in energy efficiency have largely been due to advancements in technology . New gadgets allow us to monitor and automate everything about our lives for more optimal efficiency . Appliances and vehicles are using less and less electricity . Despite the current administration 's efforts to roll back regulations on the emissions and energy standards , we are moving toward cleaner or renewable energy sources that cut our greenhouse gas emissions and make our consumption less detrimental to our environment . <h> 1 . The Clean Power Plan <p> The Clean Air Act -- introduced by Congress in 1970 to help reduce the global impact of air pollution nationally -- was one of the most comprehensive pieces of environmental legislation in the world when it was signed . Since its enactment , it has seen dozens of revisions , and the Clean Power Plan was an Obama-era revision that set lofty goals to address the effects of economic growth on the environment . <p> Many of the regulations put in place by the Clean Power Plan have gone into effect over the last couple of years despite the Trump administration 's efforts to roll them back ; but some of them have been delayed or reversed in the courts in favor of Donald Trump 's more lenient Affordable Clean Energy Rule . <p> One of the ways technology has directly affected the way we ensure clean air for communities surrounding major industrial , construction or remedial sites , is through improvements in monitoring air quality . When we know the particulate matter being released into the air by an activity , we can take steps to mitigate . <p> We used to do this monitoring manually , with filters that technicians regularly analyzed for results over a period of time . This method , while accurate , did not provide the ability to pinpoint specific activities that caused a spike in particulate matter dispersal . Now , we have automated monitoring that allows for much more individualized controls . The computerized systems can help us pinpoint when higher levels of pollution are happening so we can respond more directly to the cause . <h> 2 . Good to the Last Watt <p> Not only are we finding green energy sources like wind , water and solar , but we are also finding ways to capture and reuse energy that would otherwise go to waste . This is a largely untapped area of energy conservation and efficiency , as we have mostly been focused on finding larger sources of energy that will completely replace fossil fuels . The impact of small , incremental changes , however . can add up . A mustard seed is tiny , but a mustard tree is mighty . <p> Combined heat and power systems increase energy efficiency by up to 25 percent , by capturing the heat produced from generating electricity . As of right now , these systems can only be used to heat and cool buildings in close proximity to power plants , but they are a major advancement in the ability to make the most of every bit of energy we use . <p> Another approach to capturing energy from unlikely places , is through radio waves . The light-emitting rectifying antenna ( LEctenna ) can capture energy from devices that emit radio waves ( like nearby wifi routers ) and redirect it to power low-energy electrical devices . As of right now , its area of use is mainly for applications in space , like satellites and drones . With rapid changes in technology , we are sure to see more day-to-day applications within the next decade . <h> 3 . Smart Home Technology <p> " Hey Google , turn off the lights . " There 's a phrase no one imagined saying more than a couple of years ago , yet here we are . We now have devices that we can control with the touch of a button , through our phones or with our voices . Some devices are even automated either through timed settings or IFTT ( if this then that ) technology . While the aim of smart home devices is to make our lives more convenient , they also make our energy use more efficient . <p> Learning thermostats gather the temperatures at which you are most comfortable , then build a schedule around that . They set your home temperature to use less energy while you are away , and use your schedule or your GPS location to determine when it is time to start bringing your home back to your desired temperature . <p> Homes with lots of windows are appealing , and they can make use of natural energy to keep you comfortable all year . The trick is making good use of your curtains and blinds . In the winter , letting the sunlight into your home can help keep rooms warmer while using less energy . You want to close your blinds when the sun goes down or behind the clouds , though , to keep the heat from escaping . In the summer , however , you want to keep blinds closed during the day to avoid the sunlight warming your house too much . Smart blinds do all the work for you , automatically adjusting to maximize the efficiency of your home . <p> Smart lights are more energy efficient on a couple of levels . First , they make use of LED light bulbs -- the most energy efficient currently on the market . Second , these are much like the smart thermostat , learning your schedule and setting the mood for when you are home and when you are away . If you happen to forget to turn off the lights when you leave , you can use your phone to turn them off from wherever you are . <h> Final Thoughts <p> There is currently an awful lot of back and forth about energy regulations in the United States , but hopefully we will soon be able to move toward a more eco-friendly way of being that everyone can be happy with . Unsurprisingly , the coronavirus keeping us at home has had profound effects on our planet , making the air so clear that our ability to capture solar energy has increased since quarantines have gone into effect . Obviously , this is not a practical solution to climate change . But maybe we will be able to take some of the things we learn from this time inside , and put them into action when we can all go out again . <p> Regardless of where the pandemic takes us , we can be reassured by the fact that technology keeps propelling us toward more energy efficiency at a faster and faster rate every day . <h> Thank Suzie for the Post ! <p> Energy Central contributors share their experience and insights for the benefit of other Members ( like you ) .
