" <p> -- Students For Fair Admissions , which is led by longtime anti-affirmative action activist Edward Blum , filed its opening brief in February with the 1st Circuit Court of Appeals . The group argued that the lower court judgment should be reversed because Harvard " imposes a racial penalty on Asian-American applicants , " " engages in racial balancing " and " has not considered race-neutral alternatives in good faith -- let alone availed itself of workable alternatives . " <p> -- Now that Harvard 's response has been filed , groups will be able to file briefs in support of the university . Those groups can file them within the seven days following Harvard 's submission deadline on Thursday . <p> -- The case is widely believed to become the Supreme Court 's next opening to potentially ban affirmative action . The Trump administration has also chimed in on the legal battle , siding with SFFA . <p> HOUSE DEMOCRATS SCALE BACK STUDENT LOAN DEBT RELIEF PLAN : Citing concerns over the cost of the program in their latest relief legislation , H.R. 6800 ( 116 ) , House Democrats revised their proposal to limit the $10,000 of student loan forgiveness only to borrowers who were " economically distressed " on March 12 -- the day before President Donald Trump declared a national emergency because of the coronavirus . The new restriction would more than cut in half the number of Americans eligible for student loan relief under the legislation . <p> -- Student borrowers would be deemed " economically distressed " if their monthly payment under an income-based repayment plan was zero , as of March 12 . In general , only very low income borrowers qualify for zero-dollar monthly payments . They could also qualify if , on March 12 , they were delinquent or in default on their loans or in a forbearance or deferment based on financial hardship . <p> -- The changes , which would apply both to federal and private student loans , were outlined in a manager 's amendmentposted as the House Rules Committee was set to consider the bill ahead of a possible floor debate today . <p> -- Student loan forgiveness has largely been a non-starter with congressional Republicans , and the scaled-back plan is likely to irk progressive lawmakers , who were already pushing for more generous student debt relief .
