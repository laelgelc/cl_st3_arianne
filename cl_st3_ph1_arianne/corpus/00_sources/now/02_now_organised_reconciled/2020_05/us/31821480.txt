After the 2018 election , a tense balance of power between the regions collapsed when Republicans lost control of the state Senate , which they dominated for most of the last century . <p> ( SOUNDBITE OF ARCHIVED RECORDING ) <p> ANDREA STEWART-COUSINS : This is incredible . <p> MANN : Senator Andrea Stewart-Cousins , a Democrat from downstate , became the new Senate majority leader , the first African American woman to hold that post . <p> MANN : Stewart-Cousins and fellow Democrats began pushing more progressive legislation , including new gun control measures and a sweeping climate change bill .
