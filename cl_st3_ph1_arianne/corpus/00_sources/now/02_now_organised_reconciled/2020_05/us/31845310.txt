<h> Long-gestating ' Snowpiercer ' series finally makes it to the end of the world <p> After five years , two showrunners and at least as many flip-flops between networks , the series " Snowpiercer " is finally airing . <p> The much anticipated show is based on " Parasite " director Bong Joon-ho 's 2013 movie and the 1982 French graphic novel . <p> Advertisement <p> The post-apocalyptic thriller , starring Daveed Diggs and Jennifer Connelly and premiering Sunday on TNT , begins years after the world froze over in a failed attempt to cure global warming , with all known survivors relegated to a caste system aboard a non-stop train . <p> " They 've been put in this extraordinary position of being put in this metal tube that 's hurtling around the world , " Alison Wright , who plays hospitality crew member Ruth , told the Daily News . " Everyone on that train has lost everything . The majority of characters have lost everything they knew and everything they loved . " <p> Wright , best known for her role as Martha Hanson on " The Americans , " was originally cast in 2017 as Lilah Anderson , a wife and mother who works at the nail salon in the fourth-class cabin . In the version of " Snowpiercer " that 's finally making it to air , Lilah no longer exists , nor does the nail salon or the fourth-class cabin . <p> Instead , she 's Ruth , who has positioned herself as second in command behind Melanie Cavill ( Connelly ) , who is the voice of the train and of Mr. Wilford , the mysterious benefactor and creator of the Snowpiercer . <p> Wright said that " Orphan Black " co-creator Graeme Mason , who took over as showrunner in February 2018 , approached her about the character change early on . She jumped at the chance . <p> " Snowpiercer " is a dark , terrifying environmental experiment about the dangers of climate change , but it pairs that with a murder mystery , a high-speed thrill ride and a case study about capitalism . <p> The train is beautiful in its 1,001 cars , each with a different purpose . Some are bedrooms , some kitchens and dining halls . There 's a nightclub , a school and an aquarium . <p> In first class , the passengers are lavished ; barely noticing that they 're on a train headed for nowhere , with their luxury meals and accommodations . Moving farther back , the Snowpiercer 's social ecosystem grows dimmer until the tail section , where its passengers , who boarded illegally , are stuffed into cramped quarters , forced to beg for work and food . <p> People in the tail fight for scraps of food and to escape punishment , doled out by Ruth - who freezes people 's limbs and then smashes them to dust with a sledgehammer . It 's a delicate balance that has kept the train running for years , but one whose upset could spell the end of the world . That 's the threat that keeps everyone , from first class to the tail , in line . <p> But leaving the tail , whether by promotion or circumstance , like the murder that Layton ( Diggs ) is dragged up-train to solve , is considered treason by the others in his lot , a betrayal of their way of living . <p> Layton ( Daveed Diggs ) is pulled out of the Snowpiercer 's tail to solve a murder on the train . ( TNT ) <p> The series was already renewed for a second season in May 2019 , a year before the pilot 's premiere , which Wright said puts them in a " pretty nice position . " And the show is debuting in the middle of a pandemic that , while not quite the same as a global freeze , has changed the world as we know it . <p> " It 's a little bit of a warning in a way that if we do n't shape up here and now today , " Wright told The News , " we could end up like that . "
