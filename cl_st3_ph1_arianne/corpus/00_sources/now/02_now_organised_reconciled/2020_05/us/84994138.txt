<h> The 1975 's Latest Album Notes On A Conditional Form Has Arrived <p> At long last , the 1975 's album Notes On A Conditonal Form has arrived . Originally supposed to drop on April 24th , at midnight on Friday , May 22nd Matt Healy and his band have officially returned with a 22-track album . The band released the music video for their meme-heavy single " The Birthday Party " this past February . The album includes features from classic artists like Phoebe Bridgers and FKA Twigs , but also includes an unexpected guest vocal from none other than Greta Thunberg . Thunberg opens the album with a speech about climate change , underscored by 1975 's instrumentals . It 's eerie and oddly moving . Stream The 1975 's Notes On A Conditional Form and check out Greta Thunberg 's musical debut .
