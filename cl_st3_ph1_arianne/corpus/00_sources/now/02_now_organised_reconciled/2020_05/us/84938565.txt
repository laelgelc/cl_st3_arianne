<h> Letter : State would be fortunate to have Feltes as governor <p> Published : 5/17/2020 12:01:10 AM <p> I wholeheartedly support Dan Feltes to be our next governor . Dan turned down jobs at corporate law firms to work as a legal aid attorney in New Hampshire , right out of law school and up until serving in the state Senate . There , he listened to the struggles of everyday people all around New Hampshire ; he responded and fought for them against big corporate interests and against Wall Street banks . Then , in the state Senate he continued that fight for systemic change for ordinary folks , getting a lot done . <p> He 's received over a dozen awards for his bipartisan leadership on key issues facing our state . He knows the impact of homelessness , our housing crisis , our behavioral health crisis -- he 's seen it , he 's been there , and he 's lifted people and families up , oftentimes when they had no one else in their corner . <p> Dan has been the leader on combating climate change at the State House and he secured the biggest public school funding increase in decades , including finally full-day kindergarten . <p> There 's much more work to do , and Dan is creative and adept at finding innovative ways to make progress for all of us , including much fairer taxes and more equitable school funding . At such a terrible time in our politics , we are all so fortunate that Dan is serving . Dan 's got a great heart , he cares deeply about ordinary folks , he gets things done and I would be overjoyed if he were our next governor .
