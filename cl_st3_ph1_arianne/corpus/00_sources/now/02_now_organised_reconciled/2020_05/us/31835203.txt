<h> Which stories did the media ignore this year ? <h> From climate change to the violation of civil liberties , we look at some of the under-reported stories of 2012 . <p> There are stories that have enormous consequences on the lives of Americans but are regularly under-reported or misrepresented by the mainstream media . <p> Project Censored , the US media watchdog group , has released their annual report examining the shortcomings of reporting in 2012 . <p> Amongst the key topics : the rising police state and the erosion of civil liberties , climate change and the destruction of oceans , and the rising disparity between the one percent and the 99 percent . <p> Project Censored argues that the lack of proper reporting on issues like these by the corporate media is leading to an erosion of democracy . <p> So what were the key stories ignored by the US mainstream media in 2012 ? <p> To discuss this , Inside Story Americas , is joined by guests : Mickey Huff , the director of Project Censored , and Greg Mitchell , a media analyst for TheNation magazine and author of the book : Tricks , Lies , and Videotape.
