Both the National Forest Service and NC State Parks urge the public to be cautious during outdoor recreation and observe all social distancing guidelines . <h> ' Keep people moving ' <p> Many park visitors come with a specific destination in mind -- a waterfall or scenic overlook where they can enjoy an afternoon outdoors . But people should n't anticipate spending a day at the parks right now , Hall said . <p> " We 're going to try to keep people moving from those spots , " she said . " We do n't want people to hang out in the parks just yet , as we want to give people the opportunity to get out and move and exercise . " <p> Then , " Get home and give someone else an opportunity to get to the parks , " Hall said . <h> Record crowds , possible re-closure <p> The public needs to understand that if visitors are n't diligent about safety measures , the parks will be re-closed as necessary , Hall said . <p> " It 's going to be chaotic , " she said . " If the situation gets unsafe -- if park staff feel unsafe , if visitors feel unsafe , people are being disrespectful -- we 're going to close each park as needed .
