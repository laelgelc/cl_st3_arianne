<h> Online press briefing - the future of coral <p> Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology Graduate University ( OIST ) invites reporters to a private online briefing with two scientists who are looking at the impacts of climate change through their coral reef research . <p> The two scientists , from Okinawa and Florida , will provide insights into current global environmental issues such as ocean warming , acidification , heatwaves and changes to coral reef ecosystems .
