<p> BEULAH -- Benzie Conservation District held its annual Spring Seedling Sale this year despite delays and reconfiguration of protocol based on the current coronavirus quarantine and state orders . <p> Although the staff worked from home , coordinated a delivery and curbside service , and wrangled with vendors who were constrained because of the pandemic -- the spring fundraising event attracted more customers and orders than the district has seen in over 10 years . Seedlings sold and distributed were estimated at over 17,500 trees and shrubs . <p> " We were humbled with the overwhelming response and cooperation from our community to help our seedling sale go forward despite the complications from the pandemic situation , " Benzie Conservation District Executive Director Tad Peacock . <p> This year the conservation district added six new species for a new project called Assisted Tree Range Expansion Program ( ATREP.net ) . This program helps native trees migrate north to coincide with climate change . <p> Part of the program includes customers helping ATREP track the progress of the seedlings that were planted . The district plans to continue offering these species and hopefully others in the future .
