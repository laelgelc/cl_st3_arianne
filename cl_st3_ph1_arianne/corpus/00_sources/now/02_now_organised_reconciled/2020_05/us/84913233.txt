Unless we are going to suggest a draconian approach to reduce the population ( such as abortion ) , we need to reduce our " heat creation " and pollution per capita . CO2 is not a pollutant and it does not " trap or block " heat ! It is hotter when created and remains so until it cools . The more we put into the atmosphere the warmer the atmosphere gets , and it stays warmer longer because the thermal gradient is smaller . The denser polluted atmosphere also absorbs more energy from the sun . This affect cools rapidly if the driving force ( more hot gases/particles ) is removed . Hotter climates cool slower than cool ones . You can observe this in the desert where very hot days can become very cool nights when the sun goes down . There is low-moisture and the sandy soil reflects more sunlight that paved roads and dark roofs which turn it into heat . <p> Water vapor/CO2 increase represents more humidity and CO2 . This increase is an overall benefit for plant growth . It is especially favorable for our food supply . All " stuff " added to the atmosphere will convert some sunlight ( IR ) to heat . The concentration in the atmosphere tracks the heat sources producing it . <p> GHG theory alarmists claim there are catastrophic , unrecoverable temperature points coming quickly as these gases accumulate in the atmosphere . They use the so called " hockey stick " global temperature increases as their evidence . The theory says that as the GHG accumulates the rate of temperature increase accelerates .
