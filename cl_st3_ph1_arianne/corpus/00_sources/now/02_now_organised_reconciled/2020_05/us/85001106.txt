The Flynn case in federal court is a sad example of political forces attempting to undo dedicated public service by the FBI and federal prosecutors . <h> Trump 's wrong about the WHO <p> President Donald Trump is threatening to cut America 's support of the World Health Organization , claiming the WHO ignored early signs of the coronavirus and was too deferential to China . The same could be said about Trump , even more so . <p> The medical and intelligence communities offered multiple warnings to Trump , starting in January , that the outbreak would come to the United States . Instead of preparing for this , Trump downplayed the risk for weeks and made all sorts of false claims and predictions -- such as asserting the virus would just miraculously disappear . <p> Advertisement <p> Trump complains that the WHO did n't declare a global public health emergency until Jan. 30 , yet Trump did n't declare a national emergency for the U.S. until March 13 . <p> Trump also spent more than two months supporting China 's handling of the outbreak . In a January tweet , Trump praised China 's " efforts and transparency " in containing the virus . In a February interview with the Fox Business Network , Trump declared , " China is very , you know , professionally run in the sense that they have everything under control . " <p> Regardless of whatever mistakes China and the WHO made , other nations managed to get their outbreaks under control using widespread testing , tracking and containment programs with a test provided by the WHO . In the U.S. we still do n't have the level of testing that experts say we need to fully contain the virus . <p> The WHO is taking a leadership role in fighting the coronavirus , while Trump is undermining its efforts and blaming others for his own incompetence . <h> Why no mask ? The article itself outlines the increase in flooding over the last several years . Why not take it just one step further and report on how this problem will only get worse if climate change is not adequately addressed ? <h> Fighting Words Newsletter <p> Even in better times , it is difficult to think about climate change , but it is a problem that is not going away . In fact , climate change is contributing to many of the negative impacts of the pandemic . In addition to the flooding that will now require families and businesses to spend even more money in the middle of an economic depression , there are higher death rates from COVID-19 in areas that have high levels of air pollution . In Chicago , that often means black and Latino communities that are already being disproportionately affected by the economic downturn . <p> This is why we need climate change leaders in Congress , like U.S. Rep. Jesus " Chuy " Garcia , D-Chicago , to step up and fight for climate change to be addressed in future stimulus packages . Something like a carbon reduction initiative for any corporation receiving economic stimulus funds could go a long way toward creating more clean energy jobs and helping to revitalize our economy once the pandemic is behind us . If we do not address climate change , future floods -- and pandemics -- will be worse than they have to be .
