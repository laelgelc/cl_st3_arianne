<h> Hamburger menu <p> Ottawa has named current University of Toronto Rotman School of Management dean Tiff Macklem as the next governor for the Bank of Canada . <p> The appointment will be effective June 3 , said federal finance minister Bill Morneau , after current BOC governor Stephen Poloz completes his seven-year term . <p> Macklem was Bank of Canada 's senior deputy governor from 2010 to 2014 and was the associate deputy minister at the Finance Department from 2007 to 2010 . He has also represented Canada at the G7 , G20 and the Financial Stability Board . <p> Macklem , 59 , is a Montreal native and an expert in subjects involving sustainability in finance and the economy - especially regarding climate change and green policies in economic planning . <p> He was also among the contenders in 2013 to replace then-governor Mark Carney before Poloz was named to the position .
