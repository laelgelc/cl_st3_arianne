<p> Officials with the Lower Thames Valley Conservation Authority are welcoming a new tool at their disposal to help protect residents and businesses in the future . <p> The Chatham-Kent Lake Erie Shoreline Study -- presented to council on May 4 -- outlined various mitigation options but did not commit the municipality to any specific action at this time , as more information , and further community input , will be needed . <p> Priorities listed in the study include Erie Shore Drive and the dike ; the protection of the dike along Erieau Road at the entrance to Erieau ; a complete environmental assessment of Talbot Trail and Rose Beach Line ; and the protection of the navigation channel , commercial fishing fleet , fuel dock and marina by restoring the Rondeau barrier beach . <p> The estimated costs for addressing all of these issues range from $131.7 million to $217.2 million . <p> Jason Wintermute , manager of watershed and information services for the LTVCA , was pleased with the work on the study , adding the conservation authority will play a key role moving ahead . <p> " There 's a lot of different directions that would involve the conservation authority , " he said . <p> The municipality , along with the conservation authority , consultant Peter Zuzek and researchers from Zuzek Inc. , and the University of Waterloo collaborated on the study , which also explored the influence of climate change on future coastal hazards due to changes in storms and ice cover , and the associated challenges . <p> One focus for the conservation authority will be continuing to provide expertise on where future development occurs . <p> " One of the responsibilities delegated to us by the province is commenting on natural hazards in municipal official plans and things like that . As the municipality starts working on incorporating the shoreline plan into their other policy and planning documents , we 'll be involved in that process as well . <p> Wintermute said the conservation authority want to make sure the its messaging on permits aligns with what proponents are being told at the municipal planning stage . <p> " The next time somebody comes up and says , ' I want to build here . I want to put a subdivision on the edge of the bluff , ' we can pull ( the study ) up and there 's a whole lot of information , " he said . " It should lead in to a decision whether this is a good idea or not . I think it 's a really helpful document to have around . " <p> Wintermute said other collaborations the conservation authority would likely get involved in include potential work at Rondeau and Wheatley provincial parks . <p> " We had been assisting Mr. Zuzek with those surveys of the Rondeau barrier beach , " he said . " I imagine we will probably be doing that again this year . " <p> With respect to the hefty costs of the different options , Wintermute said , " some of the writing was kind of on the wall , " given that preliminary numbers for potential projects were provided during public meetings . <p> However , unknown at this point is what impact COVID-19 will have on the federal and provincial governments and what they 're willing -- or even able -- to contribute . <p> " That is a big question still . What will the province and the feds do ? Will they buckle down and say we spent a lot of money on the pandemic and we do n't have any more money ? Or will they try to create programs to get people back to work ? " Wintermute said . " And often those historically have taken the form of infrastructure programs . ... Then maybe there will be government money for some of this kind of stuff . "
