There are around 25,000 polar bears in the world , according to an estimate from the International Union for Conservation of Nature . They span 19 subpopulations worldwide , the majority of which live in Canada . The species is currently listed as " vulnerable " by the World Wildlife Fund -- one step above " endangered " -- due primarily to the loss of its sea-ice habitat . <p> If any species can be the emblem of climate change , it should indeed be Ursus maritimus , says Ian Stirling , a University of Alberta biologist . " Polar bears need ice to walk on to hunt seals , " he explains . " They need to be able to catch seals to live .
