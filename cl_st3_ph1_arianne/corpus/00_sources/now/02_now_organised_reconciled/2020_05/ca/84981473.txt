<h> Akeptus to Launch in October 2020 as the Future of Home Energy Management <p> May 21 , 2020 - The team of Akeptus has proudly announced that it will be launching this revolutionary new platform in October this year . Akeptus is an emerging Artificial Intelligence based home energy management platform , and it will revolutionize the way people make their homes more comfortable . Moreover , the basic idea behind this technologically advanced energy management platform is to make homes more smart , secure , and intelligent . Akeptus is a unique blend of both passionate ideals and the cutting edge artificial intelligence , and the platform truly combines innovation and technology for the residential consumers . <p> " We are looking forward to launch Akeptus in October 2020 and we are warmly welcoming folks to sign up to the wait list , " said Dr. Nana Wilberforce , the Founder and CEO of Akeptus , while announcing the platform 's launch date . " Akeptus is designed to empower you in many ways and besides controlling the energy costs , the overall benefits of this platform are endless , " he added . According to the CEO , the company has developed Akeptus as a way to solve both climate change issues and social justice concerns . <p> If a user only has a Smart Thermostat at home , then Akeptus can not only provide complete energy control of the house but can also enable the user to use energy in a more efficient manner . Moreover , users can also control the cost of energy bill , while also enjoying the benefits of home automation to save money . Furthermore , with smart plugs , smart switches , smart lights and smart water monitoring systems , the benefits of Akeptus can be maximized in many ways . <p> In addition to the goal of empowering its users , Akeptus is also aimed at making lives easier and introducing a paradigm shift in the way people live in the twenty first century homes . Every residential energy consumer can reap the benefits of this new technology and can also control the energy usage in real time . Moreover , Akeptus also educates its users about the exact amount of energy their house consumes , thus busting the myth that only the energy bills can provide that information . <p> For more information and to sign up to the wait list , please visit the website at : www.akeptus.com
