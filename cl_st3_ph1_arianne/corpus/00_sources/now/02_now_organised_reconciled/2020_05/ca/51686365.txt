<h> Court quashes government cancellation of eastern Ontario wind farm <h> Court tosses Ontario 's wind farm cancellation <p> TORONTO - Construction of a wind farm in eastern Ontario will move ahead after a court quashed a provincial government decision to cancel the project . <p> In a ruling released Wednesday , a panel of Ontario Superior Court judges said the province 's decision to scrap the Nation Rise Wind Farm in December 2019 did not meet the proper requirements . <p> At the time , Environment Minister Jeff Yurek revoked the approvals of the project near Cornwall , Ont. , citing the risk to three bat species . <p> That decision came despite a ruling from the province 's Environmental Review Tribunal that determined the risk the project posed to the bat population was negligible . <p> The judges said the minister 's decision was " unreasonable " and " procedurally unfair . " <p> " The decision does not meet requirements of transparency , justification , and intelligibility , as the Minister has failed to adequately explain his decision , " the judges wrote in their decision . <p> The company behind the project , EDP Renewables , said the 29-turbine wind farm was almost complete when its approval was revoked in December . <p> The company said Thursday it plans to restart construction on the 100-megawatt wind farm . <p> " EDPR is eager to recommence construction of the Nation Rise Wind Farm , which will bring much-needed jobs and investment to the community , " the company said in a statement . " This delay has resulted in unnecessary expenditures to-date , at a time when governments and businesses should be focused on reducing costs and restarting the economy . " <p> A spokesman for Yurek said the government is disappointed with the outcome of the case but did not comment on a possible appeal . <p> " At this time , we are reviewing the decision and are carefully considering our next steps , " Andrew Buttigieg said in a statement . <p> NDP climate change critic Peter Tabuns said the court decision is an embarrassment for the minister and the government . He urged the government not to pursue an appeal . <p> Yurek " was found to have ignored the evidence and the facts , " he said . " They did n't just lose , their case collapsed . They had nothing to stand on . Taking this to appeal would be a complete and total waste of money . " <p> Green party Leader Mike Schreiner said the ruling proves the government was acting based on ideology over evidence when it revoked the project 's approval . <p> " As we shift towards a post-COVID recovery , we need the Ford government to give up the irrational crusade against affordable and reliable clean energy , " Schreiner said in a statement . <p> A spokeswoman for the advocacy group Environmental Defence urged the Progressive Conservatives to consult experts on the province 's energy projects . <p> " Energy decisions are crucial in building a healthy , climate-safe future , " Sarah Buchanan said in a statement . " These decisions must be based on evidence , not bias , and we 're glad to see this upheld in a court of law . " <p> Last year , the NDP revealed the province had spent $231 million to cancel more than 750 renewable energy contracts shortly after winning the 2018 election . <p> The Progressive Conservatives have blamed the previous Liberal government for signing the bad energy deals while the province had an oversupply of electricity . <p> The Ford government has also said that by cancelling the contracts it would ultimately save ratepayers $790 million -- a figure industry officials have disputed . <p> At the time of the wind farm cancellation , the government also said it would introduce legislation that would protect consumers from any costs incurred . <p> It has since acknowledged it will have to pay some companies to cancel the deals and set aside $231 million to reach agreements with those firms . <p> On Wednesday , the judges awarded Nation Rise $126,500 in costs , which the government will have to pay .
