<p> Using a ' fast-track strategy ' , fire crews could be airlifted to new fires before they became too big , a group of former fire and emergency services leaders have told the bushfire royal commission . Photo : Lithgowlights/CC BY-SA/3.0 <p> Australia should deploy new " fast-attack strategies " to combat bushfires and stop small remote blazes turning into unstoppable mega fires , a group of 33 former fire and emergency services leaders have said . <p> In a submission to the bushfires royal commission , the group said climate change was increasing the risk of extreme bushfire seasons . <p> New " rapid fire-detection " technologies should be trialled , with faster mid-sized and purpose-built water bombers made available to put out fires within 24 hours of them being detected . <p> The submission from emergency leaders for climate action , seen by Guardian Australia , includes 33 recommendations to the royal commission . <p> The economic costs have been estimated at more than $4bn . Hundreds of endangered animals , plant and invertebrate species were affected , and some may have been driven to extinction . <p> Australia 's unprecedented bushfire season of 2019-20 coincided with the country 's hottest and driest year on record . <p> " It is not possible to ' adapt ' to such catastrophic and escalating conditions , and they can only be partially mitigated , " said the submission , compiled by former commissioner of Fire and Rescue NSW , Greg Mullins . <p> " The failure of successive governments at all levels to show leadership and take credible , urgent action on the basic causal factor -- greenhouse gas emissions from the burning of coal , oil and gas -- will lead to further escalation in natural disaster risks . " <p> The submission recommended Australia develop a network of sensing technologies to detect fires in remote areas earlier , and then employ a " fast attack " approach to put them out . <p> Australia should trial a purpose-built water bombing aircraft long-used in other countries that can scoop up water and " achieve rapid turnaround and constant direct attack on fire fronts " . <p> Mullins told Guardian Australia a range of early detection technologies could be used to detect new fires , including remote cameras , satellite images and spotter flights . <p> " Every big fire was once a small fire , " he said . " It 's very much like a military operation with eyes in the sky , with your ground troops that are backed up with some artillery . " <p> The purpose-built water bombers were able to refuel quickly -- either by skimming over large water storages or landing in paddocks -- and that meant they were ideally suited to a rapid response strategy . <p> Firefighting experts have warned that while larger water-bombing aircraft could help control fires , they should not be seen as a " panacea " and that a suite of responses were needed . <p> The former emergency leaders said these aircraft had failed to make " appreciable difference to extreme fires on the worst fire weather days " . <p> Other recommendations included increasing funding for climate change research , putting more electricity cables underground , reviewing building standards , and building bushfire-safe shelters in fire-prone communities . <p> The submission also highlighted the increasing number of large fires that couple with the atmosphere to form their own storms , creating deadly conditions on the ground for firefighters . <p> There had been an " astounding and deeply concerning shift in the frequency of these events " . <p> Hearings for the royal commission will begin on 25 May and run for two weeks . It has held 17 community forums and received more than 400 public submissions . <p> The final report and recommendations are due on 31 August and Dominique Hogan-Doran SC , the senior counsel assisting , said in the inquiry 's first hearing in April that it would be narrowly focused on national coordination and the " challenges associated with walking across lines drawn on a map " so as to avoid duplication with current and previous state inquiries . <p> " We detect a worrying consistency in the themes explored and repetitiveness in the recommendations made , " Hogan-Doran said . " This is no time to reinvent the wheel . " <p> The royal commission is considering recommending Australia be granted the ability to declare a national state of emergency , and released an issues paper this month calling for advice from constitutional law experts . <p> National state of emergency powers may also be able to be invoked during a pandemic , although the royal commission has explicitly ruled out considering matters relating to the coronavirus . <p> Victoria 's response to the coronavirus crisis is anchored in its state of emergency powers , which were created after the 2009 Black Saturday bushfires . <p> The issues paper said if the commonwealth had powers to declare a state of emergency it would be able to deploy national resources , such as the army , without waiting for a request from the states . <p> But it also said there were constitutional limitations in granting such a power which may mean a national state of emergency declaration would have either a limited or a very specific practical effect .
