<h> Video <h> Can Big Oil Be Part of the Climate Change Solution ? <p> The CEOs of fossil-fuel giants Enbridge and Suncor talked up what they 're doing to combat climate change at the GLOBE 2016 sustainable business summit in Vancouver this week . How 'd we get here ? <p> Environmental leader Tzeporah Berman has worked closely with both executives to find common group between climate campaigners and oil companies . She says Big Oil is only moving into the green because protesters have denied them social licence and forced them into the climate conversation . <p> The summit ran concurrent to the Prime Minister 's visit to Vancouver for the First Ministers ' Meeting , where Justin Trudeau attempted to reframe oil development as necessary for creating the wealth needed to fund for clean energy development . " The choice between wind turbines and pipelines is a false one , " Trudeau said . <p> Video shot and produced by Mychaylo Prystupa for The Tyee .
