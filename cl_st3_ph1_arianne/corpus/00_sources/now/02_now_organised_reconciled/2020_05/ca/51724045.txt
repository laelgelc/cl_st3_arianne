<h> Here are today 's Ottawa Sun letters to the editor . <p> OUTDOOR SCHOOLS AN ANSWER ? <p> Conceiving a way to safely reopen schools in September is a massive issue . Alternating school days to cut the number kids in half who attend the classroom at one time is an option in the forefront . Of course this poses a problem for parents who work full-time . One solution is to partner with outdoor schools to alleviate the days that kids will be not be in the classroom . <p> Outdoor schools have gained in popularity because of their hands-on learning style , which is both physically and mentally ideal for young children . One hurdle we 're facing is that younger children are incapable of physical distancing during recess and gym . Yet they ca n't realistically go without some sort of activity during the day . With alternating days attending outdoor schools , children will have had a full day of fresh air and exercise , and potentially be more focused and settled during the classroom days . It has long been known that sitting for hours on end , day after day , is n't optimal for anyone , least of all highly energetic kids . We need a solution , so why not choose one that will be a positive change within our current education system ? <p> We have a unique advantage here in Ontario to be able to draw from these outdoor schools . It would be prudent to examine how their model can work during this pandemic and move forward in order to get people back to work , providing an upgrade to how we teach kids while giving them the physical activity they need to thrive . <p> CAROLYN REDDY <p> GUELPH , ONT . <p> ( An interesting suggestion .
