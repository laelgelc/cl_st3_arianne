<h> Trudeau dazzles and disappoints in Saskatoon <p> Justin Trudeau addressing the audience in the Health Sciences Building of the University of Saskatchewan . Photo : Arthur White-Crummey/Daily Herald . <p> In Justin Trudeau 's Saskatoon town hall on Wednesday , two themes stuck out : his knack for playing both sides of contentious issues and his expressed commitment to reconciliation with indigenous peoples . <p> Trudeau filled a University of Saskatchewan auditorium for the ninth stop in his cross-Canada tour . After an introduction by Regina-Wascana MP Ralph Goodale , the Prime Minister entered to heavy applause and thanked the audience for getting involved in politics . <p> " For me this is part of what politics should be all about , " he said . " A collaborative , responsive dialogue about how we can all work together to build the best possible country . " <p> But things got heated right away , when Trudeau called on a man who was n't happy with the carbon tax . <p> " What 's the strategy behind choosing to pursue a carbon tax when there 's ample evidence on the record that it does n't work ? " The man asked . " Why are n't we pursuing a made-in-Canada policy to combat climate change ? " <p> Trudeau said his aim is to protect both the environment and the economy , but acknowledged that carbon pricing will cause short-term pain for some sectors . <p> For more on this story , please see the Jan. 27 print or e-edition of the Daily Herald .
