<p> With temperatures continuing to warm up as spring progresses , the likelihood of encounters with severe storms increases . <p> According to a Warning Preparedness Meteorologist with Environment and Climate Change Canada , any storm not just severe thunderstorms have the potential to be remarkably dangerous . <p> Meteorologists often do n't have the opportunity to share their message with the populous shares Natalie Hasell , so when communities invite them to speak at an event , she says they jump at the chance . <p> Hasell recently spoke to residents of Roland at a Thunderstorm Safety Training Workshop , she says most who attended were those who worked in emergency coordination , but she says identifying the severity of storms can be a vital skill for anyone . <p> " We have to consider whether it 's dangerous or not , and sometimes people are misinformed so they do n't act in time , or they do n't know . Hopefully , through these types of sessions and through the information that is available through our website , people can learn what they should do in times of severe weather . " <p> Hasell notes that though they may not put out a warning for every thunderstorm , those kinds of storms are defined by lightning and lightning can be lethal , so it 's essential to be aware that every thunderstorm can present a significant threat . <p> Although not common in the area , tornadic storms are also of concern and Hasell says people need to learn how to identify various storms and what steps are required to remain safe . <p> *Listen for weather warnings or looks for weather updates online ; <p> *Locate escape routes and safe-zones , and whether you can get in and out ; <p> *In a thunderstorm avoid windows , doors , and conductive materials ; <p> *Avoid areas which are prone to flash flooding ; <p> *For a tornado go to a room with as many walls as possible preferable a basement , avoid large buildings with flat roofs as they are n't designed to withstand strong winds . <p> However , Hasell says the first action should be communicating with friends , family or colleagues . <p> " Communication is more than just being able to receive the alerts . If you 're driving somewhere where are you going , what roads are you taking to get there , how long do you think it will take , so communicate that . Tell people so that if you do n't show up or do n't call in , somebody knows or can get help if it turns out you do need help . " <p> Hasell adds Environment Canada wants to provide residents with the information they need to remain safe while being able to explore Manitoba . <p> When the cities of Winkler , Altona , Morden , Carman , and the rest of Southern Manitoba want local news , weather , and sports online and on-demand , they turn to Pembina Valley Online . As the Pembina Valley 's website for local job listings , free classifieds , garage sales , family events , business directory and weather cancellations , Pembina Valley Online is your connection to local information .
