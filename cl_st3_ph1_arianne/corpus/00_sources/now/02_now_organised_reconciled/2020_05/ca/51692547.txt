<h> Greens elect MPs in New Brunswick and B.C. , but barely increase seat total <p> VICTORIA -- Green Leader Elizabeth May says increasing her party 's seat total by one in Monday 's federal election is a bigger victory than it seems and should not be considered a disappointment . <p> The Greens were hoping for a big breakthrough , but will become a force with three MPs , said May . <p> " We 've done better in Canada than any Green party under the first-past-the-post system , " she said , adding there are 19 Greens who have been elected across Canada when provincially elected politicians are counted . <p> May won the only seat in the House of Commons for the Greens in 2015 , followed by a byelection victory for the party last spring . <p> Jenica Atwin 's win in Fredericton gives the party members of Parliament on both coasts and marked the first time the party has elected an MP outside of B.C. <p> " It 's a big breakthrough to have a seat in Fredericton , " said May . " It breaks us out of the box of people only thinking we 're a Vancouver Island party . " <p> May said Atwin 's win also establishes the Greens as Canada 's first caucus with more women than men . <p> May was re-elected in Saanich-Gulf Islands and Paul Manly won again in Nanaimo-Ladysmith after his byelection success earlier this year . <p> May said the three Green members will be able to achieve results in the House of Commons . She suggested the Greens will also work closely with Independent member Jody Wilson-Raybould , a former Liberal cabinet minister , who was elected Monday in Vancouver . <p> " It 's certainly a lot more than we could do with one seat in a majority Parliament , and I did a lot , " said May , recounting her achievements after her first election in 2011 . " We are in a position of being the conscience of Parliament . " <p> May suggested this campaign was her last as Green leader . <p> " I 've never made any bones about the fact I want succession planning , " she said . <p> Green supporters gathered in Victoria hoping to cheer more but settled for small bursts of celebration following the three victories . <p> " We have got a big job to do to break in across the country , " said Adam Olsen , a Green member of the B.C. legislature . " There 's no doubt about it . " <p> Olsen is one of the three Green MLAs elected in 2017 when the party reached a deal to support the province 's minority NDP government . <p> " I 'd like to see more Green seats , but we have to take what we can get , " he said . <p> The Greens called their campaign " Mission : Possible , " promising science-based policies to fight climate change and urging voters to consider the future when making their choices . <p> May called the election a climate referendum . <p> " I 'm really glad I was able to be the leader in this critical election on climate crisis , " she said . <p> The Greens promised to stop the Trans Mountain pipeline expansion project and pledged to cut greenhouse gas emissions by 60 per cent below 2005 levels by 2030 . The party also pledged to plant 10 billion trees over the next 30 years to reduce carbon and recover vast areas of land devastated by wildfires across Canada . <p> At a campaign stop in Quebec earlier this month , May said the Greens will stand up with Quebecers against the construction of any fossil fuel pipeline in the province . She said the promise to cut greenhouse gas emissions by 60 per cent involves implementing a transition from fossil fuels to clean renewable energy sources that include wind , solar and hydro electric . <p> May said she expected the thousands of oil , coal and gas workers would find work in Canada 's emerging clean-energy sectors . <p> Since she was first elected in 2011 , May has gained a reputation as an effective debater with an unmatched work ethic and a politician known for speaking from her heart on environmental issues . <p> But despite May 's individual success , the long-awaited Green breakthrough has yet to arrive . <p> When May voted Monday , she said she was thinking about Canada 's children . <p> She said she had spent part of her election day sending messages of thanks to young children from across Canada who wished her luck . <p> May said many of the children say they are too young to vote but were trying to convince their parents and grandparents to vote Green . <p> STANDOFF , AB . -- Very early Sunday ( May 17 ) morning , the Blood Tribe received confirmation of new COVID-19 cases on the reserve , taking the total to ten .
