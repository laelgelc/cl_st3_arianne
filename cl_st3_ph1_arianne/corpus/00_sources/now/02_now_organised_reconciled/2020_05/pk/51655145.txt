<h> UN chief pushes for debt moratorium , more aid to poor nations <p> UNITED NATIONS : UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres has reinforced calls for debt moratorium to help developing countries combat coronavirus pandemic that has had a devastating effect on their economies . <p> " The debt moratorium must be extended to all developing countries that are unable to service their debt , including several middle-income countries ... followed by targeted debt relief ... to prevent defaults leading to prolonged financial and economic crises " , he told a virtual news conference on Thursday . <p> " The International Labour Organisation ( ILO ) reported this week that the global workforce will be hit with the equivalent of the loss of more than 300 million jobs , " the UN chief said , adding that millions of children risk missing life-saving vaccines and that those officially living in poverty could rise by around 500 million -- " the first increase in three decades " . Underscoring the " massive and urgent support " needed for developing countries , he echoed his call for a worldwide relief package of at least 10 percent of the global economy 's output . <p> He acknowledged steps taken by the International Monetary Fund ( IMF ) -- $12.3 billion in emergency financing ; the World Bank , with $160 billion of extra financing ; and the G20 leading economies , which have agreed to suspend debt service payments for the poorest countries , but added , " even this is not enough " . <p> Guterres maintained that a smart recovery from COVID-19 would help steer the world onto a " safer , healthier , more sustainable and inclusive path " , but that it was " critical to address the fragilities " . The secretary general called on governments to ensure that revitalised spending " accelerate the decarbonisation of all aspects of our economy and privilege the creation of green jobs " . <p> He also stressed that taxpayers ' money should not be used to subsidise fossil fuels or bail out carbon-intensive industries ; a price should be placed on carbon ; and public funds be invested in a future where financial institutions and investors take climate risks fully into account . <p> " Our template remains the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) and Paris Agreement on climate change " , the UN chief said . Guterres also recalled his global ceasefire appeal in March to face together the common enemy of COVID-19 . <p> " The cease-fire call has resonated widely " , he told journalists , however , as mistrust is challenging implementation , his envoys are " working tirelessly ... to turn expressed intentions into effective cease-fires " . The Idlib cease-fire is holding in Syria , " but we are still hopeful for a country-wide end to hostilities " , he said , adding that the UN is pushing in Afghanistan for a humanitarian ceasefire between the government and Taliban fighters . And although fighting in Libya has escalated , declarations made yesterday present " a glimpse of hope that a cessation of hostilities remains possible " . <p> " I believe there is an opportunity for peace in Yemen " , upheld Guterres , citing that all parties supported his appeal . Saudi Arabia has declared a temporary unilateral cease-fire , with the UN engaging with all actors to make it permanent , to restart the political process towards lasting peace . Pointing out that the first two COVID-19 deaths were registered there on Wednesday , he said , " it is time to recognize that the Yemeni people have suffered too much " . <p> " All our efforts depend on strong political backing " , stressed the UN chief , sharing his hope the Security Council would be able to " find unity and adopt decisions that can help to make ceasefires meaningful and real " .
