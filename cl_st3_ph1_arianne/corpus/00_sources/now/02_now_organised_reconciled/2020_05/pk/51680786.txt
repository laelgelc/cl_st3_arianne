<p> The Islamabad High Court ( IHC ) on Saturday reserved a verdict in a case pertaining to the improper care of animals at the Islamabad zoo , asking three government departments to submit replies . <p> The IHC sought replies in the case from the Ministry of Climate Change , the Municipal Corporation of Islamabad , and the wildlife department of the federal government , according to sources . <p> Chief Justice IHC Athar Minallah directed the government departments to submit a detailed report on the questions highlighted by the court on April 27 . The court will announce the reserved verdict on April 30 . <p> In a hearing held into the case on Saturday afternoon , Secretary Climate Change Naheed Bukhari , the mayor of the federal capital , and a wildlife department official from Islamabad appeared in the high court . <p> " The court wants to see animals treated in a humane manner and has thus exercised caution in this case , " Justice Minallah said in his remarks during the hearing on Saturday afternoon . <p> " Three government departments were given the responsibility of taking care of the animals at the zoo . The court expresses extreme disappointment in the case with a heavy heart , " he added . <p> " An elephant at the zoo , obtained as a gift from another country , is being kept at the zoo in painful conditions , " he said . " Why are animals caged if they can not be cared for ? " he asked Bukhari during the hearing . <p> " Why should the court not transfer the animals at the zoo to a temporary shelter ? " he further asked Bukhari . Justice Minallah observed that those who wanted to take care of animals were demoralised . <p> " The court had high hopes of you , madam secretary , but the ones who wanted to take care of animals have been issued show-cause notices , " Justice Minallah lamented during his conversation with Bukhari . <p> " People are under a lockdown and still unafraid . Even those who go to zoos are sometimes involved in paining animals because they are unaware of proper guidelines , " he noted . <p> " Three government departments together can not get the affairs of a small zoo in order , " he observed .
