<h> Monal Restaurant Sealed for Illegal Construction <p> The popular Monal Restaurant located in the Margalla Hills has been sealed by the Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency Islamabad and Chief Commissioner Office Islamabad . It has been sealed for cutting trees illegally for its expansive construction activities . <p> According to the Climate Change Ministry 's Media focal person , Muhammad Saleem , it came into notice of the Prime Minister 's Advisor Malik Amin Aslam that there was illegal construction going on for the expansion of the Monal restaurant . It was started by the restaurant management and it resulted in a significant tree cover loss &amp; land degradation in its vicinity . <p> He said , " Taking serious notice of the illegal construction activities and tree cutting , the advisor Malik Amin Aslam on Tuesday directed the Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency Islamabad ( Pak-EPA Islamabad ) to take legal action against the culprits . " <p> Malik Amin Aslam tweeted , " This atrocity in #MHNP has been stopped and some culprits arrested by #ICT . Further details soon ... " <p> Raid was conducted by the District Administration on 18th May 's evening and henceforth the destruction images that appeared on social media seem real . <p> Two individuals were arrested on the spot during the raid while FIR NO 224/20 has also been lodged in Police Station Kohsar , F/7 , Islamabad <p> Aslam also said , " Update -- 1 . The degraded area of #MHNP under #Monal will be totally rehabilitated and re-forested and I will initiate the plan tomorrow on site 2 . The #FIR is attached and culprits will be taken to task 3. #MHNP is a national resource which shall be protected at all costs IA "
