<h> ' Aviation sector most badly hit ' <p> TANZANIA 'S aviation sector has been the most badly hit by the outbreak of Covid-19 , with the number of cargo and passenger flights by the national flag carrier -- Air Tanzania Co . Ltd ( ATCL ) dropping drastically . <p> Minister for Works , Transport and Communication Isack Kamwelwe . <p> The minister for Works , Transport and Communication Isack Kamwelwe made this observation when tabling the ministry 's budget estimates for the 2020/2021 fiscal year here yesterday <p> He said the total number of flights is expected to further drop by 23.4 percent ( 179,283 flights ) by June 2020 compared to 234,048 flights in the same period during 2018/19 . <p> The minister said the number of passengers for domestic flights decreased by 17.2 percent to 4,398,500 compared to 5,309,611 passengers in that period during the 2018/2019 financial year . <p> Cargo flights also decreased by 12.2 percent to 21,456.6 tonnes from July 2019 to date compared to 24,444 tonnes transported during the same period in 2018/19 . <p> ATCL has secured all the permits for direct flights from Guangzhou , China as well as signing of contract for direct chartered flights from Hangzhou , China to Dar es Salaam which will be specific for tourists , he said . <p> " The number of passengers traveling to and outside Tanzania is also expected to continue dropping if the pandemic continues , but we expect all these flights will begin shortly after the Covid-19 pandemic ends , " the minister asserted . <p> Kamwele informed the House that a total of 423 airport safety officers from the Mainland and Zanzibar have been trained and issued with screeners ' certificate as part of measures to prevent the spread of the pandemic . <p> He said plans are to train more airport safety officers under the National Civil Aviation Security Training Programme . <p> The government signed new contracts for issuance of aviation services -- Bilateral Air Services Agreements ( BASA ) with Mauritius , Rwanda and Spain between July 2019 and March 2020 . Processes are on-going for signing of BASA contracts with Zambia , Turkey , Oman , Sri Lanka , the United Kingdom , Zimbabwe , Ethiopia , South Africa and Jordan , he elaborated . <p> " Signing of the contracts with 22 airline companies from 19 countries resulted into 175 direct flights to from the countries to Tanzania until March 2020 , " he remarked , noting that the outbreak of Covid-19 has generally affected performance of the transportation sector , including ports performance . <p> He said tightening security at airports and borders has been enhanced with visitors crossing the country 's borders being screened for the coronavirus . <p> Minister Kamwele mentioned vandalism and theft of railway infrastructures as one of the major challenges facing the transportation sector . The ministry is conducting campaigns to educate people residing near railway lines to engage in protecting those infrastructures on account of their importance economically . <p> He also cited climate change among factors contributing to destruction of road and railway infrastructures , with natural disasters such as floods . <p> Suleiman Kakoso ( Mpanda Rural , CCM ) the chairman of the Parliamentary Infrastructure Committee , said that ATCL is facing various challenges including huge debts amounting to 180bn/- . He said the government had only allocated 35.865bn/- for the purpose in the 2019/2020 financial year . <p> Kakoso stated that most airports countrywide have poor lighting systems and lack aviation light signals . He said the challenge has resulted into most planes landing only during daytime . <p> He commended the government for establishing the railway fund which in the coming year was allocated 289.6m/- for procurement of nine train engines and improvements of the service in the northern zone , for Tanga , Kilimanjaro and Arusha regions . <p> He however faulted the government over failure to disburse all the required funds for improvement of railway services whereas it only gave out 159.7m/- equivalent to 62 percent of the total endorsed monies for the fund in the 2019/2020 financial year . <p> Nkasi MP Ally Kessy ( CCM ) urged the government to revive the MV Liemba which has been grounded for years . He said residents in the area have no reliable marine transport as ferries which were playing in Lake Tanganyika are not functioning . <p> Kessy also suggested that the government completes the construction of Sumbawanga airport to facilitate air transport in the area . <p> The minister requested the legislature to endorse 4.9trn/- for the ministry 's recurrent and development expenditure for the 2020/2021 financial year .
