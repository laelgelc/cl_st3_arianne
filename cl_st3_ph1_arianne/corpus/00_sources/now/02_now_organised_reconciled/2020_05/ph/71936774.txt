<h> EU celebrates strong partnership with ASEAN in Europe Day 2020 <p> In celebration of Europe Day 2020 , EU Ambassador to the ASEAN Igor Driesmans reached out to the ASEAN youth in a virtual discussion moderated by Ms. Agatha Lydia Natania of the ASEAN Youth Organization and Ms. Hanny Nurmalita , an EU Share Alumna . Photo from ACB . <p> QUEZON CITY , May 13 -- The European Union ( EU ) has expressed its commitment to continue supporting the ASEAN 's programmes and projects , particularly in conserving the region 's rich biodiversity , despite the COVID-19 pandemic that is besetting the world . Driesmans describes the ASEAN as the EU 's " number one development partner " . <p> " In times when so many countries are turning inwards , it is important that we stand by our ideals of cooperation and international solidarity , " EU Ambassador to the ASEAN Igor Driesmans said on Saturday during a virtual discussion to celebrate Europe Day 2020 . <p> Representatives from various youth organisations , including the Youth Biodiversity Leaders ( YBL ) of the ASEAN Youth Biodiversity Programme ( AYBP ) , participated in the virtual talk organised by the EU Mission to the ASEAN . <p> Youth leaders fielded questions on diverse topics related to EU-ASEAN relations , including education and scholarship opportunities , impacts of the Covid-19 pandemic , and the EU 's support for various programmes , such as biodiversity conservation . <p> The EU is among the ASEAN 's staunch allies in its biodiversity conservation efforts . In 1999 , the EU supported the establishment of the ASEAN Regional Centre for Biodiversity Conservation ( ARCBC ) , which then became the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity ( ACB ) in 2005 . In 2017 , the ACB launched the Biodiversity Conservation and Management of Protected Areas in ASEAN ( BCAMP ) Project in partnership with the EU . The BCAMP Project 's programmes on mainstreaming biodiversity conservation in the region , protected area management , knowledge generation , and capacity-building , support the initatives of ASEAN Member States . The recent provision of camera traps , information technology ( IT ) and communication devices to amp up monitoring and law-enforcement activities for protected areas in Lao PDR and Malaysia is just among the many notable highlights from the BCAMP project . <p> Driesmans lauds the extensive work of the ACB in conserving a great percentage of global species living in the ASEAN region . " It 's a fantastic programme , " he said , recalling his visit to the ACB headquarters in 2019 . He cited the ASEAN Heritage Parks Programme ( AHPs ) as among the " amazing work " that ACB does to preserve biodiversity in the ASEAN region . <p> The BCAMP Project also supports the AYBP , which provides ASEAN youth with capacity-building , mentorship and resources to scale their impact for biodiversity conservation . Through its leadership and AHP internship sub-programmes , the AYBP is able to develop new generation leaders and conservationists whose voices help to mainstream biodiversity conservation in the national and regional policies . In 2019 , 20 YBLs were selected and trained , with some of them being recognised as youth resource persons by at least five AMS . <p> " Regional to regional cooperation is paramount , as made apparent by COVID-19 . What happens in this region , such as with wildlife trade , human-wildlife interactions , and decreased ecosystem resilience due to land-use change and degradation -- all leading to increased risk of emergence of infectious diseases -- can have devastating impacts not just on the communities here , but also in other regions like Europe , " said Mika Tan , AYBP coordinator . <p> Concerned with the effects of the pandemic to the ongoing programmes of the EU in the ASEAN Region , Racelle Rescordado , 2020 ASEAN Youth Biodiversity Leader from the Philippines , raised the question on the future of the EU-ASEAN partnership . <p> " We have actually taken the deliberate decision that ongoing EU-ASEAN programs should be maintained and even those programmes that have not started yet will be maintained , " said the envoy . <p> Stressing that health and socio-economic programmes are on top of the EU 's financial commitments , Driesmans called the youth sector to " address ( the issues ) together as much as we can . " He added , " what young people can do is making your voice heard . " <p> The envoy likewise addressed the climate change issue and the role that the ASEAN youth play in raising awareness . Driesmans noted that 56 per cent of global peatlands are in ASEAN , and promoting the sustainable use of peatlands is among the concrete programmes between the EU and ASEAN . " What is ahead of us in climate change is really an emergency situation , " he said . " The effects are being felt today but even if tomorrow global emissions would go to zero , we would still feel the consequences of what we 've already done so the situation will get worse if we do n't do anything , " he said . <p> A quiz highlighting the key programmes between EU and ASEAN , including educational scholarships , the newly-published Blue Book 2020 and other interesting trivia about both the EU and the ASEAN , capped the virtual talk .
