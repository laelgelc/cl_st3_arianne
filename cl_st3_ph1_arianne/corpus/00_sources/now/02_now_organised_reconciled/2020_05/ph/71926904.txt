<h> Philippines among world 's most disaster-prone countries <h> File photo <p> Metro Manila ( CNN Philippines ) -- Over the past two decades , the Philippines endured a total of 274 natural calamities , making it the fourth most disaster-prone country in the world . <p> This is based on " The Human Cost of Weather Related Disasters , " a study conducted by the Geneva-based United Nations Office for Disaster Risk Reduction ( UNISDR ) and the Belgian-based Centre on the Epidemiology of Disasters ( CRED ) . It covered the period between 1995 and 2015 . <p> According to the report , the other countries hit by the highest number of disasters were United States ( 472 ) , China ( 441 ) , India ( 288 ) , and Indonesia ( 163 ) . <p> The report also revealed that the Philippines was among the top ten countries with the most number of people affected by disasters , at 130 billion . <h> Bohol quake , Yolanda <p> The last quarter of the year 2013 alone showed how catastrophic the impact of disasters could be for Filipinos . <p> On October 15 , a magnitude 7.2 earthquake jolted Central Visayas , with the epicentre at Sagbayan , Bohol . A total of 222 individuals were reported dead , 976 injured , and eight missing . <p> The earthquake affected 3 million people -- more than 300,000 of them were displaced when 73,000 houses were damaged . It also damaged P2.3 billion worth of seaports , airports , churches , and other infrastructures . <p> Less than a month after the Bohol quake , the country faced the strongest and deadliest typhoon in its history . Typhoon Yolanda ( international name : Haiyan ) entered the Philippine Area of Responsibility ( PAR ) on November 6 and in a span of three days claimed a staggering 6,300 lives . <p> The typhoon ravaged Tacloban on November 8 , and affected 648 other cities and municipalities in 44 provinces . It changed the lives of 16 million individuals , displacing 800,000 families , damaging a million houses , and leaving up to P90 billion worth of damages . <p> As of April 2014 , more than a thousand people remain missing , based on the latest report by the National Risk Reduction and Management Council ( NDRRMC ) . <p> Accounting to four in 10 deaths , storms were the deadliest type of weather-related disasters worldwide , the UNISR said . Floods comprised half of all natural disasters for the past 20 years , affecting 2.3 billion people . <h> ' Almost daily occurrence ' <p> Natural disasters such as floods , storms , heatwaves and droughts occur almost daily in different parts of the world . <p> The UNISDR report said the 6,457 weather-related events recorded since 1995 have caused 9 in 10 major disasters . <p> Asia is the region hardest hit by disasters which killed 332,000 people and affected 3.7 billion . <p> The UNISDR estimated economic losses for all types of natural disasters to be $250 billion to $300 billion annually , which have become a major challenge for developing countries . <h> Paris climate talks to improve world disaster situation <p> During the launch of the report on November 23 , UNISDR Head Margareta Wahlstr ? m expressed hopes that the UN Climate Change Conference in Paris next week will result to an agreement on reducing greenhouse gas emissions . <p> She said this will help reduce damage and loss from disasters that are partly caused by global warming and rising sea levels . <p> " For now , there is a need to reduce existing levels of risk and avoid creating new risk by ensuring that public and private investments are risk-informed and do not increase the exposure of people and economic assets to natural hazards on flood plains , vulnerable low-lying coastlines or other locations unsuited for human settlement , " Wahlstr ? m said .
