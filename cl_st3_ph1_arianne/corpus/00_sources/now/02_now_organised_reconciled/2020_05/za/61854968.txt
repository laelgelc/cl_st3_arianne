<h> Anglo American set to exit coal mining in SA <p> Mining giant Anglo American plans to exit coal mining in South Africa , in a major shift away from carbon-intense operations . <p> The company has , in the past few years , been offloading its coal assets , which has included the sale of its Eskom coal mines in 2018 , as well as the Drayton mine in Australia . <p> In a response to shareholder questions , the diversified global miner said it would consider its exit options , with a view to listing the demerged business on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange . <p> " We are therefore working towards a possible demerger of our thermal coal operations in South Africa as our likely preferred exit option , expected in the next two to three years , with a primary listing in the JSE for the de-merged business , " it said . <p> " We will continue to consider other exit options as we engage with stakeholders as part of our commitment to a responsible transition . " <p> In light of growing concerns over climate change , the global push for clean energy has seen companies adopt greener policies , which include measures to reduce C02 emissions . <p> Anglo American , which operates diamond , iron ore and platinum mines in South Africa , said its thermal coal operations in the country " constitute a strong and attractive business with high-quality well-located assets , and with access to established export infrastructure " . <p> The mining giant in 2018 sold its thermal coal mines to a black-owned entity , Seriti Resources , in a deal worth R2.3 billion . The mines , including New Vaal , New Denmark and Kriel collieries , supply coal to Eskom 's power stations Lethabo , Thuthuka and Kriel . <p> According to mining and labour analyst Mamokgethi Molopyane , the decision by the company to let go of the coal " burden " did not come as a surprise , in the face of a growing need for clean energy . <p> Coal still viable <p> " But that does not mean that coal in South Africa is not a viable business anymore , given our reliance on coal for energy generation , " she said . <p> Molopyane believes Anglo 's exit from the coal sector is unlikely to cause any overall negative impact to the industry , as coal still forms a major part of the country 's energy policy , including exports to other African countries who still rely on coal . <p> " I do n't think the coal sector would be badly impacted by the decision ... at least here at home , and in other African countries it is still going to be around for some time , " she said . <p> Eskom generates most of its electricity from coal , and the company 's new mega-power stations , Kusile and Medupi , are coal-fired . <p> Anglo American noted that it believes that the " long term prospects of our thermal coal operations in the country may be better served under different ownership " given the shift towards cleaner energy .
