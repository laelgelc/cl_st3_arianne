<p> Local initiative Food Flow supports small-scale farmers who are not a part of the large retailer supply chain to continue earning income throughout the lockdown . <p> " We have been in conversation with a collective of small scale fisheries and will be starting to purchase from them as well . To get fish out into communities , especially those cooking up meals to benefit everyone , " co-founder , Ashley Newell . <p> Parts of the south and east coasts of South Africa , from Cape Agulhas to Mozambique , are at risk of sudden ecological disruption as early as the 2050 's . <p> What surprised senior researcher at the African Climate &amp; Development Initiative at the University of Cape Town , Dr Christopher Trisos and his co-authors Dr Cory Merow ( University of Connecticut ) and Dr Alex Pigot ( University College London ) is that the earth 's biodiversity is at risk in the first half of this century . <p> Trisos told Weekend Argus that tropical ocean ecosystems in the Indo-Pacific , Caribbean and along the west coast of Africa will face serious impacts in the next decade or two . <p> He stressed that this could negatively impact livelihoods and food security for hundreds of millions of people across these regions who depend on wild-caught fish and nature-based tourism . <p> " More than 90 percent of the warming that has happened during climate change over the past 50 years occurred in the ocean , " he said . <p> Tropical fish species moving south into the estuaries of the temperate zone have been reported . <p> As fish species shift their geographic ranges away from warming waters this can have substantial impacts on the fishing industry , according to Trisos . <p> He noted that South Africa saw the eastward shift in the distribution of west coast rock lobster . <p> This had serious negative implications for communities on the west coast that rely on fishing for their income and livelihoods . <p> Climate Change will have a devastating impact on small-scale fishing and their communities , said the Director of the Masifundise Development Trust , Naseegh Jaffer . <p> The NPO supports small-scale fishing communities . <p> " Climate change affects the breeding ground and the way fish move around . Small-scale fishers have to adapt to new ways of working and evolve in order to survive , " stressed Jaffer . <p> International nature researchers authored the paper called ' Rebuilding Marine Life ' . <p> " Whether you 're a fishery , a seafood processor , a certifier or consumer it is a timely reminder that we can safeguard our oceans for future generations , " he said . <p> There is a national legislation in place ( principally the National Environmental Management Act and the Marine Living Resources Act ) to safeguard our oceans for future generations . <p> " This is to ensure sustainable fishing , conservation of marine resources and the ecosystems that support them , " said director of communication services of environment , forestry and fisheries , Zolile Nqayi . <p> The Department is conducting assessments of the vulnerability of fisheries in regards to climate change . <p> " Together with developing adaptation plans for our local fisheries in response to climate change , it is expected that these will be made publically available later this year , " he said . <p> The department told Weekend Argus that a number of marine fishing resources which are found in the Western Cape are considered to be under pressure . <p> Some consequences include fines of R2 500 per charge with a maximum fine of R5 000 , according to Nqayi . <p> " Fishing permits and licenses are further issued subject to compliance with various other South African environmental legislations , " he said .
