<h> Amphan inflicts massive damage <p> Cyclone Amphan has left behind a trail of destruction in the country 's coastal areas , affecting more than a million people in nine districts in Khulna and Barishal divisions . <p> At least 12 people were killed and nearly 2.20 lakh houses damaged by the cyclone that swept through the south-western part on Wednesday with a wind speed of over 150 kilometres per hour . <p> It destroyed standing crops , vegetables and fruits on 1.76 lakh hectares of land , uprooted millions of trees and damaged fish farms , said officials . <p> More than 200 bridges and culverts were damaged in the coastal districts . <p> According to a primary estimate , the cyclone caused damage worth around Tk 1,100 crore , Shah Kamal , senior secretary at the disaster management and relief ministry , told The Daily Star yesterday . <p> Also , around 100 villages were submerged as onrushing water breached protection embankments . <p> Water Resources Minister Zahid Faruk said 7.5 kms of protection embankments were washed away at 84 points in 13 districts . <p> " We will repair these embankments soon , " he said in a statement . <p> Apart from the nine coastal districts , many areas in 17 adjacent districts were affected . <p> The cyclone made landfall on the Digha coast in India on Wednesday afternoon , ravaging parts of West Bengal . It then moved towards Bangladesh on Wednesday afternoon , lashing the Sundarbans in its path . It claimed at least 72 lives in West Bengal . <p> Shah Kamal said they succeeded in keeping the casualty and damages to a minimum though Amphan was an extremely severe cyclone . <p> " We had formed a coordination committee comprising public representatives and members of the armed forces to face the natural disaster . <p> " We have once again proved that Bangladesh is a role model in tackling any natural disaster . " <p> The cyclone hit 26 districts , of which three to four were very badly affected , he added . <p> More than 24 lakh people were moved to 14,636 permanent and temporary shelters in 19 coastal districts before the cyclone hit the country 's coast . <p> DEATHS <p> At least 12 people were killed and three others injured as the cyclone battered the coastal districts on Wednesday . <p> Of the dead , two drowned and five were killed as trees fell on them . <p> The deaths were reported from Kalapara and Galachipa upazilas of Patuakhali ; Charfashion and Borhanuddin upazilas of Bhola , Mathbaria and Indurkani upazilas of Pirojpur , Sadar upazila in Satkhira , Chowgachha upazila of Jashore and Sadar upazila in Jhenidah . <p> DESTRUCTION <p> Amphan totally or partially damaged 82,000 houses in Khulna Division and another 60,000 in Barishal Division . It caused tidal surges that washed away several embankments in low-lying coastal areas . <p> Our correspondents from the two divisions reported that the cyclone rendered tens of thousands of people homeless , destroying thatched houses and uprooting trees . <p> Abdur Rakib from Dakkhin Shakuchia village in Bhola 's Manpura Upazila said he and his family members spent Wednesday night at a cyclone centre . <p> When he returned home yesterday morning , he found that it had gone under water . <p> " We are yet to get any help , " Rakib told this newspaper yesterday afternoon . <p> Another affected villager , Mansur Gazi from Shyamnagar in Satkhira , used to run a small grocery shop . <p> He along with his family members took refuge in a nearby cyclone shelter and spent Wednesday night there . <p> Yesterday morning , he went out of the shelter to check on his shop . <p> To his dismay , Mansur found that his grocery shop was completely destroyed by the cyclone . His house was also damaged . <p> " I used to run my family with the earnings from the small shop . But now everything is gone . I do n't know how we will survive , " he said . <p> According to sources at Khulna Divisional Commissioner 's Office , 5.80 lakh people were affected in the three worst-hit districts -- Khulna , Satkhira and Bagerhat . <p> More than 32 kms of embankments were partially damaged and 133 villages went under water in the three districts due to heavy rains and tidal surges . Besides , mangoes worth around Tk 150 crore were damaged in Khulna Division . <p> Over 10,000 shrimp enclosures were washed away in Satkhira , Bagerhat and Khulna , according to fisheries offices in the districts . <p> Md Anwar Hossain Hawlader , commissioner of Khulna Division , said they were yet to complete the assessment of the damage . <p> " We are getting data from the district and upazila level and are still assessing it . We have not yet received information from some remote areas such as Dakkhin Bedkashi in Koyra upazila . " <p> The commissioner said they were trying their best to provide assistance to the affected people in the region . <p> Barishal Divisional Commissioner Yamin Chowdhury said onrushing water damaged protection embankments and flooded some areas in four districts . <p> Around six lakh people were affected and 60,000 houses were fully or partially damaged in the division , said officials . <p> In Bhola , two-km stretch of the embankment in Manpura upazila was damaged and 20 villages were flooded , said Mohammed Masud Alam Siddique , deputy commissioner of the district . <p> Moreover , 4.81 lakh people were affected and more than 10,000 houses were fully or partially damaged in Patuakhali . <p> Eight kms of embankments were also damaged , said Md Matiul Alom Chowdhury , deputy commissioner in the district . <p> NO ELECTRICITY <p> Around 50 lakh consumers of Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board ( BREB ) in Barishal and Khulna divisions were left without electricity till last night . <p> " About 1.5 crore clients lost electricity after the cyclone hit the country . Till last night , we managed to restore power supply to around one crore users , " BREB Chairman Maj Gen ( retd ) Moin Uddin told this newspaper . <p> DAMAGE OF SUNDARBANS <p> Initially , the forest department could not assess the extent of damage of the Sundarbans that took the brunt of the cyclone . <p> Amir Hossain Chowdhury , acting chief conservator of forest , said , they primarily learnt that many kewra trees were uprooted and all the 65 freshwater ponds inside the forest were flooded with seawater . <p> In a video message , Environment , Forest and Climate Change Minister Md Shahabuddin said the government formed four committees to find out the extent of damage of the mangrove forest . <p> The committees were asked to submit reports within three working days , he mentioned . <p> Our correspondents from Khulna and Barishal divisions contributed to this report
