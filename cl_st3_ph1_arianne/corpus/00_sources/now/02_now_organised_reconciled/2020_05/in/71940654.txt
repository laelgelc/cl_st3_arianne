( Reuters ) <h> HIGHLIGHTS <p> A lab in China 's Wuhan has denied claims that they created novel coronavirus <p> Wuhan Institute of Virology has been accused of creating SARS-CoV-2 or Covid-19 <p> There is still no conclusive answers as to where the disease started <p> Claims that the coronavirus pandemic originated in a laboratory in the Chinese city of Wuhan have no basis in fact , the head of the lab told Reuters , adding that there were still no conclusive answers as to where the disease started . <p> Conspiracy theorists have claimed SARS-CoV-2 , now responsible for more than 200,000 deaths worldwide , was synthesised by the Wuhan Institute of Virology ( WIV ) , based in the city where the disease was first identified . <p> Though the scientific consensus is that the coronavirus evolved naturally , such claims have gained traction . US President Donald Trump said on April 15 that his government was investigating whether it had originated in the Wuhan lab . <p> Yuan Zhiming , professor at WIV and the director of its National Biosafety Laboratory , said " malicious " claims about the lab had been " pulled out of thin air " and contradicted all available evidence . <p> " The WIV does not have the intention and the ability to design and construct a new coronavirus , " he said in written responses to questions from Reuters . " Moreover , there is no information within the SARS-CoV-2 genome indicating it was manmade . " <p> Some conspiracy theories were fuelled by a widely read scientific paper from the Indian Institute of Technology , since withdrawn , claiming that proteins in the coronavirus shared an " uncanny similarity " with those of HIV . However , most scientists now say SARS-CoV-2 originated in wildlife , with bats and pangolins identified as possible host species . <p> " In recent years , we have seen increasing risks posed by close contact between humans and wild animals , with global climate change and the continuous expansion of human activities , " he said . <p> All seven known human coronaviruses have origins in bats , mice or domestic animals , scientists say . <p> Yuan also rejected theories that the lab had accidentally released a coronavirus it had harvested from bats for research purposes , saying the lab 's biosecurity procedures were strictly enforced . <p> " High-level biosafety labs have sophisticated protective facilities and strict measures to ensure the safety of laboratory staff and protect the environment from contamination , " he said . <p> ' STILL NO ANSWERS ' <p> Conspiracy theories are common during epidemics . <p> Russian scientists claimed the SARS outbreak in 2002-2003 originated in a lab , and during the emergence of HIV/AIDS in the late 1970s , some political groups also claimed the virus had been " spliced " together by government scientists . <p> Though the new coronavirus was first identified in Wuhan , conspiracy theories circulating within China have suggested the virus did not originate there . <p> Chinese foreign ministry spokesman Zhao Lijian said on Twitter in March that the coronavirus might have emerged in the United States , and there has been speculation on Chinese social media that it reached Wuhan via the World Military Games , held there in October . <p> Yuan did not comment directly on the claims , but said there were " still no answers " about the virus 's origins . He cited a paper by British and German scientists published this month suggesting that the SARS-CoV-2 variant circulating in the United States was a more " primitive " version of the one in China , and might have appeared there first . <p> " Tracing the virus 's origin is a very challenging scientific question with strong uncertainty , " Yuan said . <p> China has been accused of underestimating its total number of cases and trying to cover up the origins of the disease , which the government rejects . <p> Asked whether his institute would cooperate with an international inquiry into the pandemic , Yuan said that he was unaware of " such a mechanism " , but that the laboratory was already inspected regularly . <p> He added that his institute was committed to transparency and would share all available data about the coronavirus in a timely fashion . <p> " I hope everyone will put aside their prejudices and biases in order to provide a rational environment for research on tracing the origin of the virus , " he said .
