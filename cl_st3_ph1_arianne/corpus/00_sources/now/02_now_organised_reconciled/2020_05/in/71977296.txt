<p> For almost a month now , PUBG Mobile has been sending out morse code clues on social media and curious in-game changes . All of that lead upto a fierce sandstorm on the mobile battle-royale 's Miramar map . <p> Players had an arduous challenge of competing for Chicken Dinners with limited visibility and glitchy navigation . At the end of the official 24-hour live stream for the event , PUBG Mobile left community members with a reworked map -- and a powerful message about the importance of protecting the Earth . Some areas of the update Miramar map saw the message ' More Green More Oxygen . ' <p> This event ushered in the #Fight4ThePlanet campaign in conjunction with non-profit organization Global Green and R&amp;B artist Jason Derulo . PUBG Mobile has pledged a $100,000 donation to Global Green , which will help rebuild underserved communities and ecosystems around the world by providing them with renewable energy , clean drinking water and climate rescue hubs after a climate change disaster takes place . To play your part in the campaign check out the details here . <p> " We hope to encourage more of our players to join in the ranks of protecting the earth together , " said Vincent Wang , General Manager of Global Publishing Department , Tencent Games . " With such a powerful community behind PUBG Mobile , we have the opportunity to make a real difference in helping the environment . We hope that our recent in-game sandstorm and #Fight4ThePlanet campaign has helped shed light on the on the importance of protecting the world 's current climate . "
