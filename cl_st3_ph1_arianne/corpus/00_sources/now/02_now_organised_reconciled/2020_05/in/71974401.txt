<h> Green loss to turn City of Joy into ' city of heat ' <h> Share <h> Green loss to turn City of Joy into ' city of heat ' <p> The City of Joy may get transformed into a " hot city " in the coming decades not because of any ' page three ' factor but because of the large-scale destruction of Kolkata 's green cover , experts say . <p> With about 5,500 trees felled by the super cyclone that his the city after 283 years Kolkata may witness a climate change of sorts getting robbed of about more than 5 lakh kilos of oxygen , experts at Jadavpur University say . <p> While Kolkata lost about 5,500 trees the iconic Botanical Gardens in Howrah saw about 1,000 trees getting damaged . The Pagla Gaach ( Mad tree ) and the Kalpa Brisksha -- the only surviving species of their kind in the world -- at Botanical Gardens too have suffered some damage , sources said . <p> " It is irreparable loss considering as well the fact that Sunderbans -- 100 miles down south near the sea -- has lost about 1.5 lakh trees meaning thereby that Kolkata will get that much less protection in the next cyclones , " said Partho Bhowmick an expert . <p> The double whammy is the " loss of trees coupled with carbon emission creating a condition for the cyclones to visit the city more regularly and the loss of green cover in Sunderbans ( on the verge of the sea ) denying the region the natural speed breaker for the cyclones , " said DP Mitra an environmental expert . <p> " Even if you plant trees at a brisk pace which is unlikely to happen understanding the bureaucratic velocity of work the trees that will be planted now will take at least 10 years -- if we plant some fast growing trees -- to grow to a level that can at least half-mange the situation , " said a Sutapa Saha a green cover expert . <p> Normally it takes 15 to 20 years for a tree to reach a state where it is able to absorb a good amount of carbon dioxide besides providing oxygen . A full-grown tree absorbs about 20-25 kg of CO2 which is a huge amount . <p> Considering the fact that an individual requires about 750 kg of oxygen one should be surrounded by about 10 trees . <p> A large number of trees were uprooted in the Rabindra Sarobar and Subhas Sarobar areas of the city . While the former being situated on 122 acres of land is considered to be the lung of South Kolkata the latter is called the lung of the northern part of the city . Jointly Rabindra Sarobar and Subhas Sarobar have seen a loss of more than 500 trees 50 percent of which were more than 30-50 years ' old . <p> " If we do not plant saplings now and so that they start taking shape in 2-3 years then Kolkata is really in for a problem in the decades or maybe centuries to come , " said a professor of Marine Sciences at JU.
