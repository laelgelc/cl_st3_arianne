<h> Boon for healing of natural ecosystem <p> Pollution is the introduction of the hazardous components in to the natural environment that cause adverse change and affect the environment and human health . Pollution is considered as the fifth leading risk factor for mortality worldwide . It results in 5 million deaths globally , where as it causes more than 2 million deaths in a year in India . India is the 5th most polluted country in world . The major types of pollution are air pollution , water pollution , soil pollution etc . <p> Industries , vehicle emissions , the burning of fossil fuels and other factory combustibles are the major causes of air pollution . Industrial emissions , poor disposal of wastes , mining , deforestation , use of fossil fuels and agricultural activities , fertilizers , pesticides , insecticides , dumping of solid waste etc. affect the land and water bodies and ultimately have adverse effect on human , animal , marine and wild life . <p> These sources release harmful substances like troposphere ozone ( O3 ) , sulphur dioxide ( SO2 ) , nitrogen dioxide ( NO2 ) , benzo(a) pyrene ( BaP ) and suspended particulate matter ( PM ) which have long term and short term effects on human health . The short-term effects include illnesses such as bronchitis , discomfort , irritation to the nose , throat , eyes , or skin . However , long-term health effects from pollution include heart disease , lung cancer , emphysema , asthma , fetal growth , and low birth weight , also cause hormonal and reproductive problems . Marine life is negatively affected by plastic and chemical waste from industries which kills fish , birds , marine mammals and sea turtles and destroys their natural habitats . Consumption of feed contaminated with toxic leads to the sudden death of animal . <p> Every year thousands of animals die due to plastic , chemicals or polluted drinking water . Pollution has adverse effect on our ecosystem and result in the loss of natural resources and habitat for wildlife . Pollution also causes the destruction of the ozone layer which is the protective layer that protects humans and plants from harmful Ultraviolet rays emitted by the Sun which is the cause of skin cancer . Chlorofluorocarbons ( CFCs ) is one of the main cause of the total ozone layer depletion . <p> Pollution needs to be dramatically reduced because it is destroying the environment we live in . It is contaminating our food and water which has hazardous effects on all living organism in this planet . Before it is too late we have to do combine efforts to protect our planet from all type of pollution . The current Covid-19 pandemic has drastically changed our daily lives . <p> Industrial activity has shut down . Fewer planes in the sky , travel halted and industries slowed or stopped , cars and buses are stopped due to restrictions , which according to different reports from different countries , has lead the level of Co2 and No2 emission to drastically come down . NASA satellite measurements have revealed significant reductions in air pollution over the major metropolitan areas of the Northeast United States . NASA also reported 20-year low air pollution in northern India . India is n't the only place to see cleaner air . <p> Similar reductions have been observed in other regions of the world . Many other regions are also observing significant environmental benefits as a consequence of lockdown due to the Coronavirus outbreak . For instance , researchers in New York registered a 50 per cent decline in carbon monoxide levels . There are also reports that ozone layer is also recovering . There is changes in atmospheric circulation , which have positive effect on our environment resulting in decreases in our air , water and environmental pollution . These recent improvements in air quality have come at a high cost , as communities grapple with widespread lockdowns and shelter-in-place orders as a result of the spread of COVID-19 . <p> Basically it is lockdown for people , but freedom for birds and animals . Birds and wild animals must be feeling relaxed . Due to less movement of both people and vehicles there is less noise pollution and air pollution and as such the communication between bird is more and people are now hearing more singing , chirping and chattering of birds . Movement of marine birds has increased drastically along their increased population in coastal areas . Large number of birds which were not seen earlier has been found roaming and flying over seas and beeches in different cities . <p> Due to the Covid-19 lockdown , wild animals are able to explore areas of their habitat that humans had encroached . When noise and air pollution in cities reduced so suddenly and drastically , of course , the animals living in our vicinity will be more fearless to show themselves . The movement of wild animal have been spotted since lock down , frequently in different cities while domestic animals that are dependent on people suffer shortage of feed and fodder . With a much cleaner environment , it was reported that Himalayan peak was visible after a couple of decades from different adjoining cities . <p> A healing Ozone layer and a rise in the number of animals foraying into human spaces has been observed . In true sense nature is not greedy and selfish like human being . it wants us to be respectful of nature and its creatures . " Lockdown is certainly a boon for nature and gives mother nature a chance to repair herself . <p> It is a big challenge for governments and businesses to consider how things can be done differently after the pandemic , to hold on to temporary improvements in air quality . They should certainly lay efforts to fight climate change . The pandemic has shown us how the future might look with less air pollution , and how to fight together and take important measures to tackle the different types of pollution . <p> By proper Solid waste treatment , by planting more and more trees , by switching to use CNG vehicle , by stopping use of polythene , by using bioplastic , by conservation of natural resources , by effectively saving the water , by restricting open burning of biomass and fossil fuels , by avoiding burning leaves , trash , and other materials instead of burning turn over Vermicomposting , we can reduce pollution to a big extent ..
