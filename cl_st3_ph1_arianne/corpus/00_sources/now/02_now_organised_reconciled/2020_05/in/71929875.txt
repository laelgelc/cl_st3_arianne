<p> A 20-minute documentary film on " Wetlands of Manipur " was released in Imphal on Friday . <p> The film focused on the major wetlands of Manipur both in hills and valley region namely Loktak , Zaimeng , Zeilad , Pumlen , Ikop , Waithou , Lamjao , Karung Pat and Yaral Pat . <p> It was produced by State forest department under Nature Learning Centre , National Mission on Himalayan Studies , Ministry of Environment , Forests &amp; Climate Change <p> The film also highlighted the present conditions of the wetland , role played by the wetlands in maintaining the ecosystem equilibrium and supporting livelihood , bio-diversity and threats being faced by the wetlands . <p> The film also emphasized the socio-cultural relationship of wetlands with the Manipuri society . <p> Northeast Now is a multi-app based hyper-regional bilingual news portal .
