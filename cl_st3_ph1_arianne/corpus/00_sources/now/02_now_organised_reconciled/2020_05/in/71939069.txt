<h> FACTBOX-Latest on the worldwide spread of the coronavirus <p> A mental illness crisis is looming as millions of people worldwide are surrounded by death and disease and forced into isolation , poverty and anxiety by the COVID-19 pandemic , United Nations said . <p> More than 4.4 million people have been reported to be infected globally and 299,435 have died , according to a Reuters tally , as of 2217 GMT on Thursday .
