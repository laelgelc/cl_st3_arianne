<p> The Andhra Pradesh High Court on Friday ordered the State Government to seize the company premises of the LG Polymers chemical plant at Vishakapatnam , from where the leakage of the Styrene chemical occurred during the wee hours of May 7 , leaving many persons dead and injured . <p> The Court also restrained the Directors of the Company from leaving the country without the leave of the court . <p> The Bench of Chief Justice J K Maheshwari and Justice Lalitha Kanneganti issued the following directions : <p> The premises of the company shall be completely seized ; <p> No one should be allowed to enter into the premises including the Directors of the company ; <p> If a Committee , if any , appointed wants to inspect the premises , they are at liberty to . However , they shall put a note on the Register maintained at the gate of the Company regarding the said inspection and while returning , a note regarding the act down on the premises should also be noted . <p> None of the assets , movable or immovable , fixture , machinery and contents shall be allowed to be shifted without the leave of the Court <p> The surrendered passports of the Company Directors should not be released without the leave of the Court . <p> The Company Directors are not allowed to go outside India without leave <p> The Goverment should inform the Court whether any permission was obtained to restart the company 's operations during the lockdown period . If not , an action taken report on this aspect should be filed , <p> Given the grievance that multiple Committees over the issue have been appointed ( by the NG , the Central Government and the State Government ) the Government authorities may apprise the Court as to which Committee shall fulfil the purpose to answer all questions raised on the issue . <p> The Court also noted that the action taken reports filed by the State and Central Government were silent on the following issues : <p> LG Polymers has been operating without a valid Environmental Clearance from the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) ; <p> The inhibitor concentration in the storage tank was not checked ; <p> Refrigeration system was no working properly ; <p> The radius of the vulnerable zone was exetended up to 6.3km from the source ; <p> There are several hospitals , educational institutions , places of worship , railway stations and airport within the vulnerable zone ; <p> The bystander population should have been informed of the risk that they were in and trained in evacuation procedures in the event of an accident as per Manufacture , Storage and Import of Hazardous Chemicals Rules , 1989 . But this has not been done ; <p> The siren/ alarm system did not function . <p> After recording that the Government authorities had prayed for time to respond on these aspects , the Court also direct the Government to apprise the Court on the following additional issues , i.e. : <p> What is the net worth of LG Polymers Pvt . Ltd. as per the provisions of the Companies Act , but not as per the book value ? <p> Why was styrene monomer permitted to be transported to South Korea and who is the person responsible for the same , when no Court permission was granted for the same and when no investigation/ inspection team was appointed and no Magisterial enquiry was made , despite these being a requirement after the registration of the crime ? <p> The Court passed the directions in a suo moto case taken by it following the deadly gas leak . <p> While taking suo moto notice of the tragedy on May 7 , the Court had noted that Styrene is notified as a dangerous substance under the provisions of the Environmental Protection Act and as per the Chemical Accident ( Emergency Planning , Preparedness , and Response ) Rules of 1996 , certain crisis response steps need to be taken , such as formulation of Crisis alert system , central crisis group , state crisis group all the way down to local crisis group . <p> " It is a matter of enquiry and assessment that the provisions of the said Rules have been observed or not " , the Court had observed .
