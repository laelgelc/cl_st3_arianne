<h> US election cycles : how long do US elections last ? <p> US elections are held every four years and always on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November . Electoral campaigns do not follow any official time frames and can vary in length . Still , this means US elections tend to be far more prolonged than other western democracies and can last for as long as 500 or even 600 days from start to finish .
