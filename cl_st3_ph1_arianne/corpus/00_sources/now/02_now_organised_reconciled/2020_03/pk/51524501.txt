<h> Use and sale of plastic bags banned in Quetta <p> The Ministry of Climate Change , along with the Government of Balochistan , has imposed a ban on the use and sale of polythene bags across Quetta . <p> According to Balochistan Environment Secretary Abdul Saboor Kakar , fines will be imposed on those who will violate the law . " We have directed personnel of the Frontier Corps , Levies Force and police to enable strict implementation of the ban , " he said . <p> " The high court and the Supreme Court have also given clear instructions of the ban , " Kakar said . <p> The growing use of plastic bags in Quetta has disrupted the system of cleanliness in the city . <p> " Our environment is the place where we breathe , live and eat so we should take care of it and say no to plastic , " Quetta Assistant Commissioner Nida Kazmi said . <p> Residents too have expressed delight over the ban . They have , however , demanded that the government provide them an alternative of plastic bags .
