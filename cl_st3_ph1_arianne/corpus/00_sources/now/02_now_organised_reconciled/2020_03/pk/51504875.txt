<h> Steps taken to protect ecosystem of rare snow leopard breed <h> National <p> Islamabad : Pakistani ecosystem is blessed with a rare breed of snow leopard and we have taken number of steps to protect the wild animal and the ecosystem it lives in , Adviser to the Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Amin Aslam said . <p> He expressed these views while addressing the participants of the World Wildlife Day event here as the chief guest . The event was organised here on Tuesday by the Ministry of Climate Change and Snow Leopard Foundation Pakistan at the FAUST University . Malik Amin told the participants that Pakistan Snow Leopard and Ecosystem Protection programme ( PSLEP ) launched by the climate change ministry was among the three foreign funded projects that aimed at biodiversity and environment restoration in Pakistan . These projects are very successful in Pakistan .
