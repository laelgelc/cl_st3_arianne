<h> Half of World 's Beaches will disappear due to Climate Crisis <p> According to new research , by the end of the century , almost half of the world 's sandy beaches will retreat significantly due to climate-driven coastal flooding and human interference . <p> Wildlife will be endangered due to sand erosion and it can inflict a heavy toll on the coastal settlements . These zones will not have buffer zones for protection against rising sea levels and storm surges . <p> Also , it will become increasingly expensive for the government to take measures to mitigate the damage . <p> Erosion will destroy 36,097km ( 22,430 miles ) or 13.6% of sandy coastlines in 30 years . These coastlines have been identified by scientists for the Joint Research Centre ( JRC ) of the European commission from satellite images . It is predicted that the situation will get worse in the second half of the century as 95,061km or 25.7% of Earth 's beaches will be washed away . <p> According to the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , if the world continues to emit at its current pace the sea levels will rise by an @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of beaches or 13% of the planet 's ice-free coastline will go underwater . <p> Even if we expect the best-case scenario , the UK , Australia , Canada , Chile , Mexico , China , and the US will lose 1531km , 14,849km , 14,425km , 6,659km , 5,488km , 5,440km , and 5,530km of its sandy coast . <p> As per the study , the most hit areas will be Dorset , north Devon , Great Yarmouth , Barrow-in-Furness and north-east Lincolnshire in the UK . <p> Michalis Vousdoukas , an oceanographer at the JRC and lead author of the study , published in the journal Nature Climate Change said , " The length of threatened seashores incorporates locations that will be submerged by more than 100 meters , assuming there are no physical limits to potential retreat .
