<p> Minister for Planning , Development &amp; Reform ( PD&amp;R ) , Makhdoom Khusro Bakhtiar has said that CPEC growth projections and investment thus far is built upon the successful development of Gwadar as the port city is a focal point for the corridor to take off . <p> He expressed these views while presiding over a steering committee meeting on Gwadar Smart Port City Master Plan here on Thursday . Secretary Planning , Development &amp; Reform Zafar Hasan , Project Director CPEC , Hassan Daud and officials from government of Balochistan , Ministry of Maritime Affairs , Gwadar Development Authority , Gwadar Port Authority , Ministry of Defence , Ministry of Economic Affairs Division , Ministry of Climate Change , Board of Investment , and Chinese companies attended the meeting , said a press release issued here . <p> The minister was briefed about the progress of Gwadar Smart Port City Master Plan . He emphasized on the need to determine the comparative incentives which can be given to Gwadar in order to give it a competitive edge in the region. -PR
