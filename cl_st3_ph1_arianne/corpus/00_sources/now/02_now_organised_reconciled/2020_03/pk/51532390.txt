<h> President highlights importance of agriculture in country 's economy <p> President Dr Arif Alvi on Thursday highlighted the importance of agriculture sector in a country 's economy at National Dialogue on Agriculture , a flagship initiative of Speaker National Assembly . <p> The dialogue amongst different stakeholders was organized by the National Assembly Standing Committee on National Food Security and Research , International Fund for Agriculture Development ( IFID ) and Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) . Many of the invitees including Prime Minister 's Advisor on Finance and Revenue , Dr. Abdul Hafeez Shaikh did not attend the consultative sessions . <p> The objectives of the National Dialogue on Agriculture were as follows : ( i ) identify the constraints and obstacles which continue to impede progress in realising Pakistan 's full agriculture potential ; ( ii ) propose short and medium term policy makers aimed at policy makers to effectively address structured constraints and market failures ; ( iii ) identify the pathways for uplifting Pakistan 's agro-economy with a particular focus on access , inclusivity , sustainability and food security ; ( iv ) identify the pathways for enhancing Pakistan 's agriculture export @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and inter-ministerial dialogue on agriculture ; and ( vi ) to facilitate consensus on agriculture priorities for upcoming budget . <p> Addressing the inaugural session , President Dr. Arif Alvi said that agriculture has and will remain the backbone of the country 's economy and food security is also immensely important for the national security . He argued that nations which can not produce adequate grains for their people are invariably embroiled in a host of problems . <p> He said that Pakistan should formulate policies in the agriculture sector keeping in view past failures and successes . <p> Arif Alvi emphasized the need for provision of insect resistant seeds to farmers to enhance per acre yield of different crops . For quality seeds , the research institutions will have to play their part . He said the relevant universities will also have to produce the manpower keeping in view the market demand . <p> The President said agriculture is the biggest consumer of water and there is a need to adopt modern techniques to ensure its efficient use . <p> Referring to the increasing influence of artificial intelligence and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ also need to remain cognizant of the changes taking place in the world and adopt them . <p> Arif Alvi said that modern farms with best farming practices should be developed in each district in order to encourage the farmers to emulate the same in their fields . <p> The President said the government has taken some important decisions to uplift the sector . He however said that " we will have to bring more value addition to the crops and provide better market access to the farmers community " . <p> Addressing the ceremony , Speaker National Assembly Asad Qaiser said the development of agriculture sector is indispensable for overall development of Pakistan . He said this sector has a pivotal role in poverty alleviation and food security . <p> He said a parliamentary committee on agriculture has been established to become the voice of the farmers and protect their genuine interests . <p> Minister for Food Security Khusro Bakhtiar said a national agriculture emergency program has started at a cost of 277 billion rupees .
