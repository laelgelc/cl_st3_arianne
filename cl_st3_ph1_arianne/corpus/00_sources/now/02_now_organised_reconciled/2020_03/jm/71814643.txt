<h> Jamaica Observes World Water Day <p> March 22 is observed annually by the United Nations ( UN ) as World Water Day . <p> The occasion is used to promote the sustainable management of freshwater resources globally . <p> This year , the day is being observed under the theme : ' Water and Climate Change ' . <p> In Jamaica , the National Water Sector Policy and Implementation Plan 2019 is the document which makes provision for the long-term sustainable management and development of the island 's freshwater resources . <p> It is one of 31 sector plans that form the foundation for the development of Vision 2030 Jamaica , the country 's long-term National Development Plan . <p> The policy , which was promulgated in 2000 , was tabled in Parliament last year . <p> It focuses on the nation 's water resources both in terms of quality and quantity , as well as measures to improve the provision of sanitation . <p> She explains that ' access ' , under the policy , refers to citizens having at least 50 litres of water daily , within 500 metres of their homes , at their disposal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , it is currently not financially or physically feasible to provide in-home piped water to some of the more remote areas . <p> The policy stipulates that in such cases , a potable water station must be within 50 metres of residents ' homes . <p> The Government facilitates this through the collaborative efforts of members of the water sector . <p> " It is an integrated approach that is being taken to implement the policy . These principles ensure that we effectively respond to threats to our water resources , " Ms. Gibson states . <p> Regarding the theme for World Water Day , Ms. Gibson says the policy seeks to promote the adoption of measures to address water and climate change issues . <p> She states that the Government is cognizant of the demand for improvements and expansions , and the limitations affecting operations in the water sector . <p> " We , as citizens , play a very critical role in the sustainability of our water resources ; The remaining 16 per cent is provided by surface water . <p> Approximately 25 per cent of the water abstracted is used to meet the demand for potable water and the remaining 75 per cent is used for irrigation .
