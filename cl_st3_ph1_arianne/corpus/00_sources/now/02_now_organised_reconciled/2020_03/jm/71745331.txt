<p> The project is being done through the Jamaica Social Investment Fund ( JSIF ) Disaster Vulnerability Reduction Project ( DVRP ) , with a loan from the World Bank . <p> According to a release from JSIF , the objective of the project is to enhance the resilience of the Port Royal Street corridor to the adverse effects of natural disasters and climate risks . <p> The corridor is an integral part of the east-west transportation network in the Kingston Metropolitan Area . The project aims to create protective infrastructure from the effects of storm surges , which have been projected to be impacted by climate change . <p> The scope of work will be on the coastline from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Trade building to the Rae Town Fishing Village , and will include the installation of one kilometre of composite seawall and armour stone revetment structure , upgrading of drainage and the rehabilitation and raising of one kilometre of the existing roadway . <p> Additional works include the construction of a 4.7 metre-wide boardwalk to improve the site 's aesthetics and promote recreational activity along the shoreline , and the creation of a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ along the shoreline.
