<h> Earth 's climate worsened in 2019 : WMO report <h> Last Updated : Wednesday 11 March 2020 <p> Earth 's climate deteriorated sharply in 2019 , according to a new report by the World Meteorological Organization ( WMO ) . <p> The WMO 's annual Statement on the State of the Global Climate , 2019 -- released on March 10 , 2020 -- stated accelerating land and ocean heat , a rise in sea level and rapidly melting ice were to blame for the increase in global temperature . <p> 2019 was the second-warmest year on instrumental temperature record with the annual average temperature last year at 1.1 degrees Celsius , above pre-industrial levels , according to the report . <p> Although 2016 was warmer , temperature increased because of El-Nino , a large-scale ocean-atmosphere climate interaction . <p> The decade between 2010-2019 was also the hottest , with each decade since 1980 being hotter than the previous one , according to the report . <p> " We are currently way off track to meeting either the 1.5 degrees Celsius or 2 degrees Celsius targets that the Paris Agreement called for , " said United Nations ' Secretary-General @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Excess heat from global warming is absorbed by the oceans . In 2019 , warming reached 2 kilometres below ocean surfaces , beating a record set in 2018 . <p> Nearly two months of unusually high temperatures were recorded in the oceans last year , while 84 per cent of oceans faced at least one marine heatwave , according to the report . <p> As a result of the oceans warming up , a thermal expansion of its waters led to a rise in sea level by almost 30 per cent , the report stated . This rise in sea level was the highest last year . <p> Another consequence of the ocean heating up was ocean acidification and deoxygenation . The imbalance of pH values as a result , affected marine ecosystems . <p> The ability of organisms like mussels , crustaceans and corals to calcify , affected their growth and reproduction . <p> The melting of ice sheets in Greenland and Antarctica contributed to the rise in sea level . <p> The Greenland ice sheet lost about 329 giga-tonnes of ice and recorded the seventh lowest ice @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ increasing toll on human health as well , with over 1,462 killed across the world , according to the report . <p> Record-breaking temperatures were recorded in Australia , Europe , India and Japan . <p> The heatwave in Japan led to 100 deaths and 18,000 being hospitalised , while 20,000 emergency admissions were reported from heat-related maladies in France . It also recorded an all-time high temperature of 46 degrees Celsius in 2019 . <p> Hunger and food shortage was one of the biggest fallouts of climate change . <p> An earlier aim to achieve the ' Zero Hunger ' target chalked out by the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development , was unlikely to be met . <p> Over 820 million people suffered from hunger in 2018 . <p> Another problem that complicated the issue of food security were locust attacks . Locusts multiplied because of unusual dryness in March and much of April , last year , followed by unusually heavy rainfall and floods in October-December . <p> Nearly 22.5 million people in the Horn of Africa ( 6.7 million in Ethiopia , 3.1 million in Kenya , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , 5.8 million in the Sudan ) were critically food-insecure because of locust attacks . <p> These attacks were the worst in 25 years in Ethiopia , while Kenya experienced the worst attacks in the past seven decades . <p> Floods killed over 2,200 in India , Nepal , Bangladesh and Myanmar during the monsoon season which started late but finished with rainfall above the long term average . <p> The 12-month rainfall average of the United States was 962 mm , the highest on record . Losses because of flooding were estimated at over $20 billion in the US . <p> Heavy rain caused losses of about $2.5 billion in Brazil , Argentina and Uruguay . <p> Iran and drought-prone regions in east Africa experienced heavy flooding between October and early November last year . <p> More than 22 million people were displaced because of hydro-meteorological events like cyclones and floods . <p> Wildfires raged through higher latitude regions , including Siberia ( Russian Federation ) , Alaska ( US ) and in some parts of the Arctic , where such occurrences were extremely rare . <p> Australia experienced @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , with repeated outbreaks continuing on to the next year , according to the report . <p> In 2020 , 33 people died and over 2,000 properties were lost because of wildfires .
