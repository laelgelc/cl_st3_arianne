It is a well known fact that Black Carbon ( BC ) is emitted in the atmosphere due to incomplete combustion of fossil fuels , motor vehicle and aircraft exhausts , biofuel , and biomass . It consists of elemental carbon in several forms . <p> The largest of black carbon emitters are Asia , Latin America , and Africa . Some estimates say that China and India together account for 25-35 per cent of global black carbon emissions . On a global basis , approximately 20 per cent of black carbon is emitted from burning bio fuels , 40 per cent from fossil fuels , and 40 per cent from open biomass burning . According to a reply given in Parliament by the Minister for Environment on 13 December last year , black carbon is monitored on Himalayan glaciers by various institutions . <p> As monitored by GB Pant Institute of Himalayan Environment and Development , BC concentration over the Parbati Glacier in Himachal Pradesh , is ranging between 0.34 ? g/m3 and 0.56 ? g/m3 .
