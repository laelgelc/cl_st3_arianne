<h> Indian diplomat Vikas Swarup touched up on various topics during his address at the 43rd session of the UN Human Rights Council , including terrorism , climate change , inequality , J&amp;K and Pakistan . <p> At the 43rd U.N . Human Rights Council meeting in Geneva on February 26 , Vikas Swarup , Secretary ( West ) , the Ministry of External Affairs , touched upon various topics of national interest . Swarup discussed climate change , terrorism , developments in J&amp;K and Pakistan 's increased infiltration attempts along the LoC . <p> Swarup emphasized that Jammu and Kashmir " was , is and shall forever remain " an integral part of India . The Indian delegate highlighted the developments in the Union Territory and how various restrictions have been relaxed . <p> Read the full statement of Swarup 's address at the UN Council meeting below : <p> Mister Vice-President , <p> I congratulate the President of the Human Rights Council on assuming this important responsibility .
