<h> ' Australia must sign a treaty with its First People ' <h> Last Updated : Saturday 25 January 2020 <p> It was on January 26 , 1788 , that the ' First Fleet ' , a flotilla of British ships travelling from England reached the waters of Port Jackson , the harbour of Sydney , with express orders to find a British penal colony in what is now Australia . Since that time , the date is commemorated as ' Australia Day ' by white Australians . For aboriginal Australians though , the date is a ' Day of Mourning ' , as it started the process of their gradual dispossession from their native lands . <p> Down To Earth spoke to Melbourne-based aboriginal activist Lidia Thorpe on her thoughts about aboriginal Australia on the 232nd anniversary of the First Fleet landing , especially in the aftermath of the recent Australian bushfires . We have traditional ways of caring and protecting our country . These have been ignored by the colonisers . We have ways of burning the countryside which is not similar to the bushfires that we have seen . <p> However , it is not just bushfires but all sorts of injustice going on over our land that also contributes to climate change such as logging of our forests and the taking of our water . All that has contributed to these disasters which we have witnessed . <p> A number of aboriginal activists like Neil Morris have said that the ' fires have also deepened the trauma aboriginal people feel over the British colonisation of the country in 1788 ' . The aboriginal people have one of the highest incarceration rates in Australia . <p> All of these mass injustices to our people and our land , is one and the same . We refer to our land as our mother and we believe that one has to care for the land the same way as one would care for one 's mother . You have to nourish , love and respect your mother and that has not been happening for 250 years , which is very hurtful .
