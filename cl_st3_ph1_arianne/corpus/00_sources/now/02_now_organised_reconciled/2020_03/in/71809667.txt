<p> Mumbai : Roughly one in every two individuals ( 52% ) will be living in water-stressed regions by 2050 as climate change affects the water cycle and makes it harder to manage resources , said the World Water Development Report 2020 released by the United Nations ( UN ) on March 22 , 2020 , marked as the World Water Day . <p> Countries in the tropics , mountains , island nations and those in the far north will be the worst hit , the report estimated . India , sliced down the middle by the Tropic of Cancer , and with nearly a dozen Himalayan states and union territories to the north , will be impacted by these developments . <p> Climate change is also a poverty multiplier that could force 100 million people into extreme poverty by 2030 , according to the report . By changing rainfall patterns , and precipitating frequent floods and droughts , it would severely affect those in agriculture and fisheries , as IndiaSpendreported from over seven states in India in the past two years .
