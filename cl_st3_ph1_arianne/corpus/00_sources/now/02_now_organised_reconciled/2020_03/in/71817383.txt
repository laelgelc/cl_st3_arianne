<h> Committee meets to reassess complete mining ban in Jharkhand district <h> Last Updated : Thursday 19 March 2020 <p> The periphery of Saranda has turned red due to the existing iron ore mines Photo : Sayantan Bera <p> A committee set up by the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEFCC ) to reassess mining regulations contained in the ' Management Plan for Sustainable Mining ( MPSM ) in Saranda and Chaibasa in Singhbhum district , Jharkhand ' , met for the first time on January 16 , 2020 . <p> Down To Earth has access to the minutes of the meeting . <p> Saranda and Chaibasa -- in Jharkhand 's West Singhbhum district -- had large iron ore reserves , DK Tiwari , the state 's Chief Secretary , wrote in a letter to the MoEFCC on March 2019 . <p> The letter contained a request to ' revisit ' the Management Plan for Sustainable Mining ( MPSM ) , which regulates mining in Chaibasa and the Saranda Sal forest area . <p> The Saranda forest -- the biggest Sal forest in Asia -- was once a hotbed of leftwing extremism . After evidence @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Shah Commission in 2014 , the need to draw up a plan for sustainable mining was felt . <p> This led to the formulation of the MPSM . <p> " The conservation zone is a repository to huge iron ore resources and so the stipulation in the MSPM report for complete ban on mining in conservation zone should be revisited , " Tiwari wrote in a letter to CK Mishra , secretary at MoEFCC . <p> The union ministry accepted the state 's request on July 11 , 2019 and asked the Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education ( ICFRE ) to conduct a study to reassess or modify MPSM . <p> The committee to reassess the ban was formed soon after the chief secretary wrote to the ministry to open up the conservation zone for mining . <p> The committee 's focus was on forest patches in the conservation zone that could be utilised for mining operations . <p> It also recommended a re-digitisation exercise of the forest areas -- especially in mineralised zones -- and a verification of boundaries by the Jharkhand government . <p> " The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ patches falling under MPSM designated conservation zone , shall be subject to stringent terms and conditions , " the minutes said . <p> ICFRE recommended conducting the study over a period of three seasons . The Jharkhand Mines and Geology Department , however , sought an early completion of the study , according to the minutes . <p> The committee also proposed for the following to be included in the MPSM : <p> establishment of iron ore beneficiation plant <p> a legal proposal for declaration of MPSM-designated Conservation Zones as Conservation Areas <p> guidelines for use of District Mineral Fund ( DMF ) and Corporate Social Responsibility ( CSR ) funds for the benefits of affected people <p> institutional mechanism for monitoring and evaluation of mining and rehabilitation activities at central and stat government level <p> carrying out comprehensive zonation of mineral bearing areas and preparation of master plan for mineralised zone for systematic mining as amendment /modification in MPSM <p> The committee also finalised its terms of reference and sent it to MoEFCC for final approval . <p> The terms of referene include an evaluation of iron ore and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ International cases of mining in biodiversity-rich areas and a mapping of elephant corridors . <p> Mining as a source of employment for the local population and revenue to the state , were also added to the committee 's terms . <p> We are a voice to you ; you have been a support to us .
