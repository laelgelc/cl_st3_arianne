<h> Modification of natural landscapes and pandemics <p> Let us resolve to leave the standing forests and other natural landscapes alone and revisit the provisions of forest land diversion under the legal framework to minimise biodiversity loss and buffer humanity from zoonotic emerging infectious diseases . <p> India has the second largest population in the world with over half of the territory used as cropland . India 's food production will need to increase substantially in the coming decades due to an expected population size of more than 1.6 billion in 2050 .
