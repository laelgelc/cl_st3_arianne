<h> Centre moves draft to amend EC-seeking process <p> The Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change has issued a draft notification for the amendment to the Environmental Impact Assessment Notification , 2006 ( EIA Notification , 2020 ) to completely revise the existing process of environmental clearance . <p> The process of granting environmental clearance involves four stages of screening , scoping , public consultation and appraisal . However , in the draft notification the ministry has exempted all projects under category ' B2 ' of the schedule mentioned in the 2006 notification for requirement of scoping and preparation of EIA report . <p> Scoping refers to the process to determine detailed and comprehensive terms of reference ( ToR ) addressing all relevant environmental concerns for the preparation of an Environmental Impact Assessment /Environment Management Report with respect to a project or activity for which prior environmental clearance is sought . <p> On acceptance of the application under category B2 including all highway projects in border areas , building constructions , new and expansion projects in notified industrial estates within 7 working days of its receipt , the Standard Terms of References will be automatically issued @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) by the regulatory authority . <p> The ministry has invited written objections or suggestions on the proposal contained in the draft notification within a period of 60 days to the Secretary , Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , at New Delhi . <p> It says that no EIA report shall be required for the projects listed under Category ' B2 ' . However , EMP Report as per the generic structure given at Appendix-XI shall be prepared through ACO and submitted along with the application . <p> According to the ministry , clearances from other regulatory bodies or authorities shall not be required prior to receipt of applications for prior-EC or prior-EP , as the case may be , or scoping , or appraisal , or decision by the regulatory authority concerned except for approval of mining plan , in-principle approval for diversion of Forestland , projects falling in CRZ areas and acquisition of land . <p> The ministry further proposes some changes in the preparation of Environment Impact Assessment Report which requires baseline data for one season other than monsoon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ River Valley projects . However , such baseline data shall not be older than three years at the time of submission of draft EIA Report to the SPCB or UTPCC for Public Consultation . <p> In order to expedite the process of granting Standard Terms of Reference ( ToR ) for the expansion proposals where there is no examination of alternative sites involved , the Ministry proposes to introduce the concept of generation of an online Standard Terms of Reference ( ToR ) . <p> However , the draft says that in case of all applications seeking prior EC for modernisation within the existing premises or mine lease area without increase in the production capacity , there is no need of scoping , EIA report , or reference before appraisal committee and undertaking of public consultation . <p> However , if the increase in production capacity is up to 10 per cent then there is requirement of revised EMP and if it is more than 10 and up to 25 per cent then the project proponent will have obtain EMP and also refer the proposal to the Appraisal Committee and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 50 per cent then the requirement of revised EIA report , EMP and referral to Appraisal Committee is mandatory for grant of Prior Environment Clearance . <p> Interestingly , public consultation is made mandatory for category ' A ' and category ' B1 ' projects of new or expansion proposals or modernisation with capacity increase of over 50 per cent . <p> The draft proposes to conduct public hearing at the site or in its close proximity of the proposed project and in case of the project area located in more than one district then district-wise public hearing to be conducted for ascertaining concerns of local affected persons . <p> It has also asked to invite responses in writing from other concerned persons having a plausible stake in the environmental aspects of the project .
