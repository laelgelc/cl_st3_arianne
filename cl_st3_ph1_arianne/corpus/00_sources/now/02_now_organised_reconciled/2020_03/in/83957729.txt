<p> Srinagar , Mar 1 : A new phenomenon of unseasonal hailstorms is causing huge damage to the horticulture crops in Jammu and Kashmir . <p> Data released by Meteorological Department , Kashmir ( MET ) , 29 high-velocity hailstorms have occurred in Jammu and Kashmir since 2017 . <p> " The number of hailstorms in 2017 was seven , while the number stood at nine in 2018 . The year 2019 saw a surge with 13 hailstorms reported across J&amp;K , " the data revealed . <p> A hailstorm is an unusual weather phenomenon in which balls of ice , called hail , fall from the sky . The ice balls are nothing more than solid precipitation that will form under certain conditions . <p> MET data indicated that instances of hailstorm were observed from March to September . <p> Professor Shakil Romshoo , Department of Earth Sciences , University of Kashmir said in the last three years , Kashmir has been repeatedly battered by different extreme events like untimely snowfall and hailstorm causing huge damage to crops and orchards . <p> " Among other extreme events , hailstorms are mostly observed during pre-monsoon season from March to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has baffled the scientists is that the frequency of hailstorms has increased in June , July , and September . " These are all manifestations of climate change , " said Romshoo . <p> Though occurrences of hailstorm are unavoidable , a need is being felt for better forecasting and preparations . <p> " The number of automatic observation weather stations should be increased in J&amp;K . This can help in issuing severe weather warnings when the hail reaches a damaging size , as it can cause serious damage to structures , crops and live stocks , " Romshoo said . <p> Director MET , Kashmir , Sonam Lotus said with an adequate number of Doppler Weather Radars , the department will be able to monitor and forecast better .
