<p> Officials and participants during the awareness programme on importance of Forest and Wildlife . <p> Kohima , March 11 ( MExN ) : An awareness programme on importance of forest and wildlife organized by Nagaland Zoological Park , Department of Environment , Forest and Climate Change was held at Botanical Garden , Kohima on March 7 . <p> The programme was attended by staff of Blyth 's Tragopan Conservation and Breeding Centre , Puliebadze Wildlife Sanctuary , teachers and students of Classes 6-8 of Dainty Buds and Mount Sinai Higher Secondary schools Kohima . <p> Speaking on the occasion , Director of Nagaland Zoological Park , Rangapahar , Dr Prabhat Kumar , IFS stressed on the importance of forest and wildlife and the need to conserve and protect it . He spoke on the consequences faced by wildlife due to excessive hunting for both commercial and livelihood . In this , he encouraged the younger generation to have the mindset of protecting the wildlife . <p> A quiz was conducted wherein basic general knowledge about Nagaland was asked , while field visit was also conducted . <p> The programme led by Forester-I Kiushensi S.
