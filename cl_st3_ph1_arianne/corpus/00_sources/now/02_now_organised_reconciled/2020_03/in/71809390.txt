<h> March 21 was proclaimed as the International Day of Forests by the United Nations General Assembly in the year 2012 . <p> New Delhi : The Coronavirus pandemic has taught us the importance of nature . Forests work as one of the important chains of the ecosystem . <p> To celebrate these natural sources of environment , March 21 is observed as International Day of Forests around the globe . This year , the day falls on a Saturday . <p> All the nations around the world join hands to celebrate the day by taking a pledge to preserve their forests . <p> International Day of Forests : History <p> March 21 was proclaimed as the International Day of Forests by the United Nations General Assembly in the year 2012 . <p> Today , the day is celebrated by the joint partnership of the United Nations Forum on Forests and the Food and Agriculture Organization ( FAO ) in collaboration with Collaborative Partnership on Forests and various other organisations . <p> International Day of Forests : Theme <p> Each year , the Collaborative Partnership on Forests decides a theme to celebrate the day . For the year 2020 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Day of Forests 2020 : Significance <p> Forests are a sustainable source of resources , acting as key contributors to combat climate change . This , in turn , ensures the safety and well-being of the present as well as the future generation . <p> To ensure a better living for years , we need to take care of our forests and the wildlife surviving in them . <p> Today , almost one-third of the Earth 's land mass is covered by forests . As stated by the UN , they serve as livelihoods for around 1.6 billion people , including more than 2,000 indigenous cultures . <p> They are also home to more than 80% of the terrestrial species of animals , plants and insects . <p> Therefore , it becomes our duty to celebrate the day by promising to take care of this abundant source of resources .
