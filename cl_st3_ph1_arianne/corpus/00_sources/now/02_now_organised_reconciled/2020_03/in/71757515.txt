<p> Resistance among villagers that led to the South Korean steel company , POSCO , pulling out of the 12 million tonnes-per-annum integrated steel plant with captive power plant project in Odisha 's Jagatsinghpur district is brewing again , following the decision to allow Sajjan Jindal-led JSW Utkal Steel Limited to carry out the project . <p> Alleging that the public hearing conducted for the project in December 2019 was a farce , the palli sabha or village council of Dhinkia village has already passed a resolution against the project . The other villages in the project area are expected to follow suit . <p> The earlier project , which involves an investment of about Rs 500 billion , or Rs 50,000 crore , was to be set up by POSCO-India Private Limited and had got forest clearance in May 2011 for diversion of 1,253.225 hectares of forest land for the establishment of an integrated steel plant in Jagatsinghpur district of Odisha . However , the project had faced resistance on the issue of settlement of forest rights under the Forest Rights Act 2006 .
