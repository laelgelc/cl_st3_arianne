<h> ' Gahirmatha dolphins migrated to deep sea due to climate change ' <h> Last Updated : Friday 28 February 2020 <p> Migration of dolphins from Odisha 's Gahirmatha marine sanctuary to deep sea ; climate change and unfavourable weather ; illegal fishing practices -- these were the reasons why aquatic mammals ' population saw a decline in the state -- according to officials . <p> Down to Earth reported on 25 February , 2020 , that only 62 dolphins were spotted at Gahirmatha -- down from 126 in 2019 . Their number was pegged at 307 in 2015 . <p> Gahirmatha is located within Bhitarkanika National Park in Odisha 's Kendrapara district . <p> The state 's total dolphin count was pegged at 233 , down from 259 in 2019 , according to the new census report . Their total count in 2018 was 469 . <p> " We conduct dolphin census only two kilometres from the coast , as a result of which it was not possible to count them deep in the sea . Few dolphins were spotted in Gahirmatha this year due to dense fog on the census day .
