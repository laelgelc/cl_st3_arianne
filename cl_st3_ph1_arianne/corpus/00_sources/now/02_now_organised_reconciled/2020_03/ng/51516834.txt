<p> The Senate on Thursday approved President Muhammadu Buhari 's loan request of $22.7 billion after 45 minutes closed session . Before the lawmakers embarked on the closed door session , there was tension in the plenary session as the Minority Leader , Senator Eyinnaya Abaribe , requested for line by line consideration of the projects to be financed with the loan . Abaribe insisted that the Senate considered the loan request line by line and cautioned strongly against Nigeria 's rising debt profile . But the President of the Senate , Ahmad Lawan , overruled the Minority Leader , arguing that what he wanted was against Senate procedures . Not satisfied , Abaribe returned on another Point of Order , accusing the Senate President of trying to force him and the rest of his colleagues to approve the loan without their inputs . Unable to reach agreement on the matter , the Senate , on the request of Senator Gabriel Suswam , decided to settle the matter away from prying eyes . When the doors reopened , the Senate had reached a compromise as the Senate President announced that the loan request had been approved .
