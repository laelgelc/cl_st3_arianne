<h> Youth voice vital for future leadership <p> One of the most exciting things that happened in this last local election was the number of young people who campaigned , and a number of councils have seen a real change in the average age of an elected councillor . <p> So this week I am very excited to celebrate the formal welcome of the Gisborne District Youth Council . <p> I recall the debate we had in council when we first considered a youth council . One of the councillors was adamant that we did n't need another group , and certainly not a youth group . I think I may have quoted Martin Luther King 's " I have a dream " speech when I challenged the councillor . <p> The view that young people are not interested in politics is not supported by research . Younger people may not vote , but many are clearly interested in a wide range of political issues . Having youth in governance roles encourages positive results among youth . It teaches the skills of active citizenship , such as understanding how decisions are made and how to organise , plan , and communicate . Our civil society is strengthened when youth become informed citizens . If we are to grow and develop participatory democracy , having young people involved is a key way to do this . <p> Children and young people offer important insight on matters known only to them . Hearing their views on policy and legislation helps in bridging the gap between adults ' observations and assumptions of children 's lives , and realities experienced by children and young people themselves . <p> There is a real need to create spaces for young people to share their views , and to ensure those views are respected . If it is only older people who fill out the voting forms and post them off , it will be their interests that shape the future of the region . <p> We need to address a lack of knowledge about the electoral system . We can do better in the area of civics in schools and tertiary education . Teaching civics is more than just about passing on facts about Parliament or local councils .
