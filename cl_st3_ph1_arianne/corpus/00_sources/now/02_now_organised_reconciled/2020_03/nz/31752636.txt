<h> BMW hits EU Fuel Target via Efficient Dynamics <p> Words : NZ Autocar <p> 1 Apr 2020 <p> On the day that the world 's highest profile climate change denier winds back another of his predecessor 's initiatives to rein in global warming , BMW announces that its Efficient Dynamics programme , centered on reducing fuel consumption , will enable the group to meet the European Union 's tough CO2 targets ( 95g/km ) . <p> Overall , BMW 's fleet fuel consumption and CO2 emissions have been cut by more than 40 per cent over the past 13 years . And with the ongoing electrification process , the company claims that CO2 emissions in 2020 will be 20 per cent lower again than the figure for 2019 . <p> Efficient Dynamics is a holistic approach , optimising every aspect of the vehicle . Small gains combine to produce significant fuel use reductions . Active air flap control is now in its third generation , while the upcoming electric iX3 will be fitted with the latest generation of BMW aerodynamic wheels , reducing the overall drag coefficient by a further five per cent compared with a regular X3 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cent lighter , and less unsprung weight means added agility . Aerodynamic advancements result in the latest generation 3 Series Sedan having a Cd of 0.23 , BMW claiming this is the lowest in its segment . <p> Despite increases in vehicle size and improvements in safety , successive generations of BMWs are lighter thanks to use of optimised materials for improved efficiency and performance . <p> The engines are key to Efficient Dynamics , the latest combustion mills becoming more efficient with 48V electrical systems . They also make brake energy regeneration possible and 48V mild hybrid technology is expanding throughout the range . <p> Hydrogen is a possible fourth arm of the company 's powertrain portfolio , though the technology awaits the ' greening ' of the fuel supply , among other things . <p> NZ Autocar is New Zealand 's leading automotive magazine .
