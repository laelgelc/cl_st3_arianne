<h> Plenty of room for important debate <p> Regular readers will know from the controversial topics covered on this page , sometimes repetitively , that your editor is an advocate for free speech . Those who read these pages before November 2013 -- when time was called on climate change-denial letters -- will know many of the arguments put up against the science of climate change , as well as the rebuttal of them by climate scientists . <p> One of our correspondents today would like to return to those days . <p> Responding directly , what he wants to debate is not people 's " views " . The scientific evidence that human activity is driving dangerous climate change is indisputable . <p> Yes , plenty of people think otherwise , and there is lots of information available on the internet that supports this view . Other media have contributors and sometimes staff who make these arguments too . But unless and until the purported " science " that denies aspects of anthropogenic climate change passes peer review and is published in respected scientific journals , this newspaper will keep its policy denying column space to manufactured arguments @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is : the questioning of elements of climate change have not been closed off entirely . Far from it . Debate is especially welcome in relation to political and public responses , and the actions needed to mitigate and adapt to climate change . Science is always being updated . What this newspaper does not publish any more is opinion pieces attempting to explain ( and bamboozle ) why climate scientists are wrong . <p> How we respond to the climate change our activities are causing is obviously far from settled , and our letter writer is welcome to debate that from his position of scepticism . <p> There are also many scenarios for the future impacts on our planet and life on it , mostly in relation to how quickly humanity can decarbonise our activities . Some of our contributors focus on worst-case scenarios and claim human extinction is likely because of the consequences of climate change , if we do n't act quickly . Many left-wing politicians and activists see the prescription for climate change in all kinds of policies they hold dear .
