<p> The family of conservation and sustainability leader , Sir Rob Fenwick , has announced that he has passed away earlier today surrounded by family . <p> " While we are dealing with an almost unbearable sense of loss we know we share this loss with many New Zealanders " says Jennie Lady Fenwick . <p> " Rob never lost sight of the importance of saving our precious native species . He was the strongest of conservation advocates . Rob always knew that action at a community level and genuine partnerships were the only way to make lasting change . He had an uncanny knack of pulling groups and agendas together to make things happen . " <p> Sir Rob instigated many conservation and sustainability movements . He was at the forefront of the Predator Free movement and in his final weeks publicly stated the need for urgent action to save our native forest and species from extinction . <p> Department of Conservation Director-General , Lou Sanson , a close personal friend , says : " I was lucky to be chief executive of Antarctica New Zealand when Sir Rob was chair at the board .
