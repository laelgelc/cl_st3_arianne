<h> Joan 's Picks : The Secrets of Strangers and Our House is On Fire <p> The Secrets of Strangers by Charity Norman -- originally from England , she lives here in New Zealand . A number of customers are in a London caf ? early in the morning when a man comes in with a gun and takes them all hostage . As the day unfolds , you find out what drove him to this point but you also get the back stories of all the people trapped , and see the way in which relationships develop between them under such odd circumstances -- most of all , the way in which they begin to empathise with the gunman . She 's a terrific writer , her last book was about a cult , from memory set somewhere near Rotorua . <p> Our House is On Fire : there are four authors to this : Greta Thunberg , Malena ( her mother ) , Beata ( Greta 's sister ) , and Svante Thunberg ( father ) .
