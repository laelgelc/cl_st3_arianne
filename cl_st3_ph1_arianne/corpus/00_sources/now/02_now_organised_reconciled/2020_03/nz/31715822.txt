<h> Expect more acute events -- O'Connor <p> Preparedness for dealing with acute adverse climatic events is going to have to be a part of mainstream farm practice , says Agriculture Minister Damien O'Connor . <p> With climate change , farmers can expect more acute events and the challenge will be dealing with them . He says some farmers are already doing this through good planning and management and others will need to do the same <p> His comments follow his decision to declare a medium size adverse event in the Gisborne , Manawatu , Rangitikei and Tararua districts .
