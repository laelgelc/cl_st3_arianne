<h> The Bulletin : Robertson moves to reassure with Covid-19 economic package <p> Good morning and welcome to The Bulletin . In today 's edition : Robertson announces shape of Covid-19 economic response , National grabs matches for regulations bonfire , and land use change laid bare . <p> Cabinet has approved a package of measures designed to take some of the economic pain out of the global Covid-19 outbreak . Around the world right now , markets are in freefall , and the NZ Herald reports BNZ economists are now predicting a recession will take hold in New Zealand as a result . Both they and finance minister Grant Robertson ( who incidentally does not necessarily believe a recession is likely ) predict that if it does happen , it will be " sharp and shallow " -- but even so , the government has been under pressure to respond . <p> So what is in the package ?
