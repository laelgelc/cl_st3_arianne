Or not come at all . <p> Those bans are not just a logistical and economic headache . In some places , they 're proving to be a geopolitical minefield . <p> Take Taiwan . Some countries put in place early travel curbs on people from China , and then lumped in Hong Kong , Macau and -- Taiwan , to the democratically run island 's consternation . The subext was whether these countries were somehow endorsing China 's view of Taiwan by including it . <p> President Donald Trump has said the U.S. is considering further controls at its southern border , adding to tensions over migration into the U.S. from Mexico . <p> And now a historical rift between South Korea and Japan is coming into play .
