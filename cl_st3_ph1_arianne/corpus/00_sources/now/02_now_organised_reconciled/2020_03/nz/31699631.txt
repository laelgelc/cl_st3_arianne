<h> The Path to Power in Post-Merkel Germany Is Getting Greener <h> The Path to Power in Post-Merkel Germany Is Getting Greener <p> ( Bloomberg ) -- In a Berlin pizza parlor , a secretive group of politicians is drawing up a plan to put climate policy at the center of the next German government . <p> The network , half-jokingly known as the Pizza Connection , has spent years honing proposals for an unprecedented coalition between Angela Merkel 's Christian Democrats and Germany 's increasingly powerful Greens . With the chancellor due to step down in little over a year , the group senses that their time may be arriving . <p> " There is no alternative to a Green government , " said Juergen Falter , professor of politics at the University of Mainz . <p> As politicians across Europe wrestle with the decline of traditional parties and the challenges of climate change , Green movements are emerging as serious contenders for government across the continent . Across the rest of Europe too , officials are preparing to work with the German Greens . <p> In a back room at the Munich Security Conference last month , French President Emmanuel Macron met with Green party leaders Robert Habeck and Annalena Baerbock for three hours , an unusually long audience for a pair of opposition lawmakers . <p> The French leader spent much of his time with the two Greens pressing them for their views on strategic challenges to Europe like the western Balkans and Syria , according to a person briefed on the conversation . It seemed like the French leader was testing the duo , said the party official asking not to be identified because of the sensitive nature of the talks . <p> For the Greens , the Macron meeting was part of a strategy to raise their profile beyond Germany 's borders and to show their environment-focused approach can address the full range of political challenges . <p> Before Munich , Habeck -- a stubble-cheeked writer -- was in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ protect the planet and about how a new global economy can emerge around climate-friendly policies . Building on the Pizza Connection , they 're in position to form a coalition with the conservatives , cementing ties that have been decades in the making . <p> Some 25 years ago , a small group of Greens began to meet with their Christian Democratic counterparts in an Italian restaurant in Germany 's then-capital Bonn . The Christian Democrats were the venerable party that had led Germany throughout much of its post-war reconstruction and reunited East and West . <p> The Greens arose from the anti-nuclear movement and the left-wing fringe .
