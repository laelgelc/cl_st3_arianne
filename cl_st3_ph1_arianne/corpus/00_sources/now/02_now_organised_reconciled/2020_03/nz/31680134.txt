<h> ' No children ' nonsense <p> Rowan Light 's opposition to anti-natalism is beautifully written but poorly argued . He rightly states that its main argument , to save the planet by reducing the number of humans , is simple and powerful ; but his response is weak and simplistic , confusing fewer children with none , which is nonsense . Nobody outside of science fiction is talking about " a community without children " . <p> I was delighted to learn that there is such a thing as anti-natalism . Not before time . Fertility rates in many developed countries are mercifully below replacement , but their governments , groping in the dark of competitive economics , fear it as a loss of market position . <p> Now , as climate change looms larger , many young people have seen the light and decided to remain childless , either to reduce sheer numbers or to save bringing someone into a threatened world . <p> " For hope to exist , we need children .
