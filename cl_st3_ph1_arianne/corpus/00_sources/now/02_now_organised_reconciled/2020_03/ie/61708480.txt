The focus here is on the US and the UK : their governments are floundering . Although these tendencies are present elsewhere , the unravelling in the US is dangerous for institutional investors . <p> Both the US and the UK are highly responsive to the financial sector . <p> A pandemic-free world is , undoubtedly , a good thing for markets . If financial sector employees take action in their sphere of control but also lobby for assertive government action , this could help greatly . <p> The precedents we set during the pandemic and recovery are critical .
