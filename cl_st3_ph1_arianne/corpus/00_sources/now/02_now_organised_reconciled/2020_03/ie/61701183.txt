The best predictions come from viewing the Earth as a single system . <p> Floods occur because of changes in the distribution and movement of water across the world . Again , such changes are influenced by interconnected developments in the atmosphere , the land , the ocean and water in all its forms . Adopting an Earth system approach means we can make improved and consistent predictions , not only of weather but also of the conditions on the world 's surface , including how much water is flowing in our rivers . <p> This is particularly true of global predictions , for which we need to model the water cycle across the globe . At the same time , the spatial resolution of meteorological and hydrological forecasts will increase , and our representation of Earth system processes will improve . We will also get better at representing uncertainties . Today we struggle to predict floods that are generated in small , steep valleys or across small connective systems . <p> Not only will we be able to better predict such floods , we will also be able to predict them further in advance .
