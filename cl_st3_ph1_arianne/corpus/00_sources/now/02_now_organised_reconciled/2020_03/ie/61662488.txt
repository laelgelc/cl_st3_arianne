African nations are unleashing their potential and becoming hubs for start-ups and innovation , driven by a young and ambitious population . <p> Yes , Africa has its challenges ; but the Africa I know is resource rich , creative and entrepreneurial ; and has only become more so in the past 10 years . Yet Africa has been anything but stagnant . <p> African nations have been making bold strategic decisions on investment and global engagement so that they can provide world-class infrastructure to grow their economies and create a better standard of living for their citizens . <p> If Europe waits much longer to modernise its approach to Africa , it will find it 's too late to the party .
