Underlining the urgency of the situation , the country 's central bank early on March 10 announced it had hiked its key rate 275bp to 12.0% and said it stood ready to intervene in the foreign exchange market and take additional measures to protect the cohesion of the market . <p> The announcement of rapid-response measures from Kassym-Zhomart Tokayev came after Energy Minister Nurlan Nogayev said on March 7 that Kazakhstan was working on cutting costs as the oil price plunged on news that the Organisation of the Petroleum Exporting Countries ( OPEC ) had failed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Russia . This sent world oil prices plummeting 31% to $31.02 per barrel on March 9 , marking the second-biggest oil price drop since the Gulf War in 1991 . It was hoped that OPEC and Moscow would agree on lowering oil production amid the new coronavirus outbreak and its impact on the world economy , but it was not to be , prompting the Saudis and Russians to enter into a price war for markets . <p> " We have a budgeted oil price at $50-$55 ( per barrel ) . If it falls to $40 and below , the government has a plan to optimise costs and we are already working on it , " Nogayev was quoted as saying by Reuters .
