<p> Growers have been given new advice on how best to measure food surplus and waste on their farm to help identify its causes . <p> The new guidance , developed in collaboration with leading trade bodies and industry organisations , expands the reach of the UK 's Food Waste Reduction Roadmap . It includes two practical ' how-to ' guides and dedicated tools for in-field measurement . <p> Wrap designed the new resources to help growers apply the principles of ' Target , Measure , Act ' to their operations . <p> The body 's director Peter Maddox said : " Reducing food surplus and waste is a huge opportunity for the sector .
