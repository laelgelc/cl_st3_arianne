<h> The transformation of Ireland 's energy grid is ' a revolution , not an evolution ' <p> EirGrid CEO Mark Foley discusses how Ireland can rapidly shift to renewables and why international cooperation is essential for progress . <p> Speaking with Siliconrepublic.com , EirGrid CEO Mark Foley said the transformation of Ireland 's energy grid to one based on renewables is " a revolution , not an evolution " . <p> EirGrid is responsible for ensuring Ireland 's national electricity grid is able to handle what 's asked of it on any given day , but also prepare for what 's coming , years -- if not decades -- in advance . <p> " Future generations speak to the existential threat that 's climate change and the fact that the next generation -- my children , my grandchildren -- are looking at us to solve the problems before they 're left with an environment that makes life impossible , " Foley said .
