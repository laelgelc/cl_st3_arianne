<p> Parts of England could run out of water in the next 20 years , according to the National Audit Office . <p> The NAO urged Defra to make " rapid progress " and step up its efforts to cut wastage , reduce demand , and develop a clearer sense of direction and coherence among water companies . <p> It 's research found that England will need an extra four billion litres of water per day by 2050 to counter the growing risk of drought from climate change . <p> The report singled out the south and south-east of England as the highest-risk areas , where much of the country 's fruit , especially apples , pears and berries , are grown . <p> The NAO , whose role is to hold government spending to account , pointed to research by the Committee on Climate Change warning that demand for water in England will exceed supply by between 1.1 billion and 3.1 billion litres per day by the 2050s , depending on the extent of climate change and population growth . <p> " If more concerted action is not taken now , parts of the south and south-east of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ years .
