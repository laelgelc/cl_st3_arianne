<h> Proposals to tackle climate change and make Stevenage a carbon-neutral town by 2030 are out for public consultation . <p> Stevenage Borough Council declared a climate emergency in June and has now launched a draft strategy to tackle the problem . <p> The strategy outlines actions the council intends to take to achieve its carbon-neutral target . <p> So far , two electric vehicles are now available for council staff to use in their day-to-day work . Later this year there will be 12 public electric charging bays , with four more available via E-Car Club . <p> SBC is conducting a feasability study into a cycle hire scheme in the town , and 17 traders have so far signed up to offer free water refills to help cut disposable plastic bottle use . <p> To improve biodiversity , verges and parkland are being ' meadow managed ' to support an array of wildlife and increase the carbon capture rate of the land . <h> You may also want to watch : <p> Ward councillors are also funding a new orchard in Symonds Green , which will see 12 mixed fruit trees planted along with wildflower seeds . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ calling on people to take part in a consultation on how to make Stevenage greener and more sustainable . <p> Councillor John Gardner , executive member for environment , said : " We are committed to working with a wide range of partners across the town to develop our climate change strategy and action plan for the next decade . This will bring benefits such as reduced fuel poverty , improved environment and air quality , and better green spaces . " <p> He added : " We expect all new planning applications to demonstrate how environmental impact is to be mitigated .
