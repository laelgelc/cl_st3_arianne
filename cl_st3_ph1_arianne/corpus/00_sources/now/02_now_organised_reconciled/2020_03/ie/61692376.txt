<h> Sydney-based New Forests commits to new carbon-neutral action plan <p> New Forests has committed to become a carbon-neutral business under a new climate action plan . <p> The A$5.9bn Sydney-based forestry manager -- which owns nearly 1m hectares of forestry and conservation investments across the Asia-Pacific region and the US -- also published its first climate disclosure report , documenting its efforts to align with Task Force for Climate-related Financial Disclosures ' ( TCFD ) recommendations . <p> " New Forests ' vision is for forestry to become a leading sector in the transition to a sustainable future , " the report said , noting " deep connections " between the forestry sector and climate change . <p> It said sustainable forestry and natural climate solutions were both essential to any climate mitigation pathway that could achieve the goals of the Paris Agreement . <p> The report also said the world 's forests faced real and rising physical risks from climate change .
