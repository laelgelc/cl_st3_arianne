Our single market of 446 million consumers makes us highly attractive as a trading partner . That is why countries and regional blocs all around the world are lining up to sign deals with us . <p> In recent years , we have signed tremendous " win-win " agreements with Canada , Mexico and Japan , to name but a few . The early indications are that these deals are increasing export opportunities for our businesses . <p> We estimate that every ? 1bn in exports supports some 14,000 high-quality jobs in Europe .
