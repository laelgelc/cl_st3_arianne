<h> Skandia poll : Sustainability fails to rank as key factor <p> Despite the heightened public and political focus on climate change in the last 12 months , a new survey by Skandia has revealed that sustainability does not rank among the four most commonly cited reasons for choosing funds in the country 's premium pension system . <p> The Swedish pension provider reported that , according to the results of a poll carried out on its behalf , the top consideration for Swedes when picking funds for their premium pension savings -- the defined contribution ( DC ) part of the state or general pension -- is the level of fees charged . <p> This factor was chosen by 18% of respondents as the main basis for their decision , followed by a fund 's risk level , which was selected by 14.9% .
