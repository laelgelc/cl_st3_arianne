<h> EU must demand safe return of Nigerian schoolgirls <p> Written by Mariela Baeva on 27 March 2015 <p> Borno state , known as the ' home of the Berbers ' , in northeastern Nigeria , is said to be the probable location where around 200 schoolgirls from Chibok are being kept , out of the 276 who were kidnapped by Boko Haram in April last year . <p> Some have managed to escape and have been reunited with their families . Reports indicate , however , that some of the captives have been sold as slaves in Chad and Cameroon , and others forced into marriage , or used as suicide bombers . <p> Late at night on Monday 14 April 2014 , residents of Chibok were warned by people in adjacent villages that a truck convoy was heading to Chibok . The suspected perpetrators were Boko Haram militants . <p> They made their way to the government secondary school for girls , which was being guarded by security soldiers . Gunfire ensued and they stormed into the premises , where the girls were sleeping . They had exams the following day .
