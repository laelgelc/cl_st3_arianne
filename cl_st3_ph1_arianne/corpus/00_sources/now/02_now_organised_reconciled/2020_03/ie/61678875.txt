<p> Hodder Children 's Books will publish second graphic novel by Eoin Colfer , Andrew Donkin and Giovanni Rigano following the success of Illegal . ... <p> Hodder Children 's Books will publish second graphic novel by Eoin Colfer , Andrew Donkin and Giovanni Rigano following the success of Illegal . <p> Senior publisher Anne McNeil acquired all language world rights from Sophie Hicks form Sophie Hicks Agency . Publication is scheduled for autumn 2021 , with paperback to follow in 2022 . <p> The trio 's second book is titled Global and will tackle the topic of the climate crisis . It tells the story of two young people , Sami and Yuki , living on different continents , whose lives are impacted by the effects of global warming . <p> Mirroring their first graphic novel , Global will include additional material in the form of interviews with those on the front-line of climate struggle and details on how readers can help prevent climate change . <p> Colfer , Donkin and Rigano said : " Global is not a story about what might happen in the future . Each element of climate change depicted in the story is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . It 's often children that are on the frontline of climate change and Global highlights the dangers that they face daily . " <p> McNeil added : " Books like Global can play a part in bringing to life one of the most critical issues for today 's young people , and we are so proud to be the publishers of Illegal and Global , which are a testament to modern times . "
