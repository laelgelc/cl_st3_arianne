<p> Students from the University of Northampton 's Faculty for Business and Law have pitched their ideas for products that could help fruit importer Blue Skies meet the needs of the future . <p> The FBL ( Faculty of Business and Law ) Challenge was devised by the university in partnership with the Northamptonshire fruit supplier . The initiative challenges students to come up with innovative products and solutions that are designed to address a series of future scenarios put forward by Blue Skies . <p> The scenarios include predictions on social , environmental , political , economic and technological changes that could affect the world in 2025 . <p> Four finalists pitched their ideas to a panel of judges from Blue Skies and the University of Northampton on 4 March at the Creative Hub in the University of Northampton .
