government departments . <p> The Pensions Climate Risk Industry Group ( PCRIG ) , as it was named , described the guide , as " structured sequentially based on the way a pension trustee board might typically approach decision-making " . <p> " The draft guide is designed to help trustees meet existing legal obligations to consider financially material factors in their investment decision making
