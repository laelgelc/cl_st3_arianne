when Macedonia broke away from the former Yugoslavia . <p> After the announcement was made on Wednesday , Austrian Chancellor Sebastian Kurz said that Austria , which takes over the EU presidency next month , thought Macedonia had a chance of joining the European Union . <p> Bulgaria , the current holder of the EU 's rotating six-month presidency , also said the deal paved the way for accession
