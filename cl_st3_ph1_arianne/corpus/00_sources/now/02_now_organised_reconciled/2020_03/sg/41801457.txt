Schools for girls could transform climate protection -- and so much else . <h> Share <p> If you really want to tackle the climate emergency , there 's one simple but often forgotten essential : throw your weight behind schools for girls , and ensure adult women can rely on the chance of an education . <p> Obviously , in a world of differences , some people can do more to tackle the climate crisis than others . So it 's essential to recognise how much neglected potential exists among nearly half the human race . <p> But there 's a snag , and it 's a massive one : the women and girls who can do so much to avert global heating reaching disastrous levels need to be able to exercise their right to education . <p> Bold claims ?
