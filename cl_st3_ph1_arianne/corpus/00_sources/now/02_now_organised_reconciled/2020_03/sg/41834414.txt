<h> As the coronavirus pummels the continent , Asian women are most vulnerable to job cuts , experts say . <h> Share <p> When coronavirus began to spread around the world last month , Hong Kong babysitter Tina Yeung found herself out of work - one of many Asian women whose jobs in informal sectors are threatened by the pandemic . <p> The virus has infected more than 134,000 people globally and caused nearly 5,000 deaths since it was first reported in China in December , wreaking economic havoc and impacting workers in industries from airlines to tourism . <p> Asian women in low-paid informal work such as cleaning and cooking or caring for children , many without proper contracts or social protection , are bearing the brunt of widespread job cuts , experts say . <p> Yeung is one of them . She works with an expatriate family , who abruptly left the southern Chinese city for a month to return to Britain in late January , as the COVID-19 virus spread . <p> " I was not paid for the month .
