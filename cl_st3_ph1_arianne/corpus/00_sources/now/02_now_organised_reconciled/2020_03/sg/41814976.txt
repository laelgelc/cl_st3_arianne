<h> Abu Dhabi to ban single-use plastics by 2021 <p> Abu Dhabi 's Environment Agency ( EAD ) has announced a policy that will ban single-use plastics from 2021 and will parallelly encourage the use of reusable bags . <p> The policy will ban single use plastic and introduce a fine for usage of plastics that have sustainable alternatives .
