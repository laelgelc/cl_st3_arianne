These are all groups which are more vulnerable to heatwaves and new diseases brought on by higher average temperatures . <p> In addition to heatwaves , extreme weather events such as typhoons will affect men and women differently in terms of responsibility for preparation , protection and post-event recovery . This is not always taken into account at a time of resource constraints . <p> With commonly differentiated roles in family life , women 's travel needs are often more complicated , requiring circular transport routes linking schools , shops , work and home . Most transport systems are more ' radial , ' simply linking residential areas to city centres for commuting workers like the spokes of a wheel . <p> In developed societies , women are responsible for much lower emissions than men . In Sweden , for example , studies show average emissions for men are twice those of women . This is mostly the result of gendered patterns of car use . <p> While we need more women in high level decision making roles on climate issues , the authority needs to be real .
