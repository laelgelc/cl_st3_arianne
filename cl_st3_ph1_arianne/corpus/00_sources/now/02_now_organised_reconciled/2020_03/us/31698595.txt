<p> It was the decade that gave the world the maverick New Hollywood drama , the Nixon-era paranoid thriller , the slasher flick , the all-star disaster movie , the gross-out comedy and the modern mega-blockbuster . But the Seventies were particularly kind to one specific cul-de-sac of cinema : the science fiction film , a subset category that was still buzzing from its late-Sixties head-trip phase courtesy of 2001 : A Space Odyssey . As the Age of Aquarius slowly slid into the beginning of the nation 's Watergate-and-disco period , you could still find sci-fi movies that wanted to blow an audience 's possibly addled , probably enhanced mind . But by the end of the 1970s , it was possible to have checked out postapocalyptic action-adventures , future-shock case studies , technophobic nightmares , low-budget exploitation movies about what-if scenarios and big-budget space operas -- all of which fell under the S.F. umbrella and helped turn the genre into a gamechanger .
