This popular annual event , taking place on Sunday , April 5 , 2020 , at Chicago Botanic Garden , is a fundraiser for the Hands of Peace Summer Program , which empowers young Israelis , Palestinians and Americans to be leaders of change in their communities . <p> The annual Hands of Peace Summer Program brings together youth from diverse backgrounds , many of whom come from areas divided by conflict and violence . Through facilitated dialogue sessions and leadership development activities , participants move from strangers to partners in peace , working to build a better future . Since @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more than 650 alumni have become lawyers , engineers , doctors and environmentalists . Others are legislators , diplomats , entrepreneurs and journalists . All of them are creating change around the world . <p> One of the alumni is Stav Friedman , part of the first American delegation in 2003 , who will share her Hands of Peace story at the Chicago Benefit . Friedman , who holds American and Israeli citizenship , has a BS in Marine Biology and Biological Oceanography from the University of South Florida . Her passion for the environment led her to create Plastic Free Israel , a grassroots volunteer effort that cleans up beaches in Tel Aviv , Haifa and Jerusalem and promotes plastic pollution solutions . She now oversees the operation from London , where she is studying climate change and policy for a Master of Science degree at Kings College . <p> The Magic Happens from 5:30 to 9 p.m. on Sunday , April 5 , at the Chicago Botanic Garden , 1000 Lake Cook Rd. , Glencoe , IL , 60022 . Started in the Chicago suburbs , the non-profit 501(c) ( 3 ) interfaith organization was founded in 2003 by Gretchen Grad , who believed that peace could be nurtured , person by person . Since then , Hands of Peace has expanded in size , strength and scope . The Chicago and San Diego Programs now serve nearly 90 teens each summer . Hands of Peace is unique in that Israeli and Palestinian teens are hosted by local families--providing a meaningful opportunity for cross-cultural exchange .
