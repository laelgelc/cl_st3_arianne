<h> Letter : Interconnected in the time of coronavirus <p> Published : 3/18/2020 12:01:18 AM <p> I write in grief and in hope . <p> I grieve for the millions of people suffering from COVID-19 : from illness , fear , loss of business , loss of work , loss of school . As with climate change , those who suffer the most are those who are already most vulnerable , our neighbors who have the least cushion against misfortune . The world is groaning . <p> I hope in our compassion and resilience . Communities are finding creative ways to help each other to have food , schooling , companionship . In Italy , people are singing to each other from open windows ! We are again reminded how interconnected we are in our towns and across the world . This brings risk and also opportunity . <p> Thank you to the nurses , doctors and all others who devote themselves to the health of others . May we follow their example .
