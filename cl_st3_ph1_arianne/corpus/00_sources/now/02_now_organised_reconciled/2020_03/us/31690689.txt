<h> Jessica Cisneros Should Be the Future of Her Party <p> Jessica Cisneros came so close . But as the sound and fury of Super Tuesday faded to a whimper , it became obvious that she would not unseat Henry Cuellar . The conservative Democrat will instead represent Texas 's 28th Congressional District for another two years . Cisneros , meanwhile , promised supporters that her fight would continue . There 's every reason to believe it will : At 26 years old , she has plenty of time to hone her strategy for elections to come . <p> Cuellar may have won , but he does n't have much to celebrate . His margin of victory over Cisneros , a newcomer who lacked his connections and the support of the party 's elders , betrays vulnerability . The combined support of House Speaker Nancy Pelosi , the Koch network , and the local oil and gas industry was barely enough to keep him in power . The Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee instituted a blacklist designed to deter vendors and consultants from providing their services to insurgents like Cisneros . Pelosi and others worked against the labor movement , and major party allies like EMILY 's List , all to keep Cuellar in a district that any Democrat would probably win . <p> But their political calculations may soon be out-of-date . By working against young candidates like Cisneros , even in safe Democratic districts , party leaders are working against a viable future for the party . Young voters are already worlds apart from Democrats like Cuellar . They want universal health care and colleges they can afford to attend ; they want to raise children on a planet that is n't doomed .
