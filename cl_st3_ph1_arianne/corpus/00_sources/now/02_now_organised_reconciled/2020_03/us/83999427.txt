( Source : Pixabay ) <p> March 3 , 2020 at 10:34 PM EST - Updated March 3 at 10:34 PM <p> ( AP ) - Voters in North Carolina 's Democratic primary ranked health care as the most important issue facing the country , well above climate change , the economy , race relations , foreign policy and many other social issues . <p> That 's according to a wide-ranging AP VoteCast survey of the Democratic primary electorate in North Carolina . <p> VoteCast also found voters in North Carolina 's Democratic primary were closely divided over whether they wanted a candidate who would bring fundamental change to Washington or one who would restore the political system to how it was before Donald Trump was elected in 2016 . <p> Copyright 2020 The Associated Press .
