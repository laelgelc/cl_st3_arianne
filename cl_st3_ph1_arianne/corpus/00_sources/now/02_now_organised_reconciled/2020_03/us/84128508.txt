<h> The head of the U.N . weather agency says decreased energy use due to the coronavirus is improving air quality in China and demonstrates the impact that human emissions are having . <p> The head of the U.N . weather agency says decreased energy use due to the coronavirus is improving air quality in China and demonstrates the impact that human emissions are having . <p> World Meteorological Organization Secretary-General Petteri Taalas made the comment at a U.N . briefing , where he showed satellite pictures of the much cleaner air quality over China on Jan. 30 , 2020 compared to January 2019 . <p> U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said the virus is expected to be temporary while climate change will remain for decades and requires constant action . <p> He was responding to a question on the possibility of the global coronavirus outbreak cutting greenhouse gas emissions this year , with decreased energy use , planes grounded and international trade squeezed , and the effect on climate change as soon as the economy bounces back . <p> Guterres said they should not overestimate the fact that emissions have been reduced for some months .
