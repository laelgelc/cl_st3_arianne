<h> TC Energy going ahead with Keystone XL pipeline <p> CALGARY , Alberta -- TC Energy Corp . gave the go ahead for construction of its $8 billion Keystone XL Pipeline project on Tuesday , with assistance from the Alberta government . <p> The company formerly known as TransCanada said Alberta has agreed to invest approximately $1.1 billion as equity in the project , which substantially covers planned construction costs through the end of 2020 . <p> The pipeline would transport up to 830,000 barrels ( 35 million gallons ) of crude oil daily from western Canada to terminals on the U.S. Gulf Coast <p> First proposed in 2008 , the pipeline has become emblematic of the tensions between economic development and curbing the fossil fuel emissions that are causing climate change . The Obama administration rejected it , but President Donald Trump revived it and has been a strong supporter . <p> The remaining 6.9 billion is expected to be funded through a combination of a $4.2-billion project level credit facility to be fully guaranteed by the Alberta government and a $2.7-billion investment by TC Energy . <p> The company said with pre-construction activities underway , the pipeline @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the project is complete and in service , TC Energy expects to acquire the Alberta government 's equity investment under agreed terms and conditions and refinance the $4.2-billion credit facility in the debt capital markets . <p> " We thank U.S. President Donald Trump and Alberta Premier Jason Kenney as well as many government officials across North America for their advocacy without which , individually and collectively , this project could not have advanced , " TC Energy chief executive Russ Girling said in a statement . <p> Environmentalists and Native American tribes along the pipeline route say burning the tar sands oil will make climate change worse , and that the pipeline could break and spill oil into waterways like Montana 's Missouri River . They have filed numerous lawsuits .
