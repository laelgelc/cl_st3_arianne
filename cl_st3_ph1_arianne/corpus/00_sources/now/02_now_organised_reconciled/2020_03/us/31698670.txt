There 's a lot to unpack here . In the 1990s , social conservatives wanted more censorship , particularly of sexual content . Progressive activists back then generally wanted less . Today , progressives frequently argue that social media platforms are too tolerant of vile , offensive , hurtful speech , while conservatives commonly insist that the platforms censor too much ( or at least censor them too much ) . <p> Both sides miss obvious points .
