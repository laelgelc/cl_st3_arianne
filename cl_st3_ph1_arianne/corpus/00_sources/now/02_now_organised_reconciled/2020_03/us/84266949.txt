<h> Nature 's Notebook Nature Nation <p> Each week , we feature a worthy citizen science project or nature news segment from around the nation . This week , as the seasons start to shift , is Nature 's Notebook . <p> Nature 's Notebook <p> The mission of Nature 's Notebook 's is simple and accessible for citizen scientists of all ages : to track seasonal changes in plants and animals . You can choose from a number of campaigns ( or projects ) , create your own projects , and even see how the data you gather and contribute is being used to make real change in the world . <p> Nature 's Notebook is run by the USA National Phenology Network , who also provide webinars , podcasts , and other articles to help keep you involved and up to date on how climate change is affecting plants
