<h> Watch a Documentary <p> Netflix is filled to the brim with documentaries about the ocean , animals , bees , and more that can teach children the importance of protecting the world and its many non-human residents . Of course , you should always make sure to read up on the appropriateness of a documentary for younger viewers beforehand , but plenty of these documentaries , like Mission Blue , show the real effects of climate change in an accessible format . <p> In the age of Greta Thunberg , children have never been more active in the constant battle against climate change . This smart generation of young people are the first to look for ways that they can love the earth that they live on , so adults ought to do everything that they can to give them the proper resources to be informed and live a cleaner , greener life . Obviously , children have much different choices than adults when it comes to reducing their carbon footprint . For adults , it might be taking public transportation to work instead of driving solo .
