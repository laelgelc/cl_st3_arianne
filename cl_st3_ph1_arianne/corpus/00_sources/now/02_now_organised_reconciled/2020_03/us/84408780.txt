<p> There 's no solving the planet 's mounting climate woes without solving society 's transportation problem . That 's because our cars , planes , and trucks saturate the atmosphere with prodigious amounts of heat-trapping carbon dioxide . In the U.S. -- the second biggest carbon emitter on Earth -- the transportation sector is now the nation 's leading contributor of greenhouse gases . <p> Planes , trains , and climate change is Mashable 's ongoing series about the dramatic impacts transportation has on the planet 's relentless warming , and our lives . <p> These stories show how we can power our transportation future and adapt the way we travel -- and what happens if we do n't change . <p> In any given year , Southern California 's Interstate 405 is either one of the worst or literally the worst trafficked highway in the U.S. After decades of deadlock , the 405 may be able to shed its infamous reputation . <p> Americans have been slow to adopt electric vehicles because of anxieties about how far cars can travel before running low on power .
