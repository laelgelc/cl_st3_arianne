<p> The program " In Search of Fire- and Climate-Resilient Reforestation for Butte County Woodlands " will be presented by Wolfy Rougle at 7 p.m . Wednesday , March 4 at the Chico library . <p> The forest health watershed coodinator with the Butte County Resource Conservation District , Rougle will talk about how Butte County woodlands are in transition because of climate change , high-severity fires , and 20th-century land use decisions , some Butte County woodlands face a real possibility of permanent type conversion to brushland . <p> Rougle will expand upon what communities want from woodlands , what is possible and how the Butte County Resource Conservation District is working to meet those goals with projects like the Concow Resilience Project -- a post-Camp Fire restoration vision based on a collaborative consensus of what a fire- and climate-resilient forest might look like . Another one is the Butte Forest Plan , which will be a county-scale written inventory of forest conditions that doubles as a road map of projects for the forests .
