<p> The largest financial gift in the University of California at Berkeley 's history will help build a new center to house the fast-growing Division of Computing , Data Science and Society , UC officials said Tuesday . <p> The anonymously donated $252 million , combined with a-yet-to-be raised $300 million in private funds , will be used to build a new " Data Hub " at the intersection of Hearst Avenue , Arch Street and Macfarlane Lane , according to UC Berkeley officials . <p> The center will primarily host computing and data science research , faculty offices and labs , workrooms , public spaces , a large auditorium and classrooms . <p> " The Data Hub will be a magnet , bringing together scholars from disciplines across campus to forge new collaborations and take on some of the most critical questions facing society today , from biomedicine , to climate change and sustainability , to making data-informed public policy on issues of societal significance , " Jennifer Chayes , associate provost for the division and dean of the School of Information , said in a news release Tuesday . <p> The Data Hub will enable researchers from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , legal , social and technical questions surrounding machine learning and artificial intelligence , according to university officials . <p> A detailed timeline for the project has yet to be developed but officials anticipate construction could begin sometime in 2022 , according to Kyle Gibson , spokesman for UC Berkeley Capital Strategies . <p> And while the project wo n't be subject to the city of Berkeley 's planning process , it will need to undergo an environmental review and win approval from the University of California Board of Regents , Gibson said .
