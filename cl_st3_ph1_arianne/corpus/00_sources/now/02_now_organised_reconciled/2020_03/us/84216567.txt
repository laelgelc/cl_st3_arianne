<h> Greenpeace Calls for 71 Percent Drop in Meat Consumption in Europe by 2030 <h> The environmental organization stresses the importance of slashing meat consumption to fight the climate crisis . <p> This month , environmental organization Greenpeace International urged the European Union to slash meat consumption and production by 71 percent by 2030 in order to mitigate the devastating impact of animal agriculture on the climate crisis . Currently , EU citizens consume an average of 1.58 kilograms ( 3.5 pounds ) of meat per person every week . Greenpeace would like to see that number drop to no more than 460 grams ( 15.9 ounces ) per person per week and for the EU to enforce a deeper 81 percent decline in meat production and consumption by 2050 .
