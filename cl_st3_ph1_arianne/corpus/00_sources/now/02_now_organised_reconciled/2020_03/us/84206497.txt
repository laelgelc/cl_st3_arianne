<h> Stop LNG export site as a matter of climate-change relief Letters <p> It goes without saying that residents living near the proposed liquefied natural gas gas export facility in Greenwich Township , Gloucester County , should be given more information about a project that impacts their health and safety , as indicated in the March 10 South Jersey Times editorial , " LNG train engine to N.J. port chugs ahead without adequate caution . " <p> But , beyond the issues of public input and the safety of transporting fracked shale-derived gas by rail , we must think of the larger impacts of this project , specifically on emissions that are fueling the global climate crisis . <p> We are in a race to stop developing fossil fuels , and exporting this gas means doing more of the drilling that is creating new sources of climate pollution . If Gov . Phil Murphy wants to be seen as a leader in the fight against climate change , he must stop the Gibbstown gas project and continue to push to create sustainable , good-paying jobs in clean-energy industries . <p> Jocelyn Sawyer , Philadelphia <p> Note : The writer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ &amp; Water Watch and Food &amp; Water Action .
