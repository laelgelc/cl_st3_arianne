<h> How India 's idea of forest has evolved over the decades <p> From covering 640,819 square kilometres ( 19.49% ) of India 's total land area in 1987 to covering 712,249 sq km ( 21.67% ) of the country 's geographical area in 2019 , India 's forest cover has had a roller coaster journey . <p> The 32-year-long journey witnessed the rise of a little over 2% of forest cover despite an increase in the population of the country , rapid urbanisation , and tremendous pressure on resources like forests .
