<h> How did it start ? <p> Earth Hour is an annual tradition in which people switch off their lights to spread awareness about sustainability and climate change . Started by the World Wildlife Fund , it takes place on March 28 , 2020 , from 8:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. local time . ( And it 's not to be confused with Earth Day ) <h> Coronavirus outbreak updates <p> Since then , millions of people around the world have taken part . Landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower , Big Ben , Sydney Opera House , the Empire State Building , Buckingham Palace , the Colosseum and Edinburgh Castle also have gone dark for an hour . <h> What is it intended to do ? <p> It 's meant to bring together people who share one common goal : an eco-friendly future . <h> How can people get involved ? <p> Organizers suggest you sign a pledge to decrease your environmental footprint and participate in the blackout .
