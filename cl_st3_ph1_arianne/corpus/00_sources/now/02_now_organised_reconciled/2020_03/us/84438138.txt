<h> Japan says it will pursue efforts to cut emissions further in future , but it is the only G7 nation still building new coal-fired power plants <p> TOKYO , March 30 ( Reuters ) - Japan has maintained its carbon emissions reduction target in its updated emissions plan submitted to the United Nations as part of an international process to address climate change . <p> The government stuck to its 2030 target of cutting emissions by 26% from 2013 levels in submitting its Nationally Determined Contribution to the UN . <p> The country " will pursue further efforts both in the medium-term and long-term , to reduce ... emissions beyond this level , " a statement from the government said on Monday . <p> While Britain and much of Europe aim to cut carbon emissions to net zero by 2050 , Japan is the only G7 nation still building new coal-fired power plants , one of the main sources of climate-warming emissions . <p> Investors managing $37 trillion in assets earlier this year urged Japan to slash the country 's carbon emissions , saying that a strong signal from Tokyo could help kick off international climate action ahead @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> With climate change increasingly topping lists of investor concerns , Bank of Japan Governor Haruhiko Kuroda told the World Economic Forum in Davos in January that Japan should do more to cut emissions and mitigate the risks climate change poses .
