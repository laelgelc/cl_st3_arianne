" <p> Since there is so much public support and buy-in towards solving the coronavirus crisis , Goldstone hopes that similar support can be applied towards the climate change crisis . <p> " Maybe we can come out of this coronavirus crisis with a sense that we are capable of dramatic collective action and that we can use that in other ways , " she said . <p> Hannah Uebele is a production assistant for Boston Public Radio .
