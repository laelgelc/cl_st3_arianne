<h> Will the coronavirus crisis jolt America 's political system out of partisan paralysis ? <p> America has always managed to find ways to improve itself in the midst of -- and sometimes because of -- its most difficult challenges . <p> Will that be the case with the coronavirus ? This crisis , sprawling and frightening in equal measure , undoubtedly will change public life . But how ? And can some of those changes be for the better ? <p> I put those questions to a cross-section of prominent American political and national-security figures . They responded generously , offering a variety of predictions and hopes . <p> Strikingly , by far the most frequently expressed hope was that the crisis will jolt the country 's political system out of its long bout of partisan paralysis -- that is , that America 's elected leaders now will realize that they need to start working together more across ideological and party lines , and that American voters will demand as much .
