<p> On March 11 , as the coronavirus spread silently across Florida , the state Legislature passed its very first bill directly addressing climate change . The measure is responsive rather than proactive : It prohibits the use of taxpayer money on public construction projects that are vulnerable to flooding because of sea level rise . Florida 's sea levels are currently rising one inch every three years and could rise 15 to 20 inches in Miami Beach and the Keys by 2050 . Coastal cities already sufferregular flooding , a taste of catastrophes to come . Rising temperatures are also intensifying the hurricanes that periodically tear across Florida , taking lives and destroying property .
