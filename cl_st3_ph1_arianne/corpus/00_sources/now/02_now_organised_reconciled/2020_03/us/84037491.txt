In her speech at the Conservative Political Action Conference , she asserted : " Man vastly overestimates his power if he thinks he can , with CO2 emissions , destroy the climate . " <p> In response to this statement , I want to draw attention to a remarkable document on climate change and national security . Sixty-four senior U.S. military and security leaders have endorsed " A Climate Security Plan for America .
