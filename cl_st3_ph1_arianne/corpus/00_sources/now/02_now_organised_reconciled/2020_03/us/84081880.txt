<h> Letter : Let 's reduce climate change by planting trees <p> As we know , one of the best ways to reduce climate change is to plant trees . <p> In years past , schools used to celebrate Arbor Day by planting a hand full of trees in their communities . <p> Would n't it be wonderful if each of our communities would take one day each year to plant trees on school properties , church lands , in business zones where so many old stock trees were destroyed , fairgrounds , home properties , and so on ? <p> If you agree with this idea , get serious and put together groups of people and begin planning now where you want to plant and which trees would be best for your growing zones . <p> Also , order up those trees so they are ready when you are to plant .
