( AP Photo/Rich Pedroncelli , File ) <p> Have lawmakers ever stopped to think that maybe they are making solutions more complicated than they need to be ? <p> Let 's keep it simple : if you want people to burn less fossil fuels , make fossil fuels more expensive . HR 763 , a bipartisan bill currently in the House , uses this simple method to drive dramatic reductions in carbon emissions . Let 's all come together to support simple solutions that will have big effects on curbing climate change .
