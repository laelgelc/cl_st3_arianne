<h> Cindy Pile : Clean up Vallejo <h> Share this : <p> This is a plea to the residents of Vallejo to please throw your trash away in suitable containers and recycle what you can . <p> I am sad to say that Vallejo is the dirtiest city I have lived in . There is trash everywhere . On the roads I drive and the streets I walk , I see fast food containers and wrappers , plastic bags , cardboard boxes , a used condom here , a glass bottle there . Other places , I see piles of clothing and bags filled with waste . Please stop littering . Please take the time to pick up trash near where you live . In the midst of climate change , let us still care for and show reverence for Earth .
