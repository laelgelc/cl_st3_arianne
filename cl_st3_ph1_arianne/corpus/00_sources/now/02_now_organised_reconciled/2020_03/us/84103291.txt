He is an Assistant Professor in the American Studies Department at the University of New Mexico . In 2014 , he co-founded The Red Nation , an Indigenous resistance organization . For 2017-2018 , Estes was the American Democracy Fellow at the Charles Warren Center for Studies in American History at Harvard University . His research engages colonialism and global Indigenous histories , with a focus on decolonization , oral history , U.S. imperialism , environmental justice , anti-capitalism , and the Oceti Sakowin .
