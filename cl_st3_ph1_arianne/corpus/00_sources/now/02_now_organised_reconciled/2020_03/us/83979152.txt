The legislation , The American Energy Innovation Act ( AEIA , S. 2657 ) , is the product of a collaboration betweem Senators Murkowski ( R-AK ) and Joe Manchin ( D-WV ) . Murkowski is chair of the Senate Energy and Natural Resources Committee ( ENR ) , while Manchin is the ranking Democrat on the panel . <p> In truth , the AEIA is more of a vehicle for portions of over 50 pieces of legislation that the Senate Energy Committee previously considered and cleared for a floor vote . As introduced , AEIA is an amalgam of measures sponsored or cosponsored by more than 60 Senators . The provisions are arranged under two titles -- Innovation and Supply Chain Security .
