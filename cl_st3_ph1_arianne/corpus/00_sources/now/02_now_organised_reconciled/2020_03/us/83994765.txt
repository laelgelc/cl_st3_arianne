<h> AMiOS Will Present SHOTZ ! VOL. 105 <p> AMiOS has announced the next show in its 2020 season ! They will be celebrating a decade of 100 productions and over 600 plays with Shotz Trek on March 9th . <p> AMiOS ( an acronym for Art and Music in Our Souls ) is an artist-run , process-driven , participatory arts collective with a theatrical focus , whose mission is to build community around the continual generation of art that reflects and responds to our ever-changing world . <p> SHOTZ ! is a theatrical pressure cooker that every month pairs 6 groups of actors , writers , and directors together to create brand new 7-minute plays for performance the first Monday of the month at The Kraine Theater , 85 E. 4th St , and is the best , most consistent showcase of up-and-coming talent in NYC . <p> Since its first episode aired in 1966 , Star Trek has provided a hopeful and optimistic vision of the future for its viewers . Gene Roddenberry 's idealistic rendering of space exploration has provided a contrasting point of view to more dystopic and cynical science fiction . Through its portrayal @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ long-standing ( and contemporarily relevant ) problems such as war , racism , sexism , and climate change , Star Trek showed us how humanity , at its best , could be . In choosing Star Trek as this month 's Shotz theme , we sought to subvert the current cultural climate of fatalist despair , and engender in the crew and our audience a spirit of hope for what 's next . <p> Shotz Trek will feature a new short play by Lizzie Vieh ( Monsoon Season , Rattlestick &amp; 2019 Edinburgh Fringe , originally commissioned as a SHOTZ ! piece ) , Mat Smart ( The Agitators ) , and an acting ensemble that includes Maurice Jones ( Broadway : Romeo &amp; Juliet , The Lifespan of a Fact ) .
