<h> Company recycles CO2 into soft drinks <p> Food &amp; Drink <h> The process uses carbon dioxide collectors , which can be powered using renewable energy <p> Spotted : We have previously covered Climeworks , the innovative Swiss company that has developed a practical way to remove carbon dioxide from the air . But the question facing the company was then how to recycle that excess CO2 in order to cut the cost of removing it from the atmosphere . Recently , Climeworks has hit on a new idea for this -- use the CO2 to make soft drinks . <p> Climeworks uses carbon dioxide collectors , which can be powered using renewable energy . A fan pushes air through filters in the collectors , where CO2 molecules are trapped . Once the filters are saturated , the collectors are heated to 1,000 degrees Celsius to remove the pure carbon and release the oxygen . <p> As Climeworks technology is very expensive , the key to its widespread adoption is to offset the cost by finding ways to sell the excess carbon . This includes the carbonating of soft drinks , the use of CO2 as a carrier gas in bars and restaurants for draft beer , its ability to keep food fresh as an inert gas in packaging , and as a source of dry ice , which is frozen CO2 . Using a Climeworks plant , bottling companies can generate high purity CO2 on-site , literally out of thin air , with no supply chain required . <p> The growing need to reduce the amount of carbon in the atmosphere has led to a number of exciting innovations .
