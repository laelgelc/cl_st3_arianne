After only five years , they 're already witnessing soil formation . <p> LEO research will also contribute to the growing field of carbon capture and storage , she said . The project was completed in 2012 after five years of planning and construction , and about $7 million spent . <p> The basalt was crushed into grains large enough for water to flow , yet small enough to retain some moisture when gently " rained on " by sprinklers arching over each slope . <p> Almost 2,000 sensors buried within , situated on and hanging above the slopes monitor soil moisture , temperature , CO2 levels and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sits on scales to weigh the system as water moves in and out . <p> " If you start with existing soil , it has legacy and history that you can not know , " said Peter Troch , LEO 's principal investigator and science director for Biosphere 2 . <p> But the basalt is simple and clean . <p> It lacks the diversity of materials found in natural soils , making it easier to see changes than in complex soil . <p> " Basaltic rocks have a lot of basic elements in them , " Dontsova said . " These elements tend to dissolve very fast when rained on , " and then react with other elements , minerals and biota in the soil . <p> As this process of dissolving and recombining continues , the basaltic sand of the slopes gets more complex and becomes a pool of nutrients for life to thrive on . <h> Lock it up <p> Atmospheric CO2 hitches a ride on raindrops , lands on the basaltic sand , then reacts with the elements from the dissolved basalt such as calcium , magnesium @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as bicarbonates , a chalky material that 's basically baking soda . <p> " You have carbon in the soil now rather than in the atmosphere , " Dontsova said . <p> Rainwater washes bicarbonates into rivers , which run into oceans where carbon , in it 's bicarbonate prison , can be stored for centuries or more , she said . <p> In the desert , there 's nowhere to go , so bicarbonates settle in the dirt and harden , forming caliches , the hardpan just under a layer of desert dirt . <p> At LEO , it 's washed downhill and into instruments that monitor water volume and composition . <p> After the very first rain , 11 pounds of CO2 were removed from one slope . <p> Soil also captures carbon in the form of organic material , the building blocks of life .
