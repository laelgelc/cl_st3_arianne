Libertarians should acknowledge this , if they want to be acknowledged as anything other than clever sophists . <p> For example -- in a libertarian society , there would be no top-down constraints on movement during a pandemic . This would quickly overburden a private healthcare market that is geared towards a lower level of demand , leading to people going without lifesaving care . Yes , in a libertarian society , there would be ample incentive to ramp up capacity quickly -- and to price services according to demand .
