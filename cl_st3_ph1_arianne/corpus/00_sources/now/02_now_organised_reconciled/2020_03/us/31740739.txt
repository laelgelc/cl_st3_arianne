Hyperspectral " sees " more than a thousand bands of spectrum . By comparison , our eyes only see three . Today , most plant health is diagnosed with the naked eye by farmers and crop scouts . However , imagine trying to diagnose the health of an entire farm field that is thousands of acres . Using hyperspectral imaging , we can fly over an entire field and tell the real-time health of the whole field .
