That said , polls show that U.S. Sen. Amy Klobuchar has strong backing among Minnesotans in her uphill battle to become the Democratic nominee for president . <p> Klobuchar has earned it . She 's also the Star Tribune Editorial Board 's choice in a crowded field of candidates who hold strong appeal for varying parts of the electorate . In such situations , it 's wise to be guided by values and track record rather than chase some elusive formula for " electability .
