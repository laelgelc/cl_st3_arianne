<h> We must learn from COVID-19 crisis -- Jared Schumacker <p> As the environment changes because of global average temperature increases , we will likely see viruses like this come back from the distant past . Hotter than normal temperatures in the Arctic have started to melt the permafrost in the tundra regions . Not only do we have very high levels of carbon dioxide and methane frozen in the ground , but scientists believe viruses may be there that have not seen the light of day for thousands of years -- diseases we do not have the antibodies for . <p> I admit this is a terrible way to look at something that is threatening the lives of possibly millions of people around the globe , but according to Global Health Security Index , most countries are not prepared to fight global pandemics of this magnitude . <p> Hopefully , a silver lining from this terrible tragedy will be that countries work together to shore up their responses to these pandemics , and that they focus on the more looming threat of climate change .
