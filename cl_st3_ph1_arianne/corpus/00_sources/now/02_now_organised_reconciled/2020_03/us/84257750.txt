<h> The Local 913 : Short Fictions <p> I 'd tell you all about Fates Worse Than Death , the new album from Short Fictions , but Pitchfork got there first . The band is one of a few Pittsburgh acts that have recently received positive reviews from the national music tastemaker and although he 's grateful , lead singer Sam Treber seems to be taking the attention in stride . <p> His reaction to the write-up is n't all that surprising to anyone familiar with the down-to-earth culture of the Steel City . Treber 's home city is a central topic on the new album . He says the record is " mostly about climate change . And also a lot about gentrification in Pittsburgh . And then there 's some just like lighthearted songs . " <p> One of those lighthearted songs is " Really Like You , " whose sound hearkens back to the indie pop and emo sounds of the new millennium . Treber does n't reject the " emo " label but adds that Short Fictions ' sound encompasses everything from pop to horns and glockenspiel to math rock .
