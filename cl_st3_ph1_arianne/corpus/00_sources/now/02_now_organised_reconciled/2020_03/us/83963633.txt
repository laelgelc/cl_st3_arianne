<h> Models Walked on Water at Balenciaga 's Apocalyptic Fall 2020 Show <p> The world may be coming to an end , but Demna Gvasalia still wants us to dress up . <p> View photos <p> A look from the Balenciaga Fall 2020 collection . <p> On day six of Paris Fashion Week , Demna Gvasalia echoed last season by presenting his Fall 2020 collection for Balenciaga at a film studio in a northern suburb of Paris . But this time , the designer purposely flooded the center of the amphitheater , meaning fashoin people had to watch the collection from the third row or beyond to avoid getting splashed by models as they marched through murky waters . <p> Clearly , Gvasalia has climate change and the end of the world on his mind -- and yet , he still wants us to dress up . The cold-weather range featured glittering bodysuits , strong-shouldered trench coats , priest-like cloaks , pleated skirts , shiny capes and red carpet-ready gowns .
