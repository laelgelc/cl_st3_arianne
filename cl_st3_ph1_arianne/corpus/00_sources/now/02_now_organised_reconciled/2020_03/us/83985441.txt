<h> Billionaire Tom Steyer Drops Out Of Presidential Race <p> After placing third in the state 's primary on Saturday with only 11 percent of the vote , billionaire Tom Steyer announced he would be suspending his campaign . Steyer said " " There 's no question today that this campaign , we were disappointed with where we came out . " <p> Steyer 's campaign focused on issues of climate change and racial justice ; he had spent $158 million on TV and radio ads , with $21 million of that spent in South Carolina as of last Tuesday . He also held more events in the state than any other candidate . Steyer told supporters Saturday evening , " We live in a country that is deeply unjust economically where rich people have been profiting at the expense of everybody else . And I did n't get in this race and start talking about things to get votes . I was in this race to talk about things that I cared the most about . "
