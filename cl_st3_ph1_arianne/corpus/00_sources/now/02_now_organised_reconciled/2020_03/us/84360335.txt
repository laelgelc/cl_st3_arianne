The coronavirus is hitting California 's most famous tourist sites hard . From Disneyland to Yosemite National Park , the Golden State 's iconic destinations are closed or shuttering amenities to prevent the spread of the pandemic . Tourists who have n't canceled their trips are finding restaurants and bars closed in Los Angeles and San Francisco . Major attractions like Alcatraz off San Francisco and Santa Monica Pier are closed . ( AP Photo/Marcio Jose Sanchez , File ) <p> Gov . Gavin Newsom 's announcement late Monday that parking lots are being closed at state parks in California to enforce social distancing has sparked some confusion . <p> Not all state parks are affected . In fact , only 36 of California 's 280 state parks will close parking lots . They are located in five of the state 's 58 counties . Although they are closed to vehicles , most remain open @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ state parks remain open for now , and their parking lots remain open , said Gloria Sandoval , spokeswoman for the California state parks department .
