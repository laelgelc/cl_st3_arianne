<h> Danny Glover stumps for Bernie Sanders in Jackson <p> Actor Danny Glover held campaign organization events in support of Sanders at Calloway High School . <p> The group walked around Jackson neighborhoods going door to door to campaign for Sanders ... hoping to win votes . <p> Glover says one of the many reasons he shows his support for Sanders is because of his past history with the civil rights movement . <p> " So , what are we doing out here ? We have this moment to vote what 's right . What 's in our hearts . And what 's in our hearts is a program Sanders has placed on a table for us about global warming , climate change , equity and racial injustice . All of this is what 's in people 's hearts around here . " <p> Mississippi supporters say they will continue to campaign for Senator Sanders until the primary election on Tuesday .
