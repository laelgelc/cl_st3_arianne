<p> THE Garnaut climate change report in 2008 predicted that Australia 's bushfire season would begin earlier and finish later and be more intense - but we would not see the effects until 2020 . Yet Peter Carroll can still write ( The Examiner , March 1 ) that Labor 's zero emission policy is " built on the back of the failed and often fraudulent prognostications of climate change alarmists " . It has been reported ( citation provided ) that this year 's fires burned 46 per cent more than in the Amazon forests last year . There is amongst climate change denialists and one suspects , conservative supporters , a latent anger about being caught out . Nonetheless , instead of " I told you so " , Labor is attempting to bring the country along with them to make constructive change . A difficult task when after the ferocious summer we have just witnessed there are still those who claim that predictions of climate change have experienced a " 100 per cent failure rate " .
