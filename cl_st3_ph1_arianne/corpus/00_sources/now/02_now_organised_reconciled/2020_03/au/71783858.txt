<h> Bill Gates steps down from Microsoft 's board of directors <p> Bill Gates : " Microsoft will always be an important part of my life 's work . " <p> Getty Images <p> Microsoft on Friday said that co-founder and technology adviser Bill Gates has departed the company 's board of directors . Gates left in order to " dedicate more time to his philanthropic priorities including global health , development , education , and his increasing engagement in tackling climate change , " according to a release . He 'll remain a technology adviser to CEO Satya Nadella , as well as other Microsoft leaders . <p> Gates stepped away from his day-to-day role at Microsoft in 2008 in order to focus his efforts on the Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation . He was chairman of the board at Microsoft until February 2014 . <p> " It 's been a tremendous honor and privilege to have worked with and learned from Bill over the years , " Nadella said in a statement . " I am grateful for Bill 's friendship and look forward to continuing to work alongside him to realize our mission to empower every @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . " <p> Microsoft 's board will now consist of 12 members . <p> In a blog post , Gates said : " Stepping down from the board in no way means stepping away from the company . Microsoft will always be an important part of my life 's work and I will continue to be engaged with Satya and the technical leadership to help shape the vision and achieve the company 's ambitious goals . "
