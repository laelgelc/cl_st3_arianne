<p> The health , economic and political systems of countries around the world have been exposed as being woefully unprepared for the current crisis . <p> How could they not have been , you may ask ? After all , it is a novel virus . <p> But we have been here before , several times in recent decades : SARS in 2002-2004 ; the 2009 swine flu ; several Ebola outbreaks ; and of course HIV AIDS . Arguably it was just a matter of time before one of these evil genii escaped the bottle and went global . <p> Is there a lesson here for the energy sector ?
