<p> Merge a couple of fairy tales for some judgement lines on Australia 's Pacific step-up . <p> Canberra likes to think of itself as the handsome prince . The self-proclaimed partner of choice is anxious to be chosen . <p> Looking in the magic mirror , Canberra asks , ' Mirror , mirror on the wall , who is the fairest of them all ? ' Trouble is , the mirror replies that this is a complex question with different answers .
