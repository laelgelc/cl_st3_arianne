<h> Record numbers flock to 2020 WOMADelaide <p> GALLERY : WOMADelaide attracted a record 97,000 people to Botanic Park over the weekend , with a host of musical highlights including The Cat Empire , Blind Boys of Alabama , Australia 's Spinifex Gum , Mali 's Salif Keita and Finnish foursome Tuuletar . <p> The attendance figure surpassed the previous record of 96,000 , set in 2018 , and was helped by the ideal festival weather across all four days . <p> " This record attendance result in this 60th anniversary Adelaide Festival year is just wonderful and we are thrilled that so many Australians of all ages from across the country so strongly embraced the program , " says WOMADelaide director Ian Scobie . <p> While headliner Ziggy Marley was a last-minute cancellation , that did n't seem to dampen festival-goers ' spirits , with his Monday closing night spot filled by Australian singer-songwriter Matt Corby . <p> The four-day line-up of more than 40 musicians yielded a host of memorable moments on stage and off , including roving entertainment by the delightful 4m-tall French puppets of Company Archibald Caramantran . <p> As darkness fell , Australian artist @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the park into " sculptural monuments " to environmental advocates , while the nightly aerial spectacular As the World Tipped combined aerial acrobatics with large screen projections to create a unique and captivating show warning about the impacts of climate change . <p> Part of artist Craig Walsh 's MONUMENTS .
