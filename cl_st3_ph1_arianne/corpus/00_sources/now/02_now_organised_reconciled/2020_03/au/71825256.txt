The ensuing backlash has led to an increase in terms such as " neo-Malthusian " and " populationist " . <p> While it is essential that we embrace a critical mindset and challenge some of the arguments used by people such as Attenborough , it is also essential that we do it in a way that adds nuance rather than taking it away .
