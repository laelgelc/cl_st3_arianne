<h> Summers get longer as Australia warms more than the rest of the world <p> Australia 's temperatures are increasing more than the global average , the nation 's weather bureau says . <p> Bureau of Meteorology head of climate monitoring Karl Braganza said Australia had warmed by about 1.4 degrees while the rest of the world had increased by 1.1 degree . <p> " Australia warms slightly more than the global average , " Dr Braganza told a Senate estimates committee in Canberra on Monday . <p> When temperatures across the rest of the world increased by an average of 3.4 degrees -- as estimated in a recent report -- Australia is projected to be closer to 4 degrees , he said . <p> BOM chief Andrew Johnson said Australia was expected to become drier and warmer -- conditions that would bring further increased risks of severe fire weather . <p> The sky over Mallacoota , in East Gippsland , at the height of this summer 's bushfires . <p> It comes as an independent analysis of weather data shows Australian summers are getting longer and winters shorter . <p> The Australia Institute studied BOM data for Australian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from 1950-1969 . <p> It found all Australian capital cities had longer summers and shorter winters , with summer 31 days longer and winter three weeks shorter than in the 1950s and 1960s . <p> In regional areas the trend was even more pronounced -- including the NSW town of Port Macquarie , on the Mid-North Coast . <p> " Summers in Port Macquarie have increased by 48 days , while the catastrophic 2019 fires near Port Macquarie occurred before summer as defined by the calendar , but well within the new summer as caused by climate change , " the report summarised .
