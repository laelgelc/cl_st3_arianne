Given how inappropriate it would seem for Australia 's great homes of learning and research to invest -- however unknowingly -- in the very industry that drives climate change , it 's worth considering why the university community does n't have a super fund that holds no carbon energy assets . <p> The problem with super is that it does n't command the attention .
