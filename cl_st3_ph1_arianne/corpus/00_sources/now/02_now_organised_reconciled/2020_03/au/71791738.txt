<p> On the polls , Australia 's long-time Labor Opposition leader Bill Shorten will be prime minister by the middle of next year . So a major speech on a future Labor government 's foreign policy , delivered on Monday at the Lowy Institute , was an important set of signals on Australia 's direction in a changing world . <p> There will be more Pacific , less Middle East , and sensible caution where Australia 's interests diverge from the US and China . <p> So how was it ? As a set of guiding ideas , pretty sensible , actually . Speeches like this can be a bit of a wish-list , brainstormed before the hard budget choices really have to be made in government .
