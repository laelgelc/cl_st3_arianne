However , the scale and extent of the recent fires have clearly been unprecedented . Years of drought have left the bush tinder dry . This coupled with high temperatures and strong winds have led to the recent disastrous fire season that claimed many properties and lives . But what is at the heart of such unprecedented disasters ? <p> Big data science and machine learning algorithms have suggested increasing Australian bushfire frequencies over the last decade and hint at a major climatic change in the not-too-distant future . Recent scientific results have shown that individual weather events can indeed be traced to anthropogenic climate change ( ACC ) .
