And Prime Minister Scott Morrison just is n't invested in the business of governing . <p> Sure , he is interested in the notion of being PM and all the status and the perks that this implies , but when we get down to the actual work required , well , there are other things he 'd rather do . This is because his philosophy on leadership , like many of Morrison 's policies , is notional at best . <h> THE NOTIONAL PM <p> In a reactionary move after criticism over his lack of leadership ( based on the fact that he was in Hawaii living it up ) during the bushfire crisis , the PM announced a $2 billion bushfire recovery fund .
