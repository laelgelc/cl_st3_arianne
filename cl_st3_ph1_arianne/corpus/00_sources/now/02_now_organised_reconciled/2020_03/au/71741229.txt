<h> Thousands of kilometres of Australia 's beaches at risk from rising seas <p> More than 12,000 kilometres of Australia 's sandy beaches are threatened by coastal erosion by the end of the century , with greater losses predicted if greenhouse gas emissions remain high . <p> The projections , made by European researchers and published in Nature Climate Changeon Tuesday , used satellite data that tracked shoreline change from 1984 to 2015 . They found a " substantial proportion " of the world 's sandy coastline is already eroded , a trend that could worsen as climate change pushes up sea levels . <p> Australia 's coastline has already seen significant areas of retreat in recent years , such as at Inverloch , in Victoria.Credit:Google News Lab <p> Under a " moderate " effort to curb emissions - with carbon pollution peaking at 2040 and then declining - at least 12,324 kilometres of Australia 's sandy coast will be threatened with erosion by 2100 .
