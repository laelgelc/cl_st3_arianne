Sooner or later , technology will overcome these flaws , while also making it cheaper to produce . <p> Historically , every advance in the whisky industry has been met with resistance . The continuous or column still , introduced in the early 1800s , increased production 10-fold but sacrificed the depth and palate weight of the older pot stills . Irish producers , then the world 's pre-eminent whiskey makers , shunned the new invention , only to be swallowed up by Scotland 's more open-minded industry ( which continues to use pot stills for its finest single malts ) . Are we witnessing another significant moment in whisky history ?
