<p> REPRESENTATIVES from about 50 environmental and sustainability groups from across the Central Highlands forged valuable connections in Ballarat on Saturday , with many walking away with ideas for new opportunities and feelings of hope for the future . The focus of the forum , organised by the Ballarat Environment Network and the Victorian Environmental Friends Network , was to connect the diverse range of groups with members from all age groups and walks of life to each other , to discuss the challenges associated with running a volunteer group and to share ideas for ways of addressing those challenges . Through these discussions , one point that was discussed was ways of encouraging community to be more involved with nature conservation , to ensure the likes of Landcare and ' Friends ' groups are viable into the future and the work they have achieved is preserved .
