<p> All I have to say right now is " WTF Australia " ? We used to be cool ! It was as if our nation was in its early 20s , young at heart and inherently friendly to everyone , while regularly having a tongue in cheek go at those we liked best . But we appear to have crashed headfirst into early onset midlife crisis , and the whole thing has just imploded . Only we did n't buy a Ferrari and leave our spouse for a younger model - we bought a third-hand Tarago and whacked a stick figure family of stickers on the back window . I 'm not sure what has gone wrong , or when it all went wrong , but something definitely happened to us .
