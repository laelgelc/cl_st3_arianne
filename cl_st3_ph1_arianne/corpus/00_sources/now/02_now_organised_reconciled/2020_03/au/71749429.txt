<h> How Greece and Europe can both win <p> The press is making much of the academic qualifications of Greece 's new finance minister , Yanis Varoufakis . His specialisation is economic game theory , which in this case might be described as ' the art of bargaining ' . Good bargaining skills are , indeed , important . But there are also some basic realities that ca n't be altered by the skill of the bargainers . <p> The first of these is that Greece has more official debt than it can ever hope to pay back ( 175% of GDP ) . But there is no realistic prospect that this debt can be written off ( or even written down to a manageable figure ) in the current negotiations . The second basic fact is that all parties to the negotiation would rather Greece continue as part of the euro , because ' Grexit ' would be a disruptive mess . <p> These basic realities should define the logic of the negotiation . First , the repayment should be pushed even further into the future and the interest burden should be trimmed .
