Restrictions in the free movement of people is disrupting economic activity across the world as measures to control the coronavirus roll out . <p> There is a strong link between economic activity and global carbon dioxide emissions , due to the dominance of fossil fuel sources of energy . At best , a financial crisis delays emissions growth a few years .
