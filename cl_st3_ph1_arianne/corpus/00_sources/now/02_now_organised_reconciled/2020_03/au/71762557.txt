Dressed in a gold-and-black zebra-print outfit , British singer Dua Lipa watched as lifesavers did a choreographed dance to her music . Grammy Award-winning British pop singer Sam Smith also made a surprise appearance , posing with fans as Promises played and glitter and confetti rained over the crowd . Smith later performed at the after party , where they appeared in a giant red " Equality " dress ( of sorts ) . <p> It was n't the first time the NSW RFS had a float at Mardi Gras , but this year the cheers for the firefighters were particularly loud , following their tireless work during the recent bushfires . In keeping with tradition , many floats had political messages , such as equality , homelessness , climate change and recognition for the myriad LGBTQIA+ community members . <p> Three protesters were removed from the parade by police for what they called " unauthorised entry " .
