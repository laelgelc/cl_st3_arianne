<h> Repoa : Norway grants 3.6bn/-to boost research <p> THE country 's top think-tank-REPOA has received 15 million NOK ( over 3.6bn/- ) from Norwegian government to boost the institution 's research capacity in the areas of productive sectors , climate change , gender and human development . <p> Speaking soon after signing the grant agreement in Dar es Salaam yesterday REPOA executive director , Dr Donald Mmari said the funding will enable the institution to implant its strategic plan for the next four years . <p> Dr Mmari said the four-year strategic plan also focuses on governance , accountability , citizen engagement as well as other crosscutting issues such as climate change , technology and innovation . <p> " We are focusing these essential elements to inform a comprehensive national agenda on socio-economic transformation for inclusive development and the commitments to achieve sustainable development goals ( SDGs ) , " he noted . <p> Dr Mmari said the think-tank will also continue to undertake credible research and analytical work , to provide information that is relevant for evidence-formed policy making and continue providing capacity development to diverse stakeholders . <p> He said the grant will provide the institution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ along with related activities for promoting evidence-informed policy dialogue and development and targeted capacity building for researchers and research users . <p> " Today marks a significant milestone in our partnership with Norwegian embassy ; the partnership has a long history since 2000 where it has been providing core funding along with other partners including the embassies of Netherlands , Sweden , Denmark , Finland and Ireland . We thank you for trusting us and we vow to work , " he stated . <p> He said for nearly ten years from 2001 to 2011 , the embassy had supported a " formative research programme on local government reform " , which was implemented jointly with the Norwegian think-tank Christian Michelsen Institute ( CMI ) . The programme helped to build REPOA 's competitive advantage on local government and decentralization research in Tanzania . <p> In her remarks , Norwegian ambassador in Tanzania , Elisabeth Jacobsen said that despite various challenges , Repoa has remained dedicated in the pursuit of its mission by being innovative , patient and strategic in its engagement and partnership with the government and other actors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ provide core support to Repoa , our support is centred towards promoting institutional capacity , enabling independent research agenda setting and also strength the institution 's ability to stay relevant , " she noted <p> Jacobsen further said that " We have seen very limited funding for research by private sector and government in many countries in sub-Saharan Africa including Tanzania , and therefore it is important for us to contribute to strengthening independent think tanks . " <p> She said that Repoa objectives and research themes as stated in the strategic plan fully aligns with Norwegian priorities in the areas such as private sector development , job creation , climate change , renewable energy and environment as well as governance and democratic development . <p> REPOA was recently ranked as the top think tank in Tanzania for the 6th consecutive year and the number 11 of the 612 leading research organizations in Sub-Sahara Africa in the 2019 Global Go to Think Tank Index ( GGTI ) .
