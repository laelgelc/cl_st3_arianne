<p> WASHINGTON -- Probably for most of us , the coronavirus crisis will soon enough -- Six months ? A year ? -- recede in our minds and come to seem like a hallucinatory moment . Maybe it will be like a hurricane that forced everyone to rush inland and then only glanced the coast . <p> Or maybe it will be like a hurricane that really does hit . Even then , human nature being what it is , most people will clean up and move on . <p> Yet no matter how the coronavirus pandemic passes , or how quickly , there is likely in these strange housebound weeks a new political epoch being born . <p> There are two large reasons to believe the political echo of this crisis will last much longer than the crisis itself . <p> The first is that many of the people whose expectations and routines are most dramatically upended by the pandemic are students .
