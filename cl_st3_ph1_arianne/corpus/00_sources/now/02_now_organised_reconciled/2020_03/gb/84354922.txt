We 're more considered than ever in the transport we use , the products we buy and the food we eat . The good news is that responsible fish farming is bringing environmentally friendly seafood to our dinner tables , and it 's backed by strict requirements that benefit everyone . <h> Responsible seafood is already available <p> Fish is an ideal food for a burgeoning global population . It 's high in protein , low in fat and full of nutrients . Each year , the world produces more farmed fish than it does beef , and more than half the fish consumed worldwide comes from aquaculture . Independent organisation the Aquaculture Stewardship Council ( ASC ) is a global non-profit that ensures fish farms adhere to strict environmental and social welfare conditions and its certification is internationally recognised as ensuring farms meet best practice . They 've done the hard work so all you have to do is look for the green ASC label next time you buy fish . <p> The ASC ensures aquaculture operators adopt practices that are good for the environment and workers , as well as being commercially viable . Issues such as water pollution , disruption to local ecosystems and poor working conditions must be mitigated . <p> Creating sustainable seafood practices is two-fold : it needs to consider both the impact on the environment , and the conditions for producers and their workers .
