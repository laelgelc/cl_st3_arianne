<h> Jackie Baillie 's approach to democracy is like that of a dictator <h> Letters <p> JACKIE Baillie , a candidate for the Labour party 's deputy leader , gives three reasons why the Labour party must continue with the disastrous policy which has done such damage to the Labour party in Scotland , ie denying the right of the Scottish people to have another independence referendum . <p> She claims that it would be against Labour " values " to accept the right of the Scottish people to have such a vote . This claim flies in the face of Scottish Labour history . The Labour party was put together by people who supported the right of the Scottish people to self-determination , and the Labour party in Scotland supported the claim of right . <p> She also claims that work on a referendum would damage public services and provisions , although she offers not a scrap of evidence for this remarkable assertion , which has no rational basis . <p> She also claims that if " we " do not support the " ends " then " we " should not support the means .
