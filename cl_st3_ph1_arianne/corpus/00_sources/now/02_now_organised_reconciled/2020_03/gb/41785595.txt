<p> Thousands of new homes will be needed in Leicester for the city 's growing population over the next 15 or 20 years and council bosses have now mapped out dozens of sites where they think they should be built . <p> Officials have earmarked 83 sites for housing developments as well as the building of schools , industrial schemes and offices which they say will underpin their efforts to find the land needed . <p> The list of sites has been narrowed down from 240 suggested by the council itself and private landowners around two years ago . <p> They range from larger strategic greenfield areas with enough space to take hundreds of homes to much smaller patches for potential development where a handful of houses could be built . <p> Included in the list are former industrial plots ,
