<h> This startup is using AI to investigate crime on the high seas <p> Set up by two former Israeli navy intelligence officers in 2010 , Tel Aviv-based Windward is a relentless ocean sleuth . Its technology has thrown light on a wide range of murky maritime activities -- from attempts by Iran to defy oil sanctions to Chinese expansionism in the South China Sea -- even when the perpetrators are trying to evade detection . <p> Windward uses different data sources to map events across the world 's oceans .
