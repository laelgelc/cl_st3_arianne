<h> Heathrow third runway plans ruled illegal in major blow for Government <p> The Government 's plans to allow a third runway at Heathrow airport have been ruled unlawful for failing to consider the environmental impact of the scheme . <p> In a major blow to the project , judges agreed that the runway did not take into account the UK 's commitments under the Paris Agreement on climate change . <p> But other challenges to the Department of Transport 's policy document regarding noise and air quality were not upheld by the court . <p> Ministers now have the option to scrap the project , or present a new policy document outlining the environmental impact of a third runway . <p> The decision was welcomed by campaign groups that brought the legal challenge , which included Friends of the Earth and Plan B , as well as Heathrow Hub Ltd , Greenpeace , the Mayor of London and five local councils . <p> Their case initially failed in the high court in May 2019 , but was allowed an appeal in October , with the verdict delivered on Thursday morning . <p> " We are delighted with The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the massive importance of the legal system to check the clear abuse of state power by government , such as in this case , " said Will Rundle , head of legal at Friends of the Earth . <p> " Shockingly , this case revealed that the government accepted legal advice that it should not consider the Paris Agreement when giving the third runway the go-ahead .
