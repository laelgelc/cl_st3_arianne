<p> The study , published in Nature Climate Change , says erosion is already a major problem , and will worsen with the rising sea levels brought about by climate change . <p> In 30 years time , erosion will have destroyed 22,430 miles of beaches or 13% of coastlines spotted in satellite images . <p> In the second half of next century , another 25.7% of Earth 's beaches will wash away , The Guardian reported . <p> Scientists from the Joint Research Centre ( JRC ) , the European Commission 's science and knowledge service , analysed 35 years of coastal satellite observations , plus 82 years of climate and sea level projections from several climate models . <p> The findings come from the first global assessment of future sandy shoreline dynamics .
