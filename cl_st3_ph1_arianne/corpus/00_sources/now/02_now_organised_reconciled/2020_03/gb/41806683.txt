<p> The Government is set to reverse a ban on onshore wind farm subsidies brought in under David Cameron . <p> Officials have told climate campaigners that onshore wind farms would soon be able to bid for subsidies from the Government . <p> The U-turn comes as ministers face increasing pressure to set out how the UK will hit its target of net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 . <p> Land-based wind turbines have long been unpopular with grassroots Conservatives , leading the then-Prime Minister Mr Cameron to say he wanted to " rid " the countryside of the " unsightly " structures in 2015 . <p> Onshore wind was officially blocked from bidding for financial support available to other forms of renewable energy in 2016 , leading to a 94% decline in the number of new projects up to 2019 . <p> Environmental groups have consistently protested against the ban , arguing that onshore wind is the cheapest new form of electric energy and has widespread public support . <p> According to The Guardian , Energy Secretary Alok Sharma is expected to say that ending the UK 's contribution to the climate crisis " means making the most of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ wind and solar projects " . <p> This will be achieved " in a way that works for everyone , listening to local communities and giving them an effective voice in decisions that affect them " . <p> ' GREAT NEWS ' <p> The announcement was welcomed by environmental groups such as Possible , which has been campaigning for an end to the blocks on onshore wind since 2016 . <p> " After years of campaigning , today we can finally celebrate the UK 's cheapest new energy source -
