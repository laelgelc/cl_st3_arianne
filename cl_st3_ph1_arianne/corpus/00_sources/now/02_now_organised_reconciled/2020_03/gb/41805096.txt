<p> Half the world 's sandy beaches may be wiped away by the end of the century due to rising sea levels and other climate change effects , new research has found . <p> A large proportion of shoreline in densely populated areas is projected to be lost as rising seas , changing weather patterns and other factors erode sandy beaches that now account for more than a third of global sea coasts . <p> The study showed Australia , Canada , Chile , Mexico , China and the United States will be among the hardest hit . <p> Lead author of the study , coastal oceanographer Michalis Vousdoukas of the European Commission 's Joint Research Centre in Ispra , Italy said : " Touristic areas which have sandy beaches as their main selling point will probably face strong consequences .
