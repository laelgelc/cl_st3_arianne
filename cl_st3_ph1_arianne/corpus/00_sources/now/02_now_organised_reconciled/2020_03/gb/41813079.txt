<p> Investment managers will demand to know what companies are doing to manage climate change in this year 's AGM season . <p> The Investment Association ( IA ) , whose members own one third of the value of UK listed companies , is asking companies to report the impact of climate change will have on their business model and how it will be measured and managed . <p> The IA 's institutional voting information service ( IVIS ) will track companies ' progress , with investors wanting to see significant movement towards reporting in line with Task Force for Climate-related Financial Disclosures recommendations by 2022 . <p> Andrew Ninian , director for stewardship and corporate governance at the IA , said : " Climate change could result in a significant loss of value in companies if risks are not effectively measured and managed , ultimately hitting savers ' pockets .
