<h> Join in for Earth Hour tonight at 8.30pm <p> Tonight from 8.30-9.30pm it 's Earth Hour , a yearly movement taking place on the last Saturday of March that calls attention to climate change and promote better treatment of our planet . The initiative , which was started by the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF ) , took root as a lights-out event in Sydney back in 2007 . Since then however , it has grown into one of the world 's largest grassroots environment-focused movements , with millions of people in over 180 countries now engaging every year . <p> Hongkongers have always shown support for Earth Hour in a big way , like in 2017 , when over 5600 companies and 300 schools took part in the movement , or in 2018 when more than 11,000 city dwellers pledged to turn off non-essential lighting for the event . This year , two of our city 's top restaurants , Amber and Somm , will be observing Earth Hour by replacing all lights in their dining areas with candles between 8-10pm .
