<p> Britain is still very much a nation wedded to our cars . And it 's not just long trips : 60 per cent of car journeys are for less than six miles . <p> In London , Transport for London ( TfL ) wants people to replace these car journeys with more active travel alternatives like cycling and walking , while the Department for Transport has been exploring the feasibility of safely deploying cleaner micromobility options like e-scooters by conducting trials in Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park . But the car remains the travel mode of choice for many . <p> That means that the electrification of road travel is crucial if we 're going to keep people and goods moving around our cities while protecting air quality . <p> Myths about electric cars abound . People underestimate their range , overestimate how expensive they are , and worry about how safe they are to drive . <p> We hosted the launch of BP 's On the Move report last year , which found that the big barriers in the public 's mind were the cost of vehicles ( 47 per cent ) , the availability of charging points @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( 39 per cent ) , and how long vehicles take to charge ( 37 per cent ) . <p> Both the government and industry have a role to play here in educating people about electric carss , addressing these concerns and allowing people to test vehicles to help them feel more comfortable . <p> An electric car communications vacuum is the perfect breeding ground for misinformation and fear , so industry and government must get on the front foot . <p> The need for a people-centric approach was backed up by a roundtable we hosted , which brought together automotive leaders , representatives from charities , and a major airport . <p> The consensus was that carrots such as financial incentives can be hugely effective , and we need more of them -- combined with insights from behavioural economics and a sound understanding of how different segments of society have different desires and wants . <p> For example , people with disabilities often rely on their cars , but research by TfL has found that blue badge holders have concerns about the reliability of electric cars and are typically @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ these nuances into account . <p> Finally , we need to motivate people to change behaviour , if they 're able to .
