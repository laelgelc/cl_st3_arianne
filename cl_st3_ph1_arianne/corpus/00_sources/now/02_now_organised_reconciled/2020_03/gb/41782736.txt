<h> Vital Scottish environment checks down by a quarter <h> The Ferret <p> THE number of annual inspections of sites regulated by the Scottish Environment Protection Agency ( Sepa ) fell by a quarter over the last five years , prompting concerns about pollution . <p> Data obtained by The Ferret under freedom of information law shows that the number of site inspections by Sepa officers to check for environmental problems fell by more than a thousand between 2014-15 and 2018-19 . <p> Sepa has also pursued fewer prosecutions against polluters breaking environmental law , with the number dropping from 23 in 2015 to eight in 2019 . <p> The number of samples taken to gauge water ecology has dropped by almost a third , while the number of locations where samples were taken fell by almost a quarter . <p> The falls have been criticised by one of Sepa 's former chief executives , experts , politicians and environmental campaigners .
