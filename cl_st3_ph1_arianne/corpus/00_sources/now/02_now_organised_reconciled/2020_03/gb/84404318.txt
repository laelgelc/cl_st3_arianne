First kicked off in 2007 by the World Wildlife Fund in Sydney , Earth Hour has been making waves around the world , encouraging people to switch off their lights for one hour . More than a symbolic event , it unites people together in raising awareness about climate change . <p> However , due to COVID-19 , Earth Hour will be going online with live performances and discussions on how to your part to save the planet . Beyond switching off your lights , we 're also here to help you start your zero-waste journey .
