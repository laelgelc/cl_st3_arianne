<h> Madar Farms announces new schools program to teach UAE youth the importance of a sustainable future <p> The AgTech company announces ' Sustainable Futures ' - a first-of-its-kind educational program that will increase access to sustainability-focused teaching and learning . <p> Press Release <p> The hands-on and easy-to-integrate curriculum includes six modules that will cover a wide range of topics and help students learn about sustainability challenges , opportunities and solutions specific to the UAE . <p> Launching in September 2020 , registration is now open for private schools across the country . <p> Abu Dhabi : A new program for schools to teach students in the UAE about the importance of food sustainability and empower them to make environmentally responsible choices is to be launched by Madar Farms later this year . <p> Announced today during the Global Educational Supplies and Solutions ( GESS ) Dubai event , ' Sustainable Futures ' is a localized , hands-on and easy-to-integrate curriculum that uses food as a springboard to explore a wide range of topics across the sustainability agenda . <p> Set to begin in September 2020 , registration for the program is now open to all private UAE @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ four schools across Dubai and Abu Dhabi this academic year . <p> Aimed at students aged 11 years and above , ' Sustainable Futures ' combines theory and practical learning . Climate change , sustainable consumption and 4th Industrial Revolution technologies are among the curriculum 's topics . <p> To be delivered by schoolteachers , the program consists of six modules and is bespoke to the UAE 's challenges and opportunities of food security and sustainability . The program supports existing curriculum and extra-curricular activities and provides a more flexible approach for teachers who can choose which lessons to teach from across the modules . <p> Through more than 30 hours of educational content for schools , Madar Farms aims to raise awareness of the current environmental challenges that are being faced today and solutions that can address them , helping students prepare for the future . <p> It is part of Madar Farms ' commitment to sustainability and creating a lasting change that aims to benefit the UAE . <p> As a UAE-based AgTech company , Madar Farms has been leading a new agriculture revolution by using advanced methods @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Farms also uses approximately 95 per cent less water than traditional farming and can grow produce anywhere in the UAE 365 days a year . <p> As part of the program , students will be able to grow a variety of vegetables , fruits , herbs and edible flowers using indoor aeroponic gardens . Through a step-by-step weekly guide , the ' Growing Our Garden ' module will introduce students to the entire food production process including planting seeds , transplanting seedlings , and harvesting of greens . <p> Across the mix-and-match menu of satellite modules , students will also learn what the future of food production may look like on this planet or another , and how they can play a role in ensuring planet Earth sustainably supports current and future generations . They will also explore the use of new , sustainable technologies to meet global challenges , such as how AgTech is being implemented in Abu Dhabi . <p> The curriculum will also address topics ranging from the ways we use water , plastic and energy , to climate refugees , food literacy and civic engagement . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Farms will host workshops between April and June to enable teachers to plan how to use the program for the new academic year .
