<p> " The investments made by our financial system today determine whether we will be able to keep global temperature rises below the 1.5C upper safe limit , " said Fran Boait , executive director of Positive Money . <p> " Finance is currently funding warming of more than 4C , which represents an existential threat not only to finance and the economy , but to life on earth . <p> " As the institution overseeing and underpinning our financial system , the Bank of England must lead the way and do everything it can to stop environmental breakdown being funded on its watch . " <p> The letter calls for Bailey to : make it mandatory for firms under the Bank of England 's supervision to disclose climate risk ; exclude fossil fuels assets from quantitative easing and ban it as pledged collateral at the central bank ; and penalise high-carbon emissions through the central bank 's macro-prudential framework . <p> The letter was published ahead of Bailey 's confirmation hearing in parliament on Wednesday .
