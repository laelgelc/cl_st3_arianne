<h> Coronavirus in Scotland : Deposit return scheme pushed back by over a year <h> Coronavirus in Scotland : Deposit return scheme pushed back by over a year <p> The implementation date for Scotland 's deposit return scheme has been delayed by more than a year amid fears of the effects of coronavirus . <p> Environment Secretary Roseanna Cunningham announced on Monday , the same day the regulations for the scheme were laid before the Scottish Parliament , that the " go live " date would be pushed back from April next year to July 2021 . <p> Under the scheme , people will be able to return a container to shops in return for a 20p fee which was paid when the drink was purchased . <p> The news comes as the number of confirmed cases increased in Scotland to 171 on Monday and the First Minister said new measures to stop its spread would " change life as we know it " . <p> Ms Cunningham also said the extension would allow businesses to ensure the necessary infrastructure was in place before the scheme began . <p> She said : " Our deposit return scheme will be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ UK . <p> " By including a wide range of materials , including glass , and setting a deposit of 20p , we are giving the people of Scotland a clear and straightforward way to do their bit for the environment . <p> " Having listened to the views of stakeholders , I have agreed to extend the ' go-live ' date from April 2021 to July 2022 . " <p> She continued : " This will give businesses more time to prepare their premises for the scheme and crucially , provides flexibility in the immediate term as the whole country prepares to deal with Covid-19 -- the impact of which continues to be closely monitored . <p> " One very important way of achieving our climate targets is in tackling throw-away culture and building a circular economy where our resources are kept in use for as long as possible , and our deposit return scheme is just one part of this .
