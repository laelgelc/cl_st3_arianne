<h> National Science and Media Museum hosts event for Bradford children as part of British Science Week <h> National Science and Media Museum hosts event for Bradford children as part of British Science Week <p> BRADFORD school children celebrated British Science Week at the National Science and Media Museum yesterday , as they participated in a range of action-packed activities . <p> British Science Week , run by the British Science Association , aims to celebrate science and provide a platform to stimulate and support teachers and pupils . <p> The National Science and Media Museum welcomed students from various primary schools , including Dixons Marchbank , Peel Park Primary School and Westminster Primary Academy , amongst others . <p> Stephanie Robinson , Director of Makertopia , led pupils through a creative workshop where they made circuits . <p> " Makertopia uses creative technology as a way to talk about climate change and inequality . By empowering people with practical skills , we help to improve their confidence " , Stephanie said . <p> " Today , pupils developed their art skills , which help them to work collaboratively and creatively .
