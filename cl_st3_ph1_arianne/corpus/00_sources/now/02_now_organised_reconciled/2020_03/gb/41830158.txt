<h> Viral hits : your pop cultural guide to pandemics <p> With coronavirus now officially declared a pandemic by the World Health Organization and a generation of men finally learning to wash their hands after using the bathroom , people are beginning to ask serious questions about quite how much elasticity there is in society 's ability to absorb a major viral outbreak . Luckily , there 's a wide range of feature films , TV shows , documentaries , books , websites and podcasts that deal with the exact situation we may find ourselves in in the ( very ) near future . Even fictional films can give a good rough idea of how governments , multinational organisations and companies try to deal with a viral crisis -- just look at how people are treating Steven Soderbergh 's Contagion as a guide to the Covid-19 outbreak . Meanwhile , Netflix 's Pandemic arrived at such a strangely appropriate moment that red-pill basement-dwellers are beginning to get suspicious ... <p> So without further ado , here 's the definitive list of essential outbreak-related pop culture and where you can find it .
