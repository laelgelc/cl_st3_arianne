The common perception of millennials and generation Z ( broadly , people under 40 ) is that they are self-centred and lack a sense of civic responsibility . This stereotype is about to be tested . <p> Younger people , meanwhile , have blamed their elders for failure to take radical action on climate change , and have pointed out that , with an ageing population , the state pension system as it currently stands represents a huge transfer of wealth from the young to the old . Such accusations have generally not been taken well . <p> Right now , it is younger people who are being asked -- rightly -- to curtail their social activity and make financial sacrifices for the sake of protecting the health of older generations , who are more at risk of Covid-19 .
