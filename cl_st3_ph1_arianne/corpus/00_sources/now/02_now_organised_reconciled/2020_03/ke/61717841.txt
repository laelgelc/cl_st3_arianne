<h> KEMFRI carries out fishing sustainability survey on border lakes <p> A KEMFRI Researcher tests the depth of the lake during the survey at Lake Jipe on Tuesday March 17 , 2020 . Photo by KNA . <p> The Kenya Marine and Fisheries Research Institute ( KEMFRI ) has partnered with Taita-Taveta County government to conduct a scientific survey on Lake Challa and Jipe to explore modalities of sustainable fishing in the two lakes . <p> Lake Challa and Lake Jipe , both in Taveta sub-county , are trans-border lakes straddling the Kenya-Tanzania border . <p> Fishermen in villages neighbouring the lakes from both sister States exploit the lakes through fishing . <p> The joint research will take two-weeks and will establish amongst others , the fish species and population , lake depth , water currents and strength , levels of pollution and ecological threats facing the lakes . <p> Speaking to KNA on Wednesday , County Executive Member for Agriculture , Livestock and Fisheries , Davis Mwangoma said the findings of the survey would form the basis for developing a fishing policy in the county . <p> He pointed out that lack of a comprehensive fishing policy had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the sector included weak regulatory framework , funding for fishing industry and sustainability . <p> Mwangoma said the ultimate aim of the report was to form a roadmap for commercialization of the fish industry and introduction of aqua-culture . <p> " We have not fully exploited these two resources but with the research , we will find a ground for sustainably exploiting these lakes , " he said . <p> The research will further allow the county to establish proper breeding grounds for fish where breeding could be set up for fish farming . <p> The county targets to start producing fingerlings to populate over 200 fishponds in the four sub-counties of Voi , Mwatate , Taveta and Wundanyi . <p> Dr. Joseph Kamau , a researcher with KEMFRI , said the research would answer many questions about fish farming and sustainability in the county . He further disclosed that water levels in Lake Challa has been falling over the decades , a situation that might affect fish population and behavior in the lake . <p> " By time we are through , we will be able to tell you @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ boost production , " he said . <p> The research will also establish the recommended annual fish harvest and best sites to set up breeding cages for local fishermen . <p> Preliminary tests done on the lake Challa showed that high population of large families of fish existed within the depth of 20-40 meters while smaller fish live between 50-60 meters . <p> Most fishermen were however unable to effectively harvest fish from the lake due to lack of proper equipment that could reach those depths . <p> Lake Challa is a crater lake and home to Lake Challa tilapia . The fish is listed as critically endangered by International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) . It is fed by underground rivers that originate from Kilimanjaro in Tanzania .
