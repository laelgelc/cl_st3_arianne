natural disaster in Canadian history " . <p> " Vulnerabilities include risks to life , flood damage to buildings and critical infrastructure , business and service disruptions and estimated displacement of 250,000+ residents , " the paper states . <p> The briefing paper was put together by Steve Litke , senior program manager with the Fraser Basin Council . <p> The document states that
