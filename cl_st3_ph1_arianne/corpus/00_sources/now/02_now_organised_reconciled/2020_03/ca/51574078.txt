They all love a particular patch of ocean , but only 100,000 of them can be there at a time . Each year , 100,000 of those cod were caught . Another 100,000 then move into the newly vacated space . When fishermen came back , it looked like the stock had been replenished . But really , there are only 900,000 cod left . Repeat until you fish that last 100,000 cod . <p> This is a simplistic model , but it 's what happened to cod fishermen . All seemed well until , suddenly , it was not . <p> So , while the Atlantic Canadian fisheries are n't all on the verge of collapse , it 's more important than ever that we monitor them even more closely to make sure we understand what 's actually happening around the fishery and climate change . <p> " I 'm only going to speak personally , but a lot of motivation is in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " They 're difficult problems to figure out , and there 's progress in some areas and lack of progress in others .
