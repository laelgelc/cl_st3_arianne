THE CANADIAN PRESS/Nathan Denette <p> TORONTO - Prime Minister Justin Trudeau is calling on industries , citizens , and Indigenous groups to help shape the future of Canada 's climate policy . <p> Speaking at a major mining conference in Toronto , Trudeau says the government will soon launch a formal initiative to see input from a range of groups on how Canada will get to the goal of net-zero carbon emissions by 2050 . <p> " In the coming year , we want to hear from you on how Canada should innovate and transform our economy to keep good jobs here and create new ones , " said Trudeau . <p> He says a clear way forward is crucial for both the planet and for business to provide certainty and adapt to the changing reality . <p> Taking carbon pollution out of the environment , and the economy , will be crucial for Canada 's prosperity , he said . <p> " This is a big project , and not @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We all need to roll up our sleeves and pitch in .
