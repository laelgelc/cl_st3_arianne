<p> A late winter storm in Grey-Bruce Thursday closed some schools , cancelled school buses , cancelled meetings , events and closed some businesses . With the current weather pattern forecast to hold , snow squalls are expected through Saturday . <p> Buses to public and Catholic schools boards were cancelled , and all English Catholic and French Catholic schools were closed . Ongoing , periodic strikes mean public elementary and secondary school students in the Bluewater District School Board will have the day off again today . <p> Highway 10 was closed in both directions at 3 p.m . Thursday , from Chatsworth to Dundalk , amid Environment Canada blizzard warnings which began about 1:30 p.m. for southern Grey and Bruce counties and snow squall warnings to the north . <p> Snowblowers and plows rumbled across the region to catch up . In Owen Sound , crews focused on the hills and sidewalks , with lower priority areas left for later , city public works and engineering director Dennis Kefalas said . <p> There were four road plows , four sidewalk plows and two graders running to keep on top of snow accumulations , plus front-end loaders for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the westside allowed crews to refill there , avoiding delays crossing to the east side due to detours as the 10th Street bridge work continues . <p> Kefalas and his Grey County counterpart , Pat Hoy , the director of transportation services , said if this weather continues through Saturday as forecast , it will be the biggest snowstorm of the season , which otherwise has been quite tame . <p> Owen Sound had declared a " significant weather event , " by 1:30 p.m. , due to current and forecast conditions , as staff worked to maintain minimum provincial snow-clearing standards . <p> Bruce County made the same declaration , with both city and county advising the public it was n't due to reduced levels of service , though people were told to be cautious . <p> Grey was running all 19 plows day and night , involving some 50 staff .
