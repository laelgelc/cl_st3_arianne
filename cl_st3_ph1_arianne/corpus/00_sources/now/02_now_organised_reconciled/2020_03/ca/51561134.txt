The global realization of climate change has forced all Canadians to rethink how we use and produce energy . <p> During the past two weeks , COVID-19 and the Saudi-Russian oil-price war have led to the most dire market conditions our industry has faced . We are developing Canadian technologies and processes that dramatically reduce carbon emissions and provide the world with cleaner sources of energy . Now is the time for all levels of government to put aside their past differences and implement real solutions . <p> Global scientific , governmental and industry consensus is that the world must move to net-zero GHG emissions by 2050 .
