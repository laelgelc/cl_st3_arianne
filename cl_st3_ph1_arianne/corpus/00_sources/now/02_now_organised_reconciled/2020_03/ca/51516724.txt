<p> This winter has been by far the hottest recorded in Europe , scientists have announced , with the climate crisis likely to have supercharged the heat . <p> The EU 's Copernicus Climate Change Service ( C3S ) data dates back to 1855 . It said the average temperature for December , January and February was 1.4C above the previous winter record , which was set in 2015-16 . New regional climate records are usually passed by only a fraction of a degree . Europe 's winter was 3.4C hotter than the average from 1981-2010 . <p> The unseasonal heat has led to the failure of the ice-wine harvest in Germany and snow having to be imported for sporting events in Sweden and Russia . In Helsinki , Finland , the average temperature for January and February was more than 6C higher than the 1981-2010 average .
