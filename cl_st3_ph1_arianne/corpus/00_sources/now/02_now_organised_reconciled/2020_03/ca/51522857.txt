<p> The theme of the 2020 AgKnowledge Forum , hosted by Nottawasaga Futures with support from the County of Simcoe 's Economic Development office , Simcoe County Federation of Agriculture , Christian Farmers Federation of Ontario and Holland Marsh Growers Association , was how to market and sell your product . <p> Giancarlo Trimarchi described the growth of Vince 's Market from an open-air fruit stand in Sharon , back in 1956 , to an independent grocery chain with locations in Sharon , Newmarket , Uxbridge , and Tottenham , now affiliated with Longo 's . <p> The grocery business in Canada is a " hyper-competitive market , " Trimarchi said , a " concentration of sales ... in so few hands . " <p> While there are numerous independent grocers - both single store operators and small multi-location chains like Farm Boy , Sunripe , Nature 's Emporium , Longo 's and Vince 's Markets - the top three companies ( Sobeys , Loblaws and Metro ) control the bulk of the grocery business . <p> It 's a different process for getting a product on the shelves of each type of business . <p> " Be well-versed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Trimarchi advised , starting with identifying the " decision-makers .
