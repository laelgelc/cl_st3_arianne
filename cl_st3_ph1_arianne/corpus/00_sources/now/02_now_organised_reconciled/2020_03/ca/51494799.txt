Check out how these researchers are turning waste into worth and paving the way for travel-lovers . <h> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ University chemist Stephanie MacQuarrie teamed up with Louisbourg Seafoods to investigate additional uses for wasted carcasses left behind after the snow crab fishing process . The results of their study has led the team to an innovative way to turn crab waste into fertilizer , use it to strengthen concrete , or even to neutralize acidic wastewater . <p> MacQuarrie dries and crushes the shells into small particles which are burned in an oxygen-free atmosphere to create a charcoal product called biochar . <p> Prior to the study , processors like Louisbourg Seafoods had been spending $35,000 a year to get rid of the crab waste , and can now save the material from landing in a composting site while creating a higher-value product in the process . <h> Mount Saint Vincent University <p> During her Ph.D . study , Shuyue Huang of Mount Saint Vincent University 's Business and Tourism Department , developed a tourist engagement scale that measures how tourists co-create value with service providers , employees , and fellow travellers .
