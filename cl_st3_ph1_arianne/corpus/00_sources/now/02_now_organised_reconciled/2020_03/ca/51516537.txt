<h> Why do oil and gas projects keep blowing their budgets ? <p> This came as a bit of a shock : Ernst and Young , one of the most respected management consultancy firms on the planet , took a close look a large segment of the global oil and gas industry and essentially accused it of grossly incompetent project management . <p> Peel back the polite language of a consultant 's white paper and Ernst and Young 's Spotlight on Oil and Gas Megaprojects contains a series of bombshells for any government or community that expects to build a thriving economy on fossil fuel development . <p> " Despite the importance of project performance as it relates to enterprise value and share price , a high percentage of projects fail to deliver on time or meet approved budgets , " the paper states . <p> " Longer-term industry outlooks suggest that project delivery success is actually decreasing , especially in certain segments of the industry such as deepwater , where complexity is considerably higher .
