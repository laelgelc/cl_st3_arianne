<h> Ziya Tong and The Reality Bubble <p> I talked with Ziya Tong , the Canadian television host and author of The Reality Bubble : Blind Spots , Hidden Truths and the Dangerous Illusions That Shape our World , while we were both in England in early February . Her interview with actor and activist Russell Brand was going viral on Youtube , and she told me how she 'd talked with Rowan Hooper of New Scientist and appeared on the BBC World Service . She gave talks at the AllBright Club , the How To Academy and Google . In Bristol , she spoke at the Festival of Ideas . The events were almost all sold out . <p> " All these great events , it 's been really exceptional for a first-time author . It 's really hard to get published in the U.K. when you 're a foreign writer that nobody 's heard of , " Tong told me . <p> Tong , who was born in England and grew up in Hong Kong , said she developed her love of science during her 17 years as a Canadian science journalist and reporter , working for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Channel 's Daily Planet .
