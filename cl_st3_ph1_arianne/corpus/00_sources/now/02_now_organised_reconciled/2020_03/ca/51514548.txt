<p> Lakehead University Thunder Bay is hosting its annual Research and Innovation Week from Feb. 26 to March 5 , with a theme of Our Creative Mind . <p> The various talks and activities will interest you and your family and friends , no matter how old ( or young ) they are , especially Saturday , Feb. 29 when researchers will be at the Intercity Shopping Centre . <p> Lakehead University researchers and students will host interactive booths at the mall from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. for the Festival of Ideas . When you stop by the mall , you will learn more about drones , virtual reality , science experiments , green energy , music , doggy DNA , artificial intelligence , and you can also attend an Indigenous Fashion Show .
