The UN 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) reports that from 2010 to 2016 , global food loss and waste contributed 8-10% of human-caused green-house gas emissions . <p> According to the food rescue organization , Second Harvest , 58% of food produced in Canada is lost or wasted , amounting to 35.5 million tonnes a year . Much of that could actually be rescued and eaten . But how ?
