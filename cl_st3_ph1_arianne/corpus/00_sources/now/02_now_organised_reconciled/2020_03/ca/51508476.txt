<p> There 's a new kid in town -- the Climate Vegan Campaign . Climate Vegan is a term recently coined by a group of Toronto activists whose aim is to join the dots of forest destruction , water usage , pollution , and greenhouse gas emissions to the cow in the china shop -- animal agriculture . <p> " People can really make a difference every day three times a day whenever they eat , " says Jenny McQueen , a vegan for 20 years , who has recently joined the climate vegan craze . " While watching the film Cowspiracy , I was amazed to hear about the water wastage in animal agriculture -- and then at a Climate Vegan teach-in I learned that eating one meat burger is the same as showering for 2 months !
