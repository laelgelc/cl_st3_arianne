<p> The President of Friends of Rivers and Water Bodies FRWB , Nana Kwabena Dwomoh-Sarpong , has appealed to Ghanaians to desist from building on waterways to avoid the perennial flooding with the slightest downpour . <p> He also advised persons living in low-lying areas to voluntarily move to uplands and stop the indiscriminate dumping of refuse which eventually choke gutters and cause flooding . <p> Nana Kwabena Dwomoh-Sarpong in an interview with UTV warned Ghanaians about climate change and the need to be cautious of unexpected rainfalls . <p> " Climate change is real and we might experience continues rainfall so we must protect the water body . We as citizens must desist from building on waterways to avoid flooding , " he said . <p> He also urged developers to " stop gutter engineering and adopt ecological engineering to fight the climate change . " <p> Nana Kwabena Dwomoh-Sarpong also called on government to equip the local government in the fight against flooding . <p> According to him , the impact of flooding can cause the nation thousands of cedis if the situation is not solved .
