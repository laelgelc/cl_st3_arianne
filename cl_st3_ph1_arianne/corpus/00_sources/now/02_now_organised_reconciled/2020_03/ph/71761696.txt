<h> Valuing Water through the Years <p> QUEZON CITY Mar. 3 -- The World Water Day is celebrated worldwide on March 22 of every year in consonance with the UN General Assembly Resolution A/RES/47/193 dated December 22 , 1992 and Administrative Order No. 258 dated March 4 , 1996 signed by then President Fidel V. Ramos . <p> The Philippine Government , as signatory to the Agenda 21 of the United Nations Conference on Environment and Development ( UNCED ) , has formulated and is vigorously advocating the Philippine Strategy for Sustainable Development ( PSSD ) which includes emphasis on water as a limited resource . Pursuant to these commitments and in response to the alarming degradation of the status of the country 's water resources , the government has initiated a series of intensive nationwide multi-sectoral consultations culminating in the Water Summit in December 1994 which was immediately followed by the enactment of RA 8041 , also known as the Water Crisis Act , in June 1995 . The nationwide observance of Water Conservation Year was started on 22 March 1996 . <p> World Water Day celebrates water and raises awareness of the 2.2 billion people living without @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to tackle the global water crisis . A core focus of World Water Day is to support the achievement of Sustainable Development Goal 6 : water and sanitation for all by 2030 . <p> This year 's celebration is about water and climate change -- and how the two are inextricably linked . The campaign shows how our use of water will help reduce floods , droughts , scarcity and pollution , and will help fight climate change itself . By adapting to the water effects of climate change , we will protect health and save lives . And , by using water more efficiently , we will reduce greenhouse gases . <p> With the support from our partner agencies and institutions , the annual celebration of World Water Day in the past years has been fruitful and promising through the conduct of various educational and awareness campaign activities . We hope that in the succeeding years , more organizations will be engaged in such activities and help us increase the awareness of giving importance to water . Let us value water as we value our life .
