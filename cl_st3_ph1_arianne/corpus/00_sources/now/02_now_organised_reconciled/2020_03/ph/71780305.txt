<h> DENR appoints new undersecretary for mining <p> The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) has appointed a new undersecretary for mining , which came weeks after the major reshuffle among officials of the agency that Environment Secretary Roy Cimatu ordered shortly before 2019 ended . <p> In a text exchange , Environment Undersecretary Analiza Rebuelta-Teh confirmed that she is no longer the DENR 's undersecretary for mining concerns and that the job has already been transferred to Environment Undersecretary Juan Miguel Cuna . <p> Cuna now serves as the DENR 's undersecretary for policy , planning and international affairs as well as the supervising undersecretary for Environmental Management Bureau ( EMB ) and Mines and Geosciences Bureau ( MGB ) . <p> Teh , on the other hand , gets keep her old position as the DENR 's undersecretary for climate change . This development came weeks after Cimatu announced a major reshuffle among key executives of the DENR .
