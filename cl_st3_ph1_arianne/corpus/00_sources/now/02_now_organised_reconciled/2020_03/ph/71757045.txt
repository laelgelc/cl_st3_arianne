<h> Agri NorMin explores resilient crops to adapt to climate change <p> CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY , March 2 - The Department of Agriculture - Regional Field Office ( DA-RFO ) -10 held a training on capacity enhancement and mainstreaming of climate change in increasing the capability of some DA-RFO 10 staff and its attached agencies to use technologies and practices for climate change adaptation and mitigation . <p> The three-day activity starting Feb. 26-28 , 2020 focused on climate change risk and its mitigation strategies in the city to include updating of technologies and practices for climate change adaptation and mitigation . <p> According to Lapoot , adaptation practices for crops is through the use of resistant or tolerant varieties , the use of nextGen varieties for saline , rainfed , lowland irrigated inbreed and hybrid areas , among others . <p> He further cited the adaptation practices through the use of early maturing varieties , relay or intercropping . <p> Importantly , he encouraged the participants to explore other climate resilient crops like Adlai , and the use of zero tillage . <p> Aside from crops , livestock adaptation practices is also important along with the use @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ source of feeds . <p> Aside from that , it is important to take note on the production of buffer stock seeds and planting materials . <p> On the other hand , weather forecasters of PAG-ASA shared on localized climate services and rainfall forecasting . <p> Noting on DA 's Adaptation and Mitigation Initiative in Agriculture ( AMIA ) Program enables the agri-fishery sector to adapt to the adverse effects of climate change and build climate-resilient communities and livelihood . <p> Accordingly , through climate resiliency and vulnerability assessment ( CRVA ) , communities in the country which are most vulnerable to climate change will be identified . <p> As such , it will ensure that strategic and effective investments can be made .
