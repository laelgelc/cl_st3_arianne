<h> CCC calls on all sectors to ' climate-proof PH 's water systems ' <p> In celebration of the World Water Day , the Climate Change Commission ( CCC ) on Sunday called on all sectors to unite and mainstream climate change adaptation strategies for the country 's water systems . <p> Citing the United Nations-led Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change ( IPCC ) , CCC Secretary Emmanuel De Guzman said climate change was exacerbating water shortages from increasing and competing demand and deteriorating water supplies , both in terms of quality and quantity . <p> In a series of special reports , the IPCC highlighted that climate emergency places water resources under an unprecedented threat . <p> De Guzman pointed out that , just in 2019 , more than six million Filipinos were affected by water shortages in Metro Manila , which resulted in the disruption of social and economic activities in the metropolis . <p> " This is what the future holds should we fail to deliver adaptation interventions that will ensure water security amid the changing climate , " De Guzman said . <p> CCC is calling for more efficient and united pathways for " climate-proofing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ us review our current water governance structure , fast-track reforms for the sustainable use and management of our water resources , and ensure access to water and sanitation for all Filipinos , " De Guzman said . <p> World Water Day , which is celebrated March 22 every year , is about focusing attention on the importance of water . <p> This year 's theme , " Water and Climate Change , " explores how water and climate change are inextricably linked .
