<h> Oplan Tubig para sa Kinabukasan : ' Anong gagawin mo ? ' <p> Water supply and the scarcity of it has been in the headlines lately not only in MetroManila but also in Cavite and Rizal . With Angat Dam as the metro 's sole water sourceand with a growing number of consumers , the shortage has been deeply felt byresidents throughout the past year . <p> Indeed , as population grows , water demand increases and the absence of a secondarymajor water source intensifies the need for a campaign to ensure that we have enoughwater supply .
