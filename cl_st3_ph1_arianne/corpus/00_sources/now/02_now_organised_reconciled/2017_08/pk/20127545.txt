<h> Perils of deforestation <p> Sir : There are umpteen benefits of trees , and the trees , that we see today , are the product of the seeds that someone planted couple of years ago . Trees play an important role in moderating the climate of any country . Scarcity of trees poses great threat to climate of any country . <p> And we know that climate does not respect national borders , which makes it global challenge . International standard suggests that forestation must be 25 percent of the land of any country . But unfortunately in our country , the total forest area is only 3 percent of the total land . According to some media reports , deforestation of Pakistan is 2.4 per cent per annum , the highest in the Asia . This breakneck speed of deforestation in the country is leading to drastic changes in the region 's weather systems , land and marine biodiversity . <p> To top it all , some unpalatable truths were discovered by Ministry of Climate Change , which says that 1.6 billion tons of CO2 is caused by deforestation , mainly the cutting and burning of forests . <p> To conclude , it is high time we realized the importance of planting trees , as it not only helps in curbing the menace of climate change in the country but also helps in reduction of air and noise pollution . Here an adage goes well , " The best time to plant a tree was 20 years ago . The second best time is now "
