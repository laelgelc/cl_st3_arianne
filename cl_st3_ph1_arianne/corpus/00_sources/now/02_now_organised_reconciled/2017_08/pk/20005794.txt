<h> Websites of various govt ministries hacked <p> Various government websites were hacked on Monday by Indian hackers and were defaced with pro-India messages . <p> The websites of the Ministry of Defence , Ministry of Climate Change , Ministry of Inter-Provincial Coordination and others were hacked by a group calling themselves " LulzSec India " . <p> The hackers left messages praising India and its armed forces on the websites . <p> There has been no comment from the government on the matter and the websites remain defaced till they were rendered inaccessible . <p> Hackers from both sides of the border have hacked and defaced government websites on a number of occasions . In early January , a government website run by the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa was hacked by Indian hackers.
