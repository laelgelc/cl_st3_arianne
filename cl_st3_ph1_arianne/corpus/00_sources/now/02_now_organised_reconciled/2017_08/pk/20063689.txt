<h> add The News to homescreen <h> Business <h> Share <h> All institutions tasked to materialise ' Vision 2025 ' <p> ISLAMABAD : Ahsan Iqbal , minister for interior , on Thursday directed all the institutions to play their role in achieving objectives set forth by Pakistan Vision 2025 . <p> " Pakistan Vision 2025 sets the groundwork to launch the country on a fast track of development with the ultimate goal of transforming it to become one of top twenty economies by 2030 , " said the minister keynoting the three-day ' Pakistan Development Summit and Expo . ' <p> " We need to forge a path to in an effort to achieve goals set in Vision 2025 . " <p> Iqbal said Pakistan 's biggest problem was political instability and shaky democracy because of which it was lagging behind countries like South Korea , Malaysia and Indonesia . " South Korea copied economic plans of Pakistan and see where it has reached now , " he added . <p> " Every country became developed with political stability and steady democratic process but unfortunately , whenever we embarked on the path of sustained democracy , the process was derailed , " Iqbal lamented . <p> The summit attended by top economists , academicians , politicians , diplomats and youth discussed the hardships faced by Pakistan in the past and the opportunities that were before it to attain higher economic growth and prosperity . <p> Sartaj Aziz , deputy chairman planning commission , in his speech , told audience the summit was an opportunity to review the performance of Pakistan and deliberate how to become one of the top 25 economies of the world . <p> " Pakistan hasbattled the menace of terrorism and braved other natural disasters in the past . However challenges lie ahead as Pakistan was considered the 7th most vulnerable country to climate change , " said Aziz . <p> The planning commission official said that the nation has to work together to fulfill this dream . Later the speakers discussed the strategies to make Pakistan economically strong and stable . One of the highlights of the event was a documentary that showcased milestones that Pakistan achieved in 70 years . -- APP
