<p> LAHORE : The Pakistan Chemical Manufacturers Association ( PCMA ) is going to adopt nine new international conventions for ensuring safe production and carriage of chemicals as per global standards set by the United Nations Organisation ( UNO ) . <p> The conventions will be implemented with the help of the Ministry of Climate Change . Iftikharul Hassan , Joint Secretary of the ministry , said that the protocols had been adopted by all developed nations and by implementing them , Pakistan 's chemical industry would be able to expand its business in the international market . <p> He assured the industry of complete assistance from the ministry to accomplish the task of complying with the conventions . <p> " Chemical manufacturers will also be made aware of the best practices available across the world with regard to implementation of the given protocols , " he added . <p> Earlier , PCMA Secretary General Iqbal Kidwai welcomed the ministry officials and informed them that members of the PCMA were aware of the national and international safety standards . <p> " All protection standards have been implemented for safety of the workers at factories and for safety of the people during delivery , speedy compliance is ensured with the global standards formulated by the World Federation of Chemical Associations under Responsible Care Programme , " he said . <p> He revealed that they were sensitising stakeholders on ambitious projects eg , the Naphtha Cracker Complex , which need government support .
