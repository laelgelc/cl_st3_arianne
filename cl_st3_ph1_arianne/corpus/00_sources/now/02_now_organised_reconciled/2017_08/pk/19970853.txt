<h> add The News to homescreen <h> National <h> Share <h> KP businessmen want Punjab afforestation project replicated <p> PESHAWAR : Traders and business community of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa have called for the replication of Punjab afforestation project in the province to promote forestry investment . <p> Sarhad Chamber of Commerce and Industry ( SCCI ) president Muhammad Afzal on Friday requested Chief Minister Pervez Khattak to replicate the project so that forest cover could be improved in the province besides promotion of forestry investment . <p> He expressed these views while speaking after presentation of South Punjab Forest Company ( SPFC ) at the chamber . <p> SCCI Senior Vice President , Muhammad Iqbal , Vice President Abid Khan leading industrialists including former president SCCI , Syed Zulfiqar Ali , and others were present on the occasion . <p> Director Project Operations of the SPFC , Hafiz Muhammad Arsalan , made the presentation . <p> He apprised the participants about the objectives of the projects and incentives offered by the Punjab government for investment in forestry sector of the province . <p> The company , Muhammad Arsalan added , has been formed for afforestation across six districts of south Punjab under the public-private partnership . <p> A total of 99,077 acres of land in the form of 187 forest lots of various sizes in Bahawalpur , Rahim Yar Khan , Rajanpur , Muzaffargarh and Dera Ghazi Khan are being offered to the investors for 15 years so that commercial forestry and range management activities can be done . <p> The project has ecological and economic benefits as it will help to sequester carbon emissions , adapt and mitigate climate change , provide livelihood opportunities to local communities and most importantly sustain the wood-based industry . It will help to save the remaining forests of Pakistan . <p> Hafiz Arsalan assured the businessmen that interest of investors have been ensured through the act of parliament , Punjab Forest Act and Public-Private Partnership Act 2014.
