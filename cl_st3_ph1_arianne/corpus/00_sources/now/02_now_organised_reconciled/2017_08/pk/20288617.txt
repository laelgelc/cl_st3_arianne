<h> add The News to homescreen <h> Newspost <p> August 31 , 2017 <h> Share <p> Advertisement <h> Wildlife in danger <p> The human-caused climate change has now become a major threat to the survival of the wildlife and its habitats . Unabated deforestation and the conversion of forest land into industrial areas are affecting the wildlife . Pakistan is home to a large number of species . The future of these animals is swaying in uncertainty because of changing climate conditions . It is heartening to note that the present government is carrying out necessary steps for the protection of the wildlife from both the climate and human-related threats . <p> The first step taken by the government in this regard was to launch the Rs10 billion Green Pakistan Programme . The programme is being implemented in all provinces in collaboration with the provincial forest and wildlife departments . Still there is a dire need to work more to deal with the increasing environmental challenges .
