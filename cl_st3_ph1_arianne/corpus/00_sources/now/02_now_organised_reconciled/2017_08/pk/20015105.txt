<h> Trudeau felicitates Pakistanis on Independence Day <p> Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau felicitated Pakistanis in Canada and around the world as the community celebrated Pakistan 's 70th Independence anniversary with zeal and fervour . <p> " Today , we join Pakistani communities in Canada and around the world to celebrate seven decades of Pakistan 's independence , " Trudeau said in a statement issued on Independence Day . <p> " Our two countries established a diplomatic relationship shortly after Pakistan was declared a sovereign nation in 1947 . We continue to work together to advance shared priorities , including governance and democracy , regional security , poverty reduction , polio eradication , trade , defence , and the fight against climate change . <p> " The people-to-people connections between Canada and Pakistan are older still . People from what is now Pakistan first migrated to Canada well over a century ago and , since then , our connections have continued to grow . <p> " As we mark the 150th anniversary of Confederation in 2017 , we honour the many cultures and communities that make Canada such a wonderful place to live . Pakistani-Canadians continue to make major contributions to our country , and help make Canada the prosperous , vibrant , and open place it is today . <p> " On behalf of the Government of Canada , Sophie and I offer our best wishes to all those celebrating Pakistan 's Independence Day . "
