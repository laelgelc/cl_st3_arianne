<h> GT Road rally bigger than any Bhutto , Benazir rally : Mushahidullah <p> By <p> GEO NEWS <p> Sunday Aug 13 , 2017 <p> Mushahidullah Khan talks to media in Lahore <p> LAHORE : Minister for Climate Change Mushahidullah Khan on Sunday claimed that the four-day GT Road rally held by Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz ( PML-N ) was bigger than any gathering held by the country 's popular leaders , including Zulfikar Ali Bhutto and Benazir Bhutto . <p> Addressing media here , the PML-N leader said that Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf chairman Imran Khan and Pakistan Awami Tehreek chief Tahirul Qadri ca n't fathom the participation of masses in the rally and are worried . <p> Khan also said that both PML-N chief Nawaz Sharif and Senate Chairman Raza Rabbani have emphasised on the need to have democracy in the country , adding that a democratic setup guarantees respect for all institutions , including army and judiciary . <p> PML-N led by Nawaz Sharif held a four-day " homecoming " rally from Islamabad to Lahore , in what was being dubbed as a " power show " after Nawaz was disqualified from his seat as the prime minister .
