<h> Dutch students grow their own biodegradable car <p> The structure of the car they have called Lina has a similar strength-weight ratio to that of fibreglass and weighs only 310 kg ( about 684 pounds ) . <p> EINDHOVEN : What 's made of sugar , can carry four people and travel at 50 miles ( 80 km ) per hour ? A biodegradable car , whose inventors say could be the next step in environmentally friendly motoring . <p> The lightweight electric car , created by students in the Netherlands , is made of a resin derived from sugar beets and covered with sheets of Dutch-grown flax . <p> " Only the wheels and suspension systems are not yet of bio-based materials , " said Yanic van Riel , one of the developers from the TU/Ecomotive team at the Eindhoven University of Technology . <p> The structure of the car they have called Lina has a similar strength-weight ratio to that of fibreglass and weighs only 310 kg ( about 684 pounds ) . <p> But the prototype has not yet passed crash tests , because the material " will not bend like metal , but break " , said the team 's leader Noud van de Gevel . <p> Demands to reduce air pollution and tackle climate change have pushed auto companies towards alternative designs , but most are still require a great deal of energy to make . <p> " Energy that is saved while driving the car is now spent during the production phase , " van de Gevel said . <p> The TU/Ecomotive team plans to test drive Lina later this year , once given the green light by the Netherlands Vehicle Authority . <h> Comments <p> Readers are required to have a valid Facebook account to comment on this story .
