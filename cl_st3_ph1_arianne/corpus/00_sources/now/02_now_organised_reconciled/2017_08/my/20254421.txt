<h> Sustainable engineering <p> THE message from the World Economic Forum earlier this year was loud and clear ; the fourth industrial revolution is upon us , and it is about to unleash wholesale changes like we have never experienced before . <p> Driverless cars and the increased role of robots in society are just part of the changes that we would be ushering in . <p> However , the dawn of the fourth industrial revolution has to strictly comply with other initiatives that are being pursued now -- such as sustainability -- in line with climate change agreements . <p> The challenge of balancing the onslaught of these advancements in technology has to be juggled with proper management of energy that ensures sustainable growth while ensuring profitability . <p> As such the roles of engineers are also expected to evolve at an exponential rate . It was with all these in mind that Monash University Malaysia 's Masters in Advanced Engineering ( MAE ) was crafted . <p> Associate Prof Dr Hung Yew Mun who is the coordinator of the MAE programme accentuated the benefits of taking up this course . <p> " Sustainability is still at its infancy stage in Malaysia . The government encourages the adoption of the ISO 50001 framework to improve the energy management of organisations , " he said . <p> " The target is to have at least 100 companies become ISO 50001 certified companies in Malaysia by the end of this year . The requirement is for businesses to establish and implement sustainable energy management systems , " he added . <p> As is the case with any new initiative , the lack of qualified manpower in the field to execute these goals are always a problem . <p> " We have a lack of expertise in areas related to sustainability in Malaysia . This is a genuine opportunity for graduates , as there is a bright future in this field , " said Hung . <p> The programme consists of two core units , four specialised units , and two elective units to develop students in the area of energy and sustainability with the ability to develop solutions for complex engineering problems . <p> Students will be given guidance on engineering entrepreneurship , where they will learn how to construct a business plan that balances technicality with soft skills . <p> " The goal of this unit is to impart an evidence-based methodology for those who wish to build start-ups or start their own businesses . However , the knowledge is also applicable for them to apply when working for organisations , " added Hung . <p> As a programme that includes the participation of teaching staff from different disciplines of engineering , MAE students will have an opportunity to get personal guidance from lecturers in an intellectually stimulating environment , pushing students out of their comfort zones to come up with original ideas . <p> Students will have a taste of real life working experience as Monash has collaborations with countless organisations that will provide industry engagement . Among the companies collaborating with Monash are Intel , Gamuda , Petronas , Maxis , Western Digital , Shell , Cargill , Sunway Construction , Favelle Favco , ABB , CCM Pharmaceuticals , and Bacfree . <p> The MAE programme is targeted towards engineering graduates with Bachelor of Engineering degrees from recognised tertiary institutions , with a minimum average grade of 70% , or the equivalent of a second class upper grade . <p> This one-year programme consists of two semesters , with four subjects in each semester . <p> The duration of the programme for part-time students is two years , with two subjects in each semester.
