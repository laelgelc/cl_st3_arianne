<h> Search for 8 missing in Alps called off : Swiss police <p> GENEVA : The search for eight hikers missing in the Swiss Alps has been called off , police announced Saturday , three days after they were lost in a massive landslide . <p> " We have done everything possible to find them , but ( police ) rescue official Andrea Mittner has announced this afternoon that the search has now been abandoned , " Sandra Scianguetta , police spokeswoman for the eastern canton of Grisons , told AFP . <p> " We will not now be able to find anyone , " Mittner was cited by Swiss media as saying . <p> The eight missing -- four Germans , two Austrians and two Swiss -- were hiking Wednesday on the Piz Cengalo mountain , near the Italian border , when the landslide struck , sending mud , rocks and dirt cascading down the mountain . <p> About 100 people were evacuated from the border village of Bondo , which was hit by the debris . <p> About 120 emergency workers equipped with infrared cameras and mobile phone detectors , helicopters and rescue dogs had been scouring a five-square-kilometre ( 1.9-square-mile ) area of the disaster . <p> Dramatic footage from Wednesday showed an entire mountainside disintegrating , unleashing a mass of thick mud and sludge that tore up trees and demolished at least one building . <p> The landslide was so severe that the vibrations set off seismometers across Switzerland , measuring the equivalent of a 3.0-magnitude earthquake , according to the Swiss Seismological Service . <p> Experts suggested that climate change could be partially to blame for the disaster , with melting permafrost and an adjacent glacier likely destabilising the landmass . <p> There could be more landslides in the area , local geologist Andreas Huwiler warned . -- AFP
