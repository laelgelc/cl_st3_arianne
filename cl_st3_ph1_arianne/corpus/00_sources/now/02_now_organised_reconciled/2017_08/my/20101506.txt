<h> 40,000 Perlis farmers appeal for RM300 monthly allowance <p> ARAU : About 40,000 farmers in Perlis have appealed to Prime Minister Datuk Seri Najib Razak to provide them with RM300 monthly allowance similar to that allocated to fishermen . <p> Perlis Farmers ' Organisation Authority chairman Datuk Tengku Aziz Tengku Jaafar said the living standards of farmers were almost similar to the fishermen as their income remained unchanged , apart from being severely affected by climate change . <p> " Similar to fishermen who are unable to go out to sea to catch fish during the monsoon season or during strong winds , farmers are unable to cultivate rice during severe droughts , " he told Bernama here today . <p> Tengku Aziz said excessive rain could also cause flooding , thereby damaging the crops . <p> " At every general meeting of the area farmers ' organisation , farmers raise the issue of a monthly allowance , " he said , adding that the state farmers requested RM300 monthly allowance to help cover their daily expenses . <p> " If the government says that farmers are not eligible for subsistence allowance as they already received subsidies in the form of fertiliser , pesticides and rice seeds , fishermen have also received subsidies to buy trawl nets , diesel and petrol as well as boats . " <p> He said it was only fair for the government to give due consideration to the farmers ' appeal as the majority of them depended on a less than a hectare of rice field for their livelihood . -- Bernama
