<h> Filipina shines in Hong Kong conference <p> A young Filipina social media marketer and peace advocate represented the Philippines in a prestigious five-day international youth leaders forum in Hong Kong . <p> Arizza Nocum , 22 , of Kristiyano-Islam Peace or Kris Library , was invited by Hong Kong Federation of Youth Groups to share her knowledge and experiences with local young leaders in the " Leaders to Leaders 2017 Summer " program on Aug. 1 to 5 . <p> Nocum , who was asked to discuss the issues of poverty and education to more than 1,000 students and working youths , is one of the five eminent global leaders from different countries picked by HKFYG , the city 's largest youth service organization . <p> The other speakers from Singapore , Hong Kong and Jordan talked about climate change , women empowerment , public speaking and social investments . <p> Kris Library head Arizza Nocum <p> " I am both proud and humbled to be invited here to discuss the intertwined issue of poverty and education , " Nocum said , citing the impact of poverty to the global problem of lack of access to quality education among the youth . <p> " It would be an honor and a great achievement for us to impart to the young people of Hong Kong our works and experiences in the KRIS Library . Together , we can make this world a better place for everyone , " she said . <p> Born to a Catholic father and a Tausug mother , Nocum is the overall head of Kris Library , a non-profit organization that aims to promote peace through education by building libraries , providing scholarships , and distributing educational materials in areas affected by poverty and conflict . <p> Thus far , Kris has built six libraries , provided more than 400 scholarships and spread more than 50,000 books and computers . <p> A magna cum laude graduate of UP Diliman College of Engineering , Nocum also serves as a junior partner and head of the social media arm of the public relations firm Dean &amp; Kings Communications run by her father , former Philippine Daily Inquirer journalist Armand Dean Nocum . <p> The young Nocum is also active in the international campaign to fight violent extremism ; she represented the Philippines in Geneva last year as one of the 10 young leaders selected from different countries to serve as peace ambassadors of the Kofi Annan Foundation . <p> The organization was founded by former United NationsSecretary General Kofi Annan in 2007 to promote better global governance and strengthen the capacities of people and countries to achieve peace . <p> Nocum was selected as one of Ten Outstanding Students of the Philippines in February 2017 . <p> She was also named the youth awardee during the 2017 Inspiring Filipina Entrepreneurs Awards organized by " Go Negosyo " , the advocacy of the non-profit organization Philippine Center for Entrepreneurship , in March . <p> Presidential adviser for entrepreneurship and Asean Business Advisory Council chair Joey Concepcion cited Nocum " for championing the advocacy of erasing animosity between the Catholics and Muslims through education , " especially in Mindanao . <h> Related stories : <h> More from this Category : <p> COMMENT DISCLAIMER : Reader comments posted on this Web site are not in any way endorsed by Manila Standard .
