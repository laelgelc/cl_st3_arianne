People need to realize that the oceans are endangered and we need to protect them , " Philippine Freediving Ambassador Guillaume N ? ry said . <p> N ? ry is a staunch supporter of Campagne des Ecos Gestes and Antinea Foundation , which specialize in ocean conservation . <p> Among the highlights of the month-long Philippine freediving expedition of N ? ry was his stopover at El Nido , Palawan for the Sulubaa ? Environmental Foundation Pangatalan Island project , a 4.2-hectare marine protected area surrounded by coral reefs , mangroves and seagrass meadows . <p> " It 's not just about consuming the place . It 's also about having an experience , of protecting and respecting every place we go to , " he said . <p> " We at the department are conscious of the need for sustainable practices and programs as we develop the freediving segment .
