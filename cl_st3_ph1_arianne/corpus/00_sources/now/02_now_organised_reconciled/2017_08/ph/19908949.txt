<h> DOE pushes for widening of energy base at the Asia Cooperation Dialogue <p> ENERGY Secretary Alfonso G. Cusi called for the widening of the energy base with efficient and reliable power sources at the Asia Cooperation Dialogue ( ACD ) event in Panglao Island , Bohol . <p> The Department of Energy ( DOE ) hosts this year 's ACD meet in Bohol from August 7 to 10 . <p> In his keynote address at the opening of the three-day ACD on Monday , Cusi said the strategy can help nations secure their energy needs while addressing the challenges of climate change and energy affordability . <p> " We have a diversified energy mix , with nearly 24 percent of our power being sourced from renewable sources . And this diversified mix has proven to be competitive and sustainable , " Cusi said . <p> To achieve security and sustainability requires a three-pronged approach driven by three I 's : incentives , investments and innovation , Cusi said . <p> " Governments must be willing to provide the necessary support for players who want to bring fresh energy solutions and , thus , make electricity rates affordable to consumers , " Cusi said . " All players must be willing to invest in new technologies that not only increases reliability , but reduces emissions . " <p> " Innovation is the key driver that we need in order to achieve security and sustainability . Governments must develop a regulatory environment that supports our innovators and researchers , " he added . <p> With the three I 's approach , Cusi said , " Asia can produce its own visionary problem solvers . We must be resilient and stay the course . " <p> Meanwhile , the energy chief shared the country 's solution to the hurdles faced by energy investors in Asia in the form of red tape and long waiting times . <p> As a solution , President Duterte signed Executive Order ( EO ) 30 that classifies energy projects amounting to at least $70 million as projects of national significance , Cusi said . EO 30 also streamlines the permitting process of all government agencies under the Energy Investment Coordinating Council ( EICC ) . <p> Under the EO , concerned government agencies must act on a proposal within 30 days . Otherwise , the proposal is deemed approved . <p> " Speed is of the essence here and we are determined to see that we pull in the best investments and build our projects on time to deliver on our domestic plan , " Cusi said . " But it is not just enough to focus internally . It is also important to promote closer cooperation among our countries . Our diversity is our strength . " <p> The ACD will encourage member-countries to develop the mechanisms to move forward with an Energy Plan of Action to pave way for initiatives on the nexus of critical resources , such as food , energy and water . <p> To be more resilient , Cusi said , " I believe future initiatives should be focused on technological advancement and improved management practices must also be implemented . We need future-proof policies that would allow us to cope with natural and human-induced disasters . " <p> The secretary concluded by showcasing the DOE 's consumer-focused energy campaign E-Power Mo ! or the consumers ' " Power of Choice " .
