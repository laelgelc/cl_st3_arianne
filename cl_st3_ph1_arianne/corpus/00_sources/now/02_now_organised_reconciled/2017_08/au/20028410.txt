<h> ' Ignoring climate change a missed opportunity ' <p> The Tasmanian Greens have criticised the report 's findings and recommendations . <p> Spokeswoman Andrea Dawkins said it was clear the TVPS was essential to the state 's energy security <p> However , she said the Greens were disappointed the report did not address long-term energy security issues posed by climate change . <p> " There 's no mention of climate disruption in this report -- the single biggest threat for long term security for energy for Tasmanians , " she said . <p> " We are also concerned about the Liberals ' dividend policy with regarding the GBEs . <p> " There was no discussion of that policy within this report either , and again a missed opportunity . " <h> ' Hydro Tasmania has listened and learned ' <p> Energy Minister Matthew Groom welcomed the report and said early indications were that it validated and supported the Government 's strong actions both during and after the energy challenges . <p> Mr Groom said Tasmania 's current energy supply position continued to be very sound when compared to the uncertainty in the national market . <p> He said the Government would carefully consider the report and have more to say in Parliament on Wednesday . <p> Hydro Tasmania said it was focused on a secure and strong future for the business and the state , with CEO Steve Davy stating the company had " learned , listened and responded to help give Tasmanians a secure and confident energy future " . <p> He said Hydro Tasmania had rebuilt storages to secure levels , and the business was rebounding strongly .
