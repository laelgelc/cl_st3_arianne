<h> NZ Greens co-leader resigns ahead of election <p> The co-leader of New Zealand 's Green Party has resigned after it was revealed she once falsified her electoral roll details . <p> Metiria Turei admitted to falsifying her address on an electoral roll 25 years ago so she could vote for her friend running for a joke party in a different electorate . <p> Ms Turei also faced questions about why she had listed her address on the electoral roll as the property her baby 's father lived in . <p> Metiria Turei said she never lived with her baby 's father , instead saying she falsified the electoral roll to vote for a close friend running in the 1993 election . <p> She previously ruled out seeking a ministerial portfolio in any government the Greens are part of , acknowledging the issue had hurt the party . <p> However , she refused to resign . <p> Greens members David Clendon and Kennedy Graham quit the party in protest . <p> ' I knew that by telling my personal story , it would help people hear and understand the reality of poverty , ' Ms Turei said on Friday . <p> ' I also knew that it would open the way for people to criticize me - and I knew the risks of that- but the intensity of those attacks has become too much for my family , and they are now getting in the way of our ability to communicate our solutions - not just for poverty , but for water , climate change and the environment . ' <p> Ms Turei says the decision to resign was her own and not the party . <p> James Shaw will be the sole co-leader for the election . <p> The Green Party will select a new female co-leader at a general meeting .
