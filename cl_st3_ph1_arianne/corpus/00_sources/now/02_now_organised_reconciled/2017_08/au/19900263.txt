<h> Closure of yet another local business <p> LETTER TO THE EDITOR : I was saddened and disturbed to learn of the closure of Lord 's Newsagency . <p> LETTER TO THE EDITOR : <p> I WAS saddened and disturbed to learn of the closure of Lord 's Newsagency in Raymond St , Sale . <p> Another long-serving ( at least 50 years ) and caring small business in a regional centre to join the long list of empty shops in the main streets of our shire 's towns . <p> These small businesses have been the lifeblood of country towns for years , providing friendly " have a chat " services to us and participating in our lifestyle by supporting local community and sporting activities and providing job opportunities for our citizens . <p> ' Mr Big ' retailers have taken over . <p> The costs that small shops have to bear can not be matched by sufficient increase in income . <p> Rates and rent have risen , power and gas prices are soaring and the weekend helpers they have in order to take a little time off from their long working hours are being promised by Mr Shorten to be given back penalty rates . <p> I note that our mayor is indicating council'sdesire to become active on the climate change issue . <p> We have three tiers of government . <p> Clearly state and federal governments have this issue on their plate , as they should . <p> Local government is called ' local ' for a reason . Please concentrate on local issues . <p> These dozens of empty shops and the demise of true local business is a serious local issue . <p> We need the relationships of small business with the community and the jobs and friendships that are thus created . <p> Should we not leave federal and state issues to their appropriate governments and , perhaps , strengthen our council-business group relationships to provide and assist our genuine Aussie battlers -- the hard-working small business owners ? <p> Meanwhile , when shopping down the street , spare a thought for ' Mr Small ' , even if it costs a couple of dollars less at ' Mr Big 's . <p> Help preserve an important part of our community and our lifestyle by buying at our Aussie battlers.
