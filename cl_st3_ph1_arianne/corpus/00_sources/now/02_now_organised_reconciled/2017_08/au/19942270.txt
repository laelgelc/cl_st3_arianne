<h> Rotary and Australian National University bring mini-United Nations to Canberra <p> They may be year 11 students and it may be a mock United Nations but the importance of their draft resolutions and standard of debating reflect the real deal . <p> When 70 students from around Australia gather at Old Parliament House later in August for the Model United Nations Assembly , they will be debating issues from preventing the proliferation of nuclear weapons in the Korean peninsular to tackling piracy in the Horn of Africa . <p> Students at the one of the past Model United Nations Assembly held in Canberra for the past 21 years by the Rotary Club of Canberra Sunrise . Photo : Supplied <p> Addressing climate change , eliminating violence against women and girls and protecting human rights while countering terrorism will also be tossed back and forth for the event from August 18 to 20 . <p> Held by the Rotary Club of Canberra Sunrise since 1996 , the Model United Nations Assembly is something keenly embraced by the students , many of whom wear the national costume of the country they represent . <p> " They are representing that country and its attitude and not their own views so it 's important they do some research and find out what those views are , " Mr Britton said . <p> The Rotary Club of Canberra Sunrise organises the event on behalf of the Rotary District 9710 which covers 47 clubs across the ACT and south-eastern NSW . <p> " It 's also to show that Rotary is not just about sausage sizzles and fundraising , " Mr Britton said . <p> Club member Bruce Harvey , who has been there since the first MUNA in 1996 , said some of the students went on to study international relations and seek careers in diplomacy . <p> " The kids are just amazing , " he said . <p> " Every year we are impressed by the standard of public speaking and the way they are across the issues . It 's pretty remarkable . " <p> The event will be presented on Facebook , including the streaming of much of the debate . The public is also encouraged to come along and hear the debate in the historic House of Representatives chamber at Old Parliament House from Friday , August 18 to Sunday , August 20 . <p> The students will also visit the Australian National University , a major sponsor of the event , and meet diplomats from some the countries they represent at a dinner on the Saturday night at Burgmann College .
