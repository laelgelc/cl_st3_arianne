<h> Finkel key to lower prices : power chiefs <p> Updated : 1:16 pm , Wednesday , 9 August 2017 <p> Power chiefs have warned the prime minister the best solution to reducing power prices is implement the Finkel report recommendations and offered a gentle rebuke to Nationals leader Barnaby Joyce 's call for business to build a low emissions coal-fired power station . <p> Sky News has confirmed the National Party leader urged power company bosses to build a high efficiency low emissions ( HELE ) coal-fired power station during a meeting only to be told it was n't a great idea . <p> High Efficiency Low Emission coal-fired power plants aim to deliver near-zero emissions from coal with carbon capture , use and storage ( CCUS ) . <p> AGL chief executive Andrew Vesey told the deputy prime minister that the reality was it would take more than seven years to build a HELE coal-fired power station and it was hard to get investors interested . Other chief executives backed his remarks . <p> Mr Vesey went on to tell Malcolm Turnbull , Mr Joyce and Treasurer Scott Morrison that the reality was AGL had made a commitment to its investors to get out of coal . <p> The AGL boss said that the power giant regarded climate change as a material threat to its business .
