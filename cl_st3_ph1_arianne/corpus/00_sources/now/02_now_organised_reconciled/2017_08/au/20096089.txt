" <p> There has been a big increase in bookings to Antarctica . Picture : Intrepid TravelSource:Supplied <p> This is a trend which global tour operator Contiki is witnessing too . Young travellers during the 1970s and 80s went on Contiki to see France and Italy . Today they want to see Bolivia and Chile . <p> Demands from Gen Y travellers have forced the tour company to venture into previously unknown territory . <p> She added that " getting lost in the backstreets and genuine authentic experiences with locals " is more important for a fulfilling travel experience . <p> According to the International Association of Antarctica Tour Operators ( IAATO ) , overall tourist numbers to Antarctica have increased by about 30 per cent in the past ten years and the number of Australians visiting Antarctica has increased by 50 per cent . <p> In 2016-17 there were 4,451 Australians , who made up 10 per cent of all travellers to Antarctica . <p> " The effects of climate change are also driving interest in the region , with younger , socially-conscious travellers wanting to experience this part of the world and see this last remaining , untouched frontier first-hand , " Mr Thornton said .
