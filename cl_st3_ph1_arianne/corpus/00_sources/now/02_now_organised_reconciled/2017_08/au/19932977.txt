Vice President Al Gore continues his tireless fight traveling around the world training an army of climate champions and influencing international climate policy . <p> Why you should see it : This film , like its predecessor , is very persuasive and the tenacious Al Gore is as passionate as ever about his cause . Not only does he personalise a complex , global issue , he also balances the doom and gloom with some hope .
