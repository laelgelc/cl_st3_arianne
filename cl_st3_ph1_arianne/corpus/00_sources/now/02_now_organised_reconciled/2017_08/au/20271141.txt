<h> Leveraging the catalyst for housing unaffordability <p> It is understood within pure economics that leveraging has created a false market and a false value for housing . House prices have escalated due to the ability to buy a house with just the equity in another house . The acquisition can be done without any cash . And this has and will continue to perpetuate property prices to increase . The economists that advise financial institutions and governments must have carried out studies and have the knowledge of the effects of leveraging on the economy . In addition , many economic papers have been written explaining the effects . But the financial institutions , financial commentators and governments disregard the outcomes . Why ? ? ? ? It is obvious ; increased revenue . And they have no regard to the social and economic ramifications of their practice . <p> The argument that it is an issue of supply and demand is a farce especially when the price is not right . <p> The practice of leveraging residential property against residential property must stop and be regulated . The family home should not be a speculative asset . Do n't eliminate negative gearing , do n't eliminate the CGT concessions , just eliminate the practice of leveraging and maybe the absurd amounts of foreign investment . <p> Owing a home is not a God given right . At the same time , no one should have the power or the means to create a false market that totally restricts people from buying a property that they can call home . There are many who work hard , very hard , and deserve the opportunity to acquire a home at a reasonable price . And in time it will be these hard working people who will become the majority . And if action is n't taken now , there will be a financial and political revolution in the near future . <p> Since Mr Penn became Telstra CEO on May 1 , 2015 , the share price has fallen 43 per cent against a fall in the All Ordinaries Index of just 1 per cent . It 's an achievement to be sure , but not one shareholders appreciate . <p> The issue is the faux competition that 's created with retailers offering fundamentally the same product and putting all their creativity , energy -- and cost -- into stealing each other 's customers . Industry churn is at a record high , and it comes at a cost . Between 1997 and 2012 , the number of sales people employed by utilities companies in Australia grew 500 per cent from 1000 to 6000 , and clerical and administration staff nearly doubled , increasing by 16,000 . Far from being competition leading to cost reduction , it has added to cost , with no value improvement to consumers . <p> Instead of just making energy companies notify people of price changes , how about removing the incentive to favour new customers and make all customers benefit . In the next round of price justifications , refuse to let energy companies include marketing and sales costs in their cost structures ( and they 're probably counting honeymoon discounts as a marketing cost ) and mandate that a company offers uniform consumer pricing . If they want to be ' the cheapest ' , they have to be cheapest for all their customers , so the pensioner or single mother who does n't want to waste time and effort chasing the best deal gets the same price as their neighbour who does . <h> Creating a better taxation model <p> In relation to income , the older generation is incorrectly taxed . The tax-free threshold is inequitably received by superannuation pension recipients on top of their totally tax-free superannuation pension . As a matter of fairness , the two income streams should be aggregated with non-superannuation pension income , then taxed at the appropriate marginal tax rate . <p> Additionally superannuants inappropriately receive a refund of company tax through their superannuation funds.The Labor Party would be better off pursuing these clear inequity issues rather than naively trying to alter the taxation application of trust income . <p> A hybrid income/wealth inequality issue relates to the payment of age pensions when recipients own their capital gains tax free homes . The pension payment should be in the form of a loan to be repaid from the deceased estate . It is inappropriate for the younger generation who carry education debts and can not afford to purchase a home to pay for age pension payments to the home-owning elderly . With an ageing population , the change from social security pension payments to loans is inevitable . From a budget perspective , the sooner the better . <p> The major area of inequality concern in Australia relates to wealth . It is not appropriate to attack wealth per se . However policies need to be adopted to ensure the financial and intangible benefits of home ownership are widespread . The preferable way to achieve this is to apply land taxes to all residential properties without a tax-free threshold concession or principal residence exemption . Life-time capital gains tax free caps could also be introduced . Home ownership will then become more widespread and placed in a better perspective . As a result , private debt will drop to more appropriate and secure levels .
