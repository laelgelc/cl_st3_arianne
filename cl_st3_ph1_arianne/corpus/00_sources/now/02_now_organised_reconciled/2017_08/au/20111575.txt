<p> MISSY Higgins is delving into aliens and the apocalypse for her new album . <p> The Melbourne singer/songwriter was inspired to change lyrical focus after the birth of son Samuel two years ago . <p> " I 'm reading a lot of climate change literature , " Higgins said . " Having a kid and watching where the world is going with global warming is making my imagination go wild . I 'm thinking about all these end of the world apocalyptic love stories . There 's a song written from the perspective of an alien in the future coming back to earth after it 's become this dead star . That 's the most far out song on the record but I love it . " <p> Missy Higgins is playing at A Weekend in the Gardens . Picture : Cybele **25;731;TOOLONG <p> " I 'm writing about when pushed to the limit what aspect of our humanity comes out . I was having these nightmares if Sammy was stranded on an embankment and I was being swept down the river , I still have them , it 's all the anxieties that come with parenthood . And when you start reading the news that intensifies them . " <p> Higgins is in the final stretch of finishing the record , which also moves into different musical directions . Collaborators include Pip Norman ( who has produced a lot of the album ) as well as Butterfly Boucher , who worked on Higgins ' last original album The Ol ' Razzle Dazzle . <p> " It 's at the eleventh hour , " Higgins says of what will be her fifth album and first since 2014 covers album Oz . <p> " It 's pretty different . Or different enough . I 've worked with producers who do less organic stuff and more programming and beats . I have n't gone completely down the electronic road but it 's definitely inspired by the modern electronic pop sound , but with my acoustic influences over the top . " <p> Higgins has joined the line up for the second line-up for A Weekend in the Gardens three day event , which was launched earlier this year and takes place in the Botanic Gardens . <p> Fresh from scoring his first No. 1 album Paul Kelly will play Friday November 17 ( his only Melbourne show for this tour ) with Steve Earle and Middle Kids rapper Illy plays Saturday November 17 with Thundamentals , Spit Syndicate and Birdz while Higgins headlines Sunday November 18 and has hand-picked her guests Dustin Tebbutt , All Our Exes Live in Texas and Harry Jakamarra . <p> " Dustin 's part of the family now , Harry is such a great entertainer and I 've never seen All Our Exes so this is my chance to finally catch them ! " <p> The hometown show and relaxed all ages vibe means her son may get to watch mum at work again . <p> " He usually sees me at soundchecks more than concerts because of the times I 'm on . He saw me play one time and he was on his dad 's shoulders yelling out ' Mummy ! ' I was trying to sing All For Believing on piano , it was so cute , it was very hard to stay serious . " <p> Higgins has also introduced her son to her beloved Broome -- where she 's been based for a holiday and a handful of shows . <p> " Sam is a total free range baby when we come here , we let him run in the bush and climb on the rocks and get matted hair and covered in dirt . He loves it . <p> The family are about to head to LA for the US premiere of Grey Nomad , the play written by Higgins ' husband Dan Lee . <p> " I 've been going on a lot of grey nomad forums , " Higgins said . " You 'd be surprised how many there are . " <p> Higgins has also embarked on her first musical theatre role in Miracle City . <p> The musical opens at the Sydney Opera House in October and is expected to head to other states . <p> " I 've always wanted to do a musical , ever since I was 14 , " Higgins said . <p> " It 's not a huge role , but it 's the right amount for me . I do n't have to carry the whole musical , it 's just enough to figure out if I 'm any good at it and if it 's something I want to do more of . " <p> All A Weekend in the Gardens tickets go on sale next Monday from Ticketmaster.
