<h> Melting of Greenland glacier to speed up : study <p> Miami ( AFP ) - The Greenland ice sheet is likely to melt faster in the years to come , despite a recent slowdown , researchers said Wednesday after making a new discovery about the way glaciers move . <p> Greenland contains enough ice to make the sea level mount 23 feet ( seven meters ) if it were to melt completely . <p> The findings in the journal Science Advances show that ice in Greenland moves more quickly over sediment than hard rock . <p> That means that as the world warms due to climate change , ice flow will likely speed up because the sediment beneath Greenland will become weaker , wetter and more slippery . <p> " Greenland 's margin has many outlet glaciers that act as fast conveyor belts of ice . Thousands of surface lakes act as taps that deliver meltwater to the ice base , turning it into a slippery bathtub , " said lead researcher Bernd Kulessa of the College of Science at Swansea University . <p> " This discovery leaves us concerned because we have so far accepted the exact opposite ? - that Greenland 's icy conveyor would slow down . " <p> Researchers based their findings on seismic surveys that send acoustic waves through the ice to gain insights on the sediment underneath . <p> A previous theory held that glacier melt would slow down at the end of the summer " because large networks of channels drain water away at the base , increasing frictional resistance to ice flow , " said the report . <p> But the seismic surveys showed that sediment played the key role in controlling ice sheet flow , and weakened subglacial sediment " leads to accelerated ice flow . " <p> Ice is melting across the Arctic , as the polar region warms about twice as fast as the rest of the planet .
