<h> ANU poll finds majority of Australians think science has made life easier <p> Ninety per cent of Australians think science has made life easier but nearly half of us feel that change has come too quickly . <p> The ANU was commissioned by the federal government to survey 1203 Australians about their views on science and science-related matters such as vaccinations , genetically modified foods , fracking and climate change . <h> AM &amp; PM Update Newsletter <p> Eighty per cent of respondents believed there was solid evidence the world has been warming the last few decades , with more than half of these people saying this was due to human activity , compared with a US equivalent of 46 per cent of adults . <p> Seven out of ten Australians were opposed to fracking according to the findings . <p> The proportion of Americans who favour fracking outnumber Australians by nearly three to one ( 15.7 per cent vs. 39 per cent ) . <p> The survey also asked how regularly working people used science , technology , maths and engineering skills . <p> More than 90 per cent used technology skills at least a few times per week , 80 per cent reported using maths skills as regularly , 50 per cent used science skills and 40 per cent used engineering skills .
