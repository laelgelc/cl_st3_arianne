<p> Senior scientist at the Council for Scientific and Industrial Research ( CSIR ) -Water Resources Institute , Accra , Dr Anthony A Duah , has commended President Nana Akufo-Addo for effectively tackling illegal mining , also known as ' galamsey ' in the country . <p> " This is action is hard to believe because politicians since independence are interested only in protecting their votes rather than embarking on a journey which promotes national development , " he remarked . <p> Speaking at a public lecture by Knutsford University College in Accra on the theme , ' KAITHAROS Technology : An Innovative Clean Water Filtration Technology , ' he mentioned that the President has shown commitment in this drive and the time has come for the majority of Ghanaians to join forces to fight the menace . <p> According to him , it was not prudent to destroy mineral wealth , forests and water bodies , among others , in the country . <p> " Natural resources , by their nature , depend on each other to thrive which means that whatever one does to one of the resources such as polluting water affects the rest directly , " he stated . <p> He said that the effects of indiscriminate destruction of these resources are greatly affecting water quality , destroying vegetation cover , which has resulted in climate change . <p> " Galamsey is an enemy of the land , killing farm lands , eliminating river beds and loss of aquatic life " he remarked . <p> Prof Steve Opoku Duah of the Ohio Valley University , USA averred that the KATHAROS Technology is a cost-effective method to filtering water for domestic use . <p> According to him , concerted effort is required to ensure that people enjoy good drinking water . <p> He explained that the process , which is currently under investigations in the USA , is also an alternative approach to treatment of water using electrical energy . <p> The Professor said that plans were far advanced for the Ohio University in the USA to establish a regional assembly plant in Ghana or South Africa . <p> Ben Y.Ampomah , Executive Secretary , Water Resources Commission , Accra , said that before 1996 , Ghana did not have any policy on Water Resources management which explains the difficulty in the regulation to get people to comply . <p> " Although drilling licenses for good water development in the country are being issued from time to time , regulators find it difficult , because there is no backing legislation among others to help the regulators , " he stated . <p> The President of Knutsford University College , Dr.Andrews BC Dadson , reiterated the call on Civil Society groups working for the improvement of the environment to intensify sensitization on the conservation of nature . <p> Disclaimer : Opinions expressed here are those
