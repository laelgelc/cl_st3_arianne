<p> Leading online environment and development magazine , EnviroNews Nigeria , along with several others organisations and individuals will be honoured during the 8th Environment Outreach Magazine Public Lecture and Environmental Awards Ceremony scheduled to hold on September 7 , 2017 in Calabar , the Cross River State capital . <p> The " 2017 Environmental Awards " honour individuals and corporate bodies that have distinguished themselves in various fields of environmental management . <p> According to the host of the event and Publisher of the Environment Outreach Magazine , Chief Noble Akenge , the theme of this year 's lecture is : " Nigeria 's Depleting Forests and Its Implication for Forest Resources and Climate Change . " <p> The Lecture , Akenge added , would be delivered by the eminent sustainable development expert , Prof. Hilary Inyang , former Vice Chancellor , Botswana University of Science and Technology , Papalye , as well as President/CEO of Global Education and Infrastructure Services LLC . <p> Inyang is also a 2013 Winner of the Nigeria National Order of Merit . <p> The Conservator-General of the Federation , Mallam Ibrahim Musa Goni , is to chair the occasion , while Governor Ben Ayade of Cross River State is expected as Special Guest of Honour . <p> Other distinguished guests expected at the occasion include John Odey , former Minister of the Environment ; Prof. Steve Azaiki , the President , World Environmental Foundation for Africa and the International Society for Comparative Education , Science and Technology , Nigeria ; Engr . Goni Ahmed , the Director-General of the Great Green Wall Agency ; Barrister Sam Nwakohu , the Chairman , Board of Trustees , Living Earth Nigeria Foundation ; Samuel Onuigbo , the Chairman , House of Reps Committee on Climate Change ; Prof. Florence Obi , DVC Academics , University of Calabar ; Obinna Chidoka , Chairman , House of Reps Committee on Environment and Ecology ; Desmond Majekodunmi , CEO of LUFASI Park , Lekki , Lagos ; and Barrister Iniruo Wills , former Commissioner for the Environment , Bayelsa State . <p> Others expected at the event include Dr. Alice Ekwu , Commissioner for Climate Change and Forestry , Cross River State ; Dr. Iniobong Essien , Commissioner for Environment , Akwa Ibom State ; other State Commissioners for Environment ; top government gunctionaries , the academia ; Civil Society Organisations ; representatives of various environmental groups , traditional rulers ; and members of the public .
