<h> Flood takes over 14 communities , 13 LGAs in Kogi <p> The Nigeria Hydrological Service Agency ( NHSA ) says no fewer than 14 communities have been affected by flood in Kogi State . <p> This according to the agency cut across 13 out of the 21 Local Government areas in the state . <p> Director General Nigeria Hydrological Service Agency ( NHSA ) Moses Beckley stated this in Lokoja while presenting a paper titled " The Reality of flood and the Possible Solution as it affects Kogi State . " <p> The NHSA boss who presented his paper at a stakeholders ' forum on climate/tree planting campaign with the theme " Tackling Climate Change : the need for Afforestation " recalled that on June 10th 2017 , the agency raised alarm that 30 states will be affected by flooding , most especially those residing in high risk areas . <p> " So far , we have experienced close to 20 states , currently being ravaged by flood across the federation . Most of this flood , have happened in Urban areas " , he said . <p> He added that " it is our responsibilities to make sure that when the rain come down , the run off water are controlled , managed , and harnessed for future use " . <p> Also in his presentation , the Director General , National Emergency Management Agency ( NEMA ) Engr . Mustapha Yunusa Maihaja said the Federal Government will continue to provide support to states affected by flood . <p> Represented by Idris Mohammed , the Director General of NEMA observed that hydrological Hazards , floods and drought had become a source of major concern to Government and people all over the world . <p> According Engr . Maihaja , these natural hazards were attributable to climate change , human activities and rising in sea level . <p> He gave a breakdown of the effect of the 2012 flooding across the 36 states of the federation . <p> " 7 million people were affected . 2.3 million were displaced . 597,476 houses were destroyed .
