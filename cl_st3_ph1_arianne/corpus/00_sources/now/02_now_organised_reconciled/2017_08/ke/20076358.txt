<h> Researchers raise red flag on soils in Nyeri <h> Friday August 18 2017 <p> Dickson Kahuro in his farm in Nyeri where he grows a variety of crops . Researchers have noted that soils in the county have become too acidic to sustain quality food production . PHOTO JOSEPH NGUTHIRUI NATION MEDIA GROUP <h> In Summary <p> Climate change , soil infertility and emerging pests and diseases are also to blame for the low productivity seen in the last few years . <p> Agriculture is the main economic mainstay in Nyeri with maize , beans and potatoes being commonly grown for subsistence while coffee , tea and horticulture are the main cash crops . <p> The report projects a decline in coffee , maize , beans , wheat and potato production if corrective measures are not taken . <p> Micro nutrient zinc and copper are low in most soils across the county and boron in coffee growing zones <h> Related Content <p> In the report dated last month , some soils tested had a low pH of 3.6 to 4.2 for growing food crops in the case of Tetu . <p> Soils tested for coffee in Mathira East and Mukurwe-ini are extremely acidic with an average pH of 4 , according to the report . <p> Soil infertility is attributed to soils that are strongly acidic , which renders soil nutrients unavailable for crop use . <p> The report blamed the acidity to inadequate application of manure resulting to poor soil structure , texture and water retention capacity , and inadequate inorganic fertiliser use and poor or lack of soil conservation . <p> Emerging pests and diseases have also taken a toll on the main cash crop , which is coffee . <p> MAKE USE OF ORGANIC MANURES <p> The fall armyworm and maize lethal necrosis diseases are also a major threat to food security in the country . <p> The report projects a decline in coffee , maize , beans , wheat and potato production if corrective measures are not taken . <p> " The soils get acidic due to poor farming practices and aggregated use of acidifying chemical fertiliser , continuous mining without replenishment and furrowing , " said Robert Thuo , the Agriculture county executive . <p> Farmers have been urged to shun the use of conventional fertilisers that have been excessively used taking a toll on their output , Instead , they are asked to use organic fertilisers to boost soil health . <p> The report further noted there is low soil organic carbon matter content in the soils , a situation prevails across the entire county .
