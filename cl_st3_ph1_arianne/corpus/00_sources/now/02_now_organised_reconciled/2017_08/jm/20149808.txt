<h> An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power Fight like your world depends on it <p> A decade after former US Vice-President Al Gore brought climate change to the agenda in his Academy Award winning film , An Inconvenient Truth , comes a follow up that proves how close we are to a real energy revolution . An Inconvenient Sequel makes a plea for environmental responsibility to those in power . It features a closer look at Gore 's relentless optimism about conquering the threat from global warming and his worldwide campaign of training climate leaders and negotiating behind the scenes at the Paris Climate Summit 2015.
