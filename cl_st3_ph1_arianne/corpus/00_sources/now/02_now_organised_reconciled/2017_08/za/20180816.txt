<h> News feed <h> Morehill resident found her passion in film making <p> Benoni 's very own film maker , Yakima Waner will be showcasing her documentary ' To Wake ' at the Ekotopfilm Festival in Prague Czech Republic this September . <p> 11 hours ago <p> Yakima Waner is seen with Janisha ( 5 ) the protagonist of the documentary . <p> The festival is used to promote issues of sustainable development in a comprehensive , entertaining way by screening documentary films and offering other attractive additional events . <p> The 29-year-old said she is very blessed to have been given an opportunity to convey the film 's message to the masses . <p> " Ekotopfilm festival is a great feather in our ' film cap ' because it is recognised worldwide for its powerful stories about sustainable change , " said Waner . <p> Her documentary was filmed in a place called The valley of the Gods which is found in Banjar , a small village situated in Kullu in Himachal Pradesh India . <p> " The film 's narrative follows the journey of a remarkable five-year-old girl named Janisha who dedicates her life to sustaining and saving the forests around her . <p> " The child-like inspirational journey shows Janisha teach the women and children within her village to live a more empowered , sustainable and ' aware ' life , " she said . <p> " The documentary has one core purpose which is to educate people about forest fires , climate change and women empowerment . " <p> Waner filmed the documentary with Sophie Patch who is a wildlife and human rights photographer . <p> When asked what inspired her to film the documentary she said : " I wanted to experience one of the harshest but most majestic environments known to man -- the Himalayas . <p> I also wanted to film one of scarcest creatures on the planet , namely the endangered snow leopard . <p> The Morehill resident said the documentary was commissioned by Himalayan Ecotourism , an organisation which not only offers tours in the Himalays , but also encourages sustainable living and tries to empower women . <p> " This story took me out of my comfort zone and helped me find my calling as a documentarian , " she said . <p> In 2014 , Waner graduated with a BA in Dramatic Arts at the University of Witwatersrand . <p> She then joined Africa Media in Mossel Bay as a freelance documentarian and also completed an internship at Felis Creation . <p> " That 's where we got the opportunity to create our first wildlife documentary about the African bush . <p> Waner is the Social and Environmental Awareness Documentarian and editor of Intent Conscious Media . <p> ' To Wake ' has also been shown at the Mzansi Women 's Film Festival this August .
