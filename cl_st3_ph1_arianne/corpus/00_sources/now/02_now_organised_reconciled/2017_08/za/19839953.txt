<p> British born and Cape Town based endurance swimmer Lewis Pugh has completed an epic one-kilometre swim in the Arctic , to raise awareness of runaway climate change in the region . The swim took place along the edge of the Arctic sea ice in -0.5 ? C waters and was completed in a bracing 22 minutes . <p> Lewis is able to complete polar swims as a result of intense cold water training and a condition labelled ' anticipatory thermogenesis ' by Professor Tim Noakes . This is where Lewis 's core body temperature rises as high as 38.2 ? C before he dives into freezing water . <p> However , this time Lewis was challenged to his limits , as he spent more time in the water than any of his previous polar swims . In his blog , he writes ; <p> " My cadence was off from the start . Perhaps it was the shock of sub-zero water , but whereas I usually count 100 strokes for 100 metres , it took me 130 strokes to reach that same distance . At 650 metres I was struggling to coordinate my kick , and my hands were frozen so they could n't grip the water . " <p> It was at this point that Lewis considered giving up , but famed for his perseverance and his ability to ' vasbyt ' , he forced himself to continue . Afterwards , his hands were so frozen that he could n't hold on to Kelvin Trautman , the photographer helping him into the support boat , and he had to bite down on Kelvin 's dry suit for purchase . <p> Lewis is a United Nations Patron of the Oceans , and this swim comes ten years after he became the first person to complete a long distance swim in the -1.7 ? C waters of the Arctic Ocean . <p> " During the past two decades , average temperatures in the Arctic have risen almost twice as much as they have at lower latitudes across the globe . The result : widespread shrinking of glaciers , sea-ice and snow-covered areas . This not only affects the creatures , great and small , that live here in the Arctic . It goes beyond the narwhals and the bearded seals , the Svalbard reindeer and the Arctic Fox , the puffins and the polar bears , whose natural habitats are being drastically altered . Because what happens here determines all our futures . " <p> The Arctic is an integral part of our climate system , which helps regulate the planet 's temperature . It usually reaches its lowest extent in the summer month of September each year . In his book , A Farewell to Ice , polar scientist Peter Wadhams points out that " in September 2012 , sea ice covered only 3.4 million square kilometres ( km2 ) of the Arctic Ocean 's surface , down from 8 million km2 in the 1970s . " <p> As part of his Arctic Decade campaign , Lewis will undertake a voyage to three Arctic nations ; Norway , Canada and Russia .
