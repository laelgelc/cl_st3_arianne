<p> CAPE TOWN - The Western Cape Agriculture Department has reiterated that drought-stricken farmers will be prioritised as it tries to manage the water shortage . <p> The department has spent a combined R59 million on drought relief for farmers over the 2016/17 financial year . <p> A further R5 million will be spent on managing issues such as avian flu , which has been detected among ostriches in some parts of Oudtshoorn . <p> The chairperson for the provincial government 's standing committee on agriculture 's Beverley Schafer says : " We call on all farmers in the Western Cape to be vigilant and they survive the dry winter months , which is a direct result of an increase in regional temperatures , due to climate change . "
