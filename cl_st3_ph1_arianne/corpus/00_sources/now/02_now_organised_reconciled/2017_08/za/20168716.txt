<h> Fisherman drowns in Lake Malawi <p> A 37-year-old man known as Gilbert Samuel has been found dead after drowning in Lake Malawi at Manyamba fishing dock in Nkhotakota . <p> Nkhotakota Police deputy spokesperson Paul Malimwe told Malawi24 that on the night of 15-16 August the man went on the lake using a canoe to fish . <p> According to Malimwe , it is believed that whilst Samuel was at the lake , heavy Mwera winds erupted that led the canoe to capsize due to the impact . Samuel did not return home . <p> Later , the matter of missing person was reported in all docks bordering Nkhotakota-Salima and Mkaika police unit in particular . <p> " On August 21 , 2017 the body was found floating at Manyamba fishing dock in a decomposed state and burial was ordered , " Malimwe told Malawi24 . <p> Postmortem by medical personnel from Kapiri health centre confirmed that the death was due to suffocation . <p> The deceased , Gilbert Samuel hailed from Mwanjoni village Traditional Authority Makanjira in Salima . <p> In a related incident , a 20-year-old man who was epileptic drowned in Nankhaka River in Lilongwe on Sunday . <p> In an interview with Malawi24 , Kanengo Police Station spokesperson Laban Makalani identified the man as Ganizani James from Musa village in the area of Traditional Authority Nyambi in Machinga district . <p> Makalani told Malawi24 that the deceased James went to the river to fish alongside his friends . <p> " In the course of fishing it is reported that the man , who was epileptic , accidentally fell into the river . He drowned after failing to swim to safety . <p> " His friends reported the matter to elders in the village who visited the scene and found his body submerged , " Makalani told Malawi24 . <p> Efforts to recover his body proved futile until on Tuesday when the body was seen floating on the river . <p> On Friday last week , two children both 3 years of age belonging to separate families also got drowned in the same river after they had gone to take a bath . <p> They drowned after swimming to deep area in the river . <p> Meanwhile police are strongly urging the fishermen to follow the instructions from the Department of Climate Change and Meteorological Services to avoid similar cases .
