<h> Climate change predicted to have devastating impact on NZ farmers <p> Wairarapa farmers will need to adapt as the area becomes hotter and drier over the next 70 years . ( Photo Getty Images ) <p> Climate change is predicted to have potentially devastating consequences for both the east and west coast of the lower North Island . <p> In the wake of a NIWA climate change report predicting more droughts for the Wairarapa , there is a call for rule changes to make it easier for farmers to store water . <p> The report predicts spring rainfall on the east coast will reduce by up to 15 percent and overall temperatures will rise by three degrees , with autumn warming the most . <p> Wairarapa farmers will need to adapt as the area becomes hotter and drier over the next 70 years . <p> South Wairarapa Deputy Mayor Brian Jephson said they need to get smarter around conserving winter water for stock water and irrigation . <p> " It 's going to be a challenge to regional councils , they have to look at the laws around this and how to make it easier for farmers to build these dams . " <p> South Wairarapa Mayor Viv Napier said water storage options will be looked at , but there is already some irrigation in the area . <p> " But also farming practices probably need to change too , and maybe there 's better ways of doing it with the water that they 've got now . " <p> While the Wairarapa prepares for drought , the west coast is facing the opposite problem -- more rain . <p> Kapiti Coast mayor K Gurunathan said the region needs a plan to deal with the predicted 15 percent increase in rainfall and extreme weather events over the next 70 years . <p> Gurunathan said there are currently around 4500 flood prone properties in the district , so the report 's new information will help with risk analysis and detailed work on specific areas . <p> " Then we 're going to make serious political judgments on adaptation strategies . What are we going to do where , how soon , and who 's going to pay for it . Big issues . " <p> Gurunathan is calling for more government help to develop climate change strategies . <p> " We need designs for the planners to be able to come and be able to make the right decisions . So this is not just local authorities having to deal with this , because this is an expensive and litigatious issue . We need help . "
