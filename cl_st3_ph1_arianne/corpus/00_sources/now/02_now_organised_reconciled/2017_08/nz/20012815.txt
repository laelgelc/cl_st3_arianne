<h> 13 reasons why NZ is the most progressive country in the world <p> JOSIE MCCRICKARD <p> Last updated 13:43 , August 15 2017 <p> PIXABAY <p> New Zealand has always been at the forefront of women 's rights . <p> New Zealand often feels as if it 's unaffected by the rest of the world . Perhaps it is this pure and unscathed quality that allows New Zealand to be one of the most , if not the most progressive country in the world . This is up for debate , of course , and many may object citing cost of living , housing prices and lack of basic technology such as central heating . <p> After some research , I can show you that New Zealand has been a a trailblazer in many important milestones : women 's rights , the environment and employment rights - thus solidifying the case for progressive New Zealand . <p> What do I mean by progressive ? I 'm talking about a country that favours or advocates progress , change , improvement , or reform , as opposed to wishing to maintain things as they are as well as making progress toward better conditions ; employing or advocating more enlightened or liberal ideas , new or experimental methods . <p> New Zealand was one of the last countries in the world to be populated by humans . In country terms , it 's as new as can be , and this is definitely apparent in the landscape ; the pure , crystal clear waters and the feeling that dinosaurs could possibly still live in the wild environment . Being new , perhaps means there is n't a great deal of long-entrenched historical and societal expectations - ie things can push forward unhindered . The geographical isolation must have a bearing on this too - with little political or territorial distraction , New Zealand is potentially less affected by the outside world than other countries , allowing it to push forward without too much outside interference . <p> On the flipside , there is an outdated , unfair and downright bizarre stereotype that some people have about New Zealand . The impression that New Zealand is a little sister of Australia and is slightly backwards with not much going on is a stereotype I have come to strongly disagree with . My personal experience ( as a British woman who moved to New Zealand in July 2016 ) and the following indicators show that it 's quite the opposite : New Zealand might just be the most progressive country in the world . <h> Share your news and views <p> Share your stories , photos and videos . <p> 1 . First women to get the vote <p> On 19 September 1893 , a new electoral act was signed by Lord Glasgow ( the governor at the time ) resulting in landmark legislation giving all women the right to vote in parliamentary elections . Yes , New Zealand was the first country in the world to give women the vote . In the UK it was n't until 1918 and in the US 1920 . Decades ahead , does n't seem backward to me . <p> 2 . No nuclear power <p> PIXABAY <p> New Zealand says NO to nuclear power . <p> New Zealand is working towards obtaining 90 per cent of its power from renewable sources by 2025 . Already , wind power usage is at one of the highest proportions in the world . Nuclear power , however , does not gain much public or political support , with only 19 per cent of New Zealanders choosing nuclear as a good source of energy for the next ten years or wanting more research done into it . <p> It seems that green , clean and pure image is high on the agenda for New Zealanders . New Zealand remains one of the few developed countries in the world not using nuclear power . Whether this will stay the case remains to be seen . <p> 3. 13th country in the world to legalise gay marriage <p> New Zealand became the 13th country in the world to legalise gay marriage back in 2013 . As this remains an ongoing issue in Australia , the idea that New Zealand is Australia 's little sister seems completely redundant here . Nice one , progressive New Zealand . <p> 4 . No zero hours contracts <p> With zero hours contracts high on the agenda in the UK , perhaps a leaf could be taken out of little old New Zealand 's book . From April 2016 it became illegal for New Zealand companies to employ workers without guaranteeing a specific number of hours per week . This allows for a degree of certainty for employees , enabling them to plan their lives and finances to a greater degree . It seems shocking that this is not the rule everywhere . <p> PIXABAY <p> You really ca n't beat Wellington on a good day ( and it 's not just locals who believe it ) . <p> Wellington is number one on the 2017 list of most liveable cities in a global survey by Deutsche Bank , beating Vienna , Edinburgh and Melbourne to the top spot . Measured on values such as cost of living , pollution , climate and house prices , Wellington is top of the table . And the accolades for Kiwi cities do n't stop there . Auckland was voted third in the world 's most liveable cities by a Mercer survey , following only Vienna and Zurich . Big cities such as London and New York , often considered the pinnacle of living , appear way , way down those lists . <p> 6 . First man to climb Everest <p> New Zealand is top of the world for explorers . The first man to climb Mount Everest was New Zealander , Edmund Hillary who conquered the peak with Tenzing Norgay back in 1953 . This is a pretty damn impressive mountaineering feat to come out of a small country . <p> 7 . I 'm sure this had and continues to have a positive impact on young girls and their aspirations here . It can be done , clearly . The rest of the world should take note . <p> 8 . Respect for indigenous culture <p> There is no hiding from or denying the indigenous people of New Zealand , as with the rest of the world 's colonised countries , have suffered unimaginable injustices and hardships that can not be repaired . But New Zealanders make a genuine and visible effort , rarely seen elsewhere in the world , to overcome the dark history and cherish and encourage indigenous practice . <p> The hongi , to share breath , the Maori tradition of touching noses as greeting is widely used to greet important figures from abroad . Important sports stars are greeted at the airport with a traditional Maori welcoming ceremony . These steps may be small , but are nevertheless a sign of progress and integration . <p> 9 . Safety <p> New Zealand was officially ranked the second safest country in the world this year in the Global Peace Index , second only to Iceland . With performance indicators such as the level of societal safety and security , the extent of ongoing and international conflict , and the degree of militarisation , it means New Zealand citizens and residents are almost the safest in the world . Amazing really . <p> 10 . No fake news <p> Sorry to quote Donald Trump , but this reason is highly important in the world 's current political climate . New Zealand 's media landscape is one of the most liberal in the world . The broadcasting sector was deregulated in 1988 when the government allowed competition to the state-owned Television New Zealand ( TVNZ ) . Privately-owned TV3 is TVNZ 's main competitor . Defamation cases are civil matters , and can result in both punitive damages and heavy fines . There are no government restrictions on the internet , and four in five homes have access to the web . Journalists are generally able to cover the news freely , and physical attacks or threats against the media are rare . <p> 11 . Very highly socially progressive <p> In a Washington based think tank 's social progress index report for 2017 New Zealand is ranked very highly in terms of social progress . This means it performs highly on almost all indicators , with over-performance on indicators such as press freedom , corruption , freedom over life choices , tolerance for immigrants , LGBT rights and low pollution . <p> 12 . Rocket launching <p> New Zealand has become an unlikely location for missions into space . The organisation Rocket Lab has launched its maiden voyage of a 3D printed rocket from the Mahia peninsula tin May this year . As ships and planes need to be re-routed , New Zealand makes for an excellent location for space launching as there is much less movement than the US , and only Antarctica to the south . New Zealand has set up rocket legislation with anticipation of becoming a low cost space hub . <p> 13 . Deep Green Bush School <p> Recently The Guardian ran an article about an innovative , change-making and totally progressive school in the rural south of Auckland . This was a huge inspiration for this submission . The kids at this school do n't get taught in normal classes or given homework , but instead are taken for a spot of fishing followed by cooking on an open fire . Can you imagine anything more different from the norm , or more wonderful ? <p> Founded by an ex-mainstream school teacher , the school currently has a roll of eight kids who are taught hunting , gathering and survival . The aim is to equip them for future issues such as climate change . Traditional school subjects are acquired at the students ' own pace . In this modern world is the traditional school system outdated ? Well , perhaps New Zealand can help lead the way on new models with progressive programs like this . <p> So there you have it , 13 reasons why New Zealand is an amazingly progressive country with a track record leading the way in the past , and still way ahead on many issues . I am sure there are more reasons too , so please comment below with any more points I may have missed . <p> Individually these factors might seem like small steps , but together they build the case for a country at the vanguard for political , social and environmental reform . I am impressed . <p> Please know that I am not forgetting or ruling out the issues with domestic violence , suicide , house prices or other problems like this that New Zealand faces , as these are big issues that require much more thought and reform . But on today 's global scale , New Zealand massively outperforms many other developed countries on innovation , progress and change .
