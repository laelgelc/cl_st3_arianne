<h> What is the Ardern agenda ? <p> The ' Ardern Effect ' is all about the vibe . But what about policy - what is Labour leader Jacinda Ardern 's agenda ? <p> Income tax <p> A new tax rate on higher earners on say $120,000-$150,000 could be announced in the coming days . <p> Ms Ardern says : " We do need to make sure that we treat taxation fairly . " <p> Capital gains tax <p> Labour could bring in a capital gains tax if elected - a tax on profits from a sale . <p> Ms Ardern has ruled it out on the family home , but that 's it - a capital gains tax could mean a tax when a business is sold , when land is sold , when a business is sold or when shares are sold . <p> Labour wo n't tell voters what it will do yet - it will have a review if in Government - then do it . <p> Ms Ardern says : " I will be using that prerogative because I expect New Zealanders will want me to show leadership on that issue . " <p> Retirement age <p> Ms Ardern has been sold as the ' generational warrior ' . She joked that while she may not identify as a ' youth ' , she 's ' youth adjacent ' . <p> So , in the war between millennials and baby boomers , whose side is Ms Ardern on ? <p> Millennials are calling for a change to the superannuation age . Prime Minister Bill English will raise it to 67 years by 2040 . <p> But Ms Ardern has ruled out raising it raise it - ever . <p> Ms Ardern says : " I 've already ruled it out . We 're not campaigning on it . We 're not looking to make any changes . It 's not on our agenda . " <p> TPP <p> Ardern is prepared to pull New Zealand out of the TPP . <p> TPP 11 is already on the table - that 's 11 countries getting ready to sign a deal without Donald Trump 's United States . <p> The countries have agreed not to renegotiate because it would mess up the deal - but Ms Ardern wants to open it up again . <p> In a big call , Ms Ardern says if elected , she will pull out of the deal unless Labour gets a clause allowing it to ban foreign home buyers . <p> Ms Ardern says : " We 've always made it clear that that was a bottom line for us . <p> " Unlike other countries , unfortunately the current Government had n't negotiated a carve-out for us to maintain the right of domestic residents to purchase their own land and put a ban on foreign speculators , so we will try and renegotiate that . " <p> Water tax <p> Farmers will be hit by a water tax under Ardern . <p> Labour wants to charge a ' fair and affordable ' royalty to large commercial users of water - including water bottling companies . <p> Royalties for bottled and irrigated water will be set after a consultation period , with revenue then used to clean up waterways . But farmers and horticulturalists have raised concerns about how the levies could impact on their businesses . <p> Climate change <p> Ms Ardern would charge farmers for climate-change-causing emissions . <p> This could happen in the first term of a Labour Government . <p> Today Ms Ardern revealed she could charge farmers for emissions in the first term of a Labour Government by bringing agriculture into the Emissions Trading Scheme . <p> Ms Ardern says some industries will bear the brunt of tackling climate change more than others and Labour would develop ' relief ' for those industries in the form of a financial offset . <p> Ms Ardern says : " We 're very clear that there are some industries that will bear the brunt of dealing with climate change more than others . Agriculture is one of the areas we need to do that . " <p> " We want to make sure , for instance , that they are only picking up 10 percent of what they 're contributing , because we know that they are an industry that are facing the front-end of climate change . We need to ease the path for them . If we do n't then at some point they 're going to be brought in with a sharp jolt . We do n't want that . " <p> Cannabis <p> Ms Ardern supports legalising medicinal cannabis and expanding the access of medicinal products , but wo n't be drawn on how she 'd deal with the wider topic of decriminalising recreational cannabis .
