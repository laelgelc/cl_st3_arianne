<p> Labour 's Pacific Affairs spokesperson , Aupito William Sio , said the migration policy will be reviewed for small Pacific nations like Tuvalu , Tokelau and Kiribati which feel the brunt of climate change . <p> He also said the Cook Islands , Niuean , Tokelauan , Samoan and Tongan languages will be made official community languages in the education system . <p> Aupito said quality language education had a proven record of raising academic achievement . <p> He also promised Pasifika content in public broadcasting , and goals for Pacific literacy , research and bilingualism . <p> By 2026 , Pacific people are forecast to make up 10 percent of New Zealand 's population , with just under half of those people being under the age of 20.
