<h> Green 's Zhu fighting for social justice <p> The last few weeks have been tough for Julie Zhu . The Green Party candidate for Botany says she took a day to grieve and is now continuing her fight so that former Green Party co-leader Metiria Turei 's resignation is not wasted . <p> With a background in arts , theatre , film and photography she is of the opinion that young people must be involved in politics . <p> The 24-year-old is passionate about standing up for what is right . <p> " East Auckland has such a diverse population and I think leadership should be well represented so that people have an equal go . " <p> She says that being young and Asian , it is important for her to open up the gates for other young women of colour to feel confident enough to be heard and step into public life . <p> " I never had an ambition to be involved in politics and always thought it to be distant and boring , " says the young actress , director and producer of theatre and films . <p> She first contested for the Local Board elections , Botany subdivision in 2016 . Julie is quick to point out that she has always been passionate about social change . <p> " I feel strongly about promoting social justice , " she says . <p> Born in Xi'an , China , she arrived in NZ at the age of four , living almost exclusively in east Auckland . <p> She attributes being a part of the Green Party as plain common sense . " I am standing for a party that believes in protecting the environment , social justice , reducing inequality and understanding people who are not necessarily like you , " she says , about wanting to change the fabric of society . <p> " Climate change has been the biggest existential crisis of our generation and the Green Party is committing to going carbon neutral by 2050 . Under National , our emissions have gone up by 19 per cent . <p> " Contamination of water up North is not a situation we should be facing in New Zealand . We need to attack the problem now if we do n't want to boil our water . " <p> The former Pakuranga College student says she is not one of those " loony hippies " that people think the Green Party to be . <p> Currently juggling a 20-hour freelance assignment as outreach manager at the Red Leap theatre and a 10-hour job as programme assistant at the Basement Theatre in the city , she has her hands full with a movie fundraiser An Inconvenient sequel : Truth to Power for the Green Party at the Monterey Cinemas on Thursday August 24 . <p> A three minute documentary film titled East meets East ' made by Julie Zhu who is keen to represent the diverse population in east Auckland . <p> She is also busy producing a live documentary theatre show titled OTHER Chinese at the Q Theatre on Thursday , September 7-16 . <p> It 's too close to the General Election date and she moans saying , she does n't have the luxury like some other young candidate of being a full-time politician . <p> And while she looks at the back-benchers in Parliament and marvels at " the way they can dodge the question so well " , she says she is " looking forward to helping support the Labour Party and also hold them to account " . <h> WATCH <p> Our awards winning publications continue to grow from strength to strength both in print and online .
