<p> Stepping firmly on the Greens ' patch , Ms Ardern told the more than 2000 attendees that a Government led by her will take climate change seriously , and be driven by principle - not expediency . <p> " It is a transition that can and must be made and it is a transition that can and must be just , " she said . <p> " This is my generation 's nuclear free moment , and I am determined we will tackle it head-on . " <p> On the economy , Ms Ardern said that economic success will be judged differently under her leadership than how it is today . <p> " A successful economy is one that serves its people , not the other way around ... GDP rates and numbers on a sheet of paper do n't always tell you much about the well-being of the people working to keep our economy going . " <p> While light on policy , Ms Ardern 's speech touched on a number of other issues the Labour Party is passionate about , including fixing the housing crisis and tackling poverty . <p> She said her Government would ensure everyone has a roof over their head , ending the misery that many are confronting today . <p> " I 've seen kids who sleep stacked underneath each other on beds and bunk beds because there is just no room in the house . That is our housing situation at its worst , but this is not a reality we have to accept . " <p> Ms Ardern 's address was received rapturously by those in the room , which included former Prime Minister Helen Clark .
