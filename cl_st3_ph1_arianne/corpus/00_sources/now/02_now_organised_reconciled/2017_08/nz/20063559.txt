<p> The parents of the released smolt were wild returning fish which came back a year ago , French said . <p> Fish and Game New Zealand then take the salmon to rear the eggs at the Montrose hatchery until they reach 15g , at which point they are returned to Silverstream , minus their adipose fin ( for identification purposes ) . <p> At Silverstream they acclimatise to the water and continue to grow until they weigh 50g , at around one year old . <p> With wild spawning numbers on the decline , the hatchery and release programme was very important for the salmon run , French said , not to mention the local economy . <p> Several factors impeded the salmon run including nitrate levels and climate change , and the annual salmon release helped to protect the wild population . <p> New Zealand Salmon Anglers Association vice president Larry Burke attended the release and said it was great for the fishery , and the season ahead was looking promising . <p> " All this rainfall has been good for the rivers and should last through the summer so we are looking forward to a pretty good season , as long as we do n't get too many nor-westers making the rivers dirty .
