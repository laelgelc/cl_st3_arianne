Interesting that all other tribes have successfully negotiated and settled , reinvested , and are reaping the benefits for their whanau . Are Ngapuhi somewhat out of touch with reality ? <p> In 1821 Hongi Hika , renowned Maori chief , was invited to England by King George IV . <p> Treated lavishly , returning to NZ via Sydney , he showed eternal gratitude , swapping gifts for 500 muskets , gunpowder , ammunition , swords and daggers . Armed with superior weaponry , over the next 10 years Ngapuhi took tribal vengeance over old enemies . Many were driven from tribal lands , never to return . <p> Rather than expect any special right to delay their final settlement , Ngapuhi should reflect on their history , respect the fact that all their fellow tribes have successfully settled , and make sure they apportion a generous slice of their final settlement to the tribes they so cruelly treated . <p> Perhaps we should also remember his recent calculated actions ( or inactions ) over Todd Barclay . <p> Cynically , and sadly , those on higher incomes awaiting a promised tax break , have probably forgotten already . <p> ( Abridged ) N.
