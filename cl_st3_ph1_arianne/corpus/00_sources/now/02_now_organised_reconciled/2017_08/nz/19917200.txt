<h> Hot times ahead for region -- NIWA <p> This is the latest prediction in a climate change report prepared by NIWA scientists and commissioned by Greater Wellington Regional Council . <p> Wairarapa will go from having about 24 hot days a year -- classified as 25 degrees or more -- to about 94 days . <p> More frequent and " extreme " events due to the warmer atmosphere such as severe droughts , heavy rain and lower river flows could be in the region 's future by 2090 if no action was taken to reduce greenhouse gas emissions globally . <p> By 2040 , 10 fewer days of rain a year than now is expected for Wairarapa . <p> The biggest concern for Wairarapa is the effect the severe weather events and droughts will have on agriculture , horticulture , water supply and public health . <p> These were the key findings of the report . <p> But this was the " worst-case scenario " if the region continued to do ' business as usual ' , the report 's lead author and climate scientist Petra Pearce said . <p> " Mainly , the drought projections are a concern for Wairarapa , " Ms Pearce said . <p> With the number of hot days to increase from about three weeks to over three months annually , the impacts on primary industries include heat stress on cattle , crop growth and " sleeper " pests becoming active . <p> Drought will also impact air quality and public health , including increases in allergies and disease-spreading pests . <p> " Wairarapa probably is n't going to get the heat waves say in Europe and America . . . but if we get longer , hotter spells than we are used to , then that 's going to have an impact on health , " she said . <p> Coastal erosion along the South Wairarapa coast could increase as sea levels rose . <p> " The extremes are getting more extreme -- the drought , the rain and then hot days and of course with the warming atmosphere , there will be fewer cold days , " she said . <p> In the high elevations of the Tararua Ranges , scientists predict that frosts will decrease from 30 per year to near zero by 2090 . <p> The region is already seeing the impact of climate change with more extreme weather events over the past decade . <p> " Climate change is often thought of by individuals as something that the government or the industry needs to do on a high level . <p> " But I think climate change needs to be bought into by individuals through decision-making processes , " she said . <p> Whether it be buying a petrol or electric car , or making a home more energy efficient , " every little decision counts " . <p> " So there is hope that the impacts of the greenhouse emissions in 2090 wo n't get to that level of the worst-case scenario . " <p> Wellington Regional Council chairman Chris Laidlaw said the report showed how important it was to take urgent action . <p> He said the worst impacts of climate change could be avoided but only if communities around the world acted to reduce their emissions . <p> With the concerning temperature increase , the issue of water management in the region was a priority . <p> " The availability of water in Wairarapa is under threat , " he said . <h> Collaborative approach needed <p> A collaborative approach by the three Wairarapa district councils and the regional council is needed to mitigate and adapt to climate change in Wairarapa . <p> Representatives from the three district councils attended a workshop held by NIWA yesterday . <p> Carterton District Council chief executive Jane Davis said the climate change report findings made for good discussion among council representatives . <p> " It was frightening enough that we need to do something . . . it is not overwhelmingly problematic , but we have time , " she said . <p> The way the report was presented made an " incredibly wicked problem " that is hard to think about , easier to absorb . <p> Masterton District councillor Chris Peterson said while the findings gave perspective on what the region 's climate would look like in 20 to 70 years , he wondered if more stress on the issues was needed . <p> " From my personal perspective , I 'm not sure there was the urgency , " he said . <p> The effects on the region may also present options such as the chance to cultivate different crops , he added .
