<h> Kennedy Graham denied Greens candidacy <p> The Green Party has decided MP Kennedy Graham will not be able to run as a candidate for the party in the September election . <p> Kennedy Graham . Photo : RNZ / Michael Cropp <p> Earlier this week Dr Graham and fellow MP David Clendon asked to be taken off the party 's list when they pulled their support for former Greens co-leader Metiria Turei . <p> Dr Graham then applied to be put back on the party 's list , following Mrs Turei 's resignation . <p> Yesterday the Green Party 's Executive met to consider Dr Graham 's request , but found he had breached the Party 's Candidate Code of Conduct in a serious manner . <p> In a statement , the Green Party said it decided to keep Dr Graham off the list . <p> Greens co-convenor Debs Martin said it was an unfortunate way for things to have turned out . <p> " The party deeply regrets that this situation has arisen . <p> " We believe that Kennedy has given a lot of good work to the party over a number of years and we find it very regrettable that he did this serious breach of our candidate code of conduct . " <p> Prior to joining the Green Party , Dr Graham had a diplomatic career spanning three decades , that included helping to negotiate the South Pacific Nuclear-Free Zone in the 1980s . <p> He has also lectured on international relations at Victoria and Canterbury universities . <p> Over the years , he also gained a reputation as a leader on climate change policy . He attended the first United Nations conference on the issue in Rio de Janeiro in 1992 and was at the negotiations of the 2015 Paris Agreement . <p> He headed a cross-parliamentary group of MPs , GLOBE New Zealand , that last year released a report mapping out a way for New Zealand to meet its emissions target .
