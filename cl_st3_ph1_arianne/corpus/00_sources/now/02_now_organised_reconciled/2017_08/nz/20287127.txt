<p> Infamous conservative political commentator Ann Coulter has once again landed herself in hot water - this time for comments on homosexuality and climate change in the wake of Hurricane Harvey . <p> The hurricane has been held up as an example of the effects of global warming , with many climate scientists saying it likely played a part in the severity of the storm , which has killed at least 17 people and forced 30,000 to flee to emergency shelters .
