<h> Labour 's Jacinda Ardern moment a blur in a tumultuous week in politics <p> DAVE ARMSTRONG <p> Last updated 05:00 , August 7 2017 <p> SUNDAY <p> New Labour leader Jacinda Ardern talks children , personality politics and the first person she hugged after taking the job . <p> OPINION : I know a week is a long time in politics , but a couple of days ? Early last week a new poll came out . I noticed that the combined Centre-Left vote ( Labour and Greens ) had gone up a point and that National had stayed static , with its support partners plunging . <p> Thanks to Metiria Turei 's startling admission of benefit fraud , the Greens had gone up at the expense of Labour . This pilfering of each other 's support happens quite a bit on the Left . <p> Instead the story became a crisis for the Labour Party . For those of us who remember the 1996 election campaign -- where Helen Clark came from a far worse position to almost become , there but for the grace of Winston , prime minister -- we thought ' big deal ' . <p> GETTY IMAGES <p> Labour leader Jacinda Ardern and deputy Kelvin Davis . <p> But Andrew Little , in an act of political self-immolation , admitted he had discussed the ' r ' word -- resignation -- with senior colleagues . That was it . Jacinda was leader and Kelvin Davis her deputy . <p> Forget that Davis recently defied Labour policy over Maori-run prisons and threatened to resign if Northland charter schools closed , even though Labour opposes them . A leader and deputy with vastly different backgrounds and beliefs might appear totally incompatible to you and me , but in politics that 's called a dream team . <p> Nats Gerry Brownlee , Steve Joyce and Bill English suddenly looked old-fashioned and out of touch . <p> The normally quick-to-respond Joyce said little , as if he had been suddenly struck on the face with a dildo of youthful exuberance . <p> English was dignified and respectful towards Ardern . He knew that bagging the new head prefect on her first day of school can easily backfire . Brownlee was so furious at the way TV3 's Paddy Gower enthusiastically detailed Ardern 's first day that he sent pom-poms to help with the cheerleading . <p> What Brownlee does n't understand is that our normally right-wing media have n't suddenly lurched to the Left , they simply love a good story . And if Ardern finds herself prime minister in September then what a great Hollywood epic that would be . <p> Ardern is Catherine Zeta-Jones starting her first day in the office . English is Michael Douglas playing a salty old unhelpful boss who has been there for years telling her she is doing it all wrong . <p> And there is only suave but grumpy janitor , Kelvin Davis , played by George Clooney , to help poor Ardern figure out how to use the printer . <p> When interviewing Ardern in her new job , male media commentators immediately zeroed in on the possible impact of a Prime Ministerial baby . Talk about medieval , though Ms Congeniality called them on their blatant sexism so sternly it would have warmed the cockles of Helen Clark 's heart . <p> Commentators on the Right have conceded that Ardern has an engaging personal style , but they wonder if there is any substance to the woman who can turn the world on with her smile . Excuse me , is n't that exactly what the Left said about John Key for the last nine years ? You put style above content for nearly a decade , now it 's our turn ! <p> But what about Ardern 's policies ? We will have to wait and see . Many in Labour have been looking to the UK for inspiration , where a radical socialist manifesto galvanised young voters and brought Jeremy Corbyn back from the dead . <p> But I suspect Ardern 's Labour will look more towards Canada and France . Here , centrist parties with young , attractive leaders have wowed with progressive policies on immigration , human rights and climate change , yet have done little to challenge the existing economic order or to help the poor and vulnerable .
