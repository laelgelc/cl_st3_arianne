<h> Turei and the future of the Greens <p> The Green Party co-leader 's move comes after she admitted breaking electoral law in 1993 . <p> OPINION : If Labour can change its leader only a few weeks out from an election , then so can the Greens . <p> Greens co-leader Metiria Turei 's confession of committing benefit fraud was seen by many as a brave move , and elicited sympathy from those who had experienced the hardships of getting by on the benefit . <p> Her admission of having lied to WINZ while at the same time offering the election bribe of a 20 per cent pay rise in benefits worked and delivered the Greens a bump in the polls . <p> MONIQUE FORD/STUFF <p> Metiria Turei was looking quite the heroine until Jacinda-mania came along . <p> But now that she has been found out committing alleged electoral fraud for using the father of her child 's residential address as her own so she could vote for a friend in an election , Turei seems calculated and manipulative . <p> Chloe Swarbrick is the new Infant Phenomenon in waiting , writes Jane Bowron . <p> It was convenient for the father of her child not to be in the picture , or her dwelling , when she was receiving the DPB . But he was useful when she wanted to register herself as domicile at his address so she could vote for a pal . <p> When she offered an amnesty on benefit fraud to others , who have found themselves in the same position as herself , she claimed her motive in doing so was to eradicate poverty . But you do n't have to have done Psychology 101 to suspect that Turei was feeling very alone when she confessed to benefit fraud and wanted the company of others , a show of strength in numbers , to normalise her offence . <p> Just when Turei was looking quite the heroine and stealing the limelight back off Winston Peters , along came Jacinda-mania to make Turei 's brushes with the law seem like a long drawn-out shaggy dog story without a decent punchline . <p> New Labour leader Jacinda Ardern was articulate , funny and fresh . And the appointment of Kelvin Davis as her deputy leader saw the Maori Party , who want to be in government in perpetuity , immediately give Davis the thumbs-up and send the ' Barkis is willing ' message that they can do business with Labour to form a government . <p> The day of Ardern 's leadership takeover , Peters was approached by reporters in Parliament and asked for his opinion about the change of guard . Smoke-free Winston , who has given up the fags , testily waved the question aside and bustled down the corridor saying he had more important things to concern him , namely some 450 text messages the Prime Minister sent to his former electorate staffer Glenys Dickson . <p> Dripping with innuendo , Peters made the exchange of late night and early morning texts between Bill English and Dickson sent during the Todd Barclay debacle sound vaguely sordid . Frankly , even the whiff of an extra-marital would be doing the PM a favour in a week where Bill-bore had gone from a steady pair of hands to yesterday 's dinosaur . <p> With her Persil-white chompers that would make lettuces afraid , Ardern and her relentless positivity and sense of attack seemed so much more now and relevant . In contrast , the PM looked like a snooze agent , a sleepy hare that had taken the tortoise Labour Party for granted and had just woken up to see them bounding ahead to the finish line . <p> Ardern seemed to be everywhere , dropping in to an episode of the Back Benches television show to support Labour MP Michael Wood appearing on the show 's panel . Spontaneous applause broke out as she chatted to one of the show 's host and had that strange effect that charismatic people have on crowds - she energised the room . <p> The thing that Ardern had been most afraid of - taking up the mantle of leadership and had so vociferously vowed she was n't interested in , she seemed to be assuming effortlessly . It was as if she had studied and absorbed all the Helen Clark tapes and with no blood on her hands from a clean takeover was giving women from every political hue an excuse to vote Ardern . <p> Right before our eyes , Ardern had gone from being the Infant Phenomenon to an Adult Phenomenon , taking the wind out of Peters and Metiria Turei 's inflated sails . For the Greens , co-leader James Shaw is left holding Turei 's DPB baby and trying to defend her posturing as some kind of saint for social justice . <p> Turei made a risky radical leap to the Left and took the Greens away from the comfort of climate change to the shallows of social justice . If Shaw and the party were ruthless enough , they would look at what has energised Labour and brought it back from the dead , and would pluck an earnest female from the ranks to make her the new co-leader . <p> I 'm thinking Chloe Swarbrick . With her hipster/preppie dress style , Auckland mayoral race cred and straightforward way of drilling down to the heart of the matter , she is the new Infant Phenomenon in waiting .
