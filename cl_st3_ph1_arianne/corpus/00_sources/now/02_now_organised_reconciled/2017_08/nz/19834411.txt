<h> See the Inconvenient Truth sequel for free , in cinema <p> An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power is out later this month Credits : Paramount Pictures <p> In what 's being called a first for New Zealand , thousands of tickets to an upcoming documentary are being handed out for free . <p> An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power comes a decade after the original Al Gore film that significantly raised public awareness of the perils of climate change . <p> Z Energy has partnered with Paramount Pictures to put on the free sessions for Kiwis , with 3000 being given away to sessions in 24 cinemas across New Zealand . <p> How to score the tickets and where the free sessions will be held will be announced on August 7 via movietimes.nz . <p> " Z believes climate change is one of the greatest problems facing our world and we all have a role - companies , governments and individuals alike - to play in solving it , starting in our own backyards , " says Z Chief Executive Mike Bennetts . <p> An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power will released in cinemas on August 24.
