<p> Dane Coles ruled out of Bledisloe Cup test with a head knock ... more soon <h> National announce new electric vehicle target for government fleet <p> Last updated 16:21 , August 12 2017 <p> MAARTEN HOLL/STUFF <p> Bill English pledges to roll out electric cars <p> The National Party has pledged one in three cars in the Government 's fleet will be electric by 2021 . <p> The announcement was made by Prime Minister Bill English in front of a charging station outside the The Dowse Museum in Lower Hutt on Saturday . <p> Currently the Government has a fleet of about 15,500 cars , and Transport Minister Simon Bridges said the transition will help achieve the " ambitious target of having 64,000 electric vehicles in New Zealand by 2021 " . <p> Bill English pledges to roll out electric cars <p> " Electric vehicles purchased in the Government fleet will flow through to the second hand market , which is essential to increasing uptake and incentivising more charging stations , " said Bridges . <p> The change in fuel costs mean the shift should come without any significant cost to the Government , he said . <p> BRAD FLAHIVE <p> National supporters turned out in Lower Hutt to hear the announcement . <p> " On average an electric vehicle is the equivalent to buying petrol at 30 cents a litre , compared to petrol which is about $2 . " <p> The Prime Minister shared with the crowd that had gathered his " light bulb moment " when he first got into an electric car on a visit to Australia . <p> " One thing that struck me is how quiet they are , but also how powerful they are , " English said . <p> " There 's no doubt electric cars are part of New Zealand 's future , which make a load of sense because we have an abundance of renewable energy . " <p> With over 85 per cent of the country 's electricity generated from renewable sources , and the Government 's spokeswoman for Climate Change Paula Bennett says the " emission reduction benefits of electric vehicles in New Zealand are greater than in other countries " . <p> " Driving a fully electric vehicle results in 80 per cent less emissions than driving petrol vehicles . Transport makes up around 17 per cent of New Zealand 's emissions so this is an important part of our work to reduce emissions in the transport sector . " <p> More key points from National 's electric vehicle scheme <p> * Road user charges on light and heavy class electric vehicles are exempt until each class make up 2 per cent of its vehicle fleet . * A $6 million annual contestable fund encouraging to encourage innovation for low emission vehicle projects . * Changes to the Land Transport Act will allow electric vehicles in bus lanes and high-occupancy lanes on the State Highway network . * A review of ACC levies relating to plug-in hybrid electric vehicles .
