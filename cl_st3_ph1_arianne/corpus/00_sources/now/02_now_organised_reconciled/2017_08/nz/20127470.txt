<h> Meet the candidates forums to be held in Coromandel electorate <p> Voters can put their questions to Coromandel electoral candidates at forums planned around the district . <p> Grey Power Waihi is hosting a meet the candidates forum in Waihi at 10am on August 25 at the Waihi Baptist Church , corner of Kenny and Gilmour Streets . <p> Members of the public are invited to the meeting , which will include NZ First candidate Anne-Marie Andrews , National 's Scott Simpson , Scott Summerfield , of the Green Party , and Labour 's Nathaniel Blomfield . <p> Peninsula residents concerned about the impact of climate change , coastal erosion , and sea level rise are invited to another meet the candidates forum at the Civic Centre , Thames , on September 7 , at 7pm . <p> Spokesperson Mark Skelding said T3-TransitionTownThames organised the meeting in response to the growing awareness of erosion and inundation risk around the Coromandel and Hauraki Plains , how vulnerable communities and low-lying areas would be affected , and what the local agricultural sector could expect if the government 's climate change projections came to pass .
