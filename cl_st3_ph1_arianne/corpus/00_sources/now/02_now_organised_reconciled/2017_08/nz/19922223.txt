<h> Greens in crisis as Labour rockets in poll <h> The Greens are in disarray with the resignation of co-leader Metiria Turei just as a new opinion poll shows Labour , its partner party , surging up the ratings . <p> 9 August 2017 <p> Metiria Turei has resigned as co-leader of the Greens , dealing her party another blow just as an opinion poll shows Labour surging up the ratings . <p> The Greens were trying to get their election campaign back on track after two senior MPs , Kennedy Graham and David Clendon , on Tuesday withdrew from the caucus . <p> They wanted Ms Turei to stand down because of her admission that she committed benefit fraud in the 1990s , but she insisted on staying . <p> All that changed around 5pm on Wednesday when Ms Turei and the party 's other co-leader , James Shaw , called a media conference . <p> " I knew that by telling my personal story , it would help people hear and understand the reality of poverty , " Ms Turei said . <p> " And that has happened - thousands of people have contacted the Green Party with their stories , and many have come forward to tell these in the media as well . <p> " I also knew that it would open the way for people to criticise me - and I knew the risks of that - but the intensity of those attacks has become too much for my family , and they are now getting in the way of our ability to communicate our solutions - not just for poverty , but for water , climate change and the environment . " <p> The poll showed the Greens taking a hammering - down 4.7 points to 8.3 per cent - but Ms Turei said it was n't a factor in her decision to stand down . <p> It also showed Labour up a stunning nine points to 33.1 per cent and the party 's new leader Jacinda Ardern , snapping at Bill English 's heels as preferred prime minister . <p> She was on 26.3 per cent compared with Mr English 's 27.7 . <p> NZ First was down in the poll , losing 3.8 points to be on 8.3 per cent - another casualty of Labour 's big surge . <p> Mr Shaw will lead the Greens into the election as its only leader , with a new co-leader chosen post-election . <p> Dr Graham and Mr Clendon wo n't be returning to caucus . <p> Ms Turei has been on a downward track since making her admission , which she says she did to highlight the plight of beneficiaries . <p> While gaining some sympathy within parliament , she has also been fiercely criticised and last week Labour leader Jacinda Ardern said she would n't have Ms Turei in her cabinet if Labour won the election .
