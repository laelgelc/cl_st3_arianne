<h> SC seeks clarification on Centre 's notification on cattle sale <p> The Supreme Court on 4 August sought clarification on the Centre 's notification on sale of cattle in market asking if notification was placed before the Parliament . <p> A bench of the Apex Court , headed by Chief Justice of India ( CJI ) Jagdish Singh Khehar and also comprising Justice D. Y. Chandrachud , asked if the said Union of India 's notification was placed before the Parliament or not . <p> Earlier , the top court vacation bench , headed by Justice Sanjay Kishan Kaul and comprising Justice R.K. Agarwal , had issued a notice to the Centre on hearing the Public Interest Litigation ( PIL ) filed by the Hyderabad-based NGO on the same issue . <p> The petitioner NGO had moved the apex court challenging the Centre 's notification that bans sale and purchase of cattle from animal markets for slaughter , a move that received flak since its announcement . <p> The petition was filed by a Hyderabad-based lawyer , Fahim Qureshi , stating that the order was discriminatory and unconstitutional , as it prevented cattle traders from earning their livelihood . <p> On 25 May , the Centre , through an order imposed a ban on the sale of cattle , including cows , for slaughter and restricted cattle trade solely to farm owners . <p> Minister for Environment , Forest and Climate Change Harsh Vardhan had ordered that the ministry has notified the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals ( Regulation of Livestock Markets ) Rules , 2017 to ensure that the sale of cattle is not meant for slaughter purposes . <p> Regulating animal trade is a state business , but animal welfare is a central subject , thereby providing the window for the ministry to notify the rule . <p> In lieu of this , there was widespread opposition of the order , with many states openly denying accepting the notification .
