<h> Science Express to halt at Habibganj station from 7-9 August <p> With an aim to make youngsters aware about climate change and environment conservation , the Science Express Climate Action Special Exhibition train will be stationed at the Habibganj station here from 7-9 August . <p> The train was flagged-off from New Delhi 's Safdarjang station on 17 February and it will terminate at Gandhi Nagar station on 8 September , a West Central Railway ( WCR ) spokesman said on 5 August . <p> The train has 13 coaches , each exhibiting different aspects of climate change , he said , adding that the exhibition will be opened from 10 am to 6 pm and has no entry fee . <p> From Habibganj station , it will proceed to Bina where it will be stationed from August 10-12 . <p> The train was originally introduced as Science Express in 2007 and so far 1.70 crore people visited it to aware themselves about the issues concerning environment and climate change .
