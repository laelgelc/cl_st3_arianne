In that memo , he has given a detailed list of what he called possible " non-bias " causes for the under-representation of women in the industry . He argued that the company 's left-leaning workplace culture prevents honest discussion of the issue . The document , titled " Google 's Ideological Echo Chamber , " was posted by Gizmodo . <p> Danielle Brown , the aforementioned new VP and Chief Diversity &amp; Inclusion Officer , People Operations at Google , in response to this memo , said that the worker has " advanced incorrect assumptions about gender .
