<h> US to join climate talks despite planned withdrawal from Paris accord <p> WASHINGTON ( NYTIMES ) - The White House formally notified the United Nations on Friday ( Aug 4 ) that it intends to abandon the Paris agreement on climate change , but remains open to " re-engaging " on the accord . <p> The United States will participate in UN climate negotiations later this year , despite its planned withdrawal , according to the administration 's statement of intent . <p> The letter has no legal weight and does not set in motion the United States ' departure from the pact of nearly 200 nations to curb planet-warming greenhouse gas emissions . Rather , it is a political document that affirms President Donald Trump 's declaration in June that the Paris agreement is a bad deal for the United States . <p> " As the President indicated in his June 1 announcement and subsequently , he is open to re-engaging in the Paris Agreement if the US can identify terms that are more favorable to the United States , its businesses , its workers , its people , and its taxpayers , " the State Department statement said . <h> Get The Straits Times newsletters in your inbox <p> When Trump revealed his decision to withdraw from the accord , he suggested the US might be willing to stay if he could " make a deal that 's fair " . <p> President Emmanuel Macron of France , after inviting Trump to Paris , said he believed he had convinced the US President to stay in the climate accord . <p> " We talked in detail about what could enable him to come back into the Paris accords , " Macron told reporters . <p> According to the White House announcement , the United States will continue to participate in UN climate discussions including one in Bonn , Germany , in November aimed at fleshing out the Paris agreement . <p> Continued American presence at such talks is to " protect US interests and ensure all future policy options remain open to the administration , " the statement says . <p> The announcement adds that the United States " supports a balanced approach to climate policy that lowers emissions while promoting economic growth an ensuring energy security . " <p> In language that mirrors wording the White House team was able to insert in Group of 20 discussions , it calls for working with other nations to " help them access and use fossil fuels more cleanly and efficiently .
