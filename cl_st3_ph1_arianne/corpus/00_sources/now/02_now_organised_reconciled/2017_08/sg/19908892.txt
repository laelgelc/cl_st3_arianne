<h> Dutch students grow their own biodegradable car <p> EINDHOVEN , NETHERLANDS ( Reuters ) - What 's made of sugar , can carry four people and travel at 80km per hour ? A biodegradable car , whose inventors say could be the next step in environmentally friendly motoring . <p> The lightweight electric car , created by students in the Netherlands , is made of a resin derived from sugar beets and covered with sheets of Dutch-grown flax . <p> " Only the wheels and suspension systems are not yet of bio-based materials , " said Yanic van Riel , one of the developers from the TU/Ecomotive team at the Eindhoven University of Technology . <p> The structure of the car they have called Lina has a similar strength-weight ratio to that of fibreglass and weighs only 310 kg ( about 684 pounds ) . <h> Get The Straits Times newsletters in your inbox <p> But the prototype has not yet passed crash tests , because the material " will not bend like metal , but break " , said the team 's leader Noud van de Gevel . <p> Demands to reduce air pollution and tackle climate change have pushed auto companies towards alternative designs , but most are still require a great deal of energy to make .
