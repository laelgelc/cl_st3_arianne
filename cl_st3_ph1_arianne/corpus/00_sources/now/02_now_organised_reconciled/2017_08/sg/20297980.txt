<p> Speaking to the media at the FICCI Business and Climate Summit , Goyal reiterated that people possessing black money would not be spared . <p> " I have no doubts that demonetisation has been a success . I 'd like to congratulate Modiji for attacking black money the way they did and making it clear that this government wo n't spare anyone , who is possessing black money . It took us three years to get rid of this black money , " he said . <p> He stressed that demonetisation has helped people in more than one way . <p> " A number of shell companies have been revealed . If the money is in the bank , it is a part of the economy and we can use it for the welfare of the people , " Goyal said . <p> Speaking on demonetisation 's impact on the GST , he said that nearly 66 per cent people have filed their GST returns , which is more than the limit the government had set . <p> He also pointed to the fact that the circulation of fake currencies is under the radar now and it would be stopped soon . <p> Goyal launched a scathing attack on the Congress and accused them of corruption and impoverishing the country . <p> Goyal said , " Nobody has a better idea about black money than the Congress . We were known to be a corrupt nation at first . They have been involved in numerous scams across the years . " <p> The third annual Business and Climate Summit is underway in New Delhi , India . The summit aims to showcase business leadership in addressing climate change and highlight how business can help governments achieve climate objectives .
