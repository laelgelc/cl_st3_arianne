<h> Millennials ' driving sustainable investment growth ' <p> A younger generation of investors are leading the growth in sustainable investing . <p> According to a new survey of 1,000 investors by Morgan Stanley , three quarters of individual investors and 86% of millennial investors said they were interested in sustainable investing . <p> While the figures are consistent with figures from 2015 , which registered 71% and 84% respectively , the level of interest among millennials jumped from 28% who were " very interested " during that period to 38% this year . <p> A total of 75% of millennials said their investments can influence climate change while 84% said their investments can help people out of poverty . <p> The survey also found 59% of millennials believe sustainable investing involves a financial trade-off , yet 61% have taken at least one sustainability-oriented investment action in the last year . <p> A majority ( 90% ) of millennials said they want sustainable options as part of their plans . <p> Women of all ages are also said to be showing more interest in sustainable investment compared to men , with 84% vs 67% . However , when it came to action , men were just as likely to have integrated sustainability into their investment decisions . <p> Around 71% of investors polled agreed good social , environmental and governance practices can potentially lead to higher profitability and possibly better long term investments .
