stated commitment to the Paris agreement . <p> The bank released the statement , saying it was " reaffirming our support for a responsible global transition to a net zero emissions economy by 2050 " . groups , who have attacked the bank over its previous failure to rule out financing the Adani coalmine , and its status as the country 's biggest lender to Australia 's fossil fuel industry in 2016 . <p> The bank announced it would decrease the greenhouse gas pollution caused by its lending , as well as by its own business activity , but it did n't provide details of how it would do so , or provide targets . <p> " Commonwealth Bank is not even pretending to make an effort on climate change with the position statement released today , " said Julien Vincent , the executive director of the environmental campaign group Market Forces . <p> He said they would lodge a shareholder resolution against the Commonwealth Bank on Wednesday , which would embed in the bank 's constitution its stated commitment to keeping global warming at less than 2C and force it to implement that commitment in the bank 's management . <p> " Unlike the bank 's peers in Australia and overseas that are taking concrete steps to avoid the most carbon intensive sectors , Commonwealth Bank clearly lacks either the interest or competency to fulfil its commitment to help hold global warming below two degrees , " Vincent said . <p> Vincent said the climate statement would allow the Commonwealth Bank to assist the fossil fuel industry to expand in Australia . <p> Unlike some recent announcements by other big four banks in Australia , the Commonwealth Bank did not put any limits on the sort of coal projects or coal-fired power stations the bank might lend to , making them the only one of the " big four " banks to not put some restrictions on lending to coal . <p> " We ca n't allow a situation to continue where Australia 's biggest company continues to finance a massive fossil fuel industry expansion while feigning interest in a safe climate future , " Vincent said . " That 's why we 're moving a resolution that would embed climate change risk management into the heart of Commonwealth Bank . " <p> The bank 's statement said it would finance $15bn of " low carbon " projects by 2025 .
