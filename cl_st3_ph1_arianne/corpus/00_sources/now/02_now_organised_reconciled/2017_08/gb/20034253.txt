<h> Greens throw down political gauntlet of zero emissions by 2040 <p> Green MSPs today have challenged Holyrood 's four other parties to include a target of zero emissions by 2040 in the forthcoming Climate Bill . <p> Current targets under the 2009 Climate Act are for greenhouse gas emissions to be reduced by 80% by 2050 . <p> The Scottish Government proposes increasing this to 90% but does not propose bringing forward the target date , despite the urgency of the situation . <p> The Greens say a bolder target of net zero ten years sooner will drive innovation and job creation in transport , energy and agriculture -- the biggest sources of emissions -- and other European countries such as France and Sweden have already committed to becoming zero-carbon . <p> Mark Ruskell MSP , Climate Change spokesperson for the Scottish Greens , said : " To keep the world safe from climate change , nations need to follow the science and end the fossil fuel age by 2040 . Scotland can reap the jobs benefits today if we set a clear target for a 100 per cent phase out of fossils , sending the strongest signal to drive innovation in sectors from energy to transport and agriculture . <p> " The Scottish Government is desperate for a policy re-set . By setting out a bold target for 2030 and a Zero Carbon Target for 2040 , it would leave a golden legacy for future generations . <p> " Greens in Government in Sweden have already set a Zero Carbon Target . We should join them in seizing this economic opportunity today . "
