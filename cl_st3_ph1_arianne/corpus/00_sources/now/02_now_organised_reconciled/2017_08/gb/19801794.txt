<h> Taking on Adani is not just about climate change . It 's taking back power from corporate plutocracy <p> The Adani Group almost seems like a crudely drawn corporate villain . And we should be thankful for the chance to reclaim the fight <p> Sebastian Job is honorary associate in the department of anthropology at the University of Sydney <p> " When our children , or simply our slightly older selves , ask us if we tried to salvage a liveable world for them , they are not going to be content with a bland ' what did you do ? ' " Photograph : Tracey Nearmy/AAP <p> PR problems . That 's what Adani 's proposed Carmichael coalmine in Queensland 's Galilee Basin has been having . Maybe they need a theme song . Here , with a nod to the Man in Black , is a line to get them started : Well we shot a reef in Queensland , just to watch it die . <p> Those driving the Carmichael mine have had plenty of time to learn the climate change basics . They know coal has the highest carbon dioxide content of all fossil fuels . They know a large majority of climate scientists have identified rising concentrations of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere as the major driver of global warming in the modern era . They know that extracting the bulk of existing global fossil fuel reserves is considered , by everyone from doctors to military commanders and the Davos economic elite , to spell calamity for human life on Earth . <p> Australian politicians will also have noticed that corals , like glaciers and the carbon and methane trapping permafrost , are highly temperature sensitive . So they know coral bleaching is another neon warning sign that the global climate catastrophe is underway . <p> Except , of course , that the warning sign is no longer neon . It is bone white . Courtesy of lethal coral bleaching the Great Barrier Reef is being redeveloped as a 2,300 kilometer long offshore cemetery . <p> To add to the melancholy picture , public money used to build road and rail infrastructure for the Carmichael mine are supposed to open up the basin to many more mines , while the whole operation will be serviced by expanded coal ports cutting like centurions ' lances into the side of the reef . <p> Queensland : beautiful one day , bone yard the next . <p> Any halfway rational government would be quarantining the reef from further insults and mobilising the population for an emergency transition away from hydrocarbons . An all-hands-on-deck , bring-everybody-along effort . Not just for domestic electricity production , but for our exports . <p> Instead , both major parties at federal and state levels are supporting the establishment of a coal mine with a projected lifespan of sixty years . <p> That alone says everything . <p> " Father , forgive them , " Jesus is supposed to have cried from the cross , " for they know not what they do . " In fact , as to the risk of cooking the planet , the only thing we will never be able to say is : " We did n't know . " <p> But the main cast of politicians , fossil CEOs , and all their bagmen keep finding ways " not to know . " <p> Or , to be more charitable , they know about as much about the mind-boggling complexities of global oceanography , geology , atmospheric thermodynamics and paleoclimatology as the rest of us non-experts , but they elect to hear what they want to hear , dignifying their opportunism as " skepticism . " <p> This is why we should thank Adani . Like a corporate villain so crudely drawn one might suspect it of being secretly in the pay of the green movement , the Adani Group has arrived on the scene to push our responsibility for our actions into our faces . This is the existential crossroads . This is where the gun is clearly in our hands . <p> So what is to be done ? <p> As soon as the question is posed it becomes obvious it needs refining . The first thing to note is that " we " are split . The gun is not really in our hands , it is in their hands . That is , it is in the hands of the fossil bloc , otherwise known as the " to hell with the rest of you " fossil fuel military industrial financial media bloc . <p> If global warming is to be seriously addressed then it will not be enough to speak " truth to power , " as the new documentary by Al Gore has it . The gun of state power has to be wrested from the hands of the fossil bloc . This is the great battle on our doorstep . <p> When our children , or simply our slightly older selves , ask us if we tried to salvage a liveable world for them , they are not going to be content with a bland " what did you do ? " They are going to ask us very pointedly why we dawdled so long before throwing out this criminally irresponsible band of ecocidal know-nothings . <p> It will be a good question . And it wo n't be enough to say we trusted in the sainted Elon Musk . We wo n't get anywhere without clean energy , but new technologies and cheaper prices for renewables are not going to win the struggle for us . <p> Since at least the 1980s ExxonMobil , BP , Chevron , ConocoPhillips , Peabody Energy , Shell and other diehard members of the fossil bloc have been fighting a covert war against activists , scientists and policy makers to stop action on climate change . <p> Now , after decades of windbaggery from the global political class , the transition away from hydrocarbons has become a desperate affair . We might already be doomed . Or we might make it . As for the present , two things are clear : the fossil bloc are not leaders , they are undertakers ; and if we do n't take them down , they will take everyone down . <p> Maybe it is time to turn some of that anxiety about the dark storms ahead into open revolt against what is being done to us now . <p> It is not that pleading with the powerful is useless . As the **26;1552;TOOLONG campaign is showing , major organisations can change direction . And as 350. org 's Bill McKibben put it , while the recent Paris climate agreement " did n't save the planet , it may have saved the chance to save it . " <p> Nonetheless , from the oil and gas fields of the war-ravaged Middle East , to Queensland 's Galilee Basin , to the dozen other fossil fuel projects worldwide detailed in Ecofys ' Point of No Return report , the fossil bloc are going to keep blocking the way for as long as we let them . <p> In this context speaking truth to power is really a theatre where we become bold enough to speak truth to each other . Pressuring the powerful is a school where we learn the meaning of taking back power . <p> This ca n't come a moment too soon . The great majority of us have been rendered complicit less by our petrol-fueled cars and plastic bags than by political inertia . If we ca n't even try to dispossess those who would dispossess us of our future , then , by default , the gun does end up in all our hands , and their casual indifference to mass deathbecomes our collective suicide . <h> Adani is not just another coalmine , it is a turning point for the nation Hamish McKenzie <p> Read more <p> And this , paradoxically enough , is the second reason we should thank Adani : we need this fight . It is in fights like these that we tone up , that we emancipate ourselves from waiting . <p> The Adani mine , let us recall , has it all . Not just climate change and damage to the reef , but habitat destruction , erosion of Indigenous land rights , ground water exhaustion and pollution , increased drought risk for local farmlands , loss of coastal employment , and long term health effects . And that 's without mentioning falling demand for coal and waste of taxpayers ' money that could be going on more toll roads and Joint Strike Fighters . <p> To take on Adani is not just to take on climate change . It is to take on the insane irresponsibility of the corporate plutocracy.
