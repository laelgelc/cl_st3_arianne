<h> Christian Comment with Will Mansell of The Grace Network CIC <p> THE events taking place in Charlottesville , America and the ever growing stream of words from North Korea seem to have given a sense the world is falling apart , going backwards towards old ideologies and new violence . <p> People , often Christians , tell us that the world is going to get worse and then end . <p> It might be climate change , might be nuclear war or maybe the rapture but the future is always painted as bleak and painful moving forward . <p> However , my faith and knowledge of Church history tells me something different . <p> As much as our society appears to be falling apart at times , what I really see is the same human behaviours across history ( described throughout the bible as well as seen in history ) simply done with more cameras to tell me about them . <p> 200 years ago I simply would n't have been aware of impending wars from across the world as the news would n't tell me . <p> 100 years ago people were just as racist but did n't have an iPhone to spread hate . <p> The world is n't getting worse humans are just the same . <p> This may not sound like much hope but in reality it is . <p> Reading the words of Jesus and reflecting on his works , I do n't have a sense of doom but instead a chance of renewal . <p> The Church grew from nothing in AD 30 to a world changing force 400 years later . <p> It went on to found universities , schools , hospitals , anti-poverty programs . <p> It created rules to help reduce violence in war , spoke out first against slavery , led the initial work of Rev Martin Luther King and others to bring equal rights in America . <p> At a time of chaos we need to remember that through the church renewal of society can arise . <p> We need to return to that mantle and get involved in the problems , history tells us we can lead the charge against human nature to build a better world . <p> The more dark the world gets the more it needs the
