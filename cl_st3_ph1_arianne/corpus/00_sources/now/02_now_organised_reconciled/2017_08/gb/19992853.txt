<h> Al Gore on the dangers of climate change in his new film , An Inconvenient Sequel <h> Europe has been scorching recently . Do these events make the climate change debate clearer ? <p> No question about it . Mother Nature has become by far the most powerful voice in this discussion . The climate-related extremes are far more common , far more destructive , and even people who do n't want to use the phrase ' global warming ' are saying : ' This is pretty weird and if it 's going to keep going like this , we need to do something about it . ' <h> How have you coped with climate change deniers attacking you personally ? <p> It is not at all unusual for those who are angered by a particular message to attack the messenger . I 'm not surprised about that . Living in the world of politics as long as I did , I developed a thicker skin . <h> What was your reaction when Trump pulled the US out of the Paris Agreement on climate change ? <p> I thought he would come to his senses but I was wrong . And when I heard his announcement on June 1 , I was very apprehensive that other countries would use his decision as an excuse to pull out . But the very next day , I was immensely gratified that the entire rest of the world doubled their commitment to the Paris Agreement , as if to say : ' We 'll show you , Mr Trump . ' <p> Disappointing : Donald Trump <h> What gives you the energy to continue this campaign ? <p> The hope that has now emerged gives me energy . I spent summers on a farm , learning about our connection to the land with my parents . And my parents also gave my sister and me a deep appreciation for the potential in self-government to make the world a better place . I never imagined this would become a kind of mission for me . <h> You 're a father of four . Are you worried about the world your children will be left in ? <p> Yes , yes , yes . I have four grandchildren . And another one on the way ! I think constantly of what world they will inherit and there is a part of the movie in which I say , ' When the next generation inherits the world we bequeath to them , if they find all of these horrible consequences the scientists predicted and we did not act , they 'd be justified in looking back and having serious questions about what we were doing and what we were thinking . ' <h> Have your children taken up green issues ? <p> Yes , my older daughter Karenna , who is in the movie , is the head for Centre For Earth Ethics at Union Theological Seminary in New York City and does wonderful work and my son Albert works for an electric car company . My youngest daughter Sarah is a member of the Indivisible Movement and a blogger who focuses on climate , among other things . My second oldest daughter Kristin is a screenwriter who has put climate themes in her scripts . <p> Screen break : Spotlight star Liev Schreiber <h> Your father was a politician . Did that mean you were automatically going to be one ? <p> Yes and no . When I was a little boy , I did want to do what my father did . But then I turned away from it , because of the examples of Lyndon Johnson and Richard Nixon and the lies that led America into the Vietnam War . I became a journalist for five years . But then I turned back towards it when I was writing about city government and state government in Tennessee . I began to think : ' Maybe I could do this well myself . ' Then an opportunity arose . I came back to it on my own terms . <h> Who were your heroes ? <p> My father and mother were real heroes . And my environmental hero was Roger Revelle , who I learned from in college . He was the first scientist to measure CO2 in the Earth 's atmosphere and a real giant of climate science . The premier prize in climate science is the Revelle Prize . He opened my eyes to this issue in the 1960s . <h> Are you a movie lover ? <p> I am . I work a lot so I sometimes catch up with movies on Apple TV and Netflix . I saw Arrival and Spotlight . I love to go to the movies . <h> An Inconvenient Sequel focuses on you even more than the last one . Did you ever feel self-conscious ? <p> There were points where I did think to myself : ' Why are they filming this ? ' But I learned to have great respect for their work ethic . It 's good that I did not have control over the editing of content because in my mind , I 'm younger and thinner and with darker hair ! And much of the movie would never have made it to the screen !
