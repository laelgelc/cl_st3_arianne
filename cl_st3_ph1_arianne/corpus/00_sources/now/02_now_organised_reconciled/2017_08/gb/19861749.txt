<h> Nimra on ... climate change <p> Climate change and global warming are two phenomena that will effect each and every one of us if we do n't act globally to make quantifiable changes to help reduce their effects . <p> The Met Office has warned the UK that in light of increasing global warming , the UK could face heavy rainfall and ' unprecedented ' flooding this coming winter . <p> According to the Met Office , its research has shown that there is a one in three chance of rainfall records for England and Wales being broken this winter . <p> With heavy rainfall and extensive damage caused by flooding in 2014 , the government stressed the importance of a series of tests to assess the risks of flooding from rivers and seas . <p> However , these tests did not give appropriate consideration to surface flooding , which effects homes and businesses . <p> With the Met Office 's recent warning , the pressure is now on the government to review these tests on surface flooding , in order to prevent widespread damage to houses and businesses . <p> " It should be an urgent priority for the Environment Secretary to re-open the National Flood Resilience Review with the aim of improving the UK 's preparedness against surface water flooding caused by heavy rainfall , the risks of which are clearly spelled out in this paper , " said Bob Ward , from the Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change . <h> Ipsoregulated <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
