<h> There was strong opposition to the creation of a French First Lady role . <p> BRIGITTE MACRON , WIFE of French President Emmanuel Macron , is to represent France and carry out charity work without the official status of First Lady , following opposition to giving her this title , the presidency announced today . <p> A proposal to create a new First Lady position , made by Macron during his campaign earlier this year , has been shelved , but the presidency had promised to clarify her position and the resources at her disposal . <p> The former school teacher will have two presidential advisors and her own cabinet , paid for from Macron 's budget , and will focus on working with charities that help children and those with disabilities . <p> She will also " represent France at her husband 's side during international summits and meetings " and work with the partners of other global leaders to highlight work to combat climate change or domestic violence . <p> An online petition against creating an official First Lady role -- which would have required a change in the law or constitution -- has garnered more than 300,000 signatures this month . <p> Opposition <p> An opinion poll earlier this month found that a majority of French people were also opposed to the creation of the role , even though Brigitte Macron is a popular figure . <p> Brigitte Macron said in an interview with Elle magazine last month that she would be an unofficial First Lady in a similar way to previous presidential spouses . <p> " Like all of those before me , I will take on my public role , but the French people will know the resources at my disposal , " said the 64-year-old . <p> She accompanied the president to a G7 summit in Italy and was also by his side when he welcomed US President Donald Trump to Paris in July . <p> Macron 's office had promised a " transparency charter " which would make clear how much her role would cost . <p> Today 's statement confirmed that she would not be paid , but did not reveal the overall bill or the number of staff at her disposal . <p> President Macron has seen a mixed reaction to the start of his five-year term , which has seen his
