<p> Jellyfish numbers have reached such a worrying high in the Mediterranean that one marine biologist is urging people to start eating them in order to lessen the infestation . <p> Offshore windfarms and oil and gas platforms might be to blame , as they inadvertently provide an ideal habitat for the creatures to thrive , according to a recent study conducted by Slovenian and Portuguese scientists . <p> One particularly hazardous breed , the mauve stinger , is rearing its tentacles more frequently around Britain in particular . Their sting is classified as one of the worst , often causing a burning sensation , intense pain and skin rashes . <p> Professor Silvio Grecio -- one of Italy 's most esteemed marine biologists -- has been experimenting with ways to make the harmful sea creature appetising . <p> " Jellyfish remind me of oysters , " he told the BBC . " When you eat them you experience an explosion of the sea on your tongue . They are , after all , 90pc seawater , " he explained . <p> Due to the creature 's natural salty taste , there 's no need to season it at all . <p> The number of jellyfish in the Mediterranean has risen by 400 per cent in the last 13 years due to pollution and climate change . <p> It 's a big issue for biodiversity , explained Grecio , who insists the jellyfish are a highly opportunistic species that quickly conquer empty spaces in the sea and are consequently inhibiting marine food chains . <p> " Now man must be the new predator of jellyfish , " he said . <p> His ethos is part of a campaign that he 's been promoting at the sustainable fishing Slow Fish festival , which takes place every two years in Genoa . <p> In order to encourage people to eat it , Grecio is focusing on highlighting the nutritional benefits of the species , as jellyfish are rich in protein and collagen and also happen to be fat-free and fairly low in calories . <p> It 's most commonly cooked with Asian flavourings like soy and sesame oil . <p> One popular method that Grecio has been using to cook the sea creature is by deep frying it to create a tempura-meets-calamari dish . <p> As most attendees at Slow Fish will advocate , if you ca n't beat ' em , eat ' em . <h> Related Content <h> Your Comments <p> Comments that are judged to be defamatory , abusive or in bad taste are not acceptable and contributors who consistently fall below certain criteria will be permanently blacklisted .
