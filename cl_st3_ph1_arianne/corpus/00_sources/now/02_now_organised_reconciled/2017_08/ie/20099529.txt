<h> A Savage DJ gig at Limerick 's Kasbah with Fay Milton <p> Fay Milton , drummer with top English rock band Savages , is coming to Limerick for a much anticipated DJ set <p> FAY Milton , drummer with top English rock band Savages , is coming to Limerick for a much anticipated DJ set . <p> The gig , at the Kasbah Social Club on September 16 , will be her only Irish appearance and marks an exclusive for the venue , booker Seoda Shows and the Popular Music and Popular Culture Research Cluster at UL , who will earlier host Fay as a guest speaker . <p> Savages have had two Mercury Prize nominated albums and in announcing the show , booker John Hennessy said : " Dolan 's are hugely excited to host Fay Milton 's only Irish show . In addition to her work with Savages she has been attracting huge attention in the UK as a DJ . <p> " She runs the Venus Fly Trap Club Night in London , a party celebrating music by women . This is a show not to be missed . " <p> Professor Eoin Devereux of UL said : " We are really excited about hosting Fay Milton . She is a renowned drummer , DJ , composer and film maker . As a filmmaker , Fay has made documentary films with Kieran Hebden , Mystery Jets , The Mighty Boosh and currently produces an interview series on climate change called Very Important Things . Her band Savages have produced two of the most important albums in recent music history .
