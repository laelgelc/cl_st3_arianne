<p> More than 200 people were killed when a mudslide struck the outskirts of Sierra Leone 's capital Freetown on Monday , sweeping away homes . <p> The Red Cross said at least 205 bodies had been taken to the central morgue in Freetown . Police and military personnel were at the scene in the mountain town of Regent searching for people trapped in the debris . <p> The death toll is expected to rise as more bodies are recovered , Red Cross spokesman Abu Bakarr Tarawallie said . <p> Vice President Victor Foh told Reuters at the scene : " It is likely that hundreds are lying dead underneath the rubble . " He said a number of illegal buildings had been erected in the area . <p> " The disaster is so serious that I myself feel broken , " he said . " We 're trying to cordon the area . Evacuate the people . " <p> Standing in the rain , people cried and gestured toward a muddy hillside where dozens of houses once stood , a Reuters witness said . <p> " We were asleep when we heard the noise of one of the walls falling down . By the time we got up water was flowing in and the whole house was flooded , " said 36-year-old Salimatu Bangura . <p> She escaped , but her brother died , she said . <p> In this image made available by Society 4 Climate Change Communication , Sierra Leone , mud and water cascades down the side on a hill in Freetown , Sierra Leone Monday Aug. 14 , 2017 .
