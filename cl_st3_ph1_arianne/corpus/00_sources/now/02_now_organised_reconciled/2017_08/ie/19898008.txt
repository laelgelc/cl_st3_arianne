<h> I Am A Medical Professional ? <h> Declining sperm counts in the west are " unsurprising " , claims expert <p> Sperm counts among men living in the west of Ireland have plummeted by nearly 60 per cent in just 40 years . <p> Dr Bart Kuczera , a consultant with Beacon CARE Fertility , has said that the results of a recent study , which has attributed this decline to environmental factors , were unsurprising . <p> Commenting on the results , Dr Kuczera said : " Climate change is a massive concern to the human species , but environmental pollution is of major concern to everyone 's individual health , not to mention the cost to a nation in terms of healthcare costs and its contribution to declining fertility . <p> He stressed that the food we eat , cosmetics we use , and even the air we breathe are enveloping us in endocrine disruptive chemicals , examples of which are the pesticides in our fruit and vegetables and disturbing levels of ' particulate matter air pollution ' which are caused especially by traffic and industrial sites . <p> Dr Kuczera continued : " Many studies focus on particular features of health , such as various types of cancer or reproductive function ; declining sperm counts being a relatively easy one to measure . <p> " The positive correlation of such pollution on specific aspects of our reproductive or general health does not even consider the combined effects of these multi-environmental toxins and carcinogens on all the delicate systems and organs that make us function . <p> " If we then include the self-inflicted additional damage caused through smoking , these results really do not come as a surprise . Indeed , the above factors must be contributing to the decline in the rate of increase of life expectancy never mind falling sperm counts " , concluded Dr Kuczera.
