<h> We talk to Al Gore about An Inconvenient Sequel , Irish climate change deniers and running again <p> It 's not very often we get an opportunity to speak to someone who has dedicated their lives to changing the world for the better . <p> Al Gore was Vice President of America for 8 years under President Bill Clinton . He ran himself against George W. Bush in 2000 and lost , what many believe , to be a controversial election when a recount in Florida was halted as he was within a few hundred votes of Bush . <p> Gore has been on a mission as an Environmental Activist for decades , winning an Oscar for the first documentary , An Inconvenient Truth and being awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for his work . <p> An Inconvenient Sequel is out this Friday , August 18th and our editor Mike Sheridan talks to the former Vice President about climate change , Irish politicians who are climate change deniers , where the hope comes from in more progressive world leaders and much more .
