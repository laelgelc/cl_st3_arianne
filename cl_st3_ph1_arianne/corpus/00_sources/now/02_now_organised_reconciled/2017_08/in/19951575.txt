<h> Diversion of 258 ha of forest land for Kalsa serious : SC <h> 11 Aug 201706:56am IST <h> 11 Aug 201706:56am IST <h> Report byTeam Herald <h> Asks MoEF&amp;CC whether it has received application for forest and environment clearance from Karnataka <p> PANJIM : Taking into the account that about 258 hectares of forest land will be diverted for the Kalsa-Bhandura project in Kankumbi , the Supreme Court said that the matter requires serious consideration by the Union Ministry for Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MoEF&amp;CC ) and Karnataka . <p> Addressing media persons on Thursday , Mhadei Bachao Abhiyan ( MBA ) president Nirmala Sawant said the Apex Court in its latest order directed MoEF&amp;CC to file an affidavit indicating whether any application has been received from Karnataka for forest and environment clearance in relation to the project in the Western Ghats , and if so , the stage of consideration . <p> MBA had approached SC in 2007 challenging Karnataka 's project on environmental grounds and had pointed out that the project is been undertaken without obtaining an EC from MoEF&amp;CC . <p> The Court , in its order , stated that the application by MBA is not at all concerned with the water dispute with regard to the Mhadei River pending before the Tribunal ( Mhadei water dispute tribunal ) . <p> " We may note that the report of the Central Empowered Committee ( CEC ) is to the affect that about 258 hectares of forest land is to be diverted for the project . Therefore , the matter requires some serious consideration , both by the State of Karnataka and MoEF&amp;CC , " the Court stated . <p> Karnataka has said that the project cost is less than Rs 100 crore , but the Abhiyan pointed out that it was worth over Rs 325crore and needed clearance from the MoEF&amp;CC . Solicitor general of India Ranjit Kumar told the court that the project has not been given clearance by the ministry . <p> The matter has been listed for hearing on August 17 , wherein both the authorities have to submit their affidavits . <p> Sawant pointed out that Karnataka informed the Court about almost completing the project , which MBA exposed during the hearing on August 3 through photographic proof . <p> In a fresh affidavit filed earlier this month , Karnataka reiterated that it will not utilise or divert the Mhadei waters without obtaining the necessary clearances.
