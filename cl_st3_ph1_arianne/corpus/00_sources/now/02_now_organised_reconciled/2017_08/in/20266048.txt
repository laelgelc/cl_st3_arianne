<h> Lessons of Doklam <p> The negotiated and peaceful resolution of the military impasse at Doklam between Indian and Chinese military forces has made people in Jammu &amp; Kashmir heave a sigh of relief . Even as there are conflciting claims about the terms of agreement for the disengagement process , it , nevertheless , has eased tensions and diminished the prospects of a military confrontation , that could have disastrous consequences for this region , particularly Jammu &amp; Kashmir . As a state particularly vulnerable to humanitarian , political and economic distress due to such military face-offs , Jammu &amp; Kashmir state , as a place of proximity to any prospective theatre of war that could spill across the borders , has a particular reason to welcome the Doklam disengagement and easing of tensions . With already too much to face from the internal disturbances and LoC hostilities the people of Jammu &amp; Kashmir can not afford the stresses of another military front . The Doklam disengagement also goes to demonstrate the merits of dialogue , diplomacy and peaceful resolution of conflicts between two parties with seemingly irreconcilable positions . And that is something that both New Delhi and Islamabad need to particularly realise , and commit to shun the path of zero-sum one-upmanship . There are , admittedly , strong voices in both the countries that thrive on extreme posturing , hysteria and hate . Such forces can only serve their own short-sighted interests , and not of their people , considering their apathy towards the perpetual pain and suffering that India-Pakistan confrontation is breeding not only in Kashmir but within many sections of their own populations too . Both India and Pakistan today stand at their crucial time of their histories . There are so many examples of nations throughout history that chose short sighted militarism and one-upmanship to peaceful dialogue and flexibility in resolving their conflicts and perished . And then there are the examples of nations that embraced flexibility , forgiveness and accommodation and thrived in peace and wellbeing . India and Pakistan can not afford a war . Both the countries have a big unfinished business of poverty eradication , social justice , job creation , climate change adaptation and , most importantly , contain extremism . Doklam standoff , if not handled with patience and wisdom , could have easily escalated and even spilled to others areas , including Jammu &amp; Kashmir . In spite of the cynicism about the escalation there , it does constitute a success in dialogue and diplomacy . New Delhi and Islamabad must not lose any further time and commit to a result-oriented dialogue towards normalising relations and bringing justice and peace to Kashmir .
