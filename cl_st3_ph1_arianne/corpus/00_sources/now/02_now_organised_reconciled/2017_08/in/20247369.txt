<h> President-elect of UNGA meets PM Modi <p> The need for stronger UN action against terrorism and the reform of the world body were among the issues discussed when President-elect of the UN General Assembly Miroslav Lajcak met Prime Minister Narendra Modi here today . <p> The prime minister congratulated Lajcak on his election to the post and assured him of India 's full and constructive cooperation in discharging his new responsibilities , a PMO statement said . <p> During the meeting , Lajcak shared his priorities for the upcoming 72nd UN General Assembly . <p> They discussed the need for stronger UN action on major global challenges including terrorism , United Nations reform , implementation of Sustainable Development Goals , and climate change , the statement said . <p> India has been pressing the UN to take an early decision on Comprehensive Convention on International Terrorism ( CCIT ) , a proposal which has been pending for nearly two decades . <p> Once adopted , the CCIT would make it binding on all member countries to deny safe havens and funds to terrorists and terror groups .
