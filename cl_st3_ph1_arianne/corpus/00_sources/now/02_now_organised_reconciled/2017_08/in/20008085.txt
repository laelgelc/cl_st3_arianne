<h> Several websites of Pakistan govt hacked on Independence Day <h> The hackers attacked websites of some key ministries of the Pakistan government , <p> Pakistani soldiers carry the national flag prior to be hoisted by President Mamnoon Hussain ( R ) during a ceremony to mark the country 's Independence Day in Islamabad on August 14 , 2017 . ( AFP Photo ) <p> Several websites of the Pakistan government were defaced on Monday allegedly by hackers from India . <p> The hackers attacked websites of some key ministries on the day the country celebrated its 70th Independence Day . <p> The sites hacked included Ministry of Defence , Ministry of Climate Change , Ministry of Inter-provincial coordination , Ministry of Water and Power , Ministry of Information Technology and others . <p> An official of the Ministry of Information Technology said the attack was carried out by a group calling themselves " LuluSec India " . The hackers posted pro-India material on the web pages of hacked websites . <p> Several websites are still not functional . <p> There was no formal reaction by the government so far . <p> It is not the first incident of cyber-attack by hackers targeting the Pakistani government websites . Such attacks have occurred in the past also .
