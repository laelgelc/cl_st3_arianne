<p> In year 2015 a milestone was globally achieved by way of a historic climate pact by 196 countries at Paris . The world fraternity agreed that climate change is " a common concern of humankind . " <p> The agreement that was reached at rightly provided that when the states take action , they must respect , promote and consider their obligations on human rights , right to health , rights of indigenous peoples , local communities , migrants , children , persons with disabilities and people in vulnerable situations . It also obliged the nations to respect the right to development , gender equality , empowerment of women and inter-generational equity . <p> Among some of the significant features of the treaty that sets it apart from earlier agreements in this regard are its long-term plan envisaging limiting the temperature rise till at least 2050 , holding the countries liable in the event of their failure to meet the targets set out for emission reduction and obliging the developed countries to fund technology and emission-mitigation programmes in the developing nations , which creates a room for the developing states to grow in a sustainable way . <p> Two years down the line , has anything changed for better on the environment front in the world ? May be , may be not ! But for certain in our Kashmir things remain to be so as they were in 2015 . The wetlands in Kashmir are fast turning into wastelands . As if our other problems -- political , economic and social -- were not enough to bog us down , we have now this . <p> This has officially been acknowledged that nine Kashmir wetlands , despite being protected legally , have shrunk a great deal over the last 50 years . Though notified in 1945 , demarcation of these wetlands has not been carried out so far . <p> Let us deal with them one by one : Once a glorious 100 acre Mirgund wetland on the Srinagar-Baramulla highway towards the north of the Valley has lost 20 acres to encroachments . <p> Shalbugh , on the border of Srinagar and Ganderbal districts , was 250 acres . Land sharks have already grabbed 12 acres . <p> Haigam in north Kashmir 's Sopore was over 1,812 acres . Nearly 252 have been eaten away . In Pampore , towards the south of the valley , four small wetlands had a total area of over 178 acres . To our crass indifference , only 150 acres are there . Yet again , Malgam wetland in north Kashmir Bandipore district was once spread over 1,125 acres . Today it is only 775 acres . Thirty percent of the once vast marsh has been filled . <p> In Central Kashmir , Narkara wetland is spread over around 8,000 kanals of land and is surrounded by Shiekhpora , Humhama , and Hyderpora . The wetland faces threat of extinction as reportedly over 100 illegal structures have come up on the wetland besides 4,000 kanals have been encroached upon . Now come to the famed Hokarsar wetland . Once it spread over 14 sq km . Today it has shrunk to just 5-6 sq km . The depth of its waters has also substantially reduced from 12-14 feet to only a few inches now . <p> Why has this happened ? It is believed that the wetlands in Kashmir have largely fallen prey to paddy cultivation , plantation , and residential complexes . Some other reasons being projected are : High pollution levels and encroachments into the natural vegetation . The growth of unwanted weeds , accumulation of silt , human and solid waste are some other reasons which have affected the growth of fish , water nuts and vegetables like lotus stems . <p> Environmental segregation is not possible . We can not carve out a separate zone in the world environment for our geography . We are part and parcel of the world ecology . An act against the environment in any part of the world is going to hit us and vice-versa . <p> The steps to be taken , therefore , have to be joint , cooperative and common . But equally essential is it to understand that allowing our environment to be degraded means to damage the environment of the globe . We can not sit and watch the vandalizing of our natural resources . That is why we can not shirk from the individual efforts , local initiatives , to address our environmental problems . <p> Before taking the remedial measures , we need to understand the fundamental reasons for the environmental degradation . Apart from the natural causes , one of the major reasons for environmental degradation is land damage . The weedy plant species sprout due to the rupture in the environmental surroundings . We have to take remedial measures to prevent land damage particularly in and around our environmentally sensitive places . <p> Other major reason is pollution in whatever form , whether it is air , water , land or noise . Any sort of pollution is harmful to the environment . We have been largely negligent towards the rising pollution levels in Kashmir . <p> Again the overpopulation -- that in our case is perhaps not so serious -- and overexploitation of our natural places -- indeed serious in our case -- over-stresses the environment . Landfills pollute the environment and destroy the natural beauty . The deforestation that is direct result of the rapid growth in population and urban sprawl , puts the last nail in the coffin of the environment . <p> We can not do anything about the natural causes like avalanches , quakes , storms , etc. but certainly we can do a lot about other reasons . <p> Now that the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir on a public interest litigation petition has directed the government to carry out demarcation of national parks , wildlife sanctuaries , conservation reserves and wetlands in Jammu and Kashmir , and put a bar on constructions in any of these areas , let us hope things will change for better . <p> Ours is a collective struggle that involves the future of a nation -- Kashmiri Muslims . National struggles can not be disruptive in character and content . They are reconciliatory , principled , and constructive .
