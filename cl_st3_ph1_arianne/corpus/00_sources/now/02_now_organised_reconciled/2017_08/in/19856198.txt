<h> Narendra Modi says dialogue is sole way to resolve conflicts <p> PM Modi says the solutions to a number of global challenges , from terrorism to climate change , can be find through Asia 's oldest traditions of dialogue and debate . Photo : Reuters <p> New Delhi : Dialogue is the only way to cut through deep-rooted religious stereotypes and prejudices that divide communities across the world and sow seeds of conflict between nations and societies , Prime Minister Narendra Modi said on Saturday . <p> " As the inter-connected and inter-dependent world of 21st century battles a number of global challenges , from terrorism to climate change , I am confident that the solutions will be found through Asia 's oldest traditions of dialogue and debate , " he said . <p> Modi said he is a " product of the ancient Indian tradition that firmly believes in dialogue on difficult issues . " <p> He stated this in a video message to the 2nd edition of ' Samvad- Global Initiative on Conflict Avoidance and Environment Consciousness ' being held in Yangon . <p> The prime minister said the ancient Indian concept of " Tarka Shastra " ( debate ) is founded on dialogue and debate as the model for exchange of views and avoidance of conflict . It is only natural that the search for answers be led by the humanity 's longest traditions of thought , rooted in various religions , civilizations , and multiple streams of spirituality , he said . <p> Giving examples of Lord Rama , Lord Krishna , Lord Buddha and Bhakta Prahlada , he said the purpose of each of their actions was to uphold ' dharma ' ( duty ) , which has sustained Indians from ancient to modern times . <p> Talking about the environment , he said man must relate to nature and revere it and not merely consider it a resource to be exploited . If man does not nurture nature , then nature reacts in the form of climate change , he said . <p> Environmental laws and regulations , while essential in any modern society , afford only an inferior protection to nature , he added , calling for " harmonious environmental consciousness . "
