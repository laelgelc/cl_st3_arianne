<h> Incomplete work at Kalsa brought to SC notice <p> Keri : Mahadayi Bachao Abhiyan Goa on Thursday submitted evidence of incomplete and ongoing work of Kalsa project in Kankumbi before the Supreme Court . <p> During the hearing , the Abhiyan 's counsel Bhavanishankar Gadnis pleaded before the court to restrain Karnataka from carrying out further construction work of Kalsa Bhandura Project . Karnataka has stated that they have almost completed the work . Countering this , the environmentalist group submitted recent photographic evidences of the ongoing work of uncompleted construction of the canal . <p> Karnataka has said that the project cost less than Rs 100crore , but the Abhiyan pointed out that it was worth over Rs 325crore and needed clearance from the ministry of environment , forest and climate change . Solicitor general of India Ranjit Kumar told the court that the project has not been given clearance by the MoEF . In the affidavit , Karnataka has reiterated that it will not utilize or divert water of River Mhadei without first obtaining necessary clearances or permissions under the Forest Conservation Act , 1980 . <p> Karnataka 's counsel said that they had applied for the clearances in 2002 .
