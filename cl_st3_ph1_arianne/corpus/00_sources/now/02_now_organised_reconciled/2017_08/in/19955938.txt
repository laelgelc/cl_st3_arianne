<p> The opposition has contested the numbers presented by the government saying there was a health emergency in the state . <p> Thiruvananthapuram : The Kerala government Thursday said 420 people died and over 22 lakh persons were affected by various kinds of fever in the state since January . <p> Health Minister K K Shylaja informed the state Assembly that of the total fever deaths , 74 died due to H1N1 and 24 were killed by dengue . <p> Replying to a notice seeking adjournment motion by the Congress-led UDF opposition over the issue , she said climate change and unprecedented variations in temperature level were among reasons that caused the fever to spread . <p> " As per government figures , over 22 lakh people have been affected by viral fever in the state during the period , " she said . <p> But with effective steps taken , things are under control , the minister said . <p> However , disputing the official figures on people affected by fever and deaths caused , Thiruvanchoor Radhakrishnan from the Congress party , alleged that over 1000 people had died and a whopping 50 lakh people had sought treatment for fever in various hospitals , including private ones , in the state . <p> The situation in the state was so grave that it even warranted the declaration of ' health emergency , ' UDF members said and walked out of the House when Speaker P Sreeramakrishnan declined leave for debate over the issue . The Health minister said besides H1N1 and dengue , rat fever and cholera have also been reported in the state . <p> A cholera death was reported in Pathanamthitta district and three cholera cases had been detected among migrant labourers in Kozhikode , she said . However , unlike earlier years , no case of monkey fever was reported in Kerala this year , Shylaja added . <p> Opposition leader Ramesh Chennithala alleged that the government had failed to take up any pre-monsoon sanitation drive and that led to the spurt in fever cases in the state . " Health Emergency should be declared in the state as the situation is grave , " Chennithala said . <p> If necessary , the state should seek the assistance of medical experts from the Center to tackle the situation , he added .
