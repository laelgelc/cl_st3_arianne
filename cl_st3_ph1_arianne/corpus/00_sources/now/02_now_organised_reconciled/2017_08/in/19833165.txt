<h> Green crusaders walk into Goa , sow seeds of environmentalism <p> Nida Sayed <p> TNN Aug 4 , 2017 , 03:31 AM IST <p> Edited by Jason . 400 words . <p> Panaji : Awadh Bihari Lal , Jitendra Pratap and Mahendra Pratap are on a mission . The seemingly regular men dressed in navy blue track suits have journeyed all the way from North India to reach Goa by foot . Their objective ? To bring to the attention of the state authorities that climate change is a real thing . <p> " Back then , what started as a noble gesture to spread awareness about evils of dowry , female foeticide and the importance of girl child education subsequently led to spreading the message of environment protection now , " said Lal . He has been focused on enlightening people about the perils of deforestation and importance of planting trees ever since . <p> Currently in Goa , Lal will be planting fruit and medicinal plants at St Bartholomew High School , Chorao and Sunandabai Bandodkar High School , Carambolim . Saplings are being procured from the state forest department . <p> " The issues that we fought for then are still prevalent today . Yet we have not given up spreading awareness , " Lal added . <p> The walk , which was started by Lal in 1980 , caught the attention of the central government over time , which now funds his stay in youth hostels across the country . <p> In over 30 years , the green crusader has amassed a huge number of followers , 20 of who accompany him throughout . Since then , they have covered a distance of 3.5 lakh km all over India ; and in China , Nepal , Pakistan , Afghanistan , Balochistan , Bhutan , Thailand , Burma and Bangladesh . <p> " Through our movement , we have reached 20 crore students and planted 9.5 crore plants in schools , colleges , parks and national highways , " Jitendra claimed . <p> These 20 ' foot soldiers ' are aged anywhere between the mid-twenties to late-forties . The Pratap brothers , who are now in their thirties , were incidentally the youngest to join the movement at ages 10 and 11 . <p> In their dedication towards this movement , the members have vowed to neither marry nor return home until they have covered a total of 4 lakh km . <p> The team now intends to put pressure on the Goa government to strengthen their efforts in environmentalism . <p> " We will be meeting with the chief minister , Manohar Parrikar and governor , Mridula Sinha to implement a law that makes it mandatory to replace the number of plants/trees uprooted during road widening with new ones , " said Mahendra . <p> After Goa , the trio will walk to Chennai to spread awareness , following which they will undertake a sea journey to South Korea as part of their mission . <h> more from times of india Cities <h> Comments <p> Characters Remaining : 3000 <h> OR PROCEED WITHOUT REGISTRATION <p> Share on FacebookShare on Twitter <h> SIGN IN WITH <p> FacebookGoogleEmail <p> Refrain from posting comments that are obscene , defamatory or inflammatory , and do not indulge in personal attacks , name calling or inciting hatred against any community .
