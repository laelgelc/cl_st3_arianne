<h> Monsoon Session of Parliament : Govt contemplates policy on hill region to promote development <p> New Delhi : The government is contemplating to bring a national policy on mountain hill region for its development , Environment Minister Harsh Vardhan said in the Rajya Sabha on Friday dismissing the need for a council for Jammu and Kashmir , Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh . <p> He said establishing a council was not the solution for development and listed many schemes and programmes as well as projects in Himachal Pradesh , Jammu and Kashmir and Uttrakhand to prove his point . <p> Vardhan was replying to a debate on a private members ' bill which provided for the creation of a Central Himalayan States Development Council . <p> He said a comparison of human development index data showed that despite 40 years of the creation of North Eastern Council , eight states -- Arunachal Pradesh , Assam , Meghalaya , Mizoram , Manipur , Nagaland , Sikkim and Tripura lagged much behind as compared to Jammu and Kashmir , Uttarakhand and Himachal Pradesh . <p> File image of environment minister Harsh Vardhan . PTI <p> " Even without council , we are paying proper attention to the development of hilly states . If more need to be done , we will do it , " the minister said . <p> He listed a number of schemes for the holistic development of the three states while mentioning that NITI Aaayog has also submitted a report on 12 states . <p> Pradeep Tamta of the Congress later withdrew his bill . <p> In his reply , the minister said these three states were , not only much ahead in human development index , but the government has announced several special packages for them that includes a Rs 80,000 crore package for Jammu &amp; Kashmir . <p> He said that in terms of GDP too , Uttarakhand was far ahead . <p> Road and rail networks too were better in the three states for which a Council was being demanded . <p> Presenting a comparision , the minister said Uttarakhand , Himachal Pradesh and Jammu and Kashmir have national highways network of 1,991 km , 1,208 km and 1,245 km , respectively , in comparison to barely a few hundred kms in North Eastern States . <p> Regarding the railway lines network , he said while Jammu and Kashmir , Himachal and Uttarakhand had networks of 96 kms , 269 kms and 356 kms , the North Eastern states barely had a few kms . <p> He said in 2015 , a special package was announced for Jammu and Kashmir that includes Rs 42,611 crore for rail and highway network . Besides , a Rs 12,000 crore Char Dham project is being implemented in Uttarakhand for building 900 km of highways after Kedarnath tragedy in 2013 . <p> The minister said Uttarakhand also figured among seven most developed states and that 119 research papers were awarded on these hill states . <p> Vardhan said he would also call a meeting of the members for their suggestions . <p> He said the country is providing leadership to the world on the climate change issue and members should rest assured . <p> Earlier , Jairam Ramesh ( Cong ) urged the government to review the policy of hydel projects in the upper regions of Ganga , Bahagirathi and other rivers . <p> " I urge you to relook at policy on hydel progamme " , he said . Others who particiapted in the discussion included V Reddy ( YSR Congress ) , BK Hariprasad ( Congress ) , Mahendra Singh Mahra ( Congress ) and Subbarami Reddy . <p> The reply later saw frequent interruptions by the Congress members who urged the minister to cut short his reply , saying they were convinced and he should conclude . <p> Rajeev Shukla of Congress quipped that members would fall ill due to his long reply , triggering laughter in the House . <p> " The long reply is leading to development of fatigue , " he added . <p> Pramod Tiwari of Congress then made another remark in a lighter vein directed at Vardhan . " You already prescribe allopathy . Now , please prescribe homoeopathy , " Tiwari told Vardhan who is an ENT doctor . <p> The minister then promised to cut short his reply . " Because of the homoeopathy dose , I have to cut short my reply , " he said . <p> Later , the House was adjourned till Tuesday as Monday will be a holiday on account of Raksha Bandhan.
