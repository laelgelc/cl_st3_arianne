<h> Change of climate likely to shrink fish size by 30% <p> August 22 , 2017 <p> Toronto , August 22 ( IANS ) : Fish are expected to shrink in size by 20 to 30 per cent as a result of rising ocean temperatures due to climate change across the globe , claims a study . <p> The findings showed that as fish grow into adulthood , their demand for oxygen increases because their body mass becomes larger . However , the surface area of the gills -- where oxygen is obtained -- does not grow at the same pace as the rest of the body . <p> " Fish , as cold-blooded animals , can not regulate their own body temperatures . When their waters get warmer , their metabolism accelerates and they need more oxygen to sustain their body functions , " said William Cheung , Associate Professor from the University of British Columbia in Canada . <p> " There is a point where the gills can not supply enough oxygen for a larger body , so the fish just stops growing larger , " he added . <p> Warmer waters increase fish 's need for oxygen but climate change will result in less oxygen in the oceans . This means that gills have less oxygen to supply to a body that already grows faster than them . <p> This forces fish to stop growing at a smaller size to be able to fulfill their needs with the little oxygen available to them , the researchers said , in the paper published in the journal Global Change Biology . <p> Some species like tuna , which are fast moving and require more energy and oxygen , may shrink even more when temperatures increase . <p> Smaller fish will have an impact on fisheries production as well as the interaction between organisms in the ecosystems , the researchers said .
