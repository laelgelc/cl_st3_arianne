<h> What you are scared of is also the world 's biggest fear : Jobs <p> ECONOMICTIMES.COM <p> Aug 07 , 2017 , 04.09 PM IST <p> A survey by Ipsos , a global market research and a consulting firm , of people across 26 countries finds unemployment as the top issue around the world . <p> Guess what 's the world 's biggest worry today . No , it 's not terrorism or climate change . It 's unemployment . A recent survey by Ipsos , a global market research and a consulting firm , of people across 26 countries finds unemployment as the top issue around the world . <p> Thirty-six per cent people say unemployment is their biggest worry , followed by 34% whose biggest worry is financial/political corruption . 33% people say their biggest worry is poverty &amp; social inequality . Only 20% are worried about terrorism which ranks sixth in the list , after crime &amp; violence ( 30% ) and healthcare ( 23% ) . <p> Financial/political corruption ( 45% ) is the biggest worry of Indians , followed closely by unemployment ( 40% ) . The jobs worry in India has increased 2% in July over June , most likely due to the introduction of Goods and Services Tax ( GST ) which has paralysed businesses as they are confused about its complex structure . <p> The survey was conducted in 26 countries around the world via the Ipsos Online Panel system . The countries included are Argentina , Australia , Belgium , Brazil , Canada , China , France , Great Britain , Germany , Hungary , India , Israel , Italy , Japan , Mexico , Peru , Poland , Russia , Saudi Arabia , Serbia , South Africa , South Korea , Spain , Sweden , Turkey and the United States of America . <p> Participants in China were not shown the full list of topics . Financial/political corruption , poverty and social inequality , taxes and rise of extremism were excluded from the list shown to Chinese participants .
