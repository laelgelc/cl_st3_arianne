<p> The Supreme Court put the Kalsa-Bhandura Project which threatened to disrupt peace between two neighbouring States of Karnataka and Goa , on the back-burner on Thursday , August 17 , with Karnataka assuring that there has been and will be no construction activity . <p> On August 3 , the bench of Justices Madan B.Lokur and Deepak Gupta directed the Union Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change and the State of Karnataka to file affidavits , within one week , as to whether the State has filed an application for forest and environment clearance in relation to the project in Western Ghats , and if so , the stage of consideration of its application . " If no application has been received , it should be specifically stated on affidavit " , the bench had directed . <p> The bench had taken seriously the Central Empowered Committee ( CEC ) ' s finding in its report that 258 hectares of forest land is to be diverted for the project . On August 2 , the Goa Chief Minister , Manohar Parrikar too has called the project an ecological bomb . He felt that the diversion of water by Karnataka for the project would have negative impact on Goa 's eclogy , wild life and the Western Ghats . <p> The bench had made it clear that its intervention is not over the dispute regarding the construction work , which has been stayed , and is being dealt with by the Mhadei Water Disputes Tribunal . The bench had further clarified that it was concerned with the forest-environment clearance to the project . <p> On Thursday , however , the bench did not grill the counsel for Karnataka , Fali Nariman , on the forest-environment clearance to the project , but asked him to stop construction work , oblivious of its own August 3 order . When Nariman replied that there is no construction work , and the question of stay on it does not arise , the bench did not see any merit in hearing the case further , and disposed of the IA Nos.1804 and 2526 . <p> Having succeeded in disposing of the IAs , Narman was seen hurriedly going to Court No.2 , where the hearing of the Cauvery water dispute was to resume . <p> The question whether the bench missed the subtle distinction which it made earlier between construction activity and the forest and environment clearance to the project , on August 17 remains unanswered .
