<h> VOICE OF THE PEOPLE August 9 , 2017 <p> Martin Rudy Haase ( letter to the editor , Aug. 4 ) says Omar Khadr was a victim , but many in Canada think otherwise . <p> The fact remains he killed an U.S. army medic and was found responsible by a U.S. court which awarded the widow of the slain medic a large compensation . The widow will no doubt not see one cent from Mr. Khadr while he continues to live free in Canada . <p> A man is dead as a result of Mr. Khadr 's actions , but because the Supreme Court says his rights were violated the government gave him $10.5 million . Where is the justice in that ? <p> The only victims in this ordeal are the widow and her kids who do n't have their loved one . <p> Gregory Boudreau , Halifax <p> Hail to the Tweet <p> Should President Trump 's official twitter account @POTUS be changed to @Pompous ? <p> Dennis Fitzgerald , <p> Box Hill , Australia <p> Down-zoning in HRM <p> Thank you for reporting on proposed developments mostly in peninsular Halifax . Most of the land-owning taxpayers on the Halifax peninsula own single-family and low-density dwellings , with R1 , R2 zoning . It 's what makes peninsular Halifax a liveable city with an urban forest . <p> In the election campaign , Premier McNeil promised if his party formed the next government there would be an " independent review of forestry practices " before any long-term licences are granted . <p> McNeil is premier again and presumably this review will take place . But it is August and neither terms of reference for this review nor the individual(s) who will conduct it have been announced . <p> Recommendations for improved forestry practices from previous reviews , in particular the Natural Resources Strategy , have been ignored . In 2016 , its key recommendations were scrapped . Limits on clear-cutting were removed , glyphosate spraying was allowed again and no limits were put on whole-tree harvesting . <p> So , initially , the independent review seemed like a manoeuvre to save time and face , while allowing industrial forestry as usual . <p> What has changed since the 2011 Natural Resources Strategy ? <p> Pressure has been building to decrease use of fossil fuels . Initially this led to ill-advised burning of biomass for power generation . The federal Liberal government has increased pressure for policies mitigating climate change . The McNeil government agreed to a cap-and-trade system . <p> An independent review could examine forestry practices in light of forests ' role in mitigating climate change . <p> A carbon market can assign value to sequestration of CO2 by forests . Having a monetary value assigned to standing forests ( on private or public lands ) and trading this value in a carbon market could allow Nova Scotians to be paid for leaving trees standing . <p> Advantages of standing forests , from tourism to biodiversity to alternative products , could be enjoyed . <p> The independent review should examine forestry practices in the context of a wider carbon market .
