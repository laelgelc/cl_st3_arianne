<p> Work at the Fire Management Headquarters and Provincial Logistics Centre on Arthur Street began about two years ago , and cost $25 million , the Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry said . <p> Now , all the work is done , and the renovated facility has been officially reopened . <p> " We are seeing increases in fires in some areas , not only in Ontario but in the country , because of climate change , " Kathryn McGarry , Minister of Natural Resources and Forestry said at a media event at the centre on Wednesday . <p> " We need to respond very quickly , " she said . " We need the resources , and the efficiency to ensure that everybody is able to deploy those resources . ... We 're able to respond much more easily and readily from this new fire centre . " <h> Base gutted <p> The 40-year-old facility was " gutted and rebuilt , with the spaces appropriated properly , " said Garry Harland , fire management supervisor at the base . <p> " It 's basically got the provincial logistics staff all in one part of the building , and it 's got fire management staff all in one part of the building , so the response end is separate from the industrial end , " Harland said . " <p> The facility now includes new training spaces , a new helipad , and new power and stormwater systems . <h> Other bases upgraded <p> McGarry said similar upgrades have been done at other fire management bases in the province , including those in Sudbury , North Bay , and Sioux Lookout . <p> Next up , she said , is the Dryden base . <p> " We are moving forward with procurement , " McGarry said . " We should see some plans , and a reconstruction of the Dryden base in the next few months .
