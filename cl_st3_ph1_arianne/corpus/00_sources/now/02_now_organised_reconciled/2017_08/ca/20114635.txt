<h> ' Apocalypse Summer ' at the ltd Los Angeles <p> ' What is our environment doing to us or what are we doing to it ? , Where is this mayhem leading us to ? ' - Based on this premise comes an exhibition titled " Apocalypse Summer " that questions the very essence of climate change and the challenges posed to human survival in these moribund conditions . Summers are becoming even more horrendous with the sun emanating heat as powerful as nuclear power . Our imagination is stifled turning our dreams feverish . The gas fumes stand as erect as pillars and the bio-engineered water substitutes have altered our conception of reality . The summer has acquired an apocalyptic form that is perpetually decaying , burning and melting away everything . Its destructive streak seems to know no bounds . As the blinding sun worsens our apparitions , we realize that no sun protective barrier can protect us from this monstrous attack of heat that only proliferates with each passing day . <p> Featuring the works of artists Lucas Ajemian , Andrea Marie Breiling , Ross Caliendo , Ian Davis , Hayden Dunham , Aimee Goguen , Anton Lieberman , Jake Longstreth , Lila de Magalhaes , Chandler McWilliams , Dominic Samsworth , Marian Tubbs and Alice Wang , " Apocalypse Summer " makes a provoking statement on global warming and its catastrophic repercussions . A beacon of what is by the name of ' OUGHT ' is suspended above the gallery entrance . Its sky blue Neon is barely visible during the day , but during the night its glitter is symbolic of the slow and fatal apocalyptic drip .
