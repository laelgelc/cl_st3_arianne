<p> All the conditions to establishing that commercial operations have been achieved under the terms of the power purchase agreement with FMG ( the " PPA " ) have been satisfied in full . These conditions include receiving a commercial operation certificate , successfully completing and passing certain test requirements , and obtaining all permits and approvals required from the North West Interconnected System ( " NWIS " ) and government agencies .
