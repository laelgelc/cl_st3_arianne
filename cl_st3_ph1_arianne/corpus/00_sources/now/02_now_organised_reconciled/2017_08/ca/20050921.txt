<h> O'Keefe Bows Out , Backs Breen for Mayor <p> August 17 , 2017 5:53 am <p> Long-time St. John 's Mayor Dennis O'Keefe will not be offering himself again in this year 's municipal election . <p> O'Keefe called a news conference at City Hall yesterday to announce that he will not be running , instead focusing on family and " other opportunities " . However , he is backing Councillor Danny Breen in his bid for mayor . He did not have anything good to say about one of the other candidates for mayor , Andy Wells . <p> " You 're talking about an individual who still does n't believe in climate change , " says O'Keefe . " I hope people will see ... that the city has made great strides , that we 're not going back to the old ways . <p> A new council and a new mayor will bring us into the future . I think people will vote for progress rather than regress . I hope . " <p> Councillor Tom Hann says now that O'Keefe wo n't be running , it changes the dynamic of the race . He says it " clears the field . " He says there was a sense of uncertainty , but now the race will be " quite clear . "
