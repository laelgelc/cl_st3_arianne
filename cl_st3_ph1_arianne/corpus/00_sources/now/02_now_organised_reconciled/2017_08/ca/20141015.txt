<h> Learn the economics of wood-based bioheat at WPAC 2017 <p> Aug. 22 , 2017 - Are you up to date on the economics , risks and opportunities of biomass-based heat installations in Ontario ? <p> The Ontario Ministry of Environment and Climate Change introduced new guidelines for the installation and operation of biomass-fuelled systems earlier this year . These guidelines along with Ontario 's cap and trade program have changed the policy landscape for bioheat developers and operators . <p> Ecostrat along with the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources and Forestry and Messersmith are hosting a bioheat workshop in conjunction with the Wood Pellet Association of Canada 2017 Conference and AGM in Ottawa this fall . The workshop will promote bioheat in Ontario and bring attendees up to speed on the latest government policies .
