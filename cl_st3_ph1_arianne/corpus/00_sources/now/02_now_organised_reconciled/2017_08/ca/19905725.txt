<p> In a park by a river that runs through Guelph , near some old growth trees , the Green Party of Ontario nominated its leader Mike Schreiner to run in the next provincial election . <p> From British Columbia , a Green party MLA was there . It was a sunny , warm and perfect late July day for an outdoor political meeting . Optimism was running high . <p> It was not the first nomination for the party ahead of the 2018 general election , but it was an important one . The party hopes to win at least one seat in Ontario and is pinning its hopes on Guelph . Its leader says winning even a single seat would constitute a breakthrough . <p> Schreiner , 48 , is setting his sights on Guelph for the second time . In the 2014 election , he placed third with nearly 20 per cent of the vote . He was elected leader in 2009 and has run for the party three times . <p> " We 're going to win Guelph . I 'm going to win in Guelph . That 's what I want to do , " he said . <p> Asked if one seat would be a breakthrough , he said : " Absolutely . We have seen in other provinces that winning that first seat is critical . " <h> Defining Green <p> The party defines itself as fiscally conservative , socially progressive and environmentally focused . It believes " all life on the planet is interconnected and that humans have a responsibility to protect and preserve the natural world . " <p> In an interview with CBC Kitchener-Waterloo , Schreiner said the party will run candidates in all electoral districts in the June 7 , 2018 election , but holding just one seat would enable the party to ask questions in Question Period , introduce private member 's bills and take advantage of resources available to MPPs . <p> It would have a real voice inside the legislature , he said . <p> According to a standing order of the Ontario legislature , a " recognized party " is one that has a caucus of eight members . <p> " The conversation I have over and over with Green Party candidates is : We know this is n't the easy way to win a seat in the provincial legislature , but we know it 's the right thing to do , " Schreiner said . <h> 2018 landscape <p> There will be at least 122 electoral districts in next provincial election and 20 parties have registered to run candidates . No candidates have yet registered to run in Guelph , according to Cara Des Granges , spokesperson for Elections Ontario . <p> Green Party of Ontario Leader Mike Schreiner speaks at his nomination meeting in Guelph on July 30 . ( Paige Toxopeus Photography/Facebook ) <p> Liberal MPP Liz Sandals , 69 , who won the riding in 2014 with nearly 42 per cent of the vote , has not said whether she is going to run again . In a statement , Sandals said she wishes Schreiner well in his campaign . <p> " I know from knocking on doors in Guelph and talking to constituents that folks are strongly behind the important work we 're doing right now , like raising the minimum wage to $15 an hour , making prescription drugs free for young people and making university and college free for middle and low income students , " Sandals said . <p> " Guelph is a city of communities , distinguished by citizens who are passionate about civic life -- people like Mr. Schreiner , who I know has wanted to run again in Guelph for quite some time . " <p> According to Nick Bergamini , spokesperson for the Ontario Progressive Conservative Party , the party has not yet set a date for the nomination . The NDP did not return calls . <h> Long shot <p> Analysts of Ontario politics , however , say one seat is a long shot for the Green Party in Ontario but Guelph is likely the best riding in which to run , given that the city has shown some support for its candidates in previous federal elections . <p> Nelson Wiseman , director of the Canadian studies program and a political science professor at the University of Toronto , said Green Party 's chances in Guelph : ' It has a better shot there than anywhere else . ' ( CBC ) <p> And they say one seat would make a huge difference because of the public recognition and media attention the party would receive . <p> Nelson Wiseman , director of the Canadian studies program and a political science professor at the University of Toronto , said he thinks it makes sense for the Green Party to focus its efforts on Guelph . <p> " It has a better shot there than anywhere else , " he said . <p> He noted Mike Nagy , a Green Party of Canada candidate , received more than 21 per cent of the vote in Guelph in the 2008 federal election . <p> In the 2014 provincial election , he said the PC , NDP and Green candidates each received roughly 20 per cent of the vote . <p> " If there is a reaction against the Liberals this time , and the Liberal vote drops 20 per cent , where that 20 per cent goes could determine who gets elected in Guelph . It 's conceivable they could win . They need the vote to be fractured in such a way that they could come up with the middle , " he said . <p> Wiseman said Elizabeth May , Green Party of Canada leader and a B.C. MP , has shown what one representative can do . He said a party without a seat has very little to no influence in Ontario politics but one seat means public recognition . <p> Myer Siemiatycki , a professor in the politics department at Ryerson University , said of the Ontario Green Party : ' It would be significant and a breakthrough if they could get a beachhead . ' ( Taras Karpiuk ) <p> Myer Siemiatycki , a professor in the politics department at Ryerson University in Toronto , says the party has been fielding candidates for more than 30 years in Ontario . <p> " It would be significant and a breakthrough if they could get a beachhead , " he said . <p> Siemiatycki said the Green Party is being wise in keeping its ambitions modest mostly because Ontario has a relatively stable political system in which historically the three main political parties have dominated politics . He said it 's not random or accidental that Guelph has been targeted . <p> He said major upsets have occurred when " circumstances arise that make large cross-sections of the public fed up with the options that were previously put on offer by provincial political parties . " <p> Also , he said the first past-the-post system is not kind to what he called " new parties " because it is winner take all . <p> " Whether they have a shot at this time around will depend on a number of factors , " he said . <p> One of those factors , he said , is how voters feel about the Ontario Liberals by the time the election is held . <p> Liberal MPP Liz Sandals , president of the treasury board , has not indicated whether she is going to run again in Guelph . ( Nathan Denette/Canadian Press ) <h> Guelph ' progressive ' <p> Schreiner , a former small business owner , acknowledged that Guelph is a good place to run . <p> " Guelph , for a smaller sized city , is very vibrant , very diverse , very progressive and it 's the kind of community that Greens do well in . " <p> The Green Party would support small business by lowering payroll taxes to promote job creation , protect water by making public drinking water for municipalities the top priority when the province issues water taking permits , and fight climate change through a carbon fee and dividend plan . <p> Under the plan , the party would put a price on carbon pollution , that price would be high enough to change behaviour of people and businesses , and revenue from the plan would go to individuals , not businesses , in the form of a quarterly dividend cheque . " You can decide how you want to spend your money to lower your carbon footprint , " he said . <p> The party is also calling for an independent review of costs of nuclear stations in the province . <p> Ontario Progressive Conservative Leader Patrick Brown has said the party , if elected , would cut the salaries of Hydro One and Ontario Power Generation executives . ( Paul Smith/CBC ) <p> Schreiner also said the Green Party would review the salaries of CEOs of Hydro One , Ontario Power Generation and Metrolinx . He said dissatisfaction with the Ontario Liberals and how they have run the public sector runs deep . <p> PC Leader Patrick Brown has also said the party , if elected , would cut the salaries of Hydro One and Ontario Power Generation executives . <h> ' Balance of responsibility ' <p> At the nomination meeting , B.C. MLA Adam Olsen , who represents the riding of Saanich North and the Islands , spoke to the gathering , saying a few elected representatives can make a big difference . <p> In the last B.C. election , the Greens held the balance of power . The Liberals won 43 seats , the NDP won 41 and the Greens won three . The Greens and NDP made a deal that would allow for a minority government to govern B.C. for the next term . <p> Olsen said what happened in B.C. , " has n't happened very often in Canada . " <p> " They are going to tell you that one MPP , or two or three or four MPPs do n't make a difference . Tell them , that 's not true . You have seen the example in British Columbia . Three MLAs , three MLAs . And they said to us , what does it feel like to have the balance of power ? And I said , I do n't have the balance of power . I have the balance of responsibility , " he said . <p> B.C. MLA Adam Olsen , who represents the riding of Saanich North and the Islands , spoke at the nomination meeting , says one MPP matters and three in B.C. held the balance of power . ( Paige Toxopeus Photography/Facebook ) <p> " All 87 MLAs have a responsibility to look after British Columbia . I am one of those . My two colleagues and I make up three of those . But it came down to us , " Olsen said . <p> " In British Columbia , three Green MLAs had a considerable amount of responsibility . And look what they did with it . They created a stable government . They did n't take positions in cabinet . They did n't take money for the sake of taking money.They simply tried to achieve a stable government founded on the principles they ran on . " <p> That 's kind of model he said he hopes that the Green Party in Ontario will run on . <p> But principles matter , he added . <p> " Be courageous . Stand up for what you believe in . Put it out there . And if I was to say one thing , I would say , make sure that this is the best campaign platform you have ever written . "
