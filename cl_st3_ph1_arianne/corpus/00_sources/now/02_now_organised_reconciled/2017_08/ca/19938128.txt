Given not only the dangers to right whales and other vulnerable species , but also the intensifying dangers of climate change and the increasing economic risks of further entangling ourselves in fossil fuel infrastructure , Canada must not allow oil and gas drilling in the Gulf of St Lawrence . <p> The federal government is currently reviewing the environmental review process and it is important that this review not be an exercise in making things easier for industry . <p> Both upstream and downstream impacts of increased carbon emissions must be considered for all resource-extraction projects . And the burden of proof should be on the project , not on the local community . <p> Article Continued Below <p> The deaths of the right whales is a message to stop and change direction . In the midst of the sixth mass global extinction -- precipitated by human activity -- every possible protection needs to be in place .
