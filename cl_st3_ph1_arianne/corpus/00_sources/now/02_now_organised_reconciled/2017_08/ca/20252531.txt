<h> News Local <h> Bilous announces funding for Hanna project <p> Members of the Hanna Climate Change Strategy Task Force met with Minister of Economic Development and Trade Deron Bilous to learn that they had been selected to receive $450,000 to help create 10 Community Action Teams which will focus on initiatives and opportunities identified in a recently completed report by the Hanna Climate Change Strategy Task Force commissioned last year . <p> Minister of Economic Development and Trade Deron Bilous multitasked during his stay in Hanna on Aug. 27 and 28 , hosting two information sessions as well as a press conference announcing $450,000 of funding for the Community Action to Create Diversification . <p> The funding will help the Town of Hanna partner with residents , business leaders and economic development expert to create 10 Community Action Teams which will focus on initiatives and opportunities for local economic diversification noted Bilous . <p> " The Town of Hanna has a proud history of helping power a prosperous and industrious province , " said Bilous . <p> " We want that to continue so people here can build a good life for themselves and their families . " <p> " We know that Canada 's move away from coal has created a lot of uncertainty -- that 's why we are working with local leaders and residents to support their made-in-Hanna plan for a resilient , diversified economy , " Bilous added . <p> The project will focus specifically on the issues identified in a recently completed report by the Hanna Climate Change Strategy Task Force which was commissioned last year . <p> Bilous said the Government had been working with Town for the past year on the subject of economic diversification . <p> " Part of us being here is engaging with the workers and hearing from them directly on what kind of supports they are looking for , " Bilous explained . <p> " We want the community of Hanna and this region to remain prosperous and do well . " <p> " What I want to commend the community of Hanna on is the fact that they 're not sitting around waiting for a report to be finalized , " Bilous said . <p> " They 've taken the initiative through their CARES application that there 's significant potential and we wanted to support them to get going on this effort . " <p> " This is why we 're not waiting , " Bilous said . <p> Mayor Chris Warwick , who was present for the announcement , said that he was pleased with the government 's decision . <p> " Our community members have been bringing forward suggestions for economic development opportunities for some time and this project provides a structure to take these ideas from concept to action , " Warwick said . <p> " Our message is clear -- diversification must be a priority as we work together to build an economy for the future . " <p> The Community Action Teams will take a regional approach to the task at hand , being led by the Cactus Corridor Economic Development Corporation , which includes members from the Town of Hanna , the Special Areas Board , the Village of Youngstown and the Hanna Learning Centre . The teams will also partner with the Rural Alberta Business Centre in Hanna , which helps more than 65 entrepreneurs and startups in the area each year . <p> " We accomplish more when we work together , and when it comes to creating new local economic opportunities during this transition , local people know best , " agreed Economic Development Officer for the Cactus Corridor Economic Development Corporation Trisha Sewell . <p> " Residents here have shared their ideas with our task force , and getting funding for this project means we can make those ideas a reality in various economic sectors of opportunity . " <p> " Rural communities have always been important to this province , and when our economies do well , Alberta does well . " <p> Bilous said that while the final draft of the Advisory Panel on Coal Communities report has yet to be completed , the government has seen the groups initial draft and anticipates the completed report this fall .
