<h> News Local <h> World Day of Prayer for climate concerns will include Peterborough event Friday <p> A special prayer ceremony will be held Friday at 6 p.m. at The Mount , 1545 Monaghan Rd . <p> A press releases invites the public to " come pray for the healing of the Earth . " <p> It 's part of Catholic prayer events happening around the world , led by Pope Francis , who issued a statement last year calling for Sept. 1 to be designated a new day of prayer focused on climate change . <p> " This Day offers " individual believers and communities a fitting opportunity to reaffirm their personal vocation to be stewards of creation , to thank God for the wonderful handiwork which he has entrusted to our care , and to implore his help for the protection of creation as well as his pardon for the sins committed against the world in which we live , " the Pope stated .
