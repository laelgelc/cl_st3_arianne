<h> You can get a free smart thermostat installed courtesy of the Ontario government <p> Owners and renters of detached , semi-detached , town and row homes can register to have a new smart thermostat installed . ( Photo : Green Ontario Fund ) <p> Note : As of 1 p.m. on August 30th , the Green Ontario Fund website is unresponsive , presumably due to high demand to register for the free thermostats . If you are interested in registering , you may want to wait for a few days ( there is no time limit on the offer ) . <p> If you want to save money for your home heating and cooling costs , you can now get a free smart thermostat installed in your home courtesy of the Ontario government . <p> Ontario has launched the Green Ontario Fund , a new not-for-profit provincial agency that will deliver programs and rebates to help reduce energy costs in homes and businesses . <p> The agency is part of Ontario 's Climate Change Action Plan and is funded by $377 million in proceeds from the province 's cap on pollution and carbon market . <p> " Taking strong action on climate change means making it as easy as possible for people and businesses to reduce greenhouse gas emissions at home and work , while also saving money , " says Chris Ballard , Minister of the Environment and Climate Change . <p> " That 's why our government created the Green Ontario Fund , which will give homeowners and small and medium-sized businesses improved access to low-carbon technologies and services . " <p> This includes the new GreenON Installations program , which opened early registration today ( August 30 ) . Interested and eligible households can register now to have a Green Ontario Fund-trained technician visit and install a smart thermostat at no cost . <p> The technician will also provide advice and tips as well as personalized suggestions for additional energy-saving upgrades . <p> The offer is open to both owners and renters of detached , semi-detached , town and row homes ( renters must have their landlords ' permission to register ) . <p> More GreenON programs for residents , businesses and industry will be announced in the coming months . <p> For more information about the Green Ontario Fund , visit www.greenon.ca . <h> SIGN UP FOR OUR VIP ENEWS <p> Delivered every Wednesday morning , our VIP Enews has featured news and events for the week , along with ticket giveaways and offers for our VIP Readers !
