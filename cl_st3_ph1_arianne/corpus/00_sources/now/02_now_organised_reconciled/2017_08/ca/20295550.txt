<h> New federal panel will assess Canada 's climate change readiness <h> Committee is chaired by University of Waterloo researcher , Blair Feltman . <p> Blair Feltmate is the director of the Intact Centre on Climate Adaptation at the University of Waterloo . ( Blair Feltmate ) <p> The federal government is taking new steps to ensure Canadians are prepared for the impacts of climate change with a new expert panel . <p> Headed by the University of Waterloo 's Blair Feltmate , the new Expert Panel on Climate Change Adaptation and Resilience Results will measure our readiness for major weather events caused by climate change . <p> " It 's this committee that will address the adaptation component of the federal government 's commitment to climate change , " Feltmate told CBC K-W . <p> Feltman says Ottawa is committed to looking at climate change both from the perspective of mitigating greenhouse gas emissions , as well as making sure the country is prepared for these extreme weather events .
