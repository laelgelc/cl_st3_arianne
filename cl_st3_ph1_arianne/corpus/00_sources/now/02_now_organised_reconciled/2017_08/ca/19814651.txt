<p> Bruce Power crews handled a spillage of radioactive water at the site 's Unit 3 reactor , but said there is no impact as a result of the incident . <p> " Bottom line is no impact on staff , the public or the environment , " said Bruce Power 's John Peevers in an e-mail . <p> Bruce Power said early in the day on Aug. 2 , 2017 , with Unit 3 in a planned shutdown , crews encountered an issue with a pump as they aligned the unit for an inspection program . <p> " As a result , a quantity of water leaked in the area of the pump and contained within a diked area immediately adjacent to the pump as designed , " the company said . " This area is in place to collect leakage if it occurs . Crews have cleaned up the water and there are no impacts to staff , the public or the environment . " <p> The federal nuclear regulator , the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission ( CNSC ) said in an e-mail it was informed of the incident by Bruce Power , in accordance with its licence conditions , of a failure of the primary heat transport pump seals on a pump in Unit 3 at the Bruce A Nuclear Generating Station . <p> " This resulted in a release of tritiated water , " the CNSC said . " The release was contained to the building , and cleanup is in progress . " <p> There are no safety concerns for members of the public or workers , and no releases to the environment are anticipated , the regulator said , adding the event was also reported to the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change of Ontario . <p> Peevers aid Bruce A Units 1 , 2 and 4 and the four Bruce B units continue to provide electricity to the Ontario grid .
