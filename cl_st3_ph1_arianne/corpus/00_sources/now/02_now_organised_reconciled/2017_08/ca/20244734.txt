<p> A Gravenhurst beach is closed after an unidentified substance was found in the water . <p> The town closed the beach promptly on Friday after the substance was discovered at Gull Lake . <p> An investigation by the Simcoe Muskoka District Health Unit shows that E.coli levels are acceptable for swimming , but officials are waiting for test results to come back from the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change . <p> Until more information comes from the ministry , the beach will remain closed .
