<p> The Caravan Stage Company has been a fixture in the B.C. Interior since 1970 , presenting homegrown theatre on horse-drawn wagons , performed by professional actors and musicians along with families and kids , all living and working together on Caravan 's farm in Armstrong . <p> Now Caravan has traded its Clydesdales for a tall ship , the 90-foot Amara Zee , sailing from New Orleans to anchor in False Creek , where the company is presenting its agitprop " climatopian " spectacle opera , Nomadic Tempest , as part of Vancouver 's Canada 150+ celebrations . <p> Written and directed by Caravan co-founder Paul Kirby , Nomadic Tempest is like an outdoor Cirque du Soleil and IMAX hybrid , with a smattering of J.R.R. Tolkien and David Suzuki . <p> The post-apocalyptic play is set in 2040 , after a gigantic " drowning wave , " unleashed by climate change , has destroyed much of the Earth . We see this in scary black-and-white sci-fi movie scenes on a huge scrim stretched across the ship to the top of its masts , the Amara Zee serving as both stage and screen . The audience watches from onshore . <p> Also projected onto the scrim are human survivors around a campfire . A woman , Kanandra ( Zia Musk ) , answers questions about the disaster from five children , her " Newlings , " telling them about " the Before Ones , your Olders . " <p> At the centre of her chronicle are the destructive Swallowwart twins ( JD Frost and Lindsey Sherman ) , evil servants of the petroleum industry . These " Gods of Fossil Reign , Lords of Carbon Rule " sit high atop the stage in a mini-Fort McMurray , singing of their mastery and mockery of all who oppose them . <p> The heroic resistance consists of Kanandra herself , who comes into the audience on a flying carpet/canoe to sing her opposition , plus the last surviving orca and four monarch butterflies . They 're played by gorgeously costumed aerialists Erin Cyffka , Emily Hughes , Jane Kirby and Natayu Mildenberger , who dance behind the scrim on ropes , silks and rings in a series of spectacular Cirque-like descents and ascents . <p> The monarchs also sing ( voiced by Laura Wu , Alexandra Lainfiesta , Lena Ferchichi and Victor Guerin ) in Mandarin , Spanish , Arabic and H ? n ? q ? ? min ? ? m ? , the Musqueam language , with English translations projected on the scrim . The translations are not always elegant : " Oh how wisdom is in capture ! We vacuum in the futile now . " <p> But the overall message is clear : Good will defeat evil as long as we " heed the planet 's call " before it 's too late . <p> Besides the aerialists , videographer Jordan Watkins ' kaleidoscopic , almost psychedelic projections star in this extravaganza , providing impressive visual fireworks along with the music and acrobatics . <p> The show is sold out for the entire run , but a few tickets are available at the door for each 9:45 p.m. performance , admission free , on a first-come basis .
