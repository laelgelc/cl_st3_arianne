" <p> Other gems from the scroll include , " Daenerys flies her dragon 88 miles an hour and travels back in time with Michael J. Fox to stop her father from being murdered , " as well as : " All of the main characters die before the last episode and the finale is just an hour of people farming . " <p> Colbert -- known to be a huge " Lord of the Rings " fan who actually made a cameo appearance in " The Hobbit : The Desolation of Smaug " -- ends with this one : " Next season , the series changes its name to the more honest title ' Lord of the Rings with Butt Stuff ' .
