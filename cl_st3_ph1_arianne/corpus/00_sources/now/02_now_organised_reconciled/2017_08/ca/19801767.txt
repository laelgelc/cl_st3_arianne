<h> Air contaminant not linked to incinerator , report says <p> A recent spike in air contamination at one of the region 's air monitoring stations represents a " negligible human health risk " according to a consultant 's report . <p> On June 12 , a TSP measurement at the Rundle Road Station was 126 per cubic meter air , exceeding the province 's 24-hour ambient air monitoring standards of 120 per cubic meter air . Once this exceedance was recognized , Stantec Inc. , the consultant for the DYEC , notified Durham Region . <p> TSP Measurements at DYEC 's other two ambient air quality monitoring stations on that day were 60 and 76 per cubic meter air respectively , which is below legislated standards . <p> TSP is a regulatory measure of the mass concentration of particular matter in air . <p> In a July 26 letter to Gioseph Anello , manager of waste planning and technical services for Durham Region , Stantec officials concluded , " the likely cause of the TSP exceedance was high background TSP levels combined with Highway 418 construction activities . The measured TSP concentration is not expected to result in an adverse effect on human health or the environment . " <p> Stantec 's letter indicated there was no equipment malfunctions or issues identified at the monitoring station . <p> Minor damage to the TSP filter , likely caused by birds , was found but Stantec officials say it 's not expected that this damage affected the monitoring . <p> Susan Siopis , commissioner of works for Durham Region , told The Oshawa Express the damage on the monitor has been repaired . <p> " When we pull out the cartridge , they are all inspected , " Siopis says . " We immediately do anything that needs to be done to make sure they work effectively . " <p> Stantec 's review also found the wind was blowing in a west/southwest to west/northwest direction on June 12 . <p> " Under these conditions , the DYEC was not upwind of the Rundle Road Station . Highway 418 construction activities were observed to be occurring that were upwind of the Rundle Road Station in these wind conditions , " the letter states . <p> Rundle Road Station is located northeast of the DYEC , near the intersection of Rundle Road and Baseline Road . <p> There were no air quality alerts for the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change on June 12 . <p> Siopis says this is the first time in three years TSP levels have exceeded the provincial standards . <p> She called it a " very minor exceedance " and says the region is not required to follow up in any way other than " to continue with the regular monitoring to ensure there is nothing to be concerned about . " <p> Under provincial regulations , the region was not required to notify the public regarding the exceedance . <p> Monitoring results , as well as Stantec 's report can be found , at DYEC 's website , durhamyorkenergy.ca
