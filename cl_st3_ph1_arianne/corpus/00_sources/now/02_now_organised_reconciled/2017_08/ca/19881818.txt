<h> US Secretary of State cites importance of tie-up with ASEAN <p> US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson on Sunday stressed the importance of its affiliation with the 10-country Association of Southeast Asian Nations . ( Photo by arcticcouncil/Flickr , CC BY-ND 2.0 ) <p> MANILA , Aug. 6 -- US Secretary of State Rex Tillerson on Sunday stressed the importance of its affiliation with the 10-country Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) . <p> This , after noting that he has met with ASEAN officials three times in the last six months , with the third one during Sunday 's ASEAN-US Ministerial Meeting at the Philippine International Convention Center ( PICC ) . <p> " I think , it is indicative of the importance that the United States pays and places upon this relationship with ASEAN , " he said . <p> In his opening remarks for the Ministerial Meeting , Tillerson said that among the issues that have been discussed between him and the ASEAN officials involve " a number of security interest , of mutual interest , and a number of economic areas of interest . " <p> He also expects to have discussions on additional topics concerning " threats to all of us and our common response to that " as well as " opportunities that exist among all of those to strengthen relationships . " &lt; ; p&gt;Malaysian Minister of Foreign Affairs Dato ' Sri Anifah HJ Aman , during the same meeting , said US is a vital partner of the ASEAN " with solid relations encompassing wide areas of cooperation . " <p> He said the world 's largest economy has been ASEAN 's partner for a long time now , with the ASEAN-US relations now on its 40th year . <p> He is thankful that the US has been a big help in ensuring peace , stability , security and prosperity in the region , which is the seventh largest economy in the world as of 2016 . <p> " ASEAN would like to see this relation enhance for our common benefit . Our cooperation in regional and international issues as well as traditional and non-traditional challenges continue to deepen to cover myriad of issues including terrorism , cyber security , climate change , migrants and maritime matters to name a few , " he said . <p> Aman added that ASEAN member-countries are all hopes for enhancing the partnership to another level .
