<h> U.S. officially tells UN it wants out of Paris climate deal <p> U.S. President Donald Trump speaks about his country 's role in the Paris climate change accord , on June 1 , in the Rose Garden of the White House in Washington . ( Pablo Martinez Monsivais/Associated Press ) <p> The Trump administration is officially telling the United Nations that the U.S. intends to pull out of the 2015 Paris climate agreement . <p> The State Department made the announcement late Friday afternoon . <p> But that does n't formally start the process of the U.S. getting out of the voluntary agreement . That 's still to come . <p> Still , the department describes its communication as a " strong message " to the world . <p> The director of U.S. climate diplomacy during the Clinton and George W. Bush administrations , Nigel Purvis , says countries ca n't withdraw from new international agreements , including the Paris climate one , until three years after they go into effect . The Paris agreement went into effect on Nov. 4 , 2016 . <p> Then the process takes a year . <p> The U.S. State Department says it can officially start withdrawing as soon as November 2019.
