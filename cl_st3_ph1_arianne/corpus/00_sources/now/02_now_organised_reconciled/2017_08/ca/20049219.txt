<h> Canada has a qualified approach of jovialiste <p> OTTAWA attitude found to be a bit naive and jovialiste of Canada on day 1 of the renegotiation of the NAFTA agreement is not unanimous . Some see it as a gamble and a sign of improvisation , others , a clever way to charm his opponents . <p> Canada , the United States and Mexico began on Wednesday the talks to renegotiate the free trade Agreement north american ( NAFTA ) recognizing from the outset that the task would be " difficult " . <p> The american representative Robert Lighthizer has quickly set the tone in the discussions , insisting on its willingness to review the treaty in depth . <p> " NAFTA is a dismal failure for countless Americans , he thundered in his opening statement in Washington . We will not settle for simple adjustments . " <p> In contrast , the representative of canada , the minister of foreign Affairs , asked by chrystia Freeland , has played the card of emotion . <p> In the press conference , the minister Freeland has brandi photos showing firefighters canadians , americans and mexicans working hand in hand , to illustrate the strength of the relationship that unites them . <p> She then promised to " keep in mind these images and what they represent " during the talks . <p> Apple pie <p> The economist St ? phane Paquin is asked about this strategy to mollify the Americans . Especially given that Ottawa wants to add elements to the " progressives " to the treaty , which could scald the White House , for example a direct reference to " climate change " that does not recognize the president Trump . <p> Finally , Canada could soon find itself on its side of the negotiating table , according to the expert . <p> " There is so much of apple pie and good feelings in the speech of Mrs. Freeland , who do not fit in the ideology of the president 's team , Donald Trump , the country risk of being accused of naivety and could be marginalized in the negotiation , " says Mr Paquin . <p> For the leader of the NDP , Thomas Mulcair , the approach of the minister Freeland in the negotiation feels the improvisation . <p> " The Trudeau government would be more credible if he had communicated with the public a clear vision of where he wants to go with this negotiation " , he responded . <p> Jovialisme <p> The economist of the University of Ottawa Patrick Leblond admits that the canadian approach is " a bit breezy " . <p> But , in his opinion , " it works " , and it is a clever way to look good in the eyes of the american public . <p> " Remember the solidarity between the three countries is strengthening Canada 's position in the negotiations , " according to the expert . <p> " And especially , it makes the threat of the withdrawal of the NAFTA by Mr. Trump a lot less credible , " he adds . <p> But Washington wants first and foremost to tackle the problem of the imbalance of its balance of trade with Mexico , which amounted to $ 64 billion . <p> According to Mr. Lighthizer , the NAFTA has cost at least 700,000 jobs in the United States .
