<p> A 22-year-old is dead in Burnaby from a sucker punch , the smoke is on the way out , and the NDP has enlisted the services of aboriginal rights legend Thomas Berger in their fight against the Trans Mountain pipeline . Here are five things you need to know . <p> According to witnesses , Michael Page-Vincelli confronted a woman who had tossed a cigarette butt out of her car window , reportedly throwing it back at her . Then he went into the Starbucks at Kensington Square , while the woman went into a nearby bank . But she emerged moments later with a man , and the pair allegedly followed Page-Vincelli into the Starbucks before the man hit him with a surprise punch . <p> Witnesses say Page-Vincelli hit his head on the counter as he fell to the ground unconscious . When Burnaby RCMP responded to the call , he was still on the ground , and he never recovered . Three days later , he died in hospital , and now IHIT is investigating what 's been ruled a homicide . <p> Why are we just learning about this now , as opposed to when it happened ? Apparently the public was on a need to know basis , and we did n't need to know . <p> According to a statement , IHIT only releases information to the public to address public safety , further the investigation or solicit help from the public , and " none of these applied . " <p> Fair enough . Although I 'd argue that if there 's a man on the loose who will sucker-punch you -- to death -- for chastising his girlfriend 's littering habits , public safety is a concern . But what do I know ? <p> Light rain showers and winds are in the forecast for this weekend , and they 're expected to finally provide some relief from the haze . <p> " Enough to disperse the smoke a little bit on Saturday and then on Sunday ; we 'll see some good clearing winds from the west , " said Jennifer Hay , meteorologist with Environment Canada . <p> But the smoke might not stay away . <p> " It depends a lot on the wildfire behaviour -- it does look like this is just a passing frontal system , so by about Tuesday , Wednesday , we 're back into a high-pressure ridge again , " said Hay . " So if we get another northerly outflow of wind , it could bring some of that smoke back . <p> " But we 're not predicting that to happen at this point . " <p> It better not . Beginning Monday , the only smoke I will welcome in my life is the kind that can flavour a salmon . <p> Here 's a video of a humpback whale sighting <p> Yesterday , we had the story of a close encounter between a humpback whale and a whale-watching vessel . The two collided . It was uncool . <p> Today 's close encounter is much cooler , as we bring you video of a humpback whale sighting off Cortes Island in the Straight of Georgia . <p> The best part ? No one hits the whale this time . <p> " Seeing whales in the wild is an incredible experience , " Peter Hamilton told Postmedia 's Larry Pynn , noting this humpback took the initiative to approach his vessel as he was documenting behaviour for research . <p> Got ta love a whale that appreciates the value of research . <p> B.C. NDP , Thomas Berger join fight against Trans Mountain pipeline <p> The B.C. NDP has begun an effort to " defend British Columbia 's interests " against Kinder Morgan 's Trans Mountain pipeline expansion by joining the legal fight against the project , reports Postmedia 's Derrick Penner . <p> " We know with the federal government 's approval of this project that this will be a challenge , " said Environment and Climate Change Minister George Heyman on Thursday , " but we 've committed to stepping up and fighting for B.C. 's interests . " <p> They 've added a high-profile name as well , bringing on Thomas Berger as an external adviser , which should speak to how they plan to argue their case : Berger is a lawyer with a serious history in First Nations law , including arguing the Calder case that helped establish the concept of Aboriginal title . He also famously argued against the Mackenzie Valley Pipeline in the 1970s , helping to stop a project that he said offered no economic benefit , threatened the environment , and disregarded the wishes of the aboriginal community whose land the pipeline would cross . <p> Back then , Berger argued that the " social consequences of the pipeline will not only be serious -- they will be devastating . " One imagines he 'll be helping the B.C. NDP make a similar argument now . <p> " ( Berger ) is a living example of First Nations law in Canada , " Eby said , " and we think his presence will signal a couple of things , " including the new government 's commitment to adequately consult Indigenous communities . <p> Finally , The Rock has come back ... to Vancouver <p> Did you know The Rock loves Vancouver ? <p> The wrestler-turned-actor is in town right now to film a new movie .
