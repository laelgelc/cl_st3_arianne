<h> EPA cancels sponsorship of awards for climate leadership <p> The Environmental Protection Agency has dropped its sponsorship of a climate change awards program co-sponsored by two environmental organizations . <p> " The Agency has decided to discontinue the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency 's ( EPA ) involvement with the 2018 Climate Leadership Awards program , " the EPA said on its website . <p> " This includes canceling the 2018 Climate Leadership Awards as well as EPA 's sponsorship of the Climate Leadership Conference . " <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The awards program recognizes organizations and individuals that take steps to address climate change . It also awards organizations that publicly report their greenhouse gas production levels and reduction efforts . <p> Winners in 2017 , which were announced at the Climate Leadership Conference in March , include Goldman Sachs , IBM and Lockheed Martin Corp. , as well as a California assemblyman , the climate policy director for Atlanta and a sustainability officer for JetBlue Airways . <p> Organizers of the leadership awards and the climate conference said there were " disappointed " by the agency 's decision , but that the awards program and conference will move forward without the EPA . <p> " We believe it 's more important than ever to encourage and recognize climate action by businesses , organizations , local governments and individuals to reduce the man-made emissions that are causing our climate to change , " said Ann McCabe , executive director of the Climate Registry , and Bob Perciasepe , president of the Center for Climate and Energy Solutions , in a statement . <p> " We 're eager for opportunities to build new partnerships to ensure the 2018 Climate Leadership Conference and Awards are better than ever . "
