<h> Tulsa 's guide to the Great American Eclipse 2017 <p> Weather conditions during Monday 's solar eclipse are predicted to be hot and humid . <p> Sky coverage is expected to be mostly clear with less than a 20 percent chance of rain during the morning hours of the eclipse , according to the National Weather Service in Tulsa . Conditions are expected to remain partly to mostly sunny in the early afternoon as the eclipse moves toward its max coverage . Temperatures are expected to remain in the low 80s to low 90s throughout the morning and early afternoon . <p> Later in the afternoon , eastern Oklahoma will be at an elevated risk of dangerous heat as the afternoon sets in , according to the weather service . Additionally , scattered thunderstorms will be possible late afternoon with the highest chances in far east Oklahoma and northwest Arkansas . <p> Heat indices for Monday are expected to reach between 100 and 105 degrees .
