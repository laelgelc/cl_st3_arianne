<p> From Kilimanjaro International Airport you can fly or drive the 55 kilometers to Arusha , from where you can organize tours and accommodation inside and outside the crater . <p> 4 . Paria Canyon , Arizona , United States <p> The Paria River in northern Arizona carved its own smaller version of the Grand Canyon . Some of the rock formations , including The Wave , are just as spectacular . <p> Visitors need a permit from the Bureau of Land Management -- the permit for an overnight trek comes with a " human waste bag , " so if you want to visit this natural wonder , you 'll have to prepare to pack your waste . <p> The Paria Contact Station is 69 kilometers east of Kanab . You can hire a guide through the Bureau of Land Management . <p> 5 . Volcanic eruptions at Stromboli , Italy <p> Part of the Aeolian Islands off the coast of Sicily , Stromboli is a small volcanic island with several hundred brave inhabitants . <p> Unlike most volcanoes , Stromboli 's is constantly spewing lava fountains , gas and ash . Fascinating for volcanologists , but also great for day-trippers who fancy seeing live lava action . <p> For natural fireworks , take a boat trip around the island at night . <p> Arrange boat tours from harbors on the north coast of Sicily ( Messina , Cefalu , Palermo ) . <p> The mud is thought to have medicinal qualities , so do n't be surprised if you see people stripping down and lathering themselves in the goo . Look out for the area 's Roman inscriptions and the petroglyphic rock art . <p> About 70 kilometers west of Baku . <p> 7 . Jeita Grotto , Nahr al-Kalb Valley , Lebanon <p> Eighteen kilometers northeast of Beirut , Jeita Grotto is comprised of underground limestone caves were inhabited in prehistoric times and continue to attract human visitors with their vivid colors and stalactite formations . The biggest stalactite in the world is here . <p> The caves consist of a network of chambers -- with an upper and a lower gallery -- stretching out for nine kilometers and accessible by an underground river . <p> The nearest town is Juniyah , just a few kilometers away . Cave tours last two hours . <p> 8 . Pembrokeshire Coast Path National Trail , Wales <p> This path twists 300 kilometers from St. Dogmaels to Amroth in southwest Wales . <p> It 's often wet and windy , but if you strike lucky on a sunny day this is one of the most beautiful places on earth . Scented gorse and crimson heather brighten the way . <p> Look for seals in the waters below . <p> Paths are signposted -- join the path on the coast between St. Dogmaels and Amroth . Details on guided walks and activities can be found on the National Trails website -- including self-guided walks with baggage transfer . <p> 9 . Pangong Tso Lake , India-China <p> This saltwater lake deep in the Himalayas at an altitude of 4,350 meters lies astride a disputed border area between India and China-governed Tibet . <p> Do n't let that put you off -- the rarefied air make the colors and clarity of the lake intense . <p> Pangong Tso is reached by a mountain road from the Indian town of Leh , but you 'll need to get a permit via a registered tour guide . <p> Get to Leh by road from Jammu , or by plane from Delhi . At Leh arrange a permit and travel by road 4-5 hours to the lake . Permits and tour guides can be arranged through reputable travel agents such as Kuoni <p> 10 . Geirangerfjord and Naeroyfjord , Norway <p> If you only have time to visit two fjords in your lifetime , make it the Geirangerfjord and Naeroyfjord in southwest Norway . <p> These are among the world 's longest and deepest fjords , with high vertical cliffs , deep waters and giddy waterfalls . Both are on the UNESCO World Heritage list . <p> Trips can be arranged from Bergen and Alesund . <p> 11 . South Kaibab Trail , Grand Canyon , Arizona , United States <p> Most visitors view the canyon from South Rim viewing stations . Considering that it has taken the Colorado River the past 17 million years to carve this wonder out of rock , it seems only fair to take a closer look . <p> Built by the National Park Service in 1924 , the South Kaibab Trail takes you to the wonderfully named " Ooh Ah Point " and , for the adventurous , further into the canyon 's depths . Plan carefully , heat stroke is no fun . <p> In the northwest corner of Arizona , visitors usually head to South Rim Village ( 120 kilometers northwest of Flagstaff on route 180 ) or the North Rim Village . Free shuttle buses service the South Rim in summer months . Ranger-led day hikes and walks take place throughout the year . <p> 12 . Mount Roraima , Guyana/Brazil/Venezuela <p> South America 's answer to Uluru , this impressive sandstone plateau is surrounded on all sides by 400-meter cliffs , creating an isolated and unique ecosystem . <p> If you want to follow in David Attenborough 's footsteps ( he 's filmed several times here ) , organize a trek from the Venezuelan side . <p> Hiking up Mount Roraima is best done from Venezuela . The Paratepui Route is the easiest for non-technical climbers and trips can be arranged from San Francisco de Yurani . <p> 13 . Verdon Gorge , Provence , France <p> The gorgeous turquoise waters of the Verdon River flow through one of Europe 's most beautiful gorges for 25 kilometers . <p> Swim in the translucent waters of Lac de St. Croix and stare in awe at the 700-meter walls of the Verdon Gorge . If you 've got a head for heights , it 's a popular destination for rock-climbing . <p> The Verdon Gorge is on the border of the departements of Var and the Alpes de Hautes Provence . <p> 14 . Jiuzhaigou National Park , Sichuan Province , China <p> The three valleys that form this biosphere reserve contain a network of connected lakes , waterfalls and rivers -- the most spectacular of which are the Pearl Waterfalls . <p> Spot the ancient tree trunks under the clear waters of Five Flower Lake . Wooden paths and shuttle buses help visitors get around . <p> In the north of Sichuan , the nearest town to Jiuzhaigou National Park is Songpan . <p> 15 . Lake Nakuru , Kenya <p> A streak of blue ( and pink ) in Kenya 's Great Rift Valley , Lake Nakuru is home to thousands of pink flamingoes that flock here to feed on the lake 's algae . <p> A UNESCO Heritage Site , Lake Nakuru National Park is also home to hippos , white and black rhino , giraffe and buffalo . <p> Take a matatu 156 kilometers northwest of Nairobi , or a plane to the Naishi airstrip . <p> 16 . Uluru/Ayers Rock , Australia <p> Australia 's favorite giant sandstone mass is 350 meters high and more than nine kilometers in circumference . <p> It 's a sacred and spiritual site for its custodians , the aboriginal Anangu , so climbing the rock is considered disrespectful to them . It can also be dangerous . <p> Uluru-Kata Tjuta National Park is about 440 kilometers southwest of Alice Springs . Flights are available from most major cities to Ayers Rock airport . <p> 17 . Siwa Oasis , Egypt <p> This isolated oasis has natural springs and fertile land , providing access to spectacular stretches of Sahara desert . It 's a great spot for star gazing from your tent in the sand , but bring your bathing suit for a dip in its hot and cold natural pools . <p> A 10-hour drive west of Cairo . Public buses take much longer . Desert safaris can be arranged in Siwa . <p> 18 . Seljalandsfoss Waterfall , Iceland <p> Waterfall connoisseurs agree it 's not size that counts . The biggest and the highest may be impressive , but when it comes to cascading water , Iceland 's Seljalandsfoss has style . <p> The sight of the Seljalandsa River dropping 62 meters down the sheer cliff face has made it a must-see Iceland attraction . There 's a path that goes behind the cascade , so bring your waterproof camera . <p> On Road 1 , it 's 125 kilometers southeast of Reykjavik . <p> 19 . Perito Moreno Glacier , Patagonia , Argentina <p> This 30-kilometer glacier in Patagonia 's Los Glaciares National Park ( not to be confused with the Perito Moreno National Park ) grows and contracts , often forming a natural ice dam on the " elbow " of Lago Argentino . <p> The force of the trapped water causes a spectacular rupture every four to five years . Even when the ice is n't exploding , the sight of the glacier 's blue peaks is a lifetime attraction . <p> Fly from Buenos Aires to El Calafate in Patagonia . The alternative is a very long bus journey . <p> 20 . Moraine Lake , Banff National Park , Alberta , Canada <p> Deep in the Canadian Rocky Mountains , Moraine Lake -- together with its sister Lake Louise -- is one of the most photographed landscapes in Banff National Park . <p> It 's known as the jewel of the Rockies for its deep crystalline waters that mirror pine forests , soaring mountains and endless sky . <p> Drive or take a direct bus 128 kilometers west from Calgary to Banff . <p> 21 . Cascate del Mulino , Saturnia , Tuscany , Italy <p> Steaming hot spring water comes out of the ground at 37.5 C and cascades over a series of small waterfalls into dozens of pools on consecutive levels . <p> Niagara Falls it ai n't , but the cascading thermal springs at Saturnia are a lot of fun . Soak in the natural sulfurous mineral water and just maybe cure ailments from rheumatism to muscle ache . <p> About 10 kilometers north of the small Tuscan town of Manciano , northeast of Orbetello . <p> 22 . William Bay , Western Australia <p> A five-hour drive south of Perth , William Bay in Denmark has turquoise waters that lap around white sands and the Elephant Rocks , which shelter an area of rock pools and granite terraces called Green 's Pool . <p> The calm waters are perfect for snorkeling , while the more adventurous have the Great Southern Ocean on the other side . <p> Fifteen kilometers west of Denmark . <p> 23 . Jeju Island Lava Tubes , South Korea <p> This volcanic island in Korea -- it 's on the UNESCO World Heritage Site list -- has mountains , stunning coastal rock formations and the finest system of cave lava tubes in the world . <p> The Jeju caves have towers of petrified lava , while the Cheju-do cliffs have tube-like formations similar to the Giant 's Causeway in Northern Ireland . <p> Jeju Island is 130 kilometers off the southern coast of South Korea . It 's accessible by boat from Busan or air from Gimpo airport in Seoul . <p> 24 . Angel Falls , Venezuela <p> The highest waterfall in the world , the water at Angel Falls travels 979 meters , which includes a free fall drop of 807 meters . <p> It cascades over one of the biggest table-top mountains in the Canaima National Park in southern Venezuela , known to the local Pemon people as Devil 's Mountain . Most of the water evaporates as mist before reaching the bottom . <p> Angel Falls is in remote jungle near the Canaima airstrip , which can be reached by plane from Ciudad Guayana or Ciudad Bolivar . Local Pemon people work as guides . <p> 25 . Lauterbrunnen Valley , Switzerland <p> Deep in the Swiss Alps the Lauterbrunnen Valley , or Lauterbrunnental , is a deep cleft cut in the topography running between steep limestone precipices . <p> The waterfalls that dot the rift often disappear into wisps of spray before hitting bottom , and include Staubbach Falls , one of Europe 's highest unbroken waterfalls at 270 meters . <p> Get to Lauterbrunnen , the village in the valley , by narrow-gauge train from Interlaken Ost station in the Bernese Highlands . <p> 26 . Cliffs of Moher , Ireland <p> South of the village of Doolin in County Clare , the Cliffs of Moher rise 213 meters above the Atlantic Ocean and stretch for eight kilometers . <p> They are one of Ireland 's biggest natural tourist attractions , but they also attract Atlantic puffins , razorbills and other wild birds . <p> Do n't miss the spectacular views of the Arran Islands , Galway Bay and the Burren . <p> The Cliffs of Moher are an 80-kilometer drive southwest of Galway . <p> 27 . Skaftafell National Park , Iceland <p> Formed over millions of years by volcanic eruptions , rivers and glaciers , the Skaftafell National Park in southern Iceland has a variety of striking landscapes . <p> These include an overhanging wall of geometric black basalt rocks on the Svartifoss waterfall , which inspired the architecture of Reykjavik 's National Theatre , and the majestic Skaftafellsjokull glacier that seems to have frozen in mid-flow . <p> Drive Road 1 for 326 kilometers east of Reykjavik . Buses from Reykjavik run to the park in summer . Self-guided hikes of Skaftafell are outlined on the official website . <p> 28 . Lake Titicaca , Bolivia/Peru <p> At an altitude of 3,811 meters above sea level , Lake Titicaca is one of the largest lakes in South America by volume . <p> It is also the highest commercially navigated lake in the world and is home to indigenous people including the Aymara and the Quechua . Its vast expanse of water is often cloaked in light mist and has inspired numerous local legends . <p> Lake Titicaca can be visited from the Peruvian town of Puno or from Copacabana on the Bolivian side , 150 kilometers from La Paz . <p> 29 . Wadden Sea , Germany and the Netherlands <p> UNESCO describes the Wadden Sea as " the largest unbroken system of intertidal sand and mud flats in the world . " In other words , it 's a surreal hinterland along the coast of Germany and the Netherlands that combines wide-open stretches of sand and shallow seas . <p> It 's also home to migratory birds . Go there and you 'll feel a million miles from anywhere else on earth and about as small as one of the clams at your feet . <p> This 600-kilometer stretch of coast lies between the towns of Den Helder in the Netherlands and Niebull in Germany . <p> 30 . Puerto Princesa Subterranean River , Palawan , Philippines <p> Nominated as one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature , the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River runs for 8.2 kilometers underground and boasts limestone karst formations , stalactites and stalagmites . <p> You can cruise down the river in a canoe . Watch out for the Palawan stink badger , an adorable little skunk that lives in the area . <p> The underground river is 76 kilometers northwest of Puerto Princesa city on the western coast of Palawan . <p> 31 . Plitvice Lakes , Croatia <p> This series of shallow lakes in the heart of Croatia are a nature lover 's paradise , with clear waters pooled in between rocky canyons and dramatic waterfalls gushing over cliff edges . <p> Wooden walkways make access to Plitvice Lakes for visitors . Swimming is forbidden inside the national park , but there are places for a dip outside , such as Korana Village . Look out for freshwater fish and brown bears in the surrounding hills . <p> The park is a two-hour drive north of Zadar . There are also good bus services . Guided tours for groups are available on request -- see website . <p> 32 . Mosi-oa-Tunya/Victoria Falls , Zambia , Zimbabwe <p> The Zambeze River cascades over a cliff stretching more 1.7 kilometers , making Victoria Falls , known by its local name as Mosi-oa-Tunya ( smoke that thunders ) , a stunning sight . <p> With its highest-ever recorded flow rate of 12,800 cubic meters per second , a lot of water makes the 108-meter drop into the Zambeze 's gorges . <p> Swimmers can take a dip in Devil 's Pool when water levels are n't too high -- it 's right on the edge of the waterfall . Check safety notices first . <p> Visit the falls from Livingstone in Zambia . <p> 33 . Finistere , France <p> The remote headland in Brittany really does feel like the end of the world ( its name is derived from the Latin finis terrae ) . <p> In front lies the Atlantic Ocean , while along the coast natural rocky harbors and inlets create a rugged landscape . <p> Tourists flock to Pointe du Raz , which points like a craggy finger at a lighthouse in often choppy waters , but on sunny days the abers are peaceful and protected from the elements . <p> Accessible from Brest , Brittany . <p> 34 . Fernando De Noronha , Brazil <p> Fernando De Noronha is an archipelago of 21 tropical islands , 350 kilometers from mainland Brazil , is famed for its idyllic sandy beaches , marine life and hiking trails . <p> It 's now a marine national park and UNESCO World Heritage Site , where hawksbill turtles , dolphins , sharks and endangered species are protected . <p> The island life was n't always a paradise . Between the late 18th century and 1957 there was a prison on the main island . <p> 35 . Great Barrier Reef Marine Park , Australia <p> Made up of nearly 3,000 individual reefs , the Great Barrier Reef off the coast of Queensland extends for 2,600 kilometers and is the world 's largest structure made of living organisms . It 's even visible from space . <p> A favorite on UNESCO 's World Heritage list , this delicate ecosystem is home to porpoises , green sea turtles , whales and dugongs . But the reef is threatened by climate change and coral bleaching that occurs when water temperatures rise . <p> Base your visit from any of the towns on the east coast of Queensland between Gladstone and Thursday Island . Choose a tour operator approved by the Marine Park authority . <p> 36 . Caves of Aggtelek Karst and Slovak Karst , Hungary and Slovakia <p> This vast karst landscape of limestone plateaus and more than 700 caves is home to the world 's highest stalagmite . <p> The 21-kilometer Baradla-Domica cave stretches over the Hungary-Slovakia border . The Gombasek cave is probably the more photographed , with its impressive rock formations giving it the feel of a fairy tale city . <p> Drive 240 kilometers northeast of Budapest to the Aggteleki National Park . <p> 37 . Mount Bromo , Indonesia <p> This smoking crater inside the Bromo Tengger Semeru National Park sits in a sea of sand . <p> It 's actually a small active volcano inside the much larger caldera of an ancient extinct volcano . <p> While the volcano is still active and has recently been closed off to the public , it 's still a point of pilgrimage for Javanese Hindus , who congregate every year in the Kasada festival , during which live chickens are thrown into the crater . <p> Travel to Mount Bromo by train or bus from Surubaya , or hire a car and do the two-three hour drive . <p> Hiking , snorkeling , kayaking and boating are popular pastimes , but so is lying on the beach and admiring the scenery . <p> Spirit of Tasmania run a night ferry from Melbourne to Devonport . Use Tasmanian Redline coaches to reach Freycinet National Park . <p> 39 . Iguazu National Park , Argentina <p> The Iguazu River drops up to 82 meters over a 2.7 kilometer-wide ledge of the Parana Plateau . The waterfalls are accessible from Brazil , Argentina and Paraguay . <p> A walk through the national park will take you not only to the Devil 's Throat and close to the curtain of water , it will also give you a chance to spot coral trees , butterflies , toucans and hummingbirds . <p> Buses travel from most cities in the region to Puerto Iguazu , or you can fly to Iguazu International Airport . <p> 40 . Tsingy de Bemaraha Strict Nature Reserve , Madagascar <p> Spiky limestone rock formations and mangrove forests are part of the tropical and otherworldly landscape of the Tsingy de Bemaraha nature reserve on the western side of Madagascar . <p> The reserve is home to chameleons , lemurs and endangered birds . <p> Also part of the landscape is the Manambolo River , which runs red with eroded sediment from the highlands of Madagascar . <p> The reserve is 400 kilometers west of Antananarivo . <p> 41 . Cueva de los Cristales/Cave of Crystals , Mexico <p> In 2000 , a group of miners tunneling in the Naica mine , in the Mexican state of Chihuahua , found an underground cavern containing giant selenite crystals -- some as long as 11 meters . <p> The size and beauty of these gypsum crystals brought the Cave of Crystals instant fame . <p> But the future of the cave is uncertain . While access is currently possible for short periods , the cave may be closed and flooded in the future to stop the crystals from disintegrating . <p> The Naica mine is 135 kilometers southeast of the city of Chihuahua . <p> 42 . The Bay of Fundy , Canada <p> One of four places on earth with extreme tidal highs and lows , the Bay of Fundy sees a vertical rise in sea level of as much as 17 meters twice a day . <p> More than 100 billion tons of seawater rush in and out of the bay daily , sculpting unique basalt shapes out of the rocks along the coast . <p> Travel between the bay 's islands and coastal towns is possible by small car ferry . <p> 43 . Halong Bay , Vietnam <p> Thousands of small islands and standing stacks , or karsts , overgrown with green shrubbery , protrude from Halong Bay , a peaceful bay . <p> The popular way to explore is in a traditional Chinese trading ship -- the brown-paneled sails of the junks have become as much a part of the landscape as the towering rocks . <p> Most visitors to Halong Bay come via Hanoi -- 170 kilometers away , or five to six hours on a public bus . Private cars can be hired . Even rented helicopters make the journey . <p> 44 . Punkaharju Esker Nature Reserve , Finland <p> This seven-kilometer esker , or sand ridge , was formed during the Ice Age and has been an important trade route in eastern Finland for millennia . <p> Calm lakes lie on either side and tall pines provide shade for moss , toadstools and blueberries -- easy to see how fairy tales are born . You expect a frog with a crown to hop off the nearest giant mushroom , or a mystical hand to reach out of the lake . <p> Take a train from Helsinki to Punkaharju or drive the 350 kilometers northeast of Helsinki . <p> 45 . Frozen Sea in Lulea , Sweden <p> This is n't the only place in the world where the sea freezes over , but the town 's location -- jutting out into the sea -- makes the frozen water a welcome extension of the town 's streets in the depths of winter . <p> From December to February , locals skate , snow-trek and sled across the bay , while some roads -- across the sea -- are passable only in winter . <p> Signs advise you to remove your seatbelt for a quicker exit should your car come across thin ice . <p> There are several daily flights to Lulea from Stockholm , which is easier than driving the 900 kilometers . <p> 46 . Trango Towers , Pakistan <p> Some of the highest cliffs in the world are in northeastern Pakistan in the Karakoram mountain range . <p> Trango Tower , for instance , rises a kilometer above the other granite spires in its ridge and has an elevation of more than 6,200 meters above sea level . It 's a sight that 's just too tempting for intrepid climbers and base-jumpers . <p> Take a local bus or taxi from Islamabad to Skardu . <p> 47 . Fox Glacier , South Island , New Zealand <p> Along with the 12-kilometer Franz Josef Glacier ( a short distance up the coast ) , the 13-kilometer Fox Glacier on New Zealand 's South Island is one of the most easily accessible ice masses in the world . <p> Fox Glacier reaches almost to the coast , just 250 meters above sea level . Large chucks of ice breaking off the face make it dangerous to get too close . <p> Fox Glacier is in Westland National Park , 200 kilometers west of Christchurch . Get information on guided tours here . <p> 48 . The Shilin Stone Forest , China <p> Spread over 350 square kilometers in Yunnan province , these stone needles look like an ancient petrified forest -- the rock formations stand tall and emerge vertically from the ground . <p> The karst rocks are more than 270 million years old . Local legend says the forest was created when a young woman was forbidden to marry her love -- she rebelled by turning herself into stone . <p> Shilin Stone Forest can be reached by road 96 kilometers southeast of Kunming in Yunnan province . <p> 49 . Na Pali Coast , Kauai , Hawaii , United States <p> Whether sandy beaches , giant waves or volcanic craters , Hawaii has plenty of breathtaking landscapes . But the drama and scale of the Na Pali coast of Kauai , along the 17-kilometer Kalalau trail , may top them all . <p> The lush green mountains ripple with scree slopes and ridges for more than 1,300 meters before dipping into the Pacific . <p> Accessible only by 17-kilometer hike from Kee Beach to Kalalau Valley , or by boat . <p> 50 . Lake Baikal , Russia <p> Lake Baikal in southern Siberia is not only the deepest and oldest lake in the world ( more than 1,600 meters deep and more than 25 million years old ) , it holds almost 20 percent of the world 's freshwater supply . <p> The UNESCO World Heritage Site is a unique ecosystem that supports more than 2,600 species of plant and animals . There have even been reports of a " Baikal Monster , " likely to be a giant sturgeon . <p> The nearest city is Irkutsk , about 120 kilometers north of the Mongolian border . <p> Editor 's note : This article was previously published in 2012 .
