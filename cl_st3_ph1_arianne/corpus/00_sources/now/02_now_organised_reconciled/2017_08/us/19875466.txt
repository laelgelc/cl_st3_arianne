<p> During a sit-down with CNN 's Fareed Zakaria , Gore pointed to reports of extreme weather , saying that the U.S. has had " 11 once-in-a-thousand-year events " in the past seven years . <p> " And they 're now fairly commonplace , " Gore said . <p> " Last year , Houston , Texas had two one-in-500-year events and one once-in-a-thousand-year event . " <p> Gore continued to talk about the threat of climate change , saying India just set its " all-time high temperature record . " <p> " The hottest year ever measured globally was last year , " he said . <p> He added that 16 of the 17 hottest years have happened in the last 17 years . <p> " And every night on the TV news is like a nature hike through the Book of Revelation , and , though the scientists have long connected the dots , the carbon polluters have mounted this lavishly funded , rear-guard action to pretend there 's still a debate , " he said . <p> " But again , Mother Nature is piercing that veil and convincing people that , whether they want to use the terms ' global warming ' or ' climate crisis ' or not , they can see for themselves with the evidence of their own senses that things are really changing for the worse . " <p> Gore has been promoting his new documentary , " An Inconvenient Sequel : Truth to Power . " <p> During a CNN town hall earlier this month , he criticized President Trump 's decision to withdraw the U.S. from the Paris climate agreement . <p> He said Trump has " isolated " himself from the majority of Americans who believe in the threat of climate change .
