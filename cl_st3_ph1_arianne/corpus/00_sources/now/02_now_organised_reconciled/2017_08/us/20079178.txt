<p> Two of the White House 's top corporate advisory groups disbanded in a direct affront to President Trump , as the fallout from his controversial statements about who was to blame for violent protests involving white supremacists in Charlottesville cascaded beyond Washington . The exodus of business advisers comes as a stinging rebuke to the president , who had sold himself as a businessman whose dealmaking prowess would pay off for companies and workers . It also stands as a remarkable breach in relations between Trump , the leader of the Republican Party , and a business community that historically has had an ally in the GOP .
