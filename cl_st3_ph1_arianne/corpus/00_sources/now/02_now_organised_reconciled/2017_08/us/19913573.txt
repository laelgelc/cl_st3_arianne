<h> Site Mobile Navigation <h> Do n't Ignore the Dire Threat of Climate Change <p> The coal-burning Plant Scherer in Juliette , Ga. , is one of the top emitters of carbon dioxide in the United States . A draft report by government scientists concludes that Americans are feeling the effects of climate change right now.Credit Branden Camp/Associated Press <p> We should all fear the possibility that President Trump will dismiss the draft climate change report by government scientists . The odds that this report is wrong are extremely remote , but the chances that life on Earth will be permanently affected by global warming are high . <p> Any reasonable cost-benefit analysis demands that we address this existential threat immediately . Perhaps if we rename the danger " pending calamity , " elected officials will be able to recognize the threat to civilization posed by climate change rather than politicizing scientific facts . <p> Climate change is fueling that dangerous summer heat . As a health scientist , I know that heat is n't just an inconvenience : It can kill . America 's 76 million aging baby boomers -- including me -- are increasingly at risk , since aging bodies struggle harder to regulate internal temperatures efficiently . <p> To take a giant step away from the brink of more disastrous future heat , we can insist on clean , healthy energy sources like solar and wind , and stronger support for government agencies and programs charged with protecting public health . <p> The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention , the National Institutes of Health and health professionals can lead the way toward healthier , more climate-secure communities . They deserve support from our elected officials and more robust budgets , so they can provide health protections for years to come in a changing climate . <p> KIM KNOWLTON , NEW YORK <p> The writer is a senior scientist at the Natural Resources Defense Council and an assistant professor at the Mailman School of Public Health , Columbia University . <p> A version of this letter appears in print on August 9 , 2017 , on Page A18 of the New York edition with the headline : Do n't Ignore the Dire Threat of Climate Change .
