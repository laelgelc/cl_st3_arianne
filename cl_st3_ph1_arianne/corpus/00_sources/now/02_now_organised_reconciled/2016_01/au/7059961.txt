<p> On December 30 , the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry announced that four new elements are being added to the periodic table . The group had " reviewed the relevant literature for elements 113 , 115 , 117 , and 118 and has determined that the claims for discovery of these elements have been fulfilled " . <p> What thrills us nowadays is n't growth in the body of human knowledge , but innovations that apply that knowledge in our day-to-day lives . <p> Television networks cut into their regular programming with the announcement . There was dancing in the streets . Facebook and Twitter erupted with pledges to name newborns after the physicists responsible for the discoveries . <p> OK , nothing in that last paragraph happened . You did n't jump around your living room for joy , and , to tell you the truth , neither did I.
