<h> Cosmic cycles , not carbon dioxide , control climate <p> Those who think the political war on carbon will cool the globe or keep climate stable need to study climate history . <p> Temperatures on Earth dance to a cyclic rhythm every hour , every day , every month , every season , every year , and to every beat of the sun-spot and glacial cycles . <p> The daily solar cycle causes continual changes in temperature for every spot on Earth . It produces the frosts at dawn , the mid-day heat and the cooling at sunset . It is regulated by rotation of the Earth . <p> Advertisement <p> Superimposed on the daily solar cycle is the monthly lunar cycle , driven by the orbit of the Moon around the Earth . Sunspot cycles are indicators of solar activity which causes periods of global warming and cooling . <p> Earth 's climate is also disrupted periodically by the effects of changing winds , ocean hot spots and submarine volcanism that produce the El Nino Southern Oscillation . <p> The least recognised but most dangerous climate cycle is the glacial cycle . We live in the Holocene Epoch , the latest brief warm phase of the Pleistocene Ice Age . The climate history of the Holocene , and its predecessor the Eemian , are well documented in ice core logs and other records in the rocks . Each cycle consists of a glacial age of about 80,000 years followed by a warmer age of about 20,000 years , with peak warming occurring over about 12,000 years .
