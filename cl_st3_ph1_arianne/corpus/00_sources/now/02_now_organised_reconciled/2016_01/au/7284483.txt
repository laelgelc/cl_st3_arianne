<p> I welcome The Age 's support for governments to " formulate market-based emissions-reductions mechanisms , to encourage businesses in all sectors to dramatically cut emissions , and to facilitate broad-scale development of sustainable energy systems " ( Australia urgently needs potent climate policies ) . <p> We already have in place a suite of policies that is achieving all of these objectives . <p> We have a market based mechanism -- the Emissions Reduction Fund ( ERF ) . The ERF , through reverse auctions , acquires the lowest cost emissions reduction and abatement . It is modelled on the United Nations ' Clean Development Mechanism and is an extension of Labor 's Carbon Farming Initiative . <p> Financial support from the ERF allows businesses to undertake projects to cut emissions which they otherwise would not be able to achieve .
