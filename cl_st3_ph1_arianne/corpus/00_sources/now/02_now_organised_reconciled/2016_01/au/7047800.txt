<h> Historic event or a fraud ? Critical thoughts on the Paris Climate Accord <p> Considering that so much depends on whether global warming can be arrested soon , it was no wonder that all thinking people had turned their attention to Paris , where on 13th December the COP21 " successfully " ended , with great jubilation . I too followed the process through the media . <p> Positive and Negative Reactions <p> A day after , I read the following comments : " Today is a historic day : as tens of thousands of people filled the streets of Paris , politicians finalized a major new global climate agreement " . And " It 's a fraud really , a fake , " ... " It 's just bullshit ... " 1 The first was made by May Boeve , the US national organizer of 350. org , a big NGO , that is playing a leading role in the climate justice movement , the second by James Hansen , a former NASA scientist , considered the father of climate change awareness . But at present at least , I can not , for reasons I shall present below . <p> Why Call it a Fraud ? <p> I too think , like Hansen , the accord was a fraud , but for reasons very different from those of his . I think it was only because they were afraid of being branded as the guilty in case the COP21 failed to reach an agreement that the major CO2 emitter countries reluctantly decided to sign this very weak paper . But paper , as the Germans say , is patient . If you want evidence , then look at the position with which the government of India went to Paris . " <p> Retorting to US Secretary of State John Kerry 's criticism of India 's decision to double its coal production , Javadekar had remarked it was " absolutely unfair and unacceptable , " especially since the CO2 reduction targets announced by the developed countries would fail to arrest the climate change crisis , which they had a historical responsibility of fixing after a century of pumping greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.He also warned the Americans against " bullying " India . <p> Or take the case of Norway , very rich in oil and gas .
