<p> South Australia has six plant biodiversity hotspots but threats to conservation including climate change and weed invasion are putting them at risk , researchers say . <p> University of Adelaide researchers have identified western Kangaroo Island , southern Mount Lofty Ranges , APY Lands , southern Eyre Peninsula , southern Flinders Ranges and the lower South East as areas of exceptionally high biodiversity compared to the rest of the state . <p> The university 's Dr Greg Guerin said the study used biological data collected over time , including from the State Herbarium , and numerical methods to create a map of the state 's plant biodiversity . <p> " Basically we 've relied before on expert opinion to identify where important areas were , " he said . <p> " This is the first time we 've been able to compile this large data resource and to systematically map out plant biodiversity using numerical methods .
