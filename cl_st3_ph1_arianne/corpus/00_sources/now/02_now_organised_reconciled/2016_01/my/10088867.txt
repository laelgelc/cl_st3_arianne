<p> Kuala Lumpur : AirAsia Group , which is continuing its climate change awareness initiative #GREEN24 as part of its corporate social responsibility initiatives in the Asean region , is inviting the public to participate . <p> The group said the #GREEN24 was first launched last November with a 24-hour climate awareness movement to educate on and promote climate action . <p> The initial activity saw over 150 volunteers from across the AirAsia Group and Tune Group of companies coming together to clean up the Kanching rainforest waterfall in Rawang . <p> Nearly 600 kg. of garbage was collected in just 90 minutes , said the airline . <p> In a statement , AirAsia said it has released a special video by Group Chief Executive Officer Tan Sri Tony Fernandes , available on AirAsia 's YouTube channel , inviting the public to be proactive in protecting the environment and the Earth . <p> Fernandes said on this side of the world , not many people know about the issues that climate change poses and the group feels that the best thing to do is to inform people . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ problems coming , but at AirAsia we have seen the effects of climate change such as Typhoon Haiyan that struck Tacloban in the Philippines , the floods that happened around the region and massive climatic changes over the past two to three years . <p> " We always look after our own mothers , and we always put our mothers first in many things . But now , it is time we put Mother Earth first as well and it is time to let the world know that we need to take better care of the planet we live in , " he said . -- Bernama
