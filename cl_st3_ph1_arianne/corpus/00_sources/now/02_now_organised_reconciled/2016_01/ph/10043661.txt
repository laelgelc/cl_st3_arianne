<h> ASEAN priorities for 2016 defined <p> HANOI -- An inter-ministerial meeting took place in Hanoi yesterday to review the results of Vietnam 's ASEAN cooperation in 2015 and define priorities for 2016 . <p> President Aquino links arms with his Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) counterparts at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Center in Kuala Lumpur , Malaysia where he attended his last ASEAN Summit . ( EPA ) <p> At the regional level , ministries agreed to effectively implement the ASEAN Community Vision to 2025 and master plans to strengthen the pillars of politics-security , economics , and culture-society , particularly , initiatives on enhancing connectivity between member countries and narrowing development gaps . <p> They vowed to step up the implementation of existing commitments related to freer trade , services and investment ; as well as new commitments to market opening and economic integration . The ministers agreed on participation in ASEAN 's socio-cultural activities which are suitable for the country 's resources like social welfare , disaster relief and climate change response . <p> They also agreed to promote the central role @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ partners . <p> At the national level , ministries pledged to boost communications on the freshly-formed ASEAN Community , and adjust laws to facilitate the implementation of regional commitments . <p> The year 2015 has great significance with the birth of the ASEAN Community on December 31 , which was the result of cooperation efforts of ASEAN member countries over the past 48 years and marks an important milestone in the ASEAN connectivity process . <p> Delegates to the meeting agreed that in the past , Vietnam had fully , actively and responsibly taken part in carrying out its commitments and had contributed effectively to ASEAN and between ASEAN and its international partners . <p> The country had made important contributions to completing works required to establish the ASEAN Community , and made numerous initiatives to strengthen regional connectivity and narrow the development gap between member states going forward .
