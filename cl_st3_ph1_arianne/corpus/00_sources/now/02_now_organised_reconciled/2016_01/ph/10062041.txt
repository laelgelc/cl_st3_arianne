<h> Ledesma : Power Generation <h> Ledesma : Power Generation <p> PRESIDENT Benigno Simeon Aquino III flew in to Davao City for the formal inaugural ceremonies of the coal -- fired power plant in Binogao , Toril . <p> The occasion is perfectly timed when threats for more extended power brownouts will hit Mindanao because of the bombing and felling of NGCP power pylons perpetrated by extortionists elements and the inadequacy of energy generated by the Agus hydropower plants operated by the National Power Corporation . <p> The commissioning of the 300-megawatt power plant is like heaven sent and we have to thank the Aboitiz group for saving Davao region from the misery that other regions in Mindanao are presently enduring and suffering for lack of electric power supply . <p> Establishing power generating plants does not come easy under the present administration . <p> President Aquino may take pride in having to cut the ribbon in the inaugural ceremonies of this coal fired power plant that is using the latest green coal technology but it can take years before an investor for power generation can obtain go @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ smile . Even small generating plants that use briquettes from pineapple wastes and rice stalks in Bukidnon complain that it generally takes four years to obtain permits from the government before they can drive a single nail into their projects . <p> In short despite the power crises and the opportunities government remains to be the biggest deterrent in the efforts to address the energy shortage in the country . This is a sublime irony but this happens in an era suffused with corruption . <p> It is too late now for the Aquino administration to look into why his much ballyhooed " Daang Matuwid " failed to address the problem . Author Ado Paglinawan is correct when he says that under the Aquino regime even the solution is a problem . <p> In 2012 at the height of the energy crisis in Mindanao , the national government , ( and unfortunately even the Mindanao Development Authority ) was in a stoic denial that the region is not facing any crisis related to power . <p> They passed on the shortage as " economic sabotage " aimed at discrediting the Aquino @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ notion that the shortage was artificial when in fact nearly all of Mindanao , with the exception of Aboitiz franchise areas , had been languishing from 2-hour rotational power brownouts that even peaked to 8 hours in Zamboanga peninsula and South and North Cotabato and Agusan provinces . <p> In a panic mode , the Department of Energy promised to dispatch four floating generators and when this was not enough President Aquino himself called to the energy summit to quell the impatience of Mindanao stakeholders . <p> He promised to rehabilitate Agus hydropower plants in Lanao and Bukidnon , directed the Budget Management to release hundreds of millions of pesos to address the problems . His final statement : " Mindanao has to pay higher rates for the energy they have to consume " . <p> Up to now however there is nothing perceptible and assuring the energy crisis in Mindanao is in the radar screen of the government . We never realized too that the floating generators will never come after all we suspected these were misrouted to Timbuktu . <p> What was proffered by Minda later was for local @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government-endorsed modular gensets from Singapore . <p> The energy issue has elicited debates as power generation runs parallel with environmental concerns and climate change . <p> The cleanest energy source was nuclear based , the finite are solar and wind , the viable and clean are natural gas and hydropower and the culprits are bunker-fed and coal-fired generated . Many western countries were using nuclear power but the Fukushima tragedy sent shivers to many nuclear plant users which consequently reassess their options whether to rely mainly on nuclear or shift to solar , natural gas and coal-fired generators . <p> With the new technology , among them fluidized bed that captures much of the pollutants that coal emits , a number of western countries opted for coal-fired power plants . The old coal plant facilities were refitted with the new technological components . <p> The Binugao coal-fired power plant is already equipped with the new technology .
