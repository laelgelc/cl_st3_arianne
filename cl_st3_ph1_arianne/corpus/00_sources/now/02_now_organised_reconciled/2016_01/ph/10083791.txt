<h> Green energy baseload <h> Green energy baseload <p> I DISAGREE with President Benigno Simeon Aquino III . He argued that once the coal-fired power plant in Davao City becomes " fully operational , " it can be counted on to " consistently produce 300 megawatts ( MW ) of baseload power . " His premise is that " rain or shine , there will continuous power . " Aquino seems to forget that in this era of climate change , sustainable generation of power by coal-fired plants go beyond weather conditions . There is the economic considerations . Make that the green economy considerations . <p> Worldwide , countries are phasing out coal-fired plants . UK 's Energy Secretary Amber Rudd who said that reliance on " polluting " coal is " perverse . " She added , " Let me be clear : this is not the future . We need to build a new energy infrastructure , fit for the 21st century .
