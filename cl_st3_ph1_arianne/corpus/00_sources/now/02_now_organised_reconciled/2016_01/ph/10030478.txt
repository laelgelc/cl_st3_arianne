<h> Pe ? a : Top environmental stories for 2015 <h> Pe ? a : Top environmental stories for 2015 <p> AS MY first column for 2016 , I reviewed all my articles for 2015 and selected the top 5 local and international environmental stories . Here they are in no particular order : <p> 1 . Historic climate change agreement : All 's well that ends well . A historic agreement to combat climate change was agreed by 195 nations in the 21st Conference of Parties ( COP 21 ) in Paris last December . The agreement 's main aim is to keep a global temperature rise this century well below 2 degrees Celsius and to drive efforts to limit the temperature increase even further to 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels . Additionally , the agreement aims to strengthen the ability to deal with the impacts of climate change . The agreement will enter into force after 55 countries that account for at least 55% of global emissions have deposited their instruments of ratification . <p> 2 . Haze reached the Philippines : The haze coming @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kalimantan has spread throughout the region , including the Philippines . It has become an international air pollution crisis which has also affected Brunei , Malaysia , Singapore , Southern Thailand , Vietnam and Cambodia . For this first time , the haze reached Davao and Cebu . The haze affecting Davao may have been brought by the monsoon winds blowing northeast from fires in Indonesia towards the direction of the Philippines , says PAG-ASA . <p> 3 . Cecile , Pamana and Yongki : The killing of endangered and protected animals hugged the headlines . Cecil the Lion , was killed in Zimbabwe on July 01 allegedly by Walter Palmer , an American hunter who is also a dentist . The killing and beheading of the lion has provoked outrage and widespread publicity . Pamana , a Philippine Eagle , was found dead last August 16 a kilometer from the site where it was released , with bullet wounds that are believed to be from an airgun . The killing also sparked outrage . Yongki , a Sumatran elephant , was found dead close to the camp where he lived @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Indonesia . Yongki , aged about 35 , was well known among the local " mahouts " or elephant keepers . It was poisoned and killed for its tusk . <p> 4 . Pope Francis ' encyclical on the environment : The much-waited encyclical on the environment of Pope Francis was released on June 18 . The 184-page document entitled ' Laudato Si ' gives a very strong message on caring for ' our common home ' and calls for ' ecological conversion ' . <p> 5 . New fuel specs for the Philippines : The Department of Environment and Natural Resources ' ( DENR ) issued Administrative Order No. 2015-14 requiring the use of cleaner fuel and imposing stricter emission standards for all vehicles starting July 1 . It also enjoined the Department of Energy to ensure the availability of Euro 4 fuels by this date . Euro 4 is a clean fuel which has sulfur content of only 50 parts per million ( ppm ) for both diesel and gasoline , compared with 500 ppm for Euro 2 which is what we used before .
