<h> Marco Rubio 's kind of mischief <p> Former US Vice President Walter Mondale recently wrote an editorial for the New York Times , deploring Senator Marco Rubio 's meddling in the nomination of ambassadors . Since it has become clear that America 's struggle to defend its national interests can not be won by military force alone , American diplomats assigned abroad have taken over a number of important issues , from climate change to checking Iran 's nuclear ambitions , and yet dozens of ambassadorial nominations have been indefinitely delayed . At one point in 2014 , Mondale points out , nearly one-fourth of the world 's countries lacked an American ambassador , and even today a dozen nominations have not received congressional action , including nominees to represent the United States in such strategically important countries as Mexico , Norway and Sweden . <p> A case in point is Roberta Jacobson , whom President Obama nominated as ambassador to Mexico last summer .
