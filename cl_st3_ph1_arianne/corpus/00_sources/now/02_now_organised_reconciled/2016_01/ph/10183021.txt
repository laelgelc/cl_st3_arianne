<h> We ca n't celebrate the Eucharist if we neglect the environment -- Bishop Pabillo <p> By : InterAksyon.com January 28 , 2016 7:07 PM <p> Logs swept down by flashfloods on villages are seen in the aftermath of typhoon Sendong , one of Mindanao 's worst disasters , in this December 2011 Reuters file photo . Filipinos must find in their celebration of the Eucharist the real meaning of stewardship of creation , says a bishop attending the 51st International Eucharistic Congress being held in Cebu . <p> MANILA - A country touted as Christianity 's main bastion in Asia , the Philippines is also listed among those most visited by disasters , including the ones directly caused by environmental degradation from bad human practices . This disconnect must be broken , says a leading Filipino prelate attending the 51st International Eucharistic Congress being held in Cebu . <p> " Hindi tayo makakapagdiwang ng maayos ng Eukaristiya kung wala tayong pakialam sa environment . We ca n't celebrate the Eucharist meaningfully if we do n't care for the environment . " This was the reflection of Manila @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ day of the Eucharistic Congress , in an interview with Church-run Radio Veritas . <p> Pabillo said that bread and wine come about through a cooperation of the human and the divine . " The mystery of the Eucharist makes all human labor sacred , " he added . <p> Explaining the connection of the Eucharist and the environment , the auxiliary bishop of Manila reminded all that the environment is God 's gift to everyone , and as human beings , created in " the image and likeness of God " ( Gen. 1.26 ) , we have the responsibility to respect the goodness of creation . We have the responsibility not exploit the natural world -- a responsibility not being fulfilled by many people these days . " <p> Environmental issues are also the concern of His Holiness Pope Francis in his encyclical letter , Laudato Si released last year . In the encyclical , the Holy Father appealed for a new dialogue about how we are shaping the future of our planet .
