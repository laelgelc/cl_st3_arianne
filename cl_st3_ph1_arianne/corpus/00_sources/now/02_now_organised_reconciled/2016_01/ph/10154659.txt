<h> Latip-Yusoph : Flaming change <h> Latip-Yusoph : Flaming change <p> CLIMATE change has become a phenomenon in the whole world that only a few understand the extent of damage it will cause each day . It endangers the well-being of children and youth and the forthcoming generations according to the researches of the United Nations Children 's Fund ( Unicef ) . If this concern together with other environmental problems is kept unabated , the life our children will undeniably be at high risk . <p> In the recent forest fire in Butig , Lanao del Sur , the communities nearby theorized so many things about the cause of the spread of fire . They did n't understand that by having the dried forestland for several weeks now , this tragedy will inevitably happen at an unexpected time . <p> Climate change is a concept that all of us must know . It should not be limited to those who have an access to information . How far has our government done in the awareness raising in our most volatile communities ? <p> In the country alone @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ typhoons , longer droughts and extreme weather events leading to massive landslides , flooding , outbreak of diseases , food scarcity , water shortage , and loss of livelihood and lives . In the past months , we have severely felt the strong typhoons hitting our regions and leaving a massive damage and loss of lives . <p> Outbreak of diseases like dengue has become so rampant in the communities in Mindanao . There are so many unreported cases in Lanao where young children died in the attack of such disease . According to one of the pediatric doctors in Marawi , " incidence of climate-sensitive diseases such as dengue dramatically increases with young people as primary victims . " <p> Is n't this alarming ? What is being done by our government these days ? I would like to assume that health and environmental agencies must have coordinated with barangay officials to spread this alarming effects and manifestations of climate change . <p> How often have they visited us in our households ?
