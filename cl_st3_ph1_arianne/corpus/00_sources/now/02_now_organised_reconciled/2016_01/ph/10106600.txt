<h> Obama 's last trips as US president <p> Before he steps down as US president this year , President Obama is going to make a series of trips that he has thus far been unable to work into his busy schedule that demanded his presence in Washington . These will include two trips to Asia , two more to Europe , and at least one to Latin America . He will also be making quick junkets around the United States , to campaign and raise funds for Democratic candidates , as well as to play host to foreign guests , by taking them outside of Washington -- to Camp David , Maryland , or , if there is time , to California . These trips will give him a chance to press foreign policy issues , away from the hostility of the Congressional Republicans in Washington , such as the deal with Iran , the ratification of the TPP trade agreement , the Paris Climate Change agreement , and the normalization of diplomatic ties with Cuba . <p> The president 's first major event outside Washington is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Rancho Mirage , California , to host leaders of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations ( ASEAN ) of which the Philippines is a member . In May , he flies to Japan for a Group of 7 summit meeting , and in September to China for the Group of 20 summit . He also expects to visit Laos in September , and Vietnam in May . The president 's two trips to Asia are expected to help reassure Asian countries that the United States still considers this region a priority . <p> In April , Mr. Obama will fly to Germany to meet with Chancellor Angela Merkel and attend the Hanover Messe , the world 's largest trade show for industrial technology , which will showcase American innovation and also establish the United States as a prime investment destination . <p> According to Washington officials , the European presidential trips are not only to advance Washington 's foreign policy goals , but also to highlight how well the United States is doing today , compared to European countries , which have been undergoing a fitful economic recovery . <p> In @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation ( APEC ) meeting , a trip which may also include a stopover in Cuba and Colombia . He will also visit Argentina , where the recent election of President Maurico Macri offers an opportunity to improve relations between the two countries . <p> During these trips abroad , Mr. Obama will work to capitalize on the United States ' strengthened position in the Americas -- including his normalization of relations with Cuba , and his support of peace and security in Colombia .
