<h> President Obama 's State of the Union Address <p> In his final State of the Union address , on January 12th , instead of the usual laundry list of legislative priorities , Mr. Obama told Americans , who are nervous about terrorists and a shifting economy , that they should not fear the future . Appearing relaxed and confident , he cited the success of the Trans-Pacific Partnership trade agreement , the health care reform , and the overhaul of the criminal justice system . There will also be executive actions on tougher gun control , and closing the detention center at Guantanamo Bay . But his mind was clearly on this next year and the country 's future . <p> In the realm of foreign policy , he has reached a nuclear accord with Iran , re-established diplomatic relations with Cuba , and signed a global climate change agreement .
