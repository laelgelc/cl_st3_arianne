<h> President Aquino ' a climate justice hypocrite ' -- Bayan Muna solons <p> Progressive solons on Saturday lambasted President Aquino for being a " climate justice hypocrite " after inaugurating a 300-megawatt coal-fired power plant in Davao City on Friday . <p> President Benigno S. Aquino III during the inauguration of the 300MW coal-fired power plant in Binugao , Toril , Davao City on Friday , January 8 . ( Photo courtesy of Alexander D. Lopez ) <p> Bayan Muna Party-list Rep. Neri Colmenares and Carlos Isagani Zarate , who sought cancellation of all coal plant contracts and plantation and mining permits , said Aquino has fully exposed his hypocrisy to reduce carbon emissions to put a stopper on climate change by inaugurating a coal-fired power plant built by Aboitiz Power Corp . <p> " A few weeks after he has stood up , forehead up high , in front of world leaders demanding climate justice , he is now cutting the ribbon to a top contributor to global warming -- coal-fired power plants , " Colmenares , a Senior Deputy Minority Leader , said . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fired power plant construction and expansion , explaining that the " energy resource accounts for a third of the world 's greenhouse gas emissions ( GHGs ) and considered one of the largest contributors to climate change and global warming . " <p> Bayan Muna filed House Resolution No. 692 seeking a moratorium on coal-fired power plant construction and expansion , which is still pending in the Committee on Energy at the House of Representatives . <p> For his part , Zarate said , " His criminal negligence at the wake of these climate change induced calamities is now topped by this unabashed support to coal-fired power plants , mining , and monocrop plantations . " <p> He said their group would continuously demand the cancellation of all coal plant contracts and plantation and mining permits , citing its adverse impacts to the environment and worsens climate change . <p> " As the primary endorser and defender of environmentally degrading projects , Pres.
