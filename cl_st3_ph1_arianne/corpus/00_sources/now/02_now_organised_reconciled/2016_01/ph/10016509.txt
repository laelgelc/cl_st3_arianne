<h> January 6th , 2016 12:09 AM <p> LA UNION farmer Federico Rullamas has a lot to smile about . <p> His second season crop of high-value organic red rice not only fetched him a very good price in the market , his yield of 170 cavans per hectare also won him the " Highest Yielder for Naturally Grown Organic Rice Farming " award in 2015 . <p> Rullamas used a new method of growing rice called the system of rice intensification ( SRI ) , which involves a simple set of rice management practices that farmers can easily learn in one season . He also supplemented SRI with composted chicken manure plus home-made organic sprays which he learned from SRI Pilipinas trainer Venancio Garde Jr . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> Garde calls his original formulation the " soil nutrient enhancer " but adds that this is no secret and that it can be learned for free via text to SRI Pilipinas Hotline ( 0939-1178999 ) . <p> Rullamas learned the new method from training that was jointly sponsored by the local government of Aringay , La Union , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chair Ramsey Mangaoang and the full support of town Mayor Eric Sibuma and Vice Mayor Charlie Juloya , the Aringay Sangguaniang Bayan has allotted P300,000 yearly for the conduct of SRI training among Aringay 's rice farmers every planting season . More than 100 Aringay farmers graduated from the training last November . <p> Following the method , Rullamas planted very young 12-day old rice seedlings singly , at a distance of 12 inches ( 30 cm ) . He practiced alternate wetting-and-drying and used a mechanical weeder to control weeds . <p> For his efforts , Rullamas was rewarded amply . He got an average of 26 tillers per plant ( more than double the typical number ) and 41 cavans from his 2,400-square meter field -- an impressive yield of about 8.5 tons or 170 cavans per hectare , compared to the national average of about 80 cavans . <p> Rullamas ' growing method is considered very climate-friendly , because it minimizes carbon dioxide emissions by avoiding fossil-based chemical fertilizers and reduces methane emission by drying the rice field regularly . <p> SRI is also considered climate-resilient , because @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and therefore less vulnerable to droughts , floods and high winds . <p> For farmers like 47-year-old Rullamas who are threatened with increasingly more extreme weather events due to climate change , that is a lot of benefit , considering that the method is also less costly than its conventional counterpart .
