<p> GENEVA -- The World Health Organization said it has approved a third producer of oral cholera vaccine that is expected to provide three million doses in 2016 , doubling the world 's stockpile against a disease that can kill within hours . <p> EuBiologics , a South Korea-based producer , joins Shantha Biotechnics of Sanofi Pasteur as a pre-qualified supplier , WHO expert Stephen Martin told a news briefing . The third producer , which currently does not have doses in the stockpile , is Sweden 's Crucell , he said . <p> " It has future potential in the next 3-4 years for a much greater increase in vaccine production , " he said . A vaccine campaign will begin in Haiti this month , targeting some 240,000 people , he added . <p> Globally , OCV production is low , with demands currently exceeding supply .
