<h> Early warning system info campaign ongoing in CAR <p> BAGUIO CITY : The Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) is bringing the Early Warning System-Information , Education and Communication ( EWS-IEC ) forums to the different Cordillera provinces . The project aims to capacitate local government units for them to mainstream science-based tools towards efficient and effective disaster risk reduction and management , in line with the goal of achieving a science-based weather information and climate change scenarios for a disaster resilient Philippines . The EWS-IEC forums started in Bontoc , Mountain Province on December 1 , in Kiangan , Ifugao on December 3 ; Bangued , Abra on December 10 and Tabuk City , Kalinga on December 15 .
