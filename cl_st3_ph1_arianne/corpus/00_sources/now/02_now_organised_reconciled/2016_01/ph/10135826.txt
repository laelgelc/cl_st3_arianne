<h> Pe ? a : Plastic ban in Clark <h> Pe ? a : Plastic ban in Clark <p> IT 'S a plastic-free Clark this 2016 . This is after the Clark Development Corporation ( CDC ) issued a Memorandum Circular ( MC ) banning and/or regulating the use of plastics and Styrofoam on December 18 last year . The MC will take effect on April 21 , the international celebration of Earth Day . <p> According to Engr . Roy Magat of CDC , this is one of the steps taken by their management to support the recently concluded Climate Change talks in Paris held last December . It 's in support too , I presume , of Republic Act 9003 or the Ecological Solid Waste Management Act of 2000 . Sorry for Metro Clark Sanitary Landfill , this will mean less business for them . <p> The MC covers all locators , government agencies and residents inside Clark including the Air Force City , SM City Clark and the Bayanihan stalls . There was no mention of Clark International Airport though . I was thinking , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is different from that of CDC ? Which one will SM Clark and bayanihan stall owners follow since they are also within the jurisdiction of Angeles City ? Will an MC prevail over an ordinance ? That would be another story . <p> For clarification , CDC is not banning all types of plastic . The MC zeroes in mainly on non-biodegradable plastic bags , commonly called ' sando ' bags . Mineral water bottles and other plastic products are not covered . Plastic bags will still be allowed as primary packaging material for wet goods ( i.e. meat products , fish ) . <p> Styrofoam on the other hand is not allowed to be used for food and drinks . This will definitely have an impact on the operation of fastfood restaurants and coffee shops . It is allowed to be used as insulator or other similar purposes . By the way , Styrofoam is a trade name of the Dow Chemical Company for housing insulation . The correct term for this material is polystyrene . <p> In lieu of plastic bags , CDC is promoting the use of reusable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ they have to be approved by the Environmental Permits Department of CDC . Those who have stocks of the prohibited materials have until April 20 to use them up . <p> Violation of the MC carries a penalty ranging from P1,000 to P5,000 plus community service for individuals and P5,000 to P20,000 plus revocation of business permits for business establishments . The MC is not clear on how individuals will be penalized . Will they be apprehended for mere possession of plastic bags ? What if they bought goods packaged in plastic or polystyrene outside of the Freeport Zone and merely brought them in ? If a business establishment packed goods bought by customers in prohibited materials , will both the establishment and the individual be penalized ? <p> Engr . Magat said that there was no public consultation prior to the release of the MC so some issues that need clarification might come up . In the LGU , an ordinance undergoes several committee hearings , public hearings and three readings in the City Council to make sure that all concerns are addressed and all stakeholders are heard . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the MC .
