<p> MANILA -- Therma South Inc. , a subsidiary of Aboitiz Power Corp . ( AboitizPower ) inaugurated on Friday a 300-megawatt coal-fired power plant , which is expected to give a long-term boost to the power supply of Mindanao , parts of which have been hit by rotating brownouts since 2010 . <p> President Benigno Aquino III led the inauguration ceremony , which was held at the power plant site in Barangay Binugao , Toril district , Davao City . <p> In his speech , Aquino said : " When this plant becomes fully operational , we can count on it to consistently produce 300 megawatts of baseload power , which means that this capacity can be tapped into anytime , rain or shine , with very minor fluctuations . To emphasize just how significant this is , this plant 's dependable capacity is roughly equivalent to one-fifth of the Mindanao grid 's highest peak demand in 2015 . " <p> In a statement posted AboitizPower website , Aboitiz said the P35-billion baseload power plant , which started construction in 2012 , reflected the company 's commitment @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of government reforms in the energy sector . <p> " Following the project 's groundbreaking and construction , other private power producers have started to invest in Mindanao and we hope their entry in the next couple of years will eventually lead to a steady and reliable supply of power in this region , " Aboitiz said . " For us , this is a strong indication that EPIRA is indeed working and has allowed private investments to come in and help Mindanao secure its power needs . EPIRA has positively transformed our power industry from a monopolized , politicized , and heavily subsidized structure into one that is competitive . " <p> Balance between renewable and nonrenewable sources <p> According to the ABC statement , the power plant currently supplies baseload power to more than 20 electric cooperatives and distribution utilities in Mindanao . <p> These cooperatives and distribution utilities provide a significant portion of the power supply to the cities of Davao , Cagayan de Oro , Gen. Santos , Zamboanga , Butuan , Kidapawan , Tagum , Koronadal , Digos , Pagadian , and Cotabato , among others @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ region -- the provinces of Cotabato , Sultan Kudarat , Agusan and Surigao ; the Zamboanga Peninsula ; and parts of Bukidnon and Misamis . <p> The first unit of the power plant -- with a capacity of 150 megawatts -- started commercial operations in September 2015 . The second unit -- with a capacity of another 150 megawatts -- is undergoing reliability tests and will be in full commercial operations by February 2016 . <p> Aboitiz said the Davao power plant would be reliable complement to Mindanao 's existing hydroelectric power sources , which he should continue to contribute a significant portion of the power supply . <p> " Our desire to achieve a balanced mix of renewable and nonrenewable energy sources is part of our mission to provide reliable and ample power supply at a reasonable and competitive price with the least possible adverse effects on our environment and our host communities , " he said . <p> Roots of the power shortage <p> EPIRA is short for the Electric Power Industry Reform Act of 2001 , which , as Aquino recalled in his speech , was aimed at @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ seen as the solution for the National Power Corp . to pay off its debts , which by 2000 had ballooned to P1.2 trillion , according to the president . <p> " Legislators from Mindanao , however , asked for a 10-year exemption from EPIRA , " Aquino said . " Perhaps it made sense in the short-term . They pointed out that there was an oversupply of power in their region , and that their energy was far cheaper compared to Luzon and Visayas . To be frank , maybe this exemption would have been fine if the situation had not changed . But the situation did change . " <p> What happened , he explained , was that government was able to provide cheap power through hydroelectric plants , on which Mindanao for more than half of its needs . <p> By 2001 , the government had already recovered its original investment on the plants , whose only major expense is the the cost of maintenance . <p> " This became a problem because the population and the economy continued to grow , which naturally increased the demand for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ government from putting up its own plants , and the private sector simply could not compete with the artificially low prices . The result : As the demand for electricity grew , the supply did n't . In fact , the hydroelectric plants that were once abundant sources of energy suffered from a number of factors , including the lack of regular maintenance , the vanishing watersheds , and the worsening effects of climate change . " <p> Aquino said he was well aware of the problem even back then . <p> " This was a problem we had anticipated even before we took office , which is why , from day one , we made a stern commitment to foster an environment that would encourage the private sector to make massive investments in energy in Mindanao , " he said . <p> He then thanked the efforts of those who headed the Department of Energy -- namely , Rene Almendras , Jericho Petilla , and now Zenaida Monsada . <p> " They did all this even in the face of harsh critics who would speak loudly against us during the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and completely forget about the issue during the rainy season , " Aquino said .
