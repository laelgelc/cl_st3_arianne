<h> GIZ keen to set up abattoir , waste removal and bio-gas plant in Rajshahi <p> RAJSHAHI , Jan 6 : German Development Corporation ( GIZ ) is interested to set up a bio-gas plant in Rajshahi city for its environmental development . <p> A two-member GIZ team expressed their interest in this regard during a courtesy call on Nizam-Ul Azim , Mayor-in-Charge of Rajshahi City Corporation ( RCC ) , at the latter 's office Wednesday . <p> The GIZ team members , Senior Adviser ( Sustainable Energy for Development ) Mudabbir Bin Anam and Adviser Sadequl Islam Talukder apprised Mayor of the need of the city and the ongoing project for development of the socio-economic condition of the poor living in the city . <p> The delegation said GIZ has taken up a pilot project titled " Urban Management of Internal Migration due to Climate Change " in Rajshahi City Corporation area for socio-economic development of the ultra poor who have been displaced from their homesteads due to impact of climate change , according to a news agency .
