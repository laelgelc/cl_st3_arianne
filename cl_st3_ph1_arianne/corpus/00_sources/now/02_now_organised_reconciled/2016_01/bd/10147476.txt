<h> Cold wave disrupts public life in north <p> The mild cold wave coupled with blowing cooler winds which marked a shard dip in temperature is disrupting public life in the northern districts Saturday , official and local sources said . <p> The minimum temperature of 5 to 6 degree Celsius caused unbearable sufferings to the commoners in the sub-Himalayan northern region . The sun was curtained by clouds , fogs and mists till noon on Saturday which affected the traffic . According to officials , experts and environmentalists , a behavioural change is prevalent and the weather has undergone change and became unpredictable . The farm-labourers could not conduct normal work and fewer numbers of people were seen outside homes as the thick layers of fogs covered the sun till noon . Environmentalist and agriculture expert Dr MG Neogi said the weather has been behaving peculiarly as a result of climate change causing uncertainty to every sectors including agriculture . The elderly men , women and babies were the worst sufferers , especially in the rural and remote char areas on the river basins . District Relief and Rehabilitation Officer Faridul Islam of Rangpur said the government has so far distributed over 15,500 pieces of blankets among the cold-stricken poor and distressed people in the district . Besides , different NGOs , business bodies , banks and other financial institutions , charitable and other organisations have also distributed huge blankets , sweaters , mufflers and rappers among the cold-hit people so far in the district , according to BSS. - Idrak
