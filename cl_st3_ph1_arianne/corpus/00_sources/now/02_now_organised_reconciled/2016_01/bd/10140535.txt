<h> Dhaka lost 60 pc wetland in 30 years <p> Environment expert Dr Shahriar Hossain Friday said more than 60 per cent of wetlands in Dhaka city have been lost in last 30 years . <p> " In 1968 there was 130 acre of wetland in Dhaka and it was found only 63 acre in 2001 , by now the city has lost more than 60 per cent of wetland in last 30 years , " he said mentioning a study conducted by SPARSO . <p> Dr Hossain , is the vice-president of World Alliance for Mercury Free Dentistry and the secretary general of Environment and Social Development Organisation - ESDO . <p> " Commercial and residential development , road construction , illegal land grabbing including mosquito control activities are the prime reasons of wetland loss , " he said . <p> Wetlands play a number of roles in the environment , principally water purification , flood control , and shoreline stability . <p> Conservation of wetland ecosystems is essential for fresh water supply , preserving biodiversity and ensuring other services necessary to the health , said the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Syed Marghub Murshed stressed youth engagement in preserving wetland for addressing the future challenges of climate change and building a sustainable and prosperous future for Bangladesh . <p> The experts stressed on the engagement of all actively in the wider environmental movement and the preservation of wetlands which is one of the many challenges of climate change adaptation , according to BSS. - Idrak
