<p> The Paris Agreement on climate change is a big step forward as it opens the door to further discussion on the issue , said members of Bangladesh delegation to the recently held climate conference in Paris . <p> Terming the agreement a great success , Bangladesh team coordinator , Qazi Kholiquzzaman Ahmad , said more than 190 countries reached a common understanding at the conference making the climate negotiation a success -- a negotiation that has been going on since 1995 . <p> Speaking at a dialogue titled " The Paris Agreement : A Critical Look " at Palli Karma Sahayak Foundation ( PKSF ) auditorium yesterday , Kholiquzzaman also expressed his concern on the implementation of the agreement , commenting that the " developed countries never kept their promises . " <p> " But if the Paris Agreement is implemented properly , the world will enjoy its benefits as it promises to keep the global temperature rise between 1.5 to 2 degrees Celsius by the end of the century , which will ultimately keep the adaptation options within reach , " he added . <p> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Parishad . <p> Delegation members attending the dialogue said the Paris Agreement , which was adopted on December 12 last year in the 21st meeting of the Conference of Parties in the French capital , has taken into account all kinds of climate concerns coming from different negotiation zones , including the developed , developing , least developed and island states . <p> Nurul Kadir , one of the delegates and a government joint secretary , said the agreement covers a number of topics that gives every one the scope to take the discussion on climate change and how to contain its effects further . <p> However , Kholiquzzaman said the agreement could have been made even stronger if the verb " should " was replaced by " shall " in some articles and provisions . <p> For instance , he said , the text says the balance between adaptation and mitigation of climate finance " should " be aimed by the parties , as opposed to " shall , " which makes the provision weak .
