<h> Over 10pc indigenous species of fish going extinct in Gopalganj <p> Our Correspondent <p> GOPALGANJ , Jan 25 : Over 10 per cent indigenous species of fish are not seen adequately in the waterbodies in the district due to indiscriminate use of chemical fertiliser , pesticides and adverse impact of climate change . Once Gopalganj was famous for native fishes but now the situation has changed . Various species of fish have already gone extinct . Gopalganj District Fisheries Office sources said about 75 per cent of 209 indigenous species of fish can be found in Gopalganj . Adverse impacts of climate change and other obstructions caused by human have pushed the existence of the rivers and canals into jeopardy making a sharp fall in production of the local fish resources there . If the declining trend of the fish resources continues this way the district will face serious deficiency of protein from fish in the near future .
