" Within the ecosystem , it is difficult to create a win-win situation or develop options for true ownership . <p> In most projects , support is provided to each family or a group of beneficiaries for capacity enhancement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ benefits for some time , usually till the end of the projects . Under current in situ co-management , resource user groups are encouraged to equitably share the benefits at the maturity stage of existing resources , but it requires 10 to 15 years ' waiting time to reap the benefits . <p> By that time , they are compounded with complex problems induced by climate change . In most cases , subsidies are exhausted after the end of the project , and those " groups " are compelled to find their own ways to survive through illicit exploitation of ecosystems . In situ co-management is not sustainable because its success is determined within the time-frame of projects , and it is apprehended to face threats in the long run . <p> In fact , what is urgently needed for protecting natural resources is to create options for recurrent resources and income generation to sustain livelihoods . <p> Ex situ co-management in sustaining livelihood and natural resources <p> Instead of allowing coastal communities to plunder in the existing ecosystems for earning their livelihoods , the CBACC-CF project involved them in diversified @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ project applied the FFF ( Forest-Fish-Fruit ) model outside the coastal forest ecosystem in 2012-14 , and the VFF ( Vegetable-Fish-Fruit ) model outside the aquatic ecosystem in 2014 . <p> Co-management -- outside forest ecosystems <p> Outside coastal forest ecosystems , hundreds of hectares of inundated and salinity-intruded khash land are lying fallow for years , which are susceptible to encroachment . The project employed the adjacent landless communities and empowered them by distributing these lands through special government notification and the applied FFF model in Naltona union of Barguna Sadar , Barguna in 2013 and in Jahajmara union of Hatiya , Noakhali in 2014 . <p> This FFF model comprises agriculture , fisheries , livestock , and forestry severely threatened by climate change , and is a unique integrated model to adapt to climate change risks . This model has ensured recurrent resource and income generation contributing to the sustainability of land-use and livelihood security of participating landless communities . Thus , ex situ co-management outside forest ecosystems reduces the illicit exploitation of natural resources which are very common in coastal Bangladesh due to manpower constraints in the government 's Forest Department ( one forest guard is responsible for protecting more than 1000 hectare ) . <p> Co-management -- outside aquatic ecosystems <p> It has been identified that hectares of sporadically excavated land near the embankment remains barren for a long time after the construction of earthen embankments throughout Bangladesh . Earthen embankments are eroded by tidal surges , but the Bangladesh Water Development Board is not mandated to repair partially-eroded embankments until or unless it has totally disappeared . <p> The project has applied techniques to excavate large fresh water reservoirs instead of excavating sporadically during the construction of earthen embankments at Char Kukri Mukri in 2014 . The CBACC-CF project applied the VFF model and used these large reservoirs ( excavated at the time of constructing earthen embankments ) for resource-generation by involving local , landless communities and empowered them with ownership rights . <p> Within one season ( June-October , 2014 ) , the beneficiaries of the VFF model harvested 45,000kg of different types of vegetables and more than 21,000kg of fresh water fishes from eight reservoirs by June , 2015 . After fulfilling the nutritional demand of whole island communities , surplus vegetables and fish have been sold outside of Char Kukri Mukri . <p> The result has created a win-win situation , and now the VFF beneficiaries are taking care of the partially or totally eroded earthen embankments . Availability of fresh water fish from reservoirs are minimising the exploitation of the surrounding aquatic resources contributing to the conservation of aquatic diversity . <p> The CBACC-CF project has transformed barren lands into highly productive ones .
