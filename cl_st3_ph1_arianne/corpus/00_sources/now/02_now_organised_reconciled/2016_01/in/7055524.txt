They are clogged with traffic , choked with pollution , blighted by concrete flyovers , overcrowded , suffer from power and water shortages , are prone to flooding and can at times be almost unbearable to live in . The plan to introduce ' smart cities ' to India is intended to remedy many of these problems . These smart cities will function effectively , however , only if many of the underlying issues are addressed . <p> From the crisis in farming and associated rural migration to energy concerns and an expanding population , the problems are immense and varied .
