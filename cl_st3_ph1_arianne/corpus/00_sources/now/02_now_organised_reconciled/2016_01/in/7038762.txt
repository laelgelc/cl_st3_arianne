Under the new scheme , the government intends to use new technology in a big way to assess the farm loss so that farmers can get full compensation without reduction in insured sum . <p> In the new scheme , the Centre will also ensure that the farmers will get claim within the shortest possible time by use of drone for assessing the damage and smart phone and online transmission data for cutting down the time taken by the state governments to finalize the estimated yield . <p> Sharing the details of the national conference , Union agriculture minister Radha Mohan Singh on Friday said , " The conference will identify initiatives to transform agriculture based on sustainable and efficient use of resources like soil and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ produce , risk coverage and mitigation of potential negative impact of climate change " . <p> The centre 's intention to use science and technology in farm sector in a big way was also reflected in two grand papers - Technology Vision 2035 and government 's perspective on science &amp; technology - that were presented during the Indian Science Congress during January 3-7 . <p> The Centre in both these documents talked about biotechnological solutions to maintain the contribution of farm sector to the GDP . Making projections for future demand , it also spoke about bio-fortification where nutritional quality of food crops is improved through use of biotechnology . <p> Both the papers clearly indicate the centre 's plan to explore GM technology while simultaneously using both conventional as well as organic farming wherever it is most suited in terms of availability of resources like water and quality of soil . <p> Asked about his views on the use of GM food crops in Indian context where even the field trials of various transgenic varieties of crops were denied by states , eminent agricultural scientist Prof M S Swaminathan said @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not be able to assess the benefits and risks in a reliable manner .
