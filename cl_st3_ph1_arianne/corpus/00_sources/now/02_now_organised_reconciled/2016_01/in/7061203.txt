<p> Chaitram D Pawar , a member of the Forest Advisory Committee under the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , claimed that the Baripada village in NorthMaharashtra was free from all sorts of problems . <p> Mr Pawar , who is credited for transforming a tiny village of Baripada with a household of little over 750 families in Dhule district of North Maharashtra , was speaking to reporters after delivering the 30th Rambhau Mhalgi memorial lecture here last night . <p> Replying to questions , he claimed that his village was free from all sorts of problems that one generally finds in other parts of the state . <p> He said that the only way to avoid farmers ' suicides was to reduce the requirements of a person . <p> He claimed that there was no malnutrition in the village , no farmer reeling under mounting debt , no complaint of cutting of trees , no shortage of water no politics and no political interference in the affairs of the village , because the villagers were self supportive . <p> He attributed this to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ their own rules and do not depend on the government for anything . <p> Mr Pawar also claimed that the villagers have protected over 1,100 acres of forest cover for the village which takes care of their needs . <p> He said since the last four years the villagers had started cultivating strawberries and in ane acre they produce yield to the tune of Rs 60,000 . <p> He also said that the UNDP had recently sanctioned Rs 17 lakh for implementation of the smokeless ' chula ' ( hearth ) in the village . <p> The village generates its own power using solar panels , he informed . <p> ' ' We are also going in for a common cattle shed for the village which will also benefit the villagers , ' ' he added.UNI XR SS RSA BD2223
