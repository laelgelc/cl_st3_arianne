<h> Custom Navigation <h> Adhyaksha inspects ongoing works at Little Andaman <p> Port Blair <p> 19 Jan 2016 <p> Minati Biswas , Adhyaksha , alongwith Bhagyaswari and Raj Govind , Zilla Parishad Members of South Andaman Zilla Parishad , were on a visit to Little Andaman from January 16 to 18 for inspecting the ongoing works under Rural Roads , Water supply and other facilities provided by Zilla Parishad ( South Andaman ) in the area . <p> Biswas met the Union Minister of State ( Independent Charge ) , Environment , Forests &amp; Climate Change , Mr. Prakash Javadekar who was also on a visit to Little Andaman and apprised him about the requirements of the general public of the area viz. a new electricity power house at R K Pur with all necessary provisions , granting permission by the Forest Department for cutting Bamboos , Balli etc. for construction of Katcha Houses , declaring Little Andaman as a Tourist Place , construction of a Fisheries Jetty in the Dinghi Ghat and proper demarcation of C R Zone . <p> She demanded the provision of better ship @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and better communication facility etc . She also requested the Minister for the allotment of land along with basic facilities like electricity and water connection etc. in the name of the poor Islanders of Andaman &amp; Nicobar Islands who are residing in the Forest area for a long time . <p> Biswas interacted with the local people of that area and found that many developmental works are to be carried out in the place viz. construction of Helipad in R K Pur , construction of Jetty at Bimla Creek , setting up of CHC , improving shipping services , construction of a Guest House by the Zilla Parishad , major repairs to the existing Stadium constructed by the Department of Sports , Arts &amp; Culture . It has been a long pending demand to set up an Assistant Commissioner 's Office at R K Pur . <p> A Musical Prasikshan Kendra needs to be set up there especially for the local girl children who are interested in Music , Dance etc. as they should not be left behind simply because they are residing in the far flung Islands . The Adhyaksha @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Panchayat requires certain important developmental works to be taken up <p> immediately viz. construction of a Fish Market , a Vegetable Market , setting up of R O Plant , dumping yard etc . She assured the general public of the area that all possible support will be provided to them and necessary action in this regard will be initiated immediately .
