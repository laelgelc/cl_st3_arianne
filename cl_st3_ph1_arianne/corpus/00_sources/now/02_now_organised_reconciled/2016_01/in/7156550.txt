<h> Custom Navigation <h> An octogenarian 's determination to save planet Earth <p> Report by : <p> PRASHANT ACHARYA <p> Siliguri <p> 15 Jan 2016 <p> Even though he is 85 , veteran tea planter L B Dewan of Darjeeling still plants trees with the firm belief that " saving trees saves us " . He has been doing this commendable work since the last 45 years . Impressed by Dewan 's dedication towards the environment , Union Minister of Environment , Forests and Climate Change PrakashJavadekar said , " You are becoming young at this age since you love the environment ; you will get a call from my office very soon . " The octogenarian was called to Javadekar 's office in New Delhi on November 26 last year where he briefed the minister about his efforts to save the environment . The founder , president and mentor of Mirik-based NGO ' Friends of Trees Forum ' , Dewan has won accolades for planting more than 1.5 lakh tree saplings in over a decade 's time . " I will work to save this beautiful planet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ awareness among people about this , " he says . Residents of Murma tea estate in Mirik revere Dewan and refer to him as God 's angel as he was the one who founded the primary and secondary schools in the area . He believes that without education it would be difficult to explain to people the adverse effects of deforestation on the environment . LaxmiLimbuKaushal , who is associated with the North Bengal and Sikkim chapter of the Confederation of Indian Industries , said , " I respect Dewan sir 's dedication and spirit even though he is 85 years of age . He holds excellent knowledge about tea and the environment and has written a number of articles that have been published in Nepali and English languages in both local and national dailies . "
