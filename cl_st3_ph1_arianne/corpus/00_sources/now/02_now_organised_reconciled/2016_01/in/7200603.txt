<h> Parliamentary panel set to visit Pench and Nagpur <p> Nagpur : A parliamentary standing committee on Science and Technology and Environment and Forests will be visiting Pench Tiger Reserve ( Madhya Pradesh ) and Nagpur on January 23 and 24 , respectively . <p> The parliamentary committee will be visiting Pench on January 23 around 11am . It will conduct a discussion on ' The Compensatory Afforestation Fund Bill , 2015 ' and environmental issues specific to Pench with representatives of NGOs , civil society organizations , experts and other stakeholders . <p> This will be followed by a discussion with representatives of government of Madhya Pradesh and Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change on the same issues . <p> On January 24 , the committee will arrive at Nagpur to discuss the same issue with local NGOs and stakeholders .
