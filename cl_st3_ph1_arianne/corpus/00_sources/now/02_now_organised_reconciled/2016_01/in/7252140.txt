<h> French President Francois Hollande to be chief guest at R-Day celebrations <p> French President Francois Hollande will be the chief guest at India 's Republic Day celebrations on January 26 as part of a visit during which the two countries hope to make progress in all areas of bilateral cooperation and are expected to finalise their deal for sale of Rafale fighter aircraft to India . <p> Ministry of External Affairs spokesperson Vikas Swarup told journalists at his weekly briefing that this would be the fifth time that a serving President of France would be the chief guest at the Republic Day function . <p> He said this was a record in that no other country had had this honour so many times . <p> " Our bilateral relationship with France is very comprehensive . It covers number of sectors such as defence , civil nuclear cooperation , railways , smart cities , science and research , space and culture .
