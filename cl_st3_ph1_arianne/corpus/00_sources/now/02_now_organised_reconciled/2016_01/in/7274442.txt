<p> PM Modi and President Hollande inaugurate the interim secretariat of the International Solar Alliance . Hollande says it is India 's gift to the world reeling under the impact of global warming . <p> In yet another step towards clean energy , Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Francois Hollande on Monday jointly inaugurated the interim secretariat of the International Solar Alliance , an initiative launched by both the leaders at the Climate Change summit in Paris . The initiative is aimed at increasing utilisation and promotion of solar energy . <p> India once again showed its commitment towards clean energy , when Prime Minister Narendra Modi alongwith French President Francois Hollande , jointly inaugurated the interim secretariat of the International Solar Alliance and unveiled the plaque for foundation stone of the ISA headquarters at a function in Gurgaon . <p> This is a new unique beginning of the International Solar Alliance to save the world from the danger of global warming and producing more and more energy by tapping the power of the sun . <p> On the occasion of the laying of foundation stone @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ world is concerned about global warming but India , France and U.S took a joint initiative at COP21 to promote innovation that is sustainable , affordable and accessible . <p> The Prime Minister said that although ISA is headquartered in India , it is a global institution meant for the benefit of mankind . <p> On this occasion , French President said that a new chapter opens for nations to utilize the power of the sun and the International Solar Alliance is India 's initiative and a gift from India to the world which is reeling under global warming . This Alliance intends to bring together 122 countries that have more than 300 days of sunlight in a year . <p> The most significant aspect worth mentioning is that both Prime Minister Narendra Modi and French President Francois Hollande took an eco-friendly ride on the Delhi Metro to travel to Gurgaon to take part in this programme . This ride sent a clear message about the need for clean energy .
