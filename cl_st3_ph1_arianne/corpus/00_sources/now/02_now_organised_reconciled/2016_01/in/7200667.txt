<h> Custom Navigation <h> Forestry , fishery , tourism three pillars of development : Javadekar <p> Report by : <p> MARIANNE SIMON <p> Port Blair <p> 18 Jan 2016 <p> Highlighting that protecting the fragile eco system of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands and public purpose are the only criteria for approval of developmental projects proposed for the Islands , Union minister for environment , forest and climate change , Prakash Javadekar today said that he would extend all his support to every viable project . <p> Javadekar was speaking to the media at a press conference held at the Secretariat this evening . Bishnu Pada Ray , MP , Anand Prakash , chief secretary , A &amp; N Administration , and many officials were also present on the occasion . The minister said that the Islands are abundant in natural resources . He called tourism , fisheries and forestry are the three pillars of development . If properly developed , Forestry alone will provide jobs not only to all the Islanders , but also to people from outside . " There is tremendous scope for overall development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ been able to tap the potential of the people who have come from all parts of India and settled down here and are contributing to the growth of the Islands , " said Javadekar . <p> The minister said that in the last three days he had travelled to all parts of the Islands and had in-depth interactions with people , officials and tribals . " I have realised that the then administration did not place before the Government matters in the right perspective . If we review the situation and present it again in the right perspective , it will be easier to achieve the goals of development . " He advised the officials to review the projects and support them with justifiable data and present them to the Government , in a simple , straightforward manner , for approval .
