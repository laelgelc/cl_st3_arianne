<p> The committee will go through the environmental status of Karnataka , including Bengaluru , and Goa , according to an official note . <p> Members of a standing committee of Parliament will be visiting Karnataka and Goa next week to discuss important issues related to environment and forests with NGOs and other stake-holders . <p> Parliamentary Standing Committee on Science &amp; Technology , Environment and Forests will be in Bengaluru on February 4-5 and in Goa on February 6-7 . The panel is headed by Congress MP and former Union minister Ashwani Kumar . <p> The committee will go through the environmental status of Karnataka , including Bengaluru , and Goa , according to an official note . <p> " The committee will study status of air and water quality , waste management , management of hazardous &amp; bio-medical waste , plastic waste and e-waste of industries in both States , " it said . <p> The compliance status , number of industrial units under red , orange and green categories , capacity of sewage treatment plants , status of vehicular pollution and other aspects will also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Boards of the respective states have been asked to provide relevant data . <p> In Bengaluru , the panel will hold discussion with representatives of NGOs , civil society organisations , experts and other stakeholders on " The Compensatory Afforestation Fund Bill , 2015 " and environmental issues of Bengaluru in participial and Karnataka in general . The panel will have similar discussion in Goa , too . <p> The committee will meet representatives of Union Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change and officials from Karnataka Government on the Bill . It will also visit ISRO Satellite Centre , Bengaluru , and discuss functioning of the organisation with authorities there . The House panel will visit National Centre for Antarctic and Ocean Research in Vasco town , about 25km from in Goa.
