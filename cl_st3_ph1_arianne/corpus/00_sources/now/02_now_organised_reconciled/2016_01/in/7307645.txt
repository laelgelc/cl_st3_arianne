<h> State Editions <h> Call for efforts to raise productivity of forests <p> A substantial portion of the forests in India is not producing enough timber and eco-system services . Effort needs to be focused on enhancing the quality and increasing the productivity of the nation 's forests at a time when some Indian States are struggling to achieve the vital balance between developmental works and environmental conservation . Director-General of Forests and Special Secretary in the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change , Dr Sharad Singh Negi said this while speaking at the convocation of 2014-16 batch , Central Academy for State Forest Service ( CASFoS ) at the Forest Research Institute here on Sunday . <p> Addressing the officer trainees on the occasion , Negi said , " The forestry sector has opened up in a big way with technology helping to take it beyond the traditional in forestry . Though information technology is being used for working plans , as forestry officials you should keep abreast of latest technological developments . We need to enhance the quality and productivity of our forests also because a substantial proportion of the total forest area is not producing enough timber and eco system services . However , with about 35,000 hectares of forest land being diverted in a year for developmental activities , it is vital to achieve necessary balance between environmental conservation and developmental works . Some States with considerable mining activity are struggling to achieve this balance . As forestry officers , try to change the system for the better wherever you are posted .
