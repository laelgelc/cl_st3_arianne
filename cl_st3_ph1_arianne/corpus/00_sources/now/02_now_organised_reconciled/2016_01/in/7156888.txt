<h> Grim reapers <p> Rice grains arranged in a pattern recall the loss of livelihood of weavers in Navjot Altaf 's show at The Guild gallery . Photographs courtesy The Guild <p> More than a decade ago , a retired school- teacher began collecting indigenous varieties of the paddy seed , whose significance had been dimmed greatly by the Green Revolution and its stress on industrial hybrid seeds . It was an endeavour of high impact . Natabar Sarangi , of Narishu village , 35km from Bhubaneswar in Odisha , collected seeds that were , variously , drought-tolerant , flood-tolerant , saline-tolerant and cold-tolerant ; varieties meant for different soils like red , black , hilly and sandy . It was a throwback to a time when Odisha had 30,000 high-yielding indigenous varieties of rice . Due to increased dependence on chemical fertilizers and seeds sold off a shelf , the state now has only 20 high-yielding varieties of paddy seeds .
