<h> Sports <p> MYSURU : Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Sunday espoused the principle of five Es for scientists to maximise the impact of science and technology . He also assured that his government is working to make carrying out scientific research easy in India . <p> Inaugurating the 103rd Science Congress here , the prime minister also emphasised on harnessing the full potential of traditional knowledge to restore the harmony between human and nature . <p> " The impact of science will be the most when scientists and technologists keep in mind the principle of what I call ' Five Es ' at the centre of their enquiry and engineering , " he said . The five Es stand for economy , environment , energy , empathy and equity . <p> Calling upon the scientific community to bridge the distance between traditional knowledge and modern science , Modi said " we can craft local and more sustainable solutions for our challenges . " <p> " Societies across the world have developed this enormous wealth through wisdom gathered over the ages . They hold the secrets to economic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ problems . But today , they are at risk of extinction in our globalised world , " he said . <p> Lending support to practice of traditional knowledge , he said we must recognize that science , as we see it , does not constitute the only form of empirical knowledge about the world . <p> " So , in agriculture , even as we seek to make our farms yield more , reduce the intensity of our water use , or increase the nutrient content of our farm output , we should also integrate traditional techniques , local practices and organic farming to make our agriculture less resource -intensive and more resilient , " he said . <p> Using scientific techniques and methods to delve deeper into traditional medicines and practices like yoga for more holistic lifestyle was also a key area of focus touched upon by prime minister . <p> The PM said marine science , ocean economy and cleaning of rivers are also important to ensuring sustainable future for the planet . " We are at the cusp of a new era , where oceans will become important @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ prosperity , and give us clean energy , new medicines and food security beyond just fisheries , " he said . <p> He announced that the Union government will set up an advanced centre of research in marine biology and biotechnology and establish a network of coastal and island research stations in India and abroad . <p> Lauding the agreement reached at the Paris climate talks in December 2015 , he said that India succeeded in bringing innovation and technology to the heart of the climate change discourse .
