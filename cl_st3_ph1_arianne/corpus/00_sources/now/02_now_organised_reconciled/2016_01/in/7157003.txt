<h> U.S. orders halt of new coal leases on federal land that could last years <p> WASHINGTON , Jan 15 ( ) - U.S. Interior Secretary Sally Jewell on Friday ordered a pause on issuing new coal leases on federal land as the Obama administration takes another step to control climate change . <p> The pause in new coal leasing could last three years , Jewell said , while officials determine how to protect the taxpayers ' stake in coal sales from public lands and how burning coal could worsen climate change . <p> This is the first major review of the country 's coal program in three decades . Federal land accounts for over 40 percent of U.S. coal production .
