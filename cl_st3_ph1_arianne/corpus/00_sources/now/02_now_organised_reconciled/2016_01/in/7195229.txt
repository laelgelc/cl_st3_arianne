<h> Nabard targets tea growers <p> Abhranila Das <p> Calcutta , Jan. 17 : The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development ( Nabard ) has met the stakeholders of the state 's tea industry to inform them about the schemes that the bank can offer to producers , especially small growers . <p> " As Nabard is a non-profit organisation and works for the upliftment of rural economy and status of rural people , the meeting was basically to inform about the schemes available with the bank , which can help the tea industry , " a senior official of the bank said . <p> " This is the first time that Nabard has come forward to help small tea growers and this will benefit us greatly , " Bijoy Gopal Chakraborty , president of the Confederation of Indian Small Tea Grower 's Association , told The Telegraph . <p> Several schemes were mentioned by the bank , including the producers organisation development fund ( PODF ) . <p> The bank had taken an initiative to help producer organisations to meet their needs . The PODF had @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ initial corpus of Rs 50 crore . <p> Any producer organisation , industrial co-operative societies and other registered federations set up by producers are eligible for the scheme . Credit is provided in the form of grant , loans , or a combination of both . <p> These loan-based financial products may be in the form of investment credit ( term loan ) for funding core natural resource management activities and short-term credit for supplementary activities . <p> The national infrastructure development assistance is also available for the industry . It is designed to fund state-owned institutions or corporations to build rural infrastructure . The assistance is available on flexible interest terms with longer repayment period of up to 15 years. # <p> Nabard has also been accredited by the " green climate fund ( GCF ) " as an implementing entity for undertaking projects related to climate change .
