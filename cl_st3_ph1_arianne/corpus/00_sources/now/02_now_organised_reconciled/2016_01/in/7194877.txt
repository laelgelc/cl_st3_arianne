<h> Custom Navigation <h> Union Minister visits Kamorta &amp; Hutbay <p> Discusses various developmental issues <p> Report by : <p> Agencies <p> Port Blair <p> 17 Jan 2016 <p> The Union Minister of State ( Independent Charge ) , Environment , Forest and Climate Change , Shri Prakash Javadekar , currently on a visit to the A&amp;N Islands , today visited Kamorta Island . He was accompanied by the Member of Parliament , Shri Bishnu Pada Ray , BJP State President , Shri Vishal Jolly , PCCF and other officials of the A&amp;N Administration . The Minister held a meeting with the officials of the Administration and representatives of Tribal Council , Nancowrie , at the EOC of Assistant Commissioner 's Office , Nancowrie . <p> While addressing the gathering on the occasion , the Minister underlined the need for implementing Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojana ( PMGSY ) in the tribal areas of Nicobar District . Accordingly , the PWD was directed to submit a plan proposal under PMGSY for implementation there . The Minister also stressed that all the Central Govt. schemes should be implemented in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ take up skill development trainings at Port Blair . The Minister also enquired about the prospects of oil palm cultivation on grasslands there . <p> The First Captain , Champin , Shri Andrew Paul also raised issues regarding damaged crops and plantation , post-tsunami in the Islands . Other issues that came up in the discussion included construction of a dam at Kamorta , ongoing work on the road from Champin to Tapong , water supply at Champin village , inter-island ship connectivity , implementation of MNREGA , health issues , fish can industry at Nancowrie etc . The Deputy Commissioner , Nicobar District , Smt Neha Bansal and the representatives of the Tribal Council , Nancowrie were also present in the meeting . <p> Later in the afternoon , the Minister visited Hut Bay , Little Andaman . He was accompanied by the Member of Parliament , Shri Bishnu Pada Ray , BJP State President , Shri Vishal Jolly and other officials of the A&amp;N Administration . The Minister held a discussion with the officials of Department of Environment &amp; Forests in the APWD Guest House at Hut Bay . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Hut Bay , during which issues of reviving red oil palm plantation , development of tourism , use of un-inhabitated Islands , relaxing ban imposed by Hon'ble Supreme Court on logging etc. were addressed by the Minister .
