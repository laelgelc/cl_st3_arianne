<h> Sports <p> NEW DELHI : The hot story of the winter of 2016 is that it has broken the 114-year-old record of being the warmest winter in over a century . Weather experts predict that the summer of 2016 is going to be a blazing one , bringing with it a decline in food grain production resulting in vertical food prices , farmer suicides after crop failures , heat wave casualties , summer epidemics like swine flu , typhoid and diarrhoea and a crippling power and water crisis . <p> At a time when the Modi government is committed to battling the dangers of climate change , and the PM himself having attended the World Climate Change Conference last November in Paris , a comparison of temperatures between 1901 and 2015 by the Indian Meteorological Department ( IMD ) has found that the mercury has been hovering above normal since September 2015 and the average mean temperature so far has been 1.2 degrees Celsius over the normal level .
