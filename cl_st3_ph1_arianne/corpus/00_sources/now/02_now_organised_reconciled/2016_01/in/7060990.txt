<p> By a judgement in May 2014 , the top court had prohibited the use of bulls in ' Jallikattu ' festivals , holding this practice to be an offence under the law . <p> Questioning the " necessity of such festivals " , the Supreme Court Tuesday restrained the Tamil Nadu government from conducting its traditional bull-taming sport Jallikattu , and stayed the Centre 's notification lifting the ban on it . <p> " What is the necessity of such festivals ... like Jallikattu ?
