<p> Climate change could play a spoiler as India races to increase electricity production to meet the demands of the as-yet-unserved 300 million households and fuel economic growth . While India may have secured the use of coal in its energy mix at the Paris climate change talks , new research reveals that electricity generated by coal-fired plants is likely to reduce drastically as global warming starts adversely affecting water sources . <p> Increased incidences of heat waves and droughts will endanger electricity production around the world unless steps are taken to adjust to the rising possibilities of water scarcity.It is n't just coal power plants that will be affected . Burning of coal , natural gas and oil for electricity and heat is the single largest source of greenhouse gas emissions globally , according to the US Environmental Protection Agency . <p> Using data from 24,515 hydropower and 1,427 thermal power plants across the world , scientists led by Michelle van Vliet , a researcher at both Wageningen University and IIASA , found that output from thermal power plants could drop by 81 to 86% , while production from hydropower plants could reduce by 61 to 74% of usable capacity .
