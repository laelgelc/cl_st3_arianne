<p> In December 2015 , leaders from 150 nations along with 40,000 delegates from 195 countries gathered at the 21st Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change in Paris , France , also known as COP21 . These leaders agreed to a very important mission : to keep global warming below 2 degrees Celsius . <p> Starting in the new year , Black Star Energy , a Ghanaian subsidiary of U.S.-based Energicity Corp , aspires to join these leaders as a part of the climate change solution . Black Star Energy seeks to reduce deforestation in Ghana , specifically in the rural communities of the Ashanti region , by providing solar panels as an alternative to wood fuel . <p> Deforestation has been identified as a critical environmental issue in Ghana , where 33.7 percent of forested land , equivalent to 2,500,000 acres , has been lost since the early 1990s .
