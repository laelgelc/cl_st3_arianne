<h> Footprints : Elsa May Binns - A true daughter of the soil <p> Robert Pickersgill , minister of land , water , environment and climate change , was among many who took the time to reflect on the life of Elsa May Binns recently . <p> Speaking during the funeral , held last year at the St Matthew 's Anglican Church in downtown Kingston , Pickersgill , in his tribute , noted that Binns was an extraordinary public servant who had a passion for country . <p> " Today , we celebrate a woman who has given herself unhesitatingly and generously in service to others . She has touched the lives of so many , and today we remember Elsa May Binns as a committed , dedicated public servant and a true daughter of the soil , " Pickersgill told the gathering . <p> " It was my distinct pleasure to have worked with her for several years . We shared a lot not only as a minister and as a civil servant but as analysts of national issues . Anyone who knew Elsa , knew that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to her love for her children . She made sure all of them did well , " he continued . <p> In a Footprints publication , the family indicated that Binns will be remembered as a mother , friend and counsellor . <p> " Despite being actively involved in family life , she was not prevented from accomplishing her many dreams , academic and otherwise . " <p> " She entered the government service at 18 years old and retired at 59 years , holding various positions from administrative clerk , supervisor , acting permanent secretary , and retired as chief technical director . She spoke to any and everyone . She was a woman for all seasons .
