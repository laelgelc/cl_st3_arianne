C. Even though shipping has been left out of the Paris Agreement , there is a mounting pressure on the industry players to shift to more environmentally-friendly solutions . Adoption of liquefied natural gas ( LNG ) as marine fuel has been one of the most common solutions for ship owners , and 2015 has seen numerous industry- firsts and industry records , such as the largest LNG-powered containership .
