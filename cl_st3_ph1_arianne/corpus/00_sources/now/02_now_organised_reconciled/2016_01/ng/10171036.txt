<p> The National Orientation Agency has called for responsible action from all Nigerians toward protecting the environment and mitigating the adverse effects of climate change . <p> The Director General of the Agency , Mr. Mike Omeri made the call during a strategic meeting with the Climate Change Advisor , Nigeria Infrastructure Advisory Facility , Mrs. Chintal Barot in Abuja . <p> Mr. Omeri identified the need for massive sensitisation of Nigerians on the meaning , effects and prevention of climate change , pointing out that positive behaviour change is the surest way to stem the ugly side of climate change . <p> He urged Nigerians to always keep their environment clean and avoid dumping of refuse in drainages and water ways , as such negative habits contribute significantly to environmental degradation and climate change . <p> The NOA boss also canvassed the utilisation of solar energy as an environment friendly energy source . According to him , the Agency has assisted in providing some rural communities in the country with solar energy under its Orientation with Benefits model . <p> In her remarks , the Nigeria Infrastructure @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Barot observed that very little of the global climate change financing comes to Nigeria hence the meeting was part of several on-going consultations with relevant Nigerian bodies to facilitate Nigeria 's access to the funds .
