<h> U.S. : Nigerians frustrated by corruption , extremism <p> NIgerians are frustrated by corruption and extremism , the United States ( U.S. ) said yesterday . <p> U.S. Special Representative for Religion and Global Affairs Shaun Casey spoke with reporters at the U.S. Consulate in Lagos . <p> He spoke about an initiative at the instance of the Secretary of State John Kerry to train staff of the U.S. Mission across the world on building relationship with global religious organisations and leaders . <p> Casey said the U.S. government would create inter-religious conversations where different religious denominations and groups could collaborate to fight corruption and social injustice . <p> He said : " Given its diversity , we want Nigeria to become a just society . We sense real frustration with the level of corruption in the country . From the people in the streets and senior religious leaders , there is deep dissatisfaction about corruption and extremism . That is what we want to work with .
