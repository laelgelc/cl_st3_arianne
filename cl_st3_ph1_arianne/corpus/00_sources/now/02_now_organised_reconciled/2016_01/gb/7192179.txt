<p> As Cumbria faced destructive floods at the end of last year , history seemed to be repeating . Disasters are ten-a-penny , some natural , many more due to man 's ham-fisted neglect of the planet or our inability to get by without recourse to violence . <p> The result is always the need for a new start , and how we respond and rebuild colours an uncertain future more than ever .
