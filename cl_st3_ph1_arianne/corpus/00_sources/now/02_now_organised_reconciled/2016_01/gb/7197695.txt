<h> Landlords reject ' open ' assignation of farm tenancies <p> SCOTLAND 'S LANDOWNERS do not want tenant farmers with secure ' 1991 act ' tenancies - but no successor - to be given the right to assign their tenancies , for value , to a new entrant or progressing farmer . <p> In a submission to the Scottish Parliament 's Rural Affairs , Climate Change and Environment committee , landowner body Scottish Land and Estates has strongly objected to a Scottish Government amendment that would introduce an open assignation clause to the agri-holdings section of the Land Reform Bill . <p> Loading article content <p> SLaE said that the proposed amendment posed a " major and unnecessary threat to the future of tenant farming " , because the move to open assignations would make many landlords wary of letting land in the first place . <p> Supporters of the open assignation clause idea believe it is balanced by also giving landlords the option of purchasing a tenant 's interest in their lease and improvements - however , SLaE argued that this " buy-out option " would be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ financial resources , particularly small-scale landowners . <p> In its evidence , SLaE called on the RACCE Committee to reject the open assignation amendment and instead endorse the proposal that would give secure tenants the option of converting their secure tenancies , for value , to long-term , limited duration tenancies . <p> SLaE chair David Johnstone said : " There has not been sufficient justification , consultation or explanation for the change in policy by the Scottish Government at this late stage of the parliamentary process .
