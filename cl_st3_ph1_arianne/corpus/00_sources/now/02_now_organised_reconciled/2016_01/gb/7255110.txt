<p> One of the Australian Labor party 's most outspoken opponents of offshore detention , mass surveillance and live animal exports will not contest the next federal election . <p> Melissa Parke , who has represented the West Australian seat of Fremantle since 2007 , said she would not seek a fourth term because it was " time for me to be closer to my family and to travel less " . <p> " I believe that renewal is a good tonic for our democracy , " she said on Friday . <p> Parke , a former UN lawyer , has been a frequent critic of the positions adopted by Australia 's main political parties on asylum seeker policy and laws increasing the powers of security agencies in the name of counter-terrorism . <p> Three months ago , she renewed calls for Labor to drop its support for the processing of asylum seekers on Nauru and Manus Island .
