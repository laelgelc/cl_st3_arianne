<h> Nicoll Drive being raised to stave off rising seas <p> The LTA 's road-raising project will elevate the 1km , two-lane dual- carriage Nicoll Drive , which hugs the eastern shoreline next to Changi Beach , by up to 0.8m . The project is expected to be completed by the middle of this year.ST PHOTO : SEAHKWANG PENG <h> It 's first such project undertaken to prepare for impact of climate change on Singapore <p> Nicoll Drive , which hugs the eastern shoreline next to Changi Beach , is being raised in anticipation of rising sea levels triggered by global warming . <p> The road-raising project - which elevates the 1km , two-lane dual carriage by up to 0.8m - is the first Singapore is undertaking to brace itself for the effects of climate change . <p> The Land Transport Authority ( LTA ) said : " As this stretch of road is located near the coastline , the works are being carried out to minimise the risk of seawater inundation as part of the Government 's overall coastal protection measures for climate change adaptation .
