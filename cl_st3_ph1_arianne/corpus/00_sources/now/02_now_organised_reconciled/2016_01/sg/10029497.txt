<h> Biotech expert returning here to research super crops <p> World-renowned biotechnology expert Chua Nam Hai is returning to Singapore with the ambitious goal of helping to solve the world 's problems of hunger by producing super plants . <p> Professor Chua , who has lived in New York since 1971 when he joined Rockefeller University , has been studying a class of ribonucleic acids ( RNAs ) in plants which respond to stresses caused by the environment and other living organisms . <p> And the returning scientist hopes to take it further in the Temasek Life Sciences Laboratory ( TLL ) , a non-profit research institute funded by Temasek Trust , by studying molecular biology and genetics in model organisms . <p> Prof Chua , 71 , has been deputy chairman of TLL 's management board and chairman of its research strategy committee since 2013 but has kept his ties to Singapore for far longer . <p> " Because of my frequent visits during the last two decades , I remain connected to life in Singapore and , hence , I expect the transition to be smooth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Singapore once he ties up his research at Rockefeller University . <p> At Rockefeller , he heads the Laboratory of Plant Molecular Biology , and is Andrew W. Mellon Professor , investigating the role of long noncoding RNAs in the way they respond to droughts caused by climate change , among other stresses . <p> RNA acts as a messenger in the body by transferring instructions from DNA to produce proteins . In long noncoding RNAs , however , proteins are not produced . <p> Such RNAs are not fully understood , even though they have been implicated in important biological processes . <p> But Prof Chua is on to something . <p> His lab had earlier discovered a specific group of long noncoding RNAs which are highly responsive to drought stress , suggesting that they could play a regulatory function in how plants adapt to such situations . <p> To find out if this is true , he will be creating experimental plants under a new research programme in TLL that are either deficient in this specific group of RNA molecules or over-expressing them . <p> " I plan to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can be used as tools to open up new strategies to develop crops with enhanced drought tolerance and crops with more efficient utilisation of fertilisers , " said Prof Chua . <p> " We believe that the result from these experiments could contribute to global food security as it helps to create sustainability and resilience in food supply .
