<h> Staving off threat of rising sea levels <p> Nicoll Drive , which hugs the eastern coastline , is being raised by up to 80cm , in anticipation of rising sea levels brought on by climate change . The elevation of the 1km , two-lane dual carriageway is the first road-raising project of its kind in Singapore . The Land Transport Authority said the works will be completed by the middle of the year .
