<h> HDB focus on couples , needy and elderly folk <p> Having met demand for HDB flats , the Housing Board will now make sure young couples and the lower-income get the help they need to own their homes . <p> The elderly , too , will get help moving to smaller homes as well as moving about more easily at home and in their estates . <p> The Ministry of National Development ( MND ) , which oversees the Housing Board , outlined these plans yesterday in its addenda to last Friday 's President 's address . <p> Most importantly , public housing will continue to be inclusive . <p> Said National Development Minister Lawrence Wong : " We will ensure that our housing policies continue to help young couples start a family , uplift the lower-income and vulnerable to a better future , and enable our elderly to age gracefully . " <p> He added : " We remain committed to helping Singaporeans own their homes and keeping housing affordable for future generations . More public rental flats will be built . <p> MND will also look at how to support other vulnerable groups such as divorcees and low-income singles . <p> There will be more shared spaces for people such as town plazas . And to encourage different generations to bond , the ministry will look into siting childcare and eldercare centres together . <p> Also on the agenda : more parks , boosting construction productivity , rejuvenating older estates and helping local farms . <p> Announcing plans , too , to transform the urban landscape yesterday was the Ministry of the Environment and Water Resources ( MEWR ) . <p> To improve the living environment , MEWR will tighten standards for vehicular and industrial emissions to reduce air pollution . <p> Food hygiene regulations will be made stricter too . <p> Also up for review are Singapore 's Water Master Plan , to secure Singapore 's water supply for the future , and the role of hawker centres . <p> MEWR will help industries go green and be more energy-efficient . <p> The push @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ change commitments , following its adoption last month of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> The National Climate Change Secretariat , which is under the Prime Minister 's Office ( PMO ) , will encourage the use of more efficient power-generating methods , and energy-efficient buildings , transport and appliances . <p> The Smart Nation Programme Office , which is also under the PMO , will focus on how technology can help in daily life , like tracking the use of energy in a smart home . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on January 22 , 2016 , with the headline ' HDB focus on couples , needy and elderly folk ' .
