<h> ' Worst ' gas cuts go on , paralysing life <p> Sialkot is badly hit by the " worst " loadshedding of gas now-a-days due to which the life in the region has been paralysed.The situation became worst in the last month while still there is no let-up in the gas loadshedding despite public protests . Sialkot is the native district of two federal ministers Khawaja Asif , federal minister for defence , water and power , and Zahid Hamid , federal minister for climate change.The SNGPL had earlier announced to provide gas for the public from 6:00am to 09:00am and 6:00pm to 9:00pm in a bid to enable the domestic consumers to cook meals.However , there is no implementation of the directive as the people find no gas during these timings . The low pressure of gas and hours loadshedding of gas daily have badly hit the life in Sialkot in cold weather due to which the consumers are much perturbed.A large number of perturbed women with their children , staged a demonstration to against unending loadshedding of gas . The protesting women were carrying kitchen @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ inter-city roads and chanted anti-government slogans.They said that almost all the residential , commercial and industrial areas of Sialkot city including Pakka Garha , Kotli Behram , Model Town , Gohadpur , Muslim Town , Kashmir Road , Pak Pura , Prem Nagar , Nishat Park , Pooran Nagar , Water Works , Green Wood Street , Nehal Chand Street , Dara Aaraaiyaan , Tajpura , Hajipura , Rangpura , Hajipura Ban , Naikapura , Mubarakpura , Muhammadpura , Kashmiri Muhallah , Hamza Ghaus , Shafi Da Bhatta , Imam Sahib , Fatehgarh and Muhallah Bijli Ghar were badly hit by gas loadshedding . They said the pressure of gas remained very low when they had gas for about two hours . The women revealed that there was 16 to 18 hours loadshedding of gas for the domestic consumers in Sialkot , Daska , Sambrial , Uggoki , Chawinda , Badiana , Pasrur and surrounding areas . The pressure of the gas remains very low in the rest time due to which they were unable to prepare meals , they said.The women said that they had gas for two hours daily @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ up in the nights to get their meals cooked.They said that the situation had now become unbearable for them . When contacted , the local SNGPL officials averted to give any comment on the issue .
