<h> Pak-India relations <p> Sir : The story began in Paris with a so-called chance meeting between Prime Minister ( PM ) Nawaz Sharif and Indian PM Narendra Modi on the sidelines of the historic Climate Change conference . Then the denouement took place on the sidelines of the Heart of Asia conference in Islamabad when India and Pakistan agreed to resume their long-stalled peace dialogue to tiptoe their way out of the minefield of unresolved issues that have kept them locked in an unending cycle of confrontation , tension and war . The breakthrough was as breathtaking as unexpected for it came after many false starts and sprang a surprise . <p> The time has come for the leadership in both countries to realise that war is no option and peaceful co-existence is the only way to survive with dignity in an increasingly interconnected world . The historical cycle of endless war and confrontation must come to an end . History has marched past both countries .
