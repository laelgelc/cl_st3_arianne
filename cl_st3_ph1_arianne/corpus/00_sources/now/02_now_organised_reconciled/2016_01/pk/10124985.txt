Trees and forests are in fact the factories of oxygen production and are vital for fighting carbon dioxide . This great role of the green vegetation plays an important role in enabling human beings to live lengthy life on earth . Since the beginning , trees have furnished us with two of the life essentials , food and oxygen . Also , trees are the nature 's gift for healthy human survival and have provided additional necessities such as shelter , medicines and tools . <p> Trees increase our quality of life by bringing natural elements . Besides , using trees in cities to deflect the sunlight reduces the heat island effect caused by pavement and commercial buildings . Due to massive deforestation , chlorofluorocarbon ( CFC ) gases were increasing as trees were the main source of absorbing these gases . Forests are the second largest storehouse of the carbon . The world 's forests absorb 2.4 billion tonnes of carbon dioxide each year or about one third of the carbon dioxide released through the burning of fossil fuel . Forests also represent the world 's most significant terrestrial carbon store containing an estimated 77 percent of all carbon stored in vegetation and 39 percent of all carbon stored in soils . Deforestation is the second leading contributor of carbon emission worldwide after the burning of fossil fuels . Forests can cool the planet by absorbing the greenhouse gas carbon dioxide during photosynthesis .
