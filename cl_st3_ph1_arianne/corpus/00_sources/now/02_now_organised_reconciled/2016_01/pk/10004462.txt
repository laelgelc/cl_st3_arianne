<h> Rally In Local Sugar Prices Could Put Brakes On Exports <p> Karachi : Pakistan sugar exports are likely to take a hit as an eight percent jump in local prices in a fortnight could discourage mills from signing new deals despite a government mandate for overseas sales of 500,000 tons . <p> Price of sugar increased Rs4/kg in the wholesale market after the government allowed millers to export excess quantity last month . Sugar prices were quoted at Rs52/kg on Saturday . <p> " Sugar was available at Rs48/kg before the government allowed export , " said Irfan Memon , a wholesaler . " The sugar millers have cut local supplies to achieve higher exports . " <p> The government allowed sugar export in the second week of December with a subsidy at Rs13/kg . Millers are to export the allowed quantity by March 2016 in various phases . <p> Exports were allowed after the millers said they have carryover stock of 1.1 million tons at the start of new crushing season . " Now sugar is available at Rs55-56/kg in retail market compared to Rs52/kg in early December , " Qureshi said . <p> International markets have also seen a rise in sugar prices in the recent days after Brazil , the largest producer of the sweetener , reported a decline in sugarcane due to climate change . India is also facing similar production issues . <p> Pakistan exported 524,374 tons in the first eleven months ( January-November ) of 2015 at an average price of $443.42/ton , according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics . <p> Dealers said everyone is expecting the rally in local prices to continue as millers are in no mood to increase local supplies . <p> Iskandar Khan , chairman of All Pakistan Sugar Mills Association said the recent increase in price is still not helping the millers to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one kilogram sugar is almost Rs64 , including sales tax .
