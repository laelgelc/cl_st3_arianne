<h> Govt taking steps to counter environmental pollution : Ministry <p> ISLAMABAD - Government is taking a number of steps to counter the environmental pollution and chemicals waste , official sources in Ministry of climate change.Government and private sectors had been directed to take environmental approval before setting up of any new project , they told APP.He said that Environmental Laboratories and Air Monitoring Station at Federal and provincial capitals had been established to serve purpose . He said that Environment Protection Tribunal had also been established in the country . Combined Effluents Treatment Plants ( CETPs ) had been established at Kasur and Karachi . " Government is determined to manage the pollutants and pollution in country . This is evident from the fact that the vision 2025 process builds upon the largely successful pursuit of the Millennium Development Goals ( MDGs ) " he added .
