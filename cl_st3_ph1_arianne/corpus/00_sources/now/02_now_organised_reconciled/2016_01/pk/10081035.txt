<h> Rs. 11 billions distributed amongst 2015 earthquake affectees <p> ISLAMABAD - Federal Minister for Climate Change Zahid Hamid Thursday said about Rs. 11 billion were given in compensation to affectees of earthquake in 2015 . <p> Replying to a question in National Assembly , he informed the house that 280 people were killed and 1770 were injured while 96188 houses and 497 schools were damaged due to earthquake . <p> He said Rs. 10.45 billion were distributed in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa as compensation amongst the affectees . The 50 percent of the amount was given by federal government and other share was given by the KPK government . Likewise , Rs. 756 millions were distributed in FATA , Rs. three million in Punjab and Rs. 0.65 millions were distributed amongst the affectees of AJK . He said government had made good rehabilitation efforts and did n't seek foreign aid in this regard .
