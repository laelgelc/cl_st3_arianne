<p> LAHORE - Leadership for Environment and Development ( LEAD ) Pakistan is organising a policy dialogue on Friday ( tomorrow ) to discuss successful implementation of Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) and ambitions under the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> The event titled , " Towards 2047 : Policy Dialogue on Punjab 's Climate and Growth Policies &amp; Strategies " will be held at at a local hotel . The discussion aims to sensitise key stakeholders , including policy-makers and opinion leaders , to deliberate on a set of salient questions that are critical for the growth , development and well-being of the fast growing but vulnerable population of Punjab . <p> Punjab Minister for Environment Zakia Shah Nawaz Khan will be the chief guest at the occasion .
