PHOTO : EXPRESS <p> KARACHI : Nearly 1,000 delegates from around the world registered themselves for the seventh Model United Nations at the Institute of Business Administration ( IBA ) Karachi ( Munik ) , which commenced on Thursday at IBA 's main campus . <p> For the four-day conference , heads of the various committees have been invited from seven countries , including Germany , Spain , the Netherlands , Hong Kong and the Czech Republic . The highest numbers of international delegates are from India , while delegates from within Pakistan are representing various cities from across the country . <p> The participants have been divided into 32 teams , each of which will focus on a different topic , which includes solving the problems of poverty , education , health and malnutrition , and gender inequality .
