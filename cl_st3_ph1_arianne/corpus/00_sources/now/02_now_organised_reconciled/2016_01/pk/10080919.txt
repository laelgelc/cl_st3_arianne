<p> The event titled , ' Towards 2047 : Policy Dialogue on Punjab 's Climate and Growth Policies &amp; Strategies ' , was held at a local hotel . The dicussion was aimed to sensitise key stakeholders , including policy-makers and opinion leaders , to deliberate on a set of salient questions that are critical for the growth , development and well-being of the fast growing but vulnerable population of Punjab . <p> The event featured two back-to-back roundtable discussions where experts talked about climate vulnerability of Punjab and proposed solutions to deal with it . <p> LEAD Pakistan CEO said poverty and climate vulnerability are intrinsically linked , and this reality makes it imperative to develop a pro-poor climate compatible development strategy for Punjab . This event is on step in the development of such a strategy , he said . <p> Addressing on the occasion Dr Qaiser talked about rising inequality and how our greed is leading to the degradation of ecosystem services . <p> Climate Change Programme Coordinator Basharat Saeed highlighted the drivers of climate vulnerability in Punjab and emphasised the important role of vulnerability @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Dr Khalid Mohtidullah , an expert on water policy , said that " You ca n't value what you ca n't measure , " underscoring the need for research and data on availability , productivity and utilisation of water in Pakistan .
