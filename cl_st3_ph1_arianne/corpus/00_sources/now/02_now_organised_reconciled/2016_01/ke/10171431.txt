<p> An unusually warm winter and a long dry spell in the orchard country of Kullu Manali has apple plantation growers worried ; not only is the valley yet to receive its first good snowfall of the winter season but the warm temperatures are threatening the very cropping pattern in the year ahead . <p> With apple trees not getting the required number of chill hours during winters and frequency of snowfall decreasing over the years , many diseases have damaged orchards across Kullu valley which has impacted yearly production . <p> The total area under apple plantations in Kullu district is 24,000 hectares and nearly 85 per cent of this is covered with the ? royal delicious ? variety . This strain of the royal apple variety needs at least 1,600 chill hours for the trees bearing the crop before a spring blossom . The required chilling hours not only result in good crops but also controls many plant and fruit diseases .
