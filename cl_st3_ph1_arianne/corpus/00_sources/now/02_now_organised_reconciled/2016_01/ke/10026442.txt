<h> Algerian varsity trains African experts to tackle climate change <p> The Pan African University Institute of Water and Energy Sciences is co-ordinating research and training in Africa to navigate the risks brought by climate change . <p> Prof Abdellatif Zerga , director of PAUWES , says the institute is focusing on growing technical capacity in green growth , water management and security . <p> " PAUWES is building the next generation of scientists and policy makers . Africa is currently paying a heavy price for the actions of industrialised countries , but through the talent and commitment of its young people , it can also play a definitive role in overcoming climate change in the years to come , " he said in a statement . <p> The institute is a trilateral project between the African Union , Algeria and Germany and is situated on the campus of the University of Tlemcen . <p> It offers two-year Masters programmes : MSc in Water and MSc in Energy . <p> The Institute opened in October 2014 and has 60 students from all over Africa .
