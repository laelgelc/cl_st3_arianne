<p> Gatundu North sub-county environment and health officer Beatrice Mueni has called on residents to plant trees to mitigate effects of climate change . <p> Mueni noted that people had cut down trees in the area which should now be replaced to ensure adequate rainfall in the region . <p> ' The only way we can defeat this is by refraining from cutting down trees for other uses . Trees give life , they attract rainfall that plays a role in food security thus helping in poverty reduction , " she noted . <p> Speaking at Kamwangi forest farm on Saturday , Mueni called on residents to stop cutting down trees for timber and instead engage in other productive activities . <p> ' I am here not because I have nothing to do in the office but because I want to send a message to all that we must join hands and conserve our environment through tree planting , " she observed . <p> The officer urged residents to plant at least two trees each in January , saying they would benefit the community for many years . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a way of showing a good example to others and urged them to follow suit . <p> The officer who lead a section of volunteering youths at the farm planted 256 trees and pledged to engage more tree seedlings growers to help in their supply .
