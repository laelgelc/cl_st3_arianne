They are very cost effective and generate plenty of labour . Europe buys everything it produces and there is potential for more . These crops have everything they need to expand , except for one thing : water . <p> They are currently irrigated with water from wells , with a very high cost , but this is an alternative that may soon not be available because the progressive lack of rainfall has resulted in wells becoming saline much more often . The solution lies in the dam of the municipality of Rules , which was built 12 years ago , but which still lacks pipes for the water 's supply to people for irrigation purposes .
