<h> Region of Waterloo receives two 2016 Sustainable Community Awards <p> WATERLOO REGION -- A pair of 2016 Sustainable Community Awards have been bestowed upon the Region of Waterloo . <p> Presented by the Federation of Canadian Municipalities ( FCM ) , the awards came in the Brownfields Project and Energy Plan categories . <p> " We are proud to recognize this year 's exemplary projects and encourage all municipalities to learn from these shining examples , " said Ben Henderson , FCM 's Green Municipal Fund chair , in a news release issued yesterday ( Jan. 20 ) . <p> Accolades for the region stem from its Breithaupt Block redevelopment project ( Brownfields ) in Kitchener and its Climate Action Plan : Living Smarter in 2020 initiative ( Energy ) . <p> " The 2016 recipients illustrate how municipalities can provide leadership on environmental protection and climate change in their respective communities , " said FCM president Raymond Louie in the release . <p> " The benefits of such innovative approaches accrue to neighbouring communities and contribute to making Canada more sustainable . "
