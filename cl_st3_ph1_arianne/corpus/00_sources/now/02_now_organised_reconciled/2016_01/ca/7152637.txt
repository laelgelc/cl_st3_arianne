<p> Liberian President Ellen Johnson Sirleaf had spent time in prison , been exiled and suffered all manner of abuse in her attempt to rid her African nation of political corruption . In 2005 , she became the world 's first black female president and went on to share the Nobel Peace Prize in 2011 . <p> " We must help the world to see that Africa is about to transform and that new hope is dawning for those in poverty , " she once told me . <p> Memories of that conversation reminded me just how prescient her insights were . More than 25 per cent of all languages on Earth are spoken on the that continent , and by 2040 half of the world 's youth will be African . Titled the Millennial Development Goals , or MDGs , the initiative called for a more collaborative effort from donor countries across a variety of challenges , such as education , maternal and child health , gender equality , the eradication of hunger and poverty , and environmental sustainability , to name a few . <p> With the time frame for the MDGs ending three weeks ago , the results have been mixed , but there have been unmistakable successes in what had historically been some of the poorest places on the planet . Despite the challenges , it appears the target of halving the world 's poverty by the end of 2015 has been achieved . It 's not to be assumed this occurred merely through foreign aid . <p> During the time when the economy for most affluent nations was in a tailspin ( 2007-2010 ) , developing nations saw theirs boom as their business communities flourished and entered the global marketplace , with new and emerging middle classes making their economic presence felt . <p> The overall success of the Millennial Development Goals has created a new @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ more nations and partners . The failure to fully complete the MDGs has n't blinded the United Nations to the overall success of their impact on global poverty and there is a growing desire to take the UN 's 70th anniversary as a time to finally take the plight of the world 's marginalized more seriously . <p> The knowledge that the previous MDG efforts assisted an extra 2.3 billion people to gain access to clean drinking water , and that the efforts at combating both malaria and tuberculosis show substantial improvements , has convinced many skeptics that the time might be right for something greater . <p> The result is the launch of the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , ratified by 193 nations . Instead of the previous eight target areas , there are now 16 goals and 114 outcome targets . <p> But there is a significant shift .
