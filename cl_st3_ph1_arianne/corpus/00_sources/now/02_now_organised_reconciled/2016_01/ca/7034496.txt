<p> Organic growers from Ontario and beyond will gather for four days of shop talk at the 37th annual Guelph Organic Conference from Jan. 28 to 31 . <p> The conference , to run in the Guelph University Centre , features workshops on crop planting , seed and soil management and animal husbandry . <p> There 's a keynote forum on Jan. 29 in the Thornbrough Auditorium entitled The Face of Organic Farming : Innovation , Vision , Change . It features five panellists : moderator Sarah Dobec , an organic retailer ; Dag Falk of Nature Path Foods ; Pat Kozowyk of the provincial award-winning Babalink Organic Farm ; Ekk Pfenning , who deals in wholesale organics ; and a representative of The New Farm , linked to Community Food Centres Canada . <p> The forum 's themes revolve around issues such as how to innovate in organics today ; what change is likely to come in the news ahead in the organic sector ; and how the organic marketplace can grow . <p> There will be an organic trade show with a wide selection of exhibitors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> An open mic session will follow . <p> More information about the conference and how to register is available at www.guelphorganicconf.ca . <p> *** <p> The Brant Cattlemen 's Association will hold its annual general meeting on Jan. 12 in the Best Western Plus Brant Park Inn on Holiday Drive . This is part of a larger climate change strategy for the province , Suzanne Armstrong , the federation 's director of research and manager of board and committee services , notes in a commentary titled Another Good Reason to Protect Farmland , posted on the organization 's blog . <p> " Protecting farmland is vital to a successful climate change strategy for the province , " she writes . <p> " Ontario 's strategy recognizes that agriculture has a complex relationship with emissions , since the agricultural sector both generates emissions and absorbs carbon dioxide through the normal processes of farming and food processing , " Armstrong writes . <p> Protecting farmland is important for reducing greenhouse gas emissions in other ways as well , she adds . Long-term planning needs to focus on building communities with appropriate population densities , which will reduce greenhouse gas emissions , increase opportunities to reduce carbon emissions from transportation and protect the food producing and carbon sequestering capability of farmland .
