Behind them is a map showing the transfer lands . The announcement was held at Hampton Inn and Suites . <p> The City of Brantford would absorb nearly 2,500 hectares ( almost 6,200 acres ) of land for residential and industrial development from County of Brant under a new boundary adjustment deal unveiled on Wednesday . <p> The deal would also include provision of water and wastewater services by the city to the Cainsville and airport areas in the county as a means of getting around the sticking point of multimillion-dollar compensation that scuttled boundary talks last year . <p> The vast majority of transfer lands -- 1,824 hectares -- are located north of the city between Powerline Road and Governor 's Road East .
