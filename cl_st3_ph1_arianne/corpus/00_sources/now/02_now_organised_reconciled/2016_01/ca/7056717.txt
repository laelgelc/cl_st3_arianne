<p> SYDNEY -- With nearly 1,000 signatures on his petition , sympathy from local politicians and a polite but non-committal email from Ottawa , Bill Fiander 's dream of seeing the reopening the Louisbourg-Gabarus road is inching closer to reality . <p> The road connecting the two communities was once walked by French soldiers , but was closed in the mid-1960s when work was being done on the reconstruction of Fortress Louisbourg . Since then , the drive between Louisbourg is more than 50 kilometres by a roundabout inland route instead of the 17 kilometres of the coast-hugging former route . <p> The reopening of the road would also connect the fortress with the Fleur-de-Lis Trail , giving tourists a scenic drive to the historic site from the Canso Causeway . <p> " It would be an alternate touring route for Cape Breton , " said Fiander , a Sydney resident who was raised in Louisbourg . " The whole area would benefit . " <p> Fiander has been watching the petition grow online and has been encouraged by the mostly positive comments it has received .
