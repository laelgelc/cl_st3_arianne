<h> Report calls for weir to replace Millbrook dam <p> The aging Millbrook dam should be replaced by what 's called an Ogee Crest Weir , up to 30 metres in length , along with reinforcement of the embankment dam at the site near Millbrook 's downtown , states a new report . <p> " This option maintains the present size and water level of the mill pond which is an important natural feature within the village , " states Otonabee Region Conservation Authority CAO Dan Marinigh in a press release . " Public access across the dam via a foot bridge and other amenities , such as a waterfall effect , are also incorporated in this option and reflect the community values of the site as expressed throughout the consultation process . " <p> This comes via an addendum to the environmental study report from Hatch Ltd. , contracted by the Otonabee Regional Conservation Authority to study the site . <p> The addendum will be available for public review and comment for a mandatory period of 15 days beginning Thursday .
