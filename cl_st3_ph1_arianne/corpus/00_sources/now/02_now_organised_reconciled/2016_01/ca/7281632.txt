<h> Trudeau government buys time to decide on Kinder Morgan plan to expand Trans Mountain pipeline <p> The government of Liberal Prime Minister Justin Trudeau wants more time to decide on two controversial oil pipeline projects . <p> One of these is the proposed expansion by Kinder Morgan Inc. of its Trans Mountain pipeline , which runs from Edmonton in Alberta to Burnaby in B.C. The other is the Energy East pipeline from Alberta to New Brunswick . <p> The National Energy Board ( NEB ) will finish hearing oral arguments regarding the Trans Mountain project , and will submit its report to the cabinet in May this year . <p> Under the law , the government has three months , until August 2016 to accept or reject the NEB 's recommendations . <p> Today ( January 27 ) , the federal government announced that Minister of Natural Resources Jim Carr will seek an extension of four months to the legislated time limit . <p> This means that the Trudeau government may make a decision in December 2016 on the Trans Mountain project that will nearly triple oil @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The planned extension was made known as the government revealed its " interim approach " to natural resources projects . <p> In a statement , Carr and Minister of Environment and Climate Change Catherine McKenna laid down these five principles regarding the review of resource development projects : <p> 1 .
