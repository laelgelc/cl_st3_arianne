It 's a place with a booming high-tech sector , advanced manufacturing , an educated workforce , elite educational facilities and a tolerant society . <p> In a sense , he was trying to rebrand Canada as the perfect place for companies whose futures depend on the new economy . <p> For Klaus Schwab , the German economist who presided over the forum , Mr. Trudeau was the very personification of the future . <p> " I could n't imagine anybody who could represent more the world which will come out of this Fourth Industrial Revolution , " he said . " And in last fall 's election , he promised to spend $100 million to promote innovation if his Conservative government was re-elected . <p> Mr. Trudeau , however , promised during a Winnipeg campaign stop to spend nearly $1 billion to support the country 's startup and innovation sectors . <p> There 's been a debate ever since on whether funding should go directly to individual businesses or to support the establishment of so-called tech hubs , which are places where innovative businesses can share ideas , office space , collaborate and , presumably , invent and make gadgets and technology that can be sold around the world . <p> The bedrock for the latest technological revolution , moreover , exists @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to young entrepreneurs , such as hip nightspots , rapid transit and cool neighbourhoods .
