<p> British physicist Dr David Grimes worked out a mathematical way to calculate the chances of a plot being deliberately leaked by a whistle-blower or accidentally uncovered . <p> He was able to show that the more people share in a conspiracy , the shorter its lifespan is likely to be . <p> For a plot to last five years , the maximum number of plotters turned out to be 2,521 .
