<p> INDIAN prime minister Narendra Modi 's surprise visit to Lahore , Pakistan , on Christmas Day , brought his hyperkinetic year of global diplomacy to a headline-grabbing close . It also raised questions about the fraught relationship between the two nuclear-armed neighbors . <p> Since Modi took office , in May , 2014 , India-Pakistan relations have had more ups and downs than a child 's yo-yo . <p> The victory of a Hindu chauvinist hardliner -- a man who had systematically blocked his predecessor 's peace-making efforts while in opposition and demonised Pakistan in his campaign speeches -- was naturally assumed to portend a bilateral chill . <p> Less than two months later , however , the two countries were exchanging artillery fire across the border , with Modi instructing the Indian army to respond disproportionately to Pakistani provocations . <p> Talks between the two countries ' foreign secretaries were scheduled in New Delhi , only to be called off when the Pakistanis , like so many times before , met with Indian Kashmiri separatist leaders . <p> In November 2014 , at the South Asian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was taken of Modi reading a brochure , ostentatiously ignoring Sharif as he walked past . <p> ( It was subsequently revealed , however , that the two leaders had met in the hotel suite of an Indian businessman , who enjoys good relations with Sharif . ) <p> Last February , Modi again initiated a thaw , calling Sharif to wish his country luck in the Cricket World Cup , and dispatching his foreign secretary to Islamabad . But mistrust again prevailed , and a cold peace ensued . <p> An unexpected meeting of the two prime ministers in Ufa , Russia , in July , produced little progress , with both sides repudiating each other 's statements about what was discussed and agreed . <p> Soon after , yet another scheduled meeting of national security advisers in Delhi was called off at the last minute , again following a meeting between Pakistani officials and Kashmiri separatists . <p> But , with a brief encounter between Sharif and Modi on the sidelines of the UN climate change conference , in Paris , on November 30 , the tone changed yet @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ two countries ' national security advisers conferred in Bangkok in December . <p> Just days later , India 's external affairs minister , in Islamabad for the fifth Heart of Asia ministerial conference on Afghanistan , met with top Pakistani officials , including both Sharif and his foreign affairs adviser , before announcing that the two countries had decided to restart a comprehensive dialogue . <p> It is against this background that Modi made his impromptu visit to Lahore ( to attend celebrations of Sharif 's birthday and his granddaughter 's marriage ) , raising hopes that the bilateral relationship may be undergoing a genuine sea change . <p> But within a week of the Christmas bonhomie , seven Indians were killed by militants at the Pathankot Air Force Base , near the border with Pakistan . <p> Because such an attack was predictable , it may not derail the diplomatic process .
