Already there are signs of cow price hardening which is always a positive sign for finishers , " he said . <p> " This wo n't happen however , while people continue to sell prime beef at ? 3.90/kg . Farmers need to get tough and fast , in the coming weeks when beef will be scarce . " <p> However , Mr Phelan said that the longer run picture suggests price weakness towards the end of 2016 . <p> Apart from the extra calves born last year , there was also a significant fall in live exports of almost 60,000 head compared to 2014 . <p> " We need the Government to concentrate on doing everything possible to facilitate live exports in 2016 .
