<h> Flooding - McGuinness welcomes clarity from European Commission <p> 20:00 Wednesday 13th of January 2016 <p> Vice-President of the European Parliament Mairead McGuinness MEP has welcomed clarification by the European Commission that European Directives do not prevent Member States from instigating action to address flooding . <p> The MEP who was representing the Government at today 's meeting in Brussels has released a statement saying that , " It is clear we have the flexibility we need to implement action to address the issue but that an holistic approach and not just local plans drawn up in isolation of the knock-on impacts downstream is needed to ensure that the action taken delivers the required result . " <p> She said the Commission was at pains to point out that dredging , while possible , is not the most favourable environmental approach to addressing flooding problems but could be part of an overall solution , which should also look at rewetting boglands and planting trees - not Sitka spruce . <p> " Because action in one area can negatively impact other areas it is crucial that we complete @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and finalise action plans as soon as possible , " said MEP McGuinness . " In addition it is clear that decisions could be taken to carry out works immediately where sufficient data has already been gathered to make these decisions . <p> " The CFRAM - Catchment Flood Risk Assessment and Management - process currently underway is the answer with flood plans properly co-ordinated across the 60 areas of the Shannon basin and 300 areas nationally which have been identified as suffering from persistent flooding problems . <p> " There is a massive amount of work already done and the process must yield results in areas prone to flooding . " <p> MEP McGuinness states that people need reassurance going forward . <p> " This process should provide a real solution and more importantly should give people much needed reassurance and hope for the future , " she said . " That said the process may also reveal that in some areas flooding can not be avoided <p> " The process is taking into account the likely implications of climate change on flood patterns and this is a significant development @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ indicated that risk aversion may be a feature in terms of the approach of some Local Authorities where fear about EU Directives leads to no action being taken . <p> " This does not seem to be the case in other member states where the full processes available at EU level are used , " she added . " We can not allow this to continue , the issue must be addressed with the action plans . " <p> MEP McGuinness also revealed that when it comes to emergency measures needed when flooding is at its height , the Commission confirmed these can be taken without consultation with the EU . In addition the meeting clarified that the Government has a window of three months in which to apply for financial support to address the aftermath of the floods . <p> If you have a story or want to send a photo or video to us please contact the Donegal Now editorial team .
