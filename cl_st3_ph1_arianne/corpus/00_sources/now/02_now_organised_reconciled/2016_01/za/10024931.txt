<h> Combination of Human and Computer Intelligence could tackle Problems like Climate Change <p> Joint efforts by humans and computer intelligence could solve the ' wicked ' issues of the world . According to scientists , the two working together can possibly deal with issues such as climate change and geopolitical conflict . <p> There are a number of things in which humans beat machines , ranging from simple things like pattern recognition to creative abstraction . Using computers such cognitive abilities can be combined into multidimensional collaborative networks to accomplish what traditional problem-solving can not . <p> Most of present day 's human computation systems depend on sending bite-sized ' micro-tasks ' to numerous individuals and then results are stitched together . For instance , EyeWire 's 165,000 volunteers have analyzed thousands of pictures to create the most complete map of human retinal neurons in the world . Though , tough challenges that we face today ca n't be dealt with using this microtasking approach . <p> The latest human computation technologies can be helpful . New techniques provide real-time access to crowd-based inputs in which a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the next person for enhancement work or analysis of a distinct kind . This is helpful in the creation of more flexible collaborative environments that can address the most challenging problems in a much better way . <p> In a news release , one of the researchers Janis Dickinson said , " By sharing and observing practices in a map-based social network , people can begin to relate their individual efforts to the global conservation potential of living and working landscapes " . <p> Moreover , the researchers have discovered that their idea has already taken shape in number of human computation projects , such as YardMap , allowing participants to communicate and build on each other 's work . Such a thing ca n't be achieved by crowdsourcing solely . <p> The discovery has highlighted the importance of using all tools available to solve difficult problems . Particularly , use of computers to build on research is going to be the key in the coming time .
