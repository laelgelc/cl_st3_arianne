All evidence , however , points to a continuing depression in oil prices in 2016 -- one that may , in fact , stretch into the 2020s and beyond . Given the centrality of oil ( and oil revenues ) in the global power equation , this is bound to translate into a profound shakeup in the political order , with petroleum-producing states from Saudi Arabia to Russia losing both prominence and geopolitical clout . <p> This article originally appeared at TomDispatch.com .
