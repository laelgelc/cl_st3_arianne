<p> You may have seen the headlines from Davos , Switzerland , where Oxfam 's latest global inequality report found the world 's richest 62 billionaires have as much wealth as the poorest 3.6 billion people . But what 's most shocking is not what is happening at the top of the economic ladder , but at the bottom . <p> " The gap between rich and poor is reaching new extremes , " Oxfam said . " The richest 1 percent have now accumulated more wealth than the rest of the world put together ... Meanwhile , the wealth owned by the bottom half of humanity has fallen by a trillion dollars in the past five years . " <p> This gap " is just the latest evidence that today we live in a world with levels of inequality we may not have seen for over a century , " they said .
