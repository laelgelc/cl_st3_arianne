This is because a terrestrial planet with no life and insufficient quantities of " non-condensable " gases such as nitrogen can produce oxygen in its atmosphere through a process called photolysis . This could occur when water molecules ( consisting of hydrogen and oxygen ) high in the atmosphere of the planet are broken apart by sunlight , allowing the lighter hydrogen to escape and the heavier oxygen to be left behind .
