<h> Solon wants shipment of nickel ore from Davao town probed <p> The chairman of the House Committee on Climate Change has filed a resolution questioning the P28.2-million shipment of nickel-laterite ore from the municipality of Banaybanay in Davao Oriental . <p> AKO BICOL partylist Rep. Rodel Batocabe said the legality and propriety of the shipment of the nickel-laterite ore by the Golden Summit Mining Corp. , described to be in the " tens of thousands of metric tons , " should be looked into . <p> ( Photo courtesy of google maps ) <p> " There is a need to conduct an investigation on the propriety and legality of disposing , transporting , and exporting abandoned stockpiled mineral ores with the end in view of proposing remedial legislation that will ensure the interest of the government and protect the environment , " he said in filing House Resolution 2280 . <p> An estimated 70,000 metric tons of nickel-laterite ore have been extracted by alleged small-scale miners in barangays Maputi , and Kauswagan in the municipality of Banaybanay in Davao Oriental . <p> The mineral ores were left @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ necessary transport and export documents . <p> This prodded the provincial government of Davao Oriental to enter into a P28.2-million contract of agreement with the Golden Summit Mining Corp . to undertake the clearing activities and rehabilitation of the affected areas , Batocabe said .
