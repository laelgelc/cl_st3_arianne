<p> A seemingly innocuous piece of mail arrived last month amid the heaps of Christmas cards . It was a renewal notice from Costco , letting us know our first year of membership with the grocery store giant was coming to an end . <p> I was mildly fretting over this day . It 's a great store , of course . They have plenty of free samples , a variety of products ranging from ball point pens to tabouli , and they sell lots of good stuff in wholesale quantities . That 's the problem . Everything is in bulk . <p> The household consists of me , my husband and our dog . It takes months to work through a gross of chocolate chip granola bars or 10 kilos of ground beef . <p> And I do n't have anywhere to put the half ton shrink wrapped package of Kirkland brand toilet paper rolls .
