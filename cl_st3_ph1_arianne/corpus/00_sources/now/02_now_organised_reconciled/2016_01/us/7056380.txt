<h> Story tools <p> Seven straight years of frothing-at-the-mouth Republicans . Haters pushing derail buttons on your presidency . The irritating reality that a good quarter of the population still believes you were n't born here . <p> Now , in the wake of all that , you must give your last official speech before a shark-infested Congress openly hoping for your demise . What would you do ? <p> You 'd throw a left hook so hard that it slammed Bamas into the next sequel of Creed . <p> But you wo n't get that from chronically cool President Barack Obama as he delivers his final State of the Union address . <p> In the anxious runup , his detractors seek more reasons to demonize him , as hang-in-there supporters fantasize epic , hood-style shutdowns . <p> Will there be any unprepared or beautifully timed snaps ( " I won both of them " ) ? Will he throw some well-placed policy punches ( " Deficit reduction alone is not an economic plan " ) and poke a hole in the right ?
