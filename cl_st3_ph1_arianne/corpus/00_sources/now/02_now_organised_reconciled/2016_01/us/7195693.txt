Hydraulic fracturing or fracking during the extraction process can leak methane too . The Aliso Canyon leak sprung from a pipe leading to a storage facility buried 8,500 feet underground . <p> According to a 2014 Stanford University study , methane emissions may be 50 percent higher than official projections from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) . Research published last year also found the technology that EPA and others use to measure emissions may itself be flawed , and the amount of methane leaking into the atmosphere is likely " systematically underestimated .
