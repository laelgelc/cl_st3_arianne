<h> Letters - Recycle plastic containers , do n't ban <p> WE refer to " Ban on plastic products " ( News , April 19 ) . Plastic bags and polystyrene ( PS ) containers should be recycled instead of being banned . Banning these useful , inexpensive and safe products results in higher costs for consumers in Perak and Johor with no change in society 's attitude towards waste separation and recycling . <p> More importantly , such a ban does nothing to curb the littering habit , which is the real source of pollution and harm to the environment . This is why the Malaysian Plastics Manufacturers Association ( MPMA ) launched its " Do n't Be A Litterbug " campaign in 2012 , to raise awareness and push the message that littering harms the environment . <p> Plastic bags and PS foam food containers are popular and hard to replace in Malaysia because they are inexpensive and environmentally safe to use . Food hawkers , already struggling to run their small businesses due to inflation and the impact of GST , will probably end up as the real victims of the ban on plastic bags and PS . <p> Plastics including plastic bags -- whether in terms of the total energy used , fossil fuel used or GHG emission -- have the lowest environmental impact compared to other packaging materials , as published in " Life cycle assessment of supermarket carrier Bags " , a UK Environment Agency report in 2011 . Plastic packaging has among the smallest carbon footprint , requiring 90% less energy to recycle and constitutes 80% less volume than other materials . <p> MPMA is also of the view that the usage of biodegradable bags made of plant-based materials is not the way towards being a green state . MPMA is firmly of the view that plastic bags should not be banned as they have the lowest environmental impact based on LCA when compared to other bags made from other materials . <p> PS foam has been certified under the Malaysian Food Act 1983 ( Act 281 ) and Sirim for use as a food contact packaging material . It is not the product that pollutes but the behaviour of the public that causes harm to the environment . <p> Given the above , MPMA has been collaborating with local authorities such as Kuala Lumpur City Hall and the Solid Waste and Public Cleansing Management Corporation in public-private partnerships in working towards an organised waste separation system as well as educating the public on proper plastics waste management practices . <p> To emulate the cleanliness of countries like Japan , we must learn @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of not littering .
