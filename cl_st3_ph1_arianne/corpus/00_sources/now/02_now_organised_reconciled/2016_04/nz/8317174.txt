<h> Breaking News <h> Report accuses Govt of being ' climate cheats ' <p> The Government is being accused of " wholesale climate fraud " in a new report which claims New Zealand bought worthless carbon credits from Ukraine and Russia . <p> The Morgan Foundation released its Climate Cheats report today , and Climate Change Issues Minister Paula Bennett says while she has n't seen it yet , the Government is already reviewing the Emissions Trading Scheme ( ETS ) . <p> Report author , economist Geoff Simmons , says New Zealand has been the largest buyer of the Ukrainian and Russian credits through the ETS . <p> Almost all of those credits are " hot air " and do n't represent true emissions reductions , but the Government will use them to claim it is meeting international obligations through to at least 2020 . Actual emissions will continue to grow beyond the country 's targets . <p> Credits from Ukraine and Russia were banned by the EU in 2011 because of links to organised crime and concern over their environmental integrity . <p> The ETS is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ allowances . It puts a price on greenhouse gases to provide an incentive to those in the scheme to reduce their emissions . <p> Mr Simmons says it has the potential to damage New Zealand 's clean-green reputation and as a corruption-free country . <p> In a statement , Ms Bennett says she has n't seen the report so does n't know if there was " anything new " in it , but is aware of previous claims the Morgan Foundation has made about international units . <p> " New Zealand made the best decision based on the rules at the time . Kyoto has been replaced by the newer and better Paris Agreement , " says Ms Bennett . <p> " The ETS is n't perfect , which is why we 're reviewing it . I absolutely stand by its ability to help us transition to a lower-carbon economy , and international markets have a role to play in that . " <p> The report claims New Zealand put around $200 million " into the hands of foreign criminals " to avoid its obligation to reduce emissions . <p> Companies @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ also able to use the cheap , fake credits and profit from their pollution . <p> While Mr Simmons says carbon trading is a good way to make reducing emissions easier , it only works if the credits bought have value . <p> " The sad truth is that the foreign credits New Zealand has gorged on up until now have produced little to no climate benefit , " the report says . <p> The report authors say New Zealand should three things to " put it right " : <p> cancel the credits <p> remove the two-for-one deal and freeze companies ' free share of New Zealand Units for a year to clear the backlog of banked ETS credits <p> temporarily close the scheme to international trade until the system has integrity , and also work with Pacific countries on bilateral agreements . <p> The Green Party also wants the Government to stop using the credits . <p> " The climate ca n't be cheated into not changing , " says co-leader James Shaw .
