He says we need to face up to the reality that a rising sea is inevitable . <p> " Around the world , something like 200 million people are at risk of coastal flooding , from a sea level rise of a metre , " Prof Bamber says . <p> " Just to put that in context , at the minute in Europe , we are experiencing a very serious migration crisis -- caused by the migration of around 2 million people . <p> " It actually may bring down some governments in Europe .
