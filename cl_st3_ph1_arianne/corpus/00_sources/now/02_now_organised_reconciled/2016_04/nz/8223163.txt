<h> Breaking News <h> Climate pact could be brought forward <p> A new global pact to tackle climate change , agreed in Paris in December , could come into force two years earlier than the planned date of 2020 , according to the UN climate chief . <p> " I think we will have a Paris agreement in effect in 2018 , " predicts Christiana Figueres , executive secretary of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change . <p> The agreement , originally slated to take effect in 2020 , will come into force once 55 countries representing 55 per cent of the world 's total emissions have both signed and ratified it . <p> On April 22 , at least 130 countries are expected to ink the agreement in New York , on the first day it opens for signature -- more than the 119 who signed the Law of the Sea , says Ms Figueres . <p> Getting the deal agreed and ratified , however , is just a prelude to the far more complicated work of switching the world 's energy systems over to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> That will take rapid and massive investment on a scale not seen since rebuilding after World War II , to make the changes needed quickly enough . <p> " We are two minutes to midnight on climate change . If you ask me , the Paris agreement is 10 years too late , " says Ms Figueres . <p> Delays in negotiating the pact -- which sets a goal of keeping global average temperature rise to " well below " 2 degrees Celsius -- have put " an incredible amount of pressure " on efforts to revamp the world 's systems , she adds .
