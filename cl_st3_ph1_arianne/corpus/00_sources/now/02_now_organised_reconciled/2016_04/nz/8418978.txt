<h> Historic climate change agreement being signed today <p> Representatives from more than 150 governments , including New Zealand , are today gathering in New York for an historic climate agreement . <p> Today 's signing of the Paris Agreement is the largest one-day signing of any international agreement , and also marks Earth Day . <p> The agreement aims to keep warming below an agreed target of two degrees , although each country sets its own emissions target of how to achieve that . <p> Climate Change Minister Paula Bennett said it 's an incredible privilege to be part of the signing . <p> " You could feel the buzz in the air . There were presidents and prime ministers and secretaries of state and children . A lot of people have put in a lot of work . " <p> New Zealand is now committed to cutting emissions to 30 percent below 2005 levels , by 2030 . <p> Bennett expects New Zealand will ratify the Paris Agreement at the end of this year , or the beginning of next . <p> She says it 'll @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " For us to have lower emissions and to really play our part in what needs to happen to keep this world as wonderful and beautiful as it is we are going to have to make some changes to the way we live . " <p> The signing comes days after a Morgan Foundation report , accusing the Government of buying fraudulent foreign carbon credits from Russia and Ukraine to meet climate targets .
