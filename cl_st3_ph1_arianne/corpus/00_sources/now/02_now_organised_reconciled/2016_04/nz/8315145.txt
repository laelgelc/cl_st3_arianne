<h> NZ accused of being a climate change cheat <p> New Zealand has been accused of cheating in the way it fights climate change . <p> Photo : 123rf <p> The accusation comes in a report written for the Morgan Foundation of millionaire philanthropist Gareth Morgan . <p> The Emissions Trading Scheme is supposed to make people and companies that emit greenhouse gases pay money to those that absorb greenhouse gases , such as forestry companies whose growing trees soak up CO2 . <p> But the report , written by Geoff Simmons and Paul Young , says the New Zealand scheme is a fake . <p> Gareth Morgan Photo : Morgan Foundation <p> In a caustic foreword to the report , Gareth Morgan wrote " we are , without doubt , cheats " . <p> The report said New Zealand was proportionately by far the largest purchaser of these Ukrainian and Russian credits . <p> " This was due to deliberate decisions by the National-led Government to - unlike any other country - continue allowing unlimited use of these and other foreign credits for as long as the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plans to knowingly utilise all these fraudulent credits so it can claim we are meeting our international obligations through to at least 2020 . Meanwhile our actual emissions continue to grow in excess of our targets . " <p> The government has recently banned the purchase of these credits . <p> But the surplus built up earlier as a result of the purchases is allowed to be carried forward . <p> This enables the government to claim that it will meet its targets for 2020 , irrespective of greenhouse gases being poured into the atmosphere every day . <p> The way the scheme operated was faulted for two other reasons . <p> One was that the rush of cheap credits pulled the price of carbon down so low there was no incentive for people to plant trees . <p> Second , smart people have been able to use the system to make large amounts of money overnight via arbitrage , instead of using it to improve the environment .
