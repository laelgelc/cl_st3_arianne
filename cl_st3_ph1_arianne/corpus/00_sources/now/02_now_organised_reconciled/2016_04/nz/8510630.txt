<h> City environmental strategy step closer <p> A Dunedin City Council strategy aimed at protecting the city 's environment and meeting challenges faced by climate change is one step closer to being adopted . <p> The council 's community and environment committee yesterday voted in favour of adopting its environment strategy , dubbed Te Ao Turoa ( The Natural World ) , following extensive consultation with the public . <p> The the three goals of the strategy were : <p> 1 . Dunedin is resilient and zero carbon . 2 . Dunedin has a healthy environment . 3 . Dunedin people care for the environment . <p> Cr Neville Peat highlighted the importance of the strategy , saying it ' ' belonged to everyone ' ' and not just ' ' greenies ' ' . <p> Looking after the environment was an important part of maintaining the city 's economy , particularly when it came to ecotourism . <p> ' ' They are two sides of the one coin . ' ' <p> Chairwoman Jinty MacTavish said there was still a lot of work to be done to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment . <p> She was keen to acknowledge the desire from submitters for council to set targets , so its performance against the strategy could be measured . <p> To that end she added an amendment calling on milestone targets for the strategy to be reported back to council by the end of next year . <p> These targets could not be included in the current document because of the lack of baseline data . <p> She said the level of collaboration with Kai Tahu on the strategy should be celebrated , and acknowledged the group of experts from the University of Otago and farming and business communities who had given their time and helped ' ' transform ' ' the strategy . <p> Cr Aaron Hawkins said residents had sent a clear message they wanted the council to take action around climate change and maintaining and improving biodiversity . He was pleased the draft strategy had been strengthened when it came to these two areas as a result of that feedback . <p> A staff report said the goal of Dunedin being a resilient and zero carbon was listed first @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Almost half of 97 submitters called for a reduction in carbon emissions with the aim of Dunedin becoming a low-carbon or carbon-neutral city . <p> Funding of $150,000 to deliver on the goals of the strategy had been proposed as part of the 2016-17 annual plan . <p> If the strategy is given final approval at June 's full council meeting , it will join six other strategies which have already been adopted to inform council decision-making .
