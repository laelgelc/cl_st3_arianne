<h> University of Oxford launches new Penguin Watch 2.0 <p> The University of Oxford has come up with its new Penguin Watch 2.0 . It has a camera monitoring program through which a person can witness the lives of the Antarctic penguins . The website supports the important Antarctic science project worldwide and raises awareness about the ice cover in the region . <p> Quartz has reported that the scientists from the Australian Antarctic Division and the University of Oxford have erected the cameras for the mission . The aim of the initiative is to monitor the Adelie and Chinstrap penguin colonies , presently in danger due to climate change . Rise in the Earth 's temperatures has resulted into ice meltdown that led to drop in the krill population , which is the food source of the penguins . <p> The researchers are seeking help from public by marking the pictures of adult and baby penguins captured by 50 cameras fixed throughout Antarctica . Through images ' marking , the researchers can recognize the penguins and their eggs .
