<h> Potentially dangerous marine bacteria found in Sydney Harbour <p> A POTENTIALLY dangerous species of marine bacteria has been found in Sydney Harbour and swimmers are being told to take simple precautions . <p> According to a new study , two species of Vibrio bacteria were found in high concentrations when the water in the harbour was at its warmest and in areas where it was not as salty . This included around Parramatta Park , Olympic Park and Rozelle . <p> Vibrio bacteria can cause cholera but this strain was not found in the samples . <p> However , a related strain of the bacteria was found in high quantities and this can cause skin infections and gastrointestinal infections when exposed to open wounds . <p> Another strain Vibrio vulnificus was also found . This particular strain is known in the US for causing 95 per cent of all deaths from people eating contaminated seafood .
