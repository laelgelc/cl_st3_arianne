<h> End imputation to boost growth stocks <h> End imputation to boost growth stocks <p> I was a stockbroker at the time Paul Keating introduced the tax imputation system and was an enthusiastic supporter . Now , however , I am having second thoughts and endorse the comments made recently by Bill Ferris , as reported by your newspaper ( " PM 's innovation tsar says dividend imputation saps innovation " , March 21 ) . If franking credits cease to exist and the money saved is applied to a significant reduction in company tax , there might be some positive outcomes . <p> For a start , investors would be more inclined to support growth stocks . As a case study , it is interesting to see what has happened to the Transurban share price after the company decided to reduce dividends and increase the cash flow . This occurred in 2008 and was obviously in part caused by the global financial crisis . The company , instead of being essentially a utility stock , then entered a growth phase using the increased cash flow .
