<h> Global warming may be far worse than thought , cloud analysis suggests <p> A detailed analysis of the first seven years of data from a NASA cloud-monitoring mission has unveiled that clouds may provide less cooling than previous estimates of climate experts . Clouds offer considerably lower benefit in slowing the warming of the planet . Therefore , temperature may increase faster than expected as greenhouse gas pollution gets worse . <p> Climate scientists had high expectations from clouds in dealing with global warming because they can reflect energy back into space . When temperature increase in future , clouds containing more liquid water and fewer ice crystals would make them brighter and they would reflect more sunlight . This phenomenon could have helped in reducing the impact of higher temperature on the planet . <p> Researchers said that climate models have overestimated how much ice is present in clouds .
