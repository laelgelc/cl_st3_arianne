<h> The write stuff with Reg Cribb <p> Writer Reg Cribb is probably best known for the film Last Cab to Darwin . As a screenwriter and a playwright Reg is passionate about telling stories slightly off the grid , detailing the Australia that exists outside our capital cities . His play Thomas Murray and the Upside Down River is being performed at Lismore City Hall this week and he is engaged in several Q&amp;A sessions to share his experience . <p> Why do you write ' regional ' stories ? <p> I was raised in rural Western Australia . My father was a bank manager and we were transferred all over the state . I lived in 10 different country towns and attended five different primary schools before I went to boarding school in Perth . I am drawn to stories from the far-flung reaches of our country . The characters are extreme , the struggles are many , and the more we drift towards an urban culture the more I want to tell stories from the forgotten parts of Australia . <p> How do you source @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ transform your ideas into something that will work for stage ? <p> I get out among it . It came from the stories of people who lived in the area . You have to be open to let the landscape speak to you and be fearless in finding a way to put that on the stage . <p> I love your fascination with ' ordinary ' Australians .
