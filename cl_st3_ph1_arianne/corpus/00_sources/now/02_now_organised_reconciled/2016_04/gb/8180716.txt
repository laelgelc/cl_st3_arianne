<h> First penguin chicks of the year hatch at Chester Zoo <p> Four fuzzy penguin chicks - named Wotsit , Quaver , Frazzle and Cheeto - have become the first of their kind to hatch at Chester Zoo this year . <p> Wotsit broke out of its egg on Easter Sunday , quickly followed by Quaver ( Sunday ) and Frazzle ( Monday ) , with Cheeto arriving a day after ( Tuesday ) weighing a mere 75g . <p> The tiny youngsters - who have n't been sexed yet - are threatened Humboldt penguins from South America and have been named after their keepers ' favourite crisps . <p> Staff at the zoo use a different naming theme each year to help them to keep track of the new chicks , with popular potato snacks getting the nod this time around .
