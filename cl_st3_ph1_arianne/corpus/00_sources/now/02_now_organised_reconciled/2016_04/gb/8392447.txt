<p> Putting an effective price on carbon has long been the favourite method of most economists in dealing with climate change . <p> In 2006 Sir Nicholas Stern 's landmark review of the costs of climate change found that establishing a carbon price was essential to dealing effectively with the problem . <h> Cap , trade or tax <p> Since then attempts have been made , with mixed success . These have included cap and trade schemes in many parts of the world , where a limit on emissions is established by governments and permits are issued to heavy carbon users . If they cut back on their emissions they can trade the permits on the market . <p> Schemes like this have been criticised for being too generous to polluters , often issuing them with free permits .
