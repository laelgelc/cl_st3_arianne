<p> C omprehensive aerial and underwater surveys of Australia 's Great Barrier Reef have revealed that the ecosystem is experiencing its worst coral bleaching on record . The survey , conducted by the ARC Centre for Excellence for Coral Reef Studies and the National Coral Bleaching Taskforce , indicates that 93% of the reef is affected . Whilst the severity of the phenomenon varies , the north of the reef located off the coast of Queensland state is the worst hit . <p> According to Professor Terry Hughes of the National Coral Bleaching Taskforce , the current bleaching event is far more serious than previous occurrences in 2002 and 1998 . Additionally , the cumulative effect of all three bleaching events is impacting the ecosystem , as he explained , " the mix of coral species has already changed " . The Great Barrier Reef extends for 2,300km along the north east of Australia , and is the world 's largest living ecosystem . <p> ? Coral bleaching occurs when rising water temperatures cause coral to expel tiny colourful algae , known as zooxanthellae . This causes the coral to calcify and turn white , or " bleach " . Bleached coral can recover if the temperature falls and zooxanthellae are able to recolonise the coral , otherwise they may die . <p> ?
