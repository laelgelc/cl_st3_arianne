Ever since there 's been portraiture , there 's been self-portraiture , beginning with Ancient Egyptian images and sculpture . And self-obsession expressed through images is hardly a consequence of smartphones and the Kardashians . <p> Look at Rembrandt , who did at least 85 paintings of himself . They go from moody , tousled youth to world-weary elder burgher . It 's an extraordinary record of the evolution of one character through images . It 's a " quickie " way to render yourself -- replacing the sketchbook , or even the lumbering camera , with a pinhole camera above your smartphone screen . <p> And not just for you .
