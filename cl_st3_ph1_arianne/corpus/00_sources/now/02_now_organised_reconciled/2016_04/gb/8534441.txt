Photograph : Tom Perry/AAP <p> Australians mistakenly believe the federal government gives 13% of the budget to foreign aid , about 14 times higher than reality , a new poll has found . <p> The national poll for Campaign for Australian Aid asked 1,528 Australian adults how much of the federal budget was spent on foreign aid . The average of responses was 13.28% , compared with the actual level of 0.9% of the federal budget . The aid budget totals 0.25% of gross national income . <p> Campaign for Australian Aid 's campaign director , Tony Milne , said : " Most Australians do n't realise we provide less than 1% of Australia 's annual budget to aid .
