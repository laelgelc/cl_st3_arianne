<p> The Great Barrier Reef is in the throes of its worst-ever coral bleaching event , scientists say . Only 7% of the 2,300km-long reef system has been left untouched by bleaching , with researchers expressing alarm over the health of one of the world 's greatest natural wonders . Climate change and an unusually strong El Nino have been blamed for the latest mass bleaching event , which has resulted in vast swathes of coral reefs turning white .
