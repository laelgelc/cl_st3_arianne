<p> Steak and other red meats would be taxed highest - but all food could ultimately be targeted Getty Images <p> Denmark is considering proposals to introduce a tax on red meat , after a government think tank came to the conclusion that " climate change is an ethical problem " . <p> The Danish Council of Ethics recommended an initial tax on beef , with a view to extending the regulation to all red meats in future . It said that in the long term , the tax should apply to all foods at varying levels depending on climate impact . <p> The council voted in favour of the measures by an overwhelming majority , and the proposal will now be put forward for consideration by the government . <p> " The Danish way of life is far from climate-sustainable , and if we are to live up to the Paris Agreement target of keeping the global temperature rise ' well ' below 2 ? C , it is necessary both to act quickly and involve food , " the council said .
