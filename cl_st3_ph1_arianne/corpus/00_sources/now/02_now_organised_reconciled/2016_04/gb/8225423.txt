<h> Gas made from cheese to heat hundreds of homes <p> Hundreds of homes in Cumbria will be heated using cheese from next month , as a new government-backed green energy plant starts producing gas from cheddar manufacturing waste . <p> The anaerobic digestion plant at the Lake District Creamery in Aspatria will receive millions of pounds in subsidies for turning whey and other residues from the cheese production process into " biogas " . <p> Some of the gas will be used to generate electricity on-site , while the remainder will be processed and fed into the local gas grid where it will be used by homes and business for their heating and cooking . <p> About 60 per cent of that gas is expected to be taken back out of the grid for the creamery 's own use in steam-making , leaving the equivalent of 1,600 homes ' annual gas usage circulating to homes and businesses in rural Cumbria . <p> The new Lake District Biogas plant will produce gas from cheddar-making waste.Credit : Clearfleau <p> The project is going ahead thanks to a lucrative Government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from renewable sources , in order to meet EU renewable energy targets and UK climate change goals . <p> Lake District Biogas , the developer which is funding the up-front cost of the project , is expected to receive about ?
