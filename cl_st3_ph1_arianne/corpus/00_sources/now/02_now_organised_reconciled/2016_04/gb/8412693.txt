<h> Cloud computer non-profit urges more cloud computing organisations to get behind social impact and environment <p> The paper from the International Federation for Information Processing ( IFIP ) looks at Cloud Computing as a Socio-Technical System , and explains how it is transforming the global Information and Communication Technologies ( ICT ) scenario , ie ICT becomes ICT-as-a-service . The paper explains how cloud computing represents one of the " main leaps in the evolution of computing " , and also has many consequences for the " social , organisational , and environmental " landscape . <p> IFIP , the global professional association for the ICT sector , outlines in its policy paper recommendations to ensure that cloud computing has positive social , organisational and environmental consequences . <p> For many organisations the use of ICT services " ondemand " is an opportunity that offers great " flexibility and innovation " , the paper states . <p> The policy paper , entitled , " Towards a Human-Centred Cloud Computing : an International Perspective on the Public Interest " , was developed by IFIP 's Domain Committee on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from across its network . <p> The NGO 's policy paper outlines recommendations to ensure that cloud computing has positive consequences , with a focus on issues such as privacy and security as well as positive social , organisational and environmental impact . <p> Norberto Patrignani , DCCC Chair and Senior Associate Lecturer in Computer Ethics at the Doctorate School of Politecnico of Torino , said the policy paper has been the result of 18 months work by the group to identify and articulate the key issues relating to the cloud . <p> " Cloud computing is one of the biggest leaps we 've seen in the evolution of computing and is transforming the way information is managed and accessed , " said Patrignani . <p> " The shift back to centralised architecture where users and organisations consume on-demand resources provided by very large data centres and service centres makes it critical that we take steps to ensure that the cloud and its underlying infrastructure are implemented and managed responsibly and ethically .
