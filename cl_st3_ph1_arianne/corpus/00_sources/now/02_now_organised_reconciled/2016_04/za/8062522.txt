<h> Drought not a curse for our sins : Zuma <p> DELAREYVILLE , SOUTH AFRICA -- JULY 3 : Mosalashuping Kgomo 's cattle graze on July 3 , 2015 in Delareyville , South Africa . Kgomo is among the farmers in the area who have been affected by the current drought . The drought has affected many parts of the country where farmers growing maize , soya beans and sunflowers have incurred major losses . After that , there will be floods . All of this is due to climate change . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ because of our sins . The way we live life here on earth has affected our climate , " said Zuma . <p> This was Zuma 's first public appearance after he apologised to the nation for the Nkandla debacle . <p> The President received a resounding welcome , despite being late after his helicopter could not land due to bad weather . <p> Zuma apologised for the delay , saying that the helicopter had to return to Durban and that he had to be driven to Melmoth . <p> Before he took the podium a man dressed in a black suit lay on the floor and had to be forcibly removed by Zuma 's bodyguards . It was not clear what the man 's intentions were . <p> Addressing the gathering , Zuma : " We are gathered here because we have a big problem . There is a lot that government has done and we are still meeting because the drought is still there , even though the rain has fallen .
