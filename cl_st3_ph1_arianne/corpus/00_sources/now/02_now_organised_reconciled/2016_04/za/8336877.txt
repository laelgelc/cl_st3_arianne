<h> Non-linear climate change emergency <p> If you spend a lot of time talking to scientists about climate change , there 's one word you 'll hear time and time again , yet it is hardly ever mentioned in the public discussion of climate change . The word is " non-linear " . <p> Most people think of global warming as an incremental thing . It may be inexorable , but it 's also predictable . Alas , most people are wrong . Climate is a very complex system , and complex systems can change in non-linear ways . <p> In other words , you can not count on the average global temperature rising steadily but slowly as we pump more and more greenhouse gases into the atmosphere . It may do that , but there may also be a sudden jump in the average global temperature that lands you in a world of hurt . It was the hottest January ever -- and then the average global temperature in February was a full fifth of a degree Celsius higher than January . <p> That was a huge jump since the " average global temperature " is an average of all the temperatures over the seas and the land in both the summer hemisphere and the winter hemisphere . It is normally a very stable figure , changing no more than a few hundredths of a degree from year to year . <p> But March was not only hotter than February . It was hotter by an even wider margin than February was over January .
