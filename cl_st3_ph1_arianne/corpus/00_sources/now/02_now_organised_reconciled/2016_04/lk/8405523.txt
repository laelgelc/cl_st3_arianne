<h> Sri Lanka to sign Paris Agreement on Climate Change today Featured <p> Paris Agreement on Climate Change will be signed today at a ceremony held in United Nations headquarters , New York . <p> Sri Lanka 's Minister of Science , Technology and Research Susil Premajayantha will be signing the agreement on behalf of the Sri Lankan government . <p> Sri Lanka at the 21st session of the United Nations Framework Convention held in the French capital of Paris on Environmental disaster ( UNFCCC ) last year agreed to contribute to the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> The Minister will also attend the UN General Assembly on the World Drug Problem and the High Level Thematic Debate on achieving the Sustainable Development Goals , which is being held from April 19 -21 at the UN Headquarters .
