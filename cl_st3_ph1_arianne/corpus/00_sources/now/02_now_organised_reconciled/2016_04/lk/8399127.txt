<h> Global challenges and the realities at the grassroots <p> We are faced with three unprecedented challenges in the contemporary world . While the first is the almost intractable , violent conflict between two seemingly irreconcilable world views , the second is human induced climate change . And the third is the need to find a sustainable social and economic model that can satisfy diverse needs of people without creating the kind of environmental fallout that we are saddled with today . All these challenges did not come up overnight but have a complex historical backdrop . <p> At least since the beginning of western colonial expansion centered on Europe , enlightenment thinking gave rise to a set of social and political thought that has facilitated the transformation of traditional European societies . A key outcome of this transformation was the rise of capitalism that not only flourished in Europe but also extended to many non- European colonies , notably North America , Australia and India .
