<h> Ireland is still doing badly when it comes to greenhouse gases <h> Ireland 's greenhouse gas emissions were at a four-year high in 2015 . <p> IRELAND HAS SEEN its clean air policy backslide in the face of mounting international pressure for improvements in the sector . <p> Last year greenhouse gas emissions rose by 5.5% , something that was out of step with a slight decrease across the rest of the European Union . <p> Domestically , the main culprits when it came to increased emissions were the power generation sector , which saw a 5.3% increase ; the cement sector , up 11% ; and the food and drink sector , which increased by 4.6% . <p> There was also an increase of 11% in the aviation sector . <p> In a report released today , the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) singled out the use of the coal-fired plant at Moneypoint -- which saw an increase of 20% on their emissions -- as a source of concern . <p> Is it really that bad ?
