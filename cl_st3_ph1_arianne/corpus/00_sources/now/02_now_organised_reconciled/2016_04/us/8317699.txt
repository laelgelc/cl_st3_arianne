" We live in an age of terror , or perhaps an age in which the potential for acts of terrorism have taken a central role in the perception of our reality , " she said.In the case of Christians , she argued that violence in Old Testament books is often attributed to Judaism , which has led to anti-Semitism , while only the hope and positivity have been claimed by Christians.Similarly , for many not familiar with the Koran and its precepts , the entire Islamic faith has often been held hostage and blamed for the actions of violent extremists .
