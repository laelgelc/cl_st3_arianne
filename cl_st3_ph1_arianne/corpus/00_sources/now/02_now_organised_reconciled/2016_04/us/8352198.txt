<h> Were Dinosaurs Early Victims Of Climate Change ? <p> A new study finds dinosaurs were already having trouble as a result of changes to the earth 's climate , even before the asteroid hit . ( Pixabay ) <p> Scientists widely believe that dinosaurs were wiped out millions of years ago when an asteroid hit the earth . But a new study says dinosaurs were already having trouble with the way the earth 's climate was changing . <p> The research was published today in the Proceeding of the National Academy of Sciences Journal .
