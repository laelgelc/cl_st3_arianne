<h> Climate change lawsuit by 21 youths takes big step forward <p> PORTLAND , Ore . ( AP ) The federal government and big energy companies have lost an important round in their effort to block a climate change lawsuit by young plaintiffs who contend the U.S. is failing to protect them from the harmful effects of greenhouse gas emissions . <p> U.S. Magistrate Judge Thomas Coffin in Eugene , Ore. , on Friday rejected motions by the U.S. government and trade groups representing the energy companies to dismiss the lawsuit by 21 plaintiffs ages 8 to 19 . <p> A U.S. District Court judge has the final say over whether the lawsuit will proceed to trial . <p> The plaintiffs contend the release of dangerous carbon dioxide emissions into the atmosphere is a violation of their constitutional rights . They want the U.S. government to be forced to create a plan that would significantly cut the emissions .
