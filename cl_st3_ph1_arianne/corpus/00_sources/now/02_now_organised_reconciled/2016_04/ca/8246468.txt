<p> As governments and engineers ponder how to adapt to the far-reaching effects of climate change , one local company has come up with a tool to help solve the problem . <p> The Climate Change Hazards Information Portal ( CCHIP ) allows users to access a wide variety of climate data , while contextualizing it at the same time . <p> It 's the brainchild of Risk Sciences International , a risk assessment firm in Ottawa celebrating its 10-year anniversary this year . <p> A team of seven researchers and engineers from RSI worked over the past four years to create the comprehensive and customizable climate database . <p> Users are able to enter a location to receive data from nearby Environment Canada weather stations . CCHIP then combines that data with global climate models , in order to create projections for the future .
