<h> New Democrats face a real conundrum around pipelines , says Avi Lewis <p> When the Leap Manifesto proposal was released during the election campaign , NDP Leader Mulcair did not back it but he said he appreciated the debate of ideas . Now nearly two dozen NDP riding associations are urging the party to embrace the manifesto 's plan ahead of the party 's convention in Edmonton this week .
