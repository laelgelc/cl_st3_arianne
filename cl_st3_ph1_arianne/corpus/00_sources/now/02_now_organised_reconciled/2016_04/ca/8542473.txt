" That sits much better than being a " climate change fear-monger , " a sufferer from " Harperphobia , " and similar terms of endearment he has used previously . <p> I trust he wo n't mind if I refer to him as " our local extreme right-winger , " or perhaps , " our resident libertarian . " He believes in free speech , and claims " anyone who is offended is so by choice . " So I am sure he 'll choose to ignore any negative feelings provoked by such labels . <p> More importantly , I am really worried about McLennan 's opposition to unions and to " pandering " -- giving individuals or groups special consideration . Has he thought about what our Canadian society would @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> There would n't be any women judges , MLAs or senators , for example . By the law of the land less than a century ago , women were n't persons , and therefore could n't hold such responsible offices . Allowing women to vote was seen as " pandering " to a special interest group by those in power -- all men , of course . <p> Job applicants would bargain wages individually with employers . In the early 1800s , slavery was perfectly legal . Slave owners certainly did not believe that their labourers deserved any special consideration like the rights of free men . <p> No Workers Compensation . No employment standards to regulate hours of work , minimum wage , and vacations . No Canada Pension Plan @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ programs of the " left wing nanny state " that he despises . <p> We 've already had the Dark Ages . We do n't need to return to them . <p> The most humane elements of present-day society emerged when people heard and acted on the voices of those not well-served by the laws and social arrangements of the time .
