<h> Queen 's Park Trip Could Explain Hydro Costs <p> The president and CEO of the Windsor-Essex Regional Chamber of Commerce heads to Toronto armed with a list of suggestions to make cap and trade work for Ontario businesses . <p> Matt Marchand will be one of many chamber presidents taking part in a Queen 's Park Advocacy Day at the Ontario Legislature . He 'll meet with government officials , both elected and not , and talk about the concerns businesses in Windsor-Essex have as the province moves ahead implementing its climate change policy . <p> Already high hydro rates are expected to increase , but Marchand says there may be ways the Ontario government can offset those costs . <p> " It could be tax relief . It could be regulatory relief , " he says . " We want to provide ideas . We want to provide opportunities . We want to provide suggestions to the government how best we can both implement our respective agendas .
