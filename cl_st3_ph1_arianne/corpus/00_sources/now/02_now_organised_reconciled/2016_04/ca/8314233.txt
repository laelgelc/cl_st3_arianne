<h> Study seeks to find out more about rock snot algae and its impact on salmon <p> Mount Allison University Geography and Environment professor Dr. Joshua Kurek describes his research at a Friday announcement for the Canadian Foundation for Innovation 's John R Evans Leaders Fund . Kurek received a grant of $78 , 310 for his research program . <p> Dr. Joshua Kurek was awarded more than $78,000 from the Canadian Foundation for Innovation through the John R. Evans Leaders Fund . The funding was announced by Friday federal Government House Leader and Beausejour MP Dominic LeBlanc . <p> With this new research infrastructure funding , Kurek will establish the Environmental Change and Aquatic Biomonitoring Lab at Mount Allison . <p> " My research program focuses on understanding how environmental stressors impact lakes and rivers , " explained Kurek . " Having essential equipment both in the lab and in the field will allow us to get a better picture of long-term environmental changes and what they mean to freshwaters .
