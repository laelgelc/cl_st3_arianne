<h> Robertson rips pipeline over emissions <p> The Trudeau government 's method of assessing the climate impact of Kinder Morgan 's $5.4 billion Alberta-to-Burnaby pipeline expansion is inadequate , the City of Vancouver asserted in a letter Monday .
