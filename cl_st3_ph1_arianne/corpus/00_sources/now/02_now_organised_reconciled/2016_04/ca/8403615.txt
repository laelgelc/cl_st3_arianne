<h> Local environmental groups demand halt to green space development <p> A controversy exists over the development of an area of land at Cap Nature in Pierrefonds . Conservationists say the green space is essential and should not be developed.Vincenzo D'Alto / Montreal Gazette <p> Just in time for International Earth Day on Friday , local environment groups are calling on Montreal Mayor Denis Coderre to make a real commitment to preserving the remaining natural green space on the island of Montreal . <p> On Thursday , representatives of several local environment groups posted a Charter on Green Space Protection on the door of Montreal City Hall , demanding an immediate moratorium on development of green spaces on the island and recognizing every Montrealer 's right to live near accessible natural spaces . <p> The charter is the fruit of a forum held last weekend which brought together about 100 people , including scientists and experts in biodiversity , green space protection , urban planning , health , social welfare , along with members of 40 local environmental organizations , including Canadian Parks and Wilderness Society ( CPAWS @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ? -l'Orme , GRAME , Sauvons la Falaise , the Green Coalition , and the Sierra Club Qu ? bec . <p> " Some of these organizations have been working for more than 30 years on the protection of natural spaces , " said Floris Ensink , president of the Quebec chapter of the Sierra Club . " During that period , about 75 hectares of natural space has been disappearing ( through development ) per year from the island of Montreal and even today it continues . There are still today residential development projects planned for green spaces supported by the City of Montreal . " <p> Ensink said environmental groups want Coderre , as chair of the Urban Agglomeration of Montreal that oversees land use , to show he is serious about battling climate change and protecting the rights of Montrealers to live close to natural spaces . <p> " Urban sprawl is not just bad for biodiversity , " he said .
