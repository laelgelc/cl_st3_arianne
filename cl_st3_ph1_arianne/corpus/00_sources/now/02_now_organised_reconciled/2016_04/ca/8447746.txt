<h> Mulroney says Energy East ' a nation-building opportunity ' for Trudeau <p> Former prime minister Brian Mulroney says pipeline development is an opportunity for Prime Minister Justin Trudeau to bring Canadians together by creating jobs across the country . <p> " It 's a big challenge and there 's nothing guaranteed , but that 's what leadership is all about , " the former PM told CTV 's Question Period . <p> Mulroney said that while Trudeau is n't to blame for what he calls " the last 15 years of inaction " regarding pipelines , it is up to him to bring Aboriginal , environmental and provincial stakeholders into agreement " to proceed with a nation-building exercise of moving our vast natural resources to the west and east coasts to provide employment and wealth for all Canadians .
