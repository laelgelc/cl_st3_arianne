The bleaching was the worst in the reef 's northern sector -- where virtually no reefs escaped it . <p> " Between 60 and 100 per cent of coral are severely bleached on 316 reefs , nearly all in the northern half of the Reef , " Prof. Terry Hughes , the lead researcher , said in a statement to the news media . <p> Severe bleaching means that coral could die , depending on how long they are subject to these conditions .
