Following the Paris Conference , we should be very encouraged by the potential for positive action on climate change . <p> Although coming a decade after Canada , last year 's U.S. Supreme Court support for same-sex marriage gave the LGBTQ cause globally a massive shot in the arm . <p> As the leader of the New Democratic Party I had always strongly opposed the undemocratic nature of the Investor State Dispute provisions in international trade agreements . The government is now showing the whole country what progressive leadership really looks like on issues like the minimum wage , progressive taxation and the environment .
