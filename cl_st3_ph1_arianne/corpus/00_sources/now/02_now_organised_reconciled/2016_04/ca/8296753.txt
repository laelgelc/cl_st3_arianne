<h> Bernie Sanders says he met with Pope in Rome <p> US presidential candidate Bernie Sanders , backdropped by the dome of St. Peter 's Basilica , leaves after an interview with the Associated Press , at the Vatican Saturday , April 16 , 2016 . Democratic presidential candidate Bernie Sanders says in an interview with The Associated Press that he met with Pope Francis . Sanders says the meeting took place Saturday morning before the pope left for his one-day visit to Greece .
