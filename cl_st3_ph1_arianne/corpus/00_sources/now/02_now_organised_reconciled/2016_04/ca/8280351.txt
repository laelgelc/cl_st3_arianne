<h> LEED gold for sustainable design awarded to U of T research building <p> The Environmental Science and Chemistry Building ( ESCB ) at the University of Toronto Scarborough has been recognized with one of the highest levels of achievement in sustainable building design . <p> The 10,220-square-metre building , which officially opened in January 2016 , has received a gold certification from the Leadership in Energy &amp; Environmental Design ( LEED ) . <p> " It 's a tremendous source of pride that our building dedicated to research on the physical environment has achieved LEED Gold , " says U of T Scarborough Principal Bruce Kidd . " The ESCB represents our commitment to the environment and to the benefits of using sustainable design elements . " <p> Only materials low in Volatile Organic Compounds ( VOCs ) were used in the construction of the building interior <p> Low-flow plumbing reduces water consumption throughout the building <p> The result is a facility that will achieve an annual energy reduction of 54 per cent compared to standard buildings .
