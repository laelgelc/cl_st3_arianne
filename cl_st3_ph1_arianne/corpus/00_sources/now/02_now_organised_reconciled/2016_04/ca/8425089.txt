" Sudburians know first-hand what can go wrong when we do n't care for our environment , but also what we can accomplish when we come together as a community . <p> " Climate change is a global problem that requires action on the local level , if we are to mitigate its effects . Up until today , Green Economy North has been a project of reThink Green . Now , it needs to be championed by all of us in the community .
