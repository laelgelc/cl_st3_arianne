<p> The Wildrose Party says the provincial government is misleading Albertans on the full cost of the carbon tax . <p> Shadow Minister of Electricity and Renewables Don MacIntyre says the NDP is n't giving us the full scope of how the tax will work throughout the economy . <p> " By the time it 's fully implemented , this carbon tax is going to result in somewhere between $1,000 and $2,000 extra costs to our citizens . That does not take into account the increase in municipal taxes and the cost of busing our children , things like that . " <p> Macintyre says the Alberta government has n't dared to mention how the carbon tax will affect municipal governments with things like garbage collection and heating , which will show up in our property taxes . He says the government wants to get 3 billion dollars out of this tax , despite the rebates some Albertans will see . <p> " Three billion dollars is coming out of my pocket and your pocket and everyone else 's pocket .
