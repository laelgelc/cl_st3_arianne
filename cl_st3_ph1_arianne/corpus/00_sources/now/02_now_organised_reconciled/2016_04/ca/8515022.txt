<h> MacAulay in Japan for meeting of agriculture ministers <p> Lawrence MacAulay speaks to the House of Commons in this Canadian Press file photo . <h> " Canada strongly believes that creating more opportunities for farmers through open agri-food trade is essential " <p> Agriculture ministers in the G7 countries have wrapped up meetings in Japan , where they discussed increasing farmers ' income , among other topics . <p> P.E.I. MP Lawrence MacAulay , who is the federal minister of Agriculture for Canada , was among those who took part in the G7 Niigata Agriculture Ministers ' meeting April 23-24 . <p> " Canada strongly believes that creating more opportunities for farmers through open agri-food trade is essential to increasing production and helping farmers earn better incomes , " said MacAulay . " Canada will continue to help foster collaboration across the G7 in order to build a resilient global food system that will continue to contribute as a major driver of our economy . " <p> The ministers supported the adoption of the G7 Niigata Agriculture Ministers ' Meeting Declaration , which focuses on work towards @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ also encourages improving sustainable agricultural production and productivity , as well as reducing agriculture 's environmental footprint and adapting to climate change . <p> MacAulay met for the first time with Hiroshi Moriyama , Japan 's minister of agriculture , forestry and fisheries . He used this opportunity to build on Canada 's trade relations with Japan and underline Canada 's commitment to work together on emerging agriculture issues . <p> Minister MacAulay also held meetings with his U.S. and German counterparts , Secretary of Agriculture Tom Vilsack and Minister of Agriculture Christian Schmidt , to discuss ways of increasing the competitiveness of the agricultural sector .
