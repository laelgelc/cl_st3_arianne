<h> Nunavut wildlife board ponders fate of threatened shore bird <h> Red-necked phalarope proposed for listing under Species at Risk Act <p> Officials with the federal environment department want to list the red-necked phalarope as a species of " Special Concern " under the Species at Risk Act . ( COSEWIC IMAGE ) <p> You might not know the red-necked phalarope by name , but the small wading bird breeds in all three regions of Nunavut during the summer months . <p> And Environment and Climate Change Canada is asking Nunavut 's wildlife regulatory board for support in listing the bird as a species of " Special Concern " under the federal Species at Risk Act , according to documents found on the Nunavut Wildlife Management Board 's website . <p> The wildlife board will hold a written public consultation and accept written input until June 1 , an April 1 letter from the board said . <p> In its proposal to the board , Environment Canada said the bird was listed as a species of special concern by the Committee of the Status of Endangered Wildlife in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " The bird has declined over the last 40 years in an important staging area , however overall population trends during the last three generations are unknown , " Environment Canada said in its proposal to the Nunavut board . <p> The proposal did not say what staging area or provide any other information on the bird 's population . <p> But COSEWIC submitted its recommendation to the Environment Canada minister in October 2015 ; the minister then extended the consultation period until October 2016 . <p> The red-necked phalarope falls within the sandpiper family of shorebirds . <p> The interesting thing about these birds is that the usual sex roles are reversed , according to the Audobon Society 's website . Females are larger and more colourful and they take the lead in courtship . And it 's the males who remain at the next to incubate eggs and care for the young . <p> Because the red-necked phalarope is found in every Canadian jurisdiction , the federal agency is required to consult a long list of provincial and territorial government agencies as well as Aboriginal organizations . <p> In @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ every community except for Grise Fiord and Resolute Bay , which fall outside of the bird 's breeding grounds . <p> The consultation packages , sent to local hunters and trappers organizations as well as regional wildlife boards , included a letter , a narrated Powerpoint presentation and a questionnaire asking if the community opposed , supported or is indifferent to the proposed listing , according to Environment Canada . <p> So far , only the community of Taloyoak responded by saying " indifferent , " Environment Canada said . <p> If the red-necked phalarope is listed under schedule 1 one of the Species at Risk Act , a management plan must be prepared within three years . <p> Those interested in submitting comments to the NWMB can contact the board by calling 975-7300 or by email at .
