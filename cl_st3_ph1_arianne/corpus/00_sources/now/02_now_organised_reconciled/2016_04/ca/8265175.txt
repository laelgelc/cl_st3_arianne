<p> Equating climate change to a calamity worse than the Second World War , Ontario Minister of Environment and Climate Change Glen Murray pressed municipalities to move forward as quickly as possible on climate change and sustainability projects . <p> Murray sat in the Town of Halton Hills Council Chambers Friday afternoon listening to Council and Town staff make a pitch for a Provincial funding partnership . <p> " Halton Hills is ready to be part of the coalition , " said Mayor Rick Bonnette . " Halton Hills has a long history of acting locally and thinking globally . " <p> " I see that there is a lot of opportunities between the Town and the Province , " the mayor said . " We have prepared an action plan that is aligned with provincial goals . " <p> " We look forward to establishing a funding partnership with the Province , " he added . <p> Damian Szbalski , Town Manager of Sustainability and Corporate Policy Advisor presented a history of the Halton Hills 's active environmentalism . Other actions include , LEED facilities , solar power projects , LED streetlight replacement , Green Development Standards , partnerships with FCM , Compact of Mayors , and potentially creating a net zero community in the Town 's future expansion , Vision Georgetown in southwest Georgetown . <p> " This is a a rather remarkable community , " said Murray , after the presentation . <p> The Minister said he is looking for a spectrum of communities who are looking to be net zero . Murray said the Ministry would rather fund locally-made and led plans . <p> " Decisions are better made locally , " he said . <p> Murray said there is an urgency as the world gets warmer , and predicts climate in the next 30 years will be disruptive , contributing to economic hardships and fatalities . <p> Ward 2 Councillor Ted Brown , a farmer , was intrigued by Murray @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impacts , and suggested local farmers would be interested in innovative solutions .
