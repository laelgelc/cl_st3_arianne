<h> Earth Day signing of Paris climate deal brings out 171 countries <p> New York - A record 171 countries turned out on Earth Day at the United Nations in New York on Friday to sign the Paris climate deal , raising hopes that it will be enacted much faster than anticipated . <p> U.S , Secretary of State John Kerry , with his granddaughter sitting on his knee , was joined by French President Francois Hollande and Canada 's Justin Trudeau , as he signed the Paris climate agreement . <p> " This is a moment in history , " UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon said after announcing that a record 171 countries were prepared to sign the climate deal , reports ABC.net.au . " Today you are signing a new covenant with the future . " <p> China and the United States , the world 's largest producers of greenhouse gasses , pledged today to formally adopt the Paris deal to slow global warming by the end of this year . Together , the two countries account for 38 percent of global emissions . <p> " China @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ G20 Hangzhou summit in September this year , " China 's Vice Premier Zhang Gaoli told the U.N . signing ceremony , reports Reuters . <p> U.S. Secretary of State John Kerry said the United States " looks forward to formally joining this agreement this year . " Using executive authority , President Barack Obama will formally adopt the agreement . <p> It is appropriate that the signing ceremony is being held on Earth Day , a day when the world takes the time to appreciate the Earth and bring awareness to environmental issues that we can do something about . That is what world leaders are doing today in New York , and the signing marks the first step in our keeping our promise to cut greenhouse gas emissions . <p> So what will happen after the deal is signed ? The Paris climate agreement will go into effect as soon as 55 countries , representing 55 percent of greenhouse gas emitters ratifies the deal . <p> To date , 15 countries , mostly island states , have already fully approved the agreement and will formally present the completed ratification @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ratification process could take up to a year or more . <p> But once the appropriate number of countries have formally ratified the climate deal , it will go into effect . The target date for ratification has been set at 2020 , but there is a good possibility it could be ratified as early as 2017 . " We are in a race against time , " Mr. Ban told the gathering at the UN General Assembly . <p> " More countries have come together here to sign this agreement today than for any other cause in the history of humankind and that is a reason for hope , " said Leonardo DiCaprio , speaking at the formal signing today , reports Reuters .
