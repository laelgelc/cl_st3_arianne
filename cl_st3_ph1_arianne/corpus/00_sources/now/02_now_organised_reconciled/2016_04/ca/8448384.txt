<h> City of Edmonton says no to releasing neighbourhood flood predictions <p> Edmonton now has more than 100 neighbourhood maps that predict , within inches , where water will pool and which basements will likely flood when the city is hit by the storm of the century . <p> But good luck getting a copy . <p> The maps are part of an estimated $2.4-billion project to protect all of Edmonton 's vulnerable neighbourhoods against a one-in-100-year flood . But drainage officials worry they 'll cause panic if they release the flood maps before plans to fix flooding issues are in place . <p> " Two or three billion dollars worth of work . That does n't happen overnight , " said Todd Wyman , acting head of utilities infrastructure . <p> " Instead of giving residents half a solution , I want to give them a whole solution . Because it 's more than just an individual homeowner can take on . " <p> The city created the maps using a low-flying plane and stereoscopic photos of the ground .
