<h> Curb water shortage through law : Information Commissioner <p> Mumbai : Ajit Jain , Information Commissioner and Former IAS Officer , stressed upon the need to form a water strategy to curb water loss through law at the panel discussion conducted at Brics Conclave 2016 conducted on Saturday . <p> A panel discussion on the topic ' Building sustainable cities to improve delivery of urban services ' was being conducted at the Brics Conclave 2016 . Alice Charles , Lead Urban Development , World Economic Forum who chaired the panel discussion spoke about the need for a detailed adaptation and mitigation plans to tackle the impact of climate change . The need for effective management of natural resources like water and forests was stressed throughout the panel discussion . <p> Jain who was a part of the panel discussion stressed upon the need to adopt a decentralised plan to curb water shortage in Mumbai . " Due to rapid urbanisation which is a major problem in Mumbai , there have been limited resources . " There must be a proper water sector reforms to avoid water shortage in Mumbai . There must be a mechanism formed to use limited water that can happen if usage of water falls under the ambit of law " , added Jain . <p> Jain also added that there must be strategic plan being implemented to curb water shortage through a institutional legal framework .
