According to the Vaastu norms , the bathroom should be in the north-west or west , while the water source ( well or borewell ) should be in the north-east . As the mandated setback norms are observed more in violation , the bathroom of one house and the borewell of the neighbouring house are located too close to each other . <p> This means the seepage from the septic tank ( north-west ) of a house mixes with the water of the borewell ( north-east ) of the neighbouring building .
