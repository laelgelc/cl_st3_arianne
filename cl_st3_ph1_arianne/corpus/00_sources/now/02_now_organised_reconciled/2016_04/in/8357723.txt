<h> Notices to Centre , state over declaring monkeys as vermin <p> Himachal Pradesh high court on Tuesday issued notices to the Union and state governments on the issue of declaring monkeys as vermin in the jurisdiction of Shimla municipal corporation for the next six months . <p> TNN Apr 20 , 2016 , 03.04 AM IST <p> Shimla : Himachal Pradesh high court on Tuesday issued notices to the Union and state governments on the issue of declaring monkeys as vermin in the jurisdiction of Shimla municipal corporation for the next six months . <p> The court further directed the secretary , environment , forests and climate change , Government of India ; Himachal Pradesh chief secretary ; and member secretary of National Board of Wildlife to file replies within six weeks . The matter will be listed before the court on May 3 for further orders . <p> A division bench comprising Chief Justice Mansoor Ahmad Mir and Justice Tarlok Singh Chauhan passed these orders on a petition filed by Rajeshwar Singh Negi , where he challenged a notification issued by the Union government declaring monkey @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of Shimla MC . By declaring monkeys as vermin , the government made a provision to hunt the animals . <p> Rajeshwar Singh Negi has pleaded that main purpose of issuing a notification was to check growth of monkeys , which allegedly pose a threat to people and crops . But he pointed out the notification has been issued only for Shimla town , which has less agricultural area than other parts of the state . <p> The petitioner has asserted that the notification disregards a constitutional mandate which ensures security of wild creatures and the environment . He said right to life ensured by Article 21 of the Constitution of India is extended to animals as well .
