<h> Celebrations can wait : One good monsoon will not unmake two years of drought <p> Indians have hailed twin , almost identical predictions , about a bountiful monsoon this year after two successive years of deficient rainfall . It is as if the skies will open up with good fortune . <p> In essence , 106 per cent in 2016 could mean the highest rainfall since 1999 when the IMD had predicted 108 per cent of long period average . A good rainfall is necessary for the country 's farmers and therefore , in the long run , to contain inflation and sustain overall economic growth . It is also critical to fill up the country 's water reservoirs which stand severely depleted . But a good monsoon alone can not be the panacea for India 's muted agricultural growth or to avert future water wars . We are hurtling towards a crisis which one good monsoon alone may not solve . <p> Representational image .
