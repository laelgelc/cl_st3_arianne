<h> Alleged violation of CRZ regulations : official inspects building <p> A Research Officer from the South Zone office of the Ministry of Environment and Forest and Climate Change conducted an on-the-spot inspection of a structure under construction on the seashore near the View Tower in Kanyakumari here recently , according to R.S. Lal Mohan , Convenor of the Nagercoil Chapter of the Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage ( INTACH ) . <p> Dr. Lal Mohan told The Hindu here on Friday that the Ministry had deputed the official in response to a representation submitted by INTACH during the third week of March . The official who had conducted the inspection confirmed his spot visit and said he was not authorised to speak to the media . <p> The official also held a detailed interaction with environment activists , officials of the Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation and Executive Officer of the Kanyakumari town panchayat after the inspection , Dr. Lal Mohan said .
