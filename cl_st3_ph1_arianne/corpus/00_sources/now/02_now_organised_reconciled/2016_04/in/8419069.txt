<h> Former cashier of co-op bank gets four years imprisonment for swindling funds <p> A 60-year-old man , who worked as a cashier at a Ghansoli branch of Thane District Central Co-operative Bank in 1998 , was found guilty of swindling the bank 's money . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 12.04 AM IST <p> NAVI MUMBAI : A 60-year-old man , who worked as a cashier at a Ghansoli branch of Thane District Central Co-operative Bank in 1998 , was found guilty of swindling the bank 's money . He has been sentenced to four years rigorous imprisonment by the district court . <p> Thane chief judicial magistrate R M Kulkarni , who passed the order , also fined the accused , Arun Salve , a resident of Shirol , Sahapur Rs 10,000 and in case of default , an additional three months rigorous imprisonment . <p> Salve was a cashier at the co-operative bank between May 14 , 1998 and September 26 , 1998 during which he had stolen the money . He had misappropriated Rs 5,65,000 during this period .
