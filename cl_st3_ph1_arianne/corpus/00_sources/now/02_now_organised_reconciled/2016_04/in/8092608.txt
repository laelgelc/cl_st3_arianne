It is these alternatives that the authorities could study while they try to improve the capital 's air quality . <p> Perhaps closest comparison in the matter can be found in Beijing . The Chinese capital too has cracked down on a heaving transport sector to check the smog there . Adequate attention has also been paid to taxi and urban rail systems and bicycles . <p> " In fact , the focus is now on increasing the share of bicycles in total trips as well as building more pedestrian pathways , " added Liu . <p> Other success templates can be found in Mexico City and Bogota .
