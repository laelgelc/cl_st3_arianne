<h> Geo-tagging of civic projects may curb corruption among corporators <p> Thane : The TMC has taken the help of technology to curb corruption in civic projects like duplication of works and taking up repairs of good patches . <p> The concept will probably make the TMC the first municipal body in the country to have implemented the geo-tagging system in all civic works where the projects will be digitally tracked and any repetition of works could be easily spotted not only by the administration but also by the public . <p> As per the plans , all civic projects being undertaken within TMC limits have been geo-tagged right from the proposal to the payment stage . A digital footprint of the works will be made available to the administration and the public will be able to know how and where their tax money is being spent . A test run was successfully carried out last month . <p> This concept will make contractors and officials accountable for any misuse of civic funds . <p> Officials said the BMC that is often in the midst of controversies regarding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in its jurisdiction . <p> According to the system , a contractor will have to click images of the project site and upload them on a specific web portal of the municipal corporation . <p> While doing so , the longitude and latitude of the area will be recorded digitally . <p> Once the project is completed , the contractor will have to click images of the finished product from the location from where he had clicked before executing the work . The payment will be cleared only if the coordinates match , thus ruling out any foul play . <p> Corporators have alleged that slow server speed of the corporation was making it difficult for images to be uploaded properly . <p> Civic chief Sanjeev Jaiswal , however , said the system had been upgraded and over 11,000 photos of 2,800 projects were uploaded effortlessly till March-end . <p> The project has another benefit too .
