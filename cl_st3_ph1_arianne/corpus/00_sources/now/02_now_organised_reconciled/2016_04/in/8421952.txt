<h> Panel finds 100 acres of encroachments in 4 lakes <p> The joint legislature committee headed by Congress MLA KB Koliwad , which is looking into lake encroachments in the city , inspected four water bodies on Friday . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 03.44 AM IST <p> Bengaluru : The joint legislature committee headed by Congress MLA KB Koliwad , which is looking into lake encroachments in the city , inspected four water bodies on Friday . " Both government and private agencies have encroached upon on lakebeds . We found about 100 acres of encroachment in the four water bodies . I have sought for reports from the additional chief secretary before taking any decision , " Koliwad said . <p> Koliwad and his team visited Avalahalli near Banashankari , Singanayakanahalli off Yelahanka , Amanikere and Kacharakanahalli near Lingarajpuram to inspect the encroachments . Koliwad alleged that BDA had given permission to build layouts and the education department constructed schools on lakebeds as well .
