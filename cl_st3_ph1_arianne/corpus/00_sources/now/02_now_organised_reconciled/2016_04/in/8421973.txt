<h> City gardens remain open through the day <p> Nagpur : For the first time , all 150 gardens situated in the city have been kept open for citizens throughout the day . The initiative is part of Nagpur Municipal Corporation ( NMC ) plan to help citizens beat scorching heat . <p> As temperatures rise , many people exposed to scorching summer fall ill and some even die . To allow them a place to escape the harsh Sun , the civic body is implementing a heat action plan ( HAP ) along with state government . It is the state 's first ever HAP and the government plans on launching similar plans in other cities on its lines . Keeping NMC 's 90 and Nagpur Improvement Trust 's ( NIT ) 60 gardens open will let citizens take shelter under trees there . <p> NMC garden superintendent Sudhir Mate told TOI gardens will be open throughout the day till June 30 . " We used to close gardens for citizens between 10 am and 4 pm for maintenance work . Gardens have been identified as cool @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ open from April 1 onwards , " he said . <p> Mate added all staffers at gardens have been directed to make available drinking water to visitors . " A few gardens have water coolers while some do not have any drinking water facility . Pots will be made available through NMC or other organizations , " he said . <p> NMC also came up with an innovative plan under HAP . Deputy director ( health ) Dr Milind Ganvir said green mats will be erected at all major squares with traffic signal to provide shade for drivers . " We put up mats at RBI square on a trial basis . It proved to be success , particularly for two-wheeler riders . Plan is to increase the length of mats so that almost everyone waiting at signals is covered . Similar mats will be erected at other major squares soon , " he said . <p> Ganvir also said around 150 water kiosks have been installed in various parts . NMC and public health department had trained 1,760 personnel ( 160 doctors , 600 paramedics and 1,000 Anganwadi workers , ASHA , teachers from educational institutions ) in treating heat-affected persons .
