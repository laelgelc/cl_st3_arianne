<h> At Deonar dump , Sachin bats for ragpickers ' cause <p> Cricketer Sachin Tendulkar is all set to begin a new innings by trying to find a solution to the Deonar dump issue . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 05.42 AM IST <p> MUMBAI : Cricketer Sachin Tendulkar is all set to begin a new innings by trying to find a solution to the Deonar dump issue . <p> On Friday , Tendulkar , also a nominated MP , met BMC chief Ajoy Mehta at the civic ward office in Govandi where issues related to the Deonar dump were discussed . Tendulkar had earlier expressed the need to find long-term solutions to problems relating to health and lack of basic amenities faced by slum-dwellers who live near the dump . <p> Special security arrangements were made by the police for Tendulkar 's visit .
