<h> Temp fluctuation hits size of wheat grain , brings down harvest volume <p> Deepak Lavania <p> TNN Apr 8 , 2016 , 10.24 PM IST <p> Agra : Unseasonal rainfall and hailstorms have caused significant harm to standing crops of wheat . Now a rapid increase in temperature has affected the quality of the crop and volume of harvest . While the sudden change in temperature has caused the size of grains to decrease , production of the crop will be less by more than 20% , according to sources . <p> More than 80% of the district 's wheat crop has been harvested , of which grain has been extracted from 60% of the harvest . Mohan Singh Chahar , district head of Bharatiya Kisan Sangh said , " The size of the wheat grain has become smaller and is slightly blackish in colour . Due to the small size of the grain , production has also decreased . Average productivity of 42.43 quintal per hectare has reduced by around 20 - 30% . Sudden increase in temperature after rainfall and hailstorm affected the quality of the wheat grain . Earlier , winter temperature till January 14 was high which also affected wheat crop development . " <p> Government purchase centres started procuring wheat for the current procurement season from April 1 at 42 sites in Agra district .
