<h> NTR Park will not be renamed : KCR <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 05.00 AM IST <p> HYDERABAD : Chief minister K Chandrasekhar Rao on Friday said the government had no intention of renaming NTR Park . Speaking at the launch of actor Balakrishna 's 100th movie in the city , the chief minister said he had great regard for " Anna garu " ( former chief minister N T Rama Rao ) and his family , and therefore replacing his name with someone else 's was out of the question . <p> " After Ambedkar 's statue was installed near NTR Park , there has been speculation that the park will no longer bear NTR 's name . Let me make it clear , NTR Park will not be renamed , " KCR said . <p> Expressing his desire to watch Balakrishnas new film , the chief minister made a request ' to the actor that he should be given the privilege of watching the film first .
