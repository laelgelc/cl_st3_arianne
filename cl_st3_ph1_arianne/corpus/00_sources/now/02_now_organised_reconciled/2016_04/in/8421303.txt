<h> Ten of family have narrow escape after SUV catches fire <p> Ten members of a family heading to Gingee in what was to all appearances a sturdy SUV , and not a tinder box , had a very close shave when a blaze engulfed the vehicle on 100 Feet Road on Friday afternoon . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 02.49 AM IST <p> Chennai : Ten members of a family heading to Gingee in what was to all appearances a sturdy SUV , and not a tinder box , had a very close shave when a blaze engulfed the vehicle on 100 Feet Road on Friday afternoon . <p> Showing alacrity in a crisis , the man at the wheel , Gajendran , seeing smoke billow out of the AC vents , warned the other occupants of the SUV and they abandoned the vehicle and ran for cover before fire consumed the vehicle . <p> Firemen and two fire tenders from the Guindy and Ashok Pillar fire stations rushed to the spot and battled for 30 minutes to douse the blaze .
