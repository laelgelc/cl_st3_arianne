<h> Trans-harbour services hit for two hours on Friday morning <p> Manoj Badgeri <p> Commuters travelling on the trans-harbour route had a tough time on Friday morning , as 24 services were cancelled following a snag <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 01.55 AM IST <p> THANE : Commuters travelling on the trans-harbour route had a tough time on Friday morning , as 24 services were cancelled following a snag . The suburban train services on the Trans-harbour section were suspended for nearly two hours in the morning peak hours on Friday due to a technical fault in the overhead wire between Thane and Airoli station . <p> This is third such incident reported within the 10 km-stretch in a span of 20 days . A part of the overhead wire snapped on the down line near km 38/8 around 6:50am after a Thane local had just passed the section . Following the incident , power supply to the entire stretch was immediately disconnected resulting in suspension of traffic on both the lines on the route . Officials rushed to the spot to address the problem around @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 8:30am . Meanwhile , commuters travelling morning time to their respective workplaces had to no other option but to opt for alternate modes of transport including costly autos and buses . Few others who were stranded in trains between stations were forced to walk down the tracks to reach the nearest road connect and hire a road transport to their respective destinations . <p> " There were no trains running for over an hour , " said an office goer travelling between Thane and Belapur . Regular commuters and activists also raised questions over the quality of the weekly maintenance undertaken by the railways . <p> " How come the railways are n't able to tackle such mishaps even though they ruin our weekend travel by conducting mega blocks ?
