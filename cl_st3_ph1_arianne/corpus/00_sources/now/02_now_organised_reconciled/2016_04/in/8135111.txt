Of late , the practice has picked up in the top bureaucracy where senior officials , particularly from the finance ministry , take all-expenses paid holidays at some of the best resorts around Delhi after presentation of the Union Budget . <p> But this time , all the Supreme Court judges have decided to take a three-day retreat in Madhya Pradesh , a break from mundane court procedures and " reflect on issues that directly or indirectly come during the delivery of justice " . This is the fourth retreat of SC judges , the last one being in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 14 , the judges ' retreat , divided into eight sessions , is being organised at the National Judicial Academy spread across 63 acres in the outskirts of Bhopal where attendance of all the 25 apex court judges has been made compulsory . <p> Reserve Bank of India ( RBI ) governor Raghuram Rajan , national security adviser ( NSA ) Ajit Doval and experts from the fields of business and education are some of the speakers who will give the judges an overview of the world outside the judiciary . President Pranab Mukherjee is scheduled to address the concluding session on April 16 . <p> The top judiciary will have a closed-door session with NSA Doval on April 15 on national security and the threats of global terrorism , followed the next day by a session with Raghuram Rajan who will address economic growth and globalisation . There is a session also dedicated to climate change and key environmental challenges . <p> Interestingly , the programme schedule has not been shared with all the brother judges of the SC . The court is on vacation during this period . <p> The retreat comes amid a standoff between the higher judiciary and the government over the memorandum of procedure for appointment of judges . <p> The top judiciary is believed to be against a controversial clause proposed in the MoP framed by the government where the latter has reserved the right to reject any recommendation made by the collegium on the grounds of national security .
