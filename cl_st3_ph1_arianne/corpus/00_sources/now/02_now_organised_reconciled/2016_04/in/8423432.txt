<h> Her lover set to wed , 20-yr-old hangs herself <p> A 20-year-old woman allegedly committed suicide by hanging herself after learning that her boyfriend was planning to get married to another woman . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 05.40 AM IST <p> BHIWANDI : A 20-year-old woman allegedly committed suicide by hanging herself after learning that her boyfriend was planning to get married to another woman . <p> The police said that she hanged herself from a ceiling fan on Friday evening when she was alone at home . Her parents were at work at that time . <p> A note , which was purportedly written by the woman who lived in Bhiwandi , mentioned that she had been in a relationship and that her boyfriend was now planning to get married to someone else . <p> Sources in the police said that the two had been in a relationship for five years . <p> A case of suicide has been registered at Shanti Nagar police station .
