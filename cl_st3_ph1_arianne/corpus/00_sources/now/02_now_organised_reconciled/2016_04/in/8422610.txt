<h> Inter results out : Girls outshine boys <p> Girls outdid boys in the intermediate exams , results of which were declared by the Telangana State Board of Intermediate on Friday . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 05.03 AM IST <p> HYDERABAD : Girls outdid boys in the intermediate exams , results of which were declared by the Telangana State Board of Intermediate on Friday . In the first year , 67.64 per cent girls passed the exams as compared to 58.11 per cent of boys . In the second year , 58.9 per cent girls passed the exam as opposed to 48 per cent of boys . <p> However , in a disappointment , there seemed to be hardly any increase in the pass percentage.While the overall pass percentage of second year students increased by a meagre 1 per cent from last year to stand at 62.01 , the pass percentage for first year students dropped by 2 per cent to 53.55 . <p> Experts suggested that the government needed to strengthen pri vate institutes to achieve better results .
