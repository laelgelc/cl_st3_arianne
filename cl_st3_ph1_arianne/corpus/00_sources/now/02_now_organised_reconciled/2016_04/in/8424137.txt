<h> Heat is on the cars <p> Car drivers are feeling the summer heat , quite literally.In just a month 's time , the city has witnessed eight incidents of cars going up in flames midway . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 06.18 AM IST <p> Vadodara : Car drivers are feeling the summer heat , quite literally . <p> In just a month 's time , the city has witnessed eight incidents of cars going up in flames midway . Luckily , no one was injured in the incidents but the owners did suffer damages worth thousands . <p> According to the fire brigade officials , the incidents are a result of overheating , rising temperatures and minimal attention to regular maintenance . <p> A majority of the cars that went up in flames were poorly maintained by the owners . <p> " Cars are made of highly inflammable materials . A loose connection in the circuit of the car battery can lead to sparks which later can lead to fire owing to the presence of such material . Mostly , the drivers do n't @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " said Bhargav Vyas , senior fire station officer . <p> Vyas attributed the main cause of the unexpected fires to poor maintenance of vehicles by the citizens . " Poorly maintained vehicles and atmospheric heat are a deadly combination . The electric circuit as well as fuel circuit should be checked for wear and tear . Faulty pipes usually lead to fire incidents , " added Vyas . <p> Vyas added that the main reasons of fire are conduction , convection and radiation . <p> " Presences of rubber , rexin , plastic , fibre and U foam are highly inflammable substances . A whole car can go up in flames within few minutes due to the presence of such materials , " said another fire official . <p> " April , May and June register the maximum number of vehicle fires .
