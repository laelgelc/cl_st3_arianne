<h> Admission to Jamia gets tougher this yr <p> Entry to Jamia Millia Islamia University has become tougher this year with every seat in engineering and architecture departments attracting as many as 78 applicants . <p> Apr 23 , 2016 , 12.22 AM IST <p> New Delhi : Entry to Jamia Millia Islamia University has become tougher this year with every seat in engineering and architecture departments attracting as many as 78 applicants . For other undergraduate and postgraduate courses , aspirants have some relief as only 15 students are vying for each seat . <p> The university received 1.39 lakh applications for its 6,700 seats across streams till April 21 , which was the last date for submission initially . The application date has now been extended till April 25 . Sources said that the number of applicants will be close to 1.5 lakh this year . <p> Jamia extended the last date for submission of online application forms after getting numerous requests from aspirants and their parents from different parts of the country . For BTech and BArch , which have 350 and 80 seats , respectively , the university has received 33,364 applications so far . Last year , 1.49 lakh aspirants registered online , of which 1.37 lakh completed their application process by paying the application fee . <p> " The response has been very good and the competition is getting tougher .
