<h> Hanuman Jayanti rally peaceful in city <p> The Hanuman Jayanti bike rally , taken up by members of various Hindu groups , passed off peacefully in the city on Friday . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 01.07 AM IST <p> Hyderabad : The Hanuman Jayanti bike rally , taken up by members of various Hindu groups , passed off peacefully in the city on Friday . <p> The rally started from the Srirama temple at Gowliguda around noon and ended at the temple at Tadbund in Bowenpally around 6 pm . The participants of the rally re-assembled again for a public meeting at Imperial Gardens late in the evening . <p> City police made massive arrangements to ensure that the rally passes off peacefully . Police personnel from the law and order department of traffic police , Central Crime Station ( CCS ) , Special Branch ( SB ) , City Armed Reserve and other units were placed at several corners of the city . <p> However , some stray incidents were reported from a few pockets in the city . <p> A few youngsters @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ took off , were stopped by the police in the morning as they were accompanied by DJs . This led to a heated argument between the police and participants . <p> As decided earlier , the rally was diverted from RTC Crossroads towards Gandhi Nagar to avoid the sensitive Musheerabad area en route Bowenpally . <p> Meanwhile , some Muslim groups were found supplying water and butter milk to rally participants as day temperatures soared . <p> Traffic at several parts of the city was badly-hit in the afternoon as well as evening .
