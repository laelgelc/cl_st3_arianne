<h> DU 's postgrad admissions to begin on Mon <p> The application process for MPhil and PhD admissions of Delhi University ( DU ) will begin from April 25 , 2016 . <p> Apr 23 , 2016 , 12.25 AM IST <p> New Delhi : The application process for MPhil and PhD admissions of Delhi University ( DU ) will begin from April 25 , 2016 . Around 8,000 postgraduate seats are to be filled across 60 courses and this year , DU has decided to do away with subjective questions and instead aspirants will have to appear for multiple choice questions on OMR sheets . The exams , for the first time , will be conducted in five locations outside Delhi . <p> The complete application process is online and the last date for submission is May 24 and students can opt for Varanasi , Jammu , Ahmedabad , Kolkata and Chennai as locations for entrance tests . <p> According to R N Dubey , member of DU 's standing committee on PG admissions , the committee recommended 45 to 60 questions per paper for science courses @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ departments will have the liberty to have questions up to 100 . <p> The question papers will be set by individual departments . " The examinations will be conducted by the exam branch of the university . Entrance test centres , locations and dates for PG , MPhil and PhD will be same , " said Dubey . <p> Apart from those faculties offering interdisciplinary or professional courses , other departments reserve 50% of the total seats in each programme for direct admission for undergraduate students from DU .
