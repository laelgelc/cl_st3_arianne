<h> Tar balls along Khariawada beach alarm fishermen <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 07.44 AM IST <p> Vasco : The beach stretch at Khariawada , on Friday , was covered in tar balls washed in by the waves , making it difficult for fishermen to pull their nets ashore . <p> President of the Old Cross Canoe Owners ' Association , Custodio D'Souza said that when their canoes come to shore with the catch , they find it difficult to sort the fish as the shore is covered with tar balls . When fishing nets come in contact with tar balls , the fish smell , he complained . <p> Fishermen sense further trouble for their business as customers also refuse to buy fish because of health issues . There was a similar problem last year when tar was seen on the Baina beach . <p> Locals complain that walking on the shore becomes difficult as tar balls get stuck to the feet .
