<h> New Policy on Solid Waste Management in TN Soon <p> CHENNAI : Following the Union government 's promulgation of the Solid Waste Management Rule , 2016 , the Tamil Nadu government is expected to formulate a State policy in accordance with the new laws and chart out a strategy for managing solid waste within one year , as per requirements of the new rules . <p> The State policy on solid waste management will be formulated with the help of the Central Monitoring Committee based on the National Solid Waste Management policy and National Urban Sanitation Policy with a focus on waste reduction , reuse , recycling , recovery and optimum utilisation of various components of solid waste to reduce its impact on human health and environment . <p> The implementation of the new rules will mean that group housing , commercial institutions , non-residential complexes exceeding 200 dwellings or plots with an area exceeding 5,000 square metres should have a separate space for segregation , storage , and a decentralised processing of solid waste . <p> The new rules also require industries located within 100 km @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ within six months to ensure that at least five per cent of
