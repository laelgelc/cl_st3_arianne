<h> Naveen protecting tainted minister , say BJP and Cong <p> Hemanta Pradhan <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 06.37 AM IST <p> Bhubaneswar : The BJP and the Congress on Friday once again demanded chief minister Naveen Patnaik to remove minister Sanjay Das Burma from the government in the wake of his alleged link with the chit fund scam . <p> They said the longer Das Burma is allowed to continue in the ministry stronger will be public suspicion on the chief minister that he is giving protection to corruption . <p> Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik has the track record of removing leaders like late Nalini Mohanty and others from the ministry saying they were in the shadow of corruption . Das Burmas name having figured in the CBI probe list and the central agency issuing notice to him has left no room for his continuance in the government . It is surprising that the chief minister has still not taken any decision on the issue . This shows the chief minister 's double standard , senior BJP leader Pratap Sarangi told media persons here . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The chit fund companies have siphoned thousands of crores of rupees from the poor people of Odisha . When the CBI took over the investigation on the orders of the Supreme Court , people had hoped that the culprits would be caught soon and justice given to them . But the way the CBI is taking the probe has only strengthened the public suspicion on the motive of the central investigating agency , Sarangi said . He suggested that it was time the progress of the CBI probe into the chit fund scam should be monitored by the Apex Court . <p> The state Congress president Prasad Harichandan during the day also reacted against Das Burmas continuance in the government . He suspected that the BJD had done a political deal with the BJP central leadership . The CBI has nearly closed its investigation after interrogating chief minister Naveen Patnaiks close aide , Saroj Sahu , in the chit fund scam . This is not my view , but the feeling of the people all over Odisha and other political parties .
