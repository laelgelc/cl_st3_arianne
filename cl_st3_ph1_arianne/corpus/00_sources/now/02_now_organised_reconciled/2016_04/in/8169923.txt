<h> Slow poison in our homes <p> It 's a daily battle for healthy existence that close to five lakh Hyderabadis , shrouded under a thick blanket of poisonous gases , are fighting at the moment . <p> TNN Apr 10 , 2016 , 01.24 AM IST <p> Hyderabad : It 's a daily battle for healthy existence that close to five lakh Hyderabadis , shrouded under a thick blanket of poisonous gases , are fighting at the moment . The lives of residents of Miyapur , Bachupally and Nizampet have become a living hell , with most of them forced to stay indoors or face health complications such as bronchitis , allergic asthma , acute skin diseases and even tuberculosis . This , courtesy a cluster of industrial units in areas such as IDA Bollaram , Gaddi Pocharam , Dundigal and Jeedimetla that have been polluting the air unabashedly since 2010 . <p> While protests , relay hunger strikes and repeated representations to the pollution control boards ( state and Centre ) and the ministry of environment , forest and climate change ( MOEFCC ) have brought @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ residents , while hinting at a nexus between authorities and industry biggies . <p> Fed up , members of the Bachupally-based Hill County Villa Owners Association recently dashed off a letter to Prakash Javadekar , minister of state ( MoEFCC ) , seeking immediate intervention . The letter states : " These industries obviously do not comply to the pollution norms and the controlling officers do not enforce the rules strictly , resulting in this situation ... The H2S content of gases released by these errant industries is found to be 12.3 times ( higher than ) the threshold value ( 8 ug/m3 as against 0.65 ug/m3 ) . " <p> Referring to their previous correspondence with the ministry , residents narrated how despite strict orders of the MoEFCC , the Telangana State Pollution Control Board ( TSPCB ) has failed to submit its Action Taken Report to them . It is awaited since 2014 , the letter , signed by the association president Ratna Gopal , adds . <p> The delay in any kind of action has led to hopelessness among residents . Due to the foul smell , I have to take extra precautions so that my family remains safe . I have knocked on every door to register my complaint , including the chief minister 's helpline , but nothing has changed , " said a distraught Veena K , software engineer and resident of Miyapur . <p> At a distance in Nizampet village , IT firm consultant P Nandkishore is caught in a similar situation . " Since we moved here about eight months ago , we have been forced to live with the foul smell of gases released by the industries . Even on the 12th floor , the smell is so strong that it makes breathing difficult , " he said . <p> In the last six months , residents complain , they have had as many as 37 sleepless nights thanks to the discharge of dangerous gases .
