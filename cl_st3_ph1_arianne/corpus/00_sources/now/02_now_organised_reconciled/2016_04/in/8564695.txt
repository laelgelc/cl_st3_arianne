<h> Clad in gold , girls outshine boys in agri varisty <p> BENGALURU : Women gleamed in gold at the 50th convocation of the University of Agricultural Science in Bengaluru on Saturday . Out of the 49 gold medalists in the post graduate and graduate sections , 28 are women . Amrutha Lakshmi M and Dinta Sebastian are the only two graduates who bagged seven gold medals each . <p> Hailing from an agriculture background in Kannur , Dinta was almost destined to be an agriculturist . " My father grew crops and we have rubber and coconut plantations at home . I have seen his problems in marketing the produce and getting the right prices . I have also seen how most of the families of farmers and agricultural labourers suffer from malnutrition .
