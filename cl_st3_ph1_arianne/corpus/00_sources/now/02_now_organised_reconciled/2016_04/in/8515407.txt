<h> Farming to get climate change cover <p> Taking note of the massive damage to crops that has led to unrest and suicides among farmers , the Haryana government is pondering over options to counter climate change effects on farming . <p> TNN Apr 28 , 2016 , 05.08 AM IST <p> Chandigarh : Taking note of the massive damage to crops that has led to unrest and suicides among farmers , the Haryana government is pondering over options to counter climate change effects on farming . <p> The government has been forced to pay about Rs 2,000 crore as compensation to farmers only in one year for damage to crops . <p> The recent Jat agitation for reservation is also seen as a fallout of the growing crisis in farm sector , apart from other factors like unemployment and increasing aspirations of the youths . <p> Concerned over the situation , the government is looking to various aspects to protect farmers from fresh challenges of climate change to ensure food security .
