And so it is with us today . It is the worst of times because our earth has experienced the three hottest months ever and is on course to experience the hottest year in a century . Global warming is no longer a controversy and climate change has become very real . And yet , it is also the best of times , because in spite of major differences in approach , and after years of negotiation , 196 nations have come together to agree on a landmark climate change agreement that will safeguard the future of the planet . Rarely have so many nations agreed so strongly on any subject . <p> The agreement has not come easily .
