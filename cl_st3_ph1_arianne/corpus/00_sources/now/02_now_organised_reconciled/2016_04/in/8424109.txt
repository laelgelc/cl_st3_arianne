Madhya Pradesh government has collected samples of nine products of Ramdev 's company during a three-day campaign in Guna district to check food product quality . <p> " We have taken samples of food items like cow ghee , biscuits , atta noodles from four firms . The samples were collected to check quality under Food Safety and Standards Act of India ( FSSAI ) , " said additional district magistrate Niaz Ahmed Khan in Guna .
