An internal inquiry by Bank of Baroda ( BoB ) , has found that at least 70 customers from various parts of Uttar Pradesh were duped in March . So far , 373 fraudulent transactions totalling up to about Rs 10.67 lakh have been detected in what could be the biggest cyber heist in the country . <p> A day after TOI highlighted the cyber heist in its April 6 edition , BoB discontinued international usage of Visa debit cards issued before December 2013 which did not have EVM chips . Having apparently infiltrated the bank 's network , hackers started carrying out transactions through debit cards of BoB customers last month . The transactions were between Rs 5,000 and Rs 5,100 and multiple transactions of the same value were made . In all the cases , online transactions have been facilitated through Tenpay , a Chinese payment gateway . <p> As one time passwords were not generated or needed for such fraudulent transactions , it is suspected that items were bought from non-Indian e-commerce sites .
