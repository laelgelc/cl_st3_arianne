<h> GCoE-N 's fate to be known next month <p> Nagpur : The fate of much-awaited Government College of Engineering ( GCoE ) in Nagpur would be known by April end or May first week . A team from All India Council for Technical Education ( AICTE ) visited the institution on Friday . <p> It was the second team from the apex technical education body that visited the college building at Khapri . The first team that had visited the college last month had pointed out a host of deficiencies due to apathy of Maharashtra 's Higher and Technical Education Department . <p> The new team comprising Prof Vitthal Bandal from College of Engineering in Pune ( CoEP ) , Venugopal KR , principal of Bangalore-based University Visvesvaraya College of Engineering ( UVCE ) , and architect Arun Kumar Verma , inspected the college in the morning hours . They then interacted with joint director of technical education in Nagpur , Gulab Thakre , and GCoE coordinator Yogesh Chandrana at former 's office on the premises of Government Polytechnic . <p> Prior to the AICTE team 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ made some arrangements like setting up a library with books and terminals with internet connection at GCoE . Even facilities like landline phone and proper connections for water , power and sewage were completed , before the impending visit . <p> " We have given the AICTE team an undertaking on the behalf of Maharashtra government that everything will be in place before June 15 . We convinced them that being a government-run institution , we are under compulsion to strictly follow all rules and regulations . <p> " We are not here for profit making but for providing education to poor students . We told them that 300 of them would suffer loss if the college is not started from forthcoming academic session . They were also apprised of the fact that there is no GCoE in Nagpur University for last four years , after earlier one in Chandrapur came under jurisdiction of Gondwana University , " Thakre told TOI . <p> Thakre said the faculty would be taken from other government-run college in state on ad hoc basis till the appointment of permanent teachers .
