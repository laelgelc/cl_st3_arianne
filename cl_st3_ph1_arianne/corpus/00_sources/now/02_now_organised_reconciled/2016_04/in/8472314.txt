<p> Air Castles society in Hinjewadi is not listed on the environment clearance website , making it an illegal residential premise <p> Residents of 416 apartments at the Air Castles housing society in Marunji , Hinjewadi , got the shock of their life when they found that they have been staying in an illegally constructed premises since 2011 . Apparently , the project developer has not obtained the mandatory environment clearance before doling out flats . For the same reason , the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation ( PCMC ) has denied water connectivity to the society for quite some time . <p> The startling fact came to light when residents lodged a complaint against the burning of seven-eight trees . " We had doubt on the Rama Group 's builder , who intended to destroy the trees to construct the phase II , without residents ' approval . So , we decided to take the legal action . In this process , an online search led us to the environment clearance website where we found that the society was not given clearance , " said Gaurav Malhotra , a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that is spread across more than 20,000 sq metres of land , which the developers need to take from the State level Expert Appraisal Committee ( SEAC ) that works under the Ministry of Environment , Forests and Climate Change ( MoEF ) . Without it , a builder can not give possession to the buyers . The Air Castles project is spread on an area of 49,750 sq metres . <p> The website clearly mentions that matter of environmental clearance of the Air Castles project came for discussion thrice and was refused by sending it to the MoEF for action against the builder . On April 21 , residents lodged a complaint and received an acknowledgement from the ministry . <p> The Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority ( PMRDA ) , which came into existence in 2015 , is responsible for giving out construction permissions for Marunji . The permissions , before 2015 , were granted by the town planning department of Pune collector . " We will not be able to give details of records at collector office as they are yet to hand it over to the PMRDA @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ To this claim , an official from the town planning department retorted , " All records will be handed over to the PMRDA . It will take us time to find out all the details . You need to file an RTI in such matters . " <p> Developer Rama Punjabi said that they had started the project by acquiring all the permissions . " Of course , I have the environment clearance certificate , " she claimed . <p> Residents , who have been asked by the developers to approach them with complaints , seem to be in no mood to oblige . They have straight away filed four consumer cases against the group . <p> Resident Jitin Vyas , said , " We have no idea whether they have obtained the clearance . We have filed four consumer cases and one writ petition against the builder for not providing amenities and poor construction .
