<h> Importance of reconstruction of quaternary climate highlighted <p> PANAJI : CSIR-National Institute of Oceanography , Dona Paula alongwith Ministry of Earth Sciences ( MoES ) and ' Past Global Changes ' , is holding a three-day national conference on ' Quaternary Climate : Recent Findings and Future Challenges ' . It will conclude on April 30 . <p> Prof Bhalla and Prof Talat stressed on climate changes in the Quaternary period on Glaciation , sea level changes and human civilisation . Earlier Dr Naqvi welcomed the gathering . He mentioned the importance of reconstruction of quaternary climate , which is an important area of research to understand the anthropogenic influence on earth 's climate . The conference included lectures by eminent scientists providing an in-depth review of the status of quaternary climate reconstruction . Also there was a poster session to provide insight into the recent understanding of the quaternary climate and evaluation of proxies to reconstruct past climates . A special session was also dedicated to the role of climatic changes in shaping ancient civilisations . Prof Bhalla also released an abstract volume containing all the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ lecture programme , Dr Rajiv Nigam was felicitated at the hands of Prof Bhalla , for his dedicated services rendered to the study of paleoceanography at CSIR-NIO . <p> Dr N Khare , Scientist F , MoES and the co-convener of the conference stressed on the objective of the conference and Dr Rajeev Saraswat , senior scientist , CSIR NIO proposed the vote of thanks . <p> The conference has been attended by various luminaries like Prof Som Nath Gupta and Prof M Singh . <p> The conference is basically designed to attract young students to take up climate change research as their career and it received overwhelming response from the students across the country . Many posters on diversified topics of quaternary climate change added further importance to this conference . It is expected to get a meaningful outcome after the three-days ' deliberations which may help in shaping future climate change research .
