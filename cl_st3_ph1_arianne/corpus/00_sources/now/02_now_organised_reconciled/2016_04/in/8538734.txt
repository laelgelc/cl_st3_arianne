<h> Asthma cases on the rise in Bengaluru <p> Pulmonologists are seeing a spike in the number of asthma patients this season , which they attribute to the intense heat . <p> TNN Apr 29 , 2016 , 07.38 AM IST <p> Bengaluru : Pulmonologists are seeing a spike in the number of asthma patients this season , which they attribute to the intense heat . <p> " In previous years , we barely used to see 2-3 asthma patients a day during summer as the allergy is mostly triggered during the rainy season and winter . The figure is hovering at 10-15 this year . This is because of extreme weather and the ecological imbalance , " said Dr HB Chandrasekhar , pulmonologist , Bhagwan Mahaveer Jain Hospital . <p> Dr H Paramesh , paediatric pulmonologist and director at Sirona Centre for Health Promotions , said : " Climate change is bad for asthma patients . Increasing levels of carbon dioxide in the air and ozone can affect them severely .
