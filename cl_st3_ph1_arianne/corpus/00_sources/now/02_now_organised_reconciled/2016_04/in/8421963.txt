<h> At last , state appoints special exec magistrates and officers <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 03.32 AM IST <p> Nagpur : The much-awaited nominations for the honorary posts of special executive magistrates and officers have finally been announced by the state government , with first list of 2,706 released in April first week . <p> Essentially political in nature , these posts are seen as a reward for loyal or rather influential workers of the ruling political parties , to give them prestige in public life . But , at the same time , governments in the past have been wary of going through this exercise as it invariably ends up creating disgruntlement among those missing the bus . It 's no wonder the BJP-Sena saffron government took more than 500 days since coming to power to finally clear the nominations . <p> The reluctance was more pronounced in the earlier government led by the Congress , which kept pending for years the issue of appointing special executives as well as chiefs of state-run corporations and boards . Those from the BJP-Sena eyeing these posts @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ same path , but had to wait almost for a year-and-a-half . <p> The general administration department dispatched the first list of Nagpur to the district collector on April 5 . Sources say another list having around 2,000 names would be released shortly . Not surprisingly , guardian minister Chandrashekhar Bawankule 's Kamptee constituency gets the lions share in the list , with 350 appointments , followed closely by chief minister 's Nagpur South-West with 250 . <p> According to reliable sources , West Nagpur gets 150 , South Nagpur constituency 260 , East Nagpur 170 , North Nagpur 125 , Nagpur Central 100 , Katol 140 , Hingna 125 , Umrer 200 , Ramtek 103 and Saoner 150 . Of the over 2,700 names cleared in the first list , barely 150 are women , it is learnt . <p> The release of this list has further intensified the keen tussle for more meatier posts in the state-run corporations and boards .
