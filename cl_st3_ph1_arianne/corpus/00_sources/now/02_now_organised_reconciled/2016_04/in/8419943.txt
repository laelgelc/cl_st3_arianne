<h> Delhi boys prep for farewell season with fancy beauty treatments <p> Riya Sharma <p> From mani-pedis to facials and waxing -- the guys are scrubbing up for their college farewell , and salons are happy to oblige with special packages <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 01.00 AM IST <p> With the college year about to end , many in the outgoing batch have butterflies in their stomach - and it is not just because they are finally going to step out in the real world , but also because of the pressure to look their best on their farewells . With everyone attending multiple farewells , it is not only the girls who want to look perfect . The boys , who did not care much for their looks or turned up to college in their pyjamas the whole year round , are now putting in extra effort to look good for the farewell photos that will be cherished for a long time .
