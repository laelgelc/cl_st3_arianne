<h> Rajini fans upset with Captain Vijayakanth <p> Tirupur : A group of Rajnikanth fans staged a slogan shouting protest against DMDK leader Vijayakanth at Kangayam condemning him for stating that he would not back out of politics like their hero . <p> The angry Rajnikanth fans , who had gathered at the campaign venue of Vijayakanth at Kangayam near Tirupur much before he arrived , started shouting slogans and hurling abuses at the DMDK leader . <p> " Vijayakanth is dragging Thalaivar ( Rajnikanth ) unnecessarily seeking to gain cheap publicity . He should withdraw his statement and apologise , ' ' said a protesting Rajni fan . The protest was a fallout of Vijayakanth 's statement made at Villivakkam recently . <p> " Vijayakanth is not afraid of anyone . I am good to those who are good . I am not afraid like Rajnikanth , " he said .
