<h> Colleges too had access to leaked chemistry paper <p> CID sleuths investigating the leak of the II PU chemistry exam question paper suspect that the leaked paper reached many colleges in Bengaluru and other districts . <p> TNN Apr 23 , 2016 , 06.47 AM IST <p> BENGALURU : CID sleuths investigating the leak of the II PU chemistry exam question paper suspect that the leaked paper reached many colleges in Bengaluru and other districts . Police have information that these leaked papers reached the colleges a day before the the exam . <p> Recently , CID raided 11 private colleges in Bengaluru , Mangaluru , Tumakuru and Ballari and seized many documents , including internal question papers , notes prepared by faculty and answerscripts of many students . <p> After the April 12 chemistry re-exam , CID questioned students . Based on the information given by them , police conducted the raids . <p> " Many tutorial heads had readymade answer scripts of the chemistry question paper scheduled for March 21 .
