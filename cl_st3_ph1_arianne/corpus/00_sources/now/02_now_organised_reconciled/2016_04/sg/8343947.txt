In a joint statement , the United States and Canada committed to significantly cut methane emissions from existing oil and gas systems by 40-45 percent below 2012 levels by 2025 . Three weeks later , President Barack Obama and President Xi Jinping of China jointly committed to formally join the Paris Agreement as soon as possible this year . <p> Together , the two countries represent 38 percent of global greenhouse gas ( GHG ) emissions according to the UNFCCC , more than half of the 55 percent of global emissions required to bring the Paris Agreement into force . The fact that these top emitting countries are on board sends a strong signal to others to formally join the group .
