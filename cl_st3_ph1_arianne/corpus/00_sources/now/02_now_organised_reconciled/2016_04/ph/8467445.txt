<h> Facing global challenges <p> SEVERAL of the issues we face as a nation have both national and global dimensions . The recent hacking of the Commission on Elections ( Comelec ) data involved personal information of some 55 million registered voters . Global security firm Trend Micro was responsible for bringing to light Comelec 's massive data leak . Foreign hackers allegedly perpetrated the laundering of $81 million from the Central Bank of Bangladesh through several Philippine-based casinos and Chinese nationals . The US Federal Bureau of Investigation ( FBI ) is probing into the biggest cyber heist at Bangladesh 's formal request . <p> Clearly , some national problems can not be solved by a country all by itself .
