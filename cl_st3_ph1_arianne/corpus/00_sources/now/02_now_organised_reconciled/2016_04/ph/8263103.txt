<h> San Fernando named as ' 2016 Tanging Bayani ng Kalikasan ' <h> San Fernando named as ' 2016 Tanging Bayani ng Kalikasan ' <p> CITY OF SAN FERNANDO -- The People Management Association of the Philippines ( PMAP ) Human Resources Management Foundation Incorporated has named this capital city as 2016 Tanging Bayani ng Kalikasan ' among the awardees of this year 's Bayani ng Kalikasan Awards . <p> The city was cited for its " outstanding achievement in the area of the implementation of the Clean Air Act and climate change . " <p> The city was awarded under the Local Government category . The awarding was held last April 13 at the Sofitel Philippine Plaza Hotel in Pasay City . Other recipients are Quezon City and the provinces of Cavite and Rizal . <p> PMAP President Jessie Rebustillo said their organization seeks to recognize individuals , corporations and institutions whose efforts have significantly contributed to climate change mitigation . <p> " The PMAP , with 1,500 corporate members and 25 chapters nationwide , is the only association that promotes professional people management practices at the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ companies are located . " <p> " This is the highest achievement award in the field of environmental management given by our institution " he added . <p> Through the initiative of San Fernando 's Environment and Natural Resources Office , the city has fully implemented various ordinances aimed at protecting and preserving the environment . <p> The city has also gained numerous recognitions for having committed to the implementation of RA 9003 or the Ecological Solid Waste Management and enjoining all 35 barangays to contribute in solving garbage problems . <p> Published in the Sun.Star Pampanga newspaper on April 15 , 2016 . <p> Latest issues of Sun.Star Pampanga also available on your mobile phones , laptops , and tablets .
