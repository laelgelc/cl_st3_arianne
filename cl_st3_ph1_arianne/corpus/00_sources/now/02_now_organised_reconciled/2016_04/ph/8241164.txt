<h> It 's all systems go for Baguio-La Trinidad monorail <p> Baguio City -- The proposed Automated Guideway Transit ( AGT ) system or monorail project in Baguio City and La Trinidad , Benguet is nearing realization after the Regional Development Council-Cordillera ( RDC-CAR ) approved a resolution creating the ad hoc committee for the conduct of a full-blown feasibility study for the P7- billion environment-friendly transport system . <p> Baguio City ( Photo courtesy of Google Maps ) <p> The AGT developed by the Department of Science and Technology ( DOST ) , will connect Benguet 's capital town La Trinidad and this city via Balili river route , which will ease the traffic . <p> The RDC said the project has undergone a re-feasibility study conducted by Traffic and Transport Planners , Inc. , a process required by investors eyeing engagement in such a big-scale infrastructure such as the AGT , with the funding from the DOST-CAR . <p> The train system is a fully automated electric train , to help curb the serious effects of climate change to the environment aside from being a solution @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ adjacent places . <p> The Baguio line with a total length of around 5.2 kilometers will costs at P4 billion while the La Trinidad line which is around 2.1 kilometers long cost P3 billion . <p> The ad hoc committee is composed of representatives from the Cordillera offices of the Department of Tourism , Department of Transportation and Communications , National Economic Development Authority , Department of Environment and Natural Resources , Philippine National Police , Department of Public Works and Highways , DOST , Land Registration Authority , Department of the Interior and Local Government , National Commission on Indigenous Peoples , private sector representatives , provincial government of Benguet , city government of Baguio , municipal government of La Trinidad , Itogon , Sablan , Tublay , Tuba , federation of jeepney and taxi
