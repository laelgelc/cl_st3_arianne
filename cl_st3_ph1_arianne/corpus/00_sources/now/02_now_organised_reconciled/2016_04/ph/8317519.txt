<h> 130 nations signing climate change pact <p> The Philippines is joining some 130 other nations in signing the agreement on climate change reached at the 2015 United Nations ( UN ) Climate Change Conference in Paris , France , last December . The signing ceremony will be held on Friday , April 22 , at the UN headquarters in New York City , presided by UN Secretary General Ban Ki-moon . <p> Over 60 heads of state are attending the ceremony , among them President of France Francois Hollande who led the historic Paris conference that set a goal of limiting global warming to less than 2 degrees Celsius compared to pre-industrial levels . <p> During that Paris conference , 146 nations publicly presented their proposed national climate change contributions . Leading these nations were the world 's top two emitters of greenhouse gasses , the United States and China , which account for 38 percent of total emissions in the world .
