<p> MANILA - The International Maritime Organization 's Marine Environment Protection Committee ( IMO-MEPC ) on Monday endorsed the application of the Philippines to designate the world-renowned Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park and World Heritage Site as a Particular Sensitive Sea Area ( PSSA ) . <p> Permanent Representative to the IMO Gilberto Asuque submitted the application at the plenary meeting of the 69th session of the MEPC held at the IMO Headquarters in London on April 20 . <p> The decision of the Philippines to designate the Tubbataha as a PSSA under IMO Guidelines is aimed to protect the biodiversity and ecological sensitivity of the area from the threats of international shipping . <p> Tubbataha is located in the Sulu Sea , about 80 nautical miles from the island of Palawan , and is a living laboratory for the study of marine ecological processes and climate change adaptation . It lies at the heart of the Coral Triangle , the richest biogeographic region in the world . <p> Tubbataha encompasses an area comprised of a 97,030-hectare " Core Zone " and a 350,000-hectare " Buffer Zone " surrounding @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a UNESCO World Heritage Site , and was also listed in 1999 in the Ramsar List of Wetlands of International Importance . <p> Since 2009 Tubbataha has been designated by the Philippine government as a national marine protected area through Republic Act 10067 . <p> The area is significant in terms of its unique ecological , socio-economic , scientific , and cultural characteristics . Its extensive coral reefs and associated ecosystems are very vulnerable to damage from international shipping activity , in particular physical damage and pollution arising from operational discharges and maritime incidents , as well as the introduction of alien invasive species . <p> The application will now be endorsed to the Maritime Safety Committee ( MSC ) for the consideration and approval of protective measures to be implemented in the area of the TRNP . These IMO-sanctioned measures are designed to further protect the area from the hazards of international shipping .
