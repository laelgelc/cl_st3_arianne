Datu <p> CITY OF SAN FERNANDO , Pampanga , April 23 ( PIA ) -- National Police Commission ( NAPOLCOM ) warned civilian applicants aiming to join the police service on the use of fake height waivers purportedly issued by the agency . <p> This came following the discovery of fake height waivers submitted by some applicants in the Police Regional Office 3 in the ongoing police recruitment . <p> " The discovery was made when we verified the submitted Certificates of Height Waiver where one was allegedly issued and signed by NAPOLCOM Cagayan Valley Regional Director Danilo Pacunana and which was later found and confirmed to be forged and that the dry seal used was not the official dry seal of the NAPOLCOM Region 2 , " NAPOLCOM Regional Director Rodolfo Grande Santos Jr . said . <p> In the initial investigation conducted , applicants revealed they were issued waivers through assistance from relatives whom they send money to facilitate the issuance . <p> " The issuance of height @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ not entail any payment , " Santos stressed . <p> Under the law , any applicant who was found to have submitted spurious document supporting his/her application shall be criminally and administratively charged for perjury .
