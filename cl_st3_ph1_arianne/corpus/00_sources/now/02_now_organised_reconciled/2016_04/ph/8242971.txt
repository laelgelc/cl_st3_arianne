<h> Binay blames Aquino for Kidapawan killings <p> TANGUB CITY , Misamis Occidental -- Opposition United Nationalist Alliance presidential candidate Vice President Jejomar Binay on Wednesday held President Benigno Aquino III and the ruling Liberal Party liable for the death of two farmers in Kidapawan due to their " neglect and lack of compassion . " <p> Binay said the 6,000 farmers from Kidapawan in North Cotabato were compelled to protest and set up a barricade due to poverty and hunger . <p> " They saw it coming . But they did not prepare for the impact of climate change , " Binay said , adding that the Kidapawan incident was a testimony to the country 's real situation that " poverty is still a problem . " <p> Bagong Alyansang Makabayan secretary-general Renato Reyes Jr . said in Manila many of the farmers were deceived by the Philippine National Police into agreeing to lift the barricade on the false promise that they would be accorded a free ride home and food . <p> " They were promised a ride back to their communities and were even given @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to a gym and a school where they were declared under arrest , " Reyes said . <p> The mass arrest , he said , included three pregnant women and six senior citizens and even minors .
