<h> Velez : The thing called ' pagbabago ' <h> Velez : The thing called ' pagbabago ' <p> PAGBABAGO . Pagbag-o . Change . Every election we hear that word from every candidate 's lips who promised heaven and from every person longing for a better life and a genuine government . Thirty years since EDSA , we still search for that thing . <p> This 2016 , after six years of another Aquino in government and all its shortcomings , are we going to see the change coming ? <p> The biggest draw for change has been Rodrigo Duterte , who has been the public 's choice as the alternative to the Aquino administration 's failures and incompetency to fight corruption and poverty , fanned by his call to end corruption and crime in three to six months . <p> The conduct of his campaign has been a game-changer , as pointed out by fellow columnist Andy Uyboco who said his campaign is run more by a machinery comprising of netizens and ordinary folks beating out the traditional machinery of money , image-and spin-makers and traditional @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a possible Duterte presidency , it is mind-boggling why they are picking a Marcos in the vice presidential race . The analysis from Rappler showed that Bongbong Marcos is favored among middle aged voters who saw the Marcos ' era as a time of ' peace ' and ' progress ' . <p> The thing is , people still choose leaders through sentimentalism , as what they did in 2010 with Noynoy riding on a wave of sympathy over the death of his mother Corazon Aquino . Bongbong is riding on that ' solid North ' and sentiments of old folks for a ' quieter ' era . But that also points to Noynoy 's failures in Manila to solve crime , poverty and traffic , as well as problems such as disasters and peace in Mindanao . It 's ironic that the tables are turned between Marcos and Aquino , which shows that people will ride on politicians who can challenge the status quo with words and machinery . <p> There 's no change in the senatorial race where old-timers and come-backing senators are topping the surveys . Congressional representatives in two of the three districts are running unchallenged , Karlo Nograles in District 1 and Mylene Garcia-Albano in District 2 . <p> So what 's the score with change in this scenario ? It 's an uneven inconsistent view of the change we want . People may like Duterte for the promise of change . But he has to contend with a machinery and system that favors the elite that holds influence to the local levels .
