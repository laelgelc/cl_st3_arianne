<h> Parliamentarians urged to ratify Paris Climate accord <h> Parliamentarians urged to ratify Paris Climate accord <p> SENATOR Loren Legarda urged Saturday her fellow parliamentarians from countries that are most vulnerable to climate change impacts to immediately ratify the Paris Agreement . <p> A UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction 's Global Champion for Resilience , Legarda wrote parliamentarians of member countries of the Climate Vulnerable Forum ( CVF ) , encouraging them to sign and ratify the Paris Agreement as soon as possible for its early entry into force . <p> The Philippines is the current chair of the CVF , an international partnership of countries highly vulnerable to climate change . The forum serves as a cooperation platform for participating governments to act together to deal with climate change . <p> " The Paris Agreement creates a unique opportunity to safeguard our nations ' future and that of the entire planet . Entry into force of the treaty is now a key preoccupation for CVF nations , given the disproportionate threats of climate change whose impacts already set back the lives of our people and present @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change committee , said . <p> Legarda noted that CVF members Fiji , Palau , Marshall Islands and Maldives were the first countries to ratify the Paris Agreement . <p> In order to take effect , the senator said 55 countries representing 55 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions must ratify or accede to the agreement . <p> " The early legislative action by the entirety of the CVF membership would send a clear signal to the international community , encouraging highly industrialized economies , major emitting nations and all countries to follow suit , " Legarda said .
