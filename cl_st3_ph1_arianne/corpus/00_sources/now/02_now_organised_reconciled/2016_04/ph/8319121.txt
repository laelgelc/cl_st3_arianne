<h> 3.1 million tourists visit Soccsksargen in 2015 <p> MANILA -- Around 3.1 million tourists visited Soccskargen or Sox ( Region 12 ) in 2015 , or 23 percent higher than the 2.5 million tourists who visited in 2014 , a tourism official said recently . <p> Sox is composed of South Cotabato , Cotabato Province , Cotabato City , Sultan Kudarat , Sarangani , and General Santos . <p> Department of Tourism-Region 12 ( DOT-12 ) director Nelly Dillera said the region targets a 10-percent increase in arrivals by year-end . She said this can be done by developing tourist sites , marketing efforts , and promotions . <p> " People have been going to the same places all the time and they are now looking for new places , " Dillera told reporters in an interview at the sidelines of a press conference . <p> " We also encourage all residents of Region 12 to say something good about the region and share photos on social media , " she added . <p> Of the 3.1 million tourists in 2015 , around 2.2 million were day @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have increased to 53,861 in 2015 compared to the 31,861 in 2014 .
