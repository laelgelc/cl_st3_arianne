<h> More than 170 countries sign Paris climate pact <p> More than 170 countries on Friday gathered at the United Nations to sign the Paris agreement , a milestone in global efforts for climate change governance . <p> The event also set a record for countries inking an international accord in a single day . <p> The active participation in the signing sends a robust message that the global action to deal with climate change will not cease . Signing of the Paris agreement marks a new beginning in international cooperation , the accord is expected to enter into force before 2020 , its target time . <p> The signing here and ratifications in capitals are necessary in ensuring the Paris deal to take effect at an early date , thus representing a major step toward the effective efforts to turn Paris aspirations into action . <p> The active signing also embodies the firm determination of the international community to join hands in safeguarding the shared destiny of humanity for a new sustainable future . <p> The universal agreement is mainly aimed at keeping a global temperature rise @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ efforts to limit the temperature increase even further to 1.5 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels . The 1.5 degree Celsius limit is a significantly safer defense line against the worst impacts of a changing climate . <p> " I urge all countries to move quickly to join the Agreement at the national level so that the Paris Agreement can enter into force as early as possible , " UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon said at a high-level signing ceremony at UN Headquarters in New York . " The window for keeping global temperature rise well below 2 degrees Celsius , let alone 1.5 degrees , is rapidly closing . " <p> The historic Paris accord , an outcome of win-win cooperation in long and arduous multilateral negotiations , has been hailed by a majority of UN member states as a wide international consensus reached in December last year to fairly split responsibilities between developed and developing countries . It for the first time brings all nations into a common cause based on their historic , current and future responsibilities .
