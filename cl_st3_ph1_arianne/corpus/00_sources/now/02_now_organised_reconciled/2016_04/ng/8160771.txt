<h> Covenant University partners firm on clean energy <p> The management of Covenant University , Ota , Ogun State , has signed a partnership with Africa Clean Energy Summit on the provision of a framework for research and innovation in the areas of clean energy and climate change . <p> The research will focus on mitigating the effects of climate change and energy crisis in Africa and globally , according to a statement . <p> The collaboration will also bring decision and policy makers , industry leaders , inventors , experts and media around the world together to explore and deepen understanding of the challenges and opportunities that arise from renewable energy . <p> It will also involve a world-class summit , an international exhibition , technical conferences , business forums and concurrent activities such as the incorporation of the African Climate Solution Centre . <p> The Director-General , ACES , Dr. Victor Fodeke , stated that the organisation was formed in 2013 with the aim of seeing Africa take advantage of climate change through clean and renewable energy . <p> Fodeke said , " One of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ how Africa can benefit from climate change . Climate change is like a coin , one side is crisis , and the other side of the coin is opportunities . Africa failed to see and explore these opportunities which China , India and Brazil have taken advantage of . " <p> The Vice-Chancellor of Covenant University , Prof. Charles Ayo , decried the poor knowledge of solar energy in the country . <p> Ayo said , " Nigerians are actually yearning for solutions to energy challenge , but the technocrats are nowhere to be found . Some individuals took undue advantage of solar energy and the whole project has become abandoned . It has become difficult to convince anyone that solar energy can work in Nigeria .
