<p> LAHORE : UNESCO would help Lahore College for Women University ( LCWU ) develop its premises into a ' green campus ' , a project that would be developed by United Nations Environment Programme ( UNEP ) . <p> UNESCO Regional Bureau for Science in Asia and Pacific Director Shahbaz Khan announced this in a seminar at LCWU on Monday . LCWU Vice Chancellor Uzma Qureshi , faculty members and students were also present on the occasion . <p> Shahbaz Khan said that ' Green University Network ' is a functional network of higher education institutions that incorporate environment , low carbon-climate resilience development strategies and sustainability aspects in their education , training , campus operations and enhanced student engagement . The networks are envisaged to develop capacity to assist universities in mainstreaming the Greening Universities Toolkit into their day-to-day functions through green campus , he added . <p> Vice Chancellor Dr Uzma Qureshi , in her address , said that through this joint venture between university staff and students would be provided with a selection of strategies , tools and resources , gleaned from the literature @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ green , resource-efficient and low carbon campuses . <p> Dr Qureshi said the theme for this year 's Earth Day was ' Trees for Earth ' . " Trees are protectors of the environment and save earth from a range of devastating problems . They purify air by absorbing pollutants , prevent flooding , filter water and provide habitat for biodiversity . Rapid deforestation and change in land use is triggering climate change , disrupted climatic patterns , glacier melting , sedimentation , deterioration of water catchments areas , species migration and extinction , desertification and many other environmental and social problems , " she said . <p> LCWU Environmental Sciences head Dr Arifa Tahir said that environmentalists have some major challenges to fight and overcome . She urged the civil society to join hands with the public sector in its efforts to control environmental pollution and deforestation.
