<h> China , U.S. pledge to ratify Paris climate deal this year <p> China and the United States , the world 's top producers of greenhouse gas emissions , pledged on Friday to formally adopt by the end of the year a Paris deal to slow global warming , raising the prospects of it being enforced much faster than anticipated . <p> The United Nations said 175 states took the first step of signing the deal on Friday , the biggest day one endorsement of a global agreement . Of those , 15 states also formally notified the United Nations that they had ratified the deal . <p> Many countries still need a parliamentary vote to formally approve the agreement , which was reached in December .
