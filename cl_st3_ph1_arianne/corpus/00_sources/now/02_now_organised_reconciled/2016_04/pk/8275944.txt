On rejection of the summary from Prime Minister 's Secretariat , the ministry sent another summary to the Prime Minister 's Secretariat seeking additional charge of the EPA director general 's office for Joint Secretary Aftab Ahmed Manika . The Prime Minister 's Secretariat again rejected the summary . <p> It is pertinent to mention here that the EPA was running without its head since July 2015 .
