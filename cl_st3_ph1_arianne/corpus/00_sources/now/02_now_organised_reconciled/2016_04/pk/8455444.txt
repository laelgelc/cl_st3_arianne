<h> Two ruling MPAs ' rebel ' against erstwhile chum <p> SIALKOTDifferences have cropped up between Pasrur-based two MPAs and Federal Law Minister Zahid Hamid as the formers have started criticising the minister publically , in violation of the party discipline.The issue has become talk of the town in Pasrur city and its outskirts.Both the Pasrur-based PML-N MPAs - Ch Munawar Ali Gill and Rana Liaqat - have also formed their political alliance against the Federal Minister for Law and Climate Change.The ' disgruntled ' lawmakers claimed that another MPA Rana Muhammad Afzal would also join them very soon.According to locals , both the MPAs are strongly criticizing Zahid Hamid publically for " what they called " his alleged lack of interest in resolving the problems being faced by people in his constituency.In fresh phase of the anti-Zahid Hamid move launched by the MPAs , Ch Munawar Ali Gill strongly criticised the policies of the Federal minister while addressing a gathering at the inauguration of developmental projects at Pasrur THQ Civil Hospital on Saturday.Mr Gill charged that Zahid Hamid belongs to the elite class and alleged that he @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the people in his electoral constituency ( NA 114-Pasrur ) . The MPA said that Zahid Hamid has switched loyalty to three political parties over the past 15 years , claiming that Hamid 's one brother-in-law is among the policy-makers of the PTI.Mr Gill also rejected leadership of Zahid Hamid in local politics , saying that only Kh Asif , Sialkot based Federal Minister for Defence , Water and Power , is their leader in local politics now.He also accused the federal minister having been the ' right hand of Pervez Musharraf , ' that he can not be sincere with the PML-N.Addressing the participants , MPA Rana Liaqat Ali also strongly criticized the Federal minister and said he would continue this move till provision of true political leadership to the people of Pasrur.The MPAs confirmed their differences with Zahid Hamid and said that whatever they doing is for the rights of people of Pasrur , which they said have always been neglected by MNA Zahid Hamid.About two months ago , both of the MPAs had also boycotted the speech of the Federal law minister during an inaugural ceremony held @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with their dozens of companions.Interestingly , the district , provincial and central leaderships of the ruling PML-N is still keeping silence over the issue , and appeared unable to take serious notice of the growing political differences in the party ranks.According to local pundits , the differences had surfaced when both the MPAs started criticising the Federal minister before December 5 , 2015 LB elections over nominations of the PML-N candidates.However , the federal law minister is said to have been well-aware of the situation .
