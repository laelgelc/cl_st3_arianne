<h> Half Australia 's Great Barrier Reef coral ' dead or dying ' : scientists <p> Australian scientists said on Wednesday that just seven percent of the Great Barrier Reef , which attracts around A$5 billion ( $3.90 billion ) in tourism every year , has been untouched by mass bleaching that is likely to destroy half the coral . <p> Bleaching of coral occurs where it expels living algae , causing it to calcify as a result of rising sea temperatures . Mildly bleached corals can recover if the temperature drops , otherwise the coral may die . <p> " We 've never seen anything like this scale of bleaching before . In the northern Great Barrier Reef , it 's like 10 cyclones have come ashore all at once , " said Professor Terry Hughes , conveyor of the National Coral Bleaching Taskforce , which conducted aerial surveys of the World Heritage site . <p> " Our estimate at the moment is that close to 50 percent of the coral is already dead or dying , " Hughes told Reuters . <p> The Great Barrier Reef @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ northeast coast and is the world 's largest living ecosystem . <p> Although the impact has been exacerbated by one of the strongest El Nino 's in nearly 20 years , scientist believe climate change is the underlying driver of bleaching . <p> UNESCO 's World Heritage Committee last May stopped short of placing the Great Barrier Reef on an " in danger " list , but the ruling raised long-term concerns about its future . <p> Australia is one of the largest carbon emitters on a per capita basis thanks to its reliance on coal-fired power plants . <p> U.S. President Barack Obama embarrassed host Australia 18 months ago at Brisbane 's G20 Summit by warning of the risk of climate change to the reef . <p> The report will likely place additional pressure on Prime Minister Malcolm Turnbull ahead of likely federal elections on July 2 . <p> Turnbull , who ousted Tony Abbott in a party coup last year , is a carbon-trading advocate who supports progressive climate policies , but has disappointed some with his failure to strengthen the ruling Liberal Party-led coalition 's commitment to addressing @ @ @
