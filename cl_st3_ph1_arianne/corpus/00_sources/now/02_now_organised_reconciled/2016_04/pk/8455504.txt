<h> CDA organises colourful event on earth day <p> Islamabad - The Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) has organised a colourful event at Lake View Park to observe the Earth Day 2016 under the theme ' Tree for the Earth . ' The event was participated by Member Environment CDA Sanaullah Aman , senior officers of the Authority , a large number of students of various educational institutions , representatives of civil society , environmentalists and people from different walks . Comprising of a theme-based painting competition among students of different schools , a poster competition , a debate competition and an awareness walk were organised during the event.Speaking on the occasion , Member Environment said the Earth Day is more than just a celebration of the commitment to the planet ; it sends a collective call for action to change climate change . He said that the purpose of Earth Day is to align all efforts aimed to protect and preserve planet earth for future generations . He said the CDA has also planted olive plants at the green belt along the Kashmir Highway . He said on the special directions of Chairman CDA Maroof Afzal , green belts and median strips , entry and exit points of the city are being decorated with colourful flowers . The debate completion was won by the students of Assas Foundation Schools , poster completion by the students of Imperial School System while students of Roots School System led the painting completion . Member Environment CDA distributed prizes among the position holders .
