<h> Marghazar zoo to be upgraded <p> The Marghazar zoo will be upgraded and developed to make it more attractive for the visitors . <p> The zoo administration said that more animals and birds would be brought in the zoo . He said a dispensary would also be established for the treatment of animals and birds . <p> He said there is n't a proper nutritionist and experts took care of the diet of animal , birds and other species . The visitors also demanded that more attractive animals and birds should be brought in the zoo . A large number of people daily visit the zoo but of shortage of animals and birds in the zoo they are n't entertained enough . <p> It may be mentioned here that Secretary Climate Change , Abu Akif during his recent visit to the zoo , directed the administration to take steps for providing sagacious and conducive environment to animals and birds . <p> The zoo project started in 1978 as a refuge for leopards , spotted deer and gazelle found in the region .
