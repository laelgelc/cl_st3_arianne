<h> Earthday observed at Lake View Park <p> Islamabad -- Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) organized a colourful event at Lake View Park to observe the Earth Day under the theme ' Tree for the Earth ' . Member Environment CDA , Sanaullah Aman , senior officers of the Authority , a large number of students of various educational institutions , representatives of civil society , environmentalists and people from different walks participated in the ceremony . Comprising of a theme-based painting competition among students of different schools , poster competition , debate competition and an awareness walk was organized in the event . Speaking on the occasion , Member Environment said Earth Day is more than just a celebration of our commitment to the planet ; it sends a collective call for action to change climate change . He added that the purpose of Earth Day is to align all efforts aimed to protect and preserve planet earth for our future generations .
