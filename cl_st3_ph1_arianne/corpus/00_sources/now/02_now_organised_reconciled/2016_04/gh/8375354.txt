<h> Gender equality promotes economic productivity-SEND GHANA <p> Mr Siapha Kamara , Chief Executive Officer of SEND GHANA , an advocacy non-governmental organisation has called on Ghanaians to be gender sensitive as families to support one another since such social co-existence could promote positive economic productivity . <p> He said gender equality was a very positive contributor and indicator to healthy families and economic productivity where children could be well catered for by both couples stressing that in homes where roles are evenly distributed , couples lived happily . <p> Mr Kamara who was addressing a gender model family summit at Salaga in East Gonja district on Thursday said Ghanaian families must eschew the tendency of leaving the entire household chores to women and rather assist them for quality living . <p> The summit was organised by SEND GHANA as part of the Gender Model Family project that the NGO was promoting as part of the Food Security through Cooperatives in Northern Ghana ( FOSTERING ) project . <p> FOSTERING project is being funded by Global Affairs of Canada and the Canadian Co-operative Association and implemented by SEND @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with the aim of improving agricultural productivity and economic livelihoods for smallholder farmers . <p> Mr Kamara said when the men and families share family roles without bias towards one gender , the burden on women is reduced to enable them contribute meaningfully to societal and community affairs for positive development . <p> He said the FOSTERING gender model family project is being implemented in the Nanumba North , Kpandai and East Gonja targeting 2,000 beneficiaries stressing that five more districts would soon benefit from the project . <p> He said 42,000 smallholder farmers in the East Gonja , Kpandai , Nanumba South , Nanumba North , Krachi-Nchumuru , Zabzugu , Tatale-Sangule and Chereponi districts were being supported with tools and co-operative skills to sustain food security and climate change resilience . <p> Mr Kamara said through the project , husbands and wives have been equipped with knowledge and skills to live together equitably and to ensure their boys and girls had the same opportunities to develop their potential . <p> Hajia Mary Naboku , District Chief Executive ( DCE ) of Chereponi who chaired the function urged men to stop @ @ @ @ @ performances and unity .
