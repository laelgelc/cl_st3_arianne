<h> As part of events to mark Earth Day , Environmental activists have gathered in Accra to campaign against plans by government to build a coal power plant in the country . <p> According to the group , Ghana Youth Environmental Movement , the construction of the power plant will generate about 5 million tonnes of ash waste ( per annum ) that will lead to " air pollution from poisonous and hazardous gases that are injurious to health , threats to turtle breeding grounds close to the site and the destruction of the natural environment . " <p> However , the founder of GYEM , Gideon Commey disagrees . Speaking at the programme to mark Earth Day in Ghana , he said " clean coal is a myth being spread by the coal industry to keep their businesses afloat . " <p> Some Environmental analysts have over the years argued that coal remains cheaper than renewable energy , mostly because of subsidies for coal . <p> However , the group believes that the 1.5 billion dollar investment in coal should rather be channeled into clean energy sources @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> " Anyone who tells you coal is cheap is deceiving you . It may appear cheap at this time but the amount of money that will be used to clean up its destruction and to cover medical bills of residents around the plant will eventually prove that it is not , " Mr. Commey added . <p> Mr. Commey believes that the way forward is " to highlight the adverse environmental effects of coal power in the media so that it would in turn become a national conversation . Because the ordinary citizen still does not know about this plant and we need them to understand the risks involved so that they can force positive action from politicians . " <p> The Street press conference on Coal Power was on the theme : " Keep it in the ground . No Coal to Ghana . " <p> While the campaigners chanted in Accra , world leaders met in New York at the UN headquarters to sign the Paris Agreement on Climate Change .
