<h> Alliance launches campaign to cut smoke emissions <p> The Global Alliance for Clean Cookstoves ( GACC ) , in collaboration with the Ministries of Energy , Power and Environment , has launched an awareness campaign to sensitise the public on the importance of using improved clean cookstoves . <p> The three- month campaign , which was launched in partnership with the Ghana Alliance for Clean Cookstoves in Accra , includes Women 's Advocacy Campaign , Community Market Activations , and Radio Awareness Campaign . <p> The Campaign dubbed : " Obaatan Boafo " or " Mother 's Helper , " would encourage urban and peri-urban dwellers who depend on biomass and charcoal for cooking to switch to improved cookstoves that burn fuels more efficiently and effectively . <p> The improved stoves could help cut smoke emissions , reduce the amount of money spent on fuel and also protect the environment . <p> Mr Arijit Basu , Regional Director of Market Development , GACC told the Ghana News Agency in and after the campaign so that they could find out the benefits and outputs they made from the initiative . <p> " For instance , we are expecting the sales of the manufacturers to go up with not less than 40 per cent after the campaign to stabilise certain levels , " he said . <p> Mr Basu noted that GACC would therefore continue to work closely with the Ministries to ensure the success of the initiative . <p> He said even though a number of initiatives have been made to sensitise Ghanaians on the importance of clean cookstoves , this initiative is the biggest campaign . <p> The Regional
