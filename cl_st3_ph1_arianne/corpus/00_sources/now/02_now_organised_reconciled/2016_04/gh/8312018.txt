production and policy development of the cashew sector , to increase economic value and enhance private investment . <p> She noted that it is imperative for Africa to put in place consistent and coherent strategy in the value chain by investing in research for the growth of the sector . <p> Ms Weidinge made the call at the weekend in Accra at a consultation of African Public and technical Actors in Cashew on the theme : " Opportunities of the African cashew sector " . <p> The workshop brought together public officials from the ministries related to the cashew sector , public Pan-African actors : African Union Commission and Regional Economic Communities . <p> In attendance are financial institutions delegates and other technical partners , including the African
