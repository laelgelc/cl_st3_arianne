<h> Jamaica preparing to counter cyber security threat , says Holness <p> Prime Minister Andrew Holness says Jamaica has put measures in place to counter the threat of cyber security , which is rapidly developing into a source of concern in the region . <p> " We are now in the process of improving our cyber security capabilities in terms of the acquisition of technology , the training of our people , and the development of ecosystem of security around data and the digital environment , " Holness declared . <p> However , Holness argued that because cybercrime does not pose a physical threat , sometimes governments underestimate it . <p> " Certainly in the region far more work needs to be done on the issue of cyber security , " he said . <p> The prime minister was speaking at the official launch of the Global Tourism Resilience and Crisis Management Centre at the Montego Convention Centre on Wednesday . <p> The centre is to provide solutions to emerging or existing threats to tourism across the Caribbean region and globally . <p> Apart from cybercrime , other threats are climate change and natural disasters , pandemics , terrorism @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " The overall goal of the centre will be to assess , plan for , forecast , mitigate and manage risks related to tourism resilience and crisis management . This will be achieved through research and development ; policy advocacy and communications management ; programme/project design and management ; training and capacity-building , " the prime minister said . <p> The Global Tourism Resilience and Crisis Management Centre , to be housed at the University of the West Indies ( UWI ) , is the brainchild of Tourism Minister Edmund Bartlett .
