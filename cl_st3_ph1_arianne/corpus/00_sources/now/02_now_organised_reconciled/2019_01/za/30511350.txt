The same goes for the high-tensile steel which makes up 15-20% of a 68kg truck tyre . <p> " So , when you start a business such as this , you have to anticipate all the by-products and find markets for all of them . We 've managed to develop markets for different types of rubber crumb across a broad range of industries as well as an export market for the steel which is used by the motor and ship building industries in Korea and Australia , " he said . <p> Zarrebini said the investment in Mathe Group was a perfect fit with the broader group 's environmental standards .
