That 's half of the globe if we exclude China ( off the list ) . Uncertainty is the only sure bet as social scientists , like punters at the horse races anxiously hold their breaths to see if their theories get proven to make them shine , or , in equal random improbability , sink their opinions into oblivion . The best futurists are often historians with a keen eye for patterns -- but even then , unpredictable curved balls can throw them off . What this piece does is provide a sense of the potential impact 1.85bn residents of democracies among the UN-estimated 7.7bn world population will have on our future .
