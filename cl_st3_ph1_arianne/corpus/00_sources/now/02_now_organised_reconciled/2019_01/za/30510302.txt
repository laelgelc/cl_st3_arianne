<h> Nedbank goes fully green , withdraws funding for coal IPPs <p> JOHANNESBURG -- Global finance houses are increasingly turning away from providing capital for coal-fired power stations . It 's a trend that 's been gathering pace and South African banks are no exception . In fact , Nedbank has decided not to plough any more money into new coal fire plants in South Africa . It will be interesting to see how this impacts South Africa 's new energy builds but there 's no doubt that a clean energy future is heading our way . -- Gareth van Zyl <p> By Chris Yelland and Roger Lilley* <p> Nedbank lived up to its slogan as the " green " bank in a decision to withdraw its funding of two proposed new coal-fired power stations in South Africa , Thabametsi and Khanyisa . <p> This decision fits with the banking group 's commitment to " green " funding , responsible lending and supporting sustainability initiatives .
