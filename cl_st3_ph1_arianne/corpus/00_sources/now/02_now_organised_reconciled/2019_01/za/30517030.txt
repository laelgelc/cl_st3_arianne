<h> More babies likely to be born with heart defects due to climate change : study <p> 01 February 2019 - 06:48 By Odwa Mjo <p> Experts have warned that babies born between 2025 and 2035 in the US will be vulnerable to congenital heart defects due to climate change . Image : 123RF/katrinaelena <p> A large number of infants in the US will likely be born with congenital heart defects between 2025 and 2035 due to rising temperatures as a result of climate change . <p> This is according to a study in the Journal of the American Heart Association , which projects changes in maternal heat exposure during early pregnancy , mainly between three to eight weeks after conception . <p> The study links the changes in maternal heat exposure caused by rising temperatures to possible congenital heart defects in the future . <p> CNN reported that climate change could lead to 7,000 more cases of congenital heart conditions in the US over a period of 11 years . <p> According to the researchers , the impact of the heart defects will be determined by the season , more likely in summer and spring , as well @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cautions pregnant women about extreme exposure to heat , especially in the early stages of pregnancy , as the increase in exposure to heat would increase the need for mothers to medically be prepared for their babies to have congenital heart defects .
