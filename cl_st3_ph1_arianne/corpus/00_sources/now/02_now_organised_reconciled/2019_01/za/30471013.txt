It 's recruited six young leaders , all millennials , as co-chairs to join Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella in shaping the discussion . The age of the co-chairs notwithstanding , the overall demography of the event still skews older . It 's the third year that such concerns topped the WEF 's annual global risk survey , followed by the related challenges of failed climate change mitigation and natural catastrophes . Technological risks are also keeping business and world leaders up at night , namely cyber attacks and data fraud or theft . <p> Climate change is particularly on the minds of some of the new leaders ( and leaders in waiting ) attending Davos this year .
