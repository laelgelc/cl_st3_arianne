<h> Botswana 's agricultural industry suffers in the face of climate change <h> Botswana 's agricultural industry suffers in the face of climate change <h> Botswana is facing a food shortage , with the current ploughing season experiencing dry spells coupled with above-normal rainfall in some parts of the country . <p> Speaking at a workshop aimed at encouraging farmers to adopt measures to minimise the impact of climate change , the Deputy Director in the Department of Crop Production , Barutlwa Thebenela , revealed that Botswana is struggling to meet its food security need of 300,000 metric tonnes of grain . <p> The 2017-2018 ploughing season recorded a yield of less than 71,000 tonnes , which translates to 24 percent of the required grain . <p> Thebenela said the result is that Botswana needs to import 71 percent of grains to ensure food security . <p> He advised farmers to adopt innovative ideas in the challenging era of climate change . <p> Thebenela added that because of impacts of climate change , the government has provided support to promote the agricultural sector and improve food production in the country .
