<h> Wild coffee species threatened by climate change and deforestation <p> The extinction of wild species could jeopardize the viability of commercial coffee varieties . <p> Most of the world 's wild coffee species have a high chance of going extinct in the next several decades due to more frequent and lengthy droughts , loss of forests and the spread of deadly pests , according to a study1 published on 16 January in Science Advances . <p> A farmer shows an example of forest coffee production in coffee nursery in Ethiopia . <p> The findings signal a potential threat to the multibillion-dollar coffee industry that 's dominated by two varieties -- arabica ( Coffea arabica ) and robusta ( Coffea canephora ) beans .
