<h> The school without a female teacher <h> Monday January 7 2019 <p> Muheza . Deep in the East Usambaras , at a mountain edge that overlooks Korogwe , a rural primary school is pondering over a challenge that might affect attendance of girls at school . <p> Kazita Primary School in Muheza has over 400 students , with half of them being girls , but it has no female teacher since the end of 2017 , a problem that is described by both the school management and committee as having the potential to fuel truancy among girls . <p> The last female teacher - identified only as Zainab - according to school head teacher Augustino Semweta , was forced to seek a transfer due to constant asthma attacks . <p> " Since then we have been requesting authorities to allocate female teachers without any success , " said Mr Semweta . <p> The headteacher said the lack of female teachers was seriously affecting guardianship over girl pupils whom he said were unable to confide in male teachers concerning their problems . There are a total of five teachers at the school , all of them male . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is quite pronounced among girls , especially those who have reached puberty . This is because the school has only four toilet holes , which do not afford privacy to girls , forcing them to go home during their monthly periods , thereby missing classes . <p> The problem was eased dramatically after the construction of a new toilet building with six rooms ( three each for boys and girls ) , a urinal for boys , a toilet for pupils with speacil needs and a water tank . <p> According to Mr Semweta , the new toilet was built by a Climate Change adaptation project : ' The Integrated Approaches to Climate Change Adaptation in East Usambaras , ' through its Hygiene and Sanitation intervention . <p> Generally , the project aims to demonstrate effective and efficient strategies that support poor rural households in Tanzania to adapt to the negative impacts of climate change and to alleviate poverty . <p> Its specific objectives include supporting eight communities living near high biodiversity forests in the East Usambara Mountains to increase and diversify incomes . <p> The project that is being @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with several partners since 2013 and sanitation -- the project has managed to build three toilets in three primary schools out of six primary schools in the project area serving a total number of 1,341 pupils ( 677 boys and 664 girls ) . <p> The headteacher said that truancy among girls has declined from 20 per cent to only 5 per cent due to the construction of the new toilets . <p> He said , however , the problem of lack of a female school teacher could threaten the recent breakthrough in raising school attendance for girls . <p> The role of the teacher is obvious . Alongside families and communities , teachers form the front-line of a child 's development and in many cases , they spend more time with children than anyone else , acting not only as educators but also as conduits of information and change . <p> To solve the matter , the school management handed over the issue of lack of a matron to the school committee , which decided to appoint a female member , Rhoda Hiza , to act as the school @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ chairman of the school committee , Mr Adam Bakari , said lack of female teachers seriously affected the care of girls at the school . <p> " They can not reveal their problems to male teachers . When they miss someone of their gender to confide in they stay away from school until such a time when the problem has ceased , " he said . <p> He said that they have reported to the respective authorities , including the Education Department and councilors , but there is still no action . <p> Mrs Hiza said that it was sad that the schoolgirls had nowhere to go and ask for help when problems related to their bodies came up . She appealed to the government to allocate female teachers to the school to help in bringing up schoolgirls . <p> A Standard Six girl from the school , Zuwena Yusuf , said that it was important to solve such problems to ensure that girls attended school without missing .
