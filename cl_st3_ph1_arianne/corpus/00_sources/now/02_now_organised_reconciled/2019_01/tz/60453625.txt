<h> Foundation calls for environmental education strategy for Tanzania <p> THE Morogoro based Sustainable Holistic Development Foundation ( SUHODE ) has underscored the need for provision of environmental conservation education for Tanzanians from primary to college levels . <p> ENVIRONMENTAL CONSERVATION <p> According to the foundation , community participation in environment conservation will greatly save the country from increasing environment destruction which is associated with development and human activities . <p> The foundation 's director , Frank Luvanda made the remarks recently in Morogoro Region when briefing journalists on the decisions of the organisation to start offering environment conservation trainings to the community . <p> According to Luvanda , the citizens are also encouraged to use modern charcoal stoves . He said the move is aimed at controlling tree felling as the stoves use a small amount of charcoal . <p> Luvanda said that while Africa is currently losing around 3 million hectares of forest per year , data from the Tanzania 's National Carbon Monitoring Centre , submitted to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) , indicate that the country is losing an estimated 469,000 hectares of forest area each year ? -- @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> Data from the Tanzanian Vice President 's Office , 62 per cent of Tanzania 's 89 million hectares of land is degraded .
