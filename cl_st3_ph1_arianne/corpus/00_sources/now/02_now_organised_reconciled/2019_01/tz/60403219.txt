<h> WWF renewable energy project to light Mtwara villages <h> Thursday January 3 2019 <h> In Summary <p> A renewable energy project , currently being implemented at Miyuyu Village , in Newala District by the World Wide Fund for Nature ( WWF Tanzania ) in partnership with a Mtwara-based Civil Society Organization ( CSO ) is bringing hopes of reversing the fortunes of Mbwinji Catchment Area and protect it from drying up , contributing to climate change mitigation .
