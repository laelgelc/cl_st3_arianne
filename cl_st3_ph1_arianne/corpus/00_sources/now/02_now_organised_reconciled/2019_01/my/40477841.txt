<p> Primary Industries Minister Teresa Kok Suh Sim has reportedly said that the prime minister will be writing a letter to the French president and Norwegian prime minister to protest the European Union ( EU ) parliament 's decision to ban palm oil from transport fuels . <p> According to Bernama , Kok told reporters that the government was " working on it now " . <p> She was speaking at the Reach and Remind , Friends of the Industry Seminar 2019 and Dialogue in Kuala Lumpur today . <p> Kok reportedly said that the ministry would send a copy of the letters to the Malaysian media once they have been completed . <p> In 2017 , the European Parliament voted to ban the use of palm oil in all European biofuels by 2020 , citing environmental concerns . <p> Subsequently , EU negotiators agreed to phase out the use of palm oil in transport fuels by 2030 . <p> In December last year , the Norwegian parliament voted to make Norway the first country in the world to ban biofuels that contained
