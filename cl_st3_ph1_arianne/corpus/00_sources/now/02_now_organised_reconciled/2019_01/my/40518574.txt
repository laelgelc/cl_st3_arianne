<h> Australian Minister for women to quit politics at election <p> MELBOURNE : Australia 's Minister for Women Kelly O'Dwyer said today she will quit politics for personal reasons at this year 's election , adding to the list of female politicians to leave the ruling party , some citing disunity , bullying and intimidation . <p> Women in Australia make half of the population , but they make up less than a quarter of federal politicians in the Liberal party , the senior partner in the governing coalition . <p> O'Dwyer , who is also minister for jobs and industrial relations , said her reasons for not recontesting her seat at the next national election , which is due in May , were personal . <p> " I no longer want to consistently miss out on seeing my children when I wake up in the morning and when I go to bed at night , " O'Dwyer , flanked by Prime Minister Scott Morrison , told reporters . Morrison said he supported O'Dwyer 's decision . <p> In Nov , backbench MP Julia Banks said she was quitting the Liberal party immediately to sit as an independent over @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ energy and climate change . <p> She referred to last year 's toppling of former prime minister Malcolm Turnbull , which angered voters , as " the dark days of August " and said the party was riven by personal ambition . <p> Ann Sudmalis said in Sept she would not contest the next election , citing a culture of " bullying , intimidation " in the Liberal party in her electorate of New South Wales state . -- Reuters
