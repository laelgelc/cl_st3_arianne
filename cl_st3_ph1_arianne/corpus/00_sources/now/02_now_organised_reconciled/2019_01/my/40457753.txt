<p> Kota Kinabalu : The State Environment Protection Department ( EPD ) with its partners conducted 145 programmes with an outreach of 14,962 participants throughout Sabah in two years . <p> Assistant Tourism , Culture and Environment Minister Assaffal Alian said the programmes were part of the initiatives under the Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) signed between the Ministry and the Conference of Earth Environment from Akita , Japan ( CEEA ) , in 2017 . <p> " This is a major achievement and I hope that more programmes can be conducted so that the people will be more aware that even their smallest actions are important to reduce global warming , " he said in a statement , Wednesday . <p> " The MoU which is about to end by March , this year , is a great platform for Sabah to educate the people on the importance of taking the necessary measures to curb the rise of global heat . <p> " We need to become a sustainable society by adhering to the 17 Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) adopted by the world leaders in 2015 . <p> " I hope CEEA will be able @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the awareness activities to capture a wider spectrum of the community .
