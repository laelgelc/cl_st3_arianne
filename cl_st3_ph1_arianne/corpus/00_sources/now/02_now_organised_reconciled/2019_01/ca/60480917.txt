<p> This week marks the second anniversary of Donald Trump 's accession to the presidency of the United States . It is certainly not an occasion for celebration , but it may be one for reflection on the past and the future . <p> The first thing to be noted is that Trump and his administration have presented an image of chaos to the country and to the world . The hinges on the doors of the White House must be squeaking from overuse . The turnover in senior personnel has been unprecedented . In just two years , Trump has gone through three chiefs of staff , three national security advisers , three official spokespersons , three attorneys general , two secretaries of state , two secretaries of defence and any number of legal counsels and economic advisers . This has all served to deprive Trump of what were often referred to as the " grown-ups " in the room ( Gens . McMaster , Mattis and Kelly , Secretary Tillerson and Ambassador Hailey ) . By questioning the patriotism of his political adversaries and by describing journalists as enemies of the people , he has set the country on a course of prolonged political warfare . This is evident in the current shutdown of large segments of the American government , which makes the United States look like a dysfunctional Third World country to observers beyond its borders . Trump 's irrational obsession with building a wall along the Mexican border , and lying profusely to justify the need for it , have become a litmus test of the health of American democracy . It is a sad picture indeed . <p> Trump 's foreign policy has represented repeated attacks on international agreements , institutions and alliances . Very early on in his term in office , he withdrew the United States from the negotiations aimed at creating the Trans-Pacific Partnership . This was a very shortsighted move . He has not only repeatedly questioned the validity of the science underpinning action to control climate change , he also withdrew the United States from the Paris agreement on climate change , which had been put in place by the international community at large . He appointed a well-known climate change denier as head of the Environmental Protection Agency , while at the same time slashing the EPA 's budget . On his watch , regulations intended to protect the environment have been lowered and the coal industry has been encouraged to ratchet up production . All of this has served to make the United States an environmental pariah on the world stage and to deepen political divisions within the United States .
