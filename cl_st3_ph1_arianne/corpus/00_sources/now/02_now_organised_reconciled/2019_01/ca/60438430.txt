<h> Study shows black bears need a variety of salmon species to be healthy <p> Black bears need access to different species of salmon rather than huge numbers of a single variety in order to be healthy , a new study by Canadian researchers indicates . <p> Lead author Christina Service said if bears have access to a " portfolio of different salmon species " then the animals have access to more food for a greater part of the year . <p> " It is the equivalent of humans going to an all-you-can-eat buffet for just a couple of days versus having one good meal a day for many months , " said the PhD candidate from the University of Victoria , adding that different species of the fish have different run timings and use different habitat . <p> When they have access to the fish for a larger part of the year , they end up eating more salmon overall and are in better health , she said . <p> The team of researchers used chemical techniques on hair samples from 379 black bears and 122
