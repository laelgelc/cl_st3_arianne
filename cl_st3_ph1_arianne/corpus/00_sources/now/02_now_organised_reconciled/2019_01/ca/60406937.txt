A skeptic would agree , but for different reasons . <p> The Globe and Mail devoted over two full pages to an article by Dan Gardner titled " Why do n't we care about climate change ? " The reason is apparently not selfishness or ignorance of an undeniable fact . Rather , it is " psychological distance " -- the decades it will take for climate-induced disaster to materialize , that it will affect poorer people in other countries more severely than Canadians , and that its worst impacts are uncertain .
