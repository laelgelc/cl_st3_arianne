<p> Rome , Jan 29 ( IANS/AKI ) Italian Prime Minister Giuseppe Conte has opened a new centre here set up by the government in partnership with the UN to fight climate change and boost sustainable development in Africa . <p> " The African continent represents a pillar of Italy 's foreign policy and this government considers it a priority , " Conte said on Monday at a ribbon-cutting ceremony for the Africa Centre for Climate and Sustainable Development . <p> The new centre is located near the headquarters of the Food and Agriculture Organisation ( FAO ) in central Rome . It will help develop and scale-up innovative solutions for sustainable development , implement the goals of the UN 's 2030 Agenda and the landmark 2015 Paris Climate Agreement to end reliance on fossil fuels and cap global warming . <p> A major focus on boosting the agricultural sectors and promoting rural development in Africa was needed to ensure a better future for the continent 's youth who , can be the drivers of development , said FAO Director-General Jose Graziano da Silva at the ceremony . <p> Several ministers from African countries attended the inauguration .
