It 's concerning that on one hand he talks about truth and reconciliation , he talks about implementing UNDRIP the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous People and has supported Bill C-262 , which is about implementation -- and then he 's using forceful , militarized RCMP to remove people and arrest them at Unist'ot'en and Wet'suwet'en territory . That 's what the Indigenous land defenders are talking about when they say we need to protect the land and the water . That water is sacred , water is life .
