<h> Rules changed for upcoming federal election <p> Hopefully , everyone enjoyed their holiday . I wish each of you the best in 2019 . This year will be especially exciting , since it is an election year federally . <p> It 's important to note that the rules of the upcoming election have been substantially changed by the passing of Bill C76 by the Liberals . This bill will allow the use of voter cards as identification for voting . Based on the simple fact that over 500,000 cards were sent in error during the last election , constituents have expressed concerns to me that the potential for vote fraud may increase this election . There is also concern about the amount of ineligible voters that have reportedly already received registration forms from Election Canada , when they are not eligible to vote under current electoral law . <p> In addition , there will be an increased opportunity for third-party organizations to spend up to $1.5 million advertising each to support their party of choice . Even worse , Bill C76 does not eliminate the cash-for-access fundraisers that have been questioned by the Ethics commissioner and other government @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and Prime Minister . Despite such concerns , C76 received royal assent in mid-December before Parliament rose and we will now see just how much it impacts the 2019 federal vote . <p> At the same time , the Liberals are giving the media $595 million in this election year . Oddly enough , a Liberal appointed committee will decide who gets the money -- which again raises obvious questions of bias . Some Canadian political pundits have even said this looks like an attempt to buy favor with the media in an election year . If our own local media can benefit from needed funding , it would be understandable -- but the concern I hear is that local media will be overlooked for larger media consortiums that may have more influence with the Government . <p> It 's important to note that as the calendar turned to 2019 , the federal carbon tax was put into place -- driving up costs on home heating , gasoline , and many other items like groceries .
