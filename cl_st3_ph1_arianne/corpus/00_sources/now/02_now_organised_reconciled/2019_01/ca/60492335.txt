<h> AP-NORC poll : President Trump 's marks on the economy slip <p> WASHINGTON -- The public continues to give President Donald Trump his best ratings on the economy , but a new poll finds the share who approve of how he 's handling that issue has slipped since last fall . <p> And as his promise of a U.S.-Mexico border wall brought a stalemate over funding to Washington , Trump also sees tepid support over his handling of immigration and border security . <p> The new poll from The Associated Press-NORC Center for Public Affairs Research shows the 35-day partial government shutdown took a toll on Trump 's overall performance ratings . But current assessments put Trump decidedly in the red for the first time since February 2018 . <p> Currently , 44 per cent of Americans approve of Trump on the economy , while a 55 per cent majority disapproves . In October , and for much of last year , the president saw nearly 50 per cent approval ratings on his handling of the economy . <p> Democrats are staunchly negative in their ratings of the president overall and on most issues , but about 2 in 10 had approved of the president on the economy last year .
