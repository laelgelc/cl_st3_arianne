<p> BERLIN -- In a pioneering move , a government-appointed panel on Saturday recommended that Germany stop burning coal to generate electricity by 2038 at the latest , as part of efforts to curb climate change . <p> The so-called Coal Commission reached agreement in the early hours of Saturday , following months of wrangling that were closely watched by other coal-dependent countries . <p> " We made it , " Ronald Pofalla , the head of the commission , told reporters in Berlin . " This is a historic effort . " <p> Germany gets more than a third of its electricity from burning coal , generating large amounts of greenhouse gases that contribute to global warming . <p> The 28-member panel , representing mining regions , utility companies , scientists and environmentalists , suggests a review in 2032 could bring forward the deadline to 2035 . <p> The plan foresees billions in federal funding to help affected regions cope with the economic impact , and to shield industry and consumers from higher electricity prices .
