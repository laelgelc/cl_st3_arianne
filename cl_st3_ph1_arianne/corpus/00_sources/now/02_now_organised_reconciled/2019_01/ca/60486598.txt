<p> Stepping out onto the sidewalk from Robarts Library , it 's obvious that winter is in full swing . The air has gone cold , turning that pleasant fall nip into a winter bite . And although it would seem that the freeze is inescapable , not all temperatures are dropping . <p> Despite the frigid weather , global warming is still in effect . Since 1975 , average global temperatures have been increasing at a rate of roughly 0.15 -- 0.20 degrees Celsius per decade , prompting consequences such as rising sea levels , extreme weather , disappearing Arctic ice , and severe droughts and floods . <p> Another major unseen consequence of this climb lies deep beneath the tundra soil . The frozen expanse known as permafrost is beginning to thaw . <p> According to Dr. William Gough , a climate change researcher at UTSC , the thawing process occurs annually .
