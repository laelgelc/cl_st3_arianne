<h> The Drilldown : OPEC slashes output to boost price of crude <p> OPEC significantly reduced its crude-oil production in December ahead of an official plan by the cartel to hold back output as part of an effort to rein in global oversupply and bolster crude prices , the Wall Street Journal reports . <p> OPEC , along with Russia and its production allies , agreed in early December to cut output by 1.2 million barrels per day in the first half of 2019 . But before that reduction , OPEC 's production fell by 751,000 barrels per day , led mostly by Saudi Arabia , which reduced its output by 468,000 barrels per day . <p> These pre-emptive cuts have helped boost oil prices by around 20 per cent since the end of December .
