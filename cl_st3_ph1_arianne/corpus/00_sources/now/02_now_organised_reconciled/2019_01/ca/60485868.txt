<h> Trump 's rollbacks for fossil fuel industries carry steep cost <p> U.S. President Donald Trump speaks in the Rose Garden of the White House , in Washington , D.C. , on Jan. 25 , 2019 . <p> The Associated Press <p> As the Trump administration rolls back environmental and safety rules for the energy sector , government projections show billions of dollars in savings reaped by companies will come at a steep cost : more premature deaths and illnesses from air pollution , a jump in climate-warming emissions and more severe derailments of trains carrying explosive fuels . <p> The Associated Press analyzed 11 major rules targeted for repeal or relaxation under Trump , using the administration 's own estimates to tally how its actions would boost businesses and harm society . <p> The AP identified up to $11.6-billion in potential future savings for companies that extract , burn and transport fossil fuels .
