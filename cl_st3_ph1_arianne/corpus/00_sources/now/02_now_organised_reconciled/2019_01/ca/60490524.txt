<h> Kelowna <h> Cash for fire mitigation <p> The City of Kelowna hopes to take advantage of a provincial program to help with ongoing efforts to mitigate wildfire risks . <p> Urban forestry technician Tara Bergson told council Monday the city qualifies for the maximum amount of $100,000 based on a demonstrated fire risk . <p> The money will be budgeted based on recommendations contained with the Community Wildfire Protection Plan , adopted last year . <p> About half the funds will be used for fuel mitigation across the eastern portion of Knox Mountain Park . <p> Bergson says that area was chosen because of its location and potential for fire , citing a blaze in that area of the park in July of last year . <p> " Staff are in the process of developing a fuel treatment prescription to identify areas that should be prioritized for treatment this year , which will be Phase 1 , and remaining areas we will seek to treat in 2020 , " she said . <p> The rest of the funds will be used to fund other recommendations within the CWPP . <p> These include developing a fire smart landscaping standard @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ identify opportunities for including fire smart principles . <p> Bergson says a wildfire fuel mitigation maintenance schedule will also be developed for previously treated areas . <p> " Initial fuel treatments have been completed on most natural areas over the past 20 years or so .
