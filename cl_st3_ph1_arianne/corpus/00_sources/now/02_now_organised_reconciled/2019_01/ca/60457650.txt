<h> WIN 2019 : Poets Are Essential To Change The World <p> The first poetry reading session of WIN : Writers International Network to welcome 2019 and to pray for universal peace , friendship and love in a strife-torn world was held on Sunday January 13 in Vancouver . WIN acknowledges that peace , within and without , is of paramount importance . So is health -- individual and of the community . <p> We are faced with the threats of wars , terrorism , intolerance , poverty , illiteracy , hunger , climate change and bigotry . Once Prime Minister Justin Trudeau said " we need poets to change the world . " Therefore , to fight these curses , we the poets have to act as one global family with common goals and shared vision for the protection , preservation and sustenance of humanity , nature and the earth . In an interconnected and interdependent world , the reality of oneness is to be recognized as a crucial factor , despite our socio-cultural diversities . <p> In an atmosphere of blatant distortions and manipulations of facts and truth , the serious writing plays a crucial part . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ counter-narratives . The questioning of the status quo -- at the cost of individual safety and survival is an essential part of arts . <p> WIN recognizes that " the future is fluid " therefore we attempt to bring writers to a place , common ground , where past , present and future exists simultaneously and to promote poetry reading and recital as a key to networking to embrace and understand fellow poets , writers , artists , community leaders and society at large . <p> The mission is not the self-promotion but promotion of writing community . <p> Ashok Bhargava is a poet and founder of WIN -- Writers International Network and can be contacted at bhargava2000@yahoo.com
