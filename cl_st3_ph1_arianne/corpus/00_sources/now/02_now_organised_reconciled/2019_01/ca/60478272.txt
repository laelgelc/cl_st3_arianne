FTQ and Fondaction . <p> The seed-stage fund is part of an effort hatched by one of Canada 's premier cleantech venture-capital firms , Cycle Capital , earlier this decade , to help young cleantech companies evolve into compelling opportunities for risk capital investors . Cycle first helped create the Ecofuel accelerator in 2015 to host and support young firms , which was followed by the launch of the Ecofuel fund in 2017 to fund promising early-stage firms across the spectrum of clean technology , ranging from firms that help reduce energy consumption to plastic recyclers . <p> The fund , co-managed by industry veterans Richard Cloutier and Johanne S ? vigny , provides upwards of $75,000 per investment in companies from Eastern Canada across the clean-technology spectrum . It has backed 12 firms to date , including seven that came out of the Ecofuel accelerator .
