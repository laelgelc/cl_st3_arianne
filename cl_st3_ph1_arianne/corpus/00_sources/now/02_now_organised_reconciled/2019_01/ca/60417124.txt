<h> The Drilldown : Cod in Gulf of St. Lawrence on path to extinction <p> Atlantic cod could go extinct in the southern Gulf of St. Lawrence by 2050 due to predation by grey seals , according to a study in the Canadian Journal of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences . <p> The study reveals an unsustainable natural mortality rate of 50 per cent for adult cod ( over five years old ) near Cape Breton Island , where the fish gather year after year , making them easy prey for grey seals , according to data collected off nearby St. Paul Island . <p> " In these projections , if we assume natural mortality were to stay where it is now and there was no fishing , cod would be gone by ( the ) middle of the century , " said Doug Swain , a Department of Fisheries researcher who co-authored the study . <p> A National Energy Board ( NEB ) report shows that Canada 's dependence on American refineries -- not just a dearth of pipelines -- are to blame for Alberta 's oil-patch woes . <p> The report released Dec. 27 shows that over three-quarters @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ oil with a high sulphur content .
