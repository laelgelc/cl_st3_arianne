In the past , meat , dairy and other lobbies were heavily involved in the process . Their vested self-interests prompted public suspicion and tainted the credibility of the document which should rise above commercial agendas . <p> The new guide puts the focus on protein options rather than defining meat as the primary source of protein . It does n't rule meat and dairy out , but it stresses the need to eat more plant-based foods . <p> It focuses on whole foods and directs its advice at the whole person with common-sense suggestions to cook at home and invite friends and family around to celebrate the meal and the company , rather than just wolfing down food to fill a hole and stave off hunger . <p> It wants us to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and to read labels carefully for salt and sugar content . <p> So this update was necessary and long overdue because , let 's face it , what they were doing before was n't really working if you consider the ballooning rates of obesity and related disease like Type 2 diabetes in Canadian adults and children . <p> Most of us are not nutritionists or scientists and it 's sometimes hard to know who to trust when you choose the foods in your diet .
