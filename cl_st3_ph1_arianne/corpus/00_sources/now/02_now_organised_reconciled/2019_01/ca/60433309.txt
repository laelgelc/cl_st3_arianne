It 's bad enough that the fires were several times larger with climate change 's influence , but a 20-fold increase in the likelihood of the 2017 fires is truly startling . <p> This connection is also seen in many other events , including the 2018 heat wave across northern Europe , where climate change increased its likelihood in the range of two- to six-fold .
