<p> Staff at the Sorrento &amp; Area Community Health Centre are n't usually surprised by the annual holiday uptick in charitable donations to their B.C. clinic . But in December of 2017 , however , one particular gift caused some surprise . <p> A $5,300 cheque from an anonymous donor arrived in an envelope sent by BenefAction , one of hundreds of public foundations across Canada that use donor-advised funds , or DAFs , to make charitable donations on behalf of families , individuals and organizations . <p> DAFs have flourished in Canada since first appearing in 1952 as a less laborious and less costly alternative to private foundations .
