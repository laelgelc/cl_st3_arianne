' Shields says this reflects the collective 's mandate of being of nonjudgmental and collaborative . <p> " I think that is resonates because we are just regular women . We are just trying our best , we do n't always get it perfect every time , we do n't try to say that we do , and I think that everybody 's kind of in the same boat as us . They just want to make some changes , " said Shields . <p> " We 're all kind of learning together , we 're sharing ideas together . " <p> The collective is running a 30-day challenge of collective change this month , with the hopes of getting participants to give up plastics and reduce emissions . <p> " A lot of our challenges on a daily basis are relatively small , " said McCallum .
