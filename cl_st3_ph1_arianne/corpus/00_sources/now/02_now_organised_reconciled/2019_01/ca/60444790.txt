But mostly , the public expressed their sadness along with their appreciation for Abel 's life and work . <p> Abel had after all enjoyed a 50-year career as a professional media hoaxer , hoodwinking journalists and the public on issues as diverse as the indecency of naked animals , the art and craft of panhandling and the mass fainting of television audiences . <p> In their tribute , the Yes Men , an influential media activist group , refer to Abel " as the undisputed master of modern media pranks @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as a political commentator engaged in mischief-making to reveal uncomfortable truths about the society in which he lived . <p> The Yes Men take great care to explain that Abel was n't in the business of creating " fake news .
