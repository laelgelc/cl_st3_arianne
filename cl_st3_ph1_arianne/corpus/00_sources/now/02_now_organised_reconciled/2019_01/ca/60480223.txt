The majority of new state spending on public education over the past decade has gone toward charter schools that currently serve about 8 per cent of the student population . <p> The bill from Sen. Mimi Stewart of Albuquerque devotes about $315 million in annual spending to at-risk students , extensions of annual class time and a boost in minimum teacher salaries .
