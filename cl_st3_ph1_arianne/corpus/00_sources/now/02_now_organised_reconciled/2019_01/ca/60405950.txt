The latest report by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change warns that time may be running out for effective action on climate change . Our list of globallythreatened wildlife has grown to over 26,500 species , and many wildlife populations are declining . In Canada , iconic wildlife like caribou are in trouble , and the Atlantic whitefish , perhaps Canada 's most endangered species , may be doomed to extinction . <p> Our current environmental issues -- from climate change to biodiversity loss -- are all the result of many collective impacts .
