The new year offers hints of optimism but also presents challenges for Atlantic Canadians . -- Telegram file photo <p> A new year offers the promise that things will be better . <p> And that optimistic resolve is beckoning to the 2.3 million residents of Atlantic Canada . <p> Nowhere more so than in Newfoundland and Labrador . <p> The Atlantic Provinces Economic Council is projecting that N.L. will see the top economic growth in the region this year , only second behind British Columbia , as it recovers from the painful economic contractions of 2018 . Higher oil production from the Hebron field and other major projects should result in growth , projected to be at more than 2.3 per cent . <p> There 's some good news for the other three Atlantic provinces , too , with real growth expected to continue , albeit at a slightly slower pace than in 2018 . <p> To further this end , Atlantic premiers should press on with their efforts to bring down barriers to interprovincial trade .
