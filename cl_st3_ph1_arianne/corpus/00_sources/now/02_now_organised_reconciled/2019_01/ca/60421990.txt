<h> Hundreds march in support of Wet'suwet'en anti-pipeline camp <p> Hundreds marched through downtown Vancouver on Tuesday in solidarity with the Wet'suwet'en anti-pipeline camp in northern BC . <p> The rally , one of many across Canada , comes a day after the Unist'to'ten camp was raided by the RCMP under a supreme court injunction for blocking the path of a 670-kilometre LNG Coastal GasLink pipeline . <p> Protestors marched from the provincial courthouse to Victory Square , chanting support for Indigenous sovereignty and decrying the pipeline 's construction . <p> " I 'm here because I 'm on unceded territory , and as someone who benefits from that everyday I have a responsibility when I see colonialism affecting people I care about , " said second-year UBC political science student Hannah Stanley . <p> Stanley was one of many UBC First Nations and Indigenous Studies students who took part in the march .
