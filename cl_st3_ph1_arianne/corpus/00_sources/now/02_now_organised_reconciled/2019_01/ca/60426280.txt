<h> Green deal should include vegetarianism <p> It is encouraging that new , young elected leaders , along with the Sunrise movement in the U.S. , are spearheading the new green plan to counteract the unscientific and illogical policies of the current U.S. government . Let us hope that when historians review the era of the reality-star president , they will trace the start of new movements like this , which might thrive in future . <p> I suggest that one strand of the Green New Deal should include the education of consumers and producers to move toward vegetarian and pescatarian diets . <p> Apart from the significant impact on climate change , such a move adds benefits to people 's health . This has been shown to be true in a large body of research . Like energy efficiency , this would be good for the climate and lead to new jobs and vibrant communities . <p> It would also help poorer people , as the basic cost of food would be much lower .
