<h> This is how Germany hopes to quit using coal <p> BERLIN -- Germany wants to stop using coal , a major source of the carbon emissions that drive climate change . But finding the least disruptive way to get there has been a challenge . <p> A big question is not only when the last mines and power plants will close down , but how quitting coal can be done without generating drawn-out protests or harming the German economy . <p> A government-appointed panel of experts is poised to offer recommendations .
