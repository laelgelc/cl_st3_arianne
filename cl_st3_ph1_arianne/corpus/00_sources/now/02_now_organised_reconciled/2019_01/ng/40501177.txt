<h> Buhari seeks more commitment on shrinking Lake Chad <p> Abuja -- President Muhammadu Buhari has called for more commitment from the international community on redirecting water to the Lake Chad . <p> The president made the call when he received Letters of Credence from the High Commissioner of Canada to Nigeria , Philip Baker , at the Presidential Villa , Abuja , on Monday . <p> According to him , the tragedy of the shrinking Lake Chad will continue to fuel more illegal migrations , banditry and provide willing hands for terrorism since majority of the people have lost their means of livelihood . <p> He said : " In 1920 's , an academic rightly predicted that except there 's a redirection of water to Lake Chad , it will dry up . <p> " Now whenever I go for any global meeting or visit a country , I will always draw the world 's attention to the adverse effect of climate change on the lake , and the resulting negative effects .
