<p> Several years ago when the story of the shrinking of the Lake Chad Basin first became public not much attention was paid to its implication on Nigeria 's national life but decades down the line , it is obvious that its implications are very grave . <p> Presently , Nigeria is experiencing adverse climate conditions with negative impacts on the welfare of millions of people . Persistent droughts and flooding , off season rains and dry spells have sent growing seasons out of orbit , on a country dependent on a rain fed agriculture . Alarm bells are ringing with the continuous shrinking of the Lake Chad Basin , the River Niger among other water bodies in the country . The result is fewer water supplies for use in agriculture , hydro power generation and other users . <p> The agricultural sector contributes some percentage of the Nigerian Gross National Product and majority of the rural populace are employed in this sector .
