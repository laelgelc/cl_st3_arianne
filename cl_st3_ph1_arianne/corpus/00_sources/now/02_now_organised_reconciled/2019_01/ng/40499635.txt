<p> The University of Ilorin has resettled the Fulani herdsmen living on the University land in a new settlement outside the institution . According to the University Bulletin , on Monday , the institution is committed to conflict resolution and peaceful co-existence between the Fulani herdsmen and the institution . It noted that the action taken was to make good the University 's pledge to resettle the herders . It added that the University had commenced the building of a modern primary school and sinking of a borehole at the new Fulani settlement near Okunle Village , Ilorin South Local Government Area of Kwara . It disclosed that the two projects commenced on Wednesday January 9 . The publication quoted the Vice-Chancellor of the University , Prof. Sulyman Abdulkareem , as saying " the process of relocating the Fulani herdsmen from the University land was initiated by one of his predecessors .
