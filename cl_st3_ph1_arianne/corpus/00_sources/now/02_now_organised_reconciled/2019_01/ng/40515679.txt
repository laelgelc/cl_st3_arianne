<h> FRIN reiterates commitment to more research for better production <p> Dr Adeshola Adepoju , the Executive Director , Forestry Research Institute of Nigeria ( FRIN ) , Ibadan has reiterated the commitment of the institute to intensify research on mandate trees for better production in 2019 . <p> Adepoju , who said this in an interview with the News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) in Ibadan on Friday , however , stressed for the commitment would be facilitated with adequate government funding and support of the private sector . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> " If we are adequately supported and funded , there will be expansion of herbal gene bank and development of more herbal drugs for treatment of common tropical ailments , through biomedicinal research , " he said . <p> He said FRIN would intensify research activities through multiplication of endangered and almost extinct tree species of economic importance , through modern plant breeding and genetics technology . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The executive director said the institute intended to mass produce tree species through the resuscitated three lines of seedling production : seed line , vegetative line and biotechnological line . <p> " Further research will @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ properties which will match the establishment of selected tree species in various localities . <p> " More plants will be collected from Forest Herbarium ( FHI ) to add to the over 150,000 plants specimen , to retain its status as the largest herbarium in West Africa and to continue to play the role of National Herbarium . <p> " FRIN will continue to develop innovative process technology for effective waste management ; more research focus will be on environmental management , climate change monitoring , mitigation and adaptation .
