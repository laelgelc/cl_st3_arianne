<p> The World Bank is stepping up its commitment to climate finance globally , to the tune of $200bn . <p> Kristalina Georgieva the chief executive of the World Bank disclosed this as a panelist at the 2019 Davos World Economic Forum ( WEF ) . <p> The panel which discussed " Global Economy in Transition " was moderated by the Managing Director of the International Monetary Fund Ms. Christine Lagarde . <p> According to Georgieva the investments will be placed in mitigations like battery storage , wind energy , low carbon mobility , rail transportation will be put on the same footing as investment in adaptation . <p> The World Bank boss further stated that the Bank will cover investments in resilient climate cities and coastal areas with mangroves . <p> Giving insight on the climate she said " Climate change is not only happening but it is happening faster than we thought it would . Standford University calculated what it means if temperature should continue to go up , it goes up to 2.5degree , it shaves off 15% of global GDP , it goes up to 3 degree we lose 25% of global GDP " . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ working on encouraging green global economy investment : <p> Eliminating harmful subsidies e.g energy , agriculture : In this area the World Bank has been able to so far shrink it globally from $600bn to now $300bn . <p> Recognizing a shift to the new climate economy : There is a commission on the new climate economy that came up with a very attractive pathway , which will see $26 billion created in renewable energy , new mobility , low carbon mobility , low carbon buildings and 65 million more jobs as a result <p> Helping countries and communities that have done very little to contribute to climate change but are already living with its consequences adapt : Areas like Niger , Chad , Small Island states , Coastal regions in West Africa are part of the plan to drive a sustainable environment . <p> The World Bank CEO was of the view that it was morally right and absolutely essential to scale up sustainability in the globe economy , and that there must be an increase in action on adaptation . <p> On the partnership with the private sector @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Georgieva said many business leaders are stepping up and are committed to climate action . <p> From her viewpoint many companies have realized the importance of climate action for their profitability . She believes that Central Banks can help from the monetary policy side by relentlessly pursuing the appropriate pricing of carbon emission . <p> At the moment there are 70 jurisdictions , countries , and regions that have embraced carbon pricing . <p> World Bank Carbon Pricing Dashboard <p> Source : World Bank <p> She noted that by doing so they will give wind to the sail of business leaders ready to transform towards green economic enterprise .
