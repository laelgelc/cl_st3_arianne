<h> Nigeria 's REDD+ strategy document gets validation <p> The World Bank and Forest Carbon Partnership Facility are working on an initiative to help promote Nigeria 's REDD+ agenda . <p> REDD+ stands for countries ' efforts to reduce emissions from deforestation and forest degradation , and foster conservation , sustainable management of forests , and enhancement of forest carbon stocks . <p> According to environmentalists , deforestation and forest degradation are the second leading cause of global warming , responsible for about 15 per cent of global greenhouse gas emissions , which makes the loss and depletion of forests a major issue for climate change . <p> At a recent workshop in Abuja , stakeholders gathered to discuss and validate a draft report on Nigeria 's REDD+ strategy prepared by Gotosearch.com and the University of the West England , the consortium engaged to carry out the assignment . <p> The National Coordinator , Nigeria REDD+ programme , Dr Moses Ama , said the document would help the county in engaging with the international community for the purposes of bringing benefits to the efforts in emissions reduction , as well as the country 's sustainable forest management approach . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and we do n't capture the elements and the options that we intend to use in engaging , then we are taking a wrong step , " he added . <p> A former Director of Forestry in the Federal Ministry of Environment , Philip Bankole , said the document contained familiar issues on the country 's forest reserves . <p> He said , " It is a good development that the REDD+ programme has got to this stage . It started some years ago and , despite a change of leadership , it is still going very strong because we have competent hands in place . <p> " Whatever comes out here today is to our own benefit . It is a national service . This is a policy document and thus it has a lot of bearing on what happens to our forest and government policies in the future .
