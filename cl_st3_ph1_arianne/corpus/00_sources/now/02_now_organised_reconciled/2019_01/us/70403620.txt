<p> It is said that artists are never fully appreciated until they die . The same goes for snails , apparently . <p> For roughly a decade , the land snail species Achatinella apexfulva , which used to be plentiful on the Hawaiian island of Oahu , was believed to be down to a single survivor . His name was George , and he lived his last days alone in a terrarium in Kailua , Hawaii , alongside an ample supply of fungi ( a food his ancestors liked to scrape off leaves in the wild ) . <p> But on Jan. 1 , George died , according to Hawaii 's Department of Land and Natural Resources . He lived to about age 14 -- a good , long life for a snail of his kind , experts say .
