According to one study , commuting is one of the least pleasant things we do . But it 's not just an annoying time waster -- there 's a case that it 's a public health issue . <p> According to analysis by the Texas A&amp;M Transportation Institute , the average American commuter spends 42 hours per year stuck in rush-hour traffic . In the Los Angeles area , the figure is nearly twice that , equivalent to more than three days . A 2015 Los Angeles Times poll found that among residents of that city , traffic concerns exceed those pertaining to personal safety , finances or housing costs . <p> The total cost of traffic associated with lost time and wasted fuel exceeds $100 billion per year . As time slips away , idling vehicles add pollution , which has environmental and health consequences , including contributions to climate change .
