<h> PG&amp;E has barely enough cash on hand for 1 month of expenses <p> By Irina Ivanova <p> Updated on : January 16 , 2019 / 10:29 AM / MoneyWatch <p> Billion-dollar natural disasters on the rise <p> If Pacific Gas &amp; Electric formally files for bankruptcy protection , as it is has promised to do , it will limp into court with just about a month 's worth of cash on hand , according to a securities filing . As of last Friday , the nation 's largest utility had $1.5 billion in cash and equivalents on hand , the filing said . <p> Running the company for the past year has cost $39 million per day -- costs that cover everything from the production of electricity and gas to land leases to employee salaries . PG&amp;E has enough in its coffers to handle 38 days of those expenses . <p> But the costs it faces are far from regular . It has been implicated in $17 billion of property insurance claims and predicts it could be liable for as much as $30 billion once all the losses are factored in , it said in the filing . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ claims had been filed for the 2018 Camp Fire and $7 billion from the 2017 wildfires , according to the California Department of Insurance . But $17 billion does n't capture its total liability , the company said . The filing said what 's left out is " total potential liability for insured property losses and do not include any uninsured or underinsured property losses or any non-property losses , punitive damages , fines , penalties or damages for claims related to the 2017 and 2018 Northern California wildfires that have not manifested yet . " <p> It also does n't include any punitive damages that a court might award if it finds that PG&amp;E was negligent in relation to the fires . <p> Potential economic impact of climate change on businesses <p> Unlike other states , California holds utilities liable for wildfire costs if their equipment was involved in causing the conflagration , without requiring a demonstration of negligence , said Ralph Cavanagh , co-director of the climate and clean energy program at the Natural Resources Defense Council . That would buy the company time , and it could result in lower payouts to claimants . <p> The utility had hoped for a reprieve from newly elected Gov .
