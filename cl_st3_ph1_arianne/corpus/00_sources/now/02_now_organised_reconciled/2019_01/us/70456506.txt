<h> Stojic : BWL is helping to clean up the region 's energy <p> As a publicly owned utility , the Lansing Board of Water &amp; Light ( BWL ) has always been committed to promoting the economic and environmental health of the greater Lansing community . A few years ago , these may have been considered conflicting goals , however , with advances in renewable energy technology , highly flexible and efficient natural gas generating plants and careful planning , both goals can be achieved while maintaining a high degree of electric reliability . In fact , along with our comprehensive energy efficiency program , this will allow the BWL to cease coal generation in Lansing and reduce greenhouse gas emissions 80 percent by the end of 2025 . <p> To help meet this goal , the BWL is planning to acquire 30 percent clean energy by 2020 and 40 percent by 2030 . To achieve this goal , the BWL is planning to expand its investment in solar and wind energy , ramp-up its energy efficiency programming and implement new technologies that help customers manage their energy needs . <p> The BWL has a tradition of being @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the BWL adopted the state 's first renewable energy program , two years before Michigan mandated a renewable standard for utilities . In 2009 , the BWL constructed Michigan 's largest solar array of its time , the Cedar Street array . Today , that array is less than one percent of the generating capacity of BWL 's newest solar array , the Delta Solar Park . The state 's largest tracking solar array , Delta Solar panels track the sun through the course of the day , significantly increasing daily generation . <p> As a publicly owned utility , we listen to our customers , many of whom have asked for additional ways to participate in renewable energy options . BWL customers participate in a community solar program by leasing panels from a dedicated solar farm in East Lansing and receive a credit on their electric bill for energy produced from the array . Customers can also qualify for incentives by placing panels on their home or business . <p> Much of the impetus for BWL 's goals came from recommendations by the BWL 's Citizen Advisory Committee @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ local citizens , and our connection to our customers has been instrumental in keeping the BWL a leader among Michigan electric utilities to ensure a cleaner and healthier future . The BWL will begin its next Integrated Resource Plan ( IRP ) later this year , which will provide guidance on the type and schedule of future renewable programs . <p> However , there are challenges to growing the BWL 's and Michigan 's renewable energy program in future years because of growing opposition to utility scale renewable projects in Michigan and throughout the country . The need to protect local development decisions while expanding renewable energy to combat climate change will require thoughtful planning and compromise . <p> I have spent my career in the energy industry , both as a regulator and at the BWL , analyzing industry trends and conducting long-term planning . As I look to my retirement this year , I 'm proud of the path that the Board of Water &amp; Light is on , and I want the customers we serve to know how hard we work to reflect their voice .
