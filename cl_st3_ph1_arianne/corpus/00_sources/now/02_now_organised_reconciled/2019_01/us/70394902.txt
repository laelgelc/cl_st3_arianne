<h> There is a small chance of winter storminess this weekend in the Washington region <p> Nearly eight weeks have elapsed since snow was last seen in Washington , but this weekend may finally bring another chance . <p> Some computer models suggest a storm will develop Friday into Saturday in the South before streaking into the Mid-Atlantic on Saturday night . <p> If the storm materializes , it would most likely affect the Washington region between late Saturday and Sunday morning -- although some shifts in this timing are possible . <p> How significantly the storm affects our region will depend on its track . Some models still suggest it will pass too far to our south and east to have much effect . But others indicate it has the potential to produce substantial amounts of precipitation . <p> Because a large area of arctic high pressure over southeast Canada is predicted to feed cold air into the storm , most , if not all , precipitation is likely to fall as snow and/or ice . That said , it is way too early to project how much and where . <p> If the storm takes a track @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ re-forming off the Mid-Atlantic coast ) , we would probably see more of an ice storm .
