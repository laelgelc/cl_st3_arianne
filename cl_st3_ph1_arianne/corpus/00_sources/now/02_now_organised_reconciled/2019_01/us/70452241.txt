<p> " The Arctic is currently warming two to three times faster than the rest of the globe , so naturally , glaciers and ice caps are going to react faster , " said Simon Pendleton , a doctoral researcher in University of Colorado Boulder 's Institute of Arctic and Alpine Research ( INSTAAR ) . <p> " Unlike biology , which has spent the past three billion years developing schemes to avoid being impacted by climate change , glaciers have no strategy for survival , " said Gifford Miller , senior author of the research and a professor of geological sciences at CU Boulder . " They 're well behaved , responding directly to summer temperature . If summers warm , they immediately recede ; if summers cool , they advance . This makes them one of the most reliable proxies for changes in summer temperature .
