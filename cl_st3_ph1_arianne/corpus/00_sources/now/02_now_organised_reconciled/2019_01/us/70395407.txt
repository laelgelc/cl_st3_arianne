<h> Ashland assesses citizen prioirites ahead of biennial budget <p> The City of Ashland is holding a listening session Monday from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. in Carpenter Hall at 15 S. Pioneer St. in Ashland . <p> The listening session comes ahead of the city crafting their biennial budget , and aims to gather public input on where to allocate resources . <p> The city has yet to reveal an exact figure for the upcoming budget , but the 2017 to 2019 biennial budget amounted to about $285 million . <p> Ashland City Manager Kelly Madding says much of the budget is tied up in non-negotiable spending , like funding to run the police and the fire departments . <p> However , she did say that there is always some extra room for spending on issues the community sees as priorities , and she , along with the city council , are interested to hear from residents .
