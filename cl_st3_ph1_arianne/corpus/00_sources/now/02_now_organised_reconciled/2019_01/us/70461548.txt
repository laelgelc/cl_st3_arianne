<h> U of W presentation tackles climate change effects on winter <p> Dr. Nora Casson , associate professor and Chancellor 's Research Chair at the University of Winnipeg will be making a presentation entitled Warmer , Shorter , Wilder : How Changing Winters Affect Ecosystems and Communities on Wednesday , from 12:30-1:20 pm in the Power Corporation Atrium at Richardson College for the Environment and Science , 599 Portage Avenue.Chris Procaylo / Chris Procaylo/Winnipeg Sun <p> While it is hard to think about climate change and global warming when dealing with wind chill values in excess of -50C , a researcher is hoping she can convince Winnipeggers that the affects are already being felt , even here in " Winterpeg " and the communities and ecosystems in other cold-weather areas across North America . <p> " Climate change is a defining issue of our time , " said Dr. Nora Casson , associate professor and Chancellor 's Research Chair at the University of Winnipeg .
