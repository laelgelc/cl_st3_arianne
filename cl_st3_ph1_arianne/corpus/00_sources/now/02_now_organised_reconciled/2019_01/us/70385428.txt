Ironically , although they may not be aware of it , by engaging in this egalitarianism of fools , those who decry universal solutions are obstructing access for those most in need . <p> Means tests create a fertile ground for the politics of middle-class resentment . Those just over the boundaries of means testing often feel as if they are unfairly paying over the odds to prop up those only a little behind them . This decreases political support for services , and increases support for more onerous and punitive means testing to ensure only the most " deserving " get any help at all . This increase in bureaucratic burden swallows up any efficiency gains we may get from better targeting , and often counterintuitively means that the most vulnerable are even more locked out by the wall of paperwork and eligibility testing . <p> Would making trains cheaper benefit the wealthy ?
