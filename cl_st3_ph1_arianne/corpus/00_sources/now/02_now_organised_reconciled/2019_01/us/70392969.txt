You 'd imagine European retailers are ruing the day America 's consumerist splurge , Black Friday , leaped across the Atlantic . <p> It distorts sales and profits before Christmas , and leads to an expectation among consumers that things should be on sale before Christmas as much as after , which is now the norm . <p> Interestingly , one retailer that does not discount things before Christmas , Next , reported a growth in sales of 1.5 per cent in November and December . <h> Existential crisis <p> Stories that can be catalogued under the theme " the decline of the high street " range from depressed British towns @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ serious financial issues and much-loved shops going to the wall . <p> This theme is to consumerism what climate change is to the environment : an existential crisis that we 've been told about for so long that it 's hard to grasp , until the results start to appear right in front of you . <p> The most shuddering of those results was the most unexpected .
