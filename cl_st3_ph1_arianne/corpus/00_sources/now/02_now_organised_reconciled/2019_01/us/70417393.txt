<p> California 's largest power company intends to file for bankruptcy as it faces tens of billions of dollars in potential liability after massive wildfires devastated parts of the state over the past two years , according to a filing with the Securities and Exchange Commission . <p> Pacific Gas and Electric said Monday that declaring insolvency is " ultimately the only viable option to restore PG&amp;E 's financial stability to fund ongoing operations and provide safe service to customers . " <p> The California wildfires , which have killed dozens of people and destroyed thousands of homes , have led to a surge in insurance claims . PG&amp;E estimates that it could be held liable for more than $30 billion , according to the SEC filing , not including potential punitive damages , fines or damages tied to future claims . The company 's wildfire insurance for 2018 was $1.4 billion . <p> The PG&amp;E bankruptcy promises to be more complex and political than most bankruptcies , pitting fire victims , ratepayers , bankers , insurance companies and renewable-energy providers against one another .
