A newly awarded AGU grant is slated to support plans to display the image ( in which cooler colors indicate lower elevations and warmer ones indicate higher ) outside the Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum this spring to give policy makers and the public a chance to " walk on Mars " and become inspired about planetary science .
