<p> Fresh off one of the deadliest -- and costliest -- fire seasons in California history , officials with the Santa Barbara County Fire Department addressed a group of about 100 Mission Hills and Vandenberg Village residents Thursday night to stress the importance of developing a Community Wildfire Protection Plan . <p> The town hall meeting took place in the cafeteria at the Los Berros Visual and Performing Arts Academy in Mission Hills . County Fire officials attempted to reassure residents that protecting the Lompoc Valley communities was a priority for the department , and they also went over some of the challenges facing the area as they pushed for the creation of the community-specific plan , which could bring in grant funding that could go toward fire prevention and suppression projects . <p> " The fires that we 're seeing across the state are unprecedented , " said County Battalion Chief Rob Hazard .
