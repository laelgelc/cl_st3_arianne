<h> PG&amp;E 's Bankruptcy Filing Creates ' a Real Mess ' for Rival Interests <p> LOS ANGELES -- Pacific Gas and Electric 's bankruptcy filing on Tuesday , to deal with billions of dollars in wildfire liability , set off a scramble by the company , investors and elected leaders in California to protect themselves and influence what happens next . <p> The corporate reorganization is shaping up to be one of the most complicated and difficult in recent years . In addition to traditional legal tussles between the company and its creditors and suppliers , the bankruptcy court will contend with demands by California officials and victims to force PG&amp;E to pay damage claims estimated at tens of billions of dollars for wildfires started by its equipment . State leaders and residents are also likely to seek to thwart any effort by the utility to raise electricity rates . <p> Coming up with a bankruptcy plan for PG&amp;E , which is California 's largest utility and serves 16 million people , will amount to solving an intricate puzzle . Proposals to raise rates to pay creditors will inflame residents and lawmakers .
