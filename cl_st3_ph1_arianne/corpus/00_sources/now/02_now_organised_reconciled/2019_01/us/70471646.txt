<p> In a new public art project , Camden , New Jersey , will receive $1 million for art installations , events and other creative programming along its public transportation network . <p> Several sites that are now plagued by littering , including locations along the Port Authority Transit Corporation ( PATCO ) high-speed line , RiverLine light rail , and the Camden GreenWay trail network , will be turned into dynamic art spaces . <p> Camden is the fifth and final winning city of the 2018 Public Art Challenge. ( photo courtesy of the City of Camden ) <p> The funding , announced on Thursday by Bloomberg Philanthropies , is part of its 2018 Public Art Challenge , a program that began in 2014 to develop and support innovative temporary public art projects that " enhance the vibrancy of cities " by addressing important civic and social issues and encouraging public-private collaborations . <p> " Public art has the power to bring people together to strengthen their communities , " Michael R. Bloomberg , founder of Bloomberg Philanthropies and the former mayor of New York City , said in a statement .
