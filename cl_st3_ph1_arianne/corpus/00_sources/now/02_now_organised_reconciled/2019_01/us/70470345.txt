<p> A large cavity has formed under what has been described as one of the world 's most dangerous glaciers , and could contribute to a significant bump in global sea levels , said NASA scientists . <p> A study led by the agency revealed a cavity about two-thirds the area of Manhattan and roughly 1,000 feet tall is growing under Thwaites Glacier in West Antarctica . <p> The cavity is large enough to have contained 14 billion tons of ice , most of which has melted within the last three years , say researchers . <p> " ( The size of ) a cavity under a glacier plays an important role in melting , " said lead author Pietro Milillo of NASA 's Jet Propulsion Laboratory in a statement . " As more heat and water get under the glacier , it melts faster . " <p> Thwaites has been described as one of the world 's most dangerous glaciers because its demise could lead to rapid changes in global sea levels .
