Nonetheless , the city puffed out its chest , put its downtown on lockdown and hoisted the flags of the 22 member states under a mercifully rainless sky . <p> The fourth economic and social summit of the Arab League -- or most of it , anyway -- was hereby called to order . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bring together all the Arabs , leaving no vacant seats , " the host , President Michel Aoun of Lebanon , lamented in a speech that kicked off Sunday 's gathering .
