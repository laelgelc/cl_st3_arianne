<h> Soaking rain and downpours lead to pockets of flooding in the Washington region <p> Following Washington 's soggiest year on record , the new year seems to be picking up right where 2018 left off . A strong cold front drenched the region Thursday , with widespread rainfall totals surpassing one inch . <p> The downpours have led to multiple flood warnings , many issued just as the rain was ending but creeks and streams still rising . <p> Because of the partly frozen ground , the bursts of rain flowed straight into area waterways rather than soaking into soil . <p> Flood warnings in effect Thursday afternoon . <p> As of 3 p.m . Thursday , flood warnings were in effect for much of Prince William and Fairfax counties , Rock Creek in northwest Washington , and parts of eastern Montgomery County . These warnings will not expire until Thursday evening in many areas , when water levels on creeks and streams should begin receding . <p> In addition to heavy rainfall , parts of the region also experienced strong winds as the front blew through -- especially out toward the mountains and along the Chesapeake Bay @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ clocked . <p> In the wake of the front , temperatures fell sharply . Whereas temperatures midmorning Thursday climbed to a balmy 60 degrees , they had fallen into the mid-40s by 3 p.m. across the region and were forecast to dip into the 30s by dark . <p> Temperatures on the roof of The Washington Post in downtown Washington on Thursday .
