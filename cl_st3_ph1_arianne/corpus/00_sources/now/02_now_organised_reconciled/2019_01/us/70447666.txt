<p> The global population faces diverse health challenges , ranging from serious virus outbreaks and drug-resistant bacteria , to the increase in non-communicable diseases like obesity and heart disease . <p> While some parts of the world have limited access to vaccines , patients in other regions are reluctant to use them , partly because of the anti-vaccine movement . <p> Another area of huge concern is the toll that pollution and climate change take on health . <p> The World Health Organization ( WHO ) has identified 10 of the biggest threats to world health in 2019 . That 's more than 70% of all worldwide deaths . Incidents of these diseases are dramatically higher in low and middle-income countries than wealthier nations , due to a combination of high tobacco and alcohol use , polluted air , unhealthy diet and little exercise . <p> Global influenza pandemic <p> According to the WHO , anticipating the next influenza pandemic is about " when " it strikes , not " if " it strikes . Although influenza may not be considered a serious threat by some , seasonal influenza kills up to 650,000 people each year .
