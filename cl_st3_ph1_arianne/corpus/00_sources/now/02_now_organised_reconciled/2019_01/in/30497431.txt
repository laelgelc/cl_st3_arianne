<h> The first-ever census of captive elephants , undertaken on the orders of the Supreme Court , has revealed there are 2,454 such jumbos in 28 states and Union Territories . <h> Representative image <p> New Delhi : The first-ever captive elephants ' census in the country has enumerated 2,454 jumbos , out of which , more than two-thirds are in private custody . <p> The census , undertaken on the orders of the Supreme Court , computed 2,454 captive elephants in 28 states and Union Territories . <p> Arunachal Pradesh , Telangana , Sikkim , Mizoram , Himachal Pradesh , Nagaland , Jammu and Kashmir and the Union Territory of Daman and Diu are still to submit their reports in terms of the order by the Supreme Court . <p> Out of the captive elephants , 1,809 are in private custody . Private custody would include elephants owned by individuals , circuses and temples .
