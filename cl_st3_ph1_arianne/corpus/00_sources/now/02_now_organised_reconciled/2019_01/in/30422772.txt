The flowering trees that give Bengaluru its garden city identity - Tabebuia Rosea ( with light pink flowers ) , Tabebuia Argentea ( yellow flowers ) , White Tebebuia ( pinkish white flowers ) have had an early flowering . Bengaluru is said to have the highest concentration of avenue Tabebuias in the India , a species which thrives in tropical countries and has been largely planted along avenues . <p> The Tabebuia rosea , known as Pink Pouis , originated from Mexico , Venezuela and Ecuador . They start shedding their leaves by March and then the flowers start to bloom , forming a pale pink canopy over the city . The flowering period goes on till the month of May .
