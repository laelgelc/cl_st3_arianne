<h> Punjab taking measures to increase green cover <p> CHANDIGARH : The state of Punjab , which has the lowest forest cover and percentage of land under green cover in the country , is finally taking initiatives to better its position . The 2017 Forest Survey of India assessment has already shown an increase of 66 sqkm in tree cover outside recorded forest area . According to this report , Punjab has forest cover of 50,362 sq km area and 3.65% of its geographical area under green cover . <p> After distribution of 63 lakh saplings to public , the forest department has abruptly ended the supply to prepare stock for sapling distribution in the wake of 550 th birth anniversary of Guru Nanak Dev this year . <p> Jatinder Sharma , head of forest force in Punjab said , " We achieved far beyond the target given to us in terms of afforestation for last year . Around 65 lakh saplings of traditional trees like Shisham , Khijri , Bakain , Neem and fruit trees like mango , guava , berries , papaya etc were distributed to people free of cost .
