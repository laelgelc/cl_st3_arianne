<h> House panel seeks new act to ensure proper use of tribal funds <p> The parliamentary panel has also found that ministries are not earmarking funds as per their obligatory requirements . <p> A Parliamentary panel has directed the government to stop diversion of funds meant for tribal welfare and frame a central Act to ensure funds reach beneficiaries , taking note of instances of diversion of Tribal Sub-Plan ( TSP ) funds . <p> The parliamentary standing committee on social justice and empowerment has asked the Centre to enact a law to ensure there was no diversion of TSP funds . It also asked ministry of tribal affairs to direct state governments to frame their own similar legislations . Under TSP , 41 central government ministries and departments are required to earmark 2-20% of their total budget outlay for tribal welfare . The ministries identify targeted schemes and then earmark the funds scheme-wise to different states .
