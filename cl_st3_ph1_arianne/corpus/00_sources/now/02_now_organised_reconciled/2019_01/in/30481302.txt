<h> Companies must commit to workplace equality - senior business leaders <p> LONDON , Jan 22 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Companies must commit to equality in the workplace or miss out on millennial LGBT+ talent , global business leaders warned on Tuesday at the World Economic Forum ( WEF ) in Davos , Switzerland . <p> Sander van ' t Noordende , group chief executive of products at professional services company Accenture , said a corporate culture of equality was both a human rights and business imperative . <p> " From a talent point of view , we want the best people on board at Accenture and if we do not tap into different pools of talent and have that culture of equality , we 're not going to be the best for our clients , " he said . <p> Van ' t Noordende was speaking at the launch of the Partnership for Global LGBTI Equality at Davos on Tuesday . <p> Backed by Microsoft , Mastercard , Accenture , Deutsche Bank , EY , Omnicom Group and Salesforce , the initiative is designed to implement the United Nations LGBTI Standards of Conduct for Business . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2017 , establish five business principles , including respect for human rights and eliminating workplace discrimination . <p> By 2020 , the business partnership aims to sign up an additional 50-100 companies . <p> " The companies involved in this initiative are leading by example when it comes to the rights of LGBTQ people in the context of employment , " said Michelle Bachelet , U. N. High Commissioner for Human Rights , which backs the programme . <p> " I support their efforts to extend the initiative to a greater number of private sector actors , as well as to engage with civil society . " <p> Workplace diversity remains an issue for many companies , a study from Boston Consulting Group ( BCG ) revealed last week . <p> Almost two-thirds of British LGBT+ employees said they would quit within three years because their company was not committed to a diverse workplace , the study found . <p> Open , inclusive and diverse societies were more innovative , in turn leading to greater economic growth , said Saadia Zahidi , head of WEF 's Centre for the New @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ report . <p> " Through this project we aim to provide a platform for leaders from the private sector to accelerate process towards LGBTI inclusion globally , " she said . <p> LGBT+ rights organisations welcomed the new business pact . <p> " Global brands should now take action , create inclusive workplaces and leverage their economic impact to move policymakers and cultural leaders to accept and protect all people , including LGBTI individuals , " said GLAAD president Sarah Kate Ellis . <p> " Over 200 companies have expressed support for the UN Standards , but support is merely a first step .
