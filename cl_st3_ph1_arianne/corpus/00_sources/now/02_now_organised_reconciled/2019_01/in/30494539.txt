<h> Palamuru project gets green signal <p> HYDERABAD : The ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change ( MOEF&amp;CC ) , in a significant move , has granted final approval for stage II of Palamuru-Ranga Reddy lift irrigation ( PRLI ) project . The ministry has communicated about the environmental clearance to the state government in a letter on Friday . <p> The Centre has allowed the state for diversion of 205.4 hectares of forest land in Achampet division of Nagarkurnool for works of Lift I pump house ( package 1 ) and construction of Anjanagiri reservoir ( package 2 ) , earth work , excavation and construction of tunnel between Anajagiri reservoir at Narlapur and Veeranjaneya reservoir at Yedula ( package 4 ) of PRLI . <p> According to officials , the project is being taken up by the state government at a cost of ? 32,500 crore to irrigate 12.3 lakh acres and another 1,000 villages in erstwhile Mahbubnagar , Ranga Reddy and Nalgonda districts .
