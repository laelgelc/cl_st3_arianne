<h> Apple farmers to get subsidized anti-hail nets <p> Uttarkashi : The horticulture department will distribute anti-hail nets to apple farmers to fight hailstorms which cause severe damage to the crop annually . The anti-hail nets would be provided at a 75% subsidized rate . <p> Announcing this , N K Singh , deputy director of Uttarkashi horticulture department , said , " Anti-hail nets are the best way for farmers to protect their crops . After the subsidy , farmers will get an anti-hail net for Rs 9 per square metre . At present , a square metre of the net costs Rs 35 . " <p> TOI had earlier reported that apple production in Uttarkashi district has reduced drastically , from 32,900 metric tonnes in 2012 to only 15,445 metric tonnes in 2017 . Experts have blamed excessive pesticide use and climate change for apple trees producing less fruit . In 2017 , incessant rain and hailstorms had affected the fruit in apple producing belts of Uttarkashi , Dehradun , Nainital and Chamoli districts . <p> Apple farmers in Uttarkashi meanwhile welcomed the new scheme . <p> Mohan Singh Rana , a farmer , said , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ biggest challenges .
