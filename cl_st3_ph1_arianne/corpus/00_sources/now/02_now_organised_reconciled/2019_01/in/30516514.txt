<h> Soil erosion : Three farm projects await Centre funds of Rs 400 crore <p> CHANDIGARH : Three projects to treat farm land afflicted with soil and moisture erosion , lantana growth in Kandi belt and waterlogging in Sri Muktsar Sahib area of Punjab are awaiting funds from the Centre for the last two years . People of the affected area who are living in poverty will be provided alternative livelihood avenues through capacity building programmes if these projects get funding . <p> According to D V Ratna , additional principal conservator of forest , the department is going to make a fresh attempt to remind the ministry to release Rs 408.14 crore for these projects . These include climate change adaptation through afforestation , soil and moisture conservation ; ecological restoration of lantana camara infested area and climate change adaptation through bio drainage/agro forestry in waterlogged area of Punjab amounting to Rs 232cr , 22.92cr and Rs 153.22cr , respectively . The first two projects will be implemented in Kandi area and the third in Sri Muktsar Sahib area of Punjab . <p> According to information , there are about 1,770 villages in Kandi area .
