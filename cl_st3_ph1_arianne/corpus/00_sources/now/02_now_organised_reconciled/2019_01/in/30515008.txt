C , wetlands provide a natural solution to making a daunting task more achievable . <p> " Wetlands such as peatlands and marshes , as well as coastal and marine areas like estuaries , lagoons , mangroves and coral reefs are essential to regulating the global climate along with forests and oceans , " it said . <p> Peatlands , which only cover 3% of the planet 's land surface , store 30% of land-based carbon .
