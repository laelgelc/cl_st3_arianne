<h> Jaipur Literature Festival : Devotees of the written word throng the ' Kumbh Mela ' of literature <p> On the opening day of the Jaipur Literature Festival ( JLF ) this week , celebrated feminist icon Germaine Greer spent most of her time on the stage answering questions about her controversial comments on rape and the #MeToo movement . In a heated session on the hallowed front lawns of the Diggi Palace venue of the festival , Greer defended her statements calling for lenient sentences for rapists and questioning the efficiency of the #MeToo movement . Ironically , the session , Beyond the Female Eunuch , had borrowed its title from Greer 's path-breaking book , a seminal work in the second wave of feminism more than half-a-century ago . <p> Feminist icon Germaine Greer in conversation with British journalist-author Bee Rowlatt on the opening day of the Jaipur Literature Festival . <p> The session starring Greer , resembling more of an inquisition than a conversation , reflected the anxiety and tensions in the contemporary society facing racism , bigotry , hatred for migrants , refugee crisis , terrorism , gender inequality and poverty . In a departure from tradition , the keynote address this year was delivered not by a writer , but a scientist .
