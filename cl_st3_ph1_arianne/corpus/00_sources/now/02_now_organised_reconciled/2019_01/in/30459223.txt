<h> Only 38% water use efficiency in agriculture <p> Jan 16 , 2019 , 22:23 IST <p> Aurangabad : The country records only 38% water-use efficiency in the field of agriculture and much needs to be done to improve it , said U P Singh , secretary of the water resources ministry on Wednesday . Delivering the introductory speech during the 9th international micro-irrigation conference here , he said propagation of techniques such as sprinklers , drip irrigation and precision irrigation among the farming community will help increase water-use efficiency . <p> " The NITI Aayog has painted a grim picture of the water scenario in the country in the days ahead . The availability of water is not going to increase but will be impacted further due to climate change . Rainfall pattern is likely to become weirder . Therefore , we have to focus on water resources management instead of water resources development , " Singh said . <p> He also stressed the need for making micro-irrigation techniques more affordable among farmers , especially marginal farmers . " We all should see how micro-irrigation techniques can become accessible and affordable .
