<h> Green activist gets Arctic tour call <p> BHUBANESWAR : Neelima Mishra , a 33-year-old woman who works as a consultant to the state government , has been selected for the Climate Force Arctic Expedition hosted by 2041 Foundation . <p> Mishra is the only participant from the state and will join 79 others from across the world to take part in the one-of-its-kind expedition aimed at creating awareness on climate change , renewable energy and sustainability . Mishra , who will board the ship to the Arctic in June , is busy raising money for it through crowdfunding and corporate sponsorships . <p> " I am really excited about the expedition . We have all read about the Arctic . I am looking forward to going there and sharing my experience with others when I come back , especially with school and college students . I am hopeful that after this life-changing experience , I will do something substantial for the environment , " said Mishra , an electrical engineer who has an MBA from the Indian Institute of Forest Management , Bhopal . <p> For the expedition , Mishra has to spend Rs 13 lakh ; of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ crowdfunding and arrange some of the money by using her savings . " I have approached some corporate houses to fund my trip . I am hopeful of arranging the funds by March , " she added . <p> Mishra was selected for the expedition from among 5,000 applicants . " The most exciting thing about the expedition is that it will be led by Robert Swan , polar explorer , environment leader and the first man to walk to both the north and south poles . Apart from getting first-hand experience of the fragile ecosystem there , we will learn about how to protect the environment around us , " she explained . <p> As a young environmentalist , Mishra launched a campaign called Plastic-Free July , in which she urged people to stop using single-use plastic for a month .
