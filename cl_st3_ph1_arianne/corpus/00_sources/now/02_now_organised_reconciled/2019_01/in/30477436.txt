<h> Subhash made development commissioner <p> TNN Jan 22 , 2019 , 02:00 IST <p> PATNA : The state government on Monday made 1984-batch IAS officer Subhash Sharma the development commissioner , replacing Arun Kumar Singh , who has been transferred to the water resources department as additional chief secretary . Sharma was chief vigilance commissioner in general administration department . <p> Singh will continue to hold the additional charge of DG , Bihar Institute of Public Administration and Rural Development . <p> Tripurari Sharan , a 1985-batch IAS officer and posted as additional chief secretary in the environment , forest and climate change department , has been given additional charge of chief vigilance commissioner in GAD . <p> Sanjay Kumar Singh , a 2007-batch officer posted as state project director in Bihar Education Project Council , has been made additional chief electoral officer while his batchmate Vinod Singh Gunjiyal has been given additional charge of director of fisheries directorate .
