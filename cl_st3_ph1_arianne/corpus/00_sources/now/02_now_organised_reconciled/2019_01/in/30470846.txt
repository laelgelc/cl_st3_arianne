<h> ' Having nuclear weapons makes a country stronger ' <p> TNN Jan 20 , 2019 , 11:29 IST <p> Chidambaram emphasised on reducing carbon dioxide emissions <p> PANAJI : Energy security and climate change are two of the greatest challenges of the 21st century . India can hope to become a developed country only if the per capita electricity consumption in the country goes up by at least six times , said Padma Vibushan Rajagopala Chidabaram . He was speaking on the topic ' India 's Nuclear Programme - A model for developing world ' in Panaji , on Saturday . Chidambaram also said that having nuclear weapons makes a country stronger as the weapons act as a deterrent . " India has single-handedly developed its nuclear technology and weapons without spying or stealing knowledge from other countries . The other nations whereas have shared knowledge about nuclear weapons , " he said . <p> Chidabaram spoke about how the human development index can be defined in terms of two parameters -- female literacy and per capita electricity consumption . <p> " Firstly , India can not become a developed country unless it becomes nearly 100 percent literate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ capita electricity consumption must go up at least six times before India can hope to become a developed country , " he said . <p> " This increase in electricity production must come in the context of climate change , which is a threat of global warming . That is where , according to the international panel of climate change , renewable energy , nuclear energy , carbon capture and storage and bioenergy gain importance as these are near-zero carbon emission options , " he said . <p> Chidambaram further stressed on the need for low carbon dioxide emissions . " We will probably still be burning coal in the next 20 years . From my earlier office , we had started a project on advanced ultra-super critical thermal plant where you take the steam temperature in a coal-based thermal plant to more than 700 degrees .
