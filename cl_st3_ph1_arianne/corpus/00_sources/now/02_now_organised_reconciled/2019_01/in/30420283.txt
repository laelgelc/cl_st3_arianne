<h> Share Via Email <p> KOCHI : The dawn of New Year last week saw a sudden dip in temperatures in most parts of Kerala . While the hill station of Munnar stood out by recording sub-zero temperatures , districts of Kochi ( 20 degree Celsius ) , Kottayam ( 17 degree Celsius ) , Kannur ( 19 degree Celsius ) and Alappuzha ( 20 degree Celsius ) are also experiencing chilly yet pleasant weather conditions , especially during the morning and the evening hours . In fact , the minimum temperatures in the four districts have come down by 2-3.9 degree Celsius in recent days to touch the lowest minimum temperature in at least three decades . <p> While Munnar reported minus 3 degree Celsius , several parts of the state , including Sabarimala and some pockets in Kottayam , recorded 17 degree Celsius . To be sure , this sudden spell of chilly weather condition is not a Kerala-only phenomenon .
