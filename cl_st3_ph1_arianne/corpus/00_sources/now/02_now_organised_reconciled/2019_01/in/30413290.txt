<p> Chandigarh : Punjab , which has the lowest forest cover and percentage of land under green cover in the country , is trying to improve its position . The 2017 Forest Survey of India assessment shows an increase of 66 sq km in tree cover outside recorded forest area . As per the report , Punjab has a forest cover of 50,362 sq km and 3.65% of its geographical area is under green cover . <p> After distributing 63 lakh saplings to the public , the forest department abruptly ended supply to prepare a fresh stock for distribution as part of the 550th birth anniversary celebrations of Guru Nanak Dev this year . Jatinder Sharma , head of the forest force , Punjab said , " In terms of afforestation , we exceeded the target given last year . Around 65 lakh saplings of traditional trees like Shisham Khijri , Bakain , Neem and fruit trees like mango , guava , berries and papaya were distributed to people free of cost . We achieved the target of distribution of 20 lakh saplings , which we achieved through our app , ' Ayi Hariyali ' , available first on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ plants and hybrid seeds were also given to slum-dwellers and others to improve their health under Mission Tandrust Punjab ( Mission Healthy Punjab ) . " <p> Sapling distribution , which started last April , was stopped in December to prepare a stock of 550 saplings for each of the 13,000 villages in the state . To help farmers diversify to crops that consume less water , to meet the challenge of climate change and to supplement farmers ' income , Sharma said the department is conducting scientific trials to test the durability of high-value trees , Red Senders and Agar , on Punjab soil . <p> " Red Senders are mainly cultivated in Andhra Pardesh , Telangana and Tamil Nadu while Agar is grown in Agartala . Both are high-value trees which will fetch a good price for farmers of Punjab . " Red Senders is a premium quality timber while resin extracts from Agar wood is used to make incense and perfume .
