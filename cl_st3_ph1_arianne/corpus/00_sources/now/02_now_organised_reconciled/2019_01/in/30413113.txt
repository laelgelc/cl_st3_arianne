<h> Nancy Pelosi elected as new speaker of US lower house <p> Representative Nancy Pelosi , a California Democrat , was elected the new speaker of the US House of Representatives . Pelosi , 78 , is the only woman who has held the office before and is among a few elected officials to return to the post . She was the Housespeaker from 2007 to 2011 when her Democratic Party controlled the lower chamber , Xinhua reported on Friday . <p> For her second speakership , Pelosi defeated Republican nominee Kevin McCarthy in a roll-call vote Thursday , winning 220 votes out of a total of 430 present , according to CNN .
