<h> Compensatory afforestation , an eyewash , says FBV <p> VISAKHAPATNAM : Forum for Better Visakha ( FBV ) , a civic rights group , on Wednesday demanded an investigation by the central government into the allotment of forest land by the AP government to industries in lieu of the latter doing so-called " compensatory afforestation " . <p> In a letter to C K Mishra , secretary to the Union ministry of environment , forests and climate change ( MOEF&amp;CC ) , FBV convenor EAS Sarma said , " I have been writing to you in specific cases how the AP government has been misleading your ministry in the matter of compensatory afforestation linked to diversion of forest lands for industries . On a random verification , I find that such misleading proposals have been sent to your ministry on a very large scale . " <p> According to Sarma , a former Union expenditure secretary , the state government diverted reserve forest land in Avanam in Bheemili mandal to Divi 's Laboratories Ltd . Similarly , he said , reserve forest land in Visakhapatnam rural mandal was handed over for the Karthikavanam eco-tourism project in Yendada village @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ non-forest ' land proposed for compensatory afforestation is non-existent , " he said . <p> Sarma also cited several similar cases . " In the case of Divi 's Laboratories , GOMs No 201 dated October 22 , 2010 issued by the state government violates the orders of the Supreme Court in Godavarman case , as diversion of 17.857 ha of forest land would require compensatory afforestation to the extent of 35.714 ha , whereas the state has indicated an extent of only 17.857 ha of alternate land in Prakasam district . It is surprising as to how the MOEF&amp;CC had agreed to this , " Sarma said in an earlier letter to Mishra . <p> In the Karthikavanam case , he said , the total extent of the project is 10 acres , and is classified as ' forest ' in the records of the forest department .
