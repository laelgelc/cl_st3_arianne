<h> Sundarbans , which is 10 times bigger than the city of Venice , and its many facts <p> Sundarbans , the very name casts a spell , and exploring this national park is every wild lover 's dream . <p> Of late Sundarbans has caught attention of many not because of its obvious fame of being the largest delta in the world , but because of water level issue . Researchers are of the view that the effects of climate change will possibly swallow some of the remote islands in the near future . And if this situation remains the same , it is feared that most of the islands will disappear owing to rising tides . <p> Also , a part of this mangrove also lies in Bangladesh , and Sundarbans National Park was declared a UNESCO World Heritage in 1987 . It is one of the exclusive spots for safaris and excursions in India and West Bengal . Out of these 102 islands , 54 are inhabited by people , while the rest of the area is covered by forests . Also , while India owns 40 percent of the total area of Sundarbans , the rest of the area is being owned by Bangladesh . <p> What 's in the name ? There is an amazing fact related to this . Did you know how this national park got its name Sundarbans ? It was actually named after a mangrove tree called Sundari , which translates to beautiful . And no one can deny the fact of this park being nature 's own land and being blessed with bountiful flora and fauna . <p> Tidal phenomenon of the SundarbansSundarbans experiences high and low tides twice a day .
