<h> Mango most tolerant to high temperature , teak least , says an IISER study on city trees <p> Saplings in the IISER lab that were part of the study ; PIC : MAHENDRA KOLHE <p> Survey that analysed impact of climate change on local flora found tropical trees more resistant than deciduous ones <p> Results from a recent study showed that mango exhibited the highest resistance to extreme temperatures among 40 species of trees examined across Pune . Teak was found to be the most vulnerable to extreme temperatures . <p> These results came up in a study conducted by city scientists to analyse how different varieties of trees would react to future climate change . Fig and tamarind trees commonly found in Pune also fared well in showing resistance towards extreme heat . <p> The study also revealed that evergreen plant species were more resistant to climate change than their deciduous counterparts . <p> As many as 41 plant species were studied in Pashan , Baner and Panchavati areas of the city . <p> " The study was done to understand the upper limits of tolerance to extreme temperatures among tropical trees and how they might respond @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ associate professor of biology at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research ( IISER ) , Pune . <p> Barua said that leaf mass per area and other key functional traits of the leaves were studied to determine their relationship to temperature tolerance . <p> Barua said that leaf thermotolerance of the 41species that were studied ranged between 45 and 50 degrees Celsius . Deciduous and fast-growing trees were found to be most vulnerable to future warming . <p> " Tropical trees are likely to have been already exposed to temperatures close to their upper limits and the responses of these trees to extreme temperatures are not well understood , " he added . <p> Barua said that it has been observed that broad-leaved evergreen species maintain a large proportion of green cover through the year compared to deciduous varieties which shed most of their leaves to avoid drought-like conditions .
