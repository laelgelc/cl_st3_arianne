<h> Do your bit in waste management : Yadav <p> TNN Jan 22 , 2019 , 10:58 IST <p> The MC is keen on creating awareness about the evils of dumping waste <p> CHANDIGARH : " Stakeholders of all cities should ensure effective management of waste , through a collaborative approach , " said municipal commissioner K K Yadav during a two-day regional workshop on the Waste Management Rules 2016 that was organised in the city on Monday . <p> The Federation of Indian Chambers of Commerce and Industry , in association with ministry of environment , forest and climate change , government of India organised the workshop . <p> While addressing the delegates , Yadav said that the government has revised and notified regulations for management of solid waste , plastic waste , e-waste , hazardous waste , biomedical waste , along with introducing construction and demolition waste in 2016 with an objective to accelerate the efforts for waste recycling . <p> During the presentation , the municipal commissioner highlighted the best practices of Municipal Corporation ( MC ) in solid waste management . The MC has started the construction of a waste plant in the industrial area , where it will process construction and demolition waste and make concrete tiles to be used in roads and parks . <p> Yadav also touched upon the most popular waste management measure adopted in the city : Home composting . According to him , the wet waste generated in the households and the kitchen waste are being processed through home composting methods . <p> Yadav also said that the MC has taken the responsibility of creating awareness among the people about the threat of dumping waste .
