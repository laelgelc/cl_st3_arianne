<p> Only half of 47 gharials released in Harike Beas seem to have survived <h> Only half of 47 gharials released in Harike Beas seem to have survived <p> Seema Sharma <p> TOI Jan 8 , 2019 , 07:19 IST <p> Chandigarh : Of the 47 gharials released into the Harike Beas by the Punjab wildlife dept in March only 26 seem to have survived . The reptiles were released under Project Crocodile started in 1975 by the Centre . In order to monitor if the gharials have survived to declare reintroduction programme successful , the officials revisited the Indus sites once again along with researcher of World Wildlife Fund ( WWF ) and spotted 26 in the river . The officials said that even if half of the reintroduced species are found safe and sound it means the project has worked out well . The department now plans to release 50 more gharials from the Chambal river in Madhya Pradesh . <p> " WWF ( the leading organization in wildlife conservation and endangered species ) has been monitoring the survival of gharials ever since they were introduced .
