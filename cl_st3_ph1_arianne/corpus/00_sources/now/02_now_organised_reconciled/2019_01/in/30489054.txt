<h> India will be one of top three scientific country in the world by 2030 : Harsh Vardhan <p> PUDUCHERRY : Union science and technology , environment , forest and climate change and earth sciences minister Harsh Vardhan said India will be one of the top three scientific countries in the world by 2030 . The minister was in Puducherry to dedicate the northern nearshore reef established at a cost of Rs 25 crore as a part of the beach restoration project . <p> " This model ( beach restoration project ) we are trying to make is not just a success story for India but for the whole world . National institute of ocean technology has done an extraordinary study evolving an extraordinary way for the restoration of the beach , " he said . <p> Vardhan praised India 's Tsunami early warning system saying that India has been rated as the best and number one in the world in tsunami early warning system . " We were caught unaware when 2004 tsunami struck . " We have the best fleet of research vessels in the world next only to the United States , United Kingdom and Japan . We launched a research vessel last month and will be launching one more research vessel shortly . Soon we will have seven ships that come around the clock undertaking missions with our scientists on board taking up detailed research projects . Similarly , we are ranked third in nanotechnology and sixth in scientific publication in the world , " he said . <p> The minister said the Council of scientific and industrial research ( CSIR ) , which was established as early as in 1942 in the country , was ranked ninth among 207 similar institutions funded by the governments in various countries .
