<h> Lack of papers endangers Venezuelan children in Brazil <p> RIO DE JANEIRO , Jan 15 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - F leeing poverty and hunger , at least 1,000 Venezuelan children arrived at Brazil 's main entry point unaccompanied by parents or without proper documents in the last five months , according to a report obtained by Thomson Reuters Foundation on Tuesday . <p> Some 52 percent of those who arrived at the border city of Paracaima , in the state of Roraima , were without proper documents and the rest were without parents , the report authored by the Federal Public Defenders Office said . <p> Lack of documentation leads to poor access to healthcare and other services in Brazil , while children traveling without parents are at a greater risk of human trafficking , experts said . <p> Facing a financial and humanitarian crisis , about one in every 10 people in Venezuela has fled the country in the past three years .
