<h> NGT : Set solid waste management plan in month <p> TNN Updated : Jan 13 , 2019 , 08:07 IST <p> National Green Tribunal <p> SHIMLA : On the issue of compliance with the Municipal Solid Waste Management Rules , 2016 in Mandi , Himachal Pradesh , the National Green Tribunal ( NGT ) has directed the Union housing ministry to finalize the pending proposal on time , preferably within one month and furnish an action taken report . The issue has been pending for the last six years and has been considered in more than 100 hearings , the Tribunal said . <p> Hearing the matter on Wednesday , principal bench of the Tribunal , headed by its chairperson Justice Adarsh Kumar Goel said in previous orders , they observed that solid waste was being dumped in the Beas river on account of non-compliance of rules by municipal local bodies concerned . <p> It was observed in the order dated August 8 , 2018 that the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change was not to deal with the issue of funding of the project for solid waste management , which would be dealt with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of both the Union ministries to hold a joint meeting . <p> The state government stated that matching grant as per the central government scheme had not been provided . A report dated September 28 , 2018 , was received from the Union environment ministry that a joint meeting was held as directed .
