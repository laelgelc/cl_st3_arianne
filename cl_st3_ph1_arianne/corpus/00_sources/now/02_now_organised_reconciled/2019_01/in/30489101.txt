<p> The AAP government , in its state action plan on climate change ( SAPCC ) , which was submitted recently to the Union ministry of environment , forests and climate change in compliance with NGT 's order , also proposed to increase the fleet of DTC 's CNG buses within a year . Currently , there are 4,352 DTC buses , which the state government plans to augment to 6,900 , the SAPCC said . <p> The fate of the SAPCC is likely to be decided by the Centre within four weeks , a government official said , while confirming that the 250-page document on the action plan was received by the ministry on Tuesday .
