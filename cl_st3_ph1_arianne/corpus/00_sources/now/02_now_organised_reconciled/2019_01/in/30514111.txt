In a typical Goliath-David kind of struggle , Mayilamma- the frail , fifty-year-old tribal widow , who fought for the cause of the small village of Plachimada on the Kerala-Tamil Nadu border became the symbol of the global resistance against Coca-Cola . " Dr Sreejith Varma added , " The book maps the rise of environmental activism in Kerala and it also weaves into its rhetoric the realities of consumption , globalisation , widening socio-economic inequalities and rising ecological burdens borne by the marginalised poor . " <p> Mayilamma 's life narrative simply put is that of an earth carer from the borders of the pan-Kerala struggles , who was intensely involved in the protection of livelihoods and local neighbourhoods .
