<h> UK to double participation in trial of popular HIV prevention pill <p> LONDON , Jan 11 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) - Health authorities in England said on Friday that they support a plan to double to 26,000 the number of participants in an HIV prevention pill trial , ahead of a potential national roll-out . <p> Demand for pre-exposure prophylaxis ( PrEP ) - a once-daily pill that is highly protective against HIV - has exceeded expectations , said National Health Service ( NHS ) England , which launched a three-year trial of the drug in 2017 . <p> " The trial researchers have submitted a case for increasing trial places , " said John Stewart , director of specialised commissioning at NHS England . <p> " NHS England will play its part in delivering on this recommendation by committing to fund additional places . " <p> Studies show that PrEP , also known by its drug brand name Truvada , can cut by 99 percent the transmission rate of the human immunodeficiency virus ( HIV ) that causes AIDS . <p> HIV transmission rates among gay and bisexual men fell by almost a third in the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the wide-scale introduction of PrEP , sparking calls for other health authorities to follow suit . <p> About 35 million people have died of AIDS since the start of the epidemic in the 1980s and almost 160,000 people in Europe were diagnosed with HIV in 2017 , according to the World Health Organization . <p> More than 10,000 people have already enrolled in the PrEP trial , NHS England said . Stewart said boosting the number of participants would help address questions about the need for PrEP among women and other groups . <p> " This will help ensure the learning from the trial is robust enough to fully inform the planning of a national PrEP programme in partnership with local authorities for the future , as well as protecting more people from HIV right now , " he said . <p> No date has been set for the potential national roll-out . <p> Campaigners in Britain welcomed the proposals . <p> Many sites recruiting to the PrEP trial have in recent weeks turned away gay and bisexual men away due to lack of places , with reports that some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of NAM , an HIV/AIDS charity . <p> " If we are serious about halting new HIV infections in this country we should ensure that all people at high risk of acquiring HIV should be aware of PrEP , should be offered it and should be able to access it if they so choose , " he said . <p> " Preparing for a full national programme of PrEP on the NHS is vital .
