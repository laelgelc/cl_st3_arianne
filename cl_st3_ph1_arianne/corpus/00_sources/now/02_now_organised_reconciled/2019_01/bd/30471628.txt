<h> India to become top trade partner of UAE by 2020 <p> Dubai , Jan 20 ( Xinhua/UNB ) - India is expected to become the top trade partner of the United Arab Emirates ( UAE ) in 2020 through doubling their annual trade volume , a UAE minister said Saturday . <p> The annual trade volume between India and the UAE , which stood at 57 billion U.S. dollars in the past few years , could exceed 100 billion dollars by 2020 , the official news agency WAM quoted Thani Ahmed Al-Zayoudi , the UAE minister of climate change and environment , as saying . <p> Thani made the remarks after meeting with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi in India 's western state of Gujarat , where he is attending the ninth Vibrant Gujarat Global Summit , which runs from Jan. 18-20 . <p> The summit is a platform for brainstorming on the agenda of global socio-economic development .
