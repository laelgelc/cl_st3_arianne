<h> Electronic waste disposal rules on the cards <p> The government is framing rules to ensure proper management of electronic waste ( e-waste ) such as handsets , computers and electrical items , which tend to be dumped haphazardly , posing risks to the environment and public health . <p> The move comes in the face of increasing generation of e-waste owing to rising use of electrical and electronic devices in the country . <p> The growth rate of e-waste generation is about 20 percent per year , according to a study by the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology . <p> The study carried out last year found that the amount of e-waste rose to 4 lakh tonnes in 2018 from 1.30 lakh tonnes in 2010 . The volume is projected to be 46.2 lakh tonnes by 2035 . <p> The e-wastes contain a number of toxic substances , including lead , chromium , and plastic additives , so their indiscriminate dumping poses health and environmental risks . <p> Once the rule is in place , consumers will not be able to throw away their obsolete electronic devices any place they like ;
