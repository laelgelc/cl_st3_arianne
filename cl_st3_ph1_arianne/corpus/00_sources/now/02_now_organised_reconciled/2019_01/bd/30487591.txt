<h> Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina has said Bangladesh has become an international ' role model ' for disaster management . <p> Death rates tied to natural calamities in Bangladesh have decreased considerably compared to the previous years , Hasina said speaking at the inaugural ceremony of the fourth session of the Regional Consultative Group ( RCG ) on Humanitarian Civil-Military Coordination for Asia and the Pacific at the Hotel InterContinental in Dhaka on Thursday . <p> " With undertaking of various measures , including improvement of early warning generation and dissemination , establishment of cyclone shelters and active engagement of dedicated CPP volunteers to the preparedness and response activities , the mortality from disasters has drastically been reduced in the recent times , " she said . <p> Hasina also emphasised cooperation among neighbouring countries in an effort to reduce the total amount of damage due to major disasters . <p> " We would not be able to prevent natural disasters . But we can reduce the extent of damage through our prudent work . In Bangladesh , we are also putting emphasis on mitigation programmes . Collaboration and cooperation among the nations , especially among neighbours , is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " said Hasina . <p> According to the Global Climate Risk Index , Bangladesh is in the ninth position among 10 most-affected counties . The index of 2014 and 2015 ranked Bangladesh sixth . <p> " Although we have very nominal contribution to climate change but Bangladesh is one of the most-affected countries by the impacts of global climate change owing to its geographical location . It is highly prone to natural disasters such as cyclone , flood , landslide and earthquake . " <p> " Now we are replicating the CPP model for other disasters . This paradigm shift has helped us adapt with the changes of global policy instruments such as Sendai Framework , Sustainable Development Goals and Paris Agreement on Climate Change in 2015 . As a result , Bangladesh is now known as the role model of disaster management and now moving towards building resilience. "
