The country ranks fifth in wheat imports in the world . The other four leading wheat importers are : Egypt ( 12 million tonnes ) , Indonesia ( 9.5 million tonnes ) , Algeria ( 8 million tonnes ) and Brazil ( 7 million tonnes ) . The country 's wheat import tripled in five years to 5.4 million tonnes in FY 2016-17 from 1.8 million tonnes in FY 2012-13 . The import of wheat was 2.7 million tonnes in FY 2013-14 , followed by 3.8 million in 2014-15 and 4.2 million in 2015-16 .
