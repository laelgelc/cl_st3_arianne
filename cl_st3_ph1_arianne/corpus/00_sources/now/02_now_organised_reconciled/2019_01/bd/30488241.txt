<h> UN says weak enforcement of laws worsening environmental crisis <p> Nairobi , Jan 24 ( Xinhua/UNB ) - Environmental threats like climate change and pollution are linked to lethargic enforcement of laws governing management of vital ecosystems , says a report released on Thursday by UN Environment . <p> According to the first ever global assessment of environmental rule of law , the quest to maintain a healthy and clean planet is being undermined by weak enforcement of legislation to protect it from natural and human-induced threats . <p> " This report solves the mystery of why problems such as pollution , declining biodiversity and climate change persist despite the proliferation of environmental laws in recent decades , " said David Boyd , UN Special Rapporteur on Human Rights and the Environment . <p> " Unless the environmental rule of law is strengthened , even seemingly rigorous rules are destined to fail and the fundamental human right to a healthy environment will go unfulfilled , " he added . <p> The UN Environment report says that rapid development of environmental laws and treaties since 1972 has not translated into their enactment thus escalating threats to
