Salinity mainly affects land and water in the coastal areas . <p> With the consequence of climate change , it gradually extends towards inland water and soil . This scenario of gradual salinity intrusion into the coastal areas of Bangladesh is very threatening to the primary production system , coastal biodiversity and human health , said researchers . <p> The total amount of salinity affected land in Bangladesh was 83.3 million hectares in 1973 , which had been increased up to 102 million hectares in 2000 and the amount has risen to 105.6 million hectares in 2009 and continuing to increase , according to the country 's Soil Resources Development Institute ( SRDI ) . <p> In the last 35 years , salinity increased around 26 percent in the country , spreading into non-coastal areas as well @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ areas of Bagerhat , a southwestern district , is casting a huge impact on the environment .
