<h> Window falls from HK hotel , killing tourist below <p> HONG KONG * Windows fell off from buildings in two incidents in Hong Kong on Monday , with one of them killing a Chinese tourist on a street in the popular Tsim Sha Tsui shopping district . <p> The 24-year-old tourist was shopping with her boyfriend when a window measuring 44cm by 130cm came crashing down from the 16th floor of the Mira Hong Kong hotel in Nathan Road and hit her on the head , Ming Pao reported . <p> She was taken to a hospital unconsciousness and later pronounced dead . <p> Her 36-year-old boyfriend suffered minor injuries on his left shoulder and was discharged from hospital after treatment . <p> Police arrested a Nepalese cleaner , 39 , who they said used a key to unlock the window in a guestroom and pushed it outwards to open it before the aluminium window frame came off . Reports citing sources alleged that the cleaner had opened the window to dispel the stench of cigarette smoke in the room , although smoking is prohibited in the guestrooms . <p> Senior Inspector Chan Ka Ying from the Yau @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ hotel to determine whether cleaning procedures required windows to be opened , the South China Morning Post ( SCMP ) reported . " We will look into other aspects such as the liability of parties like the hotel and its management , " she said . <p> The Hong Kong Free Press reported that the cleaner was arrested under section 4B of the Summary Offences Ordinance , on suspicion of allowing an object to fall from a building to the danger or injury of any person in or near a public place . The offence carries a fine of HK$10,000 ( S$1,735 ) and jail term of six months . <p> The tourist , who has not been named by the police , was not a guest at the hotel and had just arrived in the city from Foshan in southern China 's Guangdong province on the same day .
