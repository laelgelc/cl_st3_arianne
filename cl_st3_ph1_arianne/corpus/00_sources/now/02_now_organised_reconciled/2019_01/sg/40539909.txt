He said : " Everybody can play a part by only using antibiotics when they 're really ill . " <p> The health service has already cut its use of antibiotics by 7% since 2014 after ministers decided to make their reduction a key priority . In 2014 it was issuing 23.4 " defined daily doses " ( DDDs ) of antibiotics -- a measure of how much of the drugs people take -- per 1,000 people . That has fallen to 21.7 DDDs , a drop of 7.3% .
