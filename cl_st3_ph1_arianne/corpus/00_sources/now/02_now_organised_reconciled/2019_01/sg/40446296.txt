I conducted a survey involving 3,000 students recently during a dialogue session and found out that among their greatest worries is low wages and unemployment . <p> " These are the same worries faced by the fishermen , rubber tappers and labourers who work hard for a spoonful of rice , " he said in a statement @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ subsidies to be given out during the Budget 2019 debate . <p> Anwar also said he has been reading up on policies related to technology and digitisation to help his constituents and the country , adding that Malaysia will not be competitive if its citizens fail to adapt and change to acquire new skills . <p> " I am looking into global policies on the digital world to help the people in Port Dickson , including policies that can be implemented to ensure Malaysia remains relevant in the global economy and help us face climate change and an ageing society . <p> " The Pakatan Harapan government must focus on facing these problems together . I will continue to become the voice of the public despite the risk of being criticised , " he said .
