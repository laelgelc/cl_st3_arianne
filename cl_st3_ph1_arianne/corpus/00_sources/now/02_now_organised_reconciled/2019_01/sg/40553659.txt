<h> German government rules out an autobahn speed limit <p> A heated debate over introducing an autobahn speed limit has gripped Germany ever since it emerged last week that a committee tasked with coming up with ideas to lower transport emissions was suggesting a limit of 130kph ( 80mph ) on the country 's motorways . <p> The spectre of an autobahn speed limit riled up the media and politicians , including transport minister Andreas Scheuer , whose ministry oversees the committee that suggested the speed restriction . Scheuer said the proposal went " against all common sense . " <p> Angela Merkel 's spokesperson finally parked the topic on Monday , telling journalists in Berlin that the chancellor would not introduce a speed limit on the German autobahn . <p> " There are more intelligent methods than a general speed limit , " Steffen Seibert said , in reference to controlling CO2 emissions . He noted that there were already limits on large swathes of the motorway network , with 30% of motorways already subjected to speed limits . <p> However , not all Germans appear to be obsessed with tearing along at blistering speeds . " Speed ? ? is still the number one killer on German roads , " said Erich Rettinghaus , the State Chairman of the German Police Union in North Rhine Westfalia . He considered a general speed limit to be a necessary step in tackling the number of road deaths .
