<h> Amitav Ghosh 's novel , Gun Island , is about migration and how humans and animals have to adapt and keep up <p> Award-winning writer Amitav Ghosh keeps learning new languages in order to write his characters . His new novel has a couple of Italians in it , so he learnt Italian . <p> The 62-year-old , who is one of India 's best-known writers , says this was the most enjoyable part of writing his new novel Gun Island , which will be published later this year . <p> " I find it is hard to understand your character unless you know their language , " says Ghosh , who speaks six languages and previously picked up Cantonese to write the Ibis trilogy , an epic about the 19th-century opium trade , and Arabic for novels set in the Middle East . <p> " Most people very rarely write about characters who exist in another language . And yet that is the world we live in - it is so cosmopolitan and multi-national that we can no longer write about people who are just like us . " <p> Ghosh was here last week for a @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's Speaker Series . To a packed hall of more than 300 people , he expounded on the topic " Can the non-human speak ?
