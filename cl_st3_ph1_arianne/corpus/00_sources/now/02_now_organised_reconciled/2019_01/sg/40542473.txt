bear the brunt of rising sea levels . <p> Tuvalu , a nation of nine islands in the Pacific Ocean , is at real risk of being swallowed whole as surges in waves have affected its coastline , leaving large-scale destruction in their wake . <p> Tuvalu may vanish within a few decades , experts have warned . <p> The country 's Foreign Minister , Mr Taukelina Finikaso , told The Straits Times on Friday ( Jan 25 ) that he wants fossil fuel companies to shoulder some of the burden of rebuilding costs through what he calls a " climate change insurance " . <p> This would entail down payments to fund rebuilding , with payouts being provided according to the extent of damage . <p> " We hope that fossil fuel companies will be one of the main supporters of the initiatives , as they are the main
