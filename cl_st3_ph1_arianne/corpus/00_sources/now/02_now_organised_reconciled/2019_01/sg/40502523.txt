<h> FOLLOW US <h> Asean cities among top 20 for ' momentum ' in JLL index <p> Four Asean cities have made the top 20 list in real estate consultancy JLL 's City Momentum Index 2019 , which tracks socio-economic and commercial real estate growth in 131 cities over a three-year period . Vietnam was the strongest performer with two cities ranking the highest in Asean : Hanoi at third place and Ho Chi Minh City at eighth . The Philippines was represented by Manila in 12th place and Thailand by Bangkok in 18th place . <p> Topping the list was Bangalore , one of six Indian cities in the top 20 . China had the greatest presence with nine cities .
