<p> Local non-profit organisation Temasek Foundation has launched its Liveability Challenge this year , issuing a global call for companies to come up with innovative solutions for clean energy and sustainability in tropical cities . <p> Its Ecosperity arm is offering a $1 million prize - which it claims is the biggest in Asia for such a competition - to the winning company , which will go towards project development , crowdfunding campaigns and mentoring opportunities . <p> The challenge is being organised by Eco-Business magazine in partnership with this year 's Ecosperity Week in June when the contest 's finale will be held . <p> A total of 28 partners from business , civil society and government sectors have announced their support for The Liveability Challenge , almost double the number from last year 's inaugural competition . <p> " We believe that business can be prosperous when done in an ecologically friendly way , " said Mr Lim Hock Chuan , chief executive of Temasek Foundation Ecosperity . <p> " Last year , The Liveability Challenge was a great success with over 200 applications from 35 countries , and we are looking forward to more .
