<h> Landmark visit seen as effort to counter China 's growing influence in region <p> SYDNEY * Australian Prime Minister Scott Morrison embarks on a landmark trip to the Pacific today , hoping to mend fences and reassert Australian influence as China makes its presence felt in the region . <p> He will become the first Australian leader in decades to visit Vanuatu and the first ever to visit Fiji , where he is due to deliver a major speech on Friday . <p> In the face of increased Chinese influence - and , some suspect , creeping militarism - across the Pacific Islands , Mr Morrison 's government has stepped up efforts to secure his country 's historic role as a regional power broker . <p> The trip will be " focused on our security , economic and cultural partnerships " , said Ms Anne Ruston , a junior minister responsible for international development and the Pacific . <p> Australia is by far the region 's largest trade partner and donor -
