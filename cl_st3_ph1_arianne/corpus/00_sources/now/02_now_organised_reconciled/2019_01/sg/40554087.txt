However , in the last decade the region has experienced significant warming , causing the ice begin to recede , exposing the ground . <p> Experts have conducted radiocarbon Dating 48 plant samples , collected from 30 ice caps , and determined age , when the glaciers retreated for the last time , and temperature . It turned out that the current century is the warmest for the region over the last 115 thousand years .
