<h> Abe , Merkel push for multilateralism to solve trade woes <p> DAVOS * Japanese Prime Minister Shinzo Abe said he will seek to use his chairmanship of the Group of 20 ( G-20 ) leading economies to rebuild trust in the global trading system and foster consensus on ways to address climate change . <p> His speech to the World Economic Forum in the Swiss Alps yesterday was significant at a time when a bitter China-US trade dispute is one of several factors threatening to bring about a sharp slowdown in global growth . <p> " Japan is determined to preserve and committed to enhancing the free , open and rules-based international order , " he told delegates . <p> " I call on all of you ... to rebuild trust towards the system for international trade .
