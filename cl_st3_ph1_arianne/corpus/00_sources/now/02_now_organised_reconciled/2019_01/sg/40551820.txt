<h> Scientists call for artificial trees to fight climate change <p> Artificial photosynthesis could help propagate carbon-soaking plants without the huge impact on land and water resources , German scientists say . Image : Pexels <p> By Marlene Cimons , Nexus Media <p> Monday 28 January 2019 <h> Share this story <p> Plants are humanity 's greatest ally in the fight against climate change . Plants soak up carbon dioxide and turn it into leaves and branches . The more trees humans plant , the less heat-trapping carbon pollution in the air . Unfortunately , plants require a lot of water and land , so much that humans might need to find a new ally to help draw down all that carbon . <p> New research from a team of German scientists suggests that artificial photosynthesis could help . Scientists are urging the world to invest in the technology , which remains too costly to be practical . <p> Artificial photosynthesis imitates the process that fuels plants naturally .
