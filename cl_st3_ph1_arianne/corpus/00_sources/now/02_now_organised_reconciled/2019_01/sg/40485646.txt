<h> CPF survey booklet sheer waste of paper <p> When deforestation is a contributing factor to climate change and there is much talk about ways and means of cutting down paper wastage , it is disheartening to receive a 20-page booklet from a government agency in the mailbox . <p> I do not understand the logic behind the Central Provident Fund 's ( CPF ) A4-size survey booklet , when such a survey could very easily be done online . Even the cover letter on the CPF booklet says as much . <p> Why then is it necessary to have the print version ? <p> How many will actually take up a pen , complete the 58 questions , ( many of them open-ended ) , slot the completed booklet into the envelope provided , seal it , walk to a post box and mail it ? <p> I doubt many will and I am sure many of these booklets are headed for the trash bin . <p> Putting aside the drive to go green , how much time and money was spent on producing these booklets , which could have been spent more wisely elsewhere ? <h> The @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
