Yet , serious as these are , what may be most alarming is what WEF calls the resulting " crisis " from the world 's diminishing ability to tackle major global challenges , especially on the environmental front . <p> This gloomy prognosis is the central message of WEF 's 2019 Global Risks Report released last week . It points to a clear deterioration in the global landscape with a potentially toxic cocktail of trade disputes , environmental risks , cyber threats , and geo-political dangers .
