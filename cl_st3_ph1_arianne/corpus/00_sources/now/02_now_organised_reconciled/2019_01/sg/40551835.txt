Their love and respect for nature and its cycles and seasons and intimate knowledge of the hundreds of plants helped them survive . <p> The Aeta 's lives are , however , changing with climate change at a pace they never experienced before . <p> Life today is very different than it was about 70 years ago when there were still lush rainforests . Now the forests are almost gone . Hundreds of thousands of trees have been cut and shipped out to Japan , Korea , and Europe . The Aetas retreated further up the mountains and became subsistence farmers . <p> The Aeta people of Luzon had all of their ancestral lands stripped bare , and they being a peaceful people living in small remote villages , became planters and harvesters on the bare hills and mountains .
