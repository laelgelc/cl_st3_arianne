At scientific conferences , climate researchers engage in the same arguments and fights that characterise interactions in any other scientific community . <p> The IPCC , however , is the exact opposite of a scientific conference . It is an international body tasked with summarizing the existing consensus -- or lack thereof -- in the field of climate research . Its role , therefore , is independent of the disputes that are central to any scientific endeavor . <p> This leaves us with a fifth phase .
