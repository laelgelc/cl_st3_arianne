<h> Land use <p> Agriculture is the biggest single driver of deforestation , stripping away forests that reduce climate change by absorbing and storing carbon . Livestock takes up the most space , with land used to grow animal feed and for grazing using nearly 80 per cent of all agricultural land . <h> Waste <p> Up to a third of all food is wasted - totalling around 1.3 billion tonnes per year . It results in unnecessary emissions and means food does not reach those who need it . Experts predict the amount of food waste will rise further without action . <h> A growing population <p> The number of people on earth is expected to grow to 9.8 billion by 2050 , adding pressure on the world 's resources .
