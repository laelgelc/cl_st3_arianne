Gamble , Chevron and ExxonMobil , and voiced doubt over the effectiveness of the alliance 's clean-up plan . <p> " This is a desperate attempt from corporate polluters to maintain the status quo on plastics , " said Graham Forbes , global plastics project leader at Greenpeace . <p> " In 2018 , people all over the world spoke up and rejected the single-use plastics that companies like Procter &amp;
