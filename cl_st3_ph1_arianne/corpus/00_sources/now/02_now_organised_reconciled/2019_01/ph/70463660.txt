National Anti-Poverty Commission ( NAPC ) Secretary and lead convenor Noel Felongco ( left ) , together with Fernando Cao , head of the Research and Operations Unit of NAPC ( right ) , on Tuesday , January 29 , presented the agency 's three banner programs that aim to alleviate and reduce poverty in the country .
