<h> MRC Allied takes the green shift in power generation , waste disposal <p> IN these changing times , business leaders and entrepreneurs are rethinking their policies to remain relevant -- and commercially viable , of course -- in the constantly changing economic landscape . <p> For MRC Allied President and Chief Executive Officer Augusto Cosio Jr . there are three elements to strongly focus on aside from the annual revenues . These include the environment , stakeholders in the community and the employees . <p> " On our part , MRC Allied , we are concentrating on renewable energy , development of smart cities , solar energy and environmental stewardship , " Cosio , who has a wide exposure in the global capital markets after working in Hong Kong and Singapore for global investment banks such as Deutsche Bank and BNP-Paribas , told the BusinessMirror in a recent interview . <p> In these times of climate change and global warming , MRC Allied plays a vital role in helping the Philippines contribute and achieve a sustainable and green future by engaging in solar energy development projects . <p> MRC Allied will pursue solar development projects through its subsidiary @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ affiliates . <p> The 60-megawatt ( MW ) Naga Solar project in Naga City , Cebu , is on its pre development phase and can possibly be an electricity supplier to the Visayas grid and/or offer its production to the business sector within and around Southern Cebu . <p> Recently , MRC Allied Inc. , in collaboration with Sunray Power Inc. and Menlo Renewable got a service contract for a 100-MW Solar Project inside the New Clark City in Tarlac , a new development project of the Bases Conversion and Development Authority . The P11.75-billion project will potentially serve the energy requirements of the locators and facilities of NCC . <p> Moreover , MRC has acquired a 15-percent stake in the 50-MW Sulu Electric Power and Light Philippines Inc. solar project in Palo , Leyte , for $5 million , or P255 million . Cosio said the company recently had a meeting with a Japanese company for a potential exploration of a new technology on waste to energy . The technology converts biodegradable garbage and sewage waste into a combination of liquid , solid and methane gas . " We will be aiming for the low hanging fruit , " he pointed out . <p> " The beauty of the project is that it is subsidized by the Japanese government and is scalable , " he added . <p> Cosio said the device is housed in a 20-foot container and backed up by another container of the same size that will store the waste . MRC Allied is also interested to bringing into the country a technology that can compact plastic waste . Cosio said this is a very timely move as the Philippines has been recently ranked third biggest behind China and Indonesia as source of plastic waste found in the ocean , according to a 2015 report on plastic pollution by the Ocean Conservancy charity and the McKinsey Centre for Business and Environment . <p> Moreover , the report said the Philippines produce 2.7 million tons of plastic waste annually and 20 percent of that goes into the oceans . <p> " Again , it falls into the purview of social responsibility , investing in saving the environment , " he explained . <p> " There 's money to be made in that kind of investing , " Cosio added . <p> MRC Allied also wants to help the farmers in Nueva Ecija increase their income by introducing a solar-PV installation for rice mill buildings .
