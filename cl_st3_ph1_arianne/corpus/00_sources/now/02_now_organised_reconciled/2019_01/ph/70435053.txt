<p> The Lopez Group earned big wins at the recently concluded Asia Sustainability Reporting ( ASR ) Rating 2018 , held by the National Center for Sustainability Reporting ( NCSR ) , in collaboration with the Institute of Certified Sustainability Practitioners ( ICSP ) , in Bandar Lampung , Sumatra , Indonesia . The award was known in previous years as the Sustainability Reporting Awards . <p> Three Lopez companies brought home recognitions at the ASR 2018 . Conglomerate First Philippine Holdings ( FPH ) was awarded the platinum rank , while its subsidiary , leading clean power company First Gen Corporation , bagged the silver rank . Geothermal leader Energy Development Corporation ( EDC ) was awarded the gold rank . <p> The gold and silver ranks are awarded based on GRI Standards , while the platinum rank is awarded based on GRI Standards , disclosure on GHG emissions and energy , the SDG Compass , and as assured by a third party . <p> " The recognitions awarded to First Philippine Holdings , First Gen Corporation , and Energy Development Corporation validate the extensive efforts we have been putting into ensuring we have sustainable operations , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
