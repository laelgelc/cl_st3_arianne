<h> DepEd needs more funds to repair schools damaged by calamities <p> To be able to adapt to the changes brought about by climate change , the Department of Education ( DepEd ) is eyeing additional funds to be used for schools that are damaged by landslides and flooding even without typhoons . <p> In a phone interview with the Manila Bulletin , Education Secretary Leonor Briones said that additional funds were needed for the repair or rehabilitation of schools damaged by flooding and landslides . " We noticed that there 's a pattern now which is emerging , " she said . " We are used to typhoons and its damages so our funding is based on damages caused by typhoons but this time , we saw that the funding should apply even for tropical depression , " she said . <p> " The conclusion is that , the disaster is accelerating and this is not reversible at parami ng parami , " she said .
