<p> In a briefing , IRRI Plant Breeding Rice Breeding Platform Senior Scientist II Arvind Kumar said that in the Philippines , 90% of agricultural lands are planted to inbred seed , and only 10% at most are planted to hybrid varieties . <p> " Hybrid seed is supposed to give 10% or 15% higher yields as compared to inbred seed provided that farmers have access to better practices and knowledge about how to manage the hybrid seeds , " Mr. Kumar said . <p> According to Mr. Kumar , cost is also a factor for farmers choosing which seed variety to plant . <p> " Hybrid is still a bid costlier than inbred seed .
