<h> New initiative to drive sustainable finance in Singapore <h> Singapore sees launch of new initiative that will aid institutions to deepen their expertise in sustainable finance <p> By The Asset <p> WWF and partner organizations have launched Asia Sustainable Finance Initiative ( ASFI ) , which brings industry , academic , and science-based resources together to support financial institutions in implementing environmental , social , and governance ( ESG ) best practices . <p> WWF is secretariat to the multi-stakeholder platform that will aid Singapore-based institutions in deepening their sustainable finance expertise . The financial sector is crucial in creating resilient and sustainable economic growth through its ability to influence companies to adopt best practices and to direct financial flows towards sustainable development outcomes . As a key global financial hub , Singapore is an important conduit for capital flows into the Asia region , and as such its financial institutions have significant potential to shape resilient regional economies .
