<h> Opportunities for business to address climate change <p> Biodiversity Sri Lanka ( BSL ) and the Climate Change Secretariat of the Ministry of Mahaweli Development and Environment -- ( the National Focal Point for the UNFCCC ) -- will hold a learning event on ' Climate Opportunities for Business ' on January 24 from 8.30 a.m. to 5 p.m. at Hotel Janaki . <p> It will be conducted by recognised experts in the field and will explore four key concepts including climate financing opportunities , the national green reporting system , assessing climate change risks and vulnerabilities and the Sri Lanka carbon crediting scheme - all of which the private sector can subscribe to , with potential of adoption within their businesses to adapt to the threat of climate change . <p> BSL is a national platform set up to promote strong engagement of the corporate sector in biodiversity and environmental conservation , which facilitates the transfer of knowledge and technologies required to carry out functions by businesses , to support national priorities . <p> " Climate Change is a real phenomenon we experience on a day-to-day basis . Its impact is not only felt by us as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ they depend on . While international organisations and national governments are working hard to ensure the impacts are minimal , the effects can not be completely evaded , " the BSL stated in a press release . <p> Sri Lanka signed the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) in June 1992 and ratified the same in November 1993 . Since then we have been party to the obligations expected of Non-Annex I countries ( low-income , developing countries ) , which to a large extent are expected to adapt to the impact of climate change rather than to mitigate them , due to the comparatively low contribution they make towards the exacerbation of the global warming process . <p> The national agenda in this regard , includes the achievement of targets defined by ourselves by means of the National Adaptation Plan for Climate Change Impacts in Sri Lanka ( 2016 -- 2025 ) -- a document based on the National Climate Change Policy and Adaptation Strategy .
