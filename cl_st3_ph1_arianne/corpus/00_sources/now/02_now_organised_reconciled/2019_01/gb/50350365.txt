<h> This is the coldest permanently inhabited place on Earth <p> The Russian town of Oymyakon in eastern Siberia is home to 500 inhabitants who brave some of the planet 's harshest conditions . The town is so close to the Arctic Circle that even the national tipple , Vodka , freezes if left outside . <p> The town is named after the Oymyakon River , which confusingly means ' unfrozen patch of water ' and sees temperatures regularly fall to bone-chilling lows of -50C . A statue of a bull in the centre of town proudly records the coldest temperature of -71C in 1924 . <p> Only Antartica has recorded lower temperatures making this outpost of humanity the coldest populated place in the Northern Hemisphere . <p> While it may look like the ideal winter wonderland , spare a thought for the residents who get as little as three hours of sunshine ; and are forced to resort to more inventive ways to prevent life from becoming impossible . <p> Car engines instantly freeze unless they are kept running .
