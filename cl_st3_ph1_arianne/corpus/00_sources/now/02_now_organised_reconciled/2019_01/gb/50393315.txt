The first , established by Kuwait in 1953 , received a fixed percentage of the country 's vast oil revenues nearly a decade before the country gained its independence . The number of SWFs swelled during the 1970s , when most oil producers established ' stabilisation funds ' to protect their economies from the boom and bust of oil markets . <p> A second category appeared in the early 1980s , launched by countries enjoying large surpluses due to robust exports , such as Singapore .
