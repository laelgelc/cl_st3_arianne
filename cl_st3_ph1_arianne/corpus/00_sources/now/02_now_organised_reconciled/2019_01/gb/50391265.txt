<p> If you ever have time to kill at Terminal 5 , try leaving Heathrow and hopping on a red 350 bus . It 's a route that tells you something about how London has grown . Now it 's a mix of scrappy fields , sheds , hotels and car parks -- the detritus that makes a major world airport work .
