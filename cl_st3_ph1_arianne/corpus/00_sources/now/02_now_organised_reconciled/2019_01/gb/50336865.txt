<h> Breadcrumb <h> The end of incineration is near <p> Celebrations erupted across the city of Commerce in Los Angeles , California when it was lights out for its dirty incinerator . <p> No community should have to breathe in the pollution from other people 's garbage . <p> Environmentalists and community activists have claimed a major victory after the closure of an incinerator in Commerce , a city in the state of Los Angeles , USA - a location as famous for its smog as its celebrities . <p> The burner has been emitting contaminants above the legal maximum ever since it began operating in 1986 , damaging the lives of people in the already overburdened surrounding community , they argue . But the people fought back and finally convinced Commerce to not renew its contract with the facility . <p> Unfortunately , there are still 76 similar garbage-burning plants across the US , notorious for emitting way too much . They need to choose between a lock-in of decades of harmful pollution or leaping forward by closing the incinerators . That 's not as hard as it may seem . <p> Studies show that more than 90 percent of materials currently disposed of in incinerators and landfills can be reused , recycled , and composted .
