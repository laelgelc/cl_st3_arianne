Paris set the framework ; all countries must act collectively if the Earth 's temperature by the end of this century can be limited to 2.0 oC above that before the Industrial Revolution 150 years ago . What is noticeable is that no reputable scientist now disputes the phenomenon of Climate Change ; this was not the case ten years ago . Incheon raised eyebrows with their headline summaries . For example , ( 1 ) global temperatures are currently increasing at 0.2 oC per decade : we are already 1.0 oC above pre-Industrial levels , so could reach 1.5 oC within 25 years , ( 2 ) global emissions of CO2 need to fall by 45% from 2010 levels by 2030 and reach zero by 2050 if we are to hit 1.5 oC , and ( 3 ) the runaway greenhouse effect will kick in with disastrous consequences if the temperature rise by 2100 exceeds approximately 3.0 oC . <p> In mid-2017 I was asked to write the lead article on Greenhouse Gases for the 3rd edition of Encyclopedia Analytical Sciences , publish date February 2019 .
