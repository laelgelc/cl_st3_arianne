<h> LETTER : Urgent action on climate change <h> LETTER : Urgent action on climate change <p> On Saturday I joined almost 200 Islanders in a march through Newport to highlight the need for urgent action on climate change . <p> Despite a wealth of evidence , our environment is undergoing a slow-motion collapse , governments have , for the most part , carried on with business as usual . <p> This political inertia is n't surprising when we consider climate change most immediately affects poorer people in far-off lands and the governments focus on the more immediate needs of the economy . <p> It is , however , an unacceptable situation for a large swathe of the population who care deeply about the natural world and think beyond these shores and beyond the next election cycle . <p> Assuming that you do n't own an oil rig or power station there is very clearly no downside to improving the environment in ways that can simultaneously combat climate change . <p> Improving the environment means warm housing , locally produced food , vibrant accessible high streets and decent public transport .
