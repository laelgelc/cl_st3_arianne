It said that climate change is n't only influencing the composition of countries ' energy mix , but also the underwriting positions of large parts of the insurance market , as the major European carriers pull back from the coal-fired sector for environmental reasons . <p> This withdrawal by major insurers will hit independent and smaller coal operators the hardest , as they will be the most vulnerable to the lowered market capacity .
