<h> Hot , dry summers will increase demand for water <p> New Zealand 's recent heatwave is likely to repeat in future years as hot , dry summers become increasingly frequent -- requiring New Zealand to urgently plan to improve water storage to cope with future demand , says IrrigationNZ . <p> " New Zealand is water-rich by world standards , but we often do n't have enough water at the time it is most needed unless we store more water , " says Nicky Hyslop , IrrigationNZ Chair . <p> New Zealand 's recent average annual rainfall is estimated at around 550km3 by NIWA , and 2% of this is abstracted for human use . Just 1km3 is enough water to fill 400,000 Olympic size swimming pools . <p> " We experienced a relatively wet spring in many areas but in January our landscape has dried out .
