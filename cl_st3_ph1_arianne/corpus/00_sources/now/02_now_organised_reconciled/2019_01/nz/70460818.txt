Experts agree that a key component of plans to reduce emissions long term should involve slowing the rate of deforestation , particularly in places such as the Amazon and Indonesia , and planting more forests , too . <p> Hence , the Government 's plan to plant a billion trees by 2028 . But that scheme , although impressive sounding , will have limited effect by itself . Government documents released to the National Party late last year under the Official Information Act revealed that planting trees will reduce our liability under the Paris Agreement by $2.7 billion by 2030 , but only $900 million of that is expected to come from the One Billion Trees programme .
