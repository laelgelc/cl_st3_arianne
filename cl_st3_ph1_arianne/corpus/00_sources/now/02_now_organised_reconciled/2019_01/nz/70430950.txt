<h> Myanmar : UN expert expresses alarm at escalating conflict <p> The UN 's human rights expert on Myanmar expressed alarm at the escalating violence in northern and central Rakhine State and Chin State , and called on all sides to exercise restraints in use of force and to ensure the protection of civilians . <p> Since November 2018 the Myanmar military , known as the Tatmadaw , and Arakan Army ( AA ) , an ethnic armed organisation , have been engaged in heavy fighting , resulting in deaths and injuries to civilians . At least 5,000 people have been displaced from their homes . <p> " Both sides must take precautions and ensure the protection of civilians , " said Yanghee Lee , the UN Special Rapporteur on the situation of human rights in Myanmar . <p> She condemned an attack by the AA on the four Border Guard Police posts on 4 January 2019 , and expressed concern at the Tatmadaw 's disproportionate response to the attack .
