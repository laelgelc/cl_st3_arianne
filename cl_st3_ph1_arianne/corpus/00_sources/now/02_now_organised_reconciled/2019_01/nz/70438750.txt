<h> Report warns New Zealanders over hot weather risk <p> The increasingly hot weather , with the threat of climate change , poses serious risk to New Zealanders , it has been suggested . <p> Associate Health Minister Julie Anne Genter is urging Kiwis to plan for the spike in temperatures . <p> " Although it is great to enjoy the outdoors , getting too hot is a serious risk that will happen more in our future , " Genter said . " Our health will be impacted by climate change , and we need to ensure we are all ready . <p> " We need to plan for anticipated weather like hotter days now , " she added . <p> The minister highlighted that the number of days where temperature exceeds 25 degrees Celsius is expected to increase between 40% and 100% by 2040 and from 40-300% by 2090 . <p> In line with this , new heat health plan guidelines have been launched , aiming to assist service providers and councils in preparing plans and with predictions of weather variations .
