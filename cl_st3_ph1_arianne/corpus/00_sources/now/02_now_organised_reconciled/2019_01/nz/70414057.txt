Comparatively cruel fates should never be entertained , but the difference here is worth noting . Flooded islands lost to the rise of sea levels ; submerged hopes done by the relentless pounding of storms and water ; destroyed civilisations drowned by the supposed folly of the human species . These take a privileged if morbid position in the discussion on environmental catastrophe and climate change . <p> The more neglected aspect of modern discussion is the ice factor , and with that , its attendant literature .
