<h> Hunters ' Conservation Win With Marlborough Wetland <p> The contribution hunters have made to saving and restoring Marlborough 's largest surviving lowland freshwater wetland is being highlighted to mark 2019 World Wetlands Day . <p> World Wetlands Day is celebrated around the globe on February 2 to mark the 1971 signing of the Convention on Wetlands - the Ramsar Convention . <p> The theme of this year 's celebrations is " wetlands and climate change " , chosen to raise public awareness about the continuing threat to wetlands and encourage action to stop it . <p> The issue is particularly relevant in Marlborough and Nelson , which have lost more than 97 percent of their original lowland freshwater wetlands to drainage and development . <p> Marlborough 's largest remaining lowland freshwater wetland is now the Para Wetland . It sits alongside the main road between the Picton ferry terminal and Blenheim and is visible to hundreds of thousands of tourists every year . <p> A new billboard has been erected beside the Para for World Wetlands Day .
