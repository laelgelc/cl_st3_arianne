<h> Water pollution now public 's top concern <p> A new poll has found freshwater pollution is now worrying New Zealanders more than any other topic . <p> Photo : 123rf <p> The Colmar-Brunton poll was commissioned by Fish and Game and found 82 percent of people are concerned about the pollution of the country 's rivers and lakes , up 7 percent from last year . <p> The second biggest concern was the cost of living , followed by health . <p> People were asked how concerned they were about a range of issues , including the cost of living , health system , child poverty and water pollution . <p> Fish and Game chief executive Martin Taylor said New Zealanders connected deeply with their waterways so it was no surprise to see water pollution as the top issue . <p> He said the survey results meant the government had a mandate to make significant changes to help clean up the country 's freshwater . <p> " Kiwis are extremely worried that they are losing their ability to swim , fish and gather food from their rivers , lakes and streams , " he said . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the last 20 years , that right is being lost because the level of pollution in waterways has increased as farming intensifies .
