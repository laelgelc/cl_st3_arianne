<h> Water pollution tops list of concerns <p> More than 80 percent of those surveyed were either " extremely or very concerned " about freshwater pollution . <p> Kiwis are more worried about the levels of pollution in the country 's lakes and rivers than they are about the sky-high cost of living , according to a new opinion poll . <p> The Colmar-Brunton poll undertaken on behalf of Fish &amp; Game fishing and hunting lobby found 82 percent of those surveyed were either " extremely or very concerned " about freshwater pollution . <p> This was higher than the 80 percent who said they were very concerned about New Zealand 's cost of living . <p> Fish &amp;
