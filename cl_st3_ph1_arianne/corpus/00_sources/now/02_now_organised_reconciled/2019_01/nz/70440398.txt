<h> Solvency II : EIOPA looks at sustainability risks <h> Solvency II : EIOPA looks at sustainability risks Insurance Business <p> The European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority ( EIOPA ) has been asked by the European Commission to assess whether Solvency II presents either incentives or disincentives to sustainable investment . <p> To formulate its response , EIOPA is calling for evidence from market participants . <p> The regulator is gathering information on the integration of sustainability risks and factors in the prudential assessment of assets and liabilities for ( re ) insurers . It will examine how these risks affect investments , particularly when it comes to climate change . <p> Market practices on insurance underwriting will be collected as part of the assessment . Meanwhile national competent authorities are assisting EIOPA in seeking evidence . <p> Those who wish to submit information have until 11:59 pm CET on March 08 . <p> EIOPA said it will prepare the draft opinion for consultation during the second half of 2019 for submission to the European Commission in the third quarter .
