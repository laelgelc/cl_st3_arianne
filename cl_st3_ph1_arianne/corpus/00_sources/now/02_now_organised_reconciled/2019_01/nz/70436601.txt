<h> Tongan fibre-optic cable outage relieved by satellite <p> Auckland , 21 January 2019 -- Since 8:30pm yesterday evening ( Sunday 20th January ) Tonga Cable has been suffering an outage in the submarine cable network that connects the island nation to the outside world , plunging the island nation into digital isolation . The outage is ongoing and no timeframe is available for restoration of Tonga Cable 's service . <p> The sole international service available is currently provided via a Ku-band satellite network , operated by Kacific Broadband Satellite , and supplied in-country by ISP , EziNET . This is providing essential connectivity while Tonga Cable carries out repairs . Kacific is a provider of high speed satellite broadband and internet into the Pacific Islands for government , business and community purposes . <p> Tonga Cable Director , Paula Piveni Piukala , said that Kacific engineers are working with the company to provision additional internet and telephone capacity via satellite to enable priority communications during this period . <p> " We appreciate Kacific 's assistance , as Tonga currently has no other internet or mobile phone connectivity to the outside world , " he said .
