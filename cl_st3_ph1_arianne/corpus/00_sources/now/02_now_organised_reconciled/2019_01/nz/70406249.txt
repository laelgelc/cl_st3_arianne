<h> Myanmar opium cultivation continues to drop <p> Yangon ( Myanmar ) , 11 January 2019 -- The area of opium poppy cultivation in Myanmar dropped for the fourth year in a row to 37,300 hectares ( ha ) in 2018 , down 10% from the 41,000 ha recorded in 2017 , according to the Myanmar Opium Survey 2018 released today by the UN Office on Drugs and Crime ( UNODC ) . <p> Reductions in cultivation over the past year have taken place in practically all regions , but have been most significant in South Shan with a decline of 17% and Kachin State with a decline of 15% , followed by East Shan and North Shan declining 8% and 7% . With an average yield of 14 kg per ha in 2018 total opium production dropped from 550 to 520 metric tons , equivalent to approximately 53 tons of heroin -- with most destined for the regional drug market . <p> The report reconfirms the link between conflict and opium in Myanmar , with the highest levels of cultivation continuing to take place in unstable areas of Shan and Kachin states .
