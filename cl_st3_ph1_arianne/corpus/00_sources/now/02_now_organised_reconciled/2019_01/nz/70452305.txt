<p> While New Zealand might not be part of the European Union due to geographical limitations , we have a " priceless " friendship that will be made stronger by an upcoming trade deal , the EU Council president said last night as he shared a stage with Jacinda Ardern in Brussels . <p> Ms Ardern , who is in Belgium meeting with leaders of the EU , described the meeting with Donald Tusk as " an important meeting of minds and interests . " <p> Mr Tusk , in turn , described New Zealand 's relationship with the EU as excellent . <p> " Geographically we are far apart , but politically and economically we are close partners and friends , " he said . <p> Both share a belief in openness , co-operation , the rules-based international order and free trade , Mr Tusk added . <p> " We are currently negotiating an ambitious and comprehensive trade agreement between the European Union and New Zealand , " he said . <p> The agreement will have enormous strategic importance for the rules-based international order at a time when some are questioning it , he reiterated . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ against protectionism , " he said . <p> Tusk said that beyond trade , the EU and New Zealand have agreed to work together even more closely in many areas , such as peace and security , energy connectivity and climate change . <p> He finished by saying that in the current uncertain global environment , the friendship between the EU and New Zealand is priceless . <p> Ardern said the trade negotiations would create new opportunities for European and New Zealand businesses and help to fully realise the potential in trade and economic relations . <p> It was Ardern 's first visit to Brussels as Prime Minister .
