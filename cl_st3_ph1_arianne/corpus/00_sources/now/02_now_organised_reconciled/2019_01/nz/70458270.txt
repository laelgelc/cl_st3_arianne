<p> A new international report released today is calling for global action over obesity , under-nutrition and climate change . <p> The report says obesity , under-nutrition and climate change are usually viewed as separate but share " key drivers " and fuel each other . Photo : gcammarata/123RF <p> The Lancet Obesity Commission report was commissioned by the Lancet and co-chaired by Boyd Swinburn of the Auckland University School of Population Health . <p> It says malnutrition in all its forms , including obesity , under-nutrition and " other dietary risks " , is the leading cause of poor health globally . <p> It adds , " In the near future the health effects of climate change will considerably compound these health challenges . " <p> Specifically on obesity , the 62-page report says the prevalence of obesity is rising in every region of the world . <p> " No country has successfully reversed its epidemic because the systematic and institutional drivers of obesity remain largely unabated .
