<h> Six Young Hong Kong Fashion Labels in Focus <p> Creations from six Hong Kong fashion designer labels will be showcased at the " FASHIONALLY Collection #13 " fashion parade and three sessions of the " FASHIONALLY Presentation " at HKTDC Hong Kong Fashion Week for Fall/Winter 2019 . In addition , two other up-and-coming Hong Kong brands - ARTO. and YMDH - will take part in FASHIONALLY promotions for the first time , showcasing their 2019 Fall/Winter collections . Fashion Week is organised by the Hong Kong Trade Development Council ( HKTDC ) and runs from 14-17 Jan at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre ( HKCEC ) . <p> The three designers displaying their latest collections at FASHIONALLY Collection #13 are : Yeung Chin ( brand name : YEUNG CHIN ) , who runs a concept store at PMQ ; Jane Ng ( brand name : Phenotypsetter ) ; and Alee Lee ( brand name : ALEE LEE ) , who is launching her brand 's second collection . All three are ex-YDC ( Hong Kong Young Fashion Designers ' Contest ) finalists who studied at the Alternatif Fashion Workshop .
