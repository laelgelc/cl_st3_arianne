<h> SYRIA : Fears for children in Idlib as flooding hits camps <p> SYRIA : Fears for more than 11,000 children in Idlib as flooding hits camps <p> At least 11,000 children who have been forced to flee their homes because of war are facing a desperate plight this winter after torrential rains hit Syria 's Idlib province , says Save the Children. * <p> Heavy rains have led to flash flooding in camps for displaced people . Aid workers on the ground say conditions are miserable and dangerous for the thousands of children living in flimsy tents and half-built buildings with no windows or doors . <p> Reports of illness are increasing and in the worst-affected areas families are cut off from the outside world , unable to leave camps to access health facilities . Schools have also been closed in some areas .
