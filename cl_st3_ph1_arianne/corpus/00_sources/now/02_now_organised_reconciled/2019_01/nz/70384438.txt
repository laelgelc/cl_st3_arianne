<h> Guardian of our rarest plants <p> SAVIOUR : Graeme Atkins , East Coast Department of Conservation ranger , pictured at home near Ruatoria in the bush he has planted around his house . Graeme 's love of conservation has led him to plant numerous species around his house that are now extremely rare in the wild in the region he patrols . Pictures by Alan Gibson , NZ Herald <p> When Graeme Atkins was growing up in rural Ruatoria in the 1970s it was a long way to the doctor , especially on horseback . <p> So , if somebody got sick his kuia would send him off into the bush , with a list of natural medical supplies . <p> It led to a full-blown passion , something Atkins had to " hide " from his mates . <p> " It was hard case growing up .
