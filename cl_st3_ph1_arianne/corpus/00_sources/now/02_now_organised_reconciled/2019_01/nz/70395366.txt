The churches really need to take this on . That the voice of the churches , not only the churches but the grassroots communities , that means the island people . We need to empower these voices so that these voices become recognised in the international community . " <p> He warned that parts of Fiji 's main island , Viti Levu , were set to disappear because of rising sea levels . <p> " We can see it with our own eyes - the ocean levels are increasing each year , " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ matter of survival .
