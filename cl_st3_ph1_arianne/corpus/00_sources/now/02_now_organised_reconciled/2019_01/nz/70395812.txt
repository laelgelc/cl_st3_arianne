<p> Report from RNZ The Environment Court has given Wellington Airport more time to resubmit its resource consent for its runway extension plans . But the court says the substantial delays in the case reflected badly on the administration of justice and it was aware many participants had " had enough " . <p> In 2013 , Wellington Airport sought permission from the Civil Aviation Authority ( CAA ) to extend its runway by 355m to allow bigger planes to land .
