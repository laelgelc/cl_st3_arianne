" Branson asked in a recent blog post . " Well the real monsters facing the ocean are climate change - and plastic . <p> " Sadly , we saw plastic bottles at the bottom of the hole , which is a real scourge of the ocean . We 've got to get rid of single-use plastic . " <p> Located in Lighthouse Reef off the coast of Belize , the 124-metre deep , 318m-wide sinkhole is part of the UNESCO-listed Belize Barrier Reef System and a magnet for scuba divers . <p> About 300 feet ( 91 metres ) down , Branson , Cousteau and Aquatica Submarines ' chief pilot Erika Bergman came across a wall of stalactites - evidence , Branson says , of a cave system which would have originally formed on dry land . <p> " It @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
