<h> How catastrophe modelling can help insurers price risk <p> Catastrophe modelling has become a vital tool for insurers when analysing the scope of their risk , and risk modelling technology is constantly evolving to offer a more detailed picture of various risk profiles . <p> According to Robert Muir-Wood , chief research officer at RMS , catastrophe modelling can be vital in allowing insurers to quantify risks and determine likely costs -- however , it also faces the constant hurdle of uncertainty the further ahead it attempts to look . <p> " Catastrophe modelling is the original insurtech , " Muir-Wood explained . " It is the ability to put together the hazard , exposure and vulnerability and measure and quantify the risk cost , and we can use this modelling to plan how an entire city can manage its risk . " <p> " One can think about the consequences of climate change as those that are acute , and happen in terms of short and long-term scale events , and those that are chronic and yield a systemic impact , " he continued .
