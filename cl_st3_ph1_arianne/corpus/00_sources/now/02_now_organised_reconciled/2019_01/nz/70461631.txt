<h> Extra water source not needed , says Timaru District Councillor <p> One of the two reservoirs at Claremont . The Timaru District Council is currently in the process of evaluating its water infrastructure to ensure security of supply in the event of either a drought or a flood . <p> A Timaru District councillor has expressed doubt over whether the district needs an extra water supply as the council examines ways to ensure security . <p> Cr Kerry Stevens questioned the options in a report from council 's drainage manager Grant Hall at Tuesday 's infrastructure committee meeting that outlined various options to avoid running short of water in Timaru in the event of a flood . <p> The report presented five different options , which range from recommissioning the Landsborough Reservoir - last operative in 1984 - at a cost of about $6.5-$7.5 million , to installing new bores in Levels Plain at two to three sites , in conjunction with a new water treatment plant ( $21.9 to $23m ) . <p> SUPPLIED <p> Timaru District Councillor Kerry Stevens has expressed doubt about Timaru needing an extra point for water supply . <p> While most councillors @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , of Geraldine , was less sure . <p> " What I 'm picking up on is an illogical fear of the unknown , when in fact we have two reliable sources . We need to focus on resilience , " Stevens said . <p> He said that Auckland had only two main sources for its water , while Wellington had three . <p> " Our focus should be on the water we have . " <p> He said he was also worried that if the council implemented all five options , it could cost more than $68m , according to the report . <p> " I think we need to be very careful .
