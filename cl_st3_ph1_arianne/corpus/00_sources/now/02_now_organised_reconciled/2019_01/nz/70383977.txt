<h> Melting blocks of ice signifies climate change in Manawatu <p> Extinction Rebellion Manawatu hold a peaceful protest about climate change in The Square in Palmerston North . From left are Kirsty Porter , Fred Hirst , Maria Donoghue , Merlin Whiteman and Ava Thomas . <p> Melting blocks of ice in The Square in Palmerston North has been used to signify the melting of the polar ice caps . <p> A group of Manawatu people held a peaceful protest about climate change in The Square on Friday , waving placards , handing out pamphlets , writing messages in chalk on the pavement and leaving blocks of ice to melt . <p> It was part of a movement by a worldwide group , Extinction Rebellion , who want governments around the world to reverse environment-damaging policies and reduce carbon emissions . <p> Kirsty Porter helped organise the Manawatu protest . She said they wanted to have a " soft action " , similar to Extinction Rebellion 's non-violent demonstrations .
