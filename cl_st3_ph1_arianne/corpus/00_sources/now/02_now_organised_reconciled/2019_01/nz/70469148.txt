<h> New Speed Sign has " Huge Positive Impact " <p> A new speed sign has been installed at the southern end of Amberley . The sign is intended to target excessive speeds and provide a safer environment for a community hosting a busy state highway . The additional sign arrives following a speed testing project run by the Hurunui Youth Council ( HYC ) in conjunction with police . During this project the HYC recorded speeds through Amberley in excess of what is legal and safe . Their data provided strong support for the instillation . <p> Manager of Infrastructure and Assets at the council David Edge says the arrival of the new sign was achieved through the efforts of many .
