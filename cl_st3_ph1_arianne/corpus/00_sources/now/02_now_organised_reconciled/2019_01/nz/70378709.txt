Four percent of the respondents said they were not that concerned with the pollution of lakes and rivers . <p> The health system was third with 78 percent , followed by child poverty 72 percent , education and climate change both on 70 percent and housing 67 percent . <p> This is the second year Colmar Brunton has conducted the survey for Fish and Game .
