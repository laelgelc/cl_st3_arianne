<h> Researchers all ears as secrets of eels revealed <p> The ear bones of tiny eels might hold clues to their largely secret life . <p> They might also reveal answers about their migratory habits from New Zealand rivers across vast areas of the ocean , and the effects of climate change in the ocean , a Niwa freshwater scientist says . <p> Eimear Egan , whose fascination with eels began as a child when bitten by one , has assembled a specialist team for the three-year research project , backed by a government grant . <p> The team will be studying the ear bones of glass eels which are up to 7cm long to find out more about where their marine life originated , and the oceanic routes they took before entering our freshwaters . <p> Longfin and shortfin eels migrate down rivers to breed thousands of kilometres away in the western Pacific ocean .
