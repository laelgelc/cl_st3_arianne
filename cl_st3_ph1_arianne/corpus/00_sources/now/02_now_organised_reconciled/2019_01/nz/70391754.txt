<p> Analysis - The government is through its first year with the coalition firmly intact and the prime minister as popular as she was before the election . <p> Jacinda Ardern has climbed an astonishingly steep learning curve . Thrust into her party 's leadership just weeks before the election , she has grown into the job and now handles it with confidence at home and self-assured elegance abroad . <p> And she 's fireproof - there is n't anyone remotely likely to challenge her for the leadership , which she will almost certainly hold during the life of this Labour-led government . <p> There were doubts , in the beginning , about whether she and NZ First leader Winston Peters could forge a successful team .
