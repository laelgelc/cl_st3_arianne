<h> No threat to democracy : Zartaj Gul <p> RAWALPINDI : Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul Monday said that there was no threat to democracy and the country was heading in the right direction according to the vision of the Prime Minister Imran Khan . <p> Addressing concluding ceremony of three-day book fair organized here at E-Library Shamsabad , she said , strict accountability of those who looted the country 's wealth was being ensured . <p> Those who were making hue and cry were not involved in loot and plunder of the national exchequer then why they were crying , she added . <p> She said , there was no threat to democracy but , some elements were afraid of the accountability process due to their corruption adding , the country was facing monetary problems because of the bad economic policies of the past and incompetence of the past rulers . <p> The Minister said that kickbacks were taken on mega projects like Metro Bus and Orange Line Train .
