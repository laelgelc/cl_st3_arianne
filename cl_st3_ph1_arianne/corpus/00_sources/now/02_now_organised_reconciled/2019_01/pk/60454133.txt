Their certificates and medals were presented by Bai Chunli , president of the academy , in a ceremony in Beijing . <p> The winners of Scientific Cooperation Award are long-term participants and contributors to the academy 's scientific endeavors , academy Vice-President Zhang Yaping said during the ceremony . <p> " Various institutes from the academy should take advantage of opportunities presented by the Belt and Road Initiative and continue to collaborate with world-class foreign scientists , " he said . " This will allow us to create an innovative environment that is beneficial for producing research , talent and ideas , and accelerate the academy 's process of becoming a world-class research institute .
