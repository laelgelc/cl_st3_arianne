<h> Saplings to be planted under Green Pakistan program in GB , AJK <p> ISLAMABAD : Federal government is planning to plant millions of saplings across the Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu Kashmir under the Green Pakistan program during next five years . <p> " As many as 27 million saplings have so far been planted in all the four provinces including Azad Jammu Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan under the Green Pakistan Program " said an official of ministry of climate change . <p> A total of 100 million saplings would be planted under the ' Green Pakistan Program-Revival of Forest Resources ' during the next five years ( 2017-2021 ) with a total cost of Rs3.652 billion . <p> The ministry has got funding of US$37.46 million from Green Climate Fund for Glacial Lake Outburst Flood ( GLOF ) project in the Northern Areas . <p> The ' Clean Green Pakistan Movement ' has also been launched by the prime minister Imran Khan on October 8 and the Ministry of Climate Change is coordinating its implementation with all the provinces including AJK and GB . <p> The forest area of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has been increased by 0.348 million hectares after @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ The government plans to replicate the successful model of ' Billion Tree Tsunami Project ' all over the country , with a target of planting ten billion saplings .
