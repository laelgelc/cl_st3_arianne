He informed that the area under sugarcane cultivation had recorded a steep decline of 27 percent in Punjab this year whereas in Faisalabad it recorded 15 percent decline . <p> Explaining the statistics , he told that last year sugarcane was cultivated on 269,000 acres of land in Faisalabad whereas this year it cultivated on 227,000 acres of land . It would certainly affect the overall production of sugar in future which was currently being available in surplus quality to be exported . <p> He told that there are five sugar mills in Faisalabad with total crushing capacity of 29,800 tons per day . <p> Although the sugar mills have started crushing but they are unable to get enough sugarcane to run their mills with full crushing capacity .
