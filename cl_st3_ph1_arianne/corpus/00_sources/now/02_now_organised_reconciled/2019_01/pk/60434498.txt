<h> Masood urges youth to work for Kashmir cause <p> by APP , ( Last Updated January 12 , 2019 ) <p> MIRPUR : Azad Jammu and Kashmir ( AJK ) President Sardar Masood Khan Saturday urged the youth to devote their time and energies for the promotion of Kashmir cause and raise their voice for the oppressed and subjugated people of Indian occupied Kashmir ( IoK ) . <p> " I urge the youth of the country to work for the realization of the right to self-determination of the people of Jammu and Kashmir , who are being oppressed and brutalized and have been disenfranchised , " he said while addressing as chief guest at the inaugural session of the Model United Nations Conference organized by Public Speaking Society of the Institute of Public Administration ( IBA ) in Karachi , according to a press release issued here by the AJK Presidential Secretariat . <p> Sardar Masood said the people of IoK had been made alien in their own homeland . The occupation forces were killing , torturing and maiming the youth , while the women were being dishonoured .
