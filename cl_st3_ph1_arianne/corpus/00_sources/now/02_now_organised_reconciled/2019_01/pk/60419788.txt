<p> Ministry of Climate Change has chalked out a plan to plant over142 million saplings during the upcoming tree plantation campaign schedule to begin by the end of next month . <p> This has been stated by Advisor to Prime Minister on Climate Change Malik Ameen Aslam while presiding over an inter-provincial meeting regarding 2019 spring Tree Plantation Campaign in Islamabad on Tuesday . <p> The Advisor directed to choose a day in the last week of February for the campaign to be launched by the Prime Minister Imran Khan . <p> The Advisor said the ministry hopes to plant some five million trees on the very first day of the drive . <p> The meeting reviewed the target of previous year and current year for the tree plantation across the country . <p> The meeting was briefed that 12.85million plants were sowed during last year in Punjab , while 18.06 million in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa , 11 million in Sindh and 0.36 in Balochistan.
