<p> To enhance forest cover in Punjab , the Provincial Development Working Party ( PDWP ) has approved development funds of Rs32.426 billion for the ' Green Pakistan Programme -- Reclamation and Development of Forest Areas in Punjab ' . <p> The scheme was approved in a special meeting of the PDWP for FY2018-19 on Monday . The meeting approved the aforementioned amount as per revised estimates and up-scaling of phase-1 of the programme . <p> The meeting was presided over by the Planning and Development Board ( P&amp;D ) Chairman Habibur Rehman Gillani .
