To all Australians , particularly those who feel they are not being heard here in this place , I defer to Margaret Mead who said : ' Never doubt that a small group of committed people can change the world . Indeed , it is the only thing that ever has . ' ' ' <p> Ms McGowan , who has announced she will not contest the next election , is part of a fundamental change in our polity -- the rejection of mainstream parties and the associated rise of independents and minor parties .
