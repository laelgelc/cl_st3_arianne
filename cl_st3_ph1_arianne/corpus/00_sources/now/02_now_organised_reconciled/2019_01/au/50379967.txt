<p> Politicians of all persuasions , right around the globe , would claim to support/fight for/defend democracy and democratic processes . Yet , under this " umbrella " , they often act quite differently , indeed often undermining democracy in pursuit of their own personal interests , or to favour some particular vested interest , against the national interest , as defined by the will/aspirations of the people . Although democratic ideals are relatively easy to define , much is easily compromised , or lost , in the detail of its application . For example , it is the essence of most democratic processes that the " majority rule " , but this does not mean that the interests of the minority can , or should , be ignored . It is also fundamentally important to an efficient democratic process that the people are all fully informed about the issue on which they vote . Politicians are also wont to claim a " mandate " , when elected , even though the voter has not actually been fully informed .
