<h> Sir David Attenborough issues chilling warning about the ' frightening ' destruction of Earth <p> How urgent is the crisis of climate change ? " It 's difficult to overstate it , " says Sir David Attenborough . <p> In an interview with Prince William at the World Economic Forum ( WEF ) in Davos , Switzerland , the veteran broadcaster shared an alarming perspective not just on the future of the planet , but also the future of humanity . <p> The interview came after Sir David was honoured with a Crystal Award for his leadership in environmental stewardship ; in his acceptance speech , he warned the " Garden of Eden is no more " . <p> If ever anyone doubted Sir David 's authenticity ( why that would be the case is beyond me ) , they need n't look any further than the enormous collection of titles he has earned for his various achievements : Sir David Frederick Attenborough OM CH CVO CBE FRS FLS FZS FSA FRSGS . Phew , that 's a mouthful .
