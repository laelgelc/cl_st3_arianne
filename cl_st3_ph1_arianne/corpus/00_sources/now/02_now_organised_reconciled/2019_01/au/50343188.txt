<h> Climate and energy : both sides have some explaining to do <p> Australia is heading into another national climate change election in which carbon emissions reduction and renewable energy targets will be on the table along with other hardy perennials such as tax and welfare policies . Dispiriting though this may be , the big difference between the 2019 climate change election and those fought in 2007 , 2010 and 2013 is that this year 's election is going to be fought against the background of an energy system undergoing even more rapid change than in the past and a climate showing visible signs of man-made global warming . <p> In other words , industry and consumers are getting on with the energy transition with or without the government . The Morrison government is also confronting mounting evidence from the Wentworth by-election , the Victorian state election and its yawning 8-10 percentage point deficit in major opinion polls that its energy and climate change agnostic energy policies are not the vote winner government leaders once thought they were .
