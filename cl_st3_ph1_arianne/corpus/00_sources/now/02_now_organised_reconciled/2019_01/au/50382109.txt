<p> Greg Clark , the UK business secretary , has recognised that nuclear power is no longer cost competitive with renewable energy , but do n't expect any extra push into the cheaper technology . <p> There is easily enough solar and wind energy available to make up for the cancellation of the nuclear projects and to produce the low-carbon electricity required to make the UK 's 2030 carbon emissions targetsachievable . <p> Instead , however , the country 's incentives and regulations favour developing more power plants driven by natural gas .
