<h> City of Melbourne hits 100% renewables as 80MW wind farm comes online <p> The City of Melbourne has set a high bar for governments around the country , kicking off 2019 with the news that 100 per cent of the its operations are now powered by 100 per cent renewable energy -- an Australian first . <p> " We are immensely proud to be the first Australian capital city council powered by 100 per cent renewable energy , " said Deputy Lord Mayor Arron Wood in a statement . <p> Deputy Lord Mayor of Melbourne Arron Wood .
