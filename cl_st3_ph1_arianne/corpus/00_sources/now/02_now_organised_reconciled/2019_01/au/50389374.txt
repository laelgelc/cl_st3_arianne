<p> While I was having the time of my life leading a group of students through the sweltering jungles of Borneo in July last year , a new group of committed persons got together with Jacquie Svenson to put on the very well-attended Narratives of Climate Change Symposium . This has led to the formation of Newcastle Climate Change Response , a group of friendly passionate locals from all occupations wanting to build upon the inspiring and diverse threads on social change research and organising arising from the symposium . A few weeks ago , I attended my first NCCR meeting and found an eclectic group of some 30 persons of all ages and backgrounds . Initially I felt that I was unsuited to be part of the whirlwind of climate related conversations relating to all facets of actions that might save the planet . I soon realised that here was the genesis of what is really needed to help us all at the grass roots level understand what we need to do to slow the warming or our planet .
