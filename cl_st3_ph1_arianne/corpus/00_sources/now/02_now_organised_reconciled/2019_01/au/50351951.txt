Read more about the prize on the RMIT News website . <p> Meanwhile Academy Fellow and Murdoch Children 's Research Institute Professor Melissa Little will receive $936,221 from the National Health and Medical Research Council . <p> The funding will be used to further her research into the use of human stem cells to develop kidneys with functioning tissue as an alternative for renal replacement . The research will focus on the molecular basis of kidney development , renal disease and repair .
