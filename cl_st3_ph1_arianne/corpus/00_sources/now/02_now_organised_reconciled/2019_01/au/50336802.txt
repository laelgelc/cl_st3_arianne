<h> Paris Agreement costs Australia , but withdrawing costs more <p> Australia will pay a price in economic welfare if the world implements the Paris agreement on climate change , but will not be better off if it follows US President Donald Trump 's example and withdraws unilaterally , new research shows . <p> The research was conducted by top Australian National University economist and former Reserve Bank board member Warwick McKibbin and colleagues at ANU and the Brookings Institution in Washington . It examines the cost to each nation 's economy and welfare of implementing the Paris agreement . It then offsets the environmental benefits for each country or region to derive the net policy outcomes . <p> Research by top ANU economist Warwick McKibbin backs Scott Morrison 's refusal to bow to backbench pressure to pull out of the Paris Agreement . Michel O'Sullivan <p> The costs to Australia come overwhelmingly from other countries implementing the Paris agreement and , as a result , in effect taxing Australia 's coal and gas exports .
