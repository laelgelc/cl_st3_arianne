Its famous slogan , the best a man can get , has been given a 2019 makeover -- the best men can be . <p> You might think a cosmetics company lecturing men on how to be better people would n't be too shocking ( after all , they 've been doing it to women for years ) . But apparently it is . For all the accolades the Gillette ad has received , there has also been a backlash , #boycottgillette has been trending on Twitter and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ men . <p> So why would a company do it ? The answer is simple : millennials . <p> If you run a company today you need to be talking to millennials , the generation born post-1980 who came of age in the digital era . <p> By 2030 , millennials will account for 54 per cent of the Australian population . They will also bring in two in every three dollars earnt . <p> Selling to this generation has its challenges . Seventy-five per cent of millennials would rather spend their money on doing something than acquiring something . And they care . More so than any other generation millennials want the products they buy to align with their cultural and social values . <p> Camera IconBelle Taylor . <p> And those values ? Changing the date of Australia Day . Gender fluidity . #MeToo . Toxic masculinity . Sustainability . Climate Change . Pick just about any topic that is likely to get Cory Bernardi hot under the collar , these are the things millennials care about .
