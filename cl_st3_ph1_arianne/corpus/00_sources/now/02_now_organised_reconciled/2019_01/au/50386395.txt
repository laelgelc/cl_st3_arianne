<h> Underestimate Tony Abbott at your peril - the man knows how to win <p> In the government 's dark days following its near-death experience at the 2016 election , Christopher Pyne deployed his trademark flair to declare the Coalition an " election-winning machine " . <p> The proclamation fast became something of a joke but those three words perfectly describe the track record of the Liberal Party 's most polarising figure : Tony Abbott . <p> A 25-year political veteran , Abbott has fought and won nine elections in his blue-ribbon Sydney seat of Warringah - a byelection in 1994 and general elections in 1996 , 1998 , 2001 , 2004 , 2007 , 2010 , 2013 and 2016 . He fought and won a ballot to become opposition leader in 2009 , fought and nearly toppled Julia Gillard at the 2010 election , and was swept into The Lodge in a landslide in 2013 .
