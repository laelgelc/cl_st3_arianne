<h> Can this diet prevent the collapse of the natural world ? <p> Two years ago , 37 of the world 's top scientists got together to work out how to feed a future population of 10 billion people and avoid catastrophic climate change . <p> What they came up with was the Planetary Health Diet , launching in Australia today . <p> They say that if we all do it , we can prevent 11 million people a year from deaths caused by unhealthy eating and malnutrition , and avert the collapse of the natural world . <p> David Nabarro is a former Special Adviser to the United Nations ( UN ) Secretary-General and Strategic Adviser to EAT : the science-based , global platform for food systems transformation and Sandro Demaio is the Chief Executive Officer of EAT and founder of festival21 , a free celebration of community , food , and culture in Melbourne .
