<h> Livestock business helps Isiolo women pay fees , medical bills <p> A young woman with her two children and livestock in Laikipia- Isiolo border . file <p> Women in Isiolo county are taking up the male-dominated business of livestock keeping . <p> One of the groups , Horrijabesa Self-Help Group , buys emaciated animals from other livestock keepers for less than Sh40,000 . <p> Fatuma Wario , the chairperson , says the animals are fattened for two to three months . Then they are sold at Sh43-45,000 to the local market every Friday . <p> " We lease land and keep the animals to fatten . We deworm and vaccinate the animals and ensure they have enough pasture to feed for two to three months before we sell them , " she says . <p> Fatuma says they make a profit of Sh2-3,000 from one animal , and the profit is shared among members . In a good month , they make Sh50-70,000 from the sale of animals . <p> The group was registered in 2004 . The 16 members have been trained on savings , record keeping , developing business plans and starting other businesses .
