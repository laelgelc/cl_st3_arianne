<h> ' Festival vibe ' in court as public makes stand for climate action <p> January 22nd , 2019 <p> Court six in the High Court had a " festival atmosphere " this morning . This was according to one barrister , who , upon squeezing into the room at around 10.45am , was met with row upon row of filled seats . <p> While pews are usually taken up by fellow counsel waiting to be assigned a slot for a hearing , today was different as civilians , many of them university students , filled up the room . <p> They were there for one thing and one thing only -- to see the State in the dock over its climate record as the landmark Irish climate case brought by Friends of the Irish Environment kicked off today . <p> The journalists in the room , this one included , were clearly impressed by the numbers present .
