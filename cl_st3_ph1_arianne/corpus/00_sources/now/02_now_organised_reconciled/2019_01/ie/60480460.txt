<h> Climate change crisis - Murray 's green basin almost dusty outback <p> Saturday , January 26 , 2019 - 12:10 AM <p> In Eric Bogle 's great anti-war song ' The Band Played Waltzing Matilda ' his narrator " lived the free life of the rover From the Murray 's green basin to the dusty outback I waltzed my Matilda all over " before he was called , like hundreds of Irishmen , to fight at Gallipoli . <p> Neither he or Bogle could have imagined how like the dusty outback Murray 's green basin might be a century after WWI because of man 's misuse of nature 's gifts and climate change . Today , Murray 's green basin is choked , and dying because of poorly controlled abstraction to irrigate farmland eked out of a hostile environment and unprecedented temperatures . The catastrophe unfolds despite a river-system management plan put in place a decade ago to protect the resource .
