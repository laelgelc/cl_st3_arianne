<p> From a small premises in Harold 's Cross , richly decorated with African sculptures and paintings , the operations of the charity Aidlink are managed . Founded in Dublin in 1982 , Aidlink " exists to improve the lives of those living in poverty in Africa " . Currently working with partnerships in Kenya , Uganda and Ghana , Aidlink aims to improve the lives of some of the world 's poorest communities . <p> As an NGO ( a non-governmental organization ) , Aidlink focuses on improving educational environments , community health , agriculture and WASH ( Water , Sanitation and Hygiene ) . According to their 2017 annual report , Aidlink succeed in bringing cleaner , safer water to 15,000 people , providing " child-friendly " primary education to 8,500 children and training 677 farming families on how to protect their households ' food security . Due to the drought in Kenya , many children suffer from malnutrition and Aidlink was involved in bringing emergency food supplies to over 5000 children . Their ultimate goal is to help people , especially women and girls , to " achieve healthy and fulfilling lives .
