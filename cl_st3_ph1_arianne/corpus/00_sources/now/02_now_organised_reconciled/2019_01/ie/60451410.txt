<p> According to the study , Antarctica has lost forty million tonnes of melting ice annually from 1979 to 1989 . <p> This figure has risen to 252 billion tonnes of ice per year since 2009 , six times as much as it was losing 40 years ago . West Antarctica is the biggest sector for ice loss . <p> It takes about 360 billion tonnes of ice to produce one millimetre of global sea level rise , according to the study that based its conclusion on a trove of data of ice gains and losses across 65 areas of Antarctica . <p> Antarctica 's ice holds over 57 metres of potential sea-level rise .
