<h> Ogonnelloe NS Gives Meat the Chop <p> A SOUTH-East Clare national school is going to give meat the chop , as part of a move to save energy and help combat climate change . <p> Ogonnelloe National School will be going green by opting for an alternative to meat at least one day a week as part of the ' One Good Idea ' programme being run by SEAI Ireland . <p> The school was selected to participate in the initiative , after it came up with this innovative way to save energy and combat climate change . <p> Teacher Barry Kiely , who is the green school 's co-ordinator , said the children and community are rowing in behind the project . <p> " The children in Ogonnelloe put forward the idea of having a meat-free day once a week to cut down our meat intake to help save more energy and help combat climate change . Our idea was selected for the next round and we made the shortlist for the top 40 schools in Ireland , " he explained . <p> Ogonnelloe National School.Pic Arthur Ellis . <p> By being shortlisted , the project has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ now looking to get the local community involved in their effort ahead of a further selection process for the grand final , which takes place in Dublin on May 14 . <p> " We are running a meal tasting day in our local community hall to help encourage meal alternatives to meat and we are looking to get as many involved as possible , " Mr Kiely added . <p> The tasting day will be held on February 23 and will invite the community to taste a selection of vegetarian meals , while recipes for these will also be shared with locals so they can then go home and make them themselves . <p> The idea , Mr Kiely explained , is to reduce the energy output involved in putting food on plates and the impact the reduction in meat consumption can have on that . <p> The school has to conduct a number of tasks as part of the competitive process , including preparing a diary , making a presentation and a video .
