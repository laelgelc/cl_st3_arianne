<h> Six Longford projects in BT Young Scientist &amp; Technology Exhibition <p> With just a few weeks to go , Longford finalists of the BT Young Scientist &amp; Technology Exhibition ( BTYSTE ) are busily preparing their projects for the 55th annual Exhibition . <p> Taking place at the RDS , Dublin from January 9 to 12 , the brightest minds from across the island will gather under one roof to compete for the coveted title of BT Young Scientist &amp; Technologist(s) of the Year 2019 . <p> This year , Longford is represented by four schools from across the county , with six projects exhibited at the BTYSTE . <p> 1,137 students across Ireland submitted projects across four categories .
