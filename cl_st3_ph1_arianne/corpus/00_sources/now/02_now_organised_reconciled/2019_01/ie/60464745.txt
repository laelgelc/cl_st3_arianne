<h> Groundwater ' time bomb ' warning from scientists <p> Future generations face an " environmental time bomb " caused by the effects of climate change on groundwater , scientists have warned . <p> In many parts of the world it could take more than 100 years for the full impact of changes occurring today to be felt by groundwater systems , new research shows . <p> This has major implications for the future availability of fresh water for drinking , irrigation and industry , it is claimed . <p> Groundwater is water trapped underground in cracks and spaces in soil , sand and rock.Illustration
