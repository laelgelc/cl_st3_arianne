<p> North Cork is a region which is rich in tillage , pigs , dairy and beef , and it is readily embraced by its many customers . <p> That 's the view of IFA North Cork Chairperson , Anne Baker , as she responded to the recently published - and , for some , highly controversial - EAT-Lancet Commission , which recommended a 90 per cent reduction in red meat and milk consumption in the country . <p> While permitting variations based on local need and culture , the diet recommended in the report allows for an average of just seven grams of red meat per day - approximately half a meatball - and 500 grams of vegetables and fruits . <p> Daily poultry consumption would be confined to 29 grams - equivalent to one and a half nuggets - and fish to 28 grams , a quarter of a medium-sized fillet .
