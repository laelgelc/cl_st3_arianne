<h> Nature can save planet earth <p> Monday , January 07 , 2019 - 12:00 AM <p> By Donal Hickey <p> Everyone wants to save the planet from global warming , and the focus has been on reducing fossil fuels for energy creation . This trend is likely to continue , but it 's not the only way to combat climate change . <p> Actions to protect wildlife , to improve water quality in rivers and lakes , or to grow more native , broadleaf trees , so as to increase forestry cover , could also have an impact . <p> The Irish Wildlife Trust ( IWT ) has made a submission to the Committee on Climate Action , calling for recognition of the important role that better protection of nature can play in mitigating climate impacts and cutting greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Ireland , and the world at large , are also experiencing a nature extinction crisis : Both crises can be dealt with at the same time . There are two important ways in which better protection for nature can help us in our climate challenge , according to the IWT .
