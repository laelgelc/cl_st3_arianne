<p> Climate change is now a major threat to around 60% of wild coffees , experts have warned . <p> This includes the species used to make the popular caffeine drink around the world . <p> A total of 75 out of 124 wild coffee species globally face extinction due to the loss of forests , climate change , and fungal disease and pests . <p> Black coffee ( Image : Getty ) <p> The most popular one , wild Arabica , which originates from Ethiopia and provides around 60% of the world 's coffee trade , now faces extinction , scientists have warned . <p> Coffee farmers growing either Arabica or Robusta coffee have reported their crops being affected by the extreme changes in weather patterns , according to experts at the Royal Botanic Gardens at Kew , near London in the UK.
