<h> Modest rise in corncrake numbers but miracle needed <p> Friday , January 11 , 2019 - 12:00 AM <p> It is hard not to cheer the news that a modest increase in the population of calling , male corncrakes was recorded last summer even if that small reversal was a consequence of climate change and last year 's exceptionally -- for now at least -- warm summer . <p> The Corncrake Project Annual Report shows 151 calling males were recorded , an increase of 8% , the first since 2014 . Donegal and its islands held 90 , significantly 59 on islands . Another 39 were recorded in Mayo , 20 in Connemara and a lonely two in Sligo . <p> Sadly , none were recorded in the Shannon callows , once a species stronghold , for the fourth year . Like that river 's once-great run of portmanteau salmon , Shannon corncrakes have succumbed to progress . <p> Our corncrakes are still in a precarious position , painted into an ever-tighter corner of this island . Their survival , much less their restoration , would be a miracle . <p> That tragedy offers many lessons but one @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ too long before we intervene to try to sustain struggling species .
