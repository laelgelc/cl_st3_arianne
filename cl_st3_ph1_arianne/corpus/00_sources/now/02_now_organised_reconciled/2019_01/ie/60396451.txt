<h> Tara takes the plunge for mother nature <p> Dr Tara Shine , Kinsale , preparing for her month long expedition to Antarctica with her children , Nathan and Lauren . ( Photo : Adrian Herlihy/John Allen Photography ) <p> A KINSALE based environmental scientist is joining 80 women from around the world on an expedition to Antarctica . <p> Dr Tara Shine , co -- founder of Plastic Free Kinsale and the award winning social enterprise Change by Degrees , left Ireland on the 27th of December to join the select group of female scientists from around the world on the leadership expedition to what 's one of the most ecologically sensitive places on earth.Tara , an advisor on climate change , is the first woman living in Ireland to be selected for the programme . <p> The expedition is part of Homeward Bound , a global women 's leadership programme that aims to heighten the influence and impact of women with a science background to influence policy and decision making as it shapes our planet . <p> The mum of two who is originally from Kilkenny , applied to be part of the trip by filling in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ had to say why I wanted to be part of the programme and what I would do as a result of all the leadership training . <p> ' So some combination of my experience , passion and vision for change worked and they chose me ! ' <p> In her video , which she made at Sandycove , which she describes as her ' sacred place , ' she says she 's ' part policy-geek , part idealist and part adventure and part scientist . ' <p> ' I need to develop all those skills to be able to have the change that I want in the world . <p> ' I 'm driven by a strong sense of justice and injustice and do not think it 's fair that people that suffer the worst consequences of climate change are those that are the least responsible , particularly women , and I believe that as women we can change the world . <p> ' We have a different way of doing things that is needed to shape a new way of living on planet earth . By building leadership and communication skills I 'll be better able to do that .
