<h> Greens see surge in support in election in rebel county <p> The Green Party is set to witness a significant surge in seats in local elections in county Cork , early tallies have revealed . <p> According to initial tallies , the Greens are set to occupy as many as four seats on Cork City Council , a significant gain for the party . <p> Seasoned party politician Dan Boyle alongside younger members Oliver Moran , Lorna Bogue and Collette Finn are holding a fixed position in the city 's local elections . <p> Early tallies from Dublin , Kildare , Limerick , and Wicklow also reveal that the Green Party is set to do well there also . <p> Overall , the party looks set to be the big winners in the local elections , with early tallies confirming the estimates in yesterday 's RTE exit poll that the party could take in nine per cent of the vote . <p> Councillor Roderic O'Gorman , who is expected to gain a seat in Dublin West , told RTE earlier today that the party expects to win at least 30 of the 82 seats it is contesting . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 's electoral success is " a very clear message " from the public that they " want us to do more on climate action " . <p> Lorna Bogue ( l ) of Green Party Photo : Shamim Malekmian <p> Message clear on the doors <p> Speaking to The Green News , Green Party candidate , Lorna Bogue said that she was not expecting to gain such levels of support from voters . <p> The young politician said that " the issue of climate change was coming at people 's doors again and again " , adding , however , that the Green 's favourable position is not entirely due to the current , heightened public attention the issue . <p> Ms Bogue said that party campaigners had gone door to door to convey their message to voters for months and the results prove that the public has responded well to their relentless campaigning . <p> " We 've been running all over the country canvassing really hard ; you have to talk to people , " she said .
