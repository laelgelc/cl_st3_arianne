<h> Get up , get out and vote - Why your vote is important today <p> Just over half ( 1.7 million ) of those people eligible to vote actually made it their business to go to their local polling station and vote in the 2014 Local Elections . <p> A similar number did n't vote while many people feel disillusioned with local , national and European politics , it is important to have a say in 2019 . Get to know the candidates and what they offer in terms of representation because the role they play will have a massive impact on your life . <p> Take the local elections for example . The councillors hoisted above their supporters ' heads at count centres up and down the country this weekend will decide many facets of local life in your area over the next five years . <p> They vote on development and strategic plans which will filter policy into every area of towns and villages ; roads , housing , public amenities , health and more . They decide on the council budget which funds projects that will either inhibit or help your area . It 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ attention . <p> Councillors sit on various committees discussing issues like rural crime with garda ? ; rural isolation , agriculture , footpath and road works , health and housing . What they bring to those meetings is shaped by their interactions with constituents , so it pays to be in their ear and engaged . <p> With all this back-and-forth in mind , it also pays to know these individuals are the right people to act on those concerns , and that 's why we need to vote . <p> The next time you complain about something under the auspices of the council in your area , make a note of it ; make a call to your local councillor and demand action . That is their job ; to serve you and the community and bring your interests to the fore at decision time . You can put the right person in that role by voting on Friday . <p> Voting for European candidates is just as important , perhaps more on a national scale for Ireland , but nonetheless , decisions made in Brussels will filter down @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the work carried out by an MEP is shaping European legislation at committee level and subsequently voting on said legislation . These laws then become binding across the European Union . <p> MEPs deal with the big issues in the areas of European funding for various projects which are then acted upon by national governments , while also deciding on employee conditions , healthcare policy , agriculture regulation and funding , telecommunications costs and the environment . <p> Climate change is one of the biggest issues facing the EU and decisions made at the European Parliament will have a major impact on the entire world . The MEP you vote for on Friday will have a role to play in that and all legislation formed at EU level by way of the representations they make on Ireland 's national and regional interests . <p> Get to know the candidates , even if they are not from your county or immediate region , and send a voice you trust and believe in to represent us in Europe . <p> We are also tasked with voting in another referendum this Friday , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the regulation of divorce . Voting in a referendum is always important as it seeks to amend our Constitution , the fundamental mechanism affecting the laws of this country , laws that can only be changed by way of referendum . <p> In this case , the proposal is about two issues relating to divorce , namely how long people must be living apart before applying for a divorce , and the recognition of foreign divorces . <p> There will be one question on the ballot paper and voters can either vote Yes to allow both changes , or No to reject both changes . Voters can not accept one change and reject the other . <p> By voting ' Yes ' who will give the Oireachtas the powers to legislate on both issues . By voting ' No , ' no such legislative action will be permitted and the Constitution will remain unchanged in relation to divorce . <p> All of these votes will affect the lives of Irish people , both directly and indirectly . Therefore , an examination of the candidates and their priorities is essential @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a welcome difference . <p> Voting is the most basic , but most effective way , for us as citizens to affect change in our country , counties and communities .
