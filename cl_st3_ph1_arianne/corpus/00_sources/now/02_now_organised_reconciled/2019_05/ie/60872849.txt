It identifies weeds in between rows ( so you do need a certain amount of space in between crops ) and applies the right amount of agent only onto the weeds and not the crop . " That saves the grower money , since less agent is needed , there is no residue on the crops and it 's a way to fight resistance to crop protection products , " says Ms Brand . <p> The philosophy behind these user-friendly tools is that growers should be stimulated to adopt new technologies .
