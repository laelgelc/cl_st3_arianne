Largely rural , it is headquartered in the ancient capital of the Royal County , Trim town , and like the three urban areas in the last local elections , the electoral map had changed since the previous vote , due to the abolition of the town councils and their amalgamation into the local municipal areas . <p> This meant that councillors who were sitting on town councils were now competing with county councillors - if they did n't have a dual mandate . Last time out , there were 13 candidates for the six-seater ; this year , here are 11 vying for the six seats in the Trim area .
