<h> Bealtaine Living Earth Biodiversity Festival in the South East breaks records <p> Reporter : <p> WaterfordLive.ie <p> 27 May 2019 <p> Email : <p> news@waterfordlive.ie <p> 0 <p> Bealtaine Living Earth Biodiversity Festival in the South East breaks records <p> This year 's South East Bealtaine Living Earth Festival was a record breaking success . <p> Participants were really engaged and genuinely anxious to play their part in protecting Ireland 's biodiversity , organisers say . <p> More than 2,500 people aged from three years to their late 70s took part in over 80 free events scattered across the South East , many of them at new venues in Carlow and Wexford . <p> " The penny has really dropped when it comes to biodiversity . The people who supported our many events were aware that Ireland had declared a biodiversity and climate emergency . And what is even more encouraging is that they wanted to know how they could help . They wanted action and not just words . <p> " They want more of the facts behind the research and actively seek to engage . It was really encouraging and it shows us that the work @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ over the past 15 years is paying off . " <p> Among the most popular events this year were the Climate Change talk , a citizens dialogue with head of forecasting at Met Eireann Evelyn Cusask , EPA 's Dr Jonathan Derham and Waterford Institute of Technology ( WIT ) ' s Dr Nick McCarthy , as well as walks and talks in the region 's top gardens and parks -- all havens of biodiversity . <p> " This was definitely the most engaging and encouraging Bealtaine Living Earth Festival ever . There has been a major change in people 's level of engagement this year . We hope to more with even more partners across the counties next year , particularly our libraries who were running workshops for primary schools this year together with WIT postgraduates . <p> " There is an appetite for information and for real and genuine action from the public , and we will continue to engage with them . " <p> The coordinators of Bealtaine Living Earth Festival are Calmast , WIT 's science outreach centre . Bealtaine Living Earth Festival has had over 20,000 participants in its 15 year history .
