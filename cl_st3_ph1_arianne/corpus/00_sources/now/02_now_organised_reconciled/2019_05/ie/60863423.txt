<h> Dubai Chamber to participate in world congress in Brazil <h> DUBAI , 8 days ago <p> Dubai Chamber of Commerce and Industry will join members of the global chambers community at the 11th World Chambers Congress ( WCC ) to be held next month , in Rio de Janeiro , Brazil . <p> The event will take place from June 12 to 14 , at the Windsor Convention Centre . <p> The WCC is the world 's largest gathering of chambers of commerce and the upcoming congress in Rio will be attended by 1,200 participants from 100 countries , including chamber leaders , business heads and key government figures . <p> Dubai Chamber is supporting the 11th WCC as a Diamond Sponsor and exhibitor . From its stand at the congress , Dubai Chamber will showcase Dubai 's competitive advantages and reputation as a global business hub . <p> In his capacity as chairman of the ICC World Chambers Federation , Dubai Chamber 's president and chief executive officer Hamad Buamim will address chamber leaders at the opening ceremony of the congress , where he will highlight the importance of cooperation in addressing key challenges facing the global @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ competitiveness of chambers and their respective members . <p> In addition , Buamim will participate in high-level meetings and key sessions throughout the three-day event . <p> More than 80 speakers will take part in 25 interactive discussions , sessions and workshops during the Congress which will be held under the theme " Creating a Shared Future " and tackle a wide range of timely topics and issues such as digital transformation , trade benefits of blockchain technology , e-commerce opportunities , action taken by chambers on climate change and the changing dynamic of education and workforce talent , among other important matters . <p> The World Chambers Federation ( WCF ) is the ICC 's global forum uniting the global network of 12,000 chambers and their respective business communities . <p> The WCF provides a platform for chamber leaders to communicate and collaborate with each other on matters of mutual interest and facilitating beneficial partnerships , it stated . -- TradeArabia News Service
