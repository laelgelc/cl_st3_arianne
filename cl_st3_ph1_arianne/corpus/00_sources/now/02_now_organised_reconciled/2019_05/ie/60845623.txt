<p> A NEW innovation hub in Kilkenny city can drive the digital transformation of Europe 's Agri-food sector , EU Agriculture Commissioner Phil Hogan said today . <p> The former Kilkenny TD and minister was speaking at the launch of the Precision Agriculture Centre of Excellence ( PACE ) at the Lyrath Estate this morning . <p> Commissioner Hogan said : " This impressive new hub gives Ireland 's agri-tech companies , most of them SMEs and micro-enterprises , direct access to best-in-class technologies and research , as well as cascade funding . " Smart use of knowledge , research and innovation is the main source of productivity growth in the EU agri-food sector . The digitisation of the European economy requires the full integration of digital innovations across all sectors of the economy , including agriculture and food . " <p> Commissioner Hogan said Precision Agriculture has " never been more important " in an industry facing challenges posed by climate change , ecosystem degradation and world population growth , as well as the growing need to produce more , using less . <p> He added : " With facilities like PACE , we are building a network @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ transformation .
