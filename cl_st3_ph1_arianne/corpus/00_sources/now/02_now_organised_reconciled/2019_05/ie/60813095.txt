<p> Airports say reducing or scrapping APD would encourage more airlines to pursue operations in Scotland , boosting the country 's economy and tourism industry . <p> Edinburgh airport chief executive Gordon Dewar branded the decision " reactionary " and accused the government of leading airports and airlines " down a path of failed promises for three years " . <p> " It also raises questions about continued support for our tourism sector when airlines have already walked away from Scotland due to this failure to deliver , " he added . <p> A 2015 report by the airport suggested cutting APD or ADT by 50% would create nearly 4,000 jobs , add ? 1 billion to the Scottish economy by 2020 and prevent a loss in tourism of ? 68 million a year to the country . <p> Halving the tax and eventually abolishing it was an SNP manifesto pledge in 2016 .
