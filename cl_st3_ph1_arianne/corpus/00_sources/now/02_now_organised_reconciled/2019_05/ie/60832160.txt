<h> The 15 cities doing more than most to cut carbon <p> Cities , which are home to more than half the world 's population , are stepping up efforts to slash pollution , often wresting the fight against climate change away from national governments . <p> That 's the conclusion of CDP , a group that pushes institutions to detail their greenhouse-gas emissions . Often able to move faster than their national counterparts , metropolitan authorities from London to Sydney and Boston are among a group of 15 setting out the most rigorous plans to achieve carbon or climate neutrality by 2050 . <p> The moves are evidence of ambition by local authorities to do their part in reining in global warming , almost two-thirds of global emissions come from cities .
