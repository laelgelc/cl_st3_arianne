<p> A team of transition year and fifth year students from a number of schools around Dundalk will come together in the Market Square this Friday to hold a school climate strike . <p> The protest will take place at the Square from 1pm to 3pm on the day . <p> It has been organised as part of the global " Fridays for Future " Campaign , initiated by Greta Thunberg , a Swedish 16 year old activist , to combat government inaction on climate change . <p> Commenting on the event , De La Salle student Donnchadh Hughes told Talk of the Town : " We have taken it upon ourselves to hold the second protest of its kind in Dundalk in recent times , following on from one held in March of this year . <p> " There will be numerous similar events held across the country this Friday . <p> " More information can be found at @FFFIreland on Twitter or on our Instagram Page , @fridaysforfuturedundalk . There is also a Facebook event page . "
