" <p> ' Transformative change ' <p> For the first time , the UN body has ranked the top five causes of species lost and the degradation of nature . <p> These include : <p> Turning forests , grasslands and other areas into farms , cities and other developments resulting @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the world 's oceans -- one-third of the world 's fish stocks are currently over-fished . <p> Permitting climate change from the burning of fossil fuels to make it too hot , wet or dry for some species to survive . <p> Polluting land and water .
