<p> Some of us were adults in 1986 , some of us were children or teenagers , and some of us did n't yet exist . Because of that , we all have different memories of Chernobyl -- the world 's deadliest nuclear disaster . <p> That did n't stop the screenwriter Craig Mazin , or the director John Renck , from creating the five-part mini-series Chernobyl , and it did n't stop American network HBO , and the British network Sky , from producing the series . <p> Chernobyl is certainly a change of pace for both Renck and Mazin . Renck had directed episodes of the TV series Breaking Bad and The Walking Dead . Mazin had written the Hangover and Scary Movie movie franchises . <p> For those who do n't know much about the disaster , the series is an eye-opener .
