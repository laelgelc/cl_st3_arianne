<h> Renewed bid for fracking license in Fermanagh <p> May 9th , 2019 <p> An Australian energy firm has renewed a licensing process that could lead to fracking for natural gas in Belcoo , Co Fermanagh . <p> Northern Ireland 's Department for the Economy launched a new consultation on Tamboran Resources ( UK ) Ltd 's application for a licence to evaluate the natural gas in the shale and sandstone rocks in the south-west of Fermanagh on Tuesday . <p> Fracking or hydraulic fracturing is a process for extracting natural gas by drilling into rocks and injecting pressurised water , sand and various chemicals to force out the gas . <p> The gas , formed through the decomposition of dead organisms in the rocks , can then be captured and used as a source of energy . <p> It is believed that fracking may contaminate the environment , compromise humans ' health by polluting drinking water and trigger tremors or minor earthquakes . <p> The Republic of Ireland is part of a small cluster of countries that have imposed a ban on the controversial practice . <p> A five-year study published by the Environmental Protection Agency in 2017 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ environment and human health . <p> Protest in now DUP leader , Arlene Foster to rescind Tamboran 's gas-probing licence . <p> In light of the recent announcement , activists have vowed to unite against the project 's potential renewal . <p> "
