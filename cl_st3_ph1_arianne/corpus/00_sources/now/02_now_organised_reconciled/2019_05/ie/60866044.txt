<h> Feeding demand for less meat in diet <p> Concern about climate change is increasing the demand for vegetable products and leading to a rise in so-called " flexitarianism " , according to Shane Ryan , founder of vegan start-up Fiid . <p> He says flexitarians , or semi-vegetarians , who reduce their meat intake and eat more vegetable products , are driving much of the growth in the plant-based food product market which he says is predicted to increase by 43% by 2023 . <p> " Figures show 73% of plant-based food products are now being bought by consumers who are neither vegans nor vegetarians , " says Mr Ryan , adding that consumers are being influenced by health concerns and by reports linking animal agriculture to climate change . <p> The increased demand for vegetable-based products has led to supermarkets adding new vegan and vegetarian ranges and provided a perfect climate for the launch of Fiid 's new range of vegan lunch bowls at the end of last year . <p> " We launched in December selling three times more than expected in our first month .
