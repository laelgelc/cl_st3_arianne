<h> SIPTU to support climate action strike and protests by secondary school students on Friday <p> Reporter : <p> Leitrim Observer Reporter <p> 23 May 2019 <p> 0 <p> Tomorrow secondary school students across the country are organising rallies to highlight climate change . <p> SIPTU activists will be supporting secondary school student groups in organising major rallies across the country during their strike for action on climate change , which is taking place tomorrow Friday , May 24 . <p> SIPTU Global Solidarity Campaign Secretary , Yvonne O'Callaghan , said : " Our union is built on the belief that through collective action real and progressive change can be achieved . Our activists are honoured to have been asked to assist a number of grassroots organisations of young people who are focused on ensuring that there is effective action taken to deal with the existential crisis that is climate change . <p> " In relation to the strike on Friday , the students have agreed that SIPTU activists will assist with the logistics and stewarding of what are likely to be large demonstrations in Cork and Dublin .
