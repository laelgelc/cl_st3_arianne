<h> Starbucks , Mercon Announce Separate Sustainability Loans <p> Starbucks and Mercon Coffee Group have separately announced sustainability-focused loan facilities , signaling the coffee sector 's growing emphasis on sustainability as climate change and low global prices threaten the future of the crop . <h> Starbucks Bond <p> Starbucks announced a $1 billion sustainability bond , its third such bond , but largest to date , proceeds of which will go to supporting the purchase of ethically sourced coffee , farmer support programs , and the building of greener retail stores . <p> Morgan Stanley acted as the sustainability structuring agent and joint book-running manager on the transaction . <h> Mercon Credit Facility <p> Netherlands-based Mercon announced a $450 million sustainability-linked senior secured revolving credit facility , led by Rabobank , which will specifically fund initiatives related to sustainability , such as programs through which Mercon will help offer technical assistance to farmers . <p> Organisations and governments around the world are increasingly looking at how to support coffee farmers as coffee futures languish near 13-1/2-year lows because of a global glut . <p> Meanwhile , climate change is making it difficult to cultivate coffee in parts of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ many coffee species with extinction . <p> News by Reuters , edited by Checkout .
