<h> ICMSA : ' Vote for candidates who support farming ' <p> The rural electorate is being called on to give their votes to candidates who have shown a commitment to farming and agriculture in the upcoming local and European elections . <p> Pat McCormack , the president of the Irish Creamery Milk Suppliers ' Association ( ICMSA ) , said that this month 's elections are an opportunity for the candidates to demonstrate their support for farmers , particularity in the area of the environment and climate change . <p> McCormack argued that these candidates must acknowledge that farming is " part of the solution " to climate issues , and not a " scapegoat for fears being hyped up by people who themselves have no viable answers or solutions " . <p> The last year has seen three major issues come to the fore at national and international level , any one of which , on its own , has the potential to shape for good or bad the very future of Irish agriculture . <p> These three issues were , according to McCormack : Brexit : the Common Agricultural Policy ( CAP ) ; and climate @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and climate change , either taken on their own or as a package , will decide whether we will have a rural economy at all , " claimed McCormack . <p> He pointed out that there is a " clear issue " with the vulnerability of the local economy , and that , despite the delay to Brexit , the prospect of a ' no-deal ' is a looming danger . <p> " It is difficult to exaggerate the negative implications for the whole economy and very specifically , farming and rural areas , in the event of a no-deal or ' hard ' Brexit , and we need to see real actions on this issue .
