<h> Union calls on Govt to oppose potential visit by Donald Trump <p> The Unite trade union has urged the Government to oppose any proposed visit by US President Donald Trump to Ireland . <p> Delegates at the Unite conference in Dublin overwhelmingly backed a motion committing members to oppose any such visit , and criticising Mr Trump 's policies as " racist , sexist , homophobic , warmongering and dangerous . " <p> Delegate Memet Uludag described the President as an " equal opportunities oppressor " , saying there was not one section of society , national or international , that he had not offended , targeted or attacked . <p> He also called Mr Trump the leading climate change denier , and claimed he was happy to let climate change " go crazy " at the expense of millions of lives of workers and poor people . <p> Mr Uludag stressed that the motion did not mean that Unite was attacking American people and their choice . <p> He said they stood in solidarity with the Black Lives Matter and Me Too movements , as well as campaigning against racism . <p> He accused Mr Trump of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ being an " anti-union man " - and urged trade unionists to tell him that he was not welcome in this country . <p> Mr Uludag urged delegates to tell their political leaders that Mr Trump was not welcome to this country with his " toxic " views - and to engage with a coalition of 50 organisations which is mobilising to oppose any potential visit . <p> Francis Loughlin said listening to Mr Trump was like listening to a script from Sesame Street . <p> He noted how well the New York Stock Exchange was performing , but said that was at a cost . <p> He accused Mr Trump of eroding the Environmental Protection Act and allowing American business to " play dirty " - which would mean mortgaging the future of the planet for short-term gains at home . <p> He also cited the erosion of workers ' rights , with the undermining of health and safety laws , benefits , working hours and pay to make it easier for big business to make big profits . <p> RT ? . ie is the website of Raidi @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Public Service Broadcaster .
