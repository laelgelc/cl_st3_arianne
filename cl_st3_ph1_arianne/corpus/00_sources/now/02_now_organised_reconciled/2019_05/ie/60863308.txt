Its consequences will affect communities all over the world , not least of all in Ireland and in particular rural communities , " said Seamus Boland , Irish Rural Link CEO . <p> " The Irish Government , having signed the Paris agreement , must introduce a range of measures , including a significant increase of carbon tax and a significant reduction across all industry of carbon emissions , especially in agriculture -- the single biggest industry in rural Ireland , " he said . <p> It is expected that we all will need to change and adapt our current lifestyles in a way that will dramatically alter our lives .
