Danielsson <h> Leadership Conference in Latin America opens with context analysis and insights from member churches <p> ( LWI ) - Vibrant churches who are visible and present in their communities , empowering youth and women , and working on human rights , gender justice , climate justice , and migration , were in focus on the opening day of the Latin America and the Caribbean ( LAC ) &amp; North America Leadership Conference . The conference , which takes place in Lima , Peru , from 10-14 May brings together close to 60 church leaders from the LWF member churches in these two regions . <h> Learning from the ministry of Jesus <p> The conference opened with two keynote speeches on the context of the Lutheran churches in Latin America and the Caribbean and North America . He said the data indicates there is much inequality in the region and a high level of mistrust in political institutions . On the positive side , there is general trust in religious institutions . About 39 million people in the region are malnourished , he noted , and daily survival is difficult for many .
