<h> Bus diversion a sign of calamitous planning by TfL <p> ALL the signs point to what can only be described as the decline of Transport for London . <p> Is TfL up to the momentous job of transforming transport and street usage by vehicles in the face of the threatening climate change ? <p> A revolution in transport is essential . It will , of course , never be achieved unless it is planned centrally by the government . <p> The introduction of electric cars is a case in point -- this will not come about without the full co-operation of manufacturers , the lowering of costs , the changing habits of consumers -- as well as sound planning by transport authorities such as TfL . <p> But TfL appears to fail when it comes to public consultation .
