<h> Athy students take part in Fridays for the Future march <p> SECONDARY students from Athy 's two secondary schools , Ardscoil Na Trionoide and Athy Community College got together to participate in a global day of action to force political leaders to take climate change seriously on Friday last 25 May . <p> The rally was organised by the Global Citizenship Committee of Ardscoil na Trion ? ide with the help of their Student Council . <p> At noon , with the support of school management the students left their classes and marched , ( with a garda escort ) from Ardscoil to the Community College . On the way the well behaved group of around 500 students carried an array of colourful placards and banners demanding that action be taken to halt and reverse climate change . <p> When the Ardscoil students reached the Community College , they were joined by all the students and staff of the college , who also paraded with a great variety of well thought our banners and signs .
