<p> Wicklow Tidy Towns will hold a public meeting today ( Wednesday , May 1 ) to discuss the establishment of a Sustainable Energy Community ( SECs ) . <p> Over 200 SECs are being set up currently across Ireland with support , financially and non-financially , from the Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland ( SEAI ) so that communities can do their bit to tackle the issue . <p> Wicklow Tidy Towns is setting up an SEC and , if enough support is available , over the next 12 to 18 months the SEC will learn about current energy consumption in the Wicklow community and will plan as to how that can be reduced . Once the plan is completed the task is to implement the plan . <p> ' Climate Change is now front and centre in the media with the growing student movement started by Swedish student , Grete Thunberg , and the current protests in London and elsewhere by Extinction Rebellion , ' said Andrew Lawless , Chairman of Wicklow Tidy Towns . ' Sir David Attenborough , probably the greatest ever presenter of TV programmes on life on earth has stated that we have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impacts of climate change such as rising sea levels , animal extinction , forced migration of people from coastal areas may be irreversible . In Wicklow we are not immune to the impacts of climate change . In the past 50 years a huge chunk of the Murrough has been lost . Could it be lost forever in the future . ' <p> All energy users will be asked to take part - householders , businesses , sports clubs , local authorities , drivers , transport companies - at all stages . There are currently eight SECs at different stages of development in the county including Newtownmountkennedy and Glenmalure . Wicklow County Council will be supporting all SECs once they are in a position to proceed . <p> ' The success of no-smoking in public areas is a success because people accepted it was the right thing to do . The government never achieved public acceptance of charging for water and the idea was dropped , ' added Mr Lawless . <p> The public meeting takes place at 8 p.m. in the Pastoral Centre in Wicklow town .
