<h> ' We are living in extraordinary times ' -- EirGrid chief <p> EirGrid CEO Mark Foley has indicated that Ireland is currently witnessing one of the greatest climate change movements of this century and , that , what has happened here over the past six months is " simply extraordinary " . <p> Foley was speaking this morning , Thursday , May 16 , during his address to the Irish Wind Farmers ' Association 's ( IWFA 's ) annual workshop which is taking place in Killenard , Co . Laois today . <p> He said that much of the refocus on climate change was down to the work of the Citizens ' Assembly ; Joint Oireachtas Committee on Climate Change ; and Taoiseach Leo Varadkar whom , the CEO added , " had the courage to describe Ireland as the laggards in Europe " . <p> Meanwhile , during his address , EirGrid 's chief described Minister for Communications , Climate Action and Environment Richard Bruton as " a man with a vision ; a man who wants to take action " . <p> It is an extraordinary change from where we were 12 months ago and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ this century . <p> He continued : " We are in extraordinary times . The Government really gets it and seems to be up for creating the right vision and the right policy content . " <p> Advertisement <p> Foley went on to say that while domestically the Government is now focused on securing 70% of all electricity from renewables by 2030 " nowhere else is doing that in the world " . <p> Nobody else has that ambition . This will be a world-leading ambition and this is what we need to see from our politicians . <p> He continued : " For wind to continue to play its part in reducing carbon emissions , collaboration is critical and we have to make communities part of the energy transformation in a way we have never done before . <p> " We have to make them want the wind solution .
