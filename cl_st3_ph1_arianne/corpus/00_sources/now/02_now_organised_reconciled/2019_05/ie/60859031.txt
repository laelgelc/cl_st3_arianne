<h> Biokraft nails deal to supply liquefied biogas to Hurtigruten <p> Hurtigruten will replace conventional engines with gas-powered engines and large battery packs on at least six ships by 2021PHOTO : Hurtigruten <p> Hurtigruten signed a multi-year deal with Norway-based Biokraft , which will supply climate-neutral liquified biogas ( LBG ) to power ships with fuel made from dead fish and other organic waste . <h> Largest LBG contract in shipping <p> The 7.5-year deal is touted as the largest global contract for supplying LBG to a ship operator . It includes near-daily delivery to Hurtigruten ships , the first cruise vessels to be powered by fossil-free LBG . <p> ' This is a large and significant move for Hurtigruten , for Biokraft , for green solutions in the global shipping industry and , most importantly , for the environment , ' Hurtigruten CEO Daniel Skjeldam said . <h> Battery-biogas hybrid ships <p> Hurtigruten will replace conventional engines with gas-powered engines and large battery packs on at least six ships by 2021 . The hybrid ships will run on a combination of clean electric power , LNG and LBG . <p> ' We are proud to partner with Hurtigruten @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sustainable shipping , ' Biokraft CEO H ? vard Wollan said . <p> The first biogas delivery to Hurtigruten is expected to take place in 2020 . <p> Renewable biogas is a clean source of energy and considered the most eco-friendly fuel currently available . It is already used as fuel in small parts of the transport sector , especially in buses . <h> Norway 's opportunity <p> Norway , which has large fishery and forestry sectors that produce a steady volume of organic waste , has an opportunity to become world leader in biogas production . <p> ' Norway has a long history as one of the world 's leading maritime nations and has been living off the oceans for generations . Currently our oceans are under pressure from climate change and pollution . This is what we are working to change with dedicated partners like Hurtigruten , ' Wollan said .
