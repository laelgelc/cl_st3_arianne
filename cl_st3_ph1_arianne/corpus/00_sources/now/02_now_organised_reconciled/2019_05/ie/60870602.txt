<h> Dundalk students planning further Strike for Climate protests <p> A group of students from schools across Dundalk took to the streets last Friday to stand in solidarity with school students from across the world taking part in the " Strike for Climate " protests . <p> Speaking to the Democrat , one of the organisers Peter McGuinness explained : " I was involved in organising the school strike for climate in Dundalk today along with Emma Craven , Donnchadh Hughes , Pauric Craven , Eoghan Smyth , Eve Brosnan and Matthew Geraghty . " <p> " Myself and a group of Students from Dundalk schools decided to take part in the global school strike for climate today . We met outside the courthouse at one and protested until three .
