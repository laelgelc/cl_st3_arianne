<h> Green shoots show signs of growth in Midlands local elections <p> After a long weekend of anxious waiting going into the eight count in the Moate Civic Centre in Athlone on Sunday night , things were tense for Louise Heavin . <p> " I 'm feeling a bit nervous now , " the first-time candidate remarked , glancing about the hall . " We 're getting to the crunch phase . " <p> Running in her home county , Heavin was vying to become the first ever Green Party representative elected to Westmeath County Council . <p> Things were not looking good up to the eight count , Heavin 's sister Felicity told The Green News , as she was " kind of teetering around seventh place " . <p> That was until the last few boxes were opened , the tallies of which shot her up to the fifth and final available seat for the Athlone constituency . <p> " It 's coming down to the wire , " Felicity said at the time . And so it did with Heavin declared elected at the 11th and final count .
