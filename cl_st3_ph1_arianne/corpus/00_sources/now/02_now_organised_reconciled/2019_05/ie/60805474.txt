<h> Environment <p> Reporter : <p> David Lynch <p> 5 May 2019 <p> 0 <p> The Green Party 's local election candidate Eoin Daly has said that if elected he will propose a motion calling on the Chief Executive of Louth County Council to declare a climate change and biodiversity emergency . <p> According to Mr Daly , this will follow the lead of Wicklow 's County Council and local authorities across the UK who have already declared an emergency . <p> " Climate change will have serious consequences for a low lying county like Louth . Yet all we are hearing at the minute locally is words , there 's no substantive action " , Daly said , " Such a declaration by Louth County Council would at least show an appreciation of the scale of the problems we face . " <p> Daly adds that the declarations by local authorities in the UK , recent Extinction Rebellion protests in London and Dublin and the Student Climate Strikes in March led Westminster to approve a motion to declare an environment and climate emergency . <p> Daly says this adds their voice to the growing numbers of institutions and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , voices that include governors of the British and Irish central banks Mark Carney and Phillip Lane and climate change activist Greta Thunberg . This all comes as the UN finalises its report into the state of the planet 's natural systems set to be released in coming days and is expected to make stark reading . <p> " These elections need to be a watershed moment for us all , " continued Daly " We 're seeing growing public awareness of the challenges posed by environmental threats , but we need meaningful policies . We need to vote for Greens in these local and European elections !
