<h> Changing the atmosphere <p> Klaus Lackner , director of the Centre for Negative Carbon Emissions at the University of Arizona <p> A climate change pioneer based in the US has teamed up with two Irish businessmen in an attempt to commercialise his technology , which takes carbon dioxide from the air , writes John Reynolds <p> By John Reynolds May 26 , 2019 <p> Share this article : <p> Climate change and how to manage it may be generating huge amounts of debate in 2019 -- but for Klaus Lackner , a eureka moment on one aspect of it arrived more than 20 years ago . It was in 1998 that the German-born professor , who is the director of the Centre for Negative Carbon Emissions at Arizona State University ( ASU ) , began comparing wind energy to a technology for sucking carbon dioxide emissions from the air . Direct air capture is a concept he has spent the past two decades working on , he tells me over coffee in a quiet Dublin bar .
