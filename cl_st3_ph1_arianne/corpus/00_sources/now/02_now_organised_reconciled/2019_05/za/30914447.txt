The evidence shows that even if investors do n't flag environmental cataclysm as a major concern , those who do are reaping the rewards , and the planet will be all the better for it . <p> Although sustainable investing is still in its infancy , there is still a lot of debate about the best approach and international standards are yet to be established to govern it . <p> But the conversation is heating up , and it is all about the UN Sustainable Development Goals , carbon footprint , ethics , or some other desired social outcome . And fund managers and investors are paying attention . Today , investment in global environmental , social and governance ( ESG ) values is estimated at more than $20-trillion in assets under management . <p> But issues ranging from climate change to executive remuneration and the gender pay gap have accelerated the integration of ESG into investment portfolios .
