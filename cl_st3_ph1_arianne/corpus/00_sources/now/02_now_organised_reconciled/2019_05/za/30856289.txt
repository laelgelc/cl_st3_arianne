<h> Standard Bank shareholders asked to make the bank greener <p> Coal , which is major fuel for generating electricity , accounts for 40 percent of carbon dioxide emissions , a key gas responsible for global warming . AFP/File/Barbara LABORDE <h> The bank is looking to become more environmentally responsible . <p> Standard Bank shareholders have been encouraged to vote for a resolution for the bank to assess and report back on greenhouse gas emissions and climate change risks resulting from its lending and investing . <p> Proposed by the Raith Foundation , shareholder activist Theo Botha and supported by nonprofit shareholder activism organisation Just Share , the resolution will be tabled at the bank 's annual general meeting on May 30 -- the first climate risk-related shareholder resolution to be tabled in the country . <p> The bank 's board has , however , recommended shareholders vote against the resolution , which sets the bank out to " report to shareholders by the end of November its assessment of the greenhouse gas emissions " . <p> This includes the amount and percentage of carbon-related assets relative to the bank 's total assets . <p> The resolution also sets @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ policy on lending to coal-fired power projects and coal mining operations . <p> According to Just Share , " shareholders should be able to assess the extent to which Standard Bank is exposed to climate risk by virtue of its lending , investment and financing activities . <p> The information currently disclosed by the company is insufficient to enable shareholders to adequately assess these risks .
