Photo : Ashraf Hendricks/GroundUp <h> Not all streets are created equal , nor lit nearly as well , in Cape Town . <p> About 200 Capetonians marched through the streets of Rondebosch on Tuesday evening led by the Social Justice Coalition ( SJC ) . They demanded public lighting in their areas . <p> Rondebosch is an affluent area . Most of the protesters were from relatively poor townships in Khayelitsha , Philippi , and Kraaifontein . They demanded that the City of Cape Town stop " discriminatory allocation of public lighting in Cape Town " . <p> Addressing the marchers , who had gathered at the Rondebosch Park , SJC 's general secretary , Axolile Notywala , said : " Look how bright it is here -- all these lights . We are here to see and compare the situation here to the daily situation back home . It is a sad reality that after this march we will go back to darkness . We will go back to our dark areas where we get robbed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it had engaged the City on the unequal distribution of lighting for the past two years . But it said that to date the City had not shown willingness to address the discrimination in a clear and substantive manner . <p> " Maps showing the provision of public lighting across Cape Town , compiled using the City 's own data , reveals that effective public lighting is reserved for all roads in wealthier , predominantly white and coloured neighbourhoods . The same maps reveal that many roads in poorer black and coloured communities are not provided with the same effective public lighting . The differential treatment between these communities is self-evident . The inequality is self-evident , " read the statement . <p> Ntombomzi Phika lives in Endlovini in Khayelitsha . " I want to live like this , " she said , referring to the streets of Rondebosch . " I want to be able to walk in my street at night under the lights without fear . Instead I have to rush home before the sun goes down because I am afraid of what might happen to me @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Treat all streets equal " and " Effective public lighting " . These were waved by protesters while they marched through various streets in Rondebosch , much to the confusion of some residents . <p> The SJC claims that Mayco member for energy and climate change Phindile Maxiti in a press statement said that the City would spend R6 million on street lights and R4 million on high-mast spotlights , a total of R10 million , in Khayelitsha in the 2019/20 financial year . But in the 2019/2020 draft budget only R7 million for all public lighting has been allocated for the entirety of Area East , of which Khayelitsha is a part . <p> Questions sent to the City on Tuesday had not been answered by the time of publication .
