<h> News feed <h> Port of Durban commemorates World Day for Safety and Health at Work <p> This year is also the 100th anniversary of the World Health and Safety Day at Work . <p> May 21 , 2019 <p> Transnet National Ports Authority at the Port of Durban chose Friday , 17 May to rally port employees together for World Day for Safety &amp; Health at Work . The port 's safety mascot iSenzo was part of the commemoration . <p> Transnet National Ports Authority at the Port of Durban chose Friday , 17 May to rally port employees together for World Day for Safety and Health at Work , the International Labour Organisation 's ( ILO ) global day usually observed on 28 April each year . Postponing its own observation to a weekday event allowed the port authority to reach more employees about this important issue . <p> Port of Durban employees were invited to view real-world first aid simulations and vessel evacuation procedures . <p> Port Manager at the Port of Durban , Nokuzola Nkowane , said : " This year the International Labour Organisation 's theme for World Day for Safety and Health is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ This theme is about celebrating and building on the wealth of knowledge and action accumulated over 100 years , as we get ready to face and appreciate the changes brought forward by the future of work , such as changing work arrangements , technology , demographics , globalisation , climate change , and other drivers . " <p> Port of Durban employees were invited to view real-world first aid simulations and vessel evacuation procedures alongside the port 's N-shed passenger terminal . There were demonstrations of the correct protocol for dealing with electrical incidents in the port environment <p> Nkowane said TNPA was striving to reduce disabling injuries in the workplace through programmes such as its ZERO HARM initiative . " We must all commit to improving our knowledge of occupational safety , health and environment in the workplace so that we can protect ourselves , our colleagues and those we come into contact within the Port of Durban , " she said . <p> DID YOU KNOW ?
