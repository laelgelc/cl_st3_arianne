<h> 7 000 dead in Nigeria clashes over resources <p> Dwindling grazing pastures is the source of increased conflicts between Nigeria 's Fulani herdsmen and other communities . File photo <p> from EMEKA OKONKWO in Abuja , NigeriaABUJA , ( CAJ News ) -- MORE than 7 000 people have reportedly died during persistent violence between farmers and pastoralists in Nigeria over the past five years . <p> The conflict has cost the West African country 's economy $13 billion a year . <p> This is according to a humanitarian organisation as the clashes persist over water and land in the Middle Belt region . <p> Communities once cooperated over natural resources but are competing for increasingly scarce land and water as climate change intensifies , sparking migration further south in search of available resources . <p> Mercy Corps stated that underdevelopment and poor governance further contributed to a breakdown in traditional agreements , and farmer and pastoralist communities were fast becoming polarised as clashes take on religious and ethnic overtones . <p> Pastoralists are largely Muslim while farmers are mainly Christian . <p> Mercy Corps has initiated a programme sought to prevent violent conflict between farmer and pastoralist @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ disputes . <p> The programmes are ongoing in the states of Benue and Nasarawa , which are among the most affected by conflict . <p> Clashes between herdsmen and farmers are among a spate of violent crimes Nigeria is enduring . <p> An insurgency by the Islamist Boko Haram group has left at least 20 000 people dead and over 2 million others displaced northeast of the country . <p> -- CAJ News <p> Short URL : http : //cajnewsafrica.com/ ? p=30710 <p> Posted by Admin on May 28 2019 .
