<h> Canadian town takes up the ' Cape Town Challenge ' to reduce water usage <p> A small town in Canada is taking up the " Cape Town Challenge " inspired by the latter 's recent struggle with water . <p> According to a report on MyCoastNow , the news site of a local radio station , residents of Gibsons , British Colombia have been encouraged to reduce their water consumption to 50-litres per day , much like Cape Town during its recent drought . Residents ' current consumption is five-times higher than that . <p> " Here on the Sunshine Coast , effective water management has become a major issue , as the region experiences longer periods of drought and many residents are subject to lengthy water restrictions , " it wrote . <p> " Even in Gibsons , where we benefit from our proximity to the Gibsons Aquifer , we must be thoughtful about our water usage in the face of a growing population and the unknown effects of climate change .
