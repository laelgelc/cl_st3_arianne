They 've been visiting Fisherman 's Lane for decades , armed with rods and bait and loads of patience , hoping to catch line fish . <p> " It 's high time that we restore and protect this area for future generations and I 'm happy to say that this project will be a priority for us , " said the City 's mayoral committee member for spatial planning and environment Marian Nieuwoudt . <p> Officials within the coastal management department have already started with the planning @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will be appointed in due course . <p> " After obtaining a detailed environmental decision we now know what we can do at Fisherman 's Lane .
