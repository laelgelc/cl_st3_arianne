<h> Most Read <h> Most Read <h> LETTER : Battery booster <p> Australia is awash with misinformation about climate change and renewable energy , much of it stoked by the coal lobby- supported Liberal-National government , aided and abetted by the Murdoch press . <p> So , I am not surprised to find that Andrew Kenny is misinformed about South Australia 's Tesla big battery project , the Hornsdale Power Reserve , ( April 23 ) . <p> Financial information provided by French renewable energy developer Neoen shows that the battery cost $A87m ( $61m ) and took less than 100 days to install . In the first year of operation it generated net revenues of more than 25% of the capital cost and saved almost $A40m in grid costs . <p> The Australian energy market operator confirms that the battery has stabilised the network and prevented blackouts as a result of its speed , versatility and accuracy in responding to events , especially as coal-fired units tripped out as demand peaked during a summer of record high temperatures .
