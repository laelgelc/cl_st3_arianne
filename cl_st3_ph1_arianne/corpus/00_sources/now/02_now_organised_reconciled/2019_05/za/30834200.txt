<h> Why Girls ' Education is Key in War Against Climate Change <p> One year ago I was on an all-female expedition to Antarctica aimed at heightening women 's influence as decision-makers on key issues that shape our planet . It was n't long before I discovered I was the only African-born woman on the trip . The first thing I thought was : how did I get here and how can I use this experience to benefit others ? <p> I reflected on this over the following three weeks , even as I was awed by the beautiful wildlife , blue icebergs and the sublime awesomeness of the Antarctic Ocean . I realized then and there how my parent 's investment in my education had opened up this and many opportunities in my life and I returned home determined to advance the education of girls . But educating girls does not just unlock individual opportunities like mine -- it is a vital but overlooked solution to one of the biggest catastrophes Africa and the world has ever faced : climate change . <p> The urgency of addressing climate change is evident from Antarctica , where 25 square miles @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sea , to southern Africa , where Cyclone Idai , one of the worst recorded tropical cyclones to affect Africa and the Southern Hemisphere . Idai left at least 746 dead , over 2,390 injured and over 2.9 million people impacted in Mozambique , Zimbabwe and Malawi . Meanwhile , extremes of drought are also rising and , by 2020 , up to 250 million people in Africa may suffer from decreased access to water , and yields from rain-fed agriculture may drop by 50 percent . <p> To rise to the challenge , African and global leaders must think beyond standard solutions . Africa Climate Week , held last month in Accra , Ghana , did not go far enough . Although the meeting covered crucial topics such as National Climate Action Plans , Cities and Local Action , Energy Transition , Nature-based Solutions and Climate Finance , Girl education was not part of the agenda . <p> While rarely considered together , progress in girls ' education and climate change are integrally connected . This is calculated to be more effective than commonly promoted strategies like recycling , eating a plant-based diet or changing household lightbulbs . <p> The positive impact of girls ' education on climate resilience can be seen in South Africa 's iconic Kruger National Park , a global safari destination . Despite its abundant wildlife and a thriving ecotourism economy , hundreds of thousands of women and youth in villages surrounding the park still live in poverty and rely on smallholder agriculture , even as increasing drought threatens water supplies . <p> My colleagues at Conservation International in South Africa have partnered with the Local Department of Education and Department of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ training . Local female youth leaders are now driving activities that increase community resilience to climate change . One young woman has started a local Scouts program for 250 children -- creating the next generation of field rangers and conservationists.
