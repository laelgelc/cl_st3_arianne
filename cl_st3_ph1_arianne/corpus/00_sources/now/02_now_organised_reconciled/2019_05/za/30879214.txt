<h> Students show how solar energy can be ' Uberised ' <p> May 17 , 2019 <p> A team of students from Strathmore University in Nairobi have walked off as winners with R20 000 in prize money for an innovative concept to provide equitable energy access to remote villages based on , among others , " Uber(ising) solar energy " . <p> The team was one of four university teams participating in the African Utility Week and PowerGen Africa conference and exhibition 's first ever Initiate ! Impact Challenge . The 19th edition of the event gathered thousands of power , water and gas industry experts in Cape Town this week and ended yesterday ( Thursday ) . <p> Student teams from Stellenbosch University , the University of Cape Town and the University of the Witwatersrand also took part in the three-day challenge sponsored by the Enel Foundation , the Innovation Hub , Lesedi Nuclear Services and the Russian Nuclear Agency Rosatom .
