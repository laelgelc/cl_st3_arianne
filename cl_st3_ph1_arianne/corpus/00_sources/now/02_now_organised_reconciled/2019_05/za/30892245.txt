<h> Cleaning up Black River <p> Environmental warriors , volunteers and residents are coming together to clean the filthy Black River mouth on Saturday . <p> The mouth is located in the industrial area of Paarden Eiland below the bridge of the R27 and flows through canals in Rondebosch and Claremont . It also flows through Mowbray , Observatory and Maitland . <p> The clean-up is hosted by Sea the Bigger Picture Ocean Initiative , whose aim is to educate citizens about litter on the beaches and oceans . <p> It will start at 10:00 with a safety briefing and registration , a clean-up and sorting and then an educational talk . <p> Some of the organisations joining the clean-up are the Renew Elsieskraal River , Plastic Pollution Initiative , Mike Ryder , Friends of Liesbeek , Help Up and Friends of Black River . <p> Chavonne Snyman from Plastic Pollution Initiative says every minute a big truckload of plastic enters the ocean , this comes from land and in less than two years that will be every 15 seconds . <p> " In years to come , there will be more plastic in the ocean than fish . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ will always be on the planet . Our canals and rivers need to be taken care of and maintained for us to survive . With climate change ; it 's really only a matter of time , " she says . <p> Another group helping in the clean-up is Help Up . Georgia McTaggart , Help Up founder says they are joining the clean-up because they want to send a message across that as groups they think the river or the ocean and the environment is special . " This is all about cleaning up after our mess . We do a clean-up in our area and the things that we remove in the river are unimaginable . <p> " Joining hands with Sea The Bigger Picture is about showing solidarity and saying we want to see a change in the river . We want to set an example and to tell others that the situation is not hopeless , we can still save our environment . Though it wo n't happen overnight -- if we are consistent it will happen , " she says . <p> McTaggart says some @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ include washing machines , carpets , toys , nappies , building rubble .
