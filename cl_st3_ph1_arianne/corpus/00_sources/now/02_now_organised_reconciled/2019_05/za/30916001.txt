<h> Community rallies behind Clanwilliam cedar trees <p> Cape Town - Clanwilliam cedar trees were in abundance across the Cederberg , but only a handful of these endemic trees are left and now face extinction . <p> The Cederberg mountains are the only place in the world where the Clanwilliam cedar grows . It is listed as an endangered species on the International Union for Conservation of Nature ( IUCN ) Red data list . <p> Today , between 13000 and 15000 are left in the region . The tree is the only species that survived the last Ice Age period and are meant to grow in snowy and cold conditions . <p> Fires have also played a large part in the population reduction and the Clanwilliam cedar does n't regrow after a fire - unlike the surrounding fynbos . <p> " They have very high oil content and because of the oil the tree does n't have to even really burn because there is spontaneous combustion .
