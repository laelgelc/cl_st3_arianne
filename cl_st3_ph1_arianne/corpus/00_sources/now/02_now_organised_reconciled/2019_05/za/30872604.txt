<h> Eco-conscious Cape Town a world leader of environmental change <h> Cape Town made the A-list of top climate cities , a new report by the international non-profit Carbon Disclosure Project shows . <p> Cape Town is the only South African city to make it onto an international list of cities that stand apart as world leaders of environmental change and awareness . Forty-three cities were ranked as global climate leaders . <p> The list has been compiled by the international non-profit organization Carbon Disclosure Project ( CDP ) with the results forming part EU Green Week . <p> The organisation rated 596 cities across the globe with letter grades between A and D based on their environmental performance . This year , they recognised more than 150 corporates as pioneers in acting on climate change . <p> CDP took into consideration the corporates journey through disclosure of awareness , management and leadership .
