<h> POLITICS <h> Carbon Tax Bill will lead to further job losses -- Kevin Mileham <p> DA MP says the polluter will not be the only one paying as it will mean a higher cost of living <p> Carbon Tax Bill will lead to further job losses and higher cost of living <p> 16 May 2019 <p> In February this year , Parliament passed the Carbon Tax Bill which will see companies which exceed the threshold of carbon emissions , charged R120 per ton of carbon dioxide emissions , following the polluter pays principle . <p> However , the polluter will not be the only one paying . The passing of this Bill means that on 1 June , petrol and diesel prices will increase by 9c and 10c per liter respectively , to incorporate carbon tax . <p> Once again South Africa 's heavily taxed consumers must pay the price for others ' failures , and fund the bottomless pit of state capture , corruption and maladministration . <p> The Democratic Alliance ( DA ) is the only party which opposed the Bill . Carbon taxes will only lead to further job losses and higher cost of living @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ as it will only lead to stagnant economic growth which we can not afford . Taxes need to be decreased to attract foreign investment , stimulate economic activity and create jobs . <p> The Carbon Tax Bill is a job killing tax and it is proof that government does not know how to address climate change . <p> Instead of taking carbon emitters head on , the ANC would rather have ordinary South Africans bear the brunt . <p> The Democratic Alliance would impose heavy fines for polluters , and ensure that our country is taking active steps to reduce carbon emissions . Taxing South African consumers is not the way to solve this problem .
