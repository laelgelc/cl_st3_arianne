<h> Risk readiness drops to lowest level in 12 years <p> LONDON -- Economic and global trade concerns are challenging organizations ' ability to invest adequately in preparing for and protecting the continuity of their operations , according to findings from Aon 's 2019 Global Risk Management Survey . <p> " Companies of all sizes are struggling to prioritize their risk management efforts amid so much change and uncertainty , " said Rory Moloney , chief executive , Global Risk Consulting . " What was once a tried-and-true strategy for risk mitigation -- using the past to predict the future -- is now a challenge and coupled with a more competitive global economy , it is causing an all-time low level of risk readiness . As a result , risk management plans need to take a different approach than they have in the past .
