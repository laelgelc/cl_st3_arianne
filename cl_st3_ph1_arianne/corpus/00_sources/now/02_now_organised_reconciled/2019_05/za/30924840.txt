<h> UCT 's New recognised among 100 most influential on global climate policy <p> Cape Town -- UCT 's African Climate and Development Initiative ( ACDI ) director Professor Mark New has been listed among the world 's 100 most influential people in climate policy for 2019 . <p> The list , which is separated into Top 20 , youth , politics and government , academia and advocacy , and international and non-governmental organisations categories , also includes US congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez , Paris mayor Anne Hidalgo , African Development Bank Group climate change and green growth director Anthony Nyong , and broadcaster and natural historian David Attenborough . <p> The climate 100 list of politicians , civil servants , academics and activists comes courtesy of global network Apolitical and is drawn from hundreds of nominations from experts and leading organisations . <p> The aim is to recognise high-profile advocates whose work is indispensable to raising awareness and demanding change in respect of climate policy . <p> New , the citation said , is director of the ACDI and professor of international development at the University of East Anglia in the UK . <p> " His research career spans @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ trends , climate modelling and assessing the impact of failed climate mitigation policy . <p> " New edits the journal Environmental Research Letters and serves on the boards of the South Africa Global Change Science Committee and the Africa Future Earth Science Committee . " <p> The network said the list " comes at a time when young people everywhere are marching to demand action on climate " . <p> " It 's more important than ever to celebrate and support those with the most power to act : the people driving tangible policy change .
