<p> Moheta said the agriculture department had conducted a number of biodiversity education and awareness activities crafted to equip educators and learners about the importance of biodiversity as " we all share the same environment " . <p> Maheta said regardless of the outreach programmes presented by the city , residents still indulged in practices that impacted negatively on the environment . <p> " These practices include the burning of grasslands , which disturbs the ecosystem , poaching of wild animals and harvesting wood . " <p> He said this contributed towards the extinction of animals and plant species . <p> " Illegal dumping and littering on open spaces and in river systems and burning of waste contribute to climate change . " <p> He said what one individual did to the environment would affect all of us and people had to be aware of the dangers . <p> " The food we eat , the medicines we use for our health and wellbeing are all from our biodiversity . " <p> Moheta said that without the species diversity , genetic diversity and the ecosystem , individuals would have no benefits .
