<h> Help SA respond to impact of intensified climate change <p> Cape Town -- Environmental Affairs Minister Nomvula Mokonyane published the draft national climate change adaptation strategy for public comment in the Government Gazette . <p> " The adaptation strategy has been developed in line with South Africa 's commitment to the Paris Agreement on Climate Change to introduce measures to adapt to the effects of climate change while achieving the stabilisation of greenhouse gas emissions and limiting temperature increases to 1.5 ? C , " the department said . <p> The development of the strategy was central towards attaining the national climate change response policy objective of effectively managing the inevitable climate change impacts through interventions that sustained South Africa 's social , economic , and environmental resilience and emergency response capacity , it said . <p> There was evidence that extreme weather events in South Africa were increasing , with heat wave conditions found to be more likely , dry spell durations lengthening slightly and rainfall intensity increasing . <p> " Increases in annual-average near-surface temperatures were projected to occur over large parts of South Africa , including the western interior and northern parts . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and landscapes were being degraded , veld fires were becoming more frequent , and overused natural terrestrial and marine systems are under stress , " the department said . <p> Adaptation to climate change presented South Africa with an opportunity to transform the economy , strengthen the social and spatial fabric and build a climate-resilient society . <p> " In particular , urgent action in respect of managing water resources in a changing climate , planning for the potential impact on agricultural production and ensuring that local governments are able to anticipate and reduce the risk of extreme events ( such as floods and droughts ) on human settlements . " <p> Members of the public or organisations were invited to submit written inputs and comments within 30 days of publication of the notice in the Gazette to : The Director General , Department of Environmental Affairs , Attention : Mr Sibonelo Mbanjwa , Private Bag X447 , Pretoria , 0001.
