<h> Ex-ministers who quit Trudeau government will run as independents in Canadian election <p> MARKHAM , Ontario ( Reuters ) - Two former ministers who quit Canadian Prime Minister Justin Trudeau 's government this year over his handling of a scandal said on Monday they would run as independents in an election this October . <p> Former Canadian Justice Minister and current Independent MP Jody Wilson-Raybould speaks to supporters about her political future during a news conference in Vancouver , British Columbia , Canada , May 27 , 2019 . REUTERS/Jackie Dives <p> Jody Wilson-Raybould and Jane Philpott resigned amid allegations that senior Trudeau officials tried to interfere in a corporate corruption case by leaning on Wilson-Raybould when she was justice minister . <p> Trudeau , facing the biggest crisis of his tenure , later kicked them out of the ruling Liberal caucus , citing their lack of support . <p> Both women , speaking at separate events , said they would press for more openness in Ottawa and also stressed the importance of fighting climate change . <p> They face serious challenges .
