Read More <p> PANAJI : Massive floods will take place in Goa with low-lying areas getting submerged in the next 20 years due to rapid conversion of agricultural land and khazans into developmental land , said assistant professor , Goa University , Nandkumar Kamat . He was speaking at the K D Naik memorial lecture on ' Restoring ecological , economic and agro-entrepreneurial glory of Khazans of Goa ' held in Panaji on Tuesday . " I 've been monitoring Tiswadi island . We are the most threatened island in South Asia in terms of climate change and sea level rise . People may not immediately realise it , but all low-lying areas where there are huge constructions will face problems , " he said . Kamat displayed the hazard map of Goa that shows how much land will get submerged due to sea level rise and which are the most vulnerable areas . " Goa @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ can easily predict as well where these floods will take place - around the constructions , arterial roads around Panaji city , St Cruz and Merces . Here , khazan lands have been degraded completely , " he added . Kamat was also of the opinion that Calapor-Merces is going to be like a twin of Taleigao village , or like another Panaji city due to rapid urbanisation . Speaking about the importance of khazans , he said that one hectare of khazan gives ecosystem services worth 2 lakh dollars per hectare per year . " If one hectare of khazan lands out of 18,000 hectares is changed , reclaimed or converted , it will affect the ecosystem of the landscape overtime . Seventeen ecosystem services like pollinators , micro-organisms found in khazan lands will be lost , " he said . <p> Kamat pointed out that wherever we see submergence of fertile khazan lands , they 're taken over by mangrove seeds that enter into the khazans during high tides and germinate there .
