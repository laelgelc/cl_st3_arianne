Since this change has been wrought by the machinations of men -- namely you , me and all else -- we ca n't blame the gods , destiny or mother nature for all calamities that befall us . What is sown , is what would be reaped . <p> Reports after reports and studies after studies have highlighted how anthropogenic actions are negatively impacting the environment . There is no doubt that we are now in a Newtonian world , where every action has a consequence , if not exactly a reaction . <p> Living in such a world , should we not then assess any major event or occurrence on the impact it has or might have on the environment ? The CO2 footprint of such events must be assessed , discussed , debated and progressively reduced .
