<p> The Excelsior Club , a landmark in Charlotte 's black community since the 1940s , has been added to a list of the11 most endangered historical places in the United States . <p> The list includes sites that are " at risk of destruction or irreparable damage , " according to the National Trust for Historic Preservation , which maintains the annual list . The 2019 list includes " a diverse mix of historic places across America that face a range of challenges and threats , from climate change to inappropriate development to neglect and disuse , " according to the organization 's website . <p> Since the first list was compiled 32 years ago , more than 300 landmarks have been added , and fewer than 5 percent of those listed sites have been lost , according to the trust . <p> The Excelsior club was the only Carolinas landmark to make the 2019 list . <p> Located on Beatties Ford Road , the Excelsior Club is listed for sale at $1.5 million , according to New River Brokerage 's Facebook page .
