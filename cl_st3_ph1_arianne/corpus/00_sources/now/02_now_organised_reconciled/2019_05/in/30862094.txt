<h> Agri minister looks to students for climate change solutions <p> ( JIS ) : Agriculture Minister Audley Shaw has charged students to come up with solutions to address the impact of climate change on the sector . <p> " As farmers in training , you have felt the impact of climate change that has resulted in the number of pests and diseases . We are looking to you , younger farmers , to lead the way in developing and implementing solutions to these threats , " he said . <p> Shaw was delivering the main address at an Open Day event at the Ebony Park HEART Academy in Clarendon on May 10 . <p> He told the young people that they have a key role to play in the continued development of the sector , which is a key driver of economic growth . <p> " Our vision is to have a vibrant sector involving young , creative minds like all of you .
