<h> Protest calls for action against climate change <p> TNN Updated : May 25 , 2019 , 10:46 IST <p> Representative image <p> PUNE : The Pune Collective for Climate Action ( PuCCA ) organized a protest -- Pune Climate Strike -- at the main gate of Savitribai Phule Pune University on Friday . The demonstration was a part of a global movement seeking action against climate change . Around 40 students , professionals and activists came together for the nearly two-hour protest . Carrying placards with messages and chanting slogans , the gathering was addressed by representatives of various city-based environmental NGOs and activists . Arunima Jamwal ( 20 ) , an anthropology student and co-founder of PuCCA , said she is passionate about environmental justice . She founded the collective along with a like-minded environmentalist Radhika Castle . IT professional Jeevraj Chowdhury , who participated in the agitation , said , " I travel 35km on a two-wheeler at 2pm every day for work when the temperature hovers around 43Celsius .
