Progressive capitalism can , I believe , save capitalism from itself -- if only we can get the political will behind it . <p> Research over the past 40 years has explained why markets on their own do n't deliver rising economic benefits for all . Adam Smith , the founder of modern economics , recognized how , if unregulated , businesses would conspire against the public interest by raising prices and suppressing wages . Yet he also suggested that at times markets would lead , as if by an invisible hand , to the well-being of society . Now we understand why markets often fail to deliver on their promise and why Smith 's invisible hand often seems invisible : because it simply is n't there . Modern theories of industrial organization have taught us how firms construct barriers to entry to enhance their market power . Democracy is more than having elections every four years . It includes systems of checks and balances -- ensuring that no one , not even a president , has unbridled power -- and a deep belief that no one can be above the law . It also includes protections of the rights of minorities , and a Congress and a healthy news media holding everyone to account .
