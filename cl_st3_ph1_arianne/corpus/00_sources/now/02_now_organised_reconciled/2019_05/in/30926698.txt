While Dakshina Kannada has been forced to resort to water rationing , the situation appears to be equally grim in the Malnad Region . The River Sharavathi , the lifeline of Shivamogga , is running dry , igniting fears of a large-scale water crisis in the district . Data from the Karnataka Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre ( KSNDMC ) gives one a glimpse of the bleak future in store for Shivamogga if the rain fails : Inflow to the Linganamakki Dam has plummeted to zero . The sight of the parched river in Hosanagara , and other towns and villages in Shivamogga , has triggered panic among the locals .
