<h> Return of Labor party possible in Australia <p> Australian bookies predict a return to power for the Labor party in an election on Saturday after six years in the political wilderness and a campaign in which it has put climate change and tax reform at the top of its agenda . <p> Prime Minister Scott Morrison has made economic management the centrepiece of the campaign for his conservative Liberal-National coalition , which has held power since 2013 . <p> Polls open at 8 a.m. ( 2200 GMT on Friday ) and will close at 6 p.m. ( 0800 GMT ) . Voting is compulsory in Australia and the result should be known on Saturday evening . <p> The campaign has ended on a sombre note with the death on Thursday of the popular former prime minister and Labor stalwart Bob Hawke .
