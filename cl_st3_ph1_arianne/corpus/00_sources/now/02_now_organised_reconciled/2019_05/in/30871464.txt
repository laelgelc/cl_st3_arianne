The main reasons cited for this move are the infrastructure woes of Jakarta -- like congested streets and inadequate sewerage networks -- and the threat of rising sea levels due to climate change . Indonesian policymakers will have to answer a barrage of questions on the purpose and functions of the new city in order for this move to be a success .
