<h> High Rise In Vehicle Sale Leads To Hazards of High Fuel Combustion <p> May 31 , 2019 <p> GUWAHATI : Despite decades of efforts to control air pollution , the high rise in vehicle sales with the growing population , has led to an increase in the percentage of pollution due to emissions in the air . The Green Globe organization held a press meet on Thursday , to discuss about the issue of air pollution and its effects on the environment . <p> The organization General Secretary Rajesh Dutta Baruah informed that during the year 2017-18 , new registration of 89,085 vehicles were done , which sums up to registration of 329 vehicles per day . During the year 2018-19 , new registrations of 98,581 vehicles were done , which sums up to registration of 385 vehicles per day . <p> The large majority of today 's cars and trucks travel by using internal combustion engines that burn gasoline or other fossil fuels .
