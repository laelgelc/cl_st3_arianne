The Council exercises real executive authority in India . All the executive actions are taken by the Union Council of Ministers , in the name of the President . <p> The Council is like a body of advisers to the Head of the state . The Ministers are appointed by the President on advice of the Prime Minister , under Article 75 ( 1 ) of the Constitution of India . However , the ministers hold office during the pleasure of the President and are collectively responsible to the Lok Sabha . <p> A member of the Council of Ministers can be a member of either the Lok Sabha or the Rajya Sabha . How Union Council is different from Union Cabinet ? <p> The Council is different from the Union Cabinet , which includes only the Prime Minister and Cabinet Ministers . The Union Cabinet is the top decision-making body within the Union Council of Ministers .
