<h> 35 crocodile nests seen in Odisha 's Bhitarkanika National Park <h> Last Updated : Monday 27 May 2019 <p> Around 35 nests of salt water crocodiles were spotted inside the mangrove forest in Odisha 's Bhitarkanika National Park by forest officials recently . <p> " We have already spotted 35 nesting sites and about 40-60 eggs were seen in each nesting site . Last year we found a record number of 101 crocodile nests . We are looking to find more over the next two weeks , " said Subrata Patra , forest range officer at Bhitarkanika . <p> Many snakes , including king cobras and pythons have also laid eggs in the mangrove forests . Snakes prefer the dense mangroves and mud-walled houses in the nearby areas to lay eggs . " It 's not possible to count all of them as both snakes and crocodiles guard their nests and eggs zealously , " said the forest officer . <p> Every year , between May 1 and July 31 , entry into the forest is banned to facilitate mating and breeding of crocodiles in the Park . Many predators , including the monitor lizards , fishing cats , jackals , kites , big fishes etc feast on the baby crocodiles after they emerge from the egg shells . <p> " Very few hatchlings survive and this is a high-risk stage .
