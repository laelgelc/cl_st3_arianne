<h> ' New economics ' : the way to save the planet ? <p> LONDON ( Reuters ) - The science is in : the endless pursuit of economic growth is devouring the foundations of life on Earth , and no country -- rich or poor -- can expect to escape dire consequences if things go on as they are .
