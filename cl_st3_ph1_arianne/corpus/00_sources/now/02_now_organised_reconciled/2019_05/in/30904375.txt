<h> Students , activists urge government to declare climate emergency <p> Students and activists at the second #Fridays-ForFuture global strike at Juhu beach on Friday <p> MUMBAI : The students ' battle against climate change reached the city on Friday with the participation of several students and activists in the second #FridaysForFuture global strike . In Mumbai , hundreds urged the government to declare climate emergency at Juhu Beach . #FridaysForFuture , a Swedish movement , was started by 16-year-old Greta Thunberg in August 2018 , when she sat in front of the Swedish parliament every school day for three weeks , to protest against the lack of action on the climate crisis . Since then , Thunberg has been missing classes every Friday to protest outside the Swedish parliament . " We need to take action against climate change . I have been reading about this movement for a month , and I have come here to meet like-minded people so that I can join them and support this ( environment protection ) cause , " said Maheema , a student at Government Law College and a climate activist .
