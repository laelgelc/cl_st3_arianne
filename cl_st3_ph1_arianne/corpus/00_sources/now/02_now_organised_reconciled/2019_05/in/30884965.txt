K. Behera , HOD computer Science dept. saving trees are most important theses days , due to climate change and global warming . These trees were 10+ years old and if we plant a new trees it will take more yEars to grow , so saving the uprooted tree is the best way we could do to save environment after the cyclone . <p> " With the Help MTech , MCA students and also active help of OFDC we are able to save the trees " said Dr. P.K Behera , HOD .
