<h> Services <p> HC notice to Centre , ECI on plea against use of plastic material in poll campaigns <h> HC notice to Centre , ECI on plea against use of plastic material in poll campaigns <p> TNN Updated : May 3 , 2019 , 6:48 IST <p> SPREADING THE GREEN WORD <p> CHANDIGARH : The Punjab and Haryana high court on Thursday issued notice to the Union ministry of environment , forest and climate change ; the Election Commission of India ( ECI ) and the Punjab government on a plea to restrain registered political parties and candidates from using polyvinyl chloride ( PVC ) flex boards , plastic posters and related material during elections . The division bench comprising Chief Justice Krishna Murari and Justice Arun Palli passed these orders while hearing a public interest litigation ( PIL ) filed by Rohit Sabharwal , a resident of Ludhiana . The counsel for the petitioner pointed out that it was the fundamental right of every citizen to live in a clean and healthy environment , whereas it had been seen in past that political parties and other candidates use plastic material for election campaigns . Article 48-A of the Constitution of India specifically casts the duty upon the state governments to protect the environment , " argued the counsel . The petitioner also informed the HC that in January , 2019 , the Union ministry of environment had written to the ECI with a request to impress upon all concerned to take up sustainable practices and try using alternate option to single-use plastic during elections . <p> In pursuance to that , the ECI on February 26 , 2019 , issued advisory that all political parties should take adequate steps and measures to not use single-use plastic as campaign materials during the elections in the interest of human health and environment . However , no effective steps have been taken to restrain the political parties from usage of plastic material . The petitioner has also sought directions to stop the use of plastic material during political campaigns in the current parliamentary elections and other political campaigns in future .
