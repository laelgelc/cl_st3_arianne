Millions of children across ... Read More <p> By : Madhav Nair NEW DELHI : Hundreds of environment activists and schoolchildren stood out among the crowd at Connaught Place on Friday morning . They were demanding government action against climate change . Holding placards and banners with different slogans . They participated in the ' Global Strike for Future ' , a global movement that has been simultaneously taking place in over 100 countries , including different parts of India . The movement was started by Swedish teen-activist Greta Thunberg , who had skipped school and protested outside the Swedish parliament every Friday to demand action from the government on climate change in accordance with the Paris Agreement . Since then , millions of children across the world have skipped school every Friday to take part in similar protests .
