The UN Office for Disaster Risk Reduction ( UNDRR ) launched a Global Assessment Report ( GAR ) on Wednesday , warning countries of newer and larger impacts of disaster risk on economies worldwide . Countries in the Asia-Pacific region are especially at risk , with the region likely to account for 40% of global economic losses . Japan , China , Korea , and India may suffer the greatest economic impact , the report adds . <p> The GAR is a biennial assessment of disaster risk reduction at the global scale . It was launched at a five-day event -- a run-up to UN 's climate action summit in September .
