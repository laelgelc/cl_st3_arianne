<p> Land-use change , not climate change , is the most forceful driver of ecosystem collapse and biodiversity loss , although the latter 's impact on ecosystems is accelerating , warns a landmark new study by the UN Intergovernmental Science-Policy Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services ( IPBES ) . <p> Far more dire than the UN 's doomsday reports on climate change , the IPBES report is different in a few other aspects too . <p> Climate change reports view the ecological problem as one of excess carbon dioxide in the troposphere . That allows the global elite to deploy climate mercenaries and tech companies to convert the eco catastrophe into a trade fair for technofixes to reduce emissions and capture atmospheric carbon . <p> The IPBES report , on the other hand , draws on 15,000 scientific and government sources , and on indigenous ways of knowing , to bring the problem down to Earth .
