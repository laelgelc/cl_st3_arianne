<h> Lowest turnout for ' yellow vest ' protests in France after May Day clashes <p> 2 Min Read <p> PARIS ( Reuters ) - France 's " yellow vest " anti-government demonstrations recorded their lowest turnout yet on Saturday , days after a wider May Day rally was marked by violent clashes in Paris . <p> The downturn in demonstrator numbers on the 25th straight weekend of yellow vest protests could be a relief to President Emmanuel Macron , who last week made a series of policy proposals to address the issues raised . <p> Some 18,900 protesters took part in the latest marches nationally , compared with 23,600 a week earlier , the Interior Ministry said . In Paris , where three protests had been authorised , turnout was 1,460 against 2,600 last week . <p> " Many of them were shocked by the behaviour and repression of last Wednesday , " Herve , one protester in Paris , told Reuters . " So it 's not surprising to see that it 's lagging behind a bit regarding the turnout .
