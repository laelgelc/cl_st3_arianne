<h> Kids to take climate change battle to Juhu <p> MUMBAI : As part of the worldwide movement #FridaysForFuture , started by climate change activist , 16-year-old Greta Thunberg from Sweden , students from 14 locations in the country will participate in India 's second mute march on Friday . In Mumbai , it will be held in Juhu and is expected to see participation from students and activists . People worldwide are set to participate in the climate strike . The India chapter of Fridays-ForFuture is holding it 's second version of peaceful demonstrations in Mumbai , Bangalore , Pune , Delhi , Hyderabad , Kolkata and other locations demanding climate emergency from the government . In Mumbai , it will start from Juhu beach 's main entrance at 5pm and culminate at the beach after Birla Lane . Advocate Girish Raut , an environmental activist , will speak to protestors about the importance of the situation and the attention it demands . <p> " It 's high time we realize our climate is facing a crisis . We need to identify this problem , accept it and move towards finding a solution for it . While an organised version of the movement takes place every two months , volunteers observe a strike every Friday . In Mumbai , volunteers visit places like Marine Drive to educate people about climate change and the movement . " Given the amount of consumption , this movement is absolutely necessary . It is necessary for our upcoming generation , underprivileged people and animals . This movement is for everyone . This is not an individual cause . Everyone should come together and join us , " said Maheshwar Khetan , an architecture student .
