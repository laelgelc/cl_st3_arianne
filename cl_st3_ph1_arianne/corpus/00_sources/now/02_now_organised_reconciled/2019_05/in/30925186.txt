<p> Five apartment buildings violating Coastal Regulation Zone ( CRZ ) norms in Kochi only have a week left to be demolished as per the Supreme Court order . Observing that no demolition procedures have started at the buildings , the Maradu municipality on Thursday served notice to the five builders asking them to start the demolition of the buildings to comply with the SC order . <p> The Supreme Court in its May 8 verdict had noted that the five apartments buildings -- Holy Faith , Kayaloram , Alfa Ventures , Holiday Heritage and Jain Housing -- under the limits of Maradu Municipality , were constructed with illegally and flouted CRZ norms . <p> Maradu Municipality Secretary Subash PK told TNM that the notice was issued as the builders have not yet started the demolition procedures . " The Supreme Court verdict has not said that the municipality has to initiate the demolition procedures . So to comply with the SC order , we served a notice as per the Kerala Municipality Building Rules 1999 , asking them to start the demolition steps within the time frame given by SC , " said Subash . <p> The municipality @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " On May 22 , we sent a letter to the District Collector and the Urban Affairs Department asking their expert opinion on the steps that need to be taken if the municipality is asked to initiate the demolition of the buildings . But we have not received any reply for the letter so far , " Subash added . <p> Meanwhile , the Department of Environment and Climate Change is assessing the environmental impact of the demolition of the five buildings , which are situated on the banks of tributaries of Vembanad lake . " A detailed report on the impact of demolition has been submitted to Chief Minister , " confirmed an official of the department to TNM . <p> Residents still hopeful <p> Though the Supreme Court had rejected a plea filed by section of residents to extend the time limit for demolition , the residents are still hopeful that they will not lose their homes . " As per the Supreme Court order , the committee was supposed to hear all stakeholders concerned with the apartments , but the residents living here were never consulted , " said George . <p> The committee constituted of K Gopalakrishna Bhat , IAS , in charge of Local Self Government , Ernakulam District Collector K Mohammed Y Safirulla and Maradu Municipal Secretary Subahsh PK . The committee report stated that the area where the five apartments are situated come under CRZ 3 region , according to the Coastal Regulation Zone notification of 1999 . As per that , the buildings are situated within 200 meters from High Tide Line , which is a no-development zone .
