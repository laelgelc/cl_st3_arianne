A new analysis suggests that climate change could increase fertility rates in tropical countries , in turn magnifying the impacts of climate change on those countries and widening the gap between wealthy and poorer nations . <p> In the study , researchers built a model that combined standard economic theory with data on how climate change is likely to affect different sectors of the economy . Climate change is widely held to affect agriculture more than other sectors of the economy simply because weather conditions affect crop yields . Other parts of the economy are n't weather-sensitive to the same degree . <p> They analysed the effects of climate change on a hypothetical economy modeled after that of Colombia . They found that if high carbon emissions continue , climate change will severely curtail agricultural productivity .
