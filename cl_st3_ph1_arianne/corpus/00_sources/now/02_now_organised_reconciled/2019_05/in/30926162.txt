The biggest success in case of disaster management pertaining to Cyclone Fani was that there was successful evacuation , which is very good . But , do we understand the state of the million people who have been evacuated from their houses into temporary settlements ? They were people who lived in semi-permanent houses . The question to ask is what is it that all those who have gone back home are left with ? <p> For many , their houses have collapsed , their belongings are no more , the crops are destroyed and livestock -- the biggest capital of village economy -- has been lost or has gone missing . The reality is that Cyclone Fani has caused extensive damage on the ground , impacting so many lives . In fact this disaster caused as much damage on the ground as the Chennai or Kerala floods .
