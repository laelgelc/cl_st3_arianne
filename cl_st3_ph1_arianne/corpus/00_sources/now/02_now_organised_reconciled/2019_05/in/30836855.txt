<h> Energy giants accused of not disclosing investor risk <p> The energy sector is thought to be exposed to between $1-4 trillion in transition risks alone as the world switches to renewables and pressure grows on policymakers to slash greenhouse gas emissions in line with the Paris climate goalsAFP May 03 , 2019 , 08:06 IST <p> Paris : Oil and gas majors are failing to disclose to shareholders the full extent of risks to their business models posed by climate change legislation , experts said Friday . <p> The energy sector is thought to be exposed to between $1-4 trillion in transition risks alone as the world switches to renewables and pressure grows on policymakers to slash greenhouse gas emissions in line with the Paris climate goals . <p> Carbon Tracker , an international team of financial experts who analyse the oil and gas sectors , said fossil fuel giants were of " principal concern " to investors , particularly companies with significant upstream exposure . <p> The Paris deal enjoins nations to limit global temperature rises to " well below " two degrees Celsius ( 3.6 Fahrenheit ) , and to 1.5C if at all possible . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ targets are only realistic with a rapid and drastic reduction of emissions from fossil fuels . <p> A study last month by the watchdog Global Witness found that the $4.9 trillion oil and gas majors plan to invest on seeking out and drilling new fuel sources was " poles apart " from the Paris goals . <p> Another monitor , InfluenceMap , found that energy giants had spent over $1 billion on lobbying against greener legislation since the Paris deal was struck in 2015 . <p> " They are disclosing bits but they are not disclosing the full picture , " Woolerton , lead author of Carbon Tracker 's Reporting For A Secure Climate report , told AFP . <p> In particular , companies tend not disclose breakdowns of future investment plans into upstream and downstream activities , according to Carbon Tracker . <p> -
