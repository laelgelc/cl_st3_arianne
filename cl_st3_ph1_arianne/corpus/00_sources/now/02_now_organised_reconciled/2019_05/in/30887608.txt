<h> Workshop on ' Revision of State Action Plan on Climate Change ' held <p> 20-May-2019 <p> Our CorrespondentJIRIBAM , May 19 : Directorate of Environment has organised a one-day district level stakeholder 's workshop on " Revision of State Action Plan on Climate Change ( SAPCC-Version 2 ) " at Community Centre , Jiribam Municipal Council , Jiribam , today . Speaking on the occasion , Development Officer , Directorate of Environment , Dr N Sanamacha Meetei stated that the Central Government has taken up various measures to promote sustainable development and address the threat of climate change at National and Sub-National levels . National Action Plan on Climate Change ( NAPCC ) was formed in 2013 to protect the poor and vulnerable sections of the society through inclusive and sustainable development strategy , he added . Stating that the Directorate has been conducting series of interactive programmes with different stakeholders for revision of SAPCC in the State , Dr Sanamacha conveyed that the workshop aimed at providing platform to share knowledge on issues related to climate change.Kh Janaki Devi , Adhakshya , Jiribam ; N Mohendro Singh , Advisor , State Climate Change and former member of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ GoI ; Dr Ng Ibotombi Singh , Joint Director , Vet and AH ; Kh Nimaichand Singh , Assistant Project Officer , Department of Agriculture ; Virendra Rajkumar , Assistant Engineer ; S Shanti Devi , Chairperson , Jiribam Municipal Council , Jiribam and P Ibochouba Singh , Board member , Khadi and Village Industries also attended the programme .
