<h> After long winter , now insects killing Kesar <p> ( L ) An infected fruit ; production has already been hit due to long winter <p> Yet to come to terms with almost 50 per cent loss of fruit this year due to prolonged winter , farmers of the popular and much-loved Kesar mangoes are faced with another equally , probably more worrisome , problem . Dangling fruits are being devoured by a variety of green caterpillars . So far , this infestation has been reported only in the stretch from Talala to Una in Saurashtra . However , considering that the Gir region , where this stretch is located , is the hub of Kesar mangoes , it is enough to send achill down the spines of farmers as well as mango lovers . <p> Entomologists have identified the invader as ' shoot borer ' . According to them , it is normal for the insect to attack the mango trees when they are in their vegetative state and eat new shoots . They are not known to surface on trees in reproductive phases . " The insect is from shoot borer group which is seen during the vegetative phase of the mango tree in August-September . <p> But this year , due to climate change , shoot emergence ( vegetative phase ) could be seen on many trees recently , though the reproductive phase is still on . This has resulted in emergence of shoot borers . <p> Since the fruit is more nutritious than the shoot , they are attacking it instead of the latter . We have sent scientists on the field . They will collect samples to be sent to laboratories in Bengaluru or Delhi . <p> After that , we will know the exact identity of the insect and take necessary actions . " <p> The attack of shoot borers has been seen on a stretch from Talala to Una in the Gir region of Saurashtra at present .
