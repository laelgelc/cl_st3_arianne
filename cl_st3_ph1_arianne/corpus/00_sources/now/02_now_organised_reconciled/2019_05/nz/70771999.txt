<p> Toko Mouth farmer Simon Davies was voted in unopposed for his second term as the Otago Federated Farmers ' president during the Federated Farmers ' Otago provincial annual meeting in Alexandra on Friday evening . <p> ' ' With regard to the AGM , I think it went well with no surprises , ' ' Mr Davies said . <p> In his report he said he had not realised the amount of time and effort the president 's role took up , including the diversity of topics he was asked to comment on by the media . <p> ' ' This role has been a learning curve for me , ' ' he said . <p> He called the level of new regulation and policy being generated by the Government a ' ' tsunami , which had started to crash down on to the agricultural sector . ' ' <p> That included the new forestry and animal welfare regulations , taxation changes , Nait modifications , new gun laws and new climate change , biodiversity policy and fresh water policy statements being worked on . <p> ' ' All of which will change the agricultural sector forever . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the changes around deemed permit and water allocation , as well as minimum flows and primary allocation units for rivers , ' ' all of which has serious economic , social and environmental implications ' ' . <p> He said winter cropping would again be scrutinised this year , particularly in Southland , and new regulations were being worked on as part of the new fresh water policy statement . <p> He also talked about farm safety , the need to attract more staff and retain the ones they had . <p> Dairy farmer Mathew Korteweg , of Kaitangata , was reappointed as the dairy section chairman , with Luke Kane , of Tapanui reappointed vice-chairman . <p> Mr Kane was also re-elected chairman of the sharemilker section . <p> Simon McAtamney , of Greenfield , was reappointed as chairman of the meat and wool section with Logan Wallace re-elected vice-chairman .
