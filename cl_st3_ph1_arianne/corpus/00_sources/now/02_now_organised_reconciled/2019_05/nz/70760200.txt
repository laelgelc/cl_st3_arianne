<h> Public concern over climate change hits new high - Horizon Research <p> The number of New Zealanders who think climate change is an urgent issue facing the country has reached its highest level in 13 years of poll tracking . <p> Horizon Research says 43% of adults now regard the issue as " urgent " , up from 8% when the company first started asking this question in 2006 . <p> 26% think it is a " problem now " , up from 16% in 2006 . <p> The numbers for those who think it 's a " problem for the future " or " not a problem " have also fallen dramatically over the past 13 years : <p> - In 2006 , 37% thought climate change was a problem for the future . 13% think this now <p> - In 2006 , 37% also thought climate change was not a problem , down to 11% . <p> Overall , the latest tracking survey of 990 adults nationwide , conducted in March , finds 69% of adults , around 2,207,282 million people , think climate change is urgent or a problem for now . 24% in March @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ problem equating to around 767,750 people . <p> Horizon Research says the most dramatic change in the past year has been in the number saying it is " urgent " .
