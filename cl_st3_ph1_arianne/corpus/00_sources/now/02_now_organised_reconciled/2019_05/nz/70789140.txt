<p> " This policy will make more efficient use of existing bus capacity , make weekend travel more accessible for families , and encourage the next generation of Aucklanders to become public transport users . " <p> Patronage on all public transport in Auckland is running 8.5 per cent ahead of the previous year and is expected to reach a modern record of 100 million trips a year next month . <p> This April , patronage was 10.3 per cent higher than the previous year once anomalies were stripped out , such as holiday and special events . <p> Allowing under-15s to travel free on weekends would lose revenue of $643,000 but could generate 989,000 additional trips . <p> Similar initiatives have been backed by the climate change lobby Generation Zero in a campaign it launched in March . <p> Other items to receive additional funding if confirmed on Wednesday included $5 million to tackle homelessness through the City Mission 's HomeGround project , $200,000 to fight illegal dumping and $250,000 to wipe out graffiti on rail corridors .
