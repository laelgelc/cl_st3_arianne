<h> Climate groups to rally NZ banks <p> On Friday 10th and Saturday 11th May , grassroots climate action groups across Aotearoa will take action to call for banks in New Zealand to stop lending to or investing in fossil fuel companies . The events in Auckland , Kapiti , Wellington , Nelson , Christchurch , and Dunedin are coordinated by 350 Aotearoa , as part of their Fossil Free Banks campaign , calling for banks to cut their ties with the fossil fuel industry . <p> The events across Aotearoa will include a series of challenges for participants to be part of , to represent the steps each bank needs to take to cut all corporate ties with the fossil fuel industry . For example , on Friday 10th May in Auckland , attendees will join teams to represent three New Zealand banks in a race to go ' Fossil Free ' : TSB , The Co-operative Bank , and Kiwibank . New Zealanders need a bank we can trust to keep our money out of coal , oil and gas .
