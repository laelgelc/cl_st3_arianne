<h> Insurers support Zero Carbon Bill <h> More stories about IAG <h> Insurers support Zero Carbon Bill Insurance Business <p> The Climate Change Response ( Zero Carbon ) Amendment Bill has been introduced to parliament , and general insurance companies have expressed their support for the legislation . <p> The bill includes a target to reduce all of New Zealand 's greenhouse gas emissions to " net-zero " by 2050 , except biological methane . It also creates a legal obligation on the government to plan for how it will support New Zealand towns and cities , businesses , farmers , and the public to adapt to the increasingly severe storms , floods , fires and droughts we are experiencing as a result of climate change . <p> " We 're especially pleased to see that bill will place a legal obligation on the government to support adaptation initiatives , " Insurance Council of New Zealand ( ICNZ ) chief executive Tim Grafton said .
