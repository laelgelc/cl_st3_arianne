<p> Farmers now wo n't be hit as hard by the Government 's flagship climate change policy that was finally unveiled today . <p> It sets out the plan for the next 30 years to limit global warming to no more than 1.5 degrees Celsius , in line with other countries , by reducing greenhouse gases which trap heat in the atmosphere . <p> A new target for carbon , from things like car emissions and burning coal , is set at net zero by 2050 , meaning it needs to come down to a level balanced out by the carbon stored in soil , forests and oceans . <p> But the target for lowering methane , mainly from cows ' belching , has been softened . <p> While methane makes up about half of total emissions , the new aim is to reduce it by 10 per cent by 2030 - a one per cent reduction target each year to 2030 .
