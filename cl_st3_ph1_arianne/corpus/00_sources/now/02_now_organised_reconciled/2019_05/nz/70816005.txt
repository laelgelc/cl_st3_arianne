New Zealand food producers have done exceptionally well . <p> New Zealanders are great at leading the world in protesting against discrimination or for a better future , particularly the fight for equal rights for women and the outlawing discrimination based on race , religious beliefs and sexuality . <p> Farmers are also known to protest . It 's the conversion of fossil fuels such as petrol and coal to Carbon Dioxide that is the elephant in the room . <p> So options about what to do are limited .
