<h> More stories about NZI <p> The NZI Sustainable Business Network Awards has announced its hunt for New Zealand 's most sustainable businesses has officially started . <p> Now in its 17th year , the awards recognise businesses , local government , social enterprises and individuals for success in sustainability . For 12 years now , insurance firm NZI had been involved with the awards as its founding partner . <p> " As an insurer , we see first-hand the devastating impact that a changing climate can have on our customers and our communities , " NZI executive general manager Garry Taylor said . " NZI and its parent group IAG continue to champion the awareness of the insurance implications of climate change for New Zealand . <p> " However , tackling climate change will take a collective effort , that 's why we continue to support the SBN and the incredible work it does to inspire this change . <p> " We look forward to seeing more of the innovation and passion that goes towards making this change in this year 's applications , " Taylor noted . <p> The awards have 11 categories . These include : @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for Change <p> Hardwired for Social Good <p> Partnering for Good <p> Going Circular <p> Efficiency Champion <p> Smarter Transport <p> Revolutionising Energy <p> Transforming Food <p> Restoring Nature <p> The supreme award will be awarded to a business for outstanding performance in all aspects of sustainability . <p> " The need to act on social and environmental issues is now more pressing than ever , " Sustainable Business Network CEO Rachel Brown ONZM said . " The business case is clear , but we need examples of the solutions and stories of success to inspire others . That 's where these awards come in . <p> " One of the best ways businesses can get credit and profile for their actions is through success in our awards , " she added . <p> The awards are open to all organisations and individuals in New Zealand . Submissions close on June 14.
