<h> Hauraki District rates set to change <p> Rates will be updated in the Hauraki district after July 1 , 2019 . <p> Hauraki District ratepayers will see some changes to their rates after 1 July 2019 . <p> The changes have come about for two reasons - an average non-water rates increase of 5.7 per cent set by Hauraki District Council in its 2019-2020 Annual Plan , and new property valuations set by Quotable Value throughout the district last year . <p> Deputy Mayor Toby Adams says the 5.7 per cent average rates increase was set after seeking feedback from residents and ratepayers last year . This was reduced from a 5.76 per cent increase indicated in the Long Term Plan . <p> " The increase will cover a forecast hike in the cost of services and infrastructure , such as roading and wastewater , and the cost of upgrading services to meet new Government standards , " he says . <p> " It will also cover an expected increase in spending due to population growth , climate change and other natural hazards , like storms , earthquakes and tsunami .
