<h> Polls in Oz narrow ahead of election <p> Australia is on the verge of having a fifth prime minister in six years , Labor 's Bill Shorten , even though he is less popular with voters than the incumbent Scott Morrison -- who toppled moderate Malcolm Turnbull in a coup by the right wing of the Liberal Party in August last year . <p> Many Australians are fed up with the factional infighting of the Coalition government that has been in power since 2013 , and its inaction on climate change -- among other issues such as accusations by female Liberal MPs of misogony and intimidation from male colleagues , the party disavowing two candidates for Islamophobia , and another having to step down after calling on the party to expel gay members . <p> The election tomorrow will be a tight one , though .
