<p> But we 're old school : Here 's a traditional look at the big numbers in today 's Budget . <p> Total new spend : $25.6b over four years . This includes $15.2b in new operating expenditure and a $10.4b capital expenditure envelope . The operating allowance has grown from $2.4b each year to $3.8b since the last budget - quite a growth . <p> Mental Health package : $1.5b package for mental health and addictions over four years . The big pieces of this funding are a new universal frontline service that will cost $455m , a $213m boost for ring-fenced mental health funding in DHBs , and $200m in capital investment in new mental health and addiction facilities . <p> The surplus : $3.5b for the 2018/19 year , but Treasury forecasts that to shrink significantly to $1.4b in 2020 . <p> New schools and classrooms : $1.2b for the Ministry of Education over the next decade .
