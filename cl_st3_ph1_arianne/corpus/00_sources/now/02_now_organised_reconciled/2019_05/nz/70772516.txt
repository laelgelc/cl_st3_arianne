<h> Caring for people , land helps teacher scoop farm award <p> Working for a Maori corporation with its values of caring for land and people was a big drawcard for 2019 Fonterra Dairy Woman of the Year Trish Rankin . <p> The environmentalist , mother of four , teacher and farmer is , with her husband Glen , starting a third season sharemilking on a 143ha Parininihi ki Waitotara ( PKW ) farm milking 450 cows in South Taranaki . <p> Rankin says working for PKW fits well with their approach to farming . <p> " A big reason we came to work for them was because they care about the people and the land in quite a public way , " she says . Glen and their four boys aged six to 14 are Ngai Tahu and she had a synergy with those values through what she had learned in teaching . <p> PKW has 15 dairy farms among its primary industry ventures .
