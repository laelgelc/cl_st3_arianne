<h> Students to skip strike , plant trees for climate change <p> Blenheim school children and supporters joined a global student-led protest on climate change . <p> Young climate change activists in Marlborough are to skip the national school strike this week , opting to plant trees in their " own time " instead . <p> A nationwide strike is planned in conjunction with the global movement ' School Strike for Climate " on Friday , May 24 . <p> Seventeen destinations across New Zealand will participate , from Kerikeri to Hamilton to Nelson .
