My answer can be summarised in four words : preferences , Adani , Murdoch and condescension . <p> The Australian Labour Party ( ALP ) can not govern the country without a good result in Queensland . With this cardinal rule in mind , I clicked onto the internet at 6 a.m. last Sunday NZ time . After noting the Liberal/National Party ( LNP ) victory , I typed in ' Queensland seats 2019 ' with some trepidation . The ABC website said it all -- on primary votes , a 4.4% swing to the Liberals and a 3.4% swing against the ALP with the loss of at least two seats .
