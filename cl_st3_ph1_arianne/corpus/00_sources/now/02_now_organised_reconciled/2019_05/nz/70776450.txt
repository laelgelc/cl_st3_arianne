<h> Project Jonah 's State of Marine Mammals in Aotearoa 2019 report <p> New Zealand has always been a nation where ordinary people play a key part in caring for our environment and native species . However , it 's important that those of us who are committed to preserving New Zealand 's wildlife are up-to-date with the latest threats faced by our endemic species . <p> Whale rescue charity Project Jonah has today released a report which shines a light on the state of marine mammals in Aotearoa .
