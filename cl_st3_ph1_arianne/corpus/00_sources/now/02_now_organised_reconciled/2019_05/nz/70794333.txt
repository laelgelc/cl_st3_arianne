<h> Zero Carbon Bill passes first reading in Parliament <p> The Climate Change Response ( Zero Carbon ) Amendment Bill has passed its first reading in Parliament . <p> According to Minister for Climate Change James Shaw , the near unanimous vote to pass the Zero Carbon Bill through its first reading signals strong bipartisan support for most aspects of the proposed legislation . <p> The bill provides the framework , institutions , guidance and targets New Zealand needs to plan climate action that will help limit global warming to no more than 1.5 degrees above pre-industrial levels . It also puts in place the requirement to develop a national adaptation plan to address the impacts of climate change . <p> " I appreciate the broad support the Bill has received in Parliament to take it to select committee , " Shaw said .
