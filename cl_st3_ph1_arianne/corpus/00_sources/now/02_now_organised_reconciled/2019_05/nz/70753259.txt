<p> Wickham said the way Lynda and Jools used humour to raise important issues resonated well with people . <p> The way they had remained relevant for 40 years by focusing on the relevant issues of the day , was a feature of the exhibition , she said . <p> " They are able to draw people into their music and talk about the issues that are important to us using humour - whether it is Bastion Pt , the Springbok Tour or climate change . " <p> Wickham said the twins were inspiring and she admired their contribution to New Zealand . <p> The exhibition was created by the Te Manawa Museum in Palmerston North . <p> Te Manawa chief executive Andy Lowe said it was " a celebration of the things that have shaped us as a nation " and the Topp Twins were a reminder of all the things that united the country . <p> " Whether it 's as Camp Mother and Camp Leader , the Gingham Sisters , Westie girls Raylene and Brenda , posh socialites Prue and Dilly or the Kiwi bloke and townie combo of Ken and Ken , the Topp Twins @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to us . " <p> He said the Topp Twins had also " cleverly brought a range of sensitive and important topics to our living rooms " -- everything from lesbian rights , feminism , gender equality , Bastion Point to Nuclear Free New Zealand .
