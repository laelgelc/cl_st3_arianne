<h> Lincoln supporting Climate Change Bill <p> Lincoln University Associate Professor Anita Wreford says many of Lincoln 's activities are at the forefront of the push to address climate change . <p> Dr Wreford recently became the programme lead for the Impacts and Implications Programme in the Deep South National Science Challenge , which aims to support decision-making about climate change adaptation . <p> This includes developing ways to support sectors , communities , businesses and government to plan for climate change and ensure that industries remain productive , flexible and functioning over time . <p> " Some of my colleagues at Lincoln are involved in research exploring alternative land uses , through the Our Land and Water National Science Challenge , and the greenhouse gas emissions associated with different types of production , " she said . <p> " There are also opportunities to generate co-benefits through land-based mitigation if measures are designed carefully .
