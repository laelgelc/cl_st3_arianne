<p> " Risk-based pricing means if you buy a dodgy , risky home you take on the entire risk for that property . It 's no longer shared in the community . So far , claims for the series of seismic events have cost private insurers and the Earthquake Commission $32 billion . <p> Heath said that cost led to reinsurers re-examining New Zealand 's risk exposure to natural disasters . Some insurance companies had since begun pricing for seismic risk , and were set to begin analysing flood risk and the effects of climate change . <p> She urged homeowners to think carefully about the risks associated with where they live , where they buy and where they build , and she hoped New Zealanders would become more insurance literate . <p> Heath has set up her own risk profiling company , but said the majority of her work was unpaid @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the financial and property sectors . <p> " Because insurance in New Zealand has always been a given and has always been such a low issue , people do n't think about it ; they do n't do the necessary research , which is now essential .
