<h> Our Seas Our Future supports Zero Carbon Bill <p> Our Seas Our Future ( OSOF ) is pleased at the announcement of the Zero Carbon Bill presented by the Government today . Once enacted , this key piece of legislation will lock New Zealand to its commitment of limiting global warming to 1.5 degrees Celsius . <p> OSOF has been an active voice in addressing the global climate crisis through our Climate Action Now brand , and overall mission to protect New Zealand 's coastal and marine ecosystems . <p> Climate change , and the clear association it has with rising sea levels , has been a contributor to the loss of marine ecosystems , increasingly adverse weather patterns , and frequent coastal flooding . From 1993 to 2016 the global average sea level rose at an average rate of about 3.4 mm per year .
