<h> Budget reflects ag sector 's importance -- O'Connor <p> Agriculture Minister Damien O'Connor says agriculture is the engine room of the economy and the Wellbeing Budget , delivered yesterday , reflects how important the primary sector is to New Zealand . <p> He told Rural News that the government is investing $12.3 million over four years for a range of programmes to strengthen New Zealand 's biosecurity system : an additional $472 million is earmarked for eradicating Mycoplasma bovis and $5 million for response activities to eliminate fruit flies in Auckland . <p> He says the new funding will boost MPI 's surveillance and diagnostic capability by increasing incursion investigation and laboratory capacity and will give New Zealand greater ability to detect and respond to biosecurity threats .
