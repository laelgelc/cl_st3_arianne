<h> OPINION : Relocate Queenstown Airport <p> Monique Kelly of ONE New Zealand presented a stark and fair challenge with her article " The true cost of aviation " ( Crux and The Wanaka Sun , 9/5/2019 ) , but not all her conclusions stack up . <p> We agree that climate change is a growing and imminent " existential threat " , and that the community should challenge the " inevitability " of Queenstown Airport Corporation 's ( QAC ) forecast increase from 2.2 million to 7.1 million passenger movements by 2045 . <p> We do not , however , agree with her conclusion that the idea to relocate Queenstown Airport is a " distracting diversion " that will " come at an exorbitant environmental , social and economic cost . " <p> Our research indicates the opposite - that relocating the airport would have a significant positive effect on each and all of these sectors . Choosing not to move the airport would perpetuate exactly the wrong systemic forces that exacerbate climate change and diminish the effect of our interventions , while raising their costs . Participants aspired to develop a liveable , peaceful and connected community , but the airport obstinately blocks this outcome . <p> Inspired by this community goal , a deeper investigation of the impacts of moving the airport has shown significant positive effects across almost all sectors . It would be better for Frankton , the Wakatipu , Wanaka , and the region . Better for the community , tourism , local transport , the environment , and global climate change . Better financially for housing affordability , for ratepayers , for the airlines and for QAC . <p> So how come it 's not obvious to everyone ? Partly because change to the status quo seldom is . And because unravelling the financial and other costs and benefits of an option not favoured by QAC is not easy . <p> Concerns regarding road transport emissions miss Queenstown Airport 's changed role from destination to regional hub . Currently most travel with just 1-3 people per vehicle . If the airport were in Central , most who are destined to Queenstown would likely use airport express buses , resulting in fewer vehicles and less emissions . Investment in an electric bus fleet would reduce emissions more quickly than relying on progressive electrification of private vehicles . <p> Relocation would also allow a quicker and more sure reduction in aircraft emissions . More direct flight lines from Auckland Wellington and Christchurch would reduce current domestic flight times by 6 minutes with a similar reduction likely for international flights . There 's strong potential to reduce weather delays that leave aircraft circling or force them to divert to other airports , reducing their costs and emissions .
