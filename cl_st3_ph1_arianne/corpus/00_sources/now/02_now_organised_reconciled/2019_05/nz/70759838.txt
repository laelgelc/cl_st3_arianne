<h> Government invests in clean energy centre <p> The Government will establish a clean energy centre in Taranaki to help lead New Zealand 's transition to a low carbon future , Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern announced today . <p> To tackle the long-term challenge of climate change , the Government will also invest in early stage research into cutting edge energy production . <p> " The National New Energy Development centre will help create new business and jobs in Taranaki while helping New Zealand move towards clean , affordable , renewable energy and away from fossil fuels , " Jacinda Ardern said . <p> " The centre will look at the full range of emerging clean energy options such as offshore wind , solar batteries , hydrogen and new forms of energy storage . <p> " Our global confrontation with the changing climate requires us to face the long-term challenge of sustainably powering our economy over the next 30 years to ensure we are the best place in the world to live , to work , to raise a family and to get ahead . <p> " For decades , Taranaki has been our top energy producing region and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be a leader in clean energy as well .
