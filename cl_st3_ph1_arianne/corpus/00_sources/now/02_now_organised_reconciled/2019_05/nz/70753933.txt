<h> Media freedom in Melanesia focus of next PJR and upcoming forum <p> Media freedom in Melanesia will be the focus of the next edition of Pacific Journalism Review in partnership the Melanesian Media Freedom Forum with academics and journalists invited to submit papers on the subject . <p> The research journal will focus on the political and socio-cultural challenges and constraints for a free press in Melanesia . <p> This will follow a special double edition due to be released this July . <p> PJR editor and director of the Pacific Media Centre Professor David Robie welcomed the opportunity to partner with the forum for the conference in November . <p> " Media freedom is tracking downwards at the moment and we need a challenging forum like this to clear the air over threats to the region , " he told Pacific Media Watch . <p> -Partners- <p> " Also , those courageous journalists in the region who are holding the line need to be celebrated for their work and this will be a tremendous opportunity to uphold media freedom .
