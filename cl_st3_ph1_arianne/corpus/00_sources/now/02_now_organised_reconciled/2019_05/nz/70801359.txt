<h> Climate strikers send a clear message <p> Auckland school student Zoe Mills reports from the scene of today 's climate change strike - one of thousands across the world <p> If there 's one word to summarise the school strike for climate change in Auckland , it would be - ironically - fiery . <p> Today , thousands of students participated in the worldwide school strike for climate change led by 16-year-old Swedish activist Greta Thunberg . More than 1660 cities and towns registered to participate in the strike in 119 countries . <p> The worldwide ' lie-in ' aims to send the message to governments to ' wake up ' and take real action on climate change . <p> This is the second time students have formally striked for climate change . The first took place on March 15 , with more than 5000 students , parents and teachers showing up to Aotea square . Students in Wellington , Napier and Dunedin also took part . <p> The series of strikes were led through the social media campaign #SchoolStrike4Climate .
