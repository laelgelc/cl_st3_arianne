<p> One of the silver bullets to sustainable food production is under our noses and will achieve wellbeing , not just in New Zealand , but the world . <p> The Government continues to overlook a technology , developed here , that addresses climate change by returning carbon to the soil instead of releasing it into the atmosphere . That 's fundamental . <p> There 's more carbon going out than coming in and the world has reached a stage where the process must be reversed . <p> Although the first step is to grow more plants and trees , it 's even more important to ensure the carbon that plants absorb from the atmosphere through photosynthesis gets back into the soil and stays there . <p> The future of mankind depends on restoring the biological health of soil and making it work for us , since three-quarters of the organic matter in the world 's arable soils , grown from only 4 per cent of the earth 's surface , has already been lost . Although about 50 per cent of it is lost when arable food crops are harvested , a similar amount of carbon remains in the unharvested straw and residues after harvest . <p> These residues are about 40 per cent carbon and are begging to be retained . But we remove them by burial , burning or baling instead of leaving them to rot on the surface for the microbes and other soil fauna such as earthworms to take them back into the soil . <p> If all of the uneaten carbon that food crops create globally was transferred back into the soil the impact would be massive . <p> But it ca n't be done with conventional cultivation .
