<h> Polling stations open for Australian election <p> Polling stations opened across Australia on Saturday in elections that are likely to deliver the nation 's sixth prime minister in as many years . <p> Opinion polls suggest the conservative Liberal Party-led coalition will lose its bid for a third three-year term and Scott Morrison will have had one of the shortest tenures as prime minister in the 118-year history of the Australian federation . <p> Morrison is the conservatives ' third prime minister since they were first elected in 2013 . He replaced Malcolm Turnbull in a leadership ballot of government colleagues in August . <p> The center-left Labor Party opposition under its leader Bill Shorten has been campaigning hard on more ambitious targets to reduce Australia 's greenhouse gas emissions . <p> Australia is the world 's largest exporter of coal and liquefied natural gas .
