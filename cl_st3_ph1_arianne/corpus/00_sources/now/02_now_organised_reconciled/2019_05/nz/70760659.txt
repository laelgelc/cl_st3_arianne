<p> " The Prime Minister 's announcement today of ' $20 million over four years to establish a new science research fund for cutting edge energy technology ' is welcomed , " says Otago academic Janet Stephenson , Chair of the National Energy Research Institute ( NERI ) . <p> " This , in conjunction with the investment in a National New Energy Development centre in Taranaki , will help New Zealand address the significant developments occurring in the energy sector , " says Associate Professor Stephenson , who is also Director of the University of Otago 's Centre for Sustainability . <p> " Challenges and opportunities arise from a wide range of factors .
