<h> Dairy committed to playing its part in lowering emissions - DairyNZ <p> DairyNZ chief executive Tim Mackle has reconfirmed the dairy sector 's commitment to play its part to reduce its biological emissions , and supports the intent of the direction of the Zero Carbon Bill . <p> " Our farmers are committed to sustainable farming practices , and need long-term certainty to make business decisions based on reduction targets . We are pleased the Government has listened to the science regarding the short-lived nature of methane , recognising it has a different impact on the environment , " says Dr Mackle . <p> " DairyNZ supports a science-based approach , where each gas is reduced based on its warming impact . We have not yet seen the Government 's analysis behind the 2050 target range . The 2050 target , of reducing methane by 24 to 47 per cent , is based on global scenarios that are not grounded in the New Zealand context .
