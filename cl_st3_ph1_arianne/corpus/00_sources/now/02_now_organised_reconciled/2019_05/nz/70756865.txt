<h> Youth climate group excited their climate law brought to life <p> Greens co-leader James Shaw addresses climate change protesters at Parliament , a day after he was attacked . <p> OPINION : New Zealand based youth climate change group , Generation Zero , launched their blueprint for a new climate change law two years ago . The Zero Carbon Act campaign convenor , Lisa McLaren , explains why getting a more ambitious law through Parliament is the most important thing MPs can do to drive the immediate , transformational change needed to cope with the climate crisis . <p> Generation Zero has a history of campaigning on climate change issues since its conception in 2011 and the ' Zero Carbon Act ' campaign has been our most ambitious campaign to date . <p> The Zero Carbon Act campaign started as an idea in a coffee shop back in 2015 ... " Yeah , why not ? Let 's draft a new Climate Change Act ! " <p> We decided to draft the law because we wanted to see long term change beyond our short election cycles . <p> AP <p> Climate Change Minister James Shaw , left , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an ambitious climate change bill which aims to make the nation carbon neutral by 2050 while giving some leeway to farmers . <p> But it is just a first step , and right off the bat , things are less than ideal . <p> HENRY COOKE/STUFF <p> Student climate strikers at Parliament . <p> The Government has decided to tie the Zero Carbon Bill closely with the ETS ( Emissions Trading Scheme ) by amending the Climate Change Response Act , rather than creating a separate new law . <p> This defeats one of the key intentions of the framework as the Zero Carbon Act was designed to provide long-term certainty and political stability . <p> It needs to sit above specific policies like the ETS that are regularly reviewed and changed . <p> So , we will continue to work , around our jobs and study , to ensure that , even in it 's flawed form , the law provides the certainty and stability Aotearoa needs . <p> And , just as importantly , the Zero Carbon Act must honour Te Tiriti o Waitangi by giving effect to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ knowledge and values to inform our guardianship of the environment . <p> The law must ensure a just transition by supporting workers in emission-intensive industries ( and their communities ) , by providing meaningful pathways and assistance to transition into sustainable , secure , low-emission industries . <p> And most of all , our law needs to match the urgency and scale of this crisis . <p> So we will continue to work , around our jobs and study , to ensure that more ambition is injected into the draft law , that Te Tiriti is honoured , and that workers and communities are looked after through the transformational change we need . Join us ! www.zerocarbonact.nz <p> * Lisa McLaren is a PhD student , and self proclaimed ' reluctant activist ' on climate change issues .
