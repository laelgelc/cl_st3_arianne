<h> NZ tourism industry increases focus on sustainable growth <p> Sustainability -- of our communities , our environment , our economy and our visitors -- has been placed firmly at the heart of the New Zealand tourism industry 's updated growth framework , released today at TRENZ 2019 . <p> Releasing it to an audience of international and New Zealand media at TRENZ , New Zealand 's largest annual tourism trade event , Tourism Industry Aotearoa Chief Executive Chris Roberts said Tourism 2025 &amp; Beyond addresses current issues but also looks beyond 2025 to focus on building an industry that continues to make a positive contribution to Aotearoa and New Zealanders in the long term . <p> " The key change is that sustainability is now at the centre of Tourism 2025 , providing a clear pathway towards a sustainable tourism industry for New Zealand . We have added Visitor , Community and Environmental goals and pushed our Economic ambition out to $50 billion a year in annual tourism revenue by 2025 . " <p> The original Tourism 2025 goal was $41 billion in annual revenue by 2025 .
