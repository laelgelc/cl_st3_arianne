<h> Definitive scientific maverick Freeman Dyson turns 96 <p> Theoretical physicist and Mathematician Freeman Dyson speaks during the New Space Exploration Initiative " Breakthrough Starshot " Announcement at One World Observatory on April 12 , 2016 in New York City . <p> In December this year the British-American physicist and mathematician Freeman Dyson will celebrate his 96th birthday . <p> Although not well known amongst the general public , Freeman Dyson is amongst the greatest living physicists . <p> He has made significant contributions in the disciplines of mathematics , electrodynamics , astronomy and nuclear engineering . <p> He is the definitive scientific maverick . Science needs intellectuals like Dyson to poke and prod the establishment and to test the rigour and veracity of mainstream science . <p> Born in England in 1923 , Freeman Dyson was a mathematical child prodigy . <p> When he was five he calculated the number of atoms in the Sun , at the age of 15 he won a scholarship to Cambridge University . <p> He began his studies for a degree in mathematics when he was 17 but two years later they were interrupted by the war when he was assigned to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ analysing statistics on losses of aircraft and pilots and how these had occurred , he recommended that two gun turrets on the Lancaster be removed . <p> He showed that this would increase the bomber 's speed by 80km/hour , greatly improving its ability to evade enemy fighters ; despite this his recommendation was not accepted . <p> In 1946 Dyson returned to Cambridge , after graduating he became a member of the academic staff . He wrote two influential papers on number theory and in 1947 moved to Cornell University in America to do a PhD in physics which he never completed . <p> During his time in America he became a good friend of the brilliant American physicist Richard Feynman . Dyson recognised the importance and usefulness of pictorial representations , called Feynman Diagrams , used to depict the mathematics and behaviour of subatomic particles . <p> He was one of the first physicists to demonstrate the application of Feynman Diagrams in atomic theory ; his paper was widely read and referenced . <p> One person who read Dyson 's paper was Robert J.
