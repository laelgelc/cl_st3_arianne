<h> Govt 's tourism strategy to tackle growth challenges <p> The government will play a more active role in the future of tourism through its newly-unveiled vision . <p> The New Zealand-Aotearoa Government Tourism Strategy was launched by Tourism Minister Kelvin Davis and Conservation Minister Eugenie Sage this morning . <p> The new strategy aims to tackle challenges of growth , including the lack of visitors travelling into the regions and inadequate infrastructure while sharing the benefits more widely . <p> Davis said the strategy was designed to deliver benefits to New Zealanders through productive , sustainable and inclusive tourism growth . <p> Tourism is a vital part of New Zealand 's ongoing success , supporting national and regional economies , creating jobs and allowing us to celebrate who we are , " Davis said . <p> The strategy outlines plans for more collaborations with industry , iwi , local government and across the tourism and conservation sectors . <p> It described its tourism goals as long-term and aspirational . <p> " As a steward , government looks across the whole tourism system to make sure that it is working effectively , and that the other
