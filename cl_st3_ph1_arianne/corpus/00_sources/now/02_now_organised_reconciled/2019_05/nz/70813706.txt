<p> Mr Tabuteau said the meeting focussed on a range of issues including infrastructure development , security , fisheries , the Pacific Islands Forum , climate change and health . <p> He said it was important for New Zealand to facilitate positive long-term outcomes in these areas . <p> " And actually part of the Pacific Reset conversation around the is how do we productively spend money now around resilience , sustainability , economic transformation so that in 10 years from now were not just spending another dollar on aid or recovery or things like that . " <p> Mr Tabuteau said there was also an emphasis on improving health support for Cook Islanders . <p> " How can we do more for you in terms of what do we do in New Zealand , and how can we extend that to the Cooks in a systematic way ? " <p> It was the seventh time the meeting had been held between New Zealand the Cook Islands .
