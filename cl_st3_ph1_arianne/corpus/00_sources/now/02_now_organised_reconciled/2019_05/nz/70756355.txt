<h> Govt unveils ' landmark ' plan to tackle climate change <p> The Government has revealed the much-anticipated Zero Carbon legislation to Parliament , setting targets that could mean huge changes to New Zealand 's agriculture sector . <p> It commits to what is called a " split target " - aiming to reduce all greenhouse gas emissions , aside from biogenic methane , to net zero by 2050 . <p> Biogenic methane - the emissions created from livestock such as sheep and cattle - is not completely exempt as the bill commits to reducing it to 10% below the 2017 levels by 2030 . <p> To reach the target , some of the measures could include reducing the number of gas emitting animals and accepting technological developments to reduce emissions from animals . <p> The bill also commits to reducing gross emissions of biogenic methane to between 24% to 47% below the 2017 levels , by 2050 . <p> This range , however , is subject to a review by the Climate Change Commission in 2024 to take account of any future scientific developments . <p> The bill - officially called the Climate Change Response ( Zero Carbon @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ targets will be achieved .
