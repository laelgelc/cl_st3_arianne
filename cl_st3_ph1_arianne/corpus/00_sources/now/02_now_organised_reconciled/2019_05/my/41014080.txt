<h> Malaysia plans June meetings with Australian officials over Lynas waste <p> 01 Jun 2019 / 09:05 H. <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The Ministry of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( Mestecc ) is seeking meetings with Australian officials in June to discuss ways to manage the radioactive waste from Lynas Corp 's rare earth processing plant in Malaysia , a ministry spokeswoman said . <p> " The discussions will be about the removal of the radioactive waste from Malaysia . The conditions ( for Lynas ) are intact , " she told Reuters , referring to the conditions the ministry set in December for Lynas to remove its waste stockpiles before its operating licence can be renewed . <p> Prime Minister Tun Dr Mahathir Mohamad said on Thursday that the rare earths miner will be allowed to continue operating its US$800 million ( RM3.4 billion ) processing plant , ending months of uncertainty over the future of the Australian-based company 's business . <p> Lynas ' shares jumped as much as 15% after Mahathir 's comments . <p> Lynas is the only major rare earth producer outside China and its status drew closer @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ready to use rare earths to strike back in its trade war with the United States . <p> Rare earths are used in batteries , computers , televisions and many other products . -- Reuters
