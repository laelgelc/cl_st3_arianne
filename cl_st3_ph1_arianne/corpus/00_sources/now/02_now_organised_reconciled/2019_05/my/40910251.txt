<h> New director for state DOE in Johor to be appointed soon <p> PUTRAJAYA : There will be a new director for the state Department of Environment ( DOE ) in Johor to take charge of enforcement there . <p> Announcing this yesterday , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin ( pic ) said the new appointee was expected to assume the post " within weeks " . <p> Currently , there are 32 rivers in the country categorised as polluted under Class 4 and 5 , 20 of which , according to Yeo , are in Johor itself . <p> " That 's why I have appointed a new state director who will be tasked with increasing enforcement , especially of rivers in the industrial areas like Pasir Gudang . <p> " He is one of our best . We will give him two years to make a difference and we will provide him 100% support , " she said . <p> However , she declined to give further details , saying that there would be a press conference later . <p> The release of effluents into the rivers were due to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> The former state DOE director is Datuk Dr Mohammad Ezanni Mat Salleh , under whose tenure the toxic pollution of Sungai Kim Kim happened , sickening over 4,000 people and forcing the closure of 111 schools in the Pasir Gudang district . <p> Mohammad Ezanni is reported to have been transferred to Putrajaya . <p> Yeo said the ministry was also in the process of drafting a new legislation to replace the Environmental Quality Act 1974 . <p> The present Act , she added , was inadequate as this was drafted in 1974 when industrial areas as well as housing and commercial estates were not as developed as of now . <p> " That 's why we need a new Act to replace the Environmental Quality Act 1974 , " she said . <p> Yeo added that the drafting of the new legislation would look into the existing provisions for penalties and who could be brought to court for pollution offences .
