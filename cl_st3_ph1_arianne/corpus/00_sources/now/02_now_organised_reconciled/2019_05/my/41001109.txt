of the 2019 Petaling Jaya Homeowners Low Carbon and Green Initiative assessment rebate scheme at the council . <p> The scheme , which was first introduced in 2011 , has in total waived assessment worth RM414,380.48 for 1,240 households in the city up to last year . <p> Once again this year , the council is calling from entries to participate in the scheme , which ends July 31 . <p> " The
