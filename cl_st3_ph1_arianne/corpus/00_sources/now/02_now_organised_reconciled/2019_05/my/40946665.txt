<h> Commercial and industrial property owners urged to explore solar tech <p> PETALING JAYA ( May 14 ) : Owners of commercial and industrial buildings , especially small-and-medium enterprises ( SMEs ) have been urged to explore the feasibility of installing solar panels on roofs to reduce electricity bills and enjoy incentives offered by the government . <p> Such incentives include green investment tax allowance and the Green Technology Financing Scheme ( GTFS ) , but many are still unaware of them , said Minister of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change ( MESTECC ) Yeo Bee Yin today . <p> Yeo was speaking to the media at the Sustainable Energy Development Authority ( SEDA ) Malaysia in Putrajaya where she launched the new SEDA Malaysia website which features a renewable energy microsite and a Net Energy Metering ( NEM ) calculator . <p> " A lot of people still do n't know these incentives , so that is why we are launching the NEM calculator and microsite to raise the awareness . <p> " These incentives are especially attractive for SMEs which have factories with big roof .
