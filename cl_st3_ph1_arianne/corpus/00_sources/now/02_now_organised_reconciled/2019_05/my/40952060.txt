<h> The Hive : Opting To Refuse Rather Than Recycle <p> Tucked in the residential suburb of Bangsar is a little green haven call The Hive Bulk Foods . Established in 2015 , this social enterprise and organic food store is fast becoming a hub for those wishing to lead a zero waste lifestyle ; if not completely zero , then to be a more responsible consumer . <p> Founded by Frenchwoman Claire Sancelot , The Hive is a co-operative that encourages package-less , bulk buying of whole foods and dry groceries , in its efforts to reduce the use of packaging . <p> The Hive also serves as an eco-movement base for KLites ; its cosy , simple and modern stores ( it has another outlet in Ampang ) hold weekly workshops , ranging from kombucha and beeswax wrap-making to vegan cooking and tips on natural household cleaning . <p> Recycling is not the solution .
