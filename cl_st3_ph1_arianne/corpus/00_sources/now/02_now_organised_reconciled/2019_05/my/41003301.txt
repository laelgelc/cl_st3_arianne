<h> Poland against EU plan to ban use of palm oil for biofuels : Envoy <p> KUALA LUMPUR : Poland is against the European Union 's ( EU ) proposed plan to ban the use of palm oil as a component of biofuels , and stressed that the republic does not intend to reduce the demand for Malaysian palm oil . <p> Polish Ambassador to Malaysia Professor Krzysztof Debnicki said that Poland -- one of the major food producing countries in the EU -- is one of the key recipients of palm oil , and the commodity is widely used in its food industry as well as chemical and cosmetics industries , and to a lesser extent as a component for biofuels . <p> " Poland does not intend to reduce the demand for palm oil despite being one of the largest producers of rapeseed oil in Europe . There is also no need for any embargoes related to Malaysian palm oil . <p> " Our food companies buy Malaysian palm oil through specialised Dutch and German companies .
