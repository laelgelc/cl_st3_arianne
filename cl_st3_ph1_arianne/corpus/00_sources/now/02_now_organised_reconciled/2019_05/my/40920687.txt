<p> Western Australia 's Mines , Petroleum , Energy and Industrial Relations Minister Bill Johnston ( above ) acknowledged that the best way to dispose of such waste was to return it to its place of origin , but said Western Australia would not accept the waste back from overseas . <p> The rare earth processed at Lynas ' facility in Kuantan , dubbed the " Lynas Advanced Material Plant ( Lamp ) " , originates from Mount Weld in Western Australia . <p> " Generally speaking , the best place for contaminated material is where it comes from , which in this case would be in the mine void , but we are not going to take mine waste back from overseas , " Johnston told The Australian . <p> Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin had required Lynas to ship its waste back to Australia as part of its licensing condition , in particular , the water leech purification ( WLP ) residue .
