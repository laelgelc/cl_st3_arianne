<h> Families play important role in addressing climate change : Wan Azizah <p> 15 May 2019 / 13:22 H. <p> KUALA LUMPUR : Deputy Prime Minister Datuk Seri Dr Wan Azizah Wan Ismail ( pix ) said as climate change is largely influenced by human activities , the family institution plays an important role in helping the world address the issue . <p> She said families should pay attention to this phenomenon as the effects of climate change such as the depletion of the ozone layer , increased carbon dioxide emissions , reduction of green areas , sea and river pollution would affect them . <p> Wan Azizah who is also Women , Family and Community Development Minister said the government through the National Population and Family Development Board ( LPPKN ) would further strengthen the role of families and communities in promoting environmental awareness . <p> " Through the Family and Community Empowerment ( FACE ) programme which had been introduced to 33 selected communities nationwide , eight domains of intervention would be implemented , one of which is related to housing and the environment , " she said in a statement today . <p> According to her @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) 2030 in conjunction with International Day of Families observed on May 15 , every year by the United Nations with this year 's theme being Families and Climate Action . <p> Wan Azizah said the theme is in conformity with the government 's efforts to embrace environmental protection practices among families and communities thus enhancing environmental sustainability . <p> In this regard , efforts to disseminate information and promote awareness should be increased with the involvement of various parties including the government and private sectors , educational institutions and non-governmental organisations ( NGOs ) , she added . -- Bernama
