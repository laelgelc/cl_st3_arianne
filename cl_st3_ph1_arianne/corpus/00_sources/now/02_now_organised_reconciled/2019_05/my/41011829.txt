<p> According to BBC News , the Philippines has announced that it is sending over 1,500 tonnes ( approx 1,500,000kg ) of rubbish back to Canada . <p> The rubbish was reportedly falsely labelled as plastic for recycling when it was shipped from Canada to the Philippines in 2014 , which later resulted in weeks of diplomatic tensions between the two countries . <p> According to CNBC , the waste was sent over as part of a private commercial transaction without the Philippines government 's consent . The country 's president , Rodrigo Duterte , demanded that the waste be sent back to Canada after the North American nation initially stated it would try to take the waste back by the end of June . <p> 69 containers of trash have already been shipped from Manila on a cargo vessel bound for Vancouver , and by the end of June , Canada will have received over 1,500 tonnes of rubbish . Canada 's government has announced that it plans to treat the rubbish at a waste-to-energy facility there .
