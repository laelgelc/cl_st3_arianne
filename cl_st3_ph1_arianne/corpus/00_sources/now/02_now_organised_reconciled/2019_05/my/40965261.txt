<h> Groups : Manage and use fund wisely <p> PETALING JAYA : Setting up a trust fund where contributions can be used towards better protection of the environment is a good move , say environmentalists . However , it must have a comprehensive structure and guidelines to prevent misuse . <p> Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia Prof Emeritus Datuk Dr Ibrahim Komoo said the Kumpulan Wang Alam Sekitar , which is part of a new environment law proposed by the Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Ministry , is " an excellent idea . " <p> " Scope on collections and uses require more inputs from various stakeholders . <p> " For a start , its implementation should be innovative , including introduction of environmental cess funds , " said Dr Ibrahim when contacted . <p> He hoped that any new law introduced should be comprehensive and look beyond mere environmental pollution . <p> " It should take into account issues related to the ecosystem services and environmental conservation , " he added . <p> Malaysian Nature Society ( MNS ) president Professor Dr Ahmad Ismail said that any form of rules and regulations should be integrated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the entire system , so it has to be comprehensive and not segregated . <p> " For example , in an area , you need to look at the source of the pollution , the industry there , how much problem has been created , the types of pollutant and gases released , etc . <p> " After understanding all of these , then there must be rules and regulations , " he added . <p> Dr Ahmad also welcomed the idea of the Kumpulan Wang Alam Sekitar . He , however , stressed that its management should be pivotal . <p> " Any funding is good to help the government but you must look at how or where the money is going , the sources , how it is used . <p> " There must be proper structure and procedures , " he added . <p> Under the proposed law , it was suggested that monies under the fund -- to be placed under the Federal Consolidated Fund -- will be made up of government allocations , overseas and local donations , and all fees and payments received by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ cess levied . <p> Monies from the fund , among others , is to be used to pay informers , carry out and promote research , recycle , reduce and counter pollution and to encourage restoration from any damage .
