<h> EU-M'sia ties to remain strong despite palm oil issue -- EU Ambassador <p> Palm oil fruits are seen placed on a wheelbarrow at a palm oil farm . -- Reuters file photo <p> KUALA LUMPUR : The European Union ( EU ) is optimistic that its relationship with Malaysia will remain strong even in the midst of the discord regarding the palm oil issue . <p> EU Ambassador and Head of Delegation to Malaysia Maria Castillo Fernandez said while currently there is no ban of the palm oil going into the EU , it is open to dialogues with Malaysia in order to overcome their differences on the matter . <p> " Our relations is more than palm oil and we should n't let it hurt the relationship that had been forged for so long . We have to look at the middle and long term solutions and we are always open to dialogues , listening to our partner in Malaysia , " she said . <p> She said this to Bernama International News Service during the Europe Day celebration here , on Wednesday . Europe Day is celebrated annually on May 9 by its 28 member states @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in 1950 -- that gave birth to the European Union . <p> Malaysia and the EU had been at loggerheads over the latter 's proposed plan to restrict palm oil in biofuel starting 2021 and to completely phase it out by 2031 . <p> The 28-member bloc had said that the cultivation of the crop had led to deforestation and climate change . However , Malaysia refuted the claims , calling it misleading and economically detrimental to the industry , especially to some 650,000 oil palm smallholders in the country . <p> Malaysia is the world 's second largest palm oil producer after Indonesia . <p> Data from the Malaysian Palm Oil Board showed that the country exported a total of 1.91 million tonnes of palm oil to the EU in 2018 , down by four per cent from 1.99 million tonnes recorded in 2017 . <p> The EU is the second largest importer of Malaysian palm oil after India , buying 12 per cent of the total palm oil produced in 2018 . <p> Fernandez said the EU representatives in Malaysia will continue to engage with relevant authorities , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ gain first-hand knowledge of the issue . <p> On another note , Fernandez said the EU will continue to support Malaysia 's democracy , it 's ongoing efforts on institutional reforms agenda as well as to improve its human rights standing among others . <p> Apart from the political , trade and economic sector , the EU collaborates with Malaysia and its civil society in a wide range of fields such as education , culture , environment , and sustainable development . <p> " We are Malaysia 's very close partner and we need to work for our relations and try to overcome the challenges like the palm oil issue . <p> " We are already doing much together and there 's many more we can do together , " she said . -- Bernama
