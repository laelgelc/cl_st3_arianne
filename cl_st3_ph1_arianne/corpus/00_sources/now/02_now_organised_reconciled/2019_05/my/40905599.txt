Drawn up by a panel including lawmakers , scientists , industry officials and analysts , their 277-page report also suggests a drastic overhaul for industry , agriculture and aviation . <p> The findings mark out the ways Prime Minister Theresa May and her successors can reach a target to cut net emissions to zero by 2050 , something scientists say is necessary to prevent more violent storms and rising seas that come with climate change .
