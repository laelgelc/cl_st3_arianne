<h> Alaya Consulting receives approval for science-based emissions target <h> Hong Kong company is first ESG advisory in Asia to have its Science Based Target ( SBT ) approved by the Science Based Target initiative ( SBTi ) <p> By The Asset <p> Alaya Consulting , a Hong Kong-based ESG advisory , announced its long-term Greenhouse Gas ( GHG ) emission reduction target has been approved as a Science Based Target ( SBT ) by the Science Based Target initiative ( SBTi ) . <p> Of the 554 global companies that have committed to setting SBTs , 210 companies have received the approval of the SBTi , which includes only 10 professional services firms worldwide . Alaya Consulting is the first ESG reporting advisory firm in Asia to have an SBT approved by the SBTi . <p> Tony Wong , founder of the firm , states : " We are proud that SBTi has approved our target . As an ESG advisory , we recognize the importance of being a part of the solution . In the wake of more stringent disclosure requirements on climate change proposed by The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong ( HKEX ) and the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ portfolios , Hong Kong listed companies have to step up their efforts in terms of identifying and managing risks and opportunities related to carbon . " <p> " By setting our own carbon reduction targets , we aim to show by example to corporates in Hong Kong and China how they can contribute to a sustainable environment for business and for future generations , " adds Wong . <p> SBTi is a collaboration between CDP , the UN Global Compact , the World Resources Institute and the World Wide Fund for Nature . It defines and promotes best practice in science-based target setting and independently assesses and approves companies ' targets . Targets adopted by companies to reduce greenhouse gas emissions are considered " science-based " if they are in line with the level of decarbonization required to keep the global temperature increase below 2 degrees Celsius compared to pre-industrial temperatures .
