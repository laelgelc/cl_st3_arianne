<h> Carbon Limits Appoints New Managing Director <p> Carbon Limits Nigeria , a joint venture focused on climate change strategies and mitigation actions , has announced the appointment of James Ogunleye as the Managing Director . He succeeds Paul Parks . <p> Ogunleye has over 18 years ' experience which spans projects planning and implementation , projects coordination , business advisory and carbon projects development . He specialises in renewable energy systems , climate change and electricity . He has also worked with specialized power and renewable energy consulting outfits that played active roles in the grid and off-grid power systems and emerging renewable energy industry in Nigeria . <p> The new MD has a Bachelor ( Hons. ) in Engineering from the University of Ilorin and Master of Science in Engineering from the University of Ibadan and another Master of Science in Renewable Energy &amp; the Environment , the University of Reading , United Kingdom . <p> Commenting on the appointment , the outgoing Managing Director , Paul Parks said : " I am very pleased with the appointment of James Ogunleye into the new role .
