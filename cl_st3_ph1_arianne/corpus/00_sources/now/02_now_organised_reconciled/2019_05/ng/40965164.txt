Here , there is mutation and a lot of anomalies are seen in such cells . <p> Some people do n't believe that climate change is real . What do you have to say to such persons ? <p> The issue of climate change has become alarming and very difficult to tackle , especially in a society like ours where human orientation and education is not enough . Flooding depletes available oxygen in the soil because standing water has less oxygen than moving water and this interferes with the transport of minerals and carbohydrates in the root . As a result of this , abnormal growth occurs . Hormone levels also change in flooded plants . Ethylene increases while gibberellins and cytokines decrease . Plants can be carbon dioxide-drunk too because when carbon dioxide levels are high in the atmosphere , guard cells of many plant species lose turgor and their stomata close . We should also expect more floods to affect residential areas , both at the coastal areas and inland . Discharge of poisonous fumes from vehicles , industries and mechanical devices may cause irritation to the eyes , nose , throats and the respiratory tissues . Increase in the level of ultraviolet radiation as a result of ozone layer depletion is dangerous to humans and may cause cataract , skin cancer and sun burns .
