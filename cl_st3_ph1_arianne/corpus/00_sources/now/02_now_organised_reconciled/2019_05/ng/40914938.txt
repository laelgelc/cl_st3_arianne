<h> Nigeria 's Muhammad-Bande declares for UNGA presidency <p> Nigeria 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations , Prof. Tijani Muhammad-Bande , has formally declared his intention to vie for the presidency of the 74th UN General Assembly ( UNGA ) . <p> Muhammad-Bande made the declaration at a cocktail party attended byworld diplomats and delegates in New York on Friday evening . <p> This came barely eight days after the current UNGA president , Ms Maria Espinosa , announced him as the first candidate for the position . <p> The News Agency of Nigeria ( NAN ) reports that election of the president of the 74th UNGA will hold at the UN headquarters on June 4 . <p> In a statement on its website , UNGA said the presidency of the 74th session was zoned to Africa in " full respect of the established principle of geographical rotation " , among other reasons . <p> The current president , who is the Foreign Minister of Ecuador , was elected on June 5 , and assumed duty on Sept. 18 , 2018 . <p> Thus she will step aside on Sept. 17 , as the UNGA presidency runs on @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ informal interactive dialogue with Muhammad-Bande is scheduled for May 13 , in line with UNGA 's other stakeholders . <p> Addressing guests at Friday 's event , the candidate pledged to make the organisation stronger and work better for its member states and their citizens . <p> He said as president of the 74th Session , he would focus on the effective implementation of existing mandates , and make a contribution in all defined follow-up areas . <p> The candidate promised to promote international peace and security , prevent conflict , strengthen global action to tackle climate change , ensure inclusion , human rights , and the empowerment of youth and women . <p> He also pledged to ensure that the decisions reached and resolutions passed at the general assembly were implemented for the benefits of citizens . <p> Muhammad-Bande , who hails from Zagga in present day Kebbi , has premier institution with responsibility for training and research in public administration and management . <p> Besides other positions both locally and internationally , he was the Director-General
