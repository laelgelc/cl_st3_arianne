<h> Main EU parties adopt climate change as rallying cry <p> In a shift since the last European Parliament elections , mainstream parties have adopted climate change as a rallying cry -- spurred in part by a wave of student strikes . <p> With the " Fridays for Future " protest due to continue in cities across the continent on the second day of voting , the growing consensus for urgent climate action has raised hopes of cross-party cooperation . <p> But there are also fears populists could torpedo this if they make strong gains . <p> A Eurobarometer poll shows climate change is now a leading concern for European Union voters , not far behind economic issues and rivalling worries about migration . <p> And , amid weekly protests over what activists now term the climate emergency , Europe 's mainstream political parties have finally grasped the issue . <p> " It is fair to say that climate and environmental policies now are embedded in all the political parties , " said Dara Murphy , campaign director for the European People 's Party ( EPP ) , the largest bloc in the outgoing European Parliament . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ become one of the top issues in the European elections , " Murphy told AFP from his native Ireland after trips to other EU countries . <p> He said the centre-right EPP added climate change to its campaign programme over the last two years based on research showing growing environment concerns . <p> But analyst Stella Schaller and Laurence Tubiana , an architect of the Paris climate deal , said global warming 's rise to major political prominence is more recent . <p> " We saw the debate tipping in the last four to six months , " Schaller , analyst at Berlin 's Adelphi environmental policy think tank , told AFP.
