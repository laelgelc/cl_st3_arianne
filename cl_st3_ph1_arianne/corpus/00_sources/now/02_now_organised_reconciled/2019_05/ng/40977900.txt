<h> Biodiversity key to healthy society -- HOMEF <p> Wednesday , May 22 , 2019 11:14 pm <p> Health of Mother Earth Foundation ( HOMEF ) has called for concerted efforts toward the preservation of biodiversity . <p> HOMEF , in a statement signed by its Executive Director , Mr Nnimmo Bassey noted that biodiversity played a crucial role in ecosystem functioning , including provision of goods and services essential to human health and wellbeing . <p> Bassey , however , wondered why policymakers were at ease while biodiversity was being eroded at an alarming rate , thus posing fundamental risks to the health and stability of ecosystems . <p> " The increased use of insecticides and herbicides on farmlands and the genetic engineering of crops to be insecticides themselves , kill intended and unintended insects and poses severe threat to biodiversity . <p> " Pollination , which is an important mechanism in the maintenance and promotion of biodiversity and is critical for food production , is threatened by the use of genetically modified insect resistant crops , intensive agricultural practices , pesticides , invasive alien species and climate change . <p> " More concerns are added as humans @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in the laboratory by a technology known as gene drives , " he said . <p> The statement also quoted Joyce Ebebeinwe , HOMEF 's project officer on Biosafety , as saying that a dependence of global food production system on the few genetically uniform varieties of plants , entrenched in a monoculture system , " is dangerous for the conservation of biodiversity and healthy living " . <p> She noted the inter-connectivity of species and their collective effect on food systems , while lamenting that humans were increasingly forgetting that all human beings share the same planet .
