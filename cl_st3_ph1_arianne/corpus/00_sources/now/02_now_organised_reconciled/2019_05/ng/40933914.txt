<h> Pompeo skips visit to Greenland amid new tensions with Iran <p> LONDON ( AP ) -- U.S. Secretary of State Mike Pompeo has cancelled a visit to Greenland to return to Washington amid an escalation of tensions with Iran . <p> Pompeo had been due to wrap up a trip to Europe on Thursday with a stop in Greenland aimed at promoting the Trump administration 's Arctic policies . Those policies were criticized earlier this week for not containing the words " climate change " when Pompeo attended an Arctic Council meeting in Finland . <p> The State Department says that Pompeo will still order a restoration of a permanent U.S. diplomatic presence in Greenland . <p> The Greenland stop was the second Pompeo cancelled on what was supposed to be a four-nation tour of Europe . On Tuesday , he abruptly dropped a trip to Germany to fly to Baghdad for meetings with Iraqi leaders .
