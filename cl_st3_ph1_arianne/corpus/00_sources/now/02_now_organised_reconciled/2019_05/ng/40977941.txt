<h> US says Trump coal moves hasten but do n't increase emissions <p> BILLINGS , Mont . ( AP ) -- Federal officials say the Trump administration 's decision to lift a moratorium on coal sales from public lands could hasten the release of more than 5 billion tons of greenhouse gasses . <p> But Bureau of Land Management officials said in a report released Wednesday that the emissions would occur regardless of the administration 's decision . They also say the huge quantity of greenhouse gases involved represents a small percentage of the nation 's total emissions . <p> The report comes after a court ruled last month that the administration failed to consider the environmental effects of its resumption in 2017 of coal sales . A moratorium had been imposed under President Barack Obama over worries about climate change .
