<h> Brazil 's environment minister challenges Amazon Fund <p> SAO PAULO ( AP ) -- Brazil 's environment minister questioned on Friday the effectiveness of the Amazon Fund created to contain the deforestation of the vast rainforest , the latest challenge to environmentalists from climate-change sceptics within the far-right government of President Jair Bolsonaro . <p> In a press conference at the Brazilian Institute of Environment and Natural Resources , Ricardo Salles cited numerous alleged irregularities in contracts between non-governmental organizations and the fund , challenging a belief among scientists and climate change experts that the fund efficiently contributes to anti-deforestation efforts . <p> Salles , who has said in the past that agribusiness in Brazil was " under threat , " said there was a need to create " indicators that could tangibly demonstrate the concrete result of the use of resources . " <p> The fund was created in 2008 to receive donations to help prevent , monitor and combat deforestation in the nearly 7 million square kilometer area , which is extremely rich in biodiversity . <p> Norway is the fund 's main contributor , having given $1.2 billion between 2008 and 2018 , official data @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Brazilian oil company Petrobras . <p> The environmental agenda of Bolsonaro , who questions the reality of climate change and has pledged to further open the Amazon to development , has been harshly criticized by activists and the opposition . <p> Before coming to power , the former army captain said environmental groups in Brazil hindered economic development and vowed that if elected , " not a centimeter " more of land would be demarcated for indigenous reserves . <p> He also threatened to follow the footsteps of U.S. President Donald Trump and withdraw Brazil from the Paris climate agreement . He later backpedaled on the promise , but activists and experts say the administration 's agenda would make it impossible for Brazil to meet the accord 's objectives . <p> Environmental specialists consulted by The Associated Press said they believed the fund helped anti-deforestation efforts , but emphasized it was a tool that could not solve the problem alone . <p> " Salles says it does not work but he has never gone to the field to analyze the Amazon , " argued Adriana Ramos , director of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ committee . <p> On Friday , Salles presented to the press the summary of a ministry audit into contracts signed by the Amazon Fund in recent years . He said the ministry evaluated , over a period of 90 days , a quarter of the 103 current contracts and that they found irregularities in all of them . However , he did not give any specific cases , citing confidentiality clauses until a review by auditors . <p> In his presentation , the minister highlighted that 82% of the contracts were awarded without having gone through a tender . He said the ministry will push for new rules to increase supervision over the fund 's activities . <p> Marcio Astrini , public policy coordinator for Greenpeace Brazil , said Salles sought to " destroy the entire agenda of environmental protection " in Brazil . <p> Last year deforestation recorded its worst rate in a decade , with a 14% increase between August 2017 and July 2018 , which analyst are attributing to uncontrolled logging and land invasion .
