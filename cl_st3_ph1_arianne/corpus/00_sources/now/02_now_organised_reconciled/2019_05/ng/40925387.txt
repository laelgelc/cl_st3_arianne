<h> UN General Assembly president arrives Nigeria , to meet President Buhari <p> - The president of the United Nations General Assembly has arrived in Nigeria <p> - Maria Espinosa said the challenges in Africa were priorities for the UN <p> - The diplomat commended Nigeria for its leadership role on the issues of climate change , environmental protection , peace and security <p> Maria Espinosa , president of the United Nations General Assembly , arrived Abuja on Monday , May 6 , on a working visit to Nigeria , stating that addressing the challenges in Africa was a priority of the UN . <p> Espinosa said that Africa was at the forefront of global development and her visit to the region for the second time as 73rd president of the UNGA indicates the interest of the UN in Africa . <p> She said that in the course of her visit , she would be meeting with President Muhammadu Buhari , Ministers of Foreign Affairs and Women and Social Development to discuss strengthening bilateral ties . <p> Espinosa 's tenure as 73rd president of the UN General Assembly would come to an end in September .
