<h> Demolition Looms As FCTA Restores Green Areas <p> The Federal Capital Territory Administration ( FCTA ) has disclosed that many structures in the city centre , and those along the green belt of Asokoro district would be demolished , in its efforts to restore all green areas of the Federal Capital Territory ( FCT ) . <p> Briefing newsmen yesterday on the resolutions of the FCT executive council meeting , the executive secretary , Federal Capital Development Authority ( FCDA ) , Engr . Umar Gambo Jibrin , frowned at the manner the green areas in the territory have been vandalised , adding that the administration has resolved to restore the areas , in line with the Abuja master plan . <p> Jibrin lamented the effect of climate change on the environment , largely because of the removal of green areas fom the environment , adding that the new park policy in the FCT is to promote greening , while green parks would be encouraged . <p> The executive secretary also decried the conversion of parks and garden to beer parlours and dens of criminals , adding that the original idea for the creation of relaxation spots for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ be restored to its original purpose . <p> Also speaking , the director of Abuja Geographic Information Systems ( AGIS ) , Dr. Isa Jalo , explained that the new park policy would address the lingering issues of converting gardens for other uses . <p> On his part , the director , FCT Directorate of Road Traffic Services ( FCT-DRTS ) , <p> Wadata Aliyu Bodinga , stated that the ban on motorcycles and tricycle in FCT city centres remained in force , adding that their influx of into the city remains prohibited . <p> Bodinga also called on security agencies and the general public to remain supportive of the policy to give FCT residents good life , just as he called on the public to withdraw patronage of motorcycles in the city to discourage their activities .
