In coastal regions like San Jose , overpumping allows seawater to seep into the city 's aquifers , exposing local residents to excess sodium in their drinking water . The problem is compounded by sea level rise , which pushes seawater inland toward the city 's filtration system . <p> Liccardo calls the combined threat of droughts and floods " one of those very serious but underreported challenges of climate change .
