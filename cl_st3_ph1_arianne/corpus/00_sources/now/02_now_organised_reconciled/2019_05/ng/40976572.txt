<h> Research Links Erratic Rainfall , Flooding To Climate Change <p> A research conducted by the Michigan State University ( MSU ) has attributed heat , erratic rainfall , flooding and desertification in the country to the impact of climate change . <p> This is even as it was revealed that the heat led to a decline in crop yield , livestock stress and post-harvest storage , while erratic rainfall also contributed to decline in crop yield . <p> Associate professor of MSU , Prof Laura Schmitt Olabisi , stated this in Abuja , yesterday during the presentation of the research findings on climate change and climate change adaptation in Nigeria , an event hosted by the ministry of environment . <p> The research which was funded by the United States Agency for International Development ( USAID ) under the Feed the Future Initiative , was implemented by MSU and International Food Policy Researchers Institute ( IFPRI ) in collaboration with Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development , Nigeria . <p> Olabisi noted that MSU collaborated with Nigerian faculty and students in boosting the research.While adding that desertification triggered farmer-herdsmen conflict in Nigeria , she said the research @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in Ebonyi . <p> Olabisi disclosed that the communities were well aware of the impact of climate change and were putting measures in place to solve it . <p> She stressed that Nigeria was so diverse in terms of climate impact , agricultural and cultural practise , just as she suggested that building resilience among community was essential in addressing the impact of climate change .
