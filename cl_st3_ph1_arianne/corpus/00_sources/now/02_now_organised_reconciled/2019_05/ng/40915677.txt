<h> Italian artist Lorenzo Quinn builds bridges over Venice <p> MILAN ( AP ) -- Italian artist Lorenzo Quinn created a splash on the sidelines of the Venice Biennale contemporary art fair two years ago with a gigantic sculpture of a child 's hands reaching out of the Grand Canal , calling attention to climate change that threatens , among other things , to sink the lagoon city . <p> For this edition , Quinn has created a successor sculpture that he wants to be a call to action : Six pairs of arching hands creating a bridge over a Venetian waterway , symbolic of the need to build bridges and overcome divisions . <p> They are being erected in the Arsenale former shipyard against the backdrop of a city that stands as an historic East-West gateway -- and as Europe prepares to vote in a continent-wide election that is shaping up as a battle of populism against more open social democratic traditions . <p> The sculpture is n't meant as a campaign platform , Quinn says . But his ideals are clear . <p> " Humanity has never grown by creating barriers . " Venice is a testament to that . Venice opened routes to Asia , the Far East , with Marco Polo and the Merchants of Venice . It has been a driving force of European growth always . " <p> Quinn 's " Building Bridges " installation , like " Support " before it , is a collateral event to the Venice Biennale , which runs May 11-Nov. 24 . Both projects have been backed by Venice city officials , after the Biennale refused to accept " Support " two years ago . <p> But the lack of formal art fair acceptance has hardly mattered . The monumental sculpted hands proved to be one of the most popular works of 2017 , even though it was out of the official Biennale , one of the many side events that vie for the attention of the art world that floods Venice every two years . <p> Due to its enormous success , " Support @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 2017 Biennale , and now is slated for permanent installation on the island of San Servolo , opposite the Biennale 's Giardini . Quinn said that cities -- he wo n't say which -- already have opened a bidding process to win " Building Bridges " once its Biennale stint is closed . <p> Quinn -- the 52-year-old son of the late actor Anthony Quinn -- said the biggest compliments he received for " Support " were not for its execution or aesthetics . <p> " Quite a few people said to me that the sculpture ' Support ' struck a chord in them , in that due to the sculpture they changed their daily habits , " he said , from not running water while brushing their teeth to taking shorter showers . <p> " If we all start doing things it creates a huge change . " <p> " Building Bridges " will be officially unveiled Friday with a performance by Italian tenor Andrea Bocelli.
