<h> UN leader arrives in New Zealand on climate change trip <p> WELLINGTON , New Zealand ( AP ) -- U.N . Secretary-General Antonio Guterres has arrived in New Zealand as part of a trip to the South Pacific to highlight the issue of climate change . <p> Guterres plans to spend three days in New Zealand where he will meet with Muslim leaders in Christchurch to show solidarity following the March 15 slaughter of 51 worshippers by a gunman who attacked two mosques during Friday prayers . <p> Guterres also plans to visit the South Pacific island nations of Fiji , Tuvalu and Vanuatu . <p> In Fiji , Guterres will meet with leaders and senior government officials from members of the Pacific Islands Forum . <p> Climate change poses an existential risk to some low-lying Pacific islands . The secretary-general 's trip comes ahead of the Climate Action Summit that he plans to convene in September in New York .
