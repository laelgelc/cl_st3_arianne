Related terms include food insecurity and malnutrition . <p> Food insecurity refers to limited or unreliable access to foods that are safe and nutritionally adequate . <p> Malnutrition , which is an offshoot , is a condition resulting from insufficient intake of biologically necessary nutrients . <p> Although malnutrition includes both overnutrition and undernutrition , the focus for global hunger is undernutrition . <p> The vast majority of hungry people live in lower-middle-income regions , which saw a 42 per cent reduction in the prevalence of undernourished people between 1990 -- 92 and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are the most visible victims of undernutrition . <p> The World Health Organisation estimates that undernutrition is a cause of 3*1 million child deaths annually or 45 per cent of all child deaths in 2011 . <p> Undernutrition magnifies the effect of every disease , including measles and malaria . <p> The estimated proportions of deaths in which undernutrition is an underlying cause are roughly similar for diarrhoea ( 61 per cent ) , malaria ( 57 per cent ) , pneumonia ( 52 per cent ) , and measles ( 45 per cent ) . <p> Undernutrition can also be caused by diseases , such as those that cause diarrhoea , by reducing the body 's ability to convert food into usable nutrients . <p> For now , nations such as Nigeria will have to continue churning out relevant policies and strengthening those in place to reduce the number of persons who go hungry daily . <h> NEVER MISS A THING AGAIN !
