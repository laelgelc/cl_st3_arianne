<h> Celebrating Children 's Day <p> On Monday , Nigerian kids celebrated Children 's Day . Since they are a part of every society throughout the world , it is simply right to set aside one day every year to celebrate children . Globally , the idea of setting aside a special day for children around the world was originally mooted by Rubab Mansoor , a child in the 8th grade and was first proclaimed by the World Conference for the Well-being of Children in 1925 before it was given universal prominence in 1954 <p> Children 's Day was established as a day of worldwide fraternity and understanding among children . Countries were encouraged to pick their own days to celebrate these special gifts of God who are often referred to as leaders of tomorrow , as well as promote mutual exchange and understanding among them , and also initiate beneficial activities that promote their welfare . For us here in Nigeria , Children 's Day is celebrated annually on May 27 . <p> Every international children 's day comes bearing its own theme for celebration .
