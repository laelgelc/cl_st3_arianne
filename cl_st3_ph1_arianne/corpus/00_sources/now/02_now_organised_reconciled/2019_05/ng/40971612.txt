<h> Treasury looting as a national security matter <p> I am aghast when I hear people classify direct grabbing of public money not yet allotted for any project by public officials as corruption . Corruption is too generic word that overarches any dishonest or fraudulent behaviour by people in power , commonly racketeering , bribery , laundering , embezzlement , and so on . These subsets of corruption have transactional undercurrents . They border on surreptitious provision of goods and/or services to a third party so that the public actor can benefit from the illegitimate conduct . Corruption-affiliated crimes are practically characterised with disguise to mask the crimes as legitimate . <p> The similarity between razing the public treasury ( razing ) , and bribery , racketeering , laundering , sexual harassment and embezzlement ends with the illegal availing of public power to further personal interests . Under the prevailing classification of abuse of public power , everything goes . It is a whole different ballgame when a public official asks a central bank to issue millions or billions of dollars into his or her domestic and/or foreign accounts . It is disingenuous to subsume all official 's malfeasances under one nomenclature : corruption . Even if the malapropos typology fitted , the relationship of barefaced snatching ( razing ) of public funds to other subset crimes of public malfeasance is more of correlation not direct or causal linkage , making the intent of razing profoundly different from that of its cousins . <p> Homicide cascades into first degree murder , second degree murder , voluntary manslaughter , involuntary manslaughter along the variations in criminal intent ( mens rea ) .
