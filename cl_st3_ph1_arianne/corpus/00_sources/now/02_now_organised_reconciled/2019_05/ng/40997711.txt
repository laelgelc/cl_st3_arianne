<h> British PM 's resignation : Lessons for Nigerian leaders <p> On Friday last week British Prime Minister Theresa May declared that she would step aside having failed to come up with a BREXIT agreement to lead her country out of the European Union . In deciding to relinquish office because of her failure to deliver upon a campaign promise and the results of a referendum , she set an example which Nigerian leaders should emulate . Indeed , there are many lessons for Nigerian leaders to learn from the episode . The British Prime Minister said quite correctly that in a democracy if you give people a choice you have the duty to implement what they decide . To her and all principled political leaders it follows logically that if you ca n't deliver , you step aside . Many Nigerian political leaders will pour scorn on her for giving up the comforts , protocol and prestige of Prime Ministership without losing an election . <p> Constitutionally she did n't have to go , it was simply the right thing for her to do . They react to obvious public disapproval as expressed by opinion-poll ratings , and the disaffection of their appointees and the media . Nigerian leaders on the other hand ignore public opinion and sit-tight no matter their failures . They get carried away by the pomp , pageantry and protocol of office and pay little attention to the substance . <p> ADVERTISEMENT <p> The outgoing British Prime Minister made it very clear as to what leaders are supposed to do . She said that the unique privilege of high office is " to use the platform to give a voice to the voiceless , and to fight the burning injustices that still scar our society " . May 's downfall could never have happened in Nigeria . Firstly , because her Ministers deserted her and resigned on principle . In Nigeria Ministers do n't resign on principle merely because they disagree with their boss , invariably they resign to move upwards not downwards . They resign so as to contest elections for governorship or take up lucrative foreign appointments . In Nigeria once appointed as a Minister it 's your duty to defend the indefensible and play the role of " yes man " . Prime Minister May 's speech inadvertently revealed a major reason for her failure . She said that she believed that it was right for her to persevere even when the odds against success seemed high . She now knows better ! In Nigeria there is a long history of leaders pushing through policies which from the onset had little or no chance of success simply because they have no principled opposition from within .
