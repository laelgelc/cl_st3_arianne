<h> 29 Scientists Jostle For Drought , Desertification Prize <p> Twenty-nine scientists will vie to find long-lasting solutions to erosions , droughts and desertification plaguing different parts of the country in the 2019 edition of the Nigeria Prize for Science sponsored by Nigeria LNG Limited ( NLNG ) . <p> Speaking while handing over the entries to the Advisory Board , chaired by Professor Alfred Akpoveta Susu , NLNG 's Corporate Communications and Public Affairs Manager , Andy Odeh said the science prize was first awarded in 2004 and now in its 15th year , comes with a cash prize of $100 , 000 . <p> He added that the handover of the 2019 entries to the Advisory Board of the prize signifies the beginning of the judging process which will culminate in the announcement of a winner . <p> Odeh said , " The business of scientific innovation and research is not an easy task anywhere in the world . In Nigeria , it is even more difficult because of the paucity of research funds and the resulting increasing lack of interest . <p> He stated further that Nigeria LNG will continue through the Nigeria Prize for Science @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , " in the darkest corner " as NLNG is committed to changing the narrative of the nation through scientific prowess , through which many nations have excelled . <p> Odeh pointed out that although Climate Change remains a myth to so many people . Its impact can be seen on our environment and agriculture . <p> " We have all seen the growing rate of desertification in the northern part of our country , he said , adding " Climate Change is real and that is why NLNG believes that solutions to this threat can be engendered through a competition such as The Nigeria Prize for Science . The prize is another opportunity for NLNG to contribute to the development of the country . <p> Receiving the entries on behalf of the Advisory Board , Professor Akpoveta Susu , remarked that regardless of the number of entries is , the judging process for the competition must take its course .
