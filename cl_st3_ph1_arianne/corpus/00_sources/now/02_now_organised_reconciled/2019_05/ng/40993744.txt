<h> Edo To Partner HPECDI To Prevent Climate Change Crisis <p> BENIN -- Edo State Government has expressed its readiness to partner Health Promotion Education and Community Development Initiative ( HPECDI ) and 350 Nigeria.org , a non-govermental organization , to ensure transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy so as to prevent climate change crisis . <p> Commissioner for Environment and Sustainability , Omoua Alonge Oni-Okpaku , made this known while receiving members of the group , who were in her office to seek the ministry 's collaboration in actualizing a cleaner environment . <p> Addressing the gathering through his permanent Secretary , Mr Lucky Wasa , the commissioner said that over the years , Edo State has demonstrated its readiness and willingness to support a healthier and friendlier environment . <p> He said the ministry has been engaging youth in the protection and preservation of the environment in line with the state government 's ' Keep Edo Clean ' project . <p> He noted that transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy could be actualised , though it was going to be a gradual process , while assuring the group of government support .
