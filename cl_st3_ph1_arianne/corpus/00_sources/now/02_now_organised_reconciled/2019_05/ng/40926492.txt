<h> APPs <h> ABOUT US <h> Migration : Onyeama Seeks Support From UNGA President <p> Channels TelevisionUpdated May 7 , 2019 <p> Nigeria 's Minister of Foreign Affairs , Geoffrey Onyeama , on Tuesday , asked for support from the President of United Nations General Assembly ( UNGA ) to address the issues of climate change and migration in Nigeria . <p> Onyeama said this during a meeting with President of the UNGA , Maria Garces , at the nation 's capital in Abuja . <p> He also asked for the support of the UNGA in recharging the Lake Chad which according to him has shrinked by over 90% . <p> According to him , an estimate of forty to fifty billion dollars is required to recharge the lake . <p> Garces in response said the multilateral cooperation remains key to addressing challenges like climate change and migration . <p> She praised the role of the Nigerian government at the United Nations and added that the UN remains committed to helping Nigeria address issues of poverty and inequality . <p> They were received into the country by officials of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the United Nations office @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( PGA ) , Maria Fernanda Espinosa Garces ( M ) ; Senior Adviser to the PGA , Alexander Tope , and other UN officials , during the arrival of Maria Garces at the Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja on Monday .
