<h> Governors should be transparent in utilisation of public funds -- Former UN envoy , Prof. Gambari <p> Professor Ibrahim Gambari is a Nigerian diplomat and a scholar . Gambari is a former Nigeria 's Minister for External Affairs and Nigeria 's Permanent Representative to the United Nations . He was also the first Under-Secretary-General of the United Nations , Special Adviser on Africa to the UN Secretary-General ; pioneer chancellor of the Kwara State University and currently , the Founder/Chairman of the Savannah Centre . He tells SUCCESS NWOGU that Nigeria needs to defeat insecurity and prioritise development <p> There are indications that a Nigerian , Prof. Tijjani Bande , will soon emerge the President of the United Nations General Assembly . How do you feel about this development for our country ? <p> That is a very positive development on which I congratulate our Permanent Representative in the United Nations , Prof. Tijjani Bande . Going by his wealth of experience , candour , behaviour , one is confident that he will make the country proud . Remember , he has rich experience , he is a former Director-General of the National Institute of Policy and Strategic Studies , Kuru . He was also the Vice Chancellor of Usman Danfodiyo University , Sokoto , between 2004 and 2009 . He has good academic record as he holds M.A. in Political Science from the Boston University , Boston , Massachusetts in the United States of America . He also holds a PhD in Political Science from the University of Toronto , Canada . Globally there are such challenges as growing economic inequality , climate change as well as terrorism .
