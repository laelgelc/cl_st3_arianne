<p> The pilot project , which came as fallout of the Maritime Technology Cooperation Centre ( MTCC ) conference held in Kenya , Nairobi in March , aims at reducing fuel consumption by vessels . <p> The MTCC is targeted towards developing countries , and Nigeria was made a priority and nominated for the pilot project . <p> Dakuku Peterside , Director-General of NIMASA , while speaking at the installation ceremony , reiterated the Agency 's commitment to global best practices in the execution of its mandate of regulating the maritime industry . <p> Peterside , who was represented by the Head of the Marine Environment Management ( MEM ) Unit at NIMASA , Dr. Felicia Mogo , said : " NIMASA is ensuring that we are keeping to global best practices with a commendable track record in administration and regulation in the maritime sector . <p> " NIMASA was chosen to lead the project in Nigeria , being the chosen country in the West and Central Africa region . This pilot project will last a year . But the data will be reported to the Kenyan portal , then it goes to the IMO global portal after every @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ three-month period . The findings and analyses are then recorded and reported at the end of the year to inform the country of the amount of fuel being consumed annually on each voyage and how much GHG is being emitted . " <p> The launch of the fuel consumption data collection device is in line with the decision of the International Maritime Organisation ( IMO ) to reduce fuel consumption by vessels , as well as Green House Gases ( GHG ) and Carbon Dioxide ( CO2 ) emission on voyages . <p> NIMASA was nominated to operate the project in Nigeria , which has become a focal point and so nominated as a country of interest in Africa for the pilot project . <p> Representing Sea Transport Limited , owners of MT KINGIS , Musa Ado Ibn-Saidu expressed delight in being a part of the landmark installation initiative . <p> Ibn-Saidu said the crew of the vessel was keen to use the tablet .
