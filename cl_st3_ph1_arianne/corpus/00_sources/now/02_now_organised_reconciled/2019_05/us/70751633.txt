<h> UC Berkeley crowned Coolest Campus in 2019 Cool Campus Challenge <h> Share <p> After an intense flurry of carbon-reducing actions that had UC campuses tied neck-and-neck down to the last hours of the month-long competition , the results of the 2019 Cool Campus Challenge have been tallied and the Coolest UC Campuses have emerged . <p> The winners are : <p> Highest total points -- UC Berkeley <p> Greatest percent participation -- UC Merced <p> Health location with highest points -- UC San Francisco <p> Team with highest points -- Sustainable Squad ( UC Berkeley ) <p> Competitors had four weeks to complete actions to reduce their carbon footprint ending April 26th . <p> A surge on the final day of competition helped push the total participants to over 22,000 students , staff and faculty from across all 10 University of California campus locations and the University of California Office of the President . <p> Sheer numbers were n't the only great story to emerge from the competition .
