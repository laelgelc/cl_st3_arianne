<h> Vancouver Weather : Mix of sun and cloud <p> VANCOUVER , B.C. : Saturday , May 18 . Today 's weather is expected to be mainly cloudy , with a slight chance of rain in the morning , and clearing to a mix of sun and cloud . Environment and Climate Change Canada is forecasting highs of 18 C by the shore or 23 C inland . The rest of the long weekend looks mixed as well . On Sunday , expect a mix of sun and cloud , and slightly warmer with highs of 20 C and 24 C inland . For the holiday Monday , the region will see rain with the temperature cooling down to around 15 C.
