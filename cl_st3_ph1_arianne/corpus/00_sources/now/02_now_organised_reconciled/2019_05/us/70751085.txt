<h> The Carbon Market Potential of Asbestos Mine Waste <p> Researchers have devised new methods to turn toxic asbestos mine tailings into innocuous piles of carbonate rock and draw down atmospheric carbon dioxide at the same time . <p> A layered mat of cyanobacteria consolidated by carbonate minerals . As a result , the material has been phased out of most consumer products . But the legacy of asbestos mining in Canada and elsewhere around the world has left piles of hazardous material stranded at abandoned mines , with ongoing health threats to those living in close proximity . <p> Now researchers are looking for ways to clean up and repurpose that waste , all while trying to slow climate change .
