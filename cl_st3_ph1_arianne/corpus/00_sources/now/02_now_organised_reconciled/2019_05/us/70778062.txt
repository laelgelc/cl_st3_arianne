While the power sector and residential heating sectors have made significant strides in lowering their greenhouse gas output , transportation emissions by 2016 had actually increased about 2.4 million metric tons above the 28.9 million metric tons emitted in 1990 , according to the Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs . Annual carbon emissions overall dropped 21 percent , or the equivalent of 19 million metric tons between 1990 and 2016 . State law requires Massachusetts to reduce its carbon emissions to 25 percent below 1990 levels by next year , though no one will know whether Massachusetts hit that target until years later .
