Natural gas still emits tons of CO2 . Without doing anything else , San Antonio could meet that 45 percent goal . If you throw in electric vehicles and mass transportation ( another 38 percent of the city 's greenhouse gas emissions come from vehicles ) , we can mostly likely meet our city 's commitment to meet the Paris agreement . <p> Some say quick transition to solar and wind power would cost too much . What is the expense of not transitioning quickly ? Our own government 's Fourth National Climate Assessment states that U.S. weather disasters in the past four years come at a total cost of nearly $400 billion . In an understatement , the report says , " Acting sooner rather than later generally results in lower costs overall for both adaptation and mitigation efforts . The asteroid of climate change is approaching -- fast .
