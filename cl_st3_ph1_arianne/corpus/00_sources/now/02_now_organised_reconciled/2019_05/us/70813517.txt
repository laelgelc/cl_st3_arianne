<h> Return to sender : Malaysia to send back 60 containers of waste to exporters <p> A container filled with waste in Port Klang , ready to be returned to its country of origin . <p> PORT KLANG : Some 3,000 metric tonnes of plastic waste in 60 containers , illegally brought into the country , are ready to be shipped back to their countries of origin , Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Bee Yin said today . <p> She said they are among 123 containers in Port Klang which underwent a " labourious and costly " inspection process by the Department of Environment , police and port authorities . <p> Yeo said 10 containers weighing 450 metric tonnes would be shipped out immediately . <p> " These containers were illegally brought into the country under false declarations and other offences which clearly violate our environmental laws , " she told the press today . <p> Giving an example , Yeo said a recycling company in Britain had exported more than 1,000 containers amounting to over 50,000 metric tonnes of plastic waste . <p> " We have found a few companies from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ so-called recycling companies and will send the list to their respective governments for further action . " <p> Yeo Bee Yin . <p> She urged developed countries to review the management of their plastic waste and to stop using developing countries as dumpsites for their garbage . <p> " If they ship it to Malaysia , we will return it , " she said , adding that it was " unfair and uncivilised " to dump garbage in other countries . <p> She also warned Malaysians working with companies in the importation of waste , saying they were committing treason as their action was harmful to public health . <p> Yeo said the cost of sending back the waste will be borne by the importer as provided under the Environmental Quality Act 1974 , failing which Malaysia will invoke the Basel Convention , where the cost will be borne by the exporter . <p> " Malaysia will not be a dumping ground for the world . We will continue to work hard to make sure that whoever sends their waste to Malaysia , we will send it back .
