<h> Reader letter : What is true cost of mega-hospital ? <p> Re : Guest column : The new Windsor-Essex hospital system includes many wins <p> What is the true cost of the mega-hospital ? <p> The May 4 guest column 's statement that " moving forward with a $2 billion investment in the transformation of our region 's hospitals will be a huge win for Windsor/Essex " neglects to mention the additional costs -- land , parking lot , ancillary costs , furniture and equipment . None of them will be covered by the province . The hospital will have to fundraise to cover these costs . <p> The infrastructure costs to access the proposed site ( estimated by the city 's engineer to be $250 million ) will have to be borne solely by city taxpayers . Transit Windsor estimates it will need $2 million in new equipment and $2 million per year to service the hospital site . <p> EMS services will increase because of longer travel distances . <p> How much will the demolition costs be at the Met and Ouellette hospital campuses ? <p> What will the cost implications be in regards to P3 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ have struggled to maintain services , in part due to P3 financing costs . <p> What will the social costs be ? Accessibility issues will be created for those that do n't drive . There will be an extra burden placed upon people of lesser means trying to travel to the new hospital site . A one-and-a-half-hour ( one-way ) bus trip requires planning . <p> How will climate change affect the region ? Flood mitigation costs will be an additional cost due to the elimination of farmland . <p> We will not have any more beds than now . We will still have to travel to London/Toronto for specialized procedures . Hallway medicine will remain , albeit in a new facility . This plan does not address the total community healthcare plan . It neglects long-term care . By demolishing Met , an opportunity to increase long-term care will be missed . <p> Why has n't the steering committee revealed the real cost to the general public ? <p> Nestor Chyz , LaSalle <p> Share Your Views <p> Send letters to the editor to letters@windsorstar.com .
