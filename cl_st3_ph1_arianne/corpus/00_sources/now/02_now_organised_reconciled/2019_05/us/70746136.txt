<p> The question before the Court of Appeal was , " Will this Act be unconstitutional in whole or in part ? " <p> " The sole issue before the Court is whether Parliament has the constitutional authority to enact the Act . The issue is not whether GHG pricing should or should not be adopted or whether the Act is effective or fair . Those are questions to be answered by Parliament and by provincial legislatures , not by courts , " the decision read . <p> The federal government released its Pan-Canadian Approach to Pricing Carbon Pollution -- which Saskatchewan did not adopt -- in October 2016 . It proposed a pan-Canadian " benchmark " for carbon pricing , with a carbon price rising $10 per year to a maximum $50 per tonne in 2022 . <h> The players <p> Chief Justice Robert Richards concluded that " the Act falls within the legislative authority of Parliament . It is not unconstitutional in whole or in part . " Justices Georgina Jackson and Lian Schwann concurred . <p> Their two counterparts , justices Ralph Ottenbreit and Neal Caldwell , found that federal carbon pricing is unconstitutional . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Some ( including the British Columbia government ) supported the federal government . <p> Others , like the governments of Saskatchewan , Ontario , New Brunswick and the newly elected Alberta governing party ( then in opposition ) , opposed carbon pricing . <p> Interveners included SaskPower and SaskEnergy , the Agricultural Producers Association of Saskatchewan , Athabasca Chipewyan First Nation , the Assembly of First Nations , Climate Justice Saskatoon , National Farmers Union , Saskatchewan Coalition for Sustainable Development , Saskatchewan Council for International Cooperation , the Saskatchewan Electric Vehicle Club , and several others . <h> Majority rules <p> The Saskatchewan government asserted the Act is invalid because the Governor in Council determines the provinces where it operates , offending the principle of federalism . <p> " Saskatchewan 's arguments on this front can not be accepted , " wrote Richards .
