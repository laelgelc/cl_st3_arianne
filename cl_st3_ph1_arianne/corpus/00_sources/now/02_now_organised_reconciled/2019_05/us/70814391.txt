<h> Vancouver Weather : Long stretch of sunshine and no rain <p> VANCOUVER , B.C. : Tuesday , May 28 . Today 's weather is expected to be sunny and warm , a trend that will likely continue into next week , according to Environment and Climate Change Canada . For Tuesday , expect a mix of sun and cloud , clearing around noon , with a high of 20 C or 24 C inland . Tomorrow looks similar , mainly sunny , with a high of 24 C inland and 20 C closer to the water . Thursday and Friday are expected to be clear and dry with highs of 21 C and 22 C by the shore and 24 C and 25 C inland , respectively . The sunny , dry weather is set to continue through the weekend , with highs of 22 C on Saturday and 20 C on Sunday .
