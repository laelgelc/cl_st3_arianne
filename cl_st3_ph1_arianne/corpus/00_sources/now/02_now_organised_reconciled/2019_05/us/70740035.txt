<h> Paula Temple - Edge Of Everything <p> Paula Temple - Edge Of Everything It comes as a surprise that Edge Of Everything is Paula Temple 's debut album . After all , the Berlin-based artist has been a major part of the scene for much of the last two decades , and just about every techno fan is familiar with her punishing sound . Temple has earned her reputation through a very particular style of performing . Using what she calls a hybrid setup , the artist 's club sets blur the line between DJing and producing -- she layers in samples , and makes edits and remixes on the fly . This flexible technique is what brings depth and intensity to Temple 's sound . More importantly , it makes her sets unique . No one else is going to bang out techno tracks in quite the same way . <p> But beyond noise and ferocity , the defining aspect of Temple 's style is its narrative quality . For her , darkness has intention rather than being a means to an end . On Edge Of Everything , Temple applies this cinematic mindset to an album , with even more thoughtful and mesmerizing results . <p> Similar to her DJ sets , Temple uses each track on Edge Of Everything to build or change the mood , making for a rich and fluid listen .
