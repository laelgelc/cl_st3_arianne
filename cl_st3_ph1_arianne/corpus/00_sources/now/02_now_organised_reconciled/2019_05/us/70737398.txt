According to researchers at the Reimagining Migration initiative -- a collaboration between educators at UCLA and the Harvard Graduate School of Education 's Project Zero -- the stories of that movement , and the backlash to it , are universal . <p> In the United States , the narrative of immigration often tends toward a black-and-white story of " us versus them . " Yet , as Reimagining Migration Director Adam Strom points out , there are multiple dimensions to every story , and the classroom is the ideal place to bring these stories into conversation and confront biases .
