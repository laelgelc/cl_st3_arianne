<h> Poll : Majority of Arizonans , even Republicans , believe in climate change <p> By KTAR.com May 21 , 2019 at 4:50 pmUPDATED : May 21 , 2019 at 10:02 pm <p> ( Pexels Photo ) <p> PHOENIX -- Regardless of political affiliation or age , a majority of Arizonans believe climate change is occurring , according to a poll released Tuesday . <p> The OH Predictive Insights poll found that 69% of Arizonans said they believed climate change was happening . <p> " The existence of global warming has been hotly debated in the last decade , but climate change deniers appear to be losing the battle in Arizona , " Mike Noble , chief of research for the Phoenix-based polling firm , said in a press release . <p> " Arizonans of all political stripes told us that they believed climate change is occurring , including 51% of Republicans , who are generally portrayed as solidly against the idea of climate change .
