We depend on it to grow our crops , to source our medicines , to house us and to clothe us . When we destroy nature , we destroy the essentials on which we all depend . <p> Today IPBES ( Intergovernmental Platform on Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services , www.ipbes.net ) -- the independent global scientific body on biodiversity of more than 130 governments -- publishes its report on the current state of life on Earth . The report paints an alarming picture of species extinctions , wildlife population declines , habitat loss and depletion of ecosystem services - adding to the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a dramatic and unsustainable rate . <p> The report also makes clear the cause of this destruction : us . We are cutting down our forests , overfishing our seas , polluting our rivers , degrading our soils and changing our climate . This poses an urgent threat to all life on Earth -- including ourselves . <p> There is still time to protect what is left and to start restoring nature . But to do that , we must radically change the way we live , including how we use energy to power our societies , grow our food , and manage our waste . This is an immense task but many of the solutions are already at hand . <p> Each of us has a role to play in bringing about this transformational change . But we need you , our political leaders , to lead - and to set us on a path to a future where people and nature thrive . <p> Next year , there is an unmissable opportunity to choose a new direction for people and the planet . Important global decisions will be @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ a series of UN meetings in 2020 . Together , these form an action plan for change , a real New Deal for nature and people . <p> But for this to happen , we need decisive and ambitious action from you . That 's why today : <p> We call on you to stop funding activities that destroy nature . <p> We call on you to put an end to deforestation and land degradation . <p> We call on you to protect our oceans and marine life , especially against plastics . <p> We call on you to encourage the transition to sustainable agricultural practices . <p> We call on you to implement the Paris Agreement to halt climate change . <p> The future of all life on Earth lies in the balance .
