<h> As flood season approaches , City of Calgary has improved its emergency preparedness <p> Calgary has reduced its risk of river flood damage by a third since 2013 , the city said Tuesday as it launched its flood readiness program . <p> Since the devastating flooding nearly six years ago , the city has also improved its forecasting and monitoring capabilities , and its emergency preparedness , said Sandy Davis , flood risk awareness program manager with the City of Calgary . <p> River flooding is most likely to occur between May 15 and July 15 because of snow melt in the mountains and the possibility of heavy rainfall . <p> This year , the snow pack in the mountains is at average or less than average , Davis said . And while Calgary could be in store for more precipitation than normal this spring , the kind of intense rainfalls that can lead to river flooding are difficult to predict . <p> That unpredictability of being close to the mountains and changing weather patterns means both the city and residents need to be prepared , Davis said . <p> " The city is monitoring those conditions 24 @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 're prepared to act and escalate when we need to , and to be able to provide as much warning as we can to other city groups and to the city to prepare , " she said during a news conference near Prince 's Island Park . <p> " And it 's why citizens need to be prepared to act on short notice and that they have an evacuation plan , their properties are prepared , and they know how to stay informed .
