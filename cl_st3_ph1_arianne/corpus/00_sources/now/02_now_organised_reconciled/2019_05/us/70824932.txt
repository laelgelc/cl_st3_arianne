" <p> The last few weeks kept Dombek and his colleagues based outside State College particularly busy as one major thunderstorm after another -- and the outbreak of tornadoes they birthed -- swept across the Midwest toward Pennsylvania and on to the Northeast . <p> In a normal year , Pennsylvanians typically sees two or three tornadoes each May .
