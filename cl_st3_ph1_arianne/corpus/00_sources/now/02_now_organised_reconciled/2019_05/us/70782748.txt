<p> SANTA MONICA , Calif. , May 17 , 2019 ( GLOBE NEWSWIRE ) -- Saatchi Art 's The Other Art Fair ( " The Fair " ) , a leading international fair championing independent emerging artists , announces its features and programming for its second edition in Chicago . The Other Art Fair will run from May 16-19 , 2019 at Mana Contemporary showcasing over 120 emerging international and local artists . The programming for this edition includes Chicago-local Sandra Antongiorgi , singer , songwriter , and painter as the official Guest Artist . Joining as the official non-profit partner is Little Black Pearl , an organization that provides healing through art and serves youth and families in the south side neighborhoods of Chicago . The Fair will also introduce " Greener Future , " its 2019 global initiative promoting a multifaceted approach to eco-sustainability in the arts . <p> " We are honored to work with such amazing Chicago-local artists and activists who are inspiring the next generation to express themselves through art , " said Founder of The Other Art Fair , Ryan Stanier .
