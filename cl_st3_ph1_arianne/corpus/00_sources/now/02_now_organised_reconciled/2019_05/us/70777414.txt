They are usually driven by major environmental change , with existing species unable to evolve quickly enough to survive the change . The Earth has seen five of these events and the losses we are talking about are truly staggering . <p> The worst in terms of species loss occurred 251 million years ago when 96 per cent of all the species alive at the time were wiped out in what is known as the " great dying " . It was likely caused by a collection of natural events including massive volcanic eruptions in Siberia that turned the oceans too acid for most life and carbon dioxide release that switched on the greenhouse effect to raise temperatures and speed up release of another greenhouse gas , methane . The event nearly ended life on the planet . <p> Another occurred 444 million years ago when geological change -- the uplift of the Appalachian mountains -- triggered a severe ice age that killed off 86 per cent of species .
