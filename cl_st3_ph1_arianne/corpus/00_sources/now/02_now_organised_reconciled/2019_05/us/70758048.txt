<h> Tweed Airport 's climate problem is just the tip of the iceberg <h> The airport is only one of many Connecticut places threatened by sea-level rise <p> Jan Ellen Spiegel 's April 29 reporting on the impact of climate change on Tweed Airport exposes the tip of the climate iceberg that our infrastructure will be facing in coming years . NOAA flood maps suggest that the Long Wharf area , including portions of the railroad yard , will also be impacted by storm surge and sea-level rise . <p> Our efforts would be better spent on a concerted approach to the root cause of climate change than a piecemeal approach such as raising the Tweed runway . National proposals such as H.R.763 ( Energy Innovation and Carbon Dividend Act ) contain a carbon fee and dividend strategy endorsed by many eminent economists as the most effective way to move towards clean energy . <p> This approach has been projected to significantly slow climate change ( if it is adopted soon ) and will result in massive savings to our infrastructure and budget . <p> While local mitigation efforts may be necessary in some circumstances , prudence calls for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ widespread positive effect on our homes , climate , and government coffers .
