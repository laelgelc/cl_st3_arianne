He has held it since a byelection in 1994 . <p> With half the votes counted at 10pm , the Australian Electoral Commission was projecting an emphatic victory for the independent , on a two-candidate-preferred figure of about 59 per cent for Ms Steggall and 41 per cent for Mr Abbott . <p> The AEC results showed that 45 per cent of voters had given their first preference to Ms Steggall while 39 per cent had backed Mr Abbott , down from 51.5 per cent in 2016 . <p> Advertisement <p> Speaking to supporters at the Manly Leagues club in Brookvale , an upbeat Mr Abbott said : " Once we had the result in the Wentworth byelection , six months @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to be tough here in Warringah . <p> " And I ca n't say that it does n't hurt to lose .
