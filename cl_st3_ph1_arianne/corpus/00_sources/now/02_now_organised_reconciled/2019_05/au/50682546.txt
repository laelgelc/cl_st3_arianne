<h> Canada unveils support for Canadian innovation by small businesses to reduce plastic waste <p> Comments Off on Canada unveils support for Canadian innovation by small businesses to reduce plastic waste <p> From : Environment and Climate Change Canada <p> May 17 , 2019 -- Sydney , Nova Scotia <p> Canada is working with Canadians across the country to protect its land and water from plastic waste . Not only is plastic pollution harmful to the environment , but the disposal of plastics is a waste of a valuable resource . This is why the Government of Canada is partnering with Canadian businesses to develop innovative solutions to keep plastics in the economy and out of landfills and the environment . <p> Today , Member of Parliament for Sydney-Victoria , Mark Eyking , on behalf of the Minister of Environment and Climate Change , Catherine McKenna , announced six winners of the Canadian Plastics Innovation Challenge , a part of the Innovative Solutions Canada program . Dealing with issues related to food packaging , construction waste , and the separation of plastics for recycling , these Challenges are a great opportunity to invest in innovative ideas and technologies that @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Canada toward a zero-plastic waste future . <p> Copol International Ltd. , one of today 's funding recipients located in Sydney , Nova Scotia , is a local small business developing a food packaging solution that would incorporate biodegradable components extracted from marine waste into a cast polypropylene film . <p> Copol International Ltd. joins other small businesses from across the country who will each receive up to $150,000 to develop their idea . By working together , we can beat plastic pollution , protect nature , and tackle climate change . <p> " Investing in new ideas to solve old problems is vital when addressing our plastic waste challenges . Twenty participating federal departments and agencies have set aside a portion of funding to support the creation of innovative solutions by Canadian small businesses . <p> Phase 1 recipients , such as those announced today , who successfully develop a proof of concept will be invited to compete for a grant of up to $1 million in Phase 2 to develop a prototype . The Government of Canada then has the option to be the first buyer of any successful innovation . <p> A total of seven Canadian Plastics Innovation Challenges were put forward as part of the Innovative Solutions Canada program , each encouraging innovative solutions to a different problem area in addressing plastic waste . <p> The seven plastics challenges are sponsored by Environment and Climate Change Canada , Transport Canada , Fisheries and Oceans Canada , Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada , and Natural Resources Canada ; who each oversee the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> The Government of Canada dedicated over $10 million to advance made in Canada solutions to address plastics challenges . <p> Canada is committed to leading by example on greening government operations and delivering a low-carbon , clean-growth economy . <p> The Government of Canada is leading by example , by committing to divert at least 75% of its plastic waste from federal operations by 2030 . This will be accomplished through changing federal practices as well as in the procurement of more sustainable plastic products .
