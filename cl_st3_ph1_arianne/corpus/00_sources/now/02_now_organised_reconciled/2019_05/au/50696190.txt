<p> The coal industry has been emboldened by the Morrison government 's re-election . It has n't wasted any time in lobbying for the new government to build new coal-fired power stations . Coal Council of Australia chief executive Greg Evans came out swinging on Monday , saying it was insulting that coal workers had been treated as second-class citizens . " In coal-related electorates in NSW and Queensland , Labor members and candidates recorded strong swings against them .
