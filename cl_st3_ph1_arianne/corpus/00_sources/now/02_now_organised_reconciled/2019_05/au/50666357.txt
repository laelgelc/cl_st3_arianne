<p> One of the things you learn , working in politics , is that it is never too late to make a change . Yes , inertia is a powerful force - the press releases have been printed , the colleagues have been told , the travel 's been arranged . But if you are the leader of your party you can always shift direction , if you choose . For almost nine months , Scott Morrison has been barrelling towards an almost zero-policy campaign . The strategy has been his , and the coming result - victory or defeat - is largely on his head . Along the way , he has tried every trick to make his empty campaign seem at least half-full . He delayed it until the last possible moment , believing something unexpected might happen . It did - but the murderous attack on Christchurch made his dog-whistling much harder .
