<p> Investments in clean energy and cleantech create new opportunities and jobs for middle-class Canadians , including those in rural , remote and Indigenous communities . These investments are helping Canadians grow our economy , fight climate change and leave a healthy planet for our kids and grandkids . <p> Four key principles continue to guide Canada 's actions in developing a clean energy economy . They are : <p> Powering Clean Communities through more solar and wind farms ; more hydro , tidal and geothermal power ; and more battery storage for renewable power ; <p> Using More Renewable Fuels such as hydrogen , renewable natural gas and biomass ; <p> Powering the World by developing Canada 's resources in even more sustainable ways , including cleaner oil and gas , and bringing those resources to world markets ; and <p> Saving Energy as the most affordable way to save money and cut pollution . <p> Minister Sohi also highlighted the importance of continuing to develop Canadian energy in partnership with Indigenous peoples .
