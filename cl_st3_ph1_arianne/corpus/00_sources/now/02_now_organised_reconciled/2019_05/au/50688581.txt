<h> Climate change is the root driver of electricity prices <p> One part of ITK 's business is forecasting electricity prices . Our motto is " all forecasts are wrong but some are useful " . <p> Our hero is Steve Bradbury and our mantra is " maintenance leads to insight " . <p> When we get right down to it we forecast electricity prices in a different way to most others . We start with a view that supply will be forced into the system and work out the price from there . <p> We start with this foundation because we think that electricity policy will be driven by decarbonization -- not just in Australia , a minor contributor even to Scope 3 emissions , but globally .
