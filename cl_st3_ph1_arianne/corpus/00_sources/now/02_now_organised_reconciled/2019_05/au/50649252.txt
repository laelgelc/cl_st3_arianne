<p> Droughts , heatwaves , cold extremes or floods can all cause severe harvest losses and pose great risks to the livelihoods of farmers , as well as the food security of communities around the world who rely on agriculture . <p> Due to climate change , the frequency and severity of climate extremes is predicted to further increase in most regions worldwide . It means that our global crop yield -- the measurement of the amount of agricultural production harvested per unit of land area -- will be affected . <p> It also means that there 's work to be done to plan for , and improve the resilience of our food production systems .
