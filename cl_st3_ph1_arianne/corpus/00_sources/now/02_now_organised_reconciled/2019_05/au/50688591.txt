<h> News <h> Screenings of environmental doco 2040 will be free for students during its opening weekend <p> Director Damon Gameau 's new documentary 2040 presents a hopeful vision of the future , imagining a world that embraces planet-replenishing environmental solutions . <p> The documentary , Gameau 's follow-up to the highly successful That Sugar Film , is oriented towards young viewers . <p> So it is particularly good news that school-aged students will be able to see the film for free on opening weekend , at Palace Cinemas locations across the country . <p> Sign up for Flicks updates <p> One paying adult must attend with each student to redeem a free ticket . The offer applies at all sessions of 2040 at Palace Cinemas locations on Saturday 25th and Sunday 26th May 2019 . <p> 2040 has been well received by critics .
