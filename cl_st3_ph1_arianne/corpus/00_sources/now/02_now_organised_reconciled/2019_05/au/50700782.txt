<h> The key role of insects in crop pollination <p> Insects and particularly bees are vital to agriculture . They transport pollen and fertilise flowers which then produce the fruit and vegetables upon which we rely . With close to 1,600 bee species in Australia , Romina Rader studies the wild bee species involved in pollination of intensive horticulture systems .
