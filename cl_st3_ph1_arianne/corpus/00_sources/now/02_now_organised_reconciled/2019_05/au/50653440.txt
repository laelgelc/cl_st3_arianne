<p> Lots of promises and chest pumping was part of the format at last week 's April 30 Forster Tuncurry Business Chamber meet the candidates monthly breakfast meeting . Held at the Forster Bowling Club , six of the nine candidates contesting the seat of Lyne , which is held by the Nationals , offered up an abundance of commitments . Fourteen weeks out of the 18 weeks since the start of 2019 NSW has been in election mode , facilitator , Jeremy Thornton told the meeting . However , that aside , Mr Thornton reminded members and guests this Federal election was just as important as all elections . Incumbent , David Gillespie was the first candidate to spruik his party 's policies . The National Party representative said much had been achieved in the past three years . " There has been an explosion in aged care , and an expansion in roads and bridges , " Dr Gillespie said . He said the Federal government had had record aid in roads , and reminded people of funding to assist with construction of the Forster Civic Centre and Fig Tree on the Manning .
