But both candidates inspire less confidence than former Prime Minister Malcolm Turnbull ( 63% in 2018 ) . <p> Natasha Kassam , Lowy Institute Research Fellow , said " The Coalition are traditionally the party of choice on foreign policy , and this year is no different . But Labor has narrowed the gap and is preferred two-to-one for its climate change policies . The China relationship and foreign interference will test the party that forms government after 18 May , but Australians are split as to who would do a better job managing these key issues .
