That 's the ten-year difference in government revenues , based on their stated policies . <p> The ALP had a plan to hit high income earners and pump tens of billions into dental and child care , universal preschool for three- and four-year-olds , cancer treatment , technical education fee waivers and wage subsidies for childcare workers . Modelling by the Australian National University 's Centre for Social Research and Methods estimated that the top 10 percent of households would have been almost $12,000 worse off per year under Labor ( compared with the Coalition ) -- a hit of 4.7 percent of their disposable income .
