<p> ONCE again I would like to address the issue regarding closure of Stockton Centre . I have a brother in Stockton Centre and have participated in many meetings about this , so I am speaking factually from my experience . I am incensed at the way this government has played people right from the start . They have venomously denied any knowledge of a land grab , which I believe has now become evident ( ' Stock rising ' , Newcastle Herald 29/5 ) . Also , in my opinion their promises to staff regarding their jobs are blatant lies ( ' Nursing job losses just the start in disability centre closures ' , Herald 30/5 ) . However , getting to the point , if the government really cared about these vulnerable people then the obvious solution , at least for them , would have been to stop wasting money building new accommodation which in most cases does n't rival what they already had and use this money to let them live out the rest of their days in their home , the only home most of them have known .
