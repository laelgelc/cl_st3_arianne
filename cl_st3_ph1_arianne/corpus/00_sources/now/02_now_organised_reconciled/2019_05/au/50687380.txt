<p> Prime Minister Scott Morrison is preparing to convene the nation 's MPs as soon as possible to legislate the Coalition 's $158 billion income tax cut package , as he waits on fresh seat results to determine whether he will form majority government or have to negotiate for power in a hung Parliament . Mr Morrison intends to put the tax cut forward as a " priority " once the numbers in Parliament become clear , amid concerns on Sunday that the Coalition lost ground in key seats after earlier appearing on track to govern in its own right following Saturday 's shock election outcome . Mr Morrison would remain Prime Minister in a hung Parliament through the expected support on confidence and supply from Queensland maverick Bob Katter and independent Zali Steggall , who defeated former prime minister Tony Abbott in Warringah . But Mr Morrison is yet to open formal talks with crossbenchers while he and colleagues wait for the Australian Electoral Commission to determine a handful of seats that remain in doubt following the significant swing to the Coalition on Saturday .
