<h> Health of Great Barrier Reef and Ocean Wonders On Line at This Election <p> Comments Off on Health of Great Barrier Reef and Ocean Wonders On Line at This Election <p> The outcome of Australia 's Federal election tomorrow could decide the fate of the Great Barrier Reef and the nation 's unique marine wildlife , the Australian Marine Conservation Society ( AMCS ) says . <p> On the eve of the election AMCS , the country 's peak marine conservation group , says the poll comes at a crucial time with the next Australian government holding the future health of Australia 's oceans in its hands . <p> Darren Kindleysides , CEO of AMCS , said : " As Australians go to the polls , we are in a climate emergency , our nature faces an extinction crisis and the future of the Great Barrier Reef hangs in the balance . Time is running out and we can not afford for the next Australian government to squander time on inaction and half-measures . <p> " This is the climate election and our glorious Great Barrier Reef is right at the coal face for climate impacts . We @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ fighting chance -- policies that move us rapidly away from mining and burning fossil fuels and clean up our Reef 's water . <p> " But as well as our rapidly warming oceans causing catastrophic coral bleaching , our marine wonders are facing many other threats . <p> " We have a tsunami of plastic pollution to hold back and clean up . We still have unsustainable fisheries catching threatened species . We have a network of marine parks stripped of huge areas of protection , removing sanctuaries for our fish and wildlife like dolphins , turtles , dugongs , sharks and rays . " <p> Kindleysides said the election had seen concern for the future of Australia 's environment emerge as the biggest single concern among the majority of voters . <p> A poll by the Lowy Institute saw climate change emerge as the voting public 's primary national security concern . The ABC 's Vote Compass survey of 119,000 people found the environment rated ahead of all other issues , with 29 percent ranking it as their primary election issue . <p> Kindleysides added : " Australians have @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ leaders -- that 's why we pored over the details of policy platforms of the main parties and released our election scorecard . Our assessment has revealed stark differences between the environmental policies of the main parties . " <p> Last week , AMCS released its non-partisan scorecard assessment of the policies of the main parties across six critical issues to our oceans , reefs and wildlife.The areas assessed were : <p> Rapid shift from fossil fuels to renewable energy <p> Rejection of new thermal coal mines <p> Cut Great Barrier Reef water pollution <p> Restore marine sanctuaries <p> Protect endangered wildlife from fishing <p> Tackle plastic pollution <p> Kindleysides said : " Our nation 's $31 billion marine tourism industry needs strong policies . Our Great Barrier Reef and the 60,000 jobs it supports need leadership on climate and water quality . Whoever forms our next government will have the future of our Reef and amazing oceans in their hands .
