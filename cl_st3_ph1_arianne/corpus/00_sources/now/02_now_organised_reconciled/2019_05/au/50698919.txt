<p> THE bottom line of the PFAS red zone issue is the people that reside there and how to get them out . Liability is now secondary - we know the cause and we know what the human cost has been already . Compensation is the only answer for the affected individuals and families . This is not a natural disaster , rather a synthetic disaster ; however the outcome should be the same . The EPA is the authority on this ; they have defined the impacted areas , and as a consequence diminished the property values to zero . My solution is simple - albeit complex in sheer scale , however an effective one . Private enterprise will be the solution . Allow the local development corporations to acquire the land under a joint venture enterprise to create an extensive commercial and industrial precinct . All the local and state governments need to do is work with these developers to plan the best use of the space - and approve it . Would simply digging contaminated soil out of the open drains make any difference to the extent of the problem , or the spread ?
