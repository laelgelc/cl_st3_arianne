<h> How to increase the green spaces in our cities <p> The City of Melbourne and the South Australian Government have recently announced new funds to encourage the planting of more trees in urban areas to mitigate the impacts of climate change . <p> But a recent study shows that there has been a significant loss of green spaces in urban areas since 2013 . <p> So what else can increase the amount of green space in cities ? And what role does planting trees in your neighbourhood , with or without council approval , play ? <p> Life Matters talks to Marco Amati , environmental scientist and Associate Professor of International Planning at RMIT 's Centre for Urban Research , as well as urban food gardener KarenSutherland and town planner Greg Smith , who are both guerilla gardeners .
