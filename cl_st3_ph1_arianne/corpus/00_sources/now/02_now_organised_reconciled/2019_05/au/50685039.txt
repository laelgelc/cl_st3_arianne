" Labor 's next victory will belong to our next leader and I 'm confident that victory will come at the next election , " he said in his concession speech . With his wife Chloe by his side , Mr Shorten said he was disappointed Labor could n't form a government following the death of Bob Hawke on Thursday . " I wish we could have won for the true believers , for our brothers and sisters in the mighty trade union movement , " he said . " I wish we could have done it for Bob .
