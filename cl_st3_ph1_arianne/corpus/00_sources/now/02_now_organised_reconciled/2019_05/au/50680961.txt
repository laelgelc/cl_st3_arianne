Synonymous jargon -- examples include recyclability ; eco-friendliness ; environmental footprint -- has achieved buzzword status in the industry , sometimes losing meaning or being misused as marketing tools for brands to propagate false claims and sell their wares around the world . With terms like these increasingly thrown about on and off the runway , designers , models and consumers alike are cottoning on ( voluntarily or not ) to the harmful repercussions of mass scale clothing production and consumption .
