<h> Australians rate climate change a bigger threat than terrorism <p> For the first time , climate change has topped the list of potential threats to Australia in the Lowy Institute Poll . <p> UpdatedUpdated 08/05/2019 <p> By Nick Baker <p> Share <p> Australians rate climate change as the biggest threat to the country 's vital interests , according to a new poll . <p> On Wednesday , the annual Lowy Institute Poll found 64 per cent of adults placed climate change as number one on a list of 12 threats . <p> In second place was cyber attacks from other countries , third place was international terrorism while fourth place was North Korea 's nuclear program . <p> A climate change protest in Melbourne . <p> Getty <p> It 's the first time climate change has topped the list since the question started to be included in 2006 . <p> The poll also found 61 per cent of Australians believe climate change " is a serious and pressing problem and we should begin taking steps now even if this involves significant costs " . <h> ' Not surprised ' <p> Research Fellow at the Lowy Institute Natasha Kassam @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ was " inevitable " . <p> " I 'm not surprised . We 've seen increasing concern about climate change in our annual poll since 2012 . It 's been steadily rising ... It 's inevitable at this point , " she said . <p> Many parts of Australia have been hit by drought . <p> AAP <p> But Ms Kassam said there remained a " stark generation gap " . <p> The poll found more than three quarters of younger Australians aged 18-44 ( 76 per cent ) think climate change is a " serious and pressing problem " . By contrast , fewer than half ( 49 per cent ) of Australians aged over 45 share this concern . <p> The poll also asked respondents which political party would do a better job at addressing the threats . <p> " The Coalition is preferred on almost all foreign policy issues by the Australian public with the one exception being climate change , " Ms Kassam said .
