<h> Extra solar rebates for households who missed out <p> Hundreds of households who missed out on the first tranche of the state government solar subsidies will get their rebates after all , the new boss of Solar Victoria says . <p> Stan Krpan , who will be announced on Wednesday as the inaugural chief executive of the agency set up to run the ambitious scheme , says the government is determined not to be caught out again by a huge surge of householders clamouring to sign up . <p> Mr Krpan also says his agency will crack down hard on shonky solar industry operators exploiting vulnerable consumers and on installers who risk their workers ' safety - with six installation outfits already referred to Consumer Affairs Victoria for prosecution . <p> The $1.3 billion scheme , which aims to put solar electrical systems on the roofs of up to 770,000 Victorian homes in the next 10 years , was a major promise by the Andrews Labor government before the 2018 election , tackling the hot-button issues of power prices , the cost of living and climate change . <p> The stop-gap program , designed to tide the solar @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 1 , was expected to subsidise about 24,000 applications but
