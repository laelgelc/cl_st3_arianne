<p> THIS election I believe has highlighted a couple of interesting points on how the good values that Australia was once renowned for , such as care for one another and leave no-one behind , have slipped to a worrying level . Greed has been allowed to corrupt capitalism from what in principle is a good system to one of self-entitlement where concessions that were probably inadvisably given in boom times are steadfastly held tight in tough and changing times . Every parent wants their children to be able to afford a house to live in , but have a listen to those that increase their wealth through houses that do n't think that is a good move . I think that is shameful , If reform is suggested to help improve the lives of those that are on the poverty line or below , just have a look at those screaming because the well-off are n't getting the benefits too . What I have today I may not have tomorrow , but I do n't think others should pay a price for me to hang on to it , especially when it is obviously unsustainable or inequitable . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ climate change has featured somewhat . As is to be expected , there has been much to-ing and fro-ing between the big two as to who is going to do what and whether it will be value for money . However , neither of the big two has acknowledged why , per head of population , Australia is one of the worst greenhouse gas emitters in the world . I believe one major reason is the excessive use of road transport and in particular private motor cars . When I joined the railways in the late 1970s , I well remember the number of country passenger trains and mixed freight trains that used to run along the north coast and northern lines . Over the years they disappeared as road transport took over . The result has been congestion on our roads and more greenhouse gas emissions . What do either of the big two intend to do ; bearing in mind that rail transport is three to nine times more fuel efficient than road ? Such proposals will require a long term plan and a long term commitment by whoever is in government and they are not new . They will make transport more efficient . So who will do it ? This is an important time for media organisations to lift their game and act more in keeping with their purpose , to report the news and not to exert influence upon the Australian voters .
