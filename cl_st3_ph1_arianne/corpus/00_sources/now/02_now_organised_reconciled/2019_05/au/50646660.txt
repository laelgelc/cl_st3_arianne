<p> About 100 people took part in a protest and march in Taree on Friday , May 3 as part of a nationwide campaign against climate change . The Climate Election National Day of Action event was held outside of Lyne MP Dr David Gillepsie 's office before a march down Victoria Street . Many children , under the banner of School Strike 4 Climate , took part and were at the front of the march . Signs held up by members of the group included ' No Planet B ' , ' L/NP Out ' and ' Stop Adani ' . Similar action across Australia was designed to get the attention of sitting members and candidates for the upcoming federal election . All groups demand every federal member and candidate commit to urgent action on climate , no new fossil fuels , 100 per cent renewables by 2030 and stopping the Adani coal mine in Queensland .
