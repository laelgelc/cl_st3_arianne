<h> Why is methane so dangerous ? <p> Methane as a greenhouse gas is measured in carbon dioxide equivalents . Carbon dioxide warms the Earth over centuries , but methane will pack all its warming punch in the next two decades . <p> It is around 85 times more potent than carbon dioxide in raising the temperature of the Earth and is a candidate for causing the rapid rise in extreme weather events which are increasing faster than would be expected from the slow rise in carbon dioxide . <p> The main " forcer " is methane and , to a lesser extent , black carbon ( soot ) . Ozone and manufactured fluorinated gases are also potent forcers . <p> Methane is released from wetlands and cows burping due to fermentation in their guts and from forest fires when methane is released from trees and soil . Human flatus produces only a little methane . <p> But we know that most of existing atmospheric methane and its steady rise since 2006 is due to fossil fuels .
