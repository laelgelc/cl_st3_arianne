<p> In a race to the finish line , the final lunge can make all the difference . Bill Shorten goes into the final day of this federal election with good reason to believe he can emerge on Saturday night as the next Prime Minister of Australia . Yet Scott Morrison has fought so hard , and so cleverly , that he still has a chance to hold power . Morrison raced through Queensland on Friday like a man who could not afford to rest for a moment . He chose a defensive strategy to shore up support in seats the Coalition already holds , like Leichhardt in the state 's far north . Only one visit , to Herbert , was to a seat he might wrest from Labor . Shorten campaigned differently on Friday to mark the passing of Bob Hawke - an event that has clearly motivated Labor Party members to fight even harder for victory .
