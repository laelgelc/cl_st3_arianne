<p> It was standing room only as more than 60 energy-conscious Ballarat residents squeezed into the Main Bar to listen to Ballarat 's federal election candidates talk energy policy . The Ballarat Renewable Energy and Zero Emissions ( BREAZE ) group held Ballarat 's second candidate forum on the subject of renewable energy on Thursday night , during the ' Green Drinks ' event . Conversation moved from renewable energy targets to biodiversity , food security and climate change 's impact on the drought . The topic of coal was a repeated point of interest , in the wake of conjecture around the Adani coal mine . Independent candidate Nick Shady said he 'd support pulling all of Australia 's coal export to create a world where sustainable energy was the cheaper option . Candidate Alex Graham , who used to work in the mining industry , said Australia should not be using coal or oil mining at all and instead use hydrogen fuel . Greens candidate Karen McAloon said her party seeks to re-introduce a carbon price , and would " stop Adani " and any new coal mine developments . BREAZE secretary Ian Rossiter said the forum was important because there was a lot of " conflicting information " being bandied around in the lead up to the election . Only five candidates for the seat of Ballarat attended the forum .
