<p> A new or re-elected government will give us a chance to better support our neighbours in Asia and the Pacific . The behaviour of the last one has n't been good . It has repeatedly used the aid budget as a sort of automatic teller machine to take money from development in order to get its budget in order . A glaring example was the $55 million dollar refugee resettlement deal with very poor and dictator-led Cambodia .
