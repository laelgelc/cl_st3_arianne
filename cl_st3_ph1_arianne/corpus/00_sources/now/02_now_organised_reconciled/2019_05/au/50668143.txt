<h> Billmania : Shorten mobbed by selfie-hungry students in Gosford3:57 <p> May 13 , 2019 . . Opposition Leader Bill Shorten arrives at St Joseph 's Catholic College to chants of his name from the neighbouring St Edward 's College in East Gosford . Eager students rush to the fence to greet the Labor leader while inside the girl 's school students rush for selfies and scream as he tours the playground . Shorten speaking to a group of senior students says the political inaction on climate change has lasted their entire school life and promises , if elected , his government will take action . Shorten is mobbed throughout his visit , posing for photos and signing one girl 's school diary . Later , speaking to media , he restates his commitment to action on climate change . As he attempts to leave the crush of students he says " We better go to school- we are in school , " he says laughing and shaking his head . ( AAP VIDEO/Andrew Leeson ) <p> It was the rock star welcome that no one saw coming , especially Bill Shorten . <p> The Labor leader seemed shocked @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ visit to St Joseph 's Catholic College , East Gosford on the NSW Central Coast . <p> Mr Shorten visited the school in the marginal electorate of Robertson , with Shadow Treasurer Chris Bowen , Senator Kristina Keneally and local Labor candidate Anne Charlton , to launch his Fair Go for the Central Coast plan . <p> But there was chaos once Mr Shorten entered the quadrangle , with a huge mob of girls clambering to get close to him . <p> Seasoned photographers and cameramen had nothing on this group of iPhone-wielding youngsters . The media pack could only get out of the way as the girls fought to get close enough to snap a picture , or give Mr Shorten a high-five.
