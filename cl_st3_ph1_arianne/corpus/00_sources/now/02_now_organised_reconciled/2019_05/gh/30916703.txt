<p> President Akufo-Addo says his government has taken the policy decision to integrate climate action into Ghana 's national development agenda , i.e. the Co-ordinated Programme of Economic and Social Development Policies ( 2017- 2022 ) . <p> He said the Paris Agreement and SDG Goal No. 13 , which demand urgent action to combat climate change and its impact , is providing the framework for Ghana to forge ahead in this direction . <p> Speaking at the R20 Austrian World Summit on Climate Change , on Tuesday , May 28 , the President revealed that , at the local level in Ghana , all local assemblies have been mandated to address climate change issues in their medium-term development plans . <p> That is why , since assuming office in January 2017 , his Government decided to clamp down on the reprehensible activity of illegal mining that has been destroying the nations ' forests and water bodies . <p> He noted that a ban has also been imposed on the harvesting of rosewood timber as one of the measures to protect Ghana 's forests and endangered species .
