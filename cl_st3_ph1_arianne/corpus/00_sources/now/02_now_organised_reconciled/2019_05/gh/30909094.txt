<h> Akufo-Addo attends world climate change summit <p> President Nana Akufo-Addo has left the country on Sunday to attend the R20 Austrian World Summit . <p> He is attending the Summit in his capacity as co-Chair of the UN Secretary General 's Group of Eminent Advocates on the 2030 SDGs . <p> The President will participate in the high-level panel discussion on the need for leadership to take responsibility in the global process on sustainable development and international co-operation . <p> The Summit is an initiative which is helping regions , countries and cities to implement the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , and to meet the global climate protection targets outlined in the Paris Agreement . <p> Whilst in Austria , President Akufo-Addo will hold bilateral talks with the President of Austria , Alexander Van der Bellen , and Austrian Chancellor , Sebastian Kurz . <p> He will also hold same with the President of Hungary , J ? nos ? der ; as well as the CEO of the World Bank , Kristalina Georgieva . <p> The Ghanaian community resident in the European country will also have an opportunity to have a one-on-one with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Foreign Affairs Minister , Shirley Ayorkor Botchway , Environment Minister , Dr Kwabena Frimpong Boateng ; and officials from the presidency and Foreign Ministry . <p> The President will return to Ghana on Tuesday and in his absence , the Vice President , Dr Mahamudu Bawumia , shall , in accordance with Article 60(8) of the Constitution , act in his stead .
