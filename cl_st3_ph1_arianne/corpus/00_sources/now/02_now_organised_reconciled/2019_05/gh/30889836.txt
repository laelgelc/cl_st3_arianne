<p> The Second Lady of Ghana , Hajia Mrs Samira Bawumia , will address the High-level Panel of the 72nd Session of the World Health Assembly ( WHA ) in Geneva , Switzerland . <p> WHA is the decision-making body of the World Health Organisation . <p> Mrs. Bawumia , who is an ambassador for the UN Foundation 's Clean Cooking Alliance , will be speaking at the High-Level Side Event to launch the Global Health and Energy Platform of Action on Tuesday , May 21 , 2019 . <p> The World Health Organisation adopted the idea to establish a new multi-stakeholder Global Energy-Health Platform of Action in 2018 to achieve Sustainable Development Goal ( SDG ) 3 on health and SDG 7 on energy , starting with a focus on clean cooking . <p> The aim of the platform is to pilot closely linked sustainable energy and health solutions at country-level to create strong multiplier effects to meet the Sustainable Development Goals and the objectives of the Paris Agreement on Climate Change . <p> WHO believes the interlinkages between energy and health are numerous .
