<h> Akufo-Addo calls on Austrian President <p> In a closed-door bilateral discussions , the Austrian president described Ghana to be the fastest growing economy in the world in 2019 . <p> Welcoming the increasing participation of Austria businesses in Ghana 's economy , President Akufo-Addo , who is also in Austria to participate in the R20 Austrian World Summit on Climate Change , urged Austrian firms invest in clean energy initiatives in Ghana . <p> On the fight against terrorism , and the increasing activities of terrorists in the Sahel , the President called for more support in aid of efforts by the African Union and the G5 du Sahel in combating the menace . <p> He stressed that " the co-ordination of activities between the armies and the intelligence agencies of our countries is absolutely essential to a successful battle against terrorism . " <p> Already , the Austrian Federal Army is involved in training co-operation with the Ghana Armed Forces on " Combat Dog Training " .
