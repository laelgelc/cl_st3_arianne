During the process of harvesting , the pods are detached from the tree , and the husks burst open , to remove the cocoa beans which proceed to the process of making cocoa . The husks are what are left as waste . <p> As a means of making them beneficial , the husks are collected into heaps and left to decompose . The nutrients are also crucial since they help kill the microbial components that cause diseases on the trees . They also improve the quality of the soil . <p> In as much as the husks are beneficial to the cocoa trees , there are demerits of this process of managing this waste .
