<h> News Categories <h> Group petitions President over felling of trees , plants at Parks and Gardens <p> Date : May 25 , 2019 , 10:02 <p> BY : Della Russel Ocloo <p> Category : General News <p> Group petitions President over felling of trees , plants at Parks &amp; Gardens <h> Environmental protection advocacy group , Eco-Conscious Citizens GH ( ECC GH ) , has petitioned President Nana Addo Dankwa Akufo-Addo over the felling of over 100 trees and destruction of plants at the head office of the Department of Parks &amp; Gardens at Cantonments in Accra . <p> According to the group , checks from the Environmental Protection Agency ( EPA ) showed that there were no permits covering the destruction exercise . <p> The Coordinator of the group , Ms Awula Serwah , who spoke to the Daily Graphic in an interview , described the action as " environmental vandalism " . <p> She wondered why the various species of trees and plants numbering about 140 were destroyed on land owned by a government department whose mandate was to promote tree planting . <p> Ms Serwah also said following claims by the EPA to the effect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ it is important that trees that provide oxygen and help purify the air by absorbing carbon dioxide are cultivated and not felled to make room for buildings " . <h> Ghana News Headlines <p> The coordinator further appealed to the President to use his good offices " to preserve Parks &amp; Gardens lands for greenery purposes , particularly in the wake of the effects of climate change on the country " . <p> " We must ensure that the department 's lands are protected for botanical purposes and not appropriated for buildings . Any public , private partnership ( PPP ) agreement on that land should be environmentally focused . <p> " If the Ministry of Local Government needs space for its other functions , it can either refurbish a dilapidated government building or develop an underutilised government land . It is unacceptable to appropriate land earmarked for growing trees and plants , " she added . <p> The importance of trees to human health , the coordinator emphasised , could not be overstated , hence the need to ensure that Ghanaians had access to well-managed parks to unwind , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ advocated that every metropolitan , municipal and district assembly ( MMDA ) area should have a decent park or two for relaxation and preservation of nature . <p> Claims <p> Ms Serwah further claimed that the department 's land , about 10 acres , had been appropriated over the years for the development of concrete structures . <p> She , therefore , asked the Lands Commission for a list of areas designated for parks and open spaces in Accra to ensure that " lands earmarked for open spaces are not grabbed for other purposes " . <p> " We want to ensure that remaining lands of the department , including areas earmarked as open spaces in Accra , are used for greenery and not for buildings or the expansion of Accra 's concrete jungle , " she added .
