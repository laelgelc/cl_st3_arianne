<h> ADB lending and grant operations hit $ 21.6b <p> The Asian Development Bank 's ( ADB ) new lending and grant operations in 2018 grew to a record $21.6 billion , an increase of nearly 10% from the $19.7 billion in the previous year , ADB President , Takehiko Nakao told the opening session of the Board of Governors at the 52nd ADB Annual Meeting in Fiji on Friday . <p> Compared to $13.9 billion in 2013 this is an increase of 55% . Such an expansion became possible due to the much larger equity from the successful merger of the ordinary capital resources balance sheet and Asian Development Fund ( ADF ) concessional lending operations in 2017 , he said . <p> Responding to the changing needs of Asia and the Pacific will be at the centre of the Asian Development Bank 's ( ADB ) actions to implement Strategy 2030 , he said . <p> Nakao highlighted six areas that the ADB considers essential to the strategy 's success . <p> First , he said a key priority for ADB will be to continue addressing poverty and reducing inequalities across Asia and the Pacific . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ most vulnerable countries in the region , including those in fragile and conflict-affected situations and many small island developing states , " Nakao said .
