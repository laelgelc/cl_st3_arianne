<h> New Zealand introduces climate bill to become carbon neutral <p> New Zealand 's government on Wednesday introduced an ambitious climate change bill that aims to make the nation mostly carbon neutral by 2050 while giving some leeway to farmers . <p> However , some farming industry groups say the measures remain too onerous and threaten the future of regional communities , while some environmentalists say the bill does n't go far enough because there are no penalties for noncompliance . <p> The bill represents a campaign promise from the liberal government that was elected 18 months ago . The government has also promised to plant 1 billion trees over 10 years and ensure that the electricity grid runs entirely from renewable energy by 2035 . <p> The bill would require all greenhouse gases except methane from animals to be reduced to net zero by 2050 . Methane emissions would be reduced by 10 percent by 2030 and by between about one-quarter and one-half by 2050 . <p> Prime Minister Jacinda Ardern said climate change was the biggest single challenge facing the world . <p> " We know the climate is changing .
