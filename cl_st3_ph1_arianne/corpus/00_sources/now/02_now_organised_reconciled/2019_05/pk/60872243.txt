<p> VIENNA : Climate activist Greta Thunberg called on citizens to " change everything " to avert climate crisis , at the start of a conference in Vienna on Tuesday organised by former California governor Arnold Schwarzenegger . <p> " We young people are waking up and we promise , we wo n't let you get away with it anymore , " 16-year-old Thunberg told delegates . She was speaking after green parties emerged as one of the big winners in the European parliamentary elections . <p> Climate change was " the biggest crisis that humanity has ever faced , " said Thunberg . " It is not something you can ' like ' on Facebook . " Technological developments in fields such as electric cars and solar energy should not leave people with the impression that they could " solve the crisis without making any efforts " , she said . <p> " Once we realise , we act , we change . Humans are very adaptable , " she added . <p> Thunberg has become a figurehead for young climate activists internationally , inspiring hundreds of thousands of other youths to strike from school in " Fridays @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Austrian World Summit , an initiative launched by Schwarzenegger , which brings together some 1,200 figures from the worlds of science , politics and business to discuss ways to tackle climate change . <p> In a speech at the event , Schwarzenegger called on world leaders to " stop lying to the people about pollution and about the climate change " and to " invest in the green energy of the future " . <p> UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres regretted that " many of the countries that in Paris promised to do a number of things are not even catching up with their own promises " .
