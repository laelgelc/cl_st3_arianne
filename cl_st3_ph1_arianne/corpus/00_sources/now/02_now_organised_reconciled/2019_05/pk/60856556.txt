<p> A meeting of the Annual Plan Coordination Committee ( APCC ) has proposed development outlay of Rs 1.837 trillion and 4 percent GDP growth rate for the next fiscal year ( 2019-20 ) . A meeting of the APCC presided over by Minister for Planning , Development and Reform Makhdum Khusro Bakhtyar and attended by the provincial government representatives proposed Rs 1.837 trillion PSDP and 4 percent growth target to the NEC meeting for consideration and approval . The APCC approved Rs 925 billion federal government allocation in the PSDP for the next fiscal year while provincial allocation in the PSDP was approved at Rs 912 billion . <p> According to sources , the APCC was informed that GDP growth of 4 percent is based on 2.9 percent growth in agriculture , 4.8 percent in services sector and 2.8 percent in large-scale manufacturing .
