<p> Prime Minister Imran Khan on Friday chaired a meeting where he discussed the possible entry of electric cars in the Pakistani market owing to extreme pollution in the country . <p> Malik Amin Aslam , adviser to PM Imran Khan on climate change briefed the premier on the current climate and pollution condition of Pakistan . The Prime Minister on this occasion ordered the concerned authorities to prepare the required logistics to for a possible set-up of an electric plant . This would be done to achieve the goal of converting 30% of all cars running in Pakistan into electric cars . <p> Considering most of the major cities in Pakistan are currently polluted with extremely toxic air owing to the hazardous pollutants released by industrial and automobile emissions .
