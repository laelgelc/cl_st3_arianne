<h> LG Electronics aims to halve carbon emissions by 2030 <p> SEOUL : LG Electronics Inc. said Monday it will halve carbon emissions by 2030 compared with 2017 levels by increasing use of renewable energy and energy-efficient management systems . <p> The electronics maker said its goal is aimed at having a " zero carbon " footprint by reducing CO2 emissions and increasing renewable energy sources to offset its carbon emissions . <p> To achieve the goal , LG plans to cut its carbon emissions from 1.9 million tons in 2017 to 960,000 tons in 2030 by adopting energy-efficient systems and expanding the use of solar power in its factories and offices . <p> The company also plans to step up participation in the United Nations-led clean development mechanism ( CDM ) , which offers certified emission reduction credits for energy projects in developing countries . <p> LG said it earned 340,000 tons of carbon credits through CDM projects between 2015 and 2018 from the U.N .
