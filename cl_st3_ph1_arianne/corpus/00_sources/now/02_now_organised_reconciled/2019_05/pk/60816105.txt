<p> PepsiCo here on Wednesday signed a Memorandum of Understanding ( MoU ) with Ministry of Climate Change to play a leading role in Prime Minister Imran Khan 's Clean Green Pakistan Initiative through increase access to safe water among community members . Under the investment programme , PepsiCo has made a US $700,000 investment with leading non-profit partner WaterAid to enable access to clean , safe water to more than 140,000 beneficiaries in Pakistan by 2021 . <p> The singing ceremony was held here on Wednesday to implement PM 's Clean Green Pakistan Initiative , launched in October 2018 to tackle pollution and increase environmental sustainability for Pakistan 's citizens . PepsiCo 's partnership on this initiative aims to increase access to safe water among community members . <p> " As a responsible corporate entity , I believe this can be a solid first step for building an even stronger relationship in the future . We also urge PepsiCo to assist us in beating the challenge of plastic pollution through waste recycling . " <p> The PepsiCo-supported water programme will focus on providing sustainable solutions in underserved areas of Karachi , Lahore and Islamabad among selected communities , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ established , and local service providers will be trained to ensure proper operations and maintenance of water supplies .
