<p> A subcommittee of the Senate Standing Committee on Climate Change headed by Senator Mushahid Hussain Sayed paid a visit to water channels ( nullahs ) of the federal capital to assess environmental conditions . The subcommittee also visited a proposed landfill site in Sangjani for garbage dumping of the federal capital . The committee noted with concern that no proper sanitation system for the city is in place , for both the encroachments ( kachi abadis ) as well as for the planned sectors of Capital Development Authority ( CDA ) . <p> Senator Mushahid , Senator Muhammad Ali Khan Saif and the representatives of civil society voiced concerns over the current system of sanitation and garbage disposal . It was decided that all the stakeholders would be invited to present their concerns in the future meeting of the subcommittee where the government departments would present their plans to mitigate the situation with planned timelines .
