<h> Two more infants die of malnutrition in Thar <h> Two more infants die of malnutrition in Thar <p> MITHI : The death toll of children dying from malnutrition rose to 333 this year as two more infants died in the drought-hit Thar region of Sindh on Saturday . The government 's relief measures seem insufficient and people need more wheat , essential food items and drinking water facilities . <p> A 15-month-old boy , Dileep , son of Raman Bheel and resident of village Jagari , along with a two-day-old boy , son of Fateh Ali Lashari , died of malnutrition and diseases at Mithi Civil Hospital . <p> While the drought in Thar has adversely affected all Tharis , with the loss of grassy lands and water scarcity , the children have been the worst affected . Due to food insecurity and poverty , cases of malnutrition and diseases are on the rise . <p> The drought is being attributed to climate change which is a global issue .
