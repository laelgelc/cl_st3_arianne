The logic of a free choice -the choice of choosing between black and white -has often been questioned . The possibility of choosing something from waste is no freedom ; on the contrary , one 's decision to reject the given choices for a real alternative -- that can pacify the existence , making life worth living for humanity -defines the real freedom . <p> People have become victims of the manipulative democracy devoid of consent ; they consider themselves choosers or electors , with a false consciousness of being powerful without having any control over politics .
