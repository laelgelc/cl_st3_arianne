<p> The government has decided that Prime Minister 's Youth Programme will take Green Youth Movement ( GYM ) and Startup Pakistan Programmes to all universities . Special Assistant to the Prime Minister on Youth Affairs Muhammad Usman Dar called on Dr Tariq Banuri , Chairman Higher Education Commission , on Tuesday to discuss establishment of GYM Clubs and introduction of Prime Minister 's Startup Pakistan programme in all public and private sector universities of Pakistan , to be run under the umbrella of Prime Minister 's Kamyab Jawan Programme . <p> GYM Club project aims at civic engagement of youth , especially the university students , by making them execute GYM under Prime Minister 's Kamyab Jawan Programme . It would create a platform for social contribution of youth in raising awareness about climate change in the country , besides helping them earn decent livelihood by promoting social entrepreneurship to address challenges of climate change . <p> Startup Pakistan Programme would be a flagship initiative of Prime Minister 's Kamyab Jawan Programme to create a national eco-system for the development of Innovation-Driven Entrepreneurs ( IDEs ) in the country . It intends to train youth across universities and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ incubation platform . It would not only promote entrepreneurship culture but would also create employment opportunities for youth in the country . <p> It would forward the sustainable economic growth in the modern knowledge economy era in a great way . The meeting agreed that HEC would collaborate with Prime Minister 's Youth office for effective implementation of these programmes under the guidance of Prime Minister Imran Khan for socio-economic uplift of youth . Additionally , proposals on National Internship , National Youth Employment Exchange ( NYEX ) , Education Support for Undergrad Students and Laptop for All programmes also came under discussion .
