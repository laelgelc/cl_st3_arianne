Experiments accompanied by the self-interest of powerful groups have brought the economy to its knees . Self-interest groups are not limited to politicians , they also include all segments of society . <p> The government is searching for a feasible and affordable way to combat the economic slowdown . It is looking to revive the economy in the shortest possible time .
