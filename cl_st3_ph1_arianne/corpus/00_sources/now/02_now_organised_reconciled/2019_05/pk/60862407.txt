<p> Where urbanisation is a natural phenomenon , counter-urbanisation becomes a necessity . The ' City of Gardens , ' which it was once called , is turning into a congested place . <p> However , Lahore is not an exception -- other cities of Pakistan , including Karachi , Hyderabad , Faisalabad , Rawalpindi and Peshawar , are also hit by a growing urbanisation .
