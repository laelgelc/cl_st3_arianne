<h> Art installation Pavilion of Pakistan to be featured at the 58th La Biennale di Venezia <p> Foundation Art Divvy Creative Head Zahra Khan is curating the pavilion , the first to officially represent Pakistan at the International Art Exhibition of La Biennale di Venezia . <p> Artist Naiza Khan has been selected to present her work as part of the installation . Another first -- this is a show brought to Venice and conceived by a team of women . Art Divvy Director Asma Rashid , curator Zahra Khan and Naiza Khan will rub shoulders at the event . <p> Manora Field Notes will immerse the viewer in life upon Manora Island , part of a small archipelago off the harbour of Karachi . Responding to the overarching theme of the 58th International Art Exhibition of La Biennale di Venezia May You Live in Interesting Times , curator Zahra Khan explains , " There are few countries which have experienced the upheaval which Pakistan has in recent times .
