<h> Share this story <p> The United Kingdom has declared a state of environment and climate emergency , the first country in the world to do so . <p> The proposal is symbolic of the country 's willingness to act on climate change , but does not legally oblige the government to take action . <p> The idea was proposed by Jeremy Corbyn , the leader of the UK 's left-wing Labour party , and follows a series of large public protests by environmentalist group Extinction Rebellion ( XR ) , who have been blockading roads to protest against the British government 's inaction on climate change . More than a thousand XR protesters have been arrested in recent weeks . <p> XR says that the UK government 's pledge to reduce carbon emissions by 80 per cent by 2050 falls well short of meeting climate goals , and have demanded a rolling back of all climate-toxic policies , such as investment in fossil fuels power generation or barriers to clean energy . <p> " We pledge to work as closely as possible with countries that are serious about ending the climate catastrophe and make clear to US President @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ action on the climate crisis . " <p> Corbyn was referring to Trump 's pledge to pull the United States out of the Paris Climate Agreement . <p> The Extinction Rebellion movement has taken root in countries in Asia Pacific since it launched in London earlier this year , with demonstrations hitting towns and cities in New Zealand , Australia , Japan , and India in recent weeks .
