<h> Brazil plan to revamp Amazon Fund irks Norway , Germany - sources <p> BRASILIA ( Reuters ) - Brazil 's right-wing government has run into resistance from key donors Norway and Germany over its plans to change management of the billion-dollar Amazon Fund aimed at reducing deforestation , people familiar with the matter say . <p> If President Jair Bolsonaro 's government unilaterally alters the governance model for the sustainable development fund , it could lead the two European nations to stop donations or even take back unused funds , the sources said , asking for anonymity given the sensitivity of talks . <p> Norway and Germany responded that they were satisfied with the administration of the $1.28-billion fund by Brazilian state development bank BNDES and asked the minister to provide his proposed changes in writing . <p> " By Thursday afternoon , we had not received any written proposals nor an inspection report from the BNDES , " a spokeswoman for the German embassy in Brasilia told Reuters . <p> The Environment Ministry did not immediately reply to a request for comment . <p> Norway has donated $1.2 billion and Germany has paid out $68 million , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
