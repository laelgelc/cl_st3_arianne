<h> Climate change or climate crisis ? To really engage people , the media should talk about solutions <h> On a mission to convey the environmental crises unfolding around the world , British news outlet The Guardian has altered its style guide , spurring other media organisations to consider switching to terms such as climate emergency or breakdown . How else can the media effectively tell the story of climate change ? <p> Youth from all over the world have taken to the streets this year to demand serious action on climate change .
