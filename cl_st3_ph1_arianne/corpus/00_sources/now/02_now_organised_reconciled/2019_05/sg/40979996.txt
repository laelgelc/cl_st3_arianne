I continue to place my faith in the potential of international institutions to enable concerted solutions for equality despite the sticky floor . <p> We now have a more holistic gender architecture at the United Nations and UN Women , where women are better positioned to have a seat at the decision-making table . So even if we have sticky floors in international organisations , we need to clean them to create opportunities for as many women as possible , and to get leadership at the top to mainstream gender issues . <p> Having women leaders is absolutely vital because we find an embodied commitment to mainstreaming .
