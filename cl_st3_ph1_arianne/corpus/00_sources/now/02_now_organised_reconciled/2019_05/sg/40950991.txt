<p> JAKARTA ( THE JAKARTA POST/ASIA NEWS NETWORK ) - A survey has revealed that 18 per cent of Indonesians do not believe human activity causes climate change , while another 6 per cent believe that the climate is not changing at all . <p> Indonesia has the highest percentage of climate change deniers among 23 countries surveyed in a study conducted from Feb 28 to March 26 by global public opinion and data company YouGov . <p> A quarter of Indonesians said humans were mainly responsible for climate change , while 29 per cent believed other factors also played a role . Another 21 per cent of respondents answered by saying they did not know . <p> Eight per cent of Indonesians said human-driven global warming was a hoax and part of a conspiracy theory . <p> Saudi Arabia is second after Indonesia , with 16 per cent of people who do not believe that humans contribute to climate change , followed by the United States ( 13 per cent ) , South Africa ( 11 per cent ) , Mexico ( 10 per cent ) and Egypt ( 10 per cent ) . <p> However , Indonesians @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ imported goods ( 87 per cent ) , as well as ethical or eco-friendly products ( 92 per cent ) . <p> In comparison , Italy , which had 48 per cent of respondents say that humans were mainly responsible for climate change , had fewer people say it was important to buy local products ( 80 per cent ) and ethical or eco-friendly products ( 79 per cent ) . <p> The Guardian , which exclusively published the YouGov data , noted that " a clear majority of people around the world think climate change is happening and that it is all or partly down to human actions " . <h> The Straits Times <p> We have been experiencing some problems with subscriber log-ins and apologise for the inconvenience caused .
