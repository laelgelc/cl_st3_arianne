<h> Survey finds Singaporean millennials ambitious yet pessimistic <p> Financial advisory firm Deloitte recently researched the global mindsets and outlook of millennials born between 1983 and 1994 . Out of the total of 13,416 millennials surveyed throughout 42 different countries , 200 were Singaporeans . <p> The survey , which was conducted from Dec 2018 to Jan 2019 , also surveyed 3,009 Gen Z respondents ( born between 1995 to 2002 ) from 10 countries . <p> Called the 2019 Deloitte Millennial Survey , it revealed several interesting points about today 's millennials , although the overall picture they have of their world is quite bleak . The survey results showed that most millenials are discouraged by the prevalence of economic inequality , lack of social mobility , and uncertainty of political and environmental issues . Millennials have also grown skeptical of traditional media and are distrustful of political and religious leaders . <p> The Deloitte survey surmised that the uncertainty brought about by the recession from ten years ago along with the massive changes from the fourth industrial revolution greatly contributed to the permeating anxiety of millennials around the world . <p> Bleak outlook among Singaporean millennials <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ is to earn a high salary and be wealthy , higher than the global average of 52 percent .
