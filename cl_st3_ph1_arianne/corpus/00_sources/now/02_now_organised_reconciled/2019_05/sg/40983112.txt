Exit polls seem to suggest that the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party will remain in power , though others have warned that these surveys have hardly been reliable in the past . <p> If the BJP does return , critics of the government have argued that its willingness to attack Indian institutions and even nominate a terror-accused as a Parliamentary candidate could irrevocably change India . Supporters of the BJP , however , argue that if a motley crew of opposition parties comes to power , India will lack stability and be torn apart by internal contradictions . <p> While either of these things may end up being true , the results of elections alone will not change everything about the country .
