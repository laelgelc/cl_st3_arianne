<p> LA PENITA , PANAMA ( AFP ) - Some have died on the way , pregnant women lost their babies and one Haitian was shot in the arm , yet migrants still trek on foot through Panama 's perilous Darien forest in the hope of one day reaching the United States . <p> To reach Panama from Colombia , the migrants - mostly from Haiti and Cuba , but also Africa and Asia - brave the nightmare journey across the 575,000ha of jungle where snakes and jaguars lurk . And there are no roads . <p> Migrants , many of whom can not swim , also have to cross rivers where they could be swept away by a powerful current . <p> " In the group I came with , at least five people stayed behind ( in the jungle ) and are dying , " said Mr Pierre Louis Clivens , a Haitian who tackled the journey with his wife and seven-year-old son . <p> Yet the greatest danger stems from people and drug traffickers . <p> " They took all my money .
