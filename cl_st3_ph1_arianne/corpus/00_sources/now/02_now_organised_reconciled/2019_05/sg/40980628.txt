<h> " ScUber " launches submarine trips to Great Barrier Reef <p> A select few Australians will soon be able to briefly book an Uber submarine to the Great Barrier Reef , the ride-sharing giant said Thursday . <p> From next week -- in what Uber is pitching as a world first -- people in Queensland state will be able to order a ride through the Uber app on a two-person submarine and tour the World Heritage site . <p> A piloted battery-powered " scUber " , costing Aus$1,500 ( $1,030 ) per person , will take 20 dives between May 27 and June 18 with each trip lasting an hour . <p> The sub will take two passengers down to 20 meters below sea level and along designated dive paths around the reef , in partnership with environmental advocacy group Citizens of the Great Barrier Reef . <p> Susan Anderson , general manager for Uber Australia and New Zealand , said the company was committed to encouraging ride-sharing to make " conscious behaviour changes " to travel in more efficient and sustainable ways . <p> A scUber booking includes a helicopter trip to the submarine 's dive location @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ than 1,500 fish species and coral varieties , but has been seriously damaged by pollution and climate change .
