They will see if the gas will interact with the clay and whether a faultline will allow it to seep out . <p> The first eight-month phase of the experiment involves a tiny volume of carbon dioxide ( CO2 ) , with 500mg of carbon dioxide pumped into the rock through a borehole . <p> " If this rock has a fault @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ up through the fault .
