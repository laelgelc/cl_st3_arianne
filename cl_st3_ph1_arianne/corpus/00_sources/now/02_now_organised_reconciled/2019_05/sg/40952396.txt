<p> CHRIST CHURCH -- The 11th International Labour Organisation ( ILO ) Meeting of Caribbean Ministers of Labour officially started this week in Barbados under the theme ' Shaping a brighter future of work for the Caribbean " . <p> Hosted in collaboration with the Ministry of Labour and Social Partnership Relations of the Government of Barbados , the event launched with an opening ceremony at Accra Hotel yesterday . <p> Keynote speakers included Guy Ryder , Director-General of the ILO , and Colin Jordan , Minister of Labour and Social Partnership Relations for Barbados . Other Ministers of Labour from across the Caribbean , representatives of regional employers ' and workers ' organizations , as well as representatives of other United Nations agencies and regional organizations were also in attendance . <p> This year 's Meeting is based on the ILO Global Commission on the Future of Work Report titled " Work for a Brighter Future " . It was published in January 2019 , at the beginning of the ILO 's Centenary year , kicking off the organisation 's celebrations . It must happen by conscious action , national , regional and international , " Ryder in his opening remarks . <p> The Meeting seeks to assess the relevance of the Global Commission 's Report on the Future of Work in the Caribbean . Sessions will use interactive methods to help participants discuss the relevance of the Report to the Caribbean and share ideas on how its recommendations can help address key labour and social issues faced by the region . <p> " Undoubtedly for us in the Caribbean , intimate knowledge of how the world of work is changing is of paramount importance , if we are truly to be competitive on the world stage . There are myriad of issues to contend with such as climate change , demographic changes , geo-politics , advanced and new technologies , globalisation . They will also take part in discussions aimed at preparing the Caribbean 's contribution to the ILO Centenary International Labour Conference in June 2019 . <p> The 11th International Labour Organisation ( ILO ) Meeting of Caribbean Ministers of Labour concludes this afternoon .
