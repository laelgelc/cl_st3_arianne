<h> US births lowest in 3 decades despite improving economy <p> America 's baby bust is n't over . The nation 's birth rates last year reached record lows for women in their teens and 20s , a government report shows , leading to the fewest babies in 32 years . <p> The provisional report , released Wednesday and based on more than 99% of U.S. birth records , found 3.788 million births last year . It was the fourth year the number of births has fallen , the lowest since 1986 and a surprise to some experts given the improving economy . <p> The fertility rate of 1.7 births per U.S. woman also fell 2% , meaning the current generation is n't making enough babies to replace itself . The fertility rate is a hypothetical estimate based on lifetime projections of age-specific birth rates . <p> Whether more U.S. women are postponing motherhood or forgoing it entirely is n't yet clear . <p> If trends continue , experts said , the U.S. can expect labor shortages including in elder care when aging baby boomers need the most support . <p> " I keep expecting to see the birth @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ said demographer Kenneth M.
