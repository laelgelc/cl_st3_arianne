The comment period ended on March 13 . <p> The study was among the first of its kind since Trump 's Interior Department in 2017 issued an order that assessments under the National Environmental Policy Act be completed within one year and be no longer than 150 pages . <p> NEPA studies under past administrations have taken years and filled out thousands of pages , a major source of frustration for drillers , miners and other industries that argue the process creates unnecessary delays . <p> Experts said the effort to streamline environmental permitting , however , could also cause problems . <p> " Imposing the timelines and page limits will mean significant impacts go un-analyzed . It has been a lightning rod of contention between energy companies that want to develop it and conservationists that want to protect it since the 1970s.
