banana crop . <p> Black Sigatoka disease emerged from Asia in the late 20th century and has recently completed its invasion of banana-growing areas in Latin America and the Caribbean . <p> The study , published in the journal Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B , stated that changes to moisture and temperature conditions have increased the risk of Black Sigatoka by more than 44 per
