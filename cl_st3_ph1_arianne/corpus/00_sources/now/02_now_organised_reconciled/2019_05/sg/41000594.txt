<p> Malaysia will send back plastic scrap imported illegally from several countries , including Singapore and Bangladesh , Minister of Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Yeo Bee Yin said yesterday . <p> " We will work very hard to make sure that whoever sends their waste to Malaysia , we will send it back and we will fight back . Even though we are a small country , we can not be bullied by developed countries , " she told a news conference after inspecting nine containers of waste from Australia , Bangladesh , Canada , China , Japan , Saudi Arabia and the US at Westport , Klang . <p> Containers full of contaminated , non-homogeneous , low-quality , non-recyclable plastic waste are entering the country , contravening local and international laws , said Ms Yeo . She showed reporters plastic milk cartons riddled with maggots from Australia , while a container from China had packaging from France among its contents . <p> Other containers held mixed plastic and electronic waste , brought into the country under false declarations and other pretences . <p> She said that one recycling company in Britain exported @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ containers to Malaysia during the past two years . <p> " Garbage like what you see just now is traded under the pretext of recycling , " said Ms Yeo . <p> " What the citizens of the UK believe they are sending for recycling is actually being dumped in our country , " she said . <p> FALSE PRETENCES <p> Garbage like what you see just now is traded under the pretext of recycling . <p> " We urge the developed countries to review their management of plastic waste and stop shipping garbage out to developing countries .
