<h> Rallying cry against water crisis <p> Women throwing earthen pitchers onto the ground on Thursday in protest against a shortage of drinking water outside the municipal corporation office in Ahmedabad , India . <p> From South Africa to India , and across North Africa and the Middle East , water scarcity is creating growing stresses as rising wealth and population growth , combined with climate change , place an unsustainable strain on water reserves . <p> In Asia , 3.4 billion people could be living in " water-stressed areas " by 2050 , according to a 2016 Asian Development Bank report . <p> A version of this article appeared in the print edition of The Straits Times on May 18 , 2019 , with the headline ' Rallying cry against water crisis ' .
