Globally , crop residues -- the plant waste left in the field after harvest -- totaled 5 billion metric tonnes ( 5.5 billion tonnes ) in 2013 , according to a 2018 Brazilian study . By doing so , they hope to not only reduce adverse environmental impacts of agriculture , but also provide a new source of income for farmers . <h> Waste of good chocolate <p> Take cacao , for example . For every pound of cacao beans -- the part of the plant that ends up in your chocolate bar -- farmers produce about 12 times as much biomass .
