<h> Legarda on probable speakership : Surprised , but can do job <h> Aleta Nishimori , ABS-CBN News <p> MANILA - Outgoing Senator Loren Legarda said she was surprised but ready , after she was floated as among possible contenders for the speakership of the House of Representatives . <p> " I 'm surprised my name was even there . However , if it 's the ability to perform a good job as speaker I believe that I can do it . Humbly speaking , insofar as performance in the job at hand , " Legarda said in an interview on ANC 's Headstart . <p> " However , the speakership is a choice among the 297 elected representatives of the various districts of the country . It 's not for a person to claim it . You can vie for it , " Legarda said . <p> While at the House , Legarda also hopes to get the chairmanship of a committee she 's familiar with . <p> " I know finance very well .
