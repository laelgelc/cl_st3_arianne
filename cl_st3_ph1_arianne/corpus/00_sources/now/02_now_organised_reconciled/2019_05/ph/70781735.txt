<h> Legarda commends government resolve to send back garbage to Canada <p> Outgoing Senator Loren Legarda , Chair of the Senate Committee on Foreign Relations , commended the Duterte administration 's strong resolve to send back the shipment by a Canadian company of more than 100 containers of household trash , including soiled bags and diapers , that was sent to the Philippines in 2013 and 2014 . <p> Senator Loren Legarda ( FACEBOOK / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> Legarda leaves her chairmanship of the Senate foreign relations committee on July 1 after her stint in the 24-member Senate and joins the
