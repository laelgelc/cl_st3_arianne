<h> Camiguin receives surveillance seacraft from DENR <p> CAGAYAN DE ORO CITY -- Camiguin has received a seacraft from the Department of Environment and Natural Resources ( DENR ) for the patrol and surveillance of the island province 's coastal areas . <p> DENR-Region 10 Director Arleigh Adorable said Wednesday the seacraft will primarily be used to guard the province 's 31 marine protected areas . <p> Adorable said the double-engine watercraft has a capacity of more or less 10 passengers . <p> Funded under the government 's Coastal and Marine Ecosystems Management Program ( CMEMP ) , the vessel will be managed by the Provincial Environment and Natural Resources Office ( PENRO ) . <p> Adorable said CMEMP aims to manage , address and effectively reduce the drivers and threats of degradation of the coastal and marine ecosystems in order to achieve and promote sustainability of ecosystem services , food security , and climate change resiliency . <p> It is being implemented in collaboration with other national government agencies , local government units , and non-government organizations . <p> Mambajao Mayor Jurdin Jesus Rumualdo said he was elated with the program as it will heighten their effort to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ ( PNA )
