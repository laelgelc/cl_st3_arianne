<h> UN chief hails Pacific 's ' moral authority ' on climate <p> UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres said Wednesday the Pacific possesses " a unique moral authority " to demand global action on climate change because its people are bearing the brunt of the problem . <p> Guterres ' South Pacific tour includes Fiji , New Zealand and Tuvalu and will highlight the dangers of global warming ( AFP / MICHAEL BRADLEY / MANILA BULLETIN ) <p> Visiting Fiji as part of a South Pacific tour to highlight the dangers of global warming , Guterres said the region was on the front line of climate change . <p> " Here in the Pacific , sea-level rise in some countries is four times greater than the global average and is an existential threat to some island states , " he said . <p> He said this was despite the fact that the Pacific islands contributed little to global warming and had been among the first nations to minimize their greenhouse gas emissions . <p> " Your experiences underscore the urgency of the threat , " he said . " The Pacific has a unique moral authority to speak out @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> Guterres ' visit -- which also includes New Zealand , Tuvalu , and Vanuatu -- will see the UN chief meet locals whose lives have been upended by cyclones , flooding and other extreme weather events linked to climate change . <p> It comes ahead of the Climate Action Summit in September in New York , billed as a last chance to prevent irreversible climate change . <p> Members of the Pacific Islands Forum regional grouping commended Guterres for traveling to see " the everyday reality of climate change " . <p> They sent a strong message to the New York conference , warning " we are facing an unprecedented global catastrophe for our blue planet " . <p> " Platitudes and repackaged commitments can not be the substance of our deliberations , " they said in a statement .
