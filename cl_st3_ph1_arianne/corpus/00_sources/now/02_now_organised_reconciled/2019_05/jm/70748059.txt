<h> Gov't taking lead in net-zero energy building design <p> Minister of Local Government Desmond McKenzie says the government is not simply talking about energy-efficiency in building design , it is leading the way . <p> Addressing the Net Zero Energy Building workshop at the Knutsford Court hotel in Kingston , earlier this week , the minister said environmental efficiency was now a design element in several municipal projects . <p> " The municipal building in Portmore and in Westmoreland and the infirmaries , including the one in Manchester , which I broke ground for last week , are all based on designs that encompass energy efficiency , rainwater harvesting and other environmentally sensitive inputs , " McKenzie said . <p> Head of UN Environment 's Sub-Regional Office , Victor Sweeney said energy-efficient building design is an increasingly critical aspect of both urban planning and climate change management . <p> " Globally , buildings account for about one-third of total energy consumption , and in most countries around the world , are the leading sources of greenhouse gas emissions .
