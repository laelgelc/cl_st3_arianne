<h> Paris climate accord <h> House passes bill to keep US in deal <p> The Democratic-controlled House of Representatives on Thursday passed legislation that would prevent President Donald Trump from following through on his withdrawal of the United States from the Paris climate agreement . <p> The Republican-led Senate is highly unlikely to take up the measure , which would bar federal funds from being used to withdraw from the accord . <p> But the move by House Democrats appears aimed at framing the president as opposed to fighting global warming . <p> Trump announced in mid-2017 that he was pulling out of the accord , arguing it imposed unfair environmental standards on US firms and government , and that remaining in the deal would pose a threat to the American economy . <p> The earliest possible exit would be November 2020 , the month of the US presidential election , positioning climate action as a campaign issue . <p> The House voted 231 to 190 to pass the Climate Action Now Act , with three Republicans crossing the political aisle to vote with Democrats . <p> House Speaker Nancy Pelosi described climate action as a moral , economic @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ bill was a step in the right direction . <p> But Pelosi acknowledged the bill would be " dead on arrival " in the Senate . <p> Last year , US government scientists issued a 1,000-page report warning that climate change will cost the United States hundreds of billions of dollars annually unless drastic action is taken to cut carbon emissions .
