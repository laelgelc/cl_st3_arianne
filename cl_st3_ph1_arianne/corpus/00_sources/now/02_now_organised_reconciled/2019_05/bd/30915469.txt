<h> Plastic waste from Bangladesh found shipped into Malaysia <h> Plastic waste in a container from Bangladesh has been found to be shipped into Malaysia . <p> It was discovered that compact discs or CDs were hidden inside a container with clean recyclable scraps at the front , according to the Star , a Malaysian newspaper . <p> " I was very shocked . In front was legal waste , while the illegal waste was placed behind it . I really hope things like this would not happen again , " Energy , Science , Technology , Environment and Climate Change Minister Yeo Yee Bin said during a press conference later , when asked about the waste from the South Asian country . <p> Besides Bangladesh , the other containers came from Japan , the United Kingdom , Saudi Arabia , Canada , Australia , America , and China . <p> " We will be sending all this waste back , " said Yeo , adding that Malaysia would be sending back 450 metric tonnes of plastic in 10 containers . <p> She also said that the inspection process on another 50 containers brought in illegally is still ongoing @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ from 60 containers is expected to be shipped back , once the inspection process is fully completed , reported The Star . <p> Before this , five containers were sent back to Spain on April 29 . <p> To date , 123 containers have been inspected through joint operations between several agencies , including the Department of Environment or DOE , the customs , the police , Port Klang Authority , and the Department of National Solid Waste Management . <p> The waste smuggled in was falsely declared as recyclable . She said the process to inspect the waste was both laborious and costly . <p> Yeo said the local importers involved were traitors to the country 's sustainability , and that they should be brought to justice . <p> " These people do n't love the country , " she said . <p> Yeo said they had discovered one recycling company from the UK who had exported 50,000 metric tonnes of plastic waste into Malaysia over the past two years . <p> " We urge developed countries to review their waste management of plastic waste and stop shipping it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ China banned plastic imports earlier last year , leading to a huge impact on the global recycling system . This led to a number of Chinese companies relocating their operations to Malaysia , with some setting up shop here as soon as the Chinese government announced the ban in 2017 , according to the Star . <p> A news reports said that Malaysia 's imports of plastic waste from its 10 biggest source-countries jumped to 456,000 tonnes between January and July 2018 , versus 316,600 tonnes purchased in all of 2017 and 168,500 tonnes in 2016 . <p> Most of the plastic scrap coming into the country is contaminated and low-quality plastic from developed countries that is non-recyclable.
