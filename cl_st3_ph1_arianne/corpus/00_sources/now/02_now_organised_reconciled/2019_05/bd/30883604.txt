File photo <p> Dhaka , May 18 ( UNB ) - United Nations Secretary-General Antonio Guterres has called on the world 's decision-makers to make " enlightened " choices on climate action because " the whole planet " is at stake . <p> " Over the past week , I 've witnessed firsthand the impacts of climate change in the Pacific Island States , " the UN chief said in a statement on Friday concluding a week-long visit to the South Pacific . <p> He said : " They contribute very little to the global climate emergency and yet , they 're the ones that are being most affected . " <p> The UN chief , according to UN News , did not mince words in saying that for some of them , " climate change is now an existential threat " . <p> Pointing out that entire villages are being relocated @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Guterres lamented : " The risks are all too real " . <p> The UN chief found " remarkable " that the countries facing these enormous challenges , have decided that " they are not giving up " but are instead " determined to find solutions " . <p> Not only have they developed ways to increase their resilience and adaptation , but according to Guterres , " they are leading the way in reducing emissions and are an example that the rest of the world should follow " . <p> Reiterating the powerful message that he has continued to underscore throughout his visit to the South Pacific , the UN chief stressed that " climate change can not be stopped by the small island countries alone , it has to be stopped by the rest of the world " and that this requires the political will for " transformational policies in energy , mobility , industry and agriculture " . <p> Guterres echoed the " three urgent messages " to world leaders that he had " consistently conveyed " throughout his visit to the Pacific , beginning with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to tax pollution , not people , " he reiterated . <p> Second , he flagged that countries must stop subsidising fossil fuels . <p> " Taxpayers ' money should n't be used to boost hurricanes , spread drought and heatwaves , melt glaciers and bleach corals , " he asserted . <p> And third , he argued against building new coal plants by 2020 , saying " we need a green economy not a grey economy " . <p> Guterres said solidarity or generosity is not being sought , " it is enlightened self-interest from all decision-makers around the world " that is needed " because it 's not only of the Pacific that is at stake , it 's the whole planet " . <p> " To save the Pacific is to save the whole planet , " concluded the Secretary-General .
