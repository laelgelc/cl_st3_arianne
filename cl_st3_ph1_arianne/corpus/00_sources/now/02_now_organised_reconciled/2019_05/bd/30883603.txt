<h> Agriculture minister : ' PM concerned about falling paddy prices ' <p> Agriculture Minister Dr M Abdur Razzaque addresses a seminar on the challenges of agriculture sector at Institution of Diploma Engineers , Bangladesh in Dhaka on Saturday Courtesy <p> He also said the government is trying to address the issue of paddy price but there is no scope to minimize it overnight <p> Agriculture minister Dr Md Abdur Razzaque on Saturday said Bangladesh became self-reliance in food grains production in the last couples of years due to the innovative measures taken by the present government . <p> " Innovation , modernization and mechanization in agriculture sector uplifted Bangladesh as self-sufficient country in food grains production in the regime of the Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina , " he said while addressing a seminar on the impact of climate change . <p> The minister said Bangladeshi scientists are developing different variety crops , which are able to yield successfully overcoming the adverse impacts of climate changes such as salinity , drought and other natural calamities . <p> Referring to the recent issue of paddy price , he said the government is trying to address the issue of paddy price @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> " Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina is very much concerned about the interest of farmers and that is why the government is sincerely working on it , " said Razzaque . <p> Responding to a question , he said the government is working to export rice to address the issue of price but it is not easy as Bangladesh is not a rice exporting country . <p> Besides , the government , the minister said , could not buy paddy directly from the farmers due to different limitations , especially for shortage of godowns and silos . <p> " We do not have enough storage capacity and selection of farmers in local level is another complex issue which bars the government to buy paddy directly from farmers , " he added . <p> PKSF Chairman Dr Qazi Kholiquzzaman Ahmad addressed the seminar as special guest with Engineer AKMA Hamid of IDEB in the chair .
