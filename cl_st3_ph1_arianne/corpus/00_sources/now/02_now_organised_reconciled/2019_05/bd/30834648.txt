<h> $5bn ' healthy oceans , sustainable blue economies ' plan launched <p> The action plan will support the efforts of ADB 's developing member countries to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) , including SDG 14 : Life Below Water . Photo ADB <p> Dhaka , May 2 ( UNB ) - The Asian Development Bank ( ADB ) on Thursday launched the action plan for " healthy oceans and sustainable blue economies " for the Asia and Pacific region . <p> The action plan was laid out at the 52nd Annual Meeting of ADB 's Board of Governors in Nadi , Fiji . <p> The action plan will expand financing and technical assistance for ocean health and marine economy projects to $5 billion from 2019 to 2024 , including cofinancing from partners . <p> " The prosperity of our region depends on healthy oceans and sustainable development , " said ADB President Takehiko Nakao adding , " We must work towards a more resilient future , where humanity and oceans thrive together . protecting and restoring coastal and marine ecosystems and key rivers ; reducing land-based sources of marine pollution , including plastics , wastewater , and agricultural runoff ; and improving sustainability in port and coastal infrastructure development . <p> Asia and the Pacific is at the epicenter of a major crisis in marine plastic pollution , threatening the productivity of the region 's marine economies , which are crucial to poverty reduction , says ADB . <p> For example , it said , among the 10 rivers transporting 88 percent to 95 percent of plastics into the sea worldwide , eight are in the region . <p> Ocean ecosystems have been pushed to the brink of collapse by the threats of climate change , pollution , and illegal and unregulated fishing , among others . <p> Unless immediate action is taken , about 90 percent of Asia and the Pacific 's coral reefs will be dead by 2050 , and all commercially exploitable fish stocks will disappear @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , the global economy , and livelihoods , especially among millions of poor and vulnerable communities in the region , ADB says . <p> As a part of the action plan , ADB will launch the Oceans Financing Initiative to create opportunities for the private sector to invest in bankable projects that will help improve ocean health . <p> The initiative will provide technical assistance grants and funding from ADB and other donors to reduce the technical and financial risks of projects . <p> This will be done through instruments such as credit risk guarantees and capital market " blue bonds " . <p> The Oceans Financing Initiative will be piloted in Southeast Asia in collaboration with the Asean Infrastructure Fund and the Republic of Korea . <p> The World Wide Fund for Nature , a longtime partner of ADB , will support the design and implementation of the financing initiative .
