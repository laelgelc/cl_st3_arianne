Humans are responsible for anthropogenic climate change . One of the reasons is the excessive use of cars in our day to day lives , carbon dioxide from the combustion of car fuels are accumulating in the atmosphere . <p> Bangladesh is still a developing country . For this very reason many other alternatives , such as hydrogen fuel cells and electric cars are very difficult to import to Bangladesh .
