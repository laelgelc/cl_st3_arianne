<h> Agriculture done right <p> Both the importance of agriculture and the rising threat of climate change can not be understated <p> When it comes to the agriculture sector , it remains one of the most crucial ones for the economy , being one of the largest contributors to the GDP and by far the largest employer of Bangladeshis . <p> Keeping that in mind , and with the awareness that climate change is one of the biggest threats facing us not only as citizens of Bangladesh but as citizens of this world , it is crucial to employ practices which create a sustainable agriculture industry while also employing climate-friendly practices . <p> For example , experts have long spoken of a method called Alternate Wetting and Drying ( AWD ) when it comes to rice farming , a technique which cuts the cost of irrigation , reduces the use of precious groundwater , and produces less methane , a gas harmful for the ozone layer . <p> However , despite knowing of the advantages , this water-saving technology has yet to be adopted to the extent necessary for sustainable growth of the industry . <p> Never has it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conscious , as the detrimental effects of climate change end up affecting farmers and their livelihoods the most . <p> As a result , with scientists and experts discovering newer and more efficient methods of agricultural practice , it would be negligent of us to not incentivize farmers to adopt methods which contribute positively to the industry , and to the economy as a whole . <p> Both the importance of agriculture and the rising threat of climate change can not be understated . We hope that , moving forward , authorities take this into account and encourage industries to adapt and become more climate-smart in the process .
