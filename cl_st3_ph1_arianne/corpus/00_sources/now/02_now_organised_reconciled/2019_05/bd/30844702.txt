<h> Living within a safe space <p> How long can we continue our development along a sustainable path ? At what point do we become unsustainable ? <p> Everyone wants to be sustainable . However , do we know how long we can be sustainable ? When do we reach an unsustainable state ? The space just before the unsustainable state is defined as a safe operating space within which we can make our development sustainable and beyond which there is a high risk and disastrous consequences for humanity . <p> A few years ago , Johan Rockstr ? m and his colleagues ( 2009 ) defined a safe operating space at the global scale .
