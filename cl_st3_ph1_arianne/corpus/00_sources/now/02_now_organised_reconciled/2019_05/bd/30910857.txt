The elections were seen as a test of the influence of the nationalist , populist and hard-right movements that have swept the continent in recent years and impelled Britain to quit the EU altogether . In Germany , Chancellor Angela Merkel 's ruling coalition saw a drastic loss in support to the Greens and , to a lesser extent , the far right . Italy 's League party , led by Matteo Salvini , claimed 32% of the vote in early projections , compared with around 6% five years ago . <p> " Not only is the League the first party in Italy , but Marine Le Pen is first in France , Nigel Farage is first in Great Britain .
