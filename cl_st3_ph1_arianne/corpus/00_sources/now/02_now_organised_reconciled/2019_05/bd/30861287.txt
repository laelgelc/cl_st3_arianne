<h> Barisal city faces acute drinking water crisis <p> Many BCC residents , especially in the slums are now facing an acute crisis of drinking water <p> Barisal city folk have been facing an acute drinking water crisis and the problem has gotten worse because the groundwater level in the city has fallen . <p> Mainul Islam , executive engineer of the Public Health Engineering Department ( PHED ) , Barisal office , said the ground water level in greater Barisal city has dropped to 40 feet from 25 feet . Water pumps and tube wells can no longer draw water from underground . <p> In the last three or four years , ground water levels fall at least 15 feet every summer . People are not able to pump water since the pipes do not go down the needed 40 feet or more . In the monsoon season the water level once again goes up to the required level , Mainul Islam said . <p> Due to excessive drawing of groundwater and climate change , the water level may have dropped so drastically , the PHED official assumed . <p> Humayun Kabir , executive engineer of water supply @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to take charge of two surface water treatment plants ( SWTP ) from PHED in Rapatali and Beltala areas due to some technical problems . <p> When the two surface water treatment plants start operating , the drinking water problem may be solved , he hoped . <p> About six lakh residents in thirty wards of BCC have an average demand of 5.6 crore liters of water per day . At present BCC can only supply 2.9 crore liters , said Khairul Islam , chief executive officer ( CEO ) of BCC . <p> Many BCC residents , especially in the slums are now facing an acute crisis of drinking water . Most city folk are unable to draw drinking water using hand pump tube wells . They are collecting drinking water directly from pump houses or electric motor driven deep tube wells , said Abdullah , a resident of Kawnia in the city . <p> Shathi , a Barisal Stadium slum area resident , said she has been collecting drinking water from a private deep tube well half a kilometer away from her house . <p> Hasina Begum of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ acute crisis of drinking water . <p> Hayatuni Begum of BCC ward 2 , said she is forced to spend a lot of time bringing drinking water from afar . <p> " To meet the demand for drinking water , we have temporarily arranged to supply drinking water to slum areas by water tankers , " said Khairul Islam , CEO of BCC.
