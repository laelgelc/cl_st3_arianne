<h> Technology giant to establish digital solution centre at varsity 's main campus in Thika <p> GOING DIGITAL ; Microsoft regional director for education Simone Ouattara with Mount Kenya University vice chancellor Stanley Waudo after they signed a partnership deal at the university 's main campus in Thika town on Thursday , May 16 , 2019 Image : JOHN KAMAU <p> Mount Kenya University has signed a deal with the multinational technology giant Microsoft Corporation to digitise learning in the institution . <p> The university will inject Sh300 million into improving learning and ensuring it produces graduates who are well versed with the ever-changing technological trends . <p> Microsoft will establish a digital solution centre at MKU 's main campus in Thika as part of the deal , University Council vice chairman Vincent Gaitho said . <p> He said the center will give students a competitive edge in technology and sharpen their skills to enable them easily fit in the competitive job market . <p> " There 's a wide application of technology in all areas in the job market . Our partnership with Microsoft will ensure our students get the necessary knowledge and skills that are vital in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> He spoke after the signing of the MoU at the Thika campus . <p> Gaitho said the council had made great strides for a smooth implementation of the agreement . He said the agreement will solve societal challenges including climate change . <p> " The society will also feel the impact of the programme we are embarking on . Some of the benefits include solutions to major challenges facing the society especially climate change , " he said . <p> Microsoft head of education in Middle East and Africa Simone Outarra urged students to seize the opportunity to enhance their skills . <p> He said a team of six experts will be dispatched to the institution to train students . <p> " We will also award certificates to students undertaking computing and informatics which are globally recognised , " Outarra . <p> MKU vice chancellor Stanley Waudo said the programme was in line with the university 's strategic plan that is aimed at improving learning while offering quality teaching . <p> " As a university , we are determined to impart relevant knowledge and skills to our students @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ once they graduate , " Waudo said . <p> He said the partnership will aid the institution 's automation programme as the university seeks to become the regional transformation hub . <p> The signing of the MoU makes MKU the first university in East Africa to partner with the renowned technology giant .
