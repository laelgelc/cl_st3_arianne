Counties can tap into these talents and employ such skills in agricultural activities such as value chain and internship @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Kenyatta stadium in Embu where FAO was hosting a one day youth agriculture empowerment exhibition . <p> The official said counties have no excuse for not improving agriculture and transforming it into profitable entrepreneurship that can drive the economy . <p> He said FAO has partnered with the Embu county government to incorporate the youth into the agribusiness . The organisation targets 15 counties . <p> In addition , the global relief agency is pursuing policies to create proper linkages to bring on board marketers and financial institutions into agribusiness . <p> Arunga called upon the national and county governments to encourage farmers to embrace smart farming to cushion the country against the threat of food scarcity . <p> He said Kenya still remains a food insecure county and therefore every effort should be put in place to mitigate against undesirable effects emanating from climate change . <p> " As the world is confronted with the reality of climate change , our farmers should be encouraged to embrace smart agriculture , " he said . <p> " Farmers should make sure they utilize the little rain we receive by undertaking @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ rotation to ensure maximum utilization of the little moisture content available . " <p> Machakos county FAO representative Mark Matabi faulted the county and national governments for excluding the youth from agriculture . <p> He said despite young people fresh from college possessing great potential to boost food production , successive governments have never put into place policies and incentives to woo the youth into taking farming as a business . <p> Matabi said the country is staring at a human resource crisis in the sector with 10 per cent of extension officers set to retire without any replacements in sight . <p> " The ratio of extension officers to farmers currently stands at 1:120 . Unfortunately , the majority of these officers are nearing the mandatory retirement age and will soon be leaving the service without any replacement , " he said . <p> " We can use these youth to fill these gaps by roping them into the sector where they can offer extension services to farmers in areas such as spraying , ploughing and training .
