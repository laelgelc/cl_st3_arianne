President Uhuru Kenyatta , his South Sudan counterpart Salva Kiir , Fiji and Yemeni premiers , have committed to accelerating the New Urban Agenda , which calls for sustainable and inclusive cities for all . <p> Speaking at the UN Habitat in Nairobi , yesterday , the leaders emphasised the important role of the private sector in making governments achieve their goals and the collective approach of the UN member states and other players in saving the world from the catastrophic effects of climate change . They called for innovation approaches to allow governments and cities to cope with conflict and war and the resultant outcomes such as diseases , reconstruction and disaster risk management . Kiir condemned the conflict in South Sudan , saying it was responsible for inactive private sector and unavailability of infrastructure and unemployment ravaging the world 's youngest country .
