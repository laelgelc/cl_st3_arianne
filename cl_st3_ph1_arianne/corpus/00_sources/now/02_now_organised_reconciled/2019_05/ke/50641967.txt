<h> UK warns of IS attacks in West Africa <p> UK Foreign Secretary Jeremy Hunt has described the area around north-eastern Nigeria as an " outpost of Daesh Islamic State " and has said the violence could " escalate quickly and ... get out of control " , the Press Association ( PA ) news agency reports . <p> He made the comments when visiting the regional city of Maiduguri as part of his five-nation tour of the continent . <p> Mr Hunt said the rise of the Islamic State group needed to be " nipped in the bud " , PA quotes him as saying . <p> North-east Nigeria has borne the brunt of the violence perpetrated by Islamist militant group Boko Haram . A breakaway faction of Boko Haram has pledged allegiance to IS . <p> Mr Hunt said that the British government wanted to work closely with Nigeria to help deal with the problem . The UK has already trained 30,000 Nigerian troops and offered humanitarian and development support . <p> The foreign secretary also pledged to help tackle climate change , saying that could help prevent the escalation of the conflict .
