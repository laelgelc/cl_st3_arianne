Eight are off course to meet childhood wasting targets and only two might meet the stunting targets . <p> Climate change and conflict also have a significant impact . Three-quarters of all stunted children under the age of five live in conflict zones . <p> In 2017 , armed conflict was the biggest single cause of acute food insecurity in 18 countries , leading to 74 million people needing urgent aid . <p> Tropical storms , droughts and flooding caused food insecurity for over 21 million people in Ethiopia , Malawi , Zimbabwe and Kenya . <p> PRODUCTIVITY <p> Child hunger is fundamentally political -- the offspring of an unholy alliance of political indifference , unaccountable governance and economic mismanagement . <p> It 's a silent tragedy , largely unacknowledged but tolerated , perhaps because it is a poor person 's problem . <p> Hunger has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and suffer from low esteem .
