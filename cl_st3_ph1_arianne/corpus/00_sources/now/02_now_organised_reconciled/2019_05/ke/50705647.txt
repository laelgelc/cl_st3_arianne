<h> It will take children to save us from dangers of climate change <p> To paraphrase Pope Francis , the earth , our common home , is beginning to look more and more like an immense pile of filth . Every open space in our cities has become a public dumpsite . Environmental destruction and climate change are for real and the worst impact is being felt by the poor as we witnessed from the recent devastation caused by cyclones in Mozambique . Climate change will further increase inequalities , cause undependable rains and with unreliable harvests , we will have food shortages and probably food riots in this country . <p> Climate change is manmade so let 's not point the finger at our Creator . We will be the first generation to leave the earth worse than when we found it . We are also the first generation that cares least about the damage we are doing . Do we ever stop to think that our children will suffer the consequences of our greed , indifference and abuse of mother earth ?
