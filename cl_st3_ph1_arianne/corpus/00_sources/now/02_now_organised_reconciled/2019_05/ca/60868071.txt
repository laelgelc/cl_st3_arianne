<p> I cried to know that we have 10 years to cut our carbon emissions in half or we would be irreversibly on track for mass extinction . Article after article shot icy terror through my heart , including the UN 's declaration of one million species being critically at risk for extinction or that we will lose hundreds of thousands of human lives to climate change . This week , MPs are debating federal climate emergency motions . Canada is warming at twice the global rate and we are experiencing more frequent and severe devastating floods and wildfires . There is no doubt that Canada , along with the rest of the world , is facing a climate emergency . For those so concerned about the economy , the global transition to a clean energy economy is already underway at a rapid pace . Canada should be a part of it to help provide stable jobs , a diversified economy and a livable climate . Canada is falling behind.We need to invest in the New Green Deal , and renewable , clean energy if we want to be be leaders on the global stage .
