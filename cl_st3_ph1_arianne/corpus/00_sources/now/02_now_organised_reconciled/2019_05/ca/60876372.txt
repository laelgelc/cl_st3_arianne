<h> Aviation leaders to assemble in Seoul for IATA 's 75th annual meet <p> More than 1,000 senior officials of 290 airlines will gather to attend the 75th annual general meeting of the International Air Transport Association ( IATA ) taking place here for the first time from June 1 to 3 . <p> They will be accompanied by representatives of airline suppliers , governments , strategic partners and international organisations . <p> " The airlines will be meeting in challenging times . 2019 is expected to be the 10th consecutive year of airline profits , but rising costs , trade wars and other uncertainties are likely to have an impact on the bottom line , " said IATA 's Director General and CEO Alexandre de Juniac . <p> " The prolonged grounding of the 737 MAX aircraft is taking its toll . And aviation , like all industries , is under intensified scrutiny for its impact on climate change . The agenda will be full , " he said in a statement . <p> The AGM agenda will feature keynote addresses by Kim Hyun-mee , Minister of Land , Infrastructure and Transport of South Korea and Violeta Bulc @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Air Transport Summit ( WATS ) opens immediately following the AGM under the theme ' The Vision for the Future . In this regard , airline digital transformation , infrastructure capacity , sustainability and building the workforce of the future will feature prominently in the agenda . <p> The inaugural IATA Diversity and Inclusion Awards will also be presented during the event .
