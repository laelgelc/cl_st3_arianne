<h> Kim continues to play games with Trump <p> The crisis atmosphere created two years ago over North Korea 's nuclear weapons and ballistic missiles programs was always overblown . Did North Korea ever pose a serious threat to the security of the United States ? The answer has to be a clear and categorical no . With a few nuclear warheads and some remarkably inaccurate missiles , North Korea would never have dared to launch an attack on the United States . The North Koreans know full well that any such attack would result in massive retaliation that would have destroyed the country and its regime . Now the North Korean regime is a murderous and brutal dictatorship that readily inflicts untold misery on its population , but it has given no indication that it is suicidal . On the contrary , its main political objective is regime survival . Why risk that in a totally futile and counterproductive attack on the United States ? After a yearlong slanging match , the two decided to cool the rhetoric and hold a summit meeting in Singapore . That meeting was at least a partial success for Kim . The vaguely worded communique emanating from the meeting at least put an end to American threats and secured an undertaking to end joint military exercises between the United States and South Korea . It did not , of course , result in the lifting of American and international sanctions against North Korea , which was Kim 's principal objective . For Donald Trump , the meeting was portrayed as a diplomatic success , even @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ were full of holes ( no list of facilities , no timetable , no provisions for inspections ) . Despite these shortcomings , Trump proclaimed his " love " for Kim Jong Un and agreed to hold a second summit meeting with him in Hanoi a few weeks ago . <p> The Hanoi meeting brought the relationship back down to earth . Kim was unwilling to make concrete the steps he would take to denuclearize , and Trump was unwilling to lift any sanctions . The meeting proved to be a total failure . This outcome was not unexpected . It has been clear from the start that Kim was playing games with Trump and relying on the latter 's ego to keep the fiction alive . Kim has actually no intention of totally giving up his nuclear arsenal . Without that arsenal he is a nobody on the world scene . With it he can command the attention of major leaders , including the president of the United States . And Kim is obviously a rather vain young man who enjoys all of the attention he gets . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ attempts to spread his diplomatic wings . He has held meetings with Chinese President Xi Jin Ping and with Russian President Vladimir Putin . These meetings resulted in expressions of mutual goodwill , but nothing very concrete came out of them . That , however , does not mean to say that they did not create a situation favourable to Kim . Instead of being confined to a purely bilateral relationship with the American hegemon , he has managed to rope in two other world powers . Rather than a purely linear relation with Washington , he now has two other poles of interest in Beijing and Moscow , in effect creating a diplomatic square . Now neither the Chinese nor the Russians are particularly enamoured of the North Korean regime and are unlikely to bail it out economically , but they do see in this situation an opportunity to blunt American influence in the Far East . Among other things , they can provide discreet assistance to North Korea in circumventing American economic sanctions . <p> This is all part of a broader pattern of Chinese and Russian challenges @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ sole superpower . China 's Belt and Road initiative is making economic inroads in Asia , Africa and Europe . The Chinese navy is now spreading its wings in the Pacific and Indian oceans . And its building of bases on contested islands in the South China Sea is creating daily headaches in Washington . Russia for its part has managed to expand its control and sphere of influence in Crimea and eastern Ukraine in the face of only a very weak reaction from the United States . It has also been able to challenge the United States ' once totally dominant position in the Middle East through its involvement in the Syrian civil war and through its overtures to Turkey . And , of course , President Putin has been able to exploit divisions with the western family of nations resulting from President Trump 's ill-tempered and ill-advised attacks on major NATO allies . <p> The ground China and Russia are gaining at the expense of the United States is due in good part to their own initiatives , but also to the isolationist foreign policy being pursued by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ its disengagement from world affairs . In his two years in office , Trump has withdrawn the United States from : he Trans-Pacific Partnership ; the Paris accord on climate change ; the Iran nuclear deal involving six other major countries ; the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestinian refugees ( UNRWA ) ; the United Nations Educational , Scientific and Cultural Organization ( UNESCO ) ; the international consensus on the status of Jerusalem ; and military involvement in the Syrian civil war . <p> In one sphere after another , Trump has left the field open to America 's competitors , notably China and Russia . In many ways , America 's influence in the world is now at its lowest ebb since 1945 . And Trump 's bravado displayed in attacks on China and Iran will do nothing to change that reality . <p> The North Korean dictator Kim Jong Un appears to understand the relative weakness of Trump 's position . On the one hand , he is dealing with a vainglorious president , who proclaims himself to be the " master of the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ boasting that he had secured a deal on the denuclearization of the Korean Peninsula , a deal that had escaped all of his preclusions . Given Trump 's personality , Kim knows full well that it will be extremely difficult for him to eat these words and return to a policy of confrontation with North Korea . On the other hand , Kim has managed to secure a modicum of political support from China and Russia , both of which will be very reluctant to see the United States score a diplomatic triumph in East Asia . With all of that in mind , Kim is now playing for time and also upping the ante in his disagreements with Trump . While certainly not of strategic significance , North Korea 's recent test firings of short-range rockets and missiles are intended to put pressure on the United States to modify its stance on economic sanctions against the Kim regime . <p> President Trump has chosen to downplay the importance of North Korea 's rocket and missile launches .
