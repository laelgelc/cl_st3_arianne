<h> Learning retreat to focus on tools , skills for resolving conflicts <p> Establishing a permaculture for your community , looking more deeply at deep adaptation , and taking the principles of nonviolent communication into consideration are the subjects of a special guest speaker at the Kakwa Ecovillage Co-op . <p> Eric Bowers is the leader of the Road To Compassion interpersonal healing program and the author of the book Meet Me In Hard-to-Love Places : The Heart &amp; Science of Relationship Success . <h> Trending Stories <p> Bowers will be the special guest presenter at a weekend learning retreat at the Kakwa Ecovillage Co-op located on the Walker Creek Forest Road ( about 130 kilometres east of Prince George ) . <p> " You will be given the tools to co-create structures and systems for working through conflict with your group members - community , workplace , family , and others , " said Russ Purvis , host of the retreat . <p> " Eric 's workshops are experiential and engaging , with lots of time to practice in small groups .
