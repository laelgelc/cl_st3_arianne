Dennis Patterson penned an editorial just as the hearings kicked off in Prince Rupert denouncing the bill , which was in line with the aggressive tone many of his conservative colleagues expressed publicly about the validity of the Oil Tanker Moratorium Act in favour of the oil and gas sector .
