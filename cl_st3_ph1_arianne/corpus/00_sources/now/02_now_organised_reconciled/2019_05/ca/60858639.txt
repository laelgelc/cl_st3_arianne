<h> Youth want City of Winnipeg to declare a climate emergency <p> Dozens of youth gathered on the steps of the Manitoba Legislative building Friday for a rally to raise awareness about the environment and to ask the City of Winnipeg to declare a climate emergency . <p> Organizers said the move would help make the city consider the environment when creating legislation . <p> Most of those who gathered are under 20 years old . Many held signs showing flames and wore bright colours to highlight the urgent need for action . <p> Several high school students spoke at the event . <p> After the rally the group is making its way to city hall . <p> " This is not something the Mayor is actively considering . The Mayor is focused on doing what we can to address climate change and initiatives that will have tangible results , " said a spokesperson from Mayor Brian Bowman 's office . <h> Photos <p> Most of those who gathered are under 20 years old .
