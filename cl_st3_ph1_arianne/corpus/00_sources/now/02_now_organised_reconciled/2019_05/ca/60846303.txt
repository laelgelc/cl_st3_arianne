The arm , created by Chinese artists Sun Yuan and Peng Yu , rotates , bends to its task , rises and rotates again , as it deploys its big wiper in a futile dance : It is programmed to continually attempt containment of the spreading liquid -- blood or paint , depending on your perspective -- within a certain perimeter on the floor . <p> Can artists be replaced by painting robots ? Its nadir is a room where a near-life-size cow mounted on a small circular track rotates around a patch of fake grass , while close at hand , an ornate metal security gate swings heavily against a wall , knocking off more and more of the plaster .
