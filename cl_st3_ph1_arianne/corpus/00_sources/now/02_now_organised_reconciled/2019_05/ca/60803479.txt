<h> Workforce Training Fund grants awarded to four SouthCoast businesses <p> BOSTON -- The Workforce Training Fund , a Massachusetts public program which provides money to businesses to train their employees on new skills , awarded grants to four different businesses in the SouthCoast in the last month , in a bid to aid the economic development of the region . <p> In New Bedford , Joseph Abboud Manufacturing Co. , a men 's suit manufacturer , received $249,200 to train 158 employees , and anticipates creating 20 jobs by 2021 , and Milhench Supply Co. , a woman and family-owned maintenance supply company , received $39,000 to train 35 employees , and predicts creating four jobs by 2021 . <p> " These grants are very important to the workforce and economic development of the city , " said Rep. Antonio Cabral , D-New Bedford . " They add additional skills for the company , making employers more efficient and productive . " <p> Green Brothers Fabrication , a metal manufacturer in Taunton , will allocate its $62,400 award to train 14 workers and predicts hiring five new employees . The fund is derived from unemployment insurance paid by commonwealth employers . A small portion is redirected to the Workforce Training Fund . <p> The grants range from $10,000 to $250,000 and are administered over a 2-year period . They are matched by the employer at an at-least one-to-one ratio . <p> Kristen Rayne , outreach manager of the Workforce Training Fund Program said that these grants upscale the workforce as a whole . She said the training they receive allows workers to command better wages while making businesses more competitive . <p> The added revenue businesses get as a result allows them to then hire more workers , leading to increased job growth across the state . <p> Last year , businesses who received Workforce Training Fund grants added jobs at a rate of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of 3.8 percent . <p> " Eight new hires does n't seem like a lot when you compare it to the number of jobs a new company coming into a town might create , " said Pacheco . The finance and insurance industry came in second with 11 percent , with the professional , scientific and technical services industry in third with 9 percent . <p> Rayne said these South Coast businesses were given grants because they had a competitive grant application .
