<h> Statement by the Prime Minister of Canada on the results of the Indian general election Fran ? ais <p> OTTAWA , May 23 , 2019 /CNW/ - The Prime Minister , Justin Trudeau , issued the following statement today on the results of India 's general election : <p> " On behalf of the Government of Canada , I congratulate Prime Minister Narendra Modi on his re-election . <p> " Over 39 days of polling , a record number of Indian citizens cast their votes and more women voted than ever before in the world 's largest democracy . <p> " Canada and India share tremendous people-to-people ties , with over one million people of Indian descent calling Canada home . Our longstanding friendship , together with our shared values , will continue to bring our two countries closer and help create new opportunities for our people . <p> " I look forward to continuing to work with Prime Minister Modi to improve the lives of Canadians and Indians alike through education and innovation , investing in trade and investment , and fighting climate change . "
