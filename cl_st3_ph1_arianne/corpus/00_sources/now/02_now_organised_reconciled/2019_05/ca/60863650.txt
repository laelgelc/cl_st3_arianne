<p> Nice weather is here and you 're out and about ? Be on the lookout for eight-legged menaces on the march . Tick populations armed with the Borrelia bacteria are increasingly creeping into Canada , expanding and growing -- and so too the number of tick-borne diseases , predominantly Lyme . <p> It 's a ticking Lyme bomb , and vastly under-detected across Canada . Dr. Vett Lloyd , a researcher and biology professor at Mount Allison University in New Brunswick , says eight out of 10 cases are being missed -- along the coast , the number is higher . <p> The majority of Canadians who have Lyme disease are slipping through the cracks .
