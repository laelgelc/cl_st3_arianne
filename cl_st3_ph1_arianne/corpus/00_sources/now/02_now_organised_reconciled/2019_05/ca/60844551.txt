<p> Scientists believe that a multitude of possibilities will destroy the Earth in the future . Potential catastrophes such as climate change , death of the Sun , and an asteroid collision will bring the apocalypse on our planet . One of the projects involves humans colonizing another planet , such as Mars , which is the most probable suitor for humans . Researchers believe that relocating to Mars is pretty straightforward and that the Red Planet may even live through the death of the Sun . <p> Plans on colonizing the Red Planet are already being worked on by private companies such as SpaceX and space agencies like NASA . Elon Musk , the SpaceX founder even claimed a few months ago that the government on Mars would be founded on direct democracy .
