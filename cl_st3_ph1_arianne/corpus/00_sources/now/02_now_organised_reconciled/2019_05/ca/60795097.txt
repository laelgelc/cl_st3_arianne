<p> A nine-year-old girl from Callander is taking her conservationist message to North Bay City Hall this Friday . <p> Sunset Park Public School student Chloe Cook will hold a strike outside the downtown building to raise awareness of climate change and encourage others to not use as much single-use plastic . <p> Inspired by Greta Thunberg , the teenage girl from Sweden who started the movement Fridays For Future to protest global inaction on climate change , Chloe says she wants to strike because it 's a problem that is " affecting the future . " <p> But this is n't the first time that Chloe , who is also involved in the online kids ' conservation group Earth Rangers , has taken a stand for the environment . <p> " We pick up trash on the road on May cleanup and my mom said when I was younger , I used to make whoever was walking with me , to the mall or something , pick up the garbage on the road , " she said . <p> Her mother , Shawna Roy , also plans to join Chloe on Friday . <p> " I think it @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ themselves and that they do care so much , and I think it 's maybe a little shameful for the parents that it 's the kids who are having to raise this awareness . So I think it 's a good thing . " <p> Fridays For Future began in August 2018 after Thunberg sat in front of the Swedish parliament each school day for three weeks before deciding to strike every Friday until the government 's policies on climate change aligned with the Paris agreement . <p> Students across Canada will hold similar climate strikes at the end of this week , with hundreds of events planned in dozens of countries around the world . <p> Chloe plans to sit peacefully in front of city hall from 12:30 to 3:30 p.m. and Roy says she hopes more people come out to support her daughter 's efforts .
