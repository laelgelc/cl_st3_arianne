<p> There are two simple ( and quite obvious ) reasons why the carbon tax is so offensive to so many . First , 44 per cent of Canadians live paycheque to paycheque , which means that the smallest increases to their recurring expenses could impact them dramatically -- anything from essential food purchases , transportation and housing to the few recreational activities that may be within their means . Contrast that with the negligible impact the increases will have on the affluent . The cost burden is not specific to the dollar value , but , rather , to the impact it has on 44 per cent of Canadian families . And that ( among other things ) is what the PM does not grasp . <p> Secondly , Canada 's share of global carbon emissions amounts to approximately 1.6 per cent .
