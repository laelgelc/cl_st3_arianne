<p> Low-energy streetlights , new trees and a revamped oil change schedule for city vehicles are among the measures deployed by the City of Kelowna to fight climate change . <p> Those initiatives , and others like them , helped the city cut its greenhouse gas production to 6,021 tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent , council heard Monday . <p> " It 's quite an encouraging report , " said Coun. Luke Stack . <p> The GHG emission level was down from 2017 , but that year was an anomaly because of a relatively cold and snowy winter , city staff say . <p> Like all B.C. cities , Kelowna must produce an annual report showing what measures it is taking to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from municipal buildings and vehicles . Cities must do so in order to get a refund of the provincial carbon tax they pay . In Kelowna 's case , the anticipated refund this year is about $250,000 . <p> Main city-owned emitters of greenhouse gases include large facilities that consume a lot of energy , such as the sewage treatment plant , swimming pools , City Hall and the municipal vehicle fleet . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ electric bus fleet in the future , council heard , to Coun.
