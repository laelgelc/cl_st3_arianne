<p> Lead author professor Jonathan Bamber , from the University of Bristol , said : ' Structured expert judgment provides a formal approach for estimating uncertain quantities based on current scientific understanding , and can be useful for estimating quantities that are difficult to model . <p> ' Projections of total global subsequent sea level rise using this method yielded a small but meaningful probability of subsequent sea level rise exceeding two metres by the year 2100 under the high temperature scenario , roughly equivalent to ' business as usual ' , well above the ' likely ' upper limit presented in the Fifth Assessment Report of the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change . " <p> ' Such a rise in global sea level could result in land loss of 1.79 million km2 , including critical regions of food production , and potential displacement of up to 187 million people . <p> ' A subsequent sea level rise of this magnitude would clearly have profound consequences for humanity .
