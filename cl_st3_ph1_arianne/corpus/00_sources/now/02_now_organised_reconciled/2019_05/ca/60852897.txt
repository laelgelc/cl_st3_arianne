<p> In response to an independent U of T report that found that the university 's asbestos management practices meet legislated provincial requirements , and are even " more restrictive in some places , " labour organizations are criticizing the university over its perceived " inaction and inadequate response . " <p> The report and the university administration 's response were made public on March 26 , two years after asbestos-containing dust forced the closure of sections of the Medical Sciences Building . <p> The report is a product of an independent panel whose membership was finalized by U of T in January 2018 .
