<h> Skipping class wrong <p> I 've been very involved with environmental issues over the years . As a candidate for the Green party , and one of approximately 200 Canadians selected for a training program with Al Gore 's The Climate Project group , I 'm obviously very supportive of students ' goal of increasing awareness and pushing for action on climate change . <p> But I am strongly against them doing this during school hours . <p> Public education is a privilege that you have been given , paid for by working Nova Scotian citizens . Take full advantage of it . Go to your classes . Become an environmental scientist . Or lawyer . Or politician . Do everything in your personal life to reduce your GHG production . But go to school ! If you 're serious about protesting , you have approximately 140 hours per week where you 're not in class to do so , plus every holiday , and all summer . <p> Skipping classes does nothing to prove you are serious about environmental issues , and does nothing to help the cause .
