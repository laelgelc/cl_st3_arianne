Since the 1930s , the Liberals had won a string of elections -- 10 of 14 ! -- on the strength of their domination of the large and populous provinces of central Canada : Ontario and Quebec . More than half the Liberal caucus elected in 1980 was from the province of Quebec ; nearly nine in 10 of its MPs were from central Canada ; just two were from west of Ontario . If a cheap oil policy meant pillaging Alberta -- economists calculate the NEP in its brief life transferred as much as $100 billion from the province to central Canada -- that was a chance they were willing to take . <p> The situation is very different today , and not only because the two parties have switched places .
