<p> By Emilie Hackett , Special to the Record -- The City of Magog is the object of a unique research project led by Concordia University 's Dr. Ali Nazemi . It will observe the effects of climate change on the atmosphere , bodies of water such as Lake Memphremagog , and wetlands like the Marais de la Rivi ? re aux Cerises . The study aims to help better understand environmental disturbances due to climate change , in order to improve water , land , and energy resource management , all while developing strategies and efficient tools to reduce the impacts of climate-related natural disasters .
