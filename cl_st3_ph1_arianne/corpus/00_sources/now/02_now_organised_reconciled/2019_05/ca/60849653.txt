<p> At 6:45 a.m. on March 1 , 1954 , the blue sky stretching over the south Pacific Ocean was split open by an enormous red flash . <p> Within seconds , a mushroom cloud towered seven kilometres high over Bikini Atoll in the Marshall Islands . The explosion , the U.S. government 's first weaponized hydrogen bomb , was 1,000 times more powerful than the " Little Boy " atomic bomb blast that flattened Hiroshima -- and a complete miscalculation . <p> Scientists had underestimated the size of what became known as the " Castle Bravo " test , resulting in an explosion that was two-and-a-half times larger than expected . Radioactive ash dropped more than 7,000 square miles from the bomb site , caking the nearby inhabited islands . <p> " Within hours , the atoll was covered with a fine , white , powder-like substance , " the Marshall Islands health minister would later testify , according to the Atomic Heritage Foundation .
