Parliament who recently toured energy-related facilities in the Estevan area came away impressed with what they saw , particularly when it came to the mining operations . <p> Souris-Moose Mountain MP Robert Kitchen brought Conservative Party caucus members Ed Fast , who is the shadow minister for Environment and Climate Change , and John Barlow , the shadow minister for employment , workplace development and labour . <p> In addition to touring the Westmoreland operations , they visited the Boundary Dam Power Station and its carbon capture and storage ( CCS ) facility , the nearby Aquistore site that stores carbon dioxide , and the carbon capture test facility at the Shand Power Station . <p> Finally , they met with the Deep Earth Energy Production Corp . about their work to bring Canada
