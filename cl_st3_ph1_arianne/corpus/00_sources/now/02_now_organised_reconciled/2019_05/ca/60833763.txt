In our Financial System Review , we identify the main vulnerabilities and risks to financial stability in Canada and explain how they have evolved over the past year . <p> Vulnerabilities are pre-existing conditions that can interact with and magnify shocks . Risks are extreme scenarios that threaten the ability of the financial system to perform its core functions were they to occur . These risks are not our base-case forecast , which is presented in our quarterly Monetary Policy Report . <h> Progress on two key vulnerabilities <p> The vulnerabilities associated with high household debt and imbalances in the housing market have declined modestly but remain significant . <p> The combined effect @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ has slowed household borrowing and improved the quality of new mortgage lending .
