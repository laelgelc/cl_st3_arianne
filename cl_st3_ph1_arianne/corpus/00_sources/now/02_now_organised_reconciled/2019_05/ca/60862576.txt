In Vancouver , for instance , city council recently unanimously passed a new climate response plan explicitly intended to push the envelope as to what is possible for a municipality to achieve . The proposals include progressive limits on everything from the use of natural gas in home heating and cooking to the amount of cement used in building new homes .
