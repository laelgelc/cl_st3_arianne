<p> The crowd cheered as speakers said it was time to " stand up and fight back " against fossil fuel interests and insufficient government action . <p> " We are here today under a unified cause to protest climate change , " said Nalan Goosen , co-organizer of the event . <p> Speaking through a megaphone , Goosen said investments in the tarsands and other fossil fuel infrastructure make Canadian banks culpable for climate change . <p> To showcase this , the demonstration traced a serpentine route through the downtown to pause and protest at CIBC , Bank of Montreal , and Scotia Bank . <p> Outside CIBC the crowd chanted , " No more coal , no more oil , keep the carbon in the soil ! " <p> At Bank of Montreal the rallying cry was , " What do we want ? Climate Action ! When do we want it ? Now ! " <p> Scotia Bank received , " Corporate greed we must fight , polluting earth is not a right ! " <p> The crowd also made a stop at the office of MLA Ronna-Rae Leonard , where she and MP Gord Johns @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ impromptu speeches on the importance of protecting the environment . <p> Students put questions to Leonard and Johns about increasing climate education in the school system , protecting old-growth forest and marine areas , and fighting the Trans Mountain pipeline . <p> The answers met with some applause , and Goosen said he was hopeful Leonard would bring the demonstrators ' concerns about old-growth logging to Doug Donaldson , BC 's Minister of Forests .
