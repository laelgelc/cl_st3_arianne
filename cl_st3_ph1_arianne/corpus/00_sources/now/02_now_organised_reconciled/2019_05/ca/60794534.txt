<p> CALGARY , May 1 , 2019 /CNW/ - TransAlta Corporation ( " TransAlta " or the " Company " ) ( TSX:TA ) ( NYSE:TAC ) today announced the closing of the initial tranche of its previously announced $750 million strategic investment by an affiliate of Brookfield Renewable Partners ( " Brookfield " ) . <p> " Having achieved this important milestone , we can now start to realize the benefits of our partnership and financing with Brookfield , " said Dawn Farrell , CEO of TransAlta . " We are moving swiftly to put capital to work including advancing our coal-to-gas conversions . " <p> As previously disclosed , Brookfield will invest $750 million in TransAlta through the purchase of exchangeable securities , which are convertible into an equity ownership interest in TransAlta 's Alberta hydro assets in the future at a value based on a multiple of the hydro assets ' future adjusted EBITDA . In connection with the transaction , TransAlta shareholders recently elected to its Board of Directors two experienced Brookfield executives , Harry Goldgut and Richard Legault , at its 2019 Annual and Special Shareholders ' Meeting . <p> The investment provides the financial flexibility to drive TransAlta 's transition to 100% clean energy by 2025 , recognizes the anticipated future value of TransAlta 's Alberta hydro assets , and also accelerates the Company 's plan to return capital to its shareholders .
