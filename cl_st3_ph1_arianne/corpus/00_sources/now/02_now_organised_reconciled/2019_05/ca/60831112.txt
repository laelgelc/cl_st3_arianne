The other options presented were generally as popular with Conservative voters as with other Canadians . <p> Conservative respondents were most in favour of vehicle emissions standards and investments in clean technology ( 93 per cent ) , cleaner fuel standards ( 89 per cent ) , expanding public transit ( 88 per cent ) , investing in renewables ( 87 per cent ) , making it easier to buy and use @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ coal power ( 81 per cent ) . <p> The poll , which was conducted between April 23 and April 29 with Clean Energy Canada , surveyed 2,515 Canadians aged 18 and older . The margin of error for the overall poll is plus or minus 1.8 percentage points , 19 times out of 20 . The smaller sample size of self-identified Conservatives ( 622 ) would result in a higher margin of error . <p> Alex Boutilier is an Ottawa-based reporter covering national politics .
