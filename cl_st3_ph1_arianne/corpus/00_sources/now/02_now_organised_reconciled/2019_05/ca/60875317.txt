And each of those bills have been aimed at tearing down signature policies from the previous NDP government . <p> Last week , it was the provincial carbon tax , which is expected to be repealed by the end of this week . The tax was a key part of former premier Rachel Notley 's climate plan and was also an integral part of the national climate strategy . Mr. Kenney has long argued it was an unfair tax that punished Albertans for driving their cars and heating their homes .
