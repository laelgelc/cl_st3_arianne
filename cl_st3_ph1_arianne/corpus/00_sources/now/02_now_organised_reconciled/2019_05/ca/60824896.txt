In B.C. , both the 2017 and 2018 wildfire seasons broke records in terms of area burned . <p> A recent climate study by researchers at the University of Victoria and Environment and Climate Change Canada compared temperatures between 1961 and 1970 to current temperatures , starting in 2011 and projected to 2020 .
