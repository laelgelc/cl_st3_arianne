It was compiled by 145 experts from 50 different countries based on a review of 15,000 scientific and government sources . <p> The report blamed the stark rise of at-risk species on human land and sea use , direct exploitation of organisms , climate change , pollution , and invasive alien species . " There 's a lot of evidence for it . " <p> An example from the watershed is the Jefferson Salamander .
