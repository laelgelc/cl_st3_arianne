<h> Letter of the Day : Teen fears for the future <p> I am a 14-year-old Canadian student from British Columbia . I fear that climate change might one day permit my generation from living a full and healthy life . <p> Children who are growing up should not have to live through the polluted world that previous generations have casted upon us . On the news , I see children dying from polluted communities , and I sometimes wonder if one day that might be me . <p> We will all suffer through uninvadable damage to not only the planet , but to our own species if we do not do anything to stop climate change .
