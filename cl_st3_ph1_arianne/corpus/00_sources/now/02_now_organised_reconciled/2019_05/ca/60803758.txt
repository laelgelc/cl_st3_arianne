<p> Roughly 200 young Parti Quebecois activists gathered for a symposium on the future of sovereignty on Saturday . <p> The event , held at the Universite de Montreal and organized by the party 's youth wing , is meant to reflect on how the current reality in the National Assembly will affect the Quebec independence movement . The Parti Quebecois suffered a disastrous loss in the 2018 election , winning just 10 seats -- the same amount as Quebec Solidaire . <p> PQ youth wing president Frederique St-Jean said she believes young people must have a say in the new landscape . <p> " Young people want change and I think they will become leaders in this new dynamic , " she said . <p> Among the big questions being asked is whether young people have the same motivations for seeking an independent Quebec as previous generations . <p> " We will ask them the question to know if the subject is still of interest , " said St-Jean . " I that if young people are coming , it 's still interesting to them . " <p> The party 's youth wing has been at the centre @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ forward in their new political reality . Some long-time PQ members have accused the young activists of wanting to take up too much space , even accusing their young colleagues of ageism . <p> " This will not be at all confrontational , " said St-Jean . " It 's really a reflection symposium . We want young people to regain possession of the independence movement and make it happen again . " <p> Other topics on the agenda include the urgency of climate change and the widening gap between rich and poor .
