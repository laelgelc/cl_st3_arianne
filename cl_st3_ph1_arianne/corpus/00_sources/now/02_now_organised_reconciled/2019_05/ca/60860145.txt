<h> Sudbury research corporation to study climate change adaption <p> A Laurentian University-based research corporation will get almost $700,000 in federal money to help communities adapt to climate change . <p> Sudbury MP Paul Lefebvre , Parliamentary Secretary to Amarjeet Sohi , Canada 's minister of Natural Resources , announced funding of more than $680,000 for the Mining Innovation Rehabilitation and Applied Research Corporation , or MIRARCO . <p> MIRARCO will use the money to pay for two projects that will help Ontario adapt to climate change . <p> " Climate change is happening now , " Vic Pakalnis , president and CEO of MIRARCO Mining Innovation , said in a release . " We are seeing and experiencing the impacts globally , as well as closer to home . <p> " In Canada , we have experienced impacts such as flooding , forest fires and drought conditions .
