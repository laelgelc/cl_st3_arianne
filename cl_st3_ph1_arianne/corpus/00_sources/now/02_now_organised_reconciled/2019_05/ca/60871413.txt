<h> Sunshine Youth Senate aims to show youth ' power of their voice ' <p> Anner Yep and Noah Stong , students from Patrick Fogarty Catholic Secondary School , made a presentation to city councillors Monday about the new Sunshine Youth Senate . Dave Dawson/OrilliaMatters <p> Anner Yep believes youth need to be heard in the corridors of power . <p> It 's why she and Noah Stong and other Patrick Fogarty Catholic Secondary School students " jumped at the opportunity " to help create a new youth senate in Orillia . <p> " I want youth to be more engaged and more aware of the power of their voice , " Yep told city councillors during a presentation to council committee Monday . <p> She said a new youth senate would help youth play an active and critical role in " solving issues in a fair and effective fashion . " <p> She said the new body would " provide a safe space for youth of Orillia to voice their concerns , ask questions and tackle initiatives .
