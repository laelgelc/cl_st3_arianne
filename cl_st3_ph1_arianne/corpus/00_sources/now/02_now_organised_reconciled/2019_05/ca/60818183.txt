<p> A Toronto scientist who has helped tens of thousands of vulnerable pregnant women in Canada has been inspired by the ' strong women ' in her family . <p> Ishita Aggarwal founded Mom 's The Word , a non-profit organization that hosts free workshops for pregnant women who are homeless or on a low income , to fight ill health , illiteracy , maternal poverty and infant morbidity . <p> Doctors and counsellors refer eligible women to MTW and those who complete the four-hour program receive food stamps , milk coupons , bus tokens or prenatal vitamins in return . <p> " My mom really taught me the importance of health education , especially sexual health . She was very open and honest . My dad has always been there too . He grew up in an environment where men were expected to achieve more , but he treated me the same as my brother .
