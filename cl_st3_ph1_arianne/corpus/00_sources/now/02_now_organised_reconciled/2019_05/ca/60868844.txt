<h> Book by award-winning poet Ariel Gordon launched Monday <p> A Winnipeg author is asking readers to take note and take care of the trees around them in a collection of essays launched Monday . <p> In her book Treed : Walking in Canada 's Urban Forest , Ariel Gordon explores her relationship with nature , whether it 's in the northern Rocky Mountains or in Winnipeg 's Assiniboine Forest where the book begins . <p> " I have this idea that the best version of myself lives there , " Gordon said of the Winnipeg forest . <p> " I can take any mood I 've got in there , and I come out feeling better . " <p> Gordon said the book is a call for readers to care for their neighbourhood trees , including Winnipeggers . <p> While urban forests can help mitigate the effects of climate change by absorbing carbon dioxide , preventing soil erosion , trapping groundwater and offering shade , they 're also vulnerable to it , she said . <p> " I think we 're a treed city and we always have been , " she said . <p> " My message @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 're living alongside these amazing old beings .
