<h> Will Windsor 's new hospital heal the city , or do it harm ? <p> A citizens ' group is challenging the a proposed ' megahospital ' outside the city . <p> Brittany Greeson <p> Why would a government suck the life out of a city ? That 's the question some Windsor locals are asking , as the city 's hospital system prepares to move its services and jobs to a greenfield on the city 's rural fringe . <p> The debate is heating up as a citizens ' group challenges the planning behind a proposed " megahospital " just outside this city of 200,000 . The critics have it right : The " megahospital " scheme represents everything that is wrong with land-use planning in Canada . <p> This is about where we put our homes , our workplaces and our institutions . Too often , there is a gap between our society 's ostensible goals -- denser , more walkable , more sustainable communities -- and what we actually build : car-oriented sprawl . <p> Story continues below advertisement <p> The Windsor story is an egregious example .
