<p> Nearly two-thirds of Canadians oppose provincial governments spending taxpayers ' dollars to battle the federal carbon tax , says a new poll released Monday as the Ontario government launched a new television ad slamming the levy . <p> About 64 per cent of respondents said it is unacceptable for provinces to opt out of the federal effort to combat climate change , including the carbon tax , according to a survey done by Nanos Research for The Globe and Mail . As well , 64 per cent of respondents said they oppose provincial governments spending public money to fight the tax . <p> Ontario , Saskatchewan , Manitoba and New Brunswick are pursuing legal challenges to the levy , which the Liberal government imposed in those provinces that do not have a carbon pricing system of their own , as part of Ottawa 's overall effort to meet its international commitment to reduce the greenhouse gas emissions that cause climate change . <p> Story continues below advertisement <p> Alberta Premier Jason Kenney is expected to unveil legislation on May 22 to rescind the provincial carbon tax adopted by the former New Democratic Party government . In a spot to air Monday , an Ontario government ad says the carbon tax will cost the average family $648 a year in 2022 .
