That 's why the Star is offering free digital subscriptions to university and college students from now until Oct. 31 . Coincidentally , the Art Gallery of Ontario will now be free for visitors under age 25 . <p> Get them while they 're young and they 're yours for life , I say hopefully . <p> Given that news websites need cash to prosper and in some cases survive , it 's a generous offer but a necessary one . We ate anything that caught our fancy , flew incessantly , drove non-stop , and bought so ferociously that getting rid of wretched excess became an industry . We lived as if there were no tomorrow , and as it turns out , there may not be for you , or for your children . <p> ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW <p> If I sound overwrought , it 's the guilt talking .
