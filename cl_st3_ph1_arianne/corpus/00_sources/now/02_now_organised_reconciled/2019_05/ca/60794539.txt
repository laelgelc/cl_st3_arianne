<p> When Intact Financial Corp . reports its first-quarter financial results on May 8 , investors will be keen to learn what the Canadian property and casualty insurer says about its exposure to the devastating flooding in Eastern Canada -- the latest in a series of natural disasters that is raising the awareness of climate change as a key stock market risk . <p> So far , Intact 's share price has essentially ignored the flooding , which has affected thousands of homeowners in New Brunswick , Quebec and Ontario .
