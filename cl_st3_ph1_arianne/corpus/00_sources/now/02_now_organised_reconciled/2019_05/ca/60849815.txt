<h> Lake ice changes create biological shift in lakes <p> By Chad Ingram <p> Staff reporter <p> Among the shifts being brought about by climate change are changes to lake ice cover patterns , which ultimately affect the biology of the lakes themselves . <p> Dr. Laura Brown , an assistant professor with the University of Toronto , presented findings on changes in Haliburton County lake ice during the Celebration of Research event at Haliburton Forest on May 10 . <p> The annual event provides an opportunity for those conducting scientific research on the forest property to share their work . <p> As Brown informed the crowd at the outset of her presentation , the research she was presenting is performed by her students . " They do all the field work , all the hard work , and then I just make the graphs and share the data , " she said . <p> Lakes cover 11 per cent of Ontario . <p> " They interact with the climate , and they respond to the climate , " Brown said .
