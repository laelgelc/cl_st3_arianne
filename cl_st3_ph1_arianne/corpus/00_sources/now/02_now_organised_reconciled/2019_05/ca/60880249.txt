<h> Number of Winnipeggers biking to work behind rest of Canada <p> Winnipeggers are falling behind the rest of the country when it comes to riding a bike to their downtown workplace , according to Statistics Canada . <p> A new report shows Winnipeg has the highest number of people living within five kilometres of their workplace in the core of the city , but commuters are less likely to ride a bike to work than people in Canada 's eight largest metropolitan areas . <p> " There 's lots of opportunity but at the same time were not catching up with that opportunity , " Bike Winnipeg executive director Mark Cohoe said . " We 're really lagging behind a lot of the other cities . " <p> Cohoe believes a big reason for the lack of cyclists has to do with city infrastructure . <p> " You 're not getting across the tracks , you 're not getting across the rivers and the fact is that we 've created a circle , a ring around the downtown core and we 're not connecting through it , " he explained . <p> Coun.
