In certain commodities such as motor vehicles , the trade between Canada and the EU is significant . But China and the United States are by far the EU 's largest trading partners . Only about two per cent of the EU 's imports and exports were with Canada last year . <p> The real importance to Canada is that despite the recent rise of euro-skeptic parties across Europe , and nationalist governments in Poland , Italy , Greece and Hungary , the EU is still based on a common acceptance of democracy and human rights .
