<h> ' You see the best in people when tragedy strikes ' : Watson reflects on Ottawa 's long week <p> Mayor Jim Watson says that despite all the pain that he 's seen in the flood zones , that he is also amazed by the strength of the community , and the efforts put forth by thousands of volunteers over the past few weeks . <p> Watson told 1310s Mark Sutcliffe on Saturday morning 's special flood coverage that he has seen plenty of " pain on people 's faces , who are fighting valiantly to save their homes . They 've , in many instances , done this before in 2017 in West Carleton and Britannia and Cumberland . <p> " But at the same time , this amazing sight of 15,384 volunteers that have come out and helped over the course of the last week , since April 19 when we declared a state of emergency , " said Watson . <p> " Not just volunteers who are going out and doing sandbags and things like that -- which are important -- but also people who have donated food , church groups -- Elgin Street busines @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , donated three or four hundred meals last Wednesday or Thursday .
