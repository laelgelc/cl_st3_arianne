Saskatchewan Premier Scott Moe says the province will appeal to the Supreme Court of Canada . ( Michael Bell/Canadian Press ) <p> Saskatchewan 's Court of Appeal has ruled that the carbon tax imposed on the province by the federal government is constitutionally sound and falls within the legislative authority of Parliament . The ruling was released Friday . <p> Premier Scott Moe said the decision will be appealed to the Supreme Court of Canada . The province has about 30 days to appeal , according to Saskatchewan Attorney General Don Morgan . The provincial government would act as intervenor in other court challenges against the carbon tax . <p> " I remain hopeful for a different outcome , " Moe said in the aftermath of the decision 's release . <p> " No one in this nation should confuse climate action with a carbon tax .
