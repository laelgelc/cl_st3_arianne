<h> ' We are working towards the ambitious goal of reaching net zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2040 ' <h> Agriculture is up for the net zero challenge <p> WELSH agriculture is up for the net zero challenge , according to a union president . <p> NFU Cymru president John Davies has reiterated that improvements in productivity , carbon capture and renewable energy production are the most effective ways to realise ambitions of reaching net zero agricultural emissions by 2040 . <p> Responding to recommendations by the UK Committee on Climate Change , Mr Davies said : " Welsh agriculture contributes 12 per cent to Wales ' overall emissions and we absolutely recognise the contribution we have to make in tackling climate change . <p> " That is why we are working towards the ambitious goal of reaching net zero greenhouse gas emissions across the whole of agriculture in Wales and England by 2040 , as a contribution to the Climate Change Committee 's proposed 2050 target . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
