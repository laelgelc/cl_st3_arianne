<h> Airbus and Scandinavian Airlines to explore potential of electric aircraft <p> Airbus and SAS Scandinavian Airlines will work together to research electric and hybrid aircraft . <p> The agreement means the two firms will work on a joint research project to build an understanding of the opportunities and challenges involved with the large-scale introduction of full electric and hybrid aircraft to airlines . <p> The project will initially focus on the impact of ground infrastructure and charging on range , resources , time and availability at airports . <p> The collaboration also includes a plan to involve a renewable energy supplier to ensure ' genuine ' zero CO2 emissions operations are assessed . <p> The Global Aviation Industry ( ATAG ) , which includes Airbus and SAS Scandinavian Airlines , has committed to achieving carbon-neutral growth for the aviation industry as a whole from 2020 onwards , cutting aviation net emissions by 50% by 2050 ( compared to 2005 ) . <p> Grazia Vittadini , CTO of Airbus , said : ' We are delighted to be embarking on this partnership with SAS .
