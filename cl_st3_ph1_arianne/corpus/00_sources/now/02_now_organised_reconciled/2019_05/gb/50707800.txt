<p> I completely back the many young people who took to the streets demanding system change not climate change . <p> They have a clarity of thought that is inspiring and they recognise that this is the most pressing issue of our times . <p> It is easy for adults or politicians to pay lip service to the cause but if we are to be genuine in our attempt to make York carbon neutral by 2030 , each and every one of us will have to take difficult decisions -- including changing our individual behaviours -- over the coming months and years . <p> A climate emergency was declared in York at a council meeting in March and members of the local authority voted to make the city carbon neutral by 2030.
