Together they employ a third of the private sector workforce , and every one of them is driven by a desire to see their company and Britain succeed . <p> But the last few years have been tough , and it 's no secret that firms ' frustration with Westminster has grown . I and our members want this to change and we have a clear message for the next Conservative leader and future Prime Minister . Champion business . Work with us in a new era of partnership to build a post-Brexit Britain that is both prosperous and fair . The UK has so many strengths . It 's time to restore the UK 's reputation as the stable and trusted country to start and grow a business . <p> Second , build a bold and compelling vision for the UK 's future on the world stage .
