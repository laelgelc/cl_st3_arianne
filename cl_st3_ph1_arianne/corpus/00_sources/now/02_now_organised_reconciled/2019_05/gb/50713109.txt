<h> Merkel warns against rising ' specters of the past ' in Germany <p> BERLIN -- Chancellor Angela Merkel warned that " the specters of the past " -- such as anti-Semitism -- were rising again and said Germany had to face up to them . <p> " There is work to be done , " Merkel told CNN in an extract of an interview recorded the day after the European Parliament election , which was released Tuesday morning . The full interview will be available on Tuesday evening . <p> " We have to tell our young people what history has brought over us and others , " the German chancellor added , admitting that this task " has become harder , but it needs to be done . " <p> People needed to be reminded of the need for democracy and the importance of human rights , she said , adding that sometimes one had to put themselves " in the other person 's shoes . " <p> Merkel noted that in Germany , threats such as anti-Semitism " always have to be seen in a certain context , in the context of our past , which @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ others . " <p> She acknowledged that there still were " a certain number of anti-Semites " in Germany today and that nearly 75 years after the Holocaust , Jews still face significant threats in the country . <p> " Unfortunately , there is to this day not a single Synagogue , not a single daycare center for Jewish children , not a single school for Jewish children that does not need to be guarded by German policemen , " she said . <p> Last week , Germany 's anti-Semitism commissioner warned that Jews can not safely wear a kippah , or yarmulke , in all parts of the country . There have also been concerns that awareness of the Holocaust is falling across Europe , with one recent study finding that in Austria , nearly half were unaware that six million Jews had been killed under Nazi rule . <p> In the interview , Merkel also said that her country 's past meant that all German chancellors had an " obligation " to have a close relationship with the U.S. president . <p> " One of the most important @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ War , to give Germany and Europe a chance to actually develop themselves , well ... " <p> Merkel also said that she was aware that " for many girls , apparently , I have become indeed a role model , " adding : " We need more women in these relevant positions and that means men have to change their way of life .
