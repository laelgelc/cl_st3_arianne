<h> TfL consults on segregated Cycleway from Hackney to the Isle of Dogs <p> Transport for London ( TfL ) has launched a public consultation on a new segregated cycle route linking Hackney with the Isle of Dogs . The proposed 7.5km route -- which would feature 5.6km of segregated cycle lanes -- is the first branded " Cycleway " project to be consulted on from TfL 's Strategic Cycling Analysis , which identified the 25 areas with the best potential for increasing cycling numbers . <p> The new Cycleway would link with Quietway 2 , Cycle Superhighway 2 and Cycle Superhighway 3 , as well as the Bow Liveable Neighbourhood scheme . TfL and Tower Hamlets will consult separately on the section relating to the last of those . <p> The route is also proposed to continue onto the Greenwich foot tunnel via Canary Wharf . Again , this section of the route will be consulted on at a later date . <p> London 's Walking and Cycling Commissioner , Will Norman , said : " These exciting plans not only include a new high-quality cycle route connecting the busy streets of Hackney and the Isle of Dogs @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the local area cleaner , safer , and more welcoming to get around on foot . I encourage anyone living or working in the area to look at the plans and give us their views . " <p> Simon Munk , Infrastructure Campaigner at the London Cycling Campaign , said : " Today , the Mayor takes another step to fulfilling his pledge to the LCC to triple the mileage of protected space on main roads . This route will mean far more people will be able to cycle between Hackney , Canary Wharf and beyond via the planned bridge to Rotherhithe , connecting from Walthamstow to Woolwich . <p> " To cut air pollution , tackle climate change and create a healthier city it is vital everyone -- residents , landowners and local stakeholders -- work together to ensure this route is high-quality and goes where people want to , from key employment centres to schools and shops . " <p> Councillor Feryal Demirci , Deputy Mayor of Hackney , said : " In Hackney , more people than any other London borough commute to work by bike , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for everyone . This proposed cycle route linking Hackney to Mile End and Canary Wharf would mean more people can start to cycle for all or part of their journey . I 'd urge people to have their say on the proposals . "
