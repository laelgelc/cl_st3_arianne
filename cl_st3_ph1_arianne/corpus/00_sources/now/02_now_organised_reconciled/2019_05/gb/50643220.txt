" <p> Image credit : PA <p> Scotland could achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2045 , according to a new report from the Committee on Climate Change ( CCC ) . <p> Nicola Sturgeon has stated her intention to follow the CCC 's recommendations , declaring a state of ' climate emergency ' at her party 's conference and telling delegates : " If that advice says we can go further or go faster , we will do so . " <p> The Scottish Government then confirmed amendments have been lodged to the Climate Change Bill to set a legally binding target of net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2045 at the latest with Scotland becoming carbon neutral by 2040 . <p> The Scottish Government 's climate change bill previously targetted a 90 per cent reduction in emissions by 2050 , but the CCC advice calls on it to go further , urging ministers @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for a 70 per cent reduction in emissions by 2030 and a 90 per cent reduction by 2040 , relative to 1990 levels . Scottish emissions were 49 per cent below 1990 in 2016 . <p> The CCC said its recommendations are achievable with known technologies , alongside improvements in people 's lives , and at an affordable overall cost , with new targets covering all Scottish emissions , including from international aviation and shipping . It said the goal should be to deliver them entirely through domestic effort in Scotland . <p> The recommended net-zero date for Scotland is earlier than for the UK overall , where the CCC has recommended a net-zero target for 2050 . <p> Both targets require the same set of actions , but the CCC said Scotland is able to reach net-zero faster because of its potential to plant trees , and through use of carbon capture and storage technology . <p> Releasing its new report , the committee said the updated target would " put Scotland at the forefront of global efforts to meet the ambition of the Paris Agreement , consistent with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ C above pre-industrial levels " . <p> But the CCC warned : " Policies will have to ramp up significantly for a ' net-zero ' emissions target to be credible , given that most sectors of the economy will need to cut their emissions to zero by 2045 . <p> " The Committee 's conclusion that Scotland can achieve a net-zero GHG target by 2045 and at acceptable cost is entirely contingent on the introduction without delay of clear , stable and well-designed policies across the emitting sectors of the economy . <p> Climate Change Secretary Roseanna Cunningham said : " There is a global climate emergency and people across Scotland have been calling , rightly , for more ambition to tackle it and safeguard our planet for future generations . Having received independent , expert advice that even higher targets are now possible , and given the urgency required on this issue , I have acted immediately to set a target for net-zero greenhouse gas emissions for 2045 which will see Scotland become carbon neutral by 2040 . <p> " I have been consistently clear that our targets must @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ an evidence-based approach and balance our climate , economic and social responsibilities . " <p> Mike Robinson , board member of Stop Climate Chaos Scotland , welcomed the Scottish Government amendment to the bill . <p> He said : " Enshrining this commitment in law puts Scotland amongst the leading group of nations in the world in terms of ambition on climate change , but should now be followed by accelerated immediate action across the board . <p> " Rapidly growing public concern about climate change is sending a strong message to all politicians that rhetoric alone is n't enough and has to be matched with swift and decisive action .
