<h> Flowers of Scotland as plant hunters find rare and exotic species <h> Flowers of Scotland as plant hunters find rare and exotic species <p> The arduous trek covered countless miles , uphill , across rivers and deep into the tropical forest . <p> In a clearing , just like plant explorers from hundreds of years before , Dr Mark Hughes and his team set up a tarpaulin to work under , pitched their tents and started a fire to make what every jungle adventurer needs most -- a cup of tea . <p> Around them were lush tropical forests thick with towering trees and plant life , alive with exotic creatures . <p> Ahead lay the prospect of finding something that no-one before them had ever discovered -- rare varieties of the hardy garden favourite , Begonias . <p> For gardeners in the grip of a love-hate relationship with the waxy leaved bedding plants , the team 's discovery of new species of Begonias may not sound particularly thrilling . <p> However , 19 new species of Begonia -- including one sticky variety which , unlike the garden centre begonias found in many Scottish flower beds , has @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ list of new species of plants just confirmed by the Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh . <p> One of the identified plants is Begonia Droiseroides <p> The haul from a series of RBGE expeditions to a wide range of exotic locations around the world includes stunning gingers , varieties of fruits from the exotic mangosteen family and a minuscule moss , as well as the clutch of Begonias . <p> Among the new species are moe than a dozen that are regarded as being under threat from loss of habitat or climate change .
