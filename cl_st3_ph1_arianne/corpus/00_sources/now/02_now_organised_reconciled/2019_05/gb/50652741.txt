<p> " I feel like the world is going to end , " one woman told government television . She had lost eleven of her family members when the debris flow hit Mocoa , Colombia , in the early hours of Saturday , April 1st , 2017 . Torrential rain had fallen on the city and surrounding mountains that night , swelling the Mocoa river and three of its tributaries beyond their capacity . Multiple landslides were triggered in the unstable mountainside soil which piled into the swollen river and picked up rocks , mud , trees , and speed .
