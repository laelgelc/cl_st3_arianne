<h> Royal Court announces year-long season of work for 2019/20 <p> The Royal Court has announced its next year 's worth of work in both its Jerwood Theatre Upstairs and Downstairs spaces , with this latest season spanning from September this year to July 2020 . <p> Artistic director Vicky Featherstone said it 's a " testament to writers " that there is such a strong " range of voices , experiences , stories and provocations " included in the programming . <p> She said : " I am constantly overwhelmed by our hunger and capacity for story -- the human need to make sense of the world in which we live and our openness to be surprised and enlightened by it . This year attempts to reflect that from the intensely private to the global stories of our times . " <p> The season begins in the Upstairs space in September as Tim Crouch 's play Total Immediate Collective Imminent Terrestrial Salvation , produced by the National Theatre of Scotland , is directed by Karl James and Andy Smith .
