<p> Sian Berry , co-leader of the Green Party , said : " Our message of ' yes to Europe , no to climate change ' clearly resonated strongly with voters , many of whom will have been casting their vote for our party for the first time . <p> " We topped the poll in Bristol , Norwich and Brighton and Hove , and scored brilliant seconds in Sheffield , Cambridge and Oxford . " <p> She added : " There is clear evidence from this of strong support for the UK remaining in the European Union , but also for tackling the causes of Brexit -- the massive damage done to so many communities by austerity , tax-dodging and diminution of workers ' rights . " <p> Green parties also fared well in other parts of the EU , including in Germany , where they were on course to take second place , and in Ireland , where the party 's candidate Ciaran Cuffe topped the poll in the Dublin constituency .
