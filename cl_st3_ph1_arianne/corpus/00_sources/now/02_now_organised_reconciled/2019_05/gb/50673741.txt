<p> The conservation charity is renewing its call for visitors to puffin colonies in spring and summer 2019 and 2020 to submit pictures showing what the threatened seabird is catching for its young . <p> Along with current photos , the scientists are also keen to receive snaps of puffins with fish in their bills from any time in the past , provided the year and the place of the image is known . <p> The " clowns of the sea " are suffering declines , and are now globally listed as vulnerable to extinction , with concerns they may be facing a reduction in available food in the UK as a result of climate change . <p> Even pre-digital photos can be submitted for the scheme , which will help track how puffin food sources have changed over time . <p> Birdwatchers are being urged to submit their photos of puffins to the RSPB .
