<p> Hustings for the upcoming European Elections were held in Great St Mary 's Church on Thursday . <p> Representatives from seven of the major parties - Labour , the Liberal Democrats , the Conservatives , UKIP , the Green Party , Change U.K. and the Brexit Party - and one independent candidate discussed topics including Brexit , the environment and immigration . <p> The European Elections will take place on Thursday 23rd May . Each major party has put forward seven candidates , who will be selected in order according to the proportion of the popular vote their party receives . <p> Cambridge is part of the Eastern Region , which elects seven MEPs . Three of the seven seats are currently held by the Conservatives , with the other four split between Labour , UKIP , the Brexit Party and the Social Democrats . Stuart Agnew , the current UKIP MEP , is re-running for his seat . <p> The first issue discussed on Thursday was the possibility of a second referendum .
