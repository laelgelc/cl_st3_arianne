<p> Eight European countries have called for an ambitious strategy to tackle climate change -- and to spend a quarter of the entire EU budget on fighting it . <p> The joint statement says the EU should have net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2050 " at the latest " . <p> It was signed by France , Belgium , Denmark , Luxemburg , Netherlands , Portugal , Spain , and Sweden . <p> The group says their plan can " go hand in hand with prosperity " and " set an example for other countries to follow . " <p> The position paper comes ahead of a major summit of European leaders in the Romanian city of Sibiu , beginning on Thursday , which will discuss the future of Europe and the EU 's strategy for the next five years . <p> But not everyone is on board - there are 28 countries in the EU , and several of those absent from the joint position statement are significant players - including Germany . <h> What does the paper say ?
