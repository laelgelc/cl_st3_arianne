<h> PM call with President Joko Widodo of Indonesia : 4 May 2019 <p> The Prime Minister spoke to the President of Indonesia , Joko Widodo , today to congratulate him on the success of Indonesia 's first simultaneous presidential , legislative and regional elections . The President welcomed the UK 's support for Indonesia 's democratic process . <p> This year marks 70 years of UK-Indonesian bilateral relations , and the Prime Minister said she looked forward to celebrations in London and Jakarta . <p> Both the President and Prime Minister agreed on the importance of the relationship between the UK and Indonesia and undertook to further strengthen bilateral ties , particularly on trade . <p> They pledged to work together to tackle global challenges such as climate change and the Rohingya crisis . <p> The Prime Minister concluded by wishing President Widodo a happy and peaceful Ramadan . <h> Help us improve GOV.UK <h> Help us improve GOV.UK <p> To help us improve GOV.UK , we 'd like to know more about your visit today .
