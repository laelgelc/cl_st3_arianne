This is actually what the Stern Review is about , how should we account for things in that far future ? Our entire climate change problem is that the benefits come now , the costs in 50 to 80 years . If we use normal market discount rates then those damages are spit now -- this perhaps being not quite the way we should think about boiling Flipper in the fumes of that last remaining arctic ice floe . <p> Or perhaps we should , it 's a moral point and thus one that can and is argued either way . But do note what Stern 's result is -- that we should still be using a discount rate , it 's just not the market one . Because not to use a discount rate when considering future costs is just too stupid for words .
