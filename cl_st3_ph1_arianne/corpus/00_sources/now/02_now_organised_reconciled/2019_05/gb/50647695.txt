<h> Why this crab spotted in Dorset is an ' indicator of climate change ' <h> Crab spotted in Dorset could be a sign of climate change <p> UNUSUAL marine life findings on Dorset beaches is an ' indicator of climate change ' , according to experts . <p> Dorset Wildlife Trust ( DWT ) says low tides surfaced some unusual findings at Charmouth and Kimmeridge where the ' furrowed crab ' ( Xantho hydrophilus ) was found . This crab is very common further in the south west in Devon and Cornwall but has been very rarely recorded in Dorset . The charity said it may be a warning that climate change is changing the abundance of creatures on the seashore .
