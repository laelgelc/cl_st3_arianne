<p> The ironies keep coming thick and fast for Labour 's leadership and national executive committee , both determined to sidestep the pro-Remain sentiments of the vast majority of its membership , MEPs and MPs . Chief among them , perhaps , was the insistence this week by shadow minister Rebecca Long-Bailey that Labour was engaged in " fantastic discussions " with the government on the issue of workers ' rights . Barely had that sentence been uttered , when reports emerged that the Conservatives may be planning again to reconsider domestic commitment to the European Convention on Human Rights once the process of Brexit is concluded . <p> For many , Brexit has been explicitly a deregulation project from the outset . Astute observers might have noticed that the political declaration which was agreed with the EU in December , alongside the Withdrawal Agreement , noted that the UK would only agree " to respect the framework of the European Convention on Human Rights , " rather than the previous wording by which the UK was " committed " to it .
