Fellows will be encouraged to be adventurous in tackling tough and important research questions and opportunities for innovation . <p> " The Fellowships offer opportunities to move across disciplinary boundaries and between academia and industry . " <p> Dr Perriman , who will conduct pioneering research into modifying stem cells to allow them to ' home ' and repair the damaged heart tissue that follows a heart attack , said : " I am extremely honoured to be a recipient of the Future Leaders Fellowship . It 's @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the University and the South West . " <p> Low blood flow to the heart muscle cells after a heart attack results in cell death and scar tissue formation . Unfortunately , the human body is not very efficient at repairing the damage , and there are few treatments that are focused on regenerating the heart tissue . <p> Stem cell therapies are emerging as exciting new treatments for patients suffering from heart disease . Here , stem cells can be taken and expanded from the patient or donor and injected into the patient 's heart . To date , the results have been promising , however , there are still significant challenges associated with the distribution of the cells in the body after delivery . For example , there is little engraftment of the cells in the damaged areas of the heart . This is partly a result of the high blood flow in the heart and the various " tissue sinks " that circulating cells come in contact with . Following injection , the majority of the cells end up in the lungs and the spleen . <p> @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ focus on addressing this cell delivery problem , by modifying the membrane of adult stem cells using artificial membrane binding proteins , which will allow the cells to home to damaged heart tissue . These designer constructs are mimics of proteins that are displayed on the surface of common oral bacteria , which allow the bacteria to home to damaged heart tissue when in the blood stream .
