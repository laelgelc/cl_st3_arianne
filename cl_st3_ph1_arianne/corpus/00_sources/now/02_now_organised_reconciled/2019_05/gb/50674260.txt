<h> Date set for launch of Green City Charter in Southampton <h> Date set for launch of Green City Charter in Southampton <p> SOUTHAMPTON 's pollution-battling Green City Charter is to be launched next month after it was ' called-in ' in March . <p> The Green City Charter will be launched on June 20 , it can be revealed . <p> Details of the launch are yet to be revealed but it will coincide with National Clean Air Day . <p> The news comes after the original launch of the Green City Charter was cancelled after the Overview and Scrutiny Management Committee ( OSMC ) called the document back-in to be debated further . <p> At the time , Conservative member Peter Baillie , chairman of the OSMC , said he wanted the group to have another look at the document because he claimed " assurances " by Labour civic chiefs of " cross-party working groups " never came about . <p> The cabinet was asked to delay the approval and launch of the Green City Charter to enable the fostering of a cross party consensus on the strategic objectives within the Charter . <p> But cabinet members @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ now go-ahead . <p> The charter aims to tackle all types of pollution in the city , going further than the compulsory nitrogen dioxide-tackling Clean Air Zone . <p> Cllr Steve Leggett , cabinet member for green city , said : " It is only by using collective action that we will improve air quality and tackle climate change . By launching the Green City Charter on National Clean Air Day on June20 , we will bring together individuals , organisations and businesses to confirm our commitment to improving air quality and wellbeing in the city . " <p> He said the Green City Action Plan will be written by a working group until the end of the year . Becoming carbon neutral by 2030 is among the commitments . <p> This website and associated newspapers adhere to the Independent Press Standards Organisation 's Editors ' Code of Practice .
