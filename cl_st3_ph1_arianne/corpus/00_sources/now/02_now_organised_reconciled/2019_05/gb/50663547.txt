<h> National Grid confirms first week of coal-free electricity since 1882 <p> For the first time in more than 130 years the UK has gone for seven consecutive days without using coal to generate electricity . <p> According to the National Grid Electricity System Operator ( ESO ) the milestone was reached at 13.24 on Wednesday 8th May ( the last coal generator came off the system exactly a week earlier at 13:24hrs on Wednesday 1st May ) <p> The news is a step towards UK targets to be zero-carbon in electricity generation by 2025 . <p> This marks the first time since the launch of the UK 's first public coal power station in 1882 ( see archive box-out below ) that coal has been absent from the energy mix for an entire week . <p> Coal dominated UK electricity production for much of the 20th century .
