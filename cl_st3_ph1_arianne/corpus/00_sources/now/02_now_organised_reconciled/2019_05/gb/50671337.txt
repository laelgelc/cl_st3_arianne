<h> U.S. Renews Warning Against China in Arctic , Releases New Details on Icebreaker <p> Just days after Secretary of State Pompeo warned about Russia 's and China 's activity in the Arctic during the Arctic Council meeting , U.S. military officials renewed comments about security threats in the region . The U.S. Coast Guard aims to respond to those challenges with a fleet of new icebreakers . <p> U.S. officials continue a pattern of strong rhetoric aimed against Russian and Chinese ambitions in the Arctic . In new testimony in front of a subcommittee of the House Armed Services Committee , military leaders explained that the security environment in the region has changed . " The Arctic is the first line of defense , " explained Air Force General Terrence O'Shaughnessy , head of the U.S. Northern Command and North American Aerospace Defense Command . <p> The region is being transformed from a peaceful sanctuary to a new theater of engagement . " The Arctic is becoming the first line of defense for North America and our potential adversaries want to bring the fight to us in North America , " O'Shaughnessy warned . The region is no longer a sanctuary , but is becoming an area for power and economic competition , the general continued . It will make use of a Finnish propulsion design and use diesel-electric propulsion producing 45,200 horsepowers to move the 37,000 ton vessel . <p> In comparison , Russia 's latest and largest class of nuclear icebreakers weighs in at 33,450 tons and is powered by 81,000 horsepower . It will be designated as a Polar Class II icebreaker , one category below Russia 's most powerful icebreakers .
