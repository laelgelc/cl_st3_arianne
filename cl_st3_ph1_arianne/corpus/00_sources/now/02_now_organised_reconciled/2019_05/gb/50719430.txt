<h> Beijing is the EV capital of the world <p> The city of Beijing is the driving force behind China leading the global electric vehicle ( EV ) market , new research has found . <p> This is according to The Tyndall Centre for Climate Change Research , which has projected the expansion of Beijing 's electric vehicle market as the city targets 400,000 EV sales next year . <p> The centre says that the growth of the EV market could significantly lower China 's emissions from automobiles , which in 2017 reached 436 million tonnes and contributed between an eighth to half of the pollutants in cities like Beijing . <p> ' Introducing electric vehicles appears to benefit the environment , though the benefit is marginal compared to the total amount of vehicular emissions , ' said Chengxiang Zhuge , researcher at the Tyndall Centre . <p> ' However , with the widespread adoption of EVs , the environmental benefits could become significant , ' he added . <p> 17,000 BEVs were sold in Beijing in 2018 , raising the number of battery electric vehicles in the capital to 188,000 . <p> According to Zhuge 's research , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ one weekday if battery electric vehicles ( BEVs ) reach 40,000 sales in the city by 2020 . <p> Beijing had 130,000 charging points in 2018 with 93,000 of them privately installed for home use , while private charging demand is expected to reach 4% of total domestic energy consumption in 2020 . <p> The centre 's research suggests that Beijing 's expanding EV market could lead to the rapid addition of further facilities to support EVs such as parking lots , refuelling stations and charging points . <p> Beijing currently plans to reach its target of 400,000 BEVs by 2020 by increasing EVs in taxis , public transport and logistics , while continuing to promote the electrification of buses and vehicles for its postal services .
