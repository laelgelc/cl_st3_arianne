<h> There will be several important objectives at June Bioblitz event <h> Well into the sixth major extinction of life on this planet <p> THE recent Extinction Rebellion in London has drawn attention to what many scientists have thought for some years , that we are well into the sixth major extinction of life on this planet -- like when the dinosaurs died out . <p> All kinds of wildlife are dying out now at a similar rate . <p> And the scary thing is this time it 's not a meteorite strike or other natural disaster- it is US who have caused it ! <p> Intensive farming has been one of the driving forces that has brought this about . <p> Other effects like climate change are of course very important too . But , interestingly , the current change in economics and lifestyle meaning that more people are choosing to eat less meat , fewer people wear wool and even governments are promoting alternative energy and closing coal mines , is actually beginning to have a positive effect . <p> Locally , the economics of sheep farming is meaning less intensive grazing on some places like Halkyn @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , rare birds and butterflies are popping up all over the place . <p> This is very encouraging , so we have started a local nature conservation group and are holding a Bioblitz on June 30 , jointly with the North Wales Wildlife Trust 's Clwydian branch . <p> A Bioblitz is an event where a number of specialist naturalists record all of the wildlife species in a particular area . <p> There are several important objectives . <p> The first is to establish a baseline of records , which can be used to compare changes in the environments and habitats over future years -- and possibly to compare with historical records . <p> The second is to discover whether there are any unusual or rare species or habitats needing special conservation measures . <p> Our Bioblitz on June 30 will be based at Halkyn Parish Hall where we will have stands displaying local wildlife and fossils . <p> You can drop in any time between 10am and 4pm and there will be activities for kids and refreshments , wildflower plants , photographs and books for sale . <p> You @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ current management plan for the area and see reptiles , butterflies , moths and plants collected locally in the days beforehand . <p> There will be a number of guided walks throughout the day to go and see some of these in the wild . <p> So do come along - whatever the weather there will be new things to see and find out about !
