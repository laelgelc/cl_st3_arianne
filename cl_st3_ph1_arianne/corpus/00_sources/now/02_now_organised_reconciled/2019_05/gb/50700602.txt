<p> The announcement comes ahead of a further wave of strikes by children across the country organised by the Student Climate Network . <p> The group have called for the educational reforms and for the government to " communicate the severity " of climate change to the public . <p> Earlier this month , MPs approved a Labour motion to declare a environment and climate emergency in a bid to pressure ministers to introduce fresh legislation on it . <p> Labour leader Jeremy Corbyn said direct action protests by climate activists alongside the student walk-outs had been a " massive and necessary wake-up " call for Parliament . <p> Speaking ahead of the visit , Ms Rayner said : " Today , young people are taking to the streets to send a clear message to the government that climate change will be a fundamental and defining feature of their adult lives , and we must take the action needed to tackle it . <p> " We need to equip people with the knowledge to understand the enormous changes we face , and skills to work with the new green technologies that we must develop to deal with them . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ and that prepares pupils for adult life . Climate change should be a core part of the school curriculum , and under a Labour government it will be . " <p> The party says its new proposals will be guided by an expert led panel , who will examine how children can be best prepared to work within new green industries . <p> Ms Rayner added : " As well as teaching young people about the impact of climate change , their education must prepare them for the jobs of the future . <p> " As part of Labour 's Green Industrial Revolution to crease 400,000 skilled jobs across the country , young people will be taught the skills they need . "
