<p> It is the wild celebration that heralds the start of summer in Edinburgh -- with a heady mix of fire , drums , costumed performers and dramatic storytelling . <p> A 300-strong cast brought ancient Celtic traditions to life as the annual Beltane Fire Festival took over Calton Hill for the night . <h> Beltane 2019 .
