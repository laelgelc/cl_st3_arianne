<h> Why agri-sector needs more funding <h> Saturday May 4 2019 <p> Dar es Salaam . Even as opportunities are plentiful , Tanzania 's agriculture sector still lags behind due to lack of progressive policies , coordination and synergy of institutions as well as wrong mindset about the sector . <p> Agriculture Non-State Actors Forum ( Ansaf ) executive director Audax Rukonge said this in an interview with Mwananchi Thought Leadership Forum ( MTLF ) where he underscored the fact that Tanzania is endowed with a huge arable land on which surplus produce to feed the country and its neighbours can be produced . <p> He , however , noted that the government is yet to adequate budgetary investment to improve the sector . <p> Ansaf is a member-led forum for non-state actors to discuss and work towards solutions to improve the agriculture sector in the interests of men and women currently living in poverty , it also actively promotes accountability , transparency and citizen engagement ( inclusiveness ) within the agriculture sector . <p> Explaining , Mr Rukonge said that for the past one and half decade the country had been producing enough food for the country and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 66 per cent of the population where a majority of them 65 per cent are women .
