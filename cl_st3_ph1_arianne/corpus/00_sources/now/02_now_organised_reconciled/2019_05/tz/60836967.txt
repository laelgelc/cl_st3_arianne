<h> EDITORIAL : Increase of Credit in farming is good news <h> Friday May 17 2019 <p> Reports that lending to agriculture has somehow improved are encouraging . The Bank of Tanzania latest economic report shows that in the year ending March credit to agricultural activities grew by 45.8 per cent compared to 34.1 per cent in the year ending February . <p> Worthy to note is the fact that credit growth rate in the agriculture sector was the highest of all sectors . <p> This is quite rare because agriculture has often been considered a risky undertaking for many financial institutions . <p> But the fact that they are now putting the sector into much more consideration points to the fact that more credit could be in the offing if the right environment is created to boost agricultural productivity and markets for the produce . <p> This could be done by firstly facilitating investments in the manufacturing of irrigation infrastructures in the country , through giving various incentives . Water is the most important farming input of all . <p> But Tanzanian agriculture must be weaned off rainfall . Leaving a business undertaking at the mercy of weather @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Worst still , as effects of climate change become more and more severe letting rainy seasons determine farming is a bad idea . <p> More and more agricultural land should be put into irrigation as possible . <p> It is good to note that the government has , in the recent past , done a commendable job of allowing farmers to look for markets of their grains outside the country . This policy shift , after years of hesitations , is crucial in making agriculture a profitable endeavours . Memories of tonnes and tonnes of maize rotting in warehouses in the southern highlands regions are still fresh in people 's minds . <p> We , however , advise the government to facilitate the creation of reliable market systems , which should include establishing the commodity exchanges . Value addition and market regulation are also important to ensure farmers get a fair price for their produce .
