<h> Bank committed to farming support <h> Monday May 6 2019 <h> In Summary <p> TADB and WFP have discussed current areas of collaboration and identified potential new areas of partnership to grow the sector <p> By The Citizen Reporter @TheCitizenTZ news@tz.nationmedia.com <p> Dar es Salaam . The Tanzania Agricultural Development Bank ( TADB ) and the World Food Programme ( WFP ) said at the weekend that they will work together to foster agricultural development in the country.In a joint statement , they said yesterday that the two organisations discussed current areas of collaboration and identified potential new areas of partnership to grow the sector that employs almost 80 per cent of Tanzanians.They are already working to promote innovation in agricultural production systems , the statement said.Through their joint Farm to Market Alliance ( FTMA ) project , TADB has provided loans of over Sh750 million to eight groups of maize farmers , covering a total of 867 farmers and about 2,789 acres in Ruvuma and Rukwa regions . " The plan is to explore ways to scale up the FTMA model and have more smallholders under this arrangement , " reads the statement .
