<h> How Climate Change affects pastoralists in Tanzania <p> Global climate change is without doubt , one of the most challenges that have affected a number of pastoralists in Tanzania than any other time in history . <p> Pastoralists move randomly with their cattle in different villages in Tunduru district Ruvuma region as they look for new green pastures and water . Photo by Gerald Kitabu <p> Normally , the pastoralists earn their income and survive through Livestock keeping . However , the sector is ranked as one of the vital economic sectors in Tanzania that has been severely affected by the climate change . <p> As a result of Climate change , it has affected the livestock production , feeding and survival . In recent years , some regions such as Dodoma , Shinyanga and Singida has increased heat and at sometime claimed lives of some cattle due to their feeding behaviour and potential for heat stress . <p> According to the secretary of the Tanzania Livestock Keepers Association Makoye Maghembe , one of the challenges facing Livestock keepers today is the swift mushrooming of invasive alien species particularly the plants and grasses that are not eaten by @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ species around the Lake zone , Central zone and in the Northern zone . <p> They have been continuously causing massive amounts of economic damage to specific economic sectors such as livestock and agriculture . In some areas in Dodoma and Morogoro regions for example , they are wiping out crops and contaminate produce , in livestock sector . They also tend to devastate pastures and completely engulf and eliminate native and suitable pastures for the cattle . <p> Citing an example of Ngorongoro area in Northern Tanzania , Makoye Maghembe explained that many districts and regions are currently attracting alien plants and grasses that completely discourage the pastoralists as they reduce the size of native pastures . <p> Simanjiro and Loliondo are other areas in the Northern zone where native pastures are increasingly shrinking as a result of overwhelmingly evidence of climate change . Also in the list are around the Lake zone area such as Bunda district in Mara region and in the arid and semi-arid rangelands such as Kongwa district , Singida and Tabora areas . <p> As a result of the alien species coupled with variability @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ droughts , floods and wind storms have pushed out some pastoralists from their residence .
