<h> Plastics ban : Dar calls for use of alternative bags <p> THE Dar es Salaam city council has called upon its residents including businessmen to collaborate with the government by adopting the use of alternative bags to replace plastics . <p> Dar es Salaam Mayor , Isaya Mwita gave the city 's stance on the matter yesterday when he addressed journalists on how they prepared to implement the government 's order to ban plastics come June , 1 . <p> He said the ban can only be successful if Dar es Salaam residents can cooperate to ensure that the country can overcome climate change effects . <p> " The ban is on a goodwill base , what is needed is the civic education to our people and I have already given directives on how we can inform our people so that they are reached with information , " he said . <p> The Mayor said the sanitation issue was a mandate for residents of the city and that each and every one was part and parcel ; of the ensuring that the ban was successful . <p> According to the Mayor , the city council has issued statements @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to enforce a total ban on the use of plastic bags by June , 2019 , as the government moves to protect the environment from choking mounds of the deadly plastic waste . <p> Manufacturers of plastic bags were expected to use the two months window to unwind their businesses estimated to run into billions of shillings . <p> The ban , seen as long coming , will mean no plastic bags will be manufactured or traded in the country from June . Tanzania joins other countries such as Kenya , Rwanda and Zanzibar to enforce a total ban on the use of plastic bags . <p> Environmentalists have campaigned for many years for authorities to ban the use of plastic but business interest groups have often prevailed due to fear of job and tax losses .
