<h> Hopes For The G-7 Summit <p> Climate change , the Amazon fires , Iran and trade- these are the topics that are most likely to headline the agenda of the G-7 summit taking place in Biarritz , France , from the 25th of August . Apart from the G-7 countries , which pride themselves as seven of the wealthiest and most advanced nations in the world , this year 's summit will include representatives of the European Union , China , India , Mexico , and Brazil , as well as leaders of important international organisations like the IMF , World Bank and United Nations . <p> The summit is bound to be a divisive one- with global leaders currently being at odds on several of the issues likely to be discussed . The bitterness between US President Donald Trump and European leaders from last year 's summit is still alive .
