<h> Alternatives of banned plastic shopping bags <p> The federal government has banned the use of polythene bags in the Islamabad Capital Territory ( ICT ) since August 14 , 2019 . The ban on plastic and polythene shopping bags in the Federal Capital has yet to be fully implemented because shopkeepers and customers are having difficulty finding alternative bags . Even with the ban , in various markets plastic bags are still in use including in government controlled Sunday markets . <p> According to the Deputy Commissioner Islamabad Hamza Shafqaat , the district administration and the Ministry of Climate Change have been working to fully implement the ban . <p> The government is also running a soft campaign , encouraging vendors and consumers to avoid using plastic bags as these are detrimental to the environment . <p> Simultaneously , the Ministry of Environment has also conducted raids in different areas of Islamabad and seized over 2.5 million polythene bags . <p> Last Tuesday , the district administration sealed the famous food outlet Savour Foods at Blue Area for not complying the ban orders . The administration also imposed an Rs 200,000 fine on Savour Foods .
