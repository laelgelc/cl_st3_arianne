<h> July 2019 was the Hottest Month Ever Recorded on Earth <p> The US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ( NOAA ) said that July 2019 temperature was the hottest ever recorded globally . This makes July 2019 the hottest month ever witnessed . Also , the satellite data reveals that the polar ice shrank to its lowest levels . <p> The average global temperature for the month of July was 0.95 degrees Celsius or 1.71 degrees Fahrenheit according to NOAA . This is above the 20th century average of 15.8 degrees Celsius or 60.4 Fahrenheit . This makes it the hottest July month since 1880 . <p> NOAA said , " Much of the planet sweltered in unprecedented heat in July , as temperatures soared to new heights in the hottest month ever recorded . The record warmth also shrank the Arctic and Antarctic sea ice to historic lows . " <p> These findings have also confirmed the data that was released by the Copernicus Climate Change Service of EU on 5th August . <p> All over the world , the heatwave resulted in an increase in temperature . " <p> Since it began keeping records in 2005 , Alaska recorded its hottest July in 2019 . Same is the case in Africa . <p> UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said , " We have always lived through hot summers . But this is not the summer of our youth .
