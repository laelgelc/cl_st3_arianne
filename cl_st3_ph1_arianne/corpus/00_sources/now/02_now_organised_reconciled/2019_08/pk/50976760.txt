<h> Ban on plastic shopping bags will soon be imposed in Punjab as well : Zartaj Gul <p> ISLAMABAD , Pakistan : The Minister of State for Climate Change Zartaj Gul on Wednesday said the ban on use of plastic shopping bags would soon be imposed in Punjab as well . <p> While addressing a news conference in Islamabad , the state minister expressed the commitment to fully enforce the ban imposed on use of plastic shopping bags in the Federal Capital . <p> The state minister said Pakistan would be made plastic free as the ban on plastic bags in the Federal Capital turned into a countrywide campaign . She said other provinces were also showing the intent and commitment to make their districts plastic free . <p> Zartaj Gul said the ban would be implemented with the cooperation of the people including the civil society and the food chains . <p> The minister of state regretted the incident that took place yesterday with a food chain of the Federal Capital . <p> It is worth mentioning there that on Tuesday , as the team of Pakistan Environmental Protection Agency ( Pak-EPA ) led by its Director General @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Foods in Blue Area Islamabad to inspect the ban on plastic bags , its staff manhandled the inspection team and utterly refused to comply with ban orders . <p> Zartaj Gul said the Islamabad administration and the officials of the Ministry Of Climate Change were not acting on personal capacity but they had been fully authorized to ensure implementation of the ban on plastic bags .
