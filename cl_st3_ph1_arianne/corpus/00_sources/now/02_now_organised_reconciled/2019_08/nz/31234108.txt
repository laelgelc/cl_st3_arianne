<p> Press Release -- Wellington Chamber Of Commerce The Wellington Chamber of Commerce welcomes the economic impact report on extending the airport runway which shows great benefits for the city , the region and for New Zealand . <p> Chief Executive John Milford says the report backs up what the Chamber and many others have said for some time -- that there are great economic benefits from the initiative . <p> " The numbers in this report speak for themselves : for every $1 spent on the extension project there will be a net benefit of $7 . That is a very good cost-benefit ratio . <p> " The net benefit to New Zealand is huge : $2.1 billion brought about in large part by the 125,000 new visitors a year that bigger aircraft will bring in from more destinations that will be in reach of the airport . <p> " You can see how that will happen .
