<h> Fed Farmers ' dangerous climate strategy <p> Federated Farmers is playing a very dangerous game on climate change , says Rod Oram <p> The UK 's National Farmers Union pledged in January that UK agriculture would achieve net zero emissions of all its gases by 2040 . It beat the UK Government by five months , and went a big step further . In June the Conservative-led Government committed the country to net zero but by 2050 . This was still a significant tightening of the UK 's decade-old commitment of an 80 percent reduction . <p> Meanwhile here in New Zealand Federated Farmers is leading the passive aggressive resistance by many in the farming sector to tackling the climate crisis . Fed Farmers says its members fully support New Zealand 's efforts to meet our international climate commitments .
