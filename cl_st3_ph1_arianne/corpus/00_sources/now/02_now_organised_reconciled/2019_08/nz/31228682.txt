<h> French for Rabbits <p> Tour Information <p> Following on from the international release of their sophomore album ' The Weight of Melted Snow ' on Muscle Beach Records , an extensive USA tour and appearances at SXSW this year , Wellington dream-pop band French for Rabbits have announced a four-date New Zealand tour this August . <p> The tour will see the band trial a fresh batch of new songs ahead of recording for their third full length record . The songs cover a wide range of subjects -- climate change , feminism , memory loss , art and money , insomnia and the preservation of memories in objects . <p> The band will also release a beautifully designed limited edition cassette of The Weight of Melted Snow .
