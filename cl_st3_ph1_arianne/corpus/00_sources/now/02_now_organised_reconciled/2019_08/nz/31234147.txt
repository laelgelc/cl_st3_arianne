<h> High ranking Greens member pulls pin before election <p> A high ranking member of the Green Party is stepping down before next year 's election , citing the party 's drift to the centre as one of the reasons . <p> Jack McDonald , left , is unhappy with how Greens co-leader James Shaw is moving the party into the centre of the political spectrum .
