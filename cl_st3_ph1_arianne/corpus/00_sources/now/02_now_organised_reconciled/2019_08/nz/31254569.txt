<p> The Royal Forest &amp; Bird Protection Society told the parliamentary environment select committee significant changes to land use are essential to reach proposed emission reduction targets . <p> Forest &amp; Bird argued the targets contained in the Climate Change Response Amendment Bill are " unambitious " and said the overall emissions neutrality target should cover all gases and the date should be brought forward to 2040 to drive earlier reductions . <p> Regardless , " if we imagine we are going to hit any of these targets without significant land-use change , we are kidding ourselves , " chief executive Kevin Hague said . <p> Hague said the committee needs to " clearly reject " an approach that dairy farming can largely continue as it has and underscored " we can not afford to go easy on methane . " <p> New Zealand 's agriculture sector accounts for around half of New Zealand 's greenhouse gas emissions .
