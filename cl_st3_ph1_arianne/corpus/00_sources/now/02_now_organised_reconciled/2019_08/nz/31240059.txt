<p> It has now been 10 days since the surprise 50 basis points Official Cash Rate cut by the Reserve Bank of New Zealand . <p> This has allowed plenty of time for banks to react to the implications of a 1% official policy rate . <p> Markets have reacted by driving down wholesale swap rates . Markets have also driven down benchmark risk-free Government bond rates . <p> Locally these have shifted because of both the RBNZ signals , and the global risk aversion that is gripping markets . <p> Now we can assess how much has been passed through to the economy .
