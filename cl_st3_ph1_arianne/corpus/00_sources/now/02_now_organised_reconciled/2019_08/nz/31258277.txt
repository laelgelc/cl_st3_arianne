It would be full of sheep jokes , 15-year-old Lord of the Rings references and maybe some awkward rugby jargon dropped in here and there . You know that scene in Airplane , where people keep telling a woman having a nervous breakdown to get a hold of herself , lining up with spanners , baseball bats , and even a gun to do the job ?
