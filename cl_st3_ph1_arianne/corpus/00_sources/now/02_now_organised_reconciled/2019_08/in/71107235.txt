<h> Scientists confirm massive retreat of Pindari glacier <h> Last Updated : Wednesday 16 May 2018 <p> The Pindari glacier has retreated 51 metres each year since 1976 . Credit : Laura7581/Flickr <p> The snout of the Pindari glacier , in the upper Himalayas of Kumaon region and the source to Pindar River , a major tributary of Alaknanda river , has retreated over four decades , according to latest scientific research that adds to the data officially confirming the Indian Himalayan glacier loss . <p> The findings of the joint research by scientists from Kumaun University , Nainital ; Uttarakhand Space Application Centre , Dehradun ; and Indian Institute of Science Education And Research Kolkata , may trigger more vigorous scientific studies on quantifying glacier retreat in Pindari as well as other glaciers of the Himalayas , the report in the journal Remote Sensing and Environment says . <p> The scientists assessed the retreat of the Pindari glacier from 1976 to 2014 , using visual interpretation of remote sensing data . The annual glacier retreat rate was faster during 1976-94 than from 1999 -- 2014 . <p> " High priority research fund(s) should be raised to conduct similar assessments in other mountain ranges situated across the entire Himalaya , " the report concludes . <p> Gajendra Singh Rawat , scientist from the Uttarakhand Space Application Centre , Dehra Dun and one of the study authors , says that while his team has not analysed the glacier mass balance , they have used " actual ground truth points for some years and we also used the old people 's observation and remote sensing data . For example , in 2017 a team of scientists from Indian Space Research Organisation and geologists from Andhra University , Visakhapatnam , led by ISRO scientist Babu Govind Raj K , reported in Geomatics , Natural Hazards and Risk that " Himalayan glaciers are showing reliable evidences of climate change . " <p> These scientists had mapped the glacier status in Bhilangna basin of Garhwal Himalayas , also in the state of Uttarakhand , using Corona and Cartosat satellite images acquired in 1965 and 2014 . Bhilangna river is the southernmost tributary of the Bhagirathi river and joins it near New Tehri . <p> They reported that the main Khatling trunk glacier that receded by 4,340 metres and had fragmented into multiple valley glaciers , increasing the number of glaciers in the basin from 20 to 33 . This means " a long-term retreat rate of almost 30 metres each year over a period of 104 years , " said Argha Benerjee , assistant professor at the division of earth and climate science at IISER , Pune . <p> Banerjee , however , has some reservations about the new 2018 study on Pindari glacier . Their shrinkage can be utlised to read off the temperature change if one knows the scale or calibration of this thermometer , " he says . <p> " Unfortunately , such calibration formulas are known to us only approximately .
