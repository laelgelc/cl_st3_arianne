The Saiva faith was still practised at the time . The Islamic Sufis , who came to Kashmir , challenged the beliefs and practices of the Brahmins . Lalded made the Saivite faith accessible to the people . The outcome was the birth and growth of Rishism , which proved that spirituality was the same in both Islam and Brahminism . Even Nund Rishi paid a rich tribute to her when she was born . <p> She held discussions with the Iranian Sufi Mir Syed Ali Hamdani who came to Kashmir to spread Islam , and with her guru Siddha Srikanth .
