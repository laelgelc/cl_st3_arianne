<p> Bannerghatta National Park might soon get notified as an eco-sensitive zone ( ESZ ) with the Centre agreeing in principle on it . The draft notification for the park came last year . But the mention of the impending notification in the recent Lok Sabha sessions might hasten up the process . <p> Once the final notification arrives , a zonal master plan will be prepared in consultation with local people within a period of two years for effective management of the eco-sensitive zone . The zonal plan will integrate environmental and ecological considerations , conserve the existing water bodies , and other such aspects of the ecology and environment that need attention . It will also help in demarcating all the existing places of worship , villages and urban settlements , types and kinds of forests , etc . <p> The zonal master plan will also regulate development and activities in the eco-sensitive zone and also ensure and promote eco-friendly development for livelihood security of local communities .
