However , as against 7,000 MT e-waste that was being safely tackled till two years back , last year data shows the authorised recycling and dismantling units having handled almost 14,000 MT . <p> Though the e-waste generated continues to grow by leaps and bounds and quantities to be safely despatched still remain high , the efforts at scientific disposal have slowly started to move at bridging the gap . " During 2018-19 , almost 14,000 MT of e-waste was disposed of scientifically at two recycling and 10 dismantling @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ years , " said a senior PCB official . <p> However , the official did concede that a lot more of the e-waste was still going unattended and added that a series of efforts aimed at creating awareness were on . <p> E-waste includes discarded computer monitors , motherboards , mobile phones and chargers , CDs , headphones , television sets , air conditioners , washing machines and refrigerators among a host of other devices . PCB officials point out at lack of awareness as one of the main reasons behind e-waste piling up . To create awareness on pollution free disposal , TSPCB has been conducting various awareness programmes and melas on e-waste . <p> " There is need for the users also to exercise caution while discarding electronic gadgets and such other products .
