Coal mines which are above 150 hectares are Category A projects , and appraised at the Central level by the Expert Appraisal Committee ( EAC ) of the MoEFCC and those below 150 hectares are Category B projects , and appraised at the State level by the State Expert Appraisal Committee ( SEAC ) . <p> Based on the recommendations of the EAC or SEAC , a conditional approval letter is issued @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . The validity of these approvals is 30 years . <p> However , project proponents do not treat this as a one-time approval . More often than not , mine operators seek multiple approvals for expansion . This is to either to draw more coal from the same mine lease area or to bring additional land area under a mining project . <p> The requirement of having a public hearing is of paramount importance in case of expansions since it shows how seriously the environmental safeguards have been adhered to . Dust pollution will increase as the extraction of more coal would require more excavation , blasting and transportation , thereby increasing the overall pollution load on the area . It went on to recommend that local stakeholders should be included at every stage of mining operations and not just at the commencement of the same . <p> Seven years since , the requirement for these consultations has only been further reduced . The relaxations which have been put in place for coal mines , act as a double edged blow to the whole appraisal process since it removes the requirement to carry out an EIA study and does not record any objections which the local communities might be facing as a result of mining operations . <p> A public hearing allows the affected people to have prior information @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ .
