<h> Interactive games for climate change lessons <p> Members of Sweden Alumni Network and Samuchit Enviro Tech met at a restaurant to understand the causes of global warming and extreme weather conditions . <p> With the effects of climate change visible in the form of recent floods and excess heat during the summer , citizens have taken up an innovative way to introspect on the urban lifestyle which is harming the environment . Residents are using games and other interactive activities to promulgate the need to reduce the effects of climate change . <p> In a workshop held in a restaurant at Koregaon Park on Saturday , the members of Sweden Alumni Network and Samuchit Enviro Tech , an NGO working towards reducing carbon footprint in the city , joined hands to understand the causes of climate change at a personal level . <p> The workshop intended to bring out the behavioural patterns on using resources and selfreflecting on how one 's lifestyle is affecting the community at large . <p> Ankita Shroff from the Sweden Alumni Network said the idea is to try different methods to help people know about the judicious use of resources . <p> " @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ to represent a diverse set of community . Half the number of coins are added if it 's tails and double the amount if the toss results in heads , " said Pournima Agarkar , a researcher at the NGO . Agarkar added that each person has to at least pick up one token to survive the game . <p> The adding of tokens represents the replenishment of renewable resources .
