<h> Maharashtra did not comply with compensatory afforestation rules : CAG <h> Last Updated : Friday 26 July 2019 <p> The Comptroller and Auditor General of India ( CAG ) has , in a recent audit report , flagged non-compliance of Compensatory Afforestation Fund Management and Planning Authority ( CAMPA ) rules by Maharashtra . <p> The state diverted 65,363 hectares of forest land for 1,671 projects as on March 2018 . But , " the information of non-forest land ( NFL ) or degraded forest land provided for compensatory afforestation was neither compiled by additional principal chief conservatory of forests ( APCCF ) nor was the same available with chief conservator of forests , " observed the report , tabled in the state legislature . <p> No information was shared between the APCCF-cum-nodal officer , responsible for processing forest land diversion cases , and APCCF ( CAMPA ) , responsible for taking up afforestation on alternate lands identified during approval , the report added . <p> The CAG also found incomplete monthly progress returns ( MPR ) report with no mention of alterative land allotted , the status of compensatory afforestation and expenditure . <p> The audit team @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ land measuring 2,818.68 hectares was diverted during 2013-18 . <p> The CAG also found inconsistency in reporting in three test-checked circles .
