Human-induced CO2 emissions need to reduce by 45% by 2030 and reach net zero by 2050 ( for a 2-degree target , the numbers would be 20% by 2030 and net zero by 2075 ) . Without decisive action , the world is likely to reach 1.5 degrees between 2030 and 2052 , and warming will only accelerate from there . <p> Limiting global warming to 1.5 degrees depends on large-scale transitions in energy , land , urban , infrastructure , and industrial systems , with deep emissions reductions and significant investment in a range of mitigation options .
