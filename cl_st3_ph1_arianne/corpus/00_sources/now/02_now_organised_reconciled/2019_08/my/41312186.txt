<p> We , the leaders who represent the inshore fishermen and traditional fishermen of Penang , would like to present our case before Tuan Yang Terutama . <p> We are the inshore fisherman who make our living around Penang ( island ) and the coast of Seberang Perai . Penang has had fishing villages , from the time it was under the sovereignty of the Kedah kingdom , before being ruled by the British . From our ancestors , we have inherited the knowledge of local fishermen , including sustainable and environmentally friendly ways to fish and harvest the fruit of the sea . <p> Penang food has always been well known , one of the reasons being the quality and freshness of the fisherman 's catch . Penang is blessed with long stretches of beautiful beaches , lush mangroves and rich marine life . Sand is dumped into our oceans to reclaim new land for others . <p> Within our lifetime , fishermen have been negatively impacted by a series of reclamation projects and bridge construction works .
