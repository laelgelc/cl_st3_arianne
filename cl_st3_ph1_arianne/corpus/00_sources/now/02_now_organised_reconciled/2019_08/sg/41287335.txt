The only period in which the world 's climate has changed , everywhere and at the same time , is right now . <p> The present sustained , ubiquitous warming is unique in that it can be coupled directly with the Industrial Revolution , the clearing of the forests , population growth and profligate use of fossil fuels . <p> The finding is part of a sustained examination of global climate history , based not just on written and pictorial records but also studies of ancient lake sediments , ice cores , tree rings and other proxy evidence assembled by an international partnership called the Past Global Changes Consortium @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ like this is a tidying-up operation .
