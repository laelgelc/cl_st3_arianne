<h> DPM Heng Swee Keat says feedback to National Day Rally shows the people are aware of need to tackle threat of climate change <p> The impact of climate change and the need to tackle it early is one message from Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong 's recent National Day Rally that resonated with most Singaporeans , especially the young , according to Government feedback unit Reach . <p> Deputy Prime Minister Heng Swee Keat is heartened by the response , saying Singaporeans see the importance of tackling even challenges that are not imminent and of preparing early . <p> Mr Heng told reporters yesterday : " I 'm very encouraged by how Singaporeans are continuing to think long term , to look at how we prepare ourselves better , and at the same time to work together . " <p> He was commenting on Reach 's findings that nine in 10 Singaporeans below the age of 30 felt the climate change threat to the country is real , and that Singapore should invest in taking mitigating actions before it is too late . <p> For those aged between 30 and 54 , eight in 10 felt likewise . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ with 200 people , Mr Heng was asked how the Government plans to forge consensus among older Singaporeans on the topic of climate change . <h> Related Stories <p> Mr Heng , who is also the Finance Minister , said that while interest in the issue is strongest among the young , seniors citizens also feel encouraged the Government is taking steps early to tackle it . <p> " Many of our seniors have gone through the early years of Singapore , when our approach of thinking and building for the long term have enabled Singapore to be where it is today . <p> " So this is a very good set of values in our society that we must continue to promote , " he added . <p>
