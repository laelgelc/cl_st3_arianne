PHOTO : SEMBCORP <p> Jun 24 , 2018 Singapore <p> -- With this deal , Sembcorp is now one of the largest solar players in Singapore with over 100 megawatts of capacity . <p> As the winning bidder for the project , Sembcorp will build , own , operate and maintain grid-tied rooftop solar systems with a total capacity of 50 megawatts across 848 HDB blocks under the West Coast and Choa Chu Kang Town Councils and 27 other government sites in Singapore . Sembcorp Solar 's winning bid was put together with one of its appointed engineering , procurement and construction ( EPC ) players , Kurihara Kogyo Co. , Ltd . <p> This project is part of the SolarNova programme , a whole-of-government effort spearheaded by HDB and EDB to accelerate the deployment of solar photovoltaic systems in Singapore and drive the growth of Singapore 's solar industry .
