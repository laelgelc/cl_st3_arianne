As chairman of the Foreign Affairs Committee , Engel is considered one of the most important pro-Israel members of Congress . A fixture at AIPAC conferences , Engel has received major campaign contributions from pro-Israel donors and PACs over the years and introduced numerous resolutions condemning attacks against Israel .
