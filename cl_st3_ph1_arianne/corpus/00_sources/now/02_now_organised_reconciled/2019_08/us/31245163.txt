On one occasion , Trump suggested dropping a nuclear bomb on a hurricane , a long-circulating crackpot theory . On another occasion , Trump mentioned bombs , just not nuclear ones . <p> Trump denied the story via tweet , but an aide defended Trump 's questions to Axios .
