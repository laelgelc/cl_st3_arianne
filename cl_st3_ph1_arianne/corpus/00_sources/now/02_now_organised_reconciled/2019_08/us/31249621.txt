<p> Presidential elections are decided by many things : media exposure , financial backing , personal chemistry , timing and luck . Policy positions often are just a way of signaling where a candidate stands on the political spectrum . On the Democratic side , a robust debate about inequality has given rise to ambitious proposals to redress the imbalance in Americans ' economic situations .
