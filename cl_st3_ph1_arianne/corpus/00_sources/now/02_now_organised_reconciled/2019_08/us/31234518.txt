<h> The migration patterns of the Bracken Cave bats are changing , adapting to the rapid rate of warming , but experts say these adaptations wo n't be enough to counter the effects of climate change . <p> Bracken Cave , a collapsed sinkhole on the side of a lush limestone hill in southern Texas , is home not only to the world 's largest known bat colony but also to one of the largest concentrations of any mammal . More than fifteen million Mexican free-tailed bats live , for part of the year , deep in its recesses . One evening in late July , I visited the cave , situated just outside San Antonio , with a group of bat-curious tourists . As the sun went down , we watched hundreds of bats emerge every second , fifty yards from where we sat , swirling up in what our guide , Niki Lake , a volunteer from Bat Conservation International , called " a batnado .
