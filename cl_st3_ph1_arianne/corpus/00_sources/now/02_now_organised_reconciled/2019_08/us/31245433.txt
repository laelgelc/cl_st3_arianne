<h> The great failure of the climate models <p> Computer models of the climate are at the heart of calls to ban the cheap , reliable energy that powers our thriving economy and promotes healthier , longer lives . For decades , these models have projected dramatic warming from small , fossil-fueled increases in atmospheric concentrations of carbon dioxide , with catastrophic consequences . <p> Yet , the real-world data are n't cooperating . They show only slight warming , mostly at night and in winter . According to the United Nations ' Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change , there has been no systematic increase in the frequency of extreme weather events , and the ongoing rise in sea level that began with the end of the ice age continues with no great increase in magnitude . The constancy of land-based records is obvious in data from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration . <p> Should we trust these computer models of doom ?
