<h> The Paris Climate Accords Are Looking More and More Like Fantasy <p> Remember Paris ? It was not even two years ago that the celebrated climate accords were signed -- defining two degrees of global warming as a must-meet target and rallying all the world 's nations to meet it -- and the returns are already dispiritingly grim . <p> This week , the International Energy Agency announced that carbon emissions grew 1.7 percent in 2017 , after an ambiguous couple of years optimists hoped represented a leveling off , or peak ; instead , we 're climbing again . Even before the new spike , not a single major industrial nation was on track to fulfill the commitments it made in the Paris treaty . To keep the planet under two degrees of warming -- a level that was , not all that long ago , defined as the threshold of climate catastrophe -- all signatory nations have to match or better those commitments .
