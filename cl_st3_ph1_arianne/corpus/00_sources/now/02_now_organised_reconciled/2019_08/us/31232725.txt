And this year , it was ESPN of all places that managed to pull off a genuinely good April Fools ' Day prank , mostly because the butt of the joke was ESPN 's resident idiot Will Cain . <p> The premise of the joke was that Cain , the inexplicably smug Tucker Carlson wannabe who has said climate change fears are " intellectually dishonest " and was ready to defend Trump 's Muslim ban on ESPN , was going to fill in for Bomani Jones on Jones 's show with Pablo Torre , High Noon .
