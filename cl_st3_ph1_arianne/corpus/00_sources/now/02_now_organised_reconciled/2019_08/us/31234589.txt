Courtesy of Sayaka Kajita Ganz <p> Much of the art in the James ' Museum of Western and Wildlife Art is centered around nature , so it 's apt that the museum would host the " Environmental Impact II " exhibition , opening Saturday . The exhibit is the second installment of the project curated and produced by David Wagner , LLC . It features contemporary paintings , sculptures and photographs that address climate change , the BP Deepwater Horizon Gulf oil spill and the loss of bee populations , among other environmental concerns . It features more than 20 artists , including Kent Ullberg , Guy Harvey and Karen Hackenberg . A special installation of a hanging blue whale will make a statement . Sayaka Kajita Ganz 's 16-foot sculpture , Nanami , is made entirely of recycled plastic objects , aluminum armature , wire and cable ties . The title is Japanese for " seven seas .
