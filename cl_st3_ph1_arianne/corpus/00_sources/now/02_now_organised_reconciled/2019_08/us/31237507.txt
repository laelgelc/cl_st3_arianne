The issue offers Democrats a chance to paint President Trump as out of touch with the electorate , since he has undone several Obama-era policies designed to help combat greenhouse gases . <p> Named after FDR 's " New Deal , " the Green New Deal is a non-binding resolution introduced in Congress by Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Sen. Ed Markey . It seeks a 10-year mobilization to transition U.S. energy systems to clean , renewable sources . It calls for creating high-paying jobs in the clean economy , offering health care and affordable housing to all Americans , upgrading every building in the country to make them more efficient and promoting economic justice for communities that have suffered from pollution . And it seeks to overhaul transportation systems to eliminate as many emissions as possible , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ . <p> Have candidates called for setting a price on emissions of CO2 , either through a tax or fee ? <p> Establishing a tax on carbon emissions or creating a " cap-and-trade " or " cap-and-dividend " plan is viewed by economists as a crucial part of reducing greenhouse gas pollution . Various measures have been proposed in the past , some of which are backed by companies that burn fossil fuels . <p> Does the Green New Deal prohibit use of fossil fuels or nuclear power ? <p> The resolution itself does not specify how the U.S. should eliminate emissions causing climate change other than to promote clean energy . That would potentially leave the door open for allowing the burning of oil , natural gas and coal that is coupled with carbon capture and sequestration plants , as well as other nascent technologies such as sucking CO2 out of the air -- which many experts see as a long shot .
