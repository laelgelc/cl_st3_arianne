<p> Portland Public Schools is moving ahead with plans to pursue an offsite solar power project to provide 3,500 kilowatts of electricity to the school district . <p> The Board of Education unanimously approved a resolution Tuesday that directs the superintendent to work as quickly as possible to enter into a power purchase agreement with an offsite solar project in which the district would retain and retire the renewable energy credits associated with solar generation . <p> The resolution , which is the result of a student-led effort to fight climate change by having the district lessen its dependence on fossil fuels , also directs Superintendent Xavier Botana to consider partnering with the city of Portland and neighboring communities on the project to secure a larger scale of operation . <p> Botana , at Tuesday 's board meeting , said the project would cover 60 to 80 percent of the district 's energy needs and could potentially save around $50,000 per year . <p> SolaRISE , a coalition of students from Casco Bay , Deering and Portland high schools , led the push to incorporate solar energy in Portland schools .
