Predicting when they come is almost as easy as predicting the tide . Predicting how long these stories appear in the media is similarly easy . We 'll muster the standard amount of outrage , which he 'll then respond with another racist tweet , which we 'll then muster the standard amount of outrage . Rinse and repeat . <p> It is news that the president is saying such offensive , divisive and outrageous things . But we need to more consciously dissect the action and reaction to avoid falling prey to easy pitfalls . <p> Mustering the standard amount of outrage usually entails a clear defense of the object of Trump 's derision @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ what I 'm supposed to do here is write a personal story about how the city is n't what Trump says it is , and note how offended I am . Or if I 'm not defending Baltimore , I 'm supposed to at least say that Cummings is n't responsible for its troubles . <p> Given this , the question , it seems to me , is a different one : What are the conditions under which manufactured social media outrage can actually help defeat the force we 're fighting ? <p> While the research on outrage is mixed , the one thing we know is that it tends to generate the types of solidarity we need in order to effectively contest injustice . Outrage , in other words , can be a good thing . <p> But how good depends on how strong our institutions are . What do I mean here ? <p> Outrage , like other emotions , has the capacity to activate individuals , to make people likelier to give money , time and other resources to organizations interested in combating the president , to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ organizations on the liberal-left end of the spectrum , for example , tend to see their membership roles and donations increase when Trump 's ugliness rears its head . <p> If the institutional apparatus that comprises the resistance is strong enough , something like this can not only increase the amount of money organizations like ActBlue , RAICES or The Democracy Collaborative , receive , but can also increase the number of long-term actively engaged citizens . <p> If , on the other hand , the institutional apparatus is weak , then we 'll likely see some increase , but this increase will end up being temporary . <p> I happened to be in Albany for a meeting of Indivisible , a coalition of progressive grassroots groups , held in New York .
