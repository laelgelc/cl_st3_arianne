<p> New Delhi : With an aim to increase the green cover in Uttarakhand , a state with 45.43 per cent of forest cover , and bring down the levels of air pollution through tree plantation , the Post Office Department of the state organised a tree plantation cum awareness drive titled ' Walk For : To Save Earth ' on Sunday ( July 21 ) . The 10 km long walk was flagged off and joined by Chief Minister Trivendra Singh Rawat . Sharing the details of the event with NDTV , Colonel Sukhdev Raj , Chief Post Master General , Uttarakhand , says , <p> At the moment , our planet is witnessing environmental degradation , climate change and global warming and one of the major reasons behind this is deforestation . To overcome this we organised a plantation drive in the state . <p> 350 officials of the department along with the Chief Minister participated in the walk and together the group planted 3,000 native trees at 2,700 post offices in the state . From Tulsi , Aloe vera to flower-bearing plants , officials can have any kind of plant at their house .
