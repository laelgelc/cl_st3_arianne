As you gather more speed , you face a life-extinguishing event at the bottom unless the brakes start working again . This is an apt analogy for the greatest challenge to human existence on our small planet . As the monumental and varied environmental effects of climate change become undeniably apparent , our brakes are beginning to fail . <p> Climate change is among the topics 2020 Democratic presidential hopefuls will address during future presidential debates . It should be .
