Pierre Casiraghi , the son of Princess Caroline of Monaco , offered to skipper the Malizia II solar-powered racing yacht . Along with Casiraghi , Boris Hermann German round-the-world sailor aided Thunberg 's ground-breaking journey . <p> On August 14 , the Malizia II set sail from Plymouth , Britain . The journey took longer than expected due to rough waters in the Atlantic . On August 28 , the yacht docked at the North Cove Marina , south Manhattan .
