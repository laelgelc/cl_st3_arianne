<h> About Starting Screen <p> It 's hard to believe , but we 're nearing the halfway mark of 2019 . Normally at this time of year we 'd be taking stock of a busy spring and looking ahead to a stacked fall . But 2019 has thus far been a bit of a weird one for games . <p> This year 's release schedule has thus far been a steady drone of mid-level games coming and going , with few of this year 's big releases sticking in any meaningful way .
