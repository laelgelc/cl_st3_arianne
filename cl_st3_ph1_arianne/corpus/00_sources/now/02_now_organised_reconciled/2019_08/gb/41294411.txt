<p> Brazilian troops backed by military aircraft have begun operations to help battle a record number of fires in the Amazon , which have prompted anti-government protests as well as an international outcry . <p> Some 44,000 troops will be available for " unprecedented " operations to put out the fires , and forces are heading to four Brazilian states that asked for federal help to contain the blazes , defence minister Fernando Azevedo said . <p> The states are Roraima , Rondonia , Tocantins and Para . <p> The military 's first mission will be the deployment of 700 troops to the area around Porto Velho , capital of Rondonia , Mr Azevedo said . <p> He added that the military will use two C-130 Hercules aircraft capable of dumping up to 12,000 litres of water on fires . <p> Smoke from a
