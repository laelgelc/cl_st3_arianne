<h> Carbon offsetting is n't a cure-all for your filthy flying habit <p> Although the exact figures vary depending on who you ask , one thing is for sure : flying makes up a large portion of people 's carbon footprint . A long-haul return flight to New York from London can produce anywhere between 0.9 and 2.8 tonnes of CO2 . But a new industry is offering flyers a way out of their environmental guilt : carbon offsetting . <p> Travellers can pay a small fee into a voluntary scheme that funds environmental projects around the world in order to " offset " their own carbon footprint . Since carbon offsetting first took off , demand has grown by over 140-fold -- from just 0.3 million tonnes of CO2 in 2008 to 42.8 million tonnes in 2018 , according to Forest Trends , a Washington DC , -based nonprofit organisation that tracks the voluntary carbon market . The marketplace encompasses not only the aviation industry and includes all transactions of carbon offsets that are purchased with the intent to be resold to meet " carbon neutrality " or " net zero " claims .
