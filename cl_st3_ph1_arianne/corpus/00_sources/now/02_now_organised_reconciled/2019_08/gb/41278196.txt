<h> Teenagers trek four miles on hottest day of the year to raise money for social action projects <h> Teenagers from National Citizen Service take part in sponsored walk <p> A GROUP of teenagers completed a sponsored walk from Boscombe Pier to Hengistbury Head on the hottest day of the year . <p> The young people trekked the four-mile walk as part of their National Citizen Service and raised money for social action projects they are undertaking as part of their course this summer . <p> NCS is a government scheme created for all 15 to 17 year olds , with courses taking place during the autumn and summer holidays , offering meaningful activities out of term time . <p> Programme Leader Anna Garrett said : " They are a really lovely group of teenagers whose community projects include helping care homes , working with local cancer charities and awareness campaigns around climate change and the refugee crisis .
