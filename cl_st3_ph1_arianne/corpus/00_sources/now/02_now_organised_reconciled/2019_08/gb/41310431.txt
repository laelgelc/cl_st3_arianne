Their unusual shade of electropop incorporates a range of genres from R&amp;B to Classical - the duo describe their sound as ' moodpop ' . They got together in 2017 , after meeting in the corridor of BIMM music school in Manchester . <p> Before Manchester , Anna moved from her home in Darlington to Paris , where she worked at a magazine house .
