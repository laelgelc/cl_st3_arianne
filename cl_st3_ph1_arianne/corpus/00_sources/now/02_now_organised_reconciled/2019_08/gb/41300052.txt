<h> Sean Jeffrey in focus ahead of Malvern gig <h> Focus on local music scene <p> Sean Jeffery is a man of variety . From impressing a crowd with well known and perhaps unexpected covers , to writing his own impeccably written songs , he is a recognisable presence in the Worcester <p> music scene . Gigging regularly in several different venues , Sean offers something everyone will enjoy -- good tunes , skilful musical talent and a repertoire wide enough to be wrapped around the city . <p> 1 .
