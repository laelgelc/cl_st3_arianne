Sending freight by train cuts CO2 emissions by up to 76 per cent compared to road haulage , even where road collection and delivery legs are required at either end of the rail trunk haul . Switching freight from road to rail can offer a ' quick win ' , as it involves doing the same for less carbon , rather than having to doing things completely differently ( as is often the climate change ? prescription in other sectors ) . <p> A Cameron Bridge ? railhead would be a key building block for the whisky industry to ? diversify its transport options within Scotland .
