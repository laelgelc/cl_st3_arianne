<p> MUNICH -- There 's a new breed of environmental activists -- and their cause is onshore wind power . <p> No , not promoting it , but trying to shut it down . <p> The Bavarian Association for Landscape Conservation and Species Protection ( VLAB ) is fighting new wind towers . Spokesman Johannes Bradtka argued that wind power is an " archaic and ill-fated attempt to combat climate change , " adding that the group is currently pursuing 37 complaints against such projects . <p> Rather than wind , the campaigners want a greater focus on solar panels , biogas and geothermal energy , and are open to keeping more efficient coal power plants running to ensure security of power supply if necessary . Forest owners should also get more credit for pulling carbon dioxide out of the atmosphere , they say . <p> The activities of VLAB and similar groups spell trouble for Germany 's ambitious effort to shift to renewable energy .
