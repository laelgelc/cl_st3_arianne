<p> A French couple could face up to six years in prison after being caught with 40kg of sand they had attempted to take home as a souvenir from a Sardinian beach . <p> The pair were found with 14 plastic bottles of the sand taken from the beach in Chia , southern Sardinia , in their car as they tried to board a ferry going from Porto Torres to Toulon , France . <p> A 2017 law forbids people from taking Sardinia 's famed white sand away from its beaches , with culprits facing a fine of ? 3,330 ( ? 2,750 ) or potential jail time . Authorities are attempting to clamp down on tourists behind the theft , BBC News reported . The couple claimed they did not realise they had committed an offence . <p> " Sandy beaches are one of the main attractions of Sardinia . There are two threats : one is due to erosion , which is partly natural and partly induced by the increasing sea level due to climate change ; the second is sand stealing by tourists , " Pierluigi Cocco , an environmental scientist told the broadcaster .
