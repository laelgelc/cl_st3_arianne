<p> Shoes will line the street in Newcastle city centre to highlight the number of deaths caused by poor air quality . <p> The visual display of 200 pairs of shoes at Grey 's Monument is part of a joint protest by climate change campaigners Extinction Rebellion Newcastle and Doctors for Extinction Rebellion . <p> The demonstration , set to take place on Saturday morning , aims to raise awareness about the role of air pollution in a number of deaths in the city each year . <p> The protest is being co-ordinated by Newcastle GP Dr Jonathan Coates , who said : " If people found out that 200 people were being poisoned every year by tap water , they would be outraged , with good reason . We feel the same outrage about the scandal of Newcastle 's air pollution problem .
