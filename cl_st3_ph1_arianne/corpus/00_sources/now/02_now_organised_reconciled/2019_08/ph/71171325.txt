<h> Land Bank : Not by CAMELS alone anymore <p> This is to welcome the good news that Land Bank of the Philippines has responded boldly to the challenge of President Rodrigo Duterte during his last State of the Nation Address to intensify its support to agriculture , particularly to small fishers and fisherfolk . <p> As detailed in its full-page advertisement in the Manila Bulletin this week on the occasion of Land Bank 's 56th anniversary , the bank 's board led by its chair , Finance Secretary Carlos G. Dominguez III and its new president , Cecilia C. Borromeo , committed to specific targets and action plans to reach more small farmers and fisherfolk over the next three years . <p> In particular , it will raise its scale of lending to agriculture 15% per year , from P231 billion this year to P350 billion in 2022 . It will triple its direct lending to small farmers and fisherfolk from the current P9.3 billion to P30 billion by 2022 . And reach three million farmers and fisherfolk from the current number of 890,000 beneficiaries . <p> Thus in addition to the conventional CAMELS banking performance standards imposed @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Land Bank henceforth must measure up to those social targets . <p> These are ambitious targets and we can only wish Land Bank the best .
