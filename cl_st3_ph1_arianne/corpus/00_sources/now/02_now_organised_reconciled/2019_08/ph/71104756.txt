<h> Agrarian Reform Beneficiaries receive farm machineries <p> GENERAL SANTOS CITY , Aug. 16 -- Recipients of farm machineries and equipment ( FMEs ) expressed their gratitude to the government as the Department of Agrarian Reform-Sarangani conducted a series of turn-over ceremonies . <p> The farm implements , which include mobile rice mills , power tillers , rice threshers , hand tractors and corn shellers , will help the farmer-beneficiaries develop and sustain the productivity of their awarded lands . <p> Felix C. Frias , Provincial Agrarian Reform Program Officer II of DAR Sarangani emphasized during the ceremony that the 2-million-peso worth of project is intended to support the areas vulnerable to the impacts of climate change . <p> " As we give these projects to you , we expect you to establish sound policies , systems and procedures in the usage and maintenance of the FMEs to keep them functional for a long period , " Frias added . <p> John H. Gamad , Chief Agrarian Reform Officer for ARB Development &amp; Support said , " The project , which includes capacity building and provision of FMEs , will help the organizations become hubs of support services to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ " <p> Meanwhile , Barangay Captain Gideon Lambago of Barangay Badtasan , Kiamba sees economic opportunities for his constituents through this mechanization project . He said that more farmers will now have access to pre- and post-harvest facilities that will minimize wastage of farm produce . He is also hopeful that the members of the organization who work as mechanical operators will get additional income in the days to come .
