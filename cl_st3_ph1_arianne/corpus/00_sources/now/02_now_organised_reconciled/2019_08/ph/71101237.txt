Rafael Tatlonghari . <p> Speaking during the DOTr-organized media briefing on the government 's Build , Build , Build and the eco-technologies in place at the country 's first eco-airport , the manager said that as gateway to the islands which gives premium on eco-cultural tourism , the move towards eco-technologies is just but right . <p> ACCESSIBLE TO THE PWD .
