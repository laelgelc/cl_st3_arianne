<h> Five ways to integrate sustainability into your everyday life <p> AS the summer draws to a close , people are gearing up for back to school , back to work , back to reality ! It 's a good time of year to press restart and initiate new routines . <p> When it comes to behaving more sustainability , not knowing where to start holds many people back . Faced with the daunting reality of climate change it is easy to wonder if individual efforts are worthwhile -- but they all add up and they matter . <p> Here are five ways to integrate sustainability into your everyday life and business practices so you can future-proof your family and your work . <p> 1 . Think green when thinking of energy <p> This requires sitting at your computer for about 15 minutes with a utility bill , logging on to any of the switcher sites . They are draining energy and costing you money -- as much as 10% of your energy bill . Take time to switch off computers , printers , coffee machines , phone chargers and televisions when they are not in use . <p> 2 . Figure out your heating <p> Believe it or not around 40% of us do n't actually know how to work our thermostats . <p> Remember that every degree your turn it down saves energy and money . The advice is to set thermostats to 18-21C . Those who feel the cold should add an extra layer of clothes , rather than heating the whole room or office more . And avoid having windows open and a radiator on at the same time . <p> 3 .
