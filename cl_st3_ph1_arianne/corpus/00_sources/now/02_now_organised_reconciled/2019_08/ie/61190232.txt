<p> Former Irish president and UN High Commissioner for Human Rights Mary Robinson is the recipient of this year 's Tipperary International Peace Award . <p> Mrs Robinson was chosen for the accolade in recognition of her work to ensure that human rights issues are a central tenet of global politics and governance . <p> The announcement was made yesterday ( Thursday ) by the Tipperary Peace Convention who praised the current Chair of The Elders for " putting human rights standards at the heart of global governance by ensuring that the needs of the poorest and most vulnerable are addressed on the global stage " . <p> " I am very honoured to receive the Tipperary International Peace Award , which is a wonderful example of a local community organising a prize for peace which has achieved wide international standing and recognition , " she said . <p> Mrs Robinson , born in Ballina , Co Mayo , was elected as the first woman president of Ireland in 1990 and stayed in the role for seven years before serving as the UN 's High Commissioner for Human Rights from 1997 to 2002 . <p> She has worked in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Foundation for Climate Justice .
