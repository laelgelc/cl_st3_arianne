<p> Australia 's Great Barrier Reef is one of the most beautiful natural wonders and diverse ecosystems in the world . Considered one of the seven natural wonders , it is the largest coral reef system in the world and is the biggest living structure on the planet covering a massive 344,400 square kilometers . However , this prized World Heritage Area is currently dying . <p> In a recent study , researchers identified that 89% of the Great Barrier Reef has declined due to mass bleaching brought on upon by climate change . This mass bleaching is directly impacting the ecosystems that rely on the coral , including humans .
