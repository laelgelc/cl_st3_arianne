<h> Youth Ambassadors from 13 countries learn to fight for the Atlantic Ocean <h> Galway 's Marine Institute is helping young people from around the world fight for the future of the Atlantic Ocean . <p> Twenty-three Youth Ambassadors from countries connected to the Atlantic Ocean have been taking part in a Summer School at the Marine Institute for the past week . <p> This inaugural summer school is meant to equip young people with the skills and knowledge to fight for the ocean 's future through engagement with political , social , economic and scientific leaders . <p> The Youth Ambassadors have been learning from experts on how to develop communication campaigns on ocean issues , reach out and engage local communities and experience the challenges of those living by the ocean . <p> The Summer School has also introduced them to the the power of science diplomacy and the work of the Atlantic Ocean Research Alliance . <p> Galway TD and Minister of state for the Gaeltacht and the Islands met with the Youth Ambassadors on Saturday . <p> " With the Atlantic Ocean facing significant challenges posed by climate change and marine pollution , due to @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ on the voices of the next generation to continue the current momentum for change .
