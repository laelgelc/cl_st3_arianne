<p> If the aviation industry was a country , it would rank among the world 's top 10 emitters of CO2 . Aviation emissions have risen by 70pc since 2005 and as demand increases in rich and poorer countries , they 're forecast to increase by between 300pc and 700pc by 2050 . <p> Arresting this incline will be the first step towards a sustainable system of international travel -- but how could it be done ? A frequent flyer tax would be relatively easy to implement but it could mean the richest can still afford to fly while the poorest are priced out . <p> Most plane passengers are already relatively wealthy . Only 18pc of the world 's population have ever flown and in any given year , an elite 3pc of the world flies . That 's about 230m people , but flights carried 4bn passengers in 2017 .
