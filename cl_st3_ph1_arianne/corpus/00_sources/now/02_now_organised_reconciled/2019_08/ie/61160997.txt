Ability West held a Rock the Boat Guinness World Record Attempt , which they believe they have achieved , on Salthill Promenade today . 1,805 people of all ages ' rocked the boat ' during the event . The current record is 1,702 people . Ability West provides services and supports to over 590 children and adults with intellectual disability and autism in Galway City and County .
