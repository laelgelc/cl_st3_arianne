<h> Fashion and politics with Natalie B Coleman : this week on Smart Casual <p> Smart Casual is IMAGE 's first fashion podcast , in collaboration with Kildare Village . In our bi-weekly chats , we examine why we wear the clothes we do and deal with personal style in a way that speaks to you . <p> We are living in uncertain times , both politically and socially . From Brexit to climate change to the continued obstruction of female rights ; there has never been so much anxiety and ambiguity . However , when situations like this occur , there is never a better time to make an impact and deliver a message . <p> And one way we can do this is through our clothes . <p> This week our hosts tackle the weighty issue of fashion and politics ; how designers reflect what 's going on politically around the world , and how the clothes we choose reflect our own beliefs and ideas .
