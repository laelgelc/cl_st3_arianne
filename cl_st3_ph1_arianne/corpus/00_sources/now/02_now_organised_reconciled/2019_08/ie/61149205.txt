last week , he was shocked to observe the extent of the thaw writes Lorna Siggins . <p> Nugent , who has been to Greenland before , observed rapidly melting ice and snow on the summit of the Disko island ice cap , which is known as one of the top " ice " destinations in Greenland . <p> Greenland 's once mile-thick ice sheet began melting in the mid 19th century , but scientists reported late last year in the journal Nature that the rate has accelerated by 50 per cent since the start of the industrial era -- and 30 per cent since the 20th century alone . <p> Nugent has been observing the impact at first hand as a member of the crew of the 17m ( 56 ft ) Ilen , Ireland 's oldest sail trading ketch which set sail for Greenland earlier this summer . <p> The Ilen , now en route home to
