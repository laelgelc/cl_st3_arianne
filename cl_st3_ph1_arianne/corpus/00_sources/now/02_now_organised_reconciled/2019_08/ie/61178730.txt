<h> BlackRock grows stewardship team amid scrutiny <p> The world 's largest asset manager has grown its stewardship team to 45 employees , up from 36 around this time last year , according to its latest annual report on its engagement and voting activity . <p> BlackRock said this meant it had the largest and most global team in the industry . At the time of its annual report last year , its investment stewardship team members had 36 members . In 2008 the team comprised 13 full-time employees voting at around 8,500 meetings . <p> In last year 's stewardship report BlackRock said it planned to double the size of the team by 2020 . <p> In the 12 months to 30 June 2019 , BlackRock 's investment stewardship team voted at more than 16,000 company meetings . It said voting was " the formal mechanism " through which it provided feedback to companies on their practices , and engaging directly with companies allowed it to make " smarter and more informed voting decisions " . <p> &lt; <p> It held 2,050 engagements with 1,458 companies based in 42 markets , representing 50.4% by value of the equity @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 28 June .
