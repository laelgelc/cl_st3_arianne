<h> Largest bicycle parking facility in the world <p> Famed for its flat landscape , perfect for cycling , the Netherlands has today completely opened the largest bicycle parking facility anywhere in the world in the city of Utrecht . <p> The facility has 12,500 spaces over three floors for bike parking which are open 24/7 . The first 24 hours of parking completely free . <p> The Municipality of Utrecht , ProRail and NS ( Dutch Railways ) jointly developed the facility employing Dutch contractor BAM and will continue to manage the multi-storey known as Stationsplein over the coming years . <p> The decision to build such a facility was taken as part of a strategy in which hundreds of millions of euros will be used to improve cycling infrastructure across the Netherlands . <p> Stientje van Veldhoven , junior infrastructure minister , said " We are striving to make it the Netherlands a cyclists ' paradise and there 's still much to be done . <p> " I 'd like us to make better use of what I call this secret weapon against congestion , poor air quality in cities and climate change that is also good @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @
