<h> Global infrastructure spend ' to grow by 4.8pc ' <h> LONDON , 10 days ago <p> Global infrastructure construction is forecast to grow by 4.8 per cent on an annual average basis in real terms between 2019 and 2023 , according to GlobalData , a leading data and analytics company . <p> This will be propelled by Asia , where growth is expected to average 7.0 per cent a year in South and South East Asia , as well as 5.8 per cent in North East Asia . <p> GlobalData is currently tracking over 14,000 large-scale infrastructure projects in both the public and private sectors that are worth a minimum value of $25 million ( totalling $14.8 trillion ) worldwide at all stages from announcement to execution . <p> The power sector has the most projects in the pipeline with 5,681 , followed by the road sector with 4,004 , and railways with 1,945 .
