<h> TBWA Melbourne helps the UN welcome Greta Thunberg to New York <p> Global creative agency TBWA has joined forces with the United Nations and other partners to welcome Swedish climate change activist and noble prize nominee Greta Thunberg to New York City ahead of the Climate Change Action Summit in September . <p> Thunberg 's sailboat was greeted and escorted into New York by 17 sailboats , each representing one of the 17 Global Sustainable Development Goals ( SDG ) featured on its sail . <p> The SDGs were agreed to by all Member States of the United Nations in 2015 as global blueprint for dignity , peace and prosperity for people and the planet . <p> TBWAMelbourne partnered with others in the global creative community to bring the project to life , including Project Everyone , Freuds and Sinclair Social , who all rallied to galvanise around this moment , with support from TBWAChiatDay in New York . <p> TBWAMelbourne and Adelaide chief creative officer Paul Reardon says while the SDG logo is printed on Thunberg 's main sail , many people still do not fully understand the goals or issues they cover . <p> " There @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ 've been overwhelmed by the reaction from Greta , the team onboard the Malizia II and the broader sailing community , " Reardon says . <p> " It 's been a truly global effort -- spanning Melbourne , New York , San Francisco , Rome , London and of course The Atlantic . " <p> The SDG Flotilla met Thunberg at the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge and sailed alongside the Malizia II to North Cove Harbor in honour of her quest to raise awareness and support for Climate Action , as well as the other 16 Goals . <p> In acknowledgement of the welcoming fleet Thunberg tweeted : " The @UN has sent out one boat for each of the 17 sustainable development goal to greet us ! Thank you ! " <p> The Flotilla is led by Captain Nitzan Levy of Sailors NYC and Deputy Captain Barbara Marcoz , UN employee and member of North Cove Sailing Club . <p> It comprised 17 vessels provided by local New York City sailing clubs including North Cove Sailing , Hudson River Community Sailing , Hoboken Sailing Club , Sailors NYC and New City @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Facebook and activated across numerous social media channels using #sdgflotilla . <p> Digital advertising supporting each of the SDG 's will also roll out across numerous sites in the lead up to the UN General Assembly high level week , kicking off on 23 September . <p> " Greta Thunberg 's passion and promise to fight climate change presented a powerful platform to create further awareness of the Sustainable Development Goals , as we move closer to the 2030 deadline , " Kimberlee Wells , CEO , TBWAMelbourne and Adelaide says . <p> " Whilst progress has been made , few countries are on track to achieve the targets . Australia and America included .
