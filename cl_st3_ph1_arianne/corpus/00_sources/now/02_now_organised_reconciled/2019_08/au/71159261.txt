<h> It 's human nature to over-emphasise the here and now , to focus on the news agenda and to extrapolate the recent past . When it comes to our investments , however , it pays to take a longer view . The zigs and zags of the market are irrelevant - what matters is the underlying direction of travel . Here , then , are four mega-trends that I believe will shape markets over the next ten years . <p> The first has been brewing for some time now and is perhaps the trend that we can be most certain of - the retreat of globalisation and the rise of a more regionalised , protectionist and populist world . This matters to investors because it draws a line under the past forty years or so , during which markets have prospered , albeit with some nasty air-pockets along the way .
