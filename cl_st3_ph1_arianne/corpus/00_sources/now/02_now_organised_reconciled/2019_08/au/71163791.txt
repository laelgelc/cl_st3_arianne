<p> KEY FINDINGS OF REEF OUTLOOK REPORT Past decade : * In 2009 , the reef was considered to be at a " crossroads " between a positive , well-managed future and a less certain one . * In 2014 , it was seen as an " icon under pressure " , with continued efforts needed to address key threats . * In 2019 , it is a " changed and less resilient " reef . Scientific evidence : * Initiatives that will halt and reverse the effects of climate change at a global level and effectively improve water quality at a regional scale are " urgent " . * Reef management needs innovative approaches that are targeted , science-based and risk-managed. * Climate change is escalating and is the most significant threat to the long-term outlook . * Coral reefs that have escaped impacts of bleaching , cyclones and crown-of-thorns starfish outbreaks remain in good condition .
