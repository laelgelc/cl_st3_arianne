<p> The New Zealand government has announced a series of reforms to its emissions trading scheme , including protections for forestry projects impacted by natural disaster and the winding down of free allocations to heavy emitters , as it gears up to meet its pledge of net-zero emissions . <p> Under the reforms , the free allocation of emissions permits to industrial users will be gradually reduced over time . Initially , allocations will be reduced at 1 per cent per year from 2021-2030 , then at 2 per cent from 2030-2041 , and at 3 per cent per year from 2041-2050 . <p> The reduction industrial allowances had been flagged by the New Zealand government for some time and is seen as a crucial way of beginning to put pressure on large emitters to begin reducing their own emissions . <p> " We are all in this together .
