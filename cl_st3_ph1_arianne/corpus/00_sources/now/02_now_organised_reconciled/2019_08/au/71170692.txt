Well surprise , surprise , it was made in ( you guessed it ) , China . For goodness sake , we have our very own chocolate factory right here in Tasmania employing locals and using as much as possible Australian produce . The important lesson to learn from the above in relation to our workers here in Tasmania is to check the country of origin and only buy locally made chocolate . If we do n't , in future there may be a chocolate taste in our mouths a little different than what we know , and on a more permanent basis . Robert Lee , Summerhill .
