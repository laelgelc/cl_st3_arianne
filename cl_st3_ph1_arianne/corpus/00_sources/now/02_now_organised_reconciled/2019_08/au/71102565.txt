<h> 5 quick fixes for Turnbull and Frydenberg to change energy debate <p> A series of 28 consecutive News Polls show the Coalition is trailing Labor . So perhaps it 's time to dump the right wing policies on energy and climate Turnbull promised to cherish , and set a new path . Here are some quick and easy fixes , offered free , and without retainer . <p> The Coalition government is wallowing . It has marked up a 28th consecutive poll where it is trailing the Labor Party . Even prime minister Malcolm Turnbull is struggling to hold his head above Bill Shorten as preferred PM . <p> So perhaps it 's time to finally change the conversation , dump the right-wing policies on energy and climate that Turnbull promised to cherish , and set a new path . Here are some quick and easy fixes , offered free and without retainer from RenewEconomy . <p> Buy that EV <p> You know you have wanted to ever since you visited the Tesla factory and gushed about the new technology and the exciting transition ahead . <p> " Walking through the highly automated assembly lines was inspiring , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Tesla S model , " Turnbull wrote in a Facebook blog in early 2015 .
