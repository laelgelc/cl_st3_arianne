Numerous academic studies confirm that consumers hate the future , or more accurately , do n't value it very highly , at least economically . For example , in a study on refrigerators , 50 percent of consumers would not pay $40 more up front to generate savings of $22 per year on their energy bill . Economists regularly quantify this type of behavior in a metric called the discount rate , which is just a percentage used to convert tomorrow 's dollars into today 's dollars . High discount rates make future dollars worth less . <p> Consumers demonstrate discount rates approaching 60 percent per year through their behavior .
