<p> Lanka offers to set up Regional Maritime Information Fusion CenterPosted on August 18th , 2019 <h> Courtesy NewsIn.Asia <p> Colombo , August 18 ( newsin.asia ) : In the first meeting of Indian Ocean Region Association ( IORA ) Maritime Safety and Security ( MSS ) Working Group chaired by Sri Lanka and held in Colombo on August 8 and 9 , Sri Lanka reiterated its interest in setting up a Regional Maritime Information Fusion Centre to complement similar centers in Madagascar and Singapore . <p> Further , Sri Lanka also offered to undertake to support member states in capacity building related to anti-piracy and SAR activities as well as engaging in research connected to specific MSS challenges , a Lankan Foreign Ministry release said . <p> With the participation of 22 foreign delegates representing IORA member countries and representatives of the IORA Secretariat the working group finalized the work plan for action . <p> Inaugurating the meeting Foreign Secretary **27;4362;TOOLONG Sri Lanka 's role in spearheading the Indian Ocean Peace Zone proposal in 1971 , said at the time few recognized the importance of the concept .
