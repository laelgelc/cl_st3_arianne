Inauguration is set for August and is anticipated to be open to the public for 5 months . Over 140,000 visitors are expected to visit the exhibition . <p> " We are very passionate about our educational projects . That 's why we decided to create this exhibition and do our part in helping to ensure that our kids and their kids will have access to clean water .
