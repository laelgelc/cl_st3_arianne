<h> Ten books on work to read for Labour Day <p> Falling on the first Monday in September , Labour Day in Canada is typically associated with the last gasp of summer before the weather cools and the obligations of autumn set in . But the holiday has much frostier roots , originating in 1872 , when the Toronto Typographical Union went on strike to demand a nine-hour workday at workplaces that included The Globe newspaper . In December of that year , several unions banded together in support of the typographers to host the first Labour Day Parade .
