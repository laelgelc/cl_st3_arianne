The Blackfeet , whose reservation lies on the eastern border of the park , revere it as the Backbone of the World . Several decades before its creation in 1910 , back when bison numbered in the thousands , the park was their hunting ground . In the early 1900s , the Great Northern Railway commissioned the painter John Fery to capture Glacier 's natural grandeur as a way of enticing travellers westward .
