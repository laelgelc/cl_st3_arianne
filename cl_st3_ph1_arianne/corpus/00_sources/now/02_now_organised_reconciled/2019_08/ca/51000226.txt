The shrews were introduced to fight the larch sawfly , which arrived in North America during the Miocene period . <p> The sawfly was chewing its way through tamarack stands on the island with devastating effect , weakening and killing the trees with regular outbreaks . Adult sawflies place eggs inside tamarack branches ; seven to 10 days later , caterpillars emerge and strip new foliage , then the larvae drop to the ground , burrow into moss and spin cocoons . Shrews and other small mammals eat the cocoons . <p> The Newfoundland shrews , 10 males and 12 females , were introduced at St. George 's on the west coast of the island in 1958 .
