The regulations become increasingly stringent over time and will require industry to produce increasingly cleaner fuels . Industry can do this a number of ways , such as adding higher percentages of bio-fuels in their mix . According to the Pembina Institute , " by requiring fuel products to incrementally lower emissions intensity by approximately 11 per cent by 2030 , the clean fuel standard should result in 30 Mt of GHG emissions reductions by that time ( equivalent to taking seven million cars off the road ) .
