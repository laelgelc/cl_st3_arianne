<p> Thousands of wildfires are ravaging the Amazon rainforest , burning the " lungs of the planet " at a rapid pace and causing smoke plumes to spread millions of square kilometres over South America . <p> Since last week , over 9,500 wildfires have been sparked in Brazil , primarily in the Amazon basin , a greenspace responsible for producing vast amounts of oxygen and widely seen by experts as crucial in efforts to contain climate change . <p> NASA and the U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration ( NOAA ) have shared startling images of blazes from space , showing smoke plumes blowing across Brazil , Bolivia and Paraguay . <h> Photos <p> Fire consumes the jungle near Porto Velho , Brazil , Friday , Aug. 23 , 2019 .
