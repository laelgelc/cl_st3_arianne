<h> Kelowna <h> Protest to greet meat eaters <p> Climate protesters will greet RibFest attendees today and Sunday at Kelowna City Park . <p> Members of Kelowna Climate Save will be drawing public awareness to the association of animal agriculture to climate change . <p> They plan to show the life of a sow with a pig gestation crate demonstration and will use " chalktivism " to write their message on the sidewalk at the entrance to the park , where hungry festival-goers will pass . <p> " Our house is on fire , " the group says . <p> Various demonstrations will be held throughout the day . <p> " We need diet change to reduce climate change , " the group says . <p> Organizers cite a recent Oxford University report that stated avoiding meat and dairy is the single biggest way to reduce your impact on the Earth . <p> " Considering that animal agriculture is responsible for up to 91% of deforestation of the Amazon rainforest , we all need to pay attention , " the group said in a press release .
