<h> Volunteers needed to ' de-phrag ' along Collingwood shoreline <p> File photo of volunteers cutting down phragmites on the Collingwood shoreline . Photo by Duncan Bristow <p> NEWS RELEASE BLUE MOUNTAIN WATERSHED TRUST **25;1171;TOOLONG The date is set and we need volunteers for this year 's massive cull of Phragmites Australis , an invasive grass that grows so thick that turtles can become trapped and die . <p> This Aug. 24 from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. , the Blue Mountain Watershed Trust , the Nottawasaga Valley Conservation Authority and other partners will be going head-to-head with phragmites , a grass that grows up to 10 feet high . The effort this year is made possible in large part to a financial contribution by the W. Garfield Weston Foundation . <p> " Phragmites is an extremely fast-growing , invasive , non-native grass , " says George Powell , a member of the Blue Mountain Watershed Trust . " It continues to take over Georgian Bay shoreline , invade wetlands and threaten our most precious ecosystems , like wetlands . " <p> If left unchecked , Phragmites will cause serious damage to the biodiversity of our area . It @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ compromises desirable habitat for all manner of wildlife . Phragmites also releases toxins from its roots into the surrounding soil , which impede the growth of and even kill off neighbouring vegetation . <p> Where and when <p> Everyone is welcome to come and volunteer next Saturday , from children to seniors , for an hour or for the entire morning . At noon , volunteers and family are invited to join in a complimentary BBQ lunch . There are two locations for cutting . <p> The rallying points are the north end of Hickory Street where it terminates at the parking lot of the Collingwood Arboretum , and at 49 Huron Street in the parking lot just east of Sobeys in front of the Iron Skillet . The lunch BBQ will be held at 49 Huron Street at noon . <p> Volunteers are asked to please register here . They should expect to spend the day outside and in the water/muck , and should bring sunscreen , a hat , drinking water , clothes that can get wet/dirty , gloves ( if possible ) and sturdy footwear .
