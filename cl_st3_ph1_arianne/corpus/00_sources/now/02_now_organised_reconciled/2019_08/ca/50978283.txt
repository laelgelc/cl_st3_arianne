<h> Living Green hosts film screening to celebrate fundraising campaign launch <p> NEWS RELEASE LIVING GREEN BARRIE **28;2596;TOOLONG Living Green Barrie has been making this city a greener place to live for almost 30 years . Formally known as Environmental Action Barrie , it was formed in 1991 to increase public awareness of environmental issues and to operate a recycling depot for items not collected by the City of Barrie at that time . <p> Since its inception , Living Green ( LG ) has delivered and spearheaded several innovative environmental services and programs that have been taken up by the City of Barrie and others . Examples include the recycling depot , and Spring Into Clean . This fall , they are hosting several community tree plantings , FruitShare blitzes , educational workshops and events . <p> After a strategic planning exercise , LG has launched a fundraising campaign called 10,000 Trees for Barrie and adopted a new mission : to guide citizens , businesses and local leaders towards actions that foster sustainability and resilience in our community . <p> Please join Living Green this Thursday night at 7 p.m. at the Uptown Theatre for a free movie @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ <p> This movie is a perfect remedy to combat the powerlessness we can feel when faced with the realities of climate change .
