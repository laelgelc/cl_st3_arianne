No one denies the Earth is warming , to levels never before seen , and is on track to warm further . No one sensible denies the consensus of most of the world 's foremost climate scientists , that this trend is almost certainly caused , in the main , by human activity ; few doubt that , if it continues , it is likely to have dire and possibly catastrophic consequences for much of the planet . <p> And is n't Elections Canada 's reasoning -- that , so far as at least one of the parties in the election , the People 's Party of Canada , denies that climate change is a crisis , even the promotion of this widely accepted fact counts as an intervention in @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ -- a bit daft ? <p> Certainly that was the reaction on social media .
