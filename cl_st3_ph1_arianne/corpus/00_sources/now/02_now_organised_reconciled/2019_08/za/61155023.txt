<h> Ramaphosa talks technology -- and job losses <p> President Cyril Ramaphosa has highlighted the changing nature of the world in which we live , particularly because of the advances in technology . <p> The president was speaking at the 25 Years of Democracy Conference , at University of Johannesburg in Auckland Park on Tuesday ( 24 July ) . <p> " Many more people are going to lose jobs , " Ramaphosa said . " They 'll lose jobs because of technology , globalisation , climate change and a whole number of other challenges like low economic growth , as we have seen , in our own country . " <p> The two-day conference aims to provide an in-depth reflective analysis and report on the past quarter of democracy and how the country can map the next 25 years .
