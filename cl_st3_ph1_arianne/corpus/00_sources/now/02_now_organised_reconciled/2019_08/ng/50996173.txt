<h> Vacate flood-prone areas , Oyo warns residents <p> The Oyo State government has called on people living close to flood-prone areas to vacate the places pending the period the current heavy rains would recede to avoid loss of lives and valuable properties to the flood . It has been established that certain actions aggravate flooding such as dumping of wastes on river channels and waterways and such acts lead to blockage of drainage and river channels . The Commissioner for the Environment and Natural Resources , Hon. Kehinde Ayoola , gave this warning when alongside officials from the Ibadan Urban Flood Management Project on Wednesday visited some areas where flood wrecked havoc on residents after it rained for over two hours on August 27 , 2019 . The areas where the rivers and streams overflowed their banks in Ibadan include Ogundipe and Arikemase community at Olodo area of Egbeda local government in Ibadan . According to Ayoola , a good number of buildings in those areas were erected on waterways and road setbacks , especially in the flood-prone areas , warning that it would not be business as usual anymore .
