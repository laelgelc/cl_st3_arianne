<h> Tokyo named world 's safest city , Amsterdam tops Europe ranking <p> LONDON , Aug 29 ( Thomson Reuters Foundation ) -- Tokyo was named the world 's safest city on Thursday by the Economist Intelligence Unit , in an index ranking cities ' ability to handle everything from climate disasters to cyber attacks . <p> Singapore took second place after Japan 's capital while another Japanese metropolis , Osaka , came third -- the same top three as the two previous Safe Cities Indexes of 2015 and 2017 . <p> This year the index of 60 cities aimed to capture the concept of " urban resilience " , which is the ability of cities to absorb and bounce back from shocks , researchers said . <p> This concept has increasingly steered urban safety planning during the last decade , as policymakers worry about the impacts of climate change , including heat stress and flooding . <p> The index assessed four types of safety : digital , infrastructure , health and personal security . <p> Asia-Pacific dominated the top 10 , as in previous years , with six cities , including Australia 's Sydney in fifth place , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ in 10th . <p> Two European and two North American cities made it into the top 10 , with the Dutch capital Amsterdam in fourth place while Denmark 's Copenhagen came eighth .
