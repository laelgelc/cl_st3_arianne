<p> The 36 state governors under the umbrella of Nigeria Governors ' Forum , NGF , are meeting in Abuja to discuss update on recent directive of the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit , NFIU , on local government funds . <p> The governors , according to the agenda of the Wednesday 's meeting , are also expected to discuss and review update on Primary Health Care under One Roof , PHCOUR , and other health related matters . <p> Also on the agenda is " UN General Assembly -- Panel on Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) and Climate Change ; Feedback on Governors ' Meeting with African Development Bank ( AfDB ) and Chinese Investors . " <p> Other items on the agenda were the forum 's one-day meeting with partners such as Debt Management Office , Joint Task Board and Nigeria Stock Exchange . <p> Expected to be presented to the governors at the meeting is the " Transmission Company of Nigeria : Briefing on Transmission Expansion Plan and its potential impact in improving power supply in states . "
