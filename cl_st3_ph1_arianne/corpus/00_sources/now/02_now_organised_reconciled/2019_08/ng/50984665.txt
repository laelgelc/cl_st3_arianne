<p> Lawyer and TFAA Alumnus , Jake Effoduh has just released his fashion collection to demonstrate " sustainable fashion . " Announcing the production of his fashion debut in collaboration with bespoke fashion company , April9ine , Effoduh spoke about the inspiration for his work . <p> " By recycling fabrics ; using only environmentally-friendly materials ; and , cutting down on production waste by thirty percent , we are proving that the fashion industry can contribute to climate change adaptation in a big way . " <p> This Fall/Harmattan collection named " The Sage " showcases designs that according to Effoduh portray the globalization of fashion by demonstrating the creativity that can comes from inter-cultural engagement . <p> Speaking to the press , the CEO of April9ine , Aluwaye Ilegar said : " Having known Okechukwu Effoduh for 13 years , my team and I are inspired by his personality and his dedication to human rights . I am honored to have a great man represent our brand . " <p> The outfits as displayed at the release of the collection in Abuja , have designs that are noticeably fusing several cultures and symbols . " The creative @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Third World fashions whilst preserving the uniqueness of individuality and cosmopolitanism . This is something that Mr. Effoduh embodies " Ilegar adds . <p> Effoduh 's commitment to climate change adaptation and mitigation stems from his role as a Climate Reality Leader trained by former U.S Vice President , Al Gore in 2018 . " I believe that every outfit should be able to tell a story , and perhaps a story beyond the self . Our clothes should not come at the cost of people or our planet . It starts one Kaftan ; one collection , " said Effoduh . <p> Effoduh Okechukwu is the winner of The Future Awards Africa Prize for Advocacy in 2014 . He is a lawyer and partner of Praxis &amp; Gnosis Law . He obtained his Bachelors from the University of Abuja , and holds Masters degrees in law from the University of Oxford , and the Osgoode Hall Law School respectively . He is a World Economic Forum Global Shaper.
