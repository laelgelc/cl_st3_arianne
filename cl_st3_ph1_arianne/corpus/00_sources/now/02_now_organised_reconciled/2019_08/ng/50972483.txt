destination , will help ease tensions between the members of the elite club . <p> Here are the main topics and expected key moments during the three days of talks amid the Old World charm of the luxury Hotel du Palais , which overlooks crashing Atlantic waves . <p> -- The Trump riptide -- <p> The US president makes or breaks international meetings , depending on his mood and the content of his Twitter account . <p> The last meeting of the G7 -- which comprises the US , Britain , France , Germany , Italy , Japan and Canada -- turned into a fiasco when Trump refused to put his name to a question because it used to be the place where the old West could speak frankly , " former Italian prime minister Enrico Letta , now head of international affairs at Sciences Po university in Paris , told AFP . <p> -- Global turbulence -- <p> The Iranian nuclear crisis , protests in Hong Kong , war in Syria and fresh tensions in the violence-wracked Kashmir region will all be on the agenda when the leaders arrive on Saturday . <p> India 's Hindu nationalist premier Narendra Modi has been invited to join the summit and is expected to explain in person his decision to revoke the autonomy of Indian-controlled Kashmir which has raised tensions with neighbour Pakistan . <p> The Iran nuclear crisis will also figure prominently , with @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ via crippling sanctions seen as French diplomat told reporters on condition of anonymity . <p> -- Adverse trade winds -- <p> Fears have grown in recent weeks about the health of the global economy caused by a trade war between the
