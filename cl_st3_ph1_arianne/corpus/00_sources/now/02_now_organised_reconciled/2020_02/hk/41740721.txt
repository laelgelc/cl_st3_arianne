As a part of the peaceful protest , Anglican priest Fr . Rod Bower led an interfaith mourning ritual for victims of the widespread bushfires that have razed large areas of Australia . The ceremony of mourning was organized by the Canberra Interfaith Forum and the Australian Centre for Christianity and Culture . <p> " A religious response to climate change is essential for a healthy spirituality . It 's an expression of our connection with God , with each other , and with the Earth , " Bower was quoted as saying . " Our responsibility to care for the Earth is something that is common to all the faiths.
