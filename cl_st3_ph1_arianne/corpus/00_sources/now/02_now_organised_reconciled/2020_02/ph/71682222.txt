With this , the province wants to eliminate also its carbon footprint . <p> As host to a total of 283-megawatt ( MW ) wind power farms located in the towns of Bangui , Burgos , and Pagudpud , Ilocos Norte and approximately 26.7-MW solar projects in Currimao and Burgos respectively , including a 5-MW Agua Grande hydroelectric plant in Pancian , Pagupdud , local authorities here expressed commitment to sustain the growth of renewable energy here . <p> Among the first measures that landed at the new set of Sangguniang Panlalawigan ( SP or Provincial Board ) is a draft resolution
