<h> Hybrid agriculture starts reviving ghost barangay in Basilan <p> Conflict has displaced several families and communities in Basilan . For a number of years , the displacement also disrupted the people 's source of livelihood who were mostly farmers . <p> The farmers grew tired of the atrocities and seldom develop their farming techniques because of the conflict . But with the new farming technology introduced through the efforts of the Fourth Special Forces Battalion of the Philippine Army in Basilan , the conflict-affected communities have a better life to hope for . <p> Recently , 14 families , who already went back to their lands , were trained in organic farming together with the agriculture officers of the local government and cadres of the 4th Special Forces Battalion in the former ghost barangay of Calugusan in Lantawan town from January 28 to 31 . <p> The community was taught on how to produce wood vinegar through an improvised kiln which will be used as an organic pesticide , soil conditioner , and micro-organism fertilizer . <p> They were also taught how to make Oriental Herbal Nutrients ( OHN ) , Fish Amino Acid , and Fermented Plant @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ produced and are sustainable . <p> The farmers also learned to make improvised water filters made from activated charcoal , sand , and gravel . With the right procedure and proportion , this can filter a murky water into a potable one . This is a big help in the community where water sources are scarce and non-potable . <p> This Hybrid Agricultural Empowerment Training was introduced and conducted by Mr. Patrick Taylor who has been advocating this program for five years already as a move to fight climate change and promote food security at the grassroots level . <p> Taylor , a foreigner , found love and meaning in Philippine soil as he dedicated a substantial portion of his life to the enrichment of farmers in Mindanao . <p> " I have helped teach and empower farmers in the Zamboanga Peninsula and Northern Mindanao . In Zamboanga , I taught over 100 farmers in organic agriculture , and helped Subanen women form a cooperative so they can receive fair trade , " Taylor said on his social media page . <p> His advocacy work extended to few agriculture-centered high @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ malnutrition .
