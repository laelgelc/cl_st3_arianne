The tax base in the UK has been shrinking in recent years , thanks to the fiscal policy shared in common by the country 's two major parties -- the Tory and Labour . Even as spending rises , the number of people paying into the system has been declining . Both parties advocated that taxpayers earning less than the equivalent of $103,000 will not pay any additional income tax , although Labour has promised higher spending roughly equivalent to 4 percent of the country 's GDP . <p> The Conservatives , however , has proposed a more modest spending rate . In short , the taxpayers are getting something for nothing -- a Utopia for the working men and women who will not be burdened with higher payroll tax .
