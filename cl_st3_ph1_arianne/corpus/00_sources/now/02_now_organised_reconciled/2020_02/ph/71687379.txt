<h> ERC rejects FIT-anchored RE pricing <p> The Energy Regulatory Commission ( ERC ) has thumbed down proposals to anchor " green energy pricing " for new renewable energy ( RE ) projects to the degressed feed-in-tariff ( FIT ) , with the agency noting that such will have costly impact on the power bills of consumers . <p> ERC logo ( Photo courtesy of www.erc.gov.ph ) <p> Sharon Montaner , chief of the Renewable Energy Division of the ERC , said the agency will instead conduct its own study first before coming up with a formula on the proposed " green energy tariff , " which is designed to compensate the greenfield RE ventures that will be developed within the scope of the Renewable Portfolio Standards ( RPS ) policy instituted by the Department of Energy ( DOE ) . <p> It was also the DOE which proposed in its draft Circular that the " green tariff " shall be benchmarked on the degressed FIT rates of the RE technologies , primarily those on solar , wind , hydro , biomass and even geothermal . <p> But Montaner explained that if the degressed or reduced FIT rates of @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ kWh ) and ? 7.40 per kWh respectively -- will be adopted , such will be very expensive because these were still based on rates eight years ago . <p> " The rates where the degressed solar and wind FITs were anchored were still based on 2012 , so these were high . Any decrease in the cost of the technologies will not be captured , " she stressed . <p> The ERC official similarly qualified that in a more realistic sense , " there had been no degressed FITs for solar and wind , " but rather these are the second wave FIT rates imposed for the higher installation caps set for these two technologies . <p> Montaner said , " We only have degressed FIT rates for run-of-river and biomass , " and these were based on the ERC ruling issued in 2015 .
