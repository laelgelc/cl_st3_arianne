Epidemics always happen . They have a lifespan . Now what we need to do is minimize , contain that epidemic so we can bounce back easier , " Commission on Population and Development ( Popcom ) Undersecretary Juan Antonio Perez III told BusinessMirror . " In a way , development may take one step back .
