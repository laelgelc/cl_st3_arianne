<h> INSIDE LABOUR : The green irony at Davos <p> 07:00 31/01/2020 Terry Bell <p> The irony of climate change -- more accurately , accelerated global warming -- featuring so prominently at the annual World Economic Forum ( WEF ) meeting in Davos , Switzerland , last week , seems to have been lost . Instead , this private rich boys ' club has managed to use the issue as a fig leaf to try to cover the complicity of its members in this and other crises around the world . <p> However , it is unlikely that many activists in the burgeoning Extermination Rebellion who are fighting to halt the race to climate disaster will succumb to the blandishments of a club that represents to so-called 1% who control the bulk of global resources . Not that the WEF and its supporters will not continue to try to woo them . <p> But there now seems to exist a consensus that it would be feasible -- and desirable -- to reduce , even eliminate , reliance on fossil fuels .
