<h> Eskom waiting for government on 3GW plant that could ease load shedding <p> Eskom is making preparations for a 3GW gas-fired plant , but the debt-stricken utility is still awaiting formal guidelines from the government before it can proceed with the project . <p> The facility could alleviate a serious shortage of electricity that led to a series of rolling blackouts , as the state-owned company has been forced to implement nationwide outages while it repairs a poorly maintained fleet of coal-fired facilities and fixes defects at newly built units . <p> The combined cycle plant is proposed for Richards Bay , according to an August 2019 environmental impact assessment report . Natural gas will be the main fuel source , with diesel as a substitute . <p> The facility could alleviate a serious shortage of electricity that led to a series of rolling blackouts <p> " The timelines will be determined by the relevant government authorities " because the department of energy has n't clarified the allocation of new generation capacity as indicated by the country 's Integrated Resource Plan , Eskom said in a reply to questions . <p> The cost of the project is also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ , Eskom will be in a better position to determine costs estimates , funding requirements and strategy , gas sourcing activities and technical feasibility , " the company said . <h> ' Climate change impacts ' <p> The project would result in " climate change impacts of high significance " , though it would present the best technology option if used in concert with intermittent power from renewable energy , according to the EIA report . <p> Environmental group groundWork opposes the development due to concerns about emissions and costs . Eskom is saddled with debt of R454-billion and relies on government bailouts to make the interest payments . <p> The department of energy also has yet to determine who will build the proposed plant , according to the utility . " Eskom is currently undertaking feasibility studies to determine viability " of such a plant , along with the environmental assessment , infrastructure requirements and technical studies , it said . -- Reported by Paul Burkhardt , ( c ) 2020 Bloomberg LP
