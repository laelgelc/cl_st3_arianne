We need a rapid transition to net zero . <p> " We all want energy that is reliable and affordable , but that is no longer enough . It must also be cleaner . " <p> Irish national Looney last week succeeded American Bob Dudley , whose ten-year stint as chief executive included fallout from the 2010 Gulf of Mexico oil spill disaster - the biggest environmental catastrophe in US history . <p> " Energy markets are changing , driven by climate change , technology and societal expectations , and the board supports Bernard and his new leadership team 's ambition for BP , " the company 's chairperson Helge Lund said on Wednesday . <p> " Aiming for net zero is not only the right thing for BP , it is the right thing for our shareholders and for society more broadly . <p> " As we embark on this ambitious agenda , we will maintain a strong focus on safe , reliable and efficient operations and on delivering the promises we have made to our investors , " Lund added .
