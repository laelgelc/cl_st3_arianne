He grew up dislocated from his homeland , growing up thinking that his country was a myth that only existed in the lived experiences of his elders . The boy was schooled in foreign countries with their welcoming arms , and different languages . Fast forward ....... The boy grew into a young man who was trained with many other young men and women in the fight for an equal democratic society , grounded in revolutionary love for @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ home land with the ghosts of many comrades sown into his skin .
