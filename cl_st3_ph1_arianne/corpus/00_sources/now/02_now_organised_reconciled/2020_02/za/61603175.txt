<h> Ricoh earns top EcoVadis sustainability award again <p> Feb 5 , 2020 <p> EcoVadis has awarded Ricoh its highest rating for sustainability , continuing Ricoh 's Gold-winning streak since its inauguration to the position in 2014 . <p> Ricoh this year competed against companies from 155 countries , across 198 business sectors , including assessments on corporate policies , initiatives and achievements in environment , labour and human rights , ethics , and sustainable procurement . <p> " We ranked in the top 5% worldwide , " says Jacques van Wyk , CEO of Ricoh SA . " We 've long held the position that sustainable supply chains are paramount to doing business as we experience the effects of climate change . " <p> Sustainable performance is also a factor for many businesses in supplier selection , which is why it is more common for customers to request Ricoh 's EcoVadis assessment results as part of the selection process .
