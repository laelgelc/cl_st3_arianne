<h> EU 's top diplomat under fire again for ' Greta syndrome ' remark <p> 2 Min Read <p> BRUSSELS , Feb 10 ( Reuters ) - The European Commission had to apologise again on Monday for a comment by foreign policy chief Josep Borrell , who had questioned young people 's commitment to the fight against climate change , referring to it as " Greta syndrome " . <p> Speaking in Brussels on Wednesday , Borrell said he had doubts about young people 's genuine engagement to tackling climate change , and questioned whether they were ready to change their lifestyles to help compensate miners and others who will be most affected by measures to cut carbon emissions . <p> " It is fine to demonstrate for climate change as long as you are not asked to contribute to pay for it , " Borrell said , calling this attitude " Greta syndrome " in reference to 17-year-old Swedish climate activist Greta Thunberg . <p> Under pressure , Borrell , a Spanish socialist , later apologised .
