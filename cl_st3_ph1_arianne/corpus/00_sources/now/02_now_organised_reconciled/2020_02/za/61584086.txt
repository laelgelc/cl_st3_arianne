<h> CAF goes diplomatic in his Prestige Day address <p> Written by The Editor - <p> 3rd Feb 2020 <p> 90 <p> The Air Force Prestige Day address by Lieutenant General Zakes Msimang on 31 January was widely anticipated to shed some light on probably the thorniest question of 2020 for South Africa 's wider military aviation community : is the SA Air Force ( SAAF ) acknowledging its place in international military aviation as being 100 years old ? <p> Instead and probably diplomatically his best option , Msimang virtually ignored the anniversary , concentrating on education , cyber warfare , the environment , the fourth industrial revolution and terrorism , ethnic nationalism and fundamentalism ( TENF ) among others . These were all seen to be part of making the airborne arm of the South African military one that will protect territorial integrity and ensure the authority of the State is maintained into the future . <p> He pointed out the Department of Defence ( DoD ) " has been forced to continuously adjust its plans downward " . <p> " This is myopic and short term thinking .
