<h> Ramaphosa sees renewables as answer to Eskom 's woes <p> Among a raft of measures to stabilise SA 's electricity needs , president Cyril Ramaphosa has indicated that renewable energy can be the answer to the country 's energy supply needs . <p> Ramaphosa made the announcement last night in his State of the Nation Address ( SONA ) . <p> The president delivered his SONA speech at a time SA is facing crippling electricity shortages , as power utility Eskom , which supplies about 95% of the country 's electricity , battles to keep the lights on . <p> At its worst , the struggling state-owned company had to implement stage six load-shedding which severely impacted households and industry . <p> In his speech , the president said for over a decade , South Africans have had to contend with the effects of a constrained energy supply . <p> " I have spoken extensively about the critical role that Eskom plays in the economy of our country and in the livelihood of every South African . The load-shedding of the last few months has had a debilitating effect on our country .
