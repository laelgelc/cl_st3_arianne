<p> While some of its politicians continue to imagine that coal has a future in South Africa , two key developments from the real world show that securing coal to fuel power stations may prove a problem in future . <p> The first is the news that India - the biggest importer of South African coal - plans to cut off imports by 2024 . Secondly , key coal supplier Glencore has announced plans to exit the SA coal market . <p> All of the major coal giants have now left , or are leaving the country .
