That we strongly affirm our eternal commitment to the fundamental human right of every human being in Malaysia to the highest attainable standard of health and wellbeing , without any impediment or distinction of any kind . described @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the Federation of Malaysia and the Universal Declaration of Human Rights of 1948 , in particular healthcare , justice and solidarity . <p> 2 . That we are convinced that health is beyond just complete physical , mental and social wellbeing for an individual : it also leads to health capital that is crucial for society and nation-building in Malaysia , alongside the universal principles of justice , solidarity and prosperity . We hold that universal health coverage achieves not only bio-psycho-social good for individuals but a more holistic public good that extends to social harmony and resilience , and <p> 3 . That despite the remarkable progress in Malaysia 's health outcomes since Independence in 1957 , there remains unaddressed health needs particularly among the poor ( urban and rural ) and people in vulnerable situations .
