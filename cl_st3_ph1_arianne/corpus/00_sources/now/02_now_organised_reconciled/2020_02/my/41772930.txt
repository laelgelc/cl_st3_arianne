<h> Singapore has a S$100b plan to survive in a far hotter world than experts predicted <p> SINGAPORE ( Feb 26 ) : Singapore has a reputation for planning ahead . When it comes to climate change , it 's planning for the worst . <p> While governments around the world are struggling to meet the goals of the Paris agreement -- keeping the global temperature increase to about 1.5 degrees Celsius and the rise in sea levels to less than 0.5 meters -- Singapore is devising a S$100 billion ( US$72 billion ) plan to safeguard the city against temperatures and floodwaters several times those levels . <p> Analysis by the Meteorological Service Singapore 's Centre for Climate Research Singapore suggests that in a worst case scenario , floods could rise by almost 4 meters , factoring in effects like storm surges -- an increase that would submerge cities from New York to Shanghai and London if repeated globally . <p> Prime Minister Lee Hsien Loong has called climate change a matter of " life and death , " an existential threat to the country as important as national defense .
