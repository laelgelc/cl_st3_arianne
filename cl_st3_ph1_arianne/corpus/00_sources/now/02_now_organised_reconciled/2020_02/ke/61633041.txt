<h> Businesses in East Africa set to benefit from new programme <p> Small Market Enterprises ( SMEs ) in Kenya and Tanzania are set to benefit from the recently started programme by Kenya Climate Innovation Center ( KCIC ) and Climate Technology Center and Network ( CTCN ) that seeks to identify and support mechanisms that will enable industrial SMEs adopt environmentally sound technologies . <p> Acting as the operational arm of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change ( UNFCCC ) ' s technology mechanism , CTCN received from the UNFCCC the mandate to promote the accelerated transfer of environmentally sound technologies for low carbon and climate resilient development to developing countries . <p> CTCNaims to implement private sector innovation programmes which will focus on identification of challenges and specific solutions for the SMEs in order to support them with uptake of climate-smart technologies . <p> CTCN will therefore be working closely with SMEs in the region in partnership with KCIC which offers incubation , capacity building and financing options to SMEs that are developing innovations to address climate change related challenges .
