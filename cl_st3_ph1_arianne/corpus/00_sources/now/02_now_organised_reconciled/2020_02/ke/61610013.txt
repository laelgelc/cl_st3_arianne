<h> Earth Hour 2020 : The time for global action <p> On 28 March , as I take my place amongst millions of people around the globe to celebrate Earth Hour , I am reminded again of what Earth Hour achieved since its inception and I am in awe of the strong power of people ! <p> Of all the global movements , Earth Hour stands out for making the seemingly impossible a reality . Earth Hour has , among other successes , called for new legislation for protection of forests in Russia , mobilized public support for the creation of a 3.5 million hectare marine-protected area in Argentina , planted 17 million trees in Kazakhstan and advocated for a ban for the use of plastic in Ecuador . <p> Now the need is stronger than ever ! Global biodiversity is declining at an unprecedented and alarming rate and the planet is on the brink of runaway global warming . <p> The science has never been so clear -- in 2019 , the report by scientists at IPBES , an Intergovernmental Platform of experts assessing biodiversity threats , showed that one million plants and animal species are currently threatened @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ percent of documented species are under threat . This dire reality demands robust global action in order to secure a sustainable future for people and the planet . <p> This has been said , over and over , for many years , in one form or another . So why is this year different ? Well , 2020 provides us with a unique opportunity , which we must grab hold of and not let go . In this historic year , world leaders will come together at Forums such as the Convention on Biodiversity , UN Climate Change Conference and UN General Assembly to make critical decisions on the environment , on behalf of all of us . <p> These decisions will set the agenda for the next decade and for our future , which is why we must speak up , louder than ever . It is essential to put nature at the core of the decisions to stop increasingly rapid nature loss , environmental decline , and safeguard all our futures . <p> 2020 must therefore become the ' year of global action ' on all levels . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the people on the street , in particular the young people whose future depends on securing action at these meetings , must influence and support them . <p> Movements such as Earth Hour play a pivotal role . I , and other young people , challenged decision-makers and advocated for change leading to new laws to protect the environment . <p> Now we are facing global issues which need united global actions of the world 's citizens and I strongly believe it is the power and pressure of the youth that will once again initiate the winds of change .
