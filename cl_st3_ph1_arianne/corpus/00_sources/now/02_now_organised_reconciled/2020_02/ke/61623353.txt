<p> Many parts of Kenya have been experiencing torrential rainfall for a couple of months now . This has resulted in floods and landslides . <p> The unusual weather can be attributed to the Indian Ocean Dipole . This is the difference in sea surface temperatures between the eastern and western tropical Indian Ocean . <p> In Kenya , when warmer sea surface temperatures are experienced heavy rainfall occurs , while hot dry conditions ( conducive for wildfires ) are experienced in Australia . When warmer sea surface temperatures are experienced off the West Coast of Australia , Australia is likely to experience heavy rainfall , while Kenya experiences drought conditions . <p> The larger the difference in temperatures between the eastern and western tropical Indian Ocean , the more severe the climatic effects will be . <p> This event is similar to the El Ni ? o Southern Oscillation that occurs in the tropical Pacific Ocean . <p> Sea surface temperatures are measured by the Interagency Global Climate Observing System just off the coasts of Kenya and western Australia . It is also , in part , responsible for the very hot , dry conditions over western Australia which have contributed to the conditions suitable for wildfires . Causes <p> The Indian Ocean Dipole is caused by changes in trade wind @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ are permanent winds that blow from east-to-west in the Earth 's equatorial region . <p> When trade winds blow , they push the surface water of oceans westwards . This causes upwelling -- when deep , cold water rises towards the surface -- off the west coasts of all southern hemisphere continents . Essentially , water is pulled away from the coastline , leaving a void which is filled by bottom water which moves up to the surface . <p> Surface water <p> This upwelled water is not exposed to sunlight until it reaches the surface , and is therefore much colder than the surrounding surface water . So the water is colder along the western coast of Australia . <p> If the trade winds relax , the strength of upwelling is significantly reduced . This increases the temperature of these western coastal areas , as the cooling effect of the water from lower regions of the water column is reduced , and the sun has a larger impact in warming the sea surface . <p> The changes in trade wind strength can , therefore , result in the formation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Indian Ocean Dipole can affect the weather because sea surface temperature in large water bodies affects the temperature and dynamics of the atmosphere above and adjacent to them . <p> Cold waters cool down the air directly above them , causing the cool , dense air to " sink " downwards and the formation of a high-pressure system . By contrast , warm water heats up the air directly above it . This results in air molecules expanding , becoming less dense , and rising . This uplift brings on a low-pressure system . <p> These systems then influence the surrounding continental and oceanic regions . <p> Low-pressure cells -- caused when oceans are warmer -- are atmospherically unstable , resulting in moist air rising , condensing to form clouds , and precipitating as rainfall . High-pressure cells -- caused when oceans are cooler -- inhibit rainfall , and result in hot , dry conditions due to the subsidence , when air " sinks " downwards . <p> The stronger the Indian Ocean Dipole , the stronger these pressure cells .
