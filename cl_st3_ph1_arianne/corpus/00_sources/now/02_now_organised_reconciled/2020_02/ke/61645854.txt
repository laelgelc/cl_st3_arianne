This war is still far from being won . <p> And yet , these two forms of capitalism face the same problems . Inequalities continue to grow within these systems , and their consumption patterns are depleting the planet . Some commentators call it " capitalism for the many " . Small and medium-sized enterprises and even a number of multinational corporations are working towards this new horizon . <p> Their reasoning can be summarised as follows : growing inequalities , the damaging effects of climate change , the disruption of the technological revolution and the steady rise of protectionism radically undermine the idea of a capitalist system that is solely geared towards maximising profit . <p> The African private sector can not avoid this movement . It must grasp its necessity and seize the resulting opportunities . <p> Ensuring business longevity and sustainable practices , these are necessities .
