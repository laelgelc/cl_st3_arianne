<h> Multilateral cooperation in the area of conflict-related security and development risks in Africa <h> Attachments <p> Over the past decade the impact of climate change on people 's everyday lives have become tangible . Its effects have contributed to loss of human life , it has undermined livelihoods , destroyed infrastructure , harmed national economies and stressed state budgets . Across the globe , its impacts have contributed to widened gender inequalities in different contexts . Climate change is also transforming and redefining the global security and development landscape . The implications of climate change for security and development has become increasingly recognized within the United Nations ( UN ) , African Union ( AU ) and Regional Economic Communities ( REC ) . The framing of climate change in the security and development discourse is undergoing an important change . In some spaces it is moving away from seeing climate change as a security ' threat , ' and instead frames it as climate-related security and development ' risks ' .
