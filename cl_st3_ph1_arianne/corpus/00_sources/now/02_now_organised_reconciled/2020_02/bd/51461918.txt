" <p> It may just seem like a string of words , but they happen to be some of the most pertinent worries that came up when I asked a bunch of young adults , aged between 20 to 26 , what makes them anxious . <p> I asked them if they have ever sought help , and just how difficult it is to navigate a society that is always loaded up on information . It can sometimes be overwhelming to say the least , and is veering fast towards individualism .
