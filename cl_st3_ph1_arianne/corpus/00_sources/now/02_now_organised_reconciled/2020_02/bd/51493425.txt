<h> The tough life of char dwellers <p> Climate change would affect char dwellers and people living in coastal areas the most with the increase in floods , cyclones and rise in sea levels Syed Zakir Hossain/Dhaka Tribune <p> The fate of other char dwellers living across the country is more or less the same as 70% of them rely on agriculture alone for their livelihood <p> Natural calamities and lack of employment are two of the main factors holding char people back from participating in mainstream life . <p> These two factors are also hindering their chances of a better livelihood , education , sanitation , and healthcare . <p> Abdul Bari , 35 , who lived in Purar Char , a char on the Brahmaputra river in Jatrapur union of Kurigram Sadar upazila , had the misfortune of experiencing river erosion not once , not twice , but 10 times . <p> Mentioning that he only earns Tk6,000 - Tk7,000 per month to feed a family of three children , his wife and mother , Bari said : " I was 11 when I witnessed our home being devoured by the river for the very first time @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ the 11th time . Due to this I was forced to shift my house on multiple occasions . Providing for my family is a daily struggle for me . <p> " Though I am a farmer , I also teach at a local private community school and so my children are being able to continue their studies . But those who are illiterate and earn less than me are suffering even more . We char dwellers are in a never ending fight for survival . " <p> The fate of other char dwellers living across the country is more or less the same as 70% of them rely on agriculture alone for their livelihood . <p> Experts say that char people require special arrangements to cope with the adverse effects of climate change and also urged the authorities concerned to form a designated foundation and a national policy for the development of this ill-fated segment of the population . <p> Commenting on the issue , Habibun Nahar , deputy minister of the Ministry of Environment , Forest and Climate Change , said : " The life of char dwellers is @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ We have visited these lands to know more about the people who live there , to learn about their survival strategies and also to understand the effects of climate change on their lives . Char dwellers have their own way of adapting to the changes . We are working towards the development of these people . " <p> There are 109 char upazilas , both coastal and river chars , in 32 districts across Bangladesh . <p> According to the " Bangladesh Household Income and Expenditure Survey 2016 , " 26% of households in the coastal char lands earn less than Tk7,000 per month , and 24.1% in the river char areas , while that figure stands at 22.6% nationally . <p> More than half of the children living in char areas of the country are deprived of primary education . Only 21.4% of people living in coastal char areas have access to electricity . <p> Adding to the misery , merely 33.6% of the households have proper sanitation in the coastal chars , and 31.3% in the river chars . <p> Access to clean drinking water and healthcare are @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ every day . <p> According to research conducted by Unnayan Shamannay , a non profit and non government research organization , a student living on the mainland , on average , has to go 0.92km to get to a primary school and 1.7km for a high school . Whereas , a child living in char areas has to travel 2.5km and 5.5km respectively to get the same educational opportunities . <p> Jahid Islam , member secretary of National Char Alliance ( NCA ) , an advocacy platform comprising over 50 organisations working for the rights of char dwellers , said : " Due to the lack of a designated organization , government allocation of funds for the development of char people goes unused . A foundation is needed to properly utilize money for the integrated progress of char dwellers . <p> " It is not possible to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals ( SDGs ) leaving char people behind . We need a national policy for the development of chars and people living there , immediately . "
