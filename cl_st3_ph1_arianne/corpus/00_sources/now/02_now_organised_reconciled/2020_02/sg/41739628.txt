<h> Former TERI chief RK Pachauri dies , aged 79 <p> Globally renowned environmentalist RK Pachauri died on Thursday after a prolonged cardiac ailment . He was 79 . He was the founder of TERI and head of the UN 's Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change when it won the Nobel Peace Prize in 2007 . <p> According to his family , Pachauri had undergone a major heart surgery before succumbing to his deteriorating health on Thursday . He died at his residence with his wife , daughter and son on his side . <p> " His courageous leadership allowed climate change to be recognized world over as the most pressing issue confronting the world and launched a new era of international deliberations and actions , " news agency PTI quoted a statement from his family . <p> TERI posted a series of tweets to express its condolence on Pachauri 's death . It said , " It is with immense sadness that we announce the passing away of Dr RK Pachauri , the Founder Director of TERI .
