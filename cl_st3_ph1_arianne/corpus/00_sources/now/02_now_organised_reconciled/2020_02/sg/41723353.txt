<h> Forum : Alarmed about cutting down of mature trees <p> Singapore prides itself on being a Garden City and , over the years , I have enjoyed the beauty , shade and serenity provided by our gardens and parks , as well as the mature trees lining our neighbourhood streets and roads . <p> However , in recent years , I have observed many instances of indiscriminate felling of mature trees around the Telok Blangah precincts , including the popular Mount Faber Park . <p> The trees probably took 30 to 40 years to grow and mature into tall , shady and magnificent beauties - enhancing and " softening " our heartland landscape , as well as providing cool shade to shelter us from the constant tropical heat . <p> I witnessed at least three mature trees being cut down along Telok Blangah Rise , including two Yellow Flames , on Feb 4 .
