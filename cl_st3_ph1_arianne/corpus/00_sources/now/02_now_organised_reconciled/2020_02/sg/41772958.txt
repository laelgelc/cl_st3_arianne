<h> SIFA 2020 : Sterling slate of shows <p> STORIES TOLD through actors ' fingers . Korean singers performing for you and only you in a room . The epic history of music recounted in two hours by a drag queen . Gravity-defying dancers performing on a stage perpendicular to the ground . Ghost stories , grief stories , Gogol and more . <p> The 2020 Singapore International Festival of Arts ( SIFA ) is the third and last edition to be helmed by festival director Gaurav Kripalani . And it looks to be his best . <p> The slate of shows running in May looks set to please connoisseurs and casual audiences alike . It strikes a balance between small niche curios and flamboyant crowd-pullers , risky propositions and surefire hits . <p> Mr Kripalani says : " I had only six months to programme the 2018 edition , and there was still a lot of learning to do in 2019 ... but I feel that with this 2020 edition , people can see the clear arc of our concept and programming .
