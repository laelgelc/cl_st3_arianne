Crowdfunding , as the name suggests , is all about engaging the crowd or the masses to co-fund social projects and startup ventures . <p> Increasingly , crowdfunding has become an online activity , usually promoted through reliable platforms , where several people invest , lend , or contribute small amounts of money for one 's business or social idea . <p> In India , crowdfunding is steadily taking a strong concrete shape , becoming the fastest , easiest , and the most popular way of procuring funds . These platforms work through individuals or organisations that invest or donate to crowdfunding projects , with an intention of charity or in return for a potential profit or reward . <p> Donors that back campaigns usually receive an acknowledgement , a small reward or some other form of financial gain @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for a long way <p> Crowdfunding allows a large number of people to invest in affordable amounts , which together can amount to huge sums . It helps raise financial support for people , businesses , ventures , and charities , especially for those who have no other funding source to turn to or when other funding sources are too expensive to be borne by the fledgeling business or social ideas . <p> Funding through crowds helps raise money with little or no initial costs , which is another benefit of this resource . Not everyone can successfully get bank loans or access other ways of borrowings . Crowdfunding , therefore , plays an important role as the best possible way to fund a venture without having to gather debts . Here , funds can be procured by the promise of profit sharing or rewards . <p> It helps to set up a project or business and bring it in the media 's attention through the online platform .
