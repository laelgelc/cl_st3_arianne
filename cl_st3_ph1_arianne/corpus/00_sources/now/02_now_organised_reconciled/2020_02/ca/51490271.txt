<h> Proposed textile disposal ban approved at city committee <p> The city 's infrastructure and operations committee is recommending council ban the disposal of textiles at the local landfill . If passed by council , residents would be required to donate their used clothing and other fabrics to organizations that accept and recycle them . Nugget File Photo <p> A committee of North Bay council is recommending the city ban the collection and disposal of textiles at the local landfill . <p> A majority of the city 's infrastructure and operations committee voted in favour of the ban Tuesday , which will head to council for a final vote at a future date . <p> Along with a ban , the committee also is proposing the city undertake an education campaign to raise awareness about reducing and reusing textiles . <p> Staff have already received quotes to print posters and locate them at city facilities and local stores , such as non- and for-profit organizations , that reuse textiles . <p> Speaking to members of the committee , senior environment and facilities engineer Karin Pratte said the plan would be to put the ban in place April 22 , @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ do to promote diversion from a landfill site I would say is best practice and that 's what we 're doing here , " Pratte said .
