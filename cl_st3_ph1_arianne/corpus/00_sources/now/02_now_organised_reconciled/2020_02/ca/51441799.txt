The opulent affair , replete with lobsters for snacking , an ice sculpture , and an open bar , was one of many regular functions held by elite law firms to draw elite aspiring attorneys into the fold . But about 30 students put their job prospects at risk when they interrupted the event with a demonstration . <p> As Paul , Weiss attorney and partner Kannon Shanmugam got up to deliver a speech , a small group of students unfurled a banner that read #DropExxon and cut him off with a protest song .
