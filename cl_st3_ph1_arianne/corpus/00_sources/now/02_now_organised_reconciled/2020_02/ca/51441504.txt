<h> Rural well water quality monitored in Huron <p> An innovative program developed to sample rural well properties to monitor water quality was recommended to Huron County in 2001 . <p> County council recently discussed sampling results from 2019 . <p> After the preliminary study , representative wells called " sentinel wells " from the area were chosen for long-term monitoring . They included well clusters in South Huron , Huron East , ACW and Central Huron . <p> Of the selected wells , most are in bedrock formations and range in depth from 14 to 95 metres . <p> Recommendations to monitor these wells on a quarterly basis were initiated from the Groundwater Quality Assessment . Initial sampling of wells was completed in 2002 and in 2004 Huron GeoSciences , Maitland Valley Conservation Authority ( MVCA ) , Ausable Bayfield Conservation Authority ( ABCA ) and the County of Huron formed a partnership to continue sampling and monitoring the selected well clusters across the county . <p> It was agreed that all wells be sampled annually and analyzed for chemical parameters . <p> According to the report , most of Huron County sits on the Dundee bedrock formation @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ water . This can cause vulnerability to drinking water sources , mostly from surface contaminants such as road salt , bacteria or nutrients . <p> The more shallow a bedrock well is , the more the well is susceptible to surface activities affecting water quality . <p> According to the report , a depth of 15 metres or less is considered shallow and one of the wells identified for sampling fits that criteria . <p> Another issue is the potential for sinkholes . When dissolution of the rock by water is so well developed , bedrock is subject to air pressure changes . <p> Sampling results from 2019 have yielded data , which can be utilized to observe trends and describe water quality characteristics . <p> Based on information in the report , Huron County requested the evaluation of seven parameters in 2016 . That included testing for lead , arsenic , uranium , fluoride , sodium , nitrates and E. Coli . <p> A purpose of testing for such contamination would provide indication that activities on the ground surface might be affecting the groundwater . <p> The maximum acceptable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ applies to water at the point of consumption . It is noted that lead can be present as a result of corrosion of pipes or brass fittings . <p> Lead can also be found in the environment from a variety of sources including leaded gasoline , and naturally in sedimentary rocks . <p> Of the six sentinel wells , only two wells tested registered with 0.01mg/L of lead or less . <p> As of January 2018 , the Ministry of the Environment and Climate Change approved changes to Ontario regulations on drinking water quality for levels of arsenic . The current level is at the value of 0.010mg/L . <p> According to the report , elevated arsenic is observed sporadically in Huron County and is elevated in the Dungannon municipal system . <p> Of the six wells monitored , arsenic was found above the standard in one well located in South Huron and above half of the standard in two wells located in Central Huron and in South Huron . <p> In summary , the report displayed that surface water indicators are sporadic and low .
