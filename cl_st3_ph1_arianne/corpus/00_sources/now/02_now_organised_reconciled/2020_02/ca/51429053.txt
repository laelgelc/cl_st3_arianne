But they also said building stronger relationships overall , finding opportunities for Canadian business , and engaging on issues like climate change and the empowerment of women and girls are all part of the agenda . <p> The officials at the briefing spoke only on the condition they not be identified . <p> Trudeau will start in Ethiopia , the continent 's fastest-growing economy , which is the headquarters of the African Union . <p> He intends to meet individually with a number of African leaders on the margins of the gathering , including Ethiopian @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ equality and the environment . <p> He later goes to Senegal for an official visit , where he will meet with President Macky Sall .
