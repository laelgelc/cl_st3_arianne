Thirty-one per cent of respondents said they would never vote Conservative . <p> When asked what was the important issue facing Canada , Conservative voters list debts and deficits , government spending , and the economy and taxes , a list not surprisingly reflecting the priorities of the Conservative campaign . <p> Accessible voters , on the other hand , reflect all voters : their top issue is the environment and climate change .
