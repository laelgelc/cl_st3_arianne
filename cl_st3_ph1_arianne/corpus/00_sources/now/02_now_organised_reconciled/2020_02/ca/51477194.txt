Gino Donato/Sudbury Star file photo <p> Sudbury could be getting a lot more bike-friendly in the coming months . <p> At next week 's meeting of the operations committee , staff will ask " that the City of Greater Sudbury uses the remainder of the Ontario Municipal Commuter Cycling funding to construct the segment of the Paris-Notre Dame bikeway from Lasalle Boulevard to Wilma Street in 2020 . " <p> The bikeway is good news for cyclists in Sudbury and as staff points out , it supports two of city council 's objectives , including the mitigation of the effects of climate change . <p> " The bikeway will provide a viable @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ transportation , which may lead to increased mitigation of the impacts of climate change locally , " a staff report said . " It is also in line with council 's strategic objective of ' create a healthier community . ' " Installing safe , separated cycling infrastructure and providing connection to major destinations will help Greater Sudbury attain silver bicycle-friendly community status . " <p> The Wilma to Lasalle stretch will cost taxpayers about $3.5 million , which will be funded using cycling infrastructure accounts . <p> " The total of these accounts plus the Ontario Municipal Commuter Cycling program funds is approximately $3.9 million , " staff said . <p> The bikeway is more than 9 km long and once complete , will connect Regent Street in the city 's South End to Turner Avenue , just past the Notre Dame-Lasalle intersection . <p> " A feasibility study was undertaken for the Paris Street-Notre Dame Avenue corridor to retrofit existing asphalt boulevards to provide a barrier curb-separated cycle track for cyclists , " the staff report indicates . " This 9-km bikeway , recommended as a short-term priority @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ spine of the city 's growing commuter cycling network once complete . " <p> The remaining 9 km of the bikeway is projected to cost a total of $20 million . <p> Only a small section of the bikeway currently exists , from York Street to Walford Road in the hospital area . It was completed in 2017 . But as staff points out , there is a need in the city for more bike-friendly routes , especially connecting different parts of the city . <p> " Currently , Paris Street and Notre Dame Avenue are primary arterial roads with average annual daily traffic volumes which range from 26,000 to 32,000 vehicles , " the report said . " Both streets have sidewalks on both sides of the corridor , with the exception of the east side of Notre Dame Avenue between Louis Street and Leslie Street . " <p> The remainder of the project has been separated into two phases . The first extends from Walford Road to Regent Street , while the northern phase begins at York Street and ends at Turner Avenue . The project " also @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Terrace in the south phase and a sidewalk on the east side of Notre Dame Avenue between Louis Street and Leslie Street in the north phase of the bikeway . " <p> Council adopted the Community Energy and Emissions Plan last November . According to the plan , transportation is responsible for 43 per cent of the city 's greenhouse has emissions . <p> " One of the ways Greater Sudbury will reduce greenhouse gas emissions from transportation is by encouraging people to get out of their cars and onto bikes and transit for their daily commute and other frequent trips , " the report noted . " A key opportunity and measure of success for this project will be its ability to transition residents from travelling in single-occupant vehicles to choosing to cycle as transportation . <p> " To successfully achieve this mode shift , the bikeway project is being designed to appeal to the significant proportion -- 60 per cent -- of the population that is interested but concerned . The interested but concerned group describes people who are interested in cycling but have significant concerns that limit @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ areas with high speed and high volumes of motor-vehicle traffic and inconsistent bicycle facilities . They may be attracted to cycling by the implementation of separated bicycle facilities , which provide more space between cyclists and motorists . " <p> In addition to the environmental benefits , the bikeway will connect to existing cycling infrastructure and trails , thus providing links to downtown from College Boreal , the Terry Fox sports complex , the Canada Revenue Agency and Pioneer Manor . It is also well-situated to GOVA 's Route 1 Mainline , which links Walmart in the South End to the New Sudbury Shopping Centre . <p> Staff said the bikeway will be good for the local economy and will have a positive impact on quality of life for residents , especially those living nearby . <p> " With nearly 13 per cent of the city 's population , or 21,000 people , living either directly on or adjacent to the bikeway , and 30 per cent of all businesses in the city situated on or accessed by this corridor , the bikeway has the potential to support and encourage @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ corridors into more vibrant , connected and equitable streets that improve quality of life for all Greater Sudbury residents , " the report concludes .
