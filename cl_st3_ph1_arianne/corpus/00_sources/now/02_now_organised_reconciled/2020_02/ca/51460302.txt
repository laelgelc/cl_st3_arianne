His own state of New York placed a temporary moratorium on the brute force technology . <p> So what does Ingraffea have to say about the familiar sounding claim made by Natural Resources Canada that " hydraulic fracturing has been used by the industry to safely stimulate oil and gas production in North American conventional reservoirs for more than 60 years " ? <p> Or the claim made by the Canadian Association of Petroleum Producers ( CAPP ) on its pro-fracking website that " hydraulic fracturing is a safe activity regulated by government " ? <p> More myth than fact , these claims contain only a fractured kernel of the whole truth , says Ingraffea . <p> A small U.S. oil firm did indeed perform the @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ conventional gas well using 1,000 gallons of napalm as well as sand from the Arkansas River . <p> So the practice of pumping small volumes ( 1,000 to 10,000 gallons ) of toxic fluid into vertical wells ( 2,400 feet ) using about 600 horsepower of pressure is indeed 60 years old . <p> But that 's not what is happening in Pennsylvania , Texas or northern British Columbia today . Now industry injects millions of gallons of water into wellbores two miles deep that then angle or deviate horizontally another kilometre underground .
