<h> Antarctica 's glaciers are melting so fast , you can swim in them . In a Speedo . <p> This story was originally published by Grist and appears here as part of the Climate Desk collaboration <p> Even as someone who spends most of her time thinking about climate change , it 's easy for me to forget about the looming danger of changes happening at the bottom of the earth . But Lewis Pugh , a British endurance swimmer and ocean advocate , does n't want anyone to forget about the melting glaciers of Antarctica , and to get our attention , he decided to go for a swim . <p> On January 23 , Pugh , who 's 50 , became the first person to swim in one of the supraglacial lakes of East Antarctica . These are lakes and rivers that form on the surface of thick glacial ice as it melts from above . A study of supraglacial lakes in Antarctica published last fall found more than 65,000 of them at the peak of the summer melt season in January 2017 . We need immediate action from all nations to protect our planet , " Pugh told the BBC .
