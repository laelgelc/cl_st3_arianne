There are plenty of other ways to bring in such a levy , they add . <p> " The federal government would be able to legislate an equally stringent carbon pricing regime using the criminal law , " said law professor David Wright from the University of Calgary . <p> On Monday , the Alberta Court of Appeal ruled 4-1 that the carbon tax is unconstitutional . They 're counting on a rarely used section of the Constitution that allows Ottawa to step in over matters of " national concern " -- a clause that has come before the Supreme Court only three times since Confederation . <p> " It 's just not an area courts have explored , " said law professor Eric Adams from the University of Alberta . He compares it to an old trunk found in a closet -- nobody 's sure what 's in it . <p> A national concern has to be something beyond the ability of provinces to address .
