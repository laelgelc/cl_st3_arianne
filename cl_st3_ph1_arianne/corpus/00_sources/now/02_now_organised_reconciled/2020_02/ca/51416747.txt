" The pledge appears to be in line with Canada 's commitment to reach net-zero emissions by the same date , and adds to the pressure Ottawa is under to approve the massive Frontier oil sands mine in the face of vocal opposition from environmental groups . <p> The federal cabinet must decide to approve , reject or delay a decision by the end of February . <p> Story continues below advertisement <p> Mr. Wilkinson welcomed Teck 's new goal to be carbon neutral by 2050 , but said the promise lacks detail needed to fully understand the impact . <p> " I certainly have some questions about exactly @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ that , " Mr. Wilkinson said . For example , he pointed out that carbon neutral can be defined in different ways and because the company has defined it at the corporate level , he does n't know what the pledge means for each of Teck 's projects . <p> He added , though , that there are issues outside of the company 's carbon neutrality pledge that need to be addressed before the government will make a decision . <p> The company clarified Monday that its pledge will apply to all projects , including the proposed Frontier mine . <p> " Our objective is to be carbon neutral across all of our operations and at every operation , " Teck 's director of public affairs , Doug Brown , told The Globe and Mail . He said the company will prioritize policies that avoid new emissions and directly reduce current emissions over investing in nature-based solutions or purchasing carbon offsets . <p> The company 's pledge applies to what are called Scope 1 and 2 emissions .
