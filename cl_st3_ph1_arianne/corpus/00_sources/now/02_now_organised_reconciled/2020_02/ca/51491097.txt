Our port is cluttered , our rail lines muted , our supply chains disarrayed , and our store shelves will dwindle and our factories will idle if there is not something significant done . <p> The long-range importance of reconciliation to make peace as a country , and the magnitude of the current civil disobedience , obliges the prime minister and the premiers to resolve their differences on how to tackle climate change over the next decades to determine how to tackle reconciliation over the next months so the economy can restore itself over the next weeks . <p> It wo n't be easy .
