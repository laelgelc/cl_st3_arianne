<p> Pembroke city council has taken a few more steps into a greener world . <p> At its Tuesday , Feb. 4 meeting , council approved a motion to actively pursue the declaration of a climate emergency and to direct city staff to report back at the next meeting on Feb. 18 on the matter of a developing a climate action advisory committee for the city . <p> The motion , made by Councillor Brian Abdallah , was approved in a recorded vote by a six to one margin . Coun. Andrew Plummer voted against the motion . <p> Pembroke Mayor Michael LeMay said he was pleased to see not only the city but other levels of government beginning to take some steps towards protection of the environment . <p> " I believe it will help raise awareness of the environmental challenges that we all face whether we are a municipality , a province or a country of the world , " LeMay said . " I believe it really is on us as a council to commit to protecting our environment , our economy , and our community from the impacts of climate change .
