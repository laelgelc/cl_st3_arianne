<h> Species at risk face slow death by red tape , biologists warn <p> The Species At Risk Act is clogged with new requests to protect endangered species because of increased red tape and a government focused on economic growth that is squeezing plant and animal habitat , say biologists . <p> The 12-year-old legislation is supposed to rescue species from their slide toward extinction by having a panel of experts recommend their placement on a special list to Environment Minister Leona Aglukkaq , who after placing them on the list asks public servants to draw up a recovery strategy for the species . <p> The federal government officially added 55 new species to the pre-listing phase of the process on Tuesday .
