Climate issues are set to be one of the main talking points at the World Economic Forum in the Swiss ski resort of Davos this week , but a survey of CEOs released Monday shows that they are not even ranked among the top ten threats to business growth . <p> In its annual report ahead of the gathering in Davos , financial services group PwC said climate change and environmental issues are ranked as the 11th biggest threat to their companies ' growth prospects . Though up one spot from the same survey a year ago , climate-related issues lag way behind other concerns such as over-regulation , which ranks as the number 1 worry .
