<h> Group helps ' elevate the public voice ' for environmental rights <p> Friends of the Porcupine River Watershed director Roxanne Filion and Canadian Environmental Law Association northern services counsel Kerrie Blaise at the Porcupine Watershed open house . Maija Hoggett/TimminsToday <p> People had the chance to learn more about what 's happening at Porcupine Lake this week . <p> For the Porcupine River Watershed Open House at the Maurice Londry Community Centre in South Porcupine residents were able to talk directly with local experts . The event featured representation from Friends of the Porcupine River Watershed ( FPRW ) , City of Timmins , Canadian Environmental Law Association ( CELA ) , Newmont Porcupine , Porcupine Health Unit , Mattagami Region Conservation Authority , Lakeshore Gold and Glencore Kidd . The Ministry of Environment was also on hand to answer questions . <p> In Porcupine and South Porcupine , when the system 's capacity is exceeded sewage is bypassed into Porcupine Lake . <p> Upgrading the system is required for the city to meet a Ministry of Environment and Climate Change ( MOECC ) director 's order , and there is a two-phase project to remediate it . @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ Station 4 and installing two stormwater equalization tanks at a site on Highway 101 just east of the Whitney Arena -- is more than expected , and is anticipated to be functional in the fall . <p> Phase two is upgrading the mechanical , electrical and instrumentation systems , along with other work , at the remaining five pump stations . That work is expected to be done by the spring . <p> To clean up the lake and its watershed , FPRW has been working with CELA , a non-profit specialty law clinic . <p> CELA northern services counsel Kerrie Blaise was at this week 's open house to support the group and the public . <p> She said people were asking the perfect questions -- whether the lake is good for swimming , drinking or fishing . <p> " When those questions ca n't be answered in the affirmative and with confidence then we know there 's a lot more work to be done , " she said . <p> " There 's a lot of concern as well , people have observed changes over the years and @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ say , quite a high level of concern and not a lot of action and information available yet . " <p> As a legal aid organization , Blaise said they look for ways to advance environmental justice . <p> " A number one way to advance environmental justice is participation in local decision making and especially decision making that affects the environment , " she said . <p> She said everyone has the right to know what 's in their water , air and community , whether it 's pollutants , toxics or good things . <p> " We are a member of the PLC and through that , that 's one way we 're trying to get at that information to understand do we study the health of the lake , is that data available to people so when there is a discharge into the lake do people even know ?
