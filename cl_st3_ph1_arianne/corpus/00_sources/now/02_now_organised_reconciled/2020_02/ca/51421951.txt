<p> Rising global temperatures , worsening wildfires and more intense extreme weather events are all indicators that the climate emergency is escalating and that the world faces ecological catastrophe . <p> Climate change is no longer an issue of interest only to scientists and environmental activists . Concern about the climate emergency has gone global , with ordinary people taking to the streets around the world to demand action to halt the warming of our shared environment . <p> In this age of climate awareness , companies and industries linked to activities that contribute to global warming are coming under closer scrutiny from the general public .
