<h> Why 100 per cent renewable energy production still is n't enough <p> Canada exports two-thirds of its oil production today . For our energy sector to prosper , we should export two-thirds of our renewables tomorrow . <p> With the first one-third of our renewables production satisfying 100 per cent of our domestic electricity consumption , the other two-thirds -- equivalent to 200 per cent -- would be for export . So our renewables target should n't just be for 100 per cent of domestic use , but for 300 per cent ( of domestic use ) . <p> Over the past decade , the idea of 100 per cent emission free electrical grids has gone from infeasible to inevitable .
