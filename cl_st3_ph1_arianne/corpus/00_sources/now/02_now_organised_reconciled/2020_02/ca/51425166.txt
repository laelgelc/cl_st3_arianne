Largely , the so-called Red Tories have left the party and gone elsewhere . There is no " extremist wing " -- that 's imaginary . Fundamental changes have shaped the base of the party that reflect differences in outlook , preferences and values from the great majority of Canadians and have little to do with what someone thinks about gay pride parades . As in the U.K. and the U.S. , authoritarian or ordered populism has polarized Canada into two incommensurable camps . <p> The Conservative Party that most Canadians have known -- the political centre that previously had the ability to find centre terrain on the most divisive issues of the day -- has disappeared . What contemporary Conservative strategists and the media seem to have significant difficulty recognizing is that Canadian politics has become much more like American politics -- it 's become tribal .
