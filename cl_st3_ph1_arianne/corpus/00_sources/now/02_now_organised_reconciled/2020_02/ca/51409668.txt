<h> This is teacher reality <p> Teachers in Ontario have a starting salary of around $38,000 a year . They are required to have at least a four-year university undergraduate degree and a two-year teacher 's college degree . In this region , most have master 's degrees and continuing programs to stand out from the rest of the applicants . It takes a decade of permanent placement to reach salaries of $80,000 . <p> Contrary to what our premier and minister of education wants you to believe , teachers are not fighting for compensation . They have asked for a two per cent cost of living raise .
