" It was a staff of one , and by the time I left , we had 17 people working on stuff . They 're doing school gardens in schools all across the region with more than 850 students . The organization aims to promote legislation , projects , research and education that benefit the economic and social well-being of Canadian farmers , as well as informing sustainable Canadian agriculture practices . <p> The NFU has thousands of members across the country producing a variety of commodities , including livestock , grains , fruits and vegetables . It 's the only Canadian farm organization incorporated by an act of Parliament . <p> The NFU " promotes policies that will maintain family farms as the primary food producers in Canada , " an NFU document states . <p> " It 's an organization of farmers and people that care about the food system , that want to make sure that the food system is sustainable economically , socially and environmentally , in a way that takes some of the power back from @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ farmers , and tries to make sure that Canada has a strong food sovereignty , which means ownership over our own food production , " Shaw explained . <p> " That 's challenging , in this realm of international trade agreements that really undermine our abilities to have sovereignty over our food and control over our own food production .
