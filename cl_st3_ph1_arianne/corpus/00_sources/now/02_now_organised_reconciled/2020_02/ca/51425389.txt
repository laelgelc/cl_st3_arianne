<h> Cochranites share their pre-budget thoughts with their MP <p> Roughly twenty-five Cochranites filled a boardroom downstairs at the Legion on January 25 to participate in a public consultation session hosted by Banff-Airdrie MP Blake Richards . The Conservative MP hosts a similar session , which his team calls a ' Pre-Budget Consultation ' , every year ahead in the lead-up to budget deliberations in Parliament . <p> " Over the years I 've been able to take suggestions that come right from these meetings and get them put into budgets , " Richards said . <p> " More so when we were the government , it 's a little harder to get them in the budgets now . I 'm hoping we can put forward ideas that may be accepted , and it gives me the information I need to best represent the constituency . " <p> Richards presented locals in attendance with a form that was to guide their conversation through the hour-and-a-half session . <p> " We give people a bit of context and background on where things are at , how the financial picture of the government is , what the government has proposed as @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ of the priorities should be , and then we 're asking people for feedback , " the MP explained . <p> The local residents in attendance were split into four groups , and for an hour they would deliberate within their group . <p> " The great thing about doing them in the way we are ... is that it forces everybody to think , " said Richards . <p> " You come here with your ideas , and then when you 're in a group you find that people have other ideas , maybe even contradicting each other in some cases , so it 's trying to figure out what the solution is when you all come together .
