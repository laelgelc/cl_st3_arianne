The government gives lip service to preservation of species . <p> Ontario Nature and other environmental groups have shown that Bill 108 would : <p> * Allow developers to pay into a fund to proceed with development while harming species @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ @ for affected species . <p> * Change the assessment criteria such that if a species is not considered endangered in another region outside Ontario , then the species would not get status in Ontario even if the species is threatened or endangered within Ontario . In fact , the large majority of the almost 200 species currently classified as threatened or endangered would not meet this criterion . Among other issues , this ignores the consideration that northern range expansion of species will likely be required as habitat further south becomes less suitable for species stressed by climate change . <p> * Dilute the composition of the Committee on the Status of Species at Risk in Ontario ( COSSARO ) , the committee of scientific experts that makes decisions on the listing of endangered species , by the addition of those with a vaguely specified " local knowledge .
