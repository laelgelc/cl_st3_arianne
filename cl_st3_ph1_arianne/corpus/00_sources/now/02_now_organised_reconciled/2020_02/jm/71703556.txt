development of a Gender Assessment Report , and a Gender and Climate Change Strategy and Action Plan ; development of a Green Bond Market Assessment and Road Map ; establishment of a National Transparency Working Group ; and completion of an institutional assessment report . <p> So far under the project , a Regional Scoping Study has been completed as well as an NDA toolkit , a regional action plan for the private sector , and a GCF Country Programme developed . <p> Additionally , regional training workshops were conducted with 11 representatives , a readiness proposal was drafted and a project development manual prepared
